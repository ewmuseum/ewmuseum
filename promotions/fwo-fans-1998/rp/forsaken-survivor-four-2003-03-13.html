Forsaken - Survivor (Four)

13 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The line was in every movie, TV show, romance novel, and timeless tale of friendship. Everyone would declare love by reminding their friend or partner "I'd take a bullet for you."... How many would ever do it? Two or three, maybe...but Amy wasn't awake to count. No nightmares plagued her, just a broken sleep made better with the assistance of a heavy dose of morphine. She didn't have to deal with any horrible scenes in front of her at that moment, blissfully unaware of anything but the comfortable blackness of her sleep. Someone else in the room didn't have it quite so easy... Brandon couldn't believe where he was. Less than eight hours ago if you would have told him he would be spending the morning in the hospital he would have called you a liar. Now though the beeps and ticks that he unfortunately knew all too well echoed in his ears. The heart monitor almost had a calming affect on him. Almost. His massive hands still shook after what he had witnessed. As he looked to his hands blood could still be seen around his fingernails and in the creases of his skin. It was her blood. Amy Campbell's blood, the woman that saved his life. The woman that now lay in this hospital with a hole in her body caused by a psycho stalker intent on making Brand Frontier her own. Also, the woman that he... loved? As those thoughts tumbled in the cobwebs of his weary mind he barely heard the sheets move. Blinking, shaking his head Brandon looked over. Amy's eyes were nearly crusted shut and her head moved slightly. As she slowly and wearily blinked her eyes open Brandon's face lit up. Amy had lost a lot of blood after she had thrown herself in the way of Sandra's malicious attack. An attack that would have surely killed the mighty hero. The blood loss was easily fixed but that didn't stop Brandon from worrying. Especially now. Brandon reached for Amy's hand but the redhead moved it first reaching herself to her other hand. Before she'd even noticed another presence in the room, Amy had fingered her way to the source of her discomfort and panic: A needle shoved into her wrist, biting at an old phobia and feeding her whatever vital fluids she might need but to her, nothing in that little baggy was as important as getting that damned thing out of her wrist. And she did so, pulling the IV out with a small yelp of pain... With that yelp, the normally smoothly flowing muscles of her stomach spasmed, and the fury of the pain that emanated from her abdomen not only reminded her of where she was and what had happened, but nearly knocked her back out. Before it had that chance, though, she felt a hand much stronger than her entire body at that point squeeze one of hers to steady the little redhead. Brandon had reached quickly for her hand as the IV tube dripped on the floor. The action startled him for a second but he was more concerned with making sure Amy was all right. Holding her hand tightly, almost as if he was afraid to let her go he leaned over, a smile naturally appearing on his face. "Hey there little hero. You okay?" Trying to lighten the situation wasn't always Brandon's easy suit, but he tried. "I'm no....urgh, what the hell did they pump me full of?" A quick snap of frustration overcame her when she realized that whatever painkiller the doctors had given her had only made her sleepy, and slightly impaired her speech. Then again, they were a blessing...she didn't want to imagine how much pain she'd be in without it. Nonetheless, Amy labored to finish the statement that had instantly come to her mind before she passed out at the arena the night before. "I'm no hero. Heroes make getting shot look cool...remind me to sue Hollywood." "Well, you're my hero." Brandon reached up and smoothed back Amy's frizzed hair. The many hours on the pillow had wreaked havoc with it and he tried his best not to smirk. Remembering why she was here was really all it took for him not to though. She was here because of him. Maybe Ana was right: Everyone around him always suffers. Shaking his head a little Brandon looked back to Amy again, refocusing. "I'm so sorry dear, I... I know that doesn't fix anything but...I am. You're here because of me..." At first, Amy'd been too mixed up to tell what the look in Brandon's eyes was. She'd found it peculiar, but then realized he was staring at her with more worry and maybe even love than anyone ever had. Her need to calm him overrode the need to figure him out though, so her statement had come quickly to her lips, not even thought out. "No, I'm here because of me..." She had to pause a beat to lift the greenish, papery hospital shirt up a little to survey the thick square bandage just above her right hip. It was stained with a small patch of blood that suddenly deterred Amy from picturing what might be underneath. "I'm here because I didn't want you hurt. It's not like it was your fault that psycho was out for you. She was already coming after me anyway...you and I probably both would've wound up dead." "...No... Amy, you're here because of me..." Brandon had thought long and hard these last eight hours sitting by her side. Everything that had happened was not directly his fault but things spiraled this way because of him. "A few hours ago the police managed to track down where Sandra lived... they found... things..." The fWo's paragon hesitated, should he really be telling her these things now? "What things? What...." Another shot of pain interrupted Amy's words for a moment. It was bad, but it could have been worse: The bullet had hit the small fatty area just above her hip, barely avoiding any organs. Had it been even centimeters lower, her hip would've been shattered; Centimetres to the side and she might've had a second belly button. Now there was a concept: She'd risked her career, mobility and even life itself for the man sitting in front of her. What got her thinking even more was the fact that he was still clutching her hand... "What was she?" She finally spoke again. "An ex-girlfriend or something? She was sending me weird notes...telling me I was getting too close to 'her man'..." "Her name's not Sandra Peake, it's Brandi Taylor and... and she was this nutcase that stalked me about four years ago. They caught her and as far as I knew she had gotten some sort of help... I guess it wasn't enough." Brandon couldn't believe what he was saying. It sounded so surreal. "She supposedly was some sports writer or something. One of the reasons she managed to get a spot at the Observer." "Sandra....Sandra Peake..." The realization suddenly came. "SHE was the one who wrote all that shit about me!" The sudden flash of anger sent Amy shooting up...only to be grasped by Brandon and the now familiar searing of the pain in her stomach. "Whoa, whoa whoa..." Brandon grabbed Amy's shoulders instinctively. A few years ago he was in the same predicament young Aimz found herself in now. Amy grimaced as the hot pain shot through her stomach. Letting herself go limp, she simply sunk into Brandon's arms. Filled with mixed emotions as the pain slowly subsided, a sense of safety washed over her as she bit on her lip. "From the things I could weasel out of the captain she was trying to drive people she thought of as a threat away from me. They found a diary, some notes... she's been planning this for years. Ana and the kids were her primary concern. They... of course... believe she was the one that shot Chris. You were one of the ones she saw as a threat simply because you were female. Then we began this relationship and it just made you that much more a target." Holding on to her Brandon slid a leg up on the bed as Amy simply moved over. He leaned her back leaning with her, not giving much thought to what he was doing. It just felt right. "Remind me NEVER to try that again. And Peake..err...Taylor.......she must have followed you. She saw us kiss at New Year's...." Amy had to pause, for some reason saying that felt so awkward yet natural in the same instance, but it planted a pain even greater than the bullet hole in her abdomen. "Because...when I got back, well...the first note was sitting on the dresser in my room. It hasn't stopped since...she's been getting into my rooms.....I got nothing the week she shot your brother-in-law, and I knew something had happened." "You've... you've been getting these notes and you never mentioned them to me? Why? I... I know things have been weird since...that night but you should have came to me. I'm always going to be here for you..." Stroking back another stray hair Brandon thought about that last statement. He'd always be there for her. What exactly did he mean by that? He didn't really know, he just knew it felt right when he said it. "Okay...let's say I told you. Now, you'd want to be around me to protect me, or we'd have to get to the bottom of it together. The last thing I wanted to to was be around you. It's...hard..." She was trying to say so much, but not much at the same time, just hoping things didn't get too awkward. "You're married and, well....yeah. Besides, you had enough to deal with." "You're more than just a work associate Amy, we both know that. I... you said the last thing you wanted to do was be around me? Why?" Brandon looked to her for answers as she looked to him. Their eyes, their lips just inches apart. "You being in danger was far more an issue than squabbles between two people who grew apart and simply didn't know it. We've... she, that is Ana, well... Ana and I aren't together any more. We decided that it's been over for awhile and we were just afraid to admit it." "It hurts to think that you didn't want to be around me and got hurt because of it. I dunno.. I'm just rambling I guess." It took a second, but Amy had to collect her thoughts - not to mention composure - after Brandon's speil. "That's the thing...I thought the first note was from Ana. I thought someone had called her at the party to tell her what happened. I though....I thought if I showed you, you might think the same thing and your family would fall. Plus it hurts to be around you...and I know I was hurting you badly, straining your marriage. I was.....I'm just...I'm in the way." Just like she'd been in the way of that bullet... "You've been there for me whether you realize it or not. You have been one of the few good things I can think back about these last few months. Ana just used our closeness as a channel for everything else that was going on. I wish... I know that now, we talked... for a long time last night." Brandon continued to ramble out incoherent thoughts. He wanted Amy to understand, he wanted her to feel that being around him wasn't a burden because it wasn't, it actually lit up his heart. "Just listen to me for a second okay? You... I... I'm not going to lose you Amy. As a friend or whatever else, I want you in my life. I don't know how much clearer I can make it for you." Drowsy as she was getting, the redhead couldn't help but notice how close he remained to her - just inches away. "Wh...Whatever else, eh? Well, after all of this...now there's nothing else stopping me from ending up as 'whatever else', is there?" The redhead tried to fashion a weak grin on his behalf, "This'll only hurt for a couple of weeks, and these days in the hospital sure will give us some time to catch up. It'll be like two days - they said I'll heal up fast...nothing major, just a bit of broken tissue." Then she smiled genuinely - mostly to herself - for the first time in days, however weakly, then gestured toward her still-exposed stomach, bandage and all. "And I think you should know by now that I'm there for you, too." Brandon laughed. It was a laugh filled with a comfortable energy, one that he had grown addicted to during these three short months. Lightly Brandon let his hand fall to her stomach. Barely touching the outside of the bandage he could feel the heat radiating from the fresh wound. "Yeah, I guess that's an understatement..." "At least... at least now we get a chance, all of us, to finally heal." "Well...hopefully my healing comes pretty fast...like I said, it's just a little flesh wound." Amy smiled again, though understanding that Brandon wouldn't buy her 'I feel no pain' bit for the life of him. "But what about you? Think you can.....maybe move on? Past Ana and all of this, that is..." "With you around me, I don't think there's anything I can't do..." That was when Brandon leaned in, hoping to close the thought by finishing the kiss that the crowd on January 1st had interrupted... But that wasn't to be, as usual. A tall man clad in blue and black knocked on the door but didn't wait, just stepped into the room holding a pen and paper. Obviously he didn't feel as if he'd interrupted anything as he started to mumble "Mister Roche, Miss Campbell..." and nodded to each respectively. "There's no need to worry, Miss Taylor's been in custody since last night, as you know, and with the blood of two people on her hands I honestly doubt you'll see her anywhere but behind the bars of a cell at any time before trial. This might be rough, but as part of the investigation into her actions it's required that I get a statement from every witness. That means you, Amy..." No matter, the pain was a hurtful reminder, but it didn't bother her to remember the events that seemed to have brought her together with the Mighty Hero. Even after this, she'd need to go a long way to feel like she'd repayed Brandon for even the tiniest things he'd done to help her so much... [This has been an Aimz/ Brand Frontier collaboration.]
