The first loss in an fWo ring.

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Wow" Sean's eyes filled with awe as he looked around the nearly empty arena. Perhaps the biggest venue in this business the Tokyo Dome fills even the most hardened wrestlers with a sense of wonderment. The ring crew worked hard to put the finishing touches on the fWo Slaughter stage. And with a nod to one of the security guards Brandon and Sean made their way down to the edge of the ring. The black matting was finally being placed over the wires and cables attaching the announcers table to the sound system and the like. Sean just looked on in amazement. He couldn't believe he was here. When his Dad had wrestled before in the G.W.O. he wasn't really allowed to come along that often. His father had made many enemies in the sport and Brandon was very protective of his kids. Here though it was a bit different. Besides the one altercation with the Boston Strangler the other night he had yet to really set himself counter to anyone. At this time Brandon believed it was all right to bring the family along. He knew that it would all change soon enough though. The other reason for allowing the family to come along was for support. Tonight in the Dome Brandon would wrestle his first match for the fan's Wrestling organization. Tonight he would fulfill a career long dream. He had heard rumors about whom he would be facing, everything ranging from Austin Davis to Black Quicksilver. Time flew by though and he had not had a chance to check the website nor talk to the crew. In reality though it didn't really matter. He would do as he always did, get in the ring and stand toe to toe with whoever was put in his way. This was his first step and he knew he had to make it good. A lose wouldn't be the end of course but it sure would be nice to start things out right. He also knew that with the things happening with the Boston Strangler on the show last week that he was something he had to keep in his mind as well. Tonight would be the perfect chance for the Strangler to strike back at Brand for interrupting his little beat down of Universal. As Brandon looked down to his son he questioned himself whether the Strangler thought he would just stand aside while something like that went down? For years he had done the same thing. He had come out to defend those that needed help. Now though he was the cause of those problems. Brandon absently shook his head as he thought about that. What would make someone change so drastically? The alliance that had been formed with that psycho Austin Davis was part of the reason he knew. Whether or not he was simply misguided though mattered not. The soldiers in the war never mattered, just the outcome. If Boston Strangler wanted to set himself up for the fall then that was his decision but he had to realize that at the same time Brand Frontier would simple be who he has always been. Lost in thought for a second Frontier shakes the cobwebs out of his head as he looks up to Sean. The little boy had climbed the steel steps now and slid in the ring. He stood just over two turnbuckles high and as he bounced off the ropes Brandon was brought back to his center. His focus. Suddenly Sean stopped after bouncing off the far rope and raised the kid's eyebrow as he threw his leg out sideways and wiggled his skinny little arms and dropped one of the most perfect elbows onto his invisible opponent. Brandon slid into the ring under the ropes and came to one knee as Sean spun to his feet via the much hyped Sean-a-roonie as he ran to his Dad and took him down with a stiff clothesline. As Sean bounced off his chest and Brandon flailed about, "Superstar" Sean Frontier ascended to the second turnbuckle and dove off with a body splash that ended in a quick three count. Sean was still the Frontier Wrestling Group's champion! He jumped up and held his arms high as the ghost audience in the arena applauded their approval. The quick match was over and Brandon regained his squatting position. Sean came up to him and gave him a quick hug. "This place is awesome Daddy. Do I get to sit in the crowd and watch tonight Daddy? Please!?" "We'll see buddy. Maybe we can get you and Mommy in one of the skyboxes. We waited a little too late to get the guest seats near ringside. You know I don't like you being too close to the matches as it is." "I know Daddy. I just wanna make sure I see you beat up the bad guys." "I don't know who I'm wrestling yet bud. It might not be a "bad guy". It might just be a dark match so I can get seen by the crowd." "Oh." "But, you make sure you are wearing your wrestling gear bud. You never know when I might need some backup." The big smile returned to Sean's face quickly. "Yeah! When do we go get Mommy? She wanted to see you tonight too right?" "Sure she does Sean. She's just a little worried about being around all of this again. She is just being a good mommy is all. We are going back to the hotel to get her in a bit. I figured we could go get some lunch after this. Is that cool with you?" "I don't want any Japanese food though Daddy. Can't we go to McDonalds instead?" "Deal. We'll go to McDonalds. But, you have to promise me that you will listen to Mommy tonight while I'm working. It's going to be hectic and we need to know you are going to behave." "Sure Daddy... hey... I just thought of somethin'..." "What?" "I don't have a fWo belt. I got a G.W.O one and a W.C.W one and an E.W.A one but not one from here yet. Can I get one tonight? Please!? "Sure, sure. You and Mommy can go get one after we get to the locker rooms..." "But, Daddy... by then they will be all sold out." "I'll send one of the crew to get one then OK? It's not that big of a deal. They have hundreds of them." "But... but... I want to see all the other stuff they got. What about a Black Quicksilver mask? What if they have one of those, huh?" (laughs) "Ok...ok... you win. We'll come in the main gate and stop by the stand ourselves." "Thanks Daddy" "Sure, now come here..." Brandon picks the little warrior up and sits him on the top turnbuckle. They both look around at the huge arena. Without it being filled to capacity, as it usually was when he was here, every movement echoed throughout. The crew could be heard up the ramp and in the back. The pyrotechnic handlers packed the explosives as they yelled back and forth to one another. A few of the security guards and ring crew had started moving the barricades to accommodate one of the hand-trucks. It was definitely going to be a circus tonight. One of the toughest nights in his career. Brandon Roche couldn't wait.
