A Stoner's Awakening

1 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Everyday in America, someone of low intelligence will wake up in an unfamiliar place. This usually is caused by heavy drinking and drug abuse. Today is no exception. On this day in question, that person is none other than Steve-O; a former 21w Tag Team Champion. And oh yes, he's heavily into the abusing of drugs! So much so that after a long night of partying at Bruce Willis's house, even though he was not invited and PROBABLY not even actually there, he would sneak into a nearby MdDonald's fast-food restaurant for a quick nap. It was opening hour and David, your traditional zit-faced teenager that thinks being promoted to Assistant-Assistant Co-Manager of Wiping the Counters is a huge, life changing accomplishment. He had been trained in the ways of the soft serve machine and knew the fast food handbook like the back of his hand. Which he knew surprisingly well due to him lacking the ability to get a date for Friday night... Yeah, anyway... he knew that damn handbook really well! He had read it through a good seven times, but nothing could have prepared him for what he saw as he walked behind the counter... Some man was asleep in the deep fry machine; naked... and covered in KY Jelly. The boy's eyes bugged out of his head. "Er... Somebody get the manager," the teenager groaned in his usual squeaky voice, which consisted of many... MANY voice-cracks. ...Later That Day... ...On the mean streets of Miami, Florida, a heavy sigh of annoyance could be heard. "Dude, how many times do I have to tell you, you shouldn't take off your threads and go skinny dipping in the deep fryer. It gives you mad cock-itch, man!" Nodding in agreement, Steve-O frowned and scratched at his groin. An hour before, his brother Nate had picked him up from the police station, using their last fifty dollars to bail him out. The two brothers walked along the sidewalk, gazing up to the blue sky above them. Nate shook his head, taking another look at the latest article in the daily newspaper. "...This is a disgrace," he said in an angry tone, holding up the picture of him (Steve-O) giving the cameras the ol' middle finger as they woke him up as he slept. "I mean COME ON, page three? Page frikken' three? Why didn't you try and hump one of those news ladies... That would have got you front page for sure, dude! Those bitches on CNN eat that stuff up! ...Hang your head... Walk of shame, hoe." Steve-O's head drooped over shamefully. They stopped at a bench and took a seat.
