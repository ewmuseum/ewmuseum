Interview

11 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Zero no longer concerned him. Sure, Lint Douglas posed a serious problem. And, deep down on the inside... Sean knew it. From this point on, Zero could be lurking. Watching his every move. Planning his payback. Or he could take the psychological route. Sitting back in a cold, wet, stinky, rat-infested alley... smoking a stale smelling cigarette, plotting. Sean "triple X" Stevens wasn't the smartest man in the world... but, he was far from the dumbest. Thing is... ...he didn't care. Chris Universal no longer irked him. Universal was a piss poor, annoying as hell, promoter out to berate, humble, and humiliate Sean. From the moment Triple X inked his name on his fWo contract, Universal made it a point - unsuccessful albeit - to bring him back down to earth. He threw PrimeTime at him... Triple X beat him. He tried to scare him with an fWo legend... the Boston Strangler... Sean exposed him for what he truly is in his eyes - an over-hyped, semi-talented, waste of a main event card slot, and company promotion. Hell, Chris was even the mansolely responsible for bringing Zero into the fWo... and, in Sean's face... ...but, still... Sean didn't care. In his hand was a picture from the year 2000. It was taken right after he had beaten Ray Hagan for the SCW World Heavyweight Championship - but, that wasn't the reason for his interest in the photo. The person beside him was... Poison Ivy. On that night, Ivy left Eli Flair - if only for one match - and, helped guide Sean to a victory, some believed he couldn't attain. That was also the first night they... kissed. And, it was also the night, Sean let his guards down... the night Sean "Triple X" Stevens began to care. Before Ivy, he was a cocky, self-centered, arrogant prick. He Lied. Cheated. Stole. Hit women - Ivy included. After her... he became confident. Confident in his abilities. She taught him that he didn't have to be so hard all the time... he didn't have to cheat to win. Lie to get what he wanted. Or manipulate so much. And, he listened... and, changed... and, grew up, becoming one of the greatest wrestlers of this era. Not having her was beginning to take it's toll. Especially when he see's so often. After realizing she was the reason he couldn't fully celebrate all of his current fWo success... Sean came to terms with something he never wanted to believe. He was in love with Ivy McGinnis. Now it was just a matter of telling her. Sean raised his cell phone to his ear... dialing 10 digits... to no avail. Ivy's phone was off... or she wasn't answering, atleast. Her voicemail came on... and, once it beeped... Sean began. "Ivy... this is Sean. Listen, I need to talk to you... give me a call sometime." He hung up and sighed heavily.
