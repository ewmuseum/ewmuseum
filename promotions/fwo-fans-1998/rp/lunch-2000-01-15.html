Lunch.

15 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Lunch. January 15, 2000 :::The scene opens with a shot of the exterior of the Philadelphia Hard Rock Cafe, identifiable by the 36-foot rotating replica of a Gibson electric guitar over the front entrance. After a moment, it cuts inside. Through the clutter of moving people and the din of the blaring music, a figure can be seen moving toward a table- Geoff Green. The camera follows him, and after a moment, he arrives at a table currently being occupied by none other than Perfect. A waiter is standing and taking Perfect's order as Geoff sits down.::: Geoff: You told me to meet you here, right? Perfect: Yeah, Geoff. You oughta try the steak sandwich- good stuff. :::to the waiter::: Yeah, the pork tenderloin, Caribbean style? :::The waiter jots down the order, and turns back to Perfect.::: Will that be all, sir? :::Green breaks in.::: Four fried chickens and a coke, please. :::Perfect stares at him for a moment, dumbfounded. Before the waiter can process this, Green changes his mind.::: Actually, I'll have the steak sandwich instead. But keep the coke. :::The waiter nods, and scurries off to notify the staff of the latest order. Perfect swivels in his chair to face Green.::: Let me guess...you're wondering why I called you here, right? :::Green nods.::: Well, I figured, who among the lousy fWo production staff- you know, the guys even WCW wouldn't take- is closest to being perfect, and thus worthy to interview me? :::Green is surprised for a moment, but recovers.::: Well, thanks. I never knew you had such a high opinion of me. :::Slugger turns to Green.::: Did you say something? As I was saying, the answer that to that question is obviously nobody, so I just picked names out of a hat. You came up first. :::Green isn't surprised, really.::: And, since you have the priviledge of interviewing me, I will grant you five questions. I'm sure there's plenty you want to know about exactly what myself and Dead are doing, and plus there are plenty of big matches coming up soon...so fire away. Oh, and real quick- how did you manage to get a camera in here anyway? :::Green whispers.::: It's hidden. :::Perfect replies excessively loudly.::: Hidden, huh?! Cool. :::Green is frantic for a moment, but when nobody comes to kick him out, he turns his attention back to Perfect.::: Perfect: You gonna ask those questions? :::Green thinks for a moment.::: Why, exactly, did you give Slaughter booking power to Chris Finn? :::Perfect ignores him.::: The tag team match this Sunday- myself and Ice against Spencer and Collins? My thoughts? Good question. You know, I figured it'd be a bad idea for us to give Finn booking power for a show, but this is ridiculous. He's got me teaming with the man whose meager career has been permanently intertwined with mine just because twice I lost my mind and actually allowed him to ride on coattails? You gotta be kidding me. Tony, I'm not going to lie to you. This Sunday, we will not function well as a tag team. Matter of fact...I'm gonna kick your ass all over Philadelphia, and the only thing that's keeping me from deciding to end your career is the fact that Collins and Spencer are in the match too, and hey, like Collins says, I am an equal-opportunity ass kicker. And hey, speaking of the two Deathseses, quite frankly, both of them can cripple each other for all I care. You've got Collins, the self-righteous ass who's got his precious 60 victories, his chick, and his toadies, and he's happy as a sleeping mouse. Collins, let me tell you this- that is exactly what you are. You may decieve yourself into believing that your time hasn't passed you by, and that you're still a threat, but number one, you are weak, and number two, you don't have the balls or the brains to do anything of impact anymore. Face it- you may have started off as a big badass way back when at CyberSlam 2, but after that, everybody started seeing through your little fierce act and seeing you for what you are- a nothing, a nobody...Spanky. And Spencer? Don't get me started on this thing. This guy had two- TWO- opportunities to do what myself and Dead only need one to do, and the guy couldn't get it done. Why? Simple. Just like Collins, he doesn't have the balls or the brains. He surrounds himself with jobbers and yes-men...and then he wonders why he's going nowhere and going nothing. It's time for you to face the music, Spencer- you're a dinosaur. A relic. Straight outta the Jurassic. And you will soon be extinct. In fact, you've got a little over a week left until you are taken out for good- so I suggest you enjoy your time. And just keep in mind this phrase, Death... Dead Man Walking. That's you. Next question. :::Green thinks.::: What are- :::Interruption.::: - my thoughts on the Invasion Pay-Per-View being held in France? I think it sucks. Really- the fWo is touring France. France, for God's sake! And people wonder why everybody wants to get rid of it. God, and we're spending half a month there. Needless to say, I'm stocking up on disinfectant. Next question? Green: Finn, booking power, Slaughter...? Perfect: How does it feel to have one-half of absolute control over everybody in this promotion? It feels like it's about damn time. This is, after all, the promotion that I BUILT, so it's only fitting that I run it. Plus, it's quite a bit of fun screwing around with people's careers- like I could do to yours, huh? Huh? :::Perfect "playfully" elbows Green in the ribs, doubling him over.::: Green :::wheezing::: : Uh...Finn...never mind. Just...never mind. Perfect: What, out of questions? Oh, that's too bad. Tell you what, I'll give you a bonus question. Why did myself and Dead give Finn booking power on Slaughter? :::Green rolls his eyes from his hunched over position.::: Well, Geoff, it's all part of the reason that the FTfWo will succeed where so many others have failed...we don't come charging in with brute force, throwing our weight around, no. We are a pair of master chess players, Geoff. In chess, sometimes a move that looks like a sure-fire loser, can win you a match. We are, at this very moment, Geoff, moving our pieces forward...into fWo territory on the game board. The fWo, on the other hand, is standing still. By the time they react, it will be too late...all their pieces will be gone, from the pawns, to the bishops, to the knights and the queen...until... Checkmate. It's that simple. Perfectly Simple. :::As Perfect finishes, the waiter arrives with a tray and their food. After he sets it down and moves on, Perfect turns to Geoff. "Thanks for the steak sandwich, Geoff...and you'll be leaving now." Green is confused. "I said, you'll be leaving...*now*." Green gets the drift, and slinks off, sandwich-less. Perfect settles in to enjoy a good meal as we fade to black.:::
