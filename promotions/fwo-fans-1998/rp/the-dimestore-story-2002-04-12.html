The Dimestore Story

12 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A terrible Muzak version of Nirvana's "Smells like Teen Spirit" was playing over the muffled ceiling speakers as Mufanga Kagos walked through the rows of stores in the Montreal shopping mall. This was the best thing about having such an outlandish gimmick, he thought, once the spiralling bodypaint and the leopard-print loincloth was replaced with jeans and a sweater, nobody recognized you. And that was fine with him. After last Sunday, it was definitely fine with him. Erik Kelly had called him "Kamala Junior" during his Slaughter rant to the cameras, and that was fine... a little bit of a reach, but a good insult for someone as old as Kelly, thought Kagos. "Kelly just has to realize that it's time for the young guns in this federation to step up..." mumbled Kagos to himself as he passed a row of fWo-branded Tshirts on a chrome rack, the Erik Kelly - Say Hello to Block shirt being the most noticable style. "Both he and Ice have been going stale since the beginning of time, and now that me, Knight, Brand and the others are here... they are getting scared. Afraid for their jobs." "Can I help you?", a spectacled store clerk called out, emerging from his hiding place behind the shirtrack. Mufanga knew this type well. In white-speake, Can I help you? from a store clerk meant Please do not steal my merchandise, black man. It was the same everywhere he went, and would be the same no matter what his place in the world was... how much money he had, how much fame he aquired... There was a world of worried store clerks out there, with sweating eyes swimming behind coke-bottle glasses, terrified that the black man was about to steal his shit. "Nah, just lookin'." Mufanga said, turning away from the fWo shirts and walking further into the storefront. The storeclerk followed closely, no doubt eying Kagos' oversized sweater, wondering what shoplifted items lay within. Kagos was sick to death of people like this storeclerk. People who judged a book by its cover, or a man by his skin. People like Ice. Ice looked at Mufanga Kagos and saw the white bodypaint on black skin, saw the loincloth, saw the barefeet... and assumed that the jungleman gimmick was all there was to him. He was about to prove Ice wrong at Cyberslam... he was about to prove the world wrong. Suddenly Mufanga spun on his heel, and the wormy storeclerk, following closely, bumped into Mufanga's chest. Kagos stared down at the nerdy clerk, his uniform-like golfshirt with the tacky embroidered store logo made him look like a skinny branded cow. "Back... the fuck... up." Mufanga whispered fiercely, as the clerk skittered, backpeddling away from the tall black wrestler with the wild curly dreadlocks. Taking a step closer to the clerk, Mufanga nodded over to an overweight, white-haired, flip-flop wearing tourist looking at a long rack of Montreal postcards. "Why aren't you following that guy around? Why aren't you pissing him off, stepping on his heels? Does he not fit the goddamned profile?" "Bu-bu-but..." the clerk started, as Mufanga loomed over him. "But, nothing." Mufanga raged. "It doesn't matter anyway. There's nothing in this store, including you, that's worth shit to anyone, anyway." Storming out of the store, he passed a bargain bin complete with a sign saying Reduced to Clear - 90% off. Mufanga lashed out with a strong straight angry kick and the bin flew across the aisles. The bin, of course, was filled to the brim with "Vanilla Ice" CD's and cassettes and as the bin sailed through the store, bad rap music scattered across the tiled mall floor. "Why am I not surprised?" Mufanga sighed to himself.
