New Year's Revelations: Four - Just Another Night

1 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
January 1, 2003 2:18 AM Queens, NY "OW!" "....Sorry." "It's quite allright." Poison Ivy opened her apartment door and hobbled inside, dropping her keys on the floor as she went. "Triple X" Sean Stevens, her boyfriend, followed closely, picking them up. He locked the door behind him and helped Ivy to the couch. Her apartment is a monument to her personality - filled with practical objects like a wraparound couch and a 2001 Encyclopedia set... things like that. And it's used far too seldom. Earlier in the night, a massive get- together refused to be overshadowed by the DEA's bust at the venue. "Feel okay, Sean?" "Yeah," replied Triple X, "Just a bit drunk... nothing outta the ordinary." He began his slow walk to Ivy's bedroom, dropping his T-Shirt in a heap on the floor. "You want some coffee?" asked Ivy, hobbling to the kitchen. "No thanks," replied Trip, "Don't get offended, I'll probably be asleep when you get in." She hobbled over to him unsteadily - it was probably not a good idea to mix drink with pain pills - and kissed him full on the lips. "Happy new year, handsome.... go get some sleep." Ninety seconds later, her coffee perking, she flipped on the television with the sound way down. Countdown to Oblivion One replay - the very end of the Eli Flair/Ruben Ross match. "Ouch!" exclaimed Ivy as she watched the two men. It was a mere stroke of luck that neither of them suffered a serious injury - that they were both able to walk out of the arena. A gentle beep - the coffee is done. By the time Ivy returned with her cup, the card had moved on to the very end of the Yukio/Aimee Horihito match. Yukio. She seemed nice enough to everyone - but she also seemed completely unconcerned with how she presented herself backstage. There was no traveling with the rest of the company - there was no going out to eat or drink with the boys (or girls) she works with. There's nothing wrong with that.... it just doesn't endear her to the rest of the talent. Fucking kneebrace. She put her leg up on her coffee table and removed the brace. Still swollen, she had to hand it to the surgeons - there was a barely noticeable scar on the side of her leg. As Ivy looked back at the screen... her eyes locked on the new figure there. Eron the Relentless. Ranting about.... something. "Someone's gotta set his egotistical ass straight," she commented as she reached for her laptop. And strained her knee. And her eyesight started to spin. "Fuck it," she said, "I can do that tomorrow." Happy New Year.
