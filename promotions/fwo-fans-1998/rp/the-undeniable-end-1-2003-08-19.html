The Undeniable End- 1

19 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Black. All Voran could see was black in the completley darkened hotel room. As he laid on his back, trying desperatley to fall asleep at 3:45 AM, he ended up laying there, staring into the darkness. He kept telling himself that he had to wake up early tommorow, that there was a house show and he couldn't let the fans down, but he was still unable to sleep. Every moment brought him back to Ruahh. Back to the words of Eron, the man he had once looked up to as the perfect example of everything a wrestler should be. He shocked and appalled the fans without resorting to cheap crash TV-style tactics. He didn't forget the fundementals of wrestling, and he seemingly never had a bad match. In short, he was everything Voran Xias desired to be. His thoughts suddenly brought him back to his IOW debut. He seemed to be far, far away from that goal, made to be a mid-card bully for a silly upper-mid-card heel.... Evan Maxim stood over the groggy Shocker, and once again picked up his title belt. "This is mine, little Shocker." Evan told him. "All mine!" "Don't think so." As Evan turned around to find the source of the voice, he was met with a chair shot to the face! A man looking to be around 6'4, muscular build, and dressed in a black shirt with black pants, picked Evan up and hit a Cradle Piledriver on the chair in the parking lot! "Piece of shit." the man said. "You've just met an Undeniable End!" Whoever this man was, he helped Shocker up and into the limo, then got into the driver's seat and drove off, leaving Evan down and out, bleeding from the forehead, as Nova returned to the ring area for the co-main event. Voran shook his head at the memory of his debut, wondering what he thought with such a corny phrase. His first match didn't really go any better for him. "Voran, I have no idea who the hell you are, but as if you didn't give me enough reason to want to kick your ass last week, tonight you come out here and Tiger Bomb my older brother?!?" Evan said in disbelief. "Well, tonight, the only one who will be suffering an 'Undeniable End', as you like to say, is YOU, and you're definitley the piece of shit, so get your ass out here so I can kick it!" The crowd loudly cheered as Evan dropped the microphone and began pacing in the ring, awaiting his opponent. The lights then went completley out as "Snap Your Fingers, Snap Your Neck" by Grinspoon started to play over the speakers. After a few moments, a single spotlight shined onto the top of the stage, revealing 'Undeniable' Voran Xias standing there with his head down, drawing much boos from the crowd. After a few moments, he raised his arms in the air with his head still down, drawing more boos from the crowd. Finally raising his head, Voran slowly made his way down the rampway in the darkened arena, the spotlight following him, before finally climbing up the ring steps. At the top of the ring steps, the lights turned back on, and Voran entered the ring. Immeaditley, Evan attacked him with vicious rights and lefts before he even made it all the weay into the ring, as the bell was rung for the match to begin. Evan continued his beat-down on Voran in the corner, then Irish Whipped him into the opposite corner. Evan ran in with a Dropkick to Voran's chest, slumping him over in the corner, then used his boot to choke the life out of Voran. Evan then turned and raised his arms to the crowd, who cheered him, but this was a mistake, as he underestimated Undeniable. Voran picked himself up and immeaditley ran over Evan with a powerful clothesline from behind, knocking Evan to the mat as the crowd booed. He immeaditley began stomping Evan with angry, powerful kicks, his intense eyes never even moving as he stomped, stomped, stomped the smaller Evan. He then reached down and locked Evan in a painful modified STF he called Undeniably Painful, as Evan winced in pain. Voran pulled back on the hold harder, but Evan was close enough to the ropes to reach them, as the referee began a five count, telling Voran to break the hold at five. But Voran refused. As the crowd loudly booed, Voran pulled back on the hold even more, as if Evan's neck was going to snap right from its socket. After more screaming, the referee called for the bell, then whispered something in the timekeeper's ear. "Ladies and gentlemen, due to his refusal to release the dangerous hold, 'Undeniable' Voran Xias has been disqualified, so your winner, Evan Maxim!" Voran shook his head at the absurdity of it all. He knew he could be a young punk like Maxim easily, but he refused to release the hold and let his first North American wrestling match ever go down as a DQ loss. He was a stubborn person, he admitted that much. ....Too stubborn to admit when I'm wrong? Voran suddenly thought to himself. Was Eron, in fact, right about him? Was he just leeching off of the fans' sympathy heat, and was his lack of focus the reason why he's on a losing streak? Maybe I'm putting too much emphasis on my promos. Voran thought. I mean, back in my IOW run, my promos were nothing much to write home about, like my first one.... Nova immeaditley began with the unsettling tune of "Snap Your Fingers, Snap Your Neck" by Grinspoon. With a somewhat-loud boo eminating from the crowd, out stepped 'Undeniable' Voran Xias- as usual, dressed in all black, as the lights in the IOW Arena dimmed. The crowd was less than happy to see Voran make his usual slow, deliberate entrance so early in the show. The man who, seemingly, had entered only as Shocker's stooge, entered the ring, his well-built six-foot-four frame calling for a microphone as his music slowly died down. "I believe most of you have a slight misinterpretation of who the hell I am." Voran said- his voice was slow and deliberate and also very low, just like his manner of walking- which only served to annoy the crowd in an entirely different way than Shocker's high-pitched squealing did. "Some of you have referred to me as Shocker's flunkey, or Shocker's stooge. But I am out here to tell you that your perceptions are wrong- I'd imagine that people as pathetically un-intelligent as the IOW's fanbase would get a lot of things wrong." The fans louldy booed in response, but Voran didn't dignify this with a response. "No, I am not Shocker's stooge or his flunkey." Voran said. "I am my own man. Yes, I entered with Shocker and I am an associate of his, but allow me to make one thing clear- the only thing I have my eyes set on is the IOW World Heavyweight Championship." The fans were surprised and someone pissed-off at Voran's sudden claim. Why was he worthy of a title shot? "I don't care who the Champion is after Sinister Purposes- whether it is the Italian moron or the Angel of Annoyance, I shall beat down with a vengeance whoever leaves this event with the Championship belt I desire." Nothing special at all. Voran thought. But then, after coming to the fWo, I started putting more and more effort into them. I tried to make the fans laugh while I offended them, I tried to make myself look smart. At first, I was able to balance promos and matches quite well, but have I lost the touch? Maybe. His thoughts quickly shifted back to the IOW, however, where Voran had emerged as a World Heavyweight Title contender. His thoughts brought him back to the night he won the title, the night his career would begin to skyrocket....in a win he was not the least bit proud of. The match had officially started. Now there would be a new champion or "Ego" Greg Davis would regain his top prize. The two circle, as the roar from the crowd became louder and louder. Nothing was happening, but the fans knew it would happen shortly. And it did. The two men slammed into each other, each working from a neck tie. It quickly became into a deadlock as Greg Davis pushed Voran off. Being more veteran of the two, Greg was able to realize the lack of patience from Xias, who immediately charged Davis and found his nose stuck into the mat with a drop toe hold. Davis threw a stomp onto the right knee of Voran. He then quickly locked on a cheap chokehold that he broke soon after. Voran struggled to regain his base, as Davis was constantly sending him back down with kicks and submission holds. He finally got his footing back, but was met with a stiff clothesline that sent him from the ring. Voran sent a kick into the midsection of Voran and then nailed a quickly knee bump. Upon Davis hitting the mat, he was instantly picked back up and dropped with a chinbuster. Voran now took a chance to regain his senses, and grabbed onto the hair of the IOW Champion, pulling him up to his feet. CRACK CRACK Two right hands sent Davis reeling into the turnbuckles. Just as this happened, someone took a stroll down the rampway. Someone.....that someone was 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs. The crowd cheered at his return to the ringside area, and he picked up a folding chair leaned against the barracade, unfolded it, and took a seat at ringside, apparently to get a closer view of this matchup. Meanwhile, Voran charged at Davis, but was met with a set of shoes to the head. Voran stumbled back but was quickly erased when Davis hit a flying forearm. Davis now looked out to the crowd, their roar getting ever more so loud. He was signaling from the EGO DDT. Voran was up ... Hooked his head ... BOOM! EGO DDT! The fans went wild! Greg Davis seemingly had the match won, and thus would be holding onto the IOW World Heavyweight Title. As Davis leaned down to cover, however, a murmer was going up through the auidance. Someone was making his way through the left side of the crowd. Davis apparently didn't notice as he covered Voran Xias. The crowd that wasn't distracted by the unidentified intruder cheered and counted the fall with him, hoping this would be over. ONE! TWO! THR- The crowd erupted in boos. Why? The referee was pulled out of the ring. By Jason Winters, the intruder who had made his way through the crowd. Voran angrily picked him up and hooked him for the Cradle Piledriver- but Davis Back Bodydropped out of it, again drawing a huge reaction from the shocked crowd! Davis stood up on the other side of the fallen Voran as Voran got back up- WHACK! A chair to the back, courtsey of Jason Winters from the apron. As Davis held his back, Voran turned and kicked him in the stomach again, and set him up for the..... .....Cradle. Piledriver. He got it, and covered as the crowd booed and began to toss their garbage into the ring. The referee made the count as an irate crowd looked on. ONE! TWO! THREE! The crowd erupted with boos. A different theme played than when Voran had come out- this time, it was "Fight Song (Slipknot Remix)" by Marilyn Manson. Voran stood up, and promptly spat on the body of Greg Davis. Despite the constant interference in that match, the theme song change meant something. That was the first time the Undeniable One that fWo fans had grown to hate would be born, and Voran would leave for the fans Wrestling orginization soon after. Still without sleep, Voran turned back over in his bed, attempting to think about something else besides his wrestling career. He'd never fall asleep that night.
