Not Without My Daughter

5 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A foot whizzed by his face. David Cross didn't even blink as the breeze tickled his eyebrows. "So I hear you go to Japan soon, yes?" Master Hiroshi's leg swept low. Cross back flipped out of the way, extending his leg to catch a piece of his sensei's jaw. The elderly man easily evaded the blow by rolling to his side. Both men regained their footing in their lethal dance and launched simultaneous attacks, forearms deflecting fists, knees blocking shins. Their limbs were blurs, intertwining with each other like a choreographed fight scene in a big-budget movie. A fist caught the tip of David's nose. He was beginning to tire from the exchange, while the man twice his age seemed to be getting his second wind. Cross blocked an inside ridge-hand and countered with a deep arm drag. Master Hiroshi landed unceremoniously on the wood-tiled floor with a loud thump. All he could do was watch as David took a quick step and landed on top of him with a running shooting star press. Cross was quite pleased... until Master Hiroshi grabbed him by the scrotum and twisted. When David woke up, he was lying in a small cot with a bag of ice resting on his crotch. He started to sit up and noticed that he wasn't wearing his training Gi anymore. "You threw up all over yourself before you passed out," Master Hiroshi muttered as he walked by. "You such a baby." David gingerly felt under the bag of ice to see if he still had two testicles. One... and two. Thank goodness. "Y'know, I might need those things someday." "I do the World a favor by making sure you don't make babies," scoffed Master Hiroshi. "You no take training seriously." "Sure I do." David was stung by the comment. Out of everything that had gone wrong in his life, his training in the Phan Hiroshi Ultimate Karate dojo had always been a great source of pride for him. Master Hiroshi always gave him a hard time, but the elderly Asian man was the closest thing David had ever had to being a father. Today, his "dad" tried to tear his balls off because he felt David wasn't taking his training seriously. Lesson learned. "I see you fight in your clown suit. Why you always lose?" David slowly swung his legs out of bed and pressed the bag of ice against his groin. "That's my wrestling outfit, not my clown suit. And I don't always lose, thank you very much." He stood up, slowly, and walked over to the small counter where Master Hiroshi was preparing a pot of steamed rice. Hiroshi waved his hand at Cross. "Bah, you could beat everyone there. Why you no fight like we fight?" David would have called Master Hiroshi a mark, but since he wasn't up for another round of Unscrew the Scrotum, he kept his mouth shut. "It's just the way we have to fight. Besides, my style took you down didn't it?" Master Hiroshi placed the lid on the pot and nodded towards the nearby chair. "But my style made you throw up and pass out." Cross bowed. "I am no match for your Ball Breaker technique." He smiled as Master Hiroshi gave him a stinging slap to the back of the head. "So are you going to Japan soon? You and your other clown friends?" "We're not clowns... forget it." David stopped to think for a minute. "Yes, we're heading to Japan and the surrounding area in a couple of weeks." He saw Master Hiroshi open his mouth and quickly cut him off. "Yes, I will go to the Temple when I'm in Tokyo. I will give Master Zoogie your warmest regards." Another stinging slap. "Master Zugei. Zugei! Not Zoogie! Don't disgrace me when you over there!" "I'll be on my best behavior." Master Hiroshi eyed David suspiciously. He quickly crossed his heart and held up two fingers. "Scout's honor." "You are no scout, and you have no honor." Master Hiroshi sat down at the kitchen table. David took the seat across from him, spun it around, and sat down resting his chin on the back rest. Hiroshi lunged forward and pointed. "This is what I mean! You can't even sit properly at my table! What are you, half donkey?" Cross gave the old man a wink. "Only the good half. Now you know I won't do anything to disrespect you over there. You know that. Why don't you tell me what's really bothering you?" Master Hiroshi took the small kettle off its tin rack and poured himself a cup of tea. He looked into the bottom of the cup, lost in the dark amber liquid. "Michelle is back in Japan." Suddenly, David's groin started aching for a whole different reason.
