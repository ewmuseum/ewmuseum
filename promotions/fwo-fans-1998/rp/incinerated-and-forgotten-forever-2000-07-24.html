Incinerated and Forgotten Forever

24 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 22nd, 2001 Time: 1:45AM Eastern Standard Time Location: A trash burning facility on the outskirts of Uniondale, NY Overhead lights slowly swung back and forth from their tightly woven ropes over the narrow catwalk. The entire building was vacant, save for the rats that scurried about the trash, a smorgasbord and a paradise for the vermin. Birds slept in the niches high up in the trash facility, their excretions giving the catwalk a nice look of what could almost be patchwork between the black steel and white waste. The echoed taps of footsteps upon the catwalk were heard. The outline of a trench coat came into view. It was followed by the face of Donaven Winters. He had his body half bent over the catwalk, looking down into the giant incinerators. He was almost in a trance staring at the waste that people had created and will continue to create until humans have vanished from the face of the earth. "Thrown away without a care in the world," he spoke softly. "Billions of them do this every day. They throw everything away. Not caring about the environment, their own surroundings. They don't even know where it goes. But I know where it goes. It comes here, shipped in by the city's garbage trucks and then it's burned. Regardless of what's inside of there, it will burn to a crisp. It will burn until there is only a thin memory, a static that reaches out when the thought of what you had for dinner last week strikes into your mind." Donaven shifted his weight over to his right leg, stretching his left knee out as he did. "So few are the memories," he stated. "That they all come back slowly, but scrambled together. Much like the black and white static from a station that won't come in on your television station. Scrambled in the sense of people remembering what you did wrong in life over what you've done right. Your misfortunes will always win out over the triumphs you've accomplished. It's the way Society has been molded and bred. Generations upon generations losing their heritage because of what they've done wrong. Where do these memories of what they did right go? Right here..." His long arm extended over the railing of the catwalk, pointing directly down at the two massive incinerators. His eyes were becoming fiery as he lifted his leg back up and stomped it down. "Why?" he questioned himself. "Why do these things happen? Because people want to look down upon every single person. They will single out anybody and everybody until they feel higher than them. They need to feed their constant egos, while they let their true mind deteriorate, burning and singing much like the trash in this building. Much like the history of the fWo. Many competitors lasted a very short while, making a small dimple on the radar screen. They're also forever forgotten, thrown into the incinerator because someone with a better personality to get themselves over with Finn or Universal came along." Winters was visibly becoming angry as his eyes were piercing holes straight through the incinerators. He continued, his voice was becoming more raspy and full of angst. "It's always been about how well you can weasel your way in or be thrown into the trash," he seethed. "Careers that never got a chance to bloom, were thrown in here years ago without a second thought. My parents would have loved to have thrown me in here. But I survived onward. I won't be forgotten. I just won't let it happen. In due time I will make my impression, whether it's looked down upon or praised, I won't let it be thrown into the incinerators to be forever forgotten. I'm going to make my mark, Saturday night at SummerSlaughter. Greg Davis will be the next egotistic fool that will be thrown into the incinerator. In a week, the fickle fans will forget who Davis even was, because of me. He's just going to be another name on the list of the millions, no, billions of people who have walked this earth and will be forgotten forever. My mark will be made in the fans Wrestling organization come Saturday. But, my mark that will leave the world in shock will have to wait for another day. I've fallen hard from the heavens. I'm still trying to pick up my broken wings. But on Saturday, I won't be the only person with Broken Dreams. Davis, the incinerator waits, and NOTHING can save you." Donaven Winters roared and it echoed madly throughout the facility. A few pigeons flew out of their nests. He stomped off towards a small door at the catwalk. He had angered himself once again and he was ready for the second biggest card of the year. If Davis wasn't ready, he had better not show up to the arena for his own sake.
