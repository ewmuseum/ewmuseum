... and so he was alone again. Since he arrived back in Arizona to spend the week at home from this past Sunday to this next, he had spent time thinking, and most of that time was spent in a tub full of water. drip drip drip The water was cold. drip drip drip He couldn't stop thinking about the incident on Slaughter. When Paul put himself before them and vowed to walk away if Frontier had defeated him again. drip drip drip But in doing so he not only walked away from wrestling and the fWo, he turned his back on Austin Davis. Paul may have had the best of intentions... but he was only thinking of himself. He certainly wasn't thinking about his friendship with Davis, and the bond that had been forged between two men who had spent a great deal of time hating and despising each other. Paul didn't realize the magnitude of their friendship and what it meant to Davis. Very rarely would Davis befriend someone without the intention of stabbing a knife in their back at some point; or without trying to get something from them. Davis saw a side of Paul when they feuded with each other that he liked, and if he could get Paul to see things his way he knew it would be a relationship that would benefit the both of them. DRIP. It was Davis that was used to walking away from people. All one would have to do is look at his track record from the very moment he entered the fans Wrestling organization. Jim Dudley. Chris Kidman, Jr. now known as Wretch. Cactus Jon. Living. Heaven. Hell. Mitch Wilson. Hiroshima. Rain. Countless others. Scott Slugger. But it wasn't he who stabbed his knife into the back of Scott, it was Scott who did that to him. And that's why he had spent so much time pondering the situation. Paul had no reason to walk away from Davis... yet Scott had every reason to. Davis just didn't think he had the balls to do it; but Scott proved him wrong. Davis was using Scott... to get what he wanted. When Scott stuck the knife into his backside it's when everything Davis had wanted was suddenly gone... and now... Now that Paul is gone? Well... ...to be continued. Sunday.
