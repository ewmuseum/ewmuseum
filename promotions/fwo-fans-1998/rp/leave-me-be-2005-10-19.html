Leave Me Be

19 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Disney's Wednesday Warfare October 05, 2005 As he shut his dressing room door, he got what he had been looking for, for over 20 minutes, and that was the noise of silence. For the past while he had been in the assistance of the fWo medics, after Craig Miles' stunt in the arena car park. He looked at the needle mark on his arm, scowering in anger, an emotion that took all of what was left of his as he locked the door firmly behind him. He leaned back against the now closed door and slowly slid down it's surface, a trail of blood following him from the back of his cranium, his journey halted by the cold and harsh ground. Blood still leaked from the wound on the back of his head. It trickled down his neck and down his collarbone, before dropping onto the floor, an unaudible splash given the surface of the ground below him. This, this wasn't meant to happen. He was the mighty Khristain Keller. A man whom people feared, a man who could strike most down with a single strike to the head and make sure you were down with a kick to the head. This wasn't right. Keller breathed deeply as he grabbed the bench on his right hand side, he struggled to raise himself to his feet. Slowly, but surely, he found himself erect, standing still in the centre of his dressing room. With a short pace forward with his right foot... He fell... His knee giving way to his weight, as he lay crumbled on cold and hard floor. He howled in pain, low enough for no one to hear outside of these four walls. He slammed the concrete with his bare fist, immediately damaging the skin and causing a stinging sensation to whistle through his nervous system Anything to take away the pain of his knee. Anything. Anything to take away the humiliation that he was about to face. Anything. His bag was within reach, and as he stretched, he managed to tip the contents over upon himself. Towels, a change of clothes and a toiletry bag fell upon him, the contents of which hadn't been touched for several months, as he had no reason to delve into it as he was never competing, until now. Along with the contents however, he seen something that shook him to the core. A bottle of aspirin. The former addict looked on as they rolled along the floor with the familiar rattling sound to which he had grown to love and loathe for years. He looked hard. He stared at his memories of the places he had been, the places where he did not want to return, places, which he wouldn't wish upon his worst enemy. Enemy. Miles. His inner self raged with greed, as he looked on to what would be the solution to all his problems. His right hand slowly moved towards them, his jerking reactions causing the contents to spill over the concrete. He was gone. He picked up four, then five, then six, and shoved them into his mouth, slowly chomping away on the white gritty substance. He closed his eyes and thought of happier days...his mind slowly becoming empty of such things...and as he opened his eyes and seen the spillage upon him, his broken thoughts could not repair the damage. The stains of time had come to ruin him once again. "What am I doing..." A tear of regret ran down his cheek. This world is a drought when out of love. Please come back to us. For all of the above. I'm making a choice to be out of touch. Leave me be he said. Leave me here in my stark raving sick sad little world...
