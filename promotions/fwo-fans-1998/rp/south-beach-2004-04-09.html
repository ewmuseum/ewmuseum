South Beach

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome to South System. It's the hottest night club in South Beach. If people are in the mood to party and have a good time, you know they're bringing their asses to South System. This place used to be a dead club, hardly generating any revenue. But once Cody Williams slapped down his cash for the place, shit started to change for the better. If you can get past the line, the music will blow you away once you enter. Hip hop, R&B, house, dance, top 40 - pure mainstream. You look up at the ceiling and there are girls dancing in cages. Look close enough, and you'll see their panties. Sometimes they don't even wear them under their skirts. Believe me, if you think you've seen skin, you haven't seen shit until you've visited my club. Sexy little hoes work the dance floor as if I'm paying them. Yeah right. The place is packed every night. You're bound to bump into people. Get ready to get personal, close, and intimate with someone. Winding, grinding, back bends - sluts do it all here. I watch all the action from my office upstairs. The floor in my office is a two-way mirror, so I can see the people but the people can't see me. While they dance, drink, and grope, I'm counting the revenue I rake in every night. And it's a shitload. I've got millions in the bank. I drive an Escalade, a BMW, a Tiburon coupe, and a Benz. I just bought a huge ass house and threw a welcoming party last night. How can I afford all of this just from a little night club and a semi-active pro-wrestling career? Simple. Ecstasy. You don't see it, but it gets dealt a lot at South System. It's the number one clubbin drug. All profits go to me. It gets dealt at the bar and on the floor. People love it. Pop one and you'll be dancing all night. The only problem is when someone goes OD. In that case, I get my bouncers to toss them out of the club into a nearby alleyway. No one dies in my club. Growing up as a good boy in Winnipeg, I never pictured myself as a drug kingpin. But then again, I never pictured myself as a millionaire. I'm 30 years old now. I haven't made it as World Champion yet, but I'm the most respected name in South Beach's night life. I walk around the dance floor and have girls begging to suck my cock. They grind their asses up against me. They get low and rub their faces in my crotch. I love it all. I can go from pimp to playa in a heartbeat. David Cross had a problem with me making his ex-girlfriend just another fucking number. What he needs to realize is that messing with me is a big mistake for a couple of reasons. Number one:I can kick his ass. Number two: I have protection. My bodyguard team consists of some strong, stone-headed, rough-necked big boys. But my main gun is, believe it or not, a woman. Nerva can take down anyone who tries to pick a fight with me. She does it quickly and efficiently, hardly making a sound. She's Brooklyn's Dangerous Daughter, working for South Beach's Wild Child. She took out Cross on Monday with no problems. And if he tries to interfere in my business again, I'll be sure to put a direct hit on him. I'm a rich motherfucker and I'm the last guy Cross wants to rumble with.
