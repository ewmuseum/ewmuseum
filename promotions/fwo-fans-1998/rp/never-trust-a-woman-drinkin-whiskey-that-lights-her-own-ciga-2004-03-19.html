Never trust a woman drinkin whiskey that lights her own cigarette

19 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Captain Jack will get you high tonight. And take you to your special island. "Y'know somethin," Lindsay started, the plastic cup containing Jack and Coke being waved around in the air. She was shitfaced, but she still maintained perfect speech. Surprisingly enough. "I've never had as much fun at a concert as I did tonight. Seriously. I'm not kidding." It was early in the morning, (or late, depending on your perspective), and the Valerian's Garden afterparty hadn't stopped just yet. The group had moved to the bus where there was more liquor and more room for intimate conversations. Per Angel's request, Lindsay Troy had checked out of the hotel where she was planning on staying and was given her very own luxury twin-sized bed on the tour bus. Who needs a spacious executive suite for one when you could have a tour bus for...well however many you can fit. "So Lindz," TJ called from the couch. "Truth or Dare?" "Truth. Wait 'til I get a few more in me and we'll see how daring I get." "Truth, huh?" asked the bass player as he rubbed glitter from his eyes, "How drunk would you have to be to wash Mickey's hair?" "Fuck you, girlfriend," came the reply from outside the bus, amidst a torrent of laughter. "Seriously," continued TJ, "What's the most questionable thing you've ever lowered yourself to doing in that biz of yours?" "Let's see," she paused to think, "OK there was this one time that Andrew Medina booked me in an evening gown match against Shawn Hart. I think Shawn was more embarassed than me, since he was wearing a hot-pink garter belt underneath his dress. Hell, at least I look good in one." Two men - one straight and one gay - and two women both took very un-subtle stares in Troy's general direction. Angel laughed. "Where's Nine at?" "Still inside," replied Ivy, "I think the bouncer had some kinda sound system in his car that Nine's fallen for. Go check it out, Teej?" The glam rocker looked at Ivy with a raised eyebrow. "You too," she pointed towards the fairly tipsy drummer, "we need's us some girltalk." As the boys left the bus, Ivy leaned over and pulled another Coke out of the small refrigerator. "So tell me what's goin' on back in the states... I'm itching for some news." "I hear the Boss is ripping shit up in the fWo." Troy paused to take another swig. Craig Miles'd always be the Boss in her eyes, even though the two were just fleeting acquaintences. She's seen too much politiking go down in her time in the biz not to know who to stand behind, regardless of how close two people might or might not be. Fuck Avrey Prosser. Miles was still the man. "Dan's got a tough road ahead of him, and his dealings with Marcus aren't getting much better. It's gotten to the point where I've got to step in. CSWA's gettin rollin again. Merritt called me, dunno how he got the number, while I was leavin Virginia Beach for an A1E show. Wanted to talk to me about comin onboard to manage Ryan and Melton. I wasn't about to turn the offer down, but I don't think Marcus is too thrilled about the prospect after Dan and Eli and Joey showed up at A1E's Sudden Death." "Merritt... ummmmm," Ivy's voice trailed off, "Never mind. But Boss's been tearin' shit up everywhere for years now." Ivy exhaled sharply and took a swig of her own drink. "And that's what vacation's for... he's buildin' to something that I don't wanna have to deal with." Angel smiled at the two wrestle-women... always business with 'em. "You know my rules, Ivy... after a gig, no stressing allowed unless someone broke something. And when you get goin' about the wrestling business that's where the stress starts." "Stress never stops. Always one thing after another," Troy interjected with a smirk. "Although there's just somethin magical about seeing a grown man with a sparked Newport kicking the piss out of a man in a mouse outfit. Almost brought a tear to my eye." Lindsay polishes off the rest of her drink and throws her hair up into a sloppy pony-tail/bun. Trendils fall around her face and no longer cover the tattoos that are now exposed on her back. "Oh, stress stops," replied Angel, as she reached into a cardboard box at the foot of the chair, "It stops when you actually finish something." She tossed the object towards Troy - it was a copy of 'Stitches' - the Garden's new album. "Although once it's done, a whole new round starts. But c'mon... it's what, two in the morning? I officially declare all work talk stopped. And since the only people I spend any amount of time with are Elijah and Ivy... maybe you can tell me what wrestlerfolk do to relax." "Ah, but if I told you everything Marcus might have to kill me." The three laughed and Troy scratched the back of her head. "Honestly, when I'm not doing promo spots or autograph sessions or workin out in the gym or performing...I like to sing, dance, play guitar, and do tai-chi. I find that a healthy balance of those four things help to keep me somewhat sane. I try to read and write as much as I can, but that generally only happens on the plane, and when I'm not sleeping. What about you two?" "Weeeeellllll----" "Shaddup Ivy. She doesn't relax. Ever." Ivy threw a pillow at her. "Seriously. She doesn't relax. But me... I write, I read... that's about it. Being a mom is a full time job." She looked toward the bunks. "It's worth it." "I give you all the credit in the world for being a musician and a mom, especially with a little one like Mariella. I want kids someday. I just..." Troy's voice trails off. She clears her throat, catching herself. "Not until I'm done competing anyway." "Aye-feckin-men to that," replied Ivy, "Besides, Angelfish, you lucked out with E... some of us--" Ivy's voice trailed off, as she got up silently and left the bus. Angel watched her go with concern flashed through her eyes, but she stayed put. "Mickey's out there, she'll have a cigarette, she'll calm down, she'll be okay." "It's alright, Angel. Some of us haven't been so lucky." Troy offered a weak smile, and sighed. "I hate this time of year." "Me too. I won't see him until November." She returned Troy's glare with a rueful smile. "Don't even think about it, Lindsay... I've done my time. And I deserve my perfect boy... Ivy has her perfect boy, she's just too stubborn to call him. And I don't know what's up with you but you deserve your perfect boy... what's stoppin' ya?" "Don't get me wrong, Angel. I love Marcus. I do. But...well..." She sighs. Maybe it's the alcohol. Maybe it's the torrent of emotions running through her. Or maybe she just needs to say what she's feeling as justification that everything that's ever happened to her has happened for a reason. Maybe she needs to say it to someone who's not Alaina and who can still be objective. "A little over two years ago I was engaged to my best friend. Jaime and I were inseperable from the time we were eleven. He loved me and wanted to spend the rest of his life with me. I loved him, but only as a friend. Nothing more. Jaime got in trouble with some people back in Italy, and I knew that the only way to save him was for us to get married. I didn't want to see him die at their hands, so I accepted his proposal. I was ready to give up the rest of my life to see him live. As it turns out...he died in an in-ring accident. The UEW bookers planned a stunt and didn't tell anyone about it except the guy that Jaime was facing that night. Jaime didn't even know. None of the boys knew. The bookers showed complete and utter disregard for anyone outside their agenda. They took him from me, and all I could do was just stand by and watch as the horror unfolded. By the time I got down to ringside, it was too late. They did what I was trying to prevent, and in the matter of an instant, my best friend and my other half was taken from me. I sued the owners, the bookers, the head people in charge. And I won. The fed was ruined. They tried to reestablish themselves under new owners, but they were finished. They killed Jaime, and I killed them. The people responsible for Jaime's death are blackballed from every respectable federation from the Japan to North America to Europe. I made them pay. But my heart was broken. Then...Matthias came along, and changed everything. I had closed myself off from everyone, I only let my sister and my aunt and uncle in. They were my family after my parents died, and Jaime was as much of a member of my blood as my sister is. Matthias, in his own way, opened my eyes and let me love again. I can't talk much about him, because his past is as much of a mystery to me as it is to the rest of the world, and what I do know I'm sworn to secrecy. All I know is that we're soulmates. Then he had to leave. He had other business to take care of. He said he'd come back for me when the time was right, but it seemed like I was by myself all over again. Marcus' girlfriend Jessica really screwed him over when she joined up with his mortal enemy and former best friend, and he and I got together. Two lost souls found each other. But I'll be honest with you, Angel. There's a strain between us. Given Marcus' hatred for Dan Ryan and annoyance towards Joey Melton, the fact that Max is my cousin, and everything else that's going on...I've just got a bad feeling. In the grand scheme of things, Marcus isn't what I need. Matthias is, and I hate for this to seem like Marcus is just 'Mr. Right Now', but I know where my heart is, and it's not with him." Angel looked Lindsay Troy over. It was as if she was struck speechless by her bold-faced honesty. "Miz Troy," she finally said, "I'm a bit tipsy right now so I won't start... would tell you more than I should. But I think, before you head back to the States... you and I have to have a long, long sit-down talk about things. Suffice to say, I had a pretty fucked up teenspan that led me to an abusive drunk and finally to Eli... you'll meet your match before too long. Hell, my soulmate is a gay glam rocker who just understood.... trust me, good things are ahead." "I'm not usually this blatantly honest, but Mr. Daniels makes me lose my inhibitions." Troy looks over at Angel, and smiles sincerely. "Thanks for letting me get that off my chest." "Mr. Daniels is truth serum," pointed out Angel, "I always wondered in those really bad spy movies, why they torture the good guy. Why not just get'em drunk?" She hugged the Queen of Extreme. "C'mon... sleep, before the boys wanna come back with dates." "Or before they want to play more truth or dare. I'm afraid of some of the stuff they'll come up with." Captain Jack will get you by tonight. Just a little push and you'll be smilin'.
