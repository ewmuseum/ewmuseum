Sigh...Evidence, Part D'oh

18 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Do I really have to do this?" "Yes! Now get to it!" yelled the off-camera voice. Jon Crisp looked down at the computer screen, frowning. His right hand was grasping his hair in slight frustration, while his left hand tapped the down arrow of his keyboard every three seconds. He had been here for almost two hours... trying to find the information that would most certainly put away Wretch, and prove once and for all that a hamburger is a sandwich. "You know, this is just ripping Wretch off," Crisp moaned, beginning to get up from the chair. "Just do this, and then you can go home." The off-camera voice was losing patience. "Why am I doing this again?" "Because you are property of the fans Wrestling organization and you'll do whatever we say! Got that, cattle! Why, for every Slugger, we get 20 of you, you piece of..." "NEWMAN!" said another off-camera voice, shutting up the first one. Crisp sighed, and went back to his script. So far, it had been pretty difficult. Up to this point, the argument "It is, who cares?" had worked well for him, but if he was to pursue this more seriously... "But..." Crisp began. "SILENCE!" ... he would need a large case. A much larger case. Crisp continued to tap away, until his eyes began to grow wider... and a slight smile (one that said "This is finally over") began to creep along his face. He had found it. The information that would put Wretch away once and for all. After hours of searching, here it was... in all it's size 12, Arial font glory! "This is in Times New Roman," Crisp said. "Thanks, Mr. NitPick," came the off-camera voice once again. "Just shut up and finish this." Once he thrust this information into Wretch's big stupid face it would most definitely be over, oldskool! "Oh, um... *so* checkmate." Crisp frowned for a moment. "Wait, isn't that chess? Can I say that?" Crisp thought a moment, and then shrugged. "Yeah, sure I can. Checkmate all over your face!" "All over your face? C'mon now, there isn't such a thing as chess porn. People don't checkmate over people's faces! That's just ridiculous!" Crisp was getting upset now, and it was obvious that this could not continue. "Look, if this angle's going to stretch out for a long, long time, you're going to have to play by the rules. Otherwise, you won't play at all." "Oh?" This piqued Crisp's interest, so much so that he got up and began to leave. "Where are you going?" the off-camera voice demanded. "To do some REAL research. This thing's just a prop." "Great, expose THAT. Now we're going to have to do this all over again!" Crisp walked out the door, and that was that.
