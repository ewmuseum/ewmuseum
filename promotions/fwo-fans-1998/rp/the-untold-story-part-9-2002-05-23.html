The Untold Story(Part 9)

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Poison Ivy - "You fucking bastard! You lying bastard!" Sean Stevens stood there in disbelief. His ex- girlfriend Linda Johns shocked him by coming over to his apartment, half-naked... and, trying to kiss him. His current girlfriend, Ivy, who was SUPPOSED to be in California with her brother- in- law, Eli Flair was standing at his door, about to launch an umbrella in his direction, obviously catching the tale end of something she THOUGHT she saw... but, really didn't see. Stevens - "Ivy... wait..." CRASH! Ivy launched the heavy umbrella in Sean's direction nailing him directly in the face. Unphased... Sean chased after her as she left the apartment, full speed, steaming. It'd have probably been in Ivy's best interests to keep the umbrella, though... as the rain had picked up, heavily. Showing signs of a possible tornado. But, Ivy didn't care... she exited the building right into the eye of it. Trying desperately to get her cell phone to pick up a signal. Poison Ivy - "Hello... I need a cab. NOW. The address is twenty- two, twenty- seven Highlander Court... You make your left off of Murkirk Road." Sean ran up behind her. Stevens - "Ivy don't do this... This is a big misunderstanding... you don't--" Ivy slapped him. Poison Ivy - "Do you think I'm fucking stupid, Sean?!? I saw you! I saw everything! You've got a half-naked woman upstairs in your apartment, and you expect me to just take it? "Hornet may have done some fucked up things to me... but, he NEVER did it in my face, and so blatant." Stevens - "Ivy... you've gotta listen to me! I didn't do anything! She came to my apartment... not the other way around!" Poison Ivy - "And, you let her in!" Stevens - "NO! I'm NOT gonna let you leave! Think about it... Don't you think I would've closed the door... if I were hiding something. FUCK! Give me atleast SOME credit... if I were cheating on you, I'd have went to a hotel!" Ivy paused... staring at Sean in disbelief. Poison Ivy - "So is THAT what you do?" Stevens - "NO! I've NEVER cheated on you! I NEVER will. I love YOU and only you, Ivy. More than anything in this world. Why can't you BELIEVE me?" Ivy gave Sean a cold stare. Like she wanted to slap him again... or worse. Poison Ivy - "Because you don't give me REASON to believe you." A yellow cab pulled up next to Ivy... as she opened the door to get in. Sean, desperate man that he was, dropped to his knees burying his face in the rain. Stevens - "Please, Ivy... don't leave me. Please. Look... you say I don't give you reason... Let me try." Sean quickly hopped to his feet, and held the cab door open. The cab driver gave Sean a look, but he held his hand up... as an act of a desperate man, needing for him to wait. "Marry me, Ivy. I LOVE you more than anything... Just marry me. Let me SHOW you how much I love you. Be my wife..." Ivy began to cry. Everything she saw said Sean Stevens was a lying, cheating dog. But, for some reason... deep on the inside... she STILL believed him. If only a little bit. Poison Ivy - "... I can't. I can't trust you. We're not ready. We have MAJOR issues." Stevens - "Please, Ivy... let's get through them TOGETHER... make me the luckiest man in the world... just say 'Yes'--" Poison Ivy - "Sean... Sean... your apartment is smoking!" Sean turned his attention to the apartment... Ivy was right. The hotdogs, he thought. He must have forgotten to turn the stove off through all the confusion. Stevens - "Ivy, please... stay right here. I'll be back. Sean took off running toward the apartment... looking back, gesturing for his girlfriend to stay. But, as he turned back to the apartment... he heard the screeching of tires... as the cab took off. With the woman he loved... inside. Gone possibly forever.
