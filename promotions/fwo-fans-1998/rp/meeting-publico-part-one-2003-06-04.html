Meeting Publico - Part One

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The doorbell rang waking Nate and Steve-O from the traditional mid-day rest. "Man, some people are so rude." Nate said, forgetting that it was somewhere with in the vicinity of two o'clock and three o'clock. To them, however, this was early. They usually don't even crawl out of bed until five, when their laziness is won over by sheer hunger. They needed to eat. Steve-O groaned loudly, grabbing a nearby pillow and sandwiching his head against the cushions of their couch. Could he not read? They were pretty sure the sign they stole from some highway Motel 6 was still kicking around on their doorknob. DO NOT DISTURB. "Dude, go away! We don't want to buy any!" Instead of their doorbell chiming out the tune to 'Smack My Bitch Up' by their favorite techno band, Prodigy, it sounded more like an ice-cream truck rolling by. The pillow was thrown across the room; the cushions turned up in a race across their furniture to reach the door first. Quickly they turned the knob and opened the door. Nate peeked outside, squinting because of the direct sunlight. How he hated it. "Hello!" "Uh, who are you?" Replied Steve-O, an eyebrow raising. On their doorstep stood a man. He was around five feet; ten inches tall, and fit, very fit. He was a common competitor in various triathlons, marathons, like the one held in Boston, and was a professional rock climber. At least, it said so on his shirt. 'List of Things to Do:' it read, before naming off over ten things that Pretty Fly did not even know existed. This triathlon was a completely new concept to them. The Olympics were boring. "Oh, excuse me..." The man said, turning away from them for a moment. He ran a hand through his hair, whipped a pair of rose tinted sunglasses from his shirt pocket and put them on. Then he turned back around, switching the tone of his voice. "Dudes! My hood-brothers call me Brian, Brian Publico!" The way this Brian fellow talked, it just didn't seem natural. It was like he was reading from a pre-written script, like a poor Scott Steiner-promo. It was so obvious. "All right! This guy speaks our lingo!" Nate placed a hand on his shoulder and tugged him into their home. They took his yellow and green plaid tee-shirt, which happened to be their two favorite colors, and laid it over the back of their recliner, leading him into the living room. Each took their seat, sitting around so they could chat with a strange man that appeared to be around their age if not a little bit older. The way he was dressed told them he was a surfer like they were, riding the crystal blue waves in the evenings and smoking some serious cheeba till' daybreak. But his face, his demeanor... if they were actually capable of thought, an alarm would have went off immediately. "Guys," he nodded to them. "Publisho," both said in reply. "Actually," Brian responded with a cheap laugh, scratching above his brow, he didn't like when people mispronounced his name, "it's Publi- never mind... Would you two like to know why I'm here?" The brothers shrugged. Really, they didn't exactly care; Nate was just interested in swiping Brian's awesome shirt. The funds were low, as per usual, and stealing became a regular thing for them. Then, right before Brian was going to continue his speech, the sunglasses he happened to be wearing when he first arrived fell out of Steve-O's pocket and hit the floor. For a moment, there was silence, awkward silence. "Ahem, dude... h-how'd they get there!" Steve-O said nervously, building up a sweat. He carefully and discreetly nudged them away with his toe down under the couch, out of Brian's sight. Brian rolled his eyes when he saw Nate and Steve-O were not looking, and jumped right back into character. "Well, I'm your new personal assistant, sent by Disney." "A what-now?" Brian smiled so his teeth showed, albeit a cheesy one, it worked. "A personal assistant. Basically it's my job to follow you to around, get things for you, run errands, clean up around the house, and take down any suggestions you have for the fine folks at Disney! Isn't that just neat?" Oops, he forgot the... "Um, DUDE." Pretty Fly exchanged glances and looked at Brian, studying him. "Ah man, I always wanted a slave!" "Totally!" added an overly excited 'Narly' Nate, jumping up from his seat to give his best friend and brother a high five, "Oh dude, that means we can, like, become lazy slugs!" Publico was disgusted as he looked around their house. He found underwear over lampshades, not only unsanitary but a fire hazard as well. He also discovered several dead rats in the corner of the room, the television remote stuck in a tub of peanut butter, as well as a blow up doll lying spread-eagle next to Steve-O. This was the crudest of all places he had ever been to. "...Yes... become..." He shuddered. This was going to be such a long, hard road to walk. But, in the end, it would all be worth it. If only they knew his master plan.
