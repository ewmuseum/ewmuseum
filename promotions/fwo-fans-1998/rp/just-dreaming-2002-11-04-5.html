Just Dreaming .....

4 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"The future belongs to those who believe in the beauty of their dreams." -Eleanor Roosevelt He was furious that they did not show up. He slammed the car door shut and turned on the engine, making it churn extra hard, if any harder it would have probably stopped working. He put the car into first gear and sped off out of the parking lot. In his rear view mirror, he could see the road agent get into his car shaking his head. This was not the kind of impression he wanted to leave for the management. "Thanks for ruining my dream... thanks!" He was engulfed in hatred towards his comrades. He lit up a cigarette and began to puff at the stick of tobacco. As it slowly burnt away, he thought about the way Troy and Tisha had treated him in the past outside of the wrestling arenas. "We're going to go out to see a movie, we'll be back later!" He remembered Tisha telling him that as they left for a movie, he never got invited to come along... very rarely. "Hey we're going to go grab a bite to eat, you want us to bring you back something" It frustrated him that that was not the friendship between Troy and himself at least not, what he wanted. He took a drag of the cigarette and rolled down his driver side window. He flicked the bud out onto the side of the highway as a medivac helicopter flew overhead. He leaned forward and looked out through top part of the windshield to see what was making so much noise. He watched the helicopter move quickly right down the highway and about a quarter mile down the highway, he could see the flashing lights. He never really thought anything of it. "God, I hope this isn't on this side of the highway... good knows I don't need anyone else holding me back!" He was frustrated, soon enough he would regret everything he said. As he got closer to the accident, Seth was relieved as he realized the accident was on the other side of the highway. The medivac helicopter was just taking off as he passed by the wreckage. "God damn, that Lincoln is a Geo now!" He laughed to himself. The noise of the sirens faded out soon after he passed by. He continued down the highway towards the hotel where they were staying. He was ready to explode on Troy and Tisha for not coming when he told them they had to or else. It is too bad he failed to realize that the black Lincoln he so openly joked about was that of his own friends.
