Faith in Egypt II

1 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"We have a problem." Chris Shepherd said to Silver Fox. "What now?" Fox asked, obviously fearing that another misfortune had befallen the fwo. "It's about Egypt." Chris moved forward carefully. "Let me guess, the ring has fallen apart and they're supplying us with some knives to wrestle on?" "Not that bad... I hope." Chris said. "You brought us here in an attempt to help your image, right?" "If you say so." Silver Fox said, hoping to get to the point and then end this conversation. "Well, Deacon's image is horrible in Egypt." "That's no big deal. If the MSG crowd could boo the Rock, then we can work through this. Maybe it'd make a good one show story?" "Story... or international incident." Chris said. "Deacon could get booed out of the building, attacked, or worse. No matter what, it's going to be bad for any reaction we get." "That's no big deal, we'll make it work. If they bring those stupid signs, then we'll confiscate them. We do that stuff all the time." "Well... I'm just letting you know so that you're prepared." Chris said. "We gotcha covered. You just have Big D ready to thrill the crowd with ... what are you doing anyway?" "Just a segment... talk and the like." "OK, everyone loves your mic work." Silver said. "At least, they don't try to stone you or anything. I'm sure it'll be fine." "Thanks." Chris said, but he was anything but certain. What could he talk about? If he mentioned their faith, they would be stoned. If he didn't, they wouldn't be doing what makes them who they are as performers as well as people. ---------------------------------------- "What did he say?" Deacon asked. "For us not to sweat it. They'll make it work." Chris said. "And security?" "He was certain we wouldn't have problems." "T'en he not know much about the situation." "God is in control, D. Whatever happens, it's His will." "He better be, cause he only one t'at I trust in t'is situation." Deacon said. Faith would be tested again, this time not in the ring, but out of it. Deacon only hoped that the evidence people found was not used against a killer in a court of law. Then again, if someone killed him in Egypt, they wouldn't have to go to court anyway. History gave us all the evidence we needed to prove that fact.
