Tonight...

26 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Tonight... February 26, 2000 (The camera fades in on The Undergrounds locker room in the Kiel Center. This time, Wretch is absent. Only Mike Bear, Fear, and the newest member Hellrazor are present. Fear rests on a couch while Mike and Hellrazor are having a conversation in the middle of the room) Razor: What the hell is up with Wretch? Mike: I dont know, man. Im sorry you had to join when Wretch is in the middle of...whatever hes going through. Razor: Nah, its no problem. I wanted to join. Mike: Thanks man, I just hope we can work this out, Id hate for you to think this is how the Underground conducts itself. Razor: Just work it out, and dont worry about me. Geoff: Mike, can we start? Mike: Yeah, sure, just me right? Geoff: Yeah, you and Wretch are the only two with a match tonight. Mike: Ok, Fear, could you and Hellrazor go somewhere? Fear: Yeah, whatever. (Fear and Hellrazor exit the locker room, leaving only Mike and Geoff) Mike: I wanted to start out by saying that, in case no one knew, Hellrazor is now a part of the Underground. It really wasnt made clear on the broadcast, so I wanted to clear that up. The Millennium Men arent worth anymore of my interview time. Guano Apes, I wish you luck tonight. Jackhammer, when you gave me that powerbomb off the top of the cage, I realized that there was indeed another team in the fWo that actually had talent. That match could have gone either way, and Im looking forward to meeting you in the ring again. Ric Chronos and Fallen, I wish you luck also. May the best team win, because The Underground doesnt want the second best challengers. Now, there are a few others who need some good sentiments. Chris, I hope you tear Davis a new one tonight. Good luck out there, and dont get yourself killed. Strangler, make Rain see the error of siding with FTfWo. Just make sure he knows his place. Collins, Ice, I know you dont like me, but I just want you two to remember the other guy youre facing. Double team him, then go after each other. And finally, as much as I dont wish to say it, good luck to Eron. Just beat Wilson, ok? Thats all I have to say Geoff. I gotta cut this short to make sure Wretch is ready for tonight. Geoff: I hope you can talk some sense into him. Mike: I hope so too. (Mike exits the room and the camera fades to black)
