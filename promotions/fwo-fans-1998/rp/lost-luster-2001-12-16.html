Lost Luster

16 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Barry had a dilemma. As the owner of Gold's Gym, he had certain obligations that he had to fulfill. One of them was to make sure that the gym's patrons were happy: give them clean towels, make sure there wasn't anything growing in the shower stall walls. Occasionally, when someone was interrupting several people's workouts, Barry would approach the disruptive person and either give them a warning or kick them out. But what do you do if the guy being disruptive is as big as a house? What's worse, what do you do when you know the guy? Standing behind the counter, Barry looked across the gym. Kodiak Vic Creed had come in about an hour ago. Usually, Vic would stop and shoot the breeze with Barry before working out. Today he came in and went straight to the weights, still wearing his street clothes. As he passed by, Barry could smell the scent of stale whiskey. That immediately told Barry that something was very wrong with the Main Man. But now, things were starting to get out of hand. Vic was leaving weights all over the place, stealing towels from people, and openly cussing as he worked. It sounded like if Vic couldn't quite lift the weight, he'd 'swear' it up. Barry stepped out from behind the counter as Kodiak Vic Creed walked over to the water fountain. Creed would take a mouthful of water, gargle with it, and spit all over the floor. "Hey Vic," Barry said. "What's going on?" Creed turned and walked back to the bench. He sat down and picked up the huge dumbbell off the floor. Barry stood stunned, before following Vic to the bench. "Hey Vic, don't blow me off in my own gym." Vic lay down on the bench, the pair of dumbbells resting on his chest. His white hair fell to the side; it was longer than Barry had ever seen it before. There was at least a couple days' worth of stubble on his face as well. Puffy bags hung under Creed's eyes. "You look like crap, Champ," Barry said. The dumbbells crashed to the floor as the Alaskan Juggernaut got to his feet. "What did you call me?" Creed asked. "I, uh, I just called you Champ," Barry said nervously. "I mean, you're the fWo Internet Champion, right?" KVC stepped close. "Of course I'm the Champ! What the Hell kind of stupid question is that? Who does the fWo have that can come close to beating me?" "Black Quicksilver did it once already," chirped a voice from behind. With an incredulous look on his face, Creed turned to the speaker. A young man wearing a Gold's Gym T-shirt was mopping up the water Vic had spit on the floor. "Excuse me, runt? Did you just say something," Vic read the nametag on the young man's shirt," Lenny?" "All I said is that Black Quicksilver pinned you last week," said Lenny as he mopped the floor. "So technically, he's done more than come close to beating you. He's done it." Vic snatched the mop from Lenny's hands and snapped it over Lenny's head. The young man fell to the floor, clutching his head in pain. Vic dropped the mop segments on the floor. "Nobody beat the Main Man," Creed snarled at Lenny. "You got that? Nobody beat the Main Man!" "Dammit Vic!" Barry snapped as he knelt down to check on his employee. "Get the Hell out of here or I'll call the cops!" His massive shadow falling over both men, Creed's stare burned a hole into each of them. Vic was obviously out of control, Barry thought. Moreso than usual. The Gold's Gym employees remained on the floor, waiting to see what Creed would do next. Surprisingly, he turned suddenly and walked out the door. 'What was THAT all about?" Lenny asked as he held his head. "I don't know," Barry said. "And I don't want to know."
