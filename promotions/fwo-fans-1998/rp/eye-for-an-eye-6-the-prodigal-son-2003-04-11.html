Eye For An Eye [6] The Prodigal Son

11 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To survive in this world, you must learn to adapt. Adapt or perish... it's a simple premise, part of adaptation is learning to let go, people come and people go, we live... we die. When my father left a wife and three children behind we learned to adapt, we had to... my mother had to face the fact that she no longer had a husband and we had to face the grim reality that we no longer had a father. But there are two kinds of people in this world... the reasonable ones like my sister, brother, mother and I, who learn to adapt to the world around us. And the unreasonable ones, like my father, who persist in adapting the world to themselves. Just as we'd let out father go, just as we'd given up on the possibility of him ever returning. Back he came, to change the world again. September 18th 1992. "HOLY SHIT!" A simple precision throw... a simple touchdown as result. It's a strange world that we live in... a world where a good arm and an eye for a pass can make you a billionaire. The crowd were on their feet, their local football team was on good form and a certain quarterback could do no wrong. "Kid's good... you've got to admit that much, this trip wasn't wasted." Trey Masters spoke up to his colleague as they stood amongst a bustling capacity crowd. "Has a good arm, a damned good arm if we're honest... he hasn't thrown a bad pass in the entire game, so what do you think... do we talk to this kid?" Chet Monroe replied, as Trey looked down at a list of squad numbers in front of him. "Nah... maybe we should mail him, don't want to intimidate the boy... you think we can get him over to Miami? I mean... he's not going to be one of these kids that won't leave his family, is he?" Masters continued. Monroe let out a stifled laugh. "A Dolphins contract, millions of bucks and any woman that he wants? He'll sign." Masters looked down to the squad list in front of him... circling player details in red ink. 11. Kellen Kinkade. As the two Miami Dolphins scouts slowly made their way through the bustling crowd, they brushed past another individual who scouted the very same player, but whilst they watched his skill, his accuracy and his passing ability. Vince Kinkade watched the strength, resilience and power of his eldest son. September 20th 1992. "Hey Mom, I'm back... what are we eating today?" Kellen Kinkade voiced up as he walked through the front door of his New Jersey home, tossing his bag down in the lobby... he entered the front room to be greeted by his younger sister, who provided a less than polite answer. "Shit." Dawn Kinkade replied with a smile across her face. Kellen sighed. "Keep talking like that Dawn, you'll end up with Toby in Juvenile Hall." He replied, shaking his head as his younger sister flipped the bird in his direction and continued to flick through cable T.V. channels. Kellen turned away, making his way toward the kitchen. "Mom! You around? Mo- ... Dad?" Kinkade said, stopping in his tracks. Vince Kinkade sat in his spot at the dinner table as though he'd never left, despite his appearance being the first in years. "Son! Good to see you... take a seat why don't you?" Vince spoke up... leaning forth at the table "Come on now... I don't bite." Kellen reluctantly slid out a chair, and took a seat. "Been a while eh boy? Got anything you want to say?" Vince said with a smile. "Not in particular." Kellen replied nonchalantly. "Ah come on now... lets not do this again, you can sulk and pout as much as you want but the fact remains Kellen, I pay for all of this... this house, the food on this table... those clothes that you're wearing, hell... even your tuition." Vince spoke up, a more stern expression upon his face. "Yeah, not to mention the price that I pay... having to hear you say that every time you come home, is there anything else that you want to share with me Dad? Or can I go now?" Vince sighed. "Your home boy, go where you like." Kellen shoved back his seat and stood, but turned right into his Mother... who stood staring right through Vince. "Darling!" Vince piped up as he got to his feet and moved across the room "Nice to see you my dear, can I get a kiss?" He planted a kiss on the cheek of his wife... but his attempts to be caring were like a one-way street where the rest of his family were concerned, nothing was ever going to come back the other way. Martha slowly pushed back her husband and stared him in the eye. "What do you want?" "Hey now, what's with the cold reception? WWC is in town, I thought I'd drop by and see my wonderful wife and..." Vince leant aside for a moment, looking into the front room and at his daughter Dawn as she mouthed the words to "Smells Like Teen Spirit" by Nirvana as it played on MTV. "... adorable children." Martha scowled. "Then why not pay Toby a visit and get the hell out of here?" Martha sneered "He's behind bars you know... peas in a pod you'll be, sure you'll feel right at home." Vince narrowed his eyes and balled up a fist, but saw an intensity creep into the eyes of his son Kellen as he did. "Nah... I think I'll stay..." Vince said, his fist relaxing "Besides... that pie over there looks too good an opportunity to pass up... think I can get a slice?" "If it'll keep you quiet for a while... then yes." Martha replied sternly as she walked across the room "Sit down Kellen, I'll cut you a slice." "Sorry... can't, got to head out to meet Lisa right now." Kellen said as he dipped his finger into the pie and licked it clean "Tastes great though." "Ah..." Vince piped up with a proud smile "That's my boy... got himself a girlfriend." Kellen narrowed his eyes in the direction of his father. "Oh yeah... I forgot to ask Mom; did anything come for me in the mail today? I talked to Chris earlier and he was saying that he got a mail from some football scouts, apparently they're pretty high on his game... he said that if they were interested they'd have sent something over to me in the post." "No... not that I can remember, sorry." Martha said as the optimism in Kellen's eyes faded slightly "But maybe it's still down at the mail place, I'll drop in and check for you tomorrow if you like." Kellen smiled, leaning forth and kissing his mother on the forehead. "Thanks Mom... I need to go to the bathroom; can you listen for the phone? Lisa should call sometime soon." "Sure." Martha replied, as Kellen turned and exited the room. "The boy grew up fast." Vince started up as Martha cut him a slice of pie "Looking real strong... guess being in good shape came from my genes, lot of power there... looks like he got your mentality though, son of a bitch won't listen to a word I sa-." SPLAT. Vince Kinkade looked down, at the slice of pie that had exploded across his shirt. Standing up from the table he used the cloth upon it to wipe off the mess. "Something I said?" He sighed. "Just get out." Martha replied, turning her back on him. "I'm going... man can tell when he's not welcome, see you around." Making his way out of the kitchen, Vince entered the lobby and made his way to the front door but as he reached it, the ringing sound of an incoming call made his ears prick up. One swift motion later, the ringing ceased. "Hello?" Vince said quietly. "Hi! May I speak to Kel please?" A chirpy voice on the other end of the line exclaimed. "May I ask who's calling?" Vince replied, slowly pulling shut the door connecting the hall with the kitchen, so that his voice could not be heard. "Sure... I'm Lisa, Kel's girlfriend... is he there? He asked me to call." Vince's eyes lit up. "Girlfriend? I'm sorry Lisa... but Kellen just headed out with a girl a few moments ago, I just presumed that it was you, maybe she's a friend." Vince added as a demonic smile crept across his face. "Oh..." Lisa's concerned voice replied "Are you sure... I mea-" "Positive." Vince cut in. "Okay... goodbye then, I guess." Lisa finished. Click. Vince's sick smile grew, as he carefully placed the phone on the hook... and exited out of the front door. "Football... pussies game, I won't see my boy wasted in a not brainer profession... he's a wrestler, it's in his blood... it's his destiny... ... I'll make sure of that." Vince Kinkade stopped at the bottom of the garden, to pull something out of his pocket... ... a Miami Dolphins contract, addressed to Kellen Kinkade. One swift tear ripped in half not only the contract, but a piece of Kellen Kinkade's future. And one swift throw landed it right in the trash... ... along with Kellen's dreams. Vince Kinkade always believed that you could control your own fate, but he would never be content with just that... he would control the fate of others too. His son was going to be a wrestler... whether he liked it or not.
