Indiana Speck and the Temple of Fire: Torch Slugs Bleed A Lot

1 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Codemaster was halfway up the cage tower that they had to climb before he finally commented, "Wouldn't it just be easier to freaking let me cast a spell and let me create a Horror that, under my incredible arcane powers, would simply throw us all up there? Hell, Link, your penchant for violence would enable you to simply blast the living crap out of the horror with one of them bombs you're carryin' around." "Shut up." Link said, as he reached the block and was starting to push it over the fire pillar, "I'm trying to think here." "Ooh," Codemaster said, sarcastically, as he leapt down to where Link was, "Big thinker. Push big block over big flame geyser. You're a real frickin' Einstein." "Einstein?" Link asked. After all, Hyrule people know not of legendary physics masters. "Nevermind." Codemaster said, as he assisted Link in pushing the block down. Ken War looked up from below, and tried to direct block pushing traffic for Codemaster and Link, "ok, jest ay leetl 2 teh ryt!11 nununu!!11 2 fer!!1 2 teh lft!11" And that's when Codemaster and Link accidently crushed Ken War to death with the stone block. The sound effect you just heard was one of our personal favorites, along the lines of: SPLATTER. The Codemaster and Link looked down at the stone block, which was now saturated with the blood of Ken War, although Ken War's pieces were now trying to crawl out from under the block and reassemble themselves at this very moment. Anyway, Link turned to the Codemaster and spoke to him, "You know, the funny thing is, I wanted it to happen, and then it did!" The Codemaster ignored him and jumped down from their position, and proceeded to move the block over the flame geyser. From there, the four stood on the block and were blasted upwards into the next room. They all exited the room and came across a far more foul and cruel creature that dwell within the confines of the Fire Temple. It was a creature that was solely responsible for a large amount of incenerated bones that populated a stark corner of this multi-tiered room. It blocked the only exit in the room, aside from the one that our heroes came through, which would've led our heroes into a room filled with rolling boulders. The room required someone to hit a switch and quickly race to the top of the room and climb a fence before the flames that block the passerby can erupt again. But the creature was mean, vicious, and a towering inferno of evil. Ladies and gentlemen, meet the Torch Slug. "Growl, bitch." it said. Link shot it with an arrow. The flames stopped that surrounded the Torch Slug suddenly stopped. "OW!" the Torch Slug said, "That REALLY hurt." The Codemaster looked up, "All that buildup and he's a wuss?" That's when Link sliced the Torch Slug in half, splattering the Codemaster, Heftel, War, and himself in the Torch Slug's blood. "Aw, HELL NO. You didn't just splatter blood all over my dreads." the Codemaster said, trying to wipe off the blood from his tunic. That was when Mike Heftel pointed at the bleeding Torch Slug. "Um, guys? Are they supposed to bleed THIS much?" he asked, as they were now starting to become up to their ankles in blood. "ren waawwy!!111" Ken War shouted. And run away, they did do, as they started to go up to some higher ground. However, in order to progress, a block needed to be moved. And, well, that's kinda hard to do when blood was rapidly filling up a room. So, that's when the Codemaster busted out the Tome of Eternal Darkness and went to a specific page. When he got there, he pointed at the Torch Slug's remnants. "Tier, Aretak, Ulyaoth!" he shouted, summoning a small scorpion-like creature and surrounding he and his allies in a blue Damage Field. "GOOD JOB!" Link shouted, "You summoned a damn insect!" The Codemaster sharply turned to Link, "You shoot that insect and we're all dead. You like being dead, elf boy?" Link frowned and put down his bow and arrow, which he had previously aimed at the Codemaster's scorpion-like creature. The Codemaster directed the creature to the bleeding Torch Slug, and suddenly balled up his fist and pumped it. Suddenly, the scorpion exploded into light, and the Torch Slug, as well as a significant amount of the blood, vanished. "You know, if I weren't Mike Heftel, I'd say that was one of the more screwed up things I've seen." Heftel said. "What the hell was that, anyway?" Link asked, as the Damage Field disappeared. "It's my Summon Trapper spell. I sent the bleedin' corpse to an infinite plane of existence called the Trapper Dimension. Which is no doubt one of the more wonky things I've seen," he said, before he watched Ken War stab himself with a fork, "And trust me, after hangin' around these two? That kinda says a lot." With that, our heroes moved the block, hit the switch, and climbed up the once-flaming fence. Ken War was a step too slow, though, and he was incinerated. Oh, like that wasn't gonna happen again.
