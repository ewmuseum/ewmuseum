Tryptich Part 2 - We're All Stars Now "There were egos and politics running rampant in the CSWA when I was there for my first stint, but I let it go without passing judgement. Partly because I was a newcomer to the company and figured it would be better to be ignored until I was established, but mostly because the main perpetrators of said politics were all massive stars in the business, who had earned the right to do what they wanted. The CSWA in '98, however... prima donna shrikes who'd never main evented a televised wrestling card in their life." "Hell, the guy who approprited my gimmick name, 'The Eliminator' eventually quit the promotion - or was fired, depending on who you ask - declared himself the franchise of the company even after Kevin Powers and me had to carry him to a halfway decent match at Elvis '99." "It was one of the sorriest sights I'd ever seen before. The CSWA had lost so much talent they were willing to push anyone who could get a reaction until they carved out their new identity. That's when Ivy came up with her little buzzword, 'disposable hero' to refer to the multitude of substitute teachers the Fed was producing. Sure, there were shining gems like the Deacon and Eddy Love, but for every Eddy Love there was a Rap Man with even less of a future than I had." "And watching all of this made me realize just how sorry I was I'd come back to such a state. I openly mocked the entire concept of a star, superstar, whatever.... mainly to get a rise outta the people who took the words so seriously." "But it worked in a way I didn't think it could. I think I can honestly say it was the first time someone became a main event player in the world of professional wrestling by exxagerating myself so much that nobody could take me seriously. And for some reason, they did just that." "I'm serious. The whole Dope Show thing, pink hair, sequins pinned into my trench... it was all just me trying to be the most outrageous prima donna possible, without doing any of that typical primadonna shit." "I just didn't care anymore. And it wasn't about being a depressed kid.... I was twenty seven years old, damnitt. I mean I just didn't care. Wrestling had become a way to make a living, nothing more and nothing less. And then Ivy told me they wanted to make me World Champion." "...What the fuck?" "'Our long- term goal,' Merritt had said, 'is to put the title on Deacon. But considering your current program with Hornet, the appeal you have with our audience, and the work you've given this company over the years, we'd like you to be the man who bridges the gap between the men.' Five months of main event money? Sure, why not?" "Of course, that's precisely when I started to wonder if it was worth it." "For years, I thought that the worst possible way I could fail was to return to the Bronx before I was ready. Before I had found my place in the world and taken my turn at the plate. But now that I was there, now that I was commanding the attention of the entire world... I didn't want it. I suddenly realized that I enjoyed, even preferred my role of secondary hero, the guy you cheer for but never expect to save the day. How could the King of Extreme be a hero, for that matter?" "It probably sounds odd to you, but I really struggled with this. Very few people are in a position to speak to so many faceless people at once... and when you don't know how long it'll last you want to choose your words carefully. But suddenly, lacking the righteous determination of a Hornet, lacking the moral fortitude of a Deacon.... I suddenly felt just as fake and hollow as the caricature of Superstar I'd played to a T months before. Trying to show everyone how shallow the celebrity had become, and all I did was convince myself." "It bothered me for a long time.... I took time off, I Staked a Claim, I severed the bond I had with the fans, all in the name of trying to find something genuine." "Yeah, a bond with the fans. I can't figure it out either, though Ivy could. If a nobody like me could become the World Champion on his own terms, maybe there was hope for everyone else. So the Original Nobody was hatched, but I still felt like I was just going through the motions. Seriously felt like hangin' it all up. I even uprooted from the Bronx... and headed west toward Hollywood and the false images and atrophied souls that populate it." "But it was there... that I was saved again. Not by a McGinnis woman... but by the purest Angel I've ever met in my life." "Want to take a guess?"
