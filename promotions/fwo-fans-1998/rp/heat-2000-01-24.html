Heat.

24 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Heat. January 24, 2000 :::The scene opens up after Slaughter. Geoff Green walks the halls, heading for the exit. He pulls a large suitcase behind him, containing all his video equipment. Geoff is in an excessively good mood, as through that video equipment he has witnessed the power over the company that employs him be stripped from the man who would kill the promotion, Dead. He is in such a good mood, in fact, that he begins to whistle a happy tune as he walks. Geoff does not discern it, but a second voice in the distance begins whistling with him. Slowly, Geoff begins to hear this, and wonders what exactly is going on. Nonetheless, he continues on his way, when he turns a corner and hears-::: Hello, Geoff. :::Geoff nearly falls backwards and trips over his own suitcase, before collecting himself and looking up to see-::: Perfect. That's my name, don't wear it out. What exactly do you wa- Interview, Geoff. Now. Got me? :::Geoff nods.::: :::15 minutes later. From the opulence and luxury of the dressing room, it is quite obvious we are in the lair of Perfect. Geoff Green's camera equipment has been drug out and set up, and now we go to the view from his camera.::: Well. What a night it has been, huh? What a night indeed. One thing's for sure- it most definitely has not been a good night for the members of that oh-so-cool organization known as FTfWo. You see, tonight tuned out to be the last time we had power in the fWo. That, in and of itself, does not concern the WGA that much. Hell, the D-Man always has a plan. For the love of God, the man is the Plan King of the state of Plan-shington in the United States of Plan. And Strangler...Finn...whoever...you'd better realize that. I've fought with this man, and I've fought against him, and man is a damn pit bull- once he gets his teeth in you, he doesn't stop ripping. And I realize that in the near future, everybody's gonna try to make my life a living hell for what I've done. To paraphrase Don Henley, suppose you tell me, boys, who do you think I, think I am, and boys, don't you give a damn? In case you didn't realize, I am Scott Slugger. I am the World's Greatest Athlete. I am Perfect. I have been around this promotion since the beginning and I have not spent a single day without somebody or other trying to beat my ass. It's going to take a hell of a lot more than Scott Collins, Scott Collins' goon squad, Ice, and whoever else the fWo wants to throw at me to take me down and out. You see, I'm sure everybody wants a piece of me, but those two- Collins and Ice- are gonna be the ones bringing it at me the hardest. I would know- I've been around the block with those two more times than I care to count. I've had success against both men. I won two of my four World Titles against those two men. I have beaten both of them cleanly. Collins has never beaten me without some sort of interference, surprise, or foreign object, and Ice? Ice hasn't beaten me once. They'll definitely be bringing it at me the hardest, but as you can plainly see, this will be anything but a challenge for the WGA. Collins, for one, is pathetic. He talks about how he hasn't lost to me since February 1999, but he hasn't beaten me cleanly- ever. Collins has proven to me that he is nothing more than a self-righteous, muscle-bound moron who desperately wants to believe that he is indeed a big bad-ass, when in reality, he's never once had what it takes to be at my level. Not to mention the fact that he's Stone's bitch. Collins, listen up real good. You have been a nothing, a nobody your entire career in the fWo. You started off as Scotty Cool, a nothing. Scotty Cool started off tagging with Ultra-Violet in the most disgusting, nauseating tag team the fWo has seen since the Flying Frenchie and Primetime teamed up, and he won a few tag titles. So what? He jobbed them to Ground Zero and Heaven's Demons...not exactly stiff competition, big man. He jobbed the Internet title to the Boston Strangler because of a freaking candy statue! If that doesn't show that he doesn't have the brains God gave a doorknob, I don't know what does. And then, of course, as was to be expected, he jobbed his first World Title to none other than me. Wow, there's a shock. And then, somewhere in that little pea brain of his, the little tiny gears began turning, and, after the rust in there wore off, he got an idea- "Hey, nobody takes me seriously now, let's see if I can trick them into thinking I'm somebody!" So he comes out at CyberSlam calling himself Dooth or some such and screws me out of my belt with some brass knuckles. But he couldn't break the streak of losing his belt to crap- he jobbed it right away to Ultra-Violet. UV, for the love of God! This continued when he jobbed the International Title to Eron the Talentless...which speaks for itself. Then, you again showed your lack of balls by refusing to defend your title against me. For the love of God, man, how the hell did you ever get to sixty wins anyway?! But I digess. My point is that Scotty Cool proved that he was a nothing, and Death proved that despite his fancy clothes and dark sunglasses, he was still a nobody. I think history shows you better face the facts, Collins- YOU WILL NEVER AMOUNT TO ANYTHING. And the other man who wants a piece of me...is Ice. I think he all know what's happened every single time he and I have gone against each other. Just like his namesake, and just like you, Collins, Ice melted in the bright lights and the heat of the big time. You see, when it comes right down to it, I am the Big Time. I am Perfect. And I bring the heat. And I am white hot. The question, Collins, Barrett, is this- Can you take the heat? We'll see, won't we? :::Perfect stares into Geoff's camera a moment longer, before slashing his thumb across his throat. Geoff complies, and the camera slowly fades to black...and then to static.:::
