Making a profit...

11 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Making a profit... January 11, 2000 [The camera opens up by the entrance to an fWo arena. A wooden merchandise stand is at the front of a huge line, with many ladies in it. They are all screaming, and waving dollar bills in the air. They are yelling at the man behind the stand, who is handing out magazines. He is none other than Havok. The ladies are all yelling.] Lady: I want one! Give me one! [A lady with a British accent speaks up above the rest.] British Lady: Yes! I'll pay fifty dollars for one! Havok: Fifty? You got it. Get up here, lady! [She runs up to the front, hands Havok a fifty dollar bill and runs off with the magazine in her hand. Another lady is off to the side reading the magazine.] Lady reading magazine: Oh my god! He's not human! He's hung like a horse! [The lady next to her speaks up.] Lady: Not just any horse, like a freaking Clydesdale! [Another lady yells even louder.] Lady: Not just any Clydesdale, the Budweiser horses! Ya know, the ones at Sea World? What kind of beer company buys an amusement park anyway? [Havok yells out to her, while taking more money.] Havok: It's a great way to make money! Maybe I should buy a whale.... Yes! A whale! As long as I don't buy a big one, that's something Chris Universal would do. He's too rich, I'm just trying to make some spare change, but by the looks of things, I'll need more magazines soon! [He counts his money while the camera pans out to reveal the rest of the line, going all the way across the parking lot. The camera cuts to a commercial for Sea World.]
