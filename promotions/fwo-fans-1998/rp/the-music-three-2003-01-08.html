Ric Tatum. It took a long time comin but we waited Xzibit went from underated To now most antcipated never would of thought that I would rock your set And get love and respect without no special effects Only the rugged ruff shit that the hard rock need Lyrics must contain more then just clothes bitches and weed The song so synonymous to his situation began bumping soundwaves into Ric's head. He was victorious in his first bout and the second would be no different. Stormwindz, to him, was nothing more than a pushover woman in the world of men. What he did on Countdown 1 was nothing personal, it's a dog eat dog world we live in and he was aware of it. Wrestling is nothing more than a business where promoters rake in cash, where big men beat on other big men and where sex appeal means everything. But to some it meant more, it was their existance. Tryin to glority greed Livin life at ridiculuos speed-indeed! The main goal to achieve, is to succeed Have it laid out for your seed But the world gets colder every day by day Physically, mentally gettin drained by L.A. Trying to find piece of mind is like trying to find a gold mine it just wont happen no time for relaxin Real soldiers of fortune the front line of the action Ric Tatum wanted respect. It was evident, a major part of his whole wrestling career was to prove his family were not second rate competitors. They were big time players in anything they did. Mere bad luck was what kept his family from blowing up in success and Ric was going to redeem all of that. He was not a tool or a spoke on a wheel, spinning endlessly while the engine was running the show. Coming to the end of a street he turned left, the view was clear. It was now nearing 3 PM, since 6 o'clock Tatum had been jogging, listening to music and focusing on tonight. His intensity level could not be brought down, only lifted. He thought about Stormwindz then compared to the future, it was a must that he wins this match tonight. If not, his career would be in jeopardy, already. Xzibit lookin at the earth thru wide angles I found a stairway to heaven in a city of lost angels These lyrics signified everything he was. Only he his stairway came in the form of a city of lost wrestlers. And tonight, slowly, he would begin his climb to the top. Xzibit bring truth to the light shit is movin at the speed of life Tatum accelerated to his maximum speed.. Movin... At the speed of life It's go time. Lyrics: "At The Speed Of Life" by Xzibit
