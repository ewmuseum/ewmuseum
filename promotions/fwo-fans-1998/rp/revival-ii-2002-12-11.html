Revival II

11 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Saturday, December 7th, 3:15 AM Another weekend party at the apartment had fell victim to the early morning hours. By now, most people in attendance had either cleared out, returned home, or sought alternate locations for recreation. There were a few however who had found their way into one of the back bedrooms with Shane or Alex and decided to make use of the time with some drugs the two kept for such occasions. Jason however found himself standing in the front room with Paul conversing. It was a friendly talk, both men reeling from the effects of a night's worth of booze. Paul took one last sip from his beer and watched the last group of friends walk out the door, giving them a friendly smile and a wave goodbye. He then looked at his friend Jason. "So Jase, tell me more about the fWo, what's the story there?" he asked. Jason took a sip of his glass. "Not much to tell, you know, they finally got back to me after all this time. I got the call today, and I leave tomorrow for Colorado." Paul placed his glass on an adjacent table and did a double-take. "What? Tomorrow?" "Yeah, they've got me in a match for this Sunday in Colorado Springs... what?" Jason was getting slightly annoyed and confused by his friends odd reaction. "It's just... I mean... god Jason, are you sure you're ready?" Paul asked. Jason finished off his drink and glanced around. "What? What the hell are you talking about? Of course I'm ready, I've been ready for this since they went on hiatus months ago, I've been ready since those jag-offs kicked me out of Survivor, I've been ready-", Paul cut him off. "No, no no. I mean physically, are you ready? It's just, well face it Jase, you haven't been in a ring in damn well five months, and I can't remember the last time you worked out, or even took a jog around the block for Christ's sake." "You're fuckin' kidding me." Jason skeptically replied. "You don't even know what you're talking about. You ever wrestle? You ever wrestle a day in your life? Come on, this is my career, this is my life here, I'll be fine." "Well, I still think you should have taken a couple of weeks for yourself, just to lift, or to train, condition yourself and get back in shape." "Paulie," Jason placed his hand on his friends shoulder, "It's not all about lifting weights, ok? There's more to this than fucking biceps. It's all about strategy, style, your methods, your strategy, and your... what do ya call it... style." "Yeah, I see..." Paulie complied. "It's all up here," Jason said as he motioned with his finger to the side of his temple, smiling. "What about the drugs." Paul lowered his voice and said it again. "What about the drugs Jason." Jason's face turned serious. "I got off of that. I only did that the first couple of months back here. It was just..." "You sure?" Paulie asked. "Yeah, yeah, what do you mean am I sure? Look I'm not an idiot, I got off of that a few weeks ago, a while ago. I'm fine." Paul's eyes glared at Jason with doubt and uncertainty. "You don't see me back there now do you?" Jason said, pointing to the half open door down the hallway. "And what about this?" Paul asked pointing to the empty glass of vodka Jason held in his hand. "Well, I told you Paul, I don't like drinking out of bowls." Jason laughed until Paul grabbed the glass from his hand and tossed it on the table. "Alright, just settle down man, come on," Jason said. "A couple of drinks isn't going to kill me, I'm a wrestler, not a nun. I can drink every now and then." "Did you drink before the hiatus?" Paul asked. "Paul," Jason said with sincerity, "I appreciate your concern, but trust me. I know how to take care of myself, I've been doing it for a hell of a long time." Paul nodded his head. "Well, I hope you're right," he said. "Good luck man, we're all rootin' for you. Bring us back some gold next time you're in town." Paul grinned. "Hey Paul, can I ask for a favor?" Jason said. "What do you need buddy?" "It's just, I'll be heading out on the road tomorrow, and you know, being without work and all for this long I've kinda chewed up all my savings." Paul's grin disappeared. "I'm leaving the guys some cash for the month's rent but you know, that'll basically leave me broke. And the fWo, they don't pay for everything, so could you spot me some money for the next couple weeks? I'd ask it from the other guys but they already blew their last gig money on pot." Paul frowned. "I'd be getting a paycheck in a few weeks," Jason said, "That will cover anything you give me. I just need some to tide me over until then." Paul took a few moments before taking out his wallet and handing Jason a wad of folded bills. Jason flipped through it. "That's all I can give you, I'm not exactly overflowing here Jase." "Thanks man, believe me, this is more than enough, I'll get it back to you as soon as I can." Paul gathered his stuff and began to head out the door. "Paulie," Jason called him back, "one last thing." "The rent's been overdue for more than a month now. Make sure Shane and Alex aren't too surprised when they're evicted." "Of course," Paul said. "Take it easy, and be careful." Jason closed the door and headed to his room to pack. "Ready?" he asked himself again. "You've got to be kidding me."
