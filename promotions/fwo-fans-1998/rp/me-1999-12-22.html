fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Me. December 22, 1999 [Havok is shown sitting in a bar, drinking a soda. He is wearing blue jeans, a white TCW tee-shirt, and a blue cap. He is drinking from his bottle of soda, and watching Courage's interview. The interview ends, and Havok finishes his soda. He looks over at the camera, and, smiling, he speaks....] Courage... I may not be rock n' roll. Thank God. I may listen to it, but I'm not psychotic enough to think that I *am* it. You need to talk to Mike Heftel, buddy, he'll give ya the number of a great doctor. Anyway, speaking of Mike Heftel, all Courage seems to talk about is how Heftel pinned me. Well, Mr. [CENSORED]... [He is interrupted by a man's voice, which we cannot clearly hear. He responds to the man.] Oh, I can't say [CENSORED] on TV? Alright, I won't say [CENSORED] anymore. Anyway, back to Heftel.... The only reason Mike Heftel pinned me was because of that coward, Chris Universal. As far as I'm concerned, no man, or in Courage's case, woman in the fWo can beat me fairly. End of story. Anyway, now you want to do it at the first pay-per-view of the year, in a ladder match. Well, buddy, that's just fine and dandy with me because I will kick your sorry ass all around the arena, knock you out with a ladder, and then climb that ladder, rung by goddamn rung, and grab my title to walk away the champion. And, if by some miracle, I lose my title to Wolf Fang, then we'll go at it anyway, and we can grab something else. Don't get excited Courage, I didn't mean that. Anyway, let's make this interesting. After I beat Courage's phony French ass, I want my match with Primetime. What do ya say Courage? I beat you, I get Primetime. I'll show the whole French Foundation why I am just so damn good, and why you, my friend, suck donkey [CENSORED]. [The man mumbles to him again. He responds.] [CENSORED]! I said it again. Alright, no more. Anyway. You guys get the point, but now, I, the Millennium Man, greatness, am leaving. [He walks off as the camera cuts to an advertisement for the new fWo underwear.]
