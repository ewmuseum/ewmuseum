Somewhere I Belong - The Memory Remains (Final)

19 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Later that evening...Friday April 11th, 2003. After a few days without the Mighty Hero, Aimz was going insane. On top of being needlessly worried about the fact that the few days they were apart, Brandon was spending with his soon to be ex-wife, she'd had a lovely array of much more immediate issues to deal with. Things like getting a horrible flu, the fact that she had a cracked rib - several bruised - and a broken jaw...oh, and she'd been evicted, too! The 'lovely' few days hadn't exactly lifted her spirits about anything, which is why Amy sighed when she stepped into the house. The outside had been gorgeous...a gorgeous reminder of the life Brandon had built long before her. A few seconds after cursing herself for even thinking about that like some jealous child, she was greeted by grins from Sean and Jarod and a massive but deliberately "neutral" hug from their father, whose face was awry with worry about her. He didn't speak first, instead electing to let Amy have the first word and she took the offer, sagging a little against him exhaustedly in the embrace... "Hey, you..." "Hey Amy... can you come outside and wrestle with us again? We've got a small ring here and everything!" Sean's excitement at having "Aimz" in his house blew past his normal manners, interupting his father's sentence before it even began. Amy looked down to Sean, then cast pleading eyes up to Brandon, then back down to the boy again. "Well I just got here, sweetie...why don't you guys run out and get all warmed up? I wanted to tell your dad about my time at home." Sean nodded his head in agreement and headed off down the hall to the backdoor. Jarod however lingered about, clinging to his father's leg as he cocked an eyebrow at the new person in the house. "You really don't have to go wrestle with them. We can sit on the back porch and watch Sean jump around while we talk. He saw all the stuff with those two bast... guys... on the show so he'll understand if you can't play." Jarod loosened his grip from his father's thigh suddenly and pushed past both he and Amy. Just then an icy finger trailed down Ms. Campbell's back as she heard a familiar voice. "Hi, Aimz. I see you made it all right." For a moment, Amy stared straight ahead, somehow hoping she'd find it in her to ignore the voice but the power just wasn't there. From her first word on, Brandon'd squeeze her midsection a little tighter in one arm reassuringly but also as a slight warning not to snap. "Oh...hi, Ana." She would've left it at that, but the redhead was too far into the other woman's territory not to mark some of her own. There were a few things that needed to be outlined in bold, and Amy had the right words for them..."Enjoying these few days with your ex-husband?" "Not 'ex' yet dear. We've got another nine months before that, but yes, yes I have... just like old times. But that's neither here nor there... " Ana reached down as she said this and lifted Jarod up in her arms. Amy's mouth went dry for a second as she looked up to Brandon, who wasn't exactly looking pleased himself - but the look was more fear of what might be about to transpire. She turned back to Ana quickly, though. "Sorry, forgot you still get to cling to the name on paper. It's been just like old times, hmm? Kinda odd, I didn't see any main eventers hanging around your ro--err---how rude of me, that was so six years ago, right?" A mockingly sweet laugh followed, with only a temporary smile before Amy's eyes glazed in rage again. With her head cocked to side as if an idea had just presented itself to her Ana was quick to shoot back. "B.L.'s still a main eventer... right?" In an attempt to get the last word in the mild catfight Ana stepped closer to Amy, catching her slightly off guard. The small but fiesty red raver stood a good few inches shorter than the ebony haired mother of two but she didn't back away. She was still caught off guard as Ana came out of the blue with "Here Jarod, go to your Auntie Aimz. You all go have fun in the back. I'm going to grab that last album and head on, Brandon..." Even still, Amy couldn't help but try for a last shot - something to divert her from a jab of a more physical nature. "Actually, Brandon's been hanging around the midcard with me a bit lately. You'll see Vic Creid in the main events more often now." She smirked, "But seeing him doesn't seem to be much out of the ordinary for you, so I didn't really expect you to recall." Still, the little redhead clung to Brandon pleadingly, hoping he might've stepped in and defended Amy or even himself against Ana's 'Hanging around the bedroom' retort, but no such luck yet. Instead Brandon took a step away from both of them. Patting Jarod on the top of his head as he finally stopped fidgeting in Amy's arms. "That's fine Ana. You need to be here by six tomorrow to get the kids, our plane leaves at eight..." With that he simply turned and followed Sean's path to the back of the house. Ana's eyebrows had raised a bit as Amy's last jab but she held her tongue. Instead of firing off another retort that would probably lead to the redhead defending herself on a more physical plane Ana simply crooked a smile and and winked. She retrieved her coat from the closet and leaned in and gave Jarod a big kiss, mere inches from Amy. "Tell Brandon I'll call later to talk to the boys before they go to bed..." And with that she was out the door, slamming it slightly in her masked aggravation. Amy's eyes narrowed, finding herself alone on the porch with the son of a the man she loved, and a woman she hated more than anything in her arms. Regardless, she couldn't help grumbling to herself as she slowly followed the steps Brandon had taken a moment earlier. Brandon barely even looked when they caught up. "Hey, why don't you go play with Sean for a second, 'kay? I've gotta tell your dad somethin'." Jarod didn't even try to sound enthused. Instead, he cast Amy a lingering glare, mumbled " 'kay" and jogged off, which left her to walk to Brandon. She snuck up behind him slightly - though he'd probably noticed and decidedly ignored her presence at first - and leaned against him with her forehead in a manner that let him feel her shaking slightly. "BL...?" "Hey..." Turning slightly Brandon let his arms fall around Amy's shoulders as he tried his best to steady and reassure her. "Before we even go down this road, let's just forget about it okay? I know there's a lot of weirdness going on right now. It's not anyones fault ya'know? Not now at least..." "I... that man's name... it... shit..." Not even being able to finish Brandon simply pulled the woman to him, holding her firmly as if to reassure himself as well. "What else was I supposed to do? If I stopped, she'd've taken shot after shot until I knocked her out and made the entire family hate me. If I ever see her without the kids around..." Amy tried hard to stop herself just short of that statement, fearful of being within earshot of Sean and Jarod. "Ehm. There's just one thing, though. She was talking like you two were..." After a deep sigh Brandon looked back in to Amy's mismatched eyes. "First off, I can see that the whole 'take the high road' thing just isn't sinking in. Secondly I'm not going to talk about what Ana was implying... talking just makes what she said have power. It doesn't... or rather... it shouldn't." "Which means it was bullshit? That's all I want to know...I was so close to just getting back into the car and heading to the airport just then." She laid her head against his shoulder, "And I just...I dunno...I'm jumpy after you spending days away from me with your wife. Especially when you talk like she's the mature one, and then she comes at me with that kind of crap. She's trying to cause problems so I'll make a wrong move to seperate me from you and everyone..." "I'm not saying that she's any more or less mature than you, not one bit. I'm just saying you can control the way you act, or rather react." Brandon could see how shaken Amy was and he could also tell, heck even a pro wrestling referee could tell, that that was not all that was bothering her. "If you would have walked away from this simply because of some word games someone is playing then that would have been your own fault. When you called and asked if you could visit for a bit didn't I about fall over my own words telling you to come as soon as possible? If I wanted to spend real time with her I wouldn't have answered the door when you knocked." Again Brandon stroked a stray hair that the wind had whipped around out of her face. "I don't have anything to hide, and why in the hell would I screw us up over something you were integral in helping me get past?" "You're too good at making me feel better." Amy smiled, kissing him quickly before continuing with a thought that seemed to have startled her. "I actually almost forgot why I had to come so soon! I..uhh.....I kinda need a place to live." Brandon blinked. He blinked again. "You want me to go apartment hunting with you in Nova Scotia so you came all the way down here... that doesn't make sense!?" The obviousness of her statement must have sent Frontier in to a bit of shock as he knew nothing of what she implied. "I could look there...but I don't want to be there. I got evicted, and ended up staying with my parents for two fucking days, mainly because I knew you were with Ana and she'd have something to say if I suddenly called up and asked if I could come stay - I'd look overly paranoid or high maintenance." She cringed, trying to find more words and maybe a few that wouldn't catch him off guard. "I kinda need to live here." The raised eyebrow spoke volumes. Brandon's mind swirled at the possibilites. There was much more to consider in this series of events than simply letting Amy have a place to stay. The kids came in to play as did the possibility of trying to get his mother to move back to the States with him. All of this was happening fast. "Um...uh... yeah. Here. Here. Here... with me? Right? Right. So... sure that would be great..." "I know it's a lot to think about. I was gonna try to keep it from you and find somewhere on my own, but after I went crazy from my parents, I spent two nights with Angel and the boys - they kinda convinced me to bring you into it...I guess it's the only fair thing." Amy was searching Brandon's eyes, looking for some indication of what he might really be thinking, but she found nothing that seemed angry or hurt - just confusion. "I can't stay in Nova Scotia...and...well, I know Sean and Jarod won't take to it to well, but..." "They take after their Grandpa, Amy, don't be silly. They'll adjust. It... it just caught me off guard all of a sudden. You know, just feels a bit weird, the very day my soon to be ex-wife takes the rest of her stuff away I've got my girlfriend moving in." Brandon realized how mixed up it all sounded but he was sure Amy understood his apprehension. From her words she was just as worried. "We've got an extra room already set up. All we have to do is send for your things or what?" "Well, apparently I'm a dual citizen because of stuff I had to fill out just so I could go out 'touring' as a wrestler. So...yeah. I just want to make sure you're okay with this and I won't be a burden or a stick in the works between you and the kids. I know them dealing with the whole seperation of their mom and dad probably isn't going over too hot either," She tried to smile, easing a bit of the sudden panic in both of them. "And besides, this way they'll get used to me faster and Ana might learn to keep her mouth shu---err---I'll grow to tolerate your ex-wife." "Ha... nice save there kiddo." With a wink Brandon pulled Amy to him, it was as if he couldn't stand to not be holding her. "Just don't think I'm doing your laundry and whatnot..." "See, there you go giving yourself too much credit again...like I'd actually let you handle my underwear..." Amy smirked. "That's not what I want to handle...ehm...but we can discuss that tonight. For now let's go over and show the kids how it's done in the ring." "Works for me." As they started walking Amy paused a second when a thought hit her, with a hand on Brandon's chest to stop the progress towards the kids momentarily. "Waaaait a second...sleeping arrangements....what's that like?" "Well... like I said earlier, I've already got a spare room. It's got a king sized bed and it's own private bath, right down the hall from the kids. It should be perfect for you." "Do they even know about you and I?" Amy raised an eyebrow but still grinned, nodding toward Sean and Jarod. "And even though having to stay in a seperate room does kinda suck, I'm not gonna complain. Just for you." She stuck her tongue out at him a bit, winking the little rib to his sometimes prude-ish ways. "Do they know what about you and I?" Brandon's head tilted to the side, not sure of what she was implying. "Hmm..." She struggled to word it right, avoiding saying something stupid as she had a tendancy to do. "Like...that you and Ana aren't together anymore, but you and I are like that now? The idea of you making sure I stayed in a seperate room had me confusd." With a little chuckle, Amy finished and was thankful that Brandon's confused look had faded. "Actually... you staying in another room is more for me and you than it is for them." The quizzical look from Brandon's face had taken residence on Amy at this point. "This is still new and all... and while we've moved on to a physical level in our relationship I think us taking the step where we share living space like that should wait, just for a bit. You suprised me with needing a place to stay and I'm all for that, I just think 'moving in' is a big step and if we don't share a room right away everythings cool." Instinctively and for some reason she couldn't figure out, Amy reached up and ruffled the Mighty Hero's hair with a grin. "You're so cute...and I can handle that whole thing. God knows I drive you up the wall enough already." Brandon took Amy's hand and guided her over to the smallish ring. Stepping up to the extra padded section ringside Brandon motioned for Jarod to come over. Reluctantly the impish boy complied, slowing dragging his feet as he walked. "You want Aimz and I to wrestle with you guys?" Jarod shook his head in acceptance, eager to spend some time with his father. "How about we make it you and Aimz against Sean and I?" A raised eyebrow indicated Brandon himself waited for a denial from the little wrestler. Instead, he was shocked when Jarod shook his head and gave a bashful grin Aimz' way, just before turning and stomping back to the middle of the ring with a gesture to call the redhead back over. Amy found herself with Brandon and his kids...a family. The night before, she'd been with Valerian's Garden...family. The next day she'd be greeted by Eli, Ivy, Sean and maybe even Alex: Family. All of these families...somewhere she belonged. [This has been an Aimz/ Brand Frontier collaboration.]
