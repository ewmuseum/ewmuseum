the Untold Story(Part Two)

9 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was the perfect ending to the perfect day. After reviewing it several times... Sean was pleasantly pleased with his promo, how the cameraman edited it, added in his logo, and made his speech clear. But, he was gone now... and, all that remained... was the mess he made. Sean began cleaning... placing couch pillows in their proper place. Throwing items that belonged in the trash... in the trash. After he finished... he walked back to the bedroom... to his comfortable bed... and, the wonderful woman waiting for him on it. Poison Ivy - "How'd it go out there?" Ivy was visibly tired. While Sean was in the living room, with the camera crew, she went and waited in the bedroom, slowly drifting asleep - a result of an extremely busy work schedule, and constant hours on the road which usually left no time for sleep. Sean's entering the bedroom woke her up. Stevens - "Not too bad... the overall product is pretty damned good. And, I was more relaxed than usual. I think it flowed smoothly." Poison Ivy - "Relaxed, huh? ...wonder why..." She giggled, before realizing exactly what she had just said. Sean ripped off his t-shirt and leaped on the bed AND Ivy like a fat kid on free food. That act alone, caused Ivy to burst into tears laughing. Stevens, playfully, began tickling her. She tried to fight him off... to no avail. It only furthered the torture. Finally, Sean stopped... and, sat up beside Ivy on the bed. She slowly stopped laughing herself... and, positioned herself inside his arms. For a moment... they just laid there... in each other's arms... enjoying every moment of it. Stevens - "Seriously, Ivy... you know why everything's been so good lately, don't you?" He didn't even give her a chance to respond. "It's 'cause of somebody special. I've NEVER been this happy before in my life. You think there's a small chance YOU might have something to do with that?" Poison Ivy - "Small chance? Sean... you were a wreck before me. You know it, too." Indeed he was... Triple X smirked at the memory. "...that's why you're smiling. Because you know I'm right." Stevens - "Uh huh... sure. Next thing you'll be telling me is you know how to cook!" Ivy sat up... grabbed the pillow underneath Sean's head, causing it to crash into the headboard. She then proceeded to nail him several times with it. Poison Ivy - "You've never even seen me try! ...dick..." Sean wrapped his arms around Ivy... pulling her closer for a passionate kiss. Stevens - "awwwww... don't worry about it, Ivy... if it helps... I still love you." Poison Ivy - "Yeah. ...it does. Wanna know what helps even more?" Ivy sat up... making one of the cutest facial expressions known to man. Stevens - "Something helps more than me loving you?" Poison Ivy - "...the fact that no matter what it tastes like... there's one person on this earth that I know will eat it." Ivy smiled a confident smile. Stevens - "Yeah... that's true. ...Eli will eat just about anything! Ivy didn't budge. Poison Ivy - "I guess that makes two people then..." Before Sean could reply... Ivy stuck him with an elbow right to the rib area. Stevens - "Seriously, though, Ivy... does it get any better than this?" Ivy snuggled up inside his arms. "I mean, you above all should know, I'm alot of things... and, sentimental isn't particularily one of them. But, I couldn't picture myself with anyone else but you. And, honestly Ivy... I wanna spend the rest of my life with you. And, if I was to ever get married... I'd wanna marry you." No response. Stevens waited a moment, before looking down. He didn't want to make it appear as if he were trying to slip her a signal, or force something she didn't want down her throat... he was simply speaking from his heart... and, now... nearly thirty seconds later... he glanced down at her... to see her reaction. She was sleep. Sean sighed a sigh of relief. Because, as emotional as he was... the last thing he needed was the woman he's in love with telling him how crazy he is. Sean tightened his grip... holding Ivy's warm body even closer to his. It's amazing, he thought. How far the two of them had come in the short time they've been together. It was love in the truest sense. As Ivy slept in his arms... Sean smiled... this was heaven on earth. And, as long as he had breath in his lungs... he'd let nothing screw up what he and Ivy have. Suddenly, a pain shot up Sean's side. The letter. The 'priority mail' package sent to his apartment earlier in the day. How could I forget, he thought. He remembered exactly where he left it, too. On top of the refrigerater... out of sight... so Ivy wouldn't see it and get curious. He had to get it... but... if he moved... he'd certainly wake Ivy out of her sleep... and, she'd get suspicious. Sean just layed there... staring up at the ceiling. His beautiful, wonderful girlfriend in his arms. Hoping. ...hoping that he'd be able to dispose of the package sent to his house, without her knowing... because if she saw what he thought was in there... this feeling... this feeling of peace... of joy... would probably end. And, that's something he'll never allow to happen. ****** 6am Eastern Time Sean woke up on instinct and fear alone. Gently, he removed Ivy's arms from around his waist allowing her to sleep a bit more. She knew he had another promo shoot to make, so he was sure she wouldn't worry too much about him if she woke up and he wasn't there. But, first... business... ... before taking a shower... finding his outfit... or anything... Sean went into the kitchen, looked on top of the refridgerater, as a funny feeling came over his body. ...it was relief... the package was still there. Stevens didn't even open it... he didn't WANT to open it. Read it. Or even see it for that matter... he just tossed it into the trash can, not even turning back. Thirty minutes later. He was fresh from his shower... in his clothes... ready to go. The sound of his apartment door closing woke Ivy from her sleep. But, she knew it was Sean... and, knew he had business to attend to... so she didn't panic. She simply closed her eyes again... smiling... as if she had just woke up from a wonderful dream. She probably did. ------ 8:15am Eastern Time. XWL Studios. "I'm not gonna insult any of my fans... you don't deserve it. You know just as much about this business as I do... and, could probably teach me a thing, or two. That's not why I'm here. I'm here to ask for your forgiveness... for being lazy. I didn't put in half the work needed to successfully survive in the fWo. And, it took me being humbled before I realized it. "I've often thought about calling it quits... here atleast. I thought that maybe, just maybe, I had found a place where I couldn't be the main man. And, to be completely honest... that kinda freaked me out. But, then I looked at all the letters... all the emails... all the messages left on my machine from friends and family members." Sean smirked as his last few words escaped his mouth. Running his hands through his - now, shoulder length - golden locks, he wiped all the watery substance from his hair, now on his hands, on his 100% cotton: "Psycho Bitch" t- shirt, with Ivy's picture on the back, and glanced down for a moment... collecting his thoughts. Before looking back up at the screen. And, YOU... the viewer. "And, contrary to what you believe about this business... what you know... and, what you don't know... I... am... the best. I still believe it. I still think I can prove it. And, I will. I'll prove it by putting on heart stopping matches, that give you fans NO CHOICE but to get your asses off of your seats and cheer. I'll do things, never seen OR done in this sport. "This organization is a challenge to me. For the past three years, since I've... 'made it'... I haven't had too many, and got a little complacent. But, I know it can be done. Look at Eli Flair... one of my best friends in the business. He's doing it. Look at his manager, Ivy... she's doing it. Why can't I? "It's gonna take dedication. It's gonna take time. But, I'm gonna do it, too. That... or I'll die trying..." fade- to- black ****** 7am Eastern Time. Sean's apartment. It felt good to get a whole night's rest... and, as Ivy dried herself off in Sean's bathroom... she began to stare at herself in the mirror. She was changing... growing... her look was even a bit more mature. And, she knew the reason, too. Happiness has that effect on you. Her parents would be proud. Ivy wasn't even clothed, instead, she was covered by a white towel... when an overwhelming urge for some Orange Juice overcame her entire body. She tightened the towel, and went into the kitchen. Opening the refridgerater... she noticed the carton of "Florida Orange Juice" on the top shelf, grabbed it and immediately pulled herself some. It was good, she thought as she reached over to throw the paper cup into the trash can... Strange, she thought. The package sent to Sean yesterday was in the container... and, it wasn't even opened. Maybe it was placed there by accident? Ivy asked herself. She reached in and grabbed it out reading the name on top of the return address. Poison Ivy - "Linda Johns..." The name still didn't ring a bell. But, she opened it anyway.
