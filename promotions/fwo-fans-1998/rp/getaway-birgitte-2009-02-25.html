Getaway - Birgitte

25 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brawn started towards the night-cloaked city with a spirit of adventure. He had just stumbled from the disco, a little buzzed, but fine. His shopping bags gave him away as a tourist, but a genial coat checker stowed them away as he spent the remainder of the night absorbing the foreign environs. If there was one thing Brawn would miss after the Paris getaway, it was the women. American women were ambiguous and hard. The Parisians, contrary to popular belief, were surprisingly warm. While Brawn's conversations revealed a derelict's grasp of culture, they were friendly and happy to oblige his company. Without a map or compass, Brawn looked down the antiquated streets, dimly lit by street lamps. He immediately regretted turning down his acquaintances' offers to "continue the evening" -- after all, he had no idea where he was going -- but the dismissals somehow felt right. He was a fortress of solitude more than happy to enjoy the pleasure of his own company. He walked left down the old sidewalk. A cat ran in front of his feet as compact cars buzzed in the distance. His eyes scanned the buildings quickly, generating a flurry of snapshots in his mind for future recollection. Then he heard the scream. He dropped his bags immediately and sprinted towards the source. He arrived in a alley-way where two silhouetted figures were sparring. "Hey!" Brawn barked. The petrified man sprinted in his direction, modifying the trajectory as to avoid Brawn completely. The Wrought Iron Kid charged and drove him into a stone wall. Something in the man's body popped, and his face reflected it. Brawn looked down the canal and saw a shock of long blonde hair in the light. A woman. Something visceral overcame Brawn. The man was half his size, his mouth spitting bullets in his foreign tongue. Brawn gritted and brought a fist to his jaw, producing blood and knocking him against the wall once more. "Are you okay?!" Brawn asked in a firm and measured voice, like an officer at the scene of a crime. The woman nodded. Rapid footfalls pattered away behind him. "Come into the light," he said as he grabbed her by the arm and pulled her towards him. She was smiling. "What's so funny? That man looked like he was attacking you." "I asked him to," she said in a low voice. The light pressed harshly into her face, but Brawn saw her cheeks becoming flushed. "I don't know what you call it..." "Like... roleplaying?" Brawn asked, scratching the back of his neck. "Exactement!" She replied, standing on her tip toes and falling on her heels. He pulled her flowing hair behind an ear. "Do you... do you roleplay?" "All the time." "Where are you from?" she asked. "Iowa." "I won't hold that against you," she said, walking towards a motorized scooter nearby. "I'm Birgitte. Would you like to continue our conversation somewhere less... cold?" she asked while pushing a helmet in his arm. He silently nodded and began strapping it on. He reached for the handlebars. "Ch-- Ch-- Ch--" she clicked, her thumb popping out from her fist. "You sit in back." Brawn looked around and slowly climbed on, the entire scooter slumping under the weight. She looked back at his hands which were sitting on his knees. "Where are your gloves?" she asked. Brawn shrugged and she shook her head, clicking her tongue once more. "If you want to warm them, you can put them in my pants." "Excuse ..me?!" Brawn said, shrill. She grabbed his hands and guided them inside the fold of her waistband. The bones in his fingers began thawing instantly. "There! Much better!" she said, kicking the ignition to life and ferrying them into the pulsing city.
