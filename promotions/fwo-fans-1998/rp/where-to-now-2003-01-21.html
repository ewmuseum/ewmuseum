Where to now?

21 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tick Tock... Tick Tock... the clock kept moving. "Things are going slowly. We're trying to get it moving, but you know how it goes." The voice on the other end said. Tick Tock... Tick Tock. The clock kept moving. "Yes, I understand. I be there when you ready." The man called Deacon responded. Tick Tock... Tick Tock. The clock kept moving. "I know you will be." Tick Tock... Dialtone and Deacon's thoughts were left. He ran his fingers through his hair, stopping to rub his brow. This was the CSWA. They moved, always moved, but sometimes slowly, and given his limitations, that speed was taking time off the clock. Tick Tock... Tick Tock. The clock made his body ache, each tick causing a small twinge in his shoulder and causing his mind to wander. "You should come to the fWo, D." Ivy had told him. "The IFW is for you, man." They had said. Deacon had watched the fWo. He knew of their image problems and their backstage squabbles, but they had one thing going for them. Tick Tock... Tick Tock. The clock kept moving. Time. With their pace, he could put the message out there and keep it going. Tick Tock... Tick Tock. At least with what time he had left. He picked up the phone, dialed Ivy's number, and waited for it to ring. Tick Tock... Tick Tock. "Hello," Ivy Answered. "You t'ink the fWo could still use me?" "Hmmm... lemme see." Tick Tock... Tick Tock. "Of course, you big dope." She said, the smile evident even over the phone. "I'll call them in the morning and set things up. Just you or is Chris coming also?" "His book tour is ending, so we'll see. If I show with him, that probably be bigger for fans." Deacon said. "I would agree." "I'll see what I can do." "Later, D. And Deac, I'm glad to have ya on board." She said. "But I not on board yet." "You will be, trust me, you will be." With that, she hung the phone up and Deacon was left with a dialtone and... Tick Tock... Tick Tock. The clock kept moving forward and if things worked well, he would be moving with it.
