Flashpoint

20 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All my life I've been searching for something Something never comes never leads to nothin' Nothing satisfies but I'm getting close... She opened the door. Freedom. Sweet, embracing...coldness and rain. "Ah, fuck." Well, reasonably speaking, it was five days before Christmas. Southern California or no SoCal, Caroline Jessop should've expected this what with the storm warning on the news the past couple of days. But she was done with finals, and on her way back home. Caroline had left reason behind a long time ago. Underneath the Krispy Kreme eave, she lit up a cigarette and looked around at the middle of campus, seeing fellow undergraduates scramble madly to get away from the rain on the final day of campus. She peered beneath the red-dyed hair that covered her eyes and realized despite the fact she was doing well just how truly little she cared about it or for it; how she just wanted it done and over with. She could feel it permeate her body. She had to say something before she went back to Chicago for the holiday later. She had to tell her roomate how she really felt. The feelings of surety diminished and the unease crept in, like the beginning elements of a stomachache. She fished out an umbrella from her bag and headed back towards the dorms, away from everyone else going out to the front. She felt a little dizzy. She felt really good. She felt unsure, as if all the tightropes she were walking on in these final moments before she hoped and prayed she'd have the courage to speak the truth. It wasn't really reasonable of her. But reason had left a long time ago. And THIS is what remained.
