Indiana Speck and the Temple of Fire: An Anti-Climatic Finale

30 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All good things have to come to an end some time. Of course, all bad things have to come to an end, too, and this was just one of those bad things that needed to be mercy killed as horribly and as painfully as possible with the use of human hands. Take for example this particular tale of woe and Fire Temple-y goodness. It simply needed to be ended, if only because there were better things to do than to tell a tale about a tag team slash stable that really wasn't around any more. Now, truth to be told, the Codemaster and his allies had already fought Volvagia. And it was an epic battle. Link depleted his arrow reserves in trying to shoot down Volvagia. Mike Heftel tried to fly at him, fell off of the ledge, landed in the lava, and somehow managed to get propelled back onto the ledge with his ass on fire, like Mario in Mario 64. Ken War screamed "hardkorr!!11" at it until Volvagia got annoyed with it and chomped off his upper torso like Liu Kang's dragon fatality in Mortal Kombat 2. When Volvagia decided to pop out of one of the six holes he came out of, though, the Codemaster played Whack-A-Dragon with his skull using the Megaton Hammer. Then he cried. And whined. And Link stabbed him in the face many times until he died. With Volvagia soundly defeated at the hands of the Codebreakers and Link, it was time to leave. Because, you know, you can't get any more anti-climatic than this, can you? Well, fasten your seatbelts, Grandma Edna, because now we gotta hit some dialogue! Whoo! "So, uh... you gonna be needin' this?" the Codemaster asked, holding up the Megaton Hammer. "Are you kidding? It's too heavy. I won't be able to squash things as horribly with that thing as I would with just the sword!" Link complained. "But you might run into some rusted switches!" "I've got WD-40." Link said, holding up the afermentioned can of WD-40. "Well, dang. Where'd a elven brother like you pick that up?" the Codemaster asked, glaring at the can of WD-40. "eBay." Link casually responded. "Lemme guess... Heftel hook you up?" "Damn straight, he did." Link said. "Well, here's hoping he doesn't turn into a Dude Love wannabe." the Codemaster said. "And here's hoping you actually win your matches when you inevitably return to the ring." Link said. "Damn straight." And thus, this tale reaches its anti-climatic end. Of course, we all know this was just another twisted dream that the Codemaster had from a combination of too much alcohol and Ocarina of Time, but hell, let's not go into detail. That'd bring back the hangover.
