Writings On The Wall: I Am A Chameleon

24 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am a chamelon. Ashamed of my changing colors. Opportunism builds as my values fade. This teeter-totter trip is making me sick. Its tough to make a decision when you feel someone else is making it for you. To get more in life, you have to leave things behind. I never understood that. Both face and heel lie on opposite sides of the spectrum. I lived the latter, but abandoned it in haste. I have changed, but for whose benefit? Alcohol effect diminished, my sober mind loked for something new to hold onto. I found it in pen and paintbrush, paper and canvas. Canvasing my internal vault, locked away the thoughts I never finished and the words I wanted to say. Today, I can say them, portray them, and have no regrets. I'm happy to have my pure heart on my sleeve. All the while questioning why God didn't put it there in the first place. Yin and Yan are finally working together, hoping together, and in the end striving for something better. Our bodies were molded into compatible shapes, and we've learned to fit them together. I look up from this paper and see four white walls waiting to be coated. I realize that this sheet will be lonely as it clings by the pierce of a tack. But not for long, as it will find written and drawn friends whenever I get back... home.
