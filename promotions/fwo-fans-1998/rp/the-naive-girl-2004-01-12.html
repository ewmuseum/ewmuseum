The Naive Girl

12 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brandy had a few too many drinks. Beer, liquid cocaine, vodka, and tequila did her in good tonight. She had just turned 19 years old and was having the time of her life with a wrestling superstar, Crusader. It was 1AM and the new couple staggered out of the club. Neither of them could hear a thing after their ears had been plugged by the pounding beats in the club. He had shown her the time of her life. They were special guests and spent their time in the VIP room drinking, kissing, and groping. Completely intoxicated, Crusader reached for his keys in his leather jacket and unlocked the door to his BMW. The car made a sharp beeping sound. Before Brandy reached for the door, Crusader walked in front of the door and opened it for her. Quite the gentleman he seemed. He got in the driver's seat and struggled to find the right key. After he had solved that problem, there was also the problem of getting it into the ignition. He spent a good two minutes trying to figure it out before he finally started the car up. Off they went. Brandy screamed in joy as Crusader screeched out of the parking lot and tore the main road at 80mph. A few cars had honked at him but traffic was mostly empty. Neither of them wore seatbelts. Brandy kept weaving her head back and forth and let it fall on Crusader's crotch. They sped past her apartment, and despite her drunken state, Brandy had noticed and sat up. "Hey," she said, "you passed my place you goof." Crusader looked at her over his blue tinted night sunglasses and smiled. "I guess I must be pretty smashed, huh?" He made a sharp right turn, smacking the tail light of a parked car in the process. Brandy screamed in fear and then started to laugh as they continued down the street. Crusader pulled into the Holiday Inn and parked. He got out of the car, walked around, and opened the passenger door for Brandy. "What's going on?" she said. "I'm cordially inviting you to my hotel suite. I've got a twelve-pack of Coronas and a bottle of champagne waiting for us up there. So come on out." He grabbed her by the arm and helped Brandy out. She stumbled forward into his chest and then leaned back against the car. "Does this mean you want to have sex with me?" Crusader nodded. Brandy giggled. "But I hardly know you. I can't have sex with a guy I just met." Crusader cracked his knuckles. It was time to use force. No, not the physical force most would come to expect of a man. Crusader had a different kind of force that could control any woman: fame. "I just showed you the time of your life," he said. "I took you to VIP. Bought you some drinks. Gave you a little tongue. Now I just want to finish tonight the way I want to. If you don't want to, that's cool with me. I'll leave you here and find someone else. But if you give daddy a bit of sugar, I'll give you front row tickets to the show tomorrow. Even better, I'll make sure you get on TV." Her eyes widened. It worked every time. "Me?" she said. "On TV? Oh my god, are you serious?" Crusader nodded. She began to think of the possibilities. It was something she could brag to all of her friends about. How often would a famous man want to have sex with her? Instead of her having to call people to go out on Friday and Saturday nights, they would be calling her. It sounded very good to her. "Umm, I don't know, maybe, I think, it's just that--" He interrupted her with a kiss and made the decision for her. She wrapped her legs around him and he carried her all the way to his hotel suite, hands on the firm ass. This was Crusader, at his best. A different girl, a different night - but it was all the same to him.
