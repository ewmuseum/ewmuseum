A Hero of Justice (2)

7 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"AAAARRRRRRGGGGHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!" Waylon sat up screaming in pain, "OH GOD! OH GOD!" He clutched his chest in complete agony, falling then on his back once again and rolling back in forth. He could feel with every breath his chest getting larger and larger, expanding. "OH GOD!" He repeated again as he rolled off of the bed and hit the floor hard, immediately he tried to get up and placed his right hand on the bed and attempted to push himself up. As he tried to push his arm started to increase in size and he screamed out once again, he looked over and watched as his arm grew bigger and bigger, similar to what he used to watch Popeye do as a kid. "This can't be happening, this can't be happen-AARRRRGHHGGH!!!!!!" He let another scream go as his left arm now began to transform and to get bigger and bigger. "No..." He said in an exhausted whisper as he threw his body forward and fell on to his still expanding chest, now with his large arms he gripped the ground in front of him and began to pull his body across the floor. THUMP! THUMP! THUMP! "Shuddup in there yaw wacko!" The voice came from the other room. "I have to get help," Waylon grimaced as he pulled himself. Waylon then felt a pinch in his leg and looked back his right leg, he could see the muscles in his leg begin to twitch and almost as though something had exploded inside his body his leg started to expand as well. Sweat poured down his face as he switched into a fetal position trying to hold his body together as all parts of it were jerking and expanding, his left leg now repeated the process of his right leg. He could only think of one thing, he had to get up, he had to get help. Waylon now put his larger hand back on the bed beside him and easily lifted his body off of the ground. He moved slowly at first as his body was still sore, he moved now toward the mirror in the left hand corner of the room. As he approached it he could only see the shadow of what was a large man, looking to his left he saw the light switch and flicked it on. What he saw next would forever change him, as he was looking at his head on the body of a body builder. His chest was complete with a six pack and no such thing as fat. His arms were twice the size of what his torso used to be, and his legs were even bigger. But his head was still small. The pain had pretty much disappeared by now and he touched his face, "Damn it...what am I going to do now?" He asked himself. Only to be interrupted by his nose beginning to expand, next was his cheek bones, and then on to his forehead, Waylon stood and watched his face shift and change and broaden. And at that, he fainted and fell hard to the floor.
