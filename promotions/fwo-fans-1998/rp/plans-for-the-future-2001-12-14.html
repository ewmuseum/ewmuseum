Plans for the Future

14 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Okay, so I'm booked for Twilight?" Wretch and Scott are in the WWF's Tough Enough hummer, driving towards, presumably towards Salt Lake City, home of assorted people, mainly those who are citizens of Utah. Some people in Utah have names like Jeremy, or Brigham, or Karl... just a small fact for you guys. "Yeah... Crisp and Flair. Not Ric though... Eli." Wretch nodded. He wouldn't want to wrestle Ric Flair... he was old, and talked to walls. And Wretch was pretty sure there were no Ric's in Utah, but not positive. There was a Ric in fWo though, so that probably meant most Ric's were either really mean, or crazy... except for the "The Model" Martel, he was just a charming guy. Wretch shook his head. "Okay... where does Eli Flair stand in all of this?" Scott shrugged. "You interfered in his match... apparently that's not cool." Wretch nodded. He wished he knew that before, now Eli was angry at him too. On second thought, Wretch wished he knew a lot of things before he did stuff. Like gas was flammable, or what flammable actually meant. "Is Eli not funny too?" Scott shook his head. "No, Eli's definitely not funny. He sticks to the more serious approach of wrestling, like training... and learning moves. He listens to what people say, too." Wretch nodded. "He'll definitely go far in this business." Scott smiled. "Yeah, I think so too." Wretch nodded again... he wished he could listen to what people say, but usually he just kinds of drifts away into different, more interesting thoughts... Hey! Like now! Wretch began to think about that time awhile ago, when Donaven Winters was yelling at him for playing his N64 while he was wrestling. Wretch was really sorry, but Winters kept talking... and after awhile, he started sounding like Fabolous. And then Wretch pictured him singing "Holla Back Youngin'" and man, was it downhill from there... That punch really hurt. "Is there anything about Eli Flair we can make fun of?" Scott thought. "His name?" Wretch scratched his chin, ignoring the road. "Eli... beli, meli, feeli, geeli... nope." Scott thought as well, as the car slowly turned into an appropriately placed cornfield. "Flair... hair, fair, nair, care, bear... nothing there either." Wretch frowned. There had to be something... his manager's glasses were funny, but certainly not nothing he could base anything off of. "I guess we could just call him Pissypants... that could get a few laughs." Scott nodded in agreement, and then noticed the cornfield. "Hey, Wretch... did you notice this?" Wretch looked up. "Well, hey... this is a cornfield." Scott nodded. "Yeah, just wanted to make sure."
