Communication Breakdown - an 04/15 reAction exclusive

21 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Sup, Red?” Her fleeting moment of peace and quiet shattered, Mary-Lynn Mayweather looked up from her paperwork to the beaming smile of Karina Wolfenden. Not by accident, the K-Wolf stood just an intrusive foot or so from the Tiny Attorney. “Hi Rainbow.” Mayweather said with a sigh. “What can I do ya’s for?” “Need you to scribble down a message to Jack for me,” Kari ordered with just enough mischief in her voice to prevent her sounding like a domineering bitch. “Do I look like his secretary?” the harassed Mary-Lynn sighed. The vivid emerald eyes of the K-Wolf drew Mayweather’s down to the scribbled notes on her overloaded clipboard, ‘if the cap fits’ not needing to be spoken. Mary tossed a headset off her head and onto her desk, thinking the same thing. Just to be sure. "I'd rather you not answer that..." “I know he’s a busy man,” the K-Wolf conceded. “But, yeah, me and the Sheff have a lil’ non-title win over him and Mouthy Mask IV that we’re wanting to cash into a full-on, shiny title shot… ASAP. So if you could scribble that down and wave it in front of him tonight, that’d be chipper.” Sheff, who needed no further introduction, as his tattooed and taped arms said quite enough, stood just outside the door. He leaned against the wall, and stared at the opposite wall. Waiting. He and K had agreed, that she'd probably be the better of the two Vox Nihili fighters to make this a quick and easy visit. The visit was proving to be neither of these two things. Across The Pulp Hero's line of sight passed David Noble, and before the shorter of the two men could enter the room were Mayweather, Alias put out his arm and kept him out of the room as if acting as a bouncer to the twenty-two year old. Noble's face twisted in surprise but Alias just shrugged. "Karina wanted ta talk to Mary-Lynn about getting a shot against Flyerana, Rookie. I think it's best that the two of them just chat it out." Alias motioned his head to the opposite wall, and Noble shrugged in reply and walked to a space across the door from the Pulp Hero. "And how do you think that's going?" replied Noble, not quite sure what to say to this much... meaner looking individual. I mean, he even look rougher then Dusk, thought Noble. Alias just stared at him, looked into the room for a moment and then stared back at Noble. The Rookie could only crack a grin. Already having a good 6 inches over Mayfly, despite the perilously high scarlet heels she was wearing, Karina’s body language was operating an unashamed campaign of dominance. Mary-Lynn, however, was about to make up every inch. “Maybe killjoy dropped you on that ever-increasing, multi-coloured head of yours a few too many times,” Mayweather emphasised with a drop of her clipboard, “but David and I beat you and Christopher a few weeks ago. So, using your obviously impeccable logic, wouldn't... that make us? The number one contenders.” “I hardly think killjoy using Noble’s head and the canvas to communicate ‘blah blah something cryptic blah blah’ to me and Sheff in Morse code really constitutes a win,” Wolfenden scoffed. “Well, not to us.“ Alias and Noble continued to stare at each other, and David looked to be more visibly affected by the tension compared to the stone-faced Pulp Hero. Noble had to say something, so looking at Alias he blurted out, "Sooo... that's a pretty cool scar on your neck. Uh, how'd you get it?" For a moment Alias didn't say a word, but being finished with having his fun with this high-flying blue-chipper he finally grinned warmly and replied, "Well..." “Then how about you lose the whole ‘dominant female’ thing and go get Christopher,“ suggested the Tiny Attorney, gravity of her words belying her stature. “Then you can tell him that David and I will see you both in the ring tonight. Then, if Jack and Matthew want, they can give the winner a shot at the gold.” “Fine,” the K-Wolf casually shrugged. “Sheff wanted another warm-up match before we took our shot anyway.” “Might want to get used to those,” Mary-Lynn huffed as she snaked past Karina. “Because if David and I win those belts, your name is going to be stuck just under MegaJob’s on the title shot ladder for as long as we hold them.” Now deep in conversation about the intricacies of the business, and really only touching on the stories of the road, Alias decided to break from the professionalism for a moment. He just had to know something. "So, you hittin' that?", Alias motioned back, obviously implying Mary-Lynn. The question caught Noble off guard, but he straightened up his back, kinda proud. He smiled, shaking his head from side to side. “If only. You?" David also motioned back into the room, this time implying K-Wolf. The Pulp Hero shifted his head, a surprising softness to his face at that moment contradicting what was his usual presence. Noble raised his hand for a high five, and Alias was about to slap some skin when Mary-Lynn stormed out the room and grabbed Noble by the arm. Not even saying a word to Alias, they walked off down the hall. Noble turned around for a moment, mouthing I knew it, to which Alias couldn't help but chuckle in response. K-Wolf was now at the doorway beside him, and the Pulp Hero's attention snapped to her. K-Wolf, though still holding onto a casual air, looked more then a little annoyed. Seeing all of this, Sheff knew that the road to the World Tag Team Championships had not reached it's end. Not yet.
