Song from the Heavens, Humming from Hell - 9 - Part One

5 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The pain seemed to shoot all the way down his back as blurred images of life danced for Angel's eyes. Angel's focused came slowly back as he looked over to see a door shut to a black car and the sound of the engine roar. Angel seemed to get to his feet and tried hard to see the car clearly. He still had his gun. The car began to drive away as Angel let lose a single bullet that tore through the front passenger's side tire. 5 shots left. The car came to a halt as the passenger side door opened and out stepped Spencer Sullivan, who was still bleeding from his injuries. Angel seemed to almost want to whimper as he screamed at Sullivan, "Why can't you just die!" *BLAM* Bullet number five was sent into Spencer's chest and this time he slumped back into the car. Angel now screamed again, "Get out of the car! Get of the car Steve! GET THE FUCK OUT of the car!" Douglas' head appeared from the driver's side and he wore a wile smile, "Angel ," Steve said slowly, "I know you want this to be the end, but it's not over. Your going to let us go." Angel pointed the gun at Steve's head, "It's over." Douglas pulled a man up in front of him, who shook with fear, Steve smiled and looked at Angel from just behind the man, "No you see, we have him, and you're the good guy... which means you gotta let us go. Or I'll slit his FUCKING throat!" The man let out a small scream as Douglas held him close to his body and walked around to the passenger's side. He moved close to the almost unconscious Spencer Sullivan and grabbed Sullivan by the back of his neck and lifted him up, whispering, "C'mon," into Spencer's ear. Douglas began to walk away from Angel who still pointed the gun at him, Spencer slowly moved mainly holding onto the hostage that was in front of Douglas. Angel held the gun pointed at Steve's head which was blocked by the hostage's head. Thinking only one thing, ,if I let him go, the hostage is dead anyway. Angel fired. Four shots left. This time the bullet whipped through the hostage's head and grazed Douglas' face. Douglas dropped the body and Angel let off another shot with hit Douglas in the leg. 3 shots left. Douglas let lose a mean howl as he and Spencer tore down the remaining five feet of the alley and collapsed on car. The car came to an immediate halt and a horn of a pissed of driver followed. Douglas moved quickly around the car and got in the passenger side door, Spencer managed to crawl into the backseat and Douglas looked over at the driver, "Drive or die." A bullet hit the front end of the car. 2 shots left. The car took off in a mean speed and Angel ran to the road watching it go. Angel quickly looked around and then dropped the gun. He raced across the road and was down the opposite alleyway as a police car showed up to the scene. Hysteria slapped at the crowd, and the crime scene was frantic and sloppy.
