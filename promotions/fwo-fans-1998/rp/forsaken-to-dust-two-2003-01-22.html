Forsaken - To Dust (Two)

22 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ana pulled the phone from her ear looking at the timer display. 03:14:23 How could they have talked for over three hours and still be where they were when they began? It seemed that no matter how much they discussed their problems nothing changed. She quickly switched sides and cupped the phone between her shoulder and cheek as she continued to fold clothes. Brandon paused for a second as he realized Ana had stopped listening. A heavy sigh escaped him. He too was exacerbated with the fact that they were at an impasse. Too much had built up over the course of the last seven years. It seemed like since their engagement things had never been the same. Sean and Jarod were the two bright spots in their lives, while nothing else seemed to stand out. They did try hard. They stuck by each other through thick and thin. Through the kidnappings, the house burning down, the infidelity. You would think that if they could survive those trials their relationship would be solidified with the knowledge of how well they face adversity together. Instead each one of those incidents and so many more served to weaken what should have been strong. Two separate people fighting side by side against a common cause, instead of a team emerging victorious at the end of a great battle. They did share common goals but the way they were achieved was always different. Ana nor Brandon had ever let themselves merge in to one. The situation with Vic Creid had always been the main flaw in the relationship for Brandon. The hatred for that man ran deep. Some would look at the events that led up to Creid and Ana being together and question why Brandon stayed with Ana but kept the hatred for Creid burning so heavily. No one said hatred was always tempered with rationale. No matter how close they became Brandon could not get the image of those two together. It was always the heavy balance on the other end of the scales that no matter how much he put on the counterweight wouldn't balance out. For Ana it was the constant danger she felt her family lived under. From the connections to the Irish Army that Brandon's father had to the organized crime roots the Frontier Foundation grew out of. There always seemed to be an underlying current of emergency no matter where they were. Add on to that the celebrity that came with being married to Brand Frontier and the mix was simply volatile. The kidnapping happened because of the amount of wealth that came from the business and from different marketing strategies, taking advantage of Frontier's name power.
