Patterns (Part 1): "Liberate"

3 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Patterns Part 1: Liberate Thursday October 31st, 2002 12:34pm Home (New Minas, NS) It didn't seem like the kind of atmosphere you'd normally find a relatively lively, talkative twenty year old girl sheltered within. The absence of light would have been overwhelming and any occupant would have gone un-noticed, had it not been for the little glimmer of a voice that seemed to linger in the air, a faint hum to a rather speedy song that soon bursted with a muffled, chittering snare drum being the only thing audible outside the seal of headphones to a steel-laced ear. The darkness didn't last that long, though. The young lady soon gave in to the need for some sort of illumination and stepped toward what she assumed was the door she'd entered through earlier, where the switched she'd previously flicked off promised to be. Staying upright under her own power proved to be a challenge, however. Placing one foot in front of the other in a slow progression with hopes of steadying herself, she finally managed to take a few lone steps before shooting an arm out to prop her 150-something pound form, which just didn't want to obey her that evening, against the wall...thankfully right beside the door, as she discovered while pawing for the light switch. When it finally decided to flick up, supposedly after having taken a mind of it's own underneath shakey fingers, the girl's other hand immediately flew to cover her eyes. Staggering back, the couch had gotten in the way and the now revealed redhead soon found herself clinging to conciousness atop it. Her appearance was rather shaggy, and probably wouldn't have been any better in the day time or even sober. She just sort of clung there, half-smothered against an arm of the torn and tattered sofa in a pair of equally distressed camouflage cargo pants and a little black t-shirt that didn't seem to fit properly, either because her pants were just barely clining to her hips, or because it was cut a little too high but either way, it didn't do a thing to cover the outline of flames on her forearms, the nautical stars on the backs of her hands or the Metallica star logo permenantly put to her flesh by ink. The blatant display was the sort that most anybody would have looked away in disgust from but, judging by the fact that this young woman would put those sort of images on herself forever and show them proudly without a single attempt to conform to someone's personal taste by hiding them, she wanted the whole world to know that she honestly didn't give a damn. Her overall appearance; the tattoos, the jungle of pink-tinged hair and her rather filthy clothing would have caused a person to assume her face would be equally marred, but that wasn't the case. When she lifted her head and rolled onto one side groggily, a naturally near-gorgeous face, untouched by makeup or creams normally reached for by girls her age to 'perfect' their skin, was brought into light. Rather high cheekbones were perfectly shaped by tight muscles and skin, while un-plucked eyebrows remained ideally shaped and symetrically complimented by a strong but fitting jaw. She was pretty, but apparently trying hard not to be, with little pieces of steel jutting out from her right eyebrow, one centered between her lower lip and what seemed like an endless array, some stretched alarmingly large, clanking on the rims of her ears. When consciousness was momentarily steadied, the discman found it's way out of the abyss of her thigh pocket and onto to the floor, soon followed by the lithe, 5'9" frame of Amy Campbell... "Hmph!" She let out a groan when her shoulder slammed against the dense gray carpet, soon followed by a reluctant sigh as her hands found the floor and she used her last ounce of energy to hop back onto unsteady feet, heels dug into the ground. When she turned to see the near-full bottle still sitting there under an unlit lamp on the end table, the temptation to take another sip or two almost overcame her, but it had been almost half an hour since her last drink... Too warm by now, She thought. Then again; Cold or warm, overproof rum still tasted like shit. By that time, she wasn't drunk...just comfortably numb and not willing to sacrifice the contents of her stomach to entirely escape her thoughts, for once. Amy thought getting out of her head for a little while would have been pleasant, though. Life was changing games on her all over again: This time around her job with Old School Wrestling was on the rocks, had nearly every wrestling fan on earth, not to mention her friends and family, incredibly disappointed in her and she was letting herself get beaten by a bottle all over again. It surprised her that she'd noticed the fallout this time, but finding herself waking up every single morning feeling like ten thousand needles paralyzed her had become a sore and cursed reminder, which is why she had to catch herself: The easy way out found Aimz screwed over every single time and alcohol was a way of going down that simple path, though it may have seemed like a relief. That's why she overcame the first step in the more difficult path, darting her shaking fingers and two-tone eyes away from the bottle, instead using them to maneuver the door open as she wandered out of the room, putting her hand above her eyes yet again, this time sheilding them from the even brighter light of the hallway. She ended up wandering into the wide-open space of what appeared to be the main section to her loft appartment, complete with brick walls and barely any furniture, but plenty of posters taped up ranging from Kurt Cobain to considerably more outlandish looking musical figureheads. She still felt at home in the dreary atmosphere, and fit in with many of the posters herself: brooding, tired and uncaring in appearance but that soon all faded off when she heard familliar footfalls in the stairway, her face brightening into a grin as a tall, dark-haired man stepped through the door. Standing before Amy then was one of the few constants, one of the rare guiding lights in her life. He was just over six-foot-one, unusually muscular for his bone structure, her type of rather dark, almost 'gothic' looking guy...it was the one boyfriend she'd ever come to fully trust and his name was Darcy Markson, one she found herself thanking God for every day. "Hey ba---whoa, you look like you got hit by a train...." "I wish I had been." She blurted the only thing that instantly came to mind, following it up by going to lean against him, but instead finding herself face-first into the wall just next to Darcy's broad shoulders, which looked to be a rather difficult target to miss. "...gah....aim's off..." "I'll say you're off..." He said, pulling Amy gently away from the wall and steadying her while casting a questioning gaze at the same time. The redhead looked up, her head cocked to the side and simply muttered, "Meh, life bites...who needs balance." "You do, if you expect to even travel down the hallway in one piece....I think the bartender is gonna have to cut you off for the night." "Bah!" She caught herself slurring a little, but only very slightly, far from being 'drunk' drunk. "The fridge will never close, or run out so long as I have keys and money!" If the giggle and 'shit-faced grin' were any indicator, the fridge hadn't closed in quite a while, from what Darcy could observe. "Besides, cutting me off would only prove to get me angrier with the world...and the world doesn't need that." "Amy, I don't know what's got you so blue, but booze is no solution. You've had this discussion with me, your parents, your brothers, even your sisters...you never win, so just give up the bottles, hmm?" Darcy found himself captured in a pleading eyes, but that quickly went cold when Amy spoke again, this time with a tone just as alcohol-stained, depressed and whiney as before. "You say it like we're on Barney. I just wanted a little rest, that's all." His forehead wrinkled in an expression of genuine concern as he ran a hand through long, loose black hair and then to cup Aimz' cheek in another effort to keep her posture and attention steady. "Well, I've been there Amy, and I know you have too...depression and alcohol do not mix. Come on, why don't you tell me what's going on?" "Nothing...absolutely nothing. Nothing as far as wrestling, nothing as far as life. You know how I feel, everything's shit..nobody gets close and when they do, they fuck me over anyway. The same damn thing's happened to my so-called 'career', my relationships...everything." "I know, but you've got to stop waiting for the other foot to come down. You're making the greens, you get to go out in front of the fans every night...you've even scored wrestling superstar Darcy Crisis," He added with a wink. Amy backed up and managed to land comfortably sitting on one of the only pieces of furniture in the appartment, a small leather sofa. "I doubt I'll be going out in front of the 'fans' every week anymore...Reagan'll screw me over soon, I feel it...this whole 'new school' revolution is starting, even I'm a little too old for it. And with you...well...I don't even know, you don't seem to trust me even a little." "Whoa, whoa....you go from complaining about nothing ever sticking around in your life, and suddenly I'M the one who's untrusting?" Darcy questioned her with an angry rise in his tone, raising a brow. "Are you saying I'd be wrong not to trust you?" She was quickly taking the turn from depression to anger, as usual. Amy's temper had always matched her hair color. "You're the one who ran out on me last year, or has that been conveniently forgotten? I don't know what to think of you...you won't ever tell me a damn thing." He sighed deeply, looking exasperating. "Do you want to keep bringing that up?" He glared at her, remembering the year before, when his life seemed lost after he had to quit wrestling, the sport he loved and met Amy within, because of an injury. "Do you think that'll change the fact that it happened? I swear, I can't have even have a ten minute conversation with you anymore without you bringing that up." "Well what else is there to say, 'That's fine, Darcy, I've done nothing to lose your trust, but you can go ahead and fucking shun me'?!" "Yeah, that's it Amy. I get up, leave my home, hell, my COUNTRY to be with you, and now I'm back on the road with you just so I can 'shun you.' Whatever gave you that idea?" Amy's temper was flaring, as was Markson's, but neither of them took a single step. She remained on the couch, sagging back against it in exhaustion while praying her mouth wouldn't get quite so tired before she was through this argument. "Well look at the conversation we have! 'Hi, how're you doing?' 'Fine' and basically nothing more...you NEVER bother trying to have any actual bond, it's almost like you're just sticking around me this time to stay close to wrestling." Darcy stared through her, simply stunned. "What did you just say?" "I said," She got back onto uneasy feet, trying to literally stand her ground. "It seems like you're not with me for any kind of relationship, but just a connection to the business I'm still in." "Thank you very much, Amy." He looked just about ready to turn away and walk back out the door. "How about you rub it in my face a little more that I can't be out there doing what I love to do anymore?" "Well maybe if you'd try to divert that 'love' to something you DO have..." "Alright Amy, let's recap. I haven't been to my home, in California, for months. Why? Because I've been in fucking Nova Scotia, to be with YOU. I agree to go on the road with you, and be your manager. Why? So I can see you more than three days a week. And here I am right now, looking into your eyes, reminded now like I am everyday that I will never, ever get to live out my dream. Yet I'm still standing here, listening to you bitch about me not caring about you. So if that isn't enough....then I don't know what else to do." "You could at least try, damnit! I can't STAND hearing about wrestling all the time...I already have to think about it almost twenty four hours a day just so I can keep bills paid and food in me, but all you do is talk about it! Not life, not us, just wrestling." Campbell tried regaining composure, but the alcohol was wearing off and she was getting intensely angry, finally snapping after days of frusteration regarding life in general. Of course, she was doing what she normally did, however unconciously...taking it out on the nearest human being, who always seemed to be Darcy. That is why I think you're just in this to stay close to wrestling, because it's the only thing you seem to be capable of loving." "Let me remind you just who the hell I am, Amy. I was Darcy fucking Crisis. Maybe I didn't come anywhere close to accomplishing what I wanted, but I still did a lot, and a lot of important people still know my name. You can tear apart my dream all you want, I know you don't understand..." He paused and went soft a moment, noting that Aimz had leaned back a moment with slightly wide-eyes, even though she was exhausted. Was she getting a little worried? Even if that was a case, he couldn't stop himself from at least looking angry, and neither could she. It was the circle of a love-hate relationship, and too much pride was on the line, so Markson haulted in the middle of the lighter path he'd been taking. "...but don't you DARE accuse me of wanting you just so I can keep wrestling flowing through my veins. I could have easily done that with or without you." "Let me remind you who the hell I am, Mister Ego. I'm Amy Campbell, and that might not mean a whole hell of a lot to any wrestlers, or anybody else, but it SHOULD matter to you. Remember me? I'm that girl who got hammered one night a year ago and woke up with you, that girl who ended up actually getting attatched...and I guess I'm the girl who's starting to realize her mistake." Aimz went back to the couch, this time curling up with her legs to her chest and chin on her knees as she continued with more of a somber tone hidden amongst her words. "Maybe giving it another chance was a mistake, or maybe it was a lesson. Either way, my best guess is that you're about to walk away again, right?" If I could live without him...She thought as she started twisting and rolling her fingers against her palms, a nervous habit of hers that never ceased to annoy him.I wouldn't be having this 'conversation'. "Well, if moving across the continent and staring down my dream just to make you happy isn't good enough....the reason I don't rave about you like I do wrestling is because I didn't know you when I was 12, like I did when I saw Hulk Hogan destroy Sarge at WrestleMania. I've only known you for two years, and only been around you for a few months of them. Forgive me that you're not quite on par yet with something I've had since I was in 7th grade. I speak through my actions as much as I do my words....try looking at the bigger picture instead of waiting for me to TELL you something when I can just as easily SHOW it to you." As much as she loved wrestling, Amy liked to have what felt remotely like a real life aside from it, and she always expressed that need. As for right now, she was doing little more than once again attempting to get that point across to Darcy. "Look, you moved, big damn deal! I moved to you a year ago and you STILL walked away without a care in the world. Don't try to play evens with me...you know I'd give up wrestling in a split second if that's what had to be done to keep you here. My problem is that you won't LET me surpass wrestling in your mind...you don't let me in, you just keep me on the outside and hope I'll sit by, nice and complacent." She was starting to seriously regret that the alcohol had worn off and by that point, Darcy was ready to tear his hair out. He raised clenched fists to his head as he spoke, raising the volume and power in his voice to match and apparently startling Amy, who jolted back in her seat, in doing so. "Don't you fucking get it? That's what I'm trying to do! That's why I'm here! Not so I can keep chasing the dream that I can no longer have, so that I can try and put it behind me. You're just mad cause it won't happen overnight, because I haven't had some kind of miracle epiphany. Deal with it, Amy...I'm not Superman." Her head sunk down to her knee caps and she said nothing more than, "I just want you to make it look like you're doing something for my well-being for once instead of suiting what you want and being pig-headed about the rest." Her shakey voice and the muffled sigh that followed indicated that alcohol had died off and emotion had taken over, and it wasn't certain which factor would prove to be worse. "What do you want me to do, honk a little horn everytime I'm thinking about you? Tell you eighty thousand times every time I do something nice for you? That's stupid, childish, and quite frankly you'd get sick of that horn after awhile." "That horn wouldn't even blow..." Amy exhaled rather loudly in a backwards sigh, "I'm trying to GET you to think of me, as opposed to choke holds and planchas." "I do. I just don't talk about it." He was staring her down at that point. "Just because I don't announce to the world that you're on my mind doesn't mean you're not. I'm twenty three years old, Amy...my life is going through a huge transition. Cut me some slack, or we're both going to be very miserable." "You were injured a year ago! What the HELL kind of transition are you going through now?!" "Sorry Amy....believe it or not, it just might take more than a year to replace something I've had for half my life." "Exactly...I'm just the replacement to occupy your time. How long's it gonna take to replace me when I get sick of this shit and finally bring myself to walk away?" "I told you I wasn't leaving! You're not just a goddamn time filler! Just because I don't love you like I do my childhood dream doesn't mean I don't care and it doesn't mean that what I feel will never change. That's just the way it is right now, stop trying to force everything the way you want it to be." And that's when she made the biggest mistake of the argument, something she didn't do in their almost daily quarrels... "If you don't like me more than a fucking sport, leave." So Darcy left. Within the very same millisecond Amy realized her words, he made her regret them. With a tear only held back by anger; Anger at the world in general, at Darcy for getting so angry, but mostly rage driven by her own dumb actions, which seemed to be a recurring theme in her life, Amy let her head drift back, squeezing her eyelids shut to the universe. She was finally alone, retreating into her own little world for the first time in days. But the thoughts still overcame her. Obviously, Darcy would come back within a few hours, calmed down after a little reflection, but that fact didn't stop Aimz from once again feeling like bashing her face into the floor. She knew that frusteration to the calibur of what she'd been feeling the last few days would make her snap, and she knew it'd be on someone close...but Markson put up with her, as always, and she was thankful for it, but Amy knew damned well that the seemingly endless circle of hurt, forgive and forget couldn't continue. Probably because she'd stopped forgetting... Obviously if she didn't make life turn around, if she couldn't look for a positive and not allow herself to slip toward the negative, everything would go down the drain all over again. Have you ever been in a situation where you know something's bad for you, but you do it because it's the easiest way, or the quick fix? Try living a life of it, with the perks being found few and too far between. The same old arguments, the same old demons... The same old pattern?
