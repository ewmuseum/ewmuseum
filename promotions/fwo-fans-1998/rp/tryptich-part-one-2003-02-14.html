Tryptich - Part One

14 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tryptich Part One - Dead To The World "What did Ivy say to me? Everyone always wants to know that. What was it that Ivy McGinnis said to me that made me decide to stick with that wrestling thing?" "'Stop being such a self- pitying dumbass,' she said, 'If you can give up this easily, my sister was really fuckin' duped by you. Now get off your stupid drunk ass, dry out, and go back to work! I'm tired'a this!'" "She's so caring, isn't she?" "But seriously... Ivy kept me going for a long, long time. Wrestling was the easy part... the more I wrestled, the more physical pain I felt, the less I had to think about Alicia. So I wrestled every night, sometimes twice a day. And while some'a those matches weren't pretty, the dedication I seemed to be putting in toward my craft earned Merritt's attention, and later, his respect." "TV from that era is misleading... it'd have you think I spent every day, 24 hours, in a depressive haze. I had some good times, mostly with Bandit, and later, Troy and his Generation X-Press. And Ivy... our relationship went from her hating me for taking her sister away from home to...... Beyond friendship. Beyond family. Ivy was home for me... home's where you don't have to explain yourself because everyone there a'ready knows what you're about." "Yep, that was Ivy. Still is.... she knows how to read people and figure out what they're all about... they don't need to explain to her." "It goes without saying, but Trip's a lucky guy." "But I was usually okay, until I tried to sleep.... had nightmares about Ali almost nightly. Hell, by the time I wrestled Prizm at TWS '95, it was six days straight I hadn't slept, and it was after a match with Godreign and a match with Randalls... was seeing things by the end of the night." "It was a job, y'know? Nothin' I couldn't get at home... but I couldn't face going back to the Bronx and tellin' everyone that I couldn't make it out there. No way in hell I was gonna do that." "It was cool though, long as I stayed away from the politics... but ironically enough, thanks to Ivy, I got sucked right in. When she and Hornet got together while Hornet was the number one wrestler in the world, she got roped into all his politics. And as fate would have it... I ended up gettin' used as a pawn in GUNS' war with the man." "In the words of a great man, 'People talk about wrestling not being real... it's far more real than people think.' And there was a war raging through the CSWA in the mid 1990s that was very real, with real casualties. Real people fought the battles." "And real people emerged from the ashes and tried to move on." "I might've been a nobody, but I didn't deal with it by painting flowers on a bus and pretending not to care that I was a main- event- player- turned- joke. I didn't deal with it by throwing gimmick after gimmick against the wall to see what would stick. I just let loose on the entire world.... everything came out. Scotty Michaels asked me at the bar one night, what's my gimmick? My life, I said. I don't have the time or patience to play a part.... so I'm gonna tell my shit and wrestle whoever they tell me to wrestle that night." "I never thought it'd get over. I mean, who am I? A nobody." "Over might be a bit much... but it was certainly something for people to take notice of. But at least it kept me employed and on the road. Anything to keep from going back to the Bronx as a failure, y'know?" "And that's exactly what I did, after a misstep by Troy killed my knee. Still not sure what the problem was, but before I knew what was up, I lost my belt, lost my job, and lost my ability to walk without pain. Least it was before the fiasco between Merritt and Tallman... my contract buyout didn't get frozen in the red tape." "But it wasn't about the money.... it was about a three year stretch of my life that saw me lose my girl, my sanity, and my one ticket outta nowhere. And sure, I was goin' back to the Bronx as a failure and a nobody, without a thing to call my own... but nobody could take those three years away from me." "Everyone gets fifteen minutes of fame, right? I had three years. I had nothing to complain about." "Right?"
