Chaos- Middle (1 of 2)

19 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The war of Chaos and Eternity has waged for an incredible period of time. As long as the world continues to exist, Chaos and Eternity will continue to be reborn, no one knowing how to stop the endless cycle. Since the world first begun, the war between these two entities has taken every form; from battles between single enemies the world will never know about to gigantic wars between entire nations. However, during the most recent of wars between the two entities, somehow the very identity of Eternity was split in three different ways. These three identities would be born into a single person, each resembling a part of the overall Eternity's personality. The being known as Jessica resembled her devotion. The being known as Kali Saturos resembled her viciousness. And the being known as Rei resembled her intelligence. As long as the three personalities remain split, they will be no match for Chaos. It is our job to protect Eternity from Chaos and her legions until the day may come that she can reclaim her mask, and once again become a match for Chaos. Mark shook his head as he ran the ropes in the abandoned warehouse the two young men used to train. He looked back at Evan, sitting on a folding chair on the outside of the ring, and reading aloud. "Come on, Evan." Mark plead with him. "We came here to work out, not to worry about.....that. I know what it says already, what's the point of this?" Evan Stevenson rolled his eyes. "The point, Mark, is that I'm worried, okay? Of all things we have to worry about Eternity showing up on national TV as that Kali Saturos person, and getting followed there by one of Chaos' strongest followers. Why would fate choose us to be born into a wrestling family, is what *I'd* like to know." Mark chuckled from inside the ring before speaking. "Well, Evan, considering the old man once said that, at one point in time, Chaos and Eternity's identities were that of Broadway dancers, I don't see how wrestling is any less stupid a platform for this war. Why, I thought you liked wrestling?" "It's a decent waste of time." Evan replied. "But I'm worried about Etern-....err, Kali. Do you really think she stands a chance for Chaos' Destroyer in her current state of mind?" "I wouldn't worry about your sister, Evan." Mark began, but Evan quickly spoke up. "That....thing, is not my sister." Evan said. "Kali Saturos isn't even a person. She's a thing. Jessica is my sister. Rei, I guess, in a way is my sister. But Kali....Kali killed our parents. She-she's not even human." Mark instantly regretted his choice of words. He sighed as he exited the ring, and walked over to Evan, putting a hand on his shoulder. "Look, Evan, I'm sorry." he said. "I just met that, you don't have to worry about Kali. She represents the viciousness, the incredibly ferocity of Eternity that's allowed her to survive even with an enemy as strong as Chaos for all this time. She can handle herself against anything that Chaos throws at her." "You're probably right." Evan said after a short pause. "I just wish there was something I could do to help her." Suddenly, noise coming from a TV propped up on a cinderblock to the right of the ring caught their attention. Namely, that noise was fWo announcer Matt Panzer announcing the name of a certain wrestler. "And his opponent, weighing at 247 pounds.....VORAN XIAS!" Evan's eyes widened at once. He turned to Mark, who had the same reaction. "Vor-an?" Mark said in disbelief. "That can't be-" "It is." Evan said. "The Fourth.....has returned early." "The Fourth is...." "The Corrupter. The one who will make Eternity....." Evan and Mark realized how little time was left. They had to get to Kali. But how? Kali exited the room in the New York City hotel, wiping her hands on her blue jeans. Red stains instantly appeared there, but it wasn't as if Kali noticed. She was more than used to the sight of blood by now. "One down." she grinned as she walked off into the night, the knowledge that The Destroyer would never get in her way again fresh in her mind. Unbeknownst to her, however.....she was watching. "Three to go." Chaos silently mouthed, and smiled.
