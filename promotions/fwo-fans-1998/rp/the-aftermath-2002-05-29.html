The Aftermath

29 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ding. Ding. Ding. In Character: Triple X exited the ring - his face, telling the tale on how he felt. He was angry. Pissed off. Here he was... one of the greatest - if not the single greatest performer to ever set foot in a wrestling ring, on verge of winning... and, who blows it? Eli Flair. Sure they had become friends... but, they were still rivals. And, Triple X still wanted to win this four man hardcore match. And, once again... Eli cost him it. The outcome wasn't the usual Eli Flair victory, Sean had grown accustomed to... but, he got pinned. By Grendel - nonetheless. And, at the end of the day... that still meant one thing... somebody else won... and, Triple X lost. Out of Character: Sean slid underneath the ring and began to make his way up the aisle. Normally, he would spit... snot... and, curse his way up the aisle, threatening bodily harm on whoever had anything negative to say as a result of his loss. But, he and Chris Universal got together and brainstormed... and, realized this wasn't the way to go. Even as a heel, the fans for the most part, cheered him. It was just too easy, and seemed like the right thing to do. He was good looking... was a high flyer... had an endless amount of catch phrases that they always seemed to enjoy repeating after him. Why not take advantage? Sean turned around, once he was at the top of the ramp, almost backstage... and, shook his head in disappointment. As if he was saddened by his loss. The crowd, who was already still on there feet as a result of a pretty good match, cheered him on as he turned his back to them, and stepped through the curtain. ****** "Great match, Sean." Sean - dog tired from the battle royal and the match he just competed in - hardly noticed anybody as he stepped through the curtain, ending up on the other side. It was Chris Universal... his boss. Sean made a face... as if he were sucking on a sour lemon. Stevens - "Thanks, Chris... but, really... it could have been MUCH better. But, I guess it was okay for what it was. Eli and Grendel went over HUGE as powerhouses. And, Lance and I got to run a couple of cool spots." Universal - "Yeah... and, it also shows your versatility in there. We already know of your history with Eli. And, you and Lance have great chemistry. But, to see you two work with two big men... it was FAR more interesting than you think. Plus the reaction from the crowd was positive." Stevens - "Honestly, Chris... just BEING able to wrestle a few Hardcore Matches is versatile enough for me. That's not my forte. But, I'm not complaining." Universal - "I know. I think we may have an interesting program for you in the future, though... but, for now... you look exhausted... I'll let you go." Stevens - "Alright... I'll talk to you later." ****** Sean stuck opened his dressing room door... with ideas of a hot shower running through his mind. He took off his shirt... and, as soon as he did... he was interrupted by someone knocking on the door. It was Eli Flair. Stevens - "What's up, E?" Sean stuck out his hand... Flair smacked it. Flair - "Nothin'. Just checking up on you. You didn't seem like yourself out there." Stevens - "And, I bet you know why." Flair smirked. Flair - "I think I may have an idea or two." Stevens> - "Well, look, E. I'm not telling you to tell her anything... or to make her talk to me. I don't even think that's possible. All I want to know is... is she okay?" Flair - "You know how Ivy is, Trip. She's tough. I think she's more hurt than she lets the world in on... but, she's tough. I think THAT may have something to do with why she's not here tonight. I don't know. But, get yourself some rest tonight... I'll pick you up at the hotel tomorrow morning. I want you kicking ass tomorrow, you hear me?" Stevens - "I hear you. But, there's been a change of plans. Lance is gonna ride with you in the rental... I'm catching a flight to Orlando tonight. I just... I just need to sleep in my own bed for a couple of hours. I think that would do me some good. I won't be late for the show... There's a 12pm flight scheduled for tomorrow morning." Flair - "You sure? That's a lot of ripping and running, kid." Stevens - "I can handle it. I'm young." They both smiled at his last comment, before extending there hands again for another slap. Flair left... and, minutes later... Sean did, too. Bags in hand. Orlando, Florida in sight.
