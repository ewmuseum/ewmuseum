The One Thing

22 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kind of an obscure term, the One Thing. In law enforcement, it usually referred to the single piece of evidence which took a collection of prints and threads and other obscure clues and seemed to make them come together to form a picture of the criminal. In this case, however, it was a little bit harder. For Wippit, in fact for most of wrestling, the One Thing was that which got you noticed, put you on the radar screen and sent you to the top. It was separated you from the jobber, the curtain jerker, and lately the tag teams, it seemed. It could be a saying, like "And that's the bottom line..." or "Objection!" It could be a People's Eyebreow, or blue hair. It could even be the way you spelled your name with some obscure character instead of an O. Most wrestlers could spend their entire lives looking for the one thing, and come up dry, and never get their name in lights, never get the headlines, the titles, the money. Most wrestlers spend countless days on the road, wrestling in gymnasiums and bingo halls, a lucky select managing an arena of some sort. And the secret? The catch all, end all, be all about the One Thing? It does no exist. Because it isn't a name, or a phrase, or the way you spelled you nname witth a lower case first letter and the rest uppercase. It's the story you put forth, the quest for victory, the agony of loss, the triumph over insurmountable odds. Those cops, loking for the one thing? They always had it, they just never saw it. The broken down wrestler who could barely walk now? He always had it too. he just couldn't see it in himself, and so couldn't show it to others, couldn't define what it was. Because he didn't know. The true quest isn't for gold or money, it's for self-development. Find yourself, find your story, and you find the One Thing after all. The One Thing... is you.
