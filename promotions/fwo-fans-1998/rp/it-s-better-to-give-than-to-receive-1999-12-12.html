It's Better to Give than to Receive

12 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::We catch up to Hellrazor and Lamont as they are relaxing in a hotel room. Closer viewing shows that it is more than just a room, it is an entire penthouse with windows overlooking the great scenery. Hellrazor is busy dialing on his cellphone while Lamont is busy tearing up the bed that housekeeping just finished changing. Matt Loco is impatiently awaiting his chance to speak to Hellrazor, and is passing his time away looking at the boxes upon boxes of gifts that are piled over in the corner under a large Christmas tree.:: Loco: Hey..is there one for me in there? Hellrazor: Fool, I told you stay away from those. Those are our presents..nobody gives a damn about your sorry ass. Loco: I beg to differ, I have many close and personal friends.. and I'm quite the ladies man whenever we hit Scranton Pennsylvania.. and.. Hellrazor: SHUT UP BITCH! No!.. umm.. hi Momma..no no..I wasn't talkin to you.. there's some jackass here to do an interview.. yeah he can wait. So.. you know.. I come across some good times here in the fWo.. and was wonderin' what you'll be wantin for Christmas? ::Hellrazor listens with a small smile crossing his face:: Hellrazor: Uh-huh.. a T-shirt? An fWo T-shirt? Of course.. of your #1 favorite wrestler? And you want it autographed too? You got it Momma.. I'll send that Hellrazor special edition T-shirt right out to you today and.. what? ::Hellrazor suddenly gets a look of resignation on his face.:: Hellrazor: Oh...right..of course.. the Lamont T-shirt. Yeah.. I'll send it. Yeah.. he's fine..he's eating well.. the fans love him... look Momma..I gotta go.. I see you soon. ::Hellrazor hangs the phone up and tosses it aside with a sigh of disgust. He glares over at Lamont, who is now busy playing with one of his many catnip-filled toys.:: Hellrazor: Lamont. All the damn people care about is Lamont... ::Loco now approaches Hellrazor cautiously and sits down next to him with his notebook open.:: Loco: Care to talk about it old buddy? Hellrazor: I tell ya what I care to talk about. I've gotten myself on a bit of a roll lately, and have earned a hardcore title shot as a result of it. The fans.. they just can't get enough Hellrazor man.. and Lamont.. they dig him too. I figure, he ain't hurting my career none, in fact, his shirt is the hottest selling item right now at the fWo shows. He's outselling the Frenchie, Slugger, Primetime, that wack-ass Strangler drawstring turtleneck shirt... he's outselling everybody! Loco: Including your T-shirt which has sold a total of... 6 units. Hellrazor: 6? (looks at the camera) I just wanna thank my homies from the east side O' town..for keepin it real, and helpin me out... Loco: Actually, they were sold online... Hellrazor: Ahh.. ok.. those internet fans.. they're pretty smart..they recognize talent when they see it. Loco: Actually..they were sold as part of an offer.. "Buy a Lamont Shirt for 24.99 and get the Hellrazor shirt for just.. umm.. 99 cents." Hellrazor: 99 cents!! and only 6 were sold? And since my contract calls for how much of a percentage of the sales? Loco: 20 percent. Hellrazor: 20 percent huh.. that's umm.. that's... Loco: One dollar and twenty cents my man! I actually have a check here from merchandising... Hellrazor: ONE DOLLAR AND TWENTY DAMN CENTS???? Those shirts cost me 3 dollars each to make! Dammit! Well.. how much is Lamont's shirt? 24.99? and the royalties on that are? Loco: Yes, well, Lamont's T-shirt has a different percentage..because.. well.. he's not a wrestler. He's a cat. And merchandising felt that nobody would buy a cat shirt anyways... and since he doesn't have a contract... his share of the profits is 80% while merchandising takes a standard 20% licensing fee. Hellrazor: And how many Lamont T-shirts have been sold? Like 200? Loco: Fourteen Thousand. And at 80% of 25 Dollars that comes out to.. Hellrazor: A HELL-A LOTTA CAKE! Loco: ...comes out to... $280,000.00 Hellrazor: Awwwww Yeah! I'm rich baby! Booyeah! Loco: Actually... there's also a letter here from the fWo attorneys.. it seems some people at the ASPCA are concerned about Lamont being exploited.. therefore, effective immediately, all of his checks are going to be put into a specific trust. You will have no access to the funds, unless of course he passes away. Now rest assured.. they are keeping a close eye on him, so if some accident were to befall him... Hellrazor: Fool! What you doin readin my mail? I know..I know.. look it's all good.. this is Lamont's hotel room.. and umm.. that champagne? that's for Lamont too.. and umm... Loco: The BMW outside? I suppose he's a licensed driver as well? Hellrazor: Naww man..I'm his umm.. his chauffeur... ain't you ever seen "Drivin Ms. Crazy?" Loco: Just to let you know..all future expenses must be approved by Mr Blake Bellows Esquire, who has Power of Attorney in all of Lamont's financial affairs. I'm afraid you'll have to be picking up your own hotel room bills and other such expenses again from now on. Hellrazor: Well... that's all good.. cuz like I said.. I got me a HARDCORE TITLE SHOT baby. And to prove that I'm a good owner, I'm havin Lamont sit right there at ringside to see the whole thing. He's gonna be the Special Guest Timekeeper! Now is that a show of friendship or what? That should shut those ASPCA goons up. Now, on to my opponent for tonight.. Joy. What kinda damn name is "Joy"?? He's a bit too happy.. reminds me of some of the crackheads we used to run into down on the streets.. Loco: But he -is- the hardcore champion. Hellrazor: It'll be WAS after tonight. There's this old song by Rob Base and DJ EZ-Rock... called Joy and Pain. Well tonight.. you bring the Joy.. and I'll bring the pain. And hey.. when all's said and done.. and I got me my Hardcore title... maybe I'll step up to the plate and become a member of the "Glee Club" ... from what I been hearin, it ain't that tough to join. Loco: Anything else you'd like to add? Hellrazor: Hell yeah.. look at all these presents for me from all over the world.. Here's one from Ireland.. 'To Lamont.. my favorite.. cat' oh.. here's one from Boston Massachusetts! "Lamont. We hope the holidays bring you good fortune.. Paul" ..hmm Here's one all the way from Mexico... "Feliz Navidad a Lamont!"... Here's one from France from "Brian Pierre and Chris".. I didn't know Pierre was a French name..oh it's for Lamont... ::Hellrazor looks at the pile of presents and begins digging through them all furiously:: Hellrazor: Lamont...Lamont.. Lamont... dammit. Well Lamont may be popular here..and in most of the world.. but I am STILL the MAN in Iceland and the Philippines! And after tonight.. I'm a be the MAN in the Hardcore division. So how you like me now?? ::Loco ignores Hellrazor and turns to the camera:: Loco: You heard it here fans, tonight Lamont is the special guest timekeeper in the Joy vs Hellrazor match! This one should be great! Tune in to Sunday Slaughter to see all of the action. I'm Matt Loco with my close personal friend Lamont signing out! Hellrazor: Lamont??? You sonovabitch Loco! ::A sudden violent motion by Hellrazor sends a wide-eyed Loco straight into the camera which flashes a screen full of static before going black.::
