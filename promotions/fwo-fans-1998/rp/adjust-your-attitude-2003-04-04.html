Adjust: Your Attitude

4 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Leigh Landers calmly pulled up a chair, smoothed out her knee-length skirt, and sat down next to Allen Speck, who was still laid out like a car wreck on the bed he laid in. She smiled ever so slightly at Allen, and tried desperately to cheer the poor guy up. "How's your arm?" Strike one, Leigh. "The doctor said that I have a dislocated shoulder. If it had been a couple of more inches off, I probably would've torn a muscle to join that dislocated shoulder for a beer." Allen said, frowning. "Yeah, well, you don't wanna know what my brother's saying about you," Leigh said, "He said you made him look bad." "I made him look bad. Which match was he watching, again?" Allen asked of Leigh's older brother, Benjamin. "The imaginary one in his head." Leigh said carelessly and almost immediately. She never liked her older brother, because he was practically being handed Kyle Morris's family legacy, and leaving Leigh out in the cold since she was an adopted daughter. Kyle also refused to train Leigh, citing that she didn't really belong in the ring. Desperate to show her father that she could hang in the ring, she ended up being trained by Joey Malone at the same time Allen was. This explained the long-term friendship that Allen and Leigh have, though the two were much more akin to siblings than a couple, and had therefore never dated. Not to mention that Benjamin was a horrible atrocity of a professional wrestler that made Erik Kelly look like Chris Benoit. "Figures." was all Allen had to say about it. Leigh felt it best to change the subject, and tried once again to cheer up her friend. "How about the fWo? Did you call them to see what's up?" she asked, again with her usual carefree innocence. This would be strike two. I believe Scott Slugger would be saying something about her batting game right about now, except that this wasn't baseball and Scott Slugger wasn't here, and... you know, this was a bad analogy. "Nope." Allen said. "Why not? Wasn't this what you wanted? I don't know about you, but if I had to wrestle my brother again, I'd retire." Leigh said, sternly, and without the innocence or carefree attitude this time. When it concerned wrestling Benjamin Morris, you took it damned seriously. "I'm not wrestling that no-selling neanderthal again." Allen vowed, pointing his finger at Leigh. Leigh cracked a smile at him in response. "Right, so you'll call them up?" Leigh asked with a wider smile. "Eventually." "Wait wait wait, I thought the paperwork was taken care of? Won't they start asking questions?" Leigh asked, "I mean, c'mon, if I were a multi-million dollar wrestling organization, I'd be asking questions." "Maybe you're right. But still, I ain't exactly a hundred percent. Can't even play Megaman for Christ's sakes." Allen said, glancing idly at his Game Boy Advance. Leigh reached over and picked the device up before she giggled at Allen. "You're such a dork." "Hey, man's gotta have somethin' to do when he's bored," Allen said, "Besides, ain't you the one who collects Sinatra memorabilia?" "Hey, I think that's less dorky!" she argued, almost tempted to start a random pillow fight with Allen, before she remembered that he was still hurt and probably not up to having the crap kicked out of him with a soft, cotton cushion. Besides, he was laying on both of the available ones. With that, Leigh merely sighed, sat back in her chair, and paused for thought. Then she spoke again, "Still, though, even if my back was broken, I'd still at least call them and let them know what's up." "Damn, girl, you're persistant today," Allen said, before he indicated to her to pick up the phone for him, "Be a darling and hand the phone to me, then." She did so. With some difficulty(having only one usable arm at the moment and all), Speck took the phone off of the hook, placed it in his lap, dialed a number, picked the receiver back up, and handed the phone's greater half back over to Leigh for her to put back on the stand. Allen held the receiver on his ear and waited patiently, before he got an answer. "Yeah, hi? Silver Fox, right? No? Samuel Smooth? Well, whatever. This is that guy y'all signed a week ago, Allen Speck." ... "Uh, say, listen... y'know that part where I told y'all that I could do lots of stunts and all of that and really entertain?" ... "Dislocated shoulder. Gonna be out for four whole months." !!! "Hey hey hey, don't blame me. Blame the other guy. You haven't seen unprofessional until you come to my neck of the woods and wrestle Benji Morris." ... "Huh? You want me to go where?" Allen suddenly shouted, bolting out of his (un)comfortable position on the bed and instantly regretting it as it put strain on his arm. Allen grimaced and groaned in pain, causing Smooth to possibly question his condition, "No no, it's fine. Really. Yeah. I'll, uh, be on the next flight out..." Allen quickly cupped his hand into the phone and turned to Leigh. "Hey, Leigh... you think the airport `round here takes flights to Spain?"
