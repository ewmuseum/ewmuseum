Try to wipe the smile off my face

21 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brandon sat silent, the grin spread across his face was one of being in a good mood. He had lost last night, but he was there for one of the most memorable moments in the fWo. Machina beat Jim Dudley for the fWo World Title, the commity who had hired him, had all sold their shares back to Beaven. They thought they had destroyed the fWo, in reality all they had done was make it stronger. All they had done was make the fWo stronger against any force that could try to bring it down. This entire thing just made Brandon smile, watching Creed and Mountain beat the hell out of Jon and Spencer. Every moment was one for celebration, when last week Lance died, everything seemed so gloomy. Now, everyone was smiling as they exited the arena. Looking as Beaven came back, it was so serious, watching as Machina came back, everything just seemed to be fun again. I watched the end of the show like I was a little kid watching Hogan body slam Andre. You just watched with that smile, you hopped up out of your seat when your favorite wrestler won. Brandon hung his head down in thought for a moment, he had watched the very end of the show though. He saw who the sixth member was, and that sixth member wasn't an old wash-up like Jim Dudley, Cactus Jon, and David Spencer. Eron was something else, Brandon had met him before, he had watched what he did in jOlt. He knew Eron was an opponent not to be messed with, he could win any match, at any time. That fact, scared Brandon a bit. Dudley was gone, Jon was gone, Spencer was gone, all of the evil had left the promotion. Well, so they had all thought. Brandon laid back onto the small hotel room bed, he didn't need to think about next week yet. No need to ponder what Dragon was going to do, everything was well with the world and him. So drifting into a slumber wouldn't be bad. Brandon only needed to live day by day, and today, the smile on his face, was impossible to wipe off, even as Brandon went to sleep.
