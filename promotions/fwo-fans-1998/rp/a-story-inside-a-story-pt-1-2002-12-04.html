A story inside a... Story.(pt. 1)

4 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Glaring up at the "no smoking" sign, he flicked the remains of his "Newport" to the ground, blowing out the last bit of smoke from his nose and mouth, and sighed, shaking his head in disappointment. He wasn't necessarily angry at the fact that he had to give up smoking the cigarette; he was more disappointed because he had promised himself, and those he loved that he had given up smoking for good, only to renege. But, "life is a bitch sometimes," he thought to himself. The cards you're dealt aren't always the cards you deserve, and sometimes, you just need the feeling that a relaxing, menthol cigarette could give... "...stop thinking about the damned cigarette, Luck..." He spoke to nobody in particular. His name was Shannon Davis... but, nobody in the wrestling world would know that
