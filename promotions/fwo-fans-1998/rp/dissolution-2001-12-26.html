Dissolution

26 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ric was steamed. He shoved the small tin of Chinese food away from him, and sat, motionless. It wasn't that the chicken and broccoli was unappetizing; rather, it was that his appetite was failing him. 3vil was no more. Austin Davis had seen to that. Davis... the bastard had always been right on the heels of tragedy. Last year, at Countdown, Ric had been scheduled to defend the title against Davis. That never came to be, as Universal interfered to prevent it. And now... this. Ric knew he had Ross beaten. Davis made certai he was nowhere near the pin, which allowed Ross to fire back up and take the offensive. Ric was prepared to kick out, but Davis took out Ross - something that Ric was appreciative of, since it was Davis' fault that the match was still continuing. But then, Davis had the gall to strike Ric as well. Savior. Villian. It seemed as if Austin was attempting to make himself into both of these. Universal was a moron to have trusted a snake like Davis. Chronos would never have allowed the return of such an individual - of course, like all his other hopes and dreams, the chance to control such aspects of the fWo had faded away. So be it. Ross would probably claim that he was cheated, and demand another title shot. Austin would be throwing his hat in the ring, and he also knew that Universal would do his best to make sure that Ric didn't receive a moment's rest. That was fine by Ric - he intended to start this year off strong. Let them come - he was waiting. And, most of all, Ric would be damned before he'd go out this year the way he went out at the last Countdown. Thinking of that, he flipped open his cell phone, and punched in Pierre's number. Just because 3vil was not allowed to function as a group didn't mean that Ric couldn't plan a surprise that involved Frenchie - and this was going to be a big one.
