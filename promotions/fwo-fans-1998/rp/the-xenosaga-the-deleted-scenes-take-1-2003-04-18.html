The Xenosaga: The Deleted Scenes - Take 1

18 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE FOLLOWING ARE DELETED SCENES FROM THE XENOSAGA SERIES. THE REASON WHY THEY'RE DELETED IS BECAUSE MEGA JOB IS INVOLVED AND GOD FORBID THAT MEGA JOB BE INVOLVED IN THE REAL THING. The Codemaster immediately shrieked in horror at "Club Drive", a game for the Jaguar, and dropped the controller. Dingleberry, frustreated at this action, forcefully pushed the controller back into his hands. When the Codemaster dropped it a second time, Dingleberry groaned and turned to Cagle. "Oy! Cagle, be a good mate and fetch me a roll'a duct tape and a sea breeze, this'll take a lil'while." "CUT!" a voice off-screen shouted. As soon as the voice was heard, Allen Speck got up from his chair, breaking the rather fake ropes that bound him. He immediately reached into a pocket in his chocolate syrup-covered robes and pulled out a cell phone, before he dialed a number. "Yeah, Leigh... can we go over how much I actually make off of this stupid thing, again?" Speck said, as he wandered off. This left the cameras to deal with the director approaching Davy Dingleberry and James Cagle. This director was a man with hair that stood straight up, he wore an ugly blue body suit, a cape, and if you don't know who the hell he is by just that short description, then you... have... humor... issues. It was Beef the Slightly Annoyed. From Mega Job. "Okay, what the heck is a 'sea breeze', Dingleberry?" Beef asked. "Well.....oy saw a commerical fuh eet once!" "Well, alright then. At least it was humorous and random. We apparently like that around there. Isn't that right, Steve?" Beef asked a midget, who was seated on a stepladder called Oddjob. The midget wore a brown bodysuit and a black cape, he was Chinese, and he was pretty much the most meanacing human being in the room. We say that because Oddjob is the most meanacing THING in the room. "INDEED." Steve boomed. Beef turned off camera, "C'mon, Janito, get down here so we can have all of us on the screen at once. This is for that deleted scenes thingie, after all." "Bloody hell." Janito said, walking into the room while holding a giant key, "How come you blokes get to be the directors while I have to be the sodding 'key grip'? I don't even know what in the bloody hell a key grip does." "You grip a giant key, jerkwad." Beef said, "Would you rather be the best boy? I'm pretty sure that Will & Grace could use a new best boy. Especially Will." "Bugger." Janito said, with a sigh. "Speaking of key, where is it Janito?" Cagle asked, southern twang still evident despite being out of character for the time being. "I...uhhh....well, it's really quite funny, actually...I mean....uhhh, yeah, see..." Janito continued his incoherent rambling which was, more or less, him coming up with an excuse. Janito was never good with excuses. Beef was all "O.O!!! Oh my god, don't tell me you gave it to..." Janito gulped, "yes." They both turned, and Cagle had been replaced by Evil Smokey the Bear. Well....not replaced, from the shouts of "Gawd dayumit to heyell" inside Smokey, we can conclude that he had swallowed James. "Hey, you, gay-boy. Get me partner out o' there, less you want a swirlie!" Dingleberry said, pointing to the bathroom labelled 'Susan Eats Semen'. "Oh sod, not a swirlie!" Oddjob offered another suggestion, "...." "Bloody hell! I'll do it! I'll do it!!" Janito gulped once more, and proceeded to trot over to Evil Smokey, aka The Bear. "Yo jive turkey, what chu feelin'?" "Actually, I was wondering if you could...umm....possibly regurgitate James? Or maybe excrete him? you know....just a suggestion. You don't HAVE to, but I mean, it could really help. Oh, and save me a swirlie. Perhaps even a flogging. I've always wondered what a flogging feels like, you know? But never actually wanted to feel a flogging. Though, I've been swirlied enough to where it's second nature. Bloody sod, man! Help--" "SILENCE!" El Janito, was thusly...swallowed. Beef, ".....Dammit." Steve walked up to Evil Smokey. "RELEASE." "What'cha gonna do to me if I don't, honkey?" Evil Smokey questioned. Steve raised up a single fist. Cagle and Janito were promptly coughed up. "THANKS." Steve said, digging himself out of the gooey, saliva-covered James Cagle and El Janito. Evil Smokey then wandered off, leaving Beef and Davy Dingleberry to watch him leave. "I really wish he'd stop eating people at random." Beef said. Dingleberry nodded in agreement, before he added something, "Say, eesn't this the part where a completely random and ratha unnecessary fWo wrestluh cameo occurs?" Carson Covington wandered in. "Say, do you guys know where the coffee machine is?" Carson asked. Beef and Dave glanced in his direction. Janito and Cagle looked up at him. Carson Covington looked a little lost. "Yeah, Kermit, it's over there somewhere." Beef said, pointing in a random direction. Carson looked in that direction, "There's a stack of Pokemon Magazines and a Pikachu doll over there, not a coffee machi-- ...did you just call me Kermit?" "Yes. Yes, I did." Beef said. "Might I ask why?" Covington asked. "Because you're as green as a frog, my friend!" Beef responded with a wide smile, before he started laughing. When he figured out that he was the only one laughing, he nudged Dingleberry in the shoulder, and Dingleberry joined him in the laughter. Covington was not amused, "I've been wrestling longer than you have! Do you even wrestle at all?" "There was that one time. Yeah. That one time. Um.... Janito, help me out here." "No way, wanker." Janito responded. Dingleberry offered, "Point is, Your greener than the bathtub afta gay-boy ova there washes up!" Janito frowned, ":-(" "Riiiiight. You know, I think I'll have better chance finding the coffee machine if I gouge out my eyes or something. I hope you all get cancer or AIDS or something." Covington said, before he wandered out of the room in search of his coffee machine. "Bye, Kermit! Don't let Miss Piggy find you!" Beef shouted, before he and Dingleberry laughed again. "HA." Steve said. Oddjob agreed, "...." Allen Speck wandered back into the room, still on the phone with his friend, Leigh Landers, "Is there any way that I could get more money out of this? I'm not covered in chocolate syrup for my own personal amusement, you know." He left the room from the other side, probably not realizing that it was a bit of a fake corridor constructed for the set that the Xenosaga was being filmed in. Cagle had a light-bulb, "OH! AH GEET EET! HEH, He's new! Thus bein' green! Thus bein' lahk Kermit the Frawg, cause he's green! EL. OH. EL!!!" :: THIS HAS BEEN A MIKE RENNER / ALEX CLARK JOINT ::
