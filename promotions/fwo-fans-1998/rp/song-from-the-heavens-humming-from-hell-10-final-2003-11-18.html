Song from the Heavens, Humming from Hell - 10 - Final

18 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jeremy Roddenburg was thirteen years old, and he wasn't suppose to run. His asthma was so horrid that with even the slightest form of excitement could set off an attack. But he didn't care, he had to tell his Dad. Jeremy ran inside and into the room where his father sat on one end of a long leather couch. Immediately his Dad grew angry," Don't run Jer, you know that." Jeremy however was fine, he stopped and wore a smile so large it took him a few moments to be able to use his mouth, "Dad I just got the tickets." His Dad sat up, "What tickets?" "To the free show," Jeremy continued, "They came in the mail, it says that I qualify to be there because of my problem." His Dad repeated himself, "What tickets?" "Two tickets," Jeremy started up again, "To the Christmas show, the fWo Christmas show... I'm going to finally meet Brand Frontier, and maybe even Scott Slugger if I'm lucky." "Ah Jer," His Dad seemed to grow solemn, "I don't know if we can afford it." "No Dad," Jeremy piped up, "It's free, all free... even the plane tickets and hotel rooms." "Is the fWo funding it all?" Jeremy's Dad replied. Jeremy shook his head, "No Steve Douglas." "What a wonderful man," Jeremy's Dad cut in, "Looks like we have a show to go to."
