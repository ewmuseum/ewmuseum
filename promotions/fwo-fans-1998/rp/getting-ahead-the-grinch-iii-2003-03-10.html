in which the Fuse Bros. learn they have scared a young boy, in a non-Michael Jackson way. Conor's grin was from ear to ear, as wide as it could possibly be... whereas his Brother, Tyler, just nodded his head in a very tranquil manner. They were walking out of the Stadium just now, putting another good game in the books... a fantastic victory over James Cagle. Conor looked at his Brother before placing both hands on the door and pushing back the bar as the door opened up to the nighttime air, and they walked out. "So we're just gonna hit the hotel then, Ty?" Conor asked, as he exited after his Brother. But what Conor didn't know... was that someone was out there waiting for him. Twelve year old Bobby Smyth had approached the Fuse's, and tugged lightly on Tyler's blue jeans. Wrapped up in his brown winter coat, and brown winter hat... Tyler sparked an interest as to what had bothered him... so he looked down. And there Bobby stood, all three feet of him, holding up a small book and a pen in his hands. "H- hi... could you- you please sign this for me?" He asked in his most polite voice to date. Tyler took the book along with the pen and smiled. "You got it mushroom man." And rubbing his head, Bobby let out a little squeak of joy. But Conor just watched on as Tyler signed his name to the page, and then handed it back to Bobby. "Here you go." Right after Robert Edward Smyth got the book back, the twelve year old reached out towards Conor, asking for his signature too. "You got it kid." So Conor signed his name to it as well... handing the book back down to Bobby, as he and his older Brother started to walk off again. However, once more, they were stopped by a tugging on their jeans. "Um... misters." Bobby said softly. "Yyyyeeeessss?" Conor asked. "Good Match out there!" Sure, he was disappointed in what they had done, but Bobby needed to prolong the time his idols were standing in front of him. Conor glanced at his Brother and grinned evilly.
