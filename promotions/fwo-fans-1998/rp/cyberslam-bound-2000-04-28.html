fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization CyberSlam bound. April 28, 2000 Cyberslam 2000. The biggest event in fWo history. The French Foundation play an integral part in this big event. Courage, teaming with Lance Star to vie for the tag team titles. Primetime, facing Mitch Wilson in an I Quit match for the Internet Title. The Flying Frenchie, facing Ultra-Violet in a hair vs mask match. Cyberslam was shaping up to be a huge night for the French Foundation. They have the talent, they have the charisma, they have the determination. As the French Foundation limousine sped on a highway, inside of it, the three members discussed Cyberslam. Primetime: Enough of the hype, enough of the buildup, Cyberslam is here. The French Foundation is in full force for this event. Mitch Wilson, you could have been here. You could have been something, had you not turned your back on us. Now you pay. If it's the last thing that I ever do in the fWo, if you kick my ass and end my career, which you won't, you will still be saying those two words. You will quit. Wilson, ever since you returned in January, I've wanted your no-charisma ass inside of the ring. I've wanted to pound you into oblivion for so long. Now's my chance. Seeing as you beat Eron, a real challenge if I'd ever seen one for the Internet Title, and held onto it, I not only get to kick your ass, but I also get to take a title from you. Sounds good, doesn't it? Courage: Yeah, baby! Now onto Courage's match, the match that the millions of idiot American fans will be tuning in to, and moving their large rear ends to the edge of the seat simply to see Courage in all his glory. Jackhammer? Sphere? Chronos? Fallen? Fear? Hellrazor? Star? ..and Courage. Pick the odd one out, no don't you moron, Courage will tell you the odd one out. Five idiot jobbers and one man of pure talent, charisma and good looks, and although it seems like a waste of Courage's time, you can trust Courage when he says that he will enjoy rockin' the worlds of those four morons, while trying to teach Lance that it's Courage who can make and break this team.. and it's Courage who will be the one to carry Lance his pathetic imitation of the Frenchie's costume to a tag-title reign. Lance, Courage realizes you've been on a hot streak and sure you've improved by teaming with Courage and trying to imitate the Frenchie, but now you've got a chance of a life time, baby, and if you blow it.. then Courage might just rock your world as well, baby, yeah! C'mon, baby! Pierre, any thoughts on that idiot copying your persona? Frenchie: Actually, I feel it's a step-up for Monsieur Star. Sure, he blows in every imaginable way, but at least his choice in role models is improving. And he's not much more dead weight zan Wilson, whom Primetime was able to carry to a handful of Tag Team Championships. I have ze utmost fait' in your ability to capture some gold for ze Foundation tomorrow, as much fait' as I have in Brain's ability to make Mitch cry like...well, Mitch, I suppose. But while ze two of you battle over fWo championships, I battle for somet'ing more. On Saturday, I fight ze good fight, taking on ze most vile, wicked, despicable competitor in ze fWo. Worse zan Spencer, more evil zan Dead. I speak of ze truest villain in fWo history. I speak of Ultra-Violet. Come CyberSlam, Purple One, one of us will pay ze ultimate price. One of us will lose somet'ing dear, eit'er your hair, or my mask. One of us will truly never be ze same again. You've beaten me before, Ultra-Violet, but zose were accidents, flukes. Saturday, I come prepared, bot' wit' a game plan and some sunglasses, so I can shield my delicate eyes from ze glare off of your newly shorn head. It goes wit'out saying zat we are terribly looking forward to zis weekend. It's CyberSlam. Ze biggest event of ze year. Primetime: ..And who else to rule it than the Foundation. Courage: C'mon baby! Fade
