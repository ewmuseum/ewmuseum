...revenge...

14 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Slaughter had concluded, quite a number of hours ago, when this segment was taped. The camera stays positioned in front of room 384, the room in which Primetime is temporarily occupying. Primetime had ordered all media and reporters, out of the room, so the camera shot was limited to the the outside corridor. Though as luck would have it, the door handle turns, and the door opens, revealing Courage, still dressed in his wrestling attire. Courage takes a few steps down the corridor, with a rare semi-serious expression, before the media swarm with microphones to the mouth.) Stand back! You people don't deserve to be in the ten mile radius of Courage, let alone the same room! Get Back! (The media takes a few steps back, and Courage smiles at the achievement, until a reporter voices a question.) "How's Primetime doing?" Shut up! Primetime, the real World Champion, lost his belt last Sunday. Was this because Slugger's Big Blonde Boyfriend was better than Primetime? Of course not! Was this because the World's Most Moronic Athlete managed to out-wrestle Primetime? Dream on! Was it because The Frenchie was just too skilled? Maybe, but not on this night. So why doesn't Primetime have the title!? I'll tell you why, The Boston Imbecile, Frankenstein, wanted revenge for not receiving his title shot! You know why your not getting you shot, Halloween boy? Not because we're scared, but because you would be an embarrassment to the fWo! To think that the amazing athlete in Primetime, would have to defend his precious title against some overgrown moron, like you! Not only would the fWo drop in ratings, the French Foundation clothing sales would also decline, because Primetime would have stepped in the ring with trash like you! (Courage smirks arrogantly once again, obviously feeling uplifted.) After the Big Blonde Boofhead escaped with the title, a title he robbed off the Foundation! Courage came to help his wounded allies. Frenchie and Primetime, the men who carried Slugger and Ice to a great match, the men who had put in every inch of effort to thrill the moronic American fans! Frenchie, the fighter that he is, declined Courage's help so Courage attempted to help Primetime... until Frankenstein appeared once again. Courage fought off the Boston Loser, but being the coward that he is, he attacked Courage from behind and locked him in his little modified-arm-bar. Boston Boy, at first it was just you and Primetime. Now it's because personal, baby! Whether it be next Slaughter, or next year, Courage is going to earn revenge for the Foundation! Strangler, you don't rock, you suck, and this will all be proven, when Courage proves that he is the greatest up and coming talent this promotion has to offer, by rocking your world! ..because the Foundation.. equals rock 'n roll, baby, yeah! (Courage finishes with the smirk, and then pushes the mass of cameras and reporters out of the way, as he barges through. Primetime continued to rest in hosinterviewsal, while it seemed Courage was seeking revenge.)
