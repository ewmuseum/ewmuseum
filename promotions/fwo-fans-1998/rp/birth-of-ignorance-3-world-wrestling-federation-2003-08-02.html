Birth Of Ignorance [3] World Wrestling Federation

2 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I was surrounded by ulterior motives. Vince Kinkade didn't want me to wrestle because of the negative effects my eye could render and Robert Hailer wanted my to wrestle because of the positive effects a freak like appearance could create. All I wanted was to be treated like everybody else but at every turn I was greeted by the same desire to point out and exploit my difference. I seeked out a man whom I thought would be more interested in my skills as a wrestler than the way I looked. We all make mistakes. February 18th 1994. The sky in Stamford cascaded across the magnificent mirror like Titan Towers as Kellen Kinkade stood outside admiring the view. The CWL had been a disappointing lesson to learn but now he'd gone to a promotion that didn't seem to be folding under the pressure of a new hardcore era, maybe he could find a future with them. Following routine, Kinkade pushed through the doors and made his way to reception... unlike The CWL however he was forced to wait in line with several other potential wrestlers who also had appointments with the owner of the company. "I'm here to see Vince McMahon." A mention to the receptionist had her flicking through her notes. "I'm sorry sir, Mr. McMahon is currently unavailable... if you would like to take a seat in like you will be called shortly." The receptionist spoke, not even looking at Kinkade as she reeled off well-rehearsed lines. Kellen made his way across the room and took a seat down in a line of potential wrestlers, all nervously fidgeting in one way or another. Some of them rocked back and forth, others bit their nails while a specific individual picked at the upholstery on his seat. "You do that to the furniture every time you're a guest somewhere?" Kinkade quipped in an attempt to make conversation. "Yeah man, I mean this has to be the shabbiest, crappiest, most uncomfortable seat I've ever sat on in my life... your typically poor American craftsmanship right here buddy, they take pride in their work when they make 'em back home." The individual turned to Kinkade and flashed a beaming smile before extending a hand "I'm Chris by the way, Chris from Canada." Kellen obliged and took up Chris on his handshake "Well Chris from Canada, keep it up and you're going to be paying for that seat out of your first months wages if you get signed." "Oh I'll get signed." Chris replied "The big Mac will be creaming his slacks when he sees me bust out the shooting star press I've got on tape." "Shooting star press huh? Pretty dangerous move to bust out." Kinkade raised an eyebrow as Chris simply smiled. "Not when you're a bad ass veteran of the ring like me, buddy." Chris replied "Didn't catch your name before." "Didn't give it." Kinkade smiled back "I'm Kellen, from New Jersey." Before the conversation could continue a woman opened a door and leant out onto the corridor "Is there a Chris Irvine here to see Mr. McMahon?" "That'd be me sweet cheeks." Chris replied before clenching his fist victoriously "C'mon baby! Wish me good luck my man." "Good luck." Kellen said with another smirk before leaning back in his seat and closing his eyes for a second to relax. It wasn't too long before his concentration was broken however. "WHAT THE HELL!?" The voice of Chris from Canada echoed through the halls "Unhand me at once, that wasn't even the whole five minutes you jackasses... the part where I threw a cup of water over him was building to something I swear!" The security guards stuffed him through the doors and Kellen watched as he left the building screaming "CONSPIRACY!" at the top of his voice. "Kellen Kinkade to see Mr. McMahon?" The same female who'd called in Chris spoke out once again and Kellen swiftly got to his feet and made his way down the hall to the office. As he entered the man he'd come to see was drying himself out with a few paper towels. "I keep telling you god damn it!" Vince barked at the security guards as they returned to the room "The second they reach for anything on my desk I want you to put them down, that guy was obviously a maniac... and a homosexual if we're counting the number of times he called me baby." Kellen stepped forward and extended his hand nervously "Good to meet you Mr. McMahon, I'm Kellen Kinkade." Vince looked at Kinkade's hand then up at his face. "I shake a lot of hands kid, what I want to know is what you're going to bring to the WWE... you've got five minutes so make it good." Vince replied, wiping the rest of the moisture from his shirt and tossing the paper towels in the trash. "Right... well." Kinkade took off his glasses and placed them into his pocket, prompting a strange stare from Vince. "Nice makeup you have there, looks very real... I think that could sell well." Vince pointed out. "It's not makeup." Kellen replied. "Excuse me?" Vince said with wide eyes "You mean to say that's a glass eye?" "Yeah." A quick answer came from Kellen "But I assure you-" "Hahah, come on kid... don't waste my time, there are real wrestlers waiting out there to show me what they've got." The sound of Vince's laughter was extremely grating as he shook his head and uttered to himself "Guy throwing water, guy with a glass eye... what next?" "I'm not wasting your time at all... my eye is-" Another intervention from McMahon prevented Kinkade from explaining any further. "Is a disability, now I'm sorry that you had an accident with your pencil at high school kid... but I don't have the time for this and the WWF is not a charity, the wrestling ring is a dangerous place to be when you're able bodied. I shudder to think about the number of accidents a blind guy could cause." Vince looked over Kinkade's shoulder to the woman standing by the door "Send the next one in." "But I'm not blind, you aren't listening to me... there's nothing with my injury that alters how I perform in the ring." Kellen stepped forward with his fists clenched tight; he wanted Vince to hear what he had to say. "The day a disabled person sets foot in a wrestling ring and performs at an anything but mediocre level is the day I'll eat my hat... now scram kid, I give a lot of money to charity and the needy already without you guys standing at my doorstep begging." McMahon scoffed. "You cheeky son of a-" Kinkade drew back his hand angrily but was soon restrained by the two guards in the room; Vince got to his feet and looked Kellen dead in the eye. "You want to hit me don't you kid? Well take a number and get in line because so do a lot of people, get this waste of time out of my sight." The two security guards pulled back Kellen as he shouted back into the room. "I know another guy called Vince you know." Kellen said, struggling as the guards continued to drag him. "So what?" An angry looking McMahon questioned. "I just thought I'd let you know... ...he's an asshole, just like you."
