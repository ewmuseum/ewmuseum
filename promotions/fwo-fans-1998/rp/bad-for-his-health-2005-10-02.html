Bad For His Health

2 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cabbot Wilson lay under the blankets of his bed and stared at the ceiling of his overpriced hotel room. It was like the Japanese knew he was American and made an extra effort to mess with the Gaijin. They soaked him for the hotel room, and were probably responsible for the bad fish he had for supper. After spending the past three hours unloading his body through one cavity or another, Cabbot just wanted to ride the cold chills out until he could fall asleep. The food poisoning was the least of Cabbot's worries, but he tried to block it out. The last thing he wanted to do was think about all the stress he was under. Getting his brain all worked up would only keep him awake. Sleep, that's what the doctor ordered. Bed rest was the only thing Cabbot wanted to hear about, at least until he could travel to Osaka for the next show. It was getting harder to rein in his men, and it took all his strength to do so. Kodiak Vic Creed was barely under control to begin with. Now, with all these ideas of his daughter being alive, his behavior was erratic at best. He'd miss meetings, workout sessions, and disappear without telling Cabbot where he was going. Vic knew people in these parts, so it was relatively easy for him to vanish if he wanted to. His behavior was affecting the bottom line, too. Cabbot wasn't making much off the Main Man. Whenever Vic had a chance to step up for a belt, he kept blowing it. It was all Cabbot could do to keep Creed in any title picture, but even those opportunities were starting to dry up. Vic was a wildfire raging out of control, and it was only a matter of time before he burned himself out. That left Nick Brandish as a source of income. A poor source at that. It was becoming clear that Nick wasn't taking the meds prescribed for him. Cabbot was earning a nice little stipend simply by making sure Nick was taking his pills. Once word got around that Nick wasn't taking his medication, the money would vanish as well. The other drawback to Brandish not taking his meds was that Nick's illness would not be held in check. The Monster was sick, and if he was allowed to roam the fWo halls without being medicated it would be like giving a loaded gun to a monkey. It was a dangerous situation. Shawn Stewart had been snooping around into Nick's past. If anyone else started looking into his history, or if Nick did something he shouldn't, it would definitely reduce his earning capability. It was getting close to crunch time for Cabbot Wilson, and that though made his stomach gurgle under the sheets. When he borrowed the chunk of cash from the loan sharks, he was confident he'd be able to repay them in short order. Vic would win a title, Cabbot would make his investment back, and repay his debt with interest. But when it was clear that Creed wouldn't be winning a belt anytime soon, Cabbot desperately signed Nick to a contract. Now it appeared that Wilson was 0-2 with his talent, and deadlines were getting very close. "Have my fucking money," Cabbot had been told last week. "Or Tommy is going to pay you a visit." Tommy, a caveman crammed into a suit, was known to pry kneecaps off with a crowbar. Cabbot had grown attached to his knees and wasn't looking to lose them. So, stomach and bowel aside, Cabbot knew he had to make something happen and soon. If Tommy came knocking, Cabbot would be doing his managing from a wheelchair. For starters. Wilson bolted out of bed and dashed to the bathroom. Maybe it wasn't the fish that was making him sick after all.
