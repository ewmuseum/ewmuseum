...and they keep turning...

30 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Any idea when you're coming back onboard?" 'Triple X' Sean Stevens considered the question for just a minute before taking a long drink of beer. "Don't really know... soon, I guess. You know how it is after a long run of shows, after a big event like Cyberslam - just need to stop a minute and catch your breath." As Eli took a drink of his own, he nodded in agreement. "I know what'cha mean, kid... lord knows I could use a break, myself." "And... you're not taking one." "Can't. I've got a family to support, and until Angel gets a new contract from someone - ANYONE - I'm the only one who can keep the money flowing into the studio." They've been sitting at the bar - a little place down by the Baltimore waterfront - for a little more than an hour now. Mostly talking about nothing, everything, and all the little things inbetween. Eli Flair and Sean Stevens have known each other for four years now, and have been what you could call good friends for at least half that time. In the six months or so since the fWo came back, however, they've had little to no time to just hang out and chill. But, as the saying goes, if something is important, you make the time. "No news yet on whether or not they're gonna get another deal?" asked Trip, "I can't believe they're unsigned for this long now... too damn good for that." "It's been rough on Angel," agreed Eli, "especially since I can't be there to take some'a the pressure of the baby off her hands." Trip nodded. He's only 'o-fish-ally' met Mariella Jade once, but the affect she had on her daddy, the scary/tough brooding King of Extreme was nothing short of spectacular. MJ and Angel have truly saved his life - and Trip can only imagine what he would be like as a father someday. Someday far away he mentally corrected - one false alarm this year with Poison Ivy was enough... when they do have kids he'd like to be able to plan it. "Anyways," continued Trip, shaking the thoughts from his head, "you're probably wondering why I haven't been travelling with you guys since Cyberslam." "Not really," replied Eli, "I just got back on the road myself... but I know Ivy's been wondering. Now that'cha mention it, tho... s'up?" Trip raised his hand toward the bar - two more beers. "It's this whole 'Rat Pack' thing... I don't think it's something I should be a part of anymore," said Trip, looking down, but suddenly cutting himself off, "Don't get me wrong, I love Ivy like nobody's business, and Lance is my boy, always up for giving Team EQ a shot in the arm... and you and me go way back. But I think--" "--that it's served its purpose," interrupted Eli, "You're right." "How d'you mean?" "Think about what's gone down so far... you, me, Lance, Brandon, Ivy... we go back to last Spring and the house show circuit, just 'cuz we had a rockin' good time. And I'd like to think that we all hooked up again last fall for mostly the same reason... but also because of the situation with Dudley and company." "Watching each other's backs," finished Trip, understanding. "Exactly," agreed Eli, "Back in November when Dudley was running things, it was necessary." "And now?" "Now?" Good question. "Remember coming into New York last week? Fox and Smooth not telling us the truth notwithstanding, Cyberslam being our final date notwithstanding, this company was almost completely united. And even the ones who aren't really part of the family... everyone wanted everyone else to have the match of their lives. Point of order, it's not like you and I need something like a Rat Pack to go out for a beer. It's not like you and Ivy need a Rat Pack to be together... you and Alex certainly don't need a Rat Pack to go back out and win the Tag Team belts again if you wanted to, just because you could.. Trip laughed. Eli had a point. "Lance is my boy, EJ... you've always been my boy. And I love Ivy, you know that. But right now I need to be on my own and see where it can take me." "Hey, don't need to convince me," replied Eli, "You convinced me years ago that, on your own, you can go as far as your ambition can see. Just remember, when they declare you the greatest World Champion that the eff-Dub has ever seen... who drove ya to that point, aight? If it wasn't for me kicking your ass time and time again you wouldn't have pushed yourself this hard." "Careful, old man," cautioned Trip, "or we might have to throw down right here. I think your lucky streak's over." "Oh, I know better," replied Eli with a laugh, "this old man's gettin' brittle, kiddo. But any time you wanna work, you know y'just gotta drop me a line, cool?" "Cool." The history flew between these two men, from the brutal matches to the Perfect Circle to the CWL "shoot" to the Rat Pack and everything in between...leaves one to wonder. Can the ending ever truly be written?
