The Journey (Prologue)

1 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Monday, December 31, 2001 Los Angeles, California 11:32 PM Pacific As a hotel, it wasn't what Scott Wasner was used to. During his last stint in the fWo, Wasner made a point of using his own money to stay in the finest hotels available. Last time he had been in Los Angeles, he had stayed at the Royal Biltmore. Even when there was no true luxury hotel to be found, he would at the very least be found in a suite. Not this time. He still had his paycheck, and it was still large. Though he was no longer the promotion's top earner like he once was, the check was still plenty big, based on his name. Still seven digits. But now, as opposed to in the past, he saw better things to do with the money than to spend it for the sake of spending it. This time he was at the Radisson, on the fWo tab. Not exactly a hell-hole, but not half the splendor he had been used to in the past. No big deal, though. God knows it was better than where I was three months ago. Wasner's mouth turned up in a smile. Yeah, he had been down and out, but it had led to the chance meeting that had turned his life around. Stephanie. The beautiful woman he was on the phone with right now, in fact. "Yeah, Steph, they showed me the booking sheet... next time we come back home should be in, uh... February. ... Yeah, I know. Two months. And hell, we're going overseas in a couple days. Next show's from Tokyo. ... Yeah, no fooling. Tokyo. They got us going all over the place... I tell you what, I hope travel doesn't suck as much as I remember it sucking. Hey, Tony
