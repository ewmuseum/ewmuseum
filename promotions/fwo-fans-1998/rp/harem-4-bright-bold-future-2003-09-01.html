Harem - 4 - Bright, bold future

1 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"What is it about us, Mick," asked TJ, "that constantly has club owners tellin' us that we blew away the headliners, but we never get headliner's money?" Mickey Rodriguez laughed at his bassist as he restrung his guitar. "Price ya gotta pay, kiddo." "Oh, great... there goes the neighborhood." Mickey looked over his shoulder where TJ's gaze rested. Angel had just boarded the bus, with a friendly redheaded face behind her. "Jeez, Angel," said TJ, "No wonder we're still small potatoes... look who you're hangin' around with." Amy shot him the finger as the bassist got up to give her a hug. "How's it been, fireball?" "Tiring. Too fast. Lots of blood." She grinned, "How about you, winning any VMAs yet?" "It's all politics," replied TJ, "and since, on principle, we won't collaborate with 50 Cent, we're at a disadvantage." Angel laughed. "Amy's gonna crash with us for a day or two, that cool with everyone?" A muffled "Sure," came from the bunks. "Thanks, Nine," replied TJ, "but some of us don't like to associate with riffraff." "Oh, but we put up with you," reminded Angel with a laugh, "Couch good, Amy?" Aimz smiled, "Sounds fine." She quickly scanned 'the boys'; So male, so content...so deliciously unprepared for a mood-swinging redhead. "You sure you shouldn't warn them or something? Just don't want someone waking up without their balls." Quite suddenly, everybody turned to the two women with a slightly alarmed expression. Warning? You'd think the majority - the men - would be in charge...you really would. Wrong! "Don't forget where I lived the entire time, kiddo," reminded Angel, "They know what's up. Speaking of which..." Angel headed into the living quarters as Amy sat down on the couch. Mickey's LP was next to her, freshly restrung as he tuned the accoustic on the floor. "Go'head," he said, watching her eyeball the axe, "It's cool. What's been up with ya lately?" She picked up the guitar but didn't pluck a string, simply happy to have the familliar slab of wood in her arms. "Got engaged...found out I've been pregnant the whole time...can't eat fast food...becoming tempted to resort to cannibalism." Amy chuckled, "Y'know, the usual." "Pregnant, huh?" asked TJ, "Mick? A moment?" The two musicians slunk off about six inches. "Can we survive another pregnancy on this bus?" asked TJ, loud enough for Aimz to hear, "Even for a night or two?" "Don't worry," replied Mickey, "She couldn't possibly be any worse than Angel." "Someone say my name?" asked Angel, emerging with a very awake MJ in her arms, "Listen, I know it's a nice night because we've got two gigs in the same town back to back, but we could all still use some sleep, am I right?" She directed her attention towards Aimz, "And I think you've been looking for this little angel to say hello to for quite some time." "Ah!!!" Amy's smile was ten miles wide as Angel handed MJ over. "She's like...got hair and stuff!" Angel raised an eyebrow and laughed at the giddy redhead. "Well...more than usual! You know what I mean." She bit her lip, looking into the smiling eyes of the miniature angel. "She's all...small. Really small. See, now it feels weird to say 'I want one!' every time I see something cute, because I've got this coming to me." The rest sat silently, letting Aimz talk to herself for a moment before she called upon Angel with a glance. "How is she?" "Teething," smiled Angel, "and getting bigger and happier by the day." Amy nearly melted at MJ's grin. "She's sleeping through the night but not so much during the day anymore, so that's good. I can't believe she's nearly nine months old already. It seems like yesterday." "You'll be saying that two years from now, when BL and I have three kids in school and another in this exact position..." Aimz laughed, "And little MJ's gonna have her very own attitude. Now that will be pay-per-view material." Angel laughed. "Pay per view? Naaah... this one's not gonna be a wrestler. I wouldn't mind her learning some things, let Ivy teach her how to defend herself and all, and I'd love if she had an interest in music, but this one's not gonna be a wrestler or a musician... she's gonna be someone important. President, maybe. Or an ambassador to some other country." "Here we go again," said TJ with a laugh, "I need a drink, anyone want anything while I'm out?" Amy herself couldn't help but chuckle, though she smacked TJ on the side on behalf of the just-out-of-reach Gothic Diva. "Maybe not wrestlers..." She began, "But musicians are pretty important people. I'm sure you're gonna let her be what she dreams of being, that's an important enough person for a little girl to strive to be. Me, on the other hand..." The redhead smiled, patting her own still-slim stomach. "Well, this one's gonna be king of something. Or at least marry rich." "Hah!" Angel laughed, "Marry rich. That never happens. I could've married rich, stayed in Spencer, and been an alcoholic underweight housewife to a cheating husband, but what would that've gotten me?" A thoughtful look crossed her face as she took MJ back from Amy and kissed her daughter's chubby cheek. "This one's gonna have some cash from her parents, but she's gonna make her own fortune." She suddenly looked back at Amy. "Ever get the feeling that the men in your business are intimidated?" Amy nodded vehemently, smiling as she sprawled back in the little 'kitchen' booth. "Guys still have that old school mentality. It's bred down or something. Basically, if they see a girl doing something better or almost as good as they're doing, they either downplay that act or attack it directly. It's just part of having tits in society today." "Well," said Angel with a firm tone as she handed MJ back to Amy, "This isn't going to happen with our children." She opened up the mini- fridge and pulled out two bottles of Strawberry Melon CC that they procured last time they went north of the border. "My daughter is going to make the boys cry when she beats them at everything, and your daughter will be her best friend. Or your son will beat up everyone that's mean to my daughter along with her and they'll just own the playground." "With Eli Flair as her dad, at least you don't need to worry about too many scumbags asking her out." "EJ is actually looking forward to MJ's first date." She cracked the bottle and took a sip. "Do you know he's actually planning on sabotaging something in her room so she won't be ready by the time the poor boy is over to pick her up? Imagine this for a second: a six foot nine man, three hundred pounds of muscle, scar tissue, and tattoos... you're a skinny fifteen year old highschool sophomore who's being told to treat that man's daughter with respect?" Amy and Angel looked at each other. "I never went to a prom, Amy...and at this rate I doubt MJ is, either." Ah, the magic of fatherhood. Two children, two oversized fathers... ...Imagine Brandon's reaction!
