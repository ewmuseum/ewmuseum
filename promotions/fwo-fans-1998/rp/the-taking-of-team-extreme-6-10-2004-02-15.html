The Taking of Team Extreme - 6/10

The Taking of Team Extreme Chapter Six December 31st, 2003 Los Angeles, CA 6:52 PM local time "This is it," said Eli, "Tonight is the night. Brand wins tonight or everything is shot to hell." "You said that a month ago," laughed Angel. The two sat on their sofa in the middle of their Hollywood condo, watching the fWo's Countdown to Oblivion 1 event. In the past two weeks, Disney has purchased the fWo and placed Silver Fox in charge, with the stipulation that nobody could fire him, and he would be able to name his own successor due to death or retirement. Everything seemed perfect. Including the fact that Poison Ivy was once again on top of the world, as she introduced Knox and Gonzales to wrestle in front of the world for the first time. And he just knew... that Rosalyn Callasantos would get the biggest kick in the world over being seen on TV talking to Ivy. "Still," continued Eli, "even with the way things've twisted and turned, it's stayed pretty damn interesting. But the fact remains, tonight is Ground Zero. Brand doesn't win tonight and he's blown his chances of ever being Champion." "What if he doesn't want to be?" "Hmmm?" "You remember what Brandon told us about himself and Vic, right? About his family, and all that crap? What makes either of us think Brandon even wants to be champion? He might be just as happy getting to hurt Creed." Eli shook his head. "No way. He's a hero, the hero is supposed to triumph. Or..." "Or what?" He hesitated. "Or... my own sacrifices weren't worth it." Eli picked up Angel's bare arm and kissed the Omega-scar on the wrist. "And your sacrifices weren't worth it, either." She blushed. True, it had never been proven that KVC had anything to do with Donaven Winters' attack on the two back in July, but solely in the vein of straight-up villain, they were easily interchangeable. They were jolted out of their daze by the telephone. Angel reached back and grabbed the receiver. "Hello?" "Angel?" She sat up. "Sam?" "Yeah, it's me. I need to speak to Eli." "Uhm, sure," she said, confused, "Hold on." She handed Eli the phone, "It's for you. Sam Smooth." Eli raised an eyebrow as he took the phone. "Sam?" "Yeah, it's me. Listen, apologize to Angel for me, I just don't have time." "Sup." "Do you know if any of those kids have cells?" "What?" "We can't get in touch with Ivy, and Twitch told me someone saw Neo just a little while ago." Eli's breath quickened. "Don't know about the kids, per se," he advised, "but you could try Coop's, or you could call Ivy's place or April's place - all of 'em should be in Ivy's 'emergency call' folder. I know Fox's got Coops' number at least." He racked his brain. "It's New Year's, but these are still kids. And I know Cally just turned seventeen... her mom won't let her stay out too late. Callasantos. They're listed." "Thanks," replied Smooth, "Most likely it's nothing, but we don't want to take any chances." "Definitely," agreed Eli, "And Ivy'll appreciate the concern." A moment of silence. "Want me to try to find her?" "No, don't worry 'bout it," replied Smooth, "Ain't much use worryin' when you're on one coast and we're on another. We'll find 'er and tell'er to call you." "Aight, Smooth," replied Eli, "thanks." "Good to hear your voice, E..." trailed Smooth's voice as he hung up the phone. Eli did the same, and leaned back, his arm tightly around his wife. "What's the matter?" asked Angel, "Smooth sounded really upset." "I don't know," replied Eli, "and it's driving me crazy." The anxiety that started in the pit of his stomach continued to grow. He knew exactly where Ivy would be - Center Field - and yet he felt anxious. "I don't know," he repeated, "I just don't know..." Two hours until midnight struck the East Coast. Two Hours Until 0:00:00...
