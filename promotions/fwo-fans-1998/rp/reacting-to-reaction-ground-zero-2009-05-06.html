Reacting to reAction - Ground Zero

6 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rosie and I got to the arena pretty early. They were having a pre - show meeting today at noon. Well, what it looked like was they were having lunch at noon, with a semi - formal discussion on what's happening tonight and any questions. I looked around the room and didn't see Killjoy anywhere, but of course, since none of us know who he is, he could very well be here. One thing that surprised me, is that the big board filled with the night's schedule of events has Killjoy and myself opening up with a no-disqualification match. I wasn't prepared for that. From the look on Miss Ivy's face, neither was she. "What's that mean," whispered Rose, as she leaned in to me, "you two can do whatever you want to each other?" That's exactly what it means, I replied, as I put my arm around her, no holds barred. What I didn't tell her is that all of Killjoy's matches, so far, might as well have been no holds barred. What I didn't say is that, with no restraints, there's really no telling what Killjoy is going to try to do. What I did say, was, the match tonight is probably going to define my rookie year here in the FWO. I gave Rose a gentle squeeze and took a look around the room. Virtually ninety nine percent of the people here, I'd met and spoken to and either we were cool, or were at least in agreement not to bother each other unless a problem came out in the ring, but I was still something of a loner here, content to do my own thing and not get in anyone's way. Wait, what was that? William just said something about May 20th and a free wrestling day? Cool. Why does Miss Ivy have her head in her hands? There were a few sore spots, like Xin Xin Xiong and the briefcase shot he gave me that was wholly unnecessary, and Vince Jacobs attacking me after the match because he was sad he lost. At least SARS the Clown had the decency to just run away after he lost one match. Overall, I don't want to think of anyone in here as Killjoy. Living that kind of double life, willing to destroy the FWO in order to save it, is more than I think most of us would be capable of. "Knox?" asked William. I'm sorry, what, I replied. I wasn't even half - listening. "Before your match, we'd like you to give us three minutes on the stick," said William, "is that okay?" Live interview, huh? No problem, I said. There's a first time for everything. "Okay," said William to everyone in general, "let's have a good show tonight. I'll be in my office for the next three hours if there's any questions." He held up his hands, and everyone just sort of dispersed. "Let's go to the ring," said Rosie, suddenly, "I've never been in the ring in anything bigger than a gymnasium and I want to get some perspective." Sure, I said. Anything to take my mind off tonight. Anything to try and put as much cushion as possible between myself and ground zero. Because it's coming, far too quickly.
