Respect (8 - Stipulations)

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A new day, a whole new world of pain. In spite of the fact that my arms still didn't feel like they weren't continually being wrenched from their sockets, and that the nasty headache from the night before had carried over to this morning, I still found myself in a rather upbeat mood. I had arrived at the arena early, so that I could get to promotions in hopes of getting another pre-show promo in to set up my match with Trips for the evening. When I arrived, I was relieved that Geoff had agreed to fit me in, and so I sat, waiting for my turn to come. I had only finished four hands of Blackjack on my cell phone when Geoff yelled for me. Hastily switching it off and slipping it into the hip pocket of my jeans, I stood up, stretching my shoulders a bit so that every gesture I made with my hands in front of the camera wasn't accompanied by a grimace of pain. Striding in front of the Countdown backdrop, one thought immediately struck me: I wouldn't be able to do this. "Geoff, do you think you can reposition that light, even just a little bit? I hit my head pretty hard last night, and it's playing hell with my head every time I look towards the camera." "Sure. It's a lot easier now that we don't have to worry about the gleam off of your bald head, kid," Geoff replied jokingly, and I gave him the expected smile and even went so far as to tell him that he was number one in my book. Laughing, Geoff had one of the techs turn the light until I could look directly at the camera. "That's gonna give this shot a nice mood if you're gonna come across as a hardass, kid." "Perfect," I replied, and my smile this time around was much more sincere. "Ready, kid? In 4...3...," Geoff began, before ticking the final two seconds off with his fingers. Showtime. "Sean, we've had three matches since the fWo came back in October, and in spite of our Countdown series being tied, you've still got a two-to-one advantage over me. Tonight, I'm going to change all that." "Tonight's match won't be just any match. I'm looking to kill two birds with one stone
