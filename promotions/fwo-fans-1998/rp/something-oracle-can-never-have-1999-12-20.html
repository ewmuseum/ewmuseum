Something Oracle can never have

20 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
((The scene goes to what looks to be outside at 5PM in Hamilton, as its in a parking lot. At the Parking lot in front of Copps Coliseum, there is one specific car out there, as its the car of Eron the Relentless, license plate being "UB6 IB9" as he sits in the Corvette, smiling in the nippy cold air, which would be colder had it not been for Global Warming. Eron sits in the car, feet up on the dashboard, listening to "old-school nine inch nails" as "Down in it" plays. Eron turns the sound down as he sees Geoff Green get closer to the car. Eron sneers, but then welcomes Geoff as he turns around for him)) Eron the Relentless:Hi Geoff Geoff Green:Umm hi. Why havent you left Hamilton yet? Eron the Relentless:Ohh I thought I'd stay ya know, maybe just think about how Christmas is so close and how I gotta make plans soon. Also, the big last Warfares of the Millenium are happening, and since everyone will be in at least one of those shows, I have to plan for it. Since I do expect to be still holding the fWo International Title in 2000. Hell, if I can get through a psycho bitch, how the hell is a crippled weak fragile jerry-atric gonna take the title away from me? Geoff Green:Have you thought of the cards he has for this match? Eron the Relentless:Hell yeah, I may be young Geoff but im not stupid. Actually, youth of today, including myself, are smarter political then you freakshow middle aged losers. Oracle has never been a long time planner, its not in his blood. I dont even know why the hell he got into the fWo in the first place, what was his acheivements? Made 50 bucks a month off of 3.99 per minute Psychic Readings? Out of every wrestler in the fWo whos aged this past while, why is he the feeble one to try to take my fWo International Title? Why cant I have someone young take me on for the title? Briana? Nooooo I have to fight Oracle, whos only plan really is maybe an interference by Prophet. Hoo, those little interferences always helped. Lets take a trip down memory lane shall we? I had a slew of fWo Guys who hated me back in the day come out and help Oracle do his job, and I still beat him. Oracle cant beat me alone, with Prophet, will a Faction of Wrestlers, with anything. I am invincible to Oracle. Plan and simple, shut the book. Geoff Green:Wow, you're confident about this match Eron the Relentless:Damn straight I am. Im tired of being pissed off with such small challenges like this which somehow get on Slaughter and Warfare cards. I can fight a Hardcore Match for the International Title. I can sure as hell take a Ladder Match. And besides, Oracle just somehow, out of nowhere, comes out, claims I sold out and says he should have been International Champion? Whos he kidding? Anyways, Im gonna take Oracle, at Twilight, and show him not only what its like to fight a real Champion, but whats it like to fight a match with a man whos name itself is written down as a Legend. Geoff Green:Some people say its not fair to pick on Oracle due to age, especially since hes only 34. Do you feel you are taking the whole "Oracle is old" act too far? Eron the Relentless:Do I ever take things too far? No. And how the hell can you tell me 34 isnt an old age? Im turning 20 in 2 months for christ sakes, hes basically 15 years older than me! Do the math Geoff, he could be my Dad. Hell, my dads 40, and hes already in a Home. Geoff Green:You put him in a home? Eron the Relentless:It was for the best of our interest... Geoff Green:So you say, that Oracle is old? Eron the Relentless:Hes not just old in his body, but his mind is feeble too. Hell, thats why hes never won an fWo Singles Title. All he could muster was TCW World. Not much I say. He needed another brainless twit, who couldnt see I was using him, to just win himself the Tag Team Titles! Then he lost it soon after that, because im such a bad omen hehehe. Ahhh Soothsayer, even Mike Heftel can think up a better gimmick. That was Oracles idea and his alone. I just went along with it, because I knew my future. My future was here, and hell, Oracles seeing why his 3.99 per minute Fortune Telling Line got such craptacular reviews. CAUSE HES A FRAUD. And frauds cant be champions. Which all in all, means the fWo International Title, and a Singles Victory, is something Oracle, can never have. And if he feels Im being unfair, or underestimating what years of playing with his Crystal Balls can do, then he can just plainly take a breath...of fresh death. As for me, San Jose here I come. ((Eron gets his feet off the dashboard, brushes off his Bubble Jacket, and revs up the engine, as he drives out of the Parking lot, as Geoff just stands there, as the camera fades to black...))
