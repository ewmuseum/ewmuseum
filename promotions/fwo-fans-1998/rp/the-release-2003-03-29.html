The Release

29 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kali Saturos was trapped in the void, as if she was a caged animal. Nowhere to go. Nothing to do. It was slowly weighing deep on her mind, sending deeper cracks into her already-weak mental state. It would not hold for much longer. Sanity was not something she could depend on anymore. The only thing keeping her going was her own competitive desire. Her own desire to be the best. To be on top of the world. To be the one....to live. She rocked back and forth in the fetal position. Looking around, up, down, anywhere, all she could she was black. She desperatley wanted to see color again, she desperatley wanted to breathe air again, and she desperatley wanted to see the sunlight, the moonlight, the pale reflection of her figure in the water. She wanted to see anything but the pure and unholy black that surronded her now. "You are not .Three." .Two spoke with a slight laugh. "You are a weak and foolish child who believes herself to be something more than she is." Kali would not answer. She simply looked the other way. It was dark, as if there had never been anything there. But she knew differently. "Why will you not answer me, Kali?" .Two said, that dark grin creeping onto her face ever-so-slowly. "Do you not dispute my claims?" Kali turned her head around. "I am .Three, The Killer, The Third." Kali stated matter-of-factly. "He told me so himself." "Who told you so?" .Two asked her. Kali turned her head away again. "Chaos told me so." she replied. That elicted another bone-chilling laugh from the woman known as The Prophet. "You are a foolish girl, Kali." .Two said. "Chaos only speaks to me. You know that, The First knows that, and I know that. Why do you dispute this now?" Kali gave no response. .Two crossed her arms over her chest. "Good then." .Two said. "Maybe when Rei replaces you, you will have something to say when you are locked in here for all of eternity." That elicted a response from Kail. She turned her head with lightning-quick speed. "You would not do it." Kali said. "Rei would not obey you. More importantly, she would not obey Chaos." "She would be less of an annoyance than you." .Two snapped. "Oh well. I guess we will just have to find out then, won't we? Have fun in the darkness, Kali. I won't miss you. And I doubt anyone else will." Thoughts ran through Kali's head as she experienced the flashback. Why am I still here? Why haven't I been released yet? This is not fair. This is inhumane and unjust and- .Two came in through the void. "Just the bitch I wanted to see." Kali said with a roll of her eyes. "You should want to see me." .Two replied. "I am here to release you." "About fucking time." Kali snapped. "I knew you couldn't keep me here this long without losing to .One. That's not the rules, now are they?" .Two crossed her arms. "No, they're not." .Two said. "When Chaos assumes his rightful position as supreme diety, we will have to change that." Kali did not reply to this. She simply stood up and walked out of the void. .Two let her arms fall to her sides once again as she watched the spot where Kali had once sat. "Do I underestimate you, or overestimate you, Kali Saturos? I suppose we shall find that out tommorow night."
