3 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For the next couple of weeks Tim came and gone as he pleased, training to become a fWo wrestler, of course behind the back of Skylar Chryst. He knew that if Skylar found out that he was training to become an fWo wrestler he would kill him. It was a daily event for Skylar to ask Tim where he was going and day after day Tim had to lie to him, it hurt him more then anything, lying to his only friend in the whole world. But, with each lie that came, he came closer and closer to becoming a better wrestler. Now that the fWo was closed down for a while Tim's chances at getting a shot at the fWo were pretty much shattered. Tim was kind of relieved because now he didn't have to worry about Skylar finding out about Tim's secret life so to say. It was just an average day, I was getting ready to pack up and leave for another tough day of training. Skylar was eating breakfast before he had to rush off to his work for the day. There was a cold silence between the two men as Tim felt bad for lying, but little did Tim know Skylar had no idea what was going on. Tim tried to break the awkward silence between them by asking Skylar a series of questions about his day, Skylar answered them back and Tim still felt uncomfortable. Tim then decided that he would leave early for his training. "You know what? I think I am just going to go now hopefully something will work out today." Tim said to Skylar as Skylar slurped down his glass of milk. "All right see you later on tonight." Skylar replied back, the way Skylar talked to Tim made Tim sick. Sick to his stomach, how could Tim be like this? Was Tim being selfish for once? Tim grabbed his bags and his jacket and left the house for the day, as was Skylar was supposed to do too. The day was over and Tim had a tough day of training, after his tough day at the ring he decided to go get a few beers before heading back to the house for dinner with Skylar. That night Tim got home a few hours later then normal. He opened the door to the house and saw Skylar sitting on the couch. It was 8:00 pm Skylar normally didn't get home till 8:30 pm. Skylar sat on the couch looking like he had just lost his puppy. "What's wrong?" Tim questioned Skylar. But, Skylar didn't reply back. Skylar sat on the couch looking straight at the TV, which wasn't getting a reception. He sat there looking stoned with the phone in his hands. Tim walked over and noticed a message on the answer machine. "Hey Tim, this is Silver Fox down from the Orlando Development League, it appears that the Fans Wrestling Organization is starting up and we want you to join the roster. If you're interested in it, just call me back you know the number."
