a conversation with yourself.1-thepast

8 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There's nothing but light surrounding me. For the first time ever, I feel warm. Safe. Loved. But it's ripped open, by the black! All around me, everything black! I can't even breathe in this hole, won't someone let me out? Doesn't anyone have a little compassion? Can't someone reach down and pull me out? There's someone. They're really nice, and pretty, and I think they might just be "the one". She's got a mask, and she smells like blueberries. I love her. No, I hate her! The scent is gone, the innocence is gone, there's just this evil.....thing left. Get her away from me! I don't want to see her ever again! I'm alone again, but everything's white again. It's so warm in here. I could hug myself and never let go. They're banging on my door, trying to get me out. They need me, they say. Pssh. They never needed me, no matter how many times I bled for them, it always went unnoticed. She made me bleed for her, but I didn't want to. I swear! No, come back, I want to help you after all! Let me out of here! The warmth, it's too much, it's suffocating! I think, I think I'm going to die! I'm not dead, but I wish I was. I'm wandering aimlessly, and I can't find the light again. I'll never find the light again. It turned against me, it rejected me. They turned against me. They rejected me. I'll show them. I'll show everyone! What is your name? Voran? I think you can help me. Together, we'll show them all. We'll make them all pay. Look at the blood that's stained my hands now, Voran, this is too much! How far do you want to take this? What do we have to do to make it all go away? No, that's too much, I can't do this anymore Voran! I'm not you anymore! I'm me again. Just me. Just Xias. No more Voran. I've created my own little pocket of light, my own little happiness. I'm happy, finally I'm happy. But I'm not. It's just an artificial light- an artificial happiness. I need to get out of here. I need to find the real light again. She's keeping me here! I'll destroy her! I'll make her take me to the light! I'm finally here. I'm finally here, and I'm never leaving. There's nothing you can do to stop me from wrapping myself up in the whiteness. And there's nothing you can do to bring me back. I'm gone for good. Goodbye.
