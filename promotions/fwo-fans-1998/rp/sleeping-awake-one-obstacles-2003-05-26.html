Sleeping Awake (One) - Obstacles

26 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tuesday May 20th, 2003 9:19am Residence of Brandon Roche "GAH!" The light barely caught Amy's eye before she ducked back down under the massive, down-filled comforter. To stir, stretch and in return be blinded was not the most pleasant way to wake up. Especially when, seconds later, the empty space would remind you that your special treat of the morning was probably to spend it alone, accompanied by three small children. ...No, wait... Silence. Silence meant they weren't awake yet. Silence meant a moment to think! Normally, Amy would've been up and out of bed, wondering why she'd slept for more than an hour. Instead she laid there, equisitely aware that she was alone in the room as she relished the quiet. Wait...that sure doesn't fit. Her eyes flew open: She was enjoying peace and quiet? No chaos, nothing interesting? "...Wow..." Unsure that she'd really even verbalized the astonishment, Aimz sat up with a bemused smirk. Life actually seemed to suddenly supply her with time to waste, time to savor...what the hell was that about? Whatever had happened, it didn't please her in the least: Amy was not the sort of person who enjoyed 'slowing down'...and that seems to be what her ribs have forced. No, what Kellen Kinkade had forced! Ah, there it was again - Plain-faced anger. A good rage...no more stillness but the sudden locomotion, surge of kinetic energy and a gorgeous thud of impact when her fist hit the night table. The way she yelped and jolted at the pain reminded her suddenly why she'd slept so long. Nightmares all night long, all slowly streaming back bit by bit to bite her in the ass. So much going on, but so little she could do about it...that's what killed Amy. Not just Kellen, either... There was Lance Knight, too...but what was that? Sympathy, pity...guilt? Maybe anger, some sort of rage driven by his seemingly stupid jealousy...silly pride that, for once in her life, someone envied Amy Campbell? ...Or maybe she just wanted to break his nose. Either way! Actually, nothing was quite so simple. She certainly did want to break his nose, but it seemed like the pity, rage and pride were all bottled into one on the subject...mostly rage, though - just judging by words he chose on Slaughter. She hated two-faced 'friends' about as much as she hated... ...Interruption. A knock came to Amy's door much to her chagrin. Just then moving about and still too stiff to move, the thought of Brandon's kids jumping all over her didn't sit well. Not responding, hoping the 'knocker' would take the hint and go away, the redhead paused - fearful of even disrupting the empty, motionless atmosphere with a breath. A rap came again, causing Amy to jump once more. The heavy hand on the door could only mean one person though, so her nerves settled as quickly as they were riled - it wasn't one of the toddlers. "Amy? Lazy ass? You in there? You realize it's another day don't you? We were supposed to take in the sights today..." The old door shuddered a bit as Brandon turned the old knob and entered on his own accord. Amy grinned at the sight of the Paragon, but avoided a hug of sorts to put rest to her ribs. "I think I'm gonna stick around here today..." She yawned, "I'm just a bit sore...unless you're gonna make me stay with the kids...nothing before them had ever tired me out." "Well, I'm never going to make you do anything, not my style and all. We don't have to do the sight seeing thing. It's all been around so long, I'm sure it's not going anywhere. We can just chill at home. Make it a Blockbuster night or whatnot. Your call." The redhead sighed and slumped down against the wall. Since CyberSlam, she'd barely left this house. "I guess it's home again." Another sigh came, this time considerably more frustrated. "I wish my damned ribs would just heal! If it weren't for your fuck-up buddy and then Kinkade, they'd be fine by now..." "I... well, you're not going to get an argument out of me. I agree. You know, shouldering some of the blame yourself never hurt anyone." With a wink Brandon wanted to put that point home but move on without much of an argument. "If you stayed away from the show until you were better that would help as well..." "Alex hasn't been himself though, you're right about that too. It's like he's taken a smack to that bald head of his once too many times." "He hates me and he hates you for keeping me around, or something like that." Amy grumbled with little effort as she stood back up gingerly, moving to toy with a rack of CDs. "He's just a little bitch is all. Funny thing is, between he and Kinkade, Fox is telling me I shouldn't show for Warfare." Brandon smirked, normally he would have used whatever was handy to convince her to stay out of harm's way but it was finally getting through his thick head that she might just have what it takes to take care of herself. "Well you know me, I would tend to agree... but it IS the final Warfare for the fWo and you should be there. Careful, but there." "When am I not careful?" The redhead winked, "Well...aside from Slaughter......but hey, the final Sunday Slaughter deserved a worthy finale!" "Yeah, and your ribs turning in to tapioca was sure it huh?" Brandon continued, not waiting for the expected smart comeback. "And the whole Alex thing looks like it's going to get a hell of a lot worse before it get's better. I think Eli has even had enough he plans on pulling Knight to the side soon." "Alex is gonna get a punch in the teeth if someone other than me doesn't pull him aside soon." She growled, "And my ribs'll be fine." "Well, let's worry about that stuff later. We've got new things to look forward to in the organization. With Disney helping out it can only get better, right? I still think we'll do that party thing soon, maybe as soon as we all get adjusted to the new show schedule and whatnot..." Brandon toned in, again as the voice of reason and calm. Amy smiled, "You know you're the biggest optimist short of the Pope?" "I prefer to think of myself as a self-realizing realist." With a hug Brandon assured Amy that no matter the storm, together he knew they could weather it. For her, it was dealing with an asshole. For him, it was trying to avert his best friend from becoming an asshole.
