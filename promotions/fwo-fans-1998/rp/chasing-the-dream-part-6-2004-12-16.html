Chasing The Dream - Part 6

16 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
May 16th 1999- 11:45PM The adrenaline surged throughout Milo's body and his heart raced. He took a deep breath, then let out a sigh of relief. He sunk his body down into the bail of hay on which he was laying on, and he watched the detached railway cars fade into the dark horizon. Things were calm now, but only moments ago Milo had looked face to face with death itself. It had been a long journey, but it was only the mid-point of his long travels. For eleven very long days and nights Milo had been traveling from Southern California, to Alberta Canada in the hopes to fulfil a childhood dream. Although Milo knew it would be tough, he had no idea what sort of things happen in our very own backyards. Milo began to fall into a state of sleep, but struggled to keep an eye open. He wasn't sure how long it would be until he may run into them again. The rhythmus sound of the train on the tracks almost played the role of a relaxing lullaby. His eyes grew heavier, and his exhausted body took control over him. He fell into a well-needed sleep, as the train sped along the tracks on this cold spring evening. May 17th 1999 - 11:15AM Milo awoke and stretched his somewhat refreshed body. His eyes felt glossy, and a layer of morning frost had formed on the top of his clothing. He stood to his feet, and it was visible that the train was no longer speeding through the night. Milo quietly crept up towards the door and gazed around the corner of the boxcar. The train must have been in some sort of fueling station, but there was no one in sight at the moment. Milo hopped out of the train and quickly walked towards the adjacent building. The building was unmarked, so Milo still unaware of his location quietly crept around the building to get a look at the area. The front of the building had a faded sign hanging high above the door which read "Macdonald & Sons" in black font. Milo stood and stared at the sign, attempting to piece together some clues as to where he was. "There was frost on my clothing, so I don't think I'm in California any more" he told himself attempting to act smart. He stood for a few more moments but then heard voices coming from within' the building. Milo ran and hid in a nearby brush, and listened to hear what the voices were saying. "Damn stow aways! Detached another car." one of the voices said in an angry tone. "Not to worry, if we find any of them onboard again we got permission from Deputy Lawrence to kill them and ditch their bodies." Another voice replied in a satisfied tone. After hearing this Milo decided perhaps the rails weren't the best mode of travel today. He made his way out to the shoulder of the road and extended a thumb. He waited about five minutes when a red pickup truck sped by, the driver not acknowledging Milo's presence on the shoulder. This was discouraging to him, but he took notice to another car which seemigly grew as it made its way over the horizon. His thumb was extended, but the driver of the car didn't seem to notice as he drove by. Milo let out a sigh of disappointment "This is useless". Just as he was ready to begin walking, he saw the amber glow of brake lights and the car pulled onto the shoulder. Milo smiled, and ran in the direction of the parked car. "Where you headed lad?" the elderly woman asked out the car window. "Can you maybe get me any closer to Canada?" Milo replied as he watched the woman reach back and unlock the car door. Milo climbed into the car and rested his aching bones on the vinal seating. "Canada eh?" the man behind the wheel said as he looked at Milo through the rearview mirror. Uncertain as to weather that was some sort of Canadian joke, Milo nodded to the driver. "You from around here?" the woman asked in a sympathetic tone. "Nah, to be honest I have no idea where I am. Other than the fact your license plate says 'Oregon' on it. I'm from California, the names Milo" He extended a hand into the front seat where it was met by a handshake from the two in the front seat. "My name is Earl, and this is my wife Dorothy. You are in New Berg, Oregon. Just a few miles out of Oregon City" the driver stated as he slowly began to drive up the country road. "So, why exactly would you hitch-hike from California to Canada?" Dorothy questioned as she turned to look at Milo. "Well, I am going up there to seek employment." He answered, laying his head back and relaxing it on the side window. The man again looked back at Milo through the rearview mirror "The wife and I are leaving for Canada in the morning actually. If you would like you are welcome to come along for the drive, you'd be more than welcome." The driver offered. Milo could not believe his ears, and he was rendered speechless. "Well, you coming or not?" the woman said in an eager tone of voice. "For real? I'd love to" Milo stated. Although the thought of strangers trusting him this much seemed a tad weird, anything would be better than the rails.
