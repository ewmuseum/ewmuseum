... The End. (2 of 2)

28 February 2002

Saturday, February 23, 2002 6:26 PM Pacific Seattle, Washington The white lines were spread out neatly on the desk in front of Scott Wasner. It had disturbed him a bit but not surprised him to see that his skills at preparing this particular substance had not deteriorated. Guess it's like a bicycle. You do it once, you never forget. He looked down at the powder. Well, this is it. No going back now. Like so many before him; like Lenny Bias; like John Belushi; like Dwight Gooden and Darryl Strawberry; Wasner dropped his face into the pile. The coke went up his nose like a missile, tearing at the membranes and shooting seemingly straight into his brain. Wasner jerked his head back in a spasm of pain... then slowly leaned forward. The immediate pain was already fading, and the cocaine was beginning to take effect. "Whoa." The one word seemed to sum everything up pretty well. "Fucking whoa." No, that was better. The intense feelings generated were incredible - even more so due to the long time it had been since he had last felt them. Wasner felt like he was flying. The energy pulsing through his veins was indescribable. A man, a backstage attendant of some sort, poked his head through the door. "You and Creed are up in a few minutes, Scott." The man saw the white powder on the table but said nothing. This was not the most uncommon of sights for him, especially in this kind of environment. He left and shut the door. Wasner stood up, his eyes open wide and a smile on his face. "Alright. Me and Creed. Me and Creed. Me and Creed. WHOO! Alright. OK. Let's do this." He turned towards the door and walked out of the room. ----- Saturday, February 23, 2002 9:22 PM Pacific She could see it in his eyes, as hard as it was to believe. It was a condition she had seen many times during her life, but she never thought she'd see it in him. The pay-per-view had been over for a little over an hour, and Wasner was just getting home. Stephanie hadn't watched - as much as she loved the man, she detested his occupation. That Wasner was high on something was obvious as soon as he walked through the door. All the signs were there - unsteady walk, dilated pupils, the usual. He stumbled toward her. "Hey, babe, how you doing?!" She held out her hands to stop him in front of her. "What is it, Scott?" "What's what?" "What are you on?" "Oh, come on. I'm not on anything... really!" She shook her head. "Nope, no way I'm falling for that. Just tell me. You have no idea how pissed off I am, but we're all human. We all make mistakes. Just tell me what it is, go sleep it off, and when you wake up we're gonna have one hell of a discussion." "Nuh-uh. Not gonna happen, babe. Now, if you'll excuse me, I'm going... uh, over there." He started to walk and briefly stumbled, then righted himself. She felt the anger she had been putting aside rising to the surface as she reached out and pulled him back. "Just tell me. Got it?" "Hmm, let's see... hell no! What do you think about that, huh? Now get the hell out of my way!" He started to walk off again, and again she grabbed him. This time, instead of letting her pull him back, he instinctually reached out with a swing of the hand. The sound of the connection was a flat and undramatic smack that echoed through the suddenly silent house. Everything seemed to stop. For a moment, Stephanie felt like she was caught in some malign time warp. Was this Scott or Rick? Was this Seattle or New Orleans? She thought back to what he had told her last September. "I would never hurt you," he had said. He lied. And really... should I have expected anything else? Just when I finally felt safe... after all those years... it happens again. They stood there looking silently at each other, each in shock. It was Wasner who spoke first. "I... I'm sorry. I didn't mean to... I... I..." She felt tears beginning to come to her eyes. When she spoke, her voice was strangely calm. "No. Don't touch me. I'm leaving. It's over." He put out a hand to stop her. "No, don't leave. I'm sorry, I - " She took a step past him, but he stepped back in front of her, blocking her way. She lashed out with a kick, catching him below the belt. Wasner dropped to the floor on all fours, hitting his head on the wood paneling and drawing blood. She stepped out the door and slammed it behind her. She walked down the sidewalk and got into the Camaro. She slammed her fist against the window, then wiped away the tears from her eyes and started the car. It pulled out of the driveway and disappeared down the street. Inside the house, Scott Wasner slowly pulled himself to his feet. Despite the cocaine haze in his brain, a series of coherent thoughts still hit him. You did it again. Flushed it down the toilet. Just like last time. Can't you ever get anything right? Twice you've had the perfect life handed to you on a platter. Twice you've blown it. You stupid bastard. Then, in a culmination of the day - the cocaine, the match, the argument, the kick - he succumbed to unconsciousness and fell face-first onto the floor. The blood trickled from the cut on his forehead, leaving a small puddle around his head. Just like that, it had all fallen apart.
