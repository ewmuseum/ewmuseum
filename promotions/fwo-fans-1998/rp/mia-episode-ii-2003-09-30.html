MIA Episode II

30 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN: SILVER FOX talking on the phone with somebody and facing the wall – perfect for ‘COCKY’ CRAIG MILES as he walks in and sits down in the guest chair. He slides the boombox he’d been carrying earlier by his feet, so Fox can’t see it. MILES: “Silllllllllllllver!” (FOX swings his chair around with a smile on his face) FOX: “I got a meeting, let me call you back.” (FOX hangs up the phone, stands up with a handshake offer.) FOX: “Ah – Craig, I’m happy you could make it.” (MILES stands up returning the favor, banging his foot on something below…) MILES: “Hey anything for a man with the pride to stand tall with that name of yours.” (They both sit down, FOX smirks) FOX: “What’s wrong with my name? You can’t let go of that.” MILES: “Nothin’ – no worries. Hell, I know some guy named Golden Hawk that wrestles in Minnesota.” FOX: “I’m sure you do.” (FOX laughed at the joke, nodding his head as he filed some paperwork. SFX: Click!) “Little did he know that was exactly the truth. In Miles’ head the world really was just an absurd oyster…and it was his for the taking.” (SFX: Click!) FOX: (while looking up) “You hear that?” MILES: “Hear what?” FOX: “Someone sounding James Earl Jones.” MILES: (shrugs and then smiles) “By the way, saw some of the card last night. Great job. Someone needs to tell Better with Cheddar – that some moron copyrighted the if that’s your real name, joke.” (FOX finishes filing the paperwork, faces MILES with a face near laughter) FOX: “You got to be kidding me!” MILES: “Oh yeah, no way they could’ve known. I love that line, myself. Just this league I work for in Greensboro found some scrawny kid that says that to every wrestler and screams Avoid the 'Noid at the end of every promo. The kids llllllllove him.” FOX: “You work in Greensboro?” MILES: “Yeah, I’m trying to destroy a federation there.” (FOX laughed, but ummm…yeah, actually MILES was.) MILES: “Yeah, Merritt doesn’t like me too much.” FOX: “Oh, Chad! So you know Eli and Sean – that’s gr..” MILES: “Yeah, they work for me up in Boston.” FOX: “Work for you? In Boston?” MILES: “Yeah, I got my own promotion going.” FOX: “You’re one interesting guy, Craig – you find all the time to do these things and I’ve never even heard of you…” (SFX: Click!) “The greatest trick the devil ever pulled was convincing the world he didn’t exist.” (SFX: Click!) FOX: “Excuse me?” MILES: “What?” FOX: “Thought I just heard Kevin Spacey, sorry. Well, that’s great Craig. Obviously you know your way around the business, then.” MILES: “Certainly do.” FOX: “And you’re willing to work in the tag division?” MILES: “Its my specialty, Fox. Give me the right tools there, you’ll have a renaissance on your hands.” FOX: “So you’ve got a partner?” MILES: “Nope.” FOX: “I thought you’re a tag specialist? Wouldn’t that require a partner?” (MILES pulls out a cigarette and lights it up) MILES: “I keep things fresh if I have to. You’re damn right I’m a specialist, I’m so damn special I’ll make anyone look good on my team.” FOX: “Craig, you’re not cutting a promo right now…” (MILES blows out a smoke ring) MILES: “Promo? Who’s cutting a promo?” FOX: “You mean, you’re always like this?” MILES: “Like what?” (MILES ashes his cigarette on the table…there’s no ashtray. FOX’s jaw drops slightly…he looks at MILES and just nods his head in confusion.) FOX: “So, exactly what do you have in mind?” MILES: (while exhaling) “Not a clue.” FOX: “How the hell have you won all those tag titles?” MILES: “I’m a Professional, Fox. Read the resume, its all there start to finish.” FOX: “So what do you think of our division, you seem to have made your rounds in other places – how do you feel about here?” (MILES exhales smoke through his nose) MILES: “Well, the French with a title is a little far-fetched – doncha think?” FOX: “Actually, the W.P.O lost the titles last night to Phoenix.” MILES: “Who?” (FOX retrieves a flyer of the team Phoenix holding up the title belts in victory, both wrestlers’ names airbrushed underneath them…basically, a smaller version of the new poster that will adorn your local Magic card shops this weekend.) FOX: “These are the guys.” MILES: (while reading) “Ah, ok. Martyr and…” (MILES starts squinting through his yellow-shaded Oakleys) MILES: “Aru…Arumah…Aru-heh-meh-heh-aha-hee-ho. Hold on a sec.” (SFX: Click! CUEUP: ‘Jeopardy’) FOX: (leaning over his desk, he’s onto him now!) “What the hell?” MILES: (still trying to read) “Argula…no…Arumatherapy…no…Jesus man!” (SFX: Click! MILES puts out the cigarette on the table and looks lost and perplexed) MILES: How are the kids gonna say this?” (FOX leans back in his chair, looking at MILES like he’s a nutcase.) FOX: “It’s like Ah-Room-In-A-Dern Raal. Why do you have a boombox?” MILES: “I guess for moments like Ah-Room-In-A-Dern Raal.” FOX: “You’re a weird one, Craig.” MILES: (winks) “I”ll take that as a compliment from a guy named Silver.” FOX: “Could you do me a favor next time we meet?” MILES: “What’s that?” FOX: “Don’t bring that stereo. It freaks me out.” MILES: “Oh, its cool brother – I don’t like to hide my inner thoughts, secrets and obsessions. (MILES pats the boombox) In fact, I keep it ALL on tape.” FOX: “So you’ve got no partner, no plan and you’re making a promise that you can turn any team you’re part of into a legitimate, world-class tag team?” MILES: “They don’t call me a Professional for nothin’, Fox.” FOX: (throws his hands up in the air) “Alright, alright. You’ve come on good word. The resume and experience with the business is almost as much as I’ve got and you’re half my age. Just show up, I guess – do your magic.” (MILES stands up smiling, he pulls out a cigarette and lights it…he inhales deeply nodding in sweet satisfaction. He lets out a thick, puffy smoke ring. Damn, he’s cool.) MILES: “Fox, the moment you called…the wheels were already set in motion. In fact, right now I bet millions of eyes are wondering exactly what my next step might be. Or maybe they’re thinking I’m the world’s largest dick…which I don’t have regardless of what that videotape says.” (MILES walks towards the door, boombox in tow – he turns around and takes a quick drag) MILES: “Either way, Fox – it’s ratings for you.” FOX: “Hey Craig, there is something you could bring next time that I’d appreciate.” MILES: “What’s that?” FOX: “Shoes.” MILES: “Don’t knock it ‘till you’ve tried it. Its quite relaxing.” (MILES exits FOX’s office and FOX shakes his head, not knowing what he just dealt with and not quite sure he even wants to find out. For a moment, he sits in his office perplexed – he looks towards the floor curiously and then swings his chair around – his feet out of their shoes. He starts curling his toes on the carpet and a satisfied look comes on his face.) FOX: “Heh, maybe he’s not THAT crazy.” (FADEOUT)
