Harem - 9 - Canned.

1 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In an instant, it was over... Only days ago, she'd told Brandon about the pregnancy. About the force coming into their lives, one that would bind the families and give Amy twice the reason to wake up on a given day... It was violently pulled from them both by a man who'd caused Brandon Roche nothing but anguish in the bitter years they'd known one another. That was exactly why Brandon wouldn't - couldn't - know that Vic Creid's attack on Amy had been what caused the miscarriage. In her eyes, anyway, it was her own fault. She and Brandon had been terrified that knowing she was pregnant would only give Vic more fuel to fire at them with - After all, he basically gave up his OWN family life for the belt, and had demonstrated to Eli Flair that he didn't feel anybody else going for his title had any right to think they coul have both the championship and a family at the same time. And it was truth - Even after being reminded of the gravity, he'd taken a part of Amy and Brandon that couldn't be replaced...and now it was nobody's fault but hers that she stood in the center of the basement gym, quite literally empty. Void of even her own lifeforce - The only things binding the redhead being the black tanktop and torn jeans that hung at her hips. What gave her any right to mourn? This was her doing. Her own stupidity had brought on the attack, hadn't it? She shouldn't have been at Ruahh~!, should have realized she wasn't just endangering herself any longer... It was too late now. The first shots to the swaying punching bag sent it reeling and Amy made no effort to stop it with anything less than a full shoulder slammed back against it, quickly followed by another series of stiff fists. "I don't give a damn about my own daughter, little girl. Why would I give a fuck about yours?" "Why didn't you grab something?!" She thought, "A chair, tray...fuck, even your shoe?! ANYTHING! You just laid back, too fucking terrified to do jackshit..." The rage had her in tears - wanting to kill Vic Creed...herself, even...and this wasn't helping. A punching bag was feeling the brunt of the aggression, not any deserving human being... A red glare in her eyes and dangerous thoughts floating to the forefront of her mind, the redhead was still trembling when massive arms wrapped around and held her still by the tiny waist. Brandon had come down the steps quietly. Amy hitting the bag hadn't gone as unnoticed as she had hoped and the aggressiveness of her strikes told a story, a bad one. He had waited there for a few moments, wondering if he should approach her. He often felt as if he smothered her, always there when things were wrong. He couldn't help it though, that was him, playing hero or whatever you called it, he couldn't simply stand by while something ate away at her. Now holding her firmly, he could feel the tension in her body, not since that night when he turned up alive after the bombing had he felt her this way. It worried him. "What is it, Amy?" He felt her shudder with a light sob, "Don't tell me 'nothin' either. If it was any more obvious there'd be smoke signals or something..." "I..." Her fists balled again and she shook twice as hard. How was she to tell Brandon? Within a split second, it didn't matter - As usual, emotion came pouring in buckets... "I lost it. Him, her...whoever. Ours," She made a half-hearted attempt to pull away from Brandon, almost in shame. "The baby. I'm sorry..." As jumbled as her thoughts were, one thing rang through loud and clear - Do NOT tell him that an attack from Vic Creid caused this. Amy herself wanted to kill Vic as it was, Brandon with their history did not need this, lest he do something against his nature as she was hardly able to keep herself from doing at the thought of Creid. "Lost... the baby?" Brandon started to pull away out of instinct, shock, horror, and amazement. He didn't though. He pulled her tighter to him. "I miscarried." Amy lowered her gaze to the floor, hardly able to look him in the eye. "Maybe due to stress, travel...something. It's my fault, I shouldn't have followed you around to the show...I'm so sorry..." "Oh my God..." Brandon's blood went cold. With everything that had been going on with Creed and him he had done what he promised he wouldn't. He had ignored his family for this 'quest', this journey for revenge. If he found out that Victor Creid was responsible for all of this there would probably be no power on Earth that would stop him from taking that ultimate leap. The one that separated the Brand Frontiers from the Alaskan Juggernauts in this life. "A...Amy." The dry patch in Brandon Roche's mouth grew larger, "Don't... don't ever say something like that. It can't be your fault. It... how... when... when did you find out...?" "Officially? This morning. I went to the doctor when you were out with the boys." Amy watched Brandon's heart sink further, and the sudden urge to tear something - namely Vic - apart washed over her in a stream of hot blood rushing to her cheeks. "BL. I'm sorry..." The apology was like a mantra, "I didn't want you upset earlier...didn't want you to know until it was sure..." Brandon's head spun. This morning, while he was out with the kids, at the playground it was confirmed that the baby... their baby... was gone. It had drove every nerve in his body to bursting and at the same time let him go numb. He wanted to respond, to react some way, any way. But he didn't, he simply stared. Amy grew nervous. Normally the silence between them was comforting, this wasn't one of those. He finally looked to her mismatched eyes for some feeling of security; All he found was fear. That he couldn't have. "It's okay. I'm the one that should be sorry. I know how much you like to be on the road and I know how hard it is on someone. Ana had problems with Jarod herself..." He let his words trail off, it was too late however, he had already said her name and this was surely not the right time to do so. "Shit. I'm sorry Amy. I... I didn't mean to bring her up. I just want you to know that I don't blame you or anything. Sometimes... these things happen." He had held back for as long as he could at this point. The tears had finally grown in size and with the next blink two slowly trickled down his face. Suddenly, the smallest of the redheads reeled around and out of Brandon's reach and kicked the still swaying bag by her shin with an uncharacteristic grace and power driven by unadultered rage. "For just one second," She glared at the Paragon, "Can we forget about your ex wife? Ana this, Ana that...Ana's not Amy, and you'd better not think of it like that." Again, she attacked the punching bag. Brandon cringed... "I know that dear, much the reason I'm so in love with you. We only have our past experiences to guide us through what we're going through... and I'm sorry but that is the only thing I have to even remotely compare this to." Brandon realized that this was teatering on the edge of a full blown arugment. Not what they needed right now either. Pulling back a bit and reminding himself not to be so defensive, his tone softened, "Let's... just forget that okay. What about you? What did the doctor say about how you're doing, physically I mean. Is there anything... wrong?" He didn't know how else to phrase it. "I'm in perfect shape." Amy took another swing at the bag, "There's no explaination for what happened. Like you say, some things just aren't..." Punch. "...meant..." Right hook. "To be." With that, she drove a knee into the bag and fell forward against it, hugging it tightly to her with a painful cringe. This was breaking her down very quickly, and that was made all-too apparent by mounting tears. Seeing her mental condition deteriorating in front of his eyes, the Irishman grabbed Amy by the arm, maybe a bit too roughly. He pulled her around, now facing him again. Her sweat slick back pressing against the punching bag she wished was Vic Creid. "Stop this. You do this every time something goes wrong. You don't have to blame yourself. And you're right about the way I think. Maybe this wasn't meant to be... but that doesn't mean we're not meant to be, or that we have to kill ourselves over it." Brandon exhaled deeply, letting go of his lover's arm. "It comes down to this, Amy: There are always a million things we can all do differently...are we going to go the rest of our lives doubting every movement we make? We can play 'what if?' all we want, but that doesn't change where we are. It just keeps us from going where we're supposed to. We look behind us at what could have been, we stumble over the things that are happening now...before you know it, we're falling out of control." Amy was still pressed against the punching bag, unable to make any sort of move against it. Instead, she tried forcing her way through Brandon...which only got her tightly held once again. Without her full heart into it, she'd never get around the wall of a man. "Am I not allowed to react or something? Not everybody can just grin and bear it like you, Brandon. I'm sorry for disrupting your perfect little family environment with anger, but at least let me take it out own here on something safe rather than the living room wall." At the very least, Brandon understood why she might have seemed more unreasonable than usual - Horomones calming down again in combination with the 'news' today would be enough to drive the sanest of people around the bend, not to mention those who already had trouble containing themselves. She was still struggling to get free so she could take another pass at the bag, but Brandon was relentless still. "Listen, I'm not saying 'don't worry, be happy'. I'm mad Amy... mad as all Hell." Another tear welled up and slid down his already wet cheek. "I just don't want you to be mad at yourself." Brandon let Amy step aside finally, grabbing the top of the bag around the chain as he did. "...and stop apologizing and thinking you're disturbing some perfect life I'm supposedly having. Amy... you are my life." "I might be your life, but Ana and an obsessive preoccupation with Vic gets to come along for the ride." She sighed, spinning to kick the bag she was recently so in love with but the redhead got her leg captured when Brandon realized he was dangerously close enough to the object bearing the brunt of Amy's rage to get hit himself. After a second though he let it go, looking her dead in the eye as he did. "Let's not get off track here Amy, this isn't about my 'preoccupation' with Creid nor about my past. It's about us, and what we do right now will decide how we handle things as a couple when things go bad, because sweety... I don't think they can get much worse than this." "Things will be a lot worse if you keep putting everything aside just to get at Vic...it is about your past, about you getting some kind of revenge for what the whore you still care too much about chose to do. There're more important things to worry about, things other than your ex-wife and some moron she fucked to worry about...but you don't really notice them." "If anything," She continued, "I just pray this bullshit ends soon. I want him out of my life, out of the life you think we share. Unfortunately, while he's controlling you with the past, it's dominating your life..." A fist flew to the punching bag, slamming it hard enough to touch the wall. "It's bad when he has to touch every fucking aspect of our life. You can't go fifteen minutes without mentioning him, and he can't go fifteen minutes without clinching down his occupation of your brain with some idiotic parade of how great and tough he thinks he is." "The only time he's mentioned in this house is when we talk about business. I don't do that in front of the kids, I don't talk about their mother in front of them either. The whole fifteen minute thing is just an exaggeration on your part, you don't need to lash out at me because you're angry about this." Brandon sighed and turned away. "The thing with Creid is 'the thing with Creid'. You knew about most of the history before we ever got involved. Yes it is about getting revenge, not 'gainst Ana, us taking the kids will be punishment enough. Creid needs to be put down like the rabid animal he is." Turning back to her yet again, Brandon tried to make eye contact with the shorter red head, all he got was a slight glance from between her tossled hair. A quick one-two jab shot out at the beaten bag as Brandon stepped to her once again, blocking her from her target. "We lost something tonight Amy, a part of us. Don't let it tear us apart with it. We can mourn, we can grieve, but we shouldn't turn the hurt on ourselves, or each other. There was nothing we could have done differently... right?" With that Brandon touched the fallen chin of his fiance pulling her face up to the light, letting her red sweat-soaked hair fall to the side. She wanted to tell him everything. Tell him why she wanted Creid dead, why she was beating herself up over all of this...but it couldn't happen. "Right." Relenting did nothing but hurt.
