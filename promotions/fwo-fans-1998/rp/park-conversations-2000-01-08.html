Park Conversations

8 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Park Conversations January 08, 2000 In the park, life stands still. You can't go on like this... Rudo watched as Machina lay on the park bench and growled at the pigeons that wandered near him. Machina never did like pigeons, the filthy little birds eat garbage from the ground and when you let your guard down they go for your eyes. On this morning the pigeons were being very insistent and Machina finally had to get off the bench and chase them around the park flapping his arms. Rudo watched as this little ritual played out and Machina finally came back to the bench and collapsed on the bench with a sigh. Damn birds. Little white harvesters of death... Rudo folded his hands patiently in his lap and waited for the conversation to come back to the issue at hand. Penny. Penny. Penny. She's a doll, Rudo. The more I stay with her the more I'm sure that she's like, the one. At least for this month. She's... she's... she's just... Machina was at a loss for words. Rudo cleared his throat and tried to help. She's the exact opposite of Wynona Ryder? Machina snapped his fingers and sat up. Exactly! That's exactly what she's like. Or not like I mean. Exactly not like Wynona Ryder, which is just perfect. Wynona Ryder. Boy... that whole clan is full of icky people. The moment played out and Rudo sensed that he was starting to lose Machina. He struggled to bring the conversation back to the issue at hand. Hey Machina, if you had to do one girl which would you do... Wynona Ryder or Sheryl Crow? Machina winced. I have to be with one? Rudo nodded and Machina thought for some time before finally coming up with an answer. Sid Snow. Rudo looked at Machina seriously for a moment, and then both of them laughed. The tension had been broken, and both sat silently for a moment in the sun. It was a warm day in January, and there wasn't a cloud in sight in the Seattle sky. Some birds flew low over the nearby lake but that was one of the only signs of life in the park. Rudo picked a rock off the grass and tossed it into the water with a solid plunking noise following impact. Davis is back you know. Machina shrugged. Yeah, yeah. Bad penny, turns up, whatever. Rudo smiled a wry smile. That's awful cocky, he's like... Death or something now. Machina smiled. "Dead". We've got two "Deaths" already. Don't need a third, you know. It's sort of weird you know... he's this big bad guy with no hair and stuff, but then he goes out and surrounds himself with a bunch of losers. I mean, you give those guys trendy names all you want... but they are still the three stooges of suck. What kind of dork surrounds himself with a bunch of nobody jobbers? Machina and Rudo exchanged a glance and then broke into laughter again. They settled, and Machina tossed a rock into the lake with a plunk and splash of water. But seriously, I'm not worried. Rudo shrugged. Well, whatever you say. But you did retire him almost six months ago... hey... what gives? You did retire him didn't you? What's he doing back already? Now it was Machina's turn to shrug. Oh, who knows. The ratings are fine, the Frenchie has turned into some kind of super draw in the last three months. Good for him, masked people need all the respect we can get. Black Quicksilver gets into the title scene and you've got yourself a promotion. That Bear guy... Kidman... those two seem to be clicking all of a sudden. It's a good combo, the two work good together. The fWo shouldn't break them up... you get that Wolf Fang and Jackhammer guy, Primetime and Mitch... those were all better as pairs. So were Ultra Violet and Collins. But you know though, Ultra Violet is doing his thing with Kid Cool and people are digging that... So... Rudo smiled appreciatively. Violet's freakin' hot, man. She is amazingly hot... those tight little leather purple pants... Machina laughed and slapped Rudo on the shoulder. You dog... make sure Brandi doesn't hear about that... Rudo grinned. Oh, you know me man... I'm loyal. You know, we're trying... Machina looked startled. A baby? You? Rudo nodded. Machina grinned and slapped him on the back. Well that's reason to celebrate... so what's it going to be, boy or girl? Rudo laughed. How would I know? It's not like we've even... um... gotten to that point yet. Machina looked at Rudo slyly. Yeah, but Brandi... she seems to know this stuff ahead of time. You remember the Spencer thing? Rudo nodded. How could I forget? Dudley went on a bender over that... remember, the whole politics speech... Machina lay back again on the bench and closed his eyes. The sun was warm on this skin and he enjoyed the moment. So how did she know Death was Spencer anyway? Everyone has always wondered that... Rudo laughed. She walked in on him in the can. Machina opened his eyes. No way... Rudo nodded. Seriously! We were down in Miami and the stadium didn't have plates on the bathrooms backstage. Brandi wandered into the men's bathroom... caught Spencer in there putting on the mask. You know, he plucks his eyebrows... Machina laughed and shook his head. You know, it's now almost two years since we've started this. Long time... you and I... Sid, Billy... Rudo looked puzzled. Billy... who? Machina looked fixedly at Rudo. You don't remember Billy James? Rudo shook his head slowly and Machina looked annoyed. Doesn't anyone remember... oh hell... Machina collapsed back on the bench as Rudo dusted off his jacket. You change anything? Machina laughed and looked up into the sky. Are you kidding? Nearly everything my dear man, nearly everything. Now it was Rudo's turn to look puzzled, and he leaned forward. Everything? Certainly it hasn't been all that bad has it? Machina sighed a long sigh, and didn't answer. A minute passed, and then another. Rudo was about to say something when Machina finally spoke. Well, I'd teach people how to say my name right for one thing. MACK-IN-UH. Where people get the idea that my name sounds like machine is beyond me. And then there was that whole shoot angle thing. Jesus. You know, I still find shit in my bag over that one... Rudo nodded sadly. It was a nice break though... and I met Brandi... Machina shrugged. Yeah, so... yeah there is a silver lining. I don't know... we're a joke you know. I'm going to cut a promo on Austin Davi... um... Dead or whatever, tomorrow. You know, the usual. Blah blah, blah blah blah. You know, the interviewing would be a lot easier if they let me wear that clown outfit I asked for. A couple pies... yeah, that's comedy. Rudo laughed and rubbed his hands together. I'm supposed to wrestle Collins on Wednesday I hear. Come as you are match or something like that. What does that mean anyway? "Come as you are". I mean, can you come as you aren't? Machina laughed. That's Heftel you're thinking of... so Collins, huh. Well, he's okay to work with. Whatever you do, make fun of the candy thing. He really loves that. Oh, and he's whipped too, so make sure and make a crack about Stone carrying his testicles or something. Really gets him going. You get him all riled up and you can get three stars out of him... They sat for a moment in silence and looked out over the pond. Rudo spoke first. The best days... do you think they've come and gone? Machina shrugged. For you? No. Me? Well, I have Penny you know... but the rest? Well... come on now, I'll buy you a coffee. They stood and walked away. In the distance, Machina's voice drifted out. Pluck his eyebrows? What a pansy... Rudo's voice was the last thing heard as the scene faded away. Well, you know... Brits. Birds chirped, and people went onto other affairs.
