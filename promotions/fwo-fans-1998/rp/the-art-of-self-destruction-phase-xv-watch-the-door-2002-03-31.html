The Art of Self Destruction, Phase XV (Watch The Door)

31 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
They're coming up the stairs. Step-by-step. They are coming straight to this apartment. They're going to come right in and take you away. Remember that place you went when you were sixteen? They want to bring you back. Straight jackets, ankle cuffs, you won't even be able to walk out of here by yourself. Lock the door; or else they're going to break in. They think there's something wrong with you. They want to take you away. You can't let them do that to you. His head nodded in unison with his hallucinations. After sitting in the same corner for nearly two days; he picked himself up from the floor and walked towards the door. Each of his fingers deftly locked the three latches that he had over his door. The resounding clicks signifying that his door was as locked as it could be sent relief through his mind. I don't think that's going to hold them... Again, he nodded in agreement and for the first time in two days he turned a light on inside of the apartment. His body bent down in front of a large recliner; with all of his strength he picked it up and placed it in front of the door. He then pushed it as far against the door it would go; assuring that nothing would get through and into the apartment. That's a little better. Their steps are loud, can you hear them? They sound like they are large men. How will you keep them out? I don't know... help me Can't you do anything yourself? I... Of course you can't. You're the most useless thing on the face of the planet. I'm sorry... Sorry for what? How pathetic you are? How you can't do anything for yourself? You're about as useful as dirt. And those men outside of the door; they're coming for you. They are going to rip down that door and take you away. You've got to stop them somehow. If you don't; you're life is basically over... I don't care... He slumped back down into the corner had been in for so long. His hands covered his ears to drown out the hallucinogenic pounding at the door.
