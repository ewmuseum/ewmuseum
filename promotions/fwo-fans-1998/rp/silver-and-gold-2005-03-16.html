Silver and GOLD

16 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Leaving the arena for the night, Sonny Silver wasn't looking forward to that long drive to the airport for the next show. He was dead tired. After all, getting slammed around by Vince Jacobs was never anybody's cup of tea. Nevertheless, after a hard night's work, Sonny was ready to hit that open road. Drinking a bottle of Dr. Pepper he got from a vending machine a few moments ago, he made his way through the parking lot and searched for his rental car. After about a minute and a half of walking through the lot, he stumbled upon his car...and found a weird-looking flyer. "Huh?" Sonny asked. "What the hell? Eh. Probably some nut wanting me to praise Jesus or Heat Man or something..." Yes, Renner, that one was just for you. Sonny looked at the flyer and scanned it closely before taking notice of the cars around him. There were absolutely no flyers on any other car. Just his. "Odd," he muttered before looking down at the flyer that read the following: MORTON'S TAKE HER THERE FOR YOUR ANNIVERSARY. TAKE HIM THERE FOR THAT MAGICAL NIGHT YOU ALWAYS WANTED. LET THE MAGIC HAPPEN TODAY. He scanned the phone number and address at the bottom and also took notice of the little marking underneath the slogan that read "Free Food, Free Drinks. Morton's of Chicago." "Well, I have NO idea why this flyer is here," he told himself. "I have no idea why I want to even go to Chicago...but I just can't say no to the food!" And on that note, Sonny the glutton headed into his car and sped off into the night in search of this Morton's joint... ----------------------------- Morton's. It was a place that was known by many to be the priciest of the priciest restaurants one could dine at. And it was a place that would drain your checkbook like a leech that shall not be named known as Paris Hilton...I've already said too much. Anyhow, Sonny Silver found himself in the lobby of this place, drawn by a mysterious flyer left on his car from the previous edition of RUAHH~! Sure, he didn't make a habit of following mysterious flyers left on his car windshield because it was usually some religious thing that the atheist Silver would have no interest in. He also found it extremely, extremely odd that no other car had the flyer, but, hey. It was for FREE FOOD AND BOOZE...he didn't drink much of the latter, but the free food part sold him, so here he was now. Dressed sharply yet traditionally in a white collared shirt and black dress pants that felt foreign to him (partly because he was usually just a jeans/t-shirt man) with black loafers, he waited patiently in the lobby, hoping to find somebody to show the coupon. He wanted his free FOOD, now! That steak wasn't going to eat itself...and if it did, that'd be fudgin' weird. "C'mon, c'mon, c'mon," he muttered, looking at the gold Rolex on his right hand. He'd been waiting behind a small crowd of patrons for the better part of fifteen minutes. Finally, God smiled upon him as he made it to the front of the line to see the Maitre' D. "Here!" Sonny said, almost too eager to get into the pricey establishment. He flashed the flyer at the Maitre' D's way, who was quick to shoot him a smile. "Ah, you're here!" he said, elated. A little TOO elated, Sonny thought. Maybe he was gay, Sonny also thought. "Yes...I am. Food. Now. Eat," yeah, sentences weren't his strong point when the stomach controlled his body. "Right this way." The Maitre' D took Sonny to a private room in the back, but not before a few murmurs were muttered by a few of the dining patrons. The fWo's high status was beginning to rub off on him, he thought to himself. Cool, Sonny thought to himself. This is what it's like to be known...well, if no gay weirdos like Jack from "Will & Grace" or this fruity Maitre' D start stalking me and going for my jock straps in the gym, I'm in the clear. Sonny was shown the way to a private room where he was quickly given his seat. "What is all this?" Sonny looked up at the Maitre' D inquisitively. "Your party is waiting," he replied before leaving. "Menus will be here shortly." As the Maitre' D took his leave, Sonny took a glance around the table for a moment before scratching his head. No one yet...until now. "Didn't think you were going to show." A woman's voice with a woman's hand shoved an open menu in front of Sonny's face and held it there until he took it in his own two hands.
