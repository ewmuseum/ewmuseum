Higher Purpose

18 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As my eyes forced themselves open, I wondered what's changed since I was twenty that's made it so difficult to get up the next day. Of course, the answer is obvious: wrestling. No, I'm not exhausted from wrestling a tough match last night. I'm exhausted from the travel and the fact that I can no longer party like a rockstar without paying for it. Three years ago, I could be out all night, get home at dawn, sleep for three hours and do it all again. Now? I'm still slightly concussed from Killjoy's attack, and as much as I'm glad to be back in my neighborhood, I'm preoccupied with the biggest match of my FWO career, coming up this Wednesday at Free Wrestling Day. "What time is it?" asked Rosie, mostly asleep. She hadn't even lifted her head from the pillow. Early afternoon, I said. What time does your shift start? "Six," said Rose, "all I really need to do is shower." Okay, I told her, if you're not up by four thirty I'll wake you. "Thank you," said Rose. I eased my way out of bed - she was obviously woken up by my sudden rise - and picked a T-shirt out of my laundry bag. Tonight would be best served by staying in and recuperating for the next two days; Free Wrestling Day was following a sort of unofficial FWO convention at the arena, and Miss Ivy has asked us to try and be there for the fans. Too much distraction, all before the biggest match of my FWO career. Not just the convention, either. Last night was a distraction I didn't need. There's a gig being promoted next month in New York City to benefit cancer and diabetes research, and some of my friends who are helping out the promoter asked me if I'd be willing to make an appearance, put my name on the flyer, etc etc. That's strange, I said to them, I heard about the gig and was planning on going, but it never occurred to me to make an appearance. This whole celebrity thing is like a new pair of Doc Martens: use 'em too much too soon and they'll cut into your ankles. So to speak. So yeah, we ended up in Brooklyn at Duff's; probably my favorite place to drink besides TC's, just going over stuff. If my being there can bring more people, then I'm down for that. Besides, the promoter is good people, and our mutual friend is one of those guys you just can't say no to. That's how we ended up trekking to Manhatten after eleven to meet up with some other people and possibly get into another show. That's how we ended up getting in sometime after five AM. In hindsight, not the smartest thing I've ever done. And no, I don't have a rematch with Superstar Vince Jacobs. The biggest match to date in my FWO career, taking place this Wednesday, is against Michelle Masters. The current Cruiserweight Champion. You're naive, said Miss Ivy, when I told her that yes, Michelle approached me about a match, but no, I did not take a title shot. I haven't earned it, I told her. She disagreed, and said that everyone, by virtue of making it to and surviving the FWO, has earned a title shot, and that you need to take the opportunities as they're given and make the most of them, because you never know when you'll get another. What about Vince Jacobs, I said. He's a special case, said Miss Ivy. Vince is an arrogant, egotistical... well, I'm not going to repeat it, everyone knows Miss Ivy is somewhat of a passionate individual, but she also said that Vince got his shot at Cyberslam, and while she agrees with him over Jack's title defense schedule of late, she can't give him any kind of preferential treatment, even to shut him up, because then he'll think he can get away with anything. I get it, I told her, but that's still a morally ambiguous double standard. Okay, said Miss Ivy, then how about Eli Flair? He questioned whether or not he deserved a World Title match against Jon Crisp in the winter of 2004, but she accepted on his behalf as his manager because, like she said to me, you never know when you'll get another shot. This is true, I told her, except for one important fact: I'm not Eli Flair. Someday soon, I think Miss Ivy is going to have a talk with Rosie about all the days off she's been taking lately to come to Seattle with me for the FWO's shows, to say nothing of New Frontier Wrestling. Rosie was off last night, she's off tonight, she's off tomorrow, and she's off Wednesday. Pretty long vacation for someone who is considered an unofficial general manager of TC's. Running a bar at 21, not a bad deal. But I wish she'd give her manager's pay to go along with it. Of course, that won't happen. Miss Ivy has always believed family should help family, and she and Rose are certainly family in every which way but blood; and the way she piles the work on is her way of letting you know how she feels about you. That's why the Garden has been making do with three roadies despite the fact that their tours are getting longer and larger. That's why she hasn't hired anyone (yet) to work directly for her in the FWO, preferring instead to sideways delegate to Adrian and Philip. Miss Ivy keeps the massive amounts of responsibility she has to manage firmly balanced on herself, and the few people in her life she trusts to do things right. The fact that she's so intent on me taking this match as a title shot, I'm simply taking it that she believes if I was the FWO Cruiserweight Champion, she would never have to worry about how the division was doing. I hope Michelle Masters doesn't catch wind of this; it's got nothing to do with how she's been doing as champion. Personally, I think she's a great Cruiserweight Champion, and I know Miss Ivy agrees, which is why she sent her to me. It's just that Miss Ivy has had some kind of hand in my training, philosophy, and career, literally, since I was fourteen. She doesn't know Michelle well enough to not wonder how the division is doing. Yet. If I can pull a victory off against her, then of course I'll have earned a title shot by virtue of defeating the reigning champion, and if she wins, then I need more refining work. Simple as that. Besides, with all due respect to the champions of years past, there's other reasons to wrestle for a title besides 'the opportunity fell into my lap'. Of course, I'm still the new guy here. I'm not surprised that my message is falling on deaf ears. Disappointed, yes, but not surprised. Not surprised, because I'm the new guy. Disappointed, because the message is what matters, even if the messenger is not important. But, we'll do what we'll do because that's all we can do. I hope Michelle is ready for me, because I'm not going to give her any slack. And I don't expect any in return.
