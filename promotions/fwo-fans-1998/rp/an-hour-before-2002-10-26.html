An hour before.

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tonight's the night. In front of thousands of fans Brand Frontier will make his Cyberslam debut. The road here has been a tumultuous one to say the least, especially this last year or so. It has seemed that no matter the road traveled there was always a roadblock of great magnitude standing in his way. When Brandon Roche and his friends sat around their college dorm and watched the first ever Cyberslam, the Cyberslam from this very building, little did he realize that five short years later he would be a performer on the card. At that time he had just broken into the independent wrestling scene. He knew that he had a long road ahead of him before he would be able to compete on the level of the fWo. He also knew that there would be hell to pay to get here. That turned out to be the understatement of the decade. Everyone has setbacks. Everyone lives through his or her own version of a soap opera. To everyone his or her story is the worst, or is it the best? Brandon Roche knows that even though he has survived through so much, others have endured more. He isn't trying to play down what others have experienced, he just knows that for who he is and what he has done many would not have been so lucky. The beatings, the bruises, the torture put forth to his family he never would have imagined that it would have been so hard. He never would have imagined that it would have been so fulfilling. Here is he is though. Sitting in the locker room at the Arrow Head Pond Arena one hour before Cyberslam V goes live and makes its mark in the wrestling annals. The butterflies are expected, almost welcome. He has always performed his best under pressure. Now though it's a different matter. Now his thoughts and feelings swirl around at a hundred miles per hour. He is filled with so much it almost hurts, anger, betrayal, resentment...fear. "Daddy, can we go?" Ah. Sean Christian, his oldest son. His voice and that of Jarod's always brought him back to his center. He had gone against his mother's wishes and brought his eldest to the event anyway. Ana, his now estranged wife was forever against Brandon mingling business and family. Every time he had done so it had ended with disastrous results. He needed him here tonight though. He needed focus. There was too much going on for him to maintain by himself. With a look that would melt maybe even the black heart of Ric Chronos Sean asked again, "Daddy, can we go to the soov'neir shop now? Please?" Brand's new shirt would be released tonight and Sean was anxious to get one of the first. To him this whole thing was a game.
