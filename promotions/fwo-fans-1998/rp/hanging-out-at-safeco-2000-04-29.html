Hanging out at Safeco....

29 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Hanging out at Safeco.... April 29, 2000 Safeco Field. Cyberslam. A grand, new facility...and the most celebrated event in e-wrestling are about to collide in about 6 hours. The field crew is bustling about, covering the grass and filling the chairs into the field. The ring is just about completed, as the ring ropes are being tightened into position. Erik Kelly looks about at his surroundings. He wears his Boston Red Sox jersey with the number '6' on the back, and his name. If he ever played ball, that was going to be his number. He imagined himself playing against the Mariners...who could've had Perfect Scott Slugger on the team. He would've patroled right field. At Fenway, no one has covered that part of the stadium very well since Dwight Evans. Problem is he's just too big now. He scratches the goatee on his chin, thinking more to himself about the upcoming task at hand. And then his eyes stop while scanning the nearby bleachers. Crusader sits, apparently entranced at his surroundings as well. Both of them would enter the ring together as opponents this evening. Both would probably be nervous with the magnitude of the event and that they are in it...and both would give it their absolute all tonight. Erik walks over to Crusader. "Hey Cody", Erik calls out, as he starts to take a seat a couple seats away. "Hey Erik", as Crusader looks up. "What are you doing here early?" Erik grins. "Probably the same thing you're doing. Making sure the butterflies don't get the better of me tonight." Cody nods in agreement. "Yeah, the pressure is certainly on for the both of us. Could you imagine the atmosphere of this very arena?" Erik looks about. The more he thinks about it, the more the adrenalin begins to pump. "Well, I know we've both been around in our fair share of arenas and main events before in other organizations...but you're absolutely right...Safeco Field...this place is going to hold so many individuals. It will make anything I've done in the past pale in comparison..." Crusader is quiet a few moments, then replies. "Exactly. It's as if everything that I've ever done before has been forgotten. I've never been a part of a pay-per-view with this much hype, this much emotion, and this much meaning. I've never felt this way before in my life." Erik ponders a moment, and decides to ask this question. "You came out to help me on Wednesday night when Strangler was getting jumped by those FTfWo assholes...what do you think about the main event and what could happen to all of us with the results?" Cody doesn't waste much time responding. "It scares me half to death when I think about Dead winning. If Dead wins, both you and I know that our hopes and dreams will be discouraged and severed. I don't know Strangler or Dead that well, but I do know one thing, I stand opposed to the FTfWo." Erik simply nods, watching one crew member have a hard time assembling a few chairs that tipped over. "You got that right. This could almost be hell on earth if that son of a bitch wins..." Erik tries to change the subject. "We should probably think about our match instead. We'll put on a show for these fans, man." Crusader looks out at the empty seats. "Do you remember my first promo in the fWo?" Erik nods while Cody continues his comments. "I said that the fWo's mission was always to fill these seats with fans. The way I see it, is that it's our job to keep them there. It's our job to deliver the product. I'm not focused on ripping your head off tonight, I'm focused on raising the standards and the lines." Erik nods. "I agree. Same feelings from me, Brother. You're a good guy. We've gotten to hang out together and do some promotions. I respect what you're about. Just don't take that enraged beast approach I have in the ring personally. We both know in the ring it's all about business, but out of the ring...it's all about sharing a beer afterwards." As Erik grins. "And there ain't a daaaaaamn thing you can do about that." As he laughs Crusader grins, as Erik starts to get up to his feet and walks away. "I'll see you tonight.", Crusader calls out. "You bet. Have a good one until then, man." Erik says as he walks away. As he does, the camera fades to black.
