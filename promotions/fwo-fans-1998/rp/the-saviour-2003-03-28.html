The Saviour

28 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was dark again. Always so dark, that she could not see. And yet, there she was, blonde hair flowing and hand clutching her crucifix, praying for her saviour to come and take her away from this prision of hatred, and bring her someplace where she could extend her wings and fly far away. So very far away. Suddenly, she arrived. .Two, here to greet another alter-ego of Kali Saturos. "Hello, Jessica." .Two grinned. "Enjoying your stay?" Jessica did not reply. She simply began to pray harder. "Your God cannot help you now." .Two laughed. "Your God has no power here. I doubt He has any power anywhere." "You're wrong." Jessica whispered. "What was that?" .Two snapped, a bit surprised that Jessica would stand up to her. Jessica stood up, right hand still clutching the crucifix around her neck. "You're wrong." she said again, this time much louder. "God will save me. He will set me free." But if there was any anger in .Two, she did not show it. She simply laughed and sat down on the stone across from where Jessica was previously sitting. This seemed to make Jessica uncomfortable. "The only God here is Chaos, Jessica." .Two said with another laugh. "Why are you laughing?" Jessica asked desperatley. "Because your faith in your saviour is humorous, if not pathetic." .Two replied. "But don't worry, Jessica. Not everything you believe in is wrong. When you die, you will indeed be judged. Just....not by the judge you were expecting." With another laugh, .Two walked off, leaving Jessica to sit back down on the stone and ponder her own fate. "My Saviour......He will come." she said to herself. "He has to. I can't take much more of this." But then, someone did come. A Saviour. Just not the one Jessica was expecting.
