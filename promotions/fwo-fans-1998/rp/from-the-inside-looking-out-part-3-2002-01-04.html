He had been talking for hours; I had heard merely one word of it all... "Please..." It seemed to keep repeating all throughout what he had to say. The word would echo all around the room, bouncing off the walls and smacking right back into my face...time after time...please after please. "Please..." "Please..." "Please..." Be a man Steve, tell this fucker what you really think. He's a waste of space Steve, a mindless minion that doesn't deserve to step upon the same soil as you. Tell the fucker what he really is, tell him he's pathetic. First of all, get him to stop talking, his useless chatter is annoying me. "Shut up Angel," I turned around to look at him in the eyes. He could tell I was not amused by his presence, or even in the slightest bit happy to see him. After all, it had been nearly a month. His shock could not be wiped away from his expression, he leaned a slight bit more out of his seat, "Steve...I?" Well what are you waiting for? A fucking bell to ring? "You what?" Dalton annoyed me, his useless chatter about how good of a person I was began to make me think he thought of me more than just a friend. I was tired of listening to all the reasons on why people hated me, I was tired most of all of people, "You what Angel? You're sorry? You want to tell me everything will be okay? You want to cradle poor little Steve, poor tormented Steve and make sure he has the strength to smile? Well fuck you Angel! Fuck you!" He went to stand, I extended my palm into his chest knocking him back in his seat. I slowly leaned in closer grabbing the collar of his shirt and pulling his fragile body closer to mine. Just so he could smell my breath burning his skin. "It's so simple Angel," I gripped his collar harder causing pressure of his neck, "It's so fucking simple! Yet no one ever sees it..."
