Phone Call

17 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up inside of the French Foundation limousine, with Courage, and The Flying Frenchie, notably absent. We see Primetime talking on his cell phone, and wearing a Courage T-Shirt.) "Yeah, Wednesday. Good job, Chris. The fat man and his little friend Austin the Jobber will pay for what they've done. What's that? Yeah yeah, I know you want the fat man at Twilight, but why should a superstar like you fight a worthless piece of trash like Jake. Trash, like the Korn song. What? Payback. Yeah, but don't you think we'll get enough payback on Warfare? Fine fine have it your way, Chris. I'm not gonna argue. What? Protecting the fatman? Dammit, Chris, you must have a short term memory. Twilight. His fat ass is making me go one on one with Paul, with the Foundation banned from ringside. That fat fuck is gonna pay dearly, and you know that they say paybacks are a bitch. Title situation? Well, we've got TFF and I gunning for the World Title, and well...there's the Hardcore Title, which a real wrestler like yourself doesn't deserve. Cruiserweight? Who has that belt again? Oh. The Millenium Man. Whoop-di-do. You've already beaten Eron the Talentless. Bo-rron, whatever. So what's left? Internet? Take that thing, make it the Rock and Roll title, do something to get it off of that wacko Rain and his future men or whatever. Yeah. Internet. What? You've got to go? Alright Chris, talk to ya later. Bye." (PT hangs the phone up, finally noticing the camera.) Can't anyone get any privacy? Trying to have a conversation. Dammit, get out, I need to call Pierre. (Scene closes, as we can hear PT say "Hey Pierre, I was talking to Chris....")
