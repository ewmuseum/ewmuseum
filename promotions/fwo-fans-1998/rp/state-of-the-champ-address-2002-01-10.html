State Of The Champ Address

10 January 2002

The following is a reproduction of a commentary from www.blackplaguerr.com (NO NOT REALLY) allowed to be seen via www.fwowrestling.com. Must be over 18, rated R, patrons not admitted during the last 15 minutes, etc... ****************************************************** January 10 (11?), 2002 Paris, France Well, happy new year. Sorry I've been away so long, but between various illnesses, personal drama, and good ol' sloth, I haven't gotten a chance just to rap with all the online Plague Carriers. So, let's get into the scope of recent events... The Good Obviously, my World Title win to end last year (seems so odd saying that) was great. Nobody backstage told me Andrea was coming back, so that was a nice bonus addition. We're back together. I know, I know, AWWW. Anyways, she's going back to Berkeley, so you can send your porno to...I'm kidding, 'Dre! KIDDING! Don't hurt me. :( The match against Slugger we had on Sunday was a classic. I was nicely surprised that we were able to put together such a quality match for a first-time matchup. I got dragged kicking and screaming into the digital era, as I got more MBs for my portable mp3 player. In lieu of, you know, actual presents. Not really: I got a red Yankees cap with the flag inside the NY (the only way you'll see me wearing Yankee shit, lemme tell ya), some various clothes, and a nice new Flames jersey with Ross #1 on the back. Very cool. Also, finally gave in and had my brother buy a DVD player. He set it up and got my old VCR in return. So goodie for him. Bastard. :) The Bad As an avowed pessimist, I hate to say it, but almost nothing is wrong with my life right now. If this thing's cursed, it's taking a while to set in. Well, one complaint: I hate traveling out of the country. It's nothing personal against the countries, it's just that my time and internal clocks are FUBAR. Like, right now, it's lunch time in Paris, but I'm about to go to bed because it's like 1 back home. Bizarre, I know. Also, because I'm barely competent in English and we're not going to Spain, I stay inside a lot and run up the room service bill. On FOOD, ya pervs. Also: no carne asada burritos in France, dammit. The Ugly Some guy dressed up like the Frenchie ran towards me yelling RUAHH~! all the way, spat on me, and ran back where he came from yesterday. Bah. I suppose that's everything out of the ring that's pertinent in the life of ol' Rube for right now. I'll try not to be a stranger. And thanks to The News for calling me #1. I guess they did like it AND they sat down and wrote it. Shut up, that was funny. Catch you later, Ruben Ross
