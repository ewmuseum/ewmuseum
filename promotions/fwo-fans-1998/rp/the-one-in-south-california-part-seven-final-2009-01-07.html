The One In South California - Part Seven (Final)

7 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
And now the exciting conclusion (pages 101 to 110) of... The One In South California. *** *** *** *** *** *** INT. HONDA CIVIC Lowell sits in the car, buckled up on the passenger's side. He holds the Nokia to his ear. It sounds as though he's in the middle of a conversation: LOWELL …And I'm like, I don't wanna barter. I'm not a fuckin' kike. But this fuckin'... s'gotta be some heroin addict from Venice... he thinks he can talk me down! I'm like "Nigga, we already played this game. I knocked off fifty fuckin'-percent, what more do you want?" Those fuckin' scumbags will reach into your Chinos and haul out your dick if you let `em. Thirty bucks for a DVD player… I think that's more than fair, don't you? This was back in '04, mind you, so they were still reasonably expensive. (beat) ...No, I know we don't know each other. (beat) ...Yeah, and? (beat) I just thought you'd wanna hear about it. Seeing as I accidentally called you, we might as well make the most of it. (beat) ...Well, fine! Fuck you, too, dude! (beat) Asshole. Lowell presses the red 'END' button on the phone and sits, a bit perturbed. The glove compartment OPENS and Lowell gazes inside. He starts pulling random things out and looking them over: car manual, condoms, a roadside flare, mace, etc. A VOICE on the radio grabs his attention and he turns the volume dial up. RADIO PERSONALITY And we wanna hear your most BAD ASS story! Everyone's got one! One truly BAD ASS thing you've done! We've all got a story worth tellin'! And we're dedicating this whole hour to you, the listeners, so call in! We wanna hear it! Lowell thinks for a moment, then dials the number given after the DJ's finished speaking. RADIO PERSONALITY (CONT'D) Yes! You're on the air with Billy BAAAD and Joey Thoooorrrrnn! The two baddest DJs in the great state of Cali, baby! What's your name? Give us a name, we need a name, we don't got all day! LOWELL My name's Lowell! RADIO PERSONALITY Thanks for callin' in, Lloyd! And have you got a story so BAD ASS it needs to be shared with the rest of the world? LOWELL Do I ever! OK, so check this: I busted out of prison, like, three days ago, right? Blew that popsicle stand, now me and a few of my best buds are trying to steal a bunch of coke from this big drug lord guy and the best part is, check it: we're doin' it! We're gettin' away with it! Shit's goin' off without a hitch! We're a bunch of do-dirt niggas! So what's my prize, motherfucker? RADIO PERSONALITY (Dubious sounding) Come on now, maaan, I knew a lot of these stories were gonna be fakes; we expected fabrications. When you invite your entire audience to call in and tell a bad ass story, no one's gonna call in and regale us with some particularly exciting romp around the neighbourhood, eggin' houses and knockin' on doors. But that stuff is so far fetched... Your attempt at wowing us was just lame, dude. Sorry, but you failed. Go back to writing fan fiction crime capers in your mom's basement. Loser! The call disconnects and Lowell's ear perks up and he turns in his seat... Gunfire. CUT TO: INT. JOEY DYNAMO'S HOUSE Still crouched behind the couch, Nuñez examines his assault rifle. The last round he fired seems to have caused a jam. One of the thugs reenters the living room, holding a GLOCK 31 pistol with two hands close to his chest, leaving the other thug behind in the kitchen to reload both of their AKs. THUG #3 (O.S.) Yo! You sure y'good with that nine!? He's not. We hear the pulsating beat of his heart. He's uneasy, discomposed, arms not as steady as they should be. He wets his lips. On the other side of the couch, Nuñez, too, attempts to maintain his composure, frantically working to fix the problem that's robbed him of his only means of defending himself. Over the edge of the couch Thug #2 extends both arms, pointing his gun, moving closer. THUG #2 Naw dawg, I think he's hurt, I think I got him... probably dead. In a blinding flash, Nuñez rises, his short, compact, yet intimidating physical presence felt by Thug #2, who can't react quickly enough to pull the trigger and stop Nuñez from SHATTERING his nose with a vicious uppercut shot with the butt of his submachine gun. A MIST OF BLOOD fills the air, coating Nuñez' menacing profile. The body of Thug #2 drops straight to the floor, displaying Nuñez in full. He walks around the couch and toward the kitchen. He raises his gun, points, and FIRES. A bottle of dark rum sitting on the counter BLOWS UP. Glass shards, a spatter of alcohol, all down over the counter and onto the kitchen floor. Nuñez marches like the street soldier that he is into the kitchen, unwarily. Thug #3's hands are shaking and, as a result, he keeps dropping the bullets he's trying to load into his gun. One clangs against the floor rolls next to Nuñez' feet. He bends down and picks it up, examining it with a smirk. NUÑEZ Nigga, you fucked. You KNOW you fucked. So now lemme show you just how fucked you are. Nuñez moves closer, so that he's standing between Thug #3's legs, which make a 'V', as he sits with his back to the counter. The AK sits in front of him. He cowers, curling up and shielding his face with his arms. Nuñez bends down and casually picks up the AK. He pulls out the empty banana clip, inserts the bullet, and jams it back in. He expertly holds the gun, aiming it at Thug #3's head. THUG #3 I'm sorry, man. Just, look, I don't even know what the fuck this is, man. I don't know who you are! I don't know who you work for, I'm just here on separate business, man. Just... Wait. Thug #3 reaches around to his back pocket... and DRAWS OUT A KNIFE, attempting to stab Nuñez in the abdomen. Nuñez reacts posthaste and CRACKS the knife out of Thug #3's hand with the AK. It slides across the floor and stops when it hits a dog dish containing little-to-no food or water. NUÑEZ That was the wrong move to make, man. We could'a had this shit over quick. You coulda been makin' friends in the afterlife already. Nuñez taps the side of the AK and nods. NUÑEZ (CONT'D) Coulda' been your exit, nigga. Would'a been relatively painless too. Y'never would'a felt the bullet enter ya skull. But then again no one I know's experienced it and been around afterward to confirm or deny. What do you think, holmes? Huh? Y'think I'm right, nigga? Nuñez playfully jabs him in the chest with the end of the AK. Thug #3 cannot bring himself to look Nuñez in the eye. Nuñez examines the AK and then sets it down on the counter next to him for later use. He won't need it right now anyway, not for what he has in mind. NUÑEZ (CONT'D) I was gonna put one of those long, explosive muh'fuckas between your eyes and see what comes out the back. Guessin' nothin' judgin' from the shit you just pulled. Now I'ma' give you some street justice. You ain't gon' look pretty in the afterlife, son, that's f'DAMN sure. I'ma' stomp out yo' two-bit, coke peddlin' bitch ass like a cheap Indian cig, nigga. Thug #3's eyes widen, bottom lip quivering. The bottom of Nuñez' boot violently IMPACTS Thug #3's face several times, resulting in a broken nose, a cut lip, and several other facial lacerations. Thug #3 spits a pinkish fluid onto the tile, his gums coated with blood. His head flops around his shoulders like he's drunk and unable to support it. His head finally settles, resting against the cupboard door behind it, semi-conscious, breathing heavily and gurgling blood in his throat. Another STOMP fully CRUNCHES the cartilage in what's left of his nose, blood gushing down over his mouth and chin and onto his white tee-shirt. The final STOMP, the one that follows, is the most powerful of the series and forces Thug #3's head through the cupboard door, cracking it down the middle, splitting the two sides apart, causing Thug #3's skull to painfully land on a row of some canned foods stored inside. Nuñez kicks him in the groin, but it elicits no response, verbal or physical, confirming that Thug #3 has indeed expired. Nuñez then turns and grabs the AK off the counter. EXT. OUTSIDE IN GARDEN Dean pushes himself up out of the dirt and slowly brushes himself off. He sizes up the perimeter of the house, peeking in a side window before punching it open and climbing through, into the house. He looks over to Nuñez and then proceeds forward, only to have a bullet nearly miss him and SHATTER the glass in a large display case in the dining room. Dean immediately takes cover and nods fro Nuñez to go the opposite way. Dean, meanwhile, is completely unsure of where the other thug might be, and he continuously looks from left to right to be prepared. Suddenly, he feels the cold of a 9mm handgun against his temple. THUG #4 You muh'fuckers love to say that badass shit before you kill somebody, right? Your nigger friend can't save you now, he went out back. So it's my turn to be the badass. Suddenly, wild gunfire illuminates the room. Lowell stands in the doorframe, shooting wildly without taking aim first. It's plainly obvious he's never fired a gun before. The glass surface of a Joey Dynamo self-portrait hanging above the fireplace SHATTERS and falls to the floor. The walls SPIT drywall particles. Random things BREAK. A bullet grazes the neck of Thug #4, drawing blood, and he staggers away from Dean, one hand covering the wound, as he turns his gun on Lowell. He's hit with two more shots to the torso before he can retaliate and drops lifelessly with a resounding THUD. Lowell opens his eyes when the BANGS turn to hollow clicks. He lowers the gun to his side and scans the room, seeing the damage and the body of Thug #4 lying in a pool of blood a few feet away from where Dean's standing. LOWELL Dude, that was so ill! I, like, totally saved you there! You owe me your life! DEAN Yeah, Low. I can't wait for the day when I finally get to save yours. Lowell nods, shoving the barrel of the gun in his pants pocket as if it were a holster, a self-satisfied smirk on his face. LOWELL It'll come, Dean-o, it'll come. Watching all of this from above is the third man, who aims his AK-47 downward as he runs down the stairs, firing in all directions to give himself enough of a buffer to run out the front door. OUTSIDE He hustles toward the white Honda, which he promptly enters. THUG #1 (On a cell phone) I don't know who the fuck it is, but the house was covered. Joey's dead and they emptied the place. What? No! The van's still in the back, I'm not doing that shit alone! Dean and Nuñez chase after him, getting to the front door just in time to see the man screech away from the curb. They stop and look to Lowell. DEAN The keys. You... NUÑEZ You left the muh'fucking KEYS IN THE IGNITION! LOWELL I saved your asses! Who came in here like BANGBANGBANG and fucked up everybody's shit?! That's right, me! Lowell! I brought the mothafuckin' ruckus and you guys are so fucking ungrateful right now! DEAN Yeah. You did fuck up everybody's shit. And now we have probably about 30 seconds to get the fuck out of here before Pasadena PD comes and fucks our shit up even more. If the keys aren't in that van, we're fucked. Seriously. With a capital "fuck." (Shouting) Cassidy, we gotta get the fuck outta here. Dean leads the discouraged pack to the backyard and opens the driver's side of the van, finding the keys on the seat. DEAN (CONT'D) Well I'll be fucked. Those guys are as stupid as you, Lowell. Now let's leave while our streak of unbelievable luck has just begun. Lowell, you're in the back, because if I see your face, I can't be held responsible for what I might do to it. Dean starts the van as Cassidy opens the passenger's side door, one hand holding her new puppy, and gets in. Nuñez has a cigarette, sighing in disbelief that they lost everything. Lowell slowly pulls open the van door and crawls in, sitting silently for a moment before turning and looking in the back of the van. LOWELL Holy fuck. HOLY FUCKING FUCK! GUYS! GUYS, HOLY SHIT! DEAN Lowell, we don't have time for your shit. Nuñez drops the cigarette and peeks his head into the van. NUÑEZ God. Damn. Dean turns and looks over his seat. His eyes become unusually and unbelievably large at the sight of more cocaine, marijuana, and assorted sacks of pills than one could ever imagine. Around the front of the house, a barrage of sirens blare, lights flashing and illuminating the night sky. FADE TO BLACK.
