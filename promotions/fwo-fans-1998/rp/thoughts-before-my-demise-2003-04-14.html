Thoughts before my demise

14 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The sound of doctors and nurses, scattering around the facility best described the vibe in the hospital that evening. He had been there for two days now... the shock, the loss of blood... it all became too much for him to bear, and being a person who had never been in that situation before, he had no idea how to handle it. He was carried from his helpless position on a stretcher, and immediately placed into an ambulance. ...he didn't like ambulances. They reminded him of death, even if the person inside wasn't dying. The whole adrenaline rush of it all, made him a bit... uneasy. Medical staff, rushing to the ambulance, entrusting themselves in a stranger on a phone. The ambulance, rushes to the destination, trampling any and every person, or thing in its way. Then you get to your destination. The staff places you in the ambulance, and if the situation is bad, chances are, it's worse by the time you actually make it to the hospital, if your injury was life threatening, chances are... you were dead. ...he didn't like the ambulance. That whole ride was traumatizing. He felt like his emotional walls were closing in on him. Like he couldn't breathe. It was hot... then, cold... then, hot again. His mind was racing, as the physicians barked their commands. They asked him to stay still... to lay back... not to look at his wound. But, he couldn't help it, nobody can... and, the more he saw, the weaker his stomach got... and, the harder it was to stay conscious. He did finally make it to the hospital that Sunday night. But, his story... was like most stories, in situations similar. It was too late... he had lost too much blood, and the medical staff immediately hooked him to life support, in an attempt to save preserve whatever life he had left. In his mind, were thoughts of the life he lived. His family. His best friend. At that moment, he thought about all the terrible deeds he had done. The women he had played... the shrewdness of his business practices. But, he *had* to be he thought to himself... it was a cruel world he lived in, and you had to be just as cruel to survive, right? Wrong, a voice cried out. He tried to ignore it... his mind drifted back to his life. His parents... and, the wonderful relationship they had. His girlfriend... and, all that they had been through to get to this point in their relationship. He couldn't just leave her... she needed him, and he needed her just as much. He couldn't imagine her with anybody else... they had made some many plans to live life together. They both wanted kids... and, a less hectic lifestyle, away from all the craziness that they both had faced daily. ...they had similar careers, but played different parts in it. A tear fell down his cheek as he felt it all slipping away from him. His life... he no longer had control over it. He tried to keep his eyes open... but, eventually... he began to accept the fact that he couldn't. It didn't hurt as much anymore... and, the pain that he had felt from the wound he had received, no longer bothered him anymore. He was a fighter... he always was... but, this was a fight against the angel of death, a fight no man can win... Flat- line. The doctor's face told a tale in a way words couldn't as he left the surgery room, to face the girlfriend, and his family. She immediately fell to her knees, shivering, wondering what life would be like without her perfect mate. His parents... a bit older, much more mature, were just as saddened... but, their cries weren't nearly as hard. That was their kid... but, when it was all said and done, they had each other. The girlfriend was alone... ...again. ---- "We've definitely gotta be more careful, Sean...." Ivy began. "Do you see that? Those people are destroyed, and I would be just the same, if you left me." Triple X turned to face Poison Ivy, forcing a smile... but, his exterior was that of a mad man. "I'm not going anywhere, Ivy. I wouldn't give any of my enemies the pleasure. Plus..." he turned to her, trapping her against the wall. "I'd have to come back and haunt your ass, if you dated anyone else." Ivy giggled, fighting for position, finally... she gave in, and puckered up, as Sean leaned in and kissed her gently. "But, seriously though... you've gotta handle this at CyberSlam. I almost had a heart attack when the hospital called my cell, talking about you experiencing pain in the back of your head. Eli, too... and, let's not even get on Amy... she wanted to come here with you, herself, but I told her to stay and wrestle her match, and that you'd call her the minute you had a moment." "Gotta love, Amy." "Sean, promise me there won't be anymore late night calls." Triple X paused for a moment, almost as if he were thinking it over. "Ivy... I can't promise you that... I don't know what whoever it was that attacked us is thinking, or where he'll strike next. But, what I *can* and *will* promise you is, I'll handle this mess before it gets any messier." "Can it get messier?" she asked. "If the rumors are true... I think it can." And, with that, they stepped out of the hospital into the cold night air. Together. Whole. In one piece.
