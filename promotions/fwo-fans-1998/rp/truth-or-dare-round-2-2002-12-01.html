Truth or Dare - Round 2

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All you read and wear or see and hear on TV is a product begging for your fatass, dirty dollar... So shut up and buy. Send more money... "Fuck you, buddy." Amy was still trudging along when she walked, almost tripping over her own feet. Even then, so many outside things engulfed her head; the media, her reputation, life...but it all lead back to that article, and the woman who wrote it. Unfortunately, a man almost ten years her senior had stumbled into that little force field, approaching the still irate redhead about the very reason for her rage. The result wasn't pretty, and the man had walked away just a little more than skeptical of Aimz, who'd flipped him off and uttered those harsh words, not even stopping to realize that the guy was supporting her instead of falling into the lie. The only way to reassure her self was repetition. Over and over again...'Hey, at least I didn't lose tonight.' The redhead hadn't noticed the wet droplets that had started to adorn her forehead, but soon after her hair was dripping, she'd clued in, muttering to herself, "Damn rain." She'd left early after an unusually chaotic atmosphere broke out backstage. A need for some extra exercise and a 'sunny' weather report had saved her from wasting the money on a rental car...but the pouring, ice-cold winter rain was forcing her to regret the stupidity. At least four blocks back to the hotel now, by then she'd be guaranteed pneumonia. ...or maybe not? Just as Amy found the sense to pull up the hood on her sweatshirt, she'd heard a car slow and come closely up to the side of her. As always, her heart rate picked up. She was, for some reason, constantly nagged at with paranoia of attacks from behind...but this time it was, for all intents and purposes, her guardian angel. Instead of seeing the hardened hardcore personae that was intended the man known as Brand Frontier saw a scared kid. As much as she pretended not to be, he could see fear in her eyes. That fear, whether it was from the unknown or past horrors that have been committed, seemed to drive her. He motioned for her to get in the car...it was a risk, but she was willing to do anything to get out of the cold at that point. When Amy crawled in, Brandon continued a slow pace through the sparse late-night traffic and noticed how she remained hunched near the door, with fingers gripped tightly around the handle as she cast him a perfectly silent sideways glance. "Good job out there kid." The words seemed sincere and meant to ease the mud-thick tension in the small car. "I hit one move throughout the whole thing, the rest of the time I was on the floor. I don't call that a very good job." She stared at Brand with an untrusting, unblinking gaze but tried not to let her thoughts come to light. Brandon was almost startled that he got such a quick response from the young superstar in the making. "It took a lot of guts to simply walk out there on that stage not knowing what was going to happen. I've pretty much had it with the Councils booking on the fly myself. That's one of the reasons I left early." Amy shrugged, trying to look cool-headed by reclining back a little, placing hands in the pockets of her un-fitting sweater. "I like to keep all limbs firmly attached, I just got outta there...is everybody always practically walking on landmines?" With a knowing nod again Brandon agrees. "Ever since the contest it seems like there is always someone trying to take over the company or jerk someone off or around." It was a spiteful comment but the man known as Brand Frontier had grown weary of the politics indeed. If he didn't love the sport as much as he did it would have been cause for him to walk away and never look back. "Well...it definitely hasn't been the joy ride I expected so far." She shrugged, boasting the usual pessimistic, down-to-earth sayings. "But that's just the price of admission to the 'big time', eh?" "Here I go sounding like an old-timer already but I've been in the 'big time' over and over. From the E.W.A. to the G.W.O., it's all the same. People want more than they deserve, take what they shouldn't and to hell with everyone else." "I think people should just accept responsibility for their actions. Whether it's in the life we lead 'backstage' or here in the real world. If you put yourself into a situation be prepared to back it up." Brandon realizes the tangent he begins to get off on and lets his voice trail off. He offered the youngster a ride out of courtesy not so he could have an audience for his soapbox diatribe. "People want more than they deserve or don't get what they've earned...but I think I can manage to hold my own, I always seem to." Amy smirked, letting herself relax for a rare moment to rib the 'veteran', lightening the sincere question she followed it with. "How about you, pops? Ever found something more trouble than you bargained for?" Brandon could see the glimmer in Amy's eyes. A whimsical nature he did not expect but it was welcomed all the same. "Every day of my life dear. Every day of my life." He looked back around and noticed that they had passed the hotel. He hadn't even thought to ask where she was staying. As usual he had made arrangements with Eli and Ivy but had not even considered her travel plans. "Um... did you get booked with the troop or did you make your own travel plans?" "I don't really think I quite fit with the rest of the flock...I'd probably want to choke half, or vice versa. So no, I'm staying.... actually, about a block back that way." She pointed with her thumb behind her, smiling a little at the error. "Hmmm." the flawless Paragon looks at the 'no u-turn' signs for the next three lights before he whips into a Wendy's restaurant and heads around by the drive-thru in an attempt to correct his direction. "Not fitting in is a good thing. Ivy often says I stay more to myself than any of the so-called superstars. She commends me for my work ethic. Truth be told though I've just gotten screwed over too many times to try and make many real friends. It's just the way it is." "Heh, kinda odd how similar we are. I generally don't get close to people...they seem to find endless fun in causing me un-necessary drama, or just stabbing me in the back. But that's the way the world is, I guess." Amy looked to her feet nervously a moment, but glanced up at Brandon as if sizing him up in a way. "...I honestly don't trust anybody in fWo. I see all this betrayal, 'rebellion' and shit...it just doesn't give me reason to. Everybody's out for themselves, I guess that's the way it's gotta be to get anywhere." With a shrug of his shoulders Brandon all but dismisses her last thought. "I guess we can chalk it up to society, but I've always found that the easy way out and Heaven knows I've never been one for the easy road. I actually think you should have a choice. There isn't a responsibility that should be abandoned no matter the end results." Glancing over to his passenger Brandon realized that even as she spoke her words of distrust she wanted just the opposite. "I believe in doing what you say and saying what you do. This will probably have your eyes rolling in your head but my Mom hammered that in to me for as long as I can remember." "No no...I live by that." It was then that a rarity occurred; Aimz flashed a warm smile to someone she'd not even known an entire day. "Surprise surprise, eh? I actually listened to my mom once in a while." He returned the smile. A genuine one, not the plastic smile of The Paragon but one from Brandon Roche. He just spoke on not getting close with the other workers in the fWo and here he was actually talking about his family with a newer one than the ones he called friend. Maybe it wasn't that bad after all. Maybe he had found a kindred spirit amongst the turmoil that was their lives. Amy was thinking the exact same thing. Had she found a new friend? After all, Brand seemed awfully similar to her, and she'd never heard tell of him betraying anybody. By the time Amy had words coming to mind again, Brandon had already pulled into the parking lot of the Best Western. He gave her another smile and a nod as she stepped out, casually waving back at him while running to get into the hotel and out of the seemingly torrential downpour that had developed. Fortunately, she thought, Brandon hadn't seemed to notice the column that had all but ruined her life for the past few days. Unfortunately, Amy hadn't noticed the newssheet that had fallen out of her pocket and to the side of the stick shift, which the Mighty Hero would surely notice. Regardless, if she'd finally found a peer to open up to, it wouldn't happen as gradually as she'd hoped because, without the knowledge of either 'victim', someone else was already "opening up" the budding friendship for them... Paragonare - The discovery continues. [This has been an Aimz/Brand Frontier collaboration.]
