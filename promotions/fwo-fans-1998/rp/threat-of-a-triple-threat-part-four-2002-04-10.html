Threat Of A Triple Threat (part four)

10 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"So, what I'm basically saying, is that, because we hate each other...I think because one of us turned on the other one a couple months ago or something...it's only natural that we put our differences aside, and form a tag team for the biggest event of the year." *Shimmy* La Parka was now also getting his nails done by Claire, as he sat in a chair directly next to Scott Hart, and shimmied in agreement with Hart's statement. Soon after, Mike Heftel and Jackhammer walked into the makeup area, with Heftel greeting Claire. "Hey Claire! Kevin wants his hair highlighted and I want my head waxed. Oh, wait..." Hart and La Parka seemed angry that they were interrupted. "Hey! That's our manager you're talking to!" "Your manager?", asked Jackhammer. "Yeah! She's managing me and La Parka at Cyberslam!" "I am?", said a confused Claire. "She is? Who are you guys facing?", asked Heftel. "Uh...La Parka?" *Shimmy* Neither man seemed sure, and in walked Sphere and Mr. T. "Well, well, well. If it isn't my tag team partner for Cybaslam." "YOUR tag team partner?", Hart asked. "Yes, that's right." Hart got an idea. "Hmm...now, I'm no rocket scientist, although I may be a regular scientist, I'm not quite sure, but it seems to me that we have two tag teams without a match for Cyberslam..." Before Hart could finish, Abusah and Evil-Lyn entered the makeup area. "HAY MYKE! SHEEZ ARE MANUHGER CUZ SHEE HAZ BOOBEEZ!" Claire was still shocked. "Mike...SHE is managing you?", she asked while pointing at Evil-Lyn. "Apparently. I wonder if I have to sign more papers. I hope not, cause I have a hard time writing in cursive." Heftel continued to wonder, as Claire speared Evil-Lyn to the concrete, and Sphere sorted everything out. "Listen, listen up, all you primitive...screwheads. I just thought of a way to solve this problem. We have three tag teams, each with a manger. None of these teams have yet to be added to the Cybaslam card. I propose a triple threat tag team match. It will be Tall Big Bald Oaf and myself on one team, managed by Earl T. The second team will consist of Lord Pah-ka and Dr. Hart, who will be managed by...the wench on...top there...and finally, the third team...the two bloody re-tahds, managed by...the wench on the bottom there." Hart seemed to like the idea. "Yes, I believe a bout like that would solve everything." "Solve what, exactly?", asked Jackhammer. *Shimmy* Hart turned to La Parka. "Yeah, I don't know either. Let's get out of here." Hart and La Parka get out of their chairs, step over Claire and Evil-Lyn, and leave the area. "WEIGHT FOUR MEE!", shouts Abusah, as he attempts to run after them, but trips over Claire and Evil-Lyn. "HEH HEH PRITTEE LADEEZ VARY CLOZE!" Sphere motioned to Mr. T. "Come, Earl T. Let us inform the people that Cybaslam's comic relief match has been officially announced." Sphere and Mr. T leave, as Heftel continues to wonder about contract signings, and Jackhammer slumps down in a chair. "May I ask why the camera is on me?" The end...for now. And after hearing that this match was officially announced, you'd better pray to God that Cyberslam doesn't come too soon.
