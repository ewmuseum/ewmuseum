fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Dream on (part two) March 01, 2000 What are we gonna do? What are we gonna do? It was all fucked. They had come in here without understanding what was going on. He knew of course, and he tried to warn them. Seal the doors... torches... we need torches, now! Machina turned to the panel and looked at the flashing lights through red eyes. Atmospheric conditions are shit and the core temperature is way too high. Unless they get off this planet and fast they are all going to die. Machina? Machina turned and saw Tazz holding his bear. What is it honey? He was scared. Truth was, they all were. Bad enough the planet was still storm ravaged from the work of the colonizers started but never finished... but there were those... those things... crawling around all over the place. We should be safe in here... they mostly come out at night... mostly. Machina wished he shared Tazz's confidence. But as he looked around the control room all he saw were frightened Marines struggling to keep it together. Rudo looked awful, his eyes were wide and he was shaking. Man, we're all fucked man... game over man, game over! Machina rounded on him and summoned his courage. He pointed to Tazz This little boy survived for two weeks... Rudo's eyes went crazy wide and foolish brave. Then why don't we put her in charge? Machina started to answer, but then a red light began to blink on his sensor. He looked up, an expression of horror on his face. They are coming... okay... away from the doors! They lined up... his marines. Around one leg he could feel Tazz clutching for support. The poor boy was scared. Machina gritted his teeth and raised his gun. In front of him Sid sat, helmet on head and plasma rifle aimed at the door. Technical officer Brandi was next to him, and looked with fear at the motion sensor. Ten meters... nine... Machina braced himself. Brandi continued to count. Eight... seven... Machina turned to the others. Okay... they are right outside the doors now... be ready! Silence. Nervous silence. Six... five! Machina's eyes went wide. That's impossible... Brandi continued, and the fear was running rampant now Four! Three! Three meters! Rudo was panicking now, and looked around wildly. That's impossible man! That's inside the room! Machina shook his head. Something we missed? The floors? The... Machina looked up at the ceiling. So did the others. It was silent except... except... Scratching... my god... they're in the ceiling! Guns were raised. Scratching noises, and then... Suddenly the ceiling tiles buckled. They crashed through and they were into the room with them. Hundreds. Machina screamed and began to fire. Kill them! Shoot them all! Watch out for the blood... it's acid! The mobs of monsters surged forward. Hundreds of young, blond women. The Pennys scrambled toward them, rushing, clawing and scratching. He saw one grab Tazz and devour him whole. No matter. They shot, bullets flying. Rudo screamed. Fire! Fire! Fire! One of the Pennys grabbed him, and he screamed. The Penny growled up at him. Wrestling... baaaaaaad! Machina screamed, and then... AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH! He came awake with a start. He was still in the bed, drenched with sweat. He looked over at the bedside clock. 2:26 AM He sighed, and looked around the room. It was dark, but there was a light on in the bathroom. Rudo came out of it, a frown on his face. His nightshirt was dripping wet and he wrung at it with his hands. Machina scratched his head. What in the hell happened to you? Rudo's frown deepened. Don't ask. Machina lay back and remembered the parts of his dream that had not yet fled him. How strange that he thought of his fiancée as a monster...
