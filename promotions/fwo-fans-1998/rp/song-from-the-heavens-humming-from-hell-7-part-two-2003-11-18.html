Song from the Heavens, Humming from Hell - 7 - Part Two

18 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FLASH TO PRESENT DAY Angel Dalton ran the blade across his cheek and as he did excess shaving cream clung to it. He washed it off and looked at the mirror, "What if I fail?" Angel thought long and hard about now being able to stop Steve Douglas. Douglas had become a true wrecking force and the likely hood of Angel winning seem less possible everyday. You cannot and will not fail. He could hear and feel the presence of Noah Reily speaking to him from within his body, it was comforting to know that Noah was there, helping to guide Angel toward the right path. However Angel still had doubt, "What if all that was prophesized was a joke? What if I'm actually meant to fail?" How would that make sense? What would be the point? "Instilling hope," Angel continued, "Making us believe we have a chance when the inevitable is going to happen. Steve is going to slaughter thousands of people. He's going to start a chaotic rampage that will crumble the world, and I'm the one expected to make sure to it doesn't happen. I have to stop him from letting off a bomb that will destroy the world. Me, a messiah." Yes, a martyr. "I would say that you're the martyr my friend, considering where you are." Angel attempted to crack a joke. I am where I was suppose to be. I'm doing what I'm suppose to be doing, just like you." "Your right," Angel finished the last swipe on his face, "It's destiny." Angel washed the cream off the blade and turned the sink off. Angel had a plan to catch, he was heading to South Korea, hopefully to end this on Uprising, he had to kill Douglas.
