We're In This Together, Part II

23 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He opened the door and she fell into his arms weeping. He caught her and held on tightly. She cried and he didn't know why, and he didn't care. This felt right, holding on to the one person he cared about in life. Probably the only person he would ever care about. He held her and moved towards the couch. The room was dark, but he knew it like the back of his hand. They both sat down, and she continued to cry into his shoulder. A low sigh escaped through his mouth. "I'm so sorry, Donaven," she sobbed. "I'm so sorry for everything." He looked down at her, and pulled her face up to meet with his. "I know," was his reply. "Can you ever forgive me?" Monica asked, still sobbing. "I already have, Monica," Donaven replied. She looked straight into his eyes through the dark and smiled. He half-smiled back at her through the darkness. "Thank you for saving me," he said. "I couldn't believe it at first though..." "I had to do it, Donaven," she started. "I just remembered everything we went through together as teenagers. And I saw it happening all over again. I couldn't let Monte do that to you. I just got this feeling in my stomach that it was right to stop him." He looked at the ceiling, emotions were coming over him again. After being so stoic for so long, this was new and strange to him. He knew he would have to get used to this eventually. "I'm glad you did what you did," he smiled. "I'd be in a hospital back in San Diego if it wasn't for you." She nodded in agreement, taking pride in what she did. "Donaven, can I ask you something?" She asked. "Yeah, go ahead," he replied. "Well," she stammered. "I want to stay in the fWo. I want to be by your side and help you through everything. I want to be with you. I want to be your best friend again. Will you let me?" He scratched his chin in thought. He could tell that she was looking straight at him, even through the darkness. It was kind of hard to say no to this for him. This was something he had terribly been looking for. "Monica..." he started. "I want you to be with me. I've been so lost, so alone the past year and a half. I need this, I need you in my life. And if this is how we can stay together. I guess we're in this together, now." He smiled at her and continued to hold on to her tightly, afraid he'd lose her again if he let go. They eventually fell asleep in each other's arms. The start to the beginning of their renewed friendship. A stronger bond between the two. Differences they once had would bind them together again, and neither would trade it for the world. The End.
