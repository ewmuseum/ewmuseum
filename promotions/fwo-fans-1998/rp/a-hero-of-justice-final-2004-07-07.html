A Hero of Justice(Final)

7 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Ugh," This was the first sound that Waylon made as he awoke. Taking a few moments to fully focus on his surroundings, as he had briefly forgotten where he was and what he was doing. Looking around he noticed that he was in a room and remembered now the motel. He remembered the injection of the serum and now remembered changing into what would be somewhat similar to the Hulk. "Hulk smash," He mumbled to himself and the laughed lightly. It was all a dream, he thought. That is when Waylon rubbed his eyes with his hands and soon ripped his hands away from his face and held them in front of his eyes. "Dear god, what have I done?" He said I fear as he stared at his enormous hands. Jumping up from the ground he looked to the mirror, he saw now what he had become, he was a completely different man. I mean, his eyes were the same color, his hair was a slight lighter in color but still light brown. But all other features on his body had changed and grown into a perfectly muscular tone. "Sweet Jesus, I'm a machine." He smiled as he flexed his biceps. "What a second," He said as he turned and looked at the chair in the corner, "If I am this big," He began as he walked toward the chair, "Then I can probably do this!" He lifted the chair above his head. "Ha-ha!" Waylon laughed. "This is my chance," Waylon began again, "This is my chance to get Sara, how could she turn this down? How could she possibly refuse me?" Waylon looked at the clock and the time read 7 a.m., it had been three hours since he had injected himself. Thoughts flooded his mind, images of people that had tormented him, it was payback time he thought. Time to get those assholes back for what they did to him. Waylon smacked his right fist into his left palm and began to head for the door when he felt what seemed like "butterflies" I his stomach, as he clenched his belly he noticed his legs begin to shrink back to their original size. "No," he whispered under his breath as he body began to slowly shift back. In a few moments Waylon was back to his original body and covered in a sort of sticky sweat. He had to call her, he had to call Sara. He rushed over to the phone and picked it up, dialing numbers and then placing the receiver next to his ear, "Yes...Hello, yes hi, I'm a very good friend of Ms. Sundae and I need to speak with her. Could you tell me if she if-....oh what's that? Oh I had no idea. Working for the fWo? Can you patch me through? Thank you." Waylon hung on the line for a few moments, more nervous than he had ever been, he hadn't spoke with her in years, he stopped breathing when he heard her say... "Hello?" "Hi Sara." "Waylon!?" "Yeah, how are you?" "Wonderful! It's great to hear from you!" "You too Sara, you know I always love to hear your voice." "So you say. So are you in town? I am kind of, on the road." "I know, and actually I'm in the business." "What do you mean? You can't honestly think you could wrestle do you?" "No! Not me, my friend, I have a really good friend that I am representing and he wants in." "I don't know Waylon." "Look Sara, at least meet with him would you?" "I don't know if I can-" "Please Sara, all I am asking if for a trail run." "Well...okay...what is his name?" "His name..." "Yeah, his name." "Uhm...well...it's...uh." "He doesn't have a name?" "No he does! It's uh...Pierce." Waylon continued to talk with her for two hours after that, sharing memories and arranging to meet. Little did she know that Pierce was actually Waylon...or the other way around. It was time for him to make his last stand. It was time for him to make a name for himself.
