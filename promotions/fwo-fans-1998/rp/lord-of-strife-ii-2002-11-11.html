Lord of Strife II

11 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The old man glared at Wavyrn from across the counter. "Hey, I know who you are. I saw you on T.V. You're one of those anarchist or neo-terrorists or conspiracy theorists or whatever they're calling you this week." Wavyrn did not respond, but continued to walk through the aisles of the small convenience store. "I think you'd better leave." Wavyrn's intention had been to rob this store and leave without hurting anyone, but this geezer was pushing it. "I don't know what you morons hope to accomplish, anyway. No one believes a word any of you say. Seems to me you just go around causing trouble for no real reason." In less than a second, Wavyrn moved from the liquor aisle to the counter, directly opposite the old man. As he strode, Wavyrn removed a nine millimeter revolver from a shoulder holster and took aim at the shop owner's crown. "It may seem that way to you. Who knows, maybe that's even the way it is. Anyway, I think I'm about to cause some trouble for no reason." The old man closed his eyes and clenched his teeth, remaining brave to the end. Wavyrn cocked his head, as if to get a better view, and pulled the trigger, reveling in the warmth of blood on his face as the man's face exploded in a cloud of crimson. Hearing sirens in the distance, Wavyrn stalked out of the store and was on his violent way. More words from Trent Harrison, but this time, the truth is masked with a deceit that shows me... Never mind what it shows me. Trent Harrison is a liar. Yes, I did kill that man, and yes, it was unfortunate, but the killing was not in cold blood as Downfall would have you believe. I killed Mr. O'Brien to save him. He knew too much. The Disciples would have soon been after him, especially after he had spoken with me. With the slug, his death was quick, and if he felt any pain, it was momentary. The Disciples would have caught him
