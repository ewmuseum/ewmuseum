As for me and my house

27 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It had been a long time since Deacon had been home... any home. Born and bred for this industry as part of Greensboro's CSWA, he had learned two things. One - you save your money (because the CSWA was never known for consistency, unless you count consistently behind schedule). Two - you have plenty of time at home to relax and heal. The fWo didn't allow for such niceties. Their schedule was brutal. Yes, the fWo was the organization that everyone wanted to be apart of, nearly as much as working in New York. However, of those who wanted to be there, few realized the life they would lead. Deacon had been introduced professionally to the fWo by Eli Flair, and now, Deacon was about to be introduced to a new concept on his way to the Meltdown PPV. Home. That word seemed alien to the Deacon. He stepped through the doorway. After months of nothing but hotel rooms, any place that didn't look like the last place and the place before that... and before that... would be an amazingly nice change of pace. And if Eli was known for anything, it was NOT being what everyone else was. His home echoed that sentiment. The door had been swung open to everyone, and though Deacon and Eli went back years, their schedules had never allowed them to visit one another off the road. In the CSWA, if one man was out, the other had to be on. They were too big to let both out of the circuit at the same time. "Hi, Deac." He turned to see Angel coming into the room. In Deacon's view of Eli, she was exactly what a wife of the King of Extreme would look like. He smiled at her, that fake smile that you give to someone when you're not sure of what else to do. She returned the smile. "I'll bet you want to see MJ?" The smile turned from a forced one to a genuine, *I know you're going to be thrilled when you see my baby* smile that most parents have moments before they show you the mounds of pictures found in their purse or wallet. Deacon returned the genuine smile. He'd already seen the pictures... the mounds of them kept in Eli's wallet... and gymbag... and locker in every arena across the world. Angel went to the back, an extra step in her walk. Deacon took a chair. Normally, he would wait for the offer, but this was Eli's house and his legs might fall off before they bother to see him as a guest instead of family that should just know how to make themselves at home. And then Angel returned. MJ was bigger than what Deacon expected. As the baby sat in her mom's arms, Deacon smiled. The baby didn't smile back but hid her face in her mother's neck. Deacon stared. Jesus loved the children, but Deacon and babies never seemed to mix. It was probably something about being the size of a grizzly bear that turned the little ones off. Heck, even Mickey Mouse is fine on TV, but get a young child near the 6 feet tall one at DisneyWorld and you might as well be trying to feed the baby to a giant rat. "Hi, M...J." Deacon said. "You daddy say lot about you." Even Deacon noticed his grammar was getting more difficult. Why would a baby make him nervous? And then the phone rang and Angel looked at Deacon, then the baby, then the phone. "Here, MJ. Entertain the Deacon." She said before sitting the baby on the couch beside him. She quickly ran for the phone. Deacon wanted to run quicker out the door. "Hello" Angel answered the phone. "Uh-huh. Listen, I have ... right but" She heard a crash in the living room. The phone was hung up and she came running, not sure what happened. Deacon lie on the floor faking some goofy injury to his nose and Angel, she seemed to think the whole situation hilarious. Deacon turned his face and saw Angel standing there, a quizzical look on her face. He smiled, the sort of smile you give when you've been busted doing something either silly or improper or both. And then he looked back at MJ before trying to get back up to his feet by pushing up against the couch. He grabbed MJ's hand and had her hit him on the wrist and then he hit the floor again with a perfectly taken bump from a perfectly happy 7 month old child. MJ was learning early that, yeah... wrestling really is fake. But yeah, it's fun too.
