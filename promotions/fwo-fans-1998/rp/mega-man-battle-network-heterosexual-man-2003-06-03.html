Mega Man Battle Network: Heterosexual Man

3 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Codemaster should've known something was direly wrong with this boss when the spikes resembled penises, most of the enemies looked like ballsacks, and the platforms hung from what could only be described as a scrotum bigger than even your average porn star. Finally, after narrowly escaping the things that the Codemaster would later describe as "Ball Smashers", the Codemaster reached the hallway that connected this disturbing level with its possibly even more disturbing robot master. The Codemaster sighed and stood around in the hallway, taking inventory of his weapons. "Let's see... I've got Mr. Fecalator over here..." the Codemaster said, glancing at his large handgun that he had been doing most of his damage with, "Then I've got the stupid freaking chicken launcher." he continued, glaring at Cagle Man's stupid freaking chicken launcher. The Codemaster took just one second to know what he was gonna use in this battle, and held the Fecalator firmly in his hand, while he somehow managed to put away the Cagle Launcher. Then he entered the room. The lines made an appearance once again The boss music had been replaced by "Heterosexual Man" by the Odds. Oh yeah, that's so, SO hetero. So, yeah, this flaming heterosexual stood right in front of the Codemaster, who rolled his eyes at him, "You? You're the robot master of this three-ring circus of penis jokes? Whoever's designed this place must be runnin' out of ideas already." Both the Codemaster and Heterosexual Man looked up skywards. Then they looked back toward one another. "Hey, I don't make up my quirky personality. That's Dr. Wily's job." Heterosexual Man said, before the Codemaster raised his hand. "Say, ain't you Waru?" "No, but I'm programmed to be just like him!" Heterosexual Man said, before he turned toward the wall, "So, listen up! I'm gonna beat you. You hear me? You're so going to explode into little pellets, and then I'm going to laugh about it!" "I'm over here, stupid." the Codemaster said. Heterosexual Man turned toward the Codemaster. "Oh. So, shall we fight?" Heterosexual Man asked. "We might as well." The Codemaster shot him with his Fecalator. ping It was blocked when Heterosexual Man opened up his zipper and a huge penis appeared out of his pants, blocking the bullet(yes, the penis IS bulletproof). The Codemaster glared at the penis, "You call that a penis? If I had no morals, I'd be fighting you in a cock fight right about now." Heterosexual Man whacked the Codemaster with his penis, sending him flying into the walls, bouncing back from the walls, hitting the OTHER walls, and landing right where he originally was. The Codemaster dizzilly got back to his feet, "Okay, no more mocking the giant-penis-having idiot." The Codemaster fired three more rounds of the Fecalator. Or at least, he would have, had three clicking sounds not been heard. The Codemaster frantically checked his magazine, and found it empty. "Aw, hell." WHACK! Well, okay, that was probably NOT the word to use in a "Codemaster versus giant penis-having robot" roleplay, but the point is that the Codemaster went flying about the room again. The Codemaster slowly returned to his feet, and glared at Heterosexual Man. "You do that one more time and I'm gonna have to get all medieval on your ass." Heterosexual Man did it again. WHACK! However, in mid-air, the Codemaster whipped out his only other weapon, which was, of course, the Cagle Launcher, and he quickly fired a round, which went clucking straight for the zipper of Heterosexual Man's pants. What happened next can be left to your imagination, but let's just say that it was a horrible, horrible way to castrate someone. Naturally, without his manhood, Heterosexual Man couldn't be called Heterosexual Man any more, and this logic caused Heterosexual Man to short circuit and explode into little pellets. His penis followed shortly thereafter. Words appeared at the top of the screen. "GET EQUIPPED WITH PENIS MIGHTIER." The Codemaster glared at these words, "Man, would you STOP with the penis jokes already? I've had just about enough of them." He left.
