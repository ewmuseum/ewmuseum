Drug Diaries; One.

8 April 2009

Drug Diaries Selected entries dated between 2001 and 2004.. March 4th 2002 Sitting at my friend's house, shivering, sweating profusely, and just generally feeling shitty all around, waiting for that oh-so-important call to come in. The phone rings, and my heart skips a beat. My realisation that the pain will soon be gone is replaced by more desperation as I hear him say "not yet, I'm still waiting for him to call". It seems like hours we sit and wait, but really it's only 20 minutes. Finally, he gets the call. We're off to his hook-up to score. As we wait on the street corner for him, I wonder why it always takes him so long to come out of his house if he knows we're sitting there waiting. Finally he comes out, exchanges the bag for cash, and we're off. We hit the highway, and while I drive in a state of dope-sickness and delayed euphoria my friend/dealer rigs up and blasts his sickness away. The ride home seems like it takes forever as every light turns red, prolonging this hell that is dope-sickness. We finally get to his house, and I'm relieved, as I know the worst is over. My friend chops up the rock, turning it to a fine powder, while I wonder why he can't just cut my bag off real quick, as he knows how I'm feeling. He's finally got it crushed, and he cuts off approximately 100mg of fine brown heroin, pushing it onto a folded piece of magazine paper. I immediately empty this paper into the cap of my rig, and apply water. I stir it vigorously, and it dissolves without a problem. A small piece of cotton is dropped into the mixture, and I draw up the iced tea-looking mixture into my syringe. I yank my belt off my hips and wrap it around my arm, run my finger over the vein I plan to use, say a quick prayer that I'll hit the vein on the first try, and plunge the needle into my skin. I feel a tiny pop as the needle breaks the skin, and another pop signifying I've broken into the vein. I pull back on the plunger to be sure, and a beautiful plume of red fills the lower half of the barrel. What a sight, but I don't have time to stare in awe at it's beauty, as I have a mission. I push the plunger in, steadily forcing the drug into my bloodstream. It's done, and I pull the belt off my arm and pull the needle out. I submerge my needle in water as I draw it up to clean the blood out of my rig when a rush of warmth hits me. I feel my muscles relax, almost like the feeling of sitting down after working hard all day. No longer feeling ill, I sit down in a chair and appreciate the warmth and contentment that has evaded me thusfar today. "What a great day", I think, oblivious now to the hell I was going through just 5 minutes before.
