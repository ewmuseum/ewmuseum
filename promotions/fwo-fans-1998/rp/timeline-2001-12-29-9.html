12/29/2001 5:00AM With a start, the Strangler jolted awake in his bed... swimming in a sea of sweat as a shudder went through him. Of course it had been a dream, and even one that he had experienced countless times before, but after ten plus years it still gave him a system shock every time. A few years of sleep therapy had gotten him past the "why is this happening?" stage of the dreams... the doctor said it was guilt, and confusion... no one thinks that they will lose a parent that early in life. But there was more to it than that, according to this so-called expert. The criticisms... those were what he believed the people to be thinking as they came to him with encouraging words and comfort. There were other angles to the whole dream, but he had long since forgotten them. It all seemed like rubbish anyway. Needless to say, the Strangler rarely slept... and if he did, it was never for more than a few hours a night. He might catch an hour here, and hour there...sometimes he would go 72 hours without sleeping a wink. It was uncommon, but not unheard of. After all, Napoleon conquered Europe on 3 hours of sleep a night. The Strangler grimaced and rubbed at his stomach as he got up out of bed. He couldn't tell if it was the stress or the appetizers from earlier... but whatever it was had flared up a serious case of heartburn. After padding his way into the bathroom, and digging through the few medical supplies his travel kit contained, he had his choices limited to 4 Tylenol, a couple of band aids, and a bottle of flex-all. None of those would help his stomach one bit. After sighing, and drinking a little water in the hopes that it might help a bit, the Strangler caught a glance of his face in the mirror. He hadn't noticed how pale he had been getting. It wasn't an unhealthy pale, but he could see the signs of stress showing around his eyes and forehead. Much of it was from working the road week in and out (when was the last time he had taken a vacation?)...Some of it was probably from the recent slew of problems in and out of the ring in the fWo... and a little bit of it probably stemmed from his personal life, or lack thereof. He wasn't getting old, that was for sure. He was barely 30 years old. But in a business where your value could change in one night, he most likely wasn't getting any younger in the eyes of management. He was no longer a fresh rookie face in the crowd, and wasn't anywhere near ""wily veteran" status yet. He knew he was dangerous in that ring, and knew he still had a lot of championship gold left in his future... one way or another. This was the prime of his career... his best years... were right now. "Yep" the Strangler thought to himself as he headed back to bed, propping himself up to try to ease the heartburn. "It doesn't get any better than this..."
