Home For Now - II

23 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Eugene jumped back as the water became scalding. He'd always hated getting out of showers. If you turn the hot off first, it's freezing. If you turn the cold off first, you get first degree burns. The perfect temperature equilibrium was there for the shower, but that was it. "Everything is like that," he thought. Cold when you're getting in, hot when you're getting out, but for eight pristine minutes, the temperature was just right. Beleaguered by discomfort, the shower was an oasis. Then again, arena showers were never that great to begin with, and especially in Peru. Tired of relating life to an especially comforting shower, Eugene grabbed a towel and wrapped it around his waist, then headed for the door. He knew he only had a couple hours to get ready for Warfare, and he certainly didn't want to be late to the meeting. God knows he'd had enough trouble with management as of late. As Eugene pulled the door open, he nearly bumped into Amy Campbell. She stood with a towel hanging from just above her chest to far above her knees, ready to take a shower herself. For a very brief moment, Eugene smiled at Amy, but a scowl soon marred his face. "Watch where I'm going, sweet heart." Amy was utterly shocked by the exclamation, and nothing escaped her lips for a few seconds. "We're quick today, aren't we?" "You'd better be joking..." Amy snuffed, arms folded as she looked up to him with a furrowed brow, apparently disgusted. "You seriously don't ever leave the little character fantasy land you've created, do you? I wanna know what the hell you thought you were doing in the ring last week...In case you hadn't noticed, I'd kinda been shot a few weeks before and you were there doing the usual, 'Be rougher on the chick than you are on the guys, try to put her in her place' bullshit." Taking a pensive instant to plan his response, he answered, "I wasn't trying to put you anywhere, Amy. I got caught up in the moment, a bit. I'm sorry. But you didn't have to change the end of the match, either. Great display of professionalism, there." Amy turned a shade of pink. "Great display of professionalism, huh? Apparently I'm the only professional wrestler involved here, because it sure looked to me like you haven't got the first good God-damned clue how to do your job, buddy." Eugene tightened the towel around his waist. "Now hold on, sister. What the fuck are you talking about? I may have hit a few ill-placed kicks, but I've got concerns other than your out-of-work injuries." Again, Eugene contemplated whether he should speak more, but decided on the affirmative. "I've got a struggling relationship, management woes, and a show to put on out there." Amy had a response ready, but Eugene continued speaking, venting all of his frustrations. "I will not sacrifice the product to make you comfortable. That's not how I work. that's not how anyone works. Were it me who was shot, which was a reckless, dumbass thing to do in the first place, I would take the kicks and do the job." "I'm sorry...You have a struggling relationship? Any idea what kind of situation I'm in? Let's see...BL was stalked, then the crazy bitch SHOT me, then we got together, now I want to kill his not-quite-yet-ex-wife.............Hmm. If you're gonna play that game, I will too; I just changed the ending to get some realism, y'know...'Spice up the show' a bit. Hey, it got an emotional reaction from ya, didn't it?" She smirked with a cocky air about her that was unusual, but not so much when she was angry. ...And then his last comment sunk in. "...What kind of fucking moron are you? This whole macho act doesn't go far with me...Just because you wouldn't have the balls to go out and protect and sacrifice for someone like I did doesn't mean shit. And don't talk about the way you 'work', because you simply don't. You're the reckless one here...you're making up excuses because YOU fucked up and hit some sloppy moves to mess with me." Eugene's expression changed yet again for a fleeting second, before his grimace returned. "What kind of moron am I? I am the kind of moron that knows when his opponent is not selling, but, instead, being a tempermental little girl!" After seeing Amy's fury grow proportionately to the hue of her complexion, he toned the aggression down a bit, throwing his hands up in defense, then barely catching his towel. "Okay, well, there's no fucking end to this, is there? I try to get along with you, I try to apologize, but you'll have none of it. I'm so sorry your junior-high romance didn't quite turn out the way the stories say. We've all been there. Fuck, I am there." "My 'junior high romance' is nothing I can't handle, but I had to be shot to get here and fuck me if that's not some shit to go through. I wasn't being a tempermental 'little girl' out there, I was doing my damned job and suddenly got slammed with your errors...That shit doesn't fly at all." "I made no mistakes in that ring, sister. If you wanna talk about flying shit, think back to your missle dropkick." Eugene smiled, pleased with his quip. Amy didn't even let herself dignify it with a verbal response. Instead, she balled a fist and let it fly...Right into his lower lip. With a bit of blood flowing, she cracked him again- this time in the jaw. "By the way...If you're gonna 'force' someone to sell something, THAT is how you get the job done." As Amy reached back for a third one, Eugene lowered one hand to grab his rapidly descending towel, then caught her fist with the other one. He searched for words as Silver Fox came into his peripheral vision. "I need to talk to you two." God knows he'd had enough trouble with management as of late.
