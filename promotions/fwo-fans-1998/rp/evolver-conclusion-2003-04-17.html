EVOLVER_conclusion

17 April 2003

He hung up the phone. "Mother fucker!" Deja vu. It was happy this time. Eugene's talk with Silver Fox was productive. In an hour, he'd start filming a live feed over to England with the help of some friends at ABC. Eugene hadn't tried blackmail before, and was altogether surprised that it worked exactly as the movies portrayed it. Smooth, easy, and painless. With the knowledge he possessed, Eugene would be allowed to wrestle. With the knowledge that he had, Eugene would win. In a bold move, he planned to tell the world about fWo's fate even after agreeing not to. He will have already faxed over a signature on the match contract, and it would be too late to do anything about it. The fWo would be ruined, Aimz would be ruined, and nobody would underestimate Eugene Marder again. The plan was cunning, conniving and brilliant. The plan was a departure. Eugene Marder had changed. He was changing. He was evolving. This catalyst that plunged him into a maddening frenzy of hate, it was Aimz. And he only had her to thank for what he had become. He was invincible. i am powerful. i am capable. i will triumph. As he prepared himself mentally for his actions, he winced in pain. The muscles were not getting any better. The ring would not be any more forgiving. But the adrenaline would get him through. He would win, he would destroy her. She would be gone from his life. but, no... If she would be gone, so would the change. she has done so much. she has done nothing. He had a choice to make. What would he do to Amy Campbell? What would become of his evolver?
