Previously: Under the ruins. Part I Breathing in the heavy dust as they gulped for air Vic Creid and Brandon Roche simply stared at each, both waiting for the other to make the next move. Brandon's ears picked up the slow creak of straining supports again; they didn't have much time before the whole place came crashing down on them. It was hard to imagine where they were and how they got here. The course of events over the last day was too surreal. Missed flights, heightened security, everyone's nerves on edge. The fate of the world and whether we would be plunged in to war was heavy in the air. Brandon remembered back to they way Amy Campbell acted for most of the morning. She pranced back and forth in the hotel room like a caged animal constantly worried. She could tell something was not right. The energy that surrounded everything seemed... off. That was the best she could put it. It bothered her enough to make more than one phone call home to check on little Stephen and had even convinced him that a call home every hour or so to speak to his two little angels was a good plan. Now less than 12 hours later and Brandon Roche found himself buried alive with arguably his worst enemy in Victor Creid Jr. The past should be just that, the past. To these two though no time frame would ever separate the hatred they shared for each other. Brandon started the business when Creed has just become a breakout star in his own right. The booker's of the EWA, that bastard Russell Edwards decided to put the rookie straight in to a program with Harley and Vic Creed. The Brotherhood. On camera it was electric. Backstage was another story though as Harley disrespected the business and used it for his own personal gains and basically Creid was simply an overbearing son of a bitch.
