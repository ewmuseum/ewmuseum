Fate: Honors

30 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kevin walked out of the office, a smile painted on his face. He held an envelope in his hand, and entered the OCW training room. The other wrestlers all looked at him, confused. They couldn't imagine why he was smiling. "I'm going to the big leagues guys!" Kevin yelled. The crowd boomed with joy and congratulations. The big man, Derrick, came over and shook Kevin's hand. "Damn, if I had know they was gon' give you a spot, I'duh piledrived yer head a little harder!" he joked. The joke got a laugh out of all in the room, and they all began to shout "DY-NA-MO!" in honor of Kevin's wrestling name. He beamed with joy, and then the chants of "SPEECH! SPEECH!" broke out. "No... I don't... Okay fine." Kevin said, putting humility aside to bask in his moment. He smiled at them all, and cleared his throat. He then squinted his eyes, and raised an eyebrow as best he could, getting into character. The guys began to snicker, but they stopped as he spoke. "You know, a lot of times life throws you for a loop. Some bastard piledrives your head one minute, the next you get him down on the ground for the three count." he looked at Derrick, who gritted his teeth, pretending to be angry. Kevin continued, "But life... Life won't throw me for any more loops. Hell no. From here on out I'm my own man. I pick where I want to go. I do what I want to do. I choose my destiny." "That's right... I choose what is going to happen. And today? Today I choose a celebration. A celebration of all the guys who work their ass off, and who get thrown for a loop. The guys who live, breathe, and die wrestling. Who make this their life. I'm going to France to represent those guys. And nobody's... NOBODY'S... gonna stop me now!" With that the entire locker room became an uproar of cheers. The "DY-NA-MO" chant started again, and they hosted up Kevin onto their shoulders. He felt like a champion. For the first time in his life, he felt things were right. And... It felt good. Previous Fate: Introduction Fate: Choices Fate: Meetings
