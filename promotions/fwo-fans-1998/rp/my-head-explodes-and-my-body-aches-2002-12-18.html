My head explodes and my body aches

18 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The sunlight peeked through the blinds, and found Eli Flair's eyes. He tried to ignore it for a few seconds before rolling over in bed. It took him another few seconds to realize he was alone in the bed. He sat up suddenly, looking around the room. "G'mornin, sexy." He looked toward the window, and saw Angel at the hotel room's table with several dozen scraps of paper sprawled all over. "Mornin'," he replied, "What time is it?" "Almost noon," laughed Angel, "I didn't think you'd be up before three, considering how late we were out last night." Eli stretched, groaning at sore, straining muscles. "You got to do all the driving while me and Trips and Rudy all got shitfaced. How the hell are you so awake?" She smiled. "Inspiration." Eli rolled out of bed, grabbing a plain black T-shirt from the floor to go along with his sleeping gear of cutoff sweatpants as he headed toward Angel and her scraps of paper. He kissed her on top of her head and kneeled down, kissing her pregnant stomach as well, before turning his attention to her writing. "Chained to a notion of the way things used to be," read Eli, noting Angel's pale features turning a shade of red, "is it such a hard notion that you've been beaten and embarrassed by me?" "Okay, okay," said Angel, pulling the pages away, "so it's not exactly the best thing I've ever written, but I can only write what I feel." Eli leaned down and hugged his girlfriend, "It's perfect, Angel." She sipped her bottle of water, moving her eyes slightly toward him. "Really?" "Of course," continued Eli, "it'll sound a lot better when Nine soups up the synth--" She turned around with mock rage in her eyes. "--and of course, Mickey's axe will drown out the worst of the words." "Oh you think you're funny," taunted Angel, punching him playfully in the arm, "but you're not, y'know." She started to back him down with her physical assault. Eli found himself unable to fight back, mainly because he was so amused by this five- foot- nothing pregnant goth girl trying to beat him up, and he couldn't stop laughing. She realized it too, at least, when he caught her wrists and tickled her sides. As he pulled her in closer to continue the torture, she defended herself by exploiting Eli's one weakness. "That's.... SO unfair!" She nibbled on his neck. This little round of playtime began to wind down, with both participants lying in bed, spooning, with Eli behind her and his arms wrapped around her. "It's gonna be just another few weeks," mused Angel, "You scared?" "About the baby? Naaaah... more worried about his extended family." She turned her head slightly, "HER extended family? Who, Ivy and Sean? They'll be fine.... long as our daughter doesn't become a cocky, self- righteous, stubborn bitch from hell." "He? Her?" Eli laughed, "We should wait until the kid is a year old... an hour old before we label 'em, right? Besides, a cocky, self- righteous stubborn bitch from hell patterned after Ivy and Sean certainly isn't the worst thing that could happen." "What's worse?" "He might decide to be a singer... or worse, a wrestler." Angel turned back toward the wall, hugging his arms around her body more tightly. "Or she might decide to pick a silver- and- purple, bouncy little ball of energy as her role model. Then not only would neither of us ever get any sleep, but she'd run off with Ivan Stanislav the second." Eli cracked his neck, furrowing his brow in thought. "Whoever said either of us get any sleep NOW?" Angel smiled as she felt Eli snuggle up next to her. She reached out and grabbed the clock radio next to the bed and set the alarm. "What're you doing with that?" he asked. "Setting this," she replied, "I can tell we're gonna need something to catch our attention when you need to get to the arena." "Oh really?" he asked, fake indolence in his voice, "You're so sure? Well, I might not be in the mood." Angel turned her head back toward him. "Oh, you're in the mood, Extreme boy... or do I need to leave a bitemark on your neck?" She looks like she's trying to bite him, but at the last moment smiled and licked his lips. "I love you." He smiled back at her. "I love you too, even if you're a crazy pregnant woman." They kiss. Awwwwwh, how cute.
