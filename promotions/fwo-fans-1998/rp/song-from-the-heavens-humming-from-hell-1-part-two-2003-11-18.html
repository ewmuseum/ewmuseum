Song from the Heavens, Humming from Hell - 1 - Part Two

18 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I shall tell you a great secret my friend. Do not wait for the last judgment, it takes place everyday. -Albert Camus If only there were evil people somewhere insidiously committing evil deeds, and it were necessary only to separate them from the rest of us and destroy them. But the line dividing good and evil cuts through the heart of every human being, and who is willing to destroy his own heart? -Alexander Solzhenitsyn "Angel?" "Angel?" "Angel?" Angel Dalton stood on what seemed to be a small white platform that rested on a bed of water, where the water ended and where it began Angel could not see, the sky and the air seemed to be a faint blue and a thick heavy mist surrounded him. Angel wore nothing, he had no clothing on and his body was stripped of hair. He was neither cold nor hot and all that he could smell was a comforting feel of freshness in the air. Angel was not scared, he looked around to see all that his eyes could see, "Where am I?" Angel heard almost a giggle like laughter erupt from all around him. A sudden glow of blue from the left caught his eye as Angel looked sharply in it's direction. He saw what seemed to be a small person wrapped in blue cloths and a cloth draped over his face only so that his blue eyes stared at Angel, the person was seated upon what seemed to be a platform as well that rested on the water. Although Angel could not see his face he felt no fear from the little creature and it seemed to smile up at him. The creature's arm's were crossed and he almost bounced as he spoke. "You are no where and everywhere at the same time." A green light caught Angel's eye from the right of him and Angel looked over in it's direction, there sat another creature, this time draped in green cloth and seemingly smiling as well. This one raised out his right hand and held up what would be an index finger. The skin on the hand was gray in color and his fingernail came to a long sharpened point. Dirt seemed to caked in the space between the nail and the finger. "You are up and certainly down as well." The green creature pointed down with his hand and as he did a final red light shown upon Angel and a creature draped in red cloth was seated in front of him. This creature did not seem to smile but almost beamed of wisdom. As he spoke his voice was the deepest of all three creatures. "Welcome Angel Dalton, to limbo. The place in which all that matters, doesn't. Where nothing is concrete and all thought and rational cease to be. There is nothing but everything, all is contrast and everything is the same. The place where even good and evil do not lie." "This is a dream," Angel spoke suddenly. The creature in the green was first to respond somewhat startling Angel. "Not a dream, however you are dreaming." Angel looked to the small creature, "Why am I here?" "You already know why." Angel looked back to the creature in blue, "Who are you?" "We..." "Are..." "Clockwatchers." Angel was now surprised, "Clockwatches?" The creature in the red now took the floor as the other two stared intensely at Angel Dalton. "We are the Clockwatchers. We watch and wait for this moment to come, helping only by supplying free choice to all of those involved. This moment has been repeated several thousand times throughout the ages since the dawning of human kind. It is a constant battle that has been ongoing for all eternity, the war between life and death." The blue creature continued. "You Angel represent life. Douglas is death. Both of you have been chosen so that all angles of life and death can fully be displayed. So that when the right to be given choice has commenced, you chose whether or not you want life or death. You Angel have to see all sides of life to determine whether or not it should exist. If not, life will stop and all that you know of this world will end. If yes, then life will continue without ever knowing it's existence had been tested." The green creature cut in. "When life was created upon this planet it was decided that in order for it to thrive and build into what it will finally be, it must be tested. That every aspect of life must be brought to test. And that every aspect of death must be brought to test. In the end, the strong would survive. This has been tested multiple times and you are not the first to stand before us Angel, however you will be the last." Angel stepped back onto what was the water, however his foot did not sink into the water but it seemed to be held lightly above it. Angel moved back onto the platform, "I can't do it." "Then you won't." "Or you will." "Whatever you choose, will decide your fate, and the world's." "So then," Angel began, "In theory all others that stood before you, chose life." "And death." Angel looked to the green creature, "I don't understand." "You are not suppose to. Instead know this, you will never see us again, however we are watching you. You have the choice to save all of humanity, or let it fade. Chose wisely in every step that you make, knowing that with any step it could mean consequences that will lead to vast destruction. You have been chosen Angel, chosen to make a choice." The blue creature seemed to bounce again as he spoke. "Now you can stop dreaming." *** Angel awoke with the sound of sirens behind him and he was standing in the alleyway in which he had escaped through after Douglas fled the scene.
