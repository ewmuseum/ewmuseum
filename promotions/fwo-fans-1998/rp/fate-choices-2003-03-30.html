Fate: Choices

30 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SLAM. His head hit the mat hard. "You okay man?" a large black man took Kevin's hand and pulled him up. "Yeah, I'm cool. Watch that piledriver though Derrick." he replied. Kevin Masters stood in the center of the ring, a little dazed, holding his head. His mind was in so many places. It had been a month since he had arrived in Orlando, working at the fWo's developmental organization the OCW. It was a Tuesday, and his training was going well, but he wanted more. He needed more than this, that was for sure. He was tired of this life. Eating hot pockets and ramen noodles cooked in a shitty microwave. Driving from small show to small show. It was all so repetitive now. His world wasn't much; not right then anyway. He had spoken to his ex, Jen Marshalls, a few days before. She sounded genuinely happy. Genuinely glad to have moved on with her live. He remembered their last conversation. "So what, you're just going to do it?" "Jen... I have to. I mean he's my friend, and the company needs it. It's the only chance the aWc has to survive." "Kevin, this is INSANE. You can't just fake your death. That's ridiculous. You're choosing the promotion over your own life." "I know, but they need it. Jon needs it... It's all he has left. All he has left of his father. You have to understand..." "No I don't! Kevin, just because it is all he has left of his father, doesn't mean it's up to you to help live out their relationship." "But he needs me Jen. The whole company does, think of all those people." "I need you Kevin. You're going to choose these people, half you don't even know, over yourself. Over us. I can't let you do that, or I can't be with you when you do." "Jen... I love you." "I know, and that's why I don't want you to do this. I love you too..." "Jen..." It was vivid, easily pictured in his mind. He remembered the look on her face. The tears in her eyes. The smell of her perfume. The sound of the door slamming. And the sound of sobs drifting down the hallway. It had been ten months since then, and Jen was happy now. She had found someone else, and had a good life. He missed her though. He probably would forever. "Kev!" a voice screamed from across the room. Kevin looked over; it was his boss in the office. His stomach turned at the thought of what was going on. He had heard him say those same words before to other guys, and they hadn't come back the next day. "What? Oh, yeah, what's up sir?" Kevin said, trying to sound enthusiastic. "Come into my office, we need to talk." his boss replied. With that Kevin looked at the others standing around the ring, and rolled under the bottom rope. They threw him knowing looks of worry, as if to say "It will be okay man, there's other promotions." But inside Kevin knew that wasn't true. This was his shot. What would happen in this office would determine his future. And that made him feel ill. Previous Fate: Introduction
