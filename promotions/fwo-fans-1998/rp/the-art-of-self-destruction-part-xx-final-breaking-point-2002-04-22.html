The Art of Self Destruction, Part XX (Final Breaking Point)

22 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You beat a woman tonight. That doesn't make you good. That doesn't make you acceptable, or decent, or humane. You're not supposed to strike another woman. And it was the second time you've done it this weekend. i was doing as i was told You weren't listening to me. i just did what i heard i'm not asking questions anymore You're following something blindly now? I know where you're going. And I know where you'll end up after Saturday night. And if you don't listen to everything I tell you to do and when you're supposed to do it; the consequences of next week will be much worse than you could ever imagine. If you think this is Hell right now, you're wrong. This is like Dante's first circle. You've just past through Purgatory, and if you continue to disobey me; you will soon be suffering with the likes of Cassius, Brutus and Judas. Being eaten eternally by the greatest daemon of all: Lucifer himself. i'm sorry As you should be. Now, listen carefully. There's not much time left tonight. And He is waiting outside for you to go and get him. He's already quite mad after what you did this weekend. And he wants your head on a stake. And that's not going to happen. Do you see that piece of wood over there? yes Go grab it. You're going to bring it near the back entrance of the arena, where He is waiting. And when he comes back in looking for you; he won't know what hit him. You're going to be killing him slowly.... painfully... Just as I'm killing you. I will always control you.
