My Modus Operandi - Part 5

6 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ugh Christ, someone turned out the lights. My body feels numb and there is ringing in my ears. I must be on the ground ‘cause I feel the cold concrete on my back. I have no idea how long I’ve been going at this but it feels like eternity. I completely underestimated our resident bar bum, completely. My body feels like it got ran over by a locomotive. This guy had grip in his hands like a vice. So many times he hand those hands around my neck and had me gasping for air. His punches felt like he was hitting me with twenty five pound weights in them. But I know he’s feeling the effects of my shots. I think that’s him on the ground next to me but I’m not sure. Nothing is in focus yet; need to wait a few seconds longer. There were a few times where I had him in a submission but couldn’t finish; the bastard was strong. I’m so tired. I think I’ll just lie here awhile longer. A few innocent bystanders that were watching our fight are likely crying and wondering where the hell they are. This fight was violent and sometimes in war you have collateral damage. It wasn’t my fault that he ducked a punch from me and joe blow was standing right behind him; his face welcoming my fist. Wrong place at the wrong time buddy, sorry. So much blood on the floor; Clinton is going to have a bitch of a time cleaning that up. C’mon Steve deep breath and get your composure. UGH FUCK! Yeah, that’s a broken rip poking into my lung. I knew I felt a pop when he kicked me there. Adrenalin is wearing off and jesus christ can I feel the pain. Which I guess is a good thing eh? Means that I’m still alive. The ringing as stopped and I can hear the crowd begging for the two of us to continue “Get up, get up!” is what they’re chanting. Do I want to though? So tired. A nap sounds so good right about now. There’s my vision comin’ back to me. My stomach feels nauseas which likely means have a concussion. He’s got to be done as well. Where is this prick? God damnit! He’s actually trying to get up. Why do you have to ruin my comfort here on the cold concrete? Son of a bitch. Alright Steve, time to get up. If you die here against this guy, you die. But at least you die fighting. Never thought it would be in a basement of some pub, but oh well. I can’t choose where death decides to come take me and that is the only way I will let this bar bum beat me. Getting to my knees is one hell of a struggle. I can feel my broken rib hitting my lung; I have to take short breaths. Oh look at that, nice thick blood pouring out of my mouth. Fuck, I hope I don’t have internal bleeding. I feel like a little kid trying to walk for the first time. I think I’ll take a minute here on my knees; just a minute. How’s he doing? One knee. Ok, ok. Let’s go right leg, extend out and put that foot on the ground. Good, looks like he’s going to fall. I’ll win, as expected. Fall you prick! Whoa, my vision is coming and going and ringing is back in my ears. Let’s take another quick peek, maybe he’s decided to fall. Of course not, this asshole is going for that second foot and try to stand up. Alright, lets go left foot…plant on the floor. ARGH! Mother fucker, my god damn ribs feel like knives poking my insides. Ok, you’re on your feet Steve…now just extend your back up right and watch him crumble. He’ll never make it up; I beat him too badly. He must have several broken bones, there’s no way he’ll get fully back up. Just a matter of time before he throws in the white flag. You just needs to stand here for two, maybe three seconds and then it’s over. You’ll be the winner. I think I’m falling asleep standing. Where’d he go? It’s too dark in here. Let me shake my head, that usually cleans out the fuzziness. Show me good things eyes, show me the bar bum back on his ass. Fuckin’ eyes! You betray me. I didn’t ask to see him standing up right with his fists barely lifted up to his face. My mouth is filled with red tomato juice that I spit out in front of me while clutching my ribs. "I'd ask if you wanted to continue but judging by that popping sound your jaw just made, I think it's going to be difficult for you to answer." Aw crap. He's starting to disappear on me. Getting punched right now will certainly suck. Great, total darkness is all I see in front of me now. The prick actually muttered some words. "If you're still breathing, I'm still fighting." I have to respond. "I'm still breathing and still st-". Ah, the cool concrete floor again. Wait, what happened? I didn’t feel him hit me. Did I die? What the hell is going on? Hello? HELLO!? Shit.
