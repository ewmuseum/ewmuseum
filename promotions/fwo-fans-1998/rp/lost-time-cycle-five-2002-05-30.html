Lost Time - Cycle Five

30 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"We can't keep doing this, y'know." "Doing what?" "Having sex... every time we have a problem we start to make out, and end up in bed. Nothing is solved, we just forget about it." Dinner ended two hours ago. The plates are still on the table. The pots and pans are still on the stove. The two people who were eating are now naked, lying in each other's arms in Triple X's bed. Trip is lying on his back, with an arm around Ivy's shoulders. Ivy is resting her head on Trip's chest, an arm draped over him. Her left leg is jutting out from underneath the blankets, revealing her "Team EXTREME" logo tattoo. It's the only tattoo she and Eli Flair have in common. It's the only tattoo that Eli Flair has on his lower body. When he designed it five years ago - a simple "T" and "X" overayed on each other, he called it a "brand." He said it was a sign - that he was outside the mainstream and would never be a part of what was "popular." Four years later, Ivy got it, herself. In the interim, she'd seen it on the left calf of innumerable kids at various shows. Triple X has talked about how much he liked it and was thinking about getting it. Angel, even, has been talking about it. Ivy wonders, briefly, if any of them, save herself and Eli, will ever know what it stands for. The thought of keeping this from Triple X... from her Sean... fills Ivy with sadness. "I don't know what else you want from me, Ivy," replied Trip, "for that matter, I don't know what else I can give you." He strokes her hair, and she hugs him more tightly. "You know, you asked me something last week that I never really gave you an answer for." "What's that?" "Are you kidding me, Sean?" asked Ivy, leaning up, looking into his eyes, "You asked me to marry you. Please tell me you were trying to get me to stay after catching you with Linda." Trip considers this. "I wasn't." "You... weren't?" "I was serious, Ivy," continued Trip, "I asked you to marry me because I want to spend the rest of my life with you." He pauses, and Ivy rests her head back on his chest, running her fingertips up and down his side. "Because I love you," finished Trip. Ivy didn't look up. "I love you too," she replied. "Do you?" "I know that now. I don't know why I couldn't admit it, or why I had so much trouble with the simple word of love itself... but I love you, Sean." Don't do it. "And I've been thinking about it, too." It was a case of a good idea foiled by lousy timing. "About what you asked me, that is." Anything she says now will come from the wrong place, even if it's what she wants. Don't let her answer! "I think I've decided something." Ivy looks at him again. Before she can say another word, Trip puts his hand to her lips. "Shhhhh," he said, "Don't say another word." She arches her eyebrows in questioning. "I meant what I said when I asked you," continued Trip, "but it was a case of incredibly bad timing." "I wanted to tell you--" "So you think about this," interrupted Trip, "You take as much time as you need to decide what you want to do. I'll be here when you make your well- thought- out decision... and I know now... so will you." He kisses her on the forehead and gets up out of bed, headed toward the kitchen. Ivy wraps herself up in his blanket, smelling his scent on them. She was going to accept his proposal. When he cut her off, all he did, essentially, was tell her, "Don't say yes now. Say yes a year from now." Ivy should be angry that he cut her off like that. She should be furious that he made her feel so cheap last week when she caught him with Linda Johns, and that he made her emotions flip- flop the way he did when he proposed, and that he invalidated all of it twenty seconds ago when he told her it didn't matter. But she isn't. "I love you, Sean," she said... unsure of whether or not he heard her. Did he? Who can say. But he knows it.
