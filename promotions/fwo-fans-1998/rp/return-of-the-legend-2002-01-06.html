Return Of The Legend.

6 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Everybody knows the Tokyo Dome. It is a landmark of the proud Japanese city, and the home of countless spectacular sporting events. Legends from all types of athletic competition have made appearances there. This, everybody knows. One thing about the Tokyo Dome everybody does not know, however, it that is houses the Japanese Professional Baseball Hall of Fame and Museum. Inside here is where we find none other than Scott Slugger, a legend who has been here before and undoubtedly will be again. Slugger is dressed in street clothes - jeans, leather jacket, Trent Dilfer Seattle Seahawks jersey. He is admiring something on the wall. A slight turn of the camera reveals it to be a baseball bat. Though we cannot, of course, read the Japanese lettering that identifies the bat, it is clear that this is a precious and valuable piece of memorabilia. Slugger turns to the camera, smiles, and speaks. "Heh. It really has been a while. I've forgotten what it's like to go everywhere with the camera right there on you. One thing the WGA hasn't forgotten, though - what it's like to be in that ring. Yeah, it's been a long time. The WGA hasn't wrestled since September 2000, but hey, no big - even with a year and a half of ring rust, I'm still the standard when it comes to the fWo. You know, as long as it's been since I wrestled, it's been even longer since I've been in Tokyo, in the Tokyo Dome. Janaury twenty-second, nineteen ninety-nine. I fought the Flying Frenchie for the Internet Title and I broke my ankle. The docs, they said I'd never wrestle again. Guess I proved them wrong, didn't I? The one thing I haven't forgotten about the Tokyo Dome is this right here - the Japanese Baseball Hall of Fame. It's only fitting - after all, Halls of Fame are where you find legends. I am, after all, a baseball legend... a wrestling legend... and a legend in general. Do you see this bat right here on the wall? This bat was used by Saduharu Oh to hit his seven hundred and fifty-sixth home run - the all time world record. I don't think you have to stretch your brain to see how this connects to the WGA. For the dimmer of you out there - like Vic Creed, for example - I'll explain it. Saduharu Oh is the all-time home run leader. I am the all-time fWo World Championship leader. I've won more World Titles in this fed than half the roster has won titles, period. In other words, just consider me the Saduharu Oh of professional wrestling. And I'm back, baby. Back in black, back with a vengeance, back however you want to put it - and I'm still the best." Slugger turns to the camera and bids it follow. It does. Slugger comes to another portion of the HoF. Mounted on the wall is a jersey, number 51. Above the number, "Ichiro". "Yeah, you're right everybody - no way I'm going through my first promo back without mentioning Ichiro. But don't worry, it's not gratuitous - I've got a reason. Ruben Ross. Ruben, you're a hell of a guy. Of all the people that have passed through the fWo since I left, I'd say I respect you the most. You've got talent, you've got charisma, you've got it all going for you. In fact, you're a bit like Ichiro. I mean, think about it. You've got the spectacular skills. You've got the cool facial hair. You've got the rabid following. Hell, for all I know, you might even have the magazine offering a million bucks for a naked pic of you. And right now, you're at the top of what you do. But as much as it pains me to say it - and it pains me a lot - even Ichiro didn't end up winning it all. Yet. Even Ichiro came up against a force he couldn't overcome. Now, I'm not going to name that force because I suppose I'm comparing them to me and I'm not a heel, but still, you get the analogy. Work with me here, 'kay? No matter how bright your star, Ruben - and it's damn bright - mine is brighter. And tonight, Ruben, in the Tokyo Dome, we meet. The Tokyo Dome has seen it all. Kobashi, Kawada, Misawa, Flair, Funk, Race, Hogan, all the names. It's got a longer, more distinguished wrestling history than any facility outside of MSG. Tonight, Ruben, we add our names to that list. Ruben Ross. Scott Slugger. And when it's all said and done, I'm walking out of there with that belt around my waist. I'm walking out as a six-time fans Wrestling organization World Heavyweight Champion. The WGA guarantees it. And hey, if you should beat me, Ruben - good for you. I'll shake your hand and raise it in the air myself, because you're one of the very few people in the fWo right now that I can actually respect. Tell you right now though, it won't happen." Slugger nods to the cameraman, and they're on the move again. This time, they walk out of the HoF into the halls of the Tokyo Dome. Slugger stops the cameraman and begins to speak again. "One person in the fWo who I don't respect? Vic Creed. Vic Creed pisses me off. Vic Creed, that freakish little roid monkey who had the balls to DARE and interrupt my return. The return of a legend, the return of the greatest single force the fWo has ever seen... and that little piss-ant has the unmitigated gall to show his face? No fucking way. Vic, there are four things you don't do. You don't tug on Superman's cape. You don't spit into the wind. You don't cause people to lose to Eron. And most importantly, you do NOT interrupt a legend. Vic, you stupid son of a bitch, don't you understand anything? What you are is a wannabe. What you are is a nothing, a nobody. You wish that you could be me. You know as well as I do that deep down, way down in that shriveled little brain you have - which I'm sure is almost as shriveled as your nuts - you dream that you could one day, somehow, some way, become a shadow of the greatness that is me. Not gonna happen. I've taken dumps that were greater than you, Vic. More talented too. Better workrate, cut a better promo... I could go on. But Vic, as of right now you're on my Shit List, which is a place you do not want to be. The next time I see you, you worthless jackass, I'm gonna put you in a whole new dimension of pain, do you understand me? The next time I see you, I'm gonna beat your ass to make up for what happened at Countdown... and if you cross me again after that, I'm gonna rip off one of your inflated, pumped-up, about-to-explode arms, beat you about the head and torso with it, then shove it straight up your needlemarked ass, you understand me, pal? I know, I know, that sounds harsh to everybody, but there's one lesson that everybody needs to learn right now - I am STILL the best this fed has ever seen, and nobody but NOBODY messes with me without paying the price. Prepare to learn that lesson well, Vic Creed. And until next time... it has been your pleasure." Slugger nods at the cameraman, who hits the off button and we fade to black.
