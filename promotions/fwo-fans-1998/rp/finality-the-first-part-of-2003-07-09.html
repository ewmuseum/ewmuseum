Finality, The First Part Of

9 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was dusk in the forest. The sun was setting, as another day prepared to come to a close at this place far, far away from human civilization. The fear of complete darkness did not phase Rei, however, who remained seated on a large rock, sitting silently in meditation. She had her long blue hair tied back into a ponytail to keep it from getting it in the way, and wore a loosely fitting black shirt and grayish khakis. She was obviously dressed casually- after all, this was a meditation session, not a fashion contest. Still, her natural beauty shined through her slightly rugged appearance. She remained deep in meditation until the cackling of thunder brought her back to reality, like a rubber band snapping backwards. Her eyes opened as she began to feel the sensation of raindrops coming down onto her, drenching her hair. This rain is coming down awfully hard. she thought. It can't be a good sign. Something is wrong, as if I'm losing control of this place. Rei stood up, pulling her hair out of the ponytail and shaking it around a bit. Concentrating, the forest full of rain was gone, replaced by a dimly lit room. Its interior was empty, nothing inside of it but some dirt on the steel floor. Rei looked around, until she spotted something else on the floor. A cross. She sighed. "It's Jessica." Rei muttered. "She's lost control." And as if on cue, as soon as the words came out of her mouth, Jessica appeared on the dirt floor, lying face-down in a pool of her own blood. "Jessica!" Rei screamed out, leaning over and turning her over. Now, Rei did not like Jessica to any great degree- she found her to be every bit as ridiculous as the religion she practiced- but she did not hate her, and she certainly did not want to see her in the state she was currently in. The blood was everywhere. Crusty and old over her eyelashes, still bleeding out of gashes on her cheeks and nose, and she still coughed up blood. All in all, Rei had never even seen their mother get beaten this badly. She franticly tried to wipe the blood away. "What happened?" Rei asked Jessica, almost hysterically, her voice raising higher and higher in pitch. "How did you....who did this to you?" And to Rei's horror, Jessica smiled, and gave her answer.
