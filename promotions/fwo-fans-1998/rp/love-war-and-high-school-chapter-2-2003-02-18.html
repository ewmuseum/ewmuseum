Love, War, and High School- Chapter 2

18 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Another day. It was Janaury 30th, 1995. Matthew Vorankias stepped off the bus that brought him from his home to the Jefferson Private Institute for Gifted Children in San Fransisco, California. It was a bright, sunny day- the kind that made you glad to wake up in the morning. Matthew looked around at the beautiful plantlife growing in the gardens outside the private school's gate, and took a moment to take it all in. He breathed in deep and smiled, walking foward towards the large metal gate to the school. Today was the day Matthew looked foward to. They would find out the results of the school-wide standerdized test they took a few weeks ago, a few days after he had met....her. Then, he saw her climbing off the bus, pulling her long red hair back and smiling that perfect smile. It was over two weeks after his first meeting with Amy Casora on that same bus ride, and the two hadn't said much to each other since besides the usual awakward greetings, but for a moment Matthew considered going over there and trying to engage in some form of a conversation. He soon talked himself out of that, however, and continued on towards the metal gate. He tried to think of something else besides Amy, and his thoughts went back to what a great day it would be. Just like last year, he would find himself on the top of the class in the standerdized test, and he would once again get that warm feeling he felt everytime he succedeed. Matthew lived to hear the praise from the teachers and fellow students. It was one of his favorite things about being a so-called "child prodigy". And so, he carreid himself in a distinctly joyous matter as he made his way to the metal gate. The gate opened up right on schedule, revealing the lush campus of the Jefferson Private Institute. The students made their way through the gate and onto the pavement that brought them from the large field and garden and to the buildings. Matthew looked around at his surrondings. Green grass and beautiful plants everywhere, Matthew was suddenly happy he lived in California and not a place that got snow all the time. No way I would want to go a whole season without the beauty of flowers. Matthew thought to himself. He made his way through the pathway with the rest of the students, heading towards the bulletin board outside the main building. That was where the results would be posted, and he would be able to get all of that wonderful praise. Only a little further. Matthew thought as he pushed his way through the masses and to the bulletin board. He looked up at it, smiling, and read: 1. Amy Casora 2. Matthew Vorankias .......No. Matthew suddenly stopped. His whole world was spinning. He had been pushed down to number two....by the very girl he had a crush on? How could this happen? What vile diety could tool with his life in such a cruel and unjust manner? This can't be.... Matthew thought. It just....it can't be. And then, he saw her. Amy. She pushed him aside gently as she made her way to the bulletin board, and he watched as her eyes lit up when she read her name at #1. She doesn't deserve this! Matthew thought. Who does she think she is, coming in here and taking my spot? I studied long and hard for this! I'm number one! Matthew sulked off away from the bulletin board and into the main building. He had been dethroned....by her. And now he only had one thing left in his mind. To claim back that top spot. But to do that.....he'd have to beat the girl he was slowly but surely falling in love with from afar. Life sucks. Matthew suddenly thought, for perhaps the first time ever in his young life.
