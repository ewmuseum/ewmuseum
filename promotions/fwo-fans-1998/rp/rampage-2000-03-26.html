Rampage

26 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Rampage March 26, 2000 (The camera barely shows signs of light when we hear an almost feral scream echo through the halls of the United Center. Mike Bear runs down the hallway wielding a steel chair, smashing anything in site while yelling something incoherent. Geoff Green stands off to the side, knowing he would stand no chance against the raging wrestler. Mike stops to smash a table full of catered food) Geoff: Aw man, we were going to eat that. Oh no, no, not the coffee. Ah dammit. Here they come, thank God. (John Willis and Austin run on to the scene also wielding chairs. Willis distracts Bear by pounding the chair on the ground while Austin runs around the lunatic. Austin slams the over Bears head, but he doesnt go down, hes only dazed. Willis runs up and takes a vicious, baseball like swing at the wrestler, sending Mike down. Austin kneels on Mikes arms as John goes through an all too familiar drill to the seasoned head of security) John: Mike! (slaps Bear) Snap out of it! (slaps Bear) Remember what Talbert told you! (Mikes head starts to shake, trying to sort out a plethora of conflicting emotions. He twitches a lot, but eventually settles down) Mike: Thank you John. (Austin gets off Bears arms) And thank you Austin, give me a hand, would ya? (Austin extends his hand and helps Bear up) What would I do without you guys. Austin (chuckles): We really dont want to know. Mike (chuckles in agreement): Neither would I. How many times have we been through this? John: Too many to count. Mike (shaking hands with John): Yeah, Ill see you guys later, ok? John: Later, Mike. (John and Austin walk off) Mike: Hey Geoff! Glad to see another friendly face around, it helps after what happened tonight. Geoff: Well, Im happy to help you out, you must feel terrible. Mike: I do, I wish there was something I could have done to prevent this. Geoff: There was nothing you could have done Mike, his mind was obviously made up a long time ago. Mike: Yeah, youre right, but that doesnt make it any better. Anyway, can we shoot a promo. Geoff: You sure youre up to it? Mike: Yeah, Im sure, Ive been doing a lot of thinking in addition to the rampaging. Geoff: Ok, whatever you say, Mike. 5...4...3......... (Geoff starts filming) Mike: Wretch. Chris Kidman Jr. You just made a big mistake. Oh, man, you dont even know how much trouble you are now in. Wretch, when you got up there on the TravisTron and said that you were the team and that you were the most popular stable, I would have laughed if you werent actually doing what you were doing. Chris, you were nothing without me. Sure, you had talent, Ill give you that. Sure, you won championships before I was here. But did the fans care? No. You just didnt have that certain something. You languished in the Punisher Squad and eventually lost every bit of credibility you ever had. The Misfits? What a joke! You were on par with Mike Heftel in terms of standing. You were nothing, Chris. N-O-T-H-I-N-G. When you paired up with Falcon, still nobody cared. The Underground was the Stump Squad of the summer of 99, except without the popularity, no offense to the Stump Squad personally. Then, I came along and was invited to join the Underground. Despite the fact that you guys were drawing zero heat, I saw potential in you, Chris. I thought you could go places. What I didnt know is that you couldnt do it yourself. When I saw that tag team tournament show up, I knew that was my chance to unlock your potential. Who went and got us signed into that tournament? Me, Chris. Me. Who made you study tapes of those other tag teams instead of your usual training ritual: beer and chips? Me. Who kept on telling you that we could win because you couldnt tell yourself that? Me. Who won the tag team titles at Mob Rules? Us. We did it together, Chris. In the ring, Chris, we were equals, and in the tag ranks we had no equals. You and I were on top of the tag team division. We were three time champions. However, it was me who got us there in the first place, because you couldnt approach Travis and ask him for a match. You see Chris, you owe me everything. It was me who got the fans to notice the talent you had. It was me who got us a push. Why did I do it? To get something in return? No. I dont work that way. I saw talent wasted and I wanted to make sure it wasnt going to stay that way, so I helped you. Together we made The Underground the most popular stable in the fWo, but I was the one who started it all. Well Wretch, now I want something in return. Ive got a date on my calendar wide open. April 29. Seattle. Cyberslam. You and I will step in the ring and settle this. That is, if you have the balls to avenge that loss from so long ago, or did you forget? Lets go back to June 23, 1999. An episode of Wednesday Warfare. The opening match was my wrestling debut in the fWo, and my opponent was...you, Chris. Chris, I beat you with three moves and a rollaway. It was pathetic how bad you lost to me. Go ahead, watch the tape. I gave you a hammerlock, a shoulder breaker, and a DVD, and rollaway from your Shooting Star Press attempt, and I pinned you. Now do you remember? I think you do. Its always nagged at you, how the one time we met that didnt include interference from Falcon, you lost to me. Youve always wondered, What if that ref hadnt gone down? Could I really have beat him? I know all this because Ive seen you write about it when you thought I wasnt looking. When the three way match was scheduled between you, Fear, and I, I could see the look of excitement in your eyes, even though you tried not to show it. I remember the look of disappointment in your eyes when Fear won instead of one of us. Well, Chris, nows your chance to prove to the world that you can beat me. But wait, I know what youre thinking. I cant really beat Mike, so Ill just try and annoy him and make him lose matches by interfering. Well, Chris, this is definitely not your day. You dont have to accept my challenge, because the fWo booking committee knows a money match when it sees one. This match will be signed, and you will step in the ring on April 29. Chris, youve got just over a month, so I suggest you make your peace with everyone, because I will kill you at Cyberslam. I will kill you for what you have done. No one has ever made me as mad as you have done tonight. Prepare to meet you end. Geoff (slightly scared): ok, mike, how about the frenchie? Mike: The Frenchie is inconsequential at this point. However, Pierre, I do have to give you a hand. You gave it your all out there and I do respect your abilities as a wrestler in those rare moments when you arent cheating. However, Pierre, I proved out there that when it comes down to a fair, one on one fight, I am the better man. I won, fair and square. No tricks, no international objects, just me pinning your ass. Maybe now youve learned. Learned what, you ask? Well, its something Chris Kidman will have to learn soon. You will both learn...to Fear...The...Underground. (The camera fades to black)
