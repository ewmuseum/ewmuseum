Fight Club Part 3

29 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I remember being inside a wall. Is there anybody in there? It was nice in there. Nobody hurt you, nobody lied to you, nobody bolted when things got tough. I don't see anything. Are you there? I didn't care that April didn't want me. I was used to that. I didn't even care that I was somewhere in between retirement and insignificance at the time. When you don't exist you can't be hurt. I miss you. Liar. I don't know why I'm here, but I need to keep you around. The world is a hell of a lot more interesting with you in it. Faker. I like you, but I can't deal with your mood swings. I can sleep with you but I can't tell you how I feel about you. I've got my own life to live, and I can't spend it waiting for you to figure out your head. But my head is figured out. We're not designed to make each other happy. Nobody is. It's time we all realized that, and took a shotgun between our lips. I'm sorry I ever tried to make this relationship work. I say I because you didn't do a goddamn thing. It's all a matter of perspective. Who cares if the person you thought you could give up your career for to spend the rest of your life with bailed on you? I've got my own goals I want to accomplish. You got a girl you fell for who didn't feel the same, and a girl who feels the same but has just as much ambition as you for a very divergent, very similarly time- consuming career. I can't wait forever, I've waited a long time already. It's true. I know this because Eli knows this. So that's your plan? You'll wall yourself up to keep yourself from feeling anything for anyone? Great plan, dumbass. I am Eli Flair's isolated consciousness.
