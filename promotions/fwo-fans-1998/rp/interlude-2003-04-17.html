Interlude

17 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For some reason, we're standing before a press conference somewhere in England. Several people and reporters have gathered for what would prove to be a completely idiotic and non-historic press conference that's really only resigned to poke fun at a certain blind idiot. The podium looked like it was stolen from the White House, what with the bad paint job put over the the United States White House logo in the middle of it, which now reads "Codemaster" in bad green lettering. Tyler and Conor Fuse sat on either side of the podium, glaring at the reporters who were all checking their watches to see how long this would take. Finally, the Codemaster, in his black robes, black cape, and obviously black skin, approached the podium and poked it a few times. The multiple loud thunking noises confirmed that it was working, and now the self-proclaimed "Blackest Brother In The Hyrule Kingdom" started to speak. "Hello! I just thought I'd give a quick statement to indicate that I am indeed fine and that the Tallywhackers' torture couldn't break me. Now that I am back in the Fuse Bros' fold, I hope that we can bust a cap in some Tallywhacker asses." the Codemaster said, "I shall now take some questions." A funny little guy with "ASH" stamped on his chest stood up. "Is your cock bright glow-in-the-dark green?" he asked. The Codemaster's eyebrows rose in surprise at this first question, "NO. NEXT QUESTION." A funny big guy who could probably kick Ash's ass given half the chance, and "NICK" stamped on his chest stood up and coughed. "Why are circles round?" he asked. Tyler Fuse stood up moved in the way of the Codemaster, and decided to answer this question on his behalf, "They're not always round! Sometimes, they're squared! We kinda fight in a squared circle of fun and flowers, and stomp the heads of the bad guys. Unless, of course, they happen to have spikes on their heads, then it's less fun." Tyler Fuse sat down, and the Codemaster coughed nervously, "Just for the record, I ain't responsible for anything my collegues say. Next question?" A funny little fat guy wearing dorky glasses with "WOJ" stamped on his chest stood up. The Codemaster pointed at him, "Someone who ISN'T completely stupid?" Ash stood up, again. "Why do you look like a Police Academy reject?" "Because they weren't taking any more Crocodile Dundee rejects. Next?" the Codemaster responded. Woj waved his hands frantically. The Codemaster passed over him at least four times before he settled on asking the black man behind him, wielding an uzi, a midget, and having "ERROL" stamped on his forehead, "Yes, you... the brother in the back." "Would you hire a Latino even if you had a gun to your head?" Errol asked. "The answer, of course, is forty-two," the Codemaster quickly responded, "Next?" Ash stood up again. The Codemaster frowned, he already didn't like this nutcase. "Why are KVC's eyebrows so thick and creamy?" Ash asked. The Codemaster sighed, and turned to Tyler Fuse. With a simple nod, the Tyler Fuse stood up and jumped on Ash's head. For some strange reason, Ash flew up, and then flew down past the floor as if it weren't there. Shrugging, Tyler Fuse returned to his seat. "Next?" "Are you black?" Woj asked, without even being acknowledged by the Codemaster, this time. "No, I'm really a cute little white girl inside a big, buff, black man's bo-- OF COURSE I'M BLACK! WHAT ARE YOU, BLIND?!?!" the Codemaster yelled. Woj adjusted his glasses, before he responded, "Well, no... say, does Roland handle you?" "Who the hell is Roland?" Tyler Fuse turned to the Codemaster, "I think this is some sort of thing I was told about... uh... kay fah buh or something like that." The Codemaster shrugged and pointed frantically at Woj, "Could you PLEASE throw him out of here? He's lowering my intelligence by just being in the same room with me." With that, the Fuse Bros. leapt from their chairs, grabbed a hold of Woj, and dragged him out of the room, closing the door as he went. From behind the door, these sounds were heard: Glass breaking, a cat's screech, trash cans being knocked over, a small explosion, the sound of running water, and finally two men running and screaming toward the door. The Fuse Bros. emerged from the door and frantically shut the door behind them, trying to hold back what appeared to be a small flood. The Codemaster coughed nervously before he spoke again. "No more questions."
