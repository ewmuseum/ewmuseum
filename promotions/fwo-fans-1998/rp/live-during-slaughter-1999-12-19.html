Live, during Slaughter

19 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::The scene opens with BQ in his locker room, Stump at his side on the bench he is sitting on. As BQ watches the opening sequence, he starts to nod:: BQ: Yup, more or less. ::BQ then watches the short conversation between Silver Fox and Nikki:: BQ: Yup, it's true. He likes younger chicks and she's a whore. ::BQ is silent for a few seconds:: BQ: Wait...she? Great, just great. ::BQ seems to get a brain surge, and pops up from his seat:: BQ: I got it! ::BQ points to Stump:: BQ: You stay right here. I'm gonna go talk to her, then I'm gonna see if I can get a match tonight. Huh? Yeah, yeah. You never know with Armstrong. I know he doesn't like me, and probably won't give me the match, but it's worth a try. Remember, stay right here! ::BQ hurries out of the room as a promo for Twilight '99 airs:: "Join us, live on Christmas Eve for Twilight '99! Yes, Twilight '99, Christmas Eve! Slugger! Ice! Frenchie! The World Title! Christmas Eve! Christmas Eve? What was Armstrong thinking? What's that? Oh, yes! James is a great man! He's putting my kids through college!" ::Fade to black::
