fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization All better. April 15, 2000 ::Geoff Green reclines his seat in the parked fWo van, taking a quick break from the locale shooting that should be used as the opener to tomorrow's Slaughter. Seated in the passenger's seat is Mike Heftel, also reclining, with his hands propping up his head on the seat.::: Geoff, do you think this will work? What, Mike? This whole Stump Squad thing. I mean, it was just a week ago that Sean got all upset with me, after that miscue with Stump. Yeah, but he's not upset anymore, is he? Well, no, not after kicking my tuchus on Warfare. I'd like to think that things are smoothed over, and really, they should be. Then why so worried? Oh, I don't know. I try so hard Geoff. For almost a year, I've been doing my thing out there, and this whole Silver Slowblack thing....I don't know. It's good to have some stability, and I've got to admit, I dig the costume, but I'm not sure it's me, exactly. Oh, and MOTYC was? Or the Infallible Barry McGee? Or Job Van Dam? Point. And I guess I should admit that I've had some succes pairing up with BQ. But we just got back together, after Warfare. I'm not sure if we can function as a team. And against Wretch and Scott Hart, we'll need to give it everything we've got. Um, Mike...? They're great, Geoff. Two tag team reigns, beating teams like the Gunmen and the Foundation, not to mention all of those successful defenses... Mike.. No one could hold a candle to them, Geoff. Not to take anything away from the current champs, but I'm surprised the Guano Apes managed to wrest the titles from them. Mike, you're thinking of The Underground, Wretch and Bear. Am I? Yeah. You'll be facing Wretch and Scott Hart. *The* Scott Hart? Yeah. Oh. We shouldn't have any problems then. :::Green simply smiles, as we fade to a promo for Slaughter.:::
