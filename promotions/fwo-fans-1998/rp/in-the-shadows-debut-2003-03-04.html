In The Shadows - Debut

4 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello Shadow Junkies! What is this you're asking? Not a "normal" promo where you have your favorite wrestler acting out some bullshit. Yeah, well when the fWo killed Graphic Violence they also killed the way I'm going to do individual promos. All those other wrestlers who like to be told what to do can go and have a video camera follow them around during their escapades, but I'm breaking the norm. I'm just going to something along the lines of a "confessional" like you see on MTV's real world. Just me and the camera one on one and you get to hear what I have to say. Straight from the mouth of Steven Shadows. Not from the storyline writers of the fWo. With that being said, let's move on... For all of you idiots out there, Eli Flair and I aren't going along with a script. Eli and I truly hate each others guts. The fWo has been all over our asses for the constant riots, unscheduled matches and little flaming table match we had didn't sit to well with them. Some bullcrap about "putting the lives of the fans in danger" or something. Like you guys care. You just want to see blood as it is. I thought some second degree burns would be a nice touch for you blood suckers. Flair is and will never be on the same level as me. Hell, the same applies for the entire fWo roster. Yeah, that's right you heard me. You know it's true. Who was the biggest name in all of wrestling? Graphic Violence. Who was the most recruited wrester in all of wrestling? Graphic Violence. If you needed a quick fix on your ratings who did you call? That's right, Graphic Violence. But now the times change and GV is dead. And I'm going to prove to Flair and to the rest of you idiots that Steven Shadows can be just that big. Don't think so? Just last night I was backstage at Monday Night RAW with my good friend KroW having a few drinks with Vince. We were talking over the details of a possible contract once my time in the fWo runs out. KroW will most likely head there since he's a free agent right now. Good for him, however Vince knows talent. Did he ask Flair to join him? No. And I don't think I need to explain why Vince doesn't want to ask Flair. The guy is nothing more than a cancer backstage. Flair is the most unprofessional wrestler in the industry while I am the complete opposite. Speaking of unprofessionals I think it's safe to say that the fwowrestling.com people who make the website are complete idiots. How long ago did the fWo kill Graphic Violence and they still have that damn GV banner up on my bio along with my old pictures. I did a new photo shoot over a month ago and the fWo web crew has yet to put them up. It's just part of the scheme to make me look like an idiot like they did when they tried to ruin what I created with Graphic Violence. Do your damn job fWo. I mean why the hell am I always the last one to get what I want? To get what I deserve? It's a damn shame that you treat your top stars the way you do. Treat the guys who bring you the money in like they're pieces of crap. I'm a star damnit, not Eli Flair. Treat me with the respect I deserve! Well I have to wrap this up as my plane leaves soon. Heading to some crap hole in Algeria for Warfare. Yeah, that's right Algeria. The fWo must be in a slump if we're performing in Algeria. Where the hell is this place anyways? There better be a good movie on the plane. Until next time you guys can just continue to watch Steven Shadows amaze and fill arenas all over the world. Fuckin' idiots. Worlds Greatest Wrestler, Steven Shadows
