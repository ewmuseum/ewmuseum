The Triple X Show(part 2)

13 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 10, 2002. Unleashed. Camera rolling. Stevens - "I wouldn't expect you to know too much about me, Lance. I mean... how could you? In order to know anything about who I am, or what I've done, you'd have to actually study... watch the tapes... be a student of the game." XXXstasy sat on a stool facing the camera. The camera was positioned to see him from the waist up... so what he was wearing below was foreign to the public. All that was noticeable was his black 100% cotton, "Perfect Circle" t- shirt, his long golden locks that hung, still soaked from the shower, and the famous blue eyes. "I didn't know you personally Lance. And, if you want to be completely honest... before the fWo signed us to compete against each other in this Internet Title Tournament... I didn't give two shits about you. But, I'd be a liar if I didn't say that I noticed you. Because I did. I saw all the flash... all the hype with you coming from that impressive 'Survivor 2' cast. And, I thought that there was a small possibility of you being an actual student... someone who knew enough about the business to realize that you don't go running off at the mouth with nothing to back up your claims, but assumptions. "I can't wrestle? "Well, since you've been so high on Eli Flair lately... I suggest you go ask him his opinion of my wrestling ability since he knows me best. And, while you're at it... you might wanna get a couple of tips... stop past a church for prayer... and, hire some back up. Because, you're in for a long, long Monday. "Sure I can fly with the best of 'em. And, yeah... I can entertain on the stick if given the opportunity. But, I'm a wrestler first... I didn't get into this business because of some need to be extreme, or to curse somebody out publically. Kid, you've got a lot to learn. And, if you think TWO moves is all you'll see from me... you're SADLY mistaken." Sean sat still for a moment... looked up at the camera, then got up from his seat and pressed the pause button. ------ Ivy was in the kitchen looking on, as Sean entered. Poison Ivy - "Sean... what's wrong?" Stevens - "Thirsty. Voice was beginning to crack. Sean opened the refrigerater and grabbed a bottle of spring water. He drank it and pulled the remaining drops over his head, then went back to the camera, pressed 'Play' and sat back on his stool. ------ Stevens - "I'm not a human suplex machine... I'm probably not even in the top five... but, THAT's about all you have on me, Lance. Like I said... I'm smarter than you... faster than you... stronger than you... and, just so happen to be... BETTER than you. "Don't blame me... you're God now, right? Blame your people. They created me. "So maybe you won't throw any punches... or kicks... but, Lance... those were only TWO examples... what are you gonna do when I reverse your deathlock? Or turn one of your predictable suplexes into a victory roll for the one- two- three. And, so what if I can't counter everything you do? Worse thing that can happen is I'll actually get mad, and beat you from pillar- to-post because I'm fed up. "And, you're right... this ISN'T a house show. This isn't some event where only about twenty- five hundred people are in attendance, and it's your word against mine as to what ACTUALLY went down. Unleashed is the real deal, Knight. In front of the ENTIRE world, with everything in the world to lose at stake. I'm game. I'm prepared to recieve whatever it is you want to give, and equally prepared to give just as much. "Because contrary to WHAT you've been lead to believe, Lance... this business is all about WRESTLING... and, yeah... the nickname 'Blue- Eyed Badass' helps me sell a few t- shirts, but at the end of the day I'm a wrestler. "... Loved by few, envied by most, hated by plenty, and wanted by many." Sean smirked. "And, I don't care WHO you are, Knight... God... or the same old one trick, cheap trick, boring, Kojak lookin' Lance Knight we've all grown accustomed to seeing... I'm gonna beat you. Because this is MY world... my profession... "... the Triple X Show." Sean simply sat there... as the camera faded out.
