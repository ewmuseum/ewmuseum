The Story Of My Life.3.A

23 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
October 11th, 1978. 2:34 p.m. A small child sat silent in a room watching television. His favorite show was on the television that being "The Muppet Show." He sat watching the television clutching his Kermit The Frog stuffed doll in his clutches. Nobody could get that off of him besides his daddy, and on this day he was outside barbecuing while his mother, Martha Stevenson was in the kitchen cooking up some vegetables. Today was to be a big day, it was Martha and Daniel's first anniversary. Some family and friends were stopping by at around 3 o'clock to help the family celebrate. The young toddler, at the tender age of three continued to watch his favorite show, the occasional giggle coming from his mouth. Martha walked into the adjoining room and picked up the young child cradling it in her arms as she gave the child a raspberry right across his stomach. "Brandon, mommy loves you, your gonna be a good little boy for mommy, huh?" Martha said in a high pitched babyish sounding voice. Brandon looked up at her, his little chubby face formed into a smile as he shouted "YEA!" to his mother as she gave him a big hug. Martha put Brandon on her leg and bounced him up and down a bit making him smile. The California sun had made the house almost like a small oven, all of the windows were open letting the cool breeze blow through the house, providing brief moments of refreshment. Then slowly the front door creaked open, and in the door way stood a 6'3" hermit looking fellow. Instantly Martha put the child down on the couch and stood up looking at him. "What the hell are you doing?" Martha said as she reached to the table, going for one of her cigarettes. Instantly the man's hand shot down griping Martha around the wrist. "Listen here, give me the child!" the man shouted, his blue eyes instantly flashed to Brandon. "Rick, what the hell are you doing here? GET OUT! OUT!" Martha shouted at Rick who looked at her, a wild look in his gaze. "Give me the child, or I'll kill you to get him!" Rick shouted as Martha began screaming... "DANIEL! DANIEL! HELP!" Rick tossed Martha to the side as he stepped towards Brandon, but was quickly intercepted by a 6'6" 265 lbs. man flying through the air and tackling Rick to the ground. Daniel quickly mounted Rick and began delivering fist after fist into Rick's face each fist adding more blood to the scene. Martha struggled over to the phone and picked it up, dialing 9-1-1. Quickly the police were on the scene, and once again Rick Thompson was lead away to jail, after only being out for a total of 15 days.
