Birth Of Ignorance [2] Champions Wrestling League

2 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
My own flesh and blood couldn't even look me in the eye. I'd been told that my future in the wrestling industry was over, but in my mind it'd never really begun. I spent years being crafted into something I never thought that I could be and the only thing worse was being told that now I'd become it, I couldn't be it any longer. My father had diagnosed me incapable. Time for a second opinion. January 28th 1994. Kellen Kinkade dipped his pair of sunglasses half way down his nose to stare up at the burning CWL logo that sat high above the doorway to the promotions head offices. He swallowed hard in a nervous manor as he pushed through the revolving doors, this was the first job he'd ever applied for that his father wouldn't intervene in and ensure for him. "I'm here to see Mr. Hailer." Kellen mumbled nervously upon arriving at the reception desk. "Ah... you must be." The receptionist smiled and paused for a second to consult her diary "Kellen Kinkade? You can go up right now... Mr. Hailer has been expecting you. Take a left when you get out of the elevator on the fifth floor and you can't miss it." A polite nod from Kinkade and he was on his way, a couple of elevator button presses later he found himself standing on the hallway that lead to perhaps his only future in the wrestling industry. He drew back his hand to knock on the door sporting the name of the CWL owner but before he could a "Come in." could be heard from inside. "Well if it isn't the son a legend." Hailer remarked as he extended his hand, Kellen uncomfortably accepted the shake. Less than pleased at the mention of his father so early in the discussion. "It's nice to meet you Mr. Hailer." Kellen spoke back as he took a seat. "Please, call me Robert." Hailer replied "So I understand you're here to discuss a possible contract? I've got to tell you son... that surprises me given the relationship I have with your father." "Well... my father doesn't conduct my business, Robert." Kinkade through somewhat gritted teeth. "Independent man, that's good to hear... I just raised an eyebrow when I saw that you were interested in joining the CWL. I'm pretty sure that you're aware that your father and I do-" The CWL owner was quickly cut off in mid-sentence. "Don't get on because the CWL has a richer history behind it, nearly one hundred years in business I believe?" The words escaping Kellen's mouth brought a wry smile to Hailer's face. "Good to see you've done your homework, but yes... your father is somewhat resentful of the level of success that the CWL has achieved over the last century. I can't say there haven't been attempts to bring him on board during his more productive days but by the time he wanted to come on board the ship had already set sail." Hailer smiled to himself, nothing made a power hungry promotion owner more happy than bettering the rivals. "It's nice to hear about old times, but I'm here for the new." Kinkade tried to switch the conversation back onto the rails that took it where he wanted it to go "If it's alright with you Robert, I'd like to get the contractual stuff underway." "Certainly." Hailer replied as he reached into his desk and produced a contract "But first I want you to do something for me." "That would be?" Kinkade asked back. "Take off your sunglasses." Hailer's request brought about a few moments of extremely awkward silence before Kellen Kinkade finally reacted. "Why?" Kinkade questioned. "Because I'm asking you to." A quite stern answer came from Hailer "You're on my premises asking for a contract to wrestle in my promotion, I shouldn't need a better reason than that." Reluctantly Kinkade reached up and placed a hand on the frame of his sunglasses, slowly drawing them off his face and placing them on the desk in front of him. Hailer surprisingly to Kinkade smiled as opposed to the horrified expression he'd expected. "This is perfect!" Hailer beamed "Come on now Kellen, your story has been in just about every wrestling publication that there is since it happened. I had wondered just how convincing the injury looked but this is going to be perfect." Kinkade looked puzzled "Perfect? Perfect for what?" "For our new image."
