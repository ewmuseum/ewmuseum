Code de Cheat. IX

7 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Conor not only stood there, with anger running through him, but he built up enough will-power to actually shove Eric in the side of the shoulder. Now, where it didn't knock Eric back... not even the slightest, it still caused him to get mad... So he pushed Conor so hard, that he fell into a book shelf. "IT'S ON!!" Tyler cried, ramming his elbow into Eric's gut, forcing everyone in the library to finally take their heads out of their books, and watch. Some scrambled to other areas of the library (especially the older people - not wanting to get hurt) whereas the others drew closer to watch this great contest; one in which they had never seen in a place like this before... Tyler was able to use his legs and drive Eric into the side of the wall, before pulling back and shooting him a left hand across the face. "Dammit!" Eric tried to swat his double-sight away... but couldn't, as Ty pulled his hand back once more, and crushed him across the skull for a second time. "DAMMIT!" But Eric was a tough librarian. He had kicked out a-many men in his two years within the library, so he could take a couple of these boys. He rifled a right hand back to Tyler, whom fell a good four feet. Yet, this cued Conor Fuse... who ran and nailed a move that only his trainer could teach him. A spear... a spear so powerful that it caused some drywall to fall from the roof... and the wall in which Eric had slammed into, to indent. "Take that! Hahaha!" But surprisingly, Eric stood, smacking Conor across the face with a right hand of his own. The people whom watched went up in "awe" (finally, eh?) and Eric just dusted off his hands, thinking that that... was that. He turned his back to check on the wall he ran into... but that's when Conor jumped onto Eric's back and started to coke him out. "QUICK!" Conor yelled to his Brother. "Hit print... HIT PRINT!" Recovering at a slow speed, Tyler dragged himself to the mouse, like he had been shot, grabbed it... and clicked the print button. We now zoom across the library, to behind the old lady's counter... where the printer... Had started its printing. "We need to hold him off for... um... about three minutes Conor!!" Ty yelled back. "It's going to take that long because there are other jobs before us!" Conor sighed, before he muttered a reply. "I think we can do that-" However, at that moment, Eric had gained enough power to throw Conor over his back and into the same wall. And as more drywall fell from the roof, on top of Conor Fuse, Eric grinned. "Now you're mine... BITCH!" Once more, the people went up in "awe". How dare a librarian use such language!
