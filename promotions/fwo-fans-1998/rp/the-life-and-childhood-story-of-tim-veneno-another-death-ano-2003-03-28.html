The life and childhood Story of Tim Veneno: Another death, another birth

28 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Damn are you sure you want all this?" The drug dealer held in his hand a couple of stashes of various drugs. Tim nodded his head accepting the drugs as he pushed over what was left of his last paycheck. Tim's life was going down the drain and he knew it and he didn't care. If he didn't care, who would? Tim went off to find his own dark alley, but most of them were taken already. For 3 years Tim fell into a dark haze, everything before that point in his life he had forgotten, he couldn't fully remember any of his childhood dreams, but what did haunt him of his past were images. Images of Tim's life would randomly flash before his very eyes, startling Tim when they occurred. Tim didn't remember how or who got him out of his drug abuse, but he didn't care for he was back where he wanted to be, in the ring training to become a professional wrestler. Tim was in the ring practicing his moves as a trainer stood outside the ring encouraging Tim to try harder. "Great Tim... perfect....awesome!" The trainer rang into the ring and congratulated Tim on his workout. "If you keep on training like that, you'll be in the big league's by no time." Tim flashed the trainer a smile, before being handed a towel to wipe the dripping sweat off of his forehead. "Alright Tim, I gotta jet, I have another wrestler coming in, in like 30 minutes so I'll see you next week same time correct?" Tim nodded his head signifying that it was correct. Till next week Tim thought. Little did Tim know there would be no next week for his trainer. That night after Tim left the building his trainer was viciously murdered for reasons unknown. Tim was confused and pissed off at the world helped him discover his love for highflying wrestling. Tim knew he had to move on though, Tim thought and thought about a new gimmick for himself, and then it hit him. He remembered his training briefing Tim over the usage of masks in wrestling. Tim knew that if he wrestled he didn't want the world to see his face for it would just be another person to make fun of him for his pale, pale skin tone. Tim decided to go with a mask to hide his past, and his personality. He knew he wouldn't be the out going type of wrestler and that he would let his wrestling do the trash talking for him. All Tim now needed was a wrestling promotion. Enter Action.
