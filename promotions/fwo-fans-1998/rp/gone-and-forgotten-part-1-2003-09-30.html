Gone and Forgotten, Part 1

30 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thursday, August 21st, 2003- 9:49 p.m. EST Voran Xias looked down at his hands, covered in their black gloves, inside his locker room, moments away from his match with Eron. He realized that this would more than likely be his final match in the Fans Wrestling Organization- no, his last match in all of wrestling- and he knew it was time to meet his longtime idol. He smirked with the complete irony of his entire situation, thinking back to two days ago when he was first informed of his changing destiny..... Tuesday, August 19th, 2003- 11:47 a.m., EST Voran sighed as he lounged back in his living room chair, allowing his long black hair to fall gently across his chest. He pulled the brown wooden handle back some more to cause the chair to tilt back further, and began to drift back to sleep, thinking mostly of Chi from Chobits. Don't ask why. However, he was awakened by the sound of the door bell ringing, and he stood up, brushing himself off as he yawned and walked out of the strangely dark living room and into the space between it and the kitchen. He opened the door to his New York City apartment, and was greeted by a short man with a receding hairline and thick glasses, wearing a gray business suit and carrying a brown suitcase. He smiled at him in a most strange of ways- as if he had been looking for Voran for quite some time. "Hello, sir." he said, the stupid smile never disappearing from his face. "Who are you?" Voran asked him with another yawn. "My name is Issac D. Cantin, attorney-at-law." he said, doing a polite bow and handing Voran his business card. Voran looked down at the card and blinked. "Okay?" "I'm sure you're surprised to see me, Matthew Vorankias, but-" "It's actually just Voran." he said, cutting the strange man off. It had been quite a while since someone had called him by that name. "Whatever." he replied. "It makes no difference to me. You, Mr. Voranki-....Voran, well, I'm not exactly sure how to say this. Ummm...maybe...." "Just spit it out." Voran said, shaking his head in annoyance. "You're the sole inheritor of close to one-hundred million dollars." Voran's jaw was nearly unhinged from his face. Thursday, August 21st, 2003- 9:52 p.m. EST With his reminiscing done, Voran stood up and made the final adjustments to his wrestling costume. It was already decided. Today would be the final day of existence for Voran Xias. And he would go out not with a whisper.....but with a bang. "I'm ready, Eron." Voran said with a smirk on his face. "No matter what the outcome, I'm ready for you."
