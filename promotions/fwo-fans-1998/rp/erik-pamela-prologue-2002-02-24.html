Erik & Pamela (Prologue)

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*It was January 4th, 2002. A beautiful Friday night. But then again, any night in the beautiful and peaceful country of Aruba was beautiful. A soft trade wind blew consistently throughout the night. Tiki torches surrounding a beach setting, with the sun a few feet from touching the fabulous waters of the Caribbean Sea. A small group of people were around dressed in the colorful textures of the tropics. There were only four people who were dressed different. And for a good reason. Erik James Kelly and Pamela Denise Nava were to be wed this evening. Erik looked massive, yet sharp in a tuxedo. The tailor was apparently kind to Erik's physique, as it looked to fit well. He wore a black tux jacket with no coat tails, black pleated pants, a white shirt with platinum colored vest and bowtie, and the most important part: sandals on his feet. It is an island and he's on a beach, afterall. He was nervous as he looked over to his friend, Brian Andrews. Brian was a friend Erik grew up with. He was a wrestler himself, but never pursued it as seriously as Erik had. Brian was 6'3" and appeared to be about a hundred pounds lighter than Kelly. Brian wore his brown hair short and wavy, to compliment his dark brown eyes. He was all smiles as Erik's Best Man. He gave a playful pat on Erik's muscular shoulder as the music began. From behind a palm and grass built gazebo, out stepped Kristen Jacobs, Pamela's Maid of Honor. She looked stunning in a low cut, soft purple dress, with a see through purple scarf covering her shoulders and wrapped softly around her neck. Her auburn hair danced vibrantly as she walked down the makeshift aisle, not noticing the iguana that ran across her path and almost got stepped on. Brian swallowed hard as he watched Kristen make her way. Erik caught this out of the corner of his eye, looked over to Brian and raised an eyebrow. Brian just looked back, shrugging slightly, and the two returned to gaze at the entrance of the gazebo. "It's finally going to happen...", Erik thought. His mind was racing with excitement. As the music began to play to announce the arrival of Pamela Dee, Erik thought back to the first days they both met.*
