Domino Effect - Part 1

29 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Chances last a finite time, and you're running out of time." Part 1 - December 23rd, 11:15 PM His eyes opened and blinked rapidly. Everything in the room was out of focus and blurred. At the moment he wasn't worried about the reasons why he couldn't move, he didn't feel too concerned with the feeling of numbness that engulfed most of his body or the massive headache that staggered his thoughts and made him quiver... He simply wondered where he was. "Doctor I think he's up." A woman's voice alerted from across the room. The Magi listened to her footsteps and watched as she leaned over him. He could feel her warm hand on his arm and heard her adjust some monitors to his left side. Then, more footsteps. Two people were now leaning over him as he lied motionless. The woman was now accompanied by a bald man sporting thin silver glasses clad in green scrubs and a white overcoat. He peered deeply into The Magi's eyes for a few brief moments. "Hello? Hello, are you awake?" "Nuuhhh..." was the only response the Magi offered. A painful grunt and a powerful exhale. As he took his next breath even he could smell the foul stench of alcohol on his breath, an aroma that suddenly began to turn the rusty wheels of his memory. "What happened, did you get hit by a bus?" The man in the glasses spoke up, interrupting the failed stroll down memory lane. "Huh?" the Magi replied with confusion. The man in the glasses offered a lighthearted chuckle with a hint of sarcasm. "I guess you're not in the mood for jokes, can't say I blame you." Still groggy, The Magi turned his head in bewilderment, trying to recall any buses, trucks or other assorted vehicles he may have been struck by in the last few hours. After a moment of enlightenment, he muttered... "Wrestle..." As he trailed off the doctor leaned in closer. "Jason? Whoa, Jason, that is your name right? The rep the fWo sent along with you said that was your name. I need you to stay with me ok? I need you to stay awake. Do you know where you are?" Another look around the room and The Magi recognized nothing. He slowly shook his head and closed his eyes. "Jason stay with me. You're in Medical City Hospital in Dallas, ok? I'm your doctor, Dr. Fields, this is Nurse Davis. You've suffered a very serious concussion along with a whole list full of other injuries we need to treat you for. Right now we have you on some medications but when those wear off you're liable to be in a great amount of pain, so don't be surprised. Is any of this ringing a bell?" The Magi's head was clearing. His neck was stiff and sore bit he was able to give the doctor a slight nod. "According to the representative the fWo sent you took quite a pounding tonight Jason." The Magi exhaled with another low sigh. "Borst," he grunted. "Yes, well whatever, all I know is I won't be letting my kids watch a wrestling show any time soon. We're going to take you upstairs and get a couple of x-rays on your ribs and your wrist." The doctor paused and lifted a blood-soaked cloth off of the Magi's legs. "It look like we'll have to check out your knees too. Jason, before you go, how much have you had to drink tonight?" It was one question he knew the answer to, and it was because of this he did not answer. "Your BAC was .19, that's more than twice the legal limit. If you had been driving a car you'd be a corpse. I'd tell you you're lucky you stepped into a wrestling ring and not a Chevy, but by the looks of it I'm not so sure. Your buddy tells us you've had a pretty serious drinking problem for a while now, he asked us to refer you to some places you can seek help." Help? Who the hell would request help for him? This hadn't been going on for THAT long... "Jason? Stay awake Jason. Come on, let's get you up for x-rays." The Magi was wheeled out of his small room and into a much noisier corridor. As he was pushed through the brightly-lit hallways the wheels beneath his bed made an awful screeching noise that echoed through his skull with a pounding rhythmic tone. "Have you ever had an x-ray before?" a voice asked from behind him. The Nurse made several such attempts at small talk that were relatively unreceived. Instead the Magi gazed up at the fluorescent lights that beamed down and pierced his eyes. The sharp lights and the high pitched squeaks, each unrelenting in their own way, were beginning to give him a powerful migraine. "How did it get to this?" he asked himself silently, and began to retrace his steps and actions over the last two weeks. His headache increased in nature, and soon reached a point that he last experienced over two weeks ago before his first return match against Great Dragon. More rows of lights passed overhead as he slipped into a daydream...
