The Break Fast Club (part two)

19 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BQ stands up, walks over to the railing, and sits on it. BQ: What do you say we... BQ falls off the railing, hitting the floor with a loud thud. A few seconds pass by, and he pulls himself up, then carefully sits on the railing again. BQ: What do you say we close that door? We can't have any kind of party with Sphere checking us out every few seconds. And I think we all know that if we *do* have a party and Sphere *does* check us out, then he'll no doubt crash it and proclaim himself the Grand Poobah of the shindig. Funpants: Well, you know...the door's supposed to stay open. BQ: So what? Funpants: So, if there's a fire, I don't wanna have to OPEN a door to get out of here. Sure, I won't hesitate in knocking all of you on your asses... Funpants turns to Evil-Lyn. Funpants: Or in your case...grab it... Funpants winks at Evil-Lyn and turns back to BQ. Funpants: ...but after doing all that, I'd rather just run out the door instead of having to open it. That'd just be a waste of time and energy. BQ: Oh yeah? Well if that's the way you're gonna be...maybe I just won't bump for you! Steve-O: Dudes! There's, like, four other people in there. Funpants: There's three other people in here, dumbass. BQ: Three others? BQ looks around the room. BQ: Oh yeah! The chick! Steve-O looks at BQ. Steve-O: Dude, I can count. Funpants: Hey, asshole. I was the one who said you were wrong. Steve-O ignores Funpants and continues talking to BQ. BQ: Hey, I never said you had to be smart to be a...a wrestler. Steve-O: Dude, you know, if you, like, disappeared forever...you know, if you went and moved to, like, Canadadia? I don't think anyone would know. I know I wouldn't. Nate: Yeah, I wouldn't either. We'd be too fried. Steve-O: For sure, bro. BQ: Well...I'll just run right out and join the wrestling team. Nate and Steve-O burst into fits of laughter, most likely NOT at BQ, but he doesn't know this. BQ: Maybe the prep club, too! Or student council! Nate: Dude, you know why guys like you mock everything? BQ: Oh, this should be stunning. Nate: It's cause you're friggin' hilarious! Dude, and the mask? I wish I had one. BQ leans to his left and acts as if he just heard something. BQ: What's that, Funpants? You're feeling left out? You were in the math club? Funpants keeps his eyes on the Playboy. Funpants: Up yours, Quickjobber. Nate points to BQ. Nate: Dude, maybe it's just me forgetting that I just said you were hilarious, but you need to lighten up and enjoy yourself. I mean, like, you don't even know any of us! BQ: Well, I don't know any lepers either, but I'm not gonna run out and join one of their clubs. Steve-O: Hey, let's watch the mouth, huh, Kemosabe? BQ: Wait...isn't Mike a leper? Hmm...what, Funpants? You're in the physics club, too? Once again, Funpants comments while flipping through the Playboy. Funpants: Break my foot off in your mouth by way of your asshole... BQ: So what you're saying is that you're in the math club, the Latin club, and the physics club. BQ turns to Nate. BQ: Hey Panama Red...you in the physics club? Nate: Isn't that, like, a smart people club? BQ: Got me. Hey Funpants, what do you do for fun? Funpants: You're actually asking me a serious question? BQ: Of course. I'm quite the serious person. Funpants: Well if you dorks MUST know... BQ: That's "dork". I believe I'm the only person who wants to know. Funpants: Whatever. Anyway....I go to a bar, get wasted, and pick up a random girl. I could care less if she has a face that looks like it's been in a washing machine, as long as she's female, hey, good enough for me. BQ: So it's sorta social? Demented and sad, but social, right? Funpants: Of course it's social. I'm in a BAR. BQ: Ah. So you load up, you party... Funpants: I don't get high, if that's what you're getting at. Nate turns to Steve-O. Nate: Only burners like us get high. Nate and Steve-O go to high-five each other, and end up smacking each other in the forehead, something that most likely has NEVER happened before. Funpants: There was this one time this guy gave me some brownies, but I didn't like him, so I didn't wanna eat his brownies, and I gave them to my brother Henry. I was still pissed that he won the spelling bee in third grade, which got him ALL the attention, so I figured it was time for some payback. Anyway, he started eating really weird foods, like funnel cake, and then he just felt like he didn't belong anywhere. You know, like the Twlight Zone, kinda. Steve-O: Dude, if you keep talking, then that Satan guy's gonna come in here and like, smack all of us. BQ: I can take him. I just beat him this past Monday. Steve-O: Dude, no way! Sphere's like, super-awesome! He doesn't lose to anybody! BQ: Who told you that? Steve-O: Sphere. BQ: He isn't gonna do anything. He's probably in his office right now, watching some tape where girls are rolling around with other girls. Evil-Lyn perks up. Evil-Lyn: Huh? What? Steve-O: Dude, you're just jealous cause you don't have any, like, asirmations to take over the world and stuff. BQ: Oh, but I *do* have goals. Steve-O: Yeah? BQ: Yeah, I wanna be JUST like Sphere...or you, I can't remember which. I figure all I need is a lobotomy and tights, and I already have tights! Funpants: Heh. Tights. Steve-O: Dude, I don't wear tights. I wear clothes. Clothes that my mom buys me. Funpants: Still. Steve-O: Dude, you wear fun pants! Funpants: I do not! I wear *hot pants*. There's a difference. Everyone hears Sphere moving around in the hallway, so BQ quickly gets off the railing and dives into the laps of Nate and Steve-O. Out in the hallway, Sphere's riding a skateboard. He goes to perform a kick flip into an ollie, but the skateboard hits him in the shin, causing him to crash and burn. He crawls back into his office. Upon hearing him go back into his office, BQ gets up and walks over to the library doors. Funpants: Quickjobber, sit your ass down. BQ points at Funpants and begins to talk in a stern voice. BQ: Young man...have you finished your paper? Funpants: I ain't writing no paper. BQ: Oh. Well...have you at least finished reading that? I seriously wanna check out the interview with Tia Carrere. Funpants: You do know there's a pictorial along with it, right? BQ: And...? Funpants: Moron. BQ shrugs and walks over to the door, where he cautiously beings to remove a screw. Nate: Dude, what are you doing? Steve-O: Aw man, he's messin' with the door. Sphere's gonna strike him dead. He can do that, you know. BQ: Relax, it's just one screw. It's not like the door's gonna... The door swings falls off it's hinges, coming right for BQ. BQ: Crapcrapcrap! The door knocks BQ over and he lays there for a minute before he shoves the door off him, gets up, and dusts himself off. Luckily for him, Sphere's getting a drink of water and didn't hear anything. Sphere finishes getting his drink of water and looks up at the mirror, where he checks himself out and flexes. Sphere: Cobadonga! Back in the library, BQ is trying to put the door back in it's place. Funpants: I *would* go on a rant about how that door's school property, but... Funpants shrugs and picks up the Playboy, while BQ simply props the door up, not bothering to screw it back onto the hinges. BQ skips back to his seat as Steve-O stares at the door. Steve-O: Dude, that's hilarious. Like, you should fix it. BQ: Am I a genius? Steve-O: No, you're this super-cool pro wrestler guy. Cut to Sphere, who is walking back to his office and currently enjoying a Zebra Cake. He can hear a conversation going on in the library. BQ: I've been here before, and I still have NO idea what I'm doing! Steve-O: Dude, the door...like...wait a minute, what's wrong with the door again? BQ: Shut up, Funpants. Funpants: I didn't say anything, Bitchsilver. Cut to the library, as everyone hears Sphere in the hallway. Sphere: God dammit! Sphere kicks the door down and walks into the library. Sphere: I...just dropped my Zebra Cake. Everyone else looks at each other in confusion. Sphere: But that's not my point. Actually, it *is* my point, but I guess I can't ignore the fact that I just literally kicked that door down. I'd like to believe this happened because I'm one of the strongest men alive, but I think it's a conspiracy. One of you took all the screws out of the door. Now...who was it? BQ: I didn't take all of them out. Just one. Sphere ignores BQ and walks up to Nate. Sphere: Why? Nate: Hey, man, I thought I was your *good* student. I went out and got you Baby Ruths and everything! Sphere gives up on his "interrogation" on Nate and looks to the back of the library, right at Evil-Lyn. Sphere: Who? Evil-Lyn: Squeak. Sphere turns to BQ. Sphere: Give me the screws. BQ: Okay, but I'm telling you, I only have one. Sphere: You want me to yank you out of that seat and shake them out of you? BQ: No...no yanking, please. Sphere: Give them to me, Quicksilver. BQ: Okay, but I only have one. BQ reaches into his pocket and pulls out the screw he took out of the door, then goes to hand it to Sphere, who simply smacks it out of his hand. He confidently walks over to the door, puts it against the wall, and props a chair up against it. BQ: I don't know if that's gonna work. It's a pretty heavy door. Sphere gives BQ the finger and leaves the room. The door slams shut despite not being on the hinges and the chair being in front of it. The force of the door sends the chair flying across the room. It smacks Nate right in the face, knocking him to the floor, and then the door falls over. Sphere can be heard in the hallway. Sphere: God dammit! Sphere walks back into the library. Sphere: I...stepped on my Zebra Cake. Sphere turns around and sees the door on the floor. Sphere: Oh, and the door fell down again. Sphere points to Steve-O. Sphere: Screech. Steve-O: Me? Sphere: Isn't your name Screech? Steve-O: I don't think so. Sphere: Well, I don't see the other guy right now... Nate is still laying on the floor after being hit with the flying chair. Nate: Duuuuuude... Sphere: ...so I'm telling you to come help me prop this door back up and put this magazine rack against it. Steve-O gets up and goes over to the steel magazine rack. He and Sphere start to move it, until Steve-O spots his favorite magazine on the rack. Steve-O: Whoa, High Times! Steve-O removes the High Times magazine from the rack and starts looking at it, leaving Sphere to move the rack in front of the door all by himself. BQ: That's very clever, Sphere...but what if there's a fire? I'd hate to have Funpants throw that rack at the rest of us on his way out the door. Sphere thinks about it, then turns to Steve-O. Sphere: You sick bastard. What if there's a fire? Sphere snatches the magazine from Steve-O and gives it a backbreaker over his knee. Sphere: Let's go...go! Get back to your seat! Steve-O: But I was read...I was lookin' at the pictures! Sphere: Yeah, well I expected more from an all-state wrestling man. Steve-O: Huh? Sphere: Your t-shirt. It says you're a member of the All-State Wrestling Team. That position those guys are in looks rather provocative, but maybe it's just something I don't understand. Then again...that would be odd, considering I'm Sphere, and I understand *everything*. Steve-O: Huh? Sphere: Just get back to your seat. Steve-O walks back to his seat as Sphere points at BQ. Sphere: You're not fooling anybody, Quicksilver. The next screw that falls out is gonna be YOU. Sphere turns to leave, as BQ mutters something under his breath. BQ: Jackhammerisbetter. Sphere spins around and looks at BQ again. Sphere: What was that? BQ: I said...JACKHAMMER...IS...BETTER. Sphere: You just bought yourself another Saturday, mister. BQ: Oh, Christ... Spher: You just bought one more right there! BQ: Well, I'm free the Saturday after that...beyond that, I'm gonna have to check my calendar! Sphere: Good! Cause it's gonna be filled, we'll keep goin'! You want another one? Say the word, just say the word! Instead of doing jobs for wannabes like High Flyer, you'll come here! Are you through? BQ: But...he'll get mad if his lawn isn't done by next week! Sphere: Oh, you think you're doing society a favor? BQ: No! Sphere: That's another one, right now! I've got you for the rest of your natural born life if you don't watch your step! You want another one? BQ: Yes! Sphere: You got it! You got another one, right there! That's another one pal! Nate, still laying on the floor, encounters a rat, who starts nibbling at his arm. Nate: Cut it out! Sphere: You through? BQ: Not even close, bud! Sphere: Good! You got one more, right there! BQ: Do you really think I give a Heftel? Sphere: Another...you through? BQ: How many is that? Funpants speaks, but is still focused on the Playboy. Funpants: That's seven including the one when we first came in and you asked Sphere whether Don Johnson knew that he raided his closet. Sphere: I didn't give him a detention when he asked that question. I simply answered his question because I wanted you all to know that Don Johnson is no match for my godliness. Nevertheless...now it's eight... Sphere turns to Funpants. Sphere: ...and you stay out of it! Funpants: It's seven. Sphere: Shut up, Pee-Wee! Sphere turns back to BQ. Sphere: You're mine Quicksilver...for two months I gotcha! I gotcha! BQ: Wait a minute. This whole argument is ridiculous. Neither of us can be here every Saturday for the next two months. We have fWEo on some of those days. Matter of fact...today isn't even Saturday...it's Friday! And since it's Friday...and we're in a school...how come no one else is here? Sphere: Oh, I'm sure that's exactly what you want these people to believe. You know something, Quicksilver? You ought to spend a little more time trying to be like me. You might be better off. Alright, that's it! I'm going to be right outside those doors. Or...door. Whatever. The next time I have to come in here...I'm cracking skulls! Sphere leaves and goes to close the door, but then remembers that the magazine rack is in front of it, so he stops himself, closes his left eye, points at the rack with both index fingers, and makes a "click" noise with his mouth before leaving the room. A musical riff starts up, and it appears Nate is the only one who hears it. He looks down and notices that the rat who was nibbling on his arm is now playing a guitar. Nate: Sweeeet. The clock reads 8:45. BQ has stolen a lighter from the semi-conscious Nate, and a sneaker from the very-conscious Steve-O, and is attempting to light the sneaker on fire. Nate is still watching the guitar-playing rat. Funpants is playing with his balls. Steve-O's trying to read his shirt from an upside-down position. Evil-Lyn is chewing gum and twirling her hair around her finger. Eventually, everyone falls asleep.
