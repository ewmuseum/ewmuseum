Precursor

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
My name is Henry Clay. I wrestle under the name of Neo. For some time I've been in this business, and nobody really seems to understand why. That doesn't bother me. I wrestle for myself. This is a business, and in between all of the fancy videos and sweeping tribute shows this is a business. People come here for money and if there was no money they just wouldn't come at all. Yes, there are fights. There are angry moments. There are fights where people just show up and start swinging. Understand though, that after the blood spills once, twice, and the fight is over then people come looking for their paycheck. I fight because I have nothing else. My life is a dirty sick parade of lies and confusion. I've had an abusive relationship with those around me. I have done these things in the name of my own misguided burning anger and hatred. I have been given no father, and my mother died while I was in prison for killing a young boy. I was young myself, rash and impulsive. I did so for protection of my sister, but in the end my sister has been ruined, and I was the one who ruined her. At times I look at the end of my own road and I pray for an end for what drives me. When I wake I hope for the end of my dawn. But the sun always rises and in the end I am who I am. "Why are you doing this?" Neo looked up at Carrie and blinked. "I don't know what you mean... I like knitting... sure some people look at me funny, and I understand, but..." Carrie sighed and collapsed into a chair. She pulled a pack of cigarettes from a jacket pocket and didn't move her eyes from his. There was an angry, mistrustful glare there and Neo suspected that she knew. It didn't change things. "Why are you doing this... why are you pretending to be this way? Understand something you sick little fuck... acting nice doesn't make you nice. Nobody is going to come and be your friend just because you knit a scarf or pretend to be a better person than you are." Neo put down the knitting and looked at Carrie with a cagey expression on his face. "I am who I am. If I wanted to fight, then that is what I wanted to do. If I want to knit, I want to be nice to people... well Carrie, that is who I am now. Be careful... you shouldn't block people from their dreams." Carrie pulled on the cigarette and continued to look at him in a way that made Neo more and more uncomfortable. Why was she here? Why did she always turn up when he didn't want her? This was his story now... and the time when their paths crossed were long over. "You haven't changed. You knit now, you bite and rip now. You're the same. Maybe you fool them, but then they never cared enough to know you. And why should they after all? You're a weapon. You're a fighter. You're a body they can put on the card and you aren't particularly interesting beyond that. You're a disgusting human being who lashes out at those around you, and so why should anyone care about anything more from you than a name on a card? Your life Neo, all your battles and you're worth little more than an ink blot on a paper." Neo blinked and behind his eyes was a darkness he was most familiar with. When he jumped to his feet and crossed the room she barely had time to move, but wouldn't have if she did. He grabbed her by the neck and drug her out of the chair, slamming her against the wall. He was hoping for a noise, a crack, something to give sound to his fury. She gave him nothing, but continued to glare at him from dark eyes. He noticed for the first time the dark circles beneath the eyes, the red cracks in the whites and the drawn cheeks. She was sick, and if he wasn't so enraged now it would have worried him and he would have hated himself for his weakness. He ground fingers and thumb into skin as he choked her, the knuckles white against flushed skin. His lips were drawn from his teeth. His muscles were taught, but she made no sound. In the end, it was this silence that brought back the memories and caused him to release his grip and drop her to the ground. He turned and headed for the door. "Stay away from me Carrie. This business doesn't concern you." He was nearly out of the door before her voice came to him, and he was glad that she did not leave it as silence. "You're not a killer yet then. Not so much has changed in these years then... but you're slipping. So be it Clay... go your own way. But you don't have much time left." Neo stood for a moment, his thoughts turning over in his head. Not much time left... but there was time enough. But her eyes... her face... there wasn't as much time as he thought. Neo vanished down the hallway. Inside the room, Carrie slowly got to her feet, the red marks of Neo's hand forming into purple bruised brands on her neck. She spat, and with no surprise saw the all too familiar trace of blood. Not too much time left at all.
