Pain Part 2 (Coping and Planning)

22 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Once more, Lance Knight lay in a hospital bed, this time in Montreal, Canada, and though the country was different, the scenery in the room was essentially the same. The same drab walls, adorned only by the medical instruments that were mounted or hung on them. The same uncomfortable bed, which was as unforgiving as it was adjustable. The same ugly curtains, separating the two beds and also covering the windows in the room. And the same damned situation, him stuck in the bed while a bevy of tests were being run. Lance sighed, his rage boiling inside of him like a basin full of molten magma, ready to erupt forth from his core with a lava slide of hostility and anger. Three times he had faced Grendel in one-on-one competition, and all three times, he had come out on the losing end of the campaign, in spite of picking up two impressive wins over the monstrosity of a wrestler. His hands trembling slightly from a combination of anger and the effects of his anti-inflammatory medication, Lance opened the single drawer of the bedside table and produced a cell phone. He pressed a single digit on the phone and then depressed the Send key before holding the phone to his ear. A few rings later, a familiar voice answered on the other end, and as welcome as it was to his fatigued ears, it was still a voice that would take some getting used to. "Hello, Dad." "Yeah...yeah...did you see what he did to me this time?" "The bastard poisoned me," Lance grunted, his face overcome by a mask of anger. "Nah, I'm not letting him get to me, but... "...it's just that..." he paused, still clearly struggling for words. "...I don't know. I don't know what I need to do to him just to keep my health intact." Nodding his head, Lance responded, "You're absolutely right, and I have noticed that. It's not as though the wins inspire him; in fact, he seems more content to just inflict as much pain as possible, which is why I've got to keep my eyes open in the next few weeks. We've got CyberSlam coming up..." He paused, listening, before smiling broadly. "CyberSlam, dad, it's the biggest fWo pay-per-view event of the year, that's all." "Anyway, from what I understand, we've got a match, and I think he's more than proving that he's willing to do whatever it takes to keep me from getting to CyberSlam at all, so I've got to find some way to keep myself safe and healthy..."
