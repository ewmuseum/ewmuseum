The Art of Self Destruction, Phase VII (Color)

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The hospital was unnerving for him. Everything about the facility made him extremely alert. He had been brought to the hospital against his own will. Like Eli Flair, he wouldn't go because of a few scrapes, cuts, or even a deep laceration. He was only there because he had passed out inside of his locker room. One of the officials found him laying a pool of his own blood and decided to call for help. Eight hours later, he woke up. His vision was hazy and he had a horrible taste in his mouth. In his unconsciousness, his mind ran wild. A smorgasbord of incoherent thoughts colliding together into quasi-dreams. A mixture of past, present, future and make believe all came together to form everything right and wrong in his own little world. Color was absent in that world, none of the vivid blues, reds, greens, purples existed. One color was all he saw. The color wasn't even a color. It was the absence of every color inside of a prism, everything he saw was black. He was blind. He saw nothing. When his eyes finally opened, they yearned to go back to seeing nothing. Everything was now a shock of color flooding into his retinas, blinding him with color, forcing him to close his eyes again. After fighting it, he weaned his eyes open. The entire room was white, almost like a place he had been as a teenager. The soft humming of a clean-air machine calmed his nerves slightly. A rhythmic beeping from the heart monitor came every second or so. The entire scene was serene, peaceful, quiet. Too quiet. * * * I've got to get out of this place. Wait, how did I get here? I don't remember anything after last night. My head is killing me, my entire body feels like it's resting between two pieces of plywood that have nails going through them. It stings to even think about moving. I don't care though. I just need to get out of here. The smells, the screams coming from down the hallway. It's all too familiar. GET UP! * * * He tried to sit himself up, but fell back, wincing in pain. He lay there for another minute, teeth gritted together. The pain was unbearable, two straight nights of putting a body through hell did the trick. But he knew it was all worth it in the end. Two things mattered to him in his world now. Red, and Gold. Crimson, and Gold. Blood, and Gold. He decided to lay in the bed, it was still early in the morning, not even seven. Rest was something that wasn't much of an option, and this was the only and most convenient time to do it. While his eyes closed, and the blind returned once more, his body fell into a small state of Nirvana. At peace with himself. But to him, the peace was war. War with himself. War with the rest of the world. A world that would be covered in red. Blackness in sleep was replaced by crimson red, slowly seeping from the top of his closed eyes, to the bottom. * * * Welcome to the beginning of your end, Donaven...
