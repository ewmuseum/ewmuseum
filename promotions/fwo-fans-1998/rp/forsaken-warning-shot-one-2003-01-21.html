Forsaken - Warning Shot (One)

21 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As usual, Amy Campbell was alone in her hotel room and bored out of her skull...just sitting around, humming along to David Draiman's contribution to the Queen of the Damned soundtrack. Of course, the rest of the 'Rat Pack' were out having a good time, but she'd been rather depressed thinking about her encounter with Brandon Roche the week before, and the week before that, and the week before that...not even Ivy's persistance could conquer - or even budge - Amy's mood. I'm over it You see, I'm falling in the vast abyss... She'd stupidly forced everyone - well, not everyone - to go along without her. It didn't come as much of a surprise, more a curiosity, that Brandon had apparently decided the same as Aimz; Ivy had said he was 'up in his room sulking even more than usual', and the redhead couldn't help but question why he'd be that upset and only prayed that she had nothing to do with it. Clouded by memories of the past At last, I see... She'd heard rumblings of problems with he and Ana, even from Brandon himself...but she never could have imagined what was to come. A slip of paper slid underneath her door and when she jumped from her bed and flung open the door to get a look at the sender, she couldn't see a soul, not even footsteps in the hallway were heard. The letter spoke for whoever'd put it there, though... Dearest Obstacle, You don't need to fear any worry lines marring that pretty little face; my love will be with me soon. Tomorrow I will solidfy our destiny. The One Love shall come home to me and there's nothing that can stop me, unless you enjoy staring down the business end of a revolver. You look like a bright enough girl to know that love conquers all, and that my love for Him will not be interfered with. You are nothing to us. I'll be done with you tomorrow, don't worry...he's mine now, and again. You will be nothing more than an afterthought. No signature, no stamps, no addressing the recipient...just the obstacle. I hear it fading, I can't speak it Or else you will dig my grave... Maybe it was time to talk to Brandon...fuck her own well-being, what if this psycho was going to hurt him? It was all too hard to digest...but it was definitely time to pay a visit to her hero. You see, I cannot be forsaken...because I'm not the only one. Forsaken - To Dust (Two)
