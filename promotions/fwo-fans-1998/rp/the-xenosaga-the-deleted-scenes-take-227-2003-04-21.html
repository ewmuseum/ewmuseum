The Xenosaga: The Deleted Scenes - Take 227

21 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE FOLLOWING ARE DELETED SCENES FROM THE XENOSAGA SERIES. THE REASON WHY THEY'RE DELETED IS BECAUSE MEGA JOB IS INVOLVED AND GOD FORBID THAT MEGA JOB BE INVOLVED IN THE REAL THING. The walls of this bathroom of particular interest were carnation pink. Not just regular pink. But *pink*. Also known as the most retarded color in the world, because only elite highschool socialites of the female category like that color. Everyone else thinks it's stupid. Which it was. "CUT!" Beef the Slightly Annoyed walked on camera again, as he glared in shock of the bathroom. He forgot who had been assigned to the painting duty, but what he did know was that he didn't do it. Allen Speck pulled out another cellphone, god knows where he got it, dialed a number, and started talking into it. The lengths we go through to perserve a decent joke. "Yeah, I don't have much to complain about here, though I think I'll complain about my lack of complaining. My robes are still terribly dirty and I think I'm going to hurt the director or somethin'" Speck said into the phone, as he wandered off. "Okay, WHO is RESPONSIBLE for this paint job?!" Beef said, pointing at the pink walls. Everyone in the room, even Dingleberry, Cagle, and Speck, turned to look at El Janito. "What in the BLOODY HELL? I SO did not paint those sodding walls." Janito complained, crossing his arms defiantly. "Well, yer the most gayest person in this herre room, we feegerred you'd know" Cagle said. "I didn't paint the bloody wall!" "Then who did, Captain Homosexuality?" Beef asked. "HIM!" Janito said, pointing frantically at a pink paint-covered Hans Krueger, smiling his stained smile, holding up a paint brush, and waving at Mega Job, the Tallywhackers, and Speck. Beef pointed at him, "Nah, couldn't be him." "Ja, now if you'll excuze me, I have zome important trash canz to kick over." Krueger said, before he tossed the paintbrush over his shoulder(striking a passing Eric Chad in the face, blinding him as he fell into a nearby net trap), and walked away. El Janito and Beef watched him leave, then they turned to each other. "Aren't we scraping at the bottom of the barrel for cameos, now? I mean, we've had Kermit, and we've had Other Kermit, not to mention the usual gaggle of fWEo idiots. Where's the love? Where's the peace? Where's the A-team wrestlers?" Then Eli Flair walked on. There was a loud standing ovation from the studio audience, even though there was NO studio audience to speak of. The ovation continued, even as Eli looked around strangely to find the source of the ovation. Finally, he had to ask. "Where the flying crap did that come from?" Janito shrugged in response, as Poison Ivy walked in. Another loud standing ovation from the studio audience, even though, as we have established earlier in this deleted scene, there was none. Ivy had a funny look on her face as she, herself, looked around for the source of the ovation. She then looked up at Eli. "I haven't been drinking, have I? Tell me I'm passed out drunk somewhere?" "If you're drunk, most likely so'm I... So I think you're on your own." Beef suddenly realized who Eli Flair and Poison Ivy were, "Hey! You're the guys who, like, totally beat me up that one time." "Couldn't that be just about anyone?" Eli asked. "No no, I definitely remember actually hitting a full nelson slam on you, once. You know, before I got absolutely killed, as usual." Beef said. "Ah." Flair said. There was an awkward, uncomfortable silence. "Eli?" asked Ivy. "Yeah?" "Why are we here, anyways?" Even though the question wasn't directed at him in particular, James Cagle chose that moment to don a graduation cap, a stick, a diagram, and a tripod stand to put the diagram. "It's a rather nifty leedle prawcess, Meeyis McGeenis! See, the man goes up to the woman, and they eenerlawk bawdies, and a bunch of humpin' and such occurs. The mayan's penis is all stuck up een the woman's vaginer, and theeyis white stuff comes out and proceeds to go awn a deer hunt! When it finds the leedle booger of a dder, it'll cock it's big ayass twelve gauge shawtgun, and proceed to pump that mother full of sperm lead. Then, when it proudly comes back to kin with the deer, eet grows a fetus. And that's where the babies come from." Cagle said. There was an awkward, uncomfortable silence. Ivy blinked. Eli blinked. "Eli," started Ivy, as she pointed a finger at Cagle, then back to Eli, then to Beef, then back to Cagle again, "I think I'm supposed to kick his ass now, but I don't know. Have I become dumber since we got here?" El Janito, even though he didn't get pointed at, nervously took a step back, "Oh no, no no no, I don't wanna get my sodding ass kicked by you again. It wasn't fun the first bloody time, and I doubt it'll be fun the second bloody time." "I don't... want to have to kill these guys," continued Ivy, laughing, "Seriously. Too damned amusing... please don't make me hurt you." "Wait, there's killing, now?" Beef asked, backing away, himself. Only James Cagle was stupid enough to remain within "IVY SMASH" distance, "No no. No killing. We don't have a sponge big enough to clean the horrible, horrible mess." "Oy found one!" Dingleberry shouted as he approached Mega Job and James Cagle, holding a gigantic sponge over his head that was about the size of three "Crocodile" Davy Dingleberrys, "Eet was a reeeeeeeeal bugga to foynd, but Oy got eet!" Beef stared at the sponge for a long, agonizing moment. "Where in the off-green hell did you find that?" "In the sofa, of course. Woy d'you ask?" Dingleberry responded. Beef rolled his eyes. Ivy looked at Eli for answers. Eli looked back at Ivy and said, "What? You actually think I've got an answer?" "Fuck it," said Ivy, finally, "Do me a favor, EJ... If you rise from your drunken stupor first, make sure I'm not face first on my floor," as she walked away. Eli nodded his assent, before he arched an eyebrow and called after her. "Ivy? I just remembered... Neither one of us has had a drink in about three weeks... Where the bloody fuck are we..." as he left, his voice trailed off. Beef, confused, looked around. "Was it something we said?" :: THIS HAS BEEN A MIKE RENNER / ALEX CLARK / PETE RUSSO JOINT. POT POWER! ::
