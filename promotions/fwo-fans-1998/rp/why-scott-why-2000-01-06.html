Why, Scott, Why?

6 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Why, Scott, Why? January 06, 2000 :::The scene slowly fades in from black to a view of a cloudy, gray sky...a storm appears to be coming...or already here. Thunder rumbles ominously as we begin to pan down, and we see a large van with an "fWo" logo on the side pulling into the parking lot of a large, hulking glass building. After searching a moment for a parking space in the crowded lot, the van finally finds one and pulls in. The intrepid camera team of Green and Finelli (minus Loco, too shaken by the events of the previous night to attend) exits, discussing their previous interview as raindrops start to fall from the sky.::: Finelli: Man...that guy is sick, Geoff. Green: He always has been. Finelli: And if he's serious about that killing the fWo thing... Green: When has he not been serious? :::They stop walking a moment to share a concerned look, then shake it off and continue into the hotel, whose banner reads "Grand Hyatt New York". When they enter, they are met with the sight of the penultimate luxury hotel- plush carpeting, grand stairways, bellboys running everywhere, and a general air of professionalism and class. Noticing that the main desk is miraculously free of customers and clients, they make their way to it, and are greeted by a receptionist.::: Receptionist: May I help you? Green: Yes, we're here to visit a Scott Slugger...? Receptionist: He's specifically told us no visitors. Green: Yes, but it's important. Can you just give him a call in the room or something? Tell Geoff and Flash are here...? Receptionist :::skeptical::: : Flash? Finelli: That's my name, don't wear it out. :::The receptionist rolls her eyes, but makes the phone call anyway. "Hello, Mr. Slugger?...There's two men here to see you- they say their names are Geoff and..Flash?...You will?...Alright, I'll send them right up. Have a pleasant stay, Mr. Slugger." She hangs up and turns to Geoff and Flash.::: Receptionist: He says he'll see you. He's on the 31st floor, the Regency Club...you'll need this. :::She hands them a special elevator pass key.::: Receptionist: Only guests with these can enter the 31st and 32nd levels. You use it in the elevator. Green: Thanks. :::He takes the key, and he and Flash walk away towards an elevator. They enter, amd insert the key in its slot. It opens a small panel, with the buttons for the 31st and 32nd floors. Green presses 31 and they begin to go up.::: Finelli: You know, Geoff, there's one thing I don't get. Why would Slugger turn his back on the fWo like this? He's always been one of its biggest defenders. *Always*! Green: I don't know, Flash. It doesn't make much sense to me either, but I suppose he's got some reason for it. Finelli: I guess so, but still... :::They continue the rest of the elevator ride in silence, each thinking about what could motivate Slugger to do such a thing. Finally, the elevator bell dings and the doors open to the 31st floor Regency Club, a true embodiment of luxury. All types of decorations line the walls, along with countless fancy paintings. The reception desks are all make of dark polished, paneled wood, and the light brown carpet is made of the finest materials. It's a level fit for a king, and Green and Finelli look around in amazement at the luxury as they are approached by the level's concierge, who eyes them skeptically.::: Concierge: Key, please? :::Green shows it to him.::: Concierge: Ah, very well. And you are here to see..? Green: Scott Slugger. Concierge: He's in Suite 384. Follow the hallway to your immediate left, then turn right. It's on the very end of the hall. Green: Thank you. :::The concierge nods, and returns to his post by the elevator. Green and Finelli begin to walk the brightly polished halls towards Slugger's room.::: Green: 384. How fitting. Finelli: Wow...another thing I just noticed- there sure is a difference between these guys, huh? Green: What do you mean? Finelli: Well, you've got Davis...Dead, whatever, out in the middle of the forest in a run-down shack with one lightbulb, and here you've got Slugger in New York's best hotel, in his own private suite. Green: That's true...but even if they live differently, that doesn't make them any less dangerous. Finelli: True. Green: Here we are. :::He pauses for a moment, then knocks on the heavy wood door. After a pause, Slugger opens the door. He's dressed fairly casually, in contrast to his surroundings- jeans and white & blue "Hard Rock Cafe" baseball-style shirt.::: Slugger: Welcome to my parlor, if you know what I mean. C'mon in, Geoff. I guess you've guessed why I called you here, and I'm guessing that your guess is right. You see, I've got a story to tell. Oh, has the WGA got a story to tell. A story of betrayal...of disrespect...and revenge. :::Slugger turns back towards his room, and Green and Finelli enter, Finelli closing the door behind him. Slugger takes a seat in a large leather chair near a window with a view of the city, and gestures to Green and Finelli to take a seat on a pair of not-quite-as-luxurious chairs by the door.::: Slugger: So, I suppose you've got plenty of questions to ask me. Fire away. Green: Scott- Slugger: Hold on just a minute there, Geoff. If you wish to refer to me, you can refer to me as Perfect. Because I'm the only man to have held the fWo World Title on four occasions, and those four occasions spanned five months of last year. No other person can lay claim to that. I am quite simply... Perfect. Don't get me wrong, I'm still the same arrogant SOB I've always been...I'm still the World's Greatest Athlete...and I'm still the best wrestler, the most charismatic interviewer, and, of course, the most ruggedly good-looking man the fWo has ever seen. I'm still Scott Slugger. But you will refer to me as Perfect- which is more or less a synonym for Scott Slugger anyway. Now...what do you want to know? Green: Well...Perfect...why? What were you thinking? Perfect: Well, Geoff...it started a long, long time ago, in a galaxy far, far away. Well, not quite. It started December 22, 1999. I was walking around backstage...minding my own business...and then I heard voices, coming from what sounded like below me. Usually, I'd think that would be fitting, since basically everybody in the fWo *is* below me...but this time, it sounded like they were coming from the lower part of the arena. There was a stairway off to the side, and I decided to investigate the lower area. As I walked down the stairway, the voices got louder. And when I reached the bottom of the stairway...and the boiler room...I saw...Austin Davis. Dead, if you will. He saw me too. Quite frankly, I couldn't believe it. At the time I thought of him as a worthless jackass, and I thought he was gone from the fWo for good. Obviously I was wrong. At that point in time, Geoff, he showed me something that I had only partially begun to grasp- the fact that Chris Universal and Ice were dragging me down. Don't get me wrong, Geoff, it wasn't some kind of mind control or god knows what like he has his other three under...it was just the truth. If it weren't for him...maybe the Dynasty would still be together, who knows. But the point is that he was right...and the Dynasty died that night. Over the next few shows, I continued to talk to him, and the disrespect I got from the fWo increased exponentially. Finelli: What do you mean, disrespect? Perfect: I'll get to that when I want to, Frank. Finelli: It's Flash, actually. Perfect: Whatever, Fred. Now, as I was saying, the disrespect I got increased exponentially...and myself and Dead began to plan. During the last two weeks of 1999, Geoff...Geoff's friend, whatever your name is...we planned the demise of the fWo. In his case, he's always wanted it, well, dead...and me? Well, I don't take kindly to disrespect, especially from the promotion I BUILT. Geoff: You keep mentioning the fWo showing you disrespect, Sc- Perfect. Maybe it's just me personally, but I didn't see that. Do you mind elaborating on that? Perfect: You haven't seen the disrespect? Christ, Green, are you blind? As that appears to be the case, let me open your eyes. Geoff, for how many days in 1999 was I the World Champion? Geoff: Well, I don't know off the top of my- Perfect: One hundred and sixty six. That means, Geoff, I was World Champion for 45% of last year. Do you even comprehend how incredible that is? Nearly one out of every two days in 1999 I was the World Champion. 1 OF 2! And what do I see happening when the Freaking Frenchie is stripped of *MY* belt? David Goddamn Spencer and Rain- Rain, who's never held anything even resembling the fWo World Title- fight for the title. Two men who have never been at my level, and never will be. And OK, fine, the match was ordered by the biggest dumbass in the history of fans Wrestling organization, Travis Beaven. Fine. Whatever. Let the Brit and the midcarder have their day in the sun. So I come to the arena the next day, and what do I see? I see that I'm not even booked for one single match and that the crap they're trying to pass off as a main event is the Lone Gunmen vs. Ultra-Cool 2000. A match that involves Kid Cool, Rudo, Machina, and the Purple Idiot. 1 World Title in the bunch, and we all know he won that from. All they had to do was sign me to one match- one match, with anybody from Spencer to Rott F'ing Dog, and they would've had an instant REAL main event, simply because I would've been in it. Not to mention the fact that my deserved World Title shot was nowhere to be found. I mean, WHAT THE HELL IS THAT, Geoff? Green: Well- Perfect: Shut up! As the previous champion, I should have been offered a World Title match. Hell, I should've been awarded the title in the first place. And then, of course, the next day I'm booked in a joke of a match with Universal and Ice. Sure, I was more than happy to kick their asses- but that's not where I should have been, Geoff. I should have been right there in the main event. Hell, every single main event should automatically have a spot reserved specifically for me. But for god knows why, they don't, and the end result of that night was the former most prestigious title in the world around the waist of a man I haven't lost to once in my life. Pathetic. Absolutely pathetic. Now why, class, did all those ridiculous matches happen? Finelli: Because of Travis Beaven and David Spencer. Perfect: Exactly. That worthless sack of crap Beaven and that joke they call Spencer. Beaven I still can't get over. Here he is, some idiot in some no-name tech company, and he gets a whim to start a wrestling company. So he reads some of the dirt sheets, and he sees that, hey, there's a young kid by the name of Wasner tearing it the hell up up in Washington. What a coincidence, that's exactly where he's decided to start his promotion. So Beaven calls me- the biggest star in indy wrestling at the time- and he begs me to join his federation- a fed run by a guy with no wrestling experience and a fed with three people on the roster. Just for the hell of it, I do this guy a favor and join up, and all of a sudden, once the fed starts, it's a big hit. The brightest young star is this Cruiserweight champ, guy named Slugger. Practically the only entertaining thing on their shows. Soon, Slugger's in the mains, where he should've been all along. Soon, Slugger's the champ. Soon, Slugger carries the whole damn promotion on his back for half the year. Soon, this idiot sides up with the man who tried to destroy the federation Slugger built over a year and a half ago, and wants to do it again. He betrays me, Geoff. He sides up with the man who wants to destroy my fed. I don't stand for that. You see, when I ran off Spencer the first time, I thought he at least had the smarts not to come back. But here he is a year later, and he's back. He decides to waste his time recruiting jobbers and getting tossed out of battle royals by me. Fine. I'll allow him to do that. But all of a sudden he starts acting up- saying that he's here to destroy my fed once again. One problem- he couldn't the first time, and he sure as hell couldn't this time. Hell, the man was defeated by the BLACK PANTHER, for god's sakes! And Spencer, I know you're out cowering somewhere in England or wherever, so I want you to listen to me, and listen to me well. At Invasion, I want to show you a thing or two about exactly how you go about killing a promotion. You see, you've failed twice...and it's time for the new guard to move in. If you've got the stones, boy- I'm gonna make an example out of you. Number one, an example to all of those who would oppose myself, Dead, and his army...and number two, an example of just exactly how you take over a promotion...and eliminate it. I'll be waiting for your answer. Finelli: Perfect, what about Chris Universal and Ice? You've been feuding with them for the past half-month- are you just going to move on? Perfect: Please. If you think I'm gonna let them go, you're mad- you've got another thing coming. Universal and Ice have shown in the past that they're too stupid to learn from examples...so we're going to have to teach them...directly. Green: What do you mean by that? Perfect: Oh...believe me...you'll find out. And so will they. Green: One last thing- are you really serious about...killing...the fWo? Perfect: I built it. I'll demolish it. It's that simple to understand, Geoff. In fact...it's Perfectly simple. Green: Oh...boy. Perfect: Now, if you don't mind...I've got things to do. You'll be leaving now. Green: Of course. :::Shaken by the words of Perfect, Green and Finelli slowly get up from their chairs, and head for the door. After pausing for one last look at the man formerly known as Scott Slugger, Green opens the door and exits, followed by Finelli. Outside thunder flashes, followed by a crack of lightning...and the silhouette of Perfect can be seen from the outside against the window as rain begins to truly pour...laughing.:::
