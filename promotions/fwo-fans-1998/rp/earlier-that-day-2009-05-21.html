Earlier That Day...

21 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Earlier that day... that day being Wednesday, March 20th, 2009 in the early morning hours of 9:34 in the AM. The scene was William Beaven’s office in Key Arena, home to the weekly FWO television show, reAction. The situation was thus: Max Danger was there to sign his contract to officially join the ranks of the Fans Wrestling Organization. Beaven was confused by what was written in said contract. “Okay, let me get this straight, you don’t want to get paid a salary?” said the former Kid Cool. He was seriously dumbfounded by this stipulation. Why on earth would someone want to not get paid for the work they do. Sitting there, his feet propped up on the other side of Beav’s desk, playing a game of Bejeweled 2 on his iPhone, not really paying attention to what Willy Will was really saying, Max replied, “Nope.” Beaven cocked his head to the side and looked at Danger like, I dunno, he was an idiot or something. He simply didn’t get it. “But... why not?” Danger sighed and rolled his eyes. Without looking up he was about to answer when he changed his mind and said this, “Your breath smells like coffee.” William wasn’t expecting that. “Really?” He breathed into his palm and quickly sniffed it. “Huh. I guess you’re right.” “Mint.” And Danger tossed Beaven a peppermint across the desk, which was promptly popped into his mouth. “Thanks. Now, again, I really think we should pay you.” Max never looked up from his game but shook his head. “I’d really rather you didn’t.” William threw his arms up in the air, exasperated. Then he slammed his palms down onto the desk and leaned forward, “But... WHY!?” It was really bugging him that he wasn’t being let in on why exactly ‘the Marvelous One’ didn’t want to get paid for his services. Danger finally looked up then, with an expression on his face that said ‘I really don’t want to answer what is quite clearly so obvious’ then, instead of using any words, he ran his hands up and down displaying the light blue seersucker suit (with pink dress shirt) like he was a model on the Price Is Right showing off the next item up for bid. Kid Cool cocked an eyebrow. Max sighed and decided to quote Dave Chappelle (which was still cool, right?), “I’m rich, expletive deleted!” He didn’t curse, so he had to alter the phrasing a tad. “Oh.” In reality, the reason he didn’t want to get paid was much deeper than him being rich, which he, of course, was very much so. But that was neither here nor there, so we’ll just forget about that, m’kay? William Beaven took the contract in his hands and shook them like he was getting the wrinkles out of them or something. He spoke then, “So, all you want is plane tickets and rental cars?” Danger nodded. “You sure?” Max didn’t raise his head, only his eyes, giving Beav that ‘are you serious?’ look. “Right, of course. So, plane tickets from your home... in Tokyo, right?” “Oh, lord no! Why would you think I lived in Tokyo?” “Because that’s what your application said and what you wanted to have put up on our website and to be announced as haling from.” “Well, I can’t exactly use something called the ‘Tokyo Driver’ if I’m not announced as being from there, can I?” Danger scoffed like William was an idiot. “True,” Beaven said after a slight hesitation. “So, where do you live?” “Augusta, Maine.” “Alright,” was all the former Kid Cool could say. He nodded his head a little bit then trekked on, “So, tickets from Augusta to Seattle, or in the event we have shows elsewhere, tickets to there, then tickets from wherever we are to Montreal. Is that correct?” Mr. Marvelous, as he sometimes was referred to as, gave the thumbs up. William continued to read off the contract in a low tone, mostly to himself, before getting to, “And you want a title shot? In your debut?” “Most def,” replied Danger. “Anyone in particular?” “Surprise me.” “Are you sure about that?” “Yeah, sure,” Danger said with a shrug. See, here’s the thing, the only champion that Max knew of was High Flyer and he wasn’t even sure if he was still the World Champion at that point. He knew he was back in December when he faced him in Just Wrestling (and beat him, as Max is always happy to point out), but now? No clue. The truth of the matter is this: Marcus Alexander Dox didn’t watch the FWO. He couldn’t name three people on the roster. All he knew was that he liked championships and wanted a title shot in his debut. He felt he deserved it based on the fact that he did, indeed, beat the reigning FWO World Champion back in December on a small independent promotion’s show back before the FWO had returned onto the scene of professional wrestling. But he didn’t want to say, “Give me Flyer” when there was a chance he’d look like an idiot and him not still being holding the strap. “Give me Flyer.” Okay, nevermind, he did say it. “High Flyer? The World Champion? You want a shot at the FWO World Championship in your debut?” “Yes, tonight, I want THE Champ. The face of the company. The man who I’m sure everyone else is gunning for. I want him.” William started to fidget a little. “Um, what if I can’t arrange that?” Max shrugged, “No big, just give me someone else.” “Anybody?” “Anybody.” Beaven went to speak but Danger held up a hand, “BUT!” He didn’t say anything for a while because his game had said “No more moves!” and he was displeased by that revelation. But he had managed to get his third highest score yet, so he punched in his name. William cleared his throat. “Don’t tell me who it is.” The former Kid Cool knew at this point just to nod his head and say, “okay”, before saying, “I believe that’s everything in your contract, so if you’ll just sign,” Beav slid the contract across the desk and held out a pen, pointing at the line at the bottom with it, “right here, we’ll have everything squared away.” Danger sighed because he had to remove his feet from the desk and took the pen from William’s grasp. He was about to sign when he stopped. His eyes darted around the paper in front of him. “What’s wrong?” asked William nervously. Max didn’t say anything for a few seconds. “Um, did you all put in the part where I’ll be bringing in a manager?” “Yes, it’s right...” Beaven leaned over and pointed it out “...here.” “You’re going to have to pay them, you know.” “We know. But they’ll need to renew their manager’s license if they’ve let theirs expire or just get one if they don’t have one at all.” Danger nodded. “And the dancers?” “Taken care of.” “Excellent.” He signed the document and stood up. William Beaven stood up as well and with a smile on his face extended his hand across the desk. Danger was texting on his phone, not looking ahead, and caught William off-guard when he shook his hand. Because it wasn’t with his hand at all, but rather a prosthetic that he had held out. “Jesus!” exclaimed Beaven as he jumped back a foot. “What is that thing?” “Armsy McDanger,” Max said, saluting with the fake limb. “Well, thanks, I’ll see you tonight for my big debut.” William, still a touch unnerved by the fake appendage that the Marvelous One was toting around, nodding at Danger and said, “Welcome to the FWO and good luck tonight.” As Danger exited the office, Beaven sat back down in his chair and took a deep breath. “You’re going to need it.”
