Reacting to reAction - Ground Zero in T-Minus Three

3 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I lie awake, thinking about my opponent this Wednesday. Next to me, Rose is curled up with her brother's old teddy bear - he's been dead for eighteen years now. She's sleeping like a little angel, and I'm tossing and turning. Only, I'm not. I'm lying perfectly still, exhausted but unable to sleep, drained but active and twitchy. Killjoy is in my brain. What does Killjoy have on me that's got him so intertwined in my brain? Why can't I detach myself from this match like I have every other match I've ever wrestled? He's connected to Miss Ivy somehow, and that makes this a family affair. I want you to be so much closer to the truth, but I know you well enough to realize you don't have the proper perspective. What kind of perspective does Killjoy have underneath that mask? We are free because of him, he says. Free from what? This would probably be easier if I'd been in this company for longer than a month. Free from Disney? Free from Neo? Free from continued scrutiny from the government? All of the above, none of the above? I told Rosie last night, Killjoy most definitely has the big world in mind when he wrestles, so I think I can discount any sort of residuals from High Flyer and his pre - Neo shenanigans. And I think we can discount anything that Miss Ivy told us about Tort Media's business problems after the Nassau shows. I don't know why that is - it's just a feeling. Miss Ivy's perspective is that of the businesswoman. She has no choice but to deal with the little world issues of the day to day running of the company. Moreso than Mr. Beaven is concerned, at least: he and Mr. Hupfer deal primarily with the big world. All due respect to Mr. Beaven and Mr. Hupfer, Miss Ivy is way smarter and more savvy than they are, at least, when it comes to the business side of things. Maybe that's what Killjoy is talking about: one of those deals where she can't see the forest for the trees. But those trees are Miss Ivy's bread and butter. She laments the fact that Sean calls her 'Mama Bear' but she really is; to all of us. That's why she insisted that Rosie and I stay with her and Sean in the condo for the time being, heck, that's why Lowell is staying here. It's Miss Ivy's subconscious need to look after someone else, that's all. Maybe that's it. Miss Ivy's concern isn't the state of the wrestling business in general, or the FWO in particular. Miss Ivy's concern is with the individuals that make up the business, or the company, as it were. We are free, because of me. Killjoy wants Miss Ivy to take care of the business in general, and the FWO in particular, even if it means sacrificing some of the individuals that make the company what it is. The needs of the many outweigh the needs of the few. (Or the one, said Mr. Spock.) That's against her nature. Sometimes, though, someone comes along... and they dream bigger. The sheer scope and majesty of their dream outshines all others, burning so brightly that it is capable of drawing others to its pristine ideals even after the dreamer himself has passed. But so, so many have forgotten that. That, Ivy, that dream is what I champion. Freedom is about finding Neo, stealing everything from him for what he did…including the shirt off his back. I hope you enjoy our photo shoot, I know he won’t. I think Killjoy wants to be a hero - and in some warped way actually is, but he doesn't want to be the actual hero. I think his purpose in being here is to find someone in the FWO that's strong enough to be a hero and get them ready. Ready for what? For Neo? For something bigger? If something bigger is coming and Killjoy knows about it, by purposefully not telling us, he's part of the problem. We can't be free as long as he's holding us back. Unless... he doesn't want us held back. Unless he wants someone here to be unleashed. I'm here to free us from all the mistakes of the past, mine included, and that's the biggest part you miss. I wonder, in the midst of Killjoy's holy war, has he himself gotten lost, unable to see the individual trees that make up the forest he wants to protect? Darn it. I'm going to be lying here, wide awake, for quite some time.
