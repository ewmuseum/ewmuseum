Best Days: Avo Chavez

28 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Avo Chavez sat down at the table as Lance Knight and Luke Starkey were drinking their beers. Avo wasn't known much to be the drinker but tonight was a different night. Avo picked up his glass of beer and slowly drank a little at a time. "You know what really annoys me?" Asked a somewhat buzzed Avo. "Is that every promotion I go to, the booking team expects me to do comedy. You know I don't like being the short Mexican who doesn't speak any Spanish." Avo set his drink down on the table and cleaned his mouth. "I didn't even want to do comedy even back in Realm. The reason why I got started was because back in Mexico where I first wrestled there was a girl there who I wanted to impress. You see, but she didn't like big tough guys, but that was fine by me because I'm not a big tough guy. So I figured I could make her laugh and win her heart the old fashioned way, by making her laugh." Avo finished up his beer and slammed it on the table as the table weakly moved. "So as the story continues here uh...oh yeah well anyways we ended up dating for a couple of years as she moved with me over here to the states. We moved over here because I got signed to Realm. She didn't like it here though; she decided to move back to Mexico. I grew sick and couldn't live without her. I took a leave of absence from Realm and visited her in Mexico. But, when I got there she was with another guy." Avo lifted up his mug that was once filled up with an alcoholic beverage, someone at the table filled up his mug and Avo continued to drink away. "So by the time I got back to the states I found out that Realm was closed. But I didn't want to wrestle anymore, for my heart was broken. Instead I wanted to fight. Thus I joined the Asylum. There I thought I wouldn't have to do comedy I could actually fight, but no. Joe wanted me to do comedy seeing how at the time there were hardly any comedy fighters there. So I decided to do it since I needed the money. After awhile though I got sick of doing the comedy and left the Asylum after Joe refused to let me do what I wanted to. I took off a couple of months off from the Asylum then was signed by Silver Fox and the fWo." Someone at the table mumbled a few words. "What are you talking about again?" Avo couldn't remember, as he was to buzzed to realize what he was even talking about. And decided to stop talking and let someone else share their story.
