Punishment for my Crime

13 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was cold, damp and raining in buckets, as Shygen had me outside doing mindless push-ups in the puddles to re-building my upper strength. After every motion I was suppose to remember something about Thailand, and why I was fighting for her all these years. I was suppose to forget about my wrestling training and focus more on what I knew that was in my heart. But it was hard to focus on my heart, when I had Shygen yelling straight into the back of my mind. She made it perfectly clear that I was in the wrong, but I wonder if yelling and making me suffer was the best solution. Of course it was. I failed my country. Thailand My Home Shygen watched on, interested in what I was learning after ever motion I made with my arms. She was going to teach me a lesson. I was never to disobey my homeland again. Wrestling or not, I was their pride and joy and had to show my self in a respectful manner. Losing was the issue, but rather the way I lost. Moonasaults Hurricanrania's DDT's -=-=-=-=-=-=- "If you would have done your job at the beginning." Shygen lowered her head slowly, backing off from a threat greater then she expected. She stood alone in a group of three. Each man scorned at her failure to produce a respectable fighter, and now, their prize wasn't even promoting them. "We expected more from you Shygen." She continued to take the verbal thrashing, almost as if it was a fuel driving her temper and to seek revenge. Her chin was lifted to eye level, and each man could see her shame without her even saying a word. She dropped again and backed away towards the door. " No failures this time. We put to much time into him to lose him" Shygen nodded and escaped the loneliness and left into the hallway on her way to find her destiny. Find her Fighter Find the one Find Padrona. -=-=-=-=-=-=- "I am doing this for your own good." He constant screeches brushed away any "wrestling" still inside my body. I was around the five hundred push-ups mark when she finally let me get off the ground, still dripping with water or sweat. She paced in front, like a caged animal waiting for her meal. "This has nothing to do you as a character, rather you as a person." I had to lock my eyes ahead, like a solider grabbing orders from a drill Sargent. Her I was, a grown man taking orders from a woman. I underestimated myself again. I aloud my self to be taken by the bright lights and big paychecks and forgot about the true calling. The real reason I came to America in search of a new challenge. I came for the competition I came for the fight I came to honor my country "You made a mistake. You are to be perfect. Another flaw of this "wrestling" you have been taught to do. Another slap in the face to Thailand." She turned to face me. I was waiting for her hand to cross my face but instead she looked at me and smiled. Was there actually something beautiful behind this women, or was this all for show. I looked at her hand, believing she was just playing with my emotions or waiting for me to take the offensive. "Have we learned are lesson?" A bowed to her, waiting for her hand to cross my face. Instead came a swift kick to my arm, driving the pain up my shoulder and into my head. I looked deep into her eyes and silently asked why. "You let your guard down. Five hundred more." I lowered my self to the ground. 1...For Thailand 2...For respect 3...For Honor -=-=-=-=-=-=-
