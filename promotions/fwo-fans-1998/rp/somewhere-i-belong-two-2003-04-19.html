Somewhere I Belong - Two

19 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Asylum was......quite a place. As opposed to the rest of the world, who watched football or the show she would have been on, fWo Sunday Slaughter, Amy Campbell spent her Sunday at a tA pay-per-view actually fighting in a tournament of sorts and had it not been for interference, she would've won. But, as with everything else, something got in the way and she left that night with a barrage of bruises, bumps and a pint less blood than she'd started the evening with. How the hell they did that sort of thing each week, Amy didn't know. Then again, she'd fought a number of bouts in one night, she probably would've been alright with just one...but several had ended up being hell, a hell she willingly put herself through. Hey, at least now a few of Martyr's botched knees to the stomach wouldn't exactly hurt, not in comparison to what she felt the days after her first and hopefully last appearance at The Asylum. She hadn't even had time to think, though. The second she left the arena, it was straight onto a late-night flight through to Nova Scotia, Canada...back home to meet Brandon at the airport, who was waiting - probably not anxiously - to meet her family, friends and see the hellhole she sometimes called an appartment. As expected, the meeting with her parents was civil, but not exactly warm on her dad's part. She'd warned Brandon of that one, though, so it hadn't seemed to pose a problem...then again, they hadn't really spoken much since getting 'home' - Amy'd gone right to bed for her first near-full night's sleep in a week. Brandon rolled over on the couch. It wasn't the most comfortable of nights but that was more to do with everything swirling around in his head than it did his comfort level. The last thing on his mind, as well as the first this morning had to do with Amy Campbell. Without as much as a 'guess what I'm doing?' she had went off and gotten herself involved with those cutthroat bastards in the Asylum. Seeing the bruises and the scrapes as she got ready for bed last night really sent Brandon's thoughts reeling. If he had felt over protective before it was nothing to what he felt then. It took all he had not to turn in to 'big brother' and scold her for her decision. After all, she was just a month removed from being shot and now here she was letting half of The Asylum beat on her only to, as usual, prove something; This time it was the fact that she wasn't just a wrestler. The first thing on Amy's mind was the same thing that crossed it almost every other morning: A splitting pain in her head. Of course, she wasn't about to express that to Brandon, who walked in with his usual worried frown. She tried to smile when she saw him, making a first attempt to roll out of bed...but quickly leaned back, trying not to show how stiff and sore she was. Another cover-up came in an attempt to chuckle, "Wow...I know this place bites, but it's not that bad. You look like you just slept in a funeral home!" "The way you were treated on Sunday, I'm surprised you're not ready for one..." The biting remark was more from his illfated attempt at sleep. 'Waking up on the wrong side of the bed' was the usual catchphrase, when it was actually waking up on the wrong side of the couch it only made it worse. The raised eyebrow and squinted eyes alerted Brandon to the fight he was about to pick. Knowing what was to come he did what any male in the situation would do... he made it worse. "You should have thought this through. I mean, why couldn't this all have waited another month or so? I don't care, well... I DO care, but I would be less stressed about this whole Asylum thing if you would have waited." "It's over now anyway. They crammed it into that one night because of 'booking issues'." Amy groaned and rolled over, away from Brandon. "I walked out...sorta. I walked eventually at least. Another few nights of sleep would have me perfect again, but nooo...we get to hop on a plane for another umpteen hour flight to fucking AFRICA...yay!" The sarcasm was thick in her tone, but Brandon couldn't see the redhead's eyes shut tightly as she winced at the various aches every time she moved. Having been in the business for a few years now there was no hiding the unmistakable movement of an athlete in serious pain. Brandon's frown softened as he simply looked at his... friend. Amy was a strong one, that much was obvious but her inner strength was one of the things that attracted him to her in the first place. He decided he didn't have to be right all the time. Sometimes it was simply about being there for the ones you love when they needed you. Love? With the thought on his mind and the word rattling around in his head Brandon slipped onto the bed finally pulling his t-shirt on. Taking advantage of Amy's position, he laid his hands on her stiff shoulders and slowly began to massage. "The flight will give you a chance to sleep. I know the last one didn't go so well but we'll dope you up this time and you'll sleep like a baby." "Aww...the first guy to ever dope me up so I'll sleep." She grinned a bit, "I really need whatever I can get...spending yesterday with you and my parents on no sleep was pure hell. Dad hates you, by the way...but don't take it personally, he always tries to convince me there's a conspiracy to hurt me. Let's see..." Amy stopped a minute to 'think', as sarcastic as before...only this time not directly toward Brandon. "From what I recall, you're just looking for something young and fresh since your marriage 'got boring'." "Damn, you mean... I'm that transparent?" Rolling his eyes even though Amy couldn't see he knew that criticism was to be expected. If Sean or Jarod brought home a 'pro wrestler' as a 'significant other' he would probably be just as critical. Add on to that Amy's confessed bad luck with relationships and you have a father just waiting for the next guy to be as bad as the last. That was an okay thing though because Brandon was nothing if not someone who liked challenges. If indeed her father didn't like him then that was even more of a reason, as if he needed more, to try and make her happy. Making her happy though was something Brandon hadn't yet figured out how to do. Fact is, he didn't know if he was even on the right track. All he could do was listen to the things that didn't work for her and try to do better. "Maybe we can hook your father up with Ana for a day. Then he can see how 'not boring' things were when she was around." Realizing he really didn't want to go down the 'Ana' path again Brandon quickly changed his course of reasoning... " OR we can simply make sure that you're happy and that I'm happy and don't worry what other people think. "And for the record..." Bending over to emphasize the meaning of his words Brandon comes close to Amy's ear. "... I'm happy." "You wouldn't believe it," Amy rolled over to face him finally, biting her lip with the pressure on a barrage of bruises. "But I've never been happier...so we're even. Let him bitch and moan, it makes him feel like he's done me a service and that's fine, I guess. And forget about him and Ana..." She balled a fist unintentionally, but Brandon sure seemed to notice. "Give me a few minutes alone with her...might teach her not to fuck other guys next time she marries one." Touching on the harsh situation had been a slip and quite the risky one, but Amy wasn't about to take back her words. "Well that's all in the past now sweety. It's cool in a way that it makes you angry, kinda shows you care..." Brandon highlighted the observation with a wink. "... but I've let it go. It was six years ago and we tried our best to get past it together. We couldn't, but I can... now. It's not like there was other secrets and things getting in the way. We just didn't work." With that said Brandon cupped her face in his hands and looked longingly in her eyes and Amy......just grinned. "Y'know what I want right now?" She smirked mischieviously, winking. "I don't know..." Brandon kept staring into one green eye, another blue...they almost represented Amy's constantly contrasting moods. But that was just another thing to love about her. "...what?" "Pancakes!!!" The little redhead suddenly jolted up, disregarding how sore she was and cracked Brandon on the chest. Horribly confused, he had both eyebrows raised at the sudden outburst. "Pan..cakes..........?" Amy smiled, her rather random 'plan' had worked, now to hook him in. "Pancakes, you say? Well, if you insist on buying me breakfast..." The Paragon smiled in return. She might've broken the moment, but Amy Campbell was unavoidably and almost constantly acting adorable in the few moments she'd get into a good mood so he wasn't about to argue. Instead, he got up himself and grabbed her hands, pulling her up with him. "Just tell me where to go." Somehow, tiny moments could make even the sleepiest, most worrisome people suddenly happy....it was beyond both Amy and Brandon to question why or how, but something about those tiny moments and the people involved were just meant to be.
