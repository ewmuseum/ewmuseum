You know me, I hate everyone

12 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization You know me, I hate everyone February 12, 2000 ((The scene goes to inside what looks to be just an everyday living room inside a house. On a couch, is Eron the Relentless, wearing his usual black fleece sweater, black cargo pants, and the fWo Internet Title on his shoulder. He is sprawled on the couch, with the remote control in hand, watching something on television. Coming from the projection television, is distictly Mike Bears voice saying "I have no idea why scrubs like Eron and Courage have titles" as Eron rolls his eyes at this. He then looks over as he sees the camera, tilts his head, then puts down the channel changer)) Eron The Relentless:I must be getting popular fast these days, because now it looks as if Im the target of every single boring ass interview. Figures, shows how jealous guys like Mike Bear and that returning,scronny ass former surfer Mitch Wilson are. First of all, Mike Bear, I'm getting pretty sick of how all you can do either pull of an awful B-Movie quality skit, where all you do is act like you're receiting how you acted when you fell down on you're Hockey helmey at school, acting like you put thought into all these lines you,Fear and Chrissy are saying, while you show off your sister, and girlfriend Brittany at the same time. Either I have to stand those skits while waiting for a good commercial to pop up, or you're sitting down, "shooting" again. And really, thats all you can do, is shoot. Because of course, you have the acting ability of Barry Horrowitz. So really Mike, just keep putting drills into your skull like you did in Toronto, or just flutter around the Tag Team Division again. (Eron smiles, as he looks over at the fWo Internet Title, and grins while seeing his face in the reflection, before turning again) Eron The Relentless:Now, "Whine and Bitch" Wilson. You come back to the fWo and your target is me? Really, I think that is pretty humourous. Because the last time someone did that, his name was Oracle, and now all he does is runs around his own ass in the International Division, after I bashed in his skull in a Ladder Match. Now, what also I think is pretty funny, is you run some snazzy Wrestling Gym, which because its a Gym it has weight lifting equipment, but I think you dont use it, because Calista Flockhart has bigger arms than you. You come back and think you can just run your mouth and call me, the most enlightened superstar in the fWo a "idiotic, stupid, workrate-challenged, babbling, rambling moron"? I dont see how I could be stupid when im the ONLY person in the fWo who knows its not the New Millenium just yet. Workrate Challenge? Weird, Im the only guy who has ever put Oracle over in a matchup. Babbling Rambling Moron? Well, least I dont come back and run my mouth for 3 hours about how "Im back and ready to take on anyone who dares challenge my superpowered surfboard!" So really Dean Ma...Mitch Wilson, take your technical wrestling "superiority" and climb the fWo Ladder, and if you're lucky, you may get a shot from me. Hell, I might wanna put you out of your misery and make you go in the #1 Contenders match if I really feel like it. But right now, Im just gonna sit at home until the fWo calls me for some deeds, maybe goto Sunday Slaughter or something to give the fans something to boo, since no-one else here can hold a Heel image. Oh well, Mitch,Mike, i said it once, and I'll say it again, even though its use has been hurt by every fan repeating it on Wednesday, take a breath...OF FRESH DEATH! ((Eron then takes a drink off the table, and takes a sip, as he points to the door, basically telling the cameraman to leave, as the camera fades to black))
