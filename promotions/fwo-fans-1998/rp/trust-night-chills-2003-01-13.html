Trust (Night Chills)

13 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I found myself lying awake in my hotel room, the thin sheen of cold sweat upon my brow caused from a source other than the relative chill of the hotel room. Growing up in Pittsburgh, I'd been through my fair share of rough winters, and this particular night in Canada wasn't so bad, perhaps even balmy for this time of year. Nevertheless, I rolled out of bed, shivering as the chill air of the hotel room met my naked torso, and stumbled through the dark to the thermostat, which I dialed up a few degrees to fight off the physical chill. As I laid back down in bed, I realized that the anxiety that had forced me awake couldn't be so easily combated, but as was often the case while I was on the road, I had no idea exactly what it was that had startled me out of my sleep. Resigned to my fate, I settled back in beneath the worn comforter and attempted to get back to sleep. Slaughter was less than 24 hours away, and with it came my first meaningful tag team match in my fWo career. It was not without some difficulty that sleep finally wrapped its tendrils around me once more, pulling me back down into the familiar grasp of unconsciousness. *** I found myself staring at the clock on the bedside
