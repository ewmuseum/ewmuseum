Open Season

10 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sometimes going home isn't as relaxing or pleasant as it may seem at first glance... Sometimes it's too stressful, you get people nagging at you...your family doesn't give you a moment's peace. Other times, it's happy...you get together with your friends and celebrate. Celebrate what? Well, just celebrating for the hell of it...and that's what Amy did when she returned from Warfare on Thursday night. Of course, the fun was supposed to "help" her, but jet lag and too much fun never, ever mix well... Unfortunately, she was only just now finding that out. When Aimz finally came to reality, or what she assumed was reality, her head was in a vice, her throat had three inch screws prodding through it and her stomach had shifted to her chest. It appeared as if she was finally on her way to Heaven, Hell or wherever she was destined for... But alas, the bright tunnel of light she'd been trying to drift towards was just a beam of mid-day sun streaming in through the crack in her curtains past half-opened eyelids, and those seemingly torturous physical ailments were nothing but the after-effects of overproof Bacardi. Bah, She thought as one hand pawed for her t-shirt, which she was undoubtedly missing. No hangover can conquer me! Of course, when her shirt found her midrift again, her feet found the floor... ...and that dastardly little hangover proved her wrong by shoving all hundred and fifty pounds of redhead back onto the bed. Headaches to that degree were always a dizzying bitch...but what proved to be even worse was the conclusion she next came to, right after her eyes adjusted to the light. Whoa..........this isn't my room. and the next thought was strong enough to voice: "...aaagh...isn't even my appartment!" "No, it's not." The same old, recognizable female voice came from the wide-open doorway and soon she came within sight...it was Renee, as expected. The tall blonde had a cocky smile on her face, "Man, were you an idiot last night...take these, you might not feel so dead." Amy noticed that her best friend was carrying a glass of water in one hand, and the other was tightly closed but soon her fingers unfolded and an assortment of pills, large and small, were placed inches in front of the redhead's face. She knew Aimz all too well...knew that her body may have been used to handling alcohol, but it wouldn't have been able to safely take in the amount she'd consumed the night before, especially while she was still experiencing lag from the sudden upstart of travelling for fWo. "C'mon...Tylenol, Centrum and Ibeprofen...hopefully you won't OD and might end up feeling better." Reluctantly, Amy plucked each pill, one by one, from Renee's hand and popped them onto her tongue, swallowing with a few sips of water. "What the hell was I thinking?" "You were happy...you've got a match next week." "I know that..." She growled, "I just don't know why I let myself get smashed like that..." The events of the night were blurry, but slowly coming back to her...though a lot of people, socializing, catching up and then the sense that she'd made an ass of herself were all that really seemed to be clear. Renee started with the scorn of a mother. Though she wasn't, sometimes it felt that way around Amy. "Because you're not that bright when alcohol's in front of ya...you start and don't really have any motivation to stop, I guess. You're just lucky I have a spare room..." More groaning. "Alright, alright...don't whine, I won't nag." It wasn't the nagging that necessarily bothered Amy, it was the fact that everybody said the same thing. Each and every person had something to say whenever she drank, and they all pointed out her weaknesses. When alcohol passed her lips, it was open season on her flaws... "Ungh...." She moaned, clawing her way back to the edge of the bed, though her body was a little reluctant. It seemed content to just lay there and recover. "Take me home." "Now what would Darcy think about that?" 'Take me home' had left Aimz open for all the jokes her sick-minded friend could think of, so she had to make herself a little more concise. "I'm serious." Renee countered her once again, though. "So am I...he wasn't happy to hear you were drinking." "Nobody is...you all just want to nag me." With that, Amy grumbled something in audible and stood up, still shakey as she stepped away, leaving Renee to shout after her... "Nobody would nag if you'd grow the hell up!" This is the wake of a torn girl... She screams for attention, recognition and respect, but earns none. She cries for love once lost and loves hearts once broken. Her eyes are red from childish 'adult' games with hallucenogenic outcomes, her body is bruised from a strenuous weekly grind. She wants for security, wishes for will but gets only tribulation...only finds herself in the same old condition. This is her savior... He's felt the same love once lost, he has the same heart once crushed. His mind and arms are left wide-open to his torn girl. He leaves her freedom intact, but tries to steady her path and steer her to the right track. His body is battered from the grind he loved and lost. This is her life... A weekly battle keeps things on the thin tight-rope she walks. Other, minute-by-minute battles only prove to try shoving her off of that string but she prevails, only ever slipping, but never falling because falling off would be a sure, aching death...one that friends already just barely managed to keep her from. Life isn't easy, by no means...but there's always a hope, because there's always a will buried somewhere, deep down inside. Rekindle that will and she might grow up, the demons all might stop calling her prey. Amy Campbell could 'act' mature, but the responsibilities of life, just the responsibility to live a healthy one...they always knocked her down for the count. It was time to try turning things around...again. Time to try and banish those demons...again. Maybe it was just time to take some initiative.
