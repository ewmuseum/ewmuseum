Further passages in time

6 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Interesting thought today: if I resent authority so much why is it that I am quick to accept jobs and hobbies that place me in ultimately a subservient role? Consider this: wrestling is by and large a male dominated sport. While I have enjoyed working in some women's-only leagues, my tendency has always been to gravitate toward promotions with co-ed wrestling, which in real terms mean that men wrestle and the women come out every now and then in tight little outfits to skimp around and prance for the cameras. Whatever. I've been having a dream that I'm a butterfly. It's summer and I can feel the blades of grass under my wings. But I fly toward a table set up in the middle of a field and I suddenly find myself pinned by a needle to a board. Part of a display. I guess there is something people could read into getting stabbed by a long pointy thing. I've been meaning to go into the issue of my sexuality, but haven't really had the heart. I'm a firm believer in that it's my business. You won't catch me at the Kyoto-prefecture rallies this year. As far as I'm concerned only my therapist, my sister, my lover and my doctor know that I'm a lesbian and I try to keep it that way. Too many of these women in wrestling who are lesbians -and I promise you there are a lot more than you would expect- have to start flirting with other women in the business. That's dangerous on every level. I'm wrestling in front of actual people for the first time on Sunday. Well, okay. I've been waiting for it I guess, and now it finally gets to happen. There are some decent women in the fWo, and it looks like they are going to be pushing me as an exhibitionist slut. Well, that's fine. There are layers in wrestling and there are layers. Just because you see part of the story doesn't mean you're on the inside. What I will do today: enjoy the weather and look for trees that still have leaves on them. Colorado is cold, and I will feel the crisp air against my face and remember winters past. This is still a waste of time. Goodbye, myself.
