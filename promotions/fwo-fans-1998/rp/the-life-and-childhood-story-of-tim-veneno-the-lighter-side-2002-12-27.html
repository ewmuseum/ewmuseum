The life and childhood Story of Tim Veneno: The Lighter Side Death

27 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was later that night as Ben's friend decided to give Ben a call, hoping everything between them had cooled down. Little did he know, his problems were just starting. "Hello?" "Yes is Ben home?" "You obviously haven't heard the news yet have you Eric?" He didn't know what they were talking about, but it didn't hurt to find out. "Um no, I haven't" "Ben is dead" His friend's mouth dropped to the floor, as he couldn't believe that his best friend was dead. "HOW???? Who did it??? Argh" So much pain went through his body, as did so many different emotions. "He committed suicide. Now if you will...." With that said both ends hung up their phones, he couldn't believe it. His best friend committed suicide. "What a friend I am" The next day at school was a rough one for Eric, as everyone knew that him and Ben got in a fight the previous day over Kristen. Kristen went up to Eric and yelled at him. "ITS ALL YOUR FAULT!!! IF IT WEREN'T FOR YOU BEN WOULD STILL BE HERE! I HATE YOU! I HATE YOU!" Kristen slapped Eric and ran off crying as everyone was around Eric; Eric didn't know what to do. He broke down in tears in front of everyone. It was a very long day for the whole school. Tim walked up to Eric to show a little support. Tim touched Eric's shoulder with his hand, as Eric didn't know how to react. "Now you know how I feel, when everyone is laughing in your face and you don't know what to say, or how to react back. Welcome to my world" Eric shoved Tim's hand off his shoulder and turned around and looked Tim in the face. "Listen up you fucking freak. One, don't you ever EVER touch me again, two, you don't know how I feel right now, you never will." "That's right, because I don't have any friends, that's right because no one will talk to me at school, that's right because I walk around the school knowing that when I pass a group of people they all chant FREAK at me! THAT'S FUCKING RIGHT! You don't think I have feelings too? You don't think I feel pain also?" Eric started crying even more as he feel into the arms of Tim. Tim had finally made his first friend.
