Song from the Heavens, Humming from Hell - 5 - Part One

5 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Get on the ground! Get on your fucking knees!" Dalton held the 9mm directly at Douglas. Douglas then proceeded to move a few feet forward and dropped to his knees. "Angel you couldn't do it even if your dead whore of a mother rose from the grave and physically forced you pull the trigger." Steve Douglas now on his knees with Angel Dalton standing in front of his holding a 9millimeter to his face. Angel shook his head as sweat poured down his face, it was hot, it was Miami, "You have no idea how much I am doing it Steve." Angel spoke in a course tone, his seriousness was in his eyes. Steve smiled, "Do it you self-righteous fuck. Kill me." Angel jammed the barrel into Steve's mouth and cocked the trigger. Steve Douglas' eyes widened and Angel looked hard and deep into Steve's eyes and spoke, "I am."
