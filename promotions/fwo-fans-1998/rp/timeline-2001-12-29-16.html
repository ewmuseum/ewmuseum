12/29/2001 12:00PM "You son of a bitch!" The Strangler tried his best not to yell into the cell phone, but was quite angry at the moment. And, given the information that Darryl had just passed to him... he had every reason to be. He wanted no part of this. None whatsoever. "Easy, Paul. Take it easy. There's no use getting all bent out of shape. I was just looking out for your best interests." "Our best interests? You beat a 21 year old kid into the pavement..." The Strangler whispered harshly. "If you ask us, you're looking out for us a little too eagerly." "Well..." the man on the other end of the line began. "I wouldn't say I beat him into the pavement... I just pointed out a few areas of etiquette that he needed brushing up on." "So what was with coming to his hospital room this morning to threaten him?" the Strangler shot back. "You're lucky he didn't go to the police..." "Oh, trust me Paul. The last thing that kid wants to do is be anywhere near a policeman. He made up that description to give to them...because he doesn't want any trouble. He's like a scared deer. Very easy to manipulate. I figure you'd be thanking me right about now..." "Thanking you?" the Strangler asked, rolling his eyes. "And why should we be thanking you?" "Because, Paul... for the first time in your career, someone is actually looking out for you. Someone is going to make sure that you don't fall back into a slump like you've been in. We're a team now, you and me. For over three years, you've been fighting your battles alone. You've rarely been outclassed, or outwrestled, but you've constantly been outnumbered. The FtfWo outnumbered you... the French Foundation outnumbered you... 3vil outnumbered you when your title was on the line... I'm here to try to even up the odds a little bit, and make sure any...distractions are taken care of. Especially tonight." "Tonight? We don't have a match tonight." The Strangler replied. It was true. Chances were he wouldn't have a match on this Countdown series, unless it was a dark match. He supposed it was only a matter of time before he was doing curtain jerker duties at a PPV... "I wouldn't be surprised to see that change by show time" the voice replied smugly. "Chris Universal has this terrible knack for last-minute booking... so I'm sure you'll see action of some sort. ...and I'll be there to make sure things go right."
