Retribution.

30 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::Shock. Frustration. Confusion. Chaos. These are the things which now run through the fWo like wildfire. The newer arrivals to the roster find it difficult to comprehend what has happened in the past 24 hours, while those who have been around since the beginning bear witness to a resurrection of the darkest time in the promotion. The Riders reign supreme once more. As the suspicions begin to mount amongst the members of the roster, there is talk of leaving the promotion behind, there are bold proclamations of defying the Dark Riders and their new leader Travis, and there is talk of annihilation of his forces. But amongst the voices both large and small, there is one man who walks silently, yet with great purpose, contemplating his next move. He passes through the door of the locker room into the sea of bickering wrestlers, and a slow wave of hushing silence falls over the room, as the occupants notice that The Boston Strangler has arrived, his duffel bag slung over his shoulder. The throng of people parts like the Red Sea as he steps up to his locker, and begins to calmly unpack his belongings for this next stop on the tour- Countdown to Oblivion, Day 2. Of the group that shuffles uncomfortably around him, only Matt Loco has the courage, or utter lack of desire for self-preservation, to approach him. The entire room stops, waiting to hear what comes next.:: Loco: Strangler... you've seen what happened last night.. you've seen the Dark Riders.. and , my God, now Travis.. LEADING them. You were here before, when it happened with Taylor and Spencer... you were part of the group that defeated them... what now? What do we do? ::The Strangler stops unpacking and turns in annoyance towards Loco. As he goes to berate the reporter for asking such questions, he notices the group huddled before him. Wrestlers of various ages and talent levels, all who night in and night out give their very last ounce of energy to the promotion. His annoyance quickly fades, and is quickly replaced by a burning intensity which is usually saved for his opponent in the ring. These men, these women, they would listen to what he has to say. And so, he would tell them. Brushing Loco aside, the Strangler turns to the crowd to speak.:: Strangler: Most of you weren't here the last time this happened. It was the worst of times in the fWo. Friends and allies turned on each other, fueled by greed and lust for power. This was a time when a few brave souls were needed, those who would strike back at the Dark Riders for the good of the promotion. It was the future of every wrestler in this federation that was at stake. The best the fWo had to offer stepped up, and got the job done. It was not only a test of ability, but a test of heart and sheer determination. As our small group gained momentum, others would join in. It took a concentrated effort, but together, we were victorious. But this time... it is different. This time it is the establishment which we fought so hard to protect which seeks to eliminate us. The man to whom we swore an allegiance of honor has betrayed us, to David Spencer. ::The Strangler stops for a moment holding one of his wrestling boots in his hand and shakes his head, mouthing the words "Spencer" repeatedly. Suddenly, he roars in an almost primal rage, swinging the boot against the locker violently several times. As quickly as it started, it stops, and he turns quietly to the group before him again, dropping the boot to the floor.:: Strangler: David Spencer. The man who almost single-handedly brought your company to its quivering knees, now sits at your right hand. Beaven... you know how much we despise that poor excuse for a man. And you proclaim him YOUR champion. The king of all cowards shall carry forth your proud banner? So be it. David Spencer has been no champion. We know it, everyone in this promotion knows it. And deep down inside your black little heart, Beaven, you know it. Champions are of a different breed than the scum which you associate yourself with now. Champions are men like the Black Panther, who fight day in and day out never complaining about their losses or bragging of their wins. Champions are men like Havok, Slugger, Universal and even Primetime, who bring energy, fire, and adrenaline to every move they make, giving 100% win, lose, or draw. Champions are men and women like VL, Hellrazor, Mike Bear, Wretch.. those who care more about winning than how they're going to feel the next morning when the pain sets in. Champions are people like Machina, Hiroshima, Ultra Violet, Briana, Rudo... who stay loyal to your company no matter how you mistreat them, and brush their concerns aside. Champions are men like Lance Star, who struggle daily to function again, not so they can enjoy retirement, but so they can return to the very sport which practically crippled them. And why? Because it is the life they choose, and one they do not want to change. The drive behind these competitors... the life they give so freely to make you your money... the children at home being raised with a parent on the road... the hospital bills... the glory.. the broken bones and spilled blood... the sacrifices large and small.. this is what you try to take away, and flush down the toilet, but you shall not succeed. Not while we still breathe. For too long since we returned, Beaven... for too long we've bit our tongue, held in our outrage.. and followed your lead. You knew someday it would come to this, didn't you? You tried to push us aside... you feared what could happen if we got a hold of your precious title. You nearly choked when you realized that we were owed a title shot. But now it comes time. That title shall come to be around our waist, or the waist of another who knows the meaning of the word champion. The belt shall no longer represent your company, it shall represent those of us who fight to lay claim to being the best the sport has to offer. Maybe not today, maybe not tomorrow.. but soon enough.. you will be stopped. We will fight back, with every ounce of strength in our body, breath in our lungs, and blood in our veins. And rest assured... It starts tonight. Those of you who would see justice rather than tyranny.. put aside your petty differences. Those of you who would bring honor rather than shame to this federation, join your fellow wrestlers... band together... strike back. With everything you have, you must strike back. Tonight, we charge into a war with the Dark Riders. Those who would stand beside us, are welcome. Those who would not, stay clear of our path. The time has come for vengeance. The time is now. ::The Strangler allows his gaze to pass over the crowd, letting his words sink in. Geoff Green is standing with his mouth open holding a microphone while the camera captures everything. Slowly, some of the wrestlers in the room begin to nod at each other. The message is clear: The Dark Riders WILL be stopped. The fight for the future of the fWo shall begin.
