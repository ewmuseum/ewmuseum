Get in my head

14 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The ball thumped against the side of the wall. THUMP. "Neo." He caught it, squeezed it and thought of the time he tricked that stupid bitch Urara into thinking he was Mr. Nice Guy. Fucking bitch, she never saw it coming. Of course, the best part was the cream on the chocolates. God that was rich. He loved his job. He loved who he was. He tossed the ball hard, hitting a painting of a landscape that hung on the hotel wall. THUMP. "Plague." The ball missed his grasp and he had to reach for it. Just like him really, back when he played bitch to Scotty Collins. Stupid candy man Scotty Collins and that mean Stone woman. Always on his back, always telling him what to do. It was like he didn't have a life of his own. Why wouldn't they just leave him alone? He hated Scotty, but stupid Scotty was always one step ahead of him. It was like his tounge was tied. He hated him. Hated him. Hated him. Underhand the ball him the wall and left a small black mark. THUMP. "Neo." God, what a fucking cunt that whore was, did he actually think she was hot? Some stupid Asian chick who has herself all made up like a little China Doll whore. She was lucky she was in the fWo, all dressed up she wouldn't last three minutes on her back in Thailand. That pussy boyfriend of hers... God, he should have punked those two Pearl Harbor boys out like the cocksuckers that they were. He fucking hated them. Hated them all. He was violence in this goddam promotion wasn't he? Fucking right he was. Fucking right. The ball slammed into the wall. THUMP. "Plague." Collins was bad, Spencer was worse. God, Spencer was so bad... in the darkness... he was scared out of his wits of Spencer. Stevie said he would protect him. He said, but he never did. No, Spencer just bent them all over, did what he wanted. The business was rough, too, too rough. What happened to Stevie's little promises? His little words? Oh but he promised, promised it wouldn't happen this way... THUMP. "Neo." Like the time that motherfucker Rottdog went after him. Broke his goddam arm he did... fucked him up, broke him. Steel, Rottdog, what were all those dumbasses called again? Fucking NXS... fucking NXS... he should have broken their fucking heads in, made them squish and bleed and die... THUMP. "Plague." All these people telling him what to do, he was just a cruiserweight, just a cruiserweight and why did they pick on him? Why did they go after... THUMP. "Neo." He slapped them down, he always did. Fuck you fWo, fuck you everyone because I did it my way and always my way. Fucking assholes get in his... THUMP. "Plague." Oh but why was he even here? He liked it gone, liked it retired, liked it far away where the people didn't look and stare and... THUMP. "I'm losing my mind. Fuck if I'm not losing my fucking mind." THUMP. THUMP. THUMP. THUMP.
