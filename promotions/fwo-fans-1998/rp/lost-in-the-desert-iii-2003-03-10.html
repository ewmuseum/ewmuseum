Lost in the Desert - III

10 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The hot desert sun had disappeared. Night had fallen. By now, this weeks edition of fWo Sunday Slaughter was long past, and there was a very good chance that Scotty J. Bowman and his cousin "Big" Bobby were in very serious trouble. They had been captured by a murderous band of nomadic thieves who resided in abandoned caves in this area of the desert. The group of nomads had built a fire outside of a collection of caves and gathered around their newly captured friends, sitting and listening intently to Scotty J. Bowman who had somehow found a guitar and proceeded to serenade the vagabonds. "When you ain't got nothin' You got nothin' to lose. You're invisible now You've got no secrets to conceeeeaaaallllll..... How does it feel? Oh! How does it feel? To be on your own? With no direction home? Like a complete unknown? Like a rooolling stoooooonneeee..........." As Scotty J's out-of-tune crooning stopped "Big" Bobby went off on a brilliant harmonica solo that drew a rousing round of applause from the vagrants. Scotty J dropped his guitar to the side. "Oh, did you guys like that one? Yeah, I wrote that. Yep." "Didn't take very long either." The nomads were impressed and gave another round of applause to Scotty. "Well boys," Bowman said, "It's been a real fun time, really, I'm not just saying that... but I'm afraid my cohort and I really must be going now." "NO!" the pillagers screamed. "YOU CAN NOT LEAVE. YOU STAY HERE NOW." Scotty J loosened the collar on his shirt and swallowed a "gulp" of nervousness. "Now guys," he said, "You know we can't stay here forever." "YES," one of the larger nomads snapped. "You stay here. Forever." Scotty J was concerned. "You mean, we can't leave... ever?" "NO! Those who try to leave are killed! You stay here. Forever. You are our property now. You sing, you dance, you no leave!" "Oh," Scotty replied, "You guys want to learn a dance?" At first overwhelmed with suspicion the thieves slowly warmed to the idea. "Come on, it's the biggest thing in America right now, it's called the uh... the 'Scottarena', yeah, the BIGGEST dance hit in the world. Bobby, show them how it goes!" Scotty began to play the "Macarena" song and "Big" Bobby did his best to improvise a ludicrous dance, that involved ducking to the ground and covering his head for several seconds. "Yeah! Yeah! Great! Now come on, you guys do it!" Scotty played the song again, and true to form, the nomads danced the ludicrous dance, taking an extended amount of time to duck and wiggle around on the sand. "Fabulous! You're getting the hang of it! Come on Abdullah, take over on the axe! I want to get in on this..." Scotty handed the guitar off to one of the nomads and joined in on the dance, pushing the group farther towards the caves, while he began to slowly inch away. "Come one now, one more time with feeling!" As the thieves began to shake what their respective mothers gave them, Scotty and "Big" Bobby bolted over the hills and out of the nomad's hands. The two ran for several minutes before taking a moment off to catch their breaths. Scotty J hunched over and clutched his chest, huffing and puffing. In the distance the music still played and the shouts of the nomads could be heard. "Bobby," Scotty said with confusion... "The luggage!" "Big" Bobby ceased his panting, looked back to the camp, then to his cousin, then back to the camp once more. Finally, he lowered his head and began walking back toward the caves to retrieve the lost luggage cart.
