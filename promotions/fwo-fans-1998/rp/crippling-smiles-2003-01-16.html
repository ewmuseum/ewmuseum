Crippling Smiles

16 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Saddledome in Calgary, Alberta Just after Warfare While she'd been intently watching Warfare wrap-up from a locker room monitor, a mother's son had run off to find his favorite superstars... And to Amy's misfortune, Stephen had found what he was looking for. When she finally tracked the little boy down, the redhead felt a pair of steel blue eyes were watching her just as nervously as she was watching their owner when her son ran up to shout "Mommy! I found him!!!!!! I found Brand Frontier!!!!!!!!" and nearly got ignored for his efforts because poor 'mommy' was too preoccupied. Fate or accident, the meeting obviously wasn't intentional on either part. Brandon stayed silent, sparing Stephen a quick smile to release the nervous tension when he saw the boy's surrogate mother. To her own surprise, Amy spoke first, only giving Stephen a quick word before her train of thought wandered elsewhere. "You found him, alright." She never noticed how silent her 'son' got, or the fact that he did so after sensing the lump building in her throat. Nonetheless, the redhead looked at Brandon. "Well uhh..guess you found him for me...thanks, he usually doesn't wander like that but he was looking for you, I guess..." "Uh... um... Heya Amy, so Stephen here is a...friend of yours?" Brandon was at a loss of words. He and Amy had spoken only in passing since their kiss. It seemed as if they hadn't had a chance to catch their breaths since then. Brandon's brother-in-law lay in a hospital with the staff saying a recovery was unlikely. His wife had taken their children overseas in an attempt to protect them from a very real menace. He was on the verge of losing the woman he thought he would spend the rest of his life with. How could he be expected to sort out the mess they had made of things on New Year's Eve? But he still thought of Amy. Every day. Now seeing her he realized that he had not even begun to put the issues to rest. Little did he know, she was thinking all the same things. The urge to reach out and hug him was almost taking over, and it seemed to physically hurt just to be near him...but there again was the recollection that he did in fact have a wife and even though they were on the rocks, Amy had absolutely no right to possess Brandon's thoughts. That's why she'd avoided him...both had realized on New Year's that things were going too far, feelings were getting too deep. But now she stood there, lost for words. The best the little redhead could do was mumble, "Yeah....son, remember?" Praying that Brandon would recall the story she'd once told him without bringing up any question of genetics around Stephen. Instead of trusting her instinct, she continued to avoid being too awkward, though nervousness shook her voice. "...so yeah, I heard about stuff..." "I figured it'd make it through the rat-vine. Me to Lance, Lance to Sean, Sean to you. Sorry I didn't call, but you know..." "...yeah," She found herself fidgeting from nervous habit. "I know." "But about this little wrestler here, of course I remember about Stephen... that should go without saying." Brandon rubbed back the little boys hair in the wrong direction making Stephen duck out of the way laughing. He had found his favorite Superstar and this trip with his Mom was going splendidly - no boy his age wouldn't be smiling. "A whole mess of people are going to remember him, I'm pretty sure he's stalked nearly every fWo employee so far..." Amy laughed a bit, she'd noticed herself getting a little shakey and tried to mask it. "Seeing him makes me miss Sean and Jarod even more..." As Brandon said this he realized that the events of the last day Amy couldn't possibly be privy to, he hadn't had a chance to speak to Lance or Ivy or any of the rest. Everything had been so hectic with him coming to the show late. "See... Ana took the kids to Ireland to be with my Mom for a while. With everything that's happened she needed a kind of... vacation I guess you could say. I...don't really know if she's going to want to come back from it either..." "Why would she leave you?!" This wasn't supposed to happen. Brandon was supposed to live happily with Ana, they were supposed to work through their problems so Amy's hopes would eventually dim. "I can understand taking the kids away from the danger of a fucking psycho stalker for a while...but leaving you? I could kill he---err---sorry, probably not the most appropriate thing to say..." Stephen fidgeted a bit at his Mom's outburst. Instinctively he looked to his 'hero' to see how he reacted. Brandon too was a bit taken aback by Amy's statement but having the opportunity to hone those father instinct on the front lines for the last eight years he could tell Stephen's nervousness. "...stop teasing Amy." Brandon caught her eye and patted her little one on the head again reminding her quietly of the innocent ears that were their audience. "She's just saying that we have a lot of things to...work out. There's some issues that we haven't put to rest...and...well.. I dunno... we might have pushed 'gainst the forces of reason for as long as we could. I just don't know..." With a shrug of his massive shoulders the fWo's Paragon tried to brush off the uneasiness between them. With Ana now seemingly a world away Brandon found it harder to distance himself from his feelings for this fiery redhead. He had tried to convince himself that that night was a one time thing, an aberation. He didn't know that at that same moment, Amy was trying to tell herself the same thing and studiosly practicing a straight face, though it was hard not to smile just being around him again. But then the thought hit her, what if she was one of those 'issues' they hadn't put to rest? At the concept, the redhead groaned audibly. "Look...this doesn't have anything to do with...y'know..." When she'd started, Brandon glanced to Stephen as a reminder and Amy quickly saved her statement, remembering the curious little boy. "...us hanging out on New Year's?" "Hanging out... heh... sounds like high school all over again." Brandon couldn't help but grin, and with the grin came a wink. "And no, sweety...nothing to do with that wonderful night. She... well, she was upset of course but ya'know that's expected right? The stuff that's going on with her and I, well it just runs much deeper than I guess either one of us wanted to admit. Stuff with Creid. Stuff with things that happened with the kiddos. Just... stuff." Realizing how silly it all sounded as he tried to soften his words for Stephen's sake Brandon smiled again. Twice in one, more than the three days previous combined. He couldn't help but feel good when Amy was close to him. It was just natural. Won...der...ful? Maybe he'd just been sidestepping awkward conversation...maybe not. Still, there was another statement that made her ears perk further. "Stuff....'stuff' with Creid?" "Uhm... yeah." He mentally kicked himself for letting that slip. Not many people knew about the things that led to the animosity between Victor Creid and Brandon Roche. It was well catalogued as far as their on camera rivalry was concerned but things were not always as they seem in this world they lived in. The real hatred Brandon felt for Vic sprung from events between them nearly six years ago, events that also involved his then new wife Ana. "I think that's going to have to be a story for another day." If Stephen had been a little older and a more orderly train of thought he might have realized he has been looked at a lot lately. Instead he had taken up time messing with the straps on the side of Brandons' left boot. "We'll get the gang together soon and I'll tell you all about it, maybe over one of the poker games or something..." "I dont gamble...but if you'll be there, I could make an exception and learn." Amy had giggled, only to find Brandon trying to do the same as she'd done. His attempt to lighten the conversation was a little unsuccessful, though; He'd tried too hard and ended up grinning "Yeah, then hopefully it'll be another one of those strip poker nights..." Her smile was wide at first, but then Stephen's inquisitive eyes found hers. She'd barely silenced the initial "Mommy, what's stri---" with a nervous motion to Brandon. "Yeah...heh...definitely. But anyway, we've gotta get back...Sean wanted to talk to me, you probably know he's going through a rough spot..." "Yeah... well dear, give me a call when you've got some time...'kay?" Brandon placed a strong hand an Amy's shoulder and gave her a pat. Resisting the urge to pull her to him, he smiled down at Stephen. "It was nice meeting you Stephen, you keep an eye out for your Mom ok? She's a real good friend of mine. Make sure you root for the good guys when you're watching the shows..." "I will!" "Alrighty, we've gotta go head out now...can't be late!" She finished for her son with a swift reply, knowing full-well she'd be too afraid to call - whether it was fear of confronting Brandon or fear of confronting his wife, she wasn't sure. Brandon waved them off and let Amy notice him watching her intently, trying not to smile too much. She felt awful looking back on her statements, telling Brandon they had to leave so she could help get Sean through a problem as if the Paragon had none of his own. Why did she have to avoid him like that? Why the hell did things have to be so tough? There were too many questions to be asked, and no answers ever came...
