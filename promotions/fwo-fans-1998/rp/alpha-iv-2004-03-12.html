Alpha IV

12 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kate Young and Jack Harmen could never keep a stable relationship. Kate Young was known for going from guy to guy during their early high school days. It seemed that just when Kate had made a decision about where she wanted her life to go, she hated the first step and immediately wanted out. This garnered her the reputation of a prude slut, one of those girls who goes out with a lot of guys, but none of them seem to get anywhere. Jack, on the other hand, couldn't get a date if the life of his hamster was on the line. Which was odd, because Jack was a fairly good baseball player and had a few runs with the high school wrestling team. But no matter the sports he tried to play, he would always be the bumbling idiot that Kate met that first day in Seventh grade. Closing her locker shut, the muffled voices around them chattered the five minutes in between class away. Kate had no one to share those few moments with. No one, until Jack appeared behind her and wrapped his hand around her eyes. "Guess who?!" He said, forgetting that he had written his name on the palms of his hands in the bordum of Math class. "My stalker?" She asked, spinning out of Jack's grasp. She smiled toward Jack, and said, "Oh, it's just you." She slouched her shoulders in defeat, and tilted herself toward a locker. "You have a stalker?" Jack was confused, but shrugged it off as he went to his locker and began fiddling with the combination. "And you're disappointed it was me and not your stalker. Not your life threatening, probably insane, stalker." Jack gave up on opening the locker. It probably wasn't his anyway. "I don't have a stalker." Kate said, looking over her shoulder as if she were paranoid. "I think." She brushed off her tight t-shirt, which had a bit of dirt on it from the locker she had just leaned on. "Then take off that life threatening part. And the stalker part. And put my name in there... I guess. We're all a bit insane, but not stalker insane last I checked." Jack smiled, and Kate returned the favor. They deserted their lockers and began a slow walk down the halls of the school, as the rest of the kids around them dissapaited into their classrooms. There was a bit of distance, shown by the fact that a schoolboy actually walked between the pair. "So, what happened to Billy McSchoolboy over there?" Harmen said, noting the man who had parted them just before. He turned around and continued walking, this time backwards, as he watched "Billy McSchoolboy" walk away. Kate let out a small sigh, and just shrugged her shoulders. "Oh, I dunno," Kate said, head tilted and eyebrows raised. "I just didn't hear the click." "The click?" Jack asked, utterly confused. He twisted back around, and barely missed clocking the side of a janitorial cart. Weaving out of it's way, he wound up a bit closer to Kate than before. Kate battered her eyelashes in response. "Yeah, haven't you heard of the click? You know, when everything comes together, and bam. Sense." She met her hands together in Jack's face, ever so slightly. Jack just shrugged, and then threw his arms over his head to stretch. "The only click I've heard is the barrel of a gun called tenth grade history quiz. History quiz, part two... this time... it's out for blood." Jack made a few motions to go with the words, as if they were flying across the screen in a commercial of some sorts. Kate chuckled as the bell rang, and the rest of the students herded into their classrooms. All that was left in the halls were Jack and Kate still close as they were moments before. Jack let out a weak smile and a slight laugh, as Kate turned away just at that moment to miss it. Jack lowered his head a bit, and rubbed his eyes. Just as Kate's hand reached the knob to the door, Jack walked over and placed his hand overtop hers, every so lightly. "You know what you were talking about... with the click?" Jack asked, taking her hand gently off the door. There, he left her hand in his. "Well, I've had more clicks than a remote control." He let out a half smile, and tilted her chin up to look him in the eye with his free hand.. "And they're all around you." Kate tried to say something, but nothing came out. She was stunned, to say the least, jaw down slightly and eye brows definitly raised. Jack took this as a cue to continue. "You know, I've been playing this out in my mind over and over, and it's all Hollywood, swooning over a row boat in the middle of a lake, banjo in my hands, because in my day dreams, I can play the banjo. And you fall in love with me just as I hit the high F, because in my dreams, I can hit a High F, and I also know what a High F is." Jack let out a weak smile, once more. "But I know that'll never happen, at least not like that. And I guess I always wanted to hold onto that, because it was perfect. But then I realized that it doesn't matter how it happens, whether it's in the middle of these halls or on the middle of Lake Erie, I still know you're perfect, and that's all that matters." Kate blushed, and lowered her eyebrows into one of those adorable gazes. "I know I'm not a good man, I'm not a strong man, but I will be. Because you helped me, all those years ago when I was at my worst. And I know we're friends and you'll always be there for when I'm at my worst, but if I can have you in my life as something more, as... the women on the lake... I know I'll always be strong. I'll always be good. And I won't ever have another bad day." Cue : Kate Young swooning. "There. I got it off my chest." Jack said, taking a deep gaze into Kate's eyes. But since he was an idiot at times, he didn't realize what Kate was really looking at. "And I know I probably wasted your time and ruined your perfect school tardy record for nothing, but it had to be said. But now that I have, I sort of want to take it back and have that still in my chest, because at least then I can always have hope." Jack let out another weak smile, not sure what was next to happen. "I just hope..." And that's when Kate leaned in, and gave Jack a sweet kiss on the lips. Young love can never die. It can only grow old, and then die.
