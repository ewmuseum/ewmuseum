In the Garden Of Good and Evil / The Morning (End)

12 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"He's been screaming for hours." "I know, I can fucking hear him in my office." "So what'd we do? Notify Universal that he's fucking nuts?" "I really don't think that will be a surprise to him." "So what then? Just keep him in there till he shuts up?" "No no...I'm fucking tired of listening to it. Send some boys in to talk him down." In two minutes six men would head into the small room in which held Steve Douglas. They would stay in there for forty-five minutes. And for every minute they added a new scar to his body. *** The phone rings, and it is answered. "Willis speaking." "Willis, its Universal." "Ah Mr. Universal how are you? How's the whether over there?" "Whether is great, just need to know how Steve is? I need to get him back in the ring, or at least replace him." "I'd suggest you replace him Chris, he has made no progress in fact I just finished wiping the spit off my face that came from his mouth." A pause. "I'm sorry to hear that Willis." "Not as much as I am Chris." Click. *** What's wrong? Wrong? Nothing's wrong. Well obviously something's wrong, I know you Steve remember? I know you because I am you. Your not me...your not me at all. WHO are you fucking kidding here Steve? We already went through this before?!? I am you. I own you. You owe me everything Steve because you took everything from me. I owe you nothing Kenny! NOTHING! NOTHING! NOTHING! NOTHING! DIE KENNY! JUST FUCKING DIE! Outside stood two men guarding the door; one turned to the other, "Oh he's fucking screaming again."
