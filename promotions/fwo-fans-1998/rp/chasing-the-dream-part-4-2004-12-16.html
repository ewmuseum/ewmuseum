Chasing The Dream - Part 4

16 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The train moved onward at its rhythmus pace for several hours, and Milo continued his slumber. His dreams began to take him to a place, which was far, far, away. They took him to a place where there is no need to hide.... No need to pretend or bare any affectations... A place where you can speak your mind express your every thought with no inhibitions. Milo was one with the stars, and just observing the sheer beauty of his surroundings coated his skin a warm tingling sensation. For Milo, this place was as close to heaven as anything could be. And although he could not stay, he promised himself he would see this again. May 6th 1999 - 5:12 AM "Who the fuck is he?" Milo awoke, startled by the voice of a stranger. He opened his eyes, only to see four men standing in front of him. Their clothes were very rugged, and their pants were frayed making it look as if they hadn't changed or washed them in some time. "Who are you and what the hell are you doing on our train?" "Your train?" Although looks can be deceiving, Milo was more then certain none of the four men owned this freight train. "Yeah, this is our territory." "Train hoppers?" "We prefer the label 'Travelers' actually." The man replied, cracking a smile. "What takes you to Phoenix anyway?" "Phoenix?" Milo said in a surprised tone. "There must be some sort of mistake, I'm going to Oregon." All four of the men began to laugh, before one began to speak once again. "Looks like you traveled ten hours in the wrong direction guy." Milo was fuming with anger and disappointment. Not only had he backtracked ten hours, but he was also no further north then when he'd begun his travels several days ago. Trying his best to stay optimistic, Milo hoped the train would be going to Oregon after its stop in Phoenix. "So this train doesn't go to Oregon at any time?" "Fraid' not pal." "How about any trains in Phoenix that go to Canada?" "Well, if you want get as far north as you can from one train, the "Union 12" will be going by the phoenix station at about nine thirty. If you hop on that train, it should take you all the way to Salt-Lake City." The sound of a screaming metal was heard, as the train eventually came to a jarring halt. The men grabbed their duffel bags from the floor and hopped out of the boxcar. "See you around." "Peace, and thanks for your help guys." Milo took his time in exiting the boxcar. "Hey! Get that dirty ass train hopper." A man yelled, hopping from the train engine, running towards Milo, his intent to lash him with a bar, which he held firmly in his right hand. The thought of any wrongdoing had slipped Milo's mind for a moment, and he'd taken too long to get away from the train unnoticed. "Now is not the time to play the tough guy" Milo thought, as he turned his back and ran.
