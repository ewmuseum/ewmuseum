Too good to be true.

30 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sometimes life throws a curveball that in your wildest dreams you would have never suspected. It's not always bad stuff either; sometimes the good can come out of left field as well. Those are the times we all live for; those are the times most of us never believe. When it happens we pretty much do nothing more than stand there as it happens, awe struck for lack of a better word as this great event, this great spectacle fades away. Then we spend the rest of our lives regretting we ever missed it. Brandon Roche had promised himself he wouldn't be one of those people. He knew when he had found something good and was never one to sit idly by as the happening happened, without him. He was also did not often find himself bemoaning the missing of some life altering event, if anything he was categorized as self assure and reliant on his convictions. If anyone could feel the butterflies in his stomach now they wouldn't be so sure. The talk with Eli had worked miracles on the clouded, addled contraption he called a mind. Flair had grown to be one of his closest friends and staunchest allies and much like Brandon had made his fair share of mistakes in the business as well as his personal life. Speaking to him wiped away much of the doubt that had hindered Brandon in the past few months. After much debate, Brandon had made a decision. With Amy looking out over the ocean from the balcony, he breathed heavily. The night had gone perfectly. The dinner was exquisite, the requisite walk on the beach relaxing. They had always done everything low-key since they got together; it had almost become their mantra. They lived such public lives that when the chance came to be normal they relished it. Tonight though they would change, no matter the outcome of the evening things would never be the same. Amy had been broken since Meltdown. Her win over Kinkade had been soured by his cowardly attacks after the fact, not to mention her sudden rage against Donaven Winters who, among other things had permanently scarred his precious omega symbol over her hip, effectively souring a scar that remained a reminder of why she was with Brandon Roche - She'd been willing to get shot without hesitation for the man...it was only luck that the bullet wound had been over her hip, instead of adding another to her heart. Despite everything, though, Brandon had been making the week - this day especially - as comfortable and relaxing as possible for her. That meant, in turn, she wasn't bringing any 'work'-related problems up and not even as a polite gesture, more a helpful healing process for herself. Soon, though, the smaller of the redheads realized that neither had spoken for a solid ten minutes. Lost in thought or admiration of the scenery or not, she wasn't going to risk silence being interpreted the wrong way and appropriately made a comment, more or less grasping at straws for conversation... "I wish the company'd travel near the coast more often...I love the water. Doesn't even need to be near beaches or anything, the ocean just makes me feel...I dunno, comfortable. Odd as it sounds," She sighed, "I kinda miss it when I'm away." "I know what you mean sweety, growing up in the mountains I never realized what I was missing until we moved Mom back to Ireland, back to the bay. It's amazing there." Brandon touched her shoulder and she leaned back against him. "We'll have to go. Soon. Tell Fox that he needs to get along without the Raver and the Paragon long enough for a little vacation." Smiling to himself Brandon couldn't help but try and slip something by the unknowing Amy. "Maybe we'll spend a week or two there, celebrating or something." "The last time we went, I remember I had maybe two days there...it was beautiful, so next time I'll take a camera." Amy smiled, "But we'd need to give Fox a pretty decent excuse..." A different ending came first to that statement, but then her memory suddenly latched onto something he'd said. "I mean, really - What would we possibly have to celebrate? I don't remember the last time someone was given time off just for being happy." A pleasant sigh came as Amy smiled again solemnly. "Not even Eli and Angel get vacations. Makes me feel kinda bad, too - Those two are married and we're standing around talking about vacationing 'Just because'." Brandon reached around with his other arm, bringing Amy even tighter against his body. Smelling the fruit aroma from the hotel supplied shampoo he closed his eyes, readying himself. "Well, I guess." A lump suddenly appeared in the mighty hero's throat, ". I guess we'll have to make sure then to have a good reason. And, I have an idea, why don't we celebrate the engagement there?" As Brandon exhaled those words he gently released Amy's shoulders. The world around them had grown silent; they were the last two people on the planet. Nothing else mattered but this moment. She turned, facing Brandon, her mouth agape. A tear hovered on the edge of Brandon's eye as he reached in his pocket. The maroon felt box opened quietly as he turned it around to show the contents to his beloved. The prize within sparkled even under the dim moonlight. Glinting and glimmering, it shone with the same intensity as the fire in the heart of Brandon Roche, the fire that burned for Amy Campbell. "Amy." Their eyes met, "Will you do me the honor of becoming my bride?" And everything stopped. Literally. For about twenty or so seconds Amy couldn't breathe, speak, think or close her gaping jaw. A sudden warm chill and goosebumps prevailed over her pale, inked skin as every fibre of her being screamed "Yes!". Every one, of course, but her mouth...which remained dry and deathly silent. This is what she wanted, she just never spoke about it for fear of the thought being dismissed as silly or childish, as most of her 'dreams' were. But now without hinting or discussion, the question was before her and, despise overwhelming happiness and tears forming, from the very pit of her stomach to the hair on her head she felt terror. What was wrong with this picture? After a few seconds, it dawned on Amy... She was happy, that normally didn't occur. She was getting what she wanted out of life...that simply didn't happen. This was entirely unusual, nothing short of miraculous and finally, her throat allowed words and air to pass through again: "I...I'll..." She started, trying to get used to the sound of her own voice above the shock clawing at her brain. "I'll need time to think..." Instantly, with one look in Brandon's eyes she watched him shatter. In his mind, he was crying the exact same thing hers was - WHY hadn't she said the word her heart was screaming?! It was terror again, her instincts took over in thinking 'Too good to be true' and cut what she really felt off at the pass without so much as a second to think. It was her defense mechanism and normally did an excellent job of concealling her true feelings when they needed to be...but this time, they didn't need or want to be silenced. Amy started again, still unblinking. "What the hell am I saying?!" A smile began to form on her open lips, and Brandon's eyes brightened once again as the look in hers became sweeter than he'd ever seen. "I...well...yes. Yes, absolutely." Utter elation. Sheer joy. Pure happiness. Nothing that passed through Brandon Roche's mind could describe the feelings coursing through his heart, through his mind, touching his soul. With one simple sentence, one set of words Amy Campbell had made him the happiest man on earth. Nothing could stand in their way...
