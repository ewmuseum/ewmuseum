Mojo Risin': Epilogue

19 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mojo Risin' Epilogue: An American Prayer As Eli Flair opened the door to his Bronx apartment, he immediately reached to his left and hit the lights. He'd expected a mess of boxes piled near the entranceway, as the kids had spent most of the weekend moving his things from the truck to the gym, then, when they turned up wrecked and delayed, from the gym to the apartment. And look - things were still mostly packed but everything was out of the way and piled where it should go. Kitchen things in the kitchen. Main room things in the main room. He could've guessed but baby things went in the baby's room and he and Angel's personal things went in the main bedroom. "Knox and Cally," he said to himself - they were undoubtedly behind the gesture, "remind me to get y'all something nice 'n expensive." It was at that moment that Eli noticed the faint beep buried somewhere near his entertainment center. He shook his head with a smile. The kids had even thought enough to hook up the answering machine. From just inside the door he could see the blinking '1' on the display. **BEEEEEEEP** 'Hey, sweetie, it's Angel. I got your message, gods, what happened? Are you and Ivy okay? I mean, I know you're okay but are you okay? Sigh, I'm babbling. Anyways, please call me when you get this message, I don't care what time it might be where or when. I love you, and your little angel misses her daddy. Bye.' Eli smiled at his wife's voice. When he called her from Williams, he had gotten her voicemail. A quick bit of crude math told the tale - she was most likely onstage at the time. Now, Eli calculated the time in Berlin, compared to the time in New York. She wouldn't be onstage, but she'd almost certainly be asleep. No matter. The tour bus rumbled through the early morning air as Angel's phone beeped softly. The pale singer reached under her pillow and pulled it into view. As she saw who was calling, her mood went from exhausted to excited. "Hi!" she whispered happily, "You're home?" "Yeah," replied Eli as he sat down on the thankfully - clear bed, "Just got in. Freakin' finally, just got in." "How did everything go?" Eli laughed as he unlaced and kicked off his boots. "What part?" "Everything," laughed Angel as well, remembering what he'd been through, "What exactly happened? Your message didn't say much." "Long and the short of it, the tracker is dead." "Good, I hated that car," sighed Angel, "always felt like we were about t'tip over." "Well, we did tip over," advised Eli, "We were both banged up but I was fine - have had worse at work. Ivy, of course, busted her head on the ground--" --he stopped as Angel gasped-- "--she's concussed and was bleeding from the head for most of the time, but we got to a town, we got sidetracked in Chicago, and ended up taking a detour to Manchester, New Hampshire." "Interesting choices for a... detour," said Angel, "What did'ja do there?" "Hung out with Randalls, wrestled Rook Black for the Squared Circle," continued Eli, fully aware that she was not entirely following, "and re-learned what it meant to have fun in this business." Eli lay down on the bed, and stretched out his sore muscles. "There was a slight scene in Flagstaff involving Ivy, the steel in her knee, and a security guard who started to cry. But that's besides the point." Angel took a deep breath before her next question. "How are... you guys doing?" "Are we still fighting?" he asked, "Not exactly." The singer arched an eyebrow, and Eli was positive he could see it. "Y'ain't gonna get off that easy," she cautioned, "What happened?" "What happened," repeated Eli again, "is that we did something we should've done a long time ago. We talked shit out and found our balance." "Good," said Angel, "I wasn't sure exactly how I'd be able to deal with the two'a you at each others' throats when we get back. Goddess, we're gonna be back in two months!" "How's the tour going?" asked Eli. "We're kicking ass," replied Angel, "I can't wait for you to hear the songs we've been writing - and most of the countries we've gone to, we've gotten a sales agreement together to sell both Scarred and Stitches. And when we show Mr. Merritt the figures from the tour I know he's gonna put his influence behind this third album." Eli smiled. Chad Merritt, CSWA owner and CS Enterprises co- owner had signed Valerian's Garden to a record deal and then treated them like they were part of his professional wrestling empire. 'I won't interfere in your day to day business, go out and do your thing.' It was frustrating to know that your record company was not fighting your battles with you, but it became much less so when the band has as many stubborn members as Garden - with their queen manager, Ivy McGinnis. "He likes you guys," replied Eli finally, "and he likes making money. Those two things'll bring 'em around." "How's everyone else?" asked Angel, "How's Randy Knox and that adorable girlfriend of his?" "Everyone's good," assured Eli, "listen, I think we need to talk about something." She took a deep breath. "It's about Craig Miles, isn't it?" Silence and surprise. "How did you know?" "I keep up with your place of business," said Angel through smirking lips, "it's a lot easier for me to do for you since you're on TV. And Ivy told me." "When did you talk to Ivy?" "About Miles? Every other day since your last big event. That girl can talk, talk, talk." "And she said about Miles...?" "That you guys were both likely to be in for a long, difficult autumn," said Angel, "with a ferocity in your attack toward an old friend, tempered only slightly by the fact that this is something you simply have to do for the sake of your respective souls." Hesitation. "Ivy said that?" "No," confessed Angel, "Ivy said Miles was whacked and someone had to kick his ass. But I am a poet." "It could get rough, Angel," cautioned Eli, "I don't think Miles has it in him to be vindictive and vengeful toward anyone who's just as committed to their - our - cause as he is. And I can guarantee he wouldn't go after you like Winters, or after MJ like Ender. But all the same... this is a war that is gonna get messy." She was silent. Angel had met Craig Miles just once
