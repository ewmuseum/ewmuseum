The Magical Adventures of Steve-O - Part One, or 'CONVENIENCE STORES ARE CONVENIENT FOR A REASON!'

5 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pacing up and down the aisles of the Quick'N'Go, Steve-O threw a number of items into the basket he was holding, most of which were vaginal cleaning products that he discovered worked quite well as a make-shift hair conditioner. He trudged over to the soft serve machine, popped a plastic cup from its dispenser, and filled it with a combination of flavors. Then, when he was sure the clerk at the front was busy threatening a group of teenagers that he suspected might attempt to shop lift, threw in a collection of snack foods and buried them beneath the icy mixture. Very incognito, he thought to himself, moving towards the counter and shoving the frightened adolescence out of his way. They tumbled to the floor, and scurried from the exit, stolen pornography tucked in their jackets. Steve-O placed the cup of soft-serve on the counter. He waited for the clerk to ring up his purchase, yet it seemed to take him quite a lengthy bit of time. The clerk blinked and pushed the cup away with his conductor's wand. "We don't serve your kind here." "Uh, kay." Steve-O happily shrugged and left the store. He chased down the kids and was all, "YO, GIMME THE FUCKING PORN OR I'LL BUST YOUR SKULLS". Though, because he's stoned, it came out in mumbles and resulted in a kick to the groin. No porn for Steve-O. All right, fair enough. Can deal with that. But what else is there to do in the small town of... Miami, Florida? Cars whizzed by; muggers danced off from darkened alleys with purses in their grasp; a game show was being filmed no more than a hundred feet from where he stand... Yes, the small town of Miami, Florida. Steve-O staggered into on-coming traffic, but was all, "YO, CARS... FUCK OFF..." But, of course, he's stoned. Thus it became: "Mmmmmmmmmm mmmmmmuh uuuuuuuuuh." Drool collected at the bump in his chin, he wiped it away with his sleeve and whistled as best he could for a cab. He climbed in through an open window, working the door handle was too mentally exhausting to attempt at three o'clock in the morning. "To, like... the place I sleep at," he said with his face pressed against the glass, a haze of red and yellow streetlights shone through the window. He smiled and waved to a crowd of people that stood at a nearby bus stop. He felt the taxicab pick up speed. Turning to the front, he greeted the driver. "DRIVVVVVAAAAAAAAH!" His head fell and hit the passenger's seat in front of him with a weak thud. "Ow," Steve-O laughed, rubbing his head, "er... I hurted my thinkin-shell." The driver dare not take his eyes off the road, it was beginning to rain and due to a lacking budget the small taxi business he was employed with were yet to purchase new wind shield wipers. Water cascaded down the glass, blinding him. It probably did not help that he was poor driver to begin with, and that he happened to be clutching his eighth beer with one hand, while gripping the wheel loosely with his other. Drunk? Meh. He continued to drive blindly, pulling onto the freeway. The taxicab swerved in and out of lanes, narrowly missing cars as it sped down the long stretch of pavement. Steve-O tumbled from one corner of the back seat to the other, repeatedly smacking his head on different objects, most notably a troll that sat next to him for no apparent reason. Though faced with possible death, he laughed so hard tears flooded from the corner of his eyes. "DUDE, you are SOOOOOOOO getting a tip for this wild ride!" The driver vomited in his own lap, glanced over his shoulder, and gave him the 'THUMBS UP'. The car slid off into a ditch, the driver's foot still pressing on the gas pedal, causing the automobile to become slightly airborne as it came off of the incline on the far side of the ditch, sending it into the forest. "Shiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiii-" Glass sprayed into the driver's face, cutting him badly. Steve-O was on the floor, huddled safely in the fetal position, knees tucked to his chest. He looked up cautiously and found that the trunk of a large maple tree had forced its way through the middle of the taxicab and to the back seat. A sheet of torn bark stripped from the tree and collapsed onto his face. "Ow." After throwing it out the window, he collected his wits (or whatever he has in place of wits, if anything at all), and exited the cab. He looked to the sky. "Dude, heh... it's raining. Rain sucks." Now... where was he exactly? He spotted a taxicab that looked to have just been in a horrible accident (Yes, the one he JUST climbed out of) and scratched at his scalp. He had forgotten everything up to about thirty seconds ago. His eyes grew wide. "Woah, a CAR ACCIDENT! Sweet deal!" He stripped the cab of its hubcaps, grabbed the troll from the back seat, and pocketed the unconscious driver's wallet. With these items he was fairly certain he would be capable of finding his way back home. Except... which way was home? He turned, facing the road. Right, or left? Only a choice between A and B? That didn't seem right. So he choose the most unlikely route... And scampered off into the forest.
