Trying to figure out exactly which way Collins sucks

11 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Trying to figure out exactly which way Collins sucks January 11, 2000 The scene opened on a small staging room at a local college. It was an off-day and the drama club had been given the afternoon off from their rehearsal of "Damn Yankees". Props still littered the stage, but they had been pushed to the side to make room for the audition. A small sign was posted on an easel that read: Scotty Collins Insult Auditions Today The line wasn't magnificently long, but it was of an ample length and stretched around the corner of the building and down the hallway toward the rectory. The people in it carried props and stood shuffling their feet, many yawning and looking tired for their wait. They were lined up to get onto the stage, and seated in director-style chairs before the stage were Machina, Rudo, Brandi and Sid. Okay, next! Machina waved the next person onto the stage and looked to the side of him at Sid. Sid's chair appeared to have been covered with several rainbow and butterfly stickers. Machina shook his head in dismay. Um, Mr. Machina, sir... my name is Stephen Anderson, and I'm here to present "A mocking of Scotty's groin". The man waved to the side of the stage and a large statue was wheeled in. It was painted to vaguely resemble Scott Collins. The man addressed it and began to speak with it. Why, hello Scott, how are you? The man turned to Machina and Rudo. As you can see, the model of Scott Collins has had the groin removed. Machina and Rudo looked at the statue and nodded politely. Machina turned to Rudo. What do you think? Rudo considered and looked at the groin-less statue again. I'm not sure it says "screw you" in the right way, Machina. Besides, Scott's a little dense... plus you've seen how bitchy Stone is. This statue might not be that far off. Machina nodded knowingly. Next! A man in a leather jacket walked onto the stage holding a pile of cards. Okay, like, okay. This here is what youse say to that Scotty Baloney. AHEM. Hey Scotty, youse stink okay? Youse are the slinkiest stinker that ever stinked. You got me or is youse ears filled with the wax like substances? Well, whatda think huh? Machina looked at Rudo hopefully. I don't know Machina, his humor is a little too... well, I was hoping for some insults that didn't suck OR blow. Machina rolled his eyes. God, you're picky aren't you? Fine, NEXT! A student in a suit walked onto the stage with a large pad of paper. He flipped over the cover to reveal a near-perfect picture of Scott Collins. Here, is Scott Collins. Machina and Rudo clapped politely. The student pulled out a large black marker and hitched an eyebrow. Ah, but... look at... THIS! The student quickly drew a clumsy mustache on Scott Collins. HA! Now look... he looks stupid! Machina considered. You know, I think he looks better actually. More distinguished somehow. Rudo nodded I agree... he looks more... more... Sid leapt from his chair. Sexy! I mean... excuse me... I've got to go to the bathroom to... er... Sid ran off as Machina and Rudo watched him go. Rudo shrugged. NEXT! Adam Sandler walked onto the stage with a guitar. Machina and Rudo clapped politely. Sandler began to sing. Hey... Scot-ty Cool... you are so... ug-uly. I... think you... are stupid and dumb... You... are going to die... poor and lame... I'm... going to urinate on... your grave... Rudo thought for a moment. Well, that's not bad, but you lost me with the killing and urinating. I don't think anyone wants to hear that. So next time, more stupid, less grave urinating, okay? Now scat, you. NEXT! A young, stunningly attractive woman walked onto the stage and gave a small curtsy. Hello kind sirs. I've recently come to this country in search of someone to spend my fortune on. Of course, I ask something of return, and that is a night of sweaty passion with no strings attached. Machina rubbed his chin. Well, that's nice miss... but what does that have to do with the Collins problem? Who let this woman onto stage? NEXT! The young woman started to protest as she was escorted off the stage and a fat man lumbed up to the microphone. I begin tonight's insulting with an ode to Sir Isaac Newton... Machina and Rudo groaned as the afternoon continued to mindlessly lumber on. Luckily for all of us, this interview won't.
