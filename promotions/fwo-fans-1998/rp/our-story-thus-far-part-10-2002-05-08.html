Our Story Thus Far (Part 10)

8 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mike Bear waits for Jasmin to leave the diner, and finishes his bottle of beer, before pulling out his cell phone. First he makes a quick call to his wonderful wife, filling her in on the events of the day, and how everything went with Jasmin. After disconnecting, he dials up another number on his cell, waits for it to connect, and says, "It's done." Five minutes later, Mike Bear is no longer alone. "It went well?" "If half of what you told me is true, you're the biggest dick I've ever met. And I was in Asylum for a while." "All true, actually, every last word. But it went well?" "Better than I expected, anyway. But she seemed, not quite pissed, but almost put off by all of it. It is kind of disturbing." "She left t'ough? She's stopped looking for me?" Bear nods, and calls for another Heineken. "Zat's all I needed. Great. T'anks for doing zis for me. I couldn't...I couldn't be ze one to tell her. I couldn't look her in ze face and tell her zese t'ings." "Not a problem. You'd do the same for me, if I ever stooped to your level. So what's next?" "Get my act toget'er. I'm trying to kick ze cigarettes, get back into working shape, and get ze past behind me. I need a fresh start. I need to walk back into an FWO locker room and take ze time to learn who else is in zere wit' me. I swear, I still t'ink of Ultra-Cool and ze Strangler when I t'ink about ze FWO. It's grown and changed around me, and I haven't. I need to address zat." "Yeah, that would be a good idea. Listen, I'd love to stay and chat, but Brit's been waiting for me all day. I really want to see my wife. You're going to be okay, right?" Frenchie nods. The two friends shake hands, and Bear walks out of the diner. Frenchie pulls out his own cell phone. "'Allo, may I speak to Chris Universal, please? Tell him it's Pierre Delacroix, and zat I'd like to be added to ze house show schedules..."
