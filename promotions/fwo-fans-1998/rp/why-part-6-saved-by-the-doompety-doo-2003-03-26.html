Why? Part 6: Saved by the... doompety doo.

26 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"What are you looking so happy about?" Borst asked, seeing his lovely bride Yalenchka bound into the locker room. She just smiled and sat on Borst's lap, putting her arms around him. She was back after a short absence while she was in a strop with him. No clue where'd she'd been to, but regardless, she hadn't taken Borst's comments about her father the other week too well... "You never know, old Borislav might have made it to the arena and been crushed like Brand Frontier. So slap a smile on your face for a bit!" ... but she was back now. Huuuuuuge smile slapped across her face. Borst had a huuuuuuge frown slapped across his. "Get off! What the fuck is wrong with you, woman?" Yalenchka smiled, and stared into Borst's eyes. "I forgive you darling." She purred, giving him a peck on the cheek. Borst quickly shoved her away and stood up, looking confused. "Forgive me for what?" he asked. "The nasty things you said about my father..." Yalenchka replied. "I know you were only thinking of me, and besides, he has been found and is flying to France today!" Borst raised his arms and placed them on top of his head. "What the fuck? You hated him one minute, then you love him again, get in a strop, now you forgive me for telling you what you should think, and love him again." "No... I hate him." She said. "Now I know that he is safe and back here, I can hate him again. He had been doing business, finding new ladies to sell... so I now know he has not changed." "You're fucked up woman. But still, whatever makes you happy." Borst patted her on the head and winked. "You're still serving your purpose here, and up to a point I suppose that old man Borislav is too. He was a welcome surprise." "My purpose?" Yalenchka said, stepping towards Borst. "Do you not love me darling? What other purpose for buying would there be than love? You do not touch me, you do not speak to me, you do not love me either?" "Love?" Borst said, screwing his face up. "Yeah... yeah, sure I do. What else would I want from you?" Yalenchka smiled and began to stare at her husband, as he began to look more and more uncomfortable with the attention. Cue the Oompa Loompa tune. "Thank fuck for that." Borst said to himself, pulling out his mobile phone. "Hello?"
