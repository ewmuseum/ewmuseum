Going Home.

14 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Homewood Suites by Hilton Tuesday, March 9th 2004 The light peering in from the split in the hotel room curtain fell just so, right in the eyes of Brandon Roche, desperately trying to catch up on lost sleep from the night before. He was still sore from the events of last weeks altercation with Crusader and Kellen Kinkade. The stitches and scratch marks that covered his tattoo back were still fresh enough to cause itching. Now with the sunlight hitting him in the eyes as well it was all it took to raise the mighty Paragon out of slumber. Brandon threw back the covers, rubbing the back of his arm against his lower back. It wasn't considered 'scratching' if he didn't use his fingers, right? As he blinked away the dryness in his eyes he slowly came to remember the events that led up to this hazy morning. He and Amy had found themselves in a small local bar, both pissed to all Hell about the fWo's new management decisions. The events that led to Brand Frontier's one day suspension as well as Aimz' current disillusionment with the federation itself led them both to imbibing one too many. That (Well not just that, they are a young couple after all.) led to the late night of marital bliss and action that followed. That, probably more than anything else, attributed to Brandon's stiff joints. He heard the shower in the background. His partner in crime had already risen and was apparently washing away the events of the night before. Brandon twisted and popped a few vertebrae as he smiled, that didn't seem like too bad an idea. Padding in to the small bathroom Brandon could make out the nude form of his wife behind the thin curtain. The floor wasn't as cold as he figured it'd be, but the fog was thick, she always did like her showers hot. He stepped in to the shower, not saying a word. Comfortable in her surroundings, the littlest red head didn't even jump as he wrapped his arms around her soapy form. Instead, she leaned back against her hero warmly. Turning slightly with a wicked smile on her face, she peered up in to the bright blue and gray eyes of her lover. "I seem to have misplaced the world..." she grinned, "What day is it, again?" "Silly...it's the day I fell in love with you, all over again." Brandon smiled back. Reaching down, the two's eyes remained locked. Amy was almost afraid to blink, until finally Brandon retrieved the sponge and began his own shower rituals, winking at her as he did. Amy's smile grew wider as she watched him in near awe. She'd liked people before, but this time she was dangerously in love...the ring she kept constantly on her finger alone said that. "Are we just freaks, or is marriage always this awesome?" "After all...that..." he winked, "I'd say a little of both." Brandon's breath was suddenly taken away - not by the conversation, or even the company. The hot water had inexplicably run out. He pressed quickly next to his wife, escaping the ice cold needles as they both fondled for the water release. Finally tumbling out of the shower, both trembling, they began to laugh. He retrieved a towel for the two of them and gingerly rubbed it across the cuts and welts on his back. Nudging as close as she could, trying to nab some towel for herself, Amy sighed. "Y'know..." she began in a tone more thoughtful than before, "We really should think of another kid. It'd make...this stuff...a little less possible, but I dunno. We should adopt - it's something for both of us." Spooked by the mere mention of the word, Brandon tried to laugh it off, "Get another kid? You make it sound like we can get one from a vending machine... and who in their right mind would award an adoption to us?" "A foreign country," countered the little redhead, "We could help out. And after the loss way back...I dunno, I just think it might be neat. We make pretty good parents." She sighed, "Another one of my bright-eyed ideas, I guess." With the sigh, he realized that she was actually serious. In an attempt to ease the suddenly uneasy situation, Brandon hugged her close. "It's not that it's ridiculous, it's that it's out of the blue. You catch me off guard with all this serious stuff at times when I would never expect it." "Yeah, but isn't that how you tricked me into marrying you?" With that shot followed by a giggle, Amy quickly wrapped herself in a towel and ambled out to sit on the floor beside the messy bed. "Besides," she raised her voice slightly to keep in Brandon's earshot, but still grinned a grin he couldn't quite see, "I get the most raw 'n real reactions if I catch you off guard. Otherwise, you think too much." "Yeah... I have been accused of that time and again haven't I? I'm my father's son, what can I say." Brandon answered honestly. "Seriously though, where out of the blue did you pull this from? You really want to do this even with the limited time we've been spending with the boys?" Amy sighed, "I think it's a bad reason, actually, because I think I might want to just to prove something. Creed took away my chance to prove that I can care for another life through and through. For the first few years, Stephen was with my parents mostly...I was never really there. I wanted my chance to do right by someone. I dunno." "Do you ever just feel totally helpless in front of the world?" she finished with a poignant question. "Every time I look at you, my family. Every time." Brandon had now came out of the small bath area himself. Dropping the wet towel at the foot of the bed he slid in next to his wife. "You know me though, like you said, I have to over think everything. But with something like that I don't think I would be doing it without reason. Why don't we try and get a whole week away from the game? We can take a little trip, I'll really show you Ireland this time if you want. Anywhere you want, we'll go. We can look at the pros and cons then, do some research, talk to the kids about things." The larger of the two redheads put a heavy arm around the other, pulling her to him and resting her head on his chest. "That'd be perfect. Exactly what I need." smiled Amy, "I just need a week away. Not just taking Ruahh or Uprising off and then having to fly to another show two days later. A full week just to remember what life feels like. We could both use it, I think. When, though?" Brandon sighed heavily, that was going to be the trick. "Let's see... what's on tap for next week? I don't think management will have much problem giving me the week off after what happened Monday night... but I think we'll have to leave it mostly up to the front office." "I'm sure nobody'll mind keeping me away from Nerva until I cool off," grumbled Brandon's tiny cohort, "So I think next week will be okay by them. If not, well...we'll both come down with a horrible flu." Amy winked, then let her eyes close. "I'm not too pleased about her just trapsing in and getting something started with me...so I could use a week away." "Then we'll talk to them tomorrow at the booking meeting. Maybe we can have a family emergency come up, honestly right after our five day sabbatical after the wedding we might be pushing it a bit." Noticing that his little angel was about to drift back off to slumber land Brandon leaned her back against the foot of the bed. In one swift motion, he cleared the bed of the used sheets. "C'mere sweety," Brandon scooped her up in his arms, throwing her towel at his feet as he did, "I'll get the comforter we didn't use, let's take a little energy nap and then get going... okay?" With Amy's sleepy grin came a sigh of relief. "Deal."
