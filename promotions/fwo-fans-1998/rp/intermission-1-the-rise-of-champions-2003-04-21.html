Intermission [1] The Rise Of Champions

21 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A stately mansion in Short Hills, New Jersey. While the decorating style may have spoken class and cash ten years ago, now it spoke ruin and poverty. This was the room that Kellen Kinkade had vowed never to refurbish, the room that had belonged to his father. Dust coated the various awards and trophies that his father had won throughout the years; pictures of him in action filled the damp and peeling walls. Across the room a window was firmly shut, curtains tightly drawn as the sun came up... the darkness remained. "Welcome to life on the other side of the coin." Kellen Kinkade began as he lit a cigarette and took a drag, giving the otherwise dark room an orange glow. "This room, this scene... they may both seem very familiar, and while I'm not a poor man... I chose this room of ruin for a reason. Scott Slugger, you and I... are very much alike. We both stumbled into this industry because we failed where others did not, your inability to swing a bat effectively, my inability to throw a match winning pass... we both had to taste the bitter dish of failure before we could indulge in the main course of success. But not everyone recovers from defeat at the same pace, and certainly not everyone chases a new dream quite as quickly... while you went on from being a mediocre baseball flop to a wrestling superstar almost instantly, it's taken me a little more time to get where I am today. Its almost as though we both walked down the same path Scott, I just got there a little later while there were more obstacles in my way. You talk about achievements, five time fWo heavyweight champion and all of the rest... you harp on about what you did here and what you did there. At Cyberslam II you main evented, and in a way I'm glad that you asked me where I was because really... your presumptions were less than accurate. Like I said Scott, same road... different times. While you were jobbing to Scotty Cool, I was knocking out teeth in order to pay for my next warm meal... you see it's hard to get a job when you've only got one eye Scott, the world is an unforgiving place. You took the easy path and I took the hard one, while you were winning cruiserweight titles I was being thrown through barbed wire in Japan. But times change Scott, a few years can make any path a little more difficult to head down... the greatest irony is, we've almost switched places." Kellen Kinkade stopped for a moment, to lift his Immortal title onto the desk before him, the gold shining under the dim lamp that he'd switched on. "I'm the champion now and you're the nobody, I'm the accomplished wrestler and you're the guy that Donaven Winters tried to hang at Countdown 1. We seem to have hit a little crossroads Scott, we've both switched lanes. Your delusional views on how you'll be viewed in ten years only further my theory, you aren't talking about what you're going to do Scotty, you're talking about what you've done. You are right though Scott... people are going to be talking about you in ten years time, but not in the way that you think they are, you'll be the Berlin Wall of the fans Wrestling organization. You were in the way. I smashed you down. They'll only speak your name when they talk about they great things that I have achieved Scott, your five world championships won't mean shit because you'll be the guy that Kellen Kinkade destroyed at Cyberslam six." Kellen Kinkade lifted up his Immortal title and got to his feet, smiling to himself. "Nothing more amusing than your little tagline Scott, because for you... ... there won't BE a next time." Kinkade flipped off the light switch and the room plunged into darkness, Cyberslam VI was just hours away.
