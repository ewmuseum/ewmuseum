Second Heartbeat: Territory

11 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lawrence unfolded the cloth napkin into his lap and eyed the lamb shanks in front of him. Bimbo had done it again and prepared a wonderful meal. He looked quickly over to Troy for a second before eyeing his guests. He could tell that the sight of the main course didn't sit well with the small redhead, her alternative meal sat squarley in front of her, untouched. "Eat up my dear. Bimbo is a wonderful chef, one of his more meager talents to boot." The elder Knight's warm smile pushed the words to Amy's ear, she had gotten lost counting the number of eating utensils scattered around the table. She smiled politely back. "I'm, uhh, not the biggest fan of...meat. Despite what Brandon might tell you." Very quickly, Amy winced. That comment couldn't have gone over well. But it was quite the opposite - she was surprised, not to mention relieved when Lawrence raised an eyebrow and cracked a smirk of his own. "Yes, well... be that as it may I'm sure your subsitute will more than make up for the sacrifice our dear departed lamb made for our hearty meal this evening." Troy looked to his father, "Stop teasing old man, can't you see she's uncomfortable as it is?" Looking back at Amy, Troy tried his best to smile, "He's an old codger, you'll have to excuse him." Amy's recent mouthful of food stopped halfway down her throat with 'Troyboy's' rude comment, as uncomfortable as it had been Troy seemed to just add to it. It didn't appear he had any real intent on helping Amy fit in, it was more a chance to strike out at his father. Brandon looked up over his meal with what Troy said, none too happy at the loss of manners he always exhibited around his own father. "Mind yourself Troy, speak to your father as you would be spoken to." The words were brief and Brandon didn't even bother making eye contact, instead he simply rammed another piece of food into his mouth, glancing to the kids end of the table as he did. Troy shot him a disdainful look, before turning his attention back to his plate. Brandon was right after all, whenever they got together like this it always followed the same path. Amy avoided the silence by glancing over to Stephen, Sean, Jarod and Bobby. She was rather amazed at the astounding display of manners by the four boys. A look back to the other side of the table wasn't quite so calming, though. "Jeez!" she piped in, breaking the uncomfortable tension, "Y'know what'd be great right now? Scotch. Nice, potent scotch! Sure could use a glass...or eight. Lawrence, err...you like classical music? Mussgorsky? Borodin? Berlioz?" Lawrence's wide smile showed that she had actually brought up something he would love to wrap his mind around for the evening. Of course, that is when things never go as planned, Troy cut in. "Of course he likes classical music, he's an old englishman. It's genetic. He also likes his tea hot and early in the afternoon in his finest silver, even when there is no occasion for it. In the late evening he can even be seen rambling the halls in a smoking jacket. He's a walking cliche." The rudeness once again took away Amy's ability to speak. Not so for young Bobby Knight, Troy's son. "Daddy, you're being rude again. Why do you always do this when Uncle Brand comes to visit?" Troy's eyes darted down to the little Knight, "Mind your manners Bobby. Don't speak to your father like that." "That is advice you too should be holding dear to your heart Troy. Can't we just eat in peace and have a normal conversation for once? Any time we get together, as a family, it turns in to something like this." Brandon chided. "That's just the thing Brand... that's JUST THE THING..." Troy raised his voice. "Every time you're around Lawrence... Father wants to play family. Do you know the last time we all sat down to dinner without guests?" Amy politely pushed her chair back and stood up, quite calm and collected. Nobody would have noticed, if it weren't for the sudden change in demeanor when she raised her arm. First, she pointed to Troy. "You. Shut the hell up. I don't care if he's," she gestured to Lawrence, "Your dad or Brandon's your sister, grow up or shut up. If you can't say anything half decent for the sake of the majority in this room, don't say anything." "And Brandon, it only provokes him if you try correcting his behavior like a parent. The only person with any real right to do that is his father." She moved on swiftly, nodding at Lawrence, "Next time Troy starts mouthing off, I'd say you've earned the right to cuff 'im upside the head." "And you," Amy suddenly boomed, pointing a finger squarely at her son but quickly smirking. She had to lighten the mood somehow. "Eat your peas!" Finally, the little redhead sat back down and let out a long, steady breath. "Return to your meals, and if I hear so much as a peep of a snarky comment, I'll sit at the table with your sons and have Bimbo prepare a children's table for the rest of you." It didn't seem like many of the people in the room got the good humour in Amy's last words. No, not at all... The look on Troy's face would have stopped traffic. Brand Frontier's trollop had just smacked him in the face in his own residence, no less. A quick look at his father told him all he needed to know; even though she had called, all three of them out her words were really meant for him. Everyone always judged him and he was tired of it, especially from someone like her. Troy shot up from his char, sending the antique crashing to the floor. In one fell swoop he scooped up the rest of his lamb in one hand and grabbed his crystal goblet with the other. "You dear lady, should not be the one to address a Knight such as that. Know your station." And with those words Troy hurled the contents of the glass straight into Amy's face, causing all at the table to stop in mid motion and stare in confusion. Troy hurled the goblet to the floor, shattering it as he turned on his heel to storm away. After blinking a few times, all eyes were on Aimz. The redhead sat silently for a second, collecting her thoughts before the awaited reaction came and she let one of her bracelets slip down around her knuckles as she stood abruptly, growling, "I'll show 'im my station..." The action was cut short, though. Before she could take a step, Brandon dove to wrap arms around her waist to restrain the insulted hellcat. Amy struggled hard against her husband at first, but quickly noticed the boys who sat, excitedly watching things unfold. She eased back against Brandon and released a long, tight-eyed sigh. "I'm okay," she said finally, "I'll make no promises for him, though."
