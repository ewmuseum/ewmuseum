A Child's Lament - Prologue

30 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A television flickered in a ramshackle house. The TV itself was quite old, probably produced in the mid eighties. The faint light gave little impression of the room; however, it did illuminate one part of it. A chair. In that chair was a gaunt man. He had scraggly blond hair, piercing blue eyes, tattoos up and down his arms and wore a "wife beater" tank top and a pair of frayed jean shorts. He had a slight growth of a beard. He looked as he was about 38. He was incredibly interested in what was on the screen; in fact, his facial expression has not changed. I forgot to mention the television was blaring a sports program. I hate when I do that. A loud thud was amplified by the crummy speakers on the sides of the television. A faint chant for one name was also heard. Carraba rocks. Carraba rocks. The man was intent in his stare of the television. He was either mystified or amused by the Joey Villani vs. Chris Carter match. Something drew him to it. That's when a sinister smirk grew on his face. "Christina! Git in here!" Within seconds, a woman stepped into the room. She was in her mid thirties, younger than the man. She had dirty blonde hair pulled back in a ponytail, green eyes and wore a halter top and a pair of cargo shorts that did not leave much for the imagination. In her youth, she could have been a quite attractive girl, but the years treated her poorly. She saw what was on television and was stunned. Her mouth was agape in awe. Before you knew it, the match was over and Joey Villani was declared the winner, but the man and "Christina" stared at the boob tube some more. The man, during a commercial, turned to Christina and had an evil grin on his face. "Christie, that's our boy there. That's our boy. We gotta get him back. He's our chance."
