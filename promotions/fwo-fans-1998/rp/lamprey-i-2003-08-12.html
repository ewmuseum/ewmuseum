Lamprey I

12 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tuesday, August 12th, 2003 - 11:32 a.m. "This is it, dude... Hawaii." Pretty Fly stood in the parking lot of one of the most magnificent beaches in Hawaii. White sand, beautiful women, and a crystal blue ocean that only the most dedicated surfers could truly appreciate. Clutched underneath their arms were their personalized surfboards, each with their name printed across the length of it. The fWo had hosted their Monday Night Ruahh~!! televised event at the Maui Memorial Stadium, and while others flew home the next day to spend time with their families, Nate and Steve-O stayed behind. "It's beautiful, Nate. Look at those waves," Steve-O admired the water, shaking his head slightly. Slowly, the excitement crept off his face, replaced with a stoic gaze that could only be followed by three simple words. "Let's do this." Marching off, their bare feet sinking into the warm sand, they moved towards the water. They quickly pealed their yellow t-shirts off and threw them to the ground, before picking up a little speed. So many wonderful waves to surf, and plenty of time to do it in. Nothing would stop them from reaching their destination. If a tornado suddenly formed and blew the clothes straight off of every women currently on the beach, neither would even glance back. Then, something terrible happened. A tall, muscular man stepped out into their bath. He was the very description of what they disliked.
