Stuff that didn't go anywhere else

6 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"What's you tryin' to do, girl? Take my job as the premier straight- commentary writer in this company?" Poison Ivy shot Aimz a knowing wink to let her know she was joking - but the column the Red Raver had stuck up on the fWo Website was much more introspective and insightful than most of the world - outside the fWo - would've given her credit for. Ivy is currently linked to the Internet through her ever- handy laptop computer, a telephone wire plugged into the back and a remote device that does... something plugged into another port. The two women have been sitting, bored, in the hotel all day. After an... incident... earlier, Amy has tried to keep a low profile, sitting on the other side of the bed in Ivy's room, strummin' on a guitar Ivy bought her Egypt. Hey, when it's girl- talk and Ivy is on the computer, Aimz should be able to chill with her 'first love' too, y'know. "Hell no...no matter how much caffeine I'll ever have, nothing could make me write that weekly. It was spur of the moment, I was severely pissed off at a whooole lot of stuff...and I kinda still was when I went out this afternoon........which..well...yeah." She smiled, biting her lip and bowing her head in the same instance. With her head down, she was mumbling from underneath a curtain of bright red. "Besides, the worst they could do is keep me in this God-forsaken place...that doesn't really help Brandon any, though; I'm pretty sure he thinks I'll be dragged away and beaten in my sleep." "Ahh, you don't need caffeine," replied Ivy, "You just need a short temper and a tendancy to rant. I've noticed you do both on occasion... like today?" Ivy stretched out, cracking her knuckles as she typed something into the screen. "Want to know what the dude from WrestlingChaos.com thinks of you?" Amy grinned. With Ivy perched halfway across the room and facing her, she couldn't read the screen but would have easily guessed its contents a mile away. "Depends how many times the term 'Crazy bitch' was used. Then again, that number depends on whether or not anybody's heard about that little riot." "Hold on, let me get into my pseudo- intellectual- with- a- stick- up- my- ass persona," said Ivy, clearing her throat, "'Others, like Amy Campbell of the Fans Wrestling Organization, can't decide if she's a wrestler, a fluttery little teenager, or a target range. She should take a cue from the rest of the women in the eff-dubya-oh, pick a side, and stay there.'" Ivy looked up from the computer screen. "When I rant... please please tell me I don't sound like that." "Trust me, you don't. Hey.......does this guy happen to have an address or phone number published? He sounds like a pretty nice fella..." Amy winked, "Might need to pay him a visit when we get back to touring through America. I get a kick out - and hopefully to the ass of those kind of people; They're the only ones allowed to form an opinion, God forbid anyone else does." She grumbled, shifted around, grumbled some more and went back to pouting. "Wow...new list of people I hate: The Bush Administration, the governments in most of these countries," The redhead gestured to the window. "And now internet geeks who have nothing better to do than watch wrestling and then run to their little laptops to write articles on it - barring you, of course, because you're not the lonely brand. The lonely ones just bitch and bitch...oh, and let's not forget the one who shot me..." Of course, Amy'd just *barely* caught herself in another rant. Unfortunately it had been the tail-end of one. "Sadly not. He publishes the opinion and runs behind an alias. Opinions are like assholes, Amy... the Internet's full'a both." She pulled up one of Amy's favorite websites - www.SomethingPositive.net - and passed the computer off. "Lemmee see that geetar for a sec." They traded off, as Ivy started to play the only tune she knows how - Blister in the Sun - as Amy started to surf the computer. "So how's Sean? You two got a pay site yet?" "'Course we do. You got a credit card and sixty bucks a month?" "Why pay sixty bucks when all I need to do is walk down the hall when you're on the prowl?" "'Cuz my boy's ass is copyrighted and anyone sees it that I don't approve of, he's got some explainin' to do." "Whoa..." Amy chuckled, "Think of the cash I could make off'a BL! It'd be like one of those 'Nuns Gone Wild' sites!" "Yeah, then you could spend it all on a bodyguard when he gets another stalker. And have you even done anything naughty with the poor man yet?" Upon hearing that, the redhead nearly doubled over. "HA! You ask like it's easy...do you have any clue what I'm dealing with? He's 'playing it safe'...I'm starting to seriously consider that he's a virgin who happened to get married and adopt." "Hey," countered Ivy, "in a lotta ways, isnt' that better than a bastard with a hardon who just wants to get'cha in the sack? I've had enough experience for a dozen lifetimes with guys like that, give me someone like Brandon over someone like those college kids in Mexico any day of the week." She thought about it for a moment. "Or give me my Sean - then I'm happy." "Yeah...you keep your Sean, I'll keep my Brandon." She winked, "And yes, it is a lot better...he's sorta like me in a way, he's really afraid of giving his all to something because in most previous cases it's ended up being thrown away. It's kinda funny...we're both afraid to be hurt, and both don't wanna hurt the other, but I don't think he realizes that." "Bull, that's everyone, not just you. Did I ever tell you about the first time Sean and I kissed?" "Were your clothes on? If not, I think I can stand to let you spare me this story......." "Bitch!" laughed Ivy, "Yes, we were both clothed. But I don't know that you deserve to hear it now." She turned away from Amy, and started to play - badly - a sad, sad tune on the guitar. "If it makes you stop that..." Amy smiled at Ivy and winked, though she couldn't see it. "Then talk my ear off." "Well," began Ivy again, as she spun around on the bed without missing a beat, "it was like three, four years ago, just after we declared the 'war' officially over, and someone'd torched his apartment. Dunno if he ever found out who did it but I had rented a condo for a few weeks down in Orlando while we got settled in the SCW - s'where we were workin' at the time - and apparently I was the only person he could call." A smile - not a smirk, but a genuine smile - formed on Ivy's face. "It was a disaster after that - neither of us knew what to do about how we felt. And it showed... we've only been together for the past year." "Brandon and I were totally confused too - if you hadn't noticed." She grinned. "At first I just thought he was a preachy Bible-banger of some kind...good looking at all, but not my 'type'. Besides, I was already with a cheating bastard even though I wasn't aware at the time. Something weird was there, though...and I figured it was just the way he looked or something, but it hasn't gone away. I'm not sure if he thinks the same about me, but yeah...I'm still unsure about what I feel, and that's totally new to me..." She was finally ready to finish the thought, "And at least the first time you kissed, it wasn't while you were both tipsy, in front of everyone and while he was marri---hey, you never even told me what you were supposed to!" "I didn't, did I?" Ivy blew a speck of dust off her glasses as she hopped up and grabbed her black "granny" sweater, "What was it, again?" "Well, you were going to tell me about the first time you and Sean kissed, for some reason..." "Oh yeah! Why was I doing that again?" Ivy started to laugh - giggle might be a bit more appropriate, "In a word, Amy... and I'm being totally serious now... how'd you describe me and Sean's relationship?" Amy sighed, "Intense. That's the most appropriate term I can muster. I actually envy how well you know one another...your relationship is 'perfect' in my book, you're both so strong but really need each other to remain like that. It's really crazy and I'm almost kinda scared I've found the same thing." "That's exactly my point. It took two years after our first kiss to even realize what it meant, and another three weeks of insecurity and my mom kicking my ass while the... cancer was slowly winning, for me to even realize it." Her mood suddenly darkened at the mention of her mother. Ivy sat down in the swivel chair next to the window as she blew her hair out of her eyes. "So don't stress about it, kiddo... no matter how much pressure you think a relationship puts on ya, in the end, if it's worth it - and I can tell you that Brandon is worth it - just relax and let things flow at their own pace." "How can you tell?" She raised an eyebrow, "I guess I'm asking you kinda the same thing you asked me...I don't know how BL and I manage to work." "I can tell by the way he looks at'cha. And not just now - since you both met. I'm good like that," Ivy winked at her, "And besides... you met the kids." "Yeah, meeting them makes for the second bullet-like wound I've endured for him." Amy grinned again and finished with a bit of a laugh, though a little 'blah' on the subject of Brandon's kids - considering one seemed to hate her with a passion, and that's pretty bad considering he's only a few years old. A knock on the door startled both women. "Ivy? Amy?" "C'mon in, Eli," said Ivy, as her brother- in- law opened the door. "Ladies - beer, pizza, schnopps, poker. My room, ten minutes." He took a bow, smiling. Obviously he'd spent some time whispering sweet nothings into Angel's ear today. Ivy watched him leave, and turned back toward Amy. "Shall we?" "Pizza? Schnapps? They're big guys, haul ass before they get it first!" Ivy nodded, as she took two steps toward her... leaned in... and poked Amy gently on the tip of her nose. "Tag, Aimz. You're it." She bolted, Amy just a step behind. And in a foreign land, tired, homesick, and frazzled to the ends of sanity... ...life in the fWo seemed just about normal again.
