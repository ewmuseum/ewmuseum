The sun was setting on another day, and Rei watched from the landscape that stretched out around her. The soft orange light that shined across the grass, the wind that gently blew the leaves across the sky, making them dance in the air....all of it told her that she was no longer able to hide within herself. She was awakened at once, and for what purpose, she still did not know. But, what she did know was that she had to return to the place she hated the most. The fWo. She sighed at the very thought of it. It was the place that had taken her body and engulfed it, caused her to lose what grip on sanity she had left....it was a dark time in her life. It was a dark time in the lives of Kali Saturos and Jessica, her alter-egos, as well. Rei could no longer sit and watch as the dark shroud of hate stretched all around her. She had come outside, to stare into the red sky, and she had found that it offered no solace to her strained heart or her confused mind. It was beautiful, but it was not able to soothe her heart. There was only one person who was able to do that.....she shivered visibly at the thought of him. She looked up into the sky again. Her brother was bleeding for his sport again. She never bled for her. She never brought the knife across his forehead for anything more than his own desires....he'd feel pain for himself and for his love, but he'd never feel pain for her. Her lover never bled for her either. He refused to take the knife across his forehead for her, let the blood moisten her pale white hands....he would never do it. He'd bleed only for his sport, only for himself and his own selfish desires.... No, this wasn't true, Rei realized at once. There WAS someone else he would bleed for now. He had changed, hadn't he? Please. He hasn't changed a bit. He dosen't bleed for her- he doesn't bleed for anyone but himself. People like our lover and our brother are unable to change. The only thing you can do is force yourself onto them and make them accept your existance. All beings are capable of change, when they are influenced by God's will. Perhaps, if you approached them with love, they will change and accept your existance without needing to force yourself onto them? Anything is possible with love. Rei shook her head, trying to free herself of the thoughts of Kali and Jessica. Based on what she had learned when she had become Eternity, Kali and Jessica were nothing more than frusturating pests now. Just like Cara, and .One. She sighed and raised her head into the air, forcing her eyes shut and watching the sun's last rays try and force themselves through. She tried to remember what it was that Eternity had told her once- the true Eternity, rather. Her mind was an incredible mass of chaos, however, filled with thoughts, ideas, and memories that were not her own. Still, this one particular memory came to her suddenly, as if it was always there, bubbling on the surface. "You have fooled yourself for a long time, Rei." Eternity said, running its hands through Rei's long, blue hair. "Your mind is the true mind- your soul is the only soul. Kali and Jessica are not two beings you co-exist with inside a single being- you are the single being. Kali and Jessica are merely different parts of you." It was ironic, wasn't it? Rei had always considered herself to be the meekest of the three- the one who was never in control, and who sat in the corner, watching all of their life unfold without having any influence on it. But in the end, she was really the one in control all along- Kali and Jessica were illusions that her own mind had created out of itself. Rei smiled to herself, despite everything that went around her. She watched as the sunset was completed, and thought to herself that whatever diety had created this world had done a magnificent job. For all that was wrong with it, at least it still possesed the beauty of a sunset. It was enough to comfort any human being, and tell them that everything really was going to be okay, no matter how bad their situation was. "I've been searching for myself for a long time." Rei said quietly to herself. She looked down at the white mask that laid beside her. It was always with her now- Eternity was always with her. "I mine as well give it one last shot." She placed the mask onto her face and stood up. Eternity's time in the fWo was not yet over. Her searching....would continue.
