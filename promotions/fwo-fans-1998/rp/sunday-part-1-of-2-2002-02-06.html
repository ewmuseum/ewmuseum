Sunday (Part 1 of 2)

6 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday, February 3, 2002 12:07 PM Eastern The door opened and Stephanie Griffith stepped into the Ice Palace. Scott Wasner stepped in behind her. He walked to her side and threw an arm around her shoulder. "I present to you, the Ice Palace. Sure, it looks like crap from the outside, but hey, inside it's even worse!" Stephanie smiled. "Oh, it's not that bad. Nothing a few flamethrowers couldn't fix." "You know, you might just be on to something." "Hey, Scott!" A new voice was heard. Wasner looked to the right and saw Allen Collins walking toward them. Collins was in his early 40s and looked the part - balding with a bit of a beer belly. Still, he was a top-notch writer - the reason that Collins had been with the fWo booking staff since early 1999. Before that, he had written a predictions column for the web site, which had helped in getting him his job. "Hey, Al, how's it hanging. Steph, this is Al Collins - he's one of the bookers here. In other words, he's one of the guys who makes me into the dashing, swashbuckling superhero you see on TV every week." "Ah, so he's the guy to blame," she said with a smile. "Yeah, that's me alright. Allen E. Coyote, super booking genius. Listen, I was talking with the rest of the crew last night, and we came up with an idea. Stephanie, how would you like to be on TV?" She looked puzzled. "Me? What would I do on TV?" "Well, you know how Scott's working an angle with Vic Creed, right?" "Yeah." "Well, we think that feud needs a little more heat - a little more interest to it. Vic's character, of course, is a real bad guy - one of the true assholes in wrestling." "Fitting character, considering," Wasner said under his breath. "Hmm?" "Oh, nothing, Al. Go on." "We think, if he could get you alone, say in a dressing room, and kick your ass for the cameras, it'd really get this feud over." He continued quickly at her reaction. "Not really, of course - but you knew that. You know, get in a few pretend shots, that kind of thing. He won't even actually touch you. You'll just be acting the whole time. What do you think?" "Well..." "Excuse us, Al." "Of course." Wasner took Stephanie aside. "I wouldn't do it, Steph. I've worked with Creed. He's nuts. I mean, seriously. From what I've seen of him, he lives his gimmick. He's roughed me up legitimately the times we've worked together. I don't trust him at all." Stephanie bowed her head, considering. She then looked back up at Wasner. "I hear you. He always looks a little nuts on TV. Like he's not all there or something." "Great, then - " "I'm doing it. " - I'll just go back to Al and tell him - what?!" "I said, I'm doing it. I think it could be fun. I mean, yeah, he's not somebody I'd want to spend any time around, but what can go wrong in a little scene or two?" "I don't particularly want to find out." "Oh, come on. It'll be fine. And how many times does somebody like me get to be on TV anyway? Think of what they'll say down at the office." She turned back to Collins. "Alright Al, it's good. Let's do it." Wasner sighed. "I have a bad feeling about this." ----- 2:25 PM Eastern Wasner lounged back on the recliner in his locker room. Stephanie was on the couch, and the Super Bowl was on. It was going nicely, if he did say so himself (and he did). For all the prosaic nicety of the situation, something still felt wrong to Wasner. There was a craving in his head, a need. He tried to push it back. It didn't cooperate. "Hey, I'm gonna step out for a minute, get us some drinks - for all this luxury, they can't even put in a fridge. Pathetic, if you think about it. You want anything?" Stephanie looked up from the TV. "Yeah, I'll have a Mike's." "Ah, a woman after my own taste. I'll be back." "Thanks for warning me." Wasner winked as he stepped out the door. Seconds later, he was outside and headed to the production truck, parked in the parking lot. He climbed up the stair apperatus and knocked on the door. A tall man in an fWo Crew t-shirt answered. "Yeah? Oh, hey, Slugger, what're you doing here?" "I want to talk to R - to Tom." "Oh, sure. Let me get him for you." The man closed the door. It then opened again and Roach stepped out. "Scotty, my man. What can I do you for?" "Darvon. Five." "I can get that for you. Hell, I can get that for you now." Roach reached into his pocket and pulled out a small container. He opened it and took five small pink pills, then put it back into his pocket. He handed the pills to Wasner. "There you go. Have fun." "Yeah, whatever, asshole." "Now, now... is that any way to talk to your dealer?" Wasner's eyes went hard at the word, it clearly striking a nerve. He spoke slowly and calmly. "I suggest... you get back... in the truck." "Alright, I can do that. But you know... you don't even have a match tonight, do you?" "I said, get back in the truck." "Alright, alright. Bite my head off, jeez." Roach stepped back into the truck. Wasner walked back down the steps and popped the pills into his mouth, one after another. ----- 8:52 PM Eastern The door began to open. "Hey, Scott. Forget something?" "No, not really." The voice was unfamiliar. Stephanie turned around... ...and came face-to-face with Vic Creed. He smirked. "Well, Slugger has good taste, I gotta give him that." He took a step further towards her, his walk clearly predatory. She took in the situation and went for a kick below the belt, but he caught her foot. "Uh-uh-uh... not nice there, Steph." "Get the hell away from me!" "Hmm, let's see... no." Creed shoved her violently against the wall. She hit with a thud. Creed advanced toward her again. He reached out and grabbed her by the throat. "Oh, when you wake up... tell Slugger that Vic Creed doesn't know how he expects to defend himself at Body Count when he can't even defend anyone around him." Creed lifted her up by the throat and threw her aside. She hit a coffee table, which collapsed in upon itself with the force. Creed began to chuckle, then turned towards the door to leave the room. "CUT!" yelled the director. "We're clear." Creid nodded and started to walk out of the room. As he did, Wasner rushed through the door, shoving Creid aside as he went. Creid slammed into the wall, hitting hit hard with the back of his head. Wasner knelt down at Stephanie's side. "Are you OK? Are you alright?" She looked up at him, dazed and bleeding from a cut on the face. "I... think so. I think my cheek is bleeding. I think I... lucked out on this one... Nobody told me he was gonna do that..." "Get the EMTs, have them check her out," Wasner told the director, who left the room to do so, leaving only the cameraman and the principals. Creid grabbed Wasner from behind and yanked him to his feet. "Hey, asshole, why'd you - " Slugger spun around and punched Creid in the face, hard and real. Creid, caught unawares, was knocked back towards the wall. "You son of a bitch, that wasn't in the script! I'm gonna fucking kill you!" Wasner leapt at Creid and began pistoning his right fist into Creid's face. Creid, still caught off-guard, took the punishment for a moment before grabbing Wasner, spinning him around, and beginning to punch him. "Hey, you two! What the fuck are you doing?!" the cameraman shouted, but Wasner and Creid ingnored him, trading punches. "Stop! Now!" Stephanie's shouts also went unheard. The two men continued to fight. The cameraman ran back into the room with two security officers behind him. The officers stepped between the two to separate them. "Hey, get the fuck off me! Let me at him! I'm gonna hurt that bastard!" "Yeah, you and what army, asshole?!" "Both of you, ENOUGH!" shouted one of the officers. The two stopped their struggles and backed off, Wasner retreating towards Stephanie. At the moment, the director walked in with an EMT. "What the hell's going on here?" Creid sneered. "I was just leaving." "Not so fast, Vic," said the other officer. "We're gonna have to talk to both of you here." "No way, I got a match coming up." "Fine. If neither of you want to press charges, it can wait until the show's over. Is that OK with both of you?" The two nodded their affirmatives. With that settled, Creed walked out the door, followed by the officers. The EMT, apparently having finished his examination as the events went on, spoke. "You're OK, miss. You've got a little cut on your cheek, but other than that, you're fine. No signs of a concussion, no breaks, no other injuries. I'd say you got very lucky." "Thank God," she said. Wasner and the EMT helped her to her feet. "Now, if you want to stay here overnight, just in case - and I'd recommend it - we can take you to the nearest hospital." "No... I'll be fine. I'm leaving tomorrow, anyway." "Alright. Let me know if you change your mind." The EMT exited. "Steph, if there's anything I can do for you..." "No... I'm fine. Well, relatively speaking. I think I'm just gonna lie down. And you were right... he is an asshole."
