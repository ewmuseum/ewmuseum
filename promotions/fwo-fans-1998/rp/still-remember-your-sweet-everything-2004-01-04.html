Still remember your sweet everything

4 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
December 18th, 2003 It was a walk that went head-first into her own nightmare... The only thing that made Amy ill faster than hospitals was the notion of someone she loved being inside of one. There was always a way her subconscious could relate it back to being her fault - either she didn't stop them in time or she pushed them to it. This time, she hadn't managed to stop the trains before they wrecked and, hard as she tried to slow it, Brandon's calculated revenge against Vic Creid hadn't worked out as well as any of them hoped. They were still seperated...somehow. Nothing was official, but nothing was happening - they hadn't spoken directly to one another in a normal manner in weeks, but for some reason Amy was there, walking to his room with the most false brave face anybody could have imagined. Brandon wasn't expecting her, how could he? As far as he knew, she could very well consider him the dumbest man on the planet. Then again, Amy almost smiled to herself. Love wasn't exactly picky - After all, the man had asked her to marry him. Amy had managed to finagle a room card from the clerk and as she paused outside his room door it twisted back and forth in her hand. She tested the resiliency of the plastic for a good minute before finally sliding it through the card reader. The green light blinked on. Still, she didn't enter. Another minute passed. The light had changed back to the cautionary red LED. Another swipe of the card and twist of the handle had Amy strolling strongly into the room. If she was going to do this it had to be quickly; less of a chance to change her mind and run away that way. Steeling herself against a barrage of accusations, Amy twisted her face into a frown when she found the room vacant. He must've been released the night before, she decided quickly with infinite disappointment in her eyes. Her purpose there was to confront him on everything, but the true intent had been just to see him, just to speak to him...just to have that chance to make things right, and now Amy discovered all of her efforts would be in vain - she'd have started a conversation with a vacant hospital bed. ...That is, if it weren't for the mountain of a man ambling up behind her. The second Amy heard the heavy footsteps she shot around, expecting to find Vic Creed or someone equally as threatening. What she did get, however, was the sight of the man she loved with hair unwashed and scraggly, and stubble equally as unkept still somehow walking proudly. He was almost obviously masking a smile, which was something the smaller of the redheads absolutely refused to do as hers shone through and she tossed her arms around Brandon Roche...who, momentarily, she'd forgotten she was fighting with. When she remembered, she released him and not a word was uttered aside from a weak, "Err..hi." The hug. It melted his heart. It felt as if the last few months of his life hadn't happened, that the two of them still existed in that magical place where real life didn't exist. His breath came in short bursts. "Hi, yourself. I love you." Amy'd been terrified of his possible reactions, and this was the one she expected the least. She prolonged the hug as long as she could, speaking into his chest for fear of being released if she struggled to look up. "I love you. God, this has been bad...really fucking bad." the redhead wasn't entirely set on her train of thought, so words were coming haphazardly. "You...I don't even know. I stopped sleeping right, even for me...couldn't take time off because of how much I'd had off before. Are you..." She began, but quickly turned the question into a double-edged sword, "...are WE okay?" "We're exactly where we were before... I guess." The words confused him just as much as it surely confused her. "I love you. I can't deny that. I... I don't know, there's a lot of stuff that happened. A lot of stuff made me feel like I was going down the same path as I had before." Amy twisted her head to the side, listening and holding back her automatic responses in an attempt to understand where he was coming from and where he was going with all of this. At least she finally had him talking. "We don't need to do play-by-play over everything we've said to each other, it's a waste of time. I didn't like the way you handled things. You thought you were protecting me, I understand that. I guess... I never really talked to you about how much the lies from Ana, the lies from Chris and the rest, really affected me. Maybe if I had spoken directly instead of trying to sweep them under the rug and keep them hidden, you'd have reacted differently." "Look..." Amy stepped back from him a little, gazing up with pleading eyes. "I know he caused a lot," She couldn't even speak Creed's name, not without clenching a fist, "But the others need to pay for the lies they told. I've tried handling Vic for what he has done to you, and that's all I can do...there's nothing more to understand for me. There can be as much explaination as you'd like, but I see things as I see them and you know better than anybody that my mind's a difficult thing to change." Brandon just smiled. That was a double edged sword if he'd ever seen one. Better to let it be, for the sake of the healing muscles in his arm. "So where does this put us then? Do we just go get some pizza and play 'catch-up' like we've been on vacation, or do we sit down and plan a wedding or what?" The sarcasm stung more than he meant, foot-in-mouth syndrome was fatal, the joke looked to fall on sensitive ears. The tiniest of the redheads backed up a step again, folding her arms. She tried not to look as confused and rather hurt as she was, but it didn't work very well...Brandon could see through any veil she put up. "I..." she started and paused to think a moment, "I don't know, Brandon. It's whatever you want, and right now I have absolutely no idea what that might be." And it was Amy's turn to lay the sarcasm on with an all-too classic female move... "Y'know, a wedding's just a little thing and all. We can do it if you feel like it some Saturday, not like it matters much." "Not like it matter's much?" Brandon laughed and pulled Amy by the wrist out of the doorway, letting it slide shut on it's own. "You're funny. Sorry for the bad humor by the way, but at least you know I'm not an evil twin or something. Right?" Not waiting for an answer however, Brandon pulled Amy to him by the same wrist, bent his shoulders and lifted the firecracker up and into an embrace that threatened to crush the life from her. Her breath did huff out, and into his waiting mouth, as he turned his head to the side and leaned in. Sealing their lips together for the first time in what seemed like forever. When he let her down, Amy tried regaining her composure rather unsuccessfully and "Do you need sleep? I have a bed...err...well, a room. C'mon." Brandon paused for a thought, but soon followed the redhead through the visitation area; There were much bigger, more harmful evils to fight than a stubborn girlfriend.
