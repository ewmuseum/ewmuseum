DISCLAMER: This tale has nothing to do with the fWo. MY CONFESSIONS Part One - Scene of the Crime "There it is," said TJ as the bus lumbered down the road, "Spencer, Nebraska. The middle- of- nowhere where it all began." "Angel still sleeping?" "Yup." "Good, don't wake her." The Valerian's Garden tourbus has been driving all day in hopes of reaching their next destination before the sun goes down. An all- day festival, the first of its kind in Spencer, has drawn in musical artists from all walks of life, including cKy, Dope, Otep, Lifer, Distane, HIM(Her) and a homegrown - for the most part - band, Valerian's Garden. Homegrown only in the sense that the band's singer and bassist are from the town, even though they haven't been here in eight to ten years. And while Angel lived here... it was hardly a happy place. "Mickey, you and Angel were a couple for over a year," said Nine, "Didn't she ever tell you why she never wanted to even travel near her hometown?" "Nope," replied Mickey, "She didn't like to talk about it. What about you, Teej? You both lived here, right?" TJ stretched out on the couch opposite the rest of the band. "I've known her for a decade now... y'think she ever talked about her family any more with me? I've never even met her parents." They all stared at him for that. "Whaddaya mean, you never met her parents?" asked Troy, his eyes returning to the road, "You knew her first, Teej... how's that possible?" TJ looked back at Angel, fast asleep on the couch. She tossed uncomfortably, but three sleepless nights dealing with MJ's first fever has taken a toll, to say nothing of the anxiety of this homecoming. "Well, I have met them... but it was strictly in the 'Is Angel ready yet?' sense of the word." "Damn." Well put, Nine. As the bus ambled along, Troy turned up the radio ever so slightly. The song, appropriately enough, 'Mother' by Pink Floyd, was an old favorite, and was one of the few songs that could really keep Angel asleep. The four other members of the band talked ambiently amongst themselves as they worked on a playlist for the concert. Angel had been writing like a maniac ever since she found out about the show, and the new songs were... different, one could argue. "I know she wanted to open with 'Torn to Shreds'," said Mickey, "but I was thinking it'd probably be better for her to keep most of the old stuff in the set and maybe do one or two new ones, depending how she's feelin' when they come up." "You know how well that'll go over," disagreed Sex, shaking his head, "Angel puts down what she wants to do and that's that.. How long you been with this band?" Mickey laughed.
