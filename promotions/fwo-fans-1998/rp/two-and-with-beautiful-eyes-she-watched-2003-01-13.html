Two: And with beautiful eyes, she watched

13 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Feel what is on the inside..... Watch what is coming..... Duck for cover..... Because she will crumble, and the other will survive..... "I'm getting sick and tired of cryptic messages." Kali Saturos said quickly. She pulled her long, black hair back with one hand, feeling the fibers of her hair scratch up against her fingers. "Cryptic messages are all you will get from that one." Rei pointed out quickly. "Yes, I know that, thank you obvious-girl." Kali shot back. "But I believe I'm the one in charge right now, correct?" "Yes." Rei agreed. "You can listen to whoever you want to, whether it is foolish or not." "You should be listening to God!" Jessica yelled as she entered the dark room. Kali shook her head, while Rei did her usual arguement to this. "How do you know your God exists?" Rei asked Jessica. "He's spoken to me before." Jessica said. "It's very funny how you and I can both hear it when the other being communicates with Kali, but neither Kali nor I can hear this God talk to you." Rei pointed out. "Sinners can't hear the words of the Lord." Jessica said quickly, and then stuck her tounge out at Rei. Rei sighed, threw her hands up in a 'whatever' motion, and turned back to Kali. "What is it that it wants you to do in this wrestling orginization?" Rei asked her. "I'm not sure yet." Kali said. "I think it has something to do with this Downfall person." "How do you know that?" Rei asked. "It sent me this." Kali pushed a videotape into a nearby VCR, and turned on the TV. For a moment, Paladin paused. Downfall rose to his feet, but immediately received a kick to the stomach. With fluid-like motion and undeniable familiarity, the veteran slammed the rookie to the canvas violently. Downfall, somehow, managed to roll out of the way of a falling elbow drop. The quick recovery put him on top with a hurricanrana bridge into a pinfall. 1... 2... The third count fell short as Paladin threw Downfall off of his shoulders. Both men stood up instantly and went for the attack. Fists flailed relentlessly, but Downfall quickly took the advantage. He pushed Paladin up against the ropes, took one step back, and threw a forceful clothesline that knocked the older man over the ropes. Quickly, Downfall sprinted to the other end of the ring, bounced off of the ropes and jumped. His feet didn't even touch the top rope as he sailed into the chest of Paladin. The fans at ringside actually showed some signs of life. Apparently, the fast-paced match drew them back into what was happening. The few cheers triggered something inside Downfall, and for these few minutes, he forgot his own pain and desired only to entertain these people. He rose to his feet and stood up energetically. He threw his head back to get the straggling hair out of his eyes. "That's what they sent you?" Rei asked her. "So, what are you going to do." "I'm not sure." Kali replied. "But whatever I do, I had better do it fast." "I agree." Rei said. "The thing that sent you this.....it's not something that you displease." Kali nodded, and then the entire scene- Rei, Jessica, the dark room, the TV- it all faded. She looked around at where she was, and she found nothing. "Alone again." Kali said quietly. "But maybe I can change that. Soon." From someplace above, Rei looked down at her. "Manipulated by our own inner trauma." Rei said quietly. "You will be in for quite the ride, Kali Saturos. And when it's over....." Fin.
