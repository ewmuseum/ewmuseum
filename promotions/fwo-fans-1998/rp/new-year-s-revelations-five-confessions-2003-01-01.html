New Year's Revelations: Five - Confessions

1 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brandon's head spinned. As much as he had wanted to pretend he had dreamed it all he could still feel Amy's lips pressed against his. Everything happened so fast, they had lost control and damn him for not keeping himself in line. The attraction to Amy Campbell was immediate all those weeks ago. The night Brandon offered her a ride to her hotel he could feel emotions stirring in him. He was a family man though with children and a wife that depended on him. The kids looked to him as a hero and his wife saw him as the light of her life. They had known each other for so long, everything was so comfortable. Why then had they been drifting apart recently? It wasn't his hidden feelings for Amy, no those buried feelings were fresh while Ana and he had been unraveling from the time Brandon stepped foot in to the Fan's Wrestling Organization. He couldn't put all of the blame on his wife though; he had not fought hard enough to fix the cracks in the relationship that had so obviously grown. Now he stared at the golden band around his finger. If he moved it ever so slightly you could see the indentations in his skin. The ring, when slid on fit exactly where it always had, comfortably in the niche it had made for itself. What was he going to say? Did he hide the kiss from his wife in the hopes that it would all just go away? He knew the answer to that before it even finished forming in his throbbing skull. Neither Amy nor Ana should be disrespected like that. As uncomfortable as it sounded he was going to be honest with everyone, about everything. He needed to confess the feelings he had for Amy to his wife, it was important that she knew everything that he had done to mess things up. He couldn't live with himself if he didn't. To keep the kiss a secret would also disrespect what it meant, to both he and Amy. The sudden act needed to be talked about, resolved. Things would not work if they were kept in the dark. It was at times like these that Brandon Roche missed being on the road with the old members of The Pantheon. Chris and Jon were his best friends and if he ever needed someone he totally trusted to talk to it was right now. He had just called them both, neither of them home this early New Years morning. Both probably out celebrating another great year coming, neither knowing the things that Brandon was living through. Neither knowing how badly their partner had messed things up. Brandon realized that no matter the deep feelings that dwelled in his heart for the fiery redhead he had to put them aside. All of this time had passed and even though the tension was always there he knew he had everything under control. Blaming the alcohol was not an option, Brandon was not about to cheapen the act nor Amy by saying he was fueled by liquid courage. With that thought in his head the not so mighty hero of the fWo turned the doorknob. The door squeaked a bit as he walked in to the dimly lit hotel room. Ana was sitting back with her book-light on, not really paying attention to her husband coming in. If she had she would have seen the beads of sweat on his forehead even though it was near freezing. He tossed his jacket on the dresser next to the television as Ana finally looked up and made eye contact. Instantly she knew. "...what's...wrong...?" The hesitation in her voice struck the nerves connected to Brandon's tear ducts. His eyes welled up with water as he absently poured himself a plastic cup of water. "Brandon? What happened, is everyone alright?" Gulping back the room temperature water Brandon simply turned and looked his wife straight in the eye. His own eyes clearing of the tears with a few bats of his eyelids. "Ana, we need to talk about some things. About us..." "Yeah, I know we do B.L. it's like we're on auto-pilot or something. We're going through the motions and nothing is getting better." It seemed as if Anastasia Roche had been thinking about this a lot herself. She immediately went head first in to a conversation most people would walk in to like on eggshells. "Well, I... I just want you to know..." Brandon stopped, saying that he loved her right before dropping a bomb on her like what he had to say was not fair to her. "I just need to tell you something. Something happened. I'm not going to say I was drunk and beg for you not to be mad... because you have every right to be... it's just that..." He stammered too much, and his face turned a shade too bright red. Ana quickly pushed back the covers and tossed her book in the floor. "Brandon... God Brandon, what... what did you do?" In her mind she ran through a thousand possibilities. He had gotten drunk and Vic Creid came to that damn party. There was a fight and now Creid was seriously hurt. That was the main answer that swirled in her head. Had he found out everything and went stark raving mad? What if he had done something worse than simply beat up Vic? Nothing that was churning around in her mind though came close to what Brandon had to say. "I kissed Amy." ::SMACK:: Brandon's head jolted to the right as Ana connected instinctively with a slap to his face. No thought backed her actions. It was simply something that happened, but she made no move to apologize for it afterwards either. Instead she stood in front of her husband awaiting some sort of explanation, some sort of anything. "We were at the party. New Years hit. Ivy called out that the ball was dropping and I... kissed her." Ana pulled back and swung again, the words hitting a button inside her that triggered the response automatically. Instead of making contact though Brandon caught the hand this time and held on. Ana pulled back trying to get away from her bastard of a husband, but he held on. The confession drained the strength out of Ana Roche and she finally gave in before slumping to the ground, her hand still held by Brandon. "I've... had these feelings for her for awhile now." Ana looked to her husband and tears began to well up in her eyes as well. Not fighting hers back they trickled down her cheek. "Why... what's this all about? I... I don't really understand. You do this and come here to immediately rub my face in it? Do you expect me to just run away crying now so you can feel free to go and sleep with the tramp!?" Ana's anger had begun to show now. Even though she tried to keep it in check it was obvious that the wrong word would set her off. Brandon looked in to the moist eyes of his wife. He wondered where all of this had gone wrong. What secret thing was there that kept them from healing properly and walking down that perfect road they had envisioned together? "No! No... No... that is NOT what this is about. I, well I just messed up. Big time. I know... I knew you would be mad, I know this hurts. It's just that if I lied to you about it I couldn't look at myself in the morning. I could have hid it. After... after we kissed there was this brief second before we got separated in the mob. I could see in her eyes that she knew it was a mistake too." "You bet she knew it was a mistake, wait until..." Brandon interrupted her. He knew where that train of thought was going and would NOT let this go down that way. All everyone had to do was keep an even head on their shoulders and this could be straightened out. "No, we're not going to do the whole blame game and there will NOT be a confrontation." "I'm not going to just run away and give up on this, I made a mistake Ana. You did too once upon a time, but you came to me and we fixed things. You told me everything that happened and because of our love we worked things out." Brandon swallowed hard. It was a risky topic to bring up, especially at a time like this but it was something he felt he had to do. "I can't say that I didn't like what I did." Brandon looked to Ana. He gulped a bit at the way those words sounded out loud. She simply stared at him now though, waiting for him to either make sense of that statement or to dig himself a deeper hole. "I did like it. There was something there. But what I felt afterwards was overwhelming guilt, because of my feelings for you. That's the reason I came right here... well, not right here... I've been sitting outside for an hour thinking about everything... but still." "We need to talk about this. More than we can tonight, I can see you had one too many and we're not going to talk about this shit while you're drunk. Call Lance and see if you can stay with him tonight." Ana's eyes struck deep in to Brandon like icy daggers. There was anger there, but also a hint of compassion. "While you call around I'm going to get a drink from the machine..." Ana looked back at Brandon as she slipped her coat over her bedclothes and trekked outside in to the cold. When the night air hit her it made her shiver. At least, that is what she presumed the shiver was for, or was it her sinking feeling that everything was about to come out? The truths had been buried for too long. Maybe she knew that Brandon was right. Maybe he could have kept the secret like she has. Knowing how close this fraternity of boys are she would never have found out.
