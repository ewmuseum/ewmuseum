Dash away, dash away, dash away all.

fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Dash away, dash away, dash away all. December 29, 1999 'Twas the night of Oblivion, and all through the fed, Not a wrestler believed UV'd a brain in his head; Purple hair, and a scalp, and a big empty space Was certainly all that shone from that face. The wrestlers, the riders, the announcers and more, The bookers, the producers, and Machina's whore, The writers, the stalkers, and the fans all alike, Thought UV stupid, and indeed, they were right! From out of the dressing room arose such a clatter, Ultra sprang to his feet to see what was the matter. He made it two steps but then fell fast asleep, Forgetting his mission, but relaxing his feet. Then over the doggie a cameraman sailed, Succeeding in ways that the puppy had failed. Into the dressing room, to get in on tape, He filmed it all swiftly and let it take shape. It seemed that poor Clarence had done it again, He'd tried to count forward from zero to ten. This made him all dizzy and he fell to the floor, Hitting his head roughly on wall, dresser, and door. The damage was minor but made such a sound, That Violet ran in with a leap and a bound. Her look of concern quickly faded of course, For ol' UV's head was as strong as a horse. Then Violet remembered her most recent goal And summoned Kid Cool to yet further his role. He showed up in minutes and was met with a hug; UV looked at them vacantly, dumb as a bug. Still Violet kept holding the Kid in her hands, Stroking him softly along redheaded strands. Something finally clicked and UV's face fell, "We have to wrestle! Let's get down there and sell!" and I heard him exclaim as he tripped out of sight, "Merry Whoo-Yeah to all, and to all a Whoo-Night!!!"
