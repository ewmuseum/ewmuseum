Arriving Backstage at Bodycount

27 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He pulled up in a new Chevy Lumina. The flight had went well, not counting that drop midway over the rocky mountains... or great lakes. It was all the same to him. He switched it to park, turned the key, and rubbed his neck a few times to work the kinks out. They came much more frequently now, but unlike most guys in this biz, he knew that he would never be crippled by this profession. He opened the door, went to the trunk and got his bags before heading to the arena. The fWo had not hidden his entrance into their organization. He wouldn't be sneaking into the arena. In fact, they had quickly leaked that he would be at BodyCount to every wrestling news "source" in the western hemisphere. He walked through the door and went to security. The guy was a well-built old man, easily in his 70's. He wore dark framed glasses that dipped below his pristine white hair, at least what was left of it. "I with fWo. Where I go?" He said. "Hello." THe old man said. "You're the Deacon. My grandson is a huge fan of yours." The old guy smiled, dropping his age to maybe in his 50s. "I sorry," Deacon said. "I should have introduced myself. My name is Elijah." "I'm Alvin Romans, thrilled to meet you. Would you mind signing something for my grandson?" The old man asked, still smiling. "Not at all. What his name?" Deacon said as Alvin handed Deacon a napkin pulled off the still closed food stand. "Make it out to Warren," Alvin said as he reached out the napkin. "That not do at all." Deacon said, shaking his head. "If I not leave it at home, I have somet'ing in my bag for t'is." Deacon rummaged through his bag for a few moments before pulling out a small 'new testament with psalms' book. He signed the inside front cover to the grandson and handed it to Alvin. "Thank you." Alvin said. "I guess you really believe the stuff you say on the tv." "I do." Deacon responded. Alvin shook his head 'yes' and still, you guessed it, smiled. "Me too." Alvin said. "Down the hall to your right. And thank you." "T'ank you." Deacon said before heading down the hallway. Alvin flipped open the bible, looking for the autograph. Beside it, was the words - "Warren, walk by faith and not by sight. Fear nothing. Live for Christ today & Jesus will bless all your tomorrows. Faith is the Evidence, Deacon Alvin hoped his grandson would take these words to heart, as he himself had done in his 20's. "Deacon?!" Alvin yelled. Deacon turned around. "You gonna win tonight?" "I not tell you t'at. But if everyone have fun, t'en we all win, right?" A smirk formed on Alvin's face as the man they call Deacon stepped into his locker room. "Better get back to work." Alvin said to himself as he turned to man the back door entrance.
