Travelling Circus - Chapter 1

9 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Idly rubbing the back of his neck, John Rider picked up the small saucer containing a tea cup and marched with determined strides towards the living area of his small steel-sided RV. The tiny vehicle had a small convertible table-bed that was now laden with dirtsheets and odd wrestling texts, bearing the same disorganized look that it often bore during a late night betting session. However, this research did not involve the odds, or the ponies, or Vegas. This research was a favor for an old friend. Or, at least if not friend, someone whose trust he had earned, however tenuous that trust was, and he would at least make a marginal effort before he let them down. As he settled down onto the sofa, pulling the nearest wrestling magazine to him across the coffee table, his mind inevitably flickered back to the events that had transpired during what should have been a great Slaughter. One of many, he thought. Of course, he should have known better by now, nothing in his life was ever simple. But John had promised Mufanga Kagos that he would do everything in his power possible to help him make a name for himself, now that he had made it into the fWo. It was a bizarre twist of fate that had Mufanga's first Cyberslam appearance coincide with FOX's cancellation of the fWo's Sunday Night show. John simply couldn't accept the fact that somehow these two events had a common theme. While he certainly didn't profess to understand the decision-makers in the FOX network or how they worked, he found it hard to believe that even they could be so callous as to find it necessary to dash the hopes of so many aspiring wrestlers. Particularly one such as Mufanga... the rookies of the fWo seemed to be the hope for the future. Unfortunately, the Network didn't seem to have as much faith in them as Chris Universal did. Flipping a page in an open magazine, Mufanga's former agent sipped thoughtfully on his tea. Tea relaxed him; coffee kept him alert and ready for action. And he'd probably drank more than enough coffee while he was in the backstage of that last Slaughter that he would be feeling the effects of the caffeine for weeks to come. The Magazine was "PWI's Top 500", listing the top choices for the best wrestlers in the industry. A few fWo names sprang to the top of the list, the wrestlers listed making up most of the veterans of the federation. Erik, Vic, Ruben, Scott Slugger... Page after page, Rider flipped through the list of athletes, past LLB, past Brandon Blade, past Jeremy Howard, Ivan Stanislav and Ninja K, looking hopefully for Mufanga Kagos' name to jump out at him. On the last page, Rider traced down the end of the list with a trembling finger, mumbling to himself. "498... Bob Loblaw, 499... Bum Boozler, 500... Mike Heftel. Heftel? Five hundred wrestlers, and they list Heftel instead of Mufanga? Goddamn..."
