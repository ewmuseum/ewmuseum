Song from the Heavens, Humming from Hell - 9 - Part Two

18 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Memory from long ago...............through the mind they flow.....................until they come to a slow-mo...... "Pleased to meet you. Hope you guess my name. But, what's puzzling you is the nature of my game" - The Rolling Stones "You smoke?" Douglas smacked him in the shoulder and smiled once again. "Angel, alot has changed. I'm about to offer you the chance of a lifetime" Douglas slid passed him and the door closed. "First off, Angel, I know this has been a difficult time for you. Alot of the other guys out there, they don't know about Marla, but I do. And I can only imagine the pain you're going through" A.D. looked away. He had spent everyday since he heard trying to forget that he had. Trying to lose himself in his work. To shut Marla's death out. "And, I also know that you've been to see Noah, and I know what he told you" "How could you kn..?", he began. "That's not important", Steve interrupted. "What is important is that you believe me when I say that he's lying. He's crazy, Angel. You know that. He doesn't know what he's saying. I wasn't even in Boston that night. I havn't been there in months. Please, Angel. You have to believe me. I'm still your friend" Steve's eyes met Angel's and he saw something he hadn't seen in a very long time. He saw the pain and the suffering that Steve has indured over the years. The pain that he was finally beginning to deal with. The pain the spawned truth and honesty and love. A small tear forced it's way out of Steve's right eye and down his cheek. "I believe you", A.D. said, his lips curling into a sad smile. Steve smiled back. "Angel", Steve began again, wiping the tear away. "I've been away a very long time, and I know what this business can be like. If you're gone for too long, they forget about you. They forget and they move on to someone else. But, I'm a changed man, Angel. I know I've said that before, but this is for real. I'm healthier and I'm smarter and I'm ready to work my way back up to the top" He stopped, and took another drag of his cigarette, exhaling thoughfully. "But, I need help, Angel. I need you to be there for me. Christ, this is tough. I need you to be my shoulder again. I've learned that you can't make it alone, and I'm asking you to let me lean on you. I guess, I'm asking you to be my friend again" A.D.'s smile widened. He saw his old friend, the one with whom he sat beside for infinate hours in the hospital, listening to his laboured breathing. His idol, the one with whom he spent infinate hours bound in a metal hold through the TV when Angel was a child. His love for Steve Douglas came rushing back into him like rain, and he reached to shake Steve's hand. Steve took it, and they shook. "Welcome back, old friend", A.D. said. "Thank you, Angel" *** Angel remembered that conversation like it was yesterday, where had Douglas gone wrong? Was he ever human? Or was it all a lie? The worst part was that no one would ever know why Angel was about to kill Steve Douglas, and even if they told him, they would never believe him. Angel was going to give it all away, for a world that hardly knew his name.
