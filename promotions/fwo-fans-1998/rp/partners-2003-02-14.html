"You're different you know." Fallen blew hair out of his face and grinned like a flash before letting his expression go gray once more. "Am I?" Dina Sol nodded and hugged her knees to her chest. She used to control this man, not the other way around. Was it really so long ago when he had sold him out... no, it wasn't him then, it was John Skyler. This was Ryan, Ryan Moore and he was supposed to be the easier of the two. This was Joy, fucking Joy. When did he call the shots and why couldn't she gain control here? "You are. You're... darker. Darker than John..." Fallen turned swiftly and pointed into her face. "For all intents and purposes, I am John. Remember that. Besides, people change. People evolve. This business evolves, and people evolve. Understand?" Dina nodded and hugged her knees closer to her chest. She regreted, not for the first time, getting invovled in this. But she thought it would be different. She thought it would be... controllable. "Yeah. Yeah... I understand." Fallen turned back to the suitcase he was packing and nodded to himself. "That's good. That's very good because we're just getting started. This Sunday Neo and I will be the fWo World Tag Team Champions. Then... then it's Borst and the Dragon and Martyr and everyone else. We're teaching them a lesson Dina, and I want you to remember that. Everyone needs to remember that." Dina didn't say anything but chewed a lip in concern. Was this how John felt? Was this how John felt when it was her that was pulling the strings? God, my God... did she make people feel like this? This fear? This pain? This... "Yes," and Fallen's voice startled Dina so much that she jumped. "Yes, everyone needs to remember that." Dina Sol looked out the window and felt more trapped than she ever had at any point in her life. If hell was warm... this was worse. This was cold.
