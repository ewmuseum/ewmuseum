The Plan

1 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization The Plan April 01, 2000 (The camera fades in on a locker room. Mike Bear and his girlfriend Brittany are seated on a couch, Mikes arm around the attractive blond. Mike is wearing jeans, boots, and one of the few Wretch t-shirts ever made with a giant red no symbol over it made with red tape, while Brittany is wearing a pair of jeans and an fWo tank top. A TV and VCR sit in front of the couple. Geoff Green is there with a video tape) Geoff: Youre lucky that were friends, Mike. I wouldnt violate one of the wrestlers trusts for just anybody. Mike: But, Geoff, this is Wretch were talking about. Dont you remember what hes done to you. Geoff: Ok, ok, Im just trying to milk this for a favor down the road, happy now? Mike (smiling): Why yes Geoff, I am. Now I am mad at you, dont make me go get the bat. Geoff: I can never tell when youre joking with that grin of yours. Mike: Oh, Geoff, youre too easy. (Mike puts the tape into the VCR and hits play. The tape starts by displaying the title Gaining Respect and then Part 1. Mike, Brit, and Geoff chuckle at the antics of Scott Hart. Mike laughs especially loud when he finds out Wretch didnt accept his challenge at all) Mike (laughing): He sent Hart to deliver the message? I never knew he could be that dumb! (The scene fades out and then displays Part 2. Once again, the trio laughs at Harts antics, as well as having a good chuckle at the expense of Brian Knobbs. Mike laughs as Wretch tries to convince Finn that hes the savior of the fWo. So much in fact that he laughs into Part 3. Brit quiets Mike down, because this seems important) Wretch: For how to get out of this match with Bear. Mike: I knew it! The coward, hes too chicken to step in the ring with me! Brit: Shhh, Mike, well miss something. Wretch: A plan! We can injure Bear *AND* not have to do any work! Scott: What? Mike: WHAT?! Wretch: Alright, when I turned on Bear he got all upset, and when he got upset those Austin and Willis guys beat the crap out of him... remember? Scott: Right! Wretch: So all we have to do is get Mike pissed off so much, that the guards will have no choice but to seriously injure him! Mike: How do they plan on doing that? (As if a cosmic force anticipated Mikes asking this question, Wretch answers) Wretch: Steal... Mike Bear's... Girlfriend. (Mike is speechless. You can hear his teeth grating. Rage is building up inside him. All of a sudden he jumps up and hurls the TV across the room. It collides with the wall, and lands with a thump on the floor) Mike: ILL KILL THE BASTARD! TRY AND STEAL MY GIRLFRIEND WILL HE!? HELL BURN IN HELL FIRST! (Brittany jumps up to try and calm Mike down) Brittany: Mike! (Brit slaps him) Snap out of it God damn it! (Mike glares at Brittany with eyes of rage, and Brittany stares back with the ice cold look that most men know about. Mike holds the gaze as he tries to settle down. Finally, the two embrace, obviously used to this type of situation) Mike: What would I do without you Brit? Brittany: Taking John and Austin out to dinner and a movie? Mike: Thats why I love you Brit. So what are we going to do about Chris? Brittany: Why dont I just go along with it? Mike: WHAT?! Brittany: Think about it Mike. Chris is harmless. He certainly wouldnt hurt me. Hed only be using me to get to you. Besides Mike, Im a big girl. I could take Chris, if need be. Ive wrestled guys who were a lot bigger, stronger, AND smarter than him and Scott Hart combined. Think about it, if I didnt go along with it, hell try something else we might not be ready for. (Mike gives her that look to say Remember who were dealing with) Brittany (laughing): Ok, ok, hed do something even more annoying. Mike: Right, so we let him kidnap you, then Id get John and Austin to pretend to beat me up, and Wretch thinks that Im getting injured. Hey, thats just crazy enough to work. We could probably keep that up until Cyberslam. What could go wrong? (We can hear thunder crackle faintly in the background as we fade to black)
