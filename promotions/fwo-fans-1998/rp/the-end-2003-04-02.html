The End?

2 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Up, down, all around. Nothing but black. Nothing but hate. Nothing but fear. oh god please help me i am losing myself once again oh no please god no Kali Saturos stood there, in the darkness. She was not banished to the void this time. She had wilingly entered it. She heard .One's silent pleas, but she did not listen. She had nothing more to say to him, as she slowly rose the barrell of the gun up to point at his face. He kneeled there, silently, with his eyes closed. If he opened them, he knew what he'd be looking at, and he couldn't bear to do it. He couldn't bear to know that everything in his life was going to come down to such a pathetic end. That everything was going to come crashing down and everything was going to..... please god i don't want to die i can't die i can't face my crimes oh god almighty please come and save me and take me away There would be no saving him. From behind, .Two watched as Kali removed the safety from the gun. She smiled. Everything was working out just as she had planned, and there wasn't anything anyone could do about it now. As always, she was in control. And as she turned and walked away, removing the black mask covered with two dots in the process, all she could hear was the sound of gunfire, the sound of .One's scream, and the sound of death. The death of Darrell Purconin. The death of Exel Pure. The death of .One. The death of Chaos. Was this the end? goodbye
