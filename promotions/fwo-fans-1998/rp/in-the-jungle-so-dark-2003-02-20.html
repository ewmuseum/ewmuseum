In the Jungle, So Dark

20 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was always night in the dream. And he was running. It felt as if he were chasing after someone, but he knew that couldn't be right. At least at some point in the sequence he knew that. Running. Hunting. The maze of low-lying vines and foilage that surrounded him tore brutishly at his arms and face as he ran. Dead, dry leaves crunched under bare feet as he sprinted through he underbrush, his gaze never leaving that figure just up ahead, who was crashing clumsily through the waist-high growth. The figure ahead was so dim and distant, but he knew he had to reach it in order to be sated. He knew that if he could only reach the man screaming, cursing, falling up ahead he could- We could feast, you and I, we could- -he could find the end to this dream, and maybe then the dreams would stop. And then the jungle itself materialized in front of him, simply looming up out of the gray nether world of the dream. He always stopped when that happened, his feet suddenly reluctant, their passage through the maze and the darkness slowed by an unbearable pressure he didn't understand. Still screaming an anguished howl, the man-figure ahead never hesitated, but bowled headlong into the jungle. It seems to swallow him up, in a black throat of leaf and vine. His heart dropped as the man-figure disappeared from sight, suddenly realizing the dream would end again, without truly finishing. Again it- His scent. His scent is still on the wind. We can smell him. Find him. TASTE him. He burst into motion, now a blur along the dusty village path, and careened into the sheer jungle wall, passing through as branches grabbed at his hair like reaching, skeletal fingers. He stopped once inside the shell of the jungle, his bare feet digging into the soft soil of the jungle floor. The air seemed the press in on him. He could hear the tree-snapping, crashing, crying sounds ahead. He smelled the air, and smelled sweat, urine and fear. He followed the smell, running fulltilt on the uneven jungle floor, sometimes on two legs it seemed, other times... on four. If he could only reach the man-figure... He knew that the dream would end. The riddle would be solved. He bounded over fallen trees, the slight sound of his barefeet on the cluttered path never disturbing the soft, surrounding stillness of the night. He could hear the man-figure up ahead. Close. Halted. The man was in a cramped oval opening in the jungle, thick walls of vines seeming to naturally weave together to create a sort of living fence around the obviously frightened preythepreyman. He watched the man spin in circles, wide-eyed and whispering a steady stream of curses as he searched for a way out of the dense thicket. Now he would get his answers, he thought in the blanket void of the dream. Now he would see the end. He is ours. The prey is ours. It is time. It is time. He called out to the man... but all that emanated from his mouth was a low, throaty growl. The man screamed, searching the underbrush for the origin of the sound, his sense of direction clouded by his utter, blinding fear. Now. It is now. He felt his lower muscles go taught, his legs feeling more like tightened springs than normal limbs. He was ready to pounce, he thought. I am ready to pounce and I love it. His lips pulled back from his teeth in a mindless sneer, a thin line of drool spilling from his lips to the soft vegetation below. The low growl began again, back in his throat... and then begin to build in short, panting barks. He felt himself go just then, as if his mind went on autopilot. The idea of being outside oneself came to mind, but that wasn't it either... he was still there, he just let something else take the wheel. His barks became louder, harsher, more intense as his breath quickened. The man-figure could hear him now, but that was fine. He couldn't run. The man-figure backpedalled away from the sound, his eyes wide. He tripped over a root poking out of the earth like a rotten tooth and sat down hard. His legs still kicked into the mud, as if trying to fend away whatever he couldnt see, but could hear getting closer.... Whatever it was that he could sense hunched behind the bushes It'sme.Iambehindthebushes. The figure screamed. The creature pounced. ********** "Nnnnnnng" Mufanga Kagos half-yelled as he shot upright in the bed. He looked around wildly at his surroundings. A motel. In Peru. He was just dreaming. The dream again. This time he got further in it and saw more of it before he woke up with another scream caught just behind his lips. He still didn't understand it at all, but it was recurring more frequently now. More vivid. More real. "Fuck it. It's just a dream." Mufanga said to the dark hotel room. He needed a piss. And then, more than anything, he needed more sleep. It seemed that lately he was tired all the time. He got to his feet in the pitch-black night and crossed the small space to the cramped bathroom. Leaving fresh, muddy footprints across the cheap motel carpet.
