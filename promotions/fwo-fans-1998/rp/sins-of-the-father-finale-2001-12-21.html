Sins Of The Father - Finale

21 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Click HERE for the Prologue Click HERE for Part 1, May 31, 1974 Click HERE for Part 2, April 1, 1974 Click HERE for Part 3, Febuary 17, 1977 Ric was excited. This had been the fourth day that his father had let Ric work in the small ring that they had in the garage. Although only 12, Ric felt that he had shown his father that he was ready to learn - that he, too, wanted to be a wrestler. Ric bounded down the stairs, but something caused him to stop abruptly. He could hear his parents arguing again - something that had become all too common the last few weeks. He knew he shouldn't eavesdrop, but the temptation was too much. Sitting down behind the banister, Ric slid into a position where he could hear all, but see nothing - and hopefully avoid being seen as well. Mike: "But, Eleni, please try to understand - this is our son we're talking about." Eleni: "He's just like you, Mike. He's not MY son." Ric was shocked. They were talking about him, and none too pleasantly. Mike: "But think about what you're saying here - you'll destroy his life! Don't hold him accountable for a mistake made over twelve years ago!" The phrase hit Ric like a physical blow. Now, he was a MISTAKE? Eleni: "You should have thought of that back then. To hell with you both, you cheap bastard. I hope you rot, along with that cheap Chinese whor-." Ric clearly heard a slap, followed by a gasp of astonishment, and then the slam of a door. He paused for a few seconds, uncertain of what to do. He heard movement at the tops of the steps, and looked up, only to see his older sister, Darcy, at the top of them, crying. Darcy: "Mom just left. I saw her from the window." Ric was stunned. His mother had left - because of him? Because he wanted to be a wrestler? Because Ric was a mistake? Running down the stairs, Ric threw open the front door. He cried out for his mother, but she was already halfway down the street, the familiar taillights of her VW Beetle fading. Regardless, he pursued as far as his small legs could take him, but to no avail. Finally, when he was exhausted, he stopped, air ramming painfully into his lungs. When he turned, he saw his father there, tears streaming down his face, matching Ric's own. Ric reflected that this was the first time he had ever seen his father cry. Mike picked up Ric, and Ric was too exhausted to fight it. Hugging his son close, Mike slowly walked back to the house, speaking calm, soothing words. Mike was just as confused. He had told Eleni about the incident with Sun Chen, and Eleni had reacted much as Mike had expected. He confessed the incident because of its results, and the guilt that he couldn't live with. Of course, Eleni couldn't live with it either, and had no intentions of doing so. Once he had placed Darcy and Ric in bed, Mike contemplated his next action. He looked at their angelic, sleeping faces, and he wondered at how he would explain that their mother wasn't coming back. He knew that they were being punished for his indiscretion - and he knew that he could never make it up to them. He closed the doors to their respective bedrooms, and felt a pain seize him unlike any he had ever experienced. He was used to the various physical ailments that came with being a wrestler, but this malady was unlikely to be repaired with either rest or physical therapy. Mike headed into the kitchen, and caught the liquor cabinet out of the corner of his eye. Normally, he wasn't one to imbibe, but sometimes, extraordinary circumstances led to extraordinary activities. He opened the cabinet, and pulled out a nearly full bottle of rum. An hour later, Mike had drained most of the cabinet of its contents. This was the first time, but it sure as hell wouldn't be the last. As long as he felt the pain, the emptiness inside, he would try and numb it - with alcohol.
