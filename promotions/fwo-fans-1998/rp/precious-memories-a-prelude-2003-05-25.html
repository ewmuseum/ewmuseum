Precious Memories ~ A Prelude

25 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A simple photograph, just sitting there on the end table. Its once transparent glass cover is now caked with a thick, marring layer of dust and grime. How long has it sat there, covered up beneath layers of neglect? It certainly could use a nice bit of polishing and a new frame wouldn't hurt. The two shadowy figures beneath the dirt would probably appreciate the make over. That would only happen, of course, assuming that the owner of this photograph wants to do such a thing. Is the brief instance in time recorded on this piece of glossy paper something that the owner wants to remember? Or, is it one of those occasions or people that are meant to be devoured by the countless layers of dust? Only the owner of the memory would be able to know the answer to that question... Or maybe even she doesn't... To look back on a life lived is to be bombarded with thousands of questions all at once. It's like opening the proverbial floodgates. At that point, the only real question that makes any sense is "Will I sink or swim?" The dusty picture was gripped between two bruised hands. Small clouds of the gray particles swirled in the warm ray of sunlight. Brought closer to the body of the woman, it remained there for a few moments. Her thoughts began to do what they always did
