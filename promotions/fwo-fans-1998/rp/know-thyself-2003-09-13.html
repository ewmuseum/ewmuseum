Know Thyself

13 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Things move pretty fast these days. The World is constantly hustling by us, and it's all we can do just to keep up with it. With everything in such a constant state of Chaos, people don't get a lot of time to stop and take a good, long look at themselves to figure out who they are. That's why so many people are screwed in the head. After all, you can't control something if you don't understand it. "You bastard!" Too many people have blinded themselves with their own bullshit. Too many people have fooled themselves into believing they're something they're not, something better than they really are. The World around them knows the truth and takes advantage of it, leaving the poor idiots drowning in the undercurrent wondering what the Hell happened and how the World got so cruel. The World isn't cruel. It's reality. Cold, hard reality. "Motherfucker!" Me, I've been quite realistic on who and what I am for a long time. I'm no rocket scientist. I'll never win a Nobel, a Pulitzer, or be invited to join Mensa. Not that I'm stupid, mind you. Just average, like most folk. "You ripped my shirt!" Ignore him. I'm making a point here. Now physically, nobody comes close to me. I've got the best physique this side of the Mr. Olympia, and nobody can touch me when it comes to power. Back when I weighed in around the four hundred pound mark, I'd grab sealed cans of Pepsi and squeeze them between my hands until they exploded. It takes six hundred pounds of pressure to pop the seal like that. Since dropping about eighty pounds, I might not be able to pull that off but I'm no slouch. I'm still the guy who pressed the three hundred pound Deacon over my head like he was a cruiserweight. That's my reality, all uncontestable facts. One of my favorite movies of all time is "Fight Club." There's plenty of blood, plenty of raw, healthy violence, and a few good messages too. I like the line that Ed Norton uses: "No fear. No distractions. The ability to let that which does not matter truly slide." That's how I've lived my life. I keep my eye on the prize and leave the bullshit behind. One sec. I have to hit this kid again. The young man lunged at Creed. Standing behind him, his girlfriend held a wad of bloody paper towels against her nose and mouth. Under the street light which lit up this far section of the club's parking lot, it was easy to see the bruising around her eyes. Her two girlfriends hugged either side of her while tears ran down her cheeks. Her sobs were muffled by the paper, making it almost sound like she was choking. Part of her wanted her boyfriend to kick her attacker's ass. Part of her knew he was just going to get himself hurt. The strapping young man grabbed Creed around the waist and drove him back against the car. Vic grabbed a handful of his hair and yanked his head up. He slammed his fist into the side of the young man's head once, twice. Creed's huge arm propelled his fist with the force of a wrecking ball, and it was doing it's job. Take these two for example. Sure they're been drinking. I've never let that be used as an excuse. This "I'm a different person when I drink" is horsepiss. You drink, it drops your inhibitions, and you become who you really are. They lie to themselves, but the booze brings out the truth. The girl is clearly a shit disturber with a blurred sense of reality. I was sitting at my table in the back of the club when she staggered up to me. Nice tits, full lips, I figured I was gonna get some right there at the table. But she parts those lips and started cussing me out, saying I'm a terrible father for abandoning my daughter and having her live with foster parents. An over-zealous wrestling fan, but that doesn't excuse her making an ass of herself. First, I'm a good father. I'm just a terrible person. Instead of keeping my daughter around me, I sent her away so she'd have a shot at having a non-screwed up life. Plus, I made sure she hated me so she didn't try come back to me. I'm an asshole. We both deserved better. Did it hurt to tear my own flesh and blood's heart out? Sure, for a moment. It passed. I dropped that emotional baggage like I'd drop a turd in the toilet bowl and flush it away. Nothing was going to distract me from my goals. Not my daughter, not my emotions, nothing. I don't care, and that works for me. Second, what the Hell did the drunk bitch think was going to happen when she walked up to a guy three times her size and started swearing at him? It's not like she didn't know who I was. Did she think I'd ignore her and behave like a gentleman? Fuck that. There's nothing gentle about Kodiak Vic Creed. Nothing. So I punched her in the face. Hang on. With blood trickling from his ear, the young man finally let go of his assailant. That didn't stop Vic from maintaining his hold. He jerked the young man up by the remains of his shirt and shook him like a rag doll. The smaller man chopped down on the inside of Creed's elbows in an attempt to get free. Vic pulled him forward and kneed him hard in the balls. He let the bloody man fall to his hand and knees before kicking him in the face. Creed grabbed a handful of hair, held his head against the car door, and drove his knee forward. Repeatedly. Once he was satisfied that the door had a large enough dent in it, he let the unconscious man drop to the cold, wet parking lot pavement amongst the dirt and cigarette butts. Creed looked down at him. He was bruised, bloody, his shirt tattered and torn. He looked like he'd been mauled by a bear which, Vic noted with a smile, he pretty much had been. This kid thought he'd be a hero and defend his idiot girlfriend. He should have just dragged her away and saved himself the abuse. There's nothing heroic about defending a lie. It just makes you look like a sap. Will either of these two learn from this? In a couple of weeks, their wounds will be healed. A month from now I'll just be some asshole in a story they'll tell their friends. Meanwhile, they'll go back to being the same delusional pissants they've always been. If I didn't already hear the police sirens, I'd drag them both to my car and take them back to my hotel room. I'd tie him to a chair and make him watch while I sodomized his girlfriend for a good hour or so. I'd force her to use her mouth to clean me off, letting her choke on my seed and her stool. Now that, THAT, is a lesson neither one of them would forget in a month. Every time they looked in the mirror, they'd relive the horror. They'd repeatedly ask why, why them. It'd make them question themselves. It'd bring them to reality and force them to see the Truth. Me, I live in Reality. I know the Truth. I am the Truth.
