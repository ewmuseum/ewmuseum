Blade Job - Chapter 4 - Wildman at Work

1 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Silver Fox was already at his desk when Ben arrived. He was staring at the computer screen, chuckling and sipping coffee. "What?" Ben asked, placing his cracked and beaten travel bag on the desk. "Poison Ivy emailed me a Shakespeare Insult Kit. I can't wait to try this on some of the guys around here." Fox grinned. "This is my favorite, and it's reserved for Samuel Smooth... Thou bawdy goat-fouling shrew." "Our Front office at work," Ben said wryly. Fox glanced at him closely, taking his eyes from the green-glowing computer screen. He had one foot propped on an extra chair, and kicked it in Ben's direction, offering him a seat. "Are you still feeling okay?" "For the millionth time, yes. The doc thinks I'm fit for active duty, and I think it's way overdue. I think my mind is permanently rotted from watching day-time TV." He pushed a hand through his shaggy black dreadlocked hair. Fox tossed a couple of files on his desk. Just more promotional packages. Fuck. Ben had thought this meeting was finally the one where Fox would ask him to suit up and come out for the show... get him on the lineup... No. It was just more promo work. Ben felt like he was turning into the fWo's Terry Taylor. "Good, take a look at these." Fox said. "You get to visit a kid's hospital... sign some autographs... and hopefully get the staff interested in buying tickets. And you need a haircut." "I always need a haircut." They were silent for a few minutes as Ben speed-read the promotions. Fox went back to reading his computer. Soon, Silver Fox broke the silence with a short gasp, and turned again to Ben. "Hey, did you hear about Kevin MacKay?" Ben felt a shiver of dread skitter through him. "Philly? No, what?" "He's dead. It's on the news. Happened sometime last night. Drowned in the shower. How do you drown in the shower?" Fox clicked away at his computer absent-mindedly and began reading the files. "I don't know. He called me yesterday, and I wasn't there." Ben was disturbed. This just didn't feel right. Silver Fox looked up. "Why would he call you? I know you guys did some stuff together at IPW, but I didn't know you were buddies." "I know. We weren't. That's what's so odd about it. Are they calling it an accident?" "As far as I know. But I ask you, how does one drown in the shower?" "I don't know."
