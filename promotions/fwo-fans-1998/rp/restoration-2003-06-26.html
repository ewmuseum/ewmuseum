Restoration

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Back to American, the land of opportunity as it would seem. Those words have new meaning, as Vilas Lavagna, known better as Padrona to Americans and most of the world's population, stepped off the jet that flew from Bangkok Thailand to America. Closely behind him was Sefina or Shygen, as the public knew her. Together they moved across the landing strip and made their way into the airport terminal. Padrona's mask was back over his face as they made their way into the public eye. Not like anyone would notice Vilas Lavagna anyway, but it was a trademark. "The land of opportunity, here we are," Shygen yelled out, getting a few looks from the American's rushing to their soon departing aircraft. Vilas stopped in the middle of airport, with people bumping and shoving around him and looking at the weirdo in the mask. To them, he could just have been a burn victim, but the occasional person knew who he was. "Opportunities are not what I need. It's either I rise to the occasion or I go back to Thailand," Padrona said, looking at the people as they walked past. Sefina joined him and looked him in the eyes. "I don't think they would allow you to just walk off the job again," she replied with a smirk. Padrona did not move. "No, if I fail, I will quit and give my spot to someone who can bring more to the table then I can. Someone who will earn the respect of the crowd and give it to someone who I believe has the right to be here. It's not a cowards way out, I will go back to Thailand and continue with my training, then maybe someday make a return." "That's a very respectable choice you could make, and one that could decide your life. It would take a great man to just walk away and give his spot to someone else. That's to be admired not be placed down." "I am here for a reason. The just do my job to the best of abilities. I will not allow anyone to stop me from performing. If I am to leave, I will leave because it is my choice and not the choice of a simple mistake that could be avoided. No Sefina, it shouldn't be admired because it's the honest thing to do." "We better move then. Time is ticking and you don't want to be late this time. Not now, and not ever again." Vilas and Sefina grabbed their bags and pulled them across the floor. Padrona took a quick glance at a few people starring his direction and smiled through his mask. Of course they couldn't see it, but it was there. "You know Shygen, this may be the best opportunity Padrona will ever have!"
