The Warning

19 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kali Saturos silently sat in the void, seemingly looking at nothing. Her eyes were black and empty. Her body was cold, frozen as if there was no longer anything alive inside of it. She knew not of any plan for her downfall. She knew not of any plan to bring about her end. But she was about to find out. "Kali, Kali, Kali. Foolish little Kali." .One chuckled. "Did you expect to get away from me? Did you expect that you could beat me in my own game?" Kali had her back against the wall, a nervous look on her face, as .One only laughed again. "You will remain in the void until I see fit to release you." .One said. "You will never interfere in my buisness again." And with that, .One simply walked away. "I can't believe I let that bastard beat me." Kali muttered. "Next time I see him, I'm so going to kick his overgrown as-" "Cursing is wrong in the eyes of God, Kali." Kali let out a deep, deep groan. "There's only one bitch in the world who could possibly make this worse." And indeed, as Kali turned around, she spotted her blonde-haired alter-ego, Jessica. "That's not a very nice name to call someone who's here to help you." Jessica scolded her. Kali rose up from the chair she was sitting on as gave out a disbelieving laugh. "You're going to help me?" Kali said, again trying to hold back her laughter. "That's a new one. Since when do you want to help the murderer, Child of God?" Jessica shrugged and sat down where Kali had previously been sitting. "You're a murderer, but you're still better than Rei." Jessica said. "At least you have faith in SOMETHING. She has faith in nothing but herself." Surprisingly, the bitch actually makes sense. Kali thought. Maybe we have more in common than I'd like to believe. "So it's Rei that's plotting against me then?" Kali asked. Jessica nodded. "With Chaos." Jessica said. "They both want to put an end to you." "Can't say that I'm surprised." Kali muttered. She turned back to Jessica. "Well, thank you for the warning." Kali said. Jessica nodded again, and turned to leave the void. After all, it wasn't her that .One had captured here. As she appeared on the other side, in a red brick room, she smiled a very un-Godly smile. "To all things, a beginning. And to all things, an end." In a flash of darkness, she was no longer Jessica. She had never been Jessica. She was something far greater, and far more evil than that. "To paraphase someone else I know, the game ends soon, Kali Saturos. Because this is all just a game."
