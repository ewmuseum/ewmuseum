Mega Man Battle Network: Cagle Man

26 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
So, anyway, after a nightmarish tower-like level filled with dangerous chasms, enemies that suddenly shoot out of said chasms so that they can headbutt you and send you spiralling into the chasms, the little helmet things that shoot a fan of pellets, spikes, and the obligitory Giant Animal of Flamey Death... the Codemaster finally reached the boss of this level. As soon as the door closed shut behind him, the Codemaster leaned against the door and sighed in relief. That's when the boss music hit. At the top of the room, a black bar suddenly filled up to its top with green lines. Then HE appeared. Oh yeah, you're shaking now, biotch. The Codemaster simply glared at the green atrocity that stood before him, before he spoke the first few words that came to his mind, "Carson Covington?" "No, ye retarded goat! Ah...am.... CAGLE MAN! Ah command the powers of... well, shit, ye know.... Cagleness!" Cagle Man responded. The Codemaster blinked, and scratched his head nervously. He looked around the tall, wide, but empty room, which was featureless aside from Cagle Man, who was in front of him, tapping his foot impatiently and looking at his watch. "Cagleness? What does this imply?" Cagle Man held up a really, really big boomstick and aimed it at the Codemaster, before he spoke, "Aw sheeyit, ye know. Wavin' around mah boomstick and makin' stereotypical redneck sayin's as Ah do so. Junk lahk that therre." He fired, but the Codemaster jumped over the slow-moving shot. "GAH! Dag-gum blasted slow-movin project-ahls! Ah knew Ah paid too much for this here boomstick!" Cagle Man complained, before he had to react quickly by tossing his boomstick at the Codemaster. The Codemaster fell to the ground, stunned by the attack. That's when Cagle Man turned his arm into a large cannon, with a hen house sticking out of the top of it. He smiled and pointed it at the Codemaster, "Eat feathered farre, ye piece of chicken dumplin'!" He fired a chicken at the Codemaster. It made several frightening clucking noises as it travelled toward the Codemaster. The Codemaster tried to dodge to a side, but found that he couldn't since he's supposed to be in a two-dimensional game, so he ducked instead. "Hey, ye aint supposed t' duck!" Cagle Man said, transforming his chicken launcher back into a regular hand again, before he pulled out a large book. "Article Fahve, Chapta Fo'! The protagonist of any Mega Man game CAN_NAWT DUCK!" "Suckah, you're trippin'. Mega Man X5 had duckin'." "This ain't no X game, you fool!" Cagle aimed the chicken launcher at the Codemaster again. "Man, what the hell kind of a weapon is a CHICKEN launcher, anyway? They don't even explode!" the Codemaster said. "Union regulations. The PETA woulda killed us all if they'd a let me use explosive poultry!" The Codemaster shrugged, before he pointed his weapon right at Cagle's face. "Aw, hell." BLAM! And suddenly, for no explained reason, Cagle Man exploded into multiple little particles that didn't even match what he looked like to begin with. The Codemaster sighed and glanced idly at the only thing that was left of Cagle Man, which happened to be the chicken launcher. Words appeared at the top of the room. "GET EQUIPPED WITH CAGLE LAUNCHER." The Codemaster sneered at those words. "Oh, HELL no. There ain't no way you're making me take that weapon." The Codemaster left the room. He ran back into the room moments later, trying to fight off some killer worms, before he quickly picked up the "OKAY! OKAY! I'll get equipped with the silly-ass chicken launcher! Damn!"
