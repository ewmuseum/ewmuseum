fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Congratulations. February 07, 2000 It was two days before Warfare was scheduled to take place, but yet Dead had already arrived. He sat on the ground, slumped up against the wall of a familiar building.. The building where Goldberg defeated Hulk Hogan for the wcw world heavyweight title in July of 1998. The building where Michael Cole screamed "dog poop" repeatedly; thus making a fool out of himself. The building known only as The Georgia Dome. Over forty thousand fans would be there, and so would Dead in some capacity. Congratulations. Congratulations, Kid Cool, you've moved yourself up on my hitlist. You see, when you made it known that you were Travis' son you made yourself a target. You've got a big bullseye on your forehead now as a result. If you thought getting some gum in your hair was a bad thing to happen to you; well, you obviously haven't experienced hell. You haven't experienced me breathing down your neck in an attempt to decimate your career. And you can believe me when I tell you, your career will never be the same again. At some point in the near future, I will strike.. I will sink my proverbial teeth into you... and I won't let go. You obviously have a death wish and when the opportunity is precise... I will grant you it. Congratulations, Pierre, you managed to avoid having to face me on Slaughter which would no doubt result in you losing that treasured piece of tin of yours. I wouldn't need to pin someone else to win the match, either. Nor would I have deported you back to France. I merely would have physically broken you down in the ring before pinning you cleanly. It's that simple. In fact, it's perfectly simple, as Scott would say. Finally, I'd like to congratulate Hiroshima. Hiroshima has come to the realization that life in the fWo would not become any better, not that it was any good to begin with. Especially for him. You disrespected this man's heritage, you spit on him, you kicked him while he was down. For what? For who? This man gave you his word, he gave you his honor, he gave you his pride, he gave you his blood... he gave you all his career, and you treated him like shit. You treated him like a dog. With luck, hopefully the rest of you will come to the conclusion he finally came to. Hopefully a few of you will come to the realization that you've been disrespected, you've been mistreated, you've been misused... and that is why I and Scott offer you our hand. We offer you our help. Why would we help you? Simple. We're going to attack from within. Help us help you. From here on out, and I hate to use this overused cliche... you're either with us, or you're against us. That's not a promise, that's not a guarantee... That is my word. Trust me. Dead stood up and outstretched his arms in the infamous crucifixion pose, before rain began to trickle from the clouds over head. Thunder roared in the background loudly as the scene faded to black.
