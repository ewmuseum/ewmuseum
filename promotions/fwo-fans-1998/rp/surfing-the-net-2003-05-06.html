Surfing the 'Net

6 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wippit was enjoying a week off in his suburban Toronto home - just because he's from Dildo, doesn't mean he lives there - spending time with his wife and son while he can. His schedule is about to pick up, and he wants to get in as much family time as he can. Course, with the munchkin asleep, and his love deeply ingrossed in "Survivor" (blech!), Wippit took a little time to himself, and decided to surf the internet. After quickly checking if 3 or 4 email addresses (and clearing out his spam-address on hotmail to prevent people sending him junk mail), he pulled up Internet Explorer to check the 'smark' news. Smark: noun, "smart mark" 1. Someone who thinks he knows everything there is to know about wrestling. doesn't care much for gimmicks or angles. just likes good wrestling. What better place to start than the top of the mountain, and Wippit keyed in http://www.pwtorch.com "Need some music," he muttered, as he opened his substantial MP3 directory and loaded up a random song - "Alive" by Edwin began to play. "Ok..." he said as the Pro Wrestling Torch opened up, followed immediately by about 8 popups. "Dammit, I hate popups!" After closing all the windows, Wippit began to browse a couple of the columns, but each click brought up more pop-up windows. "To hell with this!" he said out loud, and changed pages to http://411wrestling.com By now, Edwin had stopped playing, and he just hit the random button for his MP3 player. Much to his dismay, the song that came up was the one he hated must in life: Crack that whip Give the past the slip Step on a crack Break your momma's back Wippit snorted, and let the song play as he noticed the big flashing headline at the top of the screen. AMY CAMPBELL NUDE PICTURES INSIDE!!! AIMZ GETS HARDCORE!!! ELI FLAIR CAUGHT FREEBASING!!! MORE!!! CLICK HERE!!! "Woo! Aimz nude!" exclaimed WIppit, as he clicked on the link about 10 times. But his face fell, as the link did not lead to the promised flesh of the fWo Hardcore champion. Instead, it was a bunch of points that had little to no basis in reality. "Jade has heat with Aimz backstage... Eli Flair not related to Ric Flair... Villam Ender get penis reconstruction... what kind of crap is this?" Fed up with the crap on 'news sites', Wippit shut off Internet Explorer, turned on Jedi Knight 2, and spent the next three hours thrashing people with a light saber.
