Even more pissed off

30 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(At first, all we can see is static, but gradually, we can make out the outline of a person trying to adjust the camera. As the static clears up, it becomes obvious that the man doing the adjusting is Mike Bear and it is in the Underground's locker room, since Fear and Wretch are in the background) Fear: C'mon, don't you know how to work that thing? Mike: Man shut the hell up, these things get more complicated everyday. It's been a while. Wretch: Where's Geoff? Why do we have to produce this ourselves? Mike: Do you know how much money we lost with Armstrong in charge? Travis instated drastic budget cutbacks just so we can stay on the air. Wretch (getting sad): Oh man, don't tell me Geoff is fired. We'll never be able to drive together again? Mike: No, he's not fired, but the camera crews for interviews and promos are reserved for main event wrestlers, and we're not there yet. Fear: But we'll be there soon. Mike: Damn right. Man, I almost forgot that thing was running. (gets more serious) The Underground has got a lot on its mind given recent events. First of all, there is the matter of the Tag Team championship. For a month Wretch and I tried to get the number one contenders spot we so rightly deserve, since we were never pinned for the titles, and for a month, Cactus Jon tried to argue that his team was more deserving, despite the fact they had never wrestled together. Well, we won the number one contender's spot, and Spencer I'll get to your involvement later, but just two days later we fought Ultra Cool 2000 for the belts we never lost, and Kid Cool decided that since he's an insignificant speck on the history of the Tag Team titles, and that he would have no chance at EVER beating the Underground, he walked out on our match. Well Baby Cool, that will not make us go away. You have done more than anger me, I am GOD DAMN ENRAGED! You will not get away with this! I will hunt you until the Underground has won back the Tag Team titles from you or until you die! Do you understand Baby Cool? You are noth... (Finally, Fear and Wretch step in and try to settle Mike down, since he had begun pacing around the room and yelling. Fear grabs Mike in a bear hug from behind while Wretch tries to talk some sense into Mike. Mike squirms at first, but settles down eventually and Fear releases him) Wretch: Kid, you see what you've done? You can't stand up to this man when he gets like that. There's not a whole lot of people in the fWo who can. When we get you back in the ring, it will be a war. Next time, you won't be walking out on our match, because they'll be taking you away on a stretcher. You two idiots had better defeat the Lone Gunmen, because it'll be so much more satisfying to beat the two of you. Mike: Thanks, Wretch. Now, I have two more people to address, and they're names are David Spencer and Travis Beaven. First of all, even though there were two Travises on Countdown and the one I'm addressing seems to have disappeared, I wanted to say that no one, but no one, makes fun of Collins except ME! I have that copyrighted! Although, I would like to thank you for getting rid of Armstrong, because there's not many people, if any, that are happier to see him leave than me. Now, unfortunately, I think that the wrong Travis disappeared. Travis, you better rethink who you just sided with. There's not a soul in the fWo that doesn't hate Spencer. Even his own minions hate him or are two deranged to know what hate is anymore. The entire fWo will rise up against you and your champion. You will fall. Now, onto you Spencer. I already had a problem with you when you interfered in the number one contenders match before Twilight. Now that you've formed some kind of alliance with Travis, you will be brought down as well. As the Boston Strangler said, "All empires are overthrown, every dynasty falls apart, and all foundations eventually crumble." Your day will come soon, Dave. The fWo tossed you out once, and it'll happen again. Fear, tell these fools. Fear: Kid Cool, Death, Travis, and all who stand in our way, you will all Fear...The...Underground. (With that Mike pulls out a brick and throws it at the camera. As the picture turns to static once more, Wretch can be heard asking Mike why he did that, to which he replies "Fuck Travis." The shot fades to black)
