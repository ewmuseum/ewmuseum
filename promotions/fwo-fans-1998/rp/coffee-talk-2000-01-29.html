Coffee Talk

29 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Coffee Talk January 29, 2000 Chris Finn is seen walking down the street in Miami. The camera follows him as he turns in a local coffee shop. The shop is dark and dreary, so the camera turns on a portable light. The locals look up squinting, and begin mumbling at the crew. Chris Finn spots Oracle in the back corner with a coffee and a magazine. Finn: "Oracle, may we have a few words with you?" Oracle looks up disgusted, and closes the magazine. Oracle: "What do you want now Finn? I'm trying to read ESPN Magazine here." Finn: "I'm sorry but we'd like to ask you a few questions about your upcoming match this Sunday?" Oracle: "Oh that? Another great vision by the fWo Bookers. 'Hey let's have a four way match!' Can't these idiots think up anything better than that? What is this WCW?" Finn: "Umm. Anyhow. You attacked Matt Douglas on Warfare. Any reasons behind this attack?" Oracle: "Finn. You really are stupid aren't you? Matt Douglas you got invovled with me, and now there's no escape. You're Trapped." Finn: "Speaking of Trapped...Do you have any plans for the big PPV?" Oracle: "Well, Douglas. You and me. You name the type of match and I'll be there." Finn: "Now, don't get ahead of yourself. Wretch and Courage will also be in this match on Sunday." Oracle: "Wretch? Who? Oh you mean Chris Kidman Jr?" Finn: "Umm. I guess so." Oracle: "I have no problem with this freak, whatever his name is. Courage the same goes for you, but if you fellas get in my way then I guess I'll need to kick your ass." Finn: "Well, good luck, this Sunday, Oracle. Finally, any prediction for the Super Bowl?" Oracle: "Finn, you know very well that MY Steelers should have been in the Super Bowl, but were robbed all season long. Now get out of here." Finn: "Thanks for you time." Oracle: "Hey Finn, FEAR THE DREAMER!" With that Finn signals the camera to cut as the scene fades to black
