The Art of Self Destruction, Phase V (Denial, Hate And Fear)

19 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This can't be happening. Not to someone like me. I can't go through this again. I'm weak enough as it is, this could be the final stake in my heart. I'm stronger than he is, better, mentally stronger. It can't win. I will triumph... I'll make it through... I'll see tomorrow... I can do this. * * * Those thoughts echoed throughout his mind. That hollow, empty, void between his ears. It was the only thing that he had control of. That control was hanging on by a single strand of thread that could be broken at any moment. * * * I'm so perfect inside of your head. We're the perfect fit for each other. You do all of the grunt work. I'm the master behind your madness. Nobody can see what happens inside of you, no one needs to know what you're going through. And every time you go against what I say, you'll get the feeling inside of you like a teacher scraping her fingernails across the blackboard. That hideous screeching. It will make you cover your ears and wish it all away. You're not going to win again. Don't be ridiculous. Your resistance is just a mere fight towards inevitability. You're not going to turn me into a lifeless machine. How do you know that's not already happening? I can feel it. You're changing and you don't even know it, funny. Why... do you do this to me? Why not? * * * I really hate this. I can feel the anger boiling in my blood, surging through my veins. And there's nothing I can do about it. If I could just take a gun, load it, put it into my mouth and pull the trigger to make this thing leave me alone I wou... * * * There's nothing I can do. You're finally seeing the truth. I'm scared... You shouldn't be. I can't help it. I'm afraid of what will happen to me. You're in good hands...
