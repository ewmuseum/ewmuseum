What you *didn't* see on Slaughter....

13 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tammy Faye had waited for this moment for six months. Finally, it was time. Michael was going to reveal his love for Jennifer. But, before that, Todd was going to show up and proclaim HIS love for Jennier, and claim that she was having his child. However, everyone who was anyone knew that it was truly Bryce's child. All the while, Steven and William were going to have buttsex for the first time; however, William was afraid that it would hurt. So Steven assured him that his penis was quite small, and he wouldn't feel a thing. William asked "What's the point?". So, the two thought about it for quite some time, agitating Tammy Faye. She wanted to see the ice skating rink go up in flames. However, she wouldn't be seeing any part of it. Instead, the screen was flickering. And then...he showed up. Sphere. On mid-day television. "I love to interrupt your ridiculous soap opera," began Sphere, "but it's not even an opera. It's on a television set. For the love of all things Pete. Go get a job. "However, since you're here, I want to show you, at 2:33 p.m. on Monday afternoon, what was SUPPOSED to air last night, but didn't because of something I can't talk about. That'd be all about breaking kayfabe and whatnot. So...here." *** Sphere walked through the halls, obviously looking for someone. He turned a corner, and found two people instead. Black Quicksilver and Jon Crisp. "Ah, just the idiots I was looking for," said Sphere. "Wouldn't it be 'idiots I WERE looking for'?" asked Black Quicksilver. "No...I is singular, therefore it's was," said Sphere, matter-of-factly. "No, moron," interjected Crisp, "it's were. Good Lord, you're a fucking idiot." "Whatever, Funpants," said Sphere. "What.Ever." He held his hand up in a formation of a W, which is the drunk college girl signal for, well...whatever. Crisp grimaced, as did the viewers at home (we're sure on that one, trust us). "Ahem," Sphere spoke up. "What?! We have a match tonight!" Crisp yelled. "Actually...no, I got that pushed back to Wednesday." "You...what?" Crisp's eyes narrowed. "Look, Funpants, stop burrowing your brow. I'll take the back of my hand to you if you're not careful. Remember, I sign your paychecks." "I am not Funpants!" "Who cares, those look like you need a shoehorn to get out of them," said Sphere. "Anyway, your match is now on Wednesday." "Why?" said BQ, turning to Crisp. "Sorry, I didn't have anything funny to say there." "Because the road agents were busy watching the peephole in the women's loc...erm, I mean...it's because I'm a mega-heel. I get changes done to matches of those I hate. And since we're on the second stop of the "Sphere hates Black Quicksilver and Funpants Tour", your match is now on Wednesday." "Can I bust his face open?" Crisp said to BQ. BQ shrugged. "Man, I'm just off with my hilarity tonight," BQ said, looking down, dejected. "Now, if you'll excuse me," Sphere said, interrupting, "I have to leave, so everyone can start wondering where Sphere is." Sphere walked off, leaving Crisp and BQ to themselves. "Great, we don't have a match tonight," Crisp said, rather ticked off. "Now what?" They thought for a moment. Then, BQ had an idea. "You could toss me into some boxes! That's always funny!" And somehow, Crisp smiled. "Yeah...let's go toss you into some boxes, you idiot." "Whoo!" And off went the most unlikely tag team...well, since the beginning of the sun. *** Meanwhile, Tammy Faye shot her television four times thirty seconds into the telecast. But it still played for the other 2.1 million women that were home.
