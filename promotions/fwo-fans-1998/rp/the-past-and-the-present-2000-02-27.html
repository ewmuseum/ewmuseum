fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization The Past and the Present. February 27, 2000 [::The scene begins in a far off place, a far away land. Muscle beach in San Fransisco. The sky is blue, the sun is blazing and the same two men are working out as they have for most of their lives. Tony Little aKa Thunder and Mike Johnson aKa Lightning sit across from eachother staring into a television screen, around them are weights, and exercise machines, surrounded by vast beach and water. The two men stare with great interest at the Television screen which appears to be showing fWo:Trapped. The Straightjacket Match between the Boston Strangler and Rain has just dismissed, and the two men turn away from the set as Rain is being helped off. Tony Little stands and turns off the PPV, and sits down on a benchpress table, Tony Little is a smaller man with large bulging muscles and an army style haricut, while Mike Johnson has smaller muscles, is a little taller and has longer hair. The two men begin to talk while staring solemnly at the ground.::] Tony Little: You know..... this isnt right. Mike Johnson: I know, you know we cant do anything though, he isnt the same man anymore. Tony Little: He's just too fragile now adays, What Dead just did may have been the last straw, he may be gone now. Mike Johnson: I know, I havent talked to him for months, at least not since just before he was accepted into the fWo. Tony Little: I dont get how this has happened ! You remember back a couple years ? He was our friend, I mean he was everyone's friend. After Wrestlefest and his... injury, he just wasnt the same, he just kept getting worse. Mike Johnson: Remember when he came back to wrestling ? We thought it was just a phase and that if we humoured him he would snap out in no time. Tony Little: Were we ever wrong. Mike Johnson: It seems so close that we were here joking around, now he's confused, engulfed in some strange power. You know it wasnt just the injury he suffered, he wasnt ever supposed to walk again, it was basically guaranteed that he wouldn't walk again. Something happened and we just don't know what. Tony Little: We cant ask him, I mean nobody ever knows where he is, and when he does touch into his old self, its rare. Mike Johnson: Im not sure if what Dead did tonight was the last straw or not, but I just hope he fights through it. Its out of our hands now. Tony Little: Im sure he knows what he's doing. Mike Johnson: I hope so. Crazy bastard. [Fade out]
