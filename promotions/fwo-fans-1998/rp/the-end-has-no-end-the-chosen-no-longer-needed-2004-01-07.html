The End Has No End (The Chosen No Longer Needed)

7 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dec. 25th, 2000 The cold hard barrel of the gun pressed firmly against Jack's temple. He laid on the ground of his apartment floor with his face buried deep into the carpet. The man sat on his back and with one hand held Jack's two wrist together -even though they had been handcuffed- and with the other hand he held a silver .9 mm to Jack's head. Moving across the room Noah sat in a chair tied down by his wrist as well with the barrel of a shotgun pointed at his chest. Random screaming from a woman was heard in one of the apartment rooms. That woman was Jack's wife. Lets take this back a bit. Noah and Jack moved out together to live in a small apartment, both got the marks and made post secondary school and surprisingly excelled in all classes. It was strange that since Jack had met Noah his life had changed for the better. After graduating (Jack did before Noah since he was two years older) Jack immediately got into the world of business when he signed on as an Accountant for the company "3M" -their responsible for your scotch tape and other random office supplies-. Jack became very successful at such a young age. When Noah graduated from post-secondary school Jack immediately got him hired on along with him. Noah was his second hand man, they were together always, best friends for life is what they always said. Jack met a woman, her name isn't important but nonetheless they were engaged and then married. Noah on the other hand was a charming bachelor enjoying the club scene and countless parties. However. Life isn't always *that* good. In life, whenever it seems to be going good, the forces amongst us seemed to throw those little troubles at you to see if your strong enough to get past them. On Christmas of 2000, the forces threw a little trouble. Let's get back to the story, Jack had invited Noah over to his apartment to spend Christmas with him and his wife. After all Noah had no one else to spend it with so why not? Unfortunately, five men, knowing about the wealth that both Jack and Noah attained decided to drop by as well. Jack, Noah, and Jack's wife were about to sit down and eat when the men stormed in with guns, Jack's wife was taken to a room...her screams are still heard. Noah sat in his seat calmly throughout the ordeal and is still there, he has yet to say a word. Jack on the other hand tried to struggle and was brought to the ground, handcuffed and held at gunpoint. And so on. "Now listen to me 'Shaft' you tell us the code to your safe deposit box that you and fag boy on the chair share and we'll let you two go safely." The man who sat on his back demanded this is a demeaning manner. Noah sat and watched, waiting for what Jack would say. However when Jack looked over to Noah he noticed that he was whispering, the men around couldn't hear a word he said nor did they notice that he spoke, but Jack heard every sound, Noah repeated this over and over again, "Tell them the code Jack." Jack shook his head and struggled to speak with the pressure applied to his chest by the weight of the man, "No Noah, that's our savings, I can't do it." The men holding the guns looked strangely at Jack, as they did not even hear Noah speak. They looked to Noah who had now closed his eyes, "Please Jack, tell them the code, this your last chance." "What do you mean my last chance? I am not telling them the code Noah!" Jack spoke staring at Noah waiting for another response. The man on top of Jack shook his head, "If you won't tell us then we'll have to make you," in his hand was a walkie talkie, he raised it to his mouth, "Skin the rabbit," Jack's expression changed immediately, "Skin the rabbit? What the fuck does that mean!? What the fuck does that MEAN!?!" Jack's question was answered by two gunshots and no more screams from his wife. Three men walked out from the room his wife had been taken to, one of the men had a few blood stains on his face and shirt. Jack screamed, Noah's fist tightened. The gun pressed harder against Jack's face, "Now what is the fucking code? The code! What is the fucking code!? Speak up shitface or end up like your wife!" The rope across Noah's arms split and Noah was up in an instance, he grabbed a hold of the shotgun barrel and pushing it to the side, one shotgun blast was shot off but Noah thrust his knuckle into the man's throat taking him to the ground. Noah ripped the shotgun from his hand and let go another shot into the man's stomach. Seeing this happen the man that was holding the gun to Jack's head raised the gun to point it at Noah, who by this time, had reloaded the shotgun. The man raising the gun let off one shot which hit Noah in the leg and brought him down to one knee, Jack was up at this point and he kicked the man in the back which sent him forward into the oncoming bullets by the three men that had just killed Jack's wife. The man fell dead and now only three of the men that had broken in remained alive. Immediately they took positions and Jack ran and jumped over his couch to hide from gunshot, after all he was still handcuffed. Noah braced himself and he let off another shot which hit the man closest to him sending him hard against the wall only to slide down it slowly and dead. Jack had grabbed the gun that the man had held to is temple and he jumped up from behind the couch screaming as he did and firing at the man closest to the doorway. The man took four bullets in his chest and one that ripped through his right eye and slammed his body hard to the ground. The fifth and final man dropped his gun and held his hands up high in the air. Jack immediately looked to Noah who showed visible pain and was looking at his leg, "Are you okay Noah?" Noah looked up to him, "Yeah, I think so...you okay?" Jack turned and looked at the man who held his hands up in surrender and fired a bullet into his head. The man dropped in a dead faint and Jack looked back to Noah, "I am now." Dropping the gun he walked around the couch and moved past Noah, Noah stared as he watched Jack enter the bedroom. In a few moments Jack would let lose a howling cry from the bedroom and his emotional crying could be heard echoing from the cold room. Noah dropped the shotgun and moved a few forward grabbing the telephone and dialing 9-1-1. In a few moments the phone would pick up and Noah would speak, "Uh..hello...yes, there has been a break and entering...five men are dead and I'm injured. One woman is dead as well....we need help...please." Noah continued on to give the address, after he hung up the phone he slumped down on his back. Noah did feel for Jack, Jack was his best friend and he had just lost his wife. He knew Jack would never forgive himself for letting his wife die but all Noah could think of was... How much he fucking hated Christmas.
