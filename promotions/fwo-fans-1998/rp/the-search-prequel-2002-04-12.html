The Search (Prequel)

12 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He wasn't making things any easier. Christ, if there were one constant about the man, it was that. A long time had passed since simple words had shaken her, but her meeting with Chris Universal on Sunday had done just that. Partly because the man who was, in all reality, Pierre's boss hadn't clue one as to his whereabouts. More unsettling, however was his suggestion that her friend, her confidant, her lover had somehow slipped marriage in under her radar. And, apparently, the radar of anyone he'd worked with. Jasmin Desjardin had spent several hours after Slaughter trying to find anyone on the crew who knew him well enough to have a conversation deeper than "Hey, how are you tonight?", and much to her surprise, hadn't found anyone. Seems that Pierre hadn't bothered much to associate with anyone who hadn't joined the FWO in its first six months. She'd told him years ago that he'd have to adjust to change, that it was the nature of the business. Of all of the lessons she'd tried imparting on him, he chose that one to ignore. Prick. She did come across one piece of luck. After meeting with Universal, she'd sought out Scott Wasner, recognizing his name from one of Pierre's lengthier and more frequent diatribes about the unjust positioning in the FWO. She'd written up a quick note for him, and passed it to one of the stagehands, who promised in turn to give it to Scott. He must have kept his end of the bargain, because two days later a fax came to her Paris training facility, not much more than a list of names and phone numbers, and a quick warning that some of them were, in all likelihood, terribly outdated and useless. Still, it provided a start. She recognized a lot of the names: Chris, Brian, Mike, Clarence, Vanessa. Others were less familiar, but still warranted a follow up. Jasmin would find Pierre, and when she did, he had some explaining to do. This she swore.
