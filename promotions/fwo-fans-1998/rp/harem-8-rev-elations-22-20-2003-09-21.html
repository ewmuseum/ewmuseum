Harem - 8 - Rev(elations) 22:20

21 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was days after a verbal war with her father - Who was the first man in her life to find out. Needless to say, that was a hellish experience... Hopefully this one would be a little easier, but she seriously doubted so. Amy'd perfectly planned the way she'd tell him...a perfectly indirect, very 'her' way. It began while she was curled up on one end of the couch, set against its puffy black leather arm rather comfortably - That image harshly contrasting her nervousness...hell, downright terror. ''So I'm thinking...'' She started, not even sure or caring if she had Brandon's full attention, ''That we've gotta plan those little speeches couples always have to do at weddings. Y'know, thanking people for being there, yadda yadda yadda...'' Before he could utter so much as a word, the redhead continued. The Mighty Hero actually seemed a little shocked by her organization - normally his fiancée had a tendancy to be the more scatter-brained out of them. ''Okay, wanna hear what I've got?'' Amy smiled and cleared her throat without so much as waiting for a nod. ''Ahem...'kay, here goes. 'Hey, I'm pregnant!'.'' Brandon's blood ran cold. Glancing over to the suddenly, obviously nervous bundle of energy he loved so much he was at a loss for words. That is, until he realized she was joking. Right? "Yeah, that'd be something wouldn't it? You stand up, clear your throat, everyone thinks something poetics about to be said and you come out with 'I'm pregnant'. That'd be good for a laugh." Brandon nervously smirked. ''...And then come out a week later with a newborn in my arms.'' Amy finished his sentence and raised her arm slightly to show she was shaking - This was for real. ''That's not an actual speech, not even a halfway feasible concept,'' She continued, ''But it was the least harsh way I could find to tell you.'' "Tell me wha... wait..." Brandon's head hurt. "A week later..." His head spun, "Oh my God." He rolled toward his new fiancée on the couch, closing the gap and sweeping her up in his arms and laying her across his lap as he embraced her strongly. Assuringly. ''So that's why...'' The little redhead adjusted herself, sitting halfway up. ''I've been strange, sick and had a mysterious 'injury'. Over three months along now, and I found out at the beginning of this one.'' She noticed Brandon's conufsed, suddenly slightly hurt expression. ''And why I didn't tell you? Well...remember what happened when Eli found out Angel was pregnant? He got hurt. With Vic Creid around you like he's been, I didn't want anything to get too messed up...but hiding it sort of went past a reasonable point now, and I'm hoping this won't lead to anything harmful.'' Using his large frame, Brandon moved Aimz off of him to the side of the couch and stood, not really indicating what he was thinking. Amy wrung her hands tightly, trying to discern what was going on in her soon-to-be-husband's head. "Harmful... harmful?" Brandon shot her a look, confusing her even more. He reached down and took her hand, pulling her to her feet. "Amy... honestly, I can't say that I'm too happy about being kept in the dark like this... I mean God... look what you and Winters just went through!? It's... it's okay right? The baby I mean. I mean, look at everything you've put yourself through." Brandon pulled her to him, hugging tightly again, as it would protect her from everything circling around, gathering. "I'm also a little pissed that I wasn't given the chance to hear the heartbeat and stuff for the first time either... but, I guess I can see your reasoning." Chuckling a bit under his breath, Brandon continued, "Plus, we know that you're not the poster child for well thought out plans either..." ''The baby's fine,'' assured Amy, ''I found out when I was being checked over thanks to the match with Winters, so that was the first thing on my mind.'' Amy smiled, and Brandon was smiling as an almost direct result. ''And the plan not to tell you did have thought behind it.'' She pouted, ''I was battling myself over it all month, and even Angel thought it best to keep from you for a little while...and she actually makes good choices. Ivy agreed too, but I'm not liable for how accurate she is in advice.'' With a wink, she backed up enough to lock eyes with Roche. ''So let's get this straight...you're not mad, you're not freaking out and most of all you're not going to end up hurting yourself trying to do right by this?'' "How on earth would I hurt myself by doing right by this? And, yes, I am mad. That doesn't mean I have to yell and throw things though. I'm not your last boyfriend, or the last few boyfriends for that matter. You should know that by now... I'd hope you would at least." Plucking her lightly on the nose to mimic her usual response, the tall Irishman reiterated his point, "I know you need friends to talk to about things but the fact that three people knew before me, at least, irks me a bit. But Amy... we're having a little Frontier!" The grin that followed was broad, and natural. Brandon's mind still swirled with all of the uneasiness that had been at the edge of their consciousness for so long. He pushed those thoughts back as best he could though to focus on Amy. To focus on what mattered. "Ha...wait until we tell the kids." Brandon chuckled again. ''That'll be nothing, considering I've had over half a month thinking you'd leave me for Ana if I told you. Jeez...what's she going to think?!'' "Well, after what she did during that ladder match, and everything we've been through now with the kid's custody issues it really doesn't matter anymore. I just know Jarod will freak." Brandon sincerely responded. ''Jarod...'' Amy quickly shuddered, ''You'll laugh like you always do, but he is the most intimidating person on the planet to me. I think we should both tell him, but you can handle when he tries to murder me.'' "Yeah... I agree, we should both tell him. You know he's been warming up to you though. He's not thrown a single thing of yours in the toilet in what, ten... fifteen days now?" Brandon smirked. The wolf-eyed redhead sighed, ''And yet we still can't get him past the whole 'Throw blunt objects at the new girl' phase. He's past most blunt things, though - He tried to get me with a butter knife the other night.'' ''But really...'' She continued, ''You're happy about this? Like, not upset it happened this 'early on' or whatever? I know you like pacing things and being able to plan and control every detail, but this was something neither of us expected, so...yeah.'' "So... yeah." Brand mimicked. "I'm excited. The happy part comes in a bit, don't forget that I've done this twice before. Neither Sean or Jarod were planned, the shock kinda overshadowed 'the happy'. We're together though, and this is just another step for us becoming a family. If it happens out of order, so be it. Brandon's smile served to relax the redhead beyond all belief. ''I'm here, Amy. I'm not going anywhere. Even for this." 'Till death do us part... They both smiled. For once, even the biggest curveball life could throw hardly even made them flinch.
