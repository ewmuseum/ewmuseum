Ghosts Of The Past [3] The Chance Meeting

18 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Go back to 2... September 23rd 1999. Kinkade had broken bones, issued threats and collected his money. It was the same thing that he did every night and now he was going to return to his run down apartment to pollute his system with enough drugs and alcohol to forget at least temporarily what a violent yet mundane life he lead. But as he made his way through the crowd that he'd pushed past to earn his money, he saw a face. Nothing different about seeing a face in a crowd but this one wasn't the same, once you'd been in Brooklyn around that sort of scene for a certain amount of time you got a look... a look that told someone that your soul had left your body and you didn't care anymore. She didn't have that look. Kinkade shoved past a couple of people before finally reaching her. "Hey red." Kinkade spoke up "You lost or something? Little late to be out on the streets kid... especially around here." Amy'd watched him push out into the crowd, seemingly unafraid that he was holding a large sum of bills plain to see in a practical riot of seedy 'thugs' and all-around rejects. Then again, she thought, why should he be? She saw how he fought, and most of those thugs did too... The fear of this guy was in them all...but she wasn't sure of him. Instead of using terms like 'kid' and implying she was out too late at night in a mocking way, he looked almost concerned and when she realized she'd been staring, thinking for a good solid ten seconds or more, the little redhead decided to answer before she looked even weirder to the fighter. "Bit of an odd spot for someone lost to come seeking shelter, dontcha' think?" She flashed him a grin that looked like it probably took him aback, she seemed like the only person comfortable standing in front of him. "I do this stuff...not well or anything, but I do it." Kellen cocked his head and for a second, Amy thought, he looked somewhere lost halfway between impressed and amused at the sight of the 5'6" redhead that couldn't have weighed 125 soaking wet. When he didn't say anything but continued studying her with a little chuckle, she continued as she would have with anybody else regardless of how nervous she was. With a politely extended hand, she introduced herself...'I'm Amy." Definitely not a local. "Yeah?" Kinkade said with a sigh "You're also dead if you stick around here on your own. Come on, I'll take you someplace where you can eat and dry out a little." A few seconds of silence passed as Kinkade looked around a couple of times, he was pretty sure that sooner rather than later a couple of guys would get together and decide they wanted their money back. He put a hand to Amy's elbow a tugged slightly but, hands in her front pocket, she instantly pulled away - a little rougher than intended, and a momentary glare was exchanged between the two...a little pang of fear on her part. When she finally spoke, she raised an eyebrow with a questioning glint in her blue eye. "Whoa whoa whoa..." She backed up a step, "I don't wanna sound rude, but I just watched you beat the living fuck out of a guy twice your size for a small prize of cash...why, exactly, do I want to walk through Brooklyn with you? Brooklyn, where nobody's gonna care how loud I scream..." The redhead continued to watch his expression carefully. He was a little nervous...but definitely NOT of her. He'd be good protection if she followed, but there was an entire flipside - And considering she didn't even know the guy's full name, that was a very real flipside. "Now, I don't know exactly how big the 'MUG ME' sign on my back is, but that's a little suspicious..." Kinkade took a few steps further away. "It's about as big as the 'RAPE ME' sign that you don't even know you're wearing." Kinkade replied before feeling slightly guilty as a look of concern appeared on Amy's face "Listen kiddo... I've got about a thousand bucks in my pocket. Right now I'm about the richest guy in town and you've got nothing that I want, now do you want to see tomorrow or not?" Amy took a moment and glanced around at the points, whispers and stares she was getting. Points, whispers and stares all aimed at her...all from people she'd rather not ever have to confront even in broad daylight. That made her decision for her, and she stepped close to Kinkade. "You're right, the floor's too dirty here to die on." "Good call." Kinkade said as the two left the crowd behind "And besides... if I shoot you here one of these ghetto fucks might panic and shoot me." Aimz stopped for a second. "Kidding, I'm kidding." Kinkade said with a smile as the two continued onward into the night. Continue...
