Eye For An Eye [4] Broken Home

4 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cat out of the bag and amongst the pigeons in one fell swoop. I'm not even mixing my metaphors here because that's exactly how it went... in fact I could add a third and it'd still make perfect sense. Which third you ask? Shit hitting the fan, my friend. Fuck the slut, get the slut pregnant, try to murder the slut. Then broadcast it to the world. I give you Vincent James Kinkade, the genius. January 10th 1983. "You got off lucky this time Vince, but we'll be watching you... so you might wanna make sure that you don't screw up again, you ain't always gonna have your pal here to bail you out." Sheriff Frank Broward tipped back his hat and narrowed his eyes in the general direction of Jimmy Valentine, who cracked a smile back at him. "Don't worry Sheriff, ol' Vince won't be back here again... he made a mistake, we all do from time to time." Jimmy turned to Vince, who leant over and whispered into his ear. "Lets just get the fuck out of here Jim, one more minute and I'm gonna crack that Sheriff's head until the white meat shows." The Sheriff scratched his chin and raised an eyebrow. "Say what Vince?" Jimmy turned with a look of concern on his face, quickly intervening. "Ah he was just saying how well you boys treated him over the past week, been real hospitable n' all." Valentine quickly placed an arm on Vince's shoulder and ushered him out of the station, his glare still fixed on the Sheriff as he left. Sheriff Broward turned to his deputy and shook his head. "That boy right there is trouble, I've got a feeling we'll be seeing him again." Outside, Jimmy Valentine shook his head vigorously. "God damnit Vince, what were you thinking... you and I both know that there ain't no problem with a little fun and games when you're out on the road, but pregnant? No glove no love son... you know that more than anyone, now we have a mess that even I ain't sure we can clean up." Vince pulled on his jacket and made his way toward Valentine's car. "Don't worry about it, I'll clean it up." Vince spoke out coldly, as Valentine stopped in his tracks. "Now you listen here Vince, don't you be doing none of that stupid stuff, you're my top draw... I can't afford to lose you, and what about your wife... your kids? I don't have another five thousand bucks to bail you out with Vince, this happens again... you'll be doing the time." Vince turned quickly, grasping Valentine by the collar. "I said I'll take care of it... as for the bail, just take it out of my wages, now can we get a God damned move on, I have a family waiting for me at home... like you said." Vince sneered, an intense look upon his face. "Sure thing Vince, sure thing." Jimmy answered, struggling free of his grip and opening up the passenger door, allowing Kinkade to clamber in. January 10th 1983. "Is pops coming home today?" Kellen Kinkade knelt beside his Mother as she watched the television, he noticed the twitch in her eye at the mention of his Father's name... he knew something was wrong. "Yes Kellen, he is." She spoke softly, stroking his hair before going back to watching the television. "Aren't we gonna go and wait for him in the porch... greet him proper?" Kellen replied. "Not this time son." She answered, her voice completely devoid of any emotion. "Why not?" Kellen asked. "That's not for you to worry about son, now go on... go and play with your Brother and Sister." She answered once again, still not speaking with her usual vibrancy. Kellen got to his feet and made his way across the room to sit with his Brother and Sister, they played for the rest of the afternoon. But he knew that something was wrong. January 10th 1983. Vince Kinkade unscrewed the cap of a whiskey bottle and took a deep breath, before downing the last quarter of it in one swift motion. "Dutch courage." He told himself, even though he knew it wasn't true. "Martha... I know we've had some problems lately and I'll admit that I made a mistake this time, but you have to trust me... it won't happen again." Vince slurred, rehearsing his lines before taking another deep breath and making his way toward the house. He didn't knock at the door, it was his home... he didn't need to knock. Or so he thought. "What the fuck?" He growled, finding that his key didn't quite seem to fit in the door. He narrowed his eyes, placing the key back into his pocket and delivering three swift raps on the door, followed up by the folding of his arms. Inside the house, Toby Kinkade jumped up elated at the knock... he rushed to the door and managed to pull open the latch and yank it open, greeting his father with a hug as his sister arrived on the scene to do the same. Neither of them had heard their Mother's plea of "Stop children... wait!" Vince lifted his son and Daughter up into his arms, before marching toward the living room, there he saw his Wife and Eldest son... sitting silently and defiantly across the room paying him no attention whatsoever. "Nice to see that some people around here still appreciate me." He sneered, putting down the children. "Go to your rooms." He barked, as they promptly made themselves scarce... he turned back to Martha and Kellen with an entirely different look upon his face, the reconciliation speech seemed a distant memory. "So what the fuck do we have here, the locks are changed and neither of you are talking to me?" He scoffed, tossing down his bag and making his way over to them. The silence still filling the air. "Ah I see... the cold shoulder is it? I was going to come in here and apologize to everyone but it seems like you're the ones that should be apologizing to me... so go ahead, I'm waiting." Still nothing. "Say something damnit, you... boy... look at me." Vince snarled, a little desperation creeping into his voice. Still nothing. "I SAID LOOK AT ME!" Vince screamed at the top of his voice, grabbing Kellen by the head and twisting it around. "Take your hands off him this second!" Martha roared "You're scaring him!" Vince smiled, the alcohol was kicking in now. "See what you did woman? See what you reduced me to? Is this what I have to do to get your attention now... I have to hurt our eldest boy just to get your attention?" He sneered, relaxing his grip on Kellen a little. "Oh no... believe you me Vince, what you did over Christmas more than got my attention." His eyes glazed over. "Just what the fuck is that supposed to mean?" He growled. "Nothing." She replied. He scowled. "Kel... go to your room." He barked once more. But Kellen didn't move. "I SAID GO TO YOUR ROOM!" "NO!" An awkward silence followed. "What did you just say boy?" Vince asked, a shocked expression across his face. "I said no! I won't go to my room... I'm not going to let you hurt my ma'!" Kellen cried... moving over to Martha and hugging her. "Son of a bitch, you'll do what I saw damnit... seems to me as though you need to be disciplined, see what happens when you defy your Father!" Vince growled, snatching Kellen by the ankle and dragging him halfway across the room. He yanked the belt from the loops in his jeans and wrapped it around his hand, before pulling off Kellen's shirt. SNAP! Kellen's screams echoed through the room. SNAP! And again. SNAP! And again. "Stop it! Stop it at once! Please Vince!" Martha screamed... pleading with her husband. "This boy needs to learn some respect, obviously the last eight years of mollycoddling ain't done him no good so it's time for him to learn the hard way, same way my Father taught me!" SNAP! More screams. THUD! A final scream, a terrifying scream that chilled even Vince, as the belt slipped from his hand slightly, sending the buckle whipping into Kellen's back, cutting deep. "Damn you... damn you to hell!" Martha screamed, picking up the fire poker and rushing at her husband, but he was too quick... lashing out and hitting her in the face with a closed fist. And then the rage wore off. "Oh God... what did I do... what did I do?" He sobbed to himself, dropping to his knees and cradling Kellen in his arms... he tried to hug his wife but she cast him away, rushing into the kitchen and picking up the phone, dialing for the emergency services. January 11th 1983. "Well M'am... there's good news and bad news, the bad news is... we think you may have a hairline fracture in your jaw so we're going to need to do some more tests, but the good news is... your son should be fine, no physical damage... ... listen, I don't know what went on here but there are numbers you can call, help for these sort of things... you needn't feel trapped by-" "No." Martha spoke out "We'll be fine." The Doctor nodded his head, looking back to his clipboard and moving on to the next bed, as he did so... he shot a glare at Vince Kinkade, who'd finally plucked up the courage to enter the ward. He made his way over to a bed, where Martha sat holding a sleeping Kellen in her arms. "Listen..." He began, only to be cut off. "No... you listen, and you listen good... if you ever do this again, to me... to any of the kids, I'll kill you... I swear to God I'll kill you while you sleep... do you understand?" Vince frowned for a moment, before narrowing his eyes and balling up a fist... before coming to his senses. "I... I understand." He said... sullenly surrendering. "Good... now get out of my sight, I don't want you near us." She whispered, as he complied... making his way out of the ward and back onto the corridor, quickly making a turn into the nearest bathroom. He switched on a tap and looked down at his hands, still bathed in the blood of his sun... he ran them under the tap, washing it away. Washing away the blood was easy, but wash away the scars, physical or mental, he could never do. Looking at himself in the mirror, he bit his lip. "You fucked up." He said sternly, before cupping some water in his hands and splashing it over his face.
