Guilty Conscience

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tempest perched silently on the bench, looking like a tiny raven in the stark whiteness of the hospital waiting room. She rocked herself very slightly and every so often looked up at the door to the emergency room, beyond which Eddy Fate lay in the care of doctors. The rest of the time, her eyes stayed focused on the floor, as if there were answers there. So many questions... and more sadness than she remember in years. Having found her brother, she now had to lose him, at least from the world of wrestling they both loved. That's what they said... he'd be lucky to use that arm again, much less wrestle. She heard the sound of footsteps approaching and her lower lip quivered just a bit. She looked up slowly to see Destiny as se walked out of the ER. Destiny stood over Tempest a moment and then said, "He's stable finally. They're done operating, for now at least." Tempest looked up slowly and said, "Destiny... I'm sorry." She choked a little on the last words and Destiny looked at her strangely. "Tempest," she said, taking a seat next to her, "What are you sorry for? This isn't your doing." Tempest sighed and looked at the doors forlornly again as she said, "Isn't it though? I shoulda been there. I shoulda known Ric would do something like this. I..." "No," Destiny said, cutting her off. Tempest looked up into her face questioningly. "Tempest, I know you have plenty of things to feel guilty about, but don't make a lifestyle of it. I can blame you for many problems that have occurred for Eddy, but not this one. If you had been there, Ric would have broken your ribs or something, as well as what he did to Eddy." She shook her head and said, "No this one isn't on you, you couldn't have stopped it. And Eddy would feel even worse if both of you were hurt now." Tempest nodded mutely, looking back and forth between the doors and the floor. Then she looked up at Destiny, her eyes shining with tears and glint of something else and said, "You're right about one thing. It's better that one of us is intact... because someday, Ric Chronos is going to pay for this, for what he did to us, to me. He's going to pay dearly." Tempest's characteristic smile was nowhere to be seen as she looked up at her, and Destiny moved back just a little. She cleared her throat and said, "Maybe so. But you do have other things to worry about, number one being your admirer." Destiny smiled as if the whole thing were a welcome amusement. Tempest shook her head though, for once not picking up on the joke. "He's a plague Destiny," she said, "A shallow, selfish jerk who thinks that because he's decided he needs someone it's going to be just a matter of snapping his fingers and throwing a little money around." She harumphed and fidgeted on the bench before adding, "And that's if he's actually sincere. More likely though he thinks he's found a new strategy." "You really think so?" Destiny asked, tilting her head. "Making things personal is one way to do it, but I'm not sure it'd be the best way. I mean to actually hook up with your so-called enemy." Tempest shrugged and said, "It would be if you wanted to destroy them once and for all... and you knew there was no other way. And I'm sure by now Monte's figured out that I can't be destroyed by any normal means." She smiled and Destiny nodded a little as she looked at the doors to the ER and said, "Good thing... let's hope no one thinks of a way. Because one of you belongs here at least." Tempest looked up sadly at Destiny's worried expression and patted her hand as they both turned to the doors with nothing but questions.
