10 December 2001

With his hair pulled back and tightly secured in a ponytail, it was obvious that he wasn't nervous, after all Steve had experience in this sort of situation. Sitting in a chair with a psychiatrist watching your every move was nothing new for Steve, because if you all can recall...Steven Douglas was a bonified psycho. A madman...a menace to society...a freak. Steve had no choice however, he had been made to come, to be interviewed...to be told whether or not he was "sane" enough to live and breathe in modern day society. To be told whether or not it was "okay" for him to walk down the sidewalk of a suburb filled with small children, playing with their golden retrievers and waiting for Mom to call out, "Dinner time honey!" If it was "okay" that Steve walked past the white picket fence that Dad had made with...his...bare...hands. If it was fucking okay. "Is everything all right Mr. Douglas?" Steve's head perked up and his eyes made contact with Dr. Roberts hollow stare. Steve smiled, shifting only a slight in his seat, "Of course Dr. Roberts...may I call you James?" Dr. Roberts nodded, "If you would like to, would that make you feel more comfortable?" I love their questions. Steve smiled, "Sure...but also a cup of coffee would too." Dr. Roberts grinned, "Of course, I've been wanting one myself for the past hour...just have been putting it off." Dr. Roberts stood from his seat and calmly walked over to the small wooden desk on the far left of his office. He poured two cups of coffee, turning his head a slight, "Cream? Sugar?" Steve shook his head, "No thanks...I like it black." Dr. Roberts turned his head back pouring some cream and sugar into his own cup and turning back to the seated Steve Douglas. He handed Steve his cup and took a seat back at the chair in which in sank into and it seemed to mould to his skin. "Shall we begin Mr. Douglas?" Dr. Roberts asked. "As long as you start calling me Steve, none of this Mr. Shit," Steve smiled. Dr. Roberts nodded, "Sure, so Steve I have a few questions. I am required by law to determine whether or not you are stable. Due to the fact that you attacked Kenny Rock you have been charged with attempted murder, are you fully aware of that?" Steve paused, "Yes." "Now Steve," Dr. Roberts took a sip of his burning coffee, yet the sting did not phase him, "I took a look at your record, it is my understanding that you have been under psychiatric care in the past, is this true?" Steve paused again, "Yes." Steve leaned back in his chair and closed his eyes. "Steve you don't have to do this." "No John you don't understand, this is your fault John! This is your fucking fault!" Steve jerked up in his seat and his eyes opened, Dr. Roberts failed to notice the change in breathing that occurred, "Can you tell me a little bit about that Steve?" "About what James?" "About why exactly you were committed." Steve took a sip of his coffee buying as much time as he could, but it was obvious, he was not going to escape the past, "I was placed under psychiatric care because...I kidnapped a co-worker and held him under my control." "I see," Dr. Roberts leaned in closer placing his cup of coffee on the desk in front of him, "And tell me Steve, why did you kidnap your co-worker, John Skyler?" "You could have made this easy on yourself John, you could have helped me. Now you're going to die John! You're going to fucking die!" "John and I had... had a few disagreements, and in a moment of insanity I lost myself and kidnapped him. But I went into psychiatric care...and I'm better now," The question was: Was Steve trying to convince Dr. Roberts? Or himself?
