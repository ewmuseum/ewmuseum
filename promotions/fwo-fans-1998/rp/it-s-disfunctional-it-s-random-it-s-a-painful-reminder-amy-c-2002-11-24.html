It's disfunctional. It's random. It's a painful reminder. Amy Campbell, welcome to the real world.

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The days were improving, with time. Alcohol was obsolete, friendships bloomed and situations moved forward towards solution for Amy Campbell...right? It was time to slowly bring the scattered shards of her life together. There was a journalist out there trying to ruin everything, but seeing the peristance and practical stalking the reporter, photographer, whoever had been doing, Aimz had become careful and deliberate in her actions. She intended to further her friendship with the man they called Brand Frontier, Brandon Roche to her...if not just for the fact that she was curious to see how long the tabloids could further their supposed "affair" without running out of semi-realistic material. Either way, it was time to dedicate herself to the other half of her life, the half that had only been an afterthought until that cursed article came out. "Mommy!" Something had always suddenly clutched deep in Amy's chest when she heard those words. Her entire body would flood with a wave of what felt like a sweat-inducing heat, but then the warmth would dim the moment she set eyes upon the one being on earth who called her by that name. He was a well-built little boy who stood tall among his peers, with hair as dark as night and smokey eyes to match. Those eyes were always what stopped her heart dead, and what easily cast aside any thought that he'd spawned from the same bloodline as the scrawny, "wolf-eyed" redhead. His eyes weren't hers, though he'd always been lead to believe they were. His lazy little gaze wasn't hers either...it all belonged to the mother he never knew, and never would. It bothered Amy, though...the little boy, her little boy, had been lead astray from his real roots. But it was supposedly better that way, though she couldn't look at him long before feeling as if she was staring into the eyes of her long-dead best friend. One blue eye and one brown stared deep into a pair of younger, near-blacks. His meaty, dimpled little smile, in which she noticed a few baby teeth missing, met her practiced grin and both became oblivious to everything else as he flung his smaller form dead-on into her lap. Her smile grew warmer, and his eyes screwed shut with concentration as he gripped, finger-tips nearly able to touch, around her stomach. She'd changed into an almost entirely different person the moment he'd clumsily stumbled into the room. Darcy Markson noticed that change, Aimz had fast forgotten his presence and it seemed that the little boy she was locked into a mutual bear-hug with hadn't even seen him. "Hey!" She'd smiled and sighed pleasantly, heavily into what looked as if it was a once-combed mess of dark hair. His hold on her had actually started to cut off air, so Amy gently reached around her back and broke the grip with a chuckle, ruffling a hand in his hair. "Jeez...when'd you get so strong?" "Grandma says I must have got it from you!" The little boy smiled proudly, brightly in his response, hopping down and placing two hands lined with "baby fat" on his hips. She felt the stinging need to correct him...she'd longed for the day she could tell him of his real mom, and maybe he'd understand. But not now, it just wasn't the right time...he was only 4. Or was he even that old yet? 'Wait until he's 12 or so, then he'll understand', her mom had always reassured her. Amy'd always found it in a sick way amusing that she was still practically coddled by her mother at a point where she was 'grown up' enough to need to play mom to her 'own' child. Her smile never faded, though...and when she felt a much larger, stronger hand on her shoulder, she was suddenly thankful her mother wasn't looking over her this one time. This time, she had her own strength and guidance...no more need for 'mommy', this was something Aimz had to deal with and explain on her own. "Whossat?"......Though she hadn't exactly felt ready enough when little Stephan, the namesake of his mother Stephanie, finally noticed the man so close to the girl he called mother and pointed up at him while inching closer to Amy almost protectively. Her smile soon dimmed. "This is.....uhhh...." With raised eyebrows, Amy looked to Darcy in reference, though her eyes were in a silent, pleading conversation with him. She looked to him, searching for some sort of approval in his loving but always defensive gaze. What would she say now? 'This is daddy!' sure as hell wouldn't go over well with Darcy. "...this is...umm...my friend Darcy." "Hiya! You don't look like a girl......" The boy held out his hand to Markson, but quickly retracted it, a little confused by the name. Amy tried to hold back from laughing aloud, and might have even corrected Stephen but she was getting way too much enjoyment from watching the look on Darcy's face. "You're an quick one, aren't you little fella? Kids didn't start harping on me for that till around 3rd grade...." The little boy looked at him blankly for a moment, bringing Amy to whisper in his ear while gazing up at Darcy with a grin. "Don't worry, he tries to be funny sometimes...I don't get him either." "Well, just be glad nobody's taught him any dirty words yet. With you for a mother, God knows why the little guy isn't mentally scarred." Darcy chuckled, planting a quick kiss near Amy's ear to point out the good nature of he joke. Seeing even the slightest display of affection, Stephan began jumping up and down, pointing." 'Dey do dat too!!!" "Who? What?" Amy stared, confused. "Grandma an' grampa! 'Dey kiss lots...on de lips!" He pinched his lower lip, still not old enough to recognize why his mother was growing pale, almost sickly looking. "An' hug lots! An'..." He was quickly interrupted by Aimz' flinging herself down off of the sofa she'd been perched on with Darcy, covering 'her' son's mouth. "No no nooooo...heh.......heh........" She had to barely hold back from gagging, watching Markson look down and laugh the entire time. "....that's okay, Stephan...mommy doesn't need to know!" "Good Lord..." Darcy was able to muster in between laughs. "So much for not being scarred...." Amy looked up at him, narrowing her eyes half-jokingly while she mouthed the words "I'll scar you." and grinned. The order of the day was to relax...her parents had left she and Darcy alone with Stephan, glad to see that their daughter had finally decided to maybe hold herself accountable for her responsibility. At the same time, they'd known that wasn't the case but either way they were headed to see Amy's grandmother, and Amy was headed for an afternoon of happiness, simultaneously stained by the pure hell of trying to handle both her 'adopted' son and her boyfriend in the same environment. It wasn't much of an issue, since the mood was light-hearted and any seemingly negative situations were all brought on in good-nature...it was the first relatively happy day for Amy what seemed like the longest time. She'd sheltered herself for now. Hidden away in her son's youthful innocence and Darcy's shoulder to cry on...but it still felt as if she was using these people, and that wasn't at all her intent. It just seemed that way...seemed like there was a negative undertone to everything she did. Of course, it could easily just be depression's cold hand still gripping at her, but Amy made it a goal to make it up to the 'men' in her life for the support she'd sucked out of them. Darcy, Stephan and even Brandon all provided some form of support... So maybe it was time to stop hiding away in her own little world, shielded by the efforts of others? Definitely.
