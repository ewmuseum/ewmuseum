Preparation

11 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Miz Ivy? Your brother called, he and his family will be about an hour late." Ivy sighed. "Thanks, Lou." The large, imposing Puerto Rican nodded, closing the office door off to the side of COOP's as Ivy McGinnis continued to go over some of her paperwork. She glanced up and looked through the glass windows into the gym at the kids, all of whom were doing something in preparation for the evening's show. "Ivy, ol' girl," she said to herself, "I hope you know what'cher doing." Her bags were packed - enough to survive for nearly two months on the road, a streak she didn't think she'd ever come close to again. Between the Far East tour and the Canadian tour, the next time she'd be home would be-- "December twenty ninth," said Randy Knox, startling her, "you'll be back to take over again on the 29th, right?" She flinched, surprised that Knox had entered the office without her knowledge. "The dates are always up in the air but in theory, I'll be back on December 21st. Plenty of time to have something decent planned to catch the overrun that can't get into Countdown to Oblivion." "But you won't be here." Ivy looked at him. "Why wouldn't I be here?" "Back with the fWo... we're gonna take second place again." She started to answer him but he held up a hand. "It's okay, I know. You don't have to keep an eye on everyone in the fWo like you do us." "That's not true," she insisted, "You'd be surprised how many people in that company need someone to wipe their chin for 'em. And besides... Brian knows how to run a promotion, you and Luis know what everyone's regimens and skill levels are... you're gonna be fine." She brushed off the front of her black 'nin' babydoll T-shirt - the office was ridiculously dusty. Luis was tightening the ring ropes, Rosalyn Callasantos was stretching in the middle of the ring, and the rest of the kids were cleaning up and setting up around the ring. "Don't forget, Cally needs to work on her bumping tonight, and her wind. Who's she got?" "Me," replied Knox. "Good," said Ivy, as the two headed out of the office, "Take it easy on her, but try to push her past fifteen minutes. Make sure Brian gets a program as soon as he's in, in case I'm not here... he likes to know what's going on. And please, keep everyone from working a fight in the lockerroom... he's got high blood pressure." "Got it." "Okay everyone," said Ivy, addressing the kids, "Take a seat around ringside please, this'll only take a minute." She paced the ring for a minute, trying to think of what to say. The twelve kids, all between sixteen and nineteen, sat in the front row, waiting patiently. Ivy adjusted her glasses and hooked a thumb in one of the belt loops on her jeans before she finally cleared her throat. "As you've probably heard, I've taken another job with the fWo, and I'm leaving for Japan in about three hours. What does this mean for the IWB? Several things. First of all, my brother Brian, who formerly wrestled as 'Mister Self-Destruct' in the CSWA, WCWPA, and NWC, is going to run the business end of things." No reaction from the kids. "As far as your training goes, Randy Knox and Luis Rodriguez have been taking care of that for the most part anyways... it's just official now. Listen to them, they know what they're doing. What does this mean for you? It means that yes, I'll be away most of the time, but this means that Silver Fox of the fWo is the second booker that wants to look towards this gym as a developmental territory. Craig Miles of New Frontier Wrestling's got his eye on one or two of you--" she looked toward Knox, "--and things are going to happen, I think." Cally raised her hand. This frail sixteen year old had the least amount of physical gifts working in her favor, but she also had the most drive to learn and improve. She had really bonded with Ivy, and the mature business predator felt a pang of guilt at leaving. "Yes, dear?" "Will Mr. Flair be around to oversee any training? He showed up from time to time when Mr. Cooper was..." Ivy froze. "I don't think we're going to see Eli around any time soon, but I know if Valerian's Garden is in the area he'll be in. Anything else?" No other questions? Good. "Good luck tonight," said Ivy, "and kick some ass." The meeting broke up, everyone else went back to what they were doing. Ivy leaned back into the corner and exhaled sharply. "I know this business," she whispered, "but I don't know these people. I can't relate to them on the same level, no matter how many years I've been in it, how many million miles I've logged on the road." Ivy looked toward the high ceilings. "Where the fuck are you, Eli?"
