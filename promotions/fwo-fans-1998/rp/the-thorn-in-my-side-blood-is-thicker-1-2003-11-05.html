The Thorn In My Side (Blood Is Thicker #1)

5 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Originally posted under the title "Something From Nothing" during fWo's Survivor 3.) Standing alone on a balcony overlooking the small Maryland hall, Elliot Harper observed the small promotion he'd bult from scratch in action. "Go home," he ordered to the referee, coupling his inaudible words with the pre-determined hand-signal. Elliot didn't even watch to see how his one and only 'top talent' finished the main event... Rather, Harper was looking at the crowd - or lack there of. His project... His baby... The promotion he'd thrown his life into, was coming to a halt all around him, and there wasn't anything Elliot could do, nothing he hadn't tried. Too small to make it big as a wrestler, and without the natural charisma to cut it as a manager, this was Elliot's only chance in the wrestling industry. Nobody would take him, so he had to make his own promotion. But now that too was going the same way. "Your winner," announced the badly suited ring announcer over the near-ancient public announce system, "Greg Hoffman!" The response from the crowd was lacklustre, most just seemed eager to head off home. Still getting their breath back in the ring, after giving it their all, the two wrestlers looked rightly pissed off at the apathy of the audience... But what could they do other than bar the exits? His head shaking to match the men in the ring, Elliot slowly turned away from the diorama of wrestling failure and walked down the wooden steps toward the curtain. "I'm barely going to break even on this one, " Elliot dejectedly prophesied, subtracting the wrestler pay-offs from the estimated gate in his head. Where the wrestling was failing, Harper's brain was still fine-tuned to calculating his profit. "You look sad, Elliot," drawled a near monotone voice, "I liked the show... Those bright lights make my eyes flash green!". Bounding over to him was his brother Thomas... Really not the person he wanted to deal with. For as long as he could remember, Thomas had been his anchor... Always weighing him down. Whenever he went out as a teenager, it was always "Take Thomas with you!" coming from his parents. Elliot never knew if it was because they wanted the brothers to be close, or because they wanted Thomas out of their hair themselves. Elliot loved Thomas, their brotherly bond would never be broken by any man or action... And he actually felt bad about being embarrassed by his own flash and blood. But he was just, so.... ...Remedial. And now he was here, too.
