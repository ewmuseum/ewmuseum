Free Wrestling Alcohol Day

19 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mr. Sebastian Abernathy had been tasked with an assignment. His employers, The Underground (currently consisting of the Bears, Mike & Brittany, and The Flying Frenchie) had tasked him, between beer runs and commiserating with him the fact that they couldn’t pay him without risking him losing intern status, and with it college credit, with some basic market research. Bas didn’t know relaying his findings would prove more difficult than gathering it. “If you’ll turn to page six, you’ll see that while slightly less popular than the champions, that your team name is able to be spoken by people without lengthy Catholic educations.” Mike Bear was doing his very best not to tear his hair out. Pierre Delacroix fidgeted in his chair, and slid his beret over his brow a little lower, hoping that if the movement was gradual enough, Bas wouldn’t realize that he’d covered his eyes. Brittany, historically the kindest and most patient of the three, had actually been following along, and interjected with another question. “I’m not seeing it in your written report, but your slide mentions arrogant frat boys with Asian tattoos they don’t know the meaning of…” Bas excitedly interjected. “That’s in Appendix B. I know the segment is loathed by many, but they have disposable income and a hive mentality. I mailed a dozen t-shirts to the local chapter of the Pikes, and in just three days time, purchases around college campuses have spiked, according to the FWO Shop webmaster. This sort of thing would have taken longer even five years ago, but now you have people seeing their buddies pictures the next day on Facebook wearing your merchandise, and they want to have one too, just to fit in.” “Interesting, Bas. This is real good work.!” “Liar. Zis is a lie!” Brittany turned to Frenchie. “Pierre, calm down…” “I will not calm down! I am a four-time World Champion. I will not be lied to wit’ reports and Power Point slides…” “It’s not Power Point. It’s actually Keynote, it’s a great little program for the Mac…” “Make it stop Brit. He’s hurting my brain.’ Brittany looked at her husband and his teammate, and shook her head. “You two asked him for this. The least you can do is listen.” “Anot’er lie. Ze least I can do is take a nap, and pretend zis was all a terrible dream.” “Bas, can you just bottom line this for us? I know you worked hard on this, and bang up job, but I get hit in the head a lot. I just don’t retain it the way I used to.” Bas nodded enthusiastically. “Page 87, that’s the last page before the first Index….” The Winners of This Match groaned, but Sebastian was undeterred. “…or the Executive Summary on the first page hits the main points. People love the name. People hate the entrance music. It hurts their ears, and they can’t wrap their heads around and of the songs individually, which is what they want to do. You need to find one, and stick with it.” “Zat’s easier said zan done. Ze problem is some of us,” Frenchie said, in a manner indicating he was talking about himself, “have good taste in music.” “And some of us can bridge a suplex properly.” “Whatever it is guys, you need to pick one. My research still shows that you’re much more popular than the new teams of Blitkrieg Funk and Affirmative Action.” “As we should be. Zose two are just over generalized stereotypes, mocking entire swaths of the population,” spat Pierre, as he squared his beret, put down his baguette, turned his head so that his profile was against the flapping French flag behind him, and chuckled quietly to himself about a great Jerry Lewis film he’d watched for the four hundred and thirty sixth time the night before. “Back to business, Bas, I think you’re onto something with the college kids. Get them in Seattle and send them back home to evangelize to their friends there. I think I know how to hook them, too. We’ve got Free Wrestling Day coming up this week, which means new faces to take us in. I think it’s time we do meet ‘n greet.” Brittany lit up. “That sounds like a great idea. You can sign autographs.” Pierre hemmed and hawed, game, but not entirely sold on the prospects. “We could charge them to do shots with us. C’mon Pete, it’ll be fine.” Mike mulled it over. "So, Shots with The Underground. What's the angle?" "I'm not sure I follow you, honey." "What are we trying to accomplish? What do they win?" "They win a chance to drink with you, and you get to drink for free." "Now zis is a plan. Anyt'ing to get away from ze phone book size report." Sebastian adjusted his glasses and composed himself. He would not be discouraged, even though he put weeks into the report that was so casually dismissed by the same people who had spent the entire time watching Mythbusters episodes downloaded from Bittorrent. He cleared his throat to get his employers' attention, though they continued on making a list of the alcohol they would need and he would have to carry. He cleared his throat more loudly, to the same result. Oh well. "Well that's all fine and good," Sebastian yelled over the listmaking, "but we should concentrating on other things besides drinking." Suddenly, the talking stopped. Mike and Frenchie looked up, a little confused. Bas started to get a little nervous. "Did you hear something, Pete?" "I believe I did, t'ough I am not sure what I heard." Brittany shook her head and looked over at Bas with a sympathetic eye. "Hon, you may want to phrase that a little differently. I'm not sure they understand the concept." "Oh... uh, uh, of course... What I meant was, since... the... first priority! Yeah, the first priority of drinking for free was resolved, so we need to figure out the entrance music issue." Frenchie started nodding, and seeing that, Mike started nodding as well. "I t'ink I see what you are getting at, mon ami." Mike glanced around briefly. "Oh! Oh yeah, totally." Brittany shook her head again. "Mike, what were we talking about?" Mike stroked his goatee and thought deeply, knowing this was an important one. "Cheeseburgers?" "No." "It's never about cheeseburgers." Mike put his head down and pouted. Brittany completely ignored Mike and tried her best her to lift the spirits of The Underground's unfortunate intern. "So, Bas, since you've expertly deduced that the theme music is the element that needs to change, I assume you have some suggestions?" A smile reappeared on Sebastian's face. "Oh yeah, I've got some great stuff I just downloaded from iTunes! You guys'll love it!" As he rushed out to get his iPod Touch and sound-dock, Frenchie raised a nervous eyebrow to Brittany. "iTunes?" Brittany smiled a cautious little smile. "Oh I'm sure he's got fine taste in music. Right?" "If I hear one song from zat Zach Braff show, zere will be trouble." Sebastian came back with the speakers and started setting them up. Excitedly he shouted, "I know it's a bit of a departure for you guys, but you gotta hear this new song from The Fray." Immediately Mike snapped out of his pout with one of those looks in his eyes. "That's it!" Frenchie leapt out of his chair and restrained Mike as he was picking up his chair. Brittany slid under the table they were sitting at and ran over to Bas who was still looking for an outlet to plug in his iPod accessories. "You need to go. Like NOW." Bas looked back at her, still smiling but confused. Of course, having seen Mike Bear about to attack him with a steel chair, he ran out of room faster than the average Flying Frenchie World Title reign (OH! I kid, I kid. Tip your waitresses). Brittany walked back over to Mike who was still struggling with his tag team partner. "Mike, could I see that chair?" "Fine! Just make sure tha..." And that was all he got out before Brittany clocked him with the chair. Mike went down in a heap. Frenchie, having watched this all happen, just shrugged. Brittany set the chair back down. "Well, I had better go check on the poor kid." "Are you sure anybody who listens to Ze Fray should be in ze group?" "Well how is he ever going to learn, Mr. good-taste-in-music?" Frenchie smiled, choosing to ignore the quotation fingers made by Brittany. She walked out of the room, leaving The Winners Of This Match to get back to the important stuff: what alcohol they would need for their tent. Just as soon as Mike woke up.
