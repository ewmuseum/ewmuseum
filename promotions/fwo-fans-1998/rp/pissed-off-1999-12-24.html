Pissed off

24 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera fades in on the boiler room of the United Center in Chicago, site of the last wrestling pay per view of the Millennium, the fWo's Twilight. Geoff Green is walking through the maze of pipes and heavy machinery, trying to avoid getting hurt before this big night. As he turns a corner, he spots the Underground's Mike Bear, sitting in the meditative position we often see him in. Mike is not yet in his ring gear, far from it. He's wearing a pair of Jeans, boots, and a black bandana tied very tightly. Mike looks up as Green comes near) Mike (in a somber mood): Hello Geoff. Geoff: Something wrong Mike? Mike: Not at all Geoff, but my normal chipper mood is absent because, as Mike Myers said, I'm surrounded by frickin' idiots. Geoff: Oh, what did Wretch do? Mike: Geoff, you're so naive. This is not the Dynasty. The Underground is the most close-knit stable in the fWo. I'm talking about all the other fools in the fWo. We face the two biggest tonight at Twilight. This match, provided there's no more *cough* cheating *cough* on the part of Greenberg, this match is a cakewalk. Now, I'm not saying that Kid Crap could cheat and win tonight, it'd just be a little more challenging. However, The Underground winning tonight is a certainty, because there is no Chaos to pin tonight. We beat Ultra Cool 2000 in the Tag Team Title tournament, plain and simple. Geoff: So you're just mad about the circumstances surrounding the Tag Titles? Mike: Geoff, Geoff, Geoff. Did you watch Warfare, or did you have Flash record that? The Underground is finally getting recognized as a power stable, and that means a bunch of midcarders insulting you. Well, what goes around comes around, that's what the Underground did as well. I'm not mad that it's *happening*, I'm just mad because the people doing it don't have the talent of the Underground. I'm talking about Havok, Jericho-clone number 36. Y2J-c thinks that somewhere along the way he became better than me. He speaks of how he beat me for the TCW Tag Titles, a title and organization no one cares about except for the people involved. It's also strange he brings up those titles, since we were told by Cactus Jon to drop those titles because no one in the federation could stand up to us. Havok, when you won those titles, you were in the right place at the right time. The same goes for that other title you have, or had. I don't even keep track anymore. Havok, the only fed people give a damn about is the fWo, and let's talk about your achievements. You've managed a title reign for a while, however, you've ducked opponents and don't even close to wrestle a full schedule, as well as the fact that the fWo took a hiatus during your title reign. And of course, you beat *chuckle* Micky Maliboo and the guy who had to cheat just to beat Eron the Talentless. That's not a title match, that's a joke. Other than that title, you've done jack. Havok, get it through your head. I am better than you. I have always been better than you. And I will always be better than you. Learn it, live it, love it. Geoff: Ouch, seems pretty harsh Mike. Mike: Well it's just like dealing with an animal. You feed them once and they'll keep on coming back for more until you show them the consequences of doing it one more time. Geoff: I fail to see what you're talking about. Mike: Then replay it later, I've got other shit on my mind. First of, Canadian Connection, you two gave us a good match back on Warfare. I'd consider that one of the best matches I've wrestled in the fWo. I've got some respect for you now, but don't go overstepping your bounds. Respect is a hard thing to get, and an all too easy thing to take away. Remember that. Now, there is the issue of the Dark Riders, led by a guy who got beat by Y2J-c. That's just pitiful, and you have to wonder why this guy is so feared. Dave, how DARE you interfere in The Underground's match! Where do you get off pulling down a wall of our cage and getting involved in a match that was of no concern to you? Well Davey, I'm pissed. Next time you interfere in Underground business, there will be trouble. Davey, you don't scare me. You're leading a group of no talents BEFORE they got all depressed and got even WORSE. I've dealt with manipulators before, and manipulators can only control weak minds. Well Davey, you won't find those in the Underground. Other than your power to control jobbers, you're nothing. That's it, I've said enough. Geoff: You sure that's all? Mike: Yeah, I've been wanting to get back to my old training. (Mike gets up from his position and walks over to a brick wall. He starts punching the wall with violent force, and yet, restraint. As he punches, he yells out the names of Kid Cool, Death, and the other people that were aggravating him, Geoff turns around and starts walking out, though we can still hear Mike yelling as we fade to a commercial for Twilight)
