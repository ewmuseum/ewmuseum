Kings and Queens

24 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"You're not tired, are you?" "mmmm?" "S'okay, so am I." It's still early - there's still life moving around in the hotel, and Ivy and Trip can both recognize some voices in the hallways as belonging to some of their fWo co- workers. Sounds like Aimz and Brandon are trying to organize a group of people to go to dinner together - and Eli just tactfully guided them away from Ivy's door. Good thing, too. Ivy and Trip are lying in bed, arms and legs intertwined. They're both on the verge of falling asleep, due to lingering jet- lag and the workout they just put each other through. "You hungry?" asked Ivy, as she kissed his chest, "I could order up some room service." "Nnnn," replied Trip, "Don't think this place has room service... and I really don't wanna move." "Who said we'd have to move?" "Who'd get the door?" "You." "Why me?" "I'm nekkid." "I'm not?" "You don't understand these things," replied Ivy, having lost the flow of the argument but too stubborn to back down, "You know what next week is, right?" "Next week?" "March third?" "Couldn't forget it if I wanted to," replied Trip, "Happy anniversary, almost." He kissed her on the top of her head and squeezed her body next to his with the arm wrapped around her. Ivy sat up suddenly, stretching her arms way above her head. Trip waited a moment before he "goosed" her hips, just to watch her jump. She shot him a dirty look, but instead of trying to fight him like she did earlier, she simply collapsed back into his arms. "Sean?" "Yeah?" "I'm really happy." "....Me too." They lay in silence, intertwined. "Ivy?" "Yeah?" "Lucky really liked you... he was always hoping we'd get together." She smiled, although with her head rested on his chest he couldn't see it. "He must've known something we didn't.... for a long time, at least." They lay in silence, intertwined. "Sean?" "Ya?" "I love you." Ivy couldn't see with her eyes closed, but a huge smile spread across Trip's face. "I love you, too." This, the odd couple of wrestling - some might say the oddest couple.... it's been just under a year since the eyes of the world saw them come together and become the epitome of what it means to be in love. And yet, they don't even care - it was such that there was no choice for them but to become what they are - neither Ivy McGinnis nor Sean Stevens could imagine not being together. That, my friends, is irony.
