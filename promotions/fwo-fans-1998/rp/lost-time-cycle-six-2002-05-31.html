Lost Time - Cycle Six

31 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Superior Championship Wrestling has a live Television broadcast on Monday evenings, and house shows on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday. Monday, June the Third will eminate from Tampa, Florida. Tonight they're in Orlando. Lucky for Poison Ivy and Triple X, they're right in their backyard. "I'll be in the locker rooms," said Triple X, holding the taxi door for Ivy, "Please don't do anything stupid." "Don't worry, Sean," replied Ivy, planting a kiss on his cheek, "I won't." Trip might have had his good and bad times in Superior, but he's got a lot of friends in the lockerroom. Ivy, on the other hand, has had very limited experience in this place. All she wanted to know was where Edward Brown's personal assistant was. "Ivy! WOO HOO!" A few fans hanging around by the rear entrance at this early hour recognized her. "Triple X! Triple X!" Trip, himself, still had a pretty strong following as well. Once inside, Ivy and Trip part company, and Ivy is on her own. Her familiarity with the layout of wrestling promotion backstage areas allows her to easily negotiate her way to Brown's office. She takes a deep breath, and opens the door. "Linda Johns, I presume?" The woman looked up from her makeshift desk, and their eyes locked. At that moment, both women were thinking the exact same thing. "THIS is my rival?" Linda can't imagine how Sean Stevens could be with this girl. Sean should be with a real woman, not this tart dressed in a blasck athletic bra, white tank top, and plaid pants cut off at the knees with suspenders hanging, unused, from her waist. Real women don't wear their hair in braids, and real women don't wear combat boots. Ohmigod... is that a tattoo? Poison Ivy, on the other hand, looks Linda up and down and wonders how a woman could be so.... boring. That might not be fair to Linda, as the only other girl that Ivy has really been friends with in her wrestling career is the fWo's Tempest, and she's... decidedly unique. How can Linda be comfortable in her 'power suit'? That's the biggest problem of all... the power suit reminds Ivy of 'Miss Hooters' Teri Melton... not a good thing for Linda's situation. "You're Ivy, right?" "Yeah, that's me," replied Ivy, "We need to talk." "There's nothing to say," said Linda, returning her attention to her work, "I'm busy. Please leave." Ivy walked up to the table she was working on, pulling the set of papers out of her hands. "We have to talk about Sean." "I have nothing to say about him," replied Linda, "I said everything in his apartment." "You don't really expect me to believe he did anything with you, do you?" "Believe what you want," sneered Linda, "but I wasn't wearing practically nothing for my health." That did it. Ivy turned the table over and grabbed Linda by the collar. She was still a good four inches taller than the 5'3" Ivy, but she was still looking into the eyes of a very unstable woman. "Now you listen to me," said Ivy through clenched teeth, "Sean is with me. Not you. Anything you two shared in the past is just that, the past." "Says you," replied Linda, holding her stance. "Says me... and him." "Why doesn't he tell me, then?" "Because he doesn't have the time in his day to deal with a ringrat who thinks she's hot shit because she can point to one of the boys and say 'I fucked him.' but that doesn't make you anything but a slut." "But you---" "You're a slut," interrupted Ivy, "because you don't care what the situation was. You wanted to be with Sean because he's Triple X. Any kind of emotional connection you two shared was killed the day you tried to seduce him in his apartment with your 'I'm a naked girl, so you should have sex with me' stance." "Hornet--" "Don't mention Hornet to me," interrupted Ivy again, "Hornet and I were together for four, five months the first time, six months the second time, and almost a year the third time. Every time, we broke up because he fucked things up. That's not being a slut, that's being stupid. And I admit it. But I never tried to seduce him with my womanly charms." "Semantics," said Linda. When Ivy didn't interrupt her, she continued, "If I was Sean's whore then you were, without question, Hornet's whore." Ivy slapped her. "The only difference," she said, "is that Hornet and I were an actual couple. He wasn't using me as filler for someone else." "Melton," said Linda, finally. Ivy slapped her again. "Teri was using him, which kinda evens things out. But besides that... you listen to me and you listen good. Sean is not with you. Sean is with me. Accept it.... or you and I are going to have a problem." She lets Linda's collar go. Linda backs away a bit, as Ivy turns around to leave. "What the hell is your problem?" asked Linda, as Ivy was on her way out the door. "Simple," said Ivy, "You're the only obstacle to my happiness. Now you're out of my way." "What makes you think you even deserve him?" Ivy puts her sunglasses on, tilting the frame down just slightly, "Because anything worth having is worth fighting for. And he's worth it." She leaves Linda behind... and with her... the biggest piece of her own insecurities. Poison Ivy is finally free.
