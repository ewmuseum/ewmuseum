Little Angels

22 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"This is our last song tonight," said Angel into the microphone, "and I'd like to dedicate it to someone very special to me who couldn't be here tonight." A loud cheer erupted from the crowd - at least six hundred strong crammed into the small club - as Mickey started to play a slow, steadily rising riff on the guitar. "This is a new song," continued Angel, "called 'Torn to Pieces.'" Out in the crowd, lost in a swirl of people, Tempest swayed to the low insistent riff. It wasn't her usual bouncy bubbly fare, but the power of the music was enough to move her regardless. She smiled and watched Valerian's Garden on the stage and thought, This is her wrestling ring, her screaming crowd and adrenal high. She doesn't need anything else when she's up there, just like me in the ring. No wonder she's still at it. Tempest's thoughts were echoed by Angel's boys onstage. They enjoyed playing, they lived for playing.... but none of them became the music like Angel. She had her neck bent, her hair in her face - and she didn't actually see anyone in the crowd. Bassist TJ did, however... off to the side, he caught a glimpse of the sparkly, bouncy Wrestling diva and stopped playing for just a moment and pointed at her. She waved back, as the song came to a crashing end, a chorus of cheers, and a lot of whistling. "Thank you," she whispered into the microphone, "We are Valerian's Garden, goodnight." As the roadies began to dismantle the stage, TJ tapped Angel on the shoulder and whispered in her ear. Immediately, her attention shot toward the audience, and an old friend in the midst of it. "Tempest! Get'cher ass over here!" Tempest leapt forward and bounced by the stage hands before they knew anyone was headed for the stage. She skittered up onto the stage with an oversized backpack on her back as if gravity didn't even apply to her. "Angel! Boys! How are ya?" she said as she flung her arms around Angel. "Can't complain," said TJ as he hugged her, "Usual 'on the road' fatigue but that's really not a problem. You know what I'm talking about." Angel and Tempest both looked at him, the former with a raised eyebrow, the latter with a giggle stuck in her throat. "Er... no, you really don't, do you?" Angel laughed at him. "So what's been going on with you? I haven't seen you since, what - New Year's? Gawd I was still pregnant then, wasn't I?" "Yes!" Tempest said, bouncing slightly. "And now you have an itty bitty road companion! I been pretty good. Really busy what with being the International champ and all. I didn't think I could do more public appearances, but they found a way." "Of course they did," replied Angel, nodding her thanks at someone who handed her a bottle of water, "If they want to keep you busy they'll need to schedule a few more hours in a day." Angel suddenly stopped, mid- thought. "Come with me.... there's someone on the bus you need to be introduced to." Tempest's eyes light up as she trots after Angel. "Really? Oh boy oh boy oh boy!" She chuckled and said, "So how are you holding up?" "Well, fortunately I never slept much anyways," said Angel as she grabbed her leather jacket from where it rested on the amp, "but she's a good girl. And it's been harder still, since we're not touring close to Elijah anymore, we're trapped in the midwest of nowhere and the fWo's been exiled to Canada, Mexico, and.... it's Saturday night, right? He's on his way to Africa." Tempest looked like she was about to say something, but Angel put her hand up. "But you know something? We're both doing what we love.... and for right now that's what matters." She knocked on the side of the bus, and the door suddenly slid open. Angel led Tempest inside, where Bus Driver Extroadinaire, Troy Parker, was sacked out on one of the sofas with a hand on the side of a playpen. "Mariella Jade Flair," said Angel, picking the baby up, "meet your Fairy Godmother." Tempest actually squealed with joy and held out her arms. "Ooooohhhh, she's beautiful! Hello little MJ." she took the baby from Angel and watched as she burbled happily and grabbed a lock of Tempest's purple hair. "Awww.... look at those beautiful gray eyes. Just like daddy's! You know I'm a good friend of your Daddy's and Mommy's too, right?" She nodded and smiled at the tiny face of Mariella who acknowledged her with a toothless grin. "She knows," said Angel, smiling in her daughter's face, "she's been wondering, I'm sure, where Auntie Ivy got her streak of purple hair from. Hey, c'mon into the back, I need to change." Angel dropped her jacket on a chair and began to unlace her corset as she headed back. Tempest walked behind, transfixed by MJ but not bumping into anything. "So how's Ivan?" asked Angel as she dropped the corset on the floor of the changing room and grabbed a "Hell's Belles" T-shirt from a stack on the shelves. Tempest nuzzled MJ's head and said, "Ivan's doing great. He just reformed his old stable, the red Army. Sort of a mixed bag of folks, but lotsa fun! He was really messed up after his friend Mexi turned out to be lying to him and working against him though." She shook her head and looked up. "Honestly I could have strangled him for that. Here Ivan was finally starting to relax and then that happened. He went off and brooded for a month." "Brooding men," replied Angel as she slipped out of her dress and put on a pair of black jeans, "The one thing we've always seemed to have in common." She tied her hair back with a rubber band as she reached for her daughter, "But seriously, they're worth it, aren't they?" "Yeah," Tempest said softly as she handed MJ over. "She's so beautiful Angel, just like her mommy. I bet Eli just dotes all over her." Angel smiled. "You'd never imagine a man who calls himself the King of Extreme could be so loving. I mean, he'd never show it when anyone else was around but he was actually reading to her when we were last together... just so he could have an excuse to hold her. And with my boys, Ivy and Sean, you, and Goddess knows who else, this kid is going to be so very spoiled." Tempest chuckled and said, "Somehow I can see Eli doing that. But yes... I have some spoilage right here as a matter of fact." She winked and pulled off her backpack and rummaged around in it. "It was hard to find just the right one but I think I finally did." She turned and held out a large puffy teddy bear. It was white with purple wisps of fur and sparkling angel wings. "From her fairy godmother." "Oh, Tempest.... that's too much. You knew we were coming, didn't you?" She smiled, looking around the bus, "I don't--- have anything for you, do I?" Angel re- centered MJ's weight onto one arm, snapping her fingers. "Of course." She unhooked the spiked leather bracelet from around her left wrist and pressed it into Tempest's hand. Tempest looked down at it - all the time she'd spent with Angel this had never come off her wrist. The exposed underside bore the same logo as Tempest had seen on the sleeve of the band's CD. "Now it's official - you're a Garden Keeper." Tempest grinned and said, "Cool! So I'll show up next time with this and a pair of flowery gardening gloves." She snickered and put it on as she said, "Of course you'll have to let MJ know that if she wants any pumpkins turned into coaches or mice to horses, I will require a little advance notice." "I think Auntie Ivy might take issue with that," replied Angel as she sat down on the bed so as to rest MJ on her lap, "since she's likely to turn my sweetness here into another Psycho Bitch. Don't get me wrong, Ivy's the most genuine person I know but she's a little.... high strung, I guess, is the nice way to say it? Forget pumpkins and coaches, mice and horses.... she'll have my precious on a motorcycle, beating up the boys before she's thirteen." Tempest laughed and said, "Well, we'll just have to keep me around to counter that influence! I ride a scooter and only beat up boys when they richly deserve it." She looked up and tilted her head as she said, "Speaking of boys and beating others up, how is Eli anyway? I haven't seen him in ages either." "He's doing well.... had a bit of a letdown earlier in the year when he offered an idea to the powers that be in the fWo and they got shot down for political reasons. I know you have your own opinion on the politics of your profession... honestly they're a lot closer to the music industry than you'd realize - but it was really a rude awakening for him. I think he's finally been beaten down to the point where it's just another job, and he's stopped giving it his all." "But then he does something idiotic like let himself get set on fire, and that's great for business but destroys my nerves. He told me he's still planning on retiring young so we can be an actual family but I'm not gonna hold my breath." "Personally, though? He and I couldn't be doing better." Tempest smiled a little and nodded shallowly, "That's always the trick... deciding how much you love something and how much you're willing to let it drive you crazy." She sighed and said, "When he's truly done with it, at least he'll have you and MJ. Some folks have nothing so they can never let it go." She shrugged and then smoothed a lock of the baby's hair. "But he has a future right here. And that' what's important." Tempest leaned over and kissed little MJ's head and smiled. "She's tired," remarked Angel, "C'mon, Miz Storm Grrrl, let's put my own little angel to bed and check on those fools inside, see if things are loaded up yet. Then... dunno about you, but I'm hungry." The women left the back room of the bus... and the journey would continue. Tomorrow, they would go their separate ways, their next meeting uncertain... but for one night, everything about their lives was just about.... ....perfect.
