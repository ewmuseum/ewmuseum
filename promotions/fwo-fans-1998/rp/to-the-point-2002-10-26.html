To the point.

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Kodiak" Vic Creed vs. "The Paragon" Brand Frontier. Over four years ago these two men began a chapter in their lives that neither knew would take them where they are today. Creed, always forceful and overbearing took his game to the next level then. Brandon Roche, always stoic in his beliefs was tested beyond reason. They had managed to face off in many skirmishes but nothing was ever really settled. Brandon even used the feelings that developed from those confrontations to fuel him. Yet, despite their one match against each other two years ago it has never really been settled in the ring. That night in March two years ago was a chance for them to settle their differences. Much to their chagrin though the man known as Condor had other plans. No matter how hard they tried to dismiss him they could not. He hounded both the Paragon and the Kodiak until he was included in the match. The night was set. At the E.W.A. Ultimate Gamble it would finally be Brand Frontier stepping into the squared circle against Vic Creed, this time for the E.W.A Heavyweight Championship. The night turned out to be Brandon's night and after a brutal match Condor laid defeated and Frontier's arm raised in victory. Creed was no longer the champion he had so longed to be. Brandon had taken that from him. Brutalized in the match and afterwards many wondered if Creed would ever be able to return. He had proven many wrong on more than one occasion though and return he did. Obviously we never see the stress that is put on the human vessel that is Creed but it has to be there. He is human after all. It was a hollow victory though. Brandon had let the championship belt get in the way and in a moment of weakness pinned Condor to win the match. He had won, but not the way he wanted. Now was a different story. Now Brand Frontier was poised to take another step up the ladder. On Monday, June 17th he had the chance to become the Number One contender for the most prestigious title in the world of professional wrestling, the fWo Heavyweight Championship. And it was against "Kodiak" Vic Creed. Once again Frontier had the chance to take something from the Alaskan Juggernaut and offer ounces more of payback that was long coming. All Brandon needed to do was stay focused. He need not think about his place in the fWo if he won the match. That was the mistake he made back at the Ultimate Gamble. Brandon needed to make sure he didn't think about the titles or the accolades that would follow when he won the match. He had to stop worrying about things like advancing in the Internet Championship tournament or another shot at the fWo belt. Looking beyond his match on the inaugural Unleashed show almost proved his downfall. He looked at the Internet Title and not at his opponent. Luck was on his side that night. He knew though that when facing a monster like Vic Creed that luck was no where to be found. He needed to make sure he simply focused on Creed. Nothing else mattered. Nothing, and no one.
