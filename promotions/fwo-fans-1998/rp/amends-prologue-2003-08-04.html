July 28th, 2003, 11:47 pm local time Please Lord forgive me for my sins Sitting in his rental car outside of the Arrowhead Pond, Lance Knight found himself questioning the events that had transpired earlier in the evening. Having just completed his first full show as United States Champion, to say that Knight was surprised with the largely popular reaction he had received would've been an understatement. And it was, well after the show had wrapped, that Knight began to fully realize the implications of his actions earlier in the night. And while he was tired of being booed, Lance hadn't expected to be turned back into a baby face simply because he had declined a contract with Vince McMahon. On one hand, Lance supposed that he could understand why the fans would've sided with him, because he was staying with the promotion that had originally given him a chance. On the other hand, however, most of them were probably WWE fans as well, so why would they care where he was wrestling, just so long as he was still wrestling? And that's when it hit him
