This is what's wrong with the fWo today....

20 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Havok is shown standing in an empty room. The only people or things in the room are a table, which the fWo Cruiserweight title sits on, and Havok. Havok is wearing black jeans, a white TCW tee-shirt, and a backwards black Adidas cap. He just stands with his arms folded, grinning. The one dim light in the room hangs over his head. Havok speaks....] The fWo. Once the greatest promotion in the history of wrestling. But now it is being ruined. Ruined by people like the Canadian Connection, the French Foundation, the Dynasty and the Underground. People who have turned the fWo into a joke. First, let's look at the Dynasty. They claim to be the greatest group in the history of wrestling, yet it seems they can't even handle problems within their own team. They have Ice and Slugger... fools. Universal... a rich fool. They talk about how tough they are, and yet, when it comes time to fight, they run. Universal has run from me twice, putting Mike Heftel in his place. These are signs of a coward. Sure, Ice is the World champion, but because he beat Primetime, which brings me to my next point.... The French Foundation. Another group that is slowly bringing down the quality of the Fan's Wrestling Organization. Primetime, Courage, and Frenchie... jokes. They come out here, and talk about how superior France is, and, with the exception of Frenchie, their hasn't been a true Frenchman yet, just those rejected by America for their stupidity. They hold titles, they win matches, they make a mockery of this sport. I wish that somebody would put the French Foundation in a match with a real wrestler, maybe a Millenium Men. Then they's find out what it's like to wrestle a real match. Now we've got the Canadian Connection. Two of them have only been here for a short while now, and they're already causing a commotion. Now, I've never liked Tom or the Ninja, and I never will. Now, Cactus Jon... he's a man who I once had a lot of respect for. He was my employer, my friend, and I even fought by his side at Mob Rules. It's different now. Jon, you're a fool. You can't even keep TCW running by yourself. I've lost almost all respect for you. Which brings me to the Underground. First of all, Mike Bear. Bear, I want to clear something up. I am what kept Toronto Championship Wrestling alive for all those months! I am TCW. I beat you for the tag titles. I will soon beat your old friend, the Foreign Phantom for the Canadian title. Bear, I've got you beat in all the ways. And I think you, Mike, are also a fool. Now, Wretch. Chris Kidman. You were once my friend and partner. But, since you've alligned yourself with Mike Bear, you've become nothing but a blithering idiot. Fear, also an idiot because of his allignment with Bear. The Underground is just one more team slowly bringing down the fWo with their idiocy. Now, we do have some real wrestlers here. My fellow Millenium Men, for instance. In fact, I'm scheduled to face Wolf Fang, my partner at Twilight in what will surely be a great match, possibly the best of this millenium. Of course, just wait until the next millenium. Wolf Fang, I wish you luck, because you're gonna need it, buddy. So here's what I plan to do about the trash here in the fWo. I am making a series of open challenges. It can be before Twilight, or it can be in my year, 2000. But anybody in the fWo who thinks they have a shot at beating me can bring it to the ring, and I'll be waiting. That's right, I don't care if you're the World champion, or a rookie low-carder, I'll take you on, and prove that I am the true Millenium Man here in the fWo. [He picks up his belt and walks off as the camera cuts to a promo for Twilight.]
