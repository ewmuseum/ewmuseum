fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Commercials and questions February 12, 2000 The dancers hopped and jumped into the scene, flashing lights escorting their lithe movements. The dancers were dressed in hot-red and deep-black skin tight vinyl outfits. The outfits pushed up at the breasts, lifting them up at the camera with the small perk of a nipple pressing out provocatively. They moved toward the camera, dancing in time to the hip music, hips swaying and jabbing out with the beat. Hands were placed on hips, and they slid around to the backside, pausing for a moment to feel the curves and smooth roundness of a tight butt. Mouths puckered and tongues slipped out of the mouth to lick the full lips. Damn, that's pretty hot... I didn't realize that commercials had gotten this good. Rudo sat on the edge of the hotel bed watching the television. Sid sat next to him, looking at the television commercial with only mild interest. Machina stood a few feet behind them, sipping slowly on a cool beverage. Yeah, you should have seen the cut scenes. But hey... quiet down... the best part is coming... In the commercial the camera pulled back, and you could see that the dancers were dancing on top of a giant Coke can. The silver of the top gleamed in the light, and the Coke logo was huge and magnificent in its glory. Suddenly, a blue gloved hand moved into the scene and grabbed the can. The camera pulled back much farther, and Machina came into view. He wore a Coca Cola shirt and grasped the can where the now tiny dancers were dancing with a firm grip. In the commercial Machina looked down at the can, and his smile faltered. He turned the can sideways, sending the tiny dancers plummeting toward the ground, gravity taking its toll. He looked at the camera. Always clean off the top of your cans so you can enjoy the great taste of Coke without any crud getting in the way. He popped the top and tipped the can, taking in a big swig of the dark liquid. The bubbled fizzed up from the can to his nose and he giggled. Ah... that's the stuff. The commercial ended with the Coke logo, and then went on to an ad for "The Beach" with Leonardo DiCaprio. Rudo lost interest and turned to Machina with a smile. Sid leaded forward, finally interested in something he was seeing on television. Rudo shook Machina's hand. That's great, man! How much are you getting for doing the Coke ads? Machina shrugged. I don't know... James Armstrong set the deal up for me... he's taking care of all the money. I'm just happy to be on television. It's always been my dream to be in a commercial in front of millions of Coke fans. Machina picked up the phone. He started to dial, and then turned to Sid. Hey, turn the volume down, okay? I'm calling Penny. Sid complied as Rudo tumbled toward the door. You want us to get out of here so you can talk in private? Machina shook his head. Nah, I'm just asking her to marry me... no big deal. Machina dialed as Rudo looked aghast. You're asking her to marry you over the phone? Machina held the phone to one ear as he waited for Penny to pick up. I suppose you have a better idea? I... oh hell, she's not home, I got the machine. Rudo breathed a sigh of relief. Well that's a good thing. Listen Ed, you can't just ask a woman to marry you over the phone, even if you are just doing it out of revenge. I mean... that's just not cool, right? Machina was ignoring him. Hey Penny? Machina here. Hopefully you're having a good day wherever you are. Anyway, I'm at the hotel here and I was wondering if you wanted to go out to dinner tomorrow night. Oh, and if you wanted to marry me. Well, call me back when you get this. Bye! Machina hung up the phone and turned around. Sid and Rudo were both looking at him with their jaws dropped. Machina shrugged. What?
