The End Has No End (The Ridiculed Priest)

7 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
January 15th, 1994 *** "Hey Levi catch!" The bottle smacked hard into the tough hand of Levite Prod, Levite was still a young man but his hands were as hard as sand paper. He had been working on the farm all his life and his muscles showed for it. However he had been working toward a goal and that was to win the Strong Man Competition that was coming to his local town of Rock Springs. The competition was just in one hour and Levite was sucking back a cold bottle of cream soda before he headed to the show. His Dad Roman had tossed him the bottle and was just on the other side of the tractor looking over at Levite, "You know boy, win or lose today, I'm proud of ya," his Dad spoke in a softer tone, trying to still come across as a strong man. Levite grinned wildly at first and then move quickly into a more reserved look, "Thanks Dad." He wanted to mention to his father that he had been feeling some tightness in his right shoulder since Thursday but his Dad had been so happy that Levite had qualified for the finals that he held the pain in and let it go. Roman piped up again, "All right boy, you head on up to the house, gets some grits and hop in the shower. Make yerself look a little pretty for all the ladies down at the show. We Prod's got to set an example for all the people in this town." Levite smiled slightly lowering his head for a moment, he was hesitating, instinct told him to tell his Dad about his shoulder but at the last minute Levite could feel his father's stare and caved, he look back up to his Dad and spoke quickly, "You got it Dad, I'll see ya in fifteen minutes." Levite stood in the shower and felt the hot water come down over his head and rush down his body, he went to reach behind him to grab the shampoo bottle that rested easy on the shelf but let out a groan of pain, "Fuck," he said under his breath and with his hand he nursed his shoulder, rubbing it in hopes that it would help with this unwelcome feeling in his shoulder. He shut the shower off and grabbed the towel. Levite rushed down the stairs and turned the corner to greet his Dad getting his boots on, "Let's head boy," his Dad said as he tied his boots, "We'll be late if we don't go now." One last chance. "Dad," Levite said quickly almost as though he was fighting from speaking. His Dad continued to tie his boots and not look up, "Yeah boy." "My shoulder..." Levite began. Immediately his Dad looked to him, "What about yur shoulder?" Levite and his father looked at each other for a moment, both waiting for a response that was comforting, Levite wanted his Dad to say it was 'okay' and his Dad wanted Levite to say the same thing. Instead Levite could only respond with, "It's fine. My shoulder is fine." "Good," His Dad said with a brief nod, "Let's go." Open-Close-Vroom-Stop-Open-Close Levite waited patiently at the sidelines, he was up next and all he had to do was this final lift. By now his shoulder was killing him but nothing absolutely nothing was going to stop him. He had to win, he had to prove to everyone that the Prod family was a family of champions. Today was the day that he would show his father how strong he was both mentally and physically. A buzzer sounded. Levite stepped forward and bent down low, he looked to his Dad who stared smiling back. Levite then placed his hands on the bar and closed his eyes, as he lifted he struggled to lift the bar above his head and he held it there for a few seconds. It was then that he felt his right shoulder snap and go limp immediately. Levite howled in pain as he dropped to the ground with the bar coming down hard on his back. The pain was so immense that he lay on the ground and barely could utter a sound except for a small wimpish cry. Levite could not hear anything around him, he only saw people scrambling everywhere, before he felt himself slowly slipping out of consciousness Levite let his head rest against the ground and looked up at the bright blue sky. Today he noticed there was a cloud in the sky, it was all blue, beautiful blue. Levite had lost the contest and was branded a failure. He would never be able to lift anything over ten pounds with his right arm for the rest of his life. All in all Levite knew that his life was not meant to go the direction of bodybuilding or wrestling. His life was back at the farm, safe and warm with his family.
