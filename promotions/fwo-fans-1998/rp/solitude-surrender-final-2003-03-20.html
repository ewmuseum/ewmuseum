Solitude - Surrender (Final)

20 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thursday afternoon... Brandon Roche looked over the plain brick exterior. Through weary eyes he had yet to see a place more inviting. More so from it's supposed occupant than from the surroundings. It had only been a few days since he last looked in her mismatched eyes but for him, in his heart, it seemed like a lifetime. Those two days under the rubble were pure torture to say the least. Combine that with the fact that less than half a day after the incident Amy disappeared herself. No one from the head office had made contact with the redhead since the bombing so Brandon didn't know what her physical or mental state was. All efforts to contact Amy were for naught, her parents hadn't heard from her, even with young Stephen with them she hadn't checked in since arriving back in Nova Scotia. Ivy said that her last conversation ended on bad tiding with the line going dead at the height of Amy's anxiety. Steeling himself against the worst possibilities Brandon absent-mindedly rotated his left arm. The stiffness in his ribs was still apparent, the fall after the collapse of the terrace two days prior saw to that. With a deep breath he stepped to the door, listening for a second he finally decided to knock. Since arriving back home Monday morning, Amy had barely moved from the little leather couch near her door. No sleep, no food, her travel bag was still lying where she'd thrown it. The coffee table in front of her was littered with a small army of Kleenex boxes but even though she looked sick from the days without eating following a lovely minor nervous breakdown, the redhead was happier then than she'd ever been before. The only reason she was still on the couch was to wait for the knock at the door, because the night before she heard that Brandon Roche had indeed survived, apparently physically intact, and was fighting on Warfare... Within a second of the actual knock, she'd shed the blanket that had covered her rest for days and managed to fling the door open, attaching herself with arms around the visitor's neck...she didn't even have to look at him, she'd known just by the way he knocked. As her head sunk in his chest he could feel the tears penetrate his soft cotton shirt, touching his skin. After days of acting like the tough guy the world thinks him to be Brandon could only mimic his little Amy's actions. Tears welling up in his eyes he fought them back, to no avail. As they trickled down his cheek he finally pushed under her scuffed chin to raise her eyes to look in his. Once that spiritual contact was made however nothing could stop the monsoon of emotions that swept through them both. Picking her up, the mighty hero spun her around in the air, overjoyed at the feeling simple contact with Amy imbued him with. "Oh God Amy... I... you're okay. God. Please say you're okay...?" Bringing them both back to earth with his words the dizziness subsided from their actions but a different kind of dizziness remained because of their feelings. Stroking back her matted hair Brandon looked again in her eyes. "Don't ever..." Sniffles and sobs mingled with her words, but Brandon was waiting patiently - he had all the time in the world for her. "Never go away...please." Amy wasn't even sure what she was trying to say, the words were leaving her mouth the second they entered her brain. "I've never...never felt that. You want to die so badly but nothing will kill you..." She felt crazy but it seemed like the Paragon was understanding her perfectly. "I don't fucking know...never leave..." For another few moments, her head refused to lift from his shoulder, and she didn't even care or seem to notice that she was entirely pouring out every one of her weaknesses right in front of him...it was something she'd never done, but these were things she'd never felt. "Amy...sweetie... I..." Again Brandon suffered from the same malady that afflicted his dear Amy Campbell. Too many thoughts and feelings rushed at him from so many directions he didn't know what to feel... or how to act...or what to say first. Understating the obvious again, all Brandon could repeat was... "I missed you." followed by another hug lifting her off her feet. Finally realizing that part of her shivering dealt with the cold nature outside Brandon used his current position to place an arm under the bend of her legs and carried her inside.
