Margaret was having herself one hell of a day. She had spilt a pitcher of coke all over someone's french fries. She had accidentally dropped a carton full of brand new glass mugs. Let alone the people she had served today were not of normal friendliness. One couple hadn't given her a tip, while another (the current one in which she served), had been bitching and complaining about this, that, and the other thing too. It was a long day within this six hour shift... and she just longed to go home for rest. Luckily, for her, there was only one hour left, and her table-range had downsized since it was within the later hours of the night, and more of the business staff had shown up. Miss Green continued to clean up one of her last remaining tables, as that same current family, the one's that had been complaining the whole way through, had waved her over. "Marge... Marge!" She was coming. Oh God she was coming. She didn't want to hear her name like that again... not only was that not her full name, but it showed no respect at all either. "Yes? What now?" The tone in her voice told the story, even if you knew nothing of her long drastic day. She stared at the man, who took his younger child's cup of coke, and handed it to her. "How come this refill isn't in the normal glass?" He asked, frowning as he patted his son on the head. "Little Tommy here doesn't want to drink out of it now." Little Tommy. Another damn reason why she just wanted to leave. "We're out of glasses, sorry." The man seemed confused. Why? We will never know. Some people are just asshole's in life... out to ruin your day no matter what reasonable excuses has happened. "Well..." He went on. "Why is that?" Margaret took a deep breath in. She looked at their plates. They were almost done. Soon enough she wouldn't have to put up with it anymore. "I happened to have broken the last remaining few." Margaret turned to go back to cleaning the table, as Tommy reasoned with his Father. "It okay Daddy... I guesses I can drink from this glass." "You're a trooper son." The Father spoke loud enough so that Margaret could hear him. "You do that." The night went on, as Margaret finished cleaning the table to collect her low tip of two ninety-five, and headed back over to the receptionist area. She was greeted by her friend, Cathy, whom just like her, was having a rough day too. "How's it going?" She asked. "Still pretty bad?" "Yeah. See those people over there?" Margaret pointed to the family. "Annoying... annoying... annoying." Cathy wasn't paying any attention though. Her eyes were on something else. They were on the entrance. "Did you hear me?" Margaret snapped, only to seem confused when Cathy still didn't look. "I think we have company." Cathy nudged her head in the direction of where she watched... and as Margaret turned, there they stood. The Fuse Bros., still dressed in their overall uniforms, wearing their "T" and "C" hats... taking a good gander at the walls of this restaurant. Cathy smiled before she walked off as fast as she possibly could. "They're yours." "Great." Margaret threw down her towel.
