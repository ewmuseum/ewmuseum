The Meeting

22 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*tap... tap... tap... tap* Tempest's purple polished fingernails drummed impatiently on the glass top of Chris Universal's desk. Her attention drifted from the framed magazine covers and glossy photos of some of the companies greatest moments to the computers on his desk. He's torturing me. Yep, that's it. He knows me too well and this is his little way of punishing me. Why else would he leave a desktop, a laptop, and a Palm Pilot all in plain view and leave me sitting here half an hour? Damn his hide! She stood and walked around, looking up at the pictures and trying to resist the almost overwhelming urge to hack. She stopped and said quietly, "I can't help but wonder if I'll ever appear in one of those photos. Will I ever be a big name in the fWo, or will I always be 'that cute girl they have'?" She sighed and paced, kicking her shiny black heels into the carpet, seeming out of place in the world of big business in her purple capri pants and lavender spangled halter. The door opened and Tempest wheeled around and planted her hands on her hips, glaring at Universal as he stepped in. "Well, it's about time!" she snapped. "The least you could do was call me in some day when you weren't so busy. You're really lucky I didn't get too bored or your whole network would be dotted with dancing pink elephants." She huffed and folded her arms as Universal tried to suppress a smile. He walked around his desk and sat down, casually motioning her toward a chair facing him and said calmly, "Why would I worry about you hacking into our network? I'm sure you can be trusted with the company's best interests." He ignored her a moment, shuffling through the papers on his desk as Tempest's shoulders slumped. She pouted guiltily and studied her toes until Universal leaned over the desk and said, "Now in spite of what happened a couple of weeks ago, I know you're one of our most reliable performers. Let me just start by saying that. And the fans love you! But they won't keep loving you if you turn into the wicked bitch of the east." Tempest nodded and said, "I know." Universal sat back and said, "So what was that all about? What possessed you to buck a well planned match?" Tempest was silent a moment and then narrowed her eyes and said, "Did you hear a word of what I said out there last week? Chris, I'm not here just to be Miss July for the fWo. I..." "You didn't like being July for the fWo calendar?" Universal asked. Tempest shook her head and said, "No, I'm fine with it, that's not what I meant. I don't mind giving the fWo a little feminine grace, but that doesn't mean I want to stay in the minor leagues forever, being bypassed by every wrestler-come-lately we get in here." Universal frowned at her and said between clenched teeth, "You mean to tell me that being the longest reigning Cruiserweight Champion in the company's history doesn't matter to you?" "Of course it does," Tempest said quietly, with a slight shake of her head. "It means a great deal to me. But does it mean anything to you or anyone else? Is it truly on par with the Internet title or the World Heavyweight title? Or even the Hardcore title? Or is it the title you give to the little people and hope they're content with it?" She sat back and calmly folded her arms. Universal shook his head and sat back, looking at her. "Tempest, I'm not going to try and argue with you. You know you can't just walk out there and take out Erik Kelly. Things have to follow a progression that makes sense and..." "Exactly," Tempest said. "Which is why it doesn't make sense for a loser from a promotional game to suddenly go over and get closer to a higher title than me. It's bad enough that Frontier has bypassed an entire class of us that were here first, but at least he had the stuff to win the damn thing." Her voice had gradually become more and more tense and she snapped on the last words. Universal looked at her evenly and said, "I understand your frustration, but you have to work with us instead of against us if you want to do anything about it. You know how this business works and another stunt like that is only going to make things more difficult." He stood and flipped through a large planner, snapping each page in agitation and thrust it in front of her, tapping the page. "As you can see, you've already screwed things up, even for yourself. Now I need to know whether I can count on you or whether I've got an irretrievably loose cannon on my hands. This thing with you and Kagos has to stop." Tempest looked at the page a moment and her eyelids fluttered. Her expression seemed resigned as she slowly closed the book and said. "No, that's fine. I've made my point, so I'm not concerned with Kagos anymore." She smiled and added, "But to answer your question, whether or not you end up with a loose cannon again is sort of up to you." She waited expectantly and Universal nodded. "Fine. Once this tournament is over we'll talk again. Decisions are still being made about Cyberslam, so we can see what might work best for you then. But no more stunts like that... none. Understand?" Tempest nodded and stood up, holding out her hand as she said, "Completely. Don't worry, I'll behave myself. Well... mostly." She grinned and winked as Universal shook her hand and said, "Good, it'll be a wonderful change of pace." She giggled and left, leaving Universal alone. He sighed and ran a hand over his face as he said, "But what to do with you... dammit, I knew this day was coming."
