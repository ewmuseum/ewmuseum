Growth: A Story About Change

17 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Growth: Prologue There are times in the lives of everyone where things change. Change isn't always what one expects, or even what one wants. But it happens, and you can try your damnedest, but you can't change the change itself. You can just accept it, or quit. Quit life entirely. Both are options, but only one takes courage. The other... The other is a sign of weakness. Usually the final sign... Pacing the streets of Long Beach, California at 3AM wasn't for everyone. In fact, it was for very few people, as some would consider it dangerous, and ultimately unwise. Yet Kevin Masters wasn't scared, not in the least bit. In fact, he felt good as he walked down the familiar road to a familiar place. It was his apartment, well, former apartment. The place of residence where he and his best friend Mitch Richardson lived for about four years, ever since Kevin left home at the age of nineteen. He was now twenty five years old and in a very different place. He could remember moving there, those six some odd years ago. He was at a crucial turning point in his life. The reality he had known had been yanked away from him; ripped out through his mouth by the strong hands of fate, leaving the bitter taste of bile in his mouth. During that time, he could recall dreaming. Sleep was a comfort at times, but there were always the points when it brought visions of her to his mind. The only person he ever truly loved. Donna. Donna Sandburg was beautiful. She had this dark black hair, which was perfectly straight, yet somehow wavy in all the right places. From her blue eyes to her glowing soul, she was not only the epitome of physical attraction, but she was something more. She had found a connection with Kevin, one that he never really found with anyone else. The two had been perfect together, inseparable. Yet on that fateful day, the day they were to be married, Donna tragically died. It was the day that forever changed Kevin's life, and in the weeks after he contemplated so many things. It was a dark period. He thought about suicide, just ending it all; giving into the temptation of being without pain, or not being at all. He wasn't proud of what had happened. Not in the least, but it had occurred. And he got through it, but he had to leave. He had to get out of Washington, move to a new environment. So he did. He and Mitch packed it up, and moved down to be closer to Kevin's father. Dimitri was still alive then, and he was training young wrestlers at a school in Long Beach. And it was during this time, when Kevin reunited with his father, and the two became close once more in Kevin's time of need, that he knew. He knew what his destiny was, what was calling him. He knew he had to make a decision, and it was one that would change his life... forever. Change. Change happens. What that means for the individual isn't for them to decide. That decision lies in the hands of fate, the fists of destiny. As impossible as it is to try and predict anything, sometimes it is even more impossible just to accept reality. To accept the change. It truly does take courage, and it takes time. Two things which are commodities in the world... But also two things which are fleeting. Just like life.
