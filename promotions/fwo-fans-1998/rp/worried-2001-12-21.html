Worried?

21 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kodiak Vic Creed opened the small refrigerator in his hotel room. So this was the fate of the fWo Internet Champion, Creed thought to himself. Day after day, night after night, stuck in a hotel room half drunk and jacked on the juice. It was safer this way, and Vic knew it. Better for him to stay contained than to venture out into society. The more people he came in contact with, the more people that would get hurt. Usually the fate of others was never a big concern for the Main Man. But that was before his life started to unravel at the seams. "Is there nothing to drink in this place?" Creed grumbled as he slammed the small door closed. "I'm the Internet Champion, for Crissake! You'd think they'd treat their champions better than this!" Vic picked up the phone and dialled the Front Desk. "Front Desk?" "Yeah, this is Vic Creed and there's nothing in my mini-bar. Get someone up here to fix it before I have to start kicking down doors and robbing other people's bars!" "Right away sir," said the voice on the other end. Creed slammed the phone down and walked over to his dresser. He opened his suitcase and pulled out the gold and leather strap with represented the Internet Championship. Everything in his life was falling apart, but at least he still had the belt. At least until Twilight. No! There's no way Black Quicksilver can beat me! Vic thought to himself. He's a joke; comedy relief. I'm the Main Man! The only way BQ could ever beat me would be with a gun! Even then, that idiot wouldn't know which end to aim at me. Still, stranger things had happened. There was always fluke, blind luck. BQ had beaten some top men in the fWo. It might be unlikely that he'd beat Creed. Unlikely, but not impossible. Right now, KVC was a very beatable person. "NO!" Creed roared, driving his fist into the wall. He jerked his fist out of the hole, plaster dust covering his hand. A few bloody patches began to grow from his knuckles, but he couldn't feel any pain. He reached into the suitcase and pulled out a small leather pouch. "I'll jack myself to the moon if I have to. There's no way I'm gonna lose to that runt. No damn way." But a voice in the back of his head was whispering to him. Unlikely, but not impossible. And what if you do lose to him? What then? Vic could feel his blood heat, then boil. His face was red, his fists clenched. The fuse was lit and he was ready to go off. ** knock knock ** Vic threw the door open. A young woman stood in the hallway, a hotel employee, with a cart carrying different bottles of liquor. Without saying a word, he shoved the poor woman across the hallway. She slammed against the far wall, slumping to the floor. Creed pulled the cart into his room and slammed the door behind him. Vic unscrewed the top from one of the little bottles and sat down on the bed. His father's voice rang loud and clear. You're a loser, boy. A LOSER! His fist tightened around the bottle. "BQ's going to stand for Broken Quickly," Vic muttered. "Dead frigging meat."
