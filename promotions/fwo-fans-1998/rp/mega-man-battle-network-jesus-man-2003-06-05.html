Mega Man Battle Network: Jesus Man

5 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THIS INTERVIEW MIGHT OFFEND YOU. THE WRITER DOESN'T CARE IF IT DOES, HOWEVER. The Codemaster was in mid-air right at this time. This wasn't because he was in the middle of a jump, but rather, because he just got smacked to the floor by this guy: Yes, folks, this is the power of laziness at its finest, when the author of this series just simply takes the Yellow Devil image and sticks a picture of Jesus on top of its eye. So freaking sue me. Anyway, with one huge hand, Jesus Man hit the Codemaster really hard and sent him flying into the wall, which was where were at now. "Man, someone oughtta crucify you." the Codemaster said, getting to his feet, aiming the Fecalator at Jesus Man, and shooting him. All of the bullets, however, bounced off of Jesus Man with a "ping". Then Jesus Man hit him again. Once again, the Codemaster bounced off of the walls of the room before landing with a THUD. The Codemaster had been hoping that Jesus Man were as easy to take out as Sushi Man was, but his hopes were dashed when Jesus Man introduced himself, walked on water, gave a blind robot his sight(it's a shame that it wasn't Heterosexual Man, or the Codemaster probably wouldn't be here), then proceeded to get Biblical on the Codemaster's ass. In a "constant hurtification via punching him until he's messier and squishier than usual" kind of way. The Codemaster dizzilly returned to his feet, before he pulled out the Cagle Launcher. On two seperate occasions, this weapon had surprisingly saved the Codemaster's ass, leaving the Codemaster stunned that a weapon that has to do with JAMES CAGLE would actually be useful. The Codemaster fired. Jesus Man's visage was suddenly removed in favor of a floating blob of laser power, which, in turn, blasted the bejeezus(no pun intended) out of the chicken, turning it from clucking to crisping in just 1.3812364816458 seconds. "Aw, hell." the Codemaster said, before Jesus Man resumed his usual form and knocked him around the room with a single blow. As the Codemaster laid about on the floor, he suddenly turned to look to his right, toward the audience and the button-pushing tyrant that he was faced with. "MEDIC!" Suddenly, the Codemaster got a renewed sense of strength, and soon leapt up to his feet, before he pulled out his next weapon, the Dish Shooter. "Alright, brother... let's see if what I'm about to 'dish' out will knock your ass down." The Codemaster missed, because while he was aiming for Jesus Man's face, Jesus Man fell over and groaned, on account of the horrible, horrible pun that the Codemaster just used. The Codemaster looked at this in shock, before Jesus Man stood right back up and smacked the Codemaster around the room. Why, yes, again. The Codemaster wasn't sure if he could take much more of this, so he pulled out the Iron Spatula, covered his eyes, and tossed it at Jesus Man. It hit him right in the face. Crying in pain, Jesus Man suddenly melted into thin air, leaving behind his weapon. The Codemaster's eye was suddenly seen through his middle and index fingers, as he looked back toward where Jesus Man once was. The Codemaster simply shrugged in response and then picked up Jesus Man's weapon. "GET EQUIPPED WITH HOLY WATER." The Codemaster shrugged, then left the room.
