The Game is Afoot

13 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
March 8 "His eyes just lit up, Alaina. It was like Christmas morning for him all over again." Lindsay Troy leaned back against the kitchen island, drinking from a glass of orange juice. Her half-opened gym bag could be seen thrown against the bottom of the marble spiral staircase. Normally, after a show in Ft. Lauderdale, she would have driven the three hours back to Tampa. Having suffered from insomnia since she was a kid, late nights no longer bothered her. In fact, Troy could fully function on only a couple hours of sleep...a feat that Alaina, who needed at least seven hours of sleep a night to not be cranky the next morning, hated her for. But since it was Alex Creed's birthday, Lindsay had stayed overnight in a hotel, was up at 6:30 and on the road by 8:00. Alaina had, apparantly, just woken up. Still dressed in pajama bottoms and a tank top at 11:30 in the morning, she hunched over her Powerbook as it whirred on the countertop. "So he liked the gifts then, I take it?" "Loved them. He was practically giddy. He told me to thank you for your part in the gift that you and I gave him." "Well," Alaina smiled as she sipped a cup of coffee, "A rising star like Alex needs a nice wardrobe if he wants to nab himself a steady girlfriend. Armani Exchange seems to suit his tastes." Lindsay polished off the rest of the juice and rinsed the glass out. "What'd you end up doing last night?" "Went to get Joey at the airport, but his flight was delayed. We didn't get back here until late. In fact, when you called we had just gotten in." "Was he disappointed when I said I wasn't coming home until this morning?" "He actually pouted." Troy smiled and blushed ever-so-slightly. "He was still sleeping when I went upstairs a little while ago. The man sleeps like a rock." "I'm going to leave the obvious innuendo alone for once. But come take a look at this." Alaina shifted the laptop so Lindsay could get a better look. "What exactly am I looking at again?" "Swiss was able to get an account and authorization number for the transfer." Lindsay scrutinized the screen, and scrolled down on the document. "No name though, huh. Damnit." "They tried. Even went so far as to 'check' the secure servers." Alaina said and poured herself another cup of coffee. "And by 'check' you mean 'hack'." "In so many words." She walked back to the computer and stood next to her sister. "I think the trail's blocked, sis." "No way," Lindsay said. "Just because they covered their asses well enough through cyberspace doesn't mean we've hit a dead end." "I was hoping I wouldn't have to subpoena bank records just yet," Alaina added. "You may not have to." Lindsay slowly paced the entire length of the kitchen to allow what she had just said to sink in. She turned on her heel and stared back at her sister, who looked utterly befuddled. "Dudley's got them." "Are you insane? You have been hanging around Craig WAY too much." "I haven't talked to Craig since Wrestlebowl and even then it was just a 'hey, what's up?' Just hear me out. Jim Dudley is a pretentious asshole who WOULD carry the documents on him BECAUSE he doesn't trust anyone else to let the knowledge fall into the wrong hands. I don't even think he trusts Murphy himself." She walked back to Alaina and knelt down so the two were at eye level. "I know it's him, Alaina. And I know you know it's him too. We need something concrete or else we're never going to get them back for what they did." "So what do you expect to do? Break into his office?" "No." Lindsay smirked. "It's not breaking in if he leaves it open." "You're absolutely OUT OF YOUR MIND," Alaina raved. "You've come up with some really out-there schemes but this just takes the cake. You're not a cop, y'know. You can't just invoke the 'plain sight rule' or 'probable cause' and expect it to be legal." "Would it make you feel better if I asked Sgt. Gary LeFaivre to come along with me?" "NO, it WOULDN'T. Although he was just as bad as you were in high school." "Alaina, this needs to happen, and it's going to happen when the fWo is in Tampa in a couple weeks. I want to get confirmation myself, because he has been toying with me for far too long. He's screwed you over, and that hasn't sat well with me since I found out in December. It's time I paid him back." "But is doing it this way the best way?" "Probably not. But it's my way. And I'm going to need some inside help to get it done." Alaina sighed heavily, and disapprovingly shook her head. "Blood's thicker than whatever water you're going to enlist." "You shouldn't offer to help me with this. If I'm found out, I'm finished. There's no need for you to put your reputation, or Radicomm's reputation, at risk." "If you're going to set yourself up for a fall, Lindz, then there's no way that I'm not going to be there to catch you," Alaina said matter-of-factly. "Besides, I want to see the look on that sonofabitch's face with my own two eyes." "I always knew that when you got older you'd get some of the same fire in you." "Yeah, but unlike you, I know when to control it."
