Heaven's A Lie - Three

24 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tuesday January 20th, 2004 Amy smiled despite herself. Somewhere, she thought, someone was doing the same for her. That was, of course, unless she was as insignificant as she felt. And despite that, she still smiled. Her jeans were torn, worn and stained with all manners of things. Her boyish tanktop was somehow hardly becoming, though few things short of blood were unattractive on a girl that naturally pretty. Nothing felt quite right, nothing felt adequate...nothing but her relationship which had, ironically, become the only thing she could rely on despite her past being littered by men who she couldn't. Nothing was guaranteed anymore - not even life itself. And as if it ever was, Amy could do nothing but mourn the old security in habit. But this was a new year, a new life and an almost entirely different reality than she'd lived in the three hundred and sixty previous days and those before them. Somehow, though, everything was pleasant. Even though the death count still stood from the job that had become an increasing factor in her life, she was bound to more important things now. She had a family, a support system and, soon enough, even a husband. That's a whole lot of growing up to handle all within a single year, isn't it? Not nearly enough. Her so-called demons remained; No longer alcohol or a pill of any sort, she'd fallen victim to her own diagnosed problems. Obsessive compulsive tendencies made everything she did more frustrating and only enhanced her fears and anxiety. Fixing it would take time - maybe a whole new year - but it could only have a positive effect on her. After all, she was afraid of too much spotlight, terrified of failure in her work because every time she stepped out the public's eye was staring her down - which is why she tended not to step from behind the curtain unless she was absolutely required to fight. And it was a brand new year, so there was time to work on that...providing that she didn't get shot or randomly drop dead of a heart-attack, which was entirely possible given the late string of luck fWo'd been having. But that wasn't thought for home. Unlike her fiance, Amy avoided thinking of wrestling at all outside of the company setting, if not just because she'd seen it ruin so many lives before hers. Things went from Disney's Family Channel to...well....the Family Channel as she sat on the couch across from Brandon Roche. "How'd the whole 'By the way, I'm marrying your daughter next month' talk with dad go?" "He's... a stubborn man, your father. He really and fully expected me to up and leave you after the...after we lost the baby. I still think he thinks I'm only with you because you 'put out' or something. It's a shame he can't see past himself." Brandon smiled, even given the depressing conversation. How could he not when he had someone as beautiful as her sitting mere inches away? Amy grimaced, "Oh, trust me, I know. Was mom at least nicer to you? She seems almost excited about this whole thing...I think she's just happy to have me sworn off." "Yeah...except there was the dresses she's already picked out for you. I do think it's important that you and her get on the same page about that...trust me." "At least I got her away from seafoam green." She snickered, Brand smiled warmly and they sat in silence for a few moments. Neither were angry - it was very much the opposite. Amy had turned around and leaned back into Brandon, enjoying the quiet and company. She was safe. Everything seemed so perfect. That is, except for one nagging problem Amy found within it all. She sought acceptance, she wanted nothing more than for Jarod and Sean to know that no, she wasn't trying to replace their mother...but she was going to be part of their life for a long time, and that just didn't settle right with the youngest Mighty Hero. "Hey...y'think Jarod hates me?" she questioned, still contented despite the unsettling train of thought. "I'm sure he does." The answer came with a smile, not one Amy could see however. Very suddenly upset, Amy shot around to face her fiance. "What?! But why?" Aimz' sudden change in attitude let Brandon quickly in on the fact that she didn't catch the joke. Stammering a bit, he pushed his fingers through her hair, massaging her scalp as he did, trying to comfort her. "I... I, whoa, sorry kitten. Really. I was teasing. It's just that he and Ana always had a real close bond and I think it's just taking him a bit longer than normal to accept you. He'll come around. He eventually stopped torturing the dog..." "He hates me." The little redhead let herself sag against Brandon, pouting in the same manner Jarod himself would have. At the same time, she ignored all reason as usual. "I know he hates me. Stuff's not ever gonna be right. He loves Stephen, why does he hate me?" "He's just got to get used to another parent figure around here. He's looked to me for guidance almost his entire life, it's always been him, Sean and myself. Ana's a loving mother and all, but us three connected. It's just taking him some time. And I was joking about the 'hate' word." Brand smiled down, this time lifting Amy's chin so she could see. "That's a powerful word. A destructive one, too. You're being tested dear, and so far passing with flying colors." "I don't feel like a 'parental figure'," she said, "I don't think I'm good at it. My parents helped so much with Stephen...I just don't have the backbone for it. I'm a lot younger than you, and not even half as 'grown up'. It just feels like...I dunno...like he knows I'm just a kid. Does that make sense?" Amy sighed, easing back against the Mighty Hero again. "Maybe they can smell fear or something. It's just like...gah, I'm horrible at being an adult." "Good. Don't. I don't know if I would like an adult Amy Campbell. You're perfect the way you are." With yet another sigh, her eyes closed. "I just wish I wasn't afraid of so much. I'm afraid of your kids, now your damned title. I'm really not looking forward to Thursday." "It's okay to lose, Aimzy..." Brandon just begged for a fight sometimes. "Unless you want to spend the next week on this couch, try to keep that comment behind your lips next time." she frowned, "It's not losing I'm afraid of. It's choking up and not being able to lay a hand on you, or to be treated like I'm made of glass." "You worry about yourself. Look at the prize, you pin me and you're the first female World Champion. Simple as that. Don't expect me to drop you to the mat with my heart punch, but stop worrying that'll I'll treat you like you'll break. I don't do that when we're under the covers do I?" Brandon really didn't realize when Amy was trying to be serious. He always countered with a joke. It was habit. "I don't give a shit about titles!" That snapped him halfway to reality. "They're a perk. You always say 'Anybody who doesn't want the World title shouldn't be here', so I keep my mouth shut...but I wrestle because, for whatever reason, I'm good at it and it keeps me sane and eating. I like the lifestyle, I just don't care much for the big 'prizes'." she continued, "I don't need gold to prove my worth, and don't much care to. If someone pisses me off, I'll kick the snot out of them...aside from that, I'm not out for anything. The only motivation I have going into this match is motivation not to fight you." Brandon sat up a little, "Then don't. No-show it. If you don't want to fight me, then sit in the back when the music is played and let everyone down. It's always been your choice." Aimz quickly shot up, standing in front of where he sat. "Being a champion gives you no stitch of a right to go assuming shit like that about me. How the fuck can you even say that?!" There was a good reason Amy avoided "in-ring" stuff. She was terrified of letting people down - call it an obsessive compulsive perfectionism if you will, but it was more along the lines of performance anxiety. Either way, hearing Brandon of all people say that nearly broke her... "Forget how! WHY would you say that?! You're telling me I'd be letting people down by not wanting to try taking something that means so much to my fucking fiance away from him...that's stupid. God!" she growled, "Forgive me for even caring." "I'm saying that Aimz, the pro wrestler, would be letting the fans, who paid their ticket price to see her wrestle, not her fiance, but Brand Frontier for the World title, down. You don't want to get in the ring, for the belt or not, then don't. Forfeit the match, we can get someone else to cover it. I'm just saying it's that simple." Brand sighed himself now. "I don't want to forgive you for caring either. That's one of the reasons I love you, but unless you tell me what's really in your head I don't understand why you're so upset. It's just another match." "It's not just another match. When you face Crusader or Deacon, you don't have to think about going to bed with them the same night knowing you're the cause of every bruise and tender spot." She sat back down, but bit her lip. "I don't think you get it. It's simple...I don't want to hurt you, but I also don't want harsh feelings because you treated me too rough, or not rough enough." "I guess then all we can do is trust in each other to do what we should." Brandon said it as if it really was that simple. In his mind, it couldn't be more so. In Aimz' mind, if he stepped back an inch he might've noticed the snag.
