Ghosts Of The Past [6] - Crossed Wires

18 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Go back to 5... September 25th 1999. Amy Campbell and Kellen Kinkade lay asleep beside one another having defied the odds. Their coming together had almost been like a rose blossoming in the middle of the desert, something beautiful had occurred in the most unlikely and unforgiving of places. But the unfortunate reality for their time with one another was that as something beautiful they still remained in that unforgiving place. Just like the rose their surroundings would kill everything that they were sooner rather than later. For now there was trust, it wasn't love but they both gave one another something that made the other feel better. Kinkade made Amy feel safe and Amy made Kinkade feel alive, when you walk the streets of Brooklyn alone you're insecure and when you hurt people badly to make money you begin to question whether or not you're capable of feeling anything at all. It didn't take long for their surroundings to take a deadly grip however, and their trust was soon stretched beyond its snapping point. Victims of circumstance, coincidence and confusion. Fast asleep thanks largely to fatigue and intoxication neither of them heard the window of Kinkade's small apartment being carefully slid open. "Motherfucker said it was compensation." A voice whispered from outside. "Did he now?" Another voice said "Punk ass bitch is about to compensate my broken nose with a few pints of blood." Sneaking quietly through the now open window crept the individual who Kinkade had beaten to a pulp just two days ago in a prize fight. His face was swollen and his nose sported a large bandage having been broken. He stepped slowly down onto the floor trying not to squash or step upon anything that'd make a lot of noise, as he did so he slowly pulled out a handgun from his belt and pointed it over in the direction of the sleeping Kinkade before noticing the female lying next to him. "Shit." He whispered to himself "Wondered where that ho vanished to, was looking forward to making her scream... guess she still will, just from a less pleasurable kinda pain." "What the hell you doin' in there?" His partner whispered through the window anxiously "Just grease that fuck and lets go." "He's got some slut with him." The gun toting man replied "Shall I do her too?" "Naw man... I ain't seen her around here before, how do you know she ain't got some rich parents that'll be down here waving money around and asking questions?" His partner whispered back. "Fuck it... I'm gonna do her anyways." The gun toting man said again before turning his head to see something on the table beside him. Kinkade's jacket. He reached inside and pulled out a thick pile of cash, quickly flicking through it before stuffing it into his pocket and looking back over to the sleeping Kinkade. "You got lucky tonight punk, I'll shoot your white ass some other time." He whispered, putting away his gun and quickly clambering back out of the window before sliding it shut. A few hours passed before either of the sleeping couple would awake, but the one who would was entirely expected to - She wasn't at all accustomed to these conditions. So when Amy stirred awake and rubbed her eyes, she was greeted with an unfamilliar sight. A new room, a new environment entirely...a new friend beside her? She quickly sat up to survey his face, but found herself tossed back to the pillow not by any physical means, but courtesy of far too much alcohol the evening before. With the headache somewhere next to being under control, and shortly after her stomach decided to settle where it belonged - which was nowhere near her throat - the redhead stood up to look around, seeing as she didn't entirely recall the tour she'd gotten the previous night. 'Hmm,' She thought, glimpsing out into the kitchen. 'Not too bad.' Not too bad...until a small reflective surface caught her eye. Grinning a bit at a 'Distracted by shiny things' barb that jumped to mind, Amy tip-toed her way out and into the kitchen where, rather nonchalantly placed on the table, was a small square mirror... ...And some blades... ...And a baggie... ...And she had to get out of this hell hole, away from this man - Fast. And she did so. Within a few moments, Amy was ready for takeoff with her bookbag, counting any money she'd had just to be certain before she darted out the door, wondering whether or not she dared to flag down a cab in the neighborhood Kinkade had shoved her into the heart of. As she slipped through the door and into the hall quietly, Kellen Kinkade stirred in his bed. Turning over and reaching over to the other side to put an arm around her Kinkade quickly found he was reaching for something that was no longer there. The penny dropped after a couple of moments sweeping his arm across the bed, Kinkade opened his good eye to the sight of nothing beside him. Slowly sitting up Kinkade stretched out his arms and yawned before speaking up "Red, where you at?" Kinkade slowly pulled himself out of bed before moving over to the open window across the room from where the wind was blowing his curtains furiously around. A quick slam shut followed before Kinkade turned back to the room. "Listen Amy." He began speaking out loud "I've been thinking, maybe it's better if we both hit the road and get the fuck out of this shithole. Place is eating me alive and I don't want to see it do the same to you." Moving across the room Kinkade sat at the corner of his bed to pull on some pants and light a cigarette. "Ah shit." He grumbled as though something had just dawned upon him "I guess you've seen that drug paraphernalia in the kitchen, don't worry because it isn't mine. Not in the users sense anyway... I've sold a little here and there to keep this roof over my head and now and then people want to try it out on site to make sure they ain't getting junk." Silence followed as Kinkade scratched his head for a moment and looked worried at the lack of reply "Hey look... I hope that isn't what this cold shoulder crap is about, you knew since the minute you met me that I was less than sqeaky clean. This isn't something to lock yourself in the bathroom ov-" He stopped in his tracks as he looked through the kitchen into a bathroom that didn't have a closed door, but an open door to reveal nobody inside. A quick turn of the head and narrowing of the eyes saw Kinkade shoot a glare over to the door of his room... which was still slightly open with the chain hanging off. "No." He growled to himself before making his way over to the door, opening it and looking out into the lobby before slamming it shut furiously before putting his hands upon his head "No no, fuck no." Priorities quickly took place and Kinkade rushed over to his jacket where close to fifteen hundred dollars of prize fight money was kept, he threw his hand into the inside pocket and came out empty. A few seconds of viciously shaking the jacket saw nothing drop out, something that prompted Kinkade to hurl it across the room and scream "THAT FUCKING THIEVING BITCH!" Kinkade had his wires crossed, unaware of the two street thugs who'd crept into his room during the night and taken the money he was sure that Amy had slept with him before taking the cash and getting out of dodge. "Idiot, you're a fucking idiot." He snarled at himself almost pulling his own hair out as he stormed into the kitchen to tear open the fridge in search of a Southern Comfort bottle to put him out of his misery... only to find none remaining. A glance over to the drug material on the desk saw a quick battle with self esteem fought and lost as Kinkade quickly set up a line and snorted it. Making his way into the bathroom Kinkade stared into the mirror at himself before screaming with rage and driving his fist into it with a shattering thud. He slumped to the floor consumed with feelings of betrayal and held his head in his hands. As the wound in his hand lost blood, the thoughts in his head lost faith in humanity for the final time. -Fin-
