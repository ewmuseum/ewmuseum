Interview

10 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"No. Unacceptable." Ivy paced her hotel room in her jammies - a buttoned up NY Yankees baseball jersey and black spandex shorts. A fitted baseball cap was backwards on her head, holding her hair back. "I'm serious, Nine," she continued, "You accept that deal and I'm walking." She's on the cell phone with Nine - Richard Steward, keyboardist for Valerian's Garden - giving him a verbal bitch- slapping. As other hotel guests pass by, they look in at the predatory blonde, but most are too afraid to actually say anything. "You can do better than that, so much better," continued Ivy, "Listen, we've got Warfare in Detroit, then I'll be back in L.A. on Friday morning... hold off until then and I'll point out exactly what the problem is." One person who was markedly unafraid of Ivy's rants slipped into the room and sat down on the edge of the bed. The Hardcore Champion, Aimz, leaned her elbows on her knees as she looked impatient for Ivy to get off the phone. Ivy turned around and jumped slightly, not expecting to see the Red Raver sitting there, She smiled and gave her the "Heavy Metal" hand gesture, Aimz returning it in kind. "She did?" asked Ivy, "See, Angel's always thinking. Listen, I gotta get going. Right. Will call you tonight." Ivy hung up the phone and turned toward her guest, "What's goin' on, kiddo?" "Too little." Amy looked 'frantically calm', if possible. She sighed sleepily, but at the same time her eyes were wide with a driven need to keep from being idle. "Brandon's sleeping...sleeping." "Heh," replied Ivy, "Catches up to everyone at some point or another. 'Cept you, it seems." She tossed her phone into her bag and pulled a pair of ankle socks out of it, "How's the ribs feeling?" "Like shit. But I'm back next week if I can be...no more of this just standing around to be stared at bull - gots ta earn my keep!" The redhead stretched eeeever so slightly, cringing and releasing a loud breath when her ribs 'tapped out'. "I'm sick of sitting around and being talked about." Ivy laughed. "You saw that too, huh? Guess we were right about Marder after all. Came up lame at Cyberslam, perfectly healthy two, three weeks later. That's just bad business... who's gonna want a program with him again?" "Oh, like I wanted one in the first place. He somehow had the title, that's what mattered to me. The fact that he's a snotty little fuck who wrestles like his tights should be lime green and then overplays injuries just to make himself look like a better loser came later." She laid back, glancing over to Ivy with an amused smirk. "Isn't it kinda funny how someone can just look over me, see me as an obstacle to overcome before they're decent enough to even lick Brandon's feet and then end up looking up at me while I hold 'their' title? It's even funnier how I called him on the injuries before we even planned the CyberSlam match...I knew he'd play them up so I'd look like I got an easy win." "That's what separates the men from the boys," replied Ivy, smiling, "So to speak. Wait until the end of the year, watch who wrestles at all three Countdown shows... there's no ego involved, It's just the workhorses who don't know how to say 'no' to work." "But now it's your belt - you've got the power. Feels good, don't it?" "Yep. You know you've done something when you suddenly have people coming to you going 'Oh...well I never cared about that title anyway, the fans don't care, it's just some cheapo belt.'. People always do that...they consider me a threat or somethin'." She grinned, "And nobody's even seen me work yet. Kinkade's coming close to pissing me off enough for it, though...that's why Brandon always lectures me on how if I use weapons, people'll use 'em back - he knows I'm ready to let loose with this thing..." "But yeah...how're you doing, woman?" "I'm alright," replied Ivy, unconvinced, "trying to balance about twenty thousand different jobs in only twenty four hours a day is pretty rough. Hopefully I'll be able to get Angel a deal soon and can actually get those eleven minutes of sleep a night." "You don't sleep? Poor baby..." Amy pouted, dripping playful sarcasm. "In case you hadn't noticed, some of us don't even have the luxury of eleven minutes...I'd kill for so many things to occupy my time, instead of just laying around and doing nothing while I hope my brain switches off long enough for a little shut-eye." "Haha," replied Ivy, "Want some of my day? Wanna find Valerian's Garden a record contract? Wanna muck your way through a bout of Eli Flair moodiness? Wanna write a tour diary and a column?" She clapped her hands together, grabbed Amy's, and swung her up like they were dancing. "I've got it! You can take half my attitude... it only gets me in trouble. I'll trade you for a few shots to the ribs from Kinkade... s'gotta be less painful." Ivy let Amy go, and she hit the bed, giggling. "Congrats, Ivy...you just scared me for the very first time ever." Aimz had a clap of her own, laughing. "And you wanna try living with Brandon? Let alone his kids..." She grumbled, "Twenty four hours a day, seven days a week...it's like one eternal episode of Superman, and not the cool one, either." Ivy shuddered. "Now you've scared me. I love my lil' niece MJ, but she's also five months old... not enough to make irrational noise. I can't imagine having a three year old and a seven year old." She stopped for a second. "Three and seven? That sounds right. But you're surviving, ain't ya? I'm serious, when I say I love Sean like you can't imagine... but we'd both go nuts without our personal space." "Four and seven...and then three and some change. I'm apparently a fit parent now, so Stephen's down in Virginia with us too. If it weren't for BL being himself as usual and putting me in a seperate room from him, I wouldn't be surviving...but for now I am." "Wait..." The redhead paused for a second, then let out a short burst of a laugh."I'm making it sound like hell! It's only like second or third level hell...you know how I feel about Brandon - it's worth it. Yes, even though he makes me eat actual food and not just order pizzas." "It's good for ya," joked Ivy, "actual food. I can't cook to save my life, but it hasn't stopped me yet. Oh, shame on me, forgetting about Stephen. Maybe if I've ever met him, I'd remember more clearly." Ivy shook her head. "Twenty years old, raising a kid on one hand, helping raise your man's kids on the other... I really don't envy your life, Amy... just your strength." "You're mistaking insanity for strength. I'm crazy enough to tackle all of that and almost really enjoy it. It's not like I could be doing anything better with my life...well, aside from trying to break my previous record of sitting perfectly still for an entire fifty seconds - God bless obsessive compulsive disorder...I love being a nutjob." She declared it proudly, followed by a wink. Truth be told, OCD wasn't that bad...but it wreaked havoc with her sleeping patterns, attention span and mental state - which was the exact reason why Amy Campbell really could 'Go from Aimz to bitch in .02 seconds flat'. "Could be worse," mused Ivy, "You could be an overly- aggro psycho bitch with a permanent chip on her shoulder. And they tell me I'm normal and well- adjusted." She leaned back and looked at her left leg - solid muscle below smooth, pale skin - decorated with a single tattoo and a telling circular scar. "Seriously, Aimz... we're all a little fucked in the head. Just be glad you know what to call yours." "Feels kinda weird, though. BL's the 'normal' one...he doesn't even have half of the so-called issues I do, so it's kinda trying sometimes 'n stuff. Ah, I dunno...I'm rambling." Amy shifted a little, "This is why I'm thankful for you and everyone...gives me something to occupy my time. When I first came here, I'd just be getting drunk or...ehm...worse when I got bored like this. You're a savior. Crazy, yes...but still a saint." She finished, grinning. Ivy stood up, with purpose. "Well then, shall we call it even?" Amy looked at her inquisitively. "I need to take about an hour off from being me... hang out a bit while I shower, and we get something to eat?" "Works for me." Back to the road...the injury'd held her at bay too long.
