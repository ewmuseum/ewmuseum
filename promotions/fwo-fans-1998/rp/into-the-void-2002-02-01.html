Into The Void

1 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tuesday, January 29th, 2002. 3:15 GMT ‘Tried to save myself . . . Look around you. The London airport is quite large. Probably really easy to lose yourself in its depths. You've never heard so many languages around you at once, thick english accents, french, german, spanish. It seems much cleaner than what you're used to. None of those fucking immigrants that you despise. Look at all of these people, so much more educated than anyone back home. Bilingual, they understand you and the fifteen other spoken languages that come through this place every day. Ah, you've found the gate... excellent. We'll be on the airplane and back in the States sometime tomorrow, or would it be later today. Those time zones can be quite confusing at times, can't they? Sorry... I digressed there for a moment. These new security measures really are a bitch. Like you would have the balls to try and blow up a plane. You're so worthless you couldn't even kill yourself. I shouldn't keep bringing that up to you. Must be a real killer on what little self-esteem you have left, huh? Looks like you've set the metal detector off, good one. What was it? Oh, I see it, the belt you were wearing. And through again... you're good this time. And your bags should be all set, maybe even already loaded on the plane. That's if these idiots know what they're doing. Well... I guess you can't blame them for slipping up once in awhile. I mean, they only make minimum wage at the most. What's the point in protecting everyone else if you can't even make a living for yourself? But you wouldn't know about that. You're pretty much financially set. You know, you should really get out of that cruddy apartment in Detroit. You could afford a house or something... maybe even a– "Shut up," he whispered under his breath. Hey, look over at that cute stewardess, I think she heard you say that. That's a pretty mean look on her face, isn't it? You're gonna be screwed over badly on the in-flight meal and snacks. Ah well, that food tastes like shit anyway. I'd rather starve than eat it. God, you're hopeless. You can't even find your seat? Pa-the-tic. Here comes the lady that hates you... she's coming up from behind... and... TAP! Hey, nice reflexes, I don't think she's gonna kill you for that. Be a nice guy, greet her. That's it, good boy. Now you're explaining your hopeless self. Look-at-that! She found your seat for you. These are kind of comfortable. And a window-seat at that. What a deal. Now if we happen to plunge into the Atlantic, you'll be able to see it right from your window. These fWo heads are really nice to you. Uh-oh. Just saw something out of the corner of your left eye. At least I can use your peripherals better than you can. Check it out, sitting right across from you, it's Flair. Too bad this was an airplane. Hell, who cares, why don't you just go break his legs or something. You know you want to. But then again, you've never had the balls to do anything decisive by yourself. That's what makes you such a loser. Flair's over there with that fine piece of ass McGinnis and Sean Stevens. Better yet, don't go over there, you'd be dead quite quickly. On second thought, leave Flair alone. He's not worth my precious time right now. Wonder who or what we'll be sitting next to for the next twelve or thirteen hours. Here comes someone now. A big African-American. Must be at least six-six, maybe more. Shiny bald head too. And he's the monkey in the middle. Finally, the person on the outside is sitting next to the big man. He looks familiar, can't place a name with the face though. I know I've seen that person before... hell.. Who could it be!? Dammit, this is going to bug me until I figure it out. "Hi Jon," he looked over past the black man and to the man sitting on the aisle-seat. Jon Crisp! I knew who it was. Sometimes you forget names when you don't see someone in nearly six months. I thought that guy had died somewhere in New Orleans. Too much bourbon or something for him. Anyway... the el capitan is speaking, almost take-off time. That gargantuan black man is looking at you. Keep looking straight ahead. "Excuse me... aren't you Donaven Winters?" The man spoke through a heavy english accent. He reminds me of Lennox Lewis without hair... heh. "I... used to be..." . . . but myself keeps slipping away'
