Injured and Contract and Barrier and Arrival and Shocked and Injured

11 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kate Young-Harmen continued to carry the bags, trying desperately not to have her own arm pop out of it's socket. She had been in quite the physical shape back in the days of valeting for Flyer, in case there ever was a chance she might be allowed in the ring to wrestle. Those days were rare, and even rarer now, so most of her muscles had deteriorated over time. Flyer stopped her, pointing to his key and placing it into the lock. It didn't open at first, which raised a few eyebrows. Four to be exact. He tried it again, and the electronic key card once again blinked red. Flyer tried the knob, and found it to be locked shut anyway. Flyer sighed, and began to turn away, heading back to the downstairs lobby before the door swung up. Unbeknownst to the couple, this room was actually the hotel room for the young Allen Speck. He stood there, hands on his hips as he himself raised an eyebrow. "Can... I help you?" Flyer jerked his head around, which twisted his midsection and caused him to wince in pain. He wondered to himself, even when he was healed, if he would wince while turning his body. He looked at Speck, and he knew he was a wrestler. Thick shoulders, cut arms, these things stick out like a red dress in the midst of a black tie crowd. The only thing he couldn't place was why Speck was shirtless. The sling was understandable. "Uhmmm, did you play strip go-fish too?" Flyer muttered, before Kate tried to elbow him in the midsection. She stopped herself before actually doing it, but even the motion made Flyer think about the pain it would have caused. That's when Speck was trailed by another. A young woman with a bright and vibrant smile lightly touched Speck on the arm before turning her attention to Flyer and Kate. "Hey! I don't think we've met! I'm Leigh, and this is Allen," she said, extending her hand to Kate first and shaking it. Flyer returned the favor. "I'm Jack, but everyone just calls me Fly. This is Kate, my wife..." Flyer trailed off. "Sorry if I'm interrupting something..." Leigh looked at Allen, and then back at Flyer, and then once more at Allen. Suddenly, it hit her. "Oh, no! No! We're not like that. I was just helping him change." "You know. Arm in sling." Allen said, pointing to his injury. He then groaned, thinking about the days proceedings, and most notably, the clothes he had just undressed from. "Yeah, I know what it's like," Flyer said, before lifting his shirt to reveal the bandages underneath. Allen nodded, and Leigh looked a bit concerned. "Guess we're both casualties of the business, eh?" "More ways than one." Allen muttered, leaning up against the doorway. Leigh sighed in response. "Oh, he's just mad because he gets to be on television every week." "Leigh, that's not it. It's because I need to wear this stupid robe and walk around with this stupid book and pretend I live my life in a video game." Speck sighed once more, before cracking his neck. "Ah... bad gimmick?" Flyer rubbed his goatee. "Probably the worst." "Been there, done that. Ever try selling snow? I mean, I had a great time doing it because I'm like that, but I know what it's like to walk out there and be thought of as a joke." Flyer shook his head. "But, hell, it got me over when I was dead weight. I remember hearing pin drops during my entrances, and now I can't go to arenas, even as a bad guy, and not have a few fans cheer me." Flyer chuckled to himself. "I guess every bad thing does have a bright side." Speck nodded his head, practically ignoring Flyer's thoughts on the subject. He didn't really care what anyone said on the matter though, so it wasn't anything personal. There was a brief moment of awkward silence, with Speck and Flyer both looking at their feet as Kate and Leigh both smiled at one another as if they were the Frenchie. Kate broke that silence by holding up their key card. "Does anyone know which room we have?" Leigh took it out of her hand, and read the numbers. "Ah, right down the hall, second last door." She smiled in return, before handing the card back to Flyer this time. Kate nodded, as Flyer shook Allen's hand once more. "See ya around I guess." "Hopefully without the sling," Allen paused. "Or the book or the robe." Flyer nodded, and they turned their way back toward their actual locker room. Speck sighed, and turned himself back into their room. Leigh could be heard saying "They were nice, weren't they?" before the door closed completely. On the other side of the spectrum, Kate threw her head onto Flyer's shoulders, as they slowly made their way down the hall. "They'd make a cute couple." "You think?" Flyer said, stopping their walk in mid-stride. She nodded, and Flyer shrugged his shoulders. He chuckled a small bit before continuing his walk. "Not really much of a romantic night left for our anniversary, is there?" Reaching the door, Flyer inserted the key card and opened it. He motioned for Kate to join him, and she smiled before tossing the bag into the room. "Well, why don't we make the most of our last few hours?"
