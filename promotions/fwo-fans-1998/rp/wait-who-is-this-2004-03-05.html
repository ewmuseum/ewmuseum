Wait who is this

5 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Hey Folks.. The Narrator here. We haven't been formally introduced. Honestly that's for the best, I'd rather my name not be associated with these two losers. Let's just say, nerds aren't funny until there is someone there to make fun of em'. I'm that someone, pitiful existence eh?' "John! Johnny!" The frail almost sickly looking 18 year old bolted through the halls of an empty house. "John, fWo wrote us back! We're in!!" 'Yeah folks.. He weighs 140 pounds.. Ever heard of the 'Human Dart Principle'?' Ichabod Michaels stopped in his tracks and looked up.. "Hey! You shut up pal!" 'Oh the look on your face, yeah they can hear me. I mean, if they couldn't I would worry for your mental health friend..' "John!" Ichabod stopped as he realized John's door was closed." 'Oh lord have mercy. Last time we saw a closed door in this house Ichabod was testing out his genitalia..' "Hey what the heck the did I tell you mister!" 'Hahaha Heck? Mister? You're rich kid.' 'This is the part where Ichabod ignores my warning and bursts in the door any..' At that moment Ichabod did just that as the door swung open. It became obvious; bad picture quality, the glazed look in his eye, the heavy breathing, and the slight amount of drool forming at the tip of his mouth. 'Ok here is where the TV's audio ruins the idea of John being a normal 19 year old man... and .. one, two, three...' "BURRRRRRRRRRRRRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAIN BUSTAAAAAAAAAAAAAAH!" John turned his head to look at Ichabod and then back to the match "RARIATOOOOOOOOOOOOO!" 'Yeah look at how pitiful this is.. 6'2" 225 pounds of damn near solid muscle. He could be with women right now, or even just enjoying the beach. But NOOOO John Tohru is busy watching Puroresu tapes... What a tool..' "John! We GOT IN!" 'Well folks this is where we cut off for the day.. Trust me, you would rather NOT see the victory dance that these two are about to perform.. Until next time, I'm the narrator.. And if you dislike my abuse of the period too damn bad....... <-- did that one just to piss you off, Oh wait! Lets go dream sequence so I can teach you a little about John Tohru and Ichabod Michaels.' --- Ichabod Michaels and Johnny Tohru grew up best friends in Monterey California. Both were the average picked on geek in school. By the time they were twelve Ichabod had already reached his current size of 5'5" 140 pounds. He towered over most kids his age and thus became an even better target for the "Cool Kids" Johnny Tohru was different, a half japanese half american boy. He weighed 230 pounds at age twelve but only stood 4'2". The two turned to eachother for friendship and immediately became engrossed in the world of Anime and Video games. Whether it was Anime conventions in San Francisco or the big E3 convention held once a year they made sure to make every expo they could. I these places they were surrounded by there own and felt at home I suppose. Pretty freakin pathetic huh? But what do I know I'm just the narrator. Oh and there is nothing worse than seeing a 4'2" 230 pound kid pretending he is Cloud from Final Fantasy. I mean jesus Cosplay is fine sometimes but.. Somewhere between that image and a 5'5" 140 pound kid in a sailor suit yelling Moon Tiara Magic.. I just have to draw the line. Recently however things have been looking up for young mr. Tohru he hit a growth spurt finally. Of course.. a spoonful of sugar joined by a glass of vinegar.. His new impressive physique hasn't changed the fact that he is still a damn nerd... --- 'Now you know a little more about the boys and why they are so close. Here is something not include in that snipit. John and Ichabod have always relied on eachother. They never had anyone else. Now I'm not saying their never had anyone else to care for them. That's silly but take a look at a headline from 1991 in the Monterey Sun and you might have seen "Tragic Cheese Accident Kills 5 Million" That however is highly unlikely. Check the obituaries however and you would find four names of signifigance to these boys. Takeshi Tohru, Amanda Tohru-Adams, Archie Michaels, and Marie Anne Michaels died on January 21 1991. Now you might think. 'So What?" It's understandable, people die everyday. But these deaths left a hole in the lives of two young boys. Two boys who were pulled to eachother like a vortex. Two boys who have since been inseperable. Maybe it's a bit hypocritical of me, but I feel for them when people decide to insults these two. I suppose now it's enough.. and Now I can say to you, goodnight. And heaven help the fWo...
