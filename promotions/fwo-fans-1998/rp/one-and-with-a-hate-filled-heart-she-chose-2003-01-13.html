One: And with a hate-filled heart, she chose

13 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Who am I? You are Jessica, a beautiful and pure child of God. You are Kali Saturos, a fool who has been used throughout her life by bigger fools. You are Rei. Nothing more. Nothing less. Why do I exist? You exist because God created you. You exist because hate is needed to balance out the universe. You exist because you exist. Thinking any further will only damage your fragile mind. What is my purpose? Why, to serve the Lord of course! You exist to cause pain and suffering wherever you go, just as pain and suffering has been caused onto you. You don't need a purpose. Life is simply for living. What happens when I die? You will ascend to the Heavens and meet all of the ones you loved and have lost! You will cease to be what you are now, and become something far greater. That question is unknown. Anyone who claims to give you an answer is only lying to you. Why did everyone around me that I loved have to die? Was it my fault? Of course not! God decides when it is their time. You had nothing to do with it, do not blame yourself. Yes. You did not realize at that time that your purpose is death, so we had to take those around you that you loved- how few they may be- away from you in order to make you understand this. Don't try and explain death. It's not neccessary. It just happens. Leave it at that. Is it okay to hate my parents, even now that they're gone? No. Your parents gave you life, it is not right for you to hate them, no matter what your feelings are. Sure. They were assholes, we only placed them there to be your first victims. Don't judge yourself. Whatever you felt is in the past now, especially now that they're dead. Did I REALLY kill my parents? I certainly hope not, because murder is a sin! Even if you are never suspected for such an act by the government, God will still judge you if you are truly guilty. Of course. We only placed them there to be the object of your hatred, and to be the first ones you killed. Mission succesful. You were never a suspect in the case, and probably never will be, so just let it end. Why should I listen to you? Because it is God's will! Because we have made you what you are today. Listen to me, or don't listen to me. It is of no consequence. You must make a decision on your own for once in your life. Why am I here, in this wrestling orginization, now? You are there to spread love and the word of the Lord in a hate-filled atmosphere. You are there because it is the next phase of your destiny, and there is much sorrow to spread. You are there, because you are there. Make your own purpose out of it. All of you are correct, but in the end, I must choose one. And I choose.... Fin.
