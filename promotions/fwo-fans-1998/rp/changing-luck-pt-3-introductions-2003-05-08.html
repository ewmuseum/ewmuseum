Changing Luck pt. 3 - Introductions

8 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Luke Starkey dashed forward, wrapped his arms around the man's waist, and pulled backwards before his brain caught up with his body and he realized what he was doing. The truck raced past them, right through the space the man had occupied but a split-second before. The driver laid on the horn AND flipped them the finger as he went by. Both men landed on the sidewalk in a pile while a handful of people stared, wide-eyed, at the escapade they had just witnessed. Slowly, like swimming through grape jam, Luke's heart quit racing. He looked at the man he had just pulled from the path of a speeding truck, the man whose life he had in fact just saved, and saw eyes full of terror and disbelief. "Are you okay?" Luke asked. "I almost just got killed..." the man whispered. "You did," Luke offered gently. "And you just saved me," the man said, his voice still soft, shocked. Luke paused, a hint of a smile tugging the corners of his mouth. "I did." Luke let go of the stranger's waist and eased himself to a sitting position. People were running over to them now, their combined questions and queries and comments chirping like birds fighting over breadcrumbs.
