Industrial Girls - Part 1

13 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
February 1st... Sitting alone in a dank little Mexican bar wasn't everyone's idea of a great vacation...but Amy Campbell was there to work. Well...heal, but even after a week 'away from it all' with Valerian's Garden, her environment wouldn't allow a moment's rest. The sudden appearance of a familliar face made that all-too apparent... "I read the shows have kicked all sorts'a ass. How'd they sound?" "Ivy!" Amy was startled for a second, she'd been staring off into space and failed to notice her elder friend coming up from behind. "You mean, Angel and them? They were amazing...she's got a shit load of energy for someone who's about two weeks removed from child birth." Poison Ivy smiled, as she pulled a chair away from another table and sat down backwards, leaning her elbows on the back of the chair. "That's our Angel. Bundle of energy that puts you - yes, YOU - to shame." She turned around as the waitress approached. "Dos Tequila, por favor - oh, Amy... feel like doin' a shot?" Too late, the redhead was busy trying to whip up a comeback. "HEY, are you calling me hypera-----did you say tequila?" "Yes," replied Ivy with a wink before her attention returned to the waitress. "Dos Tequila." "Goddamnitt," continued Ivy, "this whole weekend has bitten the big one. Eli gets arrested, I don't sleep for two days, some asshole tries to pick me up at a roadside diner... Next time you're gonna get shot and spend a few days hanging out with the Garden...please let me tag along." While awaiting the drinks with all the relentless enthusiasm of a small child at Christmas, Amy grinned and mumbled happily, "Don't worry...you'll be there. After I asked Sex about his name for a few hours, I really didn't have much else to do while Angel was out...you'll be welcome as hell next time." "Sex is an interesting one," mused Ivy. She stood up, taking off her suede jacket and draped it across the back of the chair, showing off toned arms and abs - she'd been working out more than usual in the past few days, it seemed. "How's my niece doing? When's she making her first trip to a wrestling show?" "Hopefully soon...I can see it now," Amy smirked, "Huge, grown men all 'woogie' and 'schmoopie'ing over the little packet of chaos..." She really had envisioned it, and a slight laugh accompanied the picture before she settled back into the little wooden chair. "So where's Sean?" "He's catching up on his sleep," replied Ivy, "When he can't sleep, he's considerate enough to get out of bed. When I can't sleep, I'm a bitch who tosses and turns and doesn't let him escape because I have a hard time sleeping anyway when he's not there." Ivy's train of thought was interrupted by the return of the waitress, with two shots on her tray. She offered lemon slices and salt to the two "non- native" drinkers, but Ivy and Amy both waved it off. "A su salud." They downed the shots, and Ivy motioned for two more. "But my sweetie is taking an early night for a change... he's been through a lot lately. And my mood's gone south so I'm no help... just needed a night to be Ivy McGinnis instead of Poison Ivy, y'know?" "I have trouble sleeping alone, too..." Amy's brow was furrowed a bit - it had been a few months since hard liquor had touched her tongue. "But since I'm alone about ninety nine percent of the time, I've kinda learned to deal with it." The second round came.....and went in as much time as it took to raise glasses to lips. Even then, the girls had started 'sizing' one another up and friendly looks were exchanged along with an unspoken challenge as Ivy called for thirds and Amy finished her thought through pursed lips... "And if your night to be Ivy McGinnis involves getting us both plastered with your own spending money in Mexico then so be it." She winked. "Not gonna torture me any...after all, I grew up in Atlantic Canada...February in Me-hi-co is heaven on earth." "I remember those days," said Ivy with a smile. Despite all their issues, Amy could tell how much she and Sean loved each other. "But it's been nearly a year now, and Sean and I have spent practically every night since then together." Ivy pulled the hair tie out and shook her head, sending blonde hair all over the table before pulling it back into another, tighter ponytail. "And don't talk to me about winter until you've spent one in New York City. I'll take twenty feet of snow over the world's biggest wind tunnel any day of the week." Shot number three: received. Shot number three: killed. "Another?" asked Ivy. "In a minute," replied Amy. "Lightweight." "It's been a minute. Another." When their fourth rounds were settling, the redhead was forced to wipe a tear from watering eyes, sending Ivy into a fit of chuckling as Amy swatted back. "Damnit, I haven't done THIS in a while...let me get my bearings again." She couldn't help but smile, "I've been hanging around Cap'n Sobriety too long..." "You're at a disadvantage anyways, kiddo... One hundred percent Irish, and my pops definitely passed the drinkin' gene onto me." Ivy's face seemed to light up with a melancholy- filled smile at the mention of her father. "We need to go out and do something some night, don't you think? I dunno how long we're gonna be south 'a the border but I'm in the mood to crash a whole bunch'a Spring Break parties, y'know? Just show up with the guys, cause a stir, bitch- slap Carson Daly." Ivy grabbed a slice of lemon from a passing tray and began to chew on it. "What?" she answered Amy's inquisitive glance, "I've managed to not have a cigarette since I quit last summer... this is how I deal with cravings. When I don't have Sean's neck to chew on, at least." She giggled mischievously. "I think..." A giggle both at her own thoughts and Ivy's comments interrupted Amy's sentence momentarily, "...I think I already did that once." "Bitchslapped Daly, that is..." She snickered again, "I'm pretty sure that's why those guys in New York arrested me last year..........ha," Another little laugh, "Brandon'd wet 'imself if he saw my 'record'..." "Believe it or not, my record's almost complet'ly clean." Shot number five ordered. "Two arrests for inciting a riot but let me tell you a secret." Amy leaned in close, as Ivy looked around suspiciously, "I was framed." "You too?!" The redhead's eyes went wide as her mouth, "They get us every time! Lemme tell you 'bout this one time I was in Philly......." She paused, blinking. "Wait...no..not Philly, it was Virginia!" Blink blink. "........no............Mexico! Yeah, Me---wait, that's where we are now............................well............." Amy grinned a little. "Okay, so I was in this army surplus store...OOH! More glasses!" The eyebrow over her steel blue eye that served as the sore thumb to the deep brown one next to it cocked up at the waitress appeared yet again, this time casting the two a questioning look as they sat there, both supported by folded arms on the table that only budged to retrieve the drinks. "I think your roots are showing, girl." Ivy's comment brought on a questioning glance. "It's a blonde joke. I'm allowed because I'm blonde." She laughed, clinking Amy's shotglass. "Amy... I need a vacation. I need a long vacation where I don't have to have surgery, where I don't have to be part of some kind of off- camera storyline for the company... need to go visit my brother and his family, maybe. Or spend more than a night in my own bed. When do I get a vacation?" "I'll make you one!" Amy grinned from ear to ear, more enthusiastic than she'd been all night. She had an urge to be needlessly perky...one she didn't even realize. "So we'll...like...wait! I'll work double and make up for you!" She leaned in, developing an invisible, however supposedly ingenious little plot in her head, and her eyes were somehow meant to convey it to Ivy. "They'll never suspect a thing!" Ivy stared at her like she had six heads, before bursting into a righteous fit of laughter. "You're sloshed." "Am not!" "Are too!" "Am not!" "Are too!" "Am too!" "Are n---HEY!" Another fit of giggles. Another round was ordered, but the waitress 'mysteriously' disappeared after checking over the state of the girls, most notably the younger, redheaded one who hadn't seemed to have eaten yet that day. "I don't think they're gonna serve us anymore," observed Ivy as the waitress looked through her hand and inquisitive glance. "Think it's time to hit the bricks? They've probably got a halfway decent bar at the hotel." "You sure you can walk?" Amy grinned, her chin planted firmly on her forearm as she looked up at Ivy. "You don't look so hot..." Of course, that statement - not to mention mouthing the words for it - was a challenge in itself. "Watch and learn, lil' girl," said Ivy with a wink. She put her jacket back on and headed for the door, stumbling slightly only once. "That didn't count." Amy's success didn't exactly surpass Ivy's...she tripped swiftly. "As long as that didn't."
