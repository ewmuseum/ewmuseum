The Friday Bash - 11 - 11:30 PM - Tallywhackers

3 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
11:30 pm, The Tallywhackers You know, back in the day... James Cagle, otherwise known as DJ Skabble Wabble around these parts, and Davy 'Crockmastuh' Dingleberry didn't have the luxury of coming to a party, getting wasted and or drunk and or fried and or hammered and or smashed. In fact, they didn't even have water to drink, dammit! They were hardcore. Hard to the MF'N, G-D sunuva B core. They were so hardcore, all they had back when they were kids to drink were rocks. Little, tiny rocks. Or so they'd have you to believe. Truthfully, these guys were about as hardcore as a scrambled egg. Which isn't very hardcore, surprisingly. Oh sure, a scrambled egg may say it's hardcore...but hell, it's scrambled!! What is it gonna do to you, make you throw-up? Psh, sorry Mr. I'M SOOOO NOT HARDCORE!!! Having said that, DJ Skabble Wabble Double-Dog Dabble (James Cagle) was all, "I'll be a sumbitch" and wasted like a piece of paper being doodled on by a three year old kid... THOSE ARE TREES, DAMMIT~! TREES! "You
