Under the ruins. Part I

19 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Burned flesh. Vic Creid hadn’t smelled it but once before, that wasn’t the most pleasant of memories but one that pushed itself back to the front of his thoughts as he stirred. His body ached as he attempted to move. Slowly recognition set in and the juggernaut sprang into a sitting position blinking a few times to adjust to the lighting. It was fairly dark, he could hardly make out his surrounding, sparse as they were. His nose tickled and a sneeze soon followed. That was when he realized he was covered in a heavy dust. Concrete dust. Shaking his head it powdered off from his hair and he rubbed his eyes. That only made matters worse as his hands too were filthy with the coarse material. It stung. Trying his best to remove the foreign matter from his eyes Creid stood and a series of curse words soon followed as the six foot tall mammoth crashed his skull against a side turned pillar. The head clearance wasn’t much as he had discovered and the little bit he could make out with the limited light source indicated that the area wasn’t big at all. It seemed to be a boon from an unknown force. His memory began to return. The last thing he remembered was the explosion. He had been taking a stroll around the complex where the fWo was showing Sunday Slaughter. There was a commotion with the security force and a bunch of shouting and yelling he couldn’t be bothered to understand. The next thing he knew a throng of the natives rushed by. Throwing his meaty paws left and right he had managed to make headway through the crowd but he was finally overpowered… that’s the only way he could be overpowered… by a mob. Then there was an earthquake. Wait… there was smoke. The memory popped up as the acrid smell once again filled his nostrils, the burned flesh. It must have been a bomb. These bastards must have tried to kill them. Another cuss word exited Creid’s mouth as he finally and fully remembered what happened next. He had made his way to near the arena floor and was looking for someone, anyone he knew. There was a shout, someone familiar. When he turned he saw someone, possibly the only one that could make his heart sink the way it did. The man everyone called Brand Frontier was sprinting full tilt at him. Words were being forcefully used but the sheer panic of everyone around silenced them. They indeed fell on deaf ears. Creid remembered taking a step back, fists closed and ready for this… unprovoked assault seemingly coming at him. His right arm had cocked back ready to plant his heavy fist in to the face of his attacker, but something hit him from behind. Something heavy. That’s when Frontier had tackled him. Then it was now. After that and as hard as he tried Vic couldn’t remember anything until now… the dust, the smell. Brand. What about Frontier? The flickering bulb that had miraculously survived the collapse of everything around him barely lit the area. The air, what there was of it, was still thick with dust. Finally putting his hand to the back of his head he felt the blood, still sticky, that matted his hair. Undoubtedly he hadn’t been out for long. Creid pulled at a large misshapen slab of concrete that blocked most of the light in the small area. His massive muscles strained and with a mighty effort the block moved, casting more of the feeble illumination out in to the small open area. That was enough and Vic Creid found what he was dreading and expecting. Pinned under another mass of deconstructed framework was none other than Brand Frontier, the man that had basically saved his life. With a sneer of six years worth of animosity backing it across his face Vic hunched over and kneeled next to the unconscious ‘paragon’. With a sigh of reluctance and acceptance Creid began to dig out his supposed savior and soon Frontier was haphazardly propped against a convenient flat surface. Dizzy from the thin air and the exertion Creid hunched down next to Brand. With a heavy breath he reached over and gently smacked at his ‘enemy’. “Come on boy, open those damn eyes.” No response, a harder smack. “Brandy, you in there!?” Another smack, this one with the back of his hand. The situation had already begun to wear on the ‘Main Man’s’ nerves. “Come on you big pussy wake up!” This time Frontier moved, a grumble at first then a few words. Soon Brand’s eyes were flittering open. “SEAN!” He bolted upright with his eldest son’s name still fresh on his tongue. “JAROD!” The youngest of the Roche family was also on the recently cognoscente Brandon Roche’s mind. With the loud noise though came more dust settling from the convenient space they were afforded with the cave in. Brandon’s heaving lungs filled with even more dust and he coughed as he instinctively tried to get to his feet. It was a lost cause though as he too was a bit dizzy from the predicament and settled down next to the guy that had slapped him in to consciousness. His son’s were safe, in Ireland with their mother and his dream realm had returned back to the darkest recesses of his mind. Wait… Vic Creid? The name popped in his head as he too realized where he was and the events that had led to him sitting next to a man he hated so. “You… you okay?” Craning his sore neck in the direction of the Alaskan Juggernaut Brandon steeled himself for what he knew would be a tirade on their current conditions and surroundings. “Just peachy Brandy… just peachy…” It wasn’t exactly what Brandon had come to expect from the loud and overbearing fWo Superstar but there was a reasonable grating to his voice to have let Brandon know even if they were in the dark who the words came from. “Now what do you expect us to do? Big hero man saves the day and traps us all at the same time. Good job.” The sneer had never left his face but with these words it deepened allowing Brandon to confirm what he always knew… Vic Creid was the worlds biggest asshole. “Tha… thanks for the compliment pal.” The stuttering in Brandon’s words came at an inopportune time. In a situation like this he couldn’t show weakness, that would be like fixing a shark a Bloody Mary using your own life’s fluid. “At least your ‘less than one percent fat’ ass is still breathing…” Rising to his feet Creid was evidently on the edge of snapping and Brandon didn’t look like he cared at this point. Smirking a bit at how easy he had flustered the big man already Brandon just blinked and waited for the next smart ass comment from the former Internet Champion. “You just wish you were looking this good Brandy. Even sitting here covered in concrete dust and smelling like the walking dead I’m looking better than your best day.” Achingly but defiantly Brandon stepped to his feet and stood mere inches from Vic Creid face. “Still doesn’t keep you from being a sorry bastard!” The hatred between the two mammoths bubbled under their words. Creid, of course, lost his cool first and shoved Brand Frontier back. Hard. Brand didn’t really expect a strike given the situation so he stumbled back father than normal and knocked against a leaning and fractured wall. More dust pelted them from the makeshift roof. Brand quickly regained his footing though and he too participated in the shoving, driving Creid back a step or two. He caught and steadied himself quickly though and the two began to step to each other when another section of the default ceiling came crashing down, temporarily separating them. As the remains of the support structure creaked and moaned under it’s own weight the two warriors stepped to each other once again. Under the ruins. Part II
