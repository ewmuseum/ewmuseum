Here we go again

9 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Here we go again February 09, 2000 (Fade in. On the screen, we can only see a sheet of notebook paper. In the background, someone is singing Burning by Manowar...that is if you could call THAT singing) TUG Productions presents (Someone tosses the sheet aside revealing another sheet of paper) An fWo film (The sequence repeats) Scott Candyman Collins  That chick with no name Stone Brian Funny nickname Cage and John Im crazy...wait, no Im not...wait yes I am Malcon in The end (not really) of the (Skittles) rainbow (That was the last piece of paper, and now we can see the inside of a locker room. Apparently, Brittany, the girlfriend of Mike Bear, had been holding the paper. Shes wearing a leather jacket that looks like it belongs to Mike, high heels, and a rather tight pair of leather pants. Her hair is dyed black. Wretch is in the background wearing a black wig, red tights, boots, and a shirt with Plague crudely written across it. Mike and Fear are arguing in the middle of the room. Fear is wearing black shorts and boots, his hair tied into numerous pony tails, since he obviously didnt want to burn his hair for dreadlocks. Mike is wearing his regular get up except for a shirt with Riders written crudely across it) Mike: Cmon Fear, please? Its not too late, we can just refilm this. Fear: You promised! I dont want to play him! It lowers my self esteem, man. When you had me come out in the apron, that was it. No more, you play Collins! Mike: Oh fine, be that way. Brit, could you hold up the last sign again? Brittany: Sure. (The last sign depicting The end of the (Skittles) rainbow is held up again and brought back down. Mike is on his knees in front of Brittany, while Fear is hitting himself over the head with a chair and Wretch is jumping of a table onto the couch) Mike (whining): Stooooone, cmon, tell me your name. Brittany: You idiot, I told you this about a dozen times when we got together. What the hell is so hard about remembering my name? Mike: Well I call...those two guys War and Plague half the time. Ummm...what are their names again? Brittany: Oh for Gods sakes, its Brian and John! Have some consistency! Mike: Ill try, if I remember their names, will you tell me yours? Brittany: What are their names? Mike: Brian and...Plague? Brittany: Idiot. Fear: Look, Im hardcore now! Wretch: I think I belong in the cruiserweight division, even though Im insane! No, wait, oh yeah, Im crazy. Actually Im... Brittany: Jeez, John, how about some consistency from you too!? Wretch: Ummmm, yes? Brittany: I think we need some better jokes. Mike: Well weve been ripping on Collins and the Riders so long, were running out. Just calling him a Cake-Baking, Pussy-Whipped Candyman just isnt good enough anymore. Fear: Ill try and work on some new stuff for him. You guys just go film the other segment. (The camera fades out)
