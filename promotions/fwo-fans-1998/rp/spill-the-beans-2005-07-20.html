Spill the Beans

20 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Not a cloud in the sky, and the sun was bright and hot. A perfect day to relax in the backyard, sitting in the Jacuzzi. Sally Apostle and David Cross let the water churn around them as they sipped their iced tea. Sally sat low in the tub, letting the water cover her bare breasts. Her hand slid under the water and rubbed Crucifix on the bare thigh. She could feel the tension in his leg as he set his glass down on the deck surrounding the tub. "Would you please relax?" she asked as she dipped her hair into the water. "You're kinda killing the mood here." David nodded and kissed her on the forehead. "You're sure Alex won't be coming home?" Sally sighed. "He's on a promotional tour in Tampa. He won't be back for days yet." The two sat in silence with their eyes closed, feeling the sunlight on their faces. "Maybe we should tell him about us." She couldn't see it, but David nodded in agreement. "Yeah. We can't keep this up." "Did you just say you can't keep it up?" Sally teased as she ran her fingernails across his stomach. David pressed his hand against hers. "You're terrible. That's one of the things I love about you." Sally dipped her fingers into her glass and flicked the iced liquid in David's face. "So you love me now, do you?" Cross wiped his face and smiled. "I always did." His eyes were still closed and the sun was warm on his face. After a few moments, David became very aware of the bubbles in the water. There was no other sound to be heard. He opened an eye and looked over at Sally, who was staring at him with an awkward look on her face. The uncomfortable feeling spread to him as well. "I... probably shouldn't have said that. I'm sorry." He tried to blow off his comment. "My mind's going in every direction." Sally seemed to welcome the distraction. "She admitted that Sarah is alive?" "She didn't hide it. Sarah's somewhere in Bangkok." Sally sipped her tea. "Brandish snatched her?" "Nick was working for someone. He grabbed the girl because he was paid to." "Who has her? Why would they want her?" "Vic isn't the most-loved man on the planet. But it doesn't make sense to grab her to get back at him. He thinks she's dead. She's suffering just as much as he is." "Probably more." Sally felt a certain empathy for the little girl that she'd never met. "That poor girl. David, you have to tell the police." "Dakota isn't exactly a credible source. Neither is Brandish, if they could even get him to talk. My history's not so hot either." David splashed water on his face. "So a crazy guy telling the police that a sociopath kidnapped a girl, and a coked-up stripper can verify it, isn't exactly going to hold water." "What then?" David took a breath. "I have to tell him." "You think he'll believe you?" "For Sarah's sake, I hope so." The two sat in silence. Sally reached over and sipped her iced tea again, while Crucifix let his hand rest on the deck. His fingers wrapped around the pair of trunks he'd been wearing earlier and considered putting them on when a voice distracted him. "Is Dave here? That looks like his car out front." Neither Sally nor David wanted to believe it when they heard the voice. They stared at each other, then turned as the patio door slid open. There stood the Natural, Alex Creed. Needless to say, he looked quite surprised at what he was seeing. "What the Hell?" Sally quickly snatched her bikini top off the deck and slipped into the middle of the tub, frantically trying to put it on. "Alex..." David grabbed his trunks and fumbled around in the tub. "I can explain this. It's not what it looks like." Alex's shocked look started to develop a bit of an edge to it. "It looks like you're sitting in the hot tub with my Mother. Naked." David finished dressing and crawled out of the tub. "Okay, it's exactly how it looks." Sally followed David out and found herself too embarrassed to look into her son's face. "We wanted to tell you, baby. We really did." The veins in the Mecca's chest disappeared into his tank top, while his eyes showed just how betrayed he felt. "So this has been happening for awhile? The two of you lying to me?" David put a hand on Alex's shoulder and hung his head in shame. "We were just waiting for the right time to tell you." Alex looked at the hand resting on his shoulder.
