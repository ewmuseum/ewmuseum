Past, Present, & Future

25 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Past "The Wild Child" Covington Carmichael prepares himself in the squared circle while the Lou Thesz, the NWA Undisputed World Heavyweight Champion slowly made his way to the ring. A chorus of jeers engulfed the small arena as Lou Thesz walked up the steel plated stares; he made his way into the ring. The Wild Child stared into his eyes as Lou Thesz slowly took the title off from around his waste and held it in the air. Lou Thesz gave handed the Undisputed World Heavyweight Championship to the referee who in return hoisted it into the air in display for all in attendance to see. Lou Thesz' left handed combed threw his thick black hair as the referee called for both to converge in the middle of the ring. Both Carmichael and Thesz listened with the utmost respect as the referee forewarned them of the guidelines for the night's bout. The bell sounded as Lou Thesz immediately challenged the Wild Child in the middle of the ring. He held his massive hand in the air; Covington Carmichael took grasp of it but was poked in the eyes. Lou Thesz capitalized with an Irish whip and a follow up clothesline... "The Wild Child" Covington Carmichael had been introduced to the world of wrestling and began down the long path that many generations in his family to come; would eventually follow. It was that night that Lou Thesz was defeated for the NWA Undisputed World Heavyweight Title. The Wild Child made his mark in wrestling but the following week Lou Thesz would win the title back. Covington Carmichael would go onto wrestle in the southern region of the United States while touring with the CWL three to four times a year. Covington Carmichael retired as one CWL's greatest wrestlers in the late 1940's through the early 1960's. During Covington Carmichael's later years in the CWL, before he retired in 1965, he began training his youngest son, "Speedy" Steve Covington. Steve Covington wasn't as big as many of the other wrestlers in the 1970's and 1980's but he was able to hold his own in the squared circle. Speedy brought a new, faster paced style of wrestling to the ring which had never been seen before. Steve Covington wrestled with the CWL right into the early 1990's but never had quite the impact his father did due to the perception that bigger men would sell more tickets to the events. Steve Covington would follow in the foot steps of his father and encourage his only son to become a wrestler. Along with the help of former tag team partner, Steve Covington and Randy Landings began training Carson Covington in the early 1990's. The Present "We've been getting complaints about your in-ring work, so we've opted to keep you down here for a few weeks." Silver Fox looked into the eyes of third generation superstar, Carson Covington. He hated gutting the wrestlers like he had to but if they aren't performing up to par like they should be; someone would have to eventually do it. "I... okay Fox." Carson Covington looked frustrated as his headed bowed into his chest. "Carson... Seth, you need to quit trying to live up to the legacy your grandfather and father created many years ago. Focus in on the now, not what happened in the past. You may never be as good as them but you are good." Silver Fox patted him on the back while he stretched out; he was ready for he was getting ready for his first match for OCW in several months. "I know you hate me saying that but... you'll never be them, it's time you create your own legacy. You started off in the fWo great; you had the fans behind you... you had your co-workers behind you... and most importantly you had management behind you. We all see it in you but you aren't seeing it in yourself." Seth grabbed a bottle of water and a towel from his locker. "The future is only what want it to be, remember that," Silver Fox said as he walked out through the door and headed towards the ring for his match. The Future He finally did it; he rolled onto his stomach and slowly pushed his body off the wrestling mat. The bell sounded as the referee held his hand in the air, he was handed the fWo World Heavyweight Championship Title as the crowd of ten thousand stood on their feet chanting his name repeatedly. Carson Covington finally had reached the top of the mountain, the pinnacle of his career. He looked around as he held the title in his hands and above his head. He was awestruck at how loud the cheering had gotten, how many people stood in their tired feet for him. His opponent laid at the waste side in defeat while he celebrated his long awaited triumph. "The future belongs to those who prepare for it today." - Malcolm X
