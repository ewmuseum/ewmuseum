Mega Man Battle Network: Disco Man

10 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Codemaster knew there was something wrong with this particular robot master when his level consisted of a large disco ball hanging overhead, shooting lasers at him, while he avoided dancing robots and spiked pits made out of broken martini glasses. However, he kinda figured out that this guy was a man of disco as soon as he heard "Stayin' Alive" by the Beegees for the fifth time since he's been here. Or maybe it was the great big yellow disco ball on his head. "Okay, now this is starting to get disturbing." Codemaster said. "Starting? I HAVE A DISCO BALL ON MY HEAD!" Disco Man yelled, "I'm a generic firebot, too! I mean, where would Mega Man be without Fire Man, Heat Man, Charge Man, Napalm Man, or Flame Man?" The Codemaster blinked. "Firebot? You're a disco man. And you're BLUE." And suddenly, from the disco ball, a blast of flames scorched the ground in front of the Codemaster and rapidly approached him. The Codemaster leapt to avoid it, but his cape was toasted in the blast. The Codemaster glared at Disco Man, then at his scorched cape, then at Disco Man again. "Oh no, you did NOT just set fire to my dreads." the Codemaster whined. "I did." Disco Man said, before he did the Saturday Night Fever dance, which included the "point at the sky in a fancy manner" dance. Disco Man then began to sing. "Burn, baby, burn! DISCO INFERNO!" Another blast of flames. This time, however, it didn't even get within a mile of hitting the Codemaster. Of course, the Codemaster was still mourning the loss of his beloved cape at the time. Then, his head shot up. He saw red. He snarled, and pointed his Holy Water weapon at Disco Man. "What the hell are you doing with a Super Soaker?" Disco Man asked. I guess I forgot to mention the fact that the Holy Water weapon looked like a super soaker. Sheesh, how'd you EXPECT the Codemaster to use it? A sprinkler system? A fire hose? Heck, no. "It's not even a good model. Can't you use something like those Nerf guns that cease to look like guns?" Disco Man inquired. "No." the Codemaster said, before he fired the weapon. A funny thing about Holy Water. It, uh, burns evil? And, well, Disco is evil. So, as soon as the Holy Water made contact with Disco Man, it set him on fire. "OW! That, that kinda... you know... stings?" "Stings?!" the Codemaster shouted, "You're on fire. Shouldn't you be, like, running around, flailing about, and whatnot?" "Oh." Disco Man said, "Right." Then Disco Man ran and screamed while running around in circles, until he was completely incinerated. He fell over and died, leaving behind only his disco ball. The words of evil appeared. "GET EQUIPPED WITH DISCO INFERNO." The Codemaster looked up at these words. "I hate you people so much right now. I'm not kidding." He picked up the disco ball and quietly left this bizarre and unwanted world of disco.
