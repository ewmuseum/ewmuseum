Restoration

24 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With Padrona's return still probable for the near future, we find are young Thai-fighter still continuing with his old ways. From his small home in Kancharaburi Thailand, Padrona looked down his driveway as a small black car pulling up to the house, hidden from the public view. A small driver exits the automobile and proceeds to open the backside passenger door, allowing a small woman to exit out. She makes her way across the small field, stopping at the flowers planted along the road and sidewalk. The morning sun shines down, and for this time in Thailand, it's enjoyable weather. "Of all the places to hide in the world, and you come here," the woman says, adding a small smile to her brightly lighten face. "This is the only place I could stay without being in the public eye. Even you know that Shygen." She walked up to the front porch and dropped down into one of the chairs that sat next to a small table. Padrona was on the other side; reading a Thailand newspaper and for once acting like a normal human being. Shygen looked on, as Padrona seemed to stand distant from her presence. "We can drop the image names Vilas. Can't you ever just call me Sefina like I have asked you many a time?" For once Padrona or Vilas Lavagna didn't care what he was being called around the world. It had been weeks since he was last seen, and only Sefina knew exactly where he was. Padrona the character was fine, still making his mark in the world of professional wrestling, but as far the man behind the mask, Vilas Lavagna, it was a completely different story. From his travels in Japan, to America then back to Japan, the journey was catching up to him and he needed a break. "How are my parents," Vilas asked, catching a surprised Sefina of guard. For months she only heard him talk about his job, his respect and his country. Never did he ask about his family. "They are doing fine, worried about your disappearance, but I have reassured them you are safe. You need to go see them Vilas. You mother has asked why you haven't been home in weeks." Vilas folded the paper and let it glide down to the table. He removed the mask over his face, and for the first time in years breathed without it covering his nose and mouth. Sefina's eyes widened as her fighter simply gave up his identity in a blink. But for an odd reason, she smiled. "Luck for you fWo officials are not around to see this or you could lose a contract," she said, getting a small smile from Vilas as he pulled his hair from his eyes. "I have come to realize one thing. I am Vilas Lavagna, not Padrona now. To long have I kept myself behind that god-awful mask," he muttered, sparking interest from Sefina. "You are finally realizing this isn't a competition. This is life. You were such a good student when it came to learning, but you failed to grab hold of reality. My mistake to you was not showing you this earlier and allowing Thailand to control your actions." Vilas laughed and shook his head, "You didn't do anything wrong. I called this upon myself. I finally realized that now. After watching my fellow competitors...colleges...friends, I finally noticed I was missing out on the one thing that made this all worth while. I missed the journey. I miss the trips from Japan to America and back. I miss my life." "Hard to believe you can be the greatest when it comes to fighting, but can't fight the greatest battle of all. The one against yourself." Sefina and Vilas sat in silence, birds chirping over head, wind blowing the trees and long weeds in the distant field. A peaceful moment, and a reason why Vilas came back home to think. "Are you going back," she asked, wondering if she would ever see the fighter known as Padrona again. Vilas looked long and hard at the possible answers and with a simple voice he whispered the word, "yes". Sefina smiled and sat back, Vilas stood from his seat and looked out onto the open plain. "Call it un-finished business, or my duty, but this isn't the time to stay at home. Not now, not ever. I will be returning to America soon enough. Not as Padrona the fighter, but as Vilas Lavagna the man." Sefina joined Vilas and placed a hand on his shoulder. Together they looked out over Thailand and a beautiful sight of perfect nature. "You owe it to yourself Vilas. No one else, and no other person. Go back and find the respect you wish to receive, and for the love of god, enjoy it. You never know when it will all end." "It may just be entertainment to some, and a way of life to me, but I know what is right. Restoring my self respect is my only option at this point." Vilas Turned around and picked up the white mask and gazed across the surface. He slides his hand across the mask and smiled. "I will find what I am looking for. Whether I am Vilas." He pulled the mask back over his face and looked back at Sefina who was smiling with delight. "Or I am Padrona!"
