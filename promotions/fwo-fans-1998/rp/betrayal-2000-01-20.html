Betrayal.

20 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Betrayal. January 20, 2000 Dead stood high above the earth, for he had finally visited the famous Eifel Tower, accompanied by Flash Finelli. He stared around at the world below him. He felt at home; even if it were a country he didn't particularly like, and it was nothing like the desert back in Death Valley, Arizona. The world was in his grasp, as it had been since he had made his return two weeks ago. He inhaled and exhaled several times, taking in the fresh air. There were several clouds about, and rain appeared imminent, but he paid no attention. Deep thoughts entrenched the inner workings of this man's mind, and it could only be assumed that even the best psychologist would have a rough time figuring out exactly what he was thinking. What made a man this cold? What would motivate a person to be this heartless? Not even those around him knew the answers to those questions; and he didn't appear ready to divulge the details. Doing so would reveal a little too much about him, and the mind games he liked to play with others. The last time he had revealed anything about his past was over a year ago on Groundhog Day, when he spoke of the time when a teacher compared him to a groundhog because it seemed as if he spoke only once a year... just as a groundhog only comes out once a year, or so folklore would like you to believe. Today he appeared in a relecting mood as it were, and he motioned for the camera to come closer. "Such a beautiful day, isn't it? It resembles May 29, 1998. You weren't with the company at the time, but I'm sure you've heard stories about it. You see, that was the first time I turned my back on somebody." Dead continued to stare down at the world below him, and the position of the camera revealed a half-attempt at a grin. "Jim Dudley. He never saw it coming. Few remember we had formed a group... but what everyone doesn't know is he wanted the spotlight on himself. In my opinion, he already had it. He was the first fWo World Champion... and it's no secret that I'm the man he had eliminated from the infamous battle royal at the fWo's very first show to win the World Title. As far as I was concerned, he had a monopoly at that point and staying with him would only hold me back. He had what I wanted, and that was the fWo World Title. I didn't stab him in the back... someone else had already put the knife there in the first place. All I did was shove it in a little farther." Dead brought his head up from the edge and looked at the camera. "Six months later I removed that knife from his back and cut his throat with it. His career ended that night." He looked back down after a brief chuckle. "After Dudley, it was Cactus Jon. I had promised him not to turn on him in a tag match, who the opponents were... I don't remember. I couldn't resist. He is, afterall, Canadian, right? No matter. Those guys were the first... they wouldn't be the last. After that, dissention spread through the lockerroom like a wildfire through a forest. Nobody could trust anybody with Spencer and myself running around. There were five men who stuck up for the promotion at Mob Rules 1998 to face The Dark Riders... and Jon & Dudley were two of them. The others? Ice, Travis Beaven and... The Boston Strangler. He's never wavered from that point. Sure, he came close a few times late last year when Beaven was jerking him around and not booking him correctly, but since day number one... he's been the fWo's leader. Their defender. Their proverbial knight in shining armor. Their heart & soul. I can't recall him betraying anyone, either. Sure, he's always looked out for himself, but he's never been one to stab somebody in the back; nor has he turned his back on the promotion he loves. Like I said on Monday, Paul... keep your friends close, but your enemies closer. It seems as if Chris has realized the grass is always greener on the other side... most notably, *this* side. Since it can be safe to say that you always have and always will defend the fWo with pride, think about what you are defending. A promotion of weak, pathetic lemmings who are all sitting on their asses while you do all the work. You've stepped to the forefront again, and maybe some people are sick of your same old song and dance. You've contributed your life into the promotion... and this is how one of the promotion's members repays you? By turning his back on you and having you drove off away in *his* limosuine full of..." Dead laughed for a moment before he continued. "... my snakes? I hope you enjoyed the ride, even if it didn't last as long as planned. No matter. Chris has seen the light, so to speak. Speaking of light... people who have experienced a near death experience talk of a light... and being drawn to it... before coming back. Seemingly, they avoid death. This Sunday, you will not be able to avoid the inevitable. You may be the heart & soul of the fWo... but in a few days, Paul... I shall take your heart and rip it from your chest." Dead turned towards the camera and lifted his right arm into the air with his hand remaining half opened as if he were using it as an example. "And after ending your career, the fWo will be once step closer to the end. After it's heart & soul are gone... it can't last much longer." Dead winked into the camera, before he prepared to walk away. Before he did, he stopped and turned back towards the camera. He looked deep into the camera, almost as if he were staring at the Boston Strangler himself. With more than a hint of sarcasm, he spoke again. "Can it?" From there the scene faded into a preview of Invasion!, followed by a preview of this Sunday's monstrous Sunday Slaughter.
