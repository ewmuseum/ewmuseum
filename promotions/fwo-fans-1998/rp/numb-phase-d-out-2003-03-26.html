Numb Phase...d Out.

26 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Mommmmyyyyyyyyyyy!!!" Poke poke. Jab....ow. Amy woke violently with a jolt after being poked in the eye. No sound or voice had really registered, so she'd assumed immediately it was Brandon just trying to be a smartass...but not quite. The finger was small and stubby, with a rather sharp little finger nail. She opened one eye - the strikingly blue right one - to find that on the other end of the arm were huge eyes, a nest of dark hair and a giant smile. "Mommy!!!!" "Ste...Stephen!" The redhead groaned but tried as hard as humanly possible to sound enthusiatic. She stretched her against the headboard, revealing a plain black tanktop that her son soon used as a seat, hopping up to perch on her stomach. "Hey, bud..." Another small groan while Amy rubbed her eyes paused when sudden confusion set in. "Hold on...how come you're here?" "Gramma dropped me off!!!" His smile was still wide as he bounced a little excitedly, partially knocking the breath out of his 'mommy'. "B'andon said he was...was...umm...I don't wemember, but he said he was comin' back soon and I should wake you up! Gramma said he had a bad assident but he said he was okay!!!!" "Yep. He was...uhh...lost for a little while, but he's back and just fine now, but he had me reaaaally scared. I bet you were too!" "Yah!!! He's my favorite." Amy mocked a surprised expression, gasping "Not me?!" "Haha! That's funny...girls don't fight!!! It's not nice." One eyebrow shot up, then a second. For a moment, she didn't look terribly impressed, but wrote it all off to the sweet expression in his eyes. "Aww, c'mon now...I'll test that out on Brandon later, we'll see who can fight!" She grinned, "So hey...what're we doing today?" Even though Stephen 'technically' wasn't her biological son, he was hers by both their hearts. She'd had other plans that day, but it was like Brandon had always said: When your kids are around, your life changes for them regardless of what they want...and Amy was soft, it's not like she was about to send him home. Besides, it'd give Stephen a good time to get used to the Mighty Hero as a person as opposed to a TV character. "I wanna meet Mar...Mard....Mardeer!!!" "........Martyr.......huh?" "Inn't he your boyfriend?" "My what?" ....Something was definitely up, and Amy's face was beet red because of it. She stared at her son with mouth agape, blinking viciously before she could even muster a word. "I...you...where'd you hear that?!" "Gramma read it from tha newspaper!" .....Newspaper said WHAT?
