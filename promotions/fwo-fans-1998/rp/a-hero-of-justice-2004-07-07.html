A Hero of Justice

7 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Thank you for coming," Waylon spoke softly as he watched Sara walk into his motel room, her long dark hair wavy in front of her face, hiding her eyes briefly as the hair slid from side to side. Waylon looked to her body watching as the fabric of her small dress seem to hang onto her skin, hugging her his and dancing in unison with her as she walked. "Oh Waylon," her voice was lustful, Waylon could hear the sexual undertone in her voice, she was aching for him and he wanted her just as badly. "Oh Waylon," Sara repeated as she lowered herself onto the body of Waylon who looked up at her from his bed, "Do you know how much I love you Waylon?" Waylon's face lit up and a smile so large emerged on his face, "Yes Sara...and I love you." "I love you Sara, I love you so much-" Waylon opened his eyes. He was lying in his bed, in the motel room, alone. He looked to the alarm clock that sat silent beside his bed on the small table, it read 4 a.m. "Fuck," he said out loud, it was another dream, just like the one he had been having for the past twenty odd years, ever since the day that he had saw her. Waylon sat up in his bed and ran his hand back through his thick brown hair, "Oh god," he mumbled as he stretched his back and heard a crack. Moving his body to the left of the bed he rested his feet to the floor. Taking a few moments to wake up he scanned the dark room, looking over at the chair in the corner a flash caught his eye, it was the reflection of the light that had come through the curtains from outside, the light came from the street and it reflected off of the container that held the serum. Looking over at the container Waylon was still for a moment contemplating whether or not he should test it again on another animal and or move on to himself. He did not quite know yet whether or not the serum would kill him or whether or not it would work as it should. He stood from his bed and moved in front of the mirror that was opposite the chair in the other corner of the room. Looking at himself he touched his skinny stomach which looked frail and feeble. He touched his face which was nothing more than average and then attempted to flex his biceps, no muscle rose on his arm and they stayed the same size even when he strained to stretch them. He relaxed his arms and then looked to the container that held the serum. What did he have to lose? What did he have to gain? Well, he would lose his life if it went wrong, but as of now he was wanted in Canada and had only one friend. His life was close to miserable and had always been so if he died in this experiment only one person would cry about it. To gain? He had everything to gain, a new body, a new life, a possibility that Sara would love him. New boundaries would open for him, a new opportunity to get ahead, to have people like him for once. He had everything to gain from it. He was going to do it. He was going to inject the serum into himself. "HA HA!" Waylon shouted out in excitement only to fall back into fear as the neighbor in the room next to him pounded on the wall in anger. "Shuddup!" He heard in response to the pounding. Waylon tiptoed over to the container and picked it up, he set it down on the bed and then pressed down on the button at the top of the tank which then responded with a low beep and the container began to open slowly. The two sides folded out and rested down on the bed and what was revealed was a syringe, some alcohol swabs, and two cylinder containers full of liquid. Once half was the larger cylinder, inside it held a red serum and the smaller cylinder held a blue serum. The serum was two parts, the red half was nicknamed Arnold for its effect. After one would inject the serum into their blood stream it would proceed to attach itself to each muscle in the body and tear the muscle to it's limit, just before it would snap and permanently rip apart. The second half of the serum, the Healer, would come after the Arnold and hear the torn muscle, rebuilding them so that they would sustained the muscle enhancement and function at full force. In order to do this correctly and not kill himself Waylon would have to inject the right amount of both serum's so that he wouldn't kill himself. In test previous to this time he had only injected small amounts of each serum and had not yet fully understood the effects of each of them. Staring down at the objects in front of him Waylon questioned what his next move would be, he stood up from the bed and moved slowly to the window. Opening the curtain with his hand and sliding it over he looked out onto the empty street, it was warm inside the room so Waylon opened the window only to be embraced by more heat with a slight sign of fresh air. Waylon scanned outside and in the distance saw a light flashing, it was the sign that hung to the building of a local establishment. The sign was labeled EAT MORE in red and WEIGH LESS in blue. Waylon focused in on the sign and then stepped back from the window touching his finger to his lip and the image of a light bulb blinking on top of his head flashed into his mind and a smile once again grew across his face. "Of course!" He shouted out again in excitement and it was soon followed by... THUMP! THUMP! "Shuddup!" "Sorry," Waylon whispered and then turned back to face the serum, "How could I have not realized that before, you need a higher dose of the Arnold and a smaller doze of the Healer, of course it wouldn't make the enhancement immediate. The Healer would in turn take about fifteen minutes to fully kick in." His eyes drew in closer and his concentration was now on the experiment in front of him. He plopped himself down on the bed and picked up the alcohol swabs and ripped open the package in which held them, he rubbed the swab on his left shoulder, as he would have to inject the serum into his deltoid. He grabbed the 5cc syringe and stuck the needle into the Healer container. Drawing out only 1cc of the Healer he grabbed the Arnold container and stuck the same needle into the container, never had the two serums been mixed before, but Waylon had a theory. If he was to inject the combined serum into his body the excruciating affect of the Arnold would be counter balanced with the Healer and in theory it would work, however for fifteen minutes until the Healer had time to kick in any exertion or strain on his body could rip and tear muscles permanently leaving him handicap for the rest of his life. Waylon then drew out 3cc's of the Arnold. He flicked the syringe with his middle finger and made sure that no air bubbles inhabited the inside of the syringe. Taking a final breath and looking outside for one final stare at the stars Waylon plunged the needle into his shoulder and injected the serum. Only to immediately fall back on his bed and his eyes rolled back in his head. A small sigh left his lips as he drifted off into a euphoric like state.
