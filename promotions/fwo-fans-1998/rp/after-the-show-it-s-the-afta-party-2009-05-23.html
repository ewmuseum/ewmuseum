After the show it's the Afta-party...

23 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“How many fingers am I holding up?” Craig couldn’t answer right away, “Come on… we need to get a board out here, ASAP.” Everything was happening soo quickly. People ushering by, Blitzkrieg Funk walking back up the ramp even tossed another look in the direction of the younger Slaughter. Roland and Junious never left his side, even after the decision was cast CJ’s condition seemed to trump it all. At least to Junious it did. Into Roland’s blackberry he tried to maintain his composure, “Ant Maybelle… it ain’t lookin’ real good.” The people around the arena didn’t know or care that one third of Affirmative Action could be seriously hurt. They continued to fill on booze and puff their weed, their lives were unaffected. Mostly white, mostly college students just looking for a place to tie one on, and it was free to boot. Junious was still nodding, since age thirteen he’d been the man of the family but this, this might have been too much. “We did… we was. Ant Maybelle I swear we was!” he pled just looking for her to take over a small piece of this burden. “I know I promised. I will! I will Ant Maybelle!” The slab was delivered and the EMTs rolled Craig over carefully so as not to put any unnecessary pressure on any part of his neck. The velcro straps were fastened tightly around his legs, then torso, finally his shoulders. “Alright everyone on three,” both EMTs looked at each other, “One… Two… THREE!” One quick jerk and the aluminum legs unfolded from under the stretcher. Those ungrateful fans, those drunk, reprehensible, ungrateful fans didn’t even cheer. Not soo much as an applause the sacrifice made by Affirmative Action’s resident risk taker, not an at-a-boy, nothing. Junious seethed with rage. Big O and Roland had the good sense to stay close to CJ and let June Bug have some space. Through the back past the infirmary, through the double doors and out to the awaiting ambulance. Through all that he still held the phone to his ear, “We bout to go to da hospital, Ant Maybelle. Aiight I’mma call you when we get there,” with that he hung up the phone. “Don’t worry Junie, we gon’ get dem Germans, cuz.” Big O broke the ice. Offering his support for June Bug. Roland’s head almost twitched with anger, “You day-UM right we is!” “We’re pulling out of here!” demanded the EMT. “Aiight,” June said stepping into the back. Craig smiled when he opened his eyes to see his cousin. “Junie, you don’t worry about shit! We gonna fuck those Germans!” Omarr smiled. “You meant, “Fuck those Germans up” right?” Roland asked. “Did I?” O replied smiling. “I think I liked you better as the strong silent type.” Washington joked, or was he?
