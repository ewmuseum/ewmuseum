New Year's Revelations: One

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Why am I even bothering with this...' Amy Campbell scolded herself for attending any social event outside of her circle of hometown friends, let alone the fWo New Year's party. But there she was, stepping into the closed-off pub it was to be held at. She even appeared to have a sudden taste for cleaner dress as she wore an uncharacteristic ensemble of tight black slacks, with small leather strapping criss-crossing down the sides, only saving her image by topping them off with a slightly loose babydoll t-shirt featuring a small orange tribal sun with "Godsmack" below, the logo of a favorite band. Why the effort to tidy her hair...why was she even there? Sadly, she knew the answer before she'd ever even asked herself the question: She was there because Brandon Roche would be. He was her security blanket. Above everyone else she'd recently known, the small redhead found herself so comfortable with the mountain of a man, even though he was a head above her, both literally and in life. She'd hear none of it, though; He was her equal and maybe, on some remote planet, her friend. However, now wasn't the time, nor the place no matter how hard she wished and wanted. For twenty years she'd portrayed herself as the sort of person who just didn't care, in hopes that the world would leave her be and not come knocking with it's deceitful inhabitants. Naturally, she'd been the high school party girl and, if she was truly happy these days, still would be...but a few nasty situations after graduation had lead her to be soured by alcohol, and now she avoided the things that soured her, both alcohol AND people, with a passion. Of course, she had a selective few close friends, but what she never could have avoided or foreseen was the presence of that damned man, a married man who broke her down each and every time he came near. Though Aimz was sure other friends knew all about her little act to keep the world out, Brandon Roche was the only one of them all to ever actually snap the barrier in two...and now she didn't know what to do about it. Especially now, when her relationship with Darcy had been severely waning, Amy found that she had to remind herself over and over again, with each and every hug, each and every second of eye contact, that the man before her was married. This was ridiculous. Coming to the fWo was supposed to give her the means to jumpstart her career in a more serious manner than the two years she'd spent before. Her name was out, and now she could concentrate on building on the foundation of her talent...but such was not the case: Since she'd come to the fWo, life had thrown her some foul balls. But when she'd met Brandon, and during the few minutes they'd since spent alone together just talking, everything had suddenly been cleared and was so right...but the reality to them both was that the whole friendship was wrong. Things weren't supposed to be 'nice', or 'special'...but they were. She'd found herself seated at the dim bar, trying to inch away from the small cliques all hunched around one another. Not a friend in sight, and those she tolerated were too wrapped up in their own affairs. Even if booze did harm her she was sure having to sit through this dreary shindig all night would only hurt more, so the order for a simple glass of rum was made the second her elbow touched the polished counter. If she was going to avoid people, she'd at least spice up her perceptions of them! But she didn't know until a few minutes later that she really didn't need the rum to keep her company because, a little while after she'd started nursing the drink, a rather striking image in a white Christian collar dress shirt, simple black trousers and a knee-length leather trench coat stepped in. He certainly wasn't her 'type', but Amy couldn't turn her head...at least not until she realized who she was studying so meticulously. A Mighty Hero had just stepped through the doorway...it was indeed her new friend, but she didn't make a move toward him: just watched him. To Amy's evident surprise, Brandon swiftly closed the door behind him...Ana wasn't there tonight. She wasn't sure if he'd known Aimz would be there, but she was relieved either way to see that he wasn't accompanied by his wife that evening; She might be able to enjoy simple company without feeling the need to claw someone's eyes out. Mismatched eyes were glued to Brandon as he stepped further into the crowd...and became lost in it. Instead of going straight to Amy, he was mingling around a little. She'd assumed it was just his way of avoiding the obvious and undue controversy surrounding their quickly becoming rather close. Brandon looked around at the packed bar. The open invitation to the fWo's New Years Party was heartedly accepted and looked forward to by what seemed to be the majority of the talent and staff. Andrew leaned over a railing trying to look studly, he might have been the best sound technician on the crew but he was failing miserably with the Rico look. Someone will have some blackmail material tomorrow, he was sure of that. Even after the beating he took from Ric Chronos during their wild brawl he forced himself to come to the bash. A skin colored bandage adorned his brow and his ribs were still heavily taped. The match against Chronos at Twilight had aggravated the old rib injury and the tumbles through the cage this night didn't help. X-rays were sure to find something not to his benefit. Tonight though he dressed in his casual best, tucking away as many bruises and scrapes as he could behind long sleeves and a dress shirt.
