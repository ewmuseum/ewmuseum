Adjust: Your Gimmick

4 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
So, anyway, despite our little travel mixup, Allen Speck and Leigh Landers ended up in Paris, France, a full day ahead of the fWo and its upcoming Wednesday Warfare card, and in a decent hotel. Along the way, Allen found out something rather interesting about his best friend. "Damn, girl, when'd you learn how to speak Spanish?" Allen asked, as he and Leigh walked along the airport, headed for the gate to take a two hour rest until their plane was ready. "My father." "Didn't take Kyle for someone who spoke two languages... hell, I always thought he had trouble keepin' up with one." Allen said. "Nono. My other father. The biological one. You know him, right?" "The crazy old man that you introduced me to when we ran that tour in London? Yeah, I know him. So, when he said that stuff to me in French, he wa--" Allen started to say, but Leigh stopped him by holding her hand up and giggling to herself. "You don't want to know what he said." she said, pointedly. "Man, that's just not cool." There was a pause, before Leigh continued, "But yeah, my father... my real father, he valued learning other cultures and languages, and wanted to pass that on to me. He just sat me down and taught me two other languages before he and my mother got a divorce and I ended up in America." Allen nodded. "Why didn't you become a citizen, then?" "I had dual citizenship `cause my mom was American, but I loved my dad infinitely more than Kyle or my mom, and elected to keep my citizenship. And as you might know, once you lose your American citizenship, you can't ever get it back. What, you thought I had to get work visas for fun?" Allen didn't say anything in response as the two made it to the gate, he just sat down and contemplated things. But that was then, and this was now, and Allen Speck was set to meet Samuel Smooth for the first time. Was he nervous? Sure, he was. He had done most of his business with Silver Fox before now, and while he was technically a member of the fans Wrestling organization's roster, such things as a creative process and a card position had yet to be fully decided. The dislocated shoulder not withstanding. Allen Speck was sitting in the lobby of the hotel room, waiting around for Samuel Smooth to show up. His leather jacket only had one arm in its sleeves, as his arm still required a sling. His sling obscured the white shirt he wore underneath and the table he sat in front of obscured his dark blue jeans. He was personally exhausted from the travel, a fact that gave Leigh a reason to just go to her room and sleep while Allen waited around for Samuel to appear. He didn't have to wait very long. "You must be Speck." a bald black man said as he walked up to Speck. The green vest he wore over the white shirt was a touch unappealing to the eyes, but Allen ignored it and smirked at him as he stood back on. "What gave you that clue?" Allen sarcastically asked, gesturing at his sling-bound left arm. The man known as Samuel Smooth just went along without missing a beat, shaking Allen's hand, before he sat down across the table that Speck sat back in front of. "So, what happened to your shoulder?" "Well, I told Silver Fox that I'd come to 'eff Dub' after I wrestled my last match for my independent organization, right? Well, I had to wrestle a guy by the name of Benjamin Morris, and to give you an idea of how bad he is, he makes Erik Kelly look like freakin' Benoit. He hit me with a Rock Bottom and my arm decided that it wanted to take the top rope with it. It didn't like that plan, and, well, this was the result." Speck said. Samuel Smooth stroked his chin in thought, and Allen chose that moment to fish into his pocket for something, and pulled out his Game Boy to fish it out fully. Samuel saw it. "Hey, is that a Game Boy?" Samuel said, before he reached across the table and picked it up. His eyebrows raised with surprise, "When did they make a new model?" Allen laughed, "Boy, you guys really have been abroad for too long. That's been out for what I guess has been a month, now. It's all backlit and stuff, so you don't have to struggle to play in the light like before." Then, Samuel Smooth had a brainstorm. He smiled and slid the Game Boy Advance back to Allen, who quickly snatched it up and placed it back in his pocket. Allen really didn't like that smile. "Hey, I had this idea..." Samuel Smooth said, leaning forward at Speck, "You know the Fuse Bros., right?" "Not personally. Why?" Allen did not like where this was going. "Well, I had this idea. We could dress you up in these black robes, and make you their manager. Since the Fuse Bros. are doing this thing with "cheat codes", we could call you the "Codemaster". Then you'd be, like, the blackest brother in the Mushroom Kingdom or something. Hell, maybe you could debut on next week's Warfare. It'll be great!" If he weren't tired from all of the travel, Allen would have objected in a major way. If he weren't tired from all of the travel, Allen would have voiced his opinion and told him how much this fucking blew. Instead, Allen Speck sighed and nodded. "Okay," Speck said, "Let's do it." He would learn to regret this mistake.
