Preparation, Part 1 (History)

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lance Knight walked into the press room that had been set up by the fWo techs at the Arrowhead Pond, and he found himself in the midst of a hurricane of activity. The A/V techs were still preparing some of the video packages for the show itself, and they also had some final "History" packages to prepare, which is why he was here. "Ah, Mr. Knight," a smiling young woman greeted him, shaking his hand efficiently and ushering him to a wooden stool set up in front of a blue screen. "If you'll just sit down right here, we can start filming. Any questions before we begin?" "Only one," Lance began, "What the hell am I doing here?" He smiled warmly at the young woman, who returned his smile in spite of the bewildered look in her eyes. "Just relax, Mr. Knight, we've prepared something for you to read, so all you have to do is look at the prompter and read along." "Hold up," Lance said, and now it was his turn to look bewildered. "I'm reading off of a card? I thought this was all ad libbed, or at least prepared by the wrestlers before they came out here." "Well, generally, yes," she responded, "We would allow the talent to speak their own words, but we weren't sure for you, and because of the last minute nature of this..." "Well, I apologize for the inconvenience, but I actually have prepared a statement of my own, and if it's alright, I'd like to use that." "Of course," she gushed, "I'm sure what you've prepared will turn out better than anything we could've done. If you've got a copy of it, I can have them type it into the prompter and we can start as soon as they're ready." "We can start now," Lance responded, pointing to his temple, "I've got it all up here." "Excellent, well, when you see the red light, go ahead." ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ The video package opened with a split screen of both Kodiak Vic Creed and "Black Plague" Ruben Ross standing victorious at CyberSlam IV, from April 28th, 2001. Creed had retained his Hardcore Title over Crucifix, and Ross had usurped then-champion Erik Kelly to win the Internet Title. Now, nearly a year later, the still images faded out to reveal Lance Knight, dressed in a black suit and seated in a plain black chair. Adjusting his tie slightly, the fWo rookie began to speak. "Nearly a year ago to the day, two men, Victor Creed and Ruben Ross performed at their first fWo CyberSlam. Like myself, they had been competitors in the fWo's annual Survivor competition, and also like myself, they both saw CyberSlam as their chance to truly make a name for themselves in the fWo." Another still image replaced the feed of Lance Knight, this time of Ruben Ross hoisting the fWo World Championship high above his head after defeating Ric Chronos. "Since then, both men have achieved greatness of varying degrees, most notably Ruben Ross' World Championship reign, which is still intact going into this year's CyberSlam.
