Second Heartbeat: Clairvoyant Disease

11 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"So how is this going to work out you two? Ana's going to be back in the States at the end of the month." Martha sipped on the hot tea. Looking at the newlywed couple, she glanced over to Ana. The tension in the room had eased somewhat, but it was still evident. "I mean, with Ana back home, the kid's will stay with her while you're in Orlando taping your shows?" Brandon glanced quickly to Amy, who anyone could see was still having a hard time adjusting to the idea. The fact that two of 'the kids' were from the previous union of Brandon and Ana and that Stephen was actually the adopted son of Amy herself had only helped to muddle the waters. "Well, Stephen's the exception to the rule. We don't want him and the boys apart for too long, but we don't think Ana having him every week makes much sense either..." The patriarch of the Roche family faltered a bit, as Ana walked back in the room with the platter of crackers and cheese. "I think Stephen'll go with them every three weeks or so...but it's still up in the air." Amy leaned in closer to Brandon, "They're his brothers. I want them raised as brothers..." she sighed, "Yeah. It's still up in the air." Ana chirped in finally, settling in next to Amy, leaning back against the sofa and placing a pillow in her lap as she did. "It's going to take some getting used to... that's for sure." She said that, and as if to emphasize it she glanced at Martha before trailing her eyes down to the intertwined hands of her ex-husband and his new wife. "It's all good though... right guys?" Was that a fake smile? "I know Stephen doesn't know me from Eve but taking him away from the boys just seems wrong, especially so often." That last remark could have been interpreted as a dig against the pro wrestling lifestyle, but was it? "Pretty wrong." was all Amy could muster. A discussion about her song with her husband's ex-wife. She wondered if it was just her, but did this vacation very suddenly suck? "Brandon," she suddenly poked the Paragon, "What's the plan for today?" Ana interjected quickly however before he had a chance to answer. "Wait. I don't want you to get the wrong idea. I didn't mean anything by it really. I was just trying to acknowledge the fact that this is a strained situation and we need to take everyone's feelings in to account. I was just saying that the boys are really getting to know themselves as a family now and splitting them up just might be a set back. Heck... it was just an opinion, I've been wrong before. Plenty of time actually." Brandon simply sat there. A deer caught in headlights as he watched. Ana leaned over to the ignored food stuff, picking up a brown wafer like cracker and using a newly acquired toothpick placed a slice of white and yellow cheese on top. "Amy?" Ana offered the item with a smile. "Maybe, if you really want me in the hospital." Brandon cringed, while his mother and Ana just stared, attempting to decipher the cryptic comment. Finally, Amy horribly faked a sweet grin, "I'm allergic. Cheese and I don't get along...at all. The reaction's rather severe..." the little redhead couldn't help but make eye-contact with Ana, "Very violent." Smiling back Ana tried to break the tension she had helped cause a bit, "I've often wondered about that. About severe allergic reactions and all... what if someone were suicidal, and deathly allergic to something. Would they consider just like... I dunno... grabbing a block of cheese for instance and going to town on it?" Brandon eyes nearly bulged out of his head on that comment. "Don't be silly dear." Was all Martha could muster before turning the conversation to a bend that Amy had tried earlier, "Son, what do you have planned for the day?" The 'mighty hero' glanced to the group of oblivious kids for a brief second, oh so wanting to be over there instead of here. "Well Ma...um... I really hadn't given it much thought, play it by ear I guess. I want to take Aimz around the 'Ring of Kerry', but that's probably tomorrow, I think it's supposed to shower this afternoon. I don't know... what about you Amy? What're your thoughts?" "Actually," Amy brightened, but had a fist clenched still, "Why don't you take me there? I'm sure Ana doesn't mind staying with our kids." Ana smiled back. She's a bright woman and totally caught the 'our kids' statement. The question now however was would she play the catfight game or try and make peace? The long seconds between Amy's statement and Ana's eventual reply left all to ponder that. "Well, I think you should wait till tomorrow for the 'Ring'. Maybe if you did the wharf today, it doesn't really a matter if ya'll get drenched down there. But whatever you want to do is fine. I'll need to get to know the boys as a group sooner or later, since I'll be spending so much time with them and all." With that Brandon reached over and put his arm around Amy to comfort her...to show his love... ...to hold her back? Only he knew.
