Heaven's A Lie - One

7 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Amy was smiling. Brandon was laughing. It was perfection... Well...close enough, right? Countdown was behind them, so was 2003. A year ago they kissed for the first time, Brandon's marriage crumbled and another got its roots when Amy took a bullet to save the Paragon. That might have been quite an event in itself, but after the rough ride the year had taken the two on, they were entirely equal as far as coming to the rescue - mentally or physically - went. Amy herself was wilder and had come into Brandon Roche's life with more problems than your average soap opera star could shake a stick at while he himself, though far more stable and less 'rough and tumble' as it were, had his own to match. Even through the year they'd endured, both had finally come to solve the issues that served to keep their relationship rocky... The last fix had come at Countdown. After far too long, Brandon finally reached a high he'd never experienced - in the same breath, he'd stomped out a man who'd almost succeeded in ruining everything that meant anything to him and could proudly say he was truly at the top of the business he'd spent his life in. Aimz, for her part, had less to speak for...but that's how she liked it. Not as career or goal oriented as Brandon, she was perfectly happy to make sure their personal life was running smoothy... ...It finally was, right? She aimed to find out. He'd brought she and the kids into Orlando to celebrate the win and help distract them both from the woes that plagued the company he was now on top of...and as they sat finally alone, Amy continued the smaller of the battles they'd fought the past month: "So is everything finally settled?" Brandon, not exactly wanting to get too 'deep' at the moment and instead focus on fun, call it running away if you will, stuck tongue firmly in cheek when he responded, "Yeah, everything's settled. I've got Sean and Stephen set to go, Jarod isn't big on the twisty rides and whatnot but he'll ride a few things." "Brandon..." Amidst her motherly name-calling, Amy sighed and closed her eyes tightly. The kids were playing in the next room, so she spared a moment to sink down onto the room's only couch. "There's hardly a thing for me to celebrate," When her eyes opened, they were half-glassy with whatever this emotion was...neither of them could quite tell. She was tired, and of a lot of things. "I keep asking if it's over like you said it would be, if it's really ended with him to the point that we can move on. And every single fu..." No, the boys were nearby. Her tone softened slightly, "Every time I ask, you just side-step it all. It's a simple enough question, but all of the effort to avoid it...it's...well..." Another sigh. "...it just makes me wonder if you want this." Brandon's eyebrow cocked, which signalled Amy to gesture around the room - Her ring, the kid's toys, the bed's tussled sheets... "Any of this." "We go over this so much Amy, of course I want all of this. If I didn't I'm sure you know I wouldn't simply settle. I never have. I think, I hope that that's one of the things that attracts you to me." Brandon slipped onto the couch next to her, kissing the nape of her neck as he did. "I think that you worry to much, I know in the past there has been reason and fuel to do so, but now, now we need to just 'be'. I just don't want every time we have a second to turn into another psycho-babble episode. We'll have enough of that to deal with when we see Disney's shrink tomorrow." Amy slipped to the other side of the couch, folding her knees to her chest and staring back at him with the least emotion she could muster. "I've gone my entire life without security in anything, Brandon. I've 'just been' with you now for so long, and I'm tired of it. You have no reason to avoid this now. Vic is done with...the only problems lie in you, and that's not analytical babble - it's the truth, and it's going to keep coming up because I feel like I'm holding onto a damned thread. The only thing I've ever asked of you is some security, and with you beating around the bush on everything...I don't have it, I haven't had it." "And I'm not going to that doctor tomorrow," She continued, still trying to keep a cold, brave face. "I don't deal with those people, and I don't want to go back yet. I'm sure you'll go because that's what you need, to discuss your problems with someone else...God forbid you to ever be honest about them with me, but I'm not that type. I won't speak to someone who doesn't know me. It's probably half the reason you're avoiding fully committing, but you're the only person I'll speak to like that...fuck doctors, they'd rather just pop us on some anti-depressants and collect their wages." As Amy sighed and leaned her head back against the plush couches arm Brandon looked on, wide eyed. It almost felt as he had been sideswiped. Stammering a bit, he sought to finally, once and for all straighten this mess out, "Amy... I'm not.. it's... I don't really know where I'm going with this so hang on... okay?" He patted her leg, rubbing it reassuringly. "Tomorrow, well, tomorrow I'm being the good little worker like I always do. I don't need to talk to a shrink about my problems. I don't think. No one ever does though really?" Another sigh escaped his lips, before a heavy breath filled his lungs, ready to continue. "You don't want to show tomorrow, that's fine. If they can't deal with it, that's fine as well. But really, that's unimportant to what's really bothering you. I'm NOT skirting around anything really. In MY head Amy we're fine. Everything's fine. Creid's done. The fWo is not in the best of shape, and there are things there for me to handle, I think they need me now, more so than I would like, or I think they expect." "You and I are on the straight and narrow. If you need to set a date we will, but I don't think that's going to help anything if you aren't settled with where we are. I don't NEED a ring around my finger to protect myself from our life crashing down around us. It would be the most wonderful thing to be able to call you my wife. What I want to make sure in all of this Amy is that you aren't rushing it because you think that will be the cement that fixes problems you see that I don't. That is what I'm dragging my feet and worried about the most." That had only eased Amy's expression slightly. She still sat away from him, but kept the distance locked with their matched stares. "You don't see it at all, do you?" She began with a silvery tone that quickly softened, almost sounding hurt... "The only problem there is is this. You left me like you did, and then came back seeming like you were suddenly opposed to us 'rushing in' to something you asked me to do months ago. Do you have any idea what that feels like? You might know in your mind that things are fine and blah blah blah, but I don't because your behavior hasn't shown it at all. I want this more than anything, and my only problem has been your holding back from completely committing. Words are fine, because I love you and trust you absolutely...but I don't trust everything, everybody else, and those measley papers you've been acting so afraid of would be reassuring." She was half broken down. Knew she couldn't be, not when they were about to take Sean, Jarod and Stephen out...but she was - she didn't care what kind of public face she had. "That's been my only problem, Brandon. I just want that security and as much as words mean, they've betrayed me a hundred before...so that's what I want - More than words." "What do you expect me to do then? We can run out and get Mayor Duck or whatever to perform the nuptuals and tie the knot tomorrow." The sarcasm, given the situation, bit more than Brandon inteded. "When I walked away, I did so because it had felt like you had left me. I guess I screwed my point when I used to term 'rushing in'. It's not rushing in Amy, it's what I want, I guess... I'm afraid as well... just like I said, you see that there are still major problems with us that this will fix." "You don't get it. Not at all. I see that it will prove to me there aren't any problems. I see NOTHING but the fact that there seems to be something making you avoid this. I need you fully, I need to know..." She swallowed hard, pausing a moment to keep composure through frustration, grief and anger. "I need to know that there really AREN'T problems you're keeping from me, because you're acting like there is an issue other than my fear." Through misting eyes, she continued, "I've dealt with weeks of everybody telling me to get away from you because you're running away from committing to me fully, people assuring me that there MUST be a problem I'm not seeing, or something's being kept from me...just like there was with everybody before you. I've taken it all in and tried believing none of it, but I'm tired of everything - the drama, people dying, everything. The one sure thing there's ever been in my life is you, and with your 'just being' stuff it's made me feel like you weren't anymore. I just want everything behind us, for you and I to really be that solid and sure thing I've never had." "Marry me then and worry that I did it just to appease you. Saying I do doesn't create a fantasy world where nothing bad ever happens. In fact, those words heighten any and every emotion that happens afterwards." It was beginning to show now, the reasoning deep inside Brandon Roche. As much as he wanted it not to, his previous failure in commitment haunted him. It hung over his head, especially after what he perceived as betrayal on her part. Amy had grasped his hand in both of hers. "They mean everything. You mean everything. We're never going to have a dream world, but I want to guarantee we'll at least have each other." "Then let's set a date." And as those words escaped his lips, Amy's eyes shone bright for the first time in awhile. "It doesn't have to be tomorrow, or next week, but let's put a date on the map. If I have to ask you again, I will. I'll ask you every day if I have to. I want you to know that I want this, all of it - your trust, the kids, the messy beds. It's home to me. You're home to me." Brandon blinked back tears of his own as he spoke. Realizing exactly what he was doing in keeping Amy at arms length, Brandon did what he always does in a situation like this. A situation where his comfort level is tested and things are gray, unable to be seen in the distance. He'll jump in, again, with both feet... and fight for it. Finally, Amy was smiling again. This time when she blinked back a tear, it was soft and her eyes only opened again to gaze brightly into Brandon's. She just stared for a second, waiting still to see if his mind took a sudden step back... ...but that moment never came. Instead, she bolted forward into is arms and smiled again, resting against the Mighty Champion.
