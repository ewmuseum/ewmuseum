Rudo says that Scott Collins is a big loser

9 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Rudo says that Scott Collins is a big loser January 09, 2000 Machina pulled up to the outside of the arena, and parked. He didn't turn off the engine however, and let the car idle. Smoke drifted up from the exhaust pipe and into the cold winter sky. Machina turned to Rudo in the passenger seat and then further back to look at Sid and Brandi. Okay, we ready? Brandi raised her hand and Machina nodded to her. Why... are we doing this again? The rest of the car groaned and Sid pulled a banana from his pocket and began to chew it with satisfaction. Machina explained again. Rudo here needs to cut an interview on Collins. Now, we would have liked to have done that at Slaughter, but I had to protest against the show with Penny. So we are going to cut an interview here. Machina pointed to the marquee sign on the front of the arena. It read: MONDAY NITRO! LIVE TONITE ONLY! Brandi nodded now, and Machina looked at Sid. Okay, this is what we are going to do. Sid, you use the rifle in the back of the car. Now don't use it unless you have to, but if you see Meng SHOOT. We can't allow his suck to walk the Earth any more. Now, while you threaten the locker room, Brandi will occupy the security guards by promising them sexual favors. Rudo will make his way down the ring so he can cut his promo. While this is going on I'll proceed to point 'A' where I will obtain and drink some hot chocolate. Now, Rudo will finish his interview and walk to the parking lot where he will hotwire a better car for us to escape in. Brandi will distract the gate guards by flashing them, while Sid uses some plastic explosive to destroy some Nitro production trucks. Sid looked up. Why do I need to do that? Machina sighed and slapped his forehead. Dammit, Sid... you know this! Because we single-handedly are responsible for the crappy production values of the WCW, for suppressing the evolution of the electric car, and for keeping Steve Gutenberg as a Hollywood star. It's our calling. Now come on men, let's do it! The four Lone Gunmen exited the car and ran into the arena. The following account is about Rudo however, and the adventures inside the Nitro arena are not relevant to the issue at hand. Needless to say, Brandi managed to lure the entire WCW security team into a small hall closet and lock them inside. Sid did not run into Meng, but one bullet WAS fired from his rifle. Machina consumed not one, but two cups of hot chocolate. Machina also ran into a tall, straggly man with a pony tail and a New York accent. After listening to the man talk about himself for twelve minutes, Machina finally bitch slapped him and then urinated on his jacket. Meanwhile, Rudo had made it to the ring and had grabbed a microphone. He looked out at the bored crowd and shook his head. The stands were half-full and the fans looked like they were either in a coma or dead. My name is Rudo, and I'm not from the WCW. This got a big pop from the crowd and faces turned to the ring with renewed interest. I'm not here to wrestle, even though I'm sure I could pretty much kick anyone's ass here. I'm a jobber in the fWo, and I'm sure all of you have heard of it. The crowd cheered again, and several "WCW FEARS FWO" signs were waved in the crowd. I'm here tonight because I'm going to wrestle this pansy in a few days and I need to get some crowd support behind me. The guy's name is Scott Collins, and he used to be Dork. I mean, Death. The problem is, Death was whipped by his bitch of a girlfriend Stone. She pretty much wore the tights in the relationship if you know what I mean. She has him cook her pies, and I'm pretty certain that because of her Sid isn't the only wrestler in the fWo that wears panties to the ring. The crowd laughed, and Rudo started to relax and get more into the interview. Hey Scotty... if you can hear me... your girl dresses you all funny and you're a girlie man. Oh, look at the little girlie man, ooh ooh! Hey Scotty, if you were a real man you would give Stone a crack on the ass and tell her who the boss is. God man, you're pathetic! But do you know what Scott? Do you know what the worst thing is for you? The crowd hushed as they waited for what the worst thing was. Scotty... I think Skittles... The crowd leaned forward. SUCK. The crowd oohed and ahhed as Rudo grinned. Well, that's about all. Girlie Cool, I'll see you on Wednesday. It's come as you are, so when you wear the little skirt and apron remember to shave those legs. Now I return you to your regularly schedule crap. I believe that it's... Rudo looked down at the fat announcers at ringside. They whispered something up to him. Terry Funk gives Scott Steiner a match? Jesus... sorry about leaving you fans. I could stand here and belch all night and put out a better show. But, the meter's running. Rudo swung out of the ring, and left the arena. Everything else went according to plan, and before the night was over Machina had a second cup of hot chocolate. He wound up drinking it too quickly and burned the roof of his mouth.
