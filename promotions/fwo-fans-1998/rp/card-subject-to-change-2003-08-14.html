Card Subject To Change

14 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Monday, August 11, half an hour before RUAHH~! The board was scripture. Who was working, whom they were working with, what segment, and how long. Would the show open with an interview, or would there be a cold open? What would be the climax of the night? The board did not hoard secrets. It broadcast its knowledge to one and all. Ric Chronos looked at the board, scanning for his title defense. Beside him, The Flying Frenchie did the same. Just four days ago, they had arranged for two title matches, one for tonight against Scott Slugger and Ice. Oddly, after repeated attempts to locate it, it refused to be found. On the bottom of the board was a note, which Ric noticed only on his third look through the listings. He snatched it, held it in his hands while he read it aloud. "Ric, Pierre, Scott, and Tony. Due to time constraints, we're bumping your match to Uprising. No time for a segment tonight to explain it, so we'll mention it on Thursday. Ric and Pierre, your scheduled match against the Codebreakers will take place the following Uprising. If any of you have any questions, do not hesitate to ask me. Silver Fox." Frenchie frowned. "Brilliant."
