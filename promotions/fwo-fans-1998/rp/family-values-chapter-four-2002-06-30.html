Family Values - Chapter Four

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
December 25th, 1991 Spencer, Nebraska 8:15 AM, local time Angela woke up in a cold sweat, her puffy, red eyes still squeezing out tears. Was it a dream, or was it real? She couldn't be sure. Wait... what is she saying? Of course it wasn't real. There's no way that it... happened. It was a crazy dream, the kind everyone has from time to time - the ones where you can't tell if you're awake or asleep. Ow... damn, she's sore. Angela crawled out of bed, and beelined to her closet to pick out her clothes for the day. Maybe... her black jeans and tanktop? It looks nice, but it's also comfortable "human" clothes - something she needs when dealing with her family. Almost as an afterthought, Angel looked at herself in the mirror. A double- take quickly followed, and she felt her knees go weak. Angela backed up a step, hitting her closet door, and looked closer at her reflection. Last night she had a very violent dream involving a dark man violating her. It was a dream so vivid she could swear it really happened. But if it was a dream, why were her wrists swollen? Why was there a huge discolored mark on her forehead? And why did her pelvis feel like it was burning? Angela put a hand to her mouth - she was going to throw up. As she left her room, she could hear the survivors of the previous evening still snoring loudly - her parents and uncle had all gotten pretty well sloshed last night out at dinner. She drove home on her learner's permit, but it was okay, because she was a good driver. Her parents were still sound asleep, but it sounded like her uncle was in the shower. She knocked on the door, leaning up against the wall dizzily. Abruptly, the door opened, and a whiff of steam escaped. "G'mornin, Angela," said Uncle Mark, wearing nothing but a towel around his waist, "Sleep well?" "Not... really," she said, still feeling sick but more confused than not. "I'm not surprised," replied Uncle Mark, "Looks like you've got a nasty bruise there." He stroked her cheek gently, then lightly slapped her. "Word of advice next time," he continued, "when someone tells you not to bite, don't." He laughed, walking past her toward the guest room. Angela's tears started to flow again as she crumpled to the floor. Bile began to fill her mouth and she leaned over the toilet, coughing violently. Memories she thought were dreams flooded the space behind her closed eyes. Being woken up from a dead sleep by the dark man - the scent of whiskey all over him. He had removed her T-shirt and boxer shorts before she woke up. A whisper that did not seem human told her to be quiet, that he would take care of her. Then he forced her mouth open and gagged her with something stiff. Instinctively, she bit down, and felt the sting of his hand across her face. And then... and then... The searing pain through her pelvis returned, like she'd been stabbed. "Shaddup," the dark man had warned her, holding her down by the wrists until she felt her hands go numb. Her knees had been forced apart... and... "It'll only hurt more if ya struggle," continued the dark man's voice echoing through her head. Angela covered her ears, still crouched on the bathroom floor, but the voice was in her head - she could not keep it out. Her uncle returned to the bathroom and pushed her aside, taking a piss right in front of her. He had put on a pair of red briefs and a wife beater T-shirt since his shower. "Why?" she asked, her voice a hoarse whisper. "Why what?" countered Uncle Mark. Angela couldn't believe his nonchalant attitude, "Why did you come into my room last night and... and..." He seemed to understand. "Look, I didn't do anything with you that you didn't want. I saw the way you were smiling at me all night long, how you were flirting with me with that short skirt on - women don't act like such a cocktease if they're not lookin' to get nailed in the end." By this point, Angela was huddled in the corner near the bathtub, hugging her knees. "That's not true." "C'mon, it's me," joked Uncle Mark, "I know you pretty well now, inside and out. You don't have to put on this vulnerable facade." He flushed the toilet and leaned over her. "It's not like your parents would believe you, anyway." Her eyes grew wide at this bold statement - her parents would believe her, right? Right? "Before your mom and dad wake up, however..." said Uncle Mark with a sadistic leer on his face. Her breath left her - her vocal cords refused to work. "...How 'bout a Christmas present for your favorite uncle?" finished Mark, grabbing her by the hair and pulling her to her feet. "If you close your eyes and hold your breath," said a small voice inside her mind, "then this time he won't hit you. If you close your eyes and hold your breath... then this time, he won't tell you that he loves you. If you close your eyes and hold your breath... you won't have nightmares tonight." The voice would say the same thing every night for three years, when Angela Davison woke up in a cold sweat with tears streaming down her face.
