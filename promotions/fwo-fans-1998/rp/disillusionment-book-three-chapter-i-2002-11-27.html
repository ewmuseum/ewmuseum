Disillusionment: Book Three - Chapter I

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Today I learned that I am a wanted man. I mean, I knew before that I was wanted by the Disciples, but today, they made that information public. I was sitting in a bar. It was one of those little ma and pop places that also serves food. I wouldn't have been there if it didn't. I don't drink. Alcohol numbs the wits, and I can't allow that to happen. Anyway, I was sitting in the bar, and watching some baseball game. I wasn't really paying attention. Any league that the Mariners can be dominating isn't worth my time. In the middle of a pitch, the screen went black. The game was replaced by a graphic expressing the fact that the station was just about to release some breaking news to the public. The first thing I saw after the graphic was a black and white artist's rendition of my weathered face. I immediately looked to the ground and tried to cover my face with my hands without looking conspicuous. I listened intently to the announcer though, as I wanted to know what this was about. "Just in from the pentagon, the United States government has put a reward out for the capture of political terrorist, Jack Lundin. Lundin's offenses include assault on government officers, resisting arrest, sabotage of government facilities, and, most recently, the rape and murder of a young woman by the name of Stephanie O'Brien." What?!? WHAT?!? The bastards trapped, beat, raped, and killed Stephanie, all with me less than fifty feet away, and then they blame it on me? SHE DIED IN MY ARMS, AND NOW THEY SAY I DID IT?!? The other charges didn't bother me. The assault on Disciplic officers, resisting arrest, and sabotage of Disiplic facilities, that was all true, though not really in the way they meant it. But Stephanie, they did that because I loved her...or...at least, they thought I did, and they wanted to punish me. You have to understand the Disciples' logic in this situation, as it is actually quite good. The first three offenses that they accused me of in that broadcast, the semi-accurate ones, the public isn't really going to care about those. Deep down, the public wants to hate the government, and if some guy is attacking only government institutions, that's not going to bother them, because the public is not in any immediate danger. But rape and murder of a civilian...that's totally different. Those crimes are much more real to the general public, because it could, in theory, happen to them. "The reward for capture of Jack Lundin, or any information on his whereabouts that leads to his capture is three-million dollars. Lundin should be considered armed and dangerous. We now return to our regularly scheduled program." Three-million dollars?!? That was a lot of money. I can't believe they want me that badly. I was still trying to cover my face, but I could here the whispers around me and feel the cold stares of those who incorrectly thought that I, not the people who made that announcement, was there biggest threat. I reached for my wallet, and I could here gasps as I did. They must have thought I was reaching for some kind of weapon. I reached for my wallet, pulled out six dollars, left it on the counter, and silently left the bar. As soon as I was outside, I ran. One man tried to follow me. He wanted the reward I guess. He was middle-aged and out of shape, though, and with me conditioned to my running, he never had a chance. When I felt I had run far away enough and was in a remote enough location to be safe, I sat on a tree stump and thought. This was going to change everything. No longer were the Disciples going to be the only ones wanting my head on a pike. Now everybody and their mother was going to be after me, not because they were necessarily afraid, although, I'm sure there will be some of that, but because people are greedy, and they would all want their share of the three million dollars. No longer can I feel safe about anyone, because anyone who thinks that they are a tough guy and has seen my picture and wants three million dollars might try to capture me. I heard from a friend of mine that even those who know as much as I do have been given an incentive to capture me. The Disciples are saying that if any of their enemies capture me and turn me in, that their slates will be whipped clean. I have to say, that if the situation was different and if I had to turn in a fellow enemy of the Disciples to have my record erased, I'd do it in a heartbeat. Potential is something I don't feel a lot of these days. Potential of torture and death is all I can bring myself to think about, and I must say, I don't enjoy it. I want to quit. To quit running, to quit wrestling, to quit living, but I can't. The memories are too vivid.
