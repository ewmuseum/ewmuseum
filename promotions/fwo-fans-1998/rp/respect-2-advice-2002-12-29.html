Respect (2 - Advice)

29 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"You sure about this, kiddo? At Twilight you both looked ready to pop." "Listen, Eli, as much as I wish we could resolve this some other way, he just doesn't respect me. At all." I strode down the hall, hoping the anger in my voice was covering the hurt that I felt. Coming off of an emotional loss as Twilight only to find that I hadn't done anything to elevate my stature in Sean Stevens' eyes had really left me in a funk for the holidays, and even my parents had noticed it. It was getting to be too much, and I had to do something about it. Something that would let us settle our issues once and for all. And what better place than the Countdown shows? "I'm not nearly as big a fan of interfering in this kinda shit as Ivy, but seriously - is there something bigger than what we know goin' on here? All this crap is just based on a little misunderstanding that turned into a big one..." The concern in Eli's voice matched the expression that he wore on his face and every moment that he made. While part of me was overjoyed to have found so good a friend more or less by chance, I wasn't about to let him talk me out of what I had resolved to do. If Stevens wouldn't respect me by his choice, then I'd leave him with no other alternative. "This is a matter of respect, Eli, and I can't just let him run roughshod on me, on my reputation, no matter how good he is or how long he's been in the business. I didn't make it into the fWo on my looks, dammit, and I think I deserve at least some respect from him!" I was shouting, drawing looks from the startled techs who were doing their thing backstage. But in all honesty, I didn't care. "I don't know if Sean and I will ever be friends, but I'm not about to let him jeopardize the friendships that I've formed with you and Ivy
