Lance and Luke's Lucky Day ( 2 of 2 )

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"I'm ... ... ... lucky." Over the cacophony of the juke box, the pool game, the multiple televisions blaring their various sports, the clinking of glasses and bottles, and the various other noises that gave testament to the aliveness of the bar, Luke Starkey spoke louder than he wanted, just to be heard. To very few people had he had this conversation, confiding in someone and admitting his gift. But after three unrelated encounters with Alex Newsome, a.k.a. Lance Knight, Luke had a feeling that Alex was to play a prominent role in his future, and Luke had learned to trust such feelings. "And?" Alex looked indifferent to the confession. In fact, he wasn't looking at Luke at all; his attention was firmly focused on the curvy brunette and her friends standing in the doorway of the bar. Luke glanced at her appreciatively, nodding approval at the gentle contours of the body, the spherical roundness of bottom and breasts. The girl slowly swung her head in the direction of their table, her long brown hair swaying softly behind her. Her gaze landed on them, taking in Alex first (of course, probably due to his large, muscular physique), and then shifting slightly to look at Luke. Even from this distance Luke could see her luminous blue eyes twinkle with satisfaction. And then she opened her mouth and smiled at them. "Ewwwww!" Alex winced and jerked his head around. Luke tried to be a little more diplomatic. He forced a return smile and matched her gaze for another second
