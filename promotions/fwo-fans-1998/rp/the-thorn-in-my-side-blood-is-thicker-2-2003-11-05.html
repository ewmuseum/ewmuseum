The Thorn In My Side (Blood Is Thicker #2)

5 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Originally posted under the title "Something From Nothing" during fWo's Survivor 3.) "I told you not to stare at he lights," a frustrated yet concerned Elliot lectured to Thomas, "They'll hurt your eyes". Without even realising it, Elliot was looking out for Thomas' safety. "Bunch of fuckin' ingrates." The tatty and faded curtain was flung aside as Elliot's star, Greg Hoffman burst through. Glazed in perspiration, and with his hair matted down across the sides of his face, Hoffman was still riled by the crowd's apathy. "They want a guy to fuckin' do gymnastics to the outside before their fat asses get off the fuckin' cheap seats!" he continued, spite and venom coming from his tongue. Pacing around, Hoffman dried his face off with an old, slightly stained towel before just flinging it down onto the dusty concrete. Seeing his prima-donna top draw needing a bit of verbal fellatio, Elliot was quick to oblige. "What can I say?" Elliot exclaimed, "They don't appreciate talent." Thomas just stood silently to his brother's side as Greg continued to rant and rave, shrugging off the praise of his promoter. "Hard to fuckin' appreciate a good match when the ropes are too loose, the ring feels like it's gonna break from under you and the ref can hardly count to three!" As the whining Hoffman kicked out at the towel he dropped, Elliot decided it was best to leave the attention-craving star to it, and went skulking off to go and see what pittance he'll be taking home... But he'd left someone behind. "I thought you were good," announced Thomas, his slow tone unfortunately taking the crispness away from the compliment. His eyes narrowing, Hoffman's brow furrowed and his head slowly turned to look at the large figure. Wearing a black t-shirt branded with the initials of Elliot's promotion, the younger Harper brother was looked up and down. "We hiring fuckin' retards now?" Greg scoffed, "Elliot's more of a fuckin' amateur than I thought he was." Thomas may not be the brightest star in the sky, but the loyalty he has to Elliot is uncompromising. It's the ultimate he says "Jump!", you say "How High?" relationship. Not happy with Hoffman's dismissal of his beloved brother, Thomas didn't remain silent. "Don't you say bad stuff about my brother!" But Thomas' defence only put a spark back into the previously frustrated Hoffman, who was chuckling to himself by now. "You're Harper's brother?" questioned Greg, almost laughing out loud as he exclaimed, "Must be a fuckin' genetic thing." Grinning at his own joke as he shook his head, Hoffman continued to let off steam at Thomas' expense. "I didn't even notice the special bus when I drove in tonight," taunted the arrogant wrestler, his character seeming to reflect his personality well. Thomas didn't understand the joke, but by now a small crowd had gathered around and were laughing along with the promotion's star. As they all chuckled, Thomas looked around, panicked, scared.
