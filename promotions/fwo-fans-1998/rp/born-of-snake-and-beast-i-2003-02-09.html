Born of Snake and Beast - I

9 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
November 15, 2002 Jake Roberts was not a happy man, thought Mufanga Kagos. Each step Kagos took was thought out and calculated, being extra careful to stay out of range of the sharp, reaching jabs the old veteran threw almost nonstop. Too much was at stake to screw it up now. He started this match with a handshake with the wrestling legend, and things had gone swiftly downhill from there. Right off the bat, Roberts had potatoed him with a right hand that made the Wildman see stars and stagger towards the turnbuckle, his right eye already beginning to swell and threatening to close over entirely. He went down on one knee, and immediately his war-painted legs were kicked out from under him and he landed flat on the barnboard mat. Roberts dropped a series of hard snakeskin boots to Mufanga's exposed neck, each one a missle of open rage and a terrifying intent to maim. This was supposed to be an easy stop on what was his one-man tour of the Eastern indy promotions. A long, winding promotional tour, said John Rider... once again acting as Mufanga's manager and agent... Make a stop at an indy joint, job to their Champion, and getchur name back on the map. This is how we get you back into the fWo's good books. And this...this was the RWO, and was a long way off from the bright lights and big crowds of the fWo. The RWO's flagship card was held Wednesday nights in highschoool gyms across Michigan, and the few fans that actually showed up were intensely proud to call Jake "The Snake" Roberts" their Champion, despite the rumors that he was a raging alcoholic and the claims that he made Bob Holly look like he didn't have a stiff bone in his body. "Rest hold..." Mufanga whispered under his breath in a rush, using the age-old wrestling code for a timeout. Put me in a chinlock so I can catch my breath, dammit, was a rough translation. "Here's your resthold, pussy." Jake said in that old familiar rasp as he picked the Wildman off the floor by a long single dreadlock. Kagos remembered that tone from WWF Saturday Night Main Event almost two decades ago, when Roberts would strike fear into the hearts of a legion of wide-eyed ten year olds with just the sound of his voice. The tone that meant that Kagos was in for a world of hurt. The knee came out of nowhere, and whistled into Mufanga's leopard-print loincloth. Instantly, a blinding white flash of pain rushed through his body as Roberts' knee impacted into the Wildman's nuts. Kagos felt like he was in a runaway elevator car, descending in a blur as he stared up at an impossibly tall Jake "The Snake" Roberts. The vertigo soon passed as Roberts then dropped Mufanga with a dismissive flair and turned to the sparce gymnasium crowd, pointing at Mufanga's fallen frame and laughing. Mufanga didn't know which was worse; the absolute inferno of pain that was formerly his groin, or the fact that Roberts had turned his back on him... showing Mufanga the ultimate disregard, and Kagos couldn't get to feet to capiltize on it. But Kagos had battled back, and as he would later admit, he threw a few potatoes punches of his own. He had launched a stiff dropkick at Roberts' knee that send the old man tumbling to the sweat-soaked canvas... it wasn't a manoeuver that he had ever done before, as he was always taught that a wrestler's knees were the first things to go... don't EVER go for a wrestler's knees. But he had, and now Roberts had a new look on his face as the two wrestlers circled each other under the bright gymnasium lights. Beneath the rose-red alcoholic nose and the weasel-like rheumy eyes, Jake the Snake wore a look of fear. Mufanga liked that look on Roberts. It suited him. They cicled again, Roberts feinting jabs at Mufanga's left eye, which was now completely swelled shut. Roberts deked right, Kagos followed with a kick that was caught and thrown easily, and then Roberts was upon him. The wind was sucked out of Kagos as Roberts' boot sunk again into his midsection, doubling him over as the crowd cheered hoarsely for their Champion. Roberts grabbed Mufanga in a front facelock and the Wildman knew what was next... the DDT. Mufanga struggled like a wild animal, clawing at Jake's forearm, which was quickly and efficiently pinching his windpipe closed. Mufanga knew this wouldnt be an fWo DDT, there wouldnt be a last-second release so you could protect yourself before flopping over in a practised fall. This was the real thing coming. Roberts fell back, twisting his body so that his weight would fall on Mufanga's neck. Mufanga was right, of course, and there was no last-second release... there was no way he could avoid being dropped right onto the top of his head. He hit the mat so hard he actually bounced, his manager told him much later.
