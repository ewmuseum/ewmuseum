fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Trouble March 08, 2000 Havok is shown walking down the street in an unknown city. This is the first time he has been seen since he was betrayed by his partner, the Black Panther at Trapped 2000. His long, brown hair rests on his shoulders, and his face is hidden by black sunglasses. He is wearing a pair of jeans, and a long-sleeved white shirt. He just walks down the street with his hands in his pockets, staring off into space. As he approaches a dark alley, he comes to a sudden hault. He looks over at the camera, takes his sunglasses off, and speaks. "A lot of you have been wondering where I've been in recent weeks. Well, I'll tell you... I had some thinking to do. You were all watching Trapped, and you all know what happened between myself and the Panther. That's what I've been thinking about. I thought we had put Fallout behind us. I thought we could've been Tag Champions, but apparently Panther thought otherwise." He sighs, then looks back into the camera. "Well, I know you're all wondering if I'm going to do something about it, and I am. But I'm not gonna jump the gun. I'm gonna wait for Panther to play his hand. I'm just going to watch, and wait, and let him make the first move. You hear that, Panther? Whenever you want, wherever you want to fight, I'll do it. The ball is in your court, all you have to do is make a play, and I'll counter. I'll be waiting, you know where to find me." He walks off into the alley.
