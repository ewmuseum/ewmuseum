Coupling - 1 of 2

6 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Mmmmm. That's the joy of having kids." "What?" "Waiting for them to spend the night at a friend's house, so you can have sex on the couch with the windows open." Angel laughed, and gently poked Eli Flair in the chest. She was mostly covered by the blanket that typically hung over the back of the wraparound sofa, and had rested her head on his chest, with her arms wrapped around his stomach. Her long, straight black hair cascaded around her face, until Eli pulled it away and rested his arm on her shoulders. Truth be told, the windows were closed, though the curtains and blinds were all wide open. It was a good thing their street was on a hill, because in order to casually look in the windows, you'd need to be right next to the house. House. It had that 'lived in' look already, though they had only been there for a little over a month. Boxes were still strewn about: the only things in the living room that had been completely unpacked and set up were the wraparound couch and entertainment center. "Well," replied Angel, "it's just as well, I need to enjoy you while I still can." "Hey," said Eli, as he cupped her chin and raised her head to meet his eyes, "I'm not leaving for a few days, and besides, a standard week'll have me gone for three days for one night of work. I'll be home every weekend, I'll be home for the little one's birthday in two weeks and yours in four." Angel smiled, but it seemed forced. "I know," she admitted, "I've just gotten spoiled with having you all to myself for the past two years." She eased out of Eli's arms and picked his t-shirt up off the floor. Pulling it on over her head, the shirt reached her mid - thigh. "I'm thirsty," she said, "want anything?" "I got it," said Eli. "No, I've got it," insisted Angel. He smiled at her. "Water would be perfect." She kissed him on the lips and walked around the couch toward the back of the house and the kitchen. As soon as she was out of his eyeline, Eli's smile dissipated. 'I need to enjoy you while I still can,' she had said. Angel would never admit what she was feeling and has never tried to make Eli feel guilty about his career, but he knew what was on her mind. Their daughter, Mariella Jade, was nearly six, and for the first four years of her life, Angel worked nearly a twenty four hour day just to keep up. Eli was on the road with one wrestling company or another, and Angel was on the road with her band in a tour bus that was far less luxurious than the string of hotel rooms Eli called home night after night. Still, when Eli left the show he could have a few drinks and go to sleep. When Angel left her own show, she had to be Mommy for however long MJ was awake. She had to be Mommy for most of the day. Working on new material was relegated to sound checks and the wee hours of the morning. Sleep was a more precious commodity to Angel than food or money could ever be. Frankly, when Eli retired after the NFW Ultratitle Season 2, she was relieved. He had planned to take over as the Valerian's Garden security chief, and he would be able to watch MJ while Angel did her business, and they would still have time to be a family. To return to the ring now, even on a limited basis like he promised, felt to Eli like a betrayal. It felt like he was telling her that she could pursue her goals only so long as he wasn't pursuing his. That's what their fight was about back in October when Hupfer called, wasn't it? His string of reproachful thinking was suddenly interrupted by Angel's return. She took a drink of water from the glass in her hand and gave it to Eli, while she snuggled back under his arm. "Hey," he said, "do you want me to take MJ with me to Seattle next week?" She picked her head up and looked at him, with a suspicious/confused glint in her eye. "Why?" "I thought it might be nice to spend some time with just me and her, and since the studio is nearly done, I thought you could use the peace and quiet to play some loud pagan rock." Angel laughed. "I appreciate that, but I don't think that'll work. MJ starts her new school on Monday, and it would probably be a bad start to be there for two days and then be crossing the country with her father for two more." She kissed him. "But the fact that you asked means the world. Next month, we'll pick a day for you guys to go, or for all three of us to go." Angel settled back into his arms and closed her eyes. Eli stroked her hair for a few seconds with his right hand, while he ran his left up and down her back. "This is still your time," he said, "this fWo thing is just something I need to do right now." "I know," she replied. Again, Eli's thoughts drifted back to the fight they had when he got the call. And he wondered if they were really okay. "I'm not going to get hurt," he said suddenly, "I don't need to take the same risks this time out." She didn't answer; she just squeezed him tightly.
