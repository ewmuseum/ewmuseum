All that they have.

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Exactly how is trust formed? Is it something that is simply given or presumed or do you have to work hard and be diligent to have such an honor awarded to you? The dictionary defines it as "assured reliance on the character, ability, strength, or truth of someone or something". That definition presumes a lot. It presumes that at times sacrifices in your self aren't called for. It makes the assumption that when those sacrifices are made those seeking trust from you wholly and unquestionably understand them. But what if they aren't? What happens to trust if the person seeking to trust you cannot and possibly will not ever understand your actions? It becomes a lost cause of sorts as you try new, different and possibly more creative ways to show that what happened, happened for a reason. You try and show that a sacrifice was made not out of greediness but out of need. Where does a relationship go once that trust is strained? Is the confrontation that ensues used a mortar to fill in the gaps and build a stronger unity? Or does the strain put prophetic cracks in it's foundation that will one day blossom and bring everything down around your ears? Ana was asleep when Brandon rolled in around 2 a.m.; it had been a long night after the show. Brand Frontier decided to walk out amongst the fans as they were leaving the building and it started a near riot. After everyone was calmed and things became a little more orderly it turned in to a fun time. A lot of autographs were signed, pictures taken and a few of the newer guys even joined in. They had came to the show for things like this, at least most of them had. Now though he was feeling the effects. The buzzer on the little portable clock tore into Brandon's ear. With a mighty effort he pulled his hundred pound eyelids open to see that it was a bright and early 7:16. He mumbled to Ana something incoherent. A guess would say he wanted the little noisemaker dunked in acid but it wasn't quite understandable. When no reply came from her he reached behind him to let her know that her tiny $4.99 heirloom from Wal-Mart was on the endangered species list if she didn't get up and cut it off. All he felt was cold sheets. That woke him more than the shrill screech of the clock. He blinked, and blinked again. The noise still taunted him as he looked at the red display. Time of death? 7:19. The travel sized morning monster would no longer trouble anyone again. Brandon tossed it into the wastebasket as he rolled out of bid. He was now a mass murderer; that was the third one in a month. Maybe the wake up call from the front desk would be more effective? Then he thought about how much the hotel would charge for a new phone and he dismissed that thought. He replaced it though with thoughts of where Ana had already gotten. The shower wasn't running and the clothes on the short dresser were gone. He padded into the bathroom, gravity and Gatorade teaming up on the mighty hero. Once done, he looked on the counter next to the sink, noticing the printout he had gotten from the rental car last week, the one that fell from Amy Campbell's pocket. The things it said about her must be a lie. This Sandra Peake was just another muckraker trying to cash in on the popularity of the fWo. If there wasn't a fWo to bash what would these Internet geeks/ reported journalists have to write about? Brandon waved the thought out of his mind as he decided he'd just talk to Amy and get the dirt straight from the source. He could see her love for the business and even though he knew it made him sound like an old man she reminded him of himself at that age. Maybe he was trying to protect her from everything he went through and more. She had so much more going against her than he did back then. Now though he needed to find his wife. She had an interview early this morning with a local newspaper about the Frontier Foundation but afterwards the day belonged to them... A few hours later Brandon had used his keen detective skills, i.e. one of the roadies pointed him in the right direction, to find Anastasia. Charlie said he had seen her walking to the park after the interview. He said that Ivy had invited a few of the girls from the show to go to the park and Ana was the only one that accepted. Brandon thought that it was odd she would have simply left after the meeting with the reporter since they were supposed to have a day together but he quickly put it out of his mind. Maybe she knew how late he had arrived and figured he was still out like a light, recharging his batteries. With a shrug of his shoulders and a wave of thanks to his informant Brandon trekked off to reclaim his wife from the thief Ivy McGinnis. It was good that the Fan's Wrestling Organization employed female entertainers. It sounded sexist at its root but it was right on so many levels. The simplest one and the one that had the most impact on Brandon though was that Ana felt far more comfortable when she came along for her little trips. Having Ivy, and sometimes-even Tempest around had eased the pain of Brandon going on the road. With the G.W.O. he only flew in for the shows and with them promoting primarily out of England he could go home to the Bay for long four day stretches. The fWo was different though and Ana and he had to make huge sacrifices to make this work. With that thought in mind Brandon looked around the small city park. This early in the morning the park was near desolate. The little man walking his shivering hairless dog waved at him as he smiled in return. Nothing like buying a hairless dog and dressing it up in god-awful sweaters. There she was. Seeing her immediately put a smile on his face. It filled him with more energy than a full nights rest ever could. Her long dark hair flowed out from under her ugly orange stocking cap, as she seemed to sense his presence and turned to look at him. She took his breath away every time they made eye contact. What would he ever do without her? Then, she turned away. The sun peeked from behind a fluffy cloud but it suddenly got colder. The mighty hero raised his mighty eyebrow. Something was going on. And that would turn out to be another understatement of grand proportions when all was said and done. He slowed his pace, as if walking on the proverbial eggshell lined path. His pace was not out of fear or the knowledge that he had done something wrong, it was more to let her gather her thoughts since she now knew he was there. He placed a hand on her shoulder. "Hey, what's wrong?" Using his hand as a lifeline he pulled himself into a sitting position next to her. She was bundled up tight and her legs were firmly pressed together, not exactly from cold but more so from her seeming reluctance to sit next to Brandon. He took the magazine off her lap and tossed it on the other side of the bench as he rubbed one of her legs vigorously, as if trying to warm her. She looked over at him from a cocked expression on her face. "Have fun last night? I finally stopped my repeated vigil of waiting for you around two in the morning. What time did she let you go?" She. Who? What? Let who, what? More stunned by the words than guilty of anything Brandon sat in silence. The early morning and the lack of anything carb-based was taking its toll on his thought processing. "I said what time did you get done last night?" Her words were icy and stung as if he had breathed in a lung full of winter air. "Two or three... somewhere in there. I didn't think I woke you." Her demeanor had put him on edge.
