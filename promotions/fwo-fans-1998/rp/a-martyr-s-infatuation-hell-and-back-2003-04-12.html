A martyr's infatuation? Hell and back.

12 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
2:02 p.m. March 30th, 2003 "Home" In a way Brandon Roche almost regretted walking in when he did. It wasn't as if he would want to avoid Amy on purpose for any real reason, but if there was the look on her face at the moment would be one of them. Scowling was almost cute on the little redhead but this look would make a gargoyle blink twice. The wrinkles in her forhead seemed to bely a lot of living for one so young. Brandon hesitated, only for a second though. His basic instincts took over. "Hey hun, why the angry face?" "Oh, me? Nothing..." She tried to shake off the expression in her eyes and color on her cheeks, but Amy knew Brandon saw straight through it, so she quickly stopped hesitating. "Just more stupid fucking online morons and newspapers printing rumors about me. This time, I'm apparently in a...oh, what was it...ah! A 'hot love affair' with Eugene!" Suddenly, Roche's eyes had squinted and lips drew back between his teeth, sealing his mouth from initial laughter and any grins. "Yeah, MARTYR." Aaaaand that much laughter wasn't going to stay bottled forever. "HA!..hehhehhahaha...ahem..." Judging by the sudden fire in Amy's eye, it'd probably be the wise decision to stop that fit of 'manly' giggles ASAP.... Thank God Brandon Lelan Roche was a wise man - he knew what was and wasn't healthy for him. "Well... heh..." Another look to the redhead had him stepping all over his tongue. "....that's pretty damn funny...uhh...if...if you ask me. Think of it this way, Amy-sweety: A few months back they said you and I were going at it... now look at us. So if we provide the same logistics to it all..." Where was this one going? "....you and Eugene will be on the way to the altar by Summerslam!" Holding back another fit of laughter, Brandon simply stood there and blinked - having already realized the pain most likely imminent. "Oh ha-HA-HA!, you're just hilarious." Amy stepped out of her chair to smack him - hard. "You know what is funny? Ever heard of blue balls?" The Paragon swallowed hard, nodding. "Keep talking like that and you'll have a pair by the end of the month...now that would be funny." She smiled a sweet, sarcastic little smile before continuing, "You shouldn't be laughing about this, the only reason people are saying this shit is because we didn't want more freaky press at the rumor pot with our names...but no, of COURSE they just have to say shit anyway to make my life that much harder." "Well... we can always hold a press conference and announce our relationship to the world. It could be the sports entertainment press event of the year... unless that's already happened or something." With a shrug of his massive shoulders Brandon reached over and attempted a warm hug but Amy pushedback against him a bit, not wanting her bad mood to be washed away but so easily. Then it hit him, exactly where was the press getting this stuff from? It couldn't be from the shows, hardly anyone was watching lately. "So what's Eugene doing about this?" "Sending me notes saying he loves me and all this bullshit. Apparently he said something to Borst about us being together - he follows me around, BL..." She groaned, "I hate this bullshit!" "...that's a fitting couple if I've ever seen one." Brandon echoed Amy's discontent with a sigh of his own. "So this isn't some stunt Fox and the rest have cooked up to build interest in your matches with him is it? This whole 'jumping through hoops' crap won't be tolerated." Amy sagged against Brandon suddenly, letting his shoulder muffle her words. "He actually thinks I'm suddenly gonna stop thinking he's a moron and fall madly in love with him. I WISH it was something Fox made up to spice things up. Hell, I wish I could tell him that I'm with you, but God knows he wouldn't keep his mouth shut and I honestly don't think it'd make a differnece anyway - he's obsessed." "Well, I'm not going to play 'knight in shining armor' or anything. It's not like you of all people need me defending you or something. It's just Martyr for God's sake. Matter of fact, I was surprised when he and Borst had their little conversation last week and Eugene said he was straight..." With another chuckle Brandon reached his hand out and took Amy's in it. "That's the point! He's like...the nerdy gross kid with the bad haircut in every biology class who asks you out every day! Except he's a prick half the time..." Eyes shut tightly, she pounded a fist gently out of frusteration against his chest. "But it's disgusting! It'd be better if I felt bad...but mostly I just want to laugh at him, and I do, which is only gonna have him whining to Fox and the rest of the staff. There should be a rule or something against people within fWo falling for someone else in the federation..." Well, maybe that last comment was a bit illthoughtout...but Amy hadn't realized that quite yet. "... yeah... You're right there. I've never seen a happy couple or anything that's come from meeting in that god-forsaken place..." The sarcasm hopefully sunk in as Brandon continued. "You don't think he'd try anything do you? I remember back in the day, during the contest, he was always an obsessive sort." "I dunno..." She started and then grinned almost satanically. "But if he ever tries to touch me, I refuse to be held responsible for the testicle search and rescue operation. And aside from that, I fully intend to beat his ass down on a daily basis until the reality that he'll never see me naked - or in any pleasant form for that matter - sinks in." Brandon grimaced with the mere thought of the unpleasant imagery. "Thanks for that, I won't be able to even pee for a week. You just be careful in the ring and make sure he doesn't go psycho on you. He might seems harmless now but I've seen things like this turn ugly... fast." A fleeting thought of Vic Creed raced through Brandon's mind. Gone as fast it had come he simply shook his head and shuddered a bit. "Just... just be careful. Kick his ass though, you CAN do both." "And I'd better not get in shit if he gets handsy in the ring and I suddenly...err..accidentally break his nose. Right? You'll back me up on that one...Borst grabbed me last week and I hadn't forgiven it for his usual 'I wanna be hated' stupidity, the autopsy photos might have scared Eugene outta ever even dreaming of touching me..." Amy shuddered quckly. "Don't worry sweetheart." Brandon used the hand he held to pull her back to him suddenly. "I've got your back. And your front. And this." Amy's eyes bugged out a bit as Brandon grabbed her butt. "That's unusually...forward of you." The redhead snickered, "See, now if you were him, parts of you would be grabbed, squeezed, twisted and then pulled in various uncomfortable directions about now, so be glad...just warn me before you make the sweet from Hero Boy to James Bond next time, 'kay? I'm not used to surprises...and you seem to have plenty." She winked. "I figured after the other night nothing I could do could surprise you..." "Well, that's true..." Her grin widened. "That was pretty..uhh...surprising, alright. Anyway.........if Eugene becomes too much to handle, think I should avoid castrating him and maybe go straight to the cops? I know your experience with something like this kinda...well...got me shot." "That's exactly what I was thinking actually, but I didn't want to come off as 'prudish' again. We'd like to think the fWo wouldn't hire a nutcase but then look at Davis... or Crucifix... or... well you get the idea." "Trust me - you're certainly not prudish anymore." Amy laid a hand on his shoulder and sighed. "Plus...well..yeah, I'm actually kinda nervous with him now. He seems to really like me, and we all know what happens when people start gettin' into that love stuff...crazies taking bullets for others, whole mess'a foolishness." Another wink ended the little joke. Well, like I said, I'm not going to step in and defend you. I trust that you can take care of yourself, but you ultimately know that if you need me... no matter what... I'm here for you." Still hugging her, Brandon squeezed her reassuringly a little, but the redhead already had a devilish grin on. "So even if I need you for...say...fifty bucks and a back rub?" The grin was contagious and Brandon shot back. "Hell Amy, You can have your way with me and I won't even charge you fifty bucks. The back rub's on me." Knowing full well he had twisted her words around on her he instinctively winced, knowing retalition was on the way. "Remember how I mentioned blue balls before? You sure you want a pair? Just keep talking..." Making a 'zipped lip' motion with his hand Brandon winked. Moving closer he tilted his head to the side and eyes closed slowly. Parting her lips as she did, Amy waited expectantly until Brandon finally kissed her... The eskimo kiss didn't go over too well, though, as Brandon rubbed his nose against Amy's. Her eyes darted open in surprise as he backed off quickly, hands raised in defense. "I take the declaration back - you're still a prude." She couldn't help but laugh, "But you're a cuddly prude, plus you could sit on me and crush me...so I'm not gonna complain." But with Martyr's 'love' on her hands, she sure had a lot to complain about... Next-> Inked [This has been an Aimz/ Brand Frontier collaboration.]
