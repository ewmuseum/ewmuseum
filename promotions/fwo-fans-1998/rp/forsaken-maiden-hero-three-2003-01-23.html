Forsaken - Maiden Hero (Three)

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brandon paced back and forth. The cold chill in the air did nothing but heighten the anxiety he felt. Before he left for the fWo’s Wednesday Warfare broadcast his whole life had began to unravel. After a three-hour conversation with his estranged wife things were left pretty clear. The last six years was just that, the last six years. There was no future for them together. Intertwined as they would be with their children’s lived there was no more Brandon and Anastasia. He felt truly alone. Moments after that realization came crashing down though another bombshell was dropped. A psychotic message scribbled in red ink lay at his feet at the doorway to his hotel room. An indicator that his life was about to get way more complex before it got better. Following the instructions in the note Brandon had returned to the hotel soon after his appearance on the show was done. No hanging out with the pack afterwards or waiting to see if he could catch a glimpse of Amy. No, he hopped in the rental car he shared with Lance and headed back to face this person alone. He had taken Ana’s words to heart everyone around him gets hurt. No matter how often he tried to play hero all he ended up doing was bringing the danger in to someone else’s life. Tonight he would face down another psycho, but this time he would make sure no one else paid for it. This was his duty and his alone. A scuff behind him spun the Paragon around and had him ready. Hands up in a classic defensive pose he readied himself for an attack. He found none. Instead standing a few meters away was a female. A combination of the late night sky and her heavy coat hid her features. Brandon slowly let down his guard, knowing full well that this was the author of note. Slowly she walked to him, finally coming in to the light. That is when Brandon’s heart fell to his stomach. Never in his wildest dreams would have had expected to see her face again, let alone see it mere feet away. Brandi Taylor. A dull ache formed at the base of his neck as he took in what he saw. Not many people he currently associated with knew about this woman. If they would have saw her walking the halls of any hotel room the fWo stayed at they would pass her by without a second thought. Brandon on the other hand knew whom, or rather what, she was. She was simply… stark raving mad. His mind raced. As if in a bad flashback sequence Brandon remembered the letters from an obsessed fan long ago. Letters that had a hint of anger and desperation in them, letters that spoke of unrequited love and betrayal. Brandi Taylor, or rather actually Barbara Taylor before she legally changed her name, was consumed with ‘The Paragon’ Brand Frontier. Brandi had shown up at an event and attacked Ana during a press conference, knife in hand she had the intention of taking away his ‘one love’. Brandon thought after the sentencing he had seen the last of this off center maniac. How was she here? His mind reeled. “Hello lover.” Her voice was calm. Knowing that she was right about them being together forever left her at ease with everything she had done, everything she would do, to make it so. “I see you have finally come to me…” “Brandi Taylor… how, what…what are you doing here? I mean, how did you get here, I thought you were… getting better… at the hospital?” The hero chose his words carefully, not sure what would set her off. “I am better lover. It’s amazing what the doctors will believe. A little acting, a little roleplaying and before you know it… free as a bird…” A smirk appeared on her face; normally such a sight on such a face would be beautiful. In this instance it sent chills up Brandon Roche’s spine. “…free to finally be with you.” “Brandi… listen, we’ve been through this, over and over. I came to a few of your examinations to try and help you. You remember that don’t you?” “Of course I remember lover. Do you think I would forget any moment that I had with you? Now… with that bitch of yours and your two little sniv…your two little ones out of the way we can be together all the time. It’ll be Heaven on Earth.” Brandi came even closer to her ‘lover’, her warm breath escaping in long streams from her flared nostrils. Brandon stood back on one leg readying himself, he knew the depths of madness she used to reside in and it seemed that she was still there or had descended even further away from the land of the sane. “It was unfortunate that Chris had to pay a price for our happiness. I never held any ill will against him. He was always so supportive of you. It was that harlot I was after. Just like that new wench that thinks she can have you… I’ll take care of her as well. I just couldn’t wait any longer for you to know that I had come to save you…” With his mind reeling Brandon put the pieces together. Anyone could have at that point. Chris. Pay a price. New wench. Brandi was the one that attacked Ana, Chris and the kids a week ago. She had come to murder his family just to have him. A physical illness washed over him as his stomach churned at the thought of his family dead. It also awoke something in him. He stepped closer to her now, anger and a fear for the safety of his family driving him. “Listen Bran… Barbara…” “Don’t call me that name GOD DAMMIT! Don’t call me that naaaaaame!!!” Her fists slammed out trying to make contact, Brandon simply stepped aside, making her stumble. She spun around her hair flailing about and across her face. “My name is BRANDI. Do you understand me… I am your other half. I’m Brandi… Brandi…” Holding his hands up as if in defense Brandon pushed on with what he was saying. “Just listen. Ok? Just listen. You want me to call you Brandi again that’s fine, but it won’t change anything. We can’t be together. There is no us. There never has been. Here… let’s walk out front and we can talk about this some more… Ok?” In hopes to bring this to a quick ending Brandon hoped to have the madwoman simply agree in the hopes of making ‘her hero’ happy. If he could get her out in the main area the police could be called and there would be a quiet resolution, an end to this nightmare. “NO! You… we… I just need to talk to you more… make you… make you understand how we are supposed to be together.” Brandi Taylor looked around frantically; having her life-long love say such things had tilted her closer to the edge. “It’s Brand and Brandi… sitting in our tree… K… I…L…” Brandon reached over and grabbed her shoulder, snapping her out of her daze. It was also a mistake though as it brought Brandon close enough for her to make contact as well. Blinking the tears that had begun to form out of her eyes Brandi reached up and threw her arms around Brandon’s neck pulling herself up to him… kissing him. The mighty hero recoiled. The thought of this demented spirit making contact with him in such a way startled him enough that he did not outwardly resist at first. Then, in a flash he responded and pushed her away. She stumbled back, in horror. Her love had pushed her. She turned quickly around and around in circles. It seemed that her life was ending. Had she just been rejected by her life-mate? Stumbling again over a bench Brandi didn’t have time to regain her footing and she hit the pavement hard. Brandon paced to her quickly, out of a natural concern. He found instead that he shouldn’t be concerned for her safety as much as he should have been for his own. For as she turned to her back and began to kneel Brandon saw something in her hand that he had had bad experiences with on more than one occasion. The world might think of Brand Frontier as a mighty hero but even he… was not bulletproof. The sleek silver firearm fit neatly in her hand. The way she held it showed an underlying confidence in herself. It was a standard revolver, more than likely the same one that put Chris on the edge of death and it was now threatening to do the same for him. “You have been corrupted lover. I can see that… I see it in your lovely eyes. I see how the world has made you hate me. They… no one… your wife… your fucking brats… all of them have brainwashed you.” The fire that burned in Brandi Taylor for her hero had finally consumed her. The words seemed of human origin but the echo in her voice belied something far less, something more animal in nature. “Brandi… wait… just wait. Listen, we can talk about all of this. About everyth…” “SHUT THE FUCK UP! You realize what’s happened don’t you? You realize that they have tainted you. While I was gone you were taken from me… and I never realized… never saw it… never wanted to see it. But I see it now. You want to be with that bitch Amy, you want to be able to have your cake and eat it too.” Her wild eyes were now accompanied by a frothing at the mouth. “That’s the reason your slut of a wife is still around. You want your fucking offspring around to make you feel validated. You’ve been lost to the way society expects you to be. You’re not who you were. Not to me at least.” The bile that was spewing from the madwoman infuriated Brandon Roche, but he was helpless to respond. His training had tempered his anger and he knew that calmer heads could prevail, even from a situation such as this. He just needed the right opening and he could stop it all. “Brandi… dear… I’m who I always was. Think about it. Look back when we first met. Think about that night at the fan awards when we ate dinner together. Ana and Sean was there, Vic was there, Chris was there. It was a fun time. Remember?” Brandon hoped to calm Brandi down with the one actual memory they shared in this reality. Brandi did remember. It was the start of her love for Brand Frontier. The man behind the superstar came later. It was a double love. She knew and had been intimate with both of them, in her mind. They were hers and hers alone. That’s when there was another noise off to the side. Brandi spun and a shot erupted from her handgun embedding itself in to the stucco side of the hotel. Nothing was there. Brandon tried to use the distraction and lunged quickly at his personal stalker. She spun around again though, her senses heightened with the fear she may be losing her reality. Screaming ‘No’ as she pressed the trigger Brandi’s mind shattered as she realized she was going to kill her destined love. As if in dramatic slow motion the next seconds of events happened… Out of nowhere and with a scream of her own young Amy Campbell catapulted to the top of the railing and launched herself at the man she loved. Throwing herself at Brandon with all her strength she covered the fifteen odd feet between them and grabbed at his waist. Alex Newsome, the man known as Lance Knight to the wrestling world had also made his presence known. As Amy threw herself at Brandon in hopes of pushing him to safety Alex grabbed at Brandi’s arms trying to knock the weapon out of her hands. A crack of thunder, a small flash of light and a searing pain all indicated that, try as he might, he did not succeed. A sudden rush of adrenaline on all parties and things seemed to resume their normal speed. Brandi Taylor fell to the concrete as Alex wrenched the gun from her trembling hands. She simply lay on her back looking to the stars repeating the words ‘my love’ over and over again. To her, she had just murdered the love of her life. As Alex stuffed the revolver in his jacket pocket sirens rang out. The real and expected cavalry had arrived too late, as is too often the norm. ‘Lance Knight’ looked over at the pooling blood, at the limp body grasped in loving arms. As desperately as he wanted everything to be all right he wasn’t sure if it ever would be. Amy Campbell looked up to Brandon. The pain tearing through her was excruciating, worse than any botched spot she had been a part of in the squared circle. She tried to form words but the clouds forming in her head kept them away. Brandon too was in shock, out of the events that transpired rather than being physically harmed. Amy saw to that, in one instant she had blocked the assault and saved his life. He looked in her mismatched eyes, tears forming in his. He couldn’t believe what had just happened. He couldn’t believe what she had done for him. It was overwhelming. Through the entire ordeal, Amy had been watching...from the moment the psychopath cornered Brandon, to the moment that Alex had to hold the redhead back from lunging out and breaking their cover when 'Brandi' kissed the Mighty Hero. At first the woman had seemed harmless enough: About 5'3", maybe 5'5" - skinny and apparently unarmed. Amy was confident she could walk up and snap the little woman in half if another move was made toward Brandon, but then she remembered the last letter she'd received; The one warning her not to interfere or she'd face the barrel of a revolver... ...The very same revolver she produced seconds later... The rest was a blur...one shot off, then she'd aimed at Brandon and in a split second, the possibility of losing the man raced through Amy's mind. Fast enough, it seemed, because she'd launched herself on some sort of emotional instinct.... just in time to come between a man she was growing to love and a tiny, precision bullet. That's when time - not to mention the pain - caught up with everybody, Amy especially. She wasn't sure what had happened, but after a second of staring up into a pair of steely blue eyes she could piece together enough: Gunshot, bullet, pain...blood? It wasn't certain, but she'd caught a glimpse of one of Brandon's hands as it went from her side where he held her to the pulse point on her throat and things looked messy. Words were forming behind her lips, but they wouldn't string together...they just sat there, random words in her mind while she watched the faces - now both Brandon and Alex - hover over her. But the searing heat and blood could only flow through her for so long...after what seemed like hours, her thoughts faded away into unusual darkness. Forsaken - Survivor(Four)
