A Beautiful Day

13 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Immediately after last night's house show, Sean Stevens grabbed his girlfriend Ivy McGinnis, their bags, and hit the road. Rochester was nice... the show was ampted... the people were really friendly... and, he planned on returning there someday... but, the fWo had the next day off. And, while most of the roster stayed in New York and partied that night, with visions of packing and hitting the road later the next day... Sean's plan was to drive that night, sleep early... and, play later. Stevens - "Ivy?" Sean smelled food. It was only ten o'clock in the morning, he usually slept to atleast twelve on the days he had little or nothing to do, in terms of his profession. He quickly jumped out of his bed, as his nose guided him to the hotel room kitchen... where Ivy was busy waging war on the stove. It looked like the stove was winning. Poison Ivy - "Hey baby! You're up early... how ya feelin'?" Stevens - "Sore. ...obviously... but, good. Surprisingly better than I imagined when Universal told me I'd be working the next couple of house shows with Lance and Grendel. Don't get me wrong... Lance is fine... but, Grendel..." Sean moved his left arm in a circular motion, symbolizing soreness. Ivy picked up on the hint immediately. Poison Ivy - "He's a good worker, but can be a little TOO hardcore, sometimes. I've noticed." Stevens - "But, I'm fine..." Ivy turned to face Sean, devilish grin and all. Poison Ivy - "... Of course you're fine. Look at my man trying to be tough!" Ivy's blatant mockery made Sean laugh... hard. It was at that time that he actually NOTICED what she was trying to do. Sean wiped the tears from his eyes, as a result of the laughter, and turned his attention to the carton of eggs on the counter, and the mess that surrounded it. Stevens - "Umm... Ivy... that carton says twelve eggs. Why do I only see four? Where are the rest?" With her eyes... Ivy looked at the floor... the mess on the counter, and the trash can. That made Sean laugh even harder. Poison Ivy - "...and, this is the thanks I get for getting up early in the morning and trying to make sure your day starts off right? You know what, Sean? Kiss my..." Ivy left the kitchen and tackled Sean onto the hotel sofa... it was soft, plush, and after thirty seconds of wrestling, provided the right amount of comfort. Although Ivy initiated the confrontation, Sean was the aggressor... and, ended up on top, with her arms pinned to the couch. Stevens - "Look what we've gotten ourselves into! I'll tell you what Ivy... you tell me how much you want me. How sexy I am... and, I might let you go." Poison Ivy looked at Sean with one of the straightest faces she could muster. Poison Ivy - "Sean... baby... you are SO sexy. So cute. And, if you let me go... I might not be able to contain myself. I want you SO bad!" 'That was easy' he thought as he let her arms loose. Sean moved in for a kiss, and surprisingly Ivy was still there. He went in for another... and then it happened... Ivy grabbed a handful of you- know- what. Poison Ivy - "Who's the woman?!?" Stevens - "YOU! You're the woman Ivy. You win." Poison Ivy - "You sure? What's gonna happen if I let you go? Huh?" Stevens - "I'm gonna leave you alone... I promise. For real!" Poison Ivy - "That's what I thought." Ivy did as promised... she let him go. Sean, being a man... WANTED to attack her again... make her cry uncle... maybe even work her up, possibly making her hot, and wanting to go in the bedroom... but, he was seriously hurting... and, decided against it. Today... just like yesterday at the house show in Rochester... he was a defeated man. Stevens - "Ivy..." McGinnis turned around flashing her pearly whites in victory, almost expecting a smartass comment. Stevens - "You have your camera? The little one you carry around all the time? I want to cut another promo on Lance, and figured I might as well do it while the creative juices are flowing. Plus he left me a LOT of openings in his last one." Poison Ivy - "Yeah... it's in my bag. You want it now?" Stevens - "Nah... I'm going to take a shower, get dressed, and then I'll use it." Poison Ivy - "Okay. I'll have it ready for ya by the time you get out of the shower. And, some food..." Sean shot Ivy a quick glance. Stevens - "...great." Sean jumped up from the couch and walked into the bathroom. Leaving Ivy... and, WHATEVER it was she was trying to fix behind.
