Getting Ahead: The Grinch. I

8 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
in which the Fuse Bros. celebrate Tyler's tainted victory. Wednesday Warfare was coming to an end, and alongso the videogame for another three days. Tyler Fuse had taken on James Cagle, in a much anticipated Match for the Fuse's. After losing back-to-back games through the glitch of not being able to realize the Fuse Bros.' Cheat Codes, revenge, as well as the upper hand between these teams, were up for grabs. And on this night, Tyler Fuse had won. Conor walked beside his rather tired Brother, as their journey to their locker room was almost there. A few of the people gave the Fuse's odd glances... because it was yet again that T and C had cheated to win. And this time, if anyone was to get technical, the Fuse Bros. had cheated illegally, since the Cheat Codes were hooked up to James Cagle system. Regardless, the Fuse Bros. didn't care. They could only remember what it was like to get screwed out of the fWo Tag Team Titles at Twilight, and then again on Countdown... And then again to The Flying Frenchie and Danny Culligan. They did not want to fall behind in the already trailing race to capture the team Gold. So Tyler and Conor would do anything to get ahead... even if it meant not being able to back up their actions this time. Finally reaching their locker room door, Conor opened it up so that his Brother could basically limp in. But before he did this, Ty stopped, looked up, and smiled. "Way to go." He said, entering. "We totally showed them who's boss." Conor walked into the locker room too, but not before taking a last glance into the hallway, where two Referee's were still staring at him, frowning about their recent actions. Nevertheless, Conor just stuck up his chin, and slammed the door shut.
