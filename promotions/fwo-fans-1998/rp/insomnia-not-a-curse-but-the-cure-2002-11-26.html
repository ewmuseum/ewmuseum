Insomnia not a curse, but the cure.

26 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I will never bother you I will never promise to I will never follow you I will never bother you... Life in a nutshell: Child, boyfriend, family, friends...and somehow pleasing them all. If Amy Campbell had ever found the need to 'grow up' like so many people had preached at one point or another, it was now. She'd never had the desire to mature and take on all the responsibilities that awaited her in life outside of that sheltered 'high school' mentality but the facts were facts: She was out on her own and, though she was the last to admit it and coincidentally also one of the few to see it, Amy relied on other people to keep her own sanity intact. It couldn't continue like that, though...because people were getting pushed away, and it became labor just to have them supporting her. This was the end of running to security blankets...time to gather her thoughts, time to become self-preserving. But in the past, when she'd tried to liberate herself from self-imposed prison walls, it only lead to blazing weakness showing through brighter than ever when it had been the one thing she sought to cover the most. The weaknesses were only apparent to the wise...and they knew that it wasn't that Amy always failed, it was that she just didn't try hard enough to overcome herself. What always brought her down, slumping back into her own little shelter of depression, was the perpetual nagging...constant babbling, people re-telling tales with morals she already knew, the same old speeches...nobody realized she could recite everything coming out of their mouth, word for word. Never speak a word again, I will crawl away for good... It was the dead of the night but she was far from dead asleep, like any relatively normal person would have been. Why? Because she wasn't a relatively normal, or untroubled person...she was Amy Campbell. Aimz was always stressed over something, which is why she was never known to be thoughtless...although when she took the time to examine things from every possible angle and outcome, she may have seemed slow. This was definitely one of those times, and she'd been spending hours at it already. Curled up and teetering on the edge of her bed, Amy was contemplating life itself...and attempting to disturb her long-asleep boyfriend as little as possible while doing so. That proved to be a difficult task in itself, however. At several intervals already in her great deliberation the redhead had to get up, roll over or devise a clever way to ooze in and out of bed; Be it to retrieve her obviously lonesome little discman whose assistance she needed the most at the time, or to get something as trivial but vital as a glass of water to give her something to dip her fingers in when the endless, random train of thought got boring. I will move away from here You wont be afraid of fear No thought was put in to this I always knew it would come to this The first issue to think her way through was Darcy. She needed an explaination in the worst way this time...it didn't make sense that something so good had come to her without a cost, things just didn't work that way. Why, out of all the guys who'd ever abandoned her, had he decided to take the opportunity of finding her again and build a decent, once-lost relationship out of it? She wasn't used to being given a 'gift' from God like that, and every day she expected something to go wrong, something to tear him away from her...but whatever it was that she awaited, it never came. They had their fights and they had their near-breakups but they also had a love that, although not fully admitted, was unrelenting and kept Amy from giving up all hope in the world. Just another miracle...but how long would it last this time before that "warm, gushy feeling" was snatched away? Things have never been so swell I have never failed to feel pain... Next were the other relationships she needed to maintain...but they were inumerable. There was Stephan, who'd been weighing on her conscience for countless months. He needed the most attention...if it was time to grow up, then it was time to help her son grow up regardless of technicalities, bloodlines and anything else that might stray her away from the truth that he was hers to raise AS hers. She'd become attatched to him, and finally admitted to herself that she wasn't just attatched to the memory of her best friend, but to the little boy himself. She'd known seventeen year olds brave enough to take on the responsibility of another life and it was time for her to do the same, to keep promises made and raise HER son. I'm so warm and calm inside. I no longer have to hide... Renee, all of her friends at home...they were sustainable, they'd known Amy since she was little. Obviously, they knew and were fully prepared for anything she threw at them...they didn't need to worry, and wouldn't be neglected. Their knowledge kept the friendships blooming...there was no labor involved, because the years she'd been so close to those people had developed the bonds, not promises and simple words. Aimz still needed to work on bringing herself around, though. There wasn't much sense in working to avoid hurting others if she was still only destroying her sanity in the process... Let's talk about someone else Steaming soup against her mouth Nothing really bothers her She just wants to love herself... Amy hadn't even realized that, before she could so much as fathom the name she needed to concentrate on the most in her situation, Brandon, she'd dozed off. And when she woke up it was with quite a jump start: A large, strong hand on her right bicep was jolting her awake. 'Damn Kurt Cobain ...' She thought at first, realizing what song was still blaring through the headphones she'd fallen asleep wearing. 'Getting into my head like that...' But it wasn't a dream, and the firm grip didn't belong to any dead lyrical genius. In fact it was Darcy's hand, intended to break her from whatever trance she'd been in. All Amy could manage was a faint groan, realizing she'd only been half-asleep, tossing and turning unconciously to the point that Darcy not only woke up, but had to steady her. Of course, all that didn't click immediately..."Whaahuh? Did I wake you up or something?" "Yeah...." He groaned, stretching for a moment before managing a comment. Even that late, the sarcasm never left Markson. "...but that's why I keep you around. I hate sleep with a passion." Aimz grumbled. He never failed to poke fun at her various little idiosyncracies. "Hey, I'm not THAT bad anymore. At least I shook the insomnia and stopped waking you up at all hours...now it's only when I'm thinking." Darcy rolled over to face her. "I may regret asking this later, but what's troubling you now?" "Meh...everything." Amy's face was grim, but she didn't feel like unloading ALL of her troubles on Darcy at that hour. Instead, she huffed when yet another issue she'd need to face donned on her. "And now I'm just sick about my so-called 'career'. Letting myself get too caught up in life outside the ring...thinking too much about it. That's why I'm not getting matches, damnit..." The knee whose injury ended Darcy's career over a year prior throbbed sightly as the subject of wrestling came up. He only now started to become used to it, and the slight wince on his face was hidden by the incredibly early morning darkness. "Maybe you should really give them something to talk about." "Do more? Shit, Darc'...I've done the equivalent of squat so far, and every paper's already got some kind of lie about me fresh off the presses..." "Well that's what I mean...maybe they need to start talking about how many bodies you topple." He'd nudged her playfully with an elbow. "And if there's no obvious target, try finding one of your own. It's what I always did." While the redhead considered a response, Darcy rolled the other way and muttered "I'm pretty much your 'manager'...let me be public relations too. You can't wrestle on no sleep, so let's try and get some, hmm? I'll worry about what people say, you just go on a tear...got it?" 'Find a target, take it out...earn your credit.' Amy pondered it a moment before laughing innerly. 'Yeah, right...just walk up to Eli Flair or the Boston Strangler. "Hey, mister...wanna lay down so I can pin ya?". That'll work.' But maybe it would...she just needed a "name" she had a chance of beating and a reason to be pissed off which, judging by her usual mood and the actions of others backstage, wouldn't be too difficult to find. "Gotcha'." {Quite choppy. I felt like writing, and the comments about Aimz' wrestling will make sense soon enough.}
