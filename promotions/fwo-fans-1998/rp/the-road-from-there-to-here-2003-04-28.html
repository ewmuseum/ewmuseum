The Road: From There to Here

28 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After Cyberslam, there'd been no celebration. Yes, the fWo was essentially 'saved' and allowed to continue...sure, every title switched hands - But everybody down the board was worn out. Months in Europe and just about anywhere that had running water (And in some cases, not even that) had taken their toll. Not to mention the injuries - of which there were many - sustained by the wrestlers and even announcers at the gruesome pay-per-view. But it was good to be home and even better with a slab of gold around your waist. Wounds would heal, and even new pains in the ass weren't really that bad if you thought about it. Naturally, some things needed to be taken care of... That brought Aimz to Slaughter. She wasn't supposed to be there, technically, with three broken ribs, a smashed up jaw and countless stitches but there was unfinished - read, newly sparked - business with a 'Master of the Mindfuck'. Kellen Kinkade had belittled her twice now on TV, counting on the fact that she was laying at home but tonight had been a little different. Almost afraid to be yet another in a line of so-called superstars who just seemed to fade off and decide not to work - let alone show up when they won a title, Amy pulled a little trick of her own and had, in turn, switched up the tables. That brought a proud Ivy to Aimz. Both girls realized they hadn't ever had that chance to celebrate Amy's newly won title after CyberSlam...save for a brief hug while they loaded bodies into ambulances - Amy's included. And with another small accomplishment in 'shaming' Kinkade under her belt, the Hardcore Champ was in no mood to go and nurse injuries because, as Ivy'd convinced her, nursing a few drinks was an enjoyable alternative. "Is it just me," asked Ivy as she took a large drink of her Jack and Coke, "or have we been running nonstop since we hit Canada in January? I mean, whether we were closing or not, was supposed to be the end of the road. Maybe a little breather?" She finished off the rest of the glass, "But between Sean, the Garden, and the fWo tour I've managed to be on a plane every day of the past week. When's breaktime?" "When you break a few ribs." Amy winked at her, looking just as weary. The trips back home were good...but they were trips, nonetheless. "Hah! I didn't even miss a beat after the bomb in the Middle East," laughed Ivy, pushing her hair up to show the scar on her forehead, "Twelve stitches, a ruined sweater, and a concussion... and I was there. So don't gimmee all that broken rib nonsense... and you call yourself hardcore." "Au contrair, Psycho Bitch..." The redhead tinkered with a straw she didn't even need, or understand the purpose of having in a glass of so many mixed liquers and eventually watched it fly across the table, past Ivy's shoulder and onto the wall behind them. She'd been aiming for an fWo event poster that had Martyr's face over it, but sorely missed and decided she could probably talk a better game... "Anywho...the only reason I didn't show up to Warfare was because they wouldn't let me. Remember a few months ago when Eugene caused some shit and I ended up trying to break his nose? Fox said he didn't want a 'repeat of that little fiasco', and gave me the time to cool off...and, y'know, maybe heal a little before I started acting like a whackjob again..." She looked up from stirring her pinky in her glass. "...smacking Kellen on the ass with the belt before knocking him out isn't acting like a whackjob, right?" "Not at all," replied Ivy, "but having never been asked to leave an fWo event because of fighting with someone backstage, I couldn't really say." She gestured to the bartender - two more drinks. "So gimmee a night, Aimzy... what show stands out for you the most?" "As far as most memorable? Between the bombing, where BL nearly died to...hmmm...nope, not even gonna discuss those other instances." She winked, "Ahem...anywayyy....I'd have to say as far as just sheer enjoyability? I dunno. Aside from the Canadian dates, I didn't have much fun." Staring off for a second, a sudden memory hit Amy with a gasp, "OH! Mexico! Mexico aaaand...Spain. They were great. Warm, breezy, littered with Lucha Libre and mornings waking up to - in Spain, at least - running water, good weather and clean air..." Ivy was on the verge of giggling at her, which reminded the redhead that the topic was the shows. "Uhh...hmm...I dunno, I hated most of that tour just because of fuckhead getting all obsessive. CyberSlam was just...whoa, probably the best night for me. What about you?" Ivy started talking, but broke out into a fit of laughter. "Not a fan of Mexico? C'mon, I've gotta propose a toast to those two other fuckheads from Mexico City. Shit, what were their names?" Aimz looked lost in thought for a moment, "Mary?" They both laughed, but Ivy slapped her hand down on the table. "Marty! And Aubrey, wasn't it? Marty and Aubrey, the spring breaking fucktards who were in waaaay over their heads. And besides, you got shot in Canada, how could that've been the highlight?" Amy cringed for a second, "Yeah, yeah I did." "It was painful and fucking weird, some crazy bitch shooting at BL." A moment's thought got her back on track, "But it was worth it. Y'see what I got out of it? Brandon, a place to live now - with my son...and his kids are even starting to like me. His divorce is going through without much trouble...I think Ana's just as territorial as I am, but probably won't go as far as that stalking whacko did in trying to get me out of the picture." She smirked, "And besides...you saw us New Years Eve 'n stuff, anything was worth saving that." "Am I ranting?" "Yeah," replied Ivy, "but you're right... all in all it seems Canada was the highlight of the tour. You got a Brandon, Eli got a wife, and we all got the most adorable little baby girl I've ever laid eyes on." "I might've been a cuter baby than MJ, had this little afro thing goin' on with the red puffball...just never repeat that I said 'cuter' before 'MJ' in a sentence when you're talking to Angel. And yeah...it was at the beginning, which was probably another reason it was so good. No 14 hour flights to fuckin'...wherever we were, Africa and stuff. What about you, though...what was the best moment?" "It sounds silly... but last Sunday was my favorite moment." Amy looked at her questioningly, as the bartender finally brought their drinks. "Well, we finally knew when Cyberslam was happening, we knew where Cyberslam was happening... and I know a lot of people were mad at Fox for not telling us that there was a problem... but I wasn't." She reached for a stray pack of cigarettes that someone left on the table next to them, but dropped them and pushed 'em away before temptation could rear its ugly head. "It was just... I felt like we were a team again, y'know? Whatever the future held, we weren't gonna go down quietly without a fight. And if it was the last night for the fWo we were gonna go out together, y'know? Like, for the first time since Hallow's Eve when the old roster stood together... the new fWo really became last week." "Now I'm ranting." Both laughed, and Amy couldn't help but smile..."It's true. Every single person in th...well...every intelligent person in there was working to let the fWo go out with the same bang it had from day one. Wins were secondary...and damn, well...it could've ended there, because everyone did a fantastic job. But hey, I wanna be able to defend that title I earned so there's no way I'm letting anything end." Her wink accented the same smile, "Everything really came together." "Y'know, the night after last year's Cyberslam was the end of an era when we lost television, and Eli had just become Hardcore Champion. Now, Cyberslam is the beginning of an era for another Hardcore Champion..." She drained her glass in one overly massive gulp. "So take care'a that belt, Aimzy... cuz look'it where it got Eli." "That belt took care of Kellen Kinkade about three hours ago...this is where shit begins for me. Forget piddly little fucktards who'll never amount to anything...little bitches who overplay injuries just so they have something to fall back on if they lose, I'm actually getting real wrestlers after me, garnering some actual attention...maybe even some respect." Something was keeping Aimz' smile from dimming in the least, "It's not like I'm calling Kellen good or any'a that shit, it's just...well...at least he can wrestle. How many times did Eugene fuck up? That sorry little piece of shit put no effort in, barely even took me seriously...too fucking busy thinking of my anatomy." The booze was starting to take its toll, it seemed. "Ah, Jesus...what we talking about, again?" Ivy looked up, her glasses slipping down her nose. "I think it was about Martyr staring at Kellen Kinkade's anatomy." More giggling. "But seriously, I don't think Kellen's in this to just fuck up. He wants that belt and what it represents... and I don't think he'll be staring at your body instead of wrestling the match... he's too good to do that. Jus' watch yer back, kay?" "Hey...when did I never..no...wait...ever........bah! When don't I watch my back?" Another wink on Amy's part brought both to a temporary fit of giggles. "Oh yeah...there were those times when stuff happened...like when I got shot...and when Kinkade attacked me from behi---BAH!" "...'Sides, it's not my title he wants. I pinned him twice - first fWo'er to do it, too. He wants to embarass me like I supposedly embarassed him." "So a two for one special, kiddo... beat his ass again." She looked at her watch. "Listen... I need to catch an early flight back to California tomorrow for the band... one for the road?" "One for the road." Ivy clinked her glass on Amy's and chugged it down. "To the journey, champ..." "...Because the destination isn't nearly as important as the company."
