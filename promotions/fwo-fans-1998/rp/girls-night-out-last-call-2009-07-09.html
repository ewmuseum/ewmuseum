Girls' Night Out - Last Call

9 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Siobhan was hammered. She was giggling. She was swaying. She was kind of green… The sun had set hours ago, the band onstage at McP’s had come and gone, and the man at the bar with the long blonde hair was being escorted out by two women. Conversation at their table was winding down and The Assistant knew it was time to make her move. She clapped her large hands suddenly and all eyes turned to her. She pointed to Siobhan, put her steepled hands to the side of her face, and cocked her head to one side. The girls all smiled and laughed and The Assistant smiled back in return. She looked at Ivy, made a circling motion around the table with one finger, rubbed that same finger and thumb against each other several times, and raised one eyebrow. Ivy smiled like a friend would. “Nope, I’ve got it”, she said. “You just make sure she’s all right.” The Assistant gave her a big thumbs up. No problem there - The Assistant would give her life to ensure that Siobhan McPhineas was all right. The Assistant stood and with one quick motion had Siobhan slung over one muscular shoulder. She smiled once more at the assembly and waved good-bye. The Assistant didn’t have many acquaintances – Mr. Reed, Mr. Werner, Mr. Yip, and Mrs. McPhineas, but she really, truly felt these women were if not friends yet still good people, and with their continued weekly interaction she’d get to know them better. And she felt really, really good about that. The stars were beautiful tonight, enhanced by the rotund full moon. Siobhan moaned softly against her back, her stomach probably upset by the simple motion of The Assistant walking down the sidewalk. The streets were nearly empty; it was very late on a Tuesday night, after all. Somewhere off in the distance a wolf howled. Satisfied that Siobhan was secured in the huge, oversized passenger seat The Assistant squeezed her large frame into the driver’s seat of their RV, realizing for the first time that the two had never had a reason to switch positions before. She could handle the RV, of that she had no doubt, but she knew she was going to have to keep her attention on Siobhan in case of an ‘emergency pullover’… The Assistant couldn’t fault her friend for relaxing and letting go of her inhibitions for one night. And besides, Siobhan would certainly pay for it tomorrow morning. But tomorrow was tomorrow and tonight was tonight, and tonight had been very, very good. The Assistant smiled and drove into the night.
