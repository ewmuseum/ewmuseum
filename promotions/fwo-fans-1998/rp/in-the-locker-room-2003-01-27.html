In the Locker Room

27 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Deacon walked into the room, and everyone went silent. He looked around at the other guys, & realized that he didn't know anyone. He knew Chris Shepherd wasn't going to be there until closer to showtime. Chris couldn't get his plane any sooner, and considering this was all action, he was there as a small extra at best. Ivy was missing, as was Sean Stevens, but that was also expected given their ongoing personal relationship. That only left Eli Flair. Deacon thought Eli was supposed to be at the show, but given that his new bride was 9 months along, he might be sending a rain check as well. As never before, Deacon was alone. He recognized those in the room - Scott Slugger, Kyle Dillon, & Rush. He had admired much of their work, but he had never met any of them. They might know his reputation ... maybe, but in spite of all he had done, he had not done it here. All they knew was stories, and now they were looking at just another worker trying to 'get over'. Deacon put his clothes in a locker. He went through his typical "arrive at the arena" ritual while he felt many of the eyes looking at him. After putting his things away, he closed the door and turned around. He glanced at the workers, trying not to stare or make eye contact. "Hey guys, I got something for everyone to see!" Eli Flair's voice blasted into the room moments after he busted the door open. Eli pulled out a string of heavier white paper that was easily as tall as he was. He tossed one end of the paper to Deacon who looked at it. "I'd like to introduce everyone to Mariella Jade Flair." Eli said through smiling lips. Deacon took the picture and thanked God in Heaven that the baby girl looked more like her mother than her father. The other guys grabbed the pictures, trying to check out the fWo's newest fan. The jokes and laughter started, and within moments, Deacon began to join in the camradarie. Maybe he could fit in after all.
