Drink Drink and Drink some more

31 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mike Bear and Pierre Delacroix are again teetering in a local tavern, this time in Seattle, headquarters of the FWO and site of last Sunday's Cyberslam IX. In case you weren't paying attention, it didn't go so well for these two ring veterans. “I di'nt want to win the Inter'et Title 'n'way.” They don't seem too broken up about it. Mike's face looks plenty broken though, with fresh stitches down the right side of his face and a heavy bandage over his left ear to compliment the scratches and gouges peppering his face and neck. Eh, he's looked worse. They're drinking tall boys, having gotten a tip that this bar had the proper Heinekens available. From the looks of the empties surrounding them, they're just getting started. “And you Pete, so the rookie gets a win, bit deal. So some punk kid had to nearly tear my ear off and drove a barbed wire chair into his own arm to keep his title. Damn! I always sober up when I'm on a role. Barkeep, keep 'em comin'!” “You are right, mon ami. Zis business with Brawn, it's done wit'. Move on, zere will always be ot'er conquests for ze Frenchie.” Another round arrives and Bear takes a long swig of his. “That's the spirit! Yeah, got to get the excitement back. I dunno if I was really feeling it fighting Scotty, you know? It's like when it was just me and Chris back in '99 and we didn't know what direction we were going in, and then we get into the Tag Title Tournament and everything good that happens afterwards came from that. Those were the days.” “I'm right zere wit' you. Running roughshod over ze division wit' Ric at my side was some of the most fun I've had in wrestling. Even wit' anot'er World Title under my belt afterwards, I don't zink I've ever recaptured zat feeling.” Frenchie finished his tallboy, and uncapped his next. “I know reuniting with Chris after feuding for so long to win the Tag Titles for a fourth time was one of the highlights of my career, right up there with beating your old pal Mitch at Cyberslam for the Title.” “For ze record, I also enjoyed you beating Wilson for ze Title at Cyberslam." Both men took deep swigs, and started talking over each other. “Maybe I should've just stuck to managing the Bear Wrestling Academies." “I should talk to Ric, maybe get a cushy back office job." "There's satisfaction in training rookies.” "I can yell at rookies. In fact, I excel at it!” “Or I could be behind the camera as a director or something.” “Perhaps I could be a gaffer on ze weekly show.” “I had a lotta fun directing promos with the old gang.” “Some of ze finest gaffers come from France: Louis Levesque, Jean-Paul Dupont, not to mention Jim Clarke." Mike finished his beer and slammed it down on the bar in a “eureka” moment. “Hold up! I think we're missing something. I think I've got the solution to both our problems.” “Really, are you t'inking...?” Could this be it? Something this huge only comes around... “MORE BEER!” They yell in unison. Oh well, they'll probably figure it out when they sober up. This could be the start of something big. Actually, to be more precise, a return.
