Tryptich - Epilogue

16 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tryptich Epilogue - Rat Pack "Who am I? I'm Eli Flair." "I'm the Ninja of professional wrestling. You don't see me discussed on the 'net, you don't hear about me considered for World Title contention, and I'm never on the 'best of' list. But my impact is undeniable... and even if I'm invisible in the main events... you always know when I've passed through. Paul Revere Adams once said to me that the reason I'm ignored by the herd is because I don't need their approval. Well, I'll take being one of the boys' favorites, and one of the real fan's favorites over getting the approval of the internet any day of the week." "One or two people have referred to me in private as the uncrowned World Champion here in the fWo. And while I appreciate the sentiment, I certainly don't need a championship to define my identity any more than I need the easy-use catchphrases 'King of Extreme' or 'Original Nobody' to get across what I'm about when that bell rings. In eight and a half years I've crossed every boundary that this business has. I've wrestled on six different continents, against every man and woman worth their salt and some who weren't. Titles and accolades are just the icing on the cake." "My legacy doesn't need any help in being established. It's already a matter of record and a part of history. Just like Valerian's Garden will still be the most honest, unapologetic band in music history, whether they remain as a fairly obscure cult band or supplant the Rolling Stones as the biggest rock 'n roll show of all time. The difference between suicide and martyrdom might merely be press coverage, but it doesn't change what happened." "The Rat Pack.... the Rat Pack started off as a joke between us backstage, and has become the 'anti- Kliq' if you will. Where GUNS, Silver, Pi, and the rest were friends outside the ring and tried to use the solidarity to take down the CSWA, Aimz, Brandon, Alex, Trip, Ivy and myself just like to hang out, drink a bit, play cards, and basically just take up space when we're not in the ring doing what nobody does better. I'm the first one to admit that the Rat Pack is simply an updated version of what the Eliminators meant to me growing up, but I'll be the last to admit that it's been anything but an honor hangin' out with these guys and gals." "For a 'fake' sport, this business inspires some of the most heated debates over who is and is not talented. And opinions are like assholes, they're both found all over the 'net. But all opinions aside, Brandon Lelan Roche is the biggest superstar just waiting.... itching to break out that I've ever seen. Alexander Lance Newsome and Sean Stevens are finally starting to gel as a team, their demeanor speaks of two men with one goal in mind instead of two men who happen to be tagging that night." "Despite a vocal minority who can't stand the fact that she holds nothing but contempt for the male- dominated business she's slowly taking over, Ivy Lillian McGinnis is still the goal of what it means to be a woman in this sport. The ones who haven't met her face- to- face can't imagine how a small, slight woman could intimidate and inspire.... but give her your attention for three and a half minute and Poison Ivy will make you a believer." "Amy Campbell will make you a believer, too... if she's given a chance. To many men in this sport, a woman who attempts to engage them on their own turf is an interloper who needs to be dealt with. Never mind the fact that this woman is an accomplished wrestler, never mind the fact that she can do more than take a bump and sell her ass off. Aimz is one breakout match away from her rightfully deserved position as a major player in this promotion... and someone to simply believe in her. Someone has to go this far." "Valerian's Garden may owe some of their fame and infamy to the fWo and the Rat Pack, but their fans are there because they rock. I don't feel guilty in the shilling I've done, because most of the people in this business are where they are because someone else thought they were worth helping out. Those who truly deserve the time put in by others will turn around and help someone else get noticed. The others... don't last too long. And I would be doing a disservice to 'Ice Cold' Jacob Hundley, to 'Devil's Desciple' Nick Rodgers, 'Devastating' Mike Randalls, 'Good God' Kevin Powers, and dozens of others who have helped me get noticed. Besides, like Ivy says... the music is what sells the show. The only thing someone else can do is turn up the volume." "And who am I? I'm Eli Flair." "And that's the only thing that matters."
