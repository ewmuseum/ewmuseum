Lionheart Fallout, complete with phone calls.

10 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
REACTION SPOILERS INCLUDED. You were warned. ------------- "So, is Willy B gonna fire 'em?" "Doubtful... he's got bigger problems right now, and without his mask he's taken out of the equation." On one end of the phone was Ivy McGinnis, sitting reclined in the Ottoman in her Seattle condo, with a cup of tea on the table in front of her. "You didn't see it," continued Ivy, "After the cameras went off, Shadows and KroW beat on each other for a bit more in the ring, Steven pretended to slit his throat, which of course brought the security team and most of the execs down to the ring, too. But it was all a ruse, and both of 'em dragged Beav into the ring and beat the living fuck outta him." "Wow... I'm surprised you didn't jump in." On the other end of the phone, sitting on the back porch of his still - new Warwick, NY home, was former two-time FWO World Heavyweight champion Eli Flair. "I would've," said Ivy, "but I wasn't out there. I was still in the back trying to separate Nog and Cheese from Astropop and Alias." Eli laughed. "That's a fuckin' mess, kiddo... what d'you think you're gonna do?" "If the Beav is up to it I need to talk to him about returning the belts - belt, Sheff still has his - to Astropop since they didn't beat the full team. Granted, I think the Legion has a legitimate claim to another shot, but they circumvented fair play to steal a title, and you know how I feel about that." Eli knew, all too well. They were business associates for fifteen years and had been closer than family for twenty. "Think he'll make it to the next show?" asked Eli. "I really don't know," replied Ivy, "He spent Wednesday night in the emergency room, of course - and Haggan picked him up and called me, he's got two black eyes, a broken nose, and he's wearing a neckbrace. So it's really anyone's guess." On the other end of the phone, Ivy heard movement. "What's that?" "Oh," replied Eli, "Studio's finally done, so Angel and the boys are recording. Mick just stepped outside." "Hand him the phone," said Ivy. There was a bit of muffled movement, as a different voice took over. "Yes, master?" "Mick," said Ivy, "Put the fucking cigarette out and suck on a lemon. I told you, it works." In the background, Eli was laughing. Ivy's voice apparently carried just as well as it always did. "Do you realize how ridiculous I'd look onstage with a goddamn lemon peel hangin' from my lip?" asked Mick. "Yeah," said Ivy, "Slightly less ridiculous than an iron lung." "I'm giving the phone back now," said Mick, "C'mon back east and hear the new stuff soon." With the New York phone near nobody's ear, Ivy could hear one of the two men say 'Good luck, bro,' and everyone laughed. "So," said Eli, finally back on the line, "let's say Willy doesn't fire Ric. What are you gonna do?" Ivy thought about it, and sipped her tea in the meantime. "Nothing." "You're jokin'." "No, I'm really not," replied Ivy, "I don't begrudge Ric anything, and I know the fact that he was supposed to end Pierre's career was something he was doing against his will. Plus, we're all currently caught up in what Killjoy wants us to do; Ric is now a liability to him and he's gonna need friends." "Who the fuck is Killjoy, anyways?" asked Eli, "I know you know." This time, Ivy didn't hesitate. Eli Flair deserved to know everything she did, and she told him exactly who Killjoy was, and exactly how she figured it out. She even told him about the night she walked out on the FWO several weeks ago, and the conversation the two had had at the bar. "Damn," said Eli, "I would've never guessed that. But I can see why you're being careful." "The thing is," replied Ivy, "I still can't believe that he'd be doing this for anything but the overall good of the company, but his invocation of Countdown 03 was way over the line. He has no idea what I did for the company after that night; what I tried to do, and what got intercepted by the Mouse. And he was there for most of it!" "So, if he's doing the lord's work, so to speak," said Eli, with good humor, "why did you push for Ric to unmask?" "Because he told me, point blank, that I have to be the control. And he shouldn't've gone into that match with the Frenchie unless he was prepared to lose his mask. Of course, looking at the footage, it's obvious it was actually Ric in the outfit, so he swerved around it." "So, what're ya gonna do?" asked Eli. Ivy smiled. "When I figure that out, you'll be the first to know." "And Killjoy'll be second."
