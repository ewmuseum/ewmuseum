Master-MIND, Phase IV (Unleashed)

9 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The darkness has been alleviated. He's close. "I will still get to him before he can get to me." The one who would wrest you from your peaceful reprieve is hunting you even now, and I cannot hold him at bay much longer. "There is no need to hold me anymore." You know who it is, Fallen One. The one who offered his open hand in friendship, and then delivered a closed fist into your trusting face. The one who opened his arms in brotherhood, and then used those same arms to beat you down again and again and again. The one who acted concerned and consoling, but brought you nothing but pain and anguish. "Flair." How many times did he hurt you? How many times did you lift yourself from the ground, only to have him knock you down harder and more viciously? How many punches and kicks and attacks have you suffered at his hands? How much pain have you endured, all because of this one individual? "Too much pain, but it will all be worth it." He seeks you, Fallen One, and seeks to continue his rain of brutality upon you. He wants to hurt you, to drive you once again to the precipice of the Great Abyss and the slam you from behind, laughing as you spiral down, and down, and down. and I cannot guarantee that I will be there to halt your descent or give you refuge. I have done my very best to keep you safe, to give you respite and sanctuary. It shames me to confess that I believe I am no longer strong enough to keep your hunter from you; I have been battling him constantly and I feel my strength waning with each contest. "He gave me the scars on my chest. He put me into this black hole. Don't worry, I will stop him tomorrow night. The time is now and the choice is yours. Will you wait in the darkness until he finds you, or will you strike first and catch him unaware? "I will strike without warning. You will have Unleashed The End."
