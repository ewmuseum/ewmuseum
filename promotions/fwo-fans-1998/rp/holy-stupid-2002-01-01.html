Holy stupid.

1 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It didn't seem REAL to him. It had been five hours. It still didn't seem real. Had that actually happened? He'd walked in bitter and walked out #1. He was the man. He was the fWo World Heavyweight Champion. He was the first-ever black World Heavyweight Champion. HOLY FUCK. It'd actually happened. He'd avenged it. All three who had gotten him at Twilight--he'd avenged it---Davis didn't win the title last night because of him. The night before, Universal put him in a position to capture it. And now....HOLY MOTHER OF FUCK! It was it. Andrea had come back to him out of the blue. They resolved the issue with a bout of sex. Tomorrow would be a fine day for actual answers and of confessions. The first day of 2002. His first day as champion. Everyone wanted what he cradled in his arms. Everyone wanted the feeling he had and what he had. He....was...the Man. He cradled his off-again on-again girlfriend of his dreams. And he gave a longing look at the title, as if it would not be there when he awoke. Or if she wouldn't be there. But it would. And she would. His body shook. Not from the effort taken out, or the reconciliation made, or such things. The magnitude of what he'd done was enough. And Scott "Hall Of Fame" Slugger was around the corner. But that was the corner. And now was now. Almost imperceptibly, he squealed with delight. It was his. All his. ALL HIS! Soon, the alcohol took hold and put him down. But as he drifted into slumber, for the first time in his life, he couldn't wait to wake up. No way could his dreams touch this. fWo World Heavyweight Motherfucking Champion. HOLY SHIT!
