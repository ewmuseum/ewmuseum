Lost in the Desert - II

10 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Back in the barren desert wasteland of Saudi Arabia Scotty J. Bowman and his cousin "Big" Bobby continued to trek and search for the stadium. Scotty began to feel the effects of the torturous desert sun as he rode upon his camel, while "Big" Bobby vigorously tugged at a heavy wooden cart filled with stacked suitcases and luggage trunks. "Boy, I didn't know the desert was supposed to be this hot." Scotty commented. Bobby stopped tugging the enormous load long enough to give Scotty a stare of disbelief. "But don't worry my man, I have a very good feeling about these caves up ahead, probably a good sign that we're close to the arena." Suddenly Scotty heard the shouts of a man who rushed up behind him and pulled him from off his camel. The man held a knife against Scotty's throat and continued to shout as six other men swarmed over "Big" Bobby, who was so weakened by the journey he had no strength to fight back. The two were tied up and held captive while the men rummaged through the suitcases. "Bobby, I'd wager to say that these nomads aren't happy with us, " Scotty said. The men opened and examined the trunks, finding several pairs of wrestling tights, numerous flashy shirts, an entire suitcase of personally marketed "Scotty J. Suntan Lotion", and an entire trunk devoted to a single hairdryer. "Big" Bobby groaned. "What? It's a very fragile piece of equipment." Finally, one of them spoke, crouching in upon SJB with a dagger held to his face. "Who... are you?" asked the bearded scavenger who wore a ragged outfit and a garment on his head. "Oh, hi, I'm Scotty J. Bowman, this is my cousin Bobby... perhaps you've heard of me? I'm a professional wrestler." "Race .... Lar?" the nomad asked through his broken english. "Yeah, I'm with the fWo, you know, we're touring through here and actually you know what? Maybe we were lucky to run into you because we're looking for this arena, I can't even pronounce it but---" "What is this ... Race Lar?" "No, no it's 'Wrestler', I'm a wrestler." "How is a wrestler? Why should we trust the wrestler?" "No you don't understand I'm with the fWo, it's a big promotion and we're.... Actually, I'm on Friends. I'm on the television show Friends." The nomad's eyes lit up. "Oh! The Friends! The Friends, yes, I see you now, you with the Rachel and the Phoebe, very good, very good." The friendly tone of the nomad disappeared for a second and he angrily lunged towards Scotty. "Wait, you no with the David Schwimmer, RIGHT!?" "NO, NO, No David Schwimmer. Absolutely not. In fact, I replaced David Schwimmer, yeah, that's it!" The nomad put two fingers up to his lips and whistled, directing his men to stop the search and seizure at once. "HALLA! HALLA GATANA! This man kill-a the David Schwimmer!" The murderous thieves began to rejoice and gathered around Scotty J in admiration. "Oh, well, you know... I'm not a hero, I just did what any of you would have done if you were in my place."
