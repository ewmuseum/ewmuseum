Elimination

28 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Recorded moments after Borst had been eliminated from the Body Count event.* Borst burst into a large locker room populated by five or six other wrestlers and their spouses, partners, families. Sitting in the corner was his lovely bride, Yalenchka, avidly staring at a small monitor. "Well that was a pile of shit." Borst grunted, as he wandered towards Yalenchka. He'd just been pinned at the hands of Brand Frontier and Eron, but he didn't seem that pissed off. "You don't look very upset." Purred Yalencka as she offered him a cigarette. Borst took one and stuck it in his mouth. "Fucking..." Borst muttered. "I've gotta learn to keep calm." "You beat Brand today though, did you not?" Asked Yalenchka as she puffed away on a cigarette. "You should be happy, no?" "Yeah, I'm ecstatic." Borst said sarcastically. "I mean, I fucking knew I'd beat that goody goody fuckhead. Granted, I proved I'm without a doubt the best in this business. Finally get a good opponent. I beat him. What a surprise. Fucking..." "But Brand pinned you?" Yalenchka asked. Most people wouldn't have dared, especially a petite woman that had spent the last few years surrounded by Russian children idolising Borst. She knew what he was all about. "Fuck you." Borst grunted, shoving her in the chest. So she shoved him back. "I'll stick cigarette so far up you that..." she snapped back. Borst just laughed and patted her on the head. "I'm way too calm woman. Way to calm." Borst paused and finally lit up his cigarette. "Hey, did you see that bloke's lip? Fuck me, that was a mess. I was well proud of that one." "I once did that to a bear." Yalenchka replied. "No so much blood... so I snapped his-" "Don't wanna know woman, don't wanna know." Borst turned to the monitor to see Brand Frontier still in there battling away. "Fucking, look at him. Fucking fuckhead thinks he's the shit now. It took two fuckers to pin me to the mat. TWO fuckers. And I bet he thinks he's so great now. Everyone's hero. He'll go out, handing out flowers to all his adoring fans and tell the tale of how he slayed Big Bad Borst. Arrogant shit..." "Oh, by the way." Yalenchka butted in. Borst sighed and sat down on a steel chair as he looked on at the monitor. "You still have not got to know me..." Borst replied with a grunt as he watched on. "My father would..." "Fuck this." Snapped Borst, throwing down his cigarette. He had just watched Brand Frontier pin Mitch Wilson. "We'll talk later woman." Borst said to Yalenchka as he stood up and grabbed the steel chair he was sitting on. "Who the fuck does this guy think he is? Borst marched out of the locker room, barging past the ever-growing amount of bodies in the room. "I'll fucking show him..." Yalenchka looked on at the monitor.
