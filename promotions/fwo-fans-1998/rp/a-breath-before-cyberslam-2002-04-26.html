A Breath Before Cyberslam

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Inhale* *Exhale* A slight woman with long brown hair shifted her book bag to the other arm as she leaned over the edge of the table in the corner of the press room of Anaheim, California's Arrowhead Pond. "Can you make it out to Sandra?" she asked. "No problem." said Mufanga Kagos easily, as he grabbed one of the freshly pressed eight-by-ten glossies of himself from the pile on the table and scrawled his name across the image. He had been here at the fan festival, dressed in nothing but his loincloth and bodypaint, since nine o'clock this morning... but this felt like something he could ever tire of. The autograph lineups were getting longer, the large press room was getting hotter and louder, but sitting here, listening to the stories and praise of the fans made up for everything. *Inhale* *Exhale* He looked up to the tiny woman who was leaning seductively over the table, the open throat of her red shirt unbuttoned low enough to give him a glimpse of a lacy black bra beneath. Ring-rat, thought Mufanga. No matter what size or style the promotion, these women seemed to flock to the wrestlers. He didn't know if it was the fan reaction they received, the lights, the pyro, the noise, but these women ate it up and wanted to be more than just the... casual fan. "There you go, Miss." Mufanga said with a broad smile on his features as he passed the photo over to the cute brunette. "Thank you, Mister Snuka". she said, taking a few star-struck steps backward, and then turning on her heel and disappearing into the throng of people cluttering around the other tables. "Snuka?" Mufanga asked himself. "I don't look anything like Snuka..." *Inhale* *Exhale* "Ow." Kagos muttered as he reached a hand to his ribs. He had suffered a hell of a beating last Sunday by Ice, and now it felt like his ribs were grinding together, every breath he took sent shockwaves of pain up his left side. He had told the doctors that he was fine during the post-show checkup... it was just a bruise, he said. He definitely didn't need some smarmy federation doctor telling him that he would be missing his first Cyberslam because of a injury.... but the fact was that he was anything but fine. Kagos knew bruised ribs, he had suffered through that particular ailment before, and these ribs were not just bruised. Everytime he took a breath, it felt like someone was pushing a knife deeper into his chest, and it had been this way since last Sunday. *Inhale* *Exhale* *Wince* Nothing would be keeping Mufanga Kagos from Cyberslam. Nothing would be keeping Mufanga Kagos from Ice, and more importnantly, Ice's future date with the back of Mufanga's hand. Nothing. He would deal with this little injury later... after Cyberslam, he could rest. As the woman drifted into the masses, Mufanga realized his lineup was now empty and he had a bit of time to himself. He looked across the room, where a large mob was surrounding Ruben Ross's table. The fWo Champion was smiling amicably and seemed to be signing autographs at warp speed with a practised, easy motion. The fans were lined up a hundred deep around the table, and Mufanga felt a weird mixture of jealousy and pride looking over at the black Champion sitting in the middle. "Soon..." he muttered to himself. *Inhale* *Exhale*
