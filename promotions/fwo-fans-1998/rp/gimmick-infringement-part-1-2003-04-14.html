Gimmick Infringement - Part 1

14 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Things had a funny way of working themselves out. Obviously, from the sight of Pretty Fly sprawled out on their living room floor, sporting the latest line of huge diaper-like bandages, it didn't go well. All they wanted to do was get a job, make some money, and buy some drugs- okay, and possibly a trampoline. That's all. But no, certain people had to ruin things for them. Borst beat them up, so... so bad, and that wasn't even that worst of it. He kicked them out of THEIR dressing room, he stole Nate's United States title, and booked them in a handicap match. Pretty Fly went to the ring, had their groins stomped on numerous times and then got hurled back into the audience. When they finally awoke, they found themselves in the hospital. Two days later they were released, their genitals heavily bandaged with cotton balls and scotch tape. They groaned and mumbled gibberish as they flicked through the three basic cable channels.
