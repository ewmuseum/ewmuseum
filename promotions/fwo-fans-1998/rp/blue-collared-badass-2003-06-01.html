Blue- Collared Badass

1 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Blue- Collared Bad#ss – prologue: Sunday, June 1, 2003 Flint Hill, Michigan 10:00am Life tends to throw curve balls at rapid speeds that can often catch you off guard if you’re not watching over your shoulder for them. One minute, you’re one thing with your life totally planned out, the next, you’re nothing… a nobody… and, the saddest part about it all is, usually… nobody cares. That’s his story. He’s Sean, and I met him about two months ago. At first, I was under the impression that he was shy… he kept to himself a lot, and never really made an attempt to befriend anyone at the site. He came to work, did his job, and then went home, nothing more… nothing less. The guys used to bag on him a lot, there was a rumor going around that he was gay, which would explain why we never saw him with a woman, let alone stare when a gorgeous babe walked by our section of work. I made an effort to be his friend, because… well… I felt bad for the guy. I don’t know, I guess I just have a soft spot for people in difficult predicaments. He seemed lost, like he didn’t have a friend in the world. Hell, I don’t even know if he’s from Flint. I kinda took a liking to him, though… he had tough skin. He took whatever the guys gave him, and either laughed it off, or simply shook his head. Soon after, they’d stop. It was almost like… he knew something we didn’t. I kinda got the feeling that if he wanted, he could eat those jokers alive verbally. Sean just had this aura about him. He oozed… what’s the word? Confidence… yeah, that’s it. You almost got the feeling that he was here for something other than work. Strange as that sounds… I can’t think of a better way to put it. Then again, I think I’m confusing myself and what I really want to say because when I think about his effort… for the two months that he’s been here, he’s been the best worker we’ve had. Funny thing is, when he applied, he wasn’t going to be hired at first, because he had no experience. He had the size, but size can only get you so far. We didn’t want to trust this man with heavy machinery and have him fuck it up. But, we gave him a shot. But, anyway… He never went into detail about his life, and I’ll admit, I tried to pry. The more I got to know him, the more intelligent he seemed. It got me to wondering… why would somebody so bright, a pretty boy, even… be working at a job like this? One that required zero brain power? From what I gather, he could’ve been a lawyer, a public speaker, in the entertainment business in some form or fashion… not working for some half assed construction company. I’m usually a good judge of character, and I’m telling you… there’s something else to him. And, I’m gonna find it out. This is Alexander Prather-El logging off for the day, I’m sure I’ll have something much more interesting to talk about tomorrow. I’m getting the hang of this daily log thing… it’s pretty cool. -AP
