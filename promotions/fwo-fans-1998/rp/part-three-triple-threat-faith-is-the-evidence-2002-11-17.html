Part Three: Triple Threat (Faith is the Evidence)

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
May 26, 1998 San Diego, California "That's how it is, huh?" "Yup." "Not good enough." "It'll have to be." Ivy shook her head and drained her glass. "If they were so intent on bringing you back with such a fanfare, why do they want you to job to the Deacon? I'm having a hard time figuring out that part." Eli sighed as he brought his beer bottle to his lips. "Have ya even watched the shows in the past month, Ivy? This guy's their gold mine, no question about it. He's big, he's got all the qualities of a hero, and he knows how to work." He drank, finally, and placed the bottle back on the table. "Are you really that upset about my putting him over?" "No," sighed Ivy, "not really. I'm just a little frustrated with Merritt and Thomas these days. How could they let that no- talent hack refer to himself as 'The Eliminator' without even contacting us?" Eli laughed. "It's probably for the best. The name was good for a midcard oddity, but that knee injury really showed me what I could be in store for - I need to get serious. Focused." "So they're putting someone else over you and you're not concerned about it? That's your definition of focused?" "Ivy," admonished Eli, "You should know by now - winning and losing only matters if the fans care about the wins and losses. I put Deacon over here, I can forget about the tournament and work on finding my hook. With Troy in rehab I need to find that perfect opponent. More specifically.... YOU need to find me that perfect opponent, Miz Manager." Ivy laughed at him. "I think I have one." She pulled a photograph out of her bag and slapped it on the table. It was of a large, physically imposing man with a goatee, a baseball bat, and two women with him - one on each arm. "Eli Flair, meet Kevin Powers. Recent signee to the CSWA roster and mega star extraordinaire from the Windy City itself. Merritt wants to put him with Eddy Love until he learns how to talk but the man's potential is unlimited." Eli took the picture from her and looked it over. "Got a plan yet?" "As a matter of fact... " said Ivy, smirking, "Merritt loved it. We sign a match between the two of you - a nothing match at some point before Fish Fund and put you over. He's currently penciled in to win the United States Championship so you'd be the obvious number one contender. Merritt wants to put the Eliminator into the mix at some point, too and see what that kid has." "Not much," said Eli sardonically, "from the look of it. But I like the setup.... just hope the kid can work. I've been itching to get back into the ring for so long I don't think I'd have the patience to nursemaid anyone. Including this Deacon fellow." "Don't worry," replied Ivy, "If you had to nursemaid him that badly I doubt he'd be here. Just take it slow and see what he's got. See what they've BOTH got." ---- November 29, 1999 Houston, Texas "Thank you for the match, Deacon," said Eli, giving the Champ a friendly hug, "One of the best I've ever been a part of." As Eli limped through the crowd of wrestlers, many of whom continue to applaud the efforts he gave tonight, his knee suddenly went out on him and he began to fall - stopped only by the strong arms of the Deacon. "I t'ink you need doctor," said Deacon, still breathing heavily from the match, "You hurt bad before match, even worse after match." Deacon's thick Mediterranean accent brought a smile to Eli's face - tonight was the first time Deacon had spoken to the people himself. "S'all good, big man," replied Eli, "just wish I could'a given you a better match." "No, no, no," retorted Deac, "You give me great honor tonight and I not forget it. Now you need take care of y'self so we have next match sometime soon." Eli smiled again - this was their third singles match in the CSWA with the World Title on the line in some capacity and Deacon had won two of them - "I dunno if I could take another one." Chris Shepherd and Poison Ivy took over Deacon's role holding Eli up and they continued to walk him to the medics. "You do this every time," scolded Ivy, "Every time you're hurt you never hold back a bit and try to protect yourself. When're ya gonna learn you nutcase?" "Never," replied Eli, "I can't go less than full tilt out there, and I know you wouldn't want me to." "So where's that leave you?" asked Shepherd. "Bleeding and hurting," said Eli, "but okay." Finally at the medical room, Shepherd left Eli to Ivy as the four looked at each other for a long minute. Breaking the silence, Ivy leaned forward and on her tiptoes and gave the new champion a big hug. "Go enjoy yourself, kiddo," she said to the Deacon, "You earned that belt. I'm just glad I was out there to see you take it home." Deacon and Shepherd start to walk away as Eli hobbled inside. Suddenly, Deacon turned around. "Eli!" he called out. Eli turned. Deacon hesitated. "You behind me from day one," said Deac, finally, "You help me every step of way, you never let me stop believing in myself. This not my championship, this OUR championship." He pondered this for a moment before shaking his head. "I had a hand in getting the office to look more closely at ya, Deac," replied Eli, "You did the rest on your own, and I don't want to hear you say otherwise." Finally, the moment broken, the new Champion headed off to cut a post- victory promo, and the former Champion headed into the medical room for necessary assistance. ---- October 17th, 2000 Greensboro, North Carolina "Claim Stakers, Mason?" asked Eli, "That's what you called us?" Mason 'Havoc' Xavier shrugged. "You said you were staking a claim, the obvious name for your group seemed to be ClaimStakers. I didn't know they were gonna run with it." Eli laughed. "I know, Mase. But it was quick thinking." Havoc limped toward the medics to get his knee looked at while the CSWA World Champion, Deacon, approached Eli Flair backstage. "I t'ink things went well tonight," he said. "Yeah," agreed Eli, "me too. I wanted to thank you for agreeing to this plan. Hornet and Mikey didn't know if you'd be up for it but I convinced 'em otherwise." "I not agree to anyt'ing," replied Deacon, "I volunteer to be sacrificial lamb for ClaimStakers. All you done for me, was least I could do." Ivy laughed. "I guess the name's gonna stick. ClaimStakers." "Sucks, don't it?" asked Eli. "Naaah," replied Ivy, "What's gonna suck is trying to remember how to play the heel. It's one thing to get cheap heat by beating down the very popular champion, it's quite another to try and maintain it." "You know this business better than anyone I've ever met," reassured Chris Shepherd, "If anyone can figure out a way to do it, it's you Ivy." "Thanks for the vote of confidence." "It not confidence," argued Deacon, "It just a matter of knowing you." Eli looked at his watch. "Shit, man - gotta run. Early flight and I need to get some sleep." He shook Deacon's hand again. "Agan, Deac- thanks for everything. This wouldn't have worked half as well if we didn't have such a great champion to pull it with." "No," replied Deacon, "I only a great champion because I win belt from great champion. If you not do so much for me I not make it this past year. I don't t'ink you two give yourselves enough credit for how much you do for locker room and for rest of roster." "Awwwh, stop it," said Ivy, her voice cracking, "You're gonna make me cry." Ivy hugged Deac and Shepherd, and the four parted company one more time.
