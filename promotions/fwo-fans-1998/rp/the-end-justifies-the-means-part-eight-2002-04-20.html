the end justifies the means, Part Eight

20 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday, September 30, 2001: INTERNET TITLE MATCH: Kodiak Vic Creed (c) vs. Jon Crisp: From fwowrestling.com: As he does so, La Parka and Mr. T climb up onto the apron in the same corner, cheering for Crisp. Crisp lifts up Creed for the Icon and starts to run out to the finish the move... One problem... Creed is no longer being carried by Crisp. When Crisp lifted Creed up and started to run, Creed was able to reach out and grab La Parka by the neck to slip out of the move. Now, Creed is arguing with La Parka and Mr. T, as Crisp turns around and begins to charge at Creed. Mr. T, upset at Creed, rolls up some of his gold chains and swings at Creed. The big man ducks, however, and T's punch instead connects with the face of Jon Crisp, knocking him down to the mat. Creed now clears La Parka and T off the apron with punches as he turns back to Crisp. Creed lifts Crisp up from the mat and drives back down hard with the Hangman's DDT. Creed calmly makes the cover... One... Two... Three. And that ended Jon Crisp's bid at becoming Internet Champion. The bell sounds and the referee hands the belt over to Creed, who jerks his arm away from the ref and poses in the corner. Crisp then begins to stir, rolling out of the ring and holding the back of his head. La Parka and T come over to try and talk with Crisp, but "The Cult Icon" walks right past them, pushing both aside angrily. They look at each confused and start to follow him up the ramp, with Crisp shouting expletives all the way up to the entranceway. This was Crisp's biggest moment in the fWo, up to that point. Crisp had asked that the match be clean, and, as far as he knew, that was granted. Crisp's expletive tirade was not directed at T and Parka. Yes, he was supposed to go on his own that night, but only after proving that he could do it by himself. That WAS the plan. That was not how it was carried out. Chris Universal saw to that. "WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING?" Crisp shouted at Universal. "WHAT THE HELL WAS THAT?!" "Jon, just listen..." Universal began. "LISTEN MY ASS," Jon shouted back, obviously upset at how this played out. By now, a crowd of wrestlers had gathered around the scene, which was obviously not scripted. "How...how could you do this to me? You broke the program with Tom. This was to make up for that. Well, how will you make up for THIS? Give me the jobber title?" "Jon, if you listen to me for a second...this builds you more as a heel, yelling at T and Parka." Universal really had no reason for doing this, other than building up Creed as a bigger heel, rather than Crisp. He was hoping Crisp wouldn't figure that out. But Crisp did. And Crisp did only what he knew to do in such a situation. He *punched* Universal. In the face. Knocking him down. Universal fell to the ground...surprised, shaken, but not hurt. He was quite surprised, as was Crisp. Crisp backed away, grumbled something, and quickly walked away, bumping Erik Kelly in the process. "Get the hell out of my way," Crisp said to Kelly as he went by. This was the wrong thing to say to Kelly, who, according to sources, had the biggest pull backstage. Kelly glared at Crisp, then back to Universal. In one period of three minutes, Jon Crisp's career had done a 180, although it seemed more like a 7-degree turn. Slowly, but surly in Crisp's mind, the other 173 degrees had happened in the last few months.
