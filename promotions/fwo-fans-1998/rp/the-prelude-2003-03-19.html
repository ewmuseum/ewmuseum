The Prelude

19 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was a dark, unforgiving night. No moon or stars visable in the sky. Nothing more than complete and total darkness. This darkness covered the forest .One found himself tearing a path through, kicking stones and fallen branches that blocked his walkway through it. Somehow, he could see in this total and complete darkness. The power of Chaos? Perhaps. Suddenly, he had found what he was looking for in this eternal darkness. A female in a black robe, concealing her face. Instantly, .One bowed down onto a single knee as he looked up at her. ".Two, The Prophet. I am here to serve you." "It is done." .Two said, ignoring his proclomation. "You spoke to Kali, then?" he asked her as he stood back up. "Yes." she replied. "She knows nothing of who actually spoke to her. She believes it to be her alter-ego Jessica." "If she believes Jessica would ever help her, she is a much bigger fool than I thought." .One laughed. "Most of what I said is actually the truth." .Two replied. "Those two have much more in common than either one of them would admit. But anyway, the plan is in motion." "Good." .One replied. "I will return to the world of the living and deliver my speech on Sunday as planned." "Very good." .Two agreed. "Now that the prelude is over, we can begin the end of Kali Saturos." .One nodded in agreement. He looked back at where he came from- the darkness. In the horizon, though, he swore that he saw a faint light. But just as quickly as it was there, it disappered. "You are darkness in and of itself, Destroyer." .Two spoke. "You must not listen to those who claim otherwise. The blood that forever stains your hands is enough." Exel slightly hung his head, looking down at his feet. "I....I don't know." he replied. "I don't want to be...." "Do you think any of us turn out the way we want to, First?" she asked him. "You are foolish. Just like your mother." Instantly, Exel's head rose. "Do not talk about my mother." he spoke in a low, hissed whisper. "She is none of your concern." .Two simply laughed. "Come, First." she said. "We have much to discuss." And just like the faint light had disappered, so did the flashback. "First, are you listening to me?" .Two asked him, obviously annoyed. "Your mind seems somewhere else." .One turned back to her. "I apologize, .Two." he replied. "What were you saying?" "I was saying that we should pay a visit to the real Jessica." she said. "She could be useful to us." .One shrugged. "With all due respect to you, I'm not sure if she's entirely trustworthy. She's....well...." "I've heard the stories, First." .Two replied. "Well....if you do not trust my judgement...." "I always trust your judgement, .Two." .One said, bowing onto one knee again to show this. "I'm certainly happy to hear that." .Two replied. "Now, come, there is much work to be done." .Two turned and walked forward through the first, but .One could only turn his head backwards again.... ....to view his past as Exel Pure one final time. "Goodbye." he whispered.
