Again.

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Look into those eyes. Opened eyes. Clear, blue crystal eyes. The type of eyes that regularly only photography magic or computer editing can create. Eyes that pierce the soul. Eyes that tell you there is something inside that just cannot be comprehended. Look upon the scar. Long scar. A freshly opened scar. A scar that was once just makeup, but turned into a true scar, once again freshly opened. A scar that was once again bleeding. A scar that ran down one of those eyes. Look upon his face. Tired face. A worn out face. A face that used to be young and beautiful, full of vibrancy and youth. A face that could convince you that every lie you have ever heard in your life was true. A face that expressed satisfaction. A face that was moulded by a fresh opened scar and blue eyes. Look upon the grin. Devilish grin. A disturbing, clean grin. A grin that expresses both confidence in him and fear in who gazes upon it. A grin that signals he is pleased. A grin that adds a signature to his face, alongside the fresh scar and blue eyes. The look upon his face was once that hadn't been seen for a long time. Even after his return, something seemed missing. Something missing from the days of when he was at his most cynical. Most sinister. The story of why that look could be missing could be drawn from his personal life, or even his own head. He wasn't insane, but he wasn't one person. It wasn't multiple personalities, but different people in his head deciding to control him when. It had taken time, but finally he was controlled. You remember. The man began to move in a full walking stride down the hill, the voices never becoming silent. The most recent voice brought up something to the attention. "But you gotta realize something, I'm not going to go out there and go we we we all of the time." He said, taking a hop down a slight slope. The other voice replied, "Like Pierre?" the other voice disagreed. "No, like Paul. We, as in me and you." The man suddenly went into a halt, a dust cloud forming around his shoes. "No, soon you'll be silenced and I'll be the only one making the decisions. You just haven't accepted me. But you will." The man took his steps once again down the hill, as the scene slowly faded away. It had taken this long, from July until April. So much time had gone by without the transition complete. So much time with the story unopened. So much time, but now, he had returned. The weak, uncontrollable soul of a man who couldn't keep a marriage together or see his own best friend die (All stories you'll know soon enough) had finally given his reigns to the mind of Eron to the man who once driven it to prosperity. The man who was once the symbol of hatred for everyone who needed something to point a finger at. Look upon that man. Eron. Relentless Eron. The man who proved that sitting on your ass and waiting for your chance will never work, as you have to seize someone else's opportunity if they are too slow to move to it first. An opportunity once again seized. Something was wrong with Eron, which made things right for him again. His scar bled again. His eyes were opened again. His grin was shining again. And nothing could stop him now.
