Desecrate Through Reverence: The Voice Within

27 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Things were getting blurry, thank God. Another shot nearly had Amy smiling. She was alone now...maybe alcohol poisoning would take over her. The familliar footsteps approaching behind her spoke harshly against that, and Amy cringed. She knew what was coming... At least, she thought she did. She sat, massaging the finger that her wedding ring hadn't left...awaiting the inevitable order to remove it as she listened to Brandon's approach. "You make me sick." The words that shot from his mouth nearly sobered her. Amy turned and blinked back the haze that had begun to creep in to the corners of her eyes. She expected the worse, she always did, but those words still dug in to her heart like a serpentine dagger. She couldn't form the words fast enough. It was okay, though, as Brandon didn't have the inclination for her to speak at the moment anyway. "You hit me with what you did, run away, and a few hours after telling me these dark secrets here you are trying to find another way to hide. When's it going to stop, Amy? Where will we be when you decide to finally stop?" "I just..." Amy stopped. "God, fuck you." The next second shocked them both as the 'mighty hero' shot out, grabbing Amy's arm firmly. In a split second she was spun away from the bar, the conglomerated shot glasses she had played with over the last hour scattering over the bar and crashing to the floor. The rest of the inhabitants turned in unison to this new brand of entertainment they were being afforded. It took him a second to calm himself before speaking again. "Fuck me? That's your response to all of this, to curse at me and act like any of this is my fault?" He released his grip on her, but only slightly. "Come here." With that he guided her to an empty booth, a bit of privacy, as much as could be expected in a place like this, anyway. "Talk through the waves/ Amy. Enough with the insults, enough with the running away. Just... talk to me." The jolt was enough to scare the normally guarded redhead. "I don't deal with things like you do, Mighty Hero. I have a daughter and she's in an orphanage back home. Maybe she's Reno's, or maybe she belongs to the guy who raped and maimed me nine months before she was born." Amy leaned forward, propping her elbows on the table and staring violently into Brandon's eyes. "Feel better?" "Yes." Surprisingly (and, let's be honest, Brand's response to most things Aimz throws his way.) Brandon reached over and plucker her on the end of the nose. "At least it seems to be the truth... finally." "Please," she inched back in the seat. "Don't touch me. Things just don't get right again. My past is full of scars, I needed to keep that from you. I didn't want you to have to deal with the shit I cou...can't. But after pulling me around like you just did, you'll be the one getting cut up if you dare to touch me again." "Who in the hell said things were right again? Of course they're not right, after everything we've been through you still think you should keep things away from me. You say, sarcastically most of the time, that I'm this hero. You seem to think I get off on playing hero as well, but you're the one that makes these decisions for everyone else. You decide that I shouldn't have to deal with something like this, yet you go and jump in front of a bullet to put to rest demons from my past." With that Brandon leaned back and, frustrated, he thumped the table hard. "Now you're doing the same thing as you did in the room, as you've done whenever you've been caught not trusting in us. You try to turn this around on me. I didn't hurt you Amy. If you're scared about anything right now it's your doing. I don't care who's... well, I don't ultimately care, whose kid it is as long as you have the faith in us to talk to me. Did you think this would never resurface? How many times has that bitten us in the ass? Everything always comes back, there is always an end to things you never think of." "You realize I can't even stand to look in the mirror, right? I felt like dying the second Reno spoke a word to me because I knew this was coming. I can't stand to face my past...I thought it was a fresh start, you and I. I thought I was doing right for you to see something in me that you loved enough to stay with forever...I was so afraid of flawing whatever image you had of me with my dirty past." As tears flowed freely, Amy tried to stand up. "Let's go and talk about this." she mumbled, glancing around sleepily to the occupants of the lounge. Brandon didn't speak a word aside from a sigh as he stood, cautiously helping his wife hold herself up as they made their way back out into the hallway...past many a curious stare. When the paragon opened and shut their room's door behind them, Amy nearly collapsed to the floor. She propped herself against the wall to speak again... "I..got shot for you because I love you. And I do. I didn't want this to hurt us. I didn't want you to hate me or run from it......" As she spoke Brandon joined her in the floor, leaning against the door as it finally clicked shut. "Well... finding out like this would be the only way that it could hurt us with any sort of lasting effect, I guess. I mean, I can't say really since you've honestly not given that avenue an option... ever... and if anything makes me doubt you, dear heart, it's that." "It's true that our pasts are different, but that's okay. I don't really know how to explain it to you any simpler. I might have only been with Ana and Rachel my whole life and you might have been with a few more than that, of your own accord or not, but that doesn't change where we are right now. I was finding out a lot about you while falling in love with you, Amy. None of that scared me away, why should this? Especially if it wasn't your fault, and even if it was... it's not my place to judge you back then. I can't put myself in that position, my place is beside you... no matter what." Amy hugged her knees, "I want to go get her. I wanna raise her right. I..." She stuttered, "I'd need you to help me. So much. She'd be ours. That's why I knew I had to say it. I couldn't have hidden her, because I'm at my end...I have to give her a proper life." "If you're looking to give her a proper life then you're not at your end... you're just beginning." The big man scooted around a bit now until he was nearly knee to knee with his burdened partner. "My Mom always said that ultimatums never found a home in a happy marriage, but... Amy I need you to stop hiding. Everything... every... thing." "Then is this the right time to tell you I was brought up as a man?" Amy chuckled nervously through tears. This time, she was the one trying to have a sense of humor. "I figured as much from the mustache bleach I found at your house when we first went to visit..." It was awkward, and for the first time Brandon could really see how out of place his humor could be. "This IS the right time to tell me anything else, though. Any. Thing." "Well..." the tinier redhead's guard finally went down. She leaned against Brandon slightly. "...What if I said that I wish it hadn't taken me twenty years to find you?" Brandon realized that Amy had once again come 'back from brink'. He was still worried however that there were more ghosts lurking in her past that would come back to haunt them. He didn't want to badger her anymore, though...the point had been made. He needed to trust, even though he hadn't been given much reason to. He also needed to love and that much he knew he could do, especially the likes of Amy Campbell-Roche. "In my mind, we've known each other forever." He knew there and then what was coming. No more playing nice and innocent...Amy was coming back out of the shell she'd snuck inside of to keep low-key and untroubled. All of this had unsheathed the edge the mighty hero knew laid deep in his wife's veins. [BACK to "Pay Up"] or [FAST FORWARD to "Mending"]
