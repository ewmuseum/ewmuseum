Becoming- Part One

24 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The laughter of a child filled the playground. It was a sunny day, a normal day, a nice day in suburbia. Many children laughed and played, but she could only hear the laughter of one. Matthew Geller. In her twelve-year-old-mind, he was the only one for her. He stood up to the bullies on the playground, and he protected her and plenty of others. He was the nice kid, the one everyone liked, but Jessica knew that she was the only one that loved him. Jessica sighed as she watched him stand up to yet another bully. Jessica's friend Melissa had figured out quite a while ago why Jessica was always staring at him. "You have a crush on Matthew Geller." Melissa said teasingly. "Do not!" Jessica shot back, giving her friend Melissa a slight shove. "Do too!" Melissa returned with a slight shove of her own. "Yeah, whatever." Jessica said, turning back around to stare at seemingly nothing. "You know you do." Melissa said. Jessica sighed. She knew Melissa was right, but what was she going to do? She was twelve years old, she didn't know what to do. But then, her problems were seemingly solved. "A school dance?" Jessica asked her. "Yup!" Melissa said. "And the girls have to ask the guys!" Jessica knew this was her big chance. She'd ask Matthew to go to the dance with her, and everything would be perfect! She walked up to him. "Hi Matt!" she said. "Oh, hi Jessica." Matthew said as he turned around. "Well....ummm....I don't know what to say, so, I'll just say it." Jessica stammered. "Will you go to the dance with me?" "I'm sorry, Jessica, I'd love to go with you but someone already asked me." Matthew said. "Again, I apologize." Matthew turned and walked away, leaving a single tear to form in Jessica's eye. I am becoming something sad and heartbroken.
