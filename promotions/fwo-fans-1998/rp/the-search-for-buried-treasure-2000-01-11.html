The search for buried treasure...

11 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization The search for buried treasure... January 11, 2000 [The scene is deep within the dark, dank basement of an fWo arena. Footsteps can be heard, and they get louder and louder. Suddenly, Havok appears in front of the camera. He is wearing black jeans, a black and red tee-shirt, and his black coat. He seems to be talking to himself, still walking around in circles.] Havok: They have to be around here somewhere. I've searched all over! Where could they be?!? I've looked in the arena, I've looked in the locker rooms, where are they?!? Wait a minute... over there! Eureka! [He stops, and looks at a door. The camera zooms in to find the door marked "Extra Merchandise." Havok runs over to the door, but it is locked.] Havok: Oh no! Locked! If only Rod was here. I need something to pry the lock off, maybe a crow bar. [He looks around, then his eyes become fixed on a table. There are many tools on top of the table, and cabinets under it. He looks on the desk, but finds nothing but nuts and bolts. He looks down, and sees oen of the cabinets is partially opened.] Havok: Ah ha! It must be in there! [He looks in the cabinet, then finds a sledgehammer. He picks it up with two hands and carries it over to the door.] Havok: Well, here goes. [He grunts, and swings the camera at the lock. He misses by about six inches, and makes a big dent in the steel door.] Havok: That's not good. Let me try again... [He swings once more, and knocks off the lock. He opens the door and sees old fWo tee-shirts, hats, and videos.] Havok: Yes! There! [He runs into the closet and finds a box on a shelf. He brings it down and opens it up. The box is full of magazines. Many issues of "Playgirl," all with Havok on the cover wearing the Cruiserweight Title, and nothing else.] Havok: Millennium Man... yes. Role model... yes. But, idiot... no. These things are going to make me richer than Ultra Violet was after winning that millionaire show! [The camera fades out.]
