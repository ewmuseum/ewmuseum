Of exodus and redemption (part eleven)

28 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You... need... to... stop... being... UNHAPPY!" Beneath the dark wig Ryan saw John, dressed up in the Joy costume and sporting his newly blonde hair climb into the ring. He was laying it on a bit thick, and Ryan smiled at John's impersonation of him. The other Dark Riders turned, all of them oblivious to their earlier switch. Ryan was surprised... but he had always heard that Spencer was egotistical and thought only of himself. The fact that he didn't notice the subtle differences in his stable member was not, Ryan supposed, all that surprising. "That's right... that's better... you big meanies with all your 'Oh, I'm going to make everyone miserable and bring you all into darkness, grumble, grumble, grunt'... you need to just get that sad stick out of your wazzoo and replace it with a pretty old candy cane!" Ryan nearly burst out laughing... Christ was this really how he sounded? A wonder more people didn't take him like a joke. David Spencer had stopped laughing and looked down at John for a moment, and then raised his middle finger. John gasped, once more Ryan nearly lost it. John looked like he was having fun at least... that was something. The others had started to walk away, and inside the ring John Skyler who was dressed as Joy stomped his foot and started up the entrance ramp after them. He reached Death just as he was starting to duck under the curtain. John grabbed Death's shoulder and spun him around, and then pasted a happy face sticker onto Death's chest. Death raged and grabbed John's arm and tossed him aside growling, "Fallen, get rid of him!" Ryan didn't understand for a moment, and then jumped. This wasn't exactly in the script, and so he backed off. Mumbling to try and get the words right, he shrugged. "There's no point... he's just a harmless nut... come on, let's just go. Tomorrow it all ends anyway..." David Spencer looked at him for a moment, and for a moment Ryan thought he recognized the switch. Then he slapped him with a stiff backhanded blow that took him by surprise. His head dazed he fell to his knees, his already damaged neck ringing with pain. Behind him there was more movement, and more shuffling. He looked up and saw the Demon pounding on John, who had been taken by surprise. John had been looking at him... worried about his neck and the blow Spencer had given him. He had been caught unaware by the Demon's punches, and was dazed. Demon didn't notice, and kept punching away wildly with reckless abandon. Ryan screamed, and he felt garbage fly through the air and strike him in the side of the face. Above him Death laughed a deep laugh, and a woman cried. Ryan's neck was screaming with pain, and he reached for John. In the Joy costume he looked horribly confused and dazed... all at once unsure of where he was. Ryan reached for Demon, determined to stop him. But his hands closed on empty air. A wildly screaming Demon picked up John and ran toward the edge of the entrance ramp, releasing John's limp body as he neared the side. John went off wrong... his body at a bad angle and unprepared. John tumbled over, and Ryan saw his face one alive for one final time. He fell with one arm bent to protect himself as he tumbled. Demon had stopped screaming and fell silent, and Ryan reached out into empty air. John fell head first, and Ryan could hear the sickening thud. He looked down, and saw that John was lying still. Behind him Demon screamed now, but this was a very different scream... a scream of anguish. Ignoring the pain, Ryan got to his feet and quickly ran down the ramp, but even as medical personnel flooded the area and Ryan Moore reached his one time partner and friend, he already knew it was too late.
