Alpha: One

30 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"We make money, that's what we do." Trey Williams sat behind his desk, speaking to him and Rhys Evans. He stared at Rhys, glaring at him, his gaze burning a hole through the side of the young man. The two wrestled in the Championship Wrestling Federation, an off the charts "indy" federation that meandered in the mid-West region of America. They did shows in High School gymnasiums and Civic centers. Rhys had been with the company for years now, a mainstay of the federation. He couldn't be happier. He had seen more, he had been to the top. To be here now, listening to this anal-retentive owner of this shit company, was demeaning. "Rhys, you've been up there with the top babyfaces for I don't know how long, but you've never gotten that one title push. Now's your chance. You..." Trey pointed at him. "...you're the man, you know I love you. I'm taking care of you here man, aren't I? You're my big draw and I'm don't want to take your heat away from you for a second. But, I need a favor..." Who was Trey Williams to talk to him like that? He had defined the wrestling business. He had wrestled people that Trey had grown up emulating. He had been what Trey and Rhys and all of the other hacks only dreamed of being. The CWF was nothing but a piece of shit on the bottom of his boot. Filled with life-long curtainjerkers and backyard wrestlers, guys that took off from their petty jobs to be weekend warriors for twenty-five bucks a show. People that were no much better than the hilljacks in the stands. Yet, he was still here. Money had become tight and the endorsements had all dried up. Everywhere he went either had no hope of making it or had long past their moment in the sun. The big companies had all been filled up by a new generation. People didn't want him anymore. "...I need you to let Rhys get one over on you. I've got this plan for a feud between the two of you that can finally get..." He finally looked towards Trey, his lip curling slightly. He didn't understand it anymore. What had happened to the sport that he had escalated to the point it's at now? Why they had all turned their backs on him? They wouldn't be anywhere if it wasn't for the blood and tears and sweat that he had given for this business. The kids that were out there now making their names were treading in the path that he had created, but there was no thanks. He had given everything and recieved nothing; and now, now he being asked to give more.
