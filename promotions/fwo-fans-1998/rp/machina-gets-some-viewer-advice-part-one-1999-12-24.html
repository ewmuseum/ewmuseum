Machina gets some viewer advice (part one)

fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Machina gets some viewer advice (part one) December 24, 1999 ::The scene opened on Machina, sitting on an oversized comphy couch in front of a fire. He sipped at a large cup of hot coco, and the whipped cream that coated the top of the cup was soon smeared over his mask. Smacking his lips, he set the cup of coco down, and it was then that the camera noticed the large mail bag sitting next to him. Machina looked at what the camera was focusing on, and beamed.:: That's right your honor... thousands of letters, all addressed to Santa Claus! Er... me! Yeah, that's right, me! And do you know why? ::The scene suddenly cut from Machina to an obviously hand-drawn sign that had been taped on the wall. In broken English it read...:: HELP SAV MACHINEA'S LOVE LIF. WRITE HIM LETTER WITH IDEAZ ::The camera swung back to Machina, who was smiling proudly over the bag full of mail.:: That's right, these are all letters from fans who are here to help me get some... wait... can I say get some on this station? I can't? Well what can I say? 'A nice hug'? What kind of network is this? This wouldn't happen if we were on FOX! What? We are on FOX? Well then how do you explain all that nudity on Ally McBeal? What? No nudity on Ally McBeal? Oh come on... I've seen it myself! I've got a copy of last night's show right here... ::Machina reached into the couch cushions and pulled out a video tape in an oversized, colorful box. He looked at the cover of the tape reading, 'Ally McMeal: the hot lawyer babe'. On the cover of the box was a woman with oversized breasts in an open business suit. Machina's brow furrowed.:: Wait a minute... this isn't Ally McBeal... ::Billy James wandered into the scene, and spotted the videotape in Machina's hand. With a quick movement Billy James reached for it.:: Yoink! ::Machina watched Billy James trot out of the area and shook his head.:: Well... anyway, back to this mail bag. You see, I've got this problem. There is this girl that I really want to do... er... "hug". So I asked the fans of the fWo to send me in some advice to help me get closer to the wonderful Penny Pureheart. If you would like to send me mail, you can do so by logging onto your computer -not that I'd ever use one mind you... the computer is full of perverts, RYDERS and weirdos- and e-mailing '[detail withheld]'. I'll read your letters on the air, like right now! ::Machina reached into the mail bag and pulled out one envelope, smoothing it out before opening it. There was no explanation as to why e-mail off the internet had been placed in an envelope with a stamp, but such logic doesn't have any place in professional wrestling anyway. Machina tore at the letter and cleared his throat.:: Okay! This letter comes from a Mr. "High Flying Zero" and he writes; ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ "Dear Machina, Just give the bitch some flowers and ask her out on a date over the phone if she doesn't like you then take off your mask and scare the bitch even more." ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Hmmm. Well, I could do that I suppose... but if I took off my mask then how would I be able to wrestle any more? It's a common fact that all wrestlers get their super powers from the masks they wear, so I'm afraid that idea won't work at all. Although I do like your idea of scaring Penny into liking me. That's how my parents met, you know. Okay! Next letter comes from a Mr. "Christopher Z" and he writes; ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ "Dear Machina, Women are a sucker for pretty sparkling things. Like a CHAMPIONSHIP TITLE! If you won yourself a little title then I'm sure she would come around....wouldn't she? Whatever you do just keep that huggy/kissy stuff off the air. Nobody needs to see any of that." ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Thanks for the idea, Christopher! I thought about just WALKING my way into her heart, but maybe if I had a title it could sweeten the pot. Of course, the girl hates wrestling so that really wouldn't work at all. Hey, whatever happened to those earrings that Ultra Violet made? I'll bet she would like those... I'll have to ask him about that. Anyway, next letter comes from a Mr. "HROX13". Huh, strange name. All those numbers in there... anyway, he writes; ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ "Hey Machina! Nice to talk with you finally! I think you should just tell her you love her. Women really like honestly and commitments. If you tell her how you fell then I'm sure he would like it but she has to make up her own mind. Remember, if you love something set it free and if it doesn't come back to you it was never yours to begin with. Bye!" ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Well HROX13, your letter sucks. If I get her why would I want to let her go? Are you insane? No my friend, if I love something it's going to be locked in a tiny little cage like veal so it can't go anywhere. That's the Machina way, pal. This next letter comes from a Mr... Slugger. Huh. I wonder if Scott Slugger knows that this guy has the same name as him. Anyway, Mr. Slugger writes; ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ "Dear Machina, You were an idiot last year when you gave me your title shot, and you still are. Give it up before you embarrass yourself any more. Sincerely, Scott Slugger" ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Hmmm... gave him a title shot... I don't remember giving any other Scott Sluggers a title shot... of course, there was that whole episode in Mexico... Well Scott Slugger, you're certainly right that I don't want to embarrass myself. Thanks for your input, and I'll be sure to mention to the Scott Slugger that I know that you said "hi". Well folks, that's about all the time I have for today... but join me tomorrow when I open some more reader mail and try to get some advice from some fWo wrestlers. Hey, maybe I'll even get some advice from Scott Slugger! Well, bye! ::Machina waved at the camera as the picture faded to merciful black.::
