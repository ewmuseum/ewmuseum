Fatherhood II

10 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A long black limo pulled up to the front of Cedars Sinai Medical Center. Vince opened the car door as he stepped out looking to help his expecting wife out of the limo. He gently grabbed his wife's hand and led her toward the door of the hospital. Vince's driver had run into the hospital to let some nurses know that Victoria was outside. He came out of the hospital with a nurse and an orderly with a wheelchair. Vince gently helped his wife into the chair as the orderly and nurse wheeled Victoria into the hospital. "Mr. Jacobs your information is already in the system so you don't have to speak to the nurse at the desk." "That's good." "But we do need to get you prepped for the birth of your child." Vince followed the orderly to a small room where he could change his clothes and meet his wife in her birthing room. He made sure she had the nicest room in the hospital. Vince also wanted nothing but the best and he wanted that for his wife and unborn daughter. While Vince was getting ready Victoria was in her room with the nurse doing her breathing exercises. You can hear Victoria doing her breathing exercises down the hall. "You're doing fine Mrs. Jacobs." Breathing hard... "It's not the easiest thing in the world." "I know sweetie but it be better in a little while." The door suddenly opened and Vince walked into the room with Dr. Nichols. Vince went by his wife's side and held her hand. "How are you feeling Mrs. Jacobs?" "Okay Dr. Nichols." "Are we doing our Lamaze breathing?" "Yes I am." "Good... How far apart is the contractions nurse?" "Every five minutes Doctor." Dr. Nichols looked at Vince and Victoria. "Won't be to long now." "Keep breathing honey. You are doing good."
