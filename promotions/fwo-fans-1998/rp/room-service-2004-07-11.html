Room Service

11 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tyrell sighed as he looked at the clipboard. Sam had blown off the evening rounds yet again. He'd left the Doctor's Lounge ten minutes before Tyrell had and wasn't there when Ty returned from his own rounds. It wasn't bad enough that Sam's rounds were minimal at best and he had just enough work to justify the position created just for him... the perks of having a father on the Board of Directors. Nepotism at it's finest. What made the matter worse was that Sam was lazy. He knew he could slack off and not be concerned about losing his job. Unfortunately for everyone else working the same shift as Sam, it would invariably fall on their shoulders to pick up his slack. Complaining never seemed to accomplish anything, and Sam just didn't care what anyone had to say about him. When he worked the Day shift he would get his work done, barely. But on Nights he'd disappear somewhere and show up about thirty minutes before shift change looking like he'd just woke up. Tonight it was Ty's turn to pick up the slack, which meant checking on a few patients in the Psych Wing. Normally not a bad deal if you don't mind poorly lit hallways and the occasional scream. Sam's patients were mostly catatonic, either through medication or through their own individual "situations". He skimmed over the names and one in particular caught his eye. David Cross. A chill ran down his spine. A couple months back, Cross was just another John Doe who'd been checked in without identification. What he did have, though, was a rather sizeable part of his face sliced away. An act done by his own hand. He was semi-vegetative when he came in, sporadically making a brief noise of minor gesture. To protect Cross from himself, he was placed in a straightjacket. Ty had only seen Cross move once, and that was once too often. Once he was identified, physical restraints were replaced with chemical ones. He rarely moved at the best of times, which made the need to medicate him almost overkill. But for a professional wrestler and trained martial artist who had done... that... to his own face, it was seen as a necessary precaution until he could be adequately observed. Unfortunately it became more and more apparent that Cross was being conveniently shelved away. Ty approached Cross's room. It was absurd, but whenever Ty got close to that door he felt like he was being watched. Impossible, since there were no cameras anywhere in the Psych Wing due to budget cutbacks. He stepped closer to the tiny observation window set in the door and recalled that eye that stared at him through that mangled flesh that was once David Cross's face. The little guy had freaked him out. Steeling his nerve, Tyrell slid the panel aside and half expected to see David staring out at him. But there was no one's face in the glass. No face but the reflection of his own. He stared into the sparsely furnished room and watched David's chest slowly rise and fall as he lay on top of the covers on his bed. It was hard to tell in the shadows if he was awake or not, but he certainly wasn't moving. Given the list of meds on his sheet, it was doubtful he could even get up to piss. "He's breathing. He's not moving. Looks fine to me." Ty jotted down a few brief notes on the page. A loud bang from the room across the hall made Tyrell spin around and drop the clipboard. What the Fuck was that? he asked himself as he dropped down to pick up the clipboard. He stood up and checked the name on his list. The noise came from April's room. Ty slid the panel aside and looked inside. The girl was lying naked on the floor with her bed slid away from the wall. "Shit!" Ty reached for the keys on his belt. No keys. "Shit!" He repeated and ran back to the Lounge. It took two minutes to find the keys and return to the room. Miracle of miracles, Sam was waiting in the hallway outside April's door. "Slack ass," he mumbled as Tyrell approached. "Don't worry I took care of it." Tyrell looked into the room. April was back in her bed, which had been moved back into place. "How did you know she was in trouble?" "I saw you take off," Sam said with a smirk. "Figured you got jumpy or something so instead of calling for you I took care of it myself." Tyrell shoved the clipboard into Sam's chest. "Why don't you take this and go fix your bed-head." Sam snatched the board and almost seemed to strut down the hallway. "Cocky little prick," Ty muttered. He wasn't thrilled about his co-worker, but the feeling of being watched suddenly overwhelmed him. Ty turned back to Cross's door and realized he'd left the panel open. He couldn't see the face, but Ty knew that Cross was standing in the room just out of the light of the hallway. A shadow moved. There was no way it could be Cross through, not with all the meds in his system. Against his better judgement, Ty moved closer. Was there someone in the room with Cross? "Dave?" He asked the door. "You okay buddy?" There was definitely someone standing in the shadows. Ty looked over at the empty bed. How was this possible? "Dave?" Despite the thickness of the door, he could hear Cross speaking to him. "I saw your friend Sam come out of April's room. He's been doing terrible things to that girl. Guess it'd be like having sex with a warm corpse, but whatever floats his boat. Probably the only woman he could get." "You saw him?" David took a step forward and let the light shine on his disfigured face. "With my own two eyes." Tyrell fought to keep from cringing. "How do I know you're telling the truth?" Cross stood with his hands on his hips. "I didn't tell you that so you could do something about it. I told you so you'd know why Sam got hurt so badly the next time he touches that girl." Something in his voice told Tyrell that Cross was serious. "Get back in your bunk Cross. Lights out." "I don't mind the dark. The dark doesn't mind me. But Sam should." Tyrell slid the panel back and stepped away from the door. He thought about making a note of the incident, the threat Cross had made, or the allegation against Sam. At least he should mention that he'd seen Cross moving around when he should have been rendered immobile. He walked back to the Lounge and could hear Sam's guffaws as he watched Conan O'Brien. With each annoying laugh, the past few minutes dissolved from Ty's memory. He'd keep a closer eye on Sam and see if what Cross had said was true. If patients were being raped, Cross was going to be standing in line if he wanted to lay a beating on Sam.
