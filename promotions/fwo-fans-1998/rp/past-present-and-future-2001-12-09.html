Past, Present, and Future

9 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For the past two weeks since his emotional visit home for Thanksgiving, Monte Burns had still not completely gotten back into his normal routine. While Burns spent time back at the office once more, his mind would often drift back to the final conversation with his father and brothers and where everything went wrong. He had not intended on making the visit turn into another verbal altercation between him and his family. Yet, when it was time for him to leave, there was Monte again exchanging heated remarks with them. Nathan's attitude in particular had upset Monte, as his brother seemed bent on making his visit a nightmare from the start. Sure, Monte had made some choices in the past that brought a great deal of grief to his family, but Monte thought that his family had finally moved past all that. He thought that they had finally understood that they had backed him into a position where he had to respond. Unfortunately, that was not the case. Burns shook his head, trying to move on from the thoughts as he looked down at the desk in front of him. Papers and folders were strewn about all over the desk, and unfortunately, it was work he could not delegate to someone else. These were deals that needed his approval, and even though his company was experiencing some decent economic times, Monte could not allow anything to pass through without checking it over first. Just as he was about to open one of the folders, his phone rang. Burns reached up and placed it on speakerphone. "Yes?", he asked. "Mr. Burns," said his secretary Aliesha, "Mr. Fortune is here to see you." "Send him up," said Burns. "Yes, sir." said Aliesha. As Burns ended the phone call, he leaned back in his chair and smiled. It had been quite a while since he been able to sit down and talk with Lance. He had called Lance upon returning from the Thanksgiving, but the two didn't have much time to chat. Now, the two best friends would be able to sit down and have a conversation for the first time in a long time. The door to the office slowly opened, and Lance walked inside, taking a seat in front of Monte. Greeting Fortune with a handshake, Burns walks over to the nearby counter and pours himself a drink. "You want a drink, Lance?" asked Monte. "No thanks," replied Lance. Burns shrugs his shoulders before grabbing his drink and walking back over to his seat. As he does so, Fortune leans back in his chair and smiles. "So, Monte," he asked, "sounds like your visit back home didn't go so well?" "You could say that," said Burns as he chuckled a bit. "Oh well," Fortune said, "it's not like they've ever understood you anyway. Besides, you've got more important things to focus on right now." "You certainly have a point there," replied Burns. "I thought I was done with Winters after kicking his ass at Halloween Hell. Now after what happened at Fallout, it's like I have to prove myself again." "You don't have to prove yourself to anyone," Lance said. "You've been fWo Cruiserweight Champion twice. You've beaten everyone in the division. You should be going after much higher things right now." "You won't get an argument from me on that," quipped Monte. "The problem is, the people in charge of the fWo seem hell bent on keeping me down." "Yeah, no kidding." said Fortune. "I don't know how in the world Tempest is the Cruiserweight Champ." At the mention of this, Burns leaned back in his chair and took a drink. Since losing the title to Tempest the night after Halloween Hell, Burns had thought long and hard about the young woman and her talents. Even though at first he saw her as nothing more than a joke as a prankster, Monte had watched her actions in the fWo and learned about what she had gone through prior to entering the company. The joking seemed to be a release, allowing her to try and find pleasure in a life that had been so rough. You knew she had to be hurting inside, but she was unwilling or unable to allow that to show. But recently, during her troubles with Ric Chronos and Eddy Fate, it had become obvious that she was having trouble coping. Her life was becoming more and more difficult. And while her personal life was increasingly confusing, her professional life was skyrocketing. The parallels between what was going on with Tempest and in Monte's own life were apparent. "I don't know," said Burns as he leaned forward. "It seems like she's much more than meets the eye." "There's a lot that meets the eye," says Fortune with a wink. "I'm serious, Lance," says Burns, while trying to fight back laughter. "She really seems to be someone who could be special." "Special? What do you mean by special?" asks Fortune. "Special as a wrestler," Monte replies quickly. "What else would I have meant?" "I don't know," says Lance with a shrug. "Just making sure." Both men share an uneasy laugh, as the scene fades out with the two best friends still exchanging conversation.
