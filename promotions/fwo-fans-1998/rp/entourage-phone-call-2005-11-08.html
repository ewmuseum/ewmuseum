Entourage: Phone Call

8 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Phoenix, Arizona... Scott Falk laid in bed at the home he and his brother, Robert, shared. Hands tucked on the back of his head, Scott was staring up at the ceiling and feeling somewhat bored. Truth be told, Scott Falk was growing tired of not having a chance. His size was preventing him from getting ahead, and in spite of being in the Southwest Wrestling Alliance along with his "twin", Robert, neither of them had gotten any opportunity from other promotions. Scott was idly wondering if he and his brother should pack up and look for work elsewhere. They were getting nowhere in the Alliance, having won and lost their tag titles so many times that it's almost lost all meaning for them to have or not have the titles, and yet they really couldn't split up because of the absolute size difference between the two. The cell phone to his left went off. Scott hesitated for a moment, surprised at the audacity of the monkey on the other end who had apparently forgotten that it was three in the morning. Who the hell calls ANYONE at three in the morning? As calmly as he could be bothered, Scott rached over with a free hand and grabbed the cell phone. Flipping it open in one hand, he casually put it to his ear and spoke. "This is the Shitty Restaurant. What's your order?" he said. No, he didn't really care about the potentially virgin ears at the other end of the line. It was three in the fucking morning. If these are kids prank calling him, then they deserve it. "I have no idea why you always greet me like that, Scott. Where's the love?" the man on the other end said, "I mean, c'mon, brother, I know it's... what? One in the morning over there, but you gotta come up with a better greeting than that." Scott recognized that voice and sat up. "Son of a bitch. Allen." he said. "That's right," the man known in the fWo as the Codemaster said, "I'm guessing you weren't asleep at whatever time it is over there, Scotty boy. So, what's your malfunction?" "I dickslapped the hooker I was in bed with so hard that I left an dent in her skull a few inches deep, then I kicked her ass to the curb and let her hopefully get run over by a truck." Scott said, as if what he just said was the God's honest truth. "Yeah, I'm sure." the Codemaster said, "Look, Scott, I'm going to cut to the chase mainly so I can skip your wonderful hooker stories. The fWo is coming to Arizona soon." Scott scratched his black mane with blonde highlights, and thought about it, "So, like, what's that got to do with me? Don't you hate that place?" "Oh, I do. With every fiber of my being. But I'm gonna fight the man, y'know? And I'm gonna need some help from the outside. People I can trust. People like you and your brother." the Codemaster said, "You hear what I'm sayin', bro?" "I guess." Scott said, before he added, "So, like, what do you want me to do?" "You know Kyle's restaurant in Phoenix? We'll meet there. Everyone involved in this little... 'revolution'... I'm planning to do. Bring your brother, too." the Codemaster said, "We'll storm fWo, make a scene, and defeat all comers. Hell, I'm a tag team champion, now. Think about the possibilities of that. So, you in?" Scott thought about it. He was going nowhere in the Alliance, and the same could be said for his brother. He had been waiting for an opportunity to do something meaningful. So, he spoke on the other line one more time, "Yeah, sure. Me, my brother, and all the bitches who refer to fucking as 'Falking' will be there." There was a very awkward pause, before the Codemaster said, "Sure. We'll be there in two days, we kinda have to fly out from China. And considering that the owner of this phone is staring at me weirdly, I should probably go." With the sound of Beef screaming "RETRIBUITION~!", the phone clicked off. Scott placed the phone back on his nightstand and resumed his prior position.
