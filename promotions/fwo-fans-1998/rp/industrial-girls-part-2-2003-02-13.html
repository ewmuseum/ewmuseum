Industrial Girls - Part 2

13 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
They got to the hotel around 9:45 that night. Tipsy and buzzed, neither girl would admit the truth. They were completely sloshed. But they both said goodnight and retired to their separate rooms. It didn't last. Twenty minutes later, Amy called Ivy to see if she wanted to hit the bar in the hotel. Ivy said sure, she needed to change, but why not? Amy, too, needed to change. So she said. And then for the second time tonight, the two women bonded, this time over a common love of Cinnamon Schnapps. Goldschlager. Nirvana. A mixed drink quaintly called a "Fuck You." There's a few people hanging about, but the girls are oblivious. The subject came up of 1980s movies, and in less than half a minute, the greatest 1980s movie song in history hit the vocal chords. Partly to see if they remember the words, partly so they have an excuse, like they need it, to drink and bond some more. Ivy can sing? "You don't need money" Drink. Amy can sing? "Don't take fame" Drink. Take it, Ivy. "Don't need no credit card to ride this train." Drink. Ignore the stitches, Amy! "It's strong and it's sudden and it's cruel sometimes" Drink. Take it home, Ivy! "But it might just save your life" Drink. Duet. "That's the power of love!" In the background of the bar, The Deacon and Chris Shephard appear to be highly amused by the girls. And the girls were equally amused by them. Amy was the first to respond to the chuckles, interrupting the senseless, drunken giggles pouring out of she and Ivy for a moment to stutter "Why're...they lookin' so.........they're laughin' at us! Ha........they've probably jussst been drinking........drunk people are funny!" "I dunno," replied Ivy, "I don' really know any drunk people." She stirred the remnants of her drink with her finger and licked the alcohol off. "Drinking in moderation is a good idea." Staring at the glass... Ivy suddenly downed the contents in one swift motion. "But we're in Mexico in the wintertime... it's like we're on vacation, don't'cha think?" "Yeah!!!!! Vacation................" Amy was blinking at an empty glass for the time being, "Like...with guys and stuff.....but I haven't seen BL yet." She pouted. Ivy giggled. "Don't start that pouting stuff, silly red- maned ball of energy.... Why don't you just go find him?" "Because he wouldn't come drink with us! He'd be all 'Oh no, you come in here and we can play Scrabble!" Amy groaned, then an unintentional power backed her complaints, "SCRABBLE, IVY, SCRABBLE!" "So play scrabble with naughty words," suggested Ivy, "or slip 'em a mickey. Or do like I did to Sean and grab him by the sides of the head and kiss him in front of everyone in the world. Sometimes these men need a kick in the ass before they realize what they've got." Ivy looked around, her gaze resting on two American- looking college- aged men who just walked in. They looked Amy and Ivy up and down very deliberately, nodding in approval. "Y'see?" asked Ivy, "I guarantee you that if we invited those two guys over here they'd sweet talk us to the end of the world and if you and I fell for it we'd be seduced tonight and abandoned tomorrow, while upstairs there's a man I love to the end of the earth and another that you could feel the same way about... and they are or were both terrified of making the first move." She leaned forward, resting her elbows on the table. "It ain't easy to catch the right one, but it's worth it, kiddo." Amy smiled, "Even though it's only been a few days of being with him, the last three months've shown me it's well worth i---HEY, that guy's lookin' at'chu' reeeeeeeeeeeeeeeally funny............" Though obviously off her ass drunk, the redhead's perception was unusually acute...she'd noticed one of said college boys nodding again toward Ivy, then winking at his buddy and pointing at the both of them. "They're tourists........I don' like the looks of 'em...." Ivy gave them a second look - completely non- shy when it comes to staring or blatantly looking. "Why not? They're... children! Watch this - I bet if one of us - just one of us smiled in their general direction they'd both come over thinkin' they're king shit." "In my defense....they're probably like my age." Amy giggled while taking an incredibly strong sip of 'Fuck You' - literally - from her newly replenished glass. Her next comment served to both command and lovingly mock Ivy, as she said it with a wink. "You rope 'em in.......you're the 'older woman'." "I was referring to their maturity level, not their age, silly Aimz." Ivy laughed, making brief eye contact again. "You empty? Want to get those two to buy us another round?" "Now Ivy," Amy made a semi-serious face, furrowing her brow and trying to contain the grin that played at the corners of her lips. "That would be wrong...using those poor college boys like th---let's do it!" Ivy made a gesture to Amy, a 'hold on' sort of thing - as she peeled her shirt off to reveal a black tank top, the new "Team Extreme" logo adorned her left bicep. Another look toward the two was all it took - they headed over. "Hi," said the one, leaning over Ivy, "You ladies come here often?" "Often enough," replied Ivy with a smirk that only Amy caught, "Usually on business. You?" "Ah, taking a break from the grind of college for a bit," replied the other, "M'name's Aubrey, that's Marty. Buy you ladies another drink?" Amy's coat had gone among the missing somewhere in the trip up to her room to get more money for this bar, so she and Ivy were almost mirror images by then - the full five or six iches in height Amy had over the older girl lost entirely in their seated positions. She watched the two skeptically, and raised a disturbed eyebrow when the followed the path of their eyes. Nonetheless, neither party aimed to disappoint...and the 'ladies' were damn thirsty by then, it'd been an entire two or three minutes since their glasses last emptied. So the redhead mumbled with a coy smirk, trying too look more interested than repulsed. "Another six, maybe?" "Six?" asked Aubrey. He looked imploringly at Marty, who shot him an intense look. Aubrey responded by motioning to the waitress for six more drinks. The two men then sat down, Marty next to Ivy, Aubrey next to Aimz. He leaned in, Amy instinctively leaned back, but not far enough to make him aware of why. "So what do you ladies do?" asked Marty, putting his arm around Ivy's shoulders. Amy laughed under her breath as Ivy tensed, her arms solid as rocks. "Uhhh...weeeeeeeeeeell.........." Amy started, exchanging a knowing glance with Ivy. "We're...in the 'entertainment' business......" "Both of you?!" Marty's eyes went wide as a kid in a ca--well, in his mind, he essentially WAS a kid in a candy store. Of course you're average nineteen or twenty year old frat boy wasn't about to assume the 'entertainment business' meant professional wrestling or fighting of any sort. "Yeahp," said Ivy, "We're entertainers." The drinks came, and Ivy clinked glasses with Marty, downing the entire thing in one shot. Marty, his manhood threatened, did the same. Only, he nearly coughed it up. The other three laughed, but Aubrey couldn't keep it down, either. "Where'd you boys learn to drink?" asked Ivy, laughing, "College, right?" Aubrey and Marty high- fived each other. "TEAK fa' life, bro!" "Frat boys," replied Ivy, "Do you know where you are? You gotta drink down here." The boys looked at each other and immediately did another of the six shots lined up on the table. They grimaced but managed to keep these down. Genius IQ or not...I didn't go NEAR a college and I can drink ten times what you guys can," Amy cracked Aubrey in the back, causing another near cough-up. "C'mon! More shots!" Another round came. The waitresses here knew who Aimz and Ivy were thanks to the multitude of fWo personalities out and about, so they weren't about to stop serving either - especially when the company they worked for was all but paying to keep the hotel from crumbling with a weekend-long stay alone. This time the number of shot glasses double, to which both girls smiled, especially when the stout Mexican lady said "On de house." in her best attempt at English, giving a knowing wink to the pair and a stifled laugh towards their 'companions'. The guys looked about ready to call it a night early - sure, they looked good...but were these girls REALLY worth the alcohol poisoning? Ivy, however, saw them pondering excuses a mile away and instantly cut in after a nudge at Amy had the ladies downing two more shots each, "Your turn, fellas." The boys look at each other and shrug, each downing their shot. Aubrey immediately began to cough, as if he was on the verge of being sick. Still, he held it down as he moved his chair a bit closer to Amy. "So, what're you two ladies doing in the bar alone tonight? Sent the boyfriends packing?" Drunk as she was, Amy still had half a brain cell left functional and she'd had just about enough of this creep inching closer and closer. She got about a split second into a statement that should have sent these guys packing: "Actually, I'm da---" Ivy's foot promptly connected with her shin. "We're not married, are we?" Ivy held out her hands to show off three rings - none of them wedding bands. Amy, too. "We're hangin' in Mexico here for the whatchamacallits.... Acrobats?" "Luchadores." She mumbled, acting unenthused. Watching lucha libre was what got her INTO the business in the first place, but nobody could be perky with a thorn in the form of a piggish frat boy stuck firmly to their side. "Yeah! The pro wrestlers...we've never seen a real wrestling show, and in America they're so few and far between we figured we'd check them out here, since we both wanted to see Mexico anyway." Well, obviously someone wanted a few more drinks and wasn't willing to let Amy go quite yet - no matter how homicidal the redhead was beginning to look. "Hehe," said Marty, "So you girls are into the athletic type?" "Of course," replied Ivy, winking, "You boys athletic?" "Are you kiddin'?" Marty got up and flexed. And while he had a somewhat impressive flex, the vacant stare on his face would've killed his chances with Amy and Ivy, even if Triple X and Brand Frontier didn't exist. "Nice!" exclaimed Ivy, her sarcasm obvious to Amy but not to these two boys who can't hold their booze. "You ladies stayin' in this hotel?" asked Marty, downing another shot. "Yup," replied Ivy, taking another drink as well. "Whaddaya say we move this party elsewhere?" continued Marty, "Like, maybe.... our room?" "Sure," replied Ivy. She held up her hand when Amy shot her a look of protest. "We'll take this upstairs, but first you need to do something for me." Marty and Aubrey looked at each other with the glance of triumph on their faces. "What's that, babe?" "Arm wrestling," replied Ivy, "You win, I'm yours." As laughable as these 'men' had been thus far, Amy had to interject for a moment with a pleading look and a suggestive grumble of "Ivy...." "Ivy?" asked Marty, looking at both women, "That's an unusual name." Ivy smirked, cracking her knuckles. "Wait a second," continued Aubrey, "Holy shit, dude! You know who them girls are?" It only took a second, but a vague look of recollection crossed Marty's face as he saw who he and his frat brother were sitting with. The Psycho Bitch herself... and the Red Raver Amy Campbell. Immediately outclassed, Marty had no choice but to revert to the more primitive aspects of the male psyche. He gripped Ivy's hand for her challenge. "One," said Aubrey, slurring his numbers, "Two.... Three-GO!" No contest. Ivy slammed his hand down to the table. Amy'd nearly doubled over in laughter when Aubrey'd finally realized exactly who the girls were and why they were that lean. Her 'buzz' was starting to dim slightly, however...nothing a few more shots couldn't remedy as she watched Ivy, again and again, embarass Marty - who couldn't stand the thought of being less than a 'little woman'. She'd turned to Aubrey, whose cheeks' shade of red put his own brand of embarassment for his friend during his SEVERAL moments of defeat on parade, and snickered "Hey, don't worry...I'm sure he's just tipsy." Ivy stood up, downed the last shot on the table, and slammed the glass facedown. "It's been fun, boys... but I think we have somewhere far more interesting to be." "Wait!" said Marty, standing up face to face with her. Face to face was actually a falsity, as she was a good seven inches shorter. "That was royally fucked up, you dumb blonde bitch." Instead of the anger Amy expected, Ivy's face showed nothing but curiosity. "You think it's funny to mess with some guys just hangin' out south 'a the border for a few days? Gonna put it on your little website now?" continued Marty, "You don't fuckin' do that shit and just walk the fuck out without gettin' something to show for it--" "Or what?" asked Ivy, flippantly, "So it's okay for you and your buddy to try and get two poor, defenseless girls drunk so you can feel like a man, but when those two girls turn it around it's mean and cruel?" She picked up her shirt and pulled it on, leaving it unbuttoned. "Shall we, Amy?" They turned around, as Marty and Aubrey looked at each other angrily. "I'm not done with you!" Marty grabbed Ivy by the arm and violently jerked her around, but she simply landed a solid fist into his chest. Marty sunk to the floor of the bar, gasping for air, as Amy looked at Aubrey, waiting expectantly, almost hopefully - for him to try something, too. He didn't move, just exchanged his glances between his fallen friend and the two girls in front of him. Ivy'd turned around to make sure Marty heard her laughter while Amy got sick of waiting. She smiled brightly, eyebrows raised and made some sort of gesture with her hand, laughing as she begged, "Oh come on...I've been the quiet one all night, give me something to walk away with." "C'mon man, how about givin' 'er a black e---OOMPH!" Marty had shouted to his buddy, but Ivy's bright red Doc Marten connected with his chest swiftly interrupted him. ...And Amy stopped waiting. Aubrey stood there, dumbfounded as towhether or not he should've put up a fight and 'save' his friend or be seen running away from two seemingly small and unopposing girls. Really, neither would have saved HIM from having to contend with the two...so as he took far too long to think it through, the little redhead dove forward and swung, tired of staring into a gaping mouth. When he stumbled, he fell back...back....back and into a table that he used to steady himself, both girls laughed and turned to one another... "Well, Amy my dear," said Ivy, steadying herself on the table, "Have we inflicted enough damage on the simple minded of the world tonight?" "Weeeeeeeeeeellllllll," said Amy, dragging it out, "....on the one hand, there's two less guys out there who think they can get laid by buying a girl a few drinks. But on the other... the gay population has gone up really damn far lately and I can't help feeling guilty about it." "It ain't our problem," replied Ivy, "I've got a cute and cuddly Sean asleep in my room... and I think he needs a squeeze. The Bitch is tired, Amy... Ivy wants to come out and play. G'nite, girl." "I think the hotel's complimentary tequila stash is getting emptied...then I'm off to bed. I'll find Brandon in the morning in case he thinks I'm dead. 'Night." And so it goes...
