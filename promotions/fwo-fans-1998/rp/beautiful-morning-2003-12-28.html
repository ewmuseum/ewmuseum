Beautiful Morning

28 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Awakening....she awakened, at once, sweat rolling gently down her cheek. She didn't know why she was awake- by all of her best guesses, she should have stayed asleep for a much longer period of time. Her body ached for the one she loved, despite her mind pleading with it that he was long gone. He was gone, engulfed into a sea of red flames, a sea of burning heat and pure, unfathomable pain. It was only within her own heart that he continued on to exist, but even that grew weak each time she fell asleep. She removed her mask, placing it down on the table next to her bed, and looked down at herself. She had experienced love and hate, all at once, a swirling mass of emotion running through her mind....and none of it made her any less tired. Tired. That's all she ever really felt, no matter what else was going on in the background. Without him, her body would shake, and cry out- she just wanted to let it rest, let it forget the sensations they created together. But it couldn't....it could never forget, no matter how long she slept. Just as her mind could never forget the harsh reality her brother slammed into her skull the next day. Perhaps what she really wished for was for him to come back and suspend reality far above her head, where it could no longer effect her, once again. But her mind knew that he wouldn't be coming. And so, it drifted back off to sleep. She'd awake again shortly, however. It'd be a beautiful morning. The morning she knew she could never sleep again.
