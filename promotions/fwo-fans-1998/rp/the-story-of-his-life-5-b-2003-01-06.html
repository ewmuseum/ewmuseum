The Story Of His Life.5.B

6 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Eli Flair walked up the stairs slowly, still huffing and puffing a bit. He was in good shape, but he was hardly a twenty-two-year-old ball of energy anymore. Chasing some crazy fan down 15 flights of stairs, and choosing to walk back up them wasn't too smart either. Eli sighed, he had to tell some of the other fWo people on the floor to watch out for crazed fans. He knocked on the door to room 27, and stood outside as he could hear his friend Alex Newsome mumbling as he tried to stumble his way to the door. Finaly the locks clicked and Alex opened the door, looking a bit surprised to see Eli standing in front of him. "Hey E," Lance greeting his friend, the sleep still heavy in his expression and on his face, "you look like you just got done running the New York Marathon." "Close, kiddo... try chasing some crazy psycho stalker down fifteen flights of stairs and then being too stubborn to take the elevator back up." replied Eli, sarcastically. "Wha...crazy?" Alex mumbled, still looking a bit confused. The combination of being awakened from a sound sleep and forcing his weary brain to puzzle out why Eli had chased someone down the hallway at 1 a.m. before one of the biggest shows of the year. "Some old freak fan booked outta Brandon Thompson's room after breaking a bunch'a shit and waking me and Angel up. I took a look - dude, the place is trashed. There's shit broken all over, crazy stuff like "Die" and "We hate you" and all, covering the place." Eli said, "Angel was pretty shook up but I wanted to let everyone know to keep an eye out... and to fuckin' watch each other's back, y'know?" Lance just shook his head, still not completely aware of what he had just been told. However, when a 300-pound man in cutoff sweats comes knocking at your door, you're a little more prone to listen and believe what he's telling you. "I'll call around, Eli. Go make sure Angel's okay." Alex said as Eli nodded his approval. "Will do," replied Eli, suddenly distracted. "Who's playing the music so loud?" He thumbed toward the door across the hall. "It's Amy - you mean the music wasn't a dead giveaway?" Alex said, shaking his head. At least the walls were thick enough that he could sleep through whatever it was that she was listening to. Eli looked up and sighed. "Night Eli," Alex finished his statement as Eli pointed at him - the Eli Flair 'hello and goodbye' - and took a few steps down the hall way and reached out to knock on the door. "I like that music," Eli mumbled as he tapped the door a few times. He waited a few moments before Amy came to the door and swung it open. Eli looked down at Amy who was wearing a pair of black cargo pants and a tight fitting black t-shirt. Her hair hung down onto her shoulders, Amy looked up at Eli. "Yes?" "Just a heads- up, Aimz," said Eli, "I chased some whackjob outta the hotel - he'd broken into Brandon's room next to mine and trashed the place. Chased his ass down the stairs and out the door, but I lost 'im in the crowd. Keep an eye when you're out and about, aight?" Eli said looking down at Amy, his face was still a bit red from running all that way. Amy shook her head letting a quick sigh out. "Wackos writing death curses on walls, eh Sounds like a good time........wait, which Brandon? Not BL, is it? I'd ki---" Amy clenched both fists as her face reddened a bit before she was interrupted. "No, I don't think Roche is staying here tonight," replied Eli, a small smile on his face, "Brandon Thompson." "Ohhhh! Well jeez, almost had me running off to murder........but yeah, does buddy need a room to hold up in? I always lock my door...and there's usually a knife or two handy, you have no idea how bitter and maniacal ex-boyfriends can get...so I'm always prepared." Amy said as the two shared a brief chuckle before Eli began to speak again. "Actually, do y'mind? I doubt he'd really want to spend the night in that room, and since you're still awake..." His voice trailed off as he looked down the hallway towards the room the police were completely ransacking. "Can't exactly say I'm a calming presence, or incredibly easy to get along with...but I'll try, I know how creepy that would be..." Amy said as she shut her door, she plopped down onto the floor leaning against the door. "Thanks, Aimzy - need to get back to my room tho... Angel is either worried sick or sound asleep and I'd rather not miss either of 'em." Eli said as he paced off down the hallway. Amy just chuckled as she put her hands behind her head. "With my luck, he comes completelyplastered with a harem behind 'im..." Amy mumbled as she receeded back into the blaring wall of sound.
