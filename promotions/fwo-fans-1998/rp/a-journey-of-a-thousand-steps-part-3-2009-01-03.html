A journey of a thousand steps, Part 3

3 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~Second Interlude~~~ October 19, 2000 The Underground has returned to the States in preparation for the upcoming Sunday Slaughter on the 22nd. Mike has just gotten off the phone with his future wife Brittany, then his girlfriend, confirming she would be his valet in the FWO for the first time on Sunday. Mere moments later, we had the first sign that something else may be wrong with Mike Bear: Fear: Why are you making her your valet all of a sudden? Mike: Lamont told me it was a good idea! I told you this already. Fear: Oh yeah, right, Lamont. (rolls eyes) Lamont: Hey you big dumb oaf, I can see you rolling your eyes! How would you like these claws in your... Fear: Ok genius, what's he saying now? Mike doesn't answer. Instead, he just looks off into space, rubbing his head. Fear: Mike? Mike?! No response. Fear walks over to Mike and waves a hand in front of his eyes. Fear: MIKE! Mike doesn't even acknowledge him. Finally, Fear grabs the hand that Mike was rubbing his head with. Mike: What? How'd you get over here so fast? Fear: What are you talking about? Mike: I swear less than a second ago you were on the bed watching TV. Fear: Maybe you should go see a doctor. I walked up and waved my arms in front of you and you acted like I wasn't even here. Mike: Hmmm, weird, guess I just spaced out there. ~~~End Second Interlude~~~
