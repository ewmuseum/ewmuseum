A good deed

6 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
December 4th, 2002 10:17 PM The McNichols Sports Arena in Denver was nearly emptied out as Horito stepped out from the locker room. The other occupants of the room were OCW and SCPW graduates who were participating in dark matches on the Warfare cards, and soon the Slaughter cards. "Everyone has to start somewhere on the card, so I guess its for the best." Horito mumbled, as he slung his duffel bag over his right shoulder, but felt a slight twinge of pain, probably from Shadows' somewhat sloppy Firemans Carry DDT earlier in the evening. "Hey, Horito," trailed off the voice behind Horito, and he turned around, staring at the figure of Grant Mitchel, known in the Wrestling industry as 'Silver Fox'. His graying hair, and slightly limping figure was indicative of years of abuse to his own body from dangerous spots in the squared circle. "We need you to come to the conference room in five for a booking meeting." said Grant, as Horito nodded, and began heading onwards, while Grant walked in the other direction. As he walked through the corridors of the arena, Horito spotted a young toddler walking around by himself, looking confused. "Hello there..." trailed off Horito, as the young child turned around, and stared at Horito. The child couldn't be more than five or six, and stared at Horito with an expression on confusion. "Are your parents here?" asked Horito, as the child merely nodded his head, and Horito asked one final question: "Where?" The child extended his index finger, pointing towards a young looking couple, who looked no older than their mid-twenties. Horito motioned for the child to follow him, and the child, not having learned yet the fact not to follow strangers, complied very quickly. Horito walked over to the couple, who were looking at some banners. "Excuse me, Madam and Sir..." trailed off Horito, as the couple turned around, and stared at the Japanese man with his crew cut style hair, and small goatee. "I believe...he is yours..." trailed off Horito once again, this time motioning to the young child. The child hobbled over to the parents, who looked at each other in shock. "I thought YOU were looking after James?" asked the man to the woman. The woman sneered at the man for a second, before she replied: "I thought YOU were!" While the couple argued, Horito sighed and walked away. He had attempted to stop the child from dissappearing completely, but with the way the 'responsible' parents were acting, he doubted the child's safety would be more guarenteed from now on. "Well, I guess its off to the booking meeting..." trailed off Horito, as he walked down the corridor, and towards the conference room.
