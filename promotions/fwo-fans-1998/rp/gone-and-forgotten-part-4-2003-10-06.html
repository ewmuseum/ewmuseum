Gone and Forgotten, Part 4

6 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wednesday, October 1st, 5:43 p.m. EST "So they let you back just like that?" Reika questioned him, eyebrows raised. Matthew smirked as he leaned back in his airplane chair, on the way to his east coast home from California. He had a return to prepare for, after all. Reika, despite her earlier objections to his decision, was accompanying him. "Not really." Xias responded, looking up at Reika's inquisitive look. "The old bastard has had it out for me since Day 1, after all. And I suppose cursing at him on my way out probably didn't help the situation much." "So why exactly are they letting you back again?" "I calmly pointed out that I was still under contract anyway and then showed them the ratings numbers for my match against Eron last month." Xias said, smirking again. "I also calmly pointed out the mass exodus of brand-name talent- Eli Flair's gone, KVC and Brand show up whenever the fuck they feel like it, Spyder's schedule is weirder than mine, Aimz is probably off giving BJs at some Canadian whore house, and Donovan Winters is out smoking crack with Kinkade's sister somewhere." "You sure are pretty funny, Matthew." Reika laughed. "Why's that?" he asked her, staring up at her with a puzzled look. "You're like two different people." she pointed out. "You're a sensitive soul when it comes to anything else but what happens in the ring, but when it comes to this wrestling garbage, you instantly turn into an arrogant jerk." "Do I?" he asked her with another grin. "You really should see a psychiatrist sometime, Matthew." Reika said with a playful grin. "It would probably do you some good." Matthew rolled his eyes. "Just give me a little time, Reika." Xias said, leaning back in his chair again. "I'll find Eron, I'll beat him, and I'll be back with you. We'll get the hell away from this crazy business that makes me equally crazy." "I certainly hope so." Reika said, grabbing hold of Xias' left hand. "Just don't forget about me while you're in that ring, okay?" He blushed. "Reika, I couldn't forget you even if I wanted to." They kissed, and all was well. At least for the remainder of that flight.
