Wake Up And Smell The Maple Nut Crunch

17 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
January 10, 2005: Post-Ruahh~! Craig Miles peered over the documents spread out before him on a table in the smoking lounge of the Minneapolis/St. Paul International Airport. The din of the lounge interrupted his thought process at intervals, and the smoke wafting from his Newport helped cloud the lies that had been hidden from himself and from the person sitting across from him for quite some time. Lindsay Troy looked out into the airport terminal, and at times sneaked sideways glances at Miles out of the corner of her eye. The bruises from New Frontier's Crashmas card had faded, only to be replaced by fresh ones at the hands of Brand Frontier at Countdown One and Token Weed and Vic Creed at Ruahh~! The ride to the airport was a mix of silence, cell phone ringtones, and conversations to other people. In fact, the two hadn't said much of anything to one another until a string of calls with the same ringtone came through to Lindz's cell phone which she ignored with a simple touch of a button. "Melton?" Miles ventured a guess with a smirk. Troy's lip had twitched and, after a moment's pause, replied, "He usually gives up around this time." When her phone beeped for messages, she slipped it back inside her coat pocket and turned the stereo up louder. Upon arriving to the airport, Miles dropped the rental off and the two proceeded through security to find that their planes had been delayed due to the snow and mechanical problems. A back table in a dimly lit lounge and bar area near their departure gates provided a temporary meeting space for treacherous business to be discussed. Troy had bought the drinks--in her mind, it was the least she could do. "Before you open that envelope, Craig," she had said right after they had gotten settled, "I want you to know that no matter what you think of me, this is not somthing that was ever authorized by anyone in my administration, nor is it anything that I personally condone or otherwise approve of." "Did you memorize that off a cue card by some chance?" The Cocky one smirked and ripped the top of the envelope open. "Well, I could have just told you that I think the whole situation is bullshit, but I figured you would have gotten that impression already." Miles nodded, pulled the envelope's contents out, and spread them in front of him. "Well, shit does stink." So there they sat, two hours after leaving the Target Center, with Jack Daniels, Sambuca, and a heavy revelation between them. Miles looked over everything and the recurring feeling from reading Lindsay's documents finally broke through in a snicker. "What?" Troy asked, almost in a hopeful way that something would be going right for her. Unfortunately as many people learn too late, Craig's laughter isn't always in joy or happiness. In fact, 6 times out of 9 its for reasons nobody knows. Craig sensed Troy's hope and maybe because she'd been through enough smokescreens the last few weeks and hadn't suddenly broke her fist on his face, he'd try this gently...well, as gently as Craig Miles could be. "Lindsay, you're going to have to learn that choices, options, decisions - you're sometimes destined to fail with what you're hoping for, rather than succeeding on what you know best." Troy blinked, and well, she looked back confused. "Alright," Miles pulled out a Newport and lit it up, while folding the papers and turning them over to make a point he didn't need to see them. "Let's try and point things out to prove this...I hoped my war was against Disney, now not only do I know its not only not them..." Troy instinctively looked to Craig's bruises from Crashmas. "But here, it was never them nor was it ever Eddie." Troy didn't know what to make of that and didn't have much time as Craig kept talking as if he were in some trance... "If that's not clear enough, we'll get personal." Troy rolled her eyes, immediately. "I know exactly where this is going..." "You found out you shouldn't be hoping for Joey Melton to be anything more than a selfish, horny, middle-aged man that looked at you just like he looked at the Ultratitle Trophy. Work hard enough, play hard enough, try hard enough - get your reward, adulation and self-respect." "I didn't need Melton to get my reward, adulation, or self-respect. I had all that before I ever inked my name to your fed." Miles exhaled a smoke ring, not even paying attention to a retort he already expected. "Are you hoping that I can help you in some way? I can't...because in the end, while you've given me some great information on just where the bounty money came from...you've only proven that your hopes on myself being wrong about these," Miles quoted the air, "businessmen were wrong. I was right months ago, great - why go back to the past struggles that have already been fought and won for no reason? They're not the enemy in this...but they are part of the machine... which gives you another choice...an option..." Miles exhaled a waft of smoke, "You can go on national television and disclose all this information as something you only KNOW as fact. Or you can sit on it, wait for something and HOPE - it all goes away from the inside." "That's all you can do, or ever will be able to do...make a choice," Miles ashed out his cigarette as he pushed the turned over documents back towards Troy. "I don't need to see these Lindz, I won't need to see them unless someone gave Murphy those orders and you can prove it. Otherwise, I learned a long time ago you can either grow from the game, or get played by it..." Miles looked in Troy's direction, "I think you need to just grow up a little." "Excuse me?" Troy responded under insult. "Between Melton and this, you've got two strikes in my world. How are you batting in yours? If you came to me for loving support, then Ivy never told you enough about me. You're here with a problem, Troy...obviously, I must be part of the solution." Troy sat in quiet contemplation for a moment, then picked up her glass to down the rest of her drink. She turned the glass over on the table and looked directly into Miles's eyes. "Do you know why I showed you those documents, Craig? It wasn't because I want your sympathy and it wasn't because I wanted to make things square between us. No. I showed you what I did because now I have an obligation, just like you. Someone ordered the transfers from the Radicomm promotional funds to this Bounty Account, and yeah...I've got a pretty damn good idea who it is, but like you said I can't prove it. Yet. But in the meantime, that means that I have to make sure that your career doesn't get snuffed out. If it does, regardless of this deal going down without Radicomm ever authorizing it or even knowing, my family is liable. I can't have that. You of all people should know the lengths someone will go to to make sure their family is out of harm's way, safe and sound." There was a flash of something behind Miles's steel blues, but Troy needed to get everything out before Craig said another word. "Whether you like it or not, Boss...I'm on your side now, at least in theory and principle. I've played enough hardball in my life to know that right now, I've got two strikes on me and somehow I keep foul-tipping to stay alive." She stood up and threw some bills on the table. "Either you and I can reach some sort of understanding, or we can keep the verbal war going. But the way I see it," she extended her hand, "the ball's in your court." "That's what I'm trying to tell you, Lindz. It's not. I'm not trying to hurt you, I'm trying to help you understand truth and perception." Miles stood up and extended his hand, "What you've told me tonight, I appreciate it Lindsay. But look deep down and ask yourself two questions right now, Can Craig Miles really do anything about this to help you? And secondly and most importantly," Miles smirked, "Should I want Craig Miles helping me with this?" Troy moved her hand and clasped Miles'. "When it comes to giving the foundation of the Mouse what it deserves, I really can't think of anyone better."
