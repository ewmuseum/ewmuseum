Drug Diaries; Three.

11 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Drug Diaries Selected entries dated between 2001 and 2004.. February 13th 2004 What a strange trip it's been... So I've started coming up and I'm talking to a neuro Doc' on the phone and just having a blast, music, talking, laughing, oscillations, shadow shifting. All in all, a good trip so far. I dosed only two hours ago and am starting to peak so the letters on this page aren't doing nothing but spinning... – Its 9:45am - about 13 hours after the drop and I'm down. Down hard. As to the quantity ingested, well let's put it this way - if 6 hits of white blotter was my first trip, and my supplier was, say in just any case, my father, whom has passed away but left all the goods that I need for a wonderful ride - why not experience something similar with 6 hits of a known dosage? So after a good chat on the phone with several people, I got off of it in a semi giddy state - laughing, happy, tripping balls, colours changing, letters spinning, shadows shifting, pictures changing - I invited over two good friends. Noëla and Gabe, and had a blast... I don't know what exactly made me dose last night, or dose them once I was tripping so hard. I gave Gabe too much as he's only tripped two times and I think I gave him approximately 500 µg., while Noëla is an experienced tripper and dosed on about 800 µg. Why take it lightly when you can blast off? Anyway, after about 10:30pm I lost my phone in the bathroom - or rather I left it there - so anyone who tried to contact me to see what was up got no trip report until this morning when I called them back... -- The walk, what the fuck was I thinking? I knew I was in for a strange trip, I dosed myself early, was tripping balls and had the bright idea to walk into the woods without a light source other then a lighter and a home-made torch that was damp from winter. We set out on a trek around the 52 acre property that my home sits on here in Louisiana, which is made up of woods, fields and backs up to a state park. It was around 12:20 when we reached the stream, at a part that I would never under any circumstances try and cross if I were sober (a fallen tree raised embankment of about 12 ft or so on the far side and the only way across is to cross the tree). While I am fairly nimble, Gabe - on the other hand - was not. He ended up in about 2 ft of water face-down, laughing which was about the most fucked up part of the night. Noëla refused to walk across so I carried her in my tripped out fashion, laughing and kissing her cheek, acting like I was going to fall. Really fucking with her, until she bit my nipple - which was just outright wonderful. Anyway, we get across after what seems to be hours, but it might have been fifteen mins. We're sitting on a big rock at the other side, watching the water come around the bend. Then to my surprise, Gabe's like “Oh yeah we have light!”, I look over and he's GREEN! Bright fluorescent green! But, its just a glow stick. I'm like “Dude you held out, man! We could've used that when we were in the woods to get here! Now were in the field and we have the moon! What are you playing with that for?” All I see is tracers from his hands moving with the green light, and I hear some garbled audio from his mouth that sounds foreign but I don't know, I'm not sure what he said as everyone just creased up laughing again anyway. Oh well, things happen, and some glass is produced, I'm like “Oh yes! Oh yes, its time to smoke a few!” Which I would, normally, be like 'okay cool' , as I smoke 3 times a day anyway. But everything is more exciting when you're tripping, don't ask me why. Gabe pulls out a bag, and of course its soaked. I laughed my ass off, and Noëla hit him. Hard. He laughed, 'Oh well I've got my travel jar in my pocket' I think to myself, but I just don't let on yet. I let Noëla yell relentlessly at him for soaking her weed. “Hold your own shit if you don't want it fucked up” I say clearly and then she turned on me with a vicious smile, and bit me again. This girl has a thing for biting me, I don't know if I taste good, or if its the sexual tension. She's always biting me. I giggle, and I'm like “Enough, enough! Save it for later, I've got something better - you know, a treat.” she smiles again. Gabe is yelling like its fucking New Year's, something like “Yes! This shits going to rock! You always hold out!” and I clearly state its because he can't smoke the shit I have. He always gets stupid on me! But since were already stupid, why not? So I pack the bubbler - which has no water in it which makes things go in reverse. I'm like “Hold up.” Then I unpack the bowl, dip the bubbler in the stream and gather some water into the bubbler. Luckily, its a cold spring and clean water, the same water we drink on a daily basis from our tap. So I wipe the bubbler dry so no one drops it, repack the bowl with half the weed that was in it (since Noëla dropped some), and added more to the top from what was still in my jar. Then I get the question. “What are we smoking?”, from Gabe. I say I forget, because honestly at this point I can't read what's written on my jar. I hand it to Noëla with a ladies first and she goes to puffing, and then coughing she says “It tastes like bubble gum and berries – what the fuck, Jade?!” I'm like “OH GOOD!” Its a little 'bogglegum shiskaberry' cross, very nice. Just to make sure, I hand Gabe the jar and he verifies what I said. He hits next and a big grin comes across his face, while holding in his hit. I'm next. I hit the bubbler hard filling my lungs with a smooth, wonderful aroma, “Ahhh!” then I hand it to Noëla without paying attention. Its cashed, I get bit again. I'm like “Okay, okay! Sorry, it happens!” I'm so spun. The green light makes everything green. Now, that makes sense, but in a fucked up kinda way, because the weeds not green really. Its greenish grayish purple. Anyway I pack another into the bubbler, and hand it back to her. When it comes around again to me, I'm good, and pass it without a hit, laughing. Then, we just get to bullshitting and laughing at each other, saying dumb shit like, “Gabe's nose is a penis”, which if you could see the tip of the dude's nose you'd understand why. Noëla brought it up, but that's another story. Gabe gets quiet and is entertaining himself with the glow stick. Noëla and I are going on about music, art, life. That kind of thing. Inebriated philosophical debates. Then, she leans up and kisses me. Gabe hollars. I'm thinking, like, 'Oh God, what the fuck is going to happen next? This girl's boyfriend is my tattoo artist, he's locked up for a warrant, and I know nothing can happen or at least it shouldn't'. Well, we break free. There's a little giggle from Noëla, and Gabe just blurts out, “Dude, you know she left Mike right?” Gabe gets hit. “Damn it, Gabe. I told you not to tell him! They have been friends for ten years, stop damn it.” I laugh and say “Yeah, right. They were together for 6 years. How, why'd that happen?”, then the next thing I hear, I can't wrap my head around at first, because things aren't clicking all the time. I think you all know what I'm talking about. But Gabe's mouth just wont stop, even with Noëla's hand across it. I hear a muffled “She needs to be fucked, and she wants you!” I laugh knowing this has gotta be a joke, because Noëla's living with Gabe and she turned down an offer to live here. Yes, sexual tension between her and I is high at all times. She's very good looking, and evidently she thinks I am, too. So whatever. Anyway, I keep laughing and I look at Noëla. She's like almost crying, which I know means one of two things, either Gabe has gotten too high, is blurting out things that I shouldn't hear or know, or she's starting to freak out. I tell Gabe to shut the fuck up, sit still and we will be right back. Figuring, if she's upset because he blurted something out I can calm her down, if she's having a bad trip because of Gabe's stupidity, then removing him from the situation for a few moments should chill things out I can talk her down. Maybe get her to start laughing and I can smoke a cigarette, because I really need one and Gabe doesn't like people smoking 'cancer' around him. So we walk from Big Rock through the field over to a tractor, and I reach in to start it up. That was a big mistake - the loud rumbling noise really freaks me out - but I manage to raise the bucket about half way to make a bench to sit in/on, and take Noëla's hand. We sit down to have a talk and a smoke, and by now her eyes look better. She's got a smile on her face again, everything seems to be cool. So we're talking about Pigpen, singer for the Grateful Dead, and his relations with Janis Joplin. Whatever led to this conversation I haven't the slightest clue, except that I talked to the neuro Doc' earlier about it, so that could have been a possibility. Or it could have been the MP3 player Noëla produced, who knows. Anyway, tunes, a cigarette, some talking, and then its quiet. We're just laying on each other, watching the stars in the bucket of this old Ford tractor. Then Gabe comes up and is like “Are you all cool? I heard something crazy about twenty minutes ago. It was short but I didn't know if something got you!” I was like “Dude, it was the tractor, chill.” I also think he got lonely but who knows. At this point, Noëla sits up, and asks Gabe to walk back to my house by himself, since he has the glow stick still - which God knows is fucking with me as its swinging around his neck. He's like, “Sure, what do you need?” “I need you to get my bag from the car, and I need the blanket.” To me it looks like we're gonna be out here for a while. Everyone might get cold, I mention. She's like “Yeah, its getting late I think, and chilly, I think.” Nothing is as it seems when you're tripping. I told Gabe that if he went to Big Rock he'd see three paths, to take the one in front of him and he could cross the stream without a problem “It's only a couple inches wide and deep there.” He says okay and walks off, now Noëla and I are alone again. Things are quiet, minus the sound of small critters running around which is normal on a small farm, right? We're just watching things happen, it's like a dream land. Everything is wonderful, and then all of a sudden I hear Noëla's voice, but I don't know if she's really talking or if I am hearing things, but then I hear it, “Jade, Jade, did you hear me?” and I come out of my never land state. “Uhhh, no. Not really I was watching the grass,” I save. She's like “Oh okay, well Gabe's right. Its true.” I'm slow this evening, and I have to ask what she's talking about I like couldn't comprehend what was happening. So after talking for God knows how long, we're kissing again, which I know isn't right for our current mental states, and I just can't seem to stop her. I break away, she comes back, again, again. Finally Gabe comes back and with everything she asked for, plus he's gone into my house and found a battery powered CD player, batteries and all the CDs he could carry. She kinda breaks off. I climb down from the bucket of the tractor, hand Noëla her bag, get the blanket and spread it out. Its huge, and soft enough. There is room for the three of us to lay down on, so I help Noëla's 5'1" self down from the bucket of the tractor. Gabe fumbles with the CD player and the CD's until something happens. Music. Not the best music for tripping, he has put in some depressing rock that totally makes me think too hard. I request a change. So, the CD is changed to a Hendrix, Joplin, Cream etc. mix that I had made years ago. We're all laying on our backs again, looking at the stars and talking. I ask for some papers, “Anyone have papers? Anyone? Papers”, Noëla digs through her bag, no luck, and Gabe's been soaked so his are out. I finally dig through my own pockets, my wallet and my bag, and find some. I'm like “Great! Two papers! What the fuck good is this? That's only two small or one large joint!” So I'm like fuck it, I ask for the bubbler. I pack it, hand it to Gabe and say “Look, dude, this is all yours. We're gonna smoke a joint, is that cool?” He's like “Yeah, that's fine. I kinda hate papers anyway, makes herb taste like a cigarette and you know I hate CANCER!” we laugh. I roll a nice, fat joint with what's left in the jar. Gabe sits up, and starts sparking the bubbler as I fire up this joint. Of course, I am laying down with Noëla, no need to sit up, right? Anyway, I get it lit. Take a couple of puffs, and start to pass it to Noëla, and she just slides over lays her head on my chest, and then accepts the joint. We're all talking, laughing, things are doing what they are doing. There's like minimal mind control at this point in my trip, I don't know about the others. Noëla and I are toasted, tripping. She's biting me, God how this keeps messing with my sexual emotions, but there's still half a joint burning in my hand so I laugh it off. She giggles, I take a couple more puffs. I try to pass it to her, she's good I think, so I offer it to Gabe - who doesn't turn it down - and then another kiss. The smoke being lifted from my mouth to hers, our faces clouded. Her eyes sparkle, things finally click. This night's not going to end in a way that either of us are going to forget. And no matter what I say, I don't think 'No' is going to work. Gabe chokes, laughs, and points at us. We don't know what's going on, so we ask. He says, “We got two trippin' hippies on a blanket, when are they gonna make love?!” We all laugh, god its been a night! The moon's starting to set, but the sun's not starting to come up, so I ask Gabe what time it is, “5:30 sir, 5:30” My God, we've been in the field all night, we're damp, it's cold and we're going to get sick. It is all I can think of, so I'm like, “Okay, dude.” It's quiet for a few moments until Noëla says she's cold. I suggest going back to the house, my sister Mira should be up making coffee, and ready to have her morning toke, before heading off to a double at the diner. We walk back, carrying everything that Gabe had brought outside. I've got the CDs in my bag, and the blanket, he's got the stereo, and the bubbler. Noëla's hands are free so I ask her to light a couple cigarettes, and put one in my mouth for the walk back. “Sure,” she says, and we're walking, smoking, talking. We see light through the edge of the woods, its the house and it seems that Mira's up! Yes! COFFEE! Which is much needed, as we only took a bottle of water each and it wasn't enough. Eventually we make it inside, and Mira messes with us, as she knows we're tripping - and she was going to trip last night too but she had to work today, so she put it off until next Friday or something. Anyway, we're in the kitchen. Coffee, music and herbs around. Mira's talking, Gabe's sitting in the next chair around the table, and Noëla and I are in the window seat. My hands feel warmer, and I'm not holding my coffee. I look down, Noëla's holding my hand. Then Mira starts in with, “Aren't you Mike's ol' lady?” She says, “Oh, that ended two weeks ago, Mira.” Mira laughs, takes a puff of her joint, and a sip of coffee. “Well, its about time for me to shower. All you kids have a good day 'sleeping'.” Okay Mom, we all say mockingly. An hour and a half, or so, passes quietly. Me and Noëla are silently staring into the farm, watching the squirrels run around, the sheep on the horizon, observing, dreamstate. Gabe, I'm sure, is talking to us but neither of us notice him until Mira returns from getting ready. Eventually, I'm like “How about we get some sleep guys? Gabe, don't you have to work tonight?” He laughs, “Yeah, but I'll be alright.” so I pull the sofa out for Gabe and Noëla, since they share a bed in the apartment which they share anyway. I figured it will be all good. Noëla doesn't like that idea, though. She helps me get the sheets, blankets, and pillows for the sofa-bed, and helps me set it up. After this is done, she looks at me, smiles and then looks at Gabe. “Are you going to be alright?” He looks at her like Oh God. I don't realize what's about to happen. I am soon to find out, as Noëla takes my hand and leads me to my room. Shouldn't it be the other way around? Anyway, needless to say, we're in bed by 8:40 and things are on fire. Just over an hour later, we collapse, Noëla falls asleep, and I begin to as well. Then my phone starts going off.
