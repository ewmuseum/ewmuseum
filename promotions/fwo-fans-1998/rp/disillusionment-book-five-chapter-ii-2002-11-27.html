Disillusionment: Book Five - Chapter II

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey! Wake up! You got us caught, you little bitch! See this little ass room we're in, this is a Disciple holding cell. I hold you directly responsible! YOU TOLD SOMEONE! Did I not tell you that you couldn't tell anyone where you were going? The Disciples caught up with you right outside the city limits. Unfortunately, I happened to be with you at the time. You were filling up your motorcycle at some mom and pop gas station. They ambushed you, pistol-whipped the back of your skull, and started to drag you off. What happened next was completely my fault......I tried to save you. The Disciples didn't know that I was inside, or at least, I thought they didn't. I only saw two of them attack you, so I thought I could take them out and get you to safety before either of them could do any damage to me. I ran out of the shop and... They used you to get at me. There were ten more guys right outside the store, who were ducking below the windows so I couldn't see them. As soon as I walked out, all ten of them were all over me. They tackled me and spent at least five minutes kicking the holy hell out of my entire body. See this black eye...I hold you directly responsible for it! Well, it looks like the Disciples won. I've been fighting this fight for ten years. I've been running for ten years. I've been putting my life on the line for ten years. Now it's over, and no change has been made, AND IT'S ALL YOUR FAULT! DAMMIT! Why the fuck couldn't you just listen to me?!? When I told you that you were a wanted man, I told you not to tell anyone, instead, you told the first person the Disciples knew to ask. YOUR FUCKING WIFE! Your fucking wife? I thought that you were smarter than that. I thought that you wouldn't be so stupid as to tell the one person they would know to check. She's dead now. You're wife is dead! There was nothing you could do to save her, but you did cause her death. I've gone weeks at a time eating nothing but bread. I've hidden in caves, sustaining on mushrooms and underground streams. I've slept on the concrete floors of high-rise basements, and now all of...ALL OF IT! was for nothing. They'll kill you first, you know. They know that you don't have as much information about them as I do, and you won't have to face even half the torture that I will endure. AND I WAS SO CLOSE! I was so close to showing the world the lies of the reality that they have been trained to believe. I was so close to winning the tournament and getting an arena in which I could speak my piece about the oppression we live our lives in everyday, and you ruined it. My life is over. My life's work is over, and it's all your fault. It's all your fucking fault. I guess Brandon Louden will move on, thinking I was afraid of him, much like I belive about Arrows. DAMMIT. I wanted to win! It wasn't about the money anymore. It became an issue of pride. But you wouldn't know anything about pride. You let the fucking Disciples kill your wife, you bastard. I hope you rot in hell.
