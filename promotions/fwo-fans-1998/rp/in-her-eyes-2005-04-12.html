In Her Eyes

12 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There wasn't very much for Nick Brandish to pack, he realized as he set his suitcase down on the hospital bed. He opened the small drawer set beside his bed and removed his few articles of clothing. The Thai Monster straightened slowly, feeling the tightness in his lower back blasting bolts of pain down both legs. Oh, Vic had done his job well, Nick thought to himself. Not as well as Creed believed he had, but a decent attempt none the less. "Need a hand there Nick?" a grinning blonde metrosexual said as he stepped into the room. "I'm fine Danny." Nicked looked at the far-too-pretty man and wondered how he'd lasted more than five minutes on the street. Ever since his father had passed away, Brandish had reached out to various criminal elements in North America. Danny was the liaison between himself and the Danelli Family out of Chicago. Mr. Danelli offered Danny up as a show of good faith. Despite all his primping, Nick had to admit that Danny had proven himself competent and reliable. Still, he was just too damn pretty for his own good. In Thailand, Nick would have him working the streets. Danny gave the man in the bed beside Nick a momentary glance. "What's with the mummy?" "Car wreck or something." Nick closed the suitcase. "I think that's everything." "So when do you return to the fWo?" Danny followed the fWo, which Nick found handy when explaining various aspects of his "other" job. "They have me on the disabled list for another couple of weeks. I don't see any point in coming back before Cyberslam, which is why I'm heading home to recuperate." Nick looked up and saw Danny disappearing into the bathroom. Could he not go five minutes without looking at himself in the mirror? A grey-haired Thai man stepped into the room. "Is the car ready, Bakkahisa?" Brandish asked. "It is ready." Both men watched Danny emerge from the bathroom, holding a small golden picture frame. Bakkahisa approached and snatched it out of his hand. "Easy there sport," Danny said as he rubbed his fingers. "I was just checking to make sure Nick didn't forget anything. Good thing I checked." Nick frowned and handed his suitcase to Danny. "Take this to the car." "Sure thing." Danny watched Bakkahisa hand the picture to Brandish. "Cute kid." Had Danny stayed in the room a second longer, he would have never left it alive. Nick's mind was already filled with different ways he could attempt to kill the pretty boy without damaging relations with Chicago. Bakkahisa knew Nick well enough to understand what he was thinking. "It's been too long since you've seen her." Brandish looked at the photo with a longing expression. "Far too long. Look at her eyes. You remember Nida, don't you?" Bakkahisa recalled the woman Nick spoke of. She had been one of his father's prostitutes, Nick's nursemaid, and his lover. She became his one true love, the woman all loves would be measured against. Nida was killed for defying Nick's father, murdered right before the Monster's eyes. Nida was also Nick's mother. But that relationship was never brought up in his presence. At all, if the speaker wanted to stay alive. "I remember her," said Bakkahisa. "A beautiful woman with a good heart." Nick nodded. "I see Nida in her eyes. She's come back to me through her. That's why we must be married. Nida and I were meant to be together, and soon we will be." "It will be a great day." Bakkahisa looked around the room. "Is there anything else?" Brandish leaned over and pressed the Call button beside his bed. "I'll just send for the nurse to bring my wheelchair. You may go to the car and make sure Danny isn't powdering his nose in my presence when I get down there." Bakkahisa bowed. "Of course." Nick sighed audibly as he stared at the tiny picture in his huge hands. "I can't wait to see you again, to smell your hair, feel your soft skin." He pressed the frame to his chest. "The things Nida used to do to me. Ah Sarah, I just hope your body can hold up to everything I want to do to it." He held the picture out and smirked. "But then, you're a Creed. Creed's are a rugged bunch." The nurse arrived with the wheelchair, and Nick slipped the picture into his coat pocket. It took her a little bit of effort to get the huge man moving, but once she started pushing the nurse had little problem guiding the big Thai out the door. The small man, his head wrapped in medical tape, lay silently in his bed after Brandish had gone. He felt an immediate sense of relief now that the Monster had gone, but that relief didn't last long. The sensation was quickly replaced with a feeling of concern and dread that twisted his stomach into knots. "Sarah... Creed?" Crucifix whispered as he stared up at the ceiling.
