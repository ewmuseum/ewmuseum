Watch Greatness.

21 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Geoff Green continues furthur down the hall inside of the luxury hotel, in San Jose, California. While walking down the corridor, he notices an open room. Inside of it, on the bed, is Primetime, wearing a Twilight '99 t-shirt, jeans, and a French Foundation cap. On the TV, Primetime is watching the Havok interview, while rewinding to one part, over and over again.) "Primetime, Courage, and Frenchie... jokes." *rewind* "Primetime, Courage, and Frenchie... jokes." *rewind* "Primetime, Courage, and Frenchie... jokes." (After doing this, he notices Geoff Green, and stops the video tape.) PT: Geoff...Geoff...Geoff. Do you find me a joke? Green: No, sir. PT: Right. Havok, the Millenium Man, well actually the Y2K BUG, because this man BUGS the hell out of me. I'm a joke, look at the stats, Havok, Winner, Meltdown 99. 3 time tag champion, Cruiserweight Champion, WORLD champion. Courage is a former International Champion, and Frenchie's done everything there is to do, save the tag titles. What have you done? The Cruiserweight title? *twirls finger* Whoopie. *That's* it. We've done anything and everything there is to do. You haven't. Now enough about that Millenium Jobber. (PT takes a sip of water from a bottle that was resting on a table, then continues.) PT: This Wednesday, in the mis-match of the year, we have the two people who epitomize greatness, and who epitomize Rock... (PT stops, to emphasize his point.) PT:...and roll, Primetime and Courage. In the other corner you have the people who epitomize.... (PT stops, again.) PT....stupidity, Jake and Austin the Giant. These two people don't deserve to be in the same ring as us. They should do what Havok should do, what the whole fWo should do, and what every fan should do. Sit down. Shut up. Watch greatness. (On that note, we fade to black.)
