Disillusionment: Book Seven - Chapter I

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Disciples still don't know that I'm really alive. The longer they think I'm dead, the longer I get to accomplish the goals that I have set out for myself. I have a friend that is a "hacker." No, he didn't start this fucking Sircam virus ordeal...that was a Disciplic creation, but I'll get into that later. My friend doesn't set out to harm anyone or their property. He knows nearly as much as I do, and in his field, he knows much, much more. My friend doesn't crack into systems and steal private information, at least, not of civilians...remember, we're the good guys. He does his share of hunting whenever I need any dirt on the Disciples, though, and he hit the jackpot yesterday. On Friday, Olaf Heersh is coming to the country, and, thanks to my gifted friend, I know exactly where and when to find him. Completion......my only goal. Hatred...for which my target is to suffer. Fear...for Olaf does not roam unguarded, and I will most likely not make it out alive. Anyway, enough bullshit philosophical contemplation. I promised I'd tell you more about the Sircam Worm. As many of you may, or may not know, it is a very potent virus that can slow down systems by filling hard drives, or, if you're an unlucky one in thirty-six who contract the virus, can delete your entire system. The Disciples created this virus, and it is accomplishing exactly what it sought out to...installing hatred for the Internet in those who's systems contract it. You see, the Disciples are aware of the vast power of the Internet and it's ability to spread knowledge. Anyone with some common designing knowledge and a voice can say absolutely anything they want to...and there are some people like me...just......dumber, who post about the Disciples on the Internet, hoping to spread the word and not realizing that they will be caught...quickly. Sircam was the Disciples' way of......limiting the growth of the word against them. They like to keep the people ignorant, and one thing I've learned is that keeping people angry keeps them ignorant. But my ignorance has been gone for a long, long time. Were it not for the deaths of Burkenstock and Stephanie, I would long to have it back, to return to the was before the tumultuous seas of the chase began to churn my stomach and ruin my mind. Long before the years of running had eaten at my soul and worn away at my being. It is my time... A time to kill or be killed, or maybe both. I move on, as I always have, and I hope to destroy, but is that enough. After destruction, rebuilding is always necessary. Who's to say that if the Disciples are pulled from power that what replaces them won't be even more powerful? What's to say that the horrors wrought from the resource of man are not even more terrifying than those that we have already seen, heard, and read about? There are too many questions...it's all too.........confusing. I've toyed with these thoughts in my mind, and I truthfully do not know what to do. I just have to keep on.
