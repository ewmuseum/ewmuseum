About To Break

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera pans in to Ric Chronos, the frayed condition of his clothing implying that Ric has not changed out of his wrestling gear from the previous week. Sledgehammer in hand, he runs his fingers through a tangled mess of red hair, never looking directly at the camera.] Ric: "What is it with you, Universal? What?" [Ric steadies himself against a side wall, dirt encrusted fingers maintaining a tight, white-knuckled grip on the cold brick.] Ric: "I tried to warn you about what happens when I get upset. You didn't listen. You let what Fate did to me at Body Count go uncontested. And when I did what I had to do, you refused me the opportunity to to do what I said - what I promised. I guaranteed on Slaughter that only Ross and I would see each other across that ring - that Fate wouldn't be a factor this time. And what did you do? You made Ross stay back, afraid of what I might do to your precious little poster boy. And worse yet, he stayed backstage, because he, also, was afraid of what would happen when he came down to the ring - scared at the certainty of my title coming back to me." [Ric stops, and starts to face the camera, but shies away at the last second.] Ric: "I haven't slept - I haven't eaten. There has been so much pain - so much that needs to be avenged. John Skyler is dead, and Ryan Moore is desecrating his memory. Victor Creid has passed - and so much more. They deserve the honor of having Ric Chronos hold the title they all wanted, at one point in time or another. They deserv-" [Ric is cut off in mid-sentence, as we see an aluminum baseball bat hit Ric a glancing blow to the back of the head.] Ric: "What the f-" [Before a now-dazed Chronos can turn to face his attacker, a second blow sends him sprawling, out cold. From the shadows, a figure, dressed from head to toe in what resembles black SWAT gear, emerges. He looks at the fallen body before him, a small pool of blood starting to coalesce around the head of Chronos.] Cameraman: "Hey, you're not allowed back here! This is a restricted area! Securi-" [The bat swings again, and the voice of the camera operator, along with the signal from the camera itself, is ended.]
