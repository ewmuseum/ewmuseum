It has come to my realization that most of you have absolutely no idea who I am. We'll call this an introduction. Trust me, the pleasure is all yours. My name is Jack Lundin, and though I will introduce myself, you will never know me. Knowledge requires experience, and you know none like mine. I used to live by the battle cry "Perception is reality," but I was full of shit. People called me a conspiracy theorist, making myths of easily explainable facts or coincidence.
