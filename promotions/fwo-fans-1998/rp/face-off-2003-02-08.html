Face Off

8 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was late Wednesday evening. Or Thursday morning. Whichever way you looked at it, Warfare had gone off the air hours ago, and a tall warrior found it hard for himself to go to sleep. Erik Kelly paced around his hotel room in Columbia. Pamela Dee was at her parents' place in Chicago because her dad was in the hospital after complaining of chest pains. He missed her more than he thought he would. The earlier events of the evening did nothing to calm him down, either. The Triple Threat was getting on his nerves. First Neo, then Fallen. Both were setups to three on one bitchslappings. But Erik knew this going in. Spyder didn't have the balls to face him one on one, and Erik knew the most important part: He had to pin Spyder to win his title back. It wasn't going to be easy, but climbing to the top never was. There was still one burning question in Erik's mind as he grabbed a bottle of water out of the mini-fridge. What the hell was Matt Loco doing back in the fWo, and why did he align himself with Vic Creed? Erik walked into the bathroom, and looked into the mirror. Something wasn't right as he looked at the reflection. Something wasn't right at all. There it was. It appeared some skin was cut in the earlier match with Fallen. It was hanging there loosely on his left cheek. Erik pulled at it, more skin pulled off. With an odd and curious expression, Erik proceeded to start ripping at the damage with a vicious demeanor. Blood began to pour from the wound. Soon, his whole face began to come undone due to his tears. And where he expected to see tissue and bone, where replaced by a new reflection... Spyder, mask and all, was now looking back at him. Erik looked down at the grotesque flesh of what was once his face down in the sink. That'll be a big mess to clean up. Looking in the mirror again, back at the face and mask of Spyder, Erik felt his face for certainty. It felt like a mask, but what was underneath it? Erik began to remove his mask. And what he saw next shocked him. Underneath the mask, the face of Matt Loco looked back at him, wild eyed and a sadistic grin. The image of Loco began to laugh back at him. And suddenly Erik felt weak, like he would faint. His chest felt heavy and sore for some reason, as he felt his consciousness failing him, the last thing he could remember was looking down at his chest, and a knife impaling him through the heart. Then darkness... "AAAAAAH!" Erik shot up from his sleeping position, covered in sweat. He looked around like a wild man on the hunt in the Amazon for his prey. Trying to seperate dream from reality, his mind began to clear as the adrenaline subsided. It was just a dream. Or a nightmare. Whichever way you looked at it.
