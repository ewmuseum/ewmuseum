Hotlanta = Title

7 February 2000

fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Hotlanta = Title February 07, 2000 fWo cameras show a shot of the fWoTron with Chris Finn waiting to interview. The fans are anxious for the start of Slaugher, but are aware of the interview tapings before going live. Finn: Ladies and Gentlemen, please welcome to Atlanta, Georgia, ORACLE!! The fans promptly boo as the arena turns blue and Oracle steps out from behind the curtain. Oracle is wearing the same black leather jacket he is always seen in. Finn: Oracle, welcome. Not a very warm welcome from this Atlanta crowd. Oracle: You know what? I've worked my ass off to please these fans and I could care less what these redneck, racist, John Rocker wannabe's think. Finn: Before Slaughter you laid out a warning to the International Champion. It looks like you stayed true to your words. Oracle: Finn, are you an idiot? I didn't "lay out a warning". I TOLD Douglas that I would kick his high flyin' ass. And simply put, his future was told. Finn: This Wednesday, right here in Atlanta, you face Hiroshima and Matt Douglas for the fWo International Title. Any thoughts? Oracle: Of course I've got some thoughts. This wouldn't be an interview if I didn't. This Wednesday Matt Douglas and the man even OLDER than me, Hiroshima. Matt Douglas got things off on the wrong foot when he quickly eliminated me from a Battle Royal. I soon showed him not to mess with me by defeating him and two of fWo's other babyfaces. I then sent Hiroshima to an early grave last week on Warfare. This clearly puts the odds in my favor. Has either man beaten me? NO! Yet, I've been both of them... Finn: ..Well that clearly doesn't give you an easy win. I mean both men will be gunning for you. It's almost two on one. Oracle: Finn, I don't even know why I bother talking to you. This Wednesday, I will have earned my FIRST fWo Title, and then I'll be one step closer to the World Heavyweight Title and my place in history. For now though, my future is bright and Douglas's only contains one thing...an ORACLE DREAM! With that, Oracle's music blares again and the arena is swept with a blue glow as the camera fades to black.
