Unleashed!... Please Don't Call the Pound

10 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Trouble! Come back here!" Tempest called after the rapidly departing Border Collie with a forlorn look on her face and a leash dangling uselessly in her hand. She tried to follow as best she could in a pair of strappy high heeled sandals and a short shiny denim skirt. She huffed and pulled off her shades, momentarily squinting into the bright sun and said, "What am I running for. I know he's only going to the hot dog stand around the corner. Sure enough as she walked around the corner Trouble was at his usual post, wagging his tail and looking up anxiously at the short round man who ran the hot dog stand. The man fussed at him in what sounded like Polish, but it was all an act for the cute little dog. The man chuckled as Trouble sat, rolled over, and played dead on command. "Try this one!" Tempest called as she walked up. "Trouble... dance!" The dog obeyed, rearing up onto his hind legs. He capered and leaped, bopping his head back and forth almost rhythmically. Tempest and the hot dog vendor laughed and applauded. Trouble wagged his tail, eagerly awaiting his reward and the hot dog vendor fished out a nice fat freshly cooked hot dog. He paused and said to Tempest, "Chili?" Tempest nodded and said, "Yup, if he likes the Szechuan that falls on the floor at my place, you can bet he'll eat that chili." The man grinned and dropped the hot dog, smothered in chili, in front of Trouble, who rapidly started cleaning up the sidewalk where it fell. He laughed and then looked up at Tempest and asked, "So you ready for that new show you're gonna be on? Whatsit... Unchained?" Tempest chuckled and said, "It's Unleashed. Yeah I'm ready for it. You should tune in. It's gonna be wild!" She grinned and said, "In fact tonight I begin my climb to the top of the Internet title ranks!" The vendor laughed as she struck a pose with a wry smile and he said, "You've already got that uh... Featherweight title right? Can you have more than one?" Tempest laughed and said, "Cruiserweight! They don't have a Featherweight division." "Well they should for you," the man said and laughed as he made Tempest her occasional treat of a smothered cheese dog. She smiled and paid him and said, "You're right, I should come here more often and I'd make it into the next weight division!" She laughed and chewed thoughtfully for a moment before saying, "Thankfully the Internet title doesn't have any kind of weight thingie attached to it. But ya know, that's the biggest pain. I mean the BIG title is the world heavyweight championship. And I'll never be a heavyweight." Her shoulders slumped and the vendor looked at her silently for a moment and then said, "More cheese?" as he scooped up some more from the dish. She looked at him, looked at the hot dog and nodded. "Please. Too bad it's not that easy to get into a higher division." She sighed and the vendor said, "Well, you done great things for them. I mean how many kids watched before you came on board? And how many girls? I swear, my two daughters got you plastered all over their room. Those guys runnin' the place gotta know that you helped them out a lot. I'm sure they're gonna push you to bigger and better things." Tempest shrugged and said, "Maybe. But sometimes it doesn't seem like it. Especially when..." She trailed off and turned briefly to the street, her eyes lighting up with mischief. "No... that's the one thing people have been missing." She turned back to the vendor and smiled as she said, "Don't you worry. The one they keep forgetting is that Tempest is full of surprises." She winked and started down the street, as the vendor smiled, with Trouble toddling along beside her. Unleashed of course.
