Opening boxes.

26 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
May 15th, 2003 10:05 a.m. The residence of Brandon Roche and now, Amy Campbell. Brandon looked over to Amy as she struggled with a box of books, over packed as it was it was providing quite an amusing sight. Finally giving up hefting it to the top of the dresser, the box bounced on the edge of the bed before sliding off the silk sheets. Amy squealed and jumped out of the way while it just barely missed her foot. "You okay there dear? That big old box not putting up too much of a fight, is it?" Stifling back a laugh, as he knew it would only lead to trouble Brandon figured veiled sarcasm would be a better approach. Oh how wrong he could be sometimes... A hardcover copy of A Midsummer Night's Dream suddenly flew by the Paragon's head. "You're hilarious, Brandon. I broke my ribs not even two weeks ago and didn't even ask you to help with moving this crap..." She tried to conceal a smug grin, amused by her own attempt at a guilt trip. "So you're in no position to be making fun of me." "Who was making fun!?" Brandon's stilted eyebrows completed his look of mock innocence. "And don't you think by now I know you wouldn't ask for help? That's the whole reason I'm here, so you don't kill yourself trying to be all 'Ms. Independent'." Amy chuckled, "I don't see you nudging any boxes, so I wouldn't start that. I also didn't see you with some psycho trying to smash more of your ribs last night, so get over here and lend a hand, bitch..." Her smirk followed a wink and a swat at Brandon's arm as she kicked a box up against his right leg, laughing all the way. "I got my share of war wounds to worry with... I can't help you're softer than me and get all bruised up. Now move and let the big Mr. Paragon get those books for you." The tongue in cheek wouldn't save him and he winced as the elbow shot out from Amy almost faster than the words left his mouth. "So did you hear the rumblings what with the new board coming on... board... so to speak? There's all sort of shakeups coming. I wouldn't be surprised it some of the troublemakers were finally dealt with... much like Borst was." "Meh...just more drama." The redhead sighed, "Fox is good by me, you know that's as far as I care about the 'higher-ups'. It sure was nice to see Borst go, though...but you knew that. I only care about you, Eli, Ivy and maybe myself, even though I kinda hope they don't get rid of Kinkade too quickly - I want him to see what happens when he tries to get away with things without me being injured." "Well, there's Lance and Sean too, even though Lance has been a bit distant lately. You can't just put everything we've been through this last year out the window and forget where we came from. That stuff last night was just so out of character for him" "Sean's 'on his own' now." Amy grumbled, "And Lance is...I have no idea. I've actually been meaning to ask..." She shuffled her feet a bit, a little apprehensive..."Have you noticed he's never really taken to me? I...like, he was okay at first and then he was all...I dunno, different? Maybe I'm crazy." "Nah, there was this thing a few weeks back... after all the craziness with the bombing. He and I were supposed to do this autograph session and I canceled because of... well, because of you. I figured he'd understand because of everything that happened and I'm telling you... I got the longest voicemail from him blowing everything out of proportion." Brandon peered through sad eyes. It was obvious that the troubles with 'Lance Knight' weighed heavily on him even outside the business. "Maybe it's just growing pains. I know that sounds weird but we're all getting used to the new way thing are around here and this is just his way of dealing with it. Maybe he sees you as more of an outsider than most. I'm not a big fan of Carson's but he and Lance have had problems since day one... and he's another 'new guy' brought in after we came all came back." "Truth be known I felt a little resentment about the whole thing myself. Back in the day... all of a year and a half ago, when we came onboard for Survivor II admittance in the fed was hard to come by. We worked our asses off to get where we were and then suddenly there's this influx of new talent and it seems like just 'anybody' could get in the place." "Anybody...like me?" "Well..." Lightening the mood again Brandon planted a trademark wink in the middle of his sentence. "... yeah... like you... or rather guys like Xias and Borst, lowlifes to spike the ratings." While he'd been speaking, Amy'd attempted to life another little box...to absolutely no avail. In a moment, she'd eased back against the bed behind her - trying not to show any weakness. She didn't like the idea of 'giving in' to Kellen Kinkade's handiwork. Nonetheless, she returned Brandon's wink. "Well, I think I've given you plenty of reason to be pleased about my presence in the company...so let's hope you're not still resenting it. But yeah...I dunno, it seems like he's a little...gah, I don't know how to word it. But a few times now I've caught him in the corner of my eye when you and I are together in the back...he'll start coming over, see me and turn back around with the single most disgusted look on his face." "Maybe he's got a crush on you like Marder did?" Wide-eyed Amy bolted out with a closed fist right in to Brandon's arm. The reverberations caused her to twinge in pain as she felt the blow herself, in her ribs. "Hey! Watch it killer, I was TEASING." Brandon rubbed the soon to be sore spot as he slumped back on the bed, three boxes unpacked to Amy's one barely moved. "I think we all need to sit down and have a rat pack pow wow...heh... or something. Play cards or billiards... and... I... just got the best idea in the world." The smirk showed the mighty paragon was sure of himself. "What about a party? Here." "As long as we keep eyes on Ivy and Sean all the time, sure..." She chuckled, "I just don't want to end the night wondering what the hell went on in what room..." "Actually...you sure that's a good idea? Like, where we both live? Lance might not take too kindly, for whatever reason..." "I think it's the perfect place. I know this is six of one and half dozen of another but you're not technically here as my girlfriend, more so as a roommate... ahem... technically...anyway, before I distract myself further, alot of the guys haven't been here before. It's not a part of the coast the federation tours normally, hell even the WWE only limp through here once a year, if that. So it'd be a perfect getaway from the road, AND Lance has been here already. Way back when Survivor started all the Riders got together down in the rec' room and hung around trying to get to know each other. Lance was a little late to the whole thing and when we were finally on the road he confided in me that he was surely the first one gone because he had let the team down in our first challenge." "I told him not to worry about it, and I turned out to be right. Maybe an invite back here will freshen his memory on our ties together... I don't know... it's worth a shot." "Awww...that's cute and waaay too just like something you'd do. Mighty Teddy Bear..." Amy's smile quickly cut off with a laugh, "Wait...if I'm your 'room mate', does this mean I have to pay rent?" "Well..." Brandon squinted. "I... was meaning to talk to you about that..." "Juuuuuuuust great...now I'm gonna have to go back prostitutin' myself for extra cash. Way to be!" She kicked at him, biting her tongue in a grin. "You're meaner than the bookers without their daily doses of Ritalin." Wincing and realizing he was getting more beaten up by Amy Campbell than he did, Borst Brandon held up his hands in defeat. "Wait... wait... wait... before you go on abusing me let me finish. I was just going to say that maybe we could work out something in trade, with those terms to be decided at a later date. For now we need to get this straightened up a bit and less of a kid hazard." "I have to take Sean to his speech therapist, remember? You've got to watch Jarod for me..." "And Stephen gets here any minute now..." That was right, too. Her son was due to arrive thanks to a ride from her brother - who'd lived in the area for a while - at any time. Amy herself winced at the thought of welcoming him into an entirely new environment and having to deal with Jarod - all alone - in the same instance... "Are you sure you can't postpone the appointment?" "Sean needs to see him, they've been out of the country for awhile." Brandon stepped over a now empty box and hugged Amy tight. "Don't be scared Super-Raver-Chick, big bad Jarod won't beat you up too bad." "Not funny...he hates me. You've got no idea. And he's always talking about that God damned ---mom of his...and how I'm not her, I don't do things like her, blah blah blah." She let herself flop back onto the bed, rubbing her temples momentarily. "Gah...I'm more afraid of a four year old than I am of any two hundred-something pound guy..." "Well, he IS Jarod Frontier after all!" A tongue darted out of Brandon's mouth so quick that Amy wasn't sure that's what it was. "He.. doesn't hate you... you three will have a blast... Mommy." Before she could react he had scurried out of the room, tail tucked firmly between his legs... Brand Frontier might have been heroic, but Brandon Roche knew when the getting was good. Hell hath no fury like a woman's scorn... [This has been an Aimz/ Brand Frontier collaboration.]
