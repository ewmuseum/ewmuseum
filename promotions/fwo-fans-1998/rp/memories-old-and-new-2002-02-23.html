Memories (Old, And New)

23 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
April 29, 2000. The night of CyberSlam 2K. His greatest night ever. And it was here, at Safeco Field. Just two years ago... he was the owner of the fans Wrestling organization, and it's World Champion. He had all the power, all the glory, and within one month... it was gone in the blink of an eye. He lost control, he lost the gold. All because of one man. Scott Slugger. His career had hit rock bottom, and within the next year he would be fired from the fWo... and involved in a car accident that many other human beings wouldn't have walked away from, yet alone survived. Yet he did. And he returned to the fWo sooner than anyone had expected. And he made an impact. He helped Chris Universal regain control... No. He *gave* control back to Chris Universal after defeating The Flying Frenchie & Primetime at Twilight, and in the same night screwed Ruben Ross out of the World Title in his hometown when Universal deemed him special guest referee. One week later he ended the career of Lance Star, breaking his neck for good. Snap. On the same evening he aligned with a man he once had hated almost as much as Universal... The Boston Strangler. The two men once had vowed to be mortal enemies until the end of time or until the sun no longer rised every morning; until the two men realized they had more in common than they had previously thought. Paul. The next night he had the World Title won; until Ross sought out his payback. But Ross did something you just don't do. You don't screw Austin Davis over. You don't screw Austin Davis over. YOU DON'T SCREW AUSTIN DAVIS OVER because there will be payback, and it would be far worse than anything you could ever imagine; and because Davis would make others pay for your actions whether they had anything to do with the situation or not. The Black Panther. Like 'The Future' found out two nights prior, the Black Panther found out that he was just a pawn in Davis' game of chess. Of course, a few minutes later, a drunk Austin Davis went to the ring and urinated in it. Pissing on the fans, his fellow wrestlers, and waging war against the fWo and it's champion once again. Andrea. And then there was Ross' girlfriend. She would find herself on the playing field, and Davis terrorized her in the backstage area just two weeks ago. Plans were foiled by Ross; because despite Davis promising not to lay a hand on his significant other, he was going to. But this only angered Davis to a level even unthinkable for him. He had used snakes, glass, plastic bags, body bags... buried opponents... but he had never, ever, hung someone. Until now. And it would all end here. At this venue. At Safeco. To get back everything that was his... it would begin with Ross and the title. After that... title or not... Scott... ... to be continued.
