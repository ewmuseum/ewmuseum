The Winding Road To Love (Part 7)

27 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On a night when many fWo wrestlers, staff, and officials were out on the town enjoying Anaheim, Monte Burns sat down on the couch in his hotel room. Alone. Quiet. The last two months had seemed like an eternity, waiting for this moment to finally arrive. There were a lot of thoughts running through his head. After all, this would be his first CyberSlam as an active participant. Last year at CyberSlam, he and the other new signees had been invited backstage for the event to meet with some of the wrestlers. He remembered meeting all of the greats - from the Boston Strangler to Mitch Wilson to Wretch. He also remembered meeting with Crusader, the man that would be making his return to the fWo at tomorrow night's event. The two men had sat down backstage and talked a little bit about wrestling and batting around ideas for a program later on in the year. When Crusader decided against returning to the fWo a few months later, Monte was one of the most disappointed. Last year was also the first time he had laid eyes on Tempest. Burns could remember it clearly: He had been speaking with Ruben Ross in the catering area, laughing at Ross's jokes about his name and the Simpsons. As the two of them scanned the room, they noticed a young woman with dark hair walk into the room, waving hello to some of the others. Monte remembered seeing her walk over to the table where they sat and introduce herself to Ruben and then him as well. He remembered looking into her sparkling eyes. He remembered the first thoughts that had entered his head. "A woman. Women don't belong in the ring with men. Women should be wrestling other women. I hope I never have to deal with her." As he looked back now, he was ashamed for thinking those thoughts. He was ashamed for making the kind of judgment about her that he always hated for people to make about him because of his wealth. Burns had struggled to reconcile his thoughts on Tempest for a long time, stretching back to last fall when he first started having feelings for her. He knew then that he had been wrong for underestimating and discounting her, but Monte did nothing to show this change of heart publicly. He kept on talking about how she didn't deserve to be in the ring with him, how she should have stayed in Hong Kong, how she had no place in the fWo. Then came his brother's accident, and things began to change. Seeing his brother lying in bed, alone at a time when love was needed most, forced Monte to see where he had gone wrong. Burns had spent so much energy hating and battling that he had lost out on the love and passion that drive the human soul. It was no surprise that Monte was feeling lost and needed some time off, because he wasn't complete and he was finally realizing that. After months of internal grief, Monte knew that he needed to let his feelings out, no matter how embarrassing it might be. At Body Count, he did just that, revealing to Tempest that he was her secret admirer. However, after all he had done in his life, Tempest rightfully questioned whether he was sincere or not. Monte had a change to prove he was, but he chose instead to sulk and fight once again. The first couple of weeks after Body Count were awful, with Burns lying to himself and to the world about his feelings. He knew that he loved Tempest. He knew that he cared deeply for her. He knew that he wanted to be with her. Yet, his pride wouldn't let him admit that after what happened at Body Count. Monte had felt embarrassed when she was skeptical, feeling betrayed by his emotions for leaving himself open to that. He took his frustrations out on others, being too weak to face his issues head on. Finally, he stepped forward to face the problem and demanded a Cruiserweight Title shot at CyberSlam. In his first CyberSlam match, he would be in the ring with the woman he cared about deeply. He knew that she would doubt him because of the title's involvement, but Monte knew what was really motivating him. Standing only a day away from CyberSlam and the biggest night of his life, only one thought now ran through Monte's head: "This is my chance to prove to Tempest how I really feel about her. This is my chance to prove what has been on my mind in the last two months. I'm not going to disappoint her...and I'm not going to disappoint myself."
