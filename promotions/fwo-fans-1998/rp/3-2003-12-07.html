Stupid writing... in a stupid little book. That's what the old Brandon Thompson would say. Now? The new Brandon had to have this thing to write in. Just to let that small part inside of him that was still the old him shine through once in a blue moon. Just to let himself know that he was still human, that he wasn't a waste of existance. Slowly words flowed into the book as Brandon wrote at a rapid pace. "I died my hair black to show the hurt. My skin is pale from mourning. My face shows age beyond it's years. My cold stare seeks vengance." Quickly the book snapped shut as Brandon sat silent. His stringy black hair came down over his face, clearly it was greasy from not having bathed. His face had blonde stubble covering it, still showing that he was in fact the same person. The once warm happy face though, was now contorted into a drunken sneer. His fist slammed into the wall crushing a hole, that continued the pattern he was forming. A giant X. Quickly he opened the book and continued his pointless markings. "I hate myself even more than yesterday. Lord why cant I just get away?" With that the book snapped shut again as he sat silent. Brandon Thompson was cold and alone. "Thank you for my chance... I'll show them all the real me one day. But until then your my only friend. Good bye." Brandon said almost crypticly. Indeed, Brandon was alone.
