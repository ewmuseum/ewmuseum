Backstage

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Ivy." "Yeah, Chris?" "Eli gonna be here tonight?" "Yeah, Chris." "Give him this for me?" "The Hardcore Belt?" "Yeah." "Willdo." "Trip hasn't checked in yet, did he get here with you?" "...." "Ivy?" "I heard you. No." Poison Ivy has been sitting in the trainer's room for an hour now, trying to write her road diary. She's all ready for the night - opting for an Elastica baby tee and tight black jeans. Underneath, her leg is still wrapped up in the saran wrap from her tattoo earlier in the day. Number three. "I can't get anything done in here," she said to nobody in particular, and headed outside. It's the usual scene on the day of a house show. Lighting and sound techs are frantically double- checking everything under the sun to make sure the lighting and music for each wrestler is as it should be. The wrestlers, of course, are just hanging out. Television cameras can make a body nervous - a house show is an excuse to have fun, work for ten to forty minutes, and move on. Ribbing is like a bodily function here - something the fWo roster had sorely needed for a long time. Professional wrestling requires its employees to have a thick skin - criticism is a fact of life. The fWo survived better than most promotions, since their tendancy to work hard produced the most satisfying results. When injury and retirement cut the ranks of the top of the card early in the year, they worked harder to fill in the gaps... and the only casualty, Ric Chronos' notwithstanding, was their sense of humor. It's back. Ivy rounds a corner, and sees Tempest coming her way, a look of mischief on her face. "Whazzup!" called out Ivy, as the two passed. "Will you please go talk to Sean?" asked Tempest bravely, "He's miserable out there. At least hear him out?" Ivy, lacking the strength, lacking the anger to truly be mad at Tempest, simply sighed and leaned against the wall. "It's not that simple, Tempest. He lied to me. Then he tries to make it better by proposing to me. What's a girl s'posed to think?" Tempest struck a thoughtful pose. "Okay. So he gets a three- for- ten on style. But seriously, Ivy... what could it hurt to listen to him? Hear him out before you tell him to go away?" "I wish it were that easy, girl." "It is." They stare at each other for a moment. "C'mon," said Tempest, finally, "Boss Man is holding a meeting." She grabs Ivy by the arm and pulls her reluctant friend along with her. But maybe... just maybe... she got through. Maybe.
