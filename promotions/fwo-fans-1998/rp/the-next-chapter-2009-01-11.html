The Next Chapter

11 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Christmas had never been a rather fond holiday for Craig. Two years ago, he had the fun pleasure of killing a woman masquerading as his wife instead of the vindictive, murderous bitch she truly was. Last year had been the time when the man investigating said murder ended up committing suicide in Craig's house. Needless to say, Christmas was a little faded and mundane to Craig. Yet, living in a house with Jack Harmon and Mary-Lynn Mayweather (Tony Davis would be somewhere breaking stuff and playing his Nintendo D.S.) always ensured that craziness would ensue no matter the time of the day or year. With fresh reminders that his life was in terrible danger, Craig finds himself sitting in a chair with his eyes closed on Christmas Eve with the everlasting reminder that the cheerful holiday was mere hours away. How so? Well, holiday music blaring in your ears, four Christmas trees in a forty foot radius of one another, and blinding lights that melt your eyes away would have to be good indicators. Somehow though, the ailing and morose Craig was intent upon falling asleep in his living room and hopefully begin to mentally prepare for his upcoming match with the Pineapple King. Just one problem though; he still lives in a house with Mary-Lynn Mayweather and Jack Harmon. "Sleep is for the weak, you fuckin' pussy!" the, for the moment, grating voice rips through his deep state of unconsciousness. Craig's eyes flicker open and nearly burn a hole through Jack who is standing there, shoulder to shoulder, with Mary-Lynn showcasing rather large grins on their faces. Never a good sign, he makes a mental note to himself. "I love it Jack that I’m never allowed to curse in your house but you're able to do it all of the time at mine," Craig retorts as he sits up in his chair while wondering to himself what exactly these two crazy people have cooked up. "Children, man, children. Look, what the hell are you doing falling asleep? It's Christmas Eve!" Jack yells back at Craig, insinuating in the process that Craig is, well, rather silly for trying to take a nap. Craig gives Jack a sideways look before looking over at Mary-Lynn. "Okay, I'll bite," Craig begins before standing up and walking over to a nearby counter before pouring himself a glass of whiskey. Lately he had taken to drinking more and more alcohol. By sheer coincidence, he had also been spending more and more time around Jack. "What the hell do you two have up your sleeves?" Mary-Lynn shuffles her feet and has this rather coy look on her face causing Craig to take a deep breath in as he knows whatever it is that they've cooked up isn't going to end up well for him. Images of chickens and Tony Davis' asshole come to mind and in doing so Craig shudders before he takes another sip of his drink. "It's Christmas time," Mary-Lynn says with a smile on her face and in her voice. Craig nods his head, letting them know with the expression on his face that he's not completely dense about how the real world actually works. "So, you know, we got you presents and stuff." The mere words causes him to roll his eyes into the back of his head as he grabs the Whiskey bottle. However, before he even has a chance to take the cap off, Mary-Lynn is grabbing his hand and yanking the bottle out of his hands. She then drags him over to the couch and forces him into his seat. Normally, when this would happen to him, it would be followed with a lap dance, but he didn't think Mary-Lynn was that kind of girl. Craig looks at Mary-Lynn and then over at Jack before deducing that these two are rather serious about this. He sighs, wishing that he had a knife in one hand and an exposed throat in the other hand. He would even be willing to be that exposed throat, but somehow he doesn't see any of those things taking place this evening. Instead, he decides to play this game a little better. "Well, you know, that's great and all, but it's not really Christmas yet." Jack and Mary-Lynn look at each other rather confused before glaring back at Craig. "So? We're opening presents!" Jack roars back, excited by the mere prospect of receiving gifts from people. The puzzled look doesn't leave Craig's face though. "Okay, I know I'm not usually the festive one, as telling," Craig says before pointing out all of the decorations threatening to strangle his house to death. "But, and I hate to sound like a broken record here, it's still not Christmas. So, really, we can't open presents tonight. It's like against the Christmas Code. I have it somewhere around here." With that being said, Craig lays his head back and closes his eyes. Moments of silence fill the air, but with the burning sensation on his skin he gets this weird feeling that the duo haven't quite left. As he opens his eyes he finds that his suspicions are indeed correct and he can feel a variety of curse words ready to fly out at them. "Okay, seriously? What now?" Craig asks them, and almost as soon as he utters those words he can feel a sense of dread nearly eat him alive as he knows that the night is only going to get worse from this point forward. "Well," Mary-Lynn begins, her enthusiasm nearly slapping Craig in the face. "We've got to get on a plane in a few hours what with you two doing publicity work at the Orlando Magic game tomorrow and then at the Miami Heat game on Friday for the big PPV." Craig just sort of sits there, his eyes fixated upon Mary-Lynn, and wondering to himself if High Flyer would tell the police if he killed Mary-Lynn in this very moment. Somehow, that urge manages to calm down just a little bit before he looks over at High Flyer. "Alright, look, I'm going to sleep. The two of you leave me alone!" he yells at them before closing his eyes hoping that would be enough this time for them to leave him along. The year past hadn't been a kind one to him and the quicker he was out of it, the better he would be for it. With every possible amenity in his house he was certain that one of them would make his life brighter in the New Year. Considering the past year, he didn't think it could possibly be that careful. However, apparently he would have to suffer through insomnia in 2009 as Mary-Lynn tapped his shoulder repeatedly like a woodpecker constantly attacks a tree in the forest. Craig once again opens his eyes at Mary-Lynn and rolls his eyes at her. "Okay, the truth of the matter is that I didn't buy any of you gifts. Sorry, but it's against my religion to celebrate Christmas," he informs them with this matter of fact tone to his words. "Aren't you Christian?" Jack pipes in, that eternally crazed look in his eyes that're staring straight at him. Craig nods his head before responding to him. "No, you must've misheard me. I said I killed Christianity. I killed God and all. I'm that damn good," Craig tells Jack who snaps his fingers before nodding his head as he remembers Craig telling him that story. A good story indeed. Mary-Lynn then looks over at Craig this stern look as if she's his mother or something along those lines. "Look, we're opening presents whether you like it or not! This was never really a question or a suggestion, and if I have to, I'll kick your ass myself. Do you understand me, mister?!" she yells at him, her eyes never wavering from him as she remains adamant as to their plans for the evening. Finally, Craig sits up and a smile appears on Mary-Lynn's face as if Craig had come to his senses. She turns around and begins to walk towards the Christmas tree in the corner, a joyful step in her walk. "Just one problem," Craig begins softly, causing Mary-Lynn to turn around so fast that Craig is almost certain that she's just going to fall over. "I have no presents for you guys. Just haven't had any time over the past few weeks. Sorry." Mary-Lynn is quick to the attack as her eyes narrow to the point that she can't possibly see out of them before sticking her left arm out and pointing to the nearby corner. Craig's eyes slowly and cautiously move towards that corner before they fall upon a stack of presents; presents he had bought for them. Craig groans, none too happy to be doing any of this, before he moves to the edge of his chair and nods his head at Mary-Lynn. "Let's open the frickin' presents already," Craig tells her and watches in awe as the largest smile appears on her face before she turns around and hurries over to the present pile where she grabs a couple of presents. Before Craig has a chance to figure out what's going on, Mary-Lynn has tossed him a present and then follows that up with throwing a present at Jack. "That one's from Jack," Mary-Lynn warns Craig as he starts to open the present. He slowly holds it out as he looks over at Jack with this cautious look. Well, not so much a cautious look as it is one that says "I'm going to kill you if an animal jumps out of this box and tries to eat my eyeball like the last time that I opened a present from you." Jack nods his head as if to give his personal seal that it's going to be okay, but it's also the same assurance Jack gave for the last present he opened. Craig takes a deep breath in and lets it all out before he opens his breath with one hand ready to punch something. As he opens it though, it reveals itself to be the first season of Angel on DVD. Craig takes a long look at the box, Jack watching in the background with great earnest, before Craig's bright red eyes glance over towards Jack with a puzzled look on his face. "Uh, Jack? What the hell is this?" Jack smiles before he nods his head. "Look, I've been telling you all year long that you need to start watching some better television programs. What you have before you and in the other boxes in that corner," Jack then points to a completely different corner that shows a stack upon stacks of un-opened Christmas Presents. "Is every show that you should watch. I've got everything here from Angel and Buffy, to Entourage and the Wire, Battlestar Galactica. You name it, it's there. I thought though I had grabbed the box of Lost to give to you since we need to start watching that, like, now." Mary-Lynn then looks down at her watch. "We've got exactly 4 weeks, Jack." "Good, because if I have to wait any longer, I'm going to fly down there, grab Carlton Cuse and Damon Lindelof myself, and punch them squarely in the face until they tell me everything I want to know about Lost," Jack tears off in a tirade, his voice becoming heightened over the time. "Fucking nanites," he whispers under his breath while Craig puts the box down on the table and nods his head. "Thanks, Jack." Without even being prompted, Mary-Lynn starts tearing into her present and opens it to reveal a certificate of some sorts. As she starts to turn it over and reads it in-depth she gasps before looking at Crag. "What the hell is this?!" she asks him before throwing it over to Jack who starts reading it, but stops half-way before he can feel his head starting to hurt from just reading a few words. "Did you read it?" Craig asks her and she begins to nod her head while tears begin to fill her eyes. Jack just sits there completely perplexed. "What the hell does this stupid thing say?!" "Mary-Lynn, would you do us the honors?" Craig asks her to which she can only nod her head. She walks over to Jack who hands her the piece of paper and she looks it over once more before looking at Craig. "It's with great pleasure that I hereby re-name the University of California - Berkley's Law Library to the Mary-Lynn Mayweather Law Library courtesy of the tremendous donation given to the Law Program from Mr. Craig Maloof in her name. From this moment forward, Mary-Lynn will always be allowed into our library and the books of said library now belong to her possession to which she has loaned to the University of California - Berkley. Sincerely, Goodwin Liu, Associate Dean and Professor of Law." Silence fills the room as tears roll down Mary-Lynn's silk like cheeks, her eyes still unable to comprehend what it is that she's reading here. She looks up over at Craig and walks over to him before wrapping her arms around his neck and the two hug for a few moments. Obviously, Jack sits there and looks around, feeling rather uncomfortable as he just sits there and starts opening his gift. "You're a very intelligent woman, Mary-Lynn, and I don't want anyone to ever forget that. You might be here with us wrestling, but that doesn't take away your exemplary gift--," he begins to tell Mary-Lynn before Jack shoots up out of his chair and starts yelling at the top of his lungs. "HOLY SHIT! YOU GOT ME A ROCKET SHIP!" Craig and Mary-Lynn look over at Jack who has the biggest shit-eating grin on his face that anyone has ever seen. Jack thinks about running over and hugging Craig as well, but stops himself short as Craig holds his hands up and shakes his head. "Before you start planning trips to the moon though, it doesn't work. Though, I might help you fix it up if you promise not to fly it, or at the very least, not to fly it with me in it." "DUDE! It's still a rocket ship! I'm going to be like Cid in Final Fantasy VIII and wait for the day when some vagrant boys and girls want to go for a ride in my rocket!" Jack proclaims proudly, one foot on his couch and the other planted firmly into the ground with a proud smile on his face. Mary-Lynn looks at Craig before looking back over at Jack. "That sounded so wrong." "What the hell is all of the commotion?!" Enter Stage Right: Tony Davis, Jack's friend and crime-in-partner. Tony looks around with this absolutely dumbfounded look on his face mixed with a drooping smile. Just plain out depressing. "Tony, why the sad face? And where's your Nintendo D.S.?" Mary-Lynn asks him as if he's her little brother. Tony walks over to a chair and just drops his body lifelessly into the chair, almost heartbroken. "Tony?" Jack asks this time, slightly pissed off that Tony just completely ruined his moment. "I dropped my Nintendo D.S. in the toilet. Again," Tony tells the group to which Mary-Lynn just shakes her head, turning from sister to mother in the drop of a hat. "What'd I tell you about trying to pee and play the Nintendo D.S. at the same time! Sometimes, I just want to pop you in the head!" she yells at him vehemently, causing Tony's attitude to get even worse. Craig then stands up and walks over to his pile of presents before tossing it over to Tony. Tony takes a long look at it before ripping the wrapping paper off to reveal a Nintendo D.S. "Dude! Are you serious?! I thought you hated me!" Tony yells out in complete and utter surprise. Craig just stands there before nodding his head. Tony then jumps up, ecstatic as everyone else has, and runs over to Craig before hugging him full on. Craig just stands there as Tony hugs him before Craig pushes Tony off of him. "Tony!" he yells at Tony before grabbing his freshly unwrapped Nintendo D.S. and throwing it out the window and into the swimming pool! Tony just stands there, dejected, having all of his hopes and dreams washed away just like that. "First off, I still hate you. Second off, what's rule #2?" Craig asks Tony who's standing there back to this previous pathetic self. Tony mumbles something under his breath to which Craig gives him this weird look like he couldn't hear him. "Rule #2," Tony says before clearing his throat while his eyes can't bear to meet Craig's. "Don't ever hug me. Ever. Or else bad things will happen to me." Tony looks like he might cry right there on the spot. "And, what happened to you the last time you hugged me?" Craig asked him to which Tony responds with a huge shrug of the shoulders even though he knows what happened last time. "Tony, what happened?" Tony look around the room for a minute before looking back at Craig. "You set my mom on fire." That's all that needs to be said as Craig walks over to his chair and sits in it as Tony just stands there with no one to love him. Craig can't take much longer as he just shakes his head. "Okay, Tony," Craig begins before he looks over to Mary-Lynn and then back to Tony. "I know a person, she'll get you a new Nintendo D.S. It'll be on the plane before we take off, I promise you. Just, don't do things that break the rules, and I wouldn't have to, you know, punish you and stuff. Merry Christmas." Mary-Lynn then stands up and walks over to Jack before whispering something in his ear. Jack looks over to Craig and then looks back at Mary-Lynn before nodding his head. "Craig," Mary-Lynn begins to say before looking back at Jack who is walking towards the balcony of Craig's house. "Jack needs to talk to you for a few moments. Then, we've got to get on the place I guess." Craig looks over at Mary-Lynn who nods her head and then gets up to follow Jack out to the balcony. As he steps out onto the deck, Jack stands on there, against the railing, with a subdued look on his face. "Look, I know PRIME doesn't intend to re-sign you. I know you've done everything possible to get Cantrell to change his mind, but we all know that he's a huge giant prick. Kind of because he doesn't have a prick. However, I intended to do this all along, but I kind of pushed the right buttons to make sure it happened now sooner than later," Jack begins to tell Craig before he hands him a folded piece of paper. "You know you're probably my best friend and I'd do anything for you man." Craig slowly unfolds the piece of paper and reads it over a couple of times before looking back at Jack. "Are you serious?" "Look, what else are you going to do? Retire? Fight Desade for the rest of your life? Come on, Craig. You're not some nobody. You're a fucking legend. I've watched you do your thing for the past 18 or so years. You're not going out this way, and seriously, I fucking refuse to let it end this way," he tells Craig. Craig looks over the piece of paper again, his eyes soft from the emotion being shown to him by Jack. "But, Jack, my knee. The fWo, that's the place where the best go. I can barely compete in PRIME, how the hell do you think I'll be able to manage in fWo?" Craig asks him before grabbing his throbbing, injured knee. Jack just shakes his head. "I know you can do it, trust me. I need you there. You've always got my back and I need to make sure people aren't just going to stab me in the back. Will you come and do it, for me?" Jack asks him and Craig takes a moment before he nods his head. Jack smiles at Craig before leaving him on the deck for himself as Craig looks out over the water and takes a moment to himself. One thing was for certain to Craig though. A new chapter was set to begin.
