Sins Of The Father - Part 3

20 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Mike pretended to have been surprised, jumping forward with a gasp, much to the delight of the small child behind him. "You'll have to excuse her. She's... she's a lot like her mother is... was." The words came out of the grieving Eldon, almost as if he were reluctant to admit what had happened, that not vocalizing the horrid truth would change it into a passing nightmare. The young doctor had obviously been crying, distraught over the death of his young wife - Sun Chen. Mike: "That's perfectly fine. I'm certain that she's - well, handling this the best she can." Mike patted the little girl on the head as she scampered off, and noticed that the other child she was playing with seemed far more distant - a serious counterpart to a carefree child. The little girl abruptly swept the feet out of from the other, causing her to giggle with laughter. Mike looked at the two, and was reminded of his own two children at home - the gregarious Darcy and the always-too-serious Ric - and of how much a blow the loss of a mother would mean to them. He looked over at Papa Liu and Wu, nodded briefly, and the trio made their farewells known. As they walked into the bright Chinese morning, Mike tapped out a brisk pace. Once he was beyond what he believed would be earshot of the little hut they had just departed, he turned towards Liu. Mike: "I want her taken care of, also. She's young, and I don't want her to face what the other girls in your country face. I know the way you view female children in this land, and I want to protect her." Liu's eyes narrowed, and he replied sharply, almost hissing out the words. Liu: "Why does the little girl matter to you? She is not your child, of that much I am certain. What do you care what happens to the child of a dead pit fighter? You and I have come to have a friendship over the years, and I would hate to strain that relationship over Chen's little bitch." Mike stepped forward, bringing his full size into bear. Wu moved to intercept, but Mike was ready, and far more seasoned than his first encounter with the bodyguard. Mike's powerful left hand seized Wu by the throat, and he lifted the Oriental until his eyes could meet Mike's own, nearly a full foot off of the ground. He didn't take his gaze off of Liu, however, even as Wu slowly felt the lack of oxygen began to shut down his brain. Mike: "Regardless of whether or not that's my child, Liu, I feel a responsibility towards Chen, as should you. After all, she died in YOUR pit. And if you don't promise me now that you'll protect that child, I'll make certain that you, yourself, are left without protection." Emphasizing his point, he squeezed harder, causing a rattling sound to emanate from Wu's throat. Liu seemed to consider the possibilities, valuing the life of his most trusted guard against his association with a far-too-moral American - and a very profitable fighter. Mike: "To make it easier, I'll forfeit my pay from the next couple of tours, and I'll send a little money along whenever I can. You won't provide for her out of your resources alone." Liu weighed this option as well, and finally, a crack appeared in the brazen armor. He sighed, and signaled for Mike to release Wu. Liu: "Very well. You will not need to send the money along - I do have a debt of honor to Chen, and to be honest, I have grown fond of the child during the past few years. You have my word that I will take care of the child as I would one of my own - or one of yours. I doubt that will be necessary, as she is the child of a doctor. I am sorry that I forced the situation to come to this, but she is Chinese, and we take care of our own. I did not want to involve you unnecessarily." Mike relaxed his grip, allowing Wu to crumple to the ground, gasping for breath. When Wu recovered, his eyes threw daggers towards Mike. Papa recognized the look, and sharply struck his employee on the back of the head. Liu: "Do not dare. It is not his fault that you are incompetent. You should be grateful that I do not throw you to the Arrows for your ineptitude, as I did with your brother. We will discuss your failings later." This seemed to anger the man, but he held his tongue, and his hand. Liu again addressed Mike, turning his back towards his disgraced employee. Liu: "Let us remember that we are friends here, Mike. What I do for that child I do in the name of that friendship. Just remember that you owe me a favor in return for this." Mike nodded his head, and began the turn - until the motion caught his eye. Reacting without thinking, Mike again found his hand around Wu's throat - this time, to prevent the knife from sinking in to Papa Liu's ribs. Wu withdrew the knife from Liu, intending to stab at Mike with it. He again proved too slow, as Mike grabbed Wu's wrist, and with a quick jerk, snapped it. The knife tumbled harmlessly to the ground. Mike: "You bastard!" Mike began pummeling Liu's bodyguard, until he felt that there would be no further resistance. Dropping a semi-conscious Wu for the second time, Mike bent over the form of Papa Liu. Liu was hurt, badly, but not dead. A relived sigh forced itself from Mike upon that discovery. Mike picked up the frail Papa Liu, and carried him at a brisk pace back to the home they had left minutes before. Eldon needed no explanation, as he set to work busily dressing the wound, stitching it up expertly. The two children watched with an intense curiosity, and knowing that Papa Liu was in good hands, Mike headed out to settle things with Wu. His search of the area of the attack yielded nothing, Wu long gone by the time Mike returned. As Mike entered the cabin yet again, he found that Liu was again awake, and now playing gently with the two children. Liu slowly, stiffly stood, nursing the wound in his back. Liu: "Favor repaid, Mike. You have my vow - neither of these children will want for anything. Ever." Mike smiled grimly. He decided to wait until later before telling Liu that Wu had escaped.
