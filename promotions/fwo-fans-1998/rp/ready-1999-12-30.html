Ready

30 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::The scene opens up to a large brown forest. The floor is littered with dying leaves and the sky looks dark and cold. Far off to the right we see a large brick wall, decaying and falling down. As the camera-man walks closer we notice that it's not just a wall, but a small house, with no roof or floor. Inside Wretch sits in the corner, head in hands.:: I can't believe this is happening... ::Wretch sits up, and runs his hands across the decaying walls.:: When I was a child me and my friends built this. We thought it was the greatest thing ever... we'd bring mattresses down here, and we'd pretend it was a great wrestling arena, and all the people would come to see us. ::Wretch kicks at the wall, as dust and pieces of rock falls onto the dirt floor.:: But this wasn't always a place of happiness. When problems got out of hand, this was where I came. When stuff just started piling higher and higher... this was my escape. Sure it was just a bunch of walls, held together by dirt and twigs... but it was mine. It belonged to me, and whenever I needed to get away, I came here. That is until I became a wrestler. Then I was too far away to come here, so I dealt with my problems differently. I took all my emotions, and bundled them into adrenaline. And I would put on the best damn matches of my career... and *nobody* on the card would be able to beat those matches... or me. ::Wretch kicks the walls a little harder, as larger pieces of rock and timber fall down.:: This place is falling apart now. Nobody's been here for at least eight years... and this place, which once experienced every emotion I ever had, has experienced nothing. The problem is, I want to keep it that way. This haven, fort, house... whatever you want to call it... it's time has come. Somewhat like the Dark Riders. They were the biggest thing... then fell... and now they're beginning to rise to power again... ... Not if I can help it. ::Wretch walks across the fort to what seems to be the strongest wall, where it has been left untouched and standing all these years.:: You see... they may have the money... the power... the fame... the strength. Hell yes, the ball is in there court. But like every strong, powerful, indestructible unit... it always has a weakness. ::Wretch pulls a single stone out from the wall, as the entire wall collapses down. Wretch walks to the top of the pile of rubble. He looks down at the camera, sad and angry:: When I was young, I was told something from my father... something that I will always remember... It's not the size of the dog in the fight... but the size of the fight in the dog. And Dark Riders, I'm ready to fight. ::The screen fades to black.::
