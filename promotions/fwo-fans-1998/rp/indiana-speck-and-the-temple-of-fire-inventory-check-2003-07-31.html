Indiana Speck and the Temple of Fire: Inventory Check

31 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On the inside of Death Mountain, there lied a large stone temple, imbeded in the rocky volcanic center. A five floor temple filled with fire, death, magma, volcanic spires, flaming bats, flame dancers, and imprisoned rock beasts known as Gorons. While this wasn't as bad as, say, Beaverton, it was still the closest personification to Hell that there ever was in the Hyrule Kingdom. Yet, the Codemaster was here, and he wasn't liking it. For one thing, in order to actually survive here for more than three minutes, the Codemaster had to wear a ridiculous red tunic that clashed greatly with his usual cape. Not to mention that he only had three main allies in this mission, all of whom were wearing the same tunic, and all of whom were greatly incompetent in their own ways. The first, you may know him, was a blonde-haired elf type who wielded a big, mean broadsword and a shield. Also, he carried with him various weapons, and, well, he was mean about it, too.
