Mega Man Battle Network: Dishwasher Man

3 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After dealing with a chicken farmer and a supposedly heterosexual robot that had a level that was obsessive about penises, the Codemaster wasn't surprised in the least that he had to go through a giant kitchen with forks that acted as spikes and running water and the whole nine yards. Finally, the Codemaster reached the end of the place, armed himself with a freshly-reloaded Fecalator, and entered the room. Now, the background of this place was a whirling laundry machine, which totally mismatched the very nature of the level, and it didn't help that Dishwasher Man himself was busy DOING his laundry, and resembling Air Man from Mega Man 2. The Codemaster glared at him. "You're the boss of this place." "Yuh-huh." Dishwasher said, holding up his hand, "Hold on a second, I gotta iron my pants." "You do realize that I'm here to shove my gun so far up your ass that you'll be flossin' your teeth with gun's barrel, right?" the Codemaster asked. Dishwasher Man paid him no mind, "One, I don't have a crack in my ass for you to insert that gun. Two, I don't have teeth. Three, even if you somehow managed to get that thing through my rectum, your hand would be sliced to pieces by my fanblades. Four, you're a sick freak to be sticking things up other people's asses." There was an awkward pause, "Sorry, I just got through beating Heterosexual Man, and I'm a little disturbed from the expierence, and... say, what do you need the fan blades for?" "Show." Dishwasher Man said, finishing his ironing and slipping his pants on, since he wasn't wearing any pants to begin with. You should be happy that I didn't note this beforehand. Then Dishwasher Man turned toward the Codemaster. "Show? What's your power then?" Dishwasher Man pointed his other hand at the Codemaster, which turned out to be a blaster arm, and shot what appeared to be a dish at the Codemaster. The Codemaster ducked, and the dish broke into pieces behind him. "Oh, man, your mom's gonna bite you if you keep breaking her dishes." the Codemaster said. Dishwasher Man continued firing dishes at the Codemaster, but the Codemaster, fresh off of his first Parappa reference, soon made others. "KICK!" he shouted, kicking a dish back at Dishwasher, "PUNCH!" he shouted, punching another dish out of the way, "It's all in the mind!" Dishwasher Man glared at the Codemaster, "Dear god, stop that right now, or I'll turn this room into spin cycle." The Codemaster stopped and glared back at the Dishwasher Man, "Dishwashers don't have a spin cycle, you frickin' moron." Dishwasher Man stuck his chest forward, and his fan blades turned on. "They do now." And suddenly, the Codemaster went flying around the room, spinning around and around in circles. The Codemaster yelled as he went flying about, as he wasn't really in control of his flying about and hit a few walls. Finally, Dishwasher Man got bored and stopped it. The Codemaster tried to flutter in the air for a few moments before gravity remembered that it had a job to do, and the Codemaster went yelling and falling to the floor, where he landed with a THUD and an "OOF!" If Dishwasher Man had a mouth, he would have smirked. "Had enough?" The Codemaster got up and walked around in circles a few times, before he looked at Dishwasher Man, threw up, then looked at him again, "More fun than I could have in just walkin' through South Central with a 'I'm Actually White, And I Hate All Of Y'all" sign around my neck." "Good. We'll do that again." The fan blades started up, but the Codemaster suddenly pulled out the Cagle Launcher, "Oh, man, the PETA is gonna bite me." He fired a chicken directly at the fan blade. A few horrified clucks, the horrifying and gut-wrenching noise of a chicken's guts being wrenched by a fan blade, and the sound of short circuits later, and Dishwasher Man realized that he had been beaten. Boy, was his face red. You know, from the chicken blood. Dishwasher Man fell over and burst into many pellets. Codemaster sighed in relief, threw up again, and saw the words flash at the top of the room. "GET EQUIPPED WITH DISH SHOOTER." The Codemaster shrugged as he picked up the Dish Shooter, "Well, this is gonna make for a really bad pun, I'm sure." He left. Again.
