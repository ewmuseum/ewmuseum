Flash Back - Countdown to Oblivion 1

2 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He could feel his balance slipping. He could feel Donaven Winters' grip tighten, as did his own. "See you in hell, Winters," said Eli, before the fall. Any response Donaven Winters had was lost in the intensity of the moment. "You're fuckin' crazy, Eli, what the fuck are you doing?" At the moment of impact, Eli Flair was no longer wrestling Donaven Winters at Countdown to Oblivion 1. At the moment of impact, when the table split his head like a melon, Eli was in Sweetwater, Texas, six years ago. "Look at the way ATTAXX is laying into him!" cried Bill Buckley, "I think the Eliminator might have finally met his match!" He was in Chapel Hill, North Carolina, five years ago. "That's sick, Sammy," said Bill Buckley, "That's really sick." "Who cares?" asked Sammy Benson, "My man Troy has the belt now, and the King of Extreme - loser - is getting carted out on the meat wagon." "No hospital, Ivy." He was in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, four years ago. "I never thought I'd see the day when someone was Eli Flair's extreme equal," said Bill Buckley, "but Kevin Powers is earning the nickname 'Emperor of Hardcore' tonight!" He was in Kansas City, Missouri, three years ago. "Between Hornet and Kevin Powers," said Stan Parsons, "between singapore canes and steel plated bats, it's a wonder Eli Flair can even walk out of this arena." "No hospital? Who do you think you are, fuckin' superman? You need stitches. You need to get a professional doctor to put them in. You are not getting a fucking mirror to do it yourself!" He was in Seattle, Washington, two years ago. "Flair and Matt Dexter trade left hands on top of that cage," said Michael Star, and that thing is buckling with the bent and torn corner support. GOOD LORD! Flair with a powerbomb, and that roof section gave way! Eli Flair and Matt Dexter crash to the ring below!" "That was poetry in motion out there, Ivy. That was one of the greatest moments of my life." "Eli, what the hell are you talking about?" "Look at me. Look at the blood running down my face, the blood staining my hair. This is it, Ivy.... this is why I'm in the fWo." "Let's just get you to a hospital, okay?" He would never go to that hospital.
