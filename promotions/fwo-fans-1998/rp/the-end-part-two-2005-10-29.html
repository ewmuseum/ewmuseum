The End - Part Two

29 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Las Vegas had long been considered "Sin City." It couldn't hold a candle to Bangkok. From seventy-three floors up, Bangkok at night was a sight to behold. The lights were as bright and beautiful as any in Las Vegas. It was thing things that went on beneath those lights that set Bangkok apart from Vegas, or anywhere else in the World for that matter. Literally anything could be had for a price. You want to have sex with a man, woman, small child, and a panda bear all at the same time? Done. You want to roll around in a mountain of coke? Done. Nothing was off-limits. Nothing. Rave McBain sat in his wheelchair and looked down at the city below. "Y'know, I used to think this place was just a toilet. Now look how beautiful it is." A gorgeous brunette walked across the darkened room wearing a flimsy black silk teddy. "It's all in how you look at things," Betty whispered into Rave's ear. She brushed her breast against his arm. "Everything looks good from seventy-three floors up." Rave looked down at the action his arm was getting. "If I could feel that, I'd be a happy man. Hard too." He looked back at the window, and saw an extra face in the reflection. "Pupad," he said to the Thai standing in the shadows. "I think I should get a bell put around your neck." Betty didn't stop rubbing up against Rave, a fact not lost on Pupad. "You like what you see?" Pupad cleared his throat. "Uh, sorry sir." Betty stood up, her pert nipples pushing against the fabric of her teddy. "I'll be in the bedroom," she said to Rave. "Come and get me when you're finished here." Rave used the mouth-stick for his chair to turn around. "She's an amazing woman. A demon in the sack too." He smiled at Pupad's uncomfortable expression. "So what's on your mind?" "It's Creed. He knows." The smile disappeared. "He knows? He knows what, exactly?" "Everything. He knows about his daughter. He knows about you." "Betty!" Rave shouted. Pupad didn't hear his employer raise his voice often. When he did, it was considered A Very Bad Thing. "Get the lights on out here!" Betty had thrown on a white tiger-skinned bathrobe and turned on a set of lights in their suite. "What's wrong?" "Pupad here was just telling me that Vic knows all about what's been going on." "Oh really," she said as she glared at Pupad. "And how does Vic know?" "I was just about to ask him that very thing." Rave looked at Pupad. "Well?" "You said you wanted him tailed, so I send two of my best men to follow him in Japan. He found them and tortured them until they gave up their information." The lack of expression on Rave's face was almost as disturbing to Pupad as if he'd appeared angry. "Someone must have told him he was being followed," he offered as an explanation. "Of course he knew he was being followed!" Rave snapped. He blew into the straw, mounted beside his immobile head. The chair hummed forward and almost ran over Pupad's toes. "He always thinks he's being followed," Rave's voice took on a raspy tone. "Creed's got himself so jacked up, his paranoia is the only thing bigger than his ego." Betty followed behind Rave as he navigated his chair back to the window. "So when he grabbed your boys... they cracked?" he asked as he looked out over the city. "He crushed their arms and legs with a big rock. It took almost a day to do." Rave rolled his eyes. "Yeah, that sounds like the kind of thing he'd do." Betty didn't find the situation as amusing as he did. "What do we do now?" she asked, a nervous tone unmistakable in her voice. Rave looked at her in the reflection of the window and smiled. "I guess we'll just have to move the schedule up." That seemed to comfort her, and she returned his smile. Pupad's nerves were less on edge, now that he knew what had to be done. Rave's instructions were clear when he said, "Vic Creid doesn't go back to the States... unless it's in a pine box."
