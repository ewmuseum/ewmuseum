"What'cha doing buddy?" "Hey Daddy!" Sean drops his actions figures as he looks up from the edge of his bed. Seeing his dad for the first time in a few days Sean was always bubbling over with excitement. As the miniature wrestlers scattered on the floor he ran to Brandon, hugging him tightly. Wow, that sure felt good. It's amazing what something so small can do for you. A simple hug and it felt like all of his worries were washed away. Almost. Brandon looked down to the haphazardly strewn toys and his eyes focused on one in particular. The Boston Strangler. Sean was saying something now but Brandon was focused on that small piece of molded plastic. When did he get that? Why would Ana allow him to buy something resembling that monster? "... so can he stay Daddy, can he?" "Hmm? What Sean, I'm sorry?" "Troy dropped Bobby off for the afternoon so he could go wrestle or sumptin. He wants to stay tonight. Can he?" Bobby... Troy Knight. Oh. OK. That little piece of twisted plastic must belong to him. Wow, he hadn't heard from Troy in over three months. Soon after his father got out of the hospital they moved back to Paris. What were they doing back in the States? Oh well, questions he would find out sure enough sooner or later. It didn't really matter now anyway. He looked around the room a bit at the stuff that hung on Sean's wall. The old Boston Strangler poster was ripped down and crumpled in the corner. Now hanging in its sacred place was an old poster of "The Avatar" Hemish McCulwch, Chris's old gimmick back in the E.W.A. days. He smiled a bit at his seven year old sons attempt at honoring his uncle. "Sure bud. When is Troy supposed to be back?" "By five." "Ok good. Where's Bobby?" "Downstairs with Momma.
