Birth Of Ignorance [1] World Wrestling Champions

1 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
And so ignorance was born. You never really notice some things until you experience them, how ironic that I needed to lose an eye before I could truly see. It's easy to discover when you're different, you learn a lot about the human race. In a world where a black sheep can remain in the flock and an albino lion can live with the pride, a human being can be exiled and abandoned by his own kind. Some people can mask their ignorance and even deny it to the death. My father didn't even try to conceal it. January 10th 1994. "Happy new year, son." Vince Kinkade's voice echoed through the room with a typical lack of sincerity as Kellen Kinkade pushed open the door to his office and made his way in "Take a seat, how's the injury doing?" "If it's alright with you, I'd rather stand and keep it quick." Kellen began in a rather snappy manor "My new year went as well as a new year can go when you spend it alone, apparently nobody wanted to sing about auld acquaintance being forgot. Kind of ironic to hear that song being sung on the eve of this year when you're a face from the past that nobody wants to remember." "Come on now boy, you know everyone has been thinking about you." Vince shifted through some papers on his desk, all the time under the suspect eye of Kellen who felt he was merely doing it to avoid engaging him. "Sure they have, in their nightmares." Kellen replied with a scowl "And why you enquire about my injury puzzles me, didn't I specifically tell you on the phone that my injury was completely healed back in November?" "I'm not sure, I don't remember." Came a quick reply from Vince. "Kind of convenient, and while the doctors haven't cleared me to wrestle I've been ready to go since the last time we spoke. Why haven't you started booking me yet?" Kellen sighed with disappointment as his father continued to try and dodge the conversation, now searching in his drawer for something. "About that, Kellen." Vince said as he stopped what had been a search for nothing in particular "I know you want to come back but, come on now." "Come on now? What the hell is that supposed to mean." An angered Kellen shot back at Vince. "It means." Vince took a deep breath "You've had a bad injury boy, a career ending injury... there's no need for you to come back when the insurance will pay your wages for as long as you desire. If you step into a ring they'll stop those disability benefits before you know it, so why don't you just go home and relax." "I've been relaxing for the last four fucking months, in fact I've had about enough relaxing to last me a lifetime... I don't want disability benefits because I'm notdisabled." Kellen replied with another growl, refusal to give up burning from within. "Fair comment." Vince replied with another deep breath that suggested he was about to say something that he didn't want to "Disability aside... World Wrestling Champions is a family promotion, there are children in the crowd that shouldn't have to see... they shouldn't have to see... you know." "Just say it." Kellen grunted. "They shouldn't have to see your disfigurement." The WWC owner added after some reluctance. "I keep telling you, the scars will heal in time." Desperation was now creeping into the voice of Kellen, who knew that once his father had something set in his mind it was often hard to change his opinion. "Time I don't have, son." Vince spoke back "We can't subject a young audience to something that could upset them, they'd be scared out of their wits." "They wouldn't be the only ones." Kellen growled stepping forth and placing his hands down on the table in front of his father, his frame had grown significantly larger in the several months he'd spent getting back in ring worthy shape. "Just what are you implying?" Vince said with his head bowed. "It's not an implication it's a statement based on fact, you haven't looked me straight in the face since I walked in here." Kellen moved closer to his father who continued to focus on a different area of the room. "Why that's nonsense, of course I have." Vince said, quickly flashing his glance across Kellen and over to the other side of the room. "What's the matter, don't you want to look at the beautiful picture you helped paint?" Kellen's voice took a more sinister turn, a provocative statement that had his father clenching his fists. "You cheeky son of a bitch, I should-" "WHAT?" Kellen roared "You should what? Take the belt to me like when I was a little boy? Not anymore old man." Several moments of awkward silence passed as the veins in Kellen's arms bulged with the heavy amount of adrenaline driven blood flowing through them. "This is your chance to make up for everything you've brought upon me, dad." Kellen's voice calmed "Just look me in the eye and tell me that I can come back, we'll forget about everything else." Another few moments of awkward silence passed before finally Vince sat back down in his seat, promptly swivelling it so that he faced completely away from his son to look out of the window. Kellen's face grew red with fury as he slowly stepped backward. "I won't forget this... not ever." He snarled whilst moving toward the door "Just remember Vince, history has a cruel way of repeating itself... the day might come where you need me to look you in the eye and show compassion... ...I'll be there to look away."
