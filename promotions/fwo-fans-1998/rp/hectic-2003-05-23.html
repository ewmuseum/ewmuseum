Hectic

23 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Norman, I'm telling you for the last time. There's nothing to discuss." Kodiak Vic Creed sat in the plush lounging chair and stared out the balcony window. He cradled the phone between his shoulder and his ear as he poured himself another drink. He took a quick glance at the nearly empty bottle, surprised it had disappeared so quickly, and set it down on the end table beside him. Vic's youngest brother Norman wasn't about to let up on his sibling. "Vic, nobody blames you for missing Mom's funeral. But you should at least come down for the reading of the Will." Creed sipped the amber liquid and let it burn its way down his throat. "Look Norm, Mom's dead. My showing up isn't going to change that. And besides, I don't want anything to do with the Will." Finishing off his drink in two large gulps, Vic set his glass down and rose from his seat. "Did Marilyn make it out to the funeral?" He walked around the dark hotel suite, navigating around the furniture only by the moonlight shining through the large balcony window. "She got in the day before. I got her hooked up with a really good wheelchair too. Heck, it's better than mine." Vic could almost hear his brother's smile. The kid was never down. Never. Despite all the crap life had dumped on him, he kept bouncing back with a determined smile. "She wouldn't be in that chair if it wasn't for me," the elder Creed told the younger. "You know that." "If she wasn't in that chair," Norman countered, "she wouldn't have met Phil." Vic blinked and found himself getting irritated with the conversation. Large quantities of both stress and alcohol could do that to a person. "And who the fuck is this Bill person?" Norman corrected his brother. "Phil, not Bill. He's Marilyn's physical therapist. I haven't seen her that happy in, well ever actually." Stopping in his tracks, Vic rubbed his forehead and ran his fingers through his growing white locks. He wasn't sure if he wanted to know the answer to his next question. "Did she say anything about me?" "She asked if you were going to show up. She knew you had a big match this week and didn't know if you could make it." She's keeping tabs on me? Vic thought to himself. He couldn't believe it. He wouldn't believe it. Someone must've told her about his match this past Wednesday. Yeah, that made sense. Vic was responsible for having her crippled. Why the Hell would she care what he was doing? He picked up the bottle, swirling the remaining fluid at the bottom. He slid the glass door open and stepped out onto the balcony and into the cool night air. There was a moment of static on the cordless phone as Vic leaned against the railing. The best suite the hotel had gave the big man a gorgeous view of the city, especially from twenty floors up. Lights of different colors sparkled in the night and made the cityscape look like a giant Christmas tree. The breeze even seemed to carry the scent of fresh forest air. Creed found himself wishing he were back at his cabin in the mountains, away from everyone and everything. There was too much going on at once; his head was spinning. Everything seemed out of control. The one thing the Alaskan Juggernaut needed was control. He lost control during his match on Wednesday. Lost it big time. Scott Collins needed a whole mess of stitches after their match. Fox and the others came down on him, and as much as it pissed him off he knew they were right. He had a short fuse and was as strong as a bull. They tolerated him wrestling a little stiffer than most. But nobody was going to put up with Vic going out and blatantly injuring people. The weight of the fWo hung on his massive shoulders. He had to prove he could carry the load, especially now that they were going to a new network and facing new competition on a national level. Vic had to forget about everything pulling at him and focus on one thing: keeping himself in check. Bad things happened when he lost his temper
