Coupling - 2 of 2

7 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Orlando, Florida. 2227 Murkirk Lane. “Clothes? Check. Itinerary? Check. Toothbrush? Where's my toothbrush?” Sean “Triple X” Stevens looked around the apartment nervously, seconds from having another infamous anxiety attack, the same exact anxiety attack he has whenever he has to pack for travel. "Check,” said Poison Ivy, his better half, as she handed him the missing toothbrush. “What would you do without me?” “I'd definitely miss my flight, that's for sure, mama bear,” Sean responded, scooping her up in his arms, before kissing her gently on the forehead. “Daddy! Daddy! My turn!” 'You bet it's your turn, little man!” Stevens exclaimed, carefully tossing their two year old, baby Shannon Joseph Stevens in the air, who laughed so hard, his face turned red. Sean held him in the air while simultaneously ticking him with his fingers, causing the young boy to squeal. “Hahahahahah!” “What do you say?” Trip asked, continuing to tickle Shannon in the process. “King daddy! King daddy!” Trip let up, and Shannon immediately scrambled away and hid behind Ivy's leg, though he peeked out from his hiding place, still laughing. "Don't get me involved in this," said Ivy, as she picked her son up in her arms and kissed him on the cheek, "Are you trying to get Daddy to tickle me instead?" "Yes!" replied Shannon. "That's my boy!" said Trip. Ivy gave him the evil eye, though there was no actual malice behind it. She sat Shannon down on the edge of the bed and leaned over so they were at eye level. "Did you pick out the books and toys you want to bring?" "Uh huh," said Shannon. "Okay, then go get your pajamas and I'll be in in a little while to help you." "Okay Mommy," replied Shannon as he hopped to the floor and scurried as fast as his two year old legs could take him around his father, who faked toward him and made him laugh again. He was two steps outside the room when he turned back. "Mommy, is Mariella coming?" Ivy's eyes met Trip's. "No, honey, I think Mariella is staying with Aunt Angel this trip." "I'm gonna marry her someday!" declared the toddler, before he turned around again. Trip grinned, “Who does he remind you of?” he asked. “I think I was the one who told you you'd have my child, two seconds into meeting you. You, of course, shot me down ... dare I say ... even laughing at the notion.” Trip's grin turned into a full on ear-to-ear smile. “... that didn't work out so well, did it?” She stared at the ceiling over the top of her glasses for a moment and appeared deep in thought. "Well, I have to watch my language, I can't drink as much as I used to, and I have to actually watch what I eat now," she exhaled sharply, "But I'm not complaining." Sean laughed out loud, before calming himself, and grabbing Poison Ivy by the hand. “But, in all seriousness, momma bear ... how're you feeling? What's going on in that busy head of yours? Any regrets about going to Seattle?” "You know what?" asked Ivy, as she sat down on the bed, absent mindedly folding clothes, "Yeah. I have a lotta regrets about it." Sean started to talk, but Ivy held up her hand, "Don't get me wrong, I know why Eli made the deal, and I love the fact that you both pushed me to go, but it just seems wrong somehow." "How's that?" asked Sean. "The line of succession was there. Beaven opened the company and he specifically left Fox, Smooth, Twitch, and Thumper in charge when he bought it back from Dudley and the board. All of a sudden Fox is gone and we're left holding the bag, and sometimes I felt like I was the only person who was trying to maintain the integrity of what he would've wanted to do with that company." She sighed as she picked a bag off the floor and placed it behind her, "And when they gave me the chance to run with the ball, everything collapsed." Ivy started to pack the bag, trying her best to look away from Trip. Trip sighed. “Ivy how long did you have the keys to the kingdom before it collapsed?” Before she could respond, he continued. “Everything that happened was already set into motion before you took the responsibility. It doesn't matter if it were you, Fox, Beaven, Merritt, or Thomas, for that matter ... nobody would've been able to save that place. Not even superwoman.” Trip playfully brushed his index finger against Ivy's chin. “Stop being so hard on yourself. You're five foot nothing, weighing about a buck ten, with a legendary bad knee ... what you did was not only courageous, standing up to those thugs ... it was admirable. And, what you're doing now ... with the condo for the youngin's? ...You're one of a kind, Ivy McGinnis, and don't you let anyone or anything make you feel otherwise." She smirked. "I'm five foot three, Sean, and I haven't been that skinny in probably twenty years." "Is that the smile I love?" "Stop it." "C'mon." "No, it's not." "Yes it is," said Trip, egging her on, "There it is." She finally broke, and smiled at him, which burst into a small laugh. "Fine," she said, "You win again. It's just gonna be hard stepping into that locker room again, there's a lotta ghosts swirling about." He smiled. “The funny thing about ghosts is, they only haunt you if you allow them to exist. Besides, I'm here to hold your hand through it, if need be. So is Eli ... E's been there, and he'll continue to be there. There's not a ghost on the planet that'll want to screw with a pissed off 'blue-eyed badass', when my backups the King of Extreme.” "I'm gonna need it, I think," said Ivy, "I'm hardly the Psycho B-word anymore. Damn, do you realize I haven't been on a full wrestling schedule in four years?" "There's the band." "Garden's a completely different animal," she replied, "Plus when they do it right, nobody's bleeding at the end of the night." Trip shot her a skeptical look. "Angel's collisions with Teej notwithstanding," conceded Ivy, "But besides that, have you seen the roster? I don't know half of these guys anymore. Talk about not having my finger on the pulse anymore." She picked a folded piece of paper off the night table and held it, still folded, in front of her, "For all the stressing I'm doing about walking back into the fWo, it might as well be a different company." "But I'm being selfish... are you happy about going back? Is this what you want, or are you just trying to keep an eye on me?" “Honestly, it's been a long time since the wrestling business took precedence over you. I won't even insult you by lying to you. Especially when you already know the truth. But I also have a lot to prove as well. This'll be my last go 'round. My last effort at grabbing the brass ring. The last chance I may get to make you proud of me.” It was Sean who was now a little misty, looking down, face full of insecurities. And, this time, it was Ivy who gently massaged his chin, lifting his face eye level to hers. “Sean, I will always be proud of you. You need to know that.” Suddenly, a voice shot out from the background. “Me too!” “Oh is that right, little buddy?!” Trip quickly cheered up. “Proud of me for what?” “--for being the best fighter EVER!” Shannon made a little muscle. “I'll take it,” Trip responded, giving his son a hug. Ivy stood as well, and wrapped her arms around both her boys. Trip looped one of his around her while he held Shannon aloft with the other, "Me too," she said.
