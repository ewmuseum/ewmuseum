Better late than never

15 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Better late than never March 15, 2000 (The camera fades in on an hotel room in Baltimore. Its nicely furnished with two large beds, a desk with a computer (which happens to be on), a couch, an armchair, loads of room, a minibar, and a large tv on which Wretch is watching HBO. We hear some moaning and the camera catches a glimpse of a barely clad female before Wretch quickly changes the channel to NBC (home of Wednesday Warfare!) when he sees new fWo camerawoman Kristen Jacobs walk in the door. Wretch looks flustered) Wretch: Ho...How did you get in here? Kristen: The door was propped open, we have an interview scheduled, I figured you were expecting me. Wretch: Oh. The door was open because Bear went to get some ice. Kristen: So hell be right... (Kristen is interrupted by Mike coming back in the room, bucket of ice in hand) Mike: Hey Chris, you check the website? Ralgin told me the card would be posted sometime around now. Wretch (rolls his eyes and breathes deep, speaks somewhat angrily): No Mike, I didnt check it, you were only gone 2 minutes. Mike: Are you ok? I thought we got past this. We have to work together if we want that rematch for the Tag Titles. I still think we can get it for Cyberslam if we just work like we did before. (Wretch mutters something about carrying his half of the team) Speaking of cards, Im going to check the site. Have a soda, man. (Mike sits down at the desk and clicks on the connection. While it dials up, Wretch walks over to the minibar and grabs a Dr. Pepper, obviously not taking sides in the Pepsi vs. Coke war. The connection becomes active and Mike goes to the fWo website, clicking on the preview of Wednesday Warfare) Hey! Check this out Chris, I got a title match and I didnt even ask for one. A shot at the International title. I really didnt think I was in contention for a singles title, but whatever. (Wretch mutters something about he deserving a title shot more than Mike) Hey, didnt you beat Douglas for the Cruiserweight belt? Any suggestions? Wretch (rolling his eyes again): Not off the top of my head. Ask me tomorrow. Mike: Ok, man, just take your...hey Chris! Did you volunteer to take on that bastard Frenchie for me? Thanks man! Wretch: Did I what?! Mike: Look here, (points to screen) youre facing the Frenchie. (Wretch mutters something about having to fight Mikes battles) Wretch (obviously making this up): Oh yeah, I did volunteer to fight the Frenchie because I know how much you hate him. Mike: Thanks Chris, Ill remember this. Wretch (muttering): Yeah sure you will. Mike: Oh man, in all the excitement I almost forgot this lovely lady in our room. Kristen Jacobs, right? Kristen: Thank you very much, yes Im Kristen. Mike: Well, Im certainly glad to have you on board here. We certainly werent expecting you. Usually Geoff interviews us and we watch a...wait, did you see what Wretch had on? Wretch (grins): Yeah, did you see that? Kristen: Well... (thinks better of it) no I didnt see anything. Wretch: Thats too bad. Mike: Itd be bad for me. (cringes) Hope Brittany isnt watching this. Id better call her after this. Hey, I almost forgot, this is an interview, how about some questions? Kristen: Questions? Mike: Well, yeah. You knew you were here to interview us, right? Kristen: Well...no. That wasnt in my job description. Mike: Ok, I guess Geoff didnt talk to you. Sure, weve got a lot of interviewers, but most are conducted by Geoff, our primary cameraman, mostly because Locos too annoying, Finns too busy, everyone hates Armstrong, and Roberts and Frankton are new to interviewing. Anyway, you should know how to interview guys, because some people arent as nice as we are, especially to women, not to mention any names *cough*frenchie. (clears throat) Must have allergies or something. Anyway, first, you have to know what the guy youre interviewing has been doing. For instance, Wretch and I have been trying to regain the Tag Team titles as well as putting that cheating, fiancé-stealing Frenchie in his place. (Wretch mumbles something about Mike being the one making enemies with Frenchie) Then you should ask them about any matches they have scheduled or challenges that are open or directed to them. Then, usually ask them if they have any other comments. Some guys are different though. Just dont let guys push you around. Kristen: Thanks, Ill remember that. Mike: Well, lets get down to... (looking past Kristen into the hallway) Geoff! There you are! (Geoff Green walks in from the hallway, since the door was left open this whole time. Geoff takes a look at Kristen, then walks up to Mike) Geoff (whispering): Ummm, Mike, whats she doing here? Mike (whispering): We scheduled an interview. Geoff (whispering): But why now? Mike (whispering): We figured youd be the one doing it, and since youd be here anyway... Geoff (whispering): Ah crap, someone must have scheduled her, not knowing I always do your interviews. Mike (whispering): Well, could you help her out, apparently no one told her shed be conducting interviews in addition to using the camera. Geoff: Well yeah, itd be my job anyway. Lets get started. Mike: Kristen, watch Geoff, this is what you should do. Geoff (walking behind the camera): Ok, Mike, you and Wretch have had your differences recently. Care to comment? Mike: Well, I dont really see it that way. Weve lost those tag matches by accident, and were reworking our chemistry. I think everythings going to work out fine. (Wretch mumbles something like For one of us) I think well be wrestling at Cyberslam for those titles the Apes have. Geoff: But what about the dark room... Mike: Weve talked about that. Geoff: Ok...what about this whole deal with the Frenchie. Mike: Chris, youve got him on Wednesday, you want this time? Wretch (rolls his eyes): No Mike, go ahead, hes all yours. Mike: Ok, whatever you want. The deal with the Frenchie is this: Pierre tried to steal UVs future wife , and I couldnt stand by as that frog tried to steal Clarences princess. I may not look like an American hero or whatever, but I stand for certain morals. Frenchie, I know you say, What the Frenchie wants, Frenchie gets. Well buddy, when it involves The Underground, you get denied. We took away the last title you needed for the Grand Slam, and were taking away your chance to ruin UVs wedding. Last Wednesday, you think you won. Well, Pierre, go ahead, give the tape to UV, or better yet, watch it yourself, youd appreciate it more. Geoff: What are you saying Mike? You think he should give Ultra Violet the tape? Mike: Geoff, do you really think Im stupid enough to wave the tape around in Frenchies face so he could grab it? Geoff: Ooooooh (grins). How about this match you have with Matt Douglas? Mike: Yeah, how about that? A singles title shot, its certainly been a long time since Ive had one of those. Anyway, I just want to say good luck to you Douglas. Youll need it, because Im bringing everything I got. Geoff: Well, this looks about wrapped up. Anything else Mike? Mike: Yes, one more thing. Follow me. (Mike grabs a bread knife and puts it into his pocket, then he goes over and grabs a foil wrapped circular object out of the mini fridge. On his way out the door, he grabs a t-shirt hanging on the end of the bed. He walks down the hallway and counts off the numbers silently. Finally he comes to room 348. Mike knocks on the door) Person inside: Who is it? Mike (in a high voice): Housekeeping! Person inside: Do you think Im that stupid? Mike: Just open the damn door! (The door is opened to reveal Jeremiah Frankton, dressed only in a pair of jeans and a white t-shirt) Jeremiah: What do you wan... (Jeremiah is interrupted by Mike throwing the t-shirt he had over Jeremiahs head. We can now see it reads Feareth the Undereth Ground) Mike: Hey Jer, I brought something for you. I know how much you like pie... Jeremiah: That had better not be... Mike: So I got Brittany to make this pie for you. Jeremiah: Mike, please tell me thats not what I thin... Mike: Its not cherry pie, its not banana cream pie, its not blackberry pie. Its... Jeremiah: Please no. Mike: APPLE PIE! Have a slice of down home America, Jeremiah! (Mike unwraps the pie and begins the cut a slice out of the home made treat. Jeremiah grabs the shirt off his head and throws it on the ground) Jeremiah: I hate you. (Jeremiah retreats back into his room and slams the door) Mike: Well thats all we have for today, make sure to tune in next time folks! (The camera fades to black as Mike waves goodbye)
