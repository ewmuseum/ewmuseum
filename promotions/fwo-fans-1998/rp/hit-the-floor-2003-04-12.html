Hit The Floor

12 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last Friday... When Amy finally woke up from her first night of actual sleep since Sunday, her head still hadn't cleared. Nightmares had cluttered and disturbed her rest, not to mention the nausea and headaches she was being put through thanks to a few straight days of nothing but strong alcohol that had only ended the afternoon before, when she'd finally been able to see Brandon with her own eyes - physically and mentally okay...not dead, not lost. Initially, she'd found herself exquisitely alone waking up but within moments she was wandering out of the small, messy room and into the open - perfectly displaying the "You know I just crawled out of bed look" in a pair of purple plaid pajama pants and a simple black tanktop, hair falling every which way...most notably, right over her eyes. And even though she'd expected to find Brandon on the phone to his kids, it came as no surprise when the redhead walked out into the living room of the loft to find him stretching on an old Judo mat he'd rolled out. Regardless, her smile was warm and the one returned was even sweeter. "You really don't go a day without working, do you?" Amy smirked sleepily, tying back scruffy hair as she watched Brandon 'going through the motions' on the mat she'd actually forgotten she had. "I don't go a day without playing. If this was work I wouldn't be doing it. Plus... I figured it would be yet another way I get to rub up on you and not get in trouble." The boyscout routine washed away every once in awhile. When it did it was normally with humorous results as Brandon tried not to act like the stuffed shirt everyone assumed him to be. With a smile, chuckle and shake of her head, Amy slowly but surely made her way into the open living room, standing across from the Paragon with hands on her hips. "I could use some extra practice...but I dunno, you sure things won't get awkward when you're on your stomach crying like a little girl?" She laughed a little again and stepped onto the mat, folding her arms and raising an eyebrow expectantly at Brandon. With a wink, she spoke again at the equally perky Mighty Hero, "Never know, though...I just got out of bed, maybe I'm sleepy enough to let myself go easy on you." "Easy?" Brandon smirked. "I knew you were easy... Eugene told me." Using the dirtiest trick in the book Brandon had hit a new low. Amy's eyes widened as the realization set in. By that time though 'Brand Frontier' had already made the first move and with a slight push on her shoulder and step in with the left foot the redhead was crashing to the mat. Amy had her own trick in mind, and used her position on the ground to kick down one of Brandon's ankles. As he went crashing down, she hopped to her feet rather gracefully for someone who, outside of a wrestling 'environment', couldn't chew gum and walk without breaking a bone. "You guys're always so into this trash talking, big manly ego thing...sometime you'll figure out that it only makes it more fun for us to see you on the floor.", she finished with a wink. The wink didn't escape Brandon's notice as he rolled to his back and kipped up to his feet, belying the affect gravity should have on his 270 pound form. "I let you do that." Humbled a bit by the fiery Superstar Brandon circled, looking for an opening. Amy guarded herself like the pro she was and with every advance from Brandon she blocked it expertly. Finally realizing another distraction was in order the Paragon shot out another bit of the trash talk Amy spoke of. "The way I see it Aimz if either of us is on our backs then the other is going to have just as much fun." She immediately fired off with an attempted kick to his 'extreme lower abdomen', but ended up with an ankle in Brandon's hand. At that point there was little else to do, so the redhead went for a sort of heel kick...only to have the other foot grabbed, landing hard enough on her back to hurt despite the mat. "Ow, damnit!!!" "My... what a predicament we have here." A sly, almost sinister grin suplanted the focused look Brandon sported mere seconds before. "Need me to kiss it and make it feel better?" "Fuck off...that actually hurt!" Amy pouted, trying to kick free of the grip he had around her legs. "Now you're just being mean." Releasing Amy's legs at a specific time, Brandon managed to avoid the thrashing and Amy huffed as she got to her feet, holding her back slightly. "Uhoh... I've pissed you off now..." Amy didn't respond. Instead she turned like she was angry enough to stop their little sparring session. Throwing Brandon off balance just enough, she quickly turned and landed across his chest with a still elbow. Her light frame landing with enough impact to push 'Brand Frontier' off his feet, landing him on his back this time. Quickly, she managed to roll him over while the wind was knocked out of him, grabbing legs that seemed to each be as thick around as her entire waist. Locking them together, Amy grinned to herself as she actually managed to lift slightly, leaning onto his back to apply just enough pressure to cause a pain. "Jesus," She panted, still obviously having to make a real effort to lift his lower body even slightly, "First it was Sean, and now you're my bitch..." Pulling his shoulder in and rolling his arm underneath, Brandon managed to twist a little, interupting Amy's retort. Concentrating on his next move though he didn't verbally respond to her insult. Instead he pulled his heel back to the base of his butt and pushed. It was enough that Amy stuck a hand back to steady herself, and that was all Brandon needed. With the one leg marginally free he pulled the other to him which brought Aimz close enough for him to lock an arm around her collarbone. "I'm yours sweetness... although I don't think bitch is the right word." Adding insult to injury Brandon kissed the top of Amy's head as he finally used his newfound leverage and powered out, patting her on the head as he did. He'd had never really taken note of her rather extreme flexibility, though, and the look on his face was that of surprise when her heel was at the base of his neck, pulling his head and shoulders down far enough to lighten up the grip he had, allowing for the lithe redhead to squirm her way out of his clutch. She didn't spare even a moment before having one of his arms pushed sideways against his back, pinned under her slight weight and the other pulled backward by her arms. "Smartass," Amy laughed in kind, leaning so she could kiss the top of his head to mock him like he'd done to her. "You don't do your homework on people, do you?" She chuckled again before going back to pulling Brandon's arm up, still straddling his back over the other. "I have you right where I want you." Amy laughed and accidentally freed him in doing so. Unfortunately for her, Brandon took the opportunity to flip over, ending up with Amy landing harder than expected on his stomach, knocking the wind out of him. "Okay, maybe now you do...but be careful what you say, all I have to do is lean back on my elbow and you'll be needing a Viagra prescription for life." "I've got other thoughts, though. Why don't you lean this way and give me a kiss...?" The sugary sweet smile sealed the deal as Amy bit her lower lip. It always caught her unawares when Brandon spoke of such things. He was almost turning her in to a prude by osmosis. She let go and slid around, ending with her face mere inches from his. He closed his eyes as she did hers in expectation. That's when it happened. The kiss. Then the flip. And Amy layed flat on her back with Brandon pinning her down. "You cheap fucker!" She yelled instantly, batting him on the shoulders, "That has to be more illegal than ANY knee to the crotch!" Amy tried, and tried...and tried some more, but it was quite a challenge to push a 270 pound man off of her, especially when she wasn't entirely sure she wanted him off. "You...just......SUCK!" "I lick, too." Sticking his tongue out in a playful manner Brandon relented his cheap shot and scampered off of her. Returning to his feet quickly he knew he was in for it now. He circled her, hands outstretched to be ready for her next move, knowing it was going to fueled by a bit of resentment. Amy regained her footing a split second later and jolted forward deliberately, seeing Brandon's guard get thrown into trying to block whatever attack he thought she was attempting, it gave perfect time for her to hop up while he was bent forward a little, wrapping her legs around his neck to execute a rather tight frankensteiner, knocking the wind straight out of the Paragon. She stood above him rather triumphantly while he caught his breath, giggling. "Hey...didn't know I could do that in here. Teach you to fake me out!" "From my vantage point though... I'm right where I want to be..." "Bite me." She came down hard with a leg drop to Brandon's gut, leaving him gasping for breath yet again. At the same time, she managed to plant herself on his chest, pinning his arms down with her knees. "I win. And since when did you stop being Mr. Chastity?" "Who said anything about being chaste? I just like to make sure I don't regret anything. Some people call it being careful, I call it living life with few regrets." "Oh please..." Amy took a moment to tug at the tuft of goatee at his chin, "You yaaaaaap and yaaap and yaaaap about playing everything safe. Y'know, you can't learn from mistakes unless you make a few...and I'd much rather get my mistakes made as early in life as possible." "I've made mine. Now all I want... is you." The redhead laughed aloud, not as sarcastically as she normally would have, though. "C'mon, not falling for that again...you're not getting up until you tell me why you're so terrified of living life." "Why do you think I'm terrified of living life? I've got two wonderful kids already, a successful business outside of wrestling, I've got a home. I like to party, I just don't need to lose myself when I do it all." "Oh yeah, you're a real partier, alright. I saw that New Year's Eve...two whole glasses of Guiness, and I think I almost saw you having fun once! Having a 'successful business'," She mocked his tone somwhat, "And 'two wonderful kids' isn't living...you just go through the motions, don't want anything to draw you off of that path of a 'perfect', safe, so-called life." "I don't measure my living by the trail of empty cans I leave behind. There's no crime in that. Two wonderful kids IS living. Have yours disappear for two months, kidnapped, and tell me he's not your life. I think, Miss Amy...you mistake my ease with myself as compliance." Amy leaned in face-to-face with him, loose strands of hair falling forward and mingling with Brandon's. "This isn't about aquiescence, it's about diluted ideals of what you think is the right thing for you. NOTHING can pull you away from being Mister Hero, Mister Goody Goody...Mister blah blah blah. You sacrifice the majority of the opportunities you have just to uphold a bunch of idealistic imagery." "And you might think you're as happy as you can possibly be..." She continued, "But I know for a fact that you'd be happier taking even an occasional chance, or doing something for you and you alone." "What would you have me do? Turn my back on how I was raised? Turn away from my father and ignore who I am. I do good, I feel good. I can't make it much simpler than that." Brandon smiled, wanting to let Amy understand he wasn't bothered by the sudden change in conversation. "I had wild days in high school. Horror of horrors I even skipped enough school to have to attend two summer school programs. About the drinking thing... my Dad was an alchoholic, it runs in the family. He'd gotten things on track by the time I was old enough to have memories but I've heard the stories." "What chance do you want me to take that you think I wouldn't? What can I do for you, to you or whatever to let you know that I'm not this stuffed shirt nerd that needs saving?" "Nothing! It's just..." Amy struggled with words for a good twenty seconds, then sighed. "You're so safe. Someday I'm going to have you take an entire twenty four hours following at least HALF of your instincts. Life's a lot more fun if you follow urges, they're actually what you want to do. Crazy stuff, that." She groaned, throwing her hands down against his chest in frusteration. "I don't know, just someday stray away from your comic book superhero regimine?" A smile was all she got in reply. Grabbing Amy by the nape of the neck, Brandon suddenly pulled her to him. She fought back at first, not realizing his intentions, fully expecting him to have lulled her in to another false sense of security but instead she found the Mighty Hero kissing her. It startled her enough that she didn't return the embrace at first, then the warmth washed over her and she melted in to his arm. Pulling back away and with her eyes still dreamingly closed Brandon planted tongue firmly in cheek. "But don't you know Aimzy-baby... you're my kryptonite?!?" At the same time, both had the same thought: Way to prove you're not a nerd! Maybe tomorrow night you can woo her with some Star Trek quotes! Amy finally collected herself and tried not to laugh, just in case Brandon had actually been attempting to be serious. "Way to go, Superman." She grinned, "Quoting crappy pop rock songs might work on other girls...but I'm not quite so radio-friendly." Smiling and chuckling, she stood up on surprisingly shakey legs, offering Brandon a helping hand to ease himself up but he didn't accept, retaining his 'manly' demeanor by hopping up to his feet triumphantly. "Pfft. You know you're melting over it." "Nope, but I'm drying up pretty quickly." She winked. Brandon shuddered instantly, and knocked the tiny woman back onto the mat in mock anger at her remark. More play fighting ensued and Amy discovered something she'd barely suspected before... Brandon Roche actually could be caught having normal fun.
