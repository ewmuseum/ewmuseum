Lack of Conscience

10 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Finally, the fWo flies me home to fix things with Clarisse. It was a long flight, but then again, time always seems to slow down when you're in anticipation. I arrive at my huge beach house in Fort Lauderdale. I can hear the constant tapping of rain on the top of the taxi cab, drops of water spilling down the windows. I get out of the cab and get my bag from the trunk. When I get to the front door I see mine and Clarisse's personal bodyguard, a big black guy who calls himself Teddy. He's got his monstrous arms crossed over his equally monstrous chest. Next to him is a suitcase with the keys to the Range Rover on top of it. "What's this?" I say. "Clarisse wants you out of the house. She made reservations for you at the Marriott hotel downtown. She told me to tell you to take the Range Rover and stay there for tonight." "No," I tell the big fucker. "I need to talk to her tonight. That's why I came here. I don't make pointless trips, Teddy." Teddy takes the keys and hands them over to me. "Please leave, Mr. Williams. This is for the best." Suddenly Clarisse appears in the window up above. "Just go, Cody. I don't even want to see you right now. I don't want you around me." I fall to my knees, the rain plastering my hair to my face and clothes to my body. "Clarisse, we have to talk. We can make it through this. We've got to do this for Matilda." "Don't you dare bring her into this," says Clarisse. "Get in the Range Rover and get the hell away from us!" Oh boy, she's really starting to make me look pathetic. "Clarisse, I don't want to let us fall apart like this." "You already have." She's actually kind of right. "I love you, Clarisse. There's no other woman I want to be with other than you. I've made mistakes, I've done stupid things, and I want to make it better. Please, Clare... without you, I'm, I'm..." "Nothing" isn't the right word, but it's the first word that comes to mind. "Without you I'm nothing! I can't imagine my life without you. You're the only woman I've fallen in love with. You're the only woman who's captured my heart and soul. I need you more than anything right now." I'd like to thank the Academy. Even with all that sappy bullshit I spewed out, she just slams the windows on me. I contemplate trying to get past Teddy, but the big negro could snap my arms in a split second. So I take my keys and my bags and get into the Range Rover. As I start the SUV up, my cellphone begins to ring. I reach into my jacket pocket and answer it. "What?" "I heard about what's happened between you and that, um, that girl you're married to." "What do you want, Victoria?" Victoria was a friend of mine before I met Clarisse. By 'friend' I mean fuck buddy. Convenience personified. "I think I've taken too many painkillers. I washed them down with a few shots of tequila and now I'm looking for someone to help me." I start to laugh as I take off down the street. "What do you mean by help?" "You know the answer to that one, Cody." I laugh even harder. "So if I come over to your house right now, am I gonna have to drive you to the hospital or fuck your brains out?" She lets out a shrieking laugh. "If I was gonna die, I wouldn't have called you." "OK, fair enough. If you were gonna die, I wouldn't have really cared. But right now you're starting to get me excited. I'll see you in fifteen minutes." I am Cody's lack of conscience. I tried to make it better with Clarisse, didn't I?
