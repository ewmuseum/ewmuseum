These Canadians deserve a shot?

12 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization These Canadians deserve a shot? December 12, 1999 (The camera fades in on the arena in Victoria, BC, site of tonights Sunday Slaughter. Geoff Green is walking down the halls of the locker room area, and comes to a door marked The Underground. Geoff pauses) Geoff: Hmmm, not on my schedule, but hell, Ill see how theyre doing. (Geoff opens the door and finds Mike Bear and Wretch sitting on a bench with their backs turned to the door. Theyre lacing up their boots while talking. Fear is sitting in a chair leaning against the wall, sleeping. Geoff decides to listen in on what Mike and Wretch are saying) Mike: Chris, remind me again, who are we facing. Wretch: Ummmm, Bob and Doug McKenzie? Mike: What? Those guys arent funny at all! Wretch: Hold on. (Picks up a sheet of paper) According to the booking sheet, were facing Tom the Bomb and the Dark Ninja. Mike: Aaah, all those Canadians are alike with those big flopping heads and ridiculous accents. (starts to stand and glimpses the door) Hey, Geoff! Dont just stand there, sit down! Geoff: Well I have a schedule... Mike (grabbing the schedule out of Geoffs hand): Let me see that. (looks at it for a fraction of a second, then crumples it up and throws it over his shoulder) Ah theyre a bunch of jobbers. Cmon, how about an interview. Geoff: Uh...sure. Mike (with a big smile on his face): Great, start that camera. (Geoff starts the interview) Mike: Well, tonight, Wretch and I face one of the most useless pieces of crap to come out of TCW, barely edging out Frank Snow and Eron the Talentless, and some shooting Ninja. Why these two Cactus Jon disciples deserve a tag team title shot is beyond me. Now if Jon was in the ring, then maybe Id say this would be a match, but no, not with two fraudulent peons facing the Underground, one of the best tag teams ever formed. The team that bested The French Foundation. We will be Tag Team champions again, and just like Slugger said, thats a guarantee. Wretch, anything to add. Wretch: Tonight? Nah, the Tag Titles is all were concerned with at the moment. Mike: Fear? (Fear continues to sleep) Fear? (Still sleeping. Mike walks over and smacks him on the head) FEAR! (Fear falls out of his chair and yells) Fear: AHHH! What? What? Mike: Do you have anything to add? Fear: You woke me up for that? I was having this nice dre... Mike: DONT finish that sentence. Just answer. Fear: Um, ok, no, I dont have anything to say. Mike (smiling again): Ok. (Fear looks at Mike quizzically, but decides to just set up his chair and go back to sleep) Geoff: So is that it? Mike: Yeah sure, fear the Underground, interview is over, see you later Geoff. (The camera fades out as Geoff picks up his crumpled schedule and leaves)
