Maybe

8 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wippit wondered if he went a little too far. Laying on a bed in his Super 8 Motel room, he again gingerly touched the raised lump on the back of his head form the chairshot Mufanga gave him. Stealing the contract right in front of him had been classic. Brilliant, even. But now he was paying for it with a headache. A lot of people might not be aware of it, but a lot of the booking done in the Fans Wrestling Organization was spur-of-the-moment. Spontaniety led to a better overall product, so beyond a few booked title matches and the odd scripted interview segment, everything was on-the-fly. Something Wippit enjoyed immesely. It gave him the freedom to just be himself and joke around with everyone, making them the target of jokes and puns, and getting over with the fans at the same time. Suffice to say, Mr Kagos was not impressed with his inpromptu change of plans. That chairshot after the match wasn't planned, and also wasn't faked - not that you can really fake a chairshot. But Mufanga also didn't pull the swing, he belted Wippit full power, and when 10 pounts of steel slams into your head at 40 or 50 mph... well, you go down. Hard. The words weren't scripted either. The fans may not be able to judge attitude, but Wip made a living out of reading how people were doing - it's a required skill in a match to be able to figure out what your opponent wanted to try. And he knew that Mufanga wasn't feigning anger. He was really and truly pissed. Which left Wippit in a bit of a bind. Should he mend fences, approach Ben (the peron, not the character) and apologize for what he did? Or should he use it as a way to put ona show, get himself over... and get Mufanga over, too. Wip wasn't selfish, and the former Survivor 2 participant, now over a year in the fWo, semmed to be left to his own devices, and not really booked. Nowdays, I'm sure it's called the Chris Jericho Syndrome. So... use it. But... would Mufanga go along with it? You can't have a match, let along a good feud, if the other participant didn't sell it. Wippit was willing, but Kagos had to be, too. He'd ask him Sunday. Maybe they could figure something out. Maybe.
