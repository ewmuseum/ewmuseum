Giving Back to the Fans

17 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::The day, Friday. The town, ah hell who knows.. all of these hick towns in Minnesota start looking the same after a few days. The hot spot of the moment is Big Mark's Memorabilia where Hellrazor and Lamont are the guests of honor as a part of an fWo fan appreciation day. Hellrazor is busy signing 8x10 glossy photographs of himself with the Hardcore title while Lamont is making some sort of ink smear on a set of 5 x 7 Lamont photos. Outside the doors, hundreds of kids are waiting for the store to open to share a moment with their favorite fWo personality.:: Hellrazor: Dammit Lamont, we best hurry up.. store opens in 10 minutes and we're sure to give out a lot of these, considering they're free. ::Upon hearing this, Big Mark, a fat fortysomething man who makes up for his receding hairline by pulling what's left into a ponytail begins laughing. As he chuckles at the two wrestlers, he thinks back on his own childhood. He was the boy who collected comics, and baseball cards. He was the teenager who did the same. He was the college student who flunked an entire year and lost his apartment because he went to see Star Wars every single day and spent his housing money on a full squadron of TIE fighters. Yes, life had been good to him, and now it was better. Today, he was the proud owner of paradise, stuffed into a 10 foot by 12 foot space. Those children.. they wanted things... cards, beanie babies, wrestling figures.. and these things they would have.. but at a price. For it was the holiday season, and parents were out looking for the toys that their children craved. But wise men, men like Big Mark, were in touch with each other, and with the UPS delivery men, and knew when to hit the toy stores to buy out the entire shipment of the hot toy, and sell it at a premium. And the parents, the adults who as children had teased him, they would pay whatever price he quoted, just so their child could be better than the one next door. Life was grand. Today, the 'Meet Hellrazor and Lamont' session would also bring in cash. The admission would be free, as promised to the fWo committee.. but the photo, or thr right to have something signed on his property... that would be a fair sum of 10 dollars. Big Mark looked at his limited edition Power Puff Girls watch.. it said 9AM.. let the fleecing begin...:: Mark: Ehhhh.. Mr. Hellrazor.. I'm going to unlock the doors now, and secure the barricades on the merchandise. We've had a rash of thefts on the Pokemon cards recently, and I'd hate to lose anymore of them... Hellrazor: Pokey Man? What kind of shit is that? Are you a sexual predator or something? Mark: It's Pokemon. PO...KAY...MON. It's short for Pocket Monsters.... Hellrazor: Pocket Monsters? Man, you really *ARE* a hoo-hoo aren't you? Damn.. C'mon Lamont.. we outta here.. we don't need some guy with happy hands getting his jollies around us... Mark: NO!.. I mean.. no, you don't understand.. Pokemon is a game.. Hellrazor: I'll bet. Mark: Really..and the kids love it... Hellrazor: Kids?? You sonova.. Mark: Please.. it..it's just a card game.. I swear.. I swear... please..just let me open my store.. I can't lose any potential customers.. they might go next door to Happy Charlie's Card Shop! ::Mark runs for the door, hurriedly unlocking it and ushering in the group of kids. He sets up his makeshift barricades, forcing the children to loop all the way around his shop to get to Hellrazor and Lamont and sets himself up at his cash register to begin taking in his profits. The first child, a shy little boy approaches Hellrazor, his large blue eyes barely peeking over the table.:: Hellrazor: Hey there little fella. How would you like an autographed photo of me and Lamont? We're wrestlers! ::The child looks at Hellrazor, looks at Lamont, looks back at Hellrazor and begins crying hysterically. This seems to rattle Hellrazor who is obviously not used to dealing with small children.:: Hellrazor: What the Jesus H is wrong with this kid? Hey.. fatboy.. yeah you... Pig in a Ponytail... do something! ::Big Mark sighs and walks over to the child, dragging him off to the side and putting him in front of a row of 1st edition beanie babies. This seems to not only calm the child down, but it places him in a catatonic state as the drool begins to run down his chin. During this time another child has stepped up to Hellrazor.:: Hellrazor: Hey-o. What say you get yourself an autograph from the baddest fWo wrestler out there? Kid: Who the hell are you? Hellrazor: My name is Hellrazor I'm an fWo champion.. Kid: IT DOESN'T MATTER WHAT YOUR NAME IS. haha hahaha hahaha. Hellrazor: Oh. Real original. Do you want a photo or not? Kid: SUCK IT! CUZ STONE COLD SAID SO! WHOOOOOO! PIMPIN AIN'T EASY! HAVE A NICE DAY! Hellrazor: Alright kid.. move along. Let someone else get in here... ::The kid leaves still spouting generic and repetitive wrestling phrases as a teenager approaches the table with a smirk on his face.:: Teen: Hey..look.. it's HELL RAYZAH. Hey HELL RAYZAH. How'd you get so bad? hehehe. Hey Jimmy..get a picture of me with this chump... Hellrazor: Chump? I'm the Hardcore champ! Teen: Oooo.. the "Hardcore" champ..how'd you get the title of "Hardcore Champ"? Hellrazor: Ask your momma.. when she can drag her skank-ass out of bed again... NEXT! ::The teen mumbles something about Japanese death matches being better and sulks away as a disheveled 10 year old stumbles into the table, his eyes glazed over.:: Kid: Jigglypuff! Pikachu! Jigglypuff!!! Hellrazor: Huh? Kid: Pokemon! Must..catch..them..all! ALL! MINE! Hellrazor: Sorry kid..this is the "Meet the fWo" line.. not the "suckers paying 12 bucks for a 10 cent card" line. Kid: I must.. have.. the newest cards.. stole 20 dollars from dad's wallet... Hellrazor: Oh really? Let's see your list here... hmm ok... well I am a Pookamen expert.. I am the Pack em in MASTER... and I know what cards you want.. here are the names of the newest cards.. ::Hellrazor scribbles down a few things on a piece of paper and hands it to the kid to bring to Big Mark. As the kid walks away, another teenager has arrived and is urging Hellrazor to give him an autograph. Hellrazor holds his hand up, waiting to see what happens when the kid makes it to Big Mark.:: Kid: I need the new pokemon cards. Mark: Ain't none yet. Not until January 11th at precisely midnight. You'll have to sneak out to get here first, but hey... Kid: No.. I know they're here.. the Pokemon Master told me.... Mark Oh, is that so? And what can I get you? Kid: (looking at the list) Do you have Lardass? Mark: Who? Kid: According to this list here.. it says you have a Lardass. Mark: Look kid.. I think someone has been playing games with you.. now beat it.. I'm busy here. Kid: What about Lyfe? Mark No, kid.. I have no Lyfe.. Hey! Who the hell gave that to you???? Kid: The Pokemon master.. he's over there..with Jigglypuff... ::The kid is pointing straight at Hellrazor who is doubled over in laughter. Meanwhile, beside him the impatient teen is trying to tease Lamont with a set of car keys. Mark begins to turn red with the pent up rage that can only come from years and years of Babylon 5 reruns. He turns and snaps angrily at the kid who still stands in front of him.:: Mark: That..that guy.. is no Pokemon Master... he's just a wrestler.. a punk.. trained to take falls on a mat... that's all... you know.. I am the Pokemon Master.. I'm the one who gets you all the cards you want... Kid: Well then you'll have this last card then.. it says every Pokemon Master has it... Mark: ...and that card is? Kid: Aleatherfetish. The other Pokemon Master wrote here that you definately have a-leather-fetish.. Mark: THAT'S IT. I've had it! EVERYONE OUT!!!! ::Mark stomps across the shop and stands fuming in front of Hellrazor.:: Hellrazor: What man? What you want? I'm busy... Mark: I'll have you know, I won't have you belittling me in front of my clients. Youare to pack up your stuff.. take that scrap of tin you call a championship, pick up that fleabitten furball and get OUT. Hellrazor: Or what? Mark: Or I'll have you removed from the premises! Hellrazor: I see.. ::Hellrazor stands up slowly, towering over Big Mark, who desinterviewse the fact that he weighs in excess of 300 pounds, is only 5'6". Hellrazon takes him by the ear and begins drumming his head off of the table.:: Mark: I ..Ow! Let go of my ponytail...please.. I'm sorry... Hellrazor: And this.. this is for that 'just a wrestler' comment... ::Hellrazor drags Big Mark over to the wall where a set of commemorative KISS mirrors have been lovingly polished and hung. Mark begins to struggle and squeal as he tries to free himself from his impending doom.:: Mark: No! Not Ace Frehley! NOooooo! ::With a resounding crash, Hellrazor flings Mark into the mirror taking down most of the plaques and trinkets which line that wall. During this frenzy, the teenager who was teasing Lamont with his keys has somehow gotten the orange cat latched onto his face and is spinning out of control trying to pull Lamont off of him. As he knocks over a glass case filled with Pokemon cards and 1st edition Star Wars figures, the crowd of children converge onto the pile of cardboard treasures like a plague of locusts. The scene begins to fade as children flee the shop with pockets full of cards and action figures while off in the distance, as usual, the sound of police sirens can be heard heading towards Hellrazor and Lamont's latest location.::
