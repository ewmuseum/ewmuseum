Of exodus and redemption (part two)

28 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"You aren't going to be walking away from here, Joy." Joy smiled, his split lip revealing the dark cavity of his mouth that was still leaking blood and the shattered remains of front teeth. "You're going away for a long time Douglas... Ryan made sure of that." Douglas blinked, confused by the wording of what Joy had said. It was all the opening that Joy needed, and he struck with a kick to the chest and quickly scooped Douglas up. Turning him over and letting him rest over his shoulder, Joy bounced himself off of the ropes and stumbled forward. He swung and slammed Douglas down to the mat face first, and smiled as Douglas screamed in pain as tacks sunk into his chest, arms, and face. He rolled him over, collapsed on top of Douglas, and let himself fade away as the referee weakly dropped his hands three times. The match ended, and Joy let himself be overwhelmed by the darkness and fade into oblivion. As both men lay still the ring crew fought their way past the screaming crowd and began to loosen the bolts that kept the cage on the floor. Slowly they guided it up, then watched as the cage once again rose back high into the air. Medical staff went to the referee first, helping him from the ring and taking care not to step on any tacks that still littered the canvas. Both men still lay still in the ring, and the crowd gasped as several large men emerged from the curtains at the top of the ramp. They carried handcuffs and a straightjacket, and then walked toward the ring stiffly. The men went after Douglas, who was starting to come to his senses. He protested, but the men quickly had Douglas into the jacket and drawn a bag over his head. They carried him from the ring, and it was only then that the crowd noticed the wording on the back of their coats: SANITARIUM. Inside the ring Joy pushed himself up on one shoulder and allowed the medical crew to inspect him. He watched as Douglas was led up the ramp, and vanished from sight. He nodded to himself, satisfied that his plan had worked in the end... Douglas would get the help that he so desperately needed. But as he let his head drop back onto the mat he couldn't help but think that perhaps Douglas should not be leaving alone. The memories of the match were already fading away... but Joy found he couldn't remember how he had gotten loose from being hung... and he hadn't remembered the sound of the bell. Joy closed his eyes, and when he drifted off to sleep he dreamed the dreams of another man. None of this was right of course... this wasn't the way it had happened at all. Joy had died a year before... a year at least. Fallen had been in the CWL, and Fallen had fought Douglas. So why did Joy see himself in the ring?
