The Job - Part 1

11 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Bad day?" Chris Universal observed, throwing a brief look Mufanga's way, as the furious wrestler stormed into his office, slamming the door behind him. Ignoring Universal, he continued towards the empty chair opposite the large mohagany desk and sat down, turning his face to the window beside him. Kagos drummed his long black fingers on his denim-clad legs in a machine-gun rhythm, clenching his square jaw, but still saying nothing. "I figure yes," the fWo Owner went on after he'd settled, and emptied his whiskey with a flourish and a rattle of ice cubes in crystal. After a quick thought and another glance at the man's face, he fixed one for Kagos too. With the glass in his hand, he headed to the desk, but when he offered it to Mufanga, he knocked it out of his grip with a swift wave of his hand, stood up from the chair and paced to the center of the room. Universal arched his brow at the scattered pieces of glass inside the stain of liquor. "That was a good carpet," he observed apathetically, then walked back to his desk and sank into his leather chair, motioning at the wrestler to start talking, "now... be my guest." Mufanga Kagos smiled, leaning back against the open window, a fresh breeze moving his heavy dreadlocks forward. "Guess who I've just seen." Universal stirred his drink with a letter opener from the mirror-like desk surface. He peered at Kagos strangely, but continued to stir his drink casually, ever the businessman. He had more to worry about than this one, he thought, remembering his words with Ruben Ross last night. "Tempest?" he finally asked. "No, not Tempest... I spoke with my agent. We went over the contract I signed with this company a few months back, Chris." "I see..." "My agent had specifically requested a clause that I advance to the second round of the Internet Title Tournament." Mufanga spat out. "I was supposed to go to the second round, Chris. Tempest and I went over the finish before the match, and it was going to be me going over. Wilddive, and pin. Just like that. What the hell was she doing kicking out of the Wilddive? You know how that makes my finisher look? It looks as friggin' effective as an armdrag, that's what." "Interesting," he observed, his expression though, remained imperturbable. "Yeah, interesting. Now what I want to know is this... what did you know of this? Sure the ref looked surprised enough, so he knew I was supposed to go over, but what did you know, Chris? I'm beginning to think this was a screwjob from the start." Universal shrugged, although by the red shade creeping out from under his collar, it was plain to see he was more than a little angry. "You want the truth, Mufanga? In all honesty, you aren't important to enough to warrant me screwing you over. You bust into my office and accuse me of masterminding some sceme to hold you down? You were never up in the first place, Kagos. Yes, you were supposed to go over Tempest. Yes, I am not happy about her changing the finish in mid-match, and yes, I will deal with it. Happy?" "I... you.. she..." Mufanga stuttered, taken aback by Chris Universal's eerily calm outburst. "Yes, I am." "Then, don't let the door hit you on your way out."
