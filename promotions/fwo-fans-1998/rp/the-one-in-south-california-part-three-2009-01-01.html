STREET SIDEWALK-LOS FELIZ-MID-MORNING THE NEXT DAY Dean and Cassidy step out of a cab with their black duffel bags and begin to walk along the sidewalk. Cassidy attempts to show affection by getting close to Dean, who cares more about his cigarette. CASSIDY I lovelovelove Los Feliz! How come you never bring me back here? It's your home! I love L.A. Your lil' home here, L.A. Ell ay. La. Wait, where are we meetin' Lowell? They reach a crosswalk, Dean stopping only to think, not to look both ways, before stepping into the crosswalk. DEAN His brother told him someplace in LA. We're gonna meet him up here tomorrow, he'll have the address then. They keep walking and eventually make a left down a long road, into an affluent neighborhood. CASSIDY So where are we going now? ARE WE GOING TO VISIT YOUR MOM?! Ohmigod, I miss her so much, do you think she remembers me?! Dean completely ignores her, continues walking down the road. Eventually, they reach a reasonably large white home not far from Griffith Park. While certainly not among the enormous homes in the neighborhood, it holds its own in both size and elegant charm. He pounds on the front door. DEAN Mom? (He pounds at the door several more times) Mom? Looking around the corner, he sees no one in the backyard, but he proceeds anyway. Cassidy follows him, peering into the massive, beautifully landscaped backyard, equipped with a luxurious pool and gorgeous floral arrangements. Dean walks over to a large glass set of doors and opens them, stepping into the home, waving for Cassidy to follow, who closes the door behind her. CASSIDY Y'know, your mom should lock her doors. Seriously. Otherwise degenerates and just dirty scumbag kids might break in. The home is decorated with the nonsensical items that only the rich might own; large, unnecessary, "progressive abstract" works of art adorn the walls, strangely-shaped vases house expensive rare flowers, and other generally pretentious decorative pieces fill the home. Dean throws the bags across the kitchen and immediately plops down on the couch and grabs the Sega Game Gear-looking universal remote control to turn it on. DEAN Cass, come check this out. They get a channel dedicated entirely to Japanese wrestling here. I'll let you know when I find it. Cassidy, meanwhile, has her head buried in the enormous refrigerator--the kind with the wooden exterior, so as to not detract from the motif of the kitchen. CASSIDY Where does your mom keep the booze? DEAN She doesn't drink. Cassidy pulls the doors of the cupboard open, cartoonishly displays her disapproval on her face. DEAN (CONT'D) Cassidy, we'll get food later, who gives a fuck? KENTA's fighting American Dragon. Cassidy looks into this pantry and finds what she's looking for. CASSIDY Jackpot. After grabbing the bottle of alcohol, she eagerly joins Dean on the couch. CASSIDY (CONT'D) So when's Lowell supposed to call? Dean is far too focused on the television to pay attention. DEAN Yeah, that's right. Go 2 Sleep, bitch. KENTA's the fucking man. CASSIDY How is he even getting here? Dean begins to flip the channel, stopping shortly on the news. CASSIDY (CONT'D) Why are we watching this shit? DEAN I'm gettin' my culture on. TELEVISION ANCHOR If it is proven to indeed be the same person, it has been said that this might be the most elusive and, frankly, terrifying murderer in American history, a figure who authorities have dubbed "The Great State Killer," with similar murders having occurred in every state in the continental United States, save California. Authorities are on the lookout for suspicious behavior, as it is expected that, according to statistics gathered by investigators of the other crimes, California might be hit within the week. CASSIDY People are fucked these days, you know? Dean looks to Cassidy blankly, finally realize what she's been chugging. DEAN Are you drinking cooking wine? CASSIDY Yeah. (A quick swig) But, no, listen, can we just fuck? Dean flips the channel and stops on HBO Family. DEAN No, Rookie of the Year is on. CASSIDY Why do you like that movie so much? DEAN It's a heartwarming summer tale of a young man achieving his dreams, discovering his identity, and dealing with new experiences both on and off the baseball diamond. It's a great ride for the whole family, Cassidy. CUT TO: INT. PUBLIC TRANSIT BUS-DAYTIME-SECONDS LATER The strung-out bus driver is clearly having a bad trip, losing his focus on driving, much to the horror of his passengers. LOWELL Fifty?! Might as well make it eighty! That way we've got a buffer, you know? Extra safe! Siren lights flash in the side mirror. LOWELL (CONT'D) THE FUZZ! Nuñez wakes up, again, and jumps to his feet, almost getting knocked over by the sudden turbulence, as the bus swerves onto the sidewalk, mowing down a row of cast iron benches. Nuñez instinctively grabs ahold of Lowell as he nears him. NUÑEZ What the fuck, man?! What the fuck are you doing?! Nuñez spots the sirens in the side mirror. NUÑEZ (CONT'D) Ya fucked us now, Low. LOWELL Dude, I'm beginning to think this bus driver is maxed out--like balls out insane, y'know? BUS DRIVER Hey Coppers! SIXTY AND CLIMBIN'! LOWELL Let's hope he crashes this beast. That's the only way we're gettin' off today. He's Keanu, now. Completely psychotic. CUT TO: EXT. CONVENIENCE STORE-1 HOUR LATER Dean loiters outside the store, smoking as usual. CASSIDY Do we have any money? Dean shakes his head, leaning against the front window. DEAN Cab ride sapped us. Just change. CASSIDY Gimme. Do you want anything? Dean pulls out a small handful of change, mostly nickels. DEAN Cranberry juice. Nantucket Nectars, none of that other shit. The store is your standard mini-mart, but Cassidy sees it as some sort of Mecca. She examines the candy, grabbing a variety and stuffing it in her pocket. Turning the corner, she notices a Kool Aid display and starts to flip through the packets, pulling out a Tropical Punch and two Roarin' Raspberry Cranberry packets, stuffing those in her pockets too. Cassidy looks up and blows a kiss to the camera hidden behind the corner mirror and then grabs herself a 40oz. bottle of Mickey's malt liquor and Dean's cranberry juice. After marching up to the counter proudly, she unloads her arms, the heavy glass bottles clanging against the glass countertop. The teenaged CLERK is slow in movement and, based on his demeanor and general appearance, in all other ways, as well. His hair is messy and greasy and his eyes have bags so heavy they look as though they're packed for a European vacation. CLERK Can I, uh, get you anything else? Cassidy ponders for a second, looking unsatisfied at the selection of items behind the counter. CASSIDY You don't have any of the peach rolling papers? The clerk shakes his head, looking almost ashamed. CLERK Nah. I've been tellin' my boss we should get 'em, man, but, nah. I wish we had the cherry, too. CASSIDY Hmph. Okay, well, I guess just the Zig-Zags then. CLERK So, you like to smoke? Cassidy fakes a giggle. CASSIDY Yeah. I don't do it that much, though, because I like to drink, too. And when I do both, I end up just doing crazy things. The clerk is impressed and enticed. CLERK Oh word? Yeah? You gid'down? (A soft, creepy laugh) Arright, your total w'tax is.. (He punches some beeping buttons on the cash register) Four dollars, twenny-three cents. Cassidy smiles and reaches deeply into her pocket, tugging her pants down a little bit. CASSIDY I have it in change, I hope you don't mind. CLERK No problem, sweetheart. At this point, Cassidy pulls her hands out of her pocket and the change clangs everywhere. CASSIDY Shit! Turning her back to the clerk, she bends over to pick up the change, giving a slight view of her light blue thong underwear. The clerk unabashedly leans over the counter a bit and looks, leaning back onto his heels once Cassidy stands up. CASSIDY (CONT'D) Most of it went under the rack overe there. All my girlfriends really wanted to get high, too. They're gonna hate me so much. She then, of course, makes a puppy dog face at the clerk. The clerk ponders the ethics of his decision before pressing a button opening the cash register drawer. He feigns putting money in; a show for the camera. He then winks to Cassidy and puts her drinks in a bag. CLERK You're all set, ma'am. Cassidy overacts her gratitude and then grabs her bag, proudly walking over to the door. Just as she grabs the door, the clerk blurts out a final question. CLERK (CONT'D) Oh, uh, do you have a phone number or like a screen name or something? Cassidy turns and looks to him with a smirk. CASSIDY Yeah. Just go to www.myspace.com slash blow me, perv. My name's "Trouble." Cassidy gets out of the store and hands Dean his drink. She pulls her 40oz. out of the bag and pockets the Zig-Zags, tossing the bag into the trash can. She tears open one of the packages of candy, shoving some into her mouth. DEAN You know you're like a four year old, right? CASSIDY No. I have a sweet tooth. DEAN It's more than your teeth. You have saccharin in your bloodstream. Your pussy probably tastes like Nerds. CASSIDY Probly. But would you ever care to find out? DEAN That'd be a big goddamn no. CASSIDY Yeah, exactly. DEAN None of the peach rolling papers? CASSIDY Don't even get me fucking started. Dean gives a nod. DEAN Candy, Mickey's and Zig-Zags. Smells like my elementary school days. FADE OUT. EXT. CITY STREETS-LATER The bus speeding down the street. CUT TO: INT. CITY BUS-MOMENTS LATER The bus driver hammers the steering wheel with his hand, sounding its horn continuously. Perspiration jewels on his forehead, crazy eyed. BUS DRIVER I'LL TAKE THIS BUS TO HELL AND BACK! Lowell sits in his seat, staring blankly. LOWELL I think I may have upset him. (Checks out the window) Cops have been chasin' us for while, now. The bus driver grips the wheel tighter. BUS DRIVER Holy fuck! (Shouting back at the passengers) Who sucks now!? LOWELL You do, bitchface! Lowell is nearly jarred from his seat as the bus goes over a pothole. Nuñez grabs the back of his shirt to keep him from tumbling to the floor, jerking him back in his seat. LOWELL (CONT'D) Are we just learning to drive?! Is that why I can see a motorcade of Rascals passin' us on the sidewalk?! The bus driver's bad trip and insanity is reaching new heights. He glances down at the speedometer, the needle holding steady at 120. The violent rattling of the bus knocks some standing passengers to the floor, their intense screaming and hollering from the back of the bus causing the bus driver to freak out even further, covering his ears with his hands and clenching his eyes tightly. BUS DRIVER Shut up! I can't take it! SHUT UP! At a distance barely within the line of vision, the bus driver sees a young girl crossing the street, prompting him to JAM on the brakes and CUT the wheel. The tires SCREECH as the entire bus shifts, turning horizontally and blocking the road, with the end clipping the back of a Chevrolet Avalanche parked on the side of the road. Those in back are struggling to hold on. More have fallen out of their seats and are tumbling about in the aisle. Nuñez is holding on to the bar above him with one hand, while restraining Lowell with the other. The bus comes to a stop, shaking violently as the tires skip across the pavement. The CAMERA PANS from the front of the bus to the back, showing the bobble-headed passengers. A few in the aisle, on their hands and knees, praying. LOWELL (Breathing heavily) That... was it...? Lowell stands up defiantly. Everyone else remains in their seat, or wherever else they were when the bus finally stopped. LOWELL (CONT'D) I just expected more, s'all... The door is KICKED open, and a THRONG OF COPS rush onto the bus, grabbing the bus driver and dragging him out of his seat and off the bus. Lowell quickly sits back down and uses his shirt to conceal his face. NUÑEZ (Whispering, covering his face) Look wha'cha' did, Low. The po lice. The muh'fuckin' cops 'is stormin' this muh'fucker. You got a lot experience in shit like this, don'tchu? I can tell. LOWELL (Whispering) Dude, dude, I don't wanna go back to prison. I like the free life too much. Stop me if I try'n do somethin' crazy. I can't do "cop killer" time, dude, that shit is too real. NUÑEZ (Whispering, not making eye contact with Lowell) Just shut the fuck up and keep ya 'ead down, nigga. One of the cops, a young blonde-headed one in a flack jacket, stands at the front of the bus. BLONDE COP Everyone try and remain calm. Those of you laying in the aisle can return to your seats, now. In just a moment, we'll start taking you off the bus, one-by-one. You'll be treated for any minor injuries you may have, and if you're up to it, we have some questions we'd like you to ask. Thank you. He turns round and exits the bus. Everyone left on the bus is a bit rattled and conversing amongst one another about what had just taken place. Nuñez stands and motions for Lowell to get up, as well, which he does. NUÑEZ Let's go. (Brief pause) Now, nigga! Before they come callin' again! Lowell and Nuñez hurry to the side door near the back of the bus. Lowell struggles to open it, so Nuñez goes to the front of the bus and pulls on the handle to open it. The door opens and Lowell and Nuñez exit the bus, disappearing into a disorderly horde of bystanders, civilians and media alike, come to find out what caused the crash. CUT TO: EXT. JOHN MARSHALL HIGH SCHOOL-EARLY AFTERNOON The grounds of the school are buzzing with students on their lunch hour, socializing and generally conversing with one another. Dean and Cassidy sit in the middle of the swarm, Dean surrounded by his typical cloud of smoke. The bottle of Mickey's lies in the bush next to Cassidy, who leans against Dean's shoulder. Students march up and down the steps past them in droves before one GIRL stops in front of Dean. She's about 16 years of age, in a black tanktop, with blonde hair held up in a ponytail. GIRL You know, you can't smoke on school property. It's illegal. And it's bad for everyone else around you. Dean looks up to her, takes a drag, and blows the smoke out the side of his mouth, away from her. GIRL (CONT'D) No. Honestly, who do you think you are? DEAN I think I'm Dean. Why? Who do you think I am? GIRL I think you're a smoker. DEAN Guilty as fuckin' charged, little lady. Dean takes another drag as the girl displays offense at her innocent, virginal ears being deflowered. Dean looks to Cassidy, who, half-asleep on Dean's shoulder possesses the innocent charm of a napping child. CASSIDY (CONT'D) Y'know what I never got to do? DEAN What's that? I wanted guys to want me to blow them under the staircase. DEAN Oh. CASSIDY And then let them fuck me in the bathroom. At this point, Dean stands up and heads into the school, with Cassidy hopping up and following him in quickly.
