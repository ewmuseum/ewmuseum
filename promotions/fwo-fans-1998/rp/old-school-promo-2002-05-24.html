Old School Promo.

24 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cleveland, Ohio. The Gund Arena. Camera rolling. "I could come out here and try and convince you with my words, Lance... I've been in this business for six years now. I've faced some of the best, some of the worst, and have learned a thing or two along the way. "You're talented, Lance... Young... and, your future here is bright. THAT I know. But, let me tell you what else I know..." Sean Stevens was in "Triple X" mode now. Playing the character to the best of his ability - which was quite easy seeing as how the character was simply an extension of himself, with the volume turned way up. Being in the business for as long as he has... he didn't have a speech prepared. In fact, scripted, or rehearsed promos never really worked out for him. He found it best to do it 'on the fly'. Make it up as he goes along. At first... many thought he was crazy by insisting on doing it in this manner so often. But, now... he's regarded as one of the most natural, gifted speakers in the industry... so obviously... he's doing something right. "... You're not ready for me. In fact, as talented as you are, and as bright as your future may be. You'll NEVER reach the level of superstardom that I've reached. "Why? I can't tell you, Lance. The world is f**cked up that way, sometimes. Some people are blessed with the gift of being mediocre... decent... just okay. But, then there's people like me. A man who can do it all. Wrestle with the best wrestlers... Fly with the highest flyers... talk sh*t with the greatest sh*t talkers. "I'm sorry the fWo front office thought you were good enough to compete in the same ring as me. After I beat you tomorrow night... blame them. Not me. Because it was there poor scouting that got us IN this situation in the first place. It was bad judgement in the truest sense. "You, Lance Knight... are a dime a dozen. *I* am once in a lifetime." Contrary to what the camera showed... Sean had JUST finished wrestling Lance Knight on the Cleveland show. He was scheduled to wrestle him twice more in the next couple of days. Upon entering the backstage area, after the matchup... he was told by a few of the brass, that cutting an "old school" killer promo could help add more heat to the matchup, as they'll play it right before the two make their respective entrances tomorrow night in Ohio. At the moment... Lance hadn't cut his... but, Sean was a different case. Earlier in the week he and his girlfriend had a MAJOR falling out. His head was wrecked... his heart was aching... and, he simply NEEDED something to take his mind off the entire situation. He and Lance had an excellent match that they were BOTH excited about. But, that simply didn't do it. Sean needed more. And, on this day... the annoying camera... that typically made the life of a wrestler a living hell, came to his rescue. "So say your prayers, Knight. Eat your vitamins... drink your milk... and, if you're religuos... pray. Because you'll need it." Sean paused for a moment. Taking a deep breath. Wrestlers are trained professionals... Usually, when they're cutting promos backstage... other wrestler stop by to view... wish another grappler well... or either just walk by, to attend to their own affairs. This particular ritual almost led to Sean's undoing... as Poison Ivy and Tempest walked by the set in route to their destination. "I am going to make an example out of you, Lance. And, this situation... it's a LOT more serious than the fWo has led you to believe. "You're in trouble, Lance. And, the sad thing about it... there's not a damned thing you or anybody can do about it." fade- to- black ****** As soon as the camera stopped rolling... Sean leaped to his feet - in search of Ivy and Tempest... to no avail. They disappeared. Sean sighed a sigh of disappointment. As he turned around and headed for someplace quiet.
