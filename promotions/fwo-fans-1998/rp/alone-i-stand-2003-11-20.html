Alone I Stand...

20 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brandon Roche looked up the long driveway. For the last seven years he had called this place home. It was built to pay homage to his childhood home and blended in with some Irish flair. It was a nice house, nothing overly dramatic, not a mansion by a long shot. Cozy and warm though. In the past, when Brandon would come home from being on the road for weeks on end, he would look up this driveway and smile. The name of the street, that one light on the entrance pillars that flickered whenever a car road by, all of it was home. Now though he sat there, for a long time, a pit had formed in his stomach. Looking up that driveway now did nothing but remind him of the conversation that would soon follow once he ended his trek up the cobbled stone. He hadn't been home in almost three weeks now. After everything that happened last week, the revelations, the betrayals, he couldn't bring himself to do it. Now though, with events careening out of control he knew that the conversation that would follow was needed. He needed to get his head on straight before the coming weeks, regain his focus, fuel his desire. The end game was at hand and afterwards nothing would ever be the same again. In a blink of an eye and with those thoughts still in his mind Brandon found himself stepping out of his car, pulling his knapsack behind him. The light in the front foyer flicked on, snapping him out of his daydreaming state. Amy was up, and apparently waiting. The door opened, a sliver of light being cast out onto the porch, illuminating Brandon Roche's way inside. Following the light from out of the darkness the family's patriarch stepped inside, the warmth tickling his face. Once there, however, he found himself standing alone. Glancing around it was apparent he was being led. The light in the library clicked on, beckoning the hero, this man, to follow. As the knapsack slouched to the floor Brandon made his way into his favorite room in the house. The library was his baby. While Ana, his ex-wife, designed the rest of the house, this was Brandon's room to do with what he would. The many first editions that lined one wall brought a smile to a tired man's face as he walked into the room. The opposite wall did just the opposite; it was decorated with the many awards that Brand Frontier had garnered over the years. The Elite Wrestling Associations Heavyweight Championship was the centerpiece. He tried to show humility whenever he looked upon these gifts, not smiling, but the glint in his eye gave away the feeling of pride he actually felt. Today was no different, although these prizes now resonated more so than normal. They had all been won on Brand Frontier's singular trek to the moment he's weeks away from. A sigh escaped his lips slowly as he realized exactly what that meant. He didn't know that at that exact same time, the girl he loved was heaving that exact same sigh. Step by step, she made her way into the light of the room with a heavy heart - unsure of the reaction she'd be facing. Yes, she'd hidden the true cause of the loss of their baby from Brandon...but there was just cause in that, wasn't there? Not by Brandon's mindset, of course...to him, it was betrayal because in a twisted way, it had served to protect Vic Creid. She skulked into a side of the room and sank into what was normally Brandon's armchair with another heavy sigh, resting her head by her fingers on her temples as she stared up at him. She wasn't happy, pleading, sad or angry...she wasn't Amy. "What happens now?" "I guess we talk. So talk." "Oh, right." She sneered, "Talk. Guess what, Brandon? I have nothing to say for once. I did what I did, it's not like you're entirely blind to my reasoning." "Reason doesn't seem to have anything to do with it. It's like you were just trying to avoid any of this. Walk away. Maybe you're parents were right..." Brandon spit out, angry, at everyone and everything lately. Amy shot up out of the chair and almost raised her hand to slap him for daring to bring up her parents' 'advice' to them, but she was just as quick to ease away. "Or maybe you're too fucking paranoid. I know how obsessed you are with Creid...you would have done something you'd have regretted if you knew about the miscarriage attack when it happened, you know that." the redhead was nearly shouting at Roche, all of her emotion suddenly catching up and being misled by fear of losing him. "I was protecting you, and I was protecting your God damned FAMILY. You're so obsessed with him over your whore ex NOW that you're forgetting about Sean, Jarod, Stephen and I...if I'd told you that Creid took your ego down another notch by stealing your CHILD, you probably wouldn't have remembered our names by the next day." Brandon couldn't exactly disagree; he did however think she was over reacting on how his reactions would have played out. In it all he had never forgotten his family, true, they were put on the back burner at times but he had reasons. Didn't he? "I'm obsessed with Creid because of what he dared do to us, it's more than just whoring around with Ana. Amy, for doing nothing more than protecting a friend he crippled my baby's godfather. Damn it, he stole from us. He stole our ability to be a family when it meant the most. You're right about one thing; finding out when it happened would have probably done me in. Now though, I can use it. The anger, the resentment, all of it's fuel. Vic's crossed the line more times than I can count and I've stood by and played the big hero boy. Always talking about fighting the good fight, some times I'm sappier than Hogan ever was. But now, now it's simple. He's done. All you did in not telling me was hurt us and give him a stay of execution." 'Hurt us? I hurt us?! God forbid me wanting to have a few extra weeks of you only HALFWAY ignoring your family, as opposed to completely forgetting that we even exist because you're so obsessed over someone wronging you. Newsflash, Brandon - he hurt me, too. You don't see me dropping Stephen or you off somewhere so I can regain the ego Vic took." "Yeah Amy, I completely forget you exist because of him. We went to your families place for the weekend. We spend time with the kids together. I can't help that you were stuck at home after he stole our baby from us. I had a job to do, yes I make it revolve around Creid, but it's a tool. It's a means to an ends, his end actually. But it's still my career. You make it sound like I'm outside his hotel room stalking him or something. I know I've taken some risks you haven't approved of when it came to him, but don't you recall when I was trying to get you to stay away from the hokey hardcore division awhile back? You didn't listen to me then either, and this is about something real." "Vic Creid isn't real, Brandon...he's a fucking game - he's trapped inside his own little 'character', pre-packaged for television drama." The redhead cringed momentarily, trying to sheath her frustration. "He wouldn't be affecting your family life if you weren't LETTING HIM affect you. You're easily forgetting which one of us was hurt most by this...remember who had bruises for a month from the attack? How about the one who had to deal with their body rejecting the best thing to ever happen to them because of Creid? Ever think about that? Maybe recall which one of us has had a nervous fucking breakdown within the last two months? It might do you some good to consider why the hell you're doing what you're doing, because it sure as fuck isn't as protective, selfless and holy as you might make it seem." "I never once said I was doing this for some holy reason. Others might be saying that crap but I've never said that. Never. This doesn't have a damn thing to do about anything holy Amy. And yeah, I let him sleep with Ana. I let him cripple Jon and burn my house to the ground, and I let him get to my kids and I let him get to you. I let him take our baby away. It's all my fault... and since it's all my fault all I've ever tried to do was make it so he wouldn't do it again. I'm tired of half-assing it. I... I'm just trying to make up for the mistakes I made the only way I know how." "I suggest you learn a new way." "There is no other way when it comes to someone like him. And this is getting us nowhere. You think you're right for what you did and I think I'm doing what I have to do. Maybe... maybe this isn't what either of us need!" "What both of us need is for you to figure out where the hell your obligation lies - in your pride or your love." Amy knew she was all too angry, but in her mind they both were. Even still, she sighed with what sounded like regret... "I've got everything necessary packed, I'm ready to take Stephen, Sean and Jarod with me to my parent's..." Another sigh, "You'll be too busy with Creed to take care of the boys, and there's no way in hell I want them with Ana..." "They're already with Ana..." Brandon knew the response that would bring so he continued quickly, all in one breath. "They're hers just as much as they're mine. If I'm doing what I'm doing I can't expect her to sit by and not have something to say about it. I took them yesterday, they'll be with her until after the show next week." "I'd think she'd be too busy with Creid HERSELF..." Amy snorted, "But fine, if it's just Stephen and I then so be it - that's not unfamilliar, considering every other relationship I've ever been in. Maybe I'll come crawling back when you clear up some space leftover from Vic in that head of yours." "You don't have to worry about crawling back Amy. I'm not asking you to leave, I am. You can stay here... it's just... right now, until this is settled, I don't think you'd be served best being around me." Brandon sighed, almost sounding defeated. "Why don't you let me make that decision for myself?" Amy snapped back. Not realizing she was almost contradicting herself about them separating, fueled by the love they genuinely shared. "Right now we shouldn't be trying to make any decisions, let alone the ones concerning us. I... really don't know if I'll be around after all this. I think it'd be best if I just wasn't here right now." With those ominous words hanging in the air, choking up both Amy and Brandon, he turned away. Perhaps for the last time these two parted ways. They loved each other. Even now, that much was evident, but was it enough to survive what had been set in motion all those years ago at the hands of a sadistic monster?
