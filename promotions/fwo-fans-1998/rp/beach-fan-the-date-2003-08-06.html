Beach Fan(the date)

6 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
San Diego, California 8/5/03 1:00am ----- "Damn," said Triple X, glancing down at his watch. "Haven't hung out this late, since ... well ..." He looked up at Carla, noticing the awkward expression on her face. "I'm sorry," he touched her hand, gently. "I'm so sorry. We came here to have a good time, if I'm not mistaken, we're having a good time. I shouldn't be spoiling it with stories of my past relationships." Carla smiled, "It's okay, Sean ... I knew about your relationship with her before I asked you on this date. I knew how you felt..." She swallowed hard. "...Or still may feel about her while my friends and I were at the arena cheering for you. A relationship is a tough thing to get over, and I understand that. ...trust me, I do." Sean "Triple X" Stevens had just come off of a very productive night of fWo action. After missing two straight events, he defended his Internet Title against an up-and-comer and felt really good about the show they put on. If anybody was upset with him for missing the two shows ... they weren't after the match was over, as he got a standing ovation. Afterwards, he was amped ... so amped that he was actually starting to look forward to his 'date' with the woman he had met earlier - Carla Lee. Because he was genuinely nice, he also invited four of her friends, who he also provided free tickets for to attend the event with her. That served two purposes, it kept everyone happy, and it made Sean a little more comfortable with the whole 'date' situation. But, it was now one o'clock in the morning. ...the friends had been gone for atleast an hour. Sean and Carla spent that time drinking and getting to know one another. It's no crime to meet new people, he would tell himself all night long. "I'm glad you understand, Carla ... it's a tough situation to be in. Do you have somebody special? A boyfriend, perhaps? I'd hate to be the only person with relationship problems, I'd feel so much more comfortable if you vented with me," he forced himself to smile. Carla could tell it was fake. "Yeah ... I do," She said very matter-of-factly. "Thing is, I'm not sure he feels the way I do, and I'm afraid to put myself out there, because I hate rejection." Stevens took another sip of his beer, before looking her directly in the eyes. "Carla, you are beautiful. ...you've got a lot going on for yourself, you're about to get your degree. Rejection can be a bitch ... but, I can honestly say ... any guy that rejects you has to either be on drugs, or gay." They both silently stared at the other, before laughing uncontrollably. "You're so funny, Sean! Not to mention charming, with a boat load of personality. This has far exceeded my expectations ... and, I can honestly say ... whoever stereotyped wrestlers as dumb, sex crazed jocks are idiots themselves." Trip glanced down at his watch again, "Shit," he said out loud. "I gotta get up early tomorrow, I have a meeting with the production team. I hate to cut out on you, Ms. Lee, I really had a good time tonight. This is something that I desperately needed." "I had fun, too. Want me to catch a cab?" Sean hissed, "Hell no! ...I brought you here, I'll take you home. I got the rental 'till tomorrow anyway, plus it's on my way, it's no biggie." Both, Sean and Carla got up from their seats and walked up to the cash register to pay the bill. From there, they walked to the car, Sean, being a gentleman, opened up her door for her to let her in, Carla, being very unselfish, reached over and unlocked the door for him. The ride home would be filled with more conversation, more laughter, and convinced Stevens that he had to keep in touch with her. He didn't know how she felt about it, but as he pulled up to her hotel room, he figured he'd ask. "So ... if a guy wanted to keep in touch with you, how would he?" Causing her to smile shyly. "I mean, you seem like good people, and that's what I need in my life right now, good people." "Let me run up to my apartment, get a pen and some paper, I'll be back with the number to my cell," She said, getting out of the car and shutting the door. Sean nodded, expecting her to take awhile ... when she came running back down, ten seconds later, he knew something wasn't right. Carla opened the door and sat down inside the car. "Oh ... my ... God. I am so embarrassed," she said, glancing over at Sean. "My roomate, she's in there right now, with a guy on the floor in our living room ... naked." That certainly caught Sean by surprise. "Wow ... does that ... umm ... happen a lot?" "No! I'm not that kind of girl and she knows that! She probably thought I'd be home later than I came in. I can't go back in there, and I won't." "That's understandable. So what do you want to do?" "Aren't you staying at a hotel nearby?" "Yeah, about four blocks from here..." "I don't get paid 'till Friday, so I'm short for cash, but if you could rent me out a room for the night, I promise I'd pay it back as soon as I got it." "Paying it back isn't my concern, Carla. It's just that ... I left my credit cards in my gym bag at the arena, because I knew I'd be leaving and coming straight to the bar with you. ...I'm sure nobody's there now. It's two o'clock. And, I have a couple of dollars in my pocket, but that hotel is expensive." She frowned, "Isn't there anything you could do?" "I mean ... other than offer you a pillow in my room, nothing. And, I'm sure you don't want to do th--" "--I would love to," Carla interrupted, trying desperately to contain her excitement. Sean knew that this was wrong, Carla was somebody he wanted to keep around as a friend because she exuded positivity. ...but, nothing more. Tonight wasn't so much about her as it was about him moving on with his life. But, this was something he didn't expect, or want. But, what other alternatives did he have? Make this poor girl corrupt herself in her roomates wild ways? Put her out on the street? Sean stuck the key in the ignition and pulled out of the parking lot. "Okay then..." He said as he began his journey to his hotel room. And, that's how this entire mess all started.
