And now's the time...

15 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"What in the fuck do you think I'm pissed off about? Don't play this bullshit with me Brandon. This is your entire fault. Every damn thing that has happened to us is your fault. Nothing ever happens to the mighty hero though, it's always us, always your family." Anastasia Roche seethed with anger. The events of the last two days defied rational definition. In one brief second Ana's entire life had been turned upside down. Her and her brother Chris had been out on the town with her two children. Trying to make the best of things with her husband off on another international tour Ana figured an evening with the former wrestling superstar would do her good. Little did she realize that life would begin a cyclical downward spiral. Just when things looked their brightest, just when you would figure it was time for things to even out, the bottom would fall out of your existence. That seemed to be the norm for her and her family, and frankly she had grown tired of it. "What are you trying to say Angel, that somehow I made some loon shoot Chris in the head? Maybe you need to rethink things, why is it always something I've done that causes problems for our family? I just don't fucking understand!" Brandon had held back as long as he could, for nearly half an hour Ana stormed through their house gathering bags, slamming doors. He simply steered clear. He knew how upset she was over the shooting of her brother. It affected her even more since she knew that the attacker had targeted her and his sons. Brandon figured a bit of space and then they could figure things out. "You don't fucking understand? How about this... that bitch walked right up to us and SAID we were in her fucking way. She said we needed to go away so she could be happy. Why is that Brandon? Why do you think she was trying to kill me and your kids? It was YOU. It's always you." Ana had nearly spit the words at her husband, the tone sending Brandon over the edge. "What!? Everything that's happened to this family is MY fault? My Dad got shot and paralyzed. My fault? The house at the Bay burned down. My fault? You fucking around with Creid. My fault?" With that line crossed Brandon stopped. He knew what was coming next and as Ana lashed out at him he instinctively caught her wrist and pulled her to him. She pushed away flailing; the anger in her strengthened her. She pulled free of his grasp and pushed against him again. She had begun to cry at this point and tears welled in Brandon's eyes as well. Once again he grabbed her, this time pulling her close and encircling her with his massive arms. "You bastard..." "Just stop. Ok? Just stop. I... I'm sorry. I'm freaked out about all of this too, just try to understand that I'm not doing this to us. Stop blaming me for everything. I've done nothing if not try and protect us." Brandon had choked back the tears at this point and held his sobbing wife close to him. He could feel the chill in the air though; this was something that was not going to be easily fixed. "But it never works Brandon no matter what, we always get hurt. You said the other day that I've been pulling away from you, I said you were just spacing out because of having to go on the road again..." "I am pissed about having to go on the road again. In the States it's a different beast, I don't mind spending the money to come home once a week but I'm not going to be able to once we go overseas. I've been pissed about everything since the whole Hernando, Tort thing fucked it up for the rest of us..." Ana interrupted Brandon's interruption. "... what I was going to say though Brandon is that you're right. I have been thinking about things and... and I think Sean, Jarod and I are going back to Ireland... to the Bay to be with your Mom..." Ana swallowed hard. The tension in her words had dried her mouth. She found it difficult to continue. "You're leaving me? What in the hell is that?!" Brandon pushed Ana away, trying to look in her eyes and understand. Trying to fathom this bombshell she was dropping on him. It was something so out of left field that it literally took his breath away. She was just upset about her brother, this was anger and confusion talking she didn't mean it. She couldn't. "I just need some time away. There's a lot of stuff I need to figure out. It's not just about what happened with Chris... although it is the thing that made my decision for me. I... we can't have Sean and Jarod around this anymore. Do you understand that? Please tell me you understand that much... please? What would have happened if Chris hadn't been there? Have you even thought about that? What if that psycho had come here two hours later, Chris would have been on his way home and we would have been sitting here defenseless." Ana looked to Brandon, trying to draw the strength from him that they had always shared. She didn't find it though; it hadn't been there lately. There was too much between them, it always interfered. "I'm getting the kids things together and Cheryl is taking Sean and Jarod to the Bay tomorrow. I'm going to stay in town with Chris for a while. You need to you know?" "I'm... going to the show tonight, I have to. There are some things that might end up happening that requires Frontier's presence. I'll be back tomorrow though, I promise. Then I have to show for Slaughter on Sunday. As soon as it's over though I'm coming home for a few days then as well..." Brandon waited for Ana to tell him it was not enough, that it was too little too late as always. Instead she just nodded her head absently as she turned and grabbed the folded clothes on the edge of the bed. It seemed that she simply didn't care what he had said or knew what his answer would be before he even said it. Maybe it wasn't worth her time to bother with it. Either way Brandon felt alone in the presence of his wife, and it was not a feeling he would get used to easily. It made him sick.
