The life and childhood Story of Tim Veneno: Betrayal

4 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Kristen has to be the hottest chick Junior ever!" "Dude, you're telling me." There they sat, the two 11th graders that Tim hated the most. Their names were not important to him, as he didn't even know them. Tim didn't know many names at his school, as he barely knew his own teachers name. The only name he needed to know was Kristen, the only name she needed to know; wasn't Tim's. She was the perfect girl, not for just any 11th grader, but for Tim. In Tim's world she was everything, she was his world, in her world Tim didn't exist. The two 11th graders caught Tim staring at Kristen, as they both didn't like that. "Hey Tom stop staring at my girlfriend!" Yelled one of the two. "Dude his name isn't Tom, its Tyler." "Whatever his name is I don't care he doesn't need to be staring at my girlfriend." Said the other 11th grader. Tim quickly tried to make himself feel like he wasn't there in the cafeteria, as he slowly slid down till his eyes could barley see the lunchroom. Mirror mirror upon the wall I'm asking you Who is the most confused of them all? Mirror mirror subservient twin screams back At me "You". Their Tim sat in his assigned seat, of course in the back of the room. No teacher would ever think of sitting Tim in the middle of the room, or the front of the room. Tim kind of liked it in the back, for he could get all his work done, and he was able to see everything that went on in the room and hear the majority of the kid's conversations. But, one conversation sparked his interest. It was between one of the 11th graders that he didn't like and his other friend. "You know that Kristen has a boyfriend don't you?" "Duh, but has it stopped me before?" "Dude, but still its like your best friends girlfriend. Isn't that different?" "No! But all I know is that, I am going to get her in the back of my car TONIGHT!" Tim couldn't believe his own ears. Even though he hated Kristen's boyfriend it still didn't make it right for this to go down. Later on that day as the school day was coming to a close Tim got up to throw a piece of trash away. He walked past Kristen's boyfriend and quickly tossed a piece of paper on his desk as it read: "Keep her with you tonight" And that was it; he didn't know what to do with it. He chuckled and then threw it back as it slammed into Tim's forehead. "You sick flawless mime, I want to break you Yeah, you're a clever one... Do you reflect me or do I reflect you, are you Inside of another world, I want to break through Yeah, you're a clever one."
