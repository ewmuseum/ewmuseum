Thursday, September 13, 2001 New Orleans, Louisiana 8:38 AM Eastern Stephanie's eyes fluttered open, slowly. They looked around, recognizing nothing. Where am I? She propped herself up, using her hands. Her headache was almost unbearable. Rick musta really tagged me good... asshole... Rick. Suddenly, she recalled the events of the previous night. The fight when she got home, the retreat to a bar, the meeting with... Wasner, I think it was... yeah, Wasner... and her acceptation of his invite to his hotel room, for she had had no place to stay. Yes, she had been taking a chance, but something had told her that Wasner posed no threat to her. Apparently she had been right. She got up off the bed and looked around. Wasner was nowhere to be found at the moment. It was a middle-of-the-road hotel room, nothing fancy but certainly no fleabag. She grabbed the remote and turned on the TV. Scenes of the senseless tragedy were still playing... God, what a mess. There were no words or thoughts that could sum it up. Shaking her head, she flipped the TV off, grabbed her bag and walked into the bathroom. 25 minutes later, she emerged with thoughts of Scott Wasner on her mind. The man was obviously going through some tough times, yet he still had enough compassion in him to give her a place to sleep. He seemed like a nice enough guy.
