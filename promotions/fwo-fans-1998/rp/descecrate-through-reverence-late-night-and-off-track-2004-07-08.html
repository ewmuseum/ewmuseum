Descecrate Through Reverence: Late-night and off-track.

8 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 28th, 2004 (3 nights after Unleashed) Orlando Amy nuzzled the pillow. Accountability. Responsibility. Responsibility not to go overboard at work. Accountability for that lack of responsibility... Accountability for a woman's broken neck. ...And, as much as she despised Nerva both personally and professionally, this was the third night that the overwhelmed redhead had gone with nearly no sleep thanks to daunting images of the limp body of that Brooklyn-born bitch. Snap. Crackle. Pop. They all knew what had happened, but it was labelled accidental. Amy...Aimz...admitted responsibility, but Disney wouldn't have that. Not with ratings the way they were. A tiny, under-the-table fine was all she got and it nearly made her sick. Not the fine, but the manner in which it was all brushed beneath the rug. What would happen if one of her opponents lost it and went even further? Would that, too, get swept aside for a measley fine not even made public? ...What of Nerva's return, if she ever did? If Amy went unpunished for 'accidentally' snapping two discs in a woman's neck, what was stopping Nerva from doing the exact same thing - or worse - to her? It's not like either of them cared or lacked the twenty-five hundred she paid for breaking someone's neck. If Nerva returned. Amy may have ended someone's career. This business kept her as sane as she could get, and she knew full well that it was the same for Nerva - this was passion, life...and she might've taken someone's away because of what? Her reputation getting stained? A few cuts on her arm? She pressed the side of her head hard against the sweat-soaked pillow. Both the fabric and her wild locks were cool to the touch from the summer nightmares - this was the salty afterglow of a big mistake. Shifting again lightly, avoiding waking her companion, she heaved a heavy sigh. ''Stop feeling guilty about it,'' came Brandon's voice. It surprised his wife, as he'd gotten out of bed and picked up the glass of water on one of the end tables with her so lost in thought that she'd not even felt the mattress shifting under his 300-something pounds. ''You need rest, Ames.'' he said, walking around the foot of the bed to sit on its edge beside her covered, bruised legs. She took the glass of water and two extra-strength Tylenol when they were handed her way, making quick work of both substances before she spoke. ''Y...'' croaked the redhead, clearing her throat from the initial grogginess, ''You're crazy. I'm not feeling guilty about anything, I just can't sleep. It's just anxiety...I mean, we're bringing Scerinn home in a few days.'' Brandon raised his brow, locking eyes with Amy in a way that had them both feeling like he was parenting, ''Yes, we're getting her soon...but you and I both know why you're sleeping even less than usual, and it's got nothing to do with our family.'' Amy held Brandon's gaze but soon looked down to the blanket she was fidgeting with, sighing heavily. ''Everyone in that place is full of shit. A bunch of wannabe roughneck fools. You, BQ...you're the only people - people - there right now who've been able to keep your heads out of your asses. Everybody else...it's just absolute bullshit. Total crap. Nobody wants to do anything but make a name for themselves...what happened to fighting for honour, not ego?'' ''I...'' Brandon began, but he was swiftly cut off. ''I broke a woman's neck, BL. Snapped her neck...two discs...she might never wrestle again. I did it because she was threatening me, not my ego...but what of the others? They're stupid and weak - unbelievably sloppy. Worse than me, even. The belts mean nothing anymore because their champions are half-assed clowns just galavanting about trying to feed their ego-centric gimmicks. As a business, it's bullshit. The other women are useless and riding on whatever waves their associates make...'' ''What're you trying to say?'' ''I'm saying that I took out one of the only decent, driven, talented people who're still around. The only woman in that entire federation that's worth a second thought to me - I broke her neck, so now what? Do I wait for her to come back and try to do the same to me?'' she sighed once more, ''I've had the hardcore title more than the majority of men, I've held the US Title...but what do I do, wait for Kinkade or Nerva to come back and give me a decent fight?'' ''You look for someone worth your time.'' said Brandon, ''Get management to book you randomly. Talk to them about things. I'm sure they don't mind if you avoid people you don't consider highly. You've been here a long time and gone through enough to earn your keep...it's just a matter of proving it. Keep going out there, keep winning.'' The smaller of the two redheads grinned, ''I'm rarely given that fighting chance. You know as well as I do that most guys there would piss themselves on TV before they'd show a girl going over them.'' ''Demand it.'' ''...And what about Nerva? After taking the only decent female competitor they had, nobody on the business end is exactly happy with me.'' ''You were given the chance to learn from your mistakes for a reason, Amy. I can't say that even I agreed with what you did, but it's what had to be done at the time...accident or otherwise...and you've been given the chance to keep going as if nothing had happened. You know how that works, so do it. Fight for whatever reason you want, but you're on the right track now...you've stopped letting people walk all over you.'' Amy smiled, ''Yeah, I guess. I'll fight who I want to, if I want to. I'll give Disney what they need to 'keep the audience interested' in me, and that'll be that. No more business getting perso---'' ''Don't set yourself up for a let-down.'' winked her husband. ''These days, it always gets personal. Hell, you just personally attacked over half of the roster.'' ''Mm...fuck 'em.'' Brandon chuckled as he watched his wife fight to keep her eyes open. They were having a conversation, but the intent - little did she know - had been to talk the frantic redhead to sleep. ''Just go through the motions,'' he kissed her gently on the forehead, ''Until things feel okay again.'' Walking back around and sliding into his spot next to her, Brandon gathered Amy close to him and spoke in softer tones as he felt her slipping away into a sleep so desperately needed. ''You've only yourself to worry about.'' Amy tried to nod, but in truth she'd barely heard a single one of his last words. ...And when she dreamed, Nerva was nowhere to be found. [BACK to "Step Up"]
