THE ROADS NOT TAKEN Chapter One: A Serpent in the Streets There's a street in the Bronx that looks like any other. It's got cars parked at the curb, a broken streetlight, and a bunch of apartment buildings all around. There's a real sense of community in the air, due to the fact that nearly every person living in the neighborhood has done so their entire lives. Sure, there's a sense of street crime, but outside of an occasional incident involving kids from other neighborhoods, it had been virtually wiped out fifteen years ago. Two landmarks of the neighborhood happen to be across the street from each other. As is the case with the rest of the street, you'd never guess anything special happened there. On the one side was an old gymnasium with a sign on the front that hasn't had a decent light in it in six years. COOP'S Terence Cooper has been running independent wrestling cards for nearly sixty years. Last winter he retired, so to speak, but people in this business never truly leave until they have to do so. "The kids are really progressin', wouldn't'cha say?" "No doubt, no doubt." Coop may be old, but he's very hands- on with his promotion, and with his kids.
