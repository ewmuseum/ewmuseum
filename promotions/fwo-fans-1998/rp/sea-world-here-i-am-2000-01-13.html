Sea World, here I am!

13 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Sea World, here I am! January 13, 2000 ::The scene- a parking lot. Not just any parking lot, the parking lot at Sea World. The camera is kind of fuzzy, and shaky. The cameraman turns it around on his face to reveal that he is none other than Havok.:: Havok: Hey. I'm here in Florida at Sea World, trying to have a little fun and maybe buy a whale. The fWo wouldn't give me a personal cameraman for my vacation, so I brought the patended "Havok-cam." That's right, the "Havok-cam," with the best damn cameraman ever... no, not Geoff Green. Hey! Did you know that he really sucks at Mystery Date??? Anyway, the best damn cameraman ever... me! I'm gonna video tape a few things for you guys while I make my way through Sea World. So, let's go on in! ::The camera shuts off, and then comes back on outside Shamu Stadium.:: Havok: Alright, I'm here outside Shamu Stadium, and I'm going to watch the Shamu show to see which whale I want to buy. I'm gonna shut off the Havok-cam now, since I don't want to get it wet. I'll see you after the show. ::He walks in, then shuts off the camera. He turns it back on outside of the show. He is soaking wet, and has an odd expression on his face.:: Havok: Dude! That wasn't cool! That stupid whale got me all wet. Wait, I have an idea! Follow me! ::The camera shows him running across the park. It shakes unsteadily, but he stops, right in front of the dolphin tank.:: Havok: I was thinking, why don't I buy a dolphin?!? ::The dolphin then jumps up and splashes him.:: Havok: HEY! Man, these animals keep getting me soaked. What animals can I buy that won't get me all wet? ::He looks over and sees a bunch of seals on the other side of a fenc.:: Havok: Nice! Seals! ::He runs over and pets the seal.:: Havok: Good seal... look! He's clapping for me! Like all my fans! I'm gonna buy me a seal! ::He turns to an attendant who works at the park.:: Havok: How much for the white one? Attendant: I beg your pardon. Havok: The white seal... how much does he cost? Attendant: Sir, the seals are not for sale. Havok: What?!? Attendant: They are not for sale. Havok: Everything's for sale, ask Chris Universal, he's a sell-out! Attendant: Who? Havok: Not a wrestling fan, huh? Attendan: Sir, I'll have to ask you to leave if you don't stop trying to buy this seal. Havok: Give me the damn seal! ::She holds a radio up to her mouth.:: Attendant: Security! Please report to the seal area. Havok: Uh oh... ::He runs away and turns off the Havok-cam. It comes back on in the parking lot. It is showing a man selling fake jewelry.:: Havok: Well, no whale for me. Hey! Look at that guy! Looks like I can spend my money after all! ::He runs over to the guy and points to a watch.:: Havok: Is that a Rolex?!? Man: Uh... sure. Havok: Nice! How much?!? Man: How much do you have. Havok: After my toy store shopping spree, I have exactly seven-hundred and thirty-two dollars and fifty-three cents! Man: Well, it normally costs more, but since you're so nice, I'll give it to you for all you got. ::He hands the man the money and takes the watch.:: Havok: Thanks, buddy! Man: Anytime! ::The man runs away and Havok looks at the watch.:: Havok: Gee... I didn't know Rolex started with an "F." I'll have to use that next time I play Scrabble! Well, this is the Havok-cam, signing off. ::The camera fades out.::
