18 March 2002

"Ivy?" "Sup?" "Let me borrow your laptop?" "Sure." "You and Trip headed out tonight or tomorrow?" "Tomorrow." "Solid. Seeya in Kansas City." Eli Flair sits down in the darkened lockerroom and opens up Ivy's laptop. Her Web Design software clicks to life with a familiar template, with a new 'Original Nobody' logo spread across the top. Before its pilfering by another wrestling promotion, the website was the home base for everything relating to Team Extreme - Eli Flair, Poison Ivy, and Randy Harders. Since its removal, it has showcased a 'political statement' about the use of intellectual property. He sighs, and committs his fingers to the keyboard.... It's been a while, my friends. Travel, injury, and personal drama has been preventing my return to this hallowed space to check in with my thoughts. This hallowed space, the only place on the entire web where you can hear the unfiltered truth about myself and Poison Ivy and where we're headed. Right now, I'm tired. Sunday Night Slaughter just ended, and I'm tired. I didn't even wrestle, and I'm tired. I spent most of the show backstage, trying to hunt down Donaven Winters and ask him about the Hardcore Title. We didn't get a winner in our match the previous week, and I was hoping to find out the odds of us having some way to find out who the better man is. Because I'll tell ya, folks.... there's never been anybody in this business who has been so identically driven as I am, except for Donaven Winters. Taking a look at the most high- profile of my feuds over the course of eight years, there's never been anyone I've been so evenly matched with. Platoon? One fluke victory and three submission losses do not qualify him. His lone victory over me was what turned it all around and got me focused on actually wrestling. Dr. Silver? It's true I never got my hand raised, but I'm taking a long- term view on it. And the long- term of it is that while I'm headlining sold- out PPV events for the two top wrestling promotions in the world, Doc is a multiple run- in victim in bingo halls and parking lots. He wants another match nowadays, that white trash bitch can't afford me. Attaxx had one victory over me as well, but it was quickly overturned due to outside interference. Every subsequent rematch saw our managers get involved. Up until the last one. With Rosenthal and Ivy locked in a cage twenty feet over the ring, Attaxx tapped the mat like a whipped dog. Troy Windham? Kevin Powers? The wars I fought with them don't have anything like winners. Troy and I have taken gallons of blood from each other, Kevin and I have spent more time as uneasy allies than we have enemies, but while both of these men and I were evenly matched, it doesn't compare to what I'm currently experiencing with Donaven Winters. I know what he's going to do before he does it. He knows what I'm going to do before I do it. His strengths are stamina, resourcefulness, and an insane amount of willpower that keeps him on his feet a hell of a lot longer than he should be. Just like me. I'd be lying if I said I didnt' care about his Hardcore Title, if I said I don't dream about it belonging to me. But there's something much more pressing that I want to know. That I need to know. Who is the better man? Who has that edge? Which of us is truly the toughest bastard in the fWo? And I'll tell ya something.... I wouldn't even mind losing that match. Being second- toughest to Donaven Winters is more of an honor than being top dog in nearly every other promotion in the world. Trust me on that. Eli hesitates for a moment, but with the modem plugged squarely into the wall, hits "SAVE PAGE AND UPLOAD" on the screen. It'll be a big step - finally breaking a measure of silence he's held for over a year. It's all part of the game, however.... all part of the game.
