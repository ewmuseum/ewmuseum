The Story Of My Life.2.A

7 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
October 4th, 1978. 2:22 a.m. The father stood silently looking over the edge of the crib, almost like his chin was what kept him hanging there. He looked down at the small child with eyes that stood open to their farthest extent, the blood shot eyes looked at the child maniacally. He hung an arm down rubbing the child as it slept, then finally he picked the child up out of the crib and began walking over towards the window. Slowly he swung the window open and looked out across the city. From this elevation the city seemed so calm and peaceful, the child looked so calm and peaceful as it hung out of the window. Only being held onto by it's under arms. Then the calm was interrupted as the child slowly began to whimper, and slowly that whimper turned into an all out cry. The mother who was sleeping quietly suddenly rolled out of bed, realizing it was her turn to take care of the child. "I got it Rick, go back to sleep," she mumbled half asleep as suddenly she let out a shriek as she looked at Rick holding the child out over the edge of the window. "What the hell are you doing!" she shouted as Rick tried to recuperate from the stunning blood curling scream. "Listen Martha, it's not what you think." Rick struggled to get that simple little sentence out from his vocal cords. "Then what the hell were you doing?" Martha asked Rick, she looked over at him intently hoping he had a good reason for all of this. "They said they would stop if I killed the babe, they said they would stop asking me questions if I let him fall from the window, they said they'd leave me alone for the rest of eternity." Rick stammered out the line, he was scared of Martha right now. She slowly reached over and took the baby from him. "Who said they would stop? Rick, what are you talking about?" Martha asked as she backed away from Rick. Rick looked over at her with a questionable expression on his face. His eyes were still stretched to their farthest point, giving the same look. The only thing that changed was the expression of his mouth. "The voices, the ones I've talked to since I was a little boy. You know them Martha, I've told you before haven't I?" Rick said this aloud, the last sentence made him sound like a maniac, and he knew thats why Martha reached for the red button on the hospital bed. "Now Rick, what I'm doing is for your own good...." Martha stammered as she leaned down to the receiver on the arm of the bed and whispered something into it. Martha looked back up at Rick who walked up to Martha and stood in front of her. "The child hurts the voices, they tell me this Martha. The voices only talk and shriek when they're in pain. I have to get rid of the baby. Now give me it!" Rick shouted as he reached for the baby. The two struggled for a few moments before finally four armed security guards came storming into the room with their guns pulled. "Rick Thompson, your under arrest." One of the officers said to Rick, who instantly put his hands into the air. He looked over at little Brandon and Martha, Rick let out a sigh as he walked towards the officers and let them cuff him. Martha clutched onto the child and curled up on the bed, hoping it was all a nightmare.
