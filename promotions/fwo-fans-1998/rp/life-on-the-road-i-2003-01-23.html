Life on the Road . I

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After a decent night of sleep, all three collective members of fWo’s Triality rose out of their hotel room beds ready to move onto the next venue in Montreal, Quebec, Canada. It was bitterly cold outside and as the morning hours rolled on all three finally gathered their belongings and bundled up comfortably. This week they would be traveling together, which is something they rarely ever did nowadays. Ever since they moved up from Orlando’s wrestling grounds, the relationship between the three friends has become rocky. The pressure constantly put on them by the fWo’s now road agents, then trainers has been tremendous. The most notable difference is how distant Seth has gotten from Tisha and Troy. If it were up to him and only him, he would have gotten to Montreal last night around 1 AM and would be sleeping, alone in his own secluded hotel room. If it were really up to him, he would have more than likely chosen a hotel that no one would have thought of to check to find him if he was needed, so he could be alone… and focus on what he wants and not what others want. “Are you guys ready?” Tisha laid her last suitcase in the back of the suburban vehicle. She reached up to close the trunk door but the massive hand of Troy stopped it half way. “This is Seth’s bag… he forgot to put it in the car before he went in to pay for last nights fee’s.” She nodded and let him put the baggage in the trunk. She was the one doing the driving this time but as she got to the driver side door, she saw the shadow of someone sitting in the drive side door. She nudged her sunglasses that cleverly hid her face so no one would notice who she was, down on the tip of her nose. Seth held out his hand through the opened window. “Give me the keys, I’m driving—” Tisha wasn’t sure what to say but muttered something, “But I thought…” Seth grabbed the keys and started the engine to the suburban. “Either get in or I’m leaving you here in Toronto.” She hesitated but got into the backset of the vehicle. Troy hopped into the front passenger seat and the car pulled out of the hotel parking lot. As the suburban moved down the road towards Montreal, Tisha could only sit back and think to herself, after all there was eerie silence growing over this friendship. What has happened to us? We were once so close, nothing could pull us apart and now… just look. God, please tell me what do I do? I love both of these men, I really do but… it’s beginning to be too much. Seth… he’s just different now, different in a bad way… She took off the sunglasses and stared out the window, watching the countryside roll by. It’s almost as if he’s hiding something from me.
