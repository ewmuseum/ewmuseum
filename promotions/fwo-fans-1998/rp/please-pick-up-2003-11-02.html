Please Pick Up

2 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am Cody's wide-known secret. Everyone knows now. There's no point living a lie anymore. I don't have to hide my dark desires. I don't have to live up to an image that I'm really not. I told Clarisse in front of the whole world that I've been fucking a different woman every night. Yes, I used that exact word. Fucking. Davis wanted the truth. So I gave the truth. Davis tore the truth out of my throat. Clarisse, please pick up. Six rings, seven rings, eight rings... I've lost count. She's not picking up the phone because she doesn't want to talk to me. But some stupid part of my brain thinks she does. Hope makes a man look weak. Why am I even doing this? Because I love Clarisse. I also love Matilda. I don't want to lose them because they are a part of me. If I lose them, a lot of me will be lost in the wind. Once again, her and I were thoroughly embarassed on national television. Our dirty laundry spilled out in the ring for everyone to see. Every fluid-filled condom I used is out in the open. I am Cody's utter shame. The phone keeps ringing and ringing. I'm wondering if she's even home. I really care, because I'm going to be on this line all night long. I'm just a pathetic man who thought fucking a different woman every night would make me miss Clarisse more. Screw this. I hang up the phone and head out of the hotel room. I need some fresh air. I need to think about how I'm going to take care of business. There are three guys who kicked the shit out of me at Legends, and then there's Austin Davis. I will get my revenge.
