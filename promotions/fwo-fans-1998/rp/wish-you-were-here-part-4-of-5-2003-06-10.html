Wish You Were Here (Part 4 of 5)

10 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
limb by limb and tooth by tooth tearing up inside of me everyday every hour wish that i was bullet proof. wax me mold me heat the pins and stab them in you have turned me into this just wish that it was bullet proof. so pay me money and take a shot lead-fill the hole in me i could burst a million bubbles all surrogate & bullet proof. *** December 25th, 2000 Location: An apartment, in the slums of Seattle Levite Prod lay on his back on the bed in the middle of the room. The room was filthy, so was the life Prod lead. The smell was sickening, to the right was fresh vomit. Only an hour ago Prod had rolled over to puke from the heroin he injected into his veins. Afterward he shot up another point, forgetting completely about the puke. In the corner of the room was urine stains from countless nights where he could not make it to the toilet and resorted to the corner of his apartment instead. It never mattered, he didn't use that corner anyway. Levite couldn't remember what had happened the night before, and or the day, and or the week... and or the year. His life had been a blur since reaching Seattle, living life on the streets. Unable to find a job, no one wants a rapist working for them. Drugs became his only friend, the one thing that never turn it's back on him. Never left him alone, never pushed him away. Wasn't that always the story? Isn't that how it always works? Or so it goes. Where was the depth? Why was he different? How was he strong? The truth, was that he wasn't. Levite Prod lay on his back looking to the ceiling and seeing nothing, his body began to raise off the bed. Prod lightly moved his fingers feeling the heavy air rub against his skin. He could not move his body, Prod however did not struggle at all. Levite could hear his heart beat, he attempted to laugh in joy of the rather unusual vibration he felt on his chest but could not muster enough energy to do so. He was immobile, in limbo, and as he floated, his heart slowed to a halt. He could not longer breath, but felt comfortable not doing so. Hello Levite. Hi. Do you know who I am? You are death. You are wrong. This isn't fair. What isn't fair? I don't want to die, this isn't fair. I never did anything, they shunned me, they wouldn't believe. I'm not bad, they made me bad, they made me do it. Do what? Drugs, they drove me to it. Please, don't let me die. You are not dead. You're lying. Wake up Levite. Wake up Levite, wake up. WAKE up Levite, wake UP. WAKE UP LEVITE! WAKE UP... "WAKE UP!" Levite shot up inhaling a deep breath of fresh air. He was immediatly blinded by florescent lights and closed his eyes, only to slowly open them back up. His lips and mouth were so dry it was hard to speak and when finally able to mutter a word his top lip ripped slightly open. His head was so heavy and pounding with pain. Suddenly a warm hand touched lightly on his chest, and the most beautiful voice he would ever hear spoke softly in his ear. "Lay back Levite, you are okay now. You just overdosed on heroin. You almost died on us." Levite felt his head hit the large pillow and his back molded softly into the bed. Levite turned his head to stare faintly at the nurse, speaking roughly with a raspy voice, "I saw God." A few of the staff laughed quietly in the background as the nurse looked down at him and smiled, "I'm sure you did." She turned to walk out of the room, when Levite reached out and grabbed her wrist, she turned back to him, his eyes still wandering, "I saw God." The nurse looked off to the rest of the staff, "He's delirious," she continued to walk away leaving Levite's hand to hang, "Typical talk from junkies," almost out of the room Levite managed to hear one last comment, "Get him out of here fast, we need the bed, and it's not like he'll have any visitors."
