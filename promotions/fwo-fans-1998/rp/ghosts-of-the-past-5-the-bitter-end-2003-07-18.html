Ghosts Of The Past [5] The Bitter End

18 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Go back to 4... September 24th 1999. The Bitter End was New York's oldest rock club, a pretty beaten up little hole down on 147 Bleaker Street that Neil Young once played. It wasn't the ideal place to take a woman on a night out if you were a regular guy. Kellen Kinkade was far from a regular guy, as he and Amy Campbell ducked inside the crowded out club to shelter from the rain that was hammering down upon them they pushed and shoved through the crowd before arriving at the bar. "Strongest whisky you've got and some one fifty one for madame here." Kinkade grunted at the bar tender who raised an eyebrow to which Kinkade replied "She's French." Kinkade turned back to Amy who was rubbing her hands together after coming in from the cold outside "You know a guy cares about you when he remembers your drink, still not worried I'm gonna get ungentlemanly are you?" "Impressive." Amy's amused smirk and a raised, pierced eyebrow topped off her somewhat sarcastic astonishment. To Kinkade's surprise, she yawned strongly...and coughed the smokey air that she'd inhaled right back out. "Gack.......sorry, I'm tired - I live with two teenaged girls I'm very close to. Naturally, they needed every detail of where I'd been before I was allowed to so much as blink last night." "No problem." A quick sip of whiskey stopped Kinkade for a second "Just don't breathe that deeply again in here, you'll get cancer." The redhead laughed, "So you're pretty used to things around here, huh?" "Yep, unfortunately." Kinkade said with a smile "So you probably think I'm a little wierd for bringing you to a shithole like this so I'd better explain, the drink is cheap and the bullets are few. This is about the only joint in the whole of New York where I don't get shot at." Amy laughed, "Well, not getting shot is always a plus - But this place doesn't exactly scream 'I come to get laid'." She grinned, then cocked her head. "Then again...neither do you, actually! You're the first guy I've met here who I've felt somewhere near comfortable with. I mean, I don't know how easy I act, but I'm not exactly the kind of girl who runs around screaming for people to try and fuck her...but that's ALL guys seem to pick up on around here." "Ah... it shows that bad?" Kinkade replied with a disappointed sigh "I guess when you've only got one eye you use it for the important stuff, if I spent too long scoping at women chances are I'd end up with more holes than Swiss cheese." "But you don't think like others..." With the realization that the subject matter could be touchy for whatever reason - She really DIDN'T know much about this guy's past - Amy backed off slightly with a meek grin and quick subject change. "Err...yeah, is there decent food here?" "Is the pope a Jew?" Kinkade replied before suddenly setting his drink down with a serious expression "Back to that eye priority thing... you might want to duck for a minute or two." Amy glanced around nervously, "Why's that?" "Couple of brothers just walked in the door, I'm gessing their ghetto asses didn't come to stare at the signed picture of Neil Young over there." Kinkade reached out and gently put his hand on the back of Amy's neck before pushing her a little closer to the bar "They're gonna spot me, but they're gonna spot your hair even quicker... you ready to dodge out of here?" The little redhead didn't respond, only flicked the hood of her Metallica sweater up over her head and glanced to Kellen. Within a second, they were out the back door and into the rain... "Well," Amy finally spoke after a few seconds, seeing Kinkade trying to plan. "We can't exactly stay outside all night..." "No we can't, I think we should go back to my place... I don't want you to be alone when these punks are tailing us around." Kinkade took a deep breath for a moment "Gonna ask you to keep an open mind about this." In a split second he'd whipped off his jacket and wrapped it around his forearm, which he promptly sent crashing through the driving seat window of a car parked next to him. He'd unlocked it, clambered in and after what seemed like no time at all managed to hotwire it to life. Kinkade looked out through the broken window at Amy who stood looking on shocked "So are you getting in or what?" "Well..........ah, what the hell - The crack dealer who owns this probably already wants your balls on a mantle for whatever reason." With a last stab at sick humor, she ran around the car and stepped in the passenger's side, looking even more nervous than before as the car sped into motion and vanished into the heavy rain.
