Song from the Heavens, Humming from Hell - 3 - Part One

5 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The doorway to the Seacoast Suites pushed open with ease and a rush of cool air suddenly smacked Angel Dalton in the face, the feeling was so refreshing Angel almost stopped in his tracks. He felt sweat cling to his hand as he brushed his hair, the smell of smoke seemed to leak out of his pours as Angel could smell the prominent scent of cigarettes on himself. That and an added layer of rum on his tongue from a few drinks he had sucked back, not to take away the edge but to help increase it. He knew what he needed to do. "Hello and welcome to the Seacoast Suites, my name is Henry how may I-" A.D. cut him off, "It's cool Henry, I'm not here to check in, I have a friend I need to deliver a message to. He's a tall guy, big, a little on the rough side. Steve Douglas, you heard of him?" Henry turned edgy and his eyes fidgeted, he looked to the right at Greg who stood firmly upright at the elevator, Henry's eyes fell back on Angel, "I'm not allowed to divulge that information." Angel smiled and lowered his head, as he brought his eyes back up he slid a fifty dollar bill on the table, "What information?" Angel said in somewhat of a mocking tone.
