fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Before the match..... January 09, 2000 ::: The scene opens with Matt Douglas dragging a metal folding chair to the ring where tonight's Sunday Slaughter is going to be held. He is dressed in his wrestling attire::: ::: When he reaches the ring, he opens the chair and takes a seat::: ::: Matt notices that the camera is filming::: Matt: Oh, hey Green. I just figured I would come out and park myself in the ring, until my debut tonight. Well, I am not going to actually stay out here the whole time. I just figured I would come out early, to get a feel of the ring. As you can see, that isn't that hard of a job, but I figured I would sit down and see if I could imagine how filled this place is going to be tonight. Now, for tonight, I am going to give Eron a new definition to the words "high flying." But hey, I am not here to brag, since this is my first match. But maybe later on that will be in order. Say, Green, you ever get that feeling that you think that something is going to happen. I feel like something is going to happen tonight, being that I am facing the International champion and all. Oh well, I will just have to wait and see. You can leave now, I might be here for a while. ::: The scene fades to black as Matt Douglas is standing in the ring, and scanning the arena:::
