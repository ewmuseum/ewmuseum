Fake.

1 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A painful realization: knowing that you no longer have anything else to give. Fingers dug into the deepest wounds as a trickle of blood dripped down the side of my face. My newfound tear ducts; a somber stare toward the ground. Allyra brought a soaked towel to my face and dabbed innocently. Despite her efforts, the tears bore a flowing ravine down my pale skin. "Allyra, it's different when you're a teenager." She removed the towel and looked at me with those sunrise eyes. "Everything's different when you're a teenage, but I'll humor you. What's different?" "When you're a teenager, most want nothing else but to belong. You change yourself, you fake yourself, and do whatever is required to maintain some sort of mask. It's acceptable then, but it isn't now. I don't know how many more days I can go out there and act the way I did," I stood up from the bench and eyed my surroundings. That same hotel room, duffel bags zipped with tattered shirts hanging from the pockets. Allyra rose behind me and stepped forward, placing a fragile and dying hand on my aching shoulder. All adversity dissipated with that touch... and a rush of apathy and optimism overcame me. With a gentle brush, she turned my weakened state toward her. Her eyes gleamed with an overcast of sunlight reflecting off of them. "Trent, I am not going to pretend like I even remotely comprehend this business you're in. All I can say is that it seems as if everyone does the same thing you're doing. They play a part, and get paid for doing so. It is a job, and everyone must suppress something in the work environment. To think you can be paid and have absolute freedom is ignorant. "So, don't let it get to you," Allyra finished. The paragraph was monument compared to previous. I brushed the comment aside with a literal motion. I stand up and run my finger down the scar on my face. "It won't last. People can sniff out a fake from a mile away, and I never was one for acting. I'll ride this out as long as I can, but it just seems so useless at this point. Everything will end when I'm read for a fake. "You and I will be worse off at the end of this ordeal." Images of burned bridges and broken bonds formed, overwhelming like a flash flood. Allyra sighed outwardly and leaned back, her stomach slightly showing under the black top. Milky white skin with no discoloration. Confliction dominated the scene as I reared back from any physical contact. The ceiling must have been a lot more interesting than I was at that moment. A hard swallow. I sat next to her and looked over my shoulder. Her eyes shifted up as her skin tone lips formed a smile. The contortion seemed foreign. "Why were you silent when we met? Why are you still silent to everyone besides me?" I asked. My knack for ruining a beautiful environment displayed. No poetic speech or soft touch could have intervened successfully. Allyra grabbed a section of the blanket from the spread and hugged the fabric to her face. She rolled onto her side and brought her knees to her chest. Everything went silent and I realized what I had asked. Fake and failure. I left.
