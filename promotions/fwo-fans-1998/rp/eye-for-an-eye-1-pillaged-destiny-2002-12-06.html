Eye For An Eye [1] Pillaged Destiny

6 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Every night, I am visited by the same nightmare. The same horrific visions, the same terrible accident... I see it all unfold before my very eye, no matter how hard I try to change the course of it, no matter how much I scream and resist... I always find myself walking down the same road, to the same horrific fate. A nightmare, which I can never change. A nightmare that was... and is a reality. December 16th 1974. "No... I won't do it." "No? What do you mean no Vince? I've known you for how long? I made you Vince... you can't do this to me... not now!" An argument, in the backstage confines of a wrestling show... it was neither new nor out of place, with sweat dripping down his chiselled body, "Iron" Vince Kinkade stood shaking his head defiantly. Jimmy Valentine balled up his fists with rage, he might have been an old man... but he knew that he was going to have to get into it with Kinkade... he looked up and stared hard at the WWC title resting over his champion's shoulder, it was the foundation of the house which he had built, but the time had come for it to relocate. Vince Kinkade scoffed furiously, before staring Jimmy in the eye and gripping him sternly by the collar. "Listen Jimmy, I've known you for as long as I can remember... you've been like a Father to me, when I was out on my ass with nowhere to go, you put a roof over my head... so you know better than anyone that I wouldn't be refusing to do this if I had a choice... I can't work the January 1st show... Martha is already a week overdue, she's ready to go into labour anytime now, and I've gotta be there for her." Vince pleaded. Jimmy looked down at the hand gripping him, and swatted it away in disgust... this time the act simply wasn't going to cut it... Vince was right, he did know him better than anyone. "No Vince... this time we do it my way, you're right... I do know you, and that's why I have to question your motives on this one... is it that you don't want to miss the birth of your child... or simply that you don't want to drop that title belt?" Jimmy questioned. Vince screwed up his face in a disgruntled manor... and turned his back on his mentor. "Fuck you, Jimmy." Vince uttered. Jimmy smiled. "Ahhhhhh, so it's like that is it pal? See Vince, you're getting way to predictable... I know you too well old friend, which is why I know that child or no child, you'd be there if this was anything other than a defeat." Vince continued to stand with his back to Jimmy, breathing heavily. "I knew it! Damnit Vince... sometimes I don't understand you... Hanson is ready! It's his time to shine, c'mon man... you've held the belt for the last eighteen months already... the time has come to pass the torch, the fans want it... I want it, you can't deny us any longer." Jimmy said with the grin of a Cheshire cat plastered across his face, he was a true businessman... and he had Vince exactly where he wanted to him. Vince turned to Jimmy, the look of rage on his face finally subsiding. "I'll be there." Vince sneered... tossing the title into his gym bag and slinging it over his shoulder, as he paced away... he could hear the voice of a delighted Jimmy in the background. "I knew you'd see eye to eye with me in the end Vince, just remember... this is for the better!" Jimmy squealed with delight. "For the better?" Vince grunted. "We'll see about that." December 24th 1974. "Listen, it's Christmas eve... some of us have families to attend to, can we please get a move on here?" Vince Kinkade lifted his boots from the conference table before him, putting his hands together he leant forward impatiently, he knew full well that Valentine and Hanson were as thick as thieves... they'd prolong this meeting for as long as they could, wear him down until he was ready to accept their terms. Hanson smiled arrogantly from across the table, Vince shot a glare at him that anywhere outside of a closed meeting would have resulted in a punch in the mouth. "Well, I've got all night." Hanson said, cracking another pearly white grin. "Me too, patience Vince... we've gotta make sure that everything in this match goes exactly as planned, it's a big night for John... it'll effect what he goes on to do in the coming months." Valentine pitched in. Vince looked to the heavens, before leaning back in his chair... Valentine smiled and leaned forward. "Alright gentlemen, so we're in agreement... Vince is going to dominate the early stages of the match, but in true Hanson style... John battles back with his heart and courage, as soon as Vince realizes that he's not going to win things with skill alone... that's when he reaches for the chair..." Valentine prepared to continue, but was quickly cut off. "See this is what I don't get Valentine, for the past eighteen months I've given my blood, sweat and tears to this crowd, why would I turn my back on them now and try to cheat like this? Why?" Vince cut in. "Because Vince, we've already talked about this, Hanson is getting the bigger pops right now, like it or not... it's the truth, to get him over as the main face... we're going to have to turn you heel." Vince simply frowned and leant back. "Whatever." "Anyway, so Vince gets the chair... and takes his swing, at this point, Hanson makes the duck and Vince spins right on into the "Pretty Picture"... 123, we have a new champion, thankyou." Valentine remarks, picturing the scene in his head. Hanson continued to sit with his mile wide smile, as Vince continued to show signs of concern. "I still think the submission finish was better... I get the sleeper on Hanson, he drops it into the "Pretty Picture" and we have the cover... it flows a lot better." Vince uttered. Valentine smirked. "Nah, we're gonna stick with this finish Vince..." Valentine stuttered for a moment "I just think it has more about it." "I agree." Hanson pitched in. Vince sat back, despite the meeting looking bread and butter to the regular passer by... there were more politics at hand than anyone could imagine, Hanson and Valentine had already discussed this well before Vince Kinkade had entered the room... truth be told, he was never going to have a say in the ending of the match. And as for his submission finish, he could forget about it too. Submission finishes in wrestling matches were becoming a lot more rare, the Japanese especially had been dead against it in recent months... the reason for which was pretty simple, submission finishes opened up a whole new window for screwing and being screwed, and everyone in the room knew that if Vince Kinkade got the sleeper locked on, he probably wasn't going to let go. Hanson knew this. Valentine knew this. Kinkade, knew this. Which is why... without breaking into argument, he simply stood up from his seat... "Fine." ... and exited the room. January 1st 1975. New Jersey General Hospital "No God damnit! He said he'd be here... he has to be here!" Martha Kinkade's screams echoed through the corridors of a New Jersey hospital, it was a cold winter night and the contractions had long since began, she was several hours into labour. "Mrs. Kinkade... with all due respect, this baby isn't going to wait for anyone... it needs to come out, now." Doctor Williams spoke sternly, with a slight concern in his voice. "But he said he'd be here." Martha sobbed... as nurses gathered around and prepared to deliver the baby. "He promised." January 1st 1975. Bridgewater Sporting Arena "Hanson! Hanson! Hanson!" Vince Kinkade swallowed hard, he never thought his pride would taste so bitter... the crowd had turned, it happened to every good wrestler at some point, new blood came along and competed, it was a fact of life... even in the wild, a pride of lions would always be lead by the dominant male... often the fittest and strongest of the pack. Vince Kinkade was no spring chicken, he knew the ropes... but "Handsome" John Hanson was the flavour of the month, he had the talent... the charisma, the look... the time had come to pass the torch. As Vince rolled onto his stomach, Hanson locked on a firm side headlock. "I told you Vince, I told you it was my time... listen to these people, they can't wait for me to wrap this up... lets give them another ten minutes, we don't need to go home yet." Vince shifted his weight and turned the tables, locking down on Hanson as the crowd booed with discontent. "Yes we do you son of a bitch, we need to go home right now... just like we planned it, my wife is in labour for Christ's sake... I need to be out of here." Kinkade quickly whipped up to his feet and hit the ropes, driving a knee into the face of Hanson as he lay out on the canvas... this was it, for the first time in his wrestling career, he slid under the ropes and snapped up a steel chair at ringside. "You'd better make a fucking good champion Hanson... these people have adored me for the past ten years, I'd better not be turning my back on them for nothing." Vince sneered through clenched teeth, as he slid under the bottom ropes and started to measure up John Hanson as he rose to his feet. By the book... to the script, John Hanson ducked the chair swing and captured Vince Kinkade by the head, bringing him down with a trademark Ace Crusher, the "Pretty Picture". The crowd exploded with cheers, as Vince Kinkade lay flat out on his back... motionless, prepared to step back and allow a new superstar to shine. Seconds passed... but Vince Kinkade did not feel the cover that he had been expecting, he peered out of his left eye and saw Hanson showboating beside the ropes. Every second that passed before Hanson made the cover was yet another blow to Vince Kinkade's credibility... "Fuck! Go home John! It's time!" Hanson turned, leaning over as though to taunt Kinkade. "One more move Vince... these people are loving this!" John barked joyously. "No damnit! We do this now... John... JOHN!" It was no good, John Hanson had already stepped through the ropes and was now ascending the turnbuckles... as he reached the top rope, he signalled to the delight of the crowd... before preparing to leap off with a splash. "I gave you a chance you little son of a bitch, and you threw it back in my face... how dare you... do you know who I am am? I'm Vince Kinkade... and when I say that it's time to go home... you go home... GO HOME! GO HOME!" It felt like a blackout, but really... the rage had simply blinded Vince Kinkade; one split second had pillaged the destiny of John Hanson. Vince Kinkade sat up and immediately noticed the cold silence in the arena... it was little wonder given the blood curdling scream which Hanson had let out, and was still letting out on the canvas... Kinkade rolled onto his stomach and slowly pressed up, as fans in the crowd grimaced, some struggling not to vomit. Kinkade slipped back to the canvas, something greasy under his hands has made them slide across the mat, as he narrowed his eyes... he realized just what the lubricant was. Blood. Blood, which belonged to John Hanson. If only blood was the full extent of the horror. Vince slowly got to his knees and was greeted by the sight of John Hanson rolling on the canvas like a fish that'd just been extracted from water, gasping for air... flailing helplessly, and still screaming from the depths of his lungs. The blood was forming in a pool around his head as he kicked his feet into the canvas, Kinkade was perplexed... a look of sheer shock on his face as the official attempting to hold Hanson down spoke out. "Jesus, Vince... how could you do this? Cover him... we need some help out here NOW!" Vince obliged, slowly rolling John Hanson over and grimacing at what he next saw... the gaping bloody wound where John Hanson's left eye once was. As the official pounded the canvas... Vince Kinkade couldn't help but focus on the sickening sight of Hanson's eyeball... bouncing up and down on the mat with every palm strike. EMT's rushed the ring... thrusting the champion aside as he stepped back slowly in shock, uttering to himself. "I told him to go home... but he didn't listen, he wouldn't listen." Vince Kinkade rolled under the ropes and snatched up his title, he did not raise it into the air triumphantly, instead... he paced slowly to the back, not looking back at the horrific scene in the ring. As he parted the curtains, he felt the wrath of many dagger-throwing glares... but only heard one voice. "You fucking son of a bitch... you did that on purpose, you threw out your arms for that splash and gouged the poor bastard's eye out... you'll pay for this Kinkade... you'll pay!" Kinkade slowly looked up and saw his former friend Jimmy Valentine, filled with rage and being held back by several other wrestlers. Vince shook his head at Valentine. "I told him to go home Jimmy, but he wouldn't listen." As Vince Kinkade brushed through his locker room door, he approached the sink and turned on the tap, looking down. The blood was on his hands. And no matter how hard he washed them, it would stay there for years to come. January 1st 1975. New Jersey General Hospital Martha Kinkade wept to herself as she held her newborn son in her arms... never before did she ever think that she would be filled with so much joy... ... and at the same time, so much sorrow. As a Nurse entered the ward, Martha looked up with hope in her eyes. "Is he here?" She asked. "I'm sorry Martha... but he isn't, we just got a call... I think he's going to be late, you see... there was an accident." The Nurse spoke softly, as Martha's eyes widened. "What kind of accident!? Is Vince okay!? He's not hurt is he!? Please God." "No no Martha..." The Nurse said frantically, placing a hand on her shoulder "Vince is just fine... but someone else was hurt quite badly, which is why he can't be here yet." The Nurse slowly turned and walked away... as Martha wiped a tear from her cheek, and looked down at her newborn son. "Everything will be just fine Kellen, Daddy will be here soon."
