Succeeded Failure

1 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The only thing on my mind as I sat down on the floor of my hotel room that wasn't fit me accommodations was the failure I achieved. Thoughts of the past ran through my mind and plagued my head with negative reactions. Anything would do for some revenge. Anything would be perfect but there was still something I was missing. I remembered back into the old days of Thailand, where I use to be something special, something the people wanted to see and I would attract crowds from far and wide. Now look at me, a lowly lightweight who did what he had to do get where he is. I dropped weight for them, and even changed my style of competition to their liking. I was a sell-out to the system and there was nothing more to say. Ah the coolness that ran under the door and touched my back brought my great joy in a pit of sorrow. There must be an easier way out, but this plague blocks my thoughts from even beginning to see where I begin. There should be a time for celebration, but what after I did; I should be banned for life. There must be an easier change, and surely there is some way to fix all this. But how, and where? "My Plague," I said out loud, jumping to my feet in excitement. I had seen a view of pure reality and now it was time to move. I ran to the window, peering down across the city sky and shouted to the world, "My Plague will arrive!" Getting small attention from the people next door and the maids making their rounds for the night. I collapsed onto my bed and smiled at the ceiling while I watched the fan spin in free air. There was a solution and a quick fix. I had given so much, but there would be a way around this failure. I had only a few options left, and now was the time to surprise the world and move forward and not deeper into this state of low self-esteem. There was someone to turn to... A place I could go, and they would take me back... I just needed a little bit of faith in me. Perfection can't always run the show.
