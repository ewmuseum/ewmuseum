Humiliation. (part 1)

fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Humiliation. (part 1) April 12, 2000 Warfare is over, and one man is pissed. This man worked in the main event of the show, but suffered perhaps the most embarrassing moment of his career - being pinned by Eron. As is to be expected after an occurance like that, this man is severely upset. Various prelim workers scatter out of his way as he walks down a hallway backstage, slinging various objects he comes across into the walls with loud crashes. When he reaches one door, he pulls it open, walks in, and slams it shut with a bang. Upon closer inspection, there is a nameplate on the door - "FTfWo". Inside the room, Perfect, sporting a large bandage on his head where Collins' fireball hit him, casually upends a table and slumps down into one of the chairs in the middle of the room. He sits there and fumes for a moment, when a knock is heard on the door. Perfect calls to the person knocking - This better be damn important! The knocking commences again, and Perfect leaves his chair and heads for the door. The knocking continues, and Perfect throws the door open and picks up the man outside by the throat and swings him inside. What?! Geoff Green gulps. Well, I was just informed that your match with Collins at CyberSlam... the winner gets a shot at the World Title. Perfect takes this in, slightly calming... but not much. Well, it's not as if I needed another reason to kick the living crap out of Collins, but hell, I won't complain about that. Perfect walks back to the chair he sat in earlier, and sits down again. After all, the last time I got a shot at the World Title I pinned the champion... no matter what any idiot out there says. And the shot before that I squashed Ice to win my fourth World Title... and then got screwed over by the Freaking Frenchie, but that's not the point. You see, Geoff, Collins did a very bad thing tonight. It wasn't the fireball to the face, no. It wasn't causing me to lose... at least not directly. What he did was cause me to lose to the biggest waste of valuable oxygen that has ever stepped foot into the fWo - and that includes Shocker AND Showstopper - Eron. Collins, in life there are three things that you do not do under any circumstances. You do not tug on Superman's cape. You do not watch a Frenchie match if your life depends on it. And most importantly of all, YOU DO NOT CAUSE PEOPLE TO LOSE TO ERON! Collins, we've been at each other's throats for over a year. We've traded titles. We've screwed each other over. You've locked me in a flaming cage. You've crippled one of my best friends. You've thrown a fireball in my face. I've done plenty to you, too. I've thrown a fireball in your face myself. I've handcuffed you to a wall and beat you until you bled. I've attacked all your flunkies one by one. I've locked you in a car and let it roll into a lake. And all of that... that's all forgiveable, if you ask me. That's business. But you stepped over the line tonight, Collins. You did something worse than all of that combined. You made me lose to Eron. And Collins... I hope you realize what you've done. You've humiliated me on national television. I do not take well to humiliation. Especially humiliation on that level. CyberSlam, Collins, is seventeen days away. In those seventeen days, Scott, you better be on full alert, my friend. You better be watching your back. You better have your lackies by your side at all times. Because Collins, the WGA is coming at you with full... damn... force. And in between now and CyberSlam, I'm gonna make your life hell on Earth... and then at CyberSlam, I'm gonna end it once and for all. Geoff Green, who has been standing in the doorway during this outburst from Perfect, poses one last question. If you do win, Scott, what are you going to do about the World Title shot? How big an idiot are you, Geoff? I'm gonna take it. Dead's a good man, an honest man - he's not afraid to give me what I've earned. We've fought in the past, when it was Slugger and Davis. We both know plenty about each other, and it'd damn sure be a good match - lord knows, it'd be a better fight between stablemates than Frenchie and Primetime. And hey, if D-Man wins the match, more power to him. If I win the match... well, it's not like it would be a surprise. After all, I've won four World Titles... how shocking would five be? And if, by some horrible accident that nothing, that nobody, the Strangler wins at CyberSlam... well, Strangler, your situation would be a lot similar to that of your city's baseball team. A hell of a lot of hype, but no substance whatsoever. If we meet, Strangler, you're gonna choke harder than Ramon Martinez against the Mariners... and that belt'll be mine. Geoff, I thank you for that information. Now... Perfect stands up from the chair and walks over to Geoff. Get. Out. Perfect grabs Green by the lapels and shoves him out the door.
