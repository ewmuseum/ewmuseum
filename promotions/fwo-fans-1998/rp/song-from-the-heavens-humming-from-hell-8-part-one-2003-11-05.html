Song from the Heavens, Humming from Hell - 8 - Part One

5 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Douglas ducked quickly around the corner and noticing a pipe on the ground he picked it up and was immediately around the corner again, just to notice Dalton looking back at the road. *SMUCK* Douglas cracked the pipe hard against Angel's hand and he fell to the ground hard. A black car pulled up and Spencer got out of the driver's side. Douglas turned to him and smiled, "Let's get the fuck out of here, but first." Douglas held the pipe high above Angel's chest and with all his strength came down on Angel, however, it did not pierce his skin. Douglas stepped back in shock, "Then it is true," he said softly. "C'mon!" Spencer yelled, "We gotta go, plus I have a present in the back seat." Douglas looked over Spencer's shoulder and at a man who quivered in the back seat upon Douglas' eyes. Douglas grinned, "Wonderful, dinner." The two men got into the car.
