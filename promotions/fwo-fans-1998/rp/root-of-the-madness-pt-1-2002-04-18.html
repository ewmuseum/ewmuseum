Root of the Madness pt 1

18 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The phone rang several times before David Cross stumbled through the dark apartment. With his arms outstretched to probe the darkness, he cursed under his breath as he banged his shins on the coffee table. He hobbled to the far wall, grabbing the receiver. "Look Pamela, you're engaged now. Stop calling me." "You must still be dreaming," said the voice on the other end. "The only thing Pamela Anderson would give you is a restraining order." It took a moment for the voice to register. "Tyler?" "Yeah, now you remember me." Tyler's voice was slurred, filled with bitterness. "But you conveniently forgot me when your were a big fWo superstar, didn't you?" Cross rubbed his eyes, trying to wake himself up fully. "Superstar? Are you high? I was a punching bag. They forgot about me in Japan, for crying out loud! They didn't even book me a flight back to the States." Tyler continued. "The checks stopped coming. Why?" David took a deep breath. "Well... your son Robert wasn't sick. I saw him, healthy as a horse. You were using me, Tyler. You didn't need the money I was sending for your son." "You ingrate!" snapped Tyler. "I got you into the fWo! I got you the chance that no other fed would give you! You owed me!" "But... but I sent you everything I made. Why couldn't you just be straight with me? Where was the money going?" Tyler's voice became quiet, subdued. "I placed a couple bets... I had to borrow some money... I couldn't pay them back... threw matches for them... sold the house..." "So you needed me... to pay off debts for you?" said Cross. "You could have told me that!" "I thought I could take care of it," said Tyler softly. "Now it's too late." David was fully awake now. "Too late? What do you mean too late?" "I needed that money!" shouted Tyler, sobbing. "I... I needed it or they'd break my leg! They broke my fucking leg David! Because of you!" A large pit began forming in Cross' stomach. "Oh God... look, it sounds like you've been drinking. Go to sleep, and we'll talk in the morning. We can get this straightened out." The other end was quiet, the silence broken by a faint gulping sound followed by breaking glass. "Is too late now, Davie boy. They're gonna kill me an' Robbie. They told me so. So we gotta go away for good. Tonight." "Tyler, you shouldn't be going anywhere in your condition." "I need t'be in my condition to do what I gotta do." Tyler almost sounded happy as he spoke. "Stupid insurance company won't let me get any money out of my policy, but you'll clean up. Ya might as well benefit from my generosity yet again. Get your face done, why don'tcha?
