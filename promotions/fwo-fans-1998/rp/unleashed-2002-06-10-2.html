Unleashed

10 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Madison Square Garden. The man known as Brand Frontier looked at the sprawling complex. A lump had formed in his throat as he took in the grandeur of the area. If you stopped and listened it was almost as if you could hear the screaming fans from past wrestling extravaganzas. He shook his head in approval at the thought of beginning the next phase of his fWo career in this very building. The hairs on the back of his neck stood on end, his arms covered in goosebumps. The night air was electric. In a few short hours the fWo would be back on the air. Even though it has been said to the point of losing all meaning tonight history would be made. Tonight he would make history. His opponent was none other than Ric Chronos, one of the most hated men in the industry, and rightfully so. Chronos had cut a swath across the wrestling business that was hard to ignore and near impossible to duplicate. The mere mention of his name made lesser men visibly gasp. Frontier though was no ordinary man. Even though he hadn't been in the business as long as Ric had been he had seen too much. He had seen enough to decide he would take a stand. A stand that has cost him a lot, a stand he would not back down from. Tonight was different though. Tonight it wasn't just about revenge or justice. It was about those things but it was also about respect and taking that next step. Even though he honestly saw his match tonight as a distraction he knew what it would mean for his career if he won. For the first episode of fWo's new show Brand Frontier would face off with Ric Chronos in the first round of the Internet Championship Tournament. A shudder raced down his spine at the thought of that prize. He shook his head though to clear it out of his mind. It had always been about doing the right thing, the accolades came second... but this title, this fWo title had the power to possibly distract him. Not about Creed or Lords or any of the rest, not about saying your prayers and taking your vitamins but about proving something to himself. Brand Frontier would enter that ring tonight against someone that had become a stern rival. He would give it his all and not back down. Who would expect any less from him? He would do something he didn't expect himself to do though, he would look for the win not to teach a lesson but to take a step. A step that would put him on the road to a level he hadn't been at in a long time. Maybe this was a time when Brand Frontier could think for himself. Not just follow a path out of honor but one out of want. Maybe this would be a time for the Paragon to step out of the shadow he had been living in for four years. Maybe this was the time that Brandon Roche could put to rest the monsters that haunted him. Maybe this was the time to let Brand Frontier be unhindered from the past and let him be truly unleashed on the wrestling world. Maybe.
