Return.

10 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Return. February 10, 2000 :::The scene opens up with a shot of the interior of a large gym, furnished with everything from weight training equipment to punching bags to a trio of wrestling rings. A large banner on the wall reads "WILSON TRAINING ACADEMY", and underneath that reads "Atlanta, GA". Men are training at all of the training equipment, and the general air of the place is that of a take-no-bs, all-business mentality. The camera slowly pans over the surroundings, before coming to rest on the blond, goateed Mitch Wilson, clad in a leather jacket, sweat pants, and a Wilson Training Academy T-shirt, standing in a ring where two young aspiring workers trade reversals and holds. One of the workers gets the other one in a waistlock, before the other reverses the hold and executes and awkward-looking takeover.::: No, no! You're going too slow on the follow-through - you'll never make it to the big time if you can't even do a simple waistlock takeover right! Here, let me show you. :::Mitch quickly shoves one of the students away, before turning to the other.::: C'mon, come and get me. :::The student charges, but Mitch quickly ducks under his arm and applies a waistlock, before taking him over in a perfect takeover. Mitch holds on to the move, before dropping the student back in a stiff release german suplex. He gets up, before helping the suplexed student up as well. He gestures to the other student.::: That's how you do it. Now, I've got some business to take care of, kid. Call me when you get it right. :::Mitch leaves the two young workers in the ring, before stepping out through the middle rope. He continues walking past many hard-working people, before finally coming to a large wooden door marked "WILSON". He opens it to a medium-size office, complete with desk (with nameplate reading "Mitch Wilson, President"), TV, computer, and various types of leather chairs. Trophies, plaques, and medals are mounted on the walls and also sit on a large set of shelves on the left side of the office. Sitting conspicuously in the one non-leather chair in the office is none other than fWo cameraman Geoff Green, holding a camera, and appearing to be somewhat upset.::: Mitch - where the hell were you? I've been waiting in here for two hours... I was out there, helping some of the workers train in here. Hell, Geoff, where else would I be? I've had to be on the job more or less 24/7 to turn just one wrestling school I started with some of my wrestling money into a nation-wide franchise. I've got one of these everywhere, you know. Right, right. Of course. Well, are we gonna get this interview started or not? Of course. Geez, Geoff, have some patience. :::Geoff's face flashes annoyance, then resignation as he flips on his camera. We go to its view, as Mitch sits down behind the desk.::: So, Mitch, why exactly did you return to the fWo? Well, that's easy. I've actually been looking for the right opportunity to come back for a month or so now, I just haven't had the chance. The reason why I came back is threefold - number one, to embarrass Pierre, Brian, and their new friend. I think that's already been plenty accomplished - and I'm still the only man never to lose to the Frenchie or Primetime in singles matches. Number two, to show a few of these new idiots a thing or two about old-school wrestling. I look out there today and I see tattooed freaks like Mike Bear, long-haired pretty boys like Courage, and walking, talking pieces of crap like Eron The Talentless representing the fWo as champions. That they have actually gotten titles shows how far the fWo has sunk since I left, and why the fWo is like a crippled horse waiting to be put out of its misery - but I'll get to that in a minute. Number three, because a certain Mr. Davis issued an open invitation to join his group, and since that fits in well with my plans, it all worked out. What exactly do you mean by your comment about the fWo being "like a crippled horse"? Well, I think that's pretty obvious if you would just take a look around you, Geoff. I've only been gone four months, and look at all the BS that's gone on since then. For the love of God, every title's been devalued - there have been seven World Title changes in just four months since I left. Whatever happened to true, long reigns like the one my FTfWo bud Perfect's? Titles don't mean anything anymore, and that's a shame. There's been practically as many people in control of the fWo since I left as there have been champions. The shows have dropped to WCW-level quality matches. Geoff, it's like growing up in a great, great city, with lush parks and good people, and coming back when you're grown up to see everything paved over and punks roaming the streets and ruling the city. It's gotten ridiculous since I left, Geoff - even I could tell that watching the shows since then - at least when it wasn't so bad I had the turn the channel - and by now it's to the point where you just need to level everything and move on. That's exactly what FTfWo is all about, Geoff, and that's exactly why I joined. I mean, really. I was on this computer in here the other day after the show and I was checking out the fWo website - it's pathetic! I have a damn goatee and blond hair now, and they have pictures of me on their damn website that are four months old! No blond hair, no goatee, no nothing. It's just pathetic. I mean, hear me out Geoff. For ten months I gave my heart and soul to this company, all the while trying to do nothing more than bring back the real, true sport of wrestling - two men locked in combat, each trying with all his heart simply to prevail... and this company spit in my face. They put me in perhaps the most disgraceful thing I've ever been a part of - the Mad Frenchie slash Flying Trucker angle. Anyone who's seen it knows what I'm talking about. And believe me, that is not what I'm about! :::Mitch is beginning to get angry, recalling his last few months.::: And then, Geoff - then - suddenly Brian wins Meltdown and he's this huge star. Give me a damn break! When the French Foundation first started, it was ME that took that 20 year old punk kid under my wing and taught him every damn thing he knows, it was ME that carried that punk kid to three tag team titles and it was ME who carried the greatest team in fWo history while that punk kid got all the glory and all the accolades just because he's one flashy son of a bitch! How does he repay me for making his damn career? By picking Pierre as his tag team partner for his tag title shot. That is not something I will stand for! If it weren't for me, Geoff, Primetime would have no career right now! And Brian, you listen up real damn good. Your time will come, count on it. Your time will come. No two ways about it. Well, uh, Mitch, if you're not going after him right away, who are you going after? Like I said, Geoff - there are a lot of the young and the worthless holding titles in the fWo today - most notably that idiotic, stupid, workrate-challenged, babbling, rambling moron Eron, who holds the number two title in the fWo. That more than anything is an utter disgrace. Eron, believe me when I say that your path and mine will cross in the very near future... and believe me when I say it won't be pretty. What about the fWo's rule that there must be six months in between a wrestler's retirement and his return? What about it? In case you didn't notice, Geoff, we are FTfWo. You realize what that stands for, right? :::Geoff nods.::: Right now I care as much about the fWo's rules as I care about seeing them survive into next year. If Finn wants to try to enforce that rule on me, well, bring it on. But somehow, Geoff, I doubt he's got the little Finnies to go through myself, Dead, Perfect, Hiroshima, Hell, Heaven, and Living. Can't say I blame him, really. As of right now, Geoff, the entire fWo is on notice. Mitch Wilson is back, and Mitch Wilson is pissed off. Mitch Wilson has been disrespected by the American culture, the French culture, his so-called friends in the fWo, and the fWo itself. No more. Believe me, Geoff, when I say - things are going to change around here. Now, get out. I've got business to take care of, and you're not a part of it. :::Geoff nods, as we can see by the motion of the camera, and flips the off switch. The camera slowly fades to black on the image of Mitch Wilson. He is, indeed, back.:::
