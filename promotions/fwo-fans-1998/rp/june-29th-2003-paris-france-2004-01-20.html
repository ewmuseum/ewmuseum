June 29th, 2003 - Paris, France

20 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 29th, 2003. 8:14 A.M. Paris, France. The home of Thomas and Jasmin Desjardin. Ring Jasmin's first instinct was to hit the alarm clock, hard. In that, she succeeded, and her victory felt good. Ring Of course, the alarm clock wasn't ringing. It never rang. Jasmin reached over the slumbering body of Thomas and grabbed the cordless phone sitting there. <> Thomas cracked open his eyes. Neither one needed to be up for another hour now, but there was his wife, answering the phone, talking instead of simply dismissing the interloper on the other end. His drowsiness was more powerful than his hostility, though, and he quickly found himself drifting off as Jasmin gingerly left the bet and padded out of the room, shutting the door behind her. His curiosity was piqued by that, but still sleep won out. He was awake, there was no doubting that, but still clinging to the bed in that last desperate hope for twenty more minutes. His mind wandered, and he thought about the last year. In three weeks, they would celebrate their first five years together as man and wife, but she hadn't truly taken her vows seriously until the last year. He knew before that he had her heart, and that when she came to her senses, the wait would be worth it. And it had been. The last year had been proven him right. His eyelids, heavy, started closing of their own accord, when his ears picked up a name, spoken by Jasmin in the other room. Pierre. In that instant, Thomas started a descent that would culminate seven months later, in a New York hospital room.
