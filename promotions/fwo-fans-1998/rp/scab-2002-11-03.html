Scab

3 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"I sold out long before you ever heard my name." - Tool "Hey, Scab." It was loud in the arena, but Mufanga Kagos heard it just the same. His bare feet leaving sweaty footprints in the grid-work, Kagos was walking up the glistening steel rampway with a forced limp after a good, solid match with Great Dragon, . He didn't have to work that hard to sell the injury... he had worked with Horito before, and when the little guy put you in a submission, it was one you were going to remember for at least a week. "Hey, Scab." Mufanga turned slightly, staring through his thick black hair at a scruffy fan in an ancient, faded Metallica T-shirt who was leaning over the railing and screaming through cupped hands. Even over the polite applause that the match was receiving, Mufanga could hear this punk's raspy voice piercing through the din. Even a mediocre match on an absolutely shit card got you applause, Mufanga thought. He'd probably make the highlight reels, cause nothing else was even coming close... but he expected that. Hell, he got top billing on an fWo Pay per View... that's nothing to complain about, right? "Hey, you fucking scab." Mufanga sighed, and turned his back on the drunken screaming fan. It seems that at least someone realized that he was the lone remaining wrestler from the old roster that was still on the payroll... He heard the tired rumblings on the Internet message boards, the rumors that said he had sold out... that he had turned his back on the veterans of the fWo in order to get a paycheck. To hell with them... he wasn't a scab, at least not literally. Nobody was on strike here. They were all fired. Mufanga Kagos remembered the days without a paycheck, when he had to wrestle in bars and high schools to pay for meals. He remembered those days well and knew he would do anything to never go back there again. He crawled on his hands and knees back to the fWo, asking to work in OCW, SCPW, hell, anywhere at all just to stay of the good side of the most powerful company in wrestling. In the end, they let him stay on to help work out the kinks in a macabre farce of a wrestling promotion and for four months he suffered botched moves, telegraphed punches and some of the worst wrestling he had ever witnessed. All, just to stay in the fWo. All... all so he could remain in the company he had worked so hard to get into, only to watch it crumble before his eyes. Was he a scab? Well, that was open to interpretation. Mufanga simply considered himself a survivor in the truest sense of the word. Mufanga Kagos just had the brains to realize that there was more to wrestling than... well... wrestling.
