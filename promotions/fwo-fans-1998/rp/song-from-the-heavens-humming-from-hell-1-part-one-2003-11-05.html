Song from the Heavens, Humming From Hell - 1 - Part One

5 November 2003

Not marble, nor the gilded monuments Of princes shall outlive this powerful rhyme, But you shall shine more bright in these contents Than unswept stone besmeared with sluttish time. When wasteful war shall statues overturn, And broils root out the work of masonry, Nor Mars his sword, nor war's quick fire shall burn The living record of your memory. 'Gainst death and all-oblivious enmity Shall you pace forth; your praise shall still find room Even in the eyes of all posterity That wear this world out to the ending doom. So, till the judgment that yourself arise, You live in this, and dwell in lovers' eyes. The air was thick and heavy... the humidity was disgusting. It was Angel's first time in Miami, but here was business and pleasure. Never in his life had he felt so powerful, ever since that moment when he sat awake in his apartment on September 18th, the night Levite Prod and Noah Reily were murdered. The night that Noah Reily's spirit and physical strength had entered his body, he felt Noah and heard his thoughts. Angel now knew everything Douglas had done, and everything that he was planning to do. Angel had to stop him. Angel slowly slid his hand into his pocket and grabbed a pack of cigarettes; he went to bring his hand out but the back of his hand stuck slightly to his pant pocket. His hands were clammy due to the heat. Angel jerked his hand out with the pack now open, he picked a smoke out of the pack and put it into his mouth and lit it, tossing the match to the ground. Angel was here for one reason and one reason only. To stop Steve Douglas before he could commit one of the most horrific acts towards humanity in history. He now knew Douglas' plan and the deal he had made with the devil. Trading his soul for a revenge that he craves with every waking moment. Angel had thought long and hard on why Steve was so angry, why he had so much hate. Was Steve always a product of misery? Or did peers cause it? Was he a child of a cruel society? Or a demon sent to Earth to destroy? Whatever the answer was, Angel knew what Steve was going to do. We cannot allow that. Angel inhaled his cigarette and his lips barely moved as he exhaled, "Obviously," exhaling heavier smoke when he spoke. Angel was on the corner of a street deep in the heart of Miami city, not fully aware of his location he moved across the street onto to the parallel sidewalk. There was a doorman outside a local club and a group of people dressed in the "Ritz" and waiting to get into the club. Angel dawned a white t-shirt and blue jeans. Taking another long drag as he moderately moved toward the doorman catching eye contact with him once and then keeping his eyes people in line up. A girl dressed in a short shiny dress looked over at Angel, and Angel smiled back. The girl quickly looked away and Angel followed suit, by this time he stood in front of the doorman. Looking up at the man A.D. keep a straight face, "Happen to know where Seacoast Suites is?" A.D. sucked back another drag off of his smoke and taking it hard into his chest and breathing the smoke out slowly. The doorman eyed up A.D. and looked him hard in the eye, "Two blocks north on Collins Avenue, can't miss it." "Thanks," A.D. was quick to respond, turning and walking in the direction he needed to go A.D. adjusted the 9millimeter rested at the front of his pants. In order for the world live Angel had to put an end to its destructor. Tonight Steve Douglas was going to have to die.
