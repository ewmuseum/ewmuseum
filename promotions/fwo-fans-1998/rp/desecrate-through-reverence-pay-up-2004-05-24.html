Desecrate Through Reverence: Pay Up

24 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Say hello to the circle's corner. It's a hotel bar...the hotel doesn't matter, but the bar does. It's the same scene again and again...perpetuating darkness allowing for cover. More or less, the watering hole of a wonderful sorrow built for a Campbell girl who just got the boot. Of course, the match to ignite the blaze was swiftly approaching... "Well here's the Aimz I know and love." Reno Davis smiled sarcastically. "Vodka martini, no olives...really dry." Amy finished mumbling to the bartender before she turned, rolling her eyes, to Reno. ' 'Pleased about bringing me to this low?" she asked plainly. "A tad," Reno looked around and sighed, "Mind if I have a seat?" "Well, fuck...might as well." After pulling out a stool for Davis, Aimz rubbed her temples and lowered her head. Brandon's reaction to her confessions, as expected, hadn't been the best. She have no clue where her husband was, but here she was having drinks with an ex-boyfriend bent on ruining her. She was handed her drink and glanced at Reno, taking it all down at once. "So..." said the redhead, trying to look as careless as she used to, "What now? I married a guy who probably hated me as of, oh, twenty minutes ago...and you're sitting here, ringmaster. What now?" Davis began to speak, but thought better of it, promting the bartender for a Keith's. He looked the red head up and down and realized how stressed out she was, and who was causing it. "Amy, you know I didn't come here to fuck you over. I've been working for this a long time and," Davis made eye contact, "I think I figured something out about the contest...I'm not sure on it, but I have a plan. Anyway, that's besides the point. I want peace between us, I want to be friends again. I mean I wanted to kill you, but seeing you again...I know where Scerrinn is." Amy called for another drink. "With a family?" "...Not exactly." "She's...wait, she's still alone?" Reno nodded, and Amy swallowed hard. "It can't be like that," she said, "What can we d---wait, what the hell am I talking about?! I don't even know that she's yours. I don't even know...fuck, Brandon's not gonna want it...I don't even know if I've got a family now, let alone the ability to take on an addition to it!" Amy's argument with herself continued, "But I've gotta, right? Right...I guess. I don't think there's any way around it. And....Jesus, I have no fucking clue what I'm doing. Where is she?" "I'd take her," Reno looked at his booze and grimaced, "But I mean I've tried AA already and stuff and there's...I don't know, Ames." "She doesn't have to be your responsibility. She could very well be Ka...be you-know-whose. This is like...all on me or something. But, shit...with you back, it's just like....damn, I don't have a sweet fucking clue." Reno took a drink of beer, " I still love you." "I......" Amy stopped. She chuckled alloud with a wide smile, laughing only at her sudden dumbfoundedness. "Well...I've got no snarky comeback for that. But..." her smile quickly ended with a glance to the ring on her finger, "I know, but I love him more than anything." Reno was hurt, angry, jealous...hating........he wasn't sure. "Do you? Or do you just love leaving your past behind? How long until the next guy gets his six months in heaven?" "I loved laeving the past behind. I loved you, I love you...just not like I love him. Despite what you are ,were and what you've become...I do. But I only married one of those guys 'getting six months in heaven', and it's been well over a year for us." Davis sat in silence for a moment, scratching at his 5 O'clock shadow in deep thought. He took a drink from him brew and let out a deep sigh, "Well then, Ames, you know I came here so pissed off at you. I wanted to ruin everything you'd achieved, but I guess you're the queen of headgames eh? I mean, you can turn someone from being so pissed off to falling in love over the course of a few minutes. Must be a Maritme thing, huh?" Reno turned and made eye contact with Amy...his lost, empty eyes replaced by those of focus and fulfillment. She had no words, though. There was no clever comeback for anything Davis could say right now. She voiced that, "I...I just don't know what to tell you. Apparently I don't have to say anything for you to go from ice to boiling..." "I fucked myself over in Survivor," Davis' fingers twitched at his arm pocket containing the flask, "the team believes I'm useless due to my...habits." Reno cleared his throat and took a deep breath, clearly trying to consider his next moves. "Am I a part of that?" Reno sighed when he nodded slightly. Very slightly. "So...what happens now?" Davis unzipped the arm pocket on his leather jacket and pulled out his lucky flask. The flask he fought so hard to keep full and emptied. He fumbled it about in his hands, shaking, as if with fear of what was to come. With one quick sweep he shoved the flask into Amy's hands, a sigh of relief following. "I've lost everything. I know I'm gone at the next show, I heard them talking. So fuck it, I'm through," Davis gazed into Amy's stunned eyes, "Congratulations, babes, you got rid of me without trying. All self-inflicted. Just wait though," Reno placed his hand on her shoulder, "I'll be back, and I'll win you back." With that, Reno stood up, patted Mrs. Roche on the shoulder once more and began to go on his way. Stunned, shocked and bewildered, Amy looked from the flask, to Reno and back again. "Wait, wow, Wait...Wait wait wait..." Amy said, rushing to her feet. When Reno stopped, she took a few slow steps forward. "Everything just coming back and walking right out the door again. I...I don't know." Another few steps had her inches from Davis. Cautiously, she raised her arms and watched Reno's attempt at an emotionless face when she put them around his neck to embrace him, speaking with her chin on his shoulder. "I guess..." she began, then choked...was that a tear? "I guess I know how you felt when I ran away, huh?" Embrace, Connection, Love, Fulfillment, Relief, Release. That was it. Reno pulled himself away as easily as he could. "I'll be back for you another time. Me, you and Scerrinn," Reno winked, "When we're ready." The cold, whiskey filled flask and a kiss on the forehead was all Reno left her as he exited the bar. Amy lowered her head and heaved a sigh. Relief or sorrow, even SHE wasn't sure. "Hey," the bartender called out, "Who's gonna pay for all the beer he drank?" "Honey," referring to Aimz, "I'm looking at you." She had a few dollars, but no strength left to support this life.
