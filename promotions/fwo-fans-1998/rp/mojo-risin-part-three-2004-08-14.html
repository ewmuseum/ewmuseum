Mojo Risin' Chapter Three: Dead To The World The armadillo was enjoying its morning. It had woken up, ate something small and living for breakfast, and had made its goal to cross the highway. The armadillo moved slowly, despite the heat of the asphalt. Tragically, the armadillo did not look both ways before crossing the road, and therefore did not have enough time, energy, or anything of the sort to get out of the way of the blood-red Tracker. Inside the car, Eli Flair and Ivy McGinnis argued passionately about what each had done for the fWo since January of 2004, when everything was turned upside down. They attacked each others' decisions, instincts, and motives on every level, and dropped insults like breadcrumbs outside the gingerbread house. Anyone who heard them arguing would have never guessed that these two had been best of friends and closest of business partners for nearly a decade, straight. And anyone who knew the history of Eli and Ivy would not believe it was them who argued. So intent were they on their argument, so intent on their stubbornness and insistence that they be right, that Eli Flair had no time to swerve out of the way of our friend, the armadillo. Point of order, he didn't even see it. But as the front passenger side tire hit the poor creature's body, the weak center of gravity, combined with the high speed of travel, left them no hope. The Tracker flipped over on its side, throwing the various bags and boxes and cooler out systematically, leaving a long trail of destruction. Poison Ivy herself, not wearing a seatbelt, was thrown from the car at the moment of side impact. Eli Flair wore his seatbelt, but it might have been more a curse than a blessing, because he was dragged along with the car.
