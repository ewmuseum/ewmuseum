Through Her Eyes

18 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For nearly a year and a half she had hated him with an unbridled passion, but she didn't really know why. The memories of what had happened at the SWL's Summer Meltdown were blurry and distant. An aluminum bat was the only thing left she had linking herself to him. They had once been the best of friends, like peas and carrots as Forrest Gump would say. Now, they were nothing. Monica Davidson sat at a bar inside of Monte Burns' gargantuan mansion. She held a cup of coffee between her hands. She was alone, waiting for Burns to get out of the shower. He had called her the other day and told her to fly down so they could discuss what to do about Winters. Ever since mid-August she had found out Monte's plan against Donaven. She never found out how Monte found her, or even knew about her. He had probably seen Summer Meltdown and what she did to him. Taking the bat and smashing it across Donaven's broken body over and over again. Her face cringed at the thought of what she had down back then. Then she did again at Monte's request. It felt good at first, she had still hated him. But she wished she knew why. It was hard to ask that question every day and not having any type of answer. What's the point of hating him for no reason? She thought. It was true, but she needed the money to help pay off her college loans. And Monte had been exceedingly generous for her to do this. She knew she was being paid so Burns could seek satisfaction seeing Donaven in pain. She laughed with Monte, drank with him, went to fabulous parties and lived the good life. Something she had when she was a child. Still, she felt very empty. Even depressed, she knew what she was doing was wrong, but hate and Burns were driving her to do this. It was only a friendship through elementary school, then high school. He fucked up his life. She thought. He's scum, suicide is for selfish people.
