The Strangler grumbled and tossed the remote aside in disgust before reaching for the newspaper he had bought earlier in the day while at the airport. Usually, after a meal in his hotel room, he liked to relax and catch up on a little television before settling in to read. Lately, it hadn't been working out in his favor. A full selection of cable television channels, and he had found nothing to watch. When Mtv seems like the best alternative... there's usually nothing else worthy of one's time on any channel. He scanned over the paper, checking on the World, National, and Local news sections before flipping to the sports section. It was tough keeping up with things in the world, what with being on the road so much. Local news stories were but a fleeting glimpse of society to him. He had no connections to the people from town to town usually, and so many of the "unique" stories ended up running together as he traveled. The world and national news piqued his interest, but even then he usually heard about things many hours after they had happened... such was the case with much of what had gone on since September 11... He felt out of touch with many aspects of life, more so than usual. A satisfying career had taken a turn for the worse, and he was being pushed into a position that he was not comfortable with. The Strangler realized that he would need to make some decisions, and make them soon, regarding his future with the fWo. Losing matches was disappointing.... Losing the respect of your fellow wrestlers and your employer? That was painfully disheartening. For years now, he had been the "go to" person whenever something needed to be done. He had been the one to stand up for the fWo when danger arose... time and time again. Through it all, he had been the loyal soldier, never questioning the strategy of his employer. Universal had, however, been the least competent of the bunch. One would think he'd have learned after seeing the promotion fall into the wrong hands on a couple of occasions... yet Universal had been so confident... and put the booking powers of the company up against 3vil. To make matters worse, after months of watching them cheat and take advantage of every situation, Universal had agreed to put the entire ownership of the company on the line... that move was beyond risky... it was stupid. "IT'S THE COUNTDOWN! COUNTDOWN! RUAHH~! IT'S THE COUNTDOWN! COUNTDOWN! RUAHH~!" He couldn't hit the mute button fast enough. While getting lost in his thoughts, he had ignored much of the raw noise and booty shaking coming from the TV, but the sight of "Wisk Londry" and their overplayed song "Countdown" was just enough to make him swear off television for the rest of the evening. For some reason, somebody at fWo headquarters thought that the band was talented enough to sign them up to do the Slaughter theme... The thought of Sunday Slaughter was a painful reminder to him again of his losing streak, now noticed by many of the sheets. They had been kind at first, but now used it as a chance to spew some venom at him. If you were winning and popular...you were untouchable. But drop a couple of rungs on the ladder, and the horde was waiting for you. And once they got a hold of you... they were merciless. The Strangler couldn't tell if he had hit the bottom yet, but knew that if he hadn't, he wasn't going to wait for it to happen. Things needed to change before he lost hope in his abilities. His chance would come soon; he knew it. Countdown was starting the next day, and The Strangler saw it as a chance to turn things around. 2001 had been a somewhat successful year for him, up until the last couple of months... and he needed to find a way to make 2002 start off on the right foot. For weeks, the plan had been forming in his mind, and all he needed was the right moment to act, and prove to the entire roster that he still had what it took to be a champion once again. Timing was everything. At that moment, the phone began to ring...
