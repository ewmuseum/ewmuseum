"So what did you think was going to happen?" "I don't know Chris. I guess I was living in a fantasy world. I thought that maybe, just maybe it wouldn't be like it was." Chris shook his head at Brandon's statement. He was a dear friend but he had to question his grasp on reality sometimes. It had been four years since they had teamed together to form the new age Pantheon but the industry hadn't changed. It was filled with the same users, abusers and opportunists as it always had been. "You have to be daft not to realize that men like the Strangler will do anything they can to get an advantage. That's the way cowards work." "I figured that my history would have spoke for me. I thought that with everything that had happened to Ana and the kids that they would know better than to involve them in their little games. I don't know. I'm just too trusting..."
