The Longest Weekend, Part II

27 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was a summit of epic proportions. Mike Bear. The Flying Frenchie. Brittany Chambers. Those three names by themselves would be a main event RP in any fed in this country. But when you add "the Firestarter" Ruben Ross, and his one-time student, the fWo Cruiserweight champion, Michelle Masters, it was a game changer. Throw in the intern, lovingly called Fish, and you have a recipe for...paintball? Some of the finest fWo wrestlers the company had to offer were currently wearing vests and holding guns. Paintball guns, to be precise. "So...ze gun shoots paint, and if zis paint hits 'ou, 'ou are dead." Frenchie summed up. "Yep! I'm so killing you! I mean, in the paintball way!" 'Chelle said with a cheery smile. Frenchie shook his head. "No one beats the French with guns!" He responded confidently. "There is something a bit unsettling about Lil Red waving a gun around." Bear said to Ross. Ross nodded. "Her husband is from Texas. They know three things down there, guns, steers and..OW." Michelle had helpfully stomped on her mentor's foot. "What is zis OW, Monsieur Ross? I thought it was ze queers!" Pierre Delacroix said. The Fiery Thunder Girl pointed her paintball gun at Frenchie and fired off a round, catching him right in the beret. "Hey, the game hasn't even started yet!" Brittany Chambers and Bear both protested at the same time. Pierre took off his beret and made a great show of using Fish's jacket to try to get the paint off it. The intern eventually slipped his jacket off to aid the Frenchie's efforts. "My $1000 Nike beret, ruined by zis harlot!" Frenchie huffed. "$1000?" Everyone, even Fish, turned to Frenchie. "Do you buy your stuff from the same store Dave Morey does?" Chelle asked incredulously, referring to the sunglasses Morey wore that he often claimed cost $1100. "She'll buy you another one." Ross peacemakered. "Right after we kick your asses on the field of battle, of course." Bear shook his head. "Sorry, Rubey, we must've won something to get that giant knock-off Snoopy, there's a good chance we're an excellent shot." Ross raises one eyebrow. "Ore we could have picked the right duck." "Well fine if you want to be negative. *pouts*" "Are we not Ze Winners Of Zis Match?" Bear saluted Pierre. "That's right, now let's go out there and give our teammate painful bruises with high powered air rifles!" *bang* "Not me! Ross!" "You could have been clearer." "Try not to shoot my wife either, ya jerk" "I'd never hurt a lady. At least not one who's not holding a title I want." And with that, the wrestlers spread out. Fish, teamed with Frenchie. Ross and Chelle, teacher and student, were on a team. The couple, Bear and Chambers, rounded out the battlefield. Michelle hid behind a tree as Ross sent a volley of red paint fire at Bear. Chambers returned fire on behalf of her beau, giving him a chance to reach the safety of a big rock before Ross could fire further. Frenchie, meanwhile, turned and shot Fish in the head. "Hey! I'm on your team!" The intern protested. "Sorry. Zis game is confusing. I'm French, we are used to ze civilized games! Like rugby!" The Frenchie then used Fish as a human shield to take two shots right in the chest. "OW!" Michelle, who had fired those shots, yelled out "no fair using the intern as a bullet shield!" "It's in zis job description!" The Frenchie retorted as he ran away, now down to being a team unto himself. Pierre popped off a few rounds at the rock Bear was hiding behind, causing Chambers to scurry away. Ross charged out from behind the tree. Ross dived, firing the whole time, and caught Brittany Chambers right in the chest. Kills: Chelle 1 (Fish) Ross 1 (Chambers) "Yippie kai yay, motherfucker! Hahahahaha!" Ross exulted as his teammate ran to another tree, firing (but missing) the Frenchie. "Hey!" Bear yelled to Frenchie. "Let's team up and be the Winners Of This Paintball Match!". Bear charged forward, giving Frenchie cover fire. Frenchie's volley ricocheted off a rock, splattering a few drops on Michelle's knee! "Ahahaha! I killed ze bitchy redhead with ze crappy washed up hu'band! WHOOOO!" "I heard that, Pierre!" -----3 HOURS LATER------ The Flying Frenchie held up a hand in the middle of the battlefield. Pierre was covered in every imaginable color of paint, while 'Chelle and Brittany lay against various trees, resting more than anything. This didn't stop Ross (or Chambers) from plastering Frenchie with more paint. "Zis needs ze new teams! Okay, everyone who's ever held ze World Title on one side, wit' all ze guns. Everyone else, stand over t'ere." *Group death glare* "Fine. Zen let me be ze first Frenchman to say "I quit." "The first?" Chelle said with a grin. "Maybe in the last week!" Frenchie pulled Fish in front of him, just in time to take a volley of paint from Ross's direction! "What about the no bullet shield intern rule?" Bear yelled out. Then he fired, and Frenchie again used Fish as a shield. "Weren't you just protesting him doing that?" Chambers asked. "Yeah, but it's still funny!" "Hey, look, everyone, it's Krow!" And the fWo World Heavyweight champion strode onto the battlefield, adorned in safety gear and carrying a paintball gun. "Wow, we weren't expecting you." Chelle said. KroW growled, and started shooting. Ross ran up to Krow and drilled him with the butt of his gun. He then covered Krow. An fWo official (also in paintball safety gear) ran out of the woods and counted the fall. ONE! TWO! THREE! "Does that count?" Chambers asked. "I don't know! It looks like it." 'Chelle responded as the ref handed Ross the fWo World Championship belt. "It's a celebration, bitches! Ruben Ross is once again the fWo World Heavyweight champion!" And then Michelle smacked Ross in the head with her gun! ONE! TWO! THREE! ---ONE HOUR LATER--- Michelle Masters, the 11 time fWo World Heavyweight champion, was running from the 28-time world champion Mike Bear. Fish, the one time fWo World heavyweight champion, was the current fWo Cruiserweight champion. "Pierre? Pierre?" ZZZZZPT! ---TWO POINT SIX HOURS EARLIER--- The Frenchie groaned, and wiped some of the paint out of his blurred vision. "Did someone get ze license plate of zis rhino t'at trampled me?" "Dude!" Bear said. "Fish shot you in the face and the paintball knocked you the fuck out. And then he passed out because he drank too much, the lightweight. It was a double paint KO!" "I got knocked out...by ze pain'ball? IMPOSSIBLE!" Chelle giggled. "You were out for like 20 minutes!" "DAMMIT!" Frenchie shook his head. "So...Krow didn't really come out in the middle of ze paintball game?" Everyone shook their head. "And Ross didn't pin ze Krow for ze fWo World Champions'ip?" "How hard was that paintball, exactly?" Chelle asked, concerned in spite of herself. "At least tell me ze pandas were real." "No pandas here." "NOOOO! Not the part about ze pandas, they were real and delicious!" A stunned silence fell over the group. Bear gamely attempted to jump to his partner's defense. "Oh, like all you guys haven't eaten Panda Express!" "Speaking of eating pandas, how about we do lunch?" Ross suggested. "I'm tired of killing all you motherfuckers!" Everyone looked at Ross. "Kidding! Kind of."
