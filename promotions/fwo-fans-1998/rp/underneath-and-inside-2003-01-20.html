Underneath and Inside

20 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Something is cold and blank behind her smile She's standing on an overpass In her miracle mile. Jessica's parents stood before a courtroom, as a 14-year-old Jessica buried her head into her hands. "Do you have anything you want to say in your defense?" the judge asked them. Her mother was crying. Her father looked up at him, a stern yet sad look in his eyes. "Your honor, we LOVED our daughter." her father said. "We would never do.....what we are being accused of!" "You do realize that the accusation comes from your daughter herself?" the judge asked them. "She's just confused!" her mother yelled through teary eyes. "She has problems, she doesn't know what to think! Please, don't take our daughter away from us!" You were from a perfect world A world that threw me away today Today to run away "I can't believe...why would she say such things?" her mother asked him as they sat in the hallway of the courthouse together, awaiting the judge's verdict. "I don't know." her father admitted, shaking his head. Then, a clerk ran up to them, panting and wheezing. "What? What is it?" they asked him. "It's your daughter." he said through breaths. "She's disappered." A pill to make you numb A pill to make you dumb A pill to make you anybody else A seventeen-year-old Jessica shoved at least four different-shaped pills into her mouth, and chugged it down with water. She was dressed in torn-up clothing and looked as if she hadn't washed for days. "Why are you taking those now?" an equally-homely looking guy asked her. "To work up the courage." she replied grimly. But all the drugs in this world Won't save her from herself She banged on the door of a large house in suburbia, angrily awaiting its response. Her father answered the door. "Jessica, honey, what is it?" he asked her. He saw what she was carrying. She smiled. "That's not my name anymore." Her mouth was an empty cut And she was waiting to fall Just bleeding like a polaroid that Lost all her dolls Two years had passed. Rei sat against the stone wall of the alleyway, attempting to cover her head from the rain with the hood of a torn jacket she had picked up somewhere. Dark bangs under her eyes suggested that she had not slept for days. "The fucking rain won't let up." she said as she shivered. "Jessica?" someone screamed. Rei looked up. "Are you Jessica?" She saw a man in his late 40s, dressed in a raincoat and wearing glasses. "That was once my name." Rei replied. "Who wants to know?" "I have news for you, Jessica. Very good news." You were from a perfect world A world that threw me away today Today to run away Rei looked around at her surrondings, a few months later. It was her parents' home. She had inherited, well, everything. Do I really deserve this? Rei asked herself. She suddenly fell to the floor, holding her head in pain. She reached for her bottle of pills. A pill to make you numb A pill to make you dumb A pill to make you anybody else But all the drugs in this world Won't save her from herself She took a single white pill, washing it down with water, and gapsed for breath, leaning against her couch. "It's coming out." Rei gasped. "And I can't stop it." Fin.
