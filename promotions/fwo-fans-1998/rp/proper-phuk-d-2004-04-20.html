Master Phan opened her eyes as she heard the faint sound of muffled footsteps. The elderly Asian woman rose from her bed and slipped into her robe without making a sound. Whoever the person was that had broken into the dojo was about to receive horrible, painful justice. If there was anything left, Master Phan would call the police. She didn't appreciate being awakened from her sleep. There would be no need to call the police. With a gentle hand and a grace that defied her advanced years, Master Phan drifted past the curtain that hung as a door to her bedroom and made her way down the hall. She stopped in the small alcove that opened up into the large dojo and looked into the shadows. There was movement there, and whoever it was knew what they were doing. Good weight distribution. She hugged the wall and closed her eyes, reaching out with her other sense. He was calm and carried himself with a level of comfort with his surroundings. He knew the dojo. He'd spent many hours, days, years here. This was no intruder. It was a lost soul who'd found his way home. "David," Master Phan said as she stepped into the room. She flipped on the light and let her eyes adjust. There was a young man standing in the middle of the room, staring at the various pictures on the wall. He made no attempt to acknowledge her presence and didn't react at all to the lights. She stepped further into the room and felt his presence on a deeper level. It looked like David Cross but something about him wasn't... right. He pointed at the picture of the Teachers. "Where is Master Hiroshi?" Master Phan gave David "The Look". "You sneak in here, wake me up, disrespect me by not facing me, and now ask questions? Have you lost your mind?" David lowered his arm and turned to face the woman who had been like a second mother to him for most of his life. Master Phan hadn't seen him in quite some time. The scar on his face and the crimson eye were all new to her. The smile David gave her was also unlike him. "Have I lost my mind?" he echoed. "Indeed I have, good lady." He bowed with his arms outstretched. "Indeed I have." She wrinkled her nose at him. "Your aura stinks of corruption." "That could be the burrito I bought from 7-11." He turned back to the picture. "Has Master Hiroshi been here lately?" "I heard he's looking for you." Master Phan moved closer to her former student. "David, your chi is out of balance." "That's what my police-assigned therapist told me. I'm Nucking Futs, and I'm way out of balance." Even as a young child training within these walls, David had always been a tortured soul. This was something different. "I can see your anger. I can see your hate. It consumes you." "It had to. He wasn't strong enough to do what needed to be done." Cross still wouldn't face her when he spoke. He'd always made eye contact when he spoke. This wasn't like David. This wasn't David. "And you are strong enough?" Master Phan queried. "What happens when your task is completed? Will the balance be restored?" Would it? For the first time, the personality known as Alex felt something he hadn't felt before. He didn't like the thought of Dave coming back, especially now that things were really starting to line up. Alex was free. He wasn't about to lose that freedom. "He needs me. Dave is too soft to survive without me." Despite hearing him refer to himself in the third person, Master Phan continued to press. "I think he has more strength than you give him credit for." Cross lost interest in the picture and stared at Master Phan. "Looks like I'm not the only crazy person in the room. He's a bitch. This prison he lives in is filled with hardcore violent criminals that would rape him at the drop of a bar of soap. I keep him safe. I protect him." "But who protects him from you?" Cross was losing patience. "Where is Master Hiroshi!" Troubled or not, there was no way Master Phan was going to let her student speak to her like that. "You make demands of me? Perhaps you need to learn another lesson?" "You can't school me in Pain." Cross sneered. "I've graduated from that class a long time ago." "Get out. Come back when you are ready to face your reality and your balance." Master Phan waited for David to come at her with an attack of some kind. Violence had a tight grip on him and wasn't about to let go. The attack never came. "Suit yourself. Tell Hiroshi I'll see him soon." David turned and walked out of the dojo without a second glance. Master Phan felt the comfort and harmony of the room return. It wasn't until David had left that she realized just how much his very presence had disrupted the chi around her. She didn't know what exactly had hurt him and what he planned to do, but if he didn't get himself under control soon he was lost. "You saw it too?" Master Phan turned. "I did," she said to Master Hiroshi as he stepped into the room. "Why does he look for you?" "He corrupted my daughter," Hiroshi said grimly. "He hurt her, and now wants to hurt me. Scores will be settled." He nodded to Master Phan. "Goodnight." She returned the nod. "Goodnight." He was lying and she knew it. Phan Hiroshi Ultimate Karate was not about deceit. Balance needed to be restored.
