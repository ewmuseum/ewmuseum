Series: Alternate Epochs Title: "Best Days" A distant smile appeared over Kevin's face. Brand's story had involved family, real family, something Kevin never really had, but always had wanted. He sat there, thinking about his good times, the moments in his life where he was truly happy. "So come on then kid," Alex said egging him on, "spit it out!" "I guess I'm a cliche kind of guy Brando," Kevin started, "because the best day of my life was the day I asked the woman I love to marry me." The others sort of looked at him a little crookedly, somewhat shocked at the fact the young man had even been in love. Avo smiled a subtle smile, almost as though he had heard something more about this story. "Her name was Donna, Donna Sandburg. We had known each other since we were kids. She and I had been through everything together. The games of tag on the playground. The countless dances of middle school. The trying times of high school. We'd done it all, and survived it." Kevin paused, taking a drink of the soda in front of him. A man walked over to the bar, but quickly turned and left. It was Jack Harmen, better known as High Flyer, and he was listening to a pair of headphones. He moved towards some magazine store, but as Kevin looked to see what he was buying, his thoughts were brought back to the table by a question. "And you asked her to marry you?" Alex asked. "Well we were young, and we were in love. We had done it all together, and there was no other person I wanted to be with. She knew me better than anyone, and we had shared so much... It was young love, but I think, deep down, it was true love." Kevin said with a sigh. Avo stared at him intently, and when they made eye contact Avo quickly looked away. "So when did you ask?" Brandon prodded as Kevin smiled, ordering another soda. "Well, it was the end of our freshmen year. We had both been attending the University of Washington..." Kevin was interrupted by Avo. "Go Huskies!" Avo said, with a grin. "Yeah, well we were packing to head home. I had the job of moving her heavy boxes. Well as I placed the last one in the UHAUL I pretended to drop it on my toe..."
