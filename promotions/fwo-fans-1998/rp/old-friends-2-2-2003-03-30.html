Old Friends (2/2)

30 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"You should try the crab, it's great. We just got in a big shipment - apparently the Dungenesses really kicked ass this year." Scott Slugger shrugged across at Ice - or, more accurately, at Tony Barrett. Ice was in the past now. "Hey, it's your restaurant." Barrett nodded. "Yeah, I guess it is, isn't it? It's really wonderful - everything I always dreamed. Business is great, I'm rolling in the dough, and... I really like doing this, too. As good as I was wrestling, I think I'm even better at this. You know, less than a year we've been open and we're already negotiating contracts to open Tony's Place up and down the west coast. Today the coast, tomorrow the world." His smile stretched across his face. They ordered - crab legs for Slugger, grilled salmon for Barrett. When the waiter left, Barrett turned back to Slugger. "So what brings you out here anyway? Last I heard, you guys were supposed to be in France right around now. It's a damn shame what happened, that the company's in the shape it's in right now..." He looked up, almost wistfully. "... but I guess it is what it is. So what's up?" "Well, it's about Mitch." "What about him?" "You've been watching the shows lately." It wasn't a question, but a statement. "Yeah, I have. I'm glad that you seem to be doing better, too... Winters and all that... God, that was a clusterfuck." At the mention of Winters, who, as recently as three months ago, had nearly single-handedly destroyed - and ended - Slugger's life, Slugger paused and looked out the window. Every day he faced hard reminders about what he had lost because of that, and today was apparently no different. Barrett saw the change in his demeanor and frowned. "Hey, I'm sorry... I just mean, it's good that you're doing better. You are doing better, right?" Slugger paused before answering. "Better than then, yeah. I'm still clean, if that's what you're wondering. But we've wandered well off the path here... Mitch. He's trying to get rid of me." "What do you mean, get rid of you?" "He wants me out." "What do you mean, wants you out? Is this a rib or something?" Barrett's eyes momentarily went dark. "Scott, if you're just out here trying to work me for some stupid reason..." "No, no, it's not that. I'm serious. We're doing a team challenge thing against Chronos, Frenchie, and some other guy..." "Hello, AWA flashbacks." "... and I think that after that's over, he's going to move to cut me out. Completely. It's not like he couldn't do it. He could probably get me completely fired, not just kicked out of the group. I can't let him do this to me." "So... what? I appreciate that you're in a predicament, but why are you telling me this?" "Because I want you back." Barrett's eyes went wide. "You... want me back." "That's what I said." Tony Barrett closed his eyes for a moment. Scott Slugger couldn't see what was going on inside them, but he had a feeling. Ice had been a three-time fWo World Champion, and glory like that wasn't let go of easily. Finally, Barrett opened his eyes again, and Slugger could see it was still Barrett, not Ice. He knew the difference. "No. Forget it. I'm done." "So that's your answer? You're done." "Did I stutter?" Slugger frowned, his anger starting to rise. "I came all the way out here, from the middle of the middle fucking east, to tell you I need your help... to give you a chance to get back to doing what you do best... and all you can say to me is 'no, forget it, I'm done'?" Barrett said nothing. "Well, thanks a whole fucking lot!" Barrett put his fists together and leaned his head on them, looking across at Slugger. "Scott... did you ever consider that maybe this Kinkade guy is working you? I mean, give me one good reason to trust him." Slugger looked at Barrett with anger blazing in his eyes. "I guess I don't have a reason to trust him any more than I ever should have trusted you, huh? I guess I don't have a reason to trust anybody. I don't know why I ever thought you'd help me in the first place. You've turned your back on me more times than everybody else has on Sting - well fuck you!" Slugger was on his feet, and on the verge of shouting. His voice had already carried, causing various patrons to look up from their seafood. "Where were you in December, huh? Where were you when Donaven Winters kidnapped her, huh, friend? Where were you WHEN I LOST MY SON?" Tony Barrett stood up, and though his eyes were calm, they were also cold. As Ice. "You keep your voice down in my restaurant - and keep it down while I escort you out. Understand?" That was the final straw. Slugger threw a right at Barrett. Barrett's reflexes were as quick as they had ever been, and he caught Slugger's arm and twisted it. There was a small crack, and a few patrons gasped. Barrett pulled Slugger towards him by the arm until they were face-to-face. "Scott... get out of my restaurant... and get help." Barrett released Slugger's arm and, in the same motion, used his other arm to push Slugger toward the door. Slugger stood there for a moment, clutching his injured arm and looking back in anger, then turned and walked out the door.
