Laughing in the Face of Pain

27 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What happened? What's going on? Where... Ow.... Tempest's thoughts were barely a whisper in the shouting match of her senses as she squinted in the bright sunlight reflecting off the sterile white walls of the hospital. Hospital... no... no... got to get out. Tempest struggled in the grip of sleep and drugs and pain and eventually realized she was struggling in the grip of a nurse as well. The larger woman tightened her grip and said, "Hey! Hey c'mon now! It's ok." "Zuh?" Tempest choked out, awaking with a start. "Where'm I?" she slurred and leaned dangerously over the edge of the bed. Her eyes widened and then she leaned back. "Nevermine... what... what day is it?" "Why it's Christmas hun," said the nurse, trying to guide her back to a prone position. "Just relax and take it easy. You're safe here." Tempest's eyes, darkened with the blood of bruises, drifted closed as she said, "Safe... I'm not sure I know what that means." I'm in the hospital on Christmas morning. Joy to the World. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Tempest woke up on the mattress in the middle of the floor of a squalid little room. Outside, the noise of the street raised a steady din that she would have hidden from if she had a pillow to do it with. She raised her head and the pain from the activities of the night before stabbed freshly into her mind. For a moment she hated it all; the smell, the place, the pain. She glanced down at the blood stained rags around her leg. The flimsy wooden walls of the pit were always an extra combatant, but last night was worse than usual. One of the planks had splintered on impact and her opponent had tried to run her through the chest with it. The crowd loved that sort of thing, sent the bets through the roof. That was the good news: she'd made a good sum of money for the fight, almost enough to eat and go dancing uptown again. The bad news was that it would have to wait until she didn't look so battered. She sat up wincing and wondered for a moment if her opponent felt even worse. After all, Tempest never reacted well to being backed into a corner. So when the woman tried to impale her on the sharp end of the board, Tempest had simply reversed it. The results had been gruesome. Suddenly she chuckled. She snorted and held her ribs, trying to stop the convulsive laughing fit that bubbled up from her stomach. She rolled back and giggled as she said, "I'd take her dancing to make up for it, but she has no butt to wiggle anymore." She snickered and rolled onto her side, wishing the memory away in the guise of laughter. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Tempest smiled weakly and the makeup artist dabbed gently at her face as she said, "I dunno Tempest. I'm good, but I'm not a miracle worker. Are you sure you want to do this? The signing could be postponed." Tempest opened her eyes and said, "What? I'm a wrestler, the fans know that. They don't expect me to be flawlessly beautiful all the time." Her face was pale and pasty, the look of entirely too much makeup caked on to hide the bruises and welts. "Yeah but they don't expect you to look like you've been embalmed," said the makeup artist. "And without it, you're just... way to frightening. That was a hideous match you went through." She wrung her hands with obvious concern and Tempest chuckled and said, "Don't worry Melissa. I bet Eddy looks pretty bad too. He..." She stopped and chuckled and Melissa looked at her oddly. She snorted and tried to hold back her laughter, but couldn't and choked out, "I... I thought Eddy couldn't possibly get any paler, but I think he did, what with the blood loss and all." She laughed until her eyes started to tear up and then she grabbed a tissue and held it to her eyes as she said, "Oh no, your work is running here." Melissa crouched down and started to fix Tempest's makeup and said in amazement, "How do you do that? How do you manage to laugh when things are so terrible?" Tempest's eyes looked haggard for a moment, but brightened with her smile once more as she said, "I have to. After all, if I stop laughing, I might cry." She took a deep breath and swallowed hard, her composure returning and said, "And if I cry, I'm afraid I might never stop."
