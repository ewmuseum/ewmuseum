Solitude - When you can't find the tourniquet...

17 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last night (Sunday, March 17th) Tel Aviv, Israel. Whatever the hell had initially happened that night, it set off a sequence of events that seemed bound to change lives forever... Nobody was aware of the death toll then, but the sound of not-so distant gunfire and shouting put a sick feeling in the pit of Amy Campbell's stomach - a feeling that it hadn't quite reached its peak. She'd retreated, though...like a coward, and obviously not proud in the least, but Kellen Kinkade had accidentally - or so it seemed - lead her right in the midst of the bloodshed and with an overwhelming fear of all things involving death, it was more than one twenty year old redhead who'd never seen that sort of gore with her own eyes could handle. Bodies, blood, bullets, bombs...bullets. However many people - peers - were out there feeling ten times what she'd felt being shot by one maniac back in January, Amy wasn't sure. Nor was she entirely sure where the 'Rat Pack' was: She'd fled like the HELPLESS kids had been, no matter how capable she was to help anyone else...the only thing on her mind in those split seconds after really seeing the 'warzone' was herself... Not Ivy, not Eli, not Sean, not...Brandon. Amy groaned at the thought of him possibly looking for her in all of that. She prayed he was helping people who needed it and found safety himself, but she'd come up to his room to pack everything for him so they could make an exit as quickly as possible when he got back...and he wasn't there yet. Until he was standing in front of her, the worry wasn't even nearly about to let up. She was running purely on adrenaline from shock and fear, moving as quickly as possible but in a momentary lapse of power, the tattooed and normally "rough 'n tumble" redhead let herself collapse onto the bed and shut her eyes to the world... But not her ears. A rattling sound came, and at first she wasn't entirely aware of it - the noise was lost amongst all the rest but it persisted, and finally Aimz opened her eyes a crack to find the door jolting and trembling the same was she was. The very second it flew open, Amy was on her feet and ready - hopefully - for whatever the intruder may have been and to her surprise, a man no older than thirty stood before her, unarmed and making an incredibly admirable effort of holding his nerves together like the tattered fWo shirt that barely covered his midrift. He looked as surprised as she did at the sudden company, though. "...Amy...?" "What?" Was the only word that she could muster, and even that was spoken in a dry, shakey and broken voice that didn't seem to be her own. She recognized him, just wasn't sure of the name - or why he was there. But it wasn't exactly the best time for introductions, and neither were about to give any. "Uhh...you..uhh....you're in here......why?" "Waiting for Brandon. I...wasn't too helpful out there." He sighed, "About that..." "Huh?" The redhead did not like the look on his face. Then again, who - aside from those who faced it almost daily - would even dare not to look horrified at the practical holocaust they had and were still enduring? It took a minute for the short man to speak but he eventually snapped to, motioning for her to sit down again. "It's about Brand..errr...Brandon..." The world seemed to be coming to an end at the hands of George Bush, a few stores of oil and an ancient holy war anyway...why not throw the death of your everything into the stew?
