Visiting an old friend?

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It all started with you. You know that don't you? It was different before. Don't get me wrong it wasn't like you made me who I am, it's more like you drove me that extra step beyond who I was. My Dad was the one, with his holier than thou attitude that led me down the road at first. You, you were just the catalyst that caused me to step further away from my family and make my life into some holy crusade. It's not like it was a long step away though. I had always done the right thing. Even when it made those around me suffer and made my life a living hell, it was hard walking that straight and narrow. I just didn't realize what it was costing me, and damn it I don't know if I would change if I could. Look at everything that has happened. Look at all I have lost and will never get back, all of those years consumed with anger and living my life in a fog because of you. I know that I can't change the past and I don't see how the future holds much either. My life was sacrificed all those years ago and I didn't even realize it at the time. I have fought the demons for all these years but what I never realized was that you had already won. One thing led to another and now I sit here without everything I have fought for. I will tell you this though, I can't change who I am now. That would just mean that the last five years was for nothing.
