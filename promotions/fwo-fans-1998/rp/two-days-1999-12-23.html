Two Days.

23 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:::The scene opens with a jiggling camera shot of a parking lot at night, moon shining bright in the sky above. It's a somewhat eerie, deserted place- almost all of the cars have left, heading off to wherever it is they head off to. In the distance, a lone figure can be seen approaching a somewhat familiar car- a blue and white sports car, though the model cannot be determined. As the camera moves closer to the figure, we hear a voice.::: Geoff Green: Dammit! He's about to reach the car- but if I can get an interview with Slugger now, it'll be huge.. :::to the figure of Slugger in the distance::: C'mon man...just hold up a couple seconds longer... :::Sure enough, as we can see by the fact that we soon get close enough to make out both the features of Slugger and the Dodge Viper, Slugger has noticed the camera-weilding figure of Geoff Green making his way towards him. He stops to wait, leaning up against the car, silhoutted in the bright moonlight as Green approaches.::: Slugger: Yeah? :::Green slows down in front of Slugger.::: Green: Scott...after what went down on Warfare, do you have any comments at all? And what the hell was up with that "he's right" stuff you said to me? :::Slugger laughs.::: Slugger: That?! You don't want to know, believe me...but I have a feeling you'll be finding out soon. Now, you want to know if I have any comments on Warfare? :::Green nods.::: Slugger: You're damn right I do. You see, Geoff, two months ago, I single-handedly brought together what could have been the greatest assemblage of talent in fWo history. Lance Star... Chris Universal... Ice... and myself. We were gonna rule the fWo, Geoff. We were gonna be on top. But along the way, something went wrong. First, not even a week after I formed this particular group, I lost the World Title I had held for nearly three months. I lost the World Title that, combined, I've held for almost half the year! Half the year, Geoff. Think about that for a minute. And after that, Lance Star- the very reason the Dynasty was formed. He showed me something- something Ice used to have, and something I thought Universal had. He showed me he had *what it takes* to make it in this business! Lance Star suffered a crippling injury, leaving our little group short one member. And in all seriousness, I hope to see him back in the ring sooner rather than later- but when I do, he's gonna be short a stable. And after I lost the World Title and Lance Star got injured, we more or less just floundered around for a while. In point of fact, Geoff, we lost *both* matches we were in at BattleBowl! Both of them. And I can't live with that. I'm not a loser, and I don't stand for losers- and that's what I had become by associating myself with this group. They dragged me down, Geoff- after I formed the Dynasty, I didn't do jack shit for two months. And when it came right down to it, I had to take matters into my own hands. You see, I made the Dynasty- and tonight, I broke it. No more Ice and Universal, dragging me down, keeping me out of the spotlight. Not anymore. Because *I*, and only I, control my own destiny now. Well, I do have a friend- but you'll meet him later. :::Geoff Green takes a moment to let this all sink in, and then moves on to his next line of questioning.::: Green: Do you have any thoughts on the World Title match on Friday? Slugger: Do I? Stupid question, Geoff, really. In the very first match of the show, I face Ice. Ice, we've been around the block- friends, enemies, back to friends again, and back to enemies again. But let me reassure you- there will be no tearful reunion, not next year, not the year after that, not ever- and I have no qualms about driving the proverbial stake through your heart and ending your career. But, seeing as it's the Christmas season, I think I'll be a giving man and save that for the future. All the same, come Friday, the WGA is gonna be a taking man. In fact, I'm gonna take *my* World Title- the one and only reason I'm even in this business, in case you didn't know. You see, Tony, we may have only fought once, but I know you inside and out- I know your every move a good five minutes before you do it! You see, you may be strong, but you lack one thing I have- intelligence. I know your playbook, Tony- and I'm gonna use every page of it against you. And once I do, you will be begging me- "Please, Scott, end it, please, please, Mr. Slugger!"- and do you know what I'll do? Let me tell you. I'm gonna lift you up, off of your knees, slap you in the face, and give you a one-way ticket on the Curveball Express. And that's not just a boast- that's a fact. Green: How about the Flying Frenchie? Should you beat Ice, you will have to face him. Slugger: The Frenchie? Well, I gotta say it's poetic justice. He's been floating around me all year, like a little gnat, trying to annoy me to the point where I finally give in and give him the beating he's been asking for. Until now, I've chosen to let him keep buzzing. But on Friday night, December the Twenty-Fourth, I will finally come face-to-face, nose-to-nose, arm-to-wing with the gnat- and I'm gonna flatten him like the little, tiny, buzzing pest that he is. You see, they call this pay-per-view Twilight- and in this case, it's very appropriate. Because once I start coming after him, Frenchie's in the Twilight of his career... Whether his loyal fanbase likes it...or not. Now, Geoff- get the hell away from me. :::Before Geoff Green can reply, Slugger enters the Viper and slams the door, peeling away in a cloud of dust which floats in front of the moon over the ghostly landscape. Geoff Green shuts his camera off, and appropriately, we fade to black.:::
