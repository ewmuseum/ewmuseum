The View aftermath

14 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In the hallway of the Homewood Suites in downtown Seattle, Geoff Green carries his camera on his shoulder, determined to make sense of the past few days. He arrives at room 312 and knocks on the door. Brittany answers the door looking a little haggard, but otherwise in good spirits. She smiles as she looks up to see Geoff at the door. “Hey Geoff! Sorry I couldn’t stay and chat before, but, you know...” “Hi Brittany. Yeah, I understand. But seriously, what happened on the show?” “Come on Geoff, we’re going to start this interview right now?” “Brit, they told me to come down here and figure this out. They knew that we were old friends. They want Mike on the show, but they’re concerned. They told me the deal was Mike could control himself, and with all the lingering bad feelings from 3 years ago, they don’t want to bring in an unstable person who could create another disaster.” Brittany’s face falls a little listening to Geoff, but she perks up a little after he’s finished, inviting Geoff into the room. “Well, let’s get this on camera then, try to alleviate management’s concerns.” Brittany leads the way through the foyer into a surprisingly large living room. A couch and easy chair on one wall border a large window overlooking the bay. On the opposite wall hangs a 45” plasma TV. A full kitchen is visible. “Geez Brit, I didn't know you guys had this kind of money.” “Oh we live pretty modestly, but we make pretty good money off the wrestling schools so we like to treat ourselves every now and then. We'll spend a little while here and when things settle down, we'll probably find a nice place to rent out in the suburbs or a small apartment in the city, but we live in Philadelphia. Nothing's going to change that.” Brittany sits on the couch and is joined by Geoff. Brittany takes a moment, closes her eyes and breathes deep, and is ready for the interview. “So this is for the website, I guess?” “Yeah, they asked me to film a quick interview, they'll have it up the day of the show, just to reassure people. So, Brittany, can you tell us what exactly happened on The View on Monday?” “Well, the segment went pretty well up until Elisabeth Hasselbeck started getting involved. She starts in about wrestling being responsible for some negligent parents' kids who perform wrestling moves on each other and hurt themselves. I tried to have a rational discussion with her, but then she just starts screeching at me. The people backstage said when he heard the sound of her voice, he just lost it, grabbed the nearest chair he could find, ran in and knocked her out with a steel chair to the head.” “And they let him go?!” “Well, come on, it was Elisabeth Hasselbeck.” The camera moves and we can tell Geoff is thinking about it, and he just shrugs. “Well, ok, you got me there. But still, I thought Mike was cured?” “Well that's what Dr. Talbert said intitially. He reevaluated Mike on Monday and he believes he's well enough to come to the show on Monday.” “Whoa whoa, wait a minute. There are some bombshells you just dropped. First, initially? Second, *well ENOUGH*?” “Ok, when we were preparing to come back with the FWO, Dr. Talbert believed he was cured, or at least that's the word he said best conveyed Mike's current condition. He said he should be able to resume a normal lifestyle. Of course, pro wrestling is anything but normal, and on his reevaluation, Dr. Talbert said that, though he might not be in as good of a condition as he thought, he should be fine for the show, at least on a trial basis.” “That doesn't exactly fill me with reassurance, Brittany. I doubt the brass feel any differently. Can I see him? Talk to him?” “Oh sure, you can see him. Talking might not be an option.” Brittany gets up and leads Geoff over to the bathroom. “Oh hey, we don't need to see him right now if that's the case.” “What? No! No, this is just where we put him.” Brittany opens the door to reveal Mike Bear in a condition long time followers are familiar with. He is in a straitjacket, strapped to a handcart, with a muzzle over his face. He thrashes a little when the door opens and subtly growls. Geoff takes a step back. “THIS is well ENOUGH?!” “What, the restraints? Oh that's nothing, he's having a ball, right Mike?” Mike shakes the cart back and forth, thrashing more violently. Brittany closes the door. “He's fine.” “Brit, I've got to go to William with this, we can't put him on the show after what happened on Countdo...” Brittany moves over to Geoff and puts a finger on his lips, interrupting him. “Geoff, we're old friends, you and Mike go way back. I need you to trust me.” She flashes a smile that has won many arguments, but it's her eyes that convince Geoff. They display the conviction of a woman who knows her man and what he's capable of.” “Ok, Brittany, I'll back you up. They won't be happy, but he'll at least make it onto Wednesday's show. Don't make me sorry I stood up for you guys.” “Geoff, that is the last thing you should worry about.” “Ok, one last thing, anything to say to the FWO fans?” “Hey FWO fans! Brittany Chambers-Bear here to tell you that you better tune in to TBS on Wednesday night, featuring the grand return of none other than 2 time FWO World Champion Mike Bear!” “Brit, that was so lame.” “Shut up Geoff! You try being in front of the camera some time.” “No thanks, I like it back here.” The segment fades to black.
