You can't always have what you want.

7 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jon Crisp wants his spot back. ...anybody that witnessed Slaughter saw him rant and rave, and bitch about things in public, instead of handling it with the people he should've handled it with backstage. See, Jon Crisp likes to portray himself as something he's not. Something he'll never be. That's why, when he goes off on his little hissy- fits, the majority of the roster laughs at him. We don't take him seriously, simply because... ...he's a puppet. That's the truth. The reason I'm a tag champion and he's not is because he allowed it to happen. One thing I've learned about the fWo, is you do have an opinion. Crisp whole comedy spiel was and is a failure, and because he has an ego the size of Texas, it'd help him sleep better at night, if he blamed the next man, instead of doing what real me do, and that's accept responsibility and move on. Crisp can't do that, because in doing that, he'd have to admit that he couldn't do something. That he's not perfect. And, even though we see him for what he is, week in and week out, he's totally oblivious to the obvious because in his eyes, he's a God. Well, let's keep it real, Jon... not only are you not... I could beg to differ on the argument that you're even talented. The cream rises to the top, as they say. When the fWo shut down, you weren't the only person screwed out of a shot at a prestigious title... Newsflash, bitch... I was, too. And, as for your five star match theory? I find it hard to take your criticism seriously, seeing as how your biggest fWo career highlight was a victory over Ruben Ross
