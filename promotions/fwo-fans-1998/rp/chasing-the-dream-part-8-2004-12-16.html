Chasing The Dream - Part 8

16 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
May 18th 1999 - 8:05AM Milo rose early to a roosters cry. He rubbed his eyes, then stood to stretch his limbs. He muffled a yawn with the palm of his hand, and walked downstairs to see if anyone else was awake yet. It sounded as if Earl and Dorothy were having some sort of disagreement. He was concerned, but felt it wasn't his business to investigate or intrude on their personal life. It was about noon by the time Earl and Dorothy had their things gathered and were ready to depart. They gave their house keys to a neighbor who would be house sitting, then they hit the road. They began to drive, and a strange silence filled the car. "This is odd from two people who have talked my ears off the last two days" Milo thought. "Maybe I should break the silence". "So, you guys go to Canada often?" He received no reply, and the car continued to drive on. Becoming very bored, Milo made an attempt to play "travel bingo" by counting the many signs which passed. The game however, grew old fast and it was rather difficult to keep score with no pen or paper. Hour after hour, the mind numbing silence continued. Running low on options, Milo decided sleep was the best way to pass the time. But was it...? Like before he was unable to move, only to watch the events unveil... He again saw the bloodstained corpse of the same boy... "There's the other one!" Milo heard as 4 men charged towards him with metal pipes, chains, and knives... Once again he was unable to move, and the men grew closer and closer... Milo awoke with a scream, jolting upward and frightening everyone in the house (including himself). The car swerved, narrowly missing an oncoming transfer truck. "What in the fuck is wrong with you boy?" Earl hollered as he looked at Milo in the rearview mirror. "You could have gotten us killed!". Milo apologized, and struggled to catch his breath. "You mind if we stop for a few minutes?" Milo asked. Earl ignored and drove by a gas station, and several restaurants. "Yeah Earl, you need a break. Lets stop and stretch our legs" Dorothy insisted. The car rolled into a rest station, and everyone got out to stretch their legs. "I'm leaving here in twenty minutes, and I'm not waiting for anyone" Earl bitched as he walked away from the others. Dorothy hurried to follow behind him, but he never slowed to wait for her. Milo realized that he was an unwanted guest, but he really wanted a drive to Canada. He sat on the ground, and leaned his back against the side of Earl's car. "They can't leave on me if I'm with the car," Milo thought. He continued to sit, starring off in a daze, as his mind slipped away from reality. "This life is tough, and my body aches. But if I can live my dreams for even a day, then it will all be worth it." Earl and Dorothy were making their way back to the car. "Good, he's not here. Come and hurry to the car Dorothy" Earl said, unable to see Milo sitting by the other side of the car. "But its only been 5 minutes, we should wait for him" Dorothy pleaded as she grabbed earls arm. "You saw the way that monster acts! He's the devil Dorothy! The devil!" Earl slammed his fist down onto the roof of the car. "We've taken this drug addict far enough. For all we know he could slash or throats at the next corner, and quite frankly I'm not will to...." Earl stopped as he noticed Milo, and knew that he'd probably heard everything he just said. Milo slowly stood to his feet, and looked directly into Earls eyes. He was very mad, but was unable to do anything but trn and walk away, never looking back. "Now look what you've done Earl!" Dorothy yelled. "Come back Milo!" Milo ignored, and continued on his quest.
