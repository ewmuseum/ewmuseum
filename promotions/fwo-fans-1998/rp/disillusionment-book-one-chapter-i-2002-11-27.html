Disillusionment: Book One - Chapter I

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ya know, it's funny what you can get people to believe when they really want to believe something other than the truth. Like that whole "War of the Worlds" thing back in the twenties or thirties or whenever the fuck that was. People wanted to believe that aliens were taking over the world because Hoover fucked it up so bad that even the rule of evil alien overlords sounded better. Heh. Maybe it would have been. I've always wondered why people assumed an alien invasion would be such a bad thing. Seriously. Let's say these aliens had discovered nirvana, looked at our world, seen how fucked up it was, and wanted to come teach us how to live our lives in perfect ecstasy. Knowing how distorted human reality is, though, I wouldn't doubt that we'd meet their peaceful offering with guns and atom bombs. Damn. Here I am, going off on a tangent, and I haven't even taken the time to introduce myself. My name is Jack. Jack Lundin. That's pronounced with a long I, like Lun-deen. If you remember, I started off talking about the human distorted sense of reality. That's just what it is...distorted. A much more educated man said it better in three words than I could in all the days left of my life. Perception is reality. Sounds like some philosophical bullshit that you'd learn in Psych 101 over at the local community college, I know, but just think about it. What do we know other than what we encounter in our daily lives? Absolutely nothing. They say there's a fine line between genius and insanity, but I don't think those geniuses are insane...just extra perceptive. They get to see more of reality than we do, and this pisses us off, so we slap them in a white coat with long sleeves and haul them off. Most people would say they have a screw loose, but not me. I was given this gift...or curse...I don't know what to call it. Sometimes it's great and other times it manifests nightmares that most human beings would not find imaginable. I know what the geniuses...the crazies, know. I'm Jack Lundin, and it's my job to save the sovereignty of the entire world. I know. It sounds crazy, doesn't it? But who are you to say, really? You can't know anymore than what you learn, and I'll tell you this right now, you don't learn an awful lot. They don't want you to know. Fuck. Now I sound like a conspiracy theorist. I promise you, I'm not. Nothing that I know is theory. It's all cold hard facts, and I hate it. Let me explain. If I asked you who the most powerful man on the planet was, who would you say? Most people would say George W. Bush. They'd be wrong. Dead wrong. Emphasis on dead. A few would be closer, as they'd say Alan Greenspan. Closer, but still wrong. Maybe not dead, but wrong. This is what these people are told...and...so, they believe it. It's not like that, though. Not like that at all. What if I told you the hierarchy of power on this globe is taller and deeper than anything you ever imagined? That the president who you all cherish and love so much is nothing more than a whipping boy who wipes the asses of those who really hold the power? You probably wouldn't believe me, would you? Eh...no biggie...your funeral. If you don't believe me, well, you might as well stop reading here, because the rest of this shit is gonna seem pretty far-fetched. Yeah. I'll see ya. . . . Oh, your staying...well then. I suppose you're here because you want to know the truth. Heh. No you don't. Jack Nicholson put it best in A Few Good Men. "You Can't Handle the Truth." You can't, ya know...handle it, that is. There are powers at work in this world that you couldn't fathom if you made it your only goal for the rest of your life. If you gave up eating, sleeping, and using the shitter all to dedicate your life to learning what I know, you'd still never come close. Yeah, I know a lot. A gift? I don't know about that, but these "powers" that rule our world, that rule our very existance...they don't know that I know. If they did, I wouldn't be here. I'd be executed just the millions of others who knew too much. I don't know why I'm so lucky. I don't know why they can't find me. There was another like me. He was a wise man. His name was Burkenstock. I could never claim to know even a fraction of what he knew, but he didn't have this ability I have...this ability to stay hidden...to run. Just before they got him, Burkenstock came to visit me in one of my many hideouts. I can still remember it vividly, like it was fuckin' yesterday. They had been on his tail for several days, and he and I both knew that it wouldn't be long before he was caught. Both of us were professional wrestlers at the time. I still am...hey, even the Earth's Last Hope needs to make a living, right? We had worked an event that very night and he knew it was only a matter of hours before he was captured, so he came to me. I couldn't do anything for him. When they want you, they get you, it's just that simple. He had been running for months. I never heard of anyone being able to run for that long before, but he was good at it. He came to my hideout...the basement of a factory in Carson City and handed me a bunch of letters. Things he had collected over the years...to help in our fight. Unlike myself, he went looking for conflict...me, I was thrust into this position. Before he left, he told me something I couldn't believe. I was the chosen one. Get that? I don't. If you know what it means, let me know, because I'll be damned if I have the slightest clue. The...Chosen One? Heh. Sounds far-fetched. I'm done with this Star Wars masquerading around like friggin' Luke Skywalker bullshit. Chosen one my ass. Anyway, he told me that and then he left. They were waiting for him outside. As soon as he left the factory he saw them. He jumped and started into a sprint. He hadn't even taken three steps before they put a bullet in his right calf. They never shoot to kill. They take prisoners. The same soldier or officer or whatever you want to call him that shot Burkenstock caught up to him as he was trying to limp away. A rifle butt to the back of the head put Burkenstock down and an unmarked white van drove up along side. Two men came out and Burkenstock was loaded into the back of the van. And he was gone. I watched it all from a window in the factory. They never even looked in my direction. I never saw him again. I don't know why they always take prisoners and never kill those that they pursue. Our best guess is that torture and interrogation are all that await once they have you. No one really knows though. No one has ever escaped. No one. For all we know they might bring people in their, give them a blow job and a meal, give them enough plastic surgery so they can't be recognized and set them free after swearing them to secrecy. I doubt it, though. If that were true, what would be the purpose of all the hiding, keeping the people ignorant, et cetera, et cetera? I don't know. I need a smoke. Yeah, it's a rough life. I'd never try to pretend that it wasn't. If you stay with me, kid, you might learn some shit that you find interesting. I might be willing to share my secrets with you. I might be willing to fill you in on how this world really works. I might tell you who's in charge and who's just pretending. Not yet, though. You aren't ready for it yet. You may not ever be ready, but someday, you will learn. I have to warn you, though, if you learn what I know, it will get you killed. Like I said, I'm the only one who can escape it, for whatever reason. You aren't like me, I can tell you that much right now. Sure, you may think you're hot shit, but you just wait...just wait.
