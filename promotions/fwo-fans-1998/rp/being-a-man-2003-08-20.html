Being a Man

20 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
My life has taught me a lot. I've gotten used to sitting here, all alone late at night in my hotel, sipping on ginger & rye. Yeah, it's a lonely life with aches and pains all over from wrestling matches, but I'm used to it. This is my life, all right. Clarisse calls. She asks me how I'm doing. I tell her I'm sore. I ask her how Matilda's doing. She says she misses her daddy. I really do miss the baby. She's the world to me. That's why I'm here. Without her I don't think I'd be back wrestling. I hit the ropes. Run over some bastard with a clothesline. This is my calling. I love this profession and I also love my daughter. In life you can't hide from what you are. I could do commercials. Maybe start acting on some sitcoms. Hah. I couldn't live doing anything else. A wrestler is what I am and I can't fool myself or anyone else. I stopped wrestling because I was tired of getting fucked around with. I remember my feud with Chris Titan. While it was the biggest nightmare of my life, it put right in the spotlight. If I hadn't quit right after, I could have been World Champion. Wouldn't that look nice? Here I am at the bottom again, waiting for something to get me noticed. Waiting for that big ticket scandal. It'll come eventually. People don't care about me as a wrestler. They're just waiting for that one moment when they can feel sorry for me. When they can think and believe that they can relate to my problems and issues. To them, I say: Don't try on shoes that don't fit you.
