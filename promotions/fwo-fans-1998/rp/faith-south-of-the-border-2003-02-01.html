Faith south of the border

1 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
His back was sore as he edged out of the bed in this four star hotel. If this was 4 star, Deacon would've hated to see what a 3 star looked like. The crowd in Mexico City was good, and the piece went off well, but Deacon couldn't help but wish he was in the United States again. He may not have been born there, but he certainly was happy to live there. He edged his feet into a pair of slippers, walking bare foot in this place would be like a deer running free through a wolf's den - you might not 'get it', but chances are, something is gonna surprise you sooner than later, something like a cockroach the size of a wolf. He walked to the door that separated his room from Chris' and knocked. Some shuffling, and the door was opened. Chris stood there, obviously more awake than Deacon. He had been awake for hours. "Sleep well?" Chris asked. The look on Deacon's face told him that answer. "I simply glad my bed not move in middle of night." Deacon said with a smirk. Chris smiled before moving back to a small table that he was using for a desk. On the table, a laptop rested, its monitor telling Deacon that Chris was once again writing. "Working on ot'er book?" "No. I just got in the habit of typing my prayers. Prayer has always been hard for me, so I thought that this might help me focus on Him." "Interesting," Deacon said. "I heading to breakfast. You going?" "Nah," Chris said. "I'm gonna hang in my room. Do some fasting." "Afraid of t'e food?" "You got me." Chris said after a snicker. "Was it really like this when we started in Puerto Rico with the CSWA?" "Yes. Worse, cause t'en we not have money to upgrade to better hotel. That hotel, my bed DID move." Deacon said followed by both men giving a chuckle. "You ready for tonight's match? He's a flyer, right? Lightweight division?" "Yes. Just like El Lobos." Deacon answered. "Now there's a name I hadn't heard in years." "T'at because after pinning me and losing Battle Royal I won, he left. Never even let me get my revenge." Deacon said with a smile. "You losing this one?" Chris asked. "Not sure. T'ey been building him up for weeks now. Depends on what they want to do wit' storyline. T'is program run for awhile." "If he doesn't leave?" "I spoke to him. He seem professional. I better go to breakfast. If I survive, t'en you can cheat on your impromptu fasting." Deacon said, flashing that wry grin.
