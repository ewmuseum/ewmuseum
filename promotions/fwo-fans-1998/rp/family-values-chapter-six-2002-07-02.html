Family Values - Chapter Six

2 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
November 22, 1994 Spencer, Nebraska 5:22 PM local time For an entire day now, Angela has been on the verge of a panic attack. She's been shaking, she's been unable to sleep. Anything she's eaten has come back up. The Man with the Dark Soul who haunts her dreams will be in front of her for the first time in three years. She's already retrieved the peace of mind she needs from her father's study, now all she has to do is survive. "Angela, honey! Time to go!" Her mother's voice echoed through the house. Time to go. To her Aunt Cheryl's place for Thanksgiving dinner. Aunt Cheryl is a good woman - always has a kind word for everyone. And she's the only member of the Davison/Price family to encourage Angela to sing in a band if that's what she wants. Angela headed out of her room and walked slowly to the front door - stopping short when she saw who was there. Uncle Mark. He was supposed to meet them at Cheryl's. "Hiya, Angela," said Mark, "Been a long time." "Mark is riding over with us," said her father, "You'll just have to squeeze together." Henry Davison had bought a sportscar earlier in the year - what Angela called his "Mid-Life Crisis" car. She thought it was funny how they could try to squeeze two people into the back seat. It was funny until she was squeezed in next to the source of her every nightmare. "Just twelve miles," she said to herself, "that's all it'll be." The trick... is to keep breathing.
