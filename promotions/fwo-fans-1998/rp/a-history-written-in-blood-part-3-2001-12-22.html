A History Written in Blood, part 3

22 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Eddy Fate spun the car into the parking lot of the Blue Spiral dance club. The cell phone call was quick, but effective. Tempest was planning to talk about Hong Kong on the air. He hadn't heard from Huang in years, but after a quick check, he found it to be true. Kristen Jacobs was meeting her here. "So, attacking me physically isn't enough for you, you vindictive little bird?" he muttered to himself. "Now you have to drudge up my past? We'll see about that..." He shut off the car, slammed the door, and headed into the club, looking for Tempest. After his eyes adjusted to the darkness and flashing colored lights, he stopped a waitress and said, "I'm looking for Tempest. Small bird, dark hair, sort of Chinese looking..." "Oh yeah," the waitress said, looking him up and down. "You're late. She's up in the first suite." She motioned up and looked mildly annoyed as Eddy rushed off. Then she shook her head and said, "Funny, I could have sworn she said they were sending a woman." Eddy knocked on the door once, and stepped in. He saw the camera, the microphone, Kristen... It was all true. In a fury he walked over to the camera. Kristen involuntarily winced, as she expected him to throw the camera like they all do, but instead he just stopped the camera and neatly ejected the tape, sliding it into his pocket. Tempest immediately got up and stormed over to him. "What the Hell do you think you're doing here!?" she growled at Fate. "You have no right to come and interrupt my interview." "And you have no bleeding right to talk about the things you have been talking about!" He nodded to Kristen. "Sorry, love, I hate to do this, but some of us are trying to put their pasts behind them..." He started to walk out, but Tempest grabbed his arm and spun him around, surprising him as she snapped, "Some of us have been trying to put our past behind us, but have had it rudely shoved in our face. I have every right to tell people about my own past. It's none of your business." She stood back a pace and said, "Unless of course you have some interest you haven't told anyone about..." She glared at him and hissed something in Cantonese while Jacobs looked between the two, confused. "You dare to call me..." Eddy snapped, and slugged Tempest, knocking Kristen out of the way. "You tell Papa that I don't buy this bullshit story, and to stay away from my family!" "Your family..." Tempest snapped as her eyes grew dark, and Kristen managed to step back as Tempest lunged for Eddy and knocked him back into the door. "Your people took away my family! Don't try and tell me you're not one of them. If you weren't, you would have nothing to fear." Eddy put his hand out to her hip, where her dress has slipped down. At first Kristen's face grew livid as a married man looked like he was fondling another woman, but she quickly realized that he was pointing out the tattoo. "That, my lovely, shows me exactly what I do fear." He slips his sunglasses back on. "Tell Papa that if he wants me to come get me himself, and not to send his skin girls after me." He turns and walks out. Tempest started to go after him, but stopped and slammed her fist into a nearby wall. "Dammit!" she screamed. Her eyes were touched with tears and Jacobs said, "I um... I still have the audio part. Don't worry. It'll be ok." Tempest looked at her and then at the door Eddy had exited through and said, "I'm not so sure Kristen. I'm really not."
