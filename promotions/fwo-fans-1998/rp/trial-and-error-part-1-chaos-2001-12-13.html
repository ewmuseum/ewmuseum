Trial and Error // Part.1 // Chaos

13 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"All rise to the honorable Judge Willis." And with that, the Court Room rose, as Judge Jason Willis entered. The long white haired, seemingly older Judge made his way to his seat, sat down, and sent the Bailiff into speech again, "You may be seated." The people obeyed, all but Kenny Rock and LLB. Rock looked over to his lawyer, who shook his head, assuring Rock that everything was under control. Shuffling some papers himself, LLB sat down as he brought both hands to his head, rubbed his temples, and then placed his (fake) reading glasses on the edge of his nose. Kenny Rock followed, looking around, coldly noticing the eyes of the courtroom watching him. Court was now in session, and it was time Steve Douglas got what was coming to him. His Verdict. LLB wouldn't have it any other way, and nor would Kenny Rock. "This case: Douglas versus Kenny Rock, in which Kenny Rock is suing for attempted murder," said the Judge, sitting back in his chair, stroking his chin, "Lawyers, if you could please make your opening statements." At that moment LLB smirked, knowing it was his time for action to be taken. He stood up with authority, took a quick glance over at Douglas, smirked again, and spoke, "Judge, and Jury, Steve Douglas is as guilty as they come. Just this past weekend, Douglas, a Professional Wrestler for the Fans Wrestling Organization, also know as the fWo, and in current competition with the Asylum, a Professional Fighting League, attacked Asylum Wrestler under contract, Kenny Rock, also the current holder of the Asylum Championship. Now, Douglas did not just attack Kenny Rock, he assaulted him, by grabbing the man's neck, and then telling him, "When you mess with people like Douglas, they get hurt"." LLB stopped for a moment, and looked down at Kenny Rock, who was rubbing his neck, giving a sympathetic look to the judge. Rock shifted in his chair, as his eyes went a little red, making it look like he might even cry. The Jury went in "aw" for a moment, as Douglas only gripped his fists tightly together, sinking a little further back into his seat. "Does that sound like a man who should walk out of this Room? Does that sound like a man who should have his freedom? But most importantly, does that sound like a innocent man at all?"
