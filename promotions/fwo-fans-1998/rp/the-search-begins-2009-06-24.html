The Search Begins...

24 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well this is a change of pace. This group of yahoos looks positively serene compared to how they usually look, although one member’s absence may be all the explanation needed. The Flying Frenchie, Brittany Chambers, and the official Underground (unpaid) intern Sebastian Abernathy are backstage for tonight’s ReAction. Mike Bear, however, is nowhere to be found. Frenchie and Brittany are just talking, still in their street clothes since the show is still hours away. Bas is busy talking on a phone and apparently doing research on a laptop, probably trying to line up a parade float or a live appearance by Loverboy for The Underground’s grand entrance at Meltdown. “So you don’t even want him at ringside, Pete?” “Non, zis is somet’ing Ze Frenchie must do alone. Saturday, Sunday, whenever zey let me fight zis putain, killjoy, I would fight him in ze parking lot if zey want. Tonight, we sign ze contract. By Monday, it is finished.” Brittany raised her eyebrows, a little shocked, mostly impressed. “Wow, Pete, I don’t see this side of you too often. You remind me more of my husband.” Frenchie cocked an eyebrow and smirked slightly. “Settle down there, tiger, you know what I meant.” Frenchie broke out the Beam. “But of course, mon cherie. If I even t’ought of zat, Jasmine would know. Plus, I have a little one on ze way.” “You remember that when you’re out there with that psycho. You want him to pay, but don’t forget the cost to you and those around you. I mean, I’ve always respected you, but you can’t take the punishment that Mike can.” “Zis is true, it is one of the t’ings I respect about him, ze mental discipline to...” And at that perfect moment, Mike Bear kicked the door open, surprising everyone in the room with the sudden noise. Brittany was shaken by the loud bang of the kick and the subsequent slam of the door into the wall and glared at Mike, visibly irritated. Frenchie merely looked up at his tag team partner, perhaps wondering where he had been since last appearing on FWO television on June 3. Bas screamed at the noise, losing control of his phone and juggling it for a few seconds before regaining control and apologizing profusely to the person on the other end. And then there was Mike, wearing blue jeans and a Cyberslam IX t-shirt, both torn to shreds. In his hands was the mangiest looking cat Brittany had ever seen. Third mangiest for Frenchie. Mike had a big grin on his face, completely oblivious. “I figured it out!” ... Silence. Mike waited for the obligatory question, but was met only with annoyed glares and the soft spoken conversation Bas was carrying on. “Aren’t you going to ask what I figured out?!” Brittany rubbed her eyes in frustration. “Mike, I...” “I thought you would never ask! You see, I was thinking, The Underground always had lots of champions in it, but we’ve been together, like, 3 months and we haven’t won anything! So I thought, what are we missing?” Mike looked at Frenchie and Brittany, expecting an answer yet again. They looked at each other and back towards Mike. Then at the sorry looking feline in his hands and back to Mike. Brittany sighed. “Proper teamwork?” Frenchie thought for a moment. “Ze plague?” Bas brought the phone directly in front of him. “WELL THEN PUT ME THROUGH TO YOUR SUPERVISOR!” Everyone turned to look at Bas, who was still breathing heavily from the sudden outburst. Realizing everyone was wordlessly staring at him, he seemed to shrink by the second. “Ummm, would anyone like a beer?” Bas offered, meekly. Mike shook his head. “No, it’s a bit early for me, maybe in an hour.” Bas checked his watch to confirm his suspicions that it was, indeed, 10 in the morning. “Anyway, like I was saying, the one thing we’re missing is the Manager of Champions!” Brittany and Frenchie stared at Mike in shock. “Mike, honey, surely you don’t mean...” “That’s right, Lamont! And don’t call me Shirley.” Frenchie was incredulous. “You don’t mean to tell me zat zis is ze famed Hardcore Feline? He would have to be 15 years old!” “No no, this isn’t Lamont. I couldn’t get in touch with Tom to him the whereabouts of the original, so I figured, how hard could it be to find a new one!” Mike uttered the last part as a statement and not the ridiculous question it should have been. Frenchie struggled for words. “Mike, you realize zat Lamont was a managerial genius who could perform a double moonsault, non?” Mike huffed and looked condescendingly at Frenchie. “Well duh, Pete, I was there. He taught me the Lamontsault.” Frenchie suppressed his irritation that he was being treated as the foolish one. “Oui, so you’ve said many times. Here’s ze t’ing. A cat with all zose qualities would be one in a billion, if zat. Ze idea zat you could find a cat like zat in... where exactly did you get zis poor creature?” Mike looked defensive, his eyes darting back and forth. “...the pound?” Frenchie and Brittany glared at Mike. “OK, I found him outside the arena eating garbage. But c’mon! Look at him! He’s the Lamont of the new millennium!” Brittany and Frenchie stared at each other. “Since Lamont was managing in 2000 and 2001 at least,” Brittany whispered under her breath, “ doesn’t that make Lamont the Lamont of the new millennium?” “I find it best not to ask zose types of questions,” Frenchie whispered back. Mike continued on. “Seriously, he’s got that edge that the kids are looking for these days!” “Mike,” said Brittany tried to interrupt, “his hair is falling out in clumps.” “He’s from the wrong side of the tracks, but he’s gonna make it big under the big spotlight, just like Tony Manero in Staying Alive!” ... And this time it really was silent. Even Bas had turned to look at Mike, joining Frenchie and Brittany in utter shock. Mike’s smile slipped a little. “What?” Frenchie was practically a blank slate. “You... have seen Staying Alive?” Mike took a step back. “No! No... of course not... I saw a... description! Yeah, on... I Love the 80s!” Bas lost his composure and let a snort of laughter escape. “You watch VH-1?” Mike turned to Bas with fire in his eyes. “FISH, IF I DON’T HAVE A BEER IN MY HAND IN 5 SECONDS YOU’RE GETTING THE 3S!” “No please, Mr. Bear! Not the Super Schlitz Stand!” “3... 4...” With practiced speed, Bas retrieved a Heineken tall boy from the cooler he had with him at all times and went to give it to his employer/tormenter, but unfortunately he tripped over his chair and sent the beer flying. The bottle ended up bouncing off the skull of the poor cat in Mike’s hands, though he showed no reaction. Mike tucked the cat under his left arm and snatched the flying bottle out of the air in one deft move. Frenchie and Brittany could only watch as the scene practically played out in slow-motion. He retrieved a bottle opener out of his pocket, opened the beer, and took a swig, finally pointing the bottle towards Bas. “You got lucky today, Fish.” Mike turned back to his confused teammates and took another swig from the bottle. Bas rubbed his knee and went back to what he was doing. “So, what do you think about giving Lamont II here a tryout?” Frenchie finally regained his bearings in the conversation. “Well, Mike, as compelling as an argument zat may be, zis feline...” “Lamont II.” “I don’t t’ink so. Zis feline cannot be our manager. Ze real Lamont definitely would have dodged zat bottle, or at least felt ze effects of it striking him on ze head. We already have someone who can be hit on ze head with a bottle and not feel ze effects. Zat’s your job.” Mike frowned. “Aw man!” He let the cat down, who proceeded to yawn and take a nap. “And he can even do a dead on Courage impression!” Mike sulked . Frenchie looked at the cat intently. “Perhaps you are right, mon ami.” Brittany observed the cat as well. “Yeah, it’s pretty dead on.” Frenchie shook his head. “Non, I mean about Lamont. Perhaps we can find anot’er Lamont to lead ze new Underground to greatness.” “Didn’t you just tell him that it was a billion to one shot?” “Oui, although wouldn’t you say it was equally as unlikely zat Hellrazor found Lamont in ze first place? I believe I’ve heard your husband say, ‘Better to be lucky zan good.’ I can t’ink of few people zat applies to more zan him. If anyone could find ze next Lamont...” They looked over at Mike, who was drinking a beer at a little after 10 in the morning and poking a mangy, feral, sleeping cat with his finger to see if it was still alive. “...it would be Mike Bear. Unbelievably enough.” Brittany rolled her eyes a little, but still nodded in agreement. Suddenly she remembered something. “Mike, hon, I assume you’ve been doing preparations for Meltdown in addition to looking for replacement Lamonts, right?” Mike looked up at Brittany, puzzled. “Meltdown? We’ve got plenty of time for that. That’s, what, like a month away?” “No, it’s more like 4 days.” “This weekend, huh? Who’s my partner, again?” Brittany smacked her own forehead in frustration. “Max Danger! I told you a million times when they did the drawing! I’ve got a dozen messages from his manager Leigh Landers, frantically trying to track you down!” “Well I definitely didn’t get any messages.” “THAT’S BECAUSE YOU SPENT THREE WEEKS CHASING CATS IN THE STREET!” “Oh well, what can you do?” Brittany sighed deeply. “Bas, could you get me a beer?” He got a beer out of the cooler and decided to just toss it over this time. Brittany caught it without an issue and had her first beer of the day. Mike was completely unfazed. “So that leaves tonight, at least. I’m sure we can get a match together to work on the team dynamic.” It was Frenchie’s turn this time. “Monsieur Danger is already booked tonight against Dave Morey.” “Well, I’m sure I’ll think of something.” Those words didn’t sit well with Frenchie, who hoped his tag team partner wouldn’t inflict any damage on his Meltdown partner. Perhaps he should... no, Mike wouldn’t do anything like that, would he? The more he thought about it, the more he came to one conclusion. He needed a beer too. The Flying Frenchie decided not to risk it and got his own beer, checking on Bas to make sure the merchandise was being made correctly and the preparations were going according to plan. They had something big planned, but Meltdown came first.
