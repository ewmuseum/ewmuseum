Knowing my Enemies.

14 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Knowing my Enemies. January 14, 2000 ::The scene opens up in front of the Delaware River. The river's edge is fenced in since there is a park in the background. Along this fence stands a man, a statue of a man, Rain. He stands with his upper frame supported by the steel wired fence, and his hand on his head. As the camera gets nearer we see Rain is donning a large black trench coat, as well as traditional black sunglasses. Rain spots the camera and turns.:: Rain: Flash, where is Geoff ? Flash Finelli: He caught the next flight down here, I got here a little early. Rain: I was looking to see Geoff, not you. But you will have to do Flash, even cameramen need to advance their careers. Flash: Something like that. I just want to get a few words about your big matches, Im sure you've heard. Rain: I'm sure I have as well. ::Rain gathers his thoughts for a moment and stares out at the river, the moonlit sky bounces off the river and Rain seems to be transfixed by it. Rain waits a moment before he speaks.:: Rain: The river.... a powerful mass of water. Has many secrets trapped along it's bottom, waiting to be let loose. Never shall these secrets be told. All bodies of water have a histroy, even I. On Wednesday, I step into the ring with the Flying Frenchie... for the secong time. The first time I stepped foot into the ring with you, the time was confusing, and you yourself know the win was not earned. That was during Wrestler of the Year tournament. I have proved time again that I am a worthy fighter, but I just don't seem to earn the respect. Not respecting the power of the Storm is dangerous. I have defeated the greatest in the sport, and I have held the cherished Internet title for longer than any man ever has. Luck ? I should say not. My last few defenses have ended in the same way, I received victory but then... then the match was disgraced by first Primetime, then Scott Slugger, Perfect, whatever name suits you until you find someone else to strive to be. Interrupting my match was a mistake. Courage will learn his lesson soon. For the time is not right for your defeat, the Storm tells me so, and the storm is wise. Primetime, could not take a superior performance that I unleashed and made an excuse for his loss. No excuse is needed Primetime... you have no known knowledge of the Storm. Ice, my third time fighting you, the first match did not end and the last two have proclaimed me victorious, the God's are bored with your inferiority. Last year I proved myself as not only a powerful force in the fWo, but as a fighting force, as I defeated my nemesis Crucifix in the first round of the tournament, then with the odds stacked against me, I defeated Mike Bear. Not odds created by me, but by the foolish viewer who once again underestimated me. Then I faced The Frenchie. I lost. Wednesday will not perceive the same outcome. During a reign of terror headed by me, I had you lose the World Title and banished from the country, I can't say I am ashamed of this, because I did everyone a favour, and almost gained the World title in the process. Now you are trying to stake your revenge ? I will not lose my title. This is your first match in front of your Frenchmen, and Im sure I will not be welcomed well. Nor will the french be happy when I pin, you and sacrifice you to the God's. Flash Finelli: At Invasion 00 you face Eron. What are your thoughts on this ? Rain: Do you see the lightning in the clouds over there ? ::Rain gestures to the sky where concentrated in one spot it appears a fearice storm is overhead.:: Rain: Currently Eron is riding on that plane, I just hope he get's the message and doesn't make an excuse for the storm. Flash Finelli: You know that Geoff's on that flight don't you ? Rain: Yes, of course I do. I have watched Eron since I was conceived in the fWo, I do not look upon him kindly and neither do the God's of the Storm. Invasion will mark the second time a man has carried two coveted titles at once, the first who did it was the Frenchie, the second man will be I. Rain. The International title will come to me and the Gods will be happy. I will speak more on you Eron, after I defeat the Frenchie. Remember....When it RAINS..... it POURS.....
