Chaos- Beginning

17 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In the beginning, there was life. With life came the eternal forces of "good" and "evil". As long as one continues to exist, so must the other. They are co-dependant, for without good, there can be no evil. Without evil, there can be no good. But sometimes, the good loses its way, and begins to mirror that of the evil. Sometimes, the only force that can save us.....is the one that's trying to damn us. Eternity is around us at all times. There can be no life, no existence, without Eternity. Directly opposing Eternity is the force that wishes to extinguish life wherever it sees it, the force that corrupts the good and strengthens the wicked. This force.....is Chaos. Chaos may seem to disappear, it may seem to be out of sight, but in reality it's always here. It's always behind a shroud of blackness, secretly pulling the strings of those who follow it. Some of them may follow it without even realizing just what it is they are following, but always they will remain loyal. Because, as long as Chaos exists, so will exist those devoted to its cause. The purpose Chaos is to kill. The purpose of the Avenging Angels is to kill the Fans Wrestling Organization. Confused yet? We're just getting started. Rei struggled to fill her lungs with precious oxygen, as she laid face-down in the mud. The rain poured down on her from high above, soaking her, and soon she felt the intense pressure of a heeled boot on the back of her head. "You were nothing before me." Cara sneered as she pressed down harder with her boot. "I'm the one who brought you out of that crack alley, and this is how you repay me? Fool. You killed your own parents, you tried to kill your own brother, and who did you do it all for? Me. And you did it with a smile on your face." Cara twisted her boot on the back of Rei's head, and Rei spit out the dirt in a long gasp. "Does the loser of my game have something she wants to say?" Cara said, a cocky smile spreading across her face. "Well, by all means." She removed her boot from the back of Rei's head, allowing her to raise her head, and Rei watched as Cara pushed the boot back into the mud in front of her. An expression of pure rage was evident on her face as more and more revelations of what she had done began to fill Rei's mind. "We don't serve you anymore." Rei spat. "You're our enemy, and you're going to be our enemy until the day we perish. And someday, Cara.....someday, we'll kill you. It might not be my hands, but one of us will kill you. Mark my words." To her horror, Cara only laughed, before speaking her last words. "You will always serve me." Her laughter filled the night air as she turned and walked away, never to be seen by Rei's eyes again. Cara turned to her masked second. She looked up at his imposing frame as she spoke. "There's no need to end it yet." she said. "Let them struggle on further for my cause. It will be time to reveal myself soon enough. And when it is time.....I want them to be the first to know. That everything they've ever believed, everything since that fateful day....is a lie. That they only person they've been serving......is me....." Cara's face twisted into a confident sneer. ".....Chaos."
