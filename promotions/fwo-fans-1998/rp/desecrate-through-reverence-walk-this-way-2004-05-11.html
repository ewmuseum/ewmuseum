Desecrate Through Reverence: Walk This Way

11 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
His hair was a little shorter than she'd remembered, and the stubble was kempt - much more than his usual 5 o'clock shadow. He'd probably look good with a beard, though she'd always asked him to shave. He'd have looked like some trashy redneck wrestler otherwise. Those turquoise eyes shone. They used to be so lost - if you looked into them, there was nothing more than the moment. All of a sudden, though, Aimz was glancing into a purpose. He had a reason to be here. He knew what he was doing. She didn't. Why was she even looking at at him? Why had she gone to all of this trouble? Before this meeting Amy had been sitting entirely content in a hotel room, dressed in simple jeans and a t-shirt. She'd reached over to check the list of Survivor participants who she'd be meeting with an hour later...and there he'd been. It had never even crossed her mind. Not even after their encounter at the arena...she still wasn't sure if that wasn't just a daydream. Suddenly, everything changed. She called the Disney reps, faking her best "I've got a cold" voice. Nobody bought it, she had to be here. She had to face this. So she did...disguised. This is when Amy Campbell, now Roche, sat across and a few seats down from Reno Davis. She had on glasses that hadn't seen the light of day in years, and didn't much care to look past their scratches as long as nobody else noticed. Her hair was pulled tight into a bun, and she wore dress pants with a fully-buttoned black blouse. A single chunk of hair left loose covered the Kanji tattoo on her neck well enough, and she could only pray that the dark-rimmed glasses took some attention from her mismatched eye color. She was trying her hardest not to even act like herself, that's the only way she'd get away without his attention. And she hadn't caught him so much as glancing at her. It was just like Reno to ignore everything else when he was determined, and that had Amy grinning smugly to herself as the guest wrestling speaker wrapped up his speech and dismissed them as if he had some ounce of power. Davis left the door nearest to him, and Aimz decidedly hung around the room for a moment. A solid five minutes had passed. She'd exchanged pleasantries with an executive she never particularly got along with, which he'd found just as unusual as her wardrobe that day...but he wasn't the one who needed to buy it. Papers had been ruffled around in a fake attempt to look busy, and then Amy was out the door in a flurried sprint to the nearest elevator. This would all work out, she was nearly there. Just a hop into the box and she was off to her room and wouldn't have to see that face again. The elevator couldn't open fast enough! Amy persisted, nearly punching the opening button in frustration. In that struggle with technology, she encountered the only man who apparently found her choice of dress attractive... "What's the hurry, sweetheart?" she could hear a smile in his tone. But whoever this was, they had one hell of a cold. She didn't dare turn around, as the raspiness in what sounded like a cold would have more than likely, considering Amy's recent luck, just been the voice of a dirty old man. "C'mon. Why's such a pretty girl so rushed to hide herself in that elevator?" Amy pounded the button again, this time rolling her eyes and heaving a sigh. Reno Davis was in the building, this fucking door wouldn't open and some mongrel was standing behind her, probably staring at things he had absolutely no right to even dream. "I've..." she began, flustered and hurried, "I've got a meeting in a few minutes, and I've gotta be ready. Sorry, I can't stop to ta--" The man rang out again in a tone was almost recognizable, and sent an instinctual shiver throughout the redhead. "You're rushing to an elevator because you've gotta prepare for a meeting, yet you're already dressed to the nines? There's something about this that I don't buy, honestly..." His voice very suddenly cleared. "Oh yeah..." Reno Davis laughed. "You're terrified of elevators." [BACK to "The End"] or [FAST FORWARD to "Backslide"]
