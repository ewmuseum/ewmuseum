Machina gets some viewer advice (part two)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::The scene opened on Machina, once again sitting on an oversized comphy couch in front of a fire. He looked frustrated, and stared just off camera with a scowl on his face.:: I said a DUST of ginger! Dammit man, I can't drink this Cappuchino! Sprinkle! Sprinkle! ::Machina noticed that the camera was on, and the patch of face under his mask turned a bright shade of red.:: Uh... I mean, anything is fine, damn I'm a man, I'll drink anything. In fact, bring me a beer. A manly beer. Yeah. ::Machina laughed nervously, and turned back to the camera.:: Well guys, the response to my request for help has been huge, and now it's time to read some of your mail-in suggestions on the air. Shall we get started? What's that? ::Machina looked confused as the off-camera director motioned something to him that he didn't understand. Suddenly a sign flew across the room and struck Machina in the head.:: Ow! Dammit, what did you... oh yeah... the sign... ::Machina lifted the sign, now bent, which read...:: HELP SAV MACHINEA'S LOVE LIF. WRITE HIM LETTER WITH IDEAZ ::Machina left it up for a couple of seconds, and then tossed it behind him.:: Okay, well let's get going then, okay? The first letter comes from a Mr. Britian. Okay, cool. He writes: -------------------------------------------------------------- You should read her a poem, Penny, Penny, how sweat you are. I I had learnt music I would have played the guitar. If I could drive, I would have a car. And if I had breasts, I would wear a bra. What I say means absolute crap, If I treat you bad, give me a slap! Whatever you do I wont be hurt, Even if you make me change my name to Burt!! Read that to her and she will feel so sorry for you that she will date ya!! Also, try giving up the fWo and letting Mr Britain join! Mr Britain! -------------------------------------------------------------- Hm. Well, I always thought that Burt guy was a little funny. Mean, he slept with Ernie for gosh sakes. Puppets in bed together? That just isn't right. And Mr. Britian, I'll put in a good word for you in the front office, promise. I... hey wait a minute! You're that Britian guy who always won the matches in the fWo aren't you? Whatever happened to that guy anyway... seemed to be always bragging about winning that then it's like he just disappeared or something. Huh. Well, our next letter comes from a Mrs. Kelly Park, and she writes: -------------------------------------------------------------- Dear baby, I think you are so sexy. You make me so hot I'd like to sing to you, cook you a nice meal, lay you down and RIDE YOU LIKE A WILD HOT ANIMAL OF LOVE! Baby feel my HOT BURNING MACHINE! XXX Kelly -------------------------------------------------------------- Er... ::Machina looked flushed as he read over the letter a second time to make sure he got it correctly.:: Holy crap! ::Machina folded up the mail and tucked it into his jeans pocket, still blushing.:: Wow, well, let's see what else the mailbag has in store for Machina. Whew! Uh, this next letter comes to us from a Mr. OMD. Man, these internet names are sure strange. Almost nobody has the same letter starting their first and last name. That just seems so strange. Anyway, OMD writes: -------------------------------------------------------------- Having gril problems. This is Daddy makk or OMD. This has never worked for me but at least you can try. Why don't you start to brag about all your carrer higlights in the fWo. That should win over the anit spy's heart. OMD -------------------------------------------------------------- Well, I have had a lot of career highlights. Many people have said that I'm the most popular guy in the history of the promotion. Who am I to disagree with them? But what's the deal with this spy thing? I'm sure she is just trying to help someone. She's too pretty to be really evil. Have you ever met a woman who was pretty and used her good looks for evil? No, I didn't think so. That's the thing about women. They are all smart and nice. Always open and never deceptive. Yeah, that's it. Okay, well the next letter comes from an [email removed], and he writes: -------------------------------------------------------------- Everyone who left any kinda of a comment on my board from the fWo was turned in to the Free servers for harrasement and I had members back it up you will be hearing from them . -------------------------------------------------------------- Um, okay. Who is this clown anyway? ::Machina dug through the mailbag, looking through the letters inside.:: No... no letters from this "Freeservers" that you speak of. Hey fans out there... I appreciate the advice, but you can't drink and post, okay? Anyway, the next comes from a "Baer", and Mr. Baer writes: -------------------------------------------------------------- Tel hir to braek it dwon n SUCK IT an take hir to the SAMCKDWON Hotel nd mak hre kwno her role!!! U RULZ MAHCINA!!! DX316RULZ!!! -------------------------------------------------------------- Hey, I've been to that hotel! It's pretty nice, but I can't afford it since I spent all that money on that unsuccessful presidental run. Great advice, thanks. This seems like the best advice I've gotten so far today! Thanks Mr Baer, and you're right... DX316RULZ does rule. Ever since that liver transplant... well, he's been really turning up the charm. Well folks, that's about all the time I have for today... but join me tomorrow when I open some more reader mail and try to get some decent coffee in this place! DAMMIT I JUST WANT A DUSTING OF... er... beer. Yeah, beer! I'm a real man, I promise! Here, you want me to prove it? Watch this... ::Machina started to unzip his pants at the camera as the picture quickly faded to merciful black.::
