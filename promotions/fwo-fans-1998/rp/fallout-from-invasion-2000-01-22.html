Fallout from Invasion!

22 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Fallout from Invasion! January 22, 2000 ::Scene opens backstage after Invasion! Geoff Green is sitting, reading some papers, when he hears some arguing. He picks up his camera and beings to eavesdrop on the conversation. We see it's the Black Panther and Havok. Panther is heavily limping after his match with the Frenchie:: BP: Where the hell were you tonight? Havok: What do you mean, where was I? Your match was clean, I was the one who was screwed over, where were you? BP: I was getting ready for my match! Why should I have concerned myself with your match? Havok: We're partners! BP: Yeah, but your match was just for the Cruiserweight Title, nobody cares about that! Mine was for the Heavyweight Belt! The whole shebang! That's the title people care about! Havok: Hey man, the Cruiserweight Title is important to me! BP: Only to you and those other two idiots you were fighting with! When was the last time you saw a Cruiserweight Title match headline a Pay Per View? Havok: What the hell has gotten into to you? BP: I'm the savior of the fWo, remember? I have a right to be angry, I just got screwed out of my title! Havok: Screwed? Frenchie pinned you cleanly! BP: What are you saying, you're joining the French Foundation? Havok: No! You're putting words in my mouth! BP: It's those damn fans! I'm the savior, they should be cheering me! I don't care if the Frenchie's from France! If it wasn't for me, there'd be no fWo! Havok: You need to settle down, man. I know you're angry about losing your title. You can win it back. All you have to do is... ::Their voices trail off as they leave the arena and the screen fades to black::
