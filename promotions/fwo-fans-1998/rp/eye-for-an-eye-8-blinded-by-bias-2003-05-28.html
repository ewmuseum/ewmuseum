Eye For An Eye [8] Blinded By Bias

28 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I was just a boy doing the job of a man. Living life through my father's rose tinted glasses. In his eyes I was the prodigal son but in the eyes of the bias free I was a waste of space. Their shoes of expectation were a pair far bigger than I could possibly fill at the time. The life of my father was like a dangerous game of chess and those around him would only ever play the roles of pawns, pieces that could be sacrificed for his greater good. When he came and took my life away I thought my sacrifice had been made. But I was wrong. August 7th 1993. "No... I can't do this Dad, there's just no way." Kellen Kinkade pleaded with his father as he paced back and forth shaking his head with dismay. He'd just come straight off the back of another less than impressive match, spots were blown and a quick finish was once again required because of his mistakes. So when he found himself sitting in the office of his father at the end of the night, the news that arrived was at the other end of the spectrum in terms of what he'd expected and anticipated. "Boy... you need to listen to me and listen good, I run this promotion and like it or not my decisions are final. You will be facing Pierre LeSmith at the end of this month and you will be defeating him for the heavyweight championship." Vince Kinkade shot back at him, drumming his fist down on the desk furiously. Kinkade sighed, after a working a match like he'd just done many budding wrestlers would be picking up their pink slip and told where they could find the door. Instead he'd just been told than in less than a month he would be the world champion. "Dad..." Kellen began only to be cut off. "Vince boy, call me Vince... when you walk through that door I'm your boss. Not your father." Vince replied with a scowl. "See now that's just it boss, if only it were that way... if you were my boss then the simple fact is I wouldn't work here anymore. You looking at me as a son and not an employee is the only problem I have right now, there are levels that determine who is and isn't championship material and I'm nowhere near at that level yet." Promising silence filled the air... it wasn't often that Vince Kinkade had nothing to say but perhaps Kellen had made him see the light. Maybe now he'd be treated like an active roster member, free of bias and politics he could take a rightful place in the low card matches and learn the ropes. "You don't like it? The door is right there boy, run back home to your mommy... there's plenty of shit jobs waiting for you here in New Jersey." Vince snarled back at his son, completely ignoring the words he'd said. It was at that very point, which Kellen Kinkade realized that nothing he could say or do would even change his father's mind. So he turned and he walked toward the door. "McDonalds." His father said with a devious smile. Kellen slowed his pace but carried on moving. "Burger King." Vince said again, each word like a driven nail that would prevent him from leaving the room. Kellen swallowed hard and put his hand on the door handle. "Taco Bell?" Vince added sarcastically, having driven the final nail into the proverbial board across the door... he watched as his son relinquished his grip on the handle and made his way back across the room to sit before him. "I just want you to know, you're making a big mistake here... I'm not ready for this and you know it. I don't know how many people are going to have to quit out of protest, how many people you're going to have to fire because they refuse to work with me or how many tickets you'll fail to sell that it's going to take before you realize it, but you'll regret this dad... you'll regret it." Kinkade looked his father dead in the eye... searching in hope of a word that'd landed and given his vision clarity. "You finished?" Vince replied bluntly. "Yes, I'm finished." Kellen replied. "Good... now get out of my sight and get some rest, busy times ahead for you champ." Kellen Kinkade left the office a changed man, this would be the last time he would try to reason with his father. Ever. August 12th 1993. "Pierre! Do come in." Vince Kinkade spoke up after being informed that the WWC heavyweight champion was waiting outside his door. He sat back with a vacant expression upon as his face as the circus of events that unfolded before him occurred, the door burst open and through it rolled a small midget clown holding a trumpet. A few blasts later the clown folded his arms. "Ladies and gents, boys and girls and children of all ages... Pepe the clown proudly brings to you the heavyweight champion of zeeeeee world; PIERRE LESMITH!" The clown exclaimed in a heavy cockney accent. As the small clown finished, Pierre LeSmith burst through the door with the title belt slung over his shoulder and a beret on his head. Clad in an entirely red, white and blue striped outfit the champion stroked his beard and looked around the room. "Please, please... hold ze applause." He remarked in a cheesy French accent. "Have a seat." Vince said with an eyebrow raised and a serious tone in his voice "And make it quick please... I don't have time for this stuff, you can drop the accent as well." "Ze accent? Ze accent is Pierre's birthright mon ami!" Pierre said, sitting in the chair and placing the belt on the desk before Vince. Pepe quickly rushed around and buffed the title with a rag. "He's roight you know guv'nor... eats crowsaunts and all that bollocks." Pepe piped up. "Right." Vince said with a sigh "Well Pierre, I thought I'd better call you in so that we can talk about your title match at the end of this month." "Oui." Pierre replied with a nod "Which of ze many jobbers 'ave you got in store for ze champeen zis time?" "Kellen Kinkade." Vince answered. "Aha! Ze... Kareer Killer non? I 'ave 'erd of zis buffoon before!" Pierre replied as Vince's expression slowly turned to a scowl. "Guv!" Pepe squealed, desperately trying to catch Pierre's attention. "Shh Pepe." Pierre replied. "But Guv!" Pete squealed again. SMACK. Pierre silenced him with a quick backhand to the head. "I said shh Pepe! Rude and insolent tiny scummer!" Pierre said furiously "Now where were we mon ami... ah yes, ze Kareer Killer... ohohoho." "Yep." Vince replied leaning forth right in Pierre's face "Or you can just call him my son." Pierre gulped. Pepe gulped. Vince sat back in his seat. "But I don't care what you or the boys out there think of him because the fact of the matter is... you all do what I tell you, which is why I called you here today to tell you that you'll be dropping the title to him at the end of the month." Pierre sat stunned. Pepe stopped buffing. Vince smirked. "That'll be all." Vince added as Pierre sat completely still with his mouth agape, without saying a word he slowly slid out of his chair and snatched up his belt before quietly making his way out of the room with his midget clown trailing behind him. As they reached the door Pierre looked down at his loyal companion, before giving him a second smack across the head. "Zis is all your fault Pepe." Vince stood up from his seat as Pierre slipped through the door so that he could make one last remark. "Oh... and Pierre." "Oui?" Pierre replied as he turned back to face the WWC owner. "Better make my boy look good..." He said with a devious grin. "... because your job is on the line." Pierre gulped again. August 31st 1993. The night had arrived. The WWC Legends event had been a roaring success... exciting matches had filled the low card and several heated feuds had come to climax in the midcard and high card matches. The crowd were totally absorbed and the new style of hardcore match that'd taken place had them on their feet applauding, despite Vince Kinkade's dislike for the contest but knew it'd forge a good competition with Philadelphia based promotion ECW. All that remained now was a stunning main event to climax the night. "Lola" by The Kinks crackled up on the speaker system and the WWC heavyweight champion entered with his midget companion to the sound of intense booing, Pierre LeSmith and Pepe made their way toward the ring. As the Frenchman stepped through the ropes that Pepe held open for him, he moved to the middle of the ring and raised the title belt in the air. Following a minute or two of showboating and enraging the crowd further it was time for the face to come in and garner their support. "All's Cool" by Mucky Pup hit up on the speakers and the crowd let up a small cheer. But it wasn't so much for the fact that Kellen Kinkade was about to make an entrance, more that Mucky Pup were a popular underground group on the New Jersey scene and many in attendance recognized the track. Kinkade paced nervously down the corridor toward the curtain but was stopped by the man with whom he'd fought against and blown many spots not more than a month ago. "Like the new music kid, your choice?" Christian Reinhard asked, as he caught Kellen by the arm and stopped him in his tracks. "Nah." Kinkade replied "I'm more of a Soundgarden type of guy, one of the boys said these guys are local and that it'd get a good reaction." "Correct." Reinhard said with a nod "Could've picked a song more suited to your character though... Mucky Pup have plenty. A boy in a man's world, Homosexual, U.R. Nothing... hey, how about Daddy's boy?" "Very funny." Kellen growled as he swatted a satisfied Reinhard's hand away and continued down the hall... it wasn't a conversation that an already low on confidence Kinkade needed. Kellen took a deep breath and parted the curtain and as he did he noticed something; the crowd reaction didn't change, it simply stayed the same. As he made his way down the aisle he extended his hands for high fives but barely anyone in the ringside area was reaching out for one. He rolled under the ropes and raised his arms in the air to a mild amount of cheers before the bell finally rang to signal the start of the match. Pierre LeSmith slowly strode toward Kellen, who extended his hand for a shake. With beads of sweat dripping down his forehead the champion replied with a firm smack across the face of Kinkade. And it was at that point which Kinkade knew his father had been in Pierre's ear. "Merde!" Pierre exclaimed after hitting Kinkade with the hard shot to the face "Excuses moi scummer... sil vous plait forgive me, I did not mean to 'it you zat 'ard." There wasn't much booing in reaction to Pierre's shot to the face of the supposed fan favourite, just as there weren't many cheers as he retaliated with several hard rights and lefts that sent the Frenchman bowling backwards into the corner of the ring. Pierre ducked under the ropes, a classical heel move which had the crowd berating him from their ringside seats... they did rise to their feet with anticipation as Kinkade went for a tope suicida from the inside of the ring to the outside but sat back in their seats with disappointment as he snagged a foot on the ropes and well short of the mark, hitting the arena floor with a thud. A few seconds of delay passed as Pierre looked on concerned as to whether or not Kinkade would get up at all... a quick attempt at time wasting with a verbal confrontation between the champion and a fan failed miserably. "I am ze champion scummer, show me ze respect I deserve!" Pierre barked at one of the front row audience. "Dude... is he out cold?" The fan replied. "Bah... ze condition of my opponent is of no importance!" LeSmith replied, turning back to Kinkade who thankfully was making his way back to a vertical base. Pierre took a sigh of relief before rolling Kinkade back under the ropes and climbing to the canvas, before slowly making his way up to the top rope and saluting the fans... an act which once again had them barking abuse in his direction. He leapt from the top and connected across the throat of the challenger with a crisp legdrop, before hooking the leg and counting out the numbers with his fingers. 1! 2! No... Kinkade kicked out firmly as LeSmith shook his head with dismay before getting back onto his feet and drilling the downed Kellen Kinkade with a few less than stiff looking kicks, after picking him up and executing a very slow and timid looking snap suplex it was becoming quickly apparent that he was trying far too hard to protect the man who just so happened to be the son of the boss. This was further confirmed when LeSmith lifted Kinkade to his feet, kicked him in the gut and hit a soft powerbomb which merely saw Pierre lift up Kinkade and place him back down on the canvas almost apologetically. The fans were quickly catching on. "Bullshit! Bullshit!" Echoed through the small arena as Pierre continued to look panicked... desperately searching for ways to get the crowd rallying against him he promptly ran at Kinkade, who caught him with a drop toe hold and sent him crashing into the match official, knocking him down and out and giving both men a chance to get the crowd back into the match. LeSmith and Kinkade exchanged some more blows before the sneaky French champion took advantage with a blow below the belt. Pierre rolled under the ropes as a man finally being booed again for the right reasons, but snapping up a steel chair proved to be one of the biggest mistakes of his career. Pierre made his way back into the ring and drew back... measuring up Kinkade as he rose to his feet before cannoning him across the head with a wicked steel chair shot... ... one that brought forth one of the biggest pops of the night. "NON!" Pierre roared to himself with despair as Kinkade hit the floor a bloody mess, not only had he busted open Kinkade with an overly stiff chair shot. He'd now got the crowd cheering his actions. More sweat beads rolled down Pierre's face as he threw the chair to the outside and brought the official around, making a swift cover on Kinkade. 1! 2! "Bullshit! Bullshit!" Pierre had brained Kinkade so badly with the chair the young wrestler barely knew where he was... leaving Pierre to almost kickout of the pin for him, the crowd weren't buying it for one second. Kinkade staggered onto his feet dazed and confused, he could just about remember the finish of the match and both he and his opponent were ready to go home... Pierre ran at him but he caught him with a spinning heel kick across the ribs, which for once was completed without error. The challenger scaled the ropes and for once had the crowd cheering for him, but all of his was about to change. Still dazed he leapt from the top rope with a moonsault to the standing Pierre, but completely overshot it and barely clipped his head before landing on the canvas with a thud. Pierre went down like he'd been shot. The crowd booed insanely. And Kinkade crawled over to him and draped an arm across his chest for the pin. 1! 2! 3! And nothing. A new WWC heavyweight champion of the world had just been crowned and the crowd did nothing, they didn't cheer, they didn't boo, they just sat there. There weren't many celebrations as people got out of their seat and made their way out of the arena, Pierre hurried to the back of the ring but was met by a vice like grip around his throat. "Get your bags and get the fuck out of my sight you piece of shit, you'll never wrestle for me again." Vince Kinkade snarled at him "Almost thirty years and we've never had a steel chair used in a world title match in this promotion... you're a disgrace to this company and you're a disgrace to wrestling, you fucked that match up royally and now you're going to pay for it." Vince released his grip as Pierre hurried away like a scalded ring. Back in the ring Kinkade got to his feet with the title in his clutches as his father looked on from the back proudly... still blinded by the light of bias he ignored the fact that his boy was failing miserably. In wrestling you're always told that any reaction is a good reaction but for Kellen Kinkade... ...there was no reaction at all.
