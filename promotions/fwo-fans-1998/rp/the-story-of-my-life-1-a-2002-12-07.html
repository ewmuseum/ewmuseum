The Story Of My Life.1.A

7 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It all began on September 23rd, 1978. A small baby boy born a month premature was brought into the world with a loving mother, and a loving father. Of course the young child's mother couldn't kick her smoking habit causing the baby to be born with under developed lungs. That though was deemed to be no problem as the child was put into an incubator. Outside of that incubator two people watched intently at all hours of the day. Doctors occasionally walked in to check on the young child, who had slowly began breathing on his own. Finally on October 3rd, 1978 the child was brought out of the incubator. A proud father looked down at the child as an enthused mother cradled the child in her arms. God had given them a gift, one she would cherish for the rest of her life, and as she cradled the child in her bosom she finally began to realize just how special of a gift this was. She was now a mother, one of her goals in life was now complete. The father continued to stare down at the child, as finally the mother offered the babe to him. He reached out and cradled it in his arms. Instantly his smile grew wider, as he entered his hands. A son of his own, some one he could take out in the back yard and teach how to catch a baseball. Some one he would be able to sit down and have a "man" conversation with. This child would be perfect. It'd excel at everything it did, and it would be able to make it's own dreams come true. The father looked over at the doctor who held out his hands asking for the baby. The father passed him over as the doctor put him down into a crib to rest, the father walked over and sat down in a chair drifting off to sleep while the mother walked over to the bed and layed down drifting off into dreams of Toys, cartoons, and many other things she wanted him to have. There on the baby crib a name was etched across "B-R-A-N-D-O-N T-H-O-M-P-S-O-N." The child looked out the window into the starry night, and maybe at that moment he knew he'd fly high with them one day.
