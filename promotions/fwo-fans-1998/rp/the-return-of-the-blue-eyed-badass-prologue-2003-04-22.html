the Return of the Blue- Eyed Badass - prologue

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I was patted on my back for things I shouldn't have been patted for. ...see, this is what I meant, when I told Ivy things needed to change. This whole... Rat Pack thing changed me for the worse. Back in the day, if I lost, no one would come near me. Why? ...because I don't lose. And, if by some small miracle, somebody got the better of me on one night, I'd be pissed to the highest level, dead set on righting a severe wrong. Focused. ...and, you'd be able to see it in my eyes... read it on my facial expression. And, the fear... it'd tear you up on the inward parts of your body, preventing you from even speaking my name. "Good job, Trip... you and Lance'll get your belts back." Was the overall sentiment. In all honesty... it hurt to lose on the biggest stage in the history of professional wrestling. But, as far as those titles went... I could've cared less. Let Jon Crisp keep it. Next time I see that bastard, I'm gonna beat the living hell out of his ass for reasons way more personal than a stupid piece of tag team tin, that's for sure. I didn't deserve to be patted on my back, because anybody can put on a good match. Sure, all the rumors were true... I defied all the odds, and competed even after Crisp, or whoever attacked me and my girlfriend... but, that meant little to me, as any real fan already knew I would show up at Cyberslam anyway. Heroic shit is what I do. I think with my heart... not with my head. ...well, depending on the situation, that is. I like Lance Knight. ...I'll probably like Lance Knight 'till the day I die. He's a good kid. ...a little delusional if he really believes his pipe dreams of almost ending my career, but nice, nonetheless. I even consider him a friend. But, see... that's as far as it goes. Team EQ is on hiatus at the moment. Down the line, if he ever needs my help, I'll gladly throw on the 'No Hype Necessary' tee and help him out. He's earned that. But, see... that's as far as it goes. People saw me one way and started to assume I was weak. Because I didn't make the immediate fWo impact many people suggested I would. Well, let me tell you something... I do what I want, when I want, under my terms. I didn't want to come in and demand title shots, because there were people on the fWo roster that was here before me that deserved 'em. Don't drown me in your praise. Sometimes I like to wake up and do honorable shit, because I can. But, let me remind you people of something... whether I kick it with a group of people, for the sake of kicking it, or not... I'm still Triple X. The same Triple X that dominated the CWL. ...the same Triple X that's running roughshod over the entire CSWA roster. The same Blue- Eyed Badass that had the killer instinct to take Eli Flair, in full fledged, King of Extreme mode to his limits in perhaps the greatest match you people will never see, the Respect Match of late '99. I'm still Triple X. The baddest of the bad. Better than the best. So don't pat me on my back because I competed and lost. Don't pat me on my back because I battled through injury. Pat me on my damned back because I did what I said I'd do from the moment I stepped foot in this promotion. ...pat me on my back because I take names in the morning, and kick ass in the evening. The "Blue- Eyed Badass" is back. ...all you pretenders to the throne had better learn to love it. I want another title, and I want it as soon as now.
