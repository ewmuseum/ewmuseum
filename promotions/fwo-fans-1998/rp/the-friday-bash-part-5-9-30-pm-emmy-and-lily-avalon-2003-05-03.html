The Friday Bash - Part 5 - 9:30 PM - Emmy and Lily Avalon

3 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
If one actually paid attention to Action! Wrestling for a half second, they might have learned a bit about Action! star, Coral Avalon. He was a man who actually commanded a bit of respect despite having a rather ridiculous name, which, fortunately, wasn't chosen via a scrabble bag and a blindfold, which gave him points over a certain Xias. He was a man who was a Match of the Year award winner for his stunning performance against "the Original" Jeff Garvin. He was a man with a large following, especially in the New Orleans area. He was a man whom, despite only being in the game for just two months, had really become a prodigy in the sport of wrestling, and could potentially be the future superstar of the new century. This particular segment of the Friday Bash wasn't about that idiot. It was, however, about his two sisters. With the majority of the Avalon family in town for business and social purposes, the youngest and oldest of the Avalon siblings had journied to this place. How or why they had heard about it were two entirely different matters that'll be explained right about... now. Why: Lily Avalon, incredibly bored and sharing her hotel room with Emmy Avalon, had recently learned a valuable lesson from Joey Malone about changing her anti-social gothic lifestyle, and one of those things she intended to change was her anti-socialness. The only problems? She didn't have a driver's license(or even a permit), and the only other person she trusted to actually take her some place fun was her older sister, Emmy Avalon. This presented a small problem, of course, in that Emmy Avalon was very, very afraid of other people. So, after four hours of convincing, Emmy finally caved in and agreed to take her, and therefore, they found a nice little party in the greater part of Landover. How: They got lost, and after an hour and a half of searching, despite protests from Emmy to just head back to the hotel, Lily just said "screw it" and found the first place that happened to look interesting, and that was this place, the Friday Bash. So, this was how, at 9:30 at night on a Friday, a twenty nine-year-old botanist, gardener, and part-time scientist and a seventeen-year-old prized conceptional artist ended up at what could only be described as the third stupidest party in recorded history. The second was that one birthday party that Sean "Puffy" Combs held, and the first was, of course, the one where Gunnar Smith showed up in a red devil suit, did the Snoopy Dance, and stole everyone's souls. Of particular note and an honorable mention was the Stevie Wonder vs. Ray Charles jousting contest party, where three innocent bystanders were killed and seventeen more were injured. This is an honorable mention due to the fact that some people thought it was a "good gig". Mind you, Lily and Emmy Avalon, aside from being the most successful people in this room even if you combined EVERYONE here, were also the only ones who... you know, dressed up for the occasion due to the fact that this wasn't even the party they had intended to come to. Emmy wore a pink evening dress(though seemed really detached and uncomfortable as she entered), while Lily wore a traditional black evening dress. "Oookay... you, uh... dragged me, um, out here..." Emmy started, before she cried out in surprise when a man who strongly resembled John Rocker brushed past her, called her a New Yorker, and then threw a ninety-five mile per hour fast ball straight at a red-headed Mexican by the punch bowl, which thankfully missed him by a mile, but did put a hole in the wall behind him. This got the Mexican's attention and sent him into a loud conversation with Rocker. It took Lily's not-completely-soothing voice to bring Emmy back to the real world that didn't involve frantically waving one's arms around while trying to ward away all of the bad people around her. Lily knew it was a mistake to actually bring Emmy inside, but it was a bit late now, since she had picked a corner and was gonna STAY THERE until she was sure that it was safe. Lily sighed to herself, and went to comfort her sister, "Okay, okay, geez, sorry for draggin' you out here, Em. I thought it'd help you become... you know. Social?" Emmy didn't say anything, she was a little more afraid of the big angry guy with the spikey black hair and the scowl on his face who had arrived in the party, being shoved in by a big Australian and an equally big Alabama redneck. "For the love of GOD, why can't I LEAVE this party!? GOD!" the man, Adam Nowell, shouted. Lily sat back next to her older sister, and sighed, "Come on, Em. How am I expected to have a niece or a nephew when my older brother doesn't have a tongue, my other older brother is an idiot who has no care for our family, and you can't seem to talk to anyone that doesn't have the last name of Avalon." "I don't... talk to Auntie Cleo." "NOBODY talks to Cleo." Lily pointed out. "Well, it's... not especially, um, the fact that I don't wanna socialize... it's more of the problem of... like, you know... the Nintendo music." Emmy said. Lily Avalon listened intently. She suddenly wished that she hadn't. "GOD!" Lily shouted, "Who the hell is running this party, anyway!" Then she suddenly saw the blonde-haired guy in the tight, tight speedo, walking past, and talking to Nowell, "Hey hey! Enjoying our party?" Nowell grunted at him, "Die." He walked away. Nate simply shrugged and walked in another direction. Lily stood up from her seat and looked at Emmy, "Okay, I'm gonna see if I can actually talk to some of these people. You gonna stay here and cower?" "Pr-probably." Emmy nervously said, pushing herself further back in the sofa. Lily didn't get more than four steps out of the room before she met Sanchis Su Idolo. Now, given Sanchis' track record in this party as it stood, and given the fact that he couldn't even pick up the most cheerful human being in this entire party in Leigh Landers, how was he going to pick up Lily Avalon? Especially when he gave off a pickup line like this: "`EEeeeyyy, bebe! Are chu wearin' de mirror pants? `Cause de Sanchis can SEE `em in dem, bebe!" Lily had walked right by Sanchis, but then she just simply spun around and walloped Sanchis right in his face, "I'm wearing a dress, idiot." She turned around and walked away. Poor Sanchis picked himself back up from the floor, cried and whined about how nobody "respect'a de Sanchis", then wandered away, thankfully missing our xenophobic botanist on his way out. Unfortunately, thirty minutes later, someone would notice her. How it happened would be anyone's guess, but an incredibly high-on-mushrooms John Rocker had attempted to engage in conversation with Emmy Avalon, thinking she was some sort of hippo. Incredibly offended by the comments and incredibly fearful of his close proximity, she finally snapped, picked up a large, heavy book that had NO business being in Pretty Fly's little house, and chased John Rocker around the house. "FUCK!" Rocker shouted abruptly, drawing the attention of the Tallwhackers, who approached Rocker and spoke. "Oy thoynk SOMEONE has had a leeeetle too much to droynk!" Dingleberry said. Cagle turned to Dingleberry questioningly, "You's a think so, Dingerburry?" Rocker punched Cagle. Cagle didn't really notice since Rocker had punched him in the shoulder, he just kind of stumbled backwards, just as a blur of pink, red, and fleshtones blasted past him, wielding what looked like a large book. Lily Avalon, who had been talking to Conor Fuse for the last couple of minutes, noticed this and realized that Emmy was on the loose, and that Lily needed to stop her. Emmy chased Rocker all the way to the second floor, whereupon Rocker flew right out of a balcony and landed in a nearby swimming pool. Emmy stopped before she, herself, fell off the balcony. Out of breath, Emmy sat down, unknowingly next to Leigh Landers, before she glanced at the book she had picked up. "Huh. Ten years out of date." Emmy commented, before she set it down next to her. Lily Avalon ended up in the second story room moments later, concerned for a moment. She saw Emmy sitting next to the frizzly-haired Leigh Landers, and she saw three men; that damn Mexican, a black guy, and that Nowell character, all seated in what appeared to be a makeshift bar. "Emmy! What in the hell was that about?" Lily asked her older sister. "He said that dandelions were not weeds." Lily blinked. Leigh blinked. Sanchis Su Idolo, Adam Nowell, and Allen Speck glanced back over their shoulders before they looked at the shotglasses of alcohol they were drinking for a long, agonizing moment, before they all asked for another helping. "You are very strange." Leigh said, scaring the hell out of Emmy Avalon as she let out a scream, collapsed off of the chair, crawled to an empty corner, and cowered there. Leigh blinked, before she raised her hand and looked at Lily Avalon, "I haven't been drinking, have I?" In response to this, Sanchis turned around and drunkenedly slurred to the two attractive women standing in the room, and the one attractive woman cowering in the corner. "De Sanchesh suggesths det chu stay `way from de punch, bebe."
