One among the flock.

6 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
11:00 a.m. April 2nd, 2003 Downtown London Black and red bondage pants had a younger girl standing out in a crowded coffee shop in the middle of London like a sore thumb. The fact that her eyes were two entirely different colors wasn't helping that along, neither was her unusually bright red hair...or the 'Fuck La Mode' t-shirt (Compliments of bargain stores in France) whose logo was only halfway covered by a single-strapped bookbag. What made it even worse was that she was entirely alone, and obviously not a local... Amy was on a mission. Though nervous in large crowds and alien streets, she'd made the trek from her hotel room after recieving a call from her grandmother to inform the redhead that she was to meet an aunt who'd lived in England for almost ten years and hadn't seen Amy in six. Of course, she'd also been directed to the place by her grandma - the same loud, elderly woman who hadn't set foot in London even once in her 80 years on the planet. All she could do was sit down and hope that this was the right spot, unable to even remember what this aunt looked like, really...so she was running on hope that she'd pick Amy out. After all, how hard could that be? To Amy and the people surrounding her, the situation was essentially the equivalent of a Zulu Tribesman wandering through a Wal Mart. To make matters worse, within a moment of sitting down, it seemed she'd been found. The massive hug indicated that, as did the markedly North American 'accent' in her aunt's victorious shouts that made Amy sincerely wonder how she'd come from the same gene pool... "Ehh..uhh....Aunt Dana!" She returned the embrace slightly, then eased back into the booth as the woman - who was dressed almost the same way as the much younger redhead, which came as quite the surprise considering the relatively insane, however 'proper' nature of the whole family. "Hey..." Taking a second look at the overexcited woman in front of her, Amy pressed herself to recall how old Dana was...her outfit certainly didn't reflect the onset of age. She definitely had the same frequent hyperactivity, that was for sure. "Damn, I haven't seen you since you were...like....yay high!" The woman flattened her hand in the air, not far from the floor and then chuckled, looking Amy over again. "Well, you're still not much bigger!" "Well I was like fourteen, maybe fifteen then...I've grown a little." "Obviously." She chuckled, "You're a pretty girl, too. Seems like you've turned out alright all over. Good job, good looks, apparently good boyfriend..." Amy quickly blushed a little, trying to sort out where she was hearing this from. "Uhh...been talking to dad?" "Sure have. He's proud, y'know...as much as he seems to want other things for you. That's all just fatherly 'insight'." Dana winked, patting Amy's hand. Things were settling in well, the redhead had seemed to have forgotten how normal her aunt was to her. "And he actually likes your guy right now...he's just too proud to admit that maybe someone's good for you. I think he's also a little pissy that, from what I hear, you...uhh...put yourself between this fella and a the business end of a hand gun?" "Yeah. Got shot for my troubles, too." "I heard!" She cringed, "But is he at least worth it?" "Yep. He stayed with me at the hospital the entire time, too. I'm pretty sure without 'im, the world would have a few less nurses today." Amy smirked pleasantly, though still entirely aliented and uncomfortable in her surroundings. "So you watch fWo?" "I've tried to keep track of everything. You're damn good at what you do...well, take that as you will, coming from someone who knows next to nothing about what you do." Dana smiled, "But hey, you must be good...you're up in the pros, and I've seen you flippin' and moving around like you do. I've never known anyone quite so flexible." Scanning the room for the looks she was getting, Aimz wondered momentarily how many people recognized her as something other than a tourist, maybe even as an fWo wrestler? "It's not enough to get me noticed yet. Well, within the company." "What's that mean?" "It means...I dunno." She shrugged. Sometimes Amy was quiet, usually she was high strung...but not today, for some reason. "I just don't get too many pats on the back outside of my friends. Maybe I'm too reserved when it comes to 'strangers'. Then again, the locker room's full of either assholes, retards or the Rat Pack." "Rat Pack?" "Just my friends." "That's who your with!" "Huh?" "Hero boy. I remember I saw you two doing a few matches...it was cute, you worked well together." The eyebrows shot up. "Brandon...yeah. Cute...?" "Cute! You two were pretty...well, occupied with one another." Dana chuckled a little, patting Amy's hand once again. At one table, an ocean away from home sat the only two 'imperfect' members of the family...if only Amy's dad could see this. The redhead was grinning over the thought of the look that'd suddenly contort his face, but the pleasant moment was interrupted. "Wait...didn't he have a wife?" Blink. "Sorry...uhh..." Blink blink. "Who?" "That Brandon fella." "Oh! Umm...yep. Bitchy technicality. She and I don't get along too well now, even though I've only seen her for all of five minutes...I think he's afraid of us killing one another." "He chea---" This was going nowhere, fast. Amy had to act quickly... "NO. She'd done some messed up things, and I guess after a while everything just kinda imploded. Then I came along, and somehow we started really getting...close. And yeah...then I took a bullet for him, bada bing bada boom...I think he realized he had a keeper." A wink closed the statement nicely. "Oooookay then........" Wow. It seemed that somehow she'd successfully made the only other 'normal' member of her family think she was a lunatic. After another moment's silence, the youngest of Amy's aunts spoke up again, apparently just like her neice - unable to stand complete quiet. "So what about your other friends over there?" "Ivy's as crazy as I am, but in a more 'adult' way. It's scary and trust me, you never want to see it." Her mood took a sudden twist at the lack of questioning, causing a bright smile. "Let's seeeeee...Sean, that's Ivy's boyfriend in case you never noticed the groping and spit-swapping, he's a big baby...tries hard to be all big and manly, but I think he secretly wears pink spandex boxer briefs or something - he's one of the sweetest guys ever, though he'd kill me for telling you that." "Who else..." She continued, jumping a little. "Ah! Alex...Lance Knight. He's...well, I don't know what he is. He's quiet - really, really quiet. And then there's Eli, he's the big brooding kinda body-guard type with the sweetest wife in the universe. She's in a band I love, great girl." "And this Brandon...?" Dana was staring intently at her, expecting an answer with an amused look playing about in her eyes. "He's....well, practically schizophrenic. One second he's innocence and practically a virgin, and the next he's....well.............." That comment came to an abrupt end, and the subject changed even faster. "Soooooo.........how's uncle Brian?" The knowing and amused look Amy was getting shot made her wish...why couldn't someone like this have raised her?
