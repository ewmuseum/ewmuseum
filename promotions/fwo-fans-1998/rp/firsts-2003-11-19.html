19 November 2003

If you found yourself across the table from one of the most amazing minds in the wrestling business (who also happened to be drop-dead gorgeous), in Nobu (Japanese Fusion Seafood), what would you do? That's the situation Lance Knight found himself in, sitting across the table from "Poison" Ivy McGinnis, waiting for the sushi to arrive. And he hated to admit it, but his palms were sweating and he couldn't stop fidgeting with his sake cup, which had already been refilled and emptied three times. Ivy, who hadn't gotten to where she was by being ignorant to the people around her, immediately picked up on his nervousness and called the blossoming star on it. "What's up, kiddo? That glass isn't going to fill itself, if that's what you're waiting for." Finishing with a wink, Ivy was pleased that she'd at least managed to loosen Knight up, as he dropped the glass suddenly, then caught himself and let out a half-chuckle. His eyes darting around, he leaned in close and beckoned for Ivy to come closer. When she leaned in, he favored her with his best conspirational whisper. "Y'know, I spent almost two years in Japan, and I've never once tried sushi. I survived on vending machine food and steaks any time I could find a steakhouse." "I'm not a real big fan of seafood, and I'm just afraid I'm not going to like it." Then, with a worried glance towards the bar, he continued, "Did you know that some of that stuff, if it's not cooked right, can kill you?" "Yeah," replied Ivy, "but they don't get any repeat business that way so they make sure it's cooked right. Besides, don't worry about it. What doesn't kill ya makes ya stronger." She laughed, a little too nervously, and drained her own cup. And while they'd been friends for almost two years now, this whole one-on-one dinner thing hadn't completely set in. They'd been out together, either to dinner or for drinks or both, every night since the fWo had landed in Japan, but with Sean Stevens and Eli Flair out of the picture, the two weren't really quite sure how to behave around one another without some type of buffer in place. Knight laughed back, nervous as well, and refilled both cups, which were emptied again nearly as soon as they'd been filled. "So, about tonight...that match with Craig, that was really something, wasn't it? I didn't know I had it in me, to be honest." "Craig is a handful," she had to admit, "I actually met him the first time I was ever in the CSWA's lockerroom, even back then... nutcase to the extreme. But he's always been just as cool a guy when the cameras were off as he was a prick when they're runnin'." Ivy hit his wrist lightly, "But what do you mean, didn't know you had it in you? I knew you were that good a year and a half ago, I just didn't want you to get a swelled head over it. Things're gonna happen for you, my friend. Very soon." "Oh, no, don't get me wrong," Lance said, wondering if was honest-to-god pride or all the sake he'd consumed already kicking in, "I knew I could do it, but with the knee being what it is, I wasn't sure I could do it tonight. But any and all kind words are not only appreciated, but also encouraged." And now it was his turn to wink. "So, since your advice about Craig proved to be so dead-on tonight, any advice about him for tomorrow?" "Be prepared for anything." She laughed. "Seriously, plenty of bread, chips, stuff like that so you can hold more alcohol down." More laughter, and Lance couldn't help feeling his ears get a bit warm. "Seriously," she finally said, "you certainly don't need me to tell you how to wrestle. Once you can accept the fact that Miles doesn't follow any kind of patterns whatsoever he becomes a little less of a mystery. Just not a lot less of one. And don't leave your cell phone out or he'll call everyone on your phone list to ask them what their favorite color is." Calculating how many people he had in his phone book, Knight slid it across the table to Ivy. "You'd better keep this, then.
