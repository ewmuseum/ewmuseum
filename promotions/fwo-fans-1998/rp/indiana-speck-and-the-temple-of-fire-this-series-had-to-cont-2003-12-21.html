Indiana Speck and the Temple of Fire: This Series Had To Continue At Some Point

21 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey. I know it's been a while, but do you remember that barely-remembered series involving the Codemaster, Link, Mike Heftel, and Ken War? The one where they travelled through a hellhole full of flaming bats called Fire Keese? The one that "comically" discussed the origins of the Codemaster's legendary weapon, the Megaton Hammer? The one that never got finished due to a dead hamster at the wheel? Yeah, it's gonna be finished now. I'm sorry. I know you probably have some mental pain at the fact that this series is going to be finished, but if it means anything, Ken War would have died exactly 3725 times between the time we last saw this series and this current edition of it. Anyway, when we last left our "heroes"(or, rather, a witty black man, a homicidal lunatic posing as a hero, a jobber, and a masochist who dies a lot), they had just witnessed Lara Croft passing them by on their quest to find the Megaton Hammer and use it to crush the skull of a giant fire dragon that dwelled within the temple's deepest chamber. So, they took down a Flare Dancer using the powerful shimmy power of La Parka, and entered an elevator. For some reason, despite the fact that he had just been crushed by the elevator, Ken War was with the other Codebreakers and Link as they entered the last chamber that they would need to enter, aside from the one they'd need to enter to take down Volvagia and pummel him until he cried. Immediately, they had to take cover, because Lara Croft elected to fire her pistols at them. Fortunately, with this being the Zelda universe and not the Tomb Raider universe, the projectiles moved so slowly that the Codemaster had time to laugh at the bullets in midair before he jumped back into hiding. Disatisfied with this, Lara Croft opted to come charging in. So, of course, Link charged at Lara Croft. And there was violence. Oh, God, was there EVER violence, with two of the most pointlessly violent video game characters in the history of pointlessly violent video game characters squared off. After all, these two were people who kill animals just to hear them cry. So, of course, Lara Croft was instantly killed by virtue of SOMEONE'S personal bias against the Tomb Raider series. That and she didn't have a close range melee weapon of intense power like the Master Sword. It's just that Link took extra pleasure in ripping out her internal organs, and even more pleasure in wearing her large intestine as a necklace. The Codemaster ignored this blatant disregard of human life(even if it was just a video game character that he didn't particularly care for) and marched up the stairs. As he did so, he noticed that the treasure chest was on fire, and there was a switch to extinguish the flame. So, naturally, Ken War walked up to the switch and blew it and himself up with his cartridge of C4. Hey, he had to use it for something, right? "Brother, you're messin' up my game!" the Codemaster shouted at Ken War's splattered remains, as the switch was now destroyed, and yet, the flames were still there. The Codemaster sighed and pulled out the Tome of Eternal Darkness, the evil spellbook made out of human body parts that he used to cast spells. He raised his fist high into the air and spoke his next five words. "Tier, Paragon, Aretek, Paragon, Ulayoth!" And thus did a blue Ulayoth zombie appear. "Word up, kiddies!" it said. Then it saw the Codemaster. "Oh, it's you. What do you want?" "Yeah. Open that chest over there." "But it's on fire." the Ulayoth zombie responded. "Yeah, which is why I didn't summon the green zombie, jackass. Now, open it, or I start to systematically remove your limbs with the Eternal Darkness targetting system." the Codemaster said, sternly. The zombie glared at the Codemaster, but soon opened the treasure chest, pulled out the Megaton Hammer, and hastily dropped it onto the ground, because he had caught fire and was running around screaming. "AHH! IT BURNS! IT BURNS! BECAUSE IT'S FIRE! AND IT'S SUPPOSED TO BURN! AHH!" it screamed. So, the Codemaster picked up the weapon that would become his trademark, and proceeded to rip the Ulayoth zombie's head right from his shoulders with the spiked end of the weapon, leaving the zombie's head on that end of the Megaton Hammer. The head glared at the Codemaster. "That really hurt." it told him. "What are you, Ken War?" the Codemaster asked, before he proceeded to slam the spiked end of the Megaton Hammer down onto the ground, splattering the zombie's head onto the floor. The Codemaster glared at the splattered remains of the zombie he had summoned, but said nothing. He just walked back down the stairs and looked over at Mike Heftel. "Look, Codemaster! I'm a birdie!" he told the Codemaster. "Shut up." the Codemaster said, before he slammed the Megaton Hammer's blunt end onto a nearby block, which knocked the block into the room below this. The Codemaster leapt down into the room. Mike Heftel shrugged, and followed him in. Then Link did, but the Codemaster only glared at Link. "Must you wear Lara Croft's liver as a hat?" he asked of Link. "What? I think it looks good with her intestines as a necklace." Link responded. "You, I... just... no. Just no." was what the Codemaster responded with, before our homicidal lunatics that were posing as heroes wandered into the next room. This lead to a switch that had to be hit with the Megaton Hammer(which was what the Codemaster just did), which dropped a staircase. They wandered down the staircase, with the Codemaster carrying small crate as he went. He placed the crate on top of the switch at the bottom of the stairs, and they all went inside, whereupon they met up with Ken War again. "hardkorr!!1" The Codemaster glared at him, then glared at Heftel and Link, "Why, in the good graces of King Dodongo himself, did we EVER decide to bring this piece of work along with us?" "Meow?" Heftel responded. "Who cares? I just wanna kill more stuff." Link said, finally and mercifully taking Lara Croft's liver off of his head and putting on his regular hat again. "bcuz ey em hardkorr!!11" Ken War answered. "I was NOT asking you." Then the Codemaster turned and hit another block with the Megaton Hammer and jumped down. He fell a story, and yet, didn't even get hurt when he landed. The others followed, and the only one that actually got hurt was Ken War. But then again, it WAS Ken War we were talking about. The Codemaster then leapt on top of the pillar that needed a good smacking from the Megaton Hammer, and proceeded to smack the pillar. The pillar went "ow". Then it fell. The Codemaster leapt on top of the pillar and rode it down. Heftel and Link shrugged and leapt on top of the pillar as well as it passed them. It landed in a pool of lava, right next to the ledge that the door which lead to Volvagia. And that's where this story of Codemastering will end. Just not in this session. :-P to you, Mr. Toothbrush.
