Plans.

2 March 2000

fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Plans. March 02, 2000 Wilson Training Academy, Charlotte, North Carolina. Mitch Wilson, clad in an FTfWo tanktop, stands in the middle of the facility's three training rings, as matches take place in the ones to either side of him. The smashes and grunts of bumps being taken punctuate Mitch's words as he speaks. Three. That seems to a prevalent number in my career lately. After all, it's just three days until I get my third Internet title shot. And rest assured, the old theory that the "third time's a charm" will not be disproved on Slaughter. Let's look at my two previous shots. Trapped, February Twenty-Six. I ripped Eron apart in there, and that's a fact. I dismantled his knee, and I damn near broke his arm. But in the end, because of Primetime's interference and one fluke move where he probably didn't even know what he was doing, Eron managed to pin me. After the match, sure, I'm pissed, but I figure because of the BS finish I'd get a rematch. Sure enough, I arrive at the arena the next day, and there it says on the card listing - Mitch Wilson vs. Eron, Internet Title. So I go out there and again I beat that incoherent freak from pillar to post. But just when the title is rightfully within my grasp, Primetime. As usual, that punk kid who would still be fighting Matt Douglas in the opening match if it weren't for me stuck his nose into my business, and believe me, he'll pay. But I'll get to that later. Eron, take heed of my words. For you, time is ticking away. Around seventy-two hours is as long as you'll continue to soil that title, and then it'll belong to the one man who truly deserves it, me. No two ways about it, little man. I'm faster than you, I'm stronger than you, I'm smarter than you, and most importantly, I'm better than you. I've got the tools, and I've got the talent? What've you got? You've got absolutely jack. And guess what? Jack just left town. Of course, this third match wouldn't even be necessary if it weren't for some punk little kid. Mitch hops onto the turnbuckle, looking down from it as if to look down upon the man of whom he speaks. He spits out the word - Primetime. PRIME-time. The very word disgusts me. It embodies all of the disrespect for real wrestling shown by all these young punks, the young and the worthless who have infiltrated this great sport. Eron's one of them, but Primetime is the very embodiment of it. And he makes me sick. Not only has he not once shown me any respect for the fact that I made his career, he also apparently has nothing better to do than ramble on, and on, and on about how he's the rightful World champion, and to interfere in all of my matches. Primetime, despite all that I taught you, I must've never gotten around to the "Don't Screw With Mitch Wilson" lesson, and that's unfortunate, because you've done exactly that. And when somebody screws with Mitch Wilson, they get screwed. You would know, considering how I screwed the entire Foundation at Mob Rules. And it looks like it's time for a reprise of that, and you've got nobody to blame for it but yourself. If you hadn't taken it upon yourself to ruin not one but two of my title matches, I would've been content just to teach you a lesson in the ring and let you go on your way. But no, you just had to interfere. Now, Brian, it is very much personal. And that is most assuredly a bad thing for you, because I've got the backup of the most powerful stable in the fWo behind me - Dead, the rightful fWo World Champion Perfect, and Hiroshima. I don't think I need to tell you that we're not above using any means necessary to get what we want, and if you don't believe that? Just ask Ice. Assuming he ever gets out of the hospital, anyway. And Brian, we've already got plans for you. Big plans. What are they? Well, you'll find out, and soon. And believe me when I say that for you, they will most definitely not be pretty. Count on it. Mitch hops off the turnbuckle, and heads off in the direction of his office as we fade to black.
