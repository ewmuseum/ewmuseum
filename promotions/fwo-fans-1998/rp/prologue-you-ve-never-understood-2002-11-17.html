Prologue: You've Never Understood

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"What the fuck was that?" You don't understand. "You had her - you could've ended that fucking pathetic Tempest ripoff with a flick of your wrist... and you let her walk away? Dude, she HATES us." She doesn't hate us, she doesn't KNOW us. "Bullshit. She's a fake fluorescent bitch." Ivy's still ranting, she's been ranting since Warfare went off the air and we were both stiffed on our payoff. Calm down, Ivy. "Calm down? Hell's no." Did you hear the gunshot? I don't think you did. How could you have heard that gunshot and be ranting - STILL - about no payoff and Stormwindz not being Tempest? What happened to you, I said. You used to be fun. "I'm still fun." She sounds insulted. "I just don't like seeing Tempest's image get dragged through the mud like this." That's not it. I told her that she's not acting like herself. "What the fuck are you talking about?" I asked her, when she had ever taken sides in the lockerroom when it comes to someone trying to do their job. "Stormwindz is a corporate tool, like you said." Renshaw was a corporate tool because he thought he could be a hardcore wrestler, all the while not knowing what 'hardcore' is all about. Stormwindz is a wrestler who is doing what any other wrestler at her level would do.... whatever it takes to have a job. And you know it. "No, I don't. I think she's a poor excuse for Tempest and I think she needs to be put out of her misery before she drags the company down." What are you, fuckin' kidding me? I asked, she doesn't know any better right now. What happened to the girl who defended Brand Frontier against the IWC who said he didn't earn his golden ticket? "That was different." Was it? "Yeah, he---" He was the new guy who wasn't given the chance to be judged on his own because of his Survivor victory. He was the guy who was being viewed - not as a separate performer but as the next Ruben Ross. "Bullshit." You argued for Brand getting a chance. Is Stormwindz so undeserving? "Yes." Have you forgotten everything? "Have I forgotten.... what?" You have forgotten. Don't worry about it, though. I remember everything.
