Two things.

29 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Godammit, let me go!" Brand Frontier snarled. The anger in his voice was akin to something alive. He struggled against his captors trying to free his arms. In the midst of the thrashing a new set of butterfly stitches popped loose and blood began to trickle down his face. Two fWo officials came to help the EMTs. Brand is a big man and the three medical technicians had their hands full trying to restrain him. With the other two helping the managed to push him down to a sitting position. Dizzy from recent actions at the hands of the jealous bastard known as Ric Chronos, Brand finally gave in. He slumped in the cold metal chair as he looked over the shoulder of one of the technicians and at a monitor displaying the carnage that was transpiring in the ring at this, the final Sunday Night Slaughter in history. The EMTs shined a pen light in Frontier eyes testing his sight as they tried to discern the extent of his injuries. It blocked his view for a second and he was none to happy about it. A grunt and a scowl let them know this and they backed off a bit so he could see what was happening. They still attended to his other injuries though and reapplied the temporary stitches to the side of Frontier's head. The two fWo referees stayed close by just in case Brand got another idea in his head to run and play hero. That was something he surely did not need to do now. Brand just watched. ~ end your the career the right way. And the pleasure will be all mine." Creed drops the microphone and charges into the ring. Creed spears him into the mat with authority, the arena being rocked from such an impact. ~ Brand winces. He has been on the receiving end of one of Creed's spears, more than once. One of the EMTs had to again push Brand back to a sitting position as Frontier continued to watch. He grabbed the arm of the chair and his knuckles turned white. ~ Creed prepared to unleash a flurry of punches into the WGA, but Erik Kelly arrives and pulls Creed off of him. Creed takes offense and pushes Kelly as Slugger rolls to the outside. Slugger watches on as Kelly looks to the crowd who are wildly into the action, telling Kelly to "kill him", more or less. ~ "Kick his ass Kelly..." , Brand again spits out the words as if he is sick to his stomach. Hatred like this cannot be good for someone, the hollow look in Frontier's eyes show that it has taken more of a toll on him than anyone has yet to realize. Is Brandon Roche even behind those eyes anymore? ~ Kelly obliges. Kelly hauls off and decks Creed, but Creed returns the blows. The two men fight back and forth and eventually go to the mat, locked in internal conflict as Slaughter comes to a close for the final time. The legend, Scott Slugger, watches as two men he's spilled blood, sweat and tears with, battle. ~ The monitor cuts to static as, apparently, the live feed goes off the air. Brand sits and stares at the static for long moments. If he answers the emergency technicians he doesn't realize it. He is lost in thought. Thoughts about everything he went through to get where he is. How his family ended up playing second fiddle to his vengeance, even though it was for them. Brand Frontier thought about Vic Creed and what it would mean to finally get to him after all this time. He remembered back to the tombstone two weeks ago and everything he had thought he let go. He sat at the edge of that stone placard and poured his soul out to the spirit of Vic Creed, only to have it thrown right back in his face when Creed reemerged, phoenix like just one week later. It made his hatred even more for that monster. Lost in these thoughts for long minutes Frontier was only awakened from them by pain. His head had started to throb and he remembered one of the EMTs saying something about a possible concussion. He needed to get that checked out if he was going to jump through the house show hoops the fWo had planned for him. He'd have to visit the family doctor when he took Sean home. Then it would be back on the road. He hadn't done a house show tour in almost a year, it would be hard as hell spending so much time away from the kids, especially with as little time as he got with them now. He would have to rearrange his visitation schedule with Ana, that would be fun for sure. Being back on the road though would get him back in fighting trim. Maybe this once a week wrestling stint had made him soft in some way. He was sure to have more than a few aches once things got going. But that was all fine and good. He had something to prove. Right now his hatred for Vic Creed would wait. It had waited for five long years, right now he had something else to do. He had promised himself he wouldn't get distracted again, luckily he didn't care if he broke a promise to himself. Ric Chronos had shown that he wasn't far off from where Creed was on the evolutionary ladder. Chronos had proven that he was as much pond scum as the Kodiak was. Since he wanted to put Brand Frontier in his place then the Paragon would have no problem standing up and letting him try. This would be a lesson to Creed. This would show him what was in store for him and that Brand Frontier was better than he was before. Better than he was when he beat him for the EWA Title at the Ultimate Gamble pay-per-view. This would show Ric Chronos that his reign of terror was over. Again Brand Frontier might have to sacrifice but he was used to it. It was second nature to him now because nothing would stop him from putting Chronos down for the count, much like he had done the Boston Strangler. Brand Frontier smiled through the pain. This is where he belonged. This is what it was all about. Tonight he had shown that he belonged here in the fWo. Tonight he had proven that he was a survivor. And there were only two things Ric Chronos or Vic Creed could do about... nothing... and like it.
