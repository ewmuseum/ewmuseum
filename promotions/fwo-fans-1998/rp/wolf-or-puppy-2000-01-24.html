Wolf or puppy?

24 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Wolf or puppy? January 24, 2000 Oracle is with Chris Finn for his first formated wrestling interview. Oracle is in jeans, a leather jacket, and is wearing an FFW t-shirt. Finn: Ladies and Gentlemen, I'm backstage with Oracle who's finally resurfaced here in the fWo. Tonight, Oracle we saw you quickly eliminated from the Battle Royal. Oracle: Finn. Shut up. You know, I know, and all the fans know, that Oracle is the best Battle Royal man in wrestling. Just take a look at my history. At Meltdown, I was eliminated because I had an idiot for a partner in Chameleon. At Cyberslam, I was on my way to victory and out lasted over half of the men. Then Sunday Slaughter, I was the first man eliminated. How can this be? The fans won't stand for it. I won't either. Finn: Then what are you going to do... Oracle: Finn. Please. Matt Douglas, you come back to the fWo in your Hardy Boyz wannabe outfit. You've finally taken that god awful facepaint off, and now you expect to be a great superstar. Please. In your series with Primetime you're what? 5,000 and 0? I've beat you before in tag action, and there's no reason why I can't beat you now. Finn: One final question Oracle. Oracle: Interview's over Finn. Matt Douglas you name the place, and I'll be there. You're future will be told. With that, Oracle walks away leaving Finn with a stupid look on his face as the camera fades to black.
