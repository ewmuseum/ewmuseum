Shattered, Broken, Never Again

29 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July, 29th, 2001 Time: 3:34AM Eastern Standard Time Location: The Radisson, Buffalo, NY A steady early morning shower was pushing its way through the downtown Buffalo, NY as Donaven Winters paced back and forth in the small confines of his hotel room. He had arrived from New York City an hour earlier and his mind was laden with too many thoughts to allow him to sleep. He had on the same clothes he had battled Greg Davis in. There were some streaks of red across his arms, face and neck from his own and Davis' blood. A lamp on a small night stand was the only light. Next to the lamp was a photograph of Donaven with his arm around a female, both were teenagers at the time. Also there was an open notebook that had been written through. "Last night, I did what I intended to do," Donaven remarked clearly. "I wanted, I needed to break every bone in Greg Davis' body. I wanted to crush him, crushing his bones as a compacter, twists and breaks a car in half. Greg Davis didn't deserve to live and I wanted him to realize that. I turned him into a vegetable in front of millions. Watching him be taken out on the stretcher was instant karma. Listening to Frankton try telling the fans what a ‘bastard' I was, it was one of the greatest things I've ever heard. Greg was only liked by the fans because of what I did for him. I manhandled him every single night. They couldn't help but like him because he was such a loser. Like all of them. The bottom feeding fuckers that rely on state welfare and drugs that keep them sane." Donaven stopped in mid pace. He sat down on the edge of the bed, thinking over what he had said, and who it was really directed to. "These people, they're all like little Ben and Mary Winters," he toned sarcastically. "They were the perfect example of everything wrong of yesterday and today. Beat your children, load up on drugs, and then kill your wife and yourself, leaving a twelve year old kid to live on his own for three weeks. And it still happens everyday. Where life is supposed to be great, everyone is supposed to look like the Wal-Mart happy face. It just doesn't happen. How many people do you think wear a vale over their faces to shroud their true feelings? How many people become alcoholics to hide their depression? How many people kill themselves just to get out of life? Millions upon millions. Why doesn't anyone hear of all of the suicides that happen each year? There's a simple reason, because the government doesn't want to know how many people don't enjoy their lives in this country. Society kills them, the government kills them and no one else can know about it." Donaven swung his head around, facing the photo on the night stand. "I haven't seen Monica in over a year," he started. "She did to me, what I did to Greg Davis last night. While I shattered and broke Greg's waste of a body, she shattered and broke a friendship. What I did to Davis is easily repairable. A few weeks in the hospital, a few months in a wheelchair, and so on and so forth. He'll be fine, he'll never walk, breathe, talk the same way again though. But what Monica did to me last August, it was unforgivable. She shattered, destroyed and demolished everything we had together. And I'll never have it again. Simply said, Greg Davis be happy I let you live, things could be much worse for you right now. And whoever steps into an fWo ring with me, be weary of what I did to the Ego. I deflated it with an anvil and I'll do the same to you..." Donaven laid down on the bed, after letting out a long sigh he reached over and turned out the lamp. Once again, left alone in the darkness.
