The way back

20 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization The way back March 20, 2000 **Filmed prior to Sunday Slaughter** (The camera fades in on a locker room in the Gund Arena. Everything has been taken out of the room except for a TV and VCR and a chair. Mike Bear is leaning next to the wall in jeans, boots, and a Wretch t-shirt. Mikes nursing a beer and seems to display a mix of nervousness and anger. Suddenly, theres a knock at the door. Mike jumps a little at the sound, but quickly settles himself and opens the door. Fear and Hellrazor are standing there holding Wretch, who is kicking and yelling obscenities at the two much larger men. Mike rolls his eyes and sighs at the display his tag team partner is putting on. Fear and Hellrazor drag Wretch into the room and tie him to the chair. Hellrazor gags him while Fear and Mike talk) Mike: Why the fight? Fear: I dont really know, we just told him you wanted to talk to him and he refused. I tried to persuade him, but he just wouldnt come with us, so Razor grabbed him and he started with that crap. Mike: Man, he must be really far gone. Fear: Good luck. Mike: Thanks, big man, Ill need it. (Hellrazor and Fear exit, leaving the former tag team champions alone in the empty room) Wretch: Waa tuh fuch ah eh her?! Mike: Whats that, Chris? Why are you here? Youll have to ask more politely than that. Wretch: Piss a thit! (Mike grabs Wretch by the shirt) Mike: Listen, punk, were here because youve got a problem. I dont know who this guy youve been talking to is, but hes been filling your head with lies. Wretch: Whiar! Mike: Thats right, he is a liar. Hes got you thinking that Im the one responsible for all our losses. Wretch: Yeh! Mike: No wrong! If its anyones fault, its yours! Lets take a look at our last match. (a tape of the March 5 edition of Sunday Slaughter plays. Its the end of match) Now, you see, I just hit Universal with the Whiplash. Match over, right? No, of course not, because you had to get in the ring and showboat. Thats all fine and good, provided you win the match! If you had just climbed up and hit that senton bomb, it would have been over. I could have prevented Star from interfering and we would have won. But thats not what you did, you had to milk a reaction from the crowd, and I want you to listen to this. (crowd barely cheers at Wretch) You hear that? (Mike pauses the tape) Do you remember the reactions we got when we were working together? Dont you remember the announcers, the internet reporters, the marks, the smarts, they all called us one of the greatest tag teams ever. The crowd exploded every time they heard our music because they knew they were going to get a memorable match. We were on top of the world. Why are you trying to jeopardize everything weve worked for? Is it because of this? (Mike fast forwards the tape and we see that its the Underground/Guano Apes match from the February 27 edition of Sunday Slaughter. This is also the end of the match) Now look, I just gave the Whiplash to both of them and I was ready to pin Jackhammer. Match over, and you had to get in the ring. Do you attempt to do more damage? No, that would have been helpful. Instead, you just wanted the pinfall for yourself, since youre being selfish. Do you run over quickly to make the cover? No, you just casually stroll over and what happens? You get small packaged and we lost the titles. That, Chris, was your fault. It was all your fault. You keep on blaming me when all you have to blame is yourself. Nod if you understand. (Wretch shakes his head in the affirmative) Good, now maybe I can take this gag off. (Mike unties the gag and takes it out of Wretchs mouth. Wretch spits out some residue left over) Wretch: Ugh, that thing was disgusting! Mike: ... Wretch: Ok, ok, Im s... Mike (interrupting): I dont want to hear it Chris. You know me, I consider actions more powerful than words. If you want to apologize and be the best tag team in the fWo again, then I think you know what you have to do. Wretch: Yeah, I think I do. Can you untie me? Mike: Yeah, sure. (Mike unties Wretch) Now are you ready for that match against Chronos and Fallen tonight? Wretch: I think so, but I might need a while, so bear with me. Mike (laughs): Trust me, Chris, I know all about the recovery process, just as long as you want to be a team again. Now go on, youve got something to take care of. Wretch: Yeah, Ill be back. (Wretch exits the locker room. Mike turns to the camera) Mike: You hear that everyone? Were making our comeback. (The camera fades to black)
