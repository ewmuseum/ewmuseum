A Road Paved In Broken Glass: Part Five

3 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"My dreams of wrestling professionally were re-sparked when I began dating a girl, who I later found out worked for a big wrestling promotion. I’d finally got to experience wrestling from the backstage, and knew then and there… I was going to become a wrestler. After a messy break-up, I decided it was time to get properly trained to wrestle. I'd hopped trains, hitch-hiked, walked, and eventually found my way to where I could learn an endless amount about wrestling…" November 19th, 2001. Milo had been in Canada for almost five months, and had landed himself a spot in a wrestling school. Things had been going great for him, and he was learning more about wrestling than he ever thought possible. Sometimes the going would get tough, but Milo knew no matter how difficult his day was, he got to go home to his beautiful girlfriend Jill Adams. She was the first person he'd met upon arrival to Canada, and he was certain this was true love. Although he'd only known her four and a half months, he couldn't wait to pop the question. "Will you marry me?" said Milo, on bended knee, holding an engagement ring in hand. Jill was very surprised, and Milo had caught her completely off guard. "I don't know what to say." Jill replied, gazing at the ring in amazement. "Don't say anything." Milo insisted, placing the ring into the palm of her hand, closing it. "Take your time, and I'll see you after training today." Milo stepped out of the car, and kissed Jill on the forehead before closing the door. Jill waved, as she drove into the distance. Milo made his way into the wrestling school, and began gearing up for what was to be a very hard day of training. He was ready to step into the ring when he took notice to Jill observing him from the corridor. He began to approach her, but she turned and made her way out the door. Milo followed, catching up to her in the parking lot. "Jill? What's wrong?" asked Milo, placing his hand on her shoulder. "Why are you crying?" "It’s you Milo…" Jill replied, as she wrapped her arms around Milo's waist. "I can't marry you Milo. The thought of giving my life to a guy with such an uncertain future, and a dangerous profession scares the hell out of me... Can't you just go back to University?" "And give up the chance of a lifetime?" "I know it's an opportunity Milo, but its still a long shot. You are shooting for the stars my love, and I'm not certain I'm ready for this space ride of a life any longer." "What are you saying?" asked Milo, his grip slacking off. "I can’t live like this Milo… If you keep wrestling I can’t be part of your life." Jill slowly moved away from Milo. "It's your choice." The answer was obvious, and the two said their goodbyes. His dream of a better life with her could not clash with something he'd loved so dearly. It was his life… His dream… And this was another sacrifice he’d have to make.
