Every Drop

13 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Every Drop January 13, 2000 ((The scene goes to whats an airplane, and from the view outside and the instructions of the flight attendant, the plane is already in liftoff. As the camera looks around with its camera, it spots a mother with her baby, two kids linked up with Gameboys mumbling something about "I told you my Level 48 Articuno could beat your Level 70 Psyduck!" the camera then spots a man who looks exactly like Fred Durst signing an autograph for some 18 year old girl. Then, the camera spots someone with their feet up on the seat looking outside, as its Eron the Relentless. Eron stares out the window, with an annoyed look as the camera then settles down beside him, as Eron takes notice. He grunts, sits up, and begins to speak)) Eron the Relentless:Hi Geoff. I didnt request an interview. Geoff Green:I know that, but since we were both on the plane, I was told by Jer... Eron the Relentless:God, the life of a profession wrestler. Always annoyed by little sh(BEEP!)s like you dauntering their camera. Match here, autograph session there, and now, I have to goto France since thats where the fWo will be for a while? What the hell were they thinking? I never knew France liked wrestling. I thought they felt it was like having baths, something you should only think about once a week. Jeez, I dispise ever going to Quebec, how the hell do you think i feel about going to France, which is smaller than Quebec, but a hell of a lot more french speaking freaks there. Geoff Green:You...dont like french people do you? Eron the Relentless:Oh no Geoff, I think they're great people. They smell like sewers and they're language is all screwed up, yeah, theres what you call the "enlightened" people. Pisses me off. Geoff Green:Well, we received word on who you will be taking on at fWo Invasion. Eron the Relentless:Oh yeah? Who? Geoff Green:Rain. Eron the Relentless:Rain? RAIN? What kind of joke is that? The "Internet" Champion? Hell, no-one cares about the internet except for nerds and people who cant get real love lifes. Hell, I never go on the internet, and neither does any real smart person. Rain really represents something great. Ha. Geoff Green:Where have you been in the last 6 years? The internet has become the most popular communication tool on the face of the planet! Eron the Relentless: ::yawn:: Yeah Geoff, and Austin Davis is back in the fWo to end the promotion. Geoff Green:So you're agreeing with my statement? Eron the Relentless:Oh my god...is anyone allowed to stay in character anymore? Hell... ((Eron then looks back out the window, as the sky now has a dark purple haze to it. Eron looks at this with an eyebrow cocked up, then turns back to Geoff as hes talking)) Geoff Green:How do you think you're Title for Title match will go against Rain? Eron the Relentless:Pretty easy. Rain will walk out, holding his recycled tin can on a leather strap, and face me in the ring. I will use my arsenal of over 5000 moves, use the ones which take the least amount of strength to use, and after that, I will pick up Rains flabby legs, put him in the Deroguer, laughs as I see him scream like a boyscout locked in a room with Michael Jackson, then when hes tapped his hand on the canvas for the 33rd time, I'll walk over to the referee holding the fWo Internet Title, grab it from him, lay the belt on Rains body, and then "Rain" on him with my "bodily droppings". Geoff Green:That is so gross and disrespectful. Eron the Relentless:And so it your wife Geoff, but no-one really complains. I will beat every drop, every "Rain" drop out of Rains 7'2" stature, and after im done, I'll then be a International Cha... ((Just then, a lightning bolt is heard from outside as Eron looks around as its raining outside the airplane. Eron smiles, and looks back at Geoff)) Geoff:A sign? Eron the Relentless:Coincidence. Or the fWo has ordered some pretty snazzy special effects over the past while. Anyways, I wanna goto sleep Geoff. I have to forget the fact I lost to Matt Douglas. I knew I shouldnt have listened to you... Geoff:What did I do? Eron the Relentless:I heard you voice for a minute too long. Thats what. Now, excuse me, I already know im gonna hear "Ze winner, and still, ze fWo International Champion, and ze new Internet Champ-eeon, Eron ze Relentlesss!" so I gotta get it out of my head now so I can feel suprised. Rain, I have a poem for you. "From every drop that hits the sand, to Every drop of every Man. As the world comes to a halt, I never feel it was my fault. Rain always disppears, leaking from your breath, you go take a breath, a breath...of FRESH DEATH!" thank you. Hell, I should be in one of those Coffee Shops saying that...naa. ((Eron then leans his head on the window, as more lightning strikes and rain pours, as the camera fades to black))
