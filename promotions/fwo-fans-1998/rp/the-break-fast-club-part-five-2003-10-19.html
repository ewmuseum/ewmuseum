The Break Fast Club (part five)

19 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Steve-O is pestering Funpants back in the library, and it's noticeable that Steve-O has a bloody lip. Evil-Lyn is nearby, hanging out by the statue in the back of the library. Steve-O: No, no, man, no. You got a middle name? Funpants: Leave me alone. You wanna make it eight? Evil-Lyn seems to take interest in the conversation, coming over and sitting down with them. She turns to Funpants. Evil-Lyn: Your middle name is "Has". Funpants and Steve-O look at her in confusion. Evil-Lyn: Your birthday is February 24th, you're six-feet-five inches tall, you weigh two hundred and sixty-five pounds, and your social security number is 319-08-3940. Steve-O: Whoa....are you psychic? Evil-Lyn: No. Funpants: Well, then would you mind telling me how you know all this about me? Evil-Lyn: Sphere was handing out your information backstage at Uprising yesterday. I have a good memory. Funpants: Well at least you didn't steal my wallet. Steve-O: Dude, Senor's got a picture of a naked lady in his wallet! Funpants turns and looks at Steve-O, who has his wallet. Punch in the mouth number eight. Cut to the bathroom, where BQ is brushing the hair that is sticking out the back of his mask. Nate is looking through the pictures in BQ's wallet. Nate: Dude, are all of these chicks your girlfriends? BQ: Huh? Lemme see. BQ stops brushing his hair and takes the wallet from Nate. BQ: This isn't mine. Funpants enters the bathroom. Funpants: How the hell did it get in here that quick? Funpants takes the wallet from BQ and leaves. He walks back into the library and notices his wallet is missing again. He walks over to Evil-Lyn and Steve-O, and sees Steve-O looking through his wallet. Funpants: Okay, seriously... Steve-O: Dude, this is the worst fake ID I have EVER seen! Funpants: That's my driver's license. It's eleven years old. Steve-O: Huh huh...what do you need a driver's license for? Funpants: So I can drive. Dumbass. Steve-O: Dude, you get to drive Dumbass? Punch number nine. Evil-Lyn: Wanna see what's in my bag? Funpants: No. Evil-Lyn: Good, because I don't have one. Funpants: What kind of woman doesn't carry around a purse? What are you, ga... Steve-O: Dude, see, everyone's home lives are unstatusfrying. That's why they move out when they're twenty-seven! Funpants: What? Evil-Lyn: Forget this. I will not sit here and be oppressed by men any longer. Evil-Lyn gets up and walks away. Steve-O follows her. Steve-O: Hey, chick girl...you wanna talk? Evil-Lyn: No. Steve-O: Aw, c'mon, you don't KNOW how many times I've heard that one! Evil-Lyn: Go away. Steve-O: Uh...where? Evil-Lyn: Huh? Steve-O: Where do you want me to go? Evil-Lyn: You have problems. Steve-O: I have problems? Evil-Lyn: Yes. Steve-O: Oh. Well, like, see, you need a boyfriend, and the Sex God...that's me...will gladly fill that spot. Over and OVER again! Sha! Evil-Lyn: You're disgusting. Cut to the basement, where Sphere and Evil Smokey are sitting and talking. Sphere: Big Giant Bear...what did you want to be when you were younger? Evil Smokey: You mean when I was just an Evil Smokey The Cub? Sphere: Whatever. Evil Smokey: I wanted to be John Lennon. Sphere: Don't be a Quicksilver! I'm trying to make a serious point here. I've been a God-like being ever since I was born, and each year, these kids get more and more arrogant. Evil Smokey: Aw bull shit, man. Come on Lawrence, the kids haven't changed, you have! You took a position as a deity cause you thought it'd be fun, right? Sphere: But it IS fun! I NEVER get tired of bossing people around! Evil Smokey: Come on...listen Lawrence. If you were sixteen, what would you think of you, huh? *begin daydream sequence* Sphere is at his junior prom, and all the girls there are at his table. All the other guys are crying on the other side of the room. *end daydream sequence* Sphere: I would think I'm so kickass. Cut back to the library, where all five are sitting in a circle. Steve-O: What would I do for a million bucks? I'd buy alot of weed. Funpants: It's what would you do *for* a million bucks. Not *with*. Steve-O: Dude, I know. Nate: Yo, would anyone here ever drive to school naked? Steve-O: I don't know, bro. I haven't been to school, in, like seven years. Evil-Lyn: I'll do anything sexual for a million dollars. Everyone turns and looks at Evil-Lyn. Evil-Lyn: Yup. I'm a nymphomaniac! Funpants: How many people are aware of this? Evil-Lyn: Just my shrink. Funpants: And what did your shrink do after you said this? Evil-Lyn: She na... Nate: Whoa! Dropped my doobie! Evil-Lyn: Okay, I finally get to talk and I get interrupted by a pothead dropping his...whatever he calls it. Nate: Are you crazy? Evil-Lyn: You're asking ME? Nate: I think. Evil-Lyn: Well lemme ask you something. Have you ever done it? Nate: Run my bike into a car? Sure. Evil-Lyn: Alright, let's see if I can handle this. So you want to, but you can't, but when you do, you wish you didn't, right? Nate: Whoa! You, like, totally understand where I'm comin' from. Steve-O: Dude, where are we from again? Nate: Didn't we live in Miami? Steve-O: Yeah, man. That was the place with the kangaroos and the wallabys and the ditcheree...ditchera...koalas. BQ turns to Evil-Lyn. BQ: Wait, how come you keep getting cut off at the wrong time? It's like we can't know something about you...but aren't you already mar... Evil-Lyn: I don't screw to get respect. BQ: Huh? Evil-Lyn: That's why I haven't fixed the door. BQ: Oh. Funpants: You know, I bet you're nothing but a tease. BQ: Excuse me? Funpants: Not you, Bitchsilver. This one. Nate: Me? Funpants: No. Funpants hits Steve-O with number ten. Steve-O: DUDE! What was that for? Funpants: You were about to ask if I was talking about you. Steve-O: Good call. Evil-Lyn: Are you insinuating that *I* am a tease? Funpants: Damn straight. You come in here with your boobs and your estrogen...and you throw a sandwich at the back of my head! You wanted to play hard to get, but now that I want some, you don't want any! Evil-Lyn: Uh, sorry if I gave you the wrong idea, but you're not my type. Funpants: What do you mean "not your type"? Evil-Lyn: You're a gu... Nate: I NEVER DID IT! Everyone looks at Nate for a few seconds, before turning their attention elsewhere. BQ: What about you, Steve-O? You ever ride your bike into a car? Steve-O: I don't know, did I? Evil-Lyn: He can't think for himself. Steve-O: That's not true. Am I right, Nate? Nate: I don't know, bro. I'm still all mad at the chick for lying to me. Evil-Lyn: When did I lie to you? Nate: When you said you'd give me some lovin'! Evil-Lyn: I never said that! Nate: Sure you did! The rat told me after he was done with his bass solo! Steve-O: Dude, dude, there was this one time, there was this guy named Havok...I snuck into his locker room, and I taped his buns together. Nate: Seriously? Steve-O: Yeah, man. And when he ate his burger, he got a mouth full of Scotch tape! Nate: Awesome! Nate and Steve-O attempt to high-five each other, but they miss. Nate's hand hits Funpants in the side of the head, even though it's physically impossible for Nate to reach Funpants from where he is sitting. Funpants: Seriously, why am I here? BQ: Because you failed shop. Funpants: What? BQ: I bet you made a lamp but couldn't get the light to go on. Funpants: Have you seen some of the dopes who took shop in high school? Nate and Steve-O perk up and start looking around upon hearing "dopes". BQ: *I* took shop. Funpants: What'd you get? BQ: D+. Funpants: Okay. What do you know about Trigonometry? BQ: I know it sucks ass. Evil-Lyn: Ha! Much like me and my gi.. Nate: Dudes! Wanna see what I can do? Before anyone can answer, Nate pulls a bong out of...somewhere...and puts it in between his non-existant breasts. He puts his head down and puts his mouth around the bong, then reaches up under his shirt and lights the bong. He sits there and takes a few hits off the bong, and then raises his head up. Steve-O claps in amazement while the other three slowly clap in bewilderment. Steve-O: Man, where'd you learn to do that? Nate: Third grade. BQ: Nate...are you wearing earrings? Nate: I don't know. Maybe. BQ: Are those real diamonds? Nate: Got me. BQ: Did your dad buy you those? Nate: Prolly. I spend all my money on the good stuff. Funpants: My God, I'm glad I only have to put up with two of you once a month. Steve-O: Dude, what about Monday, when we come back here? Funpants: We aren't coming back here on Monday. Nate: Nuh-uh! School year just started! Funpants: We don't GO to school here! None of us are even IN high school anymore! Am I the only smart person here? Nate: Well are we still friends, at least? Funpants: None of you are my friends! BQ: What about me? I could've sworn we were tag team partners. Funpants: No! I was never your tag team partner! Evil-Lyn: Well if I'm not your friend, why were you expecting to get some from me? Funpants: Cause you're easy. BQ turns to Evil-Lyn. BQ: Hey, since you're not with Funpants, does that mean you don't got a date for the prom? Funpants: The prom's not until May! Funpants has just realized what he said. Funpants: God dammit! Is this day almost over? Evil-Lyn: What if you see me in the hallway on Monday? Funpants: I WON'T SEE YOU ON MONDAY! Evil-Lyn turns to BQ. Evil-Lyn: How about you? BQ: I don't know. Maybe I'd give you a hug. And then you'd hug me back, and I'd get that warm, fuzzy feeling...and two months from now, when I tell you how I really feel, you'll rip my heart out and do a dance all over it. Nate: Dude, could I dance all over your heart, too? BQ: Nate, never say that again. Nate: Why? Sound gay? Steve-O: Huh huh. Dude. Sounds like Funpants. Punch number eleven. BQ: Now, Funpants...I know where this is going to lead. Sphere's gonna find a gun in your locker, isn't he? Funpants: I don't even HAVE a locker! Steve-O has found a flare gun, and he shoots it off. The flare goes right out the door and hits a locker. The door falls off, and several pairs of hot pants fall out. It is now later in the day, and Funpants has found a record player, with a record already on it. He puts the needle on the record, and the music starts to play. "Hello mother... Hello father..." Funpants turns and looks at everyone else, who are sleeping. BQ's face is inches away from Evil-Lyn's chest. Funpants turns back to the record player, grabs it, and throws it on the ground, then stomps all over it. This noise causes BQ to wake up. BQ: Niiiiice. Oh crap! BQ quickly hops up and jumps into a hatch in the ceiling, crawling through a heating duct once again.
