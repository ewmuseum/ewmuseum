Code de Cheat. IV

9 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
They entered. Normally, in an event such as this, and in such a quiet place too, everyone would stop and look at the two men that just ran in through the automatic doors... but no one really did. Most of the people just had their heads in a book... or were (near-)silently chatting with someone else to even notice Tyler and Conor, whom had just rushed in to make a "grand" entrance. Conor eyed Tyler. "Should I-... should I do it again?" He asked, ready and willing to walk back through the automatic doors, and run back in with a full head of steam. Yet, Tyler shot that idea down. "Na. It's alright. We came here to get business done, right?" Conor slowly nodded. "Then business the Fuse Brothers are going to do, right?" Conor slowly nodded. "Good." Conor slowly nodded. "Stop that." Conor slowly- "Sorry." And off they marched, down the hallways, with a purpose in mind. Tyler walked ahead, as Conor drifted off, glancing at the wonderful pictures of the walls as he skipped along. Approaching a front desk near the back of the library, Tyler slammed his hands down on the table to startle the old lady who was photocopying something. She turned around, taking her time (but in truth it was as fast as she could go) to get to the man in the brown "T" t-shirt. "Can I help you, sonny?" She asked, not bothering to take her eyes off of him. "Yes. Yes you can." He replied. Pause... "Where are the computers?" Conor hissed an "AW!" out of his mouth, as if the words that Tyler said were "shocking". He also expected everyone else in the library to go up in "AW!" with him, but, again, they were too busy reading or whispering to each other. "Damn." Conor muttered. "Right over there, to your right-hand-side boys." The old lady said, smiling and going back to the photocopier. "We just have a thirty minute time limit, so please do not exceed it." "We won't." Conor assured, running towards the computers. "Let's do it Ty!"
