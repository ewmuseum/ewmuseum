The Dark Side of the Moon - part 6

THE DARK SIDE OF THE MOON Part 6 - Us And Them Us and Them And after all we're only ordinary men me and you God only knows it's not what we would choose to do forward he cried from the rear and the front rank died and the General sat, and the lines on the map moved from side to side Poison Ivy sipped her Caffe Mocha, all the while flipping through the CD booklet. She's been on edge ever since arriving to her destination, but a hot drink and soem friendly conversation seems to have been all she needed to get over the friction with her brother. She nodded approvingly before replacing it in the sleeve, and turned the CD over, facedown on the table: VALERIAN'S GARDEN Corpus Noctum 1. Virus 2. Dark Embrace 3. Deepest Desire 4. God Only Knows 5. Sweet and Sour 6. Sober (TOOL cover) 7. Hunter 8. Wrecking Crew 9. No Way Out 10. Freedom Through Sadness 11. Fires of Hell 12. Inside My Mind 13. Total Eclipse "Looks good," said Ivy, "When's the release date?" She looked across the table at her current companion.... a 'Goth Goddess' whom the underground music magazine "After Midnight" referred to as the next big thing in goth/industrial music. Angela Davison. The woman whom Eli Flair is in love with. She looks tired, but in high spirits. "We're releasing the Deepest Desire single on March 1st, hoping someone will pick up on it. The album doesn't come out until the middle of April, it still has some post- production work to go through. But this is the rough cut CD that I want you to have." "Thank you," replied Ivy, "it's been tough." "I heard about your mom," said Angel, "Is there anything I can do to help?" "No.... not really. I just feel like everything's fallen apart now, with my brother and I fighting over every little thing these days as well. That's not something I need.... not something either of us should be doing." "He's under just as much stress as you are," replied Angel, "and he's probably dealing with it just as badly as you. Don't take it personally." They sat in silence for a moment. "How's Eli?" "He's tough," replied Ivy, "he's worried about my mother but he's still in there, doing his job." "Does he ever mention me?" "Occasionally." "Is he.... angry at me?" "No." "You both understand why I had to leave, right?" "Angel," said Ivy, "You really don't need to explain yourself to me, or to him. You do what you have to do. You have a dream, you'd be a fool to not follow it." More silence.... both women have things on their mind. "You know what you have to do, Ivy?" "What's that?" Ivy wanted to know. "You need to go back and find your brother, and lock the doors and yell at him and get yelled at by him until you figure out what you're fighting about, and what's irking both of you so much." Ivy sips her coffee.... mulling it over. "You know something, Angel? You might be right. I mean, c'mon.... what do the McGinnis' do if not constantly fight and argue with each other?" "It's true, Ivy." "Besides, Brian and I are all we each have left.... it wouldn't be fair to Mom to have us fighting all the time." Ivy finishes her drink and gets up, CD in hand. "Thanks, Angel." Angela leans over and hugs Ivy. "Just let me know if I can do anything, okay?" Ivy pulls away and thinks for a moment. "Actually.... there IS something you could do for me. Are you busy this weekend?" "Nothing I can't put off until next weekend, talk to me." Ivy sits back down for a moment.... but only for a moment. She's on a mission to confront her brother. No matter what the cost.
