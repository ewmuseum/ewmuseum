The Business

28 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I was once told by my mentor and teacher Jeremy Odessa, "Practice makes perfect." I say, practice makes perfect things imperfect. I've worked every state in this lowsy country. And I'm not saying that to put down the patriotic American, I'm just saying the word lowsy because, well. I'm just tired. I'm tired of it all. The wrestling, the traveling. I have been for quite some time. I'm tired of the business. Or no. I think I should rephrase that, because it's not the business that I'm tired of. It's the business that is slowly tiring me out. It's wearing me down folks. Every day I come to work and I'm covered in a new bruise, another sore muscle. And every day is another day I can't use to heal my tattered body. I'm a twenty seven year old trapped in a forty five year old body. And I don't seem to be getting younger. Damn street merchant in Los Angeles was a liar. Crook. And to think I used that back massager in the shape of a long shaft on my back for a week. Ha, cheap sexual humor. Don't you love to laugh at the cheap stuff? I said laugh. I'm not continuing until you laugh. In fact, I'm going to play T.A.T.U.'s "Not Gonna Get Us" on repeat until you laugh. And believe me, that shrill voice screaming "Not gonna get us," isn't going to help you cry out in laughter. No, it's not comedic. The perseverance in her voice is evident as she cries out. They're not going to get her. And the business, it's not going to get me. It's not going to get me like it got David Smith. It's not going to get me like Louis Mucciolo. It's not going to get me like Owen Hart. It's not going to get me like Brian Pillman. I will not be yet another tally on death's score sheet for wrestling. Because I have something those guys don't have. ... ... ... I'm not sure what it is, but it's there. Oh yes, it's there! It has to be.
