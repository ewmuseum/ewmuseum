Ascension, Prologue (2 - Familiar Reminder)

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Triple X" Sean Stevens might not have known it, but he was being watched. As he delivered the promo that was supposed to add more heat to the series of matches he and Lance Knight were wrestling on house shows, a very nervous Lance Knight stood at the corner of the room in which the fWo backdrop had been set up, his ring attire still on, and the sweat just barely drying on his bald head. Shifting his weight from foot to foot, Lance tried to steady his breathing as he took in a spectacular performance from Stevens. In spite of how relaxed and natural his opponent for the week looked, Lance was nowhere near as confident. He had been content thus far to keep his promos limited, and he had the feeling that until now, the fWo officials hadn't had a problem with that idea either. He'd only had in-ring promos on two occasions, and the first time, he wasn't even officially a member of the promotion. On the second occasion, the night after BodyCount, Lance had had his diatribe interrupted by Grendel, which led to a match between the two. And here he was, on the verge of sitting down in front of the camera and recording comments about Sean Stevens, a man whom he had become rapidly acquainted with through Poison Ivy and Eli Flair. Lance liked Sean, but at the moment, he wouldn't have been upset if Stevens had choked on a mouthful of air and completely ruined his promo, which was progressing superbly. It was then that a vision of Trey Vincent popped into his head. "The Entertainment Icon" had plagued him during his time in jOlt, going out of his way time and again to humiliate Lance at every turn. TV had always made a point of focusing on Lance's somewhat lacking (but still developing, Lance added mentally) entertainment value, insinuating repeatedly that Lance was the most boring performer on television. The worst part was that, unlike Triple X, whom Lance enjoyed hanging out with backstage, Vincent had always been exactly the same egomaniacal ass backstage that he was in front of the camera. That one fact, more than anything, had really shaken Lance's confidence, so much so that he had developed his current "camera-shy" attitude. It was with no small amount of surprise that Lance was brought from his reverie by the fWo's producer, who was in charge of the shoot, and who had just shouted "wrap" to his assistants. A distracted Sean Stevens quickly leapt up from his seat and made his way out of the set, leaving a much more somber Lance Knight to pick up where "the blue-eyed badass" had left off. Lance sighed and tried to swallow the lump that had developed in his throat, but even the slight cough that followed couldn't dislodge it. Resigned to his fate, Lance stared into the camera and began to speak.
