Cool as a cucumber

28 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The day after the show, another arena being messed about in, people putting stuff together... Borst wandering up and down the hallway. "I'm really getting fed up with this..." Borst muttered. He paused for a moment and stared across the hallway. "Are you listening to me sis?" "I'm not your sister anymore, remember?" replied the young girl holding a tray of half empty coffee cups. "I was just an extra for a couple of shows, remember? Now I'm just the plain old coffee girl again." Frown. Borst scratched his head and rubbed his nose. "Oh. I knew I didn't have a sister." he paused and tapped his foot impatiently. "Well fuck off then woman." "Sorry..." she replied, quickly shuffling away down the corridor, spilling her tray of half empty coffee cups all over the place. Borst shook his head. "Women... if only Zoe were still around. She'd know what to do." Borst didn't have a clue where she was, what happened to her, etc etc etc. He's got a very short attention span, and when he left the Asylum, he kind of forgot his wife was still working there. He hasn't seen her since. So... Up and down the empty hallway he wandered, rubbing his beard, flicking the nits away, and sighing every so often. "I don't know what I'm going to do now... this wrestling thing just ain't working. I lost my cool for a minute, and WHAM! Punched... punched... whatever his name is in the face. Oh and it felt so good..." Sigh. "I did cover it up well though... I know how this wrestling thing works." Borst smiled and nodded his head. "Yeah, I did good..." Borst stopped talking for a moment and sighed. "Did well apart from the whole losing my temper thing and twatting that guy. It was his fault. Wrestling ain't meant to hurt. The fuckhead..." Borst paced back and forth for a moment and suddenly stopped, possibly finally realising that he has no self control what so ever. "Ah shit. What am I gonna do?" "Are you talking to me?" asked a crewmember who happened to be walking past. Borst looked rather embarrassed and quickly responded, "Yeah. What do you think?" "About what?" the young man replied. "A fat lot of use you are. You can fuck off too." Snapped Borst, as he whipped his arm to the right and pointed down the hallway. The young man wandered off, whistling a happy tune, to attend to whatever duty he was originally making his way to do. "Probably gay..." muttered Borst. "Fucking queers and women... and fucking fuckhead yanks, everywhere." Borst turned to face the wall, and slammed his hands on it hard. "Right mate. This is what you're gonna do." He said firmly to himself. "You're going to keep your cool. No matter what. You're not gonna slip up again. You're gonna KEEP YOUR COOL. Cool as a fucking cucumber... be nice... be nice..." Borst leant back and took a deep breath, displaying just how short his attention span is. "Pot Noodle. I really fancy a Pot Noodle."
