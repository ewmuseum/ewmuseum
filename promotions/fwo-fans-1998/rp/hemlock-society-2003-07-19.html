Sunday, June 13th, 2003 Walking in fresh off of the street, Amy Campbell was once again the equivalent of a drowned rat. This time, though, she'd put a little more thought behind her jog and laid claim to Brandon's nylon coat to keep the rain away and, though it didn't do much to protect her scruffy ponytail and makeupless face, it was funny looking enough - being miles too large - to make her forget the excess water dripping off of her eye lashes and chin. When she finally stepped inside of the little cafe, it was to warm acoustic music and an even warmer atmosphere between wood grain walls and small displays of coffee beans...but the stares she got weren't all quite so comforting. Apparently her stumbling in, panting and water-logged ruined the mood of the customers that filled the place, the same people who soon went sneering back down at steaming mugs of extravagantly flavored coffees and teas when she returned their cold gazes. One of the looks the redhead got, however, was quite familliar and welcome - one of her 'bosses', 'Samuel Smooth' - otherwise known as Samuel Fitspatrick - was seated on the other side of the room just beside a window that was apparently one of the few glimpses into the dreary outdoors in the entire store. He looked at her. She looked at him. He gave a polite smile.............she hopped over to sit down across from him. "You don't waste much time making friends." He gave another little smile, followed by a rather hearty laugh. "But neither do I...so, how're you enjoying the city?" Amy flicked the massive jacket that'd been draped over her onto the back of her wire framed chair and tighened her pony tail after a drying shake before answering, but she certainly didn't put it off long - After all, it was a chance to talk! Finally, she stretched, yawned and toyed with an earring while speaking, "I dunno, really. It's so...American. Like New York, Jersey, Philly, even Houston - they all come off as generally the same thing when you only really have a two day frame to see the sights. How about you?" She smirked, "Getting tired yet?" Smooth laughed again, this time louder - But it wasn't at all mocking laughter, he was genuinely amused and somehow almost flattered by the notion that the question put forth. "Tired? Tired, she says! That'll be the day. 'Sides, I've only got papers to sign and wrestlers to keep in check. You're the one with the hard part, girl!" "The hard part involves less thinking than helping to run a company." "But helping on the business end doesn't beat the hell out of you too often." Samuel winked at her and leaned back, relaxing a bit before nodding as if with a sudden thought or idea. "Actually, I have a question for you, my dear..." "Go ahead." "You don't like wrestling, right?" "I like performing, I just don't like the sport so much - no." "Do you watch other promotions?" "Nope." "Hmm. Alright, here's where this is going..." He relaxed once more, smiling at the level of attention he'd garnered from the little redhead who usually seemed to have her thoughts lost among the clouds. "If I were to ask you point blank, entirely off the record, your thoughts on fWo and maybe what could improve it, if anything...what would you say?" Amy smirked, "I'd ask you what the hell Voran Xias is doing with any title in ANY division, let alone a prestigious one. Then I'd avoid my rant on people who don't earn their places..." Before finishing, she coughed Xias' name again, "...and go on to tell you all about the scum you should get rid of for reasons outside of overinflated ego." Smooth laughed and cracked his palm down on his knee. "If anybody's gonna think up that list, I'd expect it to be you! Go on..." "Vic Creid isn't exactly much different from what he portrays...he's a meddler but, sadly, doesn't do anything teribbly against the law - So scratch that one." She rubbed her temple momentarily, "Martyr just needs to get laid. Borst and Voran Xias are annoying as hell, but Xias spends too much time slapping it to his anime tapes to be anything more than that, or so I hear...oh, shit, wait! Did that need to be 'authorized' before I said it?" A wink quickly followed. "Apparently we have to clear any comments we make about him WITH him first...the boy doesn't let himself look like anything less than overglorified gold. If that actually worked, I'd be fine with it...but he's falling flat. Anyway, not worth the attention..." "Oh! Kellen Kinkade." The redhead suddenly snapped and sighed, "There's the star! I know he's here under odd conditions, but you guys really need to find a way to get rid of him...I'd imagine sleaze like that brings the company a bad name, especially considering some previous 'business ventures' on his part. There's a reason he doesn't have many fans, y'know..." "Oh, I know." Smooth sighed himself, "But about you...we've got to watch the both of you in at least some respects. You both have kind of...chemical-laiden pasts and...well...I'm actually kind of afraid you're gonna come at the guy with a spiked bat soon." He chuckled. Amy blinked, "Well, jeez...yes, actually - I'm pretty close to it." "Like I said, it shows." What people weren't seeing, however, seemed to be exactly what Kinkade had done and was now managing to do to her last nerves...
