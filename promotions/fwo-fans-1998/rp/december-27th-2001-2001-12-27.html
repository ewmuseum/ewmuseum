December 27th, 2001

27 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
God has a plan. God never closes a door without opening another. Fuck God. Fuck his plans. Fuck everything he might have in store for me, for my family, for anyone. Eli Flair is sitting on the roof of a fairly tall building in the heart of Manhatten. It's the hardest night of the year for him. December 27th, 2001 Part 1 of 2: The Id, The Ego, The Superego. It was this night, seven years ago, that Eli Flair was on the brink of superstardom. He had verbally agreed to be part of the CSWA's Ultratitle Tournament weeks earlier, and had signed the contract that morning. He was the Southern Wrestling Alliance United States Champion, set to wrestle on Pay- Per- View against 'Death' in a Steel Cage match for that very title. It was also the night that a police officer called him and told him they fished his wife, Alicia's, VW bug out of the San Francisco Bay.... and that her body was not recovered. The day the music died, if we may be permitted a small pun. At what point was it supposed to get better? At what point was God supposed to tell me that everything was okay? Eli Flair continually points to that day as a turning point for him. Marilyn Manson once said, "When all of your wishes are granted, many of your dreams will be destroyed." Eli Flair's wishes were to become the biggest star in wrestling, his dreams, to grow old with his wife. One outta two. Trust in God. Trust that He has a plan. So my plans are shit, huh? Where's the balance? I lost my dad. I lost my mom. I gained Alicia. I lost Alicia, I gained Ana Maria. I lost Ana Maria, I gained Sandra. I lost Sandra, I gained Emily. I lost Emily, I gained April. No.... that's not true. April was never mine, she'd never have been mine. Now there's Angel. Every gain I had in the people I could trust was a fucking pathetic, pale shadow of the previous loss. Now it's just a matter of time before I lose her, too. He's been teetering on a very precarious precipice the last few weeks.... and it's finally making sense in his head. His inability to committ to Angel has taken shape.... everyone he's ever loved has abandoned him in one way or another. To accept the one true God is the only path to salvation. Accept him, or perish in eternal flames! How the fuck can eternal flames be different from eternal isolation? How the fuck can anything, ANYTHING, be worse than what's currently going on in my life? The wind picks up, the cold bites into Eli's skin. He doesn't notice. If life is a series of evening things out, then someone made a mistake somewhere. The good has come in spurts, and it's always been taken away. The bad has never left. And if there is a god out there, then I've paid for sins that nobody on the face of the planet has the capacity to committ.... and he's punishing me solely because he can. Accept the Lord God into your life, and salvation is yours. We're all past salvation, we're all past redemption. There's nothing left but pain, and I'm fucking sick of it. "And if he ever did relent.... it would not be because he cared." -Paraphrased from "Johnny the Homicidal Maniac"
