plan B

18 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“They fucked us!” argued Roland Washington with the head of f.W.o officiating. “We had the pin befo’ that shit even happened!” He was definitely not impressed with the manager of Affirmative Action, “Sorry Roland, time limit draw is the official’s position on it and we support that.” Roland quickly shot back with, “Oh so that’s how it is, huh? You just ain’t even gonna look into it…” “Look into it, Roland I was watching the match,” replied the obviously aggravated official. “In real time or slow motion?” “Real… what does that have to do with anything?” he inquired. Affirmative Action appeared in the gentleman’s office like the audio/video geeks in high school with a television and VHS player. CJ pulled out the DVD and everyone looked at Roland. “Where da CD go?” “In the DVD playa… man, where’s the DVD playa?” Roland asked looking over the set up. “Umm,” whispered CJ, “S’ti ni eht kcurt.” “Huh?” “A.A.M.U.” Big O offered the assist. Roland hushed his voice, “Should I ask?” “Maybe betta if you ain’t.” CJ responded. “So um, well if you’ll just walk wit us to our car…” Roland started before being cut off. “I will not!” “O will you…” The swift cinderblock known as Omarr’s hand lumped the top of the official’s head knocking him to the floor. Big O scooped him up and tossed him over his shoulder, “Got him.” The troops pressed through the Key Arena and quickly made their way to the Affirmative Action Mobile Unit. Roland busted out the smelling sauce, after a couple coughs the official woke up belted into the back seat in front of a 15x9 high definition monitor where there is a replay of CJ covering Bastian and hooking the leg. As it appeared a small ‘Affirmative Action fairness clock’ appeared on the bottom of the screen, as the ref started his count the clock started it’s. “Now sir if you’ll just watch that clock right there in the corner.” Roland pointed to the scowling suit. “One.. Two.. THREE!” Sure enough the clock in the corner reached three before the referee had two and a half. Roland smiled with the glee of the just feeling he had clearly proved his point. The official tried talking from behind the gag, oh did I forget to tell you they gagged him? Yeah well can’t catch ANOTHER kidnapping charge, ya heard! He motioned for them to replay the segment, to which they happily obliged. Seemingly he was agreeing with them… who knew? Roland removed the gag, “See what I’m sayin’ that dude straight screwed us!” “I…” he pulled back from the red bandanna, “actually I did see…” Junious and O removed the seatbelt, “So you can do something fo’ us?” “Well if you let me get back to my office,” he started before CJ, Roland, and O jumped to open his door. Once outside he continued, “I’ll be happy to ask you what kind of idiot do you think I am?! I see that clock counting out by twos in milliseconds! Just wait… I’m talking to Ivy about this! I’ll have you fired!” “Fuuck you.” Junious stated stepping out of the truck. The official exited stage left, and in a hurry. June Bug looked at Roland disapprovingly, “I thought you said that bait and switch shit would work, cuz.” “Aiight so it’s time fo’ plan B.” Roland answered a little less boastful. CJ smiled, “damn straight!” “Yeah!” the ever vocal Big O approved. “So next week then, it’s time fo’ plan B.” June Bug nodded. “Them white folk ain’t gonna know what hit em’.”
