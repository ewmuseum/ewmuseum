Weeks Lived Through The Rearview: Farewell

15 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
July 10th, 2004. Milo stood alone in the cemetery, beads of rain dripping from his face, mixing with saline as they fell. It was hard to breathe... there was something about reading Jill's name, on a tombstone, that made it that way. The beautiful smell of summer rain reminded him of better days; ones in which he and Jill would stay out late, laughing as they played like children in the rain... ignoring the wet ground as they rolled in the grass. For Milo, these memories were quite possibly the closest to heaven he'd ever get, so he held them in his heart. Milo had seen and done some things in his life he wasn't proud of, but he knew the time he and Jill spent was a blessing. He must have done something right... and this gave him hope. Milo knew Jill was watching him, smiling down from above. His father was watching too... and he was going to make them proud. It was time for Milo to stop running from his problems. It was time to live out his dream of becoming a professional wrestler. The doctors told Jill in November that she had two months to live... but there she was, over half a year later, still fighting. She had fought till her last breath, and this was truly a remarkable display of physical and mental strength and was a huge inspiration for Milo to fight against all odds, to achieve his goal. Milo dropped a white rose, Jill's favorite flower, and it gracefully stuttered to the ground in front of the tombstone. "So long my love... I'll never forget you." Milo stood for a moment longer and let out a deep sigh, completely ignoring the rain that relentlessly beat down on him from the gray skies above. He slowly made his way out of the cemetery, never turning to look back. This was the final exit for a lover... this was goodbye.
