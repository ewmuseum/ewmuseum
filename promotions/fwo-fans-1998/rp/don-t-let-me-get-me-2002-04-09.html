Don't Let Me Get Me

9 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ruben Ross looked down at the shimmering gold that had become a part of him this year. The fWo World Championship glittered away, and what's better, it hadn't begun talking to him. That would signify he would going insane. But then again, so would this, wouldn't it? It was a mandatory evil, like a visit to the dentist or sitting through "Runaway Bride" so Andrea would give it up. A couple of worry lines appeared on his face, but they'd been there the past seven-odd years it seemed to him. He looked scruffy and the 2001 World Champion Lakers hat hung off him oddly--why was he going through such machinations? He knew what waited for him, and he was just trying to stall and delay. Sighing, he opened the door, took one of the two seats, and sat down. He had to do this for him. He was always so insecure, so paranoid, and these talks helped. He'd been driven to tears sometimes, but it was because he was a part of him, dammit, and if he blew THIS on the biggest stage in the free world, it was one step closer to the drive-bys, the roaches, the sleepless nights... Fuck THAT shit. He straightened out the 'NSync shirt and entered. He walked in. "Oh, no," Ruben thought to himself. "Here we go now..." "All right, you got pinned by Kelly. Tag match. Worthless. Meaningless. No one's coming to help him. This is about the 27th. Anaheim. History, more importantly, history repeating." "Don't let this loss get you. You got paired with a rookie, this was a nothing match. This is everything. That title is OUR LIVES NOW." "I...what about..." "What ABOUT her? She's great and everything, but you're eventually going to have to make a choice. This dream...or THAT dream. We sent her away once, you won the 2001 Wrestler of the Year and the belt. You need to get over this shit for the heart and start using that fucking brain of yours." "I've been..." "No, let me stop you right there. You're me, I'm you--we don't get over on each other. At least, you don't get over on me. You're thinking of how you almost broke up with her again. You're also thinking about how you see a future with her and kids and the house and all of those things they sell you in Hallmark cards." "Well, it wouldn't suck, you know. "It wouldn't suck IN THE FUTURE. Welcome to right the fuck now, the magical way you EARN the house and keep the girl happy for childbirth, and oh, yeah, keep you from being the one black guy at Pepperdine not pushing a mop. NEVER LOSE SIGHT OF THE MONSTER!" "The monster? I've already got enough with her, let alone everybody backstage loving me to my face and begging Universal for a shot behind my back. I'm breaking out in hives underneath the Seau jersey sometimes. Trust me, I haven't lost sight." "KELLY. That monster. You remember?" "I remember damn near beating him every time..." "What happened the one time you didn't? Silence. "Oh, he damn near ended your career, that's right. You like the aftereffects because you came back the hero and got the girl, but let's be honest: this man nearly ended it all. And for that belt? I'd be surprised if he wasn't willing to drop off Pammy in Compton with a Klan hat on. He's everything you're not. Strong, powerful. He's got you by a foot and 100 pounds. So what do we do?" Silence. "Don't make me reach..." "Cut him off at the knees. Use the stamina, use the speed." "And?" "Restrain the highspots." "Yes. That's where you fucked up last year. You shifted into overdrive. We want fast--we're already going to be faster than he is, and we've got the stamina. This thing gets past 15 and we dropkick the knees enough and peekabo enough, he'll feel that 336 and the ache in the legs. And then, we start mixing in until we beat him. Again." "Right." His eyes were getting bleary. "Hey, Kelly's big and bad, but he's the most-decorated never-was in fWo history. What's he done? Hardcore title reigns? One month with the Internet title? You're--well, WE'RE--the longest-reiging Internet Champion in history smart enough to live long enough to enjoy it. WE'RE the longest-reigning World Champion in the history of a five-year organization. We beat Chronos clean--he didn't. We beat Davis in a half hour of sucking it up and going balls out--not him. We beat Strangler--" "He didn't." The confidence was starting to flow, now. "And I damn sure didn't see him even TRY to face Slugger, let alone beat him smack-dab cleaner than freshly washed socks in the center of the ring. He can't even FACE a man you beat. You get out there, you stall a little, stick and jab, wear his big ass out. The 27th is ours. The world is ours. The title is ours. Until we deem different." "And 'Dre?" "I think we're disagreeing to disagree again. Now get out of here. Ruben Ross bustled out of the room to train. Ruben Ross rolled down his 'NSync shirt, put his feet across in the other chair, and lit up a cigarette. Ruben Ross woke up. And he knew.
