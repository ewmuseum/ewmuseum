Receiving The Card (part 1)

fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Receiving The Card (part 1) March 17, 2000 The camera fades in on an especially luxurious hotel suite - it's got the works. Everything you would expect in a hotel room occupied by one with the penchance for extravagance and the money to burn such as Perfect. Big-ass TV, genuine polished oak nightstand, plush carpeting, and a multitude of halls leading to other rooms are all visible. Said four-time World Champion's head peeks out above the covers on the large bed, barely visible. From the large window to the side of the bed shafts of sunlight pierce the curtains, and Perfect, just waking up, sees this and decides, ah, screw this, he's going back to sleep. A digital glowing clock by the bedside reads "1:11", and from the light filtering in through the window, it's obvious that this is the 1:11 PM rather than AM. Perfect twists in the sheets. He suddenly sits bolt upright as a loud knock reverberates from the hotel room door. He checks the clock. Dammit! Can't these people let people sleep until a decent time? He struggles out of bed, clad in a loose Hard Rock Cafe t-shirt and shorts, heads toward the door, yawns, yells at the door. Obviously from his demeanor he is less than a morning person - or an afternoon person, for that matter. What the hell do you want?! A muffled voice comes from outside. I've got the card for Slaughter! You asked for it to be brought to you when it was released...? Perfect groans. Yeah, yeah, yeah. I should have specified at a decent time, but oh well. He opens the door, and in steps... Who are you exactly? Jeremiah Frankton. Writer, interviewer - You're that Jazz freak, aren't you? You might say that. Loony. Give me the card already. Frankton does so. Perfect scans, finds Myself and Mitch vs. Collins? Whip ass~! A thought occurs to him. Wait a minute... you're not working me, are you? Perfect asks this as he recalls that some booker type person told him he had Slaughter off. I mean, I know Collins doesn't have the brains God gave a doorknob, but you'd think he'd at least be smart enough not to agree to a match like that! I mean, you might as well call it a handicap match. I'm not working you. It's scheduled for Slaughter... Main Event and everything. Well, that's good. Because if you were working me, I'd have to do this. Perfect shoves Frankton out the door, and a crash is heard, followed by a clatter and smash of plates and glass. Perfect calmly shuts the door, pauses, re-opens. Next time, let a guy sleep, will ya?! He shuts the door, sits down on the bed to ponder the implications of the match. Hmm... maybe this isn't as good of news as I thought. To agree to a match like this, Collins has to have some kind of plan up his sleeve. Hell, he's got his pimp slash girlfriend and those tag team guys... He attempts to think of their names. You know, that guy... the one who's either crazy or isn't depending on when you talk to him... and he's got War... but then again, good God, what is he good for? Absolutely nothing. But nonetheless, Collins has to have some kind of plan to agree to this match... Perfect briefly mulls this over. Ah, screw it, it's Collins after all. The goof isn't even smart enough to read the contract in the first place, let alone come up with some kind of complex plan! Hmm... Primetime. He's probably gonna have some kind of nefarious, evil, and generally French type of plot up his sleeve once he hears about this... ...but on the other hand, he's Primetime, so it's not like it matters anyway. Man, this is great! Collins is dead in the water... the idiot's gonna be lucky if we do to him what we did to Ice! This is gonna be fun. Perfect smirks for a moment, then leans back into bed, pulling the covers over his head. After a moment, the alarm quickly goes off and begins to blare AC/DC's "Damned" off their new album. Dammit! Perfect reaches out from underneath the covers, finds the clock, which now reads "1:18". He wraps his hand around it, and sends it flying. A crash of glass is heard, followed by the screech of a cat. Ah... much better. As he hides himself under the covers again, a thought again strikes him... Hey... what if I end up fighting Mitch for the belt? What're we gonna do then?
