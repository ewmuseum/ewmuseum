Overtures

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Cut my life into pieces" Ric Chronos is standing before the camera, an uteer sneer etched across his face. Although it is only moments before Slaughter, the trained eye can tell that he has not yet changed out of his ring gear from the previous night's Body Count main event. "This is my last resort" Ric speaks, his voice not raising above a strained whisper. Ric: "Fate... last night, I had the opportunity to reclaim what was mine. I had the chance to go on to Cyberslam... the biggest event of the year... and to face Ross for the fWo World Heavyweight Title. MY title. But you... you ruined that. And unlike Brand Frontier, Neo, The Great Dragon, or... *ahem*... Fallen, you didn't do it like a man - oh no, you did it like a the coward you've always been. You didn't face me - you lay down on your back for Kelly, giving him the pin he needed to tie me. Why? Because you knew, Eddy - deep down, you knew that if you stayed in that ring, I would eventually take you down for the three count, and then Kelly never would have been able to tie me, much less go beyond my score. You lay down for Kelly because you were afraid of me." "Suffocation, no breathing" Ric: "You have betrayed my trust for the final time, Eddy. You want my full attention? You want the opportunity to be schooled by me, one on one? Well, you've got it. But there's a saying about getting what you wish for, Fate. And I'm telling you now - you won't be making it to Cyberslam to face me one on one. Instead, I'm hunting you down - tonight." "Don't give a f**K if I cut my own breathing" Ric: "And then, I don't care what it takes, but I will head back for Universal's pet, Ross. Someway between now and then, I will become the main event for Cyberslam. Either by taking that gold off of Ross, or by putting Kelly out to pasture - I don't care. This was my time - this was my year - and I'm not about to let you ruin it for me, you ungrateful little bastard." Ric raises his arms, and the sledgehammer that has become Ric's trademark goes up with them, previously obscured from the camera's view. Hoisting it aloft, Ric makes sure that the camera gets a good view of the weapon before lowering it to chest height. Ric: "Remember, Fate - what happens fro this point on - you asked for it." And on that ominous note, we fade to black.
