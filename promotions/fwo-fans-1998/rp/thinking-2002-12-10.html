Thinking

10 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tisha sat next to Troy as they talked with Seth about the recent signing of the Mercenaries to the fWo. Both teams have a long standing history with each other and as some may think, the ongoing feud was taken out of the ring almost 2 years ago after a match in Miami, Florida where Covington's got the a controversial win over Mercenaries. Klaven and Chambers never let things go, they had complained repeatedly of the Covington's being too stiff in the ring that night. "I can't believe they're carrying on like this," gritted Troy through his teeth. "What do you expect? They still have it stuck in their minds that they're 150% better than us in the ring, nothing we could ever do would convince them otherwise." "I wish I would have been there to see the match, maybe I could convince them that they suck," said Tisha trying to be funny. Seth wasn't too amused; quickly silenced by the cold stare received, Troy stopped chuckling. "This isn't funny, you know as soon as they get some type of push with management that they'll make every attempt to make us look bad. Anyone important from OCW forgot to mention to management that they were out to get us and still are," Seth was frustrated. "You do realize that we're going to have a match with them soon enough, they complain about us caking it up so they can't get anywhere." Troy looked up at Seth; he was in his usual venting mode. "Come on man, cheer up. It's not that big of a deal." "Not that big of a deal?" Seth squinted at Troy. "Do you remember the last time we got in the ring with those jackals? Klaven nearly ruined my fucking career!" Seth wouldn't have any of it; Troy didn't want to admit that someone was trying to push them out of the business and out of their dream. "I swear on my mothers grave," it was silent for a few second. "If either one of them tries anything fishy in the ring... I'm going to up it by 10 and crush their fucking skulls!" The vein that lied underneath the skin in the middle of Seth's forehead began to pop out. "Seth, honey... you gotta settle down," Tisha tried to calm his nerves. "I'm not settling down any time soon, shit has hit the fan. I'm not happy." Seth looked at Troy, "You know that," then looked at Tisha, "And you know that as well." He stood up and began to walk out of the room, "What were you doing the other night Seth?" He spun around and looked at Tisha, he looked straight into her eyes and saw the concern that lied within. "Thinking," his eyes wondered away from Tisha's, "I was thinking." He turned around and walked away. Both Tisha and Troy looked at each other and shrugged. "Give him some time Tisha, that's all he needs."
