Meeting Publico - Part Two

5 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Okay, so we're all set?" Brian Publico sat with his two new bosses at their kitchen table, looking over a list of things that they had asked he talk to Disney about. Nate and Steve-O were sitting at the other end of the table, hands folded, watching him react as he got further down the list. His reaction, though concealed as best he could, was confusion. Disney, one of the most successful companies in the entire world, were offering to give them whatever they felt they needed to make the transition of management as easy as possible, and they suggested these things? "You want to have dinner with Micky Mouse, then battle him in one hundred straight games of Street Fighter?" Brian asked them, tapping the base of the paper with the end of his black fountain tipped pen. He kept a few in his brief case at all times, for emergencies. Steve-O nodded, leaning across the table to point to the next 'demand' in the short list. "Yeah, also..." He pressed the end of his index finger down into the writing, smearing it from one side of the page to the other as he sat back down. "That's the biggie." "Guys," he replied, trying hard to keep his cool with these two idiots that seemed to have spelt every single word wrong, "Disney is a family company. We can't, and won't, under ANY circumstances, supply you with these... substances." Nate's face scrunched up in confusion. "Uh, who said anything about substances? We just want weed. See, weed." He smiled. Pointing down to where his brother had previously directed Brian's attention to, there was a word. wede. No capitalisation, just a single misspelled word with plenty of sloppy underlines scratched beneath it. Brian sighed. He picked up the notebook and brought it over towards his brief case, then dropped it in a trash container that hanging across the counter when they were not watching him. One of those flip-top kinds, though this one was decorated with naked women. They liked naked women. Brian turned back to them, smiling again. "Great! I'll make sure they get that ASAP! And I'll be back tomorrow to run through the new calendar and agenda with you both, get familiar with the guidelines and expectations that Disney has for its product. That sort of thing." Pretty Fly agreed. "Yeah, then think we can take that sweet-ass BMW out on the town, maybe pick up some babes? I know these two hunnies, one's missing her left toe but boooooooy-" Publico cut in, raising a hand up to stop Nate. "Sure, sure... whatever, remember, I'm here to serve you." ...For now.
