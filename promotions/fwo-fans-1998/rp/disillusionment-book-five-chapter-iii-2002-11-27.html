Disillusionment: Book Five - Chapter III

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HAHA! You did it! Man, that was straight out of an action movie. I can't believe you thought of that. Who would have thought that the Disciples would be so stupid as to leave the ventilation shafts completely open to the outside? Yeah, I know you got us out of there, but remember, you go us in there too, so don't be expecting any favors from me. The last time I tried to save your life, I ended up sharing a jail cell with you, and you stink. Geez. I may not get to the time to shave everyday, but at least I bathe. Go find some hot water. I guess this means I'll be able to make it to my match tomorrow against Brandon Louden. Word on the street is, he might decide that he might catch Shawn Arrows syndrome and chicken out of our match...well, that was the word on the street before we were tossed in the slammer anyway. I suggest we part ways now. It almost seemed too easy to get out of there......like...like the Disciple's wanted us to escape. They probably have some sort of tracking devices implanted in our skin to lead them to others in the know or something. All I know is that no one who knows about the Disciples is ever going to want to get near us again...we're damaged goods now...it'd be too risky for them. So what's next for you? Me? I just have to keep trying to survive and do my duty, which is inform the world's population that the world does not operate as they believe it too. Maybe they just fear the fact that I'm a loose cannon......a man who has nothing to live for has nothing to lose, and thus, can put it all on the line. Maybe that's it. They've seen that I put everything on the line every time I go out there and they fear for their lives thinking that even with my bare hands I could......nevermind. But what then? Certainly, I have not made enough impact on the wrestling world that I could actively pursue a career in it. It's too high profile anyway. If I keep wrestling, I will get caught again...and next time, the Disciples may not be so careless as to allow my escape. I dunno, I guess I'll just go back to the same kind of life I had before this whole tournament started weeks ago...holding down little shit jobs mopping up piss in movie theatres and the like. It's not fun, but it provides me with what I need to live. There's a guy I know that thinks he could hide me in some place he's got for years. I can't do it though...I need to be on the offensive. Maybe you should go to his place...you're no use to me here, and you'll be even less help if you're dead... Oh no...you're not coming with me...I can't risk it. This is a one man job. Bringing another along doesn't help me at all and it makes the risk infinitely greater. It's a lot harder to find a single man than a pair. A single man can blend in, but a pair, just by nature, is more conspicuous. You go on......into hiding. Like I said, I have nothing to lose...that makes me dangerous.
