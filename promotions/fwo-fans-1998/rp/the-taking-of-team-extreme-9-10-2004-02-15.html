The Taking of Team Extreme - 9/10

15 February 2004

The Taking of Team Extreme Chapter Nine January 24th, 2004 Los Angeles, CA 4:22 PM local time "Well, we're loaded," said Angel, looking around the main room of the condo, "Cleared out the fridge, gave the fish to Mrs. MacKenzie, have nearly all'a MJ's toys packed... I think we're set to close up shop for the year." Eli looked around, his daughter in his arms, a bag of essentials at his feet. "I told the kids they could use the place this summer after school lets out... Brian's got a key for 'em." Angel laughed. "Sounds good." "Nervous?" "Yeah." "Don't be." Valerian's Garden would arrive in Australia in twenty-nine hours, all things considered - and arrive back home in three hundred and five days. It would be a true test of their will and of their determination, as well as a chance to play to an audience that had never heard them before. Ivy could talk anyone into booking this band... it was great. "Look, why don't you stay for the next day or so?" asked Angel, "You'll be so bored just hanging out in Florida until the show." "No, I'm driving - not flying," replied Eli, "Without you here... this is just a place. It's not home." They stood in silence for a moment, before Angel collapsed into his arms. "I really admire what you're doing," she whispered, "and what you're willing to do." "Someone has to go this far," replied Eli, "It's just hard to watch what's happened to Fox, to Twitch, to Smooth, to the company... and to see that nobody appears to care." "Ivy cares." "And what can she do? Honestly... she's so obsessed with working in the system she doesn't remember what brought us to the dance... if the rules don't work, you break 'em." She squeezed. "I've seen a small bit of the politics in the music business, and it's nearly enough to drive me mad. I can't imagine what they're like in your profession." He looked down at his wife, as she stared up at him with her bright blue eyes. "Just make sure you come back to me, y'hear?" "I hear." They kissed, long and passionately. The last kiss for these two until December. December was a long way away... a lot could happen. A lot had already happened. Another squeeze, and Angel took MJ from Eli's arms and left the condo, getting into the waiting van. The rest of the band was inside, along with all of their necessary gear. The King of Extreme watched the van drive off before he locked the door and headed for his Tracker. As he entered the car, he sat there for a moment... ...and punched the seat, as hard as he could. Taking... always taking.
