Preparation, Part 3 (Final Concerns)

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Even through the thick walls of the arena, Lance Knight could hear Biohazard's "Last Man Standing" playing through the ringside area. He knew that his friend and sometime training partner Brand Frontier, a man that he had met and grown to respect over the course of the Survivor competition. Lance was aware that Brand had his hands full with the suicidal luchadore known only as Crucifix, but he also knew that he needed to focus on his own match with the man-beast Grendel. He had fought Grendel on three occasions before, and on all three occasions, he had come out worse for the wear. Twice, he had managed to pick up the victory, but on both those occasions, Grendel had inflicted lingering damage, and those occasions were weighing heavily on Knight's mind. As that stubborn part of his mind kept reminding him, those had been in regulation matches, and Lance knew that tonight's match would be an entirely different monster. With no fear of being disqualified, there was no telling what kind of plot Grendel had hatched for the evening. Lance could only hope that his own precautions would pay off and that if necessary, Brand Frontier would be there to save him from serious injury. With a regretful sigh, Lance realized that Brand might be in no condition to offer the help that he might need, especially if his match with Crucifix was anywhere near as brutal as some of their earlier encounters had been. Maybe he should've been less insular in his backstage encounters... No. He had to rely on himself, first and foremost. He had the ability to beat Grendel; he had proven that on more that at BodyCount and on two other occasions. So why was he so worried? Why were his palms shaking? Why was the sweat continuing to pour down his forehead in spite of his best efforts to wipe it away? Because you know. You know, deep down, what he's capable of. You know that he's more animal than human, and you know that you don't have that same capacity for violence, for cruelty, that he does. You know that you've tried to put a human psyche under that mask, inside that enormous furry body. But when it comes down to it, you also know that it's not there. "But I also know that I've got the talent, the ability, and the drive that I need to put Grendel away before he's able to do any of that." Then why are you so worried that Brand won't be there to save you? If you're so confident in yourself, then why are you so worried about someone else coming to save you? And even if you do make it out of there tonight, you've got a whole different challenge ahead of you tomorrow. "I'll cross that bridge when I get there."
