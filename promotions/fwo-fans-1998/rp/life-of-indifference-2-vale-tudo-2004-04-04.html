Life Of Indifference [2] Vale Tudo

4 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greed is a basic character flaw. There isn't a human being in the world that hasn't experienced it at some stage or another; some of us however are tempted by it whilst others are driven by it. It was the greed of my father that cost me an eye. But my own greed almost cost me much more than that. January 12th 1995. Rising to the top of the J-Pro wrestling ladder had been easier than anticipated for The Jersey Devil. He'd gone over in his first ever match against one of the fan favourites within the company and had continued to do nothing but rise within the ranks of the promotion. The fact that it'd been so easy however had failed to quench Kinkade's thirst to disprove those who thought his disability was a disadvantage, in J-Pro nobody knew about it therefore he was being pushed as any normal wrestler over with the fans would be. Despite being one of the top draws, Kellen Kinkade was bored. As he peered through the window of his apartment room that sat on the very top floor of the complex he was living in, he couldn't help but think that a future somewhere on the horizon would allow him to be the best at something without having to hide his secret. As he knocked back a pair of painkillers that came with the territory of working matches almost every night, he remembered the strange man that'd told him months ago that he was wasting his time in J-Pro and a fortune awaited him elsewhere. He reached into his pocket and produced the number that'd been handed to him, the fact that he'd kept it close to him at all times meant there was an air of inevitability about the call he was about to make, a few button presses later he was greeted with "What you want?" "You came to me a while ago and told me I could be making more money if I called you." Kinkade spoke down the phone as he scratched his chin. "Ahh American! I remember... what take you so long? You got no guts?" The man at the other end of the line was almost laughing the words. "I've got guts, now were you serious or not when you told me I could make some real money." Kinkade snapped back down the line, growing somewhat impatient with the man and his constant mocking vocal tone. "Fine, then you meet me at Matsumoto gym tomorrow morning... then we see how much guts you have, American!" The man laughed a final time before hanging up, leaving Kinkade to think about the choice he'd made. January 13th 1995. Kellen Kinkade looked up at the Matsumoto gym and considered turning around and walking away... it was a far cry from the shining example of profitable, Kinkade wondered how the man he'd spoken to was going to deliver him riches when he couldn't even afford to put together a have decent training complex. Kade wiped at the dust covered windows and tried to peer through but the filth was impenetrably thick, finally he made the decision to push open the door and enter and was greeted with the sight of yet more dust covered articles... a few punching bags, a set of weights and a ring in the middle of the room that looked as though it was about to fall apart. "So you turn up afterall." It was the strange little man once again, pacing across the room to stand before Kinkade. "I want to see Matsumoto." Kinkade demanded, only for the small man to smile. "I Matsumoto." Matsumoto grinned back. "Why am I not surprised?" Kinkade looked up and down at Matsumoto, a man missing several teeth, a man wearing not a single item of clothing that didn't have a hole in it and a man in dire need of a wash "So tell me buddy, how exactly do you plan to make me rich when you can't even afford a bath?" "Appearances deceiving, American. I probably buy and sell you ten times with money I make in a day." Matsumoto wasn't too impressed with Kellen's sniping. "Right." Kade sighed, still not buying into the fact that the man before him had any real source of income "So tell me Matsumoto, how exactly do I make these riches?" "Vale Tudo." Matsumoto was swift to reply. "I've heard of that... in fact I've seen a few tapes here and there, fighting right?" Kinkade nodded his head as though he was reassuring himself "I can do that." "Oh I see." Matsumoto replied "Well before fight I ask you take off sunglasses." "Why?" An almost cautious reply came from Kellen Kinkade, nobody in Japan knew about his secret and he intended to keep it that way. "I want to see what wrong with your eye." Was the swift reply Matsumoto delivered, so much for secrets... Kellen Kinkade took off his sunglasses to reveal the marble white glass eye he was wearing. "How do you know about this?" Kinkade demanded to know. "Because I watch you wrestle, you have blind spot more or less everywhere left of nose." Matsumoto quickly answered. "Bullsh-" Kinkade didn't finish what he was saying because Matsumoto quickly swatted him across the left side of his head, a swift motion that Kinkade didn't see coming. "Motherfucker!" Kinkade growled balling his fists. "I tell you." Matsumoto grinned again "But I like bad attitude, you need it if you want make it in Vale Tudo." Matsumoto pointed to the ring, where another Japanese individual almost as small as himself had appeared. Kinkade rubbed the side of his head and scowled before climbing through the ropes and doing a few stretched. "This Yoshihiro Sawai, he new to Vale Tudo... he your first fight." Matsumoto climbed into the ring to officiate as Kinkade looked up and down at Sawai and smirked. "You're kidding right, you don't seriously want me to fight this guy?" Kinkade reached over and whispered into Matsumoto's ear "Come on, I will kill this kid." Matsumoto smiled and marched over to Sawai, repeating the words into his ear as Yoshihiro uttered something angrily back at Matsumoto in Japanese. "He say he was going to go easy on you American, but now he change his mind." Matsumoto shook his head. "Fair enough." Kinkade shrugged "So what are the rules?" "Hahaha." Matsumoto slapped his knee "This not girly European Vale Tudo my friend... this Matsumoto Vale Tudo, only rule is stay awake if you want win." Matsumoto signalled for the beginning of the fight and after a couple of seconds circling, Kinkade decided he'd take his opponent down with a hold and make him submit. As he did so however he wished he'd taken in the wise words of Matsumoto when he said that appearances could deceive. Sawai palmed Kinkade's grappling hands away and snatched his ring finger, with one precise yank the sound of snapping followed by Kinkade's scream of agony filled the empty gym. Sawai released as Kinkade staggered away clutching his loosely hanging finger. "Son of a bitch." Kellen snarled as he turned and tried to smack Sawai with a hard right only to miss, he knew Sawai had gone behind him but before he could turn he felt the heel Yoshihiro's boot connect with the back of his skull. Kinkade fell to the canvas seeing stars, the next thing he would feel was Sawai's arms wrap around his neck in a rear naked choke... then came nothing but black. A splashing of ice-cold water from a bucket awoke Kinkade from his slumber; Matsumoto was stood over him with the trademark grin upon his face. "Still think you tough?" July 4th 1995. Kenshiro Ikegami was about to move in for the kill. Kellen Kinkade was down on one knee; blood had been streaming like tap water from a shot to his lip that'd quite literally burst it like a water balloon. His good eye was another hit or two away from being completely swollen shut and a knee strike early in the fight had left his nose bloodied. These were minor details when taking into account that his ribs were black and blue from kidney shots and heel kicks and his inner thigh has taken a sickening amount of sharp kicks. The crowd at ringside were growing restless with the American and his refusal to stay down, three minutes into the third and final round meant that if he wasn't knocked out or forced to submit most of them would go home empty handed. Ikegami made a move for the killer blow, an elbow with his full body weight behind it that was aimed right at Kellen's temple. To the surprise of Ikegami and the gamblers watching on however, Kinkade slipped aside and sent Kenshiro tumbling chest first into the ropes. As he returned he felt Kinkade's hand slide across the side of his face and fishhook him in the mouth, several seconds of tearing and struggling were brought to climax as Kinkade literally used the face of his opponent to whip him into the ropes. Ikegami returned to feel a kick land directly in his groin, doubling over as reaction became ultimately his last action of the fight as Kinkade swiftly raised a knee directly into his nose that broke it and knocked him unconscious upon impact. The official confirmed a Kinkade victory, July 4th was Independence Day for the rest of America but for Kinkade it was confirmation that he'd risen to the top of the Japanese Vale Tudo underground circuit in just seven months. His exit from the ring was quick as he hobbled to the locker rooms avoiding the furious crowd who'd just lost a lot of their bets, before he could patch himself up and hit the shower he was greeted by the promotion organizer Matsumoto. "Why did you put me up against Ikegami?" Kinkade slurred from his busted up and bloodied mouth "You know we're both at a level to kill each other, why didn't you just put me in there against some new blood and make some easy money on the betting?" "Because I want make sure you are ready." Matsumoto replied. "Ready for what?" Came the reply from Kade. "You will see."
