State of the Team

6 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The RV rocked back and forth as Mouse stepped inside, bearing 4 bulging take-out bags from the Wendy's they were parked outside of. He squeezed down the small hallway to the kitchen table, where Mick was using a laptop computer. "Dinner" Mouse said, handing over a double cheeseburger and fries. Mick reached for the food without looking away from his computer, and Mouse began to removed the 5 triple cheeseburgers and accompaning fries that was his meal. The two ate in silence for a moment, until Mouse decided to bring up a subject he had avoided for a while. "Mick... are we jobbers?" Mick looked up at that comment, and swallowed his food before answering. "What would make you ask that?" "Well," said Mouse, "we don't win, and it doesn't look like we'll win for a good long while. We get little reaction from the fans, beyond being freakish... nothing we do seems to get a response." Mick took abother bite of his burger as he mulled it over, "When I signed the contract, it never said anything about not winning. As far as I can tell, the roster pretty much makes up its own angles and storylines. So, if we want to win, we have to come up with it ourselves." Mouse was thoughtful at that comment, and took the time to finish his second burger before responding. "I want to win, Mick. I want fans to either like us or hate us, but at least have an opinion. When I watched wrestling on TV, I saw people cheer for the good guys, and boo the bad guys. With is, it's more like poeple don't care, like they go to the concession stands when we come out. I want to change that." "OK," said Mick, "so what do we do?" Mouse began to think like the entertainment promoter he used to be, albeit in a different sort of venue. He managed to finish off 2 more burgers during that time, while Mick waited patiently for an answer. He know Mouse was one to take his time, coming up with an idea after examining the problem from all angles. Mouse then spoke, "Well, first... we need entrance music. Everyone else has music, and it gets the fans in the mood for what's to come. We need something which will get the fans in the mood to see us." "How about-" "And NO 80's music!" interrupted Mouse, "I get enough of that in here." Mick smirked, balling up the foil that had wrapped the burger and tossing it playfully at his friend. "Not even Quiet Riot? "Come On Feel The Noise" would be a good song to-" "Mick," Mouse interruped again, "I hate to tell you this, but... 80's music sucks." Mick chuckled, "Ok, ok... no 80's music.. something more modern..." "And a finishing move," added Mouse, "everyone else has one, something that makes the crowd say "Wow!" A grand finale of some kind... like... I dunno... something big." Mick conceded that point as well, "Ok... we'll start working on it after dinner..." Both men returned to their food and finished in silence.
