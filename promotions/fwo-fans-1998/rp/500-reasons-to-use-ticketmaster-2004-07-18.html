500 Reasons To Use Ticketmaster

18 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
July 17th, 2004. 8:54PM "Five hundred bucks!? You've got to be friggin' kidding me man". A hooded individual yelled, obviously outraged by the price of scalped tickets. "That's a full weeks pay for Christ sake." "Look man... you said on the phone you wanted to buy the tickets..." "Yeah but... five hundred bucks!? You said two-fifty on the phone." "Supply and demand brotha." Replied the scalper, flashing the Meltdown ticket in front of his hooded companions face. "You either pay the five hundred, or I sell this puppy to someone who's not a cheap ass." "Cheap ass?" A shadowy veil, cast by the hood he was wearing, covered his face but you could tell he was gritting his teeth... in fact, you could hear it. He really had no other choice but to pay up, and that was making him very, very, mad. "You're lucky I took anger management dude... seriously." The scalper received his money, double counting it to make sure it added up to five hundred dollars. "Don't worry... It's all there dickhead." Added the hooded man. If he was dishing out a weeks pay for a ticket to an fWo pay-per view, he felt he reserved the right to insult the man who was selling it to him. "Nice doing business with you." The scalper replied, rolling the money into an unorganized ball before stuffing it into the pocket of his weathered looking jeans. "Did anybody ever tell you... you kind of look like that guy... you know... from the Asylum?" There was a momentary pause. "Yeah... I get that all the time. I think we might be related... er sumpthin'." "Ah, I getcha. Anyways... Take care." The transaction was complete, but it was truly only the beginning... The prequel. And on July 30th... the real story would begin.
