The One In South California - Part Four

3 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pages 52 to 68 of The One In South California. *** *** *** *** *** *** EXT. SIDEWALK-LATER Lowell and Nuñez walk along the sidewalk, several blocks from the bus crash that garnered the attention of every squad car in the area. NUÑEZ I ought to slap you fuckin' retarded. I told'ja we couldn't afford to draw attention to ourselves and you go ahead and crash a fuckin' bus on us. LOWELL In my own defense, I didn't crash the bus, that idiot bus driver did. It's not my fault he never learned to drive. NUÑEZ 'At's where ya wrong, Low. You crashed the bus the second you got up out yo seat and taunted that fuckbrain. Your whole fuckin' life is a bus crash. LOWELL Well excuse me. I wanted to get us there faster. I didn't think he was going to drive us off the fuckin' road, dude. I'm not a mindreader. NUÑEZ Goddamn right you're not. So maybe next time you should shut your fuckin' mouth, man. You're gonna put the squash on this whole deal. LOWELL I'm cool, man! No need to worry! Things are lookin' up for us, bro! I can feel it! They stop momentarily. NUÑEZ 'And what're we gon' do about a ride, nigga? LOWELL Cab it? NUÑEZ Oh right. With all that money we got. Let's see whatchu' got in those pockets'a yours. LOWELL Not a dime, dude. I'm broke as a joke. NUÑEZ No shit. What about that iPod you lifted? LOWELL I... I sorta... threw it out the window? NUÑEZ Now, why in the fuck would you go ahead and do a thing like that? The rage in Nuñez' eyes prompts Lowell to take a cautious step back. LOWELL It had to be done! That kid had the shittiest taste in music I'd ever seen! A crime against humanity. Seriously. Awful. NUÑEZ We were gonna sell the iPod, Low. We both agreed on that. That was money in the bank. LOWELL I guess I forgot. Sorry. NUÑEZ (Stepping toward Lowell) You're gonna forget alot more after I'm done bangin' your skull against this lamppost, motherfucker. Suddenly, Lowell sees it: A Ford dealership across the street. A sign reads "TEST DRIVE A FORD TODAY!" Lowell smiles mischieviously. LOWELL Save the bangin' for my mom, Nuney... I've got an idea. CUT TO: INT. HIGH SCHOOL BATHROOM Dean steps out of a stall, still zipping up his pants. Cassidy, out of breath and glistening, does the same, buttoning up her pants and messing with her hair. She reaches into her pocket and pulls out the packages of Kool-Aid she lifted and grabs a bottle of hydrogen peroxide from a small medicine cabinet in the corner with Band-Aids, tampons, etc. She proceeds to the sink, turning it on and dipping her head under. DEAN You know, there was a shower at my mom's house. Functional plumbing. Stuff. Dean walks out, just as a young girl named AMBER enters, who looks back at him in confusion as to why he was in the ladies' room. Cassidy is running the water in the sink and letting it accumulate while the girl looks to her. AMBER (Quiet and cautiously) H-hi. My name's Amber. Cassidy smiles as she pours some peroxide over her head and rubs it throughout. CASSIDY Hi! I'm Cassidy. AMBER Yeah... I'm new here. CASSIDY Oh, me too! AMBER Really?! That's such a relief, I'm so glad I'm not the only one. My dad used to work in Poughkeepsie, but they transferred him to--What are you doing to your hair? Cassidy rinses her head and then dumps the contents of the Kool-Aid packets into the water, mixing it up. CASSIDY Giving life some excitement! CUT TO: EXT. FRONT OF SCHOOL Dean makes his way out the front of the school and through the thinning lunch crowd, sitting down against a tree on the other side of the yard, far enough away from the students not to be noticed. A few feet to his left sits a STUDENT with messy hair scribbling into a notebook. Dean reaches into his pocket and pulls out the rolling papers, along with a dimebag of marijuana, and sets them on his lap. He expertly rolls a joint and then puts the pack of papers back into his pocket. He turns to the kid. DEAN What's your deal? The kid looks up from his notebook, confused. STUDENT Huh? DEAN Your girlfriend leave you or somethin'? Dean lights up a cigarette. STUDENT Not recently. DEAN I didn't ask when. She's got another guy? STUDENT What are you talking about? DEAN Listen, I see your face hidden in a notebook. I'm pretending to give a fuck. If you cut the bullshit, things will be a lot easier. From the looks of it, we have three options: 1. You lost a girlfriend. 2. You can't get a girlfriend. Or 3. You're gay and can't talk to anyone about it. STUDENT I don't understand what's making you say this. You don't even know me, there's no way you could accurately assume my situation and assess it with any kind of accuracy based on your snap judgement of my appearance. DEAN Oh Jesus Christ, another one of these motherfuckers. You're making me feel old right now, but if you wouldn't think so goddamn much... What is this generation, defined by your insecurity and neuroses? This is just... fuck this. Y'know, you seem to have a philosophical mind. I'm sure you've heard the phrase, "Sometimes a cig--" STUDENT Yes, yes. I know. Sometimes a cigar is just a cigar. DEAN Yeah? Well sometimes it doesn't fucking matter what the cigar is. That's what you don't know. He throws the joint into the student's lap. DEAN (CONT'D) You need this more than I do. Suddenly, Cassidy, with freshly-dyed deep, almost cartoonishly-red and maroon hair, pops up from behind Dean. CASSIDY Three old men were talking, okay? They start talking about how much their hands shook. The first old guy said, "My hands shake so bad, that when I shaved his morning, I cut my face." The second old bastard one-upped him like Mario on mushrooms and said, "My hands shake so bad, that when I trimmed my garden yesterday, I sliced all my flowers!" The third old man looks to them and said, "That's nothing, you guys! My hands shake so bad that when I took a piss yesterday, I came three times!" DEAN What the fuck does that mean? CASSIDY It made me think of you. CUT TO: INT. FORD DEALERSHIP-DAYTIME Lowell sits with one of the dealership's struggling SALESMEN, a balding, middle-aged guy desperate to make his quota for the month. Once finished with an information sheet, Lowell pushes it to the salesman and stands up, offering him his hand and giving that cheesy fake smile he's become so adept at. LOWELL So, it's all mine? SALESMAN Well, it will be once we've inked the deal. But for the next fifteen minutes, yes, consider it yours. Take good care of it, because if you take care of it, it'll take care of you. Remember that! LOWELL Keys? The salesman reaches into his desk drawer to retrieve a set of keys and hands them to Lowell. SALESMAN There you go! Oh, I remember my first car... It wasn't anything like what you're getting--which is a steal by the way. No, my first car was a junker. But it didn't matter how it looked, how it sounded, or how it drove, really; it signified my independence, my freedom. (Brief pause) In fact, I just bought a cherry-red Mustang a few weeks ago. Always wanted one and now I have it. Go me! But I needed it, y'know? The divorce took a lot out of me. Sorta zapped me of my zest for life, had me feeling pretty desperate and alone, if ya know what I mean. Took a while for me to be comfortable looking at porn again on the Internet, let alone date. Barb was a one-of-a-kind lady. You don't get a second chance at that, Mr.-- (Reads off the sheet) Bill Murray? Heh, would ya look at that! Bill Murray. Just like the actor! I have to admit I love his acting. I own What About Bob? on DVD and VHS. It's great. If I hadn't taken such a beating this month, I'd throw in the under coating for free. I can't, of course. Don't have that kinda clout. Not anymore, anyway. The boss has been riding me for turning in low numbers these past few quarters. But that's all about to change with this sale! I don't even have to talk the car up, the Crown Victoria is such a beautiful vehicle, you'll be convinced the second you put 'er in drive! Yup. I'll make a nice commission off this, pay off my VISA bill, toss a few bucks my ex's way to keep her off my back about the child support I owe her, and really, all should just sorta kinda work itself out! Things are about to get better for me! LOWELL Yeah, that's cool. So, how 'bout I go test-drive this sucka and see if it suits my free-wheelin', big pimpin' lifestyle, okay? SALESMAN Alright, let's go! Lowell begins to follow him out of the cubicle. LOWELL But I've gotta pee first. Bathroom? SALESMAN Right around the corner, the hallway next to the water cooler. LOWELL Cool. SALESMAN I'll be waiting right here! Lowell walks away. LOWELL Whatever. CUT TO: EXT. SIDEWALK-LOS FELIZ-MID AFTERNOON Dean and Cassidy walk side-by-side again, each carrying a bag. CASSIDY (Singing, loudly) TOO MANY TEARDROPS. FOR ONE HEART. TO-BE CRYIN'! YOU'RE GONNA CRY 96 TEARS! YOU'RE GONNA CRY 96 TEARS! A'RIGHT NOW DO-DO-DO-DO-DO-DO-DO-DO DO! Cassidy continues to sing the synthesizer line from "96 Tears" until her phone begins to buzz. She drops her bag in the street and stops dead in her tracks CASSIDY (CONT'D) LOWELL! Dean pulls the phone out of her hands, unwilling to risk her messing up an important call. DEAN We can't afford to fuck this up. (Speaking into the phone) Low, where're we meeting you? 346 White Birch.. Yeah, I know where it is. Cass and I are gonna go scout. How much longer until you're out here? Well shit, speed it the fuck up. Get to train-hopping. Dean SNAPS the phone shut and tosses it to Cassidy before proceeding to walk down the sidewalk. CASSIDY So we know where we're going, right? Of course we do, this is your home! Aw, we're in your home. ...Hey Dean, what's your problem right now? And don't you have something to say about my hair? DEAN Yeah, I do. We need to pick up a razor for you, my joint's got worse rugburn than your older sister's kneecaps. CASSIDY I don't get it. ...Oh. CUT TO: EXT. FORD DEALERSHIP-DAYTIME Nuñez stands next to a black Crown Victoria, peering in through the driver's side window, as Lowell confidently struts out of the building, obnoxiously smiling. LOWELL Guess who came through? That's right--me! NUÑEZ You're over 21, Low. That's about all it takes to test-drive a car. Lowell pushes past Nuñez and unlocks the driver's side door. NUÑEZ (CONT'D) Whoa, whoa, you ain't drivin'. LOWELL I'm the test-driver, Nuñez. It's my name on the info sheet on stupid guy's desk. Well, it's Bill Murray's name, but I was the clever motherfucker that thought to put that on there! I'm the one takin' heat if this thing gets scratched or someone steals it or something. NUÑEZ We're stealin' it, Lowell. We ain't bringin' it back. LOWELL That's true. But I still wanna drive. NUÑEZ Not a fucking chance. Nuñez takes the keys from Lowell. They both get in the car. Nunez starts the engine up. LOWELL Roomy. NUÑEZ Nigga's gon' be pissed when he realizes` we jacked his ride. LOWELL Crown Victorious-es-es are awful big and bulky. NUÑEZ First, it's Crown Victoria, not Crown Victorious. And two, why didn't you ask to test drive a Mustang or somethin'? LOWELL I wanted something that could hold a lot of guns and dead bodies. In case the shit hits the fan and you and I have to turn on the blood sprinkler and douse some suckaz like tru' LOCs. Naw' mean? Nunez sighs heavily. The Crown Victoria pulls out of the parking lot and continues on down the street. CUT TO: EXT. SIDEWALK-WHITE BIRCH ST.-LATER Dean and Cassidy begin to walk slowly down the street with an air of caution. DEAN We're gonna take this methodically. Don't just burst into this shit, we've gotta be tactful. Strategy, Cassidy. CASSIDY I'm totally smooth, I'm all Cleopatra Jones and stuff. DEAN More like Sweet Sweetback. CASSIDY Fightin' for all the brothas and sistas who had enough'a the Man. DEAN You scout around the right side of the house. I'll handle all the delicates. They approach the house, observing casually from the outside. Dean tucks their bags under one of the hedges, peers into the driveway. Noticing no car, he walks up to the garage door slowly and peeks in the window. Cassidy, meanwhile, looks around the back yard and sees no one. Dean straightens his clothes and hair up and knocks at the front door, prepared to feign propriety. He crosses his arms in front of himself, folding his hands in a proper fashion. When no one answers, he rings a doorbell and waits. No one. Pleased, he wanders around back and finds Cassidy. DEAN (CONT'D) Nothing? CASSIDY Empty as a Poison concert back here. DEAN Check the window latches. Dean marches around front, grabs the bags and brings them around the side of the house. Cassidy's onto the third window at this point, getting it to click open. CASSIDY Oooopen sesame! Ali Baba, your forty thieves have arrived. DEAN Up and in, babydoll. Cassidy pushes the window all the way open and gets a boost from Dean into the home. Dean tosses the duffel bags in and pulls himself up and in through the window. DEAN (CONT'D) Next step: open up your cell phone. Cassidy pulls it out and does so. DEAN (CONT'D) Delete all the videos of me while I'm sleeping, the photos of you posing in your underwear in fitting rooms at the mall, your shitty Pretenders ringtones, whatever. We need pictures of every potential hiding spot in this motherfucker. CASSIDY First of all, those pictures are because mirrors always fucking lie. And I don't have any Pretenders ringtones. DEAN Oh, sorry. I meant the Go-Gos. CASSIDY When are you going to just admit that you love the Go-Gos?! DEAN Around the same time you get something done right without being so goddamn irritating. I'm searching, you document. Cassidy whimpers and skips off into the other room while Dean starts overturning couch cushions. CUT TO: EXT. HIGHWAY The stolen Crown Victoria motors along the highway. INT. CROWN VICTORIA Lowell sits playing with the car's cigarette lighter, using it to scorch the surface of the glove compartment, chuckling like an idiot. Nuñez turns to him and grabs it out of his hand, stuffing it in his pocket and rolling down the windows on both sides of the vehicle. NUÑEZ (Shaking his head) And now it smells like burnt tires in here, Low. LOWELL I kinda like the smell of burnt tires. The house I grew up in was right next to this giant tire fire. The police weren't cool with it, but what the fuck were they gonna do? NUÑEZ Doesn't surprise me in the least. That's probably why you got an I.Q. of like 50, nigga. LOWELL Come on, Nuñez, I'm smart! Remember in prison I won that spelling bee. That rapist was pissed... but he was the one who misspelled BOUND as in TIED UP. You'd think he'd be an expert on it. Served him right! Lowell spots a hitchhiker on the side of the road a few hundred feet up ahead. LOWELL (CONT'D) Pull over, Nuñez, let's pick him up. NUÑEZ This some kinda fuckin' joke? We're drivin' a stolen car; we've got a deadline t'meet. The answers's no, motherfucker. LOWELL Come on! Maybe he's on the run from the cops, too, and we can exchange tips on how to evade them. NUÑEZ No. LOWELL Stop being such a fucking dick, Nuñez. Shit. NUÑEZ Arright, fine, we'll pick him up, but don't you fucking mention a thing about this car or where we're headed, or I might hafta toss both'a you out the door. EXT. HIGHWAY The car pulls over to the side of the road and the HITCHHIKER, dressed in an early-90s Toronto Raptors windbreaker, torn blue jeans and PUMA sneakers, climbs in the back. INT. CROWN VICTORIA HITCHHIKER I can't thank you guys enough for stopping. I've been standing on the side of the road with my thumb out for sixteen hours straight. Can't get a lift in this state to save my life. Well that is until you boys came along! The hitchhiker happily drums on the back of Lowell's seat. INT. HOME-346 WHITE BIRCH STREET Dean, stomping down the last few steps of the stairs, looks to Cassidy, who sits on the couch, watching television. He tosses the duffel bags onto the couch next to her. DEAN Not a speck, not a touch. Whole house is clean. CASSIDY I ate all the good stuff in the fridge. DEAN You weigh about 90 lbs. Where the fuck does the weight go? CASSIDY My huge ass. DEAN Wow. Good point, fattie. CASSIDY Yeah. Good thing you like fat asses. Cassidy grabs Dean's arm and pulls him toward her, looking to him with a sparkling of desire in her eyes. CUT TO: EXT. CITY The Crown Victoria sits in the middle of traffic, cars as far as the eye can see. It rolls a few feet only to stop again due to traffic congestion. INT. CROWN VICTORIA NUÑEZ This is fucked. We don't need this shit, Low. I told you this would happen. LOWELL Sorry, man, I got it mixed up with another route. Look, it's 5 o'clock, give it another 45 minutes and we should be cruisin' along just fine. Just wait and see. HITCHHIKER Man, are these seats leather? Like, I never noticed until just now, but wow, leather seats, that's classy, guys. Nuñez glares at the hitchhiker in the rear-view mirror, then at Lowell. NUÑEZ We don't have 45 minutes. LOWELL 45 minutes? You're telling me don't have 45 friggin' minutes to spare? NUÑEZ Thas' what I'm sayin'. HITCHHIKER And I was like, you better not, you better not! But she's a bitch, always has been, always will be, so long story short she bought a bag of Lays potato chips instead of the Scoops I originally asked her to pick up! And it was my five dollar bill, too! Does that make any sense? It wasn't like I was just sittin' around, watching the game, snackin' on whatever. I had the cheese grated and everything! The last piece of the puzzle, the Scoops, never came, so I ended up eating a plate full of marble cheese with my fingers like a fucking douchebag. I spent the rest of the night on the shitter. I don't understand mothers, man. They're such... I dunno. But whatever, right? What's done is done! NUÑEZ So where're we droppin' ya off, man? HITCHHIKER Oh, you know, wherever. NUÑEZ No, I don't, why don't you tell us? HITCHHIKER Well, where are you guys headed? NUÑEZ That ain't none'a your goddamn business. Now you better tell us right quick where you want to be dropped, or once we get goin' again I'm gonna get Low here to take the wheel and I'm gonna toss you out onto the street in the middle of traffic. Y'feel me? HITCHHIKER Yeah, man, for sure! No prob! Hey, either of you got a Plenty-O Fish account? On the Internet? It's great. I spend hours, literally hours, combin' chick's profe's-- that's what I call 'em--for bikini pics. One time I caught a glimpse of a vag. Unbeknownst to her, of course! Just a little upskirt action. Her business was out there for anyone, even some sick perv, to see. We've all been there though, am I right!? Our testes hangin' out of our shorts on a hot summer day, sweatin' all over our lawn chairs. I don't mind. I wish nudity was more accepted, y'know? Like we didn't have to hide our bodies. I'm not big on clothes, or shopping, or consumerism. I found this Raptors jacket hung up on a telephone wire six years ago. And you know what? Still fits to this day. And not only that, it looks great! Windbreakers are comin' back, man. They're on the rise, they're taking over. They break the wind, maaan, so if you're caught in, like, a windstorm, shit... nothin' to worry about. I dunno if it makes me more aerodynamic or what, but I feel like I can run faster.
