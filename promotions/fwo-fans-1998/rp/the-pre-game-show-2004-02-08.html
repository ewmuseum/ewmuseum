The Pre-Game Show

8 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Friday February 6th, 2004 Nova Scotia, Canada Brandon Roche looked into the mismatched eyes of his soon to be wife and they positively glowed. From the grin on her face, you would think that her life's dreams had come true. Brandon knew his had. This was the eve of their wedding day and, in less than twenty four hours, they would be man and wife. But first would come one of the longest nights of their lives. Not many things had been done traditionally in the courtship of these two long lost souls, but the one thing that would adhere to tradition was neither seeing the other until the wedding vows would be exchanged. That meant a night away. A sleepless one they both thought, restless with excitement how could the seconds move any faster? Take, for example, the fact that for the last ten minutes they have done nothing but lean on each, never speaking a word, simply staring. "Daaaaaaad... you and Amy've been out there for an hour. We're cold. Jarod wants to get pizza!" Sean's voice snapped the two out of their lingering looks and back to reality. Brandon huffed a bit, the cold forming clouds of his breath as he did. The two of them had walked away from Amy's parents' house for a bit of last minute privacy and left Sean, Jarod and Stephen in her mom's care. Her parents, having acclimated themselves to these ungodly temperatures were not known for their liberal use of the thermostat and Brandon's two were freezing. It had really only felt like a few minutes, lost in each other as they were. Now it was time to go and they really hadn't said much to the other. "I guess it's time, huh?" Brandon addressed Amy first before turning back to Sean quickly, "Alright buddy, I'll be there in two minutes...promise." "Oh my God..." For one of the first and only times in her life, Amy had absolutely no idea what to say. So accustomed to expressing herself easily, she very suddenly couldn't. "This is really happening, isn't it?" She was weak with nerves and clung against Brandon by the time that sentence was complete. "I love following tradition, even the rowdy parties were okay..." she winked, "But being apart tonight...I dunno." "Yeah... I'm scared shitless too, dear heart. It's okay." The warm smile washed over his face before Amy could even respond. "You have already done this before. I can't even imagine myself standing at the alter now. I never even...I didn't think this was ever coming." Well, she was right about one thing - In grungy jeans, a wallet chain and courduroy jacket she certainly didn't look the part of a woman who'd be in a gown the next day. "I'd do it right here, right now if you'd let me. I told you, all this last minute spazzing out wasn't neccesary on my part. You deserve it though. You deserve that and so much more. After tomorrow, I'll get to work on that 'so much more', even though I've got the rest of my life to do it, I want to get an early start." Amy shivered from excitement. The inhuman temperatures didn't bother her in the least - it was her nerves. She normally would've called for a stiff drink...not tonight, though. This wouldn't be ruined. "Okay. Tomorrow changes a lot, but nothing at the same time...y'know? I dunno. It'll just be awkward. Me in a dress, all of my friends and family there to no doubt embarass the hell out of me...at least your buddies are relatively tame. Well, except Lance...but in his case, at least he knows how stiff a punch I throw." "The, 'you in a dress' part is what's really getting to me." With that barely out of his mouth Brandon pulled Amy to him firmly. Without missing a step, the two kissed. Again second floated by, or was it minutes? Either way, Sean was calling out again before they knew it. Busted. They both pulled away, Brandon being Brandon...he blushed. As cold as the air was however it wasn't noticeable, he could feel it though as his face heated up. His smaller counterpart laughed aloud into the snow that'd began falling, tossing her head back as he still held arms halfway around her waist. "Busted." grinned the redhead, but she ended on a disappointed pout... "We've gotta go now, don't we?" Her pout mirrored Sean's, only her expression was the exact opposite of his hurried gestures. Deflated, but elated at the same time Brandon nodded, "Just think... this time tomorrow you'll be Mrs. Paragon... and I'll be Mr. Red...Raver..." Brandon, corny as ever, was the only one to laugh at his bad joke. "Seriously Amy...this time tomorrow, husband and wife. Forever and always. Heh...and you know something? Now that I say it like that, that doesn't seem long enough." "C'mon, no jokes tonight. Let's do this." Momentarily, Amy's eyes bugged. She laughed again, "Sorry, it's not a match. Let's go sleep 'n get married, bitch!" At that, she planted an 'encouraging' pat to Brandon's ass. In her own mind, Aimz'd be a great coach. She only hoped she was half as good a wife.
