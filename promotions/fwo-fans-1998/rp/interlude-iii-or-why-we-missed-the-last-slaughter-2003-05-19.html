Interlude III, or 'Why We Missed The Last Slaughter'

19 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Okay, so the Codemaster and the Fuse Bros. missed the final Slaughter entirely while a walking corpse managed to actually form a stable with two of the biggest idiots in the fWo, today. So sue them. There were a lot of factors and circumstances involved in their non-appearance. Not the least of which was the fact that Tyler and Conor Fuse seemed all too fascinated by the new game they had bought for their GameCube and spent a great deal of time trying to get that much further ahead in the Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker. All the while, the Codemaster was trying to teach them the ropes in Zelda games, of which the Fuses were not precisely used to. Whereas, the Codemaster, being the blackest brother in the Hyrule Kingdom, knew how to pop a cap in a Dodongo's ass, do a drive-by on a Stalfos Knight, and bust a Wall Master's ass. "Okay guys," the Codemaster said, pointing at the very first boss of the game, a lava scorpion, "I know this guy looks all mean and whatnot, but this sucker's about as fearsome as Pikachu. Now, all y'have to do is..." "Pikachu's quite fearsome." Tyler Fuse pointed out, while Conor Fuse, the one that wasn't playing at the moment, turned to the Codemaster. "I mean, yeah, he zaps the living heck out of people with his Pikachu Thunder Shock thingamajig," Conor Fuse said, "Then he flies away like a mystical faerie on his Ponyta, while everyone gets all afraid of the possibility that the powers of Ponyta's flame and the powers of Pikachu's electricity will be combined into a power far greater than any of us can imagine." There was an awkward pause. "I take back what I said last Wednesday. THAT was the stupidest thing I've ever heard." the Codemaster said. "You're just jealous `cause you can't catch a Ponyta." Conor Fuse said. Another awkward pause. "Why am I having this conversation with you, anyway?" the Codemaster asked, "I could probably whip you in a Pokemon battle like a red-headed stepchild caught stealing in a 7-11 in the rough part of Los Angeles, but the point is, of course, that Tyler over there is getting his ass whipped by that there lava demon." "No... no, I think I've go--" Tyler started, just before Link died on him, "Crap." "Great. Now look what you made me do." the Codemaster said, throwing his hands up in the air. "Me? You're the one who inflicted this Hyrule virus upon us! We should be blaming YOU!" Conor Fuse shouted, pointing accusingly at the Codemaster. "You really want that Pokemon battle, don't you?" "I challenge you!" "Bring it." Two hours and a Slaughter later... "Okay, so the score is... me, twenty-six... you... zero." the Codemaster said, putting another stroke into his scorecard that he had made about fourteen victories ago. "Dammit!" Conor Fuse said, throwing down his Game Boy Pocket in frustration, "I thought Pikachu was supposed to always win! That's what the cartoon taught me!" The Codemaster laughed and decided to leave the room, while Tyler Fuse glared at Conor Fuse in confusion. Conor Fuse saw this, and decided to point at his brother. "Well, it did!"
