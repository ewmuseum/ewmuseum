On the horizon.

30 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With Amy upstairs recuperating as best she could Brandon found himself in an odd place. It was actually quiet in the house for the first time in a week or two. With Ana coming by every so often to gather more of her things and the kids adjusting to the new living conditions it had been customary for a low level of noise to exist in the household. Now, the kids were asleep and either Amy was asleep or on the phone with Ivy again. It was almost too quiet actually. This much calm could lead a man to think. And that was exactly what Brandon Roche found himself doing. Thinking. In the business he had chosen as his profession and the tumultuous life he had led, thinking wasn't often allowed. If you stopped to ponder an event, you were normally washed away in its tide. The last six years had been unbelievable at best, a soap opera really. Shootings, kidnappings, arson, common assault, attempted murder. No wonder his family had suffered so much. How could he expect anyone to live through those things and not be adversely affected? But here he was, doing the same thing again, forming a relationship with Amy Campbell, and bringing her into the life of Brand Frontier. How could he expect her to fair better than Ana had? He did have a suspicion though that Amy was cut from the same cloth as he. The never say die attitude that had led her to the fWo Hardcore Championship was the same factor that would probably best help her survive his life. It was sad to think of it that way, but true nonetheless. Giving credit where credit was due Ana had tried. They met in high school and immediately the bond formed. From there she was by his side through the murders and the kidnappings and everything else. He never really understood where things went wrong, but they did, wrong in every sense of the word. Marriage vows were broken and though he blamed them both he could never hate Ana, Creid on the other hand... he despised. Maybe it wasn't rational, sometimes that didn't matter. He was human after all, even though he was often portrayed as something more. At the onset of it all there loomed a jealousy of skill, a misunderstanding of intentions. It ended with all out war being declared. A war six years in the making, a war unfinished. Now Brandon found himself a one-year veteran of the Fan's Wrestling Organization, and professionally at the top of his game. He wasn't happy though, not with wrestling at least. He was disenfranchised with the way things had been on a downward spiral for so long he could barely remember a time when things didn't seem to have a black cloud over them. Three and a half years ago alongside The Last Knight, Goliath and The Phantom he had made a difference. It was as if a golden age had washed over the N.W.A. The Trinity had rid the federation of the likes of Sabian Lords, The Saint and Hunter Blackwell. All was good... then he heard rumblings of the reformation of The Brotherhood. Vic Creed, alongside Justin Lee had begun to cut a swath of destruction in a regional promotion and Brand Frontier was off again, not being able to simply enjoy the fruits of his labor. From there he had tracked Creed to the first Survivor contest, missing the deadline for applying by two days. He wasn't of the mind that he would have surely been picked, no, for the roster that entered the contest was superb indeed, it just irked at the back of his mind that he had the chance and missed it. Digressing in his own thoughts Brandon absently shook his head as he lay back against the cool leather of the sofa. He knew that the time grew near. He had so much on his plate now that it would make sense to simply wait, but he had waited for so long. Even with his mother in failing health, even with something being off center about Amy lately, all of it didn't deter him from thinking about the monster that ruined his life. Brandon also knew that being consumed like this was what led to his life crumbling around him in the first place. Ana, as confused as she was, wasn't entirely off her mark when she decided to get out when the getting was good. Brandon hadn't actually made the most intelligent decisions in their time together.
