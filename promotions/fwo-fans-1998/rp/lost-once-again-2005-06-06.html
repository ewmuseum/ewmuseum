Lost...once again.

6 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Listen Craig, I don't know how to say this...but I think you really need to get out of here." It was only a half past nine in the moring at the Chronos residence as Ric laid on his couch watching highlights on Sportscenter. Last week, he was finally released from the hospital although he was still heavily bandaged due to his grotesque encounter with the man once known as Ryan Moore at Cyberslam 8. Fallen. The last seconds of his existence still burned inside the synapses of Craig Miles' memory, but more importantly...he almost lost a friend because he didn't see the setup. Ryan Moore (Fallen) was one of the names given to Craig Miles on a list by Silver Fox. The list on the back of a resignation Craig Miles tried to give Silver Fox during the winter holiday season of 2003. Just before Ryan Moore murdered Neo, Samuel Smooth, Twitch and almost taking the lives of two notorious wrestlers -- Spyder and the Flying Frenchie. "You've been getting real quiet lately," Ric hadn't actually stopped talking as Miles leaned back in the living room recliner watching Detroit Piston players talk about the impending game 7. "I mean don't get me wrong, I appreciate all the help you've given me for the last two weeks...but honestly, Craig...you haven't missed a day with me. I think I'm spending more time with you than Lyra and that's...just wrong." Chronos shook his bandaged head as it seemed like Miles didn't even hear him. "You there Captain? Hello?" The first week Craig seemed like his normal self, but lately it was becoming painfully obvious something was amiss. Craig smelled...well...normal. He didn't talk as much... ...it seemed as if he was constantly thinking internally.. Ric wondered if it had to do with the story told to him during one of the first nights in the hospital. Craig broke down and came clean about his relationship with Fox, how it tied to the Fifth Seal...and how that never should've happened due to some unknown man named "John Smith" was the real enemy. The same "John Smith" that Miles claimed to have helped Fallen escape, return...and almost kill Chronos. Then again, whenever Smith came up between them -- the old vigor in Craig returned. Something finally clicked. "You're thinking about what you did to Fallen, aren't you?" "Everyday." Finally a breakthrough, if only one word. "Craig you never had a choice. You did the right thing. I would've done the same." "Yeah, well that's what I thought would happen." Ric was taken aback for a moment, the comment seemed to imply Miles blamed him. Craig sighed and looked up towards the ceiling, "I'm sorry, Ric. That wasn't fair. I wouldn't take back what I did for anything...I know it was the right thing. But inside Ric...inside...with what went down, I lost something inside." "Well, you're not going to find it here...not with me looking like this," Ric replied with a smirk. The silver sunglassed Dean of Thermodynamics lowered his head with a smirk, "You know...when you're right...you're right. And you...you're always right." Chronos laughed, Miles and him must've watched Spaceballs fifty times on cable the past week. Miles stood up out of the recliner, "Y'know what...maybe seeing some of the students will help me find whatever it is I'm looking for," Miles walked over to Ric exchanging a fist pound. "Be cool." "You know it." Miles turned around and exited the living room just as Lyra Chronos entered with bagels and coffee. "Dear God, you're missing breakfast? I thought we wouldn't need to have children with as much time we're looking after you." Miles laughed, exiting without a word. Lyra placed the food on the coffee table and cuddled up next to her husband. "Ric, don't think this is weird...but the fact I can smell Craig's laundry detergent really frightens me." "I miss the stench too babe."
