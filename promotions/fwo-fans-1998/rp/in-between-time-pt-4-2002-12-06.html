In Between Time(pt. 4)

6 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*** In Between Time *** Pt. 4 It had been an eventful day of training, Lucky thought, as he stepped outside the cab, after receiving his change from the driver. He was tired ... dog tired. He and Vaughn([name withheld] - Former XWL owner who gave Trip & Lucky their start in the wrestling business.) had went over everything, especially the basics. How many times can a guy practice a collar- and- elbow tie up before he had it down? Vaughn was a perfectionist in the truest sense, and he had made, turning Lucky into a successful superstar his top priority. THAT was good for Luck in the sense that, when it was all said and done, he'd know pretty much, everything there is to know about being a wrestler ... something most 'big men' couldn't say. It was bad because ... he came home sore every night. And, a certain secret caused him to be weaker than he normally was. As he exhaled, he watched the fog blow out of his nose ... but, immediately turned his attention to something else. "Hey! ...I guess it's my... 'lucky' day, eh?" Michelle rolled her eyes, but couldn't help but smile as Lucky ran in her direction. "Shannon, right?" "That'd be me. And, if I must say so myself ... you are one very blessed lady." She began to laugh. "And, how is that?" "Because, I was just getting ready to grab a bite to eat, all by my lonesome ... but, since fate brought us together ... I guess you can come with me. You've gotta come, M ... you can't play with fate." "It's Michelle. And, I can't ... not tonight. My son's babysitter doesn't do too much overtime. She's a mean old lady ... but, I have to respect her wishes, you understand, right?" "He can come, too." Lucky shot back. She raised her eyebrows, almost impressed by his persistence. "I'm gonna pass..." "Why?" "Because ... we hardly know each other!" "I'm Shannon, you're Michelle, remember? We did this earlier this morning! Besides ..." Lucky glanced down at his pants, Michelle knew where he was going and immediately cut him off. "So what does THAT mean? For your information ... I needed the money. Times have been hard, and people aren't too keen on hiring single black women without college degrees, so don't judge me!" "I didn't judge ... I just wanted to take you out. You and your so--" "Brian ... his name's Brian. And, you'd better be nice to him." She playfully made a fist and pointed it in Lucky's direction. Essentially, this was a bad move, as Lucky had plans already to go visit a bar or two with Triple X. But, as he looked at the stunningly beautiful woman lead him to her apartment ... he just couldn't resist. ------ "So I guess the cat's out of the bag, eh, Max? Well... atleast part of it. For the unbelievers... the people at home who couldn't bring themselves to picture me handling myself... saw me handle you. Say what you want, punk... I'm sure there are about ten people who buy your garbage. Probably even a few more, who still can't sleep at night, with your words echoing throughout their house or apartment walls. I'm sure of it. But, I'm also sure that none of those people..." Sean 'Triple X' Stevens sat on a tall counter- chair, backwards, tapping his right hand on the head rest every two- or- three seconds, or so. His hair was tied back into a ponytail... and, he was sporting a freshly shaven face, that smelled the part. He glanced down at nothing worth mentioning for a moment, then looked back up at the camera. "... Not one of those people are ME. And, your response won't surprise me. For someone with no schtick... someone so real... you've certainly managed to let whatever you're doing get real old. Real fast. You'll get in front of a camera... smile... tell me how foolish I am because I think the way I think. Then, you'll tell me that you have something in store for me. Something I won't see coming. Something that'll change my life, and that I'd be an idiot to think otherwise. Well, I guess I'm an idiot then. Because I honestly believe that what I've seen, not just the little that you've done to me, but your real past... is all there is to you, Max. You're a punk. You have to use schemes and plots to get inside your opponents head, not because you're dim- witted, like *I* think. ...or because you find pleasure in torturing souls, like you want us to think... No. More along the lines of... that's all you've got." "Well, let me tell you, just like I showed you, bitch... What you have in store for me, isn't enough to get rid of me. You say you'll expose the real me? ...well, guess what, Max? I'm here. I was in the ring with the real you, and I smacked your reality depraved a$$ twelve times with a steel chair because... I... could. You wanna do something about it? Try it. Don't take the route you took with your brother, Max. Don't just tell me. Don't sit on your stool, in front of your camera, and smile at me all day, like some f#cking queer. You think you could possibly muster up enough energy to outlast me? ...huh? Do you think you've been through something that actually makes you tougher than me? Prove it. Because, if you think being demented, and full of evil ideas is enough to get you by... rest assured... you will fail and fail miserably." "Not only am I the greatest, Max... I'm also a man. I have pride. And, I will never... ever... let somebody like you, disrespect me in the ways that you have and get away with it. Oh, and I've heard the ramblings... I know what people think. They think that I'm shook because you kept getting up... that on Showtime, I finally realized that I can't beat you. That I've met my match. ...not really. At UltraShowtime, I met a man who refused to stay down... a man that kept getting back up..." He glanced down at his t-shirt, it said: "The Triple X Show" on the front... and, it was something created from another one of his catch phrases. 'Max' outta get a kick out of THIS', he thought. "I saw a man who kept getting up... only to get his punk... ass... knocked back down. And, I know... I know... I didn't finish the job. I didn't keep you down. Well, in time... that'll change. Just like people's opinion of you. Just like... your facial structure. Just like... your apparent love for f#cking with people who had nothing to do with anything involving you." "You picked the wrong person this time, buddy... but, why should *I* expect you to realize that now? You're too stubborn. Too determined to prove me wrong. Well... not *MY* funeral." ------ A couple of minutes afterwards. Sean didn't even bother to review his promo ... He wasn't interested in gauging every aspect of it, in fear of imperfection. Being picked apart and lied on wasn't his concern ... it's not like he had to wrestle Scott Johnson, or anything. Then again ... as much as Johnson and Dallas Winston mentioned his name in the past week, or so ... you'd think he was lined up to face him. Sean smiled at the thought. "Guys ... keep it neat. I'm about to get out of these clothes, and go out. Don't break anything, and if a tall guy walks in ... wait ... you remember Luck, right?" A couple of the guys nodded. "Yeah, tell him I'll be ready in about ten minutes." Stevens prepared to leave the 'living room' area ... but, forgot to turn off his television. On the screen, was a close- up of Max Blackshire in the VCR's pause function. Normally, while cutting a promo on a certain opponent, he'd keep his face on the screen so that his train of thought would remain intact. Today would be no different. He left the living room, in search of a bathroom, as the camera crew scattered around, gathering utensils ... almost, immediately after ... Luck entered. "What's going on guys?" he asked, noticing about seven people too many exiting his best friend's apartment. One of the cameramen looked up, "Oh, you're Lucky." He extended his hand. "We haven't met, but I'm familiar with your work. Nice to meet you." Lucky extended his as well. "Sean asked me to tell you that he'd be ready in about ten minutes, or something like that. So, yeah... umm... I guess you're supposed to wait for him... or something." It was a severe case of bad timing ... Lucky wanted to talk to his best friend ... hang with him like they always did when they were younger. He had so much to tell him. About his in- ring growth. About his plans to re- join the ranks of the wrestling world. And, about the beautiful hooker he met, who actually was turning out to be more classy than he thought. But, as he looked at his watch, he realized he couldn't wait ... so he grabbed a piece of paper and began writing. 'Sean, something important came up ... I'll give you the details when I get back. Later, Luck' was all he had time to say, but deep down ... he knew, Sean would get it, and wouldn't be upset that he was stood up. Lucky walked the note over to the living room, with aspirations of placing it on the small coffee table in front of the television set ... ... that's when he noticed ... Him. He didn't know his name ... knew nothing about him, really. But, he knew he had to be no good. He was on Sean's television screen, which meant Stevens had just finished bashing him. But, something was ... different. He didn't look like your average, everyday, wrestler. He seemed a bit more ... serious. A tad bit more ... focused. ...evil, maybe? Lucky stared at his face ... and, the nameplate underneath him ... 'Max Blackshire' it said. "Max... Blackshire. I'll have to remember that." He exited the apartment, in route to his hot date. ------ Triple X walked out of the bathroom clad in a brand new leather jacket, Ivy had purchased for him a week ago, hair pony- tailed, and cleanly shaved. He was ready ... to hang out. To enjoy himself. To officially reunite, with night life. That is ... until he saw it. The note. The note that Lucky left for him. He picked the piece of paper up, and gave it the once over, frowning a bit once he was finished. "Damn ... maybe next time." He noticed the face on the television as he almost missed it again, turning away from it. His facial expression changed as he looked at his adversary, but returned back to normal as he turned the television off. "Not tonight, Max ... Not tonight."
