Interim

23 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The sound of a child's music box echo's about an empty room, slowly drawing Wippit from the darkness of dispair. He opened his eyes and the sound of hundreds of people talking crashed in on him, drawing a gasp from the sudden pain in his head. He sat up to a most pecular site. He was in a large church, filled with people in formal eveningwear - tuxes for the men, gowns for the women. The initial roar of sound faded to the murmer of several conversations, with the music box tinkling away in the background. Wippit struggled to his feet and tried to grasp what he was seeing. It seems that every person in the room was wearing a rubber novelty mask of a farm animal. He saw cows, pigs, goats, chickens... Shaking his head, he took a step forward, and the room began to spin. He staggered, and then his attention was drawn to the altar, in front of which was a coffen, with the lid raised. It began to draw him like iron filings to a magnet, and he stubled his way down the aisle. Halfway there, and without warning, he was surrounded by these strange people, pressing in on him from all sides. He pushed his way through even as they tried to block his progress, and the murmur of conversations had changed into laughter. They were laughing at him these animal heads! The room began to spin around and around, and Wippit lost his footing and crashed to the ground. Grimly, determined, he grabbed a nearby pew and pulled himself up, but the people had changed. Their clothes were the same, the laughter just as shrill and demeaning, but the animals masks were gone, replaced by wrestler masks. Jon Crisps, Donvan Winters', Aimz's, all manner of cheap representations. And not just fWo, but others as well - Jesus St Crux, Ivan Stanislav, Sterling Silver, Jonathan Conspiracy... their voices began to merge into one pulsating sound, HA HA HA HA HA HA HA... Wippit shoved some people aside, and with moving was suddenly at the coffin. He looked in, and saw himself, hands clasped over his chest, eyes closed, and not breathing. He was dressed in his ring outfit... and he was dead. Wippit looked down at his own corpse. The laughing sound changed, and the room seemed to shimmer in his eyes. The lights wavered as if underwater, and the pulsing sound he heard was KILL KILL KILL KILL KILL. He spun around, the room seeming to warp before his eyes, and then he focused on one who stood alone, mere feet from him. Dressed as the rest, and wearing the rubber face of LLB. This lone person suddenly raised his right hand, which held a 9mm pistol. Without conscious thought, acting on instict, Wippit suddenly shouted at the top of his lungs, "OBJECTION!!!" The room went silent, but for the heavy breathing from Wip. The smell of fear filled his nostrils, sweat beaded on his forehead, as this strange encounter took place. For what seemed like an eternity, time didn't move. And then a voice thundered through the church. A thousand people at once. A god. Eternity itself speaking. "OVERRULED!!!"
