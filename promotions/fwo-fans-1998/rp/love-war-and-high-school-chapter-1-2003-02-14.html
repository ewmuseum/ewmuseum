Love, War, and High School- Chapter 1

14 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Another day. The sun rised on the morning of Janaury 3rd, 1995. It was a typical day for most, not a day most people would be excited about. But Matthew Vorankias was not classified in the category of "most people". For the 16-year old sophmore (his birthday being in October making him miss the cut-off date) greeted each and every day as another chance to excel- another chance to greet the world and let it tell him how great he was. Sure, there were plenty of things that were working against him when he was born. He was found as an abaondoned baby, with no clues to his idenity or birth parents- except for one. A black blanket that was wrapped around him, with a red half-sun drawn onto it. However, Matthew had the luck to end up in a geniunley loving home with foster parents who cared about him as if he was their own child. With this enviorment, Matthew was able to excel and soon discovered his many physical and mental gifts. Throughout his school years, Matthew was always at the top of the class. His Grade Point Average was nearly perfect, he played soccer as a star foward, and he was a great writer to boot. Matthew had everything. Or so he thought. So, another day. He stood outside on the curb, waiting the arrival of his bus. He took a moment to look back at the house behind him- a blue, decent-sized house with a pair of cars in the garage. He smiled when his foster parents waved at him, and then turned back to wait for the bus. Matthew was athletic and blessed with naturally good looks. His short blonde hair was a bit messy-looking today, but the spikey look he usually had made him look even cuter to the girls. He wore a white undershirt underneath the school uniform- a vest with the emblem of the school on it and black cargo pants. All in all, Matthew was well-prepared to return to his domain on the first day back since Winter Break. The bus to the Jefferson Private Institute for Gifted Children had finally arrived. It was Matthew's tenth year on the large campus, which ran all the way from Kindergarten through all four years of High School, but he didn't mind it. He did, however, mind the lateness of the bus. "Only three minutes late." Matthew muttered. The door opened as Matthew climbed onto the bus. He made his way past various students, most waving hi to him, before taking a seat in the middle on the right side, an empty two-seater. He looked out through the window as the schoolbus began to pick up speed as it drove away from his house. By the time it reached the third stop since he was picked up, Matthew was wishing he left in a less populated area than San Fransisco, California. But then, he stopped thinking. Because she had just walked onto the bus. Long, red hair that seemed to sparkle as it swayed gently in the wind. Beautiful brown eyes. A face that looked like it should belong to a model. The female version of the school uniform- a white dress shirt with the school emblem and a checkered skirt- looked positivley ravishing on her. Matthew had no idea who she was, but whoever she was, she had certainly grabbed his attention. She walked down the aisleway of the bus, and took a seat in an empty three-seater. Right next to Matthew's seat. "Ummm....hello." Matthew managed to get out. The girl blushed, obviously shy, and looked down at the floor. "Hi." she said uneasily. The uneasiness in her voice made Matthew think he was being a bother, so he turned his head away. Feeling guilty watching him do so, she spoke up again. "I don't mean to be rude." she said, apologzing. "I'm sorry. It's just that I'm new here, this is my first day, and I don't know anyone yet." Matthew smiled, and turned back to face her. "Well, now you know me." he said. "I'm Matthew." "My name's Amy. Pleased to meet you." Amy stuck out her hand, and Matthew took it. For a brief moment, they looked into each other's eyes.... .....and then recoiled, letting each other go and turning to their respective windows, embarrased. She looked down at the floor again, obviously embarrased, before wondering what Matthew was thinking. And Matthew.....well, he was wondering what Amy was thinking. And as that bus continued onward on that Janaury morning, one thing was for certain. Neither Matthew, nor Amy, would ever be the same again.
