Eras: Past Revisited

9 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Soundtrack: "In This Diary" by The Ataris Series: Eras (2/4) Kevin sat down at the small desk in the hotel room. Typing on his new IBM laptop he wrote an eMail to his friend Mitch who still lived in Ohio. His fingers clicked away at the docked keyboard as he sprawled his thoughts out across a blank screen. "Mitch, Well, here I am. I am writing you right now from some hotel someplace. I guess last time we talked I had just gotten on the plane to fly to Europe, and now I am touring with the fWo. I guess the biggest news is that I got a contract! I dunno that that is particularly significant. Things are pretty neat here in the fWo. Very organized, and much more... well I don't know. There just is an air of confidence about the place. Being in the same room as guys like Eli Flair and Silver Fox. These are the kinds of guys I want to be you know? Guys my father would admire because they worked their way up the ladder to get where they are today. At the same time for as much of an organized chaos feel it has, I still feel like an outsider. Haven't really made any friends yet, but I haven't really gotten much of a chance too. I guess that comes with time, but I wish Dillon hadn't taken off so early. He said he may be back, but who knows with that guy. He said he is taking a break and then he said he is heading to Japan. He is likely to stay there forever! Haha. I've also been talking to Jen again lately. She seems really happy, but I fucked it up. I fucked it up bad. I missed my chance, you already know that though. I dunno. It's sort of pathetic you know? But I seriously do miss her. I just can't think about it. It gets me all stressed and messed up. I gotta focus on the positive you know? Keep things good, solid. Keep it up so I can do well here. I guess I have some more matches coming up, but I am not sure. I'll eMail you my schedules and stuff once I get them. I'll also send you my new address, as I will be moving to a real place at some point if we get back to the US. Anyway, hit me back sometime. -Kev. Kevin sent the mail, and folded up his laptop. He sighed as he stared out the window, looking at the stars of the night. He missed home, but he needed this. Needed a future. He couldn't live in the past forever, and it was time to move on. To finally go forward and do what he needed to do for himself. He had finally gotten where he had wanted, but the question was now, would it be worth it? Or would he just end up reminiscing of the times when he was still coming up? So many questions...
