Connections - ...And An Abruptly Ending Reunion

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Amy Campbell seemed to be the perfect image of 'abnormally normal'. Her hair shone a fiery red right alongside the changing leaves of the early Autumn afternoon, to which she was exposed slightly more than she might have liked. She'd wrapped her small treasure inside a worn Shai Hulud hoodie, leaving the redhead in a quickly cooling September breeze wearing no more than a black babydoll t-shirt and loosely fitting jeans that hung low on her hips - not only exposing a chilly section of a well-toned stomach, but a tiny army of intricately placed tattoos. She'd been known since high school as an exceptionally talented artist, and many in her elder generations claimed it to be a pity that the only way she'd display her skill was through designing her own little 'mutilations'. Worse yet, the girl piddled away her life in sports entertainment...quite possibly, so they say, the most barbaric and idiotic thing on television. Indeed, Amy wasn't in the highest of regards to the 'wise' population who knew her but, to the peers and younger groups of her home, she was a friend, a confidant and, moreover, a young woman to be respected. In just a few years, she'd gone from constant drugs, alcohol and partying - not to mention romancing almost anyone who'd give her a smile, and many did - to married life...fully-equipped with the career, future and loving relationship they all wanted when it came to the bottom line. 'And here she is,' Claire thought, 'Perfect as ever.' It was true; Nothing seemed to age Amy. As Claire - who was attractve in her own respect with short, tussled black hair and boyish yet delicate features that left little to be desired - watched and grinned in the redhead's direction, she could easily see that Campbell was as beautiful as ever. Whether Amy recognized it or not, her husband and most of the planet did: She was gorgeous. Whether you liked redheads, punky style, an attitude fitting the hair color or simply natural and classic beauty, you could find it in Amy. Only a second had passed and Claire had gone un-noticed, so she continued to watch her now grown-up friend and, indeed, it was a surprise to find her with a little girl on her shoulders. They were walking along the waterfront, laughing together as Amy occasionally jumped or dodged around to bounce the jubilant toddler. Her pin and patch-filled bag had been traded in for a purse of baby clothes and healthy children's snacks, it seemed, and her face - normally wrought with some fashioned pout or snarl - was warm, calming...content. She was so different, in fact, that Claire took a solid minute to discern whether or not it was a likely match. That woman could've easily been just a pretty face like Amy's...but it wasn't. It was the real deal, having gone through what seemed to be an overnight transformation that spanned the two years within which the girls had fallen a bit too out of touch. She slipped out of her Audi and set out on a sprint to the end of the wharf, where Mrs. Campbell-Roche (and, God, did it ever feel odd to connect her name to a man's) was just sitting down with the treasure she'd wrapped in her sweater when Claire first recognized her. That treasure, of course, being Scerinn...Davis. Amy's only child of her own blood,who just happened to share her old OSW coworker's genes to boot. Scerinn saw the stranger approach, but couldn't so much as coo before Claire had arms around Amy from behind.... Amy instantly recognized the embrace and, instead of using the sudden defense tactics Claire feared, leaned back against her affectionate friend. Words weren't necessary at that moment...except to Scerinn. "Last time I saw you," Claire's warm voice stretched out to the toddler, "You were in your momma's belly." While the little one regarded Miller with a shyness and childish skepticism, Amy just smiled and closed her eyes to enjoy the hug. "It's too good to see you again." "You too," smiled Claire, "But you haven't even turned around t' look, Ames." Truthfully, she didn't have to. But, when Amy wriggled from the embrace and opened her eyes to turn around, her smile grew ten times warmer than before. It was incredible to see her old friend and the warmth made her miss Claire sorely, despite her being right there, for all their time apart. All over again, they were reliving the love shared over so many years...it was enough to bring even the toughest little redhead halfway to tears. She adored everything about Claire Miller - from the carefree and loving fevor she lived every day with down to the gorgeously detached expression almost constantly adorning her pretty features. "So," began the redhead, "What's going on? It's been too long. How's work?" Claire chuckled. "There isn't any. It bites to have an accounting degree and no damned job. University was one massive waste." With Aimz, things happened fast. She never hesitated to change something when the opportunity arose and, as an aside, didn't realize that Claire knew that fact and intentionally planted that tiny seed of unemployment. "Have you thought'a hitting me up for work? I mean, Brandon's got a company..." Her raven-haired friend raised an eyebrow and smiled as if she'd just hit the jackpot. "C'mon," she said to that friend and her daughter, "Let's all get something to eat...we can talk this over." Unknown to her, Amy had just put her marriage in the midst of more confusion, delusion and general madness...once more. Once more, she'd brought a new burden.
