Beware of....

7 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Directly after Triple X/Zero The arena was still buzzing, as several fans sat in stunned silence. It was amazing actually... how a situation can play with your emotions. One minute... you're standing on you feet, jumping up and down in excitement... the next... you're two seconds away from crying in complete disappointment. Crying because... you wanted something to go one way so badly... and, it didn't. Crying because, you had expectations that weren't met. Tokyo Japan was filled with Zero fans. That fact became as clear as crystal the moment his music hit and he stepped through the curtain. The noise level was deafening. The "Zero" chants shook the rafters... and, fans began high-fiving,hugging, smiling, and rejoicing from what had to be a sure Zero victory. ...then Triple X beat him... Sean "triple X" Stevens nervously exited the ring underneath the bottom rope, looking back occasionally as he walked at an extremely fast pace up the ramp. His face, still in shock as he ran his fingers through his head, before turning around, confidentally thrusting his arms in the air - trying desperately to exude a charismatic aura. Before stepping through the curtain... he looked down at the ring once more. Zero hadn't quite gotten to his feet just yet... that was all he needed to see... it gave him enough time to disappear behind the curtain, without having to worry about him following him, in an attempt to seal the deal. "Lint- f#cking- Douglas," Sean thought as he stepped through the curtain, entering the backstage area. Immediately, he was bombarded by several media and industry types, with there shiny spotlights and fancy cameras. They all wanted to know how he felt... they were all believers now. For nearly two months they were looking to doubt his abilities... but, victory- after- victory- after- victory made it impossible. "Sean... how does it feel beating a cult hero like Zero in his first match back? ... is your body holding up? ... Was this one of your toughest opponents to date?" one reporter asked. Sean simply ignored him, his eyes on the task at hand - getting to his dressing room, and to an area where Zero couldn't find him. "Sean... Sean," another reporter shouted. "How does it feel to be outdone? Zero had more fans chanting for him by far. It was obvious the fans wanted to see you annihalated." That hit a nerve. Sean stopped dead in his tracks, turned to face the reporter, flashed his trademark grin, flaunting his famous blue eyes, before calmly opening his mouth to speak. "Quite the observation, chump..." Triple X grabbed the reporter by the collar, jacking him up a bit, before slinging him in the opposite direction. "...but, this is Japan. These people over here are idiots. They couldn't spot wrestling talent unless the wrestler himself was drenched in soy sauce, or some form of rice. They're idiots. Wait till we get back to the States and see who's chanting whose name." With his right forearm, he shoved the reporter aside, looked over his shoulder once more. ...once he realized nobody was there - well, not Zero atleast - he smirked, then continued on toward his dressing room. --- Thirty minutes later --- You would think he would be happy... in the mood to celebrate... or even remotely excited. The energy in the arena was electric. He, himself took on pressure head on, looked it in the eye, and won. He slowly began to tape an icepack over his right shoulder, grimacing as he did so. In the past twenty minutes, or so... the pain had tripled. What was weird, though... was he had seen nights like tonight so many times, if not far worse... What was going on? Was it still Zero? Nah... he had long gotten over that loser. Zero was living off of name value alone. Sure he had fans... and, yes... he was a tough son-of-a-bitch... but, he wasn't anything to fear. Looking in his bathroom mirror, Sean forced a fake smile of reassurance. It did nothing to make him feel better. "Get it together you fuckin' pussy," he mumbled to himself, before quickly untieing the ice patch, walking out the coat rack where his leather jacket hung, placing it over his shoulders - wrestling gear and all - and, exiting his dressing room... something wasn't right... so in order to make it right... he had decided he'd pretend as if everything was. He exited the dressing room... ...but, not before looking over his shoulder, to see if Zero lurked...
