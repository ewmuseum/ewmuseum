the end justifies the means, Part Five

20 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday, July 29, 2001: Jon Crisp was finally getting what he needed. SummerSlaughter was a success, although not specifically for him. The match he participated in was a flop, by wrestling standards. But, by storytelling standards, it was a comedic gold mine. But, there was one flaw in what was an outstanding angle: T and Parka had gotten over. Crisp, rather, had not. Credit: fWowrestling.com, SummerSlaughter webcast (match in progress): Frankton: It looks like all the wrestlers have hopped the guardrail... and are standing right in front of the ring watching the forklift headed for the guardrail near the ramp... and the forklift crashes! I don't think it'll be starting back up this time! Frankton: Wait... please don't tell me I'm hearing that... Frankton: It's frickin' Doink The Clown's music! And... something... or things... have just climbed out of the forklift! It's... Dink, Wink, and Pink? They must've been controlling that thing the whole time! But... how? Willard: Hey, looks like they like the Slip 'N Slide, too! Frankton: Yes, all three are going down the Slip 'N Slide... and Scot Hartt is approaching them? Hartt: Hey! That's OUR Slip 'N Slide! Frankton: And the midgets advance on Hartt! We got some clubberin'! Mr. T: WELL... SAVE 'IM, FOO! Heftel: No way, I'm scared of clowns! Sphere: And I'm scared of mid... little people! Stump: ... La Parka: *Shimmy* Frankton: OH MY GOD! Bam Bam Bigelow just hopped out of the stands, and he grabs Wink by the throat... and... sweet... I... I don't know how to say this... but he just bit the head off that clown! And he spits it down on the ramp! Willard: Heh heh... now THAT was funny! Those clowns sure do know how to put on a show! Frankton: Dink and Pink are now scared out of their size three boots! They're trying to hide behind Hartt! But it's no use! Bigelow has Pink... and LITERALLY RIPS HIM IN HALF! He broke him in half! He broke him in half! Willard: You know... I can't really watch stuff like this... yet I can't turn away! Frankton: Bigelow now has Dink cornered, as Hartt crawls over to his comrades... oh no. What's Bigelow gonna do NOW? Frankton: HOLY SH{BLEEP}! I don't care if that was blocked out or not! BIGELOW JUST STUCK HIS HAND DOWN DINK'S THROAT AND BY GOD, LITERALLY RIPPED OUT HIS TAILBONE!! Now Bigelow crushes it in his hands! My God, he killed him! He's dead! He killed him! Frankton: Bigelow celebrating here... as he finally got even with those... things. No! Lawrence Taylor just ran down the ramp and took out Bigelow with the Elbow From Hell! Willard: Now, how did the elbow get into Hell? Did it kill someone? Like, you know, it just kept bopping someone on the head? Frankton: Wait! It's Steve "Mongo" McMichael! Mongo: I used to be a Horseman, bay-ba, yeah! Frankton: And Mongo knocks out Taylor with his Haliburton briefcase! And NOW who's coming out here? Frankton: It's Kevin Greene! Greene clotheslines Mongo and stomps on him repeatedly! Oh, the humanity! Now it looks like Greene wants some more! Greene: RIDA-RABA! BLAH-BLAH-MA-MA-NA! STUPID PUNKS! YEAH! C'MON! Heftel: EEP! Mr. T: C'MON, SUCKAS! LET'S GET INTO DA RING! Sphere: Yeah, where it's safe! Frankton: It looks like everyone's gonna get back into the ring... wait! Sphere and Mr. T have stopped on the apron, as Crisp hits Waru with the Icon! Frankton: Crisp with another Icon, this time on Heftel! Oh, Dear God, no! Kevin Greene has made his way to the broadcast booth, and I think he's looking for a headset! Um... La Parka... shimmies in the ring... Frankton: He gets to the "spin around while hopping on one foot" stage... and Jackhammer kicks him in the gut! And Kevin Greene is still looking for a headset! I hope he doesn't find one! Willard: Hey, maybe he's color blind or something... I mean, he talks just like the dog I got at home! Frankton: Oh no! He's got one! And he's trying to put it on! Frankton: But wait! I know I've said that many times during this match, but Jackhammer just tossed La Parka from the ring, and La Parka... yes! La Parka takes out Greene with a flying shimmy! Frankton: Now Jackhammer grabs Scot Hartt... HammerFall on Hartt! Jackhammer kicks Hartt out of the ring... as Crisp decides to just toss Stump aside. Frankton: Looks like Sphere is strategizing with Mr. T on the apron... Mr. T: GET IN THE-AH, SUCKA! SQUASH THAT BUG! Frankton: Sphere FINALLY gets into the ring and kicks Waru out... Jackhammer is all alone! Willard: Now, now, I know it's already uneven in there, but wouldn't it be neat if there was a trap door in the ring? Maybe have a tiger come out from the hole? You know, might even up the sides. Frankton: Yes, but then there's the whole "it's not our fault it got loose in the arena" thing we'd have to deal with. It looks like Crisp and Sphere are going to double-team Jackhammer... Mr. T cheering them on... huh? Crisp looks at Jackhammer and points to Sphere? Crisp: Kill him. Frankton: Sphere seems confused, no surprise there. Jackhammer shrugs... HammerFall on Sphere! And now Crisp has put Stump on the chest of Sphere! Frankton: 1! Frankton: 2! Frankton: No! Crisp just took Stump off of Sphere... he tosses Stump to Jackhammer... and picks up Heftel? What's going on here? Frankton: Oh! Crisp has just put Heftel on Sphere, because everyone knows getting pinned by Mike Heftel is way more embarrassing than getting pinned by Stump! Frankton: 1! Frankton: 2! Frankton: 3! I don't believe it... the STUMP Squad has won this thing! Crisp just leaving his team to regroup by themselves... and Fred, you did a great job on calling this match! Willard: Well... thanks... it was great to be here! Say, what are you doing after the show? We'll go get a beer together! Or a milk, or whatever it is that you drink... Frankton: Well, I'll have to see about that... folks, as Mr. Willard leaves the booth, James is returning... and hear comes our clean-up crew... hope they do a good job... Armstrong: Hey, Frankton! What'd I miss? Frankton: Oh, not much. Now... time for our next bout! This was most frustrating for Jon, as he had really hoped that this would have been the springboard he needed in his career. But, where there was a dark cloud, there was a silver lining. Tonight, he would start an angle with Donaven Winters, or, as he was being called by the smarks...instant ratings. The dark character of Winters had caught on with the fans. What they didn't know was that Winters was actually what he portrayed in the ring: depressed, despondent, and, while not tough to work with, sometimes hard to get to. Crisp didn't mind. Winters was a hot angle, and that's all he needed. But T and Parka...that got to him. Chris Universal knew that. So, that's why he called Crisp into his hotel room hours before the show. "Jon," Universal greeted him, shaking his hand. "Last night was a success." "Indeed," Crisp replied, returning with a hearty shake. Universal stood aside for Crisp to come in, and Universal shut the door behind him. "Jon, I called you in here because I have some good news for you." "Oh?" "I know last night was not a shining night for you. While you appeared in what could be the comedy match of the year...well, for you, it was supposed to be a match. I understand that. That's why I have this proposal for you. "After this program with Donaven, I'd like to work you into a program with Thomas. A Hellrazor-Crisp angle would be hot with the fans, especially with a more intense Crisp. We're also working Thomas over as a serious wrestler. But, we can get the Hardcore Title involved, and..." "Whoa," Crisp interjected. Universal looked up. "What is it?" "Chris, I don't know that I can *be* a hardcore wrestler. I'm not very good at using weapons." "Well, that's where your scientific expertise comes in. We can use you as someone who will 'teach' the hardcore wrestlers how to wrestle. You know the internet knocks them as guys who can't wrestle. We know better. But, I think we can get both you and Thomas over at the same time, in the same program. And besides...we run this as an intense angle up through Countdown, and then we move you into the Internet Title scene." Crisp sat down on Universal's bed, and thought about what he was being offered. It was obvious to Crisp that Universal felt Crisp could do comedy. After all, that's what a scientific wrestler going into the hardcore division was: pure comedy. But, if it could get him to the title scene, which was what he needed to boost his career... "I'll do it," Crisp said. "What do I have to lose?" Nothing, he thought at the time.
