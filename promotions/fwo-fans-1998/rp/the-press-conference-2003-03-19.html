The Press Conference

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The noise was deafening. Camera's flashed to and fro, snapping pictures for what appeared to be quite the little event. Finally a man walked across the empty stage and stood at the podium. The noisy reporters and dirt-sheeters saw this and finally started to quiet down... The man cleared his throat... "Alright, everyone I'm Mike Westwood and welcome to the press junket for the Villam Ender and Eli Flair wrestling match for the Asylum Championship, taking place on March 19th, 2003. Today we're are going to do an old fashioned weigh in and we will be polling questions from you, the press..." The press applauded themselves. Of course. "Right." Mike said. "So, without any further adieu I'd like to present to you the holder of 12 World Championships from GWF to WCWPA to CSWA. The two time fWo hardcore champion. Winner of the 1999 CSWA match of the year award. Accompanied by everyone's favorite psycho bitch manager of champions, Poison Ivy....The King of Extreme. The Original Nobody... "Total Elimination" Eli Flair! "You know you're right" by Nirvana Filtered out over the loud speakers and entering from stage-right was Eli Flair and Ivy McGinnis. They each took there places standing to Westwood's left. Camera's flashed and people hooted and hollered...Eli and Ivy stood there taking it in. Mike held up a hand..."Alright...and now I'd like to introduce...." Before Mike could say another word..."Cosmonaut" by At the Drive In burst onto the PA. Villam Ender with microphone in hand strolled onto the scene stage left. Joe was right behind. "I am. I am the first -true- fighter to step foot in the Asylum. I am the first man to change a belt. The United Kingdom title into the United Knockout Title. I am the first man to hold both of the Asylum's T.V and U.K titles at the same time. Including having one of the longest TV title reigns...I am sure to have one of the long Asylum Championship title reigns. I raped the first man in the Asylum. I have gotten away with crimes that Charles Manson could only dream about....But, most of all...I am *your* God of Fight. I am accompanied by the single most best promoter in sports, in entertainment, whatever. The man next to me is a corporate man beast, a marketing mastermind. This man is Joe Campbell... and me? I am... Villam Ender!" The little self-introduction garnered nothing but boos from those in attendance. Villam kept him pridefully reserved and stood next to Mike Westwood. "Let's start the weigh in." Mike said as both men were instructed to pull off their shirts and jump up onto their scales. Mike looked down at Villam's digital display. "Villam is 6 foot and 209 Pounds. Looks like you put on some weight, Villam." Villam rolled his eyes as he read Eli's digital display. "Eli is 6'9 and 300 pounds." The hands shot up again and Mike randomly picked one. "John Carrollton, New York Post. I hate to sound cynical...but I really don't even believe Asylum Fighting is real. So, I've got to ask this. Is your match going to be worked?" Villam laughed. "Kiss my black ass. You probably haven't even been to an Asylum event. It's wrestling...but this isn't a game. The only game I'm playing is called Toss-Across. It's where I toss Eli's ass across the arena into the fucking TravvyTron. I've been training. Wrestling is easy and I can take it. The only that's going to get 'worked' is Eli's ass." Villam then gave Eli a provocative wink. Eli shook his head. "It wasn't worked when this man walked into an fWo event holding a shotgun. It wasn't worked when he challenged me to a fight and I told him to fuck off. It certainly wasn't worked when I realized that this wasn't gonna go away and the agents in the fWo were doing fuck-all to keep them out of the arenas. So like the rest of my life, I need to take things into my own hands and show these two the way out the door... or through the Travis Tron if Villam decides he's actually got the sac to back up his words with action." Eli winked back at Villam, and shot him the finger. "Next question....ah. You." Mike said. "Ah, Rob Shin, Hyperfighting Magazine. Uh, Eli. Inquiring minds would like to know about your connection to Dez Aragon and if-" "No comment on Dez Aragon," interrupted Ivy, "Next question." Villam looked at Ivy and Eli for a second and muttered 'Motherfucker' under his breath... Mike picked another hand out of the crowd... "Micky Waters from Fighting Weekly... What if you lose, Villam? I know that in your mind you see victory is eminent, but what happens --should it somehow happen-- that Eli Flair is able to defeat you? What will you do then?" Villam shook his head. "Look at this fucking sack of sugary shit. This guy could barely last a fucking week in *my* promotion. If I switch over to wrestling for one night...it's like a fucking step down for me. I'm not going to lose. I wouldn't risk my Asylum Championship if I thought I was going to lose. You people treat us fighters like wrestling is some complicated new frontier." Micky Waters wrote the answer down and spoke up again. "Eli...same question." Eli rolled his eyes. "I don't really care about the Asylum Championship... don't give two shits about Villam Ender and never really understood what the Asylum has besides men getting castrated after raping other men. I lose this match, Villam and Joe pack up and go home, and we can disinfect the ring and the backstage. I win, I pistol- whip this fucktard with his own title and laugh, all the way to the bank." Ivy leaned in toward the microphone, "I want to know what Joe Campbell thinks. He's a businessman.... sort'a.... so he's gotta be prepared for the possibility of Eli Flair as his Asylum Champion, and I want to know what he'd do with a WRESTLER who bitch- slaps his prized fighter." Joe laughed. "You cannot beat, my man Villam. My man will beat you like a DAWG. Is that what you wanted to hear, septic bitch? Fuck you. Hell, I bet that even *I* can kick Eli's ass. Fucking yank couldn't even take a tap from the butt of a shotgun. He definitely isn't going to be able to take a straight punch from the V man. *If* and I do mean if Villam does lose...I'm sure it will be a fluke victory. I wouldn't expect anything less from yank piece of trash like yourselves." "Right." Mike said. "Next question." Mike selected yet another hand from the audience. "Er, Ike Rogers from POWEROFWRESTLING.COM. Mr. Flair, as the self proclaimed 'King of Extreme', do you feel at all threatened by the legitimacy of a proven fighting champion like Villam Ender? I mean...this guy is a threat. You have to admit that." "First of all, I wasn't self- proclaimed," explained Eli, "it's just a dumbass label that the internet slapped on me. Second, I'm not worried at all... the Asylum is no different than any other fighting or wrestling promotion only without any kind of rule. Is Villam a tough fighter? Without a doubt. But then again... so am I." Next to him, Eli caught the smirks on Campbell and Villam's face out of the corner of his eye. "And the moment he doesn't believe that is the moment I humiliate him and put the Asylum outta business by removing its World Title from circulation." Villam put up his hand in mocking defense. "Whoa slow down there turbo. You're tough, Eli...I'll give you that. But you're a tough wrestler, not a fighter. Sorry. I realize you dream of that day that you can crawl out of mediocrity and call yourself something greater than what you really are...but today is not that day." Ike pointed his tape recorder to Villam. "Well, Villam...as far as this promotion is concerned...with recent fighters like Borst moving to the fWo, do you feel you are merely following in a trend? I mean what do you really HOPE to gain from this whole situation?" Villam shook his head. "I hope to gain entry into the fWo of course." This set off a wave of mumbling. "Fucking idiots. fWo hasn't nothing I want. If I wanted the World title, I would've taken up a challenge with the fWo's resident Kevin Nash. But, I didn't. This is about exactly what I said it was about. Humiliation. This isn't some new chapter in Fighting verse Wrestling. I'm not that fucking queer, Borst. I could generally careless about wrestling and their bullshit idea that their face soap opera of a sport is more physically and mentally dominating than mine. I know it's bullshit. Wrestler's don't have it easy. But compared to a fighter's struggle? They definitely don't have it hard. This is about Ivy running her snatchmouth and calling us a nickel and dime federation....This is about who's the better man. This is about all those times a wrestler said: 'Man, I've seen that fighting shit in bars. It's not the same as our sport. Our sport is the bees knees.'" Villam shook his head. "Wrestling is the bees nothing. I could wrestle in my sleep. This isn't about what I hope to gain. This is about that I hope that wrestler's world wide will gain. And that's perspective. And how about...a little bit of fucking respect?" "That's such bullshit," interrupted Ivy, "You talk about perspective? Respect? You don't get respect by murdering your brother for pay per view buy rates. You don't get respect by invading another organization because a loudmouthed bitch like myself insulted your manhood. You don't get respect by verbally and physically pistol- whipping Ruben Ross--- actually, you do, don'tcha? I will give credit where it's due." A chuckle rippled through the crowd - shared by all four individuals on the stage. "But much as there's fighters, then there's Villam Ender," continued Ivy, "there's wrestlers, then there's Eli Flair. And until you gain perspective on that.... nobody's going to respect you. And I'm talking about your peers.... not mine." Villam scoffed again. "That exactly the type of shit I'm talking about. No fucking respect. Throwing my half brother's death about like it's just mere fact. You don't know what took place. That was a fucking deathmatch, you bucket of douche water, three quarter slut. Not a fucking, puss-out at the last second because you don't have the guts to do what you gotta do. That's what real fighting is about. The possibility of life and death. So when I decide to use the knife or to fire the gun...suddenly I'm some kind of fucking monster? Good. Because you're right, bitch. And you don't even know the fucking half of it. But you will, soon." Ivy whispered something to Eli. Eli just nodded. Mike cleared his throat to break the eerie silence. "Right, well....next question...Ah! You." "Clark Kent, Daily Post." said the man who's name tag plainly said: 'Mike Renner' Villam frowned. But "Clark" continued. "Well, this is a question is for both Villam and Eli...I was wondering...what kind of superpowers do you guys have? Er, I just wanted to know...it's not like I'm moonlighting as Superman and I'm afraid of losing my place in society or anything...nope...not at all..." Villam looked over at Eli then smiled. "Motherfucker, I got powers up the tit. Like for example...the power to use the phantom might of the ghost of my cock to strangle usurpers. Ah, and then there's the power to provide hyper stimulation for the masses with the very sound of my highly entertaining voice. Oh, yeah...and then there's the power to KILL YOU." "Clark" quickly wrote all of that down with a worried look on his face. "Ok, what about you Eli?" Eli was speechless... "Uh, yeah." he said with a shrug. Westwood turned his microphone at the podium back on. "SECURITY!! GET THIS CLOWN OUT OF HERE!!!" Security stormed in and beat on "Clark" with tazers and clubs as "Clark" screamed. "YOU CAN'T DO THIS TO ME!! I'M SPIDERHULK!!" Westwood shook his head in shame..."Alright, well....last questi--" "I have a question." A loud British accent cut the air with an alcohol influenced slur. "Villam, why are you such a fucking twat?" The reporters cleared out of the way of the man approaching the stage and with everyone surely out of his way...he stood there...as clear as day. The man that no one really wanted to see right now...least of all Villam Ender. He stood there with a beer in his hand and the two-finger salute in the other. Borst. Eli laughed. "I've been wondering that myself, V. Why are such a fucking twat?" Villam's face twitched in rage. !! Villam leapt from the stage and tackled Borst to the ground. Camera began a renewed round of flashing as Villam and Borst got to their feet, both putting their respective guards up. Borst swung first, Villam leaned out dodging the punch and leaned all the way back in surprising the fWo United States Champion with a headbutt that busted Borst's lip to bleeding. Borst returned with a right punch of his own right into the eunuch's mouth. Borst followed up with a left and then another right and then another left until blood started to dribble out of Villam's jaw. Borst slurred something to Villam and continued to advance as Villam stumbled back... ....and spit a gob of blood right into Borst's eyes. "Fucking arselicking sept-*POW* Before Borst could finish his sentence Villam slammed an elbow into the bridge of this nose. Turning his body half away from Borst, Villam got really fancy and slammed a rapid number of back elbows into Borst's face. After that Villam clipped Borst in the side of the face with a spinning back kick. Borst fell back stumbling to the ground...but before he could get back to his feet and retaliate security was already on him. They locked the cuffs with an audible 'click'. Eli clapped sarcastically. "Way to be, you fuckin' goat!" Villam turned around and gave Ivy and Eli a bloody grin. "What's that, E? Talkin' shit?" Before Eli could answer Villam was already making his way through the crowd of reporters, throwing chairs left and right making a B-line for the Stage where a surprised Eli and Ivy stood. "ELI!" Ivy yelled as Eli hopped down from the stage. Villam shot Ms. McGinnis a bloody grin before he charged Eli and punched the Original Nobody right in the face. And continued to pound away. Only the sound of punching could be heard over Ivy's calls for security or pleas for things to stop. But reporters and dirtsheeters were keeping clear...not wanting to become a part of the story they were reporting on. Eli counter-pounded away at Villam sending him staggering backwards... ....and out of nowhere Joe Campbell jumped onto Eli's back pounding away at the top of his head causing both of them to fall into a pile of scattered chairs. Cameramen swarmed the now-brawling Eli and Joe Campbell. Such a calamity was roused that no one saw Villam slip away from the ruckus. That's the thing about Villam Ender. Unless he's right there in your face, the source of your conflict, you could miss him altogether in the bat of an eye. Almost like a ninja. So much like a ninja that Ivy hardly recognized the Ender-shaped shadow in front of her. Her heart skipped a fear-filled beat as she looked up into his face. She tried to take off to the right and was barred exit by Villam's left fist slamming into the wall. She tried to exit left. But, Villam leaned in close...and twisted a 7 inch combat knife into the wall next to her. She was speechless as he leaned in even closer, blood on his breath and the heat of violence still generated by his entire body. He pressed himself against her, terrorizing Ivy with a closeness that she only reserved for lovers and family. And here was this killer, invading that space with a playful look on his sleepishly grinning face. It was so completely sinister that Ivy stood frozen in her fear, save for her fidgeting hands. He took that cocky-solid sexual stance in front of her, as if he would "take" her against the wall then and there. The air that gusted out of his lungs, told a story of a wanting...and wanting that she was far too frightened to even consider. From her left side she heard the knife scrape against the wood wall, moving towards her until the icy blade made a home on the outside of her thigh. It was in this crucial moment that Villam tenderly buried his face into her hair, inhaling the smell of terror sweat from her strawberry blonde locks. He drew his face back ever so slightly and used his tongue to trace a line from her temple...down, down to her cheek. How many times could the words "fear" and "terror" be used to express what she felt right now? She just wanted this monster to stop. Stop playing this sick little game with her. "He's got a knife!!" Finally. Ivy let out a sigh of relief. Villam brought his tongue back into his mouth and laughed a wisp of a laugh. The people were talking about Joe Campbell. Not Villam Ender. And Joe's choice to bring out a weapon just bought the eunuch more play time. It was the equivalent of hitting the restart button on hell. It was this moment that he chose to speak. Walking the knife horizontally over her thigh and towards her pelvic region he let his first words whisper from his lips. "Strong women taste so sweet." She let herself shiver as he walked knife up and up, just a centimeter away from her navel. "A nickel and a dime, sweetness." The knife kept walking...making it's third stop just in-between the first pair of ribs, just where the bottom of her lung began. A sharp stab and her lung would be pierced. "Your life is worth less than both to me, sugar." The knife, now guided by Ivy's pulse made it's last stop just under, but just barely in-between her breasts. He directed the blade diagonally, to the right. The knife pointed at Ivy McGinnis' heart like a compass pin headed north. "I could've stabbed you five times, wiped the blood from the knife on your shirt and stuffed this knife back into my boot.... ....and no one would have known." He gave her a revolting kiss on the nape of her neck as he slowly jabbed deeper with the knife. Just enough to draw blood. "This will be our little secret." ACK~! VILLAM!! ....and as fast as the eclipsing shadow came... ....it was gone again. Eli held Joe Campbell up by the throat with one hand. Villam picked up an empty wood chair and strode up to Eli, firmly shattering it into pieces across this shoulder's and the back of his neck. Ivy yelled again as Eli went down. Finally, security came and broke up the commotion... Villam and Joe made their exit with their entourage of bodyguards... Ivy kneeled at Eli's unconscious form, but chanced a glance at the fleeing eunuch. Villam "Ender" King wasn't even looking back.
