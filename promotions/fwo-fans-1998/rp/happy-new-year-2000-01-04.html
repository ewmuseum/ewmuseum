Happy New Year.

4 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Happy New Year. January 04, 2000 :::The scene opens up on Scott Slugger, in leather jacket and street clothes, standing in the middle of a vacant parking lot in front of the arena in Washington, DC where Countdown 1 was held, its bulk standing silently behind Slugger. Debris can be seen on the tarmac of the lot, both from the earlier fWo show and from celebrations of the passing of the year 1999 into the year 2000, and in a light wind, some of these swirl in the air behind Slugger. Slugger takes a moment to compose his thoughts, and speaks.::: You know, it's amazing how things can change in the blink of an eye. For example, not more than a few hours ago, it was 1999. Now it's the year 2000. Not more than a few days ago, Ryan Moore, Joy, was alive and well. Now he's passed on. The coming of the New Year is supposed to be an occasion of joy and happiness, and Ryan would have wanted everyone to enjoy theirselves this New Year's Eve and Day. Just remember while you're celebrating what he once said...there's a little bit of Joy in all of us. Rest in peace, Ryan. :::Slugger clears his throat, and, with an obvious effort, turns his attention to fWo "business" and the tumultuous events of the last week.::: Now...Frenchie. I've got just two words for you- Ha. And, Ha. You never deserved that belt, and odds are, you'll never see it again. I just hope you've enjoyed the eight combined days of your two World Title "reigns". And speaking of the World Title, what a ride it's gone on the past few days. Almost to Rain, then to Spencer, then to Black Panther. Now, Panther, congratulations, you've reached the highest plain anyone can get to in the fWo without, well, being the WGA. Just realize that if it occurs to me that hey, I want that belt, it's mine. You see, we've fought four times, and I've beaten you without breaking a sweat all four times. But for now...I've got some other "business" to take care of, so you can sleep at night knowing that your title is safe. Next, Spencer. Spencer- you're pathetic. Seriously. You've never won the World Title without some kind of interference or distraction or having it just handed to you. The only time we've ever fought, I tossed your ass from Mob Rules like it was nothing- which it was. Face it, son- you will *never*, *EVER* deserve to wear that belt. And not only that- you're a reprehensible son of a bitch, and I hope that Collins' crew, the Strangler, Fallen, whoever, ends your pathetic little career. Hell, I'd do it myself if I didn't have bigger fish to fry. Oh, and real quick, let me touch on Travis. Travis...is an idiot. It was obvious the night he tried to fight me a few months ago, and it's been obvious every time he's stepped into an fWo arena. Quite frankly, I don't give a flying crap if he *ever* comes back from where he wandered off to. :::Slugger is silent for a moment, then gestures to the arena behind him.::: This arena right here, last night, was the site of a travesty of justice. Chris Universal somehow managed to defeat me- *ME!*- clean, 1, 2, 3, in a triangle match with Ice. Well, Universal, you may be feeling high and mighty now, but let me tell you this, money man. From the very moment I saw you, I knew you were a worthless sack of crap. The only reason I even ever let you in to *my* group was because I didn't want to lose the backup of your partner- you know, the *talented* one in the Hollywood Blondes. Universal, face it. All your life you've been a loser. From the time a good twenty something years ago when I guarantee you went crying home to mommy because you were the last one picked for the football team, to the times in high school when I guarantee the only reason you ever made *any* teams was because you came from a rich family, to the time you could not retire that worthless bald-headed bastard Austin Davis, to the times where you could never hold off the Black Panther, *to* *the* *very* *time* you joined the Dynasty and ruined the stable that had the potential to be the best in the history of sports-entertainment, you have been a Grade-A, Number One, US Approved LOSER. And just because you scored one BS pinfall on the World's Greatest Athlete in one BS match, does not make you a contender!, does not make you talented!, and does not make you any more than a sniveling, whiny, pathetic little piece of crap who has less talent in his whole body, than I have in my left pinky finger! And I'm right-handed! And Ice...well, everybody knows my feelings on Ice. He's got some talent, a very little amount, but he's spent his entire career riding my coattails, cowering in my shadow, ever since I brought him to the fWo, and that will never change. And I suppose that both he, and Chris Universal are wondering...just who is that Mysterious Man you've been seeing on fWo television lately? You know, the one I've been talking to in the boiler rooms? :::Slugger laughs.::: Well, it could be anybody. It could be...Jim Dudley. It could be...the Falcon. Hell, wasn't Mick Foley just recently, wink-nod, fired? He likes to hang out in boiler rooms, doesn't he? Maybe Mankind has found a new home in the fWo. The point is...you don't know. Only that man, and the WGA know. And when you do find out...well, heh, it's gonna knock you dead. Count on it. Until next time... you know the rest. :::Slugger walks away and out of sight, as the camera pans down to the tarmac, and slowly fades to deepest, darkest black.:::
