Fatal Cries, Part II

9 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He coughed violently as blood poured out of his mouth spilling onto the white carpet. Though the carpet was already a light brown color from the dirt that had built up over the years. Above him, a man stood laughing, brandishing a broom in his hands. The man lashed out again, striking the child across the back. The child fell down to floor, choking on his tears and the river of crimson red that flowed from his mouth. The man started to jab the broom down into the child's kidneys. He tried to scream for help, but no one would hear and if they did, they wouldn't care. This was becoming part of a daily routine for the boy, the welts across his back and his chest were like a new skin overlaying the original. "Come on boy, get up," the man demanded. The child tried to get to his knees, but the broom connected with his backside again. He slumped back down onto the floor, the newest welts showing through the back of his white t-shirt. The man finally dropped the broom from his hands and once again laugh voraciously. The insidious attacks were done for now as the man fell down onto the couch and grabbed a flask of whiskey that sat on the end table next to him. He tipped his head back and let the whiskey slide down his throat. He coughed a little from the harshness of the drink, but he smiled down at the child, struggling to get to his feet. Blood had finally stopped its regular flow from the boy's mouth, now it was only dripping off the edges of his lips. He couldn't bring himself to speak to the man that had just battered him again. He just looked up at the man, his eyes were filled with tears and a longing. The man just scoffed at the helpless boy, mockingly throwing his foot in the child's direction. The child almost instantly reacted, backing himself into a corner of the room. "What's the matter boy?" The man questioned, harshly. "Afraid I'm gonna hurt you? Haha, no, you don't have to worry about that." The boy slowly nodded his head, it wasn't clear if he was agreeing with what he believed was the truth. Or was he nodding so he could save himself from another beating. He had tried to do everything to please the man, but it was never good enough. His efforts were met with a broom, or something more solid that left larger bruises and irreparable scars. He looked back towards the father, his eyes were lonesome, there was an odd sensation of a blank void as he stared at the father. He blinked and tears fell out of the corner. He slowly opened his mouth, which was still red on the inside. "Daddy," he spoke slowly, carefully. "Please don't hurt me anymore..." The boy was begging to save his own pathetic self from being beaten within an inch of his life again. The father looked at his child and laughed wildly, his long hair whipped around as the child tried to push himself further into the corner. He was trying to become part of the wall. He wished that he had never existed, his father wished he had never existed and he never let him forget that. He watched his father drag himself off the couch. His eyes were locked onto every movement that his father made. Left. Right. One foot in front of the other, inching closer to him. The right hand reached over and grabbed hold on the broom which had begun to splinter and crack from the contact it made with the child's back. He raised the broom high into the air and the child instantly covered his face with his small arms. The man dropped the broom to the ground and laughed at his son who, was on the floor, pushed into a corner as far as he could go while rolled into the fetal position. "Get up you little son of a bitch," the father spoke gruffly. The child took his arms away from his face and nodded. He put his hands back against the wall, hesitantly inching himself back up to the point of where he was standing, just above waist high to his father. He looked up at his drunken father, tears still coming down the sides of his face. His father still looked down upon him, grinning from ear to ear, his decaying yellowed teeth silently laughing down at him. The child cringed at the smell of his father's whiskey breath. "D-don't hurt me," the child stuttered. "P-please don't hurt me." The father guffawed loudly. He looked down at his son and spit right in his face before extending out and slapping him. The child fell back to the floor, tears forming from the corners of his eyes, his cheek left a brilliant red from his father's stronger hand. The father then bent down onto one knee, staring face to face with his son. He took his right hand and grabbed his son by the chin and glared directly into his eyes, spit coming out of his mouth as he spoke. "I own you," he pronounced through gritted teeth. "Whatever I want to do, to and with you, I will. Just because you are MY child and I fucking own, you. If I want to beat you, I will. I will do whatever the hell I want to you. Do you understand me?" The child nodded his head, his father's grip on his chin tightened. "Good." He released his grip from the child's chin and stormed out of the room. The boy sat in the corner sobbing, knowing it would only be time that kept him waiting for the next chastisement from his father. The love and influence that a father figure was supposed to give to his son or daughter was missing in the child's life. He felt the cold hands around his neck daily, when his hands weren't around a bottle or flask, it was any weapon or his throat. Only the strong can survive in the world and given the adversity the boy had faced through the first eight years of his life, he was becoming stronger and stronger every day. Perseverance, of the Fallen Angel was only beginning to shape and take its true form.
