Trial and Error // Part.4 // Other Side of the Story

19 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Your Honor, I'd like to call Angel Dalton to the stand, a member under contract with the Asylum, and a former long time friend to one Kenny Rock," said Yanch. A.D. rose and walked over to the stand, taking the oath, and then sitting down. Yanch walked over to him, hands resting in his pockets, as he began. "Mr. Dalton, please tell me what happened during your encounter with Kenny Rock when you arrived at the scene, just as Mr. Douglas was being taken away." "O..ok," A.D. stammered, greeting Yanch with a warm, yet nervous glance. "I arrived just as a cop was questioning Kenny, but it was right at the tail end. When I got there, Kenny was pretending to cry." Kenny Rock rolled his eyes, as the Judge looked over, catching him doing so. Rock then slumped back into his seat, leaving Judge Willis to turn his attention back to A.D. "Then, the cop left, and that's when Kenny stopped crying. He turned around to face me, and he smiled." A.D. paused for a moment, making sure he took the time to think it all over in his head. He didn't want anything to come out wrong. He wanted his words to come out perfect, and he wanted justice. He wanted to make sure Judge Willis knew just how evil Kenny Rock really was. "He told me Steve that was in trouble, and that there wasn't anything I could do to help him." A.D. paused again, turning to Judge Willis. "Your Honor," he began. "I was this man's friend for a very long time, and I know the sick and evil games he plays. I've also known Steve for some time too, and though he might have made some mistakes before in the past, I have to say, he is a total, honest man, and I don't think he'd lie about any of this." "Go on, please," Yanch said, assuring A.D. that his words wouldn't fall on deaf ears. "Kenny had to have taken Steve out of his bed. Douglas wouldn't have been strong enough for it. Kenny had to have done something first, because I couldn't see Steve doing one thing, unless it was defending himself. Kenny says that Steve threatened him, but that doesn't sound like Steve at all." Kenny Rock laughed at Angel Dalton's statement in a staged laugh, partly because of the irony of it all, hearing such soft words come from the one-time number one contender's lips. For the most part, it was for effect, to display to the court that A.D.'s words were absurd and ridiculous. "Thank you Mr. Dalton. I believe that is all," said Yanch, not even questioning Dalton's answers for legitimacy, leaving him to walk off the stand. "And next, I'd like to call upon Doctor Roberts."
