On The Road Again. Part II

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BLAM! That was sure unexpected. It's not like something bad happening on this road trip was expected though. It was supposed to be a peaceful drive after all. The car swerves. The driver is snapped out of his daze as he wrestles for control. He was too out of it and by the time he reacts It's too late. Brandon should have been more prepared. As the car hits a soft spot on the side of the road it fishtails. Why did he have to get lost? It wasn't like he hadn't traveled this road so often before. He knew these roads but his mind had been in such a cloud lately it often took all of his concentration just to do simple chores. Damn. Headlights. The first ones he had seen in roughly half an hour. They were either in front of him or behind him, that much was obvious but with the spinning car it was not his place to tell. The other driver knew though, all too well. They had rounded a corner feeling safe in the darkness. This road was home to them and they knew it like the back of their hand. What they didn't expect though was coming head on with a spinning hunk of metal. They didn't have time to react. They braced themselves and leaned back so as not to be crushed by the airbag. The sound of wrenching metal and bending steel echoed through the nearly abandoned area. Screeching tires and shattering glass filled in the gaps of silence. In ten seconds it was all over though. The two heaps of wreckage lay joined, making a twisted sculpture in tribute to freak happenstance. **************************************************************************** Brandon Roche blinked. Had he blacked out? He remembered the accident but after the impact everything was a blur. He was outside his car, or what was left of it. He didn't remember undoing his seatbelt. Did he have a concussion? As he stood he felt his old rib injury raise its ugly head. The seatbelt did a number on him; his side would be sore for weeks. That should be fun, wrestling Chronos every night. Brandon blinked hard, trying to clear the cobwebs from his head. Then he remembered... the other car. He spun on his heels back to the wreckage.
