The Price of Freedom (Starring Killjoy!)

24 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The sun was setting in the Seattle sky as Michelle Masters sat outside on a bench near the backstage entrance of the Key Arena, watching the scenery. Clad in jeans and a “Legion of Dairy” fWo t-shirt, the fWo’s currently reigning cruiserweight champion was deep in thought. “shouldn’t you be out in Vegas holding up ring cards for dana white?” Michelle looked up to see Killjoy lurking nearby. Next to him was a long, thin black plastic case. "Shouldn’t you be wrapped in a straitjacket, telling some doctor about how your mother smothered you too much and that’s why you can’t love a woman?" ‘Chelle turned her eyes back to the darkening Seattle sky. The masked freak chuckled, sitting down crosslegged in front of Michelle. "on the surface of the sun, no matter how little we can see…the raging flames are too chaotic to control or understand. I suppose something so cliché is perfect for your persona’s mantra. Fiery on the outside, but raging out of our element on the inside." "Actually, I try to avoid chaos. And people who listen to a little too much KRZY, like you. And I don't need saving, so go pester one of the other masked guys or something." Michelle replied with her best attempt at a disinterested look. killjoy laughed again, “save you? i’m only here saving those saving their compassion for my soul. we and by we, i mean myself and someone else here…but not you…we are free because we believe. you are free to own your piece of silver because of me, but you have always forgotten the roots of our existence. i’m saving someone like you the time because i don’t have the mind to worry about what and you should never be. you won’t be free until you let go of whatever inhibitions your mask hides the identity of a plain, white vanilla family allegory.” “Thank you, Roland Washington.” Michelle answered beneath her gritting teeth. “But I don’t think you have the right tone to start making inferences about who I am in that way.” killjoy smiled back. “you haven’t had to hide from the chaos of those like myself just trying to dance around a tornado. you’re insulated, you’re buried deep in a pit of past actions. you are married, but you clearly want to bone Sheffield. such honor in that one…such backstabbing skincrawling honor in that masked belly of that man’s soul…and all to nearly cause you to burn this place down.” Michelle’s eyes widened as killjoy looked at the landscape for a moment, swallowing the silence of Masters’ curiosity about what this man really knew about Alias…and maybe moreso…herself. killjoy nodded at nothing in particular, squinting at the fainted sunlight…before looking back towards michelle. he looked deep into her eyes without blinking, an unsettling comfort between the two was shared. “Xiong couldn’t fight you because you are a woman. A moral, ethical man that given the slightest push along the most precipitous path. That didn’t stop you, I only felt that such a trendsetting evolution of gender equality get what was fair…” Michelle’s eyes squinted at some of those words by killjoy. “What did you…” killjoy reached into his pocket, michelle immediately quieted for a moment to brace herself for anything from a rose to a live grenade. It was a key hanging on a silver necklace. “i found something he needed to wear to release the truths he denied himself to experience. much like something i found for you,” killjoy pulled over the case and used the key to open it. “you’re own husband's past came back to haunt you and you’re resenting him…’cause Morey’s out to end your mentor’s career…and yet…you feel your mentor resents your success and that old, father time has claimed his wrestling life.” Click. The case laid unopen, Michelle unnerved by killjoy’s returning stare. “you think you’re hated in the back because you showed up like a late period wrapped in faded gold. big, bad ivy didn’t back you because she’s more self-righteous than a nun at a gangbang…” killjoy traced his fingers over the case, still looking at Michelle, “sounds a little bit like a chaotic life to me…” "Great, I'm chaos. Thanks for clearing that up. Bye now." 'Chelle stood up as if to leave. "if you want to be free, i can make you believe. i can give you something that you use to eradicates morey, i give you something that erases ross. we, not you…we are free because we believe in me…we believe we are free because of me. i can give you the only thing you need." The Fiery Thunder Girl cocked an eyebrow at killjoy. "I'm probably gonna regret asking this, but you plan to do that how exactly, KRZY?" Killjoy opened the black case, to show what was previously hidden from view - a hammer. And not just any hammer... Michelle's eyes grew wide. The Megaton Hammer, burnt handle and supposedly…missing. She had stolen it from the Codemaster, briefly, back in 2005 when the Codemaster had taken exception to Michelle being the promotional poster girl in the fWo for "Kingdom Hearts II." "Where did you get that?" 'Chelle asked. "do you want to believe?" "I don't want that thing. That was a crutch for Allen, a short cut someone as talented as he was never needed." killjoy experimentally swung the hammer. "it is a symbol of someone who has fallen, but risen to find their identity. it is a weapon that will strike your enemies down without conflict and serve as a reminder as to those that fell and rose to find what they needed to save themselves." "I don't NEED it. I can win my battles on my own." Michelle shot back. "then i wonder who i may then give it to…if i told morey to buy me a beer, do you suppose he’d take it? maybe keith will pay more…" Michelle walked back over to the masked madman, as many thought of him, and took the Megaton Hammer out of his hands. "I'm not going to use it, but I'll take it from you before I let you give this to Morey." The smirk on killjoy's face suggested that he had been fairly sure of this result from the start. "I have to confess, KRZY, I am more than a bit tempted to use it on you. I saw what Chris looked like after Cyberslam..." killjoy laughed. Soon he was squealing in laughter, killjoy opened his arms wide and pointed his head towards the heaven. "STRIKE ME DOWN WITH YOUR ANGER! ONLY THEN WILL YOU BELIEVE IN THE DARK SIDE!” killjoy waited patiently… "I appreciate the offer, KRZY, but I'm not *that* easy a girl." 'Chelle grinned. "I imagine you don't have much luck with women, being the masked psychotic love child of Dr. Phil and a Doberman, but let me give you a tip - you don't get to second base with just a gift." “i’ve been there before,” killjoy lowered his head with a grin, “besides its not like you hide your cold nipples much, i’m a c-man darlin’…and you ain’t a b.” killjoy stared at michelle’s chest, ‘causing her to look down to make sure she wasn’t poking out anywhere. As she lifted her head back up, she saw killjoy walking to a black car that pulled up curbside. …and that’s the last she saw of the freak now carrying an empty black case and key around his neck.
