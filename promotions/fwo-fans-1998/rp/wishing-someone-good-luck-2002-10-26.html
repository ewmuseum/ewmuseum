Wishing someone good luck.

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brand Frontier was pissed. He had been doing the good little soldier routine and wrestling whoever and however the booking committee told him too. He had made the public appearances and the talk show route all trying to keep the good name of the organization in the spotlight while his family life suffered. A birthday party planned for his youngest son was rescheduled so that he could make a house show appearance. The crowd in Philadelphia was hot and he enjoyed the show he put on but it didn't change the fact that it was his son's birthday and he was hours away. Yet he walked through those curtains and did his duty. Like always. The house show tour was something new to him. He hadn't had to go this route since his days in M.L.W. His estranged wife and him had started talking again. She realized after some time away from him that he couldn't stop being who he was. She understood that he had chosen a hard life and one that didn't have as many rewards as people thought. He still went out night after night though and did what was asked of him. That, she realized was one of the things she loved so much about him. For that reason and so many more they had begun to talk again. Maybe he hadn't lost his family yet. If that was going to happen though he needed some time to be with them, to make sure that Ana knew that he was more dedicated to the family than to the business. That wasn't happening though. A few weeks ago Brandon had managed to sneak out early from a show and head home. An accident prevented him from actually finishing that plan and even though there was a show a few hours away from home in Norfolk, Virginia he was still to busy to visit. On top of that the organization planned an event in Baltimore and Brandon and the family were to go to dinner afterwards. Things changed that night as well. Brandon fought tooth and nail with Ric Chronos that night in an impromptu match, the fans loved it and it felt good to unleash some of his anger in the ring. Chronos had other ideas though and made a hasty retreat. That pissed Brandon off even more so, doing what he always does, he followed Chronos to finish his business. The man known as Frontier had always taken this job personal and after what Chronos had done to him he was not going to let it slide.
