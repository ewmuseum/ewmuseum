Where the Heart Is

19 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The wooden bench in the courtyard was so comfortable at night, David Cross felt like he could sit on it for hours. The fact that it also faced into Michelle Hiroshi's bedroom window had nothing to do with the fact that David hadn't budged from his spot, despite the two inches of snow which had fallen since he first sat down. No, of course not. David heard footsteps moving towards him and he averted his gaze from the window. "You stare too much," a gruff voice said from behind him. Cross turned his head and saw one of the men he'd beaten at the entrance to the Golden Phoenix temple. His face still had a few bruises, and by the way he shuffled through the snow in his robe, his leg wasn't at a hundred percent. "Hey Wang," he said to the man who wasn't actually named Wang. "How're they hanging? Probably pretty high and tight, unless that nightie you've got on is insulated. You got a wool slip on underneath there?" Wang face was set in stone. "You have big mouth." David turned back toward the window. "Yeah, I've heard that before. So what brings you out here? Is Will and Grace a rerun tonight?" Wang stepped around Cross and stood in front of him. "You should not be here. You should not be at the Temple." "Why not?" David asked as he tried to look around the robed Asian man. "I'm a Golden Guy, just like you. I have every right to train here." "You have not trained. You spend all your time with Michelle. You keep her from training." Cross looked at Wang. "Have you been following us? That's, well, creepy." "You must leave here!" David stood up. "Wang, there's no need to get your panties in a bunch. I'll be heading out in a day or so." "You go now!" "Boys?" Michelle poked her head out of her window. "It's late. Maybe you should continue your argument somewhere else?" Wang turned and bowed to Michelle. As he bent over, David pretended to slap Wang on the butt. Michelle barely suppressed her laugher. Wang stood up, gave David a dirty look, and walked back into the compound. David crossed the small courtyard and stood in front of Michelle's window. "That Wang, what a guy. He reminds me of a pet rock I once had, only with less personality." "He's not so bad," Michelle said with a smile. "I think he's got a thing for me." "Go figure. You're the only woman in the Temple." Michelle took a playful swat at David. "Thanks for the vote of confidence." "Still, the man has great taste." Cross looked into Michelle's eyes and felt himself being swallowed by them. "Any man would be lucky to be with you." Oh crap, he thought to himself. What am I saying? Okay mouth, feel free to stay shut any time now. Or at least let me swallow my tongue before I say something really stupid. Was it the cool night air, or was Michelle actually blushing? "You are too kind. He's sweet, but my heart has been elsewhere for quite some time." She looked up at the moon overhead. "Quite some time." "You mean your ex-husband?" "My mother was so happy when I married him. I think it must be a Western custom, wanting your daughter to marry a Professional." Michelle sighed audibly. "But it wasn't meant to be. I wanted more than a life of servitude." "So you kicked his ass and came back here?" Michelle gave David a mischievous smile. "Something like that." "But why didn't you go with your Father to the States?" David asked as he shifted from side to side in the snow. "He wants you to come there so badly." The smile was gone. "My father is an animal. He's lower than a pig." She broke into an angry rant in Japanese. David understood many of the words she used. The girl was cursing like a sailor. She was a tempest in a teapot, with a breathtaking beauty and the heart of a dragon. There was an uncomfortable silence before David spoke again. "So your ex didn't have your heart." I'm pretty screwed in the head. David inched closer to the window sill that Michelle was leaning on. "Go on." She looked down into the snow. "He's still confused. He knows he has a Destiny, but he doesn't know what it is. He needs to focus himself." Her eyes lifted and met his. "He can become as great as he wants to be. He has the ability and the desire. He just needs to put it all together." David placed his hands on the sill, gently brushing the side of her own hands. "So that's what you like about this guy? That he can be great one day?" "He makes me smile. He's sweet. Deep down, he has a good heart." There was no more cold, no more snow. David couldn't feel his wet pants sucking the heat out of his legs. Michelle's face slowly inched closer to his. "Good people are hard to come by these days." Cross' voice was just louder than a whisper. "I'm sure if you told him how you felt, he'd feel like the luckiest guy alive. He'd never do anything to hurt you. He'd probably cherish every minute that he was with you." "You think I should tell him that I've cared for him ever since he saved me from drowning when we were both just children?" she breathed into his ear. "How he hugged me from behind and pulled me to safety, even with that stick poking into my back." Yes... stick. God bless her naivety. "I'd have to go with you telling him on this one Michelle." She reached out and hugged David around the neck. "I'd rather show him." And she pulled him through the window.
