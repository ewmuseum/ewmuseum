Through Lust and Love

7 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
While I may have existed for a long time, I have been in control for a very short time. I am the unwanted child. The hideous creation who's sole purpose was supposed to be to bring death. And hell, there's still the chance that it was me who killed my parents. I know this. However, I cannot remember actually commiting the murders. And I did not love them. That much I know is certain. Why does this matter, you ask? That is simple. You always hurt the ones you love. I have heard that quote time and time again. Love and hate aren't too far off, and yet I felt nothing towards these two adults who served as my parents. Only indifference. Why would I kill them over indifference? But now I'm going off into a tangent. That's not what this is all about, you know. That's the past. A past I had very little role in. I must think of the present, and think of the future. And when I think of both, only one name appears in my mind. Downfall. I cannot explain my attraction. But I lust for him. I need his body, and I need to feel his body on top of mine. I have never experienced such feelings before, but I can't see how they could be anything but pur lust. I need him. There's a chance- a faint, hopeless chance- that I love him as well. How much that thought makes me laugh. A hideous creation like myself in love. But I feel the faintest pulling of my heart strings when this man walks by. His quiet nature, his soft, pale skin. Maybe I do love him. I don't know. I've never felt those feelings before, and I don't think I was ever meant to feel those feeling. No, scratch that- I know I was never meant to feel those feelings. But yet, there they are. Whispering silently as I sleep, complelling me to make a move and to let him know how I feel. Rei and Jessica have felt lust and even love before. I haven't. Maybe this is my chance. To break free of my dark purpose, and embrace life for what it is mean to be. Through lust and love, I shall be free.
