The life and childhood Story of Tim Veneno: Dazed and confused

28 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Friends are only foes for Tim Veneno. Everyone that Tim had met in his life he considered a foe, his mom, his dad, his grandmother, his grandfather, everyone. Tim had never been able though to meet his grandmother, and his grandfather except for one trip his mom and dad took. But, Tim was young, really young and he barely remembers it. "You're never going to make it in the wrestling business Tim, if you keep on wrestling like that." Tim had just finished another match in the Southern Wrestling Federation, as he was getting yelled at the by owner of the promotion, like normal. Times were hard for Tim as he struggled to survive day in and day out. "What do you have to say for your shit performance out there tonight? You almost injured one of our upcoming superstars. Tim you're boring the fans out there during your promos, you need to learn how to work the crowd, and your gimmick I don't think anyone is feeling it." The owner looked around, and then looked at Tim and released a short sigh. "Tim, listen I'm sorry but I've got no choice but to let you go, I can't have you here when you're putting my wrestlers career in jeopardy. Come back when you've learned to wrestle." Tim's only way of making money was now gone, he didn't know what he was going to do, but he did know who to go to. The dark alley, a great place for a drug abuser to hide out during the nights, you don't exist to the world when your in the alley, your invisible to everyone else, its just you and the drug.
