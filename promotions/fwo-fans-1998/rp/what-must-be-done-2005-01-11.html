What Must Be Done

11 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tomei and Bakkahisa both stood and watched the Nurse carefully as she slowly unwound the gauze from their employer's head. He'd had numerous skin grafts to replace the facial burn tissue that had been torn away. The flesh still looked puffy, but for the first time in a long time, Nick Brandish almost looked... normal. His ear, however, remained unattached. "They forgot your ear," Tomei commented as he pointed to the noticeable gap on the side of Brandish's head. "I told them not to reattach it," Nick said as he stared straight ahead. "I took a piece of Vic, he took a piece from me." Tomei chuckled foolishly. "You took more than an ear though." The stare he received from he who was known as Monster locked the Thai's jaws shut and he swallowed hard. Try as he might, Tomei couldn't muster the strength to apologize. Not that it would have helped him. Bakkahisa broke the silent tension. "You show him much respect," he told Brandish. "He was like a brother to me," Nick said as he focused his attention on the wall across from him. The Nurse continued to remove the gauze. "Crazy, a sociopath, but a brother. He's going through a hard time right now by trying to be something he's not." "Will you be returning to Wrestling?" Bakkahisa asked. Nick remained silent while the Nurse finished her business. "I'll be back in a few minutes," she told the big man. The nod Nick gave her was barely perceptible, but the Nurse took her cue and left the three men alone. Brandish turned his head. "I'm not sure yet," he told Bakkahisa. "I might, after a time. Once I bury Father, and get the business back on course, I'll see if my future lies with the fWo." "Our tickets are booked," Tomei said when he finally found the strength to speak. "Three tickets back to Bangkok, as soon as you are able to travel." "Fine." Before he could say anything further, Nick felt his arms start to tremble and spasm. His chest flexed and tightened, like a huge electrical charge was being sent through his body causing every muscle to contract. His eyes grew wide, his lips pulled back and his jaw clenched. The bed shook while the EKG beeped erratically. Young Tomei looked at Bakkahisa, desperately hoping the older man knew what to do. The grey-haired Thai stood and watched Nick thrash around, quickly moving equipment and monitors out of his reach. After almost a minute, the spasms subsided and Nick weakly lifted his arm to wipe the foam from the edge of his mouth. "Go back to the hotel," he whispered aloud. "Get my medicine." Tomei looked concerned. "Are you sure you should take anything in your condition?" "Go," Nick ordered feebly. The two men started to leave, but Nick grabbed Bakkahisa by the wrist. "Stay for a moment." Bakkahisa pointed at the door. "Go get the car," he ordered Tomei. The young man nodded and quickly did as he was told. As soon as they were alone, Bakkahisa turned to Brandish. "What is it?" "Your son mocked me and questioned my decision. When we return to Bangkok, I only want his head and hands to make the trip with us. You will cancel his ticket as soon as you've killed him." Nick looked into the old man's eyes. "I trust I will not have to repeat myself." Bakkahisa cringed inside. "You will not. I apologize for my son's indiscretion." Nick nodded. "His head in my garden will be apology enough." As hard as it was to fathom, Bakkahisa knew what he had to do. He knew he'd suffer much longer than his son Tomei would, but it had to be done. Before he left, Bakkahisa asked Nick a question. Maybe he asked out of bitterness, or simply out of curiosity.
