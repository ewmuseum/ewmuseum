Ribs and Toddlers - Equal Tortures

26 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
May15th, 2003 2:48pm Residence of Brandon Roche Amy straightened her back with a sigh, flexing her sore shoulders to ease the tired muscles. The past hour of her life had been a pain previously unknown to her, having only ever needed to care for one child at a time, let alone the... "Mommy!!!" Toddlers. "Amy!!!!!" From. "...Mommy!!!" Hell. ".......AAAAAAAAAAAAMYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYY!!!!" A Ninja Turtle had somehow wedged itself into her shoe and soon became aquainted with flight. "Guys...please?" She plead, "Just one second...one second of peace?" "You no nice, Daddy say you nice." "Well daddy doesn't throw toys at me, does he?" "No, bu' mommy say---" This one definitely had to be cut off at the pass. "I know what 'mommy' says." On a bitter note that went far above the young boy's head, Amy eased back against the leather couch her own son occupied, seeing little Jarod Roche follow. "Why don't you two find something fun to do? How about that lego stuff?" Without so much as a word the boys looked at one another, then to the large box of colorful plastic pieces in the corner. The large, neat, closed box... The large, neat box... The large, block-littered floor... Maybe that hadn't been the best idea Amy'd ever had, but it earned her a moment's peace that she very gladly took advantage of by leaning back against one arm of the sofa. She had no intention to sleep, of course. All the lithe little redhead wanted was a second where she didn't have to be on-guard against something and - though Brandon's ex-wife was prone to randomly visiting when she didn't see his car in the yard, which would mean Amy was most-likely alone in the house - this was a rare moment where she had nothing to worry about. Not because Ana wouldn't probably show up, but because there were greater threats on her mind - things that dwarfed the discomfort of dealing with the jilted leftovers of a failed marriage. Things like Kellen Kinkade, for instance. Aimz had little hunches sometimes...more often than not they were dead on correct and this one frightened her more than anything: Kinkade wouldn't relent or even pause for a breath...he was animalistic and, though overly confident, a hard nut to crack. There was way too much she needed to do if there was a chance to walk through his 'wrath' with her sanity intact. Rest wasn't enough...and she got none anyway. Physically, it'd be difficult to take him one-on-one - not impossible by any means, but it meant she'd need to really push the 'hardcore rules' surrounding the title around her waist. Sunday the 18th...that's when she'd get her first real shot at him - No signing papers, no barbells, just simple physicality, 'hardcore' fighting. Kellen didn't seem terribly concerned about how well he executed his German suplexes, and neither was Amy...it was only about hurting the other now, not about how it was done and she'd proven that with a pen once already. Yeah, so nobody thought she could do it... Sure, Kinkade was insane... ...and yeah...Amy Campbell, for the first time in the longest time, was physically afraid of somebody. Somebody other than Brandon's kids.
