Rapture.

23 January 2000

fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Rapture. January 23, 2000 Geoff Green moved the camera in closer to get a better view of the man sitting in the front row. As he approached the ring, it became evident that it was the man who was in control of the company he's employed by. He approached him uneasily at first, before asking him to do an interview. Dead decided he would, and began speaking. "Did you sleep good last night, Paul? And are you going to sleep good tonight? You know, they say people dream in black and white. What are you going to see when you close your eyes? I'll tell you what you're going to see... my fist in your face and my foot in your ass as I make your life a living hell. Your life has been made out of defending the fWo... that has been your career. You've won but one title, but we all no titles don't matter that much. We know you're one of the more prominent figureheads in the fWo, with or without a title. That has been your life... that has been your career. It's what you're known for. You could've spent the last twenty years of your life preparing for today, and it still wouldn't be enough time. I gave that pathetic Chris Kidman, Jr. three hundred and eighty four hours before CyberSlam '98. You've had but a mere seven days. Not enough time to prepare for what you are about to endure tonight. You can talk about squeezing the life out of me all you want, but talk is cheap... especially coming from a man who has weakness. I know your fears... I know your weaknesses... you don't know mine; because I have none. I fear nothing. I fear no one. Not even God himself... if he exists... could keep me from getting what I want. He could stand in front of me today... I'd knock his ass down without a hesitation. You think you can defeat me?" Dead sneered as he continued. "How can you expect to defeat me when you are merely but a man... and I am immortal? That is the question... you cannot defeat me. You won't. Tonight, it all ends. Slowly. Painfully. I ended Jim Dudley's career back in November of 1998. Tonight... I end not only your career... I end your life. Trust me." Dead got up from his chair and walked away confidently, while Green began to seek out other wrestlers to interview.
