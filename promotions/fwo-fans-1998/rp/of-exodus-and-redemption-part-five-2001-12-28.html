Of exodus and redemption (part five)

28 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You... need... to... stop... being... UNHAPPY!" Inside the ring stood Joy, who had come out through the crowd and looked angry for one of the few times in his life. The Dark Riders turned, and when Death saw who it was who had challenged him he began to laugh. Joy smiled, and nodded. "That's right... that's better... you big meanies with all your 'Oh, I'm going to make everyone miserable and bring you all into darkness, grumble, grumble, grunt'... you need to just get that sad stick out of your wazzoo and replace it with a pretty old candy cane!" Death stopped laughing and looked down at Joy for a moment, and then raised his middle finger. Joy gasped, and the Dark Riders turned and started to walk away. Joy stomped his foot and started up the entrance ramp after the Dark Riders. He reached Death just as he was starting to duck under the curtain. Joy grabbed Death's shoulder and spun him around, and then pasted a happy face sticker onto Death's chest. Death raged and grabbed Joy's arm and tossed him aside growling, "Fallen, get rid of him!" Fallen started, and then backed off. "There's no point... he's just a harmless nut... come on, let's just go. Tomorrow it all ends anyway..." Death looked at Fallen for a moment, and then slapped Fallen with a backhanded blow that took Fallen down to his knees. Death pointed to Demon, and the wrestler leapt at Joy. Demon kicked Joy in the head, and grabbed him and began to repeatedly punch Joy in the skull. Fallen screamed, and the fans once again began to pelt the wrestlers with garbage. Death laughed a deep laugh, and Poison cried... as Fallen reached for Demon. A wildly screaming Demon meanwhile picked up Joy and ran toward the edge of the entrance ramp, releasing Joy's limp body as he neared the side. Joy tumbled over, and things seemed to suddenly get very quiet. Joy fell... and one arm bent to protect himself as he tumbled. Demon had stopped screaming and fell silent, and Fallen reached out into empty air. Joy fell head first, and when he landed he lay still. Demon screamed now, but this was a very different scream... a scream of anguish. Fallen quickly ran down the ramp, but even as medical personnel flooded the area and John Skyler reached his one time partner and friend, he already knew it was too late.
