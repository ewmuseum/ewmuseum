The Aftermath

2 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"So.....it's over." The words of the blue-haired alter-ego of Kali Saturos, Rei, echoed through the fragile wind of the forest, on the cold day. Everything seemed covered in gray. Jessica, the other alter-ego, nodded her head. "Kali killed .One in the void." she said. "Somehow, she found out about the Chaos thing being a hoax. How it was all .One and .Two behind all of the weirdness." "Including talking to me." Rei said. "I figured that at the time. Existance isn't something that can just be created out of thin air." "Don't give me the philosphy, Rei." Jessica snapped. "I didn't come by for that, and you don't see me preaching God's word to you, so I'd appreciate it if you showed me the same kindness in return." Rei nodded. "I'm sorry, Jessica, I don't want to fight. Not today. Not when Kali Saturos is still out there, still in that frame of mind. God, who knows what she'll do next?" Jessica nodded in agreement. "We need to figure out what her next move is before she makes it." Jessica said. Rei thought about it for a moment. "I'm pretty sure Kali would want to go after .Two next." Rei said. "But how would she do that?" Jessica wondered. "Kali's never seen .Two. Only I have." "Do you remember what she looks like?" Rei asked her. "Her hair was long and black with a red streak down the middle. About the same size as us. She wore those same contacts Kali started wearing so she doesn't appear to have pupils, so I can't tell you her eye color. And nothing about her face, either, as she wore a kind of mask over just the front of her face, like .One's only with two dots." "That's a pretty good description." said Rei. "Well, all we can hope is that whoever .Two is now can weaken Kali long enough for one of us- preferbly me- to take control." "It's not an easy thing." Jessica said, ignoring Rei's remark. "You were half-dead when Kali took control from you, remember?" "Yeah." Rei said. "And if Kali dies, all of us die." "Obviously." Jessica snorted. The two of them were left with nothing more to do than to plan out the downfall of Kali Saturos. They came together in a time of need, despite having so little in common- but the questions were numerous, and the answers were all unclear. A new day dawned before them, a day which they were not prepared for, but a day they must face anyway. The future of Kali Saturos, Rei, and Jessica remained unclear.
