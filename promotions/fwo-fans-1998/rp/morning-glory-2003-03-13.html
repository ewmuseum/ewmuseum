Morning Glory

13 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday, February 2nd... With the key swiped from the desk clerk, Brandon had made his way up to Amy's room. Rumors abounded about the events of the previous night and now in the mid-afternoon he felt it was safe to make sure Amy Campbell had indeed survived. Looking down at the keycard again Brandon twirled it in his fingers before finally sliding it down the lock. With the green light on Brandon quietly turned the handle and peeked his head in, hoping not to actually catch the object of his admiration in a state of undress. Seeing Amy in her tank top with the heavy covers wrapped continuously around her legs Brandon sighed a bit of relief. She was still out of it... and dressed. "...Amy?" The paragon whispered at first, afraid to awaken a sleeping demon of some sort. "Amy!? Sweety... it's Brandon... hey girl... you alive?" Her movements were barely noticable at first, a twitch of the hand and then twist of her hips as she rolled to face her mighty hero, eyes closed. "Mmph..." First a groan, then something that sounded somehwat like a sigh. "...'ey..." Amy's voice remained soft and groggy, until she opened her eyes to the Mexican sun filling the room. "GAH!!! BRIGHT!!!" Brandon smirked a little as his little gremlin stuffed her heavy head under a near flat pillow. Letting the weight of the door do the job on it's own he stepped to the edge of the bed and touched the bottom of her foot. "It's O.K. Amy, the bad lights are gone..." Before he could finish, Amy kicked at him, "Don't do that! I'm ticklish, damnit!" Finally, she rolled over to her back and propped herself slightly against the headboard. "Wha...what time is it?" Another groggy moan as she rubbed her temples, praying to be rid of the headache. "...remind me never to touch tequila again..." "It's just after 3." Brandon reached in his back pocket and produced a black leather wallet. Fumbling around for a second he continued. "From the looks of it you shouldn't have touched it this time..." Finding what he was looking for and with Amy's hazy eyes on him Brandon produced a small blue cellophane packet from the wallet. "I have something here though that should make you all better..." "What? You brought crack?! Awww...you're the best!" Even feeling about ten seconds from death, she couldn't help but try and joke, but laughing made her head feel like it was about to pop. "Urggggh....drinking shouldn't have consequences, something so good's not allowed to have a downside!" She shut her eyes again, trying to calm a tinge of nausea. "You sure something strong enough to make me feel better's even been created yet?" "Well, actually drinking isn't good at all, but that's a conversation for another day. Maybe after you feel better and I can borrow a soapbox from Deacon or something." With a wink Brandon retrieves a small plastic cup of water for his hapless patient and pours the contents of the packet in. With a slight fizzle the ingredients intermingle and he hands it to the groggy redhead. "It's just alka-seltzer and a powdered aspirin, not a miracle drug." "...the hell?!" Amy watched the little white particles swirl around, peered closer at the glass and then set it on the nightstand. "I don't want tampered water.....I'll take Tylenol later." At last, she sat up a little more and managed a warm smile at Brandon. "Kinda missed you, ya know...even just a couple of days being out with Angel and everyone. This isn't exactly the state I wanted to greet you again in, but meh...it'll have to do." "Yes... you WILL drink the 'creepy cocaine water', um... for me." He'd ignored her last statement to get his point - and orders - across. With another wink and smile Brandon leaned over and kissed Amy on the forehead, smoothing back her tossled hair. "You know, you'd heal a lot faster if you didn't pollute your body with all of that alcohol. When exactly did they say you could wrestle again?" "Well...I kinda pushed the date I set back..............so it's next week now, maybe the week after..." She pouted and looked up to Brandon again, but he was unrelenting and finally, she took a few sips of the water before nearly spitting it back out. "GAH! Damn you and your disgusting Aspirin!" "The taste is punishment as much as the headache is. You should have simply found me... I'm sure we could have found something more fun than some drinking contest, even if it was with Ivy..." Not waiting for the befuddled Amy to make another move Brandon nudged her over claiming a few inches of the bed for himself. Wrapping an arm around her he settled in making himself confortable as she finally let her head sink in to his chest. A little taken aback by the Paragon's move, she was quiet for a moment to size up the situation, having never really seen a 'close' side of him. After a moment though, she realized it wasn't exactly unpleasant and let herself continue, mumbling a little. "Bleh...you're no fun. We could've sat around and played canasta! WOO!" Amy stuck her tongue out at Brandon a bit to let him know her sarcasm was just for fun, then sagged back against him. "Besides...you're all..like....broken and stuff. Damn Borst..." "What the hell is canasta!?" Not waiting for a real response Brandon messed with the hair hanging in Amy's eyes. "I'll heal, Pete really messed up my nose though. That damn brace I have to wear while I'm in the ring really messes with my vision." "You'll heal...but you're still wrestling, Mister 'Sit in the hospital with Amy for two days telling her to take it easy'!" "I've taken a few shows off. I'm not there tonight and Sunday was just some verbal stuff with Pete. Trust me... I try and practice what I preach." "Damn..." She pouted, looking up at him again while whining, "So this means I can't bitch and moan at you not to wrestle, doesn't it?" "Well, it means you can't bitch and moan about me not wresting so far... I... actually have a match with Eugene on Sunday." Brandon knew that was going to give Amy all the ammunition to mess with him even more. He couldn't wait though, he missed it when he went awhile without being able to wrestle. The redhead's mouth went wide and she sat up, ignoring her head's spinning to smack Brandon half-playfully, half-aggressively on the chest. "I can't believe you! That's SO not fair...you can yell at me, but run off and do what you were telling me not to!" Brandon just smiled at her aggression, just like him she was beginning to feel like a caged animal. Stroking the back of her neck in a calming circular motion he thought to himself how comfortable this all was. "You know, you could come to the show this Sunday even if you don't have anything planned. I know it's a bit before you get going again. Call if you want and let her know that you won't be going with the band this time..." "I already planned it," She winked, "Whether you wanted me there or not...missing another show threatened to drive me off the deep end." And with that, Amy laid back again, running a hand through her own hair. "Besides...I missed you those couple'a days, I'm not going anywhere no matter how crazy I drive you. Not until you drive me nuts, anyway." A smile tied off the thought. "You don't have anything booked for Sunday do you? You're just going for support right?" A look of worry knitted itself across Brandon's brow. "You don't wrestle again for at least a few more shows... right?" Amy grinned, running a hand through the Paragon's hair instead. "I wrestle again this Wednesday." She raised an eyebrow, all but challenging him to challenge her. "Nothing wrong with that...I'm feeling pretty good, pretty much all healed 'n stuff." She watched his face for another moment, and her smirk dimmed to a look of equal concern, "Hey...don't worry, alright? I'm gonna take it easy no matter what I'm doing for a couple of weeks." He looked at her, ready to scold her. From the raised eyebrow though he reconsidered, her strong will was one of the things that attracted him to her in the first place. There was no reason to challenge it now. Instead he figured he'd make a point without being so forward about it. Leaning over Amy and getting within a breath's distance of her face Brandon stares in to her mesmerizing eyes. Instinctively and seemingly against her will Amy leans her head to the side and begins to close those eyes expectingly. Then Brandon pokes her just above the healing wound. "SHIT..." Amy bit down hard on her lip, pressing her head back against the headboard while twisting Brandon's arm almost all the way around. "What the hell was that for?!" "Just to prove a point, you need to be careful. What would happen if someone planted a knee right where I poked you at?" As soon as the words escaped his lips Brandon knew how much of a father figure he sounded like. "What would happen? It'd hurt like hell, and I'd give a little knee of my own straight between their legs like I should've just done with you!" She whimpered, resting a hand over the wound. "Kiss it and make it feel better?" With a sly smile Brandon knew he might be able to weasel his way back in her good graces. His concern for her was genuine though and he knew how the boys in the business liked to hit a stiff shot or two on the females. It irked him to no end, but he had to remind himself not to smother or there would only be problems ahead. She had gotten this far without him standing over her, Amy could take care of herself, loathe as he was to admit it. Amy, of course, couldn't help but grin...she held on as long as she could, trying to look unamused, but it wouldn't work for the life of her so she relaxed a little. "You know me too well..." She'd mumbled, "But you still deserve a ping in the nose for that." Truthfully, she was as worried about him as he was about her. He'd already busted up his nose pretty badly, and had apparently decided to get in the ring again before it could even begin to heal...which was exactly what she'd done, but at least Amy knew her boundaries...she was scared Brandon might not have learned his. Sure, her fears were irrational and unfounded - he was a veteran of the business compared to her - but it was just Amy being paranoid as usual, she knew it well enough. "How about instead of a ping on the nose I get a peck on the cheek?" He knew how much of a natural worrier she was and as much as he tried to assuade her fears it never worked. She had been around so much turmoil in her life it seemed as if the fiery redhead would never know true happiness. He also knew that with what he was feeling for her that he wouldn't stop trying to help her find it. Maybe, just maybe if he brought her in closer with his life, with his family, she would start to learn what it meant to be content. "We need to sit down and talk about all of this 'us' stuff... oh, and I was thinking next week maybe you, Sean, Jarod and myself could hang out for awhile...?" "Pecks on the cheek and meeting your closest companions? Jeez, Brandon..." There was a heavy sarcastic tinge to her voice, but not one of cruelty...just playful jostling. "I don't know if I can handle this...I mean, we've only known one another for like four months, isn't this moving a little too fast?" The sentence had been closed with a wink, but Amy'd caught herself again...she wasn't good with 'this emotional stuff' and never had been, so she tended to turn to sarcastic joking, but this time she realized it wasn't exactly giving Brandon the credit he deserved. "Sorry...I'm just wacky like that. I've wanted to meet 'em for a while...but Ana wasn't exactly about to let me near them. And I really think we should talk about this 'us' stuff...clear up a few blurred lines and all that good jazz." "Sounds like a date to me..." With a wink of his own Brandon played back with Amy. "Why don't we lay back and let you rest up a bit more. We need to get going soon though so I don't pull a Hardy and show late tonight." Despite words, it was doubtful either of them really cared if they ever moved again. [This has been an Aimz/ Brand Frontier collaboration.]
