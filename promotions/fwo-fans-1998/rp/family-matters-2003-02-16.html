Family Matters

16 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Chicago, Illinois definitely lived upto being a windy city this Saturday evening. Erik Kelly drank a cup of coffee, something he was not used to doing at all, as he sat in the waiting room of Chicago Hope Medical. He was tired. He could feel it. Hadn't slept much on the frequent voyages into and out of the United States. While he wasn't complaining about the process, going through customs just took a lot out of him for all the time it required. Pamela looked in worse condition than him. She had barely left the hospital since her dad's stroke took place. She barely ate, and her appearance was something she wasn't as concerned about right now. Erik had spent the last couple hours waiting for Pamela to get done spending some quality time with her dad. There was probably a good chance she fell asleep by his side. Erik was fine with that. He needed some time to just think about things in general. Focus on his upcoming match with Spyder this Sunday. Pamela made her way down a corridor to locate Erik. The two embraced as she tried to fight back tears.* "There's no progress.", she spoke as she wiped the tears from her bloodshot eyes. "I'm beginning to fear the worse." "I know, babe. I know..." Erik could only console her, understanding there was no words he could say to make her better. "And I'm sorry, Erik. I know you've done a great deal of travelling to come see me, and I've barely been able to pay attention to anything beyond my dad. How have you been?" Erik thought for a moment as he brushed a couple blonde curls out of her vision. "Well, the other night, I did have this dream where I tore my face off, and I was Spyder. Then I took the mask off, and I was Matt Loco." Pamela looked at Erik with a disheveled, slightly startled expression. "You sound like you're having the same nights as I've been having." "Most likely. Hey, hate to say this to you, but I'm going to need to head back. I know you can't go with me, but I know you're still there when the match goes down." Pamela nods half heartedly. "I'm glad you understand. I just can't go anywhere right now until I know what will happen." Pamela paused briefly, feeling the tears well up again. "He was always there for me. Any scrape, any cut, any heartbreak...I could always count on Dad to be there. I can't let him down." Erik smiled compassionately, understanding his wife's troubled state. "Then you stay here and be with him. And I'll dedicate my match tomorrow to the both of you." Pamela smiled, and the two exchanged a kiss and embrace. It would be long moments before they let go of each other.
