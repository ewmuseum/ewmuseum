First Aid

6 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOOooowwwwwwooooo Tempest moaned and pouted and muttered as the medical technician tried to clean a small gash just under her hairline on her forehead. "Sit still!" he said, trying not to laugh. Tempest folded her arms and said, "Well it's not fair. Guys like Creed and Chronos keep inviting me to play kickball... only I'm the ball!" She sighed and said, "Oh, it could be worse. I could be Monte." She sighed and leaned forward enough to see across the hall where Monte Burns sat with an ice pack the size of a small throw pillow pushed against his lower back. Tempest looked up and said to the tech, "Are you done yet? I'll be ok." The tech followed her gaze and then smiled. He nodded and moved back allowing her to hop down and head across to Monte. "How you doin'?" she said as she poked her head into the room, with a soft smile. "I guess I'm doing okay," Burns said with a grimace. He looked up at Tempest and tried to smile, but it was obvious he was still in some major pain. "Well, maybe not okay, but it'll have to do." "Awww..." Tempest said with a sympathetic look. "Can I help? Massage? Kiss and make better?" "All of the above?" Monte asked with a mischievous grin. Tempest chuckled and said, "Silly, you'd end up hurting yourself worse." She scooted next to him and kissed his cheek. "Any better than last night?" Burns adjusted the ice pack a little bit as he leaned back against the wall. Pain was shooting through his back, and he seemed to be showing some signs of giving in to the pain and just popping a big load of pills. "I don't know really," he said with a sigh. "The doctor told me it'll fluctuate from day to day, but it's seemingly been getting worse." Tempest leaned close and said, "And your lips are turning blue too. Cold?" "Not really," Monte said with a half-smile. A moment later, he turned and locked eyes with her. "I guess I could use a little warming up though." Tempest giggled and said, "How did I guess you'd say that? Scoot up a little." He did so and Tempest scooted in behind him and wrapped her arms around him. She scooted up close and held the ice pack against his back with her belly and nuzzled his shoulder. "There, better?" "That's much better," Burns said with a smile. As he leaned back a bit against Tempest, Monte slid his arm down to her legs and began to slowly rub them as well. He turned his head around a bit and smiled again. "How does that feel?" "Nice..." she said and nuzzled his cheek. "You'll have to kiss my head and make it better later." She pouted a little and said, "Creed and Chronos kept dropping me on it." For a moment, it appeared some anger was welling up inside of Burns. He knew that Tempest had taken quite a few hard shots from the guys in recent weeks, and he partially blamed himself for allowing some of that to happen. "Hope this helps some for now," Monte said as he leaned around and softly kissed her neck. She smiled an utterly sappy smile and sighed as she said, "Yeah... I just wish I could help more. I mean best case scenario is I'm a good distraction during a tag match. But make it against someone like Chronos and I might as well throw wiffle balls at him." "You're doing fine in there, Tempest," Monte said with a reassuring tone as he held her hand in his. "You just need to make sure you wrestle your game and not get caught up in what they want you to do. That's all this is about. Wrestle the way you know how, and you'll be fine." Tempest nodded, rubbing her head against his shoulder. "I know. I just do better with cruiserweights." She giggled and added, "In several respects!" as she squeezed him gently around the waist. "I would concur with that," said Monte as he turned around slightly to look at her face. Burns reached out with his hand and lightly stroked her cheek, but a shooting pain in his back forced him to turn around once more. Tempest winced involuntarily and then wrapped her arms around him again. "It'll be ok Monte. Don't worry about me back here. I'm not going anywhere. Just relax and feel better ok?" she said with a soft note of worry, and kissed his shoulder. Monte looked down for a moment, wondering just where he would be at this point if he hadn't found someone like Tempest to share his time with. As those thoughts swirled through his head, he reached down and lifted Tempest's hand up with his and softly kissed the back of it. "Thank you, Tempest," he said with a smile. "I really appreciate that." She smiled and closed her eyes, happily snuggling against him as the scene faded.
