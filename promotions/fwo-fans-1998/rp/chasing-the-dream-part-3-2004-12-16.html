Chasing The Dream - Part 3

16 December 2004

Welcome to Vegas... May 5th 1999 - 11:44 PM Milo began walking once again, and if luck was in his favor he could be onto the highway in less then two hours. While on his walk, Milo got to see many Casino stripped streets, which were decorated with a sea of florescent light. He'd always heard so much about Las Vegas, and here he was. "You looking for some fun tonight sweet thang?" a woman asked, slowly rubbing her hand down her stomach. "No thanks." Milo replied, not allowing his eyes to contact those of what appeared to be a prostitute. "Fuck yourself then faggot!" she hollered as Milo continued on his way, almost ignoring her existence. For some reason, he didn't seem to mind being yelled at. Living in LA. Had taught Milo many things, and one was that people would do most anything for money. It was written all over this woman's face that she was an addict starving for some sort of narcotic. She was doing what she had to, and getting what she wanted. "Live and let live..." Milo said softly, not allowing his words to make much of an intelligible sound. Milo moved on throughout the City, and was finally reaching the outskirts when he noticed a train station. Maybe it was luck; maybe it was fate, but there sat a train, which read "Jackson's Surplus. Salem OR." in large bold lettering. Salem Oregon just happened to be one step closer to Canada, so Milo quickly hopped aboard as the train was slowly pulling away. Milo gripped the ladder on the side of the boxcar for dear life, as the train continued to pick up speed. Doing his very best at fighting gravity, he managed to slide open the door, just enough so he could slip through. It was pitch black inside, but his eyes soon adjusted to the dark and he found a nice resting spot behind what appeared to be a crate. "Oregon, here I come..." the clumsy words which were tossed into the air, as Milo closed his eyes and dozed off into a cold slumber.
