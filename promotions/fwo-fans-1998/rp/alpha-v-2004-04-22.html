Alpha V

22 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jack always had a thing for graveyards. He never liked to step overtop of soil where a body was buried underneath, thinking that the dead people wouldn't care much for their grass being stomped upon. It was almost as if he believed that in the afterlife, men and women remained in their coffins and could hear everything above them, echoing out as if their graves were truly caves. There was a small gazebo in place, near the middle of the cemetery, and that's where he led the woman he loved to. They rested there for a moment, the pale moonlight shining down, but not enough to thwart the darkness surrounding them. Their eyes however, had adjusted enough to see one another, and they stared into each other's hearts for quite some time. Kate was smiling like always, and that only made things worse for Jack. Because he knew that he was the one that would ruin that beautiful smile. He sighed, lowering his head in shame before she lifted his chin back up to repeat their gaze from only seconds before. "Kate," he started off, rubbing the back of his neck with a free hand, the other attached to hers in a deathlock. "We have to talk." The four worst words in the English language. Seinfeld was truly right, as Kate's shoulders dropped and her eyes went wide. Her eyebrows pointed up, as her smile turned into her biting her bottom lip. "What is it Jack?" Jack squinted his eyes. The nights were getting too long for him, and he could feel the swaying breeze sending him into his awakened slumber. "I'm..." he started off, not sure what to say. Sure, he's been thinking of the words in his mind, running them through spell check and what have you on repeat, but when the time came, he froze like a deer caught in headlights. Not that he could ever test that theory. "I'm not going to Penn State." Kate sighed and lowered her head. She knew what was coming next. "I'm going to Mexico." Her eyes began to tear up just a small bit. "I have to do this Kate, I have to." Jack said, releasing her arm and standing to his feet to make his point more dramatically. "I've been dreaming of this moment my entire life, and if I stop now because you want me to..." Jack drifted off. "I know I'd wind up hating you." Jack looked away for a split second, but then returned to his knees in front of the now sobbing Kate. "But I love you, I always have, I always will." "You love me?" She stood up to her feet. Jack took the offensive moments ago, and the tides were now turning. "Then don't go!" Her usually beautiful green eyes seeped with tears that ran down her cheek, until one dripped onto Jack's forehead. "You saw what the business did to your family Jack, you don't want that to happen to you." Kate lowered her head even further. "I don't want you to be hurt." "But that's life Kate. People get hurt. Good people, honest people who don't deserve the pain they go through. But it helps us become better people. Because we can always improve, no matter how righteous or how heroic we become." Jack kissed Kate's hand, and she pulled away. "I know what you're going through." "Oh you do, do you?" Kate's tears and sadness had turned into anger at this point. "How could you possibly know what it feels like. Oh great Jack Harmen, why don't you enlighten me?" A single tear dropped out of the corner of Jack's eye. "You know all those long prophetic speeches you hear in those romantic movies about how it tears them up inside? I feel all of that right now at this moment. It's hard, it feels like there's a laser and a saw and a shark all at once ripping at my flesh, but I have to do this." Jack reached forward and touched Kate on her knee, but she immediately pulled back once again into a fetal position. "I love you Kate, always know that. But I have to know." "Love?" Kate shouted. "You didn't even know the meaning of the word until I showed up." She lifted him to his feet by placing her hand gently under his chin. "I loved you as best I could. Wasn't that enough?" "Then come with me!" Jack said, grabbing Kate's sobbing face with his hands, pulling her in close for what he would perceive as a kiss. She only sobbed louder now though. "Come with me to Mexico." "I can't." Jack kissed her on the lips, a short kiss to say the least. She sniffled, wiping the salt out of her eyes. "I have my scholarship." They each sniffled, and rested their foreheads together. So, this is what it comes down to. It's the choices we make that shape our worlds. Each of their eyes were closed, neither could bare the look they percieved the other to give them. So they rested there, each person trying to collect themselves. Neither being able to comfort the other. Neither at fault. Neither without love for the other. But their paths could not stay the same. Jack pulled his Dykstra jersey to his eyes to whip away the tears, and dabbed Kate's eyes clear as well. She raised her hands and grabbed his, pulling them away from his face as they looked into one another's eyes. And they kissed. For one last time. And cried with each moment.
