Gone? Forgotten?

19 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Camera fades in, and soft dark settings allow the face of Ric Chronos to appear almost as if it extends from the shadows. Ric's eyes are downcast, his face pitched slightly forward. He almost appears to sway, but the fact that little is visible below his neck makes it difficult to determine. "Gone. Gone for just a few weeks, and everyone has forgotten me. Not one call, not one letter, not one comforting word of advice the entire time I was... away. No one seems to care who I am, or what I'm capable of doing. Good. You see, I don't like being forgotten - but this gives me an excuse. This gives me an advantage - by forgetting what I do, I'll hit you by surprise when I do it. And while you're all playing the parts of the amnesiac, let one memory clearly pervade your thoughts. I have never lost at Cyberslam, and I've always had an impact. So, forget me now - because after Cyberslam, you'll never be able to make the memory go away." Chronos finally looks up, and the last image we receive from the camera is a mask of determination and anger.
