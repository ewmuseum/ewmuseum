Adjust: Finale

6 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"The... Codemaster?" Leigh Landers asked with both a considerable amount of surprise and a considerable amount of laughter that she was desperately trying to hold back. Allen Speck frowned as he sat across from his friend, Leigh Landers. After a bit of rest and a bit of planning for what Allen would be doing in the fans Wrestling organization, the two of them had elected to sit around in their hotel room and discuss the turn of events. "Yeah." Allen said. Leigh scratched her frizzly hair and smiled at Allen, "And you agreed to this because...?" Allen didn't answer that question right away. Instead, he fidgeted a bit, blinked, and tried very desperately to come up with a logical explaination for what had happened between himself and Samuel Smooth. "I think there was something in the airline peanuts." Allen said, before another frown followed, and he nervously stated his next few words, "It, uh... seemed like a... good idea... ...at the time." "See, I told you that you should've rested up before you went and saw anyone, but nooo, big bad Allen Brown has to go and be the King of Self-Dignity Mutilation." Leigh said, almost big-sisterly to Allen Speck, who just simply frowned in response and recalled some of the finer points of this idea. "Oh yeah," Samuel Smooth said to a fake-smiling Allen Speck, who was trying desperately to stay polite in this situation, "We'd need you to act... I dunno, more black?" "More black?" Allen asked, his eyebrow raised. "Bring in the lingo. The kids love that." Smooth said. "Um..." Allen said, completely unsure about the idea, but the way Smooth was talking made it seem like this could work, "Okay." Allen sighed to himself. He was such a moron when it came right down to it. "I wonder if it's too late to quit?" Allen asked. * . * Fast-forward to April 1st, 2003. A week later and another plane trip to another country later, Allen Speck found himself in Leigh's home country of England. Standing outside of the room where the Fuse Bros. were located, Allen Speck made his last-minute preperations. In front of him was Leigh Landers, who was adjusting his cape for the role. Allen frowned, not enjoying the idea of wearing black robes, nor this entire gimmick that they have created for him. Sure, on the bright side, he did know a thing or fifty about video games, but he was a wrestler before he was a God of the Video Games. "This is so stupid." Allen said. "Yes, yes, I'm sure I caught that somewhere between the seventith and seventy-first time you told me that." Leigh sarcastically said, before she shoved a small cart with a treasure chest that was made out of cardboard, and inevitably contained something within. "What's that?" "It's an Atari," Leigh said, "Some guy actually rigged this thing so that you could cheat by merely giving it a good whack, so you should be able to work some magic there." Leigh continued, "Also, as soon as Conor says "Got it", we'll cue the lights for you. From there, it's all on you. Okay?" "Okay. Thanks for the help." Allen said, giving Leigh a shaky smile. "Hey, what are friends for?" Leigh said, with a smile, before her smile grew wider, "Besides, in case you forgot, this is England, and I don't especially feel like letting you look bad in my country." "Got it!" The light. "You're on." Leigh said, stepping out of the way of Allen as he grabbed the cart and pulled it along with him. From there, he entered the room with the Fuse Bros. "Hello! You found my shop of strange and wonderful things!" And so began the trials of Allen Speck: The Codemaster. We hope you enjoy this flight, the captain has just turned on the no smoking signal, and turned off the fasten seatbelts light. The stewardess will be along at some time to give you peanuts and coffee. - fin -
