Bite the hand that feeds you...

13 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The statesmen will invent cheap lies, putting the blame upon the nation that is attacked, and every man will be glad of those conscience-soothing falsities, and will diligently study them, and to examine any refutations of them; and thus he will by and by convince himself that the war is justified. - Mark Twain Without a spring in her step, or even a slight smile after a victory like she'd had, Aimz could only be found slinking around the "backstage" area and, unusually enough, not expressing herself aloud... Her actions, however, spoke volumes. The redhead didn't even bump into another person, she seemed to be avoiding any contact with another human being with quite a passion, and didn't fail to make anybody take notice of that fact when she stepped into the catering area and perched atop a small two-seater bench in the farthest, darkest possible corner of the room. Sinking down into her seat, Amy had been perfectly content to be left alone, appearing to be innerly kicking herself for one reason or another...all the while attempting to hide her dizzy sway. She'd taken a few too many blows to the head earlier, and had actually been rendered unconcious for at least a solid minute during a match she'd confidently forced her way into, leaving Brand Frontier no option other than utilizing her as a partner. Of course, she noted, he'd objected whole-heartedly, forseeing the fate she'd suffer at the hands of two "veterans" who'd already proven that they weren't afraid to cheat, or take advantage of someone in the blink of a referee's eye. And, of course, Aimz had absolutely no intention of ever even considering admitting to Brandon that he'd been mostly right. Why? Because she was Amy Campbell...overly-independant, easily-addicted and incredibly eclectic. Her moods from minute to minute could be as different as night and day...and Brandon Roche was only just beginning to learn that. But he was learning nonetheless, she could tell by the way he seemed to leave her to herself when she had a certain look in her eye or tone to her voice. Thankfully, it looked like this may have been one of those times. The Paragon had stepped into the room when it had rid itself of a few of the 'superstars' she'd never speak to if her life depended on it, and his eyes had immediately set on hers. But it wasn't to be, Ana Roche was fast on his heels, casting Amy a look of her own that proved to be considerably less caring than her husband's. None of it phased the would-be star a bit, though. She was content to sit there until she absolutely had no choice but to go back to a bland, dank, cold and empty hotel room to lay in the dark for the night, feeling sorry for herself as usual for fucking things up so badly. She sat. Just sat and watched people come and go not paying her any mind, which she was thankful for. Though, Aimz couldn't help but notice Brandon lingering in the room, every-so-often glancing over at her with a concerned expression that seemed to plead with her for something, all the while trying to pay attention to the words of his wife. He was caught between hearing out the woman he loved and worrying over a good friend. It was then that Amy knew he'd approach, the look in his eyes said it all: the 'Mighty Hero' was both concerned and trying to take control for what he thought was her safety and best interests. Brandon glanced at Ana again after having to peel his eyes away from his makeshift tag team partner. Life sure was strange. To tell Brandon Roche that he would be sitting here tonight with what he had in the pit of his stomach would have normally brought his ire. Instead he was indeed struggling through a quagmire. Exactly what was he feeling when he looked at the young Superstar? Why on Earth was this happening now, just when everything had finally seemed perfect? Was it the strain that the fWo put on him or was it something far deeper, something far older? He couldn't tell but he did know one thing for certain, he needed to make sure Amy was all right. Looking at his wife for so long drew a smile from her as she cupped his stern jaw. He smiled back, instinctively, and warmly. It was a comfortable smile. One forged from years of trust, from years of surviving with each other and using each other for their strengths. He so loved her. With that smile though came a question. "Ana, I need to check on Amy. Lance said he saw her stumbling in the hall, he thinks she might have been concussed or something. That damn Kellen doesn't know when to quit..." Brandon's voice trailed off as he expected his wife to answer. She didn't. Instead she simply leaned over to her Mighty Hero and kissed him passionately, territorially. When their lips separated she gave him a knowing look, one that oddly made him feel a knot of guilt grow in his stomach. Ana smoothed back Brandon's still wet hair absently as she looked to Amy again. With a sigh of reluctance she slightly nodded to the 'little girl' before exiting. Brandon sat there for a moment realizing what he had done. He did feel guilty and he knew that in reality he shouldn't, the things that swirled in his head were simply random thoughts and nothing more. Why couldn't he be concerned with Amy's welfare and leave it at that? Why did it have to be something more? Looking around at the remaining few fWo Superstars and employees Brandon felt like all eyes were on him as he pushed the small blue and chrome chair away from the folding table and heaved himself in to a standing position. He had already begun to ache from the match that night. Walking over to Amy Campbell, Brandon could immediately feel the vibe she was intentionally emanating. He'd actually begun to predict Amy's moods with a bit of precision and normally gave her some room when need be, but this time he couldn't. All he cared about was her...or rather...all he cared about was how she was. Right? Shaking the question from his head he loomed large over Amy, casting her in a darker shadow than the one she purposefully put herself in. Crossing his arms and cocking his head to the side he decided to play the waiting game and see how long it took Aimz to actually acknowledge him. Amy just breathed deeply before taking what she made to look like a painstaking, monumental effort of tilting her head back to rest against the wall a little. She'd winced with the pain, but quickly tried to cover the movement by rubbing her eyes sleepily, as if she'd just had an itch. Aimz had no intention of parading for pity, so she made her first remark rather offensive and tried to chuckle to herself over the extravagant territorial show the Paragon's wife had put on for the younger girl, "Ana sure likes to let it be known what trophy belongs to what woman, hmm? I'm surprised she let you come over here; I mean, talking to me's practically a federal offense, isn't it?" "What?" The form and function of the question were successful. It nearly made Brandon step back physically with its sharpness and attitude. If he had any question before, then he certainly didn't now. He was surely talking to the real Amy Campbell. "I said..." She started again, but Brandon didn't let her finish. "I heard you, Amy...you just startled me is all." Brandon slid in next to Amy and didn't wait for her to make room for him. His broad shoulders pushed her over and she moved whether she wanted to or not. He was purposely trying to push her buttons now, if he got a rise out of her maybe that would lead to her opening up. It was almost formulaic in how she acted. He got what he wished for, she made as much of an attempt as humanly possible to put her mood on showcase by trying to inch her sore frame away...though relatively unsuccessful in her efforts, considering the arm of the bench wasn't exactly forgiving. "Well if the little bi--" Once again, Amy started a thought, but displayed a rarity in correcting herself. "...if your wife would take a second and realize you're about as un-attracted to me as a person could get, and with good reason, she might not have to bother herself with getting so pissy and leave the bitchy moods to me." Brandon thought on that for more time than he should have. Amy looked over and up at him by this time. She tilted her head slowly to the side. Partially out of wonderment and partially to relieve the pressure building at the base of her skull. When Brandon spoke it was with more uncertainty than he wished "...Yeah. If she would just take the time...she would see that... wouldn't she...?" "You need to cool your jets a bit though Red, I can't really sit here and let you call her names. Especially not for just caring about her family..." Brandon didn't mean to snap back at Amy but in the confused state he was in it was out of his mouth before he realized what he was saying. He winced himself this time, as if he was the one that was knocked for a loop. Trying to correct his verbal error in judgment Brandon quickly restarted as if he never said those dreadful words. "She's just a little tired and stressed about everything Amy, there's nothing to worry about. She just has a lot of time on her hands and sees things...that aren't there. Even though she hasn't seen you and Darcy together it's plainly obvious to everyone else that you don't harbor anything for me. Right?" A little lie would only help ease his mind right now and Amy saw that clearly, though she didn't let on. Against even her own thoughts, which she passionately tried to dismiss every time she saw or spoke to Brandon, she answered. "Yeah...that's right. But still...being 'tired and stressed' isn't any excuse for her to give me reason to want to strangle h---errr---yeah...." She'd caught herself 'revealing' things again. Airing thoughts in the open was what she'd been trying to avoid, unfortunately Brandon only seemed to draw truths and real feelings from her no matter how hard she tried to fortify her mind. His response wasn't exactly what she expected as she absently wrinkled her forehead and looked from the corner of her eye. "Heh. Well, I've felt like strangling her before as well, but only in the most liberal sense of the word." It was a comment made with tongue planted firmly in cheek. An attempt at lightening the mood a bit as well as reassure Amy that there were no real ill feelings, it worked if only to a small degree. Brandon moved over finally giving Amy some breathing room but placed his large hand in the middle of her back. Sparks flew. Literally. She was still in ring gear, ring gear that allowed for her to be as flexible as possible which meant that when Brand laid a hand on her, it was against a large and exposed patch of flesh between her shoulders. The static from the carpeted floor had ignited when they made contact and both jumped from the sudden shock, but they both also managed to crack a smile. Amy's faded first as she once again, but barely, concealed herself behind a stone visage. "Look, Angel...to put it bluntly I'm just worried. Okay?" He paused, but didn't intend to give her the time to answer. "I know you have this whole thing worked out where you keep everyone at arms length and you show up, do your job and go home. No one gets in, nothing gets out and everyone, especially yourself, is safe. But you know as well as I do that it's not working like you planned. Whether you want it or not, I'm...I'm here for you. We're...friends..." A sudden, blunt sickness hammered into Amy's chest for what she saw as little apparent reason. He'd hit the nail on the head and uncovered her relatively obvious, but still far too personal 'plan' to assure she'd not have to worry about being betrayed or hurt. She'd hoped to just be able to expect and assume that everybody would stab her in the back, thus preparing her for just about anything.... anything except for this. A peer had finally broken through the little force field and let go an onslaught of Godly force on her mental battlements. She knew she cared about Brandon, though not how she cared about Brandon and Amy was well aware that the feeling was mutual. But how to respond to something like he'd said? 'Simple. Stick to your guns, cling on and try hard as hell not to show weakness, or the fact that he's just entirely disassembled you.' Without having ask it aloud she had an answer to her own question. One she knew, one she had years to practice. "It's no plan. Maybe I just don't like nosy little jackasses poking and prodding into my business. Ever think of that?" "Truthfully, yes I thought of that. I thought about that and was beginning to be swayed. Then you go and do something like you did tonight. That changed everything." Brandon smirked because he knew he was right. He had always been able to read people fairly well and try as she might Amy was almost an open book to him. He could especially read the people that he was close to, that he cared for deeply. With that thought on his mind, maybe even clouding his reasoning Brandon leaned over to Amy Campbell. She jumped a bit at this sudden invasion of her personal space but for some reason beyond her she relaxed moments later. Brandon had come within inches of the side of her face. She didn't dare turn her head to look at him. They would be too close, what if they touched? A maelstrom of emotions erupted both as Brandon shocked even himself and kissed her... ...Square on the forehead, before rubbing the top of her head in a playful manner. The lump in his throat belied his actions though as he stood. "Just do me a favor dear, get the trainer to look you over. As soon as he got you down, Kellen took the cheap route and knocked you around pretty hard. Just get checked out, okay? Do it for me. If not, you might not have a chance to upstage me again..." She wanted to bolt up and beg him not to leave her alone, but the more egotistical side told her not to give in. After all, her accidental smile said enough...though it quickly turned to a half-pout, half-frown. "Who could upstage you? And in case you hadn't noticed, I didn't even get a clean pin. Hell, I was knocked out...I might have upstaged Graphic Violence' ego, but I..." Amy would've swerved from what she was about to say, but decided that it was obvious enough to both she and Brandon. The first words came almost painfully, the redhead would never get used to saying 'You're right'. "I...probably should've listened to you about the match. But I couldn't leave you out there without a partner, I would've fucking hated myself even more than I do now if those bastards had injured you and I hadn't done a thing to help." To Brandon it was Junior High all over again as his head swam with the words she had just spoke mixed in with a healthy dose of his own feelings. His lightheadedness spoke volumes about his physical condition as well as his mental state. "Well... I'm not the 'I Told You So' type so I won't say it. I will say though that as far as having you out there as my partner I was darn lucky." Those words brought another smile to both of their faces, as they did their eyes locked again. A silent, knowing moment passed. It was Aimz' turn to take a stab at brightening the mood and relieving the weird tension that built in those solid moments of silence. "Meh..." She teased and giggled a little, though catching herself acting a little too 'ditzy' for her own good. "You're just feeling lucky I didn't really mess with you and wear a sweater to the ring." "Ha!" It was loud, loud enough that the two remaining souls in the room turned to see the shadowed couple. Brandon's tone took a turn back towards the reserved though as he put his hand over his face continuing his laughter. "Yeah...that too..." Brandon stood and offered his hand out to Amy, both in friendship and physical support. The young fWo Superstar looked at the hand, measuring the meanings behind it being offered. Shrugging her shoulders, she laid her petite yet calloused hand in his and he helped her to her feet. Unfortunately, Amy's full range of motion had not fully returned and her head swam with stars and sparklers when she looked up. The ceiling had decided to spin from the top of the building as Aimz looked towards it. As it spun clockwise Brandon circled her counter clockwise, or so she thought. Brandon stood still as Amy's eyes glazed over and she looked frantically around the room in what seemed to be a failed attempt at regaining herself. Instead her knees began to bend and she instinctively shot an arm up and around The Paragon's neck to steady herself. He head bobbed forward and even with the bracing she began to buckle under the massively increased force gravity had applied on her. Brandon acted quickly though and as she lost her footing he scooped his massive right arm under the curvature of her legs and pulled them to him. Placing his left arm around her shoulders, right where their little 'spark' had happened, Brandon picked Aimz up and held her nearly limp body to him. "Amy?!" Brandon nearly yelled. Frantic but trying to stay focused he began to walk to the exit in hopes of finding a trainer, someone still around to help. She'd definitely been bashed in the head a few too many times...though, he knew that even after taking a dizzy spell of this calibur, she probably still wouldn't admit to even being just a little hurt. His thoughts couldn't linger on her long, though... As he looked up from his new friend he met the gaze of his wife Anastasia. She stood in the doorwell, holding it open. As wide as her mouth was with her jaw dropped to the ground. Disbelief. Shock. Resentment. Her husband was standing before her, carrying another woman like a baby doll. To make it worse Brandon, shocked himself, he didn't speak. His steps slowed and he again felt a lump of guilt knot in his stomach. His worry for Amy mixed with his distress over the coming altercation with his beloved. He picked up his pace again,w forcing himself to refocus on the task at hand. As he neared Ana he had finally found the power to speak but she simply turned away quickly, letting the heavy gray door close before her hero of a husband could get any closer.
