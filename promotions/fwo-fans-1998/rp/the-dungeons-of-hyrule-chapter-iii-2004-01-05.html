The Dungeons of Hyrule: Chapter III

5 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
September 21st, 2003; Late Morning "No. Just no." Stone said. It had been nearly three weeks since Allen Brown returned to the Dungeons of Hyrule and reassumed command of the video game establishment. The business had been doing well, and Allen's help had greatly helped out Stick and Stone. However, today was a slow business day, and it needed a certain touch to really touch with the little kiddies here and there. So, that was why Stone was dressed in a giant Donkey Kong suit and holding a golf club. His head was poking out of a hole in Donkey Kong's mouth, a la Crash Bandicoot in those Crash Bandicoot ads. "What? You don't like the color?" Allen asked with a slight laugh. "Man, screw you." Stone shot back. He turned to Stick, who was just laughing at him uncontrollably, "And screw you, too, Stick. You wear this!" Between his laughter, Stick explained to him simply, "I'm too tall to be Donkey Kong!" Stone grumbled, and turned his attention to the young man who had walked into the place. The young man took one look at Stone in his Donkey Kong suit, and simply burst out laughing in his own right. "What? WHAT? Don't make me throw barrels up your ass!" Stone shouted. "Maybe you shouldn't take all of your rage out on the customers, Stone." Allen suggested, "Besides, Donkey Kong doesn't throw barrels any more. He jumps on lizards and plays golf with freakin' Mario." "Don't you diss Donkey Kong, man." Stone shot back, "`Cause I'm supposed to be him." Stick just laughed and returned behind the counter. Allen went up to the guy who had just come in and asked him if he was looking for anything in particular. "Yeah, my friend bought a Super NES recently, and I was wondering where you keep the games." he said. So, Allen casually pointed upstairs to the balcony. Nodding, the man went up there. Allen walked back towards Stick, and simply watched Stone in amusement as he paced around the lobby of the Dungeons of Hyrule and cursed under his breath about the suit. Eventually, Stick turned to Allen and asked him, quite bluntly, "Don't you miss it?" Allen turned to Stick in confusion, an eyebrow raised if only because he could actually do that. Not that he was the Rock or anything, just because he could do that, "Miss what?" "Wrestling. Being the Codemaster." Stick said. Allen pointed a finger accusingly at his employee, "No. In fact, I take that 'no" and raise you a 'HELL no'. Didn't Stone tell you how crazy that was makin' me? When you have your dreams raided by freakin' Heftel and War, that's when you just gotta call it quits, y'know?" "Well, I admit that it's a little freaky. But come on, man, you really think wastin' your time here is gonna help you out?" Stick asked. "You guys need all the help you can get, it's getting close to the holiday season for cryin' out loud." Allen responded. "Yeah, but are you sure it's what you want, as opposed to what we'd like? Stone and I can deal with the Dungeons, Allen. We've done so for years, with you and without you." Stick said. "Man, I ain't hearin' this," Allen replied, with a huff, but fortunately, salvation came in the form of the guy with a copy of Donkey Kong Country for the SNES. How ironic. Allen grinned wildly and looked past the customer and shouted at Stone, who was still pacing and looking as irritated as a man in a Donkey Kong suit could look, "Hey, Stone! Keep it up! Someone's buyin' Donkey Kong merch!" "Screw you!" Stone shouted back. Allen Brown smiled and looked at his customer. "That'll be twelve-fifty."
