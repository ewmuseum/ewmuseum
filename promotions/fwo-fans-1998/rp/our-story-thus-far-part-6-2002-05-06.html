Our Story Thus Far (Part 6)

6 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Guess it's time to live up to my end of the bargain. What I'm about to tell you, I'm not all that fond of, so I'm guessing you're not gonna like it at all. I want you to understand this going in." Jasmin takes a second to think about it, and then nods her acceptance towards Bear. "So long as we're clear. Most every worker I've ever met had some sort of weakness on the road. Booze, coke, juice, whatever. With Frenchie, though, it was always the one thing: women. Yeah, a lot of guys go for some action on the road. No one goes out of their way to hide it. And it's not as if he was always with a rat. Weeks would pass without hearing about a new conquest, swearing off that particular habit. But rest assured, he'd always fall right off the wagon, soon as a nice pair of legs would greet him at a bar. Most times he didn't have to try all that hard. Face it, any famous guy's gonna be able to pick up chicks, and one with his accent? But every once in a while, he'd have to go that extra mile. He'd turn on that charm, he'd make promises he would never live up to, and he'd get what he wanted. But I've got to admit, even I was disgusted to hear the lengths he went to in Vegas, six years ago." "He got married."
