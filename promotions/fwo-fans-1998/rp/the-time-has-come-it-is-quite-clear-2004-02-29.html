so impressed with all you do tried so hard to be like you flew too high and burnt the wing lost my faith in everything Emulating your idols doesn't always mean that you'll follow in their footsteps of greatness. Most times you will fail. The vast majority of failures will continue to try and become what they want to be: their hero. Failures need to realize that once you fail at something, they're not good at it. There's no reason to go on, to press, to try harder. If a person has already given everything they have, how do they find the strength to give more? Even heroes and idols have dark sides to them. Secretly and neatly hung in the closet with their thousand-dollar Armani suits. The true to key to emulating the success of any hero is to find their dark side. That is what makes them tick. The pursuer cannot go by what they see on the outside, what they see on television, hear on the radio, read in the newspaper. Everything they read, see and hear is false. Though nobody could see it, deep inside they knew I wanted to be like him. A person the people could identify with on the outside as their working class hero. Yet on the inside he was just as disturbed as I am. He had a wife, a daughter, the perfect family life. Things I could never hope to have. My only problem? I could never get to the other side of that wall. lick around divine debris taste the wealth of hate in me shedding skin succumb defeat this machine is obsolete No one knew why I disappeared those few months ago. Nobody has even seen me in public. I've been forgotten by everyone. That was exactly what I wanted. Disappear without a fucking trace. Even the most hated villains in the world can be quickly wiped from memory. I was no different. When I came to the realization that I couldn't be what I had strived to be for so long, I gave up. I quit. Why keep going? It was futile to try and be him, no matter how hard I tried. No matter how badly I could taste it. Comparing him to myself, I knew I couldn't even be his shadow. We were exactly the same, yet polar opposites. While he had good in him, I was and still am consumed by hate. I've let it breed for so long, there's no way I could possibly turn back. Hate keeps me going, it's my lifeline, fused into my veins and arteries, pumping through my heart like some crude black substance. I've learned to live with it. I've learned to love it. I revel in hatred. made the choice to go away drink the fountain of decay tear a hole exquisite red fuck the rest and stab it dead True evil and true hatred can never disappear. It stains and taints even the holiest of men and women. While true evil may never rise above to sink true goodness, it will always lurk in the shadow. Though he doesn't know where I am, where I've been or gone, he knows that I am always two steps behind him. He may not worry about me, he may have even pushed me to the back of his mind. But all it takes is one simple word, or one quick image and all of the memories will return. Gushing through his veins just as if caffeine had opened them. Memories of the past, as horrible as they may seem. It's never as bad as it seems, they say. I say it's always worse than it seems. Perspective is everything. One man's pain is another man's sadistic and arousing pleasure. One man's misfortune is another's rung on the ladder to success. The upper-hand is never on top. The apex is never the summit. The best is never good enough. Through every pinnacle you will find your abyss. In every diamond you will find worthless dirt. Every hero has his anti. Every good man has his hidden agenda. Every trustworthy associate is the biggest liar. Every secret has its rumor. Pain is pleasure. Fortune is misfortune. Good is bad. Holy is evil. God is Satan. There is a freight train at the end of every dark tunnel. Nothing can be forgotten forever. Love is never all that it seems. Happiness is never without sadness. From one extreme there is always a link to the next. Read between the lines. You will see more than you ever bargained for. broken bruised forgotten sore too fucked up to care anymore poisoned to my rotten core too fucked up to care anymore I stand here today, nearly six months since anyone in public has seen me. Have I become a better man since I've associated with anyone? No. I've become worse. The hatred has been boiling my blood for far too long. It needs to be taken out on someone, something, anything. It's difficult to channel hatred into something to be used for good. In fact, it is nearly impossible. All I ever needed was for you to be there for me through the thick and the thin. Through all of those hard times when I thought I couldn't push on. Yet as you were never there, I somehow persisted. You lived your perfect life, with your perfect family. Everything for you was perfect while my pathetic existence crumbled to ruins. I was buried beneath time. You were the Vesuvius to my Pompeii. Perhaps someday I'll be found again beneath the layers of ash and dust, asphyxiated by my own beliefs and philosophies. I may have never triumphed over you, but now I know I never needed to. Our past is all I need to drive me forward to the future. You never had to say that you would always be there for me. We both secretly knew it. Now you've forgotten who I am. That will not last. in the back off the side far away is a place where i hide where i stay tried to say tried to ask i needed to all alone by myself where were you?
