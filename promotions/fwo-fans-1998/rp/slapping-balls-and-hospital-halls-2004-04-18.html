Slapping Balls and Hospital Halls

18 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It took David Cross half an hour to get across town by cab. It was going to take much longer to get past the hospital's front doors. "Listen Wang, I'm getting tired of going over this with you." The menacing Asian man stood in front of the hospital doors at the top of the stairs. He stared down at Cross, who remained on the sidewalk. David had once again insulted him by calling him Wang, which was not his name. "You will not get past me. You go back to your hole." "Charming." Cross rubbed his chin. "Now get out of my way, before I beat you like a bitch." Wang didn't show it, but he was a little taken aback by David's behavior. He knew the American was disrespectful, but he had always been soft. This didn't seem like the same man that Wang had fought before. This one was hardened. Dangerous. "Master Hiroshi told me that you no get in." Cross took a step closer. "I don't think I can put it any fucking plainer. Move your ass, or you'll spend your last few waking seconds begging for a bullet to the brain stem." Wang waited until David took a second step before he struck. He hopped forward and drove his knee into Cross's face, landing beside him on the stair. David was still standing and blood was flowing from a deep wound in his lip. Wang brought his leg up in a looping roundhouse kick, but David's head wasn't there to get hit by it. That's because David ducked down, slapped Wang's testicles up into his pelvic cavity with his palm, then yanked them back out. Even though he was unconscious, Wang continued to piss himself as he rolled down the stairs. Cross walked into the hospital without looking back. The receptionist at the front desk gave Cross directions to Michelle's room and he wasted little time in getting there, despite being told that visiting hours were over. They couldn't understand why David was so insistent in seeing her. It wasn't just love that motivated him. It was guilt. She wouldn't have even been in the hospital, or even the United States for that matter, if it hadn't been for him. David stood in the hallway and looked through the tiny window into her room. She was pale and much thinner than she'd been just a few days ago. Tubes and wires hung off her like technological tentacles. From watching her heart monitor, David could see her pulse was shallow but steady. She was alive, but she wasn't out of the woods yet. The handle was in his hand, but David couldn't get himself to turn it. As much as he wanted to be near her, to touch her cheek and tell her everything was going to be okay, he couldn't get himself to go in. "See Alex? We're a failure." Cross turned his head and stared at the thin effeminate man standing beside him. He looked around as people passed by without acknowledging either one of them. "Shouldn't you be cowering in fear somewhere? I thought you were dead Dave." Alex stepped away from the door as Dave moved closer to him. He couldn't stand his weaker and cowardly other half. Alex was in control now. He'd hoped that Dave would have done the humane thing and destroyed himself, giving Alex full control. Apparently there was still some fight in the little bitch. Dave looked through the glass. "We should go in and see her." Alex shook his head. "No. We look at her through the glass. We hope she gets better, and we hope that she wants to be with us when this is all over. Oh, and we tear out Crusader's heart. Also, his balls." A nurse happened to pass by as David Cross held his narrative. She felt bad for the poor man as he stood in the hallway talking to himself. He was obviously distraught. She stopped to try and console him. "Is that your girlfriend in there?" "Yes," David said softly as he looked through the window again. He reached into his pocket and pulled out a folded piece of paper. "Could you put this beside her bed so she can read it when she wakes up?" The nurse took the note. "There's no telling when she'll regain consciousness." She could see the hurt in his eyes, even in the red one, and gave him a reassuring smile. "I'll be sure she gets it." "Thanks." David nodded to the nurse as she walked away. Alex was dumbfounded. "When did you write that note? I didn't even notice it in there?" Dave watched Michelle's chest rise and fall. "You're not in control as much as you think," he said almost wistfully. "I just want to be with her. Forget wrestling. Forget everything else." "Crusader got a taste of what's waiting for him," said Alex with a chuckle. "What did you do?" Dave hated not being in control, but Alex could handle things much better than he could. "Not as much as I wanted to. Oh, and you're going to court for assaulting a police officer." "I did WHAT?!" Dave looked around and saw a number of eyes on him. He lowered his voice. "What did you do to a cop?" "I stole his gun and knocked him out. I almost shot Crusader in the face with it." Alex closed his eyes and relived the moment. The fear in Cody's eyes, the scent of his agony, almost got him hard. "The lawyer said you have to go see a shrink tomorrow." "A shrink? I don't like the way things are going." Alex opened his eyes. "Well that's just too fucking bad." The nurse walked over from her station. "Are you okay sir?" "Sorry about that. Just lost control there for a second." David took a final look into the room and caressed the glass with the tips of his fingers. "Tell her I was here when she wakes up, yeah?" The nurse nodded and gave him a warm smile. "Of course." Cross walked down the hallway and climbed into the elevator. As soon as the doors closed, the nurse took the note out of her pocket and walked into Michelle's room. "That boy is out of his mind," she said under her breath. She had no idea how right she was.
