Hidden Message

21 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The wrestling business plays its fans for fools. They pull the wool over their eyes. Wrestlers and promoters lie to them. They stab them in the back. Some selfish wrestlers even have the audacity to turn down an autograph. What kinda crap is that? This kid, or that kid and his or her parents buy the tickets, that fill the arena, that leads to the t- shirt sells, that ultimately put money in your pocket... and, all they ask in return is a decent show, and your signature on a napkin. Or t- shirt. What could they POSSIBLY do with that? Nothing. But, it's the memories. The memory that that kid will have on that particular day, when the big famous wrestler signed his autograph. Some guys become there gimmick. But, this business is a well oiled machine, and as long as you can work a decent match, and draw a decent reaction... they'll protect you. Eli Flair has been protected for years. Eli Flair's favorite topic is... himself. And, contrary to what he'll tell you... his favorite thing to do... is win and then tell you about it. I've been his friend for three of the four years I've known him, and while he has taught me alot, I've seen alot of things in his actions alone that let me know what and what I don't want to be. When Eli Flair was better than me... he cut promo after promo telling me as much. And, sure... the truth hurts. And, I'll always call a spade a spade... at that time... he was. But, see... not only is this sport about winning... but, it also has a business end. And, Sean Stevens the business man didn't run away. I didn't keep quiet for an entire week or two, because I knew that if I pretended to care, and lost, I'd be embarrassed. I can lose ten times straight to a guy... and, still think I'll win the eleventh. I'm just confident like that. But, not Flair. He knows what I'm capable of. While he was on top three years ago, I was soaking it all in. Getting better and better and better until I am what I am. The best. And, he is what he was... exactly. Nothing's changed. Eli Flair is still stuck in the CSWA. Stuck on him and Troy Windham. Stuck on Deacon... how he and Deacon had the war of the fan favorites, Eli the workhorse, blue collar, anti-hero, who broke a barrier he wasn't supposed to break. And, Deacon the religuos crowd pleaser, who had a following like no one else in the CSWA at that time. Stuck on Hornet and how it was supposedly his time, and nobody could stop him from winning that CSWA World Title. Wanna know how I know all of that? He told me. And, Eli will tell you, too... if you ask, and he'll tell you even if you don't. But, guess what, Flair? While all of that was impressive... ...that was 1999. THIS is 2002. You've done little since. So let me tell YOU something... this is the fWo. You are in the Internet Title tournament. Against one of the best this sport has seen. A former World Champion... wait... this should impress you... a seven month CSWA Presidential Champion... and, a guy who has taken you to your limits on countless occasions whether you want to admit it or not. You can sit at home like nothing is going on but the fact of the matter is on Monday you're in for a war. And, if your preparation is as careless as your promotion... ...it may be your last... Old man.
