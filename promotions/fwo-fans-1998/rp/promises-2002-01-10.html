Promises

10 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tempest rolled over and glared at her pink kitty alarm clock as it sang it's annoying little electric song and said, "Sometimes, even I wonder how I got to be this weird." She groaned and sat up, stretching her arms as the strap of her PJs slipped to one side revealing a dark patch of bruised flesh. Trouble trotted in and hopped on the bed, snuffling at her face which was still puffy from the last fight on Slaughter. She carefully maneuvered herself onto the floor and padded out to the kitchen in search of the sort of coffee that embarrassed even latte drinkers. As she sipped, she stared out a nearby window and wondered aloud, "How can me and Eddy work together when we can barely speak to each other. I don't know whether to treat him as the enemy or just another victim." She shook her head and watched the traffic outside move at a crawl. "So much time spent hating each other. It's a habit that's hard to break and the revelation that we're family is almost a smack in the face. I wanted to be happy if I ever found I still had family... not like this." She sat still for a few moments, her face forlorn and weary. Suddenly she slammed her palm into the table and growled, "If Ric.... Chronos knew this, then it couldn't have possibly been that deeply buried a secret. Why didn't I know? Why didn't anyone tell me? Why..." She turned and stared at the phone. "Why am I not asking the one person who can really answer that?" ~~~ Thirty minutes of circuitous connections and two cups of coffee later, Tempest was speaking to the man she thought might have the answers. "Papa... why didn't you tell me?" A long silence was followed by the careful, measured English of one of Hong Kong's most feared underworld leaders. Papa Liu said, "The reasons are complicated my little windstorm. They wind through dark corners you may not want to shed light into. Is it not enough to have your brother now?" Tempest huffed and said, "After he tried to annihilate me? After he almost broke my shoulder and left me with a concussion. Papa I can hardly eat that I don't get sick or sleep without waking up with a terrible headache anymore and now I'm just supposed to accept him?" "Yes," said the calm voice of Papa Liu. "Regardless of the circumstances you have been given a second chance at a life that need not be lived alone." "Papa, his existence was used against me. How can I accept him now. And you're evading my question. You had to know this. Why didn't you tell me?" Another long pause ensued and then Papa said, "Do you remember little one, how once I promised to take care of you if you ever got hurt in my ring?" Tempest seemed cowed and said, "Yes Papa." "And I did so," he said with authority. "I honor my promises, no matter how hard it is to do so." Tempest's voice was touched with anger as she said, "So someone made you promise not to tell me I had a brother? Even after daddy got killed? Even when I never knew my mother? How could you let me be alone like that? That would have changed everything and..." "And made you someone other than the woman you are now," he said. "Someone other than a star in the fWo. But before you tell me this is not important next to your family, I will agree. It is not. And that is why you should come and see me. Some things are best explained in person." Tempest nodded. "Ok... It's been a long time, but I know where to go. I'll meet you there." "Of course. I will meet you there and I will show you why the secrecy was needed. I promise."
