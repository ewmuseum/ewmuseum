Lost Time - Cycle One

26 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Anyone coming?" Tempest looked both ways down the hall, tipping her glitter- covered sunglasses down at the empty spaces before tiptoeing out. "Nope, we're clear." Poison Ivy quickly followed. The two had stayed behind on the day off, citing a desire to go to the art museum... and that was that. With the front desk personnel taking a break and nobody around to stop them, the two women skulked up to the computer and asserted themselves. "Okay," said Ivy, "Superior Championship Wrestling... where would they keep their--" Ivy cuts herself off when she looks at Tempest, sitting with her body scrunched down, glitter- rimmed sunglasses still perched on the tip of her nose. "What?" asked Tempest, innocently. "Please take those off," said Ivy, beginning to laugh, "We need to do this quickly." Tempest responded by leaning in very close to Ivy's ear. "The fat kitty growls in the dry summer heat." Neither one of them can contain their laughter at that one, but Tempest does remove her sunglasses and turn her attention toward the computer screen in front of them. "Look up SCWWrestling - try to get an IP number for their domain," suggested Tempest, pleased to see Ivy already giving it a shot. ENTER USERNAME: "OwnerEddie," said Ivy, "S'gotta be." ENTER PASSWORD: "Shit," said Ivy. She typed a few keystrokes, seemingly at random. INCORRECT PASSWORD. PLEASE TRY AGAIN. "Maybe you should pull up the main directory," said Tempest, "and try to work your way in from the backdoor." INCORRECT PASSWORD. PLEASE TRY AGAIN. Tempest, as if suddenly remembering something, reaches into her bag and rummages around the clothes and personal effects contained therein. "One more chance at this," said Ivy, "I'm a bit rusty but the system hasn't been built that I can't crack." She begins to type at a breakneck pace - as Tempest continues to rummage through her bag. A door slams somewhere behind them, and both women spin around. Ivy's elbow nonchalantly bangs against the keyboard, and Tempest crosses her legs and folds her arms across her chest, trying to look as nonchalant as possible. They needn't have worried - it was simply one hotel lackey going from the office on the left to the office on the right. Never even looking at the computer station. "Oh, fuck!" said Ivy, spinning back around, "My arm must've hit the keyboard. This crack is useless and if we can't get in on this try we're locked out." Ivy buries her forehead in her hands as she tries to think of what to do, when suddenly there's a floppydisk being waved in front of her eyes. "Try this," said Tempest, "It's a little toy I made up a while back. Couldn't hurt, no?" A smile crossed Ivy's face as she popped the disk in. "Now hit 'Run'," advised Tempest, "Type in Tempest- Sparkle, backslash, ShinyLoudTwo, all one word, backslash, SCWWrestling, backslash, their IP number, and the username you were working on." Ivy types, takes a deep breath, and hits enter. She exhales, relieved, when the SCW's network comes up. "Thanks, girl." "It's what I do." "Okay," continued Ivy, "they've got TV tonight in Orlando, and they're running a house show tomorrow in Tampa, and then they're taking a few days off." Voices behind them. "Got it?" asked Tempest. "Got it," replied Ivy. They backtrack quickly, covering up all traces that they had just hacked SCW's network, and quickly scurry to the other side of the counter. "May I help you ladies?" asked the woman who emerged from the back. "We're checking out," replied Ivy, handing her their room key. The past few days have been rough on Ivy and Tempest, as is her way, latched on to her to make sure she'd be okay. The hotel employee typed a few keystrokes, looked at the number on the key, and typed a few more in. "Now, according to my records, you ladies have an account here with us. Miss... McGinnis?" "That's me," replied Ivy, "What's the problem?" "No problem," replied the employee, "you simply have credit on your account for a night's stay. Your bill is already settled. Enjoy the rest of your weekend." "Why thank you, milady," replied Tempest, tapping Ivy's elbow, "Let's get a move- on, Ivy." Outside, Ivy notices the smirk on Tempest's face. "C'mon, out with it." "What?" replied Tempest, feigning a look of innocence, "You went to sleep pretty early and I didn't want to keep you up with anything loud so I put my headphones on and played a bit on your laptop." "Played a bit," replied Ivy, "Why do I get the feeling that's not all you did?" Tempest put her arms up to try and hail a taxi, "I plugged your modem in so I could download Pong, but it took awhile so I just messed around a bit, talked to a few fWo fans in the chatroom, settled our hotel bill... Messed around." Ivy's expression ran from disbelief to speechless to upset to amused... all in the span of one and a half seconds. "You're one of a kind, Tempest," was all she could say. "THANK you," replied Tempest, as the two women entered the taxi.
