Yet ANOTHER Unexpected Twist!

21 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The red LCD of the alarm clock read 2:25AM, and Alex Creed should have been asleep. But there was something...not right. Lindsay and Alaina Troy had asked, nay...INSISTED, that Alex and David Cross stay with them while the fWo was in Tampa for Ruahh~! The invitation of a free place to stay with home cooked food in place of a paid hotel room and "whatever they could find" was too good to pass up. Saturday and Sunday went by in a blur. The beaches had been conquered, Tropicana Field had been visited to take in a Tampa Bay Devil Ray's spring training game (because...well, the Red Sox play in Ft. Meyers and Lindsay would never hear the end of it from Ivy if she willingly attended a New York Yankees game when they weren't playing the Sox), Ybor City's nightclubs had been visited--with Alex getting more than his fair share of telephone numbers and David getting more than his fair share of willing dance partners, including the two Troy sisters. After two straight nights of dancing, Alex was content to just go straight to bed late Sunday night/early Monday morning instead of staying up to chat with the girls. He thought he had heard noises...the front door opening and closing, muffled voices filtering up the stairs and under the doorjamb. It was when he heard something fall in the dining room that he bolted out of bed, threw on a pair of sweats, and ran down the stairs. The spiral staircase made him dizzy, but he attributed that to the illegally consumed alcoholic beverages that had somehow found their way into his hands. He padded across the tiled floor, grabbed a vase that was on a hallway table--and prayed he wouldn't have to break it over someone's head--, and crept into the dining room. There, sitting at the long oak table with papers and a blueprint in front of them, was Lindsay Troy, Alaina Troy, and Sonny Silver. Alex lowered the vase and said, "Is everything okay in here LT? I thought I heard something and it looks to me like the sewer backed up." The last comment was clearly directed at Silver as Alex tightened his grip on the vase. Lindsay and Alaina looked at each other with an "Oh Shit" look. Lindsay was about to tell Alex that everything was fine, until Sonny decided to retort. "Everything's fine, Roidmonkey Jr. Go back to sleep with your life partner in Zen and maybe the two of you can trim your Bansai shrubs and stroke your tree trunks." The girls put their heads in their hands, and Lindsay probably would have smacked Sonny right upside his fat stupid head had Alex not been so quick on the comeback. "Wow, that's a whole lot of anger there old fellah. Are you always this cranky when you're up past your bedtime?" "No, I'm always this cranky when products of their father's shrunken roid testicles interrupt me when I'm in the middle of waging WAR," Sonny snorted. That garnered a smack from Lindsay and a, "Keep your manhands off me!" from Sonny. "Your fascination with my Dad's nuts can be something between you and your therapist." Alex looked over at Troy. "Now like I asked before... is everything okay?" If there was ever an inopportune time for someone to walk in on this particular discussion, it was right then. To make matters worse, it had been Alex that walked in on the War Counsel, and Alex was one of the last people that Lindsay wanted to know about what was going on. "Everything's fine, Alex," she said as she stood up. "Let's go in the other room." Lindsay walked over to Alex and took him by the arm, making sure to flip Sonny the finger as she escorted Alex out of the room. Once in the hallway, Lindsay pinched the bridge of her nose and sighed. "I'm sorry, kiddo. You weren't supposed to see what was going on in there." Alex rubbed the back of his neck. "In all fairness, I'm not quite sure what was going on in there. I mean, it looked like the three of you were looking at blueprints or something. Unless you're hiring Sonny to build an addition to your house, putting two and two together here doesn't add up to anything very good." Lindsay looked back into the dining room, and proceeded down the hall to the kitchen, motioning Alex to follow her. He did, still completely puzzled about what exactly was going on. Once they were alone and out of earshot, she spoke with complete frankness. "Alex, I'm not going to lie to you. What's going on isn't very good. Something's happened to Alaina and I, and we need to take care of it." The young Creed nodded. "Done. So what are we doing?" "YOU'RE not doing anything." Lindsay shook her head. "No way am I going to allow you to get mixed up in this." "Correct me if I'm wrong, but you said you're in trouble, right? By default I'm involved then." Alex gave Lindsay a mischievous grin that, for one disturbing moment, made him look a lot like his father. "After all, I'm young and prone to all sorts of mischief. You know us younger generation folk don't take the whole "no" for an answer thing very well." "Alex, you don't want to get involved in this. Not when careers are on the li...shit." Troy cupped her hand over her mouth. She had said too much. "Oh now you've got to let me help." Alex raised his hand and realized he still had the vase. He turned and set it down on the floor. "After everything you've done to help me get my career off the ground in the fWo, there's no way I'm not helping you. Especially if it involves careers." He took a determined stance. "Especially if that career is yours." Lindsay sighed, exasperated and angry at herself for letting something like that slip. She pointed to a stool that was pushed underneath the kitchen island's countertop. "Sit down for a minute, kiddo." After Alex reluctantly did as he was requested, Lindsay leaned on top of the counter and looked him right in the eyes. "Alex, you're young. You have a good thing going with Disney...both you and your mom do. You've got the opportunity to do a lot of great things with your time here, because you're getting a chance that a lot of people aren't getting. Disney hasn't done anything wrong by you. They've made you a star and you deserve all the accolades that you're getting." "Unfortunately, the same can't be said for everyone. Alaina and I found out some things that have been going on, and it's gotten very personal. A lot more personal than she and I wanted them to get. As much as I appreciate your wanting to help, it's just not your place. I refuse to let you put your own career and your mom's livelihood in jeopardy just to help me out." "You have to understand that I can't tell you any more than this. You're like a kid brother to me, Alex, and I love your mom dearly. But this is just something that you need to let me handle on my own." Lindsay could see the turmoil etched on Alex's face. "I can't... I mean it's just not... this really sucks," he mumbled with a frown. "Look, if you have to do this on your own, fine. But if you need help, I'm there before you plug the digits in the phone." She smiled, ruffled his hair, and kissed his forehead. "You're a good kid, Alex. Don't let anyone tell you differently." "Everything alright in here?" Alaina poked her head around the corner. "Sonny just left after muttering something about 'teaching the shitstain a lesson when it's not so damn late.' Remind me again why you want to work with him?" "Because he hates Jim as much as I do," Lindsay said. "And he's got nothing left to lose." Alex was about to made another comment about the now absent Sonny Silver, but Lindz's smirk silenced him. "I think it's about time we all got to bed." "You might not want to turn the alarm on just yet. I passed by Dave's room on the way down here and he's not there." Alex shrugged with a smile. "I can't say I know where he is, but I'm sure he'll turn up eventually." Alaina looked worried. "I hope the dogs are asleep. I know he's a skilled martial artist and all, but he might meet his match with three trained Dobermans." "I'll tell Will to keep an eye out for him," Lindsay said as she picked up the house phone and made a call to the front gate.
