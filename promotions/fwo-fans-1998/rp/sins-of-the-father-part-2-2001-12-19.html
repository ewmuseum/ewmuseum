Sins Of The Father - Part 2

19 December 2001

Click HERE for the Prologue Click HERE for Part 1, May 31, 1974 . "And that's final! We're through! I will NOT be married to a man who works in organized crime!" Mike had been holding the receiver away from his ear, to prevent permanent hearing loss. But this was too much. "Fine then! We'll deal with this when I get home!" he shouted back into the phone. "Didn't you hear me!" Eleni's voice screamed from the telephone. "Actually, I think people in Japan heard you!" was Mike's angry retort. "You're not coming back to my home. Over. Done. Through. Good BYE!" Mike intended to reply, but the buzz of a disconnected line was all that greeted him. Frustrated, he hung up the phone, then redialed his home number. Although the phone rang continuously, no answer came. After a few more attempts yielded similar results, Mike slammed the receiver back onto the handset, frustration taking over. He was tired, and the proposal from Liu wasn't one he had seriously considered taking, at least, not until now. It would take Eleni several days to calm down enough to even be approachable, and even then, there was no guarantee he'd be back in her good graces anytime soon, if ever. He could possibly finish the tour before she would even talk to him again. This was quite possibly the angriest he had ever seen his wife, and he knew that this was serious. She had an uncle who had been involved with the Mafia - and had mysteriously disappeared when he decided to "go straight". Doubtless, she feared the same might happen to Mike. He laid back on the bed, which was actually somewhat comfortable, and closed his eyes. He needed time to think, but more importantly, he needed time to sleep. He had been dozing for little more than a few hours, when he suddenly jerked upright. Mike had lived a long while by being able to realize when something was wrong - when something wasn't quite right - and that feeling was what had woken him. There was someone else in his room. His eyes darting about, he quickly took in the situation. Whomever was in the room with him was standing in the shadows, and had to be somewhat small. Preparing to pounce on this would be assassin, Mike pulled his legs under him... ...and Mike stopped cold, as the nude form of a slender Chinese woman stepped into the light. "What?" Mike's sleep addled brain could barely comprehend what was going on in his room - he had awoken and prepared to fight more on instinct than any form of cognitive thought, and this certainly through a wrench into the works. "So sorry, Mr. Chronos. I am Sun Chen. Papa send me to check on you - heard much shouting before. Thought you might need massage, take stress away, neh?" Mike looked closely at the face of the young woman, and noticed clearly that it was the same woman that he had seen fighting in the pit, earlier in the evening. He paused - what was the game here? That's when it occurred to him - Papa had probably bugged the phone. Mike inwardly cursed himself for a fool, and looked again at the young woman. He didn't want to offend her - after all, he'd seen that she could do some serious damage - but he needed to defuse this situation quickly. Mike: "Um... Sun..." Chen: "Sun family name. My name Chen. You need massage - no argue." With that, she stepped forward once more, and expertly flipped Mike onto his stomach before he could even mount a protest. Before he knew it, she had begun grabbing the hard, knotted muscles on his back, and adroitly began relieving the tension. Regardless of what other factors might be at play here, Papa knew how to pick women - and this one was VERY skilled with the use of her hands. He found himself fighting her touch less and less, giving in to soothing graces that Chen provided. Chen: "There much damage to back. This take some time to fix." Chen's broken English was only vaguely clear to Mike, but her intent was now crystal. Slowly, she rolled him over on the bed, and he felt himself resisting for a far shorter time than he had imagined. Soon, they were locked in an embrace that transcended any spoken language. After all, Eleni HAD broken up with him... In the morning, Mike was in for a rude awakening. Chen was gone from his bed, and Liu, the omnipresent Wu by his side, had shaken Mike awake from the soundest sleep Mike had ever experienced. A few moments later, it was clear to Mike that he and Chen were not the only ones who knew of the events that had transpired earlier the previous evening. With this knowledge as a bargaining chip, it was not long before the two reached an agreement wherein Mike would work for Liu throughout the week, with week-long return tours to happen twice a year for the next 10 years.
