Home - Ice Cream

17 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Level with me, kid," said Ivy as she dipped her spoon into her Raspberry Swirl Frozen Yogurt, "You're worried about her." "Me? Naw," replied TJ as he spoon fed MJ some vanilla with strawberry topping, "Why should I worry about Angel?" Ivy laughed. "I can read both'a'ya like a comic book. I don't know why you're worried, though... that's the thing." More yogurt. "So why don't you just tell me?" TJ exhaled sharply. "Worrying about Angel is futile - she doesn't let anyone worry about her and doesn't let anyone know when something worth worrying about goes 'n happens. You know that, too." Ivy's gaze remained on the glammed up bass player. "Stop that." She leaned back, her eyes still locked. "Seriously, Ivy... don't stare at me." Crossed her arms. "Eff you, and eff your penetrating gaze." Good for him, he edited his speech in front of the baby. "I'm waiting." MJ scooted from TJ's lap and walked under the table to Ivy. She picked the little girl up and hugged her tightly. "Angel's ambition is getting dangerous," said TJ, finally. "How so?" "Considering we know what Eli and Angel are currently doin', do yourself a favor and ask him about the scar on her side." "Scar?" "Right there," said TJ as he gestured with his hands to a point just above his right hip, "she has a small circular scar about the size of a wine bottle--" "Oh sweet mother," interrupted Ivy, as she held MJ tighter. "--and a small, teeny, tiny, cut of a knife along one of the edges." "What happened?" "Someone stabbed her in Germany," said TJ, "with a penknife, and she responded by impaling herself on a broken bottle." "Dare I ask?" "Don't bother," assured TJ, "since none of us know, either. She said something crazy about 'taking her bleeding in her own hands' and refused to let us see any of the lyrics she wrote that night, but I can't imagine she was in a good headspace." Ivy remained stoic. "Let me see if I can understand this... You're telling me that Angel... stabbed herself... to prove a goddamn point?" TJ thought about it for a moment, before he nodded noncommittally. "Yeah." "Damn." They both ate their fill for a few moments of silence, as Ivy considered what TJ had told her. “What else did I miss?” “Not much,” replied TJ, “We never got stiffed by a promoter, we never had trouble with the van that we couldn’t work around. But some of the crowds were a skosh hostile. Angel has more scars than just the bottle and knife that I don’t think she’ll willingly share. Most of the rest of us got away with just being cold and sleep deprived all the time. Angel put up with the stress of being the band’s frontperson, as well as being the only chick on the tour.” “MJ notwithstanding,” qualified TJ as Ivy laughed at him. “So you think there’s some mental scars that might need a bit’a time to heal?” asked Ivy, “And d’you think we can let’em heal just with Eli and Angel being t’gether?” “I think some time with Eli – specifically tonight – is exactly what Angel needs,” replied TJ, “I really felt for her this tour. The rest’a the guys were chilling with the fans before and after the gigs – hell, even I hooked up a few times.” Considering how shy TJ was when he wasn’t around people he knew, Ivy was impressed by this fact. “Hell, even Mickey loosened up a bit,” continued TJ, “but Angel? Never. Not only did she make MJ her first priority – no complaints with that, by the way – but she worked her ass off amidst some hostile crowds, but all the news this year about the faceless, interchangeable celebrities that had the country eatin’ outta their hands… really rocked her.” Ivy nodded. The North American Celebrity Machine was something she never understood either, and the fact that Angel was involved in a much more mainstream facet of entertainment than professional wrestling practically guaranteed that she would get affected. “You didn’t let her watch any kind of awards show, did you?” TJ looked down. MJ ate more of his ice cream. “Damnit, TJ,” continued Ivy, “That was really stupid. Our girl’s got more talent, presence, and charisma than ninety percent of the music industry, but she’s slaving away with her boys in a busted – ass van instead. I think we all know it’s just a matter’a time and hard work for you guys, but gettin’ there is gonna be a challenge.” A few people entered and exited the parlor, nodding recognition toward Ivy. Whether it was as a hometown girl or as a professional wrestling personality, she didn’t know. “Dude, despite all that, you shoulda seen her,” continued TJ, “She had the audience in the palm of her hand, every night. This was her real test’a fire, I think. No safety net of Eli being a plane ride away. No safety net of familiar territory. Only thing Angel had was the rest’a the band to fall back on, and she did great.” Ivy nodded. “I knew she could do it,” replied the Psycho Bitch, “but I am worried about her. She was a bit too quiet in the car and all… but whatever. Give ‘er a day or two to decompress. You’ll see, I’ll see, everyone will see that she’s fine.” “Agreed.” He looked at the clock. “So what now?” “Now?” asked Ivy, “Now we finish our ice cream, we take Miss Mariella for a walk, and give them two plenty’a warning before we go back to the place. How long’s it been?” “Mmmmm, ‘bout an hour.” Ivy thought about it. “How long did they go without seeing each other?” “Ten months.” “I think… maybe… we’d better give’em some more time.”
