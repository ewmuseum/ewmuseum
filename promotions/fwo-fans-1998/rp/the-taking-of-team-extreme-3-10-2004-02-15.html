The Taking of Team Extreme - 3/10

15 February 2004

The Taking of Team Extreme Chapter Three November 26th, 2003 Nashville, TN 11:22 PM, local time The Garden played with gusto tonight - they were on their third encore. Angel was in no danger of running out of songs, as for whatever reason, they decided to play a collection of covers tonight. Eli sat on the bus, his feet up, his daughter asleep on his chest. He could hear Angel singing the song she'd written to be the 'ultimate goth-pop song', whose current title was 'Whirl.' Shallow lyrics, but the people loved it. "Stitches" was selling like hotcakes, considering it was only being sold at the shows and on the website. "Listen to her," said Eli, "That's your mom in there. Someday she's gonna be the singer in the biggest band in the world, along with Uncle Mickey, Uncle Nine, Uncle Sex... can't wait to explain that one, and Aunt TJ." He laughed. "TJ'd kill me for that." MJ slept silently. "You've got a special little life ahead of you," he continued, "between the people you're gonna meet through your mom, between the people you've already met - but will hopefully remember - through your pop. But I don't want you to get a swelled head about any of it, y'hear me? You don't ever think you're better than anyone else just because you've got famous parents." Kissed the top of her head. "That's the most important thing you can learn from either of us... the fact that your actions are more important than your name or your money. And you don't ever try to tell someone else how to live." "Look at Uncle Brandon... he's about to become the biggest hero in the fWo, because he's about to beat the man that caused him trouble. He'll do a great job, I think. And look at the way your mom has handled the pressures of touring and of slowly gaining recognition. She's the real hero in this family." "I just hope we do a good job with you... there's nothing more important to either of us than you. There's nothing more important to either of us than you growing up the way you're meant to. And I want you to know right away, why you'll never meet your grandparents." Deep breath. "Daddy's parents are gone. They died when I was real real young, but they were good people. They'd have loved you... I know it. And Mommy's parents were mean to her growing up, and mean to her when she tried to introduce you to them... you never have to see them unless you want to." Eli's voice broke for a moment. "Aunt Ivy's parents were the absolute best people the world had ever seen... Gramma Lily died about a year before you were born, and Grampa Joe, a year and some change before that." MJ stirred in his arms ever slightly, and he picked the baby up to see that she was awake. Her black hair was beginning to thicken - she was going to have hair like her mother. And her steel gray eyes were as piercing as her dad's, but innocence still held them gripped. "But no matter what the future holds... I will promise you beyond the shadow of a doubt, one undeniable fact. You will never, ever suffer from a lack of people who love you, unconditionally." MJ laughed. "You've been born into a very special family," said Eli, as he heard Mickey's guitar spell out the famous opening of 'Stairway to Heaven,' "called Team Extreme and Valerian's Garden. And I can't think of anyone more proud to have you with us than me." "There's a lady who's sure, all that glitters is gold... and she's buying a stairway to heaven..." Angel's voice wafted into the bus. "Well," continued Eli, "maybe there's one person just as proud. But nobody is more proud." The baby was watching her daddy with a smile... As far as I can tell, it doesn't matter who you are... if you can believe there's something worth fighting for... the color of an eye, the glory of a sudden view.... the baby in your arms, the smile she always shoots at you..." ...and Eli returned it.
