Burn baby, burn.

13 January 2000

fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Burn baby, burn. January 13, 2000 The lightbulb hung from the ceiling over the chaotic yet peaceful atmosphere. It flickered several times, as the camera panned from the floor up to the legs of Him sitting in a chair, or as he calls it, His throne. The camera's focus continued to move upward, before His face was in full view. Standing behind him to the left was Heaven, in the middle Living, and to the right... Hell. Snakes moved about on the floor, and produced several hissing sounds. Dead motioned for the camera to capture the imagery on the floor, and the camera focused back on the floor. As the snakes moved to and fro throughout their own urine and feces, Dead began to speak in a stern tone. "Little man?" said the commissioner of the fWo, sarcastically. "My, my... how times have changed. You are becoming weaker by the second, Spencer. You are crumbling much sooner than I anticipated. You are power?" he said, the last question in a sarcastic tone. "You are nothing. You are very much like the fWo and it's wrestlers. Weak. Powerless. And ours... all ours. You see, it's very simple, Spencer. You centered your goal around a title, a title Scott *made*. A title Scott brought credibility to. Oh sure... it had it's share of great champions... like Scott & myself, and... well, that's about it. But over time... it was passed around to such weak pathetic nobodies as Collins, Ultra-Violet, the Flying Frenchie... Rain... yourself... the list could go on and on. But what you fail to understand is that it's not about one title, it's the whole promotion. You can't make an entire promotion come to an end by attempting to burn a World Title. They could always make a new one, couldn't they?" he said with a thin smile, as he continued. "Of course, their new title wouldn't have the rich, illustrious history that they like to claim it does. Pity, isn't it? No, it's much more than a title... any title. As you have learned... it's about having the power. It's about using the power to bring the entire promotion to it's knees... before holding the gun up to the head and pulling. Who has the power, Spencer?" Dead stopped as Living yelled out a mind-jarring "Bang, bang!" in the background. Dead looked at him partly amused, partly simpathetic. "We do. We can make this promotion fail at any time we want it to now that the power is in our hands because of that simpleton named Finn. But like we have clearly stated... we want to have our fun while doing so. You? You just wanted it to satisfy your ego. But you never had the power... ... and you *never* will." An evil laugh ensued from the four of them, before Dead held up a single hand, instructing them to stop. "Yes, it was a masturbatory thing for you, wasn't it? Unfortunately for you, you shot your load at the first Countdown show... and neither your bitches Demon and Skyler or your stooge Beaven are here to clean it up. Sure... you have Poison... wait, didn't you say something about not associating with slutty females? What do you call her? She's no untouched piece of meat herself." Dead laughed again, in a serious manner. "...something about wrestling and long memories... I don't know. Back to the subject at hand... your rule is just beginning? You never had any rule. You never had any 'stroke' around here... look at the two people who have all the power now. Us. Not you. Just to give you an example, we've compromised with Finn and allowed him to "book" the this coming Slaughter. Why? Because we're having fun... after the record setting numbers Warfare undoubtedly received tonight, we figured we'd let Finn have a chance to book his own show. We want to see just how pathetic of a show he can come up with. Just like this organization, we want to watch him crash... ... and burn. Burn... baby, ...burn. It's not about a title, Spencer. It's about power... and using it to your own advantage. We've got the power... ... you've got nothing. You are nothing. You always have been nothing. At Invasion, Scott will prove this to you. Trust me." Dead stood up from his chair and held his arms out in a crucifixion pose. He then shot a hateful glare at the camera, before we faded to a 15-second promo of "FTfWo Wednesday Warfare", coming in six days.
