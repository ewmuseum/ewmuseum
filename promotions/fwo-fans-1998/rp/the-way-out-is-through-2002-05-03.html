The Way Out is Through

3 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The hospital was busy in the middle of the day. But it was nearly midnight, pacific time. Eli Flair and ‘Poison' Ivy McGinnis walked into the emergency room; over Eli's shoulder was Donaven Winters, bloodied and nearly unconscious. The receptionist's mouth was gaping wide open as she stood up from behind the counter. She looked frightened as Eli dropped Donaven down into a chair. "What's wrong with him?" she indirectly asked both Eli and Ivy. "We're wrestlers... from the fans Wrestling organization," Eli started. "We went through a rough match, I found him in his locker room cutting himself up. Well... when I found him... he had a gun to his head. He just sort of passed out." The receptionist quickly picked up a phone and dialed a number. Almost immediately two doors flung open and two doctors came through with a stretcher. Eli helped them lift Donaven up onto the stretcher and they made their way to an empty room. As they rushed through the corridor Eli kept looking down at Donaven, hoping for some small sign of life. Then he saw it, his eyes opened slowly and locked with Eli's. His mouth slowly opened and Eli bent his head down so he could hear Donaven's words. "Thank you Eli... and goodbye..." Flair could barely hear the words, but he acknowledged them. "I'm not gonna let you die Winters..." Donaven's eyes closed once more as he slipped into unconsciousness. * * * The Subconscious: He was tumbling down the dark, black rabbit hole towards oblivion. There didn't seem to be any end in sight. When he seemed to slow down, something happened and he would speed up. He was falling end over end, trying to reach his arms out, hoping to grab on something. But his hands continued to grab at nothing. Faster and faster, moving deeper and deeper within the rabbit hole. This can't happen to me! We're going to die together! you're going to die... i want to live my life You're life is nothing without me in it! don't lie to me anymore... i don't want to be confused anymore I didn't confuse you. I was the voice of reason in your pathetic life. no... you were using me like i was a machine... i want my mind back You're coming with me. This darkness will end soon, we'll be together forever. i want to live my own life... i don't need you.... i never did... you made everything in my life was... you tried to kill me Because I wanted to be with you forever Donaven. guess what... you don't control me anymore... Don't be foolish! eli... i saw the light because of him... now i know i don't need you... time to say goodbye to each other... forever You can't do this to me! goodbye... * * * Ivy reached down into her purse and pulled out a cellular phone. She turned it on and dialed a number, speaking when the receiver answered. "Monica? It's Ivy, I've got some bad news," she said gravely. "Donaven's hurt, he tried to kill himself, Eli found him as he had a gun to his head. We don't know if he's going to make it. If he does, he's not going to be in a wrestling ring for a long time. He's probably gonna go back to Dearborn for some psychiatric help. Apparently he's a schizophrenic or something... at least that's what the doctor said. I'll call you in a few hours, Monica. I'm sorry you had to hear it like this. Bye..." Ivy turned the phone off and turned to face Eli; both had grave looks on their faces. "Do you think he'll make it Eli?" "He has to." He was almost through. Almost.
