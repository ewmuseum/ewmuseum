Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Six colts in the ashtray. I only smoke once in a while, and this is one of those times. It's Friday night and I'm sitting in my hotel room in a haze of smoke screen. It's hard to breathe. I just found out last night it was Austin Davis who was spreading those gay rumors about me. He was the last person I would have suspected. I thought the motherfucker was dead. Now he's back, and he's picked me as his "victim." Well he's picked the wrong dog. I haven't slept since last night because I've just been thinking about sending my fist down his throat. Screw the wrestling, I want to see that punk in the street and fight him. Lately I've been on a roll. I beat Triple X, the fWo Internet Champion. By all rights that belt should be mine now. I also beat some yahoo whose name I can't even remember. And last night, last night I beat the Flying Frenchie. I'm getting closer to being Wrestler of the Year. Things are going great, but I know Austin Davis is planning against me. He didn't just turn the tabloids against me for nothing. He wants to ruin me. The blonde in the short skirt and tank top coughs from all the smoke I've been blowing. She holds her jacket up to her face and tries to breathe under it. "Can't we go somewhere already?" I'm going to get laid tonight. Take the broad out to a club, dance away the stress, and come back here for a long night of pussy pounding. Fuck the colts. It's Friday night and I'm Cody Williams - there's no excuse for NOT getting laid. I'll worry about Austin Davis when he's enough of a threat.
