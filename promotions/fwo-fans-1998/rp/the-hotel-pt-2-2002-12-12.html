The Hotel, pt. 2

12 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"He's going to fucking hang for this," Winters snorted. He stood in the middle of his hotel room, taking in the surroundings. Everything had been destroyed in one way or another. The mattress was propped against the wall and night stand, sheets and blankets stripped off and strewn across the room. Only one small lamp and a nightlight worked in the room; the rest had been destroyed and thrown against the wall by Wasner. The small mini- bar had been opened, bottles of wine had been smashed against the dresser, merlot stained the white carpet. "Thinks he's smart," he muttered. "Thinks he has the upper hand. I'll show him." Kicking over a broken wine bottle, he sneered and noticed the window. It had been almost completely broken out; a few jagged shards stood in place along the outside of the frame. His fist came out and cleared the remaining glass away. From the street below, Scott Wasner saw Winters poke his head through the window and look at the dark alley below him. Wasner smiled and Winters balled his fist and turned his attention back to the wrecked room. Watching his feet, he moved towards the bathroom. As he got there, he saw the bat. Picking it up, he ran his free hand up and down the cold aluminum. A sick smile came across his face as he saw the fresh scar on the back of his hand. Looking up, he saw the shattered mirror. He tossed the bat into the main room and read the message. "And I'll be seeing you, Scott..." Again he sickly smiled. He closed his eyes and inhaled. In a quick motion his head crashed into the mirror, smashing it yet again. He faced himself in the remains of the mirror, smiling as fresh crimson trickled down his face. Reaching into his pocket, he produced a shaving blade. The blade glimmered in what little light there was as he flicked it open. Rust colored blood was stained onto the blade. The broken faucet moaned as water sprayed out; Winters ran the blade under the lukewarm water and ran his fingers across the blade, clearing off the blood of Ty Hughes, Token Weed and himself. "I'll be seeing you real soon, Scott," he laughed insanely. Slaughter was almost guaranteed to be a bloody mess.
