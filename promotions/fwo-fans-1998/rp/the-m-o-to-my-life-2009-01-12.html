The M.O. To My Life

12 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What is that noise? It’s so faint; can hardly make it out. Beep. Beep. Beep. Need to rub the eyes, wake myself up and gather my surroundings. Before I could do that however I saw a blurry white figure standing before me who had the sweetest voice I ever heard. “Mr. Shadows. Great to see that you’re finally awake” she says to me. “Where am I exactly?” “You’re at St. Vincent’s Medical Center. How are you feeling?” Why do doctors ask you that question? Obviously I feel like shit because I’m lying here in a hospital bed. What do you think I’m going to say? That I feel like a million bucks. You fuckin’ douche bag. I now hate her voice. “Well nurse, you tell me. I just woke up, I’m in a hospital bed, I have tubes all hooked up to me and that damn beeping noise is becoming rather annoying to me”. God damn! Now that I have washed away the cob webs and actually looked at this nurse, maybe I spoke too soon. This nurse is hot. Maybe I can convince her to give me a sponge bath. “Well Mr. Shadows, you have been out for three days. You suffered a severe concussion and three broken ribs. One of which nearly punctured your lung. You are a very lucky man to say the least.” “Out for three days you say?” Let me throw in a joke and see how that fly’s. “So, did you need to hold my junk or something if I had to take a wiz?” She laughed, but I could tell it was a ‘you’re a complete moron’ laugh. “Heh, no. We have other means of assisting our patients for their every day bodily functions.” I threw her my smile; they all fall for the smile. “Too bad”. Now let’s throw her my wink. There ya go Steve. Rope this chick in while layin’ in a hospital. Oh god, that hurts. I clutch my stomach in pain. Apparently I can’t laugh. She informs me why. “You really need to keep it easy sir. You have three broken ribs and that lung is severely bruised. Try not to make any sudden movements and as you just saw, laughing will be painful. It is time for your medication, and since you’re awake we can give you some pills instead of an injection.” She comes over to me like the loving wife I don’t have. Looks at me in my eyes, has a glass of water in one hand and my pain relief in another. “Open up now” she tells me. I comply and she drops the pills into my mouth, followed by her giving me some water and a wipe of my chin. Not to mention I got a really nice shot of her tits. Fantastic. Time for cheep small talk. “So Nurse….” “Winward. Nurse Ashlee Winward Mr. Shadows.” There we are with that damn James Bond thing again; even chicks do it too. So annoying, but given her looks I suppose I can look past that. “So I don’t see a ring or a heart necklace on you. Have a man in your life?” She looks flustered and a hopefully a tad flattered. “Well Mr. Shadows, let’s just say I do not date patients of mine.” UGH! Laughing hurts. Need to remember not to do that. “Well Miss Winward, I won’t be a patient in about twenty four hours. So how about you and I get together as soon as I’m out of gods waiting room?” Love that smile of hers. “Aw, you’re sweet. Unfortunately I do not date patients, no matter how long they are here for. It’s a rule by the hospital and if I want to keep my job I need to abide by that Mr. Shadows. Maybe in another life time.” Fuck that smile. “Yeah, maybe.” Good, she’s walking away. Stupid bitch can’t entertain me while I’m lying in this hell hole? “Oh, before I leave Mr. Shadows, I was told to give this to you.” She hands me an envelope that has nothing written on the outside of it. She flashes me a smile that says “poor thing” on it. I open it and it reads: “Being alive is a luxury. I only let you live, to see you die at my hands.” I hear the heart monitor speeding up. I feel my face getting warm with rage; the veins wanting to burst through the skin on my forehead. She looks at me with worry on her face, but sadly for her does she really realize what is coming. I sit up and let out an animalistic growl. “ARGGGHHHH!!!!” The cute nurse that I wanted to fuck two seconds ago has become an ugly messenger of my enemy, and thus deserved death. I sit up and look at her, and then fly out of my hospital bed with great speed. The little sticky circular things that were on my chest pop off and the heart monitor suggests that I am dead now. Before I knew it my hand was firmly wrapped around her throat, and her pretty head was slammed up against the wall behind her. “Who gave you this note?” I yelled in her face. Spit flying off my lips and onto the face I wanted to kiss just moments ago. “I…I don’t know” she tells me in fear. She’s lying; she must know. “Bullshit. Who gave you this fucking note?” This time I squeeze harder. I want to hear her next sentence gasp for life, so she knows that I am not fucking around. “OH MY GOD! SECURITY!! COME QUICKLY! A NURSE IS BEING CHOKED BY A PATIENT! QUICK!” I squeeze harder. “In about 15 seconds the oxygen will be cut off from your brain and you will suffocate. What gave you this fucking letter!?” Her eyes were becoming blood shot but I didn’t care. Finding out who delivered the letter was my only objective. She could die by my hands. “I..I...” she moved her neck around in my hand to grasp at air. “I really don’t know. He had no name, no address, no ID. We had to call him John Doe because of that.” I loosen my grip just a bit so I can hear her next answer. “Where is John Doe?”. “He left, he checked out on his own two days ago.” This was not the answer I wanted to hear. I grab her around the neck with both hands now and lift her off her feet and into the air. I look up at her, but I see him. I see the man who put me here; the man who got the best of me. I want to kill him. I feel a sharp pain in my back. I turn around to see what it is and I see strings coming out of what looks like two fish hooks from my back. They lead to a security guard who presses the button. It sends a slight sting through my body, kind of tickles really and dropped me to a knee. I look up at him, see his fear, and rush him. I tackle him and begin punching him in his face over and over, again seeing the enemy that put me here. How the fuck did he walk out of here two days ago? I broke his jaw, I messed him up just as badly as he did me. How did I get made to look like a complete chump? Bullshit; and I was going to take it out on whoever I could. Another nurse, male, tried to jump on me to help out his associates…but I stuck him good. Right in the neck with a needle; have no idea what was in the needle but the immediate thread has been eliminated. Two more sharp stings in my back. Great, four fish hooks in my back. This is going to hurt. Two tazers at the same time mean 100,000 volts. Not surprising they had to use two as I am juiced up on pain killers and adrenaline. My body completely stiffens and all I can think to do is scream death threats. “’I’LL KILL YOU! I KNOW YOU’RE STILL HERE!” A knee to the back of my neck doesn’t feel so good. “Get the fuck off of me. GET OFF! HE’S DEAD! HE’S FUCKIN’ DEAD!”. “You are under arrest”. Crap, cops showed up. Fantastic. “You have the right to remain silent. Anything you say or do can be held against you….” I look around at the destruction I left behind me during my blind rage. A nurse who has my hand prints around her throat, a security guard who is trying to piece together his face and a male nurse who was stuck in the throat by a needle I was holding. I’m going to jail. Fuck.
