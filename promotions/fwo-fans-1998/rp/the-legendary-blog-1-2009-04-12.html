The Legendary Blog #1

12 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I take a drink of beer and look over at the computer. It hasn't been on since the wreck, it hasn't been on since December. I set the beer down and walk over, turning the PC on. A few minutes later, the machine is on and buzzing. As I open up the browser, the homepage is set to theUSWL.com. Memories. The site is down. I sigh. When Matt died that night, the deal with FOX was as good as dead as well. It's too bad, good things where about to happen. Matt... The man who was like a brother to me. The man who brought me into the business. We spent many hours training. I shed blood, sweat, and tears. We held titles together, we won together, and we lost together. That night, we should have died together. But we didn't. As I sit here looking at the screen, a tear roles down my eye. Compose yourself John. I fumble around some paperwork beside the computer. I wish I hadn't just thrown it over here. I find what I am looking for and head to the official FWO website. In the USWL, we spoke with our fans through their USWL Universe blog system. I was slightly amused that the FWO was venturing into this as well. Since I'll be at reAction this week, maybe it'd be a good time to post something. Of course, I didn't know something this simple would start a chain reaction of emotions. Oh well, I can't let on to the fans that in my personal life I'm finding myself contemplating life as I am. I need to stay positive. The FWO is a new chapter in my life. I log in with the details provided to me from the paper I received in my contract package. Shit... I entered my password wrong. Do it again and I am logged out for 15 minutes. I hate internet explorer. Even though it is my typing while I've been drinking, I always blame Microsoft. There, I got it. I find the link for posting blogs to the site and click it. I shake off my nervousness and get in character. This would be the first time I talk directly to the fans of the FWO. Hello Fans Wrestling Organization fans. I wanted to take a moment to introduce myself to the FWO fan nation. I am... you know what? If you don't know who 'The Living Legend' is, then you've been under a rock since 2004. That's right, I'm 'The Living Legend' Johnny Legend. For five years making an impact in the wrestling world! Now, it's all paid off. The long drives, working the independent scene for pocket change, the wrestling in front of crowds filled in high school gyms who didn't deserve to see someone of my caliber. That's all behind me. Oh yea it is. The FWO, one of the most distinguished promotions in the world made one of the smartest choices in their history by inking the deal that brought them the legend himself. Make sure you tune in Wednesday and get your first glimpse of me, 'The Living Legend', as I start the road to the top of the mountain. It's time to quit worshiping dope heads and criminals. To stop watching washed up has beens, and over rated never will be's. When it's all said and done, the smoke has cleared, and I sit on top of the world of the FWO... You will then know why I AM 'The Living Legend.' - out I click submit. It's done. Maybe a fan will stumble across it, maybe they wont. Either way I have to get some rest and head to Seattle. Wednesday I join the ranks of many others who came before. This is for you Matt.
