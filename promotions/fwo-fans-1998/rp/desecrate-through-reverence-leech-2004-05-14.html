Desecrate Through Reverence: Leech

14 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'll write this so I won't cry. That has will work. If only I could find the balls to talk to him. If only I'd had those balls when I met him...this wouldn't be happening then. I wouldn't be having this argument with myself. I wouldn't be tempted to go maim Reno fucking Davis my ex. Today it rained. That's cheesy on paper, but it did. I should pay more attention to the weather...it seems to foreshadow things. I got shot in a late-February thunder storm. I got married in the snow. I broke down on a rainy April afternoon. Funny that I got married almost a year to the day I was shot to save him. Selfish little bitch, always keeping things to myself. Maybe I'll look back a year from today and giggle while I read this. Maybe I'll have another baby in my arms. Maybe I'll be staring into my daughter's eyes. Maybe Brandon will strangle me when I tell him. I could end tonight. I'm sitting in an overheated hotel room with the curtains shut. I'd watch the rain, but it's dark out and I'm afraid of the dark. That's so cheesy to write. What's wrong with me? It's the perfect setting for a murder, for a breakup, for a denial... Why didn't I tell him? I just didn't think of it. How horrible is it that I didn't think of her there, barely aware and laying in a little orphanage bed. I know she doesn't have a family yet. She needs one. I need to complete mine. I need to be honest for once. Brandon can't go on without knowing. Stephen can't go on without knowing and I can't go on without telling them. I love them both too much to keep it to myself. Brandon will be back from his merchandising meeting soon. I could distract him with sex...but he knows me too well, he'll know. Then I'd not only be a liar and a bad mother, I'd be a slut...wouldn't I? Wow. I'm scheming myself easily back into the life I left in a cloud of dust. I'll tell him then. I don't know what happens after that, but I don't have a choice in the matter. I keep getting sick. But I'm not crying. That would be too obvious. That's why I'm sitting here with a Sharpie and a paper pad. This is ridiculous, I feel like a little girl scratching away in her fuzzy pink journal. That's not the ridiculous feeling though. What's ridiculous is that I am a little girl. It's too easy to cover with a wedding ring and some tattoos. Now I have to face myself. I have to show myself. I know people can easily hate me for that. And they do. I don't understand how anyone can love me, but don't know that anyone even will after all the little sins. [BACK to "The Decline"] or [FAST FORWARD to "Signed in Blood"]
