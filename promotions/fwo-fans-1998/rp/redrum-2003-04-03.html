They told me that we would be touring out of the states for sometime, due to us being banned or some reason. They also told me that I was going to have to lay off the drug usage, yes they told me that. But, I don't know how I could ever leave you. You bring me such an orgasmic feeling that I cannot describe. But, never the less I must put you away for the time being, I'm not saying good-bye, more like till next time. With that being said Rahl had passed out onto the floor, hitting his head on that nightstand. When Rahl hit the floor a loud thud echoed through out the hotel that the fWo wrestlers were staying out. Rahl ended up being on the floor passed out till fWo officials came to check up on Rahl and noticed he was on the floor knocked out. But, on him they found a note, they spelling was terrible and some of the letters were backwards. .thEy tod mi tat we wood b touring oUt oF the state four summer time, do tWo us being banneded or some reason. thEy all so tood mi thAt I waz go ing to have to lay off thE drug usage, yA they told me that. Butt, I don't knowed howed I coulded ever leave u. u brin mi such a orgAsmiC feeling that I can not scribe. Butt, never-less I must put you a wAay fFor the ttime beiing, I'm noot sayying ggood byye, more like till next time. Rahl had killed Tim Veneno, the weapon used in the murder? Drugs, with each drug Rahl used it came closer and closer to killing Tim, after awhile Tim became Areumna'derin Rahl. Tim wasn't completely dead, he still had a little left in him, but the question now was when would Tim get the strength he needed to fight back?
