"When are you ever going to learn?" - Part 2

12 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tuesday 29th April 2003 Cyberslam had been and gone, and so had a couple of shows, and Borst had been fired. Apparently burning down the big screen and trying to set the ring alight didn't go down too well with the fWQuo big wigs. It was to be expected really, but our man Borst was just a little peeved that the show did indeed go on, and his efforts to kill off Cyberslam were somewhat thwarted. He'd gone back to the Asylum to kick the shit into a man with no willy as well, and was quite satisfied with the job he did there... but still, the whole not quite ruining Cyberslam and the fWQuo still being alive thing left a bad taste in his mouth. Similar to that of, say, dog shit. Now I'd imagine dog shit doesn't taste too great, and Borst had a similar train of thought, so he was out on a mission to get rid of that bad taste. He'd opted for that age old method of getting shit faced. A method made a lot easier when you've milked over a million dollars from your former employer. Not knowing pretty much anywhere in America very well, Borst opted for what he called a "classy bar". It was a shit hole... to put it nicely. A lovely bar downtown called "Lou's Place". Upon entering the bar, a few chairs flew past him, one smashing through a window. Borst smirked and nodded, turning to an intoxicated man lying spread eagle on a table and informing him that "It's gonna be a good night." A bit of rough and tumble, loads of alcohol, and loads of cash. It makes for a good night in Borst's books. And a good night out is exactly what that shorts wearing fine specimen of a man needed that night. "Grog me." Borst grunted, leaning over the bar. The barmaid just stared at him blankly. He sighed and sat on a nearby stool and made his order once more. "Beer please, woman. And make it snappy." Wink. She was apparently one of those feminist type women, and didn't take kindly to Borst's demands. "What makes you think you can talk to me like that wise guy?" Borst chuckled to himself for a minute. "You've got a fucking stupid accent..." he mumbled. "And you're a woman. Why the fuck am I going to treat you like a man? I know you look a bit like one and all, but..." "You arrogant pig... what makes you think you can talk to me like that?" she harped back, slamming her fist on the bar as she began to pull a pint one handed. "Hey woman, don't get me wrong. I'm not arrogant. I take great offence to that. I am however better than you, so..." "Do you want that beer or not?" she asked sternly, running her hands through her greasy blonde locks. "Do you want to get me that beer and stop hassling me? I've had a rough enough week or two as it is." Borst stood up and leant over the bar and waved his fist at her. "You, my lady, are heading for a smack, knowworrimean?" "Fuck you." She grunted, slamming a beer in front of Borst. He just flung a note of some value at her, not particularly caring what it was. He's rich. "We don't take Monopoly money here pal." Bah. Foiled. After paying for his drink probably, Borst swivelled around on his hair and had a quick look around at the yank lager louts littering the bar. "Fucking shit..." he muttered to himself. Moments later a fight broke out over something completely random, as they do in this type of scummy bar. Borst just couldn't help but get excited at the sight of a little destruction and violence, as blood splattered everywhere, and tables and chairs were being smashed left right and centre. "Now that's a bloody good fight." Borst said turning to the barmaid he'd so impressively offended. "These guys can really fight. Not as good as me of course... but fuck, I'd pay to watch guys fight like that." She wasn't listening to a word Borst was saying, she was in a doped up, drugged up, world of her own. "They'd show those fucking girly wimps in the wrestling world a thing or two..." continued Borst, as he gazed on at the carnage. "These guys are built like brick shit houses... the fat sort with no muscle, but still brick shit houses. I'd definitely pay to watch this stuff." As he talked, the cogs in his head began turning... slowly... "I'd much rather watch people like this fight that fucking wrestling like the fWQuo." The hamster is picking up speed. "Imagine a wrestling promotion with proper fucking fighting going on in the ring like that. It'd piss on the fWQuo. I fucking hate the fWQuo... I don't believe how close I came to ruining the biggest event in wrestling history. So close... it would have as good as killed the fWQuo..." Borst continued gazing at the fight going on. "The fWQuo would go out of business in no time if there was an alternative like this kicking about. Fat hairy bikers kicking the shit into each other, there'd be a huge market for it. When they get pinned for 3, they'll really be knackered, not just..." Ding. Lightbulb. Borst fact of the day: On June 14th 2002, Borst broke the legendary baseball player "Babe" Ruth's world record for not stepping on the cracks in the pavement. Borst broke the old record of 17 months by 47 days.
