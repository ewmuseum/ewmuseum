Power Shift

12 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mike stood in the hallway and stared at the door. For ten long minutes. How had things gone so wrong, so fast? Mike had been the assistant to Kodiak Vic Creed for several months now, and he'd done a great job as far as he was concerned. He'd gone above and beyond what would be considered "normal duties" for the Main Man. He'd missed family events, holidays, birthdays, all for Creed. How did Vic show his appreciation for all Mike's hard work and sacrifice? By sleeping with Mike's girlfriend. Anita was the one who told her boyfriend what was going on, how Creed threatened to have Mike fired from his dream job if she didn't do what he told her to. She felt physically ill at the thought of Creed touching her. Mike didn't feel sick. He felt angry, but this was his dream job after all. Working with Disney and the fWo had already started to open doors for him. There was no telling just how far he could go. How far they could go. Could he give that up when they were so close to success, to breaking out on their own? Of course, there was still the issue of Creed having nasty freak-sex with Anita. At the time, they felt stuck in their situation. That changed one night when Mike was approached by an associate of Darryl Vishanti. Mike was given a briefcase full of money, and would continue to do so as long as he provided Darryl with important information about Creed: people he was in contact with, injuries he had sustained, and various personal nuggets of information. Tonight was the night Anita was going to tell Kodiak Vic Creed that she was through with him. She wasn't going to be his little Love Toy anymore, and he couldn't do a thing about it. Mike had enough capital built up that he could go out on his own and start his own business. They were going to stand up to the Main Man, and had fun selling him out in the process. It all seemed like a good idea until Creed called Mike's room. "Get over here. Now." Suddenly, crossing Vic Creed didn't seem like such a great idea. Mike took a deep breath and knocked on the door. "It's open," Vic yelled from inside. "Get your ass in here." Mike opened the door and stepped into Creed's hotel suite. He heard Anita's sniffling before he saw her with tears streaming down her face. He also noticed that she was naked, sitting on top of a pillow resting on the seat of a chair. Mike looked over at the bed and saw the blankets crumpled up in a ball by the headboard. A red circle of blood stood out against the ivory white sheets, and Mike was pretty sure the stain didn't come from Creed. Vic was nowhere to be seen. "Where is he?" Mike whispered. Anita just sat there and sobbed, shifting uncomfortably on her pillow. "Hey Mike, glad you could come by." Kodiak Vic Creed stepped out of the bathroom, his naked muscular body still slick with sweat. He pointed at his still-rigid member as he passed Mike. "Damn thing just won't go down. I think your old lady broke it when I put it in her ass." Mike's mouth hung open in shock as Vic grabbed the blanket off the bed. He held it up for his assistant to see. "Look at that. You can still make out her bite marks." "You... bastard!" To his credit, Mike's right hook caught Creed right on the jaw and snapped the big man's head back. However, when punching a large naked man in the face it's best to make sure his system isn't filled with various adrenal and testosterone enhancers. Vic grabbed Mike by the throat and slammed him against the wall before throwing him onto the bed. "Do that again, and you'll be the next bitch I wear." Vic grabbed Anita by the hair and yanked her off the pillow. It too was covered in blood. Vic tossed her and the bloody pillow onto the bed beside Mike and sat down in the chair. "So Anita here tells me you've been talking to Vishanti." Mike didn't know what to say. He feigned ignorance as he hugged his girlfriend and wrapped the blanket around her. "Who?" "Don't play dumb." Creed opened up a bag of peanuts and started to snack on them. "She told me everything just before I donkey punched her." Mike looked at Anita and she started sobbing again. Vic tossed a peanut and bounced it off Mike's forehead. "Relax. It's not like I didn't already know. Darryl called me just before the Thunderdome match. He called on my new cell phone. The one that only one person besides myself knew the number for." Anita wiped her eyes. "But if you knew then... why?" Vic tossed a peanut into the air and caught it in his mouth. "Because you're a dirty whore, your boyfriend's a stupid prick, and I'm the Main Man." Mike thought about standing up but Anita hugged him tightly. "So what did you tell him?" asked Creed. "What does Darryl know?" Filled with guilt and self-loathing, Mike tried to find the words to speak. "Your knee... medication... workouts..." Vic set the bag of peanuts down on the table and leaned forward, his elbows resting on his naked thighs. "Did he ask about anything personal?" "H-he asked if you were seeing anyone regularly," Anita said through her sniffles. "And? Anything else?" Mike and Anita looked at each other and froze up. Vic caught their expression. "What did you tell him?'" Neither was in a hurry to speak, so Creed strongly encouraged them by kicking Anita in the chest and grabbing Mike by the hair. "Guess my knee's getting better," he said as he lifted Mike and threw him to the floor. Vic kicked the chair over and forced the back across his assistant's neck. Three hundred and twenty pounds leaned on the chair and, ergo, Mike's throat. "What. Did. You. Tell. Him." Mike's tongue was already rolling out of his mouth and he was turning reddish purple. "Sarah.... Told him..." "You told him where she was? You told Darryl Vishanti where my daughter was?" "I... urk..." The whites of Mike's eyes were starting to show. Anita grabbed Vic by the hair and tried to pull him off of her boyfriend. The Juggernaut stood up and shoved her on the bed. He grabbed Mike by the shirt and picked him up like luggage. Grabbing Anita by the hair with the other hand, Creed tossed them both into the hallway. "If that prick so much as looks at my little girl the wrong way..." Vic's rage was coming close to overwhelming him. He clenched his jaw as he forced himself to speak. "Her well-being is the only thing keeping you two alive right now. If something happens to her, I'll fuck you both with a chainsaw." Vic slammed the door. People started peeking out from behind their doors to see the commotion in the hallway. They stared at the young man with the bruise across this throat, and the naked woman with the bloody smear on her butt. Embarrassing? Indeed. Humiliating? Possibly. The worst thing that could happen to either of them? Nah. There's always that chainsaw.
