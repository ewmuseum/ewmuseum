Training Daze: Week 2

9 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Luke Starkey stood in the middle of the ring. Sweat poured down his forehead and into his eyes, but he was too tired to wipe it away. His hands pressed hard against his knees, holding him in some weak semblance of upright. Inhaling and exhaling in rapid succession, Luke could only stand there, barely. And besides, the man he stood over was out cold, and Luke was certain that his partner had yet to get up from the padded, ring-side area below. Luke was exhausted, but Lance Knight wouldn't let up. "C'mon, Luke! Finish it! Pin him!" Ordinarily, Luke would have smiled and flipped him the bird, but he was too tired. In fact, he thought suddenly, if Lance wanted him to pin his opponent, and that opponent just HAPPENED to be directly at his feet... Luke simply gave up his precarious hold on maintaining a vertical position. His knees hit the mat, jarring his torso forward to land atop his fallen foe. His opponent was, of course, lying on his back with shoulder blades in firm contact with the canvas ring floor. Luke's eyes closed and he managed a small smile. It felt so good to lay down; given just a moment he was sure he could fall asleep right now, if only someone would quit yelling in his ear. "One! "Two! "Three!" And suddenly Luke was being hauled to his feet. Too tired to even whine in protest, he let himself be uprighted, glad that someone had the presence of mind to support him around the waist. Luke had never been this tired, this exhausted, this weary in his whole life. He was dimly aware of the cheering and hollering around him, and Lance's voice loud in his ear. "Ha ha! You did it, Luke! You won!" Luke could only nod, weakly. Lance helped him through the ring ropes, which was not nearly as easy as it had been on the way into the ring. Hands reached out for him, clapping him on the back and shoulders, grasping his arms and squeezing once before letting go. Faces of rough men all laughing and smiling at him, happy and raucous because of him. The people, crowding in on all sides of him, pushing forward in a human wave, threatening to drown him... Lance used his free arm to push some of the by-standers back. "Give him some room!" Lance was in charge here. As soon as Luke and Alex had entered this gym Lance Knight was the topic of conversation, the focus of the local gym rats who couldn't believe that an honest-to-god star of the Fan's freaking Wrestling Organization had just walked through the door. They were respectful and almost polite, and when Lance had asked for two volunteers to give Luke a handicap match, there were no shortage of volunteers. Those two men were slowly coming around, thanks to the ministrations of their colleagues. Lance led Luke to the just-barely serviceable locker room and helped him sit on a bench. Luke was completely drained, and just sitting on a wooden bench was absolute bliss. Lance kept chatting away beside him. "...never seen anyone knocked out of the ring with a headbutt! HA!" He was so tired. So very tired. Luke's body sensed his weariness and the length of the bench suddenly beckoned, offering him a steady spot to lay down and sleep. His huge yawn must have given away his intent, as Lance grabbed him and was making him stand up again. "Shower, Luke, shower. It'll wake you up, and believe me you'll feel better afterwards. Besides, you reek." Even Luke's fatigued brain could see the sense of this. He smiled at his friend, who was somehow once again now just Alex instead of Lance Knight. "You suck. You know that, don't you?" Luke mumbled. Alex laughed, even as he gently shoved Luke toward the shower. Luke's feet moved of their own accord, his body propelled into motion by Alex's push. He'd never been so god-awful, down-to-the-bone, praying-for-death tired in his whole life. Training was going to kill him, he was sure. And Meltdown was still weeks away.
