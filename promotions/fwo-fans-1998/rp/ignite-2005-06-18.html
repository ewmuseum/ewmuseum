Ignite

18 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fire Most likely, the greatest discovery of man. Also, the first thing that comes to any fWo universal mind when thinking of Craig Miles. For Craig Miles, the very word conjures up the image of Armaghetto. Fallen laughing maniacally as Ric Chronos regained consciousness in a hell he couldn't escape alone. Countless times its been written on how little thought process is involved within a Craig Miles decision, or action. Nothing processed in Miles' mind as he tightened his grip on the crowbar, his eyes solely focused on the back of Fallen's skull. Hours afterwards, Craig Miles would be able to think while sitting in the hospital wondering if Ric Chronos would survive. Frantically, Craig called Silver Fox before even calling Ric's wife, Lyra. In fact, Craig Miles called Silver Fox a total of 39 times that night. Fox knew about Fallen, Miles knew Fox heard the news. ...Fox never picked up the phone. In fact, Silver Fox didn't return any of Craig Miles' calls. "How do you fulfill an oath to a man that's abandoned the cause?" Miles pondered the thought night after night, in the hospital. When Ryan Moore aimed that pistol at Miles, the Dean of Thermodynamics actually felt remorse that he'd fail Fox. Fox put true faith inside Miles one night long ago, now Craig was left deciding if it was worth continuing fighting for a man without faith. John Smith... The man that took the faith away. The top name on the list...a throne upon a hiearchy of names. Dudley, Fox, Spratt... ...Cowl, Tallison. The irony was that Craig Miles had lost his identity in search of an identity that could catalyze the fWo back into the hands of people who wouldn't manipulate riots, murders and blackmail. Fire... Craig couldn't even light a cigarette. Seeing the flame on the Zippo sparkle as it'd light his Newport reminded him too much of Fallen's delight of Ric Chronos' searing flesh. The man known as Lost...in his Lost thoughts. "Thank God for the Codemaster," Miles smirked to himself this past Monday Night. Miles' soul had been 'Lost,' in confusion of where to step forward. Smith finally hooked Fox, somehow. Any 'source' material was gone, but Speck reminded Miles that there were other ways. See, "The Codemaster" is really Allen Brown. Upon hacking into the fWo Human Resources Database last year, Miles was privy to alot of information. ...useful information. Such as handy Private Investigator reports that if the roster knew about, would probably take extensive legal action: "Brown is the youngest member of a wrestling family, but while wrestling was in his blood, it wasn't exactly something he wanted to do. He got in it based off of a challenge from his older brothers (to become more successful than they are). Brown doesn't get along very well with either his brothers or his dad, to the point where he didn't even bother getting trained by them and went a couple of hundred miles south to get trained by Joey Malone instead. He honestly hates the sport more than he lets on. Vehemently hated his gimmick at first, but now seems to bear it knowing the fWo employee that designed it for him, is dead. Ironically owns a video game store in Utah, when not working in the business." Simply put, Allen Brown was part of the machine. John Smith's machina of manipulation. If Fox couldn't help anymore from the inside, Miles would break them all free the only way possible. On the outside, one by one... The Truth would be known. Fire ignited Craig Miles smirked as he peered over Allen Brown's printed out profile. "I thank you, Codemaster. You shall know the truth, firsthand."
