A Road Paved In Broken Glass: Part Three

3 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"After my fathers death, I was sent to America to live with my aunt and uncle. The dream of becoming a pro-wrestler was put on the back burner for a while, mainly because it was frowned upon in my new home. I loved growing up there though, and my cousin Eli was like a brother to me… but unfortunately, he too drifted away.” May 16th, 1999. It had been a close race but it would all come to an end tonight. Although Milo and Eli had both worked very hard in their campaign, there could only be one prom king. Silence plagued the high school gymnasium as principal Silverstein stood at the microphone, with an envelope in hand. It was time to announce the winner, and that was something the entire school longed to witness. "Before I announce the winner, I would like to congratulate the boys on a successful campaign. This was the closest finish I've ever seen in my forty-four years of teaching, with a small one vote margin deciding the winner." Said Principal Silverstein, as he began to clap. Those in attendance followed and began clapping, as the suspense grew even stronger. Mr. Silverstein continued… "The winner, and the 1997 Mill River High School prom king is… Milo Samus!" Milo stood still in a state of shock, while everyone around him began to cheer. He hadn't expected for this to happen, but he had only hoped it would. His hard work had finally paid off, and it was time to give his acceptance speech. But he stood motionless, completely shocked at the outcome of tonight’s events. "Milo?" Mr. Silverstein cleared his throat, a signal for Milo to join him on stage. Milo remained standing still, until nudged by an onlooker. Milo walked up onto the stage, and took hold of the microphone. He then began to speak… "Well what can I say really, other than thank you to each and every one of you. I'd never dreamed that I would be the one up here, so thanks to everyone who voted for me." Milo caught sight of his cousin Eli, who had just stormed out of the gymnasium and passed the microphone back to Mr. Silverstein. "Well spoken Milo, congrats!" said Mr. Silverstein extending a hand to congratulate Milo. Milo ignored, and walked passed the principal, trying to catch up to Eli. As he made his way into the parking lot, he watched Eli speed away in his truck. "This can't be good" thought Milo. After hours of searching for his cousin, Milo went to the loft, a place the two had spent a lot of time while they were growing up. He heard something moving at the far end of the loft, and walked forward to investigate. It was indeed his cousin Eli, sitting with a shotgun in hand. "Stop right there asshole!" Hollered Eli, pointing the gun in Milo's direction. "What are you doing? Put the gun down you moron." Said Milo, stepping towards Eli. "I swear to god I'll blow your fucking head off! Get away from me!" Demanded Eli, still pointing the shotgun directly at Milo. "You're going to shoot me because I won the election? If you are that pathetic shoot me then." Milo said in an attempt to make Eli realize he was being stupid. "The election? I couldn’t care less if you won the election… you never even thanked me. If it wasn't for me, you wouldn't have even been nominated!" Eli yelled, as he threw the gun to the ground. "I don't need the fucking gun to kick your ass!" he continued, grabbing Milo by the shirt collar. "What the hell is wrong with you?" Milo asked, trying now to break the grip Eli had on his shirt. Eli replied with a hard right hand that sent Milo tumbling to the floor. There he lay, unconscious, and un-consoled…never to see his cousin again.
