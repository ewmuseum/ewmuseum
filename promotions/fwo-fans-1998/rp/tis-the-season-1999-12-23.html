'Tis the Season...

23 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::It is just days before the Christmas holiday, and many of the wrestlers have left the arena after packing their gear in the hopes of doing some last minute shopping before heading off to the next show... Twilight. But one man has stayed behind, content in his solitude. While others rush off to share in the joys of comraderie and family, the Boston Strangler would have none of this for himself. Losing both parents at the age of 17, and having no real family to speak of, he treats Christmas this year exactly as he has for the past 20... with indifference. But as he sits transfixed in deep thought about everything and nothing, you can't help noticing something slightly different about him this holiday season. With a strange fire in his eyes, the Strangler turns to the camera to speak... :: Finally, the wait is over. Our time has come to gain the revenge for an act committed just a mere few weeks ago. We were ready, ready to take that strap of gold from around your waist Primetime... and you knew it. You knew you could not defeat us, that we would not be stopped. So what did you do? You grabbed your little French friend and tried to destroy us and end our career in a sickening moment of blood, flesh, and twisted metal wreckage. That was a huge mistake on your part boy. But you didn't finish the job. That was your second mistake. The minute we regained consciousness, our concern was not whether or not we would leave the hospital in one piece, but whether we would send you from the arena in an ambulance, or a hearse. But self control got the better of us. We decided on a plan, and have executed it, leading up to where we are now... a showdown at Twilight, one on one, with none of your Foundation members allowed at ringside. It looks like Santa Claus has come to town a bit early this year, wouldn't you say? Perhaps we shall have to rethink this whole holiday situation... People say it is better to give than to receive. Looking back on the past month or so, we may find good reason to agree with them. The gift of a hospital stay that we received from the French Foundation was nowhere as enjoyable as giving the same treatment back to you, Primetime. Your attempt at ruining our chance at a title shot was not received well by us, but when we cost you your precious Heavyweight Gold... that was a priceless moment that we shall always cherish. And speaking of giving, champ... remember, we were the ones who softened Scott Slugger up for you, dislocating his shoulder so badly that for him to even compete that night was a tribute to his athleticism. We protected you for every match leading up to your World Title shot. We handed you that damned title on a silver platter, and when your time came to pay up for our services, you chose the road of cowardice rather than honor. So at Twilight, we intend to collect fully for what we are owed from you... plus interest. Count on it. ::The scene fades out with the Strangler returning to his meditation as a commercial for Twilight begins to roll.::
