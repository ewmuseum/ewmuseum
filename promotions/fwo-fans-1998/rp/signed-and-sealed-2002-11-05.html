Signed and Sealed

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim Dudley was the fWo Champion. Cody got up from his couch after over two hours of Halloween Hell III and went to Matilda's room to change her diaper. Clarisse stayed and watched the rest of the on-screen disaster. Wrestlers fought. Fans fought. Metal structures collapsed. Clarisse thought her husband needed some help powdering the baby. She left the TV room and joined Cody in Matilda's room. Painted on the walls were clowns, balloons, bears hearts, and building blocks with letters on them. "Well," said Clarisse, "there goes thirty bucks down the drain." Cody tossed a diaper into the garbage. "We shouldn't complain. We're rich." She took over and began to powder Matilda. "Seriously Cody, is this what you expected when you signed that contract? Did you even think about the fact that the fWo isn't what it used to be?" He handed her a clean diaper. "Actually," he said, "that's the first thought that ran through my mind. But then do you know what I thought? As long as I'm coming back, everything's gonna be fine for the fWo." "Right. Really assuring, Cody." She got Matilda dressed and picked her up. After patting Matilda on the back a couple of times, a burp came out of the baby. He sensed the sarcasm. "Think about it. Remember our last night in the fWo? The fans were going fuckin crazy when we made out on that stage. Don't get me wrong, Chris Titan sucks and won't ever amount to anything, but he gave me the big money feud I needed. Now all the fans are in the palm of my hand." She handed him the baby. "And what if they've... forgotten about you?" Matilda played with Cody's finger as he laughed at Clarisse. "Forgotten? Please! If I was a fan of the fWo and watched the crap they pulled on Halloween Hell, I'd think long and hard about my favorite wrestlers in the past fWo. The fans haven't seen enough of me yet. I wasn't stuffed down their throats. They want more out of me. They want to see all of the dream matches I never gave them. Crusader versus Flying Frenchie, Crusader versus Kodiak Vic Creed, and the list goes on and on." "That's all fine and well, Cody, and it's good to see you've got ambition. But what about those idiots in charge? What if they don't see things the way you do?" "Let me put it this way, Clare: the fans. I know for a fact that they'll choose me over the crap that's going on right now. They want a new superstar, but they don't want any green rookies. They want someone who's got ambition, desire, focus, and experience. That's me, baby. They'll chant my name during other matches. They're going to like me more than they're going to like anyone else. I am going the entertainer of this age, and that's something even the idiots in charge can understand." At this point, Matilda was fast asleep. Cody eased her into her crib and kissed her on the forehead. Clarisse came over and did the same. They went into the bathroom together and washed up. Clarisse took a towel and dried her hands off. "Okay, Cody, so you're relying on the fans. But what if you don't exactly live up to their standards? What if you don't give them what you're telling me?" Cody took the towel from her and started to laugh. He threw it over his shoulder and crossed his arms, grinning from ear to ear. "Clarisse, I told you last night that you'd have seven orgasms before we had sex. How many did you end up having?" She began to blush. "Nine." He snapped his fingers and pointed at her. "Exactly. And if I happen to tell you you'll have nine tonight, how many will you end up having?" She took the towel from his shoulder and kissed him. "More than nine." He winked at her. "Exactly."
