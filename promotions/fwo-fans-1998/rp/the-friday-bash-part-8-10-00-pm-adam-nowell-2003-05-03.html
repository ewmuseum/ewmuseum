The Friday Bash - Part 8 - 10:00 PM - Adam Nowell

3 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Adam Nowell was, of course, everyone's favorite twenty-eight year old "vampire with a puppy", even though he had neither vampirism nor a puppy. People in the fWEo called him Angel. He was one of the fWEo's few, few serious types(in fact, he was the only one of about four), and had recently fought off his long-time nemesis, Hans Krueger, in the midst of his descension into pure jobber. He had also finally rid himself of his stalker/"girlfriend", Sarah the Jobber Slayer, and was so happy about it that he was actually suckered into coming to this party. Once he realized how much this party was completely ridiculous and pointless and a waste of his time, it had been too late. So, naturally, he was drowning his sorrows vigorously with whatever alcohol beverages that he could procure. As it turned out, he wasn't gonna be alone. "`EEEYYYY, BEBE!" a loud, obnoxious red-headed Mexican shouted toward some frizzly-haired woman in a Hawaiian shirt, "Why don't chu come wit' de Sanchis to `es special place and get all FREAK-AY with `em?" The woman gave a loud sigh, "Sanchis Guilherme! That's the fifth time you've asked that to me in the last five minutes!" "Chu know what dey say about de Sanchis, bebe? De say det `e doesn't give up! `E'll be a stickler for de hembras `till dey geeve `em what `e wants!" the man known as Sanchis Guilherme, who was better known as Sanchis Su Idolo from 21st Century Wrestling and Action! Wrestling, said. The woman sighed, then spoke to Sanchis softly, "Usted es un tonto." With that said, the woman spun on her heel and walked away. She would sit down next to some black guy before engaging in some of this "conversation" stuff that Sanchis Su Idolo had heard about on some drunken Friday down in Mexico City. With nobody around for Sanchis to talk to any more, Sanchis sighed and sat down next to Adam Nowell, who was going through what was only his first tequila. It took only a half-second for Sanchis to try and strike up a conversation with Nowell, in his own magical way. "`Ey! Who do chu t'ink chu are, not introducin' choreself to de Sanchis, holmes? De Sanchis es NOT amuse-ed at de moment for chore lack of manners!" Sanchis complained, glaring at Nowell, before he turned to the bartender, "Chu there, give de Sanchis a tequila, holmes, and MAKE IT SNAPPY, de Sanchis, `e's on a hembra clock!" Nowell cast a death gaze at Sanchis, and started to think of ways to kill the annoying Mexican without drawing too much attention. Unfortunately, he could think of none, so he decided to simply ask his question, punctuating each word with contempt, "What do you want?" "De Sanchis wants chu to introduce choreself, before de Sanchis take chore shotglass, and sticks it in chore rear." Sanchis said, with a confident smirk that Adam Nowell wanted to hit. So he hit it. Thank God/Allah/Buddha/Whateveryourreligiouspreferenceis that Sanchis wasn't a jobber, or he would've gone flying across the room. "OWW! `EY, HOLMES! DE SANCHIS, `E WAS... `E WAS JES'KIDDING, ESSE! CHU DIDN'T `AVE TO GO AND HIT DE SANCHIS!" Sanchis shouted, drawing the attention of various people who just stood there, and watched Adam Nowell smile and go back to his tequila. When everyone had left, and the pain in Sanchis' jaw went away, Sanchis settled back down and settled into his tequila, all while still staying next to Adam Nowell. Finally, Sanchis spoke again. "Man, what's everyone's deal `ere, anyway? De Sanchis, `e's never been `it in `es beautiful face so much before, esse! De Sanchis jes' wanted a coupla of de hembras and `ave a drunken night of latino love, bebe! Instead, dese hembras are all lesbians, and now de Sanchis can't even enjoy a good drink wit'out some lowlife vampire wit' a puppy punchin' de Sanchis in `es face!" Sanchis whined. "Call me a vampire with a puppy again, and I'll punch you harder." Nowell said, flatly. "De Sanchis wants to know what chore PROBLEM is." Adam Nowell asked for a second tequila and turned to the obnoxious red-headed Mexican next to him, "Oh, let's see... what ISN'T my problem? I'm still stuck in the fWEo, I still haven't gotten a chance to punch Sphere in the face yet, I tried to leave this pathetic excuse for a party, but the stupid redneck and the stupid Australian shoved me back into the party... and I'm like the last serious person left in that ciyoke of idiots. Not to mention that I still don't know what in the HELL 'ciyoke' means. So, if it's all the same to you... Sanchis, please don't hesitate to piss off." "Well," Sanchis said, "Sounds like chu don't exactly `ave it as easy as de Sanchis does!" Nowell wanted to hit him again, but then two people walked in the room to interrupt. They were Allen Speck and the frizzly-haired woman that Sanchis had been bugging earlier, Leigh Landers. Leigh kept her distance from Sanchis, as she didn't want to be hit on AGAIN, while Speck moved in and sat down next to Nowell. "Bartender. Bring a brother some of your finest tequila." Speck said. "Those two stoners just simply bought the cheapest shit they could find." the bartender quietly replied, before he poured some of the alcoholic liquid that Speck requested. Speck paid him no mind, though he did pay him a tip. "Well, if it isn't de Speck! `Ow is de Speck doing, esse?" Sanchis said, instantly recognizing Allen Speck. "Well well well, if it ain't Sanchis Guilherme! Still doing the whole talking in third person schtick?" Allen Speck said, reaching in front of Nowell to shake Sanchis Su Idolo's hand, much to Nowell's confusion and wish to dismember anything that moved. "De Sanchis `as no idea what chore speakin' of." Sanchis denied. Allen laughed, before he took a swig of the tequila and nearly gagged, as the tequila wasn't very good and it nearly melted the poor man's throat. "JESUS. When you said that this was cheap tequila, you weren't lyin'!" Speck told the bartender. The bartender simply laughed him off and continued doing his bartending duties. Meanwhile, Speck turned to Idolo again, "So, Sanchis. It's been a couple of years, yeah? How's the whole Action! thing goin' for you?" "`Eh, de Sanchis, `e still hasn't found de correct, how chu say... role." "Yeah, I know how that is." Speck said. Nowell grumbled, "You two actually know each other?" he said, before he turned to Speck, "I have lost respect for you for knowing this obnoxious jackass." "We wrestled a couple of times before Sanchis over there went to Twenty-One Dubya. Though I do admit that he's quite obnoxious." Speck said, smirking at Sanchis. "`EY!" Sanchis whined, but he was swiftly annoyed by Nowell and Speck, due to the fact that John Rocker was running through the room, screaming at the top of his lungs before running outside, tripping over the waist-high guardrail that seperated the balcony from open air, and flew into the swimming pool outside. Adam Nowell, Sanchis "Su Idolo" Guilherme, and Allen Speck all glared at this before they all turned to each other. "Raise your hands if you want to get drunk right now." Nowell said. Speck and Sanchis slowly raised their hands.
