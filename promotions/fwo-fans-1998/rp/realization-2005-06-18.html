Realization

18 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sally Apostle could taste the salty sweat on her lips as her head sank into her pillow. With a sigh, she looked over at her lover, David Cross, and noticed that he was staring off into space. "What is it?" Sally asked as she rolled onto her side. She gently caressed David's bare chest and moved her leg under the sheet, draping it over his. "You've been really distracted these past few days. It's not Alex, is it?" It felt strange when Cross thought about it. One the one hand, he didn't like being deceitful to his friend. On the other, he was schtupping his friend's Mom. That was going to go over like a ton of bricks when Alex found out. "Some of it is Alex, among other things I guess." The sensation of Sally running her fingers through his hair made him tingle in his happy places. "I told you we'll tell him," she reassured him. "I just want him to get settled back with the fWo first. You think he'll be upset?" "Let's just say I don't want to be within arm's reach when we tell him." Sally rolled her eyes. "It won't be that bad." David was a little more confident of the outcome. "Oh it will. It really will." With a sigh, Sally plopped her head back into her pillow. "I guess I can see why that would bother you." "There's something else too." He sat up and leaned his back against the headboard. "But it's something I have to take care of on my own." That got her to lift her head again. "So mysterious. If there's anything I can do to help, just ask." Looking into Sally's eyes, David felt an odd sensation in his stomach. At first he thought it might have been the chalupa he had for lunch. No, it wasn't gas. This was something he hadn't felt in a very long time. He deeply cared for her. She was becoming a part of his life and made David feel not like a failure, not like some kind of comedy relief, but like a complete human being. He felt special because of her. Blessed. That's probably how Sarah made Vic Creid feel. When he lost that, he was lost. David could bring Salvation to the Main Man and perhaps turn his life around. As much as he hated to admit it, it was the right thing to do. Of course, the first thing he had to do was to verify that Sarah actually was alive. Since Nick Brandish wasn't about to divulge that information, David thought someone close to Nick might know. But who was close to the Monster? Who had ever been close to... He slapped himself in the forehead. It all suddenly seemed to make sense. It was obvious. "I need a woman." Sally proper herself up on her elbow. "What am I, chopped liver?" "No, I need another woman." "Ohhh, kinky." "Do you remember Vic's ex-wife? Dakota something. She was a stripper I think." David's enthusiasm was having the very opposite effect on Sally. "And why do you suddenly need to find her?" He reached over and held her hand. "You asked if you could help. I need your help to track her down." "Well I suppose I could ask Diane. She's a dancer downtown. She could probably find out where Dakota is working." "Diane... is that the one you borrowed those nipple tassles from?" "That's the one," she cooed. He leaned over and gently kissed Sally on the lips. "Thank her for me, will you?"
