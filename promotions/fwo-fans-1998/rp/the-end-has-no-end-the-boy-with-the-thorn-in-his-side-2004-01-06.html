The End Has No End (The Boy With The Thorn In His Side)

6 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
November 23rd, 2000 The sun had crept it's way to the highest point in the sky, but the late November chill bit into any warmth. On the west coast of Canada, winter came slow but strong. The parking lot of the high school was full. Pieces of paper and garbage littered the lot. Crows and seagulls drifted lazily overhead, pearching on the roof of the school or the overhanging powerlines. Picking at the garbage. The sounds from the mall across the street drifted faintly with the wind. A brown brick building. Older than most of the neighborhood that had sprung up around it. A testament. The grass of the back field still shone with luster of morning frost. Footprints of students dotted the field. And beyond the field, a forest. Quickly, startling the birds, one of the side doors of the school was flung open and two male students emerged. One in tears, his friend following. "That's it", the crying one said. "It's over" "It's all for the best", his friend responded. "Look out there, Steve", the crying one said, motioning to the mall. "You see all those people over there? Life is the same for them. Today is a day just like any other for them. But for me, everything is different. Everything that I've known has changed, and those people have no idea. Nothing has changed and everything has changed" His friend stood in sympathetic silence. "We are alone, Steve. There is no force watching over us, guiding us. We are here by ourselves. Alone. Today is a testament to that" "Come on", his friend said. "Things aren't that bad. Things are never as bad as they first seem" This calmed the crying one down. The two friends hugged briefly, then turned towards the mall. Leaving everything behind. (and the circle opens) "I jumped in the river, what did I see?
