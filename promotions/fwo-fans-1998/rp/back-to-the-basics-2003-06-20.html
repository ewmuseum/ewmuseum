Back to the basics.

20 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"I"m still not used to this personal assistant crap backstage. I have a whole staff... what the hell do I need a personal assistant for?" Universal asked the other person on the phone. The person's identity was unknown so far... and the other end of the conversation couldn't be heard for obvious reasons. Universal sighed. "I mean... what the hell am I paying the other five guys for then? At least Disney is footing the bill... christ." Universal sighed again. It's been nearly a month since he returned to the fWo, and he wasn't pleased with how things were going. He'd lost two big time matches... title matches. The Internet Title was something he'd held on two occasions before, and came close to winning the fWo World Title on numerous occasions. Hell, he nearly beat Creed last year. Perhaps returning wasn't such a good idea... ... but then again, wrestling was in his blood more than anything, except money. Money only because his father was bloody rich and Universal inherited all of it around three years ago. The well was running dry... sure, he wasn't going to be bankrupt anytime soon, but a return to wrestling now would help shore his future so he had some extra spending money to live on. But it wasn't about the money this time; it was about respect. It was about getting in front of the crowd with his peers and proving to himself and each and every person who ever saw a Chris Universal match that he still had it. That he could still go toe-to-toe with his peers and put on good matches, and not only that, walk out the victor. So far the only thing he was proving is that he had a knack for being on his back at least three seconds. That had to change. "Yeah, I'm still here." He'd forgotten he was having a phone conversation with someone... and decided that he was going to end the conversation prematurely. He had a plane to catch, RUAHH~! was coming up this Monday and he needed to get focused. "Look... I hate to cut this short, but I've got to go. Losing to Spyder... I don't know. I was a former Internet Champion, and I thought I'd at least be able to get that belt back. Whatever though. I'll call you after the next tv. Alright... you too. Night." Hanging up the phone, Universal began packing his bags. Once he arrived in town, he was going to head straight to the nearest gym. It was time to get back to the basics, and you know, win a match.
