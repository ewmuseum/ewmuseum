From The Inside Looking Out

20 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The handcuffs were tight around his wrists. The police officer calmly escorted Steven Douglas down the hallway at the police headquarters. Douglas had been charged with attempted murder, he had been tried and convicted...he had been found... Guilty. Steve's head hung slightly, he was tired, worn, soar and felt ill. His whole body ached, not because of what he did for a living, but because of the strain that he had been caused because of Kenny Rock. Kenny Rock, the sonofabitch that has made Steve's life a living hell for the past few months. The sonofabitch that left a permanent scar on Steve's chest. The sonofabitch that sent Steve to prison. "C'mon now Douglas, pick up the pace," The guard gave Steve a slight shove. Steve stumbled forward a few feet before catching his balance. He moved quicker, watching as his cell got closer and closer to him. His prison cell in which he would spend twenty three hours a day in. His prison cell in which all of his independence would be stripped from him. That's when it sank in, this was really happening. It had happened, he was going to prison and there wasn't a damn thing he could do about it. The door shut behind Steve, the slam of the metal colliding with each other sent a shiver down Steve's spine. He wanted to scream. He wanted to kick, to punch. He wanted to curl up in his bed. He wanted to cry. The guard leaned against the metal bars at the sight of Steve's expression, "Hey you know Douglas, it ain't that bad here. You just wait till shower time," The guard smirked and winked his right eye at Steve. Steve threw himself against the metal bars slightly scaring the guard. He held the bars tightly and he breathed heavily as anger coursed through him...yet he said nothing. The guard paused, catching his breath, and when he knew it was safe...he laughed. He laughed hard as he turned and walked back down the hallway. The guard would get to go home tonight, to get to see his wife and his young daughter. He would get to eat a good wholesome meal and then watch the Hockey Game. The guard would get to drink a few beers and then retire next to his wife in bed. Most importantly, the guard was free. And that was something Steve Douglas was far from.
