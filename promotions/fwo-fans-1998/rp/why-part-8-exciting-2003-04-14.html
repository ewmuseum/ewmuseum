Why? Part 8: Exciting

14 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here we are again. It's been a while, but Borst's lazy. He generally can't be arsed to keep things rolling along at consistent rate... but you love these huge gaps don't you? Absence makes the heart grow fonder and all that... Kind of. Yalenchka wasn't overly fond of Borst after a short absence since he'd ripped up the receipt, contract, certificate, whatever you call it. According to Samuel Smooth, she tried to drown herself in two litres of boiling hot coffee and passed out, not before sticking her fist through a wall. But that was the least of his worries at the time. He was a little peeved with Borst, who'd just yesterday told a reporter several home truths that would better off kept at home. She'd since filed a complaint for harassment, racial abuse, a few other bits and pieces, and also taken the conversation away on tape and was as we speak writing a lovely article about Borst and the sorry state of affairs when the fWo use such a man as a poster boy for the United States. Makes you proud doesn't it? "Yes sir." Borst said, drinking a can of lager as he sat in Smooth's makeshift office, in the back of a huge truck. "Borst, I just don't know what the hell you thought you were doing last night. Well no, I know exactly what you were doing, and no one was at all amused. I always said it was a mistake hiring you, especially after your brief past with the fWo." "I don't understand." Borst said, lighting up a cigarette. "What's the big mistake? Everyone loves me. And I know you do too. I even got the title belt fixed too." Borst said, holding up the shiny belt. "Well that's something at least... but I dread to think what people will say about... what the? Let me see that." Smooth said, reaching across to snatch the title belt from Borst. "Why does it have a Union Jack on the front?" "Ah." Borst said. "I got it done when we were in Ireland. You know Irish people... you ask for a square, you get a circle. Ask for an American flag, you get the UK's. Typical. Oh well..." "I've had enough. I'm gonna make some calls, and all being well, you'll be out of here within the hour, jobless. Get out of my sight Borst." "You won't fire me." Borst replied, standing up slowly. "I've been booked to wrestle at the biggest show of the year! Borst vs Frontier! Cyberslam! It's already been signed, job done. I'm going to Cyberslam, and you can't wait!" "Just... just get out Borst. I really don't want to have to deal with this... I've got enough on my plate as it is." Groaned Smooth, burying his head in his hands. "Fix that title belt, and try not to get in any trouble while you're home." "I'll try..." Borst said as he went to leave. "But you know me. Being back in Manchester excites me, beer excites me, cigarettes excite me, the sunshine excites me, shoes excite me. I guess you could say I'm just one excited young man. So I'm not gonna make any promises Sammy. Just you keep your chin up." Wink.
