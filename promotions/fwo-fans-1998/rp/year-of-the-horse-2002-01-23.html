Year of the Horse

23 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tempest stepped out of the cab and smiled in spite of herself. I thought I'd hate coming back here. But it's different this time. This time I don't have to worry about where I'll sleep or if I'll be mugged... Ok, well not as much anyway. Hong Kong is charming in its way. I guess it has no teeth to bite me with anymore. She paid the driver and bowed, the gesture was coming back almost reflexively. As she walked along, looking at the bright lights and signs and gold writing on banners, she thought of the new year about to begin. Year of the Black Horse... nothing will be the same after this year. Everything will change, everything will be in motion. It's already starting. I was so scared that my career was over, but I'm already starting to win back the people who left me. Maybe I can finally shake off all this stuff. Maybe seeing Papa will help. There's got to be a way to resolve it all, to find out what's really going on. Shame about the title though. Her shoulders slumped a little as she walked but she shook her head, tossing the broad, loose ponytail she had tied her hair into. No, it's a good year to travel light. I have too much baggage as it is. Let Golden Boy make a play for it. After all, he won't get by my brother. She stopped suddenly and looked up into the gleaming form of a black horse, outlined in gold and red, lit by footlights. The banner flapped gently in a small breeze giving the impression of impatient movement. My brother... The thought had come unbidden and with the instinctive pride of a blood bond. Could it be that easy to change her feelings? Would he find it that easy? I can't hate Eddy anymore, but can he still hate me? I hated a ghost, a memory that I placed on him. Was I the same to him, a shadow of a past he thought was over? A skeleton rattling in his closet at night? She looked around before turning down a small and crowded street leading deeper into the heart of Hong Kong and its seamier districts. I wonder how many of those ghosts are out tonight?
