Family and Friends

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~Feb. 23, early afternoon~ Tempest watched the snow fall outside the cafe's warm sun room, enjoying the discontinuity of her senses. She sipped at a thick latte and licked the whipped cream from her upper lip with a smile. This was going to be a night remember. Of that much she was sure. She had a chance to regain the Cruiserweight title, and the anticipation of finding out who her kind admirer was. Much as it made her feel strange to have someone who obviously observed her so closely, it was a warm feeling that someone cared enough to want to. Your lips speak soft sweetness, Your touch a cool caress; I am lost in your magic, My heart beats within your chest. She sighed happily. The last time anything unsolicited was left in her locker room it had been the photos that Ric Chronos used to blackmail her. That and the note from Eddy... Eddy should have been there by now. They hadn't talked much still. He seemed wary after the trip to Hong Kong, as if he didn't want to know what she had been there for. He had gotten the same invitation as she though. Trust was hard to come by, family or otherwise. I think of you each morning And dream of you each night; I think of your arms being around me And cannot express my delight. A black leather gloved hand fell onto Tempest's shoulder and she looked up to see Eddy Fate, smiling. "'ello little sis. Sorry I'm late." Tempest shook her head and smiled as she said, "It's ok. I'm glad ya came." He sat down and ordered an ale disregarding the cold outside and smiled nervously as he said, "Yeah, it's been tough to get together." Tempest shifted in her seat and watched him fidget in almost the same manner. They had yet to really sit down and talk about things and the realization that they were family had thrown them both far out of kilter mentally. Tempest took a deep breath and said, "Eddy... why didn't you come to Hong Kong? Papa said he invited you as well." Eddy looked at her, restraining a flash of anger as he said evenly, "Tempest, the last time I saw Papa was when I was very small. I understand that he may have been good to you, but outside of that fleeting childhood memory, all I know of him is that he's the most dangerous man in Hong Kong. I've seen his handiwork, what he's capable of. I'm sorry, but I can't just trust him as easily as you apparently can, not with a wife who's innocent of all this." Tempest nodded silently, studying the speckles of chocolate on top of her cup. She looked up and said slowly, "Papa was all the family I had for years. I know it's hard to believe, but he was good to me. And even now, he looks out for me. What he asked us there to talk about was where we came from." She reached down into her bag and retrieved a large manila envelope. She slid it across the table and said, "What he wanted was to tell us about our mother. Her name was Sun Chen, just as Ric said. And she was a pit fighter, just like I became." She looked at Eddy with a smile growing on her face and said, "You know what that means Eddy? It means I have a name. I'm not sure if it's the right one, the one I was born with, but I finally have one. And I don't look down her like people did me. I can't." Never have I fallen, But I am quickly on my way, You hold a heart in your hands, That was never before given away. Eddy looked at the envelope with a mixture of curiosity and trepidation. He held it out in front of him and said, "Yeah... Schreck never quite suited me either, and somehow I knew I didn't belong with those people. They were ok and everything, but..." he shrugged, "I wasn't supposed to be there. They knew it and I knew it." For a few moments they were quiet until Eddy let out a sigh. He looked at Tempest and said, "Listen, I want you to do something for me. I want you to kick Monte Burns' conniving little arse." Tempest chuckled and said, "Well, I plan to. But I feel kinda bad cuz..." "Don't," Eddy said sharply. He looked for a moment off to the side and then leaned forward towards her. "Tempest luv... one thing's real obvious by now is that I have to give my full attention to Ric Chronos. He's not going to go away and leave either of us alone until I do. He's a plague on our lives and one that I have to stamp out." Tempest nodded and said, "You don't have to do it alone at least. I'll help." Eddy smiled and said, "I know you will. But I want you to have the shot at the Cruiserweight strap and I want you to win it. After all that's happened, after the way I won it from you..." his smile faded and he shook his head distractedly, "No, it's your turn to regain what Ric stole from you, using me, maybe even using Burns. I want you to have it, and hopefully it can help in some small way to make for... everything." Tempest smiled softly and reached out, covering her brother's hand with hers and said, "Eddy, it's ok. I... we both have things to make up for. But we will. One thing that Papa said, and I know is true is this: 'If you don't forgive each other, and learn to be each other's family, then your enemy wins.' And it's true. Nothing would please Ric more than to have ruined something we both wanted and missed so much; having a family." Eddy nodded an squeezed her hand. "I know," he said, "That much I know for sure. And I know I can be a good big brother." He smiled at that and Tempest grinned. Then he added, "And who knows, you may have more friends than you know what to do with already." He chuckled as Tempest rolled her eyes and said, "Oh stop it. I know it's so weird. I just hope... you know that it isn't a trick or something. I'll feel so silly." She pouted comically and Fate smiled and shook his finger at her in mock warning as he said, "You behave now. That boy tries anything and he'll have your big brother to answer too!" They both laughed and sat back, enjoying the company, and for the first time in a long time, looking forward to the future. I have taken the pleasure to hide away, Hoping my love you would be; But Saturday is finally the day, Where my face you shall see.
