Writer's Block

16 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Love is a flame that can't be tamed And though we are its willing prey, my darling We are not the ones to blame "Nobody thought I'd be back," said Ivy, as she stared into her laptop, "but here I am. And they've given me the power of a GOD." Delete, delete, delete. Fucking delete. Ivy stood up from the hotel room table and cracked her knuckles, her elbows, and her knees. She had been trying to write another Ivy League column since she got in about six hours ago. Twitch hadn't told her, but he'd asked her to try and get another column up on the fWo website before RUAHH~! aired. The last column had gotten a huge response from the online community, he'd said, that a follow-up should do just as well. The last one was hacked into the system, Ivy had said to herself, so there was a lot of unsolicited interest. Another would simply be business as usual. Which is fine, she's done well with what she does, but the words have not been flowing as per usual. Trust is a word all lovers know The glorious art of staining souls, my darling We are not the ones to blame The more we have the more we want And the more it hurts our hearts, my baby It always ends up in tears Ivy's thoughts refused to remain in the PsychoBitch mold. They kept floating back to the Bronx, wondering what the kids were up to. They kept floating back to a bus somewhere on an American road, wondering what Eli and Angel were up to. They kept floating back to Orlando, where Sean Stevens had returned to after losing his Internet Championship to 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs, for an open- ended break. Which, despite her agreement to come back to work, is where she wants to be. An open ended break in Orlando with Sean. But she has no right to see him, and after everything that went down with that Carla girl and SVJ... she doesn't know that she really does want to after all. So keep on pretending Our heaven is worth the waiting Keep on pretending it's alright So keep on pretending It will be the end of our craving Keep on pretending It's alright "Column, column," she muttered to herself, as she pulled her hair out of its ponytail and peeled her pullover sweater over her head. She stood in front of the mirror and pulled her shirt up to just above her ribs, looking with disgust at the scar on her side. The doctors did her up right, it was barely visible, but she could tell that there was once a bullet penetrated her skin, and beneath it, there was once a kidney that was destroyed by that same bullet. A rounded, faded scar on the side of her neck was another reminder. Everything changed that day. Is that where her career 'jumped the shark'? "Screw this," continued Ivy, "there's no column this week." When doubts arise the game begins The one we will never win, my baby It always ends up in tears... Ivy sacked out on the hotel room bed and started going through her bag. There must be a book or something of the sort that she can lose herself in, forgetting who she is and where she is for the moment. She's in Tokyo. Just under two years ago, Valerian's Garden got their big break in Tokyo. The incident that put Ivy squarely in the middle of her best friend and the girl that would become his wife, the mother of his daughter. That was before she and Sean got together. History. It's behind her, however. It's over with. So keep on pretending Our heaven is worth the waiting Keep on pretending it's alright So keep on pretending It will be the end of our craving Keep on pretending It's alright Yeah, right. She rummaged through her duffel bag and pulled out a pair of warm fuzzy socks - these hotels were always ridiculously drafty. Her photo album fell out behind the socks, almost unnoticed. Except for the fact that it opened up on a picture taken just before Cyberslam VI, with Team EQ. She smiled as she looked at the image - what Lance Knight always called a 'mock-publicity' photo. They were outside, enjoying a rare night without rain in the United Kingdom. Ivy had both Tag Team title belts, one over each shoulder, and she was dressed in her working clothes. Her head was leaned against Trip's shoulder, and Lance Knight stood right next to her on the other side, a look of complete confidence etched on his face. That was the best time Ivy has ever had in her entire career. Eli Flair was a different story. Eli was her family, her responsibility. Up until the night he leaned, crouched, overlooking the water from the top of the Brooklyn Bridge, she was too busy keeping an eye on him to enjoy the business. The run with Team EQ was *fun* - it was exactly what the business was supposed to be. Ivy pulled the picture out of the book and stared deeply into it. She barely noticed the tear that rolled from her eye, until it landed on the photo. You can't go home again. So keep on pretending Our heaven is worth the waiting Keep on pretending it's alright So keep on pretending It will be the end of our craving Keep on pretending It's alright *KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK* "Ivy?" She nearly jumped out of her skin when the knock came. "Just a second," she called, wiping her eyes and putting the photos away. She gave herself a once-over in the mirror, pulled her hair back, and put her glasses back on. As far as she could tell, nobody would know she was about to cry. A quick look through the peephole, and Ivy pulled the door open. "Alex," she said, staring into the face of the former United States Champion. Lance Knight smiled at her. "A few of us are going down to the bar for a drink, and I thought I'd see if you wanted to come." She shook her head. "I don't know... I'm not really in the mood to see anyone tonight." As Ivy turned around, Lance followed her into the room. "You seemed alright before we left the States... since then you've been a ghost. What's it gonna take to snap you out of it?" She stopped walking. "C'mon, kiddo," he continued, using her word, "we used to be friends. Remember, friends? Let me buy you a drink, you can lay it all on me." "I don't know--" "I do," he interrupted, "How many times did you buy me a drink and give me advice on whatever was on my mind? How many times were you there for me? The least I could do is return the favor." Love is a flame that can't be tamed And though we are its willing prey, my darling... Something in his tone seemed to reach her. "One drink," she finally said, "and please... no questions until I'm done." "Deal." ...we are not the ones to blame... One step at a time. *Lyrics from "Pretending" by HIM
