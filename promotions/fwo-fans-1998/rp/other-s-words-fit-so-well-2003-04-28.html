"I'm starting to fashion an idea in my head where I would impress you with every single word... I... said would come out insightful or brave, or smooth, or charming... and you'd want to call me." - Dashboard Confessional. "Spend your whole life sweating in an endless fevor. And laying in a bathtub full of freezing water, wishing you were a ghost. But once you knew a girl and you named her 'Lover'. Danced with her in kitchens through the greenest summer. But autumn came, she disappeared, you can't remember where she said she was going to. "But you know that she's good 'cuz she left you a song that you don't want to sing." - Bright Eyes. "I hurt myself today to see if I still feel. I focus on the pain; the only thing that's real. The needle tears a hole. The old familiar sting. I try to kill it all away, but I remember everything. "What have I become, my sweetest friend? Everyone I know goes away in the end. You could have it all. My empire of dirt. I will let you down. I will make you hurt." - Nine Inch Nails "Why can't I feel anything for anyone other than you?" - Taking Back Sunday "Hands down, this is the best day I can ever remember. I'll always remember the sound of the stereo, the dim of the soft lights, the scent of your hair that you twirled in your fingers. And the time on the clock when we realized it was so late and this walk that we shared together. The streets were wet, the gate was locked, so I jumped it and let you in. And you stood at your door with your hands on my waist and you kissed me like you meant it. And I knew that you meant it." - Dashboard Confessional She will hurt me, just like Diana did. "So if I just exist for the next ten minutes of this drive that would be fine. And all the trees that line this curb would be rejoicing and alive. Soon all the joy that pours from everything makes fountains of your eyes because you finally understand the movement of a hand waving good-bye." - Bright Eyes. "I think I miss you most on Wednesdays... and Saturdays." - Dashboard Confessional.
