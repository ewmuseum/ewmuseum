The Story Of His Life.4.B

5 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
December 29th 1:00 a.m. *click.... click.... click....* "Never anything decent on." Eli Flair crossed his legs gently so as to not disturb the sleeping Angel, her head rested on his lap. He was clad in a pair of jeans cutoffs, her, in an oversized Type-O-Negative T-shirt and sweatpant shorts, though wrapped up in a blanket as she breathed silently. *CRASH* "What was that?" "Thought you were asleep." "I was," replied Angel, "What was that, though? Sounded like a window or something." She sat up a bit so Eli could get up. He put his ear to the wall and listened hard. "I doubt it's one of ours," he said, finally, "This company doesn't really have many party animals on the roster, but the newbies... don't know too much about 'em yet." As he continued to listen, Angel put her own ear to the wall. They could hear loud thumps, too well spaced to be random. Listening harder... ...Someone was laughing... "Who's in there?" "Thompson, I think," replied Eli, "Didn't strike me as one of the room rioters... that's surprising." "It's pretty hard to sleep with that racket," said Angel, giving him a wry smile. Eli, anticipating such a smile, had already pulled on his shirt - a faded white wifebeater. "Yes, dear," he replied. Eli headed out into the hall, his boots unlaced on his feet. Knocking on Thompson's door, he could hear someone coughing and wheezing inside of the room, perhaps an old man trying to clear his throat... or someone choking. Then suddenly the coughing and hacking stopped, and the door swung open revealing a man about 6'1", 125 lbs soaking wet. The man was creepily skinny all of his ribs were visible to the point you could count them. "What's going... who the hell are you?" asked Eli, looking down at the much smaller man. Eli then glanced to the room, the word "DIE!" was scribed across the roof in a blood red paint. In the matress a knife stood planted into the center of what looked like a large X carved into the matress. Everything in the room was smashed, the T.V. lay leaning against the bathroom door, shattered into peices. Everything in the room was stabbed and cut up. Eli looked down at the man, quickly lunging for him. The man slid side ways as Eli Flair went crashing into the room. Eli quickly hopped to his feet in persuit of the man. The man booked it right for the fire escape stairs. Eli was right on his heels in shoving the door open and beginning to run down the steps. The small man was running like the devil was behind him, and in fact, Eli Flair could of dished out a beating at that moment that would of looked like it came straight from hell. The two ran down 15 flights of stairs until the man pushed the door open. Eli followed him through the hotel loby shouting. "Yo, stop him! Grab 'im, grab that little shit!" Finaly the man made the door, Eli quickly went bursting through it, looking back and forth, he was confused, which way had the man gone? The streets were a bit crowded in Newark and the man was quickly lost. Eli let out a sigh before walking back inside the hotel. "Fuck!" shouted Eli, frustrated at losing him. He walked to the stairs, punching the sheet metal door leaving a huge dent in it before he began the long walk back to the room.
