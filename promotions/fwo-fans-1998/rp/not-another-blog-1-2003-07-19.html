Not Another Blog (1)

19 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
5:30 a.m. EST, Saturday, July 19th, 2003 If you somehow stumbled upon this page (probably linked from some damn smark messageboard. Damn smarks), yes, this is a blog, and yes, this is really Voran Xias. I just got this wireless internet thing hooked up to my laptop, and I figured a good way to pass the time with all the traveling I do would be one of these damn blog things that seem to be so popular. I've never seen another fWo wrestler use them, so hey, at least I can say I have originality on my side. Yay! I'm not actually traveling right now, though- right now I'm at my home in San Francisco. Yes, the same town I was born in. Yes, I know that's incredibly boring. Those of you reading this who are fWo fans (probably everyone not related to me that's reading this damn thing) and don't already know are probably all "You mean his hometown listed on the fWo bio page is a lie? GASP! SHOCK!". There's actually a pretty easy explanation there- back in the IOW (I always feel like I'm going to get tomatoes thrown at me every time I type those three letters together), Winters (Jason Winters, the owner at the time, for those who don't know) suggested that I go with the New York hometown to go with the tough guy anti-hero character I was doing at the time. After all, San Francisco is more well-known for having a hell of a lot of gay people than a hometown that strikes fear into the hearts of opponents, and I was too lazy to change it. Even though I don't have ANYTHING resembling a New York accent. Yeah. Let's move on! For those aforementioned smarks who came to hear me shovel fWo dirt in blog form, you'll have to bear with me for a second, because this is pop culture time! Those of you reading this probably already know my fondness for Gundam- and for those who don't, or even worse, don't even know what Gundam is (shame on you!), I'd suggest going to http://web.archive.org/web/20010708125437/www.gundamproject.com (I'll link it at the bottom for the lazy ones). It's a Web Archive link, yes, but the site's been down for a while and it's still the best explanation of anything Gundam (besides the latest one, of course). Anyway....so I come home and see that the 40th episode of Gundam SEED is finally uploaded. For the Gundam fans here who haven't seen SEED yet, I'd rate it somewhere in-between X and Zeta, personally. For those of you out there who are also following this show- am I the only one who thought the death of Kaguri's parents was incredibly cheap? I dunno, it just felt like drama for the sake of drama (why the hell couldn't they fit on the ArchAngel? It looked big enough to me), but if it gives Kagari and Kira some much-needed character development in the upcoming episodes, I'm all for it. Meanwhile, Asuran has easily become my favorite character on this show, and Deakka has gone from annoying sidekick to an even more annoying villain (Issac) to a well-developed character that I honestly can't tell what will happen next. But those three new druggie EA pilots are just horribly annoying. Okay, I'll stop with the Gundam talk so I don't lose the 99.9% of my audience who has no idea what Gundam is, and get back to the fWo. Monday I head down to San Jose for Ruahh~!!. Honestly, I was frustrated with my performance the past few weeks, but even worse, I've been constantly annoyed by some unauthorized comments a certain red-headed Hardcore Champion has been making lately. Back on-topic, though, I heard I'm going to get in there with another Champion for a non-title champion vs. champion match, and I'm looking forward to find out who. By process of elimination- I doubt it will be Aimz since we just wrestled, Stevens just won his title and they're probably going to save US Champion vs. Internet Champion for an Unleashed, so that's probably out, and I honestly doubt it will be against one of the Tag Champions, who are doing their own thing. That leaves either Creed, the World Heavyweight Champion, who rarely wrestles on weekly shows anymore, let alone non-title, and Bowman, the World Cruiserweight Champion (and, as I'll be quick to point out, a man I've beaten in the past). I honestly don't know which one it is, but I'd put my money on Bowman if it was a betting man. I'd prefer him as well- we had a good TV match last time, Bowman's a great cruiserweight, and I wouldn't mind getting in the ring with him. I just hope I can squeeze some interview time on there, because that aforementioned redhead is in for some cheap, unauthorized shots, if you get my drift. ;) So that's it from me for now, I guess. If management isn't on my ass about this sort of thing I can keep it up- we'll see. I enjoyed writing it and giving the fans another perspective from me. I still don't like any of you, but I'm sure the non-Gundam crossover fans probably don't like me either, so we're even. ;) Later. -Voran
