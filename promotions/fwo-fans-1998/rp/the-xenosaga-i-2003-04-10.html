The Xenosaga: I

10 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This wasn't what Allen Speck had signed on for, believe me. When he signed the dotted line to join the fans Wrestling organization, he had no intention on making Elmer Fudd impersonations and stalk people with a large, triangle-shaped, cheaply-painted, wooden object. Nor did he EVER... EVER, even on the graces and glories of Baby Jesus, had ANY intentions on being squirted down with chocolate syrup, feathered, and "Tallynapped" by "Crocodile" Davy Dingleberry and James Cagle, the Tallywhackers. And so, we were here. In an undisclosed, empty location, the Codemaster(Allen Speck) had found himself tied to a chair via his upper arms, while his hands remained somewhat free. Both Tallywhackers circled around the Codemaster, evil intentions on each of their minds. They had to abandon reason and intelligence to get the intentions there, but dammit, they were there. "So, you want to be tha Codemaster, eh?" Dingleberry said, with a tone of considerable interrogation, "Alroyt, then... where were YOU on tha noyt of Deecember Foyve, Noynteen Noynty Noyn?" The Codemaster said some gibberish. "Oy! Oy fo'got." With that, Davy Dingleberry reached for the Codemaster's face, and took off one of those red bondage balls that had been stuffed in his mouth. They didn't know why they put it in his mouth, but it seemed like a good effect... at the time. The Codemaster spat out in a direction that wasn't occupied by Tallywhackers, though upon further recollection, he probably should've actually spat at Dingleberry. Remain in-character, Allen... just remain in-character... "Hey! Did you even WASH that thing!?" the Codemaster shouted. "Aww sheeyit! Just answer the dag gum question you dumb sheeyit-assed cheeyipmunk!" Cagle shot back, brandishing what could only be described as a set of deer antlers, which he seemed to be wielding threateningly. "Why should I? I don't even know why you even ASKED that question." the Codemaster said. Dingleberry smirked at this, "Oy was hopin' you'd say that, mate." With that, Dingleberry walked a couple of feet away from the Codemaster before he came to something large that happened to be covered in a large tarp. For some reason, the tarp was pink and had a koala design on it. Codemaster frowned when he saw it, and frowned even more when Dingleberry tore the tarp off, revealing a large television set, a VCR, and various video gaming systems, most notably including the Atari Jaguar. Codemaster almost screamed when he saw the Jaguar, and he was about ready to crack right there. "HOLY JESUS! You're not intendin' on usin' that on me, are you?!" the Codemaster pleaded. Dingleberry smiled, picked up the Jaguar's controller, jammed it into the Codemaster's hands, and turned on the machine. "Oy hope you loyk it, oy DID pay t'ree-fitty fo' it all." "You have no idea how badly you were ripped off." the Codemaster shot back, but this just caused Dingleberry to walked over to the nefarious video game console of Satanic power and turn the soul-stealing, corrupting device on. The Codemaster immediately shrieked in horror at "Club Drive", a game for the Jaguard, and dropped the controller. Dingleberry, frustreated at this action, forcefully pushed the controller back into his hands. When the Codemaster dropped it a second time, Dingleberry groaned and turned to Cagle. "Oy! Cagle, be a good mate and fetch me a roll'a duct tape and a sea breeze, this'll take a lil'while."
