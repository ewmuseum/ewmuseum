Final Words

22 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The San Diego Sports Arena is empty, except for a few crew members milling about around the arena. The ring is set up, but right now everyone is away on break. One person, however, stands in the center of the ring. His long blonde hair is tied back. His suit and tie is nothing like the attire he will be wearing tonight. His eyes are hidden by dark sunglasses. "The Golden Child" Monte Burns is ready. "Donaven Winters." "Monte Burns." "Tonight is the night we will settle it all. One final battle between two of the fWo's most promising stars. Two men who hate each other's very existence." Burns walks over to one side, grabbing the top rope and pulling on it to check the resistance. He looks out at all the empty seats and smiles. "Thousands of fans will be here, and millions will be watching on pay-per-view. They will watch the two of us collide in our greatest and most brutal match to date. You see, they call this event 'Twilight' for a reason. This is the end of the year, the time when the calendar is ready to change. Everything that is 2001 will be over, and 2002 will arrive with a bang. And, with respect to the fWo, old battles end and new battles arise." Monte looks up and faces the camera with a straight face, the usual cocky grin absent. "For you and I, Donaven, everything will end as well. Since our first meeting back in September, you and I have been seeking to destroy one another on numerous occassions. We have battled for the fWo Cruiserweight Championship. We've battled for our respective teams at Unstable. We battled to see who would advance in the Wrestler of the Year Tournament. And now, at Twilight, we battle to see which of us will finally come out on top." Burns walks over to the opposite side, near where the announce tables are set up. He looks down at the unforgiving floor below and back up to the camera. "For months, Donaven Winters and Monte Burns have been inseparable. When one is mentioned, the other's name follows shortly. Everyone has been talking about us as the future of the fWo, the future of this business. But, tonight that could change. After this match, one of us may not have a future, Donaven. One of us could see our career come to an end. Will that be you? Will that be me? I don't know. But there is one thing I do know..." Monte pauses and looks straight ahead. "I will be the Last Man Standing."
