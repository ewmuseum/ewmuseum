Debts

11 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Huang Chin grinned sinfully, a smile that would have frightened even a shark. Huang: "You do realize that, as we prepare for the New Year, it is the time to pay off old debts, while we begin the road into the future?" A figure in the shadows murmered a thin reply to the affirmative. Huang: "And you realize that you owe me your life, yes?" Again the figure nodded their assent. Huang: "Good, good. Then, to pay your debt to me, you must allow me to pay back someone else. And you will be the instrument of that payback. You understand your orders, yes?" The silence was unbroken, as the figure acceded to Huang's statements. Huang: "Good. Now go. You will do what must be done, and it will be done before the New Year. Once you have done so, you may consider yourself forever released from your debt. In fact, if you do well, I may have an even better opportunity waiting for you in China. You would be able to go home for the first time in years, and for no price greater than serving me well." Huang watched the phantom leave, taking the spectre of ancestral honor with him. Huang had played them all, little did they know. He made certain that Ric Chronos received just enough information to cause a great deal of pain to Eddy and Tempest - who belonged to his most hated rival Papa Liu. By not acting against either of them directly, he did nothing to encourage Liu to retaliate - in fact, he felt confident that Liu was unaware of Huang's involvement in any way. And now, the final score would be settled, and then he could make Papa tremble. Vengeance would be his. They owed him for the dishonor they had brought his father, Chin Wu. And Huang Chin - or Chin Huang, in Chinese - laughed. They would pay. Oh, how they would pay.
