Etch-a-Sketch Never Looked So Good

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Flyer sat in his locker room, playing with an etch-a-sketch. It seemed at if he'd been there for hours. "Con Sarnit! I can't quite get this curve right." Flyer yelled, looking at the victorian house with enourmous amounts of detail imprinted. The shutters looked as if they were open slightly, the flowers looked as if they were vibrant without a hint of color. It could be mistaken for a grey painting if not seen inside the confines of an etch-a-sketch. Flyer sighed, as he heard a knock on the door. "Come in." Flyer didn't look up from his etch-a-sketch. "This is a wrestling locker room. I mean, seriously, who knocks anymore?" "OH! RED PLAY THING!" It was Flyer's assistant, Eddie Scott Poser. He was wearing a Burger King crown for some reason that had the word Burger crossed out and 'Poland' written on top instead. If you want to get the backstory on that one, feel free to hit yourself with a mallet a few times until you don't want to know anymore. Poser walked over to Flyer's side, and looked over his shoulder. "Ohhh! Pretty! That looks realistic!" Poser narrowed his eyes for a moment. "I asked for cartoonish! CARTOONISH!" "You didn't ask for anything!" Flyer continued fiddeling with the knobs. "You came in here a few minutes ago with your hot coffee and threw it at me. Then you yelled at me for getting you hot coffee and said you were gonna sue me because it worked for some redneck." "My lawyers advised me not to talk about the case." Flyer sighed. "You're my assistant, so the coffee making is your job. And just for future reference, coffee, is supposed to be hot." "Then how did that lady get so much green paper from her dealie-o?" Flyer stopped him for a second. "Dealie-O? Have you been watching Don't drink your juice in the hood again?" "No, I've been watching Don't Drink your Juice in the Hood that Tanya Harding..." Poser trailed off, not remembering the full title. "Fine, I was watching that movie again." "What did I tell you about that?" Flyer said, shaking his head as he continued playing with the knobs. "Bad Poser. Bad!" Flyer stopped for a bit. "Heh, this etch-a-sketch kinda reminds me of Kate's breasts." "It does?!?" "Hey Eddie. This thing is pissing me off." Flyer said, looking up for the first time. "Wanna see what Etch-a-Sketch tastes like?" "DO I!" Flyer took that as a yes, and threw the etch a sketch up in the air. It shattered down on the floor into pieces, as Poser bent down to take a look at the carnage. Flyer reached over, grabbed his gym bag, and quietly walked away. As Flyer left the locker room, Eddie Scott Poser picked up the knobs that Flyer was turning, and simply narrowed his eyes. "This doesn't feel like Kate's breasts." he said, before he dropped the knobs and chased after his client.
