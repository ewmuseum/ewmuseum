The Music - One

8 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ric Tatum wasn't the first combatant from the Tatum legacy but without a doubt, he was the most promising. His life was based around fighting: boxing, wrestling and if he knew how to do anything correctly, this was it. "You better, lose yourself in the music, the moment You own it, you better never let it go You only get one shot, do not miss your chance to blow This opportunity comes once in a lifetime" The weather wasn't welcoming at all, it was 6:24 in the morning and the wind blew a melody to knock you off your feet, literally. It was a new year, a new day, January 8th, 2003. Drops of water crashed to the ground at unequal intervales and the sky looked like it was about to reign all hell on earth. A large male silhouette floated past the area disrupting the ugly times mother nature brought upon us. His head bobbed up and down with the steps of his jogging pace. The man was dressed in a faded old light-gray sweatsuit, used and worn many times before. It was soaking slowly as the rain fell on it's cotton fabric, absorbing all water that contacted with the sweatsuit, giving the light colored clothes a darker shade on the shoulders and chest area. He went on for what it seemed like forever, not stopping for anything in this world. Taking a right at the corner of a street his face was exposed to the vision of the world. It was Ric Tatum. "Back to the lab again, yo this whole rap shit He better go capture this moment and hope it don't pass him" The bass and drums pumped shockwaves into his mind, hypnotizing him and turning him into a non-stop machine. The lyrics soothed his conscience, planning a scheme with every word being said. Each step grew stronger and stronger, faster and faster. His feet were run-away engines as he gained speed. Nobody could confront him, this was his moment, his time, his destiny. "Soul's escaping, through this hole that is gaping This world is mine for the taking Make me king, as we move toward a, new world order A normal life is boring; but superstardom's close to post-mortem, it only grows harder Homie grows hotter, he blows it's all over" Another turn around a corner brought him a new vision infront of him, a new scene to work with, a new stage to rock on. His passion grew harder and more furious, the jogging was now a full-force run. Adlibbing almost everything he's done so far in his life, Ric knew he was now coming to a new chapter. One where he would have to plot and contemplate ideas left and right if he really wanted what he tells himself. The big time was here, and he was ready for it. "No more games, I'ma change what you call rage Tear this motherfuckin roof off like two dogs caged I was playin in the beginning, the mood all changed I've been chewed up and spit out and booed off stage But I kept rhymin and stepped right in the next cypher Best believe somebody's payin the pied piper" Throughout his entire existence, he's lived in the shadow of Paul and Jake Tatum, but no more. They were dead in the eyes of the public and he was just beggining his quest. His heart was pumping hard, practically out of his chest. It's time for a change in the world where he nests in. "Baby momma drama screamin on her too much for me to wanna stay in one spot, another day of monotony has gotten me to the point, I'm like a snail I've got to formulate a plot, or end up in jail or shot Success is my only motherfuckin option, failure's not" Ric spent 2 whole years of his life training, day after day, all the routines, all the blood, sweat and tears. But for what? For him to stay in the depths of the light? No. He was out for more, he wasn't going to cease just the tip of the iceberg. What was done, was done for a reason - no matter what anyone said. And it would continue to be done, until his dreams came true. "So here I go it's my shot, feet fail me not This may be the only opportunity that I got" He ran across the street and turned left, another block, another view, the chorus hit. It's time to step it up. "You better - lose yourself in the music, the moment You own it, you better never let it go (go) You only get one shot, do not miss your chance to blow This opportunity comes once in a lifetime You better - lose yourself in the music, the moment You own it, you better never let it go (go) You only get one shot, do not miss your chance to blow This opportunity comes once in a lifetime You better.." Time to lose yourself... Lyrics: "Loose Yourself" by Eminem
