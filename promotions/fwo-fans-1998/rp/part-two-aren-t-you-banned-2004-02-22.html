Part Two: Aren't you banned?

22 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"There's nothing for me there anymore," said Ivy, "I burned that bridge when I punched out the chick. And it was cool to see Miles organize a protest... was cool to see Miles organize anything after that debacle with the Seven Deadly Sins. But still... that company doesn't want me there. They went from a wrestling company that was about wrestling to a fuckin' half-tard with a cracked bicycle helmet on." She took off her glasses and stared straight into his eyes. "There's no place for someone like me in the fWo, with what it's become." "What's the fWo?" asked Silver Fox, "but the people involved in it?" "That's Teri Fucking Melton's clone nowadays," replied Ivy, "and Marketing-Boy. And Tinkerbell and Mickey and Minnie and Hilary. The boys're an afterthought." "Didn't Jon Crisp just win the World Title?" asked Fox, shaking his head, "Isn't Eli the United States Champion?" "Yeah, but--" "Isn't the Deacon the Internet Champion? Aren't the cruisers breaking even more boundaries involving what's expected in a match? Where's the problem?" Ivy sighed. "It's the fact that we couldn't have a simple tribute to Twitch and Sam, and the others... without Disney getting involved. It's the fact that I went from an important part of the day to day business to an assistant lackey. It's the fact that the moment I'm getting through to Ted and helping him outta his depression I'm banned from the building. It's the fact that Kristen and Geoff are still stuck there--" "So your problem is you." "What?" "Who are you?" "I'm confused." "It's a simple question," laughed Fox, "Who are you? You're Ivy McGinnis, right?" "Ummmm, yeah." "Ivy Fucking McGinnis, was the term, I believe... right up there with the Psycho Bitch. The woman who stared at Ender and didn't flinch. The woman who took... what was it? A broken bottle to the leg in front of two thousand people to sell the match? You let Shannon break your ankle, you let Ruben twist your knee before your surgery - I know it was agony for you." "Poison Ivy's dead," replied Ivy, "She died the moment the fWo became a Disney subsidiary... if not before." "The boys need you, Ivy." "They don't. They're not individuals anymore... Wilson playing Deacon showed me that." "They're tired, they're stressed, and they're scared, Ivy. What happened on New Year's'd do that to anyone. Did it to you, if I remember right." She looked away. "They need someone who's not afraid, Ivy. They need someone to stand up and say 'This is our home. This is our promotion." "Fed's gonna be gone by the end'a the year. Disney's gonna turn it into Dubya-See-Dub, and Vinnie'll buy it at a grossly discounted rate. You know it, I know it." "Only if you and the rest of them let it happen." "What can we do? It's fate, the fWo has fleeting moments of perfection, only to end up in the shitter again. You can't fight fate sometimes, Fox." Silver Fox shook his head as he folded Ivy's glasses in his hands and handed them to her. "Nobody can tell the future, Ivy... not even you. The only thing the fWo is fated to do is make its own fate." As Ivy took her glasses back from him, she wiped her eyes nonchalantly. "Our fate went up in smoke in the Garden, Fox. With you gone--" "--it's on your head to shape the fate of the fWo. You and Tallison. Where is he?" "Still there. Drinking himself into an early grave because of how we couldn't change the course of the promotion." "You gave him hope, y'know." Ivy shook her head. "Naaaah." "It's true," replied Fox, "When you were talking in the bar, when you were beating down Duff - that's when he felt like things would be back to the way they were. When he felt like the fWo was everything it was supposed to be." "I can't go back," said Ivy, looking down, "I've been banned." Fox cupped her chin and forced her eyes to meet his. "Since when's that stopped you?"
