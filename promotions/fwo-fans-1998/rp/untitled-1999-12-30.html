30 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Briana is sitting in a steel chair in the corner of a dressing room as she prepares for tonight's Countdown to Oblivion show. Her blond hair is in cornrows, and she is wearing a lime green vinyl halter top and matching pants. She has a focused look on her face, and fWo cameraman Geoff Green holds his camera steadily in front of her the entire time, mantaining a single angle. As she speaks, the Women's Champion has a touch of lunacy in her voice at different points.) (She lets out a deep breath before beginning.) Travis, how could you do this? You were the last that I would expect to sell out to the forces of evil. (Briana takes another deep breath before adjusting her top.) I should've known it all along. You let James Armstrong ruin the fWo on purpose. You hate the fWo and all that it stands for. You want the fWo to die! YOU WANT THE FWO TO DIE! Well guess what, Travis? It's not going to die, not so long as Briana O'Brien has breath left in her body! David Spencer? I'll kick his ass just like I did TWICE last year! (By this point, Briana has jumped out of her chair in excitement, but now she calms down, gets back into her chair and wipes her forehead.) I used to think that you were one of the most trustworthy people in my life, Travis, but now I feel as if I don't even know who you are. All I know is that if you were in this room right now, you'd be lying on the floor, breathing for the last time. (Briana puts her head in her lap, before picking it up again slowly.) You can play all the games you want to with Spencer and his crew, but, Travis, don't you dare play games with MY promotion, the FANS' promotion, the fWo! You said that you wanted this promotion to be for the fans, but by doing what you did last night, you stuck up a big middle finger to every fan that you had or will have, and you gave it to me too, as well as everybody in your company. I will get my revenge, Travis. I don't know how I'm gonna do it, but I will. You can bet your ass on it. But I know one thing that I won't be doing anytime soon. For as long as you're aligned with Spencer, Travis, there isn't a way in hell that I'm giving Poison a shot at my title. In fact, if I really want to, I don't have to defend my title for another 29 days. Who's gonna buy a pay per view without Briana on it, Travis? Not that you care, because you want the fWo to fail. (All of a sudden, Briana's eyes light up and both of her eyebrows raise. The lights go out, and loud screams can be heard. After a minute or so, the lights come back on and the camerawork has all of a sudden become shaky, as it focused in on Briana, lying on the floor, bleeding from her forehead, and the message next to her, seemingly written in her own blood, which read "YOU DID IT". The promo then went to snow.)
