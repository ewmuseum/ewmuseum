Desecrate Through Reverence: One Last Time

23 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
She scurried to hide the steak knife in the bedside drawer when the familliar steps came skulking near the door. The comforter would served to conceal the scrapes and tiny cuts. When she tightly covered her body with it, the inklings of blood were cold as they soaked in... ...And smeared. Fucking cheap hotel linen. Shit, Amy...looks like that hurts. Why can't you feel it? Becau--- Brandon slowly pushed into the room, a bit mind-numb from the handshaking and politicking that goes in to an fWo merchandise meeting. The release of his DVD being right around the corner, the staff has decided to do a bit of marketing blitz for it and the possible Saturday morning cartoon in the works. The big Irishman steeled himself from the expected onslaught of his beautiful wife, excited and energized like normal at his return. Instead he was greeted by darkness and...quiet. Very unusual, to say the least. Brandon stood still for a second, half expecting this to be a trap by the littler red head. The sneak attack never came, however, and Brandon finally shambled in to the room, the door closing slowly behind him. As his eyes adjusted to the room he could see a form, layered in covers and lying still on the mattress. Was she asleep? If she was, she must be sick. Not exactly wanting to bother her if she was, but consumed with curiosity as well, he kneeled on the edge of the bed softly before speaking. "Amy...you okay, dear?" With all that she needed to say, how could she have expected to actually get to sleep and avoid Brandon's return when she tried? She wasn't asleep, much to her hidden dismay... "Mm, fine." she mumbled, not convincingly sleepy enough, "Can't sleep...it sucks." Brandon flicked on the bedside lamp and looked down at her, scruffy hair and a face half-hidden by the cover of blankets. However, she wasn't covered enough for him not to notice the glassy look in her blue and brown eyes. She'd been crying, or was near to it for whatever reason. And with his bad humor still intact he figured he'd probe a bit, try and figure out what was wrong. After all, if she had wanted to talk she wouldn't have obviously been trying to pretend, or get to, sleep before he made it back. He stroked her matted hair from her forehead, "What's the matter dearheart?" The tone was soft, soothing to her, almost relaxing enough for her to want to open up. Almost. "I know you've missed me and all... but sheesh... you don't have to lay in bed and cry over me..." Brandon tried to smile. Amy bit her lip and avoided the urge to smack him for the bad humor. He'd detected the tears, though...it was no use avoiding the discussion now. "We're always gonna be honest with one another, right?" "I have been so far... it's kinda the way I was built, though. This doesn't have anything to do with Kinkade or anything does it? Is there some new rumor in the mill?" The curiosity was now replaced with anxiety. "Right now, he's the last person you're gonna be upset with." cringed Amy, "It doesn't have a thing to do with that." Brandon sighed with a very slight relief, but his wife continued... "Y'know the Survivors, right? Reno Davis...ever heard of him?" "Yeah." Brandon looked on quizically, "I remember hearing about him on those message boards and the like. He was in the first one too, right?" Amy thoughtlessly raised her arms up to chew nails nervously. "Well, I was with him for about a year at one point...with him..." "Okay..." Brandon was hesitant now. Exactly where this was leading, he was unsure. Trying to lighten the mood, however, the paragon broke out his classic sense of humor. "We'll add him to the list, but why are you mentioning this now?" "Wow...you prance in here with me like this." she gestured towards her tear-stained cheeks, "Manage to get me into some explaination, which is a practical fucking miracle...and, of any time, you choose this one to poke at how many guys I've slept with?" "Ah, heck Amy, it's just that you always sit around and let these things get out of control in your head. You consider a million possible bad endings, but never the one or two good ones that can come out of something. You freak out about the way I'm going to react to something and then make up your mind about what I'm going to say or do... and you've never been right." Brandon sat down next to her on the bed as she cowered away a bit. "We can argue about my bad sense of humor OR timing later if we must, but why don't we stay on subject? Why bring up Davis now?" Tears welled up again as Amy tip-toed around the subject, "I...well...so what would make you break up with me? What's the worst thing I could do or have done?" On edge now, Brandon's notoriously hard-to-see temper finally peeked out, "So where is this going? I'm really not in the mood to play games. If you have something to tell me, tell me. I can't imagine something you could do that would make me want to leave you, and I'm not going to sit here and play guessing games on what could." "This is going...well...probably to make you hate me." Amy sniffled, "I've never expected anything less than honesty from you...but.." She finally broke, flinging her arms into the air and sitting up, away from Brandon on the double bed that barely held the paragon alone. "Look at me. I'm fucking ridiculous. I've sat around all night chopping myself up, stupid enough to think pain would make me feel better about all of this. I'm crying, I'm broken, I'm FUCKED. I'm a hypocrite and a liar besides the fact. Is that someone you're going to keep loving?" Brandon stared at the gashes zigzagging their way across Amy's porcelain flesh. The thought that she would do such a thing sickened him in a way. He could feel the pit growing in his stomach, it almost made him physically sick. In the battles he had championed he had seen some of the lowest levels of the human psyche exposed to the world, in this brief moment he couldn't think of one time that was worse. It caused him to pause long enough for Amy to fling herself from the bed and in to a dizzy stumble that brought her crashing to the floor. Brandon responded how you know Brandon would respond. He leapt from his precarious perch and lunged to protect his wife from more harm. Too little too late as it was he scooped her in to his arms as tears stung his eyes. "Amy. God... Amy, I love you." He rocked her in his arms. "Why did you do this? Why? Please stop this and tell me... please?" The Irishman had finally broken down completely. His words escaping his lips between sobs, the salty taste of his emotions welling at the corners of his mouth. Amy struggled only momentarily, then tucked her chin across Brandon's broad shoulder. "Reno's back. He's...he brought things with him." "Baggage..." she sobbed, "Really important things. I thought you'd leave me." Brandon was about to speak out again, but Amy interrupted. "Don't say you won't. You haven't heard it yet..." It took a solid minute of just being held there but, nearly nauseous again, the little redhead finally let her heart get the best of her in honesty's form. "I've got a little girl." "A... what...?" Brandon couldn't believe he had heard those words slip from Amy's lips. A girl? A baby girl? From her time with Reno? Everything got the better of the so called paragon and he pushed his wife away, to arms length. "You have a...a... a child!? What in the HELL are you talking about?" The look of shock on "Brand Frontier's" face was indescribable. He himself started to feel dizzy, leaning back on one hand in his kneeling position to steady himself. Amy got a very sudden chill. This was exactly what she expected. "I'm talking about having a daughter. My own. From...well...I don't know who." The questions forming in Brandon's head came at a tornado's pace. Who's was it? How old is she? Where is she? Why now? Why didn't she say anything about her for all this time? All questions he couldn't ask. He was overcome with emotions in such a fast procession that the next few seconds would never be clear to either of them. Did he push her back? Why was she holding her arm in the air in defense? Did he threaten to hit her? HAD he hit her? It all seemed so surreal. Finally words formed and he spat them out like they were venom. "You cut yourself over this? You stoop to such things when all you have to do is talk to me? Where's the love in that, Amy? Where's the trust? At some point in all of this, you have to lose the baggage you packed yourself all those years ago and let us be us. If you don't then there's no point in you and I pretending, about anything. I... I can't believe you." Brandon's voice was raised, gutteral almost. "I don't..." Amy groaned through tears, "I'm not strong enough for this. Just tell me if you want me to go." Brandon turned his head away. There's your answer. [BACK to "Signed In Blood"] or [FAST FORWARD to "Pay Up"]
