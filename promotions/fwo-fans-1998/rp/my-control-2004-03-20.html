My Control

20 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I can't stand it. Being the nephew of a former wrestling great, having to live up to the expectations. It's so painful listening to storys of "Well your uncle was a great wrestler..." or hearing from the guys in the fWo Staff that "your uncle should have taught you better." Why don't they talk about my dad who was a failure in the wrestling business? Or talk about my grandpa who didn't give a shit what happened to my dad or my uncle, as long as they didn't bug him. Why don't they talk about my family life? Why don't they talk about how my uncle and my dad abused me? It doesn't matter, I'm Paul Jones' nephew. Whatever he's been able to do, I'm supposed to be able to do twice as much because I'm more physically gifted than he is not to mention I'm a lot better looking than him. Maybe I don't suck as much cock as he does to get where I want to get. Maybe I'm myself and not living miserably like he is. Do I even REALLY want to be here? He gave his life to me, but it was like I was being forced. I'm not going to do things the way that "Number One" Paul Jones wants things done. I'm going to do things my way. This means I'll live the life I want to, I'll wrestle when I want to, I'm going to pimp as many bitches as I want. Paul Jones tells me about being respectable and showing class. Fuck that, I tried respecting my elders and showing class my first month here. Where the hell did that get me? Hell, they haven't even booked me. Disney wants ratings, not some bullshit fake. Frankie Villani got a steel chair to the back of the head. That was class...classy swinging. What can I say? I used to play baseball. From now on, I'm doing things the way I want to. Fuck you, Paul Jones. You don't control me.
