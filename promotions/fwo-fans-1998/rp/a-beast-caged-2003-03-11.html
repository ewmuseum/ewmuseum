A Beast, Caged

11 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The beast was startled out of his sleep by loud screaming and pounding feet. A whooping alarm was blaring in the tiny space, and he smelled smoke. He ran over to the bars of his cell and tried to see what was happening, but all he could see was moving figures in the dim haze. A guard in a ratty tan uniform ran by coughing hoarsely, and the beast thought he'd caught a coppery scent of blood as the man passed. He gripped the bars and looked around the tiny Israeli jailhouse. He had lost track of time in here, but knew he had seen the moon through the barred window at least twice since he was caught and unceremoniously thrown into this cell. (smoke. fire. we must escape.) Panic was quickly building up inside him... he pulled at the bars a little bit more but the jail was built for keeping things like him in, and the bars did not budge. The beast barked out into the smoke-filled corridor, and was answered only by a far-away screaming. He was alone. He paced around the small cell as smoke swirled about the celing, and settled nervously into the straw nest he had built for himself. He remembered the night they took him... it was dark, the beast was prowling around the outskirts of Ashdod, looking for food. He remembered chasing something... someone. He remembered the trap. The men with their beating-sticks and ropes... chasing him down, tying him down. He recalled biting a hand hard enough to draw blood, and then a startled curse and a bright flash of pain. Then he woke up here, away from the forest and caged like a rat. Now, the smoke seemed to be lowering from the ceiling, as if the room was flooding in reverse, and the beast lowered his head into the straw nest as he mewled and whined to himself. He scratched into the hard stone floor with broken fingernails, but soon saw that there was no way out there. He felt his bladder let go. (must escape. must run. dig. flee. RUN. RUN. RUN.) There was a noise at the door, and the beast instantly cowered. Was it the men again? Even in the fire, were they opening the door to beat him again? The bruises on his back and legs had just begun to fade to a purplish hue on his dark black hide. No it wasn't the guard. It was someone else rattling the key in the door... someone familiar. "Jesus Christ, Mufanga... what has become of you?" John Rider asked. The beast backed further into the corner, away from the voice that was hoarse with smoke. Glowing embers floated in the air in the small cell. "No time for that now, Kagos," Rider, Mufanga Kagos' on again-off again manager said as the pushed the door open. "This whole fuckin' country is crazy! Someone firebombed the jail... I was able to sneak in when everyone started to panic... let's get you out of here." The beast galloped past him and away from the fire, through the open cell door and into the night.
