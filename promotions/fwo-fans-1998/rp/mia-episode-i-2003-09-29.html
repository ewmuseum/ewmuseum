MIA Episode I

29 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The call had come late in the night, right away when I picked up I knew there was a problem. A horrible problem that needed attention immediately. Someone had named their child Silver Fox – another fine example of alcohol ruining a man’s life. I shed tears for this man, I told him how sorry I was – only to be cut off and called… (SFX: Click!) MILES: “Miss – earmuffs.” LADY V/O: “Excuse me?” (SFX: Click!) Only to be called an asshole. LADY V/O: “Sir, I don’t appreciate hearing that…” As I listened more, I was surprised to find out the man was content with his name – even though several times I told him this could be changed through the courts. However, this wasn’t the man’s problem. You see, in the wrestling business sometimes when you don’t pay attention – things die on you. Apparently, in Silver Fox’s case…his tag division was like a fish out of water. Not only did he want the Miles Investigation Agency to find the heat that was lost, he was willing to pay extra money for the MIA to keep it burning after relocation. Of course, my immediate response was fWo? Never heard of it. Must be some bush league out of Canadia. Then he told me about his league, I started laughing and hung up the phone. I mean, who in their right mind would work for the Magic Kingdom? I’ve made millions, I’m not wrestling some guy in mouse ears. Must’ve been some fruit on the phone, I thought. Still, the contract offer had piqued my interests… LADY V/O: “Sir, I’m trying to work here, can you please turn that down?” So, I did my research. I was shocked. I was appalled. His name was Silver Fox, he hadn’t lied. LADY V/O: “Sir, this is a place of BUSINESS...and I don’t think Mr. Fox would appreciate this.” Obviously, if the man wasn’t lying about his name – his tag division must’ve really been in trouble. So, I made a few phone calls. I couldn’t believe my ears… A team named the Tallywhackers? If their fathers had kept to that hobby, I bet we’d all be better off. I knew wrestling was infested with Dingleberries, but c’mon – that just stunk. LADY V/O: “Sir, PLEASE – I’m under a very serious deadline!” Pretty Fly? The song sucked then, it still sucks now. Narly Nate? He’s so stoned he forgot the silent G for cryin’ out loud. Legion of Dairy? I’d been given the shits before, but this was ridiculous. A black man upset with White America – how original. I mean, what’s next? A Jew that hates Germans? Brotherly Love? Yes the division was retarded, no need to exploit it. WTF? Indeed…WHAT THE FUCK. Maybe that Crisp guy needs some Caution signs around the ring – walking is difficult, but don’t go learning from that Harper kid. LADY V/O: “SIR! If you can’t turn that thing off can you at least wait outside?” And then I heard the worst – a French man was holding ½ of the World Tag Team titles. A man from the country that’s best defense has been to lay down their weapons and cry for Mommy was at the top of the fWo Tag division. And he wore silver disco pants! As a man in Wonder Bread America, I couldn’t believe this. Not only that, I heard his partner wore yellow ring attire bought from a Siegfried and Roy collection. I mean not that there’s anything wrong with it… LADY V/O: “My brother is gay, I resent that!” “Forget Silver Fox’s name, he had a very serious problem. Relocate heat? That was the least of the MIA’s problem – apparently the fWo had completely lost its mind. Sometimes in life, the money doesn’t matter – sometimes, you just have to do the right thing. So, I picked up the phone and called Mr. Fox – I told him I’d be there tomorrow and that everything would be alright. The MIA would accept his offer… LADY V/O: “FINALLY…Sir, Mr. Fox will see you now.” (SFX: Click!) (FADEIN: ‘COCKY’ CRAIG MILES sitting on a chair with his barefeet kicked up on the coffee table in the fWo HQ visitors lounge. MILES is wearing a Hawaiian shirt unbuttoned, ripped denim jeans, yellow-shaded Oakleys and a 5 o’clock shadow. Dangling precariously from his lips is a Newport cigarette – he blows out a smoke ring and ashes it out on the table, leaving it there. Sitting precariously in his lap is a boombox. He hops up with a MILES-wide grin and walks past a Secretary giving him the evil eye…) SECRETARY: “Are you going to bring that thing in with you? I don’t think that’s a good idea.” MILES: “What thing?” SECRETARY: “That boombox that annoyed the hell out of me…” (SFX: Click!) Before I walked into the funny named man’s office, I remembered the warning given to me about the fWo. One of my friends had told me that people could read your mind, your thoughts, whatever you do from getting on your knees to give head or getting on your knees to ask some herpes-infested chick to marry you. Was it true? Could the fat-ass secretary that smelled worse than a nursing home hear my very thoughts? SECRETARY: “You’re a fucking asshole.” Ok, I'll admit I was freaked out. This place really had lost its mind. (SFX: Click!) (FADEOUT as MILES enters an office door with ‘S. FOX’ on it…)
