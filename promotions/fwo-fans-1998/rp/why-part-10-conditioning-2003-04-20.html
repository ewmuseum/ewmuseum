Why? Part 10: Conditioning

20 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wow. Now this is commitment. Borst's actually been training. He's not touched a drop of booze, not smoked in 25 minutes, is off the drugs and is actually training. He's already kicked the groin to pieces on several manikins from the local department store while he was there purchasing a set of weights. Sitting in his New York apartment, you can see just by looking at him that he's really taking this seriously. Staring avidly at the clock, timing his progress as he begins his conditioning. "26 minutes." Yep, he really is taking all this seriously. Two huge matches in such a short period of time, and he's really looking forward to both of them. Brand Frontier, for want of a better summary, has just irritated Borst for months. Really really really irritated him. He can't wait to have half an hour in the ring with Frontier to smack him around a bit and make him look stupid, while proving to the world that the bad guy really can win some times. And that the good guy can't beat the bad guy... and has never beaten the bad guy, and will for the umpteenth time be beaten and smacked about by the bad guy. But for half an hour in one go. Borst's only worry there is whether he'll be able to last 30 minutes without a cigarette. I mean, the guy ran into a crumbling building just to grab his fags. He loves smoking. As far as he's concerned, smoking is cool. It makes him look cool, feel cool, and smell bad so that he can repel queers. What more could you want from one little stick of cancer? But thirty minutes without a fag? Fuck that'll be hard. "28 minutes." Come on Borst, you can do it. Anyway. Following that, he's facing that guy Vickram. It's Vickram's fault that Borst is doing all this training lark. I could be sitting around doing fuck all right now, watching TV or something, but nooo, Borst has to suddenly start doing things, meaning I do too. Some may call Borst a hypocrite really, regarding the whole Vickram Ender ordeal. As far as Borst is concerned, Vickram has disgraced the Asylum and the Asylum Title. Borst worked hard to give that promotion and title credibility, and wham, Vickram the freak gets it and makes it a laughing stock. So naturally, Borst has to do something about it and punish him for all that. Now surely, you're all saying, that's just what Borst is doing to the fWQuo and the US Title. "Fuck off is it. 29 minutes." Of course it's not what Borst's doing. Vickram did it all unintentionally. He doesn't think he's doing anything wrong... he doesn't seem to realise what a tit he is. But Borst on the other hand, is making a conscious effort to be a pain in the arse and to cause trouble. And he's done a mighty fine job of it if you ask me. So dedicated to his cause, he even bought a mail order bride from the bride's father, just to make himself looking like a sleazy fuck. That, my friend, is impressive. The fact that the mail order bride and father are both stalking him now is irrelevant. They served their purpose at the time. You see. Borst is completely dedicated to everything he does. He gives 110% for everything he does. "31 minutes." Everything. Absolutely everything. I mean, you may again be confused about the effort thing when you think about this newfound desire to train. Well, he's never given 110% to training... he's never even given 1%. He's never bothered with all that training stuff before. But now he's started this training lark, he's giving it 110%. "10 seconds..." 110%. "33 minutes!" You see what I mean? 110%. The man needs to start conditioning himself, and the first step is to manage half an hour without a cigarette. The man just did 33 minutes. 110% people. 110%. Batman Frontier, Vickram Ender. Judging by the early signs, you're in trouble guys. And look at that smile on Borst's face. Sitting there surrounded by bottled water and his weights, with a well earned fag in his gob. It's great to be Borst right now. Nod. And he can't wait to see you.
