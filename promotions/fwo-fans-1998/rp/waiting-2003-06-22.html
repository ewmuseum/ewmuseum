Waiting

22 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Things were slow lately. Room service was slow. The bell-boy was slow. And the fWo, most of all, was slow. Thankfully, Lowell, with his stellar work ethic and ear plugs tucked in, was not. He was laid back on the large queen sized bed within his hotel room, a lap-top resting on his lap, pecking away at the keys. He was busy replying to one of the many threads that was hosted on his web-site's public forums. There were nine seperate boards contained in it... all as fantastic as the other. 'The Lowell Fan-Forum #1' 'The Lowell Fan-Forum #2' 'The Lowell Fan-Forum #3' And so on, and so forth- A very diverse choice in topics. Lowell pressed the 'Post' button and smiled in triumph- Boy did he ever tell that kid off. THREAD TRANSCRIPT Why Lowell is better that all organized religions... Posted By: Lowell the WEBMUSTAH' Simple enough, tell me why you think I'm better than a Sunday morning trip to the local church! Ps. Make it detailed~! Ps, again. Diagrams may help your reasoning. Why Lowell is better that all organized religions... Posted By: Lowell the WEBMUSTAH' Reply By: SnoopDogCHYLD32 i thnk u SUCK!!1 Why Lowell is better that all organized religions... Posted By: Lowell the WEBMUSTAH' Reply By: Lowell Dot Com Hey, STUPID... Guess what? BANNED! END TRANSCRIPT He sighed. "All in a day's work." Then clicked on Word Pad, ready and willing to finish the column he was currently in the middle of. This one dealt with a species called: Women. And while these two-breasted creatures may in fact hold the power of child birth, in his mind, they were still inferior. This was the excuse he often gave when explaining why he has had very few actual girlfriends. So yes, until Uprising this was true. No woman, no matter how intelligent or how good looking, was ever good enough for him. Then, in the blink of an eye, things changed. He landed atop Yalenchka with the Shooting Star Press, felt her warm, silky smooth skin as he covered her, and then stopped. How could he be expected to put the woman he loved through the depression she would sustain after being defeated? No, she deserved better. She deserved the best. She deserved Lowell Dot Com. But how does one, even as charming as the Czar of the Cashflow, woo a lady that has been through as much trauma as she has? Does he switch into the 'shy, sensitive guy' role? Or perhaps the 'take control' guy? Well, maybe, if these two personas existed inside him. He had but one way of going about a situation like this... Using his connections. Spending money. And not giving a damn if she rejected him once, twice, or however many times it would take before she slapped him with a restraining order. Not that that would happen, right? He's got BLUE hair. A nice car. A mini-cell phone that contains three hundred dial tones! And PLENTY of money to go around. In Lowell's mind, she wanted him. She wanted him to take her far away, off to a deep meadow, where he would then tear the clothes from her sensual body... And make sweet, SWEET love to her, all night long. Oh baby, he could hardly wait until Ruahh~!! Love sure was in the air.
