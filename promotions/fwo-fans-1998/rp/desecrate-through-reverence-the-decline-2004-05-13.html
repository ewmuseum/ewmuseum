Desecrate Through Reverence: The Decline

13 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Welcome back to the present.) Jesus Christ...not now. Not here. The shove racked her sore shoulders. She fumbled for the buttons. Now if that door would only close... Ding! The elevator door shut right behind Reno, his eyes fixated on hers avoiding them. It'd been years since they'd really seen each other and the silence was uneasy to break. Reno let out a sigh...or a chuckle - it was hard to tell. "So hey, how are ya?" he began and didn't wait for an answer, "Me? Oh, y'know. I'm fine...my life's been shit for four years, I had to give up Scerinn because I'm addicted to alcohol and couldn't keep a straight job. She's in some orphanage now learning to hate her deadbeat parents and wondering why she wasn't good enough to keep. I'd go give her an answer, but I'm a tad hazy on the whole subject as well, since you left me when I was crippled and a year later I had Renee on my doorstep with a kid. Oops! I guess the accident was all of a sudden mine, huh? I mean, why not give it to the guy whose life is down Shit-Central anyway, eh? You were too busy making it big in the old fWo and getting married to your muscle-head, huh? Why be with a Survivor loser when you can be with the winner, I guess. Was that what you thought?" Too much. No. Not this. Not at once. Amy desperately fought back tears. She hated not being able to fight him. Hated everything she'd swept under the rug. Hated that he was slapping her in the face with all of it. She hated feeling. Reno, on the other hand, loved that he struck such a chord. She might've been able to hide who she was, but she couldn't ever forget it. It was time to face herself. "But seriously, how've you been? You're probably still deeply depressed over nothing, right? Everything in the world is going great for you, but you can't handle it. Really...speak up, babes! I'm anxious to hear something other than all of that 'nothing' you had to say." Aimz balled her fist at her side and slammed it back against the wall of the elevator behind her in frustration. She couldn't touch this man. Her eyes shut so tightly, fighting back tears and veiling her obvious fear. "I...have nothing...to say." she breathed deeply, eyes still shut, "This isn't happening." "Well, Ames...I'm sorry, but it is happening. The little world you hid in your closet, the people you left behind, and it's all kicking you in the ass now. I'm back in Survivor and even if I lose again my name will still be in the spotlight once more. You're going to be seeing a lot more of Reno Davis, and so is your husband." "Ooooh, your husband!" continued Davis, laughing, "Does he even know?" Reno took another step towards Amy, but she was already against the wall. "Reno, please..." Amy's eyes finally opened, glassed over with tears that wouldn't fall as she stared at him. This was Amy Campbell, the little girl with so many lives she couldn't manage. She'd found one she could, and everything was going fine...but this skeleton had to jump out of the closet and drag another life she'd hidden from herself with it. "Please..." she continued with a shakey voice, "Just go. I can't do this, not now. Life is so right. I'm finally past.....everything. Everything that happened. I can't bring back any of it." With that, the redhead let her head drop and eyes close again. "God," her fist contacted the padded wall again. This time she wasn't addressing Reno directly, but speaking to herself. "Why this. Why you? Why now!" Reno clenched Amy's shoulder, spinning her around to face him. He looked fiercely into her eyes and then moved in closely to her, inches apart. Amy avoided his gaze, staring through him. Davis probed around her body, then quickly pulled back. "Just taking back what's mine," Reno said, shaking his flask for left over booze. He was pleased to get it back after she'd taken it during their first fWo encounter, when he was piss drunk and stumbling around the arena weeks ago. "Already drained the tap, eh?" Aimz bit her lip and finally looked him in the eye. "So we were just playing nice when you were drunk at the show, huh? You needed care, now you're sober and trying to fuck me up? Wow," she snarled, "You're fucking welcome." "Oh! Well then! Excuse me! Who cares what you did to me before right? I mean, HOT DAWG! Princess Amy took care of her old washed up drunk of a boyfriend! She made herself feel some fucking nice and holy! You think you're something else don't you? You've hurt a lot of people in your lifetime and now things are going to start biting you in the ass," Reno spat at the floor. "Excuse me? I'm sorry, but in all your self induced pity and drunken sob stories did you forget exactly what I went through?" Aimz' tears were running onto her blouse. Both looked at each other in shock, shame and disgust. Ding! The elevator doors opened, allowing a man in a business suit to enter the cramped space between Reno and Amy. He inspected Amy's clearly evident tears, then looked over at Reno, who was already shooting him a death glare. Reno, although small, was intimidating. The man turned his stare onto the quickly elevating floors. Finally, when the silence couldn't grow thicker, his floor came and he disappeared as soon as the doors opened wide enough. "Everybody went their separate ways," she said finally, "You had no right coming back. You have no right digging things up like this." "No right? No fucking right?! Who the hell do you think you are? Just because you can suck enough cocks to get into the fWo, my years of hard work are shit? I'm not welcome here anymore? This place might just be another pool of men you can jump on, but it's going to be the turning point in my life. I NEED this. Not you or anybody else will stop me, either." "What you need to do is crawl back into your hole and let me have the life I worked for. I mean, fucking look at you...you've lowered yourself to whore insults, clinging to a flask of rum and trying to knock others down so your life looks like less of a waste. You know damn well things don't just fall into my lap, but now that I've finally gotten places you're trying to tear it all down. You come to fWo, fine. Then you start talking about my fucking marriage like it's ANY of your business, that's not so fine..." Amy tried backing again, but Reno was barely an inch from her. "Then you bring up something I'm rather surprised a fucking deadbeat like you would even care about. You've got no place pushing her on me." Reno laughed in disbelief, "So, wait...let me get this straight: You have a child, dump her on me after not even telling me you were pregnant or even speaking to me in almost a year, then me being bitter about it is COMPLETELY FUCKING ABSURD?" Amy began to retaliate, but was cut off- "And it's whiskey, not Rum." "I just don't know what the fuck to do anymore, okay?" Amy snapped, "I'm not the same person I was. I'm stronger, but I can't fucking deal with this shit. You've brought it all back, you try telling me what the fuck to do! You win. You're harder than me. I'm ignoring the past because it's absolute shit. Whatever. I just need to know how I'm supposed to fucking balance what I have now with all the dumb shit then that you're smacking me in the face with. I won't live a lie, but I can't...I can't sort this out." Finally, she was sobbing. Safety wasn't an issue any longer as she crouched down to the floor and buried her head in her folded arms and knees. She almost wanted Davis to snap and put her in some kind of coma...at least it was a release from this. "Can you just kick me in the fucking face or something? I don't care." she sniffed, "Just end this shit." Ding! When she finally looked up it was through teary eyes that Amy watched the elevator doors close before Reno Davis... Her heart was on the wall. His head was shaking in disgust. [BACK to "Memory Lane"] or [FAST FORWARD to "Leech"]
