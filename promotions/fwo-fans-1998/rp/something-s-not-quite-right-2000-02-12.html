Something's not quite right

12 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Something's not quite right February 12, 2000 (The camera fades in on the Undergrounds locker room in the New Orleans Arena. Only Mike Bear and Wretch are present. They appear to be in a heated debate) Wretch: We almost lost these titles back on Wednesday, and it was your fault! Mike: What are you talking about Chris? Wretch: Dude, youre supposed to be this big Hardcore wrestler and you went down with one punch from Stone for Gods sakes! Mike: Man, she had knucks on! And just because I have a disorder that lets me enjoy pain doesnt mean Im impervious to it! We still have the titles, isnt that enough?! Wretch: I...I have no idea where my head is right now, I'll talk to you later. (Wretch storms out the door, leaving a confused Bear standing in the middle of the room. He turns to the camera) Mike: Well, I guess this isnt going to go like we planned. You still want to shoot with just me, Geoff? Geoff: Yeah sure, Mike. Whats gotten into Wretch? Mike: Man, I dunno, hes been acting a little strange lately, but Im sure its nothing. Geoff: Lets get started Mike, I know you have comments about Davis. Mike: Youd be correct, Geoff. (Mike puts on a more serious face) Well Davis, looks like you got some more fools to follow you. Hiroshima and Mitch Wilson. Mitch, Ill start with you. I heard you call me a tattooed freak, and all I can ask is: Is that honestly the best you could do? Mitch, youre just afraid to admit that a guy that looks like I do is every bit as good as you were. And if you dont believe me, its time for another history lesson. With my one reign I exceeded both your Hardcore title reigns. As for the Tag Titles, The Underground has never been pinned for those titles. Ever. In addition, while we were without the titles, we were never pinned in a tag match. So, our title reign actually stretches from Mob Rules to the present, well over three months. Huh, that seems to eclipse the reigns of you and the punk. Admit it Mitch. Youre bitter because you couldnt get over with your clean cut, just wrestle image and I have by being everything you are and everything youre not. Im an excellent wrestler, skilled in the mat and submission styles, and yet, I have something called charisma. Remember Mitch, its pronounced kah-riz-ma, not char-iz-ma. I know its a foreign word, but youll get it eventually, Blackm...I mean Mitch. Now Mitch, I will agree with you on some things, I have no idea why scrubs like Eron and Courage have titles, I think the fWo website should have up to date pictures of you, and yes, Primetime sucks. However, I still dont understand why you decided to align yourself with Austin Davis. I mean, youve were here for a long time, you surely cant tell me you dont know what kind of guy Austin Davis is. You couldnt possibly be that blind to not know about the things hes done. Mitch, you are a fool to align yourself with Davis, and itll come back to bite you in the ass eventually. Now, as for Hiroshima. Every time I hear you speak, its complain complain complain. Blah blah, the fWos keeping me down, blah blah, its because Im so old, blah blah blah. Let me ask you something Akira. Did you ever ask for any help? Did you? No. You just sat there and complained. Then, you go and join FTfWo because the fWo screwed you? No, wrong. You screwed your career, Hash. You decided to walk alone and wait until your push would come up and smack you in the face. Buddy, that doesnt happen. You think I got to where I am by complaining about the management? Akira, you are a bigger fool than Mitch Blackman, so fuck you. I sincerely hope that when Davis turns on you, its too late for you to go back, because you dont deserve to be pushed. When you come crawling back to the fWo, I will make it my business to spit on you and kick you out of the fWo for good. Geoff: Ummm, ok. What about Havok? Mike: Ha! I bet he thinks he seriously beat me again. No, Havok, this is just like the TCW Tag Titles. I let you win. I insult Collins a lot, but I knew I couldnt wrestle you AND him, so I saved my strength for the match that actually meant something. Havok, just because you won the match, doesnt mean you were the better man. Wretch and I will kick your and the cats ass come Trapped, and thats all there is to it. Oh, wait, almost forgot. Rain, I saw what happened on Warfare. Please dont tell me youve sided with that piece of crap Davis too. Ive fought you and I know what kind of wrestler you are. You dont need Davis and his cronies. Dont do it Rain, you dont need it. Trust ME. I should probably go find Chris, maybe hes calmed down. Later, Geoff. Geoff: Ok, later, Mike. (The camera fades to black as Mike goes to look for the other half of the Tag Team champions)
