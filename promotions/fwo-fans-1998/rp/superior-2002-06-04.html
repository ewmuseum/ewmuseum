Superior

4 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Fleet Center. Boston, Massachusetts. Backstage, Sean stared impatiently at the monitor as Mufanga Kagos put the finishing touches on Crucifix. His body... was chissled. He had been working out vigorously since he returned to the fWo from his small hiatus, and was finally starting to see the effects. He wasn't too much bigger... but, he was a little more ripped. His arms appeared bigger, thus making his 'bible & cross' tattoo look THAT much more awesome. Kagos' music was blaring. That was his cue to get to the gorilla position as fast as possible. Sean dropped down on his hands and knees and began to do pushups, so that his muscles would peak, then threw a bottle of water on his body... giving it a little shine, dumping the remains on his shoulder length golden locks, before removing the rubberband and letting them flow. Suddenly, his door opened. It was Ivy McGinnis. His girlfriend. His... ONLY... girlfriend. Ivy and Sean had been through a lot. The tests and trials of their relationship managed to do only one thing, however... make them love each other more. They weren't fans of drama... but, they both knew and understood that relationships are give and take. And, the average couple wouldn't have been standing after going through what they went through. But, there they were... standing. Happy as ever. Stevens - "What's up, Ivy?" Poison Ivy - "Nothing. Just coming by to wish you good luck out there. Have a good match." Stevens - "A good match, eh? Haven't had one of those since the last time we were together." Sean smirked. Poison Ivy - "What are you talking about? You and Lance had three- or- four GREAT matches..." Stevens - "... I know. Just wanted to hear YOU say it." Ivy jokingly punched Sean on the shoulder. One, because he was being arrogant, which meant he was totally in character at the moment. Two, because she just wanted to touch her boyfriend. Stevens - "Seriously, though... I feel really good tonight, Ivy. Plus, they tapped me to win it, so I'm gonna do my damndest to see to it that they don't regret making that decision." Poison Ivy - "I know you'll do good. Just give it your best, y'know? Can't ask for anything else. You and Eli have amazing chemistry... and, Lance is an exciting worker... with all of you guys motivated... I'm sure it'll be a hell of a match." Stevens - "Yeah... I agree. But, anyways... let me get to that curtain, before Universal has my head. I Love You, Ivy..." Poison Ivy - "I Love You, too. Go out there and put on a show." Sean smiled as he ran out of the dressing room, in search of the gorilla position. On his way... he wondered a couple of things that he MEANT to ask Ivy. Like... what EXACTLY happened the day before when they visited Superior Championship Wrestling. Oh well, Trip shrugged. As he walked up on the curtain, just as "Renegade" by Jay Z and Eminem began. Just in time, he thought. As he stepped through the curtain, for what would surely be... a hell of a match.
