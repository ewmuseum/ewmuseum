Just Dreaming ......

4 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"The future belongs to those who believe in the beauty of their dreams." -Eleanor Roosevelt He could barely open up his eyes as he woke up. He did not realize where he was but he could feel a sharp pain in his head, he tried moving his hands but they were tightened to the bed below him. He looked to his right then his left and quickly realized he was in a helicopter but why? "What," he hesitated, "What happened, where am I?" A medic leaned over with a flashlight and checked his pupils. "You're on your way to San Jose Hospital." He was in shock, he began to fight the restraints, "Sir you're going to have to calm down, and you were in a serious accident so we ask of you to not make any sudden movements." He jerked at the restraints again but gave up when he realized they would not become any looser than they already are. "Sir please, be still and stay calm." He closed his eyes for a second then remembered Tisha, "Tisha... Tisha..." the medic grabbed his wrist and checked his pulse. "Is she alright, is she alright?" He questioned the medic hoping for the best but the way it seemed, nothing good was coming out of this. "She's fine. She took the ambulance to the hospital." The medic sighed as he looked at Troy, "You know sir, we thought we lost you back there at the scene. Your heart stopped for about 15 seconds, you're very lucky." He still did not know what happened. "She's going to be fine." He was more worried about Tisha than he was himself. The medic nodded and held his hand, "Just stay calm sir, she's fine and you'll be fine as well." The medic loosened the restraints on his body as the helicopter got near the helipad at the hospital. The medic started to pack up his medical equipment as Troy tilted his head backwards on the hospital pillow. Troy closed his eyes, only seeing what he was dreaming about before. A smile painted itself across his face as the helicopter touched down with the helipad. "Dreaming saved me."
