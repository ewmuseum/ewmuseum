Awakening.

26 October 2002

Punishment inflicted in retaliation for an injury or offense. _____________________________________________________ Brandon Roche has lived by unwritten rules for most of his life, ones that he was taught at the side of one of the most honorable men that has walked this Earth. On two occasions, and in defense of his family Brandon has felt he stepped away from his honor. He took things beyond the normal standard and took extreme measures in doing so. He nearly took the life of two separate men all in the course of protecting his family. It made him sick. He didn't like who he became during that time. He didn't like the levels he let himself slip to, but it happened. Looking back though some would reason that he had all the justification in the world for doing the things he did. Looking back some would even cheer his decisions, they would hail him as a hero and heap accolades on him like he was some knight in shining armor. They would see the vileness that existed in those men and the levels that they had sunk to and say that Brand Frontier was right in his actions. His father would not though. That is what pains him today. He knows what is in his heart right now. It is that exact feeling that churned and tumbled around his soul those two dark days. When Brand looked across the scaffold and peered inside Sabian Lords he filled with a hatred that was unfathomable. Rage built in him the likes of which he despised, the likes of which he fought every day against. Sabian had kidnapped his son, mimicking actions taken by that other monster years earlier but on a worse degree. For over a month Sean was lost to him and Ana, she had never really forgiven him for that either. He knew that. He also knew that he had begun to be a bit more thinned skin than he should be. The actions from The Brotherhood had set the wheels in motion, followed by Sabians scheme. Brand now lashed out at anyone that would dare bring his family into his fight. That was what happened with Kurt Vedder. Kurt did assault Ana at ringside. Kurt did call her names and even knocked her to the ground with a stiff punch. But did Kurt really deserve to be bent backwards over that guardrail, victim of a super powerbomb? Exactly how much like whom he was fighting against had Brand become? Had he done exactly what his father feared he would do and become just like them? He didn't really know the answer to that but he did know that if his soul was the price to pay for his family's safety then it was a price he would pay forever for. _____________________________________________________ Brandon looked over to Sean. He was curled up beside his Uncle Chris and had dozed off "reading" him a book. The doctors had said that although he had not regained consciousness that reading or talking to that patients was comforting to their family as well as possibly beneficial to them. He and Ana didn't see the harm in it and Sean was devastated by what The Strangler did to his favorite uncle, maybe this would help. Like always though he needed help with the big words and after the first page Brandon ended up reading "The Lady In The Lake" to Chris and a quickly nodding off Sean. Now though, with the book resting on the edge of the bed, Brandon Roche just looked from his son up to his best friend and back again. He didn't realize it but he had begun to scowl. He and anger were frequent companions now and the tension he felt behind his eyes was almost missed when it wasn't there. Sean tumbled to the floor and the thump he made shook Brandon out of his melancholy mood and back to the present. The little boy began to cry before he gained his composure and realized where he was. That and the fact that Chris was sitting straight up in bed! Brandon looked on, stunned. A few seconds crept by before he looked to Brandon and once again shook him from his current state into the next. Joy. Sheer joy. Brandon bolted out of his chair and began to hug his friend before a wincing of pain streaked over Chris Reid face. Brandon backed off only to be replace by an over exuberant Sean. Once again Chris' face contorted in pain but he followed through with the hug before Brandon gently pulled Sean away. "Unca' Chris! You're OK!" "........." Chris tried to speak but the dryness in his mouth prevented that for now. Instead he just smiled a weak smile and touched Sean on the side of the face. He looked to Brandon with a bewildered look on his face. As soon as they made eye contact though it seemed as if everything rushed back to him. He remembered the Boston Strangler busting through the door in the hotel room. He remembered him bringing that ball peen hammer down between his eyes. He remembered reaching out for Ana and the kids before everything going black. He remembered the horror of waking up every few minutes to a chorus of screams from Sean and Jarod. He remembered it all, and his heart rate monitor showed it. Anger filled his eyes for a second before he glanced to Sean. Seeing the happiness in his young eyes melted away the initial shock of what had happened to him. He was angry, hurt, confused, but most of all... afraid. A rush of dream images filled his head as he looked around to the room he occupied. Was Ana and Jarod alright? What happened after the last time he blacked out? Sean looked OK but was something wrong he just couldn't see? How long had he been unconscious? Had that bastard been made to pay for what he put them through? Answers to all those came as Brandon, Chris and Sean sat there and talked. Between the nurses and the doctors coming in to see the newly arrived patient he was filled in on the events of the last two weeks. The facts that Jarod and Sean were fine, and that Ana wasn't harmed either. The fact that Chris seemed to be the sole target of that deranged madman that night. The fact that this instance had drove a wedge between Brandon and Chris' sister Ana. He tried to console Brandon the best he could and convince him that they would work things out. Brandon questioned it however. She blamed him for everything her and her family has been through for the past five years. Brandon was hard pressed to argue against that though. He believed the same thing. How far was he really willing to go to fight this never ending battle? Would he sacrifice his sense of happiness? He didn't really know. Ana soon came, along with Jarod and the grandparents. Late in the evening the barrage of visitors had thinned out and between frequent naps and checkups Chris had shown that indeed he might just be alright. Ana lay asleep now on the soft leather chair, Jarod mimicking her stance but on the foot of the bed. Sean watched the small television, some movie of the week on one of the cable stations filled his eyes. This brief respite in the festivities gave Chris and Brandon a chance to turn their attention to other things, darker things. "It hurts man, everything does... I don't think he left anything else to break. Brandon..." "I... I'm sorry Chris. This is all my fault, Ana's right. If it wasn't for this stupid wrestling thing and our grandiose plans none of this would have happened." "B.L... don't. Don't get all sappy on me now, you can't, if you do what would I have done all of this for? What would everything we've done mean if you start to doubt yourself now? It's tough, but this isn't the worst that has happened." "That's just the thing. It isn't the worst but it's the latest. The latest in a long line of torture we have lived through. Why us, you know?" Chris looked at Brandon. Once again he had begun doubting himself. This had become a frequent occurrence lately, something that didn't do him any good. If he didn't stay focused bastards like The Boston Strangler would have their way with him. "Look at me Brandon... look at me.
