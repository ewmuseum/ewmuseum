We're In This Together

23 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"I've become Impossible Holding on to when Everything seemed to matter more The two of us (all used) All used and beaten up Watching fate As it flows Down the path We have chosen You and me We're in this together, now None of them can stop us now We will make it through somehow" - "We're In This Together" Nine Inch Nails He limped across the living room inside of his Dearborn apartment. The entire week off from action would be good, and would get him healthy again in time for Countdown To Oblivion. He sat down in the chair that was in front of the window, which overlooked the street that he lived on. As usual, it was dead at this time of night. He was almost always the only person awake at three o'clock in the morning. He had reason to be awake for once, though. He couldn't sleep. The thoughts in his mind had his head spinning. He sighed as he leaned back. The two normally emotionless slate blue eyes moved around the dark apartment, tears in them. They weren't tears of pain, they were from joy. His life had suddenly turned for the better in so short a time, he didn't realize it until after he had risked his body to defeat Monte Burns. And without the help he received from Monica Davidson, he would have been in much worse shape than he was at this moment. Still, he was bewildered. His mind kept asking the same questions. The sudden change of heart after nearly eighteen months without speaking to each other. Something had come over her, something made her want to change and he wanted to know the answers. He wondered if she would stay in the fWo. If she would try and talk to him, sort out the differences and the past. He had wanted badly to make everything right. Like the way they used to be. But he didn't know if it was possible at all. All of the questions that ran through his mind couldn't be answered by himself and it drove him crazy. Yet, in his heart, he still loved Monica Davidson. Even after everything that happened between Summer Meltdown, until Twilight. It was just a feeling that lingered above him. No matter how much he had tried to tell himself he hated her, the feeling remained. Because when he was at his worst in life, there was one person that had always been there for him. That person was Monica. All of the hate he wanted to feel meant nothing. The memory of everything from 1982 to 200, eighteen years of friendship, couldn't break what happened in eighteen months. And in his mind, the same had to be true for her. To him, that was the only reasonable conclusion. As tired as he was, he was still excited by this. She had helped him win, not even Monte's abysmal pockets. Money, which probably caused the entire debacle, was apparently no longer the true method. He knew how money had corrupted her in the SWL and how Monte did it. Burns had convinced her to hate him. Monte offered her an enticing sum of money to unleash a rocky year of built up anger. Bringing in the discussion of how he was just another depressed nobody. How money could make everything right. But ultimately, it proved how wrong Monte Burns was. He knew that Monica had tried to talk to him before, but Monte would always take advantage of him when his defenses were down. Forcing Monica to watch what has happened. He growled a little bit under his breath and leaned back. As he closed his eyes he heard footsteps moving around the outside of his apartment in the hallway. The doorbell rang.
