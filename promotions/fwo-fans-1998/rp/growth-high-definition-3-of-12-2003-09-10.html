Growth: High Definition It isn't easy to try something new. To let go of that fear of moving on, and to just move on. Talk is cheap, but action... action is what makes life life. Actions don't define us, but they definitely change us. They bring about everything in life. Decisions of the conscience, and actions that spawn from them. They are the important things that we remember. The actions... The sound of a body hitting the mat was all the collection of fans needed to hear. A 6'5 kid in tennis shoes stood in the ring, kneeling over the large worker, who while large, was at least six inches shorter. The kid's heart was racing, and as the fans cheered him on, a bespectacled man looked taken back at the actions of this bold youngster. He stood there in his tweed jacket, and chuckled to himself. Kevin meanwhile knelt over the man and pretended to pummel his face in. The wrestler looked shocked, but his look quickly turned to a smile. "They're eating it up kid! Keep it up, and then let me get a few in on you." The man spoke softly from his prone position on the mat. Meanwhile the man in the tweed adjusted his glasses, and laughed. He couldn't believe it. The kid had DDTed their "biggest" heel, Strom Edwards, and it looked really good. Plus he had gotten a bigger reaction than anyone else in the fed, even one of their established guys, GenoType. Strom stood as Kevin lifted him by the hair, he was completely taken back by this kid, but he chuckled to himself. "Swing at me kid." Strom whispered. Kevin reeled back, and then lunched forward with a punch; Strom ducked it, and then grabbed Kevin and kneed him in the stomach. Masters doubled over like he had seen his father do countless times before, and then jumped a little as he felt the strong man lift him overhead, and drop him with a snap suplex. Kevin sold it completely, and clutched his back. The fans erupted with boos. Strom looked out at them, and cursed more. Kevin meanwhile stood up groggily, and Strom turned back to him. They locked up briefly, but long enough for Strom to whisper, "Now kid... whip me to the ropes and then give me the big boot. Know what that is?" Strom spoke quickly, but Kevin nodded. It was a classic 80s move. Kevin did as he asked, and whipped Strom to the ropes. He then threw his leg high, and Strom collided with his boot, foot to face. Falling to the mat, Kevin improvised what to do next. He threw his hand to his ear, as the fans cheered him, then ran off a rope, bouncing back, and dropping an elbow onto the prone Strom. The fans erupted once more, and Kevin made the pin. The man outside stripped his tweed coat off, revealing pinstripes, and slid into the ring, slamming his hand onto the mat three times. Kevin jumped up, his hand raised high, and the fans cheered. The referee went to the ropes and grabbed the corded microphone laying in the corner. He lifted it to his mouth. "Your winner..." He paused, looking at Kevin and whispering, "What's your name kid?" "Uhm..." Kevin looked around for clues, glancing down at his shoes. "All-Star... All-Star Masters..." he mumbled. The man nodded, looking confused, and yelled into the mic, "Your winner... ALL-STAR MACK MORGAN!" The cheers of the meager audience felt enormous in Kevin's ears. A smile formed across his face, he pumped his fist. For the first time in a long time, things felt right. Things can work out. That's what life wants everyone to realize, that things can work out. That's why there are hardships. That's why life isn't easy. Because if it was easy, you'd learn nothing. You'd never know failure, and never know the best feeling in the world: the feeling of resolution, when things finally do work out. That feeling... Those moments... There really isn't anything better; nothing in the world. Except... love.
