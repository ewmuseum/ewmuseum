January 15, 2004 - The Citadel of Peace/Room 214

14 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Citadel of Peace Floating over the United Nations Building New York City WHOOMP WHOOMP WHOOMP The Flying Frenchie and Ric Chronos sprang up to attention as the World Peace Monitor came alive. On the massive screen was the face of Kofi Annon. "World Peace Organization. Report for duty." "Ric Chronos, reporting for duty, sir!" "Flying Frenchie, reporting for duty, sir!" Kofi scowled. "And the others?" Frenchie answered. "Dealing wit' a rebel outburst in Sout' America, sir!" "Very well, and at ease, gentlemen. There's a situation. In a secluded military base in Montana, the United States has been working on a top secret weapon. While their President has thus far been unwilling to give us any more details than that about this weapon, we do know that two members of the Assassination Squad Supreme have been hired by a mysterious third party to acquire it. Your primary objective, then, is to prevent them from acquiring this device. Nearly as important, however, is for the two of you to gather any intelligence about this weapon, and, if judged to be a threat to World Peace, destroy it. World Peace Organization, move out!" The World Peace Monitor flickered off. Frenchie and Chronos dashed towards the docking bay, watching the giant doors slide open. Chronos dove out and spread his arms out, allowing the masked and goggled Frenchie to grab him and fly them both off towards the Montana wilderness. "Ze coordinates have been sent to ze H.U.D in my googles. We can be zere in minutes." Minutes later, in Montana. Kodiak batted at another armed soldier who'd been foolish enough to shoot at him, and the blow from his mighty paw tore his head from his shoulders. "Where is it?", roared the Alaskan Juggernaut, gore hanging from his teeth. "Give us the Patriot Generator, and we'll leave. Stall...and you're lunch." The facility was littered with the bodies of soldiers and scientists, and those few who were still alive played dead. One of them was less successful than the others, and was plucked from the ground from by what was once clearly a man, but now something else. "Creed, I find one! Sluggore smash now!" Sluggore lifted his hefty club, prepared to bring it down on the frightened man, but Kodiak Creed grabbed the club at the top of it's swing. "Smash later. This one smells like fear. He knows where the Patriot Generator is. " Creed brought his ursine face within inches of the terrified scientist, and snarled at him. "Tell us what we want, and you live. And do it quick, or he swings for the fences. Do we have an understanding?" The scientist yelped and nodded, pulling a key card from his lab coat. On shaky legs he led the A.S.S.es down a series of hallways, finally stopping at a coffee machine. He slipped his card into the bill slot, and the machine lowered into the floor, revealing the entrance into another lab. Creed shoved the scientist into the room, then squeezed in after. Sluggore, stood morosely on the outside, his frame twice as big as the entrance was. "I watch. Sluggore strong." Creed fell onto all fours, and examined the Patriot Generator. "This is it, then. I thought for a second that you'd try and dupe me, but this is the real deal. Smart move." The scientist slowly backed away from Creed, finally cowering into a corner. " I don't know how it works. Just please, let me live." Creed looked quizzically at the scientist. "Let you live? That was never part of our deal. I just said he wouldn't beat you. And he won't." Creed lunged at the man, and sank his teeth into his neck. Creed's fur took on even more of a pinkish hue as this man's blood mixed with all the others he'd killed today. CRASH Kodiak pivoted, and saw Sluggore trapped in the wall separating the room from the hallway. He struggled to break away more of the wall and allow him to escape, which proved to be unnecessary as the rest of him was sent into the room via a force beam. Ric Chronos and The Flying Frenchie stepped into room, taking in their surroundings. Frenchie spoke first. "Get ze device, Ric. I'll take care of zese two." Creed jumped at the generator, hoping to cover it before Chronos could get to it, but it was for naught. Chronos seemed to get it and leave at super speed. This was, of course, utterly ridiculous. He merely slowed down time for everyone else, creating the illusion of faster movement. Who do you think he is, Black Quicksilver? Frenchie flew at Creed, delivering a solid right hook, but the Juggernaut barely registered the blow. He backhanded (back pawed?) Frenchie, sending him careening directly into the club of Sluggore. He was summarily sent flying into a third wall, where he crumpled to the ground. "Fool!" Frenchie recognized the voice, and cursed himself for not having thought to look for a third person in the crew. "We need that device. Do you realize what you've done?" Clarence Chadwick III loomed over Frenchie, holding his purple basset-hound, Ultra, in his arms. "He's coming, you know. We needed that device to save us all." "We would never have allowed you to control zat weapon, baldy," spat Frenchie. "It wasn't a weapon. The Patriot Generator is capable of establishing a sustained force field no bigger than an atom, or as large as a continent. It will allow any nation for completely barricading itself from every other." "A force field?" "Hardly. It's flawed, you see, in that it's much too powerful. No light can penetrate it, no air. You could seal off an entire nation, and in a matter of hours every living thing within it would be no longer. But, if you had access to the proper supplies, you could withstand a barrage for quite some time. Very valuable indeed, either way you looked at it. Imagine being under siege within the field generated, and suddenly removing it and placing it over your attackers. We had a great many ideas on how to use it, not the least of it, to protect us from hiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiimmmmmmmmmmm..." Suddenly, Chadwick, Kodiak and Sluggore seemed to be stuck in molasses. Frenchie looked over, and saw the smiling Chronos. "Sorry. Took me a while to safeguard it. They tell you what it does?" Frenchie nodded while he stood. He tapped his goggles, tuning in his communicator. "We're going to need a containment team for t'ree, I repeat, t'ree villains." He clicked off the communicator, and addressed Chronos. "Some sort of force field. I wish we had it on us, t'ough. I've got a feeling we could use it." Frenchie's knee suddenly turned into a fine red mist. "You could have." The lab was gone, and Pierre found himself underneath Madison Square Garden, flames creeping closer to him. Fallen stared at him, with one good eye and one ruined socket. "Why? Why did you do zis?" "You were there. I'd have done it to anyone. What, do you think you're special? No one cares. No one would go out of their way to kill you." Fallen raised the gun, and pointed it at Pierre's head. He shot up with a start, in a familiar hospital bed, knocking back a man who'd been holding a pillow over his face. Thomas sprawled to the ground, but Pierre could not take advantage of it, as his leg was elevated above his bed, and still in a cast to facilitate the healing of his reconstructed knee. "Thomas." He scanned his bedside table, and saw a vase filled with flowers. He grabbed it and chucked it at Thomas, and took a small measure of delight as it shattered against Thomas' right hand that he held out to deflect it. [[No! No!]] Thomas scrambled back to his feet and grabbed Pierre's right hand, pinning it down to the bed with his left. Thomas then took his right arm and drove into into Pierre's throat. [[You took her from me. You can't take her from me. She's mine.]] Pierre struggled to speak, but managed to do so quietly. [[I haven't...taken anything.]] [[Don't lie to me. I saw her, heard her. She chose you.]] Thomas shut up, and continued to suffocate Pierre, watching us he turned an unattractive shade of purple. He could feel Pierre's pulse slow against his arm, then released him, and backed away, sobbing. [[I love her, more than anything. I can't kill you, though you deserve it. It would ruin her. But you'd better take care of her, treat her the way she deserves. I got this close once, Pierre. Don't think I won't be able to do it again.]] Thomas stumbled out of the room, wiping his bloody palm against his jeans. In his bed, Pierre struggled to get his breathing back to normal. The struggle had affected his vitals, and in now time, it seemed as if dozens of nurses and doctors surrounded his bed, checking his heart rate. "What happened?", inquired the nearest of them all. Pierre thought about it, realizing what would happen if he simply told the truth. "He shot me again. In my dream. Zat's all it was."
