Harem - 3 - Misere Mei

1 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When Eli Flair's wife Angel called Amy Campbell the other night, it was because she missed her friend. One of them always tried to give the other a call at least once a week just to keep in touch. It was never more than a few minutes of conversation due to Angel's utter exhaustion after over an hour of singing a night. Due to Amy's utter exhaustion after wrestling a thirty minute match. Angel never expected Aimz to say "Can you meet me in Albuquerque tonight?" "Sure," Angel had answered, "We just finished up a show at a club just outside the city." But as Angel headed into the diner where they'd agreed to meet, she couldn't help but notice the worried mask over her eyes. Amy was worried. Angel was weary. Both were mothers. Both involved with wrestlers. Both in love with music. They shared common ground everywhere, but Aimz wasn't quite feeling equal tonight. In her position, Angel was a temporary mentor...but she wasn't supposed to know that. Not yet, at least. "How's MJ?" Innocent enough start to the conversation, but Angel was tipped off to a deeper underlying because Amy was not one to meet up just to ask things she could give a call for - It required too much concentration. "She's good," said Angel as she picked up the menu, "Teething... it's a hard moment. Coffee, please?" She didn't really want coffee but she wanted the waitress to leave. "So what's up? How's the tour going?" Amy shifted in her seat. "It's...uhh...not going. I'm injured." Angel looked her over. She didn't limp, no bandages or excessive amounts of stitching, her head was still connected by her neck...Amy wouldn't take time off for anything short of six broken ribs. That was, unless someone very special was forcing her to. That someone just wasn't in the open air yet, or known to Angel - whose raised eyebrow and questioning glance Amy had caught. "Well, I say injured..." "You're pregnant, aren't you?" The cards were down. Amy's mouth was agape. "You sound like me," continued Angel, "I refused to admit it for over a month and made some bad decisions like not telling Eli about it... practically forced him to chase me across the country. It's not something to be afraid of unless it's unwanted..." Angel covered her mouth. "It's a problem, isn't it?" "I'm not telling him. There's no way I can break this on him." Amy stopped Angel before she started with a raised palm and a sigh, "I know, I know...he deserves to know, but he doesn't need this. Not right now." "Always something with our men, isn't it?" Angel giggled as the waitress brought her coffee over, "Too busy or too unstable or too stressed. And the worst part of it in your case and mine...they've got legitimate shit on their mind." She sighed. "Oh, for the days of the idiots from downtown who had no excuse to be busy." Another thought. "On second thought, no... I like our guys. When do you think you'll tell him?" That was something Amy'd thought over - considering, planning and then reconsidering since she'd heard the news for sure. It needed to be decided, though, and there was no better time than now. "After the wedding. I need to plan that, on top of all of this." Angel's eyes were worrying - tired, but sympathetic. After strength and happiness for days now, Amy was on the verge of breaking down when cold reality sat in front of her. "God, Angel...what can I do? This is terrible, having to go through this - like the most important time in my life - without being able to even talk to BL about it, let alone find comfort in him. He'd be freaking out more than I am...on top of that, Creid's liable to make it his goal in life to fuck all of this up so I have to play it as an injury." She continued, "Unlike you, I don't really have any care for my career and how this will affect it...though I'm sure it's gonna fuck things up for me on TV. I could be playing guitar for you guys and be just as happy, but I want to stay with Brandon on the road. I want to do things. I don't need to work, but I need to work...you know how I am. This is gonna be just about six months without being able to stay active or relieve stress at all and I swear to God I'll be insane by the end of it." Angel considered this. "I really don't know what to say, Ames...I mean, for me it was just a matter of not being able to move around onstage too much, but I could sing without doing any potential harm to MJ. But risking damaging the baby or not being able to do what you love... it's not a decision I'd want." "Suffice to say," she continued, "if you ever need a few days off just give us a shout, okay? The boys and I are always willing to make room." "Can you make room tonight? I need to get my head straight before I see BL again." "Of course," said Angel, "But you must be hungry... my treat? Onion rings? Burger? Pancakes? All of the above?" Finally, a smile from Amy's face. "Take as much time as y'need," assured Angel, "if you can actually consider a bus a home, it's yours as long as you need it to be." Well, she had an invite and a place to rest her head in relative peace for the night. An evening spent with Angel, MJ and their boys would hopefully shed a little light on things - Especially considered how well-tuned to this sort of behavior Angel seemed, having picked up instantly on 'You're pregnant' without any hints given. They were two of a kind...one just needed a real mother's hand.
