Desecrate Through Reverence: Memory Lane

12 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I've been watching you from a distance The distance sees through your disguise. All I want from you is your hurting... I want to heal you, I want to save you from the dark. In the blink of an eye, years come to mind... For once, Reno Davis' life wasn't crashing around him. He was quickly rising the ranks of OSW, had what HE considered to be hottest girlfriend ever and was moving in with said hot chick. Davis and his love-interest, Amy Campbell, both had to cope with this new feeling overcoming them. This feeling lacked chair shots to the face and lost opportunities for a change. They felt good, They felt great, They felt happy. Amy leaned against the apartment building, beads of sweat running down her body from the intense heat and exercise. Reno heaved another box of Aimz's junk onto his shoulder from the car trunk and rolled his eyes. "You can't take a break yet, that requires you actually working first," He approached his lazy girlfriend and dropped the box onto the walkway before inching his way in for a soft kiss. "What," mumbled Amy, "Is the big, bad pro wrestler complaining?" She wriggled a bit out of his embrace to land a square, gentle punch to his chest. "You've got muscles, Rambo. I'm a fragile little girl...now let's see that box up the stairs in five seconds flat!" "I bet," Reno smiled a rare, real smile, "And you can swivel those child-bearing hips up to the kitchen and work on getting me a cold beer and something to eat." Aimz cocked a foot up. She watched as Reno hunched and shot two hands to his groin in an almost instinctual reaction. Hey, nobody ever said she was the gentlest girlfriend - he had to stay prepared. "I've got child-bearing hips that can swivel legs that're attached to my shit-kicker boots. So, unless you want the shit kicked out of you..." "Wow! Easy there woman," Reno snapped playfully as his pet monkey, ZaC, scurried up onto his shoulder, "It's two on one now, bring it bitch." His smile sharply accented the joy in his eyes, which sent a chill up Amy's spine. She faked high and tackle low, sending all three of them to the ground laughing. Reno monkey flipped Aimz over him and then pinned her to the ground where they stared aimlessly at each other for a moment. "I love you," Reno's words were fluent and smooth. Not forced. They were true. Amy's heart skipped a beat. He was real... "I lo--" Their furrier friend promptly and effectively killed the moment. The feeling was there in the gaze between them, but the words hadn't had a chance to complete themselves before ZaC was yanking Reno's head back by hairs he was chewing on. Reno stood to free himself of his friend while helping Amy to her feet with a smile. "I swear to God," she grumbled, "The monkey needs a fucking hobby." She was an alcoholic then. Drugged constantly. They were stupid kids, dumbasses without a care in the world. Now, in this split second, she frowned upon everthing they'd done - how absolutely carefree and intoxicated that life was...but with that same split second came a longing. They were stupid then. They were careless. They were drunks. But they were happy. They had nothing and nobody else to consider or care for.
