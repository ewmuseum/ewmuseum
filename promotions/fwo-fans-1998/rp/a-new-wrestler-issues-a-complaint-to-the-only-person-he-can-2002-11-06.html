A New Wrestler issues a complaint to the only person he can confide in.

6 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Silence, it was exactly how Brandon liked it the day of a match, complete silence. He knew out there in the ring anything could happen, Brandon looked down at his knee and grimaced. He's had one of those "anything can happen" moments before, and he was damn sure he hated them. Hell with the way Halloween Hell III went, this Warfare could just turn into a giant riot before Brandon even got to go on. That, would be bad. As the thoughts rounded through Brandon's head, he slowly began to realize he had a few pre-match preparations to make. 1st: Call his mother 2nd: Tape up his knee 3rd: Grab his gear 4th: Eat a hot ham and cheese sandwich, with ketchup There used to be only three things he had to do before a match, now after the injury a fourth thing jumped into the routine, making it even longer. Brandon reached out and picked up the phone quickly dialing a number. The phone rang twice before it was finally answered with a cheery "Hello!" "Mom, it's Brandon, how ya doin?" Brandon said, like it was his normal routine to say that. "Well Brandon I'm doing fine, I just got back from the grocery store, your Uncle Mike went with me to help me carry in a new lamp I bought for the kitchen. You should see your cousin Barry, he's getting big, he could be a wrestler no problem when he's older." Brandon's mother said, telling Brandon exactly how the family was doing, this was always apart of the phone call home. Brandon reached out and grabbed the role of medical tape from the table and began to tape his leg as his mother continued to talk. "Your other little cousin Jimmy is doing great in school, his family's stopping by for dinner on Friday. Any chance you could make it home for that Brandon?" His mother asked, Brandon sat, stopped taping his leg and looked down at a piece of paper sitting on a desk. He reached over grabbing the paper and scanned it before answering. "Yeah I can probably be their, don't tell them I'm a for sure though. I need to make sure I have time enough to travel, add the fact Spencer and Dudley are planning the venues, and not telling us till the last minute. It's really an unorganized place Mom. Did you see Halloween Hell?" Brandon asked as he went back to taping his leg. "Yeah honey, that was great how the old roster came out and started a fight, really a great ang..." His mom said before being interrupted. "Mom, that wasn't a work, that was real. The entire building rioting, it was real. I'm worried about what will happen tonight, I wish there was a way I could talk to the old members and find out exactly what happened. I may give Alex Newsome a call later if I still have his number from when he worked a few shows with jOlt, and find out exactly what happened." Brandon said, while his mother began to get a bit worried in her tone. "Listen honey, we can make it with you working for these men, theres other jobs, tons of other things you can do...." She trailed off as Brandon began to finish talking. "Mom, this is what I want to do, it'll get better, it has to," Brandon said, as he had finaly finished taping his leg. "Listen Mom, I gotta run, I'll call you after the show." Brandon said as his mother replied, "See ya on T.V. tonight honey, good luck." With that Brandon left the room picking up his gear and began his trek to find the best hot ham and cheese sandwich in town.
