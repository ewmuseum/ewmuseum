Becoming- Part Three

24 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When you're a fourteen year old girl, you're supposed to be enjoying your life. Hanging out with friends. Talking about guys over the phone. Not for Jessica, though. Never for her. Jessica sat on the tile floor in the kitchen, watching everything happening in the living room as if she was watching some oversized projection TV. She was attempting to distance herself from all of it. Make it all no longer effect her. Make it all some movie she was watching from a far away distance. No emotion. "You never told me you had a kid with your last boyfriend." her father bellowed at her mother. "Not one, Peter. Two." her mother said through sobs. "TWO!?!" Peter screamed at her. "You had two kids, and never even bothered to tell me? You fucking BITCH!" SLAP! Her father slapped her across the face. She held the side of her face as she cried, tears rolling down her fragile face. "You play all this crap about being the good little Christian woman, and you're nothing more than a FUCKING WHORE." he screamed at her. Jessica watched this. Unmoving. Unwilling to respond to the voice inside of her that screamed at her to come to her mother's aid. She was getting better at burying her emotions. Between tears, she stood up, and turned around. She turned her back on her mother as she walked out of the kitchen, and up the stairs to her room. Walking past the pink walls. Walking past the glass unicorn. The stuffed animals. The pink bed covers. To her nightstand. Laying there was her cross. She picked it up, necklace and all. She put it on, and looked into her mirror. One last time. She took the cross off, turned around, and walked out of her room. Into the bathroom. She walked up to the toilet, and dropped the cross in. She watched as in floated in the water after causing a splash on its inital reaction, splashing her with toilet water. Perhaps the old Jessica would have screamed "GROSS!", but Jessica didn't care at this point. She turned and walked out of the bathroom, and back into her room. Back into the room that she could no longer stand. "I HATE YOU!" she screamed out loud. She pushed the glass unicorn to the ground, shattering it. She spit at the carpeting beneath her, and tore off the pink bed covers. She tossed some of the pillows out of the window. Then, she looked up, a strange grin on her face, and walked over to her picture. With a single fist, she cracked it. "That's not who I am." she said. She was laughing. Hystrically. Over and over again, she stomped on the picture with her foot. "My name is Kali Saturos. And I am a servant of Chaos." I am becoming something insane and brutal.
