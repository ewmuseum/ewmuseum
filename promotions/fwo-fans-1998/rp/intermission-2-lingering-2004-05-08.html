Intermission [2] Lingering

8 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"What can I do for you sir?" The man behind the desk politely enquired as another customer approached the front of the line. "Two tickets to Ruahh please." Was the swift reply from the customer that prompted the desk keeper to rifle through a drawer. "You're very lucky sir." The ticket sales rep added to the conversation "We're pretty much out of tickets for Ruahh, I can probably work something nice out for you a few rows back though if you want to be close to the action." "That would be aweso-" But the customer didn't finish what he was saying, instead cutting into the words were the sounds of gargling that came with being strangled. The next thing was the sound of him stumbling away to the floor in turn with a very hefty shove that he'd received. The son of the man being assaulted looked up to see the individual making the unprovoked attack and was greeted by "Beat it kid, unless you want some too." Quickly rushing over to his father the child did exactly as he was told, the ticket salesman looked up but the individual he was selling to had been replaced by a far more familiar face. Kellen Kinkade. "I uh... you have to wait in line sir." The ticket seller informed the new arrival on the scene. The Jersey Devil looked left, then right... then turned for a second as the next five people in line took a step back. He looked back to the ticket seller and grinned "I am in line, dick head." "I... see. Well what can I do for you?" The man asked, now clutching the tickets in what was becoming a clammy grasp. "Two tickets to Ruahh, por favor." Kade growled "Not those though, front row seems far too... cliché. I want something up in one of the executive boxes where I'm not going to get pawed at by fat sweaty kids, their paedophile fathers and tit flashing slutbag mothers." "Absolutely." The salesman quickly produced some tickets that Kinkade snatched from his grasp "Those will be exact-" "Put them on my fWo tab." Kinkade glanced at the tickets as he walked away. "But you're not in the fWo anymore." The ticket seller pointed out. "I know, looks like you're shit out of luck then." Kade laughed back as he pocketed the tickets and walked away. He quickly found himself swarmed with fWo reporters. "Kinkade, we've just captured you on film purchasing two tickets to the coming Monday Night Ruahh. Care to explain to us why you will be attending?" The first reporter enquired. "Because I mark for the Frenchie!" Kinkade's sarcastic reply was snappy, but went undetected by the questioning press. "Really?" One of them quickly asked. "No, not really. He's a beret wearing cock sucker... the only thing that'd make me mark out for that loser is if Austin Davis came back, spanked his aids monkey on a baguette and shoved it up Flyboy's ass." Kinkade shook his head and continued walking. "So are you really going to tell us why you're attending?" Another reporter asked. "No, that'd spoil all the fun." Kade wasn't budging. "But-" One of the reporters began, before being snatched by the collar. "Before you continue... if this turns out to be a question about why I'm going to Ruahh, you're going to find out the answer to that question by virtue of me pulling off your head right here and taking it to Ruahh with me." Kinkade snarled furiously as the reporter rethought his approach. "I was just going to say... what do you think of Poison Ivy's recent comments?" Kade released the reporter who in turn rubbed his neck. "I only buy her columns to wipe my ass on them, what did she say?" Kellen enquired with a scratch of the chin. "She said that you’d never be as psychologically invasive as The Phoenix." Came the statement from the reporter who was now taking notes. "Well now I'd probably be really offended by that comment, if I knew or cared who the fuck he is." Kinkade chuckled to himself "But you know, opinions are like assholes. Everyone has one and in the case of Ivy... some people are one. I'm the Immortal champion... that's all that matters, what champion is The Phoenix?" "He was the-" The reporter started, again to be cut off. "He's the Kellen Kinkade has never heard of me champion, to answer my own question. Now if you're done asking me stupid questions I'm going to jump in my '67 Camaro, cower in fear about The Phoenix in the same way I would had you told me that Mike Heftel was going to beat me up, and get the hell out of this shithole." Kinkade stepped into his car and quickly sped away, between the insults and slurs one thing had become clearer than anything else... ...he was going to be at Ruahh.
