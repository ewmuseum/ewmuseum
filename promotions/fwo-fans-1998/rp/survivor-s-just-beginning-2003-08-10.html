Survivor's Just Beginning

10 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Elliot Harper never thought the day would come when he WOULDN'T be happy about signing an fWo contract. The ink had just dried on the bulky document, rather different to the ones used for on-screen signings. A lot less showy and a lot more clauses. Exclusivity rules, schedule expectations, marketing commitments, medical disclosures, drug-test details... But now all the i's were dotted and all the t's were crossed. Elliot Harper was an fWo 'superstar'. His face, however, looked like that of the silver medal winner at the Olympics. Sure he'd smile for show, present a false visage for the cameras, say how pleased, how proud, he was during interviews... But, he KNEW he'd came 2nd, and in a battle to be 1st, coming 2nd might as well be last. It takes a pretty 'unique' person to feel this way, but then again, Elliot has always strived to be number one. He went straight for the #1 promotion with his master-plan, he attempted to be #1 in Survivor III... And he won't be happy until the brightest light shines upon him, a spotlight he won't share. His eyes, lurking in the shadows from his prominent brow, then burned with ambition... ...If nobody remembers the others, he'd be the winner by default. Survivor III was anything but over, and if the truth be told, it had only just begun. The TRUE Survivor would be the last one standing in the fWo, or the 1st to strap a Tag Title belt around their waist. Elliot didn't care anymore who the winner WAS? What's important to him is who the winner WILL be. And believe this, Elliot Harper will do anything and everything to make sure he wins in the long run.
