Promotional Appearence

20 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Department stores truely sucked this time of year. All there was, is a ton of pastel colors, and a bunch of candy. Oh... cant forget all of the giant white bunnies at the end of every aisle. Now the big fWo booth, this was the place to be. I sat silently in between two of the greatest super stars in fWo history. On my right side sat a technical marvel, Mitch Wilson. Just his "Property of Slade Wilson" shirt said it all for this man. He was trained by the best to be the best, and he quite possibly was. Then on my left sat another man who has that claim engraved on his grave. The only five time fWo World champion, Scott Slugger. Just the way the two looked at each other made me feel a false feeling of intensity between the two. Just sitting at that booth, you could feel this urge that they had to get up, and try to beat the hell out of each other. These two men could main event, and sell out any arena, with any promotion, in the world. With just these looks they had exchanged. A young boy had walked up and handed Slugger his promotional photo to autograph, Slugger wrote his signature across it, and passed it down the line, the young boy looked so proud that Slugger had signed his name. He looked up as I took the photo into my hands and scribbled "Rush" across it. I looked into the young boy's eyes, he was staring over at Mitch. He could give two shits less about me. I passed the photo down and watched the child's eyes light up. That's who I've always wanted to be when I got older. Face or heel, just your intensity, just the way you look, the way you play your character on television. To make a kid that happy by signing a name onto a photograph. Just the fact it made some one's day that much. I've wanted that my entire life, I want to be like Mitch and Scott. I want people to remember everything about me, I want them to remember my smile, I want them to remember what I say, and how I said it. For people to say, "Yea, remember when Slugger beat Creed? Or when Slugger took it to Ice at 'Twilight 1999, or that time Wilson beat Wretch on that Slaughter to win his first world title?" I want those people to think "Remember when Rush beat Wilson at Cyberslam?" or "Remember when Rush took Slugger to the limit and beat him to win his sixth world title?" I know I'm not ready yet, hell I may never be ready, but taking the Cruiserweight title is a step in the right direction.
