Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Another Battle April 26, 2000 ::The scene begins mere moments before Warfare hits the air, Geoff Green is wandering the halls of the arena, on his way to the stage entrance where he will fulfill his duty as cameraman. As Geoff passes by an exit only hallway he notices something, what he notices is loud banging on the and small dents showing in the door, Geoff decides to investigate the exit only door, as the door suddenly is rocked off of it's bearings and falls away awkwardly. Geoff is measmerized by the figure standing on the outside. The 7'0 Plus massive figure of Rain steps through the door engulfed in a black trnech coat and his hair wet, the sky lighting up by lightning behind him and the Rain pouring down, he walks in at a quick pace, when he spots Geoff who has stopped in his tracks.:: Geoff Green: Rain ? Why didn't you use the front entrance ? Or the wrestlers entrance ? Rain: I have been told I am to wrestle tonight, of course the front door is filled with annoying mortals, and the wrestler entrance is full of arrogant fools. The Storm chooses a way of sercrecy. Geoff Green: Match ? Oh yeah, you fight Eron tonight he's been making quite a few waves recently, and he was the one who beat you for your internet title reign. Rain: Must you remind me Geoff ? Eron defeated me at a confusing stage in my following, and he did not grant me a re-match for the treasured title, tonight Ill show him the power of the Storm. Geoff Green: Where have you been lately ? I mean you havent been at a few shows. Rain: I dont have time for this petty talk Geoff and I am guessing that neither do you, I havent been here, and I will return to full strength again, the Storm is watching and preparing as it builds itself, to once again loom in full force over the clear sky of the fWo. Geoff Green: Well I guess- :: Rain walks by Geoff and into the arena cutting Geoff off in mid-sentence as Geoff hurries to the entrance stage.::
