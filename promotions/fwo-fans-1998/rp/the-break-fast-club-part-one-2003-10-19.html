The Break Fast Club (part one)

19 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The following events occurred on Friday, October 17th, 2003. 'Narly' Nate is by himself in a library. No, he isn't shocking us all by reading a book. He's seated at a table with his head down on it, while his arms hang lifeless by his sides. It's assumed that he's sleeping, but who knows...although it *is* almost 8:00 in the morning, and he's not used to being up that early. There are five other tables in the library, and the six tables are split into two rows of three. Nate is seated at the front table on the left row. He is awaken by the library door opening, and raises his head to see... Senor Funpants? Funpants: What the hell? Nate: Dude. Funpants: I hope to God this is the worst I'm gonna have to put up with today. Funpants walks over and sits at the table directly behind Nate. The next person to enter the library is Steve-O. He walks over to Nate and makes some unidentifiable motion. Nate: Dude, what? Someone put a wheel in your head? Steve-O: Did they? Man, I was so stoned this morning. I thought I saw this blonde guy, like, drinking all the orange juice in the fridge and eating ALL our pancakes, so I had to go down to the donut place and get a donut, but they were all out of donuts! Nate: Dude, that's nothin'. I was so stoned this morning, I thought I drank all this orange juice and ate all these pancakes, and I don't like orange juice and pancakes! Steve-O: Man....orange juice and pancakes. You know who don't like that stuff? My bro. He can't stand orange juice and pancakes. Nate: Totally. Steve-O: Shh-yeah. Steve-O sits down at the same table Nate is at, while Funpants shakes his head at their conversation. He silently tells himself that maybe he made the wrong idea in not brining a sharp object with him today. His frustration only grows when the next person enters the library. Black Quicksilver. BQ touches a few things on the checkout desk, and even takes a few things in the process. One of the things he touches, however, is a mouse trap, which snaps shut on his hand. BQ: Ow! Oh God, oh God, oh God! BQ runs back and forth, before smashing his hand against the desk, attempting to get the mouse trap off his hand. That doesn't work, so he then squats down and tries to get it off by stepping on it with his foot. That also doesn't work, so he grabs a pencil, and fortunately for him, he's able to pry it off. BQ: Whew. BQ casually walks over to where Funpants is sitting and pulls a copy of Playboy out of the back of his pants. He holds it up in front of Funpants, then tosses it onto the table next to the one Funpants is currently sitting at. Funpants, the red-blooded American male that he is, gets up and moves to the table where the Playboy is, and BQ takes Funpants' old seat. He puts his feet up on the table, and it's not long before his chair tilts back and he falls over. Nobody in the room turns to look at BQ, as Nate and Steve-O are engaged in some conversation that practically no one would understand, and Funpants is immersed in the Playboy. Once again, BQ plays casual, picking his chair back up and sitting back down, this time only putting his feet up on the chair next to him. Not a minute later, Evil-Lyn enters the library. She walks all the way around the library and sits at the back corner table on the right side, just behind Funpants. BQ, Nate, and Steve-O all turn to look at her. BQ only looks at her for a few seconds, being sure that she doesn't catch him. Nate and Steve-O, however, look at her and giggle uncontrollably. Funpants *would* look at her, but he's got pages full of naked women in front of him. Enter the man...the myth...the legend... SPHERE. Sphere: Well, well. Here we are! I WANT to congratulate you for being on time....but I won't. Nate raises his hand. Nate: Excuse me, miss? I think there's been a mistake. Someone gave me nutmeg instead of oregano. Sphere simply ignores Nate and continues to talk. Sphere: It is now 8:06... BQ: Uh, yeah, Sphere? Black Quicksilver, Sphere Heavyweight Champion. I thought this whole thing started at 7. What's with changing it to 8? Sphere: I *could* say I was misinformed, but the truth is that I can't be bothered to wake up AND be here by 7. That's just not enough time to get all the women out of my bed. Now...you have exactly eight hours and fifty-four minutes to think about why you're here. To ponder the error of your ways... BQ spits into the air and attempts to catch it in his mouth, but it veers off course and hits Nate in the back of the head. Sphere ...and you will not talk. You will not MOVE from these seats. And YOU... Sphere walks over to where BQ is sitting and pulls the chair out from underneath his feet. Sphere: ...will not sleep. Alright people, we're gonna try something a little different today. No, you're not all going to go out and win a match. You're going to write an essay--of no less than a thousand words--describing to me who you think you are. Keep in mind, it only matters who *I* think you are, so chances are this essay will be disregarded whenever I address you in the future. BQ: Is this a test? Sphere ignores BQ as he passes out papers and pencils. BQ: Can you even hear a word I'm saying right now? Sphere still doesn't answer BQ. BQ: Hmm. Hey, Sphere. Wretch is ten times the worker you'll ever be. Sphere throws a pencil at BQ's face, narrowly missing his eye, and gives him a papercut with a sheet of paper, then hands the sheet to BQ. Sphere: And when I say essay...I mean essay. I do not mean a single word repeated a thousand times. Is that clear, Quicksilver? BQ: The paper? No. I think you gave me the wrong one. Sphere: Are you trying to say I messed up? That I "goofed"? That I...pulled a Vengence? BQ: Krystal. Sphere: Excuse me? BQ: Krystal. She was this girl I knew back in elementary school. Wonder whatever happened to her. Sphere: You're an idiot. You know, maybe the five of you will even learn something about yourself today. Maybe you'll even decide on whether or not you want to return to the fWo or the fWEo. Funpants stands up. Funpants: You know, I can answer that right now, jackass. That'd be "No". "No" for me, cause... Sphere: Sit down, Funpants. Funpants: Screw you. Funpants grudgingly sits down, as Sphere points at the door. Sphere: My office is right across the hall. Any monkey business is ill-advised. If there is any monkey business, the Hand of Sphere will strike down upon thee with anger, anger that only an illuminous being such as myself is empowered with. Any questions? BQ: Yeah, I got a question. Does Don Johnson know you raid his wardrobe? Sphere: No. I snuck up on him and put him down with a tazer. Now, don't mess with the bull, Quicksilver...you'll get the shit. Sphere leaves the library. BQ: That man...is a brownie hound. Steve-O hits Nate. Steve-O: Dude, I told you! Everyone in the library tries to get comfortable, and a loud snapping sound is heard. All the men turn and look at Evil-Lyn, who is snapping her fingers. Evil-Lyn: Heh. Worked. Hmm-hmm-hmm... Evil-Lyn pulls out a nail file and begins to file her nails, as we cut to the hallway, where Sphere is spraypainting "SPHERE IS DOG" on the wall next to "his" office. Sphere: That'll show...dammit! Cut back to the library, where Steve-O is trying to force his pencil into Funpants' mouth. Steve-O: Dude, c'mon, do the walrus thing! Naturally, Funpants punches Steve-O in the mouth, and Steve-O goes back to his seat. Meanwhile, BQ goes to remove his jacket, but stops and looks over at Funpants. BQ: Well? Funpants: Well, what? BQ: I'm taking my jacket off. Aren't you going to take yours off, too? Funpants: I'm not wearing one. BQ raises an eyebrow at Funpants, which, of course, can't be seen, since he's wearing a mask, as he removes his jacket. Funpants goes back to his Playboy, and soon after, turns back toward BQ, who's still staring at him. Funpants: Is there a problem? BQ: Sort of. I'm trying to read that article but I have HORRIBLE vision. Funpants rolls his eyes and goes back to the magazine, while BQ decides to crumple up his sheet of paper and throw it at Nate, but the ball of paper doesn't get too far, and drops before it can reach Nate. With nothing else to do, BQ starts "singing" the intro to his theme song. BQ: Chug-a-chug-a-chug-a-chug-a-chug-a-chug-a-chug-a-chug-a-chug-a-chug-a-chug-a-chug-a-chug-a-chug-a-wew-WEW...chug-a-chug-a-chug-a-chug-a-chug-a-chug-a-WEW! Nate is looking at the palm of his hand and rocking back and forth. Nate: Dude, I can't believe this is really happening to me... Steve-O: Bro, what's up? Nate: There's some furry little guy livin' inside my hand and I can see him, like, chowing down on my leg! BQ: Oh crap! What do we do if we have to go to the bathroom? Nate: Please. BQ: No, really, what if we have to go? Nate: Naw man, you didn't let me finish! Please help me get the furry little guy out of my foot! Steve-O: Dude, like, I thought he was in your spleen. BQ: Well first off, don't talk. It makes it crawl back up. Steve-O: Yeah, and if you whip it out, you're dead before it hits the floor. I read it in this book one time. BQ: Funpants! Funpants turns to look at BQ. Funpants: This better be important. BQ: Go close the door. Nate might get pregnant. Steve-O: Dude, you read the same book? BQ: Totally? Steve-O: Totally. Nate: Why don't we just forget about it. I mean, like, everyone else in here isn't interested. Steve-O: "Interested"? Since when were you like, all smart with the big words and stuff? Man, I wanna be smart. Butt face. BQ: Did he just call one of us "butt face"? Nate: Nah. That's what he calls himself when he, like, has emotions and stuff. Funpants looks up at BQ, Nate, and Steve-O. Funpants: Excuse me, fellas? I think we should just write our pap...I mean, look at our nudie mags. Funpants goes back to his magazine, while Steve-O turns to BQ. Steve-O: Dude, you know, I'm allowed to call myself names. Just cause you've been hangin' and bangin' in the fWo for, like, ever, doesn't mean you gotta, like, lose all the time. Man, I could REALLY go for a danish right now. BQ: I didn't understand a single word you just said. Except for "lose all the time", which would actually be four words, so I guess I could've just left out that first sentence. Nate: Dude...Steve-O...he's tryin' to get a rise out of you. Steve-O: No way. Guys don't give me boners. Nate: What about Johnny Depp? Steve-O: Okay, most guys don't give me boners. BQ: So...so! Are you guys like...related? BQ slaps the table. BQ: Cousins? BQ slaps the table again. BQ: Lo.....vers....of Mega Job? BQ slaps the table for a third time. BQ: C'mon, Steve-O, level with me. Do you like the Hot Beef Injection? Nate: Make him stop, bro! MAKE HIM STOP! Steve-O: Dude, way to bring back old memories. *begin flashback sequence* Nate: Dude, there he is! Beef, man, Beef! Steve-O: Don't be such a pansy-ass white boy, bro. Go get his autograph! Nate: Sha! Nate fights his way through a crowd of...three people, in an attempt to get Beef's autograph. He's about five feet away when Beef throws a Pokeball at him. The Pokeball hits him in the head, and out pops Meowth, who proceeds to kick Nate in the crotch and grab him by the hair, repeatedly slamming him face-first into the ground. *end flashback sequence* Nate is now crying, with his head down on the table once again. Steve-O: You MONSTER. Cut to Sphere, who's seated at his desk. Sphere: Did someone call me? Eh? Prick little smugs. Oh, oh yeah. I went there.
