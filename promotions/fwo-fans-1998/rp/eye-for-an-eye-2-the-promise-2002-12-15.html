Eye For An Eye [2] The Promise

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
So there you have it, the great Vince Kinkade... not so fucking great afterall was he? It's funny, internet dirt sheets and news websites... they'll go apeshit when Hulk Hogan won't put over Brock Lesnar or when HHH refuses to give someone the rub, these are stupid people my friends... and every time Taker refuses to pass the torch, they start chiming along with the same volatile tune. Shame nobody listens, these dirtsheets would waste a lot less time if they realized that this sort of crap is part and parcel of the industry, people don't put each over... there are no family values, it's cold hard business... and it's been happening since the beginning. Take my Father as a fine example... he got one sniff that he was going to be yesterdays news and what did he do? He just gouged away. Vince Kinkade... so full, yet so empty at the same time. Empty morals. Empty promises. He was full of both. December 24th 1980. "Kellen! Tobias! Come down here and say goodbye to your Father!" Martha Kinkade screamed at the top of her lungs, as she touched her Husband Vince on the face and kissed him softly on the lips, he looked her in the eyes... before adoring his newborn daughter Dawn. It wasn't long after the words had escaped Martha's lips, that three-year-old Toby toddled into the room, jumping into his Father's arms. "I hope you've been a good boy Tobias, because Santa is going to leave lots of gifts if you have." Vince said with a smile. "I was good daddy!" Toby exclaimed... as his Father kissed him on the cheek and placed him back down. As Tobias rushed back into the other room to play... a slightly older boy walked quietly into his Father's view down the hall... his head hung low. "Kellen." Martha said sternly to her son "Now you come and say goodbye to your Father!" Kellen marched slowly down the hall, before raising his head to look his Father in the eye, he was the spitting image of his old man, blonde hair... blue eyes, he was a Kinkade... of that there was no doubt. "Something wrong son?" Vince uttered, as he packed his WWC world title into a duffel bag. "No sir..." Kellen said politely. "Come on now son, you know that I can tell when you aren't being straight." Vince spoke sternly, folding his arms. "Well..." Kellen sighed, "I just wanted to know if you'd be home for Christmas, daddy?" Martha looked to Vince, and narrowed her eyes. "Yes son..." Vince assured "Daddy will be home." Vince rubbed Kellen on the head to reassure him. "You promise!?" Kinkade asked with delight. Vince paused for a second. "I promise... now go on, go play with your brother." Kellen marched away with a broad smile on his face. Martha looked to Vince as he lifted his duffle bag over one shoulder, and shook her head. "Damnit Vince... don't you do this to us again, I can deal with spending Christmas alone... but not the boys, don't you make promises to them that you can't keep." Vince leant forward and kissed her on the forehead, before turning and opening the door. "I'll be here." He uttered, pulling the door shut. December 25th 1980. "Hot damn Vince! You were amazing out there... these people can't get enough of you!" Jimmy Valentine smiled, the ripples in his chin wobbling like jelly as he rubbed his hands together with delight. "You weren't saying that this time five years ago." Vince sneered as he tossed the WWC championship belt onto a bench and turned on the locker room shower. "Bah!" Valentine snorted "Five years ago is five years ago Vince, I made a mistake... we all do from time to time." "You maybe, not me." Vince laughed to himself, as Jimmy's face screwed up. "Ah now Vince, that's not exactly true... least, I don't think Johnny Hanson would agre-ERK" Valentine said no more; instead he tried to breath whatever air he could... as Vince Kinkade pinned him to the wall by the throat. "Like I said Jim, I don't make mistakes... John Hanson wasn't a mistake, it was a noble act... he was killing this fucking promotion, he had to be stopped... so I stopped him good." Jimmy continued to struggle and gasp... trying to utter "Please!" between breaths. Kinkade scoffed. "Look at you now Jimmy, five years ago you would've kicked my ass for this... but now you're just a fat old man, who has me to thank for his easy life." Kinkade released his grip, as Jimmy breathed hard and rubbed his neck. "Alright man! Alright... easy now, you're right... I don't got anybody else to thank but you Vince." Jimmy spluttered... doing his best to kiss up to the champion. "Damn straight... now get out of here Valentine, I've gotta shower and get home to my family." Jimmy opened his mouth, shocked. "Come on now Vince! Don't be telling me that you're just gonna head off home after an epic performance like that! We gotta get some drinks in, celebrate our prosperities." Jimmy said, being as demanding as possible while at the not time trying to sound too pushy. "Like I said Valentine, home, wife and kids." Vince insisted. "Ah... be a sport Vince, just one for the road... for old time's sake! C'mon man... it's Christmas, what harm can one drink do?" Kinkade gritted his teeth, anything to get rid of Valentine's whining voice. "Alright Jimmy, but just one... now please, go away." Vince uttered, sitting on a bench and unlacing his boots. "Just one." Vince assured himself, as he undressed and stepped into the shower. December 25th 1980. "Merry Christmas boys! Gee... look at all this stuff that Santa brought, you boys must have been really good this year!" Martha Kinkade held baby Dawn in her arms as her two sons, Tobias and Kellen, opened their presents. But while Tobias smiled and laughed on Christmas day, Kellen's face was filled with sadness. "Kellen honey, what's wrong?" Martha spoke softly. "It's daddy." Kellen sighed "He said he'd be here...he promised." Martha looked to the Heaven's for a second. "I know sweetheart." She said touching him on the side of the face "But sometimes promises have to be broken, for whatever reason daddy isn't here, it must be important." Kellen went on opening his presents, as Martha sighed... it was another Kinkade family Christmas, without Vince. December 25th 1980. The taste of his own vomit. "Fuck." Vince Kinkade groaned... rubbing his head and wiping his face, he had the worst hangover in recorded history... and had woken up in a pool of his own vomit. "Looks like you outdid yourself again Vince." A voice beside him spoke out. Vince looked across the sheets of a bed on which he was laying, and saw the face of Louanne Page. Louanne was a loose woman, in fact... loose was probably the kinder term, she was a hooker... a prostitute and generally a slut with which most of New Jersey had been acquainted. Vince Kinkade's eyes rolled into the back of his head as he collapsed back into his vomit covered pillow... as he fell back into unconsciousness... the last thing he saw was a calendar, a calendar with a date. December 25th "Shit." Vince uttered, passing out.
