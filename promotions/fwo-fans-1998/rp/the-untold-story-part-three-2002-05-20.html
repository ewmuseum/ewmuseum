The Untold Story(part three)

20 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Note: This(entire story) all took place about two months ago. Excuse the delay, but, real life kept me away from my writing. Enjoy! --- "I can't stand the rain. Because when it rains it always seems to... pour." Sean had jumped in his 'Jeep: Grand Cherokee', narrowly escaping the rain. The forecast didn't call for it, so he immediately assumed it'd only last a couple of minutes, but as the rain picked up, and the drops got louder and louder, dropping harder and harder on his windshield, he decided to eighty- six the rest of his plans, and spend the rest of the day at his apartment - in the arms of Ivy. He had just finished the last promo he was gonna cut, for his upcoming matchup with Kraven 'the Hunter', and he simply wanted to rest. Who knows? Maybe he could even talk Ivy into working out with him, he thought. His cell phone rang. Sean hated talking on those things while driving. It was bad enough, that they led to brain cancer, but to give it an extra advantage? He waited for it to stop completely, before taking off his pouch to check the number. "Eli? wonder what he wanted?" He asked himself as he turned his attention back to the road. Sean paid it no mind. And, the fact that the blinking red light, signalled Eli Flair leaving a message meant little to him, too. Flair probably wanted Sean to watch some footage of some of the mayhem he was wreaking over in the fWo. Stevens pulled into his parking lot... and, into an available space, as he happily jumped out of the truck, with aspirations of surprising Ivy in his head. He imagined her reaction, as he pulled out his keys, and walked up the stairs. She'd be surprised. ...scratch that... REALLY surprised. She certainly wouldn't be expecting him, so her initial reaction would probably be that of a running, leaping hug. A hot, passionate kiss. Or... even better. Stevens grinned as he stuck his key in the door. "Ivy... I'm ho--" "Ivy?" Probably sleep, he thought. It was still fairly early, and Ivy - for all her hard work, and dedication - never got a fair amount of sleep. Maybe she's finally getting the rest she deserves? He wondered, as he walked into the bedroom. No Ivy. Sean began to worry. Where could she be? She wasn't the cooking type, so the thought of her going to the grocery store to purchase food was out of the question. She had told Sean the night before, that she didn't want to be recognized... that she, wanted to remain low key... so he automatically assumed... going out into the public was out of the question. Sean walked back into his kitchen, to look for any traces of evidence. That's when he noticed it... ... the letter. He grabbed it... read it. Read it, again. And, again. It was what he thought it was. What he expected it to be. And, worse... it did what he thought it would do. "God no..." Stevens picked up his cell phone, and clicked on the 'message' button. Maybe Eli knew something he didn't know. ****** Reagan National Airport. Flight #45 to Hollywood, California. Tape Recorder in hand. Triple X's voice was low. Steady. Firm. Occasionally, he looked around. Not out of nervousness... but, simply out of respect. He didn't want to interrupt anyone's peaceful flight, just like he didn't want anyone interrupting his rather... unique... form of a promo. "In my seven- or- eight years in this business... I've seen 'em all. Faced them all. Beat them all. My biggest nemesis' were the hardest challenges. First there was Flair. Everybody knows that story by now, I'm sure. Then, there was Shawn 'SUPERMAN' Stevens. And, while... physically... he couldn't compare... mentally... he was always one step ahead. Whether it was by lining five guys up in various areas of an arena to jump me. Or he and his punk a## brother cutting my hair." Sean grimaced at the thought. But, as he spoke into his tape recorder... his voice became even more stern. "I've made millions off of my body. My ability to take extreme situations and make them even more extreme. And, the fact that I'm too stubborn to stay down, often leads people to believe I'm impervious to pain. Well, I do hurt. "I was hurt before I went into that coma four years ago. "I was hurt when Eli Flair jumped down on my shoulders, knocking them out of their sockets in our famous respect match. My point? This is my dream. This business. Being a wrestler. And, the fact that I've been in the fWo for little over three months now, and have had ZERO impact... hurts even worse. "Yeah... I've been through the wars. Yeah... I've made a name for myself elsewhere, as one of the best performers to ever lace up a pair of boots. "So? "I'm nothing here. I haven't proven anything to anybody, and I've got too much pride to expect any favors because of who I used to be. I'm simply using my past as motivation. "And, when I return to the fWo... I'm gonna stop talking about proving myself... and, actually start... proving myself. "You better believe it." Sean pressed the stop button on the recorder. And, rested his head on the pillow that the flight attendant provided him with, in hopes of getting a bit of sleep... before facing what was to come.
