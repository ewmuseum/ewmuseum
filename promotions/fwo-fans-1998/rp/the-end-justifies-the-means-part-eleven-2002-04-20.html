the end justifies the means, Part Eleven

20 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Saturday, November 24, 2001: Fallout. Jon Crisp was backstage for the first time in over two months. Many of the wrestlers had come over and said hello. Erik Kelly walked over, and interjected himself. "This time, Jon, you're on your best behavior. Or you deal with ME." "Don't worry," Jon said, smiling. "I'm over whatever the heck that was months ago. I don't even remember what happened." "Don't make me refresh your memory," Kelly said quietly, looking down at Crisp. Crisp nodded, and Kelly walked away. As Kelly came out of view, someone Crisp wanted to see did. Chris Kidman, Jr. Crisp immediately walked over to Chris, and shook his hand. Chris moved his hamburger from his right hand to his left, and shook Jon's hand. Jon was left with mustard on his hand. Crisp looked at Wretch's hamburger, and noticed there was no bun on it. "...sup wit dat?" Crisp asked. "Oh, I don't eat a bun," Wretch replied. "A hamburger doesn't need a bun to be a hamburger." "Oh, I see," Crisp replied. "You've never been to McDonald's. Grimace says that a hamburger is a all-beef patty in-between two pieces of bread." "Where?!" "It's somewhere, I'm sure. Either way, you're wrong." "Probably am. I dropped the bun anyway, so I just decided to eat the burger itself. Y'know, we should feud over this! Screw Vic Creed!" Crisp didn't quite know what to make of it, until Chris began to laugh. Jon laughed with him, as Universal came up to them. "Chris, your spot's coming up." "Great. I better go wash my hands. I'll see you tomorrow night Jon. And it's a pleasure to work with you." "The pleasure's all mine," Jon replied. Chris walked away, and Universal stood with Crisp. "How's it feel to be back, Jon?" "I missed it. Missed it a lot. Thank you for bringing me back." "Hey, we've got some solid programs coming up. Glad we could use you." At that point, Universal motioned behind him. Someone came from the shadows. Crisp peered out past the fWo owner, and saw the person he wanted to see the most. Austin Davis. Davis walked over, and shook Jon's hand. The two conversed about the angle, and about some ideas for coming shows. At this point, the angle would start shortly after the new year. Crisp came away from the conversation very optimistic. But Austin Davis had other ideas.
