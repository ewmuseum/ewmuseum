Meeting Publico - Part Three

11 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ON THE ROAD... To 'fWo Uprising'. Publico stared at the road ahead of him. His job today was to chauffuer them around town, picking up new clothes from second hand stores. He had toiled away the night before, getting them better contracts with even better pay so they could afford to live a lavish lifestyle and they decide they wanted to shop at the Salvation Army. They were now dawning their unwashed, faded tee-shirts circa 1986, while Brian sat at the wheel. The black BMW sped down the highway. Brian was humming his favorite show tunes under his breath, drowning out the incessant chatter in the background. What never, No never, He's never sick at seaaaaaaaaaaa! - Some showtune only Brian Publico would able to name. "Dude, we there yet? I gotta' use the can." Nate said, leaning over the driver's seat from the back, his seat belt unbuckled. The stench of his unwashed grey Ninja Turtles shirt, picked out from the bottom of the bundle at the thrift store, choked him. He put a hand over his mouth and nose, then replied, "Nate..." he gasped for air, "please, sit down, there is a rest stop ten miles from here." What the young, strapping Brian Publico did not know was that Nate, due to being whacked in the groin some twenty times by Pete Borst, had severe bladder problems. So when he has to go, it's then and there. Waiting ten miles was not an option. Brian was back to focusing his attention on the road, when a loud 'zipping' noise was heard. He rose an eyebrow. "What the f-" Oh, (Micky Mouse Face). That (Micky Mouse Face)er. Turning around, Brian jammd his foot down on the brake pedal, sending Steve-O, who was seated on the passengers' side, flying towards the dash board. Brian's eyes widened. Nate was pressing his pelvis up against the open window, his head flung black in an orgasmic state. "Oooooooooooh yeaaaaaaaaah... BABY!" *** "Sorry officer, my client is mentally handicapped." The BMW was parked on the side of the road. Brian stood, humiliated. The cop had just returned from his police cruiser with his book of tickets and dug in his pocket, producing a pen. "Uh huh, sure." David, the police officer, responded sarcastically. "Take a look for yourself." Brian moved out of his view. There, next to the BMW, was Pretty Fly. Nate was frantically trying to mace his brother in the eyes, defending against Steve-O's attack with the officer's billy club. Both of which they stole while David's back was turned. "...Sorry, I didn't, uh... yeah... sorry..." As the officer sauntered back to his cruiser, feeling bad that he was so inconsiderate to the needs of the mentally challenged, Brian Publico smiled. "Phew. Well, atleast their idocy is good for something."
