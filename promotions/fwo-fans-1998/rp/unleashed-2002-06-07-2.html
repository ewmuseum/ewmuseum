Unleashed

7 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Buffalo, NY June 7th, 2002 5:22 PM This is the weekend. The matches, the audience, the ratings. All will hold, or all will break. There is no inbetween. Sipping a bottle of water betwixt blood- crusted fingers, Eli Flair smiles. His money is on everything holding. Buffalo, NY is still somewhat cold, compared to the rest of the country. Eli Flair is sitting in a small diner with his feet up on the opposite side's seat. He's waiting for Lance Knight and Tempest - he's meeting them for lunch today before they take a look at the sights. That's the beauty of this particular stretch of shows - two full days off. Two full days off, in a row, in the wrestling business, doesn't happen that often. Eli's been taking some abuse on this house show tour -nightly matches against opponents with the brute force of Grendel, with the tremendous versatility of Triple X, and Lance Knight. Lance Knight.... who seems to make up new suplexes on the spot, three seconds before tossing ya. He leans back some more, tying his hair behind his head with a rubber band. Monday night is a one- on- one match against Grendel. Neither man, as far as he's been told, are a part of the Internet Tournament, so there's nothing to look down the road at. Good, said Eli to himself, I'd rather defend my Hardcore title as often as my body lets me instead of worrying about the Internet title. "Y'ready to order yet, hun?" asked the waitress. Eli shakes his head no, but motions for her to get him a cup of coffee, and begins to go over tomorrow night's match in his head. Still, he's distracted. Monday. Three days away. Time to blow this thing wide open. Eli smiles.
