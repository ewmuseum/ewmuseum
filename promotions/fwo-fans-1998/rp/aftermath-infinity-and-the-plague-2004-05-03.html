Aftermath, Infinity and the Plague

3 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Donaven Winters hated hospitals. Ever since he had been placed in his first mental facility when he was sixteen, he had an extreme disdain for them. They were too clean, too white, too sterile. The exact opposite of everything he had ever known. As he walked down the hallway towards the Intensive Cure Unit, nurses and orderlies stared at the man dressed solely in black. His head was bowed, staring at the alternating white and blue tiles on the floor. Each color represented a different section of the hospital. It went halfway up the walls and alternated with white on the floors. Blue was Intensive Care. Green stood for the normal rooms. Red stood for the emergency. Pink was the nursery for newborns and mothers in labor. The blue tiles helped distract him from the white and everything else that was going on around him. He could hear faint screaming from a floor below him, someone had been rushed into the emergency room. He reached the large protective doors and rang for the orderly. A tired woman's voice crackled through the ancient intercom system. "Yes?" "I'm here to see Miles," Winters replied, keeping his voice low. Winters waited and the woman's voice came back through. "Are you family?" "No. I'm a... friend. A close friend," he replied. "One second," she said. A large buzz echoed through the hallway and the door slowly slid open. Winters sidestepped through the doorway as soon as he could fit and started down the hallway. As Winters moved down the hall, his head kept a swivel and checked the nameplates on each of the doors. Finally he found it. "421: Miles, Craig" Winters pushed the door open and slid inside. The lights in the room were off, the shades drawn closed. The television and EKG gave off the only forms of light in the room. The Price is Right and Bob Barker's voice coming in and out with the monotone blips from the EKG. Miles was conscious, barely, but awake nonetheless. He was staring straight up, the neck brace preventing any sort of side-to-side movement. Winters stood over him, a grim look on his face. Miles looked into Winters' dark eyes and a smile brightened his otherwise downtrodden face. "Four-twenty-one, man," he coughed. "One off." Winters chuckled lightly. He was speechless for the moment, looking down at his fallen friend. Though he didn't need to say anything. He produced a Sharpie from his duster pocket and moved to the wall that Craig faced. Winters quickly went to work and began to draw on the white wall, dirtying its perfection with black lines and smudges. ∞ "Get well soon, my friend," Winters spoke. "For the aftermath of Deacon's actions will be furious. Deacon shall be hit like a Plague. He will be covered from head to toe and nothing will be able to cure him. Nothing will stop me from turning the aftermath into a nightmare. Rest easy Craig, our best days are still far ahead of us." Winters stood back up, in his heart he knew Miles would probably never compete again, but this was the one thing he was going to keep his spirits high on. After all, the aftermath was shocking, but Miles and Winters still had infinity.
