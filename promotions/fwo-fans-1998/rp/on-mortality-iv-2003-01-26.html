On Mortality IV

26 January 2003

So, go to http://www.digitallylegit.com/On%20Mortality%20IV.wma How can it be that so unliving a thing can torment me with such constancy? How is it that a sharpened steel edge would take tongue and speak, calling my name? How am I to react when such a thing does so, troubling my mind with all that is not supposed to be and yet all that is. Quiet! You shall torment me no longer. To be, or not to be, that is the question: Whether ‘tis nobler in the mind to suffer The slings and arrows of outrageous fortune, Or to take arms against a sea of troubles And by opposing, end them. To die, to sleep— No more No more. No more! No longer shall you call my name. Your voice, mute now to the only ears that should receive it, will be heard nevermore. My ears are deaf to you and all the suffering you sometime had want to interject. I loathe the very mold that made you and lay claim to your creation no longer. Those that thrust you forth to this world, never ready for your presence, shall hate their very names when they know you. They shall hate you as I do. You who’s taken so much and given so very little. There is no beauty in thee, as it has been lost eternal. What have you to say for yourself? And still you mockingly call my name, torturing my mind with your games I had no intentions of ever playing! Cease and allow the life I had before my wayward strokes enlivened you and gave you voice! You are steel and ink! The delicacies of my humanity are far beyond the scope of your knowing. And yet…you know my immortality all too well. From this day forth, I shall question me no longer. The doubts that cloud my mind bring vivacity to your resolve no more. I am Jack Lundin. iamenormous.
