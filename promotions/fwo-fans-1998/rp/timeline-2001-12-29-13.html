12/29/2001 9:00AM "Hello sir, do you have your membership card?" He was pretty well spoken for a cement head, the Strangler thought. Most of the guys working the front desk of a gym sounded like they had a mouth full of marbles, and a brain the size of one. They spent so much time lifting weights that they never took a few moments to exercise their mind. Guys like that didn't last long in the wrestling world... "Err... no actually," he replied. "Just looking for a one day pass if you have one..." "Sure. It's 8 dollars, full use of the facilities including steam room and jacuzzi. Would you like a tour?" Ugh. Not a sales pitch. Smart move though, putting the bright bulb on the front desk to try to snare as many customers as he could... "No thanks. We're from out of town and just looking for a workout for today." After a quick run through of the rules of the gym, and a last ditch effort at a sales pitch, the Strangler was shown to the locker room, and left to himself to unpack his gear. He saw a few other men wander in and out, but for a downtown location, the place didn't seem particularly busy... especially for a Saturday. A movement at the far end of the room caught his attention, and he glanced over his shoulder before heading toward it, sliding his bag along the bench. There was a door to an office, left slightly ajar, and from behind it, he could hear two men talking. He softly placed his bag in front of the closest locker to the door that he could find, and pretended to be busy unpacking as he caught bits of the conversation. "Jack, I'm asking you as a friend... just let me know. That kid got beat up pretty badly last night, and a few of the boys are wondering if you're taking things a little too far..." "Sully, c'mon... I swear to you, I had nothing to do with that. You know my business, and I've always been up front with you and the boys. You think I want to lose the best police protection money can buy?" Police protection? That was interesting... "Well other than a loose connection... a wrestler who happened to be at the bar, and is staying at the same hotel that the kid works... I've got nothing. But I had to ask, you know... just to be sure." "Sul... you gotta trust me. I wouldn't lie to you." The conversation became a bit louder, as the two men stepped towards the door. The Strangler moved quickly, pulling his shirt over his head just in time to pretend to change, concealing his face from the police officer who left the room. He recognized the face as that of the officer who had questioned him the night before. He continued to undress, pulling his workout gear out of the bag and putting it on before he turned to find himself face to face with a man in his 50's. Slightly overweight, slightly balding, and wearing a bright blue running suit. The guy looked so much like a character in a bad mob movie, that he was almost laughable. "Whadda you lookin at?" he said, nodding his head at the Strangler. "We were going to ask you the same thing." "I'm Jack Cozens... I own this place," he said, waving his hands slightly as he spoke. "I haven't seen you around here before... but you do seem kind of familiar." The Strangler shrugged, "We were looking for a place to work out, this seemed as good as any... we're just in town for a little while." "Well then, don't let me keep you from anything now. Move along..." Jack said with a small sneer on his face. "No problem." the Strangler said, tossing his bag and street clothes into the locker, before shutting it and bolting it with a padlock. "We've already paid enough to work out here, so we might as well make the most of it." "It's worth every penny..." he heard Jack call out as the two men headed in different directions. "Worth every dollar too." Of course, if dollars were minutes, this wanna-be gangster wouldn't have been worth the two seconds that it would have taken to break his jaw. But despite wanting to bring his own private investigation to a conclusion... the Strangler decided not to probe any further into the situation... at least not here. Again, he thought of what Universal had said to him. "Let someone else stand up for what was right. Let someone else be the knight in shining armor... let someone else have the spotlight.". Well, here was a chance to let whoever wanted to be involved take the risks that were associated with being in the position he had grown so accustomed to over the past few years. There was no real use to playing hero now anyway... It wasn't his problem.
