Chasing The Dream - Part 7

16 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
May 17th 1999 - 12:05PM Milo, Earl, and Dorothy pulled up to a fair sized farmhouse, and stopped at the top of a long driveway. Although it was still mid-afternoon Milo was very tired, and it was obvious. "You look tired dear, although we don't have any extra beds you are welcome to sleep in the loft" The woman said as she smiled at Milo. He was uncertain as to weather she was kidding or not, but he agreed to the offer. Dorothy and Earl had a great laugh, as they entered the house and signaled for Milo to follow. They led him to a spare bedroom that he would be staying in. Milo was so pleased with the turn of events that in a matter of moments he was sound asleep in the comfortable bed. Milo tossed and turned in a state of deep sleep, remembering the events which had occurred the previous evening... He could see the mans face... He watched the blood trickle out of the young boy's body... Unable to do anything, he was frozen... It seemed as if he was invisible... No matter how loud he yelled the man would not stop hitting the boy... Milo could not get off the train, and he saw the men's accomplices running toward him... Closer they got, but Milo was unable to run from them... Milo awoke with a scream, his body drenched in in a cold sweat. "It was just a dream" he told himself as he struggled to calm his trembling body. Dorothy now stood next to Milo and put a hand on his shoulder to attempt to comfort him. "What's the matter my dear? I heard you scream". "Its nothing, just a bad dream" Milo insisted. A few hours had passed, and it was now time for dinner. Milo was still shaken up by the bad dream, and was sort of embarrased because he had awoke in such a manner. As Milo entered the kitchen he made an attempt to greet Earl. "Hey there! Whats up?" Earl replied with a grunt, not paying much attention to anything but the news paper he was reading. The three sat and ate a wonderful meal and held a conversation involving several world issues. "Earl must be very political," Milo thought as he shovelled the remaining mound of potatos into his mouth. The table was cleared, and Milo helped Earl with some work around the farm until late that evening. After the work was finished, they returned to the house and watched a few hours of television before calling it a night. Tomorrow was to be a very busy day...
