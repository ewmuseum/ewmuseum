Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[TV-HC] {In black and white.} (VL is driving in her jeep in the middle of the day in Las Vegas, Nevada, the sight of this Wednesday's Countdown program. "What's My Name" by DMX is booming out of the vehicle's sound system, as VL has one hand on the steering wheel and the other hand pushing her hair, which is up in a ponytail, away from her face. She is wearing sunglasses, a leather jacket and leather pants. As the song changes to Dr. Dre's "What's the Difference", VL begins to speak.) Look who got the belt once again! (VL pulls the fWo Hardcore title belt out of the shotgun seat of the jeep and holds it up to the camera.) Hellrazor, you didn't stand a chance against me. I beat ch'ass all over Chicago, then raped that belt away from you. I broke you down physically and mentally. And when I hit that Illegal Activity onto all them chairs, it was OVER. You were DEAD, muthafucka. You thought that making me bleed would've ended it? It hasn't fucked me up for the past 20 years, and it won't fuck me up for the next 20. You thought that cat would give you some good luck, old man? Anytime you step in the ring with VL, you're gonna have bad luck dripping from your veins. However, seeing as how this is still the time of giving, I'ma show you that I DO have a bone of kindness in my body. This Friday, the fWo is gonna be having a show in Seattle, Washington. I'll give you a rematch, but it's gonna be on MY terms. What does that mean? Well, let's just say that you better not be afraid of heights, cause I'ma take you to heights that you've never witnessed before. This Friday. (VL continues on her journey, as the scene fades to black.)
