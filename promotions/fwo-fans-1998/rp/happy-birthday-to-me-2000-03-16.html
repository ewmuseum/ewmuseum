Happy Birthday To Me.

16 March 2000

fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Happy Birthday To Me. March 16, 2000 The water was calm and steady. It barely showed signs of movement as the dark sky overhead reflected off of it. The water was clear, and through it you could see his skin colored flesh as he stood in the middle of the pool. Water trickled down his forehead as he grinned deviously; staring into the camera with a hint of evil in his eyes. His head was just above the water, with the camera taking in the background of some small plants near poolside. He brought up a bottle of champagne from within the depths of the water before popping the cork out. He held the bottle up to his mouth and chugged back a portion of the champagne, before wiping the wetness from his lips. Slowly, he let out a evil laugh before he commenced speaking. Tonight I speak to you from a new location... I thought it was time for a "change". A breath of fresh dea... er, I mean air, so to speak. I have a few things to talk about tonight. A little of this, a little of that... first things first, however. Body Count. Thirty wrestlers will walk in, one will walk out the fWo World Champion. Whether it is myself, or Scott, or Mitch or Hiroshima, one way or the other one of us will walk away with the fWo World Title in our possession... and at CyberSlam, the fWo will come to an end. The fWo will breathe it's last breath. The fWo will hold it's final event... with one of us as it's final World Champion. We will see who will step up to the plate and accept their comeuppance. It's going to happen. Whether you fight or don't fight... it will happen. Resistance is futile. Onto other matters. Today is my birthday. I'd like to wish a happy birthday to myself. Happy Birthday, me. Sunday, I'll give myself a gift when I take care of business. I've been informed by Jeremiah Frankton that due to what happened on Warfare, they've booked me against The Black Panther for Sunday. Panther, you're a dead man. You once thought you would save the fWo, but you have yet to realize that nobody can save the fWo from us. Not the Strangler, not you, nobody. No one. Nothing. Dead began to move, and thus the water began to stir. He walked up the stairs, emerging from the depths of the water to a table nearby. The camera followed from behind, as Dead continued to speak. And if none of you have realized this, you all will at Body Count when one of the four of us emerges from that very ring the fWo World Time. Dead turned back to the table; and upon closer inspection it revealed a birthday cake with a bunch of candles, not yet lit. Whether it be Mitch or Hiroshima for their first reign, or myself for my third reign, or Scott for his unprecendented fifth reign, it will happen. Dead procured a match from nearby, before striking it upon the table and lighting each and every one of the candles. He then blew them all out, before looking into the camera. Trust me. He held the match up to his face, before blowing it out. It appeared as if the single solitary match that he grasped in his hand was in essence the fWo. In the palm of his hand. And when he blew, the flame ceased to exist. It was almost as if the flame represented what little hope the fWo had left, and it seemed as if the days of the fWo were now numbered.
