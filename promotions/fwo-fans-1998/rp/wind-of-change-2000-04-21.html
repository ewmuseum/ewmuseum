Wind Of Change.

21 April 2000

fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Wind Of Change. April 21, 2000 taped 4.19.00 The stars shine bright above the Staples Center in downtown Los Angeles... no, wait. This is L.A., and it's so damn smoggy that the stars are blotted out from the night sky. But nonetheless, outside in the cold, the dark, and the wind that blows random pieces of trash across the concrete of the parking lot, stands a silhouette. After a moment, the silhouette steps forward to reveal Perfect, wearing sweat pants, a leather jacket, and an as-of-yet non-visible shirt. Perfect turns to the camera. Collins. Perfect begins walking across the parking lot, headed out towards the street - which is quite a distance from the hulking stadium in the background. The camera follows him. Let's take a little trip back in time, if you will. One year ago. The fWo was in a bit of turmoil, as is traditional. Falcon had just made his controversial return. Austin Davis, Chris Universal, and the WWF's own Cactus Jack were mixing it up in a series of interpromotional incidents. The champions included VL, Brandi, Austin Davis, Black Panther, Chaos, Black Quicksilver... and myself. And most of all, the fWo was wondering exactly what the deal was with this huge man hanging around with the Dark Riders... and calling himself Death. Perfect walks on, steam rising up from the tarmac behind him and bathing him in a ghostly glow. The main event for CyberSlam II - Perfect unbuttons the leather jacket, revealing the official t-shirt of CyberSlam II: Path of Champions, which shows he and Collins across from one another amongst a group of superstars. - was scheduled to be the winner of No Escape - Scotty Cool - and the World Heavyweight Champion, Scott Slugger - who had won the belt from Cool two months previous. So CyberSlam rolled around with its traditional fanfare, as is deserved by the biggest pay-per-view in the world. The fans cheered, the fireworks exploded, and two titles changed hands. The crowd was rabid by the time it was time for the main event. Scott Slugger came out first, holding that big gold belt high. Scott Collins didn't show up - apparently he had been injured by Stevie Roberts. Or so we were led to believe. Death came out next, and what happened afterwards will be etched in fWo history for all time. As Perfect walks through the steam of the night, the wind begins to pick up, tattering the white fog and sending it on its way. It's been one year. Perfect stops. VL is the Hardcore Champion. Brandi is retired and married to Rudo. Austin Davis is the World Champ. Black Panther is embroiled in a bitter feud with Havok. Chaos are still Scott Collins' thugs. Black Quicksilver is teaming with Mike Heftel. And myself? Well, I'm Perfect. And me and Collins still hate each other's guts. Perfect begins walking on again, now having to make a bit of an effort to move forward against the rising wind. The Scorpions put it best. "The wind of change Blows straight into the face of time." The wind of change has made its way through the fWo time and time again in the last year. Courage, Eron, Rain, Mike Bear, Lance Star, Havok, Ric Chronos, Erik Kelly, Neo, and Crusader have all made their way into the fWo. From last year's card, Brandi, Falcon, Chris Universal, Prophet, Fathom, Oracle, Melissa Smalls, Waru, Shocker, Showstopper, Speed Demon, Crucifix, and Wild Thing have all retired. Joy and the owner of the fWo, Travis Beaven, have passed on. But through all that time, the flame of my hatred for Scott Collins - and his for me - has burned on, even as so much from last year has been extinguished by that wind of change. The wind begins to die down around Perfect, and he makes his way through the groundfog unimpeded. But just as all things, Collins, have a beginning - all things have an end. In just over a week, that flame of hate between me and you will finally be put out. It'll happen at CyberSlam. Where men become heroes... where heroes become legends... where legends become immortal. I'm up to the challenge, Collins. Are you? Are you man enough to step up to the plate and show that you have what it takes to defeat me at the event that defines this business? I don't think you are. I think you're a nothing, a nobody. I don't think you have what it takes to prove yourself on the biggest proving ground in the world. CyberSlam. Get ready to become a part of history, Scotty. Perfect has finally made his way across the lot, reaching his trademark Viper. He stops at the driver's side door. A year ago you beat me. Things change. This year... you're mine. Perfect slides into the car. A moment later, the red taillights illuminate through the fog, and the Viper speeds off, heading into tomorrow.
