The big game...

12 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization The big game... January 12, 2000 ::The scene re-opens with a knock on the door, Wretch runs over to it excited. The door opens to Havok, grinning, carrying his Cruiserweight title, his Playgirl magazine, and a brochure to Sea World.:: Wretch: Whoo-hoo! Mystery Date time! Dude, this is gonna be SICK! ::Havok sets his stuff down on a table.:: Havok: I know, we haven't played this in like 15 years, man. Wretch: And after this I can have your Cruiserweight Title! Havok: In your dreams, dude, I always beat you at Mystery Date. I'm the "Mystery Date King!" Wretch: Whatever, let's just play. Havok: Alright. ::They kneel down next to the table, and Wretch takes out the game.:: Wretch: Alright dude, prepare to go down at the hands of the Mystery Date God... ::Wretch goes to pull a card, but Havok stops him.:: Havok: Mystery Date God? You're looking a the Cruiserweight Champion... and just for that comment, I'm asking the question... ::Wretch opens his mouth to say something, but decides Havok is the only guy who'll play the game with him, so decides against it.:: Havok: Alright Wretch... You're at the prom, and the guy you like is dancing with another girl! What do you do?! Wretch: Hmmm... let's see... I, oh... I assess the situation carefully... Havok: And??? Wretch: Dude, I have to do more? I forgot how hard this game was. Havok: Just go, dude. Wretch: Alright, alright! I'm going. Anyway, I, uh... assess the situation carefully. If the girl is smaller than me, I'd slap that bitch like the skank she is. But, if she's bigger than me, I'll go up to her and politely ask if I can cut in. Then, if I can't, I call up Bear, and have him bring Muffin. That dog is sick, dude! Then, Muffin'll teach that chick not to mess with my man! Havok: Uh... okay. I'll let you move up a few spaces. ::Wretch moves it up about ten spaces. Havok slowly counts, but just ignores it since he really doesn't care.:: Havok: Alright, go. ::Wretch pulls a card and starts to read it.:: Wretch: Alright, your dream guy just asked you to the prom. What do you... what's that word, dude? ::Havok looks at the card.:: Havok: It's "wear," buddy. That's okay, it's a tough one. Wretch: Oh... what do you wear? ::Havok scratches his chin, and then his forehead. He then rubs his stomach and pats his head at the same time, seemingly in deep thought. His eyes light up.:: Havok: I've got it, dude! Alright, I'll wear that long, blue dress... the one with the lace top, and all the ruffles. Then I'll put that light blue bow in my hair, and carry that navy blue purse. The one with the extra hidden pockets, where I keep my things for when I have woman trouble. ::Wretch looks perplexed.:: Wretch: You mean that fork you carry, in case they attack you after a date? Havok: NO! Not just a fork. Not a spoon either. It's a spork! Like at Taco Bell! Wretch: You've got a point, dude. Alright, since sporks rule, you can take... damn, let me think back to my Ultra Violet counting video... uh... C spots! ::Havok moves up like 20 spaces.:: Havok: There ya go, man! Only two more spaces until I get to open the door and see my mystery date! That title is mine, I show no mercy! Wretch: Alright, my turn. Maybe if the game takes a while, I can ask my mom and we can have a sleepover. Havok: Dude, we can't have sleepovers now. We don't like each other, remember? Wretch: Oh yeah! We could lose our jobs if we are friendly. Havok: That's right, ya dirty punk! ::He winks at Wretch, who winks back.:: Wretch: Just draw the card, you... you... bad man! Havok: Fine! ::He picks up the card and reads it.:: Havok: Alright. Oooh! The Brady Bunch question! Alright. You agree to go to the dance with this nice guy, Charlie. But then the most popular guy in school, Doug, asks you, and you say yes to him too! What do you do?!? ::Wretch scratches his head.:: Wretch: Hmm... well, first, I... I, uh... I assess the situation! Havok: Then what, dummy? ::He winks at Wretch again, who winks back.:: Wretch: I'm getting there, stupid-head! Anyway, then I go out with Doug, the cute guy the night before. I call up Muffin, Mike Bear's dog, and have her pee in his drink, dude. Then, he drinks it and gets really sick! In a bad way, then I go to the dance with Charlie, and we have a SICK time... in a good way! Havok: Is that your final answer? Wretch: You're damn straight! Havok: Alright, move up. ::Wretch moves it up like 12 spaces and jumps up.:: Wretch: Yes! I can open the door and meet my mystery date! Whoo-hoo! Havok: Damn, then you might win. Come on, get somebody bad! ::Just as Wretch is about to open the little door, the Cruiserweight Championship belt falls off the table and crashes onto the game board. The parts and cards are sent flying all over the room.:: Wretch: Dude! Not fair! Havok: Wait... no winner! No contest! Title can only change hands on a pinfall and submission! I'm still the champion! Whoo! Go me! Wretch: Damn! Damn, damn, damn! This isn't over. Now what are you gonna do ?!? Havok: Well, I'll be back in time for our ladder match, but I'm going to Sea World! I'm buyin' me a whale! ::Havok grabs his stuff and leaves as Wretch starts to clean up the game, almost crying about his loss.::
