On the Eve of Victory.

23 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The Flying Frenchie is seated on the steps ascending to the CTA's LaSalle street station, where everyday, thousands of commuters make their way to work and back. This late at night, it's deserted, save for the fWo Womens Champion. He's wearing a thick winter coat, dark jeans, his mask, a beret, and a scarf, and is still shivering his ass off, due in large part ot the bitter cold winds that own this town. His expression is ecstatic, despite this.) It's been awhile...t'ough I remember zat it wasn't zis damned cold last time. Mon Dieu, how does anybody put up wit' zis crappy weat'er? Give me ze Riviera any day. Last time I stopped into zis city, it was for ze biggest Pay-Per-View in ze company's history. I battled Falcon, a man who'd been a t'orn in my side for mont's before disappearing. I beat him zat night, and it wasn't long before he went missing again. Zat's ze kind of power I have. Tomorrow night will not be ze biggest night in ze history of ze company, but it will certainly rank up t'ere in terms of personal glory. Tomorrow, wit'out a shadow of a doubt, I will lose my prestigious and coveted Womens championship, a title I've held for what seems like t'ree long mont's. Losing zis belt is a certainty, as I've decided zat it shall be put up in a battle royal. If only ze chicks posed more of a t'reat, I would be able to wrestle zem. But at t'eir skill levels, I'd be ze Chick Champ forever. So, out of fairness, I shall be relinquishing my title. To ensure zat ze officiating will be up to snuff, I will wear ze stripes for zat contest. It will be fun, no doubt. (The Frenchie smirks at the camera, before continuing.) Of course, Twilight will not only mark ze end of my dominance of ze Womens division. It will also be ze starting point of my recognized dominance over all of ze fWo, when I begin my second World Title reign. Zis is as definite as my loss of ze Womens Title is. I am at a bit of a disadvantage, since Monsieur Baseball himself will be getting a shot at Ice and his World Title first. Hey, wait a second! Is ze Dynasty fighting amongst zemselves? Zey're finished already? My, I didn't see zat one coming. (Again, Frenchie shoots a knowing glance at the camera before continuing.) I beat Ice just a few days back. So if he does beat Slugger, and I know he can, ze outcome of our match is entirely too obvious: Frenchie in a cakewalk. After all, I am undefeated against zat void of personality, zat giant walking mountain of suck. Wit' all of zat said, I t'ink I'd like to pass on facing him. While it would be just ending ze year by taking ze strap from ze man who stole it from me in January, it just isn't epic enough. I want Slugger. Yes, I want to face ze man who's been running from me since Invasion, when I broke him. Ze man who was handed ze fWo title upon his return, when it took me nine mont's to get anot'er shot, when I'd never lost it. Oui, it would only be right zat ze final Pay-Per-View Main Event of ze Millenium pit ze greatest, most agile, most talented, most adaptable wrestler to ever grace ze ring against ze embodiment of style over substance, Scott "Pushed to ze moon 'cause I don't mind ze kneepads" Slugger. But whet'er it be Ice or Slugger, one t'ing is clear. Zis year is ending ze same way it began, wit' ze Flying Frenchie taking home ze big gold. I promise you, putains... Je Gagnerai. (The Frenchie stands up and hails a cab, as the scene fades to black.)
