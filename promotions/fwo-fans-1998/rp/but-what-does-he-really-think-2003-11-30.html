But what does he REALLY think?

30 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just before our flight over to Canada, I got a phone call from Master Hiroshi. I'd been expecting it for days. He didn't have very much to say. Dog! You have disgraced my family! I thought I was very well-behaved. I don't know why Master Hiroshi overreacted the way he did. I treated his daughter with respect. I still do. She's an amazing woman, and I'm lucky that she's making the trip back to North America with me. I saw you put my daughter's life in danger during one of your clown shows! Obviously he missed the part where I took a fireball to the face to protect his daughter. I guess I singed the inside of my throat just for shiggles and giggles. Besides, if Michelle was really in trouble she's more than capable of taking care of herself. You are a buffoon! A fool! I can't really argue that. Yes, I'm not getting a lot of work because people don't want to work with someone who's seen as an "enhancement wrestler." That doesn't mean I don't bust my butt to put on a good show. I entertain. I might not be winning any titles in the near future, but at least I'm in the fight. As far as being a fool goes, I guess I'm guilty as charged. After the beating I took at Unleashed, you'd think I would have learned my lesson. It's pretty obvious that Diablo got the man who took steroid abuse to brave new levels back in the fWo. Nick's much bigger than I remembered. Much uglier too. That doesn't mean I'm not going after him. You stay away from my daughter! It's a little too late for that, but I don't think Master Hiroshi is in the mood to hear me say that. I didn't make Michelle come with me. She chose to come with me, and for that I'm very thankful. She was there for me after Unleashed, taking care of my cuts and burns better than any doctor could have (and I've had my fair share of doctors work me over). He didn't really say much before he hung up on me. I speak a little Japanese, and I caught him telling me to do something to myself that's pretty much physically impossible. I've had some time to let his words of wisdom sink in, and I'm pretty sure he's going to castrate me when he sees me. Guess that's one less Christmas present to buy this month.
