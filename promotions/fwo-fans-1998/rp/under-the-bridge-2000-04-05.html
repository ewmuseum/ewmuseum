Under the bridge.

5 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Under the bridge. April 05, 2000 It was past nightfall. Stars lit up the night sky, as did lights from the buildings scattered around downtown Salt Lake City, Utah. The sounds of busy traffic filled the air. Fluctuating temperatures frequently occured in the past week, in the span of three days it had gone from sixty degrees to near freezing; with snow covering the grass throughout the state. Today, it seemed like winter was gone, and Spring was finally settling in. Warmer temperatures... and despite his claims that his blood was cold, he enjoyed the warm climate. Immensely. Perhaps that is why Arizona was his preferred choice of residence. Whichever, whatever. It didn't matter. Tomorrow night was what mattered, as he and Akira Hashimoto - or as you know him, Hiroshima - had a big tag team match against Erik Kelly and Paul Revere Adams - or as you know him, The Boston Strangler. Dead had his coveted World Title laid over his shoulder, as he stood above a bridge, looking down fifteen feet to a emptyness gulley below, perhaps where a river once ran. He looked down, before he carefully crept over it, dropping to the ground. The warmness rushed his body. He felt at home. Last week, you and I fought where your parents and your mentor is gone and buried, Paul. We fell over the bridge, but nobody knows what happened from there. Let's just say it wasn't a pretty sight. For either of us. We both survived however... but I must comment on your statement from earlier tonight about I and the rest of the FTfWo trying to get rid of you. Do not flatter yourself. With a snap of my fingers, I could have you eliminated tomorrow. Tomorrow is only a day away, Paul. But like I stated in the beginning, I want to have fun. And I am... standing above your parents grave while I downed a bottle of Chardonnay was fun. Burying you six feet underground was fun. But what comes tomorrow night, will be much funner. You see, I've had this re-occuring dream the past several nights... and it always ends the same way. With you on the receiving end of the Crucifixion. Tomorrow night, one way or the other, it will happen. Whether it puts you on the injured list does not matter, I cannot wait to sink my teeth into you... when it happens, and it will happen, I will take great pleasure... and I cannot emphasize that word enough... in doing it to you. Dead leaned up underneath the viaduct, looking around at his cramped in surroundings. The city continued to bustle, as Dead spoke forth. As for your partner Kelly, tomorrow night... the rookie will be welcomed into a world of pain. A world of violence. A world of misery. My world. As far as I'm concerned, this is my world, the rest of you are all just living in it. But I won't allow you to live here for free, I'm not that kind of a being. My heart is blackened and has been for quite sometime. All of time will pass before the light shines upon me. Others will tell you what it's like to step into the ring with me, Kelly. And others will tell you it is not a day at Harvard Yard. You see, you can talk about your sports heroes all you want, but in my world, heroes do not exist. I kill heroes. I rid my world of them. And now you're trying to be a hero... and you've been targeted, just like Paul has. This is not a good thing for you, Kelly. The future does not hold anything good for you. Especially tomorrow night. You have aligned yourself with my worst enemy, and that is the worst decision you could possibly make upon entering the fWo. You must have a death wish. Tomorrow, Akira and I will grant you your wish. You will be erased from my world, and terminated. It will be an easy task, because I am a wily veteran, and you are but a mere rookie. I never thought I'd find myself quoting a sheep like Mike Bear, but you are so green, they should name a crayon after you. I think I've let it be known what is to come. And if you have any doubts on whether it will happen or not, Paul... I saw that earlier you had in your possession a book. Well, since you like reading so much... if you have any doubts that I will inflict much pain and punishment on you tomorrow evening, well, let me reassure you. Tomorrow... we will take Kelly' soul, and spit on it, and toss it aside as if he never existed. You can only blame yourself for allowing him to ride on your coattailes and stand at yourside... because in approximately twenty hours or so, his short-lived career will be ended, and you'll be twenty four days closer to your demise. Dead snarled at the camera, giving it one last glare as he walked away from the scene.The camera stayed where it was however, and as it looked up at the bridge with the night sky above it in the background, the bridge had simply two words spraypainted in black on it, the surrounding light from the camera equipment made it visable. Trust me. Dead continued to walk away from the scene, the final image of a shooting star piercing the night sky.
