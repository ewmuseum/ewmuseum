New Year's Revelations: Six - Sleepless in New York

2 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
January 1, 2003 7:14 AM "Miss--?" started the doctor, looking over a chart. "Missus," replied Angel, looking at Eli with a smile on her face, "Flair." "Mrs. Flair," replied the doctor, "You're going to have a very healthy baby." He took off his glasses and placed them in the pocket of his lab coat. "But not tonight." Eli looked at Angel and sighed, "Damn." "It's nothing to be concerned with," assured the doctor, "False labor is a common occurrance." Angel got down off the table and began to get dressed again. She looked tired. "Couldn't this kid have waited until we'd gotten a decent night's sleep?" The doctor laughed. "You were out, I take it?" "Yeah," replied Eli, "New Year's party for my company." "Well, just wait until the baby is born... a night like this'll be like a vacation. Have a happy new year." "Okay, thanks," replied Eli as he shook the doctor's hand and opened the door for him. He turned back to Angel, who had a look of sheer exasperation etched across her face. "I want this kid out of me... NOW." Eli laughed. "You're tired, sweetie... and cranky. We'll go home, sleep for a few weeks, and you'll feel better." She shook her head. "I dunno about that, Eli... I'm goin' stir crazy here." "I know... it's been rough--" "You know shit. You're not carrying this thing in front of ya. You're not over a hundred and ten pounds for the first time in your life. You're not prevented from doing what you love to do because of a kid. You--" She stopped and continued to get dressed, her eyes tearing up. Eli stood right in front of her and cupped her chin in his hand, pulling her face up to meet his. She refused to make eye contact for a few seconds, but eventually she looked at him. "Is that what this is about?" he asked, "You miss the stage?" "I--" "If that's not it, PLEASE tell me. I hate not knowing what's going on inside your head." Angel looked around the room nervously before openly bursting into tears. "I am SO SCARED about this baby! I'm afraid of treating it exactly like my mother treated me... I'm afraid it's going to drive you away... I'm afraid I'm not gonna be able to sing anymore... and that I'll hate the baby for it. I can't fucking do this--" Eli cut her off by covering her lips with his index and middle fingers. "Shhhh. Back up a bit." She looked at him expectantly. "It's a new year," he continued, wrapping his arms around her, "We just got married. And my feelings have not changed since the day I found out you were pregnant... I'm not going anywhere." He twirled her around and hugged her from behind. "I just worry about you... you're due any day now and this Canadian tour is coming up and with April gone wherever it is she goes there's really nobody I'd trust to keep an eye on you." "I've got my boys," replied Angel, finally smiling. "Your boys, huh? Who you gonna call when you go into labor? Mickey or TJ?" They both laughed, tho hers was short- lived. "Fuck," she said, "You've got a point there. But I'd also not wanna have to call up April. 'April, it's time!' 'Oh, fuck.... lemmeee smoke a cigarette. Maybe two. Or three.' We could have matching beds - me for delivering a baby and her for emphysema." "Point." They stood there embraced for another minute. "Hey," said Eli suddenly. "Hmmm?" "Why don't you come to Canada with me?" "I'd be in the way." "So bring tbe boys. Play some clubs. Make it a working vacation. Then we can be together when it's time." She didn't answer, but she squeezed his arms tightly as the sun began to creep in the window. "Happy new year, Angel."
