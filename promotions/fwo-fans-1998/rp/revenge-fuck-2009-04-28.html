Revenge Fuck

28 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Key Arena had expelled a sigh of relief, exhaling its thousands of fans and dissipating the cloud of tension that always hung heavy over any edition of reAction. Fists unclenched, eyes relaxed from their narrow, suspicious glares, and red mist gave way to a relaxing shower’s steam. Despite the calm that had descended upon the building, there was one individual whose aura infected his surroundings with unease. The deluge of toxic sludge polluting the pure, calm waters. killjoy. Where he took a step forward, his surroundings took a step back. Hastily scribbled memos were cut off mid-flow, loads were dropped, and doors suddenly slammed back shut. When he stepped out into the Seattle night, the breeze lost its refreshing crispness as it washed over him. Even through the stale, eye-watering odour of his tattered ring attire, killjoy’s mask-muffled nostrils sniffed up a familiar scent. “Come out, come out, wherever you are…” FWO’s masked liberator called out into the night. “I’ve had to scrub that flowery stench off of me many times before, so don’t think I can’t sniff you out.” “Charming,” scoffed Karina Wolfenden as she slithered her lithe frame out between two production trucks. “Is it any wonder you can’t get anyone to carpool with?” Deconstructed, the K-Wolf was far from her in-ring look. A black FWO hoodie blurred her shape, and a choppy mane on tricolour hair had been dampened and tamed with ties and clips. Devoid of camera-friendly make-up, the moon bleached her skin alabaster. “I have no use for you,” was killjoy’s blunt dismissal. “You served your purpose when I dropped you on that pretty little head of yours… and I got to see the look on Christopher’s face as you bounced off the concrete with this beautiful, perfect thud.” Gloved hands gleefully orchestrated his memory of the sound. “That’s lovely,” the K-Wolf sarcastically bit back. “Really, lovely. But… we’re not done.” “Explain,” came the intrigued reply. “Thing is, Smiler,” she explained, daring to tread closer to the masked man than most… if not all. “Cyberslam was meant to end things, but, it didn’t quite do it for me. You pretty much tried to smash my head through a wall which, at the least, deserves a singles match with a very lenient referee.” There was silence for a few, drawn out seconds. Then a cocking of the head. “Are you using me?” enquired killjoy, the tone from his toxic tongue almost sounding amused. “This wouldn’t have anything to do with my good, one-eyed friend cuddling up to the fiery little new girl now, would it? I like it. So, I’m your in-ring revenge fuck to keep the husband whipped, am I?” “You’re seriously fucked in the head is what you are,” Kari diagnosed. “Now, how about I help you with that by kicking your skull in next week?” “I’ll bite,” answered killjoy through a sinister grin. “But, if you’re expecting him to come grovelling to you… it’ll have to be during hospital visiting hours.” “Nice,“ the K-Wolf dryly uttered, concluding their meeting. “Always a pleasure.” From beneath his mask, killjoy watched as Wolfenden flashed him a mockingly polite wave before slipping back between the production trucks. “Nice body.” As a production worker casually strolled out for a cigarette break, killjoy’s head snapped menacingly around with a gnashing of teeth, frightening him off. “It’s a shame I may break it.”
