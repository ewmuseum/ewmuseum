Things to do in San Jose when you're bored

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Backstage, the fWo Roster has been, for the most part, at the arena for about three, four hours now. Check- in is 1 PM on TV days. Typical pre- show happenings are going on, card games, various personalities taping interview segments and vignettes to be inserted in the broadcast later, the usual. Eli Flair is sitting in the locker room, having already taped his promo for the night. He's holding a guidebook for San Jose, California, with his left hand, and dabbing at thin red bloody lines and points on his left forearm with his right. Similar minor lacerations cover his right forearm and hand. He's got his headphones on, with what sounds like Alice Cooper escaping the earpieces. So intent is he on his music and his reading, he doesn't notice Poison Ivy hopping up soundlessly onto the bench behind him, walking it like a tightrope, complete with outstretched arms to keep her balance. She's dressed in a black pleated skirt, an 'Elastica' baby tee, black fishnet stockings, and black socks. She figures - since the board won't be up for another hour there's no point in punishing her feet with combat boots unless it's a necessity. On the other side of them, Monte Burns notices Ivy's stalking, and immediately begins to laugh. She motions for him to be quiet, but it's too late. As she reaches to catch Eli in a sleeper hold, he's backed up and hooked her legs behind the knees, torturing her the way only a best friend can get away with. Yes.... she's mighty ticklish. "Bored, Ivy?" asked Eli, with just a touch of good- natured ribbing, "I'm sure if you asked nicely, Universal would give you something to do tonight." Ivy squirms out of his grasp and sits down next to him. He pulls his headphones down to his neck and does a half- turn so the two are sitting next to each other. She takes a look at his book for a moment. "Since when do you care about what there is to do in a town?" asked Ivy, "We're coming in Saturday Morning, we're staying for Slaughter on Sunday night, and we're leaving Monday morning." "I care," replied Eli, "because Angel and the boys are playing San Jose on Saturday night and she's sent me six passes to the show. I checked both our schedules and this is essentially a last hurrah until the end of the summer, so I'm looking for something to make it special." Ivy grabs his arm and hugs it tightly, sighing as she goes. "Awwwwwh, that's so sweet!" "What's with you, anyways?" asked Eli with a laugh, pulling away gently, "Since when are you in such a good mood?" Ivy doesn't answer, but her eyes flit to the other side of the room, where Triple X is lacing up his boots. "Ah," replied Eli, "The extra day here with Trip put a smile on your face." He playfully elbows her in the ribs. "Well," said Ivy, "If you're looking for something to do, I got something this morning from Tempest that you might like - that I know that woman of yours is interested in." Ivy unfolds a postcard- sized piece of paper and hands it to him. He reads it over and hands it back to her. "A rave, huh?" he asked, "Definitely doable. You think Trip would wanna go?" "I'll get him to come along. It's on until dawn, so I figure we could probably go to the Garden show and then hit the rave afterward. Cool? Cool." "Lovely," said Eli, "Saves me from having to find something to do late at night in San Jose." "Hey now," chided Ivy, "You live in Cali now, you've gotta learn what's there, y'know? Unless you're planning to make a dramatic return to the east coast." "We'll see," said Eli, returning his attention to the CD in his ear. Before he can do so, however, Ivy grabs the piece and pulls it back. "By the way," she said, "The board's up." She leaves him with that, as he gets up to see if he's got anything to do in the ring tonight. Slaughter awaits.
