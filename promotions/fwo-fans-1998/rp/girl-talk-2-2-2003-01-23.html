Girl Talk? (2/2)

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Mrs. Flair? What happened to your hospital gown?" Angel turned around, still not used to being referred to as Mrs. Flair, although the title puts quite a smile on her face. "A piece of paper," replied Angel, "that opens up in the back? And you wonder what I did with it?" She stood in front of the nurse in charge of the nursery, most certainly a sight to behold. Angel had given up on the hospital gown, in favor of an oversized TOOL T-shirt, plaid boxers, and a pair of red- and- green striped socks. As a gesture to the cold weather outside, she's borrowed an old bathrobe from Poison Ivy... but she doesn't do much of that hospital stuff. "Never mind," said the nurse finally, "What can I do for y'?" "A friend of mine is on the third floor," explained Angel, "I'd like to bring my baby down so they can meet each other." The nurse sighed and reached for a checklist. "Baby's name?" "Mariella Jade Flair." "Alright, Mrs. Flair," replied the nurse, "The orderly will be down with the wheelchair in just a minute." "Waaaaaait a sec," said Angel, "I don't need a wheelchair, I'm just--" "All patients removing a baby from the nursery are to go to their destination in a wheelchair. Hospital policy, Mrs. Flair... you'll have to deal with it." She sighed, waiting... waiting. Nobody would tell her what Amy Campbell was in for, only that she had been "shot, or something." Thanks, Eli... sometimes he could really infuriate her. But as Mariella Jade was placed into her arms... We created this. Eli and me. And she's perfect. "Where too, ma'am?" The orderly. Fuckin' finally. He wheeled her twenty feet to the elevator, down two floors, and another forty feet to the door of Amy's room. At least he didn't ask her for a tip. "Give a call when you're ready to go back," he said, "and someone'll come down for ya." "Yeah.... thanks." Angel looked back, making sure the orderly was out of eyeshot. Once he was, she got out of the wheelchair and walked toward the bed. Amy Campbell looked like she was asleep. "Hey," whispered Angel quietly, "You awake?" Amy'd heard her come in, and opened her eyes at the sound of the voice. "Mmhmm..." She mumbled, sounding more than a little drowsy. "I'm good to go...how you doing...?" "Well, honey," replied Angel, "I'd wager I'm a bit better than you are since I'm on my feet." She drew Amy's attention to the bundle of blankets in her arms. "But I thought you might want to meet the newest member of the family." She didn't need to fake any reaction, the redhead's wide smile said it all, even behind a veil of pain and drugs. Amy reached over as best she could to touch the little bundle, even an arm or a tiny finger, but found stretching herself hurt more than she wanted to admit. "She's so...little! I've never seen a baby this young....kinda reminds me of Brandon, all bald and wide-eyed..." She snickered a bit, which only came out as a hiss through her teeth as she bit down on what might have been a laugh. Angel smiled down at her daughter. "Amy Campbell... meet Mariella Jade Flair." Mariella yawned as the two women smiled at her, "I can't believe how beautiful she is... she's perfect." She looked around the mostly bare room for a place to sit, unable to find one. She sighed and dragged the wheelchair over next to the bed, holding her baby very deliberately. "So what all happened? My myriad of visitors wouldn't tell me." "Mariella? That's a different name - I like it. Anyway...uhh..." The speil had begun, "I found a note from this girl...she'd been stalking Brandon." Amy started to collect her thoughts, though it was a slow coming process. "She was sending me these weird, threatening letters. I...well, I finally decided to tell him, because the last letter indicated he might be in trouble..." After a pause, she continued. "When I got to his room...I found that letter, one for him...the next night, I went to the pool where the letter told him to meet her......I took La---Alex, you know...a helping hand." "And she...well...Brandon refused her, and she.....yeah, went to shoot." Another pause, this time to regain her composure before a tear could fall. "I saw in her eye that she'd shoot him - she was nuts. So I...I jumped and got between Brandon and a bullet, it was the only way to make sure she didn't hurt..." Her thoughts were still scattered, words didn't come as clearly as she'd hoped. They were clear enough, however, as Angel's attention had been completely captured. "Oh my goodness... I-- I had no idea. Are you okay? Did they arrest her? Are you gonna be able to go to Body Count? What did they tell you?" She regained her composure, turning slightly red, smiling. "I'm sorry, the last thing you need right now is a bunch of questions and stuff. I'm not quite... myself again, yet." Angel kissed MJ's forehead and held her tightly - MJ continued to sleep. Amy was quick to correct her, grinning "Nothing to apologize for. But yeah...I won't be back until after BodyCount. I'll be on the road next week obviously...I can't stand sitting around, plus...well...there's someone to keep me company. It would've shattered my hip if I'd jumped a little higher...it'll be fast-healing wound, especially with the shape I'm in." "The doctors say no, but I'm gonna fight the week after BodyCount if I can get booked...I will NOT just sit around, I'll go easy to let it heal, but I'm not gonna stop fighting for any amount of time because of some stupid psycho and her obsession with my--err---Brandon..." A thought seemed to cross Angel's mind. "Are you getting outta here today? Or tomorrow, even?" "Tomorrow for sure. I'll eat my way out if needed..." "MJ and I are out of here tomorrow, too... and I'm really itching to get back to work, myself. Since you're not gonna be able to wrestle... feel like hittin' the road with a marginally famous industrial band for almost a week?" Angel's eyes looked hopeful. "YES! That's perfect..." She smiled again, but quickly considered an obsession even newer than music. "You're gonna be following fWo a bit...right? Nothing too far away.......y'know..." "Don't worry," replied Angel with a wink, "We won't stray too far. But you need to get some sleep so you can get outta here tomorrow. But if you can get up and take a walk, I'm just two floors up, allrighty?" "Gotcha'." Angel wheeled herself outside in the chair, looked both ways, and let impatience override hospital orders as she carried her daughter to the elevator and headed back upstairs. She couldn't wait to get back on the road.
