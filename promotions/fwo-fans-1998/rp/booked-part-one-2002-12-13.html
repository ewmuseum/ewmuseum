Booked [Part One]

13 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
December 4th, 2002 10:23 PM Horito walked into the conference room of the McNichols arena. Inside the room sat the senior booking staff of the fans Wrestling organization, drinking coffee or tea, and discussing amongst themselves the future angle plans of the other members of the roster. Theodore Thompson and Ted Tallison, respectively known in the Wrestling industry as 'Twitch' and 'Thumper', sat on one side of the rectangular conference table, discussing some ideas for SCPW and OCW graduates towards bringing them into the main promotion. The two of them were the two younger members of the team, with a lot of energy still contained within themselves. On the other side of the table sat Samuel Robinson Fitspatrick, known as 'Samuel Smooth' in the ring. Samuel fumbled around with a lighter, but didn't dare to light a cigarette in the arena. One could see in his face signs of a deteriorated man, a man who had lost much over the years. Finally, at the head of the table sat Grant Mitchel, the man formerly known in Wrestling as 'Silver Fox'. Mitchel was the oldest member of the fWo staff, seconded by senior security member, Ken Armstrong. Grant stared up from some papers, and saw Horito, cracking a smile. "Hey, Horito, you know the guys?" asked Grant, as Horito nodded, and Grant nodded his head. "Good. You can sit over there." Horito took a seat, and watched as Grant removed some papers from a duffel bag. Grant flipped through the documents quickly, as both Ted's nodded at Horito, and continued discussing plans for the developmental graduates. "Horito, you know that on Sunday the United States championship is being crowned in a battle royale, correct?" asked Grant, as he found the document he was looking for. Horito nodded, and replied simply with: "Yes." Grant smiled, and nodded at Horito, before he passed along a sheet of paper. "Read the opening match." was all that Grant said, and Horito eyed the sheet of paper. The sheet was the official booking form that was produced following each booking meeting. The opening bout was listed as such: fWo World Cruiserweight Championship Areumna'derin Rahl vs. Great Dragon Winner: Great Dragon via Pinfall (630 Splash?) "Why am I in the Cruiserweight title match?" was all that Horito asked, as he stared up from the piece of paper, and towards the head booker. Grant responded cooly to the question, saying: "Listen, Horito, we need a veteran in the Wrestling industry to build up the Cruiserweight belt. The fact is that most of the Cruiserweights in the division at the moment are either too green or above the Cruiserweight belt. Given that you have held several Cruiserweight, Lightweight and Junior Heavyweight belts in your decade and a half or so in the business, we felt you would be the best representative as the first Cruiserweight champion." "Yes, but I...don't want to give the other people backstage the impression that I'm some sort of crotchity old veteran who is too proud to retire, or to put others over..." trailed off Horito, as Grant interrupted: "Don't worry, man, you're not going to get into any problems with this. We talked with Tim, and he was fine with this, as I'm sure it would also build up your current feud with Brandon by having the Cruiserweight title on the line, rather than just being a standard grudge match." said Grant, calmly. "So, what do you think?" Horito sat in the chair, thinking about his current situation, before he sighed, and responded with one word: "Alright."
