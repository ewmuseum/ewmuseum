Coming to America

5 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Coach? Does Villiam understand vhat a flight across de Atlantic Ocean in coach does to individuals of our stature?” The questions which Bastian Von Bismarck, one-half of Blitzkrieg Funk, asked were rhetorical but do reveal his displeasure with the fWo’s current travel budget. To be fair, it’s a reasonable question; have you flown coach lately? Comfort, was definitely not their primary design goal and the average American (you might as well add gargantuan German wrestler to this as well) has quite the grim journey ahead of them. Bastian was sifting through the latest issue of EWzine Europe until he came across a piece discussing his most recent match, ”vhat de hell is zis trash?” Bastian hollered as he tossed the issue across the aisle. “How could de press be so ignorant? Ve lost out titles because of bullshit” added Bastian, it has been nearly two weeks since Blitzkrieg Funk lost their CWL Tag Team Championships to Britain’s “Royal Flush.” The loss of their titles was beneficial for the team seeing as how they had been led astray of their ultimate goal by the immense success they experienced in the CWL. “Hans ve are on our vay. Ve vill vindicate my grandfather and return pride to de fatherland” affirmed Bastian confidently to his partner Hans. “Ich weiß” appeased Hans who was too busy trying to fidget into a cozy position he would never discover as an airplane seat can only be compared to a medieval torture device. The team was formed some years ago at a beer hall in Munich when Bastian launched a campaign to return pride to the fatherland, which had been courageously carrying a burden of shame since 1945, by dominating America’s professional wrestling tag team division. Quite an ambitious goal when you consider it is absolutely ridiculous and Hans knows this but, he doesn’t speak a word of English and to make it in America it is kind of a prerequisite. Besides, the guy can wrestle and is dynamite with the ladies. “Guten tag fräulein flight lady, VHERE ZE HELL IS MY VATER” screamed Bastian at the young flight attendant; ok dynamite was a stretch. “Take into consideration in Deutschland it is considered an honor to back hand a young lady before you stall any longer vith my beverage!” While William Beaven was devising how to develop the new fWo’s tag team division, Blitzkrieg Funk had quit the CWL and suddenly Europe’s best team was out of work. Beaven jumped at the chance to get some real foreign talent in the promotion, unbeknownst to him was just how big of a pain Blitzkrieg was going to prove to be. Regardless, Blitzkrieg Funk was on an American Airlines flight set to land in Seattle’s Tacoma International Airport at 11pm Sunday night, by Wednesday they would be two of fWo’s latest debutants.
