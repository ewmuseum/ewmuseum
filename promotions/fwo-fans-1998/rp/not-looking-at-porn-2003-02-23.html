Not Looking At Porn

23 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Richard Cross sat in front of his laptop computer with the phone cradled between his shoulder and his ear as he typed. "I'm telling you Dave, people have been uploading the clips since you left." The clips, bootleg video footage of Crucifix's fWo matches, had been made available by David's brother Richard at David's request. From what David was hearing, people really did care about seeing the Hardcore Luchador. "That's good news Rick. Really good news. Maybe if enough people see it, it'll force the fWo to give me some more ring time." "Who knows what kind of opportunities this could give you." Richard ran a search on his P2P program. "So how's things in Africa?" "Oh pretty much like I thought." "Uh huh," Richard mumbled as he ran another search. David continued. "Yeah, really hot here. Plenty of lions running down the street. Almost got run over by a zebra yesterday." "Uh huh," Richard mumbled again. David seriously reconsidered ever phoning his brother again. "How's things at the Wal-Mart?" Richard clicked on a few video clips and began downloading them. "Same old thing. We're getting some new merchandise in so I've been working late. The fWo sales have been slow though." "I'm not surprised," David said. "I just hope things get straightened out so we can come back to the States. I miss ordering food without needing a translator. I think I had goat nuts for breakfast." "Uh huh." "Stop using my computer to look for porn Rick!" Richard sat up. "I'm not! I'm, uh, making sure your videos are uploading!" "So help me Rick, if I get home and find sheep sex on my laptop I'll kill you." Meanwhile, in a basement hundreds of miles away, a young man opened his fifth can of Pepsi as he opened the video file he'd just downloaded. His skin glistened in the fluorescent light as he watched Great Dragon and Crucifix wage war. Behind him, his father paced back and forth as he lit his third cigarette. "I'm telling you Nic, firing me is the biggest mistake you could make," he said to his cellular phone. "I've got connections in Hollywood. I can make you or break you... I know I told you not to take that script... yeah but who knew it'd get him nominated for an Oscar? You can't predict these things. I'm your agent, not your psychic." The young man opened a second clip, this one of Crucifix wrestling for RNR in a hardcore match. His father continued yelling in the background. "Nobody fires Lou Forcas! Especially some prima donna who couldn't act his way out of a wet paper bag! Yeah? Well kiss my ass!" "Dad, language." The boy winced as Crucifix was thrown through a wall. Lou stuffed his phone into his pocket and began lighting a cigarette before realizing he already had one lit. "Sorry Junior. Daddy's got problems." Crucifix dropkicked Barry the Butcher through a window. "You got fired again, didn't you." Lou stared at his son. "Nobody fires..." He hung his head. "Yeah. Yeah I did. Couldn't have happened at a worse time. I've got a role but I need an actor." Junior counted the number of kicks Crucifix drove into Barry's head. "Not bad. So what kind of role is it? Another science fiction romantic comedy thriller?" "No, this one's an action flick. I really think it could make some young actor into the next Stallone. You know, a real action hero type." Lou stood behind his son and looked over his shoulder. "Downloading porn again? Save it for me. I'll look at it later." "I'm not looking at porn. I'm watching wrestling. You know, this guy might not be bad in an action movie. He can sure take a beating." Junior played the video clip again. Lou watched Crucifix being thrown through the air. "Damn. Who is that guy?" "Crucifix. His real name is David Cross. He's wrestling in the fWo right now, but he hasn't been on TV lately." Lou pulled out his phone again as Crucifix executed his Shooting Star DDT. "Holy shit," he whispered before remembering that he was on the phone. "Ray? Lou here. Yeah, do you still have the number for those wrestling promotions? I need the contact number for somebody in the fWo. I dunno, somebody in their production office." A bloody Hardcore Luchador raised his arm in victory. Lou smiled. "Yeah, I think I just found our boy."
