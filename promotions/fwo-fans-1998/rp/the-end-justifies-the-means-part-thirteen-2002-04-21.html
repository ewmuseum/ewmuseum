the end justifies the means, Part Thirteen

21 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
February 11, 2002: Jon Crisp walked into Chris Universal's office, expecting to hear some bad things. Austin Davis and The Boston Strangler had not had a good run as tag champs. Paul was fighting injuries, and there were no teams to defend against. In fact, tag team wrestling had been falling for some time, all over the wrestling business. Jon and Donaven Winters had visions of bringing the tag ranks back to when the Underground ruled the division. Sadly, this would not be. "Jon, we have to scrap the tag angle," Universal began. "Austin is leaving, this time for good. He just can't hold up anymore. And Paul needs the time off. We have no tag teams to contend with you...all in all, it's just not going to work out." Crisp just nodded. "Also, and this really stinks...Chris needs time off, for another movie. I've been incredibly and pleasantly surprised at how this hamburger program has played, mainly because I thought Chris was joking when he brought it up..." "So did I," Crisp said softly. "Well, he needs some time off, and unfortunately, we can't fly him in for vignettes and a couple of shows. So, we're going to play off the fact that neither of you two have had a clean win over each other. And we'll focus some more time on your portion of the program. It's possible that the fans could start to take your side, and you'll began a transformation of sorts." The bullshit just ran past Jon. off. possible. could. off. stinks. scrap. angle. not. work. out. These were the only words that Jon heard. There was no reason to hear the rest. This was the third and fourth times that he was being screwed. Screwed. This word, this image, this idea...ran heavily in Crisp's head. At least now he was out of comedy, and into a serious angle, one that was guaranteed to work. After all, Chris needed time off. How could this screw up? "Oh, Jon...could we do one thing?" "What's that?" "Can we drop 500 pounds of hamburger meat and Black Quicksilver on top of you?" Sigh...
