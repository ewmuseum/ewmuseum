Dreaming of the Snow/McMahon era

1 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Dreaming of the Snow/McMahon era February 01, 2000 Double S sat in a dressing room, relaxing with a bottle of water. He swallowed it down, not spitting, and looked around himself. He was dreaming, that was certain. But where was he? Hey Double S, we've got ourselves a problem! Sid looked up to see Rudo and Machina walking into the room. Except they didn't look quite right. For one thing, Machina was wearing shorts rather than his usual full-pants leggings. His shorts were bright green and seemed to have a big pink lips print on the behind. Rudo had dreadlocks rather than his usual ponytail, and was dressed in black and green jogging pants. Did you see those radicals out there? They attacked us man, they aren't even supposed to be in the building? Sid blinked and looked at Rudo. He was wearing a shirt reading "Road Dogg" and looked put out about something. Both he and Machina were looking at him expectantly and Sid felt he needed to say something. The words came out of his mouth without him controlling them, as they usually did in dreams. Oh come on you guys, stop PMSing. Just deal with big sillies, you don't need the help of the Dame. Now if you don't mind, I've got to get back to making sure the McMahon/Snow era is still on track. Double S felt a hand on his shoulder and turned to see Shane McMahon. Shane blew him a kiss and pointed to a bed that seemed to be inside the locker room with them. Come on Double S, let's see you do that piledriver again! Double S woke up with a yelp and looked around himself with a start. He was in the hotel room, alone. He sighed. He suddenly wondered what David was doing, and picked up the phone. It was late, but he thought he might give him a call just to hear his voice. He dialed David Skimmer's number, and waited as it rang. Hello? The voice didn't belong to David. Who is this? There was a pause, and then an answer came. This is Matthew J Klonston the fifth. You want to tell me your name or am I going to hang up on you? Matthew? Sid searched his memory and finally placed the name. He was the old boyfriend of David, and even though they had broken up a long time ago David still used Matthew for small, lap dog tasks. Things like running to get groceries or cleaning out the toilet. It's me, Sid. Can I talk to David? A longer pause. He ain't in. I'll tell him you called. Sid looked at the clock on the bed stand. It was 2:11 in the morning. He's not in? Where is he? There wasn't an answer, and Sid suddenly knew where he was. Is he with Rob? Is David out with Rob? The phone was hung up, and the long hum of the dial tone was all that came through the receiver. Sid looked at the phone for a long time. What was going on? Was David sleeping with another man? How could he? David had always told Sid that he would be honest with him, and that he would be straight up with him. Could David Skimmer be a liar? A cheat? Could David Skimmer be one of those people that sleeps around with any man he can get his hands on? Would David break his word, and his heart? I need some answers. Sid got back into bed and turned out the light. He didn't sleep for a long time, but when he did it was free from dreams, and restless.
