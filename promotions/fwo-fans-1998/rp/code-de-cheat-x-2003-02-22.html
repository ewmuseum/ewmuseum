Code de Cheat. X

22 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There he stood. Huffing. Puffing. Ready to finish Conor off once and for all. "Bitch... you pissed me off! THERE WERE GOOD PEOPLE THAT DIDN'T USE THE INTERNET TODAY BECAUSE OF YOU!" And everyone, was all like "damn man, calm down" with their "awe'ing" at this stage. However, Eric didn't care. There were good people turned away from using the internet. One boy even left the library crying because he needed to "eWrestle" and put out a "roleplay" for his "Fed"... but Conor ended up telling the boy to "get bent" as well as to "get a life". What's the world coming to? Eric went in for the kill... however, he didn't see Tyler coming from behind... With the fire extinguisher. Thunk. Down went Eric. "Oh yeah... that's right. Take that!" Conor said, trying his best to stand up. "He won't be getting up for a while folks... you can all go back to reading now!" And actually, everyone did, as if Conor wasn't even needed to say a word. They sat back down, picked up their books... and went about it like nothing had even happened. Three minutes then passed, as Tyler, without hesitation walked right into the old woman's booth, headed to the printer, took all the pages that were printed... and found his after tossing all the other ones around the room. "Here we go." He said, kissing the paper. "fWo Cheat Codes." Walking out from behind the table, Tyler couldn't happen to help but brush into the old woman, knocking her to the floor. "Oh well." Then finding Conor still standing above Eric (and talking smack to him), he tapped him on the shoulder. "Here we are." Conor smiled before collecting his composure and walking out of the library to silence from everyone who just read. Once at the door, the Fuse Bros. turned around before actually walking out. "Okay everyone!" Conor announced. "We're... Leaving!" Expecting the entire room to go up in "awe", which, they did not, Conor threw his hands in the air and exited first. "God dammit what is their problem!?!?" Tyler followed, while the automatic door closed, as Eric still laid out cold on the floor... but no one cared, as the entire library was silent. For everyone was enjoying their good story book...
