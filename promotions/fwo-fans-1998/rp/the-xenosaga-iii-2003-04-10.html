The Xenosaga: III

10 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Before we begin the next section of this tale of woe, tragedy, and bad video games, I would like to state this one thing to a certain Semen: Your MOM messed up this website. Sucka, POW. Anyhoo, the Tallywhackers have wisened up to the Codemaster's ploys, and have chained him to the chair this time, while at the same time taping his hands to the controller. The grimace on his face could only confirm that the Codemaster was in the middle of playing Superman 64, and that he wasn't exactly too happy about playing it. I mean, if I were trying to control a blue polygonal blotch pretending to be Superman, I'd be pretty disgruntled, too. He was also losing touch with reality, and was even openly critiquing the makeup of the game. "What the hell is this green crap?" "Oy, bloody manual called eet a bloomin' kryptoyte fog." Dingleberry explained. "Yeah, whatever. If it's 'kryptonite fog' as you so eloquently put it, shouldn't he be so weak that he's gettin' bitchslapped by a hooker on crack?" the Codemaster asked, "And another thing, this has GOT to be the product of a deranged psychopath. I know that if I were trapped in some hell dimension, I wouldn't be forced to fly through rings all day. I'd be, like, tryin' to get the hell out." "Y'aint geetin' out o' heeyer, boy." Cagle said. "Well, it was worth a try." the Codemaster said, with a frown on his face. He was somehow living through the powers that was bad video games, which was worrying the Tallywhackers. I mean, they've been at this for three hours now, and they've so far had an escape attempt, and it didn't look like the Codemaster was ready to crack just yet. Finally, the Australian, Dingleberry, marched over to the Nintendo 64 and turned it off. Then Dingleberry yanked the controller out of the Codemaster's hands, before Dingleberry glared at the chocolate syrup-covered manager of the Fuse Bros. "...Oy didn't think we'd have t' resort t' these measures, but Oy guess you're just askin' for eet." Dingleberry said, right to the Codemaster's face. The Codemaster grimaced again and spoke. "I think YOU'RE asking for a BREATH MINT." the Codemaster shot back. Dingleberry seemed offended, and pulled away from the Codemaster's face momentarilly, before he checked his breath by putting his hand right in front of his face and breathing into it. After doing this twice, Dingleberry simply shrugged and glanced at Cagle. "Cagle! Bring een the vid-yo tapes!" Video tapes? Oh, now we're entering the hardkorr!!11 torture.
