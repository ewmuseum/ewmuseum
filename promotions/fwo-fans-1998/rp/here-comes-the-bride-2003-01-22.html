Here comes the bride!

22 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A knock on the door was followed by Borst promptly opening the door of his hotel room. Before him stood a short greasy looking man holding a clipboard. "Sign for her." He grunted to Borst, holding the clipboard out for him. "For who?" "Yalenchka. You ordered her, REMEMBER?" "Yeah, of course I remember, I just wanted to check it was the right one, wise arse." Borst replied, glaring at the guy. Borst snatched the pen from the greasy short git's hand and scribbled on the paper. The delivery guy grabbed the pen back and had a quick look at his clipboard before laughing to himself and winking at Borst. "You do know why she had 20% off don't you?" Borst shook his head. "Come on. She's Russian, and she's... a bit, mental." The guy chuckled. "She's crazy. And she's yours now." He said, tapping his finger on the clipboard. Borst shrugged his shoulders. "Your funeral." The little shrimp said, before walking off. Borst peered around the corner of the door and laid eyes upon his order. She was about 6 feet tall, dressed in the shortest mini skirt you can find without it being classed as a belt, plus a tatty halter top that left very little to the imagination. A slut style make-up job, complete with red lipstick, and blonde hair tightly pulled back in a ponytail completed the look. "I am your wife, no?" she purred in a thick Russian accent. "No. I mean yes." Borst replied, before grabbing her arm and pulling her inside. Borst slammed the door shut and wandered across the room to his faithful packet of cigarettes. He hopped on his bed and lit one up, and continued to watch TV as he had been doing before his mail order bride had arrived. "So..." his new bride said. "I am Yalenchka, from Russia." "Yeah yeah yeah, I know." Replied Borst. "Take a seat, do what you want. Sit and look pretty or something woman." Yalenchka had a quick look around the room and sat on the corner of the bed. "So..." she purred. "I have seen you before. On the television. I admire you a lot. You are very popular back in Russia." "No shit." Borst mumbled. "Why did you choose me for your bride?" she asked. "Cheap. 20% off... but other than that... I just remembered I'd lost my old wife, and I could do with a new one to fill that hole. I saw you, 20% off, and from Russia. So I figured it was a good opportunity to go back to my roots." "You fight... don't you?" she asked, gazing at the United States Title sitting gracefully on the floor surrounded by empty take away boxes. "Yeah." Borst replied, as he flicked through the channels on TV. "I used to fight in Russia." She said, as she began to fiddle with her hands, almost mesmerised by her own frantically moving fingers. Borst suddenly looked slightly interested. He turned to his new wife and waved his hand, beckoning her to continue. "Bears. I used to fight bears. With my bare hands. Ripped them to pieces. Punch and scratch and kick." Yalenchka jumped to her feet and acquired a strange glint in her eye. "PUNCH!" She cried. "SCRATCH! KICK! ... KILL!" Borst at this point had lost interest in the TV and was fixated on Yalenchka. She lunged forward and grabbed his t-shirt. "I love to fight. To kill. To maim. To break. To bite!" Yalenchka suddenly decided to shove Borst hard, sending him flying off the bed. "To be in control!" "Fuck off." Borst snapped back. "I'm beginning to see why you had 20% off." He continued, as Yalenchka proceeded to throw an alarm clock at a mirror. "I..." she screamed. "I am sorry." Yalenchka sat back down on the bed and suddenly fell back. "Do you want me to pleasure you now?" "I fucking love you already." Said Borst as he picked himself up off the floor. "You're fucking crazy." "I am sorry. I cannot help it. They say it will get better if..." Borst was quick to butt in. "Fuck that. I'm loving you the way you are already. You're a psycho." "You love me?" she replied, with a small tear appearing in the corner of her eye. "No-one has ever said that be-" "I'm tired." Borst butted in once more. Yalenchka smiled and winked at Borst. "Come to bed darrrrling." She purred. "I would, but you're in it... you can have the couch. Next door probably have some spare sheets. Help yourself." Borst said as he put out his cigarette on the US Title. "I am fine. I sleep like this..." she replied, as she took Borst's duvet and stuck out her tongue. "OK, fine. Make it a quick nap anyway; we're going to Warfare in a few hours. I suppose you should see me in action, woman." Borst said, grabbing Yalenchka's arm and pulling her off his bed. "Warfare? I went to war once before. With camels. They have many uses in Russia though. You can use them for transport. You can get water from them. You can eat them. You can even use them to pleasure other camels. And if you are really lucky, you can get them to sit." Borst just stared at her blankly, before suddenly saying. "You're mental." Borst laughed to himself and shook his head. "You're fucking great. What did I ever see in that woman Zoe. You're fucking great." Yalenchka smiled. "Now shut up and leave me alone so I can rest." Borst grunted as he drove his head down onto the pillow. "You don't want to get to know me better, darling?" she asked innocently as she stood beside the couch with the duvet wrapped around herself. "I know you're blonde, not gay like my last woman... you're not gay are you?" Yalenchka shook her head. "You're not gay, you're from my original homeland, you're cheap, you're mine, and you're fucking crazy. I love you already, as much as I could ever love a woman anyway. You're the perfect replacement." Borst nodded his head. "I know enough, for now."
