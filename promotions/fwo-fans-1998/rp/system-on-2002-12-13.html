System... On.

13 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was so surreal. Every little bit of it. There they were with their "T" and "C" hats... to their nifty tight shirts... to their suspender-like pants... ready to enter a world that they weren't too sure they'd get out of. In fact, just prior to, the both of them had vowed to either walk out of there with the gaming victory... or be crushed at the hands of the heart-swallowing game over. Tyler looked at Conor. He tried to grin at that very moment. He really tried too... but all he could get was the cheesiest smile that showed his entire top and bottom set of teeth. Conor just looked at the ground in front of him, speaking in a sarcastic tone. "Really reassuming, Ty." He kicked the closest pebble with his foot, as it rolled across the ground at a frantic speed... until it was halted by some big giant object. Some object made of brick. Conor looked at the motionless pebble, taking a deep breath in, and then a deep breath out. It was okay to be nervous, he figured. It looked like a hard video game from the outside, so it was okay to shake. Besides, he had Tyler with him. Tyler, the one with the cheesy teethed smile. Tyler finally stopped his smiling, as he realized what Conor was looking at. He too, then looked. And there they watched the motionless pebble lay on the ground, in front of... that thing. Finally gathering enough courage to slowly raise their heads... it came into view. The castle. Known to everyone else as the Arena in which Wednesday Night Warfare was hosted. Regardless, there it was... in all of its glory. This time Conor attempted to smile, as he looked over at Ty. "Power." He said, holding out his fist. Tyler returned the "kudos" as both of them took on that cheesy weak grin... And took their steps forward. The camera slowly faded to black, just as the Fuse Bros. voices could be heard. "You think this game's gonna be a little too hard, Ty?" "Nothing we can't handle Conor... nothing we can't handle at all."
