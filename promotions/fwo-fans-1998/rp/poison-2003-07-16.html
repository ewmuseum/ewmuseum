Poison

16 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It just happened to be one of those mornings ... everyone's had one. That morning where everything is wrong, and nothing is right. One of those terrible mornings that eventually lead to terrible days, which usually led to a terrible night. You usually long for the next day, because ... well ... it couldn't possibly be any worse, right? Wrong. ...Sean Stevens has been having these disastrous mornings for the past four months, and although he's tried everything in his power to fix it, nothing works. His wrestling commitments were finished, and he had the day off to relax, but he couldn't. ... He and Poison Ivy had a fight. It wasn't the usual knock down, drag out because ... well ... he didn't have it in him anymore. He was sure he still loved Ivy, but there was something different about him, her, them. They weren't the same people they were last year. Sean isn't nearly as motivated now, as he was then, and Ivy is just as. They hardly spent time together, and usually when they fought, something told him that no matter how hardened their hearts were, it'd be alright. ...he wasn't so sure this time. He wasn't in as much of a rush to fix it as usual. He didn't care if he had hurt her feelings. He was tired of hearing about her issues, her problems, her busy schedule. What about his feelings? ...his issues? What about the things he liked to do? Stevens exhaled as he jogged around the track. To hell with it, he thought to himself. Then he stopped. ... in his tracks, even. What was he saying? That it was over? That he was tired of Poison Ivy? No, not exactly. Sean was tired of the Rat Pack and their bubble gum existence. Tired of involving himself with people who claimed to be one way, while their actions did the exact opposite. He was tired of feeling like the bad guy, because ... well ... secretly, he was disgusted by them. That went for all of them, save one, Lance Knight. And, it included Eli Flair. As much as it pained him to think it ... Ivy was one of those people as well, it's who she became, who she is, to this day. At the end of the day, love can only take you so far. It's a feeling. The only way a relationship can work, is if the two people want to be in it. Sean wasn't so sure he did, anymore.
