Before the So- Called...

26 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FADEIN on a shaky video, a shot of a door. "Hey." And Ivy McGinnis' face, from the side. She looked tired. "So anyways, I was gonna do up a live video from backstage tonight... try to give you guys at home a sneak peak at the final days before Cyberslam Seven... but it didn't really work out too good." She adjusted her glasses and took a drink from a water bottle. "But my absent- minded, tired ass ended up taking the night off after booking two straight nights of house shows, Chuck gave me a night off. And I left my keys at the arena." She smiled. "It's cold out here, and it's late and I'm really tired and my mind is stretched to the limits, but it's okay." "I'm doing what I want to do with my life... I'm actually accomplishing something worthwhile, and that's a good feeling. That's a good day's work. If this was two years ago I'd be smoking a cigarette, if this was a year ago I'd be on Sean's doorstep. And I'd rather be in either of those places than here." She looked down, her eyes avoiding the lens. "It's such a catch- 22. I've got respect, creative input, the freedom to do what I will with it all... and I miss the days when I'd sit on a doorstep and smoke a cigarette. I miss the days when I was nothing but some chick in the locker room... because it meant someone was there for me to come home to." Silence, nervous laughter. "I dunno... spoiled, I guess. Fox and the guys were the best promoters to work for, because they were all former wrestlers. They all knew what it was that we had to do every night, and that was the focus of the show. Now, it's just hard." "It's too damn hard." She sighed. "Lindz should be here soon with my keys... maybe I'll drop you guys another line tomorrow. Stay strong." Static.
