Checkmate

16 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday, March 10 Portland, Oregon 7:44 PM PST Scott Wasner didn't remember much of the past week, which was, he figured, a good thing. The last thing he recalled as anything less than a fuzzy haze was passing out on his floor after Stephanie had left. Not the most pleasant of memories, to put it mildly. A new low point in his life. Fuck all. As best he could piece together, the rest of the week had been spent in a coke-induced haze. That was good. Coke dulled the pain. Blurred it. Made it go away. Better than dealing with it, that much was for sure. That's my motto, it's not there if the coke says it's not there. Now he was sitting on a chair in the middle of a large conference room, surrounded by most of the rest of the fWo roster. Chris Universal was making his way to the front of the room. There had been a murmur, as you'll find in all crowds of this size, but it was dying down as Universal approached the front. The boys had only found out about this meeting when they arrived that day, and since then the rumors had been flying fast and furious. Or so Wasner gathered - for all his name and rep, he was still out of touch with this new crowd and very much out of the loop. Last time a meeting like this had been called was part of an angle - the fWo X-Box game angle back in December. He had seen that one on the TV. This, doubtless, was a more serious occasion. Universal adjusted his tie and cleared his throat as Wasner looked on through blurry eyes. "Ahem. I want to thank you all for coming here tonight." That was funny. Like they had a choice in the matter or something. "I don't want to bore you all, especially since we're on the air in just over an hour and I know some of you have things to tape and such, but the skinny of it is, it's random piss test time, everybody." No way. No fucking way. The room remained silent - certainly nobody was grumbling, for obvious reasons. "Over the course of this week you'll all be giving us urine samples, we'll test for the usual. I'm sure nothing will come of this, of course - I trust you guys, but hey, even as much as I trust you, I gotta do this. Sometimes you don't see the problems coming even when they're right in front of your eyes." How true. How ironic. "You'll all be given your dates throughout the afternoon tonight, by being called into my office. The results'll be back by next week, and if there are any problems... well, you'll find out about them next Sunday." Well. Ain't that special. I suppose that career match I just learned I'm booked in just took on a whole new meaning. Hell, it's like I'm in a movie or something. M. Night Shyamalan could be writing my life. Just when you think you know it all... boom, here's the killer plot twist! "Thanks for coming guys - I'll be seeing you tonight, and best of luck on tonight's show. Thanks." Universal stepped down and exited the room, and the throng of wrestlers followed him out. Wasner remained in his seat for a few moments, ostensibly waiting for the room to clear out before exiting. It's like I'm back in baseball, facing Freddy Garcia. First pitch, fastball down the pipe, I'm back on coke. Second pitch, big breaking curve, goodbye Steph, thanks to me. And just when I thought I was ready for whatever he could throw... hello, filthy changeup tailing away. Drug test. Swing and a miss. Strike three, I'm out. Bloody fucking hell.
