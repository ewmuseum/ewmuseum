Fatal Cries, Part III

11 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Shut the hell up," the father screamed. "You do whatever I tell you to do, you ungrateful bitch!" The child woke up from his light sleep. He listened to his young and immature parents arguing for the fourth straight night. In the next room over the child's mother was down on her knees, arms flailing in the air at the father's body. He was drunk again and she had just come home from her job. Putting things in a nice term, the woman was a prostitute. She was a cocaine and heroin addict as well. Like the child's father she would often beat him for no reasons at all. He was still in his room, but he had moved towards the wall, the left side of his head pressed against the peeling wallpaper. The argument was more intense than the previous nights. "I thought I had told you to stop being such a whore," the man yelled again before taking a swig from his flask. "I need to make money for us," the woman pleaded. "Without me we're back on the streets!" The father's eyes filled with rage, he dropped the copper plated flask to the floor and his arms dropped to his side, fists clenched so hard the knuckles had turned a bright white. She crawled herself backwards, her body was against the couch as he lurched forward, step by step. His right arm was lifted high into the air, his fist unclenched and struck down across the woman's face. A large red mark were the results of his underhanded deed. "I told you to stop fucking putting yourself out," he demanded. "Do I make myself fucking clear?" She nodded her head, as tears started to flow. For some reason something ignited inside of the man and he jumped on top of the defenseless woman. He pinned her shoulders to the floor with his knees. He began to rip off all of her clothing, leaving her in her underwear. He smiled sadistically, knowing she was helpless. He began to punch her in the face, she tried to scream, but he just kept hitting her. "The more noise you make, the more it's going to hurt," he whispered into her ear. The punching got harder and harder, she started to bleed heavily, first the nose, then her mouth. He was going insane with every fist that made contact. He occasionally took time out from the punching to take another long drink from his whiskey flask. After the drink his fists continued pummeling his wife. His hands had become speckled with blood, blotched over the knuckles. The only noise that came from the apartment was the television and the sound of hands breaking bones. He finally stopped, he had broken out into a heavy sweat, he was drenched in it. He slowly took his knees off of her shoulders and got to his feet. She was still laying in the exact position she had been in when he first pinned her to the floor. "She'll wake up in a few minutes," he attested to himself. He walked out of the living room, while his wife lay on the floor in a large red circle that had formed on the white floor. Her eyes were open, but rolled back so far only the whites were visible. Her mouth was slightly open, blood trickling out from the right corner, dripping onto the floor. Her arms were broken, just beneath the shoulders from the man's knees bearing down on her. The orbital bones had been compound fractures, just sticking out beneath the eyes. Her nose appeared to be almost pushed in, blood had been running out, but had dried and left a faint trail down the right side of her face. Bright purple bruises had already started to form over her face and where the knees had been pressed down on her upper arms. It seemed as if she wasn't going to be waking up any time soon. The child crawled back away from the wall and slid into his mattress that was actually on the floor. His body shuddered and jerked in a quick motion. He looked up at the black ceiling, then towards his door, only seeing the light the crept beneath the door and threshold. He watched the light and noticed the shadow disrupt the light. He quickly pulled his entire body underneath the sheets of the mattress and closed his eyes hard before whispering. "Mommy..." His eyes were closed hard enough that tears had started to come out. He knew it was his father walking through the living room. The footsteps sounded like they were dragging and slow. He then heard something drop loudly to the floor, glass broke. More footsteps, followed by the sound of a body hitting the floor. Next he heard the television being shut off. The sound of the man getting back to his feet. A rather loud click. Gunshot. Outside of the bedroom the man fell to his knees, then fell forward, a hole from a .45 caliber magnum had pierced his skull. Both man and wife lay next to each other in the living room. Inside of the bedroom the child could do nothing but cry. He was completely alone in life now, but he wouldn't find out until the next morning. Abandoned. The Fallen Angel would find out that his father beat his mother to death, before shooting himself in the skull with a .45 Magnum. He had nobody now.
