Hometown Hero

14 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Hometown Hero January 14, 2000 ::: The scene opens with Geoff Green scanning the crowd at the Philadelphia 76ers game. The Sixers are facing the Orlando Magic at the First Union Center::: Geoff: I can't see him. ::: Geoff continues to scan the crowd::: Geoff: Oh, he is sitting in the first row on the end. Hey, how did he get good seats? :::Geoff shrugs, and walks down the stairs, carrying his camera on his right shoulder. When he reaches the bottom, he is greeted by Matt Douglas, who is dressed in street clothes::: Matt: Hey Green, what are you doing here? Geoff: I just thought that I would get your thoughts on your upcoming matches. Matt: Sure. I would first like to say that the Flying Frenchie is a great wrestler, and in no way, do I disrespect him. I mean he came from France, and has excelled greatly in this industry. But this Sunday, I am going to show him that in my hometown, I don't lose. I believe that my match last week, against Eron, was great. I hope that it is the same this week. ::: Matt pauses, and then begins to speak again::: Matt: Now onto Primetime. I believe that he has won many matches against me, but in my mind, he has only beaten Wolf Fang. I also must remind you that Wolf Fang had defeated Primetime at CyberSlam '99. Which is, in my opinion, the most exciting event in sports entertainment today. But I am going to put all of the past behind me, so when I step in that ring at Invasion, it will be Primetime-0 and Matt Douglas-0. We will just have to see if he still has what he had back when he faced Wolf Fang. ::: Matt begins to clap as the Sixers hit a 3 pointer::: Matt: Oh, I am sorry, but that is all that I have to say. ::: The scene fades to black as the Sixers travel down the court after receiving a defensive rebound:::
