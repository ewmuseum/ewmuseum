fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization 20 Hours. April 29, 2000 The majestic purple and orange sunset over Seattle provides an interesting contrast to the rubble and ruins of the Kingdome - but then again, the two have quite a few similarities too when you get right down to it. Both signal the end of one day, and the eventual beginning of another - which is also how Perfect (who happens to be standing right in the middle of the dirt, grime, and chunks of concrete) feels about CyberSlam. Since he's a fairly talkative kind of guy - some might say he loves to hear himself talk - odds are he's about to tell you just how he feels. And look, there he goes. Hey, Collins. Question for ya. Do you realize that in less than 12 hours they'll be setting up the mats to cover the grass of Safeco Field and bringing in a bunch of new seats to add to the stadium? Do you realize that in less than 17 hours, CyberSlam will begin? And in about 20 hours, you'll step into the ring with the World's Greatest Athlete. 20 hours, Scott. Can you hear the clock ticking, Collins? Can you hear the hours, the minutes, the very seconds ticking off until it's finally me and you? Perfect suddenly gestures to the rubble beneath his feet. 20 hours, Collins, until I turn your career - what's left of it - into this. You see, you're a lot like the Kingdome. For a long time, it housed nothing but crap. For a long time, you were nothing but crap. But then all of a sudden 1995 rolled around and Seattle Refused To Lose its way to the playoffs... and 1999 rolled around and you won a few world titles. As much as it pains me to say it, the M's haven't done much since then. Can't say it pains me to say that you haven't either, though. But last year, the M's moved into a new stadium and dumped Ken Griffey Jr. on his .202-hitting ass, and all of a sudden they're successful again. The Kingdome and Griffey were nothing more than a distant memory, a part of history. And this Saturday, I'm gonna dump you on your worthless ass too, and you're gonna end up just like Griffey - doing absolutely nothing in the middle of nowhere. Your career? Well, it's gonna end up like the Kingdome. Nothing more than a pile of rubble and dirt from a once great thing. No two ways about it, Collins... the sun's setting on you. Just like it's setting on the fWo - but hell, that's another issue, and that's more of a D-Man thing anyway. But Collins, in 20 hours from now and less than a mile from here, I'm gonna rip you a new one. You see, you've been a thorn in my side for a good 14 months, and tomorrow night once and for all I'll pull that thorn out and crush it under my boot. Collins, you said a while ago - 17 days, to be exact - that you were gonna find me and leave me in a dumpster somewhere? Collins, after CyberSlam the only things that're gonna be trashed are you, your pimp Stone, and your two boot-licking ass-kissing sniveling squealing stupid sycophants. Bet on it. Perfect slowly begins to step out of the rubble of the Kingdome and walks towards Safeco Field. Why do I think that? Scratch that. Why do I know that? Simple. The World's Greatest Athlete is the ultimate big-money player in this business. When it's all on the line, I turn it up - and considering my normal level of work makes practically everybody in this two-bit fed look like Mideon on sleeping pills, that's one hell of a statement. And Collins, what event is bigger money than CyberSlam? Where is more on the line than CyberSlam? Not a one. CyberSlam, one might say, is the World's Greatest Event. It's more than just an event - it's an occasion. It's a feeling. It's a state of mind. You see, right around mid-March every year, the WGA feels actions get a bit heavier, ramifications a bit more important, the very air a bit more electrical - and when that happens, it can only mean one thing. CyberSlam - it's in the air. I've said it before, and I'll say it again. Perfect now has reached the street separating the remains of the Kingdome and Safeco Field. Traffic is low, and he crosses easily. At CyberSlam, men become heroes... heroes become legends... legends become immortal. Want to live forever, Collins? Prove yourself tomorrow night. I doubt you can. In fact, I know you can't. In 20 hours, Collins, you will proven once and for all as a nothing, as a nobody. Time is ticking away. Perfect now stands in front of Safeco Field, his silhouette outlined against the large ballpark. Tick, tock. Tick, tock. CyberSlam 2K... you're history. And I mean that... literally. Tomorrow night, Collins, it will not be your pleasure. Perfect stretches out his arms, striking a pose against the backdrop of the setting sun and Safeco Field, and we slowly fade to black.
