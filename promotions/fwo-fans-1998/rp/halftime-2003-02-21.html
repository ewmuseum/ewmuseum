Halftime

21 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"So what're ya gonna do, old man? Set your dumb ass on fire because y'can't do anything else?" Eli Flair sat on the side of the ring in the empty arena in Peru, staring at a certain spot on the floor where he and his opponent were set on fire. Eli didn't go to a hospital, but as a gesture he allowed the on- site medic to bandage up his shoulders and neck, where he has a whole family of first- and second- degree burns. He was tired, both physically and mentally. And he didn't know whether or not anything could help him feel better tonight. So engrossed in his own thoughts, he didn't hear Amy Campbell approach him from behind. She walked straight toward him until she saw his unflinching gaze, then tiptoed off to the side. "Hey," she said, to no answer. She moved closer. "Eeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeli..." Amy nudged him in the heel with a booted foot, "You're here pretty late..." "Hey, Aimz," said Eli, "'Sup?" She took a look at his blank stare, a familliar one that went through her. "Whoa...a little spaced out, are we? Want me to leave you alone...? You don't look so hot..." "No..." said Eli, ambiently. Finally, he seemed to snap out of it. "Sorry, Amy... just a little distracted. Where were you tonight?" "Well...you punch a guy for being a dicksmack and...yeah, apparently I shouldn't do that or somethin'." She winked, "I was 'given the night off to reflect on my actions and correct them for next time'...so next time, I'll just make sure not to do it in front of Fox." Eli laughed. "So I missed your show... and you missed mine. But at least I didn't get lectured on punching a dicksmack.... I got lectured for setting one on fire. Oh, and myself at the same time." He shot her a melancholy smirk. "Two for two, huh?" "For sure...probably not the greatest idea you've ever had." Amy's expression was a little critical...not of his actions, but his current mood. She'd hoped he'd pick up on it. "So how many times has Angel called to scream at you for that streak of brilliance?" Eli managed to belt out an obviously labored chuckle and shrugged the sarcasm off. The silence that followed somehow convinced the redhead to 'get down to business'. "Okay...now you're just freaking me out. What the hell's the matter?" Normally, she would've avoided that wording...but this was Eli Flair, renowned for being stubborn as hell, so her frank tone was the only way to have a chance of getting through. Eli looked at her, amazed at her direct attitude. And he nodded approvingly at it. "I've been trying to figure out what's going on, myself. What the fucking hell am I doing?" He stood up, running his hands through his hair. "I lit my hand on fire to punch Shadows. I spewed alcohol all over him and lit his ass up.... You know what that would've meant five years ago? Two years ago?" Amy wasn't sure if he wanted an actual answer, but she gave it a shot. "I don't--" "Nothing!" he interrupted, "Nothing at all. A year ago? Nothing. No idea if I'd be doing this tomorrow, nothing to keep me from living for the moment, doing whatever the fuck I wanted to because it's what I wanted to do at that moment." "I've got a wife, Amy... a beautiful wife and a beautiful daughter who depend on me. And I know Angel can take care of herself, we certainly don't need my payoffs to survive... but how... What... How fucking stupid am I to do something like this with what I have to lose?" "It means something. I think you just proved to anybody who'd forgotten that you're not called the King of Extreme for nothing. That was some crazy shit out there..." She sighed heavily a moment, "But...you do have a life that doesn't involve wrestling, and that life isn't very forgiving if you run around giving yourself second degree burns." "If you're thinking what I think you're thinking, don't. You can just tone down...Angel and MJ don't need you right there with them all the time, even I know that. When you wrestling wears down on Angel, you'll know it...as I'm sure you've already figured out. Stunts like this can't be very frequent...but this is a job like any other, it's just more demanding physically than sitting at a desk all day. Your family knows what you love...and fuck me gently if we're not gonna tell you when to cut the shit, but this isn't the time." "Yeah, a job where the fucktards in the crowd aren't happy unless I'm dead--" Eli's rant stopped short as he looked at Amy with raised eyebrows. "What did you just say?" "'Stunts like this can't be very frequent.'...?" Amy blinked, clueless as to what caught the big man as odd. She didn't say anything out of the ordinary..............to her, at least. He looked at her and tried to start talking again.... twice. Finally, he shook it off. "Amy.... I'm tired. I've been doing this stuff for nine years now, give or take... and you're right, I know you can't do this sort'a shit too often or you'd be dead, but... I don't know. After everything that's gone down so far this year in this company it's like..." Irony, the man who never missed a beat in the ring was now struggling for the words. "It's like I feel like I need to work three times as hard as everyone else or I'll get swept aside, if that makes any sense." He shook his head. "But then I feel guilty about it because of Angel and MJ and I fucking hate the fact that I let the two most important people in my life make me feel guilty about anything.... It's a vicious cycle." He looked at the redhead's concerned glance. "Am I making any sense?" 'You are." Amy started, then pause, then started, then paused...then finally gripped her wording. "You just need to...y'know, stick by here. You work three times as hard as everyone else anyway...someone's always gonna try to step over you just because you're you, and you've gotta just go with it sometimes. I don't wanna bring music into this but....you get what you put in, and people get what they deserve. It just comes later for some people...and I know you're probably not the best guy to lecture on faith, but have a little - it'll do you wonders." After another brief pause, she gathered more thoughts...leaving Eli speechless for a moment, unable to do anything but watch. "You're...it's like.........shit, I dunno." Okay, maybe her thoughts weren't as gathered as she'd thought. "You've gotta hang in here...I hate preaching like this because, fuck, you're Eli Flair...you've done this since I was ten years old. I just don't want you giving up because you've got a momentary lapse of faith...y'know? And if anybody else in this fucking mess everyone tries to tame down and call a 'business' knows about undeserving bastards walking over them, it's me. But trust me here, it WILL end...sure as I'm standing here with a shard of a bullet still left in my gut, shit might go on for a long time...but it always turns around somwhere." Amy smiled for Eli's sake and chuckled a little after that rant, "Am I making any sense?" "Oddly enough, you're making perfect sense." Eli looked over his bandages, his burns. "Don't get me wrong, Amy... this business is in my blood, I don't think I could quit if I wanted to. It's just nights like this, where I start to question whether or not it's worth it. But like you said, shit might go on for a long time but it always turns around." He laughed. "A year ago, I couldn't see myself with a kid. I couldn't see myself settled down. Angel and I were strained at best because I didn't know how to balance an actual private life with a career, and because her career had just started to explode and she needed to see where it'd take her." He stopped for a second to see Amy actually paying attention intently - he knew she had a short attention span but she also knew what was important. "This family thing really snuck up on us... but y'know, if Angel's not slowing down, why should I? Except for the fact that she probably won't be set on fire as part of her career.... what the hell am I debating, right?" "You don't want MJ growing up seeing her parents like caged lions...do what you love, she'll see you two thriving, she'll see your best." Amy winked at him, "And please don't go on with the 'We didn't know where we stood and a year later we're married with a kid' stuff...it gets me jumpy." She finished the remark and bit her lip, knowing the wink let Eli know what the hell she was going on about. "Anyway...why don't I buy you a drink? Hell, why don't we take everyone out? Brandon's out back...I'm sure we can round up the others if you'd like." "A drink, huh?" Eli stroked his chin stubble. "Tequila?" He had a knowing smile on his face. "Okay, I so drank Ivy under the table that night...NO MATTER WHAT SHE SAYS!" Amy pouted, "And besides..." She lowered her voice to a near whisper. "Ehm....Brandon won't let me have any. I kinda drove him nuts the day after..." Eli put an arm around Amy's shoulder as the two headed back down the aisle. "How 'bout.... we go grab Trip, Brandon, Alex, and Ivy.... and just get some foodstuffs?" They left the arena behind as idle conversation filled their ears. It had been a rough ride of it since the two first became friends so far, and Eli knew from experience, it was likely to be an even rougher ride for both before their association would end. But right now... taking a moment to stop and look around... was all that mattered.
