Chaos- Two of Two

10 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yeah, it's The Prophet again. The rest of the story must be told so you may all know the truth, and just as Part One was told by Him, so shall be Part Two. I turn things over to your Lord and mine....Chaos, to pick up where we left off. For a long time, I watched silently in that little cell they made for me, magically protected of course, as Azarin brought down his mighty wrath upon the planet I had created. Brothers and sisters and fathers and mothers all withered away and died at his hands. If I was in control, everyone would live immortally. There would be no more pain or suffering. Everything would be absolutley perfect. I knew that I had to take back control. From within my cell, I didn't have much power. I did, however, still have enough power to effect the birth of four humans. These four humans would be born with the sing of Chaos. Their role would be simple- take back the Earth for me, and destroy Azarin. I can't reveal how they will eventually do that, because Azarin could be reading this log, but they have the power to do so. Allow me to explain. The First is The Destroyer. He is a massive man who's purpose is to eliminate all followers of Azarin. The Second is The Prophet. He's a man with great intelligence and speaking skills who's purpose is to convince those with no alligence to follow my lead. The Third is The Killer. She's a woman with a ruthless mind, a cold heart, and the ability to extinguish life wherever she sees it. Her purpose is to eliminate the most stalwalt followers of Azarin. The Fourth is The Leader. He's a man with even greater intelligence and a special power that makes him the only one fit to lead my armies. The First and The Second already know their purpose. However, because my magics were weak at the time, The Third and The Fourth were born with their purpose buried deep down inside of them. It will be up to The First and The Second to bring out their true purpose. So, why am I telling you all of this? Simple. I'm preparing you for the arrival of The First. He will be arriving to this originization you call a "wrestling promotion" in order to eliminated the followers of Azarin on a grand stage, and he will be followed soon by The Second. Because I am a fair and decent being, I'm giving all of you a warning- do not get in the path of The Destroyer when he arrives. He is dedicated to the cause, and he will eliminate all that is placed in front of him. It is in your best interest to bow down to me now, rather than have my will beaten into you by The First. Listen to my warning carefully, those that I have created. For if you do not- you will be terminated. I'm taking this planet back. Whether you or anyone else likes it or not.
