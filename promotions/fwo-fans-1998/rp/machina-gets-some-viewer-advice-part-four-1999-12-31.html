Machina gets some viewer advice (part four)

31 December 1999

fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Machina gets some viewer advice (part four) December 31, 1999 TV-MA ::The scene opened on Machina, once again sitting on an oversized comphy couch in front of a fire. He wore his half of one-sixteenth of the fWo Tag Team Titles around his waist, and appeared to be sweating slightly.:: Hoo-doggy, that was a tough match. Good thing Ultra Violet covered the leg. Well happy campers, it appears that Rudo and I are once again Tag Team Champions. It seems like just a few minutes ago that I was wrestling in that match and winning it. ::Machina looked at his watch and whistled.:: Well, actually it only was a few seconds. In fact, I think that Countdown is still going on. ::A director said something off camera and Machina shooed him away.:: Ah, what does it matter how I got here so fast? Nobody cares about details in pro-wrestling! ::A purple cow with wings walked in front of the camera and Machina waited for it to pass.:: Well guys, today is the forth and final installment of Machina reads your viewer mail in a pathetic attempt to get laid with the hot piece of ass known as Penny Pureheart. ::Off-camera the director waved wildly. Machina shooed him again.:: Hey, I can say laid. Didn't you see the TV-MA thing at the start of this interview? I can take off my pants too, just watch! ::Machina stood and began to unbuckle his belt, and the interview went to a commercial. After some Old Navy and 1-800-CALLATT ads the scene returned to Machina who was sitting again in the couch, pants on.:: ...and that's how I met the Queen of England. Anyway, let's go to that mail. The first letter comes from an XPac069DX069. Wow, lots of numbers. Well anyway, he says: -------------------------------------------------------------- okay, i just happened to stop by this webpage, tough im from the EIW.heres what u do w/ this girl.u walk up to her,in her dressing romm,KNOCK 1ST!,or wherever she may be.amke sure shres alone.have a little conversation w. her,similair to what your gonna ask her.when she least expects it while your talking, stop talking,and wait 5-10 seconds.then pop the question.if she says yes, ur in.if she says maybe w/ a happy cheerful tint in it, shes leaning toward yes.if she says maybe w/ a dull,unsure voice,shes leaning toward no.if she says no,ur screwed -Triple X,from the EIW -------------------------------------------------------------- So it's all in the voice, huh? Well that sounds like good advice, but what do I do if she suddenly loses her ability to speak? Is there any way I can read hands? Please get back to me pally. Our next letter comes from a Gabbatotu: -------------------------------------------------------------- fake that your not really a wrestler and your against it that'll get her attenion -------------------------------------------------------------- Well Gabby, that's a decent idea and I've actually already started with this. I enacted plan: Bruce Springsteen roughly one week ago and so far it's worked well. The only problem is that the mask sometimes comes off. Without a mask she might know who I really am or who I'm really not, both of those are big problems. You understand. Right? Good. Our next letter is from HustlinB: -------------------------------------------------------------- Really you are... I am in a efed.. hell I am the world champ but I wnat a new chracter.. I am a bad ass heel now.. I want somehting new.. I ran across the fwo a while back and you immedaitely caught my eye.. I love masked wrestlers and I love people who make me laugh.. So I have decided to dedicae my new chracter to you... .. I know you think I am copying you...welll I sort of am but ole well... Similar to you I will get the stuffing kicked out of me and sustain brain damage.. you didnt do that.. you just lost your mind lol.. I to am from South Korea and where a mask... my tights look nothing lke your though.... I havent thought of a good name either any ideas? But anywho... I am going to form a stable with another wrestler who is similar to you but has never heard or the FWO.. Super Dupper gary cooper.. he talks to a sack of oranges and a rock.. The Rock and SIr Sack to be presis.. lol I will dress up manacine heads to look like other wrestlers and team with them lol... And of caousre use parodies to getme over.. nay advise for me? -------------------------------------------------------------- well, of course this makes perfect sense. I am of course the most popular wrestler in all of the fWo... I've won like... every title. The fact that there are legions of little Machina-ites out there makes perfect sense. My advice? Get yourself a stable of freaks... I mean... supporters... like me. Oh, and if you ever run for president, kill everyone with purple hair first. Our next and final letter comes from an "Schuk17". He writes: -------------------------------------------------------------- Hello, Allow me to introduce myself. My name is [name withheld], and I founded and help oversee the Fantasy Wrestling Organization, a coalition of regional e-federations known as the National Wrestling Council. Having been around over two years ourselves, we lay claim to one of the biggest pools of handlers, writers, and characters in the game today. The NWC emulates the heyday (not the current) of the National Wrestling Alliance, when men holding the World Title travelled on a cutthroat schedule to defend, and everyone involved felt connected to the fabric of NWA. It was established, pure. In the "real" Pro Wrestling ratings wars, we'll never have that feeling again. Why am I writing you? Hopefully this letter will change the course of your "career" in this hobby we call e-wrestling. Currently, a region or two within the walls of the National Wrestling Council (http://nwcouncil.tripod.com or nwconline.hypermart.net) has openings. I invite you to browse our site, get a feel for what we're all about, and put in an application.. knowing you have nothing to lose, and everything to gain, associating yourself with the Council. Please close this mail if you aren't interested, with my apology on behalf of NWC. But this could be _your_ chance to create a new character and feel the true roleplaying experience. Chasing your dreams. Finding the big show. Signing with a regional affiliate. Working out a character that can get over, locally and eventually nationally. The first series of matches. What's next? The rest of the journey is up to you, _if_ you can strive to surpass your goals in your region. That's not even mentioning the others desiring what you do.. the unified NWC World Heavyweight Title. Otherwise known as the most prestigious championship in fantasy sports, because there are few or no other titles with the lineage _and_ coverage our unilateral National Titles (World, Tag, and Cruiserweight) kept and earned. With that, I will close this letter of invitation. I assure you, if you honor it by taking time to visit the NWC, you will realize why this letter and our organization are different from any you've ever seen. Thank you very much for your time. Our site is located at http://nwc2k.hypermart.net, our cards are constantly archived online at NWCouncil Groups, and our online application (but not specific regional apps which are recommended too) is at Flashbase. Given that this mail was sent to you by me, one-on-one, I hope to hear from you. Thanks. -------------------------------------------------------------- Well, that's interesting. You know, I've often thought about going to commissioner Beaven and asking him to let me set up an affiliate to the fWo called the mWo. In it all the titles would be masked, and everyone would wrestle under assumed names. Oh yeah, and there would be lots of bongo music. Everyone gets bongo music. I think that would capture the feeling of the original fWo, and give it some of that Extreme Championship Wrestling feel. You know, because of the bongos. Anyway, that's all the mail we have right now. Thanks to everyone for writing! You really helped a lot, and I'm sure that tomorrow I'll have to make my attempt to talk to Penny once and for all. Yep, tomorrow. I'd like to close this by saying that if you've seen our Billy James I'd like him returned to us. Ever since that Rain crystal thingie broke it's like he never existed. What does that mean anyway? I... ::Machina was suddenly interupted when a producer ran into the room in a state of panic. More fWo officals ran through the room and several people began to shout. Machina turned and yelled.:: What in the HELL is going on? ::One of the producers turned and his face was white.:: Joy... Ryan... he's... ::Machina looked wide-eyed as he got to his feet.:: What's happened? ::The camera faded out.::
