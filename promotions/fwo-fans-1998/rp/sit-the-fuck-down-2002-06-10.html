Sit the Fuck Down

10 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I've heard and read enough during his time in the fWo. Ruben Ross' main part on fWo shows was to be the WWE's answer to The Rock. Come out for five minutes, yap on about this or that, and the fans ate it up. It's one of the few similarities between the fWo and the WWE. This bothers me, simply for the fact that Ruben Ross was the worst pick to be ANYTHING in the fWo, let alone a main eventer. I have evidence to back this claim up. The smarks clamor about the fWo being in a slump. When did they talk about this? During Ross' title reign. When did the ratings go down? During the last fifteen minutes of each show, when Ross would come out and make a mockery of what used to be a sport. And meanwhile, the Icon had to watch the ratings go down with a two-move jackass at the head of the class. It's time that the D+ wrestlers sit the fuck down. Tonight, I will begin sending out report cards. And Ruben Ross would get an A in "receiving ridiculous amounts of assbeatings". Just a small time remains until Jon Crisp becomes the teacher, and the People's Idiot becomes the pupil. Disrespect, Ruben. Preach it all you want. You have no fucking idea what it means. While you've been going down on Universal, he's been taking out your teeth-gritting on me. Didn't he advise you against using your teeth while servicing him? You must not get blow jobs often, or you would know that. Fact is, you're just a no-talent son of a bitch who's about to get his. You sit out during the house shows, whining about your fucking contract. You're fucking signed to it, so fucking wrestle. We all went to all the shows, and poured our hearts out, night after night after night. And what did you fucking do? Sit on your ass, and tell Frankton that you don't like your fucking contract. I'm going to beat your ass all over MSG tonight...then, after the show, I'm going to beat your ass back to your fucking hotel room. Then I'm going to beat your ass all the way to the continental breakfast tomorrow. You're disrespected? You've only BEGUN to get disrespected, asshole. When I pin you, spit on you, and take your spot on the ladder, then you'll understand what disrespect is, champ.
