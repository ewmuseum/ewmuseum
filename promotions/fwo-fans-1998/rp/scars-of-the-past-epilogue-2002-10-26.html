Scars of the Past: Epilogue

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
August 4th, 2002 Horito exited the fWo Headquarters in Seattle, a new contract having been signed just minutes before. "Finally, I will have a chance to present Dragon to a solid promotion, and not be forced to endure the scars of the past. I may have had scars from past failures on my own or someone else's part, but this is the present, not the past, and I can make things go for the better." Horito walked down the street back towards his motel. It had been a hard last few months, but once again he was going to be in control of his own destiny, and none of the scars of the past would stop him from becoming the best he could possibly be.
