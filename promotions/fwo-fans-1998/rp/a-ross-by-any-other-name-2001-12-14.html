A Ross By Any Other Name

14 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We fade in to Ric Chronos, pacing back and forth. The fWo World Heavyweight Title gleams from its perch around Ric's waist, and the black "Time Kills" t-shirt and dark jeans serve to make the belt that much more prominent. He pauses, letting the camera get a good view of his side profile, before he begins to speak. "Ruben Ross - The 'Black Plague' of the fWo - or '3 Hate Me'. Master of such great moves as 'The Black Sabbath', 'The Plague Of Darkness', "The Spin Cycle', 'The Fade To Black', 'The Blackout', 'Paint It Black', 'Plagued', and the dreaded 'Judge, Jury, & Executioner'. You surround yourself with all these colorful names. Why, Ross? I think that it's because you use these names in hopes that people will forget the other names that apply to you. Like 'Loser'. 'Might Have Been'. 'Never Was'. ...and, of course, 'Insignificant'. Poor, poor Mr. Ross. It seems that, when you have all these incredible names, that they give you power. They give you reason, a purpose. You know what they haven't given you, Ruben? A singles victory against the fWo World Heavyweight Champion - me. Which leads me to a few other names that are associated with you. 'Crybaby'. 'Whiner'. 'Choke Artist'. You want to take up the fWo's time with your tirade against me? Your claims that I've never beaten you 'clean'? Well, guess what, moron - 'clean' or not, I beat you. Period. And at Twilight, I'll beat you again, in your pathetic hometown of San Diego. And although 'your house', the Sports Arena, is horrid, Frenchie keeps reminding me that at least it's not Wisconsin. But you shouldn't be worried about that, Ruben. Oh no, that's the least of your problems. You see, at Twilight, 3vil will take home full control of the fWo. Not just the booking power, but hiring and firing as well. And, in my - in our fWo, I doubt there will be room for crybabies. So, you begin counting the days Ross... the days until you head for the unemployment line. This was Ric Chronos, and I'm outta here like I just stole your thunder." Ric unleashes a shark-like grin, and the camera does the long fade.
