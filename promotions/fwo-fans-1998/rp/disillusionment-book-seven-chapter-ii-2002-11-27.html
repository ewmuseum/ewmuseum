Disillusionment: Book Seven - Chapter II

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It is coming to an end, and I can feel it in my soul. Everything I have worked for will come to summit soon, but I know not how it will end. The climax of my being—my entire existence—is in its eve, and I go on, unafraid. Victory may not be achieved, and it’s something I realize. Tomorrow, it will end. Tomorrow, someone will die. Tomorrow, the world will be reborn…or, if my determination lets me down, the world will see tomorrow as merely another day, and it will be only the Disciples and myself who will know any different. Tomorrow, I strike. I have come to realize that failure is not an option, and I don’t mean in some bullshit Marines sense. Failure, even without my death, brings an end to my existence. I fight the Disciples, not because I want to, but because it is my destiny. Destiny……my worst enemy. Even more than the Disciples, I hate myself. I hate what I am and I hate what I represent. I am not a martyr, as my death will bring glory to no cause. With my death, only the satisfaction in my soul will remain for me to collect. As a famous author once said: ”Oh my soul, so may thy face be by me when I close my life indeed! so may I, when realities are melting from me like the shadows which I now dismiss, still find thee near me, pointing upward!” My hatred runs deep. In my life, I have loved, I have cherished, I have fought, I have run…but now, all I can do is hate. Whoever. I hate the Disciples, I hate myself, I hate you, and I hate the people I am trying to save, but my quest is not my choice. I go on, and I will fight with my life on the line tomorrow as I have every day for ten years. The worst thing of all is the thought that all this wrestling may have been for naught. If I die tomorrow, everything I have worked for in the ring will mean nothing. Every drop of blood, every rolling tear, every sweating gland, every bruise, every bump, the aches, the pains, all of it…meaningless. But if that is the way that chapter ends, completing the novel that is my life, then it must be so. For ten years, I’ve feared my death. Deny it I have, but any who believed me were truly deceived. However, I fear death no longer, and that is not a jest. My death will come. Whether it be now or forty years from now, it will come, and all I can do is prepare for it. ”Fight and you may die…Run, and you’ll live…at least a while. And lying in your beds, many years from now, would you be willing to trade…all the days, from this day to that…for one chance—just one chance!—to come back here and tell our enemies that they can take our lives, but they’ll never take, our freedom!” Those words are not mine, but they are who I am. My situation could not more perfectly be put into words, and I will not try to. Words are not my expertise. I am no poet. I shall write no novels. Even if I liberate the human race from the masters they don’t realize they serve, my story will be forgotten. Excitement? I have never felt it. Fear…despair…hatred…pain…suffering; these things are not excitement. Is that what you thought excitement is? Let me tell you something about excitement. Roller coasters and kayaking are excitement, but when your life is on the line every single day, you are not excited. If you think my life is exciting, I would be more than happy to trade it with you. Unfortunately, that is not a choice I can make. My decisions have been made for me. Not that long ago, I was visited by a friend of mine. He, like I, was on the run. The Disciples were after him, and he knew his days were numbered………What he did not know was how numbered they were. He told me something very interesting. I thought he was crazy, made delusional by the paranoia that his life had become. I didn’t believe what he told me for a long time, but now…I must face the truth in what that man, named Burkenstock, told me. I am the Chosen One.
