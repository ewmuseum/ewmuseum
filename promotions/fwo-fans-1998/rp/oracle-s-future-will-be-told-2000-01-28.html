Oracle's future will be told

28 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Oracle's future will be told January 28, 2000 The scene opens in the boiler room under the American Airlines Arena, in Miami, Florida. The room was not lit at all. Suddenly, a string was pulled and a light had gone on right in the center of the room. The cameraman noticed that it had been Matt Douglas standing under the light. He started toward Matt, and stopped when he saw the glare of anger that was coming out of the eyes of Matt Douglas. The second thing that the cameraman seemingly noticed was the bandage wrapped around the head of Matt Douglas. Matt: So Oracle thinks he is a big tuff guy by coming out and attacking me after I had won my match for a shot at the International Title. Well, I see that we are going to be meeting again in the near future, for we have a match on Slaughter. Well, I notice that Courage and Wretch are going to be there, but I am sure if they are smart enough, they will step out of my way. But if they decide not to, I will just have to go through them, and get to you. Matt starts to hold his head, and then begins to speak again. Matt: Oh, Oracle's future will be told, and I am going to tell him as well as you all, that it isn't looking good at all. Matt lets go of his head and walks over to the nearest wall and picks up a lead pipe. The cameraman sees this, and begins to back up. Matt then begins to strike the wall, that the pipe was leaning up against. He then proceeds to start striking things at random. The cameraman quickly sees this, and runs out of the room as Matt Douglas nearly misses his head. The scene fades to black as the cameraman is running down the hallway and in the background, we can still hear the sound of the lead pipe striking various items.
