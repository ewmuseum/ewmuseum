A Road Paved In Broken Glass: Part Four

3 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"The years moved on and so did I. I went to college, and threw everything away for a life full of parties and girls. One thing led to another and before I knew it, I was living on the streets in Hollywood. I wasn't there for long though, before I stumbled upon the chance of a lifetime. It might have been too good to be true... because I managed to find a way to fuck it up." July 6th, 2001. Milo had been enjoying the last six months, more than words could describe. He had been taken off the streets by famous director Robert Felman, and was scheduled to make his acting debut within the next few weeks. "How could things be any better?" thought Milo as his took a sip of lemonade. It had been a long, hot day of pool lounging; something Milo was no stranger to the past few weeks. Milo closed his eyes, and began to daydream of movie stardom. Only a few moments had passed, before Milo noticed something was blocking the sun. He opened his eyes to see a beautiful girl standing in front of him, wearing a string bikini. It was Mindy Felman, Robert's supermodel wife. "Is this all you do?" said Mindy, looking unimpressed with her hands on her hips. "You've been out here every day for the last two weeks." Milo, still a tad groggy, was uncertain if the question was rhetorical or not. So he remained sitting, paying little attention to the woman that stood before him. "You'll get a sunstroke if you are out here much longer." Mindy said as she stepped closer to Milo grabbing onto his hand. "Come on, I need you to help me with something." Milo stood and followed Mindy's lead into the Felman's Hollywood mansion, the place that he had been living the past few weeks. "It's in here." Said Mindy, leading Milo upstairs into the master bedroom. "What is it anyway?" Milo asked, trying to figure out what she could possibly need. "This..." she replied, before pushing Milo onto the nearby bed. Milo was overwhelmed at what was happening, and before he knew it he was lip locked with a married woman. Although it was a tough thing to do, Milo pushed Mindy away. "What the fuck?" Milo said, unable to think of anything else to say. "You are a married woman!" "There is no love in my marriage Milo, only money." Mindy whispered softly, kissing Milo's neck. Milo, still completely shocked by what was happening, pushed Mindy away again. "What the fuck" he said for a second time, only this time in the form of a statement rather then a question. "Why else did you think I was with him? True love?" asked Mindy, placing a finger atop Milo's lips. Milo was still on a mental roller coaster ride, and was unable to form any sort of intelligible reply. "Well... ah.. um... one would assume you loved someone if you married them. Wouldn't they?" "That's cute." Mindy replied, removing her bikini top, and throwing it to the floor. "Really cute." Milo was once again lip locked with Mindy, and the remaining half of her bikini found its way to the floor. As tempting as it was, Milo knew this was wrong, and any minute Robert would walk through the door and say... GET THE FUCK OFF MY WIFE! Robert charged into the room, and threw Mindy to the floor. Milo knew Robert had every reason to be mad, but he couldn't sit there and watch him manhandle her. *Smack* Milo connected with a hard left, that caused Roberts knees to buckle, sending him falling to the floor. He knew he had overstayed his welcome, and figured now would be a good time to make an exit. Milo pulled up his shorts, and ran out of the mansion, leaving the dream of movie stardom behind.
