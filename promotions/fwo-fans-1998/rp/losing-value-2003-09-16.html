Losing Value

16 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm at a bar. Just a regular bar. I don't want to get myself into anymore trouble than I already have. In case you got confused about Monday's show, let me lay it out for you. That segment of me on the phone was supposed to be after my match with Eron. This is what happened: Someone saw me at the strip club on September 3. That someone decided it'd be clever to make it public to the world that I was at a strip club, but he added a little bit of a twist. That someone said I was at a male strip club. Now you see my dilemma. If I don't want to break Clarisse's heart, I have to deny the rumors completely. More importantly, if I want to keep my dignity and fan support I have to deny the rumors completely. I'm supposed to be a wholesome baby face. I'm supposed to be the last hero standing in a world where anti-hero is king. You all saw me lose to Great Dragon. You all saw me lose to High Flyer. Hell, you even saw me lose to Eron. ERON. Nobody loses to Eron. My stock has officially gone down. Everybody thinks I'm gay. We all know how wrestling fans respond to that. My cell phone goes off. I look at who it is. It's my agent. I send the message straight to my voice mail. I know exactly what he's going to say. "Your stock is dropping, Cody. You're losing matches left and right. The fans are starting to turn on you. We have to come up with some sort of a plan to get you back in the driver's seat." Let's face it, I'm just a normal guy who's had a turn of bad luck lately. The problem is I don't know who to be pissed off at about it. That person is somewhere, and I will find him... or her. It's just a matter of time.
