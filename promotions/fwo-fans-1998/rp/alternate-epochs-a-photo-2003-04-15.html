ALternate Epochs: A Photo

15 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kevin Masters rummaged through his suitcase, and a picture frame fell to the floor. He looked down at it, and then smiled. He could recall everything about where that picture had been taken, and everything about the night it had been taken in. It was one of the greatest memories of his life, from a happier time in what felt like a different world, or even a whole different life. The date was January 12, 1995. The place was in the heart of Manhattan at the Waldorf Astoria in New York City. At sixteen years old, Kevin Mastersov stood at the foot of a stage staring up into the eyes of Eddie Vedder and Neil Young. Vedder had been one of the era's rising superstars with his Seattle grunge rock band, Pearl Jam. Young was arguably one of the most influential rockers of all time. He stood there as they performed a new version of Young's timeless classic, "Keep on Rockin' in the Free World." He stared in awe as his favorite band performed with a legend. His father was somewhere in the building, the reason he had the opportunity to view the concert in the first place. At his side were his two best friends. Mitch and Donna. They too were reveling in the experience, celebrating Young's induction to the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in a way they would remember forever. As the sweat poured down his face, he felt immensely lucky. This was something nobody else in the world could do, and his father, his father was the one that made it possible. His mother had objected, but pleading on his part had gotten her to grant him his wish. And it was well worth it. As Donna looked at him, smiling with magic in her eyes, his stomach dropped. His heart began to expand in his chest. He felt so happy... As Kevin stood in a hotel room in Europe, looking at his photo, he realized that he hadn't truly cherished that moment. That moment that he knew he was definitely in love with Donna. Kevin had all the happiness in the world right at his fingertips in that moment, the moment that was taken away by a crash. That crash had robbed him of so much, so many future memories. It had robbed Mitch of his legs, and Donna of her life. It had robbed Kevin of his love, and in many ways his youth. Years later, Kevin would choose that very recording of that very song as his first entrance music in REALM. It was a tribute to his friends. But still, when he saw that photo, and held it in his hands, and saw the happy looks on the faces of the four people in it. He couldn't help but smile. Smile and remember a time long past, a time of happiness.
