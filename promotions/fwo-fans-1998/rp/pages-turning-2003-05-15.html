Pages Turning

15 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Eli Flair couldn't sleep. He wrestled what he felt was a substandard match against The Dynamo last night on Warfare, and combined with the news that both the fWo's television programs are ending, as well as his wife's band unsuccessfully negotiating with Sony Records have put a lot on his mind. Hence, Eli Flair couldn't sleep. "I don't care if your world is ending today, I wasn't invited to it anyway You said I tasted famous so I threw you a heart But now I'm not an artist I'm a fucking work of art" Dressed in a black Metallica T-shirt, cutoff sweat pants, and faded, worn VANS, Eli has done something so far removed from his usual routine that nobody in their right mind would ever guess that he's taking an early- morning jog. The usual post- show routine last night of a beer with Ivy and a recap of the match with Kevin Masters went off as usual. Talking to Angel on the phone - usual. Something feels different, and he's hoping an uncharacteristic jog will help him figure it out. "I've got an F and a C and I've got a K too And the only thing missing is a bitch like you" With the Golden Age of Grotesque plugged into his ear, Eli's thinking about the last time he saw Marilyn Manson... it was during his 1999 "Rock is Dead" tour and Ivy used her connections to get them backstage. Music was taking quite a different direction back then, as rock/rap was getting more mainstream, and it seemed to coincide with a new "Attitude" in professional wrestling. Manson and Eli hit it off that night, two similar artists with drastically different canvases. Irony, then - that Eli is listening to the man during a similar upheaval of his profession. "You wanted perfekt, you got your perfekt But now I'm too perfekt for someone like you I was a dandy in your ghetto with a snow white smile And you'll never be as perfekt whatever you do" He and Poison Ivy are two of - if not the two - most travelled members of the fWo locker room. They took the extended overseas tour in stride - they took the possibility of the promotion shutting down at Cyberslam in stride - they took the news about the TV deal in stride. It's nothing new to a man who once wrestled in front of a hundred thousand in Mexico City as part of a twenty- promotion indy pay- per- view show one night, and fifty- nine bored teenagers in a bingo hall the next. It's nothing new to a woman who finished final exams in the winter at college one day and was appearing in the famous Merritt Auditorium for her professional wrestling debut the next. Things change. Life goes on. Turn the page, start a new chapter. "What's my name, what's my name? Hold the S because I am an AINT What's my name, what's my name? Hold the S because I am an AINT" Deep down, he knows he's stressing too much about last night's match. Masters looked like a million bucks - an old school wrestler who made one mistake. One. In the eyes of the fans, he can keep his head in the game and he's got all the tools. That's one of Eli's strengths - the way to make his opponent look good while keeping every bit of his heat. He's having a lot of fun, considering he's separated from his Angel and his little angel. Wrestling every show to make money to support his family until his wife is back on her feet. Wrestling good matches, too - no matter how short of his standards they fall. Considering his standards have only been reached against the following opponents: Troy Windham, The Deacon, Steven Shadows, Donaven Winters, Ruben Ross. Ouch. "I am a bonetop, a death's head on a mopstick You infected me, took diamonds, I took all of your shit Your "sell- by" date expired so you had to be sold I am a suffer-genius and Vivi-sex symbol" It's a crossroads for this man, this King of Extreme, this pillar of what it means to work hard in the wrestling business. He's earned almost everyone's respect in the locker room, in the arena, and in the sheets. Eli Flair, thanks to his nature, is one of the most well- known wrestlers outside the wrestling world. A dozen World Championships, too... and when you consider that the Asylum's World Title is in the mix, he's earned the respect of fighting fans, too. Rest in peace, Warfare. Rest in pieces, Slaughter. "You wanted perfekt, you got your perfekt But now I'm too perfekt for someone like you I was a dandy in your ghetto with a snow white smile And you'll never be as perfekt whatever you do" Poison Ivy thinks he should be shooting for more right now. His circle of friends in the locker rooms has dissolved, his "Rat Pack" has turned against itself. Lance Knight has grown envious of the way Aimz is closer to Brand Frontier nowadays than he is. Triple X, a man who has become a brother to Eli over the years, is taking a leave of absense for reasons that he still doesn't know and that Ivy isn't telling. It's his perogative - he doesn't owe Eli a thing - but he misses his boy terribly. So why not shoot for something else? "What's my name, what's my name? Hold the S because I am an AINT What's my name, what's my name? Hold the S because I am an AINT" Because it'd be too hard? He's the King of Extreme - one of a handful who revolutionized the art of full- contact wrestling in the mid- 1990s, and could easily conquer the Hardcore division of the fWo if he threw himself into it as hard as he could. But what would that gain him? He's done it twice, restoring a lot of the dignity that the belt had lost during its year of being tossed around like a prop, the match he and Winters had at Cyberslam V was the most talked- about match of the show. And to become a part of that division again would take some attention away from the current titleholder, Aimz. She's worked so hard to get to this point, she deserves all the attention she can get. "I've got an F and a C and I've got a K too And the only thing missing is a bitch like you I've got an F and a C and I've got a K too And the only thing missing is a bitch like you" United States? No way. Not gonna touch it. Too much going on right now with Brand, Lance, and Voran to get into the mix. Besides, Brandon and Lance have enough problems right now between the two of them and Voran insinuating himself on the situation. Brandon doesn't need another one of his friends trying to get over at his expense. And he would, too. In addition to being professional wrestling's ninja, Eli Flair is also wrestling's contradiction in terms. How else can you explain how some punk- ass white boy ghetto trash from the Bronx is one of the most wildly loved athletes in the sport, alongside true heroes like Deacon, like Shane Southern, like Brand Frontier. "I am a dandy in your ghetto with a snow white smile Super- ego bitch, I've been evil awhile I am a dandy in your ghetto with a snow white smile Super- ego bitch, I've been evil awhile" World Champion, Eli Flair? It's a nice thought, but it was made clear to Eli months ago that he was not welcome in the Main Event of the fWo. He dared to have an opinion of the World Champion that the World Champion didn't like, and the two clashed over it. Adding to the mix, thought he doubts Kelly knew, was that Ivy tried to do a political runaround and get a program approved without the Champ's knowledge. Personal feelings aside, such political games aren't Eli's style. He can deal with telling a man he's an embarassment as a champion. He can deal with being accused of being angry over not getting the program. He can even deal with being told he'll never make it as a champion. At least everyone knows where everyone stands in that case. But... a little voice reminded him... there's a new administration at the top of the mountain. Let it be, Ivy, he tells the voice in his head... let it be. "What's my name, what's my name? Hold the S because I am an AINT What's my name, what's my name? Hold the S because I am an AINT" He hits the pavement harder than ever, trying to keep pace with the beat of the music. For Eli Flair, as one chapter of the fWo closes permanently and another opens up... there's nothing but possibilities. And anything is possible if you work for it. Even for an Original Nobody.
