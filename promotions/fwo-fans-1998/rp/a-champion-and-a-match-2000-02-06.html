A Champion and a Match

6 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization A Champion and a Match February 06, 2000 The scene opens with Matt Douglas stepping out of a limo, and walking towards the arena. He is dressed in his street clothes, with the International Title draped over his left shoulder. The cameraman is hidden in the bushes, about 10 to 15 feet from Matt. He hears rustling, but continues to walk. As Matt walks out of view of the camera, the cameraman stands up and begins to exit out of the bushes. But as he walks the way that he thought Douglas was going, he is met by nothing but cold air blowing into his face. As he continues to scan the area, with his camera still on, Matt jumps out of the bushes and scares the cameraman half to death. Matt: Oh, it is just you. I thought someone was really following me. Cameraman: You almost made me drop this camera Matt, and that would have come out of your pay check. Matt: Sorry, but next time why don't you just approach me as a normal person. Cameraman: I will have to remember that. Matt: So what do you want? Oh yeah, probably an interview with the new International Title champion. The cameraman rolls his eyes, and begins to speak. Cameraman: Yes, that is exactly what I want. But I think I have a couple of questions written down for you here somewhere. The cameraman pulls a piece of paper out of his pocket and begins to ask the questions. Cameraman: How do you feel about winning the International Title? Matt: Well, I would have to say that it is an honor to be able to hold a title of this stature. Cameraman: Ok, whatever, just let me get to the next question and final question. Matt: Ok. Cameraman: How do you feel about your match against the Black Panther? I mean he was like the longest person to hold the International Title. Matt: Sure, I knew Panther for a long time, and he has developed into a great wrestler, who has held the World Title. So I have no doubt in my mind that I am going to lose. I always try to go into my matches thinking that I am going to lose, so that I don't make myself look stupid. Most of the time when I say that I am going to win, I am just saying that I hope I win. I don't put myself down, but I always try to ask myself one question. Is your opponent working harder than you are? Usually, that keeps me working, and striving to do my best, under any conditions. Cameraman: I didn't know you had all of that in you. Matt: I was just answering your question. The scene fades to black as the cameraman shakes Matt's hand and exits the scene.
