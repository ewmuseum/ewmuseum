Backstage ramblin'

16 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Backstage ramblin' March 16, 2000 Sid walked nervously down the hallway and toward the phone booth at the end of the hall. Wednesday Warfare had just ended, and he was anxious to call David to see how he was doing. Their relationship was rocky, but the upcoming Bachelor party was hopefully going to soothe that all out. After all, how could you not feel romantic when you were sitting on a beach in the Bahamas with David in his Speedos? Sid saw a small group of ruffians coming nearer to him and stood next to the wall as they passed. One was talking loudly to the others and seemed to be annoyed about something. Man, this American wrestling sucks! You're right Won Rainden... this fWo stuff sucks. I can't wait to get back to the computer, log onto the internet and school all the little freaks in a way only the Workrate Poo can! Let's see that ICV guy respond to that! The one who had been speaking let out a hitching laugh and slapped hands with another rather poorly dressed wrestling fan who laughed and started to speak. Huh huh, yeah that's right Danscartspee... if only that ICV guy wasn't so damn right most the time. Lousy... The first man stopped and turned to the one addressed as Rainden. Don't ever say such things! We are the Poo! ICV may be smarter than us, quicker than us, and superior in every way but we must never admit it! Now come on... The small group of wrestling fans continued down the hall and were quickly out of sight. Sid shook his head slowly. Man... they let anyone backstage nowadays... He spotted the telephone and walked over to it, fishing into his pocket for spare change. The telephone was dirty, and before Sid put it up to his ear he was careful to wipe it down with a hanky. He dialed, and listed to the phone ring. After four rings it was clear nobody was home, and Sid put the phone back in it's cradle forlornly sighing to himself. Rudo walked up and noticed Sid's sad expression. Hey Sid... why the frown? Did you see Machina's match tonight? Looks like you won your bet as to who would be the first to feature full frontal on a show. I owe you ten bucks. Who would have ever thought that Mike Bare would have not lived up to his name? Sid smiled weakly. It's Bear actually. Like the animal. Rudo frowned. Really? Well, I suppose that does make more sense. Anyway, what is bothering you anyway? David again? Sid nodded. He's not at home. it's like... eleven o' clock where he is... Rudo put a comforting arm around Sid's shoulders and then thought better of it and settled for a clap on the back. Man, why do you put up with him? You know he's no better than Rob... I mean, the only difference between Rob and David is that David smiles while he's stabbing you in the back. Everybody knows that... Sid frowned. That's not true! I may think Rob is pond scum, but David is a nice guy... I'm sure of it! Rudo rolled his eyes. Man, how many times are people going to tell you horror stories about what David is really like before you wake up? I mean, you could do so much better... I'm sure... I think anyway. To tell you the truth I'm not sure how the whole gay attraction thing works... but whatever happened to your attraction to Slugger? Sid sniffed. He's not gay... he's got some groupie girlfriend. Rudo frowned. He's not? Well, I guess I owe Ice ten bucks too. At this rate I'll have to sell off my Cruiserweight title to pay everyone off... listen, come on and cheer up. We've got the bachelor party coming soon, and I've got all kinds of great guests scheduled. Machina, myself, you of course... all those guests you just had to invite along... Rudo rolled his eyes again and continued. ...I've signed the best band in the world to play for us, have you ever heard of the Smashing Pumpkins? They agreed to come along on the boat! Plus I hear that Sheryl Crow might be there, wouldn't that be cool? You can get your collection signed! A couple guys I used to know in college are coming along... this Won Rainden guy and some other people... Sid remembered the small group of nutty wrestling fans he had seen earlier and groaned. Rudo ignored this and continued to rattle off the guest list. Big time wrestling writers like David Peltzer.. Wade Deiter, hell even this wrestling columnist Slapjack Carsons is coming! It's going to be a huge party! So come on, smile and lets go get Machina and get out of here! Rudo and Sid walked down the hallway, Sid's spirits lifting slightly as they went.
