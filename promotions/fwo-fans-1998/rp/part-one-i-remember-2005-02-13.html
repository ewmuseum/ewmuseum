Part One: I Remember

13 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Six hour layovers in St. Louis were usually the low point of any professional wrestling tour. Especially when a plane is late and the next flight back to the fWo tour is twenty nine hours away. The back and forth between the tour where her job and her passion were, and the city where the kids and their future were waiting was starting to show its effect on the Psycho Bitch. "C'mon... c'mon..." To top it off, everyone in her phone book was either busy, out of area, or ignoring her. "Hey, it's Sean... leave one." She hung up the phone instead of leaving one. Ivy stretched her legs out under the table at the airport bar and sighed heavily. The half - finished Vodka - Lime in front of her no longer appealed with nobody around to share it with. The television in the bar was tuned to ESPN2, and highlights of the previous week of fWo action. Ivy wasn't watching - she'd missed half the show but she knew what was going down. Besides, the small collection of travelers who were sitting at the bar were discussing the show in depth, apparently trying to be below - average students in the Keith Scott Zimmerman School of Analytical Thought. "This Valentine's Day, show the one you love how much you love them, with a beautiful Vermont Teddy Bear..." Commercial - and a sappy, marketing commerical at that. The kind of thing that makes people in love go "Awwwwwh!" and the kind of thing that makes people who are alone spit on the floor and drink some foul beer. For some reason, Ivy thought of the Vermont Teddy Bear that still sat on top of her desktop computer at her Queens apartment, the bear that her former lover, 'Triple X' Sean Stevens had bought for her the last Valentine's Day that they were together, two years ago. She hadn't seen him for a year and a half, when this past January he suddenly walked back into her life. Two weeks ago, they'd gone out and had a drink at the hotel bar after Monday Night Ruahh~!!, reminiscent to both of the night they first got together, three years ago in Paris. Three years had made them both older, wiser, and much more emotionally guarded. At least, Ivy was more emotionally guarded. For all she knew, Trip was up in Canada on the tour, hooking up with another ringrat. For all she knew, Trip was up in Canada on the tour, hooking up with several ringrats. For all she knew, Trip was, at this very moment... making plans for Monday with Linda Johns. Ivy's rational mind told her to stop torturing herself, that Trip wasn't doing anything of the sort - he was working. Her rational mind then continued to remind herself that Trip actually owed her nothing. Her rational mind continued to remind her that Trip dumped her, and she owed Trip less than nothing. Unfortunately, her rational mind was working on six hours of sleep since Monday, due to travel, jetlag, and getting Knox, Cally, and Lou ready for a semi - big indy show this weekend. This one was an exception to her typical rule with Knox, that she'd kick his ass if he was wrestling instead of going to school - he was on break. Her rational mind had been gagged and hogtied by her aching heart, and she missed her former lover terribly. "Hey, little lady... couldn't help noticin' you from across the room... can I buy you a drink, maybe turn that frown upside down?" While she'd been thinking about the past few years and mentally berating herself for not speaking up when Trip came back, she'd apparently been joined by some company. "Thank you, but no," replied Ivy to the man sitting across from her, "I'd just like to be alone." He responded by reaching across the table toward her face. Ivy grabbed his wrist, inches from her cheek, with a strength that seemed to surprise him. "I'm just tryin' to dry them tears," he explained, a look of mild fear in his eyes." Ivy touched her cheek - sure enough, there were damp spots below her eyes. She let his wrist go and took her glasses off to wipe the tears from her eyes. They kept coming. "Listen, darlin'," he continued, "whoever's makin' them tears come from those beautiful green eyes needs t'learn a lesson. What's your name?" She looked into his eyes - there was no perceived malice behind them or any kind of ulterior motive that she could discern, besides the obvious attempt to pick her up. "Ivy," she said, "McGinnis. But I'm sorry, I'm really not in the mood for this tonight." Without another word, she rose from her seat and left the bar area. To her surprise, her new friend wasn't following. Good thing, too, because as soon as she was out of sight, the floodgates opened. Ivy dropped her bag on the floor and began to scan through her telephone again. Everyone that she would typically call in order to unload her brain, she'd already called at least once today. Rana wasn't answering. Miles would be busy with one of his twenty to thirty projects. Eli didn't have a cell phone. Angel would answer, but Ivy wouldn't call her. She and MJ were in Hollywood at that very moment, working 'round the clock on Fire Water. Sean was out with another woma-- no, she wasn't going to do that again. Flipping through her phone, Ivy stopped suddenly... on a name she hadn't thought of calling. It was a name she'd put into her phone solely on a whim several weeks ago. A moment of hesitation was broken down by a sudden, overwhelming rush of sadness. Three rings later... "Hello?" "Hello, Dolores? This is Ivy McGinnis, do you rememb--" "Ivy, of course I remember you. How are..." She dropped her bag on the sofa, as Dolores Wakeman heard Ivy's erratic breathing. "Honey, what's the matter?" "I don't really know," replied Ivy, "I just... I needed someone to talk to. I'm sorry, I didn't want to bother y--" "Where are you right now?" interrupted Dolores. Ivy seemed a bit confused by the question. "St. Louis airport," she answered, "I missed my connection and--" "I'll be right there." The phone hung up. Ivy stood there with a look of disbelief on her face. The mother of the man she'd pretended to be dating for the sake of their relationship was on her way to the airport to pick her up and (in theory) listen to her rant about the man she was truly in love with. Ironic, no?
