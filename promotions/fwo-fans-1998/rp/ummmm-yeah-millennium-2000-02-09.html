Ummmm, yeah, Millennium

9 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Ummmm, yeah, Millennium February 09, 2000 (The camera fades in on the same locker room the Underground shot the Riders parody from earlier tonight. Though its unclear at first, we can tell that its Mike Bear standing in the middle of the room. Hes wearing his regular tights and a small black sheet with the eyes cut out over his head) Mike: I am the Black Panther. (looks around) Ummmm, yeah. (looks at his hand which has notes scribbled on it) Uh, apparently Im the savior of the fWo and...dammit, Wretch Im dying out here! (Wretch walks in the room wearing the back half of a cheap horse costume) Wretch: Are you sure theyre going to get this joke? Mike: Well, its kind of a stretch, Ill admit. He says hes *cough* hung like a horse, so you come out dressed as a horses ass. What was the joke we were going to use? Wretch: Well, why dont you just call him a horses ass? Mike: Because Im not 10 years old. If Im going to insult someone, I need justification. Wretch: Well, I think were using the costume as a cheap laugh. Mike: Ok, thatll work. Hold on, let me get back in character. (Mike turns away from the camera and turns back to Wretch) Why hello, JeriHavok, what are you up to? Wretch (whining): Panther, stop calling me that! Mike: Oh stop whining, youre JeriHavok and youll like it. You parodied Jericho and youll never live that down. Now, why are you here? Wretch: I tried to buy the ice cream off a little kid and he kicked me. Mike: When will you learn you cant stand up to little kids, much less Mike Bear, a credible wrestler? Wretch: I know, I know. Hey, have you seen me in that issue of Playgirl? Mike: Of course not. Why would I want to look at an issue of Playgirl? I am certainly not gay. I am also certain that Mike Bear would not make a cheap crack at his opponents sexuality, because thats just juvenile. Wretch: Oh no, Ive done that to the man I am honored to call my friend, Wretch, what should I do? Mike: You only have one option, to beg the forgiveness of the greatest stable in the fWo, The Underground. Wretch: Youre right, Im going right now (Wretch walks out, once again leaving Mike alone again) Mike: Ummmmm...shit. I...I used to be a World Champion? Ah just turn it off! (The camera abruptly fades to black)
