Getting Ahead: Cyber Scam. III

7 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"We're going to destroy this!" Tyler yelled, causing everyone within a forty-foot radius to turn their heads and see what the ruckus was. "We, the Fuse Bros., are going to destroy this horrible horrible videogame!" Dillon shrugged. "Destroy what Tyler?" "This!" He held the Cyber Slam IV video in his hands, shaking it madly in the air as he did. "Did you not listen to ANYTHING I just said!?" "Well yes, I did... but-" Tyler tossed the video at Dillon, who was caught off-guard by this. By reaching out his hands in a flash, Dillon was barely able to (but did) catch the video in his hands. Dillon checked the case over, smiling. "Nice cover... really nifty design fWo." "That's enough." Snapped Tyler, who waved his brother to follow him. "Now we're going for a little walk, Dillon... and you're coming." "Uh, Tyler, I'd love to... but I really have to sort through the new SJB t-shirts. We just got a lot in... and the sizes are all mixed together. This'll do no good for tomorrow when an eight year old and an eighty year old come over to this stand and want to buy one. I need to have the sizes together so the costumer doesn't have to wait, you know." Dillon looked up at Tyler, who didn't seem to be listening to any of his speech, as he was fixing his brown hat. "Did you hear anything I just said?" Dillon snapped. "Yeah. Totally. Now c'mon sprite... we need to go for a walk." Dillon huffed. "It'll only be two levels of PacMan long... okay?" Dillon sighed again. "I suppose." "Good. Follow me." Tyler lead the way, walking past fWo Crew 4, and then past fWo Crew 2. He continued to march down the hallway, with Dillon dragging closely behind him, and Conor a short ways back. "Yeah!" Conor said loudly, as he passed by worker after worker. "We're going to destroy this videogame! We're going to hop on its head!" Reaching the point he set out for, Tyler waited for Dillon and Conor to catch up to him. "Alright, let's go." Dillon looked up. An exit door? They were going outside? "Trust me." Tyler opened the door for Dillon, who left the Arena. Tyler then followed as Conor stayed back inside, waiting for the door to close behind him. "Everyone... say goodbye to Cyber Slam IV!" He screamed. "The game is officially no more! HAHAHAHAHA!!" Continuing to laugh his head off, Conor exited the Arena to meet with Tyler and Dillon.
