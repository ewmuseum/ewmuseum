I am....worried

12 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The shocked look on Xias' face was enough to make a hideous smile form on Enforcer's, and without a word, he brought the pipe square down onto Xias' forehead, busting him open and knocking him to the canvas, unconscious. Enforcer smirked down at him, and rose the pipe again. He smashed it into Xias' ribs, sending the metal crashing into flesh, again and again and aga- Xias suddenly awoke, his forehead drenched in sweat. He looked around at his surroundings- beautiful furniture in a large, designer room- and realized he was home. He felt a sudden moistness on his forehead that he realized was not his own sweat, and soon looked up to see Reika standing over him, holding a wet washcloth on his head. He found himself lost staring up into her brown eyes and then looking at her beautiful hair....she was truly stunning. "Are you alright, Matthew?" she asked him, a bit uncomfortable with the way he was staring at her. "I heard you talking in your sleep so I figured I'd come in here and see how you were doing." "Wh-What time is it?" Xias managed to stammer out. He looked over at a clock to the side of his big-screen TV, realizing it said 12:30 a.m. "I fell asleep?" he asked. "Yeah, during the start of Uprising." Reika replied. "I wasn't surprised, considering how little sleep you've got-" "Enforcer." Xias said suddenly, cutting her off. "Did he speak on Uprising?" "Yeah, he said something about revenge for something you did to him in the past. Or something. Generally came off like a great big crazy person. I mean, what kind of morbid nutcase holds a grudge for two years straight in this day and-" Xias blocked Reika out of his head. He began putting the pieces together in his mind, and realized that Enforcer must not have revealed anything about just WHY he was pissed off at him- after all, if he did Reika would probably be questioning him. No, not probably, he realized, DEFINATLEY. But if he didn't blurt it out at the first opportunity, Xias thought to himself, just what the hell was Enforcer waiting for? "-Hello, Matthew, are you listening to me?" Reika asked him, hands on her hips. "Y-Yeah, sorry." he replied uneasily. "Look, I think I need to try and get some more sleep." Reika nodded. "The doctor said you need plenty of rest." she agreed, starting to walk out of the living room. "I only wish you had started taking his advice earlier." Xias just nodded. "Well, goodnight." she said, walking out into the hallway. "Goodnight." Xias called after her. He looked around at his darkened surroundings. He was alone again. He let out a heavy sigh and grabbed his TiVo remote. As much as he didn't want to, he knew he'd have to watch Enforcer's little speech- maybe it would help him find out just what Enforcer has planned. He sighed again as he clicked the play button, and began to watch. It would be yet another long, sleepless night for Matthew Vorankias.
