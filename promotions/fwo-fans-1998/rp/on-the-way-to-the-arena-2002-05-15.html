On the way to the arena...

15 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the warm Florida sun beaming down on the limo, Monte Burns sits in the back soaking in the rays with the top open. The light breeze flows through as Burns looks out of the window at the dozens of people lining the streets. Not surprisingly, it seems that all of them are dressed in shirts, shorts, and other comfortable clothes. Burns, on the other hand, sits in the back of the limo with his traditional suit and tie outfit, dressed professionally as always. "Mr. Burns," comes the driver's voice from the front, "would you like to head to the arena right away or take a short drive first?" For a moment, Burns thought about what to do. In the past, he had always arrived the arena very early, wanting to spend as much time as possible getting ready for the night. Usually that meant arriving hours before the event, even before many of the officials arrived. On this day, however, Burns decided to go for a change. "Actually," he said while gazing out of the window, "let's drive around and check out the scenery for a bit." The limo continues ahead down the street, taking a turn to the left to head closer towards the beach. As Burns sees the edge of the beach in the distance, he notices a young woman riding a yellow mountain bike a short distance ahead. Her long black is tied back in a ponytail, and she's wearing an attractive top and matching shorts with pink and stripes. Burns smiles as he looks the woman up and down, as the limo approaches a stop light and nears the young woman. As the limo gets closer, Burns rolls his window down and leans his head out. "Hello there," he says with a smile. "Care to trade that bike in for a different ride?" The young woman turns and raises her eyebrows over the low edge of a pair of dark shades and smiles a familiar smile. "You are SO busted!" She whips off the shades to reveal herself as Tempest, her blue eyes sparkling in the sun. "Oh," Burns says with a forced smile, trying to keep things under control, "I...I knew that." Tempest folds her arms and looks at him with a wry grin. "Oh sure, I bet you say that to all the chicks on bikes in sunny Orlando." The light changes and as neither moves, a few horns honk. Tempest turns with an irritated glance and produces a horn form her bag, honking it back at the cars behind her. With a laugh, Burns looks back at the cars behind the limo and waves for them to go around. He turns around once again, this time looking Tempest in the eyes. "So what do you say?" he asks, nodding his head back. "Aww, sure," Tempest says with a chuckle. She rolls the bike toward the back of the car laughing. "After all, I'd wanna know if you really knew it was me just now!" As Burns opens his door and steps out, the driver starts to get out as well. Burns motions for him to get back in, and he does so. With Tempest waiting, Burns walks back arund to the back of the limo, while the driver pops the trunk. "That's an awful big bike you have there," he says shaking his head. "I'm not sure if it'll fit in the back here." "Sure it will, watch!" She says gleefully. She pops a few latches and the bike folds neatly. "After all, I have to get it on the bus. I have to have some way to get around." "You never cease to amaze me, Tempest," Burns says with a smile, shaking his head as he lifts the bike up. "Oooh careful," she says with a note of worry, trying to help him. "How's that back?" "It's fine," Burns says with a slight grimace as he places the bike in the trunk. "I just need to try and avoid certain things." "Like lifting mountain bikes?" she says patting his arm. "That's probably true," Burns says with a shrug, "but what's a guy to do?" After shutting the trunk, Monte turns around and smiles, putting his arm around Tempest's waist. He reaches back momentarily with his other hand to lightly rub the small of his back, then turns back to Tempest and smiles. Tempest smiles as she slips into the car and looks around. As Monte gets in she scoots next to him and says, "You gonna be ok tonight?" "I should be alright," he says as he slowly takes his seat and shuts the door. "Jon's been taking care of me pretty well. The consecutive nights really make it painful, though." Tempest nods and puts her hand over his. "I know, I was afraid that might be the case. You should take some time off. I mean I know things are tough, but still, you don't wanna hurt yourself even worse." Looking away quickly, Burns glances out at the sidewalk and sighs. Monte starts to say something but stops himself for a moment and thinks a few more moments. Tempest looks on curiously, wondering if something was wrong. After the seconds pass, Monte turns back again and looks at Tempest. "I can't take time off," he said with an uneasy look on his face. "There is no room for taking time off in this business. If I go out now, there's no telling where I will be. I have seen too many guys lose out because they just could not handle working through pain. I will not allow that to happen to me." Tempest squeezes his hand and says, "I understand. I mean I know what you mean, but you do have to weigh that against the chance you could hurt yourself worse. If you shorten your career, the roll you're on now won't matter much." She shrugs a little and says, "I've been really lucky so far, just having the separated shoulder and all after Twilight." "We'll see what happens," Burns says, grasping her hand and smiling deep into her eyes. "And I'll help any way I can," Tempest said smiling back. "Thanks for picking me up though. They can't afford a limo for me anymore I'm afraid. So I been getting a little more exercise lately." "Well, this is one that I paid for," Burns said as he poured two glasses of champagne. "I usually pay for it myself since I know they probably would not pay for one for me. It works out much better that way." Tempest raises her eyebrow, but accepts the chapagne and takes a sip. "True. But then you've heard why they paid for one for me, right?" "No," he said with a cocked eyebrow, "I didn't actually." Tempest laughs and puts her finger to her lips to catch a drop of stray champagne. "You didn't?! Oh I thought everyone knew." She chuckles and continues, "Well, I was cheaper for them to hire me a limo in any given town than to pay for the damage claims and isurence woes, when I drive myself." "I see," Burns says, coughing a bit on the champagne as he chuckles. Tempest snickers. "Yeah, so I don't even have a car now. But I get around ok. Ivy and Eli let me ride with them some and of course I fly. And that bike takes me everywhere else!" As the two of them shared a laugh, Burns sipped from his glass of champagne and looked over at Tempest with the glass in her hand. With their smiling faces looking at each other, an odd calm came over Monte, as if a lot of the tension from work and his back injury had faded away for that brief moment. As they sat looking at each other, Monte reached his hand forward and stroked Tempest's cheek. "Tempest," he said with her eyes locking on his, "I will take you wherever you want to go." Tempest smiled warmly and nodded. "I can't wait."
