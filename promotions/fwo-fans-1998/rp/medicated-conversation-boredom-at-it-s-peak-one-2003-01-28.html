Medicated Conversation - Boredom at it's peak. (One)

28 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One minute, two minutes..................two minutes, thirty seconds.......................... "Eh, fuck this..." Within twenty painful seconds, Amy Campbell went from her hospital bed to the wheelchair that had been laid out for her. She would've tried walking, but just getting out of that cursed hospital shirt and into her baggiest jeans and a Metallica hoodie had proven excrutiating enough for one day. Brandon had been with her almost every second of the past two days, but in the moments she was finally alone and could manage enough will to sit up, the redhead had decided she'd had far too much of the room's incredible view of the building adjacent to it. Sure, within ten minutes the real Mighty Hero would've been there to help with everything, but it was time to get out before Amy went crazy. Besides, she considered it fine to leave on her own...no matter how much codeine she had to ingest to get out the door. Today came the great escape; She got to go home...wherever that might be. Unfortunately, her plan to escape early didn't go so well...the hitch came in the appearance of Sean Stevens in the doorway to her room JUST before she could race through it... "Going somewhere, sis?" Stevens grinned as he stopped a wheel of the chair with his foot. "Yes." She half-snarled, half-pouted. "As far away from a hospital I can get...move." Stevens gave her the one over. The past had month had been extremely hard on him. His best friend in the world died, and he did his damndest to not worry about it, but it took a select bunch to get him through it all. And, even though he was a ways from being where he needed to be... he appreciated Amy Campbell for the enormous part she played in it all. That's why his heart sunk to the bottom of his chest when he found out she had been shot. "Excuse me for trying to come and visit you, Amy," he began."All I was trying to do was be the friend you were to me. I guess when you're not in the greatest of condition, you don't allow people to." He moved out of her way. "In that case... go on past me. I'll be right here when you realized how ridiculous you're being, and decide you want to talk." ...'Put in her place' wouldn't even begin to describe the feeling. Amy sat, staring ahead momentarily. "Damnit, you're not supposed to let me!" She tried to grin. "You win for pointing out the bad mood." She didn't like the feeling of looking up at these towering men, though standing at 5'8" wouldn't have done her much better. But, thanks to the temporary lack of painless mobility, she had to force herself to smile. Amy wasn't a fan of avoiding a subject, but the last thing she really felt like talking about was her physical condition. "How's stuff?" Sean sighed, his tactic had worked. "I'm good, kid... you had me worried, though. Sorry for such a late visit, but you know... with Luck passing away, I couldn't really face the fact that you could be gone, too. As soon as Ivy told me you were okay, I came. How are you feeling?"' "You thought I'd die from a little knick in my side?" Amy bit down on her tongue in the middle of laughing, that 'little knick' in her side hurt more than she'd expected. "C'mon, I'm not made of glass..." She put a hand over the bandage on her stomach, a dull ache the painful reminder. "I might not be made of Teflon either.....but I'm feeling fine, thanks to Brandon and painkillers." "That's good to hear," Trip replied, as he moved closer, rubbing her head gently. "I knew were a tough one, but still... it was a bullet, y'know? I dunno... maybe I'm just a sucker for situations like this. But, you're my friend... and, I still have a truck load of shit that needs to be sorted out in my own life. Heh... I need you here and healthy, okay Aimz?" "Nobody needs me...everyone's saying it, but you guys'd be just as well off without me. Not like I'm gonna die anytime soon, though...so keep putting up with me." She winked. "No... I honestly think you want to feel like you're not needed, so it'll make it easier on yourself if you ever decide to just up and... leave." Sean paused for a moment, Amy simply sat there, knowing he wasn't finished. "But, you know what? In this business, you need friends... and, Eli, Ivy, and I see something in you. I can't speak for the rest of 'em... but, I'm not letting you disappear that easily." "What could you guys possibly see in me? And if you mention taking a bullet for others, I'll seriously put my sock up your ass...." Of course, both knew her threat wasn't entirely sincere...it was the redhead's 'way' with friends, so it went practically ignored save for a soft chuckle from Sean. "What do we see in you? Let's see... you're almost always there when we need you. You didn't even know me when we first met, remember? And, since... all you've been is a great friend... a kid sister, even. Give yourself more credit, Amy... I'm telling you. You're an awesome person, and everybody in the Rat Pack knows it, but if *you* don't start to realize it, it'll drive people away. Because while being a great person is a pretty color ... negativity is an even stronger, uglier one, with a stench. ...and it just drives people away." He smiled. "And, I don't even need to mention the incident that got you here... I'm sure the stinging pain reminds you more than my words ever could." "You see too much good in the world." She smiled in wonder at the man towering over her, punching him lightly on the arm. "I don't see the same things; It's just you guys and...well...Brandon. That sums up all the good. If Brandon hadn't forced me to open up three months ago, and Ivy hadn't done it again on New Year's, I wouldn't be quite the same person..." "But what's up with this deep talk? Too many drugs, getting me all mixed up......" Amy grinned, he'd genuinely improved her mood. "So whatcha' up to all next week...on the road with us, right?" "I dunno, Amy... I guess I see your potential. But, yeah... for the most part, I'll be riding with you guys." Rubbing his chin, he continued. "I *do* have to make a stop in Detroit, though... I don't feel as if I could continue functioning if I didn't. I'm sure you know where I mean?" His eyebrows shot to the top of his head, and Amy smiled in his direction, and nodded her head at the same time. "So... are you and Brandon officially together or what? I mean... you *are* in this hospital and all... I'm assuming you wouldn't do what you did for somebody you had no romantic interest in. Am I right?" Amy shook her head a little, sighing in response. "I don't know if we're 'officially together'...it's more 'or what' right now. He's a lot different than I am in every sense of the word...I think he's trying to play it safe. And you're right about the interest...wrong as hell in saying that's the only case I'd do this in. I'd do it for any of you...when I actually get drawn into caring for people, it's not like me to let 'em get hurt." "And, we'd do it for you, Amy... that's why I can't let you leave this hospital until you're ready. I don't want you out there if your doctors are telling you not to be. Besides, if you're bored, I have nothing but time... I'll stick around with you." "But that means spending until tonight in this hell hole............" She pouted, "But fine...and ONLY because you and BL would chew my ears off if I didn't, and it's not like I have someone to drive." Sean just had to extend a hand and Amy gave in. For once, she allowed herself to be escorted back inside the little bleak room where she'd have to spend yet another four or five agonizing hours. The hospital stay could be likened to clipping a hawk's wings but analogies aside, she had every intention of paying Brandi Taylor a little visit in the cell block the second the redhead was healed enough to throw a punch.
