31 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**Scott Collins and Stone are sitting in a small, dimly lit dining room at the home they share. Stone raises a glass of wine to her lips as Scott cuts his steak... suddenly Brian Cage and John Malcon burst into the room.** John and Brian: (in shock and anger) Demon! Demon killed Joy! **Stone and Scott exchange a look.** Scott: What? Calm down. What happened? Brian: At the end of tonight's show, man. Demon threw Joy off the ramp. He's dead! Stone: Good lord... Scott: You're not kidding, are you? **Brian and John shake their heads.** Scott: We're all fucked. The feds are gonna come down on this company so hard... **Stone looks thoughtfully into her wine.** Scott: Jeezus christ. Spencer can have his fucking name. He and his boys are so fucked up... Brian: What are we gonna do? Scott: Are you kidding? We're gonna hire a fucking lawyer, and we're gonna get the fuck outta here. That's what we're gonna do. There is no way I'm sticking around when the shit from this hits the fan. John: We should do something, Scott. Scott: Are you fucking kidding?! Don't you understand? DEMON KILLED JOY. That slut spy Machina wants is gonna take this whole place down. We gotta cut the ties, and do it fast. John: No, man. Something has to be done. We're the only ones that know him well enough to take him down! Scott: You're nuts, John. Have been since the day I met you. Just 'casue you can string a sentence together now doesn't mean you're cured... **Stone looks up from her glass.** Stone: I agree with John. Scott and Brian: You do?! Stone: I do. Scott: Shit. Stone: Scott? Scott: Shit. **Stone quirks an eyebrow.** Scott: Fine. The bastards are going down. Any of 'em that aren't in jail by tomorrow, we're gonna hit. Hard. **Brian cracks his knuckles.** John: I know where his house is...
