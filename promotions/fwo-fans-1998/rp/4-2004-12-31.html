"It's a simple story Jim. You know my past, you know all about me. You know on top of being in the Asylum, which alone puts me on the terrorist watch list. Throw in the fact I was one of the largest drug dealers on the west coast, and I was on the move to the east coast during the Global Turnbuckle Tournament." Jim Interrupted "Which is why you were eliminated?" "Exactly. I had decided I would leave some one in charge out west while I personally moved the product on the east coast. This went sour as I tried working through and around the mafia at the same time. I was considered untouchable, but my little brother who was running things out west. Totally another story." I lowered my head as a tear trickled down my cheek. It was all coming back to me, images of Draven laying in the middle of the side walk. Blood pouring from his mouth and his stomach. I sniffled. An image of Draven screaming in the ambulance as the paramedics tended to his open stomach wound. One of them shook their head in disappointment. He knew this was a mortal wound. I held my head in my hands, tears now streaming down my face. "The last time I saw Draven was as he looked up at me from a hospital bed, tears streaming down his face as he held onto my hand. I looked straight into his eyes.
