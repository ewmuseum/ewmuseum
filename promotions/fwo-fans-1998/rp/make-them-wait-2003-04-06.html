Make Them Wait.

6 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It wasn't a regular cold night in which has been considered as Koshyn for the past several years. Light snow was falling on the icy ground, and the roads were slushed by the roaming trucks travelling the distance for cargo shipments, but something just was irregular. On the side of the road was a highly lit house, the exact piece of disturbance to Koshyn that made the night irregular. For over two years, the house had not been inhabited. The frost and flakes were elevated more then usual due to a lawn not cut, the green vines on the right side of the brick exterior were growing out of control, and the trees in front nearly blocked the entire view of the house from the roadside. The house, a three storey high residence made out of old fashioned brick, with a large red oak door, was one of the homes of Eron, his only home back in Canada. The house was purchased for its location, just on the outskirts of where Eron had once lived. The area was called 401 Road, but because of the unattractiveness of the name, Eron dubbed it Koshyn for good measure. With a population of sometimes four, he could call it whatever he pleased. Not much activity in the house could be seen from the window, except the reflection of the TV screen lit one of the rooms. However, from the road side, you could hear the ringing of a telephone. Inside, Eron sat down on a blue velvet recliner, watching High Fidelity for likely the fiftieth time for him. Wearing a pair of black tearaways with a white stripe down the side, and a simple white muscle shirt, it was obvious Eron had the heat turned up in his house so he could wear something simple. On the coffee table near Eron, the smoke grey phone rang constantly, being ignored. Finally, unable to hear what Bruce Springsteen was saying in one of the scenes, Eron finally stood up and walked over to the phone. Looking down upon the LCD display, the name read, "FINN C". Eron slowly moved his finger toward the phone, and applied pressure onto the volume button, silencing the rings. Eron moved back to his chair just before the answering machine began to play. Rotating his head to crack it, Eron slouched back into the chair, seemingly exhausted. The answering machine displayed a "143" number in front, which could very well be why he was strained. His voice began to kick in through the answering machine; "Leave a message" was all it had said. The machine's beeping noise hit, and the voice of Chris Finn was heard. "Eron, Eron are you there? You gotta be home, this is the only phone which I've been able to get to the answering machine through, so it has to be. Look, I'm sorry for calling so much, but we gotta get something in check, okay? Did you read the fWo website? With the report on you? Well, yeah, if you didn't, go do that now. Okay, I can't wait for you to check it, let me just give you the gist. We said on it you wouldn't be appearing on Warfare again, but you know how that is, we gotta save face for the media. But, you know, we have to settle out your issues. I need to know exactly why you left at the show, but I also need you to keep away from the publications. Look, please just call back, because I can't afford to fly down there. We may have plans for you at Cyberslam. Um, okay, call back Eron, bye." The message finally ended with the sound of the answering machine beep, as Eron simply continued to watch the television. After a moment in time, Eron glanced over at the phone, seeing the red light begin to flash once again. Letting out a sigh of disgust, Eron took the remote control and shut off the TV. Slowly standing up, Eron began to walk the opposite direction of the telephone, and into the kitchen area. The kitchen seemed to be Hollywood approved. The entire room was an ivory white, with black handles and panels to accompany it. In comparison to the living room, which had a country side feel to it, the kitchen was more modern. Everything in this room was also spotless, almost as if it was pulled right out from the appliance store. Eron moved straight to the fridge, pulling out some various fruits and set them on the counter. The beep from the answering machine had just went off, which made Eron turn his head, but he just continued to take out melon slices before shutting the refrigerator door. Eron then began placing the fruit into the juicer, set it on a mode, and walked back into the living room. Leaning against the doorway, the voice on the answering machine was in mid conversation when he began to listen, trying to ignore the buzzing of the juicer. "..your house right? This is Layne Seller, of PWLighter, remember when I interviewed you back in 2001? Yeah, well I got this house number so I thought I'd call. I read what happened on the fWo site and I just wanted to hear your opinion on it, that would be great for me, oh I mean for you to get your word across and everything. Unless this is a work. Is this a work? I don't like being worked, almost lost my job with the BS Scott Slugger always fed me. Anyhow, call me anyway. You know my number. If you don't, ask one of the guys in the back, they all know who to call for an interview. You don't even have to say a word about the rumoured returns of Rain and Mike Bear. I swear I won't ask, unless you feel free to talk about it. Just don't work me." The answering machine finished around the same time the blender finished. Walking up to the counter, Eron pulled a tall glass from the cupboard, and began to pour the fruit smoothie into the glass. Throwing the blender cup into the sink, Eron walked back into the living room with the smoothie, shutting off the light as he walked past the doorway. As Eron moved to sitting back into the chair, he looked over at the phone, as the red light began to flash again. Putting his glass down, Eron mumbled to himself, "Won't you people fuck off?" before turning the television back on, and turning it to something on Teletoon. Once again, his voice played on the answering machine and a beep played, as a new voice began to speak. "Hello? Eron! Pick up the phone! It's Reed Young! You know, owner of Action! Wrestling! We spoke back in '02 about you coming to A!W, but I guess things fell through. Hey, I heard through the grapevine that you are a free agent again! Well, not exactly free, but you know I could just buy that contract up! I got BIG plans, I mean BIIIIIIG plans for you in Action! Bill Goldberg would choke on a cock on how big these plans are, baby! Think about this: Opening night, Eron comes out, huge celebration, then BAM! You beat Fox for the Action! Championship! Oh wait, maybe that's too good guy, how about, out of nowhere, Fox is challenged, you come out, then BAM! You beat Fox for the Action! Championship! Or or, wait, maybe you don't want a title, how about we quickly scrap LLB's feud with Fox, like have Fox beat him, then Eron comes out, because we know you two's history back in jOlt, and Storylines equal extra buck, then BAM! You kick the crap out of LLB in a huge shocker and we main event you two as a surprise of the world for Juggernaut II! It'll be huge! It'll be astronomical! I'll even let you shoot on the fWo! Name the price, I'll pay it with a CA, and a CHING! Later!" As the answering machine hit with a beep, Eron laughed. He turned his head to the side, seeming to think about Reed Young's offer, and began to move his arm toward the phone, but then waved it off with a chuckle. Sitting, Eron looked back at the TV, just trying to nod out while drinking his smoothie. Without a surprise, the red light on the phone began to flash once again in the corner of Eron's eye. Putting down the smoothie, Eron walked up to the phone, picking up the receiver. Adjusting his tearaways, Eron spoke into the phone. "Look, would you people stop calling me?" Eron said, a tone of anger into his voice. The voice on the other side was too muffled to be recognized. "You ask me to call, and then you tell me to fuck off. Make up your mind you stupid red headed bastard." The phone hung up, as Eron just chuckled to himself. Putting down the receiver, Eron shut the TV off once again, grabbed his smoothie, and began walking up the stairs. As the stairs creeked at his steps, the red light from the phone came on once again. The LCD display read, "C FINN". With Eron going upstairs, it would become just another call Eron wouldn't answer tonight.
