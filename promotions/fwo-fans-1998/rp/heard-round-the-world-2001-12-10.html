Heard Round the World

10 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tempest peered out the window of her apartment as her breath fogged the cold window pane before her. She watched the streets full of people, bustling to and fro. Christmas was coming soon and it was starting to show in the form of colored lights and fresh green decorations adorning the buildings. Everything sparkled just a little more and seemed a little bit cleaner. "Everything but my belt which seems tarnished and my life, which seems dirty," Tempest said. She heaved a sigh and gazed out sadly at the scene as she said softly, "I wish I was out there with them instead of in here alone." At that, a soft whimper interrupted her thoughts and she turned to find Trouble staring up at her. He carried a squeaky ball in his mouth and wagged his tail hopefully. Something was obviously wrong with his mistress, but surely nothing that playing and copious displays of his undying love couldn't cure. Tempest summoned a smile for her furry friend and plopped down on the floor next to him. "Ya know," she said, "This is why people love animals. No matter what I might have done, you still love me." She scratched behind the little dog's ears and he licked happily at her face. She laughed and tossed the ball and he went running after it. Her smile faltered just a bit and she said, "But I still wanna be out there with people, mixing and talking and laughing and having fun. It used to be that I was overjoyed when someone recognized me or wanted an autograph. Now I'm afraid of exactly the same thing." She looked at the floor absently, but Trouble trotted back in with the ball and she smiled a little, tossing it for him again. She watched him trot off after it and her head tilted to the side a little, lost in thought as she said, "And who do I have to thank for this? Who do I have to thank for the fact that I can't show my face on fWo television without the catcalls and insults and..." She closed her eyes and shook her head sadly, "...and just knowing that I can't be anyone's hero now?" She took a deep breath and narrowed her eyes as she said, "Ric Chronos and Eddy Fate..." She jumped as the phone rang suddenly and Trouble looked at her nervously, dropping the ball from his mouth. She stood and leapt for the phone, yanking it's pastel pink arch from the cradle of a Hello Kitty cartoon phone. "Hello?" There was a long pause and then Tempest said, "Yes... yes I'll hold. No, it's no problem, thank you." As she waited her spine seemed to straighten and she paced in the short space allowed by the cord. At last something came over the line and she snapped to attention and said, "Papa?" The word hung in the air a moment sounding alien before she continued, "Yes, it has been a long time. Papa, I don't know if you've been watching the fWo and... You have... I see." Her eyelids flickered rapidly and she said, "Then you know what's been going on. I'm confused Papa." She looked around as though someone might be listening and backed up into the shadows, perhaps out of instinct as she said in a whisper, "I've seen what you do to people who try to even take a picture of your place. So how is it that someone made it out alive with a videotape?" She nodded and slid down to a sitting position on the floor and then shook her head rapidly as she said, "No no, that's not the issue. It's not about revenge. They already know; the whole wrestling public knows and the damage is done. Shooting the person who took the film is useless. But that's just it Papa..." Her eyes narrowed as she seemed to consider carefully what she was about to say. "How could anyone possibly have gotten it without you allowing it? And even then, how did Ric Chronos of all people, get it?" She nodded slowly and then a small smile came to her face as she said, "Inquiries? But you're the man who knows all in Hong Kong. Look, I know Eddy Fate is from there too. Maybe he knew something? Maybe..." She stopped and suddenly her eyes widened and the color seemed to seep from her face. "Eddy... Papa, do you know this man I've been fighting, Eddy Fate? Ric Chronos had nothing in Hong Kong that I knew of, but if Eddy's from there... well could he have been the tape's owner?" There was a long silence in which she seemed to only be half listening, her mouth hanging open and an expression of dumbfounded realization. "No, I know that they were once friends. They could still be working together.... I don't know. I don't really think so. Still, either way the tape could have come from Fate," she said, her thoughts seeming to crowd over her. Her lips trembled slightly during the next pause and then she said, "You're right I have no way to know. But if you can figure out who he really is I would appreciate it... You know what I mean Papa." She took a deep breath and the next words from her lips dripped with a suffused venom as she said, "I need to know if he's a Ghost Arrow or not. I never wanted a piece of those wars, but if indeed they have followed me here... and after what they did to my father..." Her hands shook ever so slightly as she nodded and listened to the voice on the other end of the line. She let her eyes close a moment and then said, "I know Papa... No, you don't have to worry about that. I owe you too much to talk to anyone about you. Besides, so far no investigator types have been around and..." Suddenly she snickered and said, "Oh, you took care of that? I see..." She nodded and her eyes seemed to harden once more as she replied, "No, all I really want to know is if he was a Ghost Arrow, because if he was... then Eddy Fate has a great deal more to pay for than just embarrassing me." *click*
