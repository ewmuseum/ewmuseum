January 6th, 2004 - Visiting Hours

15 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"...birthday to you! And many mooooooore!" The assembled throng, tightly jammed into the small New York Presbyterian Hospital room, ended their slightly off-key tribute to one Pierre Delacroix, aka The Flying Frenchie. Easily visible were the Frankie and Joey Villani, their stout frames producing a rather sizable package. The roughly 4' tall by 6' long package was totally covered in wrapping paper, and so awkward that it nearly upended both Narly Nate and Elliot Harper. Carraba and Jim Carter each grabbed a top corner, while Steve-O and Thomas Harper grabbed the bottom corners. "We know it's a little late," spoke up Wippit Guud, "but our fearless leader on this event insisted that everyone who contributed to the gift be here to give it." Wippit jerked his thumb towards Ric Chronos, who was standing next to his girlfriend Lyra, holding tightly to the final open space of floor. "So, instead of delaying this any longer, let's reveal what your 31st Birthday has won for you behind wrapping paper #1!" finished Guud, waving his arms in a flourish. The four people holding a corner all ripped away their quadrants on cue, revealing a sturdy display case teaming with gold and silver emblems. "What we have here is a replica of each title you've ever won in the fWo, along with a date, who you won it from, and in the case of all these tag belts, who you won it with." Guud let the last sentence come out in his best announcer voice. "Even the Women's title," pointed out Elliot. Although the smile on Pierre's face was not the trademark Frenchie beam, it still lit up the entire room. "And you'll note that the bottom reads 'From all the Tag Teams of the fWo'. You really helped some of us less established guys along, and we appreciate that a lot." chimed in Carraba. The Villanis stepped back with the display, and placed it gingerly against the far wall. "Hell" sighed out Frankie as he released his hold. "If you had won any more titles, we might not have been able to lift it." A small laugh was shared by the group then, breaking some of the nervous tension. As a rule, wrestlers don't do too well when confronted with severe injuries of fellow wrestlers - it's often an all too stark reminder of their own vulnerability, a hint of the pain that one day they will most likely share. Over the course of the next half an hour, they would make small talk, and one by one excuse themselves. Eventually, all that remained were Ric and Lyra, holding hands. Ric looked towards Lyra, and she cocked an inquisitive eyebrow back. Ric nodded, and she left the room, fully understanding the silent exchange that had gone between them. Pierre, however, wasn't quite so lucky, and the bemused expression on his face acknowledged such. "Well, um, since I'm here, there was something I wanted to ask you. I was going to do this at your Birthday party, but well...yeah. So, Lyra and I have decided to tie the knot on Valentine's Day." "About time zat you made an honest woman out of her, non?" "Well, Pierre, it would mean a lot to me if you were the Best Man, leg permitting, of course." "It would take more than a shot to ze knee before I would miss that, Ric. You know that." "Well, right, but I didn't know, what with the fact that you're still in the hospital and all, I mean... if you're not feeling up to it, that's fine, I'll understand." "Ah, this?" Pierre pointed towards the heavily bandaged knee. "This is nothing compared to the shock of being shot by Fallen. I mean, I always thought zat if anyone would shoot me, if would have been Slugger." The two friends laughed at that, but the trembling echoes of the sound didn't pass itself onto Ric for more than a few seconds. "About that... I want to apologize for you about that." "Apologize? What for?" "Well... Fallen and I... I wasn't there with you, and you had asked me to go. If I had been there, I could have talked to him... we have history, and maybe I could have..." "Ric, if you had been there, he wouldn't have been content with shooting you in the knee. He would have killed you, and I would've had your death on my conscience. I'll have no more of this guilt from you." "I.. I still feel that I'm responsible, damn it. I should have talked to him well before this. I mean, the only one who was standing up to him was Neo... where was I? Maybe if I had been reaching out to him all along, then none of this would have happened. Damn it all, Pierre, we almost lost you... and I, I can't help but feel it's partly my fault." Ric turned his face away from Pierre, hoping to fight back the tears that were struggling their way to the surface. Trying to compose himself, he was taken aback by the pressure of Pierre's hand on his shoulder. "Ric, do you remember when I came to see you in the hospital, after what Brian did to your neck? I said a lot of ze same t'ings that you're saying now. Do you remember what you said to me then? You told me that you knew who did this to you. That you would deal with it in your own way. Please, my friend, let me deal with this the same way." Ric turned, nearly upsetting Pierre's delicate balance on the edge of his hospital bed. "I understand that, Pierre. And since you're using my own words against me, I guess I can't fault their logic. But I'm telling you now - someday, somewhere, Fallen and I will settle our scores, and this will be one of the many things that I take out of him." "I believe that Ric. I really do. But for the moment, could you help me back down... my knee doesn't bend very well, and... I'm kind of stuck, mon ami." Ric stared at the predicament his friend faced, and Pierre answered his gaze, until it was too much for either of them. Genuine laughter burst from their lips, and took a long time before subsiding.
