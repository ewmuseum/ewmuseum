Back to Work (Prologue - Punch the Clock)

31 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alex Newsome found himself faced with more than a little trepidation concerning his current destination. He'd been back in Steel City USA since the fWo terminated his contract along with the rest of the roster back in July, and when he'd had almost no offers from independent promotions, Alex had gone to work with his father in one of the many mills that dominated Pittsburgh's industrial district. In the past few months, he'd realized that working in the mill wasn't bad, and, though the money was actually pretty good, it wasn't what he wanted to do for the rest of his life. However, it was paying the bills while there wasn't a good wrestling job to be found, so there was a definite sense of trepidation as he stepped into the foreman's office to find the normally stoic George "Gravel" Gavin, his foreman for the shift, smiling as he held out the phone to Alex. "Call for you, kid." "Thanks, Gav," Alex replied, unsure what to make of the smiling visage of Gravel, something he'd seen before only at the bar after work. "Hello?" This was followed by a brief pause, as Alex fielded what was apparently a question, answering with, "Yeah, this is Lance." His voice indicated that he hadn't thought of himself by that name in some time. "How'd you get this number?" Apparently agitated, Alex listened, his face first showing traces of anger, then going slack with surprise as the party on the other end of the phone delivered their message. "They are? Well, no, I haven't exactly been following lately. Work's been keeping me busy." Again, the contours of Newsome's face began to morph, tightening up as his surprise was overtaken by what was clearly anger. "They're WHAT? That's ridiculous!" and then, after giving it further thought, Alex muttered, "Bastards..." "Well, I appreciate the call, but..." Before he could continue, Alex was interrupted by his caller, and this time, when there was silence from the other end of the line, a broad smile had appeared on Newsome's face. "Well, I'm more than willing to lend a hand, if it means those bastards won't get away with trying to put everything the rest of us worked so hard for to shame." "Yeah, nice to hear from you again, too. Take care of yourself; you're going to need to be healthy, if we're really going to do this. Me? Let's just say that working in a steel mill, combined with my normal workouts, has gotten me more than ready to do this. Yeah...yeah...I'll definitely see you then." Alex returned the phone to its cradle on Gavin's desk and turned to return to work. As he reached the door of the small office, his supervisor called out to him, prompting Newsome to turn back towards the desk, where Gavin was still sporting an ear-to-ear grin, one that rivaled any Alex had ever seen on his face. "That was him, wasn't it?" And though Gavin hadn't said anything specific about the caller, Alex knew that he did indeed know who had called, so there was no point in hiding the truth from this man, one of his father's oldest friends. "Yup, Gav, it was." Smiling, Newsome turned back to the door, opening it halfway and then shutting it again. "Oh, and boss?" he began, turning back towards Gavin's desk, "I may need some vacation days." It was time to go back to work.
