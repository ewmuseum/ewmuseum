Family Values - Chapter One

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Okay, TJ... Truth or consequences?" The bus has been going since 10:40 PM, three hours ago. Three of the five band members have been drinking heavily for those three hours. One is steadily into his second blunt. The fifth and final member... is pregnant. They all have smiles on their faces - they're finally going home. They've been touring since January. That's six months nonstop on the road. Six months since any of them have seen their own beds. Six months is a long time for a band who, until six months ago, never played a show outside their home town of Los Angeles. It took a massive stroke of blind, stupid luck for them to land a pair of dates with the Misfits in Japan, and things took off from there. "Truth." This particular game of Truth or Consequences is a bit odd. These five people know just about everything there is to know about each other. "What're ya gonna do with all your free time?" TJ leans back, finishing off his beer, "Gonna go on the road with the fWo, actually. 'Lijah's got me and Nine a gig with the composers, workin' on original music for the new TV show." "Nice," said Sex, slurring his words slightly, "You're up, Travissshhhhh." "Have another one," joked TJ, "Hmmm.... Nine." "Ya?" "Truth or consequences?" "Truth." "Boring," said Angel, just beginning to show, "Am I the only one who's going to pick a consequence tonight?" "Yeah," replied Mickey. "You all suck," said Angel, remembering something she was dared to do earlier, "The next time one of you says consequences, YOUR ass is walking into a club in your undies. No, not yours, mine." This gets a laugh from the drunkards. "Okay," continued TJ, "Truth, Nine. Have you ever seen Angel naked?" "Yep." "Hey!" "C'mon," replied Mickey, "Let the boy tell the tale, Angel. That's a sight a man is lucky to see." Despite the fact that Mickey is her ex, she smiles. Her entire band, however, has seen her unclothed, which causes her to turn bright red from ear to ear. "It was at the second Misfits show in Japan," explained Nine, "Remember, the shower didn't have a curtain to it? I thought Mickey was in there and I didn't really care so I went in to get my hairspray, and Angel was there, washing the shampoo out of her hair." "And?" Mickey was pushing. "You're a fuckin' goddess, girl," said Nine. "Thanks," replied Angel, dryly. She's a little less upset now, when she realizes that these guys are too wasted to really remember anything they say, do, or hear tonight. Except Mickey. Mellow, stoned Mickey. But she doesn't much care about that part - Mickey knows almost all her secrets anyway. "Your turn, Nine," reminded Sex, "And pass me another beer." Nine leaned back and opened up the refrigerator, tossing a Budweiser toward the couch. "Angel." She sat up, drinking from her water bottle. "Truth or consequences?" "Truth." "Ever killed anyone?" Angel laughed, nervously. This is what it's come down to. Who's seen who naked, who's killed who. The only things left for these people to ask each other are the most ridiculous questions known to man. "No." However... if the rest of the band wasn't drunk beyond belief or stoned beyond recognition, they might've noticed her laughter was a bit too quick, a bit too nervous. "Mickey," said Angel, "Truth or Consequences?" Mickey leaned back in his seat on the couch, not noticing Angel's eyes shifting from side to side. "Ever killed anyone?" is what Nine asked her. "No." is what she answered. She was lying....
