Best Days: Wippit Guud

29 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Is this a private party, or can anyone crash?" said Wippit Guud. "Free beer and companionship," said Luke Starkey, "and all it will cost you is the story of the best day of your life." Wippit pulled up one of the few remaining empty chairs while Lance Knight poured him a beer. "My best day, huh? Well," said Wippit, I guess my best day was September 28th, 2000, and ironically for being a wrestler, it took place in a hospital." "Didn't start out that great though, I'd pulled a muscle working with some rookies in the ring for the UWA, the fed I was in at the time, So my leg was really sore. So I was just sitting around wasting time on the computer playing Counter Strike, when my wife said the three words that I was dreading to hear lately..." "Honey, it's time." Wippit smirked, recalling the dreaded announcement, "So... after my stomch did a few backflips, I got my wife - who you've probably guessed just went into labor - into the Pathfinder and off to the hospital. And once there... nothing happened. They hooked her up to a machine to monitor her and stuff, and she was having contractions every 5 minutes or so, but, honestly, it was quite boring." "So, I wandered around the hospital for a few hours, and they made her walk around too to speed things up, but the place was kinda drab. I mean, it was a hospital, right?" Wippit shrugged, "Not much in the way of decorations. Eventually, her other coach arrived - she had a friend who'd had a kid who'd wanted to see it from the other perspective - so I went out to grab some Wendy's and then call both parents to let them know our situation." A chuckle escaped Wippit's lips, "Little did I know that they were going to give her 'speed-up labor drugs' while I was enjoying my classic triple combo, and so when I was on the phone to my parents, the doctor came in and said 'It's time,' errily echoing the dreaded words we don't want to hear. And it was off to the delivery room. Wippit looked at High Flyer as his mouth opened, "No not THAT kiond of deliver room." He continued, "So, we're in the room with something that looks like a dentist chair from hell, when another problem springs up - every time she has a contraction, our soon-to-be's heartbeat drop from about 120 per minute to something like 20 per minute, and that's scaring the hell out of everyone. Now, you'll all probably seen deliveries on TV, well it wasn't much like it, she was only pushing for like 10 minutes, and even without drugs she wasn't in much pain..." Wippit trailed off... "even when the cut her..." He cleared his throat after a second's memory, "and then they discovered the reason - seems the umbilicle cord had wrapped around the baby's neck. Definitely not good. So, they told me I wouldn't be able to cut it cause they had to get it off now - and believe you me, I didn't want to cut it in the first place - and two more pushes and he was out." Wippit paused. "... but not crying." "While the wife was getting stitches," and Wippit winced, "Our son - oh, it was a boy by the way - was taken over to another table, and I tagged along. Then they paged for a pediatric surgeon, stat, and I began to freak out inside but stayed calm on the outside. 30 seconds later a doctor arrived, and they started giving Liam CPR... after about a minute, we heard crying, and suddenly a lot of emotions rushed through me." Wip looked around. "And when they wrapped him in a blanket and handed him to me, and I looked down at these big, dark brown eyes staring up at me, I knew there was no way that anything else would even come close to being the best day of my life."
