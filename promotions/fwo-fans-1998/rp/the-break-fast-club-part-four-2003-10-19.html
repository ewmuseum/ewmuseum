The Break Fast Club (part four)

19 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sphere walks into the hallway, with Red Bull all over his clothes. Sphere: "Gives you wings" my ass. Meanwhile, BQ leaves the library, and is followed by everyone else. They all start walking down the hall. BQ and Nate are walking next to each other, followed by Funpants and Steve-O, while Evil-Lyn is close behind. Nate: Dude, how do you know where Sphere went? BQ: I don't. Nate: That's good enough for me. Funpants: Lemme get this straight...we're going to Quicksilver's locker...even though NONE of us go to school here. Steve-O: Dude, you ask one more question and I'll spork your ass. Funpants: I didn't ask any questions. Funpants punches Steve-O in the mouth for the fourth time today, as they reach BQ's locker. He opens it and pulls out a bag of marijuana. BQ: Wait. This isn't my locker. BQ turns to Nate. BQ: It's yours. BQ hands the bag of marijuana to Nate, then continues on down the hallway. Steve-O: Dude, don't bogart the stash. Steve-O tries to take the bag, but Nate pulls away, as Funpants turns to Evil-Lyn. Funpants: Do you approve of this? Evil-Lyn shrugs and steals the combination lock from Nate's locker. Several minutes later, and the crowd of five is walking down the hallway together. BQ: Okay, fine. We'll cross through the lab and look for a Bunsen burner. Nate and Steve-O: SWEET! Nate and Steve-O high-five, but then they all spot Sphere. Various sequences of them running in and out of rooms follow, including one where they run into the History 101 room, only to be chased out by Fred, Daphne, Shaggy, Velma, and Scooby. After several minutes go by, BQ stops running and holds his arms out. Naturally, this would cause everyone behind you to STOP running, but it *is* BQ, so they barrel right over him, and only stop when they realize they might've stepped on his head. Well, except for Funpants. Funpants: C'mon! What are you guys waiting for? Leave him! BQ gets up and "shakes" himself off. BQ: We have to go through the activities hall! Steve-O: No, the activities hall. BQ: Hey, man, you don't know what you're talking about! Steve-O: No...YOU don't know what you're talking about. Evil-Lyn: Squeak. Steve-O: Dude, see? We should go THIS way! Funpants: You might want to point somewhere when you say "this way". Steve-O: Oh. Um...that way. Steve-O points down the hall. Funpants: Weren't we going that way anyway before we all trampled over Quickjobber and then he made us stop? Instead of arguing, they start to run down the way Steve-O pointed out. They end up running into a hall closed by an iron gate. BQ: Alright, alright, I got an idea. BQ reaches into Nate's pocket, takes out the bag of marijuana, and turns to Funpants. BQ: Get back to the library, keep your unit on this! BQ stuffs the bag down Funpants'...fun pants. Funpants: The hell? BQ runs down the hallway singing John Mayer's "No Such Thing", and Sphere hears him. The other four start to run in the other direction, as Sphere looks around for BQ. Sphere: That son of a bitch! Sphere finds BQ in the gym, getting ready to go up for a dunk. BQ: Three...two...one... BQ misses the dunk horribly, as Sphere starts walking toward him. BQ: Damn. I'm gonna get molested in the showers. Sphere: Quicksilver! Quicksilver! Quicksilver! Sphere stops walking toward BQ and slaps himself. Sphere: There we go. Quicksilver! What is this? What are you doing here? What is this? God dammit. Sphere slaps himself again. BQ: Oh, hi! Sphere: Out! That's it, Quicksilver! Out, it's over! BQ: What's that supposed to mean? "Out, it's over"? Sphere: Shut up. I meant to say that. I'm not one to struggle with words. I'm Sphere. BQ: Don't you wanna hear my excuse? Sphere: Out! BQ: I'm trying out for a scholarship. Sphere: Gimme the ball, Quicksilver, before you drop it. BQ gently places the ball on the floor and rolls it toward Sphere. Sphere kicks it at BQ, but BQ ducks. The ball ricochets off the backboard and nails Sphere right in the face, sending him flying down the court. Cut back to the library, where the other four are back in their seats. BQ and Sphere soon enter, with Sphere holding an ice pack over his face. Sphere tries to push BQ, but misses. Sphere: Get your stuff, let's go! Sphere turns to face everyone else, but ends up with his back to them, so BQ turns him around. Sphere: Mr. Wiseguy here has taken it upon himself to go to the gymnasium. I'm sorry to inform you, you're going to be without his services for the rest of the day. BQ: Geez, you say that to them like it's a BAD thing. Sphere: Everything's a big joke, huh, Quicksilver? The false alarm you pulled Thursday...false alarms are really funny, aren't they? What if you're home, what if you're family...what if your dope was on fire? Nate: Dude, I'd be ALL over that shit! Steve-O: Totally! Funpants: Like HELL you would! Sphere: You guys think he's funny? You think this is cute? You think he's... Sphere struggles to make "finger quotes" while still holding the ice pack over his face. Sphere: ..."bitchin'"? Is that it? Lemme tell you something. Look at him, he's a bum. You wanna see something funny? You hop in the time machine that I had six Chinese kids build for me, and you go back five years, and you visit Black Quicksilver! You'll see how God damned funny he is! You'll see because NOTHING has changed! Sphere turns to the doorway. Sphere: What's the matter, Quicksilver? BQ turns Sphere around so he's facing him. Sphere: You gonna go win a match? Let's go. Sphere reaches for BQ's shoulder, and after several attempts, BQ grabs Sphere's hand and places it on his shoulder. Sphere starts walking away with BQ, and walks right into the wall, as BQ shrugs and knocks a bunch of stuff over just for the hell of it. Eventually, Sphere finds his way out of the library, and takes BQ with him. Cut to an unidentified room, where Sphere has put BQ in a closet, and is standing there talking to him as BQ is sitting down. Sphere: So, how's Stump? BQ: He's good. Sphere: Alright then. Sphere shuts the closet door and locks it. BQ hops up and climbs into a hatch in the ceiling, then starts crawling through a heating duct. BQ: A naked salami walks into a bar, with a blonde under one arm and a two foot poodle under the other. It lays the blonde on the bartender. Table says "I suppose you won't be needing a drink." The naked salami says... The ceiling under BQ gives, and he falls through. BQ: THE CEILING UNDER ME GIVES AND I FALL THROUGH! Cut to the stall in Sphere's private restroom in his office. All that can be seen is Sphere's feet, as well as his pants and boxers, which are around his ankles. Sphere: Sphere Almighty! Cut to the library, where BQ is walking down the steps. BQ: I forgot my pencil. Sphere can be heard in the hallway. Sphere: What in my name is going on in here? Sphere enters the library. Sphere: What was that ruckus? Steve-O: Dude, the fat cruiserweight from CZW? Sphere: CZ-what? Funpants: Could you describe the ruckus? Steve-O: Alright. He's African-American. He has a belt. He...uh...wears a bandana. Funpants: Not you. If I wanted you to speak, I would've punched you. Again. BQ is underneath the first table on the left side...the one both members of Pretty Fly are seated at. He frantically searches underneath the table for a bar of soap, but is unable to find one, and accidentally hits his head on the table. Both Nate and Steve-O try to take credit for making the noise by making more noises. Nate: Noise noise! Steve-O: More noise! Nate: Was that the noise you were talkin' about? Sphere: No...this sounded more like a guy putting his head between a girl's legs... Sphere looks at Evil-Lyn and thinks to himself. Sphere: No...Quicksilver wouldn't touch that. She's too hot. Sphere looks at Nate and thinks to himself again. Sphere: And although this one *does* look feminine, I'm pretty sure even Quicksilver wouldn't stoop THAT low. Evil-Lyn: Ha! Sphere: You make book on that, missy! Sphere turns to Nate. Sphere: And you! I will not be made a fool of! My name is NOT Primetime! It is SPHERE! Sphere turns and walks away, and everyone can see that the entire toilet lid is stuck to the back of his pants. BQ crawls out from underneath the table, gasping for air. BQ: Oh...oh, man! BQ stumbles to his feet and goes over to Funpants. BQ: Gimme the stash. C'mon. Lemme bum the doobage. Funpants reaches into his pants, grabs the bag, and tosses it to BQ, who turns and walks away. Nate gets up and follows him, then Steve-O turns to Funpants and motions toward them. Funpants: Where does it say that *I* have to go with them? Steve-O simply shrugs, and starts getting nervous as he watches BQ and Nate walk to the back of the library. Suddenly, they stop and turn around, walking back toward everyone else. BQ: Okay, yeah, I don't smoke...like, anything. So who wants to take my spot? Steve-O jumps up without hesitation and runs over to BQ. He snatches the bag out of his hand, then he and Nate run off, giggling uncontrollably. Cut to Sphere falling down a flight of stairs. Cut back to the library, where Nate and Steve-O are sitting in the back. Nate: Heh heh. Steve-O: Huh huh. Nate: Heh heh. YES! Steve-O: This is...cool. Huh huh. Nate: Heh heh. Yeah. This rocks. This ROCKS. Steve-O: Huh huh. I see, like, that you have braces. I have braces, too. Nate: Dude! Steve-O: Dude...I just said I had braces! Nate: Heh heh...dude, you don't have braces! Steve-O: And neither do you! Huh huh...huh huh! Nate: Heh heh...yeah. Steve-O flicks a joint over his shoulder, and it hits a door, shattering the glass in it. Sphere is down in the basement, looking through files and talking to himself. Sphere: Mister, oh, Mister Renner...a history of slight mental illness? No wonder he's not as cool as me. Evil Smokey The Bear enters the room. Evil Smokey: Afternoon, Lawrence. Sphere: Hey, Big Giant Bear. How are you doing? Not that I care, but I still figured I'd ask. Evil Smokey: Well, since you asked anyway...I'm doin' pretty good. Just read me the latest Danielle Steele. Sphere: I'd laugh at you for reading a Danielle Steele novel, but you're a bear...you know...a bear that eats people. Evil Smokey: Now when's the last time I ate someone? Sphere: That guy from yesterday. Evil Smokey: He was trying to invade my abode. Sphere: He was the milkman! Evil Smokey: Man, whateva. So what are you doing in the basement files? Sphere: What does it look like I'm doing? I'm going through everyone's files and am going to use this information against them! And maybe I'll even find something on our good friend Jim Dudley. Evil Smokey: Who? Sphere: Jim Dudley. Evil Smokey: Who? Sphere: First fWo World Champion? HUGE asshole? Evil Smokey: His name still don't ring no bell. Must show how popular he was. How come you remember him? Sphere: Look, Big Giant Bear...this is a highly sensitive area and I..I tell you something. Certain people would be very, VERY embarrassed. I would really appreciate it if if if if... Sphere slaps himself. Sphere: WHY do I keep doing that? Anyway, this would be something that...that you and I could keep between us. Evil Smokey: What're you gonna do for me, man? Sphere: Well, I'm certainly NOT going to jump into your mouth. Evil Smokey: Got fifty bucks? Sphere: What? Since when do bears use currency?
