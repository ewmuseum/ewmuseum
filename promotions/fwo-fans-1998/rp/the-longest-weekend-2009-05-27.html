The Longest Weekend

27 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Monday, May 25, 2009. Memorial Day. It's a flashback, alright? Even announcers get off a few days a year. You can cram it with walnuts, ugly. OK, I'm sorry I lashed out like that. Crap, now I lost my place. Hold on... We're in some sort of large building. A warehouse, one imagines. It's dark, but... oh dear. Those are parade floats, and those are definitely not their intended riders. Scattered among the various debris and the ruined floats are the three members of The Underground currently drawing a paycheck: Mike Bear, The Flying Frenchie, and Brittany Chambers. Mike is laid out on the hood of a convertible decorated with patriotic designs, wearing a t-shirt and apparently a Scottish kilt. Frenchie is face down on a float decorated with hundreds of flowers, wearing what looks to be a neoprene wetsuit, although his beret is still perched on his head. Brittany Chambers is sitting up on a float platform, leaning against some kind of model set for a Z-list celebrity, I suppose. She’s wearing... good thing she’s not awake, she’d be pretty pissed to see herself wearing denim cut-offs which couldn’t really be classified as clothing if they were cut anymore. Her top appears to be a few bandanas tied together, and it’s not serving its purpose very well. Her hair is, predictably, a disaster. Everyone’s shoes seem to be gone. Also visible is at least 3 cases worth of empty beer bottles, an empty bottle of absinthe (isn’t that still illegal to even own in this country?), several half full plastic jugs of Wawa diet lemonade ice tea, and... shoe polish? Like a dozen cans of shoe polish, some open and completely empty. Scattered around Frenchie’s float, there looks to be about 10 to 12 dollars in nickels. In the driver’s seat of Mike’s convertible is a four foot tall knock-off Snoopy stuffed animal, already leaking cheap styrofoam from a burst seam in its neck. And taking a nap on Brittany’s float are at least a dozen cats. “I’LL TALK JUST TURN OFF THE VIEW!” And apparently they’re awake. Well, Mike is, but I can’t imagine the others sleeping through that. Mike springs off the hood of the car in terror, not realizing the car is parked about a foot away from the wall. Luckily, he ran head first into the wall and wound up back on the hood where he started, so he’ll be fine. Brittany stirs a little, by now used to Mike’s nightmares of being forced to watch daytime talk shows. Frenchie’s head pops right up, followed by the grimace of realizing he had been sleeping on the wire mesh holding the flowers. He pushed himself up and tried to get an idea of his surroundings. Then he noticed the wetsuit. And the gold medal around his neck with no distinctive markings. And then the barely dressed Brittany. He smirked. “Now zat is a lovely look on you, mon ami.” Mike managed to groan out, “Thanks, you too.” Brittany finally opened her eyes and stretched a little. Only after seeing the Frenchie Beam did she look down and cover herself. “What the hell am I wearing?! What’s with these cats?! WHO THE FUCK IS THIS GUY?!” She had turned around to look behind the paper mache set to find another guy I had missed. At least I think it’s a guy. He is wearing pink pants and pink cat ears. It couldn’t be... no, it’s not, I’m pretty sure that’s Mittens T. Cat. His trademark pink guitar seems to have been broken over his head, and there are a few more cats napping on him, taking no notice of the hysterical woman trying to cover herself with her hands and an few scraps of cloth. Mittens appears to be out cold, although there is some twitching. He may just think he’s chasing a mouse, or something. Who cares? Brittany storms over to Mike. “T-shirt. Now.” Mike raises his head up to take a good look at his wife and smiles wide. “Oh hey, nice look, sweetie.” “Very funny Michael. Take the t-shirt off. NOW.” “You know, my forehead’s a little sweaty. Could I borrow one of those?” Mike slides off the other side of the hood, laughing all the way as Brittany looked just about ready to drive his face through the windshield. He takes the t-shirt off and throws it to her. Brittany doesn’t break his glare at Mike until she reads the t-shirt. “Nebraska State Fair? What the... you know what, I don’t care. Now the...” She takes a hard look at what she hoped were pants Mike was wearing, only to find a kilt. “Are you... no, I’m sure you aren’t. Keep that kilt on, I don’t want to be arrested today.” Frenchie watched the whole scene with much amusement. Surveying the damage, his thoughts turn back to the past few days. “How ze hell did we get here?” Mike thought a bit. “Last thing I remember, we started drinking in the tent. Then Brit told us to get out there and help Ross. Then I’m fairly certain we were riding a unicorn.” Brittany shook her head. “All I remember was saying how something was a bad idea. I’m pretty sure it involved all this shoe polish, somehow.” “Well, ze important t’ing was we drank for free. Who was taking ze money back at ze tent?” “Well I wanted to drink too,” Brittany scolded, “so I had Bas take the money.” Mike smiled confidently. “Perfect! Our reliable unpaid intern, Fish. He’ll have kept our money safe so he can give it back to us and not keep any for himself. Now where is he, and why isn’t he getting me a beer?” Frenchie and Brittany looked around as Mike continued smiling. Frenchie looked back at Mike. “Zat is a good question, where is he?” *Meanwhile, a couple thousand miles away* The frozen tundra of the Northwest Territories, Canada. Even in May, the temperatures are still sub-zero. But sometimes that medicine just has to get through. Sometimes unforeseen supply shortages have to be dealt with. And sometimes you’re the unfortunate Sebastian Abernathy. Passed out on a dog sled in motion. That he’s supposed to be mushing. He stirs slightly, coming out of whatever stupor he was in. Trying to lift his head, he finds it very difficult, just now noticing the 1 inch thick, 5”x10” gold Canadian flag pendant hanging from his neck. Then he looks up and sees the huskies pulling his sled and grabs on for dear life. “This is not good.” *Back at the warehouse, our heroes have moved on to more pressing matters* “So why aren’t we Tag Team Champions, yet?” Frenchie really mulled over Mike’s question. “I am not sure, Mike. Zis is a question Ze Frenchie ponders each night.” “I mean, we tried everything, right?” Brittany has taken a position against the wall. She rolls her eyes. “You guys might want to try some actual teamwork for a change.” Mike was obviously not paying attention. “I mean, Damnation were 4 time Tag champs within 6 months before they broke up.” “Zat’s right, we should be at least 15 time champions within ze same time frame.” “Totally. We’re like, 6 time World Champs. What did they have that we don’t?” Brittany puts her head in her hands as Mike and Frenchie give the inane question quite a bit more thought than it deserves. Both their gazes find their way to the parade float with the unconscious Mittens T. Cat and the dozen or so felines still calling it home. And simultaneously, there’s an epiphany. Both of them turn to look at each other and are ready to say it... When suddenly the warehouse door opens, revealing the townsfolk looking to begin preparations for the annual Memorial Day parade. Mike, Frenchie, and Brittany all look up, stunned. Mike turns back to Frenchie. “Hold that thought, I got this.” Frenchie smiled a warm smile, sensing a rare sing of maturity from his friend and tag team partner. Mike took a few steps towards the angry mob gathering at the door. “IT WAS THE CAT GUY! HE RUINED YOUR FLOATS!” Mike turned back to his teammates. “OUT THE BACK!” Oh well, thought Frenchie, as he kicked open the back door, followed by Mike and Brittany, and subsequently by several dozen pissed off townsfolk who thought to bring their pitchforks, just in case. ... Thirty minutes later, The Underground is still jogging. All three occasionally glance back, to make sure they lost the angry mob. Mike smiled a little. “Well, it’s been a while since we got chased with pitchforks. At least there were no torches.” Brittany was clearly not amused. “Mike, honey, shut up.” Somebody’s ringer went off. All three were shocked they still had a cell phone in their possession. Mike reached into the pockets of his kilt and pulled out his iPhone. “Sweet, still here. Hello? Uh-huh. You don’t say. And you want us...? Right. Let me call you back.” Mike hung up the phone. “Fish is in the custody of the Mounties.” “Jacques Rougeau?” “No Pete, not THE Mountie. The real Mounties. I think they said the Northeast Territories.” “Northwest,” Brittany corrected. “Well that should be inter...” Brittany was interrupted by Mike’s phone ringing again. Mike checked to see who it was and smiled. “Oh hey! Sorry, hon, let me get this. Yo, what up, my man? Yeah? Yeah, we’re totally in. I just got to bail out my intern in the Northwest Territories. Yes really. See ya then.” Mike hung up the phone. “We’re playing paintball with Ross and the redhead chick with the Cruiser title tomorrow.” Brittany stared at Mike in shock. “Tomorrow?! Do you realize...” Mike patted his wife on the back. “Trust me, everything will work out on its own.” Brittany shook her head yet again as Frenchie leaned over towards Mike. “Mike, what exactly is ‘paintball’?” “You’ll see, Pete.” And with that, let’s cut them off before they commit anymore felonies that I’ll know about and thus be an accomplice to.
