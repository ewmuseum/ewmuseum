Of exodus and redemption (part eight)

28 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"So this is what you've come to? Shooting me like a common criminal? And over what... a bad angle? A botched job? The fact that one man was completely unable to carry out even the simplest job right? You hold that gun at me John... Fallen... but this is far too far." John Skyler looked at David Spencer and suddenly the entire world seemed to turn gray on him. This is the place he lived and these were the times he had to endure? Surely this was some kind of joke... a cosmic farce put on by an unfeeling and uncaring deity who had no more respect and faith within him than the cruelest rapist or murderer. This was a chess game of fools played by a masochist and he hated his part in it. Violence over violence over violence... all this time and suffering, the fragile crack of his mind and for what? So he could be standing here with a gun to the head of an egotistical blowhard who never cared for the people he was entertaining? Stuck in the rat race of life, John Skyler reeled against himself. The cold of the gun in his hand felt like a blizzard of ice, and was he really here to shoot this man? Really? David Spencer was a cruel man, in many ways far more sadistic and cruel than the costume gimmick he played. As the character Death David Spencer would beat people, burn championships and make boastful claims. But as the man David Spencer he was a petty minded man who crawled his way inch by inch over a landscape of bodies. David got to where he was at the expense of hundreds... thousands of people. The worst part of it was that Spencer didn't have a clue how much of a worthless human being he was. He had no concept of the cruel jokes and taunts that he used to make himself feel superior. He had no caring for those he hurt in his climb to the top, and if you asked Spencer about it he would tell you that he was in the business of entertainment. The business of helping others... while all the while it was his knife that he sunk into their backs. David Spencer was worth nothing in this world... less than the character Death, and thus it all seemed so fitting... "Too far David? Too far? Do you realize what you have done... what your actions have done?" Fallen spat at the ground and suddenly his eyes were blazing. "The choices we make in life have drastic consequences... and you should realize what it is that you've accomplished in this world before you tell me what too far is." David Spencer's nervous smile slid into a frown. "Please... spare me your bleeding heart liberalism you freak. Lest I remind you that it is your hand that the gun is in, not mine. If my words and my actions have offended you then perhaps you're too weak to be in this business... because let me tell you, this is a business of graves and blood. This is life Skyler... and it's more than past time that you came to understand exactly what that means." "To accuse others of being too weak is a convenient argument for skirting blame for sinking a knife into someone's back Skyler. People like you are never able to accept their own guilt in things... if the blame gets too near you simply move on. I'm not fool enough to think that your comings and goings in the fWo had anything to do with injuries or exhaustion... when things were not to your liking you skipped away like a coward..." "Shut up." All of Spencer's good humor was gone now, and his teeth were bared. His eyes still darted to the gun in Fallen's hand, but his cheeks were red and it was clear that his fury was quickly overtaking his fear. "Shut up freak... how dare you presume to tell me who or what I am? How dare you..." A loss for words in his anger, they looked at one another through slits for eyes. Fallen cocked the gun and raised it to Spencer's chest. The bluster and the fury ran out of him at once, his outrage exposed for just another act in a long life of presentation and facade. Spencer sunk to his knees, and when he looked back up at Fallen there were tears running down his cheeks. "Why? Why now... and why you? Who are you... who are you anyway and why are you doing this?" Fallen felt pain in his head, and the gun wavered. He struggled to hold it level and he suddenly felt cold creep into his body, up his spine and grip his heart. Fallen gasped in the air, but found he could not draw breath. "What... what do you mean? I'm Fallen... and you know who I am." Spencer looked at him, and Fallen felt fear rush into him like a dense fog. What was this... what did this mean? There was something huge here... some memory that he had tried to bury that was struggling to birth itself. In horror, he saw that David Spencer was about to speak and for one crazy moment he nearly began shooting in the hopes that the blast from the gun would mask this terrible information that was about to be set loose. Fallen reached up and ran his free hand down his face, feeling the stubble there with mounting horror. Spencer gasped, and there were more tears on his cheeks. "Your face... your beard... John Skyler and I traveled together for months in the fWo... and John Skyler did not have a blonde beard. Who are you?"
