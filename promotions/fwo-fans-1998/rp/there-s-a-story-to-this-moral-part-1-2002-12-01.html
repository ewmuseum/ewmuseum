There's A Story to This Moral - Part 1

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Amy Campbell and Brand Frontier had second night of tag teamwork ahead of them on Warfare. The first time, the pairing was spontaneous...this time it wasn't, and both were going to make certain they were ready for anything, as the Mighty Hero always did. Unfortunately, neither were ready for the situation Amy was about to tear into... Aimz had arrived that afternoon alone, as usual, and made a beeline for the ring where the man who was rapidly becoming a friend and even mentor was promised to be. Little did she know, 'Mrs. Frontier' had accompanied her husband to training that day... "B.L!" When she saw Brandon standing between the ring posts, Amy had slid under the bottom rope with considerably less grace than she would have during any match, stood up and embraced the much larger 'Mighty Hero'. She noticed a raven-haired woman seated in what would have been the announcers' position who, or so it seemed, immediately straightened a little from her leisurely pose when Aimz entered. Amy observed the sudden tension and, for a reason even unknown to her, lingered near Brandon with an almost possessive stance. Brandon instinctively returned the warm embrace. When something that feels that good suddenly happens it's hard to tell how many grains of sand fall through the hourglass. As Brandon regained his composure, he felt as if it had been way too many. "HEY swee..a..a..Amy.. Hey, Amy...Aimz.." Rubbing back the hair on his head, he could no longer tell if the dampness in his palms was from the recent workout with Cody Williams or an after affect from that modicum of affection. Ana on the other hand could care less. She peered through the ropes at this little girl, a little girl that felt it necessary to hover around her life-long love like a lost puppy. The smile that had been spread wide across that 'little girl's face had suddenly dimmed when her eyes met the cold glare of Brand's wife, who was definitely trying to get a message across. "...aaaaaand, that little change of heart tells me that this is...damn, what was it? Ana?" Of course, Amy was still Amy and as long as someone was opposing her, she'd stand her ground even...no...Especially if it that someone was the Paragon's very, very disgruntled wife. Everyone knew full well that she knew Ana's name, but they also kept quiet and civil to watch the meeting unfold. Brandon never got his chance to respond, though. The hairs on the back of his neck stood up as he saw Ana slide her makeshift lounger out of the way. "Yes, It's Ana." The stagehands jabbed each other in the sides. A smirk appeared on the face of Robert. He just finished running the wires under the ring and was the closest to the action. He was going to get a ringside seat for this catfight! Ana took two steps and with her long gait for her height those steps brought her to the edge of the ring. Being a professional of the sport, even one so new, Amy immediately shifted her weight to her forward leg. If Ana decided to slide in she was sure as hell ready to be on the elder woman in a minute. Instead Ana simply grabbed the tan towel that had fallen from the ropes and tossed it to her husband. "Here... B.L... Dry off. The next time she grabs hold we won't want her to slip off and possibly hurt herself now do we?" Now it was most definitely 'on' in any sense of the word. Aimz stared in disbelief that Ana was taking things that far with her verbally. Then again, she didn't entirely know the Paragon's wife...for all Amy knew, she could be a black belt. She just wasn't used to a 'normal' woman showing any sort of offense up against her. Instead of shooting any insults as she normally would have, Amy made a slightly more daring move and leaned closer to rest a hand on Brandon's stomach while the other went up to ball a fist in an almost threatening manner around the towel he'd slung about his neck. The rules of engagement were passed, and she spoke directly at Ana without pausing a moment. "Don't worry yourself over me," She smiled a smile about as cocky as anybody could have dreamed. "...I'll make sure to hold on tight." That's just the way it was...everyone was all smiles, except for Brandon. He was the territory being warred over, but both Amy and Ana kept grins and manners intact just so things didn't blow back in their faces. Double knots tied themselves in Brandon's stomach as Amy's hand came to rest on it. Claustrophobia and anxiety crept in as well as his dry throat decided to peel and flake. Even without the audience of workers now actually gathering around the ring this moment would have ranked supremely close to the top of the 'most uncomfortable'. Surprising to himself and to Ana, though, he made no movement to change the situation, instead he put his arm on Aimz' shoulder. That sent a shock through Ana and she hopped up on the edge of the ring. Her fluid motion belied an expert handling of herself and her surroundings. Brandon though simply used the hand on the shoulder to finally disengage the situation and move Amy over to the side of the ring. "Just put your bags over there and we can get some time in the ring done before the locker room meeting tonight." As Amy finally complied with Brandon's desperate request Ana stepped under the second rope and brought herself full on in the ring. In another swift motion Ana was now at Brandon. The movement of the mat jostled Amy and brought her from her tilted over the rope position, back to her feet and prepared for anything. As if marking her territory, Ana laid a kiss on him that hinted of lust and showmanship more than it did of love and compassion. While Amy was backed up a bit, debating how to retaliate, Ana took her leave with another little stab wound to the redhead. "Well, it's getting a little late...I'm going to catch the trainer while he's not busy and get a little massage to relax while you're getting your own little rub-down." With that, she patted the dumbfounded Paragon on the cheek and stepped past Aimz, through the ropes and onto the ground. She waved Brandon off and made her mistake just then... ...She'd given Amy time to capitalize on. At first, Ana only heard a "Hey..."...but it wasn't directed to her. She watched on in a stupor as the younger girl took a full step over to a husband that didn't belong to her. She gave him a little peck, no...kiss on the neck and then glanced back at Ana, though still speaking to Brand and her statement was perfectly worded to spark another flinch of rage in the Paragon's vowed counterpart. "Thanks for agreeing to spend more time training with me." Brandon couldn't say a word. Those knots were still there and now his throat had completely dried up. Was it excitement, fear, anger, discomfort...affection? What exactly were those soft cotton balls swirling around in his chest? He didn't know. All he knew was that he was in for a world of trouble no matter which way he turned. Ana glared at Amy for a second, a really long second. Turning away, she happened to catch the eye of one of the stagehands and he quickly looked away. A little embarrassed at the way she had acted, 'Mrs. Frontier' simply walked briskly to the backstage area. Brandon swallowed finally and his breath returned. He had laid a hand gently on Amy Campbell's shoulder when she had leaned in to kiss his neck. Not knowing what she was doing didn't seem to hinder Brandon's acceptance of the resulting actions. Questions swirled about in his head, almost making him dizzy as he looked down and in to Amy's miss-matched but bright eyes. "What was... that!?" Something within Amy stirred and suddenly her simple 'Thank you' didn't seem quite so simple. There's A Story To This Moral - Part 2 - The discovery continues.
