Chasing The Dream - Part 5

16 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
May 6th 1999 - 8:27 PM The familiar sound of a speeding train was heard in the distance, and Milo began to run even faster. Phoenix had been kind, and he managed to score himself a full meal at a reasonable cost. He had savored the moment...possibly a bit to long because if he weren't fast he would be running at the train's heels. As he drew himself closer to the train, he leaped inward grasping at a ladder that was attached to a passing coal car. "Ah fuck!" Milo's voice echoed throughout his desert surroundings, a product of sheer pain caused by the dragging of his leg across the rocks, which lay beneath him. A miscalculated leap had resulted in him falling lower on the ladder then he had planned, but he was still able to maintain his grip and pull himself up above the ground. Luckily he wasn't cut very badly, but the pain still shot through his veins only to be subtly eased by an accompanying burst of adrenaline. After a brief moment, Milo scaled the ladder trying his best to make without using his distressed right leg. Once he was at the top of the ladder, he quickly lifted the tarp that covered the top of the rail car, and climbed in, falling asleep atop a soft bed of coal soon after. May 7th 1999 - 6:05 AM "Screechhhhhhhhhh" Milo awoke to the scream of the train's halting wheels, as it began to brake. Learning the dangers of delayed departure in Phoenix, he hopped up and lifted the tarp slightly to observe his surroundings; if he was going to make a break for it, he'd better know what the area consisted of. "A strip of warehouses...." Muttered Milo, fearing the very worst. The train was almost stopped, and Milo wasted no time in making his way to the ground. Although his leg was still a tad stiff, he began to run for dear life, knowing a group of warehouse workers would soon follow. "Hey! Get back here!" One of them yelled, initiating the chase, which was beginning. Several other workers soon followed, and Milo found himself narrowly dodging a shovel that had been swung violently at his head. Hearing the sound of a train whistle, Milo knew there was only one way he could leave this yard without taking a thrashing. He turned, and ran towards the speeding train, not knowing what the next few seconds would hold. ... His youth... ... His hopes... ... His dreams... ... His entire life had flashed before his eyes, permanently etching a still frame of this moment in his mind. Milo lay on the gravel on the opposite side of the speeding train, narrowly racing it and escaping the clutches of the angry mob which was furiously waiting for the train to pass... But by the time the train had passed, Milo was nowhere to be found. He had escaped, and was headed North once again.
