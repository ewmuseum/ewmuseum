Mission Accomplished...?

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Cyberslam V ended about 45 minutes ago, with a fantastic finish at the end. The Main Event lived up to all the expectations one would expect from the most prodigious spectacle in the wrestling industry. The camera panned from right to left in a locker room, where Erik Kelly sat on the sofa. He was still in his wrestling gear, the only noticeable difference being that he had his injured leg wrapped up in ice. His hair was still matted down in sweat, as he had yet to take a shower. He was leaned forward, his arms resting on his knees. In his hands was the most prized possession he could ever imagine holding. The fWo World Heavyweight Championship. He had barely spoken a word. He hadn't taken any phone calls. Even Pamela Dee called, and he promised to call her back. He needed a moment. An opportunity to become acclimated to the idea that he had accomplished what he set out to do. He finally became the Champion. The leader of the pack. What he found amazing was all the thoughts that had gone through his head during this time of just staring at his new belt. There's a fingerprint from where he first was handed it. The gold was shining a magnificent glow. Staring into the brightness of the belt warmed his innermost feelings. It wasn't 'The Eradication' that beat Ruben. It was 'The Holocaust'. A frogsplash by a 6'11", 336 lb. man. A move people thought would be nuts for a big man to do. A move he won't do often anyways. But it's ironic that the win wasn't by the most feared finishing move in fWo. Who would be his first opponent he would defend the title against? When would he get the chance to return to Boston and have himself a "Ray Bourque moment" in Government Square? Then Erik remembered all the rumors and concerns going about. FOX was planning to pull the plug on Slaughter, and the fWo would be left without a television contract unless they could find a new home. He knew there wasn't much to worry about there... But what if...? As he finally decided that he had enough time to himself, and that he should take a shower and get back to Pamela at the hotel, Erik couldn't help but keep wondering if things couldn't work out for fWo and a new network home. Erik Kelly was the Champion...but would he have a title to defend after tomorrow?
