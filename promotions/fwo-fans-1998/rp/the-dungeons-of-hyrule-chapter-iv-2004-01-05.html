The Dungeons of Hyrule: Chapter IV

5 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
October 3rd, 2003; Early Afternoon Today was just one of those days, because Stick had called in sick today, leaving Allen and Stone to handle things in the Dungeons of Hyrule. Luckilly, it was another "calm before the storm" day, because there were only two people who were browsing about. One was a mother who was trying to find a birthday gift for her eight-year-old son. The other was a average Joe. Except that his name wasn't Joe. I'd tell you his name, but he's rather unimportant to this story anyway. Allen was sorting the video games behind the counter, while Stone was waiting for the customers to come to him. A third individual, who would be terribly important to at least this part of the story, stormed into the place. He wore blue overalls over a green shirt, white gloves, and brown shoes. He also wore a green hat with an "L" within a white circle, and he had a big black mustache. Yes, folks, this guy looked like Luigi. And he marched right up to Stone and pointed his finger at him, "You-a. Stop selling Mario Sunshine-a. Now-a." He was speaking in this REALLY obviously bad Italian accent. However, Stone just laughed out loud and couldn't really formulate a response. His laughing, though, brought the attention of the Codemaster, who spun around and saw Luigi standing right there. The Codemaster laughed, as well, but was able to maintain his composure long enough, "Can I... help you?" "Mama mia!" he said, in that horrible Italian accent again, which just caused Stone to fall over in laughter, "I-a came to make you-a stop sellin' the Sunshine-a!" The Codemaster covered his eyes in embarassment. No, this freak wasn't happening. But he had to work through it, "Why?" Luigi scratched his head, "Uh, well, I'm-a tired of the Mario! And-a I want my own game-a, Luigi's Mansion, back on the shelves-a!" "Are you planning on buying anything?" Allen asked. "Well-a..." Luigi started, then he hung his head low, "No." "Then please, for the love of the children, Christmas, puppies, and King Dodongo himself, leave." Allen said. Luigi frowned like he was a chat smiley in a chat with Daniel Adams, and he just kind of walked away. Allen turned to Stone, who was just now getting up, and wiping the tears from his eyes after all of that laughter. "The freaks we seem to attract, huh?" Allen asked, with a small grin on his face. "Seriously." Stone responded. There was a pause between the two. For a while, nothing really happened. Nobody came to them for a purchase quite yet, and therefore, Stone decided to change the topic, "So, why the hate on wrestling? I mean, if you're ever-so-obviously gonna attract freaks like that." "Man, don't start. Don't EVEN start. I had this discussion with Stick already, I ain't comin' back to that." Allen said, sternly, to his cousin. "Yeah, well, I'm gettin' phone calls from wrestlers and people asking where you went like I'm some beacon of direction. That disturbingly chipper Landers girl called. Then that Knight fellow. Hell, I got this one call from a guy named Senor Funpants." "Senor Funpants. Who the hell is that?" Allen asked. "Dunno. But after he fumbled about tryin' to find you, some guy named Spear took the phone." Stone said. "Spe--" Allen said, and then he paused, and realized with all manners of horror as to whom Stone was talking about, "Sphere." "Yeah, that's the guy. Asked where I worked. Gave him an address." Stone said. Allen felt sick, "You invited SPHERE to the Dungeons? Are you out of your MIND?!" "I just thought he'd want to buy somethin', you know?" Stone replied. "But it's Sphere! SPHERE! He's, like, at the core of my insanity! Well, him and Heftel, but that's beside the point!" Allen argued. Stone sighed, as Allen left the counter and started to head upstairs. "Sorry, man." Stone called out, but Allen ignored him. He simply went through to the door upstairs, and left Stone working the register. He probably would've gone after him had the average Joe not slid a copy of Kingdom Hearts for the PS2 along the counter. Stone grumbled. If there was one thing he and Allen Brown shared, it was the fact that they both hated that game.
