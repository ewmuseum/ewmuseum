Trust (Doubt)

26 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In spite of how well things were coming along with Trips and I (evidenced in part by the fact that I had actually taken to calling him Trips anymore), I still had my doubts. However, they were no longer in my partner, but in myself. I mean, I had done extremely well for myself in the BodyCount match, but the fact remained that in the weeks between then and now, I'd had only a single victory, against a guy who was in one of his first televised matches with the company and who was seemingly having more identity issues than my old trainer Duel had. And one of the losses had come against Carson Covington. Deep down, I knew I was a better wrestler than Carson, and I'd been in the fWo nearly a full year longer. Yet he'd still managed a victory over me somehow. And meanwhile, Trips was taking Borst to the limit in contests for the US Title, and he and Brand were really cementing their status as threats to the established upper echelon of the fWo, something I really felt I had yet to do, in spite of my BodyCount performance. I'd talked to my parents a lot over the past few weeks, running through calling cards at a pace I hadn't even come close to touching when I was in college, and the fact of the matter was that I was homesick, just as I had been while wrestling extensively in Japan. That certainly was playing a part in my inability to pick up the meaningful wins of late, but I wasn't willing to use an excuse, any excuse, to justify the losses to Carson and Voran Xias. They shouldn't have happened. And while they did, I was committed to getting my mind back into the game and making sure that I didn't cost myself another match, much less cost Trips and I our shots at the tag titles. Triality had promised to bring a bevy of superstars and legends against us in order to make us prove our worth in their eyes, and while Ivy, Trips, and I all knew that we'd have Triality beat before a rematch ever started, we still wanted to prove it to them and to everyone else. And tonight's show was going to be no different, I was certain, as it would be our first match together as a true tag team, and not just two guys tagging together in a tournament. What better way to impress the tag team champions than by handily dispatching whoever they threw at us? Lying in my hotel room, I realized that I was really, genuinely excited about the prospect of proving ourselves tonight. After all, what had the past year been for me, if not a continual effort to prove myself to the fans and to my peers? And part of me hoped that Triality would somehow get Triple Threat to agree to face us, because there was still the small matter of some unfinished business between myself and Fallen. And while things had changed between us, that was all the more motivation for me to get into the ring and prove to Fallen that everything I'd said to him thirteen months ago still held true. Yeah, tonight was certainly going to be a big night for Lance Knight, and more importantly, a big night for Team EQ... ...now if I could only get rid of those damned doubts.
