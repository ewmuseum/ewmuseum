Here comes trouble

18 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Here comes trouble April 18, 2000 (The scene fades in on a locker room backstage at an fWo house show. Geoff Green is with Mike Bear, who just finished his match with Matt Douglas. Mike is still in his ring tights and boots with a towel around his neck. A gym bag sits over in the corner) Geoff: You guys did great out there. Mike: Yeah, I think so too, Matts a great competitor and I think we got those fans excited. Geoff: Yeah, I heard them cheering. So, you feel like doing something? Maybe a movie? Mike (slightly dejected): Yeah sure. Geoff: Aw cmon man, I know for a fact that you two have been apart longer than this. Mike: Yeah, but it just seems different now. I miss her. (Geoff is about to speak up, but a knock on the door interrupts. Mike looks surprised) Mike: Come in! (The doorknob turns and in walks Brittany, wearing jeans and a Wretch t-shirt. Mike brightens up immediately, and runs over, giving Brit a giant hug) Mike: Man I missed you. Brittany: I missed you too. (The two exchange a kiss) Mike (jokingly): Hey, whats with the shirt? Brittany: Oh, like Id wear this by choice. (Brittany separates from Mike) Now Ive got something to tell you. Mike (worried): Wha...what is it? Brittany (feigning distraught): Well, just now, Wretch...Chris tried to put a move on me. (You can almost hear Mikes teeth grating from across the room) Mike (extremely angry, though controlled): What? Brittany: He...he touched me. Mike (biting his lip): Ok. I have something to remedy the situation. (Mike walks over to the gym bag and throws out the clothes on top. Finally at the bottom, he reaches his target. He grabs the handle and lifts it out of the bag. The light gleams off the metal. Mike points it up in the air and pulls the trigger...and the bit spins. Mike, paying no more attention to anyone, silently walks out of the room with an electric drill) Geoff: Um, Brit, do you have any idea what you just did? Brittany: Well yeah, he is my boyfriend. Geoff: But what you told him was a lie! Brittany: No I didnt. All I asked was a simple question: had he worked out? He then proceeded to think that I liked him and tried to feebly hit on me. Then, when he sat next to me, his arm brushed against mine. (Brittany grins that same evil grin that Mike does) Geoff: Youre bad, Brit. Now cmon, were going to miss the show. (The camera fades out as Brittany and Geoff exit the room and walk in the direction Mike was heading, towards Wretchs locker room...)
