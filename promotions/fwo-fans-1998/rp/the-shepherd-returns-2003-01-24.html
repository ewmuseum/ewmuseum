The Shepherd returns

24 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Deacon walked across the floor, looked at the clock, and then walked back to the phone. He did the same thing again. And Again. He glanced at the phone, then the clock, then the phone. "T'is ridiculous. He call soon." Deacon said, looking down at the phone as if he was waiting for it to respond. The phone rang. "Ello?" Deacon said after he snatched it up, cradling it to his ear. "Hey Big D, I heard you called." The voice on the other line said. Deacon smiled, it was a voice he had not heard in awhile. Chris Shepherd had done better staying in touch, especially after they had discussed how their career separation had affected both men. "Yes, Chris. I have a favor to ask." "Anything... shoot." "I would like you to come back. I need someone to be near the ring with me because I'm going to be wrestling in two companies." Deacon said. "TWO?! Elijah, you must be crazy. You do know that you were just cleared to wrestle a few months ago." Chris said, obviously irritated. "Yes, but CSWA not moving and time is running out. The fWo is having legal problems... they can use me to- -" "Excuse me?" Chris interrupted. "The FWO?! Their schedule is NUTS... AND they can't come back to the US." "That why they want me." Deacon said. "They need my image, and I need their schedule." "You need to stay healthy, Elijah. I know you want to do as much as you can, but come on." Chris pleaded. "I... know. But I use time I have left. I will wrestle there. Only question, you wit' me?" Neither man spoke for several long moments. Deacon could do this without Chris, he had carried it off in the CSWA for several, albeit mostly inactive, months. He knew the microphone and how to work the crowd. Even the words Chris had spoke for him for all those years were mostly Deacon's. He could do it, but he wanted Chris to be there. He knew the pace was killer, and he knew that a friend who understood him could be the difference between burnout and tearing down the house... in Canada, Mexico or anywhere else. "I'm in, D. But only if you promise two things." Chris stated, obviously hoping to have an ace up his sleeve to use if Deacon's stubborness overcame safety in the ring. "What is it?" "First, you have to promise that if your body starts to fail," Chris proceeded carefully. "Then you will walk away from the ring." "Done." "And second, that you'll let me do all the talking." Chris said as Deacon laughed. "I not have it any ot'er way, but only if you promise me one thing." "What's that?" Chris questioned. "That you not go on that weird 'Christianity Today' show. They weird me out. T'at woman wear too much make up. Great Dragon mask covers less of his face than her makeup covers hers." Deacon said, and Chris laughed. They talked the rest of the night. Not about the business, but about life, dreams... and yes, faith. From the beginning, that was the truest evidence of their friendship. And in this case, faith wasn't the evidence, it was the cornerstone. This time, with their conversation, friendship was the evidence.
