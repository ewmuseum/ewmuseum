The 4th Wall

26 March 2004

Wippit bolted upright and screamed, and then stopped because it sounded strange. And upon looking around, things were very strange. Everything was white. It was as if there was no ground, no ceiling, no walls, just a blank, never-eneding white. And yet, he seems to be standing on a firm surface. He then noticed that although he was breathing, no air was filling his lungs. "Am I dead?" he asked, but the sound seemed to be absorbed, like a soudproof layer was overtop of him. He began to walk around, not really knowing direction, and although his feet were pushing against something, he didn't feel like he was moving. No sound of steps, no rush of wind. And evertying the same blank white, so no visual references of passage. It was think than a small sliver of fear seemed to slide into his spine. "Where am I?" he said out loud, and again his words were sucked away. "AAAAAAAAAAAA!" a scream at the top of his lungs, but no louder than his voice was a moment before. Now he really began to panic, and started to run, randomly changing directions, but still he didn't think he was moving, even though he was breathing heavily from the exertion - but not breathing anything either. Just when he felt his mind was going to break free from sanity, he saw something! Just a black dot in all this white, but it was something different. He tried to make his way to it, and it started to get bigger. Eventually, he noticed he could see other things, and the realization that it was a window occurred to him. A way out, maybe? After an eternity, he stood next to a single giant sheet of glass, which extended over his head about twice his height, and maybe double that in width. Beyond it, he looked out into a strange site. It looked like a den or an office, but he couldn't quite place the angle he was looking at. And everything seemed larger, as if the glass also magnified the view. Before Wip's started eyes, a veritable giant walked into view. His mind instantly tried to grasp the familiar, noting what was normal outside of the size - white t-shirt, grey sweat pants, glasses, almost-shaved head. He had a can of Coke in his hand, and sat down in a chair, right in front of the window. "Hey!" shouted Wippit, waving, "Hey, can you see me? Hellooo!?!"
