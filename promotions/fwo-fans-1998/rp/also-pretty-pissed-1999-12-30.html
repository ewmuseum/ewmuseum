Also pretty pissed...

30 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Also pretty pissed... December 30, 1999 [Havok is shown walking down the streets of Anaheim. He is wearing khaki pants, and a white Millennium Men shirt. He has his long, brown hair pulled back into a ponytail, and sunglasses cover his eyes. He walks past a homeless man, who greatly resembles James Armstrong, reaches into his pocket, and tosses some spare change into the man's cup. He puts his hands in his pocket and continues walking. He stops in front of The Pond, site of the fWo's Countdown. He looks into the camera and begins to speak.] Frenchie was deported. I'm not gonna miss him. Scott Collins tried to quit. I wouldn't have missed him, but now Travis Beaven says Scotty Cool has to stay and wrestle. I don't care. Armstrong was fired. Whoopy! Once again, I won't miss the guy, just the hookers and blackjack. Rain almost got the title. Pitiful. When the best you have to throw into the World title scene has an army of fake superstars, something is wrong. Now this brings me to my main point- David Spencer is the World Champion. Does he deserve to be World Champion? No. Should he be World Champion? No. Is he? Yes. Why? God only knows. David Spencer is no more deserving of that title than "Crazy" Mike Heftel or Sid Snow. Yet, he has the title. I, however, am not going to sit on my ass, and watch David Spencer run around with that damn title around his waist. I've beaten David Spencer before, and so have many other fWo wrestlers. And believe me... [He takes off his glasses and looks into the camera.] That title won't be around his waist for long. [He walks off as the camera cuts to a commercial for Dick Clark's "New Year's Rockin' Eve!"]
