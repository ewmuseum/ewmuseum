Eye For An Eye [5] Self Loathe

9 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I've said it before and I'll say it again. In recent weeks it's applied to me but back in the day my Father learnt the hard way that when you break something, you couldn't ever put it back together the way it was. But I've also said before that while I shared a lot in common with my Father our differences would always be what defined our relationship, case in point; the way we react to certain situations. When I was broken down I chose to rebuild myself and accept the fact that I would never be the same. When my Father destroyed our family, he couldn't accept that he might not be able to rebuild it the way it was. So instead? He chose not to rebuild it at all. December 20th 1985. "So Vince... what have you got planned for the festive season?" Brian Watts removed his mask and tossed it into his gym bag, it'd been a hard night and he'd been faced with a green opponent and a rough crowd, being new to the industry himself he'd struggled to carry the match, the Masked Marvel... despite his gimmick suggesting otherwise, had no super powers. "Nothing." A stone-faced Vince Kinkade replied as he packed away his gear. Brian gulped for a second and glanced past Kinkade, in the direction of his WWC championship belt... which sat shining like an emerald on the bench beside him, Brian may have been new to the industry but he knew exactly what that title belt signified, he knew exactly the prestige that it held and he also knew that talking to the most respected man in the promotion could do nothing but good for his career. Correct on all counts, but the final one. "Nothing?" Brian continued nervously "Not even going to head home to see the family? I mean... we've been on the road for a while now and I haven't seen any of-" He jerked to a halt. Vince Kinkade's wrist pressed firmly into his throat, cutting off his oxygen supply. "Listen punk, I come back here for quiet time... not to talk to some wet behind the ears kid about personal things like my family, now in case you're deaf... I said that I'm doing nothing over the festive season and unless this is a quiz show that I don't know about, that's my final answer." Vince sneered, staring a nervous Brian Watts dead in the eye. "S... s... sure thing Vince." Brian stuttered in reply, as Vince released him to stumble a few paces away clutching his neck. "So stay the fuck out of my way son, don't ever talk to me again unless it pertains to this business, because if you do I promise... your career will go down in flames quicker than Hindenberg did." Brian Watts nodded his head, grabbed his bags and made a quick exit... as he left; the sound of Jimmy Valentine's sarcasm filled clap filled the locker room air. "There goes another kid that we'll probably never see again." Valentine sighed, shaking his head. "Punk deserved it, I'm sick of people kissing my ass then popping the 'lets work down the line' question ten minutes later." Vince scoffed as he pulled on his shirt and buttoned it up. "Well geez, maybe the kid was just looking for some tips or advice... or wow, maybe he was just being polite." Valentine replied. "They never are Jimmy, they never are and you know it, there is no polite in this industry... no kind gestures, it's dog eat dog, there's business... and anything around or in-between is bullshit." Vince grumbled, stooping down to tie his shoes. "Well... then... here's some bullshit Vince, how much longer are you going to play this game of hide and seek with your family? You've got to go back there sometime and patch things up, they need you." Vince shook his head. "They don't need me Jim, they've made it perfectly clear that I'm not welcome there anymore, all they need is my paychecks... everything else I do around there ends up hurting someone that I love." Vince answered. "Paychecks put food on the table Vince, but a Father they aren't... they don't tuck your kids into bed at night... they don't read them a bedtime story... and they don't teach them what's right and wrong." Jimmy added with a troubled look upon his face. "You know what Jimmy... you're right, the paychecks don't do any of that stuff and for that I'm truly sorry... but there is one thing that those paychecks don't do..." Vince picked up his bags, paced across the room and leant right into Jimmy's face... whispering. "... they don't get drunk or unfaithful and they certainly don't beat the shit out of my wife and eldest son, now if you're quite finished, this conversation is over." Vince Kinkade turned and left the locker rooms, leaving an uneasy Jimmy Valentine to reflect on his words. The WWC champion did not return home that Christmas, his money paid for the turkey but he was never there to sit at the table and eat it... for the Kinkade family, life continued without a Husband or a Father. Kellen Kinkade's Father may as well have been Superman, because he would only ever know him as a character from a television show, he wasn't home for Christmas and he wasn't there for his Birthday, unbeknownst to the Kinkade family, his scarcity would continue for many years to come. He stayed away because while he was there all he could ever give them was hurt... but as he would learn one day, feeling something is sometimes better than feeling nothing at all. Kellen Kinkade felt nothing for his Father then, and the real tragedy was... ... he never would.
