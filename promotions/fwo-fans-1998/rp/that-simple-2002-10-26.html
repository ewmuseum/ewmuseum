That simple.

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Was it really survival of the fittest? Or was it survival of the damn lucky? The hurt of his father's passing was still with him. It was something he would never get rid of. Somehow though he knew that it would work to make him a better person. Build character. Just like his Dad always said about everything Brand hated. "It's OK son. It'll build character. Broccoli isn't really that bad." He still wouldn't eat it. Matter of fact, Sean and Jarod have probably never tasted broccoli in their life. Be it as it may though he knew he had to get on with his life and meet his obligations. After these past few months surviving had become second nature, whether by skill or chance Brand had completed the obstacle course. With his signature on the contract he had taken that final step. Now he just needed to wait, to wait his turn. Be patient. It would happen. It would happen before anyone knew what was going on. By then it would be too late to stop him and Brandon would have finally gotten his revenge. Just thinking about it filled Brandon with hatred. He knew he had worked long and hard to put everything behind him. He had prayed for the ability to forgive, to forget the pain and anguish that had become so much a part of his life. Try as he might though every time he looked at Sean, every time he looked at Anastasia he couldn't help but feel that month of hardship all over again. Sean had yet to be born. In fact he was merely a few months old in Ana's womb when it happened. Brandon was in the ring during a match when the large screen flickered on. Brand had Justin Lee right where he wanted him when the first horrifying image flashed on screen. Ana sat bound and gagged. Nude, three months pregnant and covered in whipped cream. That monster had her and there was nothing he could do about it. He looked on as the demands were made. He looked on as his family was put in jeopardy simply because one man had no heart, simply because one man was so afraid that he had to resort to such tactics. Fear, anger, loathing all swelled in Brand Frontier that day. Four years later and it is still a bitter taste. It's something he wishes he could get rid of but it has permeated his very existence. It has held him and guided him to great heights and embarrassing lows. Two years ago Sabian Lords struck out at his family too. Another coward grasping for a sense of power, someone afraid to admit that they were in over their head. Much to his constant self-resentment Brand defended his family with extreme force. Challenging Sabian to a scaffold match Frontier launched himself and Lords off of the tower to the arena below. Sabian would be crippled for life. Kurt Vedder would also be another casualty in this war. Vedder assaulted Anastasia and found himself bent backwards over the guardrail, the victim of a powerbomb across the metal bars. Instances such as those would haunt Brandon Roche for the rest of his life. He knew that is was time to try and put his demons to rest. This quest has been for nothing if not to bury the past once and for all. All around him hatred and evil surrounded him. From the acts of desperate men committing suicide to others destroying each other in shards of glass this sport has become nothing more than a shadow of itself. It had to stop, even if it meant destroying himself to do it. He did feel a sense of pride though in his accomplishment. Survive he did. That was a feat itself. Fifteen amazing talents vied for the spot that he now has and cherishes. He would do it all over again if he had to. From the moment he put on those bright blue tights and was announced to the ring as Brand Zuma Dalton he knew what he had wanted. What was it that aligned his life long dream with his soul-searing mission? What ever it was he would not question it further. He had a job to do. One he could not wait to start. "Daddy, when will you be back?" Sean walked into the room and removed Brandon from his thoughts. He looked around at the seven-year-old boy and smiled. Once again simply looking at him made him think about that monster. Shaking his head slightly to drive those dark images out of his head he winked at his son as he motioned for him to come to the desk. "Just a few days Sean, I'll be back before you know it." "Why can't I go too? Mommy gets to go." "You have school buddy, you know that. Chris and Halo will be here. You all will have lots of fun. You won't even know we're gone. OK?" "Do I get to watch you on TeeVee? It's been forever since you wrestled on a show!" Brandon smiled again. He seemed unable to do much but smile when his family was around. It was almost enough to drive away the quagmire of feelings in him. Almost. He shrugged his shoulders as he answered truthfully. "I don't know what I'm doing bud. I was just asked to show up." "Pwomise me you won't get hurt and if you get tha chance, beat up some bad guys. OK Daddy?" "Sure thing Sean, sure thing." Beat up the bad guys. He makes it sound so simple. Maybe it is after all. A dream fulfilled, his destiny waiting. Brand Frontier had arrived.
