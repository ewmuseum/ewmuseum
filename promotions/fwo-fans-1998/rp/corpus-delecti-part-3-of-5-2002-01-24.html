Corpus Delecti - part 3 of 5

CORPUS DELECTI Part 3 - Trials "Stop! I wanna go home! Take off this uniform and leave the Show. But I'm waiting in this cell because I have to know Have I been guilty all this time?" -Pink Floyd At first, Eli Flair doesn't recognize where he is. The sky is a deep shade of reddish - orange, the color of a perfect sunset, only there's no sun. There's a fire off to the west, that looks like it's blazing out of control. The people milling about on the flat, grassy fields seem oblivious to it, as well as oblivious to the massive monolith in front of them. No.... it's not a monolith.... It's more like.... A cold sweat begins to form on Eli's brow, despite his sudden realization that he's asleep. Dreaming. This is no dream. He's standing face- to- face with The Wall. "Welcome home, Eli," says a voice behind him. Eli spins around to see a faint outline of a person. The Faceless, he's come to know him as. "What.... the FUCK.... am I doing here?" asked Eli, "I've been here before. I beat this. You tried to drag me back in and I beat this again. I broke the wall down and chose life with Angel over isolation." "And where is Angel now?" asked the Emily- voice, "You chose life with her, and she left. She's probably on her knees in front of that guy with the nasty backhand again. But hey, she gets what she deserves." Eli walked toward the Emily- shape rather expediently, anger flashing in his eyes. But no matter how fast he walked, he didn't get any nearer to her. Finally, he stopped short - Emily- shape's path backwards had taken her straight through the small opening in The Wall. "Go ahead," said the Faceless, "Chase her in there and beat the shit outta her like Mickey does to Angel. That'll make you feel better, won't it?" Eli turned toward Emily- shape, then back toward the Faceless. "No.... I'm past that. I'm above it. You're not gonna trick me again." The Faceless began to walk around Eli in a circle. "Oh, but I don't have to trick you, do I? The truth has always been far more fun. And it pains me to hear you talk about trickery.... you entered the Wall of your own free will. You walled yourself in, and you found yourself guilty. I notice you never carried out the verdict, did you?" "No," smirked Eli, "I didn't. Someone very important to me convinced me I was worth more if I stuck around." "Then you should direct your anger at that person.... because they lied to you," replied The Faceless, "In the past year you've destroyed another life, as well as her hope for the future." "Angel?" "No, Blanche," said the Faceless, exasperated, "Yes, of course, Angel. Do you really think her heart is in the music she's making right now? All she can think about is the lie you told her before she left." "I wasn't lying." "You love her?" "Yes." "How is that possible? You can't know love unless you love yourself. Can you honestly tell me that you love yourself?" No reply. "I didn't think so. Hence," said the Faceless, matter- of- factly, "you lied." "STOP IT!" Eli, the Faceless, and the unknowns wandering around outside the Wall all look toward the reddish- orange glow as it gets brighter and brighter.... Finally, Eli has to shield his eyes from the glow. "Look at me please, Eli." The sudden change of voices startles Eli Flair, as he uncovers his eyes to find himself.... in familiar surroundings. His old apartment in the Bronx. Eight and a half years ago. The way the place looked when he and Alicia lived there. Sitting on the couch.... is Alicia herself. "You're safe for the moment," said Alicia, "so please, sit down." "Yes, dear." In spite of himself, Eli can't help but crack a smile at the old, familiar phrase.
