Ghosts Of The Past [1] The Prize Fight

18 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
September 23rd 1999. "These vagabond shoes are longing to stray... what a crock of shit." Kellen Kinkade growled to himself as he stepped side to side in order to avoid deep puddles of filthy water that'd filled heavy holes and cracks in the road underneath his feet. The rain continued to thrash down upon him as he made his way towards a large group of figures, all gathered around various empty barrels that'd been set alight not only to warm the area but light it up as well. Despite the words of the song that he'd started to speak to himself this area of New York was certainly not one that you traveled to in order to make a live for yourself. It was an area that you traveled to when you had nothing left to live for. Brooklyn wasn't a nice place and the fact that the most honest living you could make there was to be a rapper spoke volumes. "Here comes our boy." One of the figures in the crowd uttered as Kinkade moved toward them "Quite a turn out tonight eh?" "Yeah." Another figure spoke up "Some rich white boy coming down here to get his ass whooped got a lot of people talking." Kinkade made his way through the crowd, finally pushing to the front to be instantly greeted by a large black male who cracked his knuckles and sneered. "Two ways we gonna do this white boy." The man spoke up "Hard way or easy way... I'm about to hit ya'. If you go down and stay down we can go the easy way." Kinkade smirked. "If I wanted the easy way I'd have turned around as soon as I saw six of your brothers over there, waiting until this is over to mess me up and take the cash win or lose. As it stands... they're gonna be too busy scooping you into a jar to pay any attention to what I do when this is finished." Kinkade replied. "Hard way it is motherfucka." The man replied, balling up a fist and smacking Kinkade in the face with it to a rapturous reception from the small crowds that'd gathered around. "Yo Keon... get me some lights up in here, I want y'all to see this punk go down." The man shouted, as another individual obliged and turned on his car lights. Kinkade had staggered back from the blow and was now holding his hands out in front of him. "Wait!" Kinkade cried out as his opponent came towards him with a menacing look on his face, it worked though... his request delaying the man for a second as he turned to the crowd to interact. "Can y'all believe this piece of trash? Wait? I ain't waitin' for shit!" SPLUT. Spit flew through the air... hitting the man right in the eyes as he turned back to Kinkade, who'd suddenly gone from a cowering downed man to one that was firmly on the attack. The man rubbed his eyes in disgust trying to get the spit out of them, something that Kinkade had bargained on. CRACK. "Fuck." Someone in the crowd hollered "There goes my fifty dollars!" Kinkade had ran forth, headbutting the man in the hands as he tried to wipe his face clean. The impact had not only broken the other fighters nose but probably several of his fingers too. If the fingers hadn't broke from the headbutt they had as the man tried to push himself up, Kinkade quick to step forth and deliver several crushing stamps to both of his hands. The other fighter held them tight to his chest in agony but it simply left him open as Kinkade grabbed the chain around his neck and pulled him into three damaging punches to the face. Laying back on the ground the man coughed, spitting blood up in the air as he did. Unfortunately for him Kinkade knew that if he was going to walk out without being shot he'd have to make sure the other fighter couldn't be awake to see it. Kellen drew back before sending a sickening boot straight into the man's groin... in agony he sat up straight and screamed out but his own momentum was used against him. Kinkade drove a knee straight back into his face as he rose. "God damn white boy." Someone from the crowd piped up "You the filthiest motherfucker I ever saw." As several people crowded around the other unconscious fighter Kinkade quickly made his way over to a man who was nervously starting to walk away from the scene, spitting him around by the shoulder Kellen Kinkade grasped ahold of his collar. "Money." Kinkade growled at the man as he fumbled nervously in his pocket. "H... here you go playa, it's all there bro." The man replied nervously as he pulled out a thousand dollars in cash. Kinkade released him and slowly flicked through the bills to make sure that it was all there. He frowned to himself before reaching into the man's pocket and taking out his wallet. "Compensation and insurance." Kinkade said to him with a wry smile as he took another hundred dollars and read an I.D. card "I got blood on my shirt and now that I know where you live, I wouldn't be sending any of your boys here to see me." "Sure man... sure." The individual replied, quickly hurrying away after Kinkade tossed his wallet back to him. Content with the night's work, Kinkade stuffed his money back inside the leather jacket he was wearing before zipping up the front halfway. All he needed to do now was get home and finish the bottle of Southern Comfort that he'd started. Little did he know... that a face in the crowd that he'd yet to see was going to change his plans.
