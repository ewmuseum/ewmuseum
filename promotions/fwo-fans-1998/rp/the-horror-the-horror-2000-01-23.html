fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization The horror... the horror... January 23, 2000 Rudo walked up the hotel hallway with a box of crushed ice. He whistled as he went. The English hotel was pretty nice... not at all like what was represented in National Lampoon's European Vacation. Rudo chuckled to himself. Rusty... will you never learn? Rudo turned the corner and looked at his watch. Still a few hours before he had to go to the arena for Slaughter, but he had better deposit the ice in the hotel room, collect Machina, and head over. The two of them had to go over some of their plans for the next few months with management. It sure would have been nice if they still had the tag team belts. Stupid Mike Bare and Letch... they had better grant us a rematch. I think we should get a rematch at Warfare... I mean, that would only be fair... Rudo reached his hotel room and fished around in his pocket for the key. From inside there was a funny sound that he could just make out through the door. What was Machina doing in there? He inserted the key and slowly opened the door. Slightly concerned about what he might find, Rudo decided to call out for Machina. Hey Machina... you in here? The mouse is in the house isn't it? Because I don't want any of that... No answer came, but Rudo thought he could hear sobbing. He put down the ice and glanced over at the television. On it appeared to be some wrestling Pay Per View. It wasn't the fWo, and Rudo squinted his eyes to see what it was. Royal... Rumble? Now why would Machina be watching this? Rudo could hear the sobbing louder now and his brow furrowed in concern. The shower was running, and Rudo walked to the bathroom slowly and pushed open the door. Machina was in the shower, fully dressed. He was soaked, and appeared to have a bar of soap and was rubbing it over himself, over the costume and all. He was crying, and scrubbed furiously at himself with the soap. Rudo was taken aback. What in the hell? Machina continued to soap at himself furiously and cry, and through the tears Rudo could just make out the occasional mumbled word. Young... Young... no no no... May Young... no... Rudo frowned. He had no idea what Machina had seen or been up to, but it had clearly broke Machina's fragile little mind. Somewhere in the distance a tune floated through the room.
