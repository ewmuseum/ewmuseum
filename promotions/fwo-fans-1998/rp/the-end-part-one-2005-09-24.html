The End - Part One

24 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The skyline of Bangkok was much the same as any other major city, filled with glass and steel and lights. High-rise buildings dotted the cityscape, and people bustled in the streets below. When the people themselves appeared different than those in North America, their wants and needs were pretty much the same. Over six million souls were crammed into Asia's "City of Angels", and every one had a story to tell. Two of those souls rode the elevator up to the elusive seventy-third floor of the Baiyoke Tower. They took a private elevator from the twenty-fourth floor, an express elevator that took them straight to the Executive suite. When the doors slid open, they stepped out into the short hallway which ended at an unmarked brown door. The polished stone floor matched the reflective surface of the walls. So many earth tones gave the hallway a subterranean feel, yet let to a room hundreds of feet off the ground. Sarah Creid walked in front of Pupad, her assigned caretaker. She wore no makeup today. No form-fitting costume for clients to admire her young curves. Today, she wore a simple white t-shirt and faded blue jeans. For all intents and purposes, Sarah appeared to be a regular American fifteen-year-old. She didn't look like a girl who had been abused, both physically and mentally, for almost two years. Well, there was that slightly dead look in her eyes. The sunglasses hid that, though. "Move," Pupad said as he shoved her toward the door. Pupad was an angry Thai man. Why he always seemed angry was a mystery to Sarah. Maybe the suit he wore bunched up in the wrong places. Sarah looked at the door. This couldn't be another client. It didn't feel right. She was dressed normal, not like the whore she'd been made into. It's not like she chose to hop a plane with her godfather, Nick Brandish. No, Nick had simply tossed her inside a private plane and said "Sorry kid, nothing personal." For two years, she'd been dragged around by Pupad. [withheld — a passage held under the museum's content rule] When she was taken out of her small hotel room / jail cell, it was to service a client. [withheld — a passage held under the museum's content rule] Now, here she was in a fancy hotel. To add to her surprise, a rather strikingly beautiful woman, despite the stuffy business suit she wore, opened the door for her. "Please come in," she said and stepped aside. American! Sarah's brain screamed. Someone who could understand her! Someone she could beg for help! She fought back tears as she stepped into the suite, slipping off her sunglasses and hoping to get the woman alone at some point. A whistling noise, followed by some humming, turned young Sarah's attention to the kitchen. A man in a wheelchair looked back at her, blowing into a straw to engage the motorized chair. The frail-looking man had his long-brown hair pulled back in a ponytail, and his suit was much like the woman's. The way he looked at Sarah made her feel uncomfortable, much like many of her clients. Her hopes of freedom and escape seemed to disappear in her stomach when she finally recognized the man sitting in front of her. "Hello Sarah," Rave McBain said. "My how you've grown." Surprise didn't begin to describe the way Sarah felt right then. "I heard you were dead." "Yeah, funny story about that. Seems your old man and Nick Brandish decided to grow a collective brain. So your Dad pays Nick to break my neck. Only the big idiot doesn't kill me. He just leaves me," he looked down at his paralyzed form, "like this. For no good reason at all." "Didn't you sell my Dad out to the Mafia or something?" Rave squeezed his eyes shut. "It's not important. What is important is that your Dad, the prince that he is (when he's not slapping you around), has made my life a living Hell." His eyes opened lazily and he stared at Sarah. "So I gave him a little taste of my pain by making him think you're dead." Sarah almost fell to the floor as a wave of nausea washed over her. "So... you did all of this?" Rave smiled. "Of course dear. Nick did as I told him to because he's an idiot, as I said before. An expensive idiot, but he usually gets the job done. He snatched you, and I arranged to have you start a new life here with me. And I must say, you've been quite a good earner." "You fucking bastard!" Sarah lunged at the crippled man with tears in her eyes. Pupad managed to grab her by the hair before she could reach him. He yanked her away and punched her in the stomach, dropping the girl to the floor in a weeping heap. "Why?" she gasped. "Why me?" Rave looked up at Pupad, and the Thai stomped down on Sarah's upper back. He pinned her under his foot while Rave rolled up beside her. "Two reasons dear. First, because of your old man I can't have a family. I gave up so much when I managed that ungrateful prick. I had no personal life, and what I did have, Vic managed to piss all over. All I wanted was a family. Now, that won't happen since my dick works as well as the rest of my body. I'll never be a father, so Vic can't either." Rave looked into Sarah's red eyes. "You're his baby girl. His pride and joy. I turned you into a little toilet slut because I don't like your Daddy very much." Rave took the straw in his mouth and navigated his chair about four feet backward. "Second is that I'm a twisted, bitter man. Sue me." Sarah squirmed under Pupad's foot as the woman walked up to Rave with a handful of grapes. She stood beside him and popped one into his mouth. He chewed it and smiled. "You know what the real hoot is?" he asked as he watched the girl struggle. "Your Dad, the mighty Vic Creed himself, is going to be wrestling just down the proverbial road from us. He's going to be closer to you than he has been in years, and not even know it." The thought of Vic being so close seemed to aggravate Rave, and he snapped at Pupad. "Get her up!" he ordered. Pupad grabbed Sarah roughly by the upper arm and yanked her to her feet. Her tears had disappeared, but the anger continued to burn. Rave noticed it more than anyone. "Oh, you're a chip off the old block, aren't you? I bet you'd like to push me down that elevator shaft out there." Sarah remained silent, but her eyes answered for her. "I thought so." He took another grape. "These things are delicious, Betty.
