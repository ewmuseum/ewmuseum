Of exodus and redemption (part one)

28 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fallen sat in the unlit car and looked out at the dark street lights. His head was silent. There was a faint ringing, but this was something he was very used to. In all of the years he had been wrestling there was always this sensation in his head following a packed house. The screams of the fans... most in the locker room saw this as validation for their feats. Fallen wasn't so sure about this. The screams and noises served to do little more than give him a headache. Yes, it was for the fans... but their expressions meant far more to him than their noises. He had been sitting in the car for over an hour. The 22nd of December, it was cold outside and sure enough, the snow had begun to fall in tiny whispering drifts. It was late... very late. How deep the night had grown Fallen couldn't quite say, but there was always the car clock if he decided he wanted to turn the key and see for himself. For the moment though, the silence and the dark was pleasant. He had lost tonight. Fallen suspected as much. Ric Chronos was always three steps ahead of everyone else. Three steps ahead on a good day anyway... but Fallen had seen far too many cracks in Ric Chronos to assume his days were good lately. Ric was running ragged, and while he managed to walk away with the belt tonight it wouldn't be much longer. Ric was running on luck and a prayer these days. Who would take the title from him? Fallen found suddenly that he didn't much care. It wouldn't be him... even after things had been explained in a few weeks he wasn't fool enough to believe that management cared about him. Management... politics... was he really going to go through with this? Of course not Fallen... John if you like. Of course not. You listen to me... I'm you're friend and I always have been. You do this... you go through this and you kill me... don't you understand that? You kill me and you don't want that... Fallen knew this was desperation talking and not sanity. It wasn't even really true anyway, was it? Hardly... he knew what he was doing. Of course he did. This wasn't death, but setting the truth free. You tell yourself that John. You just go on and tell yourself that. It's a lie and you know it. You know how it is... and if that is what it takes to get you back in then why do you want back in anyway? You and I... we were a team back in the CWL. We made that promotion... we put it on the map with our feud with Douglas and we can do it again, anywhere! Just think about it... think about what your alternative does. My God John, do you want to kill me? Do you? It was tempting to listen to the voice... but Fallen knew better than that. The voice was a lie... brought on by the stress of seeing his best friend fall to his death... but a lie all the same. To listen was madness... not the other way around. To listen... well, it lead to bad things. Things like what happened with Spencer. Fallen shut his eyes, tried to ignore the ever insistent voice in his head, and cast his mind back to the business in England... business he wanted to forget. No good deed goes unpunished is what the saying says... but what about bad deeds? Fallen suspected those were punished just fine. How long ago was it? Just forty days or so... a week before he showed up in that dammed mask at Twilight. Forty days ago... horrors that seemed like yesterday.
