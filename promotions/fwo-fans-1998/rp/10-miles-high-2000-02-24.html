10 Miles High

24 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization 10 Miles High February 24, 2000 ((The scene opens to what looks to be a huge party. Everyone looks to be in their early twenties and look from all different sides of style and fashion. Gothics,wiggers,skaters and preps alike all partying with almost each person with a cup of alcohol in their hand. The music playing is "Baby Got Back" by Sir Mix a Lot as the sound begins to die down and a man begins to hollar. The camera centers to the man as its Eron the Relentless.)) Eron the Relentless:Welcome, everybody, to my small little party, held for my 21st birthday. Even though it was yesterday I had to be in Nashville to annoy Mitch Wilson enough for his to lose whatever type of concentration he has. Everyone get drunk, have fun, it doesnt matter if a lot of you are underage drinkers and weed smokers and water bong people and crackheads and all that stuff, I am a bonified celebrity and will do in all my power to use it if we get caught. Also, the music is up full blast, I hope no-one cares. Lets get this party started! (As people begin to party again and drink, a huge smashing sound is heard at the door as 8 officers walk out in full riot gear and begin to shout as people go completly mad and begin to try to run out, while others stay due to the fact they are so waisted they think the officers look like purple elephants jumping on roofs. Many others look at Eron to do something, as Eron cocks an eyebrow and whispers something to himself) Eron the Relentless:They're fault for believing me... (Eron then walks out to the fire exit and walks out of wherever they were partying, and begins to flee the scene. The camera follows him on every footstep as he walks up a hilled slope. He looks back and just sneers at the scene as the camera man begins to talk to him) Matt Loco:Why are you leaving? Eron the Relentless:Do I look like a freaking superhero to you? When I see pig, I dont grab a fork, I grab a train ticket to freedom, its the smart thing to do. And since Im one year older, that means my intellect as a being is risen to levels no average wrestler could reach. Matt Loco:So you are telling me fleeing is the smart thing to go? Will you retreat from your match against Mitch Wilson at TRAPPED? Eron the Relentless:Look you underpaid incompetent loon,those are two extremes. One extreme is guys with plastic shields and rubber clubs, ready to smack any punk ass kid they walk by, and the other extreme is a surfer with a nickel for a head, which of course, if his head is a nickel, thats more than anyone would ever pay to see him headline a card. Of course im not gonna "flee" from my match with Wilson, like, come on, an Internet Champion must represent everything the Information Super Highway has to offer, and "retreating" may let me keep the fWo Internet Title, but it doesnt add the respect it needs, after being in the hands of so many disrespectful wrestlers, who cant read, let alone turn on a computer and connect to the Internet. And besides, I smashed up Mitch Wilsons head like a nickel on a railroad track with that chairshot at Slaughter, so I have nothing to worry about. NOTHING. Matt Loco:Do you feel that you are underestimating Mitch Wilson? Eron the Relentless:What? Of course not! Im estimating that I have a 99.9 chance of taking Wilson 10 miles high, and then dropping him so hard to the mat, we will have to call him "The Late" Mitch Wilson. So of course, thats not underestimating, because I think thats a pretty high number if you ask me... (Then, one person from the party walks up to Eron as he gives him a tap on the shoulder. The man has wild gelled paroxied blonde hair, and is dressed up like some type of Skater. Eron notices him right away) Eron the Relentless:Hey Kyle whats up? Kyle:Nothing much man, hey got any smokes? Eron the Relentless:No I dont, I sold my last pack to Rachel. Go ask her for one or something. Kyle:Shit man I need smokes bad! Uhhh where is she? Eron the Relentless:Hmmm, check the closet which was close to the door. If shes not in there, then just ask Dan, hes got a whole load of cancer sticks. Kyle:Ok thanks man, hey and uhh great party. First one i've been to of yours which wasnt busted in the first 12 minutes. Thats what you get for bringing every single person from one side of Kent County to the next, including every god damn groupie. Do they follow you everywhere? Eron the Relentless:Yes they do, see this one right here with the camera, she can do some very interesting stuff with that camera. Well see ya man, I gotta finish up an interview. Kyle:Jeez, you take that shit too serious. Well Peace man see ya. (Kyle leaves as Matt then just kinda lets the camera stare at Eron, as Eron really never realizes that Loco is in shock) Matt Loco:What the hell did that have to do with Wrestling? Buying smokes? We're gonna get in a lot of doo doo for that Eron. Eron the Relentless:Ohh well Im sorry Chris Finns whipping boy, just cut it after like I care. They're only buying the stuff, its not like im promoting the smokes. I just encourage it by selling it to those kids for cheap. Matt Loco:You're lucky you're a heel, because that means no small 11 year olds look up to you. Eron the Relentless:Okay cut the shooting crap man. Lowest Line, Mitch Wilson will get a 16 Hole Boot jammed so far up his ass the heel will be showing out of his mouth. The Internet Title, and the respect, is staying with the most enlightened "mega"star to ever grace a ring of any size or type. And if Mitch Wilson, or anyone else really has a problem with that, they can take a breath...of FRESH DEATH FROM THE BIRTHDAY BOY! ((Eron then hops into a car close, which is a black convertable, as he revs up the engine and drives away, leaving Matt Loco alone with the camera and film to edit, as the camera fades to black))
