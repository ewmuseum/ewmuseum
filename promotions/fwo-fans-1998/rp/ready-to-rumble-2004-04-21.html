Ready to Rumble

21 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was 9:45AM. A black Escalade pulled up at the fWo training center in Orlando and parked. Nerva came out of the driver's door wearing dark blue track pants and a red tank top. She shut the door and started walking towards the training center. She had a black duffel bag slung over her shoulder, which had a water bottle, a towel, some shampoo and body wash, and extra clothes to change into. After pushing through the clear doors, Nerva took a look around the facility. Up ahead in the middle of the gym was the wrestling ring. Inside was a woman barking orders with a French accent at trainees. It must have been Jasmin Desjardin. "Twelve minutes early," said the woman, looking straight at Nerva. "I like zat." "Miss Desjardin?" said Nerva. The woman nodded. "Oui, c'est moi. Leave your t'ings at ze side and get warmed up. When you're ready, come into ze ring and we'll begin." After ten minutes of light calisthenics and stretching, Nerva stepped into the ring. "So Frenchie tells me you're a cocky one," said Jasmin. "He tells me you t'ink you're already good. Show me what you can do." Jasmin nodded at one of the wrestlers that was standing behind Nerva. He wrapped his arm around Nerva's neck and immediately felt her elbow immediately jam into his ribs. She grabbed one of his legs with both arms and tripped his other leg out from under him. With him on his back, she dropped her knee straight into his crotch. A hand came on her shoulder. Having no idea that it was Jasmin's, Nerva turned around and knocked her in the jaw with a right handed fist. Jasmin's face turned to the side and Nerva held her hands up apologetically. "Sorry, I didn't know it was you." "Zat's okay," said Jasmin. But it wasn't okay with her. She took Nerva down fast with a drop-toe-hold and climbed on top of her. Within a second she locked Nerva in an STF-hammerlock combination. Nerva screamed as her arm was being twisted behind her back and her neck was being wrenched back. In her years as a fighter in the Asylum, she never encountered a submission move this complex and calculated. The fighters knew chokes, arm locks, and basic leg bars, but they were far too stupid to try anything else. "Alt'ough I t'ink you are a strong, tough, and talented woman, zere is still plenty you have to learn about ze ring. Like how to escape from a painful hold like zis." Jasmin twisted the arm harder against Nerva's back, smiling when she heard Nerva howl in pain. "Zis wrestling t'ing doesn't seem so easy now, does it?" Jasmin spent the next three hours teaching Nerva the fundamentals of the mat. Combining this knowledge with her past experience, Nerva's confidence to step into the fWo ring was growing. Working for Cody was paying her great, but earning an fWo contract would help her make even more money. After the training session was over, Nerva and Jasmin sat against the ropes in the corner. Jasmin put a hand on Nerva's sweaty shoulder. "So why did you come to me? Why do you want to learn how to wrestle? I hear so many bad t'ings from fighters about ze fWo. Many of zem would call you a sellout. What made you want to wrestle?" Nerva pushed her hair back, straining all the sweat she could out. "I never did want to wrestle in the fWo. I just started out as Crusader's bodyguard. But in the past few weeks, things have been getting pretty personal between Amy Campbell and me. I never wanted it, but she brought it on. Now I have no choice but to face her in the ring." "Ze ring is her domain," said Jasmin. "Do you t'ink you can beat her?" Nerva took a drink from her water. "I know I can beat her, and I know it's the only way I can finish this between her and I." She looked at Jasmin in the eye. "But I need your help. You're a qualified wrestling trainer. Put in a good word to the fWo management. Tell them I'm ready to wrestle. I want more than a manager's license from them. I want to get in the ring and rumble." Jasmin drank her water and looked up at the ceiling. "I will t'ink about it. Meet me again tomorrow and we'll work on your ring tactics." "Thanks, Miss Desjardin." Nerva slipped out of the ring and headed to the showers.
