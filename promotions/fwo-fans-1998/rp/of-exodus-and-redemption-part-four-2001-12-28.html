Of exodus and redemption (part four)

28 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
John Skyler drove the rental car through the Yorkshire countryside, following the instructions he had gotten on a piece of ratted paper. The printing on the top was an advertisement for a local smoke shop in London, and beneath this John had written the directions given out over the hotel phone. John had called asking for information about the Wrestling Academy that David Spencer was trying to get off the ground and had been surprised when Spencer himself had answered the phone. John had covered his surprise well enough... Spencer hadn't noticed him anyway. That was good... it kept the element of surprise on his side a little longer. He hadn't bothered to shave before he left, and John felt the scratchy stubble that lined his face with the back of a hand. That was how big a hurry he had been in... he had shaved every day for the last two years like clockwork. The car turned a grassy corner and came face to face with a tall English manor. This wasn't a school at all... this was David's house. John took nothing away from Spencer... he had clearly done well for himself. He was already a wealthy man before the fWo got hold of him, but the money he had made in that first year was enough to set him up for life. That was what being a savvy businessman like Spencer could do for a person... Spencer was one of the best politicians in the game. He walked into a promotion like the fWo that was trying to get off its feet and did better for himself than anyone else. Dudley was always jealous of it, and Davis is still trying to get at that level of salary. Spencer knew how to play the game, and he knew how to play it well. All the same, this school idea didn't seem like it was exactly going over gangbusters. Spencer was still giving out his house address for interested parties and that meant that he hadn't got enough buyers in to make a real go out of it. John parked the car and got out, surveying the landscape and tucking the pistol into his jacket pocket. He walked carefully up the walkway, looking from side to side to get a sense of the neighbors. There didn't look to be any of interest... the nearest house was a good mile down the winding road. This was countryside, nothing less. Spencer liked his privacy, and that was all well and good for a wealthy Englishman, but there were times when close quarters served a person well. John suspected that Spencer might wish he had lived in the city in a few moments, but for now just walked up the stone path and attempted to look casual. He stopped briefly in the small shed outside of the house where a wrestling ring had been set up, along with several lockers and crates. John looked inside one, and sighed without surprise. Leaving the shed, John knew now that his path was set. He reached the door without incident and knocked the door solidly. The sound was dull against the gray English air. John remembered the bit where Collins had burned down David's house... in truth just a set in California but it had certainly done wonders for that quarter hour in the ratings. Was it in bad taste to spin Ryan's death into such a spectacle? Perhaps... perhaps... but it was a tough question for him to answer. John waited for the door to answer and tried to think of why he was doing this. He knew what would happen when the door opened, and perhaps the good thing... the sane thing... would be to back up, run back to the car, and leave all of this behind. Now. While he still could. This was a dangerous path he was on, and doing this would mean events were set into motion that would put him right back into the heart of the life he had worked to get out of. This life had brought him misery... he should run from it, flee from this set course of action before it consumed him. The door swung open. David Spencer looked down into the face of John Skyler and his jaw tightened. "You."
