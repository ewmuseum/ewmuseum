Why? Part 3: Beatlemania!

25 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Fuck me!" Borst yelled as he pushed past a good twenty screaming females to try and get into his Johannesburg hotel. "It's like Beatlemania in this fucking place!" he said, turning to the camera to point the one T in Beatles. Borst eventually pushed through the groupies and managed to get into the hotel lobby, which the security of the hotel had done well to stop manic fWo fans getting in. This wasn't something Borst was used to. People loving him. Women especially. Any little sign of affection and he'd generally jump a mile. Just look at the way he shoved Yalenchka away when she tried to grab his hand moments ago so as to not get lost in the crowd. Yalenchka had however managed to literally fight her way through the masses and join her man at the reception desk. "Fucking hate women..." Borst muttered as he leant on the main reception desk, before turning to look at Yalenchka and see her standing before him in her panties. "Where's the belt?" Borst asked as he grabbed her top, pulling her towards him. Borst looked down at her black undies and sighed. "I don't mind you using the belt as a fucking skirt, and I know I often moan about having the fucking thing, but I ain't forking out for a replacement." Yalencka's face went bright red as she looked down at her waist. "Sorry..." she muttered. "It must have got lost in the crowd of people." She purred, rubbing her hand down Borst's chest in the hope he'd forgive her for losing the US Title belt. Didn't work. Borst shoved her hard before throwing her out of the hotel main entrance back into the mob of screaming fans. "I don't know why I bother..." grumbled Borst as he went back over to reception. The young man behind the desk nodded suggestively at a slightly confused Borst, before saying quietly, "You've got several lipstick marks on your neck." "Fucking hell." Borst groaned, wiping his neck. "And someone's fucking spilt something on my t-shirt." Borst attempted to wipe the stain off of his shirt, but eventually gave up and sighed. "Women..." he grumbled. "Fucking hate women." The young man at reception nodded. "I know what you mean." "Finally!" Borst cried. "Finally someone who understands me!" "I know sir..." the young man said, putting his hand on top of Borst's. "I know exactly what you mean. I just can't stand women." Wink. Borst pulled his hand away quickly, told the guy to fuck off and punched him hard in the face. A shower of blood covered the reception and young man back into the shelves of keys, knocking them all over the floor. "They always have to rub it in your fucking face!" Borst yelled, turning around to see a security guard charging towards him. "I fucking HATE queers!" he yelled before smacking the security guard in the face, knocking him clean out. The other five security guards didn't quite know what to do now. Borst had decided to attempt to destroy everything in sight in the hotel lobby, and had just thrown a chair through a window as it became clear that Yalenchka was causing trouble of equal standards outside amongst the screaming fans as she searched in her panties for the US Title. Two security guards had to peel away from the door eventually to try to stop the rampaging Borst, and attempted to pin him to the floor. Unfortunately, it needed more than the three remaining guards on the door to stop the legions of fans barging into the hotel, hoping to see their favourite fWo superstars. There was screaming, pushing, shoving, people falling down, getting crushed. Absolutely chaos. Borst had eventually been pinned to the floor by the two security guards. "Why the fuck did you do that?!" one of the security guards bellowed at him. "Why?" Borst just smiled.
