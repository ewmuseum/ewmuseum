Faith in Egypt

1 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"You didn't look at the schedule?" Chris asked Deacon the obvious question. "I thought you would be ready for it after all these years." Deacon looked at him, eyes that normally never showed an emotion in the ring, were now screaming. He wanted to say something, a lot of somethings, but the consequences to their friendship could be huge. Chris didn't understand; he couldn't. Deacon looked down for a moment, attempting to regain his composure. After what seemed an eternity, he looked back at his friend from before they had ever left the place of torment. "I can not go back t'ere." Deacon finally said, speaking slowly as if he was having to force the words out. Chris did know the problem, he had been there also. He had felt the pain of losing his friends, the people that had helped him in finding the truth that is found in Jesus the Christ and helping him walk through the early days of his life. Chris had lost his friends, but Deacon had lost his parents. "I know, Deacon. I don't want to go back either, but they've already signed us into the show. We're obligated." "T'en I will tell t'em what t'ey can do wit' their obligation." Deacon said. As with most the fwo roster, Deacon was tired of being outside of the country. He had made the best of South America, God even using the situation to speak to him about why God allowed Deacon to do what he loved to do
