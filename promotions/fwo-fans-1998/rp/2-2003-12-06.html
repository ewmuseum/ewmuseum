That one call that you hate The police calling your cell phone Saying it's an emergency They need you to identify a body That phone call is the worst thing in the world. It's as simple as that. Brandon Thompson had hopped never to have that call be made to him again, this time he was on the road, between Los Angeles and Phoenix when it came. Let's just say he returned to California in a double quick like fashion. The room was dark and dreary, just like in the movies. The simplicity to it was there of course, green tiled floors, cream white walls, and dank yellow lights. The man who stood silently in the middle of the room fit the cliche of coronor workers. Tall, and dark, very depressed looking. Hell being around dead people all day anyone would be depressed. "Brandon Thompson I presume?" The man said as Brandon nodded as he ran forward towards the man. "I'm John Spitz, I'm the head honcho around here. We just need you to do a quick idenftification and give you a few things to look over." John said as he looked at Brandon who looked back even more worried. John walked over and pulled open a large drawer in which Brandon watched his mother's body be pulled out so only her head could be seen. "Did some one kill her?" Brandon stated as John shut the door. "Now Brandon thats what I wanted to talk to you about. She left you this note," John said as he passed a slip of paper to Brandon, who read the first line out loud. "Don't hate me for what I'm about to do..." with that Brandon instantly had tears streaming down his face, he finished the rest of the letter and crumbled it up and tossed it over his shoulder. "Mr. Thompson your apartment is considered off limits by the LAPD, they have supplied a hotel room for you at the Hilton," he stated as he passed Brandon a slip of paper with the address. That day Brandon Thompson became a changed man... for ever.
