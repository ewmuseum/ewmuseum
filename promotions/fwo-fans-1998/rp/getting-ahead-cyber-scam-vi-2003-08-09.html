Getting Ahead: Cyber Scam. VI

9 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
They had to be kidding? Just look at their faces. And Dillon did just that. He stared at both Tyler and Conor as they stood side-by-side. Their facial expressions showing no signs of laughter... just emptiness and anger towards what they were speaking of. "We want you..." Tyler said slowly. "To take that part out... and change it around so the Fuse Bros. win the game instead." Conor nodded. "And then we want you to sell this videogame to the Mushroom Kingdom." Dillon interrupted. "I can't just change what's on the tape." "Game." Conor snapped. "It's a videogame." "... Right. Videogame." He paused while beginning to sigh. "And even if I did, how am I going to change ALL of the tap- I mean videogames? Plus the fWo won't even allow me to sell them!" Tyler raised an eyebrow, looking over at Conor. It was clear that they didn't understand. This game had a glitch in it... ALL COPIES MUST BE SENT BACK AND CHANGED ASAP! "Guys, look, I'm sorry... but you just have to live with what's in the past and move on." Dillon stated. "So Pretty Fly won and they weren't supposed to... you still have the future, right? You're still able to change what is going to happen tomorrow. Go focus on that guys, not this." Dillon was right. But in all that was playable, the Fuse Bros. didn't agree. There had to be some other way. This was a glitch! It was clearly written in the fWo Instruction Booklet that this game was between the Fuse Bros. and The Tallywhackers. NOT Pretty Fly too. NOT Pretty Fly too, dammit! "NOT PRETTY FLY!" Conor screamed, throwing his hands up to cover his face. Dillon could only frown, and by shrugging his shoulders soon after, did he say "Sorry" and leave the fWo production truck to go back to work. Tyler, all the while, just stood there, in a frozen stance. "This isn't right brother." He said madly. "This game has a glitch and it needs to be changed!!"
