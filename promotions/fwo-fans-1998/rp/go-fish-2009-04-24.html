Go Fish

24 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Destiny. This is my destiny. These were the thoughts of the man standing outside the Key Arena, lying in wait for the man responsible for the show tonight, William Beaven. He had everything he needed. He had gone over this scenario many times in his head. After tonight... there he was! The man gathered his resolve and ran up behind the new owner of the FWO, setting his master plan into motion. “Mr. Beaven!” William turned around to see a young man of maybe 20, dressed in khakis, a pale blue dress shirt/sweater vest combo, and clean brown shoes. He clutched a folder to his chest with his left hand, breathing heavily. He adjusted his wireframe glasses with his right and brushed his parted brown hair out of his eyes. William appeared slightly confused at the sight. “Is there something I can help you with, son?” William smirked to himself. Have I really gotten old enough to be calling college kids “son”? William preferred to not answer his own rhetorical question. “Just... a minute.” “How far did you run? Maybe 20 feet? Are you OK?” He collected himself and took a deep breath. In truth, he was out of shape, but the hyperventilating wasn’t helping. “I’m fine. I have this for you.” William took a step back. With the history of the FWO, even a college kid like this couldn’t be trusted. However, all he did was reach into the folder he carried and retrieved a piece of paper, offering it to William. The FWO owner cocked his head slightly, taking the paper and reading it over. “Sebastian... Abernathy? Am I pronouncing it right? This is your resume?” Sebastian nodded on both counts. “Well you don’t seem like the wrestling type, although we did hire Fairview Reed. Of course, most times we prefer to scout prospects in the ring, rather than read over resumes.” “Oh no, I’m not a wrestler, though, big fan. I was hoping to apply for one of those internships I had heard about internships being offered and I need an internship for college credit and I’m a big fan of the FWO going way back and I was...” William had a look of sadness on his face when he realized the news he would have to break to this young man. He held up his hand for him to stop. “Let me stop you right there, Sebastian. I hate to break this to you, but we already filled all our intern positions a few weeks ago.” All the color seemed to drain out of Sebastian’s face as he heard the news he had refused to believe could be true. “Hopefully we’ll be offering internships again next year. I’ll make sure to hold onto your resume.” “Thank you, Mr. Beaven.” Sebastian just stared at the ground and shuffled off. William watched him walk away, having done his best to cheer the young man up, but Sebastian wasn’t of the mind to hear it. But there were three others in the Key Arena parking lot who had heard it. Heard quite a bit in fact. As The Beav turned to walk to his car, Mike Bear, The Flying Frenchie, and Brittany Chambers waved and called to Sebastian. “HEY KID!” Sebastian continued his sullen walk away. Mike Bear was a tad irritated. Usually when he yelled at someone, he drew their attention. “YOU IN THE SWEATER VEST!” That one startled Sebastian. He turned around to see two of his favorite wrestlers waving at him and his previous disappointment just melted away. It was all he could do to wave back. Now Frenchie was irritated. “In what country does zat not mean, ‘Come here?’” Now it was Brittany’s turn. “Get over here you dork!” Sebastian’s eyes went wide. He sprinted over to where The Underground was standing. Well, sprinting may be a little generous. Let’s just say he was moving faster than normal. Mike, having the most infantile sense of humor out of the three, could barely contain his laughter. “Wow kid, that’s some stride you got there. I especially loved the way your arms flail out to you oof!” Brittany elbowed Mike in the ribs. Frenchie continued. “What my partner is trying to say is t’at, t’ough ze FWO may not have any internship left, Ze Underground may have an opening.” Sebastian’s brain took a vacation as he stared at the three Underground members in utter shock. The Frenchie smiled. At first. Eventually, the three exchanged concerned looks and Mike just decided to reach for the folder in Sebastian’s hand. “Uh-huh. Uh huh. Says here you’re 21. Valid driver’s license?” Sebastian managed to nod slightly. “Well that’s all I needed to hear!” Frenchie nodded in agreement. He looked over at the folder in Mike’s hand. “Welcome aboard... Sebastian Abernat’y?” Mike’s expression turned to confusion, looking down at the sheet in his hands. “Wait, seriously? That can’t be your name.” Sensing things going bad, Brittany jumped in. “Well, Bas is a traditional nickname for Sebastian.” Mike smiled. “Perfect! Welcome aboard, Fish!” It was Frenchie’s turn to look confused for a moment before drawing the connection, then joining Mike with the Frenchie Beam. Brittany ended her hand to Sebastian, who was still in shock from all this. Finally, Brittany slapped him across the face, shaking him out of it, then grabbed his hand and shook. “Welcome to The Underground, Bas.” He smiled wide and returned the handshake wholeheartedly. He looked back and forth between the wrestlers he had grow up watching, his expression a mixture of gratitude and hope. “Oh thank you thank you Mr. Bear! Mr. Delacriox! Mrs. Bear!” Mike rolled his eyes a little. “Yeah that’s great kid, now c’mon, we all won tonight.” Mike turned and smiled a big smile at his wife. “Some wins bigger than others. We got to get some tall boys before last call.” “OK.” He followed along with the group, not even processing what was going on anymore, though he made sure to ask the most important question. “This will count for college credit, right?” Frenchie smiled wide. “Oh oui, definitely.” Mike briefly glanced at Frenchie. “Yeah, now let’s get a move on so we can figure out where you can get us those beers!” Sebastian hesitated for a moment, trying to process exactly what that statement meant, but thought it was a bad idea to rock the boat this early. A better question would have been what college would accept those credits he was promised, but hindsight is 20/20. Welcome to The Underground, Sebastian (or Fish). Hope your liver and wallet survive the experience.
