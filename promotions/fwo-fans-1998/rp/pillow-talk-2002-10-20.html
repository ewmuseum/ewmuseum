Pillow Talk

20 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WE ARE NOT AFRAID -Lost Souls? It was one hundred and forty three miles between Tacoma, Washington, and Portland, Oregon. One hundred and forty- three miles is child's play to drive when you're in the right business. A truck driver. A salesman. An entertainer. Eli Flair and Angela Davison had been soaking up as much of the Tacoma nightlife as they could during their mutual week off. Neither one of these two sleep very much. However, playtime is over. They figured they'd drive the distance from Tacoma to Portland on Saturday, sleep all day, roam all night, and Sunday morning, Eli would drive to the airport where he would say goodbye to Angel and hello to Ivy, as she would be returning to the fold following her personal business. 5:18 PM - The Lazy Bones are lying in a hotel bed, entwined in each other's arms. Some of Angel's tattoos are visible for the first time - a tribal design on the back of her neck and the Chinese Symbol for "Chaos" on her left shoulder. An Egyptian pyramid with an open eye on her back. She's asleep, and Eli is running his hands through her hair. Passively, she makes a contented sigh, squeezing him tightly. "Hey," she wispered sleepily, "What time is it?" "Just after five," replied Eli, "Go back to sleep." "Naaaah, not tired. Just don't wanna move." "Well," said Eli, "Lucky for you you don't have to for another six to seven hours. Thought maybe we'd check out that club later on." "Sounds good." They lay there in silence for a few moments, holding onto each other. "Angel?" asked Eli, finally. "Yeah?" "Why'd you want to be a singer?" "What?" "Just curious," explained Eli, "What about it interested you so much?" Angel shifted in bed slightly, thinking. "I'm not really sure," she finally answered, "I think it was partly lust over a guy, partly an act of rebellion against my dad." "How's that?" "He was never very close to me," said Angel, "one of those 'I spawned you, now leave me the hell alone' types. Plus he had a problem with the way I dressed - Goth to the core since I was fourteen or so - and the fact that the majority of my friends seemed to be gay males. I don't understand that part, you'd think they'd be the least likely to try and take advantage of his little girl. But anyways, I don't know what his beef was against my being in a band, but when TJ and I got to be friends and he started showing me little bits of guitar after school, he lost it." Angel picked her head up slightly and pulled her hair back tightly from her face. Even though her skin had a creamy, pale tone to it, the thin white line of scar tissue on her forehead was clearly visible from this close a distance. "TJ and my first band, Iron Lung, was playing in a battle of the bands kinda thing. Shitty drummer and shitty guitar player, but it was decent enough to get through a few half- assed Siouxie and Pretenders covers. But my father didn't want me singing, and he never really gave me a reason. I told him I was singing whether he liked it or not, and he backhanded me right in the face." Eli, having nothing to say to this, simply held her closer. Undeterred, however, she continued. "I knew, that night - the first time I'd ever sang in front of anyone outside my parents' church - that I wanted to sing for the rest of my life. I wasn't the most popular girl in highschool, I was the wierd girl that nobody except the faggots talked to, y'know the type? I was the one that everyone said was a vampire, that everyone said was the walking dead, you know the drill. But when I was up there singing.... I dunno. I felt so fucking beautiful up there. I felt like I could do anything. I was the master of the fucking universe, and everyone in the crowd was living and dying at my say- so." She shakes her head, laying it back down on Eli's chest. "I dunno, it's stupid. Maybe I'm just out of my mind." "No," countered Eli, "You're not out of your mind - I get the same rush when I'm in the ring." "I used to joke," mused Angel, "that the only thing worse than having no father at all was having mine. Tell me, am I right?" "I really don't know," replied Eli, "My father never hit me, and I really don't know if he ever hit my mother, but he also died when I was thirteen, so I can't judge him one way or the other." "I left home when I was seventeen," said Angel, "and hitchiked from Ohio to California with about three hundred dollars to my name. Eventually I was able to get in touch with TJ and he came out to check the music scene - it was a lot easier getting a gig when you were a singer and a bassist, not just one or the other." Eli sat up suddenly, and cracked his elbows. Angel adjusted, wrapping her arms around a pillow in his absence. "Have you spoken to your parents since you left?" "Nope," said Angel, thoughtfully, "It's been seven years since I talked to them. I sent them almost every poster and flier that's had my various bands listed, but there's never been a reply. I guess we're both orphans, huh?" Eli leaned back, and kissed her gently on the forehead. "Whatever we are, kiddo.... I'm glad we're a we. Just two lost souls meeting in the night." Angel couldn't help but smile. "Don't worry, Eli.... I promise I won't tell." Eli laughed at this, and as he lay back down, feeling the comfortable weight of Angel's arm across his chest and her head resting comfortably on his shoulder.... he marvels at what he's got before him. A year ago.... he was spitting blood every night before washing it down with several cold beers or several warm shots of whiskey. A year ago.... he was getting stitched up every night, to get busted open again.... stitched up again.... and the cycle would continue. A year ago.... his entire life was the wrestling business and causing as much damage as he could to entertain as many people as he could before his body would inevitably give out on him. Today.... his priorities are grossly shifted. Today.... everything he needs is in his arms. Today.... the King of Extreme is thinking about something he never even considered before. Life.... after wrestling.
