Holiday Wishes pt. 2

24 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Tuesday, Dec. 17 Location: Gold's Gym It was just after ten in the morning, and Gold's Gym was already quite busy. Spandex and sweat suits, though, weren't on the machines. They weren't grunting and sweating on the free weights. There were, in fact, crowded around a lone workout station watching nothing more than a testosterone-filled pissing contest. "Beat that, bitch." Kodiak Vic Creed sat up from underneath the bar, having just completed a single military press of nearly four hundred pounds. The crowd was shocked and amazed at the awesome display of raw power. Vic smiled and slowly stretched his shoulders. He'd always been strong. Very strong. Whenever he worked out in the gym, he liked performing more of the Olympic lifts to work on his explosive power. He trained heavy. He trained intensely. He trained often. Unfortunately, a body can only take so much. Even the body of the Alaskan Juggernaut. Forcing a cocky grin, Vic Creed tried to shut out the throbbing pain in his shoulder. It hurt like Hell to move it at all. Pushing it to the limit like this training session, when he should have been resting it, probably wasn't the smartest idea Creed had ever had. But as Vic liked to say, he was a wrestler not a rocket scientist. But even lifting like this was out of the usual range of what Vic would normally do. However, today he was being pushed and pushed hard. His new training partner and longtime friend was good at doing that. Still, there was no way Creed was going to be showed up in the weight room. The clanging of steel plates against steel places. The sonofabitch was adding more weight. Creed took a brief glance at the weight on the bar. "You look nervous Vic," Nick Brandish said as he sat down underneath the bar, grabbing it with his huge, chalk-covered hands. Vic flipped Nick the bird. Of all the people Creed knew, Nick Brandish was one of the few who actually made the Main Man look small. Vic's muscles were shaped, sculpted like a bodybuilder. Nick was a block of beef, wide as a barn door without much of a neck. He'd never be able to pull off some of the high flying moves that Creed did in the ring. But Nick never needed to. All he had to do was get his hands on an opponent, and that was usually the end of things. In Japan, he had earned his name as the Grave Lord. People were lucky just to survive a match with him. The two men were great friends, having first met while Vic was on tour overseas. Both men had similar styles, and as a tag team they were hard to stop. Outside the ring, they fed off each other's raw attitude. Nobody messed with them if they wanted to stay healthy. Nobody could push them, motivate them, better than each other. The weight was slowly lifted out of the rack. Nick scowled as he lowered the weight behind his neck and exhaled hard as he thrust it upward. Even Kodiak Vic Creed was impressed by the display. Nick stood up and motioned toward the seat. "Your turn sugar." He had to add weight. He was the Main Man. "Throw those two plates on," he told an awestruck fan standing near the bench. Creed's shoulder throbbed almost down to his elbow. "Time to suck it up Vic," Creed told himself as he chalked up his hands. Fellow gym-goers cheered as the big Alaskan sat down under the weight and grabbed the bar. Bolts of pain shot through his body with the effort of raising his arm. "Get mad. Get tough. Get mad. Get tough." Creed silently chanted his mantra and turned his mind to something else. Something to block out the pain. He closed his eyes and thought about Christmas this year. His daughter Sarah would be with him for the holidays, staying at his home. She'd unwrap her presents in front of his tree, in front of his fireplace. She'd never done that before due to their formerly estranged relationship. But this year, his daughter was coming home. And her smile would light up his world. The bar rose out of the rack, lowered by trembling arms, raised back up, and slammed back in the rack. Creed roared as he stood up. Not from the successful lift, as most people mistakenly thought. It was from the pain that burned in his shoulder. Something was hurt, and hurt bad. Vic felt nauseous. "Your turn," he dared Nick, hoping with all his might that the big man wouldn't accept. "You da monster today Vic," Nick said as he slapped Creed on the shoulder. The Main Man fought the urge to pass out. "Looks like I'm buying lunch." "Damn rights," said Creed as the crowd dispersed. The last thing he wanted to do was eat. He needed to get to a hospital. But he couldn't, he wouldn't, show weakness in front of his friend. "But I'm gonna go grab a shower first." "No problem. I'm just gonna finish up out here." Brandish walked over to the dumbbell rack and grabbed a pair of 85's. Vic walked into the locker room and collapsed against the wall. He couldn't even close his hand without incredible pain. With Twilight only a week away, it was going to be tight to schedule a match. He'd have to make some calls, but there was no way he'd miss it. Injury be damned. Outside in the gym, Nick watched his friend stagger into the locker room. He set the dumbbells down and walked back over to the military press bench. Eyeing the weight, Nick sat down under the bar and lifted it out of the rack. He pumped out four repetitions and set the bar back in the rack. "What a pussy," Nick said in his gravelly voice. "I'm picking the damn restaurant."
