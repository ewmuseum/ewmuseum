Desecrate Through Reverence: Life Under The Weather

27 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Amy struggled with the sticky outter rim of the bandage across one of her forearms. Cleaning and changing bandages was a bitch to do alone, but she could manage. She'd done this type of thing before she'd had him around, right? It wasn't that big a deal. When she pulled the first strip off, a trickle of bloodflow began. "Shit!" growled the tiny, tattooed redhead. The blood from wounds given to her by Nerva - despite popular belief - began flowing fairly freely again, and she grabbed for toilet paper to dab on the gashes while sitting back against the edge of the shower. Her streak of independence came crashing down as her eyes glassed over with the beginning of tears. Tears that her husband saw second as he stormed in to the small bathroom. The vivid red painting itself across the white tile drew his eye first. Seeing her in this condition so often had begun to wear on him. It hurt him to no end and made him hesitate when he shouldn't have, because instead of rushing to her aid he simply stared for a long moment. Blinking away the haze of confusion and thousands of questions that had swarmed his mind the 'paragon' croaked out his beloved's name, "A...Amy..." "Don't even start," she said, "I know what you've been going to say since we left that fucking hospital and no, I didn't do this to myself. But apparently that's what everyone wants to believe, so go ahead...but help me change these fuckin' bandages before I bleed to death." There. That was all she had to say. Anybody could recognize her roundabout way of pleading for help. And with that realization Brandon simply grabbed a new sterile pad and applide pressure to the stitched but flowing wound. The pressure was enough to staunch the flow, but the amount that had already accumulated had seeped through and on to 'Brand Frontier's' hands. The sticky warmth stirred in him feeling of hatred, feelings he normally tried very hard to control. Smacking his free hand against the marble countertop, he pleaded with his wife, "Why did you have to let this get this far? Why'd you have to bend to their levels Amy? I know you didn't do this. We've lived in this world too long for me not to see how much of a setup that was. Plus... after... before... I don't believe you'd go down that road again." He soon found his wife thrashing her arm away. She stormed out into the bedroom and gently shut the door so as not to disturb their sleeping sons in the surrounding rooms, but Amy - as usual - was still quite liberal with her language when she saw Brandon following her out with the same fustrated questions lingering in his eyes. "Who in the hell are you looking at? It's not like I had to do anything to get to that level, I'm already there! I always have been!" When the redhead felt her energy receed, she quickly sat on the edge of the bed. "From putting up with Kellen Kinkade to taking a fucking bullet in the gut for you, I'm an intense person and you knew from the beginning of this that I wasn't going to back away just because some godzilla dyke decided to make business personal." "I..." she continued, shaky frrom the prior blood-loss, "I've held back a lot, changed a lot because of my love for you, Sean, Jarod and Stephen...but there's only so far I can pull myself. I can't sit by and let someone stamp on the side of my head - I did that with men my entire life and it got me nowhere." "But you can temper your actions with forethought. Everything does not have to be reacted to in a violent way. Don't get me wrong, dear... I'm not saying that you brought this on yourself by any means. You're dealing with the same crap I'm dealing with, I... I'm just worried is all, Ames. You need to realize that this is new to me as well. I might have been married before and have had to deal with this whole 'family in wrestling' thing, but this is the first time I've had to watch my own wife actually go out there and fight, instead of just defend. It's... jarring." That last revelation startled Amy a bit. In all their discussions over the business, this had never been a topic. "I've seen worse happen to you." she said, "It goes both ways, but there's nothing either of us can do. I'll never stop bleeding and getting bumped around, and I don't think I want to. I know it upsets you...that's why I try not to complain about even the worst of aches and pains, but what will be will be. You know just from training with me that I could probably snap your last wife in half..." Amy sighed, laying back and running a hand through her hair. "Just...Jesus, I don't know, Brandon. This kind of shit's gonna happen, and I'm gonna take it as usual. There's very little we can do about it. The worst pains I've ever felt have had nothing to do with title defenses or pay-per-views or wrestling at all, that's what I concern myself with. A bitch and a bunch of little cuts isn't worth the time you're spending worrying." "It is if she's willing to take the steps she's taken, what won't she do?" "Fuck with my family, hopefully." Brandon stroked her hair back, he hated that it would ever cover her beautiful face. "We already know there are those that'll do that though. That's exactly what I mean. It's like, in this day and age nothing we have is safe. And yes, I realize how much of a double standard I portray sometimes dear, but I watched my father do it for so long it's almost like second nature." Amy tried to smile, and actually managed a natural grin despite the circumstances. "We've got one thing that's for sure. One thing that you and I - and only you and I - can control. That's what I run on." Brandon smiled as well. "I know, Ames." "Then why are we even having this discussion? There's so much else you and I enjoy, to forget us because we're so caught up in one or the other thanks to a bunch of fuckheaded wrestlers is ridiculous." "Just never forget who you are in all of this, that is the thing that plays on my mind the most. Through it all, that is the only real thing we have to fight with, what's inside of us. I get so close to losing myself in it Amy that I think, and this might hurt your feelings, that if I'm getting close to losing it then you must be even closer..." Brandon arched his eyebrows with the comment, expecting retaliation of some sort. The only reaction he got was a kiss on the lips and a sly wink, "BL...I lost it long ago. Sanity's not my strong suit." "Neither is balance, agility... OR grace... explain to me exactly what is your strong suit dear?" He smirked. Amy laughed, "In the immortal words of Kellen Kinkade himself, I'm only good for one thing...and it ain't washing dishes." "Don't you know mentioning that name is like looking at a picture of your ugly uncle before having sex? Try as you might, Amy Roche, you won't spoil this mood that seems to washing over us." With the words barely out of his lips he leaned forward and they kissed hard. The smaller redhead soon pulled away with an "I...", but her husband's lips cut the retort short. In those moments every problem - every other person - faded away. Amy smiled when she thought to herself, "This is what marriage should be." Chances were, though, with yet another toddler on her way...wedded bliss would get bumpy once more. [BACK to "Plotting The Course"] or [FAST FORWARD to "Mending"]
