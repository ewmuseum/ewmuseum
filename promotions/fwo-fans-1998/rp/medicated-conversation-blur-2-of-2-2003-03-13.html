Medicated Conversation - Blur (2 of 2)

13 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Fresh air!!!!" Amy smiled as she craned her head out of the open passenger's side window. Sure, it was about -25 Fareinheit, but she'd grown up in Atlantic Canada...the temperatures in the west didn't bother her in the least. "That'll help me heal quicker than anything...I felt like I was gonna die, sitting in that hospital room all fucking day for two days straight." She'd been shot, yeah...but what was a little stitch in her side going to do to keep her from enjoying herself, as physically inept as the morphine she was still on made her. "I'm surprised they didn't keep you heavily sedated for their own sanity..." A quick wink out the side of Brandon's eye let Amy know that of course he was ribbing her. "You were the one with me all that time..." She snickered a little, trying to get comfortable in the leather seat of the little rental car only to occasionally wince with a wrong move. "The nurses were smart enough to get in and run as fast as they could." Before Amy finished, another unfinished thought escaped her lips. "Thank you for that, by the way..." "After... everything, it was the least I could do sweetheart. Selfish reasons notwithstanding you needed someone to be there so I was." Brandon's massive frame shrugged his response. "Plus... it had to rank as one of the best times I've ever spent in a hospital." She tried to eye him sincerely, but ended up biting back laughter. "..........you didn't have your way with me when they had me knocked out on all those painkillers, did you?" A giggle finally escaped when Brandon's cheeks turned red as her hair. "Nah, seriously though...I would've gone nuts without you there. Hell, I would've had a nervous breakdown three months ago if it wasn't for you..." "Thanks for that." With a jerk of the steering wheel Brandon refocused on the four-lane highway ahead. With a sigh of reluctance the mighty hero realized he'd have to stare at Amy Campbell at a later date. "Seems to me we helped each other out. I don't think one deserves a bigger pat on the back than the other." "Well...except that you did the whole jumping in front of a bullet thing for me..." Feeling a wave of guilt wash over him Brandon tightens his fingers on the steering wheel and slowly shakes head. "Hey hey hey..." After pinging Brandon's ear in an attempt to relieve the tension he was obviously feeling, Amy smiled and laughed again, with another joke coming to mind to rid them of the awkward silence. "I'm sure my breath on New Year's evens it up, we've endured equal horrors in trying to.....do whatever we're doing..." Her mind had trailed off with the realization that neither was quite sure of their 'status'. Brandon looked again to Amy. This time with a slight smile spreading across his face. He too wondered exactly what their label should be, or even if there was something there to really label at all. Maybe this was an attraction born out of circumstance, he didn't really know. He did know that spending time with her was wonderful. He also realized that they needed to take their time in whatever they were doing. Rushing headfirst in to something would only mess things up. "...I think... we're being great friends. I don't really know what else to say." "You know, it's been this wild coaster ride and neither of us has had a chance to really take a breath. You... I... I know I can't really stop thinking about you when you're not around. And when you are nothing, save for my kids, really matters..." Another shade of red faded on to Brandon's face as he uncharacteristically put his emotions on display. That was all he needed to say to put a slow smile on Amy's lips. She wasn't sure why she trusted what he was saying, but she did...maybe it was time to put her emotions out there instead of pure opinion. "You know...we're more than great friends, because I feel the exact same way. I've never had that sort of light in my life...even seeing Stephen hurts me in a way, but the only time it's ever hurt to be around you is when Ana was in the picture, because I knew for sure that we'd have to just stay in one spot..." "And it was so touchy because I couldn't say or do anything I wanted to. Normally I would have, but...I didn't want to give you anything more to worry or stress about in that marriage." "You know... Ana's brother Chris said that. He said he could tell you were holding something back. He didn't know what it was but he could tell that when you were around us you were guarded." It felt amazing for the paragon to finally be able to talk about this as well. "Ana and I had been through for so long neither one of us realized it. We were going through the motions. I think if she would have let go of everything it would have been alright... I dunno... it's like she had other secrets..." Another wave of super-honesty washed over Brandon Roche. Locking his eyes on the road and steeling his grip he let out another secret. "You know... that night in the rain, back when all of the stuff with Brandi...er... Sandra started?" "I fell for you then." That was it. No joking to beat around the bush this time. He had her cornered and she knew it. Rarely was Amy ever put in that kind of situation, and she'd never left one without screwing up somehow. On top of that, Brandon's sudden outpour had caught her by surprise...she hadn't expected the nervous 'boy' he'd always been around her to lift at any point, but it certainly had. As she stared for a moment, a thousand possibilites raced in and out of her mind...for the life of her, she couldn't choose appropriate words, so finally they all just came out and it was her turn to be the nervously fumbling one. "I've..we.....you...well, I don't know. You're..." The redhead pounded her knee in frusteration. "Fuck...I'm an imbecile, sorry. I just...yeah...there's a lot I pictured saying if this ever came to be, and now I'm just drawing a blank...guess I didn't think it'd ever happen. You're...well......I don't know what to say except for I feel the same. I didn't admit it to myself until New Year's, though....I guess I couldn't possibly ignore it after that, but damn did I ever try." "Yeah... that was amazing. Even given the state we were in, I just knew it was real." Brandon had finally let the flood gates open and months of pent up thoughts and feelings were surfacing. "I know the obvious thought from all of this is that we go the natural course from here. But, we have a lot of things to consider, I think we have a lot to talk about..." "Talk, eh? Damn, and here I was already drawing up plans for the porn site..." Finally, she couldn't help but crack a bad joke. It was difficult to work through these situations for Amy, though she ended up trying mainly because Brandon deserved an adult conversation from her then if ever. "Really though...there's not much for me to take into consideration, but I know you've got the kids and...Ana..." "I've... well, I've never really jumped head first in to anything my whole life. The fact that I'm even talking about this instead of sitting on my hands for months is a miracle in and of itself." With a nod of his head Brandon continues. "See... that's what I'm talking about right there, you make me better than I am by simply being you. Ana and I will always be there for each other. That's a given Amy, that doesn't mean I have to check things with her before I make plans for my own life. I'm... I'm just trying to be careful for everyone's sake." "Okay...so define careful?" He was confusing her quickly. It seemed like he was just 'sitting on his hands' in being so careful...but she'd never totally understood. "Well, hmm... I guess I just don't want to start flaunting our personal life for everyone to see. It's... never really turned out for the best you know..." He looked over to his travelmate. A smile again spreading on his face, realizing the bad humor in what he had just said. Taking her hand in his and laying it on her leg he glanced back to her and then again to the road. "There are just so many variables. We know each other and have these feelings from being together at the events and those few rare times when we get out of the shadow of the show. I think it'd be great to get to really know each other... I don't know what you'd call it... 'date' I guess. Make sense?" "You're really Mister Daredevil, eh grandpa?" She grinned and prayed that hadn't offended him. "It makes perfect sense, don't worry. I'm just used to....I actually have no idea what I'm used to." "Yeah, Daredevil, that's me." Brandon didn't normally like to be chastised about his moral code but coming from her he took it accordingly. "You're probably used to having do a lot more for a lot less..." "What can I say, guys bite....just try not to leave a mark for my sake, okay?" "Anything for you sweetness... anything." Brandon smiled again. He didn't realize how comfortable he was with this fiery redhead until this moment. He thought about everything that had been said, about all he had laid on the line, about how vulnerable he had let himself be. He couldn't have been happier. Amy couldn't believe the situation before her. All she knew was that this version of Brandon was one she'd only seen once before, and she certainly hadn't minded how the conversation ended back then. "I said this already...on New Year's..." She smiled, already warmed up to the 'new', more open side of Brandon he was putting out there for her. "...but you're pretty damned cute sometimes, did you know that? You're too perfect..." "Yeah, that's me, Mr. Perfect. Now I'll have to be paying Vince's circus a royalty..." With a smirk Brandon laughs at his own joke, which falls on Amy's deaf ears. Sighing at his own bad sense of humor Brandon looks at the signs on the side of the road. The conversation had taken up more time than he had thought. The few miles had already been transversed and the airport loomed in the distance. "Looks like we're here sweety. Time for us to get back to the real world..." "I almost wish we didn't have to." [This has been an Aimz/ Brand Frontier collaboration.]
