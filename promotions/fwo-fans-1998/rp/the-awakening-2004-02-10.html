The Awakening

10 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday February 8th, 2004 'I now pronounce you...' It wasn't a dream. Amy blinked awake. She didn't care to look at the clock, and she wouldn't have even recalled the situation at first had it not been for the dress laid across the chair directly in her field of vision. The sleepy bride ran a hand across her scalp to straighten out her hair, but to no avail. Hairspray was still sticky in it. She grinned, they'd really wasted no time to get alone as Mr. and Mrs. Roche. The grin quite suddenly widened. Aimz sighed warmly, Mrs. Anything sounded odd, however pleasant. It'd take some getting used to...that is, getting used to smiling so brightly every time she heard it. With that thought fresh in mind, she rolled over moving as gracefully as she possibly could, not wanting to disturb the company. A Mighty Hero was dead asleep with a pleasant smirk, and she couldn't help but stare. Biting her lip after what seemed like a solid four or five minutes, Amy couldn't hold back from chuckling lightly - she was a sap and, sappy as it was, she stirred her husband awake with a kiss. The pleasant sensation washed through Brandon as he moved slightly. It took him a split second to bring himself up to speed on the who's and where's of the situation. He pressed back against the tender lips held to his, their mouths opened and they melted into each other. Everything about the two was now joined, two souls brought to one. They enjoyed themselves immensely for a very long time. To them it was an instant, any time they were together it never seemed long enough. Finally laying back against the head board and locking eyes yet again, resisting the temptation to embrace once more their silence was finally broken, Brandon couldn't hold back any longer. He smiled the smile of contentment forever, "Good morning, my wife. Be with me forever?" "I'm not sure, sir...I mean, I've got a husband now. He might have a problem with that." Her smile matched his. "I've got an idea," she said, propping her head up with her elbow and arm to meet his eye level. "You and I skip out on fWo all week. Of course, that'll mean we have to stay in this room for a week." Amy scanned their surroundings. She'd have smiled then, but it had never ceased to begin with. "Darn, huh." He too followed her gaze around the room, "I'm not sure if there's enough for us to do here. We'd get awfully bored, there would have to be something to fill our time. I... hmm...," Brandon reached under the thin sheet. Amy's eyes grew wide and her mouth fell slack for a second before returning to her seemingly permanent grin. "I don't feel any board games or anything under here. Maybe cards?" The redhead cracked Brandon's chest lightly, yanking his arm up between them as she bit her lip, laughing ahead of time at the line she'd carefully plotted... "I won't break the surface as a spade, but you've got my heart." She laughed again, "That could've gone in the vows. I'm damn good...but you've turned me sappy as all hell. What happens now, anyway? I want this feeling all the time!" Brandon's eyebrow raised at that. He bit his tongue however, instead of going down the usual perverted line of jokes and innuendo he turned a bit more serious. "Just never leave, that'll ensure that I feel like this all the time." Brandon's check twitched a bit as he screwed his mouth tightly together. Amy reached out and poked her husband on the nose, trying to get his attention as he had suddenly drifted away. It did the trick as the mighty hero heaved a sigh. "Sorry. You said vows, it made me think about yesterday and all the commotion before we exchanged our vows. I'm...really sorry about that, dear heart." He sat up a little, the guilt really washing over him. As he leaned forward, even Amy's naked form couldn't shake him. She slid up next to him as quickly as he had moved away. It felt too good to be together. She grabbed the edge of the sheet and covered her legs up as she laid her head against his arm. "I promise you Mrs. Roche...this won't be a 'different road, same trip' deal." Amy's smile faded for the first time, albeit only slightly. She bit her lip and spoke reassuringly - both to herself and her new husband... "It isn't your fault, and he was dealt with quickly. Of course, I expect he'll pay next time he sees either of us..." she snickered, "But for now, let's drop it. Kinkade's never been able to ruin us, and he never will." "It just makes me think that now you're my wife, now that there is a bigger 'target' things will travel down simialar paths. History has a tendency to repeat itself. Even if we fight it every step of the way. For the first time in a long time sweety, I'm thinking what life would be like 'outside' this world." Her expression went slightly grim, "We can leave it, if you'd like. We've got the money to. But we'd probably go insane without it, even with one another." "Besides, it only makes us stronger. History can repeat all it'd like, but I've learned from every mistake either of us have made in the past. Everything else can repeat, but we don't have to. Kinkade, Creed...any of them can test that, I could care less. Now, they're nothing more than a prick in our side. We're really together now - we're not fragile, not unsteady on our feet. It might take some getting used to, but we're unbreakable and we told one another that a thousand times over in everything we did yesterday." Brandon couldn't speak. He had already began to tear up as he listened to Amy's sweet voice. Sinking back against his pillow he sighed, content. His fiesty red-headed wife laid over on his stomach, sticking her finger in his belly button randomly. "You know Amy...I love you." "And I love you." came her soft response. "A year and some change ago, I'd have laughed if someone'd said I'd be here right now. It would've broken me, too...it seemed so impossible. I know now that it isn't, nothing is..." Their eyes never strayed from one another. "...and I think we're both all the better with that knowledge."
