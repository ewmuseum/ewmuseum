No More Wrestling

9 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I wonder what my people think of me? Have I shamed them? Do they look up to me anymore? Have I lost my touch with reality? I must admit, this Deacon has been an honorable opponent, and it's times like these I need to cherish and not regret, but apart of me wants to believe I am not doing the correct job. The years of training and surviving through all the pain and suffering seems worthless now. I stand here as a small little high flyer, with no real trademark. Just another fly on the wall that is easy pickings. Deacon has brought some of the best traits out of me, but is that enough for me? I spent my life training and now I worry about the next "Big Risk" to take. This is a joke and with that makes me just as funny. Why do I even bother worrying about something I have no interest in? I could be one of the great competitors here, and no, I am not just pushing my self. I have a greater ability, something you can't just pick up at a school, but rather from years of punishment and training. Thailand has been great to me and this is how I repay them. Shooting Star Press... Moonasault... Hurricanrania... What am I thinking? This isn't Padrona at all. This isn't any body. I think tonight will be a new start. Deacon, .One, .Three all have there own way, but I sit here behind a mask and practice the "normal" wrestling moves. What a joke I have be come. All I need is the jester hat and I could be the prince's comic relief. I think tonight will be much different then normal. I'm not even sure if fWo knows what I can do, and I am wasting my time fighting, no sorry, "wrestling" Deacon. This is ludicrous. Deacon should know, but he just thinks I'm normal. I'm supose to represent Thailand with Respect and Honor, but as I see it now, I don't even respect the years they put into my training. It's time for a change. NO MORE WRESTLING.
