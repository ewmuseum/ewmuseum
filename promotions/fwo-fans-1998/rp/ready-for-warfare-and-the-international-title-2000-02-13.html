Ready for Warfare, and the International Title

13 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Ready for Warfare, and the International Title February 13, 2000 Havok is shown driving his Chevy Camaro down the street, on his way to catch a flight to the site of Warfare. The camera is focused on him, and he is talking while he drives. "I just found out that I get to face Matt Douglas on Warfare for the International Title. This is great. I mean, I know I'm going to win. I'm more talented, I'm a lot cuter, and much, much cooler. Normally, I'd buy the title from him, but, I'll just beat him for it. You see, the odds are obviously stacked in my favor, since I've beaten Wolfie Douglas twice already, and he's beaten me... wait... ummm..." He starts to count his fingers. "Oh yeah! Zero! You suck, Douglas, and Wednesday night, I'm gonna show everybody why when I take that International Title off of you. After all, I am the Millennium Man of the fWo, and I need some gold around my waist. Sure, I could beat the Frenchie, or Eron, but they gave me you, and I'll take what I'm given. So, Wednesday night, that title is as good as mine. Now, onto long-term plans. Primetime and Courage somehow won the Tag Titles from the Underground. But, the Underground have a return match this Wednesday. So, whoever wins, let's see if you have the balls to face myself and Black Panther at Trapped. What do ya say?" He drives off as the camera cuts to a promo for Trapped.
