Eye For An Eye [3] Exposure

27 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Two years later, nothing changes. Christmas day 1982... and the Kinkade family are gathered around the fire, well... all but one that is, guess who? Yep... shit for brains is out fucking whores again whilst my Mother tries her best to hold together what's left of our family. Only the old bastard his losing it now, he's not only fucking the prostitutes... he's taking them out to dinner as well. Pathetic isn't really the word... but hey, that's Daddy dearest for you... if only he knew that he was about to be fucked in more ways than one. December 25th 1982. "Oh god yeah!" Vince Kinkade's cries of ecstasy echoed through the cheapest motel room that money could buy, a little cash got you a long way in Short Hills, and Vince Kinkade had plenty... cheap cars and cheap women, the only thing burning a hole in his pocket was the cocaine. Vince rolled off of Louanne Page; a cheap hooker with whom he'd been collaborating his efforts once a week for the past two years... the fact that it was Christmas day and that his Wife and Children were waiting for him didn't matter anymore. "So Louanne, what'd you say we head out on the town tonight... I'm runnin' low on supplies." He grunted, as he took a razorblade and casually arranged a few lines of coke on the glass bedside table before him. "I think you'd better be fixin' to pay me up before you head out picking up those supplies Vince, you got debt... and you know Tommy don't like debt." Louanne shuddered at the very mention of his name, Tommy Sorelli wasn't just a pimp... he was one of the most violent men in the New Jersey area... sometimes she thought that the entire myth of the New Jersey Devil was based around someone that'd met Tommy in the wrong time and at the wrong place. "Bah, you think your little gangster friends concern me babe? I'm Vince Kinkade... I own New Jersey and everyone in it." He scowled for a moment, before rolling up a twenty-dollar bill and promptly snorting all three lines in front of him, after wiping his nose and squinting his eyes for a moment or two, he shook his head and continued. "So come on woman..." He began, reaching into his wallet and pulling out a few hundred dollars "... how much do I owe?" Louanne shifted nervously on the bed... sliding slowly away from Vince, she wasn't sure how he'd react... not after what she had to say. "Well? Come on woman... out with it!" Kinkade bellowed, throwing the cash at her. "It should be five hundred dollars... but..." Louanne began, a tear rolling down her cheek. "But what? You want a tip or something? Jesus." Vince scoffed. "... but Tommy wants more." She sobbed... squirming further away from Vince, who narrowed his eyes. "Oh... I see..." Vince spoke leaning forward "Fucker is getting greedy his he? How much more... one hundred, two?" Vince tossed another few hundred dollars onto the bed, but Louanne shook her head, taking a deep breath. "Ten K Vince, he wants Ten K." Vince scowled again, before busting out an insane laugh. "Yeah right, you've gotta be shitting me... we've been fucking Lou, last I checked your pussy wasn't made out of gold." He chortled... but soon his laughter ceased, because she wasn't laughing with him. "It's not that Vince... it's nothing like that, he says we can exploit you... he says that we can more money from you because you're famous, because you have more to lose if he tells anyone about what you and I have been doing... and he says that you'll have to pay up... now that... ... now that I'm pregnant." She sobbed again... making a desperate move and inching towards Vince, planting a kiss on his lips... but it was a one way kiss, he wasn't reacting whatsoever, because what she'd said had struck a chord inside him, a chord which chimed a destructive note... and a chord which last time played, had lost another man his eye. Kinkade quickly pulled away from the kiss, wiping his lips in horror as his eyes widened... Louanne lay on the bed extending her arms and trying to tempt him back, but it was wishful thinking at best. "YOU FUCKING SLUT!" Vince roared as he balled up his fist and drove it straight into her face... the impact was shocking as she sprawled backward and onto the table on the other side of the bed, knocking off a lamp and several empty champagne glasses along with a half empty bottle, all of which fell to the wooden floor with a hideous shatter. "Please Vince! The baby!" She cried... but he heard none of it, it wasn't a matter of emotion anymore... it was a matter of business, she was John Hanson... she was trying to steal his career away from him and ruin his life. He took off his belt, wrapping it around his hand and whipping across her back with the leather strap as she let out a scream. "Don't do this Vince... we can be together!" She screamed, as Vince threw down the belt. "Be together!? I love my wife you cheap slut! I love my kids... the only factor here is that you think you're pregnant... but we can fix that, we can fix it good." Vince was foaming at the mouth; the cocaine, alcohol and rage had sent him into frenzy. Louanne's eyes widened... as he yanked open the closet and pulled out a wire coat hanger, "No!" she screamed, "You can't kill my baby! HELP! PLEASE GOD HELP!" "Stay still bitch!" He snarled "The more you move the harder this is going to be." CRUNCH. Suddenly his eyes widened, Louanne had send a hard knee into his groin... he staggered across the room gasping for air, and never saw her scrambling across the bed to snatch the cracked champagne bottle from the floor. SMASH. The last thing Vince felt was the bottle breaking across his head. Then came the darkness... ... followed by the sirens. December 25th 1982. "Is Dad coming home this year Mom?" Kellen Kinkade asked politely, unwrapping his presents. "I don't know Kellen, you know how busy your Father gets... just remember, he only works so hard because he loves us and wants to give us a better life, now go help Toby and Dawn with their presents, there's a good boy." Kellen nodded and make his way back over to the tree where his younger Brother and Sister opened their gifts. Martha did her best not to cry, she knew all too well that Vince wouldn't be coming home again this Christmas... and she also suspected that he wasn't working either. She left the children to open their presents, and made her way into the kitchen... checking on the turkey to see if it was almost done, as she did... she switched on the radio. "-nd in other news... a local man was arrested today and charged with assaulting a pregnant young woman Louanne Page, who was also taken to hospital following the attack... Vince Kinkade, wrestler for a local promotion World Wrestling Champions, was initially charged with attempted murder... until the woman, who has also been arrested previously and found guilty of prostitution, dropped the charges against him. Mr. Kinkade has been released on bail and will be asked to appear in court on January, he was unavailable for comment." Martha Kinkade hung her head and wept, behind her... the Christmas turkey blackened and burned to a crisp giving off a thick black smoke that engulfed the room. "What did you do Vince, what did you do?" She sobbed, sitting at the table and crying into her hands. Christmas was ruined; again... and maybe just maybe, Vince Kinkade was ruined too.
