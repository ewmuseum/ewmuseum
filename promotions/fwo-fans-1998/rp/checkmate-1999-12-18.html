Checkmate

18 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Originally recorded: 12/15/99 [The scene begins after Warfare, Rain is walking backstage, Internet Title dangling loosely in one hand, and none of the strange men from the fWo past are following him. Rain is still in full ring uniform though his particapation tonight was not required. He dons his jet black sunglasses as he also sports a deathly straight face, showing no sort of care. Rain begins to head towards the exit of the building as a tired Geoff Green jogs after Rain, Geoff not having the best of nights as fWo World Champion Ice gave him quite a shock walks timidly towards the currently reigning Internet Title Holder.] Geoff Green: Hey Rain.... care to have a couple words in front of the camera ? Of course if you don't want to Ill just go over there..... and Ill leave and yeah.... it's alright, it's fine if you don't want to. [Rain stops his walking and wathces as Geoff pleadingly turns away.] Rain: Are you going to let me answer Geoff. Get your camera ready, I want to go outside, walk beside me and you may interview me. [Geoff instantly turns himself around and holds the camera up as Rain pushes the door open and waltzes out with Geoff Green close on his tail. Rain looking as if he was making an appearnace to the squared circle. Geoff trods up in front of Rain and points the camera in his face. Behind Rain are few parked cars and the dark moonlit sky of San Fansisco.] Geoff Green: Im glad you could give me the interview, I mean the people on the internet are going crazy with you, I mean what's going on ? Rain: Geoff you know from past experiences that I am not one to mind an interview unless my mind is clouded. Though now my mind is clear, and my goals are in reach, though tonight I made mistakes and it cost me a major setback that I dearly did not need. My choosing of Havok against Havok was not the best one as I should have forseen this outcome. Havok did overcome the odds though which he will be credited for, but I will not overlook a chance like this once again. The Cruiserweight title would only have struck another nail into the coffin I am sealing the fWo in. Geoff Green: What about the men after the match ? I mean Ripper's dead, we all saw him die last year, and those other men ? They are long gone. Another Havok whats' going on ? I mean what is up with that crystal around your neck ? You guard it more than you do the Internet Title. Rain: Geoff, some questions are not meant to be asked, and you just asked one. But Ill answer it nonetheless, you do make many mistakes Geoff, but I know life is as hard as the next. The crystal is not the source of my power, the God's of the Storm give me my power, and the rest is derived from my superior mortal strength. The origin of the crystal ? I believe you more than others Geoff will remember a distorted dream of the fWo, a very realistic dream that distorted reality, the history buffs of the fWo surly will remember. I also beleive this is where the crystal was formed, then it was placed around the neck of Violet, the manager of Ultra-Violet, and a sample of it's power was unleashed upon it's destruction. Which ultimated created another crystal, the crystal I control now. All derived form you Geoff. Then men I control are the pieces on my chess board, gradually I will move up the pawns, then the rooks and knights until.....checkmate. Geoff Green: This just doesn't make any sense ! Am in the twilight zone ? Rain Im saying this as nicely as possible... you are insane. [Rain stops in his tracks, and faces the now stopped Geoff Green who has just realized his mistake.] Rain: Insane ? That is when you have lost control over the real world, why, I am far from insane. I control universes. Watch yourself Geoff, I am a forgiving man but do not question that which you do not understand. [Geoff Green searches for a logical question and speaks.] Geoff Green: Sorry, what about your upcoming title defense against fWo Superstar Black Panther ? What about your effect on twilight ? What will it be ? [Rain glances down at his title and then looks back up at the camera.] Rain: This title is one less than the world Title. An important title to say the least. It gives me a high status in the fWo, making my takeover that much closer to reality. I have defended this title many times since my proclamation as champion, though I look at this as not just another title defense as this will be my toughest challenge since defeating the Crazy Trucker. Black Panther has carried this title twice before, and I will not give him the satisfaction of carrying it a thrid time. His Millenium men may have had all the spoils on this night, but on Wednesday, this will not be the case. The army that which you saw was nothing. Nothing compared to the future. Panther, I realize your morals as long ago before I became the man I am today, I was a man much to the same as you. Though I have been strought with the power of the Storm and am a new man, though I do see and respect honour, Wednesday will not be about honour. Twilight ? We will see. [Rain throws the title over his shoulder and begins walking away as the scene fades to black.]
