TGI Friday's. Sure, it wasn't the best their money could buy... but they'd live with it. Tyler and Conor entered the receptionist area, as they began to check out the restaurant from head to toe. Tyler would always glance at the roof first, and Conor would always check out the floors. They didn't really know why... it was just an idiosyncrasy that they normally did. "This sure isn't El Paso." Tyler laughed. "But I guess it can do." Conor's stomach growled at the comments made by his Brother. "No kidding." As the two continued to look about, two waitress were seen glancing over their shoulders at The Fuse Bros.. They didn't seem too thrilled, as one of them quickly walked off and the other one came towards them. "Hi." She spat out. The Fuse Bros. could tell they weren't even welcome. Yet, they had to eat... so they were going to. "How many?" "Two!" Tyler snapped happily. He never liked it when a "mushroom person", any mushroom person for that matter, was feeling a little down. "Smoking or non?" This time it was Conor's turn. "Non!" "Right this way..." The Fuse Bros. followed, as Margaret led them to their seats. However, as they walked past the tables, everyone began to look at the two oddballs, wearing brown and green overalls, with blue t-shirts underneath, and of course the brown and green hats. They would get a lot of looks since their outlook on life had changed along with their attire about one year ago. Everyone felt the vibe... the weird, strange, utterly different vibe. Some of the people laughed as they passed, while others just turned their heads. They didn't want to pity the Bros., let alone look at them. Yet, to Tyler and Conor, the looks didn't bother them. Actually, they took it as a different gesture. "The mushroom people are looking at us again, Tyler." "I know. Just act all happy... and they won't think anything is wrong." So Conor did. He used that weak cheesy smile Tyler conveyed at the beginning of Warfare. It wasn't much help. "Here are your seats." Margaret showed the both of them to their table as The Fuse Bros. sat down. Margaret then handed them menus. "Can I get you boys anything to drink?" She sighed, opening up her notebook to jot down what they wanted. "Yeah. I'll have a coke." "Me too. A coke." Margaret began to walk away, but that's when she was called back. "Oh, Miss. We're ready to order too." Taking in another deep breath, Margaret walked to the table. She looked at Tyler first. "What will it be?" "Can I have the all American burger please?" She began to write it down, as her unenthusiastic voice sounded again. "Yes you can..." She turned her head to Conor. "And you?" "The same." Margaret stormed off once more, only to be called back for another time in the night. "Oh, and Miss." Thus, she came. "Sorry to bother you once more but..." Tyler looked at her sternly, this time seeming uptight when he spoke. He seemed like he really meant what he said. "Can we have a lot of mushrooms on them?" She reluctantly nodded. Conor then spoke. "Yes. Please. Lots of mushrooms." Margaret sighed... heading off to the kitchen to submit their orders.
