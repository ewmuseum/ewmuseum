Untouchable // Part. 4 // Points of Authority

8 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Steve's head smashed hard against the wooden table. It was obvious that Lieutenant Hank Rogers wasn't a huge fan of Steve, considering the punch to the gut and kick to the back of the chin that Steve received from him earlier on. Steve's head slowly raised and a small puddle of blood formed on his bottom lip. Steve smiled of course, afterall, what else was he expected to do, "You know officer...you could have just asked me nicely to sit down." Rogers let go a short grunt and then turned to face Steve, "Yeah but I had to contain you after you tried to pull a punch on me." Bullshit Steve grinned, "Oh officer, don't tease me...you know that would be way to perfect. But considering how fucking tight my cuffs are I'll be lucky if I can still get in that jab that I was aiming for." Rogers grabbed Douglas by the back of his head and slammed his nose hard into the table. Steve's heard the snap and felt the blood pour from his nostrils. Rogers eyes grew fierce, "Still with the humor Steve? Keep it up and I'll be forced to take action against you." Steve blew out his nose hard spraying blood all over the table in front of him, "Action? You coming on to me already? Jesus...I thought you guys waited until lights out." "That's it!" Rogers winded up but his fist was stopped by his co-worker, Captain David Harrison. "Knock it off Hank, " David spoke sternly, he knew what Rogers had done, it was quite common at the headquarters. He didn't blame him, people like Douglas made him sick. Douglas was just another crazy fuck that was a waste of humanity, another pitiful pathetic piece of shit that he'd rather see die than breathe another breath of fresh air. David Harrison was not a violent man, he was just bitter. Harrison took a seat across from Steve, calmly taking a cloth and wiping blood from the portion of the table in front of him. Steve grinned showing his yellow teeth coated in his thin red liquid, "Sorry bout that...had no kleenex." Rogers fist tightened as he hung in the background, meanwhile Harrison just smiled, "Okay Steve...enough of the bullshit. Exactly what were you trying to pull back at the hospital?" Steve paused, "Trying to pull? Nothing. I was merely greeting a friend." Harrison shook his head, "Do you normally greet them with your hand grabbing their throat?" It was a love tap. "No officer," Steve continued, "Kenny's a special friend." Harrison shifted in his seat, "I'll be honest here Steve, your up for attempted murder...which is a big fucking deal. So let's cut to the chase, as of right now a Mr. Angel Dalton has posted bail for you. But your not to leave town, court dates will be soon." Steve smirked, "Leave town? Never...afterall, Mr. Big Shot," Steve gestured towards Rogers, "We've got a date." Rogers moved in, "You fucking-" A stern glare from Harrison made Rogers back off. Steve leaned back in his seat and sighed. This is not going to be fun.
