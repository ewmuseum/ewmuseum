Of exodus and redemption (part twelve, and final)

28 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A car horn sounded, and he looked up into the empty parking lot. The lights advertising Twilight were out now, and he found himself back in the present, his memories banished for the time being. The sound had come from many blocks away, and he breathed a deep sigh to himself. Idly he brushed the tears away from his cheeks without realizing he had done it. All of these memories... Is this what you really want then? The voice inside his head was weak, and he recognized it for what it was. The dying words of a companion that had traveled with him for many days and months. He had been alone... guilt ridden... it was supposed to be him that had died that night, not John. Not dear John... who had just wanted to be his friend and had just wanted to protect him. He had left the arena that night in darkness, and the darkness had lasted for years. His hair had grown, and he had darkened it. He traveled alone, and the words that were spoken about him were harsh. He was alone, he was all alone. They had begun to say that he was living his character... caught up in his own gimmick. He didn't care. When it had gotten too bad in the fWo he had left, but it was no better anywhere else. In the CWL he had run into Douglas, and all of the memories had come flooding back into him. All of the pain, all of the darkness... He had run for years, he had taken on the identity of his friend. He had thought he was letting him live on through him... but this too was a hollow dream. But I never blamed you... never. "I know that John, I know." Goodbye... friend. "Goodbye, John. Goodbye." Ryan Moore sat in the rental car, the cold and darkness around him. The voice inside of him fell silent for the final time, and Ryan closed his eyes against the intensity of the night. Ryan was alone. But in his own way, Ryan was together with his old friend at last.
