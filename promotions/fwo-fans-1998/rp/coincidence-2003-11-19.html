Coincidence

19 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Master Yodah watched intently, his chin resting on his duck-handled walking staff. "Tzu Ni, David, continue please." David Cross extended his right arm and stood straight. Tzu Ni, or "Wang" as David had taken to calling him, launched into a barrage of punches. His fists were almost a blur as he peppered David's chest, or tried to. Cross moved equally as fast, often using the same hand to parry multiple blows at once. He almost appeared to be showing off his superior skill, a fact not lost on Master Yodah. "Enough!" the elderly man barked. David stopped but continued to face Tzu Ni, fully expecting the young man to throw another crushing blow. His arm throbbed painfully. He might have made it look easy, but blocking those sledgehammer punches was more about saving himself from being pulverized that looking good. Of course, if he managed to piss Wang off, all the better. Tzu Ni stared at Cross with murder in his eyes as he bowed. David bowed back, trying to keep his own emotions in check. Wang had gone from antagonistic to outright threatening ever since he found out that David and Michelle were now more than "just friends." Cross counted himself lucky that he'd trained under the expert eye of Michelle's father. Without that level of skill under his belt, David might not have survived their numerous skirmishes over the past two days. The tension between the two men was not lost on Master Yodah. "Tzu Ni, thank you. That will be all." Both men turned to leave the alcove. "David, you will stay." Tzu Ni flinched. Yet another slap in the face. It was bad enough that the gaijin stole his woman. Now he was stealing his Master's favour. This American thief was going to be punished, one way or another. David stood beside the stone wall lined with flickering candles. "What can I do for you?" Master Yodah shuffled to where David was standing. He paused to pass his hand over the candle flames. "You are leaving us, yes?" David nodded. "I have to get back to work. But it was great to see the Temple, and it was an honour to train under your guidance." The wall of light swayed in the breeze. "You have done your Master Hiroshi a great service." David bowed again.
