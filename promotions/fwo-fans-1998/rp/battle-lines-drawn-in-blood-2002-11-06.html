Battle Lines Drawn in Blood

6 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wednesday was drawing to a close, as was the show that the fWo had just put on in front of the fans of San Jose. The doors to the arena had opened, and people were beginning to trickle out of them, signalling that the event had indeed come to end. In the parking lot, however, things were still in full swing. Eli Flair's impromptu fan protest/concert/drinkfest had managed to regain momentum after the altercation earlier in the evening between Flair and Renshaw. In the middle of what looked like a cross between a mosh pit and a tailgate party, Eli Flair and Poison Ivy stood watching the fans float by on their way to their cars. Some stopped briefly, and others stayed to join in the fun. It was then that one figure headed in their direction caught their attention... "Shit, Paul...." Flair said, getting a good look at the current state of the Boston Strangler. "Ivy! This man needs asprin and a band-aid! And beer! STAT." "...Thank you..." the Strangler managed to mumble as he leaned against a van and studied his face. It had been quite some time since he had felt the pain and tasted the blood that still trickled from the cut on his forehead... this was going to take some getting used to again. "And I thought I looked like shit..." Flair said through a bloodstained grin as Ivy handed some Advil and a beer to the Strangler. The Strangler quickly popped the tablets and swallowed some beer, making a face as he did so. He glanced over at Flair for a moment and almost chuckled. "Well... you do..." "Here..." Ivy said, pressing some gauze and band-aids into the Strangler's hand. "If you need some ice or anything... there's some in the coolers. Speaking of looking like shit... Eli... how about a trip to get that glass pulled out of your face?" "In a bit," Flair said with a wave of his hand, "Angel made me swear to a trip after the crowd goes." Ivy looked like she wanted to argue over how long that would take, but she knew better. "Paul?" "We'll be fine. Thanks..." the Strangler said quietly. "We just need to sit down for a bit..." That having been said, he wandered away from the crowd and settled down at the edge of the gathering. There the Strangler sat, pressing the gauze against his head as he thought through the events of the evening. The deception had gone over well, luring Spencer into the ring was effortless at best. The ego of the man was so great, it was predictable that he would want to be able to gloat... the hurried script they prepared for him to read was riddled with shots at the former management. Of course the Strangler had decided to ad-lib at that point... "That was too easy" he thought aloud. "Jon and Dudley gave in too quickly..." Then again, he did have Spencer in a tough situation at the time. They had little choice but to agree to his terms, right? "Those bastards wouldn't save each other unless there was something to gain by it..." the Strangler said to himself, folding the gauze over to put better pressure on the cut. The beating in the ring was just the beginning, he knew that. They would come up with more ways to try to humiliate him. But he had to persevere... this was his only way to accomplish what he had set out to do. "They're sure to have something in store for us, so we're going to have to be prepared for what comes next. The easy part is over..." "...Now our war begins."
