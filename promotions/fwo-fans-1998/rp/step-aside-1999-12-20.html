Step Aside.

20 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(A quality hotel in San Jose, California, is the scene. Located downstairs in this hotel, is a restaurant, used only by the people who are currently staying in the building. A flock of men dressed in suits, and women wearing formal dresses flood the area, though there seems to be one man wearing unique attire. Courage. A sharp beam of light reflects off the metallic silver shirt of Courage, and attacks the lens of the camera, causing Geoff Green to rapidly move his face away from the recording view. Courage grins arrogantly, while he rests his feet on the table, and sits in a relaxed, casual and slouched position. Green eventually begins the filming.) Havok, we've been over this. I! am the greatest cruiserweight in the fWo! I! am the greatest athlete in the fWo! You! suck. Havok, don't avoid me, because even the millions of idiot American fans, remember that at Mob Rules, it was Courage and NXS vs Cactus Jon and his jobbers. NXS was out of there faster than, Heftel against Primetime. So it was Courage, and Courage only! The savior of the fWo! ..having to carry the idiotic USA team.. and Courage did! Some Big Ben idiot, some Phantom moron, they simply couldn't outwrestle the skill of Courage, and they were defeated. Leaving Havok and Cactus Jon against Courage, many men would have run, many men would have hid.. though not this man, not Courage! Courage was tired, Courage was sore, but Courage is the greatest cruiserweight in the fWo, and therefore Havok, you were defeated also. You were pinned by Courage, even though he was worn out. You lost, you idiot, and it was proven that Courage is better than you! Havok, when you won that title, you pinned Micky 'Moron' Maliboo. You didn't pin Courage, because you can't, Courage is too good for you. In fact Courage had just performed the greatest move in the fWo, Courage Required. The only move that equals buy rates! The only move that equals rear ends in the seats! ..and baby! The only move the equals rock 'n roll! Yeah! ..So when the time comes Havok, Courage is going to do what he has done so many times, and that is kick your Y2Jobber physique all over ring-side and claim, what is rightfully his, the cruiserweight title. C'mon baby! (A waiter enters the scene, and offers Courage a beverage. Courage accepts, and almost on cue, the waiter extends his hand, in question for a tip. "Tip? The fact that you are in the same one mile radius, is enough tip for you, you idiot!" The waiter shrugs, and walks away, as Courage returns to the same position.) Jake, the only reason people know who you are, is not because you hold the Mc Donalds Big Mac record, and it's not because you've got the fattest ass in America, it's because you had the absolute pleasure of walking to ring-side with Courage! You were in the same ten-meter radius of Courage, and therefore you're a star! Well, Jake, you fat bitch, you are also the reason Courage isn't main event material! It's obvious that Courage has the skill and talent to defeat jobbers like Ice, but because Courage had you wobbling beside him, Courage was degraded. You will pay fat boy, this Wednesday, Courage and the true fWo champion, Primetime, will walk to ring-side and take Austin the Idiot's fat head, and stick it right up your chubby ass. Austin who? Who is this idiot? Why is he even being allowed to be in the same ring as Courage? It doesn't matter, because come Wednesday, Jake and his boyfriend will learn that Courage and Primetime equal rock 'n roll! ..And let's not forget the Frenchie! Although Primetime was robbed of the title, it matters not, for the Frenchie will regain the title for the Foundation this Friday. Whether it be Slugger, and his stealing of Courage's catch phrases, whether it be Slugger's Big Blonde Boyfriend, it doesn't matter.. because the Frenchie, is going to rock their world! C'mon baby! (Courage finishes with his ever-so arrogant grin, before pushing the the camera lens into the face of Green. Courage chuckles, as the scene fades to a Warfare promo.)
