Indiana Speck and the Temple of Fire: Sponsored by the Cameo Telethon

6 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The room that stretched out before the Codemaster, Mike Heftel, Ken War, and Link was filled with rotating flamethrowers, a maze where walls of flames erupt when one draws near, and more of the afermentioned physics defying boulders. In the very back of the room, the one true exit of the room, a wall of flames blocked the way, and the only thing that can stop them is a switch that had to be stepped on. Not to mention that it was a switch that could reset itself in about ten seconds. In the center of the room was a large pillar that went down to the lava room where the pillar would be needed in order to leap to the ledge where Volvagia, the evil Goron-eating lava dragon, resided. Of course, the only thing that could remove the pillar from its rest would be a good solid smack from the Megaton Hammer, which our heroes did not possess. "Well, shit." was all Link could say, when they entered the room. They walked into the room. They proceeded to walk toward one of the fire walls, which suddenly shot up out of the ground(without so much of a crack to come out of). It actually looked really flat and flimsy and didn't seem like a wall of fire, just a crappy two-dimensional polygon. Ken War walked into it and died instantly. Not from the flames, but from the fact that he ran into it at high velocity(five miles per hour). It truly was hardkorr. "Man, how the HELL does this block our progress?" the Codemaster asked, as he approached the fire wall. "It just does, man." Link said, before he turned around and shot a Fire Keese that came into the room with his bow and arrow. He did this twice more, before he got bored, lit a bomb, stabbed it with his arrow, then shot it at another Fire Keese. As soon as impact was made, it exploded, taking a lot of other Fire Keese with it. The Codemaster could only glare at Link. "Man, was it YOUR idea to bring this honkey along?" the Codemaster asked of Mike Heftel. "Shrug." Heftel said. Yes, he said shrug. But then, suddenly, out of nowhere, Lara Croft swung down from a rope, kicked the fire wall down, revealing that it was really a piece of cardboard that a bunch of Keebler elves were enslaved into raising, and jumped down on the switch. "Hey. She's going after the Megaton Hammer." Heftel said. "det esnt hardkorr!!11" Ken War shouted, and elected to go after Lara Croft. Of course, this might have been a great strategy had the wall of actual flames that the switch was responsible for hadn't blasted upwards and caused Ken War to burst into flames and die. "Man, how many times has that guy died now?" Codemaster wondered outloud. Well, it didn't matter since he quickly ran over to the switch, hit it, and ran into the next room. Link and Heftel and even Ken War followed him soon thereafter. And that's when the white-haired half-demon man known as Dante appeared. "Hi. I'm Dante. Welcome to my inferno." he greeted. "Doesn't this story have enough random video game characters? Especially since Captain Violence over here has an itchy quiver finger and we just met Lara Croft?" Codemaster asked. "No, I'm just here for the lousy Dante's Inferno pun. I'm actually supposed to be at an AA meeting right now." "You're an alcoholic?" "Oh, no. I'm just going to laugh at all of the alcoholics. Especially Mother Brain. Byebye!" he said, before he quickly got onto his motorcycle and left. He was replaced by a big black turd-like ball that erupted into flames and started dancing. Mike Heftel glared at it. "You suck." he told it. "WHAT?" it boomed. "Seriously, you just suck at dancing." "MY MOVES ARE TIMELESS." "So's your face." Codemaster said, crossing his arms. "HOW DARE YOU DEFILE MY GOOD NAME. I AM THE FLARE DANCER, MASTER OF THE FIRE TEMPLE. SACRED GUARDIAN OF THE MEGATON HAMMER. I HAVE BEEN TASKED WITH THE DUTY TO PROTECT THE HAMMER, SET THINGS ON FIRE, AND SCREAM AT PEOPLE IN ALL CAPITAL LETTERS JUST SO I CAN SOUND MORE IMPORTANT THAN I TRULY AM." the Flare Dancer said. Ken War pulled out his flaming barbed wire 2x4 and charged at the Flare Dancer before he began smacking the Flare Dancer with it, all while screaming "hardkorr!111". "IT'S A FLAMING 2X4, YOU IDIOT. WHAT DO YOU THINK THAT'LL DO TO ME?" the Flare Dancer asked of Ken War. Then the Flare Dancer horribly killed Ken War. "I'm just saying," Heftel said, completely ignoring the fact that Ken War had just been horribly killed, "La Parka could take you." "BRING HIM ON." And then La Parka randomly appeared. "*Shimmy*" "WHAT? THIS CAN'T BE! NOOOOO!" And then the Flare Dancer was defeated. "*Shimmy*" La Parka shimmied, before he, too, left as randomly as he had appeared. Link turned to Heftel, disappointed that he didn't horribly kill anything in the thirty second span in which he wasn't horribly killing something, "Hey, what the fuck was that all about?" Heftel shrugged, "What? What did I do this time?" Codemaster shrugged and jumped onto the elevator that was once occupied by a flaming pillar of flames. Heftel jumped up there, followed up by Link. The elevator rose to the top, but Ken War remained there and actually stepped up to where the elevator once was. "hay, were ded dey gu??//" SPLATTER. If you have to ask why Ken War just got splattered, you missed the part where the elevator came back down and splattered him.
