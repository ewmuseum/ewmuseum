Lost Time - Cycle Four

30 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Ivy... this is really good." Poison Ivy can't cook. She's never been able to cook, and she's never hidden this fact from anyone. However, her 'make- up' meal for Triple X and herself is actually pretty good. Hopefully Trip won't question the phone calls to Tempest's hotel room for step- by- step advice on how to make spaghetti. "Thanks. Told ya I could cook." She doesn't plan to tell him. While dinner finished its final touches, Ivy took a quick shower to rid herself of the sweat and nervousness that had overtaken her mind. Triple X waited patiently outside the bathroom with the door cracked - as she asked him questions about his relationship with Linda Johns and everything that the two went through. Trip stayed outside the door. It shouldn't have mattered - he's seen Ivy in the shower before... and then some. But out of respect for the situation they're currently in, he decided not to push his luck. "Sounds like you and Linda were a pretty hot item," said Ivy, soaking everything in, "and she even quit her job with Superior to be with you." "We were," lamented Trip, "but I couldn't stop thinking about you. I kissed her, I pretended she was you. I looked at Hornet in the CSWA with you and I wanted to go down there and take his ass out." Ivy looks down, apparently holding in her memories of Hornet. "I'll be honest with you, Sean... the entire time I was with Hornet this most recent stretch, my mind kept racing back to the kiss you and I shared in Superior as part of the Perfect Circle." "We're good at thinking the same thing," replied Trip, with a sardonic smile, "and acting in the exact opposite fashion." His face suddenly becomes serious. "Ivy," he said, "I'm sorry I hurt you the other night. If it means anything, when Linda kissed me I knew without question that it was you I wanted to be with, not her." He looked up at her, to see she had stopped eating, and was holding a hand to her stomach as if she felt sick. "Ivy?" "Sean..." she began, looking unsure of what direction to take, "I'm willing to believe your explanation for what I saw the other day in here with her... but I hope you understand I needed to sort some things out." He does understand. "What did you figure out?" "That I care about this relationship too much to let a few bumps in the road kill it. That we've overcome some hurdles far more damaging than a crazy woman in a trenchcoat forcing herself on you." "Ivy--" "That I..." She chokes on the words. Trip gets up and walks to her, concerned. "That I---" she finally gets out, again, "... love you." He sinks to his knees in front of her, amazed at her words. All the problems they've encountered. All the hurdles they've had to cross. All the other men and women that have tried their best to derail the two. All of it.... at this moment.... seems worth it.
