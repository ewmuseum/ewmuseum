Of exodus and redemption (part nine)

28 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Don't do this... please. You don't need to." Fallen looked at Joy with wide eyes. It was nearly the new year... only two more shows to go and the fWo could close another year. It had been a rough year... and Fallen was looking forward to it being over. But this show tonight... it wasn't a good idea. He knew it, but Joy was insistent. "Come on John... I've got to do this. You know that... it's for the good of the company. Hell, it's good for me! I don't like to say I'm in the business for myself, but this one bump tonight can mean quite a nice start for me... I'm finally going to get into the upper card level, you know? No more comedy stuff... or at least less of it." Fallen shook his head. "It's too dangerous... don't you hear me? Speed... William... he's always been clumsy and never has been a safe worker. You talk to Scotty... Scotty was out for two months because of him." Joy nodded, and finished taping up his wrists. "I know... and with my already injured neck it is dangerous... but John, what choice do I have? If I say no to this tonight they aren't going to look at me seriously. Not ever if I say no. You... you've got the whole gimmick and the future for you but I'm still trying to get going in this business. Besides... you're going to be out there to make sure that nothing bad is going to happen." Fallen sighed in frustration and pounded his fist into the table. Then he turned, and smiled. "Of course." Joy's eyes widened and he waited as Fallen got off the bench and smiled broadly. Fallen had an idea... Joy knew this look well. "It doesn't have to go down this way... with your neck injured I don't want you out there taking bumps. Not from a good worker, let alone Speed Demon, understand? Look, I can't as your friend send you out there to do this. I couldn't live with myself if something happened to you, and luckily there's another way. When we teamed together months ago we had done this... so it isn't going to be tough now." Joy was confused, but Fallen's smile was infectious. He beamed back and shrugged. "I guess I don't get it... what are you proposing?" Fallen was rummaging around in a duffle bag he had brought to the arena with him and withdrew a pair of scissors. He turned back to Joy and tossed the scissors to him. "I'm proposing that you let me help you tonight... you're in no shape to take that hit with your neck. I on the other hand, am just fine and will be able to walk away from it... without a problem."
