The Xenosaga: X

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It wasn't the fact that they'd fallen off of a stupid cliff that pissed them off. Hell, they didn't even mind that. They were the 'Whackers, dammit, and nothing penetrates their armor~! Nor was it the fact that they had been chasing the Codemaster, aka the Blackest Brother in the Hyrule Kingdom, all around the planet Earth. After all, they had the stamina of a latin bull, babeh~! And that's a mighty amount of stamina. So what were they pissed off about? Well, quite frankly, they were tired of the Codemaster bragging to his homies in the Hyrule Kingdom about how he fooled the Tallywhackers. So they were gonna show him... *smacks chest* How they roll in Compton, biotch. Thusly, they had chased him all the way to America. "Sumbeeyitch!" Cagle yelped as he stubbed his toe after chasing the Codemaster across a bed of a hot coal. What? Why did he hurt himself stubbing his toe, rather than running across hot coal? CUZ I'M VINCE MCMAHON, DAMMIT! Regardless, Cagle had finally lost track of him, but Dingleberry was hot on the Codemaster's tail. It was a sprinting chase down the road only known as 'Main Street', tipping over trashcans and such whilst they ran in the following order: 'Codemaster' Allen Speck, Dingleberry, David Flair, and... Wait, David Flair? Oh, no, that's just James. Silly James. "Hehehe." Yes, people were staring, blabbermouthed and dumbfouded to the point of sheer, unadultured stupor only seen by the likes of special ed. teachers, in which they shrieked like horror the words "OH EM GEE!" until finally, some since was made out of all of this... Via New Jack jumping off of a building and clocking the silly piss out of Codemaster with a guitar. The guitar quickly turned to powder and chalk over Codemaster's head, who dropped to the ground quicker than a two-ton sack of shit. "Ho-lee Sheeyit! Dag gum! New Jack done gawn awf and smacked ol' Codemaster with the geetar!" James said, in awe. "I_AM_NEWJACK~! AND_THAT'S_HOW_WE_GANGBANGIN_UP_IN_HERE!" "Ahem," Dingleberry proceeded. "Well alroyt Mistuh Jack, Oy'd loyk to thank you." "BEE YI BEE YI! FUCK THA PO-LICE!" "Ameeyin to that!" Cagle said in response to New Jack's terrible microphone skills. Then they shared a laugh, as Codemaster almost regained conciousness. "Royt...leet's geet 'im to Slaughter~!"
