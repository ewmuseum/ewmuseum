Mega Man Battle Network: Intermission

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Codemaster was back at Dr. Light's lab, which Dr. Light insisted that he call a crib, but when the Codemaster pointed the Fecalator in his direction upon the suggestion, he shut up rather quickly. Then he wondered why the Codemaster wasn't going through with fighting the other four robot masters, yet. The Codemaster explained to him that the weapons he just acquired would be completely useless against the next robot master, who just happened to be Jesus Man. Dr. Light didn't know what he was talking about, but Dr. Light was also drinking vodka, and when the Codemaster made the realization of this, he entered the next room, locked the door, and made sure he could escape from the window before he continued. Then he dialed a number. Who's number, you ask? His. "Who the hell is this, and how the hell did you get my number?" Voran Xias asked. "Hey hey, brother, settle down. This is just the Codemaster." the Codemaster said, calmly. "The Whatmaster?" Xias asked, completely confused. "The CODEmaster." the Codemaster said, getting the feeling that he wasn't going to like the direction in which this conversation was going. "What do you want?" Xias asked with a bit of contempt. Which means that it has more contempt than what Voran Xias usually put into his statements. "Well, you see... I was hoping that I could borrow your Atheism Ray so I could beat Jesus Man. I could trade it for this 'Iron Spatula' that I collected." the Codemaster said. There was an uncomfortable pause. "I have no idea what you're talking about," Xias said, "I'd spend some time discussing the fact that there is no Jesus Man, but I seriously don't care about you, and I personally hope you die from cancer. Or AIDS. Bye, moron." Click. The Codemaster sighed and put the phone back. "Man, I feel unclean after talking to that joker. I think a shower is needed." So he showered. Afterwards, he went to fight Jesus Man. Then he came back, because he forgot to put his clothes on.
