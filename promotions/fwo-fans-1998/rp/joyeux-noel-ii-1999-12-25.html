Joyeux Noel II

25 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens in a luxurious hotel room, in the World Famous Drake Hotel in Chicago, Illinois. Dressed in casual street clothes and his mask, TFF's seated Indian-style, his torso angled towards the ground. He's staring intently at the fWo World Title, which he won just last night, at Twilight.) What a wonderful Christmas. As always gifts were exchanged. Brian restocked my wine cellar back home wit' a case of Dom Perignon. Chris got me zat Mazaratti I've had my eyes on. But ze greatest gift of all was not given to me by my partners and friends in ze French Foundation; rat'er, it was given to me by one of my most hated foes, and was delivered to me a day early. Yes, I speak of ze inept one himself, Scott Slugger, and his gift of ze World Championship. He did everyt'ing in his power, and none of it was even close to good enough to prevent me from fulfilling my destiny. I, Pierre Delacroix, am ze Champion of ze World, following in ze footsteps of Primetime and...well, myself. Ze ot'ers, zey were merely warm bodies, designed to hold ze title while we in ze Foundation were ot'erwise occupied. And while it was quite painful to lose my coveted Womens Championship, which I'd held wit' pride for t'ree mont's, I truly feel zat ze World Title will adequately compensate for ze loss of zat rejuvenated belt. (Frenchie breaks into a wide grin, then picks up the World Belt, cradling it in his arms.) Nobody, not Slugger, not Ice, not Universal, not even ze much bally-hooed Strangler could prevent me from taking zis belt. None of zem could steal my spotlight last night. But one man had more audacity zan ze ot'er. I do, of course, speak of ze British Deat', David Spencer. After ze single greatest accomplishment in my storied career, you took it upon yourself to try to mooch off of my glory. Do you have designs on zis belt, ma soeur? Because if you do, let me remind you of a few t'ings. First, you and your Riders may concern ot'ers in zis organization, but you hold no advantage over ze Foundation. We are a cohesive unit, and not just a gat'ering of petty talent under a megalomaniac. Second, ze only ot'er time we fought, two days after you lost your last World Title to Dudley, I pinned you. Oui, I'd been on quite a roll at ze time, but I had not hit ze heights zat I am at now. And I'm still not at my peak, putain. And t'ird, and most importantly, you are, in fact, British. I, on ze ot'er hand, represent ze proud and magnificent nation of France. My people are so superior to yours, zat I can see where any anger you have towards me stems from. But it won't be enough. You want a shot? I'll give it to you. And you can do your worst, Brittney, but I promise you, it won't be enough, not by a longshot. Ze Frenchie is now ze official standard in ze fWo. I am king of ze mountain. T'ink you can knock me off? T'ink again. (Frenchie smirks, then kisses the Title, as the scene fades to black.)
