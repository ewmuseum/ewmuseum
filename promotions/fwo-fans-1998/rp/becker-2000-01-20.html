fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Becker January 20, 2000 **Cameras film a well tended, if not overly lavish, kitchen. Seated at the kitchen table is Ultra-Violet's father, Infra-Red. As always, the older, now retired wrestler seems content to dress him in cherry red wrestling tights and to keep his hair dyed in a similar color. A far-off look fills good ol' IR's face as he stares directly into the camera. His eyes are unblinking and his expression unchanged. As he gazes into nothingness, the lovely Violet runs into the kitchen.** Oh "dad" I just don't know what to! What have I done? I just wanted Clarence to take notice of me, and now Kid Cool thinks I'm his girl. He's a nice fellow, but he just doesn't have the same boyish charm as your son. I think Clarence might be mad. I want him back, but I want to keep his interest in me too! This was all Nightshade's plan. I knew I should never listen to her advice but I was desperate! What should I do dad? Should I just wait a little longer and see what happens? That might be a good idea. My nerves are all affray, but I think I could wait just a little longer. Yeah, that's what I'll do. Oh thanks "dad" you're a life saver! **Infra-Red continues to focus his undivided attention on the camera, ignoring all of Violet's ramblings and the hug she gives him. As Violet steps out a screen door, Nightshade slips into view from the shadows. She glares at Infra-Red.** You'll not interfere in this old man. My sister came to me with a problem. I've solved it. I care little for the pain it causes her or for the pain it causes you and your son. Such pain is miniscule to the tempestuous torment that troubles my tarnished, tortured soul. I will hear no objections you will stay out of this! **Infra-Red's silence is mistaken by Nightshade as consent; she disappears from whence she came. IR's "1000 mile stare" makes it clear to the viewing audience that he paid the young vixen no mind, neither seeing, nor hearing her. At this point a rope drops from the kitchen's vaulted ceiling. A mysterious man swiftly lowers himself onto the floor below, moving with the silence and grace of a feline on the prowl. He glances about him and turns to IR, speaking in a hushed frenzy.** Tell me, where did the women go? You know the ones Violet and her sisters? They've been here, and recently. I must know where they are. **This strange stalker waits a moment for a reply, frowning when he receives none.** They've sworn you to secrecy then? I feared as much. No matter. I'll use other methods to obtain what I need. Those women will be mine! **Infra-Red actually rolls his eyes for a moment, but then quickly reasserts his focus on the camera filming him. As the stalker stalks off, the voice of Ultra-Violet's mother, Tina, calls from the living room.** Floyd honey, come quick! I just chipped a press-on nail! **Infra-Red is unruffled by his wife's protests. Her further utterances are drowned out by a crash from the door. Ultra-Violet then walks in carrying a screen doors that's been somehow pulled off its hinges. UV's eyes grow wide.** I DIDN'T DO IT! **He discards the screen door and brings himself back to a "rational" state.** Well Pop, if you don't care I don't care. Say I need your advice. Remember how you told me you once feuded with that Larry fellow over mom when you guys were young? How did you settle that? Oh yeah, now I remember! You ah challenged him to a fight! That's it! The winner got to keep mom! I think I'll do the same; if that no good big meanie Kid Cool thinks he's an all-star, he can put the nice lady on the line for a match at the end of the month. 1 on 1, Q people in the ring winner takes the nice lady. Hey Kid you a man? **Ultra Violet pauses.** Hey neato! That actually sounded kinda tough and mean and stuff! Swell! **UV skips out the door frame. Infra-Red sighs, finally speaking to the camera.** Too many people know where I live.
