Words of wisdom.

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"What did you think you were doing in the ring this week Brandon?" Tobo looked quizzically at his young apprentice. He tried to gleam the answers from Brand Frontier's steel blue eyes, but they were dropped to the floor almost in embarrassment it seemed. The question would have seemed vague to most but not to the young wrestler. He and Tobo Yamato had known each for more years than not, twenty almost. Tobo had always been stern with Brandon. He knew that despite Brandon's passion and willingness to learn he lacked the tempered attitude that would prove successful in his chosen field of battle. The older Japanese man had taken it upon himself to guide him that is known as Brandon Roche into he who would be called The Paragon. It has not been and probably will not be an easy task. He looked though at the mountain of man, dwarfed by the 6'6" frame, and saw he had been as close to getting the job done as he had ever been. There had only been one other, years ago that had shown this much energy and this much determination. That time failed. This time Tobo knew he would not. It was as much for his own talents as that of what he was working with. This time the raw material was better to begin with. "Well?" The aging sensei used the tip of his metal cane to push on the chin of Brand Frontier. Brandon pushed it away a bit as he looked at Tobo for a second. He seemed perturbed by the treatment at his sensei's hands. Maybe he felt ashamed to be treated this way given his age and all that he accomplished or maybe he was just tired of feeling like everyone had the answers expect him. "I screwed up. I don't really know what else to say Master Yamato. It's that simple." A shrug of the shoulders didn't play the events off like he had wanted them to and the events from Monday night's Unleashed show still hung heavy in the air. Tobo was angry for more than one reason; he was concerned for more than one reason. He was saddened for more that one reason. "Screwed up as you say Brandon would be what I would call a grand understatement. You had Victor Creed in the ring with you and I could tell you were thinking as much about that title shot as you were about getting to him. You took way too long in that ring. You didn't mount any sort of offense. Everything you did was in retaliation or to counter something Mr. Creed was allowed to do... something you allowed him the time to do!" The words stung Brandon Roche's ears as if he had been slapped on them. Tobo had been reaching the short end of his fuse over the last few days.
