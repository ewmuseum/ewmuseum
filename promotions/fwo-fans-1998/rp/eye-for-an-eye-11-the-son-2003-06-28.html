Eye For An Eye [11] The Son

28 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
They say that the sins of the father are visited upon the son. In nineteen ninety three I found out that after almost twenty years the saying still held firm ground. In nineteen seventy five my father took John Hanson's eye and ruined his life. In nineteen ninety three John Hanson took my eye and ruined mine. August 28th 1993. "Well Mrs. Kinkade, we've run some tests on the substance that Kellen was hit in the eye with and the results that came back suggest that it was more than likely chemical lye." The doctor sat before a sobbing Martha Kinkade and flicked through documents attached to a clipboard. "Please... you have to explain this to me in a way that I can explain back to Kellen, I don't understand what any of this means... will be he blinded?" Martha asked as she wipes the tears from her eyes. "I'm afraid blinded isn't the worst of it, you see when the lye made contact with his eye it started to react with the moisture and burn... if perhaps we'd got him here in time and found something to neutralize the burn then partial blindness may have been the worst of it. As it stands the additional water that was used to try and clear away the substance only provoked a more violent reaction... as result very little of Kellen's eye was left by the time he reached us, so we had to remove all of it." Martha's tears continued to flow "You mean he has n-" "No eye in the left socket, unfortunately he'll have to use a prosthetic replacement or some form of patch for the rest of his life." The doctor added as a few moments of silence passed "If you like I can explain this to him... there are also various routes of counselling that we can ex-" "No." Martha cut in "I'll tell him." "If you're sure... you can go and see him right now, he woke up around a half an hour ago but you'll have to bare with him because the pain killers will have him a little confused." Martha didn't say anymore, she slowly approached the burns ward that Kinkade was on... pushing through the doors and making her way over to his bed. He was sitting upright with half of his head completely bandaged up. "Mom!" Kinkade exclaimed leaning forward "What's going on? They won't tell me anything." "Shh... sit back and rest." She replied, pushing him slowly back into the pillows behind him "Everything is going to be just fine." "Am I going to be able to see? Did they tell you anything else?" Kinkade asked adamantly. "No, just that you need to rest and get back your strength." Martha answered back as Kinkade laid his head back on the pillow. "Has dad been here yet?" Kinkade struggled to ask as the painkillers kicked in further and he slowly started to slip into unconsciousness. "No Kellen... but he will soon." As Martha spoke her last words Kellen didn't answer, but it wasn't because he was incapable of doing so. He'd just realized that his father wasn't the only member of the family that could pervert the truth; he knew deep down inside that there was something he needed to know.
