Scars of the Past: Part Two

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
August 19th, 1998 Kings of Wrestling was holding a Televised show in Macon, Georgia, which had Yojin Musahiri taking on the KoW Hardcore Champion, Mr. Diamond in a Barbed Wire Death Match. The match was not a scientific contest, as you couldn't utilize the ropes, since they were made out of Barbed Wire. However, more than half way through the contest, Mr. Diamond took out wire cutters, and began to cut the Barbed Wire down after he hit Yojin with a sloppy looking Ace Crusher. "Oh my god, what the hell is Diamond doing?!" screamed commentator James Carlton, as Diamond grabbed the barbed wire carefully, and wrapped it around Yojin's legs, and Diamond was tossed a chair by his tag partner at the time, Mark Phelps. Diamond placed the chair over the barbed wire laden legs of Yojin, and ran to near the barbed wire ropes. Diamond then executed a sprint dash, and jumped into the air, and went for a Senton Bomb. And that's where he botched up. Diamond landed on his neck and head onto the steel chair, knocking him out cold. However, the impact of the head hitting the chair caused the Barbed Wire to tear Horito's tights, and impale itself into his skin.
