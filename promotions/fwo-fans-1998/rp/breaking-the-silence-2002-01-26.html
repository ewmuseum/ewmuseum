Breaking The Silence

26 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It had been nearly four weeks since the accident had occurred that changed Monte Burns' life forever. For the next couple of weeks following the injury to his brother Nathan, Monte had been in a bit of a funk. He wrestled at the first Slaughter of the year and lost to Black Quicksilver. His involvement with Platinum Gold, Inc. was so little that board members sent out one of the secretaries to look for him and make sure he was alright. The fWo's officials fined him for arriving late at the shows in France and Japan. Even his best friend Lance Fortune, the man who Burns confided in about everything, had only spoken with him on a few fleeting occasions. It seemed that the injury to Nathan had taken a great toll on Monte, something no one would have figured even a few weeks prior. Then, something clicked inside of his head again. The competitive drive that had made him a two-time fWo Cruiserweight Champion and one of the fWo's fastest rising stars suddenly returned. Burns looked at the current Cruiserweight Champion Eddy Fate and knew that he could beat him and regain the title. Great matches and title belts had helped define Monte's career, not worrying about his brother's condition. As much as he had found a way to make something of a connection to Nathan again, Monte wasn't about to let that get in the way of becoming the greatest wrestler in the world, a status that Monte knew he could attain. This evening, seated at a table in a German restaurant, Burns was about to go through an interview for fWoWrestling.com. While he usually took the interviews in stride, lately Monte seemed uneasy about talking with members of the press. Mostly it had to deal with non-wrestling reporters who had taken to harrassing many CEO's like Burns in regards to the Enron fiasco. Perhaps with the interview being done with an fWo reporter things would go more smoothly. As Burns took a drink from his glass of champagne, the fWo's own Kristen Jacobs comes walking towards the table. "Sorry that I'm a little late," said Jacobs as she took a seat across from Burns. "I had some problems with the shower. These European hotels are a little small." "You think they're small?" asked Burns with a raised eyebrow. "How do you think I feel coming from the kind of life I live?" Jacobs simply smiles at the question as she gets some of her papers in order. Burns grabs the glass that sits in front of her and fills it with champagne, before refilling his own glass. With her pen in hand, Jacobs looks up and sighs. "Ready to begin?" she asks. "Yes, let's get this completed," says Burns as he takes a drink. "Monte, this Sunday night at Slaughter, you'll be facing Eddy Fate for the fWo Cruiserweight Title," Jacobs begins, "how do you see the match turning out?" "How do I see the match turning out?" asks Burns rhetorically. "Well, obviously, I see myself beating Eddy Fate in the center of the ring and reclaiming the fWo Cruiserweight Championship that was stolen from me back in October. The only reason I lost was because Donaven Winters interfered." "Do you think you'll have to worry about any interference in this match?" asked Kristen. "I'm not really sure about that," he said with a shrug. "Fate has some enemies, but he also has some friends. My past with Winters is well-documented, but he seems occupied with other things right now. I think it'll be a clean match." As Jacobs jotted down some things on her notepad, Burns took another sip from his glass of champagne. He took notice that Jacobs had not touched her glass, although she seemed more interested in the interview. "What are your thoughts on Tempest, Eddy Fate's sister and the person you beat to earn this title shot?" asked Kristen as she looked up from the notepad. Strangely, Burns seemed to take some time to ponder the question, rubbing his finger across his chin while doing so. "With all the things that have happened recently, it's tough to get a beat on Tempest," said Monte with a gaze into the distance. "She comes across as this really easy going, fun loving young woman. But then you learn these things about her past, and you wonder about her. Is she stable? How has she been affected by all of this? I have to admit, she's a great athlete, but I'm not quite sure she belongs in the ring opposite someone like myself, if that's what you're asking." "In light of what happened recently with your brother," began Jacobs, "what can..." Jacobs' question is quickly cut off as Burns sits up and leans forward with his hand up in between them. "I'm not going to comment on anything regarding my family or personal matters," said Monte sternly. "But, Monte, you..." Suddenly, Burns slams his fist down on the table, almost tipping over one of the glasses. Jacobs and the rest of the people in the restaurant jump slightly with a startle at the thud made by Monte's fist. Burns slowly stands up from his chair and walks over next to Jacobs. He leans over, his face a few inches away from hers. "I said that I was not going to comment on anything regarding my personal life," forced Burns through clenched teeth. "Now, I understand that you're friends with Fate and Tempest, and I'm not surprised that you would take this opportunity to further an agenda..." "Monte, I was just..." "...THAT THEY HOPE WILL THROW ME OFF COURSE!" bellow Monte loud enough for the entire restaurant to hear. "However, I thought maybe you would have the decency to leave my private life out of it. It's not enough that I have to deal with a political conspiracy trying to hold me back from achieving greatness, but I have to deal with this kind of shit from the fWo's two-bit reporters. This interview is finished." After glaring into Kristen's eyes for a few moments, Burns calmly turns and walks slowly to the exit and leaves. Jacobs runs a hand through her hair and tries to calm herself, as a waiter comes over to see if everything was alright.
