Back to Work (Part 1 - Bigger)

6 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As I stood in front of the mirrored wall, I inhaled deeply and pulled the easy curl bar towards my chin. It wasn't the heaviest weight I'd ever done, but it did represent almost thirty pounds added to my normal set weight, which wasn't bad for a couple months' work. It was actually nice to have a couple of months off from touring so that I could get back onto a consistent workout schedule. Granted, I missed the camaraderie and being in the ring, and even the long nights on the road with my traveling companions, whatever the combination happened to be. Eli, Ivy, Tempest, Brand, even Sean, there were a lot of good times to be had over the summer, and it was really too bad it was all cut short by the greed of a few men. But was it truly over? It started with the phone call last week. When I first picked up the phone, I thought someone else at work was ribbing me, but when Gav assured me that it was from an outside line, the truth started to sink in. It wasn't a rib, it wasn't a prank, it was a man I considered to be a friend asking me to give him some assistance. How could I say no? I'd been off work since then, taking a leave of absence to make sure my cardiovascular health was up to par with my improved physical conditioning. After all, if I was going to be fleeing for my life from security guards, I needed to make sure I was up to the task. It'd all be so much simpler if we could've just gone back to work.
