And Redemption

5 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The events of the last week were overwhelming. Brandon had faced many challenges in his life but everything that had developed recently was both scary and life affirming. As opposite as that sounded that was the best definition he could come up with. Ten minutes ago the small talk had fallen to the wayside. All that was left was the issues at hand, the issues that drove his life forward. He and Forrester Campbell had tried their best to steer clear of the situation, of the engagement between Brandon and Amy, but here they were discussing things like there was even a chance of them changing their minds. Brandon sought confirmation, acceptance, whatever it was Forrester was willing to give, all for Amy's happiness. Her father wasn't exactly a friendly sort, not ill tempered by any means but not the first person you would seek out to pass time with either. His past weighed heavily on him and at some points in the conversation it seemed he unknowingly tried to over compensate for that past. "I'm just saying Brandon, you and her don't know what your getting in to. Well... you might, but she hasn't done nothing but make mistakes when it comes to things like this." Forrester sighed heavily, "What makes you think that you know what's best for her? From everything she's been telling us you seem to have issues of your own to work out. What about that ex-wife of yours? What's going to happen when things aren't so keen with Amy, are you going to find the next fresh face in that locker room and toss my little girl to the side?" Again Forrester's tone sounded argumentative to say the least. Brandon however sought nothing but the total diffusing of the difficult situation. He spoke as evenly as he could, "I... I can understand your worry Mr. Campbell, all I'm saying is that life is nothing but a bundle of chances. What would you expect her to do, just walk around like a zombie and ignore her heart, ignore her feelings? How is that supposed to make her happy? Is she supposed to sit idly by and not live because you're afraid?" "That might make you happy, but that's not going to do her any good. How many mistakes did you make in life? How many times do you wish you could go back and change what you did, and how many of those things made you who you are today?" Brandon realized the thin ice he treaded on. Amy hadn't shared much of her childhood but he had managed to put a few pieces together to realize that Forrester Campbell hadn't exactly been father of the year, and he tried to make up for that now. The furrowed brow only hinted at the displeasure Forrester felt. "Hell, I've made plenty of mistakes. That is exactly why Amy should listen to what I have to say. Now, you might not be half bad Brandon, but you've got a lot of baggage. That's baggage that she shouldn't have to deal with. I don't believe..." Brandon interrupted, "You're right sir, you don't believe. You need to stop thinking that you can protect her. If you had someone to protect you from everything who would you be today? Probably a carbon copy of your own father. Is that what you want Aimz to be, just like you? Or do you want her to be herself? I might hurt her, I might up and walk away, leave her at the altar or even bare-foot and pregnant. Or, I might love her, protect her in my own way, give her everything she could ever desire and make sure she never wants for anything. Are you really wanting to be responsible for her being deprived of that chance?" The two men, generations apart, stared silently at each other as Brandon's words echoed in his own head and reverberated in Forrester's. The patriarch of the Campbell family ran his hand through his hair, looking up to the sky; another sigh escaped his pursed lips. "Brandon son, listen, you might have your head on straight. Let's pretend that for a minute, but have you seen how she handles herself? What if she's the one that's messed up? You think you're so close to her, have you any idea how she handled herself when she was a kid, all of a year or so ago? She tramped around and got herself in such trouble." Forrester rolled his eyes, emphasizing what he was saying. He continued, seemingly now trying to convince Brandon that it was he that was in trouble of getting hurt. "I know what she's capable of. She might be a bright girl and all but when it comes to things like this she doesn't think." Brandon again interrupted, "She'll never be able to think for herself if you don't give her a chance. Maybe she made those mistakes because you hovered over her for so long she couldn't breath. We're in love sir, I think she wants your blessing but to be honest about it, even without it I can't simply walk away from her. I simply can't." "If we need to agree to disagree that's fine, but you need to take a step back and realize that this past year she has made some leaps and bounds. I'm not going to take one inch of credit for that though. I was just lucky enough to be there to bear witness. I'm also going to be there to bear witness to the rest as well. I love your daughter Mr. Campbell, and there really isn't a thing that's going to change that."
