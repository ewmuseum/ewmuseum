Meltdown...

26 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fade- in: Just when they thought they could no longer rely on the wrestling industry as an effective form of entertainment ... we prove them wrong. The scene opened up with Triple X standing in front of an fWo Presents: Meltdown backdrop ... no special effects ... no fancy editing ... just the fWo Internet Champion, clad in a black "Why? ...Because I Can" tee, blue jeans, black and blue Nikes. His hair was tied back into a pony tail, several wisps covering the front of his face. He brushed it aside, and propped the fWo Internet Title up on his right shoulder, and stared even harder into the camera. I can't say that I'm surprised ... believe what you want, but I've been waiting in the wings to do my thing for a little over a month, honestly. Despite the rumors circulating around the 'net ... I didn't politic my way into anything. I stand before you, the Internet Champion, for the same reason's why ... in the future - the not-so-distant future ... I'll stand in front of a camera, before you as fWo World Champion. ...and, it's because I'm the very best this industry has to offer. I didn't dupe anybody into anything ... Spyder made his decision to face me, he had every opportunity to duck me if he wanted. But, he looked at my resume' ... he witnessed first-hand that I won't quit, that I refuse to lose ... and, it interested him. Because if he could do the one thing nobody in this industry before him has been able to do, it'd mean big things for his future. But, I'm not here to talk about Spyder ... I'm here to talk about the future, this title on my shoulder ... and, Meltdown. He paused for a second, gathering his thoughts, forming his next couple of sentences ... clearing his head. He glanced down at his title, before looking back up at the camera and continuing. I never liked the fact that my fate, in the Meltdown main event could be determined by another man. Ken War, I don't know you, I don't plan to get to know you in the next forty-eight hours, but what I do know is ... we have the opportunity of a lifetime. You and I, together ... if we take advantage of all of the opportunities presented to us, we can do great things. Judging by the fact that we've never spoken, and that you've never made the attempt that I've made ... I could easily be in for a rude awakening, but I refuse to believe it. Because ... no matter what you do, it's not gonna take anything away from the fact that I'm coming to win. I don't think I'm the best wrestler on the fWo roster ... I'm not confessing it 'till I believe it. ...I know I am. I know it in my heart, and I'll prove it. To you, any fan, to any promoter. The fact that Flair n' Creed are in the main event, wrestling for the World Heavyweight Championship, to me ... is a minor technicality. But, don't get me wrong, I'm not greedy. I've just made the decision that I'm no longer in this business for other people. ...Not for any female, Rat Pack, of friend. I'm here for me, and I'm dedicated to achieving everything that's owed to me, because I am what I say I am. ...and, at Meltdown, whether you're with me, against me ... or somewhere doing whatever it is you ... characters do, it's not gonna make a difference. Honestly ... it would just make my job a tad bit easier. Smirking, Sean turned his back on the camera, as the screen switched to a Meltdown commercial before fading out. fade- to- black
