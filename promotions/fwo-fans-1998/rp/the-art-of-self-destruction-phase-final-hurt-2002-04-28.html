The Art of Self Destruction, Phase: Final (Hurt)

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
i hurt myself today to see if i still feel i focus on the pain the only thing that's real the needle tears a hole the old familiar sting try to kill it all away but i remember everything He sat in the darkness of his locker room. He was bloodied, scabbed, soon to be scarred. He was also a loser. Eli Flair was the better man on this night; he failed himself in the biggest match he had ever had. His hair was matted with blood; his face was caked with dirt, sweat and blood. His rival's blood, sweat and energy had poured into him and his did the same. Two crossed souls meeting for one final time. Every single adrenal gland pumping towards destroying each other. One man succeeded. The other sat dejected, beaten, bloodied and lost. As he sat alone, his fingers dug into the skin on his opposite arm, drawing fresh blood to mix with the blood that had dried onto him. Tears streaked from his closed eyes. Nothing but the darkness surrounding him beckoned; calling for something that was closer than it was all of those other times. Through his mind, images of everything that had been came to him. A noose hanging from a support beam in the attic, under it a milk crate that could easily be kicked away. A kitchen knife gripped in his right hand, which was shaking violently, slowly drawing blood across his left wrist. Six empty bottles of vodka scattered around his unconscious body. A .9mm under his mattress, beside it a noose, knife and a small capsule of cyanide. His eyes snapped open to face only more darkness. His fingernails dug deeper into his skin, breaking through the layers. He began to vigorously dig into the bar code that was tattooed into his left arm, just above the wrist. He began to bleed as it was cut open. Quickly, he got to his feet. His feet made it to the doorway where he locked the door to the room. Nobody was ever going to get in. A hand reached over and flipped the light switch. The dark blue eyes closed to avoid being shocked by the brightness. Looking down, a dark smile crossed his face as he looked at the bleeding tattoo and the open wounds across his arms. In a corner of the room he saw a vase filled with fake flowers. Moving swiftly, he threw the vase and plastic flowers to the floor, breaking the glass vase. He picked up one of the shards and placed it on the small table where it had once sat. Thinking again, he took one of the chairs and set it underneath the door's lock, further ensuring that there was no way in. He brought his arms up and ripped the shirt off of his back. He moved over to the one full body mirror that sat in the room. He picked up the shard of glass and smiled to his reflection; carefully noticing the puncture wounds from the barbed wire. what have i become my sweetest friend everyone i know goes away in the end you could have it all my empire of dirt i will let you down i will make you hurt "What the fuck have you done to yourself?" His hand moved diagonal across his chest, blood quickly came to the top of the wound. "ANSWER ME, DAMMIT!" The shard of glass moved to the other hand, another diagonal cut made a lacerated red X across his chest. "STOP STARING AT ME!" A sneer formed across his face and he took the glass and began digging it into the mirror. Horrible scratching sounds came and he kept working at the mirror. "WHY WON'T YOU JUST LEAVE ME ALONE?" His eyes were black, filled with hate. The mirror returned the look. He took his fist and swung at the mirror. It shattered and fell into pieces onto the ground. Just like his life. "YOU'RE RIGHT! I AM NOTHING!" He fell to his knees as tears came to his eyes. Blood streaked down his chest; his white skin had been tinted red. He put his hands down into the glass, more blood began to flow onto the white carpet which had been a change from the usual concrete floor. One of his hands rushed through his jet-black hair, blood dripped from his hairline and onto the carpet as he did so. Tears continued to flow, mixing with the blood. He brought his head up momentarily at the fluorescent lighting above him. "FUCK YOU GOD! FUCK YOU! YOU'VE NEVER DONE ANYTHING FOR ME! FUCK YOU! YOU WERE NEVER THERE WHEN I NEEDED YOU! WHERE THE FUCK WERE YOU!?" He fell over onto his back, covering his face with his bloodied hands. "Where was my salvation, God? Where was my Promised Land? I believed in everything they told me, God. They told me you would help me seek everything I need. EVERYTHING! I believed them. I believed in you. I believed all of their empty promises. Their promises and lies, God. They fucking promised me. They said everything would be okay. Well, it's not fucking okay anymore, God!" He was completely numb, oblivious to everything around him. He didn't realize that he had fallen on the pieces of glass from the mirror, until they were cutting into his back. The sweet crimson wine was plentiful. "Nothing I ever did meant a thing to them, or you. And everyone around me, watching every thing I did. Reading my thoughts. How could you let them get inside of me? I don't understand it. Why did you always fail me? They said you would always be there for me. Yet you never once were." He closed his eyes again. More images flashed through his brutally shot mind. Drinking at a party on July 14th, 1993. Fighting with Diane Wilson over a beer while driving down a highway. Missing the turn, careening off of the side and smashing through the barrier. Diane, being killed instantly when she was shot through the windshield. "Why couldn't you have killed me, God? I didn't deserve to live. She did. Why can't all of this just go away, God? Why can't you just grant me what I want? Just this one time. All of those times I tried, I failed. You held me back, God. You made me fail, you just tried to keep me around. You wanted to make my life more miserable than I ever imagined..." i wear this crown of shit upon my liar's chair full of broken thoughts i cannot repair beneath the stains of time the feelings disappear you are someone else i am still right here More images. A broom coming up and connecting with his backside. His father lighting a crack pipe just for another two minute high. His mother, with another man, while his father was out. The man paying his mother for her services. His father coming home, beating his mother... To death. "What did I ever do to deserve parents like that, God? They didn't want me, and you let them have me. They tried to kill me, beat me until I couldn't feel anymore. They turned me into this, just like you did. It's their fault, your fault. I didn't deserve this, God." The locker room fell eerily silent as that last breath escaped his lungs. He stirred slightly, his eyes making contact with a bag. The bag that he kept his clothes and personal items in. Slowly, and unsteadily he got to his feet. Something ran through his brain and he moved lethargically towards the bag. It was all he could focus on. He could feel himself beginning to fall in and out of consciousness from the blood loss. Over the last three months his brain had been pounded into submission from his disease. X-Rays would have shown something that resembled an egg being thrown into a frying pan. His true conscious and thought were gone, what was left was pushing him to reach that bag. And he did. The sound of his breathing, which had degenerated into wheezing, was all that filled the room. His fingertips reached the bag; he tried to pull it towards him without success; he tried to pull his body across the floor; the shards of the mirror dug into his legs; his right hand finally reached around the bag; he pulled it to him. A deathly smile came across his face as he struggled to get the bag open. He succeeded and slowly began to throw clothing out of it. Until he reached the bottom. His hand came out of the bag and produced one thing: A fully loaded .9mm. He tried to get to his feet and did with a struggle. His legs were trying to give out, but he propped himself onto the small table, which barely held his weight. His eyes moved across the gun, his bloodied fingers caressed the barrel. One simple commanding voice rang through his head over and over again. DO IT i am the bullet in the gun (and i control you) i am the truth from which you run (and i control you) i am the silencing machine (and i control you) i am the end to all your dreams (and i control you) I control you... Donaven.... His hand trembled as he gripped the handle and brought the gun to his temple. He closed his eyes tight and sucked in his last few breaths of air from the Earth. Sweat began to form again, mixing with the blood; it ran down his face and around the end of the gun and his temples. "erase me..." what have i become my sweetest friend everyone i know goes away in the end you could have it all my empire of dirt i will let you down i will make you hurt He gritted his teeth and a nervous index finger went to pull the trigger. There was a pounding on the door behind him, but he couldn't hear it. His entire body was fixated on the gun, his temple and the end of life. The door was broken open. He still had his back turned and didn't hear it smash open. He was about to pull it, but he was interrupted. "Winters? What the hell are you doing?" Eli's voice rang through his head. No reply came from him. "Don't do this. You can't kill yourself," Eli pleaded, "People do care. They respect you." Eli took a hesitant step toward him. "Donaven. I'm one of them. Don't do this to yourself." Something struck a nerve inside of him. Slowly he turned to face Eli Flair, the man that had beaten him over an hour earlier. His eyes opened to see the man that was begging for him not to do it. Maybe something happened inside of what used to be his mind. And maybe it was the blood and sweat that encompassed him, but the gun fell out of his hand and dropped harmlessly to the floor. Flair quickly got over to the gun and picked it up off the floor. He emptied the barrel and slipped the bullets into his pocket. Eli looked over at him, and got him to his feet. "We're gonna get your ass to a hospital," Flair said. It was ironic, because Eli would never allow himself into a hospital. He was dead weight to Eli, but for some reason, he felt like he had to care. He didn't realize that Eli Flair was trying to save his life. What he could no longer feel, he could sense. He knew that someone was helping him move around, but he couldn't figure out who. Inside of his head, he could slowly feel everything that had taken control was dying away. His heart was a bloodstained egg that had finally cracked. if i could start again a million miles away i would keep myself i would find away The End.
