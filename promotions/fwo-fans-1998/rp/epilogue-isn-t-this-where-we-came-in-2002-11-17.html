Epilogue: Isn't this where we came in?

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
November 13, 2002 (10:29 PM) Los Angeles, California You didn't answer my question. "What question was that?" Why are you so against Stormwindz? "Why should I be FOR her?" Why? Why not? Because you don't know anything about her but that she apparently hates the gimmick she's been given? Because you don't know anything about her but that she's only been given the gimmick so the Board can say, look at the Tempest type character, doesn't she suck? Because she's in the same position we were in seven years ago? Five years ago? Three years ago? Two years ago? Fighting for respect in a harsh business. Fighting for a niche in a harsh business. "She's not fighting for anything. She's making a ton of money off Tempest's name." How do you know? "I just...." How.... do you know? "...." You don't know. You don't have any idea. ".... You don't know either." That's right. But I'm willing to give her the benefit of the doubt. Just like we gave Troy. Just like Deacon. Kev. Austin. Barry. Just like Sean. "That's not fair." Why not? "Sean isn't...." You're stuttering, Ivy. "No I'm not." Yes you are. "Fucker." Bitch. "Asshole." Psycho. ".... I'm really not being fair, am I?" No. But it's okay. You miss Tempest and nobody has the right to deny you that. You see Stormwindz as being a cheap imitation of your best friend. And you see Stormwindz' being here a direct reminder that Tempest isn't. "....Yeah." But none of that is her fault. "....I know." Do you? "Yeah." The board doesn't have the best interests of the fWo at heart - they have their own interests in mind. And that doesn't include having a positive backstage atmosphere. That doesn't include wrestlers working WITH each other instead of AGAINST each other. "I miss my best friend, Eli." I know. But give Stormwindz a chance. "It might be moot - we got stiffed tonight, you really think I'll be in San Diego next week?" Oh I'll be there, Ivy, and so will you. "You think so, huh?" Gotta watch Paul's back. Gotta watch Brand's back. You remember those two, right? Our friends? Paul introduced us to everyone our first night in the fWo locker room? You took Brand and Lance under your wing and made them your personal projects - to get them used to the road trips and loosen them up a little? Hell, anything Lance knows about ring psychology worth keeping he learned from you. "And Ruben will probably be there, yes?" Of course. "I'm not that big of a bitch, am I, Eli?" You can be, but so can we all. Don't worry about it so much. "Yeah?" I have a feeling.... this week is gonna be mighty interesting. ~FIN~
