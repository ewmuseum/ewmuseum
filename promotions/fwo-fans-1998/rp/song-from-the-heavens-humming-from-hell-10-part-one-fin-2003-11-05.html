Song from the Heavens, Humming from Hell - 10 - Part One Fin

5 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Please don't kill me....please, I have children." The man seemed past the point of begging, it was more of desperation, the man wanted to believe something he already knew wasn't the case. Currently he was held tightly against a wooden board. With his arms nailed into the wood as well his legs and feet, blood poured down his white skin and stained it with red. He did not feel pain anymore, he had gone numb. Two men, as bloody as he is now had gotten into his car and made him drive them to a house, in which they took him inside and nailed him to this wooden board. He did not know who they were, but he knew they weren't human. They were monsters...especially the scarred man. "Please don't..." "Oh be quiet," Douglas said with annoyance as he lowered Spencer's body down onto a table in the middle of the room, Steve ran his head through Spencer's hair, "It's going to be okay Spencer, we will be eating soon, and our wounds will heal, and our strength will return." Spencer looked up to him and smiled as he closed his eyes, he was almost dead. Douglas would fix that, with the gentlemen that was nailed to the board. Douglas then looked back to the man who could barely hold his head up, "I'm not too fond of blood, that's why I have taken the liberty to try and drain as much of you as I could. I can't stand when that guck gets in my eye. I'll start by eating your eyes first, the world doesn't understand why they are such a treat but I do." The man mumbled, "You monster..." Douglas laughed, "Me!? A monster! I'm a product of society. I'm the child who didn't get enough attention, the child who's Daddy came in at night time and played with, I'm the pervert in the alleyway looking for love because I just need to be touched. I'm the one who rapes your sister and gives her AIDS. I'm just like you and your just like me, but I've been rejected too many times by your filth. It is all of you who are the monsters, just because I never fit in you outlawed me. You pushed me away when I needed you. You made me what I am." The combination of blood and saliva dropped from the man's mouth, "I never did anything..." "Oh no?!?" Douglas was now getting angry, "Typical... when your with a group, you'll scorn, you'll spit, you'll follow the other sheep. But on your own, your forgiving, your sorry, your not at fault. Because it's not you who did it right? That's why today is not just about you. Because I am going to get you all." "I don't..." The man tried but was losing consciousness, "...understand..." "Of course not," Steve replied as he walked over to the corner of his room, "You see this?" Steve pointed at a corner of the room but the man had slipped out of consciousness by this time. Steve continued on as if the man was listening, "This is enough explosives to knock out a whole state. In fact to blow thousands upon thousands of people to hell, but certainly not back. Heh... once I set this off, and I start the cleansing, other cleansing will follow and soon no one will know who the enemy is. Complete chaos will reign and the world as we know it will end with a single breath. All existence will be over and no more will people like me have to suffer at the hands of your cruelty." Douglas turned and walked back toward the man and he pulled out a dagger that he seemed to carry around everywhere. Douglas reached the man, "I told you I'd start with your eyes."
