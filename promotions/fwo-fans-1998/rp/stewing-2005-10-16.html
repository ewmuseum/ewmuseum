Stewing

16 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He should've been happy. He really was. His daughter, only three short nights ago, gave birth to Gabrielle Jessica Silver. His wife was home tonight, now resting. He had some time off... But... "FUCK THIS!" Well...something was clearly amiss in the Silver household. Throwing a pair of his workout shoes against the wall of his living room, Sonny sat there quietly to himself, realizing the error he just made. Eme didn't need to be around for this. She had nothing to do with the business, so there was no need to involve those that got in his way. After a few minutes, he realized that there was still no sound in the house. SOMEHOW, SOMEWAY, no one heard his outburst. After grumbling a little more, he walked over to the sliding glass door and went outside onto the deck, dressed in only a pair of black sweats. The cold didn't affect him. Not when he was burning mad, still. He wanted nothing more right now than to simply walk up to fWo headquarters, find Allen "Codemaster" Brown, and make him be the first black man to have his face turn blue. But he couldn't. The doctors told him he'd suffered a big concussion and he wouldn't be allowed to even do so much as walk near the arenas. The fWo was half a world away, anyhow. They were still in Asia. Sonny's heart right now, had to be back here in Seattle where his wife and new daughter lay. "Eme...Gaby..." He turned back to the house, looking back inside. The house was all but quiet. Eme was a fighter, though. 34 hours of pain inducing labor, but she made it. She came home and she was fine. Truth be told, maybe the sneak attack by the Rogues was a good thing. Instead of being consumed by his job, he could focus on his family, something he knew deep down he'd been putting off for quite a while. He didn't even know how he'd managed to keep his life a secret. The first time a smark had gotten wind of even the most trivial account of a wrestler's life, it was blown up all over the place. Peers and fans alike would look at them differently. He'd managed to compose himself very well against Eme, somehow. She hadn't sensed that something was wrong. At least not yet. While tapping on the black metal railing of the deck for a moment, Sonny turned back to see his cell charging on the kitchen table. After pondering a moment, he went back into the house, picked up his cell phone, and dialed a number. "Come on...fucking international charges..." "Hello?" the other voice said. "Lindsay, it's me." "Oh, joy. How's the baby's daddy?" His tag partner said on the other line with a hint of goofiness/sarcasm in her voice. "Stewing. Troy, is there, any word yet on me getting back there?" "I told you when you last called me an hour ago. No." He set the phone down for a moment and gritted his teeth before going back to the conversation. "Look, Mr. Sunshine, it may be none of my business, but don't you have a wife and a kid to get back to? I mean, she just got out of the hospital. Just chillax, get a diaper-changing lesson or two, then I'll call you if there's any word yet, alright?" Glancing at his cell phone for a moment, he released a deep sigh and watched his chilly breath escape his body. "You know what, Lindsay?" "What?" "...You're right." "Well, it's about time you realized as such. Now, get back to your wife. She needs you." "No, Lindsay...you're right. It IS none of your business." And before she could get in a word edgewise, he shut off the phone and tossed it back in the pocket of his sweats before pacing around the deck. Sure, lots of people had wished death upon him for being a jerk to everybody. Others wished he would just go away. But this Codemaster...this Allen Brown...he underestimated him. And it came back to bite him in the ass, big-time. He'd been set up, humiliated, injured, and maligned. Finally, Sonny Silver knew what it was like to be one of the countless people he'd committed any of the previous atrocities to. No wonder a lot of people couldn't stand him. "You're gonna pay, you little fucks..." He turned his head to the side and looked at his clenched fist. "When I get back to the fWo... ...YOU MOTHERFUCKERS ARE GOING TO PAY WITH YOUR BLOOD! YOU HEAR ME, ALLEN BROWN? FOR AS LONG AS I DEEM IT SO, YOUR ASS OFFICIALLY BELONGS TO ME!" ------------------------------------------------------------- Meanwhile, over in China, one Allen "Codemaster" Brown was sitting in his hotel room, playing the new "Castlevania: Sorrow" game on his DS. Coral and Annabelle were out for most of the afternoon taking in some of the sights while he cursed the name of that Dracula asshole. It was then he sneezed. And with it, came something. He didn't know. A sense of dread, perhaps? Nah. And that, as they say, was that.
