Getting Ahead: Cyber Scam. II

7 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dillon was gaining.... the Fuse Bros. were trapped. Tyler shot Conor one last stare before he knew it was all over. And Conor, who couldn't look his brother in the eyes right now just lowered his head and stared at the ground. "Hello?" Dillon asked again, as he was only a few steps away from the corner in which the Fuse Bros. hid. Tyler decided that it was time to give himself up. "Yes?" He replied, as this actually seemed to scare Dillon. He took a step back, not expecting anyone to answer. Then why bother asking in the first place? Jesus... these people... "Who's there?" Dillon asked again, but this time he was ready for a reply. "Uh... Tyler and Conor, the Fuse Bros.." Tyler said as he came out of the shadows and walked into the open. "Come on, Conor. Come on." He waved his brother out, as Conor emerged from the corner, dragging his feet on the floor as he did. "Jesus guys." Dillon said. "I thought it was someone else." Tyler shrugged. "Ah, well, no it isn't." What a great conversation. "Alright... well what did you guys want anyway?" Dillon asked, turning around to wave Clifford and the other members of Crew 4 to get back to work. "It's almost eleven at night... and we're a little behind schedule." "That's alright..." Tyler went on. "We'll only be a minute." The oldest Fuse Bros. walked forward, heading over to where the shopzone stand was being set up. He then looked behind the display cabinet, only to find numerous boxes of merchandize labeled to the according wrestlers that they belonged to. Tyler frequently moved the boxes around, as Dillon rubbed his head, seemingly very confused. "What are you doing?" He asked awkwardly. "We're still selling your Fuse Bros. t-shirts if that's what you wanted to know. In fact, they're selling really well these days!" Conor pumped himself a fist in the air, followed by a "Yes!", as Dillon and Tyler both turned around, shaking their heads at him. "Sorry." Tyler continued to scrummage through the boxes... placing the ones he didn't want on the left-hand side, and the ones he might end up going through on the left. "Come on, Ty... just tell me what you're looking for and I can find it-" Dillon was cut-off. "WHOO!" In finding the correct box, Tyler opened it up, and pulled out the first thing he saw. "This is it!" He shouted. "This is what we've come here for!" Dillon, once more, rubbed his head in confusion... for Tyler was holding a VHS version of Cyber Slam VI.
