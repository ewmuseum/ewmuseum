Hearing Voices

10 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tempest rubbed her neck as she watched the sun set over the jagged horizon of the city. She sighed, here eyes tired even as they reflected the golden light of the sun's last rays. On the rooftop of the building she was staying in, she sat on the edge and dangled her feet off, kicking a little. Next to her lay a stack of papers, magazines and dirtsheets on the fWo and other wrestling news. She couldn't stand the droll commentary and presumptuous posturing of those who couldn't wrestle critiquing those who could without the benefit of an exhilarating setting. The wind picked up and she watched a few papers fly away, drifting to the ground as she said, "It's all so much litter anyway eventually." She pulled her legs up and wrapped her arms around them musing to herself, "Everything we do can be whittled down to a few sentences in an article, or picked apart by an uncaring critic who couldn't survive what we did, much less understand it." She sighed. "Everyone wants to talk about who will win, me or Monte... I'm not sure either one of us really does." She reached over and pulled one of the magazines off the stack and looked at the cover. On it was Vic Creed and R.I.P. It was one of many tribute issues that had come out lately. "Funny how dying does such wonders for one's public relations," she said bitterly, her jaw clenching just a bit. She shivered involuntarily and said, "I suppose I shouldn't speak ill of the dead, but... God, how damned convenient." She fumed, her eyes narrowing as she stared at the picture and said, "You got to live your dream, even if only for awhile. You destroyed as many people as you could get a hold of, ruined as many lives as you touched and now... NOW you leave your little girl alone in this world. How dare you... HOW FUCKING DARE YOU!!" Her scream echoed as she flung the magazine away and watched it wing its way over the street and flutter to the ground. As she stared at it a voice came from behind her. "Tempest?" She turned and saw the slight Asian figure of Miyuki Matsudaira, her business manager. Miyuki had no interest in coming to ringside and being part of the show. She was too demure for that. But when Tempest needed someone calm, composed and organized to keep her affairs in order... that was where Miyuki shined. "What's the matter?" Miyuki asked, taking a seat next to her. "You only come up on the rooftops when you want to be alone." Tempest grumbled and said, "So why did ya come up her to ruin it?" Miyuki smiled and said, "Because I know you never really want to be alone." Tempest chuckled and said, "OK, you win. I'm just... Miyuki, what the Hell is Monte Burns thinking? Why couldn't he just... like me or something, so I could get to now him better?" Miyuki looked at her quizzically and said, "I thought you didn't want to get to know him." Tempest seemed startled and said, "Well I don't. But... well if he wasn't being a butthead I might feel differently." She huffed and folded her arms. Miyuki smiled at her and said, "I know that. But I don't know what you mean specifically." "Gee I dunno!" Tempest said, exasperated as she threw her arms up. "He says how much he cares about me then kicks the crap out of me, then defends me, then turns on me and now challenges me! What does he think is gonna come out of this? Why does he think I can't see he's just playing me?" "Maybe you should ask him," Miyuki said matter-of-factly. Tempest gaped at her as she shrugged and said, "Well, why not? If he does care about you, he should be willing to talk to you, or at least listen. But consider this... the only way he can get near you is in the ring right now. Maybe he hopes to earn your respect that way." "He's wasting his time," Tempest growled. "Now wait, why..." "Because he already has it," Tempest said and folded her arms around herself. "I never said I didn't respect him, I said I didn't trust him. I know he has a lot of heart, maybe even genuine heart, in the ring. It's just that... I've never seen him do anything that wasn't to hurt someone." Miyuki nodded, taking this in and said, "And... what has he seen of you?" Tempest said, "That made him decide to tell me that he cared? I have no idea. I..." "He watched you almost destroy your brother," Miyuki said. Tempest stopped in her tracks and stared at her. "He watched you be humiliated in front of the world and come back from it with all the fury that scorned women are heir to. If he wasn't terrified, he must have been quite impressed." She smiled a little as Tempest seemed to shrink and contemplate this. Miyuki stepped forward and put her hand on Tempest's shoulder as she said, "I know all that business with Chronos and Eddy Fate hurt you. I watched you fight back, as if in terror that the pit from Hong Kong would seek you out and swallow you up. But it didn't. It's over and you must stop running from it. You climbed from that pit not once, but twice! So leave it for good." Tempest nodded and said, "I know. You're right. But still, that doesn't mean I can trust Monte Burns." "True," Miyuki said with a smile. "But at least you should listen to him with your whole heart instead of the little bit that isn't walled off after all that." Tempest nodded and the silence hung heavy for a moment. Then Tempest looked up as her trademark grin flashed across her face and said, "Of course that doesn't mean I'm gonna let him have my Cruiserweight strap." Miyuki smirked and shook her head as she said, "Oh of course not. Kick his ass and then decide if you want to pat it and make him feel better." They both burst into laughter and headed for the stairs back into the warmth below.
