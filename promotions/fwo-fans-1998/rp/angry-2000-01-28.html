Angry...

28 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Angry... January 28, 2000 Havok is shown walking into his hotel room in England, tired from a hard day of doing absolutely nothing. He walks in, collapses onto the couch, and pushes the button on his answering machine. A voice comes from the machine. "Hey Chris, it's Bob. I just wanted to thank you for last night, you were great, and don't worry about your little size problem, it'll grow someday." Another message plays. "Hey Brian, it's me, Erin. Sorry about last night, you just got excited. I won't tell anybody, so they don't make fun of you and call you "Quick Draw." Stunned, Havok looks over at the camera. "Damn! They keep getting my number mixed up with the other wrestlers'! I just got more calls for Wretch and Primetime. Anyway, I'm here to talk about a few things. First of all, it seems that I'm fighting Primetime and The Frenchie on Slaughter, along with Panther. Well, this is just great. I can fight the World Champion, but can I get a shot? No! I don't get any respect! Well, I'm gonna prove myself Sunday, and pin the Frenchie in the ring, one, two, uh... damn! Hey cameraman, what letter comes next?" "Three, Havok. Three." "Thanks. Like I said... I'll pin the Frenchie, one, two, three! And prove that I can beat the World Champion! Now we've got Wretch talking smack about me. I don't listen to him. He sucks at Mystery Date! So, it looks like I'll have to take him out at Trapped, and take those Tag Titles from the Underground! And finally, we've got a quadriplegic, Lance Star talking about me. Who is he to talk about anybody? He got his CENSORED ass paralyzed by Scott Collins! Well, bring it on, Star, and I'll kick your CENSORED ass all over any CENSORED arena in the world. And when..." As the ranting, raving and censoring goes on, a little caption appears on the camera. It reads "The fWo does not condone the action of attacking cripples. The views expressed by Havok are not necessarily the views of the Fan's Wrestling Organization. Thank you, and be sure to order Trapped 2000(Live on Pay-Per-View)!!!" "CENSORED!!! All this yelling has me tired. Get out, I'm taking a nap." The camera fades to a promo for Trapped 2000(Live on Pay-Per-View!)
