A Review of Warfare

24 February 2000

fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization A Review of Warfare February 24, 2000 The scene opens in Matt Douglas's locker room. Matt is sitting in a chair watching the tape from his match on Wednesday Warfare. He looks up when he sees the cameraman. Matt: Hey, how are you doing? Cameraman: I am good. Say, that was a good match last night you had against Oracle. It was good that you did not let the referee call it a draw, but instead you persuaded him to continue the match, and you won! Matt: I am glad you are so happy. You must have brought that up, because I am watching the tape right now. Lets watch a clip of the best part. The camera pans down to the TV. It shows Matt Douglas giving Oracle a superkick to the face, and when Oracle hits the mat, Douglas rolls him up for the victory. Matt: Now that looked good. I mean how many people do you know that can do that? The cameraman begins to count with his free hand. Matt: Nevermind, it was only a joke. You cameramen take everything so seriously. Cameraman: I am just using my wrestling knowledge. Matt: I imagine you see a lot of everything. From broken bones to bumps and scratches, right? Cameraman: Maybe. Matt: Ok. Matt turns off the TV and puts down the remote. Matt: I can't wait to meet Oracle again in the ring. I am sure that it will be sometime soon. Considering he is so eager to win my International Title. Until then, anyone who thinks they can beat me, just let me know, and I am sure that I can fit you in for a title shot. The scene fades to black as Matt and the cameraman continue to talk about the match from Warfare.
