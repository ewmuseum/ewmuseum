Departure

4 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Do you know who Reverend Shaw is? He tried to ignore the nagging question, but David Cross kept hearing it. He left everything behind and travelled back to the Golden Phoenix Temple in Japan to continue his martial training and taming of his inner spirit. That question kept him from doing anything overly constructive. David's shin struck the leather padding which had been wrapped around the wooden post. The impact reverberated through the round piece of timber, quickly followed by several more blows. Shin strikes, knees, fists, elbows, all struck the worn padding with bad intentions. "Focus David!" Master Yoeda shouted, the old man wagging the end of his cane at his disciple. His body went through the motions, but the mind of David Cross had slipped back to a time months previous. A time when he was employed by the Fans Wrestling Organization. Arguably, it was a happier time... until that fateful day that he met with his boss, Ted Tallison. "Reverend Shaw?" David nodded. "That's that religious nut, isn't it? One of those guys who thinks God is punishing us with hurricanes because we allow homosexuals to breathe?" Tallison leaned forward in his chair and rested his elbows in his desk, pressing his fingertips together in front of his face. Ted looked at the space between his fingers as if there was an answer waiting there, an answer to a question no one had asked. "Reverend Shaw is more than that. His views are quite... extreme. His congregation has also been protesting the funerals of American soldiers who had been killed in Iraq because they defend a country that he feels is morally corrupt." "And I thought I was unstable." David caught the look Tallison gave him. "Not anymore, of course. I mean I used to be unstable. I'm much better now. Much."
