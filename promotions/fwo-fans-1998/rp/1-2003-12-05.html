The clock on the dresser of the dank and dusty apartment read 11:59. Infront of the dark red of the alarm clock light sat an elderly women. Her eyes faded downwards to a picture, the moment captured was one of the happiest of her life. Her and the love of her life arm and arm exiting the church. Rice in the air, the smell of car exhaust flowing down the busy city street. She could remember the day like it was yesterday, the beautiful sun shining down on her in her extravagant white wedding gown. She could remember the huge smile Daniel wore that day. She could remember her own son bringing the rings down the aisle. She could remember the fun the two had at the reception, and how it continued long into that night. With that she reached down and picked up a blank piece of paper. In her right hand was a pen. Slowly she wrote in bright red ink. "Dont hate me for what I'm about to do. My best days are long gone. Brandon I'm sorry. I love you, and good bye. Love for ever and always, Mom" The red light of the clock glowed as she reached down and picked up the Colt .44 magnum. The gun dealer had said it was to big for her to shoot, it was time to find out. She placed the gun against her temple, pulled the hammer back and let go as it clicked back in place. Her delicate and fragile hands squeezed the trigger with all of her might. The clock glowed 12:11 a.m. Martha Thompson lay dead on the floor of her and her sons apartment.
