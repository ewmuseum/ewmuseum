Another day, another flight. The nomadic life of a professional wrestler wasn't all glitz and glamour. Sometimes it was about living in airports, tracking down lost luggage, or getting re-routed to the wrong city. An hour into the flight, he decided to try and get some much needed sleep. The plane wasn't as full as he thought it'd be, but Kodiak Vic Creed still found himself feeling cramped. It wasn't due to the other passengers as much as it was the single one wedged beside him. "Peanut?" asked Nick Brandish as he held the open bag towards Creed. Vic held his hand up and leaned back in his seat. "I'm good, thanks." Nick folded the bag closed and stuffed it into the pocket of the seat in front of him. His eyebrows furrowed as he stared at the side of Creed's head. "Hey Vic, I've gotta ask you something." "Shoot." Vic closed his eyes and tried to drift off to sleep. Last night was long and sleep was short. He didn't actually care about anything Nick Brandish had to ask him anyway, although he suspected he could guess what the big man was going to say. Nick continued to stare. "I'm no manager. I don't know dick about arranging photo shoots and personal appearances for you. Besides, that's not why I'm here in the first place and we both know it." Creed continued to stare at the inside of his eyelids. It had been nice having Nick around. On the road, it helped to have a good friend with you to help you out and watch your back. But that was before Vic found out Nick had been tipping off the wannabe gangster Darryl Vishanti. Darryl showed his appreciation by crippling Vic's sister. Vic's good friend needed his neck broken. "Nick, I got you a wrestling gig. You've had some ring time. Isn't that what you wanted?" "A contract," Brandish said in his gravelly voice. "I wanted a contract, not this pay-per-appearance bullshit. I helped you in Japan, and you won't help me now. You owe me the chance. After Mitch Wilson got you your precious deal with the fWo, it's the least you can do." Vic's eyes snapped open and he sat forward. The fucking nerve. "Mitch got my foot in the door. I got me my precious deal by busting my ass. And I'm paying you back for Japan by giving you your shot. You've been in the ring. You've been working matches.
