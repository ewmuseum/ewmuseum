Somewhere I Belong - Where The Wild Things Are (Six)

19 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What happens when someone doesn't get along with their parents? They move out. What if they have no choice but to go back? Well if you're Amy Campbell, you end up seated at a bus depot in the dead of night... Thursday April 10th, 2003: "Finally... people should not be forced to spend that much time together unless they genuinely enjoy each other's company." Aimz wasn't there to eavesdrop on people. Truth be told, she didn't want to be there, period. But right now, what choice did she have? Darcy? "Fuck you, Mick." Renee? "See?" Back to her parents' house for another round of shit about how she's wasting her life? No thank you. And there were far worse places she could spend the night than the bus station. It was warm here, she could get some coffee or something to eat, and there was nobody around to lecture to her about her lifestyle. "I need coffee, guys. Want anything?" And that's what mattered right now. Time to think. "Cigarettes, if you don't mind." What Aimz really needed right now, however... whether she knew it or not... "Naaah, I'm good." ...was simply someone who could listen. "Be right back--- AMY?!?" Aimz' train of thought was broken at the sound of her name, as she looked up at the voice the word was attached to. She rubbed her eyes once... twice... because this person could not be here. "...Angel?" Angel hoisted her daughter up into one arm and hugged the frustrated redhead. "You look like hell, honey... what's up?" "I've got no place to live until I get cleared to go to the states, and just spent a day and almost two whole nights with my parents because of it." Upon jumping into the meat of the story, she groaned and noted Angel's surprised, but similar reaction. "And now I'm here...it's quiet, and nobody 'wins' by my being here." "Wow," replied Angel, "You look like you could use a cup of coffee and about twenty years of girl talk. C'mon, MJ's buying." Amy pointed to a small castle of styrofoam cups in the plastic chair beside her, "I've had enough coffee for a milennia. Help yourself to what they've got, just don't expect Starbucks." Her grin was a welcome one - hadn't happened for days. "But talk...yeah, talk is good. I need sleep, too. You tend not to get much with people bitching at you." The two women headed for the small convenience store/stand in the middle of the atrium and looked at the wares. Amy was right - the coffee was more like toxic sludge than a yuppie- friendly Starbucks caffe mocha, but she also looked impressed at the way Angel poured out a cup and took a refreshing sip. "When you're on the road nine days a week with little money, you tend to get used to shitty coffee. You understand." Angel handed MJ to Amy as she pulled out her money clip to pay - and she definitely caught the smile holding the little baby brought to Aimz' face. "Pack'a Marlboro reds, too," said Angel to the cashier, and turned back toward Aimz, "and no matter what I might say or do, you are not to let me have one." "You smoke and I'll kick your ass..." The redhead suddenly looked motherly, but in a way that reminded neither girl of their parents. She shifted MJ in her arms slightly. "You smoke around this little angel, and I'll make sure you won't have lungs to polute with that shit." "Hey, it was this little one that forced me to quit about eleven months ago when she was much littler," replied Angel, but it don't mean I don't have an occasional craving." She reached out to take MJ back, but realized she had hot coffee in her hand. "Do you mind holding her a bit?" Dumb question, as Aimz kissed the three month old on the forehead, as they found a quiet place to sit. "I'm ready whenever you are," said Angel, sipping the toxic- sludge coffee, "and I've got plenty'a time to talk." "Okay, so I got home Wednesday night because I left the arena instantly since Brandon was off to do something with his kids and, since that also involves a degree of Ana, I bailed...I don't wanna maim someone in front of their own kids." She stopped just short of an entirely unrelated rant. "Anyway...I woke up yesterday at like noon, and Renee had this eviction notice...one she'd found on my door. Land lord didn't give much reason, and I would have made him bleed thirteen seperate ways if I didn't know he was a dirty bastard who was just groping for a reason to get me into more trouble..." Angel nodded, MJ stretched and Amy extended a finger to be clung to before she continued... "I didn't want to go to my parents at all, but I had no choice. I knew they'd be so fucking smug and thinking they'd won some great battle against my 'free spirit'...they were. They just played that game for a day, the one where they walk on glass - pleased with themselves and not risking getting their egos shot down because they think I'm too stupid to realize what they're thinking..." "They lost. Tonight I started getting little 'polite suggestions' from my mom and blew...as soon as I said I saw through their playing nice, she didn't waste a second telling me how much I owed them, how I supposedly can't survive on my own because I'm such a fuck u--well, I don't have to tell you. You know what it's like..." Angel nodded thoughtfully, "I know. You didn't have anywhere else to go?" "Nowhere. I can't move to America until I get a few more shots. I don't wanna live in that system...but Brandon's there, so that's where I've gotta be." She lowered her head for a second, "Shit...he doesn't even know. I forgot to call..." "Call him tomorrow," replied Angel, "you don't want him to worry in the middle'a the night, do you? Besides... you don't wanna give the ex any sort of sign that you're 'high maintenance' , do you? Anyways... you've got a place to stay for at least two nights now." "Two nights is exactly what I need. I take off on Saturday night, Sunday morning-ish for Slaughter...but, the bus? Have you guys got room? I've got a few weeks to get my stuff outta the appartment - but apparently I'm not allowed to actually live there...so I wanna see if Fox 'n Thumper can set me up with some kinda way to stay with Brandon stateside and then get my stuff and a dual citizenship kinda thing goin'." "Then it's settled. And yes, we have room as long as you don't mind your choices being either sleeping on the couch or bunking with TJ. And, of course, hanging out with us and Choke - they wanted us to play a show with 'em tonight so of course we drove all night from New York to get here." Angel laughed at her wanderlust, "Do you need some metalheads to shake down your landlord?" "Choke...good people." After the momentary lapse of fandom, Amy's smile dimmed only slightly at the prospect of resting her injured ribs on a couch. Then again, what were her options? A couch and a group of people she actually enjoyed, or a warm bed and more time with her parents...the choice was obvious. "I'm not sure what BL'd think of me sharing a bed with another guy...so I'm thinking the couch sounds pretty inviting. Are you guys used to Canadian spring time yet? The weather changes about every fifteen minutes in Nova Scotia, I know..." "Trust me... those couches are much more comfortable than you'd think, long as you can deal with incessant music coming from the sound system," Angel shivered, pulling her leather jacket tighter around her, checking on MJ's bundlings as well. "But I guess you're used to that. Just tell me one thing... what are the odds of us gettin' snowed in here?" "You'd have a better time trying to get into the shower at my parents' house. God knows they'd love a bunch of 'my people' over there." An evil grin spread across Angel's face. "Think your pops would get along with a bunch'a degenerates who're making a decent living despite the fact that they're going against the grain?" "Well from what I've heard, if you take your dad and make him ten times as rude and egotistical, you've got mine." Amy cringed with another thought, "And something tells me his frequent jokes about homosexuality wouldn't be the biggest hit. What can I say? He's a fucking asshole." MJ seemed to be slowly drifting to sleep, so Aimz lowered the tone a little. "But yeah...let's AVOID that, my mom gave birth to a degenerate who's made a decent living despite going against the grain." She smiled, "So y'think the bus might be a little warmer here?" "I think your dad and my dad need to go bowling," said Angel as she took MJ back in her arms, "or if I've had a few drinks, listen to what really happened when I went back there last month. C'mon, though... the boys'll be happy to see you." Well...it was a decent first step. [Continue...]
