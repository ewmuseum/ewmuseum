Origins - [Busted] A Prologue

1 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
She would hear that sound, so clear, so sharp, for the rest of her life. It seemed like an all too perfect, yet so unwelcome layer of icing on the proverbial cake. The fact that this certain fWo Superstar had been fizzling out as of late was no secret. Hell, it was painfully obvious. Jade just wasn't sure that she cared anymore. What was the point, really? For months, she had been trying to establish herself to some degree. Her plateaued ascent could only be likened to walking up the wrong escalator. Lots of small, quick steps leading to absolutely nowhere. But, was that such a bad thing, really? Whenever Jade thought about it, she merely shrugged her shoulders and mustered a disinterested "meh." Did she even care about the fact that she didn't care? Up until Unleashed, she might have said no. Might have. That's when the direction on the escalator suddenly switched. The attendant somewhere jolted awake and realized that he had wired the thing all wrong. A few rusty wrenchings, cogs and sputtering gears...the machine did a complete one-eighty, finally clicking into place with one popping crack. Her arm throbbed dully. Warm waves of pain spread like some great undulating jelly through the marrow of the cracked bone. Jade wanted so badly to grab hold and rub the limb, but layers of hard plaster wouldn't let her come anywhere close. "Don't even think about it..." Jade warned herself as one would their young child. The harsh light coming from the small lamp next to her bed draped her scarred face with an overly-dramatic quality. She caught the odd atmosphere out of the corner of her eye in the mirror. For a second, she couldn't help but stare...not really sure why. As if it was aware of this, the arm screamed again, instantly breaking Jade from the distraction offered by a face she rarely looked at. What now? Here she sat, back propped against peeling wallpaper, on a stained mattress. The small matchbook in the round glass ash tray read "Motel 6." Another glorious night on the road. A half-drunk glass of cheap Bourbon sat next to it, sweating on the muggy end-of-summer night. It had been too syrupy, her head hurting too badly to finish. Everything usually looked much clearer through the bottom of a glass, but not tonight. For more than ten years, this had been her life. Wake up, lace the boots, bruise your knuckles, get drunk, fall asleep. It was her credo. Many criticized her for it, but what else was new? If nothing else, Jade always took pride in the fact that her interests were so narrowed, her mind that incredibly focused on what she wanted. For those ten years...no, her entire life, she had lived by that mantra. Fight...and keep on fighting. People could call it what they wanted, and make up their reasons for why she did it so...lovingly, but they would never understand the actual motivations...no, something more than motivations. It was breathing. But now...things were changing. The bottom of the glass was growing cloudy and the breaths were being slowly muffled and gagged. Jade could feel herself being slowly cut off from the respirator that had sustained her all of these years. All that it took for her to actually realize the thing that she had been grasping for in these last few months was one broken arm. Alone, prone and exposed to her own thoughts, with the throbbing bone keeping time, Jade sat in silence that night. No drugs, no bottles...not even a cigarette. It had been the first time in years that she would reach lucidity. It was also the first night that the Voice seemed to go to sleep. ~To be Continued~
