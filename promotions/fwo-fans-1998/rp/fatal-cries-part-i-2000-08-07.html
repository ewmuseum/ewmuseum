Fatal Cries, Part I

7 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History is everything. History is everything man lives off of. Man needs history so mistakes aren't made twice, so that he may only improve himself and others. History creates, it evolves it allows man to relive the past, as good or bad as it may be. Without history, man would be nothing. Man would be faltering at every turn, a complete downward spiral, never ending, leading off into the darkness. The unseen darkness, every man's fear. If man can see something, he will set goals to overcome what he sees, or work with what he sees to make everything better. But if man cannot see something, what is there to aim for? Nothing. When darkness shrouds man, he panics. His heart pounds faster, his breathing sporadic and unstable. He grows dizzy, unsure of what will happen next. Man is lost. He is lost forever in the darkness. Man will sit in this darkness, waiting for a light to come down upon him. A lost hope. The revelation that will never come. Every man waits for that one point in his life where he realizes that everything behind him has gone wrong. Man waits for his epiphany. If this epiphany never happens, man continues to do wrong. He never realizes where it all went sour. Will man, ever wonder what happened? Perhaps he will. Perhaps not. But when exactly did it all go wrong? It's all just a matter of opinion. Those are the types of questions with infinite answers. Man is neither right, nor wrong. His answers are justified by his means. And when man finally figures out what had gone wrong... what happens when he cannot correct what he had done in the past? Then, once again there is no light at the end of the tunnel. What man thought was a light was just a freight train bearing down on his helpless self. The train, going full tilt with no signs of slowing down, crushing man underneath the iron and steel. Man has killed man. How, you may ask? Man has created the train. The train has killed the man. Only, the man has somehow survived this wreckage. He can never truly even be a shadow of his former self. Perhaps he has been paralyzed by the power of the train. He can never walk again, or maybe he is just a vegetable, only able to move his tongue. Being fed through needles and countless tubes. Is this really a way for man to live? No, but it was his own undoing that caused this ‘tragedy.' Let's pretend that man had never been run down by the train. Instead he was still left in the darkness, all alone. Man is waiting for to die. He has nothing at all to live for, everything is gone, destroyed and battered. Being assaulted and abused both physically and mentally every single day, weaken man down to nothing. Man is now nothing. And nothing is exactly how he's feeling. He feels as if he's constantly falling. Falling into the unknown. He fears the unknown. His pulse and heart are beating out of control. He can't breathe, every time he gasps for air he chokes on nothing but the thick cloud of darkness. He hears voices that are breaking him down. The voices make him feel completely useless. He looks up, hoping to see anything but black. But there's nothing there. His hope is completely futile. Man is lost among the black waves of Society. The waves beat man around as a cat would play with a ball of yarn. The waves are the calm before the storm. He can feel himself being ripped apart, piece by piece until there is nothing left. Man has been broken down to mere atoms. The atoms are trying to split apart, making man just a faint memory. The memory that every other man has, but they can't quite put their finger on what it truly is. He has been killed from the inside. His heart once red and vibrant, now cold, black and motionless. His eyes are closed permanently, but through the eyelids they still strain and yearn to see the light. But, once again he can only see black. He waits to be brought to a better life. The better life will never come. Man is not completely dead yet. Brutally scarred on the inside, his outer shell continues to live. Man learns to hold back his feelings, hate, angst, love, joy. Instead man shows one face and the face is unemotional. A stoic, unbreakable stonewall. As immortal as man seems on the outside, on the inside he is crying for help. Man is just a lost little boy, starving on the streets, looking for salvation. These invisible tears are known as the Fatal Cries. The cries for help that no one hears, no one acknowledges or knows about. When man is alone the Fatal Cries become more and more apparent. The tears are filled with hatred, rage, angst, deceit and revenge. Man wishes he were dead now. He wants all of the pain to stop and go away. Because of this, he has a story. A story of: One man. One life. One chance. These are the Fatal Cries of the Fallen Angel.
