One year ago...

4 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One year ago, I was to be given the ball. One year ago, I was to be given the keys to the car that is the fWo. One year ago, I was to be called "The Man". One year ago, I ended up in a piece of shit BRITISH company, getting paid in fish and chips to give their shows some star power. I took part in the biggest show in that wanker company's history. Come to think of it, the butch company...the one with all the homosexuals without penises and the shows that air 48 hours after they're supposed to...it was their finest show ever. And they thought they had the upper hand... ...because of ME. Three companies, in the span of three months, looked to ME to give them credibility and star appeal. I walked out on two, in the biggest match in both of their histories. Why, you ask? Sheer boredom. It kind of sucks knowing you're the only person of talent in a ring, especially when there's 15 of people nobody has heard of before or seen since, and especially when they smell so much (after all...one team is British, the other is supposed to be brawlers. You think any of them have seen soap since...ok, so they've never seen soap, big deal). The other--the one that mattered--walked out on ME. Can you imagine that? Right when they were ready to FINALLY make the fans happy, bust out of their slump, and rally around the Icon...they dropped off the face of the planet, and told me to...go to England. Now, you have to find it interesting. Over the last year, I won my first title. I got my first cheers. And now, one year later...nothing has changed. The fWo is still littered with people would-be hobos, hari christnas, and, well, people that would probably be dead if it weren't for this idiot company that doesn't know when to say no to a contract. And yet, here I am...one of the biggest stars in the world...and they don't find the time to even put out a release saying I was even injured after I beat the piss out of Ric Chronos. So now, because of this company's ineptness...no, I won't make threats I have no intention of following up on. I could say I'll stalk every person in the company, and break them so they don't look at wrestling the same way ever again. The truth is...I find damn near everyone in the company boring. Why start something with them, something that will culminate at a pay-per-view, if it means that, for X amount of weeks, I have to deal with that idiot, or idiots? No, I won't do that. This year, I'm doing things my way. This year, I will do what I set out to do one year ago. I *will* be the Wrestler of 2003. The stage from last year is still set up. Only now, the curtain will go up. And everyone in the world will finally see why the Icon deserves the lead.
