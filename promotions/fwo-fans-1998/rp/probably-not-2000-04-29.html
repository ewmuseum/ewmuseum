fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Probably Not April 29, 2000 [TV-HC] It is 6AM on a Saturday morning and VL is standing outside of a Motel 6 in Salem, Oregon. Within 5 hours, she will be entering Seattle, Washington, the site of fWo's CyberSlam III. She has gotten some rest and taken a shower, but is ready to embark on a few more hours of driving. The clerk at the desk was suprised to see her leave so early, after having stayed at the motel since 10PM last night, but he may have been more suprised by the fact that she was departing the hotel wearing only a pair of black heels and a black towel, with a leather purse slung over her shoulder and a backpack on her back. She makes her way over to her jeep, throws her purse and backpack in the back, which is free from any hardcore wrestling equipment. She then stands in front of the back of the jeep and begins to talk. "I told y'all I wasn't finished. I just needed a little rest and some time to get my ass to the state of Oregon. I'm in the capital city, muthafuckaz. This is the same town where they passed them laws which basically banned wrestling from the state of Oregon. Well, them lawmakers can ban wrestling, but they can't ban VL from coming here. I'm the one bitch that can get hardcore without even wrestling. Neither of my two opponents tonight can say that. Fuck, neither of them two muthafuckaz even get hardcore while they IS wrestling." "But them Oregon muthafuckaz is smart, banning wrestling means that they banned the fWo, which means that the fWo loses money that they could make running in Oregon. There's a lot of country muthafuckaz out here, and any muthafucka knows that a country muthafucka is an automatic wrestling fan. So fuck the fWo and I hope every other state and country bans wrestling, so them muthafuckaz get shut down. I don't need wrestling. Wrestling needs me." "So what I'ma do for my grand finale is I'ma beat the shit out of both of you with your own weapons. Then, I'ma find your little kids and your wife and whatever in the crowd, and I'ma beat the shit out of them. Then, I'ma track down the rest of y'all extended family and beat the shit out of them. Hide your fuckin' valuables, primarily your lives, Snow, Jensen, cause VL's comin' over for dinner. When you step in the ring with VL, y'ass gets eaten alive." "In a few hours, when my ass gets to the shit city of Seattle, I'ma show y'all just how to be hardcore without even kicking some muthafuckaz ass. Bring your tissues." VL enters her jeep and drives away.
