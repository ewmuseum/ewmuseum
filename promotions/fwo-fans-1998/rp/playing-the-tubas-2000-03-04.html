Playing the Tubas

4 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization Playing the Tubas March 04, 2000 The scene opens in North Carolina on Jones Beach. FWO superstar Primetime is walking along the beach side as he spots Wretch sitting on the sand staring ahead at a bunch of boulders. As Primetime walks up to him, we see that Wretch is in actuallity holding a tuba. Wretch: Primetime, what are you doing in North Carolina? Primetime: Well I just so happened to be at the same place you are, at the same time! Isn't that amazing? Wretch: Quite. Primetime: Then why are you holding that tuba? Wretch: Because I play a little. Primetime: Whoa, how long have you been playing? Wretch: Since I was eight... I wanted to be really cool, and my Uncle Fred said tubas attract the ladies. Primetime: Don't I know it. So anyway, what are you doing out on the beach? Wretch: Beach? Oh this thing? I... hmmm... I don't really remember. Primetime: Oh, cool. Primetime notices that there is not a tag team title laying in the sand next to Wretch Primetime: So, I see that you lost the title. Wretch: Yeah, to the Guano Apes... they're pretty mean Wretch begins to blow into his tuba as seagulls fly off in different directions. Wretch: You want to here a song? Primetime: Sure. Wretch: You may already know it. Primetime: If I do... then I'll play too! Primetime hands the camera over to Jeremiah Frankton, who also just so happens to be there, and shows Wretch and the viewing public that he too is a master of the tuba. Wretch: Here it goes. Primetime awaits as Wretch begins to play the tune of "Twinkle Twinkle Little Star" Wretch takes one last puff, then sits back, about to pass out. Primetime: Aw, come on. A lot of tuba players know that one. How about this one?! Primetime begins to play "The ABC's", as Wretch looks on amazed. Primetime: Man, if I wasn't doing this wrestling thing... I could very well be selling theatres out nation wide. Wretch: Yeah, I know what you mean... Sometimes, late at night. I sit and debate whether or not I should just give up and spend my days jammin' Primetime nods, and begins to play a blues-type tune Primetime: Yeah... if I wasn't trying to be so serious all the time, I'd ask to be able to involve the tuba every once in awhile. But you know what they say... "People don't take kindly to guys who play the tuba." Wretch shakes his head sighly, and plays a blues-type tune as well Wretch: Yeah... it's a tough business, ain't it? Primetime: You got that right, brah. The two young superstars sit down, and sadly play their tubas as the screen fades to black.
