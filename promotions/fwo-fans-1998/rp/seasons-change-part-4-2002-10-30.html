Seasons Change (Part 4)

30 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
2:18 AM There was a slight chill in the October night, the kind that makes people turn their collars up and walk a bit faster to avoid it. But no matter how fast they tried to move or how well they bundled up... it was there, nipping at their ears and noses, whispering of the coming winter. Paul Adams stretched slightly and put the window of the rental car down far enough to let some of the cool air make its way into the vehicle. He had run out of coffee hours ago, and couldn't bear to sleep. Going back to the hotel was not an option for him at this point... instead, he sat alone in the car staring ahead, watching. Waiting for something to happen, but not quite sure what it was or what he'd do if he saw it. Across the street, the house stared back at him. Silent, and in its own state of slumber, it too waited... while neither side would make a move. The place was unwelcome to him, and always had been, but he now felt some need to protect it. 2 pieces of his life had burned away tonight, and he wasn't quite ready to watch this last tie to Boston disintegrate before his eyes. After all, now with his house in Colorado being little more than a smoldering husk, this house was all he had left... The air wasn't helping as much as he thought it would... and exhaustion was slowly beginning to take its toll on him. Paul stifled a yawn and shifted his seat a bit to stay comfortable, yet not too comfortable. It was then that he saw it, moving along the side of the house... creeping swiftly, silently, unseen by all... except for him. He thought he saw a hand move towards the side window... ...and he jumped from the car, being sure to grab the tire iron from the passenger seat on his way out. The Boston Strangler raced across the street and kicked the gate open, the slightly rusty hinges squealing in protest and heralding his arrival. He raised the tire iron high and felt his lip curl back into a primal snarl. Whatever that was, it wasn't getting him without a fight. Not this time... Ah, but if only there were something there to fight! Instead, he was greeted by the breeze filtering through the yard, and nothing more. "Perhaps it was a trick of the light, and exhaustion setting in..." he thought to himself. The Boston Strangler then turned to head back to the car... and heard a soft clink and smash of glass breaking as he nearly tripped over a bottle that had been carelessly left behind. However, the smell that assaulted his nostrils was familiar... gasoline. Slowly he bent down, dipping his finger into the mess of glass and fuel to pull forth a soaked rag. Curiously, he held it up to the dim light, noticing that it had some sort of writing on it... "Wo" It was a piece of an fWo banner. And had he allowed himself to believe it, he would swear he heard the wind quietly laughing.
