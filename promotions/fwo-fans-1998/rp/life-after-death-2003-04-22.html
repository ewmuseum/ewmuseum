Life After Death

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He was tired, beaten, and bruised. The year's biggest event had come and now gone. Everything was said and done, the smoke had all cleared, now that was all left was the life after cyberslam. *** Rahl sat down in his locker room that he shared with a couple of other fWo wrestlers as he iced his eye. Rahl's corner which was perfectly cleaned, but yet somehow dark and gloomy was all by its self, separating himself from the others. *** "See ya later Tim." One of the janitors who were cleaning the arena had exited the locker room, Rahl just shrugged it off. He had thought about that night's event. How could he have lost? He felt like his life was over, his one chance to shine, to stick out and get noticed he messed it up miserably. He let someone else take HIS spotlight, he couldn't stop thinking about earlier that night when Skylar Chryst approached him. Vivid shades of memories flashed through his mind as he remembered slightly conversations that him and Skylar had held. *** The question now was, what was left for Rahl at the fWo? What else could he try and accomplish only to set out and fail at it. Failure is all that Areumna'derin ever knew in his life. The fWo was now back in the states- for the time being. Rahl was finally allowed to wrestle again as he always forgot why he was sent home early. He would randomly look down at his arm and see nothing but a sea of scars across his arms from his days of abusing drugs. But they weren't days in the past, they were days in the present. He had allowed the drugs to take over his world, his mind, and his body. There was little anyone could do for Tim, for in his mind he believed he was Areumna'derin Rahl. Tim wanted to be loved, but Rahl wants respect. *** Where ever Rahl would go there would always be a dark feel to the scene. Anywhere he would go it didn't feel like a happy place. He felt like someone or something had taken control of the environment and everything that could possibly go wrong would go wrong. What would the future bring to Tim Veneno and Areumna'derin Rahl, if anything?
