Introducing... Oh, Wait, He's Gone?

23 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
...During the 5/20 edition of reAction... After he’d left the medical station, Max Danger went to his locker rom, changed into a fancy charcoal wool one-button window pane suit; olive suede vest; white jacquard shirt; olive python belt, and came out carrying his bag and Armsy McDanger. He was annoyed, agitated, tired, aching, and wearing an eye patch over the right eye that had not only been blackened by the Hardcore Champion Krow’s vicious right hand but also split open just above his eye brow when the action spilled to the floor. All of this in his debut, no less. “Skip. Skip. Skip. Skip.” Those were the words and actions of one Leigh Landers, as she came up behing the Marvelous One, hooked her arm into his, and said... well, she said nothing. It was the first she’d seen of his appearance. So, instead of whatever she was going to say, she said, “Ahoy, matey!” And she laughed. Max did not. “Why such a sourpuss?” Danger looked at Leigh with one of those ‘you’ve gotta be kidding’ looks and pointed at his covered-up eye with the prosthetic. “Oh.” Landers patted Max on the back, which would’ve been comforting if he’d not been still so sore from the beatdown he’d recieved. He winced and closed his eyes, letting the jab of pain subside before he re-opened them. He turned his entire body so he could see Leigh and said, “Where were you? I could’ve really used some help out there.” “Well...” began Leigh and she got that faraway look on her face which Max knew to mean only one thing: she was going to re-tell every single thing she’d done to the tinest detail. Danger put the finger of the fake limb to her lips. “Let me reprhase.” Leigh just smiled at Danger. She could make a Jew about to go in the gas chamber during World War II feel happy. Or want to get into the chamber faster. It could go either way, such was the power of Leigh Landers. “What were you doing right when I was being pummeled in the ring?” Landers smiled brightly and held up a laminated badge that hung around her neck like a backstage pass at a concert. It had her picture on it and the word “MANAGER”. “I was getting my manager’s license!” She hopped gleefully. “Neat-o-keen, right?” She was very proud of her badge and displayed it proudly before Max. He shook his head before pushing her hand (and thus the badge with it) back down. He took a deep breath before he said, “I’m afraid you’ve wasted your time.” With those words, Leight remained where she stood and her face went like this :(. As Max walked away from Leigh and headed for the back exit, he was intercepted with these words, “If you wanna go, go.” Ivy McGinnis always seemed to have her hands full these days and this time was no different. Rumors had circulated the backstage halls of Key Arena in a hurry about the new guy’s departure. “You're not a rookie, Max - you know how things go. They rarely happen exactly the way you want 'em to." Danger was standing, arms-crossed, not really wanting a lecture at that moment. What he wanted was to hop on an airplane, got back home, climb into his bed, and be cared for by his beautiful wife Jessica. “I could tell how much you love this sport, but if you really wanna take your purse and go home just because your debut was a little off... Well, you ain't the only one. A lotta boys've come and gone just as quick. Some better than you..." Max rolled his eyes, which thankfully for his sake, Ivy chose to ignore, otherwise he might have gotten smacked across the mouth for disrespect. “...some not, but the FWO is bigger than you, or me, or any of 'em. If you're gonna stick, I'd like you to think - really think about what you're lookin' to do while you're here." Ivy looked the man in front of her right in the eye. He was a professional who’d been in the business for thirteen years. He might have been a rookie to the FWO but he wasn’t to wrestling. “Do you really want tonight to be it? People think of Max Danger in the FWO, they think of an afterthought, the other guy that Krow went through while he tried to find meaning in his title? I'd hope you have more self - respect than that; maybe I'm wrong.” Max looked down at the ground and playfully kicked at some imaginary dirt on the floor. “But, despite popular opinion to the contrary, I'm not your mother. I'm not even officially your boss, so I'm gonna leave it there. Hopefully you'll be here next week.” Danger took a deep breath and looked Ivy straight in the eye. After a long pause, he said, “We’ll see.” Ivy stood, with her arms folded, and looked upon the Marvelous One, before he nodded his head and continued on his way, exiting the building, and hopping on that plane out of Seattle.
