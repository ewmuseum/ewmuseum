Justice is Blind

4 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It’s a long silent walk down this hallway. My ankle chains clank together as I walk, my hands bound by handcuffs at my waist. They’re taking me to another holding cell as I await trial most likely. The phrase “you’re fucked” immediately comes to mind. “You’re fucked Shadows” said the fat balding corrections officer. The thought of giving him a head butt comes to mind, but I’ll pass for now. Perhaps being on my best behavior will cut me a break, so I simply say nothing and continue my slow walk to what is likely death row. How did I get to this point? How did I let this man Krow get me to a mental state that I never thought would be possible again? “Stop. Face the wall.” It’s the usual routine in this joint. I walk to door and they tell me to stop. Next, they’ll tell me to walk inside and stop. Then it’ll be turn around and walk to the door, followed by put my hands through the door hatch so they can undo my chains. Watch. “Step inside Shadows. Stop.” The sound of steel on steel is all I hear while looking at the white empty wall in front of me. “Turn around and place your hands through the door.” He takes off my chains and I rub my wrists. They haven’t put me in general population yet because of my celebrity status which I guess is a blessing. I don’t feel like going in the hole because I beat some child rapist up nearly to death just yet. Prison is lonely and suicide has become a natural thought at this point. Wonder how I could pull that off in here, hmm. “WHAT!? Are you kidding me? That piece of shit walks after what he did?” Interesting, suddenly my attention is drawn to the yelling just outside my door. “This is such bullshit! Shadows…” I close my eyes and look up. I can foreshadow the words about to be spoken. “I don’t know how but every god damn charge against you has been dropped. You’re a man who will be amazingly walking out of here…free, in a few hours.” I smile and he smacks the door with his night stick. “What’s so god damn funny Shadows?” “It just is amazing, as you point out, that I will be walking out of here a free man. Just in time really, I was thinking of a way to hang myself if I had to be in here much longer.” The officer appears to be visibly angry, and I wouldn’t blame him. “I highly advise you to still do that, fuckin’ shit bag.” Oh, how I’d love to crush his silly little skull right now. “Now now officer, you have every right to be upset that the system has flawed you…but not me. Justice is blind sir, while you just want blind justice it would seem. “ I can’t help but ask “so tell me, who shall I thank for this kind gesture of freedom? Hmm?” “You wouldn’t believe me if I told you. Sally, bring ‘em in.” He turns around and looks at me and flashes a smile which doesn’t irk me…but that sarcastic tone does. “You two play nice now Shadows, you have a visitor.” I think I just felt my mouth drop to the floor. He’s right, wouldn’t have believed him if he told me.
