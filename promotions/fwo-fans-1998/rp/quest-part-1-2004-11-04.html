Quest - Part 1

4 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With heavy eyes, Kodiak Vic Creed looked down at his watch. He still had two hours before his flight left for Anchorage. The best way he could think of to kill the time was to drink the hours away. Ignoring the people clamoring around him, Vic was in his own little world as he sipped his whiskey press. In Vic's world, there was no Light. Once upon a time there had been a beautiful ray on sunshine, but almost a year ago that light was extinguished. It had been snuffed out, buried, and left to rot. When the light disappeared, everything that was Good and Pure fled into the Night and hid in the Darkness. But it couldn't hide for long. The Beast found every hiding place and devoured everything it found. It wasn't long before all that was left was the Beast and the Bodies. A cold wind moved through the blackness and, as the Bodies crumbled into dust, blew everything away. The Beast laughed and disappeared as well. Vic was left with only an empty void. For a time, he wandered through the emptiness. The numbness wouldn't leave, and Vic forgot what it was like to feel anything. He decided to try and fill the void with companionship. Even a case of the Crabs didn't help his emotional apathy. Liquor, drugs, nothing seemed to work. His workouts suffered. He suffered, he knew he suffered, yet felt nothing. The voices seemed to echo in the abyss when they asked "Who are you?" and "What have you become?" He turned and drifted in the direction of the sound. "Do you remember who you are?" I know who I am. I know what I am. "We'll see." Blood rushed into Vic's head as he fell. His plummet lasted for a minute before landing in a huge hill of snow. The white mound trembled before exploding, leaving only a cold Kodiak Vic Creed shivering in the crater left behind. There was a bright grayness surrounding him, and the crunch of snow underfoot told him that he was moving. What the Hell is this? "Hey Pops," Alex Creed said as he stepped out of the fog. "Pretty cold in here." Where is this? What are you doing here? "I think we're hunting." Alex stood beside his father. "Age before beauty." Like crumbling hard plaster, Vic felt parts of his face break away as he smiled. The intercom announced the departure of yet another flight. More people fleeing the scene. Vic looked at the bottom of the empty glass and slid it over to the bartender. "Do that again, and this time put some liquor in it."
