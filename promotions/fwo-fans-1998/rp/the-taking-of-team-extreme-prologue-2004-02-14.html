The Taking of Team Extreme - Prologue

14 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Taking of Team Extreme Being What Happened, between Then and Now Prologue: Now. So why did I do it? That's been the question on everyone's mind. I'm a wrestler. I'm the King of Extreme. I'm a violent motherfucker. But when you think about it, why does anyone do anything? To make a difference? To right a wrong? To make a buck? To change the world? Why did I drop Crisp like a third-rate trophy wife? Because he was there. Because he was closest to the ropes, and therefore the most convenient. Because he was the end result of a disease that's spread through the wrestling business for years - the ascension and praise of a badass as a hero. And a cheap imitation of a badass, to boot. For some reason, the people cheered me for the things I did. We'll see if they want to keep doing it when I've taken the rest of their badasses and twisted their necks. If the heroes think things are fine the way they are, I'll make them listen. Some people will probably wonder about this choice. Some people will probably think I'm wrong, or I'm doing this for selfish reasons, or any number of things. I had to sit back and watch Team Extreme be taken from me. That prompted the choice. And you never know whether a choice is right or wrong, until you can look back on them. And if I had to do it all over again, I'd do the exact same thing. That makes me a bastard. So be it.
