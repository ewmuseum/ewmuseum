Unleashed

6 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I just don't understand some people. I'm Kodiak Vic Creed, record setter with every real title in the fWo (I don't count the Cruiserweight as a Real Title, since it only exists because small people can't hang with the big dogs). I've spent a better part of my fWo career with a title of some kind around my waist. That says it all right there. I come out and tell people how good I am, then I prove it. I put asses in the seats. People know I do what I want, when I want. I'm unpredictable, and people eat that crap up. So who does Chris Universal think he is by chewing me out for being too rough on the talent? He was on my as soon as I walked back through the curtains after my match in Worcester. I expected him to give me his two cents after I stomped the crap out of BQ the way I did. I wasn't disappointed. "We've got a lot of house shows yet," he told me. "Take it easy out there. People are going to get hurt." Well Chris, it's not my fault that we're doing all these damn house shows, is it? You want someone to thank for getting us off TV, talk to Chronos. And as for me being too rough in the ring... bite me, fanboy. This is wrestling. I get paid to beat people up. If they can't take it, they shouldn't get in the damn ring with me. I don't play.
