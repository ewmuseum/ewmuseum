My day in Yankland: 10:00

10 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
...fffffzzzzz... - 10:00 - "Fucking this... I think it's working now." Muttered Borst. You couldn't quite see him though. Just a big blur of his t-shirt as he moved the camcorder around. "Aha. It screws on the tripod. Clever." The camera was now stationary, but only about three feet of the floor. Borst had obviously failed to work out how to make the tripod legs longer, so he lay down on the floor and faced the camera. "Hello viewers. You may or may not have seen me go ape shit last night, due to the inconvenience of winning a piece of shit." Borst held the US Title up in front of the camera for a second before tossing it out of sight. "But I'm not letting it get me down too much." Borst rubbed his beard and thought for a moment before he continued. "I've not had a lot of fun over the past few months... you see, I've tried this wrestling stuff, and you probably guessed, trying to show up the industry as being a complete sham, make the fWo look bad, losing, blah blah blah, but I've come to realise that everyone already knows the wrestling industry is complete shit and no-one cares either." Borst raised his right forefinger and nodded intelligently. "However, a week or so ago, I remembered how fun it is to hit people for real instead of pretending when I hit... er..." Borst rifled through his pocket for a moment and pulled out a scrap of paper. He squinted for a moment and read out the name. "Eli Flair. I don't remember hitting a woman... they must have had the wrong name on TV or something. And then last night, I had even more fun. I decided to experiment a little with just hurting people, let my hair down, run wild, and it was a blast. Seeing all these new faces, lots of people with girly hair... ahem, but I did not, however, realise that this big smack-a-thon that I joined in was in fact for the US Title." "I'm sure you all know how I feel about the sceptic already, but I'm not giving up on this thing yet. I enjoyed myself last night, and I'd really like to kick people's heads in on a regular basis, so rather than sack it all in, I'm using this here camcorder today to undertake some research into yank culture. See what makes these fascinating creates tick." "And then, on Wednesday Whatsit, I'll probably repeat what I've just said, and report back with my findings on yank culture. I shall also let you all know what I'm going to do with this..." Borst looked around for a moment but quickly gave up. "With that, wherever it is, piece of shit."
