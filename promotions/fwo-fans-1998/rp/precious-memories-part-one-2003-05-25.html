Precious Memories ~ Part One

25 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Back to 1995. The day had been unseasonably cool. An August day with temperatures somewhere around 45. That was almost unheard of, even in Philadelphia. It was the perfect day to curl up beneath a warm blanket with a mug of soup...or punch things until your knuckles bled. It depended on your preferences. Jade, only on the verge of her nineteenth year, sat in a squalid alley between the Shipman Press building and Smithtown Enterprises. Neither building had had any tenants since the 1970's. It was the perfect place for squatters and homeless bums to congregate. In fact, many of them were there on that afternoon...most huddled together, passing around a fifth of whiskey to keep warm. A young Jade wanted nothing to do with them. They had offered her the bottle twice already, and both times she had growled her refusal. She had her own, after all. She sat about fifty feet away from them, right in the back corner of the alley, her back pressed against a chain-link fence. Five empty beer bottles were scattered around her body. Jade had been there all afternoon. There was nowhere else to go. Certainly not home. Home. That was a place she hadn't been to in literally years. There was no reason to ever go back there. ~*Local man found dead in his own home...Police say that the cause of death is still undetermined*~ The sweet burn of the whiskey ran down her throat, racing to get to her stomach. It was one of her favorite sensations in the entire world. The only thing that could compete was the sensation of driving her fist into tenderized flesh. Simply orgasmic. Jade trembled a bit at the thought. "Are you sure you don't want some of mine? Its pretty strong shit...make ya' forget about the cold out here." The old man spoke up from beneath a tattered gray blanket. He thrust a bottle out and shook it in Jade's direction. Her gaze snapped over to the old alcoholic. It caused him to recoil back. There was something in her eyes that could make just about anyone stop dead in his tracks. Behind those eyes was something clearly not human. It was feral, but at the same time...more than human. There were and still are no words to describe it. Jade slowly rose to her feet. ~*...What is notable about this is that the victim was a severe alcoholic. Authorities speculate that Mr. Hunter could have ingested at least three times the legal limit last night, along with other substances*~ She stood only a couple of feet away from the old man. Looking down on him, Jade saw that the bottle was still in his hand. "So, you're finally gonna take me up on the offer, huh?" He was so drunkenly oblivious to the signs. The young woman "wasn't quite right" as one of his friends would later put it to the local newspaper. Poor old guy never saw it coming, nor ever suspected it. "Something like that." Said the younger Jade. With the other hand, she reached out and snatched the whiskey bottle from his hand. She took a massive pull from the bottle and wiped her mouth off with the back of her hand. It looked like she was about to walk away and return to her little spot in the corner. But, with an amazing speed, she whipped around and absolutely shattered the bottle over the old man's head. Glass shards flew everywhere as thin trails of blood fountained into the air. There was a collective gasp among the other bums and derelicts. "Thanks for the whiskey, asshole." ~*There was some blood spatter, but we can't say that it came from the deceased at this time*~ Jade then spat on the old bum. He wasn't dead...just unconscious. A swarm of his friends and acquaintances swarmed around him. Others jumped from their dirty mattresses and approached Jade. She would remember it feeling like being in the middle of a pack of stampeding wildebeests. They circled in around her, screaming at the tops of their lungs. "What the fuck is wrong with you, crazy bitch!?" "You're gonna pay!" "That was Frank's best whiskey!" "I'm gonna gut you!" She knew that she couldn't back up anymore. The fence was getting closer and closer. This was going to be bad. There were too many of them for her to fend off. Jade wouldn't have had a problem if there were only three or four, but there were probably seven or eight men closing in on her. One of them was brandishing a box cutter and another had one of the larger shards of broken glass. Jade had nothing but her hands, feet and knowledge of fighting to help her out of this one. At that moment she had regretted skipping out on last week's session with that Bill Randolph guy. The bum with the glass shard lunged at her, but Jade was able to trip him and take the glass away from him. She was about to jab it between his shoulder blades when she got clobbered from behind. The blow caused her to lose her balance and she stumbled forward into the fence. The shard of glass fell from her hand and shattered into pieces too small to be of any use. Pressed against the fence with nowhere to go, all that Jade could do was try and land some good blows. And she did. A couple of the bums had been rendered incapacitated by some of her vicious lefts. Even at that age, Jade was her present height of six feet even. Her muscular development was not anything close to its present condition. Still, she knew how to use what she had. Just as the second bum fell to the ground, the one clutching the box cutter got to her side. He grabbed her by the neck and slashed at her left eye. The blade missed her eye by a small margin. When she felt the cut, she turned her head and managed to kick the bum in the stomach. The blade sliced all the way down her cheek as he fell to the ground. There was no pain. Only the iron taste in her mouth from the pouring blood and a release which she had never felt before. Jade felt...alive. As she was reveling momentarily in this new awakening, the rest of the group jumped on her all at once. Fists and boots pummeled her to the ground and Jada Marie Hunter just laughed. It was all over in a matter of one minute. The bums had no intention of killing her. They just wanted to teach her a lesson. On the ground, Jade was laughing as the blood poured from the side of her face and her mouth. Her sides hurt and she could already feel the bruises forming. A couple of her ribs were most definitely broken. Despite all of that, Jade got up to her knees. ~*Police have not released any suspects at this point. They are reluctant to call it a homicide.*~ "You better stay down if you know what's good for you, girlie." Snarled one of the undesirables. He had no front teeth. The teen-aged girl looked more like a war casualty than a teen-aged girl as she kneeled before the congregation of her batterers. A disturbing trill of laughter still clung to her words. "When your friend over there wakes up...make sure you give him my thanks." A confused murmur rippled through the crowd. They knew that this girl wasn't sorry for what she did and definitely didn't learn any kind of lesson. But, that was to be expected from Jade. They had all heard of her reputation. Yeah, she had quite the reputation that preceded her. About all that they could have accomplished had been done. There was no sense in keeping her around for another pummeling. The collective of bums and winos allowed her to walk away that afternoon. Jade got to her feet after some struggle and smoothed the wrinkles out of her green pants. Blood splattered onto them from her nose and mouth. In fact, blood trailed her each and every motion. She barely noticed. Such a thing was typical by this time. Without giving a second glance to any of the homeless, including the man whom she had rendered unconscious, Jade strode out of that alley. She never noticed the horrified gasps of the people whom she passed on the street.
