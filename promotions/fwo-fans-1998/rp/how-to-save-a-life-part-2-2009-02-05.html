How to Save a Life: Part 2

5 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"If you ask me--" "That's why I'm here, Fox." "--you shouldn't go." Ivy raised an eyebrow at her former mentor. She sat on the bed in his room while he sat at the desk, looking through her collection of still photography from the most recent reAction. "Why not?" she asked, "We survived a tour through a warzone before, Fox. Did you forget that?" Silver Fox shook his head. "No matter how hard I've tried. And you've evidently forgotten the three stitches in your head and hairline fracture on your skull from the suicide bomber in Tel Aviv." Instinctively, Ivy's hand went to her forehead. "This is different, Fox. We'll be on a military base. We'll be in the safest place in the country. I really think William has his head on straight with this one." Fox handed the stills back to Ivy. "But this is the FWO. Don't ever forget that." "What's that supposed to mean?" Fox stood up, and slowly walked to the window. He had a room on the outer wall of the building, so he had a fairly expansive view. "You saw, firsthand, the kind of chaos that can erupt in that company," replied Fox, his mind racing to answer Ivy's question without answering it, "at both Countdowns." "No, you meant something specific," said Ivy, as she stood up to join him. She leaned against the wall next to the window and looked at him, "What's the deal?" Slow and steady, said Fox to himself. "Isn't that enough? I haven't had an active hand in professional wrestling for more than five years; I don't have the stomach for it anymore." He looked at her. "I admire you, though - you do." "Then why are you so adamant thatI don't go?" Fox leaned against the opposite side of the window and looked at her. "As pedantic as it sounds, you're the closest thing I've ever had to a daughter, and I care a lot about your safety, and I just have a bad feeling about this one." Ivy smiled and hugged him. "You're just being paranoid. This is a new FWO, Fox." She stopped for a second, "After we get back, why don't you come to a show?" He shook his head. "No, thank you, Ivy... but my place is here." "There's still a world out there that you could contribute to, Fox. You'd be a genuine asset. But beyond that, you're my friend and I'd like to show you a good time." Silver Fox stepped toward Ivy and hugged her. "Good to see you, hun. I hope you're right about the overseas tour, and I look forward to hearing about it when you get back." It was clearly a dismissal. "Okay, Fox," replied Ivy, as she hugged him back, "I surely will." Ivy left the room, more confused than ever.
