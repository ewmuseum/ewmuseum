Realization - I Need You.

17 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The night had been unpleasant to him... it was spent tossing and turning, a rare case of insomnia if he ever had one. Sad thing was, when his body finally did allow him a moment of rest, he could barely sleep in peace. He had seen him in his dream's... and, it was torturing him. He replayed their last conversation over and over in his mind, until he began to believe whole- heartedly that everything that had went on between the two was his own fault. He sat up... eyes baggy... hair a mess... face and chest full of sweat... his heart pounding at a pace that could best be described as irregular. He began to cry... and, no... it wasn't the one tear and done, that he had participated in the past few days, for the sake of public appearance... he began to let it all out. Tears were running down his cheek at a rapid pace, his noise, filled with snot... while he tried his best to calm himself. But, the more he thought about it... the worse it got. Using his sheets, he began to wipe his face... until finally... he stopped. He got out of his bed, and dialed ten digits on his hotel phone... he had to talk to... somebody. And, while Amy Campbell was a nice girl with tons of good advice, and somebody he had considered a friend. He needed something more. And, there was only one person that came to mind. "Hello," he began. "Ivy... did I wake you?" Poison Ivy was asleep... barely. She hadn't heard a thing from Stevens since she saw him last
