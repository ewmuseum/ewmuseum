Somewhere I Belong - Better Than You (Five)

19 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With short, tidy chestnut hair, Beverly Campbell was exceptionally pretty and well-aged for fifty three. But somehow, she never ceased to look old and weary upon catching sight of her youngest and most unruly daughter standing in the doorway. It wasn't any different that night. Amy'd shown up in the middle of a rain storm at around 10, not long after more than a few sips of cinnamon schnapps and almost an entire bottle of NyQuil had convinced her to allow herself to make this gesture. At about midnight, she was just getting around to doing so... Sitting in her parents' living room, little brother having been sent to play on his computer, the redhead was trying to look proud regardless of the fact that she was half drunk and not exactly awake thanks to flu medication. Her dad had only criticised her minimally tonight, which had come as an incredible surprise, but that stance of his would most likely be dropped after the following words: "I need a place to stay for a few days." "A wha--" Her father was quickly cut off. "Absolutely, sweetie." Amy and her dad looked surprisesd - both at the fact that the helping hand had been extended. If Forrester had his way, she'd be on the sidewalk to live - no child of his would shun what their future should've been just to be some delinquet (Read: Wrestler/Guitar player/Artist/All-around fiery spirit) and then come crawling back for a place to live. But with the stern glare of his wife, he too tried to look accepting for once. "Anytime." It was bullshit. The smiles said that they thought they'd "won" the struggle...but she couldn't refuse the acceptance whether or not it was genuine - there was no other choice but to play along nicely until she could talk to Brandon or think of something. Oh well... So she 'moved in'. Brought a bag and slept for a night in a comfortable bed... By dusk the next day, everybody was insane and, naturally, her parents were starting to leak a few 'opinions'. She had no idea where else to go, or what the hell to do or who to talk to, but her 'home', as usual, wasn't where she belonged - her family made that apparent. ...So Amy escaped all over again in hopes of catching up to her real family...
