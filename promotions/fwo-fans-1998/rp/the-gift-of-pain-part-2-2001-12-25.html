The Gift of Pain - Part 2

25 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bruno smiled as he watched his girlfriend kneeling in front of the Christmas tree. She was positively glowing as she admired the diamond necklace he bought for her. She scrambled to her feet and rushed to kiss him. They embraced tightly, pulling each other close. Ah, it was good to be bad. As Darryl Vishanti's head enforcer, Bruno was well paid for his services. It worked out well, since Bruno really enjoyed his job. There was nothing better than being paid to work out and break people's bones. Bruno had earned a ferocious reputation, and it was a hard-won one. Bruno saw it as more of a public service: he was only hurting people that had wronged his employer, usually by breaking their word to him. If a person couldn't live up to their word, they deserved to be hurt. "Oh Bruno," Missy said with a smile. "This has been the best Christmas ever." "I'm not complaining," Bruno replied. "Besides, you've been a good girl. Santa does right by good girls." They kissed again. Missy watched the diamonds sparkle in the sunlight as Bruno got to his feet. Time to get the newspaper and settle down for a nice breakfast. Bruno tied his bathrobe tight and walked to the front door. He had a gorgeous girl in the living room, housekeeper cooking up breakfast in the kitchen, and newspaper on the front step. Plus, with the nearest neighbor about five miles away, he had all the privacy he ever wanted. Life was good. Bruno opened the front door and bent down to pick up his newspaper. For a brief moment, his attention was held by the shoes on his front step. The huge man standing in those shoes quickly had Bruno's full attention as his leather gloved fist crashed into the enforcer's face, crushing his nose with a cinderblock-like blow. With his eyes watering, Bruno was blinded as more blows hammered his face. Before he knew what was happening, he was being dragged down his driveway by his ponytail, blood bubbling from his nose and mouth. "Beautiful day, isn't it?" The big man asked. "You're dead," Bruno spat. "Do you know who you're messing with?" Bruno was dropped at the end of the driveway. As he rolled onto his back, a heavy foot stomped down on his chest, quickly repositioning itself on his throat. His eyes bulged, but at last he could look his attacker in the face. Kodiak Vic Creed kept his foot on Bruno's throat as he opened the trunk of his car. "Yeah Bruno, I have a pretty good idea who I'm messing with. I'm messing with the guy who didn't have the guts to face me man to man. I'm messing with the guy who tried to cripple my sister." Bruno gouged at Vic's calf, punching it with all his might. The foot finally slid off his throat, and Bruno rolled away. "I did what I had to do," he said between coughs. "I'd do it again." Creed pulled out a large length of chain. "No, you won't be doing it again." He began wrapping the length of chain around his fist. "Ever again." Bruno turned and looked at the house. Twenty feet to safety. Twenty feet to his twelve gauge shotgun, .45 Magnum, and many other weapons of destruction. But that twenty feet may as well have been a mile. He began scrambling up the driveway, his bare legs slipping in the freshly fallen snow. He struggled for grip, fought to get to his feet. Fifteen feet now. He was going to teach Creed a lesson he'd never forget
