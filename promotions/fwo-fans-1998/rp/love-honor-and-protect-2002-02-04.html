Love, honor and... protect?

4 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Are you happy now? ARE YOU!?" Brand stares ahead. "Answer me Damn it! Are you fucking happy?" Brand stares ahead. "It's your entire fault you know that right? Don't get high and mighty and tell me that he was doing what he wanted. You know he looked... looks... up to you. You think he wanted to play bodyguard for you just so you could play hero? You think he put his family on hold just to "fight the good fight"?" "He's in there right now tied down and breathing through a machine because you asked him to protect your family. Isn't that your job? Isn't that what all of this is supposed to be about? Getting back at that monster for what he did was all we were supposed to do. You lied to me. Look at me, damn you!" Brand stares ahead. "What would have happened if he really wanted to get to the three of us Brandon? What he did to Chris was nothing. He sat right next to Sean and you did nothing! The old Brandon Roche would have stomped him. Remember what you did to Lords? Remember!? You didn't use Chris then. Remember what you did to that monster? You didn't need him then either. Why this time?" "Was it that you were too starstruck from being in the fWo? Why won't you answer me?" Ana reaches out in the heat of the moment and makes contact with Brand's face. As if a jolt of electricity surged through his body he shot up from his sitting position to be within inches of Ana. Startled by his sudden movement she flinches a bit, but like the fighter she is she resumes her position staring at the Paragon. "What now Brandon? We sit back and preach about how we are better than him? We play all high and mighty while my brother fights to breath? We talk about the never ending battle while Sean is afraid to go to sleep because of that monster? This is just like before. Just like it always is. Why do we have to always pay the price? When will it be time for us? When will it be our time to..." Harshly, Brand interrupts. "Now! Now is our time...OK? I screwed up. I let them get close again. I let the demons in. Our time begins next week. I'm going to Slaughter and I'm ending it. Ending everything. They will both be there. Both the monsters will pay!" Every vein on Brand's body seems to be on fire as the rage in his system has boiled over. "They wanted to jerk me around fine. They decided to use my family 'gainst me though. That was their first damn mistake. You know what? You're right Ana. It ends." Staring at one another a link that was forged by the word of God seems to weaken a little bit. Nothing too much, just a little, unnoticeable to the two it is happening to. Is it too late to make the bonds stronger and move beyond this most dreadful ordeal? Time will tell...
