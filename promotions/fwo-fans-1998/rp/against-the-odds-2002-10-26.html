Against the odds.

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is where it all began. Brandon looks around Tobo's training facility. The stark white walls decorated with red satin relief painting reflect the light from the three round skylights in the ceiling. It adds a mysterious glow to the entirety of the room. The large bag in the corner hangs resolute, almost challenging someone to try and move it's heavy frame. Brandon remembers the first day at that bag. His feeble muscles propelled his open hands against the bag in rapid succession. Powder flew from his taped hands but the bag bounced back his attack upon him. Ah, to be seven again. Tobo promised him that someday he would master the heavy bag. He also told him that it didn't matter the size you were, it only mattered your determination. Later in life it was obvious that God did gift Brandon Roche with that size but he also allowed him the determination to match. Never once has he faltered. Brandon always knew the right direction and the right things to do. Some of the choices were tough but that was OK. Tough was good. Now though he questions even the most simple of actions. For instance it had taken him a good half hour to decide to come back here this day and look around. He knew Ana would be mad that he had left but this is something he needed, a recharging of the batteries so to speak. As Brandon walks through the empty dojo he makes his way to the sparring room. Padded weapons and the like adorn one of the walls and the massive warrior reaches over and retrieves a short pair of nunchaku. Absently he twirls them around, under the arm, over the back, around the waist. The repeated pattern makes him smile as he recalls the first day he and his best friend Chris ever fought each other. It was in this room and they were both 14. Chris lunged at him with a sectional staff and the wire in the far staff reached its limit. That caused it to snap at Brandon, he brought his shin up to block it and as he spun to the left for an attack Chris easily followed with a counter spin that locked his legs together and sent him crashing to the mat. A bloody nose later and Brandon was defeated. Chris always did get the better of him. He really missed those sparring sessions. That wouldn't happen anymore though, thanks to the Boston Strangler. Brandon's nostrils flared and his jaw tightened at the mere thought of that one. With a bit of anger fueling his actions the pair of nunchaku hurled to the far wall before landing on the padded mat with a clump. Chris still lay in the hospital. Closer to home though thanks to a transfer and a speedy flight by the Uva. Pegasus Unit. Chris was still in a bad way. Critical but stable. If there was something to be punctured, torn, ripped or bruised it was done to him that night. He had regained consciousness though, even managing a nice conversation with Brandon's sons. That helped them a lot he knew. Even with his mouth wired shut he made sure the kids could hear him. He wanted them to know he would be OK. This all just seemed surreal. Not two months ago Brandon lost his strength when his father passed away, now this was happening. Where was the fairness in that? What else could possibly go wrong now? Hadn't the people around him sacrificed enough for him to be able to do what he is good at without being punished by someone? He knew there was no real answer. He did know that because of the past few weeks things were strained at best between him and his earthborn angel. He knew that she was not in favor of his coming to the fWo in the first place. Now though with the events perpetrated by the Boston Strangler and those others she was not happy at all. Nothing but personal attacks had been done and he was tired of it too! If the Strangler wanted to play it this way then so be it. If he wanted to make a point, it was made. If he wanted to scare Brand Frontier away... he failed! Brandon wouldn't walk away. He couldn't. He wasn't made that way and that was fine by him. It was just everyone else that had to understand that. Even Chris told him not to feel guilty. Even Chris told him to take the Strangler out and make him pay. The other could wait. This was now. He was right too. It was about doing what's right and there was no way in hell that The Paragon would sit around and let bastards like Boston Strangler run wild. He didn't think so. This was taking a toll on his family though. Ana blamed him for what happened to her brother. She was fuming mad and they hadn't really talked since the day after it happened. Did she realize that he didn't do this on purpose? Did she know that he loved Chris as a brother and felt terrible that this had happened? She had even heard Chris say it wasn't his fault. She had heard him through clenched teeth telling Frontier to make him pay and to stand up for them. To defend them. She had heard all of that but did she believe it? Maybe in her mind Chris was just trying to be the big brother again and not show the pain and hurt that he felt over being used as a roadblock between The Paragon's family and his enemies. Maybe she thought that Brandon just used her brother and now that he was literally clinging to life by a thread he would be discarded amongst the confusion. He didn't know and that bothered him. For so long Anastasia and Brandon had been on the same page. For so long they were a united front against anything that threatened their family or their ideals. This time though, this time it had struck Ana in the heart and it might have proven too much even for her. She didn't show that she believed in her husband any more, it didn't show that she would always be there for him. That is what worried him the most. Brandon has gotten used to fighting against the odds. He has been forged of fire and pitted against some of the most evil people to walk the earth, yet he has never been more scared. What would all of this have accomplished if one of the prizes he was fighting to earn walked away? That was one of the reasons he was here. He needed to talk to someone who seemingly had all the answers. He had to talk to Master Tobo. Yet he wasn't here. He was always here. Now though Brandon walked through the empty hall and realized that in fact maybe he was more alone now then he had ever been. Against the odds can one man, even a purported paragon, turn back the tides...alone?
