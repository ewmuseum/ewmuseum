Reflections

6 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Okay, so what happened to me? I sat there, on the Fourth, watching the fireworks over Triangle Park from the roof of the gym. Nicole sat there with me, huddled up on the folding canvas... couch would be generous, but it was fold-up camping furniture. So the two-spot loveseat we had put on the roof to watch the fireworks go off. We'd both taken this weekend off. I'd opted to exercise a day off from my fWo contract. She'd watched the little girls from her dance class in one of the events that went on at something like noon that were part of the parade. Now? Now we sat here, drinking whatever we had and watching the fireworks. But my mind kept wandering. What had happened? I was the fWo Hardcore champion. I'd had everything in control until it came time to scrap with Gianni. I'd made mistakes. He'd capitalized and I hadn't stopped him. I just didn't do what I'd needed to. Why not? I took a long slug from bottle of Diet Pepsi in my hand. Nicole was enjoying her own drink, but she had wanted a Long Island Tea tonight. I hardly ever drink alcohol anymore. A couple of fights in college that had come about because someone that may have been me, but often was someone else, had gotten a little too much liquid courage in them, had really soured the whole thing. I drank sometimes, a beer here or there, but nothing major. So what was it? Why had Gianni gotten me? Why hadn't I kept on the ball? Ideas popped off in my skull, one after another with each exploding munition in the sky. Was it that I wasn't prepared? The wrong frame of mind? Was the Hardcore environment really not suited to me? These thoughts kept coming up and I didn't think any of them really fit. That is, until a huge gold blast went off and it all clicked in my skull. I hadn't even considered it, but I had been facing a very narrow range of opponents. Ever since my bouts with Sphere and Cheno, I hadn't really taken on new challenges in the Hardcore realm, and it had cost me against Gianni. The drive to regain the title was there. I had held it once, taken it off a man that had held it for seven or eight months. That was no small feat, and I was worthy of holding that belt. But I was NOT ready yet. And there was no way I'd get that shot until I proved that I was. The fWo had accepted me, true, but I hadn't made any kind of name for myself yet. In my time, I've been a frontrunner in various places, but in fWo, I was back to being a small fish. So I'm faced with building real credibility again. I've got to start pulling down wins against real warriors in the ring and I've got to prove myself against the nastiest the fWo has. After I do that... maybe, just maybe, I'll be ready.
