Untouchable // Part.5b // Feigned Sanity

10 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kenny smiled as he saw the receptionist. He walked over to her, and she began to speak: "Mr. Rock, Dr. Roberts will see you now," said the Receptionist, a middle aged woman who appeared to only be doing this job because it was the best job she could get. Kenny nodded, and entered the large office of one Doctor James Roberts, State Psychiatrist. The office was very small, yet gave off the impression of being an office of psychiatry. Perhaps it was the pale green that painted the walls, or perhaps it was the fact that there was the 'staple' of a Psychiatrist's office: the pendulum, that rested merrily on the table. Kenny preoccupied himself by mumbling his innermost thoughts to himself and sifting his thumbs around in a circular motion. Dr. Roberts smiled. Kenny saw Dr. Roberts as a pitiful excuse of a man, noting his small frame. He wore black-rimmed glasses, and a cheap suit, as he sat in his 'power chair.' Amused by Dr. Roberts' appearance, Kenny's mind wanders, not caring so much about what Dr. Roberts thought of him, but more about earning a clean bill of health from this psychiatrist so that he can bestow upon Douglas the ultimate revenge: pAIN. "Please, Mr. Rock, have a seat," said the Psychiatrist, motioning to the chair across from him. Kenny reassured Dr. Roberts of his feigned sanity by smiling at him, and took a seat the way men of royalty would, based on what he saw in movies. "Now, Mr. Rock, do you know why you are here?" asked Dr. Roberts to Kenny Rock. Kenny shook his head, and he responded: "No, Dr. Roberts, I don't know," said Kenny, knowing the true purpose of this encounter even before Steven Douglas was apprehended for attempted murder. Dr. Roberts then placed a folder on the table, relatively thick in its height. "This..." began Dr. Roberts, "is Mr. Douglas's Psychiatric records. You see, Mr. Rock, as you may or may not know, Mr. Douglas was a former patient at a Psychiatric facility." Kenny nodded, again to reassure Dr. Roberts of his feigned sanity. "Now, it appears he suffered at the time from severe psychosis, violent tendencies and perhaps schizophrenia. He was committed to the Kings Park Psychiatric Center at Kings Park, New York following a Championship Wrestling League Pay Per View entitled 'Revolution'. He spent several weeks at the facility, allegedly receiving the help he needed. However, it appears he has relapsed," said Dr. Roberts sternly, as Kenny nodded once more, feigning concern. "Now, I wish to perform a interview with to find out how exactly the strangulation attempt was reached, and if he will require a recommitment to a psychiatric facility. Then, I will interview Mr. Douglas and determine fully if such options are required, rather than imprisonment. Do you mind?" asked Dr. Roberts, and Kenny shook his head. "Not at all, doctor, not at all. Anything to help my friend..." said Kenny, trying his best to hide his smile from the doctor's view. Kenny chuckled, then tried to mask his elation with phony coughs. He was relieved. Initially, he had thought that the purpose of the Psychiatrist interview was to determine his sanity, not Douglas. This was going to be fun.
