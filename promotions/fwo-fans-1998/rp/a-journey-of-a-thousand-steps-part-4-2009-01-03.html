A journey of a thousand steps, Part 4

3 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~Third Interlude~~~ April 22, 2001 The FWO was live in the TD Waterhouse Centre in Orlando, FL for Sunday Slaughter. It was the Sunday before CyberSlam, and revenge was on Mike Bear's mind. Two weeks before, Mike had leveraged his eventual CyberSlam opponent Mitch Wilson into signing over his Wilson Training Academies to an intermediary, which were then passed along to Mike's father, Steven “Grizzly” Bear, an old and bitter rival of Mitch's father Slade Wilson. As payback for the deceit, Mitch and Slade had attacked Steven and used weight-lifting equipment to shatter the ulna and radius bones in his right arm. Mike had been on a rampage through Central Florida searching to Mitch, and it all came to a head backstage, mere moments before the main event: There was something different about the air backstage. Workers could sense it, though they couldn't pinpoint when it changed. Still, it was clear. *** Mitch Wilson stalked the backstage halls, a man alone in his hate. He hadn't even been in contact with A.S.S. recently, such was the depth of his anger. At this moment, he was a man ground down to his most fundamental essence. At this moment, Mitch Wilson was a killing machine. That and nothing more. *** Mike Bear stalked the backstage halls, a man alone in his psychosis. He had been under the watchful eye of Wretch since arriving at the arena, Wretch, the man who was trying to keep him from hurting himself more than from hurting others. But Wretch had had to leave for his match, and Bear was left to his own devices. His own madness. At this moment, he was a man ground down to his most fundamental essence. At this moment, Mike Bear was a full-fledged psychopath. That and nothing more. *** Mitch Wilson turned the corner. *** Mike Bear turned the corner. *** They almost ran into each other, they were paying so little attention to their surroundings. Both men looked up at the same time, each a man lost in his own personal Hell. "Bear," growled Wilson. "Wilson," growled Bear. They each took a step back and stared at each other, like two gunslingers. One could almost expect to hear the theme from "The Good, The Bad, and The Ugly" in the background. But nothing so silly happened. Mike Bear, finally confronted with the man he had been searching for, simply stared. Mitch Wilson, finally confronted with the man he had been searching for, simply stared. It was Mitch who broke the silence. "You just don't get it, do you, Bear? It's not about your father... oh no. It's about you... and me. You want to know why you sicken me, Bear? Because you're a waste. You could've been somebody... you could've been me. You have all the tools... you just don't know how to use them. Because you have a conscience. A conscience what the hell use is that in the real world? No use, that's what. And that's why I succeed I care about myself above all else. You have compassion, you have feelings... not me. I simply dispense pain to those who get in my way. You could've been that, Bear. You could've been me. But you're nothing more than a waste." Bear stared. Spoke. In a whisper. Barely audible. "Mitch... I may have a conscience... but not... when it comes... to... YOU!" Bear screamed the final word and swung at Wilson, who expertly dodged his blow. Wilson lashed out with a strike of his own, which Bear blocked. Bear struck, and Wilson blocked that. They traded a series of blows, each one blocking the other's attacks. Finally, Wilson backed off, but the two continued to circle each other. "I see you like to dance, Mike... isn't that what's it all about in the end? The dance? And the pain? And nothing else. That's exactly what it's all ABOUT!" Mitch lashed out at Bear, but Bear ducked. Mitch fist slammed into the wall, and Mitch tried to jerk it free. No dice. He was stuck. Bear smiled an insane smile. "Get ready, Mitchy... you're about to get screwed!" With that, Bear reached into his tights and whipped out... his drill. He switched it on. It made a high, keening, buzzing sound in the hallway. He pointed it at Wilson. "Come on, Bear," said Wilson, with a bizarre smile on his face. "Do it, Bear... do it and stop being a waste. Do it. Take your drill! Use it. As a wise man once said, strike me down with all of your hatred and your journey towards the dark side will be complete!" *WHACK!* This was the sound of Mike Bear being hit with a chair. Strangely enough, Bear didn't go down, but simply stood in place, not moving, as if the chairshot had severed some critical link between his brain and his body. "I'm sorry I had to do that to you, Mike, but it was for your own good," said Wretch. "Yeah, for his own good! Big freaking deal, Kidman. Big freaking " *WHACK!* "You," Wretch said, gesturing with the chair to Wilson, "I'm not sorry I had to do that to." Wilson slumped to the ground, his hand finally freeing itself from the wall. He was bleeding, and some pooled on the floor by his head. Wretch tossed the chair aside and turned back to Bear. He had yet to move. "Mike? C'mon, Mike. You in there?" Wretch waved a hand in front of Bear's face. Nothing. "Shit, Mike... I have to get to the ring for the main event. I'll be right back, though... count on it. Don't, uh... go anywhere." Wretch ran off, confident that, though Mike appeared to be in bad shape, he'd come out of it ok. Wretch had seen Bear take a lot of punishment in their years as teammates and rivals. 10 minutes passed, the battle royale main event was still underway. Mike had yet to move. It was at this moment that Mike's girlfriend Brittany finally caught up to him after weeks of following his path of destruction: Despair. Loneliness. Helplessness. You could go on and on about the feelings running through the head of Brittany Chambers. It had been 2 weeks since she had even seen Mike. Sure when he was on the road she had gone longer than that. Much longer. But things seemed so much different now. Could it have been their talking about...no, she wasn't going to let herself think about that and start crying again, especially not here where anyone on the roster could see her. She wasn't... As she turned the corner, she caught sight of someone who looked remarkably like Mike about 30 yards down the hall. She couldn't believe it. Was he really here? Could it be? She started running towards him. “Mike?” As she got closer she realized it had to be Mike. Tears of joy were in her eyes as she called out to him. “Mike!” She ran around him to confirm what she already knew. “Oh my God!” She hugged him as if he would float away were she not there to hold him. She cried into his chest. And all the while, Mike maintained the same blank stare. Showed no recognition that the love of his life is even there. Brittany still hadn’t noticed. “Oh God Mike I was so worried. I don't know why but I was afraid I'd never see you again. I'm just so glad you're ok and I know that you're angry about what happened to your father but we'll get through this together, right?” An uncomfortable silence passed until Brittany stepped away from Mike. She looked at the face that didn't recognizing her presence. And she remembered the words of the man she's looking at. ****Flashback to December 9, 2000.**** Mike and Brittany had just been on a night out and Geoff Green had told Mike he wanted him to catch some recent promos for reaction shots, and the first one had been some punk who had called Mike whipped. Mike had sat there seething with rage, not acknowledging Brittany's presence when she was right in front of him, and after Brittany had brought him our of that trance, Mike said something that stuck in her head. “If I EVER do anything like that again, I want you to hit me. Hit me as hard as you can to knock some sense into me, because without you, I'd be still be sitting in a mental institution somewhere, screaming about metal screws and wolves, hopeless and alone.” “Mike I...” “Promise me!” “Ok Mike, ok. I will.” **** Well, she was a woman a woman of her word... WHAM Brittany sent a blow right into Mike's jaw. Luckily for Mike there was no crack, but that didn't affect that fact that he was sent into the wall hard by the force of the blow, or the fact that there was a blood stain on the white wall thanks to the wound Brittany opened in Mike's mouth. Mike went down in a heap. Like a lifeless doll. Brittany stood there in disbelief. Tears of sadness, of fear now stung her cheeks. “Mike?” She knelt down, looking at the glazed over look in his eyes. She checked for breathing and a pulse, both there, yet it was as if Mike himself wasn't. “MIKE!” Brittany shook Mike, trying to get a response out of him. Still nothing. Despair. Loneliness. Helplessness. They threatened to overtake her again. She ran off to get help. And Mike just sat there, staring off at nothing. Seconds later, after Brittany had left the corridor, fWo Cameraman Geoff Green turned the corner to find his friend Mike crumpled in a heap, bleeding from the mouth. “Mike? MIKE?” Geoff knelt down and checked for a response, finding none, he whipped out his cell phone. “Corridor 3 in the north corner of the arena, we've got an emergency.” ~~~End Third Interlude~~~
