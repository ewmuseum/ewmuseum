The other side of the King of Extreme

2 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Oh, sweet mother of mercy... if you stop that, I'll kill you." The threat to Eli Flair's life was not the first he's heard. He's been threatened by fans, wrestlers, fighters, and an ages- old rival with a gun. But this particular threat, was the one he took most seriously. "I wouldn't dream of it, Angel." Eli's wife, Angel, was lying facedown on their bed, eyes closed, dark circles that were not due to makeup under them. A black T-shirt was lying on the floor, as was a pair of vinyl pants. She lie there, dressed only in a pair of Eli's boxers, as he gave her back a vicious rubdown. "Twenty two goddamn hours in the studio," sighed Angel, "and we only got two songs done. We can rip through these songs nightly... it's so damned frustrating! Mmmm....." Her frustration was headed off at the pass by Eli's technique. He was working her shoulders over vigorously with his battle-scarred hands. "Well, they're mixing now, right? That gives you, how long off?" "A week," replied Angel, "It's the standard sanity pact. I don't enter the studio while Nine and Teej are mixing and in return, they get to live. Of course, by that definition, so do I." Eli chuckled. Angel's impatience for the technical end of the recording process had become legend amongst their small circle of associates. For a singer who typically nailed her vocal takes by number two at the latest, the tedious studio toys drove her crazy. "How'd little A deal with it?" "MJ? You kidding?" laughed Angel, "She's got a little room all to herself at the studio, she's the only one of us who got a decent amount of sleep. And a decent meal, to boot." Eli stopped rubbing. "Are you hungry?" "I'm tired, more than anything," explained Angel, "I feel like I could sleep for a week, and I'm strongly thinkin' about it." She curled up with her grandmother's quilt - the one item of sentimental value she took with her from her past - and her body language told Eli to curl next to her. He did so, putting one massive arm over her body and held her tight. "Why don't you come to the show on Monday?" he asked. Angel's head perked up a bit. "Are you sure? I know there's a lot going on this time of year." "Yeah, the build to 'Slam is pretty tight," agreed Eli, "but after last week's brawl I don't feel too bad about takin' a week off t'heal. Besides, you wanted to meet Craig Miles, didn't you?" She giggled. "Yes, because he's the perfect person to meet MJ. Seriously - Ivy told me how tense things are with Deacon... are you sure I wouldn't be in the way?" He didn't answer immediately - he simply kissed the back of her neck. More to the point, he kissed the back of her neck for a good four to five minutes. "I'm pretty sure," he finally said. Angel sighed - the type of sigh that gets the person who caused it out of several years' worth of purgatory. "What did I ever do to deserve you?" she asked, her eyes closed and her voice sounding half asleep, "You put up with my mood swings, you rub my back, you hold me just right... whatever's out there watchin' over me, man... made up for my teenaged years in spades." "Shhhh," said Eli, "no talking. Just close your eyes..." He stroked her hair with his free hand. "Listen to my voice, nice 'n soft..." The regularity of her breathing told him that she'd finally fallen asleep. Eli gently pulled the quilt away from her chest and covered her entire body with it, before he lay down again next to her, and held her close. Every day, it seemed, this woman found a new way to completely captivate him. All it took was the sound of her breathing, or the way in which she looked completely at peace... and he fell in love with her all over again.
