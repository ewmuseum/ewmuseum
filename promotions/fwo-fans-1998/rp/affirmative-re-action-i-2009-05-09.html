Affirmative re-Action I

9 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The bitter taste of defeat wasn’t completely removed from their mouths but the sweet taste of victory went a long way to help. Once they hit the back there was a sentiment of celebration. Junious would hear nothing of it, as of tonight the pecking order within the group was changing. Slaughter, his cousin and Big O had come to far now. “Let’s pop them bottles and get the hoes up in here!” Roland boasted prancing about the room looking for his i-Phone. “Hold up Ro…” June was serious. “Look here man, we got love fo’ you. No doubt there but homie this shit ain’t free ride. We just showed everyone out there tonight we can compete with these mothafuckas cuz. It’s time to step our game up.” “So no mo’ bitches?” Roland questioned very disappointed. “an’ no mo’ Wine?! I dunno bout this…” “Fuck dat Ro,” CJ was never one to shy away from his opinion.”So what if Junie’s lost his damn mind… we still gonna’ have fun cuz.” Big O again made his presence known, “Yeah.” “All yall listen up, yall wanna compete? Name the last black fWo tag team champions? Better yet how many BLACK fWo World Champions there been?” June questioned seriously. “Tag teams? I don’t know cuz.” CJ answered. “I think Ross was champion…” June Bug inserted, “One black World Champion, Ross back in 01… 01 man! You mean to tell me no BLACK man has been worth of being fWo Champion?! What the fuck this look like?! A white man’s paradise! That’s what!” The jovial mood of victory had left the building with Elvis. The merry band of delinquents was under new, more serious, management. Big O and CJ started pulling their street cloths out of their bags. Junious was first in the shower as usual. With the ensuing quiet Roland sat on the bench waiting and thinking. He thought about the roads they’ve traveled in the indies. He thought about the stunts he’s helped his crew successfully get away with. He knew things had to be different. Times changed, and he accomplished his original goal. Affirmative Action was signed, sealed and delivered into ‘the big time’. There was a lot that could be said for that. He got to see his boys capture their first win on this stage. Even more could be said for that. The more he thought the more he knew that eventually times had to change. It happens in every company, every business in every economy across the country. The guys who get you there aren’t the ones who need to be in charge to keep you there. At least with Affirmative Action it wasn’t a new manager being brought in to rally the troops. Junious walked out of the shower with a towel wrapped around him, CJ and O each quickly took off with their towels and toiletries looking to be next in the shower. Their skirmish over ‘next’ was quickly drowned out when June Bug was about to pass Roland. “Hold up,” Roland muttered. “Look man I’m in. If it’s you callin’ the shots… me at least it’s us ya’feel me?” “As far as the world’s concerned ain’t nuttin different Ro,” Junious added as he started dressing. “See Ro, you’s an institution unto yo’self. An’ Affirmative Action will always need that ‘guidence and direction’ on getting unda peoples skins.” SNAP! “Fuck! O get outta here! I won nigga!” CJ’s voice rang out from the shower. “You ain’t posed to be in here! JUNE BUG!” “Damnit! O this ain’t back in the joint nigga! You can’t just be strong armin’ showas nigga!” Junious shouted running back into the stall. Times like this made Roland extremely happy he never had a reason to be undressed around Big O. Roland’s record running AA in fWo, 0-1. June’s record 1-0. Maybe it was time for a change…
