The Story Thus Far - III

20 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thursday, June 4th, 1998 Jordan Copeland had been interning at the fairly new Kiss 92 FM radio station in downtown Toronto for just a few weeks. The station had opened just a few months earlier, and they were bringing kids in fresh out of University intern for just above minimum wage. They would listen and watch the hosts up close, pass out mail, sweep the floors, basically pay their dues in the radio business until the brass decided they were ready and deserved to be given a role as a co-host on one of their late night slots following the show of Kiss 92's sex guru, Dr. Love, whose show usually garnered the lowest amount of listeners. As a lead in, it was poor, but being on the air with little to no boundaries would give some of the younger disc jockeys the experience needed to host a morning show or an early evening timeslot. Jordan wanted desperately to host a radio show, it had only been a few months since he had graduated the University of Toronto with his degree in broadcasting, and he wanted to get started. He knew that he had the charisma, and the talent to be ten times more entertaining than the people that were on the air. Shabazz entered the studio cautiously with the fan mail for the shock jock radio show host: Tarzan Dan. He acted immature, stupid, and horribly unfunny on the air, and that wasn't too far away from his real personality. Jordan knew, from firsthand experience, that Dan liked to make fun of the interns on air, it gave him the feeling of being better than everybody. Jordan went into the studio pushing the mail cart. He neared Dan before reaching into the cart to get Dan's fan mial. Dan was on the air, which was a rarity for Jordan since he usually delivered the mail while Dan was on commercial break smoking a cigarette (which was illegal in the building) or drinking a cop of coffee. The only one other time had Jordan delivered the mail to Dan while he was on the air, and Jordan got made fun of while he did it. He wasn't in the mood for any type of BS today, but he knew that Dan would give it to him. As Jordan dropped a box full of mail on the desk in front of Dan, Dan began to laugh. "Ladies and gentlemen, the world's ugliest intern just entered my studio, and for some queer reason, he's avoiding eye contact with me. I think he's trying not to fall in love with me. Because ladies and gentlemen, not only is this intern ugly, but he's a queer. He's an ugly queer!" Dan began to laugh at his own horrible joke. Jordan Copeland didn't want to hear it, he shoved the mail cart out of his way and he grabbed Dan by his cheap flannel shirt. "Don't talk to me like that," Jordan said as calmly as an angry man possibly could. "I'm a human being and an employee of this company, I think I deserve to be treated fairly like any of your other co-workers." Jordan moved a few inches away from Dan and held an intense glare. Dan pressed the 'panic' button that he carried with him in case he ever got hassled in public by an overzealous fan who'd seen his picture on the internet. Dan looked around and laughed nervously, "Ladies and gentlemen, we'll be back right after this brief commercial break." Dan pressed the button that led to the commercial. He saw security coming around the corner at an intense pace. "You'll never work in radio in your lifetime, buddy," Dan said like an evil movie director with an insubordinate actor. Jordan knew he was going to be fired. Out of frustration, Jordan shoved Dan back into his seat. Dan fell backwards and rolled, head over heels. He came to a violent stop when his head slammed violently against the glass wall that allowed people to look in from the hallway into the studio. Tarzan Dan's eyes rolled into the back of his head as security barged through the door and tackled Jordan Copeland. One of them radioed the call into the police station and requested an ambulance and two officers be sent to the Kiss 92 headquarters, which was just a few minutes away from both the St. Joseph's hospital, and the police station. Jordan Copeland was under arrest.
