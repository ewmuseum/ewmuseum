Get in the Van: First Stop

13 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Here, this should do it." TJ tossed an icepack to his best friend. She caught it in midair and held it to the side of her head, drawing attention away from the notebook in her lap and the headphones in her ear. The bus moved swiftly through the night, far away from a small club in Boise where things... happened. "Thanks," replied Angel, "Did Mickey get through to Ivy?" TJ shook his head as he sat down on the opposite couch. "Ivy's phone was off - my guess is that she's taking everyone's advice and taking a day off." Despite herself, Angel laughed at the thought of Ivy *not* working. Quickly, however... she softened. TJ saw the shift in her mood and backed off a bit. "Mickey's awake, I'll be up hangin' with Troy, just let us know if you need something." She smiled as he gave her knee a gentle squeeze and headed toward the front of the bus. Angel put down the icepack as she pulled off the headphones - revealing a black eye and a small but pronounced bruise on her cheek, not to mention swollen knuckles. She crossed the bus and knelt down next to the playpen tethered to the side - Mariella was having trouble sleeping the past few days but thankfully, her eyes are closed and she's breathing gently. GOD she's changed so much... she's beautiful. Angel stood up and ran her hands around the waist of her pajama pants, picking her notebook back up off the floor. Tonight, to put it mildly, was a fucking disaster. The notebook is unfamiliar. Blank. Angel kissed MJ's cheek and returned to her chair, her knees curled up near her chin. She pulled her headphones back on and opened up the book to the first page, pen in hand. I've been raped more painfully than anything I can imagine, and I certainly imagine quite a bit. Tonight's show was easily one of the worst I've ever been a part of. We loaded in without incident, the owner of the club let us wash up in his loft above the place before the show, let me take MJ out for a bit. Nice guy. TJ and Nine headed out to find something to eat, while the rest of us did soundcheck and scoped out the place. I should've known there was a problem the moment TFG got there, primadonna wannabe industrial rockers with a much higher impression of themselves than they had any right. It was their town, so they assumed they'd be headlining, but in all honesty, they've got nothing on us. Not that it mattered. The biggest bouncer on staff had a massive TFG tattoo on his arm, and told me point blank that he didn't like us or respect us. He said that it was a mockery of underground music that Garden had one fan, let alone the sheer number that had been supporting us on this tour. Point blank, he told me that if I got into any trouble, with a laugh, I was on my own. Immediately, I was terrified, but I wasn't going to back down. I had a show to play and I'll be damned if I'm gonna be intimidated out of it. TFG sucked as per usual, but their entire set was one long cheapshot on us, all of Moxie's in- between chatter was about how we'd had everything handed to us, how we were robbing the fans of a full set from TFG and how we needed to just "go back to LA" because we didn't belong there. It was the first overly- hostile crowd that we had experienced since before the Misfits experience. Since then, we've played plenty of shows where nobody was into us, where nobody reacted to anything we sang, but a local band had gotten them so riled up that they were actually trying to make us crack. Apparently, wearing black eye makeup and a dress onstage makes me a whore. Or just being a woman in an industrial band that rocked harder than their local boys. A group of jocky- looking guys were fucking around with TJ from the get- go. They were saying, "Get off the stage before we break your fingers, fag," and other things that I couldn't believe. How can this be 2003 and have so many people still so hate- filled? We tried to keep things mellow, playing mostly older stuff that wasn't as anger- filled as the stuff from the Scarred album, but it was a lost cause. These people were spitting on us, throwing trash at us, and when Mickey threatened to end the show if one more piece of garbage flew up, the show was over. We kicked into 'Dirty' and I was in the middle of the second verse when one of the jocks right up by the stage grabbed my ankle and tried to pull me off into the crowd. The bouncers couldn't care less, my buddy was right in the center and actually moved over to give the jock easier access. I cracked him in the head with my mic stand and finished the song, just in time to see the cops show up. The promoter had been watching from the side and saw me get grabbed and the bouncer doing jack all about it, and called the cops because he knew this was a ticking time bomb. They shut the show down and we tried to load up the bus as fast as we could to get out, and that's when they showed up. This girl and about nine guys surrounded us near the side of the bus and started talking some shit about not getting their money's worth, about how we were overrated and not worth a shit. There were six of us to ten of them, but I was afraid for my daughter inside and what they might do to her when the girl took a swing at me. She was apparently the girlfriend of the guy I busted open onstage, and she wanted her pound of flesh. I'm still not sure what kind of guts it takes for two large men to beat the shit out of a small, thin girl who's just trying to defend herself, but the cops were still out front and broke things up. They took a look at TJ and at me and warned us to not come back again. I said no problem, we know when we're not wanted somewhere, and we loaded up the rest of our junk. That's when I noticed my bag was missing. My bag contained my phone, my address book, my wallet, all my identification, all my lyrical scraps and my former journal. My hope is that they took my money and threw out everything else. The thought of someone reading through my most intimate thoughts in my journal and the artistic translation of them in my lyrics is nearly enough to bring on a panic attack. It's nights like tonight that make me realize that there are some audiences that don't want to be reached. There are some places where outsiders are simply not welcome. But this is the measure of a singer. This is the test of a frontwoman, how to deal with the shows that don't go how you hope. That's what I think, at least. I've taken everything that's been thrown at me so far and turned it into an art form, turned it into a way to get rid of all the shit that's rolling around inside my head. But even now as I'm writing I feel the same way I did the last time I saw my parents, and I know that we have to make it, if for no other reason than poetic justice against everyone who said we didn't have a chance. Angel closed the notebook and pulled the blanket around her bare arms. Soon, she was fast asleep.
