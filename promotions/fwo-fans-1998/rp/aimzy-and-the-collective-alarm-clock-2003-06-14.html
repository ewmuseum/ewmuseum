Aimzy and The Collective Alarm Clock

14 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*THUMP* "Hey!" *THUMP* "Amy!" *THUMP* "Wake Up!" Eli leaned against the wall of the hotel hallway and laughed. They'd played this game day after day, week after week. Poison Ivy continued to knock on the door, but there was virtually no sound of movement on the inside. "It's a hopeless cause," said Eli, "She's never risen voluntarily before three. Even when we've got ridiculous amounts of driving to do." *THUMP* "Five seconds, Campbell," warned Ivy, "We've got three hundred miles to get through today and we ain't gonna stop for food if you make us late!" Could it be... movement behind the door? A first groan, followed closely by another preceeded the lock clicking open by a solid thirty seconds, but the redhead soon emerged - finding Eli and Ivy dressed to entirely contrast her flanel pants and miles-too-large, longsleeved Cold shirt. "Nnngh......I'm sleeping in the car..." Eli laughed again. "A lil' too much fun at the beer festival? Looks like someone slept through their hangover to find that it didn't go away like they'd hoped." He leaned into the room just a bit. "Are y'even packed?" Amy raised a finger - a rather select finger - and turned around, trudging back into the room. Seconds later, a duffel bag flew past Eli's head. "Am now." She re-appeared, grumbling. "Did Brandon seem a little less pissed about me skipping off for beer fest this morning?" "I think so," said Ivy, "He had to get on the road early with Chris and D, but he called me at like seven thirty to make sure you were taken care of." "Never mind that you don't drive," said Eli, as he started to walk down the hall, "so he called me, too. He's alright, kiddo... just some stressful times lately." The redhead nodded, "Yep...and these last few months have been hell. He hates that I'm working hardcore stuff...that's fine, but he doesn't trust Kinkade at all - which would be okay, if he didn't get upset every time I left his sight." Ivy looked, as usual, like she was on the verge of having something constructive to say, but Amy continued instead - grinning at the Psycho Bitch as Eli strode slightly ahead of them, mind most-likely centered around the Angel he was missing at the moment... "I'm feeling pretty good about it, though...he just doesn't know I can handle Kellen. It's my lucky time of year, so there's nothing much to worry about...hell, Meltdown's like a week after my birthday - what could possibly happen?" "Famous last words," cautioned Ivy, "Someday--" She put her arm around Amy's shoulders in a 'big sister' kind of way. "--when you're older--" Amy glared at her. "--I'll tell you some stories of what could possibly happen." "Well God forbid I'm someday as close to my old age pension as some people." Aimz nudged Ivy with a sharp elbow to the ribs. "Call it being niave, but I know Kinkade pretty well...plus, who am I to expect the worst?" She winked an oncoming statement, "And to think, I didn't even need you to teach me not to tip toe around anybody, regardless of how dangerous they - or even others - think they are." "Besides," Amy continued, "I might be playing with fire in Kellen Kinkade, but you don't see me buddyin' up to Vic Creid, acting like I don't remember what he and Brandon's ex-skank did to him." Even Eli turned his head to her, raising an eyebrow as the redhead celebrated her imparted 'wisdom', "Yeah, now who's the preacher?! Mmmhmm, that's right: Booyaka, baybee!" "Tiptoeing is one thing," replied Eli, walking backwards now to face the girls, "but I'm not stupid enough to go up against someone like Kinkade with busted ribs." "Yes you are," replied Ivy, without a seconds hesitation, "You're just not smart enough to admit to anyone that you're actually hurt." He laughed, "And as far as Creed's concerned... it's a lot easier to work with a man if you can have a drink with him." "Apparently, you can't," replied Ivy. "Hey now," Amy started, pausing from her strut. "I admitted when I was hurting, just not how badly...at least, until I bled from the mouth...that sorta sucked......." She pouted, folded her arms and continued walking slowly beside the King of Extreme, her head angled up to glare at him - there was a point to be made...somewhere...somehow..........in a galaxy far, far away. "And I'll have you know, Kellen's weaseling out of matches with me now...I've got him reeling backwards, I don't see you doing anything but getting drunk with a sick bastard. Now, I might have the business side to this a bit skewered, but usually I choose not to make friends with weirdos - no offense, Ivy - even if I have to work with them." "You'll corner him," assured Eli, "if you can even walk straight after your own night of beer-B-Qing. And don't get on me about Creed. Brandon's problems with him are Brandon's problems with him. Might understand 'em a bit better if he ever... y'know... talked about it. But that's his business." Ivy rolled her eyes. "Enough with the fighting. I'm hungry." "There's no fighting," Amy grunted, rubbing away the headache beating her temples, "I'm just saying, Eli's gotta watch you and Angel especially...Vic's not the most professional guy a lotta the time." Before either could speak or scold her, Aimz groaned. "And I'm hungry already. This is what happens when people wake me up early...so whatcha' buying for breakfast?" "Apples. Lots of apples. So many apples," continued Ivy, "you'll think your at a festival." The Hardcore champion grew up in the 'Apple Capital of the World'...she'd also been made to attend an 'Apple Blossom Festival' every May of her life. She was not amused, and Ivy's grin gave away the deliberate joke from miles off. Amy's raised eyebrows weren't so jovial, "I should really, really hurt you for that........" She glanced up at Eli, who was in the midst of a failed attempt to supress laughter..."You know where I'm from, you should know how many ways I can kill a man with an apple...don't mess with this." As Amy passed both of them by, muttering something about "Going to KFC if it kills her," Eli and Ivy looked at each other. "...Now that's hardcore," said Ivy. [To the constantly wonderful Peter Russo...without you...well...this funny little pile of nonsense wouldn't have been written, but thank you for SO much more than that. Here's to the next three months, years, decades and eternity after it.]
