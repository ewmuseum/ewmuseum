Chasing The Dream - Part 9

16 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
May 23rd 1999- 4:52 AM Milo walked aimlessly in the darkness. It had been almost five days since he had left Earl and Dorothy, and his travels certainly hadn't gotten any easier. Four days of failed hitchhiking attempts meant that he had been walking for what seemed like an eternity. "Fuck I'm thirsty". Milo stopped and climbed into a near by ditch, cupping his hands and drinking the slightly discolored water which lined the bottom of the ditch. Although the days were plagued with fastidiousness, he was aware that making this long journey alive would be a task in itself. Milo walked down the shoulder of the almost empty highway, too fatigued to think about anything but the road beneath him. His set his focus on a distant road sign, which grew slightly larger every few steps. "Calgary 20 Mi" Milo muttered, as the sign came into focus. He continued to stumble through the night, each step hurting more then the last. After what seemed like hours, the light of a car shone from behind him casting a blanket of light over his body and surroundings. It may have been mental exhaustion, but for a moment it felt as if the light was of Alien origin. But reality set in as the car sped by, its taillights eventually fading into the distance. The zombie like staggering drug on, as Milo made his way down the shoulder of the road. After what seemed an eternity of walking, Milo gazed down at his watch to realize only 20 minutes had passed. This annoyed him so much, that he unfastened the watch from around his wrist, and cast it into the ditch. "Time is cursed time, when one wishes for it to pass". Step-by-step his body grew more and more fatigued and the journey continued to ravage both body and mind. The pain was agonizing, but the anguish he was feeling only seemed to further fuel his desire to live a dream. Eventually, the sun began to peek over the horizon and break the veil of darnkess which cast itself over everything. The sound of an automobile was heard, and Milo extended a thumb. The car passed Milo but the driver slowed and came to a stop about 50 feet in front of where he was standing. He was in an endless amount of pain, but he dug deep and somehow found the strength to run to the car. The young female who was pilot to the automobile reached over and unlocked the passenger side door. Although the door was unlocked, Milo stood outside the car staring into the woman's beautiful face in amazement. He was frozen, unable to form any words to justify how beautiful she really was. "I think I'm in love" Milo thought, still unable to move. "Um... are you gonna get in?" Milo nodded and climbed into the car. "Oh my god, you look exhausted! Are you okay?" She extended a hand and touch Milo's arm. "Save you energy... I'll take you for breakfast". Still overwhelmed by he beauty of even her clumsy words she was speaking, Milo smiled and agreed. "My name is Jill... Jill Adams." "N..Nice to meet you. I'm M..Milo Samus." Perhaps it was fate, but Milo soon found out that Jill was from Calgary. Exactly where he was headed...
