Four: Ambition

26 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Lust is an inordinate craving for the pleasures of the body." Ric laughed. It was a good, wholesome sound, and the type he hadn't used in quite some time. Lira, likewise, set on the edge of the bed, her head rocked back in laughter that was slowly peeling itself down to giggles. Ric looked over at her, and saw the familiar curves of her body, the defiant waves of curly blonde hair. He felt the comfort of being with someone who he knew he could talk to, who would be open with him, who wouldn't judge him, and realized just how lonely he was. In the few hours she had been here, he had forgotten all the old insecurities, and even the resentment he still occasionally felt towards her for abandoning him. Instead, he remembered the talks, the times together, the fun and spontaneity that young love had prompted. The look in his eye must have betrayed him then, as he found himself staring back into Lira's. She wasn't laughing anymore, but the smile had not left her. Ric leaned forward, drawn towards her almost against his will. He pressed closer, and she didn't resist. He felt the heat of her closeness, the sweet breath, the soft tactility of her lips. Not one to leave anything alone, he pressed on, and soon found that they had locked arms and lips, falling into one another. Passion swept over Ric, and it had been so long... the toils of the road, the lack of emotional connections, the touch of a lover... all so very long... And then she pushed him back, refuting his advance. "I.. I think I had better go now." "What?!?" Ric was screaming inside, but his voice didn't reflect it. He felt the anger welling up - he had found something he craved, he needed - and she was turning him away. He could not even comprehend the depth of this newest of betrayals. "It... It's just too soon, Ric. I mean - we've been apart for four years now. You've been in the fWo, and I've been making my own way, and, and... I just can't. You've changed since then, and so have I. We can't pretend that one day is going to wash over everything from the past. If we're going to... we need to come to know each other again. We need to wait a little - we can't jump right back in where we left off." "Now, listen to me, Lira. You think this is just about sex? I can get that anywhere on the road, anytime - there are ring rats everywhere. This is nothing like that - and I'm not about to have you trivialize the -" "Trivialize?!?" Lira stood up abruptly, and only Ric's quick reflexes kept him from being thrown to the ground in the upheaval. "This is anything but trivial, you stupid bastard! If it was trivial, I wouldn't be asking you to wait!" Tears streamed down her eyes, as she shouted back at Ric. He stood up, and grabbed her arm as she turned, almost reflexively, for the door. She tried to pull away, but his grip was like iron. "Don't... don't take this away from me. Don't leave. I've got so very little to hold on to anymore..." Ric spoke softly, although the storm behind his eyes didn't imply anything that made Lira feel less threatened. "No. I'm leaving, and that's that. I'll get a different room, and we can talk about this in the morning. That's final." Ric limply let go, and Lira strode quickly to the door, opening it with one fluid motion. She turned her head back to look at him, and the tears abated for a moment. "I'll let you know what room I'm in. We'll talk about this tomorrow, and if - *if* - we can agree to a few basic things, we can discuss resuming our relationship. But we're starting from ground zero, here. I'm sorry, but I can't have it any other way." Ric glanced at her briefly, and the intensity of his eyes threw her. Without waiting for a response, she exited the room, pulling the door shut. That slight glare, more than anything else, had caught her off-guard, and for the first time ever, she knew a new emotion that came from dealing with Ric. Fear.
