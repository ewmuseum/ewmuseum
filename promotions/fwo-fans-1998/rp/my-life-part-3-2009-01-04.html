My Life - Part 3

4 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Things have been going well with this whole underground UFC thing Clinton and I had going on. The betters were becoming big time bankers and high ranking government officials. The money we were raking in was enormous. And I… Well, I was its ‘champion’. A quick six months have passed and I was fifty seven and zero. Some were quick fights and some were surprising tougher than I anticipated. I was beginning to fight guys who had jujitsu backgrounds. Lucky for me, I studied in the Army and never stopped my training. It was a sport I fell in love with. I figured I’d give this wave a ride until I could bread back into the wrestling industry. The FWO and its talent were still damaged goods that no one wanted. Given my lack of college degree, I refused to go work at an IKEA or train douche bags at the local gym for crap pay. So for now, this worked me. It was also good to be home, back in New York City. Oh how I missed you. Your smell, your noise, your verbal abuse and your Staten Island whores. I take a deep breath in outside the bar; nothing like that sweet smell. I opened my eyes but all I saw was darkness. What the hell is that ringing? Why can’t I see? What the fu-…there’s a warm sensation running down the back of my neck. Then ringing is fading and there is something muffled that sounds like a dog barking. As the ringing that I am hearing continues to fade my vision starts to come back slowly into focus. “Get up! Get up you punk bitch!” is now what I hear. My vision has now near fully returned and that ringing is gone. I’m on my hands and knees looking down at the concrete. That warm sensation is my blood flowing down and around my neck onto the concrete. I’ve been jumped. Mother fuckers. I was just in a peaceful state of saying how much I miss this place. What timing. “Get up! I want my money back and I want your take. Otherwise me and my friends here are gonna kill ya and stuff ya in the dumpsta for the rats to pick at ya.” I run my hand over the back of my head and feel the massive gash. I must have been hit by a bat or pipe. I shake off the cobwebs but apparently not quick enough for my attacker. “Johnny, pick this piece of shit up” he says. Someone puts me in the weakest full nelson I have ever been in, but I let him do it so I can gauge my surroundings and just how many people have jumped me. Looks like to be three. The one who is holding me and two other jokers. The one who must have hit me over the head had a small pipe in his hand. He is apparently the one who likes to run his mouth too. “Now, Stevie boy…I’m gonna to need those twenty g’s back.” Wanna be tough guys only makes me laugh which I uncontrollably do. He asks me what the fuck is so funny so I tell him. “Let me think that through for a moment. Give you the money you lost back or you’re going to kill me and stuff me in a dumpster for the rats to eat.” He comes closer to me not knowing that is exactly what I wanted. “That’s right, glad you can fuckin’ hear.” “I’ve got a better idea.” I told this punk with a slight smile on my face. I thrust my head forward into this punk’s nose and then knee him in his vagina. I then thrust my head backward into man with the weakest full nelson ever, grab his right arm as it becomes lose and hip toss him onto the concrete. The pipe is available and I see the third man run for it. I kick him in the face and he drops backwards. I quickly turn around and stop on the face of Mr. Full Nelson a few times to ensure he’ll stay down for a bit longer. These guys were nothing but two bit wanna be thugs and I was going to show that to them. The talker of the group was attempting to get up so I assisted him, and then I threw him head first into the concrete wall likely giving him a concussion. I turn my attention to the guy I kicked in the face, lifted him to his feet and proceeded to punch him several times in the face. Then, I threw him next to his full nelson buddy. I notice some rope on the ground. Time to send a message. I take the rope and tie it around the talker’s neck and throw the other end up and over the last rung of a nearby fire escape ladder. I begin to pull down on the rope which lifts him off the ground. At this point I am so blinded with rage I might actually kill this man. But I haven’t made up my mind just yet. I look down at the other two who are trying to shake off the very quick beating I just gave them. They see their buddy strung up, gasping for air. “Hey man…take it easy. Just let him go and we’re outta here. Swear.” He says to me. “How about you two just get the fuck out of here now and leave me and Mr. Loud Mouth here to have a little chat.” They were indecisive and I could tell they were thinking about rushing me. I yank on the rope again; this guy is turning purple…cool. “One strong yank on this rope and I snap his neck.” The two thugs in training look at each other and scatter. I continue to hold the rope, holding this man’s life in my hands. “I’m going to cut you the deal of your life right now you two bit piece of crap. I don’t know who the fuck you think you are, or what gave you the stupid idea you could come down here and try to fuck with me…but if I ever see your face in this town again I will make sure you continue to hang here and have the kids come and use you as a human piñata.” I let go of the rope. His body falls to the concrete and he begins gasping for air. I stomp on his head once more to get my god damn point across. When I turn around I see our resident bar bum just standing them looking at me. I run my hand over the back of my head again; god damn headache is bound to come shortly. I hate when people stare. “What a fuckin’ waste of human life you are; just standing there staring. If you’re not going to help then turn around, go back inside, get your drink and go back to sleep in your usual booth.” I star at the bar bum expecting a response but got none. I call him a prick as I brush past his shoulder so I can head inside to get a towel to soak up my blood. God damn useless bar bum.
