Slow Motion

17 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It all seemed to happen in slow motion. Now ONE! TWO! ***BOOM*** Ten Minutes ago "C'mon, Twitch," prodded Eli Flair, "Team EQ's gettin' the straps, right?" Twitch laughed and shook his head at the King of Extreme. "Both teams wanted to keep the finish silent... suspense, y'know?" Eli laughed. He didn't work tonight - taking a rare show off to rest up. But he still went out during the Deacon vs. Steven Shadows match to support his old friend - and he was here for post- show autographs, etc. But his thoughts remained half a world away... ....with his wife and daughter. Now "Ivy!" Amidst the smoke, the panic, and the stampeding crowd, Eli was overcome by a sense of deja vu - nine years ago, give or take, a riot at an IWB show in Coop's gym inadvertantly opened the door to the business for him. "Lance! Where's Ivy?" Lance Knight wasn't in the ring when the bomb went off - but sheer luck had him out of the line of fire completely. Eli dodged several civilians as he hit ringside. "Trip! Sean!" Eight minutes ago "You sound good," said Eli into the phone, "how's Greensboro look these days?" "Finally out from under the ice," replied Rudy Seitzer, "Merritt thinks we could be runnin' shows again as soon as the weekend if all goes well. You comin' home soon?" Eli shook his head. "I don't really know... I miss Angel and MJ like you can't believe... but with the CSWA on temporary shutdown I have to be where I can make money." "I hear ya. Ivy around?" "She's at ringside with Trip and Lance, actually," said Eli, taking a look at the monitor, "Fucknozzle, I'll call ya when we get to the next shot, Rudy... don't wanna miss the end of the match. Later." Now "Ivy!" Sean Stevens hit the deck as soon as he heard the blast - his immediate concern was the wellbeing of his partner, his opponents, and his girlfriend. Out of the corner of his eye, he saw his boy, Eli Flair, rushing toward the ring. Six minutes ago "Forget the rest of the plans they've been puttin' together, Twitch," said Eli as he and the product manager watched the two teams wrestled a spectacular match, "This right here is the definitive tag team match of Cyberslam." Twitch nodded, "This is the money match, definitely... but everyone's been contributing so much despite the circumstances... its' really still anyone's to win or lose." Eli nodded, watching intently. Now "Eli!" called Triple X, "Where's Ivy?" "She was at ringside - maybe she's out already?" "I didn't see her go!" Eli looked around haphazardly. "Fuck." Four minutes ago "Twitch? Fox? Anyone?" Twitch picked up his Nextel and hit the button. "Thumper? Twitch here. What's up?" Eli looked inquisitively at the agent - he didn't like the tone of Thumper's voice on the other end. "You heard from Geoff?" "Nope," replied Twitch, "Something wrong?" "Security said there was a breach," came Thumper's harried response, "Geoff was checkin' it out but he hasn't reported in." Twitch shook his head. "It's probably nothing... but find Geoff." As Twitch clicked off the phone, he returned his attention to the monitor, a worried look on his face. "As a precaution," suggested Eli, "shouldn't we get some more security around ringside?" "As a precaution," retorted Twitch, "remember where we are. No provocation." Now "....Sean?" Poison Ivy didn't see the blast. She didn't hear the death cry of the bomber. Chunks of debris flew from the upper deck to the lower - some of which made it as far as the ring. "Shit, Eli," said what sounded like Triple X, "She's bleeding bad!" "Sean?" "Shhhh, Ivy," he said, "It's me... we need to get the fuck outta here!" Two minutes ago "Deac? You all ready?" "Yes, Eli," replied Deacon, "I t'ink we have room for your bags, too. But Chris wants to leave as soon as the show is over." "No problem," replied Eli. He patted Deacon on the shoulder and returned his attention to the match - only instead of watching the monitor he had his eyes on the ring itself. There wa a feeling of.... something in the air. Aimz rushing around a moment ago didn't help, either. She seemed convinced that something was gonna happen. Considering where they were - and the fact that she and Brandon had managed to just miss each other all night so far, he couldn't blame her. Trip looked as sharp as ever out there, and Ivy couldn't keep her eyes off him. In or out of character, they were so crazy for each other. So intent was he on the match, he didnt' even notice- "What's that?" asked Twitch. -the man running in the upper deck- "Oh, shit!" the agent exclaimed. -until it was too late. Now As Ivy tried to climb to her feet, she immediately fell over. Eli Flair's timely arrival on the scene kept her from landing on the floor. "Eeeeli? I can't... see...." Eli wiped her forehead - there was blood gushing from a nasty gash. Her eyes fluttered open - her pupils were about the size of pinpricks. "Trip - where's Lance? Where's the Covingtons?" Somehow, Eli had managed to keep a calm head. Outwardly, at least. "They're out," replied Trip, as he took Ivy's other arm, "I don't think Desire was hurt but Victor got hit with a piece'a somethin'." Eli looked around - there was absolute pandemonium in the arena. "Here - take Ivy. Can you handle her on your own?" Trip hoisted her up with one arm - he could handle her. "Where do you think you're going?" "Wanna see if anyone needs a hand." Everyone around them seemed to move in slow motion, as Trip gave his friend a quick hug. "Don't get yourself killed, bro... get out of here." Five Minutes from now "BRANDON!" Flashback to 1998 - a fire in the bowels of Fish Fund Park Arena in Sweetwater, Texas. The entire company was outside except for Timmy Windham. Teri Melton was beside herself with grief. "Ma'am, we can't let you back into the building." Aimz paced nervously, her eyes darting from side to side at a dizzying rate, "Not everyone's out! I can't find Brandon!" Most of the company has been accounted for - medics are on hand to try to deal with the wounded. Team EQ and Eli Flair are waiting for news on Poison Ivy - they correctly assumed a concussion. She sat inside an ambulance, holding a towel to her bleeding head, with Victor and Carson Covington getting checked out, just as a precaution. Twenty feet from them, Amy Campbell looks to be on the verge of a nervous breakdown. In the passing of time... the only sounds are the wail of sirens. And the promise of tomorrow - maybe for better... "BRANDON!" ...most likely for worse.
