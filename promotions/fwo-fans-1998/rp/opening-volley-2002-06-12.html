Opening Volley

12 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Ze Golden Child, ha! I doubt zere's been a less adequate nickname since ze Showstopper first curtain jerked around zese parts. Certainly, ze fool has gone and t'rown away a golden opportunity. "I know of only t'ree reasons to do most any'ting in our industry, and for life as well: sex, money, and power. "Jump to anot'er company? You want more money, more power, or both. "Go for a title? Ze trifecta of sex, money, and power. "Hang out at ze local bars after a show? Definitely sex. "Turn heel or face? All t'ree again. "Why'd you do it, Monte? For ze fish? Foolish. I learned to late how bad an idea of giving ze business to someone in ze business is. Besides, she's in ze industry. She'd understand some basic dickish qualities. She's not some outsider, she knows ze job. "Was it ze money? Certainly, ze heroes move more merchandise, earn more royalties. Admittedly, zose few mont's when I sought ze cheers, my pay days were excellent, and zat was wit' splitting royalties four ways. But heroes in zis business are a dime a dozen, easily replaceable. Now a good villain is somet'ing special, somet'ing a career can be built on. "Power? Zere's no power in playing on ze side of ze angels. You merely set yourself up as a target, one zat will eventually be beaten on so much zat you lose ze fleeting fancy of ze fans. And zen when you want your old status back, you'll be a mere shell of what you were just a few mont's ago. "Monte Burns, you could have had it all. Our dastardly tradition gives us ze upper hand, and you toss it away for a piece of ass? Inexcusable. Ze eye gouges, ze hair pulling, ze manipulation of officials, doesn't it mean anyt'ing for you? Sadly, it would appear not to. "I'm disappointed in you. Ze only upside I can see, Monsieur Burns, is zat I won't feel so bad next week when I hold not'ing back to advance in ze Internet Title Tournament. "As far as my motivation goes...I want it all, and no one will stop me. Least of all a shortsighted fool such as yourself."
