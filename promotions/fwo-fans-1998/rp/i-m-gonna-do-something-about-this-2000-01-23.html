I'm gonna do something about this....

23 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fWo: Fans Wrestling Organization I'm gonna do something about this.... January 23, 2000 ::Havok is shown geting thrown out of Buckingham Palace. Some palace attendant is shown yelling at him from inside the gates of the palace.:: Lady: And remember, Buckingham Palace is not for sale! ::Two large men who threw Havok out into the street return, as Havok dusts himself off.:: Havok: Pah! England... if it were any worse, it'd be France. ::He turns around to face the camera, which follows him as he starts walking down the street.:: Havok: Look at this place, it's almost as bad as France. People drive ont he wrong side of the road, and sit on the wrong side of the car! I'll tell ya one thing they don't do in England that they do in France, though, and that is handcuff people to the ropes, and watch them lose their titles. I should still be the Cruiserweight Champion right now! I had that match won until Primetime decided to come out and help his little bitch. Well, I'm not just gonna sit on my ass and do nothing about this. I don't care what it takes, I'm getting Primetime in that ring. Primetime, buddy, you don't know where. You don't know when, but I assure you... I'll get ya! Oh, and Courage, I believe I'm entitled to a little thing called a rematch. So, I say this. We take our re-match, not in France, not in the US, but in neutral territory. We do it here in England. Wednesday Warfare in Manchester, I'll be there, will you? Do you have the courage to face me one-on-one for that title? Do you have any courage, or is it just a clever name? We'll find out soon enough. French Foundation, watch out, because I'm coming... for you! ::He walks off as the camera cuts to a promo for Trapped 2000(live on Pay-Per-View).::
