Best Days: Luke Starkey

29 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"And you, Monsieur Starkey? We have not yet heard from you," Pierre said. "Are you kidding?" Alex interjected. "With his luck, ALL of his days are good ones!" Luke Starkey smiled, but it was a small, sad smile. He was suddenly aware that all eyes were on him, everyone waiting for him to share what they themselves had already shared. Luke cleared his throat. "That's not entirely true," he began. "While it's true that I DO seem to have more than my fair share of good days, I did have one exceptionally bad day that more than makes up for all the good ones..." He raised his beer and took a long, slow swallow. "I've never told this to anyone, but you've all been honest and open and the least I can do is be just as honest and open with you. This happened almost seven years ago. It was a Sunday, November 3, 1996. I remember it like it was yesterday
