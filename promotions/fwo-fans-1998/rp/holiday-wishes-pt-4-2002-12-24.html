Date: Saturday, Dec 21 Location: Hooters Kodiak Vic Creed frowned as he struggled to remain calm. "I'm telling you Sam, I'm fine. The doctor is wrong." Nick Brandish finished off his beer and leaned back in his seat. Vic had been talking to one of the fWo head gringo's for the past twenty minutes. With every minute, he was getting more and more angry. Some of the other people in the bar were getting uneasy with the big guy getting loud, but Nick thought it was funny. Apparently, Vic went to get his shoulder checked and he had pulled or torn something. He wouldn't tell Nick the extent of the injury, but the doctor notified the fWo about Creed's status. The argument made sense: even if Vic froze the shoulder, most of his offense was lifting and throwing. Why risk further injury on a last minute match, pay per view or not? Nick knew that one of his friend's flaws was his pride. Pride was going to get him hurt one day. But not at Twilight from the sounds of it. "Fine. Yeah, talk to you later Sam." Vic hung up the phone and sat down at the table. A waitress wiggled over and cleared the empty pitchers off the table. "You guys sure know how to put them away." She leaned forward as she reached for the pitcher across the table. "Can I get you another one?" Nick stared at the cleavage staring back at him. "Get me a pair of jugs." As the waitress walked away, tray in hand, Creed stared at the table. "What gives Vic?" asked Brandish in his deep, gravelly voice. "They cancelled my Twilight match against Crucifix." Vic picked up his straw and started chewing on it. "Bastards. After all this time and now I'm off another Pay Per View." The waitress returned and set the two chilled pitchers down on the table. Nick refilled both of their glasses and drained his. "Probably for the best," he said before belching with satisfaction. "Start fresh for the new year. Rest up yer gimp wing." "But it was against Crucifix! I could have taken him with only one arm! Damn it, now they won't book me in anything until January. For my own good, they said." Creed grabbed the pitcher and drank out of it like a huge, frosty mug. "Couple more of these and I think I'll call it a night." "You tuckin' in yer skirt so early Vic?
