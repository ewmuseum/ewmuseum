Mr. Tony Davis (...excerpts from The Chronicles of Fairview Reed)

31 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What a breath of refreshing air is Tony Davis!!! At last I see real potential in this wrestling nonsense! Would that EVERYONE I’ve come into contact with were as forthcoming and clear-minded as Mr. Davis! Mrs. McPhineas has told me before that she has no real say as to whom I face week after week in this deplorable waste of time and energy and thus my meeting Mr. Davis was, indeed, happenstance…or was it? As we approached each other inside the ring I instantly realized a vast, untapped intelligence in his eyes – something unrecognized and unappreciated by others around us. He was (and rightfully so!) utterly and completely fascinated by My Assistant, instantly mistaking her for a Giantess (which, as we all know, have completely and sadly vanished from this material plane). As we sat upon our stretched canvas battleground we had as refreshing and enlightening conversation as I’ve had in a great long while! It was if he and I had been old friends, parted for a time and brought back together without missing a thing. We conversed about a plethora of topics that touched closely to my search and research – giants, vampires, leprechauns and unicorns… I had even touched upon our collective flight from The Hunting Party… And when Mr. Davis happened to mention ‘the phoenix seeking the witch’ I knew that was the reason that we were destined to meet. Nothing in this world or any of the other worlds that exists happens randomly; like dominos our actions and reactions are placed before us in intricate patterns that are not ours to view, only to act upon, follow, and react afterwards... Mr. Davis’ earnest comment was the next piece in our difficult enigma and I have now but to determine how best to use this new information… I should like to converse with Mr. Davis again and soon – he seems the most intelligent and rational man I’ve encountered so far in this Fans Wrestling Organization.
