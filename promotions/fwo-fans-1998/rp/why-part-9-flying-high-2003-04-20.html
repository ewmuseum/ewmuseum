Why? Part 9: Flying high

20 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, he's on his way back to the USA with the fWo. But quite frankly, he's not looking too upset about it. He's been here there and everywhere through Europe, performing in what he looks upon as third world countries, with the small bonus of being able to set a great example for the fWo as their self proclaimed American Ambassador. However, the European tour wasn't all bad. He got to go home to greet his hometown fans for the first time in a year. And he insulted them. Good times eh? But now he's on a plane travelling back to America as Cyberslam looms. But like I said, that lovable cunt isn't actually too peeved about it. "Success." Borst said to himself, stretching out in first class section of the plane before turning around and taking his seat in economy. Ever since the impromptu announcement that Cyberslam will be the last fWo show, Borst has been on such a high. He feels that all that time and effort fucking around, not quite knowing what he's doing and setting a bad example in the process was far but wasted. No more fWo. The US Title in disarray. And Borst's wrestling at Cyberslam. Surely that's asking for trouble? Well, you'd have thought so wouldn't you? He turned down any alcoholic beverage while on the plane and opted for orange juice and mineral water throughout, politely telling the stewardess that he's watching his weight. It seems that being out classed by someone Borst doesn't even consider half a man, due to him not having a cock was something of any eye opener. I mean, sure Villam is a talentless tit who's brought disgrace upon the name of the Asylum Title, with ass raping, castration and a porn star mask, but Borst suddenly realised that all that drinking, smoking, drugs and a training regime that consisted of walking down the off license for a six pack had done very little in improve his abilities. For some strange reason that bewildered Borst, he was slightly over weight and a complete slob. The in flight meal was served. Very descriptive eh? "No ta." Borst said politely, tapping his stomach. "Gotta watch what I eat. I'm in training." A big couple of weeks for our man Borst. Not only has he got to try and condition himself to last half an hour in a wrestling ring, but he's got to kick the shit into Villam Ender too. Something he was particularly looking forward to. That cunt has disgraced the Asylum Title, largely because Villam's a twat. Borst's got no interest in returning to the Asylum for good, or in winning the Asylum Title again. It's damaged goods now. For all he cares, Villam can keep the bloody thing. Borst just wants to kick his head in to prove a point. He had considered just giving Villam a good ticking off and a slapped wrist in hope that Villam would change his ways... but hey, this way's more fun. Although... as Borst stared down the aisle at other passengers in first class enjoying their champagne, you can't help but wonder if it's all too much effort. Bah! Come on! Nothing's too much effort for our man Borst... he's been waiting for Monday all his life. As far as he's concerned, he's killed the fWo, and is planning on giving it a grand old send off, with the post Cyberslam celebrations consisting of maiming a dickless porn star. Mr "Mighty Hero" Batman Frontier, the bad guy is gonna win this time. And Mr Vickram Ender... where've your bollocks gone? Where've-your-bollocks-gone? Where's your bollocks gone? Yep. Training aside, it's great to be Borst right now.
