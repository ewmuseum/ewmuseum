Breaking A Sweat

30 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alex Creed had plenty going for him: youth, an athletic background, and at only eighteen years of age he had two years of professional wrestling experience. He was no stranger to the gym either, boasting a physique most men would be envious of. You could grate cheese on his abs. It was this mix of youthful exuberance and physical well-being that kept the young man known as "The Mecca" from throwing up out of exhaustion. That, and pride. As sweat trickled down his body and slowly formed a puddle around his feet, Alex felt the burning exhaustion throughout his thighs and calves. He'd been squatting for almost an hour, and it was taking its toll on him. At first the exercise seemed trivial, and Alex noticed many people even younger than himself were doing it. They were simple, deep squats with only bodyweight as resistance. Being familiar with squats in the gym, having done numerous sets with over twice his own bodyweight, Alex decided that this was going to be a breeze. How very wrong he was. "C'mon Alex!" barked Tiffany Valentine, Alex's personal instructor/torturer. "Get your butt down to the floor!" With a deep breath Alex did as he was told, mentally cursing the name Lindsay Troy for getting him involved in this session of suffering. Of course it wasn't Lindsay's fault. She'd done Alex a huge service by getting him involved in her uncle Brent's Martial Arts Academy. A modest facility, Brent took in many underprivileged youngsters and helped them to use Martial Arts as therapy of sorts. The discipline helped them to control their anger and gave them a sense of accomplishment and purpose. It kept them out of trouble and got them into shape. The first time Alex met Brent, he knew he was in for some serious training. In his mid-fifties, Brent looked at least ten years younger. He wore baggy workout clothes which hung of his impressive physique, given his advanced years. Brent quickly sized Alex up, having been told a little about the young man's background and what he was looking to gain out of his training. "I know you're a professional wrestler," Brent told him matter-of-factly. "But what we teach here isn't just for show. If you're just here to look good in the ring, go rent some Jean Claude Van Damme movies and study those. What we do here is about more than just kicks and punches." Alex nodded his head. "Point taken. I'm here to learn." "Good." Brent took Alex around the facility and gave him a quick tour. "Most of our classes are pretty full, and I can guess your own schedule can be pretty erratic. Luckily one of my old students has agreed to work with you exclusively. She's a former wrestler herself so she can help you incorporate what you learn. But as I said, you're not learning flashy Hollywood fighting. You're learning functional martial arts." Brent and Alex faced each other and bowed. He could see why so many of his students respected him. "I just want to thank you for giving me this opportunity." "We'll see how thankful you are after the first couple of sessions," Brent said with a smile. At the time, Alex wondered what he meant by that. Now he knew. Oh how he knew. To shut out the pain, Alex forced himself to concentrate on something else. The easiest thing to focus on was his instructor. Tiffany was about Alex's height, with long black hair and hazel eyes. After their first round of kicking drills Alex could see her balance and poise that came from professional wrestling. They'd spent several days going over kicks, elbows, and knees. While not heavily influenced in the traditional aspect, Tiff did show Alex the proper way to perform the wai khru dance which was done before fights to pay homage to the fighter's teachers. Throughout their days of inflicting punishment on each other, Alex had picked up quite a bit of her history. Tiffany had actually trained with Lindsay Troy and they both had wrestled out of Florida for a time. But while Troy went on to bigger and better things, Tiffany felt her place was with the kids at the Academy. She helped instruct them, guide them, which gave both teacher and student a sense of fulfillment. Alex watched the sweat soaking through her navy blue sports bra. He guessed that a healthy chunk of her male students certainly felt fulfilled watching her train with them. Her toned body never seemed to show fatigue. Her abs, while not "cheese grater" material, were still impressive. "Okay that's good," Tiffany said and tossed Alex a bottle of water. "I'll go get a couple of towels. You stay there and cool down with some light stretching." The Mecca watched Tiffany walk away and tried to decide where he should dump the water
