12/29/2001 8:00AM Three scrambled eggs, a few slices of bacon, some pancakes and a couple of cups of coffee had passed since the Strangler first sat down at the table for breakfast. His hunger satisfied for the time being, he slipped his notebook out of his back pocket and glanced at his schedule for the day. Other than a couple of hours in the early afternoon, he noticed that he was booked up for the day... as usual. By now, he had pretty much given up on finding out what had happened to Darryl. He couldn't see any easy way to get some news without having someone come back around to ask him what his concern in the matter was. He figured he'd just put it in the back of his mind and think about it later. That is, until he saw Joseph walking by pushing a cart of dishes. The two men made eye contact and the Strangler nodded, before motioning for Joseph to come back his way. Joseph cleaned up a couple of tables in the room, which was still mostly empty due to the time of day, and stopped at the Strangler's table. "Hey, Mr. Adams" he said. "I thought I saw you at the gig last night. How did you know I was playing O'Leary's?" "Actually... it was just a coincidence" the Strangler replied. "We just happened to be in the neighborhood and stopped in to meet a... friend." "Ah, cool...cool. Did you catch much of the show?" "A few minutes, it was a bit crowded...which is good for you, of course." "Well... we, my band and I, get a percentage of the draw, a small percentage, but a percentage nonetheless... so yeah it's good. So what's up? Just saying hello, or did you want me to get you something from the kitchen or whatever?" "To be honest... we need a little help from you" the Strangler said. "Do you know Darryl Bailey?" Joseph nodded affirmatively, and allowed the Strangler to continue. "Well Joseph, we're looking to get in touch with him, and we know the hotel doesn't give out any information, which is understandable... but we would like to speak with him." "It's about what happened to him last night, isn't it..." Joseph began to shift nervously, before taking a seat, and glancing over his shoulder. He leaned in and whispered "I can't talk for long, if they see me slacking off I'll get written up... but there's more to Darryl that you need to know." "Like what?" the Strangler asked, raising an eyebrow slightly. "Well... he's into some side troubles of his own... so it's not a big surprise. But it's got me a little worried for myself..." "And why is that?" Joseph sighed, paused a moment and clasped his hands together. "Darryl and I owe someone a lot of money." The Strangler nodded. "Gambling debts?" "Worse... drugs. Well, steroids actually." "You don't say." The Strangler's eyes went a bit cold, and he felt himself losing respect very quickly for the two young men who had impressed him with their polite demeanor and speech. "It's not... like we were using the juice. We don't do that, I swear, we're clean... and we don't deal either. We were couriers. Well... one time. Or that was the plan..." "We don't care to be discussing this. Not here. Not now..." the Strangler said, wiping his mouth with his napkin. "We'll be going." "Wait!" Joseph said, in as hushed a voice as he could. "Please... let me finish." The Strangler stayed seated for the time being, but didn't take his eyes off of the young man. "Fine. Continue." "It was about 3 years ago, Darryl was 18, I was 20... we headed down to Cabo for a little break from school. A weekend thing, you know... beers, girls... the college stuff. While we were down there, someone offered us some money... a lot for a couple of semi-broke college students... to bring a package back over the border. I know, it's the oldest line in the book, and trust me... we knew it was something illegal, but it was so well disguised inside a piece of Mexican artwork that we figured it was worth the risk." "And what happened?" the Strangler asked, having suddenly gained some interest in the sob story. "Border guards decided to search the luggage... They heard something rattling in the box, broke the artwork open, and found the shipment. We were busted, cuffed and in deep shit. It wasn't a huge shipment, but it was enough to convict us for trafficking. So... we were given a choice. Jail time, or assist the cops. We chose the only real choice we had at the time. They sent us on our way, wired of course to finish the delivery. We got details... got in deeper... and they sent us back to Baja. This went on for a few trips, while the DEA gathered their information... and finally, they had enough to make a sizeable bust. They were happy, we were happy to be free from jail time... there was one problem. The guy who was buying that particular shipment that the bust occurred on got word of it, somehow. The feds had cast us aside by then, we were useless to them after that... but this guy wanted to have a few words with us. He had a couple of his goons wait for us outside of our apartment... next thing we knew, we were grabbed, gagged, and tossed in a van. They brought us to a warehouse outside of town... and if he had given the order, I'm sure they would have killed us right then and there. But for some reason, he pitied us. Just a couple of jackass college kids who didn't know shit as far as he was concerned. So he struck a deal. We pay him back what he lost on that deal, plus a high interest rate of course, and we'd have full function of all of our limbs. We took the deal... and we've been paying for it ever since." Joseph stopped and looked down at the table solemnly. "I don't know why I bothered telling you the whole story... but anyway, we've been late a few times in paying him, and I'm wondering if what happened to Darryl was his way of telling us to be more prompt with payments." "What's his name?" the Strangler asked. "Whoa now... you're not gonna go all vigilante on anyone now, are you?" Joseph actually looked frightened. "I mean, we've had some trouble... but it might make things worse, y'know?" "No. This has nothing to do with you. As far as you and Darryl go... you can both rot for all we care. We have our own reasons for wanting to know." The Strangler pushed his chair back and stood to leave. "Now, again... what's his name?" "Cozens. Jack Cozens. He owns a gym near here. Twelfth Street at the corner of Seaside Drive" "Fine. Thanks." "Sacred Heart Hospital... room 516. That's Darryl's room. Visiting hours start at 10AM... if they haven't released him yet. It's a concussion, and a couple of busted ribs. They want to be sure there's no internal bleeding... last I heard." The Strangler nodded, and left Joseph behind at the table, alone. He pushed his way past a few patrons who were beginning to file their way into the restaurant, and stopped at the elevators to head back to his room. He had been planning a workout this morning anyway... maybe he could learn something at the gym. Why he was bothering to pursue it any further was a mystery. Maybe he just wanted to be sure... "Stupid kids," he thought to himself as he stepped into the elevator. "At least in jail, your sentence would have ended a hell of a lot sooner."
