Gone and Forgotten, Part 3

1 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Monday, September 15th, 7:49 p.m. PST It was another day of relaxation for Matthew- since leaving the fWo, he had plenty of them. He sighed happily as he leaned back in his lounge chair overlooking his in-ground pool, staring up at the sky that still burned with crimson red. "You know, a man could get used to this." Matthew said with a smile, sipping some alcoholic liquid out of a cup adorned with tropical designs. He looked past his new pool at the large, four-floored white mansion he had purchased just outside of his birthplace in San Francisco. It was certainly a beautiful atmosphere, and Matthew liked spending time on these days just to take it all in. "Is this really all you do nowadays?" came a soft female voice from behind him. Matthew turned around to look at who was entering his pool from the back gate- and he smiled when he realized who it was. "Reika!" Xias said, smiling. "I couldn't have asked for a more pleasant surprise." The young Japanese girl smiled from beneath her sunglasses, watching as Matthew looked her up and down. She's as beautiful as ever. Xias thought to himself. The 22-year-old was wearing a blue dress that came down to her knees and was adorned with a beautiful floral pattern. She ran her hand through her long brown hair, and blushed a little when Xias stood up, removing his towel- his muscular body was covered only by a very small bathing suit, almost Speedo-like. It was Matthew's turn to blush next. "Sorry, I wasn't expecting guests!" he cried out, turning and running back towards the large white pool house. Reika's mood changed from embarrassed to amused as she watched him hurry back into the pool house. "What brings you to my side of the world?" Xias called from inside the pool house as he got changed. "You do, silly." she replied from outside. "I couldn't stop thinking about you ever since you left Japan." "That was quite the trip." Xias said, sticking his head out from the pool house and smiling. "I suppose it was." Reika returned his smile. He finally emerged from the pool house with a blue muscle shirt and black shorts. "Come on in." Xias told her. "I've got a full bar in here, mine as well have a drink while you're here." "As long as you've got more in there than just sake." Reika laughed. Matthew returned her laugh as he turned and walked back into the pool house, and she followed him in. "This is quite the place." she remarked, looking at the long bar with eight stools in front of it, and the collection of alcoholic beverages behind it. "Who says money doesn't buy happiness?" Xias jokingly asked her as he finished up drying his hair. "What do you want to drink?" "A wine cooler." she grinned. "Why, that's the same thing I'm having. We sure are alike." She grinned at him as he removed a pair of wine coolers from the fridge, then grabbed a small remote. Hitting a button, the wall unit behind them opened up, revealing a large plasma television. "Wow." she said. "Nice TV." "It had better be, for what it cost." he joked, then flicked on ABC. "What are we watching?" she asked him as she took a sip from his wine cooler. "Just going to catch up on an old friend." Matthew responded. She rolled her eyes. "Oh. Eron again?" He didn't answer. Suddenly, the mood in the room had changed. "Honestly, Matthew, when are you ever going to let him go?" she asked him. "You've got everything you could want- money, friends.....me....you don't need to wrestle anymore. You don't need to worry about Eron anymore." "I know that." Matthew replied. "Voran was born out of necessity- because I needed to wrestle to find out who I am. I know who I am now, and it's not Voran Xias. Voran is dead." Reika took hold of Matthew's right hand, and looked up at him. "So let it go, Matthew." Xias turned his head to look at her, then looked back at the TV again. There he was, delivering the Rogue DDT to Crusader. He leaned over and pinned Crusader, just as he had done to him, and Xias watched as Eron celebrated with the Legion of Dairy afterwards. When Eron smirked at the camera, it almost seemed like he was laughing in Matthew's face. "I can't." he finally responded to her. He stood up. "So what are you going to do?" she asked him. "You don't need to wrestle anymore. You don't need to be Voran anymore. Stay here with me." Matthew looked down at her and smiled. "I have to go back." he told her. "I have to beat Eron." She sighed. "I'm not going to be another wrestler's wife that doesn't see her love more than twice a week." she told him sternly. "When you finally get this wrestling bullshit out of your head, come back to Japan. I'll be waiting for you." She kissed him, and then she walked away. Matthew watched her leave. He wanted nothing more than to run out there and tell her he'd be with her forever. But he couldn't do that. He had one last thing he needed to do. Voran may be dead, but I'm not done with wrestling just yet. Xias thought to himself. I will defeat Eron.
