Three: And with a confused mind, she buried her past

13 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Three: And with a confused mind, she buried her past It was a scene that looked normal enough. A little six-year-old girl, playing with some toys outside. A father, flipping burgers on a grill. A mother setting the backyard table for lunch. Jessica looked over at her male and female dolls. "Mommy, daddy, look!" Jessica said. "They love each other, just like you two do!" But suddenly, this normal scene was gone. It was replaced by arguing parents, a father that raised the spatula he had been using to flip the burgers. WHACK! WHACK! WHACK! Three times. He struck her left cheek with the burning spatula three times. She screamed out for him to stop, as little Jessica held her head in pain. She closed her eyes.....and when she opened them, the happy scene was there again. Father flipping burgers. Mother setting the table. But was any of it real? Kali sat on a single green chair in the dark room, staring straight into space. She suddenly held her head, trying to figure out what was the truth, and what was fiction. She saw her father, looking at her mother with a look of pure love.....and then a look of pure hate. "I don't know......what to think.....anymore." Kali said quietly. "That's ridicolous, our parents loved each other, and us!" Jessica said. "Do not listen to the foolish child." another voice came. "They hated each other, just as they hated you. And you kill-" "STOP!" Kali screamed. All of it stopped. "I don't want to hear this anymore!" Kali screamed out. "You can't expect me to want to remember.....don't make me remember! "You bitch!" her father yelled at her mother. "I'll teach you to cheat on me!" "I love you." her father said, kissing her mother passiontley. "I SAID STOP!" Kali screamed. But she could not stop these memories from entering her mind, no matter how many times she screamed. She looked down at the chair she was sitting in- blood stained it's fabric, but just as fast as the blood appeared, it disappered as well. "Remember how much our parents loved us, Kali!" Jessica said. "You have to remember!" "Remember how much your parents hated you, Kali." the voice said. "You have to remember!" "Remember!" "Remember!" "Remember!" "NO!" Kali screamed. A single hand dropped a blood-stained knife. Kali's parents laid in a bloody heap, and the knife fell to the floor. The person holding it, was..... Kail fell of her chair, and dropped to her knees. "I am a fool." Kali said quietly. "In the end, we're all fools." Rei said, looking on. "Until you have the strength to remember, Kali Saturos, you are perhaps the biggest one of them all." "I will NEVER remember!" Kali screamed as she stood-up. "I will never let any of you tell me who I am, and what I have done! My name is Kali Saturos, and I am......I am life." The scene disappered as Rei quietly clapped from above. "Well, that's quite the start." Rei whispered. "Perhaps, perhaps there is hope for you yet." Fin.
