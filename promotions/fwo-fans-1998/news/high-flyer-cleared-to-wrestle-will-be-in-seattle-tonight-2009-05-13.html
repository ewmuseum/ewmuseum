High Flyer Cleared to Wrestle! Will Be in Seattle TONIGHT!

13 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
High Flyer/Rana Venenosa vs. Vox Nihili for the Tag Team Titles Hello everyone in the Seattle area! We have tremendous news for you, late breaking news! High Flyer has been CLEARED to wrestle! Yes, our World Heavyweight Champion returns to action TONIGHT, in a tag team match up with his partner and fellow tag champion "The Poison Frog" Rana Venenosa, against the number one contenders, K-Wolf Karina Wolfenden and the "Original Pulp Hero," Alias! While the match was tentatively booked on last week's ReAction, the doctor's clearing of the Neighborhood Lunatic back to action means that match WILL take place, and the tag team titles ARE ON THE LINE! So set your VCR's(editor's note: Tivo's... what's a VCR?) and get your popcorn, cause it's gonna be one HELL of a balmy night in rain soaked Seattle, Washington!
