I. C. WRESTLING SUPERSTAR INJURED By: Zech O'Donnell (7/20-01) On July 21st a local independent wrestling promotion called Pittsburgh's
Independent Circuit had its debut show. It was filled with filthly language, crude insults and high impact, dangerous wrestling. But on that night, indy wrestling Ian "Insayne Assayne" Parker was serverly injured by a Fisherman's
Suplex off the top rope thur a table.
Though a common spot in most wreslting matches, another indy star Jake Lowe, who was just coming off a couple month break from wrestling, used a non pre-cut table to use in the spot. Jake Lowe and P. I. C. Officials have no comment on the ordeal.
However, Ryan Delaney, current owner of P. I. C., has given Parker a private hospital room and has thus far paid all of Ian's bills. Ian currently has a brused spine from the move, and suffering from a concussion from a Implant DDT onto a steel chair.
Currently, Ian has not taken legal procedures to recovery damages and has told us, "I'm not looking to sue any over this. What happens happens, I just wanna get back in the ring." Though the bruised spine is going to keep Ian off P. I.
C.'s current schedule for the next few weeks, if he does not suffer from post concussion syndrome, he should return with ease.
This does, however, causes a wrinkle in the next show of "Body Count" seen in
New England area on a NBC affliate station. The scheduled match between
Parker, Xavior and Lowe has now been turned into Xavior against Lowe, in a singles match up.
