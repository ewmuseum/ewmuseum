Backed into the corner, Penoso is reeling as XYZ runs and leaps, and connects with a huge, crowd pleasing tope con hilo with XYZ riding Penoso, in the non homosexual sense, hard into the mat. XYZ springs to his feet, causing the fans to cheer "X! Y! Z!" over and over again as he tags in his partner Xavior.
Xavior waits for Penoso to climb to his feet. Xavior charges, and gets decked with a strong clothesline. Xavior's head bounces off the mat a few times as the wind goes out of the fans. Penoso leaps and tags in Paul, the fresh man.
