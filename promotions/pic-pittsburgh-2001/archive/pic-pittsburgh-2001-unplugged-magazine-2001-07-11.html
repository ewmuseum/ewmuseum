Unplugged, and its pretty empty. But next week's edition will be chock full of rumors, reports, fan accounts, and even ratings for our inagural Body Count! I have put together a solid crew of wrestlers to start this 'biz off with a bang. Six bright wrestlers have been signed and handed their $250, half of the $500 promised to them for the night of wrestling. $500 per wrestlers, now that motivation eh? You will see high flying action, you will see technicial wizardry, and even a dirty Mexican wrestle! Hey, its under _my_ contract, I'm allowed to insult him.. But even more important than that, you will be seeing two Shootfire Pro wrestlers in action! Yes, the acclaimed "Futeki Gai" XYZ and his protege "Your Savior" Christopher Xavior will be wrestling as a unit this week! Will it be the last we see of them? Who knows! But I do ask one thing from you, the fans.... ENJOY THE SHOW! Or else... ...I'LL RAPE YOU AND YOUR LITTLE DOG TOO! - Delaney * Raping dogs is F-U-N~! 2. GOALS FROM COMMISHIONER SHAWN RYDER When a man decides to lace up the boots, put on the tights and step into the wrestling ring, there are many goals that wrestler has in mind. He wants to secure a job, collect as much pay as possible, etc., etc. But there is a lofty goal all wrestlers dream about, from unknowns like myself to the big bad Chris Courtade, we all wanted to be famous! We all wanted the crowd to know our name and feel blessed ot have seen us in action! I, unlike Courtade, failed in the ring. I've enver done much with my talent between those ropes, sadly. But now, I have been given another chance... that unheard of second chance! Born and raised right here in Pittsburgh, I know what these fans want! I know what these fans deserve and I'm set to give it to them! I'm the head scout, the head booker and basically, the fall guy here. If things work, its me. If things go wrong, Delaney wants my head. But will things go wrong? Will thing go awry? Not at all folks! Few men get a chance to make it big in this sport and even fewer get a second one, think I'm gonna waste it? I've put my own blood, sweat, and tears into this federation for the last two months. Working witht alent, signing deals, getting a TV contrat, and a bunch of other imporant shit. I've taken the time ot make tis work, and make it the best wrestling product here in the 'Burg! Only one question remains - will the fans love us? And that, that right there is my goal. To make the fans cheer "PIC! PIC! PIC!" when we start the show, and "HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT!" after a big move. That is what I want from you the fans! No, that is what I need from you the fans! So are you guys up to the challenge? Are you guys ready to rumble and make this project work? Are you ready to buy the merchindise, and flock to the shows? I sure hope you are, but with the amount of talent I've collected, I don't think you're going to be disappointed. 3.
