What is P.I.C.? 1.1 Fantasy Explanation One day a man by the name of Ryan Delaney was scratching off one of those gray circles on a PA Lottery ticket. Ryan, a man of habit, never won a single dime before but continued to play, never hoping to win. But on this day, Ryan’s luck did change and he finally won.
He won 6 million to be exact, and chose to get close to $11,540 dollars a week for ten years. Seeing his bank account grow weekly and never really deciding what he wanted to do with the cash, he went out on a search and realized wrestling fucking rocks. So he decided to pool together a bunch of guys, give them paychecks, put on shows and just watch people enjoy the show.
So that’s is how Ryan got his foot in the door of the wrestling business. 1.2 Real Explanation Hi, I’m Ryan Delaney, the man running this proud old ship. I’ve been in the game for about three years now, have mild success with all of my characters and I’ve gotten an itch to run a federation! Just like almost every other person who gets into this game. So, yea, that’s about it…
2. Who runs P.I.C.? I run P. I. C. and I’m basically you friend. I’m hear to listen, and to have fun. I run this fed ‘cause I enjoy it, not because its works. That’s how I work, cool?
3. What flash system does P.I.C use? The flash system I prefer most: the closed system. We have a flash packet that comes out two days before P. I. C. Body Count, giving you an extra day to get me in-rings, on-cards and a chance to finalize angles.
4. Where is P.I.C based? P. I. C. is of course based in my hometown of Pittsburgh, PA. We will be venturing out into the backcountry of this area, but for right now, Pittsburgh’s very own Rose Bud is where the P. I. C. calls home, along with shows at Club Laga and M.
5. How are winners decided? Winners are decided by whoever works the hardest. I take into account flashes, strats and on-cards. I take into account consistency, and if it’s way too close, I work around an angle that’s going on. But more than likely, if you flash better, you’ll take the match home.
6. What does P.I.C demand from me, the handler? We demand flashes, but accept strats and angle ideas. 6.1 Flashes Flashes should not be as long as a Stephen King novel, nor “I’ll beat you. You suck.” Flashes should be creative, original and in on time. I will allow leniency due to myself because a drug addict and an absent minded ninny, but still, two days after the deadline? Nah, not going to count. A few hours?
Most likely. 6.2 Strategies Also known as strats. They consist of entrances, mindsets, attitude, moves, etc., etc. Not a must but a big help to me, since I’m going to writing a lot of the matches here in the P. I. C.
6.3 Angle Ideas Another way to take the stress of your dear old president. Sending in angle ideas is a big help because then I don’t have to run to people and try to work out angles. I’ll include contact lists, so you guys can try to work out angles yourselves.
6.4 Other stuff not covered above Like I’ve said, I like consistency and participation. I’ll take a good flash, a strat and in-ring over one great flash because it shows me you’re looking at the whole picture, not just a individual match up. But then again, if one week you do nothing, then flash once, and then do all three and you are going up against man who has done all three for three weeks, it is going to be harder for me to look at the match without leaning towards the man who’s been busting his balls for three weeks.
7. What are the weekly shows in P.I.C? There is one weekly show in P. I. C. and its called “Body Count”, read below for more information on it.
7.1 Body Count The main show of P. I. C. It contains three to five hand written, PAW style. And if you never read PAW, Perris just basically wrote out the match without announcers commenting on it.
And oh yea, in case you wondering if the P. I. C. has announcers, we do. They just comment on each match from a little TV room, the basement, in Delaney’s private home, complete with a P. I. C. backdrop and two stools.
7.2 Free For Alls Not really a weekly show, but more of a bimonthly show. It is our big show, showcased in the famed Monroeville Expo Mart. It’s a giant warehouse building used for all types of conventions.
8. Who works behind the scenes in P.I.C? 8.1 Ryan Delaney Delaney is the owner of this ship, but is just a giant fan. Doesn’t really do much other than sign the talent Ryder recommends. Delaney is about six foot five, two hundred twenty-five pounds, and a basic Goth, just to give you an idea of the man.
8.2 Commissioner Shawn Ryder The talent scout, the big boss when handling talent and the man to answer when things go wrong. He books the shows, checks out the talent and really makes the ship fly. Ryder is about six foot seven, two hundred seventy-nine pounds and has wrestled continually for about two years in local federations across the nation. He has now decided to take a turn at running the show, just not wrestling in it.
8.3 Ray Robinson The main play-by-play commentator here in the P. I. C. This man definitely knows a arm bar from a salad bar. Considered a nerd by many, and whiny little bitch by some, Ray is normally a very dull, boring man.
8.4 Mollie Marcus Mollie is, well, the candy here in the P. I. C. She is the very beautiful, sexy and gothic mistress of our dear Ryan Delaney. She is the color commentator basically. But takes over for her step brother Dustin as ring announcer from time to time, just to give her older step brother a chance at stardom.
8.5 Dustin McKee The step brother of Mollie, and the ring announcer. Knows a lot about wrestling, but normally freezes up on camera. Trust me, he's down it before in other federations. But he gets his shots every once in a while, normally when
Mollie is out partying.
8.6 Refs The P. I. C. has two refs. One is Chris Issac and the other is Chris Douglas. Both have worked minute amounts of time in the ring, but both have referring licenses, so Ryder picked them up for his federation.
Issac is a leant, green haired, punk rocker. He’s all up into the hardcore style wrestling and basically allows anything to go on in his federation.
Douglas is the complete opposite. He loves the technicians and the rules established by the wrestling commission. He is basically the big, bad, evil ref, while Issac is cool.
9. What the status of gold in P.I.C? I don’t believe in handing every member of the federation titles, so there is only one title here in the P. I. C. It is the Television title and its described more down below.
9.1 P.I.C Television Title The Television title is the main title here. It’s held by only the best here in the P. I. C. It takes more to win this title than to lose, but I totally agree that a champ has an advantage in a wrestling match. So when this title is up for grabs and it will up for grabs a lot, the challenger will have to prove to me they deserve it by beating the champion by a bigger margin than to beat a normal guy, though if its close, the challenger will normally win by DQ or a double count out will go down.
10. What are the main rules of P.I.C? The standard rules of every federation from EMWC to CeWA apply here. Have fun, don’t bitch just to bitch, flash weekly, don’t slack, etc., etc.
11. Website There might be a website, there might not. Depends if this federation gets moving and off the ground, making my time of building the site worth it. But trust me, if I do build it, its going to be sweet!
