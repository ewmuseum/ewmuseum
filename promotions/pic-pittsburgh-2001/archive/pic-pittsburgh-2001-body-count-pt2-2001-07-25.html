P.I.C. Presents.... BODY COUNT~! (part 2)

July 25, 2001, 12:00:00 AM

NATE DAWG (Developmental) @!@@!@! !!@ !@! !!@!!! !!! !!! !!: !!: :!! vs. :!: :!: :!: :: :: ::: ::: : : :::: "KAOTIC NIGHTMARE" SID SAVAGE (Loner) ^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*
Nate Dawg, with his hands wrapped in chains, is facing the mad mailman in Sid
Savage, know for his hardcore antics with the HGW and most recently Indiana
Championship Wrestling. The two men go two to two, spouting off some foul words. Savage is the first to jump on the offensive, nailing a quick right to the head of Nate Dawg.
But Nate Dawg laughs, as he belts Savage with a left and then a right. Savage is reeling as Nate Dawg moves in, looking to hurt Savage more. But Savage has other plans. He bites Nate Dawg in the calf, bringing blood to the surface.
Nate Dawg falls to the ground, grabbing at his legs as Savage stands up. He runs over to one of the cars, and pops up the trunk, thanks to a key from his pocket. He grins as he pulls out a canoe paddle. What the hell? A canoe paddle? This guy is a nut case... Nate Dawg, who is limping towards Savage, doesn't see the paddle and as he approaches, gets clocked.
The paddle breaks into two over Nate Dawg's head, as Nate Dawg is sent sprawling into a car. Savage giggles with joy as he pulls out a kitchen plate.
Not the sink, a plate... Geez... Anyhow, as Nate Dawg tries to get to his feet, Savage breaks a plate over his head and then proceeds to use the car as a weapon.
Savage drags Nate Dawg onto the back of the '99 Toyota Canary. Savage nails a right to the already dazed Nate Dawg and goes to lift in the air, probably for a powerbomb. But Nate Dawg, being a evil little dude, claws at Savage's legs, causing Savage to push Nate Dawg away. Nate Dawg falls into the space between the two cars and pulls Savage's legs out from under him. Nate Dawg then climbs the '98 Ford Escort, getting on its roof and jumping, going for a elbow drop on
Savage.
But Savage quickly moves out of the way, rolling to the hard cement. Nate Dawg lands hard on his left elbow, clutching it as he walks out into the center of circle. Savage goes back to the trunk of that there Ford Escort, this time pulling out a tire iron. Whoa, a somewhat normal item!
Nate Dawg turns around and sees the iron, he ducks Savage's first swing, and nails a wild, fury of punches to Savage's jaw, forcing Savage to drop the iron.
Nate Dawg then kicks the iron away, heading back to the trunk himself. And pulling out a... VACUUM CLEANER! He he... Nate Dawg pulls Savage by his shirt towards the car, where the cleaner sits. And with a hard shot by the remains of that paddle, Savage isn't all that wiggly.
Nate Dawg places the sweeper across Savage's throat and climbs up onto the near by For Navigator. Lots of Fords eh? Anyhow, Nate Dawg stands perched at the top of the SUV and leaps off, going for a leg drop on the vacuum cleaner, and connects. Savage's legs kick to in the pain and he rolls over. Nate Dawg gets to his feet, but clutching at his left leg, the one that connected with the move.
Issac pulls the mangled pieces of the sweeper off Savage and checks for vital signs. Yup, he's alive and Nate Dawg knows it. He goes for the pin, and gets the three. Savage doesn't even move to kick out.

[Nate Dawg's arm is raised into the air, as Savage gets to his feet and grabs another weapon from the Ford Escort. The weapon of choice this time is a glass jelly bean jar. Savage sneaks up quietly behind Nate Dawg and breaks the jaw over his head. Jelly beans go flying as Nate Dawg drops to the ground, hurt.
Savage then scampers off into the darkness, giggly the whole time as the feed cuts way... ...but not to the P. I. C. Television Studio! Oh no! This time we're trailing
Ryan Delaney, our owner, as he walks through _the_ back hallway of Rosebud.
His is walking briskly, looking for something you take it. And then, after a few seconds, he finds Bubbah Bromowitz Jr. and stops. Delaney pulls up a near by chair and sits down across from Bubbah, who is sitting on a table, eyes closed relaxing.]
RYAN DELANEY: Bubbah! What a awesome match you did have out there! [Bubbah opens his eyes, and shuts them again.] RYAN DELANEY: Ya know Bubbah, I watched your Daddy wrestle and I've got you quite few times. That is why, yea, that is indeed why I'm gonna make you this offer.
[Delaney pulls out a piece of paper from his pocket.] RYAN DELANEY: Now this contract here, its not like the others I've been handing out. Nah, this one... Its a real contract. You get $500 up front and $500 for every match, including tonights if you sign. Anything else ya want we can work on, so [Holds out a pen, and the paper towards Bubbah.] will you sign with us here at the P. I. C. and give your career another opportunity?
[Bubbah opens his eyes, and leans forward. He takes the pen and signs the paper, as the camera cuts away from this scene...
...but still not back to the P. I. C. television studios! This time we are in the official P. I. C. locker room with Chris Steele, the P. I. C. developmental star, and Mr. Hornz, his inflatable rhino. Steele is petting Mr. Hornz, giving him that extra special attention. But then, XYZ walks up to the two.]
"FUTEKI GAI" XYZ: Hello Steele, this is XYZ. [Steele looks up, but continues to pet Mr. Hornz.] "FUTEKI GAI" XYZ: I want to congratulate you on choking out Xavior... [Steele looks at Mr. Hornz.] and you two Mr. Hornz. That kid just needs a wake up call, that's all. So,
[XYZ extends his hand, trying to shake with Steele.] are we friends or foes "Steeleman" and Mr. Hornz? [Steele looks at the hand, and then at Mr. Hornz. He accepts the handshake as the feed finally cuts back to the official P. I. C. studios, where Mollie is back! Yay! The lovely Mollie Marcus is back, hosting the show as you hear distant screams of extreme torture. Umph, I wonder what they're doing to Ray.]
MOLLIE MARCUS: Whoa dude... That Steele dude talks to inflatable rhinos and can get some serious ass! Go Steele! Beat the BLEEP out of that bitch called
Xavior all you like! And man, did you guys catch that street fight? Pretty
BLEEPing hardcore eh? I loved the leg drop onto the sweeper! SWEEEEEEEEEETNESS
BABYT! But now, oh yea, its TIME FOR THE MAIN EVENT! Whooooooooo! Disgruntled and
Deadly are taking on Penoso Muerte! Ha ha! Penoso's dead dudes, no way anyone can survive D & D by themselves... And isn't this match, like, punishment for
Penoso? I think it is... I mean, he did choke the hell out of Douglas. Though
I hate that BLEEP, it's not cool choking people
[A loud scream is heard from the background.] But torturing people is cool! [A sick smile comes over Mollie's face as the camera cuts to the ring, waiting for Dustin McKee's intros.]
DUSTIN MCKEE: We have seen a thirty minute classic [Fans cheer.] And we have witnessed a hard to the BLEEPing core match up [Fans cheer even louder.] But now, it is time to break all the chains. It is time to break all the records [Fans cheer even louder.] It is now time for the maaaaaaaaaaaaaaaiiiiiinnnnnnnnnn eeeeeevvvvvvveeeeeeeeeennnnnnnntttttt!!!!
[The fans cheer loudly, waiting for D & D to kick the shit out of Penoso Muerte, that damn Mexican!] Coming first to the ring... Oh yea, it is that damn Mexican! It is the long haired, burrito eating, evil Mexican Technician...
P E N O S O M U E R T E ! ! ! ! [The low, brooding tones of "Killers are Quiet" by Slipknot rings out in Rosebud, causing the fans to jeer the hell out of our least favorite Mexican wrestler. Penoso comes out and laughs at the fans booing him. He flicks a couple off, makes a few graphic hand gestures telling a few girls to suck his dick, and...
...moons the crowd! It's starting a riot! Security runs out! The fans want this man's head right now! And he's enjoying it! Penoso laughs as he strolls into the ring, the fans screaming "You're gonna die!"]
DUSTIN MCKEE: And now... Now coming to the ring... One of them is hailing from Texas, the other from across the Ocean. One of them is a drunken addict, the other a famous wrestling from Shootfire Pro... Oh baby, it is the one, the only and the infamous...
D I S G R U N T L E D A N D D E A D L Y ! ! ! [With a rattle of the cymbals and a beautiful guitar solo, with demonic keyboard sounds, "Surfacing" by Slipknot blares and the fans go wild. XYZ, decked in a P. I. C. muscle shirt, with black tights, comes out from behind the curtain, the fans go nuts. But then, the beer drinking, cigarette smoking, coke snorting Texas hick comes out, head banging to the music...
...only to the fans delight! Paul leans over the edge off, letting his tongue hang out, ala Gene Simmons. He stands back up and walks over to XYZ. They look at each other, and look at Penoso, and...
...head bang to the music! The fans go nuts! XYZ and Paul's hair are flying back and forth, to the music. They stop and shake hands! The fans are busting a nut! Paul and XYZ shakes hands, continuing to add to the show, as the fans cheer them on, waiting for them to get their hands around the slimy neck of
Penoso Muerte.
The two enter the ring, with a round of applause. XYZ removes his shirt, and
Paul goes to the apron, allowing XYZ to start. Oh boy! This is gonna be one hell of a fight!]

DISGRUNTLED AND DEADLY @!@@!@! !!@ !@! !!@!!! !!! !!! !!: !!: :!! vs. :!: :!: :!: :: :: ::: ::: : : :::: PENOSO MUERTE ^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*
XYZ and Penoso tie up, XYZ nails a knee to the gut, bounces off the rope and nails a beautiful kick to the throat of Penoso. Penoso falls backwards, and
XYZ once again bounces off the ropes. He goes for a drop kick, but Penoso moves and XYZ slides out of the ring. Penoso then goes to the top rope and leaps off, nailing a splash on XYZ. Issac lets the two go, as he moves out of the ring, making sure both men are okay.
But they aren't because here comes Paul Barker! The Texan bounces off the ropes and leaps to the outside, spearing Penoso back into the guard, causing the crowd to chant, "HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT!" as Paul lifts his arms into the air. Paul then rolls XYZ and Penoso back into the ring, as the fans are truly backing D & D in this one.
XYZ charges in at Penoso, nailing a stiff kick to Penoso's right knee and then another to Penoso's ribs. And then a beautiful standing drop kick to Penoso's chin. Penoso backs up, into the ropes, and then runs forehead, cleaning XYZ's clock with a hard clothesline. XYZ's head bounces off the mat, as Penoso punches Barker right off the apron, and onto the guard rail.
The fans are furious at Penoso! Barker scrambles to his feet as Penoso eggs him on! But XYZ is up! He spins Penoso around, knees him in the gut and connects with a double arm DDT, brining Penoso's head down hard down onto the mat.
Penoso rolls over, obviously hurt, as XYZ bounces off the rope. ROLLING SENTON
BOMB ON PENOSO! The pin! Damn, only gets a two.
With the fans cheering "X! Y! Z!" and Penoso hurt bad, XYZ makes the tag to
Barker, allowing Barker to get some revenge for Penoso's actions. Barker drags
Penoso to his feet and brings him to his knees with a few forearm shots to the back of the head. Penoso, on his knees like usual, is dazed as Barker bell claps him.
Penoso falls to the mat, as Barker... BARKER GOES TO THE TOP ROPE! NO WAY! THREE STAR FROG SPLASH ON PENOSO! The ring shook! The fans are amazed and chanting, "BARK-ER! BARK-ER! BARK-ER!" as he goes for the pin. But Penoso, having some fight left, kicks out.
Barker gets to his feet, pumping his fist in the air, to the fans delight of course, and tags back in XYZ to finish the job. XYZ enters the ring, as Penoso is crawling into the corner, hoping to get away from the attack.
But before XYZ can get to Penoso, Xavior runs out from the crowd and attacks
Barker! He pulls Barker off the apron and to the cement on the outside! Barker, caught off guard, is defenseless. Xavior nails a spinning heel kick and connects with his finisher "The Saving!" on the cement. Barker's neck is bent into a 90 degrees angle, and his knocked out cold.
XYZ is screaming at Xavior, as the fans are booing like crazy. Penoso sees his chance and uses it! He walks up behind XYZ and locks on PENOSO MUERTE! The
Ryuto Jime, the same hold that has choked out Xavior twice! XYZ limbs are flying in the air, trying to break the hold, but Penoso brings XYZ to the mat and combines the move with a body scissors.
XYZ finally gives up and taps out, as Xavior is spitting on Barker on the outside. The fans are on their feet booing. They are beginning to toss trash into the ring, as Penoso finally lets go of the hold on XYZ.

Chris Steele, the new guy who talks to inflatable rhinos, has had enough of this and he's showing his PIC spirt by coming down to the ring the same way
Xavior did -- through the crowd! But Mr. Hornz makes the first move by going for a splash off the railing!
But Xavior catches Mr. Hornz and tosses him aside, thinking nothing off it. This upsets Steele who attacks Xavior with a flying clothesline! It takes
Xavior off his feet! And then Steele sits on Xavior's chest and beings to slug away at the bitch! Rights! Lefts! The fans are loving this! Steele is getting his!
[Penoso Muerte exits the ring, as XYZ is breathing hard in the ring, rubbing his throat. But, sadly, the medics are taking Barker away on a stretcher.
That is the final image of this weeks "Body Count" as the camera cuts way from the carnage in the ring, and to blackness. Yea, sadly, the show _is_ indeed over, on such a sad note.. Damn, good show eh?]
