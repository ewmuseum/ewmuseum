Nate Dawg ^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^ .___ . ____ \ / \ ___ , . | / \ ___ .___ | , ___ .___ |,_-' / ` | | | |,_-< / ` / \ | / .' ` / \ | | | | | | | ` | | | ' |-< |----' | ' / `.__/| `._/| /\__ `----' `.__/| / / \_ `.___, / [Fade up...] [A uncomfortable, ugly green hotel chair next to a stand littered with empty cig packs and beer cans. The bed unmade and totally destroyed. Sheets and comforter tossed about, pillows on the floor. Carpet stained, curtains pulled shut. Paul "Tacks" Barker laying on the ground, staring at the ceiling.] [It is quite a amazing site, but nonetheless, there he is. In all of his drunkenness, here is our reckless Southern bad ass. His face is unshaven, his hair unwashed and his clothes tossed about. Paul is clad in only boxers, making this site even more painful to the human eye.] " I wonda' what it'd be like to sit 'top of a feda'ation, ya know... Be 'dat one 'da fans do cheer and love. I sapose I have 'dat here, in 'da P. I. C. wit' XYZ as my partna'. But, the yearnin'... The desire... Nah, hasn't left me... Ah wanna be the star, 'da main eventa'... Will it eva' happen? " [Pause.] " Probably not... Ah mean, it's me... Paul "Tacks" Barka', nuthin' 'dat important. Though, if it would happen and Ah I would be 'dat 'dere star, I wonda' how long Ah stay on top. With XYZ as my partna' and Disgruntled 'n' Deadla' workin' out for 'da best I guess I'm doin' aight right now. " " But is it realla' me leadin' 'da team? Is it realla' me 'dose damn fans are cheerin' for? Or... him, XYZ? 'Da Shootfire-Pro rasslin' star? Umph, I guess I'll neva' realla' know, since I've neva' won a match wit' out him in 'ma corna'... " [Paul sits up straight, looking at the near by wall.] " What am Ah sayin' here guyz... Ah ain't just anotha' bum off 'dem streets. Nah, I'm Paul "Tacks" Barka', and to 'ma, 'dat's as good as gold! " [Paul slowly gets to his feet, still feeling the effects of last nights binge. Just then there is a knock at the door. Paul stumbles over to the door, using the wall for support. The man knocking is XYZ, Paul's esteemed tag partner.] [XYZ walks into the room, following Paul. He notices a bible on the table.] "FUTEKI GAI" XYZ: A bible Paul? [Paul looks at the bible and smiles, saying only the word "joints" to XYZ as the camera fades.] .___ / \ ___ , __ __. ____ __. |,_-' .' ` |' `. .' \ ( .' \ | |----' | | | | `--. | | / `.___, / | `._.' \___.' `._.' __ __ . | | , . ___ .___ _/_ ___ |\ /| | | .' ` / \ | .' ` | \/ | | | |----' | ' | |----' / / `._/| `.___, / \__/ `.___, ================================ " Trickery 'n' Traps " By: Penoso Muerte ================================ [Click... The camera fades on, and we see nothing. Darkness has filled the screen and no lights are to be sought after. You hear the low, brooding sounds of Slipknot's "Killers are Quiet" in the background, but yet, no light. Then the light clicks on, and the room is shown.
A dog cage sits there, against the wall. It houses to Doberman Pinchers. Both of which are laying quietly down, head in paws, sleeping. Their coats shiny, their tail short and Penoso Muerte watching over the dogs, carefully.
Muerte, still hiding his face behind his mask, is strumming his chin, thinking you guess. He then reaches out for the near by bat, laying on the desk. He picks it up, and slams it onto the cage. The dogs spring up, coming to life from the sudden noise.]
PENOSO MUERTE: It hard enough, make enough noise, anything responds. [Penoso then tosses a slab of beef over the top of the cage. The dogs spy it and gobble it up, until only a little bit remains. Dog #1 goes for it, Dog #2 blocks it, and they begin to growl.]
PENOSO MUERTE: Feed the beast, wait for your chance to slay. [The dog then go out into a full blown fight, as Penoso turns towards the camera. The dogs are fighting still, harshly too, as you hear whines and cries from the background mixed in with growls and barks.]
PENOSO MUERTE: Place the bait inside, and even the most noble of beasts will fall victim to the trap. Even those in the P. I. C...
[Fade Out.] _ _ _ _ _______ `. / `. / ' / \,' `./ .--' ,'\ ,' / / \ _-' ,'______/ [It is indeed a small, usual room. Walls painted green with white trim. A desk in the corner, complete with notes and lamp. The carpet a green that matches the wall. Pictures of flowers, men and women cover the walls. A bookcase near the end of the room, opposite of the desk. Rows filled with books, the top littered with green plants. And to the right, on a small little stand, are pictures. You see ones with such PIC stars as Paul "Tacks" Barker and even...
Christopher Xavior.... And in the middle of it all, sitting off to the side in a comfortable light green La-Z-Boy recliner is the Japanese wrestling legend, the Shootfire Pro star and of course our futeki gai. Cloaked in casual jogging shorts with a loose fitting gray Old Navy shirt, XYZ is relaxing. His eyes are shut, his mouth locked and his breathing is in a rhythmic beat. But then, XYZ's eyes open, and his mouth begins to move.]
"Futeki Gai" XYZ: There are many decisions in the road of life. Some you make you regret, other you rub in on others, and then there is some, some that just feel so right you wish to scream like a little girl.
[A smile flashes across XYZ's face.] "Futeki Gai" XYZ: On Body Count, one of those decisions presented itself. I could have stayed with my protege and my former friend. I could have spent the reminder of my days here in the P. I. C. with the young, brash and highly talented Christopher Xavior
[And with a smirk,] "Futeki Gai" XYZ: But that, that would be the equivalent to shooting myself in the foot! [XYZ puts down the leg rest, brings the back of the chair up and stands up. He briskly walks over to the pictures, picks one up. It is of Paul "Tacks" Barker and himself standing in front of the Alamo, after a stay in Texas. XYZ rubs his hands on the glass, remembering.]
"Futeki Gai" XYZ: I, instead, choose to relive the golden days of D & D with my long time friend Paul "Tacks" Barker, the man who has helped my out more times than I can count. I choose to walk down a road that I've always been comfortable on, the past. I was presented with a chance, and I choose the safe route, the route that I knew would lead me to success.
Many of you might consider me chicken or stupid for leaving Christopher for
Paul. And I would too. Why would a old timer like myself want to spend the last years of his career in a tag team tandem with a brash talented wrestler who has ten years on him? I would look shriveled, old and washed up. I would look imperfect, useless and worthless. But then again,
[XYZ places the picture down, picking one up this time with Xavior in the shot.
XYZ stares coldly at the photo of Xavior. The picture is of them in front of a Shootfire-Pro banner, and wearing SPW shirts. This was taken after a hectic night in a tag team match, one of with XYZ would have lost without his student.]
In our little tag match, I was the one kicking ass. Christopher, well, he was the one getting his nuts crunched.
[XYZ brings the picture down, hard, on the stand, breaking it. Glass shards fly about and the frame is cracked. XYZ takes the now bent photo into his hand, dropping what is left of the frame to the ground. He then walks over to the desk, and picks up a pair of scissors.]
"Futeki Gai" XYZ: Such a shame too. Xavior does _actually_ have talent, it is just that... He'd rather show boat, and hit the big moves, instead of working slowly and work the opponent. It is the simple saying of you cannot run before you can walk.
[Xavior snips the picture, cutting Xavior out.] Too bad Xavior will never listen to a saying that has stood the test of time... [Fade Out.] _____ _ _____ ( ` ___/ ( ___ _ __ ___ ___. ___ `--. | / | `--. / ` | / / ` .' ` .' ` | | ,' | | | | ` / | | | | |----' \___.' / `___,' \___.' `.__/| \/ `.__/| `---| `.___, * Loner * ` \___/ [As the camera fades in you, you see "Kaotic Nightmare" Sid Savage sitting in a bay window. His knees pulled up to his chest, his arms wrapped around his legs. A bottle of boose is in his left hand.
Rain is pounding the window as thunder booms and lighting flashes. Sid is staring out the window as music fades into your ears. You recgonize the song as 'Poor Twisted Me" by Metallica released on, arguably, the best Metallica album... Load. Sid just continues to stare out the window as thelyrics cut in.]
LYRICS: Oh poor twisted me Oh poor twisted me I feast on sympathy I chew on suffer, I chew on agony Swallow whole the pain Oh it's too good to be That all this misery Is just for oh poor twisted me LYRICS: Poor mistreated me Poor mistreated me I drown without a sea Lung fills with sorrow, lungs fill with misery Inhaling the deep dark blue Oh woah is me Such a burden to be The poor mistreated me [Sid finishes off his beer and takes the last one out of a twelve pack below him on the floor. Sid pops it open with easy and then takes a long ass swig.
The song then comes back from a guitar riff.] LYRICS: To finally reach the shore Survive the storm Now you're bare and cold The sea was warm So warm, you bathe your soul again LYRICS: Good to feel my friend Oh woah is me Such a burden to be Oh poor twisted me [As the final notes are played out, the world is left in silence except for the dripping of the rain. Sid's head turns towards the camera and with a smile, he speaks.]
"KAOTIC NIGHTMARE" SID SAVAGE - UEW.. you will be welcomed into the twisted, crazy world in my head. You will welcomed by poor twisted me!!!!!!!!
[Sid then takes a sip of the beeer and then continues to look out the window as the camera fades.]

His tanned skin, his boyish looks and his million-dollar smile - all not there anymore. His skin, still tanned, has lost its zing. And his face. Unshaven and grizzled. Even his smile, even that wonder has been tainted.
He seems to be in a state of shock, or repression. His spoon, the metallic food-eating item, is hovering over his bowl of Honey Nut Cheerios. His stare, oh his stare, it is so distant. He seems to be looking past the bowl, through the table, at something. Oh my, what is wrong? What is wrong with our Savior?]
"YOUR SAVIOR" CHRISTOPHER XAVIOR: I'm so tired of it all, anymore. I wrestle my heart out. I wrestle to prove to the fans that there is no need for tables, for chairs and the ladders. There is no need to leap off balconies or throw each other into cement walls. I wrestled so hard and so dear for two weeks straight. I turned the heads at the front office; I made the fans realize that
I, Christopher Xavior, was a force. But for what?
[Sighs.] To face Jake Lowe in a opening match! [A puppy dog face comes across Christopher's face, as he looks up at the camera.] What did I do to deserve this? I rarely swear. I don't drink. I don't smoke. All I do is save the minions from themselves. I try to take the violence out of this sport to protect the kids across the nation. I'm trying to save society by myself, without help. I'm trying to change what the world demands and make it something wholesome. I'm trying to make it wrestling.
But now todays wrestling. Oh no. I'm talking wrestling, the time honored tradition. Not the barbaric chair swing, table breaking, ladder climbing show of today.
[Christopher, still saddened, looks back at his bowl, trying to get the strength to eat.] I just so no point. If I work so hard, and so dear for something, and get Jake
Lowe in return. If I put some much love and heart into a project of purity, and get Jake Lowe in return. Why bother. Because when you are facing Jake
Lowe, you are obviously doing something wrong.
[Christopher's face drops into the cereal bowl, as the camera fades out on his lost soul.] ___ |\ | ___ _/_ ___ / ` ___ , _ / ___. | \ | / ` | .' ` | | / ` | | | .' ` | \ | | | | |----' | | | | ` ^ ' | | | \| `.__/| \__/ `.___, /---/ `.__/| \/ \/ `---| * Developmental * \___/ [Fade the BLEEP up you sonuvabitch...] [And you see, a young black man called Nate Dawg on a stoop. He is dressed like Tupac Shakur and is drinking a forty. The camera zooms in to hear Nate
Dawg's words.]
NATE DAWG - Everyone one of ya'all has been labled as 'dis big bad motherBLEEPer, and ya know, ya'all be right on the damn nose boy. Cause, like it been said, its just me 'gainst this big, bad world.
[Smiling.] NATE DAWG - Wit' my partner out and injured and with most of my boyz back home in 'da slammer, where is a young blood like me suposed to go? Jail? No way
BLEEP. Crack house? Maybe, I mean, 'day got fine bitches. But nah, man, me and my childhood obession.. Ya, it be wrestling for me!
[Nate Dawg does a little dance, wierd...] NATE DAWG - I don't play no games and I don't front. I am what I am, and whatever that might be.. cool dawg. Long as I'm collecting my pay and working hard, damn dude, I could give a flying BLEEP about anythang anymore. My boy
Parker is out cause of that sleeze bag Lowe, big deal... I'm stil here and that, that is _all_ that matters!
[Nate Dawg smirks.] NATE DAWG - Cause, after all, I am... Da million dollar playa', da billion dollar b-balla' and of course... the trillion dollar wrestling supa'star biaaaaaaaaaatch!
