P.I.C. Body Count - 3 Days Early (part 2)

July 17, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Paul raises Xavior to his feet and pummels him into the corner. Paul then proceeds to stomp a mud hole in Xavior's face. Xavior rolls out of the ring, trying to get a breather. Xavior lies on the outside, on one knee, breathing heavily. Paul bounces off the ropes and leaps onto Xavior, executing a perfect suicide dive.
Paul, hitting his head off the concrete, is just as hurt as Xavior, who now lies on his chest, barely moving. XYZ walks over to check out the two and
Penoso Muerte senses danger, so he hops off the apron and walks over to XYZ,
Paul and Xavior. XYZ is helping Paul to his feet, but Penoso taps him on the shoulder, and as XYZ turns around, Penoso decks him, causing jeers to come from the crowd.
Penoso drags the shaken XYZ to his feet and whips him into the steel steps. Referee Chris Douglas is screaming at Penoso to get his ass back in his corner and leave XYZ alone. Penoso flips Douglas the bird, and continues to attack
XYZ. He drops XYZ on the guard rail and then locks in a Cross Face Chicken
Wing on XYZ. XYZ is kicking and screaming, as Penoso just smiles and has a wild look in his eyes.
But on the other side of the ring, Xavior has gotten to his feet and has a chair set up on the outside. Paul gets up, and shakes his head. Xavior runs and leaps, going for a spinning heel kick to Paul's jaw. But Paul sees it soon enough, and ducks. Xavior lands on the guard rail, one leg on either side and his family jewels got in-between. Paul and the fans laugh together, prompting only Barker to lift that chair in the air. However, Douglas takes the chair off Paul and orders the two back in the ring. Barker follows the orders.
Back on the other side, Douglas breaks Penoso's hold on XYZ and sends him back to his corner. XYZ falls limp the mat, and Douglas stands tall. He regained control on a wild bunkhouse brawl, something not too many refs accomplish on a night's work.
Back in the ring, Barker whips Xavior into the ropes, and goes for a body slam, getting only two. Xavior, who is still feeling the affects of his missed aerial spot, slowly gets to his feet, only to receive a bulldog by his opponent
Paul.
Paul then tags in Penoso, who's eager to shut Xavior's trap. He drags Xavior to the corner, and locks on another one of his trademark submission holds.
This time he has Xavior trapped in a underhook leg trap neck submission. The move is compressing Xavior's body and forcing him to balance his weight on his head, his arms being held together by Penoso.
Xavior is screamingly bloody murder, as the fans begin to chant "XYZ!" hoping to revive him, knowing that is Xavior's only way out of this painful move.
Douglas continues to check in on Xavior, but Xavior is doing nothing but screaming his head off.
However, life has indeed come back to XYZ, who is now standing on the apron, seizing up the situation. He then enters the ring and baseball slide drop kicks Penoso's head, breaking the hold. The fans applaud in true fashion.
Penoso gets to his feet, allowing the limp Xavior to crawl to the corner. The two prepare to fight, but Douglas forces XYZ out of the ring, knowing full well
Xavior is the legal man.
Xavior is crawling towards his corner, as Penoso begins to stalk him. Penoso latches onto Xavior's boot, hoping to drag him into the center of the ring.
But Xavior kicks like a mule, catching Penoso in the jaw, freeing his leg. He then crawls faster to XYZ, knowing this is his chance to get out of the ring, and allow XYZ to extract serious damage.
Penoso makes one final dive at Xavior, but misses, and Xavior does indeed make the tag. XYZ picks Penoso up and scoop slams him to the mat. Penoso quickly gets to his feet, only to receive a right, then a left, and another right. XYZ then hip tosses Penoso. Penoso drags, arm drag takedown. Penoso gets up and charges again. Another arm drag takedown. Penoso bends over, exhausted, as
XYZ stands tall, his arms folded across his chest and the fans cheering his name.
XYZ then charges Penoso, going for a knee lift. Penoso grabs XYZ's knee and lifts him into the air, going for a modified Death Valley Driver. XYZ can't escape and is planted hard onto the mat. Penoso then drags XYZ and applies
"Sangriento Lona" the first of Penoso's two finishers. XYZ is screaming in pain, as his whole body is being stretched and bent in ways it shouldn't be.
Xavior has had enough. He slingshots himself over the top rope, landing a double foot stomp on Penoso's head, breaking the painful submission hold.
Barker tries to enter the ring, but thinks twice as he sees Douglas immediately escort Xavior out. XYZ gets to his feet first, a little sore and a little wobbly, but still able to make the tag to Xavior, as Penoso gets to his feet, shaking his head.
Xavior charges Penoso, but Penoso grabs him and lifts him up, dropping his head straight onto the top turnbuckle. Xavior falls flat on his ass in the center of the ring, and then proceeds to fall backwards. As Xavior lays in the center of the ring, XYZ outstretches his arm, trying get to the tag. Dang, out of reach. But the fans are with XYZ as he starts up a "X-AVE-OR" chant.
Penoso climbs to the top rope, and leaps off, nailing a perfect elbow drop to
Xavior, taking a page right out of Xavior's book. Penoso makes the cover, and the Douglas counts to two, but XYZ makes the save in the nick of time, nailing
Penoso with an elbow drop of his own. Penoso then gets to his feet, looks at
XYZ and then at the ref. Penoso then chokes the ref to hell and back, tossing him out of the ring with one hand, and then going back to work on Xavior.
By this time, Barker has entered the ring, and began to work over Xavior, with a serious of kicks to Xavior's head. XYZ walks up behind Barker and taps
Barker on the shoulder. Barker turns around, as both men close their fist and get ready for a strong right. But as the two move to punch each other, the open their fist and high five each other, as the fans cheer, "D & D!", citing the reunion of Disgruntled and Deadly.
As Penoso picks up Xavior and whips him into the ropes, XYZ and Barker turn their backs on the action, as Penoso locks in "Penoso Muerte," a judo choke, on
Xavior. Xavior's limps are flying in the air, as he screams, "HELP ME XYZ!"
countless times. XYZ just laughs, as Issac runs down to the ring. PIC's
"other" referee signals for the bell, as Xavior finally taps out.

Penoso Muerte releases the hold as Issac raises his hand in victory. XYZ and
Barker then turn around as Penoso reaches out to shake their hands. XYZ and
Barker look at each other, and then at Penoso and nod in unison. They then deck Penoso with double rights, only adding to the delight of the crowd.
Barker and XYZ walk around the ring, raising their hands, as the fans cheer their asses off. The feed then fades away from the D & D reunion, and back to the PIC Television studio.
