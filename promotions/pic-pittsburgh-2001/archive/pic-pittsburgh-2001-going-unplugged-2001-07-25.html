RANKING OF P. I. C. SUPERSTARS TOP FIVE P. I. C. STARS 1. Penoso Muerte 2. Jake "The Slasher" Lowe 3. "Futeki Gai" XYZ 4. Paul "Tacks" Barker 5. "Big" Bubbah Bromowitz Jr. TOP FIVE HARDEST WORKERS OF P. I. C. 1. Penoso Muerte 2. Jake "The Slasher" Lowe 3. Nate Dawg 4. "Futeki Gai" XYZ 5. "Big" Bubbah Bromowitz Jr. HOT P. I. C. WRESTLERS - Penoso Muerte - You hate this guy, we all truly do. From choking out Xavior on the first Body Count to spitting on the P. I. C. name to just last night, defeating D & D in a handicapped match, Penoso has proved himself as a true wrestler. Sporting a 2-0-0 record and putting on two great shows, Penoso is definatley on a hot streak, despite how many want his head. - "Big" Bubbah Bromowitz Jr. - A little unknown wrestler that came out of the crowd to fill the slot that
Xavior didn't want. Bubbah put on a show for us in the P. I. C., showcasing untapped talent. That talent got this man signed to the P. I. C. after just one match. What a shocker!
- Jake "The Slasher" Lowe - Dominated Ian Parker last week, and wrestled a hard fought battle with Bromowitz this week. Two back to back weeks of awesome wrestling! This man is truly a talented individual, such a shame he doesn't stop worrying about the big bucks and fame and do what he does best -- wrestle!
- Chris Steele - The new guy to the P. I. C. but he left his mark on the second installment of
"Body Count." He showed up in Delaney's office with a inflatable rhino and was signed right away. Mr. Hornz then decided Steele should attack Xavior, and that he did, twice in fact. From choking Xavior out, to Mr. Hornz attacking him, Steele put on a show. Steele has made friends with XYZ and that, that is a very good thing!
COLD P. I. C. WRESTLERS - "Your Savior" Christopher Xavior - The coldest wrestler here in the P. I. C. He lost last week and refused to wrestle this week. But he _did_ leave his mark by "Saving" Paul Barker durign
Barker's match. But still, that one move doesn't overshadow the beating Xavior took at the hands of Chris Steele and Mr. Hornz. Steele got the best of
Xavior, twice, and that leave a sour taste in Xavior's mouth for sure. - Paul "Tacks" Barker - Never really got the place rocking this week, and ended up getting injured at the hands of Xavior. Barker reunited with XYZ last week, reforming D & D, but this week, he got his ass handed to him. Such a shame too, Barker really deserves the nod as a great wrestler.
