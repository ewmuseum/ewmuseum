I have put together a solid crew of wrestlers to start this 'biz off with a bang. Six bright wrestlers have been signed and handed their $250, half of the

Or else... ...I'LL RAPE YOU AND YOUR LITTLE DOG TOO! - Delaney * Raping dogs is F-U-N~! 2. FAN REVIEW OF "BODY COUNT" Written By: [name withheld], Student My god does P. I. C...... suck? I dunno, really. I went to the show just because I had nothing better to do and I'm not a huge wrestling fan. I did see any nudity and no hot chicks were there. Just ugly bar hoppers, way disgusting.
The first match between that big man, I think his name was Lowe, and the green haired, free beer giving dude called Parkay? Yea, that match was pretty decent.
I really liked the sound of Parkay's head breaking the steel chair, I really commend Lowe for that idea. That move and that suplex shit coming off the top rope. Man, that was bad ass! I heard the table go crunch and boy did the splinters fly! I was laughing when Parkay was taken off in a stretcher. Ya gotta be a dumb ass to get injured in wrestling, its all fake and shit.
Then we had to wait around for a bit.... They had to clean up the ring and get rid of all the wood I guess. They did have some monitors placed around. I got a few drinks, so I didn't really watch much of the commentating shit.
Other than that hot chick, who I think was called Holly... Yea, that gothic chick was hot. But then like, some dudes pulled out some reefer and, yea, the rest of the night rocked.
The last match with the Japanese midget, the Ken doll look a like, the hick and the dirty Mexican was pretty sweet. I was right near the guardrails when the Ken doll look a like got his nuts crunched on the guard rail... Pretty funny shit. But I loved the dirty Mexican flicking off the ref. A total bad ass man! What a man!
So, yea, I enjoyed the night. I wish I would be able to remember the wrestlers names, I was too stoned to care.... I think, like, I might come back, actually. I wonder if those reefer dudes will be there again....

RANKING OF P. I. C. SUPERSTARS TOP FIVE P. I. C. STARS 1. Penoso Muerte 2. Jake "The Slasher" Lowe 3. "Futeki Gai" XYZ 4. "Your Savior" Christopher Xavior 5. Paul "Tacks" Barker TOP FIVE P. I. C. WRESTLERS - "Futeki Gai" XYZ - XYZ and his new tag partner Paul "Tacks" Barker have reformed the dynamite duo called "Disgruntled and Deadly." XYZ wrestled a great match againt Barker and Penoso Muerte at the inaugural "Body Show", really taking it too Penoso in XYZ's infamous puro style. - Paul "Tacks" Barker - The ruff and tumble brawler from down South really proved himself at "Body Count." Taking both pain and dishing it out. Barker used his head countless times during his tag match, resulting in the "Nut Crunch of the Century" spot. Love him, laugh at him, but you'll always run when he's drunk. - Jake "The Slasher" Lowe - Coming off a huge win over Ian Parker, Lowe must be a happy man. He used his strong Implant DDT to send Ian thru a chair and cultimated the match with his finisher "Slice and Dice" thru a table. Both moves were beautiful and have made this man a fan favorite around Pittsburgh. COLD P. I. C. WRESTLERS - Ian "Insayne Assayne" Parker - Took a brutal defeat at the hands of Jake Lowe. Lowe put Parker's head thru a steel chair and suplexed Parker thru a table, causing Parker to need to take a ride out on the stretcher. Parker, who's held up at a Mercy Hospital, is injured and needs a win this week over Lowe and Xavior to get his carer back on track. - "Your Savior" Christopher Xavior - His fellow Shootfire Pro friend and mentor turned his back on him at Body Count. With the reformation of D & D, Xavior is left out in the cold Pittsburgh air. And with it beign rumoar that he's asking for a release from P. I. C., the fans and the office officially hate this man. 7. RUMORS IN 'DA BURG - Rumor has it Ian Parker has suffered a concussion and a bruised spine from his match with fellow P. I.
