[NCW] WRESTLING REVOLUTION NIGHT 2

2 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
W R E S T L I N G R E V O L U T I O N R E C A P [Sitting in front of a desk is NCW's own Jeremy Tuttle. Behind him on a monitor is the NCW Logo. It changes to the Wrestling Revolution logo and has the word RECAP underneath it.] Fans, welcome back to NCW's recap of the events of WRESTLING REVOLUTION! Tonight we'll see the events that transpired on Night Two. There was one less match on the card than expected since the World and International Tag Team Belts had been unified, and by coincidence, the same team had been voted to receive a shot at BOTH sets of belts on Night Two. Still the action was hot and heavy and several surprises happened on this night as well. Let's take a look at the card for Night Two, from the Civic Center in Seattle, Washington on July 20th! NIGHT TWO: 12 Matches 1. The Phoenix Foundation vs. The Masters of Motion 2. "Incubus" Ron Collins vs. Kathy Summers 3. Murder Incorporated vs. The Hibernians 4. Icemaiden vs. Famine 5. "Heartbreaker" Matt Foxx vs. War 6. JUNIOR'S TITLE DEFENSE "Ambusher" Mike Trent vs. "The Preppie" Tiffany Van Heusen 7. INTERNET TITLE DEFENSE Mandarin vs. The A-Man 8. INTERNATIONAL TITLE DEFENSE "Method Man" Kurt Ryder vs. Tank Turner 9. WOMEN'S TITLE DEFENSE Blaze Taylor vs. "English Assassin" Arian Ash 10. WORLD LIGHTHEAVYWEIGHT TITLE DEFENSE The Jammer vs. Fallen Angel 11. WORLD TAG TEAM TITLE DEFENSE Sudden Impact vs. The Stylistics 12. WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE DEFENSE Adam vs. "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor This card also started off with autograph signings and a photo shoot for the fans. Once the matches started though, the intensity level was high! Match 1: The Phoenix Foundation vs. The Masters of Motion These two teams faced off and kept the action fast and furious. Multiple tags, double teaming, and quick pinfall attempts got the crowd worked up. The end came when one member of the Phoenix Foundation had gone for over twelve minutes in the ring, then made a hot tag to his partner. All four men wound up in the ring, but it was the Phoenix Foundation that pulled off the win, with a sunset flip by the legal man while his partner was brawling outside the ring with the other. Winner: The Phoenix Foundation via pinfall (sunset flip) at 22:38 Match 2: "Incubus" Ron Collins vs. Kathy Summers The Collins Clan, angry from Night One's losses came out in full force tonight as Ron Collins squared off against Kathy Summers in this Junior's Matchup. Kathy did her best to mount an offensive, but with the entire Collins Clan outside the ring distracting, and occasionally tripping her, it was just too much of a task. Shortly after the seven minute mark, "Incubus" Ron Collins nailed a superplex to secure the pinfall. Winner: "Incubus" Ron Collins via pinfall (superplex) at 7:27 Match 3: Murder Incorporated vs. The Hibernians This could have well been called a grudge match, as Jason Speece was still hot over the Hibernians painting the Irish flag on his head at the beginning of the year. The Hibernians came to the ring, as expected, a little tipsy. Once in the ring though, they were all business. They had rigged fireworks to go off at a certain time over the ring, and when it distracted Speece, they nearly scored a quick pinfall. Lankford made the save and after that, it went downhill for the Hibernians. Once Lankford was tagged in, he went to work on his opponent's back. After a superplex and a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker, Lankford tagged in Speece, who locked on The Display. A submission soon followed. Winner: Murder Incorporated via submission (The Display) in 12:39 Match 4: Icemaiden vs. Famine This Women's Match pitted the Icemaiden vs. one of Adam's Pale Riders. Curiously enough, Adam did not accompany Famine to the ring. Famine looked agitated and distracted, and it showed. Several times when it seemed as though Famine could have capitalized, she didn't. As a result, the Icemaiden was able to methodically wear down Famine with suplexes, takedowns, then soften up her leg with kicks and holds before locking on The Frozen North. Famine fought hard not to give up, but wound up submitting. Famine looked very angry, though not at Icemaiden. Winner: Icemaiden via submission (Frozen North) in 10:23 5. "Heartbreaker" Matt Foxx vs. War Another of Adam's Pale Riders appeared without Adam at his side. The crowd definitely noticed the agitation and distraction on the part of War. Matt Foxx was pumped and ready to go, though, and took the fight to War. War put up resistance and actually got a two count after a Napalm chokeslam, but Foxx got his foot on the ropes. After that, Foxx unloaded with a series of clotheslines. A dropkick sent War through the ropes and Foxx launched himself from the top turnbuckle to flatten War to the floor. After pushing War back into the ring, Foxx planted the big man with a piledriver. War kicked out at two, but only barely. Foxx signaled to the crowd that he was ready to end it with his Attitude Adjuster Superkick. It was then that "Iceman" Brian Knight, who was not even scheduled to be on the card, showed up. He came down to ringside with Vogue, Foxx's former valet, and began making advances towards Knockout, Foxx's current valet. Foxx nailed the Attitude Adjuster on War but then noticed Knight standing near Knockout. He came over to yell at Knight and Knight, wearing a Matt Foxx Fan Club T-Shirt simply shrugged his shoulders and acted innocent. Meanwhile Vogue had snuck into the ring and revived War with smelling salts. As Vogue exited, Knight invited Foxx to step out of the ring and talk face to face. Foxx made the mistake of starting to step out just as War grabbed him from behind and executed an Abrams Gourdbuster for the pinfall. After the match, Foxx was heard challenging Knight to a match on Night Three. Knight accepted. Winner: War via pinfall (The Abrams) in 19:21 6. JUNIOR'S TITLE DEFENSE "Ambusher" Mike Trent vs. "The Preppie" Tiffany Van Heusen The title defenses started once again with the Junior's Champion, "The Preppie" Tiffany Van Heusen defending her belt. The challenger was "Ambusher" Mike Trent. Trent was on the offensive from the opening bell and only rarely did he relinquish that advantage. He came very close to winning the title after the first ten minutes with a surprise DDT, but Lady Rose put her charges foot on the ropes without the referee noticing. When the referee broke the count, Trent thought he had won. While he was celebrating, Lady Rose slipped Van Heusen a bag of powder. When he turned back around, "The Preppie" nailed him in the face with it, while Lady Rose conveniently distracted the referee. Tiffany went on the offensive and did considerable damage to Trent in the second ten minutes of the match. Tiffany got two-counts on three occasions but the "Ambusher" held on. After the twenty-five minute mark, Trent began to mount a comeback. He unleashed a rain of punches on Tiffany, who could only respond to half of them. With an Irish whip he sent her into the corner then monkey flipped her back out. He went up top and managed to nail her with a dropkick off the top rope as she was standing up. Trent went for the cover, but Tiffany was too close to the ropes and Lady Rose again reached in to put Van Heusen's foot on the ropes. This time, Trent saw her and dove halfway through the ropes to grab Lady Rose by the hair. As he pulled Angela up onto the apron, Tiffany recovered and caught Trent with a German Suplex for the pin. Winner: "The Preppie" Tiffany Van Heusen via pinfall (German Suplex) in 27:29 Match 7: INTERNET TITLE DEFENSE Mandarin vs. The A-Man Again the fans' votes produced a strange matchup as this match pitted to Stablemates against each other. The A-Man was to face his partner in crime, The Mandarin of the Internet Title. The fans were curious to see how this match would progress, but unfortunately, it wasn't going to happen. After the ring introductions, the two men locked up. A-Man Irish whipped the Mandarin into the ropes.....and the Mandarin promptly somersaulted over them, landed on his feet, and sat down in the lotus position. A-Man backed into a corner and the two men awaited the referee's ten-count. As the fans booed viciously, the referee irately counted to ten and declared the A-Man the winner. Mandarin got up, entered the ring, shook A-Man's hand, and they walked to the back together. Winner: The A-Man via COR at 0:15 Match 8: INTERNATIONAL TITLE DEFENSE "Method Man" Kurt Ryder vs. Tank Turner After the previous match's lack of action, the fans were hungry for the next match. They weren't disappointed. The "Method Man" and Tank Turner put on a display of wrestling skill that lasted over a half-hour. Both men held the upper hand at various times during the match. Ryder wore down the champ with vicious suplexes and really worked over his arm with a variety of armbars and armlocks. Turner responded by working the neck over with neckbreakers and full nelsons. Each man gave it his all in this battle, but it looked grim for Turner when Ryder locked him in the Methodical Pain Chamber after the thirty-first minute. Turner managed to reach the ropes after a minute of the hold, and surprised Ryder with an inside cradle from nowhere to secure the three count. Winner: Tank Turner via pinfall (inside cradle) in 32:20 Match 9: WOMEN'S TITLE DEFENSE Blaze Taylor vs. "English Assassin" Arian Ash Blaze Taylor launched an aerial assault against the woman who had beaten her once already, but Ash kept Blaze grounded with mat wrestling. Knowing that Blaze had a reach and speed advantage, Ash wisely kept the challenger on the mat and worked over her leg with a series of kneelocks and anklelocks. Blaze nearly scored an upset with a surprise reversal of a kneelock into a boston crab, but Ash managed to kick out. Blaze was able to turn the tide though and hit Ash with a spinning leg lariat, a superplex, and a frankensteiner off the top rope. Blaze seemed on the verge of victory just after twenty minutes when she placed Ash on the top turnbuckle and went for her finishing combo, known as the Flameout. Blaze connected with the top rope frankensteiner, but as she went for the moonsault her leg gave out and she collapsed to the ring apron and fell to the floor outside. Her husband came out and tried to help her up, but the referee finished the count before she could make it back into the ring. Winner: "English Assassin" Arian Ash via COR in 21:22 Match 10: WORLD LIGHTHEAVYWEIGHT TITLE DEFENSE The Jammer vs. Fallen Angel If the fans wanted blood, they were in for a treat, because this match, plus what happened afterwards, was nothing short of tragic. Jammer came out to the ring by himself. No music, no rapping, nothing. He looked like he hadn't slept in a week. Fallen Angel came out and Jammer didn't even wait for him to get his entrance attire off. Jammer was all over Fallen Angel, using tactics usually reserved for the lowest of the low. Jammer raked Angel's eyes, bit him, choked him on the ropes, and barely broke each hold at the count of five. After several warnings from the referee, Jammer threw Angel through the ropes and began pounding on him outside the ring. Jammer smashed Angel into the ringsteps, the guardrail, and the timekeepers table, each time making sure to break the referee's count before he got counted out. Rolling Angel back into the ring, Jammer went for a pin, but unbelievably, Fallen Angel was still able to kick out! Jammer went ballistic, choking the life out of the Angel. When the referee tried to get Jammer to break the hold, Jammer broke the hold and went into his tights. While the referee checked on Angel, Jammer donned a pair of brass knuckles and waited behind the referee. As the Angel staggered to his feet, Jammer rushed past the referee and blasted Angel's already bloody face with the brass knucks. Angel fell in a heap and Jammer jumped on him for the pin. The referee started to count, then uncharacteristically stopped and asked to see Jammer's fist! When Jammer refused to show his fist, the referee began a five count. Before he could finish, Jammer forsook any pretense of winning the belt and simply started hammering away on Fallen Angel with the brass knuckles. The referee called for the bell. Winner: Fallen Angel via DQ (foreign object) in 14:17 Unfortunately for Fallen Angel, Jammer didn't stop when the bell rang. Pretty soon the crowd boos were deafening, but the referee was powerless to stop the carnage. Eve charged the ring and pulled Jammer off of the unconcious Fallen Angel. As the medical staff attended to Fallen Angel, Jammer turned on Eve in a scene which you can judge for yourself..... [The monitor shows the scene.] Eve comes out and grabs Jammer's arm, pleading with him to stop. He does, and instead stalks Eve (a la Savage stalking Liz, finger out accusingly and everything. He asks for a mike.) JAMMER: You...you have the GALL to come out and act like you care about me...after you SPURNED me just a few days ago?! Have you FORGOTTEN what that <BLEEP!>er DID to you? And you not only go back to him...YOU THROW IT IN MY FACE IN FRONT OF THE ALLIANCE! (turns back, laughing and shaking his head) I can't BELIEVE I fell for it a second time. I thought you loved me...but now I see...you're just like all the rest. Just tryin' to ride my coat tails when I'm ridin' high, then cast me off at the first sign of trouble. (Jammer faces Eve, rage in his eyes) EVE: Jammer, I-- JAMMER: (whispering menacingly) get out of my face...before I forget I ever cared for you. EVE: Jammer, listen. I-- (Jammer charges Eve, who ducks out of the ring. Evie and TALM rush the ring. Evie goes to Eve while TALM tries to calm Jammer down) JAMMER: I should have known...just like the lap-dog she is, Evie goes where Eve goes. Go back to Adam...you both DESERVE that breed of scum! TALM: Jammer, calm down! Adam was-- JAMMER: And YOU, ya little brown-noser! You're no worse than them. The only reason you're here is cuz you're biding your time, ain't ya? TALM: What? JAMMER: You're just waiting for your chance to come after me. Did you wanna wait until I won the belt, so you could get me into a title shot? Or did you just wanna get me from behind, so you could have the honor of bein' the man to take out the Jammer? HUH?! (Jammer shoves TALM, who stumbles back and falls. Chris and Blaze Taylor rush the ring.) BLAZE: Jammer, what the HELL are you doing?! JAMMER: What I should have done long ago...I woke up. I see it all so clearly now...you were playing me and Chris for fools, weren't you? You loved the idea of getting your man so jealous that he'd fight to prove his love, DIDN'T YOU?! BLAZE: You're sick! HSCT: That's my WIFE you're talking about, PAL! Now, calm down before you say something we'll both regret... JAMMER: Like what? Like you're nothing but a self-centered jerk who doesn't trust his WIFE, let alone the man who stood by you when you married her? That the only reason you stood by me was because you knew I wasn't intersted in the Heavyweight title? That you came to NCW not to help me, but to get a chance to stoke your own pathetic ego? HSCT: That's enough! JAMMER: I guess you're right... (Jammer turns away, then spins back around and nails Taylor with the brass knuckles. Taylor goes down and Blaze charges at Jammer. Jammer throws Blaze over the top rope onto Eve and Evie, then hammers on Taylor again before Phipps chases him away with a chair. He then vaults over the top rope, mike still in hand.) JAMMER: The Alliance is DEAD! You're all NOTHING! [The monitor fades.] Truly a sick image.....the Jammer has turned his back on his friends, his Alliance, and the fans. It's ironic that after Adam tried so long and so hard to tear down the Jammer.....all it took was for him to turn face. The two simply can't exist on the same side of the line. The medical staff cleared away the carnage and made way for the next match. Match 11: WORLD TAG TEAM TITLE DEFENSE Sudden Impact vs. The Stylistics The Stylistics came loaded for bear as they stepped into the ring against Sudden Impact. In a hard fought contest, the two teams went back and forth as each team executed move after move to perfection. This match, by contrast to the last one, was surprisingly clean. All four men went at it in a true contest of tactics and strategy. Both teams put on quite a display, but in the end, Sudden Impact finished the match with A Sudden End to score the pinfall. Winner: Sudden Impact via pinfall (A Sudden End) in 27:29 Match 12: WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE DEFENSE Adam vs. "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor Normally, this match would have been one for the record books, but due to Jammer's attack on Taylor, the match was nearly cancelled. At the last minute, Taylor got the doctor's approval to wrestle and came to the ring to the cheers of the crowd. Adam found himself being cheered by the fans as well, since word of his noble rescue of Evie had reached even Seattle. Adam got on the house mic and asked Taylor if he was sure he was up to a match, and gave Taylor fair warning that if he said yes, Adam was going to go after him 100%. Taylor took the microphone and said that he wouldn't have it any other way. To the cheers of the fans, both men shook hands and the match was underway. Taylor seemed to still be feeling the effects of the repeated shots from Jammer's brass knuckles, but managed to maintain the upper hand for the first five minutes by grounding Adam to the mat. Taylor used various submission holds to try to wear down Adam and keep the match on the mat. Adam withstood all of them and managed to break free around the nine minute mark. Adam then took over with high-impact suplexes, whips to the corner, chops, and uppercuts. Taylor tried to respond but seemed to only be fighting defensively. Adam inadvertantly reopened the cut on Taylor's forehead by moving out of the way when Taylor tried to charge Adam in the corner. Adam rolled Taylor up but Taylor kicked out at two. Taylor fought back with a series of inside cradles and small packages, but Adam managed to escape or reverse all of them. Taylor, wiping blood from his eyes made one attempt at a Figure-Five, but Adam kicked him off and kipped up. As Taylor came back from the ropes, Adam kicked him in the midsection and nailed the World Champion with a powerbomb that drew a massive pop from Adam's newfound fans. Adam then went to the turnbuckle and set up for the Revelation. Just as Taylor was standing up, the Jammer ran down to ringside, brass knuckles on both hands. Adam connected with the Revelation and was one count shy of becoming the NCW World Heavyweight Champion when Jammer grabbed him and started pounding him with both sets of brass knuckles. The referee immediately called for the bell. Jammer took turns pounding on both men with the brass knucks until security charged the ring and broke up the fight, but not before Adam and "Hot Stuff" were both bloody heaps in the ring. Blaze and Eve rushed out to help, but the damage was done. Winner: NO CONTEST (Jammer attacked both men) at 18:12 Quite a night for surprises, fans, and with one night left to go, WRESTLING REVOLUTION was already seeing an upset in the balance of power in NCW.
