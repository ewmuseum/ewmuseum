[NCW] Rankings through 4-11-96

14 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I thought it would be cool to post NCW's rankings to RSPWF. It has never been done before. These rankings are through 4-11-96, after the last NCW broadcast (NCW WaRzOnE, broadcast on that
day from Independence Arena in Charlotte, North Carolina.) +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ These are the NCW rankings, as told to me by President [name withheld]: Heavyweight Champ Justice 1 "American Original" Tank Turner 2 Taifun 3 Adam 4 "Heartbreaker" Matt Foxx 5 "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor Light-Heavyweight Champ VACANT (1) 1 Fallen Angel 2 The Jammer 3 Scorpio 4 Marcus Magus 5 A-Man Women Champ Jessica Justice 1 "Terminator" Tara Collins 2 Eve 3 Miko "Steel Rose" Mizadori 4 Lady Rose 5 Blaze Tag Team Champ VACANT (2) 1 Stylistics 2 Murder Incorporated 3 Badd Company 4 Sudden Impact 5 Roundhouse Internet Champ "Terminator" Tara Collins 1 Adam 2 "Heartbreaker" Matt Foxx 3 Miko "Steel Rose" Mizadori 4 Scorpio 5 Blaze Taylor International Champ VACANT (3) 1 Scorpio 2 "Method Man" Kurt Ryder 3 Dr. Mortis 4 Marcus Magus 5 A-Man International Tag Team Champ Sudden Impact 1 Arsenal 2 Badd Company 3 Roundhouse 4 Hellblazers 5 Ghetto Boyz Juniors Champ "The Ambusher" Mike Trent 1 Airborn 2 Van Heusen, "The Preppie" Tiffany 3 "The Incubus" Ron Collins 4 Hellfire 5 "The Succubus" Chrissy Steele ++++++++++++ 1: The Light-Heavyweight title was confiscated by President [name withheld] from "OG" Garu Trent, do to the
constant match interfence and controversy in the LH division. Moore declared that an "undisputed"
champion would be crowned at RingStorm, when the winner of the Fallen Angel v. "OG" Gary Trent v.
Marcus Magus v. "Dashing" Derek Lanfkord Bunkhouse match takes on the winner of the Scorpio/Jammer "I Quit" match. Fallen Angel and Jammer won their respective matches. 2: The Tag-Team titles were vacated after NCWF and NCW Champions Fatal Attraction did not resign
with NCW. This tittle will be filled at RingStorm as well, in a 1 v. 2 top contenders match.
3: The International title was won and vacated by NCW announcer "The Black Sheik" defeated
fellow announcer Thomas N. Tyler at First Fight. There has been an ongoing tournament to determine the champion. The Finals will be at RingStorm. Taifun will face "The American Original"
