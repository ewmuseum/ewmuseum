NCW — Card

NCW Mayhem — the earliest surviving 1997 card

12 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Network Championship Wrestling presents NCW Mayhem (The scene is a large function room, filled with screaming fans. One fan holds up a 'L-Train is gonna derail the Trooper!' sign. The scene shifts to the announcers' table where "Wildcat" Joe Drexel and Marcus Magus sit.
Drexel is in a University of Iowa sweatshirt while Magus is in a white poet shirt and a black cape.)
DREXEL: We are LIVE from the Triangle Ballroom in the Iowa Memorial Union on the campus of the University of Iowa! Welcome to NCW Mayhem! I'm The
Wildcat Joe Drexel along with Marcus Magus. So Marcus, what news of your status with NCW?
MAGUS: My agent is getting closer to an arrangement that will be mutually beneficial. DREXEL: Well, tonight the Jammer, as interm NCW Commissioner, will have a few words, as well as a match involving Jammer's tag-team partner and fellow Troublesome Trio member, Thomas N. Tyler, against Tony "The Tiger"
Nelson. MAGUS: I am very interested in hearing what "The Boss" has to say. After all, a wrestler that threatens his plans to get the NCW Title laid hands on him. I see the career of the Trooper coming to a screeching halt.
DREXEL: Well, you get to ask him yourself, since he wants to speak to you as well. MAGUS: You don't say...in that case, if you'll excuse me. DREXEL: Let's get to the ring. Take it away Marcus! MAGUS: Ladies and gentlemen...and i use those terms in the loosest sense, it is my dubious honor to grace your presence as I introduce
Interm NCW Commissioner...The Jammer! (A generic rap beat starts up as the Jammer comes to ringside. He's in a trenchcoat with JAMMER on the back, and his purple shades. His hair is pulled back to a ponytail.)
MAGUS: Welcome, Mr. Commissioner-- JAMMER: Cut the bull, Magus! I'm here for two reasons. The first is to address what happened yesterday, and the second...is to address YOU.
MAGUS: Very well. For starters, why the sudden ending to the match? JAMMER: Because I've seen guys like the Trooper before...or have you forgotten K.I.A.? I've also been heaved into the crowd by an opponent.
Luckily the people in the chairs Marcus landed on got out of the way. Marcus suffered two bruised ribs and a mild concussion from that fall. Had the match continued, it could have been worse. It was my duty as
Commissioner to stop the match. As it stands, the doctors are holding
LaShawn Marcus for observations before giving him clearance to wrestle.
MAGUS: And what will you do about the Trooper? After all, he laid his hands not only on the referee, but on yourself.
JAMMER: I am slapping a $1000 fine on the Trooper, and a warning that I will not tolerate attacks on non-wrestling members of NCW.
MAGUS: Well, that about-- JAMMER: Where do you think you're going?! I ain't done with you yet! MAGUS: I beg your-- JAMMER: You were named Commissioner of NCW, yet I hear report after report of unreasonable demands before you take the position.
MAGUS: What's unreasonable about my requirements? JAMMER: Oh, where to start? Let's start with a percentage of gate and concessions. You get a salary, same as everyone else.
MAGUS: I was unaware of that clause in the contract my agent was negociating. JAMMER: Next, you wanna wrestle-- MAGUS: On this I am firm. I understand that I cannot wrestle in NCW, but why not in other organizations?
JAMMER: The same reason I'm not wrestling elsewhere: conflict of interest. If I wrestle in another organization, suddenly I'm not keeping my full attention on NCW, and we go down the crapper, just like NFW.
MAGUS: Very well, but in that case I must protest the length of my contract. JAMMER: Would you prefer the contract to be renegociated every three months? MAGUS: Well... JAMMER: The only change would be salary in renegociation. MAGUS: If I leave, I get to pick my successor. JAMMER: Done. MAGUS: Ladies and gentlemen...the NEW Commissioner of NCW...ME!!!!! (The crowd looks like they have no clue what just happened) DREXEL: Well...that was...err, enlightening. MAGUS: You're just jealous due to your lack of prestige. DREXEL: Whatever, let's go to the ring for tonight's match. STONE: The following match is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first...accompanied to the ring by the Jammer...from Las Vegas, Nevada, weighing 292 pounds, from the Troublesome Trio, The ALWAYS explosive
Thomas N. Tyler! (The crowd erupts as "TNT" by AC/DC plays over the PA. Tyler is wearing a tiger-print robe with TNT on the back and an NCW doorag. He removes the robe to reveal tights with a similar pattern. Jammer is dressed as before. They high-five each other)
DREXEL: The always irrepressable TNT in his NCW debut. MAGUS: That's amusing, considering every title this guy's ever held was in original NCW. STONE: His opponent...accompanied to the ring by "Gentleman" Jimmy Clifford and "Luscious" Linda Lyon...from Charlotte, North Carolina, weighing 250 pounds, Tony "The Tiger" Nelson!
("Eye of the Tiger" plays over the PA. Nelson wears orange trunks with black tiger stripes on them, orange boots, and orange armbands. He has a muscular build and intensely walks to the ring. Clifford is a tall redhead in a black three-piece suit. Lyon is a tall, muscular blonde wearing sunglasses, six inch stilletto heels and a long black dress generously slit up the sides to the waist and then from the waist up to her shoulders.)
DREXEL: These three were talking serious trash yesterday. Let's see if they can back up their words.
MAGUS: You didn't seem to mind Lyon talking trash. DREXEL: Erm...ahhh...Lyon will see her debut tomorrow against a woman known only as the Golem
MAGUS: You are a wealth of information today. DREXEL: There's the bell. Nelson in to lock up, but TNT sticks his body through the ropes to threaten a fan.
MAGUS: That's one thing I like about Tyler. He always makes his opponent wrestle at his speed.
DREXEL: Tyler back in. There's the lockup, no. Tyler once again through the ropes, this time threatening to leave the ring to get the fan that just flipped the bird at him.
MAGUS: Nelson looking more than a little impatient, but Clifford trying to keep his man calm.
DREXEL: Let's try again, no. He's out on the apron. Nelson's had enough of this and drags TNT over the top rope, and Jammer screaming at the referee about it.
MAGUS: Well, technically Tyler was out of the ring, but delaying tactics do not win over officials.
DREXEL: Nelson with a front-face neckbreaker on the Explosive One, and follows it up by dropping a couple of fists between Tyler's eyes.
MAGUS: The Jammer is livid on the outside. I'm waiting for him to start throwing his weight around.
DREXEL: That may be why he was so eager to get rid of the Commissioner title. You know what they say about power...Tyler rolling to the outside, but in the wrong end of town!
MAGUS: Tyler's usually smarter than that. DREXEL: Jammer bolting to the opposite end of the ring...Lyon whipping
Tyler to the ringpost...Jammer just threw himself in Tyler's path, taking the brunt of the collision with the post.
MAGUS: I have NEVER seen the Jammer sacrifice his body for someone else! DREXEL: Tyler springs out with a double clothesline on Lyon and Clifford, and Tyler is the only one standing at ringside!
MAGUS: You spoke too soon, as here comes Nelson! DREXEL: Tyler climbing back into the ring...but Jammer grabbing Nelson's ankles so he can't get back in! Come on ref!
MAGUS: Lyon to her feet and driving her heel into Jammer's back...that'll make you let go in a hurry!
DREXEL: Nelson back in the ring barely in time as Tyler drives the elbow into the back of his neck. TNT picking Nelson up...atomic drop...and a clothesline followup!
MAGUS: That was his trademark signal for you-know-what. DREXEL: No, he wouldn't...he couldn't...that move was BANNED! MAGUS: It was banned by NetBlood, not JammerWolf. DREXEL: MY GOD!!!! THE PAIN EXPLOSION!!!! (standing figure four leglock) That move has broken limbs before! How could Jammer allow it?
MAGUS: Tyler's his best friend. DREXEL: Lyon just nailed TNT between the eyes with that stilletto heel and the ref is calling for the bell!
STONE: Your winner by disqualification, Thomas N. Tyler! MAGUS: Better a DQ loss than losing a leg. DREXEL: And Jammer in the ring...and he and Lyon are going at it! Jammer with a dropkick, and Lyon spills out of the ring! MAGUS: Nice view...I mean she was seriously off-balance with one heel off. DREXEL: I don't think we've seen the last of this encounter. For Marcus
Magus, I'm "Wildcat" Joe Drexel...good night!
