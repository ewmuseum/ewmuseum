[NCW] WRESTLING REVOLUTION NIGHT 1

2 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
W R E S T L I N G R E V O L U T I O N R E C A P [Sitting in front of a desk is NCW's own Jeremy Tuttle. Behind him on a monitor is the NCW Logo. It changes to the Wrestling Revolution logo and has the word RECAP underneath it.] Fans, welcome back to NCW! We apologize for being unable to bring you NCW WRESTLING REVOLUTION as it happened live, but due to circumstances beyond our control, the PPV turned out to only be seen by those in attendance. All money that was paid in expectation of seeing the PPV was refunded entirely. Still, the three sold-out arenas around the country saw some amazing wrestling, some fantastic title defenses, and a few surprises as well! Night One took place at the USAir Arena in Landover, Maryland on July 13th. Night Two took place at the Seattle Civic Center in Seattle, Washington on July 20th. Night Three took place at The Pyramid in Memphis, Tennessee on July 27th. Here's a run down on what happened on Night One! N I G H T O N E NIGHT ONE: 15 Matches 1. "The Succubus" Chrissy Steele vs. "The Cheerleader" Julie Johnson 2. "Desirable" Danny Dynamite vs. Marcus Magus 3. Dixie Hart vs. Sonya 4. CAGE MATCH Jana the Spider vs. Nurse Brenda 5. WARGAMES MATCH The Alliance vs. The A-Stable (Jammer,TALM,Eve,Evie) (A,Mandarin,HRM,Kevin Richards) 6. TITLE UNIFICATION MATCH Sudden Impact vs Arsenal 7. NUKE vs. "Powerhouse" Scott Rogers 8. "Nature Boy" Todd Newkirk vs. "The Professional" Nick Coletti 9. JUNIOR'S TITLE DEFENSE "Divine One" Cat Collins vs "The Preppie" Tiffany Van Heusen 10. INTERNET TITLE DEFENSE Black Flagg vs. The A-Man 11. INTERNATIONAL TITLE DEFENSE Taifun vs. "The American Original" Tank Turner 12. WOMEN'S TITLE DEFENSE "Terminator" Tara Collins vs. "English Assassin" Arian Ash 13. WORLD LIGHTHEAVYWEIGHT TITLE DEFENSE Scorpio vs. Fallen Angel 14. WORLD TAG TEAM TITLE DEFENSE Sudden Impact vs. Murder Incorporated 15. WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE DEFENSE "American Original" Tank Turner vs. "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor The night began with great fan appreciation festivities. All of the NCW Superstars signed autographs and posed for pictures with the fans. Then, everyone got ready and the event began! Match 1: "The Cheerleader" Julie Johnson vs. "The Succubus" Chrissy Steele These two junior ladies put on a great performance as both wrestlers went all out for the crowd. The match was very fast paced with each wrestler coming close to victory on several occasions. As the ten minute mark approached Johnson went for a frankensteiner which Steele reversed into a powerbomb, which she held for a pin. At the two-count, Johnson pushed forward an shifted Steele onto her back. Two counts later Steele bridged up and caught Johnson in a backslide which resulted in a three count. Winner: "The Succubus" Chrissy Steele via pinfall (backslide) in 9:28 Next up was two of NCW's hottest Lightheavyweights, in a match that seemed sure to ignite the fans! Match 2: "Desirable" Danny Dynamite vs. Marcus Magus 3D was ready to take it to Magus, and from the opening bell, he did! Magus was his usual cocky self and tried to stall outside the ring, so Danny launched himself from the top turnbuckle and smashed Magus into the guard rail. Tossing Magus back into the ring, Danny began to inflict high impact moves one after the other. Magus caught DDD napping however, and after a quick thumb to the eye, Magus took command of the match. Magus used quick takedowns and suplexes to keep DDD off his feet. Once DDD was grounded, Magus began to work over DDD with elbows and knees to keep him there. Magus tried to end the match early but DDD managed to keep one shoulder up or a foot on the ropes. In frustration, Magus started slapping DDD and taunting him, which only seemed to fire DDD up. DDD started firing back with closed fists and soon the two arialists were pounding on each other like two brawlers. Danny got the upper hand again and Irish whipped Magus into the ropes. Magus managed to hold on and DDD missed a frankenstiener. Magus quickly grabbed a handful of tights and went for the pin. The referee saw this and ordered Magus to break the hold. Magus got up and shoved the referee. The referee shoved back and as Magus stumbled backwards, DDD rolled him up for a three-count. Winner: "Desirable" Danny Dynamite via pinfall (rollup) in 18:32 Match 3: Dixie Hart vs. Sonya This battle of NCW's strongest women came about after Sonya had defeated both Eve and Blaze of The Alliance. Dixie, a friend of both, challenged Sonya to take on someone her own size. Sonya accepted. This match was full of power and not much on finesse. The two muscular women started out with a contest of strength. Sonya got the upper hand and after forcing Dixie down to the mat, backed up and flexed at her. Dixie responded with a dropkick that knocked Sonya out of the ring. After Sonya got back in, the two locked up and began trading bodyslams, powerslams, and other high impact moves. At one point, both women were down from a double clothesline! As they both pulled themselves back up, Sonya surprised everyone with an inside cradle that nearly pinned Dixie. Dixie escaped at the two-count, and retaliated with a backslide for two. Sonya nailed Dixie with a kick to the gut and picked her up into a military press. As she did her trademark repetitions with Dixie in the press Dixie grabbed her arm and rolled sideways, armdragging Sonya to the mat. As Sonya stood back up, Dixie grabbed her and, flexing her own muscles, military pressed Sonya over her head. To the delight of the crowd, Sonya found HERSELF being repeatedly pressed overhead before being dropped to the mat. Soon the crowd heard the signal for the Rebel Yell, and just after the ten minute mark, Sonya was draped across Dixie's shoulders. Ten seconds later, Sonya submitted. Winner: Dixie Hart via submission (Rebel Yell) in 10:11 The second match was a special cage match challenge. The mysterious woman who refers to herself as "the spider" challenged one of NCW other female wrestlers, the vivacious Nurse Brenda, to "step into her web". Nurse Brenda accepted the challenge. Match 4: CAGE MATCH CHALLENGE Nurse Brenda vs. Jana the Spider Nurse Brenda came to this match with the Doctor well prepared. Hidden in her very brief tights was a rag soaked in chloroform. Jana turned out to have a trick or two up her sleeve as well. The match began as soon as the cage was lowered and Jana leapt upon her opponent. With uncharacteristic rage, Jana tore at the face and eyes of Nurse Brenda. Brenda retaliated as best she could and eventually managed to break free of the Spider. Brenda fought back with a series of moves hoping to slow down her quicker opponent and met with some success as a series of backbreakers took the wind out of Jana's sails. Brenda made the mistake of trying to climb the cage walls too soon, though and Jana nailed her with a belly to back suplex right off the cage wall. Jana seemed more interested in punishing her opponent than escaping and continued to pound on Brenda. At one point she was raking Brenda's face against the cage wall. Brenda managed to fight back again and whipped Jana into the cage wall several times herself. As Jana staggered back from the wall, Brenda pulled out the chlorform-soaked rag and locked on a sleeper hold with the rag directly over Jana's face. Jana fought back but seemed to be passing out. At last she went limp and Brenda started making fun of her and taunting her. Apparently playing possum, Jana suddenly spewed a red mist into Brenda's face, blinding her. Jana quickly sprang up and rammed Brenda face first into one of the support poles of the cage. Jana then climbed the cage and escaped to the floor as the Doctor tried to get medical attention to his Nurse. Winner: Jana the Spider via escape (over the wall) in 14:21 Following the match, Jana approached the announcing booth and made a surprise interview..... The next match required there to be another ring set up and a second cage brought down. To entertain the crowd while they waited, footage of past NCW events was shown on overhead monitors. Once the two rings and two cages were set, we were ready for our next event. Match 3: WARGAMES RETURN MATCH The Alliance vs. The A-Stable (Jammer,TALM,Eve,Evie) (A,Mandarin,HRM,Kevin Richards) This match was a return match from RingStorm as Jammer chose to lead his Alliance on a mission of revenge against The A-Stable. As many remember the last meeting of these two stables saw The Amazing Love Machine, Eve, Evie, and Blaze Taylor losing to the team of The A-Man, The Mandarin, The Hard Ridin' Man, and Kevin "The Cobra" Richards. Evie sustained considerable injury from The Hard Ridin' Man even after she had submitted. Now Evie was back and said that she was looking for revenge. The match started out with Jammer himself entering the cage to face Kevin Richards. Jammer began immediately pummelling Richards with a pair of brass knuckles. Since the match was a Wargames Match, there was NO DQ possible and Jammer took full advantage of it. Richards finally was able to slow Jammer down with a low blow and started raking Jammer's face across the cage. Jammer was about to mount another offensive when the two minutes elapsed and the next member of the A-Stable, who had won the toss, came out. It was the Mandarin. While Jammer was getting set to piledrive Richards, Mandarin kicked him in the back of the head with a vicious flying side kick. The two began to take turns hammering on their foe in the corner. When the two minutes elapsed again, Eve came charging out. She hit the ring and dropped Richards with her Original Sin Superkick. When she turned to The Mandarin, he responded with a kick to the midsection and a Single-Arm Throw which sent Eve into the cage wall. Jammer launched himself off the turnbuckle and brought Mandarin down with a flying clothesline, but Richards had recovered and pulled a chain from his tights. Richards began hammering on Jammer while Mandarin slammed Eve into the cage wall. The time elapsed again and the A-Stable sent out the Hard Ridin' Man. HRM hit the ring and grabbed Jammer for a piledriver while Richards went up the turnbuckle to make it a spiked piledriver. Eve managed to break loose from Mandarin for long enough to shake the ropes, causing Richards to lose his balance and land straddling the top turnbuckle. Jammer recovered enough to backdrop HRM and began pounding on him with the brass knuckles. Mandarin pulled Jammer off him as Eve climbed to the top rope and brought Richards down with a frankenstiener. Things looked well in hand until HRM pulled a bag of powder out of his jeans and nailed Eve in the eyes with it. As she fell back blind, the other three took turns pounding on The Jammer. The time elapsed and Dane Phipps came charging to the ring. The Amazing Love Machine hit the ring and was a house afire. He dropkicked Richards into the cage wall, and nailed HRM with a kneebreaker before falling prey to a legsweep from Mandarin. With Eve still blind and Jammer bloodied from repeated punches with the chain, Phipps soon was completely on the defensive from Mandarin, HRM, and Richards. As all three were pounding on Phipps, Eve recovered enough to catch Mandarin from behind with a sleeperhold. Before the other two could notice, Eve had Mandarin almost unconcious. As Richards was charging to help, Jammer leapt up and headbutted him in the stomach. Grabbing him by the tights, Jammer fell back and propelled Richards head first into a steel support, busting Richards open as well. Mandarin broke free of the sleeper by twisting in and catching Eve with a side suplex, and as HRM turned around, TALM clipped him in the back of the knee. Jammer started pounding on HRM with the knucks, Phipps raked Richards face into the cage wall, and Eve was whipping Mandarin into the opposite wall. Then the time expired again and out came the NCW Internet Champion, The A-Man himself. He was carrying a chair and charged the ring with it. Once in the cage, he nailed Phipps from behind, rammed it into Eve's stomach and blasted Jammer right in the face with it. Jammer crumpled to the mat and fell victim to repeated chairshots to the back. Richards took over on Phipps and began pounding him with the chain. Mandarin had mesmerized Eve with The Chinese Riddle. HRM was still favoring his knee but made his way over to Eve and was about to set her up for a spiked piledriver with Mandarin. The final time limit elapsed and the last member of the Alliance team came out. It was Evie herself, carrying her bat, Mighty Casey. Evie charged the ring and brandished Mighty Casey at every member of the A-Stable. The A-Stable momentarily retreated, but soon re-grouped and were moving to surround Evie. Evie managed to connect with a wild swing and staggered Richards. Suddenly Jammer caught Richards from behind and took him on the Tour of the Buckles. TALM grabbed A-Man and smashed him right between the eyes with the chain Richards had dropped. Eve tackled the Mandarin and started pounding his face into the mat. HRM lunged at Evie but she nailed him in the gut with the end of the bat, then, to the amazement of the crowd executed a piledriver on the large man! The crowd went crazy, and with the crowd roaring it's approval, Evie picked up the Hard Ridin' Man, then blasted him in the knee with Mighty Casey. As Jammer nailed Richards with a Buckledriver, Eve nailed a dazed Mandarin with the Original Sin, Phipps torpedoed A-Man into the cage wall head first, and Evie locked HRM into, of all things, a Figure-Five Leglock! Shortly thereafter, Evie completed her revenge as the Hard Ridin' Man submitted. Winner: The Alliance via submission (Figure-Five Leglock) in 18:43 After the match, the carnage wasn't over, though! As the victorious members of The Alliance exited the cage, Evie lingered to kick HRM once more. That turned out to be a big mistake as A-Man quickly lunged at the cage door and shut it. As he quickly pulled the chain around the door and replaced the official lock with one he had smuggled in, Mandarin and Richards separated Evie from Mighty Casey. HRM struggled to his feet and began slapping Evie while Mandarin held her. Richards took the bat and used it to strike the cage walls, which a horrified Eve, Jammer, and Phipps were trying to climb. "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor and Blaze rushed out and tried to help get the door open as HRM began punching Evie in the midsection, screaming at her. Just when things looked thier worst, help came from an unexpected source. ADAM, the former leader of Eve and Evie in The First Family, charged the ring with a pair of bolt cutters. Just as HRM was about to take a swing at Evie with Mighty Casey, Adam got the door open and charged into the ring, swinging the bolt cutters. The Alliance was on top of the cage, climbing the walls or in the aisle in shock, so the A-Stable was able to make a clean getaway. Adam emerged from the cage to the cheers of the fans, carrying Evie in his arms. Phipps and the Taylors were calling for medical assistance, Eve was in tears and Jammer was in shock. Things took an ugly turn, however once Evie was placed on a stretcher. Eve leapt into Adam's arms and embraced him in gratitude. The Jammer looked stunned. As Phipps and the Taylors shook Adam's hand and Eve thanked him over the PA system for rescuing Evie from serious injury, Jammer gave a vicious look at his one-time rival and stormed out of the arena. Reports confirm that Jammer went straight to his car and left, bound for who knows where. Adam seems to have seen the light and for the first time in NCW, enjoyed the cheers of the fans. Even before the title matches had started the audience had seen some great matches, but the action was just getting warmed up, as the title defenses started with a Tag Team Title UNIFICATION MATCH! Since the fans votes determined who got shots at what titles, and since the current World Tag Team Title Holders were set to face the current International Tag Team Title Holders, a challenge was made by The Arsenal. They agreed to put their World Tag Team Titles on the line, only if Sudden Impact would put their International Tag Team Titles on the line....WINNER TAKES ALL! Sudden Impact agreed and the match was set only minutes before the match itself! Match 6: TITLE UNIFICATION MATCH Sudden Impact vs The Arsenal The International Tag Team Champs stood toe to toe in the ring with the World Tag Team Champs of over thirty minutes. Multiple tags on both sides resulted in a see-saw battle that threatened to spill out into the audience. Each team executed their respective finishers, only to have the other team make a save before the pin. Tempers ran high and several times, all four men were in the ring, hammering away on each other. Just after the thirty minute mark, it looked as though the Arsenal had things well under control. A clothesline had taken Steve Ramsey of Sudden Impact over the top rope to the floor but he managed to make the tag literally on his way over the top rope. Scott Taylor came in and was a house afire until he missed a dropkick and it looked like things were over for Sudden Impact. Then, as The Arsenal were setting up for a double-team finisher, Ramsey reappeared on the apron, busted open and bleeding from his spill to the floor. As the legal member of Arsenal was holding Scott Taylor in position for an assisted powerslam, Steve Ramsey knocked the other half of Arsenal from the turnbuckle and took his place. As the legal Arsenal member turned to whom he thought was his partner, Steve Ramsey dropkicked his own partner in the back, causing the legal member of Arsenal to fall backwards with Scott Taylor on top of him in a lateral press. Taylor managed to hold on for the three count and Sudden Impact became the NCW World Tag Team Champions in a title unification! Winner: Sudden Impact via pinfall (lateral press) in 34:23 7. NUKE vs. "Powerhouse" Scott Rogers Two more matches had been scheduled to allow the winners of the tag title unification match to recover before the rest of the title matches began. In the first match, two of NCW's most powerful men squared off in the ring. The awesome NUKE had faced "Powerhouse" Scott Rogers in the Octagon and had been the reason that Rogers was eliminated, thus ending Rogers' shot at the NCW World Heavyweight Title. The two men engaged in several tests of strength with mixed results. Rogers' raw power seemed to equal NUKE's superior size. NUKE took advantage early with a quick kneelift that stunned Rogers. After that NUKE went on the offensive with a series of powermoves: suplexes, powerslams, powerbombs, all of which took their toll on Rogers. After the ten minute mark, NUKE signalled for his finisher, The Atomic Bomb. Amazingly, Rogers managed to power out of it, backdropping NUKE over the top rope to the floor. Rogers collapsed in the ring and NUKE was slow to stand on the outside. The cheers of the fans seemed to revive Rogers and he climbed slowly to his feet as NUKE slid back under the ropes. As NUKE charged Rogers, Rogers caught him in an amazing Spinebuster and went for a pin. NUKE kicked out at the one-count. Fired up by the cheers of the crowd, Rogers went for a powerslam on the 300+ pound monster and when he connected, the crowd exploded. He was signalling for his finisher, the Power Plant, when NUKE suddenly shoved him through the ropes to the floor. NUKE attempted a splash on Rogers, but Rogers moved out of the way and NUKE collided with the guardrail. The two men pounded on each other in the aisle and slammed each other into every object at ringside. As the referee's count neared ten, Rogers sidestepped a charge by NUKE, who ran into the ringpost, and slid back in the ring just in time to beat the count. Winner: "Powerhouse" Scott Rogers via COR at 13:52 Match 8: "Nature Boy" Todd Newkirk vs. "The Professional" Nick Coletti By contrast, the next match was an exhibition of technical skill. "Nature Boy" Todd Newkirk came to the ring with his FWLI World Heavyweight Title, even though it was not on the line. "The Professional" Nick Coletti taunted Newkirk with the "belt around my waist" guesture, but Newkirk was unfazed by it. The two men traded hold for hold in a surprisingly clean match. Each man seemed to want to prove his superior technique. The crowd was very into the match, leading this reporter to deem them well versed in mat wrestling. Coletti, after his fifth consecutive two-count, seemed frustrated by his inability to put Newkirk away and resorted to rule-breaking. Newkirk fought fire with fire and proved that he, too, was capable of throwing the rules out the window. The end came when Coletti went for a powerbomb only to have Newkirk counter with a frankensteiner, then lock on his finisher, the Nature's Pain. Coletti held out for a full minute, but finally submitted. Winner: "Nature Boy" Todd Newkirk via submission (Nature's Pain) in 15:23 Match 9: JUNIOR'S TITLE DEFENSE "Divine One" Cat Collins vs "The Preppie" Tiffany Van Heusen In the second title defense of the night, the crowd saw the first of many strange pairings. This match saw a member of the Collins clan take on one of Lady Rose's Coterie. Both Juniors went right at each other and soon had the crowd in a bloodthirsty mood. Cat tossed Tiffany outside the ring and began pounding her head into anything she could get her hands on. Tiffany surprisingly countered by attempting to claw Cat's eyes out. Lady Rose and the rest of the Collins clan seemed content simply to watch the carnage as the battle moved back into the ring. After a vicious looking piledriver, Cat Collins seemed on the verge of winning the Juniors belt, but went to the top rope while the referee was distracted by her clan-mates. Lady Rose chose that moment to leap up onto the apron and push Cat off the turnbuckle. Van Heusen recovered and cradled Cat for the three count. Winner: "The Preppie" Tiffany Van Heusen via pinfall (cradle) in 7:32 Match 10: INTERNET TITLE DEFENSE Black Flagg vs. The A-Man The odd pairings continued as a member of the Profit$ faced the leader of the A-Stable for the Internet Title. A-Man had already fought in a grueling Wargames Match earlier in the night and was facing a former NCW World Heavyweight Champion. Flagg went to work on A-Man's neck early on. A-Man tried countering with every dirty trick at his disposal, but seemed to be unable to turn the tide against the much larger Flagg. Flagg went for neckbreakers and full nelsons to work over the neck of the champ. Shortly after the ten minute mark, Flagg nailed a piledriver and went for a cover. Just before the three count hit, A-Man managed to get his right shoulder up. Flagg turned to complain to the referee and didn't notice Mandarin sneak down near ringside and hand A-Man a foreign object. A-Man was just pulling himself up on the ropes when Flagg turned back to him. A-Man seemed to be begging off, but when Flagg came in, A-Man swung wildly at Flagg with the object in his hand. Flagg ducked and caught A-Man from behind, lifting him for an atomic drop. A-Man quickly jabbed Flagg in the eye with the object and Flagg fell backwards with A-Man on top. The referee never saw the object and counted Flagg's shoulders down. After the match, A-Man immediately left the ring, with Mandarin's help, as Flagg's cries of "foul" went unheeded by the referee. Flagg seemed angry but had sustained no serious injury from the object. Winner: A-Man via pinfall (foreign object) in 13:32 Match 11: INTERNATIONAL TITLE DEFENSE Taifun vs. "The American Original" Tank Turner In a match which the fans demanded, the two men who fought at RingStorm for the International Title squared off once again over the belt. Taifun came to the ring and waited for Turner's entrance. When Turner entered the building, the fans erupted in cheers for the member of the Hard Corps. This match between the two men seemed as loaded with hard feelings as their previous matches had been. Both men fought with a mix of brawling, martial arts, and technical skill. It appeared that Tank Turner had learned a trick of two from his most recent co-star, martial arts legend Jackie Chan. Taifun was surprised by this and it earned Turner the upper hand at the eight minute mark. For the next few minutes Tank Turner dominated the Thai wrestler with high impact strikes and takedowns, then signalled for his finisher, the 911. This time, though, he came up empty as Taifun pushed free of the top-rope DDT and countered with a front layout superplex. At the thirteen minute mark, it seemed that Taifun would have his revenge and the International Title, but he got greedy. Taifun decided to humiliate Turner by using his own finisher against him. He loaded Turner up onto the top turnbuckle then prepared to bring him down with the 911. Turner managed to counter though by grabbing Taifun, jumping off the top and landing with him in an inverted atomic drop. The men in the crowd all groaned simultaneously as Taifun staggered back. Turner went back up top and leveled the Thai with a clothesline off the top rope. The three-count followed soon after as Turner covered with a lateral press. Winner: "The American Original" Tank Turner via pinfall (press) in 14:42 12. WOMEN'S TITLE DEFENSE "Terminator" Tara Collins vs. "English Assassin" Arian Ash Former Internet Champion Tara Collins was voted by the fans for the honor of attempting to be the first woman to defeat the "English Assassin". Ash was wary of all of Tara's tricks, and fought a very defensive battle, pacing herself. The two women traded suplexes, clotheslines, and dropkicks before Tara finally managed to get the upperhand with a high-impact backbreaker out of nowhere. Though the move only got her a two-count, the damage slowed Ash down considerably. Tara continued to punish Ash's back. Elbowdrops, kneedrops, and a painful bow and arrow hold took their toll on the champion. Tara locked in an abdominal stretch at the nineteen minute mark, holding the top rope while the referee wasn't looking. The referee finally caught her at it and forced her to break the hold. As Ash was sprawled out on the mat, Tara decided to go for it all and went to the top rope. Hoping to drive her knees into Ash's back, Tara flew into the air, but Ash rolled out of the way. Tara seemed to suffer damage to her left knee and was rolling around in pain as Ash pulled herself up in the corner. Ash waited for Tara to try to stand, then charged out and nailed a dropkick to Tara's bad knee. As the twenty minute mark passed, Ash took Tara by the leg over to the bottom rope and crashed down on it several times. Ash attempted to do further damage with a figure-four leglock but Tara suddenly caught her in a small package. The referee was slow getting to the count because he was warning the other members of the Collins clan to stay off the apron. Tara managed to kickout in the nick of time and gave Tara a rake to the eyes. At the twenty-minute mark, Ash locked on the Assassination STF. Tara held out valiently for nearly a minute, but finally gave in and submitted. Winner: "English Assassin" Arian Ash by submission (STF) in 21:55 Match 13: WORLD LIGHTHEAVYWEIGHT TITLE DEFENSE Scorpio vs. Fallen Angel This matchup proved to be an amazing display of aerial skill, and fast paced technique. The former Internet Champion used his entire lucha arsenal against the Lightheavyweight Champion, who responded with a mix of high risk manuvers, aerial skill, and sound mat wrestling. The two traded hold for hold in rapid succession, almost more than the crowd could keep up with. Lightning fast reversals and pinfall attempts highlighted this matchup. At one point there were no less than eight "two-counts", in less than twenty seconds, as each man traded pin attempts. At the end, Scorpio was going for a top rope powerbomb when Fallen Angel reversed it into an amazing Angel Death Roll from the top rope. Scorpio managed to reverse it somehow before the third count, leading to a two count of his own, before Fallen Angel reversed it again, hooking both legs and bending forward to ensure the clean three-count. An amazing display of sportsmanship and technique from two outstanding wrestlers. Winner: Fallen Angel via pinfall (re-reversed Angel Death Roll) in 13:48 Match 14: WORLD TAG TEAM TITLE DEFENSE Sudden Impact vs. Murder Incorporated Due to the unpredictable result of fan voting, many wrestlers pulled double duty on night one, and the newly crowned UNIFIED World Tag Team Champions Sudden Impact were no exception. They faced the formidable duo of "Perfect Male Specimen" Jason Speece and "Dashing" Derek Lankford, wrestling together again as Murder Incorporated. Speece started out against Steve Ramsey seemed more interested in showing off to the crowd than going after Ramsey. This turned out to be merely a ploy however as Speece turned all business after Ramsey engaged him. Speece went to work on Ramsey's back with a vengeance. Each time Ramsey tried to tag out, Speece and Lankford made sure he didn't make it. Lankford and Speece tagged in and out frequently, making sure to keep Ramsey in their corner of the ring. Ramsey's forehead cut had re-opened and soon he was bleeding freely again. Lankford went to work on the cut, using the ropes, his fist, the turnbuckle and even the ring steps once. At each turn, when Scott Taylor tried to enter the ring, Lankford and Speece kept him from helping his teammate. At one point, a legal tag was made by Ramsey, but the referee didn't see it, due to Lankford's distraction. At the twenty minute mark, Ramsey had been in the ring the entire time for his team. He tried to mount an offense but the toll of the night's earlier match was too much. Speece and Lankford punished him with high-impact moves and illegal double-team tactics while the referee was keeping Taylor out of the ring. Victory seemed in the grasp for Murder Inc. when Lankford nailed one last tilt-a-whirl backbreaker and tagged in Speece. Before Speece could lock in The Display, Ramsey made a lunge for the corner and got the hot tag to Taylor. The crowd erupted with applause but the referee was busy telling Lankford to leave the ring and didn't see it. When he ordered Taylor to leave the ring, Speece locked Ramsey in The Display. Hearing the screams of his partner, Scott Taylor plowed straight through the referee and attacked Speece. Lankford came in and the two of them started pounding on Scott Taylor, but the referee called for the bell, disqualifying Taylor for assaulting an official. Sudden Impact retained the titles but took a severe beating from Murder Inc. Winner: Murder Inc. via DQ (Taylor assaulted an official) in 24:22 15. WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE DEFENSE "American Original" Tank Turner vs. "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor The main event of the night pitted yet another two titles against each other. This time however, there were no unification stipulations. The fans votes had pitted the International Champion against the World Champion and the fans were anxious to see the outcome. Tank Turner came to the ring to the cheers of the fans and chants of "Hard Corps". Chris Taylor came to the ring to cheers and chants of "Hot Stuff". Both men shook hands before the match, much to the delight of their mutal fans. Despite a rough match earlier, Tank Turner showed no sign of fatigue and displayed more of the martial arts he had learned from Jackie Chan while making a movie together. Taylor gave grudging respect to Turner when Turner connected with several combinations of strikes. Taylor then returned the favor with a flurry of kicks and strikes of his own, ending in a bizarre Jujitsu takedown into pin. Turner kicked out at two and the two men smiled and shook hands again. After locking up again, they went to more traditional holds as each man went for various holds, takedowns, suplexes and armbars. Neither man seemed able to capitalize and both were only able to get at best a one-count out of each pin attempt. At fifteen minutes tempers seemed to flare as Turner knocked Taylor out of the ring with a massive clothesline. Blaze rushed to her husband and gave Turner a wicked look, but Taylor calmed her down. Climbing back in, Taylor nailed Turner with a series of chops and kicks then Irish whipped him into the ropes and nailed him with a hard superkick to the jaw. Turner went down and rolled out of the ring, getting a moment to breathe and discuss things with Union Kane. Turner rolled back in and the two locked up again. Turner caught Taylor with a kneelift and hit a quick powerbomb. The kid gloves were obviously off. Turner started dropping elbows on Taylor and not letting him up. Taylor made it to the ropes and got up firing back with hard chops and open hand strikes. Taylor nailed a side suplex on Turner then grabbed his leg. The fans popped as Taylor went to work on Turner's knee. Taylor used knees, elbows, the bottom rope, a half crab, and even an STF to take Turner's wheels out from under him. As Taylor seemed ready to lock on the Figure-Five, he went for a kneebreaker only to have Turner block it and nail him with a short clothesline. Turner then took over and started working on Taylor's neck. The same fans popped when Turner hit a reverse neckbreaker on Taylor. After a wicked looking piledriver, Turner signalled for the 911. Turner set Taylor up on the top turnbuckle and began to climb up after him. Taylor recovered and nailed Turner in the face with a forearm shot. He followed this with a chop and soon both men were fighting each other on top of the turnbuckle. Taylor connected with a headbutt that made Turner almost fall backwards into the ring. Turner caught Taylor executed a surprise belly-to-belly superplex. Taylor barely kicked out before the third count. Turner lifted Taylor up into a gorilla press position, but his bad knee wobbled and Taylor managed to drop down behind Turner. As Turner took a limping step towards the ropes Taylor grabbed him in a full nelson. Turner attempted to get to the ropes but Taylor executed a Dragon Suplex before he could. This time the three-count landed. After the match, both men shook hands again and held their respective titles up for the cheers of the crowd. Winner: "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor via pinfall (Dragon Suplex) at 28:32 Night One had some incredible matches and several surprises.
