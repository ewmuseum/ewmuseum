— [NCW] is dead, the operator's own closure post (msg_id O3P94tnlvmAJ, 25 November 1997) —

As I announced to my handlers and fans earlier this week, I've recently closed the door on NCW, v.2
As the founder of the Anti-SHEF, I became more and more apalled at what I was not only allowing for flashes, but was writing myself...simply because my time was short.
When I first re-started NCW, people thought I was "cashing in" on the success of the old NCW. Well, as the original founder, I think I had that right! :) However, I saw it more as continuing a legacy that never should have ended.
The "new" NCW was nothing like the old, but the same old trappings (from a booker standpoint) were there: big egos, "shoot" interviews, and requests for run-ins galore! Add to that the fact that this summer my spare time dwindled to almost nothing and never returned like I thought it would in the fall, and we get a formula for failure.
Thus ends the legacy of NCW. However, I am not done with e-wrestling by a long shot! I'm going to be taking a few weeks off to figure out how deep I want to get with e-wrestling (I appreciate the Anti-SHEF still kicking.
I'll be returning to help out there :). I am, however, offering my wrestlers on the "block" for hire. I'm only going to submit them to one fed at a time, and will take the "best offer"
(send me the web address and/or FAQ, plus any "signing bonuses" you wish to throw in :) The following are my people and their availability: The Jammer: Given that the NCW fiasco ruined his credibility, the Jammer will likely retire.
Thomas N. Tyler: TNT is spoken for. Can't say much on it now, but he'll be around ;) Dixie Hart: Rumored to be heading for WWW, but nothing can be confirmed. Marcus Magus: Returned to England, giggling about what happened to the
Jammer. He's likely available, tho' he may remain with the Empire.
The Power Tools: Black & Decker are available I plan on converting the old NCW page to a tribute page for my wrestlers. We'll see what happens in the near future. [name withheld] ex-NCW Head Honcho Founder, Anti-SHEF All around nice guy .sig lost in the mail
