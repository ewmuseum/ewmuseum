NCW — Community discourse

"The Jammer's place in E-Wrestling history.."

20 – 24 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— The Jammer's place in E-Wrestling history.., a former NCW head booker's own reply (msg_id MuEYHdtTJgcJ, 20 June 1997) —

It's amazing to me how some idiot like 'DaDoctor' could say that John
'Jammer' Moore is not a good e-wrestling talent, and hated by 90% of
RSPWF.. if he IS hated, i wish more would come forward so i can see it.. i am sure he is not universally loved, but i think MOST would agree that his creation, the original NCW (and, of course, its pre-cursor RSPCW) went along way in creating what we know today as a 'good' e-fed..
how? why? well, john's style easily can be seen in 'most' good feds on the net right now.. by this i mean the presentation, jokes, and types of characters.. by
NO means, is he singularly responsible, but by KNOW means was the classic
NCW far from an influence.. as people who were around then know, NCW was
THE e-fed for almost a year.. EVERYBODY was trying to catch up, spin-off, ally with, or copy.
Funny thing is, MANY people now base their feds on the RSPCW/NCW model, and don't even KNOW it.. they are using styles they have seen, and don't know where they come from.. it is easy to START a fed, but unless you are totally original, you have to base it on something.
lineage: RSPCW begat NCW.. which led to: NCWF.. which then spawned WWW,
AAPW, and the current NCW.. i am listing only feds that started as a
DIRECT lineage of NCW.. not the many 'side' feds that only partially owe allegiance..
this being said, for some guy like the 'DaDoctor' to diss Jammer is ridiculous.. you may disagree with his politics, but you HAVE to respect his contribution to e-wrestling.. I myself hold him PERSONALLY responsible for my appearance and rise in e-wrestling, as he was the first person i talked to, and whom i obviously based my style on when i took over NCW in
'95.. just some thoughts.. [name withheld] ps: DaDoctor.. if your minions are numerous.. where are they? why do you speak in such generalities? you sound like some right-wing militia guy..

— a reply (msg_id ElQgNZqmXY4J, 20 June 1997) —

> NCW was > THE e-fed for almost a year.. EVERYBODY was trying to catch up, spin-off, > ally with, or copy. That's a bold thing to say, but it's all in opinion. From what I've seen, I still think the PCW is the best hands down, and it's something you won't know until you go in yourself and see what it's all about. It's been around for quite some time, and it has never ceased to entertain me, or most of the members of the league. I'm not bashing NCW, but I am letting you know, there is more than what there seems to be out there, and you can never judge it all until you've seen it all.
> Funny thing is, MANY people now base their feds on the RSPCW/NCW model, > and don't even KNOW it.. If they don't know it, they aren't basing it on the fed, but on their own ideas that happen to be the same as others. I'll use PCW as an example, it started as a regional, and had the pefect regional feel to it. It later expanded and went on it's own when it conflicted with the EWA and other leagues of the RSPWF labeled LOOP, which never actually existed in the way some people from RSPWF made it seem. PCW has no real affiliates, just a damn good league. What does that last line have to do with any of this? Not much.
> lineage: RSPCW begat NCW.. which led to: NCWF.. which then spawned WWW, > AAPW, and the current NCW.. i am listing only feds that started as a > DIRECT lineage of NCW.. not the many 'side' feds that only partially owe > allegiance.. PCW started as a weekly segement in the old FASW. Every Sunday they'd run off a match or two, it was REALLY regional. Then when FASW merged with EWA,
PCW became a stand alone regional to the EWA. [name withheld] did a great job running it, and when he left it was just before PCW split apart from the rest. [name withheld] is now the president, and the name PCW still stands.
> this being said, for some guy like the 'DaDoctor' to diss Jammer is > ridiculous.. you may disagree with his politics, but you HAVE to respect > his contribution to e-wrestling.. His contribution to e-wrestling would also involve what goes on in RSPWF. If he is slamming other feds because of a word or two in the name, then his contribution to e-wrestling won't be noticed, and he will be thought of as an asshole. I don't LIKE feds that close down immediately, I don't like ones that have dead people, or the words Dangerous in the name for some reason, but I don't get on here to let them know I think they're wasting their time. If this is what they want to do, let them do it. It doesn't matter what you've done in e-wrestling, it's how you maintain the image you have.
~Mike
