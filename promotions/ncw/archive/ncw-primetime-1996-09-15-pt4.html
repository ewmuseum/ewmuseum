[NCW] PRIMETIME Part 4

15 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It only happens once a year....... <Shots of superstars Chris Taylor, Todd Newkirk, Mark Destructo, Kurt Ryder, Mark "Executioner" Engel and "Mr. Electricity" Chris Edwards> An event so big, it interweaves four federations together...... <The logos for FoPEW, FWLI, NCW, and NCWF all soar by> It's The Blue Heaven Open, and this year it features what the fans have been demanding.......ONE CHAMPION. <Chris Taylor is shown wearing the NCW and FoPEW World Championships. He is profiled against another shot of Big Body Brown.> Chris Taylor, the most successful champion in recent history meets the challenge head on of Big Body Brown, the man who shocked the world with his victory over Mark Destructo for the NCWF World Title. And now, for the first time ever, the NCWF, NCW, and FoPEW World Titles will be defended IN ONE MATCH.....at the end of the BHO, there will be a Triple Crown Champion. And for the first time ever, it's Stable Wars, an event so big, it's almost unbelievable...... The Blue Heaven Open 1996: The Stable Wars. You'll never see anything like it again........ ************************************************************************** T-Bone: We've got ourselves a barnburner of a main event coming up here, buddy. And let me tell you something, Jack. These four men may have a lot in common, but they sure as hell hate each other. Flagg: And after what we saw on our last edisode of Primetime, it doesn't surprise me one bit that we would see this match. It features our own World Heavyweight Champion, "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor teaming up with his best friend, "Nature Boy" Todd Newkirk. T-Bone: Yeah, but they got a couple of bad ol' boys, you know what I'm sayin, facing up against them. And I hate both of 'em! It's those two scumbags, "Iceman" Brian Knight and his best friend, "Silent but Deadly" Doug Fury. This one is going to be hot as hell. Announcer: WRESTLING FANS.....THE FEATURED MAIN EVENT IS SCHEDULED FOR ONE FALL, WITH A TWENTY MINTUTE TIME LIMIT!!!!!! FIRST, AT A COMBINED WEIGHT OF 525 POUNDS....FIRST, FROM ATLANTA, GEORGIA, HERE IS "SILENT BUT DEADLY" DOUG FURY.....HIS PARTNER, FROM ASPEN, COLORADO, HE IS THE FWLI EUROPEAN HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, BEING LED TO THE RING BY VALET, VOGUE, HERE IS "THE ICEMAN" BRIAN KNIGHT.....TOGETHER, THEY ARE THE AAAAAAAAAAAAAWWWWWWWWWWWWWWEEEEEEEEESSSSSSSSSSSOOOOOMMMMMEEEEEEEEEEEEE AAAAAAAAAAAAAAALLLLLLLLLLLIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIANNNNNNNNNNNNNCEEEE!!!!!!!!!! <"Little Things" by Bush begins blaring over the system, as Knight, Vogue, and Fury make their way to the ring. Knight is playing to the crowd, trying to rile them, and it's working. Fury is focused on the ring only. Knight is a caucasian male with long blond hair flowing all over the place. He is athleticly built, wearing light blue tights with ice sickles running down the sides, and white boots. Vogue is wearing a DANGEROUSLY short mini-skirt and a "Brian Knight" halter top. Fury is a well built caucasian with short black hair. He wears black ring trunks with the thirteen stars of the Confederacy on the back. Both men hold open the ropes for Vogue, and Knight poses while fireworks go off around the ring.> T-Bone: I'll tell you one thing, these two are a dangerous combination, but the fact is that they are messing with WORLD Champions, and I don't think they can handle it. Flagg: Normally, I would agree with you, but there is something about Knight that causes things that shouldn't happen to occur. It's going to be close, and it should be a good match. Let's hear the introduction of their opponents! <"Little Things" by Bush dies down, as "Hot Stuff" by Donna Summer kicks in. The crowds booing explodes into a cheer as their heroes appear along the rampway.> Announcer: AND THEIR OPPONENTS, BOTH HAILING FROM STARKVILLE, MISSISSIPPII, AT A COMBINED WEIGHT OF 515 POUNDS........FIRST, HE IS THE FWLI WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION......HE IS "NATURE BOY" TOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOODDDDDDDDDDDD NEEEEEEEEWWWWWWWWKIIIIIIIIIRRRRRRRK!!!!!!! HIS PARTNER IS WRESTLING'S GREATEST CHAMPION, THE TWO TIME NCW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION OF THE WOOOOOOOORRRRRRRRRRRLLLLLLLLD, "HOT STUFF" CHRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRIIIIIIIIISSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSS TAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAYYYYYYYYLOOOOOOOOOOOORRRRRRRRR!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! <Accompanied to the ring by Blaze Taylor, Chris Taylor and Todd Newkirk walk to the ring, slapping the fans' hands. Neither wrestler is wearing their normal pre-match clothing as it's obvious these two want to get the match going. Newkirk and Taylor slide into the ring, and get face to face with Knight and Fury. Knight takes a step back, while Fury gets nose to nose with Newkirk.> T-Bone: Whoa! Looks like this one is going to be serious! Newkirk and Fury are butting heads like a couple of wild bulls! Taylor surely wants a piece of Knight after what happened at FoPEW's Hurricane Havoc. This one may be too much for the building to take! Flagg: It really might be. Got a new referee being thrown into the fire on this one, too, as Julio Rameriez steps in between these four men, and forces Taylor and Knight out of the ring! That's guts ball, folks! T-Bone: I have a feeling Rameriez isn't going to allow this one to get out of control. He calls for the bell, and we are set to go! DING DING DING Flagg: Newkirk and Fury circle each other and lock up! Fury shoves Newkirk off, and delivers a savate kick! Fury quickly going back to his UFC training, and executes a headscissors submission! T-Bone: Ah, but there's loyalty, as Taylor jumps in and makes an immediate save! Newkirk hits the ropes, as Rameriez orders Taylor out of the ring. Newkirk drills Fury with a kick! Newkirk props himself and tries one of them enzuilariatoes, but Fury counters it with a duck-down! Flagg: You're getting better with some of these names, T. Now it's Fury to the ropes, but Newkirk drills Fury with a chop knocking him to the floor! Newkirk grabs Fury, and sends him to the corner, tagging in Chris Taylor! Taylor and Newkirk put Fury on the top rope! DOUBLE SUPERPLEX BY CHRIS TAYLOR AND TODD NEWKIRK!!!!! T-Bone: But it's not enough to keep Fury down, as he is already getting up. Taylor and Newkirk whip Fury to the ropes, and attempt a double clothesline! Fury ducks, and delivers a double clothesline of his own! Newkirk leaves the ring, but Fury executes one of those enzuilariatoes on Chris Taylor! Flagg: There's a stir in the crowd. MARK DESTRUCTO IS COMING TOWARDS THE RING!!!! T-Bone: And who's side is HE going to be on? We know that he's no longer a member of Damage Control, Inc., but he has a hatred for members of both teams here. Doug Fury, meanwhile is trying to lock on that cross-face bow and arrow he calls The Southern Cross, but Taylor is fighting it. Taylor is up, and he nails a boot to the midsection of Fury! POWERBOMB TIME BY CHRIS TAYLOR!!!!! Rameriez is in position, but can only get a two count! Flagg: And we almost saw why Chris Taylor is the World Champion. He can pull out those moves. Taylor whips Fury to the ropes, and sets for a Hot Shot, but Fury drills counters with a lariat. Fury quickly tags in Brian Knight, who was asking to tag in. T-Bone: And he waited until Taylor was on the mat to do it! He comes behind Taylor, and begins slapping him in the back of the head! This is just wrong, people! Knight tries to whip Taylor to the ropes, but Taylor reverses, AND DESTRUCTO TRIPS KNIGHT UP!!!!!!!! Flagg: Well, we can see an early loyalty so far. And Knight doesn't look happy about it! He's yelling at Taylor for Destructo's interference! And now there's a slap fight going on in the ring! T-Bone: And now Taylor is going for Knight's finisher, The Northern Lights Suplex, but Knight blocks it! He lifts him up for a Tombstone! Taylor is kicking, AND REVERSES IT!!!!! Knight's head bounced off the mat! Taylor grabs Knight's leg, and tries for a figure four, but Knight turns it into a modified monkey flip! Flagg: Knight quickly delivers a standing dropkick! Taylor gets up, and delivers another dropkick! Knight leaps to the top rope, and launches off with a flying clothesline, but Taylor ducks it, with Knight bouncing off the mat! T-Bone: And now Newkirk is in the ring, as Taylor picks Knight up! Newkirk goes for a superkick, but Knight ducks, AND IT CONNECTS RIGHT WITH CHRIS TAYLOR'S CHIN!!!!!! Flagg: And that's not sitting well with Taylor, who's up quickly, and yelling at his partner! Newkirk is trying to calm his partner down, and quickly shoves him out of the way of an oncoming Brian Knight! Newkirk took the full blast of that clothesline, and Taylor retaliates on his opponent with a swinging neckbreaker! T-Bone: What a sacrifice by Newkirk! Fury comes in to doubleteam Taylor, drilling him from behind with a bulldog! Fury and Knight deliver a double elbowdrop, and Fury is quickly out of the ring again. Flagg: Solid doubleteaming early on from Knight and Fury. Knight picks Taylor up, and delivers a short clothesline! Knight scoops Taylor up, and this time he connects with a Tombstone! Knight begins to taunt the crowd, but Taylor is up to his feet! Knight turns around, and Taylor scoops him up, and delivers a Hot Shot! T-Bone: And now Taylor is signalling for the Figure Five! He's almost got it on, and Knight counters it with a small package! Rameriez counts it....1.....2......Taylor is in the ropes! Flagg: And now it's Knight with a swinging neckbreaker! Knight climbs onto the second turnbuckle, and leaps off with a flying clothesline! Vogue is up on the ring apron, AND KNIGHT KICKS TAYLOR IN A VICIOUS LOW BLOW!!!! T-Bone: That Vogue is like a third tag team partner in this match! Taylor is now crawling over to his corner, and tagging in Newkirk! Now, we've got all three in the ring! Taylor and Newkirk grab Newkirk, and fire him to the ropes! They nail him with a double elbow, and that has Knight staggered! Flagg: Now it's Newkirk scooping up Knight! Taylor hits the ropes, but Knight ducks the clothesline, and Newkirk gets nailed! Knight turns it into a roll-up! 1.......2.......and Taylor makes the save! Newkirk and Taylor still having problems early on in this match! T-Bone: Tempers are flaring, and it's causing mistakes to be made! Newkirk rolls to his feet, and sidesteps a dropkick by Knight! Newkirk let's out one hell of a "Whoo", and Knight is backing off! I swear, if Newkirk had a strap on his trunks, he'd have pulled it down right there! Flagg: Newkirk launches a superkick, but Knight dives out of the way of it! And now Doug Fury is in! He grabs Newkirk, and launches him to the ropes! Powerslam by Doug Fury! Rameriez is ordering Fury out of the ring, but Knight is already capitalizing on the move. He's trying to set up a piledriver, but Newkirk is blocking it! T-Bone: And Vogue is back up on the ring apron, but this time Blaze Taylor is up there with her! This could get interesting! Rameriez is ordering both women off the apron, but they're not listening to him! Flagg: Newkirk meanwhile has taken over, and whips Knight to the buckle! Newkirk charges in, but Knight takes him down with a superkick! Knight tags out to Fury, but Rameriez is still having a problem with Blaze and Vogue! T-Bone: We really threw this referee into the fire with this one, didn't we? Fury and Knight fire Newkirk to the ropes, but it's Newkirk this time with a double clothesline! Newkirk grabs Fury, and sends him over with a snap suplex! Newkirk now tags out to Chris Taylor! Taylor and Newkirk deliver a sharp double chop, and Newkirk leaves the ring! Flagg: Taylor grabs Fury, and locks on a waistlock! Fury can't get out of it, and is crushed to the mat with a German suplex, but Rameriez is still tied up with Blaze and Vogue! Taylor releases it, and climbs the ropes! He's going for a flying senton, but Knight runs over, and shoves him off the turnbuckle! What a cheap shot! T-Bone: Taylor is clutching his ankle! He might have sprained it there. Fury spins Taylor over, and locks on a half Boston crab! He's signalling for the Southern Cross! He's got it on tight, but Taylor digs his hand into the face of Fury, which causes "SbD" to release the hold! Taylor goes for a victory roll, but Fury turns it over into a powerbomb! Flagg: But Rameriez is STILL trying to get Blaze and Vogue off the ring apron! Fury whips Taylor to the ropes, and crushes him with a shoulderblock! And now Knight is BACK in the ring, and delivers a dropkick at the same time Fury executes an enzuilariato! And Taylor is still clutching his ankle. T-Bone: Rameriez is ordering both women off the apron, or he's going to disqualify both teams! Fury picks Taylor up, and executes a DDT! Now it's an enzuigiri by Fury! Fury is becoming the methodical monster we're used to seeing! Fury picks Taylor up, and tries a cobra clutch, but Taylor turns it into an armdrag takedown! Fury is back up quickly, but not for long, AS TAYLOR PLOWS HIM WITH A SPINEBUSTER SLAM!!!!! 1.......2......Fury just able to get a shoulder up! Flagg: And now Taylor dives to the corner, and tags in Todd Newkirk! Newkirk jumps into the ring, and charges Fury! Fury ducks, and drills Newkirk with an enzuilariato! Fury grabs Newkirk and fires him to the ropes, and Knight pulls down the top rope! Newkirk tumbles out of the ring, and hits the floor! T-Bone: That was NOT fair! I swear, I'm about ready to go down to that ring, and take care of things myself! Fury is now climbing out of the ring, and executes a knee to the back, sending Newkirk into the ringsteps! Fury charges in, and nails Newkirk in the head with a boot! Flagg: What in the hell is Fury doing? He's setting up a table! He's pulling Newkirk up on the table! He's setting up for a DDT, but Mark Destructo grabs Fury, and pulls him off! Fury and Destructo are in a vicious staredown, as Newkirk rolls back into the ring, and tags in Taylor! T-Bone: Fury climbs back into the ring, looks at Taylor, and tags in Brian Knight! This match may go the distance, with the timely tags these two teams make! Knight locks a waistlock, and goes for a German suplex, but Taylor retaliates with an elbowsmash! Flagg: And he follows it up with a back brain kick, which sends Knight to the ropes! SPINEBUSTER BY CHRIS TAYLOR!!!! Taylor climbs to the second rope, and delivers a fistdrop! Taylor hooks a leg! 1......2..... and Knight is able to get a foot on the rope! Taylor's questioning the count, and Knight delivers a dropkick to the back of Taylor's head! T-Bone: And Rameriez is taken down with the same shot! Knight tries to put Taylor on the top turnbuckle, but he can't do it! Knight swings his leg around, and delivers a sharp kick to the ankle of Taylor! Taylor drops to the mat! Knight is parading around the ring, but Chris and Blaze are close to each other, and she's handing something to him! Flagg: He's got one of those fireballs! He's going to light Knight up like a Roman candle! Knight sees it, and takes it out of the hand of Chris Taylor! HE LAUNCHES IT AT CHRIS TAYLOR!!!!! T-Bone: Taylor was able to block most of it, but it was a painful move! Knight goes for a pin! Rameriez is slowly getting to his feet, but he doesn't see the pin! Fury is now back in the ring, and delivers a savate kick, while Knight rolls Taylor up from behind! Newkirk is in, but Fury blocks him! Flagg: And this time Rameriez sees it! He's counting a pin! Taylor badly injured in this match, now trapped in this pin! 1........Newkirk can't get to him..2.......DESTRUCTO IS IN THE RING, AND HE GRABS KNIGHT!!!!! CHOKESLAM BY MARK DESTRUCTO!!!!!!!!! T-Bone: And Rameriez is calling for the bell! He's going to disqualify Taylor and Newkirk, but now Destructo has ahold of Fury! ANOTHER CHOKESLAM BY MARK DESTRUCTO!!!!! Flagg: But here comes some help for Knight and Fury, as "Dashing" Derek Lankford and "Perfect Male Specimen" Jason Speece hit the ring, and begin double teaming Destructo! Knight and Fury are back up, and also begin to attack Destructo. T-Bone: Speece, Lankford, Knight, and Fury are being hit by the fans with cups and popcorn boxes, as the four of them attack Destructo, Taylor, and Newkirk. This is just wron......"HEARTBREAKER" MATT FOXX HAS HIT THE RING WITH THAT STEEL CHAIN!!!! Flagg: The fans are on their feet, as he swings the chain over his head, and lunges towards Knight! The Awesome Alliance dives from the ring, and is making their way up the aisle. Foxx now has the house mic. Let's listen in! Foxx: HEY, KNIGHT! What the hell is the matter, pal? You got three guys behind you, and you still gotta duck for cover? Well, if you'll look behind me, you'll see three guys too. I'm challenging you, pal. Your Awesome Alliance, versus me, and the men behind me. Four on four, Knight! You up to the challenge, or are you as yellow as I KNOW you are? <Crowd pops, as Knight gets a real nasty look on his face. Lankford, Speece, and Fury are all trying to get Knight to calm down. Charles Ikern heads down the aisle way, where The Awesome Alliance is standing.> Ikern: Brian Knight, you look like a man posessed right now. You've heard the challenge.... Lankford: Hey, Bri, man, we don't need this. We've got BHO to worry about, not these clowns. Speece: Yeah, "Ice". These guys are nothing. We've got bigger fish to fry. Fury: Brian. C'mon, brother. Let's get out of here. Knight: NO!!!!! Matt Foxx, you've been a thorn in my side for so DAMN long, I can barely feel the sting of it anymore. Yet, everytime I show up, there you are. Well, FINE!!!!!! You want a match? Four on four? My Awesome Alliance against a bunch of hasbeens? FINE!!!!! You got it, pal. <Fury, Speece, and Lankford have a look of exhasperation on their faces, as The Awesome Alliance leaves the ringside area.> T-Bone: Well, it looks like we've got ourselves a future match-up. Taylor is up, with help from Mark Destructo and Todd Newkirk, I never thought I'd say that statement! Flagg: Well, Knight's squad didn't look too happy about it. We'll have to see what happens when the sides are even. That's it for tonight fans! See you next time!
