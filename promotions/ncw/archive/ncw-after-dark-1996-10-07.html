[NCW] After Dark

7 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The following is a house show report for the NCW Oct 4, 1996 Dallas, Texas (Reunion Arena) Match 1: Overgiver v. "Boogie Man" Chuck Disco The night's opening bout pitted two youngsters to the Light-Heavyweight Division...The Overgiver dominated early action with a series a high impact suplexes and bodyslams, but Disco quickly go things under control and began to work on the knee area of The Overgiver...Disco slapped on his patented finisher the Saturday Night fever (STF) and the Overgiver surprisingly held on for three minutes until he could reach the ropes and then began to mount a comeback..That comeback fell short with Disco countered a superplex with a high cross body and got the pinfall.. Winner: "Boogie Man" Chuck Disco (via Pinfall at 10:27) Match 2: The Eves of Destruction (Eve & Evie) v. The Rage Sisters The Eves started this match out with real intensity, quick tags, and lots of double teaming...But the Rage sisters quickly recovered and began to work on the younger Evie's back area..At one point Dalbello suplexed Evie on the steel steps...Out of desparation Evie raked Medusa eyes and tagged in a fresh Eve who quickly finished off Medusa with three piledrivers in a row.. Winners: Eves of Destruction (via Pinfall <Eve pinned Dalbello> at 8:43) Match 3: Airborn vs. Cardiac (NCW Juniors Title Match) Cardiac dominated Airborn throughout this match with a series of high-risk moves, most of which were done outside the ring...Cardiac got 6 very questionable two-counts during the match...Unfortunately for Cardiac he became too overzealous and missed a moonsault which gave Airborn the advantage he needed...Airborn wins thanks to the Superplex and retains his title Winner: Airborn (via pinfall at 14:27) Match 4: "Too Slick" Rick Brown v. Marcus Magus In a match that never really was, the popular newcomer Brown easily dominated Magus on all fronts and finished him off with the "Slickbuster", a belly to belly off the top turnbuckle.. Winner: "Too Slick" Rick Brown (via pinfall at 2:31) Match 5: "Method Man" Kurt Ryder v. "The Nature Boy" Tood Newkirk (NCW International Title Match) In a battle of painful submission holds, both sportsmen put on an excellent show for the fans...The Nature Boy took control in the early going, using suplexes, bodyslams, armbars, and other wrestling move in his repetoire to weaken Ryder..Ryder regained the momentum after Newkirk made a rare mistak of putting his head down...Ryder began to work on Newkirk's armbar with various armbars, hammerlock slams, and his favorite HIJAACK SUPLEX...Somehow Newkirk managed to recover and late in the match he slipped on The Nature's Pain, but Ryder slipped out after only a minute and the two traded moves back and forth...Newkirk gained the advantage again but Ryder reverse a clothesline into a crucifix pinning combination and retained his title Winner: "Method Man" Kurt Ryder (via pinfall at 26:50) Match 6: Blaze Taylor v. Famine THis match was very exciting with both women putting together nice moves, but soon after the match begun, members of the Awesome Alliance came out and disrupted action by jumping Chris Taylor on the outside...Todd Newkirk, still a little groggy after his match, came down with a chair and action spilled into the ring..The ref had no choice but to call this match a no-contest... Winner: Referee Nothen declared the match a No-Contest due to outside interference.. Match 7: Adam v. "Dashing" Derek Lankford (NCW Internet title match) Another great title match as two very established stars wrestled for Lankford's Internet title...Adam took early control and still showed signs of his mean streak...Adam bloodied Lankford with a running bulldog and worked on Lankford with a series of rest holds...Lankford gave Adam a low blow and changed the tide by using high-impact wrestling moves along with some chokes and eye gauging...Adam slowly recovered and gave Lankford a piledriver that almost pinned him, Adam also made the mistake of putting him head down, Lankford nailed Adam with a cradle suplex and retained his title.. Winner: "Dashing" Derek Lanford (via Pinfall at 21:13) Match 8: "Powerhouse" Scott Rogers v. "Iceman" Brian Knight Unfortunately this is another match that never became...As Rogers was walking down the aisle he was attacked by members of the Awesome Alliance..Nothen called for the DQ.. Winner: Scott Rogers (via DQ <outside interference> at 0:00) Match 9: Kid Cobra v. Jammer The disdain these two athletes have for each other made for an exciting match..The disciplined Kid Cobra took early control with a series of chops, kicks, and aerial manuevers..But Jammer used a spinebuster to end Kid Cobra's assualts and used his repetoire of power moves to weaken the lighter Kid Cobra...Kid Cobra regained control after breaking out of a chinlock...Kid almost pinned the Jammer after a reverse savate kick but he made the mistake of going after TNT who slipped some brass kuncks to Jammer while Nothen was breaking TNT and Kid C up..Jammer nailed Kid C. in the back of the head with the knucks and slipped them in his tights before Nothen could catch it..Jammer easily pinned Kid Cobra afterwards Winner: Jammer (via pinfall at 13.05) Match 10: Jana the Spider v. Dixie Hart (NCW Women's title in a cage) The wily cage veteran Jana the Spider took action in the beginning of this match...Jana used a her leaping abilities and her cage pyschology to confuse the champ..But Dixie quickly got the feel for the cage and began to mount a heavy offense...Although Jana gave DIxie a frankensteiner off the top of the cage for the move of the night, Dixie gave Jana a tour of the cage walls and exited out of the door, bloodied and beaten but still champion... Winner: Dixie Hart (via exiting out of the Cage Door at 23:34) Match 11: Fallen Angel v. Mandarin (L-H title match) After an exciting entrance the Fallen angel unleashed a new ground attack which kept the Mandarin off-beat and confused..The Mandarin recovered and used his various martial arts skills along with a painful pressure hold to take control of the match...But the Angel broke out and used his customary Lucha Libra-type moves for 5 two counts...The end came when the Hard Ridin' Man's interference backfired on him as he nailed the Mandarin with the chair..The Angel quickly rolled Mandarin up for the win. Winner: Fallen Angel (via pinfall at 16:34) Main Event: "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor v. Thomas N. Tyler These two rivals began their match quickly by pounding on each other with right hands...Tyler took the early advantage due to some outside interference by Jammer and his array of illegal chokes and throat holds...Taylor quickly rebounded and began to nail TNT with his favorite Suplexes, Bodyslams, and a very powerful tilt-a-whirl backbreaker...Taylor had TNT screaming in pain when slapped on the Figure FIve leglock...But Jammer entered the ring and stopped the match...TNT regained himself as he and Jammer worked pounding on Taylor...Out of the stand came Gary Trent with a chair and the four men fought back into the dressing room..Nothen called for the DQ due to Jammer's interference and Taylor retained the title.
