[NCW] Open message to NCW and Chris Taylor

28 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Memorial Day has come and gone. I hope all of you took the time to remember those brave men and women who made the ultimate sacrifice for their country. But I'm sure there is one whom none of you thought of yesterday, since his name has no association with bravery or sacrifice whatsoever. I'm talking to you, "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor. This feud between you and I, it's been going on close to 2 1/2 years now. And if you take a straw poll of the people in e-wrestling and asked them who was gotten the better of this feud, I bet nine out of then of them would saw it was you, Chris. And I can understand that, afterall, you've won most of the matches, and you've managed to grab more title belts than I. I won't go into the reasons for that, but facts are facts, and those cannot be denied. And yet, throughout these two and a half years, whenever there has been a Taylor vs Petrow title match, it has always been The Heartbreak Kid defending his title, always been the Heartbreak Kid putting his ass on the line. Whenever I have a belt around *my* waist, you're the first one in line asking for a title shot! But when you get a belt? It becomes a desperate obsession to avoid me at all costs! Tell me Chris Taylor, tell me one time when you went into a match with the Heartbreak Kid with anything to lose? What, you say? The loser leaves RSPCW match? Please...that was a set-up and you know it! A spur of the moment challenge made in your hometown with everything to your advantage. And I still had you pinned for a full minute! Funny how the ref was blind to that happening though. You knew you couldn't lose that match, just like you knew you couldn't lose back in South Africa. You may have all your fans snowed over, but I know what you're all about. And you know it too. So I come to you with a chance to redeem yourself. You owe this to me, you owe this to history, and you owe this to yourself. I want a shot at the NCW World Heavyweight Championship. Just a simple match, no special rules, just you and me, one title shot, and let the chips fall where they may. If I win, I will fulfill my obligations and defend that belt to the best of my ability. And if you win...hey, you're the champ! Whatever you want. Name your stipulations, and I will accept them. The ball's in your court, Chris. I await your response. "The Heartbreak Kid" Joe Petrow
