NCW RINGSTORM REVIEW

14 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
My Review of NCW R I N G S T O R M !!! - hope yah like it/appreciate it ------------------------------------------------------------------------- -- Cardiac Interview -> I thought it was a great interview! I especially liked the hitting the punching bag showing what he was going to do to Pierre Lambert. Pierre Lambert vs Cardiac -> A total brawl other than the butterfly suplex, a side russian leg sweep, and a slingshot suplex. I figured it would end in a DQ.. making this feud not end, but probably escalate even more! I look forward to their next match. Rage Interview -> I was glad to see Rage making a return! And with a nice interview to boot.. letting us know what he did when he was away.. getting a title in another league is cool.. and don't think I didn't miss the "original bad boy" tag in there.. <grin> WAR vs Rage -> Wow! Talk about a FAST match! I couldn't believe it! I expected Rage to win it in his return match and all, but Geeze! Clothesline.. Whip into Adam.. The Ragin' Slam, and that's IT! Wow! Like I said.. shocking! "Heartbreaker" Matt Foxx Interview -> He's in the Octagon match.. but he's having women problems. The interview was a basic, "i'm gonna win!" type one, but good none-the-less. "American Original" Tank Turner vs Taifun for the International Championship (Backlot Brawl, No DQ, No Countout, falls count anywhere in Georgia. Guest ref, Jackie Chan) -> Chan gets hit by a car, and then gets back up?! Come on.. :-) I liked the idea of the match, but was not expecting the cutting away for another match.. that part I did not like.. although obviously this made the match longer, yah missed a lot of action while the next match was taking place.. the match comes back with hot wings in the face.. some might think it humorous, but I didn't like it. It comes back once again and Taifun with a trash can over and over onto Turner, but couldn't take him out. I woulda thought it'd been over by now with all the brutality out on the streets.. A stop sign to the head doesn't seem to phaser Tank? Wow. It comes back again to a laundramat.. Turner beats him up in there a bit.. Taifun onto a bus, and Turner takes the NCW van and chases after him.. geeze! Eventually the match ends in what must have taken hours! Into WCW studios.. although it was amuzing, I dunno.. the whole match just seemed hokey. 911 from Tank Turner gets the win, and the International Championship! Famine vs Dixie Hart -> This match started with Famine ruffing Dixie up with her brawling moves.. but then a mistake turned it to Hart's favor.. and she kept it for a LONG TIME! Eventually she executes a Piledriver to perfection, and it should have been over right there! The Rebel Yell follows and of course, that was all she wrote! A nice match if you enjoy brawling.. there was also a bit of wrestling, as well as cheating.. by Hart even.. kinda surprized me. Jammer Interview -> Kinda short and to the point. Basically saying he and The Alliance were gonna clean up at Ring Storm. Jammer vs The Fallen Angel for the Light-Heavyweight Championship -> Scorpio, The Amazing Love Machine, and "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor were all at ringside for this one. The match itself was a very exciting matchup! The Jammer started off strong, and would have gotten the win by countout, but since there must be a decisive winner, the match continued.. The match was a great back and forth battle, and ended with a great scientific wrestling move! The Fallen Angel wins it and is for the 2nd time, NCW Light-Heavyweight Champion of the World! And you can bet, I'm as extatic as all the Angel fans out there! <grin> "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor Interview -> It was a nice interview.. Recapping the recent past probs with Blaze and Jammer, as well as Hot Stuff's insight into the other participants in the Octagon Match. Jammer comes in and messes up the interview by attacking a ref! Then, what do we see? Jammer sobbing because of the loss to Angel! Definitly unexpected! And maybe Jammer is still face, as I thought he might be turning heel because of his recent actions. The Icemaiden vs Jana the Spider in a Steel Cage Escape Match -> Nice comments by Airborne about the good looks of the women in the match. Too bad those cage bars somewhat block the view, eh? Nice flying moves off the cage by Jana.. gotta like that in a cage match. I liked the figure 4, trying to keep Jana from climbing the cage.. a nice strategy. And an even nicer strategy when Jana uses mist on Icemaiden. I was amazed when Icemaiden powered out of the Spider's Prey! WOW! A win with some brass knucks for Jana the Spider. A Stable vs Alliance in a War Games Match -> Evie started out against Kevin "The Cobra" Richards. I figured Richards would take it to Evie, and he did. But then Eve comes to the rescue. For a short time, until Mandarin comes in and takes out Evie again. The Amazing Love Machine comes in next, as Jammer and Chris Taylor clean up the scum on the outside. TALM cleans house. Lots of blood in this one, as expected. Blaze comes in and Frankenblazes the Mandarin! But Mandarin comes right back. Even more Brass Knucks.. I think i'm seeing a theme here.. hehe. The Hard Ridin' Man comes out last and Forces Evie to submit. The A Stable continues the assault after the match. I liked the comments by 'A' during the match. Dixie Hart came down! She and the Hard Ridin' Man back together!? Well.. no.. that would be HRM's hands! Mandarin Interview -> I liked it.. basically the men of the A Stable fought a couple women, when who they really wanted was Jammer and Taylor... especially Jammer. Jammer and Dixie Hart Interview -> Jammer accepts challenge from Mandarin.. Dixie wants Hard Ridin' Man in the ring. Pretty short flash. Arsenal vs The Stylistics in a Barbed Wire Cage Match for the World Tag-Team Titles (no DQ, no count out) -> Silo X and The Phantom Striker come out early, just as the Barbed wire comes into effect. The Stylistics use their great wrestling ability and teamwork to pound on Arsenal. I love their moves. Murder Inc though comes into the cage. I can't believe what happened to the Stylistics! Arsenal become the NEW NCW World Tag-Team Champs! Wow! This match obviously favored Arsenal, and the Stylistics should get a rematch under rules more to their tastes... that is.. if they ever wrestle again. Airborn Interview -> Nice little interview. Basically saying he's still undefeated, and will win the match. Airborn vs "The Incubus" Ron Collins -> The best match on the card so far! A great back and forth battle! Powerbomb wins it for Airborn gets the win! "Black Panther" Paul Paris vs The Profit$ in an LA Riot Match -> Paul Paris not a dumb man, he gets a replacement team.. The Devil's Reverse! I can't believe these 2 new guys took care of Centurion and Bodybag Jones so easily! And that they were so tough! Man! And after the match, another SHOCKER as his men turn on Paul Paris! I have an idea who The Devil's Reverse are.. but I'll keep it in.. :-) Arian Ash Interview -> Definitly interesting.. I like the intentional mispellings of words in it to help the effect. "The English Assassin" Arian Ash vs Jessica Justice for the Women's Title -> Nice strategy by Ash to work the leg relentlessly. A nice clean matchup by both women. Nice series of pinfalls at the end to finally crown a new Woman's Champ! Very nicely written match! "The Preppie" Tiffany Van Heusen vs "The Ambusher" Mike Trent for the Juniors Title -> A criss-cross in this one! Haven't seen that in a while.. An all around great match that went back and forth. Distraction by Lady Rose gives The Preppie the Juniors Title! Pestilence vs Miko "Steel Rose" Mizadori -> Lots of action commentated by Kane. It was a little hard to read because of the long paragraphs. Maybe break it up a bit more with Ka$h and Airborne. Miko wins it but then Adam attacks her! But it doesn't last long, and we don't see what was done. Scorpio vs Tara "The Terminator" Collins for the Internet Title -> I don't really like men vs women matches such as this, but I was happy with the outcome. Scorpio wins it with The Brazilian Bombshell! Lankford/Speece Interview -> Poorly placed as Lankford said whoever wins the lightheavyweight title match would be #2 on his list.. but that match was earlier in the card, so he would have known. Other than that, I thought it was a great interview! Derek Lankford vs OG Gary Trent -> Spinebuster on the concrete from Lankford! Wow! Then with a chair nails him repetedly! DDT on the concrete! Trent is dead! Well.. he shoulda been, but comes right back! OG wins it with the Torture Rack! Thomas N Tyler and Black Flagg vs The Armed Forces (Guest ref, "The Wolf" Lucas Garou) -> Some funny comments on the age of these men in this one. Especially liked the "Wheelchair Smash". Belly to belly off the top was a great move from Black Flagg. Flying Bulldog by Jack Flagg wins it, and.. as suspected, The Armed Forces win it. TNT didn't like it though and took out Jack with The Pain Explosion! Black Flagg turns on TNT! Now that, was not suspected. Octagon Match Promo -> Nice little promo shot showing the 8 men in the match. "Powerhouse" Scott Rogers vs "Iceman" Brian Knight vs "The Professional" Nick Coletti vs NUKE vs "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor vs "Heartbreaker" Matt Foxx vs Adam vs Mark Desertucto in an Octagon Match for the World Heavyweight Title -> What can I say? WOW! This one definitly lived up to expectations! We saw many of these wrestlers best moves and finishers.. We saw by far the best match on the card, and possibly the best match of any card in the recent past! It was... GREAT! This review was brought to you by Mark Stritar, and although handler of The Fallen Angel, he tried not to have that interfere in his review of the PPV, especially the L-H match. <Big Grin!>
