NCW — Card

NCW Mayhem — three titles on the line

26 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Network Championship Wrestling presents NCW Mayhem ANNOUNCERS: Shawn Tyler & Thomas N. Tyler Shawn asks TNT if there is any tension within the Pack due to Mists's entry. TNT says that the Jammer knows what he's doing, even if he
[meaning TNT] doesn't. WORLD WOMEN'S TITLE MATCH (Gauntlet, part 1) Eve v. Lupa (w/the Jammer and Fang) LADY ROSE: The following match is for the faux-NCW World Woman's Championship. The challenger is ranked #5 by the NCW Championship Committee...and who says the Jammer doesn't have sway? She hails from under a rock in the Appalacian Mountains and weighs 68.2 kilos. She is escorted to the ring by her sugar-daddy the Jammer and her mate Fang. She is Lupa.
LADY ROSE: The champion is the only singles titleholder worthy of the title Champion...but it doesn't change the fact that the title she holds is second-rate and beneath my attentions. She is a former TRUE NCW
Women's Champion. She hails from Paradise and weighs 72.2 kilos. She is
Eve. Lupa jumped the top rope and began attacking Eve before she got halfway down the aisle. However, a couple of swats from Eve's Singapore cane halted Lupa's offensive. Eve whipped Lupa into the ring apron and pulled her in the ring by her hair. Eve began to methodicly work over Lupa's head and neck area to soften her up for the Edge of Night (back brain kick).
However, Lupa managed to duck Eve's finishing blow and threw her to the outside, executing a suicide dive that nearly hit Lady Rose (and she made
SURE Dixie was there to keep them away from her!). At that time a fan nailed Lupa with a chair. It was revealed that the "fan" was Dan Special.
Mya then pulled Lupa away and executed a DDT on the conctete. TNT dropped his headset and joined the fray as Jammer jumped Mya.
RESULT: Double COR. Eve retains title. The action spilled into the ring after Tony Nelson and Linda Lyon ran to ringside. They pulled Lupa in the ring as Lyon slapped on the Lion's lair
(scorpion Deathlock) and Nelson stomped her head. Then (just to add to the confusion) Jana, Mistress of Spiders jumped into the ring via a flying clothesline to Lyon. Jana then dragged Lupa from the ring. She stared at
Eve for a while, then turned and walked away as Dixie Hart tried to clear the ring (only to be called back for "protection" any time any wrestler came within ten feet of Lady Rose)
WORLD INTERNET TITLE MATCH: Lord of Darkness v. ... LADY ROSE: The following match is for the faux-NCW World Internet Championship. The so-called champion...*sigh* if I must, then I must...is from Parts Unknown, because no self-respecting locale would have him. He is led to the ring by the Devastator and weighs 145.9 kilos. He is, to coin Shamrock's pseudonym for him, Fred.
LADY ROSE: His opponent...oh my. I give this match thirty seconds! He is from Chicago, Illinois. He weighs 131.4 kilos and is a member of the faux-NCW World tag-team Champions. He is "Deadly" Dirk Venom.
Thirty seconds was all it took. Venom nailed the Lord of Darkness with an uppercut and whipped him to the ropes, but "Dashing" Derek Lankford appeared from under the ring and yanked on the ropes, causing the Lord of
Darkness to spill over the side. The referee saw it and called for the bell.
WINNER: the Lord of Darkness by DQ Venom then stormed out of the ring and took a swing at Lankford, but
Lankford ducked the blow and nailed Venom on the head with a pair of brass knuckles. Lankford was out of the ballroom before Venom hit the floor.
NCW WORLD TAG-TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: Bad Company v. Sinister Squad LADY ROSE: The following match is for the faux-NCW World Tag-Team Championship. The Champions are the only REAL team in NCW. They weigh
271.8 kilos. They are "Flashy" Freddie Massey and "Downed" Dirk Venom,
Bad Company.
LADY ROSE: Their challengers are the best thing to come out of Germany since Hitler, Volkswagens, the Berlin Wall...hmmm...I guess nothing good
HAS come out of Germany except these two. They weigh 230.9 kilos. They and Karl "the Krippler" Kruger and "Violent" Victor Frost, the Sinister
Squad.
It was obvious early on that Venom was groggy from Lankford's attack as he sat out most of the match. Frost and Massey started things off.
Massey grounded Frost with a drop toe-hold and began working on his back. Massey with a whip to the ropes and catches Frost with an elbow to the throat. Cover, but broken up by Kruger. Frost tags Kruger.
Kruger nailed Massey with a shot to the "lower abdominal region." Venom came in, and took a swing, but his shot went wide and he was tripped by
Kruger. At that point, Frost re-entered and both men began working over
Venom.
Massey, apparently tired of being ignored, grabbed Kruger and threw him into the timekeeper's table (which threw Lady Rose into a fit!), then nailed a reverse DDT on Frost. He was about to cover when Barbie Special made her way to ringside and jumped on the apron. Massey took a swing at her, but Mya jumped clear. Frost grabbed Massey from behind for the 3 count
NEW CHAMPIONS: Sinister Squad (c) 1997 JammerWolf Productions
