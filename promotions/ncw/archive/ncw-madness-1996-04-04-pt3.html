[NCW] Madness! 4-04-96, Pt3

14 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TNT: Hey! This is Japan! Anyway, I understand A-Man will be unveiling that A-Stable soon.. Fletcher: The following contest is our MAIN EVENT.. A BUNKHOUSE MATCH!! The winner of this event will be determined to be the last man standing in the ring.. Competitors can bring and use ANY items they can carry on their person.. The winner will face the winner of The Scorpio/Jammer “I Quit” match on WarZone at RingStorm, for the NCW World Light-Heavyweight Championship! (big pop) Introducing first.. From Compton, California.. representing STREET LEVEL.. (big pop) 5’11” tall.. 243 lbs.. “OG” G A R Y T R E N T !! (“Damn It Feels Good To Be a Gangsta” by Scarface blares on the PA. Gary Trent walks down to the ring, shaking hands with all the hardcore Japanese fans. He has on shiny black military style boots, long black and grey camos, a flannel shirt with ripped sleeves, and a black bandanna. He also has a knapsak on his back with a baseball bat hanging out) TNT: Look out! That guy might have a GUN in there! Fletcher: Next.. From Mesa, Arizona.. 5’10” tall.. 244 lbs.. THE FALLEN ANGEL!! (A thunderclap is heard.. The Fallen Angel is carried into the ring by a throng of Japanese children, as a heavenly singing is heard. He is wearing a heavily padded variation of his usual attire) Fletcher: Now.. from Bloomsburg, Pennsylvania.. (the crowd starts booing) representing Murder Incorporated.. 5’9” tall.. 245 lbs.. “Dashing” D E R E K L A N K F O R D !! (“Murder Incorporated” by Bruce Springsteen plays, as Derek comes out. He is wearing a motorcycle helmeet, a Murder Inc football jersey with pads, some ripped jeans, and cowboy boots.) Fletcher: The final opponent.. from Stonehenge... 5’11” tall.. 236 lbs.. This.. is.. M A R C U S M A G U S !! (“Exodus” blares on the PA, as thunder and lightning strike. Magus comes out, wearing a long dark shroud with ancient symbols on it. His face if barely visible) Tuttle: Marcus Magus makes his usually startling arrival.. We are almost underway, as each competitor is stationed in a corner.. there goes the bell.. Tim Nighthawke, Ivan Nothen, Art Sementelli and Paul Frasier are all at ringside.. HERE WE GO!! All the competitors climb in the ring.. TNT: OH NO!! Tuttle: EVERBODY IS HEADED FOR DEREK LANKFORD!! I don’t think he was counting on THIS in his master plan! Trent, Magus and Angel are hammering away! TNT: Don’t count Derek out! He’s well padded! Tuttle: Trent grabs that helmet! *Thud* He just CRACKED it on Lankford’s head! He’s gushing blood! (Big pop) Fans, we should also note that The Jammer and Scorpio are BOTH at ringside.. TNT: Not sitting beside each other holdin’ hands, I might add! Tuttle: Trent is pulling Lankford out of the frey.. BIG clothesline by Gary Trent.. he has pulled that BASEBALL BAT out of his sack! MAGUS NAILS TRENT!!! HEY!! LOOK AT HIS HANDS!! THEY ARE BULGING!! TNT: Looks like he has something taped to his fist, if you ask me! Tuttle: Gary Trent and Derek Lankford are BOTH down! Where is The Angel? (Big pop) FLYING CLOTHESLINE FROM THE TOP ON MAGUS!! Fallen Angel is back up.. Has Trent and Lankford... NOGGIN KNOCKER!! (The crowd goes nuts) The Angel is pumping his fist for this Nagasaki crowd! TNT: I HATE THAT GUY!! Tuttle: Angel off the ropes.. DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE ON TRENT AND LANKFORD!! Off the ropes again.. Angel-- MAGUS CAUGHT HIM WITH A REVERSE ELBOW!! Magus digging under that shroud.. HE HAS SOME ROPE!! HE IS CHOKING THE ANGEL!! TNT: WHO CARES?!?! Derek Lankford is jabbing some metal object in Gary Trent’s head! Tuttle: WHAT?!?! Yes! Derek Lankford is coming around! He is jabbing Gary Trent with something! Blood is all over the ring! TNT: GOUGE ‘EM!! KILL ‘EM!! Tuttle: This isn’t PHILLY!! Angel is elbow Magus to the chest! ANOTHER! ANOTHER!! Magus lifts him up.. He’s HANGING HIM OVER THE RING ROPE!! This might be IT for Angel! TNT: Let’s hope so! Tuttle: Trent is a bloody mess, as Derek Lankford is trying to set him up for a piledriver! Lankford is digging in Trent’s bag.. LOOK AT THE RING ROPES!! ANGEL HAS TIED HIS LEGS AROUND THEM!! HE WON’T GIVE UP!! He is starting to go limp.. TNT: Dammit! Gary Trent has come back on Lankford! Tuttle: This action is fast and furious fans! Trent slings Lankford into the ropes.. AND OVER MAGUS ONTO THE FLOOR!! TNT: Dammit! Lankford tooka nasty spilll! Magus and Angel had that rope down, and Lankford flipped over it! Fletcher: “Dashing” DEREK LANKFORD has been eliminated.. Tuttle: THREE Left! Angel is almost under the rope.. HE HAS MAGUS BY THE ARM!! Magus is stomping away! (Big pop) GARY TRENT IS ATTACKING MAGUS!! He let’s Angel go! ANGEL PULLS HIM OVER FROM THE BOTTOM ROPE!! MAGUS IS OUT!! Fletcher: MARCUS MAGUS has been eliminated.. Tuttle: Down to two! TNT: Well, Derek Lankford is crawlin; over near Jammer.. Those Japanese security guard are.. Tuttle: Hey! That little guy isn’t Japanese! He’s Chin-- TNT: HEY!! THAT AIN’T A SECURITY GUARD!! THAT’S THE MANDARIN!! Tuttle: The Mandarin?!?!?! That guy doesn’t even WRESTLE in NCW anymore! He was deported! Look! The other one is Taifun!! TNT: The Japs don’t seem to mind that Mandarin was deported from The U.S.. Murder Incorporated GOT him deported, so Derek is in BIG trouble! Rice boy just snuck th’ riddle on Derek Lankford and is carryin’ him out! Tuttle: Get some REAL security guards down there! Oh! Back to the ring! Trent and Angel are both battered! They are exchanging right hands and chops! TNT: Jeremy, we got a camera in the back! (Camera opens on The Mandarin and Taifun tossing Derek Lankford in the back of a Korean made van. It peels off, just as NCW Security get there) Tuttle: Fans, we have a critical situation, as Derek Lankford has just been abducted by The Triad! Back to the ring! Angel with a sling in!! FLYING FOREARM to Trent! Angel still trying to get the crowd hyped, but he is almost out of it! TNT: Forget those two! Somebody save Derek! Tuttle: Trent with a shot to the body! Angel is tryign to get him over to the corner.. ANGEL with a chop! Angel SMASHES Trent’s bloody head into the cornerpost! AGAIN!! AGAIN!! Who said this guy can’t be vicious? TNT: Me. Tuttle: Scorpio is cheering on his friend Fallen Angel! Jammer is sitting there observbing both.. TNT: Why doesn’t Scorpio and Jammer jump in there so we can end this once and for all! Tuttle: Angel is trying to set Trent up on the turnbuckle.. European Uppercut by Gary Trent! Trent slinging Angel into the other corner-- REVERSAL by Angel! REVERSAL BY TRENT!! HE FOLLOWS WITH A VICIOUS KNEE TO THE GUT!! TNT: Kill HIM!! Tuttle: Which one? TNT: BOTH!! Tuttle: Angel hasn’t use a foreign object yet.. Is THAT to his disadvantage! Gary Trent digging in his pocket! He has a roll of coins! TNT: He is about to CASH in! Tuttle: Trent swings.. AND MISSES!! (Big pop) COINS ARE FLYING ALL OVER THE RING!! Angel goes down and SCOOPS TRENT UP!! WHAT SPEED!! TRENT IS FIGHTING HIM EVERYSTEP OF THE WAY!! Remember these two men where the finalists in the LH Random Rumble as well! TNT: Because A-MAN was cheated! Tuttle: Angel trying to set Trent.. Losing his balance!! ANGEL FALL DOWN WITH GARY TRENT ON TOP!! TNT: Git up Trent! You can’t PIN to win this thing! Tuttle: Gary Trent still on top.. reaching for that knap sack.. Angel isn’t moving! Gary’s GOT it! He just pulled out a little night-stick! He is about to.. MISSED!! Angel is the fasting man I have ever seen! Trent is incensed! ANGEL WITH A STANDING DROPKICK!! Angel going up top.. Trent back up.. ANGEL HAS THAT NIGHTSTICK!! ANGEL WAY UP IN THE AIR!! *WHACK* ANGEL JUST BEAMED GARY TRENT WITH THAT NIGHT-STICK FROM ABOUT 15 FEET IN THE AIR!! TRENT IS OUT COLD!! Angel lift him up.. Tosses him over the top!! This one’s OVER!! *DING* *DING* *DING* Fletcher: The winner of this Bunkhouse match.. F A L L E N A N G E L!! Tuttle: Angel with a BIG though incredibly messy victory in this Bunkhouse! On to RingStorm! TNT: On to a massage parlor! This guys damn near KILLED each other! Tuttle: Scorpio has come in to embrace Fallen Angel, who is checking out the condition of Gary Trent.. Jammer has headed back to the locker room.. Fans, that’s it for us this week! We will try to have an update on The Derek Lankford situation, but at this point, we have no news.. For TNT, and everybody at NCW, good bye from Japan! (A Japanese song plays over the credits, and scenes of Derek Lankford being taken out of the arena, and Fallen Angel’s hand being raised.)
