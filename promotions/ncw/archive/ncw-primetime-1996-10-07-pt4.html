[NCW] Primetime Part 4

7 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What happens when ten of wrestling's greatest stables meet in a once in a lifetime event? A N Y T H I N G The Player's Club, The Hard Corps, The Awesome Alliance, The Black Power Movement, The Body Shop and a host of wrestling's greatest superstars will join together to do battle in the first ever STABLE WARS, an event so shocking, so incredible, so unimaginable, it may leave you breathless. . . . . STABLE WARS, the final night of Blue Heaven Open 1996, there'll never be an event this big ever again. . . . . . =============================================================================== T-Bone: Jack, I gotta tell you, The Hard Corps are looking forward to Stable Wars, but I'm looking forward to this next match. Flagg: Me, too. Last time on Primetime, Kurt Ryder took Tank Turner to the limit and beyond, by capturing the International Title. Now, tonight, Ryder looks to face a mystery opponent, which Thomas F. Jones III, the leader of Damage Control Incorporated, picked out for him. However, at this time, Jones has not made an appearance. We don't even know if he's even in Mississippi, much less The Hump. T-Bone: Sounds like an easy night for Ryder. We'll see him make his way to the..... Flagg: What's that?! Can we get a camera back there? T-Bone: What? What's going on, Jack? Flagg: Somebody get a camera back there! <Shot cuts to the back, where Doug Fury has Ryder locked in The Southern Cross (Modified bow and arrow, with a chinlock). Knight is kicking Ryder in the exposed ribcage. Speece and Lankford await their turn.> Flagg: The Awesome Alliance is attacking Kurt Ryder! T-Bone: Dammit! That's it, I'm going back there! <Slams his headset onto the desk, and leaves the broadcast booth> Flagg: Ryder must have been jumped from behind, he still has his ring jacket on! My broadcast partner has taken off from here, and he is charging towards the back! Lankford and Speece are now taking turns slapping Ryder....HERE COMES DAMAGE INCORPORATED! Ramos and Porteaux grab Speece and Lankford, and we've got a brawl in the back. There's T-Bone! He grabs Knight, and gives him a hard slap! Fury releases the hold, and the four members of The Awesome Alliance make their way out of the area. Ryder looks bad. I almost don't want to show this, but since the airtime has been paid for, let's hear these words from "Iceman" Brian Knight. =============================================================================== <The shot is in greyscale. It's on a deserted street. Knight walks down the street in jeans, t-shirt, and trenchcoat. He is soon joined by Derek Lankford, Doug Fury, and Jason Speece.> Knight: Ever since I first showed my face in NCW, I've been on a quest to ruin some lives. Matt Foxx, you were the first. Now, the entire federation is under notice. These men behind me and I are tired of waiting for our shot. Instead, we're taking our shot. You don't like it? I don't care. From this point on, you're going to see random acts of violence. Pain? Suffering? Believe it. We're taking punishment to a whole new level. You wanna fine us? Go ahead. Money means nothing to us. We're looking for trophies. Titles, or careers. That's all that matters. <Knight stops walking, and gets a very serious look on his face.> Treat us like criminals, and we're going to perform criminal acts; Assault and battery, vandelism, nothing is too low. You've called us bad seeds from day one. Well, you're about to see what's developed. Random Violence. It's just the beginning. =============================================================================== Flagg: Fans, what seemed to you as a mere few moments, for us was ten minutes. The fans were informed of our lack of International Title match, as well as the attack in the back. T-Bone is back in the booth with me, and I'm glad to have you back, T. T-Bone: I gotta tell you something, Jack. I hate getting involved in other people's business, and you know how much I dislike Damage Inc., but the fact of the matter is this: when guys like Brian Knight pull stunts like that, it pisses me off. And I'll be damned if I'm going to sit here, and wait for something like that to happen again. Flagg: How's Ryder doing? T-Bone: He'll be fine in a day or two. Those Awful Alliance members jumped him from behind. I gotta feeling that Ryder and DI are going to have a long talking with those punks in the Awful Alliance. Flagg: Well, we've got a hometown crowd waiting to see the World Champion. Let's go to Fletcher in the ring! Fletcher: Wrestling fans, the following is our main event for the evening! It is scheduled for one fall, with a thirty minute time limit, and is for the NCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP!!!!! First, the challenger.....<"King of the Mountain" by Bon Jovi begins playing>.... Weighing in at 370 pounds, from Chicago, Illinois, here is M A R K D E S T R U C T O ! ! ! ! ! ! <A strong mixture of cheers and boos come from crowd as Destructo makes his way to the ring. Destructo, the barrel chested monster, slaps the hands of the fans on his way to the ring.> Flagg: Well, not the reaction Destructo is used to hearing, is it? T-Bone: Destructo's committed a lot of crimes in the past. Looks like the fans are quite ready to forgive him for them. Flagg: Wait for this place to go completely bananas. Here comes the introduction of the champion. Fletcher: AND NOW WRESTLING FANS!!!!!!! <The Mississppii State fight song cranks up from their marching band, as they make their way down the aisle> IT IS TIME FOR YOUR HOMETOWN CHAMPION!!!!! <Major crowd pop> WEIGHING IN AT 268 POUNDS........STANDING 6'5" TALL......AND TONIGHT, WEARING THE HOME COLORS PROUDLY......HERE IS THE NCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION.............................................................. "H O T S T U F F" C H R I S T A Y L O R ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! <There isn't a fan in The Hump on his or her seat, as Taylor comes out of the dressing room wearing a MSU Varsity jacket. Blaze is right behind her man, also sporting the school colors, and a cowbell. The fans are all ringing cowbells, and the band can hardly be heard over it. Taylor jumps into the ring, jumps onto the ring ropes, and busting open the jacket proudly displays the NCW World Heavyweight Title.> Flagg: AND THIS PLACE HAS ERUPTED!!!! THE ONLY WAY TO HEAR YOURSELF THINK IN HERE IS TO SCREAM!!!! T-Bone: <Waits for the noise to die down> But not into the headset! Damn, that smarts. Flagg: Sorry, got carried away. The fans at Mississppii State University were asked what match they wanted to see, and they all wanted this one. An incredible 85 percent all said they wanted to see this match, and NCW has it for them. This should be a hot one. T-Bone: And look at Destructo and Taylor get head to head with each oth....huh. Never noticed that before. Flagg: What's that, T? T-Bone: Destructo is shorter than Taylor. I don't know why, but I always thought of Destructo as a seven foot monster. Maybe he just stood next to short people when I saw him. Flagg: Where in the hell did that come from? T-Bone: I have no idea. Flagg: Destructo and Taylor circle each other, and lock up! Destructo fires Taylor to the ropes, but it's Taylor with a shoulderblock! Taylor goes for a pin! Nothen has the count! One, kickout by Destructo, but it's Taylor leaping on the ropes, like he's won the match! These fans are loving it! T-Bone: Yeah, but Destructo is back up! Taylor leaps off the ropes, sweeping a leg at the same time! HE'S GOING FOR THE FIGURE FIVE ON DESTRUCTO!!!!! Flagg: He tried, but Destructo saw it coming, and pulled him into a small package! One, two, he's in the ropes. Destructo is up first, and delivers a swinging neckbreaker! Destructo goes for a cover! One, two, shoulder up by Taylor! Destructo picks Taylor up, and delivers a Samoan drop! Destructo hits the ropes, and plows through Taylor like he was the Notre Dame defensive line! T-Bone: Well, we'll never be able to wrestle THERE! Flagg: And now it's Destructo on the ropes, trying to get the fans pumped. And they are, but it's because Taylor is up, and hitting the ropes! Taylor delivers a flying clothesline, but Destructo simply takes a step backward! He hardly felt that! Destructo grabs Taylor, and delivers a short clothesline! T-Bone: Taylor felt that. Freshmen felt that. Flagg: Destructo executes a reverse flying elbowdrop, and goes for a cover! One, two, kickout by Taylor! Taylor is up on one knee, and Destructo hits the ropes! TAYLOR NAILS HIM WITH A NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX!!!!! One, two, kickout by Destructo! T-Bone: He almost had him! Taylor gives the sign for the Figure Five! The Hump is going crazy! He grabs the legs of Destructo, but it's the big man with a small package! One, two, kickout by Taylor! Man, lots of pin attempts in this match! A real sign of respect between these two wrestlers! Flagg: It certainly is. Destructo whips Taylor to the ropes, and nails a backdrop! Destructo attempts another elbowdrop, but Taylor is able to get out of the way! Taylor grabs Destructo, and executes a dragon suplex! One, two, shoulder up by Destructo, as he is able to escape the pin. T-Bone: Taylor is setting something up. He's waiting for Destructo to get up, and he hits the ropes! Destructo counters a flying clothesline with a power toss! Taylor hits the mat, and bounces out of the ring! That was a toss! Flagg: And Destructo is following him out. Taylor grabs Destructo, and delivers a tiger suplex on the hard floor, and quickly rolls back into the ring. Taylor jumps onto the top rope, and waits for Destructo to climb back in. Destructo climbs back into the ring, and Taylor executes a Frankensteiner! T-Bone: But look at Destructo! He's brushing himself off, and is up on his feet BEFORE Taylor! He grabs Taylor, but it's Hot Stuff able to send Destructo to the ropes! Destructo is nailed with The Hot Shot! Taylor grabs Destructo quickly.....HE'S GOT THE FIGURE FIVE ON DESTRUCTO!!!!!! Flagg: But look at Destructo! These fans are cheering for Taylor, and Destructo has completely fazed it out, as he BREAKS the Figure Five leglock! Taylor grabs Destructo, and snaps him to the mat with a Russian neckbreaker. T-Bone: And Destructo responds with a lovely Undertaker impression. He sat up! Taylor executes a thrust kick to the head of Destructo! Taylor whips Destructo to the ropes, and delivers an enzui......a back brain kick! Flagg: Flair for the different, T? Destructo reverses an irish whip, and delivers a powerful dropkick! Destructo grabs Taylor, and launches him out of the ring! Destructo follows him, and slams Taylor into the guardrail! Taylor is knocked into the ringpost! Blaze is there, trying to get the fans going with her cowbell! These fans are responding in kind! T-Bone: Hell, even the drum corp is helping out! Destructo throws Taylor back into the ring, and follows him in. Destructo whips Taylor to the ropes, and nails him with a swinging punch! Looks like the rules are out the window, now. Flagg: Taylor is up, and tries to nail a Hotshot, but Destructo nails him with a lariat! Destructo fires Taylor to the ropes, and set up for a powerslam! But it's Taylor with a lariat! These two could go the time limit with this kind of fire. What's going on now? T-Bone: I'll tell you what's going on now! Two wrestlers have come to ringside wearing OLE MISS VARISTY JACKETS!!!!! BRIAN KNIGHT AND DOUG FURY ARE HEADED TO RINGSIDE! Flagg: Well, the fans will kill those two. Meanwhile, Taylor attempts a spinebuster slam, but Destructo counters it with a swinging neckbreaker! Destructo goes for a legdrop, and a cover! One, two, kickout by Taylor. Taylor has started to hear the cowbells being rung, and he lifts Destructo up in a bodyslam! FLYING SENTON BY TAYLOR!!!!! But he only gets a one count! T-Bone: Taylor goes for another bodyslam, but Destructo counters it with a small package! One, two, we almost had a new champion! Destructo lifts Taylor high in the air, and nails an inverted atomic drop! Destructo heads towards Taylor, but Taylor delivers a kick! TAYLOR WITH A TIGER SUPLEX!!!!! One, two, shoulder up by Destructo! Flagg: Taylor whips Destructo into the ropes! Taylor hits the opposite side, and goes for a flying clothesline, but Destructo counters it with a duck down move! Taylor sails through the ropes, and hits the floor hard! T-Bone: I don't think Destructo saw what happened, either. Nothen has asked Destructo to go towards the corner, and begins a count on Taylor. One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, and Destructo has asked that the count be stopped! He wants the match to end in the ring! Flagg: He also knows that a countout won't help him! Nothen is trying to keep Destructo in a neutral corner. Meanwhile, Brian Knight has hit the ring area, and grabs Taylor! PYRO-PLEX ON THE ARENA FLOOR!!!!! This crowd is booing him loudly, but he looks more serious than I've ever seen him! T-Bone: Nothen is back on the job, and begins another count on Taylor. The fans are chanting for Taylor, who is suddenly on his feet! The count is up to five, but Taylor is up on the ring apron! Taylor leaps into the ring, and charges at Destructo, nailing him with a kick! Flagg: And Taylor follows it up with a Frankensteiner! Destructo is just barely able to get his shoulder up at the two count! Taylor hits the ropes, AND DESTRUCTO CATCHES HIM IN A POWERSLAM THAT ROCKS THE CHAMPION!!!!!!! T-Bone: He's not getting up from that one! Nothen makes the count! One, two, WHAT THE HELL?!?!?!? Flagg: Brian Knight just climbed to the top rope, and leaped off with a legdrop, which nailed Destructo from behind! Nothen is calling for the bell, as Fury and Knight begin to double up on Destructo, the referee is calling for the bell! T-Bone: Dammit, these guys piss me off! Blaze is at ringside, and is trying to get into the ring, but something is holding back! Flagg: Not something, someone! Derek Lankford has hit the ringside area, and is holding her from getting into the ring! Jason Speece is headed to the ring with something in his hand. Fury has Destructo locked up in a vicious armbar, similiar to a Code Red. Meanwhile, Knight has grabbed Taylor by the hair, and has dragged him over to where his wife is held captive. T-Bone: The band has the aisleway blocked, Jack! No one can get to the ring! Flagg: Knight is holding Taylor's head, so he can see Blaze! Speece grabs something from under the ring. It's two buckets. What the hell is he doing? THAT'S PAINT! HE'S GOT RED AND BLUE PAINT BUCKETS! THAT'S OLE MISS' COLORS! HE'S NOT........OH, MY GOD!!!! T-Bone: Dammit, that's it! I gotta go, Jack! Flagg: Not without me, T! We will see you again at Nightmare on the Net! <Both men slam down their headsets and begin to muscle their way to the ring. The shot remains on ringside, as The Awesome Alliance leaves the area, the final camera shot is Blaze Taylor covered in paint, crying, as her husband holds her in his arms, fire in his eyes.> *roll credits* A Hailstorm/New Jack Production ****************************************************************************** NCW is brought to you by: Chris Taylor - < [detail withheld] > Head Booker, Heavyweight, Ladies booker, and Miss.
