NCW — Operational addresses

The Monthly State of the Promotion addresses

20 March – 30 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Monthly State of the Promotion address (msg_id 7ycNgmlBoiwJ, 20 March 1997) —

I'll try to do one of these every month, just to let the fans and wrestlers know what is going on with NCW.
Again, due to Iowa's Spring Break, NCW will not air any cards next week. I'm going to try to get a bit ahead on cards so that I don't burn myself out. Speaking of which...
Get me match requests! I can't generate great matchups that you want to see without input! Also, I know I'm getting sick of hearing Jammer rant every other card, so get me a few interviews (in-ring interviews only!
I'm not doing pre-recorded flashes or on-location bits. NCW is supposed to be *small-time*, and we can't afford to jet poor Joe Drexel all over the world!)
Matches and interviews will be done on a first-come basis with the following exceptions: -"gimmick" matches will be done on Main Event Friday. Try to keep these requests to a minimum, or reserve them for Free-for-All, as they are VERY time-consuming
-A wrestler is allowed to wrestle once per week, whether as a singles wrestler or in a tag team. Therefore, only the first request involving a wrestler will be honored. Any others will be pushed back to the next available week (this means that if I get a dozen requests for matches involving the Jammer, for example, he'll be VERY busy for the next three months!)
Run-ins are always welcome. They don't get you any extra victory points (which are good for that all-important placement in the Free-for-All matches), but they WILL further a feud. Please tell me WHO you will be running in on, HOW the run-in will be done (thru the crowd, down the aisle, under the ring, etc. inside or outside the ring), and WHY the run-in is taking place (run-ins done 'just because' will get throw out, but run-ins for revenge or to keep a said wrestler from getting too high in the standings are legit reasons). Multiple run-ins on one match could wind up like recent RAWs involving Bret Hart or SCSA (i.e. wild melees)
If you have something "special" for opponents (i.e. an ambush on the way to the ring), let me know so I can write it in!
FREE-FOR-ALL NEWS: This was not announced due to my not having access to a calandar. NCW Free-for-All will be June 9. The last match before FFA will be (I think) May 30. This will give the tabulators time to get the final points up and final rankings posted before the big event. Any match requests MUST be in my Inbox by May 23, or they WILL NOT be in FFA!
Due to the sheer size of the FFA matches, I will only be taking 10 non-FFA matches. I will also be putting out feelers for interested parties in writing said matches (The FFA matches are MINE! ;) )
As the date draws closer, more info will be available. WEBPAGE NEWS: The NCW Webpage has been updated. The address is still the same ( http://pubpages.unh.edu/~jfm/NCW.html ), but I haven't quite got the frames working. If you are having difficulty with the page, the frameless page is http://pubpages.unh.edu/~jfm/JammerWolf/NCW.html
The roster page has been updated with victory point totals (remember, only the top 10 (top 5 tags) point-scorers will be in the FFA matches!
All the matches are available. However, once Spring Break is over, older matches may be deleted for space considerations. An old match MAY be available for me...I haven't decided yet.
All up-to-the-minute Free-for-All information will be on the Webpage. RANKINGS NEWS: Those weekly questionares are IMPORTANT! The info I get from them not only tells me who you want to see, but the winners in each catagory recieve a victory point! I tally the results on Wednesdays, so make sure
I get them ASAP! That about does it. See you next month. -The Jammer President, JammerWolf Productions

— Monthly State of the Promotion address (msg_id gsbsNfv6Pi4J, 26 April 1997) —

Well, to those of you who have been reading AAPW, the Fed wars have begun! This is a friendly gave of "The Dozens" between AAPW head [name withheld] (with his trusty sidekick [name withheld]) and myself.
With FFA fast approaching (June 9, True Believers), I have the minimum number of matches I would like, but there's still room! Note that it is not a definate that ANYONE will get into a FFA match (though there is a
STRONG possibility that no one will be able to knock Lupa from the standings since she is consistently winning the polls...good job to
Lupa's handler, [name withheld]!). Therefore, if you want a match at FFA, PLEASE send in match requests! In late May, I'll be sending out a special questionare conserning FFA as to who you think will win the matches (and for the World Internet FFA match, who will actually be in the match!)
Speaking of FFA, this is the card that will end a lot of feuds, therefore, if you WANT to continue any feuds after FFA, let me know.
Otherwise, it's on to bigger and better opposition! In about 2 weeks, the Men's ranks will re-open until I get about 18 in the ranks, but I still need more female wrestlers! In addition, those who, in my opinion, are not contributing to NCW (i.e. not ginving me feedback) will be considered for cutting from the ranks. Thus far, most of you are doing a GREAT job at keeping in touch, but many of you I'd like to hear more from.
See y'all on the mat! ****************************************** * The Jammer * * http://pubpages.unh.edu/~[detail withheld] * ****************************************** * 1995 NCW Light-Heavyweight of the Year * * 1995 IEWL Wrestler of the Year * ****************************************** * NEW NCW Homepage: * * http://pubpages.unh.edu/~jfm/NCW.html * ******************************************

— State of the Promotion address (msg_id -JWm7UIjO9UJ, 30 July 1997) —

Well, summer is haalf over and I'm officially in Iowa (and dealing with the joys of cross-country spam as I deal with UNH from UIowa. Did I also mention that the UIowa telnet sytem is VERY fickle about a number of things?). So on with the address:
Next week (August 4), I'll begin debuting the wrestlers I've recieved and approved over the past few weeks. These will be the last until after
Overseas. Speaking of Overseas, I have 4 matches scheduled thus far (including three title matches). If you wish to get your wrestlers involved, get to me by August 18. PLEASE have a match in mind! I will accept no "Put my wrestlers in a match. I don't care who they face" posts. Also, if you'd like to help me write this monstrosity, let me know (I'm putting the call to the Usuaal Suspects for their Usual Assistance)
As promised, the new NCW website will be up by Overseaas. Myself and [name withheld] have been working on the test page (mostly Bryan with technical aadvice form me), and with luck all the old files will be ready for transfer before Overseas. The old page (from UNH) will be up until
UNH shuts my account down. I'll post the new page URL as soon as it's ready for viewing. The generaal consensus seems to be towrd haaving contracts. I'll start negociations for the next quarter ASAP. Feel free to open talks if I don't get to you soon. There will be a clause in the new allabout conserning contracts. The gist will be as follows:
Contracts are *optional*. You will now be aable to wrestle in other leagues if you are NOT under contract
Benefits of being under contract are: -Bonus Character Points and Victory Points upon signing. -Guaranteed "employment" with NCW (non-contract wrestlers may be removed if space is needed, depending on input) -Better pushes (contract wrestlers will likely get pushed over a non-contract wrestler) -Minimum maatches (I know I'm guilty of forgetting about certain wrestlers. This will help me remember about you, by guaranteeing a minimum number of matches per month (between 1 per month and 1 per week.
Supercard and cross-fed matches may be used in this respect)) -Main Event matches (given there are about 10-12 Main Event shows per quarter and three matches per Main Event, being guaranteed spots on the Main Event can be enticing...)
Requirements for a contraact: -Minimum flashing (this will be spelled out in the contract, but count on at least one flash per calander month as a minimum. You *may* send more, by all means!)
-Minimum input (as it's hard to work in a vacuum, I'd like a minimum of posts saying where you want the wrestler to go.) -Minimum maatches (this is also a bennie, but in this respect I mean a minimum number of match ideas you must give me that are *not* on the supercards or cross-fed events)
Failure by either side to hold their end of the aagreement may result in termination of the contract (aat the injured party's option).
A non-contract wrestler must give 1 week's notice before leaving NCW. NCW will give 1 week's notice before removing the wrestler from the roster. Hopefully everyone understands this. See you in a week! -John
