[news] Fatal Attraction has LEFT NCW!!

18 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ITEM: Fatal Attraction, one of e-wrestling's best and most respected tag-teams has left NCW, in a contract dispute. Their contracts expired March 1st, and they did not resign, making them free agents. At the time of their departure, they held BOTH the NCW and NCWF World Tag-Team titles, the first tag-team or singles wrestler to ever accomplish that feat.. History: Fatal Attraction are best known for their feud with Damage Incorporated, whom they dispatched from NCW after winning at "Happy Trails" match at NCW's First Fight (1/96). There is no comment from either side of this coflict as of yet, but NCW sources say Fatal Attraction asked for more money, as they felt they had been underpaid over the course of 1995. FA were rookies in that year, and NCW has a policy against paying high salaries to rookie wrestlers. Fatal Attraction are said to be considering officers from booked leagues. They can be contacted at < [detail withheld] >, where their agent resides. They are also said to be on the way to compete in Japan, until another e-deal can be worked out.. It is uncertain whether they will continue to wrestle in NCWF, which is currently on hiatus.. This is the news guy, and I'm outta here!
