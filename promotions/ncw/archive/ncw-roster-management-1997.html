NCW — Roster operations

Roster openings and closures

7 April – 31 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— [NCW] Announcement from you-know-who: Roster filling fast! (msg_id LylSec2uRoIJ, 7 April 1997) —

OK, here's the deal: The batch I got today (including ones I asked redos for) will be the LAST male singles wrestlers I'll accept for a while. I wanted to limit the men's division to 20, and we're almost to that point now. Any all-male tag team that has NOT said they wish the members to compete singly will be removed from the singles roster. This is essentially Bad Company and Lucifer/Wyrm.
The tag roster is almost full, but I can take one or two more before I close the doors for a while.
What I really need are female wrestlers! Lupa is the only women's wrestler not tied up in a tag team!
Due to the amount of wrestlers in the ranks, I obviously won't be able to use everyone every week. Therefore, I'll have to prioritize. The order of priority for getting matches will be:
1. Those who send match requests 2. Those who send interviews 3. Those who are corresponding with me 4. Those who are being actively pushed (tho' this will likely be among at least one of the other three) Because of this, it is IMPERATIVE that you get to me at least once a week to let me know you're out ther (even if it's only to send in the weekly poll...and I *do* read them! Those who are consistently winning the polls are being considered for pushes if not already being pushed)
Also, I need matches for Free For All. I have one in the works, plus the four FFA matches themselves, but I need more! I'd like 10 matches plus the FFA matches. Get to me ASAP.

— [NCW] Tag division closed (msg_id AyYxqNgk-OEJ, 14 April 1997) —

The tag roster, like the male singles roster, is nearly full. Both Men's singles and tag ranks are closed until further notice. Any new wrestlers from either division will be on a waiting list until the ranks open up again (around mid-May), and will be filled on a first-come basis. There are still a number of openings in the women's ranks, so let's see some new women in NCW! — [NCW] Ranks re-opening (msg_id t4skRC5JBGcJ, 5 May 1997) —

NCW has re-opened the tag and men's singles ranks. We have room for 5 men, 1 tag team, and about 10 women wrestlers. Please remember that wrestlers in NCW must be exclusive to NCW, and there is a limit of 3 wrestlers per handler. — [NCW] Calling all wrestlers! (msg_id N9FvxDEbQYIJ, 31 May 1997) —

Network Championship Wrestling is about to have its first supercard and is gearing up for our big European Tour. If you'd like to be among the first to debut after the card, join now! We are currently running 5 short cards a week (tho' we may go back to 1 big card fairly soon).
