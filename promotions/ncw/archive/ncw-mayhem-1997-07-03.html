NCW — Card

NCW Mayhem — a title defence in Homburg

3 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene is the locker room at Waldstadion.) SHAWN: I am standing with the the newest addition to the NCW, "The Terminator" Davin Varth and Lord of the Dark, collectively known as The
Deathstalkers, with their manager Devastor. Now my first question is why have you come to the NCW?
DEVASTATOR: We have come here to test the best competition in the world of wrestling today. We are here to take over as well.
SHAWN: How do you think that you will stand up to this first-rate competition here in NCW? LORD: Well, you see I think that we will stand up pretty well because we have been training for years to get ready for this moment. You see I already have taken over the biggest and baddest in the universe. For I
RULE the universe and next the NCW.
VARTH: You see we can take on the most talented people that can be thrown at us. We are the best and we will prove it.
SHAWN: Well, you say you have been training for years. What kind of training would that consist of?
DEVASTATOR: Well, I have hade these men in the bowls of hell getting ready for this moment of the best competition in wrestling today.
SHAWN: You are new what are you looking forward to doing here in the NCW? VARTH: Well, that would be TERMINATING some careers and winning a title or two. I know that it will not be easy, but I think the both of us can do it.
LORD: For me its time for the Dark Forces to arrive in the NCW. I have the coming of Dark Forces helping us through the whole NCW and winning some titles. Prepare for the Dark Forces to come!!!
DEVASTATOR: This interview is over! (They walk out of the locker room.) SHAWN: Thanks guys, I think. Network Championship Wrestling presents NCW Mayhem (The scene is a small arena, filled with screaming fans. The scene shifts to the announcers' table where Dierdre O'Connel and "Wildcat" Joe
Drexel sit. O'Connel's wearing a grey suit. Drexel is wearing a suit coat with an NCW European Tour t-shirt.)
DREXEL: We are LIVE from Waldstadion in Homburg, Germany! Welcome to NCW
Mayhem! I'm "Wildcat" Joe Drexel, along with Dierdre O'Connel.
SHAWN: Please welcome the Specialists!!! ("We didn't start the Fire" by Billy Joel plays over the PA. Dan Special is a very tall, strong, handsome man with blue eyes. He wears red tights and a blue jacket with "The Specialists" emblazoned on the back. Mya
Special is a very tall, beautiful, well toned woman with braided red hair.
She wears blue tights and a red top with a red jacket with "The Specialists" emblazoned on the back. The Specialists stop momentarily half way to the ring while Mya laughs at a fan wearing a Sinister Squad shirt)
SHAWN: Mya, I'd like to address the incident that occured before FFA where you made that poor woman break down and cry...how can live with yourselves after how you treated that woman at Blockbuster.
MYA: First off, She deserves it for wasting my time by fumbling around... SHAWN: (shocked) How can you say that!!! MYA: It is amazing that NCW pays you to do interviews when we have to pull you back to the real subject at hand...
SHAWN: What about... Dan: (turning towards Shawn) Hold it right there, Tyler. Before you go any farther, I will give you a chance to leave the ring right now...
Shawn: (disgusted) Fine, here is the house mic, I am not going to finish this (Shawn hands the mic to Mya and leaves the ring) DREXEL: Of all the-- MYA: Getting back to important details... DAN: It seems that we have made many enemies since we have our made our debut in NCW. Well, If you got a problem with us, you need to learn to live with it because there comes a time when it rains on everybody's parade...
MYA: However, we have just two words for these people... BOO HOO!!! They can cry all they want but we are still the best wrestlers! (a soda-filled cup flies past Mya and just barely misses Dan) MYA: The next order of business is X LOSERS. We beat them and they thought it would be really funny to distort the stipulation by kissing me.
(One section starts a "Kiss me" chant) MYA: (annoyed) Well, unlike Extasy, I do not enjoy getting kissed by that mental midget Barnes
DAN: X Factor, you better listen up real good, since Justice will not hold you to your stipulation, we will...
MYA: ...and X Factor will learn to like kissing our feet even if that means we have to kick their butts all over Europe to make them do it.
(Mya paces around the ring) MYA: The next issue is Dork Vermin and Freddie Massey, We beat them in
April and proved that they are washed up...
DAN: Now don't get us wrong, Bad Company have proved that they are still top-notch street fighters with their victory over Mist, Golem, and Magma, but they have lost an edge when it comes to wrestling ability. We will be glad to face Bad Company again...If it is for the tag-team titles.
MYA: But nooo!!! Bad Company has shown that they are ducking us by giving the Dynamic Duds the first title shot!
DAN: Bad Company has better rectify this very soon. YOU HAVE BEEN FORWARNED!!! MYA: Finally let's talk about the German wimps called Sinister Squad... (The crowd begins to get rowdy and a chant of "Kick them out" erupts. Trash litters the ring) DREXEL: I think Dixie Hart needs to get The Specialists out of here before this get's even uglier.
(Dixie Hart runs to the ring and escorts The Specialists out to a chorus of boo's.) DREXEL: while the ring gets cleaned up, I understand Shawn has found hios way to the locker rooms and has caught up with the Trooper. Are you there
Shawn?
(The scene opens in Shawn Tyler next to a locker room. He seems very scared to be there) SHAWN: Joe, as you can see, The Trooper IS here, breaking everything... Hey, Trooper, got a minute? (The Trooper gives Shawn a look that could desintegrate him.) TROOPER: WHAT...DO...YOU...WANT??? SHAWN: I-I-I-I-I-I...W-w-w-w-wanna know about your attack on Gary Trent... I mean, you almost KILLED him with a knife... TROOPER: ALMOST??? DAMN!! I think I should had checked if he was dead before leaving... SHAWN: You mean that you WANTED to kill him??? That's insane!! TROOPER: INSANE??? I'LL TELL YOU WHAT IS INSANE, BUG!!! Insane is these little gangsters from out of nowhere trying to make fun of me...Do you remember the Ultimatum? They gave me one week, or my legs would be broken, and I'M STILL WALKING!! VICELORD!! YOU GOT A *REAL* PRESENT WHEN YOU
DEFEATED ME!!! MARCUS!! YOUR CAREER IS OVER IF I GET YOU AGAIN IN THE
RING!! AND TRENT COST ME THE BELT!! YOU HIT ME FROM BEHIND WHEN I WAS
GOING TO *TERMINATE* THE KINGE SCUM!!!
SHAWN: He also made a challenge to you... TROOPER: I heard it...and I couldn't stop laughing after it...YOU WANT ME,
VICEDORK??? YOU GOT IT!!! With one condition...Loser is suspended for A
MONTH!!! SHAWN: And...What about Kinge... TROOPER: HE'S NOT SAFE EITHER!!! I CAN HANDLE HIM *AND* STREET LEVEL!!! KINGE, ANY TIME, ANYWHERE, ANY RULES!!! YOUR BELT IS *MINE*!!! SHAWN: Now...It looks like that Tony Constanza is also aligning against you... TROOPER: WHO??? THE PUFF DRAGON??? I DON'T CARE!! THE WHOLE NCW COULD
ALIGN AGAINST ME, I STILL DON'T CARE!! CONSTANZA!!! YOUR FATE IS
DECIDED!!! AND...IT...WILL...BE...CCCCCAAAARRRRRNNNNNAAAAAGGGGEEEE!!!! SHAWN: Thanks for the interview... (whispering) Can we get out of here? DREXEL: Thanks Shawn. The ring is finally cleared, so let's go to the ring. Boy am I glad we got another commentator...
STONE: The following match is for the Network Championship Wrestling
World Tag-Team Championship! It is set for one fall. Introducing first, the challengers...at a total combined weight of 538 pounds, from the
Troublesome Trio, The Jammer and the ALWAYS explosive Thomas N. Tyler, the
Destructive Duo!
("Whipping Post" by the Allman Brothers plays over the PA. Tyler is a bald caucasian wearing a TNT doorag and a black overcoat with EXPLOSIVE
ONE on the back. Jammer is a caucasian with shoulder-length brown hair.
He's wearing purple shades and an overcoat with JAMMER on the back. They remove the overcoats to reveal black tights with JAMMER down the left leg and TNT down the right.)
STONE: And now...introducing the Network Champuionship Wrestling World
Tag-Team Champions...at a total combined weight of 519 pounds, "Flashy"
Freddy Massey and "Deadly" Dirk Venom, Bad Company! ("Bad Company" plays over the PA. Massey has long, peroxide-blonde hair, a well-tanned and well-"cut" body, and blue eyes. He's wearing black tights trimmed with gold, matching boots, a black leather jacket with a
Bad Company logo on the back, an unbuttoned silk shirt, and small round- lensed sunglasses. Venom is a big, brawny guy with dark hair (slicked and pulled into a small ponytail) and mustache. He's wearing a Bad Company muscle shirt, wrap-around sunglasses, black boots, and black leather chaps over his black tights bearing the image of a poison-dripping dagger down each leg.)
DREXEL: These two faced each other a month and a half ago, and it was a draw then. With luck, we'll have a definate winner.
O'CONNEL: Let's do a checklist...Mya and Dan were just clamoring for a title shot, Lupa's in the building...I think I saw Eve somewhere around here...Marcus Magus has Jammer on his shit-list...yup, no chance of interference!
DREXEL: These two teams will be part of the Walsh Cup tag-team tournament tomorrow, so they need to conserve their energy...
O'CONNEL: With luck, NCW will give a good showing, but I can't for the life of me figure out why the Duo aren't favored to win...
DREXEL: Nice sarcasm, Sham-- O'CONNEL: DON'T CALL ME SHAMROCK!!!!! DREXEL: And this match is underway as Massey and the Jammer start things off. Collar and elbow...no, Massey with a rake to the eyes of Jammer, and a kick to the midsection...and a whip to the WRONG part of town!
O'CONNEL: I think the Jammer's more worried about showing up AAPW than winning this match!
DREXEL: You may be right as Massey tags in Venom. Venom with a grinding headlock on Jammer, wearing him down...Jammer trying to push off Venom, but to no avail!
O'CONNEL: Jammer's getting himself in trouble FAR too early in this! If he can't break that hold when he's FRESH...
DREXEL: Jammer grabbing the ropes to break the hold, but Massey slapping
Jammer's hand away before the official can see it.
O'CONNEL: That's one of the reasons these two are champions...they're both in the right place at the right time. These two train together, eat together...heck, I THINK they share bed part--
DREXEL: No need to go there! Jammer trying once again to push Venom off, but Venom still has a hold of that headlock!
O'CONNEL: Well, when you have a move that works, why fight success? DREXEL: Venom with a spinning spike-slam (modified belly-to-belly suplex) and tagging in Freddie! O'CONNEL: I'm not totally sure on this strategy...I mean, the headlock was working well for Dirk, but he's right in saving his strength.
DREXEL: Massey with a whip to the ropes...SPIN WHEEL KICK SENDS JAMMER INTO THE ROPES! O'CONNEL: But out of TNT's reach! Nice placement by Massey! DREXEL: Massey placing Jammer on the top turnbuckle...he's going for his
DDT from the top turnbuckle...BUT JAMMER PUSHES HIM OFF! JAMMER WITH A
SPLASH FROM THE TOP!!!! 1...2...Venom in for the save!
O'CONNEL: But that took a lot out of him...hell, out of both men! DREXEL: Venom dragging his partner to the corner so he can make the tag, while Jammer is trying to get to the corner...Venom grabs Jammer and gives a slap to Tyler...AND HERE COMES TNT!!! Tyler with a dropkick...and a powerslam...KNEEBREAKER!
O'CONNEL: But Massey came in with a knee to Tyler's back! DREXEL: Tyler taking on both members of Bad Company! O'CONNEL: Jammer's still in the middle of the ring where Venom dragged him! DREXEL: The offical has no clue who's legal by this time! Tyler with a backdrop OH NO!!!! Venom just landed on Jammer! The official making the count 1...2...3!!!!!!
STONE: Here are your winners...and STILL Network Championship Wrestling
World Tag-Team Champions...Bad Company!
DREXEL: TNT accidentally causing his team the loss...hang on, Massey's calling for the mic
O'CONNEL: When did WCW buy us out? I've never seen so many interviews in one night! MASSEY: As some of you fans out there know, we're about to be involved in a tag team tournament hosted by the Wild West organization. There's a few things we want to set straight before we get there! First of all, we'd like to let our opponents this evening...(Pauses for the crowd pop)...know that we hold them in the highest respect as wrestlers and as wrestling promoters.
(Crowd pops higher) MASSEY: Secondly, we'd like to address this point AND the next to WWW commentator, FORMER NCW world tag team co-holder, Eddy Ramos! Pal, I don't know where you get off, but let me set you straight! These two men we just wrestled AREN'T just two chumps who walked in off the street and thought they were wrestlers! "...They've been wrestling jobber teams and might not be up to the competition they'll be facing the tournament..."
was the gist of your bogus analysis of us! Jack, NCW has some of the finest tag teams wrestling for the promotion! Were you watching this match? Are you kidding me? And if the Sinister Squad had beaten us at the
PedMall, they'd be in our slot in the tournament and would STILL have dominated the way WE'RE going to! And even the mixed tag teams of the NCW could take any one of the teams in this tourney, guaranteed!
(Jammer grabs another mic) JAMMER: It still amazes me that the Amazing Loss Machine and the Floppin' Angel are even IN the tournament. I mean, Dwayne Gill and Barry Hardy could beat these yahoos...and they're among the BEST All Around Poor
Wrestling has to offer?! Why don't they just let us fill the ranks with some REAL teams from a REAL promotion so the fans can get their money's worth!
(Major pop) MASSEY: And finally, pal, you said we've been taking on a "light schedule" with these same "jobbers." Pal, you and your chickenshit partner weaseled out of the sport before we could PUT YOU OUT permanently! You chumps, the Stylistics, Fatal Attraction, and most of the rest of the headliner tag teams of the old NCW *DUCKED US* for two and a half years!
The old matchmaking crew kept having to sacrifice Sudden Impact to the cause of keeping Bad Company booked on the tours! And we had to jump up and down and scream to get matches because YOU JERKS WERE AFRAID TO FACE
US!!! Bottom line! Now that you're safely "retired" where we can't get you, you use your new commetator platform to disrespect Bad Company one more time! Pal, you're the straw that's broken the camel's back!
JAMMER: Ramos, when you and Porteaux came to me saying you had something to say, I thought you'd be announcing you were joining NCW, but NOOOOOOO!
You announce your RETIREMENT, and THEN run off to the NFW rehashes to play
Siskel and Ebert giving NCW, the tribute to the organization that MADE
YOU, a full thumbs-down! You boys couldn't take the heat earning a REAL living, so you decided to get McJobs instead. Well you boys have fun working at the fast-food wrestling leagues. When you Cajun cowards are ready for gourmet wrestling action, y'all come back and we'll give you a feast of hurt!
MASSEY: I promise you this - BAD COMPANY WILL END SOMEONE'S CAREER AT THE
WELCH CUP!!!!! We're tired of being put down by everyone and his brother, and we're not taking any more crap off of ANYBODY! You'll be lucky if we don't come slap the spit out of you when we're done crippling some poor chump in the ring! To any of the other teams out there listening, take this advice to heart... RUN AWAY WHILE YOU CAN! IT'S ABOUT TO BE A *BAD*
DAY FOR YOU ALL! AHHHHHHH YEAH!!!! DREXEL: OH MY! NCW has thrown down the gauntlet in a BIG WAY! Folks, we are WAY OVER time, so for Dierdre O'Connel, I'm "Wildcat" Joe Drexel...
Good night! (c)1997 JammerWolf Productions
