For the first time EVER.. ANYWHERE.. The Stancil Brothers, Vernon and Jayson, are tagging up! Is this a sign of things to come? Abd: Could be. I would not want to speculate. What a contrast in styles. Jayson, though gifted with technical skill, is a streetfighter. Vernon is more of a technician, though larger. He also holds a major title. Deidre: The Dynamic Duo aren't slouches either! Do they still take lessons from TNT? Abd: Ask the cretin yourself. Deidre: I will. Let's here these words from BPM.. Wonder what's up with The Dragon? Well, I have been doing my work with The Black Folx College Society.. training.. and thinking.. Wrestling had gotten old to me.. That's not good. It makes you soft. That is why I haven't been in the ring.. I was missing something.. Watching my brother Vernon GROW showed me what it was.. I am not an old man, hell I ain't even 30! But, I need the fire my brother has.. And he needs the knowledge I can bring.. We are BROTHERS. First and foremost. I have been negligent in my duties to him.. That is over. Big Body Brown has led my brother to gold.. I am thankful. Now, it is time for him to lead HIMSELF. And, for me to be with him, giving him guidance when he needs it. (he looks around his environment, and up at a hovering LAPD chopper) He and I both have survived these mean streets.. Vernon: So true. NCW, I have only showed you a taste of what I have to offer.. Now with Jayson with me, "The Hands of Stone" will dominate whichever division I choose to get into. I am 18, so I can be a junior.. I'm in the 260 range, which makes me a heavy.. Jayson: And TOGETHER, we form a mighty lethal tag-team.. Vernon: True. We are from the streets.. But, that ain't what its all about.. It's all about making that money.. so you won't have to return to the streets! I am in NCW full-time now, fans.. All hell is about to break loose. WIWF, it was fun while it lasted. (Takes the belt off, and tosses it in his Viper.) Jayson: NCW.. Some sternums are about to get cracked.. Vernon: And some heads are about to get CRUNCHED!! (The brothers hug, as the camera fades.) Deidre: Whoah. That sounds like trouble. Though his win/loss record wasn't that impressive, NOBODY wanted to get in the ring with The Black Dragon. Abd: Really? That was before my time. Wendigo: Our next contest is a special tag-team attraction.. Introducing first.. from Chicagoland.. at a combined weight of 482 lbs.. accompanied to the ring by "The Golden Armlock" Trophy.. (confused) "Fly Boy" Daniel Arrogance.. "Hot Shot" Paul Pompous.. T H E D Y N A M I C D U O !! Tyler is obviously still involved with these youngste--wait a minute...that ego-driven orangataugn would NEVER let that trophy out of his site....is he HERE?! (clears his throat) err.. and The United Black Folx College Society.. Vernon.. Jayson.. T H E S T A N C I L B R O T H E R S !!!! (The arena goes black. I can't believe the crowd is loving this! Jayson with vicious chops to Arrogance! Vernon with big right hands to Pompous! Jayson and Vernon look at each other.. Vernon slings his man off the ropes.. (taps headset) WHERE'S TYLER?! I will NOT appear on the same show as that MISERABLE... Deidre: No it ain't!! Jayson and Vernon haven't noticed the count!! Still, observers agree that it is only a matter of time before it comes out.
