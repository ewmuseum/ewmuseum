[NCW] Primetime Part 2

7 October 1996

******************************************************************************** O U T S I D E T H E R I N G featuring Thomas N. Tyler TNT: YEAH!! YEAH!! YEAH!! This the the man TNT here with tha' first Nightmare on the Net report... TNT: So far we got a load of matches for you sorry ass fans...The main even has been annoucned is it's a TRIPLE CAGE TOWER OF DOOM MATCH!! Basically it's three cages stacked on top, 10 men, they beat the ever loving S*** outta each other in order to get to the bottom and get it..The participants ahve yet to be named..BUT...Rest assured when they do I will beat there to get the dirt..Not like that sorry ass wallflower T-Bone... Other matches on the card: The man who is putting people out..Gemini will take on the Fallen Angel for the L-H title IF THE ANGLE CAN BEAT THE JAMMER TONIGHT!! WHich he won't, and I know why <TNT grins> The sorry ass Mike D wanna be..."Original Gangsta" Gary Trent takes on one of my favorite wrestlers...THE JAMMER!! in a Coompton Street Fight, this match is not sanction by the NCW...Both wrestler com as they are...bring what they want...The winner is determined when he came pin his opponent and keep him out for 30 seconds after he pins him....Falls count anywhere!1 For the Internet title..A TRIANGLE MATCH...As the champ "Dashing" Derek Lankford, but heads with former Champions: Scorpio and The A-Man, the special guest ref is that backstabbing snake Kevin "The Cobra" Richards.. That's it for now..I would tell you some more, but I got some business to attend to..Back to Jackass Flagg and that bumbling idiot T-Bone... T-Bone: Why do we put up with that man? Anyway, no wasting time, Flagg what's your thoughts on our next match up? Flagg: This next match will a real barnburner. These two have not liked each other from the first day they met. They've fought many times with an even split in decisions. Now with Eve still being friends with Blaze, the Lady Rose really has reason to hate Blaze. T-Bone: Yeah, you got that right. Rose has tried to interfere with both families, the Taylors and the First Family with Eve and Adam. But it looks as though things are finally calming down, so count on the Lady pulling some stunt out of her bag of tricks. Flagg: Here's Fletcher with the intro's. Ding...ding...ding...ding...ding...ding...ding Fletcher: Ladies and gentlemen. This match is one fall with a 30 minute time limit. Now walking down the aisle, accompanied by her ward, "The Succubus" Chrissy Steele, from Thornefield Manor, United Kingdom, at 6"3 and 160 lbs, the former NCW Womens' Champion, the ever-dangerous Lady Rose!!!!! (The fans boo and jeer as the two strut down the aisle, ignoring the fans except for a sneer. Rose wears a Vidtorian dress, black and deep red, which she slowly removes to reveal a new high-cut leotard, red and royal purple, with matching boots. She has a striking figure and lovely face to match. Her blond hair falls to her shoulders, thick and waved. Chrissy wears a maroon Victorian dress, but looks prepared for action.) Fletcher: And introducing (the cheers start up and get louder) from Starkville, MS, accompanied by her friend Eve (more cheers), at 6'0 and 150 lbs, the Queen of Mississippi State, here's your own former RSPCW and future NCW Womens' Champion, Blaze Taylor!!!!! (The fans explode in cheers as Blaze and Eve run down the aisle high-fiving the fans; Blaze jumps over the top rope, climbs to the top and does a perfect backflip to the center of the ring, then struts around the ring like a model; she wears her MSU letter jacket for gymnastics and diving which she removes to reveal a new bright red leotard with flames around the base and matching boots with Blaze written up the sides. Eve wears a long, slinky, blue dress with a long slit.) Flagg: This could be a real nasty match. Hell, it will be a nasty match. But I gotta tell ya T, the NCW doesn't have any dogs does it? Look at these four beauties. T-Bone: You got that right. These four models are real eye candy. Here's the bell. Both Rose and Blaze rush out and lock up. Rose with a quick hip toss and dropkick as Blaze gets up. Another dropkick and Blaze goes down again. Rose waits and grabs Blaze's hair as she stands and throws her across the ring. A big shoulder block and Blaze flies into the ropes and down. Rose moves in, but Blaze kicks her belly as Rose tries to grab her hair. Rose backs off and Blaze stands. Flagg: Rose moves in, but Blaze hammers her with a series of forearm smashes to the head. Rose backs off with Blaze chasing her. She grabs Rose and clamps on a side headlock, but Rose throws her against the ropes. WOW! Rose catches Blaze in the chest with a foot and she's down. Rose knows a lot and she'll always land on her feet. T-Bone: Yer right. Rose yanks on Blaze's hair and stands her up for a big chop across the throat. She pulls Blaze up and throws her against the ropes, catching her across the throat with a perfect clothesline. Blaze better do something besides just flopping around. Again she throws her against the ropes and nails her in the face with a big flying dropkick. Rose is all business in wrestling. Flagg: She grabs Blaze's hair and stands her up, then jumps up and falls back, pulling her hair with her and smashing Blaze's face into the mat. WOW, that's gotta hurt and Blaze is rolling around in pain. Rose pulls her up again and yes, she repeats the move. Blaze's face is smashed into the mat. She doesn't look good and the fans are very quiet. Eve is trying to get the crowd to chant. It's working as they yell "Blaze, Blaze, Blaze", but Rose seems to have things under her control. She tosses Blaze across the ring by her hair, then stands her up and throws her against the ropes. Holy Cow! Did you see that spin kick to Blaze's head? She's really hurt and this may be over very shortly. T-Bone: It sure looks it, although Blaze is tough and will come back. Rose plays with her, teasing her as Blaze tries to stand and get her bearings. Another dropkick and Blaze flies into the ropes and down. Rose drags her to the center and covers her, 1..........2....., and Blaze kicks out. How on earth did she do that? Rose looks frustrated and punches her head. She picks up Blaze in a suplex and slams her down. Another pin, 1..........2....., and Blaze lifts a shoulder. Rose pulls her up and throws her against the ropes. She sends Blaze flying with a backflip. Another toss against the ropes and She catches Blaze, no, Blaze caught a leg as Rose tried a spinkick, and she twisted it, sending Rose to the mat. A quick reverse leglock and Rose is screaming in pain. Blaze has those legs pulled back hard and now reaches for her chin, yanking back. She's asking Rose if she'll quit. Flagg: Fat chance of that happening. Blaze has those fans yelling now and loves it. She releases Rose's chin and keeps after the legs. She twists Rose over and steps into a toehold, twisting the leg and pressuring the knee. Another twist and she has a half Boston crab, yanking the left leg high and back. Rose can't get away from the leg pain and may have a hard time standing. But she reaches the ropes and Blaze lets go. Ooooohh, not before she stomps her head and back. Wait, Chriisy just climbed to the apron and hit Blaze with a small bat or stick. T-Bone: Blaze is down and blood is running from her forehead. Eve just grabbed Chrissy and rammed her head into the guardrail, then bodyslammed her into the rails. That will keep Chrissy down for a while. But Blaze is hurt and Rose is now kicking and stomping her as she tries to cover up. Rose stands her up and nails Blaze with a big spinkick to her head. Blaze is down like a sack of rocks and Rose just keeps coming. Blaze is pulled up and tossed out of the ring. Boy, she landed hard on her back. Rose jumps down and kicks Blaze, then grabs her hair and rams her head into the guardrail. Rose is climbing back into the ring and rests while Blaze tries to get up. Flagg: Eve climbs into the ring and chops Rose across the neck from behind, then kneelifts her, dropping Rose. She picks up Blaze and helps her back into the ring Blaze grabs Rose's hair and yanks her up, walking her to a corner and rubbing her face over the turnbuckle. She rams Rose's face into the turnbuckle, then puts on a front headlock. Whoa! A big spinning neckbreaker and Rose is laying flat on her back. Blaze kneedrops her chest and then kicks the left leg repeatedly. She really wants to kill that leg. A figure-four leglock has Rose screaming in pain, but she soon grabs the ropes. Blaze won't let go, but has to at the 4 count. She has Rose down and keeps after her with stomps on her chest and head. T-Bone: Blaze pulls up Rose and kicks the leg out from under her. Rose hangs on to the ropes, but Blaze grabs both hands and kicks her belly. Rose is hurt, but still deadly and Blaze knows it. She keeps after her with chops and kicks. She stands up Rose and throws her against the ropes, then drives a foot into her chest. Another toss into the ropes and a big dropkick sending Rose crashing to the mat. Blaze pulls her up and throws her into a corner, following with a spinkick to the head. Rose just fell flat on her butt and sits there dazed. Blaze pulls her up and throws her into the ropes, grabbing her around the waist and flipping her over, then dropping her with a backbreaker across a knee. Rose looks done. Flagg: But she may come back. She's like a fox and tough. Blaze pulls her up and carries her to a corner, setting her on the top rope. Blaze climbs up and lays out Rose with a big top rope suplex. Here comes the moonsault. No, Blaze lifts Rose and carries her to a corner. She's climbing with Rose. Blaze is yelling to the crowd and they love it. She flies off with a big powerslam, driving Rose into the canvas. There's the cover, 1..........2..........3, and this one's over. Blaze wins a big one in front of the home crowd. T-Bone: She's jumping and prancing around the ring with the crowd going nuts over her. She calls that move her "Bulldog Slam" in honor of Mississippi State University. I haven't seen a meaner bulldog than that. Rose is just starting to roll around with Chrissy Steele trying to help her guardian get up. I'm sure this isn't over yet. Maybe Rose can get Blaze in a match in the U.K. Flagg: That might help her. Have you noticed how the oldtimers are still hanging in there, winning and takin' care of business? They know how to deal with the youngsters and keep winning. Fletcher: The winner in 14:47, Starkville's and MSU's own, the greatest woman wrestler alive, Blaze Taylor. (The crowd loves it and cheers as loudly as possible; she climbs out and signs autographs). Flagg: She is one classy lady. Look at her, she's dead tired and still has time for autographs. She won't turn anyone away, either. T-Bone: She and her fans have a big love affair and it really boosts her performance. Let's go to this interview, then to our sponsers! Reed: Here we are backstage with the A-Man and the.... (The Duke of Flushing hands Reed a note.) Reed: Oh, I apologize. Here we are backstage with "The biggest thing that's happened since the invention of sliced bread and the electric toaster, and since the creation of *A*," the... (The Duke of Flushing grabs the microphone.) Duke: That's right punks, it's the Muthaf*ckin' A-MAN!!!! (The Duke of Flushing hands the microphone to the A-Man. The A-Man is wearing a black leather jacket with A-Slogans all over it and "The ReAl Internet ChAmpion" on the back. ) A-Man: Thanks dude. Now listen Reed, there's a lot of wild sh*t goin' on around here. The very first of which is the status of Kevin "The Dead Cobra" Richards. Richards, you're a gutless punk. You let down me, you've let down the A-Stable, and you've let down yourself. You see, one a snake, always a snake. Unfortunately, now you'll end up like this. Duke, bring it out! (The Duke of Flushing brings out a meat cleaver and a garden snake. The A-Man gives Reed back the micrphone, takes the cleaver, and places the snake on the floor.) Reed: Your not going to do this, right? OH MY GOD! (The A-Man chops the snake to bits, then throws the cleaver to the ground.) A-Man: Cobra, next time it's you. No one runs from the A-Stable. The Mandarin, the Hard Ridin' Man, or myself, or all at once. Anytime, anyplace bitch. Reed: That was revolting! A-Man: What's your stupid questions anyway? Reed: Well, now that Richards has resigned, what's the status of the A-Stable? A-Man: That was a stupid question, but since you know you're stupid, that almost makes you smart. The A-Stable are in serious negotiations with an interfed star. A couple, actually. The A-Stable will have to decide who will be the fourth member. We're broadening our horizons. We're not looking for some fly-by-night punk like Richards was. Then again, we may not be adding a new member after all. And that's all you need to know, punk. Reed: What about comments about your Nightmare on the Net match, the Triangle match between Derek Lankford, Scorpio, and yourself for the Internet title? A-Man: Nightmare on the Net is going to see The A-Man winning back his Internet title, the title that is rightfully mine. And what better match to win it back in than a Triangle match. The Triangle match was made for me. Reed, if you take a Triangle and add a horizontal line midway through it, what do you have? Reed: Well, you have an *A*. A-Man: Exactly. This isn't a Triangle match, it's an A-Match. Who's the King of the A-Match? Me! Lankford, you know you couldn't beat me on your best night. Scorpio, you're old news, a punk I've destroyed on numerous counts. Richards, you're the one I'm concerned about. You better do your job and do it right or I'll turn this into a four-way dance. You already shown that you can't be trusted, so in the NCW method of fairness John Moore has made you referee. So if you interfere in any way I'm going to send my A-Lawyers to protest any and all decisions. The A-Match, The A-Stable, The A-Lawyers, all to win back the A-Belt. NightmAre on the Net, *A* comes full circle. *A* will rule! *fade to black*
