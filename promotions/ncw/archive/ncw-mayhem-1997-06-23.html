NCW — Card

NCW Mayhem — the European Tour's opening card

23 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Network Championship Wrestling presents NCW Mayhem (The scene is a small arena, filled with screaming fans. The scene shifts to the announcers' table where Dierdre O'Connel and "Wildcat" Joe
Drexel sit. O'Connel's wearing a grey suit. Drexel is wearing a suit coat with a FFA t-shirt.)
DREXEL: We are LIVE from the Trud Arena in Novorossiysk, Russia as we kick off ouyr European tour. Welcome to NCW Mayhem! I'm The Wildcat Joe
Drexel, along with Dierdre O'Connel.
O'CONNEL: Why RUSSIA?! Why couldn't we start in Paris, Rome, or someplace with CLASS?! DREXEL: Well, we're on a westward trek, eventually winding up at Wembley
Stadium on Labor Day for NCW Overseas. I'd also like to announce the debut of a new show on NCW: NCW Friday Night Main Event, hosted by Shawn
Tyler and TNT
O'CONNEL: Great...so now the Tylers are congregating. JUST what this world needs! DREXEL: Right now, let's go to a special announcement from our NEW Commissioner, Justice. (The scene is the NCW office in Iowa City. A caption reads TAPED 6-13-97. Justice is in a grey suit and his mask with the Scales of Justice on the front) JUSTICE: We have entered a new chapter in Network Championship Wrestling...and age of Justice. No longer will the Commissioner's office allow the wrestlers of NCW to endanger the safety of the fans. Beginning with our return show, NCW Head of Security Dixie Hart will maintain a permanent place at ringside to maintain order. Should the need arise, I will assist ms. Hart in maintaining order. While I can do little about the high number of outside interference, I will not allow the fans to be hurt as a result. That is all.
DREXEL: And if you take a look at the timekeeper's table, Dixie is sitting right there to jump up and take control if necesary.
O'CONNEL: Big deal. DREXEL: Let's go to ring announcer Robert Stone and his translator, Alexi
Denisovich. O'CONNEL: Is that a guy or girl up there? I mean, I know Russian women shave at least once a day...
STONE: The following match is wet for one fall Introducing first... accompanied to the ring by Mr. Mike Surimoko and Magma, from the island of
Okinawa, weighing 210 pounds, the Golem!
(The theme to Mortal Kombat plays over the PA. Magma is a tall tanned caucasian with short black hair and blue eyes. He wears black tights with red day-glo flames along the sides. Golem is an Oriental woman in a black full-singlet with red Japanese runes on the front. Surimoko is an
Oriental man with short black hair and is dressed in a martial arts gi with a black belt. His face is emotionless)
DREXEL: Golem coming off a big loss to Bad Company and Victoria Venom. O'CONNEL: The LEAST she couid have done was break a few of Vicki's bones before she lost...
STONE: Her opponent...from Paradise, weighing 160 pounds, the first lady of wrestling, Eve!
("Iron Man" plays over the PA. Eve is a long-legged, fit blonde with long blonde hair and deep blue eyes. She wears a pair of black shades and a denim jacket with "TROUBLE IN PARADISE" Airbrushed on the back, a black halter top and black bikini bottoms to wrestle in. She is carrying a
Singapore Cane.)
O'CONNEL: Now HERE is a woman with true class! She got the better of
Jammer AGAIN at Free-for-All. I wonder if she's signed with anyone?
DREXEL: Collar-and-elbow tieup and Eve with a knee to the midsection. A palm thrust and a roundhouse send Golem to the mat!
O'CONNEL: This is a VERY different Eve from her days with NetBlood. She's a LOT more vicious. DREXEL: Eve with a slingshot that vaults Golem RIGHT INTO THE RINGPOST! ORIGINAL SIN!!!! (back brain kick) 1...2...this one's over! STONE: Here is your winner, Eve! DREXEL: Uh-oh...here comes Lupa with murder in her eyes! O'CONNEL: What is she wearing?! DREXEL: That kind of looks like the jacket Jimmy Clifford was wearing at
Free-for-All, but with a few tears in it...AND EVE AND LUPA GOING AT IT IN
THE CENTER OF THE RING!!! Folks, we're out of time...GOOD NIGHT!
