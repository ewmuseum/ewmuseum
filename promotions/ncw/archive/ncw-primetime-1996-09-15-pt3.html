T-Bone: Welcome back! Flagg: Fans, we've got a rematch from last PRIMETIME! It's for the NCW International Title, and here's what happened last time these two faced each other, just two weeks ago in Montreal! [Footage is shown of the match from last PRIMETIME, including the repeated damage to the arm of Tank Turner, the near pinfalls, the Methodical Pain Chamber being applied to Turner, and the Nothern-Lights Superplex onto Turner's hammerlocked arm, which put both men through a table at ringside. Jack Flagg beckons for Jeandra's attention. She is still dressed in her halter top and shorts while Ryder is covered in sweat and has a bandaged cut on his left arm.
