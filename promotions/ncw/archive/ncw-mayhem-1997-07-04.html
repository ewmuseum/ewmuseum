NCW Mayhem — two title defences in Bonn

4 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TAPED 7-3-97 (Scene is outside the arena, shot on a hand cam. Into the picture rides a leather-clad biker. As the rider nears, the familiar face of Victoria can be seen. She pulls up to the camera on her Harley parks and disembarks.)
(off camera) FREDDIE: Hey, babe! You're a tad early aren't you? VICTORIA: Oh, YOU'RE here. Call me babe again and I'll clean your clock. And I'm a bit early. I've got a few words I wanted to put to the NCW matchmaking committee, but since you're here, I've got a couple of things to say.
(still off-camera) FREDDIE: Lay it on me, sugar! (Victoria winces with a frown) VICTORIA: One of these days, blondie... Yeah, I've got something to lay on
Ms. Devereaux and Ms. O'Connell. Girls, I'm so glad I've gotten your collective attention. Before I came to the NCW, I got to watch several tapes of the old NCW that Freddie and Dirk had from their early days. They also had a smattering of FWLI/RSPCW tapes as well. Somewhere in that mess,
I saw BOTH of you. Ms. Devereax impressed me as being a fine competitor with an angle similar to mine. Ms. O'Connell, you just struck me wrong from the beginning. You're quite the Little Miss Ego, aren't you? Let me explain one thing to you quite plainly. If I hear you or hear OF you calling me "Vicki", you'd better sign up for classes on eating soup SANS
TEETH because I'm going to smash your face so hard, your dental hygenist is going to have nightmares for a month. And for Ms. Devereax, I knew you'd be between me and a title shot when I came here. I've studied your tapes, and I'm familiar with your style of wrestling. If you think you're going to step over me and look past me to the NCW Women's title, you're going to have a quite violent wake-up call when we cross paths, honey. And don't worry about my...focus...on you - you're not my type. And I'll tell you this: Ray Kinge is WAY more man that you can handle. You better stick to the wrestling. See you in the ring.
(Victoria walks away toward the arena. The shot follows her walking off into the distance.)
(off camera) FREDDIE: What a woman! Devereaux and O'Connell, GO TO YOUR ROOM! Network Championship Wrestling presents NCW Main Event (The scene is an arena filled with screaming fans. The scene shifts to the broadcast table where Shawn Tyler and Jana, Mistress of Spiders sit.
Shawn is wearing his old NCW blazer (like NetBlood has any use for 'em...) and a white turtleneck, while Jana is wearing a black body suit.) SHAWN: WELCOME to Sportpark Nord in Bonn, Germany! This is NCW Main Event! I'm Shawn Tyler, and sitting in for Thomas N. Tyler, who's competing at the Welsh Cup, Jana the Spider!
JANA: That's Jana, Mistress of Spiders, worm! And I know for a fact that
Shamrock and Monique Devereaux are an item. The question is just WHO wears the pants in that relationship.
SHAWN: While I have you here, could you PLEASE explain your actions last week in your match with Glory Flagg--
JANA: I do not understand our new commissioner! Why will he not allow me to play with my food?
SHAWN: Hoooooooookay...let's go to the ring with Joe Drexel. JANA: He needs a little more meat on his bones... DREXEL: Will you please welcome the man who will face "The Dragon" Tony
Constanza later tonight, "Hitman for Hire" Jason Stone! Well Jason, it looks like you finally got what you wanted out of the Dragon.
STONE: Well Joey, it seems as if Constanza's been practicaly forced into this match, because I know he wouldn't accept it if he thought on his own.
Tony, you and I both know that you're scared shitless of getting into the ring with me, because you know that I'm the meenest, toughest, and baddest motherfucker in the world...and just a word of advice, I hope you're wearing black pants tonight if you know what I mean.
DREXEL: And if you do win the match, what future goals do you have in the NCW? STONE: If!!! IF!!! there ain't no if about it pal!!!! Do you actually think that a little pussy like Tony has any chance in hell of beating me?!?!?!?! I didn't think so. But WHEN I beat Constanza to a bloody pile of shit my next goal is to be hired to kick some ass, and then make even more money by winning a title.
DREXEL: Exactly which title do you want? STONE: I don't care which danmed title I win, hell I might as well take all of 'em, God knows nobody here can beat me.
DREXEL: Well thanks for your time, and thanks expecially for not throwing me out of the ring again. Do you have anything to add?
STONE: Just that everybody better be ready to see some blood tonight, and
Tony better be there, or else.
SHAWN: We're going to start things off with our Internet title match, so let's go to the ring!
JANA: That cage match sounds tastier all the time... STONE: The following match is for the Network Championship Wrestling
World Internet Championship! It is set for one fall. Introducing first, the Network Championship Wrestling World Internet Champion...accompanied to the ring by Karl "The Krippler" Kruger, from Dortmund, Germany, weighing 268 pounds, "Violent" Victor Frost!
(The crowd goes ballistic as Beethoven's "Ode to Joy" plays over the PA. Kruger is a tall, lanky caucasian with a shaved head and piercing blue eyes. He wears long, black wrestling tights with a red and a yellow stripe on the right leg, a black elbow pad on the right arm, black boots, and has taped wrists. Frost is a muscular caucasian with short blond hair and a five o'clock shadow on his otherwise rather handsome face. A tattoo of a cobra covers his left shoulder and upper arm. His ring attire consists of black trunks with stylized silver lightning all over it, black boots and a black vest with "VIOLENT" in crimson letters on the back. The NCW World
Internet belt is around his waist.)
SHAWN: No question who the crowd is behind tonight! Frost's partner a native of Bonn. STONE: The challenger is accompanied to the ring by "The eXpert" Edward
Heath...from Indianapolis, Indiana, weighing 180 pounds, from eXcellence
Inc., eXtasy!
(eXtasy is a muscular black woman with long black curly hair and green eyes. She wears a black silk robe with violet "X" all-over print. She removes the robe to reveal a black t-back leotard with "eXtasy" in violet across waist, then begins posing for a VERY appreciative crowd.)
SHAWN: AND FROST GRABS ExTASY BY THE HAIR AND DRIVES HIS FOREHEAD INTO
THE BACK OF HER HEAD! Frost spinning eXtasy around...SHORT-ARM
CLOTHESLINE! AND AN UPPERCUT SENDS ExTASY OUT OF THE RING AND TO KRUGER'S FEET! JANA: Slowly...if you rush, she'll be underdone! SHAWN: Frost coming to the outside as Edward Heath coming around to help eXtasy...KRUGER JUST CLOTHESLINED HEATH! HE'S GOT THE CLAW ON THE
ExPERT!!!! JANA: Hmmm...a second course! SHAWN: Frost rolling eXtasy into the ring...CHOKESLAM!!!!!! And that should be enough, but he's taking her to the top rope...
JANA: The dressing is everything. SHAWN: VIOLATOR!!!! (fallaway slam from the second rope) 1...2...3!!!! STONE: Here is your winner...and STILL Network Championship Wrestling
World Internet Champion, "Violent" Victor Frost!
SHAWN: eXtasy's mind was simply not on this match. JANA: Pity...the thrill of the hunt makes the meat that much sweeter. SHAWN: Ummmm...sure. Let's go to our next match. STONE: The following match is for the Network Championship Wrestling
World Women's Championship! It is set for one fall. Introducing first, the challenger...accompanied to the ring by Dan M. Special, from
Sweetwater Tennessee, weighing 139 pounds, Mya Special! ("The Look" by Roxette plays over the PA. Dan Special is a very strong, tall handsome, with blue eyes, and blond hair. He wears red tights and a blue jacket with "The Specialists" emblazoned on the back. Mya Special is a very beautiful, tall, well toned women with braided red hair. She wears blue tights and a red top with a red jacket with "The Specialists"
emblazoned on the back. They are pushing something to ringside.) JANA: Oh joy, Dan's inflatable woman is about to release some more hot air. SHAWN: We've seen this before, where Mya brought out a bucket under that tarp and gave Lupa a bath, the hard way!
JANA: One does not treat predators lightly. And thought Lupa is my prey, she is a potent predator in her own right.
SHAWN: So you pick Lupa to win this? JANA: I pick Lupa to hurt Mya...badly. STONE: And now...introducing the Network Champuionship Wrestling World
Tag-Team Champion...accompanied to the ring by Fang, from the Appalachian
Mountains, weighing 150 pounds, from the Troublesome Trio, Lupa!
(A wolf's howl, followed by Native American drums fills the arena. Lupa is a short caucasian with shoulder-length brown hair. She is wearing a tanned leather singlet and has the NCW World Women's title over her shoulder. Fang is a grey wolf with a rope around his neck. She ties Fang to the guardrail near Dixie Hart and warns her to watch Fang. She warily looks at the contraption the Specialists pushed out, then climbs the turnbuckles and stares at Mya.)
SHAWN: Mya taunting Lupa, but Lupa not budging...Dan pulled the tarp off...IT'S A BUCKET! LUPA DIVES AT DAN, BUT DAN NAILING LUPA WITH THE
BUCKET!!! IT WAS EMPTY! JANA: Lupa needs to watch the trickery of her prey better. Even the most fierce hunter can be defeated if caught off-guard.
SHAWN: Mya crawing out of the ring and grabbing the half-concious Lupa. She doesn't want the Champion counted out, she needs to pin Lupa or make her quit. Mya with a reverse DDT and a cover 1...2...and a kickout by Lupa.
JANA: I've met Lupa's trainer when he was still feared in the wrestling world. She learned a lot from him, including his survival instincts.
SHAWN: You're speaking of course of JammerWolf VP "The Wolf" Lucas Garou. Mya trying to wrap this up as she sets up the bulldog...LUPA PUSHES MYA OFF! MYA FELL THROUGH THE ROPES TO THE OUTSIDE! JANA: Next to Lupa's packmate. SHAWN: Mya just looked nose-to-snout with Fang and is scrambling back... LUPA WITH A PLANCHA ONTO MYA! Lupa rolling Mya in and climbing up top...SPLASH FROM THE TOP TURNBUCKLE! 1...2...3!!!!!
JANA: Dinner is served. STONE: Here is your winner...and STILL Network Championship Wrestling
World Women's Champion, Lupa!
SHAWN: Lupa's not done yet! She just nailed dan with a plancha and returned the favor by nailing HIM with the bucket! She just grabbed the timekeeper's hammer. She's climbed back in the ring...She just placed the bucket over Mya's head AND IS HITTING THE BUCKET WITH THE HAMMER!
JANA: Leave her be...she's playing with her food! SHAWN: As the ring is cleared and we set up the cage, "Wildcat" Joe
Drexel has located Marcus Magus, who has a few things to say.
DREXEL: Shawn, I'm here with-- MAGUS: That will do, plebian! I can take it from here! It seems that little Kid Sick is a little upset at the Magus's treatment of him. Boy, you are not worth breaking a sweat over. You are beneath my notice! Come back when you grow chest hair! As for LaShawn Marcus...the Magus is impressed by your spirit, if not your mental capasity. You apparently wish the same fate as your gang-mate Curtis Fredrick. You need to remove yourself from such a crowd as the Street Level, as they are headed toward self-destruction. I have a proposal for you...the magus is looking for an apprentice. One who is willing to serve the Magus in return for his tutilage. You could well be that apprentice. Forget the Trooper. forget the Street Level...and forget the Light-Heavyweight title, as we all know that the Jammer is not interested in reinstating that title as long as he can't make the weight requirements! Join me, and we will rule NCW.
Oppose me...and the beatings you've gotten since your arrival will seem like child's play...no one refuses the Magus and lives.
SHAWN: Looks like losing his job didn't stop Magus's ego. The cage is set, so let's go to the ring.
STONE: Ladies and gentlemen, the following match will be held inside a steel cage! It will continue until one competitor is unable to continue or submits to the other! There is NO DISQUALIFICATION! Now entering the cage, from Los Angeles, California, at 6'3", and 254 lbs, the "Hitman for
Hire" Jason Stone!
("King Nothing" by Metallica blares over the PA as Stone comes to the cage. Stone is a white male with brown hair and 5 o'clock shadow. He wears jeans and a grey ripped muscle sweatshirt to the cage and hops in.)
STONE: And now introducing, accompanied by his manager 'Shamrock' Deirdre
O'Connel, from the North End of Boston, Massachusetts, at 6 feet even and
275 pounds, that Bad Boy from Boston, "the Dragon" Tony Constanza!!
("Train Kept A-Rollin" by Aerosmith cranks up as Tony and Dierdre come to the ring. Deirdre O'Connel is a blonde in a grey Paola Moretti business suit, while Tony is dressed in a red karate gi with a black belt carrying nunchaku.)
SHAWN: And no chance for the Dragon to get in as Stone takes the fight to him!! Stone wades in with a SHORTARM CLOTHESLINE---DUCKED BY COSTANZA!!
JANA: It's interesting the way the Dragon lured Stone into his web... SHAWN: Stone launches a quick PUNCH, the Dragon trades him a DISCUS PUNCH and now both wrestlers circling around. Stone commits to a grab...ducked by Constanza. The Dragon with a EUROPEAN UPPERCUT...ELBOW SMASH...
BACKFIST! Three quick blows stun Jason Stone! Stone staggered a little bit, Costanza WHIPS him to the CAGE WALL! Now Constanza across the ring...
JUMPING CHOKESLAM!! Stone hits the mat here! JANA: Apparently Stone is a shell with no substance... SHAWN: Constanza reaches in with headlock...looks like he's talking trash to Stone now. Stone not doing much...STONE COMES ALIVE with a LOW BLOW to
Constanza...
JANA: The basics always work best, it seems... SHAWN: Stone folows with an EYERAKE and he's out of that headlock!! Stone mounts the offense with a STOMP to the foot of the Dragon...KNEE LIFT takes some wind out of the Dragon's sails! Now Stone is in the driver's seat...BIG BULLDOG by Stone!
JANA: The Dragon appears to be dragging. SHAWN: Stone hoists Constanza up for a BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX...BLOCKED BY
CONSTANZA!! Stone lifted him up and Tony took Jason's legs out from under him...
JANA: Desperation move, but it averts a failed hunt. SHAWN: Constanza is up first, he lashes out with a SPINNING HEEL KICK... snaps into a SAVATE KICK and it looks like there's a cut on Jason Stone! Constanza opened him up over his left eye! Stone backpedals away from the
Dragon, he's reaching outside the cage...Stone has a baseball bat!! He must have had it in his corner!! Stone swinging that thing wildly, you can hear it clang against the cage as the Dragon dodges out of the way! Stone swings again...
JANA: And the prey staggers the predator. SHAWN: Tony Costanza takes a bat to the ribs!! Constanza drops like a sack as Stone pulls back for another shot...this could end this fight!!
JANA: It could end Constanza!! SHAWN: Stone smashes that bat down, nothing but mat!! Constanza rolls away as Stone follows, pounding the mat behind him. One hit and that bat's gonna break bones!!
JANA: It's nice to know Monique's lap-dog doesn't throw like a girl. SHAWN: Tony gets an assist from outside the ring as Dierdre tosses his chucks to him... Stone winds up again, Constanza GRABS THE TOP OF THE
CAGE!! What a dodge!! Constanza now drops on Stone and they're scuffling for that bat, rolling over and over...Stone with another LOW BLOW and he gets the bat!! Constanza snaps Stone's hands with the chucks!! Stone drops the bat, the Dragon steps in and TILT-A-WHIRL SLAM to the CAGE!! Stone dazed here as Cnostanza clamps on a NUNCHAKU CHOKELOCK and he's calling for Stone to submit. Stone is waving the ref off...
JANA: The prey clinging to life...however much in vain... SHAWN: Constanza still holding that headlock against the cage...Stone with a BACK HEADBUTT that smashes Constanza's nose!
JANA: Spilt blood merely makes the meal that much tastier... SHAWN: Constanza is bleeding from the nose now, while Stone and Constanza are trading blows...Stone with a grab and FALLAWAY SLAM...REVERSAL!!
Constanza slid that into a LEG SWEEP TAKEDOWN! Costanza backpedaling as
Stone comes charging in...Stone has got to be pissed that this isn't working according to his plan...
JANA: The Hitman doesn't seem too stony now... SHAWN: Stone with a huge HAYMAKER... MISSED!! Constanza answers with a
KNIFEHAND CHOP! Snaps it back to a RIDGEHAND CHOP! HIP TOSS by Tony
Constanza and Stone bounces off the cage again. Stone comes flying off the cage and NAILS A SHORTARM CLOTHESLINE!! Constanza drops and Stone is on him with a CHOKELOCK!! Now Stone is calling for Constanza to submit!!
JANA: There are some creatures for whom pain does not matter, and Stone is one such creature. Constanza will have to put him down to win.
SHAWN: Constanza looks like he's fading here...Constanza LIFTS STONE UP
AND SLAMS HIM INTO THE CAGE!! AND AGAIN!! Stone lets go of the chokelock, now Tony wades in...KNEE LIFT...DISCUS PUNCH...FOOT STOMP! Stone wobbly in the ring, Constanza bringing it home now...SAVATE KICK...KNEE LIFT...
PALM HEEL to the face! Tony stepping back, Stone looks right out of it,
JUMPING SIDE KICK by Constanza!! Stone bounces off the cage again...CRUCIFIX POWERBOMB to Stone by Constanza!! Constanza is up quick, but Stone is struggling...
JANA: I'm amazed he's still moving! There is more to this prey than meets the eye... SHAWN: Constanza with a KNEE LIFT to the FACE of Stone!! And ANOTHER!
