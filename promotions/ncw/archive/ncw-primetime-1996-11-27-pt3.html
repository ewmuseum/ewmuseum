[NCW] PRIMETIME part 3

27 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Flagg: Fans, we've got a special match coming up....it's the NCW debut of a wrestling legend! We'll let the announcer tell you who it is, but we'll tell you that he's going to be facing a man who gave it his all at Nightmare on the Net, the "Nature Boy" Todd Newkirk! T-Bone: This one's gonna shock you! I just wish that Newkirk were at 100% 'cause then this would be a match of the year candidate! Flagg: It might still be! Let's go to the ring! Fletcher: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN....this match is scheduled for one fall with a 15 minute time limit.....Introducing first....from Starkville, Mississippi.....at 6'3" and 250 pounds....the current FWLI World Heavyweight Champion.....T H E " N A T U R E B O Y " ............. T O D D N E W K I R K !!!!! ("Nature Boy" is a caucasian with brown hair and brown eyes. He has a lean build that has some muscular definition to it. He wrestles in red trunks, which have the words "Nature Boy" written in white on the left side. He has red wrestling boots, which have the letters "TN" in white on both of the boots. "Nature Boy" wears a white sequined robe that has the words "Nature Boy" written in red on the back. "Nature Boy" enters the arena to the theme from the movie "2001". He is bandaged on the forehead, and around his left shoulder. He is also wearing a kneebrace.) Flagg: "Nature Boy" really pumping up the crowd with those "WHOOOO"s! T-Bone: He looks to still be moving a little slow from the Tower of Doom Match! Fletcher: HIS OPPONENT....a multiple-time World Champion from various federations around the world.....from Toronto, Canada.....at 5'8" and 226 pounds....." B A D B O Y "....M A R K B A G W E L L !!!!! (As the "Theme from Bad Boys" by Mark Mancina begins to play over the PA system, the arena goes black, and "black" lights turn up. There's a "Bad Boy" logo which is shown in the middle if the ring, doing 360o turns. Mark Bagwell comes out from the locker room with a cocky smile on his face, hold is hands up high, occationaly giving the finger to the fans when the camera isn't on him. He's wearing his usual wrestling attitre, which consists of white jeans, with a silver belt, has got black cowboys boots, and also wears a black jean shirt with riped off sleeves, with "Bad Boy" written on the back of it. He's also wearing a pair of silver Oakley shades. As he gets about half way to the ring, he'll lower his shades a bit, and begins to insult a few fans at ringside. Once he begins to walk up the ring stairs, he replaces his shades back to normal, flick his chewing gum out, and jumps into the ring. Once in the ring, he takes off the shades, fixes his hair, and gives out a loud "Wwwwwooooo!!!". The fans boo him loudly.) T-Bone: For better of worse, the one and only "Bad Boy" is in the house! Flagg: The fans may be mad at us for that, but he's a multifederational superstar, so we couldn't turn him down when he offered to join up. *DING* *DING* *DING* Flagg: There's the bell and both men are circling each other....They lock up! QUICK armdrag takedown by the "Nature Boy", but that seems to have hurt his shoulder as well! T-Bone: If "Bad Boy" was watching the Tower of Doom Match at Nightmare on the Net, then he'll know right where to hurt Newkirk! Flagg: Both men back up....Newkirk goes for another lockup....BUT BAGWELL KICKS HIM IN THE MIDSECTION....AND NAILS NEWKIRK WITH A SWINGING NECKBREAKER!!! T-Bone: Newkirk is rolling around on the mat holding his neck! Flagg: Bagwell picks Newkirk up....BODYSLAM! Newkirk wasn't ready for this onslaught! Bagwell now fires Newkirk into the ropes....sets for a Hiptoss....NEWKIRK BLOCKS IT! NEWKIRK WITH AN ABDOMINAL STRETCH ON BAGWELL!!! T-Bone: If Newkirk can just keep on the pressure! Flagg: NEWKIRK APPLIES AN ABDOMINAL CLAW AS WELL....but the strain on his left shoulder makes the Abdominal Stretch not as powerful as it should be! BAGWELL REACHES DOWN AND HAMMERS AWAY ON NEWKIRK'S KNEE WITH HIS FREE HAND! T-Bone: Newkirk's knee was torqued way out of place by multiple leglocks placed on it during the last PPV.... Flagg: Bagwell is hammering on it, and Newkirk is feeling the effects! Newkirk tries to adjust his leg...AND BAGWELL HIPTOSSES HIM TO ESCAPE THE ABDOMINAL STRETCH!!! Bagwell bends down to pick Newkirk up....INSIDE CRADLE BY NEWKIRK!!!! 1...2...Bagwell kicks out! T-Bone: Newkirk almost ended it right there! Flagg: Bagwell picks Newkirk up....Irish whip...REVERSE BY NEWKIRK!!! NEWKIRK WITH A CHOP THAT STAGGERS THE "BAD BOY"!!! Newkirk: WHHHHHOOOOOOO!!!!!! Flagg: ANOTHER CHOP! SNAP MARE BY NEWKIRK! Newkirk backs up....RUNNING KNEEDROP BY NEWKIRK!!! T-Bone: Good thing that was his GOOD knee! Flagg: Newkirk picks Bagwell up....RAKE TO THE EYES BY BAGWELL!!! Bagwell hooks Newkirk....VERTICAL SUPLEX! Bagwell picks Newkirk up.... back him into the corner....Irish whip...NEWKIRK HITS CHEST FIRST AND STAGGERS BACK OUT....INTO A BACK SUPLEX BY BAGWELL!!!! T-Bone: Bagwell is really taking control here! Flagg: Bagwell grabs Newkirk's legs....AND LOCKS ON THE BOSTON CRAB!!! T-Bone: He calls that the "Bad" Crab! Flagg: NEWKIRK IS IN PAIN!!! Newkirk's knee has been through so much lately, it's a wonder he hasn't submitted immediately! T-Bone: HE'S GOING FOR THE ROPES!!! Flagg: FANS, NEWKIRK IS FIGHTING THROUGH THE PAIN TO REACH THE RING ROPES......HE MAKE IT! Bagwell refusing to break the hold....Referee Timothy Nighthawke is administering the count.....Bagwell breaks JUST before the five-count! T-Bone: Newkirk is in real trouble here! Flagg: Bagwell picks Newkirk up....STRAIGHT RIGHT HAND FLOORS NEWKIRK! Referee warning Bagwell about the closed fist....BAGWELL IS GETTING IN THE REFEREE'S FACE! THEY'RE ARGUING IN THE MIDDLE OF THE RING!!! T-Bone: Throw the bum out! Flagg: BAGWELL COCKS BACK A FIST AT THE REFEREE....AND NEWKIRK ROLLS HIM UP FROM BEHIND!!! 1.....2.....thr-NO! Bagwell kicks out at the last second! Newkirk hearing the chants of the fans now! He picks Bagwell up! BIG CHOP! ANOTHER! NEWKIRK DUCKS A PUNCH....AND HITS A BELLY-TO-BACK SUPLEX ON THE "BAD BOY"!!! T-Bone: He needs to end this now! Flagg: Newkirk locks on a side headlock....BULLDOG BY NEWKIRK!!! Newkirk goes for the cover....1...2...FOOT ON THE ROPES! Referee telling Newkirk to break the cover! Newkirk sees the foot on the ropes and hooks that leg for another cover! 1....2....BAGWELL RAKES THE EYES TO ESCAPE THE PIN!!! T-Bone: Nighthawke was looking at the shoulders and did'nt see Bagwell reach up for Newkirk's eyes! Bagwell is one slick rulebreaker! Flagg: One of the dirtiest in the game! Bagwell picks Newkirk up again, sets Newkirk up for a suplex....NEWKIRK BLOCKS IT! NEWKIRK REVERSES IT!!! Newkirk pulls Bagwell back to his feet....BUT BAGWELL NAILS HIM WITH A LOW BLOW!!! T-Bone: YOW!!! That's gotta change yer tune! Flagg: Newkirk doubled over in pain....Bagwell sets him up for possibly another vertical suplex....HE HITS THE FALLEN EMPIRES (brainbuster)!!!! T-Bone: What an impact Newkirk's head made on the mat! Flagg: Bagwell covers! 1....2....NEWKIRK GETS A FOOT ON THE ROPES!!! Bagwell sees the leg of Newkirk's on the ropes....AND POUNCES ON IT!!! T-Bone: That was his bad leg, too! Flagg: Bagwell jumps up and crashes down on it again! Newkirk is in trouble again, as Bagwell takes advantage of that weak knee! Bagwell drags Newkirk into the center of the ring....HE'S GOING FOR THE "BAD" CRAB AGAIN....BUT NEWKIRK USES HIS GOOD LEG TO KICK HIM AWAY!!! T-Bone: BUT BAGWELL FELL BACK INTO THE REFEREE!!! The referee is down! Flagg: Which means Bagwell can cheat all he wants now! Bagwell sees the referee down....goes back over to Newkirk....BUT NEWKIRK CATCHES HIM WITH A HEADBUTT TO THE MIDSECTION!!! Newkirk grabs him in a front facelock.... AND DRIVES HIS HEAD TO THE MAT WITH A DDT!!!! T-Bone: Stay on him! Flagg: Bagwell is staggering back up....NEWKIRK PULLS HIMSELF UP BY THE ROPES....AND CATCHES BAGWELL WITH ANOTHER BULLDOG!!! T-Bone: That was DOUBLE TROUBLE!!! Newkirk is coming back! He's limping on that bad leg, but he's coming back! Newkirk: WWWWHHHHHOOOOOOO!!!!!! Flagg: Bagwell is stunned! Newkirk covers...........BUT THERE'S NO ONE TO COUNT!!! Crowd: 1....2....3....4....5....6.... Flagg: Newkirk is looking around....he sees the referee and crawls over to him..... T-Bone: BAGWELL IS PULLING SOMETHING OUT OF HIS JEANS POCKET!!! Flagg: Newkirk is trying to revive the referee! The referee is slowly coming around! BAGWELL SPINS NEWKIRK AROUND AND SWINGS....BUT NEWKIRK DUCKS!!! NEWKIRK WITH A SUPERKICK THAT DROPS BAGWELL!!!! T-Bone: But Newkirk's leg buckled under him! Flagg: The referee is starting to pull himself up by the ropes....NEWKIRK IS STAGGERING BACK UP....HE GRABS BAGWELL'S LEG....NEWKIRK SIGNALS FOR THE NATURE'S PAIN (figure-4, scorpion combo)....BUT BAGWELL REACHES UP AND BLASTS HIM BETWEEN THE EYES WITH WHAT LOOKS LIKE BRASS KNUCKLES!!!! T-Bone: DAMN! The referee didn't SEE THAT? Flagg: Apparently not, as Bagwell quickly LOCKS ON THE "BAD" CRAB!!!! Referee staggers over and sees Newkirk out cold in the submission hold.... T-Bone: THIS IS A TRAVESTY!!! Flagg: Referee checking Newkirk....he raises the arm...it drops once.... ..twice......and that's it! Newkirk is out cold! *DING* *DING* *DING* Fletcher: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN.....the winner by submission at 11:23.... "BAD BOY" M A R K B A G W E L L !!!!! Flagg: Newkirk will have something to say about this when he wakes up! T-Bone: What a cheap victory by one of the dirtiest players in the game! Flagg: Where in the hell is TNT? Oh well we have a barn burner for our next match! T-Bone: Hell, this should be a good match. It's Brian Knight versus Chris Taylor in a non-title bout. Knight asked for this match after his team LOST to Taylor's team at Nightmare on the Net. Flagg: And Taylor was quick to accept it. Taylor recently lost his FoPEW World title to Knight, and would love nothing better than to get his hands on Knight again. T-Bone: After the Starkville Homecoming incident, Taylor wants Knight any way that he can get him. Let's see if Knight is on better behavior than he was a few nights ago. Flagg: Well, Knight was fined over $300,000 for his actions against "The Heartbreak Kid". Looks like Knight won't be able to afford that stereo system for his lodge. Both of these men were wounded pretty badly after Nightmare on the Net, so neither may be at 100%! Fletcher: Wrestling fans, the following contest is scheduled for one fall, and is a non-title match. First, being led to the ring by the lovely Vogue, weighing in at 245 pounds, and hailing from Aspen, Colorado, he is the FoPEW World Heavyweight Champion, "T H E I C E M A N" B R I A N K N I G H T ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! <"Little Things" begins blaring through the arena. Knight is a well built caucasion, with long blond hair, and ice blue eyes. Knight sports long blue tights with ice sickles running down each leg. Knight is also wearing a "Blaze Taylor: So Hot" t-shirt, and the FoPEW World Title Belt. He has a bandage on his forehead, as well as a heavily taped left arm.> Flagg: Well, Knight's going to get in trouble for that little outfit. We know how protective Taylor is about his wife. T-Bone: Knight is the scum of the Earth, but he knows gets people angry. He's a cold individual. Fletcher: And his opponent......<"Hot Stuff" by Donna Summer kicks in, as everyone leaps to their feet.>.....hailing from Starkville, Mississippi........here is YOUR N.C.W. WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION...... "H O T S T U F F" C H R I S T A Y L O R ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! <Taylor charges the ring, wearing only his wrestling trunks and wrestling boots. He is bandaged heavily across the ribs and right arm, and has a bandage on his forehead as well. He slides into the ring, and immediately attacks Knight. Blaze follows behind with the NCW World Title in hand.> *DING* *DING* *DING* Flagg: And this match is underway! Taylor begins a barrage of punches and chops on the man who orchestrated a series of attacks on Blaze Taylor. T-Bone: Taylor drives a spin kick to the midsection of Knight, AND LAYS HIM OUT WITH A D.D.T!!!!! Taylor quickly grabs Knight! FIGURE FIVE BY CHRIS TAYLOR!!!!! This one could be over in record time! Flagg: Not quite, as Knight is able to reach the ropes. Knight is backing away from Taylor, but Taylor is on fire here! Taylor with a flying clothesline rocks the FoPEW World Champion! Taylor with a pin! One, two, and Knight is able to kickout! Taylor now with a forearm smash! Taylor from behind! GERMAN SUPLEX BY TAYLOR!!!! One, two, and Knight is able to roll out of it! T-Bone: Have you ever seen Taylor more dominant? Taylor and Knight are both up, and Taylor executes a standing Frankensteiner! One, two, and another kickout by Knight. Taylor hits the ropes, and nails Knight with a bulldog! Taylor grabs Knight, and fires him to the ropes! Knight ducks a clothesline, and grabs Taylor. PYRO-PLEX BY "ICEMAN" BRIAN KNIGHT!!!!!! One, two, thr...oh, what a move there! Flagg: And this match has suddenly changed direction! Knight picks Taylor up, and fires him to the ropes! Knight goes for a swinging neckbreaker, but Taylor counters it with a side suplex! These two men are fighting like they were FRESH, not like they just got through with a match like the Tower of Doom! T-Bone: I think it says alot when Knight's only offensive move has been his finisher. Maybe he's underestimated Chris Taylor. Taylor picks Knight up, and delivers a Northern Lights suplex of her own. One, two, kickout by Knight. "Iceman" was almost pinned by his own move. Flagg: Taylor quickly grabs Knight up in an armlock leglock submission. Knight is struggling.....he's reaching.....and he places his chin on the ropes, forcing the break! Taylor grabs Knight, and hurls him from the ring! Knight is slow to get up, but he makes it back into the ring. T-Bone: But Taylor is waiting for him, catching him in a hooked powerbomb! One, two, and Knight kicks out again! Vogue is up on the ring apron! Taylor makes a move towards her, and it's giving Knight a chance to regroup! Flagg: And Knight is back up, and he grabs Taylor! He scoops Taylor up, and delivers one of the fastest Tombstone piledrivers I've ever seen! Knight grabs Taylor up, and hooks him! German suplex by Knight! One, two, but Taylor is in the ropes. Knight hits the ropes, as Taylor gets to his feet. Leapfrog by Taylor! Go over by Knight! Leapfrog by Taylor! ENZUILARIATO BY CHRIS TAYLOR!!!!! What a move by "Hot Stuff"! T-Bone: And Knight's head bounced off the mat with that shot! Taylor executes another standing Frankensteiner, and hooks a leg! One, two, kickout by Knight, who is once again backing off! Taylor delivers a forearm smash, looks to the crowd, and executes A THIRD FRANKENSTEINER!!!! Taylor heads to the corner, and waits for Knight to get up. Bulldog headlock by Chris Taylor! Taylor's going for the pin! Flagg: But he just isn't getting a three count! Taylor jumps onto the ropes, and throws his hands into the air! He's got this crowd behind him! Taylor grabs Knight, and delivers a dragon suplex! One, two, kickout by Knight! Taylor makes a move, but Knight delivers a low blow, stopping the champion cold! T-Bone: Taylor is sent to the ropes, and is nailed with a clothesline! Taylor is trying to get up, but Knight is there, and he places his opponent on the top turnbuckle. Knight nails Taylor with a painful belly-to-back superplex! Knight grabs Taylor, and executes a German suplex, and keeps the bridge! One, two, kickout by Taylor! Flagg: Knight goes for a forearm shot to Taylor's back, but Taylor side steps it, and hooks on a swinging neckbreaker! Taylor picks Knight up, and drives him back to the mat with a spinebuster! One, two, and another kickout by Knight! I've never seen so many pin attempts in one match. Taylor grabs Knight, and throws him out of the ring. T-Bone: And now this match is about to get ugly! Knight is up to his feet, and Blaze is right in his face! KNIGHT DESTROYS BLAZE WITH A CLOTHESLINE!!!!! TAYLOR IS FUCKING PISSED NOW!!!!! Taylor levels Knight on the outside with an enzuigiri! Taylor picks Knight up, and hurls him into the guardrail! Flagg: Taylor grabs Knight and goes for a bulldog headlock, but Knight blocks it, lifting Taylor up, and hurling him at the guardrail! Taylor hit hard! Blaze Taylor is back up, and she grabs Knight, but Knight delivers a kick to her midsection, AND THEN A SUPERKICK!!!!! Oh, God, Blaze Taylor is hurt. T-Bone: And if you thought Taylor was pissed before....Taylor fires Knight to the ringpost! Taylor grabs Knight's head, and slams it THROUGH the wooden ringsteps! Knight's head just exploded open with blood! These men are adding damage on top of damage! Flagg: But Taylor is checking on his fallen wife! Knight grabs Taylor, and charges towards the guardrailing! Knight picks Taylor up, but it's Taylor with an irishwhip into the ringpost! Vogue is now on the scene, as she's challenging Taylor! T-Bone: Go ahead and hit the bitch! Flagg: T, you didn't have to go there. Knight uses this chance to grab Taylor, and fires him back into the ring. Knight climbs back into the ring, but Taylor grabs him, and sends him to the ropes! Taylor levels Knight with a shoulderblock! Knight is once again begging off, but Taylor won't hear it. Taylor delivers a powerful tiger suplex! One, two, and yet another two count enters this match! T-Bone: Taylor hits the ropes, and executes a flying clothesline, but look at Knight! He's bleeding like hell, and he doesn't care! Taylor whips Knight to the ropes, and delivers a sharp elbow! Taylor goes for a pin! One, two, AND ANOTHER KICKOUT BY BRIAN KNIGHT! Taylor hits the ropes, and ducks a lazy Brian Knight clothesline! Flagg: But Taylor connects with a shoulderblock! Fans, Brian Knight's blood is all over the place! He's bleeding like a faucet! Taylor scoops Knight up, and delivers his Hot Shot! This could be it, fans! Taylor grabs Knight AND LOCKS ON THE FIGURE FIVE!!!!!! T-Bone: Jack, it's obvious that the adreniline that was pumping in Taylor at Nightmare on the Net has not subsided. Knight is reaching for the ropes, and Vogue is pushing them inward towards him. Knight has them, and Taylor is forced to break the hold. Flagg: And Knight is pulling himself out of the ring. He's grabbed ahold of his FoPEW World title.....HE LEAVING?!?!?! Fans, Brian Knight, to the jeers of this crowd, is leaving the ringside area. Vogue is walking behind him, and Taylor's in a state of shock. Knight is taking the coward's way out. *DING* *DING* *DING* Fletcher: The winner of this match....as a result of a countout at 15:34 ...."HOT STUFF" C H R I S T A Y L O R !!!! T-Bone: He's not taking it, he's taken it! Knight is gone! There is blood everywhere, and it's all Knight's. Taylor is standing in the ring, and he is fuming! Well, stop right there! Jonathan Byrd is ready to give you the Byrd's Eye View on Network Championship Wrestling! Coming soon to NCW. =============================================================================== Flagg: I'm standing in the ring with NCW World Heavyweight Champion, "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor. Chris, a solid match, but not the victory you would have preferred. HSCT: You can say that again, Jack! You know, I am sick and tired of having to chase Brian Knight. Everytime we step into the ring, he runs around, and cheats his way to either a victory or a disqualification. Brian, let me tell you something. You took my FoPEW World Title. For that, you'll pay. You know what happens when you play with fire, Brian? You get BURNED! Well, "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor is on fire! <Knight makes his way from the back, and climbs onto the interview podium, well away from the ring.> IBK: Hey, Boyscout! You talking trash to ME? Here's the big picture, pal. I've got your number, and that number is two. No matter where you wrestle, you're not the number one most talked about anymore. That honor goes to the "Iceman". It's a new dawn, pal. The era of the "IceAge" is upon us. You know it, and you can't handle it. HSCT: It's pretty easy to talk like that when you just walked out on a match with me. Seems to me you're running scared! <Chants of "Coward" begin to rise> IBK: Taylor, get serious. You think after Random Violence....you think after the War Games in NCWF....you think after taking on a guy like Zack Malone in FoPEW inside of a week, that I'm going 100% against YOU?
