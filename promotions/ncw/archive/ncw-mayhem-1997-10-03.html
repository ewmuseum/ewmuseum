10-3-97: Jammer/Lupa v. Magus/Masters, Eve v. Jana, Varth v. Izcleta Network Championship Wrestling Presents NCW Main Event ANNOUNCERS: Shawn Tyler and Thomas N. Tyler Shawn immediately asked TNT why he felt the need to try to cripple Sean Darring. TNT said that a lot of his fans were thinking he was going soft, so he had a point to prove. He apologized to Darring (resting nicely in his home nursing his leg) for possibly ending his career, but in this business, these things happen.
LADY ROSE: This tag-team match is for one fall. Introducing first, the king of
JammerWolf Productions and his favourite pawn. Their combined weight is 179.5 kilos. They represent the Rat-Pack and are Lupa and the Jammer.
LADY ROSE: Their opponents are true countrymen and are destined for greatness both individually and as a team...whenever Jammer allows them to officially form one. They are escorted to the ring by Shamrock. Their total combined weight is 232.7 kilos and are "The Doctor" Jim Masters and Marcus Magus.
Magus entered the ring first, but when Jammer entered as well, he tagged out to Masters. Masters overpowered Jammer quickly, but Jammer managed a quick rollup for 1. Jammer tagged to Lupa, who delivered a missile dropkick that stunned Masters long enough for Jammer to clothesline him. Lupa covered, but the count was stopped when Magus broke the cover. Masters tagged to Magus.
Lupa attempted a dropkick, but Magus grabbed Lupa's feet and literally threw him over the top rope by them. All four men began brawling on the floor, well beyond the ten-count. The brawl was finally ended when Magus and Masters retreated following a Lupa suicide dive on Masters.
RESULT: Double COR LADY ROSE: The following match is for the faux-NCW World Internet Championship. The so-called Champion is from Parts Unknown. He is accompanied...Oh, he's
Fred. LADY ROSE: The challenger is from the Mecca of culture, the Bronx in New York
City. She is accompanied to the ring by the man whose true love can be derived from his name, Tequila Morales. She weighs 68.2 kilos and is a member of Vatos
Locos. She is Iz...what is this word? I'm not sure, but you peons know who and what she is, so I shall leave it at that.
Shawn noted that Dixie hart was on the watch from "Dashing" Derek Lankford, who has been influencing the Internet title by essentially picking and choosing who would win every match. TNT wondered how one could face someone who was 150 pounds heavier than you were. The Lord of Darkness dominated the match early, delivering a devastating powerbomb early on. Tequila tried to distract the
Lord of Darkness, but the Devastator made sure Tequila's influence was minimal.
True to form, Lankford made his presence known, but this time he ran (literally) into Dixie Hart. Hart was keeping him from the ring until Gary Trent appeared and began attacking Lankford. The brawl began to spill into the crowd, so
Dixie was forced to interject herself. This gave Lankford the opportunity to rush the ring and deliver the Lankford Leglock (standing Figure Four) to Izcleta
WINNER & NEW CHAMPION: Izcleta by DQ
Lady Rose then complained that now she would have to learn the pronunciation of her name. LADY ROSE: The following match is for the faux-NCW World Women's Championship. It is for one fall. The challenger has been determined by the band of simians known as the NCW Championship Committee to be the #4 Contender. She hails from parts unknown. She weighs 62.3 kilos and should have been squashed long ago.
She is Jana, the Lover to...I mean the Mistress of Spiders. LADY ROSE: The Champion...and I feel comfortable referring to her as such, though I do not think the title is worth mentioning...hails from Paradise. She weighs 72.7 kilos. She is Eve.
Shawn noted that the winner of this match would go on to face Mya Special next week. TNT wondered what would happen should Eve have to face her friend
"Blondie".
Jana immediately tied up with Eve and nailed a reverse neckbreaker. Eve was then tied up in a body scissors until she reached the ropes. Jana was nearly disqualified before she released the hold. Eve nailed a roundhouse as the two got up. Eve repeatedly hit Jana with martial arts kicks and chops until she tumbled out of the ring.
Eve followed Jana out of the ring, but was nailed by a springboard splash from the barricade. Jana then placed Eve against the ringpost and climbed onto the timekeeper's table (much to Lady Rose's chagrin). She then dove at Eve, but crashed shoulder-first into the post when Eve dodged the dive. Eve then rolled Jana in and attempted the Edge of Night (back brain kick), but Jana ducked. However, Eve managed to nail her on the way up for the three count.
WINNER: Eve (c) 1997 JammerWolf Productions
