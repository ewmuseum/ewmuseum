EVIE FALLS BACK BLIND!!! T-Bone: DAMN, NO DQ EITHER! Flagg: Jana pouncing on Evie and.....SHE'S RAKING HER FACE AGAINST THE STEEL CAGE!!! T-Bone: Phipps is going crazy on the outside.....
