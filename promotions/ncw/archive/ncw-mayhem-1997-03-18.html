NCW — Card

NCW Mayhem — the title-structure announcement

18 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Network Championship Wrestling presents NCW Mayhem (The scene is a large function room, filled with screaming fans. One fan holds up a sign reading "THE DRAGONS WILL TORCH NCW". The scene shifts to the announcers' table where "Wildcat" Joe Drexel and Marcus
Magus sit. Drexel is in an NCW sweatshirt while Magus is in a dark blue poet shirt and a black cape.)
DREXEL: We are LIVE from the Triangle Ballroom in the Iowa Memorial Union on the campus of the University of Iowa! Welcome to NCW Mayhem! I'm The
Wildcat Joe Drexel along with the incomparable Marcus Magus.
MAGUS: You know, I'm sick of Jammer coming out every other day just to bad-mouth his former employers. That kind of thing had ruined careers in the past.
DREXEL: You actually care about Jammer's career? MAGUS: No, mine! I could get blackballed for being associated with him! DREXEL: You're such a humanitarian, Marcus. MAGUS: Thank you. DREXEL: Our match tonight features The Destructive Duo challenging the team of Mist and Golem, but first, let's go to the ring for a special announcement.
STONE: Ladies and Gentlemen, it is my pleasure to introduce JammerWolf
Productions' General Manager of Operations, Mr. Lucas Garou!
GAROU: Thank you Robert. Well, as promised, we have news on the title scene in NCW. We are instituting four title belts. One for each division, as well as a unisex Internet belt that will be defended every week.
DREXEL: No smartass comments, Marcus? MAGUS: Why should I? I had to approve everything Garou is saying. GAROU: Rather than a long, drawn-out tournament like some other promotions might try to pull off, we've decided to pool the top wrestlers and teams as of June 1 and let them battle it out in a series of what we call Free-for-All matches. These will be held at our first supercard on
June , appropraitely named NCW Free-for-All. As the time gets closer, we will release more about each Free-for-All match, as well as any other matches that are signed. Now, on with the action!
(The crowd pops as Garou leaves.) DREXEL: Well, the stage is set, and now every victory is ever more important. MAGUS: Like anyone wants to lose in the first place... DREXEL: Tonight's match was set last week as the Jammer challenged the team of Mist and Golem after Mist defeated him.
MAGUS: I never laughed so hard in my life! DREXEL: However, there appears to be morte nthan a little dissention among the ranks of the Troublesome Trio.
MAGUS: A little dissention? More like Lupa is babysitting Jammer. Hey
Stone, let's get this fiasco underway!
STONE: Tonight's match is set for one fall. Introducing first... accompanied to the ring by Mike Surikomo, from the island of Okinawa, at a total combined weight of 435 pounds, the team of Mist and the Golem!
(The theme to Mortal Kombat blares over the PA. Mist is a lanky Japanese youth with blue-green tights. Golem is a tall, muscular Oriental woman with black hair tied back to a shoulder-length ponytail, wearing a black full singlet with red Japanese runes on the front . Her face shows no emotion. Surimoko is an Oriental man with short black hair and is dressed in a martial arts gi with a black belt. His face is also emotionless.)
DREXEL: Quite a contrast in styles between the pair, wouldn't you say
Marcus? MAGUS: But then, so are the Destructive Duo. However, Mist and Golem are giving up over a hundred pounds in this encounter.
STONE: Their opponents...accompanied to the ring by Lupa, at a total combined weight of 540 pounds...from the Troublesome Trio...The Jammer and
Thomas N. Tyler...The Destructive Duo!
(The crowd erupts as "Whipping Post" by the Allman Brothers blares over the PA. Tyler and Jammer are wearing a purple robes with tiger stripes.
Tyler is wearing a TNT doorag while Jammer is wearing purple shades. They removes the robes to reveal tights with a similar pattern. Lupa is a short woman with brown hair dressed in a tanned leather outfit which appears to be hand-sewn. Jammer grabs the mic from Stone.)
JAMMER: Hey...Misty...Rockhead! You two idiots just bought yourselves a world of hurt! Riceboy got lucky last Friday, but now we'll show you what it means to be in the ring with the BEST in the world today!
DREXEL: Jammer and TNT just charged Mist and Golem and are pounding on them heavily, but it looks like Mist and Golem are trying to fight back.
MAGUS: Meanwhile, Lupa and Surimoko-- DREXEL: Surikomo. MAGUS: Whatever. They are engaged in a contest to see who can do a better statue impression.
DREXEL: TNT has dumped Golem to the outside, and the official is trying to get someone from the Duo out of the ring, and it looks like Jammer is going out.
MAGUS: This could get ugly... DREXEL: Very true. Mist may be the heavier of his team, but he is far from being the powerhouse! Tyler with a hiptoss...and a bodyslam...and a side suplex. TNT is having his way with Mist!
MAGUS: And now the obligatory posing for the fans... DREXEL: TNT dragging Mist over to the corner...BY THE HAIR!? COME ON REF! MAGUS: For once I agree with you, but the official is on Tyler's case. I hope that the fact that Jammer is in charge won't stop the official from doing his job!
DREXEL: It didn't last Friday as we have a tag and Jammer going up top... and TNT slams Jammer onto Mist! Jammer with an Irish Whip...Mist ducks a clothesline...BUT TYLER PULLS DOWN ON THE TOP ROPE! And down goes Mist to
Lupa's feet!
MAGUS: And true to Friday's form Lupa pulls Mist to his feet and rolls him back in. DREXEL: And Jammer right there waiting for him, and a whip to the ropes, but Mist having enough left to pull on the brakes and tag in the Golem.
MAGUS: Jammer charging right into the dragon's mouth, as it were. Golem ducking a clothesline, and jammer is in no-man's-land.
DREXEL: NOw Mist and Golem out for a little revenge as both members pounding on Jammer's head and midsection. The official trying to restore order, but that's not an easy proposition, and Sorimoko looks...well, I guess he looks pleased.
MAGUS: For all we know, he could have heartburn. DREXEL: Jammer finally out of the corner, but collapses in the center of the ring. Golem with the cover 1...2...and Tyler with the save.
MAGUS: And Jammer still not moving. This is definate trouble for the
Duo. DREXEL: Golem now setting up a figure four BUT JAMMER WITH A SMALL PACKAGE! The official is still trying to get TNT out of the ring! He doesn't see it! MAGUS: 1...2...3...4...5...6... DREXEL: FINALLY the referee sees it! 1...2...and a kickout! MAGUS: Tyler's disregard for the rules potentially deprived his team of a win. DREXEL: Jammer making the tag to TNT, and he comes out in an explosive way! A clothesline...and a sidewalk slam...and an elbow drop, and the Duo are on a roll again!
MAGUS: Uh-oh...he's signaling for you-know-what! DREXEL: Atomic drop...and a clothesline!!! And Mist now in the ring... and here comes Jammer!!! MAGUS: Jammer with a flying shoulderblock into Mist, sending both men to the outside, and the official...is counting THEM?!
DREXEL: TNT WITH THE PAIN EXPLOSION!!!!!!!! (standing figure 4 leglock) Golem is screaming, but the official is counting Jammer and Mist! MAGUS: Lupa trying to get the official to turn around...AND SUKIYAKI--
DREXEL: SURIKOMO JUST THREW SALT INTO TNT'S EYES!
MAGUS: NEVER INTERRUPT THE MAGUS AGAIN! DREXEL: TNT clawing at his eyes as Golem covers, and FINALLY the referee turns around 1...2...3!
STONE: Here are your winners...the team of Mist and the Golem! (The crowd boos heavilly) DREXEL: And Jammer has lost it! JAMMER: That was bullshit! The most bullshit acll I have EVER heard! While you were counting out two guys who weren't even legal in the ring, my partner had the match WON! And THEN...THEN you didn't see salt being thrown in his eyes! Hey reef, HOW MANY FINGERS AM I HOLDING UP?! Should
I allow you to bring you SEEING FUCKING EYE DOG?!
(Mist grabs a second mic) MIST: Excuse me, Jammer, but isn't the motto of Mr. Tyler 'Whatever it takes to win'? DREXEL: THAT DID IT!!! Jammer and TNT are charging Mist and Golem, and we're out of time! For Marcus Magus, I'm "Wildcat" Joe Drexel, good night!
