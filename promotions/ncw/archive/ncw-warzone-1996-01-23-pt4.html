There is NO TIME LIMIT, NO DISQUALIFICATION, NO COUNTOUT, AND NO PINFALLS....the only way the match can end is if one wrestler says... "I QUIT" in the ring!!!! [cheers from the audience] This match will feature the return of the sub-vocal microphone patches, so that everyone can hear when one wrestler says the words "I Quit" to end the match! We recommend parental guidance for sensitive young ears as ALL words spoken by the wrestlers will be LIVE and uncensored!!! NOW, HERE ARE YOUR PARTICIPANTS.... [Approximately half the audience cheers as "Hot Stuff" by Donna Summer plays over the PA system, while the other half boos.] DEREK: FROM STARKVILLE, MISSISSIPPI....weighing in at 269 pounds and standing 6'6" tall....a two-time former World Heavyweight Champion in RSPCW and a former NCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION...."HOT STUFF" CHRIS TAYLOR!!!!! ["Hot Stuff" enters the arena to a mixed reaction from the crowd. Some are cheering for him and holding up "Hot Stuff" signs and "Hot Stuff" action figures. Others are booing him. "Hot Stuff" stands on the runway while pyrotechnic displays shoot flames into the air behind him. He is wearing red trunks with orange flame patterns on them, black boots with a similar flame design, and red kneepads. Over this he wears a black leather trenchcoat with "Hot Stuff" and flame designs on the back, and a black "Hot Stuff" headband. He has stubble on his normally clean-shaven face, and walks purposefully towards the ring without stopping to shake hands. Blaze is nowhere in sight. Some fans even have signs saying "Don't Fight, We Love You Both" and are cheering both men. The lights start to strobe and Jammer begins singing "She's Hot" into a portable microphone as he walks toward the ring.
