Redz()ne-1: The CWF/XWF Re-union Show

27 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Redz()ne-1 The CWF/XWF Re-union Show New Beginning... [It’s in my head. I’m full of doubt. Trapped in myself. I’m singled out!] - Diecast The Corporate Wrestling Federation Red-z()ne theme song hits. A giant collage of old CWF clips plays over a huge tron in the center of the jam packed arena. After a few minutes, the words CWF is 2003 appears on the screen. The camera zooms out and the sold out Allstate Arena in Chicago, Illinois is shown. With every seat in the house full, everyone one is on their feet screaming out their lungs. The camera goes through the stands. Fans are seen with their signs. One reads ‘Welcome Back!’ Another one reads ‘Mike Diamond IS CWF’. The camera switches over to the announcer table. Former Xtreme Wrestling Federation announcers, John Jameson and Joey Harrington are seen sitting there. They get a large ovation from the crowd and they begin the new season of the Corporate Wrestling Federation. John: Welcome back to the Corporate Wrestling Federation LIVE! Joey: You know John, I never actually got to call a CWF show, neither have you. But we remember most of these guys from the XWF and well, I know nothing but good things can come from this new brand of CWF! John: Well Joey, I’ve missed calling matches with you, but we better make the best of this, because no one ever thought these two great federations could die, we were wrong. But nothing can stop us from coming back! Tonight, we will have a lot of one time appearances and a lot of surprises. Joey: A huge surprise at that. Hellfire X, formerly Hellfire back in the day, says he has an announcement that will shock the world tonight.... John: Did we get any idea of what that will be? Joey: Not yet, but if he says its huge, than I’ll take his word for that... The lights in the arena go out. A mans voice is heard. ‘I am the God of Hellfire. And I bring you...’ Seizure of Power by Marilyn Manson hits... The crowd explodes with cheers as the CWF owner, Hellfire X steps on the stage. Camera flashes come from every which way and Hellfire has to rub his ears because the sound is so loud! Hellfire X walks down the Corporate ramp just as he did two years ago. He walks over to the ring and he hops onto the apron. He enters the ring and he poses for the crowd. He is not dressed up, he just wants to have fun. Former XWF Ring announcer, Mary Frank announces Hellfire X’s name and then she hands him the microphone. Hellfire receives the mic, and then he stands in the middle of the ring. He waits a few minutes for the crowd to settle down. He has a huge smile on his face. Hellfire puts up the microphone to his chin as he kicks off the Rebirth of the Corporate Wrestling Federation. Hellfire X: Welcome to the Corporate Wrestling Federation, 2003!!!! The crowd cheers yet again as the cannot stand the excitement that is filled within them. Some are jumping up and down, and some are losing their voices. Hellfire X: Tonight, LIVE on FOX broadcasting, we have a two and a half hour special show for you. Their will be no matches, but just being here should be excitement enough! I have three major announcements tonight! One of those announcements I will address here in one second. But the other two will involve the first CWF show that features wrestling matches, and the other, is a man no one would expect to see back in the Corporate Wrestling Federation. And well, the important thing about him, well he will have more than one job in this business! The fans cheer even though they have no idea what to expect. Hellfire X: Now, my first major announcement. Oh yeah, this is a major announcement... You see, I am a busy man. I will be wrestling in the CWF in some occasions. And as you all probably know, I wrestle for Dynasty wrestling as well. So, I have decided to announce a General Manager for CWF Redz()ne on Monday Nights!! This man knows how to run a business. He knows the CWF because he watched it every week. And no, it is NOT Dax.... Ladies and Gentlemen, I give you your General Manager of the Corporate Wrestling Federation.... Invasion hits the Speakers and the crowd screams their lungs out... Hellfire X: Former XWF Owner, MARK RHODES!! Mark Rhodes struts down the entrance way. Fans cheer and boo for the former XWF owner. Rhodes walks up the steel ring steps and into the ring. Rhodes enters the ring and he shakes Hellfire X’s hand. Rhodes poses for the crowd a little and signifies that he is back in power. Joey: The last time we saw these two in the same building, Hellfire was working for Rhodes! Hellfire X hands Mark Rhodes the microphone. Rhodes: Well well well. Look who has finally made his prestigious return to the top of a federation. Yeah that is right, me, Mark Rhodes! I want to thank Hellfire X for giving me this opportunity. If it wasn’t for him, I wouldn’t be here tonight. As your new General Manager, I will make sure this is the most entertaining show on FOX and on television! Tuesdays at eight o’clock will never be the same again now that I am here.....Thank you... Seizure of Power hits the speakers. Mark Rhodes and Hellfire X walk together to the back discussing some matters about tonight’s one hour special. The show goes to a commercial. John: This is gonna be an exciting night, I know that.... ---- Betrayal, Lust, Envy, Greed, Sloth, Fate, and Hatred. The Corporate Wrestling Federation presents its own Seven Sins..... LIVE!, February 1st, only on Pay Per View. ---- Canadian Disaster... The show comes back on air. Over the speakers you hear the Canadian national anthem. ‘Oh Canada!’ static....’Slammin’ by Buckcherry hits over the sound system. ‘The Human Suplex Maching’ Mark Kippler makes his way to the ring. The crowd begin to boo him. John: What a minute...he was not scheduled to come out to the ring.... Hell, why did we even call him back for the reunion show...? Mark Kippler gets into the ring. He grabs a microphone. Before he even has the chance to speak, he is cut off by Invasion. Mark Rhodes steps onto the stage. Rhodes: Look Kippler, you are lucky Hellfire even called you. But no one said you could come out here and cut a promo. So, seeing as this is a CWF Reunion Show, lets give you a taste, of the NEW CWF... The lights start to flicker. ‘Disasterpiece’ by Slipknot hits over the speakers. Former CWF Heavyweight Champion, Starter, steps onto the stage. He runs down to the ring and he slides into it. He kicks Kippler in the stomach and hits a modified DDT of what he calls, ‘The New Abortion’. Starter climbs to the top rope and hits an aerial assault splash entitled the ’Disasterpiece’. Starter’s music hits again. Starter leaves the ring and he heads up the ramp. He gives the camera an evil look. Then, an explosion is heard. Starter looks in the direction of the explosion. He looks up towards the rafters. He sees another former CWF Champion lurking up top. Chain. Starter runs to the back and look to persue him. Food Fight... Backstage, the camera is taken into a dining hall. Most of the seats are filled. A whole bunch of old names are seen. Including, Lt. TJ, Sephiroth, Ganjaman, Nemesis, Raider, Wave, Former CWF Champion Killa, The XWF’s former Outlawz, Dark Child, ’Supasta’ Allan Shaw, Greg Steiner, The Hunter, Antoine Hagans, Lucifer, Jeff Frank, ‘Air Attack’ Eric Zaytoun, Lil’ X, and New CWF GM Mark Rhodes’ son Tommy Rhodes. They all give a shout of to the camera as the continue their reunion dinner. All of a sudden Lil’ X throws a dinner roll at Killa. Killa throws a piece of chicken. After that most of the guys in the room start to throw food. This all turns into a giant food fight before the camera is forced to high tail it out of their. Joey: Food fight! John: Grow up... nTs... The camera picks up Hellfire X walking down a corridor heading back to his office. Former XWF Hardcore Champion, Beast walks down the hall and he runs into Hellfire. From a different direction, Lord of Darkness ‘Lod’ comes into the camera view. All three men look at each other. Lod: Well it looks like a little nTs, never Take Shit, reunion we got going here... Beast: Looks that way doesn’t it... We are only missing one guy, and that would be....Kid Gangster... So Hellfire, why isn’t he here tonight? Hellfire X: Kid Gangster? I did not invite him. I invited only the people who I thought would be a good edition to tonight’s show. And Kid Gangster, that slim ball, was not called. I do not want him ruining my show, because that is what happened before. Sure he draws crowds, but out feud is to on going to bring him here tonight.... Now if you would excuse me, I have some work to do... John: Too bad, no nTs reunion... Joey: What if Kid Gangster is here? Unexpected Visitors pt.1... Hellfire walks away from his former nTs friends. Beast and Lod talk away from the camera. Hellfire walks into his office. He sees new comer to CWF, Xtreme Max sitting in his chair. Hellfire looks at him like he is crazy. Hellfire X: And who would you be? Xtreme Max: You don’t recognize me? Maybe it is because I changed my wrestling name.... I used to be... Hellrazor! Hellfire X: Oh my god. Hey man what is up? I knew something was wrong when I couldn’t get a hold of you on the phone..... They catch up on old times as the CWF Reunion show goes to a commercial. ----- CWF Redz()ne Tuesdays at 9/8c only on FOX! ------- EPWA... John: Welcome back... Were seeing Starter right now! The show comes back on air and Starter is seen walking high above the Allstate arena. He walks above the ring in the rafters looking for Chain. Chain pops up from behind on Starter. They face each other and they start walking forward. Then from behind Starter again, Phantom walks out. From the side, Mr. X walks in the camera view. The former EPWA trio stare down each other, all ready to make a move. All of these men have crossed paths in the ring. But who are they with now? They all look around to see who will make the first move. They all charge at each other at the same time. Chain uses a chain on Mr. X. Phantom and Starter start to throw lefts and right at one another. Another former EPWA to CWF star, Evil Santa walks into the camera view, along with him is ANOTHER EPWA star. Hardcore Z. They all look their way. Evil Santa shakes his head in a ‘No’ motion. All six men start backing up. As they all the leave from the rafters in different ways, the show cuts into the middle of the ring. Kevin Gable is standing their. He looks around at the crowd before he begins to talk. Old verus New... Kevin Gable: Ok look everyone. I never really got into CWF at a right time. Hell, I was one of the last characters in the federation before it closed. So what I want, is a challenge for the SeVen Pay Per View event... Who is man enough to get in the ring with me? As that is said, ‘Ride of your Life’ hits over the sounds system. The first CWF Star ever, M4Dness makes his way from behind the curtain. He steps onto the stage with a microphone. M4Dness: You know Kevin Gable, I was just about to come out here and make a challenge myself. But I had someone else in mind. But I’ve changed my mind. Why? Because we are in an interesting predicament here. You were the LAST CWF star to enter the company before its final closing. And I was the FIRST CWF star EVER! So how about at SeVen, me and you go one on one, first versus last? Kevin Gable: Well then lets sign it because I am in it..... Just then, Hellfire comes up on the giant CWF tron. He is signing papers. He looks up at the camera and he begins to speak. Hellfire X: Just on time guys. I was just about to make my second out of three BIG announcements tonight. My announcement was going to be that on Tuesday morning at eight A.M, I will have my staff put up the CWF SeVen Card on the CWF Website! Until a minute ago, no matches were signed. But now however, it is official. At SeVen, M4Dness will take on Kevin Gable one on one! As for the rest of the card... Tuesday, 8 A.M.! Joey: Gable/M4Dness at SeVen, wow! The crowd begin to cheer. M4Dness and Kevin Gable leave the ringside area. Mary Frank gets into the ring. She begins to smile. Legends & Kool-Aid... Mary Frank: Ladies and Gentlemen, let me introduce to you, XWF Hall of Famer, and Four time World Champion, ICE! Ice is shown in the back. He is at a podium with a few hundred people standing in front of him. He is looking in a good mood and in good shape. He puts his mouth a little lower to the microphone. John: What a great guy.... Ice: Thank you everyone for bringing me to this reunion show. I can’t believe that we are back. The CWF was one of my favorite federations, XWF too. And I hope the best to everyone who competes and..... All of a sudden, former XWF Champion, Mick walks up next to Ice. He looks at Ice with disbelief. Mick begins to talk on the mic. Mick: How the hell could they put you on this show before me? I was a better champion. You never had to beat anyone hard. Mark Rhodes was practically your but buddy... Mick is then interrupted by Another former XWF Champion, Paco! Paco, who will be part of the new roster, pushes Mick and Ice out of the way. Paco: Mick. Why are you here? What did you have ONE title reign? Wow! I did better than that you peice of crap... Then, the crowd literally begins to jump in joy. Paco’s friend, and former FIVE time XWF Champion, ‘The Natural’ Mike Diamond walks onto the camera view. He asks Paco to move out of the way for a second. Paco steps aside and Mike Diamond takes center stage in front of the podium. The crowd begins to chant his name. Mike Diamond: Now, FINALLY Mike Diamond is in the Corporate Wrestling Federation. Yes, I, Mike Diamond, am back! Ice, Mick, and Paco. I am better than you guys. I am the five time XWF Champ. Ice, you are just a four time champ. And your telling me that I did not get in the Hall of Fame? I’m Mike freakin Diamond. Man you guys piss me off, I need some Kool-Aid, damn... John: Mike Diamond and his Kool-Aid... Joey: Didn’t he switch to powerade? John: That was so two years ago..... The crowd laugh as the show has to take a commercial. ----- Remember the old CWF? NO!?! What is the matter with you? Well then you can always check out the CWF Website, http://corporate.projectefed.com for a little help on remembering the past. ----- Unexpected Visitors pt. 2... Back in the arena. Former CWF Star and Announcer, Y2K is heading down the ring. He walks around the ring and over to the announcer table. He picks up Joey Harrington with force. He throws him against the barricade... He starts to scream at him. The camera can pick up the conversation. Y2K: What the hell?!? You stole my announcer position you asshole. I was the foundation of the Corporate Wrestling Federation. I know what to say and when to say it. You guys are just morons, especially you... Security guards come out and they pull Y2K off of Harrington. Joey gets in his chairs and starts talking. Y2K is kicking his way out of the arms of the guards. He is forced to the back and out of the arena. They walk him to the parking lot and toss him onto the ground. Joey: Idiot... Still in the parking lot, Y2K gets up and he opens the door of his car. He opens the door and he drives off. Coming from the same direction, a limo pulls up. The driver gets up and he opens the car door. Andrew Wright steps out of the car. Quickly, an interviewer runs up to him. Interviewer: Andrew Wright, you are late you know. The show is just about over... Andrew Wright: I know, traffic... Well you just tell Hellfire X something. First, good luck with the federation. And second. I will be back. Hellfire, we have a long score to settle. And trust me, I WILL FINISH IT! Joey: Strong words. And coming from him, they will be proven true... John: Someday yes, I believe it. Andrew Wright gets back in the car. The driver puts the car in reverse and the car is on its way. ANOTHER car pulls up. It comes to a quick stop, and Dax gets out of the car. Two guards walk up to him and tell him that his invitation has been revoked. Hellfire took it away because many wrestlers want to kick his ass. Dax, who is very upset, gets back in his car, and he drives away... The next scene comes up. Hellfire is getting ready to leave his office, when who comes in, AJ! This former CWF legend has fought and beaten the best. And he has been busy since last time he was in the fed. He goes up to Hellfire for a brief conversation. AJ: I can’t stay, but I said I would make it. Good luck... Hellfire: Same to you man... They give each other a hand shake and then AJ is on his way. X is the next guy to walk in. Hellfire X is just a little frustrated. He didn’t know he invited so many people. X: Hey man, I’m just like AJ now. I’m so busy. Good luck man. I might be back some day, who knows. But keep in touch ok? Hellfire X: Yeah man, see you later... good luck to you as well... They also shake hands. X leaves and Hellfire follows behind him. They both go separate ways. Hellfire X is now on his way to the ring! Sean Manning cuts him off by surprise. Manning: X, you know me in Dynasty. And I want to be here. So, what kind of match do you have set up for me? Hellfire X: You will find out Tuesday.... Hellfire continues to the ring. Manning looks like he wants to know so bad. Manning turns and leaves as the two CWF Announcers are shown. ------ Joey: Tonight the Corporate Wrestling Federation is being brought to you by Snickers, Xbox... John: And also Projectefed. Where dreams become reality.... --------- The Announcement... ‘Seizure of Power’ by Marilyn Manson blares over the speakers. Hellfire X makes his way down to the ring. He gets into the ring and is handed another microphone. The crowd prepare and cheer as the know that the third and final big announcement is ready to be told to the world. It could be anything. But Hellfire X did say earlier that it is someone.... Hellfire X: Well I hope you are all ready. Because I sure am. My BIG announcement will shock the world. Mark Rhodes as the General Manager was good, but this is better! I need all the help I can get. And how else can I get that unless I hire a commissioner. A commissioner that can also wrestle! I know you guys don’t have a clue who it is but here he is. I give you quite possibly the greatest wrestler and commissioner ever.... The crowd start to chant Kid Gangster’s name... Joey: I told you, Kid Gangster IS here!!! Hellfire X: Ladies and Gentlemen, H.... Hellfire X is cut off. Former XWF Commish and XWF Champion, Mad Dawg makes his way to the ring. Behind him is Mike Payne. Mike Payne looks at Mad Dawg with a confused look. Mad Dawg sends the same look back. They both get in the ring and they start arguing. Hellfire X: What are you two doing here? Mike Payne: You said that I could be the commissioner remember? Mad Dawg: I thought you were talking about me. I mean I was a great wrestler and commissioner... Hellfire X: First off, Payne, I said I would THINK about it, and Mad Dawg, you are just a moron. Now you two, get out of my ring please.... Mike Payne and Mad Dawg leave the ring in a disappointed fashion. Hellfire X look a little upset as he stands in that ring. Hellfire X begins to speak again.... Hellfire X: Now then. What you have all been waiting for...The new commissioner, Ch..... For some reason, Really Really Great Rick Hassan starts to come to the ring. Hellfire X looks very mad and confused now. Hassan gets to the ring and he starts to clap. Hassan looks very happy, but Hellfire X does not. John: Was he invited? Joey: I’m gonna say no..... Hassan: God, I knew it was me all along. I was the best commissioner EVER in XWF and a decent wrestler at that.... Thank you for this honor, I promise I won’t let you down..... Hellfire X: Ok, you weren’t even invited. So I am going to let the REAL commissioner take my anger out on you, you son of a bitch.... I give you the NEW CWF COMMISSIONER, ‘Hollywood’ Chris Harris! Joey: OH MY GOD! The lights flicker on and of as the fans begin to stand in anticipation. The flickering stops and the lights go gold. The main base in Saliva’s Superstar II picks up on the sound system. The fans stand and boo as 'Hollywood' Chris Harris steps onto the platform atop the ramp way. He holds his hands out to his side taunting the crowd as he slowly makes his way down the ramp way. "Now I'm a superstar in the making. I ain't fucking around and there ain't no mistaking. I never ask for something worth the taking. Cause I'm a superstar, baby." Chris walks up the steps and hops into the ring. He struts over to the opposite corner and ascends the turnbuckle. Chris puts his arms out as if to say "look at how great I am," He taunts the crowd and hops down from the corner. He goes over Rick Hassan and he laughs a little. Hollywood kicks Hassan in the Gut, he follows it up with a Double-Under hook Jack-knife Power bomb, Hollywood lands on his knees and his opponent lands on the back of his head/neck and folds up. John: The ‘Hollywood Bash’ to Hassan! Dear god! Hellfire X hold up Hollywood’s hand in the air. The crowd boos and cheers at the same time. 'Bawitaba' by Kid Rock hits the sound system! Joey: I told you he was here! The crowd starts to go nuts. An incredible ovation comes from the crowd. Hellfire X and Hollywood look very upset. A midget dressed like Kid Gangster walks onto the stage! Hellfire X and Hollywood start cracking up. The midget gets into the ring via the steel steps and he walks over to Hellfire X. He starts talking. 'Kid Gangster': You know what. I hate you. You never even invited me you asshole. I'm gonna kick your ass... John: Funniest thing ever!! The midget swings at Hellfire X. Hellfire blocks it and lifts the midget up and chokeslams him! Hollywood Chris Harris falls on the ground laughing. Hellfire X taunts Kid Gangster at the camera with a throat cut as he will never be on CWF programming again... Hellfire X raises Hollywood's hand in the air as the show fades to black...
