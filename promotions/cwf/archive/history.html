History

April 2003

HISTORY The Corporate Wrestling Federation started in 1998. Back then there were three people. Those three people were Hellfire X, Starter, and M4Dness. The roster built up as efeds became more popular. Then came the soon to be Co-Owner of CWF, Kid Gangster. He helped run everything and he kept all the shows going smooth. Kid Gangster then started his own fed with the money he made wrestling in hundreds of federations. Hellfire and Kid Gangster wrestled in eachothers shows all the time. Then Rattlesnake (a.k.a. Justin-X-Treme, a.k.a Dax) came to the fed to really build it up. He built the CWF some incredible sites for display online for online users to view. At the end of the year 1999, The CWF was number 54 on the top efeds EVER! And it won the efed of the year award that year. Then one day, a fed called the EPWA closed down. Still with the CWF, Starter was the comissioner of that fed. He recruited all the guys from it and most of them became CWF Legends. Some of those names included, AJ, Evil Santa, Chain Mr. X, as well as others. The fed was going perfect until it started to lose money. Starter, as well as others quit the fed. And soon the CWF was no more... Then one day in late 2000, many old CWF'ers met up in a parking lot. They became to talk. And with the help of some money, CWF came back. We were back at full force. That was nothing compared to what happened next. The XWF had just opened. And all their guys found time to compete in the CWF. Name from there included, X, Lil' X, Ice, Andrew Wright, Mick, Antoine, Lord of Darkness, Beast, as well as others. Storylines grew and the fed was back at the top. Later in 2001, the fed shut down due to significant money loss and lack of fan interest. Plus Kid Gangster and Dax weren't around to help. Dax and Hellfire couldn't get along. Dax and Kid Gangster couldn't get along. And that was the end. Hellfire and a couple guys from CWF, joined the XWF. It stayed open until the work got to hectic. Everyone went there own ways. Hellfire and Kid Gangster worked a whole bunch of federations together. And then they joined Dax's fed late in 2002, it was called HATE. Dax who was C.E.O. ran it while the owner was away, The owner never came back and that was the end of the fed. Hellfire and Kid stopped fedding for awhile. Hellfire eventually joined Dynasty Wrestling and he remains there to this day. And then the unthinkable happened. Dax and Hellfire restarted CWF! Just a week after opening, Dax left because he had alot of unfinished business. He remains in good terms still though with Hellfire and CWF. Hellfire paid the final fees, and the CWF is ready to open. Some names who are coming back from the past are, Chain, Starter, Evil Santa, and some others in time. But at this point, we are BACK!
