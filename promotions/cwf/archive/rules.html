Rules

April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RULES 1. After you have joined The Corporate Wrestling Federation, your wrestler is under contract and we reserve the right to fire your wrestler at any time. If you disappear from The Corporate Wrestling Federation we also reserve the right to use your wrestler for another 4 shows. 2. Do not sign up as a real life character because you will not be accepted. CWF only accepts original characters so rip offs will not be tolerated. Also be reasonable with your characters information and do not copy a real life wrestler. The weight limit is 150-500 lbs. The height limit is 5'5"-7'4". 3. If you make a stable/tag team the other members must agree to be included in it and the owner must register it, so you must e-mail me to tell me what is happening. A stable must contain three wrestlers at the least and 6 at the most. 4. The management of The Corporate Wrestling Federation will decide when and where to give you a push and if you deserve it. However, should you abide by the rules and are easy to work with then your push will come much sooner than someone who complains a lot. 5. After being accepted into The Corporate Wrestling Federation you must role-play at least once a week, Should you fail to do so without notifying the management in advance then you will more than likely be fired after the second time. 6. You may not include any wrestling matches in your role-plays or you will be warned or you could be fired from The Corporate Wrestling Federation. If you are going to include any fighting or even any dialog or just an appearance of another wrestler then we MUST have an email with that handlers permission before it is done or it is invalid. If we do not get this you could be fired warned or suspended depending on the managements thoughts and how far the other handler wants to pursue the matter. You may use the commentators as interviewers, but you may not attack them with out my permission. Also you may not submit a role-play with characters from WWE or NWA-TNA etc... unless you again have my permission and in this case it will only rarely be given. 7. Swearing is allowed just don't say "fuck" every other word, but all foul language should be censored if used often. ex. "fuck" = "f--k", "fucked" = "f---ed". 8. Do not demand a title shot! You can only demand shots after mailing me about it. The Owner will grant or deny title shots at their convenience. 9. Role-plays are judged on about 90% quality and 10% quantity. Even though that is the case the minimum number of lines allowed is 35 and even though there is no maximum number of lines. But if you are writing a really long role-play, please make it interesting to read. 10. Try to be creative in your role-plays. Don't do every single one in the arena. Something else like at your house, at the gym, parking lot of the arena, a restaurant, etc. Don't submit a role-play anymore in an arena unless it's to be on the show. This is a really good way to get on the bad side of the management so please just don't do it. If you need help thinking of a location then ask other handlers or me for advice. 11. The deadline for the role-plays for shows are posted on the shows page so stick to them. 12. Do not request any matches unless you are not booked for the next card and the person that you are challenging is not booked. If you want a match for a show then e-mail the owner. 13. No sexual harassment or racism is allowed. You will be warned on your first violation. A second violation WILL result in you being fired. 14. Don't e-mail the owner complaining that you lost your last match. This will reflect very badly on you and you will probably end up losing your next match as well. If you are unhappy about losing lots of matches there is only one thing to do. Improve your role-plays. 15. It is in your best interests to read all the RP's on the RP Board, especially the posts from The Corporate Wrestling Federation staff. You never know when you might find an opportunity to get into an interesting angle. 16. Kidnapping, torturing, sexually abusing or the like to ANY Corporate Wrestling Fed eration staff in your RP's is a violation of the rules and will carry serious penalties. 17. You may not advertise other e-feds or WEBSITES in your role-plays. If you do so, you will be fired immediately. (Some exceptions will be made in some circumstances) 18. Keep checking back to the Redz()ne, and PPV pages to check if you are booked for any matches. 19. Do not post any OOC on the role-play board. Use the OOC board! If you have a problem then e-mail me and we will sort it out or post it on the Forums. 20. If you plan on leaving CWF for a period of time or just leaving altogether then please e-mail me. You will not get flamed or blasted on the boards or anything like that if you tell me that you are leaving. We can then sort out a departure for you in the storyline and you can then leave on good terms. This would be greatly appreciated. 21. Do not constantly ask management about the same things such as jobs, matches or anything of that nature. Asking the same question over and over is another good way to get on the bad side of management. 22. Only one roleplay may be posted on the last day before the deadline. (There is NO limit on number of roleplays posted before the last day.) The Golden Rule 23. Have fun! *These rules are the same as the ones from Dynasty Wrestling , as we have a bold efed partnership.
