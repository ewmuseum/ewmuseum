Alignment: Face Move Information Top 15 Moves: Drop Kick, Top Rope Hurricanrana, Frog Splash, Split-Legged Moonsault, West Side Press (Shooting Star Press), DDT, Tornado DDT, Sharpshooter, Seanton Bomb, Superkick, Spinning Heel Kick, Belly to Belly Overhead Suplex, F5, Top Rope Shoulder Block, T-Bone Suplex Wrestler's Finisher: 'Youth of the Nation' Wrestler's Finisher Description: Pheonix Splash Style: Highflyer/Daredevil Bio Growing up on the streets of West Side Los Angeles, Sean Manning saw it all. From drugs, to rape, to murder, all of it was experienced by this young boy. An orphan at the age of 7, Manning was taken in by an African-American woman, who loved him like he was her own. Manning who had come out of the City of L.A., was now in the streets. The Woman, whose name was Mariella Tomlinson, fended for young Sean while he was picked on for being white. The kids would beat him up and steal his possessions. "Sean would always comes home with tears in his eyes, and say, 'Mom, they did it again!'. I couldn't stand seeing that!" Maryells described Manning's environment in vivid detail. "I tried to do something about that, but what could I do? This went on for about a year, until Sean finally realized that him running to me wasn't working, he finally figured out, that for him to survive he was gonna have to start fighting back." she went onto say. So, as Summer finally ended and all the kids went back to school, Manning, age 9, was in the third grade. He was in a new class, and he had a new attitude, and his 'take no prisoners' new persona, was the thing that carried him through these hard times. Manning was the only cacausion on his block, but not in his school. The first day back, Manning was outside, alone, and he say 3 boys, ages, 10, 9, and 9, beating up a smaller white child, age, 7. Manning got up from his seat, and quickly ran over to the three boys. Manning grabbed one of the younger ones and tossed him to the ground, and then he proceeded to punch the other two until they were on the ground and bloody. Manning was suspended from school for 5 days. But, he wasn't punished by Maryella. Instead, she praised him for standing up for himself. "He finally did something about the bullying problem he had run into. But, instead of hiding, he ran at it and beat it. I wasn't to happy about the suspension, but the actions that caused it, although violent, proved to me, and to Sean that, he was able to stand up for himself, and that he was going to survive, and be ok." Marryella finished with a proud smile on his face. Over the year Manning was suspended several times for fighting back when he was bullied. But, he was never expelled. "At first I was glad he was standing up for himself, but after i while I got nervous. I realized that him being suspended so many times, could get him in serious trouble, especially if it has to do with violence. And the day I got the phone call, that he had once again been involved in a fight, but this time the penalty was expulsion. My jaw just dropped. And I said my boy is just standing up for himself! And the principle responded, he hasn't been expelled for fighting... Mrs. Tomlinson, your son set fire to a class room... and he knocked another student out, and left him for death! He is being expelled for attempted murder, he could face time in a juvenile detention centre, and most likely will!" "I just couldn't believe my ears. I dropped the phone, grabbed my car keys and ran out the door and sped to the school." As Maryella Tomlinson walked in the front doors, of Manning's school, she saw Sean sitting on a bench, head in hands, sobbing. "I didn't mean to do it!" he said, his voice crackling. Tomlinson hugged Manning, and knocked on the door of the Principle's Office. The two of them discussed the situation for over an hour. Finally Tomlinson came out, slowly grabbed Sean's hand and sat down on the bench beside him. "I softly asked Sean to tell me what happened, as I whipped a tear from his eye. Then, he told me the most shocking, and moving story I had ever heard. He said he was inside finishing up some class work, because he didn't want to go outside, because there was a boy in grade 5 who wanted to beat him up, and he didn't want to get into a fight and get suspended again. So he stayed in. But, about 15 minutes into lunch time, the boy came into Sean's classroom. Sean said, he had a string and a knife. And, that the boy said he was going to tie him to a table and slaughter him. Sean said he ran to the door that led outside, but it was keylocked, and he couldn't get out. He said the boy charged at him and stabbed him in the arm. He showed me the wound, and I gasped, he then went onto say that the boy grabbed his arm, and wrapped the string around it. He then told me, that the boy tried to throw him onto the table but Sean managed to get free, but he was off balance, and he accidently ran into the classes fish tank that was on the counter. The school was very low-budget, and it had a propane powered pump, and when Sean ran into the pump fell on the floor and caught fire. He jumped back, but the boy was behind him and he stabbed him in the back. He again showed me a very deep wound. Sean said he continued running but was in tremendous pain. He said the boy ran after him and tackled him, but the knife fell of his hand. Sean said he picked the knife up, and hit the boy as hard as he could with the blunt end. He said he got up and ran out of the classroom straight to the office. He told the principle that he was bleeding and to goto the classroom. But, when the prinicple arrived the room was up in flames, the principle ran in and grabbed the boy, but it was to late to do anything about the room. 'No one believes me!' said Sean, and that just broke my heart, and then I said 'I believe you'." said Maryella describing the scene in vivid detail. Manning was never charged, or expelled, but the other boy served 3 months in a detention center. A couple years went by, and Sean Manning was finally respected. He had not had very many fights in school, and he had no suspensions. But, on January 19th, Manning's birthday, his bestfriend, 15 year old Dayton Carlson, was shot 4 times in cold blood, by two men, who wanted revenge, for a drug deal that went wrong. But, it turned out the Dayton, was the wrong man. This infuriated Manning, 14. He went ballistic. He immediatly found ou the mens names, and rushed home. He threw open him door, and grabbed a semi-automatic machine gun. He slammed his door, and ran downstairs. HE grabbed a pen and a piece of paper. On it, he wrote, 'Mom, I'm sorry I have to do this, but it has to be done. Dayton was killed out of cold blood, and as they say, payback's a bitch!' Manning put it on the counter and ran out of the house. He ran down the road, with the gun in his hand for all to see. He went down a few blocks, until he got to a yellow, and white house. He ran up the steps and knocked on the door. A 16 year old kid answered. "Did you hear what happened?!" Manning asked with a look of hate on his face, but not directed towards the boy at the door. "Yeah! Those bastards killed him!" The kid responded... "Let's go!" Manning ordered. "Where?" The man asked, Manning looked down at the weapon. The kid nodded his head, and grabbs his jacket, and a set of car keys. Manning and the boy hurried into a 1968 Camaro. The car pulled out of the drive way, and then sped down the road. About 5 miles after they had started driving, Manning and the kid, whose name was Vince Dupont, spotted the two murderers. The two culprits, ages 16, and 18, were looking back and forth over their shoulders, checking for police. The car screeched to a halt and Manning jumped out of the passenger side. He ran after the two men, as the car sped away. The two continued running, one going down an alley, and the other jumping over a fence into a backyard. Manning made a decision, knowing the area really well, he took the fence hopper, he ran towards the fence, he saw the 18 year old hung up on one of the posts... "Please, man... don't kill me, we're sorry! We're sorry!" Boom! Boom! Manning fired two rounds into the man's head, killing him instantly. Then he turned and chased after the other man. He ran down the alley, checking behind dumpsters, and trash cans. Then he saw the other boy, climbing up a large, 8 foot fence. He shot once, and twice. Hitting the boy in the arm and the leg. He fell 8 feet, breaking his leg. Manning walked over to him, and fired four rounds into the man's chest. He had killed the accomplice, but slaghtered the shooter. Police were soon to arrive. They found Manning sitting on the street curb, with the semi-automatic on the ground at him side, but out of ammunition. He directed police to the bodies, and peacefully was taken into custody. Vince Dupont turned himself in 4 hours later. Because the boys were so young, Manning was given 3 in Juvi, and 300 hours of community service, and Dupont, was given 10 months in Juvi, and 100 hours of community service. After Manning had served his time and done his community service, he said his good byes loaded a duffle bag, and went to Long Beach, to train for a career in the area of professional wrestling. Well he was in the Detention Center, alot of the kids had posters up, and watched the events. Over his incarseration, Manning studied wrestling and perfected the moves. There an isolated weight room in the Center, and for 8 hours a week Manning would life weight, then go back to the regular part of the Center and practice his moves. From his tosses, to hammerlocks Sean Manning knew it all. HE was allowed to read books about wrestling history, and it's training. So, now that Manning was out of the Center, he headed to Long Beach to get an apartment by the beach, and train at a little, but well known facility in the city. So Manning trained steady for three months, and then one day he decided to travel back to L.A. to see family, and to attend a Dynasty Wrestling event and right then and there, Sean Manning knew he was destined for Dynasty... Manning went on training, and training, a couple months went by, and still Manning was not signed to a contract. So, he went out looking for one. He came across a small backyard federation, called the BWF, he sent in an application, and he was accepted. He won various titles, and soon jumped ship to the nWf, where he also won various titles, and defeated some great competitors. But now Sean Manning enters... CWF!
