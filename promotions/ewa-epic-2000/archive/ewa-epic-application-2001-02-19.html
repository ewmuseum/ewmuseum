eWa Application

19 February 2001

Final Word - Farewell - Application I N T R O D U C T I O N [Opening] Hello everyone and let me welcome you to the Epic Wrestling Alliance (eWa). Instead of wasting time making promises that none of you care all that much about, I think I'll just get right into what counts - the promotion! The Epic Alliance is a completely new booked e-wrestling league run by me, that's right, your's truly, Hobbs. As a fed-hed and a writer for countless promotions in the past, I know my way around for sure. What I'm hoping is going to really seperate eWa for other federations though isn't so much me and my experiences in the business as it is you - the handlers. The Alliance is going to depend on handlers that are looking just as much to have a good time as they are to see their characters win titles and beat people to a bloody pulp. Here in the eWa we're not so much trying to create a big old roster.. in fact, the roster will probably remain small.. but we're really trying to construct a whole federations filled with superstars that are sure to add excitement to the mix. Angles and originality reign supreme here. So if you're at all interested, please continue and really think about filling out the application at the bottom. Thanks for your time. Hobbs [The Staff] (Real) The eWa staff consists of me, Hobbs, annnnd.. you guessed it, you guys! Being I've found in the past that having anymore then one person in control tends to lead to trouble anyway, I'd just as soon do things my way from the start. But, let's be reasonable, I can't do it alone. Ever so often I will need those of you who can to write a match here and there and help me out with things. Give me some ideas and angles and all that other fun stuff. In short, the eWa is truly going to a promotion for the handlers and by the handlers.. hehe, but let's not forget, I get the last word :) (Fake) Epic Alliance Owner and President: - Mr. Jamerson Deal has been in the business for a long time and he's seen every side of it. From being right in the squared circle as a wrestler, to being a manager, an executive, a commish, and now the big man in charge. Mr. Deal is without a doubt an extremely business savvy man who's got his mind set on making the eWa something larger then life. He knows what people want and he intends on giving it to them. Misc. other Staff Color Commentators: Johnny Midas and Jack Holiday Referees: Jeff Jones, Mitchel Hayes, and Kyle Cross Ring Announcer: Theodore Thomson Interviewers: Jeff Akkins and Missy Sweets R U L E S & R E G U L A T I O N S [Role Playing Rules] 1.) Original characters only! This should be pretty obvious, but just incase any of you where thinking about it for some unknown reason, I thought I'd stress it right here. Though I don't like the term, I won't be accepting anything but "fake" wrestlers into the eWa - the more creative the better. No "Stone Cold" Stew Boston or "Big Sexy" Ken Mash please. 2.) Role plays do NOT, and I repeat, do NOT have to be novels! As much as I love description and detail, myself, and probably everyone else will agree, you don't have to write over two hundred or some odd lines for it to be good. As far as I'm concerned, if you can get your point across and make it interesting, you can write yourself a fifty liner. 3.) Though I know most people will be a bit shocked - don't worry about role playing ALL the time! In the eWa we're looking for consistent RPers not people that put out a dozen a week. One or two RPs per card is PERFECT! Besides, we're going to run things through a flash session (I'll get into that later), so don't worry about hurrying to see who can write one more RP to beat the six or seven that your opponent put out - that's just moronic! 4.) Cuss all you want - just be reasonable. Absolutely no racial slurs. 5.) Don't use other wrestlers in your role plays unless you have their consent - same goes for President Deal. [Misc. Information] - As I mentioned above, the eWa will be sending its role plays through a flash system. What this means is you will send your RPs directly to me and I will stock pile them and then send them all out in a packet twice a week. Role plays will be sent out on the Epic Alliance's Thursday night broadcast - "Breakdown".. while other RPs will be mangled into the weekly card, Epic Xtreme - held on Sundays. - I will be looking for a few of you who are interested in writing matches for me. Whether I can get one or two of you to help me on a regular basis or just a handful of you that I can call on when I need you, it should work out fine. Some weeks I probably won't need any help, but it's good to have the back up there just in case. S H O W S & E V E N T S [Weekly Shows] Monday Night is Epic Xtreme: Epic Xtreme is the big daddy of the eWa events. eX, as it is dubbed, will be where you find all of the big matches and all of the angles taking place. Title defences will be pretty regular and you're looking at anywhere from four to seven matches. Thursday Night "Breakdown": Breakdown is pretty much just a mini card. You'll probably only find two or three matches, occasionally only one, a few clips, news and rumors, and all of that like. The real reason for Breakdown is to send out the flashes. This is where I will be posting the RPs you guys write and sending them out to you all. Sort of like ECW Pulp Fiction style. You know :) [Super Cards] Right now the only supercard being held for eWa is its grand openning event which will be held sometime in January. Once things get started we're probably looking at a pay per view every other month. T I T L E S [Championships] - eWa Heavyweight Title: Obviously this is the grand daddy of titles in the eWa. It's the most prestigious and thereby is also the most covets. Not only will it take one hell of an athelete to get a hold of it, it'll take one amazing superstar to be able to hold onto it. - eWa Television Title: Though some seem to think second place is the first loser, that is hardly the case in the Epic Alliance. Some federations may spend all of their time building angles around the Heavyweight belt, but I can assure you, the eWa TV title holder will have just as much fun trying to keep his hands on this title as the Heavyweight champion is his. Even more difficult to hold onto, this title will be defended quite often. - Titles to be considered: Tag Team, Xtreme, Cruiserweight. C L O S I N G [Farewell] So there you have it, the eWa in a nutshell. I realize it doesn't look all that spectacular on paper, but give it a chance. I won't bother bullshitting you with promises and guarantees, but I will say this - the Epic Wrestling Alliance WILL be an innovative and enjoyable experience. So give it a try, what do you have to lose? And if you don't feel like filling out the application now, you can always send me your email address and I will be happy to add you to the list so that you can see all of the action and perhaps decide to join later. Thanks for your time. Hobbs [Application] - cut here - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - eWa Epic Wrestling Alliance - Application I.
