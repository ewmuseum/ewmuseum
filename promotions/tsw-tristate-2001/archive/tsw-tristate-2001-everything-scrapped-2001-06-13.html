TSW Everything Scrapped, basically.

June 13, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Listen, guys... We've been doing this thing for about a week now, and we have only 3 men on the roster. That is very bad, in my view. However, I will still continue with this. The one thing that NEEDS to be changed is the date that our first show, Addiction, will happen on. I mean, there is no reason to have a show on June 20th, if we don't have enough guys to do it, and if we don't have enough guys to properly set matches up, so everyone can flash for Dissention. There is really no use.
Thus, the date has been changed to protect the...innocent? Well, not innocent, but to protect us all from this thing becoming, what I like to call, crap. It hasn't gone down the crapper yet, and I'm not about to let it. Neither are any of you, I would hope. The date has not been finalized yet, and won't be until we get enough wrestlers. Whether it takes this week, next week, however long it takes to get the roster up to about 8 or 10 guys. That way, we can have a proper card, and hopefully, the guys will start pouring in.
I am updating the pages as we speak, and hopefully, we will get enough wrestlers to start in like two weeks. That would be ideal, but at the rate we are going, nothing is sure right now. Just keep recruiting, and this will get started THE RIGHT WAY. I don't want this to be started prematurely, because then, the fed will drop faster than the women when
I drop my pants...but that's a different story, in and of itself.
Stick with us, we'll get there soon enough...and everyone will be notified with updates of what is going on.
Thanks for your patience in this matter, and let's get this started right! -- Josh http://www.efeduniverse.com/tsw - Shouldn't have added that women dropping comment.. - Will get ribbed by G all the time... - For the next year or so, at least. - Just like Gillberg... - Right? - :)
