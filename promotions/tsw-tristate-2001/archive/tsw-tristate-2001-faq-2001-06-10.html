\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\       Basic Information \=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\ 3.1 Roleplaying     Roleplaying is done by ‘flash packets’, or e-mail, in Tri State Wrestling. You, as a wrestler, will send us your roleplays:
TSW Office Roleplays aren’t really judged greatly, because they are considered promos that the wrestlers do…and, in actuality, TSW is kind of based the way ECW was. In that, they emphasized wrestling over interview skills. I’d really like TSW to work like that, and I really think that everyone will be interested in this. One roleplay a week is needed, and should be sent in. It doesn’t have to be long or anything like that, just something that gets your point across. I don’t want lengthy interviews that drag out, just to get a certain number of lines.
    Before sending your roleplay, check it over…YOU read it. Read it thoroughly, and while doing it…say to yourself, “would I want to read this?” Don’t just give it to me if it’s not something that you would want to read because it just drags on. Chances are, the longer it is, the more it drags on…and, the more I may send you down on the rankings.
3.2 Strategies     Strategies are the most important part in Tri State Wrestling. Why are they the most important part of TSW, you ask? Simply because, it shows me what you would do in the match. It shows your WRESTLING ability, with nothing else being put in higher regard. Keep reading, and you’ll get the idea of how to do it. All strategies will be sent to:
TSW Office Here is a basic outline of what we need on those strategies. WRESTLER: Your wrestler’s name is needed, so we know who to add this stuff for. MATCH: Put the match that you are sending this strategy in for. ENTRANCE: Put your entire entrance for the match. What music plays, is there pyro, what happens to the lighting, what does your wrestler do…all that kinda stuff is involved in this.
MOVES: Here is where you give us moves that you want to use in your match. You need to include at LEAST 10 moves, but more is appreciated, and make your match all that more effective.
SPOTS: Match spots are exact things that happen in the match. It is something that you can’t really put in for just a ‘move’. You need to explain it, and things like that.
Explain what happens to you and your opponent with the move, just as if
I were to copy and paste it into the card. Things like run-ins, new move introductions, manager things, big moves, reversals, finishes. All those included.
ENDING: What happens when your match is over? Does your wrestler shake hands with his opponent? Or, does your wrestler beat him up? Is there a run-in? Whatever you want to happen at the end of your match, put that here.
3.3 Angles     A big part of Tri State Wrestling is also the angles/storylines that are involved with it. If you didn’t win a match, it might not have been because your roleplay didn’t meet standards, or because your strat wasn’t as good as we would’ve liked it…it could be because of an angle. You have to understand that there is no reason to freak out over a loss in a match. It is just that, a loss. Everyone has them. Everyone has to take them, so they are done for angle/storyline advancement. Deal with the losses, and deal with the angles. If you really are not liking the current program that you are involved in, then please let me know, and we can work something out.
\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\            Shows \=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\     There are two shows a week for Tri State Wrestling. One show is solely for interviews, and that is called Dissention. The other show is our main program, Addiction.
Sometimes there will be Pay Per Views, but only for big purposes. There won’t be any every single month or anything like that. Instead, there would be one every other month, or one every 3 months. It all really depends on what goes on.
4.1 Dissention     The show where your interviews are placed. They are compiled together, and given out in one lump. They will be sent out on either Monday or Tuesday, depending on how much I have together. All interviews for the show must be sent to us by Sunday Night. Any given to us after that will be considered, but may not be put on the show.
4.2 Addiction     Tri State Wrestling’s primary show, Addiction, takes place on Wednesday nights. Addiction will have 4 matches, but maybe a bit more. More like 5, but it just depends on how many people are active on the roster. I’d rather not have a ton of matches, so that the wrestling itself is showcased, and is special, instead of just another common thing. Makes for a better fed in my opinion.
4.3 Pay Per View     PPV shows are not every month, and will probably instead, be every other month. When a Pay Per View is held, everyone is notified, so nobody will be in the dark. It is a special occasion, and because of that, there shouldn’t be an annual one every single month. Makes everything look better, in my opinion.
\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\        Championships \=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\ 5.1 TSW Heavyweight Title     This is the big thing in Tri State Wrestling. The thing that everyone is gunning for in here. You got this title, and you are a marked man. You will not turn the corner without the thought of someone jumping you in your mind.
Nobody is your friend…they all want that title. 5.2 TSW Tag Team Titles     The Tag Team Titles signify that you ARE the tag team of Tri State Wrestling. Every team will be lurking in the shadows, trying to pry the titles from your grubby hands. The only question that remains is…can you defend these titles all the time, successfully?
\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\             Rules \=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\=\     Yes, even Tri State Wrestling has a set of rules that all you guys will need to follow. There aren’t that many, so it’s not a big deal to just go through and read ‘em.
- If you lose a match, it’s either because of the way an angle was going, or just because your opponent did a better strategy for the match than you did. If you lost, face it, everyone has to lose. Pick yourself back up, and keep going.
- No using other wrestlers in your interviews, unless there is a circumstance involved. If you get his approval, then that is fine, and you can go ahead with it. If not, don’t do it. Just be sure to ask.
- Participate as much as you can in TSW. Talk to me about angle ideas for your wrestler, or talk about what I can do to make the fed better. Or, as a big plus, help us write some matches once in a while. It’d help out a lot, and definitely lighten the workload that we have.
