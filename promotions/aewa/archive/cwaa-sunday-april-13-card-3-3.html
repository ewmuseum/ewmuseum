Sunday, April 13, Card (3/3)

13 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EL: Very funny. Paul Langford now with a scoop slam. MN: So I take it you're upset about not being in the title match? Maybe if you two could have lasted ten seconds last week... MA: We weren't ready. MN: Weren't ready? You attacked my guys as they came through the ropes? MA: Then they weren't ready. Either way, someone wasn't ready, and you are honor-bound to surrender the belts to us. EL: Now why is that? Langford with a vertical suplex. MA: Because in addition to being the number one contenders, rightful champions, and the prettiest team you ever did see, we are positive role models for today's youth. EL: Not in that get up, you aren't. You look like the good humor man got sick. MA: It's called "style"- you just wouldn't understand. EL: You know, I think we're going to have to crack down on the number of people who come out here and sit at this table- no one can follow the action with this constant chatter. If it isn't you, it's Max Robertson or the Major. MA: Where is the Major, anyway? We need him out here more often. The Major knows the score. MN: The Major claims he ran a crack hand-to-hand combat unit, but from what I've seen the Major couldn't run a faucet. EL: McNultey in control now, with an abdominal stretch. Michael, is there a reason you're out here? MA: Well, I wanted to talk about what happened last week. See- MN: You just said you weren't going to complain about. MA: That was then/this is now/shut up and listen/you fat sow. EL: (to MN) All right, all right- sit back down, don't let him get to you. MA: Ok- here's what happened last week: we'd been coming out here for weeks handing out whippings to those boys like volunteers hand out food in the soup kitchens. And we started getting complaints from the save the whales people and greenies because they don't like it when people beat up dumb animals. So we had to hold back. EL: The only thing you've held back has been my ability to call this match. Byron McNultey with a n Irish whip- catches him- Northern Lights suplex, it looks like. MA: Hey, kids- try this at home! EL: All right, I think we've heard just about enough from you. If you're going to stay out here, I'd rather you just call the matches or give us some insights into your training and strategy, that kind of thing. MN: This should be good. MA: OK- here's how we got ready for this one- we work out in our secret gym- I can't say where because once it becomes common knowledge, it'll be flooded with all the bored housewives out there tired of waiting for their low-class fat-(expletive deleted) blue collar out-of-shape husbands to come home with their minimum wage checks, and while we don't mind a little female company, there is a time and a place for everything, and the gym is a place for us to concentrate on winning the gold. Then we have our big strategy session, and today, for instance, I said to Byron, "Byron," I said, "Byron, I dare you to go out there and break a sweat today. In fact, I double-dog-dare you," because we know what the ladies like. MN: This lady would like a sick bag. MA: You could just wear an ordinary paper ba- EL: Byron McNultey goes for a suplex- can't get him over- the Tornado reverses- got him! MA: I just want to say, Mother Nature, of all the hillbilly girls on "Hee-Haw", you were never my favorite. EL: Langford going for the airplane spin now- MN: This is the Tornado Drop- an airplane spin followed by a swinging neckbreaker! MA: I need to discuss something with the referee. (exit MA) MN: Oh, no you don't. (exit MN) EL: Finally a little breathing room. Michael Anderson up onto the ring apron, and Mother Nature grabs him by the jacket- she jut ripped that tuxedo jacket off of him. Langford drops McNultey and heads over- slugs Anderson! (Big pop as Michael falls off the ring apron) (as Langford tries to get Chris Herman to stop arguing with Mother Nature about her and Anderson's near interference, Anderson removes a shoe and tosses it to McNultey) EL: Look out- the Pretty Boy just smacked Langford in the back of the head with that shoe! And referee Chris Herman turns around- he's been caught red-handed! Chris Herman calls for the bell, and Byron McNultey is standing right there holding the shoe, Langford slumped over the top rope. Now he's hiding the shoe behind his back, pretending he doesn't know what Chris Herman's talking about. Now wait a second! Marvelous Michael Anderson- who looks ridiculous in that get-up, by the way- in the ring now, and he and McNultey start kicking and stomping at the Tornado. (Big pop, as a not-yet-dried Conley races to the ring in his jeans and with a towel draped around his neck) EL: And here comes Travis Conley to even things up, and the Pretty Boy and Marvelous Michael scatter like roaches when someone turns on the light. It looks like Langford will be OK- when we get back, we'll have our special feature match: Lt. Lawson versus Kore Wrench, for the CWAA TV Title! (This Wednesday night at 8, the WYXZ TV 39 Movie of the Week- Pam Dawber and Robert Hayes in "The Girl, the Gold Watch and Everything".) EL: All right, before we get to our special feature match, e sure to order the ACWF's Rule Britannia! pay per view spectacular. Stars you've seen on Championship Wrestling who'll be in England tomorrow nigh: Joey Valachi, Randy Sheradin, Joshua Barnes, and Max Robertson. And the AEWA has agreed that in 2 short weeks, right here on April 27th, we will have either the AEWA World Tag Team Champions, the Machines, defending against Barnes and Sheridan, or AEWA World Heavyweight Champion Crimson Joe Reed facing off against CWAA Heavyweight Champion Randy Sheradin. Also, less than a week away, Randy Sheradin defends his CWAA Title against Max Robertson on AEWA Wednesday Night Rumble! Next week, right here, we will have Joshua Barnes and Randy Sheradin against CWAA Tag Team Champions, Twister. And Max Robertson goes for the TV Title against occasional ally, Lt. Lawson, or Preacher Kore Wrench, depending on what happens next! (Gregorian Chant plays, and the popping begins) DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this is our special feature contest. It is set for one fall with television time remaining, and is for the CWAA Television Championship! Introducing first, the challenger, from New York, New York, he weighs 275 lb., "the Preacher" Kore Wrench! (The 6'5"-275 Preacher is clad as described earlier) DM: And his opponent- ("When Johnny Comes Marching Home Again" plays, and the booing follows) DM: -is the CWAA Television Champion. Accompanied by Major Victor Augustus Mayhem, he hails from Poughkeepsie, New York and weighs in at 219 lb., Lieutenant Daniel "Lethal" Lawson! (The flag-waving septuagenarian Major leads the 5'9"-219 lb., fatigue-clad TV champ to his first-ever title defense. The blue-eyed, brown-haired (crew cut, d'accord) has an 'all business" expression as he enters the ring and is promptly frisked for foreign objects) DM: Your referee for this contest, Jeff Howard. EL: OK- Television title action, and you can be sure that all CWAA Television Champions will be fighting champions- the title has to be on the line each and every week. And who better to be the first contender than the last man eliminated last week, the man who has the most reason to want to get his hands on the Lt.- and the Major, too- than Kore Wrench. There's the bell, and we're underway. The Preacher goes for a lock up, and he tosses the Lt. back into the ropes. Now Lawson complaining his hair was pulled- MVM: It was. EL: I see I'm joined again by Major Mayhem, who no doubt reports to General Nuisance? MVM: Corporal, I don't have time to go into the whole chain of command to you, and I'm sure you'd just get it all bass ackwards anyway. EL: Wrench with the hip toss now, and Lawson back up- another hip toss- and Lawson scrambles under the bottom rope to the floor. And I'm not a Corporal, by the way. MVM: Demoted again? You're a real disappointment, son. What was it this time? A little indiscretion with the local women? Don't you listen when we get the medics in to lecture to you all? You could contract a social disease and go blind. Where's your head, boy? Do you think we go over this with you for our own benefit? EL: Lawson back in there, and the Preacher takes him down with a shoulderblock. Now Wrench picks him back up- atomic kneedrop- but he doesn't let go- he picks him up a second time- another atomic knee drop- now a third! And bounces into the corner. Wrench follows him in, spins him around- big chop across the chest. Now a cross corner whip- and Wrench follows him in- smashes the Lieutenant's head onto the top turnbuckle- a second time- now a third- now into the middle buckle- again- again! Now the bottom buckle- again, and again! Now he slams Lawson's head onto the mat below the bottom buckle! (Big pop) EL: The Preacher drags him by his boots to mid ring. Off the ropes- leg drop across the back of Lawson's head! MVM: That's illegal. Call in the cavalry! EL: You're not going to start talking into your wristwatch again, are you? Now Wrench stands him up- sets him- powerbomb coming up- he stuck him! New Television Champion coming up? No- kickout on the 2 count by Lawson. Wrench sends him for the ride- knee to the midsection doubles Lawson over. Wrench backs up- big running kneelift! (Crowd pops louder than they have all night as Wrench signals for the Crucifix slam) MVM: Dis-missed! (MVM heads for the ring) EL: As the Major hops onto the apron, the Preacher runs into the ropes- and he knocks the Major to the floor, but it looks like he hit his own head as he stumbles. Lawson back up, on spaghetti legs, I believe the term is. Rake of the eyes- Irish whip- he tales Wrench over with a back body drop. Lawson now with an elbow drop on Wrench. The champ picks him back up- legsweep- the Major doesn't want me calling it a Russian legsweep anymore, if you remember. Now Lawson with an Irish whip, and he catches the Preacher in a sleeperhold! As always, near perfect precision on the Lt.'s execution. Senior Official Jeff Howard checks and verifies that it isn't a chokehold. He's checking for a submission- Wrench's arm drops once- (Remembering his request from earlier in the evening, the crowd starts cheering loudly) EL: -twice- (it gets louder) EL: -thr- no! (Even louder as Wrench keeps his arm up at the last possible instant) EL: Elbow to the midsection by Wrench- and another- and a third- and now the hold is broken! Kore Wrench off the ropes- clothesline! And- hey! What are THEY doing here? (The crowd starts to get a little nervously confused as Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas head to the ring, and stop at the foot of the aisle) EL: I know that there has been trouble between Thomas and Wrench before, but Hammer wants the TV title for himself- are Hammer and Thomas here for different reason? Kore Wrench hooks the Lt. in a bearhug, and Hammer and Thomas are whispering to each other about something. Maybe they're just scouting future opponents? Wrench squeezing the air out of Lawson- the Preacher has incredible power, and he has that hold almost perfectly applied- Lawson's feet are off the ground, Wrench's arms are low enough to get to the lower ribs. The Major looks a little groggy still out on the floor. Wait a minute now- Thomas just nodded and here he and Hammer come to the ring. Marc Thomas in there now, Hammer behind him- I smell a rat- two rats, more precisely! And Marc Thomas with a double sledge on Kore Wrench! (Serious booing) EL: Senior Official Jeff Howard calls for the bell, and referee Chris Herman heading for the ring now, as Marc Thomas works over Kore Wrench while Jak Hammer watches. Lt. Lawson now drops top his knees- Hammer helps him up- and a short clothesline by Jak Hammer! What's his game? These two just stroll right out here, like they own the place or something, and we've got Thomas going after Wrench- he's choking him on the that bottom rope with his foot on the back of Wrench's neck, and his ally, his partner, goes after Wrench's opponent? Jak Hammer now with Lt. Lawson in that Rebel Rack. I mean, it couldn't happen to a nicer guy, but he was in the middle of a title defense, and he had been thrown around like a rag doll, and now someone with a 100-plus lb. weight advantage comes down here and attacks him like this. Get these guys out of here! (As the closing credits roll, Twister, security guards, and Joey Haven come down to break things up, and Hammer and Thomas surrender to greater numbers and hop to the floor, hands raised in victory. Thomas grabs the Television title belt and straps it around Jak Hammer's waist, and Hammer poses. Then he turns and mockingly salutes the Major, seated on the floor with a look of utter incredulity on his face. Hammer removes the belt and drops it on the floor in front of the Major as he and Thomas leave.)
