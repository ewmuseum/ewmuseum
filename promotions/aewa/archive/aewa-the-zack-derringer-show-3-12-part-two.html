The Zack Derringer Show (3/12, part two)

15 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * CONTINUED FROM PART ONE * * * * --------------- (The camera opens on Deathwatch, sitting behind his desk at the ICU building in New York. His hands are folded on top of his desk and he wears a nice gray business suit with a red tie. The office is dimly lit and there is a drink bar over on the right hand side, and a couch on the left. Deathwatch grins at the camera and then looks at the AEWA North American Title that rests atop his desk.... and a separate title that sits next to it. He begins to speak with his casual and sophisticated accent.) Deathwatch: Look what Deathwatch brought home to show all of the world. The MLWO United States Championship. I guess that means that I'm the BEST in North America. I have the North American title and just in case that doesn't prove it, I have the MLWO US title-- isn't life just grand? (Deathwatch grins and pats the titles that sit at the edge of his desk.) Deathwatch: Now I have some matters to go over. I saw what Mr. Furnish said about the match, and I am _so_ glad that he agreed to these terms, because I wouldn't have settled for anything less. So, it looks like I have quite the challenge ahead of me. Nightshade, you are truly an experienced wrestler, but you are merely a light-heavyweight-- do you honestly think you can defeat me? Do you think that just because you have a demon guiding yourpath that you will be led to a victory? I don't think so. I allow you this opportunity to OBTAIN a title shot and I am not allowing you to step through that barrier. You shall remain behind the gates of time until you rot away as all things do in life. You aren't my priority. (Deathwatch taps his fingers on the desk and then shakes his head.) Deathwatch: Justin Trouble, you're up to bat next and I dare you to take the hardest swing you have to offer, that is, as soon as you get out of the hospital from what your "big bad brother" did to you. It's nice to see family reunions, but I wouldn't have wanted to be on the short end of that stick like you were, Trouble. Besides, I don't _have_ a family.... anyone who has done their homework would know that they were (sarcastically) brutally murdered at the hands of a deranged psychopath. (Deathwatch sighs and laughs at this thought.) Deathwatch: Next, we have Primal Fear. Sir, you're always bragging about your one pathetic ACEW title reign, Well, how much does ACEW mean in the long run? That's right, it means NOTHING! The only thing that I even remotely care about is the task ahead. What's done in the past.... is done. Don't relish in that past for too long, Fear. Bad things happen with old memories. (Deathwatch laughs and pauses.) Deathwatch: Super Blonde, I saw how upset you were when you found out that your own cousin was in love with someone like me. (laughs) Someone like me, there's something to think about, Super Blonde. Just who am I? Why do people hate me so? Why do people think of me as the "bad guy", when in actuality.... I'm the one trying to make things right around here. You're just lucky you have a cousin that's smart enough to realize this, and don't worry Blondie, I wouldn't dare try anything with your cousin.... I hardly know her yet. (Deathwatch chuckles and then suddenly gets a cold glare on his face.) Deathwatch: Finally, The Caretaker.... you, my own brother. I never saw why you couldn't just shape up and come with me in ICU. You've always had to be the good guy. You've always had to be the one to "clean up the place." I don't know WHAT you see in that lifestyle, but I can assure you one thing, it doesn't pay as well as working for me. Hell, there would have been a good vice presidency in the corporation, but you HAD to be the good guy. This is the reason why I must dispose of you yet again. You stand in my way and you wish to take me out of action and ruin my corporation. Well, the only man that is going to come out of this match ruined.... shall be you. Good luck, Caretaker, because it will take a lot more than your Knights to get rid of me. (Deathwatch grins and looks back down at his desk, and begins filling out some documents. The camera fades to black) ------------ HT: His chances in the match are extremely good, but what happens if NONE of them can defeat him in the match? Can one man even go THROUGH five men in a row and win? ZD: You bet your ass he can. I don't know what happens if he defeats them all, maybe the last man in it gets the title shot or something. And as much as I respect Deathwatch, I think all hell is going to break loose as soon as The Caretaker hits that ring. Man, is he ever pissed. Let's take a look in this flash from him. HT: He's been silent for quite awhile, so it should be interesting to find out what he has to say. --------------- (The camera opens on The Caretaker, standing in a graveyard at night. He wears a gray vest over a white shirt, black boots, black pants, and a pair of green gloves. His long, white hair hangs to the sides of his head. He has a shovel in his right hand and he stands beside a headstone. A cigarette lies on his lower lip, as he grins to the camera and takes a drag off of it. The Caretaker puts the shovel into the dirt and begins to speak.) The Caretaker: Deathwatch, I see you've decided to allow myself to get my revenge on you. It's really gonna be fun rippin' you up like I could have done before if I had gotten the chance. My days of darkness and ignorance are long since forgotten, but one thing I still owe you from those days.... is revenge. You hit me over the head with my own shovel, you locked me into a casket and tried to kill me, you wanted me out of the picture and you got just that. One thing you didn't expect though, was that I would come back. I'm a LOT harder to get rid of than you might have thought. (He takes a drag off of his cigarette again.) The Caretaker: (talking while the smoke from the cigarette flows out into the night's air) It's nice to see that you think you'll be able to take on five people at a time, but this isn't going to be the case. I hear that we're drawing for positions. I sure hope I get number one, because I want to show the world that I can still take you out anytime, anyplace. You're a sore loser, Deathwatch, and I know that you won't be able to handle it if I'm the first one out and the first one to beat you. You'll probably complain and say it wasn't a fair match, but that's not the issue. (He puts his foot up onto the headstone and takes another puff off his cigarette, then breathes it out.) The Caretaker: I'm gonna give you a Burial Plot, Deathwatch, right here in my personal cemetery. You've cost me a half a year's worth of sleep and I'm not about to let this go unpunished. Deathwatch, when we meet in the ring, it's going to be a war that I never got to finish, but I can promise you.... this time is going to be different. Oh, and congrats on your new MLWO title, enjoy it while it lasts because once I get through with you, you're going to need an excuse to get out of defending it with all those broken bones. (The Caretaker flicks his cigarette onto the ground, grabs his shovel, and then walks off as the camera fades to black.) ------------ HT: Whoa, he looks like he wants at Deathwatch BAD! Who could blame him? He's been brutalized by Deathwatch his entire life, and now finally it's time for him to get his revenge. ZD: Caretaker is just jealous of Deathwatch and everything he has. You mentioned that Deathwatch has brutalized him his entire life, well, it's going to be a repeat of The Caretaker's entire life. Deathwatch will brutalize him once more. HT: That's really tough to say though, because Deathwatch has to go through five guys in one night. I doubt he can do it. ZD: Listen, never doubt Deathwatch 'cause he'll prove you wrong every ti-- *RING* ZD: GOD DAMMIT! Who the hell is that? *RING* ZD: Well? Answer the cocksucker! (Thompson puts it on speakerphone.) HT: Hello? [Man's Voice]: Hello, is this the Zack Derringer Show? ZD: Yeah it is, what the hell do you want? [Man's Voice]: Well, I'm Doctor Skidmore, Legacy's doctor. ZD: Oh! Why didn't you say so? How is he? Will he be able to return any time soon? [Dr. Skidmore's Voice]: Well, he has a severe spinal injury and I'm afraid that he's paralyzed from the waist down. He has trouble talking, as well. ZD: He doesn't talk, Mr. Skidmore. His throat was slit when he was a child, damaging his voice box. [Dr. Skidmore's Voice]: Oh, well that explains that. Now, I'm sorry to tell you this, but Legacy will not be able to return to wrestling.... at least until we can get his spine fixed. He will be undergoing some surgery routines tomorrow and then we will have to wait a few months before we can go into more extensive surgery. Until we can find the exact problem and get in there to fix it, we can't help this young man. HT: When is the soonest that he will be able to return? [Dr. Skidmore's Voice]: Well, that is very hard to say. The severe damage in his lower back could either be something easy like a slipped disk pushing up against a nerve or it could be something like a broken nerve which would make it impossible for him to ever return. We are hoping it's the first option, because broken nerves are almost impossible to fix. ZD: Well, thank you, Doctor. Keep us posted on his condition, will you? [Dr. Skidmore's Voice]: Of course, I'll be giving you another call in a couple of weeks to tell you how the surgery went, but right now I have another patient to get to with a bullet in his head, so I gotta run. *CLICK* HT: Well, hopefully Legacy can return sometime soon, but right now it isn't looking very clear. ZD: Always nice to get calls like that though, just so long as it isn't those stupid Whore-Men. Now let's take a look at the promo for the newest tag team in AEWA.... hit it. ---------------------- (In a darkened ring, a tag team match is going on. One team is clearly dominating. We cannot recognize the team at this point, but from the shadows, we can see that one man is a large, powerfully-built wrestler, and the other is shorter, with a more compact build. The larger wrestler is seen leveling his opponent with a clothesline as the voice-over begins.) Voice: When two men focus on a common goal, nothing is impossible. The whole surpasses the sum of its parts. Divergent skills coalesce into a unified, unstoppable machine. (The large man hits an atomic drop on his opponent, who staggers into a drop toe hold from the smaller man, who spins around into a hammerlock, then into a front facelock, while the larger wrestler drops an elbow onto the opponent's back.) What can be done in the face of such a force? Can anyone, no matter how ruthless, no matter how tough, stand in the way of two men acting in concert? Ask yourself, what would I do if I were facing this combined unit? (The other opponent charges into the ring, but is quickly scooped up by the larger man. The smaller man stuns his opponent with a belly to back suplex. The larger man connects with a running powerslam, just as the smaller man turns his opponent over, locking in an STF.) Graphic Violence.... you're about to find out. (fade to black) -------------- ZD: Interesting. HT: They seem good enough, but the question is-- can they handle themselves well enough in a federation like the AEWA? ZD: We'll just have to wait and see, not to mention the fact that all these new ICU hopefuls are coming in. Speaking of newcomers, let's take a look at Winter Freeze, who has yet to make any kind of impact here in AEWA, but he wants to clean it up.... What a dumbass. -------------- (The camera opens up in a meat locker. In the background, Winter Freeze is seen pounding on large slabs of meat. Mother Nature is standing in the forefront, obviously feeling no effects from the cold.) Mother Nature: Thank you for taking the time to listen to us. I will be as brief as possible, for I must get back to training Winter. We all watched the carnage that passed as wrestling at Kickoff '97. We all saw how time and time again, the rules were blatantly violated, with foreign objects, illegal double teams, and obvious interference. We had stated that we would make our presence known, but unfortunately, we were not given permission by the AEWA to assist in rules enforcement. So Winter and I have taken it upon ourselves to begin cleaning up the AEWA. The Knights of Deliverance stated a similar goal, and we would be happy to assist them. Our first task was to create a list of wrestlers who decided not to follow the rules at Kickoff '97. That list is a lot longer that I would have liked and our job now is to shorten it. But Winter is only one man, so he must take them down one at a time. The first person we have chosen is The Twilight Strangler. Ben Slocum is a fine wrestler, and you assaulted him before your match could even begin. This is unacceptable to us, so it is you, Twilight Strangler, who will feel the cold force of Winter. Anytime, any place, you will become the first person to be eliminated. (The camera fades to black.) --------- ZD: Like I said, he's a moron. Winter Boy, you wanna clean up the AEWA? Grab a broom and a dust pan, you can be the janitor. HT: You have to admit though, he seems to be extremely strong. He wrestles with POLAR BEARS, for cryin' out loud! ZD: I don't have to admit anything until he proves he has what it takes. HT: Well, let's just go to the next flash from the West Coast Connection. ZD: MORE losers!? -------------- (The camera opens on three rather large men, leaning on a fifteen foot tall brick wall. The camera pans back to show they are leaning on the outside of a brick building, with a large black sign on the top proclaiming "KWCC- The Home Of The West Coast Connection" in large purple letters. The three men go inside the building, via a glass door, with the letters KWCC on it in black. The camera follows them into the building.) Hood Lum: Hey, guys, it looks like the camera guy isn't gonna go away. You better come here and say something. (The two of them walk over to the camera, and they both look mad.) MC Read: Something. Axl Sharkman: Okay MC, calm down. Anyway, Machines, we had the two of you beat, and you are lucky the ref stopped the fight. That little kid Sam, or whatever his name was, he was right. We kicked your asses. We wont stop until we get another shot at you, whether it be for the belts or not. MC Read: Okay, now I got something to say. It seems that Kevin Larkin is no longer able to be part of the AEWA administrative board. Well, we don't like that. Kevin has dome much for the WCC, and we don't want him to leave. So, I just thought up a good idea. We invite Kevin to become the manager of the West Coast Connection. Axl Sharkman: Wow!! Good idea, MC! I also invite Kevin to be our manager. What about you, Hood? Hood Lum: Well, I haven't really dealt with him much, but you two seem to like him, so what the hell, why not. MC Read: Freeman, if you try to deny us this opportunity if he accepts, we will still find a way to make it so. If he accepts, then Kevin Larkin shall be the manager of the WCC, and you cant do anything about it. Axl: Now if you excuse us, we have a radio show to do. (The three men move to the inside of the studio and sit down at their stations. Music fills the studio as they go on air.) --------------- HT: They've always been great here in the AEWA, but lately they've been going fairly unnoticed. Maybe they can pick things up soon. ZD: I'll give 'em credit, they haven't been whining like babies or anything. HT: So, who's next? ZD: Well, we now have a flash from Paul Smith. I'm not sure what I think of this guy yet. Roll it.... ---------------- (Paul Smith is standing in a ring in an empty arena; which is dimly lit. Smith is still wearing a neck brace from neck injuries sustained after getting whacked in the neck by a cue stick at Kickoff '97.) Paul Smith: Jeff Hawkins, I see you couldn't go anywhere without 8-Ball. I had you racked, which means I had you beat. If it wasn't for that G-D bodyguard of yours, I would have won that match. SO, if you're not going to do it voluntarily, I'm challenging you to a steel cage death match; with Klash and 8-Ball banned from ringside. Steel cages are so wonderful. 15 feet high, chain link fence, and a top so nobody can interfere. (A steel cage is lowered down onto the ring.) Smith: Hawkins, after the cage is lowered onto the ring, there'll be no saving you, and there'll be no escape. It'll be one on one, you and me. I'm going to show the AEWA that I belong here by whooping your sorry little tail. What do you say, are you man enough for the fight or are you going to run away with your tail tucked between your legs? (fade to black) -------------- ZD: This guy is NOTHING material. I couldn't see any stable wanting this piece of trash and personally, I think Hawkins is going to kill him AGAIN. HT: I think he's a decent wrestler, but I agree.... Hawkins has the upper hand and I don't see him losing. ZD: Well, next up is a flash from The Assassins. --------------- (The Assassins, Banshee and Shockwave, are standing in the locker room. They both look angry, and have icepacks on various parts of their bodies.) Banshee: Simon O'Neal, you little punk, you almost cost us our belts lst week! You caused Shockwave here to get a concussion from that chairshot. You caused me to get one of the worst beatings of my life from two former AEWA World Heavyweight Champions! The only thing is, no matter what you caused to happen to us, WE WERE NOT BEAT! Shockwave: O'Neal, you proposed a little deal with us! We keep the other Knights away, and you let the referee search you before the match. Well, as long as the referee is a normal AEWA referee, we accept your deal! Banshee: But don't think that we won't be on our guard. Your own partner said to you that you were up to something. We will both be prepared for anything you can bring to us. Shockwave: Machines, we still say that you do not deserve those belts! Simon, you said that Paul is not a cheater, but he really is if he lets YOU cheat. Many of your losses are tainted ones, and your "victory" against us was from the help of a chain wrapped fist. Banshee: Machines, at the next pay-per-view, you two are going to lose those belts, and we will unify the titles! (fade to black) --------------- HT: The Assassins seem to be VERY angry with Simon O'Neal over that incident from last week, don't they? ZD: They're all talk, no action. Those guys might be the National champs, but they're an embarrassment to this federation. HT: So what's next? ZD: We've got an interview with the best wrestler in the world today, the reigning Exodus Heavyweight Champion, here is-- HT: You really don't need to introduce him, Zack, we all know who it is.
