Wednesday Night Rumble (11/6)

7 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power Bomb, and a look at Mister Crowley DRIVING the head of some guy into the concrete with the Premature Burial! Then the camera switches to the Lovely Bertha, Matt Wilson, and "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt in a packed arena) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Wednesday Night Rumble! We have plenty of GREAT matches scheduled for tonight, so get ready for a GIGANTIC night of action! In the main event, Rick Showboat will be defending his Light Heavyweight championship against Joey Valachi! BD: That should be a great match, folks. Valachi is going to DOMINATE, and he'll be going home with the title. LB: Think again, Big Daddy. MW: Okay guys, please don't start arguing again; I'm REALLY not in the mood for that. Let's just get ready for our next match, as the Machines put their National tag team titles on the line against Graphic Violence! BD: Those are two EXCELLENT teams, so I'm really looking forward to this bout. One thing's for sure-- anything can happen. LB: Let's get down to ringside.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA National tag team championship! Introducing first, the challengers, at a combined weight of 520 pounds, here are Booze Murphy and Leech Rogers, GRAPHIC VIOLENCE! (Murphy and Rogers make their way toward the ring to a chorus of boos from the crowd. Their manager, Miss DeMeanor, follows closely behind.) LB: These boys are gonna become the National tag team champions tonight! MW: What makes you so sure of that? LB: I've just got a good feeling about this one. MW: I wonder if that good feeling has anything to do with the fact that Rick Showboat's match with Joey Valachi is after this matchup. LB: Could be. RING ANNOUNCER: And their opponents, at a combined weight of 514 pounds, here are the National tag team champions, THE MACHINES! (Simon O'Neal and Paul Wong make their entrance, and they get a mixed reaction from the fans. Simon looks particularly angry tonight.) BD: What crawled up O'Neal's shorts and died? MW: He does look pretty angry tonight. I wonder if he and Wong had another disagreement backstage before the match. BD: Why can't these two guys get along? MW: I don't know. Ask them. The Machines now are in the ring, and they're deciding which of them should start the match. LB: Maybe they'll get in a fight right now. MW: No, Wong decides to let O'Neal starts the match. Murphy starts for Graphic Violence. They lock up. Murphy shocks O'Neal with a KNEE to the midsection! BD: I hope he didn't have too much for dinner. MW: He looks like he's fine. Now Murphy's HAMMERING O'Neal with a series of double axehandles to the back! Murphy whips O'Neal to the ropes, and nails him with a BIG BACKDROP! LB: Looks like we're going out to party with the new National tag team champions, Graphic Violence tonight! MW: Don't be too sure of that. Murphy picks up O'Neal and sets him up for the DEATH DROP! Can he do it? LB: You don't think so, huh? Watch this. MW: I'm watching. Murphy gets him up, but O'Neal slips out and INTO A BACKSLIDE! One...Two...Kickout! MW: The Machines almost got away with their titles there, but not yet. BD: Not yet? I don't think these guys are gonna leave under there own power. Graphic Violence is going to send them out of here on stretchers. MW: I seriously doubt that. O'Neal is trying to make the tag. Murphy's trying to stop him---but he CAN'T! O'Neal MAKES THE TAG to Wong! LB: Uh oh! BD: Look at Wong go in there! He's DECKING both members of Graphic Violence with BIG right hands! This is just wrong. MW: It's not wrong for The Machines. Wong whips Murphy into the ropes and hits him with a BIG clothesline! And now a DROPKICK from Wong to Rogers! Rogers is knocked out of the ring! LB: I don't believe this guy! Now he's whipping Murphy into the ropes and KICKS him in the midsection! OUCH! Wong's going for a DDT---but Murphy REVERSES IT INTO A NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX! One...Two...Kickout! BD: That was a great move! Graphic Violence deserves to win this match tonight. MW: If they deserve to win, they've got to pin The Machines in the ring or make them submit. BD: Don't' worry, they will. MW: Now back in the ring, Murphy tags out to Rogers. Murphy's holding Wong's head neck-first over the middle rope. Rogers comes off from the ropes, and COMES DOWN across the neck of Wong! LB: I wouldn't be surprised if Wong ends up sounding like Brian Pillman now. MW: Could be. Rogers now setting up Wong---he DELIVERS a vertical suplex! Wong is holding his back after that one, and Rogers LAYS in some VICIOUS KICKS into the aching back of Wong! BD: Those kicks seem to be taking their toll. Rogers is now going for a bodyslam on Wong, but Wong grabs his leg and ROLLS HIM UP! One...Two...Kickout LB: That was a close one. MW: The Machines almost got away with their National tag team championship. Wong picks up Rogers and WHIPS him into the ropes! Rogers comes off and gets hit with a BACK ELBOW! And Wong MAKES THE TAG! BD: Simon O'Neal is in there now. Hopefully he'll get beat up as bad as his tag team partner did. MW: I doubt that. O'Neal is LAYING big right hands onto the side of the heads of Booze Murphy and Leech Rogers! LB: (yelling) C'mon, Violence! Fight back! MW: Settle down, Bertha. O'Neal now seems to be setting up Murphy for a PILEDRIVER---but Murphy just nailed him in the crotch! What a vicious move! BD: I guess he'll have a higher voice now for a while. MW: That was a cheap way to avoid getting beat. LB: Win if you can, lose if you must, but always cheat. That's my motto. Anyway, I don't have to explain myself to you, Wilson. Let's get back to the action. MW: O'Neal is still doubled over in pain. Murphy's going over for the cover. He's covering him---but THE REF'S TIED UP WITH WONG! We could have NEW National tag team champions, but there's NO COUNT! BD: Come on, you stupid referee! Get over and make a count! LB: Now Murphy gives up his pin, he's going over to talk to the ref! Murphy just SHOVED Wong! The ref's trying to HOLD BACK WONG! MW: Murphy's going back over to O'Neal now. O'Neal seems to be moving.... Murphy's picking up O'Neal for some kind of slam---O'NEAL JUST POPPED MURPHY WITH A HUGE RIGHT HAND! BD: He HAD to have something in HIS HAND! LB: HE DID! HE HAD BRASS KNUCKLES! He's putting them back into his tights now! The ref can't see them! MW: Here's the cover! One...Two...Three! (DING DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Here are you winners, and STILL AEWA NATIONAL TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS, THE MACHINES! BD: What a way to defend their titles---I LOVE IT! But why did they have to do it against Graphic Violence? MW: They didn't want to lose those titles to anyone, even Graphic Violence. That's why. LB: Well, I'm sure we haven't heard the last from Graphic Violence on their challenge to the National tag team titles. But now I CAN'T WAIT for what's next! Rick Showboat is gonna defend his Light Heavyweight title against Joey Valachi! MW: You just can't wait to see Showboat. LB: I just want to see a talented wrestler defend his title against another talented wrestler. That's all. BD: (sarcastically) Yeah right. What does this punk Showboat have that I don't have? LB: For one, the Light Heavyweight title. And for two, charm, good looks, a nice smile.... BD: That's more than two. LB: Sorry, I guess I just got carried away. MW: I hate to break up this stimulating conversation, but we've got a match to get done here. Let's go up to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, this contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA Light Heavyweight championship of the World. Introducing first, the challenger, he weighs in at 202 pounds, and is a FOUR-TIME former AEWA Light Heavyweight champion of the World, here is "THE CARDIAC KID" JOEY VALACHI! ("The Heartbreaker" starts to play as Valachi makes his entrance to a LOUD chorus of boos. He has an arrogant smirk on his face.) BD: These fans certainly don't like Valachi.... BUT I DO! Come on, Valachi, knock some teeth out of the mouth of that punk Showboat! LB: He's not a punk! BD: Yes he is! LB: No he's not! MW: KIDS! Can we just concentrate on the action, please? RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 226 pounds, and accompanied to the ring by his manager Doctor Love, the AEWA Light Heavyweight champion of the World, here is RICK SHOWBOAT! (Showboat makes his way to the ring wearing his blue robe. He has the Light Heavyweight title wrapped around his waist. Doctor Love, wearing an expensive tuxedo, is next to him.) MW: Showboat looks pretty intense and seems to be ready to defend his title here tonight against the former FOUR-TIME Light Heavyweight champion, "The Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi. LB: Of course, he's gonna successfully defend it. He's the best champion to hold that belt in a long time! BD: I'm getting a little sick and tired of watching this punk Showboat. He came back into the AEWA and got an immediate at the Light Heavyweight title. Was that fair? The Holy Ghost had no time to prepare-- that's why he lost the title. He'd have whooped Showboat's ass if he had had time to prepare. MW: Well, I don't know about that, but Showboat is the Light Heavyweight champion of the world and he won it in the middle of the ring. Fair and square. LB: Okay, let's get down to business. Showboat's in the ring and he's taking off his robe---what a physique! MW: While Bertha wipes the drool off of her chin, I'll take over. Both men lock up. Showboat backs Valachi into the ropes, and gives a clean break! BD: See, that's how stupid this Showboat guy is. He should have dropped Valachi with a punch right there. LB: Rick just has class. MW: (to himself) Why do I feel like I'm in divorce court? BD: What? MW: Nevermind. Both men lock up again, and Valachi puts on an armbar! But Showboat REVERSES it, and then delivers a BACK KICK to Valachi! LB: Great move, Rick! BD: C'mon Joey! MW: Apparently I'll be handling all of the play-by-play while my two colleagues act as cheerleaders. Showboat now delivers a THRUST to the throat of Valachi! BD: Joey's better than this. Joey, kick him below the belt! LB: (to Big Daddy) Stay out of this! (to ring) Come on, Rick! MW: (looking confused) Talk about a double standard. Showboat now WHIPS Valachi into the corner! Showboat goes in after him, but Valachi puts up a BOOT TO THE FACE! BD: That's my man Joey! MW: Valachi is going to the top turnbuckle. Valachi comes off the top---but Showboat puts up HIS FEET TO BLOCK IT! Valachi was READY for it though! He blocks the feet and ROLLS Showboat over. He puts Showboat into an armbar! Valachi now goes for a SHORT CLOTHESLINE---but Showboat DUCKS, and puts Valachi in a GERMAN SUPLEX! One...Two...Kickout! LB: (to Big Daddy) Your boy Valachi almost got it there. BD: Don't worry, he'll come back. MW: Showboat picks Valachi up and whips him to the ropes! Showboat goes for a chop, but Valachi HOLDS ON to the ropes! LB: Rick would have dropped him with that chop. BD: Valachi's gonna be a five-time light heavyweight champion before this match is over. MW: We'll have to see. Showboat's going into the corner after Valachi, but Valachi nails him with an EYERAKE! Now Valachi connects with a BIG RIGHT HAND to Showboat! Valachi whips Showboat to the ropes and DROPKICKS him in the knee! LB: Rick doesn't look too good here, but I'm banking on him. BD: Banking on him? Does that mean you're going to tell everyone about your gambling problem? LB: Gambling problem? I don't have a gambling problem. What are you talking about? MW: Back in the ring.... Valachi seems to be in control now. What's he doing now? He's signaling for the SCORPION DEATHLOCK! That's SHOWBOAT'S finisher! BD: Alright, Valachi's gonna rub it in Showboat's face. I love it. LB: Somebody's gotta do something. He's doesn't need to be showing off in there. BD: Will you give it a rest? If it was anybody else but Showboat, you'd be cheering for Valachi. MW: People, I'd like to call the action if you don't mind. Valachi's going for the Scorpion---and he GOT IT! Valachi's got Showboat's own finisher ON HIM! BD: That's exactly what Valachi's gonna do---finish him. MW: Valachi has a big smile on his face, but Showboat's REVERSING IT! LOOK AT THIS! LB: Go Rick! Reverse it on the little twerp! MW: Valachi's trying to hang on---but SHOWBOAT DID IT! He REVERSED the SCORPION DEATHLOCK! This is his patented "Love Lock"! The ref's asking Valachi if he's gonna give it up! BD: Hang in there, Joey! MW: And Valachi... is done! He just submitted! (DING DING) Valachi's given this one up! RING ANNOUNCER: Your winner, and STILL AEWA LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION OF THE WORLD, RICK SHOWBOAT! MW: Showboat in the ring celebrating with Doctor Love now.... BD: I've had enough of this crap! (throws his headset down) MW: WAIT A MINUTE! "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt's GETTING IN THE RING! He's ATTACKING SHOWBOAT! LB: What the hell is he doing? MW: Hurt and Showboat are REALLY going at it! They're pummeling each other! LB: (on the verge of tears) MIKE, DON'T DO THIS! MW: This is absolutely insane, Bertha! Hurt is just HAMMERING into Showboat with a series of punches, and he won't stop! LB: Can't we get any CONTROL in there? MW: Here comes the security! They're HANDCUFFING "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt and taking him away! LB: I'm really shocked by this whole incident.... What was he THINKING? Rick Showboat didn't do ANYTHING wrong, and Mike just completely lost it. MW: Unfortunately, he's going to be heavily fined for that attack. A commentator can't just attack a wrestler like that-- we have a JOB to do here in the broadcast booth. LB: I know.... MW: Well, we have a lot left to talk about, but this show's almost over. Right now wrestling fans, we're out of time. So this is Matt Wilson, along the Lovely Bertha, saying goodnight!
