Wednesday Night Rumble (11/20, part two)

22 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ CONTINUED FROM PART ONE ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ MW: Big Ron Raider is defending his TV title against another recently returned former World Champion, The Caretaker. LB: Why is this guy even allowed back here? Deathwatch whooped his butt and pinned him in the center of the ring in a "retirement match". Doesn't that mean anything? MW: Ever hear of coming out of retirement? LB: Yeah, but not after only a few months. MW: In the world of wrestling, stranger things have happened. LB: I guess you're right, but that doesn't mean I have to like it. MW: No one ever said you did, but this return has been HIGHLY anticipated, and personally I can't wait to see him back in that ring again. So let's NOT wait, let's go to the ring! RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA North American Title! Introducing first, the challenger, he weighs in at 328 pounds and is a former AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, The Caaaaaaaaarrrretakeeeeeeer! (A sound like a huge EXPLOSION comes out from the speakers throughout the arena, as the Caretaker appears from behind the curtain. He is wearing black tights, a black hat, a green vest, and smoking a cigar. The crowd POPS wildly.) LB: Boy, the Caretaker sure has changed since the last time we saw him. MW: He has indeed. He's like a completely changed man, and for the first time, he truly looks happy doing what he's doing. LB: He won't look so happy after Raider smears the mat with him. MW: I don't think that'll happen. LB: Sure it will, and just in case it doesn't, Raider's got the Church to back him up. MW: Ah, but Caretaker is a member of the newly formed Knights of Deliverance. If the Church tries anything, you can bet they'll be here. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from the Rocky Mountains, he weighs in at 416 pounds and accompanied by his manger, Million Dollar Mark, he is the AEWA North American Champion, Big... Ron... Raaaaaaiiiideeeeeer! (Lynyrd Skynyrd's "Saturday Night Special" begins playing loudly as Raider makes his way to ringside, with the belt draped over his shoulder. The crowd boos loudly. Million Dollar Mark is at his side.) LB: And Raider doesn't look phased in the least by the Caretaker. He has the same look in his eyes as always, and he is ready to DESTROY! MW: Well don't forget what happened the last time these two met. Caretaker defended his World Title, successfully, against Raider several months ago-- back when he was still champion. If Raider wants to win this time, he REALLY has to be ready. LB: Don't worry, he is. MW: We're about to find out, as Raider just stepped into the ring! The two men just stare into each other's eyes, and it's OBVIOUS that they don't like each other. And Caretaker opens the action with a chop! LB: What a wimpy opening move. He will be destroyed shortly. MW: Don't count on it. Caretaker, still on offense, takes Raider down with a football tackle! Raider is quick to get back up, but Caretaker takes him down AGAIN with a legsweep! LB: Boy, I've NEVER seen Caretaker move this FAST before! MW: He's like a whole different wrestler. Raider is up now, and Caretaker NAILS him with another chop! He picks up the champion now, BACK SUPLEX--BUT RAIDER COUNTERS WITH A CROSS-BODY BLOCK!! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Look at how fast he turned that one around! MW: That's why he's the champ. Raider picks him up off the canvas now, and NAILS a PERFECT belly-to-belly suplex! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Raider's not gonna let up now. MW: And he'd BETTER not if he plans on winning. Raider picks Caretaker up again, and TAKES HIS HEAD OFF with a short lariat! The cover! One..Two..Thr..Kickout! LB: Just look at this! It's almost over! MW: Don't count the Caretaker out yet, he's been here before. Raider helps the Caretaker to his feet again, and whips him to the ropes! He comes back, LARIAT--DUCKED BY THE CARETAKER!! Caretaker comes back off the opposite ropes, and FLOORS Raider with a jumping elbow thrust! LB: Wow! I didn't think he could get up that high. MW: Neither did I, to tell you the truth, but he did it, and now Raider's in trouble! Raider is up now, and Caretaker whips him to the ropes! He comes back, AND GETS CAUGHT WITH A BRUTAL POWERSLAM!!! The cover! One..Two..Foot on the Ropes! LB: Raider always knows where he is in there. MW: And Caretaker's trying to remedy that problem. Caretaker picks him up off the canvas, REVERSE NECKBREAKER! That had to hurt! And he picks Raider up AGAIN, and whips him to the ropes! Raider comes back, AND GETS CAUGHT IN ANOTHER POWERSLAM!!! BUT NO COVER! LB: That's a big mistake. MW: It very well could be, but Caretaker is continuing the assault. Raider slowly gets back up, but is taken back DOWN with a legsweep! Caretaker picks Raider up now, and grabs him around the waist, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX!!! And NOW he covers! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: It'll take more than that to put down this monster. MW: I guess SO. Caretaker is visibly upset, but he goes back to work. He picks Raider up now, BELLY-TO-BACK SUPLEX--BLOCKED BY RAIDER! Caretaker tries to re-adjust, but Raider grabs him by the arm, and LEVELS him with a short clothesline! LB: That's it! Now it's Raider Time! MW: We'll see. Raider picks up the Caretaker now, and LEVELS him with a short lariat! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: It won't be long now. MW: That's what you said a while ago. LB: But this time, I mean it. MW: I see. Raider picks the Caretaker up now, and he's going for a sleeper! He's got it in, but Caretaker reaches his leg back, AND KICKS RAIDER IN THE GROIN!!! THAT made him let go in a hurry! LB: Too bad it's ILLEGAL! MW: Like Raider wouldn't have done it if he had the chance. And now Raider is DOUBLED OVER in pain! Caretaker takes quick advantage, and picks up the champion, AND NAILS HIM WITH THE BURIAL PLOT PILEDRIVER!! Where did THAT come from?? The cover! One..Two..Thr..Kickout! LB: HA! Raider kicked out of the Burial Plot! He's INVINCIBLE! MW: I wouldn't say THAT, but he's pretty damn tough. Even Caretaker can't believe it! But he goes back to work, he picks up Raider, and NAILS him with a belly-to-belly suplex! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: You've got him right where you want him now, Raider. MW: Somehow I fail to see what you're basing that on. Caretaker is trying to put Raider on the top turnbuckle, but Raider won't have any part of it. He FINALLY breaks free, and Caretaker charges at him! Raider catches him, and lifts him up, GORILLA PRESS--BUT CARETAKER COUNTERS WITH A ROLLING REVERSE CRADLE!!!! One..Two..Kickout! LB: (yelling to the ring) You can't let him do that to you-- you're Raider! MW: I think he knows that. Raider tries to get back up, but Caretaker catches him with a kneelift! Caretaker picks him up off the mat now, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX!! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: Caretaker's really taking it to the champ, but Raider won't go down! MW: No he won't, and you've got to give him credit for that. Caretaker picks up Raider now, and tosses him out of the ring! What's Caretaker doing now? He runs towards the ropes on that side of the ring, AND DIVES OUT WITH A PLANCHA--BUT RAIDER MOVES!!! And Caretaker hits nothing but guardrail and concrete! LB: That's what he gets for trying to be Rey Misterio or somebody. MW: And now Raider is firmly in control! He picks up the Caretaker, and whips him to the ringpost! Caretaker is in La-La Land! Raider pulls him back, and now throws him into the ringsteps! LB: That's it! BREAK HIM! MW: Your level of tolerance for brutality never ceases to amaze me. LB: Thank you. MW: Raider picks the Caretaker up now, and LEVELS him with a short clothesline! Caretaker is in trouble! Raider rolls him back into the ring now, and he's going for the cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: What??? He should be dead by now! MW: Well he's not, and Raider is as upset as you are. He picks the Caretaker up now, and whips him to the turnbuckle! Raider follows him in for an avalanche, BUT GETS CAUGHT WITH A BIG BOOT TO THE FACE!!! LB: Raider's stunned! MW: He sure is! And Caretaker takes advantage, and NAILS Raider with a chop! Caretaker now KICKS Raider hard to the thigh, and is picking him up! He's got him in position now, and PLANTS HIM WITH ANOTHER BURIAL PLOT PILEDRIVER!! THE COVER! One..Two..And Million Dollar Mark puts Raider's foot on the ropes! LB: HA!! I knew he'd get out of it! He got his leg on the ropes again! MW: Only because Mark PUT Raider's foot on the ropes! LB: When?? I didn't see that. MW: You NEVER see that. But Caretaker did, and he's UPSET! He's really giving it to Mark, who's acting like he did nothing wrong! LB: He didn't. MW: Oh yes he did, and Caretaker looks about ready to come out after him.... BUT RAIDER ROLLS CARETAKER UP FROM BEHIND, AND HE'S PULLING THE TIGHTS!! One..Two..Three!!!! (DING, DING) LB: That's what the big dummy deserves for paying more attention to Mark than his opponent! I love it! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of nine minutes and fifty-six seconds, here is your winner, and STILL AEWA North American Champion... BIG RON RAIDER!!!!!!! MW: Now Raider and Mark are bailing out of there so fast that you'd think the cops were after them, and after robbery like that, I wouldn't doubt it. But it's not the cops they're running from, it's the Caretaker, who looks like he's ready to KILL someone! LB: He's just mad that he lost. He's such a sore loser. MW: No, he has every reason to be upset. In the purest sense of the word, he was RIPPED OFF. Caretaker is RUNNING back to the locker room to get at Raider, but there's someone in his way! LB: Oh, this is gonna be good! It's his BROTHER, the man who retired him from wrestling, the HEAD of International Contracts Unlimited, There's only one man who fits this description, and it's DEATHWATCH! MW: And we've got quite a confrontation out there! The two men stand face to face, looking straight into each other's eyes, and it's obvious that NEITHER man likes what he sees in them! AND DEATHWATCH SLAPS THE CARETAKER ACROSS THE FACE!!! What an INSULT! LB: That was GREAT! I can't WAIT to see Deathwatch kick his brother's ass again! MW: Caretaker turns his head back to face his brother, and LEVELS HIM WITH A CLOTHESLINE!! Now THAT'S what I call retaliation! Caretaker picks Deathwatch up now, GORILLA PRESS!! And now he just DROPS him neck first across the steel guard rail! LB: This isn't fair! Get security out here! MW: That's not what you were saying a minute ago. And now Caretaker calls to one of the fans, WHO HANDS HIM A CHAIR!!! DEATHWATCH is slowly getting back up to his feet, and the Caretaker winds up, and just PULVERIZES him with a HUGE chair shot! LB: Somebody have this man arrested! MW: Calm down, Bertha. It's about time somebody showed Deathwatch that he just can't walk in here and take over! And now The Caretaker picks up Deathwatch, AND EXECUTES THE BURIAL PLOT PILEDRIVER ON THE CONCRETE!! LB: He could've cracked his skull open! MW: Yes he could have, but this crowd LOVES it! Deathwatch is FINALLY getting a dose of his own medicine! And now Caretaker just SPITS onto his brother, and raises his arms as the crowd EXPLODES!! (Crowd: "He's Hardcore, He's Hardcore.....") LB: I can't believe the things some people will cheer for. MW: You would, if this was the other way around. And now the Caretaker is walking back to the locker room to a standing ovation, and we've got some paramedics out here to take away Deathwatch. LB: What a disgusting scene. Just tell me what's next, and hopefully I'll get over it. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ TO BE CONTINUED IN PART THREE ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- American E-Wrestling Association The AEWA-- We Deliver!
