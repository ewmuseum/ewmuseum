AEWA — Card Archive

CWAA Sept 7th part3

8 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Tonight, after Championship Wrestling, the team saves a girl from a killer cult- but then the cult targets BA and Dirk and the rest- a deadly game of cat-and-mouse on “The A-Team”, right after Championship Wrestling) EL: OK- we’re back, and ready for our special feature match- the first-ever meeting between Max Robertson and Crimson Joe Reed! Let’s not waste any time, but go right to the ring! DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next bout is set for one fall with television time remaining, and is for the AEWA World Heavyweight Championship! (Pop) DM: Introducing first, the challenger- (Darth Vader’s usual “Imperial March” plays and the arena resonates with boos) DM: -accompanied to the ring by Lt. Lawson. Sgt. Sherrick, and Major Mayhem, here is the former CWAA Heavyweight Champion, the current CWAA Television Champion- the Professional, Max Robertson! (Pause) EL: Max Robertson, known by many as the Professional- I remember another championship wrestler in California called the Professional- not the same man- this was 20 years ago, and he wore a mask. What was that name, again? DM: And his opponent- (Enormous pop as “Bad Company” plays) DM: -accompanied by his manager, the Lady in Red, here is the AEWA World Heavyweight Champion, Crimson Joe Reed! EL: And there he is, the certain hall-of-famer, the legend, one of the all-time greats, Crimson Joe Reed! DM: Your referee, Jeff Howard. EL: OK- Max Robertson has his reinforcements out an in place as he gets ready for the biggest match of his life. It was, what, four months ago, that- Doug Gilbert- that’s who I was thinking of. There’s the bell, and we’re underway. Four months ago we saw Max take on then-World champion Heavy Metal, but the match was originally signed to be Reed-Robertson. Collar and elbow tie-up, Max to a standing wristlock. Two very technically sound athletes in the ring tonight, a real marquee matchup for fans of scientific wrestling. Max with a standing wristlock still- Reed turns the arm over- reverses the hold. (Pop) EL: And Max slips behind him now into a hammerlock. Has that arm pinned behind his back, and Reed to the ropes. Reed with a definite height and power advantage in this one. Jeff Howard calls for the break- and he gets it. Lock up- Max to a fireman’s carry takedown, headlock. Max and Reed down on the mat, Robertson with the headlock. Reed up to his feet, but Max still with the headlock on. Reed around behind him now- waistlock takedown. Max scrambling as Reed goes for the chinlock, but Max to the ropes- Jeff Howard calls for the break- and Reed backs off. The Major arguing that Reed pulled the hair- please! (Max is bald, for those of you who were not aware) EL: I still don’t see why someone who takes things as seriously as Robertson associates with the Major- we’ve seen Max team with and then distance himself from McNultey and Anderson when he decided they were getting too silly for his tastes. Lock up again- and Reed with the hip toss. (Pop) EL: Reed hip tosses him over again- now he sends him for the ride- back body drop! (Pop) EL: Now he sets him up- vertical suplex! Reed covers- 1, kickout. Reed now to a headscissor submission hold. Really squeezing those legs. (Lawson and a still-not-all-there Sherrick pound the ring apron to rally Max.) EL: Crimson Joe Reed with the leg scissors applied- Max trying to get free. (You know how- he rolls over onto his knees, his head still stuck between Reed’s legs, and, still trapped, gets his feet beneath him, until he can slide his head out) EL: Max escapes, double leg cradle- 1, 2, the champ kicks out. Even matchup so far, neither man able to get a sustained advantage. Collar and elbow tie up- Reed to an arm bar, twists the arm again- Max in real pain. Reed really applying the pressure, and Max reaches for the ropes- Jeff Howard with the 5 count- and Reed breaks. Look out! Max a little frustrated, and he gives Reed a shove in the chest- and Reed shoves him back! (Pop as Max is knocked into the corner. Reed stands in mid-ring with his fists up.) EL: OK- Reed is letting Max know he’s ready- if Max wants to mat wrestle or if he wants a fight, the champ is ready for anything. (Max, in the corner, leans his head and torso out between the middle and top rope for safety and asks Howard to warn Reed about the closed fists. Howard does.) EL: OK- Max back in the middle of the ring- they lock up- and Max with a handful of hair pulls Reed back to the ropes- Jeff Howard calls for a break- Max backs up- no! Kick on the midsection stuns the champ! (Boos) EL: Max with a chop now! Sends him for the ride- clothesline by the TV champ, as he goes for the fabled 10 pounds of gold! Max off the ropes- elbow drop- hooks the leg- 1, kickout. Max now to an abdominal stretch, but he can’t get the leg grapevined. The hold can still hurt, but he can’t get it applied 100% correctly. He’s got a handful of tights, though, for the leverage advantage. Reed struggling to reach the ropes, but he’s at least 6 inches away as he stretches his hand out. (“Go, Joe, Go!”, as Sherrick and Lawson and the Major try desperately to shut the crowd up) EL: Max Robertson has Joe Reed in that abdominal stretch, the crowd trying to rally the World Champion- and he hip tosses the Professional over! (Pop) EL: Max up again and charges Reed- scoop- and the champ slams Max! Covers- 1, kickout! Sends Max for the ride, back drop- no! Max leapfrogs- rolls him up- (Pushes Reed forward to the ropes from behind, then rolls back as they bounce backwards) EL: 1, 2, kickout! Almost a new World Champion right there. Both me up now, and Max with a rake of the eyes- and Jeff Howard with a warning. Max with a forearm in the back- sends Reed into the corner, and drapes his arm over the top rope- and tugs, using the top rope as a weapon. 5 count, and he breaks the hold. 5 count, breaks it again. Cross corner whip- reversed! (Pop) EL: Max goes hard into the corner, Reed with a running clothesline! Picks him up- sets him- snap suplex! Covers- 1, 2, no! Max kicks out again. Reed sends him for the ride- reversed again- back drop by Max- and Reed out over the top rope to the floor! (Crowd groans) EL: Reed takes a nasty spill, and right into no-man’s land! Max now starts getting in Jeff Howard’s face- (Opening the door for Lawson and Sherrick to go to work) EL: Lawson and Sherrick putting the boots to the World Heavyweight Champion, and now Lawson holds him back, and Sherrick with a big right hand! (Boos) EL: And Reed is rolled back into the ring. Max stands him up- Russian legsweep! 1, 2, kickout! (Pop) EL: Max stretches out the arm- and a kneedrop across the shoulder! And again! Max targeting the arm early on. And now he throws the champ back out to the floor, and heads over toward the Lady in Red! What is he doing? (Lawson puts Reed in a hammerlock, and runs him into the ringpost shoulder first as Jeff Howard gets between the Lady in Red and Max Robertson) EL: Lawson and Sherrick continue to work on Reed on the outside- and look at this! (Boos greet the arrival of Marc Thomas and Jak Hammer as they stand at the edge of the aisle) EL: Reed surrounded by sharks now, Lawson, Sherrick, the Major, Max, Thomas and Hammer all out there now. Reed back in the ring, and Max to a hammerlock down on the mat- trying to soften him up for the double chickenwing. Thomas now down by the ring, pounding on the ring apron- he’s had his run-ins with both of these men- whom is he supporting now? Reed reaching for the ropes- and Thomas pushes the bottom rope in to him! (Pop as Jeff Howard orders the hold broken) EL: Max now with words for Thomas- and Thomas belts him with the CWAA Heavyweight Title, right in the mush! Jeff Howard lets it go, and Reed rolls Robertson up- 1, 2, kickout! Lawson and Sherrick and the Major complaining now about the interference, and Jeff Howard trying to keep everyone separated, as Jak Hammer stands alone, arms crossed across his chest. His face betrays no emotions. OK- Max furious, and he takes a wild swing- missed! Reed counters with a punch of his own! (Pop) EL: Another! (Pop) EL: Reed sends him for the ride- big boot to the jaw! Reed sets him up now- powerbomb on the 200-pound Professional! (Pop) EL: Reed in control now! Lawson and Sherrick beside themselves, but with Thomas and Hammer at ringside they are obviously very concerned about trying any funny business. In the meantime, Reed sends Robertson to the ropes- belly to belly suplex! 1, 2, kickout! Max now with a chop across the chest- and another-backing Reed up to the ropes- sends him for the ride- Max with an iron claw- a claw hold to the head! Digging those fingers into the temples! Reed in trouble now, and he sinks to one knee! Obvious that Thomas is itching to do something, but not with Lawson and Sherrick too close- we have a stalemate at ringside, but in the ring, it’s the challenger who has taken over. Max with that clawhold on now, and Reed sinks to the mat- he’s on his back- 1, 2, gets the shoulder up! Sinks back- 1, 2, up just in time again! Back down- 1, 2, again- he just gets the right shoulder up, and now he has his hands on Robertson’s wrist, trying to pry his hand off and relieve the pressure. Reed knows his World Title is in jeopardy here. Reed straining to get free- Max’s hold is loosening- he’s getting free bit by bit! He’s got the claw off- but Max with a boot right in the solar plexus! Now Max pulls him up by the hair- sends him for the ride- knee to the midsection! Max behind the champion now- going for it- he’s got it on- the double chickenwing submission hold! (“Go, Joe, Go!”) EL: Max Robertson could become the next AEWA World Heavyweight Champion any second now, as he hooks Reed into that lethal double chickenwing! Nowhere for Reed to go- Max has both arms pinned behind his back! Reed nowhere near the ropes- see the look of concern on the face of the Lady in Red! (Jeff Howard checks for the submission) EL: Lawson and Sherrick, Thomas and Hammer, still all in a kind of stalemate- no one will get away with affecting the outcome of this one now! Reed in trouble- and he pushes off the mat with his feet- drives back- and pushes Max all the way into the corner, from that double chickenwing hold! (Pop) EL: Jeff Howard calls for a break- and Max gives him a clean break- no! Elbow jab to the top of Reed’s head! Now he sends Reed across the ring- and Reed bounces out of the corner and nails him with a clothesline! (Pop) EL: Max Robertson on his back! Reed pulls him up- Irish whip- dropkick by the World Heavyweight champion- now he grabs it- is it? Could it be? Yes! Kentucky Backsmasher! The cover- 1, 2, 3! He got it! (Big pop) EL: Crimson Joe Reed scores the win- another successful title defense, another grueling encounter, but once again, Reed is just that much better- all you have to do is be the better man for 3 seconds! Thomas admitted Reed was, and Robertson would have to, as well! Let’s get the official word! (Thomas leaves, and waves for Hammer to follow. Hammer follows.) DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, and still AEWA World Heavyweight Champion, Crimson Joe Reed! (Pop as “Bad Company” plays and Max and the Mayhem Militia leave.) EL: There he is, in the ring now, soaking in the cheers of this great Sportatorium crowd, the one and only Heavyweight Champion of the World, Crimson Joe Reed! Next week, we’ll have for you Little Yv- wait a second. (Pop, as Reed celebrates in the ring, and "The Dreamer" Randy Sheridan enters, offering his hand to Reed, who takes it. They exchange a few words, then Sheridan obtains a house mic from David Myers.) Randy Sheridan: Nice job tonight, champ. Whatever anyone else says, I think we all know that the REAL world champion is the man with the AEWA belt, and that's Crimson Joe Reed. (Crowd pops) RS: Now, I know you're anxious to get back to the AEWA, but I'm here to ask you to stick around in the CWAA for just one more week. You see, three men have held the CWAA Heavyweight title. Two of them, Thomas and Robertson, have gotten shots at the AEWA gold. I'm here to ask for the same thing...a shot at your belt. (Crowd pops, as Reed looks a little surprised and a little annoyed at Sheridan's challenge. He asks for, and receives, the microphone.) Crimson Joe Reed: Look, Randy... it's Randy, right? (Sheridan nods) OK, here's how it is. I've already defended the title against the current champion, and then I stuck around to shut up that obnoxious little punk Robertson. But I can't go around offering title shots to every wrestler in this league like candy. I have to draw the line somewhere, and unless you've got a damn good reason, I have to turn you down. Thanks for the words, and maybe next time. (He hands the microphone back to Sheridan and starts to leave, but is stopped by Sheridan, who motions him to "hold on") RS: You see, I've been pulling double duty up until now. I'd wrestle here in front of these great fans, then pack up, have Josh rush me to the airport, and catch a redeye flight to the east coast to wrestle for the ACWF. (Negative pop at the mention of the ACWF.) RS: I know, I feel the same way. It got real old, wrestling in main events in front of these great fans, then to bust my butt getting to the coast just to open a show, while guys like Joey Valachi strutted in the spotlight. And when the ACWF folded, they didn't even have the decency to offer me a contract with the UWF. They believed that my primary loyalties lay here. (Pause) RS: They were right. (Huge pop.) RS: Joe, these people are my fans. These are the people I bust my butt for week in and week out, and I want the chance to win the most coveted belt in sports in front of them. And despite the lies that people like Paul Reed have told about you, I know that "Crimson" Joe Reed gives the fans what they want. So I'm asking you, will you give us what we want, what we deserve...will you face me for that belt next week? (He hands back the microphone to Reed. The Lady in Red is now talking to Reed, and they seems to be agreeing on something. Reed finally speaks) CJR: Got screwed over by the UWF, huh? I've been there enough. Look, if I just give you a title shot, after last week, then everyone who's stepped in the ring will start demanding shots. So I'm not going to give you a shot. (Sheridan looks downcast, and the crowd starts booing) CJR: But I'll give a chance to earn that shot. (The crowd now starts to pop.) CJR: Here's the deal. I'll sign a non-title match with you next week. If you last ten minutes with me, I'll give you the title shot. But this is the only chance you'll get until the next time I come around here. If you're still up after ten minutes, I'll say that you deserve a shot. Do we have a deal? (Sheridan and Reed shakes hands, and "Crimson" Joe and the Lady in Red leave the ring, allowing Sheridan to celebrate with the fans.) EL: Wow! The World Champion is going to be hear again next week, and we’ll see if Sheradin can go 10 minutes with Joe Reed, or not! Here’s the full lineup for next week’s edition of Championship Wrestling: Casey Spahn is in action, the exciting newcomer who joins Twister to form the Forces of Nature team; the ACWF Tag belts will be on the line as Sgt. Mark Sherrick and Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson take on- get this- Jimmy Dangerous and Elektro- what a combination! Dave "the Freak" Johnson and Little Yvette meet one more time, and what a hot feud that one has been. The TV title is on the line, as Max Robertson defends against the Nagoya native, the man who many people feel stole the show on the Saturday half of the Reunion Arena supershow, the Samurai, Kensei Tokugawa! And, as we just learned, Joe Reed will give Randy Sheradin a crack at the World Title if only he can last 10 minutes in the ring with the champ! What a night that will be- be sure to join us then, for Championship Wrestling!
