Sunday, May 4th, 1997, Card (3/3)

4 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(...and if you are a veteran, you cannot be turned down- guaranteed!) EL: OK- we're back with our special feature match, as Max Robertson, fresh of winning the CWAA Title from Randy Sheradin, goes up against brand new AEWA World Champion Heavy Metal! But first, here is what we have signed for next week: Joshua Barnes is back in action, and he takes on "Hotshot" Gary Jackson; Mark Sherrick is here again against "Hardcore" Joey Haven; Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas in tag team action against the Preacher and Little Yvette; Travis Conley squares off with Byron McNultey. And our special feature match next week: CWAA TV Champ Lt. Lawson against, get this- former CWAA Heavyweight Champion, "Dreamer" Randy Sheradin! (The Imperial March starts, and CWAA Champ Max Robertson and his 5'11"-200 lb. body, in the red and black tights as always, walks the aisle, followed by TV champ Lt. Lawson and Major Mayhem, both still looking a little frazzled. The crowd boos vociferously.) EL: The biggest night of his short career so far, as Max Robertson tries to use the CWAA Title as a stepping-stone to the biggest prize there is! DM: This next contest is for the AEWA World Heavyweight Championship! It is set for one fall with television time remaining. On his way to the ring now, from San Francisco, California, at 200 lb. he is the CWAA Heavyweight Champion- Max "the Professional" Robertson! (Max climbs into the ring, and rather than make a grab for the mike, stands at Myers' side and waits to be addressed) DM: Tonight, you will be facing the best the AEWA has to offer. We've all seen Heavy Metal in action before. He doesn't pin wrestlers, he destroys them. . . What do you think your chances are of winning this match? Max Robertson: I know that Heavy Metal is a big man. I know that Heavy Metal is a powerful man. But the bottom line is that I know Heavy Metal is a man. Just like you, just like Barnes, and just like Sheridan. I know that I can out wrestle any man on the face of the Earth. I've proven that against the best in this league, and I'm going to prove it tonight against the best in the AEWA. DM: Which match were you more excited about, your CWAA debut against Sheridan, or this one here tonight? MR: What kind of question is that? Excitement has nothing to do with it. I'm a professional, and I'm here to do my work. This isn't a vacation to me. This isn't some trip to Disneyland; this is what I do. If you mean which match do I care more about, I'll tell you: This is the opportunity of a lifetime. The title carried by Heavy Metal is indisputably the single most prestigious title in the world. We all know that. It's a shame that the bureaucracy of the organization so often obstructs its strongest talent. But tonight, everything has worked out. You can't keep talent down, and you'll know you've seen a wrestling match after I'm through defeating the second best wrestler in the world for the AEWA World Heavyweight Championship. (MR hands his belt to the Major for safe-keeping) DM: Max Robertson, ladies and gentlemen. And his opponent- (Metallica blares over the PA system, and the popping begins. No one cares about the ICU here- all they know is that 460 lb. of Heavy Metal has arrived to face 200-lb. of the most hated man in the CWAA) DM: -from Las Vegas, Nevada, he weighs 460-lb. here is AEWA World Heavyweight Champion- Heavy Metal! (The 7'4" giant lumbers to the ring. He stands almost 1 1/2' and over 250 lb. more than his foe.) DM: Your referee is Jeff Howard. EL: OK- it looks like Max has once again struck a deal with the CWAA's mercenaries, Lt. Lawson and Major Mayhem, calling out all the reinforcements for this one. Heavy Metal up on the ring steps, soaking in the cheers- running knee by Max Robertson! He hit him in the small of the back- Max wastes no time getting started! Metal staggers into the ring- hard chop by the Professional- now the whip- reversed! Heavy Metal pulls up a boot- right in the Professional's face! (Big pop) EL: European uppercut by the giant. Now he pulls Max up by the throat- he grabs him in a chokehold and pulls him all the way up until they look eye to eye- headbutt by the champ! Now Heavy Metal with an Irish whip- clothesline! Did you see Max's head bounce off that mat? Another Irish whip- shoulderblock puts Max back down on the mat! The crowd is really loving this- they haven't seen Robertson handled like this, and he's had it coming for a long time. Now Heavy Metal sends him into the corner. look out, Max.... avalanched by Heavy Metal- that's a whole lot of champion coming at you! Max Robertson flattened in the corner, and Heavy Metal has had his way so far. Now Heavy Metal with the cover- 1... 2... Lt. Lawson just draped Max's boot over that bottom rope! Jeff Howard breaks the cover, but Heavy Metal really had him there. OK- Heavy Metal gets him up- vertical suplex- Max has a long way to fall... down he goes! Heavy Metal now with an elbow drop, and he almost drove Max all the way through the mat and to the floor! Heavy Metal up now. (HM raises his arms in triumph as the fans cheer like CWAA fans have never cheered before.) EL: Heavy Metal all over Max Robertson so far- and look out! Eye rake by the challenger- that'll equalize a 300-lb. difference anytime. Now he rakes the back. (Big-time booing) EL: Knee lift by Max- he looks so small in there against a man more than twice his size. Now Max- he's going for a hip toss- it wasn't pretty, but he got the man down. Heavy Metal on the mat now, and Max Robertson, normally a very scientific wrestler, goes to the throat- 2... 3... 4... break. Now he starts stomping away- concentrates on the bicep. Is he planning on the double chickenwing already? Now he drapes Heavy Metal's arm over the bottom rope, and jumps up- comes right down on the extended arm- again- and again! Now he cinches on an arm bar on the mat-pushing against the giant's body with his feet. ("Go, Metal, Go!") EL: Now Robertson turns it into a hammerlock, he's behind Metal who is up on one knee. Robertson really turning on the pressure on the champ. Heavy Metal up on his feet, though- he's backing up! And he just backed right into the corner- sandwiching Max between a rock and a hard place! Almost a reverse avalanche! Now Heavy Metal smashes his head on the turnbuckle. Hip toss to mid ring. Max up on his feet- bad idea- Heavy Metal with the Irish whip- kick in the midsection- the challenger doubled over- Heavy Metal sets for a powerbomb! (The crowd is frenzied) EL: He got it! He got it! He just planted Max in the canvas- 1... 2... Lawson reaches through the ropes and pops the champ one! Heavy Metal is unfazed, though- he grabs Lawson by that khaki jacket of his- pulls him into the ring! Max is down, and now Heavy Metal has the TV Champ! (Pop pop pop) EL: Heavy Metal kicks him in the gut- now he has both men by the heads- double noggin knocker! (Comedy pop as Max and Lawson are forced into a double head butt) EL: Heavy Metal now grabs Max by the throat- chokeslam! He just chokeslammed the CWAA Champ! Now he grabs Lawson! The Major is out of here! He has Lawson up- another chokeslam! Heavy Metal has just chokeslammed both the CWAA Heavyweight and TV Champs! He tosses Lawson out over the top rope! Heavy Metal now bounces off the ropes- falling splash! 500-plus lb. of Heavy Metal just fell from the sky on top of Max Robertson, who must feel a bit like chicken little at this point. 1... 2...3! It's over! (Huge, huge pop as Heavy Metal holds high the AEWA World Title and Metallica plays again) DM: Here is your winner, AEWA World Champion, Heavy Metal! EL: Well, judge, jury, executioner, grave digger, undertaker, and he's probably also the lawyer in charge of the probate for the wills, as Heavy Metal just buried, absolutely buried, Max Robertson and Lt. Lawson. The Major couldn't even stand to watch as the CWAA Champs were chokeslammed by the Heavyweight Champion of the World, Heavy Metal! I can hardly hear myself think in here, between this guy's music and the crowd- remember, next week, this ring, Lt. Lawson and Randy Sheradin! Be here next week- you won't want to miss it!
