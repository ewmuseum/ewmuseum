Sunday, May 18th, Card (2/3)

18 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(This week, on the WYXZ TV Ch. 39 movie of the week, Gamera is back, and this time he's here to defend the earth against a deadly creature from rainbow valley- it's Gamera vs. Barragon, Wednesday at 8 PM, here on WYXZ TV Ch. 39.) EL: OK- Double Jeopardy are still down in the ring area, as now it is Eddie Intellecta's turn. Let's go back to David Myers. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next bout is set for one fall with a 15-minute time limit. Introducing first, in the ring to my left, from parts unknown, weighing in at 250-lb., here is Eddie Intellecta! (The crowd is unimpressed) DM: And his opponent- ("My Wave" by Soundgarden starts up, and the popping begins) DM: -from Seattle, Washington, weighing in at 245 lb., here is Little Yvette! (Yvette is decked out in blue and white overall type wrestling tights (the style worn by Scott Steiner and many others). In the middle it says "Little Yvette" in fancy writing and on the back a picture of an eagle because "it flies gracefully through the air", and stands about 5'10"- his opponent, on the other hand, satnds 6'7"-250 lb. and wears his trademark blue mask). DM: Your referee, Chris Herman. EL: OK- it looks like Little Yvette has something to say first... Little Yvette (taking the house mike from DM): Hey gang. I don't know much about his great competitor I'm about to face, but I do know I'll have to work my but off and you can count on that. That nasty Jak Hammer has been somewhat of a thorn in my side, but other than that things have gone really well and I'm a very lucky man. Lets hope that work and dedication will keep paying off. Thanks for letting me say a few words guys. EL: And Intellecta and Jackson with a pair of double sledges as he finishes! Double whip- double clothesline! Come on, ref, get him out of there! All right, Chris Herman sending Gary Jackson away and the bell rings- we're officially underway now, but Eddie Intellecta had the big head start. Snap mare by the Intellecta. Now an elbow to the top of the head. Sends him for the ride, big back body drop. Yvette likes to fly, but not like that. The youngster in trouble, as he was jumped by both members of Double Jeopardy before this one even started. The cover- 1... kickout. Intellecta now with a hard right hand. Whips him to the ropes- back drop again- no! Little Yvette stuns him with a big kneelift- sets him up- suplex- no! He just dropped him face first- front layout suplex by Little Yvette! (Big pop) EL: I know Eddie Intellecta sometimes keeps a small piece of metal in that mask to make his headbutts all the more dangerous- if so, it just backfired! Monkey flip by Little Yvette sends Intellecta flying- and another one! Eddie Intellecta is not accustomed to opponents who so favor the aerial style of wrestling. Now Yvette hooks him- what's he doing? Bridging back suplex! 1... 2... kickout by Intellecta. (Pop) EL: Very nice move from the youngster, who seems to show us something new each and every week. Arm drag takedown now, into a standing arm bar. Little Yvette giving the alleged mensa member a wrestling lesson here tonight. I just heard that Jak Hammer is clear to wrestle later this evening- he has that big title match with Lt. Lawson. And speaking of clearances, Paul Langford has been given the go-ahead to get back in the ring tonight, but with the provision that he come up with some special equipment to protect his broken nose, and that he get a medical release- the CWAA will not be held liable for any further injury he suffers, as a result. The Tornado taking a big risk, I have to say. Intellecta now with the whip- and Little Yvette nails him with a dropkick right in the mush! Now Yvette into a bow-and-arrow submission hold on his more experienced opponent. Pushing hard on the back and pulling on the arms, an incredible strain on the biceps for Intellecta. He rolls back into a surfboard- be careful... 1... 2... Yvette almost got himself pinned and had to release the hold. All right, both men back up. Yvette with a forearm uppercut- and Intellecta goes downstairs. Irish whip- and Intellecta jabs a knee into the midsection. Knocked the breath clean out of him. Eddie Intellecta now motioning for a piledriver, and Hotshot Gary Jackson egging him on. He sets him- Yvette kicks and kicks- he can't get him over- back body drop by Little Yvette! He reversed it- Yvette spins around- hooks him around the waist- Northern Lights Suplex! He stuck him! (Pop) EL: Eddie Intellecta in serious trouble now- Yvette hops up- did you see that? Cover... 1... 2... 3! He got him! He just jumped from the mat to the top rope, bounced off and flipped in mid air landing in a legdrop! I can't believe he did that! ("My Wave" plays again as the crowd pops) EL: Let's get the official word from David Myers. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, Little Yvette! EL: OK- we're going to take another break, and when we come back, we'll have the tag team title matchup! Don't touch that dial! (New from the Franklin Mint... be sure to order your commemorative plates of Demons of Dante. There's Geryon, Minos, and more....Plates are not guaranteed to increase in value, but one plate in our series on Godzilla's Vanquished Foes, the Rhodan plate, increased in value by 42% within one year...!) EL: EL: Ok, everyone, we're back, and ready for the first of our three big title matches in just a minute, but first I have to tell you about something that happened during that last matchup, as Little Yvette pulled off the impressive win, Marc Thomas was found on the locker room floor, a dented chair lying at his side. He says he was attacked by that masked man who has come out here attacking Kore Wrench and Randy Sheradin in recent weeks. We've already seen Lt. Lawson attack his ally, Jak Hammer, earlier tonight- could Lawson and the Major be behind this, as well? We thought earlier that this individual might have been Marc Thomas himself, but now we know it can't be him- wait a second, here comes Jak Hammer now. I guess the interruptions haven't ended yet. (Jak Hammer limps to the ring to a chorus of boos and yanks the mike out of Myers' hand.) JH: Lawson, this is round 2 and I know this outcome will be different from that of our last match. The Major played an important role in that match, but I am going to ignore him his time and take care of my business with you. Don't think you're cute little plan earlier is going to change anything- even on one leg, I'm still too much man for you. And Masked Man, whoever you are, you're next on MY hit-list. You took out the one person in this business that I considered an ally! (Coincidentally enough, we see Marc Thomas rolled out on a stretcher in the background) JH: Don't even think about trying to jump me from behind with a chair because I'll break you're sorry (bleep) in half! Lawson, prepare to get HAMMERED!!! (JH tosses the mike back at Myers' feet and limps back) DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is for the CWAA Tag Team Championship! (Pop) DM: It is for one fall with a 20-minute time limit. First, the challengers- (ZZ Top's "Sharp Dressed Man" plays as the challengers in their sequined robes strut and dance to the ring before an unappreciative crowd) DM: -they come from parts unknown, at a combined weight of 497 lb, here are "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey and "Marvelous" Michael Anderson! MA: Why don't you take a seat, Finkel. The "Pretty Boy" and myself have a poem to read to these people. BM: That's right. As y'all know, last week was like Mother's Day or something, and we've sat down and written a special Mother's Day poem in honor of Mother Nature. (Clears his throat). Here, you do it. MA: OK. M is for the mold on her tooth, O is for the odor that accompanies her wherever she goes, T is for the one tooth she has, H is for her halitosis, E is for her ear hair, R is for her rotting tooth. (The booing increases) BM: Give it up for Marvelous Mikey, boys and girls! EL: Well, that was- uh-oh! Look out! (The champs don't bother to wait to be introduced and hit the ring in an expeditious fashion, and the crowd erupts.) EL: Here we go! The challengers still in their robes- and what is that Paul Langford is wearing? A catcher's mask? (It is. The CWAA Office insisted he wear it to protect his broken nose. By the way, McNultey is 5'10"-243 in the red robe and blue pants; Anderson 5'11"-254 in the green robe and red pants; both Langford and Travis Conley wear brown trunks. Langford is 6'3"-244 and Conley 6'8"-315.) EL: Langford head butts the Pretty Boy with that mask on! (As Conley slugs Anderson) EL: And again! (Ditto) EL: Byron McNultey now swings at Langford- he just punched him right in the mask! (Big pop, and Conley follows an Irish whip with a clothesline on Anderson) EL: Travis Conley meanwhile nails a powerslam- the cover 1... 2... kickout- and Langford with a death valley driver! The Pretty Boy head first into the canvas! Referee Jeff Howard has his hands full here- he sends the Tornado and Pretty Boy back to their respective corners, as Travis Conley, the ex-Husker football star, goes to the side headlock. Grinding Marvelous Michael's head now, and they think that poem was so clever. They don't look so clever now, do they? Mother Nature on the outside, and she is just fuming. Just... beside herself. it was a disgusting display, I'm sure you'll all agree. Anderson pushes off- and Travis Conley with the shoulderblock! (Pop as Anderson falls over unceremoniously) EL: And there's the tag- Paul Langford, catcher's mask and all, in the ring now. Anderson with a right hand- he just punched the mask, too! (Another pop) EL: If this keeps up, he and his partner will both end up with broken hands. The Tornado grabs him by the head- smashes it into the turnbuckle! Hefts him up- airplane spin- he's going for the Tornado drop already! No! Byron McNultey in there with a knee in the back to break it. He knew what was coming next. Here comes Conley- McNultey bulldogs Langford- Conley with a running clothesline on Michael Anderson. There goes the Tornado, thrown to the floor. Conley with a slam on Anderson. And now McNultey with a running knee in Conley's back. Jeff Howard has lost all control in this one- Langford still on the floor, Anderson and McNultey both working over Conley. Double clothesline. Langford back on his feet- coming back in- no! The Pretty Boy with a kick in the head as he tried coming back in. Anderson now with a scoop slam on Conley, and the Pretty Boy follows with an elbow drop. How long is this going to be allowed to continue? ("Go, Travis, Go!") EL: Langford crawling back to the apron- looks to be in bad shape- I think he may have landed badly on that floor the first time- and Anderson kicks him back to the floor again! Double snap suplex now, and Conley in serious trouble. They stand him up- ddt by the Pretty Boy! 1... 2... kickout! And Anderson still in the ring! Now he's getting on Jeff Howard's case. Byron McNultey with a slam- look out! Paul Langford back up on the apron! He grabs Anderson- headbutt again with that catcher's mask! (Big pop) EL: Now one for the Pretty Boy! And back to Anderson! Another for the Pretty Boy! Conley has no idea- Jeff Howard- look out! He just head butted the referee! I don't think Paul Langford knew what he was doing- Jeff Howard just tapped him on the shoulder, trying to restore order, and Langford must have mistaken him for Anderson or McNultey! There's the bell- the challengers are running for their lives! Here's David Myers- let's get the official word. DM: Your winners, as the result of a disqualification, "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey and "Marvelous" Michael Anderson! (The crowd boos vociferously as "Sharp Dressed Man" plays again, even though the poets-cum-challengers are long gone) EL: Well, an unpopular decision, but the titles do not change on a disqualification. We've got a TV title match up next, but first we're going to talk to the CWAA Heavyweight Champion- OK, Jeff Howard looks all right, Twister helping him back up. (The Imperial March is greeted with boos and the 5'11"-200 lb. bald headed heavyweight champion strides to the ring, glaring at the tag champs as they exit, eyeing him warily.) Max Robertson: Well, well, well. . . Here we are again. Max Robertson and Randy Sheridan- (Crowd cheers, but Max ignores them) MR: -in the feature match wrestling for the CWAA Championship. Ever since the beginning, I've had my suspicions about this federation, and now I have proof. During Sheridan's title defenses, the rules (and the officials) favored the champion. It wasn't until we wrestled in the AEWA that I got a fair shot and won my belt. And then, last week, while I was preparing to defend my most prized possession, I learn that this match is a no disqualification, no countout match. (More cheers) MR: Let me tell you, do any of you think that _I_ would sign such a match? I didn't. This was mandated from the mysterious CWAA President. Who the hell is that? But that's all right. I'm still the Champion, and I will protect what's mine. It doesn't matter what the officials do now; it doesn't matter who Sheridan bribes anymore because once that bell rings, his (bleep) is mine! (Robertson exits before Myers has time to question him) EL: All right, we are just about set for the TV title match, but first, a few messages. (Coming soon on Pay Per View, the ACWF's Spring Fling- Joey Valachi, David Graves, Ricky Reno and many more of the top ACWF stars, available only on pay per view- contact your local cable operator for details)
