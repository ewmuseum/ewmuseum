The AEWA Report (11/4)

7 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
--------------------- ************** AEWA REPORT --------------------- ************** Welcome to the third official edition of the AEWA Report! We have plenty of big news to tell you about, so read on.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ RECENT NEWS ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ --THE WORLD TITLE IS VACANT! On the most recent edition of Saturday Night Slam, the scaffold match caused plenty of controversy. After "Superman" Mike Stewart and Heavy Metal both fell off the scaffold at the EXACT same time, there was mass confusion at ringside. No one knew who the winner of the match was. After reviewing the videotape, President Freeman had no choice but to vacate the World title. When will a new champion be determined? President Freeman told our reporters that a BIG battle royal will be held, this weekend, and the winner will be crowned champion! --DERANGED WINS THE TV TITLE! Deranged finally got a victory over Super-Blonde, defeating him for the Television title on Saturday Night Slam. Super-Blonde looked good throughout the match, but the odds were really against him. Deranged's manager, Angel, shook the ropes at one point during the match, causing Super-Blonde to come crashing down to the canvas. Deranged quickly finished him off with a flying elbowdrop, becoming the first-ever THREE TIME Television champion. --MEAN MASSIMO BETRAYS AMERICAN AMBUSH! In one of the most shocking incidents in AEWA history, Mean Massimo attacked American Ambush-- his best friend. The incident happened on Saturday Night Slam, and Massimo teamed up with "Ultimate" Jose Gomez to viciously attack Ambush. No one has ANY idea what provoked this disgusting attack. When we tried to call Massimo for a comment on the situation, he yelled "no comment" and hung up. --TWISTED STEEL ATTACKS VICKI! After noticing Vicki at ringside on Saturday Night Slam, Twisted Steel viciously attacked her in front of a stunned crowd. He then referred to her as a "bitch", and stormed off. This was a horrible act of misogyny, and the AEWA will NOT tolerate this kind of sick violence. President Freeman plans to take action against Twisted Steel because of this assault. --THE CRAZY CANUCK LOSES HIS LUNCH! We were all disgusted to watch the Crazy Canuck regurgitate on national TV recently, following a brutal attack from Primal Fear. It was absolutely revolting, and we apologize to any of our "faint of heart" viewers. The Canuck is currently in the hospital because of the attack, and we hope to update you as soon as possible. --CARETAKER RETURNS TO THE AEWA! After months of absence, the Caretaker has finally made his return to the AEWA. He joined up with the newly-formed Knights Of Deliverance (which consists of Ben Slocum, Randall Osbourne, The Gryphon, and Sir Gallade), and immediately issued a challenge to Deathwatch. We're glad to have the Caretaker back! ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ INJURY REPORT ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ "SUPERMAN" MIKE STEWART suffered severe spinal injuries, after falling 15 feet from a scaffold, and landing on his head. He currently appears to be in some kind of a coma, and this situation does NOT look good. It's doubtful that Stewart will ever wrestle again-- the doctors aren't even sure if he'll ever WALK again. THE CRAZY CANUCK spent the weekend in a local hospital, after violently regurgitating on a recent episode of Saturday Night Slam. Primal Fear had been choking the life out of him with a baseball bat, and the Canuck suddenly started vomiting everywhere. He is currently wearing a brace around his neck (due to the basbeall bat attack), but the doctors are optimistic. If all goes well, he'll be back in action very soon. ******************* CHAMPION LIST******************** AEWA World Heavyweight Champion-- VACANT AEWA North American Champion-- Big Ron Raider AEWA Television Champion-- Deranged AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion-- Rick Showboat AEWA World Tag Team Champions-- The West Coast Connection AEWA National Tag Team Champions-- The Machines *******************THE TOP 10 (SINGLES)**************** 1.
