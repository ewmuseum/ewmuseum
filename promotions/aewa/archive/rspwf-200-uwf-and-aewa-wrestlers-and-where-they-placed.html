(RSPWF 200) UWF and AEWA Wrestlers and Where They Placed

22 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi, everyone. Just thought I'd show how current and former members of the AEWA, the parent organization for my fed (CWAA), and current and former members of the UWF, the only other fed I still play in, fared. The players and administrators of both groups should take pride in their showing here, esp. the UWF, which, despite having very little presence on this newsgroup startin small boasted a dozen and a half members- and the higher up the ladder you go, the greater the UWF presence: 9% of the total (18/200), 10% of the top 100 (10/100%) and 16% of the top 25 (4/25). Thanks to Gib for the time and effort this obviously took, and to all who voted for Kelly and Brian Maloney. :) Without further ado: UWF 9. Bishop 11. Serge Annis 18. Brewster Cogburn 22. "Crimson" Joe Reed (AEWA, UWF) 32. Youth Gone Wild (UWF) 46. Alex Kidd (UWF) 53. Tom Landis (UWF - Don't call him Maverick!) 71. Brett Greene (UWF "Oh, the Humanity!") 87. Kelly Maloney (UWF) 89. Brian Maloney (UWF) 102. "The Enigma" Takezo Musashi (IIWF, UWF) 116. Bad Boy Randy Acorn (IIWF, UWF) 138. Rock Ramon (UWF, WAIWA) 161. "Falcon" Robbie Greer (UWF, WAIWA) 164. Demolition (UWF, 1/2 of the Darknight Express) 168. Dimento (UWF, 1/2 of the Darknight Express) 186. Psychosis (UWF) AEWA 22. "Crimson" Joe Reed (AEWA, UWF) 29. Heavy Metal (AEWA) 34. Mr. Crowley (AEWA, 1/2 of the AEWA's Heaven & Hell) 48. Cardinal Chaos (AEWA, 1/2 of the second Heaven & Hell team on the survey) 61. The Extremist (EWA) 87. Kelly Maloney (UWF) 89. Brian Maloney (UWF) 91. Volcano Jackson (ACEW) 117. Rick Showboat (AEWA) 126. Big Ron Raider (AEWA) 136. Primal Fear (AEWA) 162. Grave Dweller (AEWA, 1/2 of the Graveyard Shift) 166. Mortician (AEWA, 1/2 of the Graveyard Shift) 185. Holy Ghost (AEWA) -kl
