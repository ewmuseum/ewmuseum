Sunday February 2 Card (1/3)

2 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Last week, on Championship Wrestling Association of America.... EL: Haven is running at him again- Haven grabs him in a side headlock and bulldogs him right onto the chair! This match ended a minute or two ago, folks, and the referee is trying to get both men out of there. Haven picks up the stunned giant and whips him into the ropes- the referee grabs the chair and pulls it out of the ring, where it won't cause any harm, and Haven catches Hammer- looks like an airplane spin or Samoan drop- no! He drops Hammer right on his head, and right where that chair was a few seconds ago, and the fans are on their feet! Hardcore Joey Haven has been ddted into the ground, and just came back and nearly Death Valley Drove Jak Hammer into that chair, and Hammer rolls under the bottom rope.... ******************************* EL: Welcome back the world-famous Sportatorium, everyone, for our second week of Championship Wrestling Association of America! We have some new faces here in action again tonight, and two exciting rematches from last week. First up will be an impressive rookie, Stan "The Powerhouse" Stevens, taking on Tip Smith's Healer's Havoc. Then, Twister takes on Double Jeopardy, who used an ankle brace last week to secure their win. BD. "Big Dog" Brown will be here, and he takes on perhaps the biggest name to have joined the CWAA yet, Marc Thomas! "The Professional", Max Robertson, is back, and if he and his opponent, former bouncer Joshua Barnes, can put on half the show Robertson and "The Dreamer " Randy Sheridan did last week, we're in store for something really special. And in our special feature match, we'll bring back Jak Hammer and "Hardcore" Joey Haven. Right now, I'm here in the locker area, trying to get a word with- there he is. Step over here, please. With me today is one of the biggest competitors here in the CWAA. His name is Jak Hammer. He recently defeated Joey Heaven in what can only be termed a wild match. These two men will face each other again here tonight. Jak, how do you feel about this rematch? JH: First of all Eric, its Mr. Hammer to you. Secondly, Joey Heaven, you say you are "Hardcore", yet when the going got tough you got going. We've got a rematch this Sunday and at that time, I'll show you what "Hardcore" really is. Obviously, I've got the size advantage on you, but just because I'm big doesn't mean I'm dumb. You can't just come to the ring and expect to beat on me. I've been in fights with women that would make your effort last week look like a walk in the park. Bring you best Joey, because when its all over, you'll be in a cast like the rest. EL: Errr....Mr. Hammer, can I ask one more question? JH: Yeah and the answer is over there...that's where the door is for you to leave. (Hammer escorts Lane to the exit for the lockeroom by the collar of his jacket.) (X's "You Got the Power" strikes up as Lane takes his seat by the ring) EL: And here he comes, "The Powerhouse". (Stan Stevens checks in at 6'7", 335 pounds, all of which is pure muscle. He wears his chestnut hair short, dresses in blue spandex tights with gold trim, and "Stan the Man" is written on his boots.) EL: The rookie is getting a nice reaction from our fans here, tonight, but I think the mood here is about to change for the worst... (The dog-collar wearing Healer's Havoc, led by Tip Smith, takes the same walk Stevens had. Havoc is about 6 1/2 feet tall, between 250-275 pounds, and completely, utterly, toothless.) EL: Havoc eyes Stevens, and seems unimpressed. There's the bell. They lock up, Stevens looks more impressive physically, but there is some terrific power in Havoc's sinews and thews. And it helps when you do that- Havoc has a handful of Stevens' hair- there isn't a lot to grab in all honesty- and uses the advantage it offers to force his opponent into a neutral corner. The ref is calling for a break, and both men release their hold of the other- hard slap across the chest by Healer's Havoc, and they heard that one all the way back at the Reunion Arena. Havoc turns around, quite proud of what he's accomplished so far, apparently, and- well, there's Stevens grabbing Havoc by the shoulder, he spins him around, a right, a left, a right, and Havoc's legs are looking like overdone spaghetti. Stevens scoops him up- big bodyslam! Stevens with an elbow drop, the cover, he hooks the leg, 1...2.. kickout. It'll take more than that, I'd wager. Stevens pulls Havoc back up, and there's a rake of the eyes by Healer's Havoc, the madman from Tucson, AZ. Havoc with an elbow smash in the head, and he drags Stevens over tp the ropes, and he's rubbing Stevens' face along the top rope- that's got to sting. We're going to take a time out here, but if there is a finish while we're gone, we'll show it to you when we get back. (Commercials for the Franklin Mint, a correspondence school, and Summer's Eve run.) EL: While we were gone, Healer's Havoc has kept up the attack. With a clothesline and elbow drop, he has Stevens on the mat, and has locked him into an armbar, wrenching it back. While The Powerhouse tries to escape, he has Tip Smith in his face, a state no one should want to be in. Havoc keeps the armbar on, but lets Stevens up. Havoc whips him into the corner, and Stevens goes right into those turnbuckles. As he comes out, it's Healer's Havoc with a forearm shiver in the lower back. Stevens drops to one knee, and Havoc turns to play it up to the crowd, who do not share his enthusiasm. One day he'll have to explain to us where the name comes from, or what happened to his teeth. Healer's Havoc back on the offensive, as he lifts the 335-pound Powerhouse, Havoc being something of a powerhouse himself, and brings him right back down on his knee- atomic drop! Havoc bounces off the ropes, clothesline- Stevens ducks! Havoc comes back off the ropes again, but Stevens catches him, this can't bode well for Healer's Havoc...he thrusts backward and drives Havoc down on his head- Northern Lights Suplex by the youngster from San Jose! Healer's Havoc just landed right on the crown of his skull! Stevens throws him into the ropes again, catches him- military press- he has what has to be at least 250 pounds of Healer's Havoc over his head, and there is only one direction to go from there. Tip Smith is a veritable picture of anxiety outside the ring- he knows what's coming next, as Stevens just throws Havoc back to the mat, and Smith grabs his charge by the arm and pulls him to safety out on the floor. There they go, heading back to the locker, and the ref is threatening them with a count-out. Havoc is clutching at the back of his neck, and Smith is arguing with the referee from the aisleway; I can't quite make it all out. Well, Smith and Havoc have thought better of it and are heading back. We're back in the ring, and Stevens wants a test of strength. I would advise against this, but Healer's Havoc, after a moments hesitation, agrees. They lock hands, and Stevens is overpowering Healer's Havoc- drives him back and down until Havoc is on his knees, howling. The crowd is really enjoying this part. Havoc leans back and sticks out his foot until it hits the bottom rope, and the referee calls for a break. Stevens with a boot in the chest, and now he pulls Havoc back up by the hair and whips him into the ropes, catches him in a belly-to-belly... he doesn't hook the leg though...1...2...kickout. Stevens pulls Havoc back up again, looks like a suplex coming up- no! He leans forward and drops Havoc on his face- front layout suplex. Stevens back up and a quick pose for the crowd who are cheering him on. Havoc staggers back to his feet, and Stevens with another Irish whip. He ducks and- ow! DDT by Healer's Havoc, and he just drilled Steven's face into that mat. Havoc looks to Smith and asks "Now?" I see Tip Smith nodding, and Havoc grabs hold of one leg, he turns around, now he rolls Stevens over- Scorpion Death Lock! Stevens is in utter agony, pounding on the mat with his right fist, every nerve and muscle in his body straining against the pain, his face turning beet red, but he's nowhere near the ropes- Havoc has it cinched in mid-ring, and he's got it locked on good and tight. Stevens is powering out of it though, he's got both arms underneath his body now, and he's pressing up....Havoc looks worried as he's about to be lifted off the mat... Havoc leans back and- Stevens drops back to the mat again. He can't go on with the hold in place like this... and there it is. The referee signals for the bell, apparently Stevens has submitted. Havoc breaks the hold, and gets his arm raised. It's got to be a good feeling to get your first win here, and that really, really unsettling toothless grin flashes. Your winner, by submission: Healer's Havoc. We'll be right back after a few messages. (If you like to doodle, you can take a free in-home art ex- oh, forget it!) EL: We're back for some more Championship Wrestling Association of America action, and we have two men making their CWAA debuts coming up next. Here comes the Big Dog, BD Brown. (BD., no relation to the Doonesbury character of the same name, is a 6'3"-250 pound rookie with obvious power. His blue eyes surround a nose that looks to have been broken on more than one occasion. He does the standard fan-hand-slapping thing on his way down.) EL: Brown is an impressive-looking youngster, but he'll be facing a man who has been around the ring a little more often. Let's see hear some words taped earlier this week from his opponent, Mr. Marc Thomas. <Camera opens with Marc Thomas lifting weights. He finishes his reps and then faces the camera.> MT: You guys are probably wondering why I wanted you to come here. Well, fellas, I got a message to deliver and I want to make sure everyone hears it. I've been wrestling for a while now. I've lost some matches but I've always won more. For some reason, certain people want give me the respect I'm due. I am a former tag team champ as well as the holder of the highest title in another fed and yet I still don't get any respect. I've worked my a** off to get where I am today and no one...I repeat no one is going to tell me what to do or where to do it. One person comes to mind who thinks they can just order me around. President Freeman of the AEWA, yeah buddy I'm talking to you, when you called me the other day and threatened to fire me if I didn't go to the CWAA you really pissed me off. Its not my fault that the wrestlers in the AEWA are either too afraid or just happen to not want to sign matches against me. To tell the truth, I think its all your fault that I couldn't succeed in the AEWA. You never gave me the chance to do well and now you want to demote me!!! I'll go to the CWAA, but I promise you that you and your fed have not heard the last from me. After I get finished taking out these pansies, your scouts are going to be all over me asking for me to return to the AEWA. Dumb Dog Brown, you are going to be the first of many to feel the pain of the ICEBREAKER!!! I hope you have plenty of friends, 'cause you'll need someone to come see you in the hospital. (Camera fades to black as Marc Thomas returns to his workout.) EL: Some very confident words from Marc Thomas. I've never paid much attention to many of the bigger organizations, the IIWF, summit feds, etc. I have always enjoyed seeing the independent regional groups in action, and that is where I first saw Marc Thomas, in the CCWF, a group up in the Prince Edward Island area of Canada, where Marc Thomas went from tag team champion to heavyweight champion in about a month, during a period of expansion in the CCWF. He has not yet been able to translate that kind of success into companies like the AEWA, but he is probably the most accomplished wrestler in the CWAA today, and we're going to see him in action in just a few minutes. (Commercial break. Silvertones records presents Abba's Greatest Hits. $17.99 for 2 CDs or 3 cassettes. No COD, please.) ("Life in the Fast Lane" by the Eagles (yuck) plays as the Iceman arrives. He has Benoitish body, black trunks, a sleeveless leather vest and black boots.) (The bell rings, and for once EL doesn't feel compelled to point it out) EL: The two men circle on another... Thomas with a feint to the Big Dog's leg, but he backs off. They lock up, and Thomas immediately pulls out. He's complaining to the referee about something, and the official motions for them to get started. Another lock up, and the two men jostle for the leverage advantage. Brown is able to push with his more powerful legs and gets under Thomas, driving him back to the ropes. The ref is calling for a break- they let go, and Brown backs up as Thomas covers his face. A clean break for the Big Dog. I want to take this opportunity to mention that next few weeks we will be starting the first round of the CWAA Heavyweight Championship Tournament, a single-elimination event- they lock up again, and - a handful of hair by Thomas offsets any power advantage Brown had, and Thomas pulls him back into the ropes. The ref calls for a break- single elimination, 16 of the CWAA's stars in an unseeded bracket- hard slap across the chest from Thomas, as he doesn't return the clean break given by Brown earlier, and Brown comes back with a big right hand, now a left, another right, and Thomas has a bad case of rubbery legs... Now Brown comes behind Thomas, hooks his leg, legsweep faceslam, and Marc Thomas gets an up close and personal meeting with the mat. Now Brown sits on his back, and hooked him a painful Boston Crab. I was talking a minute ago about the Heavyweight Title Tournament- Brown really leaning back with this hold- where 16 men will fight in a single elimination format over several weeks. We'll be showing the first four or five matches from the first round here in just a few weeks, and we'll have more information before then. The only man in the CWAA right now to have worn gold as a solo wrestler is on his stomach right now, reaching for the bottom rope, and it looks like he has it. Randy Sheradin has worn a tag team title belt, also up in Canada, but with a different organization, but only Marc Thomas has been a Heavyweight champion. Right now, he has his hands full with the Big dog, who sends him for a ride- Brown with a cross body press...1...2...kickout! What a feather in Brown's cap if he can start his career with a win over the CCWF champion. Brown sends him into the corner, and charges in- no! Thomas gets his elbow up in time, and Brown runs headlong into it. Now Thomas with a kick in the chest, and he traps the Dog in a side headlock, holding him in position for a fist right in the eye. Now he sets up beside Brown, and brings him crashing to the mat with a Russian legsweep. I better be careful about calling that move if the Major's around, but Lt. Lawson has the day off. Brown rolls over onto his stomach, and Thomas reaches down and slips on a full nelson. He has a full nelson hooked on an opponent on the mat. Thomas is lifting him in that full nelson to a standing position, and- no- he's not stopping there! He takes him over the top- Dragon suplex! 1...2...kickout! Thomas releases the hold. He straddles Brown's chest and slaps a few fists to the head. He grabs the Dog by the hair, and pulls him up- he;s got him draped over his shoulders...Samoan Drop...1...2....another kickout. Thomas takes him back up, Irish whip, he's setting up for a back body drop- the Dog slams on the brakes, reverse neckbreaker! Thomas was not expecting that, and the Dog may have just permanently changed the momentum in this one. Brown sends him into the corner, charges in- this is where he made his mistake last time- Stinger splash, and he just crushed Thomas up against the turnbuckles. Brown heads up to the second turnbuckle and starts punching away. (Of course the crowd counts along to 10, with a few weirdoes going on to 11 and 12 even though the punching has stopped.) EL: Thomas looks just abut out on his feet. BD sets up for a full nelson- wait! What was that! (What it was: Thomas performing a Diamond-cutter on BD as he went for the full nelson.) Devastating maneuver by Thomas... he covers... 1... he's got his feet on the middle rope for added leverage!...3! It's over. Marc Thomas with an Icebreaker over Big Dog Brown, and the front row fans are trying to convince the referee that Thomas used the ropes, but if he doesn't see it, he can't call it. With all of these big bodies flying around in the ring, and with the need to ensure that the shoulders are indeed down on the mat, sometimes the officials don't see everything that goes on, but I'm sure you'll find that our referees here in the CWAA do a just fine, and thankless, job. (Thomas leaves while "Life in the Fast Lane" plays. BD stands in the ring and receives a polite applause when he exits.) -kl
