Saturday Night Slam (5/11, part two)

13 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MW: Well up next, we've got a bout that combines the two matches from Wednesday Night Rumble that were inconclusive. Big Ron Raider beat Twisted Steel by DQ, and Primal Fear fought Lord Humongous to a double countout. LB: But tonight, Big Ron Raider and Primal Fear will face Twisted Steel and Lord Humongous in a Survivor Series style elimination match! MW: This match should be very interesting, as there is a lot of hatred here, and none of these men have tagged together before! So without any further ado, let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following tag-team contest is an elimination match. It will continue until BOTH members of one team are eliminated by means of pinfall, submission, disqualification, or countout. Introducing first, from the Badlands, Sudan, weighing in at 360 pounds and accompanied by his manager, "The Beautiful" Miss DeMeanor, here is Primal Feeeeeeeeeeeeear! His tag team partner, from the Rocky Mountains, weighing in at 416 pounds and accompanied by his manager, Million Dollar Mark, here is Big... Ron... Raiiiiiiiideeeeeeeer! (Lynyrd Skynyrd's "Saturday Night Special" cranks on the PA as the unusual duo makes their way to the ring. The crowd boos loudly and throws garbage. Miss DeMeanor and Million Dollar Mark follow behind, arm in arm) MW: And what a combo these two make. We've got an insane psycho and a former world champion. They are both forces to be reckoned with. And it looks as if Million Dollar Mark is trying to put the moves on Miss DeMeanor. LB: I think they make a cute couple, they should go out. MW: I dread the thought of what their children would be like. LB: Fine upstanding citizens? MW: Not exactly. RING ANNOUNCER: And their opponents, first, weighing in at 304 pounds, Lord Humongooooooooooooous! And HIS tag team partner, accompanied by his manager, Karma, and weighing in at 425 pounds, Twisted Steeeeeeeeeeeeeel! (The popular twosome make their entrance, and the crowd explodes. They head straight for the ring and Karma is right behind them) LB: What do these stupid fans see in these two? MW: Strength, skill, and honor. LB: Really? What would give them that idea? MW: Oh nevermind! We've got Primal Fear in there starting it off with Twisted Steel. These two split a pair of matches last week. The two lock up and Primal Fear whips Twisted Steel to the ropes. Miss DeMeanor pulled down the top rope! LB: And Twisted Steel falls to the outside! Great managing! I love it! MW: Twisted Steel is down on the outside, and Primal Fear jumps off the apron, and SQUASHES him with a big splash! Primal Fear picks Twisted Steel up, and WHIPS him to the steel guardrail! LB: Now there's a great technical wrestling move! MW: What are you smoking? Now Primal Fear rolls Twisted Steel back into the ring. He gets back in as well. Now he whips Twisted Steel to the ropes for a CLOTHESLINE--DUCKED! Twisted Steel comes back, and catches Primal Fear with a clothesline! LB: Come on Primal Fear! Don't let this big dummy beat ya! MW: Twisted Steel picks him up, BODYSLAM--NO! Primal Fear turns it into a small package! One..Two..Roll over! MW: Now Twisted Steel has reversed Primal Fear into a small package! One..Two..Kickout! LB: This is exciting action! MW: The two are up, and Primal Fear takes Twisted Steel down with a lariat! Primal Fear now tags out to Big Ron Raider! Raider picks Twisted Steel up, and whips him to the ropes. Twisted Steel makes the tag to Lord Humongous, but I don't think that Raider saw it! LB: That's illegal, you can't tag when your opponent's not looking! MW: Of course you can! Raider POWERSLAMS Twisted Steel, but gets up and is NAILED by a clothesline from Lord Humongous! Humongous picks Raider up PILEDRIVER! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: It'll take more than that to pin Big Ron Raider! MW: Lord Humongous waits for Raider to get to his feet. Now Humongous runs to the ropes, on the rebound, AND GETS CAUGHT IN A BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX BY RAIDER! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Finish him, Raider! MW: He's trying to do just that! Humongous gets up, and gets doubled over with a fist to the midsection! Raider is really dominating this match so far, and now he goes in for the kill.... HE'S GOING FOR THE RAIDER BOMB! He lifts Humongous up, BUT THE MOMENTUM CARRIES HUMONGOUS OVER AND INTO A BACKSLIDE ON RAIDER! One..Two..And a half! LB: Raider's mad now! MW: He sure looks like it! Now he gets up and BODYSLAMS Lord Humongous! He whips Humongous to the ropes, who tags out to Twisted Steel. Twisted Steel enters the ring, AND RAIDER CLOTHESLINES THEM BOTH! LB: Now THAT'S the Raider I know! MW: Raider tags in Primal Fear, but doesn't leave the ring! Primal Fear is holding Twisted Steel from behind, and Raider runs to the ropes, he comes back, with a full head of steam, BUT TWISTED STEEL DUCKS! AND RAIDER BRUTALLY CLOTHESLINES PRIMAL FEAR! LB: NO!!!! MW: Primal Fear is LAID OUT! Courtesy of Raider's clothesline! Miss DeMeanor is upset with Million Dollar Mark, she slaps him! Now Raider is brawling with Lord Humongous, and Twisted Steel SPLASHES Primal Fear! The cover! One..Two..Three!!! RING ANNOUNCER: Primal Fear has been ELIMINATED! LB: That's not fair! MW: Fair or unfair, Primal Fear is gone. Miss DeMeanor and Primal Fear are headed for the showers! Now Raider is forced to face two men by himself! LB: Yeah, but look who the two men are! Raider will eat these two for breakfast. MW: That remains to be seen. Twisted Steel goes right to the attack on Raider! He's pounding away, but the blows don't have any effect! Raider's shouting, "NO PAIN!" Raider grabs him in a bear hug, but he's near his own corner! Twisted Steel reaches out, and tags the out reached arm of Lord Humongous! LB: Look out Raider! MW: Lord Humongous goes up top, and NAILS Raider with a double axehandle. Raider lets go of the Bear Hug on Twisted Steel. Now Humongous picks Raider up and SLAMS him to the mat! He waits for Raider to get up, and doubles him over with a kick to the midsection. HE'S GOING FOR THE POWER BOMB! LB: But Raider BACKDROPS him before he can get the chance! MW: Now Raider runs to the ropes, and SPLASHES Lord Humongous! But he's not done yet, He's going up top! MOONSAULT! AND HE HITS IT! The cover! Twisted Steel is trying to get in and break it up but Million Dollar Mark has him by the leg! One..Two..Three! RING ANNOUNCER: Lord Humongous has been ELIMINATED! LB: I told you that this would be no problem for Raider! MW: He still has Twisted Steel to deal with, but now it's one on one. Lord Humongous is leaving, but he doesn't look happy! LB: Of course he doesn't look happy. He just got humiliated by the man they call Raider. MW: DON'T start with THAT. Twisted Steel is wasting no time as he runs into the ring and CLOTHESLINES Raider! But the big man doesn't go down. So Twisted Steel picks him up and slams him down! Now Twisted Steel throws Raider out of the ring! And Million Dollar Mark jumps up on the apron! LB: That's it Mark, get the big dummy to come after you and buy your man some time! MW: Twisted Steel goes to confront Mark, and Raider is getting a chair on the outside. This is the EXACT same thing that happened in their last match! Raider comes into the ring with the chair, and Karma yanks Mark from the apron and the two get into ANOTHER brawl. The ref is trying to separate them. LB: Deja Vu! MW: Twisted Steel turns around, and sees Raider with the chair. The ref turns his attention back to the ring. Raider swings, but Twisted Steel moves! The chair hits the ropes, bounces back, and NAILS RAIDER IN THE HEAD! Raider falls to the mat, he is out! LB: Disqualify Twisted Steel, ref! MW: Because Raider hit HIMSELF in the head? Are you insane? Now Twisted Steel SPLASHES Raider, the cover! One..Two..Three!!! (DING, DING) LB: This isn't fair! RING ANNOUNCER: Here is you winner, and sole survivor... TWISTED STEEL!!!!!! MW: I love it! Raider defeated Twisted Steel on Wednesday in the very same scenario as the way this match ended, but he tried it again today, and it backfired! Twisted Steel got his revenge! LB: Shut up, Wilson! Now what's up next? MW: Well, next we've got a TV Title defense by Volcano Jackson as he takes on The Punisher. Both of these men are coming off outstanding victories against Super-Blonde. LB: Mark my words, the TV Title will change hands tonight. The Punisher is just too much for Volcano Jackson to handle. MW: The Punisher is indeed a worthy opponent, but Volcano Jackson has proven time and time again to be a great champion.... It should be an outstanding matchup. Now let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is for the AEWA Television Title and is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, the challenger, weighing 301 pounds and accompanied by his manager, Bootknife Kelly, The Punisheeeeeeeeeeeer! (The music from "Star Wars" plays as the hated Punisher makes his entrance to loud boos. He wears black shades and is accompanied by Bootknife Kelly.) MW: The Punisher looks cocky as ever. LB: What's wrong with that? He believes in himself. I'd say that's an asset. MW: There's a difference between confidence and arrogance. Confidence can win you matches, arrogance can get you beat. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 290 pounds and accompanied by his manager, Aaron Proctor. He is the 2-time and current AEWA Television Champion... Volcanoooooooooooo Jackson! (Queen's "Another One Bites the Dust" plays as Jackson makes his entrance and the crowd erupts. Jackson holds the belt high above his head and Aaron Proctor walks alongside him.) MW: And take a look into the eyes of the champion. He is ready for this one. LB: Ready to drop the strap? MW: No, ready to take on the world. LB: I'll never understand why the fans like goodie two shoes like Jackson. MW: No, you won't. Both men are in the ring now. They go to the center, and we have a lockup. Punisher grabs Jackson in a Belly-to-Belly Suplex--NO! Jackson breaks it with an elbowsmash right to the head of the Punisher. LB: And the Punisher is stunned! MW: Volcano Jackson runs to the ropes, and NAILS the Punisher with a clothesline that carries him over the top rope and out of the ring! Jackson waits for him to get back into the ring, SHORT LARIAT! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: That was close, but WAIT A MINUTE! Here comes Wild Thing to ringside! What's HE doing here? MW: He's a TV title contender, he's scouting the competition. Look, he's even taking notes. But nevermind that now, as we've got action in the ring. Jackson hits the Punisher with a Spinning Mule Kick! Now he grabs him, FISHERMAN SUPLEX! One..Two..Kickout! LB: He's not gonna beat the Punisher so quickly. MW: Volcano Jackson runs to the ropes, BUT BOOTKNIFE KELLY PULLED DOWN THE TOP ROPE! And Jackson falls to the outside! LB: Those are the kind of moments that make you feel good to be a manager. MW: Punisher waits for Jackson to get back in the ring, and NAILS him with a forearm in the back! Now he whips Jackson to the ropes, LARIAT! Jackson gets up, and Punisher hits him with a fist in the midsection, HE'S GOING FOR THE POWER BOMB--BUT JACKSON REVERSES IT INTO A BACKDROP! LB: He almost had him there! MW: Jackson hits Punisher with a BIG short lariat! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! MW: Jackson waits for Punisher to get to his feet, he runs off the ropes, ducks a clothesline from the Punisher, off the other ropes, AND CONNECTS WITH A BIG CLOTHESLINE ON THE PUNISHER! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Jackson is not letting up! He really wants to put the Punisher away! MW: You can say that again. Jackson whips Punisher to the ropes, but the Punisher reverses it. Jackson comes off the ropes, and gets doubled over with a fist to the midsection. Punisher's going for the Power Bomb, AGAIN! He gets Jackson up, BUT JACKSON TAKES HIM OVER WITH A BACKSLIDE! One..Two..Kickout! LB: God! How does Jackson keep doing that? MW: He just knows how to get his opponent's shoulders to the mat. Jackson up, and he kicks the Punisher, and now he takes him in an octopus hold! Punisher is in pain. LB: But he just made it to the ropes! MW: Yes, the ref orders the break. Jackson lets go, and gets hit with a BIG roundhouse right on the break! Now Punisher grabs Jackson in a cobra clutch! LB: Submission holds are his specialty, you know. MW: Yes, I know. But Jackson just got to the ropes. The ref orders the break, and Punisher obliges. They move to the center of the ring, lock up, and Volcano Jackson takes him down with ANOTHER fisherman suplex! One..Two..Kickout! LB: You gotta admire Punisher's resilience. And Wild Thing is still at ringside taking notes, how many notes does he need? MW: Apparently, a lot. Jackson drops an elbow, but Punisher moves! Punisher gets up, and knees Jackson in the back. Now Punisher whips Jackson to the ropes, and catches him in a sleeper! LB: Goodnight Irene! MW: Jackson is staggering, but reaches the ropes. Punisher lets go, and FLOORS Jackson with a jumping side kick! Now he grabs Jackson in a half Boston Crab, The Punisher is not letting up! LB: Come on Jackson! Give it up! MW: Not likely. Jackson crawling, crawling, HE'S GOT THE ROPES! Punisher quickly lets go. He picks up Jackson, whips him to the ropes, AND WILD THING PULLS DOWN THE TOP ROPE! LB: Where did that come from? Volcano Jackson just FLEW to the outside! MW: I don't know, but Wild Thing is trying to say it was an accident, I don't know about that. Wild Thing rolls Jackson back into the ring. Bootknife Kelly just jumped up on the apron, and the ref is trying to get him down. Now Jackson his up, and he is sticking his head through the middle rope giving Wild Thing a piece of his mind. LB: Apparently, he doesn't buy that it was an accident. MW: Jackson is yelling, and Wild Thing is yelling right back. They both look extremely mad. Wild Thing turns his back to walk away, NO! He quickly turns right back around and NAILS Jackson right in the head with that steel clipboard that he was taking notes on! LB: Yes! It's about time somebody did that! MW: Jackson staggers back, HE'S OUT ON HIS FEET! Kelly just jumped back down and the ref turns around in time to see the Punisher catch Jackson with a German Suplex! The count! One..Two..Three!!! (DING, DING) LB: We have a new champion! RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner, and NEEEEEEEEEW AEWA Television Champion... THE PUNISHER!!!!! MW: I can't believe what just happened! What is Wild Thing's problem? He just cost Volcano Jackson the TV Title! LB: Maybe Wild Thing finally saw the light. Jackson was afraid to grant him a title shot, and he was just sick of it. Can you believe that Jackson was gonna defend the title against RASTA PETERSON before Wild Thing? The nerve of that guy! Jackson got exactly what was coming to him. MW: The Punisher just grabbed the TV Title, and he holds it up to a shower of boos. Now Wild Thing is coming over to congratulate him, can you believe this? And now Punisher, Wild Thing, and Bootknife Kelly are all walking back to the dressing room together. This was a set up! LB: And Volcano Jackson was the unknowing victim. Isn't it great? MW: Not really. Now Jackson is finally getting up, and Proctor is telling him what happened. Jackson looks REALLY upset. He gets up, and BOLTS back to the locker room. After Wild Thing, no doubt. LB: Too late though, the damage has been done. MW: That's for sure. Now that the ring is cleared out we are ready for our Main Event. LB: Yeah! I can't wait for this one. That punk, Ben Slocum, is defending the World title that he stole from Mr. Crowley on Wednesday against "The Spanish Lover" Juan Nalley. MW: It should be an outstanding matchup. Let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is for the AEWA World Heavyweight Championship and is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, the challenger, weighing in at 286 pounds and accompanied by his manager, Doctor Love, "The Spanish Lover" Juan Naaaaaaaalllllllley. (Juan Nalley makes his entrance to loud boos by the crowd, but many women in the audience are going wild because they LOVE the way he looks. Dr. Love is right behind him.) MW: Nalley is arrogant as ever. He thinks that everyone loves him. LB: They do. He is sooooooooo hot! MW: I don't, and most of this audience doesn't. LB: You're all just jealous. MW: (sarcastically) Yeah, right. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Austin, Texas, weighing in at 302 pounds and accompanied by his sister, Sondra, he is the AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World... "The Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slooooooooooocum! (The crowd goes nuts as the new champion makes his entrance to the tune of Laura Branigan's "Self Control". He wears the belt proudly around his waist, and he and Sondra high-five all the fans on the way to the ring.) MW: It's nice to FINALLY have a World Champion that you can be proud of again. LB: I was proud of Mister Crowley. MW: Yes, I know, you don't count. LB: Well thanks a lot. MW: Anytime. And this one is off in a hurry as Nalley attacks the champion before the bell. LB: Nalley wants what Slocum's got, and he's gonna pull out all the stops. MW: Nalley puts Slocum in the corner, and NAILS him with an avalanche. Nalley whips Slocum to the ropes, but the champ reverses it. On the comeback, Slocum FLOORS him with a clothesline, the cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Nalley's not going down THAT easily. MW: Slocum drops an elbow, BUT NALLEY MOVES! The Champ hits the mat hard. Nalley whips Slocum to the ropes, CLOTHESLINE! Nalley picks him up, BRIDGING BACK SUPLEX! One..Two..Shoulder Up! MW: Nalley, not wasting any time, picks Slocum up, NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Boy, Nalley almost won the title TWICE there. MW: But Slocum's not done yet! Nalley whips the champ to the ropes, FLYING LARIAT! Now he locks Slocum in a cobra clutch! He is just not letting up! LB: Why would he, this one's for all the marbles! MW: But Slocum reaches the ropes, and the ref forces the break. Nalley lets go, and whips Slocum to the turnbuckle! Nalley charges, BUT SLOCUM MOVES! Nalley hit the turnbuckle HARD! Slocum moves in, he hits Nalley with a chop, and ANOTHER! Slocum jumps, SPINNING MULE KICK--BUT NALLEY DUCKS! LB: This action doesn't stop! MW: Nalley moves in, ANOTHER NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX! One..Two..Kickout! MW: Another close call for the champ. Nalley puts Slocum in the corner, AVALANCHE! Nalley, still on the attack, whips Slocum to the ropes, on the comeback, FLYING FOREARM! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: Slocum is just getting pounded, but he keeps kicking out! How does he do it? MW: Your guess is as good as mine. Now Nalley grabs Slocum in a standing wristlock, but Slocum quickly gets to the ropes. Nalley goes for a gutwrench suplex, BUT SLOCUM BLOCKS IT! Slocum tries to grab a front facelock, BUT NALLEY TURNS IT INTO A BACKDROP--NO! SLOCUM TURNS IT INTO A SUNSET FLIP! One..Two..Kickout! LB: That was the champ's first offensive move in quite some time. Nalley is DOMINATING. We're gonna see a new champion tonight! MW: That's very possible. Both men up, and Nalley NAILS Slocum with a knee in the back. He grabs him, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: I can't believe this! MW: I can't believe I'm saying this, but me neither! Nalley immediately up, SCORPION DEATHLOCK! But too close to the ropes, as Slocum grabs them almost immediately. LB: If the positioning was a little better, Nalley could've turned that into his "Spanish Kiss" finisher, and we would have a new champ. MW: I'd have to agree. Both men up, armdrag takedown by Nalley--NO! Slocum turns it into a LARIAT! Slocum grabs Nalley, DDT! Now Slocum hits him with an uppercut! Slocum whips Nalley to the ropes, AND CHOPS HIM DOWN on the comeback! LB: Boy! Has this one turned around in a hurry! MW: That's for sure. Slocum picks Nalley up, POWER BOMB! The cover! One..Two..And Dr. Love places Nalley's foot on the ropes! LB: Managing at it's finest! MW: Yeah, sure. Slocum picks Nalley up, brings him to the middle of the ring, DDT! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! MW: I can't believe he just kicked out of that! And Slocum, much like Nalley earlier, is not letting up. He goes right to an armbar submission, that's the move he won the title from Mr. Crowley with! LB: But Nalley quickly gets to the ropes! MW: Both men up, and Slocum CHOPS away at Nalley. He picks him up, SPINNING POWER BOMB! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Where did he find the energy to kick out of THAT one! MW: He had to dig deep, but he still has something left. BOTH of these men have been just unbelievable. Slocum drops a BIG LEG on Nalley. Slocum waits for Nalley to get to his feet, he runs to the ropes, SHOULDERBLOCK! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: WOW! MW: Ditto. Slocum goes for a facerake--BUT NALLEY BLOCKS IT! Nalley grabs him, BACK SUPLEX--NO! SLOCUM TURNS IT INTO A CROSS-BODY BLOCK! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder up! (barely) LB: What will it take to put one of these men away? MW: I don't know! Slocum waits for Nalley to get to his feet, he runs to the ropes, AND NALLEY CATCHES HIM WITH A BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder up! MW: Nalley on the attack now. He takes Slocum, BACK SUPLEX! Now he grabs Slocum with a hammerlock! But he lets go of it! Nalley runs to the ropes, FLYING FOREARM--NO! SLOCUM DUCKS! And Nalley hits HARD! Slocum picks him up, PILEDRIVER! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: This is UNREAL! MW: Slocum goes up top! He dives, AND NALLEY MOVES! Slocum hits the mat HARD! Nalley up, He picks Slocum up, POWERSLAM! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Can either of these men be pinned? MW: It's been done before, but these two are putting on quite a show tonight! Nalley picks him up. Bear Hug! He's squeezing. Slocum is in pain! Now Nalley takes him over, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One..Shoulder up! LB: He kicked out at one! How the HELL did he do that? MW: I really don't know. Slocum gets up, staggers, and falls against the ropes. Dr. Love jumps up on the apron, and he's holding the champion there! Nalley runs, HIGH KNEE--NO! Slocum moves! And Nalley nails Dr. Love with that knee! LB: NO!!! MW: Nalley charges at Slocum, and catches a dropkick in the face! Both men up, and Slocum doubles Nalley over with a Kick to the midsection! He picks Nalley up, BRUTAL POWER BOMB! The cover! One..Two..Three!!!! (DING, DING) MW: He did it! Slocum defends the championship! RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner, and STILL, AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World... "THE MODERN DAY WARRIOR" BEN SLOCUM!!!!!! LB: As much as I hate to admit it, that was a GREAT match, even though that chump Slocum is still the champ. MW: Not how I would have put it, but yes, it was a fantastic matchup. Neither of these men have anything to be ashamed of. I have a LOT more respect for "The Spanish Lover" than I did before. That boy can wrestle! LB: But I still have to put up with crap like this. Look at this crowd, they actually like this Slocum guy. MW: He deserves it, and Nalley deserves it too. Slocum fought to hang onto that belt until his last breath, and this match DESERVES a standing ovation. Now Ben grabs his belt, hugs his sister, and the two walk out together to that well deserved ovation. LB: I think I'm gonna be sick again! MW: Well that's all the time we have fans, so for the Lovely Bertha, this is Matt Wilson saying so long everybody! (fade to black)
