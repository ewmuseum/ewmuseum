Saturday Night Slam (1/11)

13 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen, set on fire. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Rock music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... David Graves TAKING SOME GUY'S HEAD OFF with the Gravestone, Penance PLANTING some guy with the Final Judgment, The West Coast Connection DRIVING some guy's head into the mat with the West Coast Killa, Cardinal Chaos STICKING some poor soul with the Inverted Crucifix, Shane Matthews BRUTALLY hitting the End of the Road on some hapless jobber, and Heavy Metal notching yet ANOTHER casualty courtesy of the Megadeth Power Bomb. The camera then shifts to Roy Stanley and Hank Thompson in the Concord Pavilion, the site of AEWA Saturday Night Slam!) HT: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Saturday Night Slam! We have some GREAT matches scheduled for tonight, folks. RS: And in the main event, Primal Fear will be wrestling the Twilight Strangler, his longtime enemy. These two guys REALLY hate each other. HT: That's going to be an incredible match, Roy. They want to KILL each other, so that should be a wild match. RS: But what's first? HT: Well, Penance is about to defend his Light Heavyweight title against "Ass Kicker" Adam Anderson! This should be an excellent match. RS: During their last match, Penance came out on top with his "Final Judgment" piledriver. Can he do it again? HT: I hope not. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is for the AEWA Light Heavyweight title! Introducing first, weighing in at 221 pounds, here is "ASS KICKER" ADAM ANDERSON! (The Eagles' "Desperado" begins playing, and Anderson makes his way toward the ring. Bootknife Kelly follows closely behind him.) RS: Their first match was amazing, and this bout should be a good one, too! They're both superb athletes, that's for sure. HT: Maybe Penance will FINALLY lose that belt of his! RS: I don't know. I hate to admit it, but he has been a GREAT champion so far. Penance is one of the best wrestlers in the AEWA today! HT: And here he comes now.... RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, coming down the aisle, weighing in at 223 pounds, the AEWA Light Heavyweight champion of the World, Penance! (Skid Row's "My Enemy" begins playing loudly, as Penance walks down the aisle. He is dressed totally in black, and holds the title high above his head. The audience showers him with boos.) RS: Penance is SUCH a good wrestler! Why is he even in the ICU?! If he would just ditch those losers, there wouldn't be ANY limit to his potential. HT: Well, this match is about ready to begin! Adam Anderson takes Penance down right away with a single leg takedown! And he quickly locks on a reverse headlock! RS: He seems to be going for a totally different strategy. It looks like he's trying to wear Penance down, instead of trying his usual high-risk maneuvers! HT: Good point. And Penance reaches the ropes, forcing Anderson to let go of the hold. Anderson whips him to the ropes, and takes him down again with a drop-toe hold! Now he locks on an armbar submission! RS: It looks like he's frustrating Penance, doesn't it? He's not using any moves that Penance can actually reverse! HT: And now Anderson lets go of the hold. He picks up Penance and SLAMS him to the mat! He gets back up, and DROPS a leg on him! RS: Anderson is looking a LOT better than he did a few weeks ago! Penance has done NOTHING in there, folks. HT: Anderson picks up Penance and NAILS him with an over-the-shoulder armbreaker! That was DEVASTATING! RS: Wow, that move REALLY hurts. Penance's arm may be broken, but he appears to be getting back up. HT: And Anderson whips Penance into the corner-- he runs at him, but Penance moves out of the way! Anderson slams into the turnbuckle, and flips right out of the ring and onto the floor! RS: Anderson hit HARD on that concrete! Penance should make his move now, if he wants to win this match. HT: I agree. Anderson is staggering out on the floor, and Penance gets on the apron-- AND NAILS HIM WITH AN ASAI MOONSAULT! RS: Just when you think Penance is out, he comes right back. That was an amazing maneuver! HT: Well, Anderson is back up, but he's not looking very well. Penance enters the ring, runs off the ropes, AND FLIES OVER THE TOP ROPE INTO A CROSS BODY BLOCK! BUT ANDERSON CATCHES HIM! Adam Anderson lays Penance out with a backbreaker! RS: That was absolutely AMAZING! Jeez, that's probably going to be our "slam of the week", folks. HT: Do we even HAVE a "slam of the week"? Anyway, Anderson just pulled Penance back into the ring. He picks him up, holds him there-- and nails him with a power bomb! One.......Two.....Kickout! RS: Another amazing maneuver! Anderson is using EVERYTHING he's got to take out Penance, that's for sure. HT: Now Anderson pulls Penance to his feet, and NAILS him with a short-arm clothesline! He drops a leg on him and goes for the cover-- One.......Two......Kickout! RS: Another close count! Penance is really getting his ASS kicked, isn't he? After tonight, we might be seeing a new Light Heavyweight champion. HT: I'd love to see that, buddy. And Anderson LEVELS Penance with a series of left hands! He picks him up, and nails him with a German Suplex! One.......Two......Kickout! HT: Penance gets up, but gets NAILED again with a Dragon Suplex! Anderson isn't wasting any time, folks. RS: And he goes for another cover-- One.......Two......Kickout! RS: Penance is getting absolutely HUMILIATED in there! He just BARELY managed to kick out; this fast-paced action must be taking its toll on him. HT: Now Penance is whipped into the corner, but he stops-- AND WHAT IS HE PULLING OUT OF HIS TIGHTS?! RS: It looks like a pair of brass knuckles! HT: Penance turns around, AND PUNCHES ANDERSON IN THE HEAD WITH THE BRASS KNUCKLES! The referee rings the bell-- it's all over! (DING DING) RS: But Penance obviously didn't hit him hard enough, because Anderson just got right back up! Are we gonna see a brawl? HT: It sure looks that way, Roy. Anderson just jumped right on top of Penance, and he's swinging away! The two men roll to the outside of the ring.... RS: (laughing excitedly) Are we having fun yet? HT: Well, Penance sure isn't! Anderson just threw him OVER the timekeeper's table-- AND PENANCE LANDS ON A STEEL CHAIR! RS: Now Anderson dives right over the table, and lands on top of Penance! They're PUMMELING each other with lefts and rights.... HT: And security has finally come down to ringside, and they're trying to get some control. This situation really went too far, if you ask me. RS: But I DIDN'T ask you, Hank. Let's just go to the ring announcer for the official result of this match.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner, due to a disqualification, ADAM ANDERSON! However, a title cannot change hands on a disqualification, so Penance is STILL the Light Heavyweight champion. HT: Well, that was an excellent match, but I wish there had been a clean ending. These two men are probably going to meet again.... RS: Without a doubt. Adam Anderson isn't even officially in the AEWA right now, but the ICU is going to be looking for him. HT: Well, up next, Wild Thing is about to square off against "The Shotgun" Stan Crawford! This should be a great bout. RS: Yeah, I can hardly wait to watch Wild Thing kick that rookie's butt. He's gonna PUMMEL that poor punk! HT: But they've wrestled before, and Crawford easily won. How will tonight be any different? RS: Believe me, that match was pure luck. It WON'T happen again tonight, because Wild Thing is out for blood. RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, at a weight of 320 pounds, from New York City, here is "THE SHOTGUN" STAAAAAAAAAAN CRAWFORD! (Filter's "Hey Man Nice Shot" plays loudly as Crawford makes his way toward the ring, to a BIG pop from the crowd. He is dressed in a black leather jacket, and wears black tights.) HT: Stan Crawford has been on a roll lately, folks. He recently beat Primal Fear by countout, and now he's taking on the man he first wrestled, Wild Thing, a former Television champ. RS: Do I HAVE to repeat myself? Stan Crawford has had luck on his side lately, and that's ALL. He's gonna lose tonight.... HT: Well here comes Wild Thing now! RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing next, at a weight of 246 pounds, along with his manager, Bootknife Kelly, this is WIIIIIIIIIILD THIIIIIIING! (Nine Inch Nails' "Hurt" begins playing loudly, and Wild Thing makes his way to ringside. Wild Thing wears a black leather vest, and his bare chest is covered in scars. Bootknife Kelly was still at ringside from the previous match, of course, so he just waits at ringside.) RS: Wild Thing looks ready for action tonight, folks. He also appears to be drug-free right now, and that could help him out. HT: Are you kidding me? Look at those scars-- I wouldn't be a bit surprised if he's high on heroin. This guy shouldn't even be allowed to wrestle. RS: You don't have a CLUE what you're talking about, baldie. Let's just watch this freakin' match, okay? HT: There's the bell now.... They lock up; Crawford easily has the strength advantage, and catches Wild Thing in a side headlock! He takes Wild Thing to the mat! RS: Why did Wild Thing try to match strength with Crawford? He can't win a battle of strength with him, but he IS faster, and can out-wrestle him! That was his first mistake right there. HT: Now Crawford lifts Wild Thing high above his head-- AND HE DROPS HIM HARD TO THE MAT! Here's the cover.... One......Two..Kickout! HT: Crawford picks Wild Thing up, and NAILS him with an atomic drop! He follows that up with a BIG clothesline to the back of Wild Thing's head! "The Shotgun" isn't wasting any time, is he? RS: He seems to be in a hurry, Hank. I hate to admit it, but Crawford is really kicking Wild Thing's butt! HT: And Crawford whips Wild Thing to the ropes-- but he ducks his head too early, AND WILD THING PLANTS HIM TO THE MAT WITH A DDT! The cover.... One....Two...Kickout! HT: Crawford was getting careless, and he paid for it. Now Wild Thing takes him down with a side suplex-- and he follows it up with an elbowdrop! RS: If Wild Thing wants to win this match, he needs to capitalize on Crawford's weaknesses. This is the perfect opportunity.... HT: Wild Thing whips Crawford into the corner; he runs at him, and DRIVES a shoulder into Crawford's stomach! That HAD to hurt! RS: Wild Thing is the MASTER of cheap shots, and "the Shotgun" should have been prepared for that. Crawford is showing his inexperience right now, folks. HT: Good point. Now Wild Thing grabs Crawford, picks him up, and NAILS him with a side suplex! He goes for the cover-- One......Two....Kickout! HT: Now Wild Thing is back to his feet, and he's going up top! He climbs to the top turnbuckle, jumps, AND CLOBBERS CRAWFORD WITH A FLYING ELBOW! RS: Crawford is finally getting what he deserves; Wild Thing is about to finish him off. (yelling) Just ring the bell, referee! HT: It's not over YET, Roy. Now Wild Thing nails Crawford with a swinging neckbreaker, and he goes for the cover.... One.....Two...Kickout! RS: How the hell did Crawford kick out? What's wrong with that kid-- doesn't he know when to quit? HT: He has COURAGE, Roy, but you probably haven't ever heard of that. And now Wild Thing hits Crawford with an uppercut, sending him back down. RS: This is the beginning of the end, buddy. HT: Wild Thing is pulling Crawford back to his feet-- he sets up Crawford for a piledriver, BUT CRAWFORD REVERSES IT INTO A SMALL PACKAGE! One.......Two......Three! (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, at a time of ten minutes and fourteen seconds, here is your winner, "THE SHOTGUN" STAN CRAWFORD! HT: Stan Crawford did it again! He continues his undefeated streak here in the AEWA, and the crowd loves it! RS: Once again, Thompson, that was LUCK! HT: (laughing) I figured you'd say that. Anyway, it's now time for our main event! Primal Fear is about to square off against the Twilight Strangler! RS: These two guys are both maniacs, so I'm really looking forward to this match. HT: The last time they wrestled, Primal Fear didn't have any trouble beating him with that Bump Splash. Do you think history will repeat itself, Roy? RS: Lightning never strikes twice. Shane Matthews has been training the Strangler lately, and that might have a BIG effect on the match. HT: Good point. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, at a weight of 360 pounds, PRIMAL FEAR! (Primal Fear walks down the aisle, to a BIG pop from the crowd. He is wearing a black vest, with brown tights. As always, he has an extremely determined look on his face.) HT: They're finally going to settle the score, and Primal Fear looks ready. He's prepared for war, folks. RS: But as I already said, the Twilight Strangler has been training with Shane Matthews. That HAS to be an advantage. HT: I'd have to agree with you there, Roy. Here comes the Strangler now.... RING ANNOUNCER: Next, coming down the aisle, accompanied by Miss DeMeanor and Johnny Terror, this is THE TWILIGHT STRANGLER! (The Twilight Strangler walks to the ring, smiling. Miss DeMeanor and Johnny Terror follow him, locked arm-in-arm. They all get a HUGE round of boos from the crowd.) HT: The Twilight Strangler quickly attacks Primal Fear and levels him with a roundhouse right! They both go to the mat, punching each other! RS: They're ALREADY starting to brawl, folks-- that didn't take long. HT: The Twilight Strangler takes control and SLAMS Primal Fear hard down to the mat! He drops a knee on Primal Fear and goes for the cover! One......Two....Kickout! HT: They both get back up, and Primal Fear is whipped to the ropes.... The Twilight Stranger takes him over with a back body drop! RS: WOW! Shane Matthews must have done PLENTY of training with the Strangler, because I'm noticing a BIG difference. HT: And now the Strangler whips Primal Fear into the corner-- he runs in after him with a BIG clothesline! That had a lot of impact, folks. RS: Primal Fear is being absolutely HUMILIATED in there. HT: Unfortunately, I agree. The Strangler goes to pick Primal Fear up, but he can't do it! AND PRIMAL FEAR NAILS THE TWILIGHT STRANGLER WITH A BIG UPPERCUT! RS: I spoke too soon, Hank. HT: Yes, we both did. Now Primal Fear grabs the Strangler, picks him up, and SLAMS him with a vertical suplex! One....Two...Kickout! HT: Primal Fear appears to be a little frustrated, but he picks the Strangler back up-- AND NAILS HIM WITH A BELLY-TO-BACK SUPLEX! RS: And now he's giving the sign for the Bump Splash! If he connects with this move, the match is OVER-- no doubt about it. HT: He jumps, but the Strangler rolls out of the way-- AND PRIMAL FEAR FALLS DOWN HARD ON THE MAT! RS: YES! Now the Strangler goes for the cover.... HT: AND HE HAS A HANDFUL OF TIGHTS! The referee doesn't see it, and here's the count! One......Two........Three!! (DING DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, at a time of six minutes and twenty seconds, here is your winner, THE TWILIGHT STRANGLER! RS: Well, Shane Matthews must be doing a hell of a job with the Strangler, folks. Primal Fear didn't even stand a chance. HT: But it wasn't exactly FAIR, either. RS: Do you ALWAYS have to bring down the party, Hank? The Strangler kicked Primal Fear's fat ass, and EVERYBODY knows it. HT: Well, I'd love to have another argument with you, but the show's almost over. That's all the time we have for tonight, fans. For my broadcast partner, Roy Stanley, this is Hank Thompson saying "see ya next time"! RS: So I can go home now? There IS a God.
