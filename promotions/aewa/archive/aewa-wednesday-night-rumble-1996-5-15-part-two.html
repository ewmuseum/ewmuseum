Wednesday Night Rumble (5/15, part two)

16 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LB: What's next? MW: We're about to have an interview with "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews, the current AEWA Light Heavyweight champion. This should be nauseating.... (Shane Matthews is standing in the ring with his AEWA Light Heavyweight Title Belt. Johnny Terror is with him. He grabs the ring microphone) Matthews: You know, the competition here in the AEWA is really a joke! I waltz in here, and go straight to this baby, (points to title belt on his shoulder) The AEWA Light Heavyweight Championship of the World! Terror: That's right baby, no one can stop Sudden Death! You know how long he's gonna hold this belt? As long as he damn well wants to! Matthews: That about said it. You know, when I first came here, I KNEW the competition would be easy, but I never could've dreamed that it would be THIS easy! The Light Heavys here are a joke! In fact, they're scared of me! So scared, that no one challenged me to a title match this week! I guess that they're finally facing up to the truth, that Sudden Death is an unstoppable force, and they can't do a damn thing about it! But you know what? I'm not satisfied with just sitting here on my ass with this title belt. I'm a fighting champ, and there ain't a single Light Heavyweight in this pathetic excuse for a fed that can beat me, so I'll tell you what I'm gonna do. I'm issuing an open challenge right now, to any light heavyweight in the building, come on down, and get your shot at the gold! I don't care who you are or what you're ranked, all I know is that you are about to experience Sudden Death, and you are going DOWN! (Super Blonde suddenly charges out of the audience, and sneaks up behind Matthews) LB: What the heck is going on? What's he doing out here? MW: Super Blonde just nailed Matthews with an awesome uppercut! He's really on fire.... LB: We need some help in there! Can't that referee get any control? MW: The Blonde just shocked Matthews with a frankensteiner, and goes for the cover....One....Two....Three! (ding ding) LB: Are you kidding me? This wasn't even an official match, was it? RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner-- and new AEWA Light Heavyweight champion-- Super Blonde! MW: (cheering) Matthews just got his ass kicked! He doesn't even know what happened.... LB: The poor guy is hurt bad right now! Super Blonde must have used a foreign object or something. MW: I don't think so, Bertha! He only applied two moves, but that was enough.... LB: (crying) I can't believe what just happened! Was that actually legal? MW: Of course it was! Now Matthews is grabbing the microphone, and he looks pretty mad. MATTHEWS: (yelling very loudly) Super Blonde, that title doesn't belong to you! I never signed any damn contract.... My lawyers are going to have a field day on you, boy! Give me that belt back, or suffer the consequences! (Super Blonde grabs the microphone from him, and begins to yell) BLONDE: You want this belt? MY new title? Then come and get it! MATTHEWS: What's your problem? Are you afraid of experiencing "sudden death"? You attacked me from behind-- like a damn coward! There's nowhere to run now..... (The two men begin to brawl, and Super Blonde nails Matthews with the title. A group of security guards finally break it up) LB: That was horrible, Wilson! Those two guys are going totally insane! MW: Before tonight, they didn't even know each other.... That has certainly changed now! LB: What's next? MW: Coming up, we've got a match between two men who on Saturday, were a tag team. Primal Fear and Big Ron Raider. LB: Yeah, they were in that elimination match with Twisted Steel and Lord Humongous, Raider accidentally clotheslined Primal Fear during that match, causing him to get pinned, and eliminated. Primal Fear wasn't too happy about that. MW: No he wasn't and that's why they're here tonight. We won't see much technical wrestling in this one, but it should be one heck of a brawl. Let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from the Badlands, Sudan. He weighs in at 360 pounds and is accompanied by his manager, "The Beautiful" Miss DeMeanor, Primal Feeeeeeeeeeeeeeear! (Primal Fear makes his entrance to a loud chorus of boos. He is tearing apart a large slab of raw meat and Miss DeMeanor follows closely behind him.) MW: I will never get used to that disgusting habit. LB: What disgusting habit? MW: The "eating raw meat" disgusting habit. Perhaps you've seen Primal Fear do it before? LB: Oh, THAT disgusting habit. I thought you were talking about some OTHER disgusting habit. MW: (frustrated) Why do I bother? RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from the Rocky Mountains. He weighs in at 416 pounds and is accompanied by his manager, Million Dollar Mark, Big...Ron... Raiiiiiiiiiiideeeeeeeeeeer! ("Saturday Night Special" begins to play as Raider makes his entrance, also to a large round of boos. Million Dollar Mark is at his side.) LB: And Raider looks prepared. MW: Looks can be deceiving. LB: Not with Raider, He's just as mean as he looks. MW: Usually. We'll see how prepared for this madman he REALLY is in just a minute. The two mastodons meet in the center of the ring, and lock up. It's Raider with the upper hand as he whips Primal Fear to the ropes. On the comeback, CLOTHESLINE! LB: But Primal Fear does not go down! MW: Raider grabs Primal Fear by the throat, he's choking him! He lifts him up, CHOKESLAM! How did he get Primal Fear up like that? The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Raider is just amazing! But Primal Fear is far from done. MW: Raider picks up Primal Fear, and whips him to the turnbuckle--NO! Reversed by Primal Fear! Raider to the buckle, Primal Fear charges, BUT RAIDER PULLS UP A KNEE! Primal Fear is staggered! Raider charges, SHORT LARIAT! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! MW: Raider, not letting up, picks up Primal Fear, POWERSLAM! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Primal Fear is one tough competitor. Raider's throwing his best stuff at him and he's not going down. MW: Raider runs to the ropes, BIG SPLASH--BUT PRIMAL FEAR ROLLS AWAY! AND RAIDER HITS THE MAT HARD! Primal Fear up, and he drops a big elbow on Raider! He picks Raider up, on the comeback, POWERSLAM! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: What a quick turnaround by Primal Fear! MW: And Million Dollar Mark doesn't like it, he's yelling at the ref! Wait a minute, Raider just pulled something out of his tights! It's a pair of brass knuckles! He winds up, and CLOCKS Primal Fear with them! LB: What brilliant strategy! MW: Raider goes up top, MOONSAULT! The cover! LB: But the ref is still arguing with Mark! MW: Well, when you play with fire, sometimes you get burned. Raider gets upset, and yells at the ref. The ref turns around, and Raider places Primal Fear on the top rope. He climbs up, BELLY-TO BELLY SUPERPLEX! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: But Primal Fear will NOT stay down! MW: Raider picks him up, BODYSLAM--NO! Primal Fear is biting him! Raider falls back, and Primal Fear falls on top of him! The cover! One..Two..Foot on the ropes! LB: But Primal Fear is still hungry! MW: Yes, He's digging his teeth into the head of Raider! Raider's busted open! And Primal Fear is STILL chewing away! This man is SICK! Primal Fear gets up, and starts putting the boots to Raider! LB: Primal Fear has that crazed look in his eye! Raider's in trouble! MW: Primal Fear drops a big elbow on Raider! Now he's up again, BUMP SPLASH! He nailed it! The cover! One..Two..And Million Dollar Mark places Raider's foot on the ropes! MW: Primal Fear didn't like that, he's going after Mark! And Mark NAILS Primal Fear with a chair as he is coming through the ropes! The ref saw it! He's calling for the bell! (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner, as a result of a disqualification... PRIMAL FEAR! LB: Why'd the ref disqualify him now? They've been cheating this whole match! What a crappy way to end such a great brawl! MW: Well, we might've had a conclusive end to the brawl if Mark hadn't put Raider's foot on the ropes earlier in the match. Don't blame the ref, he's just doing his job, blame Mark. LB: Oh shut up, Wilson! MW: Anyway, we've got more action coming up. Up next, we've got a match between Volcano Jackson and Wild Thing. As you know, Wild Thing's interference cost Volcano Jackson the TV title on Saturday. LB: Yes, Wild Thing finally realized that Jackson was ducking him, and he decided to do something about it. MW: Jackson was not ducking him, there are many worthy competitors out there, and Wild Thing just had to wait his turn. He already had a shot a couple of weeks ago anyway. LB: But it was inconclusive, He deserved another shot, and Jackson wouldn't give him one, that's what I call ducking the competition. MW: Let's just go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, weighing in at 279 pounds and accompanied by Stan Parsons AND Bootknife Kelly, Wiiiiiiiiiiild Thing! ("Sweet Home Alabama" begins to play on the PA as Wild Thing makes his entrance, to a mostly negative crowd response. Bootknife Kelly and Stan Parsons walk behind him, arguing and trying to tell each other to go back to the locker room.) MW: What's this? Can't Wild Thing make up his mind about who he wants to be his manager? LB: Yeah, he should dump Parsons. MW: Why? I think that Stan Parsons is a GREAT manager. He should dump that snake in the grass-- Kelly. LB: Kelly helps his guys win, he's very crafty, can Parsons say that? MW: No, and that's why he's the better manager. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 290 pounds and accompanied by his manager, Aaron Proctor, He is the 2-time former AEWA Television champion, Volcanoooooooooooooooo Jackson! (Queen's "Another One Bites the Dust" begins to play as Volcano Jackson makes his entrance to a huge crowd pop. Aaron Proctor is at his side.) MW: And Jackson has been waiting to get his hands on Wild Thing since Wednesday. He's running to that ring. He slides through the ropes, and IMMEDIATELY goes on the attack. LB: This isn't fair! The bell hasn't even rung yet! MW: (sarcastically) Gee, that's too bad. Jackson is pounding away, BACK HEEL KICK! Jackson puts Wild Thing's head between his legs, pulls back on his arms, DOUBLE UNDERHOOK PILEDRIVER! LB: Come on Kelly, get your man together! MW: Jackson goes down, and puts Wild Thing in a leg scissors submission! LB: But Wild Thing was able to get his arms in there before Jackson could cinch it in! MW: You're right, and Wild Thing is powering out of it! He's done it! Now Wild Thing takes Jackson over with a fireman's carry. He grabs Jackson, GUTWRENCH SUPLEX! And Wild Thing runs to the ropes, Jackson to his feet, FLYING BODYPRESS BY WILD THING. The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: He almost had him there! MW: Now Wild Thing whips Jackson to the corner, he follows him in, AVALANCHE! Wild Thing whips Jackson to the ropes, FLYING FOREARM! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: Jackson won't go down yet, and Bootknife Kelly is screaming instructions at Wild Thing. MW: So is Stan Parsons, now they're screaming at each other! But Wild Thing is still on the attack in there. He picks Jackson up, REVERSE NECKBREAKER! He picks Jackson up again, and places him on the top turnbuckle! Wild Thing climbs the buckle, takes a double underhook, DOUBLE UNDERHOOK SUPERPLEX! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: What does he have to do to put Jackson away? MW: I don't know, but he's really frustrated now. HE'S CHOKING JACKSON! Parsons is yelling at him to stop! LB: But Kelly is ORDERING him not to stop! MW: He's not letting up! The ref threatens DQ, and Wild Thing FINALLY releases the choke! Now Wild Thing picks Jackson up, and PRESSES him over his head! He goes to the ropes, and THROWS Jackson to the outside and into the steel guard rail! Wild Thing follows him to the outside. LB: This is where it gets fun! MW: Wild Thing is pulling away that protective mat. What is he going to do? LB: Don't tell me you're THAT stupid, Wilson. MW: Now Wild Thing picks Jackson up, AND PILEDRIVES HIM ON THE CONCRETE! Now Wild Thing picks Jackson up, he places Jackson over his shoulder. Stan Parsons is telling him to throw Jackson back in the ring! LB: But Kelly is telling him to keep him out here! MW: Wild Thing runs, and RAMS Volcano Jackson into the ringpost! Kelly just handed Wild Thing a chair, but Parsons is trying to take it away from him! Wild Thing YANKS the chair away from Parsons, and SHOVES him out of the way! LB: Yes! MW: Wild Thing winds up, and LEVELS Jackson with that chair! I can't believe that the ref is allowing this! LB: He's not, take a look at Kelly! MW: You're right! Kelly must've started an argument with the ref while Parsons was yelling at Wild Thing! The ref didn't see that assault with the chair! Now Wild Thing places Jackson on a Table, and climbs up on the apron! LB: He's gonna execute the Party Time Splash through the table! I love it! MW: But Parsons just stepped between Wild Thing and that table! Wild Thing is yelling at him to move, but he won't budge! Parsons is telling him to go back in the ring! LB: But here comes Kelly! MW: Yes, Kelly runs, and PUSHES Parsons out of the way! Wild Thing dives, BUT JACKSON MOVES! Wild Thing CRASHED through that table! HE IS HURT! LB: If Parsons would've just let him go, Jackson wouldn't have had time to move! MW: Jackson climbs back in the ring. Now Wild Thing slowly crawls to the ring! He slides in, and slowly gets to his feet, but Jackson's on him. Jackson doubles him over with a kick to the midsection, now he picks him up, ERUPTION POWER BOMB! The cover! One..Two..Three!!!!! (DING, DING) LB: It's all Parsons' fault! RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner... VOLCANO JACKSON!!!!!!!! MW: That was amazing! Wild Thing was dominating! He was going full force and pulling all the stops, but he made one mistake, and Jackson capitalized on it! LB: HE didn't make the mistake, PARSONS made the mistake! If he didn't get in the way, Jackson would've went through that table, NOT Wild Thing! MW: Parsons is Wild Thing's manager, he was giving him advice. If he would've taken that advice, and not jumped, then NOBODY would've went through that table! LB: You always have to get technical, don't you? MW: Yes. LB: So what's next, anyway? MW: Well, The Punisher is defending his newly won TV Title against 3-time former World champion, Mister Crowley. LB: Why do we have to keep putting two guys that I like against each other? MW: To confuse and annoy you. LB: That's what I thought! MW: Let's just go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA Television Title. Introducing first, the challenger, weighing in at 328 pounds, he is the three-time former AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World... Mister Crowleeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeey! (The arena turns black, and bells are heard, as Mister Crowley makes his entrance, to a shower of boos) LB: Mr. Crowley just doesn't look right without some gold around his waist. MW: Well he has held the World Title more times than any other competitor here in the AEWA. LB: Of course he has, he's practically unstoppable. MW: The key word there is "practically." RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 301 pounds and accompanied by his manager, Bootknife Kelly, He is the AEWA Television champion... The Punisheeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeer! (The music from "Star Wars" plays as the Punisher makes his entrance. He holds the TV Belt aloft and Bootknife Kelly is at his side.) LB: The Punisher looks determined not to lose that belt in his first defense! He is ready. MW: But Mister Crowley is a force to be reckoned with here in the AEWA. He has achieved more than any other wrestler. LB: I didn't say that he wouldn't lose, I just said that he looked determined, which he does, right? MW: I guess so. LB: So there. MW: Both men are in the ring now and we're underway. Punisher confronts Crowley, and SLAPS him across the face! LB: Bad Idea! MW: Mister Crowley is upset! He picks up Punisher, GORILLA PRESS! And DROPS him like a bad habit! Punisher up, and Mister Crowley whips him to the ropes--NO, Punisher reverses it! Crowley comes off the ropes, CLOTHESLINE--DUCKED BY PUNISHER! Punisher turns around, AND DELIVERS A KNEE TO THE BACK OF CROWLEY! LB: Punisher's actually doing pretty good, I'm proud of him. MW: Okay, but we're less than a minute into the match. Punisher going for a side suplex, BUT CROWLEY BLOCKS IT! Crowley now, GUTWRENCH SUPLEX! LB: There's something going on at ringside! Someone just came out from under the ring! MW: IT'S CARETAKER! Has he been under there all night? Anyway, Crowley whips Punisher to the ropes--reversal by Punisher! Crowley to the ropes, AND CARETAKER PULLS DOWN THE TOP ROPE! HE'S ATTACKING CROWLEY! The ref's calling for the bell (DING, DING). LB: It's over already? RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner, as the result of a disqualification... MISTER CROWLEY!! MW: Now that was COMPLETELY uncalled for! The match had barely begun when Caretaker interfered. He didn't want to keep Punisher from losing the title, he wanted to keep Crowley from winning the title! This guy HATES Mister Crowley! LB: And they're still going at it! Punisher doesn't seem to mind though, as he quickly gets out of there with his title. MW: And now we've got several wrestlers and officials out there to separate the two, and they're escorting them back to the locker room! Caretaker cheated Mister Crowley out of his TV title shot, and Mister Crowley is upset. They are both being heavily restrained from tearing into each other! LB: Crowley didn't really want that belt anyway. Once you've been World champ, you can't go back and be TV champ! It just doesn't feel right! You gotta win it ALL! MW: I suppose you're right, but we were still cheated out of that title match, and Caretaker should NOT have intervened, no matter HOW much he hates Crowley, but let's move on. LB: Yes, Let's. Up next, the World "chump", Ben Slocum is taking on the Head of the Church of the Pounding Light, Cardinal Chaos! MW: Yes. Cardinal Chaos is a former World Champion, in fact, he was the first AEWA World Champion. But Ben Slocum has shown to be a great champion in the short time that he has had the title, it should be a very interesting matchup, let's go to the ring! RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA World Heavyweight title! Introducing first, the challenger, from the Church of the Pounding Light, he weighs in at 313 pounds and is a former AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World... Cardinal Chaaaaaaaaaaaaaos! (The Rolling Stones' "Sympathy for the Devil" starts up on the PA as the hated Cardinal Chaos makes his entrance to a chorus of boos.) LB: And here comes the Cardinal. MW: Ben Slocum's gonna teach that no good "Cardinal" a lesson that he won't soon forget. LB: Are you insane? The Cardinal's gonna tear Slocum apart! MW: I highly doubt that. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Austin, Texas, weighing in at 302 pounds and accompanied by his sister, Sondra, He is the AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, "The Modern Day Warrior" Ben Sloooooooooooooocum. (Laura Branigan's "Self Control" starts up on the PA as Slocum makes his entrance to a HUGE crowd pop. He and Sondra high-five the fans all the way to the ring.) LB: I guess the rodeo's in town again. MW: What are you talking about? LB: Well take a look at that cow! MW: Are you referring to Sondra? She does not, in any way resemble a cow! She is a very attractive young lady. LB: That's YOUR opinion. MW: You know, I'm REALLY starting to miss Big Daddy. LB: Too bad, cause he's not here, and I am. MW: Oh goodie. Both men are in the ring now, and Cardinal Chaos has finished his traditional pre-match "prayer". They lock up, and the Champ whips Cardinal Chaos to the ropes. On the comeback, Cardinal Chaos FLOORS Slocum with a BIG boot to the face! He's going for the pin already! One..Two..Shoulder Up! MW: He can't pin Slocum that quickly! LB: Sure he can, but he's just toying with him. MW: If that's what you want to believe. Chaos back on Slocum, locks him in an STF! LB: It's over! MW: Not exactly, they're too close to the ropes, and Slocum has already got a hold of them. Chaos breaks. He waits for Slocum to get to his feet, AND DELIVERS A BRUTAL KNEE TO THE BACK! LB: Look at the way the Cardinal is dominating your precious champion. He doesn't have a chance! MW: The Cardinal steps through the ropes and is on the edge of the apron! Slocum gets to his feet! SLINGSHOT BY THE CARDINAL--BUT SLOCUM SIDE STEPS! AND THE CARDINAL HITS THE MAT HARD, Slocum covers him! One..Two..Kickout! MW: Both men up, and Chaos knees Slocum in the back again! Chaos whips the champ to the ropes, FLYING CLOTHESLINE! Now he's going for that slingshot again, THIS TIME HE NAILS IT! Chaos picks him up, HE'S GONNA GORILLA PRESS HIM!--BUT SLOCUM REVERSES INTO A ROLLING REVERSE CRADLE! One..Two..Rollover! LB: Cardinal Chaos reversed it! One..Two..Kickout! MW: Slocum up now, and he takes Chaos down with a Hotshot! Chaos up, and he knees Slocum in the back! He picks him up, HE'S GOING FOR THE GORILLA PRESS AGAIN!--BUT SLOCUM COUNTERS WITH A CRADLE AGAIN!!! One..Two..Kickout! LB: You'd think he woulda learned about that move! MW: Chaos up, and NAILS Slocum with a roundhouse right! Chaos is going for the Inverted Crucifix! LB: NOW it's over! MW: But Slocum starts kicking away at Chaos! And he let's go! LB: HE HAD HIM! MW: Slocum jumps on Chaos' back. SLEEPERHOLD! But Chaos staggers back into the corner, and CRUSHES Slocum! LB: Look at how hopeless and pathetic Slocum is! MW: Chaos now, he's kicking his boot against the ground, what is he doing? HE APPEARS TO BE LOADING IT! Now Slocum staggers out of that corner, and Chaos NAILS him with a kick to the midsection with that loaded boot! Slocum DOUBLES OVER IN PAIN! LB: His boot is NOT loaded! He just has a very strong kick! MW: Yeah right. Now Slocum is bent over, Chaos runs up to him, AND KICKS HIM IN THE HEAD LIKE HE'S PUNTING A FOOTBALL! SLOCUM IS OUT COLD! NOW YOU CAN'T TELL ME THAT HIS BOOT'S NOT LOADED! Chaos picks him up, INVERTED CRUCIFIX! The count! One..Two..Three!!!!!! (DING, DING) LB: HE GOT HIM!! RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner, and NEEEEEEEEEEEW AEWA Heavweight Champion of the World... CARDINAL CHAOS!!!!!!!! MW: Well that just makes me sick! Cardinal Chaos just stole the World Title from Ben Slocum! LB: He did not STEAL it! He won it fair and square! You have no proof that his boot was loaded! MW: Ben Slocum was knocked COLD by a kick in the head! I'd say that's proof enough. LB: Not me, and look, now here come all the members of the Church to celebrate! What a beautiful picture! MW: Not really. In fact, let's get out of here before we have to see any more of this. We've had an interesting show, to say the least, so for the Lovely Bertha, this is Matt Wilson saying so long everybody! (fade to black)
