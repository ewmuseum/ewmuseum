Sunday, March 30th, Card (1/3)

30 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last week on Championship Wrestling Association of America *************************************************************************** Eric Lane: Now Thomas rolls him over- hammerlock on the mat. Valachi in obvious pain, and Marc Thomas in complete control right now. Thomas applying more pressure on that arm now, and Valachi kicking the mat in pain and frustration. Now Thomas stands him up- and sends him for the ride- knee in the midsection. Valachi doubled over- and Thomas with a ddt- the cover... 1... 2... kickout! Thomas picks him up- side suplex. Lateral press- hooks the tights... 1... 2... kickout! Thomas backs him into a corner- drives his shoulder into the midsection- snap mare out of the corner. Thomas with a superkick now, and he waits for Valachi to get back up- Icebreaker! (i.e. Diamond Cutter) EL: The cover- again hooks the tights- 1... 2... Valachi gets his boot over the bottom rope. We're starting to run out of television time here- I can tell you now that our special feature match next week will see Marc Thomas and Jak Hammer in tag team action against Joey Haven and a mystery partner, and we should have more information on the Barnes-Robertson match in England, and perhaps footage from AEWA Extreme where Haven and Hammer go falls-count-anywhere. Thomas stands Valachi in the corner- chop across the chest- cross corner whip- reversed- Valachi whips Thomas into the corner- Stinger splash- no one home- Valachi bounces out to mid ring- folks, I'm sorry, but we are out of time and this match is over- Icebreaker again- there's the bell- we just plum ran out of time- we'll see you next week! ************************************************************************* Eric Lane: Hello, everyone, and welcome to another edition of Championship Wrestling Association of America. I'm your host, Eric Lane, and we are here in the world-famous Sportatorium in Dallas, Texas, and we have a full slate of action here tonight, and hopefully we won't run out of time like last week. As you just saw, last week's special feature bout between Marc Thomas and AEWA World Light Heavyweight Champion "Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi was just heating up as time was winding down, and I honestly believe that with just a few more minutes, we would have seen a new champion crowned. No titles are on the line this week, but next week we will have all three titles decided. "Three?", you ask. Well, after tonight, there will be just two teams left in the running for the CWAA Tag Team championship, and those two teams will meet next week. Plus, "The Dreamer", Randy Sheradin, will make his first-ever CWAA Heavyweight Title defense, and he steps into the ring one more time with "The Professional" Max Robertson. And the rest of the CWAA's competitors climb into the ring for a battle royal to crown our first television champion next week, as well.ÊBut first, we have the second round of the tag team title tournament to complete, and when we get back, Randy Sheradin and Joshua Barnes will square off against "Marvelous" Michael and "Pretty Boy" Byron. (Tuesday's at 8:00, WYXZ TV 39 presents Bob Newhart as the captain of a cruise ship in "Love Boat: The Next Generation"! This week, see Norman Fell, Gary Coleman, Florence Henderson, Dana Plato and many more!) EL: We're back, and ready for semifinal action in the CWAA Tag Team title tourney. Let's go up to ring announcer David Myers. David Myers: This contest is set for one fall with a 20-minute time limit. On their way to the ring now, at a combined weight of 497 lb. "Marvelous" Michael Anderson and "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey. (Boos fills the arena as "Sharp Dresses Man" plays and the men strut their way down the aisle, Anderson in his red tights and sequined robe, McNultey in the blue tights and red robe. As they pass Eric Lane, McNultey is head to say to him, "Sorry, son- no autographs.") (As McNultey and Anderson continue to strut around/dance in the ring, removing their robes in a striptease fashion.) DM: And their opponents, weighing in at 462 lb., Joshua Barnes and CWAA Heavyweight Champion, "The Dreamer" Randy Sheradin! (Ecstatic popping ensures as "Dream On" by Aerosmith and the Philadelphia Philharmonic plays and the big, shy Indiana ex-bouncer follows the champ.) DM: Your referee for this contest, Jeff Howard. EL: OK- Randy Sheradin looking to add to his gold collection as he and Joshua Barnes face off against one of the most hated duos in Texas. There's the bell, and it looks like Joshua Barnes and Michael Anderson will start for their respective teams. Anderson and Barnes circle one another, Anderson motions for a test of strength- finger lock. And Barnes with the upper hand. (Crowd laughs at the comically-anguished expression on Anderson's face as he is driven to his knees.) EL: And Anderson sticks a foot out through the ropes, and senior official Jeff Howard calls for a break. Barnes with a clean break- now we have a collar and elbow tie-up- Anderson with a handful of hair pulling Barnes into a neutral corner- Jeff Howard calling for the break- and Anderson with a pop in the mush for Joshua Barnes. And Joshua Barnes spins him around and cocks a fist- Anderson covers his face, and Barnes goes downstairs. Anderson clutches his abdomen- and Barnes with a shot to the face! Anderson covers his face- and Barnes goes downstairs again! And here comes Byron McNultey- and Barnes with a uppercut for the "Pretty Boy"- and he wheels around and sends Michael Anderson to the floor. Now he whips Byron McNultey into the ropes- and Randy Sheradin in with a dropkick, and McNultey follows Anderson to the floor! (Big pop as Barnes and Sheridan stand alone in the ring.) EL: Michael Anderson climbing back up those ring steps- being a sharp dressed man has not helped one iota yet. Anderson and Barnes lock up again- Anderson with a single leg takedown- elbow drop- he misses! Barnes waits on him- scoops him up- slam! Here comes McNultey- Barnes catches him- slam again! (Another big pop) EL: This one has not gone well for the McNultey-Anderson duo tonight. Joshua Barnes tags in Randy Sheradin as Anderson and McNultey talk things over. It looks like the "Pretty Boy" will try his hand with the champ. Sheridan with an armdrag takedown- and he lands in front of Joshua Barnes. A confused McNultey looks for a tag- and Barnes tags him all right. (A big night for big pops) EL: McNultey back up- and Sheradin with a hammerlock. McNultey to the ropes- clean break. Sheradin with an Irish whip- cartwheel kick! ("Oooh! Aaah!") EL: Now Sheradin tags Barnes- and before he goes snap suplexes the Pretty Boy over- and Barnes with an elbow drop... 1... 2... kickout. Barnes with an Irish whip- a bib clothesline, and McNultey does a 360-flip! Barnes off the ropes now- and Anderson sticks a knee right in his kidney! Barnes slowed by the shot to his back- McNultey with a kick in the midsection- now he sets him up- swinging neckbreaker! McNultey tags in Anderson- we have to take a quick break- we'll have more of this semifinal tag team action when we return! (The Psychic Friends network...only $2.99 per minute! You must be 18 years or older...) EL: OK- Michael Anderson in control of this one right now- he sets Barnes up- backbreaker- right across the knee. The cover... 1... 2... kickout. Anderson tags in McNultey- the Pretty Boy with a spinning toe hold. And- wait a second! I see "the Professional", Max Robertson coming down the aisle- he grabs Sheridan by the tights and pulls him to the floor- and slams his head onto the ring apron! Referee Jeff Howard checking for a Barnes submission as Robertson now whips the Dreamer into the ring steps! And here comes Twister! (Pop for Travis Conley and Paul Langford, followed by Mother Nature) EL: McNultey tags Anderson back in who snap suplexes Barnes over. Travis Conley in pursuit of a fleeing Max Robertson up the aisle as the Tornado tends to the fallen Sheridan. McNultey now walking along the ring apron and kicks at Langford. Now Paul Langford goes after Byron McNultey- Travis Conley comes back and joins in the chase- Joshua Barnes clotheslines Anderson- heads to his corner to tag- there's no one there! Sheradin still on the floor, Anderson on the mat- Twister chasing McNultey around the ring- and Joshua Barnes and Jeff Howard are two confused men. McNultey rolls in under the bottom rope- Langford right behind him- and he catches the Pretty Boy- Irish whip- back body drop! Senior official Jeff Howard calling for the bell as Langford airplane spins the Pretty Boy, and Travis Conley stands up Marvelous Michael in the corner- Joshua Barnes has just found his partner out on the floor. Langford continues the Tornado drop on McNultey- Conley working over Anderson- we've got two too many people out here at ringside- David Myers is one brave man as he heads into the ring- let's hear the official decision. DM: Ladies and gentleman, the referee has rules that, as the result of a disqualification, your winners are "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey and "Marvelous" Michael Anderson! (Boos rain down as "Sharp Dressed Man" starts and the winners make a quick exit and Joshua Barnes and Randy Sheradin re-enter the ring to question the referee. Sheradin has a small cut on his forehead, and Barnes' expression betrays his incredulity.) EL: OK- the referee has disqualified Joshua Bar- wait a second. Mother Nature grabs the house mike from Dave Myers. Mother Nature: Randy and Joshua, We're sorry this incident had to happen. But we're tired of those two cheating to win, and I guess our tempers just got the better of us. I'll tell you what we'll do to make it up to you, though. When we take down Midnight Rampage tonight, and those two pretty boys next week to take the titles, we will give you two the first shot at the belts the following week. It would be great for the fans to see two great teams in a fair match. Deal? (Conley and Langford then hold out their hands to Barnes and Sheridan.) EL: Barnes looks like he's asking them if they were the ones who jumped his partner, and they're pointing up the aisle, and Sheradin is saying something- looks like he simply said, "Max." And Joshua Barnes is out of there! Randy Sheradin quickly slaps hands with Langford and Conley and takes off after his partner. When we come back, Twister and Midnight Rampage fight to see who goes to the finals next week with McNultey and Anderson.
