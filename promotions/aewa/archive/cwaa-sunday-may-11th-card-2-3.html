Sunday, May 11th, Card (2/3)

11 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EL: OK- the situation about the TV title and just who is the rightful contender is starting to spill over and involve more and more people- now we have a tag team match between "Preacher" Kore Wrench, who has really taken the measure of the champ more than once, and his new ally, Little Yvette, and Jak Hammer, who last week also took it to Lt. Lawson, and his partner in crime Marc Thomas. Let's go to David Myers for the introductions. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is set for one fall with a thirty-minute time limit. Introducing first- ("Bad to the Bone" plays and the crowd expresses its disapproval.) DM: -from Detroit, Michigan, weighing in at 310 lb., Jak Hammer! And his partner, from Charlotte, North Carolina, at 240 lb., Marc Thomas! (Hammer has a Sidish build, and dresses in black leather pants, black boots, and a black v-neck leather sleeveless top (making him the unofficial champion of attire-modifiers) and black fingerless gloves. His 310 lb. body stretches to 6'10" tall. His partner is diminutive by comparison , at a mere 6'0". Black appears to be the team's officiak colors, as Thomas, with his Benoitish body, dons black boots, black trunks, and a sleeveless leather jacket.) Marc Thomas: (taking Myers' microphone) Tonight, we have to face Wrench and his little friend Yvette. Neither one of these wrestlers has presented a problem for us in the past. In fact, Hammer has destroyed both of them in the last two weeks! Wrench, we've met before and I know what you are capable of and Yvette, just because you are new doesn't mean we'll be easy on you. We are here for only one reason, to show who the best wrestlers in the CWAA are. Hammer would have that TV title around his waist now if it weren't for the Major and that flagpole. When we beat Wrench and Yvette tonight, I demand that one or both of us get a title shot. Right now, we are the two hottest wrestlers in the CWAA and if the Professional or Lawson or the tag champs feel up to it, then sign a contract for a title match. Otherwise, there will be hell to pay!!! Jak Hammer: Wrench and Yvette, I've beat both of you and tonight will be no different. Lawson, I want another shot at you and that belt, except this time, it will be you and me without any interference. Get in the ring with me again and I'll make boot camp look like a walk in the park. (Hammer drops the mike at Myers' feet) DM: And their opponents- (Gregorian Chant plays and the crowd erupts in a frenzy of cheers) DM: -from Seattle, Washington, at 250 lb., Little Yvette! And from New York, New York, at 275 lb., he is "The Preacher" Kore Wrench! Your referee for this bout, Chris Herman. (I have already described these two elsewhere...) EL: OK- Little Yvette looks raring to go, but first Kore Wrench is, as is his habit, performing a small act of benediction on one of the youngsters in the crowd. All right it looks like we're all set. All four men in the ring now, and Chris Herman will have his hands full. A lot of distrust, bad blood, between these combatants, and no one looks ready to leave the ring until one of his opponents does. Chris Herman really wants to get it down to one on one to start- OK. Looks like Jak Hammer and Kore Wrench to kick things off. Of course, they met here in the ring last week, and that mysterious masked man interfered- some think it may be Marc Thomas, but we just had him out here- collar and elbow tie up- out here a few minutes ago, and if it is him- Jak Hammer with a handful of the Preacher's flowing locks, pulls him back to the ropes- if it is him, then Marc Thomas is not only a crack- Chris Herman calls for a clean break- and Jak Hammer with a shove, and the Preacher shoves him right to the mat! (Pop) EL: Jak Hammer on the mat, and the Preacher stands over him, fists cocked! Like I said, if that masked man is Marc Thomas, not only is he a crack wrestler, he's also a quick change artist. Another lock up- and a nice hip toss by the Preacher. Back up- arm drag sends Jak Hammer right back down. Hammer charges- clothesline by the Preacher, and he has some huge arms, maybe the biggest in the CWAA, and Jak Hammer is going to take a little break. (Jeers from the front row ticket buyers on the side of the ring where Jak Hammer paces on the floor shaking his head.) EL: Chris Herman counting, and Marc Thomas, a real crafty one, breaks the count by instigating some sort of discussion with the referee. OK- Jak Hammer back in there now. A lot at stake in this contest, but only unofficially. Wrench and Hammer and Thomas are really three of the top contenders for the CWAA TV Title, and, by extension, the CWAA Heavyweight Title, and should one pull of the victory over another in this one, it will give their demands for title shots that much more credibility. Of course, Little Yvette is just starting out- Jak Hammer tags out- but making some big waves already, with that impressive upset win over Mark Sherrick a week ago. Thomas in there now- lock up- knee lift by Thomas. And a forearm uppercut on the chin, and the Preacher may have had a filling or two knocked loose. Now a hip toss on the stunned Preacher, into an arm bar. And Thomas tags back out, and Jak Hammer with just a vicious stomp on the Preacher's extended arm as Thomas holds it out. Scoops him up, slam by Jak Hammer. And so far Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas working very effectively as a team. This is not the first time we have seen them team together like this, but they seem to mesh like a real veteran team. Jak Hammer tags back out, as he and Marc Thomas keep nice and fresh, taking their turns in there with the Preacher. Marc Thomas bounces off the ropes- elbow drop, right in the sternum. (Little Yvette starts rhythmically stomping his feet, and the crowd chants in time with "Go, Kore, Go!" Don't look so surprised.) EL: Thomas hooks him up- snap suplex! What an impact! Thomas up now- where is he going? Ooh- a hard slap right on the cheek for Little Yvette, and Yvette into the ring now- Chris Herman is not going to allow this. Jak Hammer now in behind the ref's back- holds Kore Wrench still- and Marc Thomas with a big superkick! (Crowd boos) EL: Jak Hammer now back into the corner while his partner covers- he's got the tights, too- 2... kickout. The Preacher isn't done yet, but he really needs to get his partner in there. Thomas tags out, and Jak Hammer, making sure he is between Wrench and Yvette, slaps on a front facelock. Jak Hammer obviously knows that the Preacher needs to be softened up, worn down, a little more. Awesome power that Jak Hammer has, and a front facelock is a deceptively painful hold. And here comes Little Yvette to his partner's rescue! (Yvette smacks Hammer with a double axe-handle and is promptly escorted to his corner by Chris Herman- so....) EL: And Marc Thomas in the ring now behind Chris Herman's back- spin kick on the Preacher! And the Preacher slumps to the mat, Jak Hammer to the corner- there was no tag! Marc Thomas now with a side suplex- cover... 1... 2... Wrench gets a foot on the bottom rope- well, Jak Hammer kicked it back off, but Chris Herman saw it was there. Marc Thomas picks his foe up- Irish whip- reversed- and a claw! (Pop) EL: The Preacher catches him in a clawhold! Marc Thomas screaming in pain as the Preacher cranks the pressure up- and Jak Hammer with a kick in the lower back breaks it up. And here comes Little Yvette- clothesline from the top rope on Jak Hammer! (Big pop) EL: He just dropped right out of the sky on Jak Hammer- no one saw him coming, he was that quick! Yvette and Wrench up now- double clothesline on Jak Hammer! Turn around- double clothesline on Marc Thomas! (Big pop) EL: And Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas look like they've had enough. Hammer and Thomas out on the floor trying to shake it off. Chris Herman starts the count as Little Yvette heads back to his corner. OK- now he tags in legally. Marc Thomas climbs back into the ring now. All that time working over the Preacher and now they get a completely fresh opponent. Little Yvette with a hip toss- that boy is just so quick. Now an armdrag takedown! Marc Thomas is one of the real veterans of the sport compared to a lot of other CWAA stars, but Little Yvette, such a newcomer, really taking the match to him. Arm bar on Yvette- Thomas kips up and reverses it. Thomas with the arm bar- backflip by Yvette- now he reverses it- very ni- ow! Marc Thomas just short clotheslined his way right of it! All those nice acrobatics, and Thomas just decks him. Tag to Jak Hammer. Jak with a forearm shiver in the lower back. Classic Jak Hammer- power moves to the lower back. Hammer now whips him right into the Hammer-Thomas corner. Now he turns and shoves the Preacher, and Chris Herman will have to intervene. (Which of course means...) EL: And Marc Thomas now just choking Little Yvette with that tag rope while the ref's back is turned! Blatant disregard for the rules by Thomas! Jak Hammer now turns his attention back to his legal opponent- shoulderblock in the midsection. Now he hefts him up- at 275 or so, Yvette is not really so Little. But Hammer tips the scales at 350-plus, and Yvette just looks so small next to him- he's got him up- ow! Just dropped him, belly first, right over a knee. That'll knock the wind right out of you, and once that happens, you're helpless until you can start breathing normally again. Cover- 1... 2... kickout. Jak Hammer stands him up- punch in the head, and Chris Herman warns him about closed fists. Now a kick doubles him over- Hammer sets him up- snap suplex! Picks him back up- Irish whip- cross body press! (Crowd pops) EL: 1... 2... Jak Hammer kicks out- that was so close! And Hammer is irate- goes right to the eyes- that draws a warning. Kneelift staggers Yvette- uh-oh- Hammer sets him up- ddt! Rolls him over- tag to Thomas- wait a second... Jak Hammer holding Little Yvette by the legs on the mat, and Thomas goes up top- moonsault on the prone Yvette! 1... 2... and the Preacher breaks it! The double team maneuver almost spelled the end for Little Yvette. Thomas picks him up- scoop sl- small package! 1... kickout. Little Yvette caught him asleep at the wheel. Now Yvette with a dropkick- and another! (Pop, followed by pop, followed by pop) EL: Little Yvette gets his second wind, and Marc Thomas is on the run! Yvette grabs him- Northern Lights Suplex! He just about dropped him right on the top of the head! 1... 2... Jak Hammer breaks it. Here comes the Preacher now! All four men in the ring! Wrench and Yvette- double clothesline on Jak Hammer- out to the floor! Now they send Marc Thomas for the ride- double back body drop! Now Chris Herman trying to restore order- sending the Preacher back to the corner- now Jak Hammer back in- clips Little Yvette! He just dove right into Yvette's knees! Marc Thomas now- Icebreaker! He hit it- no one has ever kicked out of this! Jak Hammer bails out- the cover- 1... 2... 3! The Preacher was just a second too late- Marc Thomas gets the pin! ("Life in the Fast Lane" plays to a raucous booing) EL: Let's get the official decision... DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winners, Marc Thomas and Jak Hammer! (Hammer and Thomas leave as Wrench attends to his partner and gives Herman a frank appraisal of his performance.) EL: We have to take another break- when we get back, we'll talk with the CWAA Heavyweight Champion! Don't go away! (...veterans, you cannot be turned down for this policy...) (Darth Vader's theme (The Imperial March) begins to play, and Max "The Professional" Robertson begins to make his way to the ring. He is wearing black jeans, a black t-shirt, and dark sunglasses. He meets David Myers in the ring. The crowd boos and hisses constantly for several moments. Once they finally settle down, Myers begins.) DM: Mr. Robertson, right here, one week ago you stood here and promised a victory... (Robertson snatches the mike from Myers and begins to speak) MR: Alright, that's enough. You and your vulgar little friends out there (he motions to the audience, and, right on cue, they start back up again with the boos), don't seem to understand who I am. I am Max Robertson, and I am the champion of the Championship Wrestling Association of America. . .. DM: We know perfectly well... MR: No, I don't think you do. I know I've proven my place in this federation, and you (he points to the fans who respond with boos and hisses) idiots out there should be treating me with the respect I've earned (this does not win them over). But that's alright. You're just like Barnes, you're all too stupid to know good wrestling when you see it. Last week, you all proved how disgustingly stupid you are. Now I know where I stand - and it's not with you! (suddenly, Max removes his sunglasses, gives the mike back to Myers and moves to the edge of the ring where he spits a huge glob of saliva into the crowd!) DM: Now that's uncalled for! (Robertson then turns and spits at Myers 'feet before exiting the ring and heading back to the locker room) EL: Well, it has been a long time since I have seen such an... such an "Unprofessional" display. I don't know who he thinks he's impressing with behavior like that, but it is pretty clear that his thrashing at the hands of the AEWA World Heavyweight Champion last week, and the way the crowd just ate it up, still rankles. Well, I could just go on and on about this, but we've got some more exciting action coming up in the ring- let's go back to David Myers. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is set for one fall with a 30 -minute time limit. First- (The Twister theme plays as the blonde-haired blue-eyes Travis Conley, CWAA Tag Team Title belt nicely polished, follows Mother Nature to the ring as the crowd cheers. Conley is 6'8"-315, and dressed in his customary brown tights with the Twister logo on the back.) DM: -from Lincoln, Nebraska, he weighs in at 315 lb., and is one half of the CWAA Tag Team Champions- Travis Conley! ("Sharp Dressed Man" by ZZ Top strikes up and the cheers are suddenly transformed into jeers) DM: And his opponent, from parts unknown, at 243 lb., the "Pretty Boy", Byron McNultey! (McNultey is of course accompanied by Marvelous Michael Anderson. Byron stands 5'10"-243, and the sequined red robe he wears covers blue full-length tights bearing the sign "Pretty Boy". Anderson, from whom "style" is a word still to be learned, is decked out a powder blue tux with a ruffled shirt and huge black bow-tie. The duo, as is their wont, strut about to the ring.) DM: Your referee, Jeff Howard. EL: OK- we're just about ready. I have an uneasy feeling that I'm about to have some unwelcome company- here he comes now. Michael Anderson: Can I see your tickets, sir? You appear to be in my seat. EL: Very funny. Sit if you must, but please, stay out of the match, and try to be civil to my other guest at this time. Mother Nature: (taking a seat on the other side of Lane) Good evening, Eric. I see someone has had an accident with the Easter egg dye. EL: Conley and McNultey lock up- a test of strength, and the big Husker easily overpowers the Pretty Boy- MA: The only thing overpowering about this Conley character is that underarm aroma, and you can see the effect it's having on the Pretty Boy, who, as you know, has a highly sensitive sense of smell after all the expensive French perfumes and what not. (Meanwhile, Conley is winning the game of mercy and McNultey is on his knees) MN: And which one is he wearing today? EL: OK, OK- I asked him to be civil, Mother Nature and I'll have to ask you. MA: Sibyl? Listen, Tony Schiavone, that one ain't even got one personality, never mind multiple ones, all right? You know what I'm saying here? EL: The Pretty Boy on his knees now begging for mercy- big kick in the chest by Conley. Pulls him up by the ears- MA: Instant dis-qualification in my book. MN: Well, your book apparently isn't the rule book... (Conley has just punched McNultey in the face) EL: Irish whip now- Conley scoops him- fallaway slam- 1... 2... kickout! MA: See that? The Pretty Boy is just lulling him into, what is that thing called again? MN: A true sense of security? EL: Conley now with a side headlock... MA: Look at that? You see that? That's called counter-wrestling- (McNultey has escaped the headlock with an atomic knee drop) MA: -and the pain shoots right up the spine, OK, and it goes into the brains and it causes a whole short circuit kind of thing in the brain and the whole body just spazzes out and he can't keep that headlock on. (Byron follows with a European uppercut) MA: Course, this Conley kid don't have much brain in his head anyway. He's kind of like Paul Revere's ride, you know what I mean? MN: What in the world are you talking about? (McNultey chokes Conley against the top rope) MA: A little light in the belfry, get it? Huh? Get it? EL: Byron McNultey with a Russian legsweep now. MA: Boy, tough crowd. EL: Byron McNultey now stomping away. Now he grabs Conley by the foot- spinning toe hold- spins again- MA: He was taught that move by- uh- who's that guy- Hoss Funk, Jr. EL: I think he's talking about Dory Funk, Jr. MA: Whatever- it was the guy who used to do that hold. (McNultey spins again) MN: Are you trying to tell us that Dory Funk, Jr. trains you two? MA: I guess you could say that. MN: You could, but you'd be lying. (Spins again) EL: Tell us about your training sessions with Dory Funk, Jr., then. MA: OK- well, you see, it was like this. OK- there's Byron and me, and we're at home- MN: In Parts Unknown? (Spins again) MA: Never you mind. So there we are, and we're flipping through the channels, looking for some good cultural programs, 'cause we're well-rounded guys, Byron and me. And we get that French show- what is it again? The one with the skunk? Pepe le pew- that's it. And the show's over- I'll tell you later how it ends- that guy always gets me- OK, the show ends, and then the wrestling show comes on, and there's Hoss Funk, Jr.- EL: "Dory". MA: Who's telling this dory- I mean, story? So there he is, and he and the Funk who wore the mask, Jimmy Jim Bob, or whatever, and then, right there on my tv, he does this very hold. MN: So you think watching Hoss and Jimmy Jack Funk on tv for the WWF 12 years ago is a "training session" with a former World Champion? MA: You said it, toots. EL: OK- McNultey still applying the pressure- and- oh! (Big pop) EL: Travis Conley just reached up and popped him one right in the mush! The Pretty Boy is stunned- comes back with a stomp, though. Now he's choking him out again! MA: Modified sleeper. Modified sleeper. Try to call these matches right, OK? MN: You wanted a disqualification before, no you should be getting it. MA: You tell me where it says you can't use a modified sleeper. EL: McNultey stands him up- bulldog! Now the Pretty Boy off the ropes- legdrop. The cover- 1... kickout. Stomp again by the Pretty Boy, who has really taken control of this one. MA: Well, duh... MN: Settle down, Beavis. MA: (whispering to Lane) Hey, I think she digs me. EL: McNultey know going for- a figure four?- and- wait! Small package attempt! MA: Hey, referee. He's grabbing the tights- see? See? EL: Shush- 2... kickout. MA: I have had just about enough if these blatant violations! (Slams down headphones and heads to the apron) MN: Oh, no you don't! (Follows suit) EL: Deja vu all over again, Yogi Berra said. Michael Anderson up on that ring apron- and Travis Conley just smacked him one! (Big pop) EL: The Pretty Boy takes advantage and knees him from behind. Now the Pretty Boy getting on Jeff Howard's case- and Michael Anderson choking Travis Conley on the top rope! Turn around, Jeff! OK- the Pretty Boy covers- 1... 2... kickout! And the Pretty Boy kicks Conley to the floor. Now he feigns toward Mother Nature- and Jeff Howard is going to put a stop to this! But no, Michael Anderson stands Travis Conley up- and belts him one! Now he smashes his head onto those ring steps! Rolls him in- McNultey picks him up- full nels- no- dragon suplex- 1... 2... kickout again. Travis Conley showing a lot of guts- this is the second time in two weeks he has been out here with both of those characters around the ringside area. Pretty Boy now with an Irish whip- devastating knee in the midsection. Now he whips again- sleeper! He caught him in a sleeper, and this one is real! ("Go, Travis, Go!") EL: Travis Conley fading fast- Mother Nature exhorting him to hang in there- he's reaching, reaching... He's got the rope- he's got the rope. Jeff Howard calls for a break. And now he and McNultey exchanging words- and Michael Anderson reaches in and pops Conley again! The cover- 1... kickout. Now McNultey throws him to the floor again! Right out at Michael Anderson's feet. Anderson with a punch- and Conley fires back! (Big pop) EL: Anderson with a rake of the eyes, now. This getting out of hand. Clothesline on the floor by Michael Anderson- rolls him back in- wait! Next week, we'll be ready, will you? (Twister and Mother Nature storm out of the ring) EL We have to take on more break- and then we'll be back with our special feature match- Lethal Lawson and Randy Sheridan- you won't want to miss this one!
