Sunday, March 9th, Card (1/3)

9 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last Week on Championship Wrestling Association of America *********************************************************** EL: I don't think it'll be a problem OK- The Preacher, Kore Wrench, has Randy Sheradin caught in a bearhug, and he's just squeezing the air right out him. Now- hey! Max Robertson just took Joshua Barnes down with a Russian legsweep on the floor! Now he's stomping away on him, a feral grin on his face, and no one in the ring notices yet, it seems. What is that all about? Randy Sheradin now clubs his way out of the bearhug, and now he stuns Wrench with a kneelift. Sheradin off of the ropes now- flying body pr- Wrench ducks, and Sheradin sails out to the floor through the ropes! Now Robertson over to Sheradin- watch out! No- he's just helping him back up, and, having checked him out, aids Sheradin back up to the apron, and- a forearm shiver in the lower back, and Sheradin drops back to the floor! Robertson rams his forehead onto the ring apron- and a double chickenwing submission is slapped on! *********************************************************** Eric Lane: Hello, everyone, and welcome back to the world-famous Sportatorium for another week of Championship Wrestling Association of America. This week, we have the semi-finals of the CWAA Heavyweight Title Tournament, as "The Dreamer" Randy Sheradin faces Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson for the right to face "The Professional" Max Robertson to determine our first-ever CWAA Heavyweight Champion. We're going to take a look at those two mystery men who attacked Paul Langford last week. And we are going to talk about the CWAA Tag Team Title Tournament, coming up in just a few weeks. But first, "The Dreamer" Randy Sheradin has been the subject of quite a lot of speculation lately. Allegations have been made that his ACWF Tag Team Championship belt was won by underhanded methods- namely, bribery. Joshua Barnes was sent a series of anonymous messages about this situation, and was asked to meet his informant in the basement of the Sportatorium last week. Instead, he was locked in the boiler room, and when he broke out, he was involved in a scuffle with "The Professional" Max Robertson as both men watched the match between Randy Sheradin and Kore Wrench. Robertson then interfered in the match, assaulting Sheradin and raising the ire of the Preacher. Tonight, we hope to clear the situation a little bit, with these words from the ACWF's executive offices- we apologize for not bringing you this statement last week, but we ran out of time. Without further ado... ***************************** (A man in his early thirties, who looks completely too friendly to be anything but an executive, is standing in front of a large screen which reads "Atlantic Coast Wrestling Federation".) Man: Hello, my name is Allan Sharpe, and I am the head of public relations for the Atlantic Coast Wrestling Federation, or ACWF. While watching the fine telecasts that the CWAA puts on such as this one, my department heard allegations that one Randy Sheridan bribed an official in order to win a title. Mr. Sheridan was indeed a tag team champion for two weeks in the ACWF, and his win was somewhat controversial, so we conducted a thorough investigation. Well, we are quite pleased to announce that no wrongdoing was discovered, and that Mr. Sheridan has been completely exonerated of any accusations against him by our organization. We at the ACWF would hope that no further allegations will be made against our officials or our wrestlers, as it only mars the great competition that both the ACWF and the CWAA offer. We wish good luck to Mr. Sheridan and this organization as a whole, and we hope to put this whole ugly incident behind us. Thank you for your attention. ***************************** EL: There you have it- the ACWF's office has determined that the allegations are false. But the issue of who has made the allegations, why were they made, and what is happening to the relationship between Joshua Barnes, Max Robertson and Randy Sheradin are still unsolved. But let's get back to the ACWF for a few seconds- I have been informed that the CWAA and ACWF are negotiating for an ACWF tag team to appear in the CWAA Tag Team Title tournament. Additionally, the CWAA has arranged for a representative team from the FWA to also appear. AND, a third team, yet to appear in the CWAA but familiar to us all, is on its way for a chance at CWAA gold. I'll have more information on that next week for you. This week, we are in the semif- wait a second. I see Mark Sherrick coming down the aisle- he was eliminated last week, along with Healer's Havoc; what could he possibly want here? (Sherrick, clad in black jeans, black boots, and a white T-shirt with "Never Forgotten" painted on in red walks down to the ring, to his hometown crowd's boos. Sherrick heads over to Lane, and glowers at the crowd.) EL: Well, Mr. Sher- MS: Shut up! (grabs the microphone from Eric) First off, I want a match with Healer's Havoc. I want to show the CWAA that I can kick his sorry ass. As for BD Brown, well, why don't you and Healer bring yourselves on against me and a mystery partner right here, right now? That will really show what you two are made of. (Sherrick heads for the ring and waits in the middle.) EL: Well, Mark Sherrick says he wants a tag team match with Midnight Rampage. I'm no math whiz, but there's only one of him and two of them, and here they come! (Tip Smith leads Healer's Havoc and BD Brown to the ring, to a warm reception from the paying audience. Brown is 6'3"-250, with the frequently-broken nose between the blue eyes. Havoc, toothless as ever and in his trademark dog collar, is 6'6"-260 with black hair. After recent events, Smith carried a Haliburton briefcase with him.) EL: Healer's Havoc and Big Dog Brown here now to face... Mark Sherrick? Sherrick has clashed with both men in the ring in recent weeks, and it looks like no one is quite finished. Sherrick backing into the corner as Havoc and Brown stand in mid-ring, and- hey! Those two look familiar! (Two men in the 6'6"-300 range, dressed in street-gang-style garb hit the ring from behind Midnight Rampage.) EL: It's- it's- the West Coast Connection! The- three-time, is it?- former AEWA World Tag Team Champions? Why are they here- these two are not affiliated with the CWAA! MC Read and Axl Sharkman have just hit Midnight Rampage from behind, and the bell rings while Mark Sherrick heads to the floor. Sharkman had Brown down in one corner and is leaning all of his weight through that size 13 boot on the Big Dog's Adam's apple, and Read tosses Healer's Havoc to the floor. Mark Sherrick pulls him back up and rams his head onto the ring apron, but backs away as Tip Smith wheels around with that metal briefcase in hand. Back in the ring, Axl Sharkman whips the Dog into the ropes- clothesline! Axl tags in MC Read, who drops an elbow. MC Read now with a camel clutch. We're going to take a quick break- when we get back, we'll have more of the West Coast Connection and Midnight Rampage. (This Monday at 10:30 PM, on WYXZ TV 39, the conclusion of the demolition derby episode of "Happy Days", as Fonzie is forced to face the Malachi Brothers on his own when Pinky Tuscadero is hurt by the Malachi Crunch.) EL: OK- MC Read still has BD Brown locked in that camel clutch, and Healer's Havoc in with a rake of the face to break the hold. The referee is ushering him back out of the ring, and now Axl Sharkman comes in- the WCC with an Irish whip- double back body drop! MC Read sets the Bog Dog for a ddt- he hit it- the cover... 1... 2... Havoc breaks it with a kick. And that'll bring Sharkman back in again. The referee is sending Healer's Havoc back to the corner again- Sharkman holds Brown's arms back, and Read with a running kick in the midsection. Now the referee sends Sharkman back to the apron. Read whips his man into the WCC corner, and now he heads across the ring and slaps Healer's Havoc in the face! Havoc follows him to midring- but the referee is cutting him off! Sharkman chokes Brown with the tag rope, and Read drapes Brown's leg over the middle rope (in a standing position), and kicks away at the inner thigh. Read takes him to midring now- legsweep faceslam. Now he applies a Boston crab. They appear to be working on taking out the Big Dog's legs. I wonder how the WCC prepared for this one- Midnight Rampage are a very new team- there could not have been much of an opportunity to scout them, but Tip Smith and his men must have seen the WCC on TV and pay per view often enough to be well-familiar with them. Read still has that Boston crab locked on. If Midnight Rampage can pull this off, having already defeated Double Jeopardy, they would have to be instantly acclaimed as the favorites for the CWAA Tag Team Title tournament- we'll have more information on that later. Read releases the hold- Irish whip- no! Brown with the reverse- Read off the ropes- death valley driver! (Big pop) EL: He needs to tag out now, while he can- he covers... 1... 2... Sharkman breaks the pin with a kick in the head- and here comes Healer's Havoc- but the referee cuts him off! Sharkman drags Brown over to the WCC corner- and Mark Sherrick starts choking Brown on the bottom rope! He releases the hold as Read tags Sharkman in. Sharkman with a punch, now a kick in the midsection, and he pounds him to the mat with a series of double axe handles. Sharkman showboating a bit now- and Brown takes out his legs and rolls him up! 1.. kickout! The Big Dog almost caught him sleeping! Sharkman with a whip into the corner now- and he follows with an avalanche. Now he grabs the Dog by the hair and slams his face into the top turnbuckle. Now Sharkman spins him around and lashes a reverse knife edge across the chest. Now the Dog comes back with a kneelift! An elbow to the head- now he sets him up- swinging neckbreaker by Brown! Both men are down! Brown staggers to his feet- leans back- tags in Healer's Havoc! (Huge pop) EL: The Real Deal in there now- stomps Sharkman- Read comes in and Havoc lays him out with a big right hand! (another pop) EL: Elbow drop on Sharkman- now Healer's Havoc with a big elbow to the sternum knocks Mark Sherrick off of the apron! Now he picks up Axl Sharkman- Irish whip- kick in the midsection! Havoc with the ddt- the cover- but the referee is trying to get MC Read out of the ring. Here comes Mark Sherrick now- Havoc still has Sharkman pinned- this would have been a 3-count by now. Mark Sherrick drops an elbow on the back of Havoc's head- BD Brown in the ring after him, and as Sherrick bails out, Brown follows him to the floor. Now the referee is trying to separate Sherrick and Brown out on the floor, and Tip Smith is heading over with that briefcase- in the ring Read is helping Sharkman up- Sharkman places Havoc on his shoulders, and Read goes to the top- he bulldogs Healer's Havoc off of Read's shoulders! The West Coast Killa! Sharkman covers, and Read heads over to where Brown, Sherrick, Smith and the referee are all gathered- he points out the cover to the referee- he goes in to make the count- Brown and Sherrick start grappling up against the ring apron, and Read is trying to tug that briefcase away from Smith. Back in the ring... 1... 2... 3! Your winners are the three-time AEWA World Tag Team Champions, the West Coast Connection- Mark Sherrick's big surprise for Midnight Rampage. Smith and Brown into the ring now to see what happened as the WCC head out to the lockers. As his partner helps Brown up- well, what do you want now? Mark Sherrick: (thrusts a videotape into Eric Lane's hand) I am sick and tired of listening to you talk about my father who "allegedly" died in the ring! You want the proof? Here it is! I want you to play this tape right here, right now! EL: Listen, Mark- I am not the director around here. I just call the matches and conduct the interviews. I can have this sent to the production team, but I am not in a position to play thi- MS: You'll play that tape and you'll play it now! (Exit Sherrick) EL: Well, it's obvious he wasn't listening, but- I mean, I'm sitting out with the announcer's equipment- how does he think I'm going to play a videotape? I'll send this back to the director, we'll let him decide what to do. For now, he's decided that we're taking a quick break. Back in a few minutes with the semifinal match for the CWAA Heavyweight Title tournament, as "The Dreamer" Randy Sheradin meets Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson.
