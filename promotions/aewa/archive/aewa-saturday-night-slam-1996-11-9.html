Saturday Night Slam (11/9)

11 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power Bomb, and a look at Mister Crowley DRIVING the head of some guy into the concrete with the Premature Burial! Then the camera switches to the Lovely Bertha and Matt Wilson in a packed arena) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Saturday Night Slam! "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt isn't with us in the broadcast area tonight, because he'll be facing Rick Showboat for the Light Heavyweight title later on! LB: I'm really nervous about that match, Wilson. Those two guys are fighting over ME, and I wish it would just stop. MW: Well, don't worry, because they're finally gonna settle the score tonight. Frankly, I'm looking forward to it. LB: But what's happening first? MW: We're about to see a big triangle match between El Aviadore, Penance, and Matt Master. This should be GREAT! LB: These kind of matches are EXTREMELY unpredictable, so anything can happen. Here we go.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is an elimination-style triangle match! Introducing first, from Mexico City, weighing in at 180 pounds, here is EL AVIADORE! ("La Bamba" begins playing loudly, and El Aviadore makes his entrance. He is wearing a blue mask, and waves to the adoring crowd.) MW: El Aviadore sure looks ready for this triangle match! LB: Yes he does, but he's no match for Matt Master. MW: And here comes Matt Master now! RING ANNOUNCER: And next, weighing in at 255 pounds, he is a former Light Heavyweight champion of the World, please welcome MATT MASTER! (Matt Master storms to the ring, without any entrance music playing. He is wearing black tights with "MM" on the back. He has a complete "stone cold" expression on his face, and the crowd doesn't give him any reaction at all.) MW: Wow! Matt Master looks pretty angry tonight. I'd hate to be in the ring right now! LB: Here comes Penance, the last contestant in the triangle match! He's made an awful lot of enemies in the AEWA lately, El Aviadore being one of them! RING ANNOUNCER: And finally, weighing in at 223 pounds, he is a former Light Heavyweight champion, PENANCE! (Skid Row's "My Enemy" begins playing loudly over the PA, and Penance makes his way toward the ring to a BIG round of boos. He is dressed completely in black.) MW: He's looking for some revenge tonight. El Aviadore is ready to go, and he'll be starting off against Matt Master. LB: Master is going to destroy both of these twerps! MW: They lock up! Matt Master gets the advantage and takes El Aviadore down with a back suplex! Aviadore jumps back up, but Master whips him into the ropes. BIG Clothesline! LB: That almost took El Aviadore's head right off! That move could end it right now! MW: Master hits Aviadore with an elbow drop! Now he's pulling him up! LB: I told you Master would dominate this match! MW: Matt Master is pounding El Aviadore with a series of left hands! He's punching him right into the corner! LB: He's lifting him onto the turnbuckle! Now he's going up with him! MW: BIG Superplex! El Aviadore folded up like an accordion! The cover! One......Two.....Kickout! MW: El Aviadore is in trouble! Now Matt Master is kicking him in the small of the back! LB: I love it! Matt Master is showing NO mercy! MW: Master whips El Aviadore into the ropes again. Aviadore comes back with a kick to the stomach, and connects with a big DDT on Master! How did he EVER find the energy for that move? But that took as much out of El Aviadore as it did on Master. Both men are sprawled out on the canvas! LB: One of them should make the tag in to Penance! MW: It looks like it will be Master! Yes it is! He dives at the last minute and barely makes the tag! LB: He can easily pin El Aviadore now! Matt Master just picked apart Aviadore! MW: Penance hits Aviadore with a bodyslam. He pulls him to his feet, and.... Short- Arm Clothesline! Aviadore landed right on his head! LB: Now Penance is climbing the top rope! What's he gonna do? MW: FLYING LEGDROP FROM THE TOP ROPE! And El Aviadore is knocked out! Penance with the cover! One.....Two...Thr..Kickout! LB: He just barely kicked out of that one! Wow! MW: Penance picks up El Aviadore and hits him with a Backbreaker! And now he hits him with an Atomic Drop! LB: He's really working on Aviadore's lower back! I'm surprised he can even stand up straight! MW: El Aviadore is whipped into the ropes.... Wait! Aviadore slides right through Penance's legs! LB: El Aviadore NAILS Penance with a huge right hand. He hits him with another right, and a left, and another right! MW: Penance is stunned, and Aviadore hits him with a standing dropkick! He's pulling him over to the corner! LB: El Aviadore is placing Penance on the top rope! MW: Now El Aviadore is following him up! He goes for a Top Rope Frankensteiner...NO! Penance countered with a huge Super Power Bomb! Aviadore is SCREAMING in pain! LB: What a beautiful counter! El Aviadore was DROPPED right on his back! MW: Penance is now getting El Aviadore into a Boston Crab! He's really applying the pressure, and Aviadore submits almost IMMEDIATELY! (ding ding) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen! El Aviadore has submitted in eleven minutes and forty-four minutes! Now, the match continues.... MW: Matt Master enters the ring and attacks Penance! He didn't even give him time to recover! He is stomping him repeatedly! LB: Penance has NO chance of beating him! Master had plenty of time to recuperate! MW: Matt Master whips Penance into the ropes-- HE FLIPS PENANCE RIGHT OUT OF THE RING WITH A BACKDROP! LB: Penance landed hard on that concrete floor! And now Master hits him in the face with a baseball slide! MW: Penance enters the ring, but Master hits him right away with a back suplex! He hits him with a Belly-to-Belly suplex. Matt Master covers Penance and hooks the legs! One....Two....Kickout! MW: Penance just barely kicked out of that one! Matt Master picks him up again, and slams him down with a Brainbuster! LB: That's it! It's over! MW: Not yet, Bertha. NOW MATT MASTER GETS PENANCE IN THE MASTER LOCK! There's no way Penance can get out of this! LB: Master is applying some major pressure! The referee is asking Penance if he wants to give up, but Penance refuses! MW: Penance is reaching for the ropes! He's slowly crawling towards the bottom rope! Can he reach it? LB: Yes! Penance reached the ropes, and Matt Master is forced to release the hold! MW: Penance really took a beating on his back from that hold! Now Master is setting Penance up for a Power Bomb! LB: LOOK! Penance countered Matt Master's Power Bomb with a Frankensteiner! Where the hell did THAT come from? MW: He picks up Matt Master and gets him in a sidewalk slam! Jeez, that guy is FAST! The Cover! One.....Two.....Kickout! LB: Don't count Matt Master out yet! MW: No one is! But now Penance is climbing the top rope! Big Splash on top of Matt Master! Why doesn't he cover him?! LB: I don't know. Penance hits him with an elbow drop, and now he's pulling him up to his feet! MW: POWER BOMB ON MATT MASTER! Penance quickly jumps on top of Master and covers him, hooking the leg! One.....Two.....Three! (Ding, Ding) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen, here is your winner, at the time of 16:19, PENANCE! MW: What an awesome match that was! Every competitor gave it their all! LB: Because of this victory, Penance is now the number one contender to the Light Heavyweight title. That should make him a VERY happy man. MW: And speaking of the Light Heavyweight title, it's now time for our main event. Rick Showboat is about to put his belt on the line against "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt! LB: OH NO, I'm not ready for this! MW: Just calm down, Bertha. This match should have happened a LONG time ago, because Showboat and Hurt have NEVER liked each other. Here we go.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, this contest is scheduled for one fall, and is for the AEWA Light Heavyweight championship! Introducing first, weighing in at 239 pounds, here is "BIG DADDY" MIKE HURT! (As Johnny Cash's "Ring Of Fire" plays loudly over the PA, Mike Hurt makes his entrance to a mixed reaction from the crowd. He wears an "AEWA Rocks" T-shirt, and waves to Bertha as he walks past the broadcast booth.) LB: I can't wait until my man Rick beats the daylights outta Big Daddy. MW: Well, Big Daddy looks pretty confident tonight. By the way, he's looking a little thinner. He had to lose about thirty pounds in order to get this match. LB: What a shame.... losing all that weight just to get his butt kicked by Rick. MW: Speaking of which, here comes the champion now. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 226 pounds, and accompanied to the ring by his manager Doctor Love, here is the AEWA Light Heavyweight champion of the World, RICK SHOWBOAT! (Showboat makes his way to the ring wearing a blue robe. He has the Light Heavyweight title wrapped around his waist. Doctor Love, wearing an expensive tuxedo, is following closely behind him.) LB: Boy! He's looking good tonight! MW: This should be a very action packed matchup! Both men are extremely skilled, but I think that Big Daddy has something up his sleeve tonight. LB: Well, no matter what he tries, no one can beat Rick Showboat. MW: We're about to find out! The opening bell has rung, and the two men lock up. Showboat overpowers Big Daddy and whips him into the ropes. LB: See? Rick is already taking control. MW: Big Daddy ducks under a clothesline by Showboat. Showboat quickly turns around and hits him with a flying body press! LB: (yelling to ring) Cover him, Rick!! MW: Showboat quickly goes for the cover.... One.....Two....Shoulder Up! MW: Showboat had a sloppy cover that time. He didn't even hook the legs. Jeez, he's NEVER going to beat Big Daddy like that. LB: He's just sizing up Big Daddy. He'll pin him eventually. MW: Okay, Bertha. Now Showboat whips Big Daddy to the ropes-- BUT BIG DADDY COMES BACK AND ROCKS HIM WITH A BIG ELBOW!! Showboat is staggering! LB: Big Daddy had BETTER not mess up Rick's face.If he even leaves a bruise, I'll kill him! MW: It's good to see that you care about what's inside Showboat, and you don't just like him for his looks, Bertha. LB: What's that supposed to mean? MW: Nevermind, let's just get back to the action. Big Daddy takes Showboat down with a Vertical Suplex. Now he hits Showboat with a BIG Elbow Drop! LB: Get up, Rick! MW: Big Daddy is pulling Rick Showboat up by his hair. He picks him up-- DEVASTATING NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX! The referee gets into position. One....Two.....Kickout! MW: He just barely kicked out of that one! LB: Nonsense, that wasn't even close to a pin. MW: Sometimes I wonder what match you're watching, Bertha. LB: The same one as you. Why? MW: Well.... nevermind. Big Daddy picks him up to bodyslam him-- but Showboat counters with a beautiful small package!! One....Two...Kickout! LB: That was a beautiful counter by Rick! Now you see why he is the Light- Heavyweight Champion! MW: Don't celebrate too soon, Bertha. Big Daddy jumps right up and starts to stomp away on Showboat. He grabs Showboat and whips him to the ropes. Showboat ducks under a clothesline, and counters with a big dropkick! Big Daddy is stunned! MW: Showboat whips Big Daddy into the corner-- AND FOLLOWS HIM IN WITH A BIG CLOTHESLINE! Showboat then grabs the staggering Big Daddy, and hits him with a belly-to-belly suplex! The cover! One....Two....Kickout! MW: Showboat takes Big Daddy over with a snap mare. OOOH! Showboat just NAILED Big Daddy with a standing dropkick to the back of the head! LB: That's it! It's over! MW: Showboat walks around to pull Big Daddy up. OUCH! Big Daddy just hit Showboat with a cheap shot to the midsection! LB: Hey! That's not fair! MW: Big Daddy shoves Showboat backwards! Showboat slams right into the referee, who falls out of the ring, onto the concrete floor! He's out cold! LB: What the hell is Big Daddy doing?!? He's pulling something out of his tights! MW: They look like brass knuckles! AND BIG DADDY JUST CLOCKED SHOWBOAT WITH THEM!! Showboat is knocked out! LB: No! I hate Big Daddy's filthy guts! Of all the wrestlers, why did he have to do it to MY favorite one?! This can't be happening! MW: Big Daddy is now exiting the ring and trying to revive the referee. He picks him up, and carries him into the ring! LB: (on the verge of tears) Please, don't let this happen! Don't beat him that way, Mike! PLEASE! MW: It looks like Big Daddy has revived the ref. He's going for the cover! One....Two....Three! (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, at a time of eleven minutes and fourteen seconds, here is your winner, and NEW AEWA Light Heavyweight champion, "BIG DADDY" MIKE HURT! LB: No! That was unfair! Big Daddy cheated! MW: Funny, that's really never bothered you before. LB: I didn't actually CARE about his opponents then. It's different this time! MW: Well, either way, we have a new Light-Heavyweight Champion. We're completely out of time, so this is Matt Wilson signing off! Be sure to watch Wednesday Night Rumble, when we'll have some even BIGGER matches! LB: (wiping the tears from her eyes) See ya next time, folks.
