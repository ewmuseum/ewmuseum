Sunday February 23rd Card (1/3)

23 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last Week, on Championship Wrestling Association of America: Eric Lane: He just dropped Intellecta headfirst onto that object loaded in his mask! Havoc rolls him over- scorpion deathlock! He has it locked in! Henry King is helping Gary Jackson back to his feet and BD Brown and Tip Smith cheer on their man! King is rolling Jackson into the ring, but the bell rings- I think we have a submission victory for Healer's Havoc here. Gary Jackson up on his feet behind Havoc, and BD Brown up in the ring, too. Jackson with a weak punch on Havoc's back- Jackson still hasn't shaken the cobwebs out, yet, and couldn't get full force behind that blow, I'd reckon. BD Brown and Healer's Havoc now take Jackson- Irish whip- double boots in the midsection! Jackson staggers out of the ring and falls unceremoniously flat on his face on the floor. Intellecta now back up, and BD Brown and Havoc with a double running clothesline- ****************************************************************** EL: Welcome, everyone, to another edition of Championship Wrestling Association of America. We've got more CWAA Heavyweight Title tourney matches tonight, we're going to talk with Randy Sheradin about allegations regarding his professional past, a special announcement about the AEWA, and, after last week's incident in the Healer's Havoc-Eddie Intellecta match, we're going to see Healer's Havoc team up with the Big Dog as they take on Double Jeopardy in our special feature match. Before we begin, let's take a look at the tournament results from last week. *********************************************************** (CWAA File Footage: 02-09-97) EL: Langford with the clothesline! Robertson is flattened- Langford picks him up- Irish whip. He catches the Professional in an airplane spin- this is how he sets up his Tornado Drop! He starts spin- no- he just dropped to one knee- his leg was too badly hurt by that figure four to bear both his and Robertson's weight! Robertson slips down, big back suplex. Robertson back on his feet, and he waits as Langford staggers back up, now Robertson looks like he's going for that double chickenwing- he's got the Tornado locked in it, and he leans back, taking the Tornado off of the mat. Nowhere for Langford to go, the ref checks for a submission. Did he get it? There's the bell, and it looks like Langford has submitted... *** Barnes up on both feet now, and powering his way around- he's reversed the arm bar, and is now applying the pressure to Jackson! He's backing Jackson into the corner, cross-corner whip, and Barnes with a big clothesline as Jackson comes off the ropes. Intellecta up on the apron again, and Sheradin follows him- this will grab the referees attention as the two begin shoving. Henry King is watching with an ominous look on his face, and Barnes again drops his opponent to get involved with goings-on on the apron! Gary Jackson now removing that ankle brace as everyone else tussles on the apron! He's got it off- here comes Max Robertson! Jackson is about to slam Barnes with it, but Max Robertson wrenches it out of his hands! Robertson swings it at Jackson, who ducks and runs across the ring! Jackson ducks another blow, and runs back! The referee turns around as Jackson brushes him, and he sees Robertson raise that ankle brace- he swings it again- a glancing blow on Gary Jackson! The referee is signaling for the bell, as Eddie Intellecta is hoofing it out of here, with the Dreamer not far behind him. Barnes is trying to get that brace from Robertson, as Jackson stumbles through the ropes and races away with Henry King. The referee is signaling for disqualification, and, from the safe distance of 40 feet or so, Hotshot Gary Jackson raises his arms triumphantly. Barnes can't believe what is happening, Robertson looks dejected as he tosses the brace to the floor, kicking the bottom rope. So it'll be Max Robertson and Gary Jackson in the second round. When we come back, Eddie Intellecta- someone is going to have get him to stop running from Randy Sheradin long enough to get back in the ring- takes on Healer's Havoc. *** Intellecta has something in his mask, though, and I'm guessing he's going to try that headbutt attack of his. Healer's Havoc with a shot to the midsection, though, and Intellecta doubles over! Havoc with a ddt! He just dropped Intellecta headfirst onto that object loaded in his mask! Havoc rolls him over- scorpion deathlock! He has it locked in! Henry King is helping Gary Jackson back to his feet and BD Brown and Tip Smith cheer on their man! King is rolling Jackson into the ring, but the bell rings- I think we have a submission victory for Healer's Havoc here. *** Brown picks him back up- Irish whip- clothesline- no, Sherrick ducked- Sherrick off the ropes, clothesline- no, Brown ducked- Brown with a clothesline of his own- and over the rope they both go, landing out on the arena floor. This is the second time these two have taken their match out there. Brown with a kneelift, and Sherrick replies with a fist. Rake of the face by Sherrick, and now he grabs Brown by the head and slams him down on the ring apron. The referee must be up to at least 5 by now. Both men now trying to back in the ring. Up on the apron- Sherrick with a low blow, and Brown falls back to the floor- Sherrick in the ring- there's the bell! I don't know what the decision is going to be- either a count-out win for Sherrick, a double-count-ut, or perhaps Brown by disqualification on account of the low blow. Well, the referee has raised Sherrick's hand- *************************************************** EL: This week we finish the first round of the CWAA Heavyweight Title tournament, as "The Dreamer" Randy Sheradin, quickly becoming a controversial figure here in the CWAA, squares off against Marc Thomas; "Hardcore" Joey Haven, coming off that vicious matchup with Jak Hammer last week, takes on "The Preacher" Kore Wrench; Stan "The Powerhouse" Stevens meets Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson, which no doubt means a visit from the Major is impending; and a battle of two bulls, as Twister's Travis Conley and Jak Hammer finish out the first round. ("Dream On" by Aerosmith and the Philly-harmonic strikes up) EL: And now, we have a man who's got a lot of explaining to do, not only to Joshua Barnes, but to his fans. "The Dreamer" Randy Sheridan! (Sheridan enters to a modest pop, but some fans definitely have their doubts about the Dreamer. Sheridan slaps a few hands, then heads directly to Lane. For those who care about these types of things, Sheridan, hailing from the bustling metropolis of Des Moines, is a whopping 5'8"-170. There may not be a whole lot of him, but what there is of him is pretty well-built.) Randy Sheradin: Before you say anything, Eric, I want to say something to Josh in front of everyone. Josh, I have no idea what that video's about, or what's going on, but I have never, EVER bribed a referee. That other tag belt I won fairly, with skill, teamwork, and a lot of luck. I was hoping you and I could take a stab at winning the tag belts here in the CWAA. Apparently somebody doesn't want that to happen. That's the only explanation I can think of, Josh. EL: Another concern that Barnes has is your apparent friendship with "The Professional" Max Robertson. Despite Robertson's claims, Barnes doesn't seem to trust him. RS: During this whole thing, actually, Max has been cool. He understands Josh's concerns, and so do I. But wrestling has too many people who take the low road, and if I can help a good wrestler become the athlete that fans can be proud of, I'm going to do it, and I hope Josh understands that. Max has been helping me trying to figure out who's behind my recent troubles, and helping me prepare for tonight. EL: That leads into possibly your biggest problem. In the first round of the CWAA tournament, you're facing Marc Thomas, who's got the longest resume of anyone in the CWAA. With all the distractions, can you pull this one off? RS: You know, I didn't like him at the time, but when Max Robertson called me the man to beat in the CWAA, I made copies of that tape and sent them to everyone I know. I'm not sure if I ever was the man to beat, though, and I know I'm not now. That man is unquestionably Marc Thomas. But you know what? In all the chaos, this is something I've been ready for. I've got personal problems on the East Coast, I've got personal problems here, but tonight I get to step into the ring as an underdog against an opponent who thinks he's going to get an easy ride. Finally, I've got something I can handle. Getting things straight with Josh, I hope I can do it, but I honestly don't know if I can. Pulling off the upset in the ring? That's why they call me the Dreamer. (Sheradin shakes Lane's hand and hops into the ring, fists raised, to a louder pop, now that Sheradin has personally reassured the crowd as to what side he's on.) EL: When we come back, we'll have Randy Sheradin and Marc Thomas, one on one. (WYXZ Channel 39 brings you your favorite episodes of Happy Days every Monday night at 10:30- Monday night, has Joanie fallen for Potsie? Then, at 11:00, Ponch and John ride around on motorcycles, and, well, I don't really remember any plots. Maybe this is the one with the role skating competition where Todd Bridges was one of the guest judges?) ("Life in the Fast Lane" by the Eagles plays. The 6'0"-240 Iceman (as he is known elsewhere), removes a sleeveless leather jacket to reveal a well-chiseled body as he climbs into the ring, oblivious to the negative response he receives from the paying crowd. Having his own list of priorities, Thomas heads over to the house mike before entering the ring, and addresses the audience.) MT: Eric, I'm not real worried about the guy I'm going to be facing tonight. You know why? He calls himself the "Dreamer!" When I get finished with him, he's gonna wish it was just a dream!! Now I've fought some light heavyweights in my time, but I didn't realize the CWAA had a paper weight division. (The crowd endeavors to express its disapproval.) MT: (Pointing in the ring) This kid is real exciting to watch. He likes to bounce around the ring like a da** racquetball! Well Sheridan, one mistake can put an end to your high flying antics! You miss one move and the match could be over right then. That's right boy, you let your guard down for one second and the next thing you know the ref will be helping you to your feet after the bell has rung. Could the kid pull and upset? (The paying audience, not recognizing a rhetorical question when hit with one, replies in the affirmative.) MT: Maybe. Is it going to happen tonight against me? I don't think so. I hope your insurance is paid up, 'cause after the Icebreaker you'll need some medical attention! (Heads to the ring.) EL: Here we are in the first round, and already the two most decorated men in the CWAA are in the ring together. Thomas seems very unimpressed by this week's opposition. Then again he seems unimpressed anytime he looks anywhere except in the mirror. Collar and elbow tie-up, as each man jostles for the leverage advantage. Thomas pushes Sheradin back into the corner- the referee calls for a clean break- and he gets it, as Thomas backs into mid ring. Both men in the middle of the ring, now. They lock up aga- no- Sheradin with a single leg takedown- elbow drop- he missed. Thomas back on his feet with a quick stomp as Sheradin gets back up. They circle each other warily, lock up-- and Thomas with a handful of hair as he pulls Sheradin to the mat- elbow drop right into the Dreamer's sternum. Now he stomps away again- Thomas off the ropes- fistdrop! Advantage to Marc Thomas here in the early going. Now Thomas picks him up- Irish whip- and a big knee right into the midsection, doubling over the Dreamer. I have to wonder if the accusations made against his integrity last week are affecting his in-ring performance. For those of you who didn't see last week- snap suplex by Thomas, and now he locks the Dreamer in an armbar- last week Joshua Barnes received a packer of photos which showed Sheradin and a referee- not one of our CWAA officials- apparently in some kind of exchange of cash. There was an anonymous note suggesting that these photos- Thomas really applying the pressure on Sheradin here with that armbar- that these photos were somehow related to Sheradin's biggest career achievement to date, his winning a tag team title in an organization operating in Canada- long commute for the Dreamer to get here to the world-famous Sportatorium each week. Last I saw, Joshua Barnes was looking for Randy to ask some questions- Joshua is not scheduled to be here this evening, so I don't know if we'll get a chance to ask him if he's had any success. Okay, Marc Thomas brings Sheridan back to his feet- he still has that armbar on- now he smashes his elbow onto Sheridan's extended, twisted arm. Now the Dreamer flips over, and reverses the pressure on Marc Thomas! Marc Thomas with a handful of hair as he drags Sheradin back up against the ropes, and they're going to have to break this hold. Ref calls for a clean break- ooh! Right cross to the chin by the Canadien Heavyweight champ. Now Sheradin spins him around, and a chop across the chest- another, and another! (Big pop) EL: Now Randy Sheradin with an Irish whip- dropkick! What height! Thomas back up- back down, as its an armdrag takedown. Thomas slides out under the bottom rope- he's going to take a little break, it seems. (Thomas takes his sweet old time walking around the ring, ignoring the jeers of the crowd, before climbing the ring steps, pausing before entering to remind the referee that Sheridan has to leave him room to get back in the ring) EL: Both men back in the ring now, lock up- hammerlock by Sheridan. Thomas ducks around behind him, reversing the hold- no he lifts the much smaller Sheridan- back suplex? No- atomic drop, right on the knee. Now he hooks Sheradin- Russian legsweep, and both men are back on the mat. Lateral press... 1... 2... kickout! Both men up- European uppercut by Thomas. There's another as he drives Sheradin back to the corner. Cross corner whip- reversed, and Thomas crashes into the turnbuckles. Sheradin after him- monkey flip brings Thomas back to midring! Sheradin off the ropes- wow! He went into a cartwheel as he kicked Thomas in the head- and Thomas bounces out to the floor. (Bigger pop) EL: Thomas slowly gets back up, and- oh, right. He's complaining about a hair pull. I suppose he thinks Sheradin pulled his hair with his toes on that last kick. And the referee plays it safe and cautions both men. Thomas up on the apron, and this time Sheradin isn't giving him a chance to get back in- he comes right up to the ropes- a punch by Thomas, and Sheridan returns one of his own! (Another pop) EL: Thomas' knees are a little wobbly- Sheradin sets him up, snap suplex, as he brings the man back into the ring the hard way. Randy Sheradin now with a frog splash... the cover... folks, we have to cut to a commercial! 1... if it ends here, we'll show it when we... 2... get back... (Sunday! Sunday! Sunday! Oh, you figure it out...) EL: We still have Marc Thomas and Randy Sheridan in the ring. Sheradin now with an Irish whip- Sheradin goes up, spins around on Thomas' shoulders- back down again- Victory roll! Let's see if the name really does apply... 1... 2... kickout! Sheradin off the ropes- cross body- Thomas spins him around into a belly to belly- 1... Sheradin kicks out. Thomas rakes the eyes, and he gets a stern warning for it. No warning ever undoes the damage, though. Thomas with a kick in the abdomen. He sets now- swinging neckbreaker- he got it. Devastating maneuver by one of the best technicians here in the CWAA. Marc Thomas heads for the corner, now. Up Thomas goes- big moonsault he hit it! This could be it- 1... 2...- kickout! Had he hooked the leg, he could have been one step closer to becoming the first ever CWAA Heavyweight Champion. He takes Sheradin back up- snap mare, into a reverse chinlock. He hasn't been able to finish the Dreamer off, so he's trying to soften him up a bit more. And, I see I'm being joined by one Max Robertson. Can I help you, Max? Max Robertson, over the last few weeks you've formed quite an alliance between yourself and 'The Dreamer' Randy Sheridan and with his friend, Joshua Barnes. My question to you is, what will become of this alliance if you and Mr. Sheridan should face one another to compete for the CWAA Heavyweight Championship? Max Robertson: Eric, you need to understand something about me. First and foremost I'm a professional wrestler. If I were to face Randy in the ring, I would do my best to beat him, and I'm sure he would do the same. That's the Professional Way. Don't get me wrong. My second goal is to help Randy anyway that I can. So long as that brings mine and Mr. Barnes' interests together, I will assist him as well. Ultimately, however it comes down to one thing. I wish them both, Randy and Mr. Barnes, all the best so long as their interests and mine do not conflict. That's the Professional Way. That's the only way. EL: Well, that's the kind of thing I like to hear. Both men putting out a very solid effort here tonight, Thomas in particular showing quite a lot. What he's showing at this moment is that a chinlock can be a very painful, wearying hold when applied correctly. (The generic "Go, Randy, Go!" chant starts) EL: Both men in excellent condition here, and each has shown an ability to withstand punishment, Very evenly matched, as each has very well-honed mat skills with the ability to go to the air when needed- that was a well-executed moonsault earlier by Thomas- you know when a football is well thrown it has a tight spiral, well, he had a very tight, crisp revolution on that maneuver, which just adds to the momentum with which he hits. And- well, here comes Joshua Barnes to the ringside area, supporting his friend, perhaps? Or maybe things HAVE changed. Joshua, come on over here, if you would? I ought to start charging people admission- this ringside area seems very popular of late, for some reason. Joshua, you've already been eliminated from the tourney, so I doubt you're scouting. Are you here to lend support to Randy Sheradin? (Joshua has trouble finding the right words, and is a little flustered by the attention he gets.) EL: Any word on those photographs you received last week? JB: Well... I wanted to ask Randy about the photos, but he's been busy, and our paths haven't crossed much this week... and it wasn't fair to ask him right before the tournament match...I want to get this straightened out... I mean, come on now... Randy wouldn't bribe a referee like that... would he? EL: I would find it hard to believe- elbowsmash to the cranium now by Thomas- he takes the Dreamer back up- Irish whip, and a big elbow right in the chin. Now he goes around behind the Dreamer- grips around the waist- release German suplex, dropping Sheridan right on the back of the neck, already softened by that neckbreaker. Thomas, very methodically, off the ropes- drops a knee right across the head. Now he picks him back up again- vertical suplex- no- Sheridan hops back down behind Thomas- Thomas turns- savate kick right under the chin, and Marc Thomas goes down! (pop) EL: Like I said, I don't think Randy Sheradin would use such tactics, and he denied it here tonight. I hope this all gets resolved before long, before the distractions start to interfere with match outcomes. At a time like this, everyone needs to be able to focus on their goals, and I need to focus on this match- we just saw a flying sunset flip by the Dreamer get a one count- people need to be able to focus on the task at hand, namely winning the CWAA Heavyweight Championship. JB: Umm... I'm going to... I think I'm going to go watch the match. EL: OK, Joshua, thanks for your time. JB: Umm... yeah. OK. (Heads back to the ring) EL: Sheradin now with a side headlock- into a running bulldog, and the tide in this one may have turned. I can't believe this, but we have to take another break. Should this one finish while we're gone, you'll see it all when we get back! (This Saturday, on WYXZ Channel 39, it's a Hercules film festival: back-to-back, "Hercules", "Hercules Unchained", "Hercules Against the Moon Men", and "Hercules and the Captive Women". All Hercules, all day. Staring at noon.) EL: OK- while we were gone, Randy Sheradin wen for a Suplex, and Marc Thomas came out of nowhere with his Icebreaker. (Diamond Cutter) He covered Sheradin too near the ropes to get the pin, though. Now Thomas grips him around the waist- Northern Lights suplex! He just dropped him right on the head, and its obvious that the neck has been an important target for Thomas in this contest. He pulls Sheradin back up- ddt coming up- no! Sheradin hooks his leg- small package! 1... 2... 3! (Huge pop) EL: He got it! Randy Sheradin pulls it out! Marc Thomas looks livid! All that effort, and he has nothing to show for it, as the Dreamer moves on to the second round of the title tournament! Marc Thomas pulled out all the stops in this one, and while he may not have gotten the duke, there can be no doubt in anyone's mind that he is going to be a major player here in the CWAA. -kl
