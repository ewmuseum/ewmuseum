Sunday, April 13, Card (2/3)

13 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Monday nights at 11:00 PM, here on WYXZ TV 39, join Fonzie, Potsie and all the gang for Happy Days. This week, the whole gang goes to a dude ranch. and are Richie and the Fonz competing for the same girl? Find out Mondays at 11, here on WYXZ TV 39. and at 11:30, it takes diff'rent strokes to move the world, yes it does.) EL: OK, before we head to our next big matchup, featuring "Hardcore" Joey Haven, let's take a look at this week's AEWA Extreme event, and the falls-count-anywhere-in-the-building match between local boy Joey Haven and Jak Hammer. (A still is shown of Joey Haven coming to the ring) EL: The AEWA was in Joey's hometown of Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, where the style of wrestling he likes to use first originated. (A still shows Haven kneelifting Hammer as Hammer came through the ropes.) EL: As always, the official start of the match mattered little to these two men, and they wasted no time in getting right down to it. (A still shows Haven choking Hammer with a t-shirt.) EL: Seconds into the match, and already disqualifications were threatened, as the brawling was out of control from the get-go. (A still shows Hammer slamming a forearm into Haven's kidney area.) EL: At times, getting the win seemed secondary to inflicting pain. (A still shows Hammer positioning to powerbomb Haven) EL: Jak Hammer's clear power advantage threatened to put a quick end to this one. (A still shows Hammer on the floor pulling away the safety mats to expose the floor) EL: Both men were prepared to do whatever it took to hurt their opponent. (A still shows Hammer grimacing after being whipped into the guard rail) EL: It took little time for this one to go out to the floor, and before long anything in or around the ring became a weapon. (A still shows Joey Haven with Jak Hammer in a small package on the floor) EL: On the floor, the same instincts that work in the ring are at work, and the real objective remains putting your opponent down for a three-count, no matter where. (A still shows Hammer with Haven in the Rebel Rack on the floor, while Haven grabs the middle rope) EL: It often seemed as though Jak Hammer's power and size advantage was still too much for the plucky home favorite to contend with. (A still shows Hammer arguing with the ref on the floor while Haven stands behind him on the ring apron, draping an arm around Hammer's neck/shoulders.) EL: The referee called for a break as Haven had the ropes, and when Hammer turned his attention away from Haven and to the official, Haven saw an opening. (A still shows the referee bailing out as Haven is bulldogging Hammer from the position shown in the prior still) EL: A dramatic moment, as Joey Haven, leaping from the ring apron, slammed Hammer's head into the arena floor. (A still shows Haven sweeping the announcers' equipment from their table.) EL: Joey Haven had his endgame planned, and was now in position to execute it. (A still shows Haven Joey-bombing (powerbomb from the middle rope) from the ring apron onto the empty announcers' table) EL: The Joey-bomb is a devastating maneuver on the canvas, but on the hardwood table, it was positively lethal. (A still shows the referee in midcount as Haven covers Hammer atop the table, which had not broken (the AEWA does not score its tables)) EL: And in the end, Joey Haven got his revenge. And here comes now! ("Highway to Hell" plays and the crowd pops like mad while Haven comes to the ring.) DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is set for one fall with a 15-minutes time limit. On his way to the ring now, from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, at 245 lb., here is "Hardcore" Joey Haven! (Haven is a mere 5'10"-245, and wears a simple back t-shirt bearing the word "Hardcore", and black monogrammed tights.) DM: And his opponent- (Marvin Gaye's "Sexual Healing" plays and the boos begin) (The beer-bellied ex-mechanic, in his grease-stained undershirt and grease-stained blue jeans) DM: Your referee is Jeff Howard. EL: Joey Haven and Gary Jackson in there. I don't see Jackson's partner, Eddie Intellecta, but you can be pretty certain that he and Henry King would be around here somewhere. So Joey Haven coming off perhaps the biggest win of his career, but he won't have time to rest on his laurels- the Hotshot is a tough customer. They lock up and Jackson goes to an arm bar, and Haven reverses it- and he twists it around once more- Irish whip- clothesline. Joey Haven whips him into the corner now- Jackson bounces out, and right into an Iron Claw! Haven just clamps it right on the head, and Jackson in obvious pain. He drives the Hotshot onto his back with the clawhold- referee Jeff Howard with the count, but Jackson gets the shoulder up. Jackson now with a rake of the eyes, and that'll break any hold. Gary Jackson now with a kick in the midsection. He backs Haven into the corner. Big chop by Jackson, and now he hip tosses Haven out of the corner. Jackson looks like he's looking to make the tag, but this is a solo contest- he's in there alone with Haven. Elbow smash by the Hotshot- now he whips Haven into the ropes- belly to belly... 1... 2... kickout by Hardcore Haven. Joey Haven in trouble now, as Gary Jackson chokes him on that top rope. (You guessed it: "Go, Joey, Go!") EL: Jackson now takes him out to midring- sets him up- swinging neckbreaker. He got it... 1... 2... kickout again. Snapmares Haven over- reverse chinlock. Haven reaches for the ropes, but he can't get them. Coming up in just a few minutes, we're going to have Lt. Lawson out here to defend his CWAA TV Title against "The Preacher" Kore Wrench. From now on, every week, we're going to have a CWAA Television Title championship match, and already Max Robertson has been signed for next week- if he isn't already the CWAA Heavyweight Champion- we'll talk about that in a few minutes, too. Jackson releases the chinlock and he stands Haven up- small package! Haven just wrapped him up...1 ... 2... Jackson kicks out, and he looks very frustrated about that- he sends Haven for the ride- running knee- Haven tumbles to the side to avoid it- Jackson spins around- dropkick by Haven! Back up- and another! Now a third dropkick, and Jackson's looking a little woozy. Haven sets him up- vertic- no! Brain buster- he just dropped the Hotshot on his head! Let's face it, it's not the strongest part of his body. Now Haven balances himself on the middle rope- sets him up- Joeybomb coming up I think- yes... 1... 2... 3! He got it! (Big pop as "Highway to Hell" resumes) EL: Joey Haven, on a roll. First beating Jak Hammer at AEWA Extreme, now picking up the duke here, When we get back, Paul Langford and Byron McNultey- without their partners! (...if you're a veteran, you can't be turned down- regardless of your condition!) EL: Ok, we're back, everyone, and we've got half of a rematch from last week, where Twister defeated Byron McNultey, the Pretty Boy, and Marvelous Michael Anderson in the finals of the CWAA Tag Team title tournament. This week, Paul Langford- who with his partner, Travis Conley, puts those belts on the line against- get this- Joshua Barnes and "Dreamer" Randy Sheradin!- goes one on one with the Pretty Boy. (Big pop as the "Twister" theme starts anew, and Langford, previously described, comes to the ring with Mother Nature) DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is set for one fall with a 20-minute time limit. Introducing first, from Omaha, Nebraska, weighing in at 244 lb., he is one-half of the CWAA Tag Team Champions, Paul "the Tornado" Langford! (I've already described him once tonight) DM: And his opponent- (ZZ Top's "Sharp Dressed Man" plays, and the cheers are magically transformed into jeers as McNultey and Anderson emerge) DM: -from parts unknown, he weighs, 243 lb., here is "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey! (Byron stands 5'10"-243 in his sequined red robe clashes with the blue pants (which bar the inscription "Pretty Boy" roughly where Langford wear a little Twister logo). Anderson, at 5'11"-254, is dressed in a white tuxedo with a teal cummerbum.) DM: Your referee, Chris Herman. EL: Ok, we are just about to start, and- well, I see Mother Nature is joining me to call this one. Good evening, I trust you're well. MN: Hello, Eric. Can't complain- I do manage the CWAA Tag Team Champions, after all. EL: Indeed you do. OK Langford and McNultey locking up, and- oh, would you look at this. Michael Anderson: Scootch over a little, will you? There's barely enough room for two people, never mind the three of us. EL: Who invited you? MA: Same person who invited her. Am I on? Testing... testing. EL: OK- Paul Langford with an armdrag takedown- and another, into an arm bar now. Interesting the way Langford's quickness and mat skills blend with Conley's power game, Mother Nature. Did you set out to develop a team which combined these skills, or did your charges just come together like that? MN: Well- MA: Oh, just get a room, you two. Now I'm out here watching this Langford character break every rule in the book and a few that won't be in the book until the second edition comes out, and not a peep from you. But if I just happen to use a handful of quart- I mean, when I go for a simple side headlock, the bells are ringing like cry, women and children are running and screaming, and you, David Crockett, sound like civilization is crumbling. EL: I'm not even going to answer that, as Lang- MA: Well, you just did. Woo-hoo. Can I tell you something, Lane? EL: No matter what I say, I'm sure you will. McNultey with a side headlock now. MA: Other people may come out and complain about what happened last week- MN: People who lose title matches in less than ten seconds, you mean? MA: If you'll excuse me, I believe I have the floor. See, a lot of people who come out here and complain- EL: Irish whip- shoulderblock by Langford. MA: Are you listening? EL: Don't have a choice, do we? MA: I'm not out here to complain about wrongs committed against us last week, because Byron and I are the kind of people who are always thinking forward, never looking back. Do you know why, David? EL: That's Eric. MA: Byron and I are always looking forward to tomorrow because we just get prettier every single day. Can you imagine how we'll look in a week? A month? MN: Could we please concentrate on the match? EL: Yes, thank you. Paul Langford with an armbar on the Pretty Boy. MA: As long as they don't hit him in the face- he's got a shoot tomorrow, you know. MN: I never heard about his being a model. MA: Well, try reading something other than the John Deere catalogs or Hayseed's Digest. EL: All right now, there's no call for that. You know, Michael, your partner is in there and not looking very good right now against Paul Langford. MA: Not a worry, David, not a worry. What I am worried about is this- I hear that next week you've got a title match signed between Blister and Jack and the Beanpole- EL: I believe you mean "Twister versus Joshua Barnes and Randy Sheridan". MA: Is this a mixed tag match or something? I thought Sheradin was the CWAA Midget's Champ? (to be continued)
