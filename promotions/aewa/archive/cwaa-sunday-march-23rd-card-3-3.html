Sunday, March 23rd, Card (3/3)

23 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Since Midnight Rampage are already in the ring area after the Twister-Lethal Weapons encounter...) DM: This next contest is the final first round match in the CWAA Tag Team Title Tournament, set for one fall with a fifteen minute time limit. In the ring to my left, accompanied by their manager, Tip Smith, at a combined weight of 520 lb., Healer's Havoc, Big Dog Brown, the team of Midnight Rampage! (Crowd pops) DM: And their opponents, representing the ACWF, here are Hans and Franz, the High School Toughies! (Crowd boos, not for anything the Toughies have done (although some of the Texas fans are a little familiar with them, at least by reputation), but because they are facing a team for the fans are accustomed to cheering.) DM: Your referee for this bout, Chris Herman. EL: OK- I don't have a lot of information on the Toughies to share with you, but I can tell you that those jackets they are hanging in their corner are a source of great pride for them and they are sometimes more interested in making sure no one touches them than they are in winning their matches. There's the bell, and the Big Dog to start off against Hans, I believe that one is. Lock up, and a hip toss by the Big Dog. Hans right back up, charges BD- and BD scoops him up- body slam. Hans is insisting that Brown grabbed him by the tights. Hans back up, and he quickly applies the standing arm bar- and the Big Dog reverses it. Brown with the Irish whip now, and a clothesline. Hans rolls out to the floor for a breather. Well, the Toughies come to us from the Atlantic Coast Wrestling Federation, and on April 14th, the ACWF will be presenting it's pay per view extravaganza Rule Britannia! from Wembley Stadium. An agreement has been reached- Hans back in the ring now, and a lock up leads to a side headlock applied by the Toughie- an agreement has been reached for the ACWF to fly in some CWAA stars to make an appearance on the event. Nothing is definite yet, but it looks like a Joshua Barnes-Max Robertson bout may happen there. By then, Max may be the CWAA Heavyweight Champion- BD with an elbow to Hans' midsection, and another, and that'll break the hold. (Franz complains about the legality of the elbows.) EL: Among the issues that need to be resolved are whether or not Max Robertson would be forced to put the CWAA title on the line in that match should he defeat Randy Sheradin in their match the previous week. BD tags in Healer's Havoc, the Real Deal, and Havoc immediately goes for a boot to the midsection that knocks the breath out of Hans. Back to the Rule Britannia! event, Joshua Barnes had asked for a bunkhouse match, as you heard earlier, and Max has come back with his own preferred stipulations. There are one or two other points with should be taken into consideration- Healer's Havoc now with a snap suplex... the cover... 1... kickout. Healer's Havoc snap mares him over- elbow smash to the head, now a kick in the back- and he whips Hans into the Midnight Rampage corner and tags in BD Brown again. Like I was saying, a few things to consider; first, the ACWF event in England will b on the same day as a CWAA event here in the world-famous Sportatorium, so there is little chance of a Lt. Lawson or someone else getting involved, except, and this is my second point- as BD Brown takes Hans down with a legsweep faceslam- one other CWAA who will be there is the one CWAA wrestler who fights with the ACWF. You guessed it, "The Dreamer" Randy Sheradin. (While Lane continues to go on about a yet-to-be-signed match, Brown whips Hans into the Toughie corner, where Franz tags in) EL: Now, one of Max's counter-proposals was for a lumberjack match. Well, if that is what's signed, both me will be surrounded by up to 20 strangers, plus the Dreamer. Franz in there now, reverses an Irish whip- back body drop by Franz- lateral press... 1... 2... kickout. Franz now with a snap mare into a reverse chinlock. Coming up later tonight, we still have the AEWA World Light Heavyweight Championship match, and we still have to hear from challenger Marc Thomas- it will be interesting to see which man has the crowd's support, or even if either does. Franz now tags in Hans- double whip- double elbow in the chest by the Toughies. Hans with the cover... 1... 2... kickout. We're going to have to take a break- when we get back, we'll have the conclusion of this match. (...if you're a veteran, you can not be turned down. No medical exam, no embarrassing questions. Just piece of mind and guaranteed coverage....) EL: Hans has BD Brown in the neutral corner- cross corner whip, and Brown hits the turnbuckles hard. Now Hans follows him in- an no one's home! The Big Dog bailed out! Now the Dog with a kick in the midsection- he sets him up- powerbomb by the Orlando native. he makes the tag to Healer's Havoc, (Pop) EL: Franz in to break it up, but Brown catches him with a leaping clothesline. Referee Chris Herman checks for the submission- and he's calling for the bell! Midnight Rampage advances to the second round. DM: Here are your winners as the result of a submission, Healer's Havoc, BD Brown, Midnight Rampage! EL: So Franz looks very unhappy out on the floor, and he pulls his partner out under the bottom rope-and Healer's Havoc in what was the Toughie corner now, and he has one of those two jackets- he's motioning that he's going to tear it, and the Toughies are just beside themselves on the floor. Havoc asks the crowd if he should do it. (They are answering in the affirmative.) EL: And the tosses it out at the feet of the Toughies. Franz scrambles to grab the other one. So next week McNultey and Anderson meet Sheradin and Barnes, and Twister faces Midnight Rampage. But we still have our special feature match this week, as Marc Thomas goes for the AEWA World Light Heavyweight Title. Earlier today, we caught up with Thomas in the locker room, and here is what he had to say. ****************************************************** EL: Marc, you are about to be in the biggest match so far in CWAA history! What are your feelings about this match with Joey Valachi? MT: Eric, I've been in many different feds over the past few years and no fed can hold a candle to the CWAA. I respect good competition and good wrestling and the CWAA has both. Look at the AEWA, Valachi has held onto his title by beating on guys who would be pushovers here in the CWAA. Valachi thinks that since he's got a belt that he is god's gift to wrestling. Well, let me tell you something Joey! I've beaten some of the best wrestlers around and I'm having trouble thinking of you as on par with them. Yeah, you look at the CWAA and see guys like Joshua Barnes and Joey Haven and think that these guys are amateurs. They may be new to wrestling but they've got more guts in their little fingers than you've got altogether. Remember the Italian Loan Collection Agency Joey? How about all of those goofy disguises you used to hide from them? Not to mention that toy belt you used to carry around with you. Just because you've got a belt, doesn't mean you deserve it. You beat guys that make Aldo Montoya look like Hulk Hogan. Just remember Joey, you can't call yourself a champion until you've beaten the best and Joey, I'm a Champion. EL: Are you nervous about this match? MT: Nervous.....Please! Joey is probably hiding in a closet now hoping I can't make it to the ring. You know that the execs of the AEWA are really nervous because I finally have a well deserved title shot, something that always seemed to allude me in the AEWA. Freeman, you and your yesmen can't do anything about this match. I'm gonna beat Valachi and take the belt to a place where the wrestlers know the meaning of competition. Once that belt is mine, I dare you to send your best wrestlers to get it back. Let's see...you could send the Holy Goat or El Idiot to try and retrieve it. And if that's the best you got, then that belt will remain in the CWAA for a long time. ******************************************************************* EL: OK- let's go to David Myers for the introductions. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this contest is for the AEWA World Light Heavyweight Championship, and is set for one fall with television time remaining. First, the challenger, from Charlotte, North Carolina, weighing in at 240 lb., here is Marc Thomas! ("Life in the Fast Lane" plays, and the Valachi-haters cheer while the Thomas-haters boo; neither man has any real fans in this arena- just people who hate his opponent more than they hate him. Thomas slowly walks to the ring and, after he removes his sleeveless leather jacket starts stretching his arm and leg muscles.) DM: And his opponent, weighing in at 202 lb., he is the AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion of the World, "the Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi! (Pat Benatar's "Gettin' Paid" begins playing loudly, and Joey Valachi makes his entrance to a slightly more negative reaction from the crowd than Thomas had received. He's wearing his "AEWA Sucks" T-shirt, and appears to be regarding the entire state of Texas with a colossal contempt.) DM: Your referee for this contest, Jeff Howard. EL: OK- a couple of guys who don't seem to care much for following the rules are in there with senior official Jeff Howard, and after the incident with the Lethal Weapons, I don't know that Jeff Howard is going to be in he mood to take much guff. There's the bell, and Valachi still has the t-shirt on as he stands on the ring apron. Jeff Howard ordering him into the ring, and Valachi does not seem at all interested in listening. Howard motioning for this match to start as Thomas waits on the other side of the ring. Valachi wants the ref to make sure Thomas gives him plenty of room to get in the ring; I don't know what he's so worried about- Thomas is way over on the other side of the ring. Valachi coming through the top two ropes now- and Thomas feigns a lunge at him and Valachi slams on the brakes and waits on the apron again! He's laying into referee Jeff Howard about this. Something about how the bush leagues should treat world champs with more respect, it sounded like. He's about to find out what life in the so-called bush leagues is like, I'd wager. OK- he's coming back into the ring again- and Thomas takes two steps towards him and Valachi again stops midway through the ring and stays on the apron. Jeff Howard starts to count now- well, I suppose Joey Valachi could take the count out loss and go home none the poorer. 4... 5... Valachi seems unimpressed- but Thomas is not going to wait anymore, as he ropeflips the Cardiac Kid into the ring! (And Marc Thomas gets the first face pop of his CWAA career.) EL: Valachi still in that offensive t-shirt, and that title belt still around his waist- Thomas with an Irish whip- dropkick, and the Cardiac Kid hits the mat. Now Thomas with a spin kick as Valachi gets back up, and Valachi is sent to the floor. Now where's he going? Joey Valachi heading up the ring aisle as Jeff Howard starts his count, and Marc Thomas out after him- Thomas grabs him by the collar of that t-shirt and by his hair and runs him right back to the ring- rolls him under the bottom rope. Now Thomas coming in under the bottom rope, and Joey Valachi with a stomp, and another. Joey Valachi kicking away at Marc Thomas, now he gets down and starts choking him on that bottom rope- Jeff Howard starts the 5-count, and Valachi breaks on 4. Now he does it again! He breaks on 4, so there was no grounds for disqualification, but that in no way ameliorates the pain Marc Thomas was just suffering. OK- Joey Valachi finally takes off his belt, and off comes the t-shirt, and now he wraps the t-shirt around Marc Thomas' neck and starts choking again! Irish whip by Valachi, and a knee in the midsection. Valachi backs up running kneelift. (Valachi turns and asks the crowd, "Is this the best you can do?") EL: Joey Valachi whips Thomas into the corner now- and a big reverse knife edge slicing across Thomas' chest. And another. Now Joey with a hip toss out of the corner, and a reverse chinlock. He pulls back on the chin while leaning forward with his knee high up in Thomas' back. You now, Marc Thomas is the one wrestler in the CWAA who has been in the AEWA, and he says he was sent here by Bob Freeman. I don't know what that is all about, but I do know winning this title was his goal, was why he was in the AEWA in the first place, but the opportunity never came up. He deeply resented what he saw as a demotion to the minor leagues, and two months later, here is in the ring with the holder of the belt he wanted in the first place. Now Joey Valachi with a neck snap. Lateral press... 1... 2... kickout. Valachi stands him up now- abdominal stretch. He grapevines the leg, but can't get the optimal leverage until he gets that right foot on the mat. As referee Jeff Howard checks for a submission, Joey Valachi pulls on the top rope for leverage! Now he lets go before he gets caught, but surely Jeff Howard must see the rope jiggling. We're going to take a break now, but don't you dare touch that dial! (...an' if you kin fine lower prices anywhere, my name ain't Nathan Arizona!)) EL: OK- we're back, and Joey Valachi still has Marc Thomas in that abdominal stretch, and once more he has gotten away with grabbing that top rope while you were gone. He goes for it again- and this time he's caught red-handed! Jeff Howard with a kick that dislodges Valachi's hand from the top rope- and it allows Marc Thomas to hip toss out of the hold! (Pop again from the parts of the audience who regard Thomas as the lesser of two evils.) EL: Valachi gets right back up and kicks Thomas in the chest. Irish whip now- Thesz press by the champ... 1... 2... kickout. Valachi complains about the slow count- he picks Thomas back up- small package by Thomas... 1... 2... kickout. A frustrated Valachi with a right cross now. Irish whip- Thomas leap frogs- Thomas off of the opposite ropes- clothesline by Thomas! Now gets down in a half Boston crab on the champ, pulling back on that left leg. Both men seem to be favoring the slower pace. Thomas releases the hold- stomp in the back. Thomas sits him up now- sleeperhold! Jeff Howard checks to make sure it isn't a chokehold as Valachi struggles to get to his feet. He wastes valuable energy flailing his arms and legs to break loose to no avail. Valachi fading... he elbows Thomas in the midsection. Hits it again- a third time. Valachi free now, but clearly worn down. Marc Thomas now with a full nelson- into a suplex- dragon suplex, that is... 1... 2... kickout! (Some popping from the Thomas-haters and groans from the Valachi-haters.) EL: Now Thomas rolls him over- hammerlock on the mat. Valachi in obvious pain, and Marc Thomas in complete control right now. Thomas applying more pressure on that arm now, and Valachi kicking the mat in pain and frustration. Now Thomas stands him up- and sends him for the ride- knee in the midsection. Valachi doubled over- and Thomas with a ddt- the cover... 1... 2... kickout! Thomas picks him up- side suplex. Lateral press- hooks the tights... 1... 2... kickout! Thomas backs him into a corner- drives his shoulder into the midsection- snap mare out of the corner. Thomas with a superkick now, and he waits for Valachi to get back up- Icebreaker! (i.e. Diamond Cutter) EL: The cover- again hooks the tights- 1... 2... Valachi gets his boot over the bottom rope. We're starting to run out of television time here- I can tell you now that our special feature match next week will see Marc Thomas and Jak Hammer in tag team action against Joey Haven and a mystery partner, and we should have more information on the Barnes-Robertson match in England, and perhaps footage from AEWA Extreme where Haven and Hammer go falls-count-anywhere. Thomas stands Valachi in the corner- chop across the chest- cross corner whip- reversed- Valachi whips Thomas into the corner- Stinger splash- no one home- Valachi bounces out to mid ring- folks, I'm sorry, but we are out of time and this match is over- Icebreaker again- there's the bell- we just plum ran out of time- we'll see you next week!
