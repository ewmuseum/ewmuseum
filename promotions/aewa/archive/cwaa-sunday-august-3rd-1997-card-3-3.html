Sunday, August 3rd, 1997, Card (3/3)

6 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EL: OK- we're back and ready for more action- but first, we'll talk with the other half of tonight's Heavyweight Championship bout. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome my guest at this time, the mysterious Masked Man! (As Alan Parsons Project's "Siris" plays, the crowd boos so loudly that the rats in the basement have the scare of their lives) MM: Well, here we go again! Me and Robertson for the CWAA Heavyweight Title. We all know who won the first match. Did Freeman really think that his little tournament was going to change the fact that I am the best wrestler here in the CWAA. All he did by creating this tournament was breaking up Hammer and Thomas and giving Max and myself warm-up matches before tonight's match for the belt. Not only has this tournament been a complete joke, it has done nothing to change the course of events in terms of the CWAA Heavyweight Title. After tonight, I will once again be the Heavyweight Champion, just like I was before that idiot Freeman and his band of merry (expletive deleted)kissers decided to have this little tournament. I am putting the world on notice that this title is just a stepping stone to greater things in my career. I hear that the AEWA Champion is scheduled to face the winner of tonight's match in a couple of weeks. (pop for Crimson Joe Reed) MM: Reed, polish that belt up real nice and take a couple of Polaroids of it because in a few weeks those will be the only reminders you have of being the AEWA Heavyweight Champion. As for Robertson, all those technical moves won't save your (expletive deleted) when I put your shoulders on the mat for a three count. You call yourself "The Professional," but after tonight the only professional you'll be linked to is the one that removes my foot from your (expletive deleted.) (Exit MM as the booing continues) DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is for the CWAA Television Championship! (pop) DM: Introducing first, the challenger- (The Twister theme plays) DM: -from Omaha, Nebraska, at 315 pounds, accompanied by his manager, Mother Nature, he is former co-holder of the CWAA Tag Team championship- Travis Conley! (pop as the 6'10" youngster follows his manager to the ring) Travis Conley: Hammer, I hope you do not think that I am taking this match lightly. Just because Paul and I are the best tag team does not mean we each don't want a piece of the individual gold that is in the CWAA. You were knocked silly last week by a 200-pound man. How do you think you are going to fare against a man over 100 pounds heavier. Ask McNultey how it feel to be out cold from the power of the Husker Slam! 1..2..3, that belt will be mine! DM: And his opponent- (More popping as "Bad to the Bone" plays and the leather clad 310-pounder arrives) DM: from Detroit, Michigan, at 310 pounds, he is the reigning CWAA Television Champion, Jak Hammer! Your referee, Chris Herman. EL: OK- a pair of real giants in the ring, both 6'10" and well over 300 pounds. Collar and elbow tie up- look at these two athletes straining muscle and thew- and Conley drives Hammer back on his heels- back to the ropes- what power! Chris Herman calls for the clean break- and he gets it- Conley backs off cautiously. they circle each other, looking for an opening- lock up- and Jak Hammer to a standing hammerlock, behind Conley. Travis Conley reaches for the ropes- Chris Herman calls for the break. They lock up again- and Jak Hammer slaps on a side headlock. Conley caught in that viselike grip. What about that big feature match tonight- the Masked Man and Max Robertson tangle once more, and this time the Preacher is the referee. No monkey business with him in charge, I'm sure. Conley pushes off- Hammer off the ropes- shoulderblocks the challenger- couldn't budge him an inch! (pop for an actual CWAA no-sell) EL: Jak Hammer to a standing wristlock- and Conley turns it right over! Jak Hammer sends him for the ride- clothesline! And Conley was just about knocked out of his boots. Mother Nature shouting encouragement, but Jak Hammer off the ropes with a knee drop. Now a front face lock by Hammer, with those amazingly powerful arms. Hammer squeezing away- and Conley grabs him by the ankles- double leg cradle! 1... kickout! Both men up now- and Jak Hammer shoves the challenger- Conley pushes back- tempers frayed now- Hammer with a right hand now- Conley fires back! Trading blows in mid-ring now- and Hammer misses a right hand! Conley scoops him up- body slam! (pop) EL: Off the rope- big elbow drop! Travis Conley takes control, can he walk away the new TV Champion? Down in a three-point stance- big football tackle knocks the champ on his back! Picks him up- drops him across the knee- backbreaker by the Husker, who has the champ on the run! 1... 2... kickout! Conley sends him for the ride- running clothesline! Now a standing arm bar- he's going to try and wear down the champ's arm strength, it looks like. Conley could well become the first two-time champion in CWAA history if he pulls off the win tonight- he and Paul Langford were tag team champions before, and could become champion again on Sunday the 24th as they take on the Pretty Boy and Michael Anderson in Reunion Arena! What an all-star lineup that will be- we'll be talking about that show a lot more in the coming weeks. Jak Hammer reverses the arm bar- sends him for the ride- back body drop, and Conley hits the canvas. As he gets up, Jak meets him with a kick in the midsection- knocks the air clear out of his lungs. Big kneelift, and now Jak Hammer applies the bearhug. Travis Conley's already had the air kicked out of him, and now he's caught in that tremendous bearhug. We're going to take a quick break, and when we get back, we'll show you the rest of this exciting matchup! Stay tuned! (Clap on! Clap off!....) EL: OK- we're back, Jak Hammer has retaken control of this one, with Travis Conley caught in that bearhug. ("Go, Travis, Go!") EL: Mother Nature leading the crowd as they cheer the young ex-Husker on- Jak has his share of fans here tonight as well. Travis Conley now with a forearm clap! And again- and Hammer drops him! (pop) EL: Travis Conley off the ropes- Hammer ducks- back body drop- and the momentum carries him right over the top rope! Travis Conley all the way out to the floor- and he hit the apron on the way down! Jak Hammer now waiting in the ring- Mother Nature helps her charge to his feet- and Jak with a kick in the head! Travis Conley in a bad way out on the floor, now. He's up on his feet- he looks a little woozy- up on the apron- and Hammer off the ropes- he just clotheslined Conley to the floor from the apron! Conley landed on his back on the arena floor! He looks like he hit his head on that concrete floor! (Chris Herman begins his count) EL; He's up to 4 now, and Conley just now regaining his footing. He's staggering up the ring steps- it was one nasty spill that kid took, but he's a tough one. Up on the ring apron- 8- and Jak Hammer with a fist right between the eyes- and Chris Herman breaks the count and warns him to let the man back in the ring. Jak Hammer brushes him aside- sets him up- suplexes the kid right into the ring, over the top rope! Jak Hammer in complete control, now. Covers- 1... 2... no! Conley kicks out! (pop) EL: Jak Hammer sends him for the ride- reversed- no! Re-reversed- and Hammer catches him in a sleeper! He's got him in the Noose- the new sleeperhold Jak Hammer has been working on. Travis Conley hanging on, but he's fading. Mother Nature urging him on, but he took that terrible fall, and now he's stuck in mid-ring. Chris Herman checks- once... twice.. yes, he's out. Jak Hammer has just put Travis Conley to sleep. Let's get the official word. (As Chris Herman slaps Conley's back to snap him out of it) DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, by submission, and still CWAA Television Champion, Jak Hammer! (The reaction is mixed, as the crowd seemed to warm up to Conley as the match went on, but Hammer still has his supporters) EL: OK- when we get back from these messages, we'll have the Championship match- will the next champion be the Professional or the mysterious Masked Man? There's only one way to find out! Stay tuned! (Scene: The vacant Sportatorium, with a ring in the center. In the middle of the ring is a bed, and in front of the bed is "The Dreamer" Randy Sheridan.) Sheridan: Hi, I'm Randy Sheridan. People call me the Dreamer, because I dream of doing big things as a wrestler. But in order to have those dreams, I need a good night's sleep, and to get that, I need a mattress from Peggy Hanson at Hanson's Mattress Outlet Store. (Camera concentrates on the bed as Sheridan continues. It is kind of a nice mattress.) Sheridan: At Hanson's they have the right kind of mattress to fit your body...and your wallet. Because this week only, you can get a Sealy mattress for only $59. Mattress and box springs start at only $99! (Cut back to a wide shot. Sheridan is now standing on the top rope.) Sheridan: With deals like that on top quality mattresses...it's no contest. (Sheridan leaps off the top rope, and lands on the bed. Upon landing, he sighs, and appears to instantly drift off into a pleasant slumber. Suddenly, Peggy Hanson, wearing referee's stripes, comes into the scene.) Peggy Hanson: 1, 2, 3! Score another victory for Hanson's Mattress Outlet Store. Only 15 minutes from Downtown! (Fades to a map of the greater Dallas area, showing you the fastest way to the store. A phone number is also included for your convenience. Wow, these local businessmen think of everything!) EL: OK, we are back and ready for the special feature match, and, when it's all said and done, we will finally once again have a CWAA Heavyweight Champion. it has been over a month since AEWA Commissioner Bob Freeman overturned what looked lie a title win for the Masked Man and declared the championship vacant. Randy Sheradin and Jak Hammer have both gone down in defeat since, and now it is back down to Max Robertson and the Masked Man! DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next bout is set for one fall with television time remaining, and is for the vacant CWAA Heavyweight Championship! (pop) DM: Introducing first, the special guest referee, the Preacher, Kore Wrench! (enormous pop as generic Gregorian chant plays and the Preacher arrives in refereeing garb) DM: And now the competitors. First- ("Siris" plays again, and the crowd's mood goes dark) DM: -from parts unknown, weight unknown, here is the mysterious Masked Man! (He looks to be within a few inches of 5'11" and somewhere in the vicinity of 235-245 pounds, dressed in all black, and with a new black mask- Sheradin has the old one, of course.) DM: And his opponent- (The Darth Vader "Imperial March" plays, and the crowd apparently is rooting against both men in this one as the red-and-black-clad 200 pound bald former champ arrives at ringside. During this, the Preacher inspects the Masked Man's tights and hands for foreign objects.) DM: -from San Francisco, California, at 200 pounds, here is former CWAA Heavyweight Champion, Max "the Professional" Robertson! (Max is accompanied by occasional partner Lt. Lawson. Neither Lawson nor Wrench has fully recovered from their encounters earlier in the evening.) EL: We're underway, and the Masked Man doesn't even want to let Max in the ring, meeting him with a kneelift as he comes through the ropes! Now the Masked Man with a right hand to the head. Kicks him in the ribs- the Masked Man sending the message that this one is going to be a fight! Irish whip- reversed- and Max catches him off the ropes with a sharp chop across the chest! (something of a pop) EL: Now a spin kick! Amazing agility in the former champ- the Masked Man could never see that coming! Sends him for the ride- back body drop by the Professional! The Masked Man's early attack profits him nothing! Max Robertson to an arm bar- the Masked Man does not want to match him hold for hold- he'll never be able to keep up. He needs to fight his style of match- brawling tactics mixed with the science, and to take advantage of his size and power advantage. The Masked Man reverses- and Max re-reverses! The Masked Man in trouble early on, and he reaches for the top rope- got it. The Preacher calling for a clean break. He's got at least 50 pounds on both of them- these competitors are going to be sure to not arouse his anger, and they comply. Collar and elbow tie-up, and Max with a rake of the eyes! Wait a second! (Mixed reaction as Jak Hammer strides down the aisle and stops at the foot of the aisle, on the opposite side of the ring from Lawson) EL: Jak Hammer out here now, and the Preacher warning him to stay out of this match. In the meantime Max has hit a hip toss, and now an arm drag takedown. Why is Jak Hammer here- he's had his problems with Lawson, but I wonder if he is out here again to come to the aid of the Masked Man- with whom I thought he had no friendship. Max with the Irish whip- stomach claw! (pop) EL: Max clamps his grip on the Masked Man's midsection, and the Masked Man in excruciating pain. Max tightening his grip- look at the concentration in his eyes! Lawson and Hammer, both down at ringside- Lawson accompanied Max, Jak just arrived now. The Masked Man goes to the eyes, and that breaks the hold, but the damage was done. The Masked Man know smacks him with a kneelift. And he tosses the former champ out to the arena floor- perhaps buying himself a quick chance to catch his breath. (pop) EL: Lawson over to watch his friend's back, but Hammer hasn't budged an inch. Lawson helping the Professional up- looks like Max landed a little awkwardly. Lawson rolls Max into the ring, and the Masked Man is waiting there to stomp away- puts the boots to him. Picks him up now- Samoan drop! 1... 2... kickout! The Masked Man now to an abdominal stretch, going to work on the midsection. (camera cuts to the crowd momentarily) EL: And there's the Lady in Red- manager extraordinaire, representative of AEWA World Heavyweight Champion. Crimson Joe Reed, and he'll be facing the winner of this contest in a 2-of-3 falls bout in just a few weeks in Reunion Arena. I guess the Lady in Red is here scouting the opposition for the champ, who always takes great pains to be thoroughly prepared for his defenses. The Masked Man still with that abdominal stretch, but he doesn't have the leg grapevined- he only has the one foot on the mat. Max could hip toss his way out of this- and here he goes- no! The Masked Man was one step ahead and reverses it into a backslide- 1... 2... kickout! (some popping, some groans) EL: And the Masked Man not at all happy with the Preacher's count, and he gets right in his face about it. And the Preacher takes no guff- he's giving the Masked Man what for right back. And now the Masked Man gives him a shove- and the Preacher shoves him right back! (pop) EL: And as the Masked Man stumbles back, Max catches him in a roll-up- 2 count only! Now both wrestlers are getting in the Preacher's face over his officiating- look out! (huge pop) EL: And Kore Wrench just gave both a double-noggin knocker- a meeting of the minds, if you will, as the Preachers smacks their heads together! Lawson up on the ring apron now- and the Preacher is out of patience- he just slugged the Preacher and knocked him back to the floor! Meanwhile, Max with a monkey flip, now a legdrag takedown on the Masked Man, into a half-Boston crab. (Appreciative pop. Like him or hate him, the Professional is the best technical wrestler in the CWAA today.) EL: The Masked Man in trouble now as the Professional is now wrestling his type of match. He has his opponent down on the mat, tied up in a submission hold. Max tugging back on the right leg. And look at Jak Hammer! He's pushing the bottom rope toward the Masked Man so he can reach it to force a break! But the Preacher caught them in the act! He kicks the Masked Man's hand off the rope! (pop) EL: And now we know whom Jak Hammer is here to help- that's the first time he's moved since he came down here a few minutes into this match. The Masked Man still trapped in that half-Boston crab as the Preacher gives Jak Hammer a real earful. The Masked Man now reaches the rope on his own, and the Preacher instructs the Professional to release the man. Max now stomps away- picks him up- side suplex. Max off the ropes now- wow! (pop) EL: Somersault legdrop! He covers- this will be it- 1... 2... kickout! He almost had him there! Almost had a new- and an old- champion right there. Max with the Irish whip now- catches him- flying hangman! Dropped the Masked Man's throat right over that top rope/ Back and forth action all evening in this one- the stakes are so, so high. Now Max with a superkick! Superkick! (pop) EL: Lateral press- 1... 2... kickout again! Max picks him u- small package! (pop) EL: 1... 2... Max reverses it! 1... 2... the Masked Man rolls him back.... 1... 2.... kickout! Three or four near falls right there- so close, so close! Both men up now, and the Masked Man with a closed fist to the head. The Preacher warns him- and he hits Max with a closed fist again. (pop) EL: But the Preacher with a punch for the Masked Man instead! The Masked Man is stunned! Max with a back suplex- 1.... 2.... no! He kicks out again! Up again- hip toss by Max- blocked! The Masked Man with the hip toss now- now a lariat! He just clotheslined Max and the momentum has shifted again! The Masked Man sets him up- 1... 2... swinging neckbreaker- he hit it! (pop) EL: Covers- 1... 2... no! And- hey! Out on the floor, someone has just come down the aisle and jumped Jak Hammer! He looks familiar... (A bald gentlemen in a black t-shirt, black jeans, and black boots, sporting a van dyke, and standing about 6'2"-240 is the man in question) EL: Kore Wrench now heading to the scuffle to break- is that Ron Stern? The partner of Marc Thomas? In Canada, they wrestled together as the Icemen- why is he here? who's with whom anymore? The Preacher runs off Stern- Jak Hammer is down, shaking the cobwebs out. In the ring, the Masked Man reaching to his left boot! He has something- brass knuckles? Wrench still trying to chase off Stern- Lawson in the ring now, behind the Masked Man! He grabs the hand- he's got the knuckles! He took the knuckles! (pop) EL: Stern has disappeared- the Preacher back to the ring- and Lawson is in the wrong place, at the wrong time! Wrench spins Lawson around- Max still down- and decks the Lt.! (pop) EL: Spins him again- atomic kneedrop- those knuckles went flying! Lawson falls to the floor! And the Masked Man has picked the knuckles! He has them back! Wrench making sure Lawson stays out of the ring! Max up again on shaky legs- behind the Masked Man- back suplex- and the Masked Man jams his fist in Max's face! He used the brass knuckles! They fall back- 1... 2... 3! I don't believe it! Max Robertson was nailed with those brass knuckles, and the Masked Man is the new champion! Max looks just about out of it! Let's get the official word- look at the Masked Man stuffing those knuckles in his tights now. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, and new CWAA Heavyweight Champion- the mysterious Masked Man! (mixed pop) EL: Wow! And Kore Wrench raises the hand of a man who once jumped him from behind! Jak Hammer, Lt. Lawson and Ron Stern all climbing in the ring now- we can't go anywhere while this is going on. Lawson puling Max to the outside, where it's safer. Stern and Hammer standing nose to nose- and Stern has o look up to do it, but he looks completely unintimidated. The Masked Man holds his title belt high- the Preacher spins him around- pops him right in the mush! (pop) EL: Referee Kore Wrench just nailed the Masked Man, and this crowd is on its feet! Wrench pulling on the mask now! The Dreamer got it last week- can the Preacher unmask the new champion? Ron Stern and Jak Hammer slamming double sledges into the Preacher's back now! Are they cooperating? What is Jak Hammer's game? Lt. Lawson back in the ring now- and here comes Max Robertson- and he looks beside himself! They're pulling Hammer and Stern off of Wrench! Kore Wrench lunges at the Masked Ma again! The Masked Man with a punch- blocked! Kore Wrench fires a big right hand at the Masked Man and knocks him silly! He's going to the mask again! Here comes security, as Lawson and Max scuffle with Stern and Hammer! Security in the ring breaking things up- and the mask is halfway off! The mask is almost off! (pop) EL: He has it! He has it! The Preacher has unmasked the CWAA Heavyweight Champion! (pop) EL: It's... it's... it's Marc Thomas! Marc Thomas is the Masked Man- the new CWAA Heavyweight Champion- and he's off and running! I can't believe it! Folks, we've got about two minutes left- we'll take one last break, and when we get back, we'll have a quick word from Fuse. Don't go away! (Ads for Chia Pets, the Bedazzler, the Garden Weasel, and the Braidini) EL: OK- the ring has finally been cleared. If you've just joined us, the new CWAA Heavyweight Champion is the Masked Man- and the Preacher has revealed him as Marc Thomas- in three weeks' time, then, we'll see Marc Thomas take on Crimson Joe Reed- what a tremendous matchup that will be! Let's go to the ring, though, and hear from Fuse about what happened earlier tonight. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome my guest at this time- Fuse! (pop ad "Children" plays and the Masked Englishman returns) Fuse: Well, it would seem that I was wrong. Just as I thought I had evened the odds two on two you take the advantage again by bringing in a third- the Major. Well, that's it, enough is enough, you've just removed the cornerstone and now the whole house is gonna fall on your (expletive deleted). (pop) F: I didn't want to do this but you have left me no choice. Next week we end this- if you three have the courage to walk the aisle and meet us; next week I want you three in a three on three cage match. You can bring all the back-ups you want but they will not be able to interfere cause it will be you three against The Preacher, Myself and the one wrestler I know that could even beat me. Next week, this ends!!
