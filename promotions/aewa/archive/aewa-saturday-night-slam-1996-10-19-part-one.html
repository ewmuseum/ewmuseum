Saturday Night Slam (10/19, part one)

21 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power Bomb, and a look at Mister Crowley DRIVING the head of Volcano Jackson into the concrete with the Premature Burial! Then the camera switches to the Lovely Bertha, Matt Wilson, and "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt in a packed arena) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Saturday Night Slam! We have plenty of great matches scheduled for tonight, so this should be a VERY interesting show. BD: We're coming to you LIVE from the Oakland Coliseum, and these fans just WON'T shut up! This is such a trashy city-- I'd rather be back in Tokyo. LB: You're not the only one. So what's coming up first, Wilson? MW: Super-Blonde is defending his TV Title against Deranged-- it should be an outstanding matchup. BD: Are you serious? This is going to be boring as hell! MW: Oh, come on Big Daddy, you haven't even given it a chance yet. BD: Trust me, I don't need to. All I can hope for is a quick ending. MW: Well I don't think you're going to get it. These guys are going to fight till the last breath. As I said, it should be a great matchup, so let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA Television Title! Introducing first, the challenger, he weighs in at 260 pounds and is accompanied by his manager, Angel, He is a two-time former AEWA Television Champion, Deeeeeeeeeeeeerrrrrranged! (The Theme from "Halloween" plays loudly over the PA as Deranged makes his way toward the ring to a BIG crowd pop. Deranged seems to ignore the fans, however, and just heads straight for the ring. Angel is following closely behind him.) LB: Deranged looks EXTREMELY confident tonight, as well he should be, since he's only facing the Blonde. MW: What's that supposed to mean? LB: Just that the Blonde sucks, that's all. MW: But he's the Television Champion. LB: Not for long. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 202 pounds and accompanied by his manager, Kismet, he is the AEWA Television Champion, This.... Is.... Suuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuper-Blooooooooooooooonde! (Elastica's "Connection" resonates throughout the arena as Super-Blonde, with Kismet at his side, comes out to a HUGE crowd pop. His belt is strapped neatly around his waist and he slaps hands with several fans on his way to the ring.) BD: I hate this guy. LB: Me too. BD: Don't agree with me. LB: Why not? BD: Because you're dating Rick Showboat. LB: What has that got to do with anything? BD: Everything. LB: This is STUPID. I think you're just jealous. MW: I think you're right, but we've got a match to call here guys-- this isn't a show about relationships. BD: It's about whatever I want to talk about. MW: Um, no, it's not. The Blonde is in the ring now, and the announcer is leaving, but Deranged is stopping him. LB: Why? MW: He wants the microphone. The announcer just gave it to him, and it looks like Deranged has something to say. DERANGED: Before this match gets underway, I just want to say something to all my fans out there. I know there's a lot of you that have stuck with me through thick and thin, through the good time and the bad, through the title reigns and the title droughts. I see all the little kids out there with the Deranged T-shirts on, cheering me on, night after night, completely and totally dedicated to me. They look up to me, perhaps even worship me and I just wanted to tell you all how much you mean to me.... YOU DON'T MEAN JACK SHIT TO ME! I've wasted WAY too much time caring what you morons think. It's about time that I start doing what I WANT to do, and not what you think I should do, because it's not about you people-- it's about ME! It's MY career; I win the matches, I win the titles, and you people just sit there on you're asses and claim that you're helping me. Well you're NOT helping me, you've never won me ANYTHING! But I know some people who CAN help me, and I've joined them. So now, allow me to introduce my new brothers, the only people in this world I give a shit about, THE ANGELS OF DEATH! (The Grim Reaper, Twisted Steel, Ricky Roberts, and Red Dog make their way to the ring, to a HUGE round of boos.) MW: I can't BELIEVE what just happened! Deranged just turned his back on all the fans of the AEWA! BD: I know, isn't it GREAT? The membership in the Angels of Death is getting bigger every week! MW: That's not a good thing. LB: Sure it is. MW: No, it's not. The Angels are at ringside, and it appears as if all four of them are going to be in Deranged's corner for this match. This is totally unfair. BD: What makes you think they're going to get involved? MW: Just a hunch. The wrestlers are meeting in the center of the ring now, and Deranged just reaches out and SLAPS the Blonde. LB: Deranged is showing the champ NO respect whatsoever-- I LIKE IT! MW: You'd be confident too if you had four other guys backing you up. But that slap just ENRAGED the Blonde. He stuns him with a series of right hands, and now he grabs Deranged, SMALL PACKAGE! BUT DERANGED ROLLS IT OVER ON HIM! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Deranged almost got him there. MW: To bad you don't win anything for ALMOST getting him. Both men are quickly up to their feet now, and the Blonde just NAILS Deranged with a spin kick! Deranged takes a stunned step backwards, but the Blonde just FLOORS him with a standing dropkick! LB: Deranged is in trouble now.... NOT! MW: I'm afraid he IS, Bertha. The Blonde runs to the ropes now, and Deranged has just reached his feet! The Blonde comes back, and LEVELS Deranged with a diving shoulderblock! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! BD: The Blonde is taking it to him now, but I doubt this'll last long. MW: We'll see. The Blonde picks up Deranged now, and NAILS him with a jumping neck snap! Deranged is slowly trying to get up, but the Blonde grabs him, SMALL PACKAGE! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Just keep on trying to pin him, Blondie, you know you can't do it. MW: I think he CAN. The Blonde lifts Deranged to his feet now, and hooks both of his arms from behind, BACKSLIDE! BUT DERANGED IS FIGHTING IT! He's staying up, AND HE TAKES THE BLONDE OVER WITH THE BACKSLIDE! One..Two..Kickout! BD: The Blonde can't even do a backslide right. MW: That's no easy task when you're in there with Deranged. Both men quickly get to their feet, but Deranged still looks a little dazed. The Blonde charges in first, and ROCKS Deranged with an elbow. Deranged staggers back, and the Blonde goes for another backslide, and gets him over this time! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Why doesn't the Blonde just give up? MW: Because he has no REASON to give up. The Blonde picks Deranged up now, and whips him to the ropes! Deranged comes back, DIVING SHOULDERBLOCK FROM THE BLONDE--DUCKED BY DERANGED! Deranged now comes back off the opposite ropes, the Blonde just got back up to his feet, AND HE GETS LEVELED WITH A HIGH CROSS BODY FROM DERANGED! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Now Deranged has him! MW: We'll see. Deranged gets up now, and he's STOMPING away on the Blonde! Deranged lifts the Blonde's head up by his hair now, and he's just slapping him across the face, and now he SPITS in the Blonde's face! How disgusting! BD: I thought it was kinda cool. MW: I know, which usually means it's pretty darn despicable. Angel just jumped up on the apron! What does she want? LB: The referee's attention. MW: Well he's coming over to her, and Ricky Roberts is creeping into the ring! Angel is totally distracting the referee, BUT KISMET JUST YANKS HER OFF THE APRON! And we've got a catfight going on out there! BD: This should be cool, maybe they'll tear each other's clothes off. MW: If they do, we'll have to go to commercial or something. The referee turns back towards the action now, and Roberts DIVES for cover out of the ring! Well, I guess Kismet successfully killed that attempt to cheat by the Angels of Death. LB: That's okay, there's more of them. MW: Unfortunately, you're right. Now Red Dog jumps up on the apron, and the ref goes over to send HIM down! BD: And here comes Ricky again, and this time he's got a chair! MW: Yes, and Kismet is still brawling with Angel! Deranged is still working over the Blonde in a very demeaning manner, and now he lifts the Blonde up off the mat! LB: I think they're about to introduce the Blonde to that chair. MW: I KNOW that's what they want to do. Deranged is holding the arms of the Blonde now, and Roberts is winding up that chair, he swings, BUT THE BLONDE MOVES-- AND ROBERTS ACCIDENTALLY LEVELS DERANGED! BD: What? That wasn't supposed to happen. MW: Well, it did. Roberts just drops that chair, and he just looks SHOCKED at what he's done. He's trying to revive Deranged, but the Blonde is up, and he picks up the chair, AND NAILS ROBERTS WITH IT! LB: THAT CHEATER! MW: He tosses Roberts out of the ring, and now he goes over to the other side and punches Red Dog off the apron! He's got the ref now, and he's got an unconscious Deranged! He picks Deranged up off the mat, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One..Two..Three!!!! (DING, DING) BD: This is the biggest rip-off I've ever seen. RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of nine minutes and eighteen seconds, here is your winner, and STILL AEWA Television Champion... SUPER-BLONDE! MW: What an AMAZING victory! The Blonde really pulled that one out against ALL odds. And now he's grabbing Kismet and getting out of there before the Angels Of Death know what hit them! LB: But he cheated! MW: He did not! He used NO illegal tactics whatsoever against Deranged. There's nothing in the rules that says you can't hit a guy who's not in the match with a chair, and ROBERTS is the one who knocked out Deranged-- so if you wanna blame this on anyone, you've gotta blame it on the illegal tactics of the Angels Of Death. BD: I don't. I can blame it on whoever I want. MW: As we've seen you do on several occasions, no matter HOW illogical your reasoning is. BD: That's right. MW: The Angels are really upset now, having some arguments amongst themselves, but they're heading back to the dressing room, and we're about ready for our next match. LB: Which is? MW: Heavy Metal versus Wild Thing. BD: We've been waiting for this one ever since Mega Combat. Can Wild Thing POSSIBLY wrestle with a broken arm? And who knows what ELSE is injured. MW: I'd venture to say a LOT. But that's never stopped Wild Thing in the past when he could help it, and I don't believe he's gonna let it stop him now. LB: Then he's crazy. MW: Well that's a given, but this one still has the potential to be quite a matchup. So with that in mind, let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, weighing in at 234 pounds and accompanied by his manager, Bootknife Kelly, here is Wiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiild Thing! (Nine Inch Nails' "Hurt" plays loudly over the PA as Wild Thing makes his entrance to a big round of boos from the crowd. Bootknife Kelly follows closely behind him. Wild Thing is wearing a cast on his right arm and a brace around his neck.) BD: Just look at this guy! How can he be coming to the ring to wrestle like this? MW: The guy's NUTS, Big Daddy. But it IS interesting to see Bootknife Kelly back at the side of Wild Thing. LB: It sure is. Where has 'ol Bootknife been all this time, anyway? MW: Hell if I know. You two are supposedly his friends, you should know. BD: Well we don't. MW: Then I guess you're outta luck. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 450 pounds, Heavy Metaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaal! (Metallica's "Battery" begins to crank over the PA as the MASSIVE Heavy Metal makes his way toward the ring to a huge round of boos. He has a look of pure rage on his face.) LB: This one ain't gonna be pretty. MW: That's for sure. It's gonna be a knock down, drag out, brawl, and someone's gonna be seriously injured when this is over. BD: Someone, namely Wild Thing, is ALREADY seriously injured. MW: Good point. Heavy Metal steps into the ring, and Wild Thing charges in at him, BUT HEAVY METAL LIFTS A LEG! And Wild Thing charges RIGHT into a BIG boot! That FLOORED Wild Thing, and now Heavy Metal just SQUASHES him with a big splash! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Wild Thing's already in a LOT of trouble. MW: You can say that again. Heavy Metal picks Wild Thing up now, AND HE'S GOING FOR A POWERSLAM! BUT WILD THING ESCAPES, AND COUNTERS WITH A LARIAT! Heavy Metal is trying to get up now, but Wild Thing steps out onto the apron, and sends him back down with a slingshot! BD: Boy, how can he still do moves like that? MW: I don't think he's quite as injured as he wants us to think he is. I mean, there doesn't appear to be anything wrong with his neck. LB: Sure there is, there's a brace on it. MW: A brace that is apparently there for little or no reason. Anyway, back to the match. Wild Thing picks the big man up off the mat now, and takes him down with a reverse neckbreaker! Heavy Metal is slow to get up, and Wild Thing runs to the ropes! He comes back, and NAILS Heavy Metal with a flying forearm! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: He almost got him. MW: Yes, he did. And he's complaining about a slow count, but the ref won't hear any of it. He FINALLY turns his attention back to Heavy Metal, WHO CATCHES HIM WITH AN INSIDE CRADLE! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Wild Thing better start paying attention to his opponent and not the ref if he wants to win this one. MW: That's for sure. Heavy Metal gets up now, and Wild Thing starts to punch away at him with his left, and only free, hand! But those punches aren't having any effect! Heavy Metal just reaches out and scoops up Wild Thing, POWERSLAM!! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: And now the control has DEFINITELY shifted back in favor of Heavy Metal. MW: It sure has. Heavy Metal lifts Wild Thing to his feet now, AND WILD THING JUST CLOCKED HEAVY METAL UPSIDE THE HEAD WITH THAT CAST HE'S WEARING! And Heavy Metal drops like a sack of rocks! LB: Hey, if it works, go for it. MW: You wouldn't expect anything less from these two. Wild Thing has his chance now, and he's going up top! He dives, AND JUST NAILS THE PARTY TIME SPLASH! The cover! One..Two..Foot on the Ropes! BD: If they were two feet further towards the center, this would be over. MW: Yes, but they weren't and Heavy Metal always knows where he is. Wild Thing helps Heavy Metal to his feet now, and brings him to the center of the ring! Wild Thing runs to the ropes now! He comes back, FLYING FOREARM--DUCKED BY HEAVY METAL--BUT THE REF WAS RIGHT BEHIND HIM, AND WILD THING JUST NAILED THE REF WITH THAT FOREARM! LB: And that poor zebra is OUT! MW: And that just lit up Bootknife Kelly's eyes. He's coming into the ring! BD: Just as any fine manager would. MW: Whatever. Wait, he's stopping! LB: And getting a table! MW: You're right! He's bringing that table into the ring, and now he and Wild Thing are setting it up! They're putting Heavy Metal on top of it now, and Wild Thing's going back up top! BD: If he hits this, it's OVER! MW: It sure is. Kelly is holding Heavy Metal down on that table, and Wild Thing is on top! He dives, PARTY TIME SPLASH-- BUT HEAVY METAL MANAGES TO ROLL OFF THE TABLE, AND IN THE PROCESS PULLED KELLY ONTO IT! WILD THING SPLASHES HIS OWN MANAGER THROUGH THE TABLE! And BOTH of them are hurt! LB: Boy, did THAT one backfire! MW: It sure did. Heavy Metal is up now, and he picks up Bootknife Kelly, AND GORILLA PRESSES HIM! Wild Thing is slowly getting up, but Heavy Metal just tosses Kelly down on top of him! BD: Kelly better get out of there before he does any more damage to his own guy. MW: And now Heavy Metal grabs Wild Thing by that broken arm, AND RIPS THE CAST OFF! Heavy Metal grabs that unprotected broken arm now, AND SLAPS ON A BRUTAL ARMBAR SUBMISSION! LB: And look at the PAIN Wild Thing is in! His arm just can't take this! MW: The ref has finally come to his senses, and he's checking Wild Thing now! Wild Thing is just SCREAMING, he's gotta be in agony! The ref asks him, AND WILD THING SUBMITS! The ref's calling for the bell! (DING, DING) BD: He just couldn't take it, and frankly, I don't blame him. RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of three minutes and fourteen seconds, here is your winner... HEAVY METAL! MW: Just out of curiosity, how can the ref call a clean decision when there's a shattered table and an unconscious manager in the ring? LB: He didn't see anything happen. MW: No, but that's pretty convincing evidence, but I guess there's nothing that can be done about it now. BD: You got that right. MW: Wild Thing and Bootknife Kelly are being helped back to the dressing room now, and as we clear the ring of table fragments, we're just about ready for another match.
