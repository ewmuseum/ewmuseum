The Zack Derringer Show (3/12, part one)

15 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * T H E Z A C K D E R R I N G E R S H O W * * * * Live, from the ICU Studios in New York (A screen appears with a black background and red text that reads: "WARNING! The following program has language and material of an adult nature. If you are under 14 years of age, then please change the channel now. This is definitely not for your viewing. Thank you.") ("King Nothing" by Metallica begins to play slowly over a black screen, as the guitars and the bass kick in, and then the drums hit and the entire screen BURSTS with a white blast. "International" shows up on the screen in the back, really small, and then it gets bigger and flashes past, as the screen shows Sewerdog driving someone's head into the mat with a DDT. "Contracts" flashes past the screen, and then it shows Damian "The Predator" Steele drilling someone back with The Shining. "Unlimited" flashes past the screen, and it shows Heavy Metal SLAMMING someone down onto the mat with The Megadeth power bomb. Finally, it shows Deathwatch DRIVING someone's head into the mat with the Death's Vengeance. "The Zack Derringer Show" comes up onto the screen, and then drips away like blood, turning red as it goes. The last thing you can hear is Deathwatch's sadistic laughter, as the camera fades in on the ICU Studios in New York City. Zack Derringer and Hank Thompson are sitting behind the desk. Zack has his feet kicked up on the desk and is wearing black jeans and a white shirt. His long, black hair hangs to the sides of his head, and he flicks a rubber band at the camera. Hank is wearing a sharp looking blue suit with a black tie, and he smiles at the camera. Derringer begins to talk.) Zack Derringer (ZD): Hah! Check out the kick-ass new opening, losers! It's only QUALITY shows like this one that get something like that. How ya doin' today, Hank? Looks like you gain weight after every show. Hank Thompson (HT): Well, I.... uh.... I try to cut down on eating, but it's..... uh.... ZD: Spit it out! We haven't got all f*cking day! HT: Sorry, I just didn't know what to say at first. Anyway, I said I've been trying this great new diet plan called "Trim Quick" and it says you can eat all you want as long as you take a shake with each meal. It doesn't seem to be working, though. ZD: You moron, you can't eat ALL you want. You can eat ANYTHING you want. Doesn't mean to feed your fat ass all day long. Why doesn't someone just serve your dinner in a hog trough and call it a night? Now, before we get to the first flash of the day, I'd like to take this time to brag about how WELL my show did last week. Here are the official ratings: (Zack holds up a piece of paper and the camera zooms in on it.) AEWA Saturday Night Slam: 2.1 The Zack Derringer Show: 3.0 ZD: And just to let you know, the best Straight Talk has EVER done was 1.5, SO BEAT ME IF YOU CAN, MORONS! HT: We sure did good last week. Maybe we can do it again this week, too? ZD: _I_ did good last week. You just sat there on your fat ass babbling to the viewers. We probably woulda got point five more percent without your stupid ass. HT: Alright already, can we just get to the first flash? ZD: Of course we can, Honk. As always, we're starting this one out with a flash from a member of ICU, and that's World Title prospect, Heavy Metal. Roll it. --------------- (The camera opens on a huge crowd inside of the Phoenix Underground in Seattle, WA. Heavy Metal stands on the stage with a microphone in his hand. He's wearing a black leather vest, black leather pants, black boots, a black leather glove on his right hand, a pair of shades over his eyes, and a black "Queensryche" T-shirt underneath the vest. He yells out "ROCK ON!" to the crowd, and they ALL go nuts. He smacks a few hands and then begins to speak.) Heavy Metal: Hey all ya rock-a-holics out there! Most of you know me from the AEWA-- how many people here know what it is? (About half of the crowd roar out with excitement) Heavy Metal: Well, that's good. Now.... how many people here know who I am? CROWD: ROCK - ON! ROCK - ON! ROCK - ON! ROCK - ON! Heavy Metal: Hah! Great! Well, my good friend, Jeff Tate, offered to have me come out and support the pre-party before they release their new album called "Hear in the Now Frontier", and I bet each and every one of ya is exited for it's release, right? CROWD: YYYYYEEEEEEAAAAAHHHHH!!!! Heavy Metal: Right on! Now, basically what I'm gonna do here is talk about an upcoming match that _I_ have against "Crimson" Joe Reed.... (Half) CROWD: BBBBBBBBOOOOOOOOOOO!! (Other Half) CROWD: YYYYYYYEEEEEAAAAHHHH!! Heavy Metal: "The Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum.... (Two Thirds) CROWD: BBBBBBBOOOOOOOOOOO!! (One Quarter) CROWD: YYYYYEEEEEAAAHHHH!! Heavy Metal: And finally.... Mister Crowley! (All) CROWD: BBBBBBBBBOOOOOOOOOOOO!! Heavy Metal: My sentiments exactly. Now this match isn't for awhile, but still, I like to do these kinds of things.... it always helps out my chances. Now, Mister Crowley, you're the first person I want to talk about cause you're the WORST person TO talk about. I don't even understand what the hell Freeman was thinking when he said you could be in this match, what a waste of time and effort. Reed might now want to underestimate you, but I really could care less. Enough about you. (Heavy Metal walks over to the left side of the stage and sits on one of the speakers.) Heavy Metal: Now, Ben Stroke-um.... God I love that name. Benny boy, you're going to be the second man out of this match as soon as it begins, and I'm not just sayin' that, I'm sayin' it cause it's true! Crowley is the easiest task, so I figure he'll be gone first. Stroke-um, you're going to be more difficult, I saw and felt what you could do in the cage match, but now it's payback time, Benny boy. I want your back on the mat for a one, two, three, and there's not a damn thing you can do to stop me! (Heavy Metal walks over to the right side, and smacks hands with a few more people as he goes. The crowd is still cheering.) Heavy Metal: Now, Joe Reed, Crimson Boy-- whatever you want to call yourself, I don't care. The only thing I want from you is that title that's strapped around your waist. You know and I know that you don't have what it takes to keep that title, especially from someone like me. I'm seven feet, four inches of RAW power and if you think that you're gonna stop this runaway train, then you got another thing comin' to ya. Reed, I'm gonna set you up like bowling pins and knock you down for a strike with my fist. No, it's not going to be pretty, but it sure will get the job done. Reed.... I'm coming for the title that I lost a long time ago and shouldn't have. As far as I'm concerned, that title is still mine. You're holding a hot item and I'm coming to reclaim it, so Reed, don't stand in the way of the train unless you intend on committing suicide. (The crowd ROARS with excitement, and Heavy Metal raises his hand into the air with the standard rocker insignia. He then begins to speak once again.) Heavy Metal: Now, I'd like to take this time to introduce the guys you've ALL been waiting for.... here.... are.... QUEENSRYCHE!! (All the members of Queensryche come out, and Jeff Tate walks up to Metal, who leans down so Tate can put an elbow on his shoulder.) Jeff Tate: I'd like to thank Metal for comin' out here and doing this for us tonight. Thanks a lot man, means a lot to us! Heavy Metal: Anytime man, anytime. (Heavy Metal stands up and smacks hands with all the band members, and walks off the stage into the crowd. Queensryche starts to play "Signs of the Times" off their new album. Fade to black.) ------------ ZD: Man, that guy never ceases to amaze me. You kick ASS, Metal! Why doesn't he ever take ME to one of those concerts with him? HT: Probably because you'd annoy the hell out of him. ZD: Hey, you better shut up before I fire your ass, it's not like you have the CHOICE to be on this f*cking show. If I didn't hire you, the AEWA was going to lay you off, so just sit tight and watch the shit, Grandpa. HT: I'm not that old! I'm only 54! ZD: Yeah? Well I'm 25, so shut the hell up, gramps! This next flash is from Maxx Trouble, the guy who beat the living hell outta his own brother- you gotta love that. ---------- (The camera opens on Chuck Carlson, standing in a ring at a recent house show. He is holding a microphone, getting ready for the interview.) Carlson: Ladies and Gentlemen, please welcome my guests at this time, Maxx Trouble and his manager, Traci! ("Bad to the Bone" blast over the PA. The crowd sees Maxx and Traci and they erupt in boos. As Maxx and Traci walk down the aisle, a fan flashes a "Justin Trouble" T-shirt and when Maxx sees it, he grabs it, rips it up, and throws it back in the fan's face. Traci just smiles. Maxx gets to the ring and lowers the rope for Traci. Maxx walks up to Carlson and stares at him.) Carlson: Maxx, I have to say that in the week you've officially been here, you've made quite an impact on the wrestling world, namely by putting your own brother, Justin in an emergency room. Do you care to comment on your intentions here in the AEWA? Maxx: Pure and simple, it goes like this, Carlson.... Traci: Uh, Maxx, you're not obligated to tell this idiot anything. He's a nothing, a nobody. All Carlson and the rest of the AEWA need to know is that we were brought in by a higher power. A higher power that knows where the future of wrestling is going. A higher power that knows what he.... uh.... I mean IT wants.... Carlson: He? Who's he? Who's this higher power? Traci: What are you talking about, Chuck? I didn't say "he." I think you need a miracle ear, moron. Carlson: No, I distinctly heard you say.... Traci: Maxx.... (Maxx grabs Carlson by the jacket, lifts him, and sits him down on a turnbuckle. Maxx points his finger at Carlson in such an intimidating way, that even Carlson was at a sudden loss of words.) Maxx: You didn't hear anything, do you understand? (Carlson nods.) Maxx: All that matters is that we are here in the AEWA, and we are going to change the face of professional wrestling forever. Believe me. We will. And I'm going to start with anybody stupid enough to sign a contract against me. And after I've taken care of them, I'm going after the AEWA World champion. And whomever it may be, when I get a hold of him, I hope he's had a lot of fun during his reign, I sincerely hope it's all been worth it for him; because when I get a hold of him, the fun and games are over. The AEWA World title is going to be around my waist. And when it is, and the cash is overflowing from my pockets, I might give that lousy bum, Justin, a couple of bucks. I mean, he IS my brother, and I wouldn't feel right about him collecting welfare now that he's unemployed. Ya know, it's just not fair to working people. They shouldn't have to pay for him screwing up and losing his job here. I promise you this, Justin, when I'm champ, I'll buy you the finest slum money can buy. (The crowd boos loudly.) Traci: (laughs) You're such a sensitive guy, Maxx. And nobody tops your family values. I bet all these women out here are wanting to be me at this very moment, just to be next to you. And not to mention the fact that I'm prettier than any of these fat, hick, hee-haw rejects that these stupid people call women. (Crowd boos loudly) But, I digress. Bottom line, Maxx is the future pro wrestling, and whether you like it or not, he's the next AEWA champion. C'mon, Maxx we have to go sign some autographs for our fans. Let's go.... (Maxx lowers the rope for Traci, and they leave as the camera fades on a confused Chuck Carlson. Fade to black.) -------------- ZD: Yet another dumbass that thinks he'll be able to beat Heavy Metal-- what a shame.... and I was beginning to like this loser. HT: What I want to know about is what's this "higher power?" ZD: They're going with the Church.... everyone who's afraid of ICU is going to the Church nowadays. It's pathetic. HT: If you say so. Who's our next flash from? ZD: Looks like.... um.... it looks like.... Hah! Primal Fear, that fat piece of crap, has prepared some footage for us. Roll it. ------------------ (The camera opens on Primal Fear, standing in front of the Mall Of America. He is wearing a black leather jacket, and appears to be in a very bad mood.) Fear: Stan Crawford, here is the place that I will tear your ass up! No more Mr. Nice Guy! I'm sick and tired of being the fans' man! It has gotten me nowhere. I mean, ever since I got rid of Miss DeMeanor, I have been stuck in feuds with no namers and losing to the big boys. That's that! I will have my new manager with me, and he is somewhere in this mall. Lets go find him! (Primal Fear walks into the front entrance, and soon as he walks in, the people stare at him.) Fear: What the hell are you all looking at? Haven't you seen a 360 pound wrestler before? (Primal Fear starts walking off through the mall.) Fear: Let me think here. Where would he be? There are so many stores.... wait a minute.... Hooters!! He loves women! Let's get going! (He walk through the mall and come up to Hooters. Inside, we see an older man flirting with a waitress.) Fear: There he is! Always trying to get with the ladies. Hey man, what's up? Messin' with the women again? Man: Primal Fear, my old friend! Finally, you smartened up and returned my phone calls! I am going to make you as extreme and hardcore as you were when you first entered the AEWA. I am going to make you so god damn mean, that NO ONE will want to get in the ring with you. Stan Crawford will regret signing a "falls count anywhere" match against you. Fear: You were right! I mean, yes, the fans are there, but where are they when you're getting split open right on the forehead? I need someone pushing me to my limits, and making me a bad son of a bitch again! Man: That's what I am here for! I am the great Xavier, the best manager in the world. I am going to make you the best god damn, extreme wrestler on this planet! Stan Crawford, we might just drown you in one of those water fountains! Fear: Xavier is the man who will make me become something I have never been in this federation-- a champion. I have yet to become a champion, and I hope Xavier can help me with that. So Stan Crawford, I wouldn't show up if I were you, cause you just may not walk out alive! Man: (grins) I'm not that bad.... or am I? (The scene fades to black as Primal Fear and Xavier shake hands and begin to laugh.) ------------- ZD: Give him a new manager, dress him up like a girl, try to make him extreme, he turns out the same stupid Primal Fear. HT: Maybe a new manager will be good for him, who knows. ZD: I know, because I said I do! Primal Fear is a PRIMATE and won't even last thirty seconds in the ring with Stan Crawford, folks. He's also got that "first blood" match coming up at AEWA Extreme, and that's going to be a big JOKE. HT: Primal Fear has been on quite a losing streak as of late, I'll give you that. ZD: Let's just roll this next flash from Sir Gallade; it should be interesting. ---------------- (The camera opens on Chuck Carlson, standing in the middle of the ring, with a microphone in his hand. He is ready to begin the interview.) HT: Ladies and gentlemen, here is the man who turned on the Knights of Deliverance, and you fans as well, he is the AEWA Television Champion, Sir Gallade! (Sir Gallade steps out from the curtains to a round of boos, with the Singapore cane in hand. He looks around the crowd, then slowly makes his way toward the ring.) Carlson: Sir Gallade, the one question on everybody's mind is why, why did you do what you did at Kickoff '97? Gallade: First of all, Chuckie, don't think that I didn't hear the way you announced my entrance, I heard you, and next time you disrespect me like that, I'll shove this cane up your ASS! Now, you want to know the reason why I joined the Church, huh? Well, Cardinal Chaos is the best guidance that any man could want. He will lead me, as well as the rest of the Church, to the greatest heights. The Knights were and always will be all talk and all losers! They will all get what's coming to them. Carlson: Now, you're going to be facing Randall Osbourne in a steel cage match at AEWA Extreme. Any thoughts on the match? Gallade: Just last week, Shane Matthews and I defeated Randall in a handicap match, remember? I ALREADY beat him, so why should I have to do it again? Can you answer that question, Chuck? No, of course you can't. The AEWA is starting to really anger me. I have kicked Randall's bloody ass SO many times before, it's starting to get tiresome. Chuck, maybe I should make an example out of YOU.... (Just as Chuck Carlson starts to speak, Sir Gallade hits him in the butt with the Singapore cane, and throws him out of the ring. Carlson doesn't appear to be very hurt, but he runs away in complete terror. Gallade picks up the fallen microphone.) Gallade: I wasn't finished with you, peon! I told you not to disrespect me again! Osbourne, are you ready to get EXTREME? You're going to feel what it's like to get extreme when my cane goes up your backside! (He begins to laugh hysterically, and walks back to the locker room. Fade to black.) --------- ZD: Yep, another Church turnout. This isn't lookin' good for ICU.... maybe we need to (chuckles) RECRUIT some new talent! HT: Oh god, no. Who do you guys have in mind? ZD: You'll find out soon enough. We have our members lined up and ready to join, but we aren't doing it all at once, no, we'll do it slowly. In the meantime, here's a flash from The Hustlers. --------------- (The camera opens on the Hustlers, "Wild Card" Jeff Hawkins and 8-Ball, standing together in their dressing room.) Jeff Hawkins: First of all, you have to be saying to yourselves "How can somebody so BAD be so GOOD?" Well, it would seem my prediction is coming true, as I am quickly rising up the ranks of the AEWA, and should have a Light Heavyweight title shot, or possibly, another title. Hell, who's to say I can't beat some of these big freaks-of-nature holding belts? They sure can't outwrestle me, and I'm darn positive they can't outfight me. So why don't you ante one of those belts onto the high rollers' table, and see if you can beat a Royal Flush, because NOTHING beats a Royal Flush. 8 Ball: Um, Jeff.... Hawkins: Oh yes, that brings me up to my main problem. As I dominate the singles ranks, it seems my tag team, the Hustlers, have not been allowed to take a bit of this pie. I forget that my main man 8 Ball here is so sensitive. 8 Ball: Must be all those Barbara Streisand flicks. Hawkins: Anyway, what you have is a bunch of poseurs who don't know what they are and play themselves as something they can't be. Assassins? Who have they killed? 8 Ball: Nobody. Hawkins: You see, the Hustlers are just that-- trash talking, slick moving, underhanded smartasses who will kick your butt and talk you into liking it, and in the process, take everything you own and then some. We are the masters of dirty pool, you are playing against a stacked deck, and if you want to roll the loaded dice and take your chances, there's always room for another sap at this table. 8 Ball: Check and mate. Hawkins: So we're demanding that one of these two teams that hold belts give them up, or else we're going to have to come collect, and we all know that you boys want to save yourselves some pain. To all the members of ICU, you better stay in line. Deathwatch, if you need a Praetorian Guard, someone in your inner circle to help out, you got my number, just give me a ring. AS for our old friends the Horsemen.... hell, we've kicked your butts all around two other federations, why not a third? Come on boys, sign the contract, they're always open, and you too will be saying "How can somebody so BAD be so GOOD?" 8 Ball: That is, in between you begging for mercy. (8 Ball picks up a remote control and switches off the camera. Fade to black.) -------------- ZD: I must admit that The Hustlers are looking good, and it's also a plus that they still think highly of ICU. Maybe future prospects, who knows. HT: Yeah, well, let's hope not. I don't want to see ICU get any bigger than it already is. ZD: Well, I ain't no damn genie, so your wish ISN'T my command, Hank. It'll be fun watching ICU grow and grow and grow until it can't grow anymore. You'll be surprised with some of the people we have in mind for recruiting. HT: Anyway, here's the next flash from an ICU member, Sewerdog. ----------------- (We see Sewerdog sitting on a black leather couch, in a room decorated completely in black, with the exception of bright blood red letters that spell out ICU. Instead of the usual ring attire, he's wearing a suit with his mask. Casually he sips at a glass of champagne while some blonde bimbo massages his shoulders. He hands her the glass and pats her on the behind as she walks away.) Sewerdog: All I have to say is that I LOOOOOVVVE being in the ICU. The only reason the lovely Bertha says it's stupid is because she wasn't allowed to be the one to bring me my champagne. Kisses, honey. (Blows one at the camera.) But enough about that. I'm here to talk business. With the intense training that the ICU is putting me through, I am looking very forward to my next confrontation. Unfortunately, the Extremist's little speech about Power Rangers and those silly little troopers had me confused, so I felt bad when I was beating on him, because it was like beating up a seven year old. I wanted him to feel good that he won, because I didn't think they allowed children in the AEWA. (He picks up a remote control and begins watching a match with "Crimson" Joe Reed. Then he flips the channel to some infomercial.) Sewerdog: Well, this is certainly more exciting than watching "Cotton Eye" Joe Reed's match. I can't wait to see how you do against my new pal Heavy Metal. I think someone's going to get ROCKED. Actually, three of them, if the rumors I hear about the four way match are true. Anyway, I just wanted to ask Reed if the "crimson" nickname is for the color of your blood that's going to spill. And notice, I didn't say WHEN it would be spilled. I do like to hide in dark places, therefore, that explains the decor. (Sewerdog waves his hand to show off the new living conditions.) Sewerdog: And I know what everyone else is thinking, "Thank God he's going after the heavyweight belt." (He grins an evil smirk and waves his finger back and forth.) Sewerdog: Not so. I just love gold, and I don't have any jewelry. So now, anyone with a belt is on my hit list. Metal deserves the Heavyweight, I just haven't decided which belt would look best around my waist. And anyone that thinks they can stop me, all you punks just get ready for straight up SEWASIDE!! (The blonde brings him another glass of champagne, and the scene fades to black.) ------------ ZD: Great man, that Sewerdog. He's finally realizing how great it actually is to be in ICU, and one of the prime benefits.... you don't have to rely on an AEWA paycheck. Deathwatch funds all ICU members, which is one of the reasons it's so limited to members. If Deathwatch wants you in, usually he'll get you. HT: Really? So ICU is actually supplied by DEATHWATCH!? He must be rich! ZD: You've been to his building, hell, he owns all of that. He has more money than he knows what to do with, but he doesn't want to waste it all on wrestlers, so that's why there's a limit. We have five already, and he can only support five more.... max. HT: I never knew it was that detailed. Well, who's our next flash from? ZD: YES! Deathwatch. (Zack grins to the camera and Hank shakes his head.)
