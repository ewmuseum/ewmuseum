Wednesday Night Rumble (4/2, part one)

5 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * A E W A W E D N E S D A Y N I G H T R U M B L E * * * * Live, from the Manhattan Center in New York (We see the AEWA logo on the screen, set on fire. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Rock music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... David Graves TAKING SOME GUY'S HEAD OFF with the Gravestone, Penance PLANTING some guy with the Final Judgment, The West Coast Connection DRIVING some guy's head into the mat with the West Coast Killa, Cardinal Chaos STICKING some poor soul with the Inverted Crucifix, Shane Matthews BRUTALLY hitting the End of the Road on some hapless jobber, and Heavy Metal notching yet ANOTHER casualty courtesy of the Megadeth Power Bomb. The camera then shifts to Matt Wilson, The Lovely Bertha, and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt in the PACKED Manhattan Center, the site of AEWA Wednesday Night Rumble!) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Wednesday Night Rumble! We have plenty of great matches scheduled for tonight, and in the main event, "Crimson" Joe Reed will be squaring off against Joey Valachi! That should be an excellent bout, folks. BD: Yeah, but I'm more excited about our first match, as Jimmy "Vegas" Cage is about to square off against Justin Trouble! This should be a classic. LB: Well, Cage looked impressive in his debut against Mister Crowley last week, so this is going to be EASY for him. MW: You might have a point there, Bertha. Let's get down to ringside for the introductions.... RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from Las Vegas, Nevada, weighing in at 275 pounds, led to the ring by his valet, Sugar, here is Jimmy "Vegas" Caaaaaaaaaaage! ("Kinder Words" by the Mighty Mighty Bosstones begins playing over the PA system, and Cage makes his way toward the ring to a mixed reaction from the crowd. He is carrying a deck of cards, and randomly tosses the cards to members of the audience.) MW: We don't know too much about this Cage guy, largely because he hasn't said a whole lot since his joining the AEWA. LB: Give him time, Wilson. He's probably still getting used to his new home. BD: More like he's just drunk off his ass. RING ANNOUNCER: And finally, from Atlanta, Georgia, he weighs in at 238 pounds, he is a former North American champion, please welcome Juuuuuuuustiiiiiinnn Trrrrrrroooooooooooooublllllle! (Tom Petty's "Makin' Some Noise" can be heard over playing loudly, and Justin Trouble makes his entrance to a HUGE pop from the crowd. He appears to be in a great mood, and slaps hands with the fans in the front row.) MW: Justin Trouble looks pumped and ready for this match. BD: Two words-- who cares? MW: Well, these fans obviously care, Big Daddy. BD: All right, let me rephrase that. Who cares that I care about? MW: (sighs) Let's just get to the match. BD: If we must.... Cage and Trouble are locked up in the middle of the ring, Cage whips Trouble into the ropes-- clothesline by Cage, but Trouble ducks, and nails him with a clothesline of his own! LB: Oh come on, Cage, don't let this loser win! Trouble picks up Cage, and nails him with a short lariat! Cage is down.... Trouble grabs him, whips him into the ropes, and nails him with a powerslam, but he picks Cage right back up! MW: What's he doing? Trouble picks Cage up-- whoa, Cage just nailed him with a blatant low blow, and the referee is warning him, but Cage appears not to really care. BD: HA HA HA! Go to work, Jimmy! Now he goes and grabs Trouble, whips him into the ropes -- dropkick by Cage! Here's the cover! One.. Two.. Kickout! LB: Come on, ref, that was three! MW: It most certainly was not, Bertha. Now Cage hits Trouble with a left, a right, another left, and now Cage headbutts him! Cage grabs Trouble, and puts him up on the top turnbuckle. BD: I can sense the end coming for Justin Trouble! Cage climbs up with him-- superplex! Cage is going for the cover! One.. Two.. Thr.. Kickout! LB: Can this referee count to three? BD: It doesn't seem like it, 'cause there's no way Trouble should've been able to kick out of that! MW: Maybe he's just tougher than you think he is, Mike. BD: I doubt it. Cage is looking pretty angry in there now-- he's right in the referee's face! MW: Man, Cage is REALLY bitching out the referee, but he doesn't see Trouble getting up behind him! LB: (shouting) JIMMY! TURN AROUND, JIMMY! MW: Cage still isn't looking! Trouble grabs him by the hair-- INVERTED DDT! Cage is out, and Justin goes for the cover! One.. Two.. Three! (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner, at a time of six minutes and thirty-two seconds, Justin Trouble! LB: No way! Trouble cheated! MW: It was Cage's fault for badgering the referee, Bertha. Anyway, Justin Trouble picks up a big momentum-building win in only six minutes, and he'll have some force going into AEWA Extreme. BD: Big deal. Maxx Trouble is going to break Justin in half, and I'm going to enjoy it. LB: Well, I'd love to keep arguing with Wilson about that last match, bu isn't it time to move on? MW: Well, our next match is going to be-- (Without any warning, the arena lights begin flashing on and off. A muscular masked man, wearing a long black cape, walks out from behind the curtain and begins making his way toward the ring. His face has been painted black, successfully keeping his identity a secret.) BD: Who the hell IS this guy? He interrupted our last program and attacked a referee, but now it looks like he's back again. MW: Well, Big Daddy, I have absolutely NO idea what this is all about. He looks slightly familiar, but I still haven't gotten a close enough look at him to tell. LB: Let's just see what happens.... (The man pulls a cordless microphone out from under his cape, and enters the ring. He begins to speak.) Man: Yes, it's me again, I know you're all glad to see me. (crowd boos) Ah, shut your mouths! Listen, you worthless fans, just quiet down and let me say my piece. Anyway, I am out here once again to talk about a couple things. First of all, Wild Thing, congratulations for finally seeing the light. Now you know what the AEWA is all about, and you probably have some idea of what I feel like. The fans are worthless, no one needs 'em! All they do is sit there and distract you and throw things at you, showing no respect. They can shove it. Second of all, the AEWA president, who's name is not worth being mentioned by me, he'll only let you move up in this organization if you kiss his skinny ass! I knew that a long time ago and that's why I left. Now I'm back, and I'll have my way no matter what anyone says, and I won't be kissing no one's ass! (The crowd begins to throw garbage into the ring.) Man: See what I mean? That's exactly what I'm talking about. The fans think they can do whatever they want because the wrestlers can't do anything to 'em. Well you know what? Ask me if I care, because I don't! (The man steps through the ropes and walks over to some fans in the front row. One of them, a teenager wearing an ECW T-shirt, had tried to throw a soda can at him. The man grabs this fan by the collar of his shirt, and violently pushes him down into the guardrail. Several security guards run over and restrain the man, but he breaks loose and climbs back into the ring.) Man: You see that? You see how much I care? Come on, AEWA, try and do something about that! And also, on a more.... surprising note.... I do owe one man for all he did for me during my last stint in the AEWA. Super Blonde, you've done a lot for me in the past, and I feel I owe you a lot in return. I would like to be your tag team partner to take on the AEWA tag champs. I scared the AEWA championship committee into giving me a title shot of some kind, and they told me they have a slot for the AEWA World Tag Titles-- that's all they can offer me right now. So, Blonde, if you're up to it and would like to take the tag titles with me, then let's do it! Me and you.... we shall rule the AEWA! (The crowd continues to boo loudly, and the ring is being filled with garbage. Another fan in the front row throws a hot dog at him, and the man charges out of the ring to go after him. AEWA officials and security guards quickly block him from getting at the fan, and they immediately escort him back to the locker room.) MW: That guy is totally out of his mind, folks. He's going to be heavily fined for what just happened, that's for sure, and I hope he gets banned from all AEWA shows. We don't NEED this kind of crap around here. BD: And I agree with you, Wilson, he does look really familiar to me. I'm pretty sure I've seen that guy before. LB: Yeah, but what's this connection with Super Blonde? He claims to be an old friend of the Blonde, so that should help us identify him. Wilson, you should review some old videotapes and see if you can figure out who he is. MW: Bertha, why do _I_ always have to do the homework around here? Would it be too much to ask for YOU to do it? LB: (chuckles) It's not like you actually have a social life, right? MW: Sadly enough, that's a good point. Anyway, it's now time for our second match of the evening, as Deathwatch is about to defend his North American championship against Mister Crowley. BD: These two guys are both legends around here, and they've both been around here for over a year, so ANYTHING could happen in this one. LB: Well, let's get this sucker started! RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is for the North American Title! Introducing first, the challenger, from the Church of the Pounding Light, the former AEWA World Champion, Misssstttteerrr Crooowwwlleeyyyy!! (Crowley's music starts playing in the background, but no one appears.) MW: Where's Crowley? LB: He must be plotting something. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, the president of the ICU, here is the North American Champion.... this is Deatttthhhhwwwwaaaatttcchhh! (Deathwatch walks down the aisle, wearing the North American Title around his waist. Kismet is standing by his side. Most of the fans boo him, but a few give him a respectful applause, and a few more applaud Kismet. Deathwatch steps through the ropes into the ring.) MW: Well, this is interesting. The champion's here, but Mister Crowley is nowhere to be found. BD: Maybe this is some kind of mind game by Crowley. He's a master at that kind of thing, ya know. LB: Yeah, but Deathwatch is no slouch. Maybe he and the rest of the ICU attacked Crowley beforehand. MW: I just spoke to some of our people in the back. Mister Crowley is not in the back anywhere.... BD: There he is. I spotted him! LB: Where? BD: Up in the bleachers.... over there. (points to the back of the stands) MW: That's not him, Big Daddy. That's just a fan in a Crowley T-shirt. BD: Oh. (mutters) Well, it looked like Crowley to me.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen, if Mister Crowley does not appear in the next thirty seconds, the match will be forfeited, and Deathwatch will be declared the winner. MW: I don't understand this. A title shot, and Crowley's disappeared to who knows where. (A clock counts down the thirty seconds, and a bunch of fans join in the countdown.) Fans: TWENTY-SIX.... TWENTY-FIVE.... TWENTY-FOUR.... LB: Well, I'm impressed. These people can even count backwards, with a little help. MW: Deathwatch and Kismet look as confused as anyone, but that could be an act. BD: Deathwatch beat four men last week, remember? Why should he be worried about just one man, even if it _is_ Mister Crowley? Fans: FOURTEEN.... THIRTEEN.... TWELVE.... LB: Still no sign of Crowley. Does anyone know if he showed up earlier? Maybe he couldn't make it to the arena. MW: Maybe, but no one has heard any word from him. Fans: THREE.... TWO.... ONE.... ZERO!! MW: The time's up, and Crowley didn't appear. RING ANNOUNCER: Due to Mister Crowley's no-show, the winner of this match, and STILL North American Champion.... Deeeaaatttthhhwwwaatttcchhh! (Deathwatch, happy for having an easy night, walks down the aisle with the title, Kismet, and a grin on his face.) MW: I know the fans are disappointed, because Crowley and Deathwatch would be a great match. Hopefully they will be able to meet again in the near future. BD: Yeah, I was really hoping Crowley would get his hand around the guy's neck, and squeeze.... MW: I didn't know you despised Deathwatch so much, Big Daddy. BD: Deathwatch? I was talking about Zack Derringer. I'd pay money to see Crowley get him. LB: (laughing) Me too.
