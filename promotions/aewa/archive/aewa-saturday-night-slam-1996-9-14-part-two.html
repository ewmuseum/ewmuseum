Saturday Night Slam (9/14, part two)

17 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
****CONTINUED FROM PART ONE**** MW: Now is the one we've been waiting for-- Titles versus titles. The World Tag Team Champions, Skull & Crossbones, hook up with the National Champions, the Shadow Warriors. And after the events of the last Kevin Larkin Show, I think the world champs want their challengers more than the challengers want the champs. BD: But both teams are challengers and both teams are champs in this one! (Wesley Cantrell, CEO of the Cantrell Corporation, and his valet Lianne lead "Superfly" Aaron Ladd and Reuben "Thunder" Slade. The crowd boos them loudly.) MW: I hate to say this, but I think Cantrell wields the real power in the tag team division. Cantrell appears to have Larkin in his pocket, and it looks like whatever Wesley wants, Wesley gets. He wanted a National title shot, he got it. He wanted a guaranteed successful defense, Larkin named him referee and let him name his opponents. He wanted the National title challengers "taken care of", and the Icemen neutralized the Judicial Powers and the Horde "cleaned up" Skull & Crossbones. I hate to say this, but it looks as if money talks around here. BD: Well, of course it does. LB: Duh. ("Wherever I May Roam" begins playing, and Skull & Crossbones and Master Wu-Tang head for the ring. ) AEWA World Tag Team Champions Skull & Crossbones versus AEWA National Tag Team Champions The Shadow Warriors (Aaron Ladd and Reuben Slade) As Skull & Crossbones slid under the bottom ropes, the Shadow Warriors met them with boots and fists. From the very beginning of this match, the referee had absolutely no control. The two teams appeared to think they were wrestling under "street fight" rules, and the referee tried on several occasions to convince the four men to calm down. The 396-pound Crossbones grabbed the ref by his shirt collar and hurled him out of the way (into the corner), and resumed his attack on Slade. As the four men traded blows in mid-ring, with no one achieving any real advantage, the referee called for the bell. The match was over quickly, as the Shadow Warriors won by disqualification at 1:13, but the brawl continued until security was able to separate everyone. MW: What the HELL just happened? That match sure didn't last long, folks. LB: Those teams went absolutely crazy in there, because they just weren't mentally prepared for a match. BD: The Shadows Warriors are NOT done with Skull & Crossbones, and they'll meet again someday. LB: Until then, what's next? MW: Well, Hank Thompson is about to interview Primal Fear. BD: I wonder what that moron who threw away his career has to say. MW: He didn't throw away his career, I think he's better off. And as far as what he's got to say, let's go find out. (Primal Fear walks up to the interview stage, wearing a nice tuxedo. Hank Thompson is already there, ready to interview him.) Thompson: Hello sir, thank you for agreeing to this interview. Over the past few weeks, you have made quite an impression on the fans. They really seem to love you. Now that Miss DeMeanor is out of your life, you seem like a whole new man! How does it feel? Fear: The fans are very important to me, buddy. My life has completely turned around, and I'm very happy. Thompson: Now, Miss DeMeanor recently introduced a new wrestler-- the Twilight Strangler. This guy wants to tear you apart, and he DOES look rather intimidating. What do you think about him? Fear: The Twilight Strangler doesn't scare me. Miss DeMeanor is getting desperate, so she hired that lunatic to stop me. Well, I have news for everybody-- with the fans on my side, NO ONE CAN STOP ME! Thompson: Miss DeMeanor has also threatened to sue you, sir. She claims that your contract is still with her. Is there any truth to those allegations? Fear: Well, yes. She still has legal control of me, but that doesn't mean ANYTHING. Right now, I'm on my own. I'll NEVER go back to Miss DeMeanor. Thompson: The Twilight Strangler recently threatened to KILL you. How do you respond to him? Fear: Like I already said, he doesn't scare me. I'm not even going to dignify those comments with a response. If the Strangler is so damn tough, why doesn't he ever meet me in the ring? (The Twilight Strangler emerges from behind the interview platform holding a baseball bat. Hank Thompson sees him, and bails out. Primal Fear doesn't see him, but turns around to see what Hank is running from, and the Twilight Strangler BASHES Primal Fear in the kneecap with the bat, knocking him flat on his back.) MW: OH MY GOD! The Twilight Strangler just came out of NOWHERE and nailed Primal Fear in the leg with a baseball bat! He took his legs right out from under him! LB: This is cool! MW: It is NOT! And now the Strangler climbs up on the platform where he just laid out the big man, AND BEGINS TO POUND AWAY ON HIM WITH THAT BASEBALL BAT! HE'S BEATING HIM TO A BLOODY PULP!!! BD: That's what he gets for disobeying Miss DeMeanor. That oughta teach him a lesson! MW: And now the Strangler is lifting up the head of Primal Fear, and he's pulling that bat across his throat, AND PULLING BACK ON IT! HE'S CHOKING THE LIFE OUT OF HIM!!! BD: Having trouble breathing, Primal Fear? MW: This is no time to be making jokes, Big Daddy. Primal Fear is in serious trouble here! The Strangler FINALLY removes that bat from the throat of Primal Fear, and tosses it aside, BUT HE GOES RIGHT BACK TO CHOKING PRIMAL FEAR! THIS TIME WITH HIS BARE HANDS!!! LB: It looks like he's got a lot of experience doing this too, look at the way he digs his fingers into the throat. This guy's a real pro. MW: A pro WHAT-- killer? This man is a madman! He should be locked up somewhere! WHERE THE HELL IS SECURITY? Primal Fear could be dying out there! BD: Here come the boys in blue! MW: You're right, that's not even security, those are police officers! They're pulling that madman off of Primal Fear, but he doesn't want to go! LB: I don't think he has a choice. MW: No, he doesn't. They're forcefully removing his hands from the neck of Primal Fear, and handcuffing them behind his back! He's going to do some well deserved time in jail for that assault! BD: What did he do to deserve being arrested? MW: Are you serious? How about attempted murder? BD: Awww, he was just having fun. I thought it was great! MW: You're a VERY sick man. How do you sleep at night? BD: Oh, on my side, usually. MW: That's not what I meant. And now Primal Fear is being attended to by paramedics, and we can only hope that no serious damage was done. LB: Speak for yourself. MW: You're sick too. LB: I know, and I'm proud of it, so what's next? MW: Deranged is getting his rematch with Heavy Metal, with the stipulations that he wanted-- no disqualification, two-out-of-three falls for the AEWA World Heavyweight title! BD: This is gonna be funny! Deranged lost in under two minutes last time; this time, he's gonna lose BOTH FALLS in under one minute! MW: I highly doubt that, Big Daddy. Deranged was caught by surprise last time, and remember that he DID somehow manage to power bomb Heavy Metal in that matchup, and almost pulled off a quick upset. LB: That doesn't matter, Wilson! Heavy Metal's gonna mop the mat with him tonight! MW: There's only one way to find out, let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is a two-out-of-three falls matchup for the AEWA Heavyweight Championship of the World! The first man to score two pinfalls will win the match, and the title. Introducing first, the challenger, he weighs in at 260 pounds and is accompanied by his manager, Angel, he is a two-time former AEWA Television Champion, Deeeeeeeeeeerrrrrrranged! (The theme from "Halloween" plays loudly over the Public Address system, as Deranged makes his way to the ring to a large crowd pop. Angel is at his side.) BD: Look at how confident the little moron looks! In a couple of minutes, Heavy Metal is gonna wipe that look right off his face. LB: Oh, come on Big Daddy, Metal will do better than that-- he'll wipe Deranged's face right off his head. BD: Good point. I stand corrected. MW: You two are VERY strange. You really deserve each other. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 450 pounds, he is the AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, Heavy Metaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaal! (Metallica's "Battery" plays loudly over the PA as the MASSIVE champion makes his way to the ring to a heavy downpour of boos from the crowd.) LB: Just look at the statistics. Heavy Metal's NINE inches taller and almost 200 pounds heavier than Deranged. He's gonna kill him! Again! MW: We'll see in just a moment. Heavy Metal steps into the ring, AND IMMEDIATELY GETS ATTACKED BY DERANGED!! He's pounding away on the big man! Deranged NAILS him with a kick to the midsection, AND HE'S SETTING HIM UP FOR THE POWER BOMB!!! BD: No way! Not this time, buddy! MW: He lift's the champ up, AND HITS IT! I can't BELIEVE he did it again! He's going for the cover! One..Two..Three!!!! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of twenty-two seconds, the winner of the first fall is DERANGED!!!! LB: TWENTY-TWO SECONDS? How the HELL did he do that? MW: I don't know, but I guess it just goes to show you that on a given night, ANYONE can suffer a VERY short upset loss! BD: (yelling) But HEAVY METAL, to DERANGED, in TWENTY-TWO SECONDS? MW: Stranger things have happened. LB: I find that hard to believe. MW: Well they have, and the match isn't over yet either, but Deranged has a distinct advantage, because he only has to beat Heavy Metal one more time. Heavy Metal's still gotta beat him TWICE. BD: You're right, but there's NO WAY Heavy Metal is losing twice in the same night to THIS moron! MW: We'll see, as Heavy Metal is back to his feet, and the bell to begin the second fall has just rung! AND HEAVY METAL CHARGES IN AT DERANGED AND BEGINS TO POUND AWAY ON HIM!! LB: Okay, now you've pissed him off. MW: Heavy Metal hooks the arms of Deranged now, BACKSLIDE--BUT DERANGED TAKES HEAVY METAL OVER WITH IT! One..Two.. BD: But Heavy Metal manages to pull himself back up and he takes Deranged over with that backslide now!!! One..Two..Kickout!! LB: Come on Heavy Metal, take the punk out! MW: He's trying! Both men up, and Heavy Metal LEVELS Deranged with a clothesline! Deranged is down, and Heavy Metal DROPS a big elbow! BD: There we go. Deranged is finished now for sure! MW: Don't be so sure! Heavy Metal picks Deranged up off the canvas, BELLY-TO-BACK SUPLEX!!! And he's not done! He whips Deranged to the ropes, Deranged comes back, AND GETS CAUGHT WITH A CLOTHESLINE FROM THE BIG MAN!! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Deranged is wishing he never asked for this match right now. MW: That's a distinct possibility, Heavy Metal is REALLY taking it to him now! Heavy Metal picks Deranged up now, AND GORILLA PRESSES HIM! AND TOSSES HIM OUT OF THE RING TO THE CONCRETE! BD: That had to hurt! And Heavy Metal's going after him! This should be good! MW: Heavy Metal picks up Deranged, and whips him to the ringpost! Deranged looks like he REALLY hurt his shoulder there! Now Heavy Metal picks him up again, AND DOCTOR BOMBS HIM ON THE CONCRETE!!! LB: Say goodnight, little buddy! MW: Heavy Metal tosses Deranged back into the ring now, and Deranged has got be badly hurt! Heavy Metal kicks the prone Deranged in the kidney a few times, and he's going for the cover! One..Two..And Heavy Metal pulls him up! MW: What did he do that for? He could've won the second fall right there! BD: I think that he wants to make Deranged suffer for humiliating him last fall. MW: Well I think it's a mistake. Heavy Metal lifts Deranged to his feet now, and whips him to the ropes! Deranged comes back, AND GETS CAUGHT WITH ANOTHER CLOTHESLINE! The cover! One..Two..and Heavy Metal pulls him up again! MW: He has victory in his hands! Why doesn't he just take it? LB: He doesn't want it, yet. MW: Well that's just plain stupid. And now Heavy Metal lift's Deranged up again, AND HE'S SETTING HIM UP FOR THE MEGADETH POWER BOMB! He lifts Deranged up, AND PLANTS HIM! The cover! One..Two..Three!!! BD: That was awesome! Deranged TOTALLY got his ass kicked! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of six minutes and eleven seconds, the winner of the second fall is HEAVY METAL!!! The match is now tied at one fall apiece! The next fall wins the match and the title! MW: It's not over yet, Big Daddy. Deranged has some time to get back to his feet before we start the third fall, and you remember what happened in the first fall! BD: Yeah, I remember what happened-- a complete and total FLUKE happened! MW: I don't think so, and it could happen again. LB: In your dreams, Wilson. MW: It could so. Both falls so far have been total domination by one man, the third fall is really a toss up. BD: Just you watch. Heavy Metal will win this fall just as easily as the last one! MW: We'll see, as Deranged is up, and our third fall is underway! Deranged charges in at Heavy Metal, and this barrage looks remarkably like the first fall! Deranged doubles him over with a kick to the midsection, AND HE'S SETTING HIM UP FOR THE POWER BOMB AGAIN!! LB: NO, NOT AGAIN!!! MW: Deranged goes to lift him up, BUT HE CAN'T! He's too worn out from the last fall! And Heavy Metal counters with a backdrop!!! LB: I knew he couldn't do it again. MW: Sure you did. Heavy Metal goes over to the downed Deranged, and DROPS an elbow onto him! And now Heavy Metal gets back up, and he's going for a figure four leglock! BD: Boy, this guy shows you something different every time out! MW: He sure does, but he doesn't have it in yet, AND DERANGED COUNTERS WITH A SMALL PACKAGE!! One..Kickout! LB: That didn't hurt Heavy Metal, it just made him mad. MW: Maybe so, but it bought Deranged some valuable time! Both men quickly up, and Deranged STUNS the champ with a series of right hands! Heavy Metal is staggered! Deranged grabs him now, ANOTHER SMALL PACKAGE!! One..Two..Kickout! BD: (shouting) Come on Heavy Metal, don't let this punk treat you like this! MW: Deranged now brings the champ to his feet, and whips him to the ropes! Heavy Metal comes back, and LEVELS Deranged with a shoulderblock! LB: That's the way, Heavy Metal! MW: Heavy Metal picks Deranged up now, grabs onto him, FRONT LAYOUT SUPLEX!! And Deranged is in trouble! He slowly makes it to his feet, and Heavy Metal takes him down with a backslide! One..Two..Kickout! BD: It's only a matter of time now. MW: Apparently Heavy Metal thinks so as well. He bodyslams Deranged with ease, and he's going up top! He waits for Deranged to get to his feet, MOONSAULT BODYBLOCK!! Just look at that 450-pounder fly! The cover! One..Kickout! LB: WHAT???? MW: He kicked out at one. LB: I repeat-- WHAT? MW: He did it, I don't know how, but he did. And Heavy Metal's not happy! He's going back up top! He's on the top rope, and he's setting up for another Moonsault! The big man takes off, and CRASHES down--BUT NOBODY'S THERE! DERANGED ROLLED AWAY! And the champ's in trouble now, Deranged is getting he second wind! BD: Come on, that's gotta be like his third or fourth wind. MW: Deranged picks the champ up, and NAILS him with a jawbreaker! He picks him up again, PILEDRIVER! LB: This can't be happening! MW: It IS happening! And Deranged lifts the champ up one more time, AND HE'S GOING FOR THE POWER BOMB ONE MORE TIME!!! BD: (whispering, very quickly) Our father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy.... MW: He's got Heavy Metal in position, he lifts with all his might, AND GETS HIM UP ONE MORE TIME--BUT HEAVY METAL SHOCKS DERANGED--FRANKENSTEINER! The cover! One..Two..Three!!!!(DING, DING) LB: HA HA! I told you! Heavy Metal rules! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of nine minutes and forty six seconds, the winner of the third and deciding fall, and STILL AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World.... HEAVY METAL! MW: Another gallant battle, but AGAIN, the champion prevails. These two put on one hell of a show though. And Heavy Metal did a great job of coming back to win after losing the first fall in such a quick and embarrassing manner. BD: I never doubted that Heavy Metal would win for a second. MW: Really? Then what was that you were muttering when Deranged was about to power bomb Heavy Metal? BD: Oh THAT? Well....um....you see.... MW: Nevermind, I think that you've already answered my question. LB: What an IDIOT Deranged is, huh? He could've had Heavy Metal in a regular match, one fall, and he would've won. But he had to have two-out-of-three, when he knows damn well that NO MAN can beat that mastodon TWICE in the same night! MW: I think it could be done. LB: Then you're a moron. What's going on in the ring? Are those officials taking out the ropes? MW: They sure are, and they're putting in barbed wire in it's place! LB: This is nuts, I guess we know what's coming then. MW: You've got that right. It's a barbed wire steel cage match between Twisted Steel and Ben Slocum. BD: You'd have to be CRAZY to wrestle in that! And now they're putting up the cage, and that's got barbed wire across the top too! How are they supposed to get out after they lock that thing? MW: They're not. That's the idea-- no one gets in, no one gets out. LB: This one is gonna be a bloodbath! MW: That's for SURE! BD: And now I think they've got it all set up. Slocum's in for a world of hurting tonight. MW: I think both of them are going to be in pain before the night is over. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is a special barbed wire steel cage match, there are only TWO ways to win-- pinfall or submission! Introducing first, weighing in at 425 pounds and accompanied to the ring by his manager, Karma, here is Twisted Steeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeel!! ("The Funeral March" plays loudly over the PA as Twisted Steel makes his way to the ring, and the boos rain down. Twisted Steel is wearing an "Angels of Death" T-Shirt, and Karma is at his side.) LB: And here comes that monster of a man, Twisted Steel! He's gonna tear Slocum apart! MW: That's what you said last week, and he needed the help of Ricky Roberts and the Grim Reaper, the other "Angels", in order to get the win. LB: What are you talking about? I never saw any interference. MW: Are you blind or something? Reaper nailed Slocum with a chair while Roberts rolled Twisted Steel on top of him! LB: Oh THAT interference. I thought you were talking about some OTHER interference. MW: Why do I bother? RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Austin, Texas, he weighs in at 302 pounds and is accompanied by his sister Sondra, "The Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slooooooooocum! (Laura Branigan's "Self Control" plays loudly over the PA, as Slocum makes his way to the ring to a HUGE crowd pop. He and Sondra slap hands with the fans, but Slocum looks all business.) BD: The fun is about to begin, Slocum is gonna get ripped to shreds! MW: Slocum can handle himself in that ring just as well as anyone else. LB: But this type of match is styled towards a lunatic! And that's just what Twisted Steel is! MW: You've got a point there. Slocum steps into the ring, and the door is locked behind him, there's no getting in or out from here on in until the match is over! BD: Get ready for the rage in the cage! MW: Twisted Steel charges in at Slocum, but Slocum catches him with an armdrag takedown! Twisted Steel gets back up, and Slocum LEVELS him with a lariat! LB: Get up, you big oaf! MW: Don't let Twisted Steel hear you call him that. And now, Slocum picks Twisted Steel up, and whips him to the barbed wire! And Twisted Steel really got some nice gashes in his back there. The blood is just flowing out! BD: WHAT? Slocum can't do that to Twisted Steel! MW: He just did. And now Slocum picks Twisted Steel up, AND HE'S GOING FOR THE DEATH DROP!!! LB: How can he?-- there's no ropes there to climb, just barbed wire. MW: You're right, and it appears that Slocum just realized it as he got to the corner. I think he's just gonna do it without climbing the ropes, BUT HE'S TOO LATE!! TWISTED STEEL NAILS HIM WITH A KICK TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD, causing him to let go of Twisted Steel and stagger head first into that unprotected ring post! BD: What a moron! He took too long deciding what to do, and Twisted Steel burned him! MW: He certainly did that! And now Slocum looks out from that collision with the ring post, Twisted Steel picks him up, AND HITS HIM WITH THE PAIN BOMB!! LB: Oh, yeah! It's over already! MW: The cover! One..Two..KICKOUT!!! BD: What the hell? MW: You can say that again. Slocum, with his amazing resilience, manages to kick out of the Pain Bomb, even after that brutal collision with the ring post. LB: That's because the moron doesn't know when to quit. MW: I don't think so, it's because he's got a lot of heart, and that's something that you just can't learn, you've gotta be born with it. LB: Well when this match is over, Slocum is gonna have some scars on him that he WASN'T born with. I can tell you that much. MW: That's entirely possible. Twisted Steel picks Slocum up now, and begins to GRIND his face into the cage! And now Slocum's busted wide open!! BD: Look at all that blood covering his ugly face, it's a beautiful sight to behold. MW: You're sick. And now Twisted Steel picks up Slocum after that, and lifts him up, he runs, AND PLANTS HIM WITH A POWERSLAM!!! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: He's just about finished now, and look, here comes Ricky Roberts to ringside! MW: And even though he can't get in that cage, this CAN'T be good for Slocum. Twisted Steel brings Slocum to his feet, and whips him to the barbed wire--BUT SLOCUM REVERSES IT! And Twisted Steel gets cut up again! BD: That looked like it hurt a little. MW: That looked like it hurt a lot! And now, what's Slocum doing? He's ripping off a section of the barbed wire, AND WRAPPING IT AROUND THE FACE OF TWISTED STEEL!!!! LB: OUCH! MW: You can say that again, buddy. Twisted Steel's face is a bloody mess! Slocum picks the big man up now, AND BODYSLAMS HIM! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: And Roberts just shoved something through the cage, and Twisted Steel picked it up! MW: It's a set of brass knuckles! Slocum goes to pick Twisted Steel up off the mat, and gets CLOCKED with a brass uppercut! Slocum is out! This is horrible! LB: I think it's great! Now Twisted Steel's peeling that barbed wire off of his face, that's GOTTA hurt! MW: He's got it off now, and what's he doing? HE'S WRAPPING IT AROUND THE NECK OF SLOCUM, and pulling back on it! THAT COULD SLICE OPEN A VEIN IN HIS NECK! BD: I think that's the idea. MW: Twisted Steel is wrapping that tighter and tighter around the neck of Slocum, but now he just stops, AND DELIVERS A BIG SPLASH!!! The cover! One..Two..Three!!! (DING, DING) LB: That was the bloodiest match I've ever seen! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of five minutes and ten seconds, here is your winner.... TWISTED STEEL! MW: And now the caged door has been opened, and Roberts rushes in to join his partner in crime. And now they're double teaming Slocum! They're really putting the boots to him!! BD: This is great! Now this is what wrestling's all about. MW: I know I've told you this a lot tonight, but you're sick. And here comes Sir Gallade to the rescue! He's into that cage like a flash, and he's taking it to Steel and Roberts! He punches Steel, then punches Roberts! He bodyslams Steel, then bodyslams Roberts! He's really cleaning house in there! LB: But here comes more trouble-- the 6' 10", 435-pound Grim Reaper! MW: The Reaper climbs into the ring, and Gallade is still taking on two men, BUT HE GETS NAILED FROM BEHIND BY THE MASSIVE REAPER! And now the Reaper picks Gallade up, AND POWERSLAMS HIM! BD: When you mess with one of the Angels of Death, you mess with ALL of them. MW: And now Twisted Steel is getting up, and he's telling the Reaper something! The Reaper nods, and goes to pick up Gallade, while Twisted Steel goes to pick up Slocum! Twisted Steel's setting Slocum up for the Pain Bomb, and Reaper's setting Gallade up for the Power Bomb! They lift them up, AND PLANT THEM!!! LB: Power Bombs in stereo! I LOVE IT! MW: And now what are they doing? They lift Gallade and Slocum up again, DOUBLE GORILLA PRESS! They let go, AND BOTH MEN DROP NECK FIRST ONTO THE BARBED WIRE! THIS IS DISGUSTING! And now the Angels of Death are walking away, and laughing hysterically! Something has to be done about these men, they're out of control! BD: And I like 'um that way. MW: And I've already told you a million times that you're SICK, S-I-C-K, SICK! And now the paramedics are attending to poor Ben Slocum and Sir Gallade in the ring. They're gonna be VERY upset when they regain consciousness. I'm sorry fans, but we're out of time, so for The Lovely Bertha and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt, this is Matt Wilson saying so long everybody!
