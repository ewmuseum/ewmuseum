Saturday Night Slam (3/1, part one)

3 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * AEWA SATURDAY NIGHT SLAM * * * * Live, from the Cow Palace in San Francisco (We see the AEWA logo on the screen, set on fire. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Rock music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... David Graves TAKING SOME GUY'S HEAD OFF with the Gravestone, Penance PLANTING some guy with the Final Judgment, The West Coast Connection DRIVING some guy's head into the mat with the West Coast Killa, Cardinal Chaos STICKING some poor soul with the Inverted Crucifix, Shane Matthews BRUTALLY hitting the End of the Road on some hapless jobber, and Heavy Metal notching yet ANOTHER casualty courtesy of the Megadeth Power Bomb. The camera then shifts to Matt Wilson, The Lovely Bertha, and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt in the PACKED Cow Palace, the site of AEWA Saturday Night Slam!) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Saturday Night Slam! We have plenty of GREAT matches scheduled for tonight, and in the main event, Damian Steele will be squaring off against Red Dog. BD: Those guys are both brawlers, so I'm interested to see who comes out on top in that match. It's going to be an all-out war, that's for sure. LB: But what's first? MW: Well, we have some exclusive footage from Mike Stewart's press conference, which was held earlier today. I still don't know what happened, but I heard it was rather important. BD: He probably chickened out again. Stewart was never cut-out for the AEWA, and after those allegations that he made about President Freeman at Kickoff '97, he's probably history. MW: I wouldn't bet on that, Big Daddy. Anyway, I don't feel like having another pointless argue with you, so let's just watch the tape.... ---------- (The camera opens on a press conference room packed with reporters. They have all been called in to a press conference for Mike Stewart. In the front of the room stands a wooden podium, and behind it stands Stewart in a black suit, and beside him stand a man in his early to mid 20s with a black suit, and a woman in her early to mid 20s with a white suit jacket, white short skirt, and white high heels. Stewart steps up to the podium.) Stewart: Ladies and gentlemen of the press, if you'll all take your seats, we can get this press conference started. (The reporters all take their seats, and Stewart begins.) Stewart: Thank you. The reason I've called you all here today is to bring you some news. Recently, I was consulted by Dr. Jim Andrews that a injury I had suffered in a scaffold match in the AEWA was not as well healed as was thought. Returning to the ring when I did, it just furthered the injury. Dr. Andrews then told me that if I still wanted to be able to play sports with my kids when I was in my 40s, that I would have to retire from professional wrestling. (Reporters begin to buzz.) Stewart: And that's what I've decided to do. (Reporter begin to buzz more.) Stewart: I, along with my girlfriend Michelle Anderson, and my parents, have decided that this would be the best course of action, so right now, I am pulling out of all active competition in the EWA, DPW, and AEWA, and also any federations which I might have been joining. (Reporters buzz once more.) Stewart: (smiles) However, I have decided that I will throw my support from now on to a man who I have seen train, and have actually wrestled with. (looks to the man behind him and points to him) This man behind me _will_ take my place in these federations. A few of you might know him, but I'm sure in a short time, all of you will. Ladies and gentlemen, I'd like to present to you, the newest sensation in professional wrestling, along with his beautiful wife Ericka, meet Kyle Hatcher! (Reporters stand and take pictures as Hatcher and Stewart hug, and Hatcher steps forward to the podium with Ericka hand-in-hand, and Stewart steps back.) Hatcher: Ladies and gentlemen of the press, I'd like to make this short and brief. First of all, I'd like to thank Mike for his support, and I'll try to live up to everything he's achieved. Although Mike has gone down a path of doing what he has to do to win, I'm not quite that way. I know that the only reason I'm here to wrestle is because of the fans, and I plan on bringing respect for the fans back into wrestling. Furthermore, I will be taking over Mike's contractual obligations against Matt Harris, Jason The Terrible, Deathwatch, and any other matches he may have had scheduled. (smiles) And that's about all. Now I'll give it back to Mike. (Stewart steps forward and Kyle and Ericka step back.) Stewart: As some of you may or may not know, there were also two friends of mine who have been wrestling along my side in the EWA and DPW, those being Mark Washington and David Kennedy, better known as American Thunder. Well, Mark and David have decided not to compete in the EWA and DPW due to other commitments outside of the pro wrestling world. They have, however, left behind their name for two young men those in the AEWA might recognize. Ladies and gentlemen, I'd like to bring out, not the _new_ American Thunder, but _the_ American Thunder. You might have known them as The New Gods, but they are now known as Eric Blake and Steve Walker! (Eric Blake, a tall muscular man standing around 6'6" and looking to weigh around 320 pounds, walks into the room in a custom-fit black suit with his long blonde hair in a ponytail. Walker, also muscular, and standing 6'5" and weighing around 265 pounds, walks in beside Blake, wearing a gray suit. They both shake Hatcher and Stewart's hands, and then both step up to the podium. Blake speaks first.) Blake: I would first like to thank Mike for a great introduction, and I'd like to thank Mark and David for giving us their name. As Kyle said, Mark and David also took a different path than we will. We also know we're here for the fans, so we will fight for them and what's right. (Walker steps up to the podium.) Walker: Some of you might know us as The New Gods. I myself wrestled as Superior, and Eric as Thor. Now, we're going to be who we are and we plan on being on top of the tag team scene in the world of professional wrestling. (Kyle Hatcher steps back up to the podium as Eric and Steve step back.) Kyle: In closing, I'd like to say that I welcome all of the fans' support, and I welcome all challenges, as I'm sure do Eric and Steve. We look forward to bringing the titles of professional wrestling to the fans and to giving the fans the best matches possible. Thank you. (Kyle steps back and then he, Ericka Hatcher, Eric Blake, Steve Walker, and Mike Stewart all walk out of the conference room. The reporters get up and go to the phones to call in their story, and the camera fades to black. We cut back to our broadcast team.) ----------- LB: So let me get this straight-- Kyle Hatcher, a totally inexperienced rookie, is going to get an automatic shot at the North American title on pay-per-view? That hardly seems fair. BD: And why did Stewart make his little speech at Kickoff '97, if he's just going to drop out again? I knew he was a loser, but jeez, I didn't know he was THAT bad. MW: He must have visited his doctor after that speech, folks. You really need to cut him some slack, this isn't his fault. LB: Yes, actually, it is. And now this Hatcher guy is going to get the North American title shot? How the hell can Stewart DO this? BD: Hatcher didn't EARN the shot, so he shouldn't be allowed to just "slip" into it. That just doesn't seem right. MW: I have to admit, Big Daddy, it really doesn't. If I was Deathwatch, I'd have a BIG problem with this decision. LB: Anyway, what's next? MW: Primal Fear is about to square off against the Gryphon, folks. These two guys actually have quite a bit in common, if you think about it. I'm not sure who to root for. BD: Then here's an idea-- why don't you just shut the hell up? Nobody really cares about this match anyway, right? MW: Well, actually-- BD: Just stay quiet, Wilson. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, weighing in at 360 pounds, he is the most extreme wrestler in the AEWA, PRIMAL FEAR! (Primal Fear makes his entrance to a HUGE pop from the crowd. He is wearing a black vest and brown tights. He has a determined look on his face, and quickly climbs into the ring.) MW: And here we go, all we have now is the entrance of The Gryphon and this thing can begin. BD: Oh crap, look who's coming over here.... (Loud boos can be heard from the audience, and most of them stand up to see one Zack Derringer walking down the isle. He spits at people and curses at them as he goes, and finally he reaches the announcer's booth and sits down next to Bertha.) ZD: Hey babe, how's it goin'? LB: Give me a break.... Wilson!? MW: What!? It's not like I can do anything about him, he can fire any of us. Him and his damn connections. ZD: That's right, kiddies. Not only can I FIRE your asses, I can also KICK your asses. Ain't that right, "Big Dorky" Mike Hurt? BD: Whatever, can we just get this match started? ZD: Hey, I ain't holdin' ya up. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 310 pounds, from parts unknown, here is THE GRYPHON! (The Gryphon makes his way to the ring, wearing his long black cape. He receives a BIG pop from the crowd, but stares directly at Primal Fear. He appears to be in a very bad mood.) ZD: You know, this guy holds more sh*t in him than a latrine. I'm really sick and tired of the Gryphon. MW: Please, Zack, try to keep the language to a minimum, alright? ZD: What'd you care? It isn't risking your job. LB: Yeah, Wilson. Listen to him, it's risking HIS job, not ours. BD: Just keep talking, Derringer. Maybe we won't have to put up with you for very long after all. ZD: You'll never get rid of me, losers. (DING, DING) MW: Well, here we go. The Gryphon and Primal Fear lock up, and Gryphon really sends Fear HARD into the turnbuckle! He runs in, AVALANCHE!! BD: But no one's home! ZD: Yeah, no one's home alright. BD: Shut up, Derringer! LB: Kids, kids.... can we just commentate? BD: (sighs) Primal Fear takes the Gryphon down with a snap mare, and here's the cover! One... Two... Kickout! MW: Close one for Gryphon, but I don't think you can get Gryphon THAT early. ZD: Wanna bet? Ten bucks says Exodus could take him out in less than a minute. BD: Would you just be QUIET? ZD: Make me, buck-o. MW: (shakes head) ANYWAYS, back to the match. Gryphon and Fear are on their feet now, and Fear runs in with a clothesline, DUCKED by Gryphon! Gryphon locks on a sleeper hold and Fear is slowly, but surely, going down. BD: No way, the Gryphon has a weak lock on him-- he'll be lucky if he even GETS him down. LB: Gryphon almost has him down now.... and Fear breaks the hold! BD: Told ya. MW: Fear bounces off the ropes, and he catches Gryphon with a HUGE cross body press! The cover! One... Two... Thr.... KICKOUT! ZD: Make up your mind, ref! The Gryphon lost, there's no denying it. BD: The referee is only saying two, so this thing is still going. LB: Shows how much you know, Zack. ZD: Hey, I know everything, babe. I also know that the dress you're wearing tonight has a rip in the back. (snickers) LB: What? WHERE!? ZD: Right in the back.... near your ass. LB: Oh my god! I've got to go to the bathroom for a second! Get out of the way, Derringer! (Zack just smiles and motions for her to go, without moving.) LB: Oh, no way. You don't EVEN expect me to climb over you, do you? ZD: Hey, I'm not the one that wants to leave, am I? (grins) LB: Big Daddy, tell this pervert to move! BD: Get out of her way, Derringer-- before I pop you in the face! ZD: Try it and I'll sue you. LB: Fine! (Bertha climbs over Derringer spread eagle, but before she can get the other leg over him, Derringer blocks her.) ZD: How'd ya like that position, babe? (snickers) (Bertha SLAMS her fist into Derringer's crotch, and his face turns bright red. Bertha climbs over and leaves the broadcast area.) BD: (laughing) Serves you right, Zack. ZD: (with a restrained voice) Sh.... shut up! MW: Can we PLEASE get back to the match? The Gryphon takes Fear down with a suplex! Now he's going up top.... ELBOWDROP! Gryphon is really on a roll here tonight, folks! BD: The Gryphon picks Fear up and here's the set-up, PILEDRIVER! He goes for the cover! One... Two... Kickout!! MW: The Gryphon picks Fear up again, and he sets him up on the top rope! Gryphon bounces off the ropes, and he runs into Fear with a fist RIGHT in the lower abdominal area! BD: I bet THAT hits closer to home, right Zack? (chuckles) ZD: Hey! I told you to SHU-- LB: (just arriving back at the booth) Excuse me? ZD: Oh, um, (stands up) here ya go. (Bertha sits down.) LB: That's better. MW: And now the Gryphon is climbing up top from behind Fear, and he picks Fear up and turns him around. Gryphon lifts him into the air.... SUPER BOMB! It's over! Here's the cover! One... Two... THREE!! He got him! (DING, DING) ZD: All he's doing is showing off for what he did to Legacy at Kickoff '97. This is disgusting. BD: Yeah, isn't it great? ZD: How many times do I have to tell you to shut the hell up? RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner as the result of a pinfall.... THE GRYPHON! (The Gryphon gets a HUGE crowd pop as he leaves ringside, and he slaps hands with the fans. All of a sudden, however, Exodus runs out of the locker room area and nails him with a clothesline!) ZD: YES! Kick his ass, Exodus! MW: It's complete chaos out there! Gryphon and Exodus are brawling it out, and both of these big men are just slamming into each other like two ton trucks! BD: Gryphon drives Exodus into the steel barricade, and Exodus counters it with a faceslam on the concrete! But the Gryphon is UP! ZD: Exodus runs in with another clothesline, but he's CAUGHT with a dropkick! I don't believe this! LB: Officials are coming out now and breaking this thing up, but both men want at each other REALLY bad! MW: You aren't kidding! Exodus just PUNCHED an official, and that's going to cost him. Gryphon isn't trying as hard, but both men have been separated and are being escorted to the locker room area. LB: Well, now that that's over, what's next, Wilson? MW: Hank Thompson is about to conduct an exclusive interview with the "Rebel Of Rock" Randall Osbourne. As you all know, Sir Gallade caused Osbourne to lose that "chain of pain" match at Kickoff '97, so I'm really interested to hear what he has to say. BD: I'm not. He's just an overweight windbag who needs to get some new friends to hang around with. This is gonna be SO boring. ZD: Well, I hate all these guys anyway, so it really doesn't matter to me. Let's just get this crap over with. MW: Take it away, Hank! (The camera shifts to Hank Thompson, who is standing in the ring, about to start his interview.) Thompson: Ladies and gentlemen, at this time I'd like you to welcome the Rebel of Rock, RANDALL OSBOURNE!! (The crowd explodes as AC/DC's "It's a Long Way to the Top" plays over the PA. Osbourne enters the ring, wearing normal street clothes and a leather jacket saying "Knights" on the back. He wears a pair of dark sunglasses and has an angry look on his face.) Thompson: Thank you for coming, Randy. Osbourne: No problem. Thompson: That was quite a match you had against Shane Matthews at Kickoff '97. Your thoughts? Osbourne: It was a good match.... too bad it didn't end. Thompson: What exactly do you mean? Osbourne: Throughout that match, I had Matthews under control. He was getting the beating of his life at my hands, but there was one thing I learned that night. The ONLY way for Matthews to ever get the edge on me is if he handcuffs me to an immovable object-- or if he has other people help in the attack. Thompson: Are you referring to Sir Gallade? Osbourne: That man is hardly a Knight. I will NEVER refer to him as a sir. He has made a mockery of the AEWA and the Knights, and he completely betrayed us. Gallade, humiliation will come for you soon. You don't betray the Knights and go unpunished. There will be DELIVERANCE! (The crowd roars with agreement) Thompson: Are you issuing a challenge? Osbourne: I sure as hell am. But what kind of challenge am I going to issue? I want to beat the fire out of both Matthews AND Gallade, so what do I do? Easy. I want them both in the ring, so the challenge goes as follows. I want BOTH of them, Gallade and Matthews in a HANDICAP match. If I win, I take the TV title and some revenge. I am waiting for your answers, punks. And as an added bonus, all the Knights will be at ringside to prevent any outside interference. Thompson: Are you serious? Osbourne: Serious as a bullet to the head. (The crowd cheers loudly as Osbourne walks away, and the camera returns to our broadcast team.) MW: Well, Osbourne is VERY angry with Shane Matthews and Sir Gallade, so that handicap match should be great. BD: Are you kidding me? He doesn't even stand a chance, Wilson. It's a simple fact-- one man cannot possibly beat two. LB: I'd have to agree, Big Daddy. ZD: I hate to interrupt your conversations, guys, but can we POSSIBLY move on to the next match? * * * * TO BE CONTINUED IN PART TWO * * * * \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- "A New Beginning" American E-Wrestling Association The Official E-Wrestling Federation of Wrestle World and TNM The Best E-Wrestling Fed of the Year 1996 in the TNM Year-End Awards Be sure to visit our Event Center at: http://www.california.com/~bullet/index.html
