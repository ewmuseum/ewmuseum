AEWA Straight Talk: Kickoff Plus (2/27)

2 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera fades in on the MGM Grand locker room, the AEWA logo falls onto the screen, and the words "STRAIGHT TALK" slide in underneath it. The whole logo then tilts, and falls off the bottom of the screen. Chuck Carlson and Ken Wilson are standing in the middle of the room, clutching their microphones.) CC: Hello everyone, we're here backstage at the MGM Grand, two days after the exiting Kickoff '97 pay-per-view. As you probably know, "Crimson" Joe Reed captured the World title, cleanly pinning Deranged in the middle of the ring. It was a GREAT show, and I'd like to encourage you all to order the replay. KW: Well, there were definitely some highlights to Kickoff, like when Deathwatch kicked Justin Trouble's butt and won the North American title. That was great. CC: Justin had ALL the odds against him, Ken. It was just a little too much for him, don't ya think? KW: No, I have a more scientific explanation for his loss-- HE GOT HIS BUTT KICKED! Deathwatch was just MUCH more prepared for the match, and it cost Justin the title. CC: I'd love to keep arguing with you, Ken, but we actually have a show to do here. In just a little bit, I'm going to be showing you footage of Kevin Larkin, which was taped immediately after the pay-per-view. It's VERY shocking, folks. KW: I was really disappointed with the outcome of Kevin's match, though. The Visigothic Horde were just about to beat President Freeman and Heaven & Hell, but poor Kevin had to get dragged into the action. Mister Crowley hit him with a leg drop, and it was all over.... CC: Kevin Larkin brought that upon himself, Ken. After MONTHS of tormenting Freeman in the corporate office, Larkin FINALLY got what he deserved. That was my favorite match, without a doubt. KW: You've got a big mouth, Chuck-- just shut up. Anyway, our cameraman got an EXCLUSIVE interview with Kevin, and you won't BELIEVE what happened! I've already seen this footage, and I STILL can't believe it! CC: Yes, it's definitely outrageous. Let's take a look at the interview, and then we've got some more information for you afterwards. ------------- (In his private locker room, Kevin Larkin sits on a bench, sniffling, as he stuffs assorted personal items into a sack.) Larkin: (sniff) It isn't fair. I was here first. I shouldn't have to wrestle, I was here as Vice President. (sniff) But they made me wrestler against those guys, which isn't fair, and now I have to go, which isn't fair, because I was the one who was here in the beginning and I was supposed to be in charge and everybody else started it, not me. (sniff) It's all one person's fault, too. The whole thing can be blamed on one person who couldn't mind his own business. Coffin, the Grave Dweller and the Mortician. I was working really, really well, and they didn't like that they lost because they stink and they blamed me and they threatened me, and did anyone do anything? No. They made a wax me and stuck it in a coffin, and no one cared. Then I get suspended-- I, who was just trying to do my job. (sniff) So I get some wrestlers and I come back and I manage them, and I get a perfectly valid manager's license, and I know it was because I approved it myself because I was the Vice President. And we beat the Graveyard Shift fair and square all over the country, and I got my job back and I got my paychecks back, and I got my own show. Then Bob Freeman stepped in and it's all his fault now that I have to go, (sniff) which isn't fair because I'm not a wrestler and shouldn't have to wrestle and I was here first. (sniff) He cut the budget for my show, and I didn't have a telestrator and had to use the etch-a-sketch, and the sign was falling apart and he wouldn't fix it, and when I had guests he wouldn't give me more microphones. The Graveyard Shift even broke in and attacked me, but they weren't suspended like I had been, even though I had never interrupted THEIR show-- because they never had a show-- and when I was injured, no one was suspended. I tried to play videotapes, and someone changed them to tapes of the Graveyard Shift threatening me, and then the show was canceled, which wasn't fair because I was here first. (sniff) And then Bob signs tag team title matches behind my back and right under my nose for two solo wrestlers, just because they were breaking his hand, and I bet it was a set-up from the beginning, you could tell because they were on his side. And when I said I was the Vice President and they couldn't have the match, they fought with my team and tried to attack, but they didn't get suspended because the only person who gets suspended around here is me, I guess. That's probably not even legal, because I am-- was-- the Vice President. And Bob kept sticking his nose in my business, and so did Heaven and Hell, and it's all their fault, and the Shift's, and Bob's. And then we had a tag team title shot, and Bob got the board to say that we couldn't, and the Machines got it and they won so it.... but it was our spot that won, so WE would have won, so we should be the champions now. (sniff) So then Bob said I had to wrestle because we beat him up, and it isn't fair because I'm not a wrestler and I was here before him-- ask Tommy, he knows, he was here, too. And then, and then, and then.... um.... we had to fight against Bob and his team, and his team was Heaven and Hell and I never agreed to wrestle them, because I'm not a wrestler anyway, and now we lost because they cheated and now I have to go, which isn't fair. (sniff) You guys were no help (to the Visigothic Horde, who are sitting in the corner with their customary vapid look), and now I have to go-- but you guys don't. Well, Deathwatch and the ICU wanted me to join them and to bring the Horde with me, but now I can't. But I can do this-- Deathwatch, I am signing their contracts over to you. You can take them, and make them in your stable. All you have to do, is feed them and stuff like that, and they'll be okay. They've had their shots, and they're house-trained and stuff. They're stupid, but you can make them attack people and things like that. (sniff) And-- HEY! (The door explodes inward, and a sooty red glow emanates from the hall. Cardinal Chaos and Mister Crowley stand in the doorway. Larkin utters a quick squeak. Chaos and Crowley stride past him, without giving the slightest indication that they notice his presence. They cross the room and stand before Athanaric and Fritigern, who silently drop to their knees and bow their heads. Chaos lays his hands on their foreheads and mutters a near-silent incantation or blessing. As he finishes, he raises his hands, and the Horde return to an upright position. Chaos and Crowley withdraw to the hallway, again taking no notice of Larkin, and the Horde follow silently, without looking back.) Larkin: Um.... you can't.... they.... um.... Deathwatch has them now. You can't take them-- they're not yours! This isn't fair; you don't have the right! You don't have the right! (whispering) You don't have the right.... (The camera fades to black on Larkin, and cuts back to Chuck Carlson and Ken Wilson.) --------------- CC: In a shocking turns of events, the Visigothic Horde appear to have joined the Church. Of course, there's only one problem with that-- Kevin Larkin already gave them to ICU! KW: So what happens now? Which group has the Visigothic Horde joined, ICU or the Church? CC: Well, I'm not sure. This definitely presents a conflict of interest for everyone involved, doesn't it? I wonder what Deathwatch thinks about this situation. KW: We'll probably find out soon. Anyway, it's time for the next interview now. Let's move briskly along.... CC: Up next, we have some special footage from the Machines. They spoke at an elementary school yesterday, and we'd like to show you some scenes from that. KW: An ELEMENTARY school? Jeez, are they going to visit a local nursing home next? These guys used to be cool, but they're really going soft on us. CC: Just watch, Ken.... -------------- (Paul Wong and Simon O'Neal stand outside a Lincoln Elementary School second grade classroom in their wrestling outfits a day after Kickoff '97. Each man is carrying an AEWA World Tag Team Belt. Simon does NOT look happy.) Simon: I can't believe I agreed to this! Paul: Look, my niece Amy and her class have been inviting people with unusual careers to speak to the students, and she put in my name. She said that there are lots of wrestling fans in the room. Simon: Also, you spoil your niece rotten. Why couldn't you refuse? Paul: Yeah-- and I notice that you were able to turn her down when she asked you to come. (Simon grumbles a little more, and then they are invited in to a room with about 25 seven year olds, most of whom are cheering when The Machines enter the room. Some of the children are even wearing T-shirts of the various AEWA wrestlers. The teacher introduces them, and then the kids start asking questions.) Kid #1: (wearing a Ben Slocum T-shirt) Do you get along? Every time I see you, you're always fighting. Paul: (laughs) Simon and I disagree on a lot of things, but we've always worked well together. And while we argue a lot, it never gets personal. It's kind of like fighting with your brother. Kid #2: (wearing a Primal Fear shirt) Who's your toughest opponent? Paul: We've had lots of problems with the Assassins and Graphic Violence. (the kids boo, which brings another grin to Paul's face) But I'd say our toughest tag team match was against the West Coast Connection. I don't like them, but they're very tough, and have been the champs several times. (A third kid stands up. He's wearing a West Coast Connection T-shirt.) Kid #3: I saw Kickoff, and the West Coast Connection kicked your ass! You ought to just give up now. Paul: Well, I th.... (Simon's stops Paul by raising his hand and walks over to the seven-year old, and gives him his trademark smirk.) Simon: Kid.... what's your name? Kid: Sam. Simon: Sam. (shakes his head) Sam, sam, sam, sam, sam. You saw Kickoff '97? (Sam nods, Simon faces the class) Did anyone else? (about half the class nod) Good. Then here's what you saw.... (Simon picks up Sam under his shoulders, lifts him up, and stands him on top of his desk, so that Simon and Sam are standing eye-to-eye.) Simon: You saw us take on those punks in a three-on-two match, in a street brawl that's more their style. We took them on in their turf, on their type of match. And we're still the champions. If the referee hadn't stopped the match, we would have won, and WE kicked THEIR asses, not the other way around. Those Red Hot Chile Pepper wannabees can't hold a candle to us. Do you understand, Sammy boy? (Sam, speechless with fear, just nods.) Simon: Good. I'm glad we had this little chat. Now sit down and shut up. Paul: All right.... any more questions? (Everyone is afraid to speak for a moment. Finally, Amy Wong raises her hand.) Amy: Uncle Paul, why did the Assassins call you a rulebreaker? You don't cheat. Simon: It's called title envy, and.... Paul: Be quiet, Simon. Amy, we took on the Assassins several months ago, and while it was a controversial match (glares at Simon), we came out with the win. They want to be the World Champions again, and I guess they're challenging us. Well, we'll take on the Assassins at any time. The West Coast Connection didn't stop us, Heaven and Hell couldn't stop us, and while the Assassins are very good, they're not going to stop us. Simon: Also, let's discuss cheating. Everyone repeat after me. (he grabs a piece of chalk, and writes on the board as he talks) IT'S NOT CHEATING.... The entire class: IT'S NOT CHEATING... Simon: .... UNLESS YOU GET CAUGHT. Class: .... UNLESS YOU GET CAUGHT. (A frustrated Paul starts pulling Simon out of the classroom into the hallway. Simon can be heard saying "This was fun. We'll have to do this more often...." as the camera fades. We cut back to Ken Wilson and Chuck Carlson.) ------------ KW: Well, the Machines definitely made an impact on that second-grade class, but can they make an impact on the Assassins? That remains to be seen, folks. CC: That would be an EXCELLENT match, Ken. Both of those teams have superb technical wrestlers, and that alone would be worth the price of a pay-per-view. KW: You'd pay twenty bucks to see ONE tag team match? What have you been SMOKING, Chuck? CC: I didn't mean-- KW: Look, I really don't care what you meant. Let's just get to our next interview, because the Extremist has a few things to say. CC: He has been VERY angry with Leroy Brown lately, folks, so this should be interesting. Let's roll the footage.... ------------- (The camera opens on a shot of The Extremist. He is standing inside his gym, which he rarely visits. The Extremist wears a pair of ash gray sweat pants, a white T-shirt with black lettering that simply reads, "F*ck Off", a pair of black Asics wrestling shoes, and a pair of The Grappling Glove, the type of gloves worn in UFC or Extreme Fighting. The Extremist stands next to a boxing/wrestling ring. The camera begins to move in on him as he looks back at the camera. He meets the view with a wide smile, which is clearly seen because his black hair is pulled back. He takes a deep breath as he stares into the camera, and he begins to speak in a low, but calm tone of voice. The camera shows a view from his shoulders up....) The Extremist- Leroy, my boy! Haven't heard much from you at all. Let's say that it is probably better for you that I don't hear from you, Leroy Brown. When you start to run your mouth, you pick up the shovel. And with each breath, with each word, you dig yourself a hole. And by the end of the week, you're going to look up, and you're going to find yourself SO deep in this hole, that you can't climb your way out. And at that point, I will cover you with dirt. (The Extremist gets up to the ring apron, on his feet.) The Extremist- Can you do me a favor, Leroy? Review the tapes, son. I am not your average run of the mill hotshot, sh*t talker, that has just come in off the streets looking to start a fight. You set a fire off, Leroy, _I_ AM that fire, and the heat is something you will not be able to take. Come hell or high water, Leroy Brown, your head will roll. And by the way.... hell is a long ways off, and I am not seeing a forecast for any rain. I can not wait. AEWA, you are going to experience first hand the meaning of the word Extremist. Actions speak louder than words, and my actions will make you deaf. You are going to see a BOLD statement. Leroy, when you wake up, and you're seeing the referee above you, after bringing you back to a living state, I want you to remember, YOU set it off. You brought me here.... ....and you CAN'T make me leave. (The Extremist steps into the ring.) The Extremist- This is where the action takes place, this is a place that I am sure you're familiar with. But it is time for a change, Leroy, I am going to introduce you to something new. It is called the hospital. Now, I am sure you have heard these threats before, but you had better not take me as a run of the mill sh*t talker. If you do, you're in for the WORST awakening of your life. I don't go to the ring for nothing. I am not in the quick loss club. What happens, happens, but when I go out there, I go out there to bang with the bad asses, and I don't lose. Leroy Brown, five months ago when you ran your mouth in ACEW, I heard. And now, those words are coming back. And son, I am going to show you the truth, and as they say, the truth just might kill you. (The Extremist stops pacing and looks directly into the camera.) The Extremist- I don't know when we will fight, and I don't really care. Stop the bullsh*t, to hell with the mudslinging. You bring your ass to the ring, and I will kick it right back out, but you make sure you show up. The AEWA has never seen anything like this before, and trust me, it will be a LONG time before you see something like it after I'm gone.... (The Extremist looks into the camera as the scene fades to black. We cut back to Ken Wilson and Chuck Carlson.) ------------ KW: Well, the Extremist still seems VERY angry with Leroy Brown, and it's all because of some incident that apparently occurred in ACEW. CC: This is a really heated situation, Ken, and these two guys are eventually going to settle their differences. That would be a great match, don't ya think? KW: It sure would, but it'd be a little bit too fast-- because the Extremist would whip his butt in less than a minute. CC: I seriously doubt that. Up next, we have an interview from Leroy Brown himself, so let's get HIS thoughts on this situation. ------------- (The camera opens on "Big Bad" Leroy Brown and Street King, sitting together in their dressing room. They both look absolutely furious. Street King is holding his trusty lead pipe, as always.) Brown: You know, when I first came to the AEWA, I made a bad decision to be called "The Masked Yorel", and that has cost me some of my great fans. I've been in the ACEW for over a year now, I've been through the uproar that took place-- and I came out all right. Then I came to the AEWA, hoping I could carry over some of my success here, but I mainly wanted to show the people here what Leroy was all about. I was goin' good here, but then the Extremist goes and says that he is angry about some comments I made about him. WHAT WAS HE TALKING ABOUT?? King: He was the one who started things. I know what I say, and Leroy knows what he says, and we didn't even bother with the Extremist, but then he goes and wants to start something. Well, when you start something with Leroy.... Brown: Leroy finishes it! Extremist, you don't have any idea what you're stepping into! This is BAD, buddy. King: (lifts up his pipe) Mr. Lead Pipe and me will do what it takes, and we'll show you what you're made of-- a sack of skin and bones with the brain of an elephant! Brown: Extremist, you think you're extreme? Buddy, I come from the streets and that's where it's EXTREME!! And Leroy is the founding father of extreme! So, Extremist, bring it on.... (They both begin laughing hysterically, and walk off camera. We cut back to Ken Wilson and Chuck Carlson in the locker room.) ------------- KW: Well, Leroy Brown is definitely upset about the Extremist's comments. I wonder if President Freeman will sign that match for the next pay-per-view. CC: I sure hope so. Their "war of words" has been going on for so long now, it's time for them to finally settle it in the ring. KW: I hate to change the subject, but I was wondering-- do you know anything about Legacy's condition? He took a REALLY bad fall at Kickoff '97, and it look pretty serious. CC: Well, I hate to tell you this, but the news is disappointing. The doctors examined him immediately after that accident.... KW: And what did they say? CC: Legacy has had a severe spinal injury, and he may not be able to compete for an EXTREMELY long time. In fact, it looks like he might have to retire because of this. KW: I'm really sorry to hear that. Legacy would have been a dominant force in the AEWA, but he never even got to make his debut. That's really unfortunate. CC: I might not like the ICU, but this kind of thing is always heartbreaking. (checks his watch) Well, we're running out of time, because this was a short episode, so let's wrap this sucker up. KW: Good-- I've got a date tonight. CC: There's a first time for everything, I guess. Be sure to tune into Saturday Night Slam, folks, when Heavy Metal will be stepping into the ring against his former stablemate, Penance! I bet you never thought you'd see that match, huh? KW: But Metal outweighs him by more than 200 pounds, for cryin' out loud! That's absolutely INSANE-- is Penance stupid or something? CC: We're about to find out, Ken. Anyway, that's all the time we have for this special post-Kickoff edition of Straight Talk, folks. For my broadcast partner, Ken Wilson, this is Chuck Carlson saying see ya next week for MORE Straight Talk!
