Straight Talk (11/8)

11 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera fades in on the AEWA Studios, the AEWA logo falls onto the screen, and the words "STRAIGHT TALK" slide in underneath it. The whole logo then tilts, and falls off the bottom of the screen. Behind the desk in studio are Chuck Carlson and Ken Wilson.) CC: Hello again fans, and welcome to yet another edition of AEWA Straight Talk. I'm your host, Chuck Carlson, and at my side, as always is my co-host, Ken Wilson. KW: Yo. CC: Yo? That's all you have to say? KW: Yup. CC: Okay, I just can't believe that you have nothing to say. Not that that's a bad thing of course, but you're just usually so talkative. KW: Uh-huh. CC: This is too weird. Let's just get started, and to kick things off, here's an interview with three members of the Church-- Cardinal Chaos, Mister Crowley, and the Holy Ghost. ---------- (The camera fades in on a familiar site, Cardinal Chaos' Church. The Cardinal is seated behind the altar, to his right is Mister Crowley, and to his left is the Holy Ghost.) Chaos: (looking to the sky) Angels of Death??? Angels of Death??? Why has thou forsaken me? I have asked for the world and you have given the excrement of the earth. I have asked for a challenge and you have given a disturbance. I have asked for a federation and you have given a group of children. Hast thou no mercy? Ghost: Fear not, my father. The light of the Lord shines deep within you. Whatsoever happens to the least of my brothers shall happen tenfold unto me. As you once preached to the fold, when the everlasting day of atonement is upon us, we shall reign in the holy water of eternal life. Crowley: (slightly confused) Chaos, I don't understand what the Ghost is trying to say, but I promise you this-- when Heaven and Hell walk into that ring, when we look across that ring and see those belts, when the bell rings and the action starts, then and only then will you feel the power of the darkness. That night we shall once again hold the AEWA gold. Many a night one of us walked out of the arena a champion, but this night will be different. This night we shall walk out of the building hand in hand. We have unified to devastate, and by all that I hold sacred, I swear WE SHALL RULE THE AEWA! Chaos: Thank you, Brother Crowley. As always, you are correct. The time for talking is almost over. Our reign as the newest AEWA tag team champions is about to start. Brother Ghost, do you have any words of advice for the current champions? Ghost: (making the sign of the cross) May the lord have mercy on their souls. (A loud knocking can be heard from the front of the Church. The sounds of the front door creaking open fill the interior of the building. Seconds later, Curate Kill enters the main hall.) Kill: Master, the tombstones you ordered have arrived. There seems to be a problem though. Chaos: What problem could there possibly be? Kill: The names are spelled incorrectly. Chaos: How are they spelled? Kill: A-N-G-E-L-S O-F D-E-T-H. Shall I send them back? Chaos: No, Kill. They will work fine. Begin to dig the graves. Once Mister Crowley and I are in possession of the belts, we will bring you the bodies. Crowley: (laughing) Rest in peace.... (The camera fades to black.) ---------- KW: Cool. CC: Cool? Could you possibly analyze this a bit deeper? KW: Nope. CC: Are you gonna be like this the whole show? KW: Maybe. CC: Don't you have anything to say about the Church's intent to bury the Angels of Death? KW: (thinks for a second) Nah. CC: Okay, whatever. Now let's get a word from Justin Trouble. ---------- (The camera opens to Justin Trouble on the set of "Renegade". Standing next to him is AEWA broadcaster, Hank Thompson.) Thompson: Hello fans, I'm Hank Thompson, on the set of "Renegade" with AEWA wrestler, Justin Trouble! So how's it going, Justin? Trouble: It's never been better, Hank.... Thompson: So tell us, how did you get this guest spot on the USA network's "Renegade"? Trouble: Well, it's simple. Lorenzo (Lamas) is an old friend of mine, and he gave me a call and told me there's a part on this week's episode that would be perfect for me.... Thompson: And that part is? Trouble: I play a vigilante who goes around getting the bad guy, and sometimes goes a little too far. Lorenzo's character, Reno Raines is sent to arrest my character in exchange for a big bounty; however Reno decides that my character is on the right side of the law and decides to help him. Thompson: Any fight scenes? Trouble: Fight scenes? (Lorenzo walks by) Yeah, I kick Lorenzo's ass in a couple of scenes. (laughs) You know, keep him in line. (Lorenzo looks at Trouble and slaps him on the back good heartedly and laughs along). Thompson: Well we can see you're having a great time here, but you saw Saturday Night Slam. What are your thoughts? Trouble: Saturday Night Slam was one of the greatest I've ever seen. Great matches with great wrestlers. However a couple of people took it a little too far. Thompson: Which wrestlers are you referring to? Trouble: (looks at the camera) First off; Twisted Steel. I've got no beef with you.. But what you did to your ex-wife was despicable. When "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt and I agree on something, you know it must be bad. And what you did to Vicki was a disgrace. I don't care what your problem was with her, a 425-pound man does not brutalize a 120 pound woman. That's pure cowardice, man. Pure, sheer, cowardice. Thompson: I agree, Justin. But there's something I have to ask you. Where has YOUR valet, Tracy been? Trouble: Tracy was a great manager. And the deal is, Tracy got a better job offer. An offer she could not refuse to let go of. Tracy is now a big time business executive for a certain wrestling organization. Thompson: Do you care to mention which one? Trouble: No, it's not of importance. But what is of importance was the epic battle between Mike Stewart and Heavy Metal. I just hope Mike Stewart's career isn't over. He was one of the greats. As for Heavy Metal.... All I can say is at least he's not the champion. And I've got a challenge for the ICU's leader, Deathwatch. Deathwatch, you talk a good game, but you know what? Actions speak louder than words. So why don't you face me in a match at Saturday Night Slam? And as for you, Heavy Metal, I still haven't heard a response to my challenge to face you at the next pay-per-view. Thompson: We have a few moments left, so could I get your thoughts on the vacated title? Trouble: Here it is, quick and simple. It'd be great if I got into the World title battle royal, and won the AEWA championship, but, it's not my main priority right now. My main priority is to see that the AEWA title stays in the AEWA, not in the hands of the ICU. Thompson: There you have it, folks! (They shake hands, and the camera fades to black.) ---------- KW: Interesting. CC: WILL YOU STOP THAT! KW: What? CC: You know what. KW: Huh? CC: You're stupid little one word answers! It's driving me nuts! KW: Sorry. CC: You did it again! Say something! KW: Why? CC: (visibly upset) BECAUSE THAT'S WHAT YOU'RE PAID FOR!! Now fans, while I try to bring Ken out of this coma, why don't you watch this interview with Sir Gallade. ---------- (The scene opens with Sir Gallade pumping iron in a gym in Great Britain) Sir Gallade: Whew! Oh yeah, I am getting prepared for the big event this weekend. I am adding a few quality pounds of muscle so it will be harder for anyone to throw me out of the Battle Royal. I am looking forward to this Battle Royal, because everyone fends for themselves and the man who wins will be a true champion. I believe that that man will be me-- Sir Gallade! I have been waiting for a chance like this, and now it has come. So look out all, because this weekend, it will be medeival times all over again. (Fade to black.) ---------- (Just upon returning to the studio, we see Chuck Carlson slapping Ken Wilson upside the head) KW: Ouch! (Takes a moment to regain his senses.) CC: Do you have anything to say yet? KW: (Ken shakes loose the cobwebs, looks around for a second, then pulls his T-shirt up over his head) I AM CORNHOLIO! CC: Uh-oh, now we're in trouble. KW: Are you threatening me? You will face the wrath of the almighty bunghole! CC: Now I wish I hadn't made him say anything. Just watch this interview with the manager of the Grim Reaper, Ric Verrano. ---------- (We see Ric Verrano in his office, wearing a nice tuxedo. He scowls at the camera, and begins to speak.) Ric Verrano: Looking over the participants of the Battle Royal for the championship, I see that there can only be one conclusion-- the last piece to our puzzle, the AEWA Heavyweight Championship will be placed! One of my men will undoubtedly win, and the reign of terror brought down upon the AEWA will be fought across this very sport. It will be far more shocking than some Brian Pillman threatening to shoot Steve Austin.... The corpses will be piled high. The Grim Reaper will send off their souls one by one and take the gold back to the Crypt of the Angels of Death. (fade to black) ---------- KW: Do you have TP? Perhaps some Crapachino? (Chuck Carlson SMACKS Wilson across the face so hard that it leaves a mark. Ken shakes it off, and pulls his shirt down off his head) WHAT WAS THAT FOR??? CC: For impersonating Beavis, badly I might add, instead of doing your job. KW: I've got NO IDEA what you're talking about. CC: Don't you remember? You were doing that stupid little "Cornholio" routine. KW: I was NOT! Are you on something, Chuck? I mean, hitting your broadcast partner for no reason, accusing him of things he didn't do.... They have self-help groups for that kinda thing, you know. CC: I'M not the problem, YOU'RE the.... Oh, forget about it, just comment on the interviews from now on, okay? KW: I've been doing that the whole show, Chuck. Are you sure you don't need any help? The Betty Ford clinic's right down the road... CC: I'm just fine thank you, although YOU drive me crazy sometimes. Now let's get a word with the Sewerdog. ---------- (Sewerdog is reading the AEWA Report, as a big smile crosses his face. He speaks as he reads....) Sewerdog: Look at this, the heavyweight title is vacant. Oh, and look at this as well; Heavy Metal is gonna be in the same ring with me and 24 other fighters. I think it's time to party. Metal, you're gonna be the first person outta there, whether I do it or everyone helps me. Losers gotta go first, that's when we find out who the big boys really are. And trust me, you and all your ICU boys are going to go down. And don't forget, me and my mystery man are waiting, whenever you're ready. I'm sure someone's going to be buried, after all, my partner would have to be a super guy. (Camera fades as Sewerdog sits there laughing.) ---------- KW: And Sewerdog could very well come out of there with the gold around his waist too. CC: Anyone could, these battle royals are SO unpredictable, it's really anyone's game. KW: And nobody's gonna go out without a fight. All you gotta do is toss the other guys over the top rope and you could be the champ. You don't get many chances like this one. CC: You sure don't. This battle royal is bound to be something special. And now let's hear from Randall Osbourne. ---------- (The camera opens on Randall Osbourne, outside a biker bar in Kansas City. He begins to speak.) Osbourne: There is a bit of disappointment that I am not in the Battle Royal for the WORLD TITLE, but I'd rather have a chance to strike at something the Angels of Death hold onto, the TV title. Deranged is a powerful foe but when he turned on his fans, he lost the thing that can play a greater role in a match than strength or skill. It was not a wise mistake. The Knights of Deliverance have come on in a strong way. I'm here for the TV title and the Caretaker will easily defeat the Church's Big Ron Raider for the North American Title. In the utmost confidence I know that either Gryphon, Gallade or Ben Slocum will win the World Title. It's time to hit overdrive, 'cuz it's a long way to the top if you want to rock and roll. (fade to black) ---------- KW: Why's this guy getting a TV Title shot? CC: Because he's a great wrestler, and a former North American Champion, that's why. KW: Well that's not good enough for me. CC: Nothing's good enough for you, except cheating and brutal sneak attacks. KW: That's right, title shots should be awarded solely on that basis. CC: The sad thing is that many times, they are. And now let's hear from Primal Fear. ---------- (The camera opens on Primal Fear in the AEWA Studios. He smiles at the camera, and begins to speak.) Primal Fear: Everyone said that Primal Fear was mellow and that I didn't have what it takes to hurt people anymore, well just ask the Crazy Canuck how mellow I am! Canuck, you should know by now, you don't MESS with me! I show NO remorse whatsoever for what I have done, because frankly I just don't give a damn! Canuck, you don't want none of this! (fade to black) ---------- KW: Oh please, in a technical wrestling contest, the Canuck would wipe the mat with this trained gorilla. CC: Are you sure you want to be calling him a gorilla? KW: Why? Is he here? CC: No. KW: Then yeah, he's a gorilla. But if he ever shows up here, I didn't say that, you did. CC: Whatever. And now I believe Graphic Violence has something they'd like to say, so let's roll it. ---------- (We see Booze Murphy and Leech Rogers in the parking lot of the Oakland Coliseum. It's nighttime, and they're holding flashlights up to cars as they walk. Leech appears to be carrying a can of gasoline.) Booze: Yo, it's over here somewhere! Leech: Yeah, we'll show 'em not to mess with us! Booze: Yeah, Simon O'Neal is gonna get what he deserves! I've been waiting to do this for a LONG time! (Booze and Leech finally stop next to a red BMW, which belongs to Simon O'Neal.) Booze: Here it is! Leech: Well, say good-bye to your nice car, Simon! (Leech Rogers lifts up the can of gasoline, and begins to spray the car with it. Then, from a distance, he throws his lit cigarette onto it-- and the car BURSTS into flames. Leech and Booze run back out of the way of the flames, and turn to the camera.) Leech: Well Simon, you cheated us out of the National titles on Wednesday Night Rumble, and this is the PAYBACK. Was it worth it? Booze: HA HA HA! (As the car continues to burn, we slowly fade to black.) ---------- KW: COOL! They just set a car on fire! FIRE! FIRE! FIRE! CC: Not this again. KW: I'm just messin' with ya. CC: I hope so, but I can't believe what we've just seen. Not only did Graphic Violence just DESTROY Simon O'Neal's car, but they broadcast it on TV! They're not very good criminals. KW: Why not? They got away with it, didn't they? CC: Perhaps initially, but now that everyone has SEEN them do it on national television, who do you think the cops are gonna come looking for? KW: Your mother? CC: Very funny. And now let's get some comments from El Aviadore. ---------- (The camera opens on El Aviadore in an AAA ring. The crowd is quietly awaiting his announcement) Aviadore: Fans, here in Mexico, I have some news for you-- it could be good, it could be bad, but it is BIG news. As you know, I have been kinda touring the AEWA and wrestled a few matches against their lightweights, even getting two title shots which were screwed up by some chump. I have been talking to the AEWA and we have reached an agreement; I have signed a long term contract with them, and I begin wrestling there FULL time. (El Aviadore starts walking out, and as he is leaving, the fans break loose and begin hugging him and saying their good-byes. Despite his mask, we can still see that Aviadore is teary-eyed. He waves to the fans, and slowly walks back to the locker room.) ---------- KW: Oh goodie, another illegal immigrant to join the AEWA full time. Don't I feel special. CC: You should. El Aviadore is a fantastic aerialist, and any federation should be PROUD to have him. KW: Not if I ran a fed. CC: That's why you don't run a fed. KW: I'd be a GREAT fed president. CC: (sarcastically) Sure you would, and I'd be a great Miss America. Let's just watch these comments from the Twilight Strangler. ---------- (We see Miss DeMeanor and the Twilight Strangler on a plane. They both look completely overwhelmed.) DeMeanor: WOW! I just got done watching what Primal Fear did to the Crazy Canuck and I am shocked; I don't think the AEWA should put up with that. I mean, a man beating another man up with a bat, well that's just plain sick! Strangler: Primal Fear, I don't care about you, you don't have any gold. Gryphon, you may have beat me, but you aren't anything special, I just like kicking ass! DeMeanor: AEWA, this is what I am offering-- an OPEN contract to face my Twilight Strangler. Who's got the guts? (fade to black) ---------- KW: No one, Miss D! No one's got the guts to take on the Strangler. CC: I think you'll find quite a few wrestlers in the AEWA to disagree with you on that statement. KW: And I think you're a moron. CC: GREAT comeback. KW: Thank you. CC: And now let's get a word with Jose Gomez. ---------- (The camera opens on "Ultimate" Jose Gomez, sitting alone in the Church. His long hair is tied back into a ponytail, and he's wearing dark black shades. He lowers the shades, and begins to speak softly.) Gomez: Last week, the entire world was shocked when I joined forces with Mean Massimo. Folks, that was the best decision of my life. Now that Massimo has joined the Church, we are the MOST DOMINANT force in this entire league! NO ONE can stop us! Now there's only one problem-- American Ambush is still alive. That guy has been a thorn in my side for TOO long now, and Massimo's going to finally finish him off.... I can hardly wait. (Gomez turns away from the camera, and disappears into the darkness. Fade to black.) ---------- KW: WOW! Massimo's an official member of the Church now? CC: I guess so, Gomez said it. KW: Woah, American Ambush is REALLY in trouble now, he's gonna have the entire Church after him before long. CC: That's very likely, and that's one place you DON'T wanna be. KW: That's the understatement of the year. CC: And now let's get a word with the Assassins. ---------- (Banshee and Shockwave are standing on a street corner in front of a building identified by a sign as a Karate Studio. Inside, a class can be seen practicing.) Banshee: Lately, a few rumors have been going around about us. Many people are saying that we have lost our edge and wrestling abilities. Well, we want to tell everyone that we haven't! A few cheap losses were handed to us, and Shockwave and I decided to take a little time off and work out a little. Ever since we left the AEWA a few months ago, we have wanted a return grudge match against the team that beat us for the titles, the Graveyard Shift. Shockwave: Now, don't get us wrong, we respect and honor them as a tag team, but we feel that we need to fight them again to regain our confidence in the ring. This is the team that almost caused the doom of the Assassins, and we just want to prove to them and ourselves that we can still fight, and are still contenders for the AEWA World Tag Team Titles. Banshee: Heaven and Hell! You two are next! The way you got the title shots was very disrespectful. It doesn't matter if you beat the West Coast Connection or not, we WILL go after you for your acts manipulating the AEWA front offices. (Banshee and Shockwave bow to the camera, and walk off screen. The camera slowly zooms in the Karate Studio's window, focusing in on the students fighting each other.) ---------- KW: HA! The Assassins want a shot at Chaos and Crowley? They're gonna get KILLED! CC: I don't think so.... KW: It doesn't matter what you think, because my word is the law, remember? CC: (sarcastically) How could I forget. KW: It's quite alright, just don't let it happen again. CC: Whatever. Now let's get a word with a newcomer to the AEWA. This guy calls himself the Masked Yorel. ---------- (The camera shows a shot of a person practicing in the ring. He then turns, and comes over to the camera) Yorel: People of the AEWA, you most likely do not know who I am, but you will soon find out what I am about! I hope that you're not too scared now that I, The Masked Yorel, am now in AEWA. All you no good, fan favorite wrestlers, don't think for a minute that you have a chance at beating me. And for the dirty heels, I may have the same reasons for not liking the "good" guys, but do not think that you're safe from me either! I come from a superb wrestling family and I have been Champion in other federations, which not only proves that I am one of the biggest guys to step into the ring, I am also a hard worker. It's not gonna be long before you see some Gold around The Masked Yorel's waist, and when that gold gets there, it's never, EVER coming off. Once again, AEWA, beware of the force that is The Masked Yorel! (fade to black) ---------- KW: I'm not quite sure what to think of this guy. He looks like kind of a weirdo. CC: Yes, but he looks strangely familiar as well. The guy claims he can wrestle with the best of them though, and we'll certainly find out this weekend when he gets in on the AEWA World Title battle royal. KW: Yep. That battle royal's gonna separate the contenders from the pretenders, and soon enough, we'll find out which one this guy is. CC: And now let's get a word with our new TV Champion, Deranged. ---------- (Deranged sits in the AEWA locker room, alone with his TV Title. He seems very happy and for some reason he has changed. He has a smile on his face and a towel over his shoulder. He is drinking water from his water bottle then he begins to speak.) Deranged: Huh? Oh, hey guys. You like this gold here? For the third time, I am your TV Champion! Now let me make a BIG announcement-- I am no longer part of any stable! I'm turning my back on the Angels Of Death. I am for the fans 100% and this belt is what changed all of that! I am here for this purpose and to let everybody suffer at the hands of Deranged! (Deranged turns on the TV, and watches the promo for his upcoming bout with Randall Osbourne.) Deranged: Well Randall, you want to be next punk? Well you are going to fall! I have done some things in the past that I have paid for. Now I am ready for the big leap! With the fans on my side once more, Randall, you do not stand a chance! I am a legend in the AEWA and I will beat you like no one has beaten you before! (Deranged sets his water bottle down and stands up. He wipes his forehead and puts on the title.) Deranged: Randall, you do not deserve to lace up my boots! You are not going to be able to take this belt away from me. It is life and it is for the people! You are now going to fall before me as I pin you 1-2-3 for the win! The Glow is a devastating move and you better keep that in mind. (Deranged is getting dressed and is about to head out the door.) Deranged: Randall, you are facing a new man with a title. This belt is going nowhere! Whether you like it or not, I am the AEWA TV Champ! That is how it is going to stay! (Camera fades to black.) ---------- KW: WHAT THE HELL DID HE JUST SAY??? CC: That he's quitting the Angels of Death!!! KW: How can that be? He just joined them like three weeks ago or something! CC: I don't know. This is indeed a VERY unusual development. Just a few weeks ago he COMPLETELY berated the fans and basically told them that they didn't mean crap to him, and now, just like that, he wants them back? Sorry pal, it doesn't work that way. KW: You're damn right it doesn't! What are the Angels gonna say to this? CC: All I know is, they're not gonna be happy, but I don't think that the fans will be so eager to take him back either. Deranged might just be a man without ANY friends. KW: And that's a BAD place to be. CC: It sure is. Now let's hear from our Light Heavyweight Champion of the World, Rick Showboat. ---------- (The camera begins to roll as we see Rick Showboat's locker room. Showboat prevails again, not without a couple of bumps and bruises, but the good ol' chiropractor will take care of that once I get back. And now we are packing up. Hurt, you are a worthless commentator who's opinion nobody cares for. The only good commentators are the ones who don't talk. Well, except for Jerry Lawler and good ol' Bobby Heenan, two of the greatest men in the world. They can commentate my matches any day!! (The Lovely Bertha comes into the room.) Bertha: Hey, what about me? Showboat: Oh and of course you. Next time Little Daddy says anything to you, you come tell me and I will style all over the ring with his face. Bertha: Okay, here's a kiss for the win. I have more, but we can't do that in front of the cameras or the kids couldn't watch anymore. (Bertha kisses him then leaves. Love gets up and signals at Showboat to get up. They both do, and the movers take the coach.) Showboat: Yeah Valachi, you beat me at my own game during my first title reign, but I knew you couldn't beat me this time. You see, I am a man of class, dignity, money, and power. A man of the WORLD, so to speak, and I think a man of the WORLD should have a little more than a Light Heavyweight belt. A man of the WORLD needs only one thing, and that's the World HEAVYWEIGHT belt! And that's what I have my eye on now. You see, the Light Heavyweight title is old to me. It's like a woman you just call up every couple of months to do a duty for you. It's always there when you need it, but really serves no purpose at all. Yes, it is very prestigious, but I've done this Light Heavyweight thing already, so I am going for the big boys, and the big boys won't know what hit 'em. I mean, you guys couldn't wrestle if you tried. So watch out, because this show is coming to get ya! (Showboat walks outside the room and someone is taking his name off the door. There are fans outside a near door, so he combs back his hair once more and smiles at them as they are taking pictures.) Showboat: Now I am not saying that I'm forgetting about this here Light Heavyweight belt. It is just not a priority. I will defend it, but I will be scouting the competition, moving up in the rankings, and then, winning the AEWA World Heavyweight Championship!! (Camera fades out.) ---------- KW: Woah! Showboat's moving onto the Heavyweight division, while he STILL holds the Light Heavyweight Title? CC: Apparently so, and I'm not so sure that it's such a good move, ESPECIALLY since he still has that Light Heavyweight Title to defend. I think it's more work than he can handle. KW: I don't think so, I think he'll do great. CC: But you're a moron, so your opinion doesn't matter. KW: Why can you use that, but when I said it about you, you say how I couldn't come up with anything better? CC: Because when I say it, it's true. And now for our final interview, let's hear from American Ambush. ---------- (We see Hank Thompson in the locker room, holding his microphone. He is standing with American Ambush, who looks rather angry.) Thompson: Ambush, I don't want to come off like a smart-ass, but I do have kind of a feeling that your career is headed straight down the toilet. Ambush: Yeah, right; go rub it in. Look, I may have been overconfident when I came back.... thought I had figured everything out. Well, apparently that wasn't the case. Everyone has his ups and downs, and I will definitely be making a major comeback. Thompson: Let's talk about your last match against Jose Gomez-- or rather, what happened after the match ended. Ambush: Let me ask you one question.... If you can't even trust your own tag team partner anymore, who can you trust? Mean Massimo, you made a major mistake when you turned on me. Ambush: I don't care if Massimo has half of the AEWA in his corner. When I get him in the ring, I'm going to kick his butt so bad he'll go swimming back to Milan! Thompson: Don't underestimate this newly-formed terrible threesome. They.... Ambush: Shut the f*ck up! I have a cool surprise for all of you-- an ICY surprise to be exact. You name the time and place, I'll be there! You produce those goons and I'll reveal my surprise. Can you deliver? Thompson: We'll do our best.... They haven't been returning our calls. Ambush: Hold up your side of the deal, that's all I'm asking! I'm outta here. (fade to black) ---------- KW: So Ambush is forming some kind of alliance to help him battle the Church? Don't we have enough stables yet? CC: For once, I agree with you. These alliances are just getting way out of hand, and they only show signs of getting worse. KW: No, that's not what I was talking about. The reason I don't want any more alliances is because I'm never sure which one to root for. They're all so cool! Except for Slocum's alliance, though.
