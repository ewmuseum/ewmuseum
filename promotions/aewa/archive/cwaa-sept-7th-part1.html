CWAA Sept 7th part1

8 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last Time, on Championship Wrestling: Crimson Joe Reed (to Max Robertson): One day you'll learn that an important part of becoming a world champion is overcoming your mistakes. But I've got to be honest, overestimating yourself like this and presuming to dictate to the World Champion and the AEWA are mistakes you're going to find it real tough to overcome. I'll see you in 2 weeks. **************************************************************************** * Eric Lane: Hello, everyone, and welcome back to another exciting week of Championship Wrestling! We’re back in the world-famous Sportatorium in beautiful downtown Dallas, Texas, and if you missed our broadcasts from Reunion Arena, you missed an awful lot. We have Six Man Tag Team champions now, and they are none other than Joshua Barnes, Randy Sheradin and Kore Wrench! The Trailer Park Assassins split the grand prize in the big over-the-top-rope battle royal! We have new- and old- CWAA Tag Team Champions, as Twister, with their old friend Larry Allen, exacted revenge on Byron McNultey and Michael Anderson. And the crowd was thrilled by the spectacle of AEWA World Heavyweight Champion, Crimson Joe Reed, and CWAA Heavyweight Champion, Marc Thomas, battling for over an hour, until Joe Reed emerged the victor with his patented Kentucky Backsmasher. But his celebration was interrupted by Television champion, Max Robertson, who along with his preferred partners in crime, Lawson and Sherrick, accused Reed of having ducked Robertson when HE held the Heavyweight Title- and tonight no one ducks anyone, as Reed and Robertson go one on one for the biggest prize of them all- the AEWA World Heavyweight Champion! On a more somber note, the AEWA and CWAA officials met this week, and the AEWA Board has announced that Joshua Barnes has been suspended for 30 days from today for striking referee Chris Herman. Joshua is a popular young star, someone whom I have always held in the highest regard, and I’m sure he is very remorseful for his actions, but laying hands on a referee just will not be condoned under any circumstances, and I’m sad to say that it will be one month before we see young Joshua back in the squared circle. Tonight we have an action-packed evening- The Maniac, Marco Moura, and eccentric figure indeed, will be in action, as will the duo of Gary Jackson and Eddie Intellecta. Marc Thomas and Jak Hammer were set to face Barnes and Randy Sheradin- we’ll talk about that in a few moments. Elektro and Sgt. Mark Sherrick will meet in one-on-one action, and, as I mentioned, the special feature match pits CWAA TV Champ Max Robertson against AEWA World Champ Crimson Joe Reed. Normally, the CWAA TV Champion is required to defend his title once per week, but Max has received special permission from the AEWA’s Board of Directors to not defend his title this week, so he could prepare for the biggest match of his life. But let’s stop wasting time and head right to the ring for some Championship Wrestling! Dave Myers will give us the introductions for this, our first contest. David Myers: Ladies and gentlemen, all matches tonight are sanctioned by the Championship Wrestling Association of America and the American E-Wrestling Association. Our first bout is set for one fall with a ten-minute time limit. Introducing first, in the corner to my left, from Lafayette, Louisiana, at 245 pounds, here is Dwayne Griffith! (A mild pop greets the man with the Sherman Helmsley haircut. The 6'3"-245 pounder sports a pair of red trunks with his forename inscribed on the part upon which he sits when the time to ponder the poor start to his career) DM: And his opponent- (Faith No More’s “Epic” plays and mild booing follows) DM: -weighing in at 234 pounds from Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, here is 3M- The Maniac, Marco Moura! (The solidly built 5'9"-234 pounder proudly wears his green hair- you read that correctly- and his striped t-shirt and blue jeans. He wears a self-satisfied smile, despite the reception he receives.) DM: Your referee, Jeff Howard. EL: OK- there’s the bell, and we’re underway. An unusual human specimen, this Moura. Apparently fond of lemons. And that hair looks like something Michael Anderson has hanging in his tuxedo closet. OK- Dwayne Griffith locks up with the newcomer from Brazil. A good-looking 20-year-old, despite the hair. Obviously takes pride in the shape he keeps himself in. Hip toss by the Maniac. Griffith back up- kneelift. Moura sends him for the ride- monkey flips him over. Griffith to the outside, and- well, look at this. (Boos as Moura applauds his own virtuosity) EL: Marco Moura now to the ring apron- kicks Griffith in the head. Out on the floor now- rams his head on the ring apron. Risking a double count-out here. OK- rolls him back in, and now Marco Moura follows. Sets him up- gutwrench suplex. Nicely done. Hooks the leg- 1, 2, hey! (Boos as Moura pulls Griffith’s head back up and waves his finger at Howard, saying “No, no, no.”) EL: Well, that was uncalled for! Marco Moura could have- and should have- won this match right there and then, but he wants to punish the man some more. Sends him for the ride- dropkick- nice elevation, and his legs just explode outward- caught him right on the jaw. Moura now stands his man up in the corner- he just slapped him right in the face! Come on! A second time! Now he sends him across the ri- no! (pop) EL: Dwayne Griffith on the reversal- Marco Moura out of the corner- back body drop by the kid from Lafayette! Dwayne Griffith on the offensive- Moura up now- and Dwayne with the big right hand! Now a left- a headbutt! (pop as Moura falls flat on his back) EL: Moura backing into the corner, begging for a timeout- Griffith after him with a kick in the chest! And Jeff Howard now getting between the two and ordering Griffith to make the clean break, to let the Maniac out of the ropes. And Marco Moura is reaching into his tights! Dwayne Griffith trying to get around Jeff Howard- turn around, Jeff, turn around! Marco Moura has some kind of foreign object- brass knuckles? No it looks like a little black chain! He’s up- and a right hand puts Griffith down- he looks like he’s out cold! (Boos) EL: Marco Moura just hit him with that black chain! Covers- 1, 2, 3! He got him! (Boos) EL: Marco Moura gets the nod with the help of a foreign object- let’s get the official word from David Myers. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, Maniac Marco Moura! (Boos, as Moura exits to “Epic”, returning the chain to his trunks and running his fingers through his green hair, winking into the camera with that same grin on his face) EL: Well, Marco Moura is certainly an impressive athlete, but that’s not why he’s won this match. OK- we have to take a quick break now, and when we get back we’ll see Double Jeopardy in the ring and talk with the World Heavyweight Champion! Stay tuned! (Monday nights at 11pm on WYXZ-TV Channel 39, “Three’s Company” as Jack, Janet, and Chrissy and the whole gang live through all their wacky hi-jinx. It’s comedy at its zaniest as Stanley tries to set Jack up with his visiting niece. Find out what happens, Monday night at 11, right here on WYXZ-TV Channel 39!) EL: OK- we are back and ready for more action. In just a few minutes, we’ll see the always-dangerous combination of Eddie Intellecta and “Hotshot” Gary Jackson- Double Jeopardy. But first, we’ll show you some pre-recorded comments from the Lady in Red- manager extraordinary of the Heavyweight Champion of the World! *********************************************************************** (The Lady in Red stands in the locker room with a microphone in hand and the AEWA World Title over her shoulder.) Lady in Red: Joe's getting ready for his title defense against Max Robertson. You know, confidence is a good thing. In this sport, a person has to think that he- or she- is capable of being the best in the sport, or they'll never be able to win. But this is ridiculous. Max Robertson, a man who only has been in this sport for six months, is challenging Joe Reed? "Crimson" Joe has been in this sport for thirteen years. Thirteen Years. Max, do you think your six months of experience is enough to even consider challenging the World's Champion? (Shakes her head) Your poor, pathetic, conceited little man. We've studied the films, Max. Despite your size, you're pretty good. You know a lot of technical wrestling. And someday, maybe five or six years down the road, you might be ready to take on the champ. But doing so now is a foolish mistake on your part. You made a challenge that you can't back up. You need a lesson in humility... and "Crimson" Joe will be your teacher. ************************************************************************** EL: Wow! I cannot wait for that one tonight, Joe Reed and Max Robertson, one-on-one. The Lady in Red says Max does not have the experience to beat the champ, and Reed cautioned Max against overestimating himself. We’ll see who’s right in our special feature match, but first, we have tag team action. Let’s go down to David Myers. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is set for one fall with a fifteen-minute time limit. Introducing first, to my right, at a combined weight of 490 pounds, here are Ed Farmer and Curtis Hansen! (Mild pop for the duo. Hansen is the bigger of the two, and his doughy body is topped by a rapidly balding head. He boasts the pastiest and thinnest arms in the CWAA today. Farmer has sandy blonde chest hair pouring over the top of his black singlet (which has one shoulder strap).) DM: And their opponents- (Boos as “Sweet Home Alabama” plays and Intellecta and Jackson arrive) DM: -at a combined weight of 500 pounds even, here are Eddie Intellecta and “Hotshot” Gary Jackson- Double Jeopardy! Your referee, Chris Herman. (Jackson and Intellecta are both 250 pounds; Jackson, at 6'7", stands about half a foot taller. Intellecta’s yellow trunks and “AEWA Rocks” t-shirt are accessorized by the trademark blue mask, and Jackson’s fashion preferences run in the schlitz-spotted undershirt and oil-stained jeans area, but different strokes for different folks.) EL: OK, there’s the bell, and- look out! (Intellecta and Jackson charge their foes head on; Intellecta with a boot in Farmer’s mid-section and Jackson with a double sledge for Hansen) EL: Double Jeopardy very aggressive at the outset, here, going to work on Ed Farmer and Curtis Hansen- and Intellecta just tossed Farmer out over the top rope! Hansen for the ride- Jackson with a knee to the mid-section and Intellecta with a bulldog as Hansen is doubled-over! Rolls him over- Jackson off the ropes-elbow drop! Covers- (As Intellecta punches Farmer off he apron back to the floor) EL: 1, 2, 3! They got it! Almost record time! Here’s David Myers for the official word. DM: Here are your winners, Eddie Intellecta, Gary Jacks- (Jackson grabs the microphone) Gary Jackson: That’s “Hotshot” Gary Jackson, to you, bucko! (The eloquently made point is punctuated with a forefinger jab in the chest) DM: (rubbing his chest where he was jabbed) -and “Hotshot” Gary Jackson- Double Jeopardy! (Jackson shouts something which sounds vaguely like “Yee-hah!”) EL: OK- we’ll be right back in just a few minutes with more championship wrestling!
