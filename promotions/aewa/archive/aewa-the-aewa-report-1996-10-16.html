The AEWA Report (10/16)

19 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
--------------------- ************** AEWA REPORT --------------------- ************** Welcome to the first official edition of the AEWA Report! We have plenty of big news to tell you about, so read on.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ RECENT NEWS ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ --THE GRAVE DWELLER ATTACKS REFEREE! In a shocking display of violence, the Grave Dweller attacked a referee during Saturday Night Slam. Along with his partner, the Mortician, he then challenged the West Coast Connection to a rematch next week. We are still awaiting word from the championship committee on whether this proposed match has been signed or not. Of course, the Graveyard Shift will obviously be fined for the blatant act of "referee beating", and we hope to bring you more news as it develops. --RED DOG JOINS THE ANGELS OF DEATH! On the latest episode of Saturday Night Slam, Red Dog shocked the world by announcing his membership with the "Angels Of Death". Red Dog, whose career has been slipping since losing the "dog pound match" to Sky Walker, said this was the best decision of his life. When we called Ricky Roberts to confirm that Red Dog had officially joined, Roberts simply said "of course it's true" and hung up. One thing is for sure-- the "Angels Of Death" should be taken seriously. --HEAVEN & HELL WILL GET THEIR SHOT! Cardinal Chaos and Mister Crowley, the formidable duo now known simply as "Heaven & Hell", have officially been recognized as the number one contenders to the World tag team titles. They will get their shot next week, with Curate Kill as the special guest referee. --WILD THING IS READY! After spending almost a week in a Tokyo hospital, Wild Thing has finally arrived back in the United States. His plane flew into Los Angeles early this morning, and we immediately noticed that his arm was in a cast. Will Wild Thing be able to wrestle Heavy Metal as advertised? We'll find out soon.... ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ INJURY REPORT ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ CURATE KILL suffered a broken rib at the recent Saturday Night Slam show, after the Phantom executed his feared "Chandelier Kneedrop". The impact was great enough to send Curate Kill to the hospital for the night, but he should be back in action very soon. RICHARD PILLSBURY, one of the AEWA's top referees, was rushed to the hospital after he was power bombed by the Grave Dweller at Saturday Night Slam. He had fallen unconscious after the attack, but he was released from the hospital after a quick examination. Pillsbury plans to return to his refereeing duties as soon as possible. WILD THING suffered a broken arm after he was attacked by Heavy Metal at Mega Combat. He also apparently had some spinal injuries, mostly due to the fact that Metal power bombed him on the concrete. We are still rather uninformed regarding Wild Thing's condition, but he plans to return to action at the next card. ******************* CHAMPION LIST******************** AEWA World Heavyweight Champion-- "Superman" Mike Stewart AEWA North American Champion-- The Grim Reaper AEWA Television Champion-- Super Blonde AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion-- Rick Showboat AEWA World Tag Team Champions-- The West Coast Connection AEWA National Tag Team Champions-- The Machines *******************THE TOP 10 (SINGLES)**************** 1.
