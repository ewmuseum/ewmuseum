The Zack Derringer Show (3/12, part four)

15 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * CONTINUED FROM PART THREE * * * * (Derringer gets up out from behind the desk and walks over to another studio; he shuts the door and then the camera switches to a view of inside the studio. Derringer stands with a microphone in-hand in front of a small studio audience. A downpour of boos come from the audience as they throw small pieces of wadded-up paper at him as "King Nothing" by Metallica BLASTS over the PA. Derringer stands in a pure black set that has another microphone laying on the ground. Derringer begins to speak.) Zack Derringer: Ah, how I love to absorb that hatred. Listen up, morons, I have a VERY special guest this week. He's been the EWA World Champion among other federation's titles, but right now, he's the PCW World Heavyweight Champion, and as he would say.... he's "Tha Baddest Thang Runnin'"! Here he is, from Portland, Oregon.... GUNNAR.... THE GRIZZLY.... GAINES! ("Bad to the Bone" by George Thorogood and the Destroyers plays. Gunnar "The Grizzly" Gaines walks out, holding a bottle of wood grain alcohol. He clutches his hands together, then shakes them in the air to one side, then the other. He sits on a stool.) Gunnar: Hey, Zack.... care for a little shot of the wood grain? Zack Derringer: Heh, you ain't foolin' me with that stuff man. I see what it does to those non-drinking losers that think they're bad asses. Speaking of which, how 'bout that Alex Rio moron who thought he could handle the shit? I hear he even had the GALL to challenge you to a match after Christian Kingsworth pussied out of the event that was supposed to take place at PCW's Live at the Grapplearium and vacated the title to ya. (Gunnar takes a gulp out of his jug.) Gunnar: Well, Zack, I don't call it gall. I call it stupid. You see, we all just learned why Kingsworth won a lot of matches, and Rio has lost to people like Otto Verhoeven and the Ultimate Maniac. It's because Kingsworth is smarter than Rio. That's why Kingsworth pussied out, and Rio didn't. What is going to happen to Rio shouldn't happen to a dog .... but it SHOULD happen to an idiot like Rio, and danged if I'm not going to enjoy it. Zack Derringer: I agree, the thing about most wrestlers today is that they're all STUPID f*ckers. Now what about T.R. Ela? This guy's a f*cking joke from what I hear, so why the hell are you even taking him on? So what if he's a charter member of PCW, I don't think that shit is deserving of a title shot. What's YOUR opinion on Ela? Gunnar: Title shot? Who said anything about a title shot? I'm just going to _wrestle_ Ela. He hasn't done shit lately, so I agree that he doesn't deserve a title shot. If he can even look good against me, then that could change. But look, you're being a little bit harsh on old Donut Boy there. Sure, he's fat. Sure, he's stupid. And sure, he needs books on audio to read someone their rights. But the thing about T.R. Ela is.... Zack Derringer: He has redeeming qualities? Gunnar: Right. The thing about Donut Boy is.... he works hard. Or rather, he USED TO. It's just that since he came around here to Portland, Oregon, the competition has gotten a lot tougher, and he hasn't. No.... Ela's gotten WEAKER. He's cowered in the corner as bigger, stronger men have eaten his lunch and shoved his Sesame Street lunch pail down his bloated throat. You want to know why I'd fight someone like that? I'm fighting him because he opened his mouth. That's why. Gunnar Gaines will NOT back down from anyone, and least of all from some doofus with a badge so cheap looking, that Kellogg's wouldn't stick it in a cereal box. Zack Derringer: Okay, I get it. Now, recently the Extremist appeared to join the Devil's Advocates.... a group that doesn't appear to exist anymore, but a group that attacked you with a branding iron. Gunnar: Well, I thought that was most interesting. You had the Extremist going around claiming he was a neutral party.... then you had the Dark Destroyer, the very same day, claiming that the Extremist was his pal, and a member of the Devil's Advocates. Since then.... the Dark Destroyer has been silent, and we get word that he's in a car wreck or something, with a spinal injury. So you know what that means? Zack Derringer: That he's a completely disloyal and untrustworthy sack of shit? I honestly don't know. Gunnar: It means that the Ex-f*cking-tremist stabbed me in the back all for nothing. That's what it means. You know something, Extremist? I was watching all your matches in the back, every night, the past several cards. I was sitting there with a beer in one hand, and a baseball bat in the other. Zack Derringer: I was close. So, what was the baseball bat for? Gunnar: What was I going to do with it? I'll tell you what. I was going to go out there and beat the living crap out of anyone not man enough to take on the Extremist one on one. That's what I was gonna do. See, I had a back-watching agreement with the man. I watch his, he watches mine. Well, no more. Extremist.... you're on your own. You know, pal, I remember when we FOUGHT the Dark Destroyer and his pals, side by side. You OWE something to Casper? I think you'd better EXPLAIN _that_ one. I'm listening, and if I were you, I'd start talking. If I like your answer, cool. If not.... (Gunnar smirks.) Zack Derringer: Wow, that's the thing about people these days though, you can't trust damn near anyone. Hey, did you know that The Extremist has even been wrestling a little bit here in AEWA? Gunnar: No, I didn't. Maybe he's trying to be the next Sabin Figaro and see how many places he can wrestle at once. Maybe he's just that dominant. That's his business. Being the PCW World Champion, I have enough to deal with defending THAT belt, to worry about what he's doing in AEWA. Zack Derringer: Just felt I should bring it up. Now, I have a question that I just HAVE to ask ya. I want to know what you think of Heavy Metal. Probably one of _the_ most dominant wrestlers in the AEWA. He held the AEWA World title longer than anyone else in the history of this federation, he's up for the title again later this month in a four-way dance, and he's the only competitor, besides The Extremist, that's originally from PCW that is in AEWA right now. Your thoughts? Gunnar: You just told me more than I knew about him. I do remember his fight with the Mafia last fall. I don't think he could have taken the Mafia then, but perhaps he could take Extremist now, if he were to work really hard. Zack Derringer: What is your opinion of AEWA? It's been around over a year and doesn't appear to get the publicity of an organization such as EWA or PCW. Do you think it's a decent federation, or is it just another fed among the rest in your eyes? Gunnar: I don't worry much about the other federations.... there's enough going on in the PCW to worry about. I didn't get to be the Baddest Thang Running by trying to join 40 federations like Sabin Figaro. Hell, no. I always stuck to one place and beat the shit out of everyone there. Zack Derringer: Well, I'd like to thank you for coming out here tonight, it's been a real honor to me and I hope maybe we can do it again some time. But one thing before you go.... can you do the Grizzly Grin? I've always loved that. (smiles) Gunnar: Sure.... and while you're at it, are you going to tell me to roll over, or sit up and beg? No f'n way, son. No way, no how. Now, I KNOW why you like the Grizzly Grin. It's because it's an expression of humanistic emotion, pathos, and comedy, not to mention a bit of irony.... (He glares at Zack....) But, son ... The Grizzly is NOT here to entertain a twerp like you, he's here to beat UP twerps like you, UNLESS they wisen up and treat the Baddest Thang Running with the PROPER amount of respect. You want to see a Grizzly Grin, then watch me after I just beat the shit out of Alex Rio, T.R. Ela, or any of the other piss stains on a newborn's diaper that are walking around the PCW calling themselves wrestlers. Then, you'll see the man no one wants to wrestle just a grinning as wide as can be. Thanks for having me on the show, Zachary! (Gunnar gets up and leaves.) Zack Derringer: Man, what a guy won't do for his fans. What a jerk, but man.... that guy kicks ass, don't you morons agree? (The fans boo) ... Bah, shows how much you pathetic morons know. He'd fit right in with ICU, but unfortunately, as he said, he's a one-company kinda person. Maybe someday. Till then, later, losers.
