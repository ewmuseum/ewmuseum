Event Center (10/6)

19 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see Hank Thompson and Skim Hoss in the studio. They are both wearing "Mega Combat" T-shirts.) Thompson: Hello everyone, and welcome to the Event Center! We are just HOURS away from Mega Combat.... Hoss: We have a few interviews to show you, so this should be pretty interesting. Thompson: Up first, we have an interview with some members from the "Church Of The Pounding Light". These guys are ALWAYS unpredictable. Hoss: Let's watch.... ------------- (The camera opens on a familiar scene. Curate Kill and the Holy Ghost are standing guard by a locked door in the church vestibule. The sounds of fighting can be heard from beyond the door) Curate Kill: This can not go on much longer. When will the master see fit to stop this madness? Holy Ghost: This is really strange, how the heck are they making it to the arenas to wrestle? I haven't seen them open this door yet. Hey Jose, come here a minute. ("Ultimate" Jose Gomez enters the room) Gomez: What's up, Ghost? Holy Ghost: You've been watching the door while we can't be here. Have you seen them come out at all? They must have, they were at the arena the other night. Gomez: I did exactly like Curate Kill told me. I sat here by the door, and NOBODY opened it. I mean NOBODY. Curate Kill: Brothers, do not question the power of the master. A wooden door is a physical object which holds no meaning to an immortal. We were told to guard the door to make sure no one entered. We failed with Primal Fear and let him enter. For this, we will be punished. We failed our master once. I shall make sure that we DO NOT fail him again. Gomez: What's gonna happen to Primal Fear? How come we get punished and he gets nothing? Curate Kill: Primal Fear shall receive the worst punishment of all. The master will make sure that he receives exactly what he deserves. (The sounds of fighting cease. Footsteps can be heard coming towards the door....) Holy Ghost: This is it. Looks like the time for talking is over. Gomez: I'm outta here. This is freaky. (he bolts out the front entrance) (The footsteps get closer. Suddenly, the door is ripped from it's hinges and flies across the vestibule. Standing in the open doorway is....) Curate Kill: Oh my god! (The camera fades to black) ----------- Thompson: Jeez, we didn't even get to see what happened. That seemed pretty strange, Hoss. Hoss: Up next, we have an interview with the West Coast Connection. Considering what happened on the recent "Kevin Larkin Show", this should be VERY interesting. Thompson: Let's watch.... ----------- (Fade to two men standing in front of Studio K, just after the "Kevin Larkin Show". They seem to be in good spirits, and are laughing and talking to a few people they are standing near.) MC Read: Ha! Did you people out there see what we just did? We finally got the monkeys off our backs! We are so glad to have them away from us, and now our first match without them, and we can prove to those punks that we can win without them. Axl Sharkman: Dude, man, you're turning into a mushy 123-Kid-like "I can't believe it" kinda guy. Return to normality. We are the best and meanest team here. They know that we just got rid of our managers. The only thing they don't know is what we have planned for Skull & Crossbones during Mega Combat. No matter what the fans think, we will always be the best, and we will continue to be the meanest punks we can be! (fade to black) --------------- Thompson: What do they have planned for Skull & Crossbones at Mega Combat? Hoss: I don't know, but I can't wait to see it! Thompson: Up next, let's watch this interview with the World Heavyweight champion-- Heavy Metal. -------------- (Heavy Metal comes walking out of the locker rooms with his belt draped over his shoulder, and is just walking straight to the interview area. He gets to the stage and walks up to the announcer, he then RIPS the microphone out of his hand and begins to speak) Heavy Metal: WILD THING! You are in for one HELL of a beating, because tonight it is a knock down, drag out match! You say yer not feelin' any pain anymore? We'll see about that when this match is over, you are going to be in more pain than any KNIFE will EVER do to you! I don't make threats, I make promises! And I PROMISE to all of this crowd here tonight that I will KICK....YOUR....ASS! (The crowd pops as Heavy Metal throws the microphone down in front of the announcer. Heavy Metal raises his arm and yells "ROCK ON!" to the crowd.) ------------- Hoss: He looked REALLY angry, buddy. I would hate to be in Wild Thing's shoes right now. Thompson: So would I. Anyway, we're running out of time, so let's wrap this show up. (turns to the camera) Folks, call your local cable company NOW and order "Mega Combat: The Rising Sun"! Hoss: See ya there!
