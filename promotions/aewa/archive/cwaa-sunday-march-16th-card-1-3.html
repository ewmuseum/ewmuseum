Sunday, March 16th, Card (1/3)

Last Week on Championship Wrestling Association of America ******************************************************************* EL: Now Lawson hooks him in a bow-and-arrow submission hold, and rolls back- surfboard! Sheradin straining to get free, but Lawson is one of the best in the CWAA at applying his holds properly. MVM: And we know who taught him about that... EL: Well- did you teach him to keep his own shoulders off of the mat? 1... 2... he has to release the surfboard. Lawson with the Irish whip- shoulderblock. Now he sets for an atomic kneedrop- Sheradin slips out and lands behind Lawson- rolls him back... 1... 2... kickout! Sheradin back up- savate kick- now he bounces off the ropes- cross body press- Lawson rolls him over... 1... 2... kickout. Sheradin now with a small package- Lawson reverses... 1... 2... kickout! Sheradin with an elbowsmash in the face- now he sets for a German suplex- he nailed it! Both men's shoulders are down, though... 1... 2... 3! That's it! ******************************************************************** Eric Lane: Hello, everyone, and welcome to another week of CWAA action. We have a full slate for you this weekend. A return match between Healer's Havoc and Mark Sherrick will kick things off, and we all saw last week how Sherrick used the West Coast Connection to help him in his plan to get rid of Havoc and Big Dog Brown. Another return match will show "The Preacher" Kore Wrench do battle with Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson, who came a hair away from making the finals of the CWAA Title Tournament. Twister looks for revenge against the duo we first saw last week, "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey and "Marvelous" Michael Anderson. The we have two very important matches: Joey Haven, coming off of that steel cage match last week and the attack by Marc Thomas, meets Thomas for the right to go for the AEWA Light Heavyweight Title here next week. Now that title is held by "Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi, and we'll hear from him later this evening. Then, in our feature bout, Max Robertson and Randy Sheradin get in the ring together, this time both knowing where the other stands, to determine the first CWAA Heavyweight Champion. First, we have to take a break, and, when we get back, we'll look at that video Mark Sherrick brought to us last week. (This Monday Night at 10:30, only on WYXZ TV Channel 39, is Howard taking the Fonz to court? Find out on "Happy Days". Then, at 11:00, Linda Lavin is a waitress trying to start her life over again in "Alice." "Happy Days" and "Alice", Mondays at 10:30, here on WYXZ TV 39!) EL: OK- we're back. You may remember last week that Mark Sherrick came out here and demanded that we play a video which purportedly shows the in-ring death of his father, Kenny Sherrick, right here in the Sportatorium. We're going to take a look now at the incident which Mark Sherrick says made him decide to look for revenge against all modern wrestlers. ************************************************************** (An amateur 8 mm film with badly-faded color rolls (without sound). Then when he falls down, smashing him in the face a few times. Sherrick is bleeding from the nose and mouth. The camera switches to the young man in the crowd, and he is crying and yelling, presumably for help. The video switches once again, to the waiting room of a hospital, where the young Mark Sherrick and his family appear to be hearing bad news from a doctor.) ***************************************************************** EL: I am joined now by Mark Sherrick, who- MS: Now do you believe me?? How's that, huh?? That man has been proven by the CWAA historians to be my father. So much for the theory that I was making this up to scare people huh?? Whaddya think of that??!? I promised my father I would exact my revenge, and I will, starting with Healers Havoc and BD Brown!!(looks at the ceiling) I promised you father, that I would exact revenge for your death. They already have to use briefcases to stop me from getting into the ring to kick some (expletive deleted). I can't tell what else they will do, but I promise you that I will destroy all of them, and become the champion. This interview is over!! (MS hops into the ring for our first match- what a happy coincidence!) EL: Let's go to our ring announcer, David Myers. David Myers: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall, with a 15-minute time limit. In the ring to my left, from Dallas, Texas, at 275 pounds, Mark Sherrick! (The crowd boos) DM: And his opponent, accompanied by Tip Smith, at 260 pounds, from Tucson, Arizona, Healer's Havoc! (Big pop for the toothless wonder) DM: Your referee for this contest, Jeff Howard. EL: OK- Mark Sherrick and Healer's Havoc, and these men have crossed paths before. Just last week- and there's the bell, and Sherrick meets the charging Havoc with a kick in the midsection- just last week, Mark Sherrick called Havoc and his partner, BD Brown, out, and they were jumped by the West Coast Connection. OK, Mark Sherrick takes Healer's Havoc into the corner and slams his head on the top turnbuckle. Referee Jeff Howard warns Sherrick about pulling the hair. Now Mark Sherrick rubs Havoc's face across that top rope, and rope flips Havoc onto his back in mid ring. Now Sherrick having words with Tip Smith, and if I were Smith I would keep that briefcase handy. Now Sherrick turns his attention back to the ring and stomps away at Healer's Havoc. It was just two weeks ago that these two men fought to a double countout in the quarterfinals of the CWAA Heavyweight Title Tournament, and we'll see the finals tonight. Mark Sherrick now with a clothesline, and so far he has had his way with Healer's Havoc. A cover, a lazy cover, 1... kickout. Now Mark Sherrick stands Havoc back up- full nelson. Like I was saying, tonight's feature match will be the final match in the title tournament, as either Max Robertson or Randy Sheradin will walk out of here tonight as the first-ever CWAA Heavyweight Champion. Two youngsters trying to make their mark in the world of wrestling, and what better way to do it than by wearing gold. OK- Mark Sherrick has to release the hold, as Healer's Havoc got his foot on the second rope. Jeff Howard counting to 5- and Sherrick drops Havoc. Now he kicks the man until he tumbles to the floor under that bottom rope. (Big heel pop as Mark Sherrick stands alone in the ring with his hands raised) EL: And Jeff Howard starts his count. Tip Smith helping Healer's Havoc back to his feet. Very slow start for the Real Deal tonight, as Mark Sherrick has been in command since the opening bell. OK- Havoc coming back in, and this match will continue, and we'll be back, right after this message. (Catch the news every weeknight at 6:00 from the award-winning WYXZ TV News Team. Anchorman Mark Powell brings you the news, Dave Clark with sports, and weather-bunny Tai West brings a little sunshine into all of our lives, evenings at 6:00, here on WYXZ TV 39.) EL: We're back in the ring with Healer's Havoc and Mark Sherrick. Collar and elbow tie up, and Havoc shoves Sherrick into the ropes. Mark Sherrick is not please at that- they lock up again, and Sherrick slips him into a side headlock. Jeff Howard checks to make sure he doesn't drop his arm beneath the chin to start choking Havoc. Sherrick is not what you would call "sculpted", but he has definite power in those arms, and he grinds, applying more pressure. Jeff Howard checks for a submission, but Havoc fights it off. He grinds a little more, and- Havoc lifts him off his feet! Atomic kneedrop! (Big pop) EL: You can see the look on Sherrick's face as pain shoots right up his spine. Now Havoc with a forearm in the back- Irish whip- powerslam! The cover... 1... 2... Sherrick kicks out! Havoc up again, hooks him- snap suplex! Havoc covers again... 1... 2... kickout. Havoc whips him into the corner, now he follows him in- Stinger splash! And Sherrick drops to the mat- Havoc rolls him over- he signals for the Scorpion deathlock... (Crowd noise starts to pick up) EL: He's got it! He hooks it on! Jeff Howard checking for the submission, and Healer's Havoc leans back. Perspiration pouring down Sherrick's face as he fights off the pain. He's very close to the ropes- if he can reach them, he'll get out of this hold. Jeff Howard checks again for the submission, but Sherrick hangs on. Sherrick now reaches with his right hand- and he grabs the bottom rope. Jeff Howard now tapping Havoc's shoulder. Havoc releases the hold, and- well, he thinks he's won the match, I guess as he stands there with his hands raised. Jeff Howard trying to tell him that he just had to break the hold- it was not a submission. Sherrick up now- and he clotheslines Havoc in the back! Now he picks up Healer's Havoc- Irish whip... spinebuster by Mark Sherrick- he hooks the leg... 1... 2... 3! He got him! ("Beautiful People" by Marilyn Manson plays as the crowd boos) DM: Here is your winner, Mark Sherrick! (The boos grow louder) EL Mark Sherrick, your winn- hey! There's no call for this! Mark Sherrick just tossed Healer's Havoc out over the top rope, and now he's backing Tip Smith into the corner. And here comes the Big Dog! (Big, big pop) EL: Big Dog Brown in there now, and he spins Sherrick around and plants a big right hand right on the kisser, and Mark Sherrick runs for the safety of the locker room. But, despite what you see in the ring, it is Mark Sherrick who gets the duke over Healer's Havoc. Well, we are just a few weeks away from AEWA Extreme, and on that pay per view spectacular, you'll see a falls-count-anywhere-in-the-arena bout between Joey Haven and Jak Hammer in Haven's hometown of Philadelphia, PA. Let's take a quick look at the events leading up to this encounter. **************************** (CWAA File Footage: 01-25-97) EL: Hammer follows him now, and this one is going to be battled out on the floor, Uh-oh! Hammer's got himself a chair, but our referee'll have none of this. Haven is still trying to collect himself on the floor, Hammer is standing about 20 feet away with a folding chair in his hand, and he's glaring at the referee, who is giving him a 5-count to drop the chair or risk the DQ. 1..2...the crowd counts along...4...Hammer tosses the chair down again. DDT, right on the floor! The crowd is stunned. Now Hammer is hurrying back to the ring to beat the 10-count! And there's the bell! I don't know if he beat it in or not; we've either got a double count-out or a victory for Jak Hammer here. OK, I see the referee raising Hammer's hand. Jak Hammer defeats Hardcore Joey- wait a second! ("Bad to the Bone" plays again) EL: Haven is back on the apron, chair in hand, and his hair is matted with blood, adhering to his forehead.
