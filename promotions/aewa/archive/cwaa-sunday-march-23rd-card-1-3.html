Sunday, March 23rd, Card (1/3)

23 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last Week on Championship Wrestling Association of America EL: the Major calls to Robertson, who abandons his heckling of Joshua Barnes and stands Randy Sheradin up- double chickenwing submission! Robertson leans back to pull Sheradin off the floor- we've seen Randy in this hold before, and he couldn't shake free. Referee Jeff Howard checking for the submission, as Sheradin kicks his legs- he keeps kicking- he reaches the mat and yanks his whole body forward- Robertson is carried forward by the momentum- his head hits the middle turnbuckle! (pop) EL: The impact breaks the hold as Robertson staggers back! Sheradin on his feet, sweat pouring down his face- savate kicks flattens the groggy Professional! Sheradin climbing to the top rope- and Lt. Lawson up on the apron- Barnes pulls him back down and slams a big meat hook right hand in the mush, and Lawson is laid out with one blow! Randy Sheradin off the top rope- the Dream Come True! (i.e., Shooting Star Press) EL: The cover- hooks the legs- 1... 2... 3! He got him! He got it! ***************************************************************** Eric Lane: Hello, everyone, and welcome to the world-famous Sportatorium. Yes, we have our first-ever CWAA Heavyweight Champion in "The Dreamer" Randy Sheradin, but the situation between he and Max Robertson did not end last week. We'll take a look at some footage from a match between Sheradin and his partner Joshua Barnes against Lt. Lawson and Max Robertson that took place last night as the FWA celebrated it's one-year anniversary, and the CWAA was there. Also tonight, we'll bring you first round action in the CWAA Tag Team Title tournament, AEWA World Light Heavyweight Champion is here to defend his title against our own Marc Thomas, and we'll have an announcement which could send bring the CWAA to London, England. Before that, let's talk to two of our first-round entrants, and here they come now! (It's Cooter Lee Roy and Orville Ray Bob Tucker, aka "Orbit", collectively known as the Trailer Park Assassins. It takes them a while to get to the announcer's table, as they have to shake hands with every fan in the place, but eventually they get there, and join David in the ring.) DM: Hey guys, good to have you here. (Cooter and Orbit shake hands with David, apparently a little hard, because David is wringing his hand afterward.) Cooter: How're you doing, David? (Orbit waves to the camera) DM: I'll be fine in a minute. (wrings his hand again) So, I understand congratulations are in order. Cooter: Someone's getting married? (He looks suspiciously at Orbit, who emphatically shakes his head no.) DM: No, you guys. On winning the New Frontier Wrestling World Tag Team Titles. How do you guys like it there? Cooter: Oh, we love it. The wrestling's good, the fans are great, and we just love the phrase "New Frontier." It reminds Orbit of Star Trek. (To David) Watch this. Space...the final frontier...these are the voyages of the Starship Enterprise... (Orbit concentrates really hard, then holds up his hand, with his fingers spread in the Vulcan "live long and prosper" position. The crowd applauds as Orbit smiles, obviously very proud of himself.) Cooter: We also learned something else. (Gestures to David to lean in close, then shouts right in his ear.) BELTS ARE COOL! Winning titles was a lot of fun, so we're looking for more. That's why we're here in the CWAA tonight, to beat...who was it, Arn Anderson and Matthew McCaugnahey? DM: (Chuckles) Close enough. Cooter: My thoughts exactly. We're gonna give those boys a good old fashioned Weasel whupping. Orbit, tell 'em how it is. (Orbit stands up, as the crowd starts chanting "Orbit! Orbit!" He looks at the crowd...smiles, and spits, as the crowd erupts. The crowd, obviously, doesn't have to clean the place up. Orbit's unhygenic trademark completed, the TPA's head back to the locker room, pausing to shake the hands of every fan in the place again.) EL: OK- before we get to the tag team title tournament, we want to show some footage from last night's encounter, courtesy of the FWA. Those unfamiliar voice you'll hear belongs to Kip Thomas, whom I joined at the announcing table. A little background: Max Robertson and Lt. Lawson squared off against Randy Sheradin and Joshua Barnes, and it was clear from the beginning that Max wanted no part of Joshua Barnes, avoiding tags when Joshua was in the ring, even when his own partner was in trouble. Now Max has come out here before and told Joshua that he was more or less not worthy of his attention, that Randy Sheradin was the one he was really after. And last night, when Randy Sheradin did get in the ring, only then was Max Robertson willing to tag in. Eventually, Joshua Barnes did his hands on the man who played him like a violin, and we pick up the closing minutes from there. ************************************************************************** FWA File Footage, 03/22/97 KT: Lawson back in the ring now- double sledge on Barnes. The referee in now to get Lawson out of there, and turnabout is fair play as Sheradin takes advantage of the opportunity to lay out Max with a big savate kick! Barnes covers... 1... 2... kickout! Barnes and Robertson in the ring- Irish whip by Barnes- spinebuster slam! EL: That's his finisher! KT: Cover... 1... 2... Lawson breaks it with an elbow drop! Lawson now with a kick in the groin, and Barnes doubles over. As the referee gets Lawson out of there, Barnes tags out. Robertson and Sheradin in there now. Sheradin with a backslide... 1... 2... kickout. Both men up now, and Sheradin with a dropkick! Now he goes up top- EL: His finisher, the "Dream Comes True", is a shooting star press, and he's had terrific success with it so far. KT: There he goes- and Robertson pulls up his knees- Sheradin just somersaulted right across Robertson's bony knees! And now Sheradin down on the mat- Robertson with a half nelson, rolls him...1 ... 2... 3! That's it! *************************************************************** EL: Max Robertson pinned CWAA Heavyweight Champion Randy Sheradin last night, and in two short weeks, they'll meet again, and this time, the title will be on the line! Let's go to ring announcer David Myers for the introductions for our first match. (ZZ Top's "Sharp Dressed Man" begins to play, and the crowd starts booing well before either McNultey or Anderson show their faces.) DM: Ladies and gentleman, this is a first-round match in the CWAA Tag Team Tournament, set for one-fall with a 15-minute time limit. On their way to the ring, at a combined weight of 497 lb., here are "Marvelous" Michael Anderson and "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey. Eric Lane: Well, it looks like the "Pretty Boy" and the "Marvelous" one, Byron McNultey and Michael Anderson, are more interested in the television camera than the ring right now, as here they come. Gentleman, can I help you? MA: How are you this week Lord Alfred- EL: Er- MA: Whatever? Well, it looks like we have a first round bye in this tournament for the tag team titles. BM: I'd say. Our first opponents have a combined IQ that is smaller than the number of hairs left on Mr. Solie's pretty little head here. What are their names? Billy Bob and Cooter? Wasn't he a character on the Dukes of Hazzard? Talk about a show that could have used a "Pretty Boy" and a "Marvelous" one. MA: It seems we have about a minute left before the match starts, so I think we should go into the ring to give the ladies what they came here to see. So, if you will excuse me Dok. EL: Eric. (Michael proceeds to enter the ring to strut for the ladies present in the audience.) BM: Details, details. Well, while "Marvelous" Mikey gives the ladies something to dream about, I want to point out one little detail for you. Since we have joined this little league of yours, the Nielsons have increased dramatically. And, that can only be due to one thing, or actually two. "Marvelous" Mikey and myself. Now, for all those beer guzzling, overweight, drooling Texans watching this show, don't hate us just because we're beautiful. (Byron joins Michael in the ring, strutting for the female members of tonight's crowd.) (As "Sharp Dressed Man" resumes playing, Anderson and McNultey strut about in a manner reminiscent of ostriches in the mating season.) EL: We'll be back to introduce their opponents in just a minute- don't touch that dial! (Monday nights at 11:00 PM on WYXZ TV 39, back to back, "Happy Days" and "Alice". This week, Richie says, "Sit on it, bucko!", and hear Flo tell Mel, "Kiss my grits!") EL: OK- back up to David Myers. DM: Their opponents, from Weasel, AK, at a combined weight of 595 lb... ("Freebird" plays, and let's not dwell on the irony a face pop in this arena for this song...) DM: ..., the New Frontier World Tag Team champions, Orville Ray Bob Tucker, Cooter Lee Roy, the Trailer Park Assassins! EL: The tag team title tournament is under way, and McNultey and Anderson are not going to wait for the TPAs to come to them- as Cooter and Orbit come through the ropes, Byron meets Orbit with a kneelift, and Anderson with a double sledge on Cooter. Byron whips Orbit into the corner, open handed chop on the chest, and Anderson has Cooter in the opposite corner, drives a shoulder in the midsection. The Pretty Boy calls to Anderson- and they whip the TPAs toward- not, reversed, and McNultey and Anderson- well, Anderson ducks and McNultey with the leapfrog, and they avoid a collision. But they didn't avoid that- Cooter with a double clothesline, and the Pretty Boy and Marvelous One are sent to the floor! (Big pop) EL: They're talking it over- is Byron McNultey crying?- and they're going to pass on this one, it appears- they're on their way back to the locker area. Senior official Jeff Howard starts his count, and this should bring them back. no one wants to be eliminated by countout just seconds into the first match. It looks like they've reconsidered, and we will have a match after all. Michael Anderson wants referee Jeff Howard to warn Cooter about something, but I didn't see any infraction. Marvelous Michael Anderson in the ring now with Cooter Lee Roy. They circle one another- McNultey slipping through the top 2 ropes- Jeff Howard catches him- collar and elbow tie up, and Anderson uses a handful of hair to gain a leverage advantage on Cooter- but pulls him back into the TPA corner, where Cooter tags Orbit. Anderson has Cooter up against the ropes- cocks a fist- Orbit holds the fist back- leaving Anderson wide open for a pop from Cooter! (And a pop from the crowd) EL: Orbit now with a head lock on Michael Anderson. Anderson gets out by sending Orbit into the ropes- Orbit with a leapfrog- lands behind Anderson- and puts him right back in the headlock! Orbit may not have a whole lot upstairs, but his skills cannot be denied. Now Orbit tags Cooter, and Cooter with a boot to the midsection. Cooter with a headlock takedown now, and referee Jeff Howard. Anderson in pain, but he makes it to his knees, now up top his feet- an elbow in the midsection breaks it. Anderson tags in McNultey- Anderson with an Irish whip- ducks- Cooter leap frogs- and McNultey with a knee in the midsection. McNultey with an Irish whip- and again he goes downstairs with the knee. Now McNultey letting the crowd know what he thinks about this action, with Cooter on the mat. Irish whip again- shoulder in the chest. Now McNultey rakes Cooter's face over the bootlaces, now a stomp on the stomach. McNultey with a snap mare into a reverse chinlock. Jeff Howard in to make sure this doesn't turn into an illegal chokehold. (We have our first-ever "Go, Cooter, Go!" chant) EL: Cooter with an elbow in the midsection- now another, and that'll break the hold. McNultey and Anderson have kept most of the action in their corner, though- and Anderson tags in. Anderson with double sledge, and now a kick as Cooter dropped to all fours. Anderson picks him- backbreaker! And he picks him up- a second one! And now a third! Three successive backbreakers by Michael Anderson on Cooter Lee Roy! He covers... 1... 2... kickout. Now Anderson rolls him over- camel clutch. Cooter in big trouble now, as he can't reach the ropes from here, and those backbreakers have softened his back up- he won't be able to power his way out. And here comes Orbit with a kick in the back to break the hold! (Crowd pops) EL: As senior official Jeff Howard sends Orbit back to his corner, Anderson and McNultey swap places. McNultey with a kick for Cooter, now he takes him over to a neutral corner- smashes his head onto the top turnbuckle. Sets him up- release German suplex. Now McNultey grabs an ankle- spinning toe hold- and Cooter kicks him right back into the corner! Both men down, and Jeff Howard starts a double-count. 4... 5... McNultey back up, and he grabs Cooter in a standing side headlock- Cooter pushes him off- McNultey off the ropes- and they bang heads in mid ring! Both men down again, and I bet they heard that one all the way back in Weasel, Arkansas. A double count starts again... 3... 4... McNultey again the first one up- and he tags in Michael Anderson. Anderson with a double sledge- but Cooter stops him with a shot in the midsection! Anderson with a rake of the eyes, though, and he stands Cooter up in the neutral corner- smashes his head right onto that turnbuckle. Snap mare takes him over- and now he drives a knee right into the small of the back. Cover... 1... 2... Cooter kicks out again. Anderson rolls him over into a chinlock on her mat now- I can see Orbit pacing up and down the ring apron- his partner has been in there a long time. (Again, "Go, Cooter, Go!" And McNultey's admonitions to the crowd only make them chant that much louder.) EL: Cooter powers his way to his knees- now he's got his feet under him- a bodypunch, now another, and that breaks the hold. (Medium-sized pop. They can't all be big.) EL: Now Anderson with an elbowsmash, and a whip into the corner. running clothesline- and Cooter ducks, as Anderson runs straight into the unoccupied corner! He hits the mat, clutching at his right arm. Anderson back up and tags out, but he can't catch Cooter as he makes the tag to Orbit. (OK- this pop is big.) EL: Orbit in there now- big right hand on Anderson- now a kick for the Pretty Boy- an elbow on Anderson- a punch on McNultey- know he whips Anderson into the ropes- and here's Cooter with a clothesline knocking McNultey from the ring. Orbit with a boot in the midsection on Anderson. Orbit and Anderson tangle in mid ring- and Orbit is sent for the ride- Anderson ducks, and Jeff Howard sends Cooter back to his corner- Orbit with a sunset flip, but Cooter and the referee are still arguing- McNultey in there now, and he pulls something out of his tights- (Crack! As he rabbit punches Orbit, quarters shower all over the ring. As McNultey rolls the pinning predicament over, he starts kicking some of the coins under the bottom rope.) EL: OK- Howard turns around, sees Anderson with Orbit pinned- the count- can't he see the coins everywhere?- 1... 2... 3! It's over! DM: Here are your winners, "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey and "Marvelous" Michael Anderson! (Many, many boos as "Sharp Dressed Man" plays again. McNultey hands a handful of quarters to Orbit and says "Here, you dropped something." Anderson follows with "Keep the change.") EL: Well, Byron McNultey and Michael Anderson got away with one there. When we come back from this break, we'll talk to the Heavyweight Champion.
