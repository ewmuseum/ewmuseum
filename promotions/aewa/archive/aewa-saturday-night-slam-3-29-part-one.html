AEWA — Card Archive

Saturday Night Slam (3/29, part one)

1 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * A E W A S A T U R D A Y N I G H T S L A M * * * * Live, from the Arco Arena in Sacramento (We see the AEWA logo on the screen, set on fire. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Rock music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... David Graves TAKING SOME GUY'S HEAD OFF with the Gravestone, Penance PLANTING some guy with the Final Judgment, The West Coast Connection DRIVING some guy's head into the mat with the West Coast Killa, Cardinal Chaos STICKING some poor soul with the Inverted Crucifix, Shane Matthews BRUTALLY hitting the End of the Road on some hapless jobber, and Heavy Metal notching yet ANOTHER casualty courtesy of the Megadeth Power Bomb. The camera then shifts to Matt Wilson, The Lovely Bertha, and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt in the PACKED Arco Arena in Sacramento, the site of AEWA Saturday Night Slam!) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Saturday Night Slam! We have plenty of great matches scheduled for tonight, and in the main event, "Wild Card" Jeff Hawkins will be squaring off against Pestilence! This is going to be an excellent program, that's for sure. BD: Well, I'm really looking forward to that main event bout, because wherever Pestilence goes, the other Apocalypse members are sure to follow. LB: Yeah, and 8-Ball is gonna be watching Jeff's back, so ANYTHING could happen. I hope we've got plenty of security out here tonight. MW: Well, let's get started with our first-- (notices someone coming down to ringside) Wait a minute, it looks like we've got a visitor out here, folks. I'm not sure who it is yet. BD: Oh no.... it's Justin Trouble. What the hell does _HE_ want? He always comes out here and delivers these BORING speeches, and quite frankly, I don't have the stomach for another one. MW: Let's just see what happens.... (Justin Trouble unexpectedly makes his way to the ring with an enormous crowd pop. He has a microphone in one hand, and is wearing his blue and black tights with his hair pulled back in a ponytail. Trouble steps to the center of the ring.) Trouble: How's everybody doing tonight? (the crowd goes crazy) Whoa, whoa, whoa-- that's not loud enough. I can still hear myself think. Let's try this again.... How's everybody doing tonight?! (the fans go absolutely nuts this time) That's better! Now, I came down here for a reason tonight. Well, actually two reasons. First off, I have some things that I have to get off my chest, and secondly, I'm here to talk about my upcoming match with my brother, Maxx. (the fans boo at the mention of Maxx's name) But before I get into that, I'd like to address a few other things. As everyone knows, I was fired by Bob Freeman a couple of months ago. After being fired, my duties were limited to defending the North American title. Unfortunately, I lost the NA title to Deathwatch at Kickoff '97. So right now, as it stands, I've lost the NA title, I'm wrestling, but not getting paid for it. I hope my fans understand that this speech is not about greed, but necessity. Like everyone in this arena, I work damn hard and give 100% every time I step in the ring to make sure YOU the fan gets your money's worth, which in my mind you do. The truth is, like everyone here, I've got bills to pay. The bills are getting higher and higher, and I'm getting deeper and deeper in debt. And to me, it's not only unlawful, but it's unethical for me to perform a service five days a week at house shows and on live televised cards, and not get paid for it. Equal pay for equal work. It's that simple. And if President Freeman isn't willing to cooperate, well then, I'll have to take my business somewhere else. And fans, I want you to know that that's something I really don't want to do. Also fans, if you support my reinstatement into the AEWA, there's a toll free hotline that Chuck Carlson and Hank Thompson so generously set up for me. Just call 1-888-TROUBLE. Simply press "1" to vote yes to reinstate, "2" for no. Now YOU the fan has chance to show President Freeman and the AEWA what YOU the fan wants. Now, I'm going to change the pace of this interview real quick. Later this week, I'm taking on Jimmy "Vegas" Cage. Vegas, you're a newcomer to the AEWA, and I'm sure you'll do fine tonight against Mister Crowley. I take nothing away from you in that respect. But Vegas, on Wednesday, you're wrestling me. Cage, bring everything you've got, because when you wrestle me on Rumble, you're going to "crap-out" in a major way. And one final reminder to my big, bad, brother Maxx. Maxx, you maybe over seven feet tall, and 360 pounds, but Maxx, as we all know, the bigger they are, the harder they fall. And big man, at our big match at AEWA Extreme, you're going to see a whole new side of me that you thought never existed. Trust me. Maxx, I'll see you at Extreme. (Tom Petty's "Makin' Some Noise" blasts over the PA as the crowd explodes. Justin slaps hands with the crowd as he makes his way back to the locker room. The camera shifts back to "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt, the Lovely Bertha, and Matt Wilson.) MW: Well, Justin seems prepared for his upcoming matches, but what do you think about his new hotline? How are you two going to vote? BD: I'll probably call it tonight and vote "no" about fifty times, buddy. I'd HATE to see Justin reinstated in the AEWA. LB: Same here. MW: Well, his fans will probably outnumber you. President Freeman made a big mistake when he fired Justin. BD: I'd love to keep arguing about that idiot, but isn't it time for the first match yet? MW: Our next match is the AEWA debut match for Jimmy "Vegas" Cage, whom Justin just talked about. I've seen him before, and he's a very impressive individual. But I think that he's.... hey, where are you going? BD: I've got to make a phone call.... to my investment broker. Yeah, my broker. (Big Daddy leaves the announcer's booth) MW: Investment broker? LB: He's calling his bookie. MW: Great. Anyway, the newcomer is facing perhaps the greatest wrestler in the history of the AEWA, Mister Crowley. I think Mr. Cage has bitten off more than he can chew. Let's get to the ring! RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, the man from the dark side, weighing in at 328 pounds, he is the four-time former AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World.... Mister Crowleeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeey! (The arena turns black, and bells are heard, as Mister Crowley makes his entrance, to a shower of boos. He is dressed completely in black, as always. He gets to the ring and the lights turn back on.) LB: I wonder if the other members of the Church are going to be getting involved with this match. MW: That's a definite possibility, Bertha. Folks, I think it should be noted-- Crowley is the current ACEW champion. He's been dominating in that federation for several months now. LB: Yeah, and as everyone knows, he's already won the AEWA World Title multiple times. I think that he'll win it again at Extreme! MW: So you think he can win the four-way match? LB: Slocum and Reed are nothing more than annoying pests. Heavy Metal is the real challenge. But Metal-- as great as he is-- can't match Crowley's experience. It's close, but I like Crowley. MW: Interesting analys.... LB: Besides, I'd throw up if anyone associated with Zack Derringer won the title. MW: As always, another poignant observation by the Lovely Bertha. Now, before they introduce the newcomer, we have a special pre-recorded interview to show you. LB: Roll it.... -------------- (Fade into BWI Airport in Maryland. The camera shows a scene of mass hysteria outside Terminal 6. A bunch of reporters are seen huddling around as a private jet is seen flying in, in the background. A couple of minutes later, the door opens and a man walks out, wearing sunglasses, an Armani Suit, and carrying a briefcase at his side. He is approximately six feet tall and looks rather built, with short auburn hair and a "GQ" look to him. A woman comes out of the doorway, standing about 5'7", and she looks to be in very good athletic shape with a tone body and long blonde hair. Her green eyes scan the crowd as the man gets a cocky smirk on his face. The pair makes their way down the steps and into the crowd of reporters.) Reporter #1: Mr. Cage, is it true that you are coming to the American E-Wrestling Association? (Cage turns around and looks at the camera, and gets a devious smile on his face.) Cage: (in a authoritative voice) Alright gentlemen and ladies, I will make this very quick and to the point. Yes, I am here in the AEWA-- after I left that sorry-ass league known as the GEWA. Those bunch of has-been's there bitched and whined the whole time and it took the president to help out a few of his friends. If you want to watch pathetic wrestling at it's best, tune into the GEWA. Now back to the AEWA; I hear that this is the "finest" in wrestling, and it doesn't get any better than this.... (The female cuts Cage off as she puts her finger to his lips.) Sugar: (in a seductive voice) What my man is trying to say is that yes, he is here, and yes, he will be showing people why he is one of the "pinnacle" wrestlers of today. Any more questions? (the reporters stay silent) That's what I thought. (The two make their way to an awaiting limousine. Cage opens the door for Sugar, then gets in himself.) Cage: (looks over at Sugar) Ya know, I really hate the paparazzi. (Sugar starts to rub Cage's hair, trying to relax him.) Sugar: Don't worry, handsome, you are going to do just fine in this league. You have everything going for you-- power, agility, a fantastic wrestling background, and of course, the brains (Cage turns to Sugar and smiles, as he rest his hand on her thigh.) Cage: (gets back his cocky smirk) Well of course, doll, just wait 'till I prove myself in the ring once again. Now would you mind passing me the Vodka and Orange Juice, it's about time that I get liquored up. (fade to black) -------------- MW: Well, he definitely seems confident, that's for sure. I'm interested to see how this young kid does against a veteran like Mister Crowley, though. LB: Personally, I think he's about to get his butt kicked. He doesn't stand a snowball's chance in hell. MW: That remains to be seen, Bertha. Anyway, here he comes! RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, making his AEWA debut, from Las Vegas, Nevada, weighing in at 275 pounds, led by Sugar, here is Jimmy "Vegas" Caaaaaaaaaaage!! (As "Kinder Words" by the Mighty Mighty Bosstones blares over the PA system, the well-built wrestler walks down the aisle. Sugar is by his side. They both look like they belong on a model's runway. The fans are uncertain how to treat the couple, but give polite applause, figuring that anyone is better than Crowley.) LB: A very nice looking guy, this Jimmy Cage. I wonder if he's related to Nicholas Cage. MW: Stop drooling over the wrestlers, Bertha. The bell rings, and Cage and Crowley lock up. Crowley's a much bigger man, but Cage is powerful, and might even have a strength advantage. Cage muscles Crowley over the corner, and the referee orders a clean break. It looks like.... NO! Cage with a hard right to Crowley's chin. LB: I like him already. MW: Cage is sending a series of lefts and rights to Crowley, trying to make this a brawl. He whips Crowley into the other corner.... reversal by Crowley.... as Cage bounces off, Crowley nails him with a belly-to-belly suplex! LB: Crowley now begins methodically working over the youngster. He picks him up, and slams him down with a bodyslam. Now he grabs Cage, and puts on a nerve pinch! Crowley's really locked it in. MW: And those long fingernails are adding even more pressure. I think.... yes, he's even cut into the skin. Jimmy Cage is bleeding from the shoulder. LB: Cage is in pain, and Sugar is yelling at Cage, telling him the that he's right next to the ropes. He grabs them with his legs, and the referee orders a break. Crowley finally lets go on the four count. MW: Crowley picks him up, and whips him into the ropes. A dropkick-- NO! Cage holds onto the ropes, and Crowley doesn't connect. LB: Odd to see Crowley use an aerial move like a dropkick, and it cost him. Now let's see what Cage can do. MW: Cage picks him up into a powerslam, and drives him into the mat. It looks like Cage is going to use a power game, even though Crowley outweighs him by fifty pounds. He lifts him up for a side backbreaker, and drops Crowley across his knee. LB: Cage picks him up again, and lifts him up for a suplex. He's holding him up there for five.... ten.... fifteen seconds! And he finally drops Crowley. I don't think Mister Crowley was expecting such a fight. He's rolling outside the ring. MW: Cage is following him, which is not a good idea. Crowley can pull a lot of tricks out of his hat. LB: What hat? MW: Never mind. Cage has caught up with Crowley, and the two are brawling outside the ring. Cage lifts him up, and powerbombs him onto the floor! Cage throws him back into the ring, and makes a lazy cover.... One.. Kickout! LB: A stupid mistake by Cage. No one, and I mean no one, will ever pin Crowley that easily. MW: Cage picks him up, and throw him into the ropes-- he goes for a spinebuster-- but Crowley catches him with a spinning DDT! Crowley's regained the momentum in the match, and slaps on a crucifix pinning combination! One.. Two.. Kickout! MW: Crowley picks him up again, and plants him with a belly-to-back suplex. Another cover, but Cage kicks out before the one count.... LB: Cage has some endurance-- he'll need it. Crowley picks him up for a gutwrench suplex, and nails it! Now he goes to the top rope, and comes off with a flying elbow.... MW: And nails it! The cover! One.. Two.. Foot on the ropes! MW: Sugar put Jimmy Cage's foot on the ropes, and called the referee's attention to it. The referee stops the count, and Crowley's not happy. He's going out to confront Sugar. LB: Cage's manager is trying to flirt with the former champion, but that won't work with a man like Crowley. MW: What it will do is give Cage a chance to recover. He leaps over the ropes, and nails Crowley with a plancha! Crowley is down on the floor. LB: Cage picks up Crowley, and throws him into the ring. He picks him up.... running powerslam, followed by a spinebuster! He's giving some sort of signal.... and he picks him up for a torture rack! MW: That's his finisher, the Blackjack! But he's too close to the ropes, and both men tumble over the ropes. LB: Cage looks like he was hurt more in the fall. Crowley's already getting up, and he's grabbing a chair.... but Sugar's trying to pull the chair away from Crowley! She doesn't have a prayer against the former champion, and is sent to the ground when Crowley pushes her away. MW: Look at Cage-- he's furious! He's grabbing the ring bell off the table, and hits Crowley with a glancing blow. LB: It's a good thing that Crowley saw that coming, or he could have been hurt. Now he's using that chair as a weapon, and both men are beating each other senseless. MW: The ref's calling for the bell-- but since Cage has the bell, it can't ring! LB: Look, the rest of the Church is heading out towards the ring.... and Sugar is pulling Cage away from the ring. Smart move-- don't bother fighting when you're outnumbered that badly. MW: Cage and Sugar walk away from the ring into the crowd, as the rest of the Church gathers around Crowley. Let's hear the official verdict. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen, the referee has disqualified both men. The match is ruled.... A DRAAAAWWWW!! (Boos by the crowd, who wanted a winner.) MW: An unpleasant decision, but a necessary one. Both men used illegal objects, and this wasn't a street fight, but a wrestling match. Still, Jimmy Cage has made an impressive debut by standing toe to toe with Mister Crowley. (Big Daddy comes back to the table, in a pissed off mood.) MW: What's going on, Big Daddy? BD: (mumbling) Damn Kansas.... can't even get by Arizona. MW: Well, I see that Big Daddy's "investments" aren't going well. BD: Shut up, Wilson. (looks over some notes) The next match is Jimmy "Vegas" Cage against Mr. Crowl.... MW: Sorry, Big Daddy, that match is already over. It's now time for our next bout, as-- ("Eye Of The Tiger" by Survivor suddenly begins to play loudly over the PA system, and the crowd instinctively pops.) BD: What's that damn music playing for? Wait a minute, the crowd seems to be reacting to something in the aisle. It looks like somebody is coming out here.... LB: ANOTHER disturbance? Who the hell is it this time? * * * * TO BE CONTINUED IN PART TWO * * * * \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- "A New Beginning" American E-Wrestling Association The Official E-Wrestling Federation of Wrestle World and TNM The Best E-Wrestling Fed of the Year 1996 in the TNM Year-End Awards Be sure to visit our Event Center at: http://www.california.com/~bullet/index.html
