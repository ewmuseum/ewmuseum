The Zack Derringer Show (4/12, part one)

17 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * T H E Z A C K D E R R I N G E R S H O W * * * * Live, from the ICU Studios in New York City (A screen appears with a black background and red text that reads: "WARNING! The following program has language and material of an adult nature. If you are under 14 years of age, please change the channel now. This is definitely not for your viewing. Parental guidance is advised, but if you don't want to ask, you don't have to.") ("King Nothing" by Metallica begins to play slowly over a black screen, as the guitars and the bass kick in, and then the drums hit and the entire screen BURSTS with a white blast. "International" shows up on the screen in the back, really small, and then it gets bigger and flashes past, as the screen shows Sewerdog driving someone's head into the mat with a DDT. "Contracts" flashes past the screen, and then it shows Damian Steele drilling someone back with The Shining. "Unlimited" flashes past the screen, and it shows Heavy Metal SLAMMING someone down onto the mat with The Megadeth power bomb. Finally, it shows Deathwatch DRIVING someone's head into the mat with the Death's Vengeance. "The Zack Derringer Show" comes up onto the screen, and then drips away like blood, turning red as it goes. The last thing you can hear is Deathwatch's sadistic laughter, as the camera fades in on the ICU Studios in New York City. Zack Derringer and Hank Thompson are sitting behind the desk. Zack has his feet kicked up on the desk and is wearing black jeans and a white shirt. His long, black hair hangs to the sides of his head. Hank is wearing a sharp looking blue suit with a black tie, and he smiles at the camera. Derringer begins to talk.) Derringer: Welcome, all you monkeys and lemurs, to another edition of the Zack Derringer Show. Now, as you all know, this is a special pre-game version of the Zack Derringer Show, so most of these flashes will be directed to the AEWA pay-per-view.... AEWA Extreme! Order it to see ICU kick everyone's ass! Thompson: Yeah, okay, Derringer. The pay-per-view should be a spectacular event, so let's go over some of the main event matches, shall we? Derringer: Bah, well.... I don't see what it could hurt. Let's see here, we have Damian "The Predator" Steele taking on "Big Bad" Leroy Brown-- now this is an easy match to declare a winner for. Brown sucks and Steele kicks ass, so it's a no-brainer. Thompson: I happen to think Brown stands a very good chance against Steele. In any event, it should be a great match-up. Derringer: Yeah, well, I still think Steele's gonna whoop him. Let's show the first flash of the evening now, by going straight to a person in the AEWA who isn't in ICU and is actually good, if that isn't an oxymoron. Thompson: Roll it.... ------------ (The scene opens in a biker bar, somewhere on the outskirts of Philadelphia. The camera pans across the bar, which looks to be filled with the scum of the earth, some of the roughest, meanest looking dudes that you'll ever see in your life. As the view reaches the bar itself, the camera stops on the back of a man wearing a black leather jacket. While it's not unusual to see someone wearing this particular article of clothing in a joint like this, the lettering on the back of the jacket it what catches your eye. Two simple words adorn it, in blood red lettering. The words? SUDDEN DEATH. The man then whirls around on his stool to face the camera, confirming his identity. It is indeed the AEWA's own "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews. He wears black sunglasses, black jeans, a white Shane Matthews T-shirt, and steel toed leather boots. He holds a bottle of Molson Ice in one hand, and has a evil grin on his face that looks to be about a mile wide. He begins to speak.) Matthews: So you guys found me, huh? I can't go anywhere without SOMEBODY with a camera trailing me, anymore. That's okay, though. It just means more camera time for me, and you KNOW I've never been one to shy away from a mic. I guess you're wondering what I'm doing here. I know I usually hang at the more "in" clubs-- God knows I'm not gonna find any women here, at least not any remotely attractive ones-- but I like to stop by places like this every now and again. This particular dump is my favorite. There's no better place to get plastered and beat the <BLEEP> out of anybody who gets in your way than good old "Lenny's" here. You'll notice that Johnny's not with me today. There's good reason for that. If he was here, he'd get his ass kicked. He knows that from experience. You don't last very long in a place like this when you're a hundred and ten pounds and throw a punch like a girl. (Matthews stops and takes a sip from his beer.) So anyway, I should probably get around to discussing the AEWA. That's why you're here, right? So what should I talk about, so many things I have to say. Let's start with my last match. My last match was a couple of weeks ago, I think you all remember it. That MORON, Randall Osbourne, actually had the STUPIDITY, to challenge ME, Shane Matthews, former Heavyweight Champion of the World, and Sir Gallade, the current TV Champion, to a HANDICAP match! Can you believe that, a handicap match, with us two? HELLO, are you mental? I don't know WHAT you were thinking when you signed for that match, son, but me and Gallade sure did have one hell of a good time whooping your ass. (Shane takes a LONG chug on his beer.) Yeah, people say we had the advantage, and we still had to cheat to win. But that's not the way I see it at all. If the ref is dumb enough not to notice it, why SHOULDN'T we cheat? The only thing that means <BLEEP> in this sport we call wrestling is that notch in the win column. You think there's some asterisk next to the "W" on my record that says "Used a foreign object"? Hell no, there's not, and that's why I do what I do. I use whatever I can to give myself the edge over my opponent, because anything that I can get away with is perfectly legal in the eyes of the wrestling world. That's just the way it is. Deal with it. The fact remains, Osbourne got a beating, which is exactly what he deserved. The man just doesn't know when to give up. The day he beats me is the day that I roll over and die, and that ain't happening anytime soon. So Osbourne, if you wanna continue to pester me, I'll beat you again.... and again, and again, but I think you should try and save a LITTLE face, and just give up now. Persisting in this hopeless feud is just gonna drag your career further and further into the toilet, and you're already in deep water as it is. (Shane finishes his beer, and calls for another, which is quickly handed to him by the bartender.) As for me, Shane Matthews is looking forward to bigger and better things, like AEWA Extreme. Or at least I WAS looking forward to it, 'till I found out I wasn't even ON the damn thing. What the hell is that? How can you have an event, call it AEWA "EXTREME", and NOT have Sudden Death on the card? Shane Matthews was the ORIGINATOR of extreme in this sorry excuse for a wrestling federation, and this is how you treat me? By not inviting me to a pay-per-view that has MY name written all over it? Shane Matthews is the MOST extreme wrestler in the game today, bar none. Primal Fear, Stan Crawford, the Extremist-- hell, even Tommy Dreamer or the Sandman, ALL of them pale in comparison to Sudden Death. Well lemme tell you something President Freeman, Commissioner Gehringer, VP Furnish, and any of the other pitiful excuses for officials we got in this damn fed, Shane Matthews WILL be at AEWA Extreme, whether you want me there or not. I've never missed a pay-per-view since I joined up here, and I'm sure as hell not gonna start with THIS one. You won't know when, and you won't know where, but I'll be there, and trust me, if I get my hands on somebody, I'll SHOW ya the meaning of the word "Extreme", and it ain't gonna be pretty. You may be able keep me from wrestling, but you can't keep me out of the building, and as long as I'm there, I'm gonna be wreaking havoc on whoever I want, whenever I want. You can't stop me. You can only hope to catch me.... before it's too late. (Matthews twirls back around on his stool to face the bar, and resumes his regular drinking. Before long, a big biker with a long brown beard, and wearing a black leather hat, and a black leather Harley Davidson jacket, comes up and taps Shane on the shoulder. Shane turns back around.) Biker: I know you. You're that 'rasslin fella, "Sudden Death" somethin' or other, ain't ya? Matthews: Gee, what was it that clued you in, Einstein-- the jacket with my name on it, the T-shirt with my picture on it, or the twenty guy camera crew following me around? Biker: Don't get smart with me. You're the guy that's been ripping off Randall Osbourne in all his matches lately, right? Matthews: Yeah, so? What about the tub of lard? Biker: Now you listen here, Mr. Death, or whatever the heck your name is, Randall Osbourne is one of the greatest 'rasslers in the HISTORY of the AEWA, and he does us bikers proud. Matthews: Really? I ride a Harley, and I really don't give a <BLEEP> about him. Why is it that the simple minded are so easily attracted to blubbering idiots like him? Biker: You no good punk. Why I outta.... (The man winds up his arm as if to deliver a punch) Matthews: I wouldn't do that if I were you. Biker: And why not? Matthews: Because your shoe's untied. (The biker looks down for half a second, but that's all the time Shane needs to grab his beer bottle, and SMASH it over the head of his distracted assailant. Blood immediately begins to stream from the man's forehead, but he still stands. Shane is quick to take care of that, as he picks up a wooden chair, and SHATTERS it over the back of his much bigger foe. The man drops with a THUD to the floor, and before long, the whole bar has turned into one massive brawl. Shane punches a guy, then looks back at the camera.) Matthews: I love this place, now THIS is extreme! (Shane goes right back to the brawl, and looks to be having the time of his life. In the midst of the brawl, someone is hurled towards the cameraman, knocking him over and shattering the lens. The image turns to static.) ----------- Derringer: Always nice to see someone start a barroom brawl. Those are so cool to watch. Thompson: Matthews says he'll be at Extreme, so we'll just have to wait and see what develops. Derringer: Boy, you're just Mr. Optimistic today, aren't ya? Thompson: Well.... I.... uh.... Derringer: Alright, that's enough, just shut the hell up. Up next we have a flash from this Cage guy, who seems decent enough. Let's watch. ------------- (The camera opens on Jimmy Cage's West Palm Beach house, and we get a panoramic view of his private beach as the sun starts to peak over the horizon of the ocean. The sun rays glimmer off of the ocean, cascading a reflection for as a far as the human eye can see. The camera then starts to descend to the back patio of Cage's house, where Cage and Sugar can be seen. Cage is dressed in white sweats and nothing else, his hair is all messed up and his hand clenches a bottle of Smirnoff Vodka. Sugar is seen picking up some left over trash from last night's party.) Cage: (looking over at Sugar) Doll, you don't need to do that, just wait for Juanita or Maria or whoever our maid is. (Sugar throws the rest of the empty beer bottles away then takes a seat and relaxes.) Sugar: You okay, honey? (Cage takes a small swig of his Vodka and puts it down.) Cage: Yeah.... I'll be okay. It's time for me to stop drinking, 'cause I got to go to the gym and work my bod eventually today (He stands up and throws the bottle into the trash can, and stretches out. Sugar pats him on the back.) Sugar: How is your back doing? Cage: It's getting better, I just can't believe that bastard Justin Trouble had to sneak up behind me to beat me. What a chump. In my book, he isn't a real man. Justin, son, you gotta problem on your hands now. I know I am a rookie and all of that, but I have a reputation around the big leagues. And I always get revenge! (Cage motions to go inside, and Sugar rises to her feet. They both head into the luxurious house, and enter into the living room. It's decorated with a Hawaiian motif.) Sugar: (smiles and kisses Cage on the cheek) I am going to go take a shower, I hope you will join me in a little while (Sugar walks off, as Cage heads over to the refrigerator.) Cage: (looking into the fridge, and pulling out some orange juice) Well, I think I proved myself by taking on the freak show Mister Crowley. That guys was disgusting! I proved my point alright though, I broke his dammed nose. AEWA, I am soon going to be on top of this league, but for now, Justin, you better watch yourself at AEWA Extreme. I am going to tell you right now, I wasn't too happy on the way you beat me the other day. And you will pay, son. (Cage finishes his orange juice and walks out of the camera's view.) ----------- Derringer: Eh, maybe not so good. At least he has his priorities straight, wanting to take out Crowley, that is. Listen up Cage, to be at the top of this league you'll have to get through ICU, and the chances of that happening? Not very good. Thompson: Like always, we'll have to see what develops. Derringer: Like hell we will. Let's just roll this next flash from the West Coast Connection. Yippie. ------------ (The camera switches to The West Coast Connection, sitting on a couch in what appears to be a recreation room of an arena somewhere. They are flanked by their bodyguard Hood Lum, who has a broad smile on his face. MC and Axl are staring intently at the television, which is showing the latest AEWA show. They view the camera.) MC Read: Hey, get over here! Yeah, you. Get over here now! We got some words for two people, namely Paul Wong, and Simon O'Neal! We ain't done with you punks yet. You might think that this war is over, but you have what we want, our AEWA World Tag title belts. Don't be surprised to see us show up at the pay-per-view to make sure you retain those titles, just so we can have the pleasure of taking them back from you! Axl Sharkman: Jeez, MC, calm down. You are right, though. There isn't anything more that I want than to rid the Machines of their title belts. We got one thing to say. We will defeat you for the titles, and we will defeat you soon. You don't know what we have cooked up for you two punks just yet. You will find out pretty soon. Hey, why don't the two of our teams get together on Saturday, in the ring, you pick if you want to defend the titles. What do ya say, punks? Hood Lum: Machines, I suggest you watch out. Things aren't always what they seem. (Hood Lum grins again, and winks at the camera, as it fades to black.) ----------- Thompson: They seem pretty determined to get those titles back. Derringer: Screw them. The next World Tag Team Champions? The Hustlers, now THAT is a tag team! Thompson: They do work great as a team, and Hawkins is an awesome Light Heavyweight-- you could be right about that. Derringer: Of course I am, now let's discuss this next match at AEWA Extreme. Randall Osbourne, the wannabe Heavy Metal, is going to take on Sir Gallade, the wannabe Church member. Who do you think wins? Thompson: I think that-- Derringer: (interrupting) Bah, who cares what you think? I think they should kill each other and do the world a favor. Good riddance to them both. Now here's a flash from Randall Osbourne.... ----------- (Chuck Carlson stands in the ring, prepared to do an interview. He is holding a microphone, and as he turns to the camera, he begins to speak.) Carlson: Ladies and gentlemen, at this time I would like to introduce to you the Rebel of Rock, RANDALL OSBOURNE! (AC/DC's "It's a Long Way to the Top" plays as the crowd pops with an incredible ovation of cheers. Osbourne comes to the ring, wearing blue jeans, a black T-shirt and a leather jacket with "Knights" printed on the back. He slaps the hands of his fans and high fives a fan who holds a sign saying "Knights Rule") Carlson: Thank you for being here, Randall. Osbourne: Not a problem. Carlson: Randall, last Saturday we saw you attack both Sir Gallade and Shane Matthews and have words with Joy Valachi. What are your thoughts? Osbourne: First thing, Chuck, it felt SOOOO good to lay Gallade out. It felt GREAT to beat the fire out of Matthews. They've had their turn to mess with the Rebel and the time has begun for the Rebel's revenge. (The crowd pops.) Carlson: What about Valachi? Osbourne: If he wants a match, then he can bring it on. It doesn't matter to me. Carlson: How are you preparing for your upcoming steel cage match? Osbourne: The purpose of the steel cage is to keep out interference, but if history shows us anything, that's a bunch of bull. I know, you know, Galladeknows, and the fans know that he's not going to come into that match without some sort of interference or cheating planned. Matthews could get involved and so could the Church. Even ICU or the Apocalypse could get involved. So like any person having this knowledge, I go into the steel cage match prepared for war, and if it escalates beyond any sort of control, then the rest of the Knights will be on hand if anything breaks out. The Knights will go into the pay-per-view prepared for anything. (Crowd pops louder.) Carlson: Anything to say to Gallade? Osbourne: Gallade, for our last few matches now you have brought all sorts of blunt objects to the ring to put me down. Now it's a steel cage match where anything can go. So I'll say this.... (yelling) Bring your brass knuckles! Bring your Singapore cane! Because I can GUARANTEE you that anything you bring, I will bring something two times bigger and ten times more painful. The bottom line is, Gallade, prepare for DELIVERANCE!! (Osbourne leaves the ring to the loudest ovation of the night. Fade to black.) ----------- Derringer: Blah, blah, blah. Same 'ol stuff as usual, ON WITH THE SHOW! Next interview is from your next World Heavyweight Champion, Heavy Metal. Thompson: Roll it, James. ---------- (The camera opens on Heavy Metal in the ring, with a local wrestler in Atlanta, Georgia. He is preparing for the four-way match at AEWA Extreme and is completely manhandling the man who is dressed up like "Crimson" Joe Reed and looks to be about the same height and build, but is obviously not, by the facial features and such. Heavy Metal is wearing his usual black leather pants that read "Heavy" down the left pant leg and "Metal" down the right pant leg, black boots, a black leather glove on his right hand, a black elbowpad on his left arm, and a black "Heavy Metal" T-shirt, that has him on the front raising his hand with the typical rocker sign. Heavy Metal throws the wrestler into the ropes and nails him with a huge boot to the head, he then picks him up and brings him down with a powerslam, finally he sets him up in the Gutwrench position and SLAMS him down with The Megadeth powerbomb. He covers him and an official counts the one, two, three. Heavy Metal grins and walks out of the ring. He slings a towel over his right shoulder, and then stands in front of the camera.) Heavy Metal: Joe Reed, that man in there might not bethe most reasonable facsimile of an opponent as you, but rest assured, you will be thrown around the ring in the exact same fashion. I want my title back and I am going to do whatever it takes to get it, no matter what the consequences are. You say you got your name because of your "crimson" blood from your first match, eh? Well, you're about to see that blood all over again, because by the time I am through with you, you'll be bloody all over and wishing you were dead. I'm surprised you just don't forfeit the title. Ah, well, better for me.... I get to destroy someone before I take it back. (Heavy Metal chuckles and tosses the towel away.) Heavy Metal: Reed, you may have the championship now and you may have turned down the UWF title shot because you needed to "focus" on the AEWA title.... well, you'll be wishing you hadn't turned that title shot down, because after AEWA Extreme, I'm going to leave you with nothing but your body. I feel sorry for you, Reed, you're about to disappoint all of those little "Reed-a-maniacs" out there, you know that? How could you do that to such innocent minds? (Metal laughs and then gets a look of sincerity in his eyes.) Heavy Metal: You may think that you're on top of the hill right now, but you're about to become just another victim of The Megadeth powerbomb. This isn't just my opinion, it's a fact. You have too much to worry about with these UWF morons to defend the title properly and I am going to take advantage of that every chance I get. See ya in the ring, Reed, it'll be the last time we face each other. (Heavy Metal smirks and then walks off camera as it fades to black.) ------------- Derringer: Hah! Eat that, Reed. You moron, you aren't going to win jack at Extreme! Thompson: Just because Heavy Metal says so, doesn't mean it's true. Derringer: Sure it does! Haven't you ever listened to that Houston guy or Dallas or whatever his name is..... ya know.... Houston 4:15. He says so and it's true. Thompson: Sorry, I don't follow those "other" federations. Derringer: Well, anyway, next up is a flash from Paul Smith-- YES! Thompson: What? Derringer: Another loser to rank on! * * * * TO BE CONTINUED IN PART TWO * * * * -- \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- "A New Beginning" American E-Wrestling Association The Official E-Wrestling Federation of Wrestle World and TNM The Best E-Wrestling Fed of the Year 1996 in the TNM Year-End Awards Be sure to visit our Event Center at: http://www.california.com/~bullet/index.html
