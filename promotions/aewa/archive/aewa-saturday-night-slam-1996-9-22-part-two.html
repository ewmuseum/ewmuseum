Saturday Night Slam (9/22, part two)

24 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
****CONTINUED FROM PART ONE**** MW: Well, Randall Osbourne is defending his North American Title against "Slim" Jim Coolridge. LB: What is this crap? First, two guys I LIKE have to wrestle each other, and now I have to sit through two guys I HATE wrestling each other? MW: I'm afraid so, that's what you're paid for. LB: Fine, I'll watch this match, but I won't like it. MW: Whatever, let's just go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA North American Title! Introducing first, the challenger, he weighs in at 350 pounds and is accompanied by his manager, "Flashy" Freddy Hall, here is "Slim" Jim Cooooooooooooooooooooolridge! (Jim Coolridge makes his way to the ring to a good-sized crowd pop. Freddy Hall is at his side.) BD: This guy is pretty big, too bad he has no talent. MW: Come on now, Big Daddy. This guy lasted till the very end in that recent battle royal, and he also has a victory over Primal Fear to his credit-- and that was when you used to like Primal Fear. BD: Well now I realize that Primal Fear sucks too. MW: You guys....... RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 362 pounds, he is the AEWA North American Champion, Randall "The Rebel of Rock" Osboooooooooourne! (AC/DC's "It's a Long Way to the Top" blasts out over the PA as Osbourne makes his way toward the ring to a big crowd pop. His belt is draped proudly over his shoulder.) LB: And Osbourne looks ready to "Snap into a Slim Jim". BD: (laughing) Good one, Bertha! LB: I know. MW: Why do I put up with you two? BD: Because you have no choice? MW: Yep, that's about it. And now Osbourne is in the ring, and the two men shake hands. LB: What a disgusting show of sportsmanship. MW: Whatever. And now the two men lock up and they're battling for control in there, but who's that coming to ringside? BD: It's Kingpin, the manager of Organized Crime. MW: What interest does HE have in this matchup? LB: Well, his guys have been talking about going into singles wrestling; maybe he's scouting out the North American champ. MW: Well he DOES appear to be just watching, so you might be right. But back in the ring, Jim Coolridge NAILS Osbourne with an elbowsmash! Coolridge picks him up on his shoulders now, SAMOAN DROP! BD: He's not Samoan! MW: It's the name of the move, Big Daddy. Get a clue. And now Coolridge picks him up, and he's going for a Hotshot--BUT OSBOURNE COUNTERS WITH A LARIAT! Coolridge gets back up, and Osbourne NAILS him with a fist to the midsection! LB: Osbourne turned that one around in a hurry. MW: He sure did. And now he nails Coolridge with a spinning mule kick! Coolridge does not fall, Osbourne grabs him now, GUTWRENCH SUPLEX! And Coolridge is in pain! Osbourne picks him up now, and nails him with a chop! BD: Slim Jim better find a way to turn this sucker around if he wants to get his grubby little hands on that gold. MW: And now Osbourne picks him up off the mat, SNAP SUPLEX! And now the champ's going up top! He waits for Coolridge to get to his feet, and dives, FLYING BODYPRESS--BUT COOLRIDGE SIDE STEPS, AND OSBOURNE LANDS FACE FIRST ON THE MAT! LB: This is Jim's chance if he wants to win. MW: He's going for it too. He locks on a half Boston Crab, but he's too close to the ropes, and Osbourne quickly grabs them, forcing the break. BD: He should've brought him to the middle of the ring before trying that. MW: Maybe, but it's too late for that now. And now Coolridge brings Osbourne to his feet, and NAILS him with a kick to the thigh! He grabs Osbourne now, AND HE'S SETTING HIM UP FOR THE JACK-KNIFE POWER BOMB! Coolridge gets the champion up, BUT OSBOURNE USES HIS MOMENTUM TO CARRY HIM OVER--AND TURNS IT INTO A BACKSLIDE ON COOLRIDGE!!! One..Two..Three!!! (DING, DING) LB: Looks like the champ caught Slim Jim off guard. RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of two minutes and twenty-nine seconds, here is your winner, and STILL AEWA North American Champion... RANDALL "THE REBEL OF ROCK" OSBOURNE!!!!! MW: And now the Kingpin is leaving ringside, without doing anything. I guess he WAS just scouting. BD: Why would you suspect him of anything else? MW: Just a hunch. But that was an impressive victory for Osbourne. It appeared as if Coolridge had him, but the champ caught him off-guard with that counter, and by the time Coolridge knew what hit him, his shoulders had been on the mat for the three count. LB: You gotta be more aware than THAT if you wanna get to the big time in the AEWA. MW: And I'm sure that Jim Coolridge will be the next time that he wrestles. He just got caught with one mistake, going for his finisher too early, but it was enough to cost him the match. BD: So what's next, Wilson? MW: I believe that Hank Thompson is going to interview Shane Matthews. LB: We haven't seen Shane since that match with Heavy Metal a few weeks back. I wonder what he's got to say. MW: Well there's only one way to find out. Hank, take it away. (The camera shifts to the interview platform where Hank Thompson is standing.) Thompson: Thanks, Matt. Fans, I'm here today to interview the man who claims that the light-heavyweight division was too easy for him, the former two time AEWA Light-Heavyweight Champion, the man who calls himself "Sudden Death", Shane Matthews! (Anthrax's "Looking Down the Barrel of a Gun" blares over the PA as Matthews makes his way to the platform to a HUGE round of boos. Shane steps up onto the platform with Thompson.) Matthews: Hey Hank, how's it going? Thompson: I'm just fine, thank you Mr. Matthews. Now let's get on with the interview. First off, I'd like to ask where you've been. We haven't seen you at all since you lost that World Title match against Heavy Metal, and then, with the help of Sewerdog, brutally attacked him afterwards. Not even an interview since than-- what gives here? Matthews: You wanna know where I been, I'll tell ya were I've been. I've been RIGHT HERE! You haven't seen me around, because the IDIOTS running this Mickey Mouse organization haven't let me come on TV till now. President Freeman and the entire AEWA Board of Directors, with the possible exception of Kevin Larkin, have been refusing to give me matches or allow me any air time at all. I'm surprised they even let me come out here today. But they can't keep me out of that ring forever. Sudden Death will be back in there VERY soon, whether it is as a legal participant or otherwise. So I'd suggest that you schedule me a match soon, President Freeman, or there'll be Hell to pay. Thompson: I see. Well in the meantime, you've received several challenges. One of those challenges was from the Mercenary of Organized Crime. What do you have to say about that? Matthews: What I've got to say abou that is that talk is cheap! You think you're so bad, Mercenary? Then how come you never stepped into the singles circuit before? While you've been riding the coat tails of the Bounty Hunter, I've been fighting my ass off. I won two, count 'em, TWO Light Heavyweight titles, and I probably coulda won even more, but that shit was just too damn easy. And now I've moved onto the Heavyweight division, and while just about every guy I face outweighs me, I still remain in the top ten contenders, and I'm still winning most of my matches. What do you have to brag about in the singles division, Mercenary? I'll tell ya what you've got, ABSOLUTELY NOTHING! So until you DO, I'd suggest keeping your big mouth SHUT. But if you still want a piece of me, I'm not a hard man to find. Step into that ring, and you'll KNOW why they call me "Sudden Death". Thompson: You've also had challenges coming from Ronnie Hoffa's stable, what about that? Matthews: Oh yeah, that moron, "Birdman" Koko B. Ware wanted a piece of me. Thompson: Uh, his name is "Birdman" Rob Yvette. Matthews: (grabs Thompson) If I say his name is Koko B. Ware, then his name is Koko B. Ware. Understand? Thompson: I guess so. Matthews: Good. Now, as I was saying before I was so rudely interrupted, this Birdman's a friggin' joke. I already told the boy that if he wanted me, all he's gotta do is step in that ring, but maybe he don't listen so good. Maybe I gotta beat him upside the head with a brick a few times and knock it into his skull. I've beat up so many light heavyweights that it ain't even funny. One time I even beat twenty of them in the same match. If this Birdman thinks he's any better than them, then tell him to get his ass in that ring, and I'll show him he's not. And having Frankie, I mean Hoffa, at ringside isn't gonna help ya either, Koko. Thompson: Well now that we've addressed that, do you have anything else to say before we conclude this interview? Matthews: Yeah, as a matter of fact, I do. No matter what Freeman, or anyone else in charge around here says or does, Sudden Death ain't gonna go away. No way, no how. If I have to buy a ticket to get into the arena, I'll be here. And as long as I AM here, no one is safe. Sudden Death strikes when you least expect it, and you can EXPECT to be going to the hospital after it does strike. As long as I'm here, every man, every woman, and every title is in jeopardy, and I won't stop till I've beaten them all, and won EVERY title. This interview is over, Thompson. I'll see ya later, and I'll see every one of you AEWA wrestlers in your nightmares. (waves to crowd.) See ya, morons. (Matthews, with a big grin on his face, walks back toward the locker room to a HUGE accompaniment of boos.) Thompson: Back to you, guys. (Our camera shifts back to our announcing team.) BD: Whoa, Shane's really upset. I'd hate to be the next guy to have to step in there with him. MW: I think he's just blowing hot air. I still don't think he can seriously compete in the heavyweight division. LB: Aw, come on. He beat Ben Slocum, and that guy's won two TV Titles and a World title. MW: Maybe, but he still has to prove himself to me. BD: He will. MW: We'll see. LB: So what's next? MW: Marcus McTeague is defending his TV Title against Deathwatch. BD: Well he can just about kiss that title goodbye. MW: Why? Deathwatch is certainly a tough opponent, but Marcus McTeague is a great champion. LB: (laughing) You're joking, right? MW: I am NOT joking. Marcus McTeague is a great wrestler, and he's gonna fight for all he's worth, especially against an opponent who has such little respect for the AEWA competition as Deathwatch. McTeague is gonna show Deathwatch that there's no place tougher than the AEWA, or he's gonna die trying. BD: I'll go with the second option. MW: I don't think so. Let's go to the ring and find out. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA Television Title! Introducing first, the challenger, from New York City, and weighing in at 315 pounds, Deathwaaaaaaaaaaaaaatch! (Metallica's "Master of Puppets" begins to play as Deathwatch makes his way to the ring, wearing an "AEWA Sucks" T-Shirt. The boos storm down from the rafters.) LB: Hey, you think these people like Deathwatch? MW: Somehow, I doubt it. BD: Boy, he's really getting it from these fans, but he seems to like it! MW: He is a very disturbed person. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Northern Ontario, he weighs in at 412 pounds and is the AEWA Television Champion, Marcus "Natural Wonder" McTeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeague! (The Screaming Trees' "Sweet Oblivion" plays loudly over the PA as Marcus McTeague, belt in hand, makes his way to the ring to a HUGE crowd pop.) LB: Tonight, the lumberjack's gonna fall. Timbeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeer! MW: Very funny. LB: I thought so. MW: And now McTeague is in the ring, and he hands the belt to the referee. And now the ref shows the belt to Deathwatch, WHO SPITS ON IT! BD: I can't believe he just did that! That's the TV Title we're talking about. MW: He apparently doesn't care! McTeague is upset! He charges in at Deathwatch, but gets caught with a boot to the face! And now Deathwatch nails him with a forearm to the back, and McTeague is stooped over in pain! LB: Deathwatch ain't wasting any time. MW: He's sure not. It looks like he wants to make quick work of McTeague. Deathwatch nails him with a knee to the back, and now a kick to the back of the head! McTeague is stunned, and Deathwatch is going up top! BD: He's going for the Death's Vengeance already! MW: He IS! Is this how little respect he has for the competition here in the AEWA? He thinks he can end it right now? LB: Apparently so. MW: That's disgusting. Deathwatch is up top now and he IS going for the Death's Vengeance. McTeague has got himself into position, and Deathwatch dives, but McTeague turns around, AND CATCHES DEATHWATCH IN A POWERSLAM! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder up! BD: That one almost cost Deathwatch BIG TIME. MW: It sure did. He got too cocky, going for his finisher already, but Marcus McTeague wasn't done yet, and he made Deathwatch pay! And now McTeague is waiting for Deathwatch to get to his feet, and charges in at him, FRANKENSTEINER--NO! Deathwatch catches him! POWER BOMB BY DEATHWATCH! The cover! One..Two..Foot on the Ropes! LB: WOW! Do these guys know anything OTHER than high impact moves? MW: Not from what we've seen so far. I'm surprised that Deathwatch had enough mental facilities to counter that frankensteiner so soon after being powerslammed. BD: He's one tough hombre. MW: That's for sure. And now Deathwatch is just standing, waiting for McTeague to rise to his feet, he does, and Deathwatch runs to the ropes! He comes back, AND GETS CAUGHT WITH A DROPKICK TO THE KNEE!! LB: That staggered him, but does McTeague have enough left to take control? MW: We'll find out. McTeague gets back up to his feet, and SLAPS Deathwatch! Giving him a little of his own medicine! And now McTeague picks Deathwatch up, BACKBREAKER! LB: I guess that answers my question. MW: It certainly does. Marcus McTeague leaves Deathwatch lying on the mat now, and he goes up top! He dives, FLYING KNEEDROP! He NAILED it! And he's not done! He picks Deathwatch up off the mat, and lifts him overhead, JACK-KNIFE POWER BOMB! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! BD: He almost got Deathwatch there, but Deathwatch isn't gonna go down easy for ANYBODY. MW: You know it. McTeague looks like he thinks he's got him now. He brings Deathwatch to his feet, takes a step back, and LEVELS him with a clothesline! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Deathwatch better start thinking about doing something. MW: That's an understatement. And now Marcus McTeague is going up top! What's he gonna do now? He waits for Deathwatch to rise to his feet, FLYING SUNSET FLIP! He's trying to pull him over with it, BUT DEATHWATCH JUST SITS DOWN ON HIS CHEST! One..Two..Kickout! BD: See, Deathwatch will always be able to outsmart McTeague here. MW: We'll see. Both men are quickly up, and McTeague picks up Deathwatch, BODYSLAM--BUT DEATHWATCH COUNTERS WITH A SMALL PACKAGE!! One..Two..Kickout! LB: I think Big Daddy was right-- just look, the lumberjack's toast. MW: Not yet he isn't. Both men up again, and Deathwatch grabs McTeague by the head, JUMPING DDT! McTeague is down, and Deathwatch DROPS an elbow! BD: Deathwatch is picking up steam, and McTeague is running out. MW: It appears that way for the moment. And now Deathwatch lifts McTeague off the mat, he picks him up, BELLY-TO-BACK SUPLEX! Deathwatch DROPS a knee onto McTeague now, and he's going for the cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: He might have some fight left in him, but not for long. MW: I don't know about that. And now Deathwatch is going up top, HE'S GOING FOR THE DEATH'S VENGEANCE AGAIN! BD: And I don't think McTeague has enough left for a counter anymore. MW: Deathwatch climbs up to the top, and he waits for McTeague to get to his feet. He dives, BUT MCTEAGUE DUCKS UNDER THE FLYING DDT, AND DEATHWATCH HITS THE MAT HARD! LB: I think McTeague dodged a bullet by accident, because he just collapsed! MW: It doesn't matter HOW he dodged it, he did, and now it's Deathwatch who's in pain, and McTeague has made it to his feet! He picks up Deathwatch, lifts him into the air, DOCTOR BOMB! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Wow! You know, I can flash back to when I used to use that move! MW: (laughing) Sure you can, Big Daddy. And now McTeague is going up top, and Deathwatch is prone on the mat. McTeague has reached the top rope, and he dives, FLYING KNEEDROP--BUT DEATHWATCH ROLLS AWAY, AND MCTEAGUE LANDS HARD ON HIS KNEE! LB: He's gonna be limping away from this one. MW: That a definite possibility. And now Deathwatch is rising to his feet slowly, as is McTeague. They're both up now, and Deathwatch grabs McTeague from behind, GERMAN SUPLEX! One..Two..Shoulder Up! BD: McTeague ALMOST bit the dust there. MW: ALMOST. Deathwatch picks McTeague up off the canvas now, and lifts him up, POWER BOMB! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: But McTeague WON'T go down. MW: No he won't, and Deathwatch is UPSET! He brings McTeague back to his feet, and nails him with a chop! And now he pick the champ up, BACK SUPLEX--BUT MCTEAGUE COUNTERS WITH A CROSS-BODY BLOCK, AND ALL 412 POUNDS OF HIM COMES CRASHING DOWN ON TOP OF DEATHWATCH! The cover! One..Two..Three!!!!(DING, DING) BD: He pulled the tights! MW: He did no such thing. RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of eight minutes and twenty seconds, here is your winner, and STILL AEWA Television Champion... MARCUS "NATURAL WONDER" MCTEAGUE! LB: Looks like he caught Deathwatch when he wasn't looking, and SOMEHOW, McTeague manages to escape here with the belt. MW: And Deathwatch is none too happy about it. He's throwing a fit to the referee, but he won't hear any of it, this one's over. The ref is calling for the belt, and he goes to hand it to McTeague--BUT DEATHWATCH SNATCHES IT FROM HIM, AND CLOCKS MCTEAGUE WITH IT! THIS IS DISGUSTING! BD: This is cool. MW: And now once again, Deathwatch SPITS on the title belt, and drops it onto the chest of McTeague! And he's stomping away on him. BUT HERE COMES SOMEONE TO THE RESCUE! LB: It's only Mike Stewart. MW: We can use all the help we can get. Stewart STORMS into the ring, and nails Deathwatch with a dropkick from behind. Deathwatch is staggered, and Stewart clotheslines him out of the ring! And Deathwatch is bailing out! BD: He who fights and runs away, lives to fight another day. MW: I suppose you could put it like that. Deathwatch is headed to the locker room, and Stewart is tending to the fallen Marcus McTeague. It's nice to see somebody FINALLY sticking up for the AEWA against this MENACE called Deathwatch. LB: He had to attack Deathwatch from behind to get him to leave. If they ever met face-to-face, Deathwatch would KILL Stewart. MW: I don't think so. LB: I DO think so. MW: We could very well find out soon. BD: So what's next? MW: All we've got left is our main event-- Heavy Metal is defending the World title against four-time former champ, Mister Crowley. LB: This should be one heckuva matchup, huh? MW: It sure should be. The seemingly unstoppable young champion against the veteran former champ. Nobody's held that belt as many times as Crowley, and he's always a threat to win it again. BD: I can't wait for this one. MW: To tell you the truth, even though I don't like either of these guys, I can't wait either, so let's go to the ring, and let's get ready to rumble! RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA Heavyweight Championship of the World! Introducing first, the challenger, From the Darkside, he weighs in at 328 pounds and is a FOUR-TIME former AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, Mister Crowleeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeey! (The lights go off as Ozzy Osbourne's "Mister Crowley" begins to eerily creep into the arena, and the dreaded former champion makes his way to the ring to a chorus of boos. He gets to ringside, and raises the lights.) LB: We haven't seen Mister Crowley in quite some time, I almost forgot how creepy his entrance is. It brings back lots of good memories. MW: Lots of championship memories. BD: That's for sure. MW: This one's gonna be a classic! RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 450 pounds, he is the AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, Heavy Metaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaal! (Metallica's "Battery" cranks out over the PA as the MASSIVE World Champion makes his way to the ring to a chorus of boos of his own.) LB: And here comes the champion. MW: He is certainly the best in the game today, and he proves it every week out here. Can Crowley take him? BD: Crowley's one mean bastard, I wouldn't doubt it for a second. MW: You're right on that point. And now the champ is in the ring, he puts the belt down, and he's all business right now! The two meet in the center of the ring, and lock each other in a dead stare! LB: I'd hate to step between those two right now. MW: Me too. And Heavy Metal just reaches out, and grabs Crowley by the neck, CHOKESLAM! BD: Opening with a choke slam? This guy means business! And wait a minute, somebody's coming to ringside. MW: It's Sewerdog! As you know, he attacked Heavy Metal after his match with Shane Matthews a few weeks ago, and Heavy Metal has been none too happy about it. LB: Would you be? MW: No, of course not. And now Sewerdog has just pulled up a chair, and he's just sitting there, watching the match. BD: Maybe he wanted a front row seat. MW: Doubtful, but let's get back to the action in the ring. Heavy Metal lifts up Mister Crowley now, FALLAWAY SLAM! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: Heavy Metal's really taking it to the ex-champ. MW: He sure is, and now he's going up top! We all know how agile this big man is. He waits for Crowley to get to his feet, and jumps, FLYING CROSS BODY PRESS! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Crowley's far from done though. MW: We know all too well how fast Crowley can recuperate, AND HE JUST SAT UP! He gets to his feet, and Heavy Metal can't believe it! He charges in at Crowley, DROPKICK--BUT CROWLEY SIDE STEPS, AND THE CHAMP HITS THE MAT HARD. LB: That's our Mister Crowley. MW: It certainly is, that's Crowley at his best. And now Crowley DROPS an elbowsmash onto Heavy Metal, and he's going up top! He waits for Heavy Metal to reach his feet, FLYING CROSS BODY PRESS! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: He almost won the title there! MW: He sure did. And now Crowley brings the big man to his feet, and Crowley runs to the ropes! He comes back, and Heavy Metal puts his head down for a backdrop--but Crowley stops short, picks up Heavy Metal, AND POWER BOMBS HIM! I can't believe he got him up! The cover! One..Two..Kickout!! LB: The champ refuses to go down though. MW: The sign of a good champion. And now Mister Crowley ascends to the top rope. He waits for Heavy Metal to rise, FLYING BODYPRESS--BUT HEAVY METAL SIDE STEPS, AND CROWLEY LANDS HARD ON THE MAT! BD: Both men are fighting as if their life depends on this one. MW: And to an effect, it does. Both men slowly make it to their feet, but Heavy Metal appears to have it more together. And now Heavy Metal picks up Mister Crowley, SAMOAN DROP! Heavy Metal picks Crowley up off the mat now, and takes him DOWN with a faceslam. LB: This is a great match! MW: You can say that again. And now Heavy Metal brings Crowley back up, and CHOPS away on him! Heavy Metal grabs Crowley by the neck now, lifts him up, and BRUTALLY chokeslams him! BD: Crowley's in trouble now. MW: He sure is. Heavy Metal DROPS a kneeling headbutt onto Crowley, and now he's going up top again! He waits for Crowley to rise, and dives-- FLYING CROSS BODY PRESS! The cover! One..Kickout! LB: Did you see THAT?? Crowley just got the crap beat out of him, and he still kicked out at one! What is he made of? MW: I don't know, but that certainly upset Heavy Metal. Heavy Metal picks Crowley up, and whips him to the ropes! Crowley comes back, AND LEVELS HEAVY METAL WITH A JUMPING SIDE KICK! BD: What a match! MW: It certainly is. Heavy Metal is quick back to his feet, but Crowley takes him down with a jawbreaker! Crowley brings him up again, and NAILS him with a fist to the midsection! LB: The title is in jeopardy now! MW: It sure is! And now Crowley NAILS Heavy Metal with an elbowsmash! Crowley grabs him, and takes him down with a jawbreaker! Heavy Metal is down and out. BD: And Crowley is going to the top! MW: Yes he is. He's on the top rope, and he waits for Heavy Metal to get to his feet! He does, and Crowley dives, FLYING BODYPRESS! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: That certainly is a popular move today. MW: And the funny thing is, it keeps working. Crowley brings Heavy Metal to his feet now, and takes him down with a legsweep! Crowley's going up top again, what's he going for this time? He again waits for Heavy Metal to rise, and jumps, FLYING DROPKICK! The cover! One..Kickout! BD: And now HEAVY METAL kicks out at one! These guys just don't stop! MW: No they don't. And now Crowley's going up top again! He's taking his time this time, but I don't think he can afford to, Heavy Metal's already on his feet! Crowley has just reached the top rope, but Heavy Metal's right there behind him! He NAILS Crowley in the stomach, and Crowley comes crumbling down off the buckle! LB: How quickly the tide changes. MW: That it does indeed. And now Heavy Metal is in control. Heavy Metal picks Crowley up off the mat, and NAILS him with a faceslam! And now Heavy Metal's going up top! He waits for Crowley to rise, and jumps, FLYING CROSS BODY PRESS! The cover! One..Kickout! BD: AGAIN? MW: Yes, again. And Heavy Metal is fuming. He brings Crowley to his feet, and SLAPS him right across the face! That was insulting! And now Heavy Metal just jumps straight up, STANDING DROPKICK! LB: This guy surprises me every time out! MW: He sure does. Crowley is down now, and Heavy Metal just FLATTENS him with a Big Splash! The cover! One..Two..Three! (DING, DING) BD: He got him! The young champion beat the legend! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of nine minutes and forty-eight seconds, here is your winner, and STILL AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World.... HEAVY METAL!!!!! MW: He EARNED this one. What a matchup! Heavy Metal gets up and raises his arms in victory, AND GETS LEVELED WITH THE HOOD DROPKICK BY SEWERDOG! I almost forgot that Sewerdog was out there! He was just waiting to attack the whole time! LB: And now he's got a chair in there with him! MW: He does, and he's CRACKING it over the head of Heavy Metal! That was devastating! And now Sewerdog places the chair on top of the head of Heavy Metal, and he goes up top, FLYING LEGDROP ONTO THE CHAIR ONTO THE HEAD OF HEAVY METAL! He could be seriously hurt down there! This Sewerdog is NUTS! BD: You can say that again! He already had Heavy Metal pissed, now Heavy Metal's gonna KILL him! MW: And now Sewerdog is walking away, smiling at the havoc he has caused. The end of a great match has been marred by the interference of a psycho, and it's a shame that THIS is what this match will be remembered for, instead of the great action. LB: I can't wait to see what happens when Heavy Metal finally gets his hands on Sewerdog. It's gonna be wild! MW: You can say that again. But for now, we're out of time fans, so for the Lovely Bertha, and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt, this is Matt Wilson saying so long everybody!
