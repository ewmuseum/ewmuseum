Saturday Night Slam (11/30)

1 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power Bomb, and a look at Mister Crowley DRIVING the head of some guy into the concrete with the Premature Burial! Then the camera switches to Hank Thompson and Roy Stanley in a packed arena) HT: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Saturday Night Slam! We have plenty of great matches scheduled for tonight, folks. RS: That's an understatement, buddy. In the main event, Penance will be defending his Light Heavyweight title against Marc Thomas! That should be an EXCELLENT bout! HT: But before we get to the matches, we're going to watch a special interview with President Freeman. After Kevin Larkin's recent action, I'm VERY interested in this. RS: Yeah, Larkin had some unkind words for Freeman on Saturday night. I wonder if we're going to see a response tonight. HT: That's definitely a possibility. If it wasn't for Kevin Larkin's recent actions, the Graveyard Shift would still be tag team champions. RS: Yeah, but you have to admit-- President Freeman has caused a LOT of trouble since he took over the AEWA. If it wasn't for HIM, these problems wouldn't have started in the first place. HT: I totally disagree with you on that subject, but I don't feel like arguing. We're now going LIVE to the AEWA Headquarters in Sacramento, where Freeman would like to address some things. RS: Let's watch.... ------------- (The camera opens on President Bob Freeman, sitting alone in his office. He is wearing an expensive white suit, with a black tie down the middle. There is an "AEWA Rocks" mug on his desk, presumably filled with coffee, and he takes a sip. He smiles at the camera, straightens his tie, and begins to speak.) Freeman: Good evening, ladies and gentlemen. I'd like to talk about the recent events from last week's Saturday Night Slam, when Vice President Kevin Larkin interfered in the tag team triangle match. Because of his actions, the West Coast Connection walked away with the championship belts. That was absolutely unfair, and I'm not going to tolerate it. Immediately following the match, at the conclusion of the program, Larkin made several comments about me. I RESENT those insinuations; his statements were completely unfair. (takes another sip of coffee) Over the past eight months, I have had the opportunity to work with Kevin Larkin. Until just a few months ago, he showed nothing but complete professionalism toward his fellow employees and myself. However, recently, he has become an embarrassment to the AEWA Board Of Directors. His insanity has gone FAR ENOUGH, and I'm not going to stand for it anymore. Kevin, I am PLEADING with you-- please stop this. You can't continue to embarrass our organization like this. I am immediately stripping you of your refereeing license. You never should have gotten one of those. Next, I want to warn you.... (suddenly begins speaking louder) I am YOUR boss. I have the power to fine you, give you orders-- and FIRE you. If you don't start working in a professional manner, I will have no choice but to fire you from the AEWA. I'm trying to run a BUSINESS here, and you're making us look like a CIRCUS. I will be reviewing your performance for the next few weeks, and if you don't start doing your JOB soon, I will have no other choice but to fire you. I know that Commissioner Gehringer feels the same way about this decision, so it's not just me. I'm sorry to publicly confront you, but you haven't been returning my calls. In fact, you really don't even seem to care about our Board meetings any more. (turns to his calendar) Jeez, Kevin, I haven't even TALKED to you in over two months. If you want to have an active role in the AEWA's Board Of Directors, you need to start acting like a mature adult. Now, I apologize for taking up so much TV time, but I had no other choice. Without further ado, let's get back to the arena. (The scene fades to black, and the camera returns to our broadcast team.) --------------- HT: That HAD to be done, folks. President Freeman has CONSTANLY been insulted by Kevin Larkin, and he's finally ready to take action. RS: But Larkin has the RIGHT to insult him. This is America, and we have RIGHTS in this country. Larkin just said some bad things about his boss-- everybody in the WORLD does that. HT: But they don't say these "bad things" on national television, Roy. Let's get serious. It's almost like Larkin has been CHALLENGING President Freeman to fire him. RS: He HAS been, and that's the funny part. Freeman is too much of a coward to fire him, so it really doesn't matter. HT: Well, I'd love to continue arguing with you, but it's time for our first match. Primal Fear is about to square off against his longtime enemy, the Twilight Strangler! RS: These two guys have wrestled once before, and Primal Fear somehow managed to pull off the victory. It's going to be a different story tonight, folks. HT: Let's just watch.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, at a weight of 360 pounds, PRIMAL FEAR! (Primal Fear makes his entrance to a HUGE pop from the crowd. He is wearing a black vest and brown tights. He has a determined look on his face, and quickly climbs into the ring.) HT: Primal Fear is once again ready for a fight! RS: Well, he ANNIHILATED the Crazy Canuck a few nights ago-- I don't want to see what he does tonight! HT: Here comes his opponent, The Twilight Strangler! RING ANNOUNCER: Next, being accompanied by his manager, Miss DeMeanor, weighing in at 274 pounds, here is THE TWILIGHT STRANGLER! (The Twilight Strangler makes his way toward the ring, to a BIG round of boos from the crowd. He is literally foaming at the mouth, with spit dripping down his chin. Miss DeMeanor follows closely behind him.) RS: The Twilight Strangler is one weird dude! I like him! HT: You would. Primal Fear starts right on him as he enters the ring, taking him down with punches to the corner. Primal Fear DRIVES his shoulder into the Twilight Strangler's stomach! RS: That knocks the wind right out of you! HT: Now Primal Fear NAILS him with a side suplex! He follows it up with a BIG leg drop! Here's the cover! One....Two....Kickout! RS: He's not going down that easily! Primal Fear had better do better than that! HT: It's not over yet, Roy. Now Primal Fear picks him up, and brings him DOWN with a piledriver! RS: The Twilight Strangler may have a broken neck from that maneuver! Why doesn't the referee get some damn control? HT: Because Primal Fear hasn't broken any rules. Now Fear grabs the Strangler again, and NAILS him with a swinging neckbreaker! RS: Primal Fear whips the Strangler to the corner-- and begins to SLAM his head into the turnbuckle! He's really POUNDING him! HT: And now Fear hits him with a snap mare! As The Twilight Strangler gets up, Primal Fear NAILS him with a running clothesline! RS: Primal Fear is really controlling this match! The Twilight Strangler hasn't done ANYTHING to him yet! HT: Here's the cover! One....Two...Kickout! HT: The Twilight Strangler managed to kick out of that one. Now Primal Fear picks him back up, and CRUSHES him with an atomic drop! He seems to be working on the back and neck of the Strangler-- and it's been EXTREMELY effective so far. RS: But it's not FAIR! HT: Quit your whining, Roy. Primal Fear whips the Twilight Strangler to the corner again.... He puts him on the top turnbuckle, AND EXECUTES A SUPER DDT TO THE MAT! RS: That HAD to break the Strangler's neck in two! No human being could POSSIBLY survive a maneuver like that! HT: Primal Fear grabs him, SWINGING DDT! Now Fear picks him up again, and NAILS him with a power bomb! That was INCREDIBLE! RS: NOW HE'S GIVING THE SIGN FOR THE BUMP SPLASH! HT: AND HE HITS IT! Here's the cover! One......Two......Three!! (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner, at a time of only four minutes, PRIMAL FEAR! RS: Wow, what a short match! The paramedics are attending to the Twilight Strangler, bringing a stretcher to the ring with them! I'm afraid he's severely hurt! HT: Yes, it looks like the Strangler is going to the hospital tonight. Primal Fear has been putting a LOT of people out with his Bump Splash lately, folks. RS: Yeah, how's he doing it? What's the trick? HT: There IS no trick, Roy. It's called talent. RS: Never heard of it. HT: Somehow that doesn't surprise me. Up next, "The Shotgun" Stan Crawford will be making his long-awaited debut against Wild Thing. We've heard quite a bit about this kid. RS: Actually, I haven't heard ANYTHING about him. He's going to get MASSACRED by Wild Thing, who happens to be a former TV champion. HT: Let's just wait and see.... RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, at a weight of 320 pounds, from New York City, this is "THE SHOTGUN" STAAAAAAAAAAN CRAWFORD! (Filter's "Hey Man Nice Shot" plays loudly over the PA, as Stan Crawford makes his way to the ring. He gets a mild crowd reception, since most fans have not seen him wrestle. He is dressed in a black leather jacket, and wears black tights.) RS: Man, this guy is BIG! He must be over six and a half feet tall! HT: He's listed as six foot eight inches. RS: Jeez, that's just TOO big. And here comes his opponent! RING ANNOUNCER: Next, accompanied by his manager, Bootknife Kelly, weighing in at 246 pounds, this is WIIIIILD THIIIIIIING! ("Hurt" by Nine Inch Nails begins playing loudly, as Wild Thing makes his way to ringside. Bootknife Kelly follows behind, in a red tuxedo. Wild Thing wears a black leather vest, and his chest is COVERED in scars.) HT: This should be a great match! RS: I guess, but I'm betting on Wild Thing. Stan Crawford has no experience here in the AEWA. HT: Keep in mind, he DOES have about five years experience in other federations. He has almost as many scars on him as Wild Thing does! RS: I have a scar. HT: Really? Where? RS: (sticks his arm out) Here on my arm, see? I got into a knife fight once. HT: No you didn't, Roy. You probably cut yourself buttering your toast last week. Anyway, I'd rather not sit here talking about scars.... RS: Neither would I. Let's just watch this match! HT: And the bell sounds! Crawford takes control, whipping Wild Thing to the ropes! Wild Thing gets hit with a shoulder, and goes down! RS: Crawford has the size advantage, but Wild Thing can outrun him! HT: Wild Thing is whipped to the ropes again-- and Crawford hits him with a BIG clothesline! RS: Now Crawford slaps on an armbar submission! Wild Thing is WRITHING in pain, but he manages to crawl to the ropes! HT: But now Wild Thing is hit with an armringer! He immediately goes down to his knees! RS: And Crawford just nailed him with a BIG kick to the head! Here's the cover! One...Two...Kickout! HT: He continues to work on that arm! Crawford bounces off the ropes, comes back, AND NAILS WILD THING WITH A SHOULDERBREAKER! RS: That had a LOT of impact. Now he whips him to the ropes, but Crawford misses with a clothesline-- and Wild Thing comes back with a dropkick! HT: And he connects with an elbowdrop on Crawford! The Cover! One......Two....Kickout! HT: Now Wild Thing picks him up, and executes a BEAUTIFUL vertical suplex! And he doesn't waste ANY time, and locks in a reverse chinlock! RS: But Crawford quickly manages to break the hold.... Wild Thing comes right back with a double underhook suplex! HT: And Crawford is whipped into the ropes, but Wild Thing gets caught with his head down! CRAWFORD NAILS HIM WITH A PILEDRIVER! RS: And now he NAILS Wild Thing with a gutwrench suplex! He's doing better than I thought he would, folks. HT: Crawford drags Wild Thing to the corner.... He exits the ring, and SMASHES Wild Thing's knee on the post! RS: First it was the arms, now the legs! "The Shotgun" is very methodical in his work! He picks an area, and works on breaking it! That guy is one sick puppy! HT: Back in the ring, Crawford hits Wild Thing with a kneebreaker! I see what you mean-- he's REALLY working on the legs. Now he SPITS on him, and applies a figure-four leglock! RS: Wild Thing has got to give up! His legs have been DESTROYED! HT: Wild Thing manages to use all of his strength to reach the ropes! He gets back to his feet, but he's still limping badly. Crawford whips him to the ropes again, BIG BOOT! The Cover! One....Two....Kickout! HT: And now Crawford hits him with a Front Layout Suplex! RS: Wild Thing can't possibly take any more of this punishment. Bootknife Kelly should just throw in the towel. HT: I agree. Now Wild Thing is whipped to the corner-- BUT AS CRAWFORD RUNS AT HIM, WILD THING LIFTS THE KNEE! RS: And Wild Thing hits him with a BIG uppercut! Crawford falls to the ground like a rock! Jeez, How did Wild Thing EVER find the strength for a comeback like this? HT: I have no idea. Now he's going for the Party Time Splash, BUT HE MISSES! Crawford gets back up, and hits him with a back suplex! He pulls him to the corner, lifts him on the turnbuckle, and hits him with a BIG superplex! RS: That's the setup move for his finisher, the 12 Gauge! HT: AND HE EXECUTES THE 12 GAUGE! What a DEVASTATING gourdbuster that move is! Here's the cover! One.....Two.....Three! (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner, "THE SHOTGUN" STAN CRAWFORD! RS: I hate to admit it, but Crawford looked pretty damn good in that match. He might actually do okay for himself around here. HT: For once, we both agree. RS: I'm not exactly proud of that, Hank. Let's just get to the next match, so I can LEAVE this hellhole! HT: Good idea. Penance is about to defend his Light Heavyweight title against Marc Thomas! This should be an EXCELLENT match. RS: Marc Thomas has been fairly impressive lately, but he doesn't even have a CHANCE against Penance. I mean, let's get SERIOUS. HT: Don't underestimate him, Roy. RING ANNOUNCER: This contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA Light Heavyweight championship of the World! Introducing first, the challenger, weighing in at 240 pounds, here is MARC THOMAS! (Thomas makes his way toward the ring, to almost NO reaction from the crowd. He is wearing a black sleeveless leather jacket.) RS: This guy is probably the best "all around" wrestler in the entire AEWA. I don't like him very much, but he IS good. HT: He's a MASTER of technical wrestling, and that could be the deciding factor in this match. RS: But here comes the champ.... RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 223 pounds, he is the AEWA Light Heavyweight champion, PENANCE! (Skid Row's "My Enemy" begins playing loudly over the PA, and Penance makes his way toward the ring to a BIG round of boos. He is dressed completely in black and the belt is around his waist.) RS: Penance has looked good lately! He hasn't needed any assistance from the ICU in defeating his opponents lately! HT: But the crowd wants Penance to lose the belt tonight! They're SICK of those ICU thugs. RS: And so am I, but we need to give Penance SOME credit. He's been a GREAT champion. HT: That's true. The bell has sounded for the start of the match, and these guys are ready to go! Penance takes control, and takes Thomas down with a single-leg takedown! RS: Penance is just so damn fast! HT: Yes, there's no doubt about that. Now Penance hits Thomas with a standing dropkick! He goes for the cover! One....Kickout! HT: And Penance nails him with a BIG superkick! Penance quickly runs over to Thomas and gets him into a Texas Cloverleaf! RS: That move hurts like hell! Thomas just broke the hold, though, and he's getting back up.... HT: Now Penance whips him to the ropes-- Thomas ducks a clothesline, and comes back with a jumping clothesline of his own! RS: And he NAILS Penance with a roundhouse right! Thomas lifts him up, and executes a BIG suplex! One....Two...Kickout! HT: Penance gets up first, and hits Marc Thomas with a kick to the stomach! Now he nails him with a Russian legsweep! RS: And Penance is going to the top rope! He jumps, AND CONNECTS WITH A FLYING LEGDROP! He hooks the leg! One....Two....Kickout! HT: Penance whips Thomas to the ropes-- Thomas comes back, and gets caught in a spinebuster slam! RS: Now Penance picks him up, and brings him down with a German suplex! That was a DEVASTATING move! One....Two....Kickout! HT: Penance jumps back up, and hits Thomas with a bulldog! He levels him into the corner with a series of rights, and places him on the top turnbuckle-- FRANKENSTEINER! RS: Thomas landed hard on the top of his head! That was INCREDIBLE! HT: Penance grabs him, and executes a BIG side suplex! He quickly goes for the cover.... One....Two...Kickout! HT: Now Thomas is thrown over the top rope! Penance climbs up to the top turnbuckle, gets in position-- AND EXECUTES A BIG MOONSAULT TO THE FLOOR! He HAMMERED Thomas with that move! RS: That was absolutely amazing! He must have flown twenty feet! HT: Now Penance drags Thomas back into the ring! He lifts him up.... RS: AND HE HITS HIM WITH THE "FINAL JUDGMENT" TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER!! It's over! Penance goes for the cover.... One....Two....Three! (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner, and STILL AEWA Light Heavyweight champion, PENANCE! HT: Well, Penance is STILL undefeated, and the ICU must be very happy with him. He has really been on a winning streak lately. RS: That's for sure, Hank. Can ANYBODY beat him? HT: Of course.... it just might take a VERY long time. RS: Well, he'll be defending the title against "The Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi next week, so his reign might be coming to an end soon. HT: Good point. Well fans, that's all the time we have for this week's show. We've got to go now, so for Roy Stanley, this is Hank Thompson saying so long everybody!
