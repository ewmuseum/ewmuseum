AEWA — Card Archive

Straight Talk (1/15, part two)

17 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~~ CONTINUED FROM PART ONE ~~~~ ---------- (The cameraman can be seen walking the cold lonely dark streets at night, as he steps into an alley. A man in a black trenchcoat stands there with his long black hair down to his sides, and you can see his breath because of the cold air around him. He begins to speak.) Deranged: Well now, I have tried many things to satisfy you people and fans in the AEWA. I have tried to show you how much I would love to return to my first state in the AEWA as a fan-favorite. But with recent results, I have no choice but to go alone. I may have no friends. But then again, friends are what got me in trouble here in the first place. Let me tell you people something.... (Deranged points at a brick wall where scenes from his AEWA matches can be seen.) Deranged: My first TV title win. I remember it well. The fans were cheering me on so much they had to stop and catch a breath. That is when I had the brutality and force to do what they wanted. Nevermore. *CLICK* Deranged: Here is the second TV Title reign of mine, where I beat Ben Slocum. The man who went for the World title at December Doom and did not come out on top. Well, now I want a chance at that World title, Shane Matthews. *CLICK* Deranged: Here is when I lost the TV title to Heavy Metal, who then dropped it because of his world title. Later I regained it to start my third title reign. Then Randall Osbourne got the best of me. Then I was attacked by those thugs. (The footage ends, and Deranged just stands there with a cold look in his eyes. A shadow falls over his face.) Deranged: I have done many other great things. Beat people nobody ever thought I could. Beat Heavy Metal in under five seconds. But now it is MY time. It is my turn to be at the top in the AEWA, and I am making this a challenge to you, SHANE MATTHEWS. You may be a good champion, but I would make an even greater one. The title that you wear around your waist has been there long enough. Now I have no choice but to take it from you. (Deranged turns as he lifts his head, but the shadow moves with him. He paces slowly around, then stands still once again.) Deranged: Shane Matthews. That is a name that many may shiver at, but he is nothing compared to me. I have what it takes to be a fighting champion. I have the charisma, skills, agility, and everything else that it takes to be a champion. You'd just better watch out, because I will be lurking in the shadows watching your every move as I prepare. If I were you.... I would do the same. See you soon, Matthews. (The camera fades to black) ---------- KW: Well, Deranged is getting his wish on this week's edition of Rumble, and when he does, he's gonna be sorry that he ever asked for this match. Matthews is gonna tear him limb from limb. CC: Don't be too sure of that. Matthews has indeed shown himself to be a great fighting champion, but Deranged is hungry, and that could be the difference in this one. KW: Oh PLEASE! It won't even be close! Squash victory for Matthews. CC: Deranged is MUCH better than that, and if Matthews isn't careful this week, Deranged is gonna walk away with the title. KW: Maybe in your little fantasy world. CC: We'll see. And now let's get a word with an old face from the AEWA's past who may be returning soon, the man called Jawbreaker. ---------- (The camera opens on Jawbreaker, sitting alone in the locker room. He appears to be in a VERY bad mood.) Jawbreaker: I'm coming back to the AEWA and I'm ready for some serious action! The AEWA has changed quite a bit, but it really doesn't matter. When I make my return, everybody will be forced to bow down at my feet. Look forward to a lot of broken jaws, fools! I don't care when and I don't care how, but I'm ready to bust everyone's head wide open. (Fade to black.) ---------- KW: What a dork. I don't care when and I don't care how, Marvin K. Jawbreaker would you PLEASE go now. CC: Ah, Dr. Seuss. It's nice to know you have such an advanced literary background, Ken. KW: You know me, I always stick to the classics. CC: Anyway, Jawbreaker has not OFFICIALLY been rehired by the AEWA just yet, we are in contract negotiations with him. He should be a great addition to our roster if we can sign him. KW: If you say so. CC: And now let's hear from the man who until recently has been masquerading around as the Masked Yorel. Of course, I'm talking about Leroy Brown. ------------ (The camera opens on Leroy Brown in his locker room, after the pay-per-view. He begins to speak.) Brown: Well, AEWA, what do you think? Even though my ability has been here for some time now, my persona, Leroy Brown, has finally arrived! Now, the fans are gonna get what they want-- great wrestling! I have won the ACEW Championship Title TWO TIMES, so I know what it takes to be a champion. Justin Trouble, you'd better watch out, because you will be the first to feel the power of Leroy Brown. It seems that the match "big wigs" here in the AEWA know what power I have, and they have given me a shot at the North American Title! Trouble, that's kinda ironic, because when you step into the ring with me, they will call you "Just In Trouble!" Now, I'd like to introduce you all to my manager. Some of you know him from ACEW, some of you know him as an old-time champion from leagues gone bye, he's a great man and he loves the fans-- here is Street King! (Street King walks into the room, wearing a white shirt and ripped jeans. He is an older fellow, but still tough looking. He is carrying a lead pipe.) Street King: Ah, not only has Leroy arrived, but I also have arrived, with my trusty lead pipe! This pipe has bashed some of the worst rulebreakers in ACEW history, and now it's gonna break some skulls in AEWA! You know why? Because the fans love it! (smiles) And because the fans love Leroy Brown! Brown: You're right, big guy. And if you fans have any doubts about me, just wait until I win the North American title, then make your decision. You'll decide that I am the one you want to cheer for-- "Big Bad" Leroy Brown! (Fade to black) ---------- KW: This stinks. That guy wrestled under a mask as someone I thought I might actually like, and now he turns out to be another goody-goody who kisses those moron fans' butts. CC: Aren't you being a little harsh, Ken? I mean there's no denying that he's a great wrestler, and he has reigned over ACEW on two occasions. He's a great addition to the AEWA's already deep roster. KW: I don't care. The point is, he tricked me. I liked him, and now he's gotta be that moron, Leroy Brown. CC: Then don't cheer for him, it's as simple as that. KW: I don't know if I can get over this.... CC: While I console Ken on his loss, why don't you take a look at these words from Sewerdog. ---------- (We see Sewerdog working out in a gym, still wearing his mask-- it's like it never comes off. After a few reps, he sits up and stares at the camera.) Sewerdog: You know, it's about damn time I finally got another match. Unfortunately it's with some rookie who's trying to prove himself in the AEWA. Well, kid, welcome to where the big boys really do play. Who talked you into this match? Didn't they tell you that I have to be thrown through a table and bleeding in order for me to lose? Do you have a death wish or something? You should fight one of those losers like Super Blonde or Sir Gallade, then maybe you can have a WINNING debut. (he leans back and starts lifting weights again) And just remember, Mr. Steele, like the song says-- I AM HELL! (fade to black) ---------- KW: Sewerdog looks ready for his match against Damian Steele, the highly touted newcomer from WWEW. That one should be a great matchup. CC: Indeed it should, but don't you think that Sewerdog is taking Steele just a BIT lightly? KW: That's the way you gotta think in this business. You gotta think that there's nobody in the WORLD who can beat you. I'm sure Steele thinks the same thing. CC: You may just be right. And now we've got an interview that was recently taped during a local house show. Hank Thompson caught up with the new AEWA North American Champion, Justin Trouble. ---------- (The camera opens on Hank Thompson in the middle of the ring, with a jam-packed crowd at hand. He waves to the fans, smiles, and begins to speak.) Thompson: Alright fans, I have a VERY special interview for you! I'm about to bring him out, a good friend of mine and yours, please welcome the NEW AEWA North American Champion, JUSTIN TROUBLE!! (Tom Petty's "Makin' Some Noise" blasts loudly over the PA as Justin Trouble walks down the aisle, wearing a black leather jacket, with the North American Championship around his waist. The fans go absolutely nuts. Trouble slaps hands with the fans, and two beautiful women jump over the barricade and hug him. He smiles, and talks with them until security comes down and escorts them back to their seats. Trouble gets into the ring and shakes hands with Thompson.) Thompson: First off, congratulations are in order. Justin, you did what seemed to be the impossible by beating Big Ron Raider for the North American title. That's gotta feel good. Trouble: Well, I'll tell you, Hank; every bone, muscle, and ligament in my body is in agony, but it's a good feeling. And buddy, I've finally proven it! Thompson: Proven what? Trouble: That Tom Cruise isn't the only one that could pull off Mission Impossible, because I did it at December Doom! Thompson: (laughs) You sure did. I have to say, though, I thought Raider had you beat when he Raider-Bombed you. Previously, no one had ever kicked out after the move had been applied. How'd you do it? Trouble: Well Hank, it's like this-- You gotta want it, and I did. I reached deep inside and pulled out those reserves, and I did what it took to win the North American title. But let's pause on my winning the NA title for a second. (pulls out a shiny metal object out of his jacket and shows it to Hank) Do you know what this is? Thompson: It appears to be a padlock. Trouble: That's because it is, Hank. After my match, I went back to the locker room to celebrate. I was watching Bob's interview on TV when I heard a kind of clanking noise at my door. I went to door and tried to open it, and I couldn't. It was then that I heard the laugh.. Thompson: Laugh? Trouble: Yes, a distinct laugh. Next thing I know, Freeman's getting attacked, and I can't do anything about it. Then I figured out where I'd heard that laugh before. Thompson: Where? Trouble: Out of Larkin's mouth. The whole attack on Freeman was premeditated, but let me tell you something right now.... Larkin, I know you and your Visigothic Whores are listening, (Hank turns red like he's about to laugh, but holds it in) so here it is. I challenge your boys to a tag team match. A one time deal. Thompson: Justin, who will your partner be? Trouble: If he'll accept, and I hope he will, because he owes Larkin and his thugs one too.... "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews, I want YOU to be my partner against Larkin and his boys. We may not agree on anything, or even like each other, but you know what? We respect each other. Larkin, if you listen to anything I say, listen to this, because payback's a b*tch! (fans go nuts) Thompson: Alright fans, we're out of time, so see you at Saturday Night Slam! (fade to black) ---------- KW: Justin Trouble want's to team with SHANE MATTHEWS? CC: It is an unusual pairing, but they do both have scores to settle with the Horde, so it makes sense, sort of. KW: I don't think Matthews will agree to this. I mean, why would he want to team with a pansy like Justin Trouble? CC: He is NOT a pansy, he is our North American Champion, and he deserves some respect. Shane can trust Justin, but is the reverse true-- can Justin trust Shane? KW: We'll find out if Shane accepts this offer. CC: We certainly will, and now let's hear from the man who was the victim of the Horde while Justin was locked in his dressing room at December Doom, AEWA President Bob Freeman. ---------- (The camera opens on President Freeman, sitting alone in his office. He is wearing a neck brace, and appears to be in a great deal of pain. He manages to force a smile out, and suddenly begins to yell at the camera....) President Freeman: JUSTIN! What the hell happened at December Doom, Justin? You were supposed to be watching my back. The Visigothic Horde came out from behind, slapped me around, and power bombed me through a table. AND WHERE WERE YOU, JUSTIN? You were too busy celebrating with your buddies, sucking up the champagne, huh? I'm really disappointed in you, buddy. I gave you just a LITTLE bit of responsibility in this federation, and you BLEW it. While I was getting my ASS kicked, you were probably in the back of a limo with two blondes. Now that you're the North American champion, Justin, does that make you TOO COOL to do your job? (shakes his head in disgust) Apparently it does. I hope your happy with that title, because you sure as HELL have lost your job as my bodyguard. I never want to see you anywhere NEAR my office again. In fact, Justin, I'm going to punish you for this.... (pauses) Due to your irresponsibility, you are temporarily FIRED from the AEWA. I'm sorry to be so harsh about this, but it's really my only choice. I hired you to PROTECT me, and you messed up; big time. Please quit making your lame excuses, just accept the consequences. You're still the North American champion, so don't worry, but from this day forward, you're not going to be getting any paychecks from the AEWA. I still expect you to defend the title, but that's ALL. Maybe someday, after you've learned some reliability, I'll rehire you. But until that day comes, you're no longer an employee of the American E-Wrestling Association. I'm sorry.... Ladies and gentlemen, I'd like to apologize for my rudeness just now, but I had to get that off my chest. Next, I'd like to send a little warning to Kevin Larkin. (stands up, and slams his fist against the desk) Kevin, you've finally sunk too low. I knew you hated me, but I never DREAMED you would do something like this. You sent the Visigothic Horde to attack me, they did, and now you're laughing about it behind my back. You make me SICK, Kevin. I run this federation, and there's NO PLACE for you in it! I'm going to make a little challenge for you, Kevin. Bring your damn Visigothic Horde to Kickoff '97, and I'll bring a team of my own. If my guys win, you have to leave the AEWA forever. If YOUR goons win, you can have full control of the entire federation. It's your choice, big mouth; put up or shut up. I'm the PRESIDENT of this league, but you're forcing me to act like a wrestler. I'm going to make you PAY for this, Kevin.... Watch your back, because the Larkin-Freeman war is almost over. After Kickoff '97, there will only be ONE leader in the AEWA. It's going to be me. I'll see you there, Kevin. (Fade to black.) ---------- KW: Can you BELIEVE that? Justin Trouble was just FIRED! Isn't that cool! CC: No, it's not. Apparently, President Freeman doesn't buy into Justin's "locked up" explanation, and now Justin is restricted from doing anything but defending his title. I guess that leaves his proposed match with Matthews against the Horde out of the question. Don't you think this is going a little far? KW: No, not far enough. Justin should be stripped of his title, too. CC: I'll never get anywhere with you. And what about Freeman's challenge to Larkin? Who do you think that he's got to counter the Horde? KW: I don't know.... The Flying Nuns? CC: God, I hope not. Anyway, this is obviously a very volatile situation, and it's only gonna get worse from here on in. It should make things very interesting in the weeks to come, however. But right now, let's get on with the show. Here's some comments from the Assassins. ---------- (Banshee and Shockwave are standing in an empty ring. They wear no masks, and have smiles on their faces.) Banshee: Once again, The Assassins proved to everyone that they can still fight with the big boys! We beat the NATO Powers, one of the best teams in AEWA history! Shockwave: That victory was just the start of our new rise to the top of the AEWA! We want to issue a challenge to the Shadow Warriors, now! Banshee: What do you two guys have to lose by fighting us? You can only move up in the tag ranks if you beat us, and if you lose, which you will, you can still work on that "conspiracy theory" of yours! Shockwave: It's time for The Assassins to get their World Tag Team Titles back! We will mow over every tag team in the AEWA if we have to! Banshee: So how about it, Shadow Warriors? Are you up to the challenge of The Assassins?! (The camera fades to black) ---------- KW: Are these guys SERIOUS? The Shadow Warriors would mop the floor with them. They're former National Tag Team Champions, you know. CC: I'm well aware of that, but I believe you're forgetting that the Assassins are former World Tag Team Champions. It would be a great matchup to say the least, and the Assassins have a good shot at winning. KW: Maybe the OLD Assassins would stand a chance of winning, but this team hasn't done ANYTHING since they had that change of heart quite a while back. It'll be a squash, trust me. CC: I wouldn't trust you as far as I could throw you. And now let's get a word with the new AEWA World Tag Team Champions, The Machines. ---------- (Matt Wilson and The Lovely Bertha stand outside the arena just after December Doom. A crowd of fans are standing around. The doors opens, and the Machines walk out in their normal clothes. Paul is covered with bruises, and Simon's left eye is still closed, but both are grinning. They are holding their AEWA World Tag Team Titles above their heads. Paul starts walking by the crowd, trying to stop and shake everybody's hand. To the amazement of everyone, Simon joins him. Finally, they go up to Matt and Bertha for an interview. Paul shakes the hands of both; Simon shakes Matt Wilson's hand, but gives the Lovely Bertha a kiss.) MW: We'd like a quick word, Simon.... (Simon's a little busy, so Matt turns to Paul, who gladly takes the microphone.) Paul: Thanks, everybody. Simon and I are heading for a celebration, and everyone's invited. (the crowd cheers) But first, I want to say a few words. I know I haven't been acting very well lately. I've been losing my temper a lot, and not acting very unprofessional. Well, I want to apologize to everyone for that, and I plan on making up for it. Besides, I'm going to leave the rulebreaking to the professionals. (points to Simon) Simon and I are going to best the greatest tag team champions in the AEWA. We're going to take on every team, and beat all of them. Simon.... Hey, Simon, let up. You're on your way to a fourth divorce there. (Simon breaks off his kiss of the Lovely Bertha, and takes the microphone.) Simon: When Paul and I first started teaming together, everyone said we wouldn't last. Most people gave us a week. Well, look where we are. The best tag team in the best federation in all of wrestling. We said that we were the best, and now we've proven it. And folks, listen up, because I won't ever say it again-- thanks for following us. Now, on to the celebration!! (They leave, with several members of the crowd following them.) ---------- KW: Looking at those guys with the belts just makes me sick! The West Coast Connection were ROBBED, plain and simple. The Machines weren't even supposed to BE in that match! CC: But Bob Freeman PUT them in that match, and they won. It's official, so learn to live with it. KW: I won't have to live with it for long. The Connection weren't ready for the Machines, and when they get their hands on them in a fair match, the titles are coming back to where they belong. The WCC are gonna tear the Machines apart, count on it. CC: Don't listen to Ken, people, he's just babbling. It should be a classic encounter if and when these two teams meet again, and either team could come out wearing the gold. We'll just have to see what happens. And now, for the first time in the AEWA, let's hear from Damian Steele. --------- (The camera opens on a white backdrop that has "AEWA" on it in reddish-orange colors. At first, that's all there is, but then Damian "The Predator" Steele walks into the shot, with his blonde hair resting behind his ears. He cracks his famous cynical half-smile as his dark blue eyes stare into the camera. He wears a blue pair of jeans and a black vest, and you can't see his feet. He then begins to speak.) Damian Steele: Hello, my dear and wonderful fans. I've been informed that I'm FINALLY making my debut here in the AEWA. I'm so excited! I can't wait. Mr. Sewerdog, or whatever your name is, I wish you the best of luck in our match, because you're going to need it! Not only am I going to hit you with everything I've got, and THEN some, but I'm going to have the FANS behind me, which will surely lead me to victory! I'm tellin' ya, I'm a changed man, and I like the new me. If anyone doesn't.... well.... talk to the fans about that! And hey, Caretaker, where the hell are you, man? I wanna join your stable. The Knights of Deliverance, is that it? I wanna help you get revenge on Deathwatch and the rest of those ICU thugs. There's no room in wrestling for these losers. C'mon, decide whether you want me to join. I'm waiting! (grins) (fade to black) ---------- KW: What? THE PREDATOR has gone soft and sided with the fans? What is this world coming to? CC: I don't know. The change of heart does appear rather strange, but I suppose anything's possible. This should be a VERY interesting situation to follow. KW: Yeah. I guess that Sewerdog's just gonna have to kick his butt and show him the RIGHT way. CC: We'll see. And now let's get a word with our newly crowned TV Champion, Sir Gallade. This interview was taped at a recent house show. ---------- (Hank Thompson is standing in the center of the ring, holding a microphone. He turns to the camera and smiles.) Thompson: Lady and Gentlemen, please welcome the NEW AEWA Television Champion, Sir Gallade! (The British National Anthem plays as Sir Gallade receives a huge crowd pop. He walks down the aisle, high fiving the fans.) Thompson: First, I would like to congratulate you on winning the Television Title. That was an EXCELLENT match. Sir Gallade: Thank you, my friend, but I must admit-- it was not easy to win it. Randall and I had one of the greatest matches of all time, do you all agree? (a huge crowd pop) I give Randall, my partner, a round of applause, because he is one of the greatest wrestlers I have ever fought. Thompson: You will have a lot of upcoming matches, of course. You know that you will be a marked man now. Sir Gallade: One thing is certain-- I cannot lose as long as I have my fans behind me. (crowd pop) I have matches lined up already, so bring them all on! I won't back down one bit. Like I have said before, I back down from no man! Thompson: There have been rumors going around that you were offered a spot with the Angels of Death. Is that true, and if so, will you join them? Sir Gallade: Do I look like a man who would stoop so low as to join the Angels of Death? I don't think so! You see, Hank, to be in the Angels of Death, you need to have an IQ of a bloody hamster! That is what I think of the Angels of Death; they are just a bunch of trouble-making morons. Thompson: According to a rumor on the AEWA web site, you supposedly met with Rick Verrano backstage a few weeks ago. Sir Gallade: That is true. He came up to me and started rambling on about how the Angels of Death are short on wrestlers, and then he offered me a spot and a new car. I refused, of course; I just walked away and have not thought of it since. I do not want to hear anymore about that from anybody! Angels of Death, ICU, the Church, and anyone else, watch your backs because Sir Gallade and the Knights of Deliverance are out for JUSTICE! (The British National Anthem plays and Sir Gallade receives a huge crowd pop as he walks back to the dressing room.) ---------- KW: PLEASE, SOMEBODY beat this weenie, or I just might go insane from listening to him spout this crap off! CC: Oh calm down, Ken. Sir Gallade is a GREAT champion, and he should defend the pride of the AEWA well. I think it's great. KW: Well your opinion doesn't count, because you're a moron. CC: Gee thanks, I love you too, Ken. KW: Just shut up! CC: And now let's get a word with Scorpion. ---------- (The camera opens on Scorpion in the AEWA locker room. He wears his mask and wrestling tights, and Johnny Terror is at his side.) Scorpion: That was complete BULLSH*T! Heavy Metal, you outweigh me by like a MILLION pounds, you fat piece of SH*T, and you can't even give me a fair shot at the match. I call that fear, buddy! I mean did you REALLY need to attack ME from behind, before the bell? I didn't even have time to get my vest off! Terror: To think, the big, bad Heavy Metal is SO scared of the puny little Scorpion that he can't even give him a fair match. I thought you were a tough guy, Heavy Metal. I thought WRONG. You're nothing but a tub of lard who uses his weight to push people around. If you had given this guy here a fair match, he would've cleaned your clock, and you KNOW it. Scorpion: My first Pay-Per-View match, and you have to go and be a jackass and not even give me a chance. Well that's the LAST time anybody gets the jump on me. I'm gonna be ready for action-- anytime, anyplace, anywhere. I just hope you are too, Heavy Metal, because it's not over. Not by a longshot. Terror: That's right, Heavy Metal. Wherever you go, whatever you do, Scorpion will be right there, waiting for you. Now you may recognize that line, but it kinda takes on a whole new meaning now, doesn't it? You'd better not turn your back for a second, because this man is just waiting for an opportunity. Scorpion: And in the meantime, I want some competition! I don't care who he is, or where he's from, it makes no difference to the Scorpion. Just step into the ring, and prepare to be to meet your doom! I may be small, but my sting is DEADLY. You may have thought that at December Doom you had seen the last of the Scorpion. Nothing could be further from the truth, for it is only the beginning of my rise through the AEWA ranks, one wrestler at a time. (fade to black) ---------- KW: Scorpion STILL wants a piece of Heavy Metal after that SQUASHING at December Doom? Is he psycho? CC: Perhaps, and perhaps he's just confident, but I think it's a little bit of both. KW: Probably, but he can still be a threat to Heavy Metal if he's going to continue to attack him, as he indicated he might. It should be a very interesting situation. CC: Indeed it should. And now for our final interview, let's hear from the AEWA World Heavyweight Champion, Shane Matthews. ---------- (The scene opens on Shane Matthews, sitting in a hot tub with several women. He is talking to them and the girls are giggling like crazy. Shane notices the camera, and begins to speak.) Matthews: Well hello there, AEWA. It took you long enough to get here. I called for that camera crew, what, THREE DAYS ago? It don't matter though, now's as good a time as any to talk to y'all. Well, I came through on my promise, and I beat Slocum right in the center of the ring, right in front of his mamma. I bet she was so proud. She got to see her son STOMPED by the one and only "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews. But after the match is another story. What the hell were you doing there, Osbourne? I thought I already kicked your ass. Guess you didn't learn. Guess I'll have to do it again. You think you can get your hands on that belt, Osbourne? You're DREAMING. You couldn't even beat Sir Gatorade-- how the hell do ya expect to beat me? We'll get it on, and when we do, you're gonna wish that you were STILL handcuffed to that railing I handcuffed ya to before, because it's gonna be a HUNDRED times worse. You're gonna be SCREAMING for mercy, and you won't get any. This time, Osbourne, you're not gonna be able to leave under your own power. You're gonna experience Sudden Death. And now coming up this weekend, we got this fool Deranged who can't even figure out what side he's on. Well Deranged, you been BEGGING for a match with me for so long, now you're finally gonna get it, and remember you brought this on yourself. And if ya still can't figure out which side you're on, I'll tell ya which side you're gonna BE on-- THE BOTTOM! The bottom of the winning pinfall, after you hit the end of the road. I've had a long and tough road to the top, and I ain't about to be giving up this belt to some freak like you. Shane Matthews RUNS this federation, and don't you forget it. And now if you'll excuse me, I've got some, uh.... deep sea diving to do. (Matthews' head goes down underwater and the girls begin to giggle again as the image fades to black.) ---------- KW: That looks like some rigorous training that Matthews is going through for his match with Deranged. I wish I could be there to uh.... train with him. CC: I'm sure you do, but that kind of "training" is gonna get Matthews beat if that's all he's doing. You can't afford to take ANYONE lightly here in the AEWA, and especially not someone as experienced as Deranged. KW: Shane Matthews can take any man he wants lightly, because he's the man, and nobody can beat him. CC: Nobody's invincible, even Shane Matthews. Just like all champions, his time will come. KW: We'll see. CC: Yes we will. And that's all the time we have for tonight, fans. For my broadcast partner, Ken Wilson, this is Chuck Carlson saying see ya next time!
