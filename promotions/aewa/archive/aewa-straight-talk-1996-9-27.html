Straight Talk (9/27)

30 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera fades in on the AEWA Studios, the AEWA logo falls onto the screen, and the words "STRAIGHT TALK" slide in underneath it. The whole logo then tilts, and falls off the bottom of the screen. Behind the desk in studio are Chuck Carlson and Ken Wilson.) CC: Hello fans, and welcome to yet another episode of AEWA Straight Talk. As always, I am Chuck Carlson, and this is my co-host Ken Wilson. Say hello, Ken. KW: Hello, Ken. CC: Well, nobody ever said that he was our brightest broadcaster. KW: I did. CC: I know, but you don't count. And now, to kick off the show, let's get some comments from a 2-time former TV Champion, Deranged. ---------- (Deranged is sitting in an office of his own, where he is at work for the Wrestling Resource center.) Deranged: McTeague, I see you have the TV Title. Well guess what-- I want it back! I am tired of all this with Heavy Metal. I beat him once and I think that right there made history! President Freeman, if you are listening, I want McTeague as soon as possible. Or whomever the TV Champion is. This time the fans will be with me and give me the strength, power, and anything else I need to bring that belt back. The TV Championship is for the fans and that is what I am all about. McTeague, you are marked man and I am coming to get you. You'd better enjoy that TV title, because the Big Man is coming back to get it! This time, it will go nowhere once in my grasp! (Deranged continues to work, and we fade to black) ---------- KW: Deranged HONESTLY thinks he deserves a shot after getting his ass whipped TWICE by Heavy Metal? Is he nuts? CC: No, he's not. Heavy Metal IS the World Champion, and there's no shame in losing to him. And if you remember correctly, Deranged DID pin Heavy Metal in the "2-out-of-3 falls" encounter that they had, and in only twenty-two seconds. I think that kind of performance warrants a shot at another title, don't you? KW: No. CC: Why do I waste time making rational arguments with you? KW: I have no idea. CC: Let's just watch this interview with the wrestler formerly known as Red Wolf, The Amazon Monster. ---------- (As the scene opens, we see the Amazon Monster in front of his house with Hank Thompson. Amazon Monster is wearing a sports shirt and Dockers pants. He looks very angry) Thompson: I am here with the Amazon Monster. Amazon, ever sense you wrestled Dennis Windham, we haven't seen you in the ring much. What happened? Will we ever see you wrestle again? Amazon Monster: (he seems to be laughing to himself) Hmm.... Well, nobody seems to want to get in the ring with me. I think that the wrestlers here in the AEWA are afraid of me. I have been here in the AEWA for a long time, and I have faced a lot of people, but for some reason, I never got a title match. I work hard, I win matches, and I have only lost once. I keep asking for a title match-- well, this time, I AM DEMANDING A TITLE MATCH! Thompson: Who will you face? Amazon Monster: I don't care! As long as they have a belt around their waist, I don't care who it is! Thompson: Do you feel that you have to send a message to the other wrestlers? Amazon Monster: NO! I feel that I have to send a message to the announcers and the President! (Without any warning, the Amazon Monster reaches over and slaps Hank Thompson. He spits on Thompson, then grabs the microphone) Was that a clear enough message, AEWA? I hope so! You'd better give me some title shots, or this will happen again! (As we fade to black, we see the Amazon Monster chase Hank Thompson into the darkness.) ---------- KW: That was GREAT! It's about time someone gave Thompson a good smack upside the head. I wonder how 'ol Hank got out of that one. CC: I don't know, but I'm sure he's alright. KW: Sure he is, but let's get to the point-- why HASN'T the Amazon Monster been given a title shot? He's certainly been around long enough and has won enough matches. CC: That's an interesting point, and to tell you the truth, I really don't know the answer. You'd have to ask the AEWA Championship Committee. KW: I already know the answer; it's because those morons who run this federation wouldn't know talent if it jumped up and bit them in the ass. That's why he hasn't gotten a shot. CC: I don't think so. Let's just get a word with the West Coast Connection. ---------- (We see the West Coast Connection standing in front of a large building, that looks like a dormitory at some college.) MC Read: Hey, punks, we haven't had interview time in a while, and we have some things to say. Right now, we are outside the Wesleyan University Radio Station, ready to go in and do an interview, and spread the West Coast feelings out to you suckas on the East Coast. Axl Sharkman: But first, we want to say that the matches we lost because of the interference of our two managers here, are forgotten. We know even they make mistakes. But, this weekend, when we face The Crew, we will be cautious, because things do happen. But we will also be just like we always have been, and that is dominating all the punks who think they are better than we are. (They walk into the station, and we fade to black) ---------- KW: I love it! The West Coast Connection is the top team in professional wrestling today, and anybody who thinks otherwise is gonna get stomped! CC: That theory will certainly be put to the test when they lock up with the Crew this weekend. KW: And it will hold true, too. It's only a matter of time before those guys wear the belts again. CC: We'll see. Now let's hear from Joey Valachi. ---------- ("The Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi is standing in the middle of a forest, wearing a flannel shirt, carrying a huge ax, and looking extremely stupid.) Valachi: A lumberjack match. Put a bunch of guys around the ring, stick me and the Flying Mongolian in the ring... Cameraman: HE'S CALLED THE FLYING MEXICAN, JOEY! Valachi: Whatever. Just Lopez, me, and the baddest boys in the AEWA to keep his hired goon squad off me. I know the ILCA is going to be in the building, and if Lopez tries to bring them to ringside, I'll know he was in league with them. But this time, I'm one step of ahead of EVERYBODY! With the lumberjacks I selected, those thugs aren't getting anywhere near me. And I remember all the trouble Lopez has caused me since he showed up in the AEWA. This is the first real chance I've got to put him out of my hair for good. After this lumberjack chops him down, he'll be going back to Hungary in a box. Cameraman: MEXICO! He's from MEXICO, for Pete's sake! Valachi: Whatever. Get ready to go the way of the spotted owl, Lopez. (At this point, Joey starts singing to the tune of the "Lumberjack Song" from Monty Python.) Oh, I'm a lumberjack and I'm OK! I'll beat his French butt Saturday! Cameraman: He's not French! He's.... oh, why do I bother? Valachi: The mob gets screwed, I get my win, you'll see it on TV! And then my belt will come back, to where it's supposed to be! But until I do get my belt back, I'll need a proxy. Good thing I never threw this away. (Valachi pulls out the toy WWF belt that he carried around when he first started in the AEWA. He raises the belt high, and celebrates alone in the forest. Fade to black.) ---------- KW: Valachi DESPERATELY needs to win this match. It could finally get the Italian Loan Collection Agency off his back for good, and he can stop all this silly hiding and disguises. CC: Unfortunately, his behavior is purely genetic, and will likely remain the same. KW: Not funny. But it'll be VERY interesting to see what happens in this one, don't you think? By the way, I was impressed that Valachi knows the "Lumberjack Song"-- do you think that he does the rest of the things described in that song? CC: Let's hope not. Here's an interview with the National Tag Team Champions, the Shadow Warriors. ---------- (The scene opens with Wesley Cantrell, the Shadow Warriors and Lianne down at the Cantrell Training Centre.) Ladd: Machines? WHO THE HELL ARE THE MACHINES? I have never heard of them and still they are scheduled to get a beating. I suppose the commissioners must really have a hard time finding worthy opponents for us. Cantrell: They shouldn't be any kind of problem at all, since they can't really keep their minds on the current match they are wrestling anyway. All I have ever seen them do is fight among themselves over who is the worst of them. I could easily settle that problem-- they BOTH stink! Slade: Not that it really matters, 'cause we have never really cared who our opponents are, as long as they step in the ring, they are the prey and we are the predators. That's the way it's always been and that's the way it's gonna stay. (turns to the camera) You Machines had better reserve a couple of good spots at the local hospital, 'cause that's where me and Aaron are gonna put you before this is over. Lianne: Those overdressed chickens will never know what hit them, and that's probably for the best anyway. Cantrell: You know that if it wasn't for my bad back, I could beat up both of those narrow-minded freaks myself. Still, it's almost as much fun seeing them become totally humiliated over and over again by the Shadow Warriors. Hey, isn't life great? Ladd: Always has and always will be, just as long as you aren't one of those unfortunate souls getting in the way of the Cantrell Corporation's plans. The Machines will definitely experience pain, the hard way. Their style may intimidate others, but we couldn't care less. We are just out there to hurt them, and that's what we do best. Lianne: I wonder if they could have ever imagined what they got themselves in to when they decided to try and steal the titles from their rightful owners? I guess not, but soon they will-- that's for sure. Cantrell: The Cantrell Corporation has always made headlines across the world, and this time will be no different. So please, all of you worthless beggars who think you can afford the tickets, crawl out of the sewer systems where you are hiding and go and get all of your tramps and paupers and prepare for the worst onslaught you have ever faced. Now get out of our faces! Slade: The simple fact is that we are the best tag team the AEWA has ever seen. The Shadow Warriors have survived far worse opponents and crisis than you could ever dream of, so this is simply another day at the office for us. Just make sure you are there when we storm through you. (fade to black) ---------- KW: I love it! The Shadow Warriors are the top team in professional wrestling today, and anybody who thinks otherwise is gonna get stomped! CC: Didn't you JUST say that about the West Coast Connection? KW: No, I JUST said it about the Shadow Warriors. CC: No, I mean earlier! KW: I have no idea what you're talking about, man. CC: Oh nevermind! Let's just watch these comments from Sewerdog ---------- (The camera pans around an empty alley on a rainy night, and then zooms in on Sewerdog walking towards it. He's wearing his usual ripped jeans, mask, a brown leather jacket and a T-shirt that says "R.I.P. Heavy Metal") Sewerdog: Hey, Heavy Metal, like my new shirt? (he opens up his jacket and starts posing with it) I got the idea from the Graveyard Shift. So, you finally decided to stand up like a man and fight instead of ignore me. Your mistake. We're gonna have some fun on Saturday night, and I must warn you. It's going to hurt. (fade to black) ---------- KW: So FINALLY this feud is gonna be settled in the ring! I can't wait for this one. CC: Me neither, Heavy Metal has been attacked by Sewerdog TWICE now, and it's about time that they got it on legally. KW: And when they do, all Hell is gonna break lose. CC: That's for sure. Now let's hear from the Machines. ---------- (The scene in The Machine's workout area. "THE ASSASSINS" have been added to their Wall of Conquests. Simon O'Neal is on the phone, and appears very agitated. Paul Wong speaks to the camera.) Paul: We'd just like to take a moment and thank everyone who has supported us up until now. We're getting ready for our shot against the Shadow Warriors, and plan on taking their titles. Simon: (to the phone) You're asking for ANOTHER alimony hearing?! The last one was only three years ago.... Paul: We've been wrestling for several years now, and it's finally our chance to prove that we are the best tag team out there. That is, as long as Simon isn't distracted by his problems with his ex-wife. Simon: (slams down the phone, then joins Paul up front) Of course, it doesn't help that my so-called partner is still friends with that she-devil. Did you tell her about my other bank account? Paul: Forget about that, Simon. We have the Shadow Warriors coming up. Our big title shot-- remember? Simon: Of course. Shadow Warriors, you're very good. But we're better, and we're going to own those AEWA National Tag Team Belts. Hell, I've already started preparing for our victory celebration. The local bar has a great deal-- rent twelve strippers for the price of ten. (Paul and Simon start arguing over "Appropriate Behavior" as the camera fades) ---------- KW: Boy, is that Paul a weenie or what? CC: No, he's not. Simon could learn a thing or two from him. KW: Yeah, he can take one look at Paul and realize that nice guys finish last. Paul's the living proof. CC: Paul hasn't lost a match yet in the AEWA. KW: But SIMON'S the one who has won all their matches for him. CC: And his methods will come right back to him sooner or later. And now let's get a word from Simon O'Neal's most recent victims of deception, The Assassins. ---------- (Banshee and Shockwave are both standing in a darkened room. Instead of their usual masks, each are wearing red headbands. They both have VERY angry looks on their faces, and Shockwave is quickly swinging around a pair of nunchucks.) Banshee: On Saturday, a travesty was committed against the Assassins. We were cheated out of a win all because of the cheating tactics of the Machines. Simon, you used that chain on me, and everybody saw it. It's just that you've pulled the wool over your partner's eyes. You made him think that WE were the ones with the weapons, and WE were the cheaters. Well, I don't think that will EVER happen again, because we want to challenge you two to a "no disqualification, no countout, weapons match". You have shown us something, Machines, you have shown us that no matter how honorable and respectful we are, some of our opponents will not respect or honor us. Now it's payback time. Shockwave: Machines, be ready for us! We tried to wrestle you fairly, but you insisted on cheating. We had you clearly beaten, but Banshee got punched right in the head with that chain. Well, this time, its not gonna be us that gets their asses kicked. You both better be ready for us! (Camera fades to black as Shockwave continues to swing the nunchucks fast and furiously.) ---------- KW: Why can't they just accept the fact that they lost? Banshee was pinned right in the middle of the ring! CC: After being clocked with a chain. KW: And your point is? CC: If you didn't pick it up already, you're beyond help. KW: I know, ain't it great? CC: Yeah, just lovely. Now let's get some comments from "Slim" Jim Coolridge. ---------- (We see "Slim" Jim Coolridge in the locker room, and he looks very angry.) Coolridge: Damnit! Damnit! Damnit! I'm a hell of a lot better wrestler than that! I'm not going to lay down again. The past is done with. Osbourne, I was the better wrestler and you know it. Kingpin, you can scout on your own stinkin' time. When I'm wrestling, you WILL stay away from the ring and let the MAN take care of business. Evidently, you didn't take me seriously. You're next on my hit list, boy! If you're going to start something with me, I'm gonna finish it! (fade to black) ---------- KW: You can't HONESTLY tell me he's gonna blame this one on Kingpin. What the hell did HE do? Does Kingpin not have a right to scout the competition that his men could possibly be facing in the near future? CC: He certainly does, but he could do that from a monitor in the locker room. Perhaps his presence caused Coolridge to lose his concentration. KW: If having people watching him is gonna cause him to lose his concentration, then he's in the wrong damn sport. I just think that he's so upset about losing that match, and finding out that he's NOT the greatest wrestler in the world, and he's gotta blame it on something. Kingpin was a convenient excuse. CC: It goes a little further than THAT. I'm sure Jim doesn't mind people watching him, but Kingpin is another story. Kingpin has been known to interfere in matches, and his presence would certainly put anybody on edge. KW: Excuses, Excuses. CC: Let's just watch this interview with Marcus McTeague. ---------- (Marcus McTeague is in a gym, riding a stationary bike. After a few moments, he gets off. He's breathing quite heavily.) McTeague: So I'm facing someone called Super Blonde. Well, come on then, we'll see what you've got. Just keep in mind that you'll be facing the hardest working and toughest TV champ the AEWA has ever seen. A lot of people have been saying I don't deserve to hold the title, and others have been demanding title shots. You can all talk as much as you want. It won't change the fact that if you face me, I will be forced to take you down. If I lose fairly, I can accept that. It's happened before, and will no doubt happen again. As soon as I lose this title, I am setting my sights on Heavy Metal. A lot of people have been trying to take him out, but nobody has just yet. Just leave that to me. (fade to black) ---------- KW: He's challenging Heavy Metal AGAIN? Maybe he should just worry about Stupor-Blonde for now, because Heavy Metal's WAY more than he can handle. CC: The Blonde certainly is a tough opponent, and McTeague certainly should focus his efforts towards him for now, but further down the road, I don't see why he CAN'T face Heavy Metal. It would be an outstanding matchup. KW: Says you. I think it would be a squash. CC: Well you're an idiot. KW: I know you are, but what am I. CC: Can we please grow up? Let's just get a word from Sir Gallade. ---------- (Scene opens to a very ready and determined Sir Gallade, and a very excited Lady Guinevere) Sir Gallade: Oh yeah! Angels of Death, are you ready to get it on? Slocum and I found a third man and we are ready to kick some Angel of Death butt! Lady Guinevere: Come on boys, and I mean BOYS, you wanted it and now your going to get it. Sir Gallade: You three cowards jumped Slocum and myself, and now the odds are even. But what is this I hear about a fourth man in the Angels of Death? Well, bring him on too. I want this match so bad that if anyone who has joined with you three dares come into the ring, he is going to get flattened by "The Guillotine". Now, Grim Reaper, you hit me from behind, and that is an act of cowardice. For that, you will be my main target, so be afraid-- be very afraid. Lady Guinevere: If there is any attempt at outside interference, then believe me, I will let my man know, and they will get what they deserve. (Scene fades to black) ---------- KW: Well Sir Mix-a-lot here.... CC: That's Sir GALLADE. KW: Whatever. As I was saying, Gallade here talks a good game, but when he gets in there with the Angels of Death, he's gonna get flattened like a pancake! CC: That remains to be seen. KW: Just wait. CC: I will. And now for our final interview, let's get some comments from "Superman" Mike Stewart. ---------- (Camera opens on "Superman" Mike Stewart and his girlfriend/manager Michelle Anderson.) Stewart: So Deathwatch wants to have his way in the AEWA. He wants to run around and spit on championship belts and beat our champions up. Deathwatch, somebody's going to stand up for the AEWA and somebody's going to be me. This is my home. This is where I wrestle. I know that Heavy Metal sure as hell isn't going to stand up and defend the federation, so I'll do it. Marcus McTeague, Randall Osbourne, Ben Slocum, and whoever else believes in this federation is welcome to join me in fighting off Deathwatch and whoever he gets to follow his cause. Deathwatch, you may be a "Hulk Hogan NWO-wannabe", but here in the AEWA, we fight back against people like you. You want a piece of me? I'm not hard to find. I'll be right here in the AEWA. (fade to black) ---------- KW: Deathwatch won't have any problem finding him, that's for sure. And then he'll have no problem squashing him like a bug either! CC: I don't think so. Mike Stewart is an outstanding rookie, and I think that he'll go very far in the AEWA. It's about time someone stood up for the AEWA against Deathwatch, and Mike Stewart might just be the guy to put an end to this disgusting tirade. KW: (laughing) That's funny! Did you make that up just now? CC: I'M SERIOUS!!! KW: (bursts uncontrollably into laughter, and actually falls out of his chair laughing. You can hear him yelling from the floor.) THAT'S A GOOD ONE! CC: Well, it looks like that's all I'll be getting out of my colleague for this week, so I guess this is where we end the show. For my laughing hyena of a broadcast partner, Ken Wilson, this is Chuck Carlson saying good night, and see you next week for MORE Straight Talk!
