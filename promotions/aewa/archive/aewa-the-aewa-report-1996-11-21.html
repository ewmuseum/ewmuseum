The AEWA Report (11/21)

24 November 1996

------------------------ ******************* AEWA REPORT ------------------------- ******************* Welcome to the fifth official edition of the AEWA Report! We have plenty of big news to tell you about, so read on.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ RECENT NEWS ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ --SHANE MATTHEWS WINS THE WORLD TITLE! Yes, ladies and gentlemen, the Armageddon is upon us-- "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews has captured the World Heavyweight title. Unfortunately, he didn't do it alone. Near the end of the recent battle royal, Scorpion (the special referee) pulled down the top rope, causing Heavy Metal to get eliminated. Matthews had nothing left to do but toss Brian Rice out, and it was all over. After the battle royal had ended, Scorpion shook hands with Johnny Terror and Matthews; and they left the arena together. --RANDALL OSBOURNE IS THE NEW TV CHAMPION! During a recent episode of Wednesday Night Rumble, Randall Osbourne pulled off an upset victory against Deranged for the Television title. It wasn't exactly a FAIR match, however. The Angels of Death had come to ringside and blind-sided Deranged with a steel chair, while the referee was unconscious. Osbourne went for the cover, not knowing what had happened, and the referee woke up--three seconds later, a new TV champion was crowned. How does Osbourne feel about winning the title in this manner? Look for a special interview with him on the next episode of Straight Talk! --PENANCE TAKES HOME THE LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT STRAP! Penance defeated "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt for the Light Heavyweight championship, on a recent episode of Saturday Night Slam. He finished Hurt off with the "Final Judgment" piledriver, then celebrated with his fellow ICU members in the locker room. Penance will be defending the title later tonight, when he steps into the ring with El Aviadore. --WHO IS THE MASKED YOREL? Since he arrived in the AEWA several weeks ago, the Masked Yorel has been confusing quite a few people. Many wrestling experts seem to "recognize him" from another organization, but no one is exactly sure WHO he is. He does seem oddly familiar, and we've been trying to find out his identity. If we receive any information on this man, we'll let you know. ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ INJURY REPORT ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ "BIG DADDY" MIKE HURT is out with a broken arm. He injured it during his recent match against Penance, and doctors have ordered Hurt to stop wrestling. He plans to return to broadcasting soon. ******************* CHAMPION LIST******************** AEWA World Heavyweight Champion-- "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews AEWA North American Champion-- Ron Raider AEWA Television Champion-- Randall Osbourne AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion-- Penance AEWA World Tag Team Champions-- The Graveyard Shift AEWA National Tag Team Champions-- The Machines *******************THE TOP 10 (SINGLES)**************** 1.
