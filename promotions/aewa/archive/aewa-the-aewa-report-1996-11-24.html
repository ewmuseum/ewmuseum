The AEWA Report (11/24)

26 November 1996

------------------------ ******************* AEWA REPORT ------------------------ ******************* Welcome to the sixth official edition of the AEWA Report! We have plenty of big news to tell you about, so read on.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ RECENT NEWS ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ --KEVIN LARKIN RETURNS; NEW TAG CHAMPS! During the recent tag team triangle match, all hell broke loose. The Graveyard Shift were defending their World tag team titles against Heaven & Hell and the West Coast Connection, but things quickly got out of control. Kevin Larkin appeared at ringside with his Visigothic Horde, who immediately climbed into the ring and attacked the other teams. The original referee left, leaving LARKIN to do the officiating. After several more minutes of utter anarchy, MC Read (of the West Coast Connection) went for a cover on the Mortician-- and Larkin made an incredibly fast count. We had new World tag team champions, but the action continued. Mister Crowley charged after Kevin Larkin, and chased him into a storage room backstage. Larkin was able to barricade the door, and the Visigothic Horde attacked Crowley from behind. It didn't matter, however, because Crowley was somehow able to fend them off. At that point, we had completely run out of TV time, and the show ended. Now, here's what you DIDN'T see on television.... Larkin called 9-1-1 on a phone from inside the storage room, and a few minutes later, the police arrived at the arena. Mister Crowley didn't seem to care, though, and he CONTINUED to pound on the door. The police officers arrested him, and he spent the rest of the night in jail. Cardinal Chaos bailed him out early the next morning, and Crowley was absolutely ENRAGED with Kevin Larkin. We will bring you more information on this situation as it develops, folks! --"CRIMSON" JOE REED JOINS THE AEWA! After two weeks of contract negotiations, "Crimson" Joe Reed has officially joined the AEWA. He reached an agreement with President Freeman several days ago, and will be making his debut against Brian Rice on a future show. Reed is accompanied by his longtime manager, The Lady In Red, and we're looking forward to seeing them both in action! --PAUL WONG GOES BERSERK! On a recent episode of Saturday Night Slam, the Machines were disqualified in their match against Graphic Violence. This time, however, it wasn't Simon O'Neal's fault. Paul Wong caused the disqualification, after nailing both Booze Murphy and Leech Rogers with a steel chair. We have been trying to contact the Machines for a comment on this situation, but they're not answering our calls. ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ INJURY REPORT ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ THE CRAZY CANUCK was rushed to the hospital after his match with Primal Fear on Saturday Night Slam. Primal Fear had executed the Bump Splash, bruising one of the Canuck's ribs. Luckily, the injury is minor, and the Crazy One won't miss any action. ******************* CHAMPION LIST******************** AEWA World Heavyweight Champion-- "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews AEWA North American Champion-- Ron Raider AEWA Television Champion-- Randall Osbourne AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion-- Penance AEWA World Tag Team Champions-- The West Coast Connection AEWA National Tag Team Champions-- The Machines *******************THE TOP 10 (SINGLES)**************** 1.
