Wednesday Night Rumble (9/18)

19 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen, set on fire. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power Bomb, and a look at Mister Crowley DRIVING the head of some guy into the concrete with the Premature Burial! Then the camera switches to the Lovely Bertha, Matt Wilson, and "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt in a packed arena) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Wednesday Night Rumble! We have plenty of great matches scheduled for tonight.... BD: There's going to be some great tag team bouts, and the Holy Ghost is defending his Light Heavyweight title in our main event. LB: What's first, Wilson? MW: Graphic Violence has been trumpeting about their modest success lately, but tonight they face their biggest challenge to date-- three time former world tag team champions, the Graveyard Shift. The Shift were hoping for a suspended ring match with the Visigothic Horde, but we'll hear more about that later. BD: Let's get this match started.... (Leech Rogers and Booze Murphy, with "the Beautiful" Miss Demeanor, come out to a very cold reception from the audience.) MW: These guys are looking really determined tonight, folks. They want to get noticed by our championship committee, so this match is very important. LB: And here come their opponents, the Graveyard Shift.... (The lights dim, the opening chords of "Thriller" blare, and Coffin leads his wrestlers, the Mortician and Grave Dweller out. After posing for their fans, they break into a dead sprint for the ring, and slide under the bottom rope.) MW: They're not wasting any time in there, buddy. The referee won't have much control, so this should be interesting. BD: Let's get ready to rumble.... Graphic Violence (Booze Murphy and Leech Rogers) versus The Graveyard Shift (The Mortician and Grave Dweller) The Grave Dweller went for Leech, while Booze was targetted by the Mortician. A big brawl ensued, and while this is Graphic Violence's preferred genre of match, they were woefully outsized. When order was restored, Dweller and Booze started for their teams, and after about two minutes Booze was flat on his back on the floor. After he dragged himself into the ring, he went to tag Leech, who had a sudden inspiration and decided to share his great idea with his partner-- his idea was that Booze should stay in the ring. Leech's encouragement appeared to work, and Booze resumed his attack on the Grave Dweller, but was quickly rebuffed and got chokeslammed for his troubles. Miss Demeanor dragged him out under the bottom rope for a much-needed respite, and on the count of 9, Booze was back in. Again he went for the tag, and again Leech had a better idea. This time he opined that the Grave Dweller had the same weakness as all men, and Booze was quick to understand. As he went to lock up with the Grave Dweller again, he made the focus of his attack a sharp knee to the groin. Now Leech was aching to get in, and made the tag. Leech went straight to the fallen Grave Dweller with a series of kicks and punches, then progressed to suplexes and even a body slam, which appeared to put a stress on his lower back which his lower back was just not prepared to handle. As Leech staggered to his corner and tagged out, the Grave Dweller did the same in his corner. The Graveyard Shift regained control, and things stayed that way until Fritigern and Athanaric stormed to ringside. As they jumped onto the ring apron, the referee tried to head them off, as did the Mortician (from his corner) and the Dweller (the legal man). When an arguemnt ensued between the 5 men, and shoves were exchanged (which sent Athanaric flying into the guardrail) and a tussel developed, Leech blindsided the Grave Dweller with a knee in the back. He then slapped on his Electric Lock (scorpion deathlock), while Booze stomped away at the Dweller's head. As the Mortician chased off the Visigoths, and Booze ducked back into his corner, the Grave Dweller had no option but to submit, at 12:17. MW: That was the biggest upset in a LONG time! Graphic Violence have defeated the Graveyard Shift, via submission. The tragedy of this is that when the record book is written, that's all it will say, but everyone in this arena or watching on TV at home knows the real story of this match. BD: What are you TALKING about? Graphic Violence EARNED this victory tonight, and I love it! LB: What's next, Wilson? MW: It's time for another tag team match, so let's get down to ringside.... BD: Good idea.... (The Icebreaker and "Birdman" Rob Yvette follow their leader, Ronnie Hoffa, to the ring) MW: These two have been trying to get a hold of the ILCA and Organized Crime for weeks, but neither alliance has been willing to meet them yet. They do get to try their hand at another criminal organization in the AEWA this week, though-- former world champs, the West Coast Connection. BD: This should be a VERY interesting match, buddy. (Cyprus Hill's "Throw Your Set in the Air" marks the arrival of Axl Sharkman and MC Read, with managers DJ Muggs X and Chris Grohl in tow) MW: They look EXTREMELY angry right now, so we might have a bloodbath out here. The AEWA is becoming a VERY dangerous league anymore. BD: And that's exactly how I like it. Here we go.... The Icebreaker and "Birdman" Rob Yvette versus The West Coast Connection (Axl Sharkman and MC Read) This was the first time the Icebreaker faced someone his size, as MC Read at 325 pounds actually outweighed him by 6 pounds. As things got underway, Hoffa waved his cane threateningly at the opposing managers while yelling about his turf. As Grohl and Muggs went to respond, their own wrestlers interceded and ordered them out of the ring, at which point the Icebreaker and Birdman took advantage of the momentary distraction, launching a blind-side attack, and we were off. In the mayhem that ensued, the managers once again got involved, and as Hoffa chased Muggs with his cane, Chris Grohl grabbed a chair and went for the Icebreaker. Sharkman grabbed it from him, tossed it aside, and as he berated his own manager for the attempted chairshot, the Icebreaker grabbed the discarded chair and dented it over Sharkman's head, causing a disqualification just seconds into the match. The West Coast Connection won over the Icebreaker/Birdman by disqualification at 0:16. MW: They went completely berserk! Axl Sharkman REALLY got nailed there, and the referee quickly called for a disqualification. I'm glad that we FINALLY have some good officiating around here! BD: Shut up, Wilson. LB: Yeah! MW: Whatever. Up next, the Holy Ghost is going to defend his Light Heavyweight title against newcomer El Aviadore! BD: WHAT? Why would a newcomer get a title shot? That just doesn't seem very fair, Wilson. MW: El Aviadore is a top star in Mexico, so he wanted to showoff his skills here in the AEWA. LB: But think about it-- this is the AMERICAN E-Wrestling Association! Why would we care about some damn illegal immigrant? MW: (turns to the camera) Ladies and gentlemen, I apologize for the racist views of my colleague here, and her views do not reflect those of the AEWA. LB: If you can't take the heat, stay out of the kitchen. Anyway, let's get down to ringside.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is for the AEWA Light Heavyweight title! ("La Bamba" begins playing loudly, and El Aviadore makes his entrance. He is wearing a blue mask, and looks very determined.) MW: I'm really excited about this guy's debut, folks. He has been winning titles all over the world, so this should be interesting. BD: I still don't like him. (As "Amazing Grace" plays softly in the background, the Holy Ghost comes out. He wears a white mask, with the Light Heavyweight title strapped around his waist.) LB: Now HERE is a good role model! He has been meditating with the other Church members lately, so NO ONE can stop him! MW: Wait a minute-- El Aviadore just nailed the Holy Ghost with an uppercut! He's not wasting any time in there, folks. BD: This is an OUTRAGE! LB: Why doesn't that referee call for a disqualification? El Aviadore just whipped the Holy Ghost to the ropes, and he comes back with a big clothesline! MW: Calm down, Bertha. Now Aviadore is going up top-- AND HE COMES DOWN ON THE HOLY GHOST WITH A FLYING DROPKICK! That was incredible! BD: This rookie is pretty exciting-- and stupid! Those high-risk maneuvers are worthless; he should save his energy. LB: Now El Aviadore climbs the turnbuckle again--AND GOES FOR A FLYING MOONSAULT--BUT THE HOLY GHOST ROLLS AWAY! Aviadore just went crashing to the canvas! MW: Now the Holy Ghost gets back up, and starts kicking him. He looks REALLY angry right now.... BD: He just nailed Aviadore with a kick to the midsection! (As the match continues, Deathwatch walks to ringside. He is wearing an "AEWA Sucks" T-shirt, and has a wild expression on his face.) MW: What's THAT guy doing down here? Why would he be interested in a light heavyweight match? BD: I'm not sure-- this DOES seem rather strange. LB: Anyway, let's get back to the match. The Holy Ghost is STILL kicking El Aviadore, and that must be doing some MAJOR damage! MW: He just bounced off the ropes, and comes down on Aviadore with a big splash! He goes for a cover.... One..Two..kickout BD: El Aviadore just barely got out of that one, and it was pure luck. (turns to ringside) Wait a minute-- Deathwatch just stood up, and he's walking closer to the ring. MW: Why the HELL did he come down here? I really don't understand what this situation is all about. LB: WAIT A MINUTE! Deathwatch has climbed into the ring, and he NAILS the Holy Ghost! What's he doing THAT for? BD: Now he turns around, and grabs El Aviadore by the tights. He lifts him up, AND HE POWER BOMBS HIM! MW: The referee has called for the bell, but this attack isn't over yet. Deathwatch just reached down for the Holy Ghost, and he slams him into the turnbuckle! LB: This is a complete massacre! (screaming) Where the hell is security? BD: Deathwatch runs toward the turnbuckle, AND COMPLETELY CRUSHES THE HOLY GHOST! MW: We finally have some security guards in there now, and they're trying to get this situation under control. Jeez, it took them long enough! BD: They are escorting Deathwatch out of here-- good riddance! That psycho bastard should be PERMANENTLY banned from the AEWA! LB: You know, this is actually starting to make sense. Deathwatch was still pissed off about his recent fine, so he decided to take out his aggression on some little lightweights! This is absolutely pathetic, and I'm disgusted. MW: Some paramedics are in the ring now, and they're trying to help out El Aviadore and the Holy Ghost. Those two guys are both unconscious right now.... BD: This whole attack was COMPLETELY unprovoked. MW: El Aviadore must have REALLY landed on his head after that power bomb, because these paramedics are putting a neckbrace on him. BD: Jeez, I really feel sorry for him. This was his first match ever, and he's already going to the hospital.... that's not a very good start. LB: So what's the official result? MW: This match was declared a "no contest", of course, so the Holy Ghost will keep his Light Heavyweight title-- but he's not thinking about that right now. (looks at the camera) I hate to change the subject, but we're running out of time. Be sure to watch Saturday Night Slam, because it's going to be a packed show! BD: See you there, folks!
