AEWA — Card Archive

CWAA Sept 7th part2

8 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Thursdays at 10, here on WYXZ-TV Channel 39, come on down to Hazzard County, and pay a visit with Bo, Luke, Uncle Jesse, and, of course, Daisy! It’s the “Dukes of Hazzard”! This week, a mysterious hitchhiker and a carful of moonshine could get Bo and Luke in deep trouble with Boss Hogg. Thursday at 10, here in WYXZ-TV Channel 39!) EL: OK, we’re back, and, as I’m sure you know by now, two weeks ago at Reunion Arena, Joshua Barnes snapped, and struck referee Chris Herman. As of today, Joshua has been suspended for 30 days- handed down by the AEWA- and we’re going to get his reaction- it was recorded earlier today. Let’s hear what he has to say. **************************************************************************** * (The video shows Joshua Barnes standing up in the locker room of the CWAA. He's wearing his street clothes (jeans and a red t-shirt), and is holding a microphone in his hand. As he speaks, he paces up and down nervously.) Joshua Barnes: Hi. Um... as you all saw, at the supershow I was wrestling Max Robertson, and everyone knows how he's been getting under my skin. He's used foreign objects, outside help, lies, and just about everything he can find to attack me. And I finally got him where I wanted him, and I was finally getting my revenge for the last eight months, and... Chris Herman was doing his job, I had Max tied up in the ropes and he was trying to separate us... and I lost my temper, and when Mr. Herman tried to separate us, I... I lost my temper, and I hit Chris. I just wanted to say one thing- I'm sorry. I want to apologize to Chris Herman, who I never should have hit. And I want to apologize to Randy and Kore, because I felt like I let them down. And I want to apologize to the fans of the CWAA. What I did was wrong. No matter what's gone on between Max and me, I shouldn't have hit Chris Herman. I know the CWAA is suspending me, and I won't fight it. Thanks for listening to me, and I plan on returning as soon as I can. **************************************************************************** EL: OK- you heard him make reference to Randy Sheradin and Kore Wrench. They were concerned about how this impacted their Six Man Tag Team titles, and I’ve been told- this is unofficial so far, mind you- that they can use substitute partners if they need to defend their titles before Barnes comes back from his suspension. I also know that the CWAA is working on arranging another match between Barnes and Robertson when the 30 days are up- we’ll see what happens there. Let’s go to Dave Myers for the introductions to this next match, as Jak Hammer takes on Randy Sheradin! DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next bout is set for one fall with a twenty-minute time limit. Introducing first, from Detroit, Michigan- (Loud, loud boos as “Bad to the Bone” strikes up and the black-leather-clad duo of Jak Hammer and CWAA Heavyweight Champion Marc Thomas emerge from the locker area) DM: -at 310 pounds, he is the former Television Champion, Jak Hammer! (Hammer and Thomas make a detour and visit Eric at his table) EL: Marc, you tasted defeat at the hands of Joe Reed two weeks ago in this building. Have you mentally and physically recovered from that match? MT: I've had two weeks to think through the match I had with Reed and I can honestly say that he beat me. He was the better wrestler that day and I can respect that. I gave it my all, but it wasn't enough. I'll get another chance at the AEWA Heavyweight Belt eventually and I'll know what to expect when I do. As for tonight, Dreamer and Barnes are in for some serious pain. I'm still pissed about losing two weeks ago and I can't wait to take some of this anger out on someone. Jak and I challenged the tag champs to a match, but I guess they were to afraid to accept the challenge. After our match tonight, everyone will know who the greatest tag team in the CWAA is...the Alliance of Power! JH: Robertson, I want another shot at that TV belt. After your loss tonight, are you gonna be man enough to give me a shot. I'm the best wrestler in the CWAA without a belt and if you won't give me a shot, then I may just have to take one on my own. (Exit JH and MT from the announcer’s table area) EL: Well, I guess they hadn’t heard about the suspension to Joshua Barnes, and that this contest will be between Jak Hammer and Randy Sheradin. They mentioned Twister- I’ll get to that in one second. DM: His opp- (Thomas and Hammer interrupt him to demand to know why they were billed as 310 pounds and Thomas wasn’t mentioned. Myers explains to the duo, who look genuinely unhappy at not being informed ahead of time.) DM: His opponent, from Des Moines, Iowa- (Earth-shaking pop as the Dreamer comes to the ring) DM: -at 170 pounds, he is the former CWAA Heavyweight Champion, the Dreamer, Randy Sheradin! (The ex-champ in his purple tights gets a reception befitting the CWAA’s most popular young star- which he is.) Randy Sheridan: Well, it looks like we won't have the tag team match tonight, due to Josh's suspension. Hey, he's young, he's got a temper, stuff like this is bound to happen. So, tonight, I'll wrestle Jak Hammer. You're bigger than me, you're stronger than me, but you know what? I've been there before, and I've come away with the win. So do your worst, but, Jak! EL: Uh-oh! Jak Hammer with a double axe handle to the head, and he’s not waiting for the bell! Throws Sheradin out to the floor in front of Thomas now, as Hammer argues with Senior Official Jeff Howard, and Thomas putting the boots to the man who used to wear the belt Thomas carries now! (Boos) EL: Thomas now with a chair- slams it right across Sheradin’s back! Thomas puts him back in the ring, and Jak Hammer stomping away! Pulls him up by the hair- Irish whip- clothesline! (Boos) EL: Jak Hammer in control early on- he’s had help, though. Now he grabs Sheradin in a painful bearhug! Clamps it on, squeezing like a boa constrictor. Jak Hammer has not let Sheradin get started, as he went right on the attack. This may be the first one-on-one meeting between these two- a former Heavyweight Champion and a former TV champion in the ring right now- and on the outside is the reigning champion, wrestling’s biggest star in the state of Texas today, Marc Thomas. And, I’ve been told today, Hammer and Thomas may be the next TAG TEAM champions of the CWAA, as they have been declared the new number one contenders, and they just beat the new champs, Twister, last month. Hammer and Thomas really cleaning up here in the CWAA, and still undefeated as a team. Hammer putting the pressure on Sheradin, and now they’ll be putting the pressure on Twister. (“Go, Randy, Go!”) EL: The crowd cheering their favorite on, and Thomas doesn’t like it. Sheridan fighting to get a second win-claps his forearms around Hammer’s head! Again! (Pop as the hold is broken) EL: Sheradin off the ropes- cross body- no! What power! (Hammer catches Sheradin and turns it into a stomachbreaker) EL: Jak Hammer just dropped him right across the knee. Sheradin in trouble. Hammer takes him to the corner, and slams his head onto the top turnbuckle. Really rang his bell with that one. Sends him for the ride- powerslam! Shades of Travis Conley! 1, 2, no! (Pop) EL: Sheradin just kicked out. Jak Hammer now sets him up- front face lock. He realized it was too quick to get the pin on a top star like Sheradin, so he goes to wear him down a bit first. Jak Hammer with that vice-like grip. No one in the CWAA boasts a physique like his, and we may have the biggest man in the CWAA in there with the smallest- only Travis Conley may be as big as Jak. A couple of young lions in the ring, with Jak Hammer on top so far. Both men off to a terrific start in what could be a pair of long career- a lot of gold in the future of both men- there’s been gold in their past, as well. Hammer now releases the hold-pounds him to the mat with a series of double sledges. Sends him for the ride- back bod- no! (Pop) EL: Randy Sheradin with a sunset flip- 1, 2, kickout! Hammer with a big right hand- sends him to the corner and Sheradin comes back with a right hand! (Pop) EL: Sheradin now off the ropes- dropkick! (Pop) EL: Off the ropes- splash! (Of the frog variety) EL: 1, 2, kickout again! Sheradin sends him for the ride- vicious chop lays the big man out! He’s going to the top! (Popping builds) EL: He waits- dropkick from the top rope! (Pop) EL: Covers- 1, 2, wait! (Sheradin gets back up) EL: Marc Thomas up on the ring apron, and- (Pop as the Dreamer ropeflips him into the ring) EL: Sheradin sends Thomas for the ride- leg scissor takedown, drops him right on his head! Travis Conley and Paul Langford, the new tag team champions, come to the ring to provide backup in case Thomas attempts to interfere again.) EL: Thomas rolls to the floor- Sheradin sends Hammer for the ride- and one for him, too! 1, 2, kickout! Sheradin now to a side headlock, and Hammer drives to the ropes- Jeff Howard calls for the clean break, and- oh, no! (Boos) EL: Jak Hammer with a forearm shiver in the kidney area! Now he chokes Sheradin on the top rope- Jeff Howard giving the 5 count- break. And now he’s doing it again! (Boos) EL: Runs his face along that top rope- that’ll burn like heck, I can tell you. Sheradin’s a tough little son of a gun, but that’ll really take it out of you. Hammer backs him into the corner now- drives a shoulder right into the mid-section. And again! Now he sends him for the ride- sleeper hold! Hammer’s got him in the noose! Twister at ringside- I never saw them come- leading the crowd as they try to rally the kid from Iowa. (“Go, Randy, Go!”) EL: And Sheradin starting to fade- this is Hammer’s favorite new way to put his opponents away. (Pop as Sheradin counters with a jawbreaker) EL: But Randy Sheradin’s not done yet! And Jak Hammer better have a good dentist! Sheradin off the ropes- cartwheel kick! Sheradin now hooks him in a waistlock- no! German suplex! (As Thomas hops onto the ring apron, Conley grabs the back of his shirt and holds him back and Langford helps by holding Thomas’ right arm) EL: 1, 2, 3! (Pop) EL: He did it! Randy Sheradin with the victory! Let’s go to David Myers for the official word. DM: Ladies and gent- EL: Look out! Jak Hammer after Paul Langford now, and the Alliance of Power and Twister are going at it! (Sheradin stands in the ring and cheers on his pals as Langford and Conley engage in a typical tussle with Thomas and Hammer) EL: Conley and Hammer all tangled up against the ropes, and Thomas and Langford trading blows on the outside- we have to take a break, now- but when we get back, we’ll see Mark Sherrick and Elektro! Ooh-Hammer with a hard right hand for the Husker- here comes security- we’ll be right back! (Your favorite stars in your favorite films, every Wednesday at 8- and again, Thursdays at midnight, on the WYXZ-TV Channel 39 Movie of the Week! This week, John Ritter is a womanizing alcoholic who needs to jumpstart his writing career and his life, in “Skin Deep”. The Wednesday Night Movie of the Week!) EL: OK- we’re back and things have calmed a bit now- someday soon Twister and the Alliance of Power will be in the ring, head to head, but for now we have other business to take care of. Randy Sheradin with the duke, and we’ve got another big match up for you, as Sgt. Sherrick tackles the masked superstar, the internationally renowned Elektro! Let’s go to David Myers. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next bout is set for one fall and a thirty-minute time limit. Introducing first, from London, England- (Pop as Robert Myles’ “Fable” plays) DM: -weighing in at 275 pounds, Elektro! (Pop as the masked man with the lightening bolts on his shorts trots to the ring. Elektro stands 6'7"-275, and as he walks to the ring area he slaps hands with all the fans on the way. He climbs into the ring and takes the microphone.) Elektro: Mark Sherrick, you're one of the reasons I came to the CWAA and you're one of the reason I've stayed here. You've already met my bother Fuse- (Pop) E: -one-on-ne and lost; tonight you will suffer the same fate at the hands of the second brother of electricity, at the hands of Elektro (Pop) E: The newest star in the CWAA is about to start shining and you, Sherrick, will be just another stepping stone on my way to the CWAA title. I am going to spill your blood around the ring and then mop it up with your head; and no-one’s gonna stop me. You wanna bring that lame (expletive deleted) Major or that piece of (expletive deleted) Lawson with you, go ahead; I'll beat them up, too (Pop) E: Sherrick; you are about to find out first hand why I am a legend in Europe, and why I will soon be in this country. You are about to be hit faster and harder than ever before. You are about to meet Total Elektrification. Now Sherrick, get your fat lazy (expletive deleted) out here now, cause it's time it got a whipping! (Pop) (David Myers is having a bad day, as Sherrick never gives him time to announce his arrival or the presence of Chris Herman. The 6'4"-275 pounder is followed closely by Lt. Lawson, and by Max Robertson, and not as closely by the decrepit Major Mayhem. Without bothering to remove his fatigue-colored trench coat, Sherrick rolls in under the bottom rope) EL: Here we go! Sherrick charges right at him- and they’re trading lefts and rights- Max Robertson, Lt. Lawson, the Major- Sherrick has all his friends here tonight- Elektro and Sherrick going toe to toe- and Sherrick with a kneelift to get the advantage. A kick now, and Elektro has really riled him up. I was talking backstage with Sherrick earlier- an unpleasant man but a little more grounded in reality than some of the company he keeps- and he says they feel Elektro has stuck his nose in someone else’s business and that he’s the man to stick it right back out. He sends Elektro for the ride- clothesline. (Sherrick looks to the Major for approval and grins and nods as Elektro pops back to his feet behind the Sgt.) EL: Sherrick not paying attention, and Elektro catches him in an atomic knee drop! Now a forearm uppercut, and he sends Sherrick for the ride, hip tosses him over- now Elektro to a full nelson. And I’m joined now by the reigning CWAA Television Champion, former CWAA Heavyweight Champion, and perhaps the next AEWA World Heavyweight Champion, Max Robertson. Max Robertson: You know, something seems different around here this week. EL: Different? Sherrick with an elbow jab to break the hold. MR: Like there’s something missing? EL: I think I know where this is going. And Sherrcik now with a snap mare takeover, into a chinlock. MR: It feels- less dumb. Any idea why this is, Lane? (The chinlock continues) EL: If you’re talking about Joshua Barnes, then- MR: That’s it! I haven’t seen him all evening. I hear that he’s been given detention or something for bullying defenseless referees? EL: Clearly Joshua Barnes has been pushed past his limit... MR: Well so have I. That idiot just doesn't care anymore. He doesn't wrestle; he brawls, and I don't see how the CWAA administration can stand for it. (Sherrick with an elbowsmash to the top of Elektro’s head) EL: I wouldn't say that they are standing for anything... MR: I try to exercise my rights as Heavyweight Champion, and I got stripped of my belt. Without warning! But this jerk... (Sherrick now backs Elektro to the corner and chokes him out with a boot on the Adam’s apple). EL: The CWAA front office has made it clear that outbursts such as Barnes's last week will not be tolerated... MR: How! How have they made it clear? Oh, maybe he'll get a little slap on the wrist, but that's just now good enough! EL: Well I don't see how you're in any position to dictate... MR: I demand that Joshua Barnes's 6-man tag title be stripped, and that he be expelled from the CWAA! Otherwise what's to stop Wrench, or that Joe Reed fellow, or any of those powerhouses from just punching an official whenever those non-wrestling fools get outclassed by someone like me? (Sherrick with the cross corner whip into an elbow smash to the sternum) EL: I hardly think that Reed would visit our league and begin beating up our officials! MR: Oh, I suppose you think Reed has earned the right to wear the AEWA Championship belt! EL: Of course! MR: You would. But I've earned that right too, and tonight I'm going to prove it! That guy is just another dopey fanboy, and I intend to prove that tonight. I've got the speed, I've got the skill, and I've got the drive. After I've slapped him in my chickenwing submission and the whole world has heard him submit, no one will ever be able to doubt me again. Then, I can begin to tidy up some unfinished business. (Sherrick hits a gutwrench suplex) EL: Unfinished business? With whom? MR: They know who they are. Tokugawa for one. I haven't forgotten what he did to me last month. Marc Thomas is the other. That belt he carries is mine, and I will reclaim it. That's... EL: (interrupting) let me guess: That's the Professional way MR: You got it baby. That's the only way. (with that, Robertson gets up and leaves the table) EL: Well, that was enlightening. OK- Mark Sherrick has taken control of this one, and he sends Elektro for the ride, and- (Pop) EL: Elektro with the clothesline! Scoops him up- body slam! Covers- 1, 2, kickout! Elektro back on top-off the ropes- and Lawson grabs his ankle! (As Elektro staggers on his one unfettered foot!.) EL: Sherrick with a ddt! Cover- 1, 2, kickout! Sherrick and Lawson almost got away with murder, there. Max conferring with the Major- I don’t know what he thinks he can get out of an association with that birdbrain, apart from free use of the Major’s two thugs. All right, Sherrick now to the cobra clutch, cutting off the flow of blood to the brain. There goes Max, back to the locker area- pretty confident about Sherrick’s chances, I guess. Sgt. Sherrick with the cobra clutch on, Elektro trying to lean over to the ropes. (“Go, Elektro, Go!”) EL: We’ve got a World Title matchup still to come after this contest, so you won’t want to go anywhere when this one’s over. And Elektro gets a foot on the bottom rope- Sherrick releases and now shoves him to the corner, and now a series of body blows. The super-tough Sgt., a charter member of what I like to call the Mayhem Militia- co-holder of the ACWF Tag Team Championship. Sherrick pounding away in the corner- and Elektro spins him around! (Pop) EL: Elektro has Sherrick backed up in the corner, taking the fight to him! A right, a left, a right, a left! Elektro really giving him the business! (Pop) EL: But Sherrick grips him around the waist- inverted atomic drop! (Boos) EL: And Elektro comes back with a clothesline! (Pop) EL: Now a spinkick, and Sherrick is down! Elektro sets him up- brainbuster- no! A brainbuster into a piledriver! The Brain Hemorrhage! (Pop) EL: What a devastating maneuver! 1, 2, oh, no! (Chris Herman calls for the bell as Lawson stomps away at Elektro’s back) EL: Lawson attacks from behind, and Elektro will get the win, but Lawson is giving him a real good working over. (Boos) EL: And here comes the cavalry! (Dwayne Griffith, Ed Farmer and Brad Gorman come to the ring. Griffith and Farmer are summarily dispatched by Lawson (the Major then stomps them and stands over them with his fists cocked and ready), while Gorman instead stomps Elektro!) EL: What in the world is Brad Gorman doing! Lawson has cleared the ring of the wrestlers who’ve come to Elektro’s aid- and now he levels Gorman, who was attacking the fallen man, too! And Gorman is sent to the floor, and- here we go! (Pop as Little Yvette and Kore Wrench and Jimmy Dangerous run to the ring and Lawson scrambles. The Major has no idea what is going on as he is busy menacing the parade of jobbers, and Sherrick is still in wonderland) EL: The Major now backing up- and he just bumped right into the Preacher! (Pop) EL: And the Preacher grabs the Major by his lapels- headbutt! (Pop as the Major sells like someone who’s stepped on a landmine before flipping gracelessly out of the ring) EL: OK- here’s David Myers with the official decision. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, as the result of a disqualification, Elektro! (Pop as “Fable” resumes player, and Sherrick rolls to the floor, while Yvette and Dangerous help Elektro to his feet and raise his hand in victory.) EL: All right, when we get back, we’ll see Joe Reed and Max Robertson in action- the World Heavyweight Championship will be on the line!
