Sunday, March 2nd, Card (3/3)

2 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The "Twister" theme song strikes up, and the crowd pops exubertanly for Travis Conley, Paul Langford and Mother Nature. Conley, the actual participant in this one, is 6'8"-315. Like his partner, the blonde-haired, blue-eyed rookie wears a pair of brown trunks with a tornado-logo on the back.) EL: Safety in numbers. We've seen Major Mayhem get a little too hands-on when it comes to Lt. Lawson's matches, so Twister is out here in full force to counter any such tactics. ("When Johnny Comes Marching Home..." plays, and Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson arrives on his own to a heaping helping of heel heat. The crewcut 5'9"-219 pounder in his military outfit asks for the house mike as he hits the ring.) DL: My commanding officer, Major Victor Augustus Mayhem (crowd jeers), in unable to make this match because of the attack by the cowardly Stan "Powerhouse" Stevens. (crowd cheers) Because he was such a yellow coward that he had to quit in his match against me, he attacked a seventy-year old man and injure his neck. I hope that the cowardly civilian is proud with himself. My question to Travis Conley is this; do you have the guts, the courage, the inner strength, to meet me in a true one-on-one encounter? Then leave your manager and your partner behind. They stay in the dressing room, and only you and I meet in the ring. Or are you as yellow as Stan Stevens? EL: Strong words there from the Lieutenant. We all know what really happened between the Major and Stan Stevens last week. And, well, Travis Conley is sending his partner to the back He's going to take this one without Langford in attendance. With the absence of the Major and a 90+ pound weight advantage, Travis Conley is in excellent position here. There goes the Tornado. We're set to go- Max Robertson is on his way to the finals, and he'll face either Randy Sheradin or one of the two men in the ring right now. There's a collar and elbow tie up- and Conley just tosses Lawson back into the corner, and Lawson crashes hard! (much popping occurs) EL: Now he's complaining that Conley pulled his hair- he doesn't have enough hair to pull! Lawson back to the middle of the ring. They lock up again- same result! (the crowd also responds as they did a few seconds ago) EL: Lawson is livid about the alleged hair pulls, and heads back to his opponent. He wants to lock up again- how much more of this does he want to take? Conley locks up with him- and Lawson with a knee to the "upper thigh", I suppose. Conley doubles over, and Lawson with a chop across the chest. Snap mare by Lawson, and now he slips on a sleeperhold on Travis Conley. The referee checking to make sure it is not an illegal chokehold. He looks like he has is applied very skillfully. (Time for "Go, Travis, Go!") EL: The ref checks Conley- his arm drops once- checks again- no, Conley is fighting it! Conley up on one knee, now. Now he's getting back to his feet- stands at full height, and Lawson is dangling from around his neck- at least a foot between Lawson's feet and the mat! Conley backs into a corner- ramming Lawson into the turnbuckles. The impact forces Lawson to release the hold. (The crowd cheers) EL: Conley turns around now- driving shoulderblock into Lawson's midsection. Cross corner whip by Conley- and he follows him in- avalanche! Now an elbowsmash to the top of Lawson's head drops the Lt. to one knee. He gets back up, and another elbowsmash puts him right back down. Irish whip now by Conley- powerslam! 1.. 2... kickout. Now Conley gets around behind him for a chinlock. He's also leaning all 315 pounds onto Lawson, forcing the Lt. to bear both men's combined weight while trying to endure the chinlock. The referee checks for a submission. Lawson reaches back- and there's a rake of the eyes! Conley has to let go, and that's one place you never want to be hit. The referee is warning Lawson about breaking the rules like that, but no warning is going to put Lawson back in a chinlock and alleviate Conley's pain. Now Lawson with a kick in the midsection. Irish whip now, and a clothesline by Lawson puts Conley back on the mat. Elbow drop by the Lt., who before long is going to have Stan Stevens to answer to- if you saw what happened last week. Now he sits Conley back up- and a bow-and-arrow submission hold, expertly applied. He's really stretching Conley's upper body and digging that knee into his back. Conley straining to hang in there. I want to take this opportunity to mention that the Jak Hammer-Joey Haven feud is about to move from the CWAA to the AEWA, as they have been signed to meet at the AEWA Pay Per View extravaganza AEWA Extreme later this month, and we are negotiating to get a CWAA Heavyweight Title match on that card as well. And speaking of the AEWA, congratulations are in order for new world champion, Crimson Joe Reed, who defeated Deranged to complete what has been a meteoric rise through the AEWA ranks for the veteran. Of course, we still the AEWA World Light Heavyweight Champion on his way to the world-famous Sportatorium later this month, but it is no longer the Holy Ghost whom we'll be seeing, but "Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi. We'll have more details on these developments in the coming weeks. Wait a second! There's Major Victor Augustus Mayhem, skulking down the aisle, dressed in fatigues, and with camouflage markings on his face, and he's carrying a flagpole with the flag rolled up as he sneaks his way down here. Now the Major kneeling on the floor behind the ring steps. Lawson with an Irish whip- reversed! And Travis Conley catches him in another power slam! 1... 2... kickout. Conley back up now- and Lawson grabs him by the tights and yanks him over, through the ropes and down to the floor, right at the feet of the Major! Now the Lt. getting in Mother Nature's face, and as the referee gets between them- there's the Major smashing that flagpole across Conley's back! (Another expression of heel heat from those members of the crowd whose view is not obscured by the ring) EL: Conley in serious pain- look at that welt raising on his back now. And here comes Paul Langford for the Major, who beats a hasty retreat around the ring- the Tornado right behind him! And Lawson now dragging Conley back in by the hair. And Mother Nature just clocked the Major, who was taking evasive action from the Tornado, and her big right hand just slapped him silly. (Biggest pop of the night) EL: The flagpole went flying, and Langford grabs it! He winds up and swings- his partner is locked in another bow and arrow hold in the ring- he swings that pole at the major- hitting nothing but ringpost- that pole just got bent right in the middle, where the impact occurred- I've never seen that! (Crowd groans in disappointment) EL: There's the bell- it looks like Conley has submitted- let's face it, after that shot from the flagpole then a bow and arrow hold, he has plenty of good reasons to submit. Well, the Major and Lt. pulled a fast one. ("Johnny Comes Marching Home Again..." plays as the Lt. and Major hit the road in a most expeditious fashion. In other words, they run like Hell.) EL: Paul Langford and Mother Nature checking the condition of Conley, and Langford arguing that the Major interfered, but all the referee sees is that Langford has the flagpole now, and it's twisted like a pretzel almost, right where it smacked the ringpost. (While all three members of Twister, well, two members, plus a manager, remain in the ring, "Sweet Home Alabama" kicks in, and out comes Eddie Intellecta, Hardcore Henry King, and Hotshot Gary Jackson. Intellecta is 6'7"-250 and dons a blue mask. The heel heat is poured on good and thick, the events of the Dallas Children's Hospital notwithstanding.) EL: Well, we've got 6 people out here for a one-on-one matchup, which can't be good. Technically, this one is supposed to be Paul Langford versus Eddie Intellecta, but we've got Jackson, King, Mother Nature and Conley all out at ringside, and Conley is in a bad way. There's the bell and Intellecta with a double axe handle for the Tornado- but Langford fires back with a series of kicks and punches, and now an Irish whip- leapfrog by Langford- dropkick! Now a snap suplex by the Tornado, and he hooks Intellecta in an armbar. Out on the floor, now, Gary Jackson coming around to Travis Conley- who greets him with a right cross, and the Hotshot goes down, and now the referee is heading in their direction. And he's ordering both men out of the ringside area. I think his patience has been exhausted for the evening, and Mother Nature has learned her lesson from earlier this evening- Conley goes nowhere until Jackson leaves. And Gary Jackson now, finally, heads for the aisle. Conley follows reluctantly- hopefully we'll have some sense of order out here now. When we get back from this commercial break, we'll see the rest of this, our special feature match. (Time Life Home Video Presents: A Century of War. A new 12-cassette video library of the most important military engagements of modern times. Order Volumes I and II: Grenada, and the Falkland Islands. Try each for a 30-day trial period. If you like these presentations, Vol. III: Azerbaijan, will arrive. Or return Vols. I and II for a complete refund. Other Volumes will include: Panama; The Boer War; Cyprus. Order now; supplies are limited.) EL: OK- we're back, and Paul Langford in complete control of this one, and the distraction of Gary Jackson and Travis Conley has been dealt with. Side suplex by Langford. The cover... 1... 2... kickout. Standing armbar now by the Tornado. Intellecta reverses- and Langford re-reverses, and Intellecta is obvious pain. You know, later this month we'll be holding our tag team title tournament, and Twister and Double Jeopardy have to be considered the two favorites, but remember, it was Healer's Havoc and BD Brown- Intellecta pushes Langford back into the corner, and Langford will have to break the arm bar here. Ref calls for a clean break- and Intellecta with a slap in the face! Langford with a right, and a left, and a right. Now Intellecta- this is more his style of match- spins his foe right back around into the corner and connects a few body punches- now he's using that size 12 boot to choke Langford out. Like I was saying, Havoc and Brown had an impressive win over Double Jeopardy last week, and have to be considered a threat in the upcoming tag team title tournament. and rumor has it that Randy Sheradin and Joshua Barnes may team up for a shot at the gold, and don't be surprised if a few teams active elsewhere in this country make a try as well. OK- Eddie Intellecta with a brain buster on Langford- and now he signals to the crowd that this one is done. 1... 2... kickout. Intellecta berating the referee over the speed of that count. A couple of stomps by the masked man on Langford- now a side headlock- into a bulldog. A little on the sloppy side, but it works. The cover... 1... 2.. kickout again. (The crowd goes into chant mode "Go, Paul Go!" Anyone else remember the children's book "Go Dog Go!"? Oh well...) EL: Intellecta now with a front facelock on his opponent. Langford tries to get on his feet- he's getting up there now- now he's driving Intellecta back into the corner- Intellecta still has a weak facelock on- now the ref calls for a break- Intellecta swings, Langford ducks and smacks him with a European forearm uppercut. Irish whip by the Tornado- big clothesline! (Crowd pops) EL: Now Langford with a shoulderblock, and Intellecta goes back down! Langford off he ropes- another shoulderblock! Langford now off the ropes again- sunset flip! 1... 2... kickout! (Crowd groans) EL: Langford waits for Intellecta to get back up- thrust kick! Langford picks up Intellecta's inert form- sits him on that top turnbuckle. A right hand by Langford, now he climbs to the second turnbuckle- belly to belly superplex! 1... 2... kickout! Langford with an Irish whip, and- who are those two? (A pair of unknown blonde gentleman, both about 6'0"-250, hit the ring. One wears red trunks, the other wears blue pants with the words "Pretty Boy" on the back.) EL: I have no idea who these two are, but as Langford got Intellecta in position for his airplane spin, they appear to be targeting the Tornado. The bell is ringing, and Mother Nature has run for the locker area, and Eddie Intellecta is heading for the hills. Now they're both stomping away at the Tornado. The bell is still ringing like crazy, and so is Langford's bell by now, I'm sure. Now they're whipping him into the ropes- double clothesline! And it looks like we're out of time- we'll be back next week, where we'll find out who'll face Max Robertson for the CWAA Heavyweight Title- good night, everybody! (As the closing credits do what closing credits do, we see Langford tumble from the ring- the two men follow and set up for a spiked piledriver, but Travis Conley, Mother Nature, and Kore Wrench race from the locker area, and the two mystery men scatter like cockroaches when the light is switched on.) -kl
