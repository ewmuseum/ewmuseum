15 November 1996

------------------------- ******************** AEWA REPORT ------------------------- ******************** Welcome to the fourth official edition of the AEWA Report! We have plenty of big news to tell you about, so read on.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ RECENT NEWS ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ --NEW WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS! In an exciting match against the West Coast Connection on Wednesday Night Rumble, the Graveyard Shift managed to win the World tag team titles for a FOURTH time! The victory came moments after the Mortician executed a BEAUTIFUL belly-to-belly superplex, knocking Axl Sharkman completely unconscious. The Shift's next match will be against Heaven & Hell, with Curate Kill as the special guest referee, so stay tuned! --CAN ANYONE STOP BIG DADDY? "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt defeated Rick Showboat for the Light Heavyweight title on Saturday Night Slam, and he's been on a roll ever since. Just last night, he managed to pull off a victory against Marc Thomas; but only after hitting him with brass knuckles. One thing is for sure-- Big Daddy has come a LONG way in the last week. He will be defending the title against Penance this weekend, in what promises to be a classic match. --IS MIKE STEWART DEAD? After falling 15 feet and landing on his head during that recent scaffold match, Mike Stewart's future didn't look very bright. Now, unfortunately, there is some doubt that he even HAS a future. We have been trying to contact Stewart's family, but they still haven't returned our calls. His doctor has completely ignored our reporters. Now, the question remains-- is Mike Stewart even alive? He had landed very awkwardly on his head, so he COULD have died. We hope to FINALLY get an interview with his doctor soon, to put an end to these rumors. Hopefully, we'll find out that Stewart is alive and well.... Keep him in your prayers, folks. ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ INJURY REPORT ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ "BIG DADDY" MIKE HURT got into an automobile accident last night, as he was leaving the arena. Another car side-swiped his pickup truck, then sped down the road at about 70 MPH. Mike Hurt was relatively unharmed, but he did have a rather nasty bruise on his forehead (from his head slamming down onto the steering wheel). He wasn't able to get the license plate number of the hit-and-run driver, but the local police are currently investigating. Hurt isn't going to miss any matches because of this incident. ******************* CHAMPION LIST******************** AEWA World Heavyweight Champion-- VACANT AEWA North American Champion-- Ron Raider AEWA Television Champion-- Deranged AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion-- "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt AEWA World Tag Team Champions-- The Graveyard Shift AEWA National Tag Team Champions-- The Machines *******************THE TOP 10 (SINGLES)**************** 1.
