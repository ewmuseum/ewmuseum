Sunday, March 9th, Card (3/3)

9 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(This week, Sky Whatley moves to a new night and time: Thursdays at 7:30 PM, on WYXZ TV 39. He'll have a special report on the state high school basketball championships, and exciting new chili recipes. Plus, the grand opening of Whitman's Whiskey, a new drive through liquor establishment in Glanville, TX.) EL: Last week, we saw Paul Langford by a pair of strangers to the CWAA. Let's take a look at exactly what happened. ********************************** EL: Langford waits for Intellecta to get back up- thrust kick! Langford picks up Intellecta's inert form- sits him on that top turnbuckle. A right hand by Langford, now he climbs to the second turnbuckle- belly to belly superplex! 1... 2... kickout! Langford with an Irish whip, and- who are those two? (A pair of unknown blonde gentleman, both about 6'0"-250, hit the ring. One wears red trunks, the other wears blue pants with the words "Pretty Boy" on the back.) EL: I have no idea who these two are, but as Langford got Intellecta in position for his airplane spin, they appear to be targeting the Tornado. The bell is ringing, and Mother Nature has run for the locker area, and Eddie Intellecta is heading for the hills. Now they're both stomping away at the Tornado. The bell is still ringing like crazy, and so is Langford's bell by now, I'm sure. Now they're whipping him into the ropes- double clothesline! *********************************************************** EL: Tonight, Langford and his partner, Travis Conley, take on the two mystery men. (ZZ Top's "Sharp Dresses Man" plays, and the crowd boos as the two newcomers strut. One stands 5'11"-254, neither particularly muscular nor flabby, in flowing green sequined robe. His partner, at 5'10"-243 with a Dean Malenko-ish face (but not his attitude), in a red robe, follows.) EL: Gentlemen, who exactly are you, and what was the reason behind your cowardly attack on Paul Langford last week? #1 (the one in the green robe): Who we are is not any business of yours, Mr. Pettingill, but I suppose if we didn't give our names, all of these lovely ladies here tonight would have no idea who it is they'll be dreaming about all day while their husbands are slaving away at the odor-eater factory. I am "Marvelous" Michael Anderson, and this is "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey- EL: And I'm Eric Lane, not Todd Pettingill. MA: And I'm sure you're very proud. Now I have to go make sure the ladies are getting their money's worth of entertainment, so the Pretty Boy is going to have take over the talking chores here. (Anderson hops into the ring and begins strutting around, gyrating and wiggling in ways that I do not care to describe as he removes his robe, revealing a pair of full-length red tights with "Marvelous" written on the back.) BN: So, you want to know why we beat up on poor Pauly Langford last week? well, he and his little pal would actually be below our notice, but you see, every time we turn on the CWAA at home, who do we see? Do we see the likes of Marvelous Michael and the Pretty Boy? No, we do not. We see an endless of parade of cornhusking hicks, taking up valuable airtime that could be spent on us. Now, we're trying to do you a favor, David Crockett, or whatever name your maladjusted parents saddled you with, by increasing the beauty quotient on your programming. Once word gets out that specimens like us can be viewed on free TV, your ratings are going to shoot through the roof. And we are just generous enough to provide this service to the likes of you, Lord James Blears. EL: Well, if you think meeting both halves of Twister head on in a match is going to be as easy as a 2-on-1 attack on a man after a grueling match of his own, you're mistaken. BN: We'll all see just how easy it is in a minute, Lance Russell. Now I have to get in the ring and start showing my stuff, too. Just remember- don't hate me because I'm beautiful. (hops into the ring) EL: If people want to hate you, it looks like they'll have plenty of other reasons. (The Twister theme plays, the crowd pops, and Mother Nature leads the 6'3"-244 washboard-stomached Langford and the big old Husker lineman, the 6'8"-315 Travis Conley, who slide into the ring and charge right at their opponents.) EL: Twister not waiting for the bell to ring, and Travis Conley goes right to Marvelous Michael with a series of punches, and Michael covers his head shouting 'Not the face!' Paul Langford with a kick in the midsection for Byron McNultey, now he pulls Byron's robe off and whips into the ropes, and catches him in the neck with that rope, choking him out! (The crowd roars) EL: Travis Conley headbutts Anderson, who tumbles over the top rope to the floor. The referee pulls the Tornado off of Byron, and Travis takes the robe and throws it out of the ring- and it lands on Anderson's head- who starts swinging wildly, even though no one is on the floor with him. Paul Langford now with an Irish whip- dropkick! Langford pulls him over to the Twister corner-tags out. Now an Irish whip- and Langford drops down- Byron has to hurdle him- and runs right into a Travis Conley clothesline! Now Travis Conley with an armbar- and he tags Langford back in, who has a kick for Byron's exposed ribs. Now Langford with the armbar, and he tags in Conley, who connects with an elbow smash on the exposed, extended elbow. Now Conley scoops him up- fallaway slam... 1... 2... kickout. Now Conley with the Irish whip- powerslam... 1... 2... Anderson breaks the pin with a kick in the head. Now it is Byron McNultey on his feet- a stomp for Travis Conley- now he drops to his knees and begins choking him. Now a leg drop by the Pretty Boy. I wonder if his choice of nickname is an homage to Doug Somers. Byron now with a spinning to hold- first time we've seen this in the CWAA. He grinds that leg again. Now Byron drags him to the Anderson-McNultey corner. Tags in Marvelous Michael. Byron holds Travis' legs spread as Mikey drops a leg across the upper thigh region. Now Mikey picks him up and stands him in the corner. Byron chokes him with the tag rope and Mikey throws a series of body punches. Langford coming in to break this up- but the referee cuts him off. Byron comes back in- no tag there- double clothesline. ("Go, Travis, Go!" We've regressed to a single chant) EL: Gutwrench suplex by Byron... lackadaisical cover... 1... 2... kickout. He should have tried hooking the leg there. Byron drapes Conley's ankle on the bottom rope and stomps away at the exposed leg. Byron now goes for a figure-four leglock- has he got it?- yes! Figure four by the pretty boy, and Travis Conley in a heap of trouble now. He cannot reach the ropes from where he is- he needs to try and roll over to reverse the pressure- and hey! The referee checks for a submission as Mikey Anderson leans over the ropes and pulls on Byron's hands for added leverage. The referee turns around just as they let go. He checks on Conley again- and they're doing it again! The referee looks up, and Anderson lets go. Mother Nature is screaming now. While we have tag team action in the ring, let me talk about a few teams who will be joining us in two weeks for the CWAA Tag Team Title Tournament. From the FWA, the up and coming team of Lethal Injection will be here, and we understand that they are not exactly upstanding young gentlemen, but nonetheless are rising stars. Plus, from the ACWF, out on the Atlantic Coast, I understand the High School, Toughies, Hans and Franz, if you'd believe it, are here, and apparently they don't want any of us to touch their jackets! And Michael Anderson AGAIN helping his partner with this hold- and now they've been caught! (The referee kicks at the joined hands, to an enormous crowd pop, and Byron McNultey releases the hold and gets up to give the ref a 2-handed shove. Rather than call for the bell, the ref shoves back, and McNultey lands on his posterior as the audience erupts. McNultey springs back up and throws a right hand, spinning around with the force of the blow as the referee ducks- and falling to the mat.) EL: I think we can expect our newcomers to pay for that one, and they're lucky this match hasn't ended now. Byron doesn't know where he is anymore. He heads to the Twister corner for a tag- and what a tag he gets, in the form of a right hook! (another big pop) EL: And he stumbles backward, and Conley rolls him up... 1... 2... kickout! Travis Conley up again- and he tags in Paul Langford- who runs in and drops an elbow on Byron, and now locks him in an armbar down on the mat. And Michael Anderson starts heading down the ring apron to where Mother Nature is stationed- and he just blew her a kiss! He's winking at her, and now she's on her way to his corner. He's pointing to his cheek, expecting a kiss, I guess- and she slaps him, right where we has pointing! (big pop) EL: Anderson is stunned, and now he takes a swing at her- she ducks and runs. Langford saw none of this but sees her heading for the safety of her team's corner, and he releases the hold, and turns to see what was wrong. Uh-oh- Byron McNultey now up behind him- full nelson- no! He suplexes him back- Dragon Suplex! The ref starts his count- and Michael Anderson leans over the ropes again and pulls back on Langford's feet! He can't kick out because Anderson has him by the feet! That's it- a three count! ("Sharp Dressed Man" plays, and Byron slides to the floor, where he and Mikey mug for the camera for a second before saying "Don't hate us 'cause we're beautiful".) EL: And a big win for Michael Anderson and Byron McNultey, who make a hasty exit as Travis Conley and Paul Langford try to explain to the official what happened, but it looks like this one is going to stand. When we get back, we'll see Kore Wrench and Marc Thomas, then Jak Hammer and Joey Haven go into the steel cage! Plus, more on the situation between Randy Sheradin, Max Robertson and Joshua Barnes. (Coming soon: AEWA Extreme. See AEWA World Champions, Crimson Joe Reed; the AEWA World and National Tag Team Champions, the Machines and Assassins, in a title unification match; Sir Gallade and Randall Osbourne in a steel cage; and falls count anywhere as Jak Hammer and Joey Haven make their ppv debuts! Contact your local cable operator.) EL: That's right- the CWAA's "Hardcore" Joey Haven and Jak Hammer are on their way to AEWA Extreme, later this month. But here tonight, we're going to lock them in an 8' high cage. On his way out here is Jak Hammer, to tell us what he thinks about tonight's feature matchup. Mr. Hammer? (As he heads for Eric Lane, few fans begin throwing debris, and the arena fills with condemnations of the Detroit native) JH: Haven, this is the third time we've met not counting that time when I introduced your skull to a steel chair. You are 0-3. What makes you think this match will be any different? Now we are in a steel cage where there are even more things to bounce your head off of!! It would be different if I actually thought you had a chance of beating me. There isn't much left to say to you Haven. I've beaten you three times and tonight is going to be no different. Prepare to get Hammered Haven! Your time in the CWAA is up! (Exit JH) EL: OK- later tonight, inside the steel cage, Jak Hammer, Joey Haven. But first, the Preacher takes on Marc Thomas. Let's take a look at the Preacher's Parish. ******************************************************************* (Cut to total blackness with the exception of a glowing cross - Then a lamp from above ignites, and illuminates "The Preacher" Kore Wrench in ghostly light.) The Preacher: Welcome, once again to my Parish. I cannot express how angry I am with Max Robertson right now. He took my trust, he took Randy Sheridan's trust, and he betrayed us both. Betrayal does not sit well with me. It is not my place to seek vengeance in this matter, but I have learned my lesson. I guarantee that this will not happen again. Angry as I am, I must put this entire matter behind me. I must continue moving toward the Championship. So, to whomever wins the tournament, whether it be Lieutenant Lawson, the Betrayer Max Robertson, or even my friend Randy Sheridan: enjoy your victory while it lasts, because The Preacher is coming to take your title away. Even with my loss to The Dreamer, I'm still the number one contender, and I intend to stay that way with a victory over Marc Thomas this Sunday. His strength and experience is irrelevant against my faith and will. (The overhead light is extinguished leaving the glowing cross for a moment before fading completely to black) ************************************************************************ ("Life in the Fast Lane" plays, and the crowd shows its displeasure with the most decorated man in the CWAA today, Marc Thomas. The 6'0"-240 pounder comes to the ring in his sleeveless black leather vest. Thomas continues today's trend and pays a visit to Eric Lane, a popular man today.) MT: Everyone seems to think that since I lost to Sheridan that I must be on a downhill slide. Well, the reports of my demise have been greatly exaggerated. Yeah, Sheridan beat me! So what. No wrestler can go through his career undefeated. I can except that I've lost, but the rest of the wrestlers in the CWAA better be on their toes. I am now more determined than ever to win the CWAA championship. I'm not looking past Kore Wrench at all. He's a big man with a lot of power, but he does have his weaknesses. No one else in the CWAA has as much ring experience as I do. I've been around this sport long enough to learn what it takes to be a champion. It takes more than just size and power to win matches. You've got to be able to out think and out wrestle your opponent. Preacher man, you do this goody goody stuff with children and everything, but that don't mean squat when you step into the squared circle. I've studied your previous matches and I know what it takes to beat a man like you. Bring your best big man, but when its all over, I'll be the one walking away with the W. (Without waiting to hear a word from Lane, who I guess really isn't very popular- it must be the microphone they all come to see, he climbs into the ring. Gregorian Chant fills the arena, and the crowd pops like mad for the priestly Kore Wrench. As the 275-pounder stops to bless a small child- Marc Thomas slips from the ring and comes up behind Wrench.) EL: Look out- Marc Thomas spins the preacher around and knocks him with a European uppercut! Now Thomas with the Icebreaker- out on the floor! And this match hasn't even started yet! Thomas drags Wrench into the ring, and the bell rings to start this one. Thomas sets for an Icebreaker again- he got it! (BTW, an Icebreaker is Diamond Cutter) EL: The cover... 1... he picks him back up. Marc Thomas now with an Irish whip- big elbow smash! Now Thomas with a hammerlock. We've been seeing a lot of the Preacher of late. if he isn't in the ring, he's marshaling other CWAA stars for the hospital visit, or charging out of the locker room to even the odds when people are outnumbered, or mediating between Max Robertson and Randy Sheradin- but we've seen how that went. And he came very close to being the one in the semifinals against Lt. Lawson tonight as well. Marc Thomas has been keeping more of a low profile- perhaps concentrating on the CCWF Title up in Canada. Now Thomas turns the hammerlock into an armbar- Irish whip- knee in the midsection doubles the Preacher over. Now Thomas sets him up- side suplex. Cover... 1... 2... kickout. Thomas goes back to an armbar. Wrench's greatest asset in the ring is his power, and I guess the former Iceman wants to take some of the muscle out of the Preacher's game. Thomas continues to wrench that arm bar. Short clothesline by the Preacher! (nice pop for that one) EL: And he just about took Thomas out of his boots with that one! Thomas tumbled backward, and the Preacher meets him with a kick in the chest- Irish whip, and Wrench catches Thomas, and presses him over his head! With his arms fully extended, he has Marc Thomas 9' off the mat! Nowhere to go but down- Gorilla slam! The cover... 1... 2... kickout. Wrench picks him back up- bearhug. He has Thomas up off the mat and those powerful arms wrapped around the midsection, squeezing the life out of him. Thomas in obvious pain here, as Wrench squeezes harder and harder. Thomas is going to have a lot of trouble if he thinks he'll power out of this one. By the way, next week, we'll find out who gets the shot at Joey Valachi's AEWA World Light heavyweight Title, as Marc Thomas and "Hardcore" Joey Haven fight for the shot, right here, next week! Well, no matter how strong the arms are, the eyes are always vulnerable, and Thomas gets free with an eyerake. Now Thomas backs up a step- superkick sends the Preacher out over the top! Thomas out after him as the Preacher hits the arena floor! Thomas now with a stomp, and he pick up the Preacher and slams his head onto the ring apron. Now he's setting up for that Icebreaker again! Wrench pushes off- Thomas hits the ring post head first! Wrench follows after him now and gives a shot to the midsection, and slams his head on the apron now, too. Thomas slumps to the floor, and Wrench climbs up on the apron- but Thomas has a hold of his left boot! Wrench is trying to get back in the ring, but Thomas hanging on- there's the bell- it looks like we have a double count out here. Yes, the referee is signaling that both men have been counted out. (The crowd groans- they always think this is a cheap result.) EL: Wrench is in the ring now, protesting that he was trying to get back in but Thomas was holding him back. Now Marc Thomas is coming back into the ring- and he nails an Icebreaker! He came right up behind the Preacher and- he's doing it again! And here comes Joey Haven! Thomas sees him coming and gives one more stomp before bailing. Haven in to check on Kore Wrench, and here come Randy Sheradin and Joshua Barnes, as well. We're gonna take another break, now, while the crew starts putting together the cage, and when we come back, the next chapter in the feud between Joey Haven and Jak Hammer will be written. (Another AEWA Extreme ad. They're really trying to ram it down your throats!) EL: OK, we're back, and Joey Haven is still here. Let's get a few words from him. Haven: <speaking in a low voice> Jak Hammer....<speaking louder> Jak Hammer...<screaming> Jak Hammer! The time is coming near! The time is coming where I scrap your fat head right across this chain (points to the big chickenwire fence-style cage being erected behind him) and give you a cut right across your forehead, giving you a scar so big, then when you look in the mirror and see it, the first thing that comes to your mind, is "Joey Haven". I am going to go into this cage, this deathtrap, having and knowing that the fans in Texas are definitely behind me! Hammer, who is supporting you? Not even a jock strap would support you, little p-...no, I won't go there. Not in front of all the beautiful Texan women watching. Jak, this is short and to the point, but remember that its time for a little whoopin......NOW THIS IS HARDCORE!!!!!!!! (JH hopes back in the ring, even though the cage is still not yet complete.) EL: While we wait for the ring crew to finish putting the cage together, lets go to the locker room to see if we can get some words out of the competitors in next week's big title matchup. (EL and his camera man head to the back. After a few turns in the back hallways, they find Joshua Barnes instead. Joshua Barnes is seen picking up a chair and slamming it against a locker, denting the locker door. He slams the chair against the wall twice. When he spots the camera, he drops the chair and faces the camera. He's too angry to give his usual stammer.} JB: Robertson! You lying piece of {beep}! You caused me to lose in the tournament, you tried to get Randy out of the tournament, you lied to both of us, and you spread these idiotic rumors about Randy. I've had it! {Joshua starts to storm out, but Randy "The Dreamer" Sheridan runs into the room and grabs Barnes' arm.} RS: Hold on, Josh. Where are you going? JB: I'm finding Robertson. I'm going to end it now! RS: No you don't, Josh. JB: But... RS: No buts. We'll get him in the ring. Look, I'm as angry as you are. He tried to ruin my good name, and I've worked hard to earn it. But we'll get him in the ring, not in the locker room. We won't go down to that level. {Josh looks around. Finally he nods his head} JB: Fine. I'll wait to get him in the ring. But I'm not promising anything when I finally get my hands around him. I may not be as good as you or he is in wrestling, but one thing I can do is fight. I'm sorry- I can't believe I fell for that garbage. RS: No problem- I also thought I could trust him. And don't worry- we'll get him back. {Randy and Josh leave the room, with Joshua stopping to toss a chair across the floor as he leaves.} (EL and crew and head back into the hallway and go back in the opposite direction. Before long, they find the Professional.) EL: Mr. Robertson, what is the meaning of all this?! What could have caused you to turn on your friend and comrade, Randy Sheridan? MR: Okay, listen up, because I'm only going to say this once! Randy Sheridan is not now and has never been a friend of "The Professional" Max Robertson. When I made my professional wrestling debut here in the CWAA, that match meant more to me than anything in the world. I'd been waiting for that for my whole life. Then, The Dreamer comes along and ruins it for me! How dare he! You better watch your back from now on, Mr. Dreamer, because this is FAR from over. You ruined one of MY dreams just a few weeks ago, but I'll be DAMNED before I'll let you ruin this one. I'll do whatever it takes to walk away as the Heavyweight Champion next week. That's the Professional way. That's the only way. (Exit MR) EL: OK- they should be done with the cage by now, let's go back out to the ring area. (They are done, and Joey Haven, who could probably stand to take a few less chair shots in the near future, is testing the strength of the obviously low-budget cage but hurling his body against the walls. This is a cheap, WCCW style cage: chicken wire, and only abbot 1-2 feet taller than the wrestlers themselves. You do not want to climb these walls.) ("Bad to the Bone" plays and Jak Hammer heads to the ring to an avalanche of heel heat. He climbs the ring steps and hesitates at the door, instructing the referee to make sure Haven leaves him enough room to get in unmolested.) EL: Ever since the opening minutes of our first appearance, Joey Haven and Jak Hammer have just had nothing but trouble from each other. we've had matches that couldn't be started, foreign objects, a vicious no-disqualification and no-countout match, and now we've put them in a cage. On AEWA Extreme, they'll take it to toe other extreme, as instead of penning them in a cage, they'll let them loose all over the arena. OK- Jak Hammer heads in- and Joey Haven with a running kneelift, knocks Hammer into the cage wall- Irish whip- clothesline by Joey Haven! Now he straddles his opponent and starts unloading a series of punches on Jak Hammer's head. Now he grabs Hammer by the head and rams him into the cage wall- no- Hammer blocks it. Haven tries again- blocked again. Haven spins him around- a kick in the... uh... lower abdomen, I suppose- (crowd pops. Hammer does not.) EL: -and now he rams Hammer's head into the cage wall, and rubs it across the chickenwire. Now Haven with a snap suplex. Covers... 1... 2... kickout. Joey Haven off the ropes- fistdrop. Now- eww- he's biting Jak Hammer's forehead, and I see a little blood on Jak's forehead. Now Joey Haven sits him down in a reverse chinlock. (Yes, in my quest for at least semi-realism, I give you rest holds in a cage!) EL: That cut on Jak Hammer's head doesn't look like it would cause him much trouble, but I think a tetanus shot is in his future. Jak Hammer, with a 70-pound weight advantage in this one, is starting to get his feet underneath him. He's standing up, with Haven hanging on his back with that chinlock- Hammer backs up and rams Haven's back into the cage wall! Haven drops to the apron, between the ropes and the cage. Hammer grabs Have by the hair and pulls down, snapping Haven's neck on the top rope. Haven standing limply on the apron, and Hammer backs up- avalanche- and he crushes Haven against the cage wall- which almost came loose! Now Hammer picks him up- big backbreaker- just dropping Haven right across the knee. He does it again- now a third time! He covers... 1... 2... kickout! Now he pulls Haven back up- piledriver coming up- and he plants him in mid ring! That move is so devastating, it has actually been banned in 37 states, plus several US territories- lateral press... 1... 2... kickout again! Now Jak Hammer pulls off Joey's t-shirt and wraps it around Joey's neck. Joey flailing wildly as he tries to breathe and the referee begins to count. Hammer lets go now- oh- just to break the count, as he's choking again! (The boos are getting very loud now) EL: Now Hammer takes that t-shirt and wipes the blood from his forehead with it and tosses it back in Haven's face. Now he locks on a full nelson- locks the fingers and swings Haven from side to side. The referee is checking for a submission as Hammer swings Haven around. Joey Haven pushes his fee off against the cage wall, and he and Jak Hammer fall backward- a pinning predicament here... 1... 2... Hammer kicks out, and releases the hold, and boy does he look frustrated. He whips Haven into the corner. As Haven bounces out, Hammer whips him back across into the opposite corner. Now he takes Joey Haven- power bomb coming up- he slams him right down... 1... 2.... kickout. Now Hammer pulls him up by the hair, drags him over to the ropes- big right hand to soften him up- whips across the ring and headfirst into the cage wall! Now he reaches into his pocket- pulls out a chain, or some brass knuckles- hard to tell from here- whatever it is, he's wrapping it around or putting it on his knuckles- and he nails Haven with a fist drop, opening a laceration on his head, it looks like. Now he drags Haven over to the cage wall and starts raking his forehead over it. I can here him taunting Haven from here- he's telling him he picked the rustiest part of the wall- someone else is going to need that tetanus shot! Irish whip by Hammer- elbowsmash. He picks Haven up again- backbreaker. Now he's signaling for the Rebel Rack- he hoists up Haven onto his shoulders- he's got it locked on! Blood dripping down Haven's face, a back softened up by all of those backbreakers and being thrust against that cage wall. The referee checks for a submission- Joey Haven hooks his feet on the top rope, hooks an arm around Hammer's head- looks like he;s going to get out of this one- no! Hammer leans back and sandwiches Haven between Hammer and the cage wall- ning. And also next week, Joey Haven and Marc Thomas for the shot at Valachi, Robertson and Sheridan for the CWAA Title! See you then! -kl el Rack and countering with a bulldog! (AC/DC's "Highway to Hell" plays) EL: Without that cage wall, he wouldn't have had anything to push off of, but he got it! Going into AEWA Extreme and next week's big matchup with Marc Thomas, he has some real momentum now! Jak Hammer sits dejected in the ring as Joey Haven leaves- and here comes Marc Thomas down the aisle! The referee opens the door to let Haven out- and Thomas slams it on him! That door hit him right in the face! Haven is knocked on his back, and now he's in the cage with Marc Thomas and Jak Hammer! Jak Hammer back up and stomping away- now he whips Haven into the ropes- double clothesline! Now Thomas with an Icebreaker! We're going to have to go- we're out of time! Someone needs to get in there and help Joey Haven! Next week we hope to show you some of the videotape Mark Sherrick thrust at me earlier this evening. And also next week, Joey Haven and Marc Thomas for the shot at Valachi, Robertson and Sheridan for the CWAA Title! See you then! -kl
