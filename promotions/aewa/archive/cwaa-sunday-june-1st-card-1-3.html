Sunday, June 1st, Card (1/3)

3 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last Week on Championship Wrestling Association of America David Myers: Ladies and gentlemen, the time limit has expired- this match is ruled a draw. (The crowd always hates these decisions) Eric Lane: Randy Sheradin asking for five more minutes- pleading with official Chris Herman. We've got one more match to get to tonight, though, our big special feature match still to come. Chris Herman talking things over with Marc Thomas- Thomas nods. David Myers back in the ring- what's going on? DM: Ladies and gentlemen, referee Chris Herman is allowing this match to continue for five more minutes, (Pop) EL: Look out! Sheridan still talking to the timekeeper, and Thomas nailed him with that Icebreaker from behind! **************************************************** Eric Lane: Hello, everyone, and welcome back to the world-famous Sportatorium in beautiful downtown Dallas, Texas, for another exciting week of Championship Wrestling. As you saw just a moment ago, last week the Dreamer and Marc Thomas took the action all the way to the time limit and beyond, before a sneak attack, vintage Marc Thomas, helped put away the former CWAA Champion, and ever since the action in the CWAA Headquarters has been fast and furious, as Thomas and his people have begun negotiating for a match with CWAA Heavyweight Champion, Max "the Professional" Robertson. Later this evening, we'll be talking to Max about these developments. But we'll have plenty of action in the ring this week, as we get our first look at Jimmy Dangerous, plus it's champion versus champion as Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson defends his TV title against CWAA Tag Team Champion Paul "the Tornado" Langford- you won't want to miss it! But before we get started, let's look at some footage of two of the young stars we'll be seeing in action here tonight. *********************************************************************** [Fade up on a medium sized, fairly well built man. He has medium length brown hair, obviously in the process of having it grow out after a real short trim previously. He has on a black t-shirt with the words "Danger Zone!" across it in thick blue lettering. The man speaks into the camera.] Man: Hello to everybody in the CWAA, fans and wrestlers alike. My name is Jimmy Dangerous, and I'm on my way to your fair league. I am currently in the Universal Wrestling Federation as well, and I've watched the action that the CWAA puts out with interest. Men like Paul Langford and Travis Conley of Twister, and "The Dreamer" Randy Sheridan have shown the fans that great action doesn't always come from the big leagues, that the CWAA is right up there talent-wise. I've heard the ramblings from Joe Reed of the AEWA about the UWF, and please don't think I'm in a similar frame of mind. I respect the competition here in the CWAA, and I think it's just as tough as in any other league. My journey begins tonight with Eddie Intellecta, and I intend to start my career here off with a bang. See you in the ring, Eddie. Bring your fighting spirit. ************************************************************************ (Eric Lane is standing in the aisleway of the CWAA Sportatorium, and the crew is setting up things around him. A man is seen walking towards Lane. He has jet black hair, with the sides shaved, and the top pulled back into a pony-tail. He is wearing a black sleeveless trenchcoat, jeans, and a black T-shirt. Lane recognizes the man to be Mark Sherrick.) EL: What are you doing out her so early, Mark? MS: Well, it seems that this is the only time that I can get some time to talk without the crowd or somebody yelling or screaming at me. EL: They can't help it, you sort of goad them into it. MS: So what. Anyway, right now, I don't know who I am facing tonight, and if I am facing anybody. But if I am, then they should know that they are in for the butt-kicking of their life. I may look like the same Mark Sherrick right now, but I have a different take-no-prisoner attitude, and I will destroy all in my path of wherever I want to go. EL: Well, I'm sure that the CWAA was able to find you an opponent, even if you don't know who it is- will you be ready? MS: Oh, I'll be ready, to kick some major (expletive deleted), no matter who it is, when it is. As a matter of fact, if somebody came out right now, I'd kick their butt, and then wrestle again later tonight. You can bet that I will rise to the top of the CWAA, by any means necessary. No matter who, where or when, I will give all a beating to remember. (Sherrick turns, and walks away, disappearing through a door in the wall.) **************************************************************************** EL: So Jimmy Dangerous takes on the CWAA's resident genius- Eddie Intellecta- later tonight, and, well, Mark Sherrick may not know who he'll be facing, but we do- 315 lb. of CWAA Tag Team Champion. That's right, Travis Conley, one-half of Twister, has put his name on the dotted line of Sherrick's one contract, and what a collision it will be when those two get into the ring. And when we get back, that is precisely what we'll see. Don't touch that dial! (Coming June 2nd, only on pay per view, it's ACWF Spring Fling! Joey Valachi tackles the Schooner, Nick McGill. The tag champs have Deadly Intentions for the New Sensations. Ricky Reno goes for the gold. And the Scholar and Ian Mantle will spin the wheel and make a deal. But there's only one place to see these and all the rest of the ACWF stars- call your local cable operator today!) EL: All right, let's go to David Myers for the introductions for this, our first big match. David Myers: Ladies and the gentlemen, welcome to the world-famous Sportatorium, All matches this evening are sanctioned by the American E-Wrestling Association. Our first match is set for one fall, with a ten-minute time limit. Introducing first- (Marilyn Manson's "Beautiful People" starts to play to a chorus of boos) DM: -from here in Dallas, Texas, weighing in at 275 lb., Mark Sherrick! (Mark may be the same surly jerk, but at least he has a new look. The 6'4"-275 lb. not-quite-favorite-son here in his hometown wears a black and purple version of Bret Hart's style of tights, with his (Sherrick's, not Hart's) initials on the back of the tank top part. He also wears a black sleeveless trenchcoat to ringside and he now has a glove on his right hand) DM: And his opponent- (Pops aplenty as the theme to "Twister" (the movie, not the game) plays) DM: -accompanied to the ring by his manager, Mother Nature, from Omaha, Nebraska, at 315 lb., he is one-half of the CWAA Tag Team Champions, Travis Conley! (The short-blond-haired and blue-eyed ex-Husker is as always clad in his brown Twister trunks.) DM: Your referee, Jeff Howard. EL: OK- and Mark Sherrick goes right on the attack- a double sledge as Conley was handing his title belt to the ring attendant. Now Sherrick whacks him with a kneelift, that knee right into the solar plexus. I don't care how big and tough you are, any blow there packs a wallop. Now he scoops him up- slam. Lackadaisical cover- 1... kickout. (Mother Nature takes a seat next to Lane) EL: Good evening, Mother Nature. MN: Hello, Eric. EL: Now it's Mark Sherrick with a big clothesline. May I ask, while we have you here, why the champs would sign for solo matches this week? MN: Well, Paul was contacted by the CWAA and AEWA about a possible match for the TV title several weeks ago- get him, Travis! (This in response to a punch by Conley, but Sherrick counters with an eyerake) MN: Well, of course, Paul wanted to jump at the chance. Who wouldn't? To keep fresh, Travis wanted a match for himself, and we saw Sherrick come out here bragging about an open contract, and Travis thought if he couldn't be out here defending with pride his tag team championship, he may as well take this Sherrick character down a notch or two. EL: And now Sherrick just tosses his man right out onto the arena floor. Travis looking very displeased, but unhurt. And look at that! (Sherrick raises both hands in victory and howls something, and the crowd reacts very badly) EL: Travis back in the ring now, and- watch out! Both men in there now, standing nose to nose, toe to toe, yakking away at each other. Mark with a big two-handed shove- and Conley pushes him right back! (Pop as Sherrick is flung into the corner) MN: The best way to handle a bully like this is to stand right up to him and look right in the eye. They always do talk better than they can fight. EL: Mark with a right hand- blocked- and Travis fires back! (Pop) MN: He's got that Husker fire going now. EL: Sherrick fires back- blocked- and Conley scoops him up- what power! That is at least 325 lb. he has up there- slams him right down! (Pop) MN: Travis definitely has a power advantage over just about anyone here in the CWAA- benches up to 600 lb. now. EL: Impressive indeed. Elbow drop- 1... Sherrick kicks out. MN: He should have hooked that leg- when we show him this video, he'll be awfully embarrassed. EL: Now he goes to the side headlock. MN: A deceptively painful hold, the headlock. It looks so simple, but think about arms that big, that powerful, squeezing your head like boa constrictors. The blood to the head gets cut off, so the hold not only hurts now, but it saps your strength as the match goes on. EL: Sherrick now, with some pretty impressive power himself- gets Conley up- atomic kneedrop! That'll slow Travis down. Now Sherrick slams his face into that top turnbuckle. MN: There's real steel on those buckles beneath that padding- if you're in the ring, you can't make the mistake of thinking that they're soft. EL: And now he rakes the champ's face across his bootlaces! Jeff Howard warning Sherrick. And now Sherrick chokes him on that top rope- Jeff Howard issuing a 5-count MN: I have nothing but the utmost respect for the work he and Chris do in that ring every week, but Jeff is on the verge of losing control of this, and he really ought to be stepping in there and asserting his authority. EL: Irish whip- sleeper! Sleeperhold by Sherrick! MN: Come on, Travis! Hang in there! Hang in there! EL: Senior official Jeff Howard right on top, making sure that arm does not slip under the chin and become a choke. Conley in serious trouble- jabs an elbow to the midsection- and another- he broke it! (Pop) EL: Sherrick doubled over- snap suplex! Conley measures him now- knee drop, right across the forehead. Stands him up- Irish whip- powerslam! Wow- he just slammed 350 lb. of Mark Sherrick- 1... 2... no! Kickout! MN: No! I thought he really had him there! EL: Stands him up- scoop- Sherrick wraps him up- small package! MN: He has the ropes! He has the ropes! EL: 1... 2... 3! Mark Sherrick got him! MN: Eric, you saw it he had a hold of that middle rope for leverage- Travis couldn't roll out of it because Sherrick had that rope! EL: Let's get the official word from David Myers. DM: Here is your winner, Mark Sherrick! (Boos, boos, boos) MN: I'm going to have to talk with Jeff Howard about this. (Sherrick leaves as MN pleads her case with Jeff Howard, but the decision stands.) EL: Mark Sherrick got away with one there. When we come back, Jak Hammer takes on Little Yvette. Stay tuned! *************************************************************************** [The screen fades in to a studio with over two dozen monitors in the background with the 1997 King of Wrestling logo on them. A man, approximately 180 pounds, 25 years of age, with a dark blonde mushroom cut sits at a control desk in front of the monitors. The man is a well-known UWF wrestling host, Moe Owens. Moe looks to the camera and speaks.] MO: "Hello all you great wrestling fans out there. For those of you that do not know me, my name is Moe Owens. Some may know me because of my UWF work, but right now I come to you as the official spokesperson for the upcoming spectacular, the 1997 King of Wrestling tournament and battle royal. This is your first notice of the King of Wrestling. Allow me to explain a little about this inter-federation spectacular: The 1997 King of Wrestling is a special Pay Per View extravaganza to be held on Sunday, August 17th, live from Sky Dome in Toronto, Ontario. It will be a 30 man over-the-top-rope royal rumble to declare the 1997 King of Wrestling. However, there are 60 slots available for wrestlers to sign in to. Now, I am sure you are wondering: what the heck I am talking about- 30 wrestlers, but 60 slots? Let me explain. 60 wrestlers will be officially entered into the tournament. There will be a one-round, single-elimination tournament to be held. The rule is simple: in order to make it to the battle royal, you must first qualify by beating an opponent in a one on one contest! 30 men will be narrowed down from the 60 and will be entered! And in case of double elimination, a list of reserves will be kept! In July, there will be three nights of singles action, with ten matches each! And these will be aired for free on the RSPWF wrestling network! Now, here is why I am talking to you right now... we need wrestlers! Before I tell you how a wrestler can enter, I will go over the list of names already entered in the 1997 King of Wrestling tournament... Our first entrant is a UWF newcomer, Sebastion Jericho. I've seen this guy folks, and he is awesome! And a HUGE name in the wrestling industry has signed in... coming out of retirement, former IIWF World champion, now known as 'The White Angel', Dan Kauffman!!! What a name that is! And also signed in are former UWF World champion, the tricky Brett Greene and a mysterious man from ACWF, the Mexican competitor known as Vortex! And that is just the beginning! Well, folks, my time is just about out. Don't forget wrestlers, to sign in to earn the right to be called the 1997 King of Wrestling! Thanks for your time, I am, as always, Moe Owens!" [The studio lights drop as the following legal stuff flashes on screen] [All handlers interested please send in the following character information for ONE wrestler, to [detail withheld] . Please only one wrestler per handler, but, if required, that may change. Until then, only one. More info will be e-mailed to those interested. TNM will be used, but role-playing is __STRONGLY__ encouraged and I shall book to those who provide quality role-playing. Therefore, the golden rule of: good writing ='s better push applies to this. Again, any questions or comments to be sent to: [detail withheld] (Thanks to [name withheld] for the character sheet) Name of Wrestler: Name of Handler: e-mail: Federation wrestler is from: theme music(optional) type of character:(standard TNM styles) finisher: move that finisher resembles: setup to finisher: weight: (total of 240 points for next three categories) worker: push: stamina: manager(if one): does the manager interfere?: if so, what weapon?: alignment(face/heel/neutral): does he use chairs?: ******************************************************************************* EL: OK- we're back, and let's hear from two top stars who've returned for another engagement here in the world-famous Sportatorium. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, my guests at this time, returning to the CWAA, please welcome Cooter Lee Roy and Orville Ray Bob Tucker, also known as "Orbit." They are The Trailer Park Assassins! (Lynyrd Skynyrd's classic "Freebird" starts up, and the crowd pops big time for Cooter and Orbit. The cynics among us might argue that Texas wrestling fans would cheer Josef Stalin if he came to the ring with "Freebird" playing, but the TPA's really seem to have a great rapport with the crowd. Whether this reflects the down home charm of the TPA's or the questionable taste of the crowd is a question for another day. David Myers receives a handshake from Cooter and a bear hug from Orbit. Both are a little harder than they need to be, so Myers takes a moment to recover before continuing.) DM: Thanks for coming, guys. How does it feel to be back in the CWAA? CLR: It feels great, David. There's just something about wrestling in the Sportatorium that Orbit and me really like. (Orbit gives a big thumbs up sign, to more cheers.) DM: Tonight, you get another crack at Byron McNultey and Michael Anderson, who beat you in the CWAA Tag Team Title tournament. Have you been looking forward to getting revenge? CLR: Man, we're just looking forward to wrestling again. For a while now, we've been "in negotiations" with various organizations, but I think something would have been worked out by now. Maybe it's our agent. Have you ever heard of Vito Tusconi? DM: The Vito Tusconi who was just indicted by a federal grand jury for 32 counts of fraud and embezzlement? (Cooter's jaw drops. Orbit looks confused, as he struggles with the meaning of the world "embezzlement.") CLR: Uh-oh. Anyway, we called the CWAA up, and asked if we could wrestle, and they said "Sure!" So we get another crack at these two cocky pretty boys, and if there's one thing me and Orbit love, it's whomping the tar out of cocky pretty boys. (Orbit gets a sadistic little grin on his face.) CLR: So if MacCauley Culkin and Lord Byron are listening, I hope you wrote down how to get home, cause we're gonna beat you about the head and shoulders so hard you'll forget. And don't think you can do the same, because Orbit doesn't know how he got here to begin with. Tell 'em, Orbit. (To thunderous applause, Orbit smiles and spits. With that, the Trailer Park Assassins exit as "Freebird" continues to play.) EL: There they go, two youngsters the fans here in Dallas, and everywhere across the great land of ours, just love. Now we're ready for some more action between two competitors who have met before, and who are both right in the thick of the TV title situation. Let's go to David Myers for the introduction.
