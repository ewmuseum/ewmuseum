CWAA Supershow pt 7

3 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EL: Let's go to ring announcer David Myers to hear from impressive newcomer, the Samurai! DM: Ladies and Gentlemen let me introduce to you to a wrestler who debuted just two weeks ago, "The Samurai" Kensei Tokugawa. (Sammy Hagar's "Marching to Mars" plays over the PA. Out from behind to curtain comes a beautiful oriental woman. She has long black hair, wearing a black spandex outfit with black heels. She enters the ring.) DM: Ahh! Uum! Miss, you're not Mr. Tokugawa! Mysterious lady: I certainly hope not. (As she gives a big smile and runs her hand down David Myers chest.) DM: (Taking a deep breath) Wel,l who are you? Are you a manager of Mr. Tokugawa? I mean your oriental, he's oriental.... Mysterious lady: My name is Ms. Miki. I'm not the manger of "The Samurai" Kensei Tokugawa, merely a friend. (David gets ready to say something) Ms. Miki: Shhhhh! I'll do the talking. (She runs her hand down the side of David's face, and with her other hand takes the microphone.) Let me introduce a Legend of Japan, and future CWAA Heavyweight champion. His heritage is that of greatness. His ancestors ruled Japan during the Edo period, in which we experienced over two hundred years of peace. They ruled with honor and respect and now he's come here to deliver the same--"The Samurai" Kensei Tokugawa. ("Marching to Mars" plays over the PA as "The Samurai" in full Samurai garb with Kendo stick enters the ring to a mix of boos and cheers, as fans remember his confrontations with both Jimmy Dangerous and Max Robertson.) Ms. Miki: (She walks over to "The Samurai" and starts an interview.) Mr. Tokugawa-san do you have any words for the CWAA fans and wrestlers? Kensei Tokugawa: Yes, I do? I want to make one thing clear. The last two weeks I've attacked a fallen opponent after the match, for this I apologize. I did not do that to injure them, I did it to send a message to CWAA wrestlers...I will not tolerate any interference in my matches. I stand for honor and the belief that the best man wins. However, in the CWAA it seems that Max Robertson and Marc Thomas don't understand that. I now challenge Max Robertson to a match, and should you not want to play by the rules ("The Samurai" pulls his Kendo stick off his back and points to it.) I'll be forced to fight fire with fire. As to Marc Thomas...after your match with the AEWA champion, I'll be ready to defeat you as well. Ms. Miki: Enough of that my sweet, what about the question on the lips of all the CWAA fans...What is your finisher called? KT: Let it be known as "Shinzo Mahi"!!! Ms. Miki: For all the American fans, in English it stands for "Heart Attack". (Ms. Miki turns to David Myers and waves him over and hands the mic back to him, while "The Samurai" drops down, rolls out the ring and leaves with Ms. Miki.) DM: Kensei Tokugawa, ladies and gentlemen. (The Samurai leaves to a mixed reaction) EL: All right, we are all set for CWAA Tag Team Championship action- let's go back to the ring! DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next bout is set for one fall with a twenty-minute time limit, and is for the CWAA Tag Team Championship! (pop) DM: Introducing first, the challengers- (pop as the "Twister" theme plays) DM: -at a total combined weight of 559 pounds, here are "The Tornado" Paul Langford and Travis Conley- the team of Twister! Paul Langford: Anderson, McNultey, ever since you stole the belts from us, we have wrestled you week after to week, and those belts would be ours if not for your underhanded tactics. So, tonight, in front of this great crowd, we decided to bring someone out here to watch our backs, and prevent you two from any sneak attacks. And he isn't who you think it is. Kelly, would you please bring down our special guest. (The "Twister" theme begins to play again, and out from the locker room area comes Kelly Perich, and coming down the aisle with her is Larry Allen, Offensive Lineman for the Dallas Cowboys, wearing his Cowboys jersey. The crowd goes wild! Kelly and Larry step into the ring) Travis Conley: I knew Larry back from my days of playing football, and I would like to publicly thank him for coming out tonight. Larry Allen: No problem, Travis and Paul. I have made my reputation protecting Troy Aikman's back, and I am glad to put that reputation on the line tonight protecting yours. (Kelly and Larry exit the ring) DM: And their opponents- (a round of boos greets the opening chords of "Sharp Dressed Man") DM: -at a total combined weight of 497 pounds, they are the CWAA Tag Team champions, "Marvelous" Michael Anderson and "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey! (In keeping with the seriousness of the event, the duo have again resumed wearing their traditional sequined robes in favor of the oddly-hued tuxedos and Hawaiian shirts) DM: Your referee, Chris Herman. EL: Ok- Chris Herman to call this, the latest in the series of battles between these teams. The Tornado and he Pretty Boy to kick things off. They lock up- arm drag takedown by Langford. He's lightning quick, the Tornado. Another arm drag takedown, into an armbar, and Paul Langford taking the fight to his man right from the get-go. Byron McNultey now pushing his opponent back against the ropes- referee Chris Herman calls for the break- and McNultey with a forearm club! And Langford responds with a chop- and another- sends him for the ride- cross body block! But such is his anger that he doesn't make the cover- he punches and punches and punches! Irish whip- and the Pretty Boy hits him with a clothesline! Stomps away, and now the tag to Marvelous Michael Anderson. Both men stomping away now- Chris Herman with the 5 count. Anderson sets him up- vertical suplex. Putting the boots to the Tornado again now. And now he tags the Pretty Boy back in. Frequent tags- these men work like a well-oiled machine. If you don't believe me, just ask them- they'll tell you. McNultey now stomps at the upper thigh area. He's got the foot- he's going for it- spinning toe hold. Twisting Langford's ankle. Byron McNultey going to one of his favorite maneuvers. (Conley, Perich and Allen lead the crowd in a chant of "Go, Paul, Go!") EL: And Paul Langford catches him in a small package- 1... 2... kickout! And The Pretty Boy is furious! Stomps away, now he tags the Marvelous One in. They send him for the ride- double back body drop! Again- doub- no! Langford ducks the clothesline, and- (pop as Conley runs in and steamrolls the champs with a double clothesline) EL: The champs bail out for a timeout, and Langford and Conley make the tag! And look out! (the sight of Larry Allen bearing down on them convinces the champs to adjourn their meeting and get back to the ring) EL: Michael Anderson slaps a headlock on Travis Conley now. Conley pushes off- Anderson off the ropes- Conley with a shoulder block! (pop) EL: Anderson off the ropes again- Husker Slam! (Power slam) EL: 1... 2... no! He kicks out! Almost ran right into the end of his title reign! Travis Conley sends him to the corner- in he goes! (pop as Conley finishes the avalanche) EL: Sends him back across to the other side- no one home! (Conley misses the second avalanche attempt, and Anderson covers as Conley falls on his back) EL: He's got his feet on the middle turnbuckle for leverage- 3! No! He used the ropes for leverage! Paul Langford and Travis Conley protesting, but referee Chris Herman indicating that the champs have retained their titles, raises Anderson's hand. Here's Dav- wait a second! Larry Allen on the arena floor explaining to Chris Herman what happened, and- (pop as Chris Herman throws Anderson's hand back down) EL: Chris Herman ordering the match restarted! Michael Anderson over by the ropes- in Larry Allen's face- and Allen pops him! (pop) EL: Anderson has just been decked by the all-pro lineman- and Conley covers- 1... 2... 3! New champions! New champions! (pop as the Twister theme plays) DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winners, and once again CWAA Tag Team champions, Paul Langford, Travis Conley, the team of Twister! EL: The crowd is just erupting for this duo as they regain the CWAA Tag Team titles- and Michael Anderson and Byron McNultey are beside themselves. McNultey now shoves Allen- and Larry Allen with a right hand for him! Larry Allen clearing the ring like it were the Redskins' defensive front! Once again, the CWAA Tag Team champions are Twister! When we get back, we'll see Elektro and the Freak in action! Stay tuned!
