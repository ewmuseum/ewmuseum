Mega Combat Countdown (10/4, part two)

5 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
****CONTINUED FROM PART ONE**** MW: Joey Valachi is taking on Juan Lopez in that much anticipated lumberjack match. LB: Yes, and finally Valachi gets to wrestle a match against a man without having to worry about the Italian Loan Collection Agency beating the crap out of him. BD: Yep, there'll be twenty wrestlers surrounding the ring to make sure that nobody gets in or out. MW: I'm sure that Valachi is relieved by that, and he'll be even more relieved if he wins this match, because if that happens, Lopez has promised to pay Joey's debt to the Italian Loan Collection Agency. LB: That's worth a lot more to him than any title right about now, MW: I'll bet. And now, without any further ado, let's go to the ring! RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is a lumberjack match, and is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, in no particular order, the lumberjacks! Reuben "Thunder" Slade and "Superfly" Aaron Ladd-- The Shadow Warriors! "Crazy Horse" Clint Worrel! Red Dog! "Slim" Jim Coolridge! Sewerdog! Sky Walker! "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews! The Gryphon! Twisted Steel! Scorpion! "Texas Wild Fire" Ricky Roberts! "Superman" Mike Stewart! The Twilight Strangler! "The Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum! Deathwatch! Paul Wong and Simon O'Neal-- The Machines! Sir Gallade! And finally, Primal Fear! (The men come out one by one and take their places at ringside.) BD: There's a lot of bad blood in this group. MW: Tell me about it, you've got half the feuds in the AEWA down there! Slocum and Gallade with Twisted Steel and Roberts, Primal Fear with the Twilight Strangler, former tag team partners Red Dog and Sky Walker.... I could go on. LB: Please don't. RING ANNOUNCER: And now to introduce the combatants! First, weighing in at 202 pounds, "The Cardiac Kid" Joey Vaaaaaaaaaaaaalachiiiiiiii! ("Heartbreaker" begins playing, and everyone looks back toward the curtain, but no one emerges.) BD: Where the hell is Valachi? MW: There he is-- he just crawled out from underneath the ring, wearing another very bad disguise! What is that, a Santa beard? He looks ridiculous. Now that he's in the ring, he's removing that disguise. He must've been hiding from the Italian Loan Collection Agency! LB: Has he been under there ALL NIGHT? MW: Apparently. Those Italians really have him petrified, and he's finally gonna get his chance to get rid of them tonight. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 228 pounds, "The Flying Mexican" Juan Loooooooooooooooooopez! ("La Cucaracha" plays loudly over the PA as Lopez makes his way toward the ring, to a big crowd pop.) BD: This Lopez makes me sick. MW: You don't need him to do that, you're already sick. BD: And that's the way I like it. MW: And now Lopez is in the ring, and Valachi wants to get this one started in the worst way! Valachi charges in, but Lopez side steps! Lopez is behind Valachi now, and he jumps onto his back, CRUCIFIX! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: He almost caught Valachi with a quick move there! MW: He sure did, both men are quickly back up to their feet, and Lopez jumps onto Valachi's back AGAIN! ANOTHER CRUCIFIX! One..Kickout! BD: Valachi was ready for it that time. MW: Yes he was, and now we're gonna have to get down to some wrestling. Both men up now, Lopez grabs Valachi, AND TAKES HIM DOWN WITH A FACESLAM! Lopez brings Valachi up now, and whips him to the ropes! Valachi comes back, and Lopez jumps onto him with ANOTHER crucifix! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Okay, that's enough, Juan. We've established that you can do a pretty good crucifix. MW: Lopez brings Valachi to his feet now, and LEVELS him with a leg lariat! Lopez whips Valachi to the turnbuckle, and charges in shoulder first behind him-- BUT VALACHI LIFTS HIS KNEE! And Lopez runs right into it! BD: Great thinking, Joey! MW: Valachi grabs Lopez by the head now, and runs his head along the top rope, ROPEBURN! And now Valachi just dumps Lopez over the top! LB: And he landed right on top of Twisted Steel, and he looks pissed! He's beating the crap out of Lopez now! MW: And Ricky Roberts is helping him, this is disgusting-- this isn't why these guys are here! But here come Ben Slocum and Sir Gallade from the other side of the ring! They're going to work on Steel and Roberts, and we've got an unscheduled, 4-way brawl going on down there! BD: But our match is still going on too. Lopez has just been helped back into the ring, and Valachi looks very happy with what's transpired. MW: Of course he is; he doesn't care HOW he wins, he just wants to win. Valachi picks up Lopez now, takes him to the other side of the ring, and tosses him out again! LB: Right in front of Red Dog! MW: How convenient. Red Dog and Sewerdog, quite an interesting combo, are now putting the boots to Lopez-- and they toss him back in! And Valachi couldn't be happier! BD: I would be happy too. MW: That's because you and Valachi BOTH have no morals. I think that brawl between the Angels of Death and Slocum & Gallade has finally subsided, by the way. Those guys were really tearing into each other. And now Valachi picks Lopez up again, AND TOSSES HIM OUT AGAIN! LB: And now he's in front of the Shadow Warriors! This is funny! MW: And the Shadow Warriors get their licks in, and toss Lopez back in again. Valachi is letting the lumberjacks fight his match for him! BD: I know. Great strategy, huh? MW: Not really. Valachi picks up Lopez off the mat now, and he's gonna toss him out AGAIN! He brings Lopez over to the ropes now, BUT SCORPION GRABS VALACHI BY THE LEG, AND PULLS HIM OUT! This is great! Now Valachi's gonna get a taste of his own medicine! LB: This isn't fair! There's like five guys teaming up on him now! MW: And it WAS fair when Valachi kept tossing Lopez to the wolves? LB: Well, yeah. That's different. MW: Sure it is. Anyway, from what I can see, Scorpion, Mike Stewart, Jim Coolridge, Clint Worrel, and Sky Walker are now extracting revenge for Lopez, and they're doing a pretty darn good job of it. And after a good pounding, they now toss Valachi back into the ring. BD: And Lopez is on his feet now. This sucks! MW: Now Lopez picks Valachi up, and takes him back DOWN with a faceslam! He's waiting for Valachi to rise now, and he pounces on him, CRUCIFIX! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Gee, I wonder where we've seen THAT move before. MW: (sarcastically) Couldn't tell ya. And now Lopez brings Valachi back to his feet, and whips him to the ropes! Valachi comes back, and gets nailed with a kick in the face! Lopez quickly grabs him now, and executes another faceslam! BD: Valachi needs to do something. MW: No kidding, but right now, he CAN'T. Lopez lifts Valachi back up again now, and nails him with a roundhouse right! That staggered Valachi, and now Lopez charges in, and LEVELS Valachi with a clothesline! LB: And Valachi looks like he doesn't know where he is right now. MW: And that's unusual? Anyway, Lopez DROPS a kneeling headbutt onto Valachi. Lopez picks Valachi up now, and grabs him by the head! He sinks in a front facelock, JUMPING DDT--NO! Valachi counters with a backdrop! BD: He's always thinking, believe it or not. MW: In the ring he is, in real life though, his brain is usually out to lunch. Valachi is quick to deliver the stomp to Lopez, and now he's going up top! Valachi is on the top rope, and Lopez is flat on the mat! He dives, FLYING ELBOWDROP! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: For a second there, I thought we were gonna see the "Heartstopper". MW: So did I, but Valachi chose to execute the elbowdrop instead, possibly a mistake. Valachi lifts Lopez to his feet now, and takes a step back. He's setting up for something, BACK HEEL KICK--BUT LOPEZ SIDE STEPS, AND JUMPS ONTO VALACHI WITH A CRUCIFIX! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Boy, when did Lopez learn THAT move? I've NEVER seen it before. MW: Okay guys, I get the point. Both men are quickly up now, and Lopez grabs Valachi and executes a ropeburn! Lopez lifts Valachi up now, FRONT SLAM! LB: (yelling) You can't let the flying Australian beat you, Joey! MW: That's Flying MEXICAN. LB: I know that, and you know that, but Valachi doesn't. I'm speaking his language. MW: I see. Well anyway, Lopez picks Valachi up now, GERMAN SUPLEX!! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Well what do you know, he actually DOES know another pinning combo besides the crucifix. MW: I assure you, he knows a LOT of them, he's just chosen to use the crucifix a lot in this match. Lopez picks up Valachi now, and NAILS him with a chop! He picks Valachi up now, BODYSLAM--NO! Valachi counters with a small package! One..Two..Kickout! LB: What a BRILLIANT counter by Valachi! MW: It was a good move, but not a terribly painful one, and Lopez is already on his feet, and going up top! Valachi has reached his feet, and Lopez dives, FLYING BODYPRESS--BUT VALACHI CATCHES HIM! AND EXECUTES A BACKBREAKER! BD: And Lopez just got caught, big time! MW: He sure did, and Valachi's trying to capitalize. He brings Lopez to his feet, and nails him with a fist to the midsection! And now he's picking Lopez up, JACK-KNIFE POWER BOMB! He PLANTED Lopez! The cover! One..Two..Three! (DING, DING) LB: Joey pulled it off! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of thirteen minutes and fifty seconds, here is your winner.... "THE CARDIAC KID" JOEY VALACHI!!!! MW: And Valachi is going nuts! I don't think I've ever seen him this happy. LB: Me neither, but I think he's entitled to it tonight. MW: I guess he is. Our Lumberjacks are leaving, and it looks like a lot of them are fighting with each other now, but they're still moving towards the exit. BD: Yep. They're leaving, and look who's coming. MW: (sarcastically) What a surprise-- it's "Machine" Gun Ciraulo and "Big" Antonio Sabatini, the Italian Loan Collection Agency. I wonder what they want. LB: Some lasagna? MW: I don't think so. They're heading towards the ring, and Valachi doesn't see them, he's still celebrating, and holding up that toy belt. BD: I think he'd better turn around soon. MW: Too late, they're in the ring. Valachi is posing on the turnbuckle, and Ciraulo pulls him down from behind! Joey looks petrified! They're about to do a number on him, BUT LOPEZ STEPS IN BETWEEN THEM! He's saying something to the Italians. LB: What's he saying? I can't understand a word this guy says. MW: I think he's saying that he always keeps his word. And now he's stepping out of the ring, and he picks up a metal briefcase. Now he's going back in the ring with it. BD: Is the money in there? MW: I guess, because he's handing it to the Italians. Antonio opens the briefcase, and WOW! Look at all that money. The faces of BOTH of the Loan Collectors just LIT UP. Antonio is closing the briefcase now, and the Italian Loan Collection Agency seems very pleased. LB: As well they should be, I know I would be if someone handed me a suitcase full of money. MW: Gun picks up the briefcase now, and he's shaking hands with Lopez. I think he's saying "It's been a pleasure doing business with you." And now he's extending his hand to Valachi. Valachi seems VERY nervous, but he shakes it, and I guess that's it. BD: No more Italian Loan Collection Agency on Valachi's back? MW: I guess not. And now Valachi turns to walk away, AND GETS CLOBBERED OVER THE HEAD WITH THAT BRIEFCASE! AND SABATINI AVALANCHES LOPEZ INTO THE CORNER! They're beating BOTH Valachi AND Lopez to a bloody pulp! What call is there for this? They got their money. LB: They're just plain mean. MW: You've got that right. Ciraulo is calling for a microphone. CIRAULO: (Begins to talk while Sabatini is working over both men.) I know that you paid us and everything Mister Valachi, but this is just TOO much fun. It's even more fun with another guy here to beat up on. You see, we deal in pain, and while we'll accept this money, that doesn't mean that you're other form of payment gets to stop-- not by any means. Our job is to make sure that you are in a state of constant pain and suffering, from now until the day you die, which might be sooner than you think, if you know what I mean. Now Lopez, you're another story. This is your punishment for trying to interfere in our dealings with Mister Valachi. You can't buy us off, we'll beat the hell outta whoever we want to, whenever we want to, because we CAN. This wasn't your business, but you chose to involve yourself in it. Well now you're involved, hope you're happy. (Just after he says this he cracks the briefcase over the skull of Lopez, who was being held up by Antonio.) Come on Antonio, they get the picture. Just remember fellas, we'll be back. (The ILCA walk back to the locker room, as they get pelted with boos and garbage. The paramedics come out and take away Valachi and Lopez.) MW: I can't believe they're gonna continue this vendetta against Valachi after they've been paid several million dollars by Juan Lopez. And now Lopez is on their hit list too? BD: That's what it looks like to me. These guys just don't care, they just want to hurt people! MW: No kidding. These guys CLAIM to be official members of the AEWA, but they haven't wrestled a single match yet, they just come down and beat people up. LB: But they're good at their job, you gotta give them that. MW: That's not all I'd like to give them. BD: Too bad you're too much of a wuss to be able to back up that kinda talk. LB: Yeah, just shut up and tell us what's next. MW: Fine. Let's see here. Marcus McTeague is defending his TV Title against Super-Blonde. BD: Super-Blonde? Hasn't he had like a million title shots already, and lost them all? MW: No, he won the light heavyweight title. LB: And lost it a week later. MW: Well yes, but he's just had bad luck when it comes to titles here in the AEWA. Otherwise, he has an outstanding record, and there's no denying his ability. BD: I'll deny it. MW: You don't count. LB: Do I? MW: Nope. Let's just go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA Television Title! Introducing first, the challenger, weighing in at 202 pounds and accompanied by his manager, Kismet, he is a former AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion of the World, This... Is... Suuuuuuuuuuuuuuper-Bloooooooooooonde! (Elastica's "Connection" booms out over the PA as the Blonde makes his way to the ring, and the crowd goes NUTS! His lovely cousin Kismet is at his side.) BD: I hate this guy-- why do these fans like him so much? MW: Because he's a great athlete and is of fine moral character. BD: Yeah, and that's why I hate him. MW: You have the most messed up logic of anyone I have ever met. Did you eat paint chips as a kid? BD: Who told you that? RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Northern Ontario, he weighs in at 412 pounds and is the AEWA Television Champion, Marcus "Natural Wonder" McTeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeague! (The Screaming Trees' "Sweet Oblivion" plays loudly over the PA as Marcus McTeague makes his entrance to a HUGE crowd pop.) LB: There's NO WAY the Blonde can win this match. He's outweighed by 210 pounds! MW: There's more to wrestling than size. BD: I believe we discussed this earlier, and as Bertha and I concluded, no there's not. The Reaper won, didn't he? MW: Not without help. BD: That's besides the point. MW: No it's not, so just be quiet, and people won't know how stupid you are. BD: Really? MW: Yes. And now McTeague steps into the ring, and the two fine athletes shake hands. LB: Oh joy. MW: You sound SO excited about this match, Bertha. LB: I know. MW: And the bell has rung, and we're underway! The Blonde is starting this one off quickly with a series of right hands that stuns McTeague! And now the Blonde steps out onto the apron, and slingshots himself onto the top rope, SPRINGBOARD DROPKICK--BUT MCTEAGUE SIDE STEPS! And the Blonde lands HARD on the mat! BD: He'll feel that one tomorrow. MW: He's feeling it NOW. The Blonde still makes it back to his feet though, and McTeague takes him down with an armdrag! And now McTeague picks the Blonde up off the mat, and whips him to the ropes! The Blonde comes back, and gets backdropped! LB: I told you the Blonde didn't have a chance. Just look at the way he's being manhandled. MW: We've only just begun. And now McTeague picks the Blonde up off the mat, and grabs him by the neck, CHOKESLAM! He NAILED him with it! The cover! One..Two..Thr..Kickout! BD: The Blonde just escaped by a hair that time. I'd say it's just about over for him. MW: I don't know about THAT, but he's definitely not in good shape right now. McTeague picks the Blonde up off the mat, and he's setting him up for a power bomb! He lifts the Blonde up, AND HE'S GOT IT--NO! THE BLONDE COUNTERS WITH HIS DEATH-BY-BLONDE FRANKENSTEINER!! WHERE DID THAT COME FROM?? The cover! One..Two..Three!!!! (DING, DING) LB: What? It's over already? RING ANNOUNCER: At an official time of FORTY-EIGHT SECONDS, here is your winner, and NEEEEEEEEEEW AEWA Television Champion.... SUPER-BLONDE! MW: The Blonde really pulled that one out of his hat! I don't think ANYONE saw the "Death-By-Blonde" coming, least of all McTeague. And McTeague is STILL stunned by it. I don't even think he knew what happened. BD: I'll tell you what happened-- he got his ass kicked by a 200-pound pansy. MW: That's not the way it happened. McTeague looks upset, but in a great show of sportsmanship, he's shaking the hand of the Blonde again, acknowledging that on this day, he just got caught. And the Blonde's Heavyweight Title Drought is over! LB: Don't worry, he won't have it for long. My sources tell me that at Mega Combat, he'll have to defend it against the very man who took his last title from him, only a week after he won it, "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews. MW: Well that should be quite a match-up. Heck, that ALONE will be worth the price of the Pay-Per-View. LB: You think it's worth $40 to watch the Blonde get his butt kicked? MW: That's not the way it's gonna happen. LB: Sure it is, but we don't have time for your mindless babble. What's next? MW: MY mindless babble? You should talk. LB: I did, now just tell us what's next. MW: Heavy Metal is gonna go one-on-one with Sewerdog in a No Countout, No DQ, Falls-Count-Anywhere, "Revenge Match". It should really be something. Sewerdog has been on Heavy Metal's case for WEEKS, he even stooped so low as to sneak attack him TWICE. BD: And don't think Heavy Metal's gonna forget that. He's gonna try to make Sewerdog suffer, and then some. MW: You don't have to tell me that. LB: Sewerdog made a big mistake attacking the champ, because if there's one type of guy you don't want to face, it's a pissed off 7'4" 450-pound MONSTER. MW: I couldn't think of better words to describe it if I tried. So let's just quit talking about it, and let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is a special "Revenge Match" for the AEWA Heavyweight Championship of the World. There will be NO countouts, NO disqualifications, and falls will count ANYWHERE! Introducing first, the challenger, from the Sewers of New York City, and weighing in at 275 pounds, Seeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeewerdog! (Sewerdog makes his way toward the ring to a large chorus of boos. He doesn't seem to care about the fans' reaction, though.) BD: I think Sewerdog has just bitten off more than he can chew this time. MW: Well he wanted a World Title shot, and he's been claiming for weeks what a wimp Heavy Metal is. Now it's time to put up or shut up. LB: That's the long and short of it. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 450 pounds, he is the AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, Heavy Metaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaal! (Metallica's "Battery" cranks out over the PA as Heavy Metal also makes his entrance to a large round of boos.) MW: And this is the first time I've EVER seen Heavy Metal RUN to the ring. He REALLY wants to get his hands on Sewerdog. BD: And he's just about to. MW: Heavy Metal climbs into the ring, and charges at Sewerdog, who's waiting for him, AND HE CATCHES HEAVY METAL WITH AN ELBOW! That caught him right on the chin and knocked him FLAT! And now Sewerdog drops an elbow! LB: Actually, this is a pretty good start from Sewerdog. MW: It sure is. And now Sewerdog drops a knee onto the champion, and he's really going to work on him. Sewerdog waits for Heavy Metal to get to his feet now, and takes him back down with a single leg takedown! BD: I didn't think Sewerdog was capable of taking it to Heavy Metal like this. MW: No one did. He's out to prove us all wrong, and he's doing a pretty good job of it. And now Sewerdog picks Heavy Metal up, and tosses him out of the ring! And he's going out after him! LB: This is where these matches get fun. MW: And Sewerdog is looking for something under the ring, and he pulls out a fire extinguisher! He winds up, and POUNDS Heavy Metal over the head with it! And now he's spraying it into his eyes! BD: Why's he doing that? Heavy Metal's not on fire. MW: It amazes me how the simplest concepts seem to elude you. And now Sewerdog whips the champ to the guardrail! Heavy Metal just falls flat on the concrete now! Sewerdog delivers a few stomps, and he's going for the cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: The champ's a fighter. MW: That he is. Sewerdog is now climbing back up on the apron, and Heavy Metal's still on the ground. Now Sewerdog gets a running start, FLYING ELBOWDROP TO THE CONCRETE--BUT HEAVY METAL ROLLS AWAY! And Sewerdog looks like he hurt himself, BIG TIME. BD: That's the risk you take when you try moves like that. MW: I'd have to agree. And now the champ is up, and he picks up Sewerdog, AND EXECUTES A FALLAWAY SLAM! And now the champ picks him up again, DOCTOR BOMB ON THE CONCRETE! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Sewerdog's gonna fight till the end too. MW: That's why this one has the chance to be an epic. Heavy Metal picks up Sewerdog now, and nails him with a chop! And now he grabs him, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! BD: But he DOES appear to be weakening. MW: They both do. Heavy Metal waits for Sewerdog to get up now, and LEVELS him with a clothesline! Heavy Metal scoops the challenger up off the concrete, and lifts him up, RUNNING POWERSLAM ON THE CONCRETE! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: You gotta give credit to Sewerdog for that one. Not just anyone could've kicked out of that. MW: No sir. Heavy Metal picks Sewerdog up now, and NAILS him with a faceslam! Heavy Metal lifts him up again, grabs a hold of him, SNAP SUPLEX! BD: But Sewerdog REALLY is in trouble. He NEEDS to get this thing turned around, and NOW. MW: That's the truth. Heavy Metal goes up onto the apron, and waits for Sewerdog to rise, MOONSAULT BODYBLOCK ONTO THE FLOOR! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Getting closer. MW: Yes, he is, but not yet. Heavy Metal picks him up now, FALLAWAY SLAM! Sewerdog's REALLY taking a pounding. Heavy Metal waits for him to get up, and FLOORS him with a lariat! And here's another cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! BD: Heavy Metal is unbelievable, but Sewerdog is maybe even more so. He's absorbed everything the champ's thrown at him, and he's still kicking out. MW: That's quite an accomplishment. I believe that Heavy Metal is complaining about a slow count on that last one, but the ref will give no ground. Heavy Metal FINALLY turns his attention back to Sewerdog, AND GETS CAUGHT WITH A SMALL PACKAGE! One..Two..Kickout! LB: I didn't think he had enough left for that. MW: Think again. He's up already, and he just LEVELED Heavy Metal with a shoulderblock! The man is amazing! Heavy Metal gets back up, but Sewerdog grabs him by the head, BACKSPIN DDT!!! BD: Well what do ya know? MW: Not as much as we thought we did, apparently. And now Sewerdog grabs him around the neck, and begins to choke away! LB: I never said he was the most technically sound wrestler in the world. MW: No you didn't, and he's not, but he's STILL taking it to the champ. Sewerdog has just choked the champion to the ground, and now he's getting a few stomps in, and he's going for the cover! One..Shoulder Up! BD: WOW! I told you, this guy's not human. MW: And I'm starting to believe you. Sewerdog lifts Heavy Metal to his feet, but Heavy Metal reaches out to the timekeeper's table and grabs the ring bell! He just CLOCKED Sewerdog with it! And now HE'S going for the cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: And still, Sewerdog kicks out! MW: Yes he does, and you've gotta give him that. But now Heavy Metal is pounding away on him even MORE with that ring bell, and Sewerdog is busted open! The blood is flowing like a river! BD: I've never seen a red river. MW: You know what I mean, Big Daddy. And now, Heavy Metal is moving over, directly in front of that timekeeper's table! And now he's picking up Sewerdog, AND HE'S SETTING HIM UP FOR THE MEGADETH POWER BOMB! LB: If he hits this, it's OVER. MW: Heavy Metal has him in position now, he lifts Sewerdog up, AND TURNS, AND POWER BOMBS HIM THROUGH THE TIMEKEEPER'S TABLE!!! That table just shattered everywhere! I can't BELIEVE that impact! And we've got a cover! One..Two..Three! (DING, DING) BD: It was a good fight, but in the end, the champ was just too much-- as he always is. RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of thirteen minutes and thirty-one seconds, here is your winner, and STILL AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World.... HEAVY METAL!!!!!! MW: And the champ pulls it out again. Can anyone stop him? LB: Not that I'VE seen. MW: It's gotta happen someday though, and I'm just wondering who it's gonna be. WAIT A MINUTE, A FAN JUST JUMPED THE GUARDRAIL AND NAILED HEAVY METAL WITH A CHAIR! BD: That's no fan, that's Wild Thing! MW: You're right, it IS Wild Thing. What was HE doing in the crowd? LB: Waiting to attack Heavy Metal? MW: I know THAT, what I mean is.... oh, nevermind. But Wild Thing is STILL pounding away on him with that chair! And NOW what's Wild Thing doing? He's sitting Heavy Metal in that chair, and he's climbing into the ring! BD: And he's going up top! Looks like it's party time! MW: You're right! Wild Thing dives, AND EXECUTES THE PARTY TIME SPLASH ONTO HEAVY METAL, KNOCKING HIM OUT OF THE CHAIR AND ONTO THE CONCRETE! WHAT A DEVASTATING MANEUVER! LB: You can say that again. MW: And now Wild Thing is walking away, laughing! What a sick and disturbed person this man is. BD: I think we've already established that on numerous occasions. MW: Yes we have, and now, as the paramedics attend to Heavy Metal, unfortunately we're out of time. That'll have to be it for now. So for The Lovely Bertha and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt, this is Matt Wilson saying so long everybody! (fade to black) \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- American E-Wrestling Association!
