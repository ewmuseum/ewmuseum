Sunday, May 25th, Card (3/3)

25 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Now, available for the first time, Zamfir, Master of the Pan Flute, performs all of Barry Manilow's Greatest Hits. "Copa Cabana", "I Write the Songs", "Through the Rain" and all the rest of your favorite Barry Manilow smash hits. 2 CDs or 3 cassettes. MasterCard and Visa accepted....) EL: OK, time for a match up between two of the top contenders for the CWAA Heavyweight Championship, and this should be a real barn-burner. Let's go to David Myers and get the introductions. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, his next contest is set for one fall with a twenty-minute time limit. Introducing first, from Charlotte, North Carolina- (Boos ring out as "Life in the Fast Lane" plays.) DM: -weighing in at 240 lb, here is Marc Thomas! (Thomas has already been described once tonight. He arrives in the ring without his partner, Jak Hammer.) DM: And his opponent- ("Dream On" plays and the rapturous pop begins for the most popular CWAA star) DM: -from Des Moines, Iowa, weighing in at 170 lb., former CWAA Heavyweight Champion, "the Dreamer', Randy Sheradin! (Sheradin does all the usual things on his way to the ring, shaking hands, greeting fans, etc.) DM: Your referee, Chris Herman. EL: OK- both men have left their partners behind for this one, it looks like. A very big match for both competitors. Both men circle each other warily. Finally they lock up-Thomas to a standing side headlock and takes him over. Thomas with a headlock on the Dreamer. Sheridan breaks out with a neckscissors. Marc Thomas now slips free- double leg cradle- 1... 2... kickout. Both men up again. Almost a quick one for Thomas. I'm sure somewhere, the Professional, Max Robertson, is keeping an eye on this matchup. Collar and elbow tie up- monkey flip by the Dreamer. Thomas up now- and he shoves the Dreamer right in the chest! And Sheridan shoves him right back! Tempers flaring already- and Thomas goes right to the eyes! (Boos) EL: Irish whip- and Thomas goes downstairs with that knee. right to the solar plexus, and if you've ever been hit there, you know how it feels, having the wind knocked right out of you. Side suplex now by Thomas. Now he goes to the half Boston crab. Thomas a patient, methodical worker in that ring, and he is going to need these qualities to slow down the Dreamer, a quick aerial specialist who relies on his leg strength and conditioning. Thomas putting the pressure on. Now he releases the hold and stomps away at the thighs. Marc Thomas in firm control- Irish whip- dropkick to the leg. Now he takes the Dreamer into the corner- and slams his head right into the top turnbuckle. The Dreamer in trouble early on in this one. Again- blocked! And Sheridan smashes Thomas' head! (Pop) EL: And now- ooh! Low blow by Marc Thomas, blatantly and right in front of referee Chris Herman, who issues a verbal warning. Now Thomas goes for the Icebre- and Sheridan quickly grabs the ropes, Chris Herman calls for the break. Disaster narrowly averted by Sheridan. OK- both men in mid-ring now. So far Marc Thomas has looked very impressive. This is the man who- single leg takedown by the Dreamer- double leg cradle- 1... 2... kickout. And the Dreamer with a near fall. Lockup, and Thomas back to the side headlock. As I was saying, this was the man who had AEWA World Light champion Joey Valachi on the run- Sheridan gets him up- atomic drop! Thomas bounces into the corner- back out, and the Dreamer catches him- back suplex! (Pop) EL: Thomas in trouble now. Sheridan hooks him-backslide- Thomas slams on the brakes- reversal- 1... kickout. And now Sheradin with another double cradle- 1... kickout. Both men looking for the quick pin attempts, keeping to the basics. I think the first one to make a mistake may find himself on the short end of the stick. OK, lockup again, and the Dreamer with a sharp kneelift. Snap mare takedown into a chinlock. Look at the focus, the concentration in his eyes. Marc Thomas reaching for the ropes- he's got it. Chris Herman calls for the clean break, and the Dreamer is one man who'll give it. Now a European uppercut by Sheridan- Irish whip- and a big dropkick! (Pop) EL: Very nice elevation by the highest flyer in the CWAA. Speaking of high flyers, how about that Little Yvette- arm drag takedown by the Dreamer now, and into an arm bar- Thomas reverses- Sheridan turns it around- another reversal by Thomas- Irish whip now- and the Dreamer with a cross body- no! Thomas rolls him over- has the tights- 1... 2... kickout! Thomas clearly had a handful of tights there, Sheridan still kicks out. Now a hiptoss by Randy. Thomas back up- and Sheridan nails him with a bulldog! (Pop) EL: Sends him for the ride- back body drop- no! Thomas hooks him- ddt! He drilled him! They've been going for at least 15 minute now- need to be mindful of the time, we have a 20-minute limit on this on. Snap suplex by Thomas. The cover- 1... 2... kickout- Thomas perhaps feeling a sense of the urgency now. Irish whip- catches him in an abdominal stretch. I was trying to say about high flyers- Thomas has that top rope for leverage- how about Little Yvette, almost pulling off that TV title victory- I see him creeping closer and closer to championship belts each time he comes out here. And next week, we have another exciting young high flyer arriving here in the CWAA- Sheridan hip tosses his way free. (Pop) EL: We're going to get our first look at one Jimmy Dangerous, and see if 1997 will be the Year of Living Dangerously. Drop kick by Sheridan- he has Thomas on the run now- hooks him from behind- German suplex- 1... 2... kickout again. Time running out on this one- savate kick by the Dreamer- Thomas is just about out of it- Randy to the top rope- he's going to have to hurry- there's the bell! I think time has just expired! Let's get the official decision. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, the time limit has expired- this match is ruled a draw. (The crowd always hates these decisions) EL: Randy Sheradin asking for five more minutes- pleading with official Chris Herman. We've got one more match to get to tonight, though, our big special feature match still to come. Chris Herman talking things over with Marc Thomas- Thomas nods. David Myers back in the ring- what's going on? DM: Ladies and gentlemen, referee Chris Herman is allowing this match to continue for five more minutes, (Pop) EL: Look out! Sheridan still talking to the timekeeper, and Thomas nailed him with that Icebreaker from behind! There's the bell- we're back underway, but Randy Sheradin has just been felled by that Icebreaker in the meantime! Thomas sends him to the ropes- knee to the midsection. Thomas with a full nelson now- into a suplex- 1... 2... Sheridan kicks out! Thomas sends him to the corner- Sheridan hops onto that second buckle- cross body press! (Pop) EL: 1... 2... kickout! Now an elbow smash to the head by the Dreamer- and he sets him up- swinging neckbreaker! The cover- 1... 2... no! Kickout again! Sheridan stands him up- small package- 1... Thomas rolls him over- he has the rope! He has the rope! No! That's three! Marc Thomas grabbed the bottom rope to reverse the hold and to keep a leverage advantage! Chris Herman never saw it! DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, Marc Thomas! ("Life in the Fast Lane" plays) EL: Randy Sheridan protesting the call, trying to explain to Chris Herman what happened, but it is going into the record books- Marc Thomas gets the win. And I think notice has now been served to the rest of the CWAA- Marc Thomas has arrived. We're going to take a quick break, and when we get back, the tag team titles are on the line! (An ad for UWF Luck of the Draw is replayed.) EL: OK- it's time for our big feature matchup, a Texas Tornado match for the CWAA Tag Team Titles- let's head up to the ring and get the introductions! DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is for the CWAA Tag Team Championship! (Big pop) DM: It will be fought under Texas Tornado rules! (Pop again) DM: Introducing first, the challengers- (Boos start as "Sharp Dressed Man" by ZZ top plays and the challengers in their sequined robes strut to the ring) DM: -from parts unknown, at a combined weight of 497 lb., "Marvelous" Michael Anderson and "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey! And their opponents- (The theme to "Twister" plays and the popping resumes) DM: -accompanied to the ring by their manager, Mother Nature, at a combined weight of 559 lb., they are the reigning CWAA Tag Team champions, Travis Conley and "Tornado" Paul Langford- here are Twister! EL: The challengers waste no time this week- Anderson kneelifts Conley as he comes in to the ring, and McNultey on the Tornado! Punches and kicks- I don't know how I'm going to keep up! All four men in the ring at once! (Anderson kicks Conley, Langford punches McNultey; Anderson scoop slams Conley, Langford slams McNultey) EL: Anderson unloading with the right hands, and the Tornado sends the Pretty Boy for the ride- dropkick! And the Pretty Boy falls over the top rope! (Pop) EL: Langford with that nose brace on- eww! Anderson pulling on Conley's nostrils- now the tornado to the rescue- he holds Anderson open- Conley with the body punches. Double whip- double clothesline! Michael Anderson to the floor! The champs stand alone. (Pop) EL: And out they go- taking off after the challengers- round and round the ring- Anderson and McNultey back in- here come the champs- and the Marvelous one and Pretty Boy greet them with stomps and kicks to the head as they come back in. Anderson punching away at Conley in the corner, McNultey works over Langford. . They whip the champs into each other- dosey-do - and the champs swing around- the Tornado with a dropkick and Conley with a clothesline! (Pop) EL: Langford with an arm drag on Anderson, and now into an arm bar- slowing things down. Look at Travis Conley- big backbreaker. He goes to the full nelson now- those powerful arms putting the pressure on the Pretty Boy's neck. Anderson reverses the arm bar- whips Langford- right into Travis Conley! Paul Langford thrown right into his partner- both go down, and McNultey with them! Michael Anderson now- pulls the Tornado up by the hair- Mother Nature urging her champions on- ddt! Anderson just ddted Langford! He doesn't go for the cover, though- he takes Conley- ddts him, too! He helps up his partner- double cover now- 1... 2... both champs kick out. Anderson with a snap suplex. (McNultey with a legsweep on Conley) EL: The challengers in real control now. Anderson covers- 1... kickout. Meanwhile, the Pretty Boy has just stomped Conley right out of the ring. Anderson holds Langford up- McNultey with punches him. Conley needs to get to his partner's aid. They're choking him on the top rope! Oh no! Anderson tugging that protective brace off of Langford's nose- the nose they broke! McNultey has him in a full nelson- Anderson off the ropes- Langford ducks- Anderson just blasted his own partner! (Big, big pop) EL: Right where that broken nose was seconds ago, Anderson just decked his own partner! Conley catches him as he falls back- 1... 2.... 3! They did it! (The Twister theme plays) EL: They did it! Twister retain the CWAA Tag Team Championship! Folks we are just about out of time- join us next week when it's champion versus champion- Lt. Lawson and the Tornado for the TV Title! See you then!
