[AEWA] Saturday Night Slam (11/2, part one)

4 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By
Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power
Bomb, and a look at Mister Crowley DRIVING the head of some guy into the concrete with the Premature Burial! Then the camera switches to the
Lovely Bertha, Matt Wilson, and "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt in a packed arena)
MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Saturday Night Slam! We have plenty of great action scheduled for tonight, so this should be an EXCELLENT show. And we're all really excited about the main event, when
"Superman" Mike Stewart will be defending his World title against Heavy Metal-- in the AEWA's first ever scaffold match!
BD: I'm really looking forward to that one, folks. I can't wait to see Heavy
Metal DOMINATE the match, and win his title back.
LB: But what's up first, Wilson? MW: American Ambush and Jose Gomez are gonna get it on one more time and see if they can FINALLY settle their differences.
BD: I don't know if these guys can EVER settle their differences. They've been feuding ever since Championship Challenge, and Gomez even shaved off Ambush's HAIR at one point.
MW: It is indeed a VERY heated feud, and I've got a feeling that they're gonna turn it up another notch tonight-- this is gonna be an all out war!
LB: You've got that right. MW: And so let's not just sit here talking about it, let's go to the ring! RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, weighing in at 243 pounds, American Ambuuuuuuuuuuuuuuush! (American Ambush makes his entrance to a chorus of boos; he just smiles at the reaction. He has a VERY intense look in his eye.)
BD: And Ambush looks like he's ready to go to war tonight. MW: And it's an international war-- America versus Mexico! LB: We're at war with Mexico? BD: Yeah, how come no one told me this before? We should kick Mexico's butt though. MW: Calm down guys, we're NOT at war with Mexico. I just meant that the two warring factions in this feud are FROM the US and Mexico.
LB: But I thought you said.... MW: Just forget it, remind me never to use metaphors around you guys again. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 247 pounds and accompanied by his manager, El Diablo, here is "Ulllllllltimate" Jose
Gooooooooooooooomez! (Gomez makes his entrance and receives a reaction very similar to Ambush's from the crowd. El Diablo walks closely behind him.) BD: And things are just about ready to explode. MW: They sure are. Gomez is wasting no time getting to that ring. LB: He wants the Ambush in the worst way. MW: Yes he does. Gomez quickly slides into the ring, and grabs him! We've got a lock up, and Gomez whips Ambush to the ropes-- where Ambush gets tripped by El Diablo!
BD: Those are the kinds of things that make managing such a fine profession. It just makes you SO proud to have been one.
MW: Whatever. Ambush is relatively unhurt, but he is VERY upset with El Diablo, and he's letting him know it. But he's not concentrating on Gomez, who grabs him from behind, and whips him to the turnbuckle! Gomez follows him in, and just CRUSHES him into the corner!
LB: Gomez is really pouring it on. MW: That's what usually happens when guys hate each other as much as these two. And now Gomez picks up the Ambush, BACK SUPLEX! That took a lot out of him. Gomez picks him up again, and this time nails him with a
SNAP suplex! BD: Is he putting on a suplex clinic? MW: I don't think we've seen enough of them to call it THAT. Gomez picks Ambush up off the mat now, SPINNING BACKBREAKER--NO!! COUNTERED
WITH AN ELBOWSMASH BY THE AMBUSH! LB: So American Ambush IS alive. MW: Alive and kicking. Ambush now grabs Gomez, and NAILS him with a Soviet Suplex! Ambush runs to the ropes, Gomez reaches his feet, and gets LEVELED with a shoulderblock from the Ambush!
BD: The shoe appears to be on the other foot now. MW: That it is. Ambush grabs Gomez now, HEADLOCK TAKEDOWN! And he raises Gomez back to his feet again! He whips Gomez to the turnbuckle!
BUT GOMEZ PUTS ON THE BREAKS, AND SCALES THE TURNBUCKLE! Ambush charges in behind him, AND GETS CAUGHT HEAD-ON IN A MOONSAULT BODYBLOCK! Gomez hooks the leg!
One..Two..Three!!!! (DING, DING) LB: Is it over already? RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of one minute at fifty-one seconds, here is your winner.... "ULTIMATE" JOSE GOMEZ!!!!!!!
MW: Woah! Gomez just caught Ambush, and everyone in this arena, COMPLETELY by surprise. He was able to pull off a VERY quick win there. BD: And the Ambush isn't happy AT ALL. MW: He most certainly is not. He's arguing about a fast count, but the ref is showing him no sympathy. Ambush is completely ENRAGED. The ref raises
Gomez's hand in victory, BUT AMBUSH JUST CHARGES IN AND LEVELS GOMEZ WITH A
CLOTHESLINE! LB: Maybe he didn't win the battle, but the war is far from over. MW: That's for sure. Ambush is REALLY doing a number on Gomez, and he locks him in the Camouflage Clutch, and Gomez is in MAJOR pain. And look who's coming to ringside-- it's Ambush's partner in crime, Mean Massimo.
BD: Oh BOY is Gomez in trouble now. This feud originally started LONG ago when Gomez broke Massimo's leg in a match, and then Ambush brutally attacked Gomez during Massimo's return match.
MW: You said a mouthful. There's a lot of bad blood here. And now Massimo climbs into the ring, and Ambush still has the Camouflage Clutch locked in! LB: It looks like Ambush is telling Massimo to put the boots to Gomez. MW: That's exactly what he's saying. Massimo nods, takes a step back, winds up his kicking leg, and nails AMBUSH in the head!? What's going on here!
BD: Massimo is kicking field goals with American Ambush's head. MW: I know THAT, what I mean is, WHY is Massimo attacking Ambush? They're close friends, and Massimo and Gomez HATE each other. BD: How the hell should I know? MW: Why do I even bother? Gomez is free from the Camouflage Clutch, and Massimo is really lacing into the Ambush now! He's just repeatedly putting the boots to him, and now he's picking Ambush up off the mat, he lifts the Ambush up, PILEDRIVER!
LB: Gomez is up now, and he looks like he doesn't know what the Hell his going on. MW: You're right. Gomez is looking at Massimo attack Ambush, and he looks as stunned as the rest of us. He's yelling at Massimo now.
BD: Are we gonna have a THREE way brawl? MW: It's possible. Gomez has Massimo's attention now, and they appear to be having a very heated discussion. We can't make out what they're saying, but something Massimo just said really sparked the eye of Gomez.
The Ambush has just reached his feet, but Massimo and Gomez are joining hands, and they LEVEL the Ambush with a double clothesline!
LB: What? Did I miss something? MW: Apparently we ALL missed something, because Gomez and Massimo are now shaking hands and grinning widely. And they go to work on the Ambush,
TOGETHER! This is absolutely shocking.... BD: And now here come's El Diablo into the ring, and he's got a chair! MW: That can only mean more trouble for the Ambush. Gomez now grabs the chair from El Diablo, and lays it down in front of Massimo! Massimo now picks up the Ambush, AND EXECUTES THE PASTA BUSTER, DRIVING AMBUSH
FACE FIRST INTO THAT CHAIR! LB: I think that one might've hurt a little. MW: No kidding? But they're not done! Gomez picks up the chair now, and scales the ropes with it! Massimo lifts Ambush to his feet, and Gomez dives, AND HITS AMBUSH WITH THE LATIN LEAP, WITH THE CHAIR BETWEEN THEM!
BD: Gomez just CRASHED down on top of Ambush with that chair. Ambush is hurt, big time! MW: He most definitely is. Now Gomez and Massimo are leaving together, with big smiles on their faces, leaving Ambush in a pool of his own blood on the mat.
LB: That's the way the cookie crumbles, buddy. MW: I think Ambush will have something to say about that. BD: Well right now he CAN'T. He can barely MOVE. MW: Yes, and as the paramedics are helping him out of the arena, we're just about ready for our next match.
LB: So what is it? MW: Big Ron Raider is defending his newly won North American Title against the Crazy Canuck.
BD: It should be interesting to see if the Canuck's style changes at all, because his attitude sure has recently.
MW: That's an understatement. The Canuck recently attacked BOTH Primal Fear and Mike Stewart, for no apparent reason. LB: How do you know he didn't have a reason? MW: I didn't say he didn't have a reason, I said no APPARENT reason. Or no CLEAR reason. Do you understand English?
LB: Not the way you speak it. MW: Ain't that the truth. Let's just go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA North American Title. Introducing first, the challenger, he weighs in at 253 pounds, the Crazy Canuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuck!
(Pat Benatar's "Hit Me With Your Best Shot" begins to play loudly over the PA as the Canuck makes his way to the ring. He is very loudly booed by the capacity crowd.)
BD: This is a new experience for the Canuck. He's never been booed before in the AEWA. MW: We'll if he's gonna keep acting like he has been, he can expect a lot more of these kinds of welcomes.
LB: Who cares? You don't need the fans, the only thing that counts is who gets the win. The Canuck has finally realized that.
MW: Well he has certainly upset a lot of people with this new attitude, namely Primal Fear and Mike Stewart. And you can bet they'll be looking for him in the future. This may look like a good move to you now, but there is a very high probability that things are going to come back to haunt him.
RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from the Rocky Mountains, he weighs in at 416 pounds and is accompanied by his manager, Million Dollar Mark, he is the AEWA North American Champion, Big...Ron...Raaaaiiiiiiiiiiiiiider!
(Lynyrd Skynyrd's "Saturday Night Special" cranks out over the PA as Raider, belt draped over his shoulder, makes his way toward the ring to a large accompaniment of boos. Million Dollar Mark is at his side.)
BD: And Raider looks imposing as ever. MW: Even more so with that piece of hardware hanging over his shoulder. LB: I don't know how the Canuck's gonna handle this guy. MW: He'll have to use everything he's got, that's for sure. Raider now steps into the ring, and is IMMEDIATELY attacked by the Canuck!
BD: That may be the only way he can win this one. MW: You're right about that. He's gotta take control early, and keep it. The Canuck grabs the big man, VERTICAL SUPLEX--NO! He couldn't get him up! And Raider counters with a vertical suplex of his own!
LB: Well that didn't last long. It's Raider game now. MW: It most certainly is. Raider picks the Canuck up, and nails him with ANOTHER vertical suplex! The Canuck gets up, but Raider grabs him by the arm, SHORT CLOTHESLINE!
BD: The Canuck is NOT where he wants to be right now. MW: That's an understatement. Raider picks the Canuck up now, and places him on the top turnbuckle! Raider climbs up there as well, and he grabs the Canuck around the waist, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPERPLEX!! The cover!
One..Two..Kickout! LB: The Canuck just needs to escape and re-group right now. MW: Raider's not letting him. Raider lifts the Canuck to his feet now, and whips him to the ropes! The Canuck comes back, and STUNS Raider with a superkick!
BD: He pulled that out of nowhere! MW: And Raider is staggered! The Canuck whips Raider to the turnbuckle now, and follows him in! AVALANCHE!! Raider is REALLY wobbly now! He staggers out of the corner, and the Canuck runs to the ropes! He comes back, and FLOORS Raider with a flying forearm! The cover!
One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: He almost got Raider there! MW: The Canuck is never an opponent to be taken lightly-- if you do, you'll get beat. Raider slowly makes it to his feet, and the Canuck runs to the ropes AGAIN! He comes back, and LEVELS Raider with ANOTHER flying forearm! And another cover!
One..Two..Million Dollar Mark puts Raider's foot on the ropes! BD: Boy, the good managers are out in force tonight! MW: In YOUR opinion. The Canuck is VERY upset with Mark right now, but he's gotta keep wrestling! He drags Raider to the center of the ring, and NAILS him with a legdrop! The cover!
One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: But Raider STILL won't go down! MW: That's why he's an AEWA Legend. The Canuck gets up now, and just STOMPS away on the champion. He's REALLY breaking his gameplan now, he's UPSET! The Canuck lifts Raider up now, and NAILS him with a neck snap!
BD: He's gotta keep on him! MW: And that's what he's doing. The Canuck DROPS a kneeling headbutt onto the fallen Raider, and now he's going up top! He's positioned himself on the top rope, and he dives, FROG SPLASH! HE NAILED IT! The cover!
One..Two..Kickout! LB: WOW! That is just a DEVASTATING maneuver, and Raider kicked out like it was nothing! MW: He's not gonna go down easy for ANYONE. The Canuck waits for Raider to get to his feet now, and runs to the ropes! The Canuck comes back,
AND GETS LEVELED WITH A HUGE CLOTHESLINE FROM RAIDER! BD: And just like that, Raider's back in control. MW: He most certainly is. Raider takes a moment to regain his senses, and the Crazy Canuck is almost to his feet! He gets up, but gets NAILED with a swinging punch from Raider, AND ANOTHER! Raider grabs the Canuck now, SHORT LARIAT! The cover!
One..Two..Kickout! LB: I think the Canuck's just about done. MW: Don't count him out yet. Raider gets up, AND DELIVERS A DEVASTATING BIG SPLASH TO THE CANUCK! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: The Canuck's got a hell of a lot of fight in him too. MW: Raider brings The Canuck to his feet now, and delivers a BIG backhand smash to the head of the Canuck! Raider picks up the Canuck, and places him on the top turnbuckle! He once again climbs up there with him, AND
DELIVERS ANOTHER BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX!! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: And the Canuck still hangs on! MW: And Raider is calling this one over! He picks up the Canuck, AND HE'S SETTING HIM UP FOR THE RAIDER BOMB! He lifts him into the air, AND PLANTS HIM!! The cover! One..Two..Three!!!! (DING, DING) BD: The Canuck put up a heck of a fight, but the power of the big man was just too much for him.
RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of seven minutes and twelve seconds, here is your winner, and STILL AEWA North American Champion.... BIG RON RAIDER!
MW: And Raider IS successful in the first defense of his title. He and Mark are celebrating and leaving, but look who's coming to ringside! LB: It's Primal Fear! And he's got a baseball bat! MW: This is DEFINITELY bad news for the Canuck. Primal Fear most definitely wants revenge for that heinous sneak attack last week by the
Canuck. BD: But this isn't fair! The Canuck is barely conscious! MW: So was Primal Fear last week. Paybacks are a bitch. Primal Fear steps into the ring now, and just POUNDS away on the Canuck with that bat! Would you look at the GIGANTIC welts it is making on his back!
LB: I'd rather not. MW: Primal Fear CONTINUES to pound away on him with that bat, and now he just CRACKS it over the head of the Canuck!
BD: He could break open his skull! MW: I don't think Primal Fear cares, he's out to DESTROY! And now he picks up the Canuck, and whips him to the ropes! The Canuck comes back,
AND GETS CLOTHESLINED WITH THE BASEBALL BAT!!! The Canuck is down, and grabbing his neck! And Primal Fear delivers a BIG Bump Splash!
LB: He's just decimating him! This is horrible! MW: This is revenge. And now Primal Fear sits over the Canuck in a "camel clutch" like position, AND BEGINS TO CHOKE HIM OUT WITH THE BASEBALL BAT!!! And the Canuck is in AGONY! BD: This is going too far! He could KILL the man! MW: He most certainly could! And now there are several officials trying to get Primal Fear to stop, BUT HE WON'T! HE'S CHOKING THE LIFE OUT OF
THE CRAZY CANUCK! LB: Someone has to stop this, the Canuck's turning blue! MW: You are absolutely right! This is nuts, the Canuck is on the brink of DEATH, and Primal Fear will NOT let go, and-- OH MY GOD!!! The Crazy
Canuck just regurgitated all over the ring! THAT gets Primal Fear to stop; he doesn't want the Canuck's throw up all over him.
BD: And look at those officials scatter. Let's see if we can find out what the Canuck had for dinner. There's some corn, looks like some mashed potatoes, I don't know WHAT that is....
MW: That's quite enough Big Daddy, this is sickening! LB: I think I'm gonna puke. MW: Please don't. Well fans, we've had a rather revolting development here, as Primal Fear caused the Crazy Canuck to lose his lunch all over the ring. Primal Fear is gone now, but a LOT of damage has been done.
This is kind of reminiscent of when Primal Fear set the Crazy Canuck ON FIRE in a match quite some time ago. BD: It sure is, only then, I liked it. MW: We'll as the paramedics attend to the Canuck and our janitor staff cleans up the, uh.... mess, let's talk about our next match-up!
LB: Tell me what's next, tell me ANYTHING to get my mind off what just happened, and so I don't puke.
MW: Well, we've got a rematch from last week as Super-Blonde once again takes on the newest member of the Angels of Death, Deranged.
BD: Well last week, the interference of the Angels backfired, and Deranged lost a match that he was completely in control of. It should be interesting to see what happens when they hook it up again.
MW: Yes it should, but I'm still VERY upset with Deranged for turning his back on everything he has stood for all this time. It's really sickening.
LB: Well get over it. This is the new and improved Deranged, and tonight is gonna be his night.
MW: We'll just have to see about that, and we will, let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA Television Title! Introducing first, the challenger, he weighs in at 260 pounds and is accompanied by his manager, Angel, he is a two-time former AEWA Television Champion, Deeeeeeeeeeeeeerrrrrrrranged!
(The Theme from "Halloween" plays loudly over the PA as Deranged makes his way toward the ring to a HUGE accompaniment of boos. Angel is at his side.)
BD: Deranged looks the best I've seen him in a long time! MW: I hate to admit it, but he really DOES. But just listen to this reaction from our crowd, they just HATE him!
LB: Who cares, they're supposed to. Deranged doesn't care about them anymore, he's just looking out for number one.
BD: And trying not to step in number two. MW: And you're a PROFESSIONAL broadcaster? BD: Yup. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 202 pounds and accompanied by his manager, Kismet, he is the AEWA Television Champion,
This... Is... Suuuuuuuuuuuper-Bloooooooooooonde! (Elastica's "Connection" blasts out from the PA, and the crowd just EXPLODES! Super-Blonde appears from behind the curtain, and the cheers only get louder! He makes his way to the ring, with the belt around his waist and Kismet at his side.)
MW: And just listen to this crowd erupt for our champion! LB: Don't worry, he won't be champion much longer. MW: We'll see. The Blonde steps into the ring, and we've got a stare down BIG TIME. The two competitors are just eyeballing each other. They remember last week's encounter. Super-Blonde had to fight off the entire Angels of Death to retain his title, and he may just have to again.
BD: Oh, don't expect history to repeat itself. He's gonna lose that TV title tonight, mark my words.
MW: Let's just watch. Now we've got a lock up in there! The Blonde gets the early advantage, and takes Deranged down with a backdrop driver! And now the Blonde just DROPS an elbow!
LB: Get up Deranged, this guy sucks! MW: No, he does NOT. Super-Blonde lifts Deranged to his feet now, and whips him to the ropes! Deranged comes back, and the Blonde STUNS him with a big elbow! Deranged teeters back, and the Blonde hooks him from behind, BACKSLIDE!
One..Two..Kickout! BD: Super-Blonde tried to sneak in a quick win, but Deranged won't let him. MW: No he won't, he is very well prepared for this match. Super-Blonde picks up Deranged now, and whips him to the ropes--BUT DERANGED REVERSES
IT! The Blonde comes back, and gets LEVELED with a clothesline! LB: Okay Deranged, go to work! MW: Deranged picks the Blonde up off the mat now, lifts him up, FALLAWAY SLAM!! Deranged picks the Blonde up again, and nails him with a Fisherman Suplex! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Okay Deranged, now just get a chair, and CLOCK him upside the head! MW: Isn't that just a LITTLE illegal? BD: Hey, if you want to talk legal, drive 35 miles per hour. MW: Point taken. The Blonde slowly reaches his feet, and Deranged runs to the ropes! Deranged comes back, and gets ROCKED with an elbow! And just like that, Super-Blonde regains control.
LB: Darn it! MW: Super-Blonde picks up Deranged now, and begins to choke him against the ropes! BD: Disqualify him, ref! MW: He hasn't broken the rule yet. The Blonde breaks at the count of four, and goes back to work. The Blonde grabs onto Deranged now, and
NAILS him with a backdrop driver! Deranged attempts to quickly get to his feet, but the Blonde nails him with an armdrag takedown!
LB: Wait a minute, here comes somebody to ringside! BD: Is it one of the Angels? LB: No, it's.... Deathwatch?!? MW: What's HE doing here? BD: Scouting the competition, most likely. MW: Well you know what happened the last time he just "scouted" a title match. Remember the Holy Ghost-El Aviadore match?
LB: Yes I do, but he appears to be just watching. MW: He does, but who knows what he's up to. But we've still got action in the ring! The Blonde NAILS Deranged with a headbutt! The Blonde grabs him now, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover!
One..Two..Kickout! BD: Deranged better do something to get himself back in control, and fast! MW: He should. The Blonde picks Deranged up now, GORILLA PRESS--NO! DERANGED COUNTERS! ROLLING REVERSE CRADLE! LB: That's what Deranged needed! Now get him! MW: Both men are back up to their feet, but Deranged grabs the Blonde, and nails him with a backdrop driver! And now Deranged picks up the Blonde,
JACK-KNIFE POWER BOMB!!! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! BD: We were INCHES away from a new champion there! MW: We were indeed. Deranged lifts the Blonde to his feet now, and runs to the ropes! He comes back, and FLOORS the Blonde with a high cross body! The cover!
One..Two..Kickout! LB: Come on Deranged, just a little more. MW: Deranged picks the Blonde up again, and whips him to the ropes! The Blonde comes back, and FLOORS Deranged with ANOTHER surprise elbow! The quick cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Lucky move. It won't happen again. MW: We'll see. Deranged quickly to his feet, and Super-Blonde grabs him with an armdrag--COUNTERED WITH A LARIAT BY DERANGED!
BD: I told ya. MW: Yes you did. And now Deranged picks the Blonde up, and NAILS him with a brain buster! Deranged lifts the Blonde back to his feet now, and just INSULTS him with a slap! Now he picks up the Blonde, and hits a slingshot facebuster!
LB: They're pulling all the stops tonight. MW: Yes they are. And Deranged picks the Blonde up again! He lifts him into the air, ANOTHER JACK-KNIFE POWER BOMB! The cover!
One..Two..Kickout! LB: What the Hell? MW: The Blonde's gonna fight 'till his last breath, Bertha. That's just the kind of guy he is. Deranged picks the Blonde up now, BACKDROP
DRIVER--NO! The Blonde counters with a backward kick! BD: Where'd he get the energy to do that? MW: DEEP down, that's for sure. Deranged steps forward, but gets NAILED with a standing dropkick from the Blonde! Deranged is staggered, and the
Blonde takes him down with a fireman's carry! LB: Super-Blonde's a fireman? I didn't know that! MW: It's the name of the move, Bertha. You don't have to be a fireman to use it. Super-Blonde whips Deranged to the ropes now, he comes back, and the Blonde catches him with a Hotshot! Deranged is slow getting up, and the Blonde grabs him with a small package!
One..Kickout! BD: Deranged is still too in control of his senses to fall for that one. MW: Perhaps he is. Both men are up, and the Blonde whips Deranged to the turnbuckle! The Blonde CHARGES in behind him, BUT DERANGED MOVES!
And the Blonde charges right into the turnbuckle! LB: Quick thinking there by Deranged. MW: It's amazing that EITHER man has the ability to think so fast this far into the match. I think that one was on instinct alone. The Blonde staggers out of the corner, and Deranged nails him with a slap! Deranged now whips the Blonde to the ropes! He comes back, and gets LEVELED with a BIG elbow from Deranged!
BD: Okay, it's over now. MW: That remains to be seen. Deranged begins to put the boots to the Blonde, and he's going for the pin! One..Two..Kickout! LB: All he needs to do is string a couple of moves together, and he's got this one. MW: You could be right. Deranged brings the Blonde to his feet now, and delivers a leg lariat--DUCKED BY THE BLONDE! And Deranged hits hard!
BD: NO ONE can maintain control of this one. MW: That's what it looks like. The Blonde now, picks up Deranged, and nails him with a stomachbreaker! Deranged quickly gets up, but the
Blonde LEVELS him with a savate kick! LB: And Deathwatch STILL stands at ringside, doing nothing but watching. MW: Yeah, it's kinda freaky. And now the Blonde is going up top! If he hits this, it could be over! The Blonde takes his perch, BUT ANGEL
SHAKES THE ROPES! The Blonde loses his balance, AND FALLS OFF--RIGHT ON HIS HEAD! BD: Boy, the good managing just keeps on coming tonight. MW: I can't BELIEVE the referee didn't see that! Is he blind? LB: Probably. MW: Not funny. The Blonde is hurt, Deranged is up now, and he goes over and delivers a few stomps to the midsection of the champion. He lifts the Blonde to his feet, and takes him DOWN with a legsweep faceslam! And now DERANGED begins to scale the ropes!
BD: He doesn't usually go up there. I wonder what he's planning? MW: I don't know, but the Blonde still appears to be OUT. Deranged stops at the second turnbuckle, and jumps, FLYING ELBOWDROP! The cover!
One..Two..Three!!!! (DING, DING) LB: We've got a new champion! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of eleven minutes and thirty-three seconds, here is your winner, and NEEEEEEEEEEEEW AEWA Television Champion...
DERANGED!!!!!!!!!! MW: It was a hard-fought battle by both men, but too bad it's tainted by interference. BD: The Angels of Death didn't interfere. MW: No, but Angel, the manager, did. BD: Big whoop, we've got a new champ! I don't have to call the Blonde champion anymore! MW: And now Deathwatch is just leaving. Quite strange. LB: Yeah it is, but now here come the Angels of Death, and they're all celebrating back to the locker room!
MW: Yes they are, and the Blonde is just now waking up in the ring. Kismet is telling him what happened, and he's not happy. BD: But there's nothing he can do. Deranged is the TV champ, for the THIRD time! MW: You're right about that, and as the Blonde finally gets up and leaves, let's talk about our next match.
