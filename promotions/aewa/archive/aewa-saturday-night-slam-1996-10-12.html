Saturday Night Slam (10/12)

19 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power Bomb, and a look at Mister Crowley DRIVING the head of some guy into the concrete with the Premature Burial! Then the camera switches to the Lovely Bertha, Matt Wilson, and "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt in a packed arena) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Saturday Night Slam! We have some GREAT matches scheduled for tonight.... BD: In our main event, the Phantom Of The Opera is going to square off against Curate Kill! I'm really looking forward to that match. LB: But what's first? MW: The West Coast Connection will be defending their new World tag team titles against the Graveyard Shift! This should be a great match, especially considering that "weight lifting contest" from a few months ago. BD: Axl Sharkman and MC Read are going to be the most dominant champions ever, and they won't have ANY trouble tonight. LB: The Graveyard Shift is an inferior tag team, and they'll NEVER capture the titles tonight. The West Coast Connection will DESTROY them. MW: Let's get down to ringside.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall, and is for the World tag team titles! Introducing first, at a combined weight of 731 pounds, THE GRAVEYARD SHIFT! (The Mortician and Grave Dweller walk toward the ring to a BIG pop from the crowd. Their manager, Coffin, follows closely behind.) BD: These guys have been on a winning streak lately, but it's going to end tonight. LB: They don't even have a CHANCE against the West Coast Connection, so why even bother? It's going to be PATHETIC. RING ANNOUNCER: And their opponents, at a combined weight of 625 pounds, here are the World tag team champions, the West Coast Connection! ("Throw Your Set In The Air" begins playing loudly, as Axl Sharkman and MC Read make their entrance. They are proudly waving around the championship belts.) MW: Jeez, they seem a little TOO confident. This will NOT be an easy match, I can guarantee that. BD: Are you KIDDING me? The Graveyard Shift won't last five minutes with them. MW: Well, it looks like Axl Sharkman is starting off against the Mortician. They're locking up, and Sharkman seems to be overpowering him.... BD: He has brought the Mortician down, and now he HAMMERS him with a kick to the head! LB: Jeez, Sharkman's not wasting any time in there tonight, is he? MW: No, he never does. Now he picks the Mortician up, and executes a CRUSHING piledriver! The Grave Dweller is desperately trying to enter the ring, but the referee is holding him back.... BD: And Sharkman is capitalizing on this distraction. He just pulled a steel chair into the ring-- AND CRACKED IT OVER THE MORTICIAN'S HEAD! LB: Now the referee has turned around, and Sharkman just conveniently tossed that chair out of the ring. Jeez, he's one sneaky guy-- I love it! MW: You're a sick puppy, Bertha. LB: Thank you. Sharkman just tagged out to MC Read, who immediately starts UNLOADING on the Mortician! Now THAT'S teamwork! BD: He picks the Mortician up, and plants him DOWN with a power bomb! That had a LOT of power, folks. MW: Now Read just SLAPPED him across the face, adding insult to injury. This isn't even a wrestling match, it's a street fight! BD: (yelling) Wait a minute! The Grave Dweller couldn't wait anymore-- he just ran into the ring, and NAILS Read with a clothesline! LB: The referee is trying to get him out of there-- BUT GRAVE DWELLER JUST PUNCHED THE REF! That's an automatic disqualification, folks. MW: The Grave Dweller has gone totally INSANE! He just picked up the unconscious referee-- AND POWER BOMBS HIM! BD: We need to get some damn security out here! LB: Now the Grave Dweller grabs Axl Sharkman, lifts him up, and NAILS him with a powerslam! This mayhem needs to be stopped.... MW: He just pulled that chair back into the ring, and POUNDS MC Read with it! It actually BENT over Read's head! BD: What the hell is he DOING this for? The Grave Dweller needs to be locked up-- he's a madman! LB: And now the Mortician is up, and Grave Dweller is helping him out of the ring. They appear to be walking over to our broadcast area.... MW: The Grave Dweller just grabbed a microphone from the table-- apparently he has something to say.... Grave Dweller: We just kicked their ASSES, didn't we? I'm sick and tired of being ignored by the championship committee, so we made an EXAMPLE out of the West Coast Connection just now! That was sweet revenge, buddy. Remember when they attacked us during that "weight lifting contest" a few months ago? Well, now we're even! Mortician: This match ended in a disqualification, but it really doesn't matter. We are DEMANDING a rematch, at the NEXT card, for the titles. West Coast Connection, those belts WILL be ours! (They both pose for the crowd, and get a big pop in response. After a few minutes, they slowly walk back to the locker room.) BD: Jeez, what was THAT all about? Have they been waiting ALL this time, just to get revenge? MW: It sure seems that way, Big Daddy. The Graveyard Shift should NEVER be underestimated, especially when you piss them off. The West Coast Connection just learned that-- the HARD way. LB: I'm still shocked, folks. The Grave Dweller completely SNAPPED-- but he'll be fined for attacking the referee. MW: Well, the West Coast Connection won by disqualification, so Sharkman and Read have retained their titles. BD: What's next, Wilson? MW: The Phantom is about to wrestle Curate Kill in our big main event! This match should be VERY interesting.... BD: The Phantom is a former co-holder of the tag team titles, so he definitely has enough experience. But let's get serious-- can he REALLY complete as a singles wrestler? LB: I don't think so. MW: Well, there's only one way to find out. Let's just hurry up and get down to ringside.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, weighing in at 240 pounds, here is the PHANTOM! (As opera music plays softly in the background, the Phantom makes his entrance. He gets a big pop from the crowd, but doesn't actually seem to care. He just completely IGNORES the audience, and walks to the ring with a stone-cold expression on his face.) BD: Jeez, he doesn't seem very excited about this reaction. Why do the fans even BOTHER to cheer him? MW: Because he's a great role model, Big Daddy. But of course, you wouldn't know anything about that. BD: Do you want to get slapped, Wilson? RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 275 pounds, here is CURATE KILL! ("Amazing Grace" starts playing, and Curate Kill walks toward the ring. He appears to be in some kind of a trance.) LB: This guy has been in the AEWA for a LONG time, and his experience will undoubtedly pay off. MW: Don't bet on it, Bertha. The Phantom has been VERY impressive in tag team action, so he shouldn't have any trouble with Curate Kill. BD: But this ISN'T a tag team match! The Lord Of Midnight isn't even in the building tonight, so the Phantom doesn't have ANY advantage. MW: Well, I don't feel like arguing with you, so let's just watch this match.... LB: Curate Kill just NAILED the Phantom with a clothesline! Now he picks him back up, and executes a CRUSHING piledriver! BD: Kill whips him to the ropes, and comes back with a jumping shoulderblock! MW: This sudden attack seems to have really taken a LOT out of the Phantom. He's desperately trying to get back up.... BD: But Curate Kill kicks him right back down with a kick to the head! Now he covers the Phantom.... One..Two..(kickout) LB: Curate Kill picks him up, and executes a SMASHING power bomb! Did you SEE that power? MW: He spits on the Phantom, slaps him, and goes for another quick cover! Can he get the pinfall this time? One..Two..(foot on the ropes) BD: Jeez, why doesn't the Phantom just stay down? What's WRONG with that fool? MW: He's courageous, Big Daddy-- but I won't try to explain that to you. Curate Kill climbs to the top rope, and comes down with a flying elbowsmash! That was DEVASTATING! BD: Of course it was, Wilson. LB: And now Curate Kill is going back up AGAIN! He climbs to the top rope, jumps-- BUT THE PHANTOM ROLLS OUT OF THE WAY! MW: Curate Kill really SLAMMED against the canvas, and he's not looking very good right now. The Phantom gets to his feet, and SPITS on him! BD: That's disgusting! MW: The Phantom just gave the signal for his finisher, and he's standing over Curate Kill-- AND EXECUTES THE "CHANDELIER" KNEEDROP! He NAILED him with it! BD: The Phantom is going for the cover.... One..Two..Three! (DING DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner, at a time of four minutes and twenty seconds, THE PHANTOM! LB: How the hell did he do that? Curate Kill was DOMINATING this match since the opening bell-- and the Phantom actually WON? That's not fair! MW: Of course it is, Bertha. This guy is going to be a BIG threat as a singles wrestler, and I'm not a bit surprised that he won. BD: I am. LB: Well, isn't it about time for this show to end? The main event just ended, and I'm tired, so can we wrap it up? MW: No, not quite yet. Red Dog wanted to make an important announcement out here tonight, so we're going to listen to that. BD: Really? He hasn't been wrestling much lately, so what could he possibly have to say? MW: I have no idea. Let's just welcome him out here, and we can find out right now. (Red Dog walks up to the interview platform, wearing a blue tuxedo. His black hair is a little bit longer than usual, and he has it tied back into a ponytail. He smiles at the camera, grabs a microphone, and begins to speak.) Red Dog: Ladies and gentlemen, I have come out here to make a very special announcement. During my recent absence from the American E-Wrestling Association, I have noticed a very disturbing trend around here-- there are more "bad guys" than "good guys" anymore. The Angels Of Death have been running wild around here for over a month, and they need to be STOPPED. So here's what I want to do.... (pauses) JOIN THEM! Folks, I am the NEWEST member of the Angels Of Death, whether you like it or not! Since I lost that "dog pound match" to Sky Walker, I have been HUNGRY for revenge! Now, Sky Walker, I'm going to kick your little ass! Every dog has his day, and my day has finally arrived! I'm the NEWEST member of the Angels, and now NOBODY can stop me! (He removes the vest from his tuxedo, exposing an "Angels Of Death" T-shirt. The camera shifts back to our announcing team.) BD: That's great! Red Dog will make a GREAT addition to the Angels-- I'm really proud of him! MW: I'm not surprised, Big Daddy. Red Dog is ALWAYS for sale, and he sold out to the highest bidder again. It's not the first time. LB: But this was a good career decision for him, Wilson! He needed to get noticed, so this was an awesome idea. MW: I'm sorry fans, but we're out of time, so for The Lovely Bertha and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt, this is Matt Wilson saying so long everybody!
