Sunday, June 8th, Card (3/3)

8 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(...veterans, you cannot be turned down for this policy, regardless of your medical history...) EL: OK- Lawson pushes off- Wrench into the ropes- shoulderblock by the Preacher. MVM: He had the hair. EL: On a shoulderblock? They lock up again- Lawson to an arm bar, twisting that arm of the Preacher's- and Wrench reverses! He turns until Lawson collapses to the mat. MVM: Code blue! Code blue! EL: What are you talking about now? MVM: Classified, private. Classified. EL: Now Kore Wrench with a knee drop on the exposed arm! And again! And again! (Yes, if the right person does it, the world-famous Sportatorium crowd will pop for a combination arm bar-kneedrop) EL: I can hear Kore Wrench telling Chris Herman to ask Lawson for a submission. The champ hanging in there. Lawson back to his feet, Wrench still applying the pressure- Irish whip- shoulderblock by Wrench- just steamrolled right over him. Lawson on his knees, backing to the corner, begging for mercy. MVM: He's just retreating to better terrain for him to defend himself in against these illegal attacks, all in contravention of the Geneva convention and the Paris Peace treaty and the Emancipation Proclamation and the Declaration of the Rights of Man and the Code of Hammurabi. EL: Wrench in the corner after him- but Chris Herman calls for a break. collar and elbow tie up- the Preacher pushes the champ to the ropes- Lawson with a handful of hair pulls Wrench to the corner. Chop by the Lt. and Chris Herman calls for the break. And again- he runs the Preacher across the ring- smashes him right into that top turnbuckle. Irish whip- backdrop- no! DDT! (Pop) EL: The Preacher drills him! 1... 2... kickout. Almost had a new champion there. MVM: He had the ropes with one hand, the tights with another, and clearly was gouging his eyes with the other! EL: And the Lt. back on his knees in the corner, begging, as Chris Herman keeps between him and the challenger- who has those big fists cocked and ready. Speculation abounding, Major, that you and the Lt. are behind the mysterious Masked Man and his attacks on so many other CWAA stars lately. Any truth to that? MVM: If we were behind something, you'd never know it until it was too late. EL: Is that a "yes" or a "no"? MVM: To quote the great patriot, Nathan Hale, "That's for me to know and you to find out. Ask me no questions, I'll tell you no lies." EL: Major- MVM: Loose lips sink ships. For all I know, you could be working for the Red Brigade, Baader-Meinhof, the Council of Trent- all of them! EL: Kore Wrench back to a side headlock. Lawson pushes off- Wrench to the ropes- Lawson catches him in a hip toss- reversed! Wrench pounces on him- side headlock down on the mat. Awesome power. MVM: Exactly what he'll use to break out of this. EL: I meant for the Preacher. Lawson with an handful of tights rolls him back- 1... 2... Wrench rolls back into the headlock position. Tightens the hold- it's like being trapped in a vise when someone with arms like that puts you in a headlock. Another handful of tights- Lawson rolls back- 1... 2... Wrench back to a sitting position, and Lawson still trapped in that hold. The Lt. has been a series of draining holds- pretty soon he'll start running out of gas, while the Preacher looks fresh as a daisy. Lawson back to his feet. MVM: Let's hear it... EL: What? MVM: Every time the chaplain does something, you go on about "awesome power." EL: Well, there is no taking away from the skills of the current- and so far, only- CWAA Television champion, the man who has signed to face the AEWA's own Fuse- Lawson pushes off- Wrench to the ropes- shoulderblocks the champ. Now the Preacher- wrestling a very methodical match- MVM: Agreed- he is a very slow man. Probably why he had to take a non-combatant position. EL: -to an arm bar. Grinding, twisting, turning the arm- and the Lt. with an eyepoke! He jammed a thumb right into the eye! MVM: It was a highly localized clawhold! I learned that one in Operation Porpoise in Sumatra, when- wait, I've said too much. EL: I couldn't agree more. MVM: I may have to kill you now- you know too much. Wait- what am I saying? EL: In the meantime, Lawson has just flung his challenger to the arena floor, and he hit that concrete hard. Kore Wrench shaking the cobwebs out on the floor as Lawson rests, Referee Chris Herman's count up to six now, seven- the Preacher has pulled himself to the ring apron- and Lawson springs into action. Hooks him- snap mares him into the ring- nicely executed. We have neared the fifteen-minute mark now. Lawson with an open-handed chop. Drives the Preacher against the ropes- forearm blow- chop, and another forearm. The champ in a very aggressive mode now. Now the Preacher fires back! (Pop for Wrench's first offense in a while) MVM: Illegal closed fist punch! Ring the bell! EL: He rang the champ's bell! Another- and another- I counted at least six- seven- eight punches from the challenger! The champ on the retreat-- dragging himself back across the mat into the corner- begging for the Preacher to relent. Kick to the midsection. The Preacher- what power! Threw him right into the corner. Back body drop- the champion is down! Lawson back up, and- ooh! (Crowd groans) EL: Jabs a knee into the groin. Always thinking. Another chop, and the Preacher's chest has been flayed all evening, Welts growing, and the skin has been broken. Snap mare takedown, and the champ to a facelock- reverse chinlock. Tightly applied. Lawson putting on the pressure, forcing the challenger to support his weight while fighting the effects of the hold. Plus the frustration factor, as Lawson has those arms across the face, making it difficult for the Preacher to breath and cutting off his vision. ("Go, Kore, Go!") EL: Very little about the Lt. is flashy, very little that makes a big splash or a highlight film, but he knows the nuances, he's experienced beyond his years. He has these little details, and that is why he is the TV Champion, and no one but him has held this title, despite the unbelievable defense schedule. He is out here every week putting this title on the line, almost always against men who are bigger and stronger- Kore Wrench, Jak Hammer. Wrench starting to power out... (Another old-style forearms-locked test-of-strength- which always made for good photos in the mark mags) EL: And the Preacher getting the better- hey! (A handful of hair helps Lawson pull Wrench back into the facelock) EL: An illegal use of the hair for a leverage advantage- MVM: But the Lt. overcomes the chaplain's cheating ways and pulls him back into the hold. EL: It was Lawson who pulled the hair! MVM: Why would the Lt. pull his own hair? Think, man, think. This is why after all this time you're still a Private! EL: I- MVM: Any more lip, and it's KP duty for you! EL: Lawson off the ropes- kneedrop. Right across the head. Lawson in control now, cruising along. Drags him to the corner now- chop. And another- three. Now he goes to the closed fist. Wrench fires back- the two men trading blows- the challenger stronger, but the champion fresher! The Preacher to the headlock- right away the champ pushes off to the ropes- shoulderblock by the Preacher. Off the ropes- the Lt. ducks- now Lawson with an elbow to the sternum- covers... 1... 2... no! MVM: These Communist referees- EL: What are you talking about? MVM: East German- EL: That's "Chris Herman", Major. Lawson with a scoop slam. Off the ropes- no one home- misses the elbow drop! Both men up- Lawson sends him for the ride- the Preacher catches him in a stomach claw! (Pop) EL: He's clamped that right hand onto the abdominal muscles of the TV Champ- digging into him. Lawson in serious pain, as the Preacher pushes it up one notch. Mark Sherrick must be enjoying this- next week he has a match with the Preacher- could be for the TV title if Lawson can't counter this painful hold. This move by the Preacher on Lawson could be the biggest moment in Mark Sherrick's career right now. Lawson in big trouble right now- and he goes back the eyes! Each time he finds himself in real jeopardy, two fingers right in the eye. From down on his knees, Lawson with a punch in the stomach. Back up- harsh chop- he's thrown those all night, the Preacher with a roundhouse right- ducked! Lawson catches him as he spins- back suplex- 1.. 2.. no! The Preacher kicks out! MVM; Always the "eins, zwei", never the "drei" with Mr. East German. EL: I said before, "Chris Herman." The Preacher up- clothesline! And another! The Lt. on woozy street, we have to take a break now, when we get back, we'll have the conclusion of this contest- don't go anywhere! (July 25th, the King of E-Wrestling Qualifying Matches come to San Antonio, TX. All the top stars in Wrestling today: Dan Kauffman, Otto Verhoeven and many more. Tickets still available.) EL: The Preacher sends Lawson for the ride- clothes- duck! Lawson from behind hoists the challenger- drops him right on the top rope- throat first. A flying hangman by the champ. (The Major lets out a sigh of relief) EL; A little worried there, were you? Snap mare takedown- off the ropes- kneedrop by the champ- hooks the leg- 2 count again. He just has not been able to put the Preacher away. Wrench punching wildly- not even close. Lawson sets him up- cradles the leg- fisherman suplex. 1... 2... and the Preacher kicks out again! What resilience! Lawson in a state of disbelief. He goes for it again- he's going to suplex him again- no! He can't get him up! The Preacher now- back body drop! (Pop) EL: Now a kneedrop to follow! Lawson punches- ducked- one from the Preacher! He's going for a clawhold- iron claw- Lawson blocks- fighting to keep that claw off- and Lawson jabs with an elbow. Wrench leans on the ropes- the champ now with another off those chops. And again. Now a punch. Lawson chops again- just all over the challenger. Chris German warning him to let the challenger out of the corner. MVM: This is easier than invading Grenada. EL: Another chop, and a punch. The Lt. brawling his way to- and a right hand from the Preacher- the Preacher firing back. (Pop) EL: Chris Herman in between- and Lawson shoves the referee aside- into to the corner and chokes the Preacher! Chop- punch-chop- Lawson all over him- (Pop) EL: And Chris Herman all over the champ- on his back, pulling him away! MVM: The General will hear about this! EL: Chris Herman pulling Lawson back and Wrench nails him! Swings again- no! Lawson bailed out, and Chris Herman nailed by the Preacher! The referee is out of it, folks. Chris Herman in serious trouble. The Preacher meanwhile straddling Lawson's chest- 1... 2... 3.... 4 punches- (The crowd's blood-lust reaches a fever pitch) EL: 5... 6... 7... 8... 9... 10- I've lost count! Wrench flailing away- blood dripping down the champion's face, such is the force of these blows. Lawson drives a shoulder into his gut- into the ropes- whips him across- chop- ducked- the Preacher now with a cross body press- hooks the leg- referee Chris Herman still down- there's no one to count! You stay right where you are! MVM: Privates don't order Majors! EL: You stay out of that ring- kickout- there was no count- no referee- Lawson with a body punch- sets for a suplex- Wrench hops over- lands behind the champ- Senior Official Jeff Howard in the ring now- and the Preacher with a sleeprhold! A sleeper! (The crowd pops noisily) EL: Sit back down! Lawson can't reach the ropes- it's in the middle of the ring- we are running out of time for this one- tag titles still to come- Lawson struggling to get to the ropes- but the Preacher holding firm- Lawson just hanging by a thread at this point- he's going down- down- Chris Herman back on his feet- Jeff Howard checking- Lawson sinking fast- (The Major sputters) EL: Lawson losing consciousness- on his knees- down to the mat- Jeff Howard calls for the bell! (Huge, huge pop) EL: Kore wins! The Preacher is the new CWAA TV Champion! (Jeff Howard raises Wrench's hand as Gregorian Chant starts to play) EL: Wait a second! (Chris Herman throws it back down) EL: What's going on? A discussion now- the Preacher holding that title belt aloft as the officials talk- Lawson is out cold- the Major is pulling him out under the bottom rope. Still talking it over- OK- here's David Myers. Let's here the decision. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner as the result of a disqualification, and still- (Boos) DM: -CWAA Television Champion, Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson! EL: Listen to this crowd- no one in the arena can believe it! Chris Herman ruling that the Preacher deliberately hit him! This is daylight robbery! (When Johnny Comes Marching home Danny and Victor run for the lockers dragging the belt behind them) EL: The Preacher is going just absolutely nuts- he's going nuts in there- Jeff Howard and Chris Herman still talking it over, but Lt. Lawson has been declared the champion still. Jeff Howard the Senior Official raised Wrench's hand, but Chris Herman was the official on record for this one, and his decision is final. Kore Wrench just irate. We're going to take one last break- the tag team titles in a steel cage- our special feature match- comes next! (...for just the cost of one cup of coffee a day, you too could support an organization who will take a tiny fraction of that cost to provide minimal support for a hungry child...And spokeswoman Sally Struthers appears not to have missed many meals...) EL: All right, we have only a few minutes- I've been told that if there is a finish after television time expires, we'll show you what happens next week. (David Myers enters the ring, surrounded by the Sportatorium's answer to the steel cage- a chicken wire fence which stands a mere 7' off the ring apron- hey, if you write the matches, you pick the cage- these were so much better than the ones the WWF needed so you could see Hogan more clearly on your TV....) DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is set for one fall with a thirty-minute time limit, and is for the CWAA Tag Team Championship! (Pop) DM: Introducing first, the challengers- (ZZ Top's "Sharp Dressed Man" starts up, and the infamous duo come out with the now familiar strutting and dancing. Of course, they are wearing their lovely robes, but the fans still boo them) DM: -from parts unknown, weighing in at 497 lb., "Marvelous" Michael Anderson, "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey! BM: Hey there. You can give us the microphone, Eric. (They finally remember Eric Lane's name, but they are, of course, addressing David) MA: Well, it seems that we have to face that team again. BM: Which team? MA: Oh, I can't remember their names. They're the two ugly guys, with that chick that digs me. BM: (Nods) Oh, them. MA: They want to lock us up in this cage (shakes the wall) like we're some kind of animals. The only animals here are those fat and out of shape geezers sitting in the audience here. (The crowd didn't like this remark very much.) BM: No wonder the ladies need to come to see us each and every week. We are oh, so beautiful. (The ladies don't seem to be backing these comments.) MA: True. True. Anyway, they're putting us in a cage, because those two no-gooders keep cheating to win, and it's just so pathetic. Ain't that right, Pretty Boy? BM: You know it is. Plus, they made us face those two side show freaks with a combined IQ smaller than the number of teeth in that guy's mouth. (He says, pointing to a front row audience member.) MA: It's about time though, that they just give the belts. I mean, it would just look so much better on us than those other two. With our good looks, great style, and the way we walk, the gold would just complement us. BM: But enough talk, I see two ladies that need to be shown a good time. (With this, the duo proceed to do all sorts of gyrating and "dancing" for the benefit of some disgusted women in the front row.) DM: And their opponents- (The "Twister" theme pays, and the challengers demand that the crowd shuts up, but the pops are inevitable) DM: -from America's heartland, accompanied by Mother Nature, at a combined weight of 559 lb.- they are the CWAA Tag Team Champions, Travis Conley, Paul Langford- Twister! (Pop, pop, pop. Hop on pop) DM: Your referee, Jeff Howard. (The crowd is relieved to hear this after the last decision...) EL: Ok- here they come- we are just about of time- we'll show as much as we can- Anderson and McNultey on the attack as the champs come through the door- if we don't see the finish tonight, we'll replay what happened next week- ooh! Byron McNultey just rammed like a battering ram into that cage wall by Travis Conley- Paul Langford raking Michael Anderson's face across that chicken wire- we are out of time- next week, Kore Wrench and Mark Sherrick, Lt. Lawson., Battle Klash news, the finish to this match, and Max Robertson versus... somebody! See you then!
