Saturday Night Slam (2/9)

13 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen, set on fire. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Rock music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... David Graves taking some guy's head off with the Gravestone, Penance PLANTING some guy with the Final Judgment, The West Coast Connection DRIVING some guy's head into the mat with the West Coast Killa, Cardinal Chaos STICKING some poor soul with the Inverted Crucifix, Shane Matthews BRUTALLY hitting the End of the Road on some hapless jobber, and Heavy Metal notching yet ANOTHER casualty courtesy of the Megadeth Power Bomb. The camera then shifts to Matt Wilson, The Lovely Bertha, and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt in the Cow Palace, the site of AEWA Saturday Night Slam!) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Saturday Night Slam! We have plenty of GREAT matches scheduled for tonight, and in the main event, Big Ron Raider will be squaring off against Bishop Bedlam! BD: Well, my mind is on something else-- are we going to find out about President Freeman's team tonight? He STILL hasn't picked the guys for that tag match at Kickoff '97, and it's really starting to bug me. MW: Yes, he's definitely taking his time, Big Daddy. Hank Thompson is going to be talking with Freeman later tonight, so we might FINALLY get some information. LB: But what's taking him so long? Justin Trouble offered to help him out, but there still hasn't been any response from Freeman or the other board members. What's going on? MW: He just doesn't want to rush into this match, Bertha. It's a VERY big deal, and the entire federation is depending on him. Naturally, he wants the BEST team. BD: Wait a minute-- somebody appears to be coming over here.... (A delivery-man walks up to the broadcast booth, holding a cardboard package. He hands a clipboard to the commentators, and appears to be in somewhat of a hurry.) Delivery Man: Is there a Matt Wilson here? I have a package for him, and I'll need to get a signature. MW: I'm Matt Wilson; what's in the package? Delivery Man: I don't have any idea, all I know is that you have to sign here. There's no return address either, so it's yours no matter what. MW: (signs the clipboard and hands it back) Thanks, buddy, but next time, wait until after we're off the air to make your deliveries. Delivery Man: (walking away) Yeah, whatever.... MW: Before I start to open this, does anyone think I should get security down here, just in case? I'm mean, it seems a little suspicious. BD: Would you just open the damn thing, Wilson? You're such a baby! It's probably just another stupid surprise from the ICU. MW: Okay, but does anybody have a knife or something? There's a LOT of tape on this damn thing.... LB: (hands him a pair of scissors) Will you just open it? The suspense is killing me, for cryin' out loud! MW: Keep your pants on, Bertha. (Wilson opens the box, looks somewhat confused, and pulls out a video cassette. He isn't sure what to make of it, and hands the cassette to Big Daddy.) BD: What's this thing for? MW: Somebody wants us to watch some footage, apparently. I guess we could give this to our producers, and check it out, right? LB: But it might not be appropriate for television, Matt. Shouldn't we review this thing before we put it on the air? MW: Well, I would usually agree with you on that, but we really don't have much time. I'm not sure who sent this to me, so I'd prefer to watch it as soon as possible. I don't want to get anybody angry. (Wilson stands up from the booth, and hands the videotape to someone offstage.) BD: Okay, so are we gonna get to watch it tonight? MW: The producers just need to get it ready, Big Daddy. I really don't have a CLUE who might have sent that-- this is a complete mystery to me. LB: It sure seems strange, that's for sure. Everybody's getting excited because of Kickoff '97, and some WEIRD things have been going on around here. MW: That's for sure. Well, the video is ready now, so let's watch this footage. I don't know what it is, but there's only one way to find out.... ---------- (The camera opens on total darkness, except for the faint silhouette of a man in the background. We can't see who it is, but there are small green, glowing orbs where his eyes should be. The figure begins to speak.) Voice: This message goes out to all the AEWA superstars, however few there truly are. I've recently witnessed the past few weeks of the AEWA and have some things to say about them. First of all, I still have not seen the names of the wrestlers on Mister Freeman's team, unless you count the brave effort of Justin Trouble to get back into the AEWA. Now to start off, Mister Freeman, I would also like to convey my desire to be on your team. The likes of Kevin Larkin and the Visigothic Horde are a black spot on the image of the AEWA. (A low drone is heard in the background, along with a few screams of pain. The figure makes a motion with his hands, and "Hell's Bells" by AC/DC comes on to drown out the noise.) Voice: Even if Freeman decides he doesn't need me, I will be at Kickoff '97. And believe me, you will know who I am after that event. Some people will recognize me, others will not. But if Kevin Larkin gets out of line, I'll be there to bust his head open. I hope Mister Freeman accepts my offer, because it could be quite interesting. I'm the enforcer of the AEWA, and the Visigothic Horde can't do a damn thing about it. Are you ready to rumble, boys? (The figure begins to laugh hysterically, and he suddenly vanishes into the darkness. The video ends, and the camera returns to our broadcast team.) BD: What the hell was that all about? MW: Well, we heard the same voice on the last episode of Straight Talk, I believe. Whoever this guy is, he REALLY wants to get back into the AEWA. LB: But do you have any idea who he is? His voice seems somewhat familiar to me, but I just can't place it.... MW: I agree, Bertha. Anyway, it's time to move on. Our first match is going to be between two newcomers, Paul Smith and "Wild Card" Jeff Hawkins. BD: This should be good, folks. (The ring announcer climbs into the ring and adjusts his tie, as he lifts a microphone to his lips and speaks.) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen the following contest is a double debut match! First off, coming down the aisle, weighing in at 268 pounds, Paul Smith! (The PA comes alive as "Welcome to the Jungle" plays, and through the curtains comes Paul Smith. His bodyguard, Mister Klash, is following closely behind. Smith is a very muscular man, with blonde hair and black tights. He walks to the ring and climbs in, as Mister Klash stands silently at ringside.) BD: This kid looks promising! LB: You're telling me! He looks VERY promising-- but my money's on the other guy, Wild Card. MW: This one's too hard to call, we've got two great newcomers here. But I'm goin' with Smith for this match. RING ANNOUNCER: And now, his opponent, weighing in at 240 pounds, here is "Wild Card" Jeff Hawkins!! (The crowd begins to boo slightly as Wild Card comes through the curtains with his bodyguard, 8-Ball, following behind him. He slowly makes it to the ring, and then climbs in and throws a hard glance at Smith.) (DING, DING) MW: This one's under way and there's a lock up! Oh.... and Smith just threw Hawkins back into the corner. LB: Come on, Wild Card, don't take that! Oh, and look at Smith flex-- does he have to show off? I mean, I know he's great looking, but come on. BD: Wild Card comes back out of the corner, and locks up with Smith again. This time Hawkins wins the exchange and sends Smith back into the corner. MW: These guys are very well matched. BD: I agree, this is gonna be one helluva match. LB: They lock up again.... NO! Wild Card just poked Smith in the eye, and now he's pounding him in the face. MW: Come on, ref, get in there! Make Hawkins open up that fist! LB: I don't think the referee wants any part of that guy, buddy. He seems to be rather intimidated by Wild Card. BD: Hawkins lays off of Smith, and then grabs him and sends him into the ropes. Smith comes back and there's a clothesline from Hawkins-- OH, he took his head off!! LB: Now Hawkins is following Smith down with an elbow to the small of the back! Look at Smith writhe in pain, I LOVE IT! MW: And Hawkins is back up once more, and now putting the boots to the lower back of Paul Smith. So far, Wild Card's controlled this match. BD: That's right, buddy. This kid's great. LB: But look to the outside, folks! 8-Ball and Mister Klash seem to be having words. Those guys look pretty tough.... MW: Forget the outside, check out the inside-- Wild Card's just put on a camel clutch. He's really going after the small of the back on Smith. BD: That's a brilliant move. It looks like Wild Card's done his homework, because by attacking the back of Smith, he prevents Smith from using his finisher-- that Torture Rack. MW: You're right. BD: As usual! LB: Not quite, Big Daddy. And now Smith is powering out of the Clutch. He's on his knees! BD: And Hawkins breaks the hold, leaps into the air, and comes down hard onto the back of Smith. Smith collapses on the canvas once more. MW: And Hawkins is back down with the Camel Clutch. BD: But.... I don't believe it.... SMITH IS POWERING OUT AGAIN! He's back to his knees, and is almost out of the hold! LB: And Hawkins just did the same thing again-- released the hold and jumped up and landed on Smith. Now he's back down with the Camel Clutch. MW: Smith's pounding his leg on the mat and the crowd's getting behind him, as he's up to his knees once more. BD: And here goes Hawkins.... He releases the hold, there's the jump and he's coming down on Smith-- NO!! I don't believe it! MW: Smith rolled over and Hawkins landed on his knees! Oh, that's GOTTA hurt, folks! LB: Ya know, the referee really needs to get some damn control in there. This just is NOT fair. MW: Paul Smith hasn't cheated ONCE in this match, Bertha. LB: Yeah right! Well, anyway, both men are down and the ref's counting. We might see the match end right here.... 1... 2... 3... 4... MW: And Wild Card's to his feet, as Smith makes it to his knees! Who's gonna make the first move? BD: Here comes Hawkins, and he swings with a BIG right hand! BUT NO-- it was blocked by Smith! MW: Who lands with a right of his own, sending Hawkins to the mat! This is turning into a complete BRAWL, folks. BD: Smith's to his feet, and so's Hawkins. Here comes Wild Card, and Smith catches him. SCOOP UP AND A BODY SLAM! MW: There's the cover.... 1.... 2... KICKOUT BY HAWKINS!! BD: Smith is back to his feet, and he lifts Hawkins up! He looks to be setting him up for a Powerslam-- but no, his injured back couldn't support Hawkins' weight, and he just fell. LB: Now Wild Card's up! There's a BIG kick to Smith's back, and now he's got him by the head. BD: He's going for a Piledriver here.... NO, Smith reversed it with a backdrop, and now he's down on Hawkins with an elbow drop. LB: But look down at ringside, folks. Once again, 8-Ball and Klash are having words; they're in each other's faces. MW: And Smith just nailed Hawkins with a Powerslam, and that's the set-up for the Torture Rack. But where's the ref?! SMITH'S GOING FOR THE RACK! LB: The ref is on the outside, trying to get control with the two bodyguards. 8-Ball and Klash have gotten into a fight; they're brawling on the floor.... BD: And back in the ring, Smith just dropped Hawkins! Now he's going out to ringside-is that an intelligent move? MW: I don't think so, Big Daddy. And Smith just attacked 8-Ball from behind, but the ref has climbed back in the ring! Come on, Paul, go cover Hawkins! LB: The ref's putting the count on Smith, but he's coming back in.... BD: But he didn't make it-- Hawkins knocked him back to the outside, and now HE'S following him out of the ring! We've got complete anarchy down at ringside, folks! MW: And now both men are brawling down there; this match has gotten totally out of control! The referee's counting them... 1.. BD: I don't think they're paying attention! 3... 4... 5... 6... 7... 8... 9... 10!! (DING, DING!) MW: I don't believe it-- a double countout! BD: Let's get the official word! RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen, this match has been declared a draw, due to a double count out! LB: But they're still fighting! Here comes 8-Ball to get in on it! But he's stopped by Klash! Now it's a four-man brawl on the outside of the ring, as the officials continue to ring the bell! MW: All hell is breaking loose over there, folks! Here come some security guards, and they've gotten the four men separated. Now they're ushering 8-Ball and Wild Card back to the locker room.... BD: That was an exciting match, and I think the AEWA's gonna see a lot more from these two young up-and-coming superstars! LB: So what's next? MW: Well, Hank Thompson is about to conduct an exclusive interview with President Freeman, folks. This should be VERY interesting! BD: Has he finally picked a team for Kickoff '97? I sure hope so-- he's taking SO long to make that decision. MW: Well, let's just watch and find out. Take it away, Hank! (The camera switches to Hank Thompson, standing with President Freeman in the ring. He is holding a microphone, as usual, and begins the interview.) Thompson: Hello everyone, I'm here with President Freeman, and I have some very important questions to ask him. (turns to Freeman) Sir, I wanted to ask you about the current situation with Justin Trouble. You recently fired him, however, many people thought you were too harsh about it. Any thoughts on the matter? Freeman: I really don't understand what all the controversy is about. Justin was hired to work as my bodyguard, but he never even showed up when I was getting attacked by the Visigothic Horde. Naturally, like any reasonable employer, I fired him. What's wrong with that, Hank? Thompson: Well, according to Justin, it wasn't his fault. He claims that Kevin Larkin locked him in a room, so he COULDN'T have been there. He even has the padlock to prove it! Freeman: Frankly, I don't give a damn what he says. The point is, he wasn't doing his job, and now he's making lame excuses about it. He can't even take his punishment like a man, I guess. Thompson: I've become good friends with Justin over the past few months, sir, and you're being way too hard on him. I really wish you'd stop. Freeman: When I want your opinion, Hank, I'll ASK for it. This thing is between me and Justin, so please-- mind your own business. Thompson: (looks slightly hurt) I'm sorry for offending you. Anyway, as you may have heard, he recently offered to join your tag team at Kickoff '97, with some special stipulations. What's your answer? Freeman: When I first heard him say that, I thought it was a joke. After all he's done wrong, he wants me to give him ANOTHER job? (shakes his head) Not a chance in hell, buddy. Justin Trouble will never work for the AEWA again. Thompson: I would comment on what you just said, but I'm afraid of losing MY job. You sure have developed an attitude lately, sir. Freeman: Hank, I do NOT need to listen to your criticism. I've just been under a lot of stress lately, because of all this stuff with Kevin. (puts his hand on Hank's shoulder) I apologize for being so rude. Thompson: It's okay, Bob, I understand. Since you mentioned him, though, I was wondering if I could get your comments on Kevin Larkin. Have you picked your team for Kickoff '97 yet? Freeman: Yes, I have. I'm not going to reveal WHICH team, however, because I want to maintain an element of surprise. Thompson: Can't you tell us ANYTHING about the team? Freeman: No, I can't. Thompson: Well, I really don't have any other questions for you, so unless you have any other comments-- Freeman: Yes, I want to say one other thing. (turns to the camera) Kevin Larkin, it's almost time for your judgment day. After Kickoff '97, there will only be ONE law in the AEWA-- me. Bring your damn Visigothic Horde, it doesn't matter, because my team WILL emerge victorious. I'm ready.... are you? Thompson: I'm sure he is, sir. Thank you for your time, and folks, be sure to order Kickoff '97 on pay-per-view! (Both men leave the ring, and the camera returns to our broadcast team.) MW: President Freeman seems to be VERY tense, folks. Unfortunately, we still don't know much about his team. BD: I don't think he even HAS a team. He's being too damn secretive about this whole thing, it just seems fishy to me. LB: Freeman has some BIG problems, that's for sure. Anyway, it's time to move on with the show. BD: What's the next match, Williams? MW: It's Wilson, and.... never mind. Up next, the former Curate Kill, now called Bishop Bedlam, takes on Big Ron Raider. LB: FINALLY! A match between two real competitors. You won't see any of the garbage that you see with the Knights of Deliverance. MW: You mean like a clean match and good sportsmanship? BD: Exactly. MW: (rolls his eyes) Whatever. Let's get to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from the Church of the Pounding Light, weighing in at 275 pounds, Biiiiiishooooop Beeeeeeeeeeedlaaaaaaaaaaaaam! (Bishop Bedlam, wearing a red robe, makes his entrance to a HUGE round of boos. He appears to be in some sort of a trance, and stares directly at Raider.) BD: Look at the eyes of Bedlam, folks. He's totally whacked-out right now. I wouldn't want to be receiving that stare. MW: Maybe not, Big Daddy-- but somehow, I don't think his opponent cares. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, From the Rocky Mountains, he weighs in at 416 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his manager, Million Dollar Mark, here is Big.... Ron.... Raaaaaaaaaaaaaaaiiiiiiiiideeeeeeeeeeer! (Lynyrd Skynyrd's "Saturday Night Special" cranks over the PA as Raider makes his entrance to a HUGE chorus of boos. He spits at several ringside fans, and enters the ring. Million Dollar Mark is at his side.) MW: Raider's not even waiting for the bell. He storms into the ring, and starts pounding away at Bedlam. He whips Bedlam into the ropes, and connects with a vicious clothesline! Bishop Bedlam hasn't even removed his robe yet! LB: As much as I like Bishop Bedlam, I think Raider's going to win this match. His size advantage will probably be the deciding factor. MW: I have to agree. Big Ron Raider is one of the most accomplished wrestlers in the history of the AEWA, while Bishop Bedlam hasn't achieved nearly as much. Meanwhile, Raider keeps stomping away at Bedlam. He picks Bedlam up again, sends him into the ropes, backdrops.... NO! Bedlam stops in his tracks, picks Raider up, and piledrives the big man into the mat. BD: Bedlam needed that. Up until now, Raider had been eating him alive. MW: Bishop Bedlam finally removes his robe, and he starts choking Raider with the robe. The referee's trying to pull the robe away from him, but Bishop's not letting go. The referee could disqualify Bedlam at this point, but I guess he's going to let these men bend the rules. BD: Good choice. They're going to, whether the ref allows it or not. MW: Regardless, Bishop finally lets go of the robe, and the referee tosses it out of the ring. Bedlam rakes the eyes of Raider, and whips him into the corner. Bedlam follows him, and connects with a dropkick! With Raider already in the corner, he took the full force of that dropkick. Bishop whips him to the opposite corner, but Raider reverses, and gives Bedlam a short clothesline. He then tosses Bedlam out of the ring! LB: Raider is giving himself a chance to recover. He's still holding his throat from being choked. BD: And look at Million Dollar Mark! While Ron is arguing with the referee, Mark hits Bedlam with his shoe! He's a brilliant manager. MW: Now that Mark has done his damage, Raider rolls out of the ring. He picks Bedlam up, and drops him, with his neck right across the guard rail! He stands Bedlam up, AND RUNS RIGHT AT HIM WITH A CLOTHESLINE! He connects solidly, and Bedlam crumbles to the ground. Million Dollar Mark is applauding his wrestler now, folks. LB: Speaking of managers, where is the Church? Why wouldn't they be in Bedlam's corner? MW: I don't know, maybe they wanted him to fend on his own. Raider rolls Bedlam back into the ring, and makes a lazy cover. 1.... 2... BEDLAM KICKS OUT! BD: Bedlam somehow managed to get out of that, folks. Raider picks Bedlam up-- he's setting him up for the Raider Bomb, and.... OOOH!! (Matt Wilson and Big Daddy both flinch, while the Lovely Bertha simply laughs.) LB: Come on, guys. Bishop just punched Raider in the groin, and the referee was out of position. Lighten up. BD: You wouldn't understand. MW: I speak for all of our gender when I say that our deepest sympathies go out to Ron Raider. Meanwhile, Bedlam has taken control of the match again, hitting Raider with a series of uppercuts and elbows. He picks Raider up, AND SLAMS HIM TO THE GROUND! BD: I'm amazed that he could get the big guy up there. Look, Million Dollar Mark is up on the apron! MW: Bedlam gets away from Raider, and pulls Mark into the ring. He picks him up, and powerslams him into the ground. Million Dollar Mark isn't moving! LB: That's a mistake on Bedlam's part. He has to beat Raider, not his manager. MW: I agree. While Bedlam and the referee are occupied with Mark, Ron Raider has stood up, and is stalking toward Bedlam.... BD: BUT BEDLAM SEES HIM, AND CLOCKS HIM WITH A FIST! MW: And the fist is loaded! He pulled a pair of brass knuckles from his tights, and the ref didn't see it! LB: (laughing) Brilliant on Bedlam's part. He takes out the manager, which distracts the referee long enough to hit Raider with a foreign object. MW: Bedlam quickly climbs the ropes, and hits Raider with a fistdrop from the top rope! He's still using those brass knuckles! The referee finally gets Mark out of the ring, and turns around to see Bedlam covering Raider. He makes the count.... 1.... 2... 3!! (DING, DING) BD: It's all over, folks. This has to be the biggest victory of Bishop Bedlam's career, wouldn't you say? LB: Definitely. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, after nine minutes and six seconds, here is your winner, BISHOP BEDLAM! MW: Bedlam wins, with the help of a few illegal actions. Now he rolls out of the ring and walks out of the arena.... LB: He's still showing that odd trance that he walked into the ring with. Meanwhile, Ron Raider is sitting up, but Mark is still out. We may need some medical attention for him. BD: Raider picks up Million Dollar Mark, and carries him over his shoulder. He's taking him to the back himself. LB: And listen to him holler! This is not the last we've heard between Bedlam and Raider, folks. MW: Not by a long shot. Well, that's all the time we have for tonight, so for Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt and the Lovely Bertha, this is Matt Wilson saying so long everybody! LB: And be sure to call your local cable company and order Kickoff '97, people! It's gonna be GREAT!
