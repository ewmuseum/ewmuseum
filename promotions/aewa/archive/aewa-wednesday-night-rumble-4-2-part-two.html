AEWA — Card Archive

Wednesday Night Rumble (4/2, part two)

5 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * CONTINUED FROM PART ONE * * * * MW: Let's move on. Ladies and gentlemen, in our main event match of the evening, "Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi will be squaring off against "Crimson" Joe Reed in a non-title match. Before you start asking questions, Big Daddy, let me make this clear-- the World Heavyweight belt and the Light Heavyweight belt are NOT on the line tonight. BD: Jeez, why does President Freeman keep signing these matches? I want to see some damn CHAMPIONSHIP matches! MW: You'll have to wait until AEWA Extreme, Big Daddy. LB: By the way, Valachi's been on quite a winning streak lately, hasn't he? I might not really like the little guy, but you have to admit, he's tough. MW: That's for sure, Bertha. Anyway, the ring announcer is ready to start this thing off, so let's listen to the introduction.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following non-title match is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, weighing in at 202 pounds, he is the current AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion of the World, "the Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi! (Pat Benatar's "Gettin' Paid" can be heard over the public address system, and Joey Valachi begins to walk down the aisle to a huge round of boos. He's wearing a black shirt with "AEWA Sucks" in huge red writing, and the crowd begins to throw trash at him in disgust. Being the sneaky fellow he is, of course, Valachi ducks out of the way. He enters the ring, and spits at several fans in the front row.) MW: Ya know, this guy has absolutely NO respect for our great organization or the fans. I really think President Freeman should fire him for this. BD: I might not like him much either, Wilson, but he IS the Light Heavyweight champion. He deserves SOME respect, I guess. MW: Good point. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, From Lexington, Kentucky, he weighs in at 257 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his manager, The Lady in Red, here is the AEWA World Heavyweight champion, "Crrrrrrriiiiiimson" Joe Reeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeed! (Bad Company's "Bad Company" begins playing loudly throughout the arena, and the crowd instantly begins to cheer. Reed and the Lady In Red make their entrance, slapping hands with their fans along the way to the ring. Reed grabs the microphone from the announcer's table, and rolls into the ring. In addition to his usual attire, he is wearing an "AEWA Rocks!" T-shirt.) REED: Most of the time, I have a live and let live philosophy. I don't get involved when someone shoots their mouth off. But tonight's different. Joey, I've heard you run your mouth off for a month about the AEWA. And tonight-- it stops. (Thunderous pop) Being the champion and ambassador of the AEWA, I will not tolerate these slurs by you. So I'm going to give you two choices right now, Joey. I suggest you make the right decision. Option one-- Take off the shirt, throw it away, and apologize to everyone in the AEWA for your recent comments. Then you and I can have a nice, simple match, with the better man winning. Option two-- And Joey, you REALLY don't want this option. Option two says you keep the shirt, and you and I will fight. And sometime during this match, Joey, I am going to rip the shirt off your back and make you EAT the damn thing! (He throws down the microphone, and awaits a response. Joey Valachi picks it up, stares at Reed for a few seconds, and begins to speak.) VALACHI: You want an apology from me, Reed? Do you have any idea who I AM? I'm "the Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi, buddy, and that means one thing-- I don't apologize to ANYONE. So if you want to make me eat this shirt, well.... I dare ya. (Valachi tosses the microphone out of the ring.) MW: Well, it looks like we're ready for the match to begin now, folks. Reed appears to be pretty angry with Joey right now, so this will probably resemble a street fight more than a match. LB: Well, I really wish Valachi would stop wearing that damn shirt. What's the point of it? Until he stops receiving an AEWA paycheck, that shirt shouldn't be allowed in the arena. MW: Unfortunately, he considers it his "freedom of expression." Unless President Freeman wants to argue with the US Constitution, there's no way to stop Joey from wearing it. BD: Valachi's been waiting for this, and I think he's gonna.... wait a minute, who are these morons? (Four men come out of the crowd and into the ring. All look like wrestlers, especially the huge guy in the leather jacket. It looks like both Reed and Valachi recognize the intruders.) MW: Well, I don't know if I'm glad they're here, but I can't say I'm surprised. As some of you may know, "Crimson" Joe Reed also competes in the UWF, and he recently won a shot at the UWF World Title, which he turned down in order to fulfill his commitments as the AEWA World Champion. In the process, however, he said a few things that may not have sat well with the UWF'ers. Those four men are four of the most popular wrestlers in the UWF. They're the members of a stable called Legion. The smallest one is Youth Gone Wild, next to him is Tom Landis. The big guy in leather is Black Diamond, and the man who just picked up the microphone is Alex Kidd, the UWF World Champion. ALEX KIDD: All right, Reed. After hearing your comments about the UWF last Sunday, we figured we should have a talk with you on your own turf, the AEWA, which you seem to be high and mighty about. You may be the AEWA World champion, but I'm the UWF World champion. And do you see me wandering around other feds making fun of them? I don't think so! YOUTH GONE WILD: (taking the microphone from Kidd) You know, normally I wouldn't have any beef with the AEWA. You've got some good wrestlers. But when you run down the organization that WE built, I feel the need to point a few things out. (points to Valachi) Like this dork, for instance. If the AEWA's so freaking great, how come this putz is your Light-Heavyweight champion? He's not good enough to make the UWF. He's been trying to get in for months, but YOUR STAR doesn't make the grade. (The partisan AEWA crowd cheers, always glad to see Valachi insulted. An irked Cardiac Kid steps forward.) VALACHI: Hey, I beat you, you glam-rocking wannabe. But hey, Reed, guess what? Even these losers can see that the AEWA SUCKS! BLACK DIAMOND: (grabs Valachi by the lapels) Nobody asked you! (The big man tosses Valachi into the corner. In a rare fit of common sense, Valachi stays there.) TOM LANDIS: Hold on big guy, we aren't here to cause any trouble, at least not in the physical sense. For all of you out there that think this is a typical takeover deal, wrong! I respect the AEWA, because of its great competition. All we are here for is to send ol' Crimson Joe a message. If you want to compete in the UWF, more power to ya. But don't think for a minute that because you are AEWA World Champion that you're untouchable. If you keep up with these comments about the house that Legion built, then prepare for a major league butt-kicking. (Landis hands the microphone back to Black Diamond.) BLACK DIAMOND: Reed, you beat me in the triangle match at Gold Rush last week and I almost had some respect for you. But now, I see that you're just a loser who wants to stay in his loser fed, where he can be King of the Losers. That World Title you've got don't mean a thing. The only World Title that matters is that one around Alex's waist. (Diamond throws the microphone at Reed, who immediately picks it up. He stares at these four men, and begins speak.) REED: Now, hold on, fellows. Let's clear up a few things. First of all, I want to congratulate you on having the courage to confront me when you have a FOUR to ONE advantage. Now, I did win the triangle match, beating both you (points to Black Diamond) and Running Eagle Feather. But I couldn't accept the title shot. Quite frankly, I don't have the time. I have to defend this (points to his belt), the AEWA World Title. I have dozens of the toughest wrestlers in the world, people like Heavy Metal and Ben Slocum and Mister Crowley, trying to take me down. It came down to concentrating on the AEWA or the UWF, and I chose the AEWA. As for my comments about the UWF. Did I wear a shirt that said "UWF Sucks," like this (points to Valachi) little piece of garbage does? No, I didn't. All I did was explain my position that the AEWA was the best league of wrestling, and that it was a better league than any of the others-- even the UWF. Hey, I like the UWF. I think it's a great little league. And I like you guys. I think that someday you'll be ready to wrestle here in the AEWA full time. (At this point, a group of officials show up to break up the party. As Legion starts leaving the ring, Reed finishes his speech.) REED: So guys, stick around, enjoy your visit up here, maybe you can even learn a few things by watching the match. After all, you are our guests here. (Reed drops the microphone, and watches cautiously as the Legion members walk back into the audience and take a seat. He then hands the microphone to the ring announcer, and prepares to begins to the match.) BD: What the hell was that all about? If those guys have a problem with Reed from the UWF, they need to deal with these problems elsewhere. We don't give a damn about their petty little problems. MW: Well, Big Daddy, don't rush to judgment quite so quickly. I was among the thousands in attendance at Gold Rush, and I have to admit-- Reed was a little bit rude with what he said. I'm not a bit surprised that Legion wanted to get a measure of revenge here tonight. LB: But this is the AEWA, the best wrestling promotion on Earth, and those losers don't have the right to interrupt our program. It makes me sick. MW: I'd love to keep discussing this situation with you two, but the match is about to begin. (DING, DING) MW: Reed and Valachi get in a collar-and-elbow tie up. Reed muscles Valachi over to the corner.... the referee's breaking them apart.... Reed gives a clean break.... BD: And Valachi rakes his eyes! Reed's blinded, and Valachi hops up to the second turnbuckle, and hits an elbow on top of Reed's skull. He picks up Reed, and whips him into the ropes. Valachi goes for a dropkick-- LB: But nobody's home! Reed held onto the ropes, and Valachi crashed to the mat. MW: Reed walks over as Valachi gets up, and gives him a double sledgehammer blow across the back. Valachi's down.... and legsweeps Reed. Now he ties up Reed's legs. BD: Valachi's a punk, but he's smart. If Reed's legs are hurt, he's an easy target for Valachi's air attack. MW: Reed grabs the bottom rope, and the referee orders a break. Valachi gives Reed a few kicks in the legs and he lets go. Valachi bounces off the ropes.... a dropkick, right into Reed's knee! We're just a few minutes into this match, and Reed is already favoring one leg. (Joey Valachi turns and taunts the crowd, who shout back some derogatory comments about the "Cardiac Kid". Valachi turns around....) MW: Clothesline by Reed! Now Reed tackles Valachi, and starts hammering away with lefts and rights. BD: If this stays a brawl, Reed will win. He's too big for Valachi to beat in a slugfest. MW: Reed's now grabbing Valachi's shirt.... I think he's trying to rip it off. LB: Do it! Make the punk eat his words. MW: Valachi knees him in the midsection, and spins around into a Russian Legsweep. But Valachi's shirt is ripped, and is just barely hanging on. Valachi bounces off the ropes, and delivers a legdrop! Here's a cover... One.. Two.. Kickout! MW: Kickout by the World Champion. Now Valachi is motioning-- he's going for Reed's "AEWA Rocks!" T-shirt. He stands Reed up, and nails him with a belly-to-back suplex. Now he's going for the shirt.... LB: Bad mistake on both men. Always go for the win, instead of this T-shirt nonsense. MW: He's pulled it over Reed's head, blinding him. Now Valachi runs off the ropes, and nails Reed with a jumping side kick! He goes for the shirt again.... and pulls it off. LB: Oh God-- look at that punk. What's he yelling? BD: Something about eating some words.... he's trying to shove the T-shirt down Reed's throat! MW: He must not have liked Reed's comments earlier. He's trying-- but Reed nails him with a right hand! And another! Valachi's stunned.... Crimson Joe picks him up, and slams him to the canvas. But instead of covering him, he's going for the shirt again. BD: Why? Just pin the man. I'm not a Joe Reed fan, but he's definitely the lesser of two evils here. LB: Yes, but this time he's got the shirt ripped off! Now what? MW: Reed kicks Valachi in the midsection, and as Valachi bends over, Reed shoves the "AEWA Sucks!" T-shirt in the Cardiac Kid's mouth! He did it-- he made Valachi eat his words. The referee's breaking this up now; I guess he considers the shirt to be a weapon. Reed lets up the attack, and Valachi rolls out of the ring, coughing and gagging. BD: How did you like that, Joey? MW: Valachi's furious! He's demanding that the referee disqualify Reed, but the referee has tossed both shirts out of the ring. LB: Maybe now we can have a normal match. MW: Valachi reluctantly re-enters the ring. Things have slowed down now. Reed and Valachi have another tie up, and Valachi armdrags the World Champ! Reed stands up, but Valachi takes him down with a pull of the hair. Reed again stands up, and Valachi pulls his hair again. Reed bounces up, shoves Valachi into the ropes, hits him with a backdrop.... look at the elevation Valachi got! BD: Not to mention how high he flew from that backdrop. MW: Reed has control of the match. He picks up Valachi, and hits him with a backbreaker over the knee. Instead of dropping him, he lifts him up, and plants him over his knee again. Here's a cover.... One.. Two.. Kickout! LB: Like I said, the punk is tough. Knock him around some more, Reed! MW: I can't believe I'm hearing this. LB: I agree with Big Daddy. Crimson Joe is annoying, and I expect him to lose his title at AEWA Extreme, but anyone is better than Valachi. Even those UWF punks.... MW: Let's not go there again. Joe Reed has locked the "Cardiac Kid" in an abdominal stretch, and with Reed's height advantage, Valachi is in serious trouble. He's locked in the middle of the ring, and can't reach the ropes. The referee is checking for a submission, but Valachi is refusing. He's trying to maneuver his way out.... he rolls Reed up! One.. Two.. Kickout! BD: That was close. Crimson Joe almost got stunned with a loss, and Valachi is out of that abdominal stretch. MW: Valachi bounces off the ropes.... Reed tries for a clothesline, but misses.... Valachi keeps the momentum, bounces off the other side of the ring, and nails Reed with a bulldog. A cover! One.. Kickout! MW: Valachi pulls Reed up, and whips him to the corner. Reed reverses the whip, and charges in after Valachi. He misses! Reed was trying for a kneelift, but Valachi moved, and Reed took the full force on his leg! LB: That was the same leg that Valachi dropkicked earlier. Reed is in serious trouble. MW: Valachi rolls out of the ring, and grabs Reed's knee. He slams it against the steel ringpost.... and again! The referee finally break up the attack, but the damage is done. BD: Valachi's not even looking at Reed. He turns his back to trade insults with some of the fans. MW: Now Valachi turns back towards the ring. Crimson Joe is in trouble. He's moving away from the turnbuckle, but he can't stand right now, so he's just crawling away. LB: Valachi's hops up to the ring apron, and climbs the ringpost. He hops off.... and nails Reed with a kneedrop! The cover... One.. Two.. Thr.. Kickout! BD: Valachi's complaining to the referee that the count was slow. LB: The fool should be attacking instead on complaining. MW: Valachi grabs Reed's leg, spins around for a figure-four.... Reed rolls him up in a cradle! One.. Two.. Kickout! (The crowd groans.) BD: Damn! I thought he had him. MW: Valachi levels Reed with a series of kicks. I don't think the champ was expecting this much of a fight. Valachi bounces off the ropes.... an elbowdrop! The cover.... One.. Two.. Kickout! BD: Reed's tough, but he's off tonight. I think all of the pre-match comments shook him. MW: Valachi stands Reed up, and bounces off the ropes.... Reed caught him! A spinebuster slam! The cover... One.. Two.. Valachi grabs the ropes! LB: Valachi's lucky he was so close to the ropes, or that move might have put him away. MW: Reed hits Valachi with a gutwrench suplex, and follows it up with an elbow to the small of Valachi's back. Now Reed is signaling for the Kentucky Backsmasher! He lifts Valachi up in a vertical suplex, and drops him on the ropes.... LB: Valachi knows what's coming.... he's grabbing the ropes, and staying outside of the ring apron. Reed tries to slingshot him, but can't. MW: Valachi grabs Reed's head, and falls to the floor, giving Reed a modified Hot Shot in the process. Valachi is scaling the ropes, and is perched on the top turnbuckle.... BD: Reed runs into the corner, causing Valachi to fall! Valachi is just sitting on the top cornerpost now. MW: Reed climbs up to the second turnbuckle, grabs Valachi's head-- he's going for a superplex! But he's struggling.... BD: His leg won't hold both of them up! MW: Reed's leg is collapsing, and both men are falling.... Valachi is on top.... Reed uses the momentum and rolls over.... One.. Two.. Three! (DING, DING) MW: Valachi had pivoted his body to land on top of Reed, but Reed kept rolling and cradled Valachi for the three count. Let's hear the official announcement. RING ANNOUNCER: At nineteen minutes and twenty-three seconds, here is your winner, the AEWA World Champion, "Crimmsssooonnnn" Joe Reeeed!! MW: A hard-fought victory, and I don't think he was expecting such a fight. The Lady in Red hands Reed the belt, which he holds high above his head to the cheers of the crowd. LB: He's pointing to the UWF stars and saying something to The Lady in Red.... I can't hear what he's saying. MW: The champion and his manager leave the ring and shake hands with the crowd. We'll see what develops between Reed and the UWF superstars. BD: Forget them! Between Crowley, Heavy Metal, and Slocum, Reed's got enough on his plate to worry about. He's going to be crushed. MW: I thought you were getting to respect Crimson Joe. BD: Me? No-- I just didn't want Valachi to win. Can you imagine what he'd be saying if he did? So Reed beat Valachi, and now he can lose his title. MW: Thanks for the insight, Big Daddy. Well, that's all the time we have for tonight, so until next time-- Wait a minute, it looks like something is happening at ringside. (The camera suddenly switches to the front row, where Wild Thing is seated on a steel chair. He notices the cameraman and smiles cynically, then stands up. The crowd immediately begins booing.) BD: This does NOT look good, folks. We're just about to go off the air, and Wild Thing has just been spotted at ringside. We all remember what happened the LAST time he made a surprise appearance, right? MW: How could we ever forget, Big Daddy-- it was a complete embarrassment to the AEWA. The FCC fined us over two thousand dollars for that little stunt. LB: He's doing something down there now. It looks like he's about to jump over into the ringside area. Let's watch.... (Wild Thing climbs over the guardrail from his front row seat, and slowly begins to walk around the ring. He is wearing a white polo shirt and blue jeans, just like he wore for his last appearance. This time, however, there is a large button pinned to the upper right-hand corner of his shirt, which clearly says "Back Off" in black writing. His short brown hair has been slicked with gel. He finally stops pacing around the ring, and enters it, to a huge round of boos from the crowd. He holds his left hand up and flashes the middle finger at the crowd, much to their dismay. Finally, he signals to the timid-looking ring announcer, and grabs the microphone. After a few seconds of silence, he begins to speak in a low voice.) WILD THING: Well, AEWA, here I go again. They didn't want me to come out here tonight, in fact, they were were AFRAID of it, but I don't give a damn what they think. I've got a few more things to get off my chest, so shut the hell up and let me talk. From now on, I'm doing whatever I want to do, whenever the hell I feel like doing it. If I feel like coming to the ring in my boxer shorts and an undershirt, nobody can do a damn thing about it. If I want to wrestle in a tuxedo with a rose in my mouth, that's perfectly fine. Hell, I could even have sex with a woman in the middle of the ring, and the censors couldn't even stop me. That's the beauty of live television, and that's the beauty of my new view on life. I'm not gonna be a cartoon character anymore-- I'm 100% genuine Wild Thing, and if President Freeman doesn't like it, he can kiss my ass! Ya know, they fined me for what I said on Saturday's program, but quite frankly, I don't give a damn. I felt like saying it, so I said it. What's so bad about that? I'm probably the only HONEST wrestler in this entire federation. For the first time in my life, I feel free. There aren't any chains holding me down anymore, and I can finally tell it like it is. I'm burning all my bridges, biting the hand that feeds me, and guess what-- nobody can stop me. I didn't appreciate that fine, though, so I went down to the AEWA Headquarters in San Francisco to complain. I DEMANDED that they let me in President Freeman's office, but just as I expected, they wouldn't even let me in the damn building. Well, let me send a personal message to you right now, Freeman. You can keep ignoring me, buddy, but I'm never gonna quit it. From this day forward, I want you to STOP selling the "Wild Thing" merchandise, because as far as I'm concerned, I'm no longer an employee of the AEWA. And I've been waiting to say this for a long time, Freeman.... You can take this job and shove it right up your ASS! I think Freeman's still a little bit angry about that time I went on a vacation with his family. We were all good friends back then, but it turned ugly when he caught me screwing his wife. I was the best she's ever had, just like I was always the best in the AEWA. Well, times have changed. You hardly ever hear about me anymore, because Freeman doesn't want people to remember that I ever existed. I used to have my own web site on the Internet, but he cut that off. I used to be making plenty of money, but he cut that off too. I used to have a big home in the Hollywood Hills, but guess what-- he cut it off. What more do you want to take from me, Freeman? You think I'm an embarrassment to the AEWA? Well, that's fine with me. Your wife's a BITCH and so are you. (The crowd absolutely explodes with boos, and they begin to chant "WILD-THING-SUCKS!" loudly. He holds his middle finger up at them, and continues speaking in his usual monotone.) Folks, why do you think guys like "Crimson" Joe Reed and Mister Crowley are getting all the spotlight around here? Because they've never done anything to piss Freeman off. He plays favorites, and whichever wrestler kisses his ass the most, that's the guy who'll get a title shot. It's not fair, it's never been fair, but to tell ya the truth, I don't really care. (chuckles) Hey, I'm a poet and don't know it. But seriously, somebody needs to put their foot down, and if nobody else wants to help me, I'll do it all by myself. The AEWA can go to hell.... And just for the record, you fans don't mean ANYTHING to me. I don't have any regrets for what I said, so just shut your damn mouths. You people hardly have enough money to feed your kids, but you always find enough cash to pay for a front row seat at a pro wrestling event. You morons make me sick! I have absolutely no respect for you jerks, and I never really did. You think it makes me angry when you boo? Wrong. It only makes me stronger, because I know I must be doing _something_ right. You can throw your damn garbage at me, it doesn't matter, because you'll never make me leave. In fact, let me be a little bit more clear.... You're all a bunch of ASSHOLES! How did that sound, morons? I'm sure the FCC is gonna fine me again, but I really don't have any money left to pay 'em, so who cares? After I get done with the AEWA, they'll probably throw us off the air. It doesn't matter to me. So before I leave, let me leave you with one final thought-- SCREW YOU ALL TO HELL! (There is an absolute uproar in the audience, with everyone throwing every bit of trash they can get their hands on. Wild Thing is quick to make his escape, running down the aisle to the locker room. The camera shifts back to our announcing team, who seem to be in a state of shock.) MW: Once again, I'd like to apologize on behalf of the entire AEWA organization for what he just said. Our censors apparently didn't have time to keep that from going on the air, so I'm VERY sorry to all the families out there. BD: That guy has gone too far, buddy. I never really liked him before, but we can't keep allowing him to interrupt our shows with this crap. Why the hell did security let him come out here? MW: They probably just didn't seem him in the audience, Big Daddy. I really can't believe what just happened-- he did it AGAIN. (shakes his head) I'm almost embarrassed to be associated with this promotion right now. LB: So am I, Matt. President Freeman needs to do something about Wild Thing NOW, because imagine what would happen if he showed up at AEWA Extreme! It would be horrible. MW: Yeah, it would be. Anyway, we're completely out of time, so I'd just like to remind you all to order AEWA Extreme while there's still time. Call your local cable company right now, because it's all happening this weekend! See ya there, folks.
