AEWA — Card Archive

Straight Talk (12/3, part two)

6 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
******************************************************** CONTINUED FROM PART ONE ******************************************************** -------------- (Deranged sits in the AEWA locker room area, and watches the replay of him being attacked by the group he was once in. He frowns and then later watches as he loses his title because of this interference.) Deranged: There, AEWA. Is this enough proof for you and the fans that I am not with those guys anymore? What more do you need for me? Now that the fans know the truth, I want to start hearing some cheers again! All I know is that Randall Osbourne has the TV Title now. He also made an interesting offer to me. Let me show you. (Deranged picks up the remote for the VCR and he forwards it to Randall's interview. Randall comments on winning the title in a dirty way. He also offers Deranged a rematch because of this victory. Deranged pauses the VCR and sets down the remote.) Deranged: Randall, I am willing to accept your rematch. I agree about winning the title non-fairly and you offered the rematch. Well if you get by your opponent next, then I will take you on. But now I believe that it is time fore something different. Bob Freeman. You sir. I need to have a talk with you. Now that I feel I am ready, I want a world title shot. I believe Deranged can beat your world champion and become the new one. You have avoided that idea long enough. (Deranged un-pauses the VCR and rewinds to the part with the Angels Of Death. One of them makes a comment about never turning your back on them. Deranged begins to get furious, but calms down.) Deranged: Bob Freeman, I would like a world title shot, plain and simple. But I want this stipulation-- if anybody interferes during my matchup for the world title, they will be SUSPENDED from the AEWA forever! I have been cheated out of titles long enough and now I will not tolerate it any longer. I want the world title and I will get it! The fans now should support me 100% because I am going to do this for them. I will also have a manager at my side during the world title match. You will all see a very Deranged World Champion soon! (fade to black) -------------- KW: What a joke! Deranged expects the fans to like him after he turned his back on them?! CC: I'd have to agree with you there, Ken. And now he wants the WORLD title! He may be a good wrestler, but I don't think he's proven himself. He lost his last match-- it was because of interference, but he still LOST. KW: And I don't believe that the fans, no matter how stupid they are, are going to start cheering for Deranged any time soon. He really hurt them, and now, because he left the Angels of Death and has no allies, he hopes to win some support. CC: Well, he's in a tricky situation right now. Now, we move on to a man I swear I have seen before, the Masked Yorel.... ------------- (The camera shows a huge dark figure watching some tapes of AEWA action. He turns to the camera and says....) Yorel: Ah, AEWA, you don't know what you are about to feel. You can never imagine the force that I have brought to this league, but you will soon feel the force. I am going to take the North American title very soon and after that, the titles will just keep going around my waist. Heavy Metal and Ron Raider, you don't even have a chance against The Masked Yorel! I am here to show the AEWA what real wrestling is. There is only one other person that has my style, but I wont mention any names. People have been going around wondering who I am. Don't you worry about my identity, just worry about what I will do here in the AEWA! (fade to black) --------------- KW: Man, I KNOW I've seen him before! CC: Yes, I can't shake that feeling either. KW: Oh well, it's only a matter of time before SOMEONE recognizes him enough to reveal his true identity. CC: Up next, here are some comments from Justin Trouble. He was recently interviewed by Hank Thompson, and we have the footage. KW: Boy, Thompson's been pretty busy this week. Let's watch.... ------------- (The camera opens on Hank Thompson, standing in the ring. He is holding a microphone, prepared to conduct the interview.) Hank Thompson: All right folks, we have a special interview with the one, the only, JUSTIN TROUBLE! (Tom Petty's "Makin' Some Noise" blasts loudly over the PA. The fans are going absolutely nuts. Trouble comes out, with his hair tied back in a ponytail. He slaps hands with the fans on his way to the ring. He's wearing a T-shirt that says "AEWA & Justin Trouble Forever", with Trouble's vital stats on it, a brief history of him, and his signature. Trouble finally climbs into the ring, and shakes hands with Thompson) Hank Thompson: Welcome back, Justin! Trouble: Nice to be back, Hank. Hank Thompson: (points to Trouble's shirt) Trying to merchandise now? Trouble: (laughs) Sort of. I'm just showing my support for the AEWA. But I'm not here to talk about merchandising. I'm here to talk about this.... (pulls out a few sheets of paper) This here is an open contract. It has become apparent to me that the ICU and ANY of it's members are too chicken to face me. So here it is-- I'll face ANYONE. Whether it be a title holder like "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews, or Big Ron Raider, or even a rookie like Brian Rice; I'll take on anyone, but I'd prefer it to be Death-Breath or Heavy Metal. Hank Thompson: Why the open contract? Trouble: The open contract just proves that if the ICU wants to, they have every opportunity in the world to face me. It just proves that if they don't sign, they are indeed scared. Scared of the fact that I'll put each and every one of them in a REAL ICU, not International Contracts Unlimited, but an Intensive Care Unit. (fans go wild) Hank Thompson: How are your bodyguard duties going? Trouble: That's another thing. Freeman hasn't been attacked lately, has he? Hank Thompson: No. Trouble: And that's because the ICU, Angels of Death, and the Church won't go near him! If they DO, I'll shove my foot so far down their throats that they'll be sh*tting shoe polish for a week (crowd goes crazy yet again) Last week, if you'll remember, I also challenged the ICU to a five-man tag team elimination match.... and they haven't responded to that challenge for the next pay per view. Hank Thompson: Wait.... (puts his hand to his ear) I'm getting something. She's here?! Right now? (Trouble looks confused) Uh, Justin look into the TV set at the commentator's table.... (Trouble leaves the ring and looks at the monitor. He appears to be absolutely SHOCKED by what he sees.) Trouble: It's TRACI! What's she doing here? (Trouble looks toward her seat in the audience, but she has already gotten up to leave.) Hank Thompson: What the hell's going on here? Trouble: I don't know, but I'm going to find out. (Trouble jumps over the barricade, and quickly leaves the arena to try and find Traci. Hank Thompson still looks somewhat confused.) Hank Thompson: Well you saw it here first, folks! We'll have more news on the hotline about this late-breaking development! (fade to black) --------------- KW: What the hell was THAT about? CC: I'm not sure, but hopefully we'll find out soon. KW: I sure hope so. Why was Traci there in the first place? We haven't seen her for several months, so it seems rather strange. Could it possibly involve the ICU? CC: We'll have to wait and see. Next, lets get some comments from "Slim" Jim Coolridge.... ---------- (The camera opens on "Slim" Jim Coolridge, sitting alone in the locker room) Coolridge: Shane Matthews, I'm not wasting my time any longer. When I get my hands on you, I'll make sure you remember it. It's going to be VERY soon. You keep avoiding me and it will catch up with you, like it or not! THE TIME IS COMING, BOY! (fade to black) -------------- KW: Man, that guy sure is JERKY! CC: You really think you're funny, don't you? That was one of the stupidest puns you've EVER made, Ken. KW: Get it? Jerky? Slim Jim? CC: Yes, and I don't think you'd be telling that to his face. KW: Of course not! What kind of an idiot WOULD? I do my insulting behind someone's back, where no one can get hurt. CC: I've noticed. Next, Kevin Larkin has graced us with an interview with The West Coast Connection, who he helped win the Tag Team Titles. KW: You sound sarcastic, Carlson. CC: I am. Let's watch.... ------------- (We see Kevin Larkin in the ring, ready to conduct an interview. He scowls at the camera and begins to speak.) Kevin Larkin: Ladies an gentlemen, I want all of you to shut up, and let me interview my guests this week. (crowd boos loudly.) They are none other than the AEWA's current three-time tag team champions, THE WEST COAST CONNECTION! ("Tonight, Tonight" begins playing loudly. The West Coast Connection walk down to ringside, with the AEWA World tag team belts strapped around their waists. They stop in the middle of the aisle and seem puzzled as to why the fans are cheering for them. Nonetheless, they continue down to ringside, and into the ring.) Larkin: Welcome, gentlemen. Now, I understand that you two have some things to say. MC Read: Shut up and ask a question. Larkin: Is that any way to talk to the man responsible for you regaining the tag titles? Hmm? Anyway, my first question is-- what were you thinking when you beat up and fired your managers on my show a few months back? Axl Sharkman: Well, you see Larkin, it's simple. We kept losing our matches because they didn't know how to swing a chair right. They kept hitting us instead of our opponents. So, we showed them the RIGHT way to do it. Read: Exactly. Then after they were gone, we captured the tag team titles. But then, because of what BOB FREEMAN did to you, Kevin Larkin, we lost them. Bob Freeman is nothing but a dumbass who can't run the tag team division for his life. Look what happened while you were gone! Larkin: Exactly. The Graveyard Shift won the tag titles! Yikes, why did he EVER give them a title shot? But when I came back... Sharkman: The titles came back to where they belong-- with the West Coast Connection. And, to make sure that the belts stay where they belong, we have a surprise for the AEWA. We aren't going to unveil it just yet. Maybe at the end of the interview, if it's good. Larkin: Hmm.... This surprise wouldn't be a gift for me, would it?? Read: NO! Now ask another freaking question!! Larkin: Calm down. Okay, what would you say to your managers if they were here right now? Sharkman: What kind of question is that? We wouldn't say anything to them. In fact, I would punch both of them square in the nose! Read: I would have to say that we would let them have a taste of the Overdose, and then the West Coast Killa. Larkin: Well, my friends, you will get a chance to do just that if you wish. (yells to the locker room) Come out, boys! (DJ Muggs X and Chris Grohl are walking down the aisle. Before they get to the ring, MC and Axl run toward them, and attack them in the aisle. They drag them into the ring, and execute both of their finishing maneuvers on the two of them. Then, Axl and MC put a couple chairs in the ring, unfold them over the two fallen men, and sit down.) Read: My, that was fun. Hey, Axl, do you think we should let them know the surprise now? Sharkman: Yeah, I think Larkin has been a good little boy today. First of all, Larkin, we realize that when you are around, things are good for the WCC. Therefore, we are prepared to offer the Visigothic Horde a title shot. But, they must first defeat us in a non-title match. Larkin: Hmm... Maybe this IS a gift for me. Was that the surprise? Read: NO! The surprise is here in the arena right now. We want to make sure the tag titles stay where they belong, around out waists. So, to make sure this happens, we have enlisted the help of a bodyguard. He has been called the most psychotic wrestler in the FoPEW, where he is currently a member of the greatest wrestling stable in the world. Sharkman: He is the wrestler formerly known as the Supreme Wrestling Machine, here is HOOD LUM! ("Insane In The Membrane" by Cypress Hill blares throughout the arena, and the crowd erupts into a mixed reaction, depending on whether they have seen Hood Lum in action before. A huge man, around 6 foot 10, 350 pounds, walks down the aisle. He is wearing a black warm-up suit with "The Hood" on the back in dark purple, and wearing mirrored wraparound sunglasses.) Larkin: And what does this big dummy do? Hood Lum: (grabs the microphone) This "big dummy" is here to make sure that the WCC don't lose the tag team titles. It doesn't matter who the challenger is, I'll be there to keep control. I have wrestled the biggest guys in the FoPEW, so the wrestlers in this league are NOTHING to me. The West Coast Connection are going to remain champions for a long time! ("Tonight, Tonight" starts playing again. The West Coast Connection hold up the tag titles, and Hood Lum raises their hands in victory. Kevin Larkin drags Grohl and Muggs X out of the ring, and onto waiting stretchers.) ------------- KW: Wow, that Hood Lum guy is huge! CC: He CLAIMS to be the West Coast Connection's new bodyguard, but he's probably going to get involved during their matches. That's just not fair. KW: Oh well, either way, The West Coast Connection is a damn fine tag team. Hood Lum just ensures that they'll KEEP the belts. CC: See, that's exactly what I mean. Also, I sensed a little tension between the West Coast Connection and Kevin Larkin. Did you notice it also? KW: Ah, they were just a little excited. Those three are the best of friends. CC: And once again, we heard some rather disturbing comments about President Freeman. The West Coast Connection blamed ALL their past problems on him. Why is EVERYBODY doing that lately? KW: Who CARES? Jeez, it's human nature to make jokes about your boss. Man, sometimes you are SO paranoid! CC: Well, let's just move on. Up next, we have some words from The Assassins, Banshee and Shockwave. -------------- (Banshee and Shockwave are standing in front of the AEWA logo. Both are not wearing masks.) Banshee: It's been quite a long time since we last entered the ring, so now Shockwave and I are going to do something about it! Shockwave: A few weeks ago, the NATO Powers said that they had an open contract and would face any tag team in the AEWA! Well, it's time to put up or shut up, NATO Powers-- we want you! Banshee: This is the second rebirth of The Assassins! This time, we are back in full force, and we will dominate the AEWA tag team ranks! Shockwave: Machines! Our challenge was never met! I hope you two don't think you are out of the water yet! You cheated us out of a victory! Now, it seems, you two have your hands full with Graphic Violence, but don't forget us! We want those National tag belts of yours! Banshee: NATO Powers, do not think that we are looking past you! We know how skilled both of you are, but you will not thwart our rise to the top! (fade to black) --------------- CC: That was yet another tag team in the constantly heating up tag division here in the AEWA! KW: They're nothing but a bunch of pretty boys. Ever since they turned good, I've had NO interest in them. CC: Well, The NATO Powers have finally been challenged. Let's hope they respond. This could turn out to be a great match! KW: The Powers will win, hands down! CC: Next, we have comments from a newcomer who just beat Wild Thing on Saturday, "The Shotgun" Stan Crawford! ------------ ("The Shotgun" Stan Crawford is standing on the deck of a boat. In the background, the Statue of Liberty can be seen, along with the New York City skyline. "The Shotgun" is wearing a black leather jacket and a pair of jeans. His brown hair is pulled back into a ponytail, and he wears a pair of black sunglasses.) Stan Crawford: Hello, fans of the AEWA! Allow me to introduce myself. I am "The Shotgun" Stan Crawford! For five long years I have waited to enter the Mecca of wrestling, the AEWA! For those five years, I fought all over the United States, starting here, in the streets of New York City. From there, I went to some small time wrestling organizations, wrestling in run-down buildings in some of the most dangerous matches ever thought up! Barbed wire cage matches, weapons matches, first blood matches, and much, much more! Now, in the AEWA, I hope to put all of my experience in wrestling to use, rising to the top! But first, I want to talk about a few things! ("The Shotgun" pulls off his sunglasses.) Stan Crawford: First off, as soon as I enter the AEWA, I find it taken over by a gang of thugs, the ICU. Listen up, you pieces of human waste, I plan to wrestle in the AEWA for a long, long time! I REFUSE to let you wimps destroy the AEWA! I will take on all of you if I have to! Next, I want to issue a challenge to anyone here in the AEWA who wants to wrestle me! You know what you are getting into, as you saw what I did to Wild Thing! Watch out, however, you never know when "The Shotgun" will fire at you! (Camera fades to black) ---------------- KW: I really don't like that guy, Chuck. What's his freaking PROBLEM? He's been in the AEWA for less than a week, and he's ALREADY challenging the ICU? What a stupid FOOL. CC: He's a brave young man, and bravery is something that you wouldn't know ANYTHING about. Give the guy a break. KW: No thanks. CC: Well, I don't have time to argue with you. That's all the time we have for this week, fans! So for my broadcast partner, Ken Wilson, this is Chuck Carlson saying "see ya next week" for more Straight Talk!
