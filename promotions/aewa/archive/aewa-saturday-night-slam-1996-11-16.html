Saturday Night Slam (11/16)

19 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power Bomb, and a look at Mister Crowley DRIVING the head of some guy into the concrete with the Premature Burial! Then the camera switches to the Lovely Bertha and Matt Wilson in a packed arena) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Saturday Night Slam! We have a great program scheduled for tonight, and Deranged will be defending his TV title against "Amazon Monster" Augno Montone in the main event! LB: Those two guys are excellent competitors, so that should be a great match. We might be crowning a new TV champion tonight! MW: It's definitely a possibility. LB: But there's one match that REALLY interests me, Wilson. "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt is about to wrestle Penance for the Light Heavyweight title! I hope Penance kicks his ASS! MW: Don't count on it. We're ready to get things started here, so let's get down to ringside.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA Light Heavyweight championship! Introducing first, the challenger, weighing in at 223 pounds, he is a former Light Heavyweight champion, PENANCE! (Skid Row's "My Enemy" begins playing loudly over the PA, and Penance makes his way toward the ring to a BIG round of boos. He is dressed completely in black.) MW: Penance held the Light Heavyweight title several months ago, and he's hoping to regain it tonight! LB: He'd have NO shot if Big Daddy didn't cheat and beat the best Light-Heavyweight Champion ever, Rick Showboat. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 239 pounds, he is the AEWA Light Heavyweight champion of the World, here is "BIG DADDY" MIKE HURT! (Johnny Cash's "Ring Of Fire" plays loudly over the PA, and Mike Hurt makes his entrance to a nice pop from the crowd. He is proudly waving the belt around.) MW: Big Daddy looks ready to fight tonight! LB: Well, he'd better be! Penance looked pretty good in that triangle match last week. MW: There's the bell! Big Daddy takes control early. He whips Penance to the ropes, HIPTOSS ON PENANCE! Penance jumps right up! LB: He's not gonna beat him that way! MW: Penance whipped to the ropes again, he blocks a hiptoss by Big Daddy, and goes for one of his own! Big Daddy counters with a BIG clothesline! LB: Wow! Penance was turned inside out! MW: A scoop and a slam on Penance, and Big Daddy hits him with an Elbow Drop! That had a LOT of impact. The cover! 1....Kickout! MW: Just a one count! Big Daddy whips Penance to the ropes, and Penance comes back with a high cross body! Big Daddy just catches him and throws him right over his head, out of the ring! LB: What power by Big Daddy! MW: Big Daddy follows him out! He connects with a BIG uppercut on Penance, followed by a kick to the gut! Now Big Daddy nails him with a double underhook suplex! LB: He hit his back HARD on the concrete floor! MW: Big Daddy whips Penance into the guardrail! He follows that up with a BIG clothesline over the guardrail, into the front row! LB: The referee is being pretty lenient in this one! I guess it's because it is a title match! MW: Big Daddy is dragging Penance back into the ring. The referee stops his count. Big Daddy hits Penance with a reverse neck breaker! LB: Big Daddy's climbing up to the second turnbuckle! He jumps, AND HE MISSED WITH AN ELBOWDROP! That took a LOT out of him! MW: Penance should capitalize! "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt came down hard on that elbow! It could even be BROKEN! LB: Penance takes Big Daddy to the mat with an armdrag takedown. He gets back up, runs to the ropes, and comes down with a big legdrop on Big Daddy's arm! That was DEVASTATING! MW: Penance climbs the ropes! He connects with a flying splash on Big Daddy! The Cover! 1.....2...Kickout! MW: And now Penance throws Big Daddy out of the ring! LB: Why is he giving Big Daddy time to recover outside the ring?! That doesn't seem like a very intelligent move to me. MW: He's not! Penance NAILS him with a BIG flying jumpkick from the top rope to the floor! Big Daddy is out! That was INCREDIBLE! LB: Penance is taking it to Big Daddy! MW: He picks him up, and SLAMS him down with a snap suplex! Now he THROWS Big Daddy right OVER the ringsteps! LB: Penance had better get Big Daddy back into the ring if he wants the belt! MW: He must have heard you, Bertha, because Penance is dragging Big Daddy back in. Big Daddy gets whipped into the ropes, but he holds on! Penance misses with a dropkick! LB: He hit the mat HARD! MW: And Big Daddy connects with a kneedrop! Now he picks him up, HUGE backbreaker on Penance! The Cover! 1....2...Kickout! MW: Big Daddy continues to work on the back! He gets Penance into a Bearhug! LB: He's got it locked in tight! Will this make Penance submit?! MW: Penance rakes the eyes! He takes Big Daddy down with an armdrag takedown! HE PICKS HIM UP, AND DDT! LB: Big Daddy is in a lot of trouble! MW: Here's the cover! 1.....2...Kickout! MW: Penance scoops him up, and executes a power bomb! That took a LOT out of Big Daddy! Here's another cover! 1......2...Kickout! MW: Penance picks up Big Daddy-- BIG GERMAN SUPLEX! No bridge, all impact! LB: WOAH! He just dropped Big Daddy right on his head! That might have really injured him.... MW: It's over! Penance is signaling for his finisher! AND HE HITS BIG DADDY WITH THE "FINAL JUDGMENT" TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER! Big Daddy is out! The cover! 1.....2.....3! (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Your winner, at 11:11, and NEEEEW AEWA Light Heavyweight champion of the World, Penance! LB: WE HAVE A NEW CHAMPION! That was a great match, but Big Daddy just couldn't hold onto the title for long. MW: Penance didn't even really WANT the title, but he's our new champion. Unfortunately, that title now belongs to the ICU. LB: Yeah, that's the bad part. MW: Penance is walking back to the locker room now, and the other ICU members are joining him. Jeez, I didn't realize how MANY of them there were! LB: So what's next? MW: It's time for another edition of the Terrordome, folks. Johnny Terror is going to interview Bootknife Kelly this week. LB: Johnny and Bootknife? That's a DREAM TEAM! For once, the AEWA has decided to put some QUALITY stuff on the air! MW: Well, the music is beginning to play now, so let's just watch..... (Public Enemy's "Welcome to the Terrordome" plays loudly throughout the arena as the camera shifts to a familiar set near the back of the arena. Hanging over this set is a large half-dome. Underneath the dome, there is a very dark, dank room FILLED with various gruesome looking torture devices. AEWA manager Johnny Terror emerges from behind the curtain, and the boos rain down. Johnny seems honored by the crowd response and he begins to take several bows, as the booing only gets louder. Johnny then goes over to the set, and picks up a microphone.) Terror: You morons are SOOOOOOO predictable. Can't you recognize the managerial GENIUS you see before you? Why must you persist in booing me every single week? Is it because you're JEALOUS? Because you know that I'm a big success and you'll never amount to anything more then a lump on somebody's couch? In fact, don't even answer, because I already know it's true. So anyway, let's get this show on the road here. Right now, you have the EXTREME pleasure of watching the greatest show in the history of wrestling, The Terrordome! And I am, of course, your host, the one and only, Johnny Terror!!!! (LOUD BOOS) I told ya you're predictable. Anyway, my guest this week has recently returned to the AEWA after a long hiatus, he has managed such AEWA greats as The Punisher and Colonel Fear, and he is one of the most recognizable managers in the HISTORY of the AEWA.... BOOTKNIFE KELLY!!! (Aretha Franklin's "Respect" begins playing over the PA, and Bootknife Kelly emerges from the locker room. He is wearing a red tuxedo, with a blue rose sticking out of the pocket. As he enters the Terrordome, he stops, and spits on the ground in disgust. He then GRABS the microphone away from Johnny, and begins to speak.) Kelly: Johnny, I'm not in the mood for your bullshit tonight. I came here for a specific reason, so you need to shut the f*ck up, and let me do the talking. (looks around) First of all, this is the cheapest interview show in the history of professional wrestling. This is an EMBARRASSMENT to the entire sport, Johnny, and so are you. You know, when I left the AEWA a few months ago, things were a LOT different. (steps closer to Johnny) There weren't any skinny little BASTARDS running around, that's for damn sure. Do you know why? Because me and my boys wouldn't have ALLOWED it. Back then, we were the only law here in the AEWA. Now, I'm back-- and I don't like what I see. The AEWA has given too much power to guys like YOU, and it makes me sick. Johnny, you're the worst manager in the history of the AEWA. There's no comparison between you and me. So here's what I want-- introduce me again, the RIGHT way. Get down on your f*cking knees, and lick the sh*t off my boots. Terror: (grabs back the microphone) Thanks, but I already ate. Now let's get this straight. This is MY show, and I'M running things-- if you just wanna shoot your mouth off, go do your own damn interview. When you're on my show, you'll say what I LET you say. And regarding when your boys were running things around here, they don't call them the dark ages of the AEWA for nothing, pal. If I recall correctly, and I believe I do, the number of titles your stable amassed in that time was exactly ONE. Ohhhhhh, I bet those guys were REAL scared of you. And the Punisher, the guy who DID win that title, decided he'd be better off on his own and left! So how many title winners did that leave ya with, Bootlick? Now don't bother to get out your fingers and toes to count, because I'll tell ya right now how many that left you with, NONE! Shane Matthews has already won the Light Heavyweight title TWICE, and believe me, the Terror dynasty is only just beginning. Sorry pal, but you're old news. How does it feel to be a has-been, or more appropriately, a never-was? Kelly: Are you kidding me? I'm the most respected manager in AEWA history, buddy, and I make TEN TIMES more money than you. There's only one reason that Shane Matthews ever won ANYTHING-- you bribed the referees. If he had ever gotten into the ring with one of MY men, like Wild Thing, it would have been a different story. (steps closer to Johnny) I didn't come here to listen to your pathetic sarcasm, Terror. If you piss me off again, we're gonna have trouble. Terror: If you try and start anything, then believe me, you're the one who's gonna be sorry. And for the record, Shane Matthews could CRUSH any and all of your pathetic bunch with BOTH hands tied behind his back, but that's not what we're here today to discuss. So anyway, let's talk about your career. After failing so miserably in your last go in the AEWA, why are you back? You say that it's not for the money, so what's your story? Kelly: First of all, I did NOT fail during my last stay in the AEWA. In fact, I lead the Punisher to the TV title, and he held it longer than any other wrestler in history. I DON'T think that's a sign of failure, do you? Anyway, let me make this very clear-- I'm ALWAYS in it for the money. Terror: Can never have enough, huh? That's always a good philosophy to have. It's always been mine, well.... that, and hurting people real bad. So anyway, now that you ARE back, what are your plans? I know you're back with that nut case, Wild Thing, but what else do you have in mind? More wrestlers? Titles? Just plain beating people up? Or perhaps a combination of the three? Kelly: Well, I'm only in this business for one reason, and that's MONEY. I really don't care about anything else. Didn't I already explain that to you, Johnny? Anyway, since my wrestlers have always DOMINATED this little league, I plan on getting even MORE money in the near future. Terror: Sounds like a good plan. But what happens when all your wrestlers are pathetically embarrassed and make you into the laughing stock of the AEWA again? Do you just go back to your old job, at McDonald's? Or if you're interested, I've been looking for a chauffeur for quite some time now. I think you'd do nicely. It even pays five bucks an hour. That's probably more money than your morons ever made you in a whole year. So what do ya think? Kelly: That's it, Johnny, you went TOO far that time. (puts up his fists) Now I'm gonna do what your daddy should've done a long time ago-- beat the HELL out of you! Come on, little man, LET'S DO IT! (Kelly reaches out and just SLAPS Johnny across the face. The expression on Johnny's face turns to one of utter RAGE.) Terror: Of course you know... THIS MEANS WAR!! (Johnny DIVES forward and TACKLES Kelly! He climbs on top of Kelly and begins to POUND the HELL out of him.) MW: We've got total chaos out there! LB: I know, ain't it great? MW: Not really. Johnny Terror right now is just beating the CRAP out of Bootknife Kelly, and Kelly's barely even fighting back! Hell, he's barely MOVING! LB: But here comes some reinforcements! MW: Yes, Wild Thing is now RUSHING to the Terrordome area, and he just SHOVES Terror off of Kelly! And now Wild Thing is attacking Terror! He's STOMPING on him HARD! LB: Woah! Wild Thing almost kicked Johnny's head off of his body on that one! MW: But now here come Shane Matthews and the Italian Loan Collection Agency to JOHNNY'S rescue! And all three of them just AMBUSH Wild Thing from behind! LB: This is nuts! MW: You can say that again! Matthews is working over Wild Thing now, and the ILCA are picking up Kelly, and they're carrying him! Where are they going? LB: It looks like they're going deeper into the set! That's were all the torture devices are! MW: Yes! And now they're strapping Kelly into the torture rack! This is insane! They could break him in half with that thing! LB: Naaaaah. They'll just stretch him a little. He may even be a few inches taller before the night is over. MW: Meanwhile, Matthews just WHIPS Wild Thing into that Iron Maiden! And Ciraulo has turned on the rack! Kelly is SCREAMING!! And Matthews is bringing Wild Thing over near the rack as well! LB: I don't think Kelly can take much more of this! MW: That's for sure! Terror is up now, and he's just egging his men on! What a SICK man! Matthews hands Wild Thing over to Sabatini now, who picks him up, AND POWER BOMBS HIM ON TOP OF KELLY, RIGHT THROUGH THE RACK!!! LB: I wouldn't count on Kelly or Wild Thing getting up anytime soon. MW: They'll be lucky if they can get up TODAY. This is out of control! And now Terror has picked the microphone back up! Terror: (begins to talk while his men are STILL working over Kelly and Wild Thing) Okay, now let this be a lesson to all future guests-- this is what happens when you mess with Johnny Terror, so save yourself a lot of pain, and don't. Now I'm sure security'll be out here in a minute to get us, so we gotta get out of here, like now. Don't miss next week when my guest will be The Twilight Strangler! Come on, boys! (Johnny signals for his men, who follow as he leaves the set and heads for the locker room. They all have BIG smiles on their faces. The paramedics come out and take away Wild Thing and Bootknife Kelly. Our camera returns to our broadcast team.) LB: What the hell was that all about? Bootknife Kelly came onto that set with a BIG attitude, and it just went downhill from there. The Terrordome has been wild before, but that was out of control! Somebody could have been KILLED. MW: This whole situation seems rather odd, folks. I'm not sure what to think. LB: Well, one thing's for sure-- Bootknife Kelly and Johnny aren't very happy with each other right now. Now, I hate to change the subject, but we really need to move on. What's next, Wilson? MW: The Graveyard Shift are about to defend their World tag team titles against Heaven & Hell! I'm really looking forward to this one.... LB: This match was signed a LONG time ago, under very strange circumstances. If you remember, it all started at the Hall Of Fame award ceremony, when Mister Crowley and Cardinal Chaos attacked President Freeman. MW: Yeah, how could we forget? They BLACKMAILED him into signing the match, with one special rule-- Curate Kill would serve as the special referee! He shouldn't even be allowed NEAR the ring, but now he's going to be refereeing a match? That's insane! LB: Well, the match is finally going to happen, so just calm down and watch.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen, before the ring introductions, I would like to introduce the Special Referee of this match! In the Ring, Curate Kill! (Curate Kill raises his hand in acknowledgment. He is wearing a typical referee's uniform, striped shirt and black pants) MW: Think he'll be a fair ref? LB: Most likely, yes. MW: (sarcastically) Sure he will. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall, and is for the World tag team titles! Introducing first, at a combined weight of 731 pounds, THE GRAVEYARD SHIFT! (The Mortician and Grave Dweller walk toward the ring to a BIG pop from the crowd. Their manager, Coffin, follows closely behind.) MW: The Graveyard Shift look ready tonight! LB: But there is no way in hell that they can beat Heaven and Hell! MW: Time will tell. RING ANNOUNCER: Here are the challengers, weighing in at a combined weight of 641 pounds, here are Heaven and Hell! (Mister Crowley and Cardinal Chaos walk down the aisle to a chorus of boos. "Sympathy for the Devil" by the Rolling Stones plays over the PA system.) MW: The Graveyard Shift has the size advantage, but can they beat two of the World's best single wrestlers? LB: I doubt it. MW: The match has started! Grave Dweller and Cardinal Chaos start together in the ring. Grave Dweller takes control early, taking Chaos to the canvas with a single leg takedown. The Cardinal gets right up! LB: There is NO way Heaven and Hell can lose! MW: The Dweller takes Chaos to the corner with a series of swinging right hands! He climbs the ropes, punching Chaos. The crowd counts with his punches-- these people are hyped! But Chaos pushes Grave Dweller right off of him to the mat! LB: Chaos runs at him and hits him with a BIG clothesline! He nails him with a double underhook piledriver! The cover! 1......2...Kickout! MW: Cardinal Chaos is stomping on Grave Dweller mercilessly! He hits him with a Brain Buster, followed by an elbowdrop! Jeez, he's on a roll! LB: Cardinal Chaos is just destroying the Grave Dweller in there! This is absolutely PATHETIC! MW: The Cardinal comes off the ropes-- LEGDROP ON GRAVE DWELLER! LB: Chaos picks up Grave Dweller and hits him with a gutwrench suplex! MW: Cardinal Chaos tags out to Mister Crowley! Mister Crowley whips him to the ropes, and comes back with a jumping clothesline! LB: Mister Crowley is picking up where Cardinal Chaos left off. He sets him up for a piledriver. MW: Grave Dweller reverses the hold with a BIG backdrop! The Dweller dives to the corner-- AND TAGS THE MORTICIAN IN! LB: The Mortician is pounding on Crowley with punches and kicks! Now he connects with a German suplex! 1.....2...Kickout! MW: The Mortician hits Crowley with an atomic drop! Now he lifts him up, and SLAMS him down with a back suplex! LB: Crowley rolls out of the ring to get a well deserved breather. MW: There are no timeouts in wrestling! The Mortician follows Crowley out of the ring-- HE WHIPS CROWLEY TOWARD THE RING POST! Crowley reverses! LB: The Mortician just got his head smashed HARD on that ringpost! That's gonna leave a mark! MW: Crowley hits the Mortician with a right hand! AND HE JUST EXECUTED THE PREMATURE BURIAL ON THE CONCRETE! The Mortician's out like a light! LB: Crowley just tosses the Mortician right back into the ring! MW: Here's the cover! 1...2...Kickout! MW: That seemed like a pretty fast count from Curate Kill! Luckily, Mortician managed kick out just in time! LB: I don't know what you're talking about, Wilson! That looked good to me! MW: Crowley whips The Mortician into the ropes! The Mortician holds on to the ropes, and dives to tag Grave Dweller! LB: The Grave Dweller jumps right into the ring! He mows Crowley down with a clothesline! The Cover! 1..........Kickout! MW: What a SLOW count by Curate Kill! This is absolutely RIDICULOUS! LB: It looked just fine to me! MW: Grave Dweller thought it was slow too. He's cornering Curate Kill now, yelling at him. Crowley comes from behind, but Grave Dweller decks him! HE HITS CROWLEY WITH A BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! 1.............Kickout! MW: ANOTHER slow count! Grave Dweller has had it! HE PUNCHES CURATE KILL IN THE FACE! Now he's all over him! Curate Kill signals for the bell! (ding ding) LB: And Cardinal Chaos attacks the Grave Dweller from behind. He knocks him to the mat, and NAILS him with a kick to the head! HE'S BEATING HIM SENSELESS, AND CURATE KILL IS JOINING IN! MW: Now Mister Crowley is getting involved-- But Crowley is cut off by the Mortician, who clotheslines him! They both topple out of the ring! LB: And back in the ring, Cardinal Chaos just hit Grave Dweller with the Inverted Crucifix! That was AWESOME! MW: The Mortician is smashing Mister Crowley with a chair down at ringside. We need some control out there! Here comes a group of security guards to break up this wild brawl! RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen, here are your winners, as a result of a disqualification, Heaven and Hell! LB: Jeez, that was CRAZY! The Grave Dweller has a real problem with referees, doesn't he? Remember what he did a few weeks ago? MW: Of course, but this was a special circumstance. Curate Kill was a HORRIBLE official, and he DESERVED to get punched. LB: I'd like to punch YOU right now, Wilson. MW: Well, don't get any ideas. Anyway, they have all been escorted out of the building by security, and it's time for our next match. Deranged is going to defend his Television title against "Amazon Monster" Augno Montone! LB: This should be a great match. I'd love to see Montone kick Deranged's butt, and take home the TV title! Wouldn't that be great? MW: Ah, not exactly. Let's just watch.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall, and is for the AEWA Television title! Introducing First, the challenger, weighing in at 450 pounds, here is "The Amazon Monster" Augno Montone! ("Welcome to the Jungle" by Guns n' Roses plays as Augno Montone walks down to the ring. He wears black tights and has an unrecognizable animal mask on his head.) MW: This is the man formerly known as Red Wolf. We haven't seen him in a while, so this should be interesting. LB: Let's just get it started! RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, he weighs in at 260 pounds and is accompanied by his manager, Angel, he is the AEWA Television Champion, Deeeeeeeeeeerrrrrrranged! (The theme from "Halloween" plays loudly over the Public Address system, as Deranged makes his way toward the ring to a small crowd pop. Angel is at his side.) MW: Deranged is looking good tonight! LB: Yeah, but Augno Montone is gonna destroy him! MW: Okay, here they go.... Deranged uses his speed to his advantage, and hits "The Amazon Monster" with a big dropkick! LB: But that didn't even hurt Montone! MW: Deranged comes off the ropes, and connects with a running clothesline! MONTONE DOESN'T EVEN MOVE! LB: I told you, Montone is the bigger man! He is gonna destroy Deranged tonight, once and for all. MW: Deranged comes off the ropes again, and he goes for a flying cross body! But Montone catches Deranged! LB: He's CARRYING Deranged around the ring! MW: Now Montone takes him down with a big bodyslam! Montone gets back up, and drops a BIG elbow on Deranged. The Cover! 1.....2...Kickout! MW: Montone hits Deranged with a Vertical Suplex! He picks him up, and hits him with a big powerslam! That had a LOT of power! The Cover! 1......2....Kickout! MW: Deranged will not give up! Montone gets him into a BIG bearhug! LB: He's squeezing the life outta Deranged. MW: But Deranged hits Montone with an Earringer! Montone drops him, and staggers back! LB: Deranged hits Montone with a big dropkick! MW: He comes back with a big clothesline! Montone is staggering! Deranged hits him with another clothesline! MONTONE GOES DOWN! LB: No! How the hell did that happen? MW: It's called gravity, Bertha. Deranged is climbing the ropes, he jumps, AND HE CONNECTS WITH A FLYING LEGDROP! The cover! 1....2...Kickout! MW: Montone just threw Deranged off of him! Deranged looks shocked, but he gets to his feet, runs to the ropes-- AND NAILS MONTONE WITH A SUPERKICK! Montone goes down again! Deranged pulls him up, leaps, and GETS HIM INTO A FRANKENSTEINER! How did he do that? Montone is such a big guy! LB: That was pure luck! But don't worry, Montone is still in control! MW: Deranged comes off the ropes, goes for an elbowdrop, BUT MONTONE MOVES OUT OF THE WAY! LB: Here we go! I smell a new champion! MW: Montone whips Deranged into the corner, and runs in after with a BIG splash! Deranged is staggering! Montone hits him with a BIG clothesline! The Cover! 1.....2...Kickout! MW: Deranged still wants his title! He's fighting to his very last breath! LB: He may have it in this match! RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, there are FIVE minutes remaining in this match. MW: Montone heard that announcement, but he still seems to be taking his time in there. He hits Deranged with a piledriver! Montone isn't even attempting to cover him-- in fact, he's yelling at the fans! LB: The fans are booing Montone heavily! He doesn't need them anyway! MW: Montone picks Deranged clear over his head! He drops him down hard! LB: Deranged is out! There is no way he can recover! MW: Montone is wasting WAY too much time! This match has a fifteen minute time limit, and we have less than five minutes left! LB: Don't worry, Wilson! He'll get the job done! MW: Montone whips Deranged to the ropes-- BIG BOOT ON DERANGED! Montone goes for the pin! 1.....2....Kickout! MW: WOW! How did Deranged find the power to kick out of that one? LB: Luck, Wilson, sheer luck! MW: Deranged is staggering against the ropes, and Montone comes at him with a clothesline! Deranged moves out of the way! LB: And Montone flies outside of the ring! He's in trouble now! MW: Montone is laid out on the floor-- he landed right on his head and back! This is NOT a good place for him to be. LB: Don't forget about Deranged; he's still laying there in the ring! MW: Both Montone and Deranged reach their feet at the same time. Montone enters the ring, and Deranged meets him with a BIG right hand! LB: Montone fires right back with a right of his own! MW: The two are brawling in the center of the ring! Montone whips Deranged into the corner! He follows him in and hits him with a clothesline! RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, there is only ONE minute remaining in this match.... LB: Montone lifts Deranged up, AND EXECUTES A POWER BOMB! That should be the end of the match right there! MW: The cover! 1.....2...Kickout! MW: Deranged just won't give up! Montone throws him into the ropes, but Deranged comes back with a foot the midsection. He hits him with a savate kick, and Montone goes crashing down! LB: Deranged is climbing the ropes.... MW: He jumps, AND HE CRASHES DOWN ON MONTONE WITH A FLYING ELBOWDROP! That was INCREDIBLE! (Ding, Ding) MW: Oh! The time limit has expired! I wasn't even paying attention to the clock. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen, the referee has declared this match a 15-minute time limit draw! LB: Deranged retains the Television title, but Montone impressed a LOT of people tonight. He could have won it. MW: Montone looked VERY good tonight, and the Championship Committee can't ignore him anymore. That guy has a LOT of potential. LB: For once, we both agree. MW: Well, they'll be having a rematch soon, but THIS show is over. Folks, be sure to tune in to Wednesday Night Rumble-- in the main event, we're finally going to have that championship battle royal! LB: I'll see you there! (fade to black) \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- American E-Wrestling Association The AEWA-- We Deliver!
