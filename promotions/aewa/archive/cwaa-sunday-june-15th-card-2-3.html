Sunday, June 15th, Card (2/3)

16 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(....would you like to make more money? Sure, we all would...) EL: OK- let's go to David Myers for the introduction to this one. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next bout is set for one fall with a ten-minute time limit. Introducing first, in the corner to my left, weighing in at 254 lb., he is one-half of the CWAA Tag Team champions, "Marvelous" Michael Anderson! (Boos) DM: And his opponent- (Kenny Loggins' "Danger Zone" plays and the crowd pops) DM: -from Edmonton, Alberta, weighing 215 lb., Jimmy Dangerous! (Jimmy heads to the ring exuberantly. The 5'9" brown-haired youngster wears a white tank style top with the words "About to enter the Danger Zone" across the front in blue lettering. He wears regular white tights with "JD is OK" down the side in blue.) DM: Your referee, Chris Herman. EL: Michael Anderson still hasn't taken off his tuxedo, yet, and- well, I see you;re back. BM: That's right, because there's one thing almost as lovely as my face, and that's my voice, and why should I deprive all the short-sighted and visually-impaired people of my beauty when I can come out here and let them bask in my voice. EL: There's the bell, and right away Anderson to work with a double axe-handle. Why is he still in that tuxedo? BM: Well, Mikey's got a lot of class- (Anderson with another double sledge) BM: -and he'll make such short work of this kid that there ain't no point in going through all the effort- (Anderson with an Irish whip and an elbow jab) BM: -off getting changed. There you go, Mikey. That's what we like to see. (claps) EL: Anderson choking Dangerous on the bottom rope now. Chris Herman with the count, and he breaks the hold. BM: I think the kid submitted. EL: He did not! BM: Stop the match, ref! He's killing him! EL: Anderson sends him for the ride- belly to- no he just dropped him neck-first on that top rope- a Hotshot- 1... 2... kickout! BM: Thought I heard the fat lady sing, but I guess it was just you. EL: Anderson with a fireman's carry takedown, into an arm bar. BM: Submission hold, there. Hey, ref! Ask him! Ask him, ref! EL: Don't tell Chris Herman how to do his job. He doesn't tell you how to do yours. BM: Ain't no one who could tell me how to my job. Me and Mikey, we are so skilled, so graceful, so lethal, we can't even find sparring partners, so you know what we have to do to ensure sufficient competition in our comprehensive training regimen? EL: Spar with each other? BM: What kind of question is that? Of course, not. We're a team, man. We don't wrestle one another- although it would be the greatest scientific battle you'll ever see. Now- we have to do our workout in front of mirrors, so we can look across and see an opponent worthy of ourselves. EL: This comes as no surprise. BM: You know, I've got like a three-game winning streak against myself. Not too shabby, not too shabby. Unlike this kid. He's been in this arm bar for like 45 minutes. Ref, ring the bell. This kid's arm's gonna fall off, and we don't wanna get sued. I can't believe this place. At least that Sheridan guy came close once or twice to nearly attempting a move. I think I slipped one, the ring's here are in such a state, and I think that Sheridan tried to pass it off as something he did, but, let's face it, he's no idiot, though he acts like one, and he knows her can't touch me, so he must've been more surprised than anyone when I fell. I doubt anyone here really believed that the punk did anything to me, anyway. Who did he think he was kidding? EL: Dangerous back on his feet, still caught in that arm bar. Now he does a backflip- lands on his feet- and reversed the arm bar! (Pop for a reversed arm bar. Welcome to the Sportatorium) BM: Is he allowed to do that kind of nonsense? EL: Arm drag takedown. BM: Does he know he's in there with the tag team champion? How dare he throw Mikey to the mat like that! Some manners! EL: And Anderson with a low blow! BM: Perfectly legal forearm uppercut. EL: Illegal low blow! Now he punches Jimmy Dangerous- a right uppercut, into the chin! Scoops him up- slam. Off the ropes- drops the elbow- 1... kickout. Sends him for the ride- and a right cross to the face! Chris Herman warning him about the closed fist. I can't believe Anderson is still in that tuxedo. Sends him for the ride- knee to the midsection. Kicks him right in the ribs- this is not the style of match in which a Jimmy Dangerous excels. BM: Yeah- a professional match with a skilled heavyweight. He ought to go back to his wimpweight class or something. Finish him, Mikey. EL: Into the rope- back body dr- no! He ducked too soon- Jimmy Dangerous with a kneelift! (Pop) EL: Now he grabs Anderson by the sleeves- pules them down around his arms- Anderson's arms are stuck to his sides! Anderson defenseless like a turtle on hind legs as Dangerous kicks him in the midsection. Belly to belly! 1... 2... kickout! BM: See that- he kicks out with no hands. But the ref ought to be doing something about this- I'll go see what I can do. (Exit BM) EL: Anderson, his arms fastened to his side with those sleeves pulled down off his shoulder- his own fault for wearing that ridiculous tuxedo in the first place. Dangerous sends him for the ride- off the ropes- wow! (Pop) EL: Canadien Cannonball kick! (Springboard spinwheel kick for those of you playing at home) EL: Anderson was completely defenseless! 1... 2... 3! He got it- but the Pretty Boy with an elbow drop as Chris Herman counted three! The Pretty Boy now sends him for the ride- clothesline! Pretty Boy Byron McNultey in there too late to save his partner, going to work on young Jimmy Dangerous- (Pop) EL: And here comes Travis Conley, Paul Langford! Twister! Randy Sheradin and Joshua Barnes close behind! Byron McNultey heading for hills! (Anderson, still with his sleeves down around his elbows, waddles after him as 'Danger Zone" plays) DM: Your winner, Jimmy Dangerous! (Twister and Sheradin help Dangerous up and he raises one hand in recognition of the crowd support while Joshua Barnes watches for any returns from the aggressors.) EL: A big win for Jimmy Dangerous over Michael Anderson- and he looks to be OK now. We have more great action coming your way, so don't touch that dial! (Monday nights at 11:00 here on WYXZ-TV Ch. 39, it's "Three's Company" and "The Ropers" back-to-back. Join Jack, Janet, Chrissy and Mr. Furley in all their zany hijinks. This week, Mr. Furley thinks Jack and Chrissy are having an affair! Then it's Stanley and Helen, everyone's favorite married EL: OK- a bad day for the tag champs, as Randy Sheradin and Jimmy Dangerous each score a win over them in solo action. And after what we saw earlier, I'm hearing that we may see the Dreamer and Joshua Barnes fighting for the CWAA Tag Team titles next week. This week, we have Max Robertson defending the CWAA Heavyweight Title against the mysterious Masked Man. First, let's hear from Mark Sherrick. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next bout is set for one fall with a 20-minute time limit. Introducing first, from New York, New York, weighing in at 275. lb., here is "The Preacher" Kore Wrench! (The Preacher's entrance is accompanied by Gregorian Chant. The 6'4"-275 Preacher wears his golden episcopal robe and carries a crosier. The long-haired powerhouse also performs his standard child-blessing rituals) DM: And his opponent- ( "Beautiful People" by Marilyn Manson starts up, and Dallas, Texas' favorite son, Mark Sherrick comes walking down the aisle, in his new outfit of black and purple tights, with the MS insignia on the back, which is unseen, due to the sleeveless trenchcoat he is wearing. His upraised right hand is covered by a black glove. He starts jawing with a fan, and after a minute, spits in his face, and reaches the ring, grabbing the microphone from David Myers) Mark Sherrick: Its about time I didn't have to challenge anybody to get myself a match! I don't care who, when, or how, just like I said last week, you will all get your <expletive deleted> butts kicked!! Kore Wrench, its a good thing you are a "preacher" because you are gonna need God to get your <expletive deleted> out of the mess you just got into by stepping in the ring with me. Right now, I am issuing another open challenge, for next week. Anybody, any type of match, any time in the show. Now, I just have to beat the crap outta this so-called CWAA superstar that is stepping in the ring with me this week. I will destroy him, and all others by any means necessary! (Mark throws down the microphone and graphically motions to the crowd, and his hands are blurred out, do to the gesture he is giving, and his mouth is moving, but no sound is heard. He takes off his coat and waits for Kore Wrench, still playing to the fans, drawing major boos.) EL: Look out! The Preacher won't take any more of this! (The Preacher slides in under the bottom rope and unloads a series of punches on Sherrick) EL: Kore Wrench a house of fire! (Pop) EL: Kore Wrench all over Mark Sherrick- slams him into the corner! (Not literally- just a real hard Irish whip) EL: The Preacher with an elbow smash and Sherrick eating his words early on, and the great fans here in the world-famous Sportatorium enjoying every minute of it. The Preacher now with a standing wristlock. Two of the most powerful, two of the biggest men, in the whole state of Texas in that ring right now. Same height, same weight. The Preacher, turning that wrist over, may have the power advantage, but when it gets down and dirty, I think Sherrick may be the better fighter, Right now, though, he's put up no offense, no counters. The Preacher now with a devastating clothesline! This one could be over- 1... 2... no. Kickout by Sherrick. Kore Wrench, the Preacher, now to a front facelock, using that awesome power- the Major hates when I say that. Speaking of the Major, his man has a big rematch with Jak Hammer later this evening- you wont' want to miss that. Of course, our special feature match- the Preacher applying the pressure now- is the one everyone here tonight has been buzzing about. CWAA Heavyweight Champion, the Professional, Max Robertson, in the ring with the mysterious Masked Man. We hope to get a word with this mystery man this evening before the contest. The Preacher sends Sherrick for the ride- back body drop. (When was the last time an e-wrestler successfully executed one of those...?) EL: Kore Wrench with an Irish whip- clothesline- got him. Wrench backs him up into the corner- whip across the ring- Sherrick into the corner. The Preacher follows him- ooh! (Crowd groans) EL: Mark Sherrick gets the boot up in time, and the preacher ran right into it. Now Sherrick at work- pounding him into the canvas with those double sledges. Now he grabs the Praetor by the head and rubs his face in the canvas! Mark Sherrick showing his contempt- an insulting maneuver. Now he walks across the Preacher's back! This man has just no respect for his opponent, the officials, the fans- anyone! (Crowd boos) EL: Sets him up- snap suplex by Mark Sherrick. The cover- 1... 2... kickout. Still some fight in the Preacher. Now Sherrick steps over the shoulder into an arm bar. The Preacher seated on the mat, Sherrick with that arm bar applied. How about the new CWAA Tag Team Champions? That brutal steel cage match which we showed you the ending to this evening, the use of those brass knuckles- Sherrick telling Senior Official Jeff Howard to check for a submission- only he isn't quite so polite about it- those brass knuckles to score the pin, and right away they get into a situation with Randy Sheradin and Joshua Barnes- we may be looking at a tag team title match between those teams, while the former champions, Twister, wait to see who survives. A lot of big matches coming up in the coming weeks, as Sheradin and Barnes meet Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas coming up at AEWA Battle Klash II. Speaking of Battle Klash II- the Preacher back to his feet- and Sherrick using a handful of hair to pull him into the corner- Jeff Howard calls for the break- (Do you think he'll get it?) EL: -and Sherrick with a shoulderblock into the midsection- just operating like a battering ram. Like I was saying, Lt. Lawson- should he still be the CWAA TV Champion- has that big chance at Battle Klash to show off his skills on a National- even International- telecast as he does battle with the hot English up-and-coming Light Heavyweight, Fuse. Fuse brings an impressive resume to the ring, should be interesting to see how that one goes. It could be the most exciting match on the program that night. Mark Sherrick hip tosses his opponent out to mid-ring. Diving elbow smash to the top of the head. Covers- 1... 2... kickout. Irish whip- reversed- clothesline- Sherrick ducks- turns- wow! Both men, Sherrick and the Preacher just clotheslined each other! Both men down, and it looked like Wrench hit him harder- caught him right under the chin- let's see what happens- Senior Official Jeff Howard starts a ten-count- both men still down. What a collision- like two trucks in a head-on crash. Sherrick up on his hands and knees- the Preacher pulling himself up by using the ropes. Both men up, but woozy- Sherrick staggers over- and the Preacher greets him with a kick in the midsection! (Pop) EL: The Preacher still leaning on the ropes- Sherrick up again- and the Preacher with another of those kicks! The cover- 1... 2... kickout! The Preacher picks him back up- body slam. Measures him up- off the ropes- knee drop, right across the forehead. Now he's signaling to the crowd- powerbomb coming up- hooks him- look out! Sherrick grabs the ankles- rolls him back- he's got a handful of tights- 1... 2...3! Jeff Howard never saw it! Mark Sherrick using the tights for leverage- Kore Wrench protesting to the referee- let's get the official word. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, Mark Sherrick! ("Beautiful People" resumes playing) EL: Mark Sherrick gets the win- and a big win it is. And- look out! (Boos) EL: The Preacher was arguing with Jeff Howard, and Sherrick just blasted him from behind! Mark Sherrick stomping away now- Jeff Howard trying to get in between. Mark Sherrick grabs the Preacher- MS: It's time to take out the trash! EL: - and he just threw the Preacher out to the floor! Chris Herman and security out there to prevent any further extracurricular activity- Mark Sherrick gets the win- by underhanded means- over the Preacher, then blindsides him and tosses him to the concrete floor. He looks real proud of himself, and there he goes to the lockers. The Preacher just getting back to his feet- took a nasty spill- that floor is all concrete. We're going to take a break now, and when we get back, we'll talk to the Masked Man and have some TV Title action. Don't go away!
