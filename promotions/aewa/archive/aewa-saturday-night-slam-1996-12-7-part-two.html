Saturday Night Slam (12/7, part two)

8 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
************************************************************************ CONTINUED FROM PART ONE ************************************************************************ MW: Well, we've got quite a big one next. It's a triangle match-- The Masked Yorel versus Heavy Metal versus Big Ron Raider, for Raider's North American Title! BD: I've REALLY been looking forward to this one! I can't decide who I want to win, they're all great! MW: Well, someone's gotta win, and we'll find out who it is tonight! All of them are big men, and it's going to be a great big battle of the bulk in there tonight! LB: Quit talking about this match and let's get it started, Wilson! MW: Well let's go ahead, you heard the lady. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is an elimination style triangle match, and is for the AEWA North American Title! Introducing first, from Parts Unknown, and weighing in at 370 pounds, The Masked Yoooooooooorrrrrrreeeeel! (Smashing Pumpkins' "Disarm" begins to play over the PA, as the big masked man makes his way toward the ring to a round of boos.) BD: I STILL can't get over how familiar this guy looks! MW: Me neither; I KNOW I've seen him before, but I just can't place him. LB: Whoever he is, he is one BAD dude! MW: That's for sure, and he's gonna need to be, because he's stepping in there with two of the BADDEST tonight. Heavy Metal and Big Ron Raider. RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing next, weighing in at 450 pounds, he is a former AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, Heaaaaaaaaavy Meeeeeetaaaaaaaaaaaallll! (Metallica's "Battery" begins to play loudly over the PA as the MASTADON makes his way to the ring to a HUGE round of boos.) BD: He's the biggest man in the AEWA, and quite possibly, the world! That's 7'4", 450 pounds of Heavy Metal. And he's NOT the kinda guy you wanna run across in this kinda match! MW: That's for sure. He's not the kind of guy you wanna run across in ANY kind of match if you can help it. He's big AND skilled; a VERY rare combo! LB: That's why he was World Champ for so long, and that's why I think he's walking outta here with the North American belt tonight! MW: That's a distinct possibility. RING ANNOUNCER: And finally, the champion, from The Rocky Mountains, he weighs in at 416 pounds and is accompanied by his manager, Million Dollar Mark, he is the AEWA North American Champion, Big... Ron... Raaaaaaaaiiiiiiiideeeeeeer! (Lynyrd Skynyrd's "Saturday Night Special" cranks out over the public address system, and Raider also makes his entrance to a HUGE round of boos. He wears the belt over his shoulder, and Million Dollar Mark is at his side.) BD: And now here comes the champion. Raider has been a dominant champion in his own right thus far in his defenses, but this is most definitely his toughest challenge so far. MW: Yes it is, and Raider's not gonna have an easy time in this one, win or lose. LB: He's in the ring now, and it's obvious that these guys don't like each other! MW: It sure is, and now Heavy Metal is stepping to the outside, and it appears as if Raider and The Masked Yorel are gonna start this one off. The two meet in the center of the ring, and we've got a lockup! Yorel gets the upper hand, and whips Raider to the ropes! Raider comes back, and gets LEVELED with a clothesline! BD: And Yorel's not afraid to go toe-to-toe with the AEWA's best, so he's DEFINITELY someone who's got a lot of experience! MW: It sure looks like it. And now Yorel's trying to go for the Power Bomb already! LB: There's NO WAY he can get Raider up! MW: He lifts, AND HE GOT HIM UP, BUT RAIDER'S MOMENTUM CARRIES HIM OVER, AND HE COUNTERS WITH A BACKSLIDE! One..Two..Kickout! BD: That's why Raider's the champ. He's always thinkin'! MW: Yes he is, but he might be in trouble now, as Yorel just made the tag to Heavy Metal! Heavy Metal charges in, but Raider catches him with an elbowsmash! Raider NAILS him with a roundhouse right, and ANOTHER elbowsmash! LB: He's really taking it to Heavy Metal! MW: And that's what he's gotta do. Raider grabs Heavy Metal now, and LEVELS him with a short lariat! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: If Heavy Metal manages to get up, he's gonna be REALLY upset! MW: That's an understatement. Raider lifts Heavy Metal to his feet, and whips him to the turnbuckle! But Heavy Metal comes RUNNING right back out of the corner, and ROCKS Raider with an elbow! LB: And now it's Raider who's gonna be in trouble! MW: Heavy Metal picks the champion up now, and NAILS him with a powerslam! The cover! One..Shoulder Up! BD: Raider's one of the toughest guys on this planet! He ain't done yet! MW: No, but he IS hurt. And now Heavy Metal gets up and tags out to The Masked Yorel! And Yorel comes in just as Raider is getting to his feet, and he catches him with a belly-to-belly suplex! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: He's got some power if he can do that to Raider! MW: And he's gonna do it again! He lifts Raider up, and NAILS him with ANOTHER belly-to-belly suplex! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: And Raider is in deep doo-doo! MW: It appears that way. And now Yorel is setting Raider up, and he DROPS a knee--BUT RAIDER ROLLS AWAY, AND YOREL HITS HARD! Raider gets up, and NAILS the Yorel with an elbowsmash! Raider WHIPS him to the buckle, and CRUSHES him in with an avalanche! LB: BOY! Raider sure took advantage of that fast! MW: Yorel staggers out of the corner, and Raider NAILS him with a swinging punch! That stunned the Yorel, and now Raider LEVELS him with a lariat! The Yorel is down, and Raider NAILS him with a falling splash! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! BD: The Yorel's gotta be tough to get outta that! MW: He sure does, and now Raider's looking to finish the job! He lifts Yorel up, JACK-KNIFE POWER BOMB!! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: And AGAIN he kicks out! MW: But Raider isn't willing to accept that! He CRUSHES Yorel with another falling splash, and another cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Raider's really killing the masked man, but he won't quit! You gotta respect that! MW: Yes you do, although Raider's not giving it too much respect! And he goes right back to work! Raider picks him up now, and NAILS him with a bodyslam! And now Raider places the Yorel on the top rope, and NAILS him with a superplex! LB: I don't care HOW tough the Yorel is, even HE can't stand this much longer! MW: And now Raider nails the Yorel with a swinging punch! Raider picks him up now, and hits ANOTHER Jack-Knife Power Bomb! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: And AGAIN he kicks out! I think Raider is fed up with this Yorel! MW: Yes, he is, and he's actually LETTING him make the tag to Heavy Metal! Heavy Metal comes RUNNING in, and gets caught in a belly-to-belly suplex by Raider! The cover! One..Kickout! LB: That's not NEARLY enough to pin Heavy Metal. MW: No, it's not, that's why he's already on his feet! Heavy Metal NAILS Raider with a faceslam, and now NAILS him with a chop! BD: But wait, someone's coming to ringside-- IT'S SCORPION! MW: Yes, Scorpion cost Heavy Metal the World title in that battle royal, and Heavy Metal's gonna be none too happy when he sees him! Heavy Metal picks Raider up now, and NAILS him with a Samoan Drop! Heavy Metal brings Raider to his feet, and SLAPS him, and now he picks Raider up by the neck, CHOKESLAM!! WHAT IMPACT!!!! LB: And Heavy Metal just looked up after that chokeslam, and he saw Scorpion standing in the aisle! MW: You're right! Heavy Metal is FUMING, but he knows he's gotta concentrate on the match at hand. But now Scorpion is mocking him! He's making belt motions around his waist, AND HEAVY METAL JUST LOST IT! He's CHARGING down the aisle after Scorpion, who is retreating to the locker room! Heavy Metal show's no signs of turning back, and he just went into the locker room after him! BD: And the referee has just counted Heavy Metal out! MW: You're right! RING ANNOUNCER: Heavy Metal has been COUNTED OUT!!! LB: Well that changes things just a bit! MW: Yes it does! Scorpion showed up at ringside, and so INFURIATED Heavy Metal that he chased after him, and got himself counted out! That's the SECOND title that Scorpion has cost Heavy Metal now! BD: And I'd hate to be Scorpion when Heavy Metal gets his hands on him! MW: Ditto. But now we're down to two, The Masked Yorel and Big Ron Raider for the North American Title! Yorel charges in, and NAILS Raider with a facerake! And now Yorel NAILS him with a forearm smash! LB: The Yorel wants that title so bad he can taste it! MW: And he just might get it! Raider slowly gets up, and Yorel LEVELS him with a shoulderblock! The Yorel picks him up now, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One..Kickout! BD: And Raider's not ready to go out yet! MW: No he's not, but the Yorel's looking to finish him! The Yorel DROPS a knee onto Raider, and now he picks him up, and NAILS him with a bulldog headlock! Raider is down, and the Yorel is going up top! LB: This is unfamiliar territory for him, as far as I know. I hope he knows what he'd doing! MW: The Yorel has reached the top rope, but Raider reaches up, AND SHAKES THE ROPE! And the Yorel comes CRASHING down, groin first, on the top turnbuckle! BD: That one probably hurt a little bit! MW: I would say so! And now Raider looking to take advantage! He goes up top with him, AND NAILS THE YOREL WITH A BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPERPLEX!! The cover! One..Two..Three!!! (DING, DING) LB: Raider caught the Yorel in a mistake, and it only takes one if it's at the right time. Raider retains the title! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of thirteen minutes and one second, here is your winner, and STILL AEWA North American Champion... BIG RON RAIDER!!!! MW: Well, that certainly was interesting. Raider retains the title in an impressive victory, but still, Heavy Metal was cost the shot at a title AGAIN by Scorpion. These two are gonna have to collide sometime soon, and it ain't gonna be pretty! BD: No it ain't and I can't wait to see that one! Speed and agility vs. size and power; those are the kinda matches that make this sport great! There's no way to tell who would win! MW: Yes, another case of the irresistible force vs. the immovable object-- somebody's gotta give! LB: Well that's all well and good, but what's coming up next? MW: Yet another title match, as Randall Osbourne defends his TV Title against Rick Showboat! BD: I don't usually like Osbourne, but I hope he kicks Showboat's ass! LB: Don't say that about Ricky! It's only a matter of time before he achieves just as much success in the Heavyweight division as he has in the Light Heavyweight Division! MW: Well he's certainly a good enough wrestler, but can he handle the huge weight difference? LB: Shane Matthews did, so why not Ricky? BD: No way! Showboat couldn't even beat ME! MW: It appears we have some conflicting opinions here, folks. I guess the only way to settle it is to go to the ring! RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA Television Championship! Introducing first, the challenger, >From Palmdale, California, he weighs in at 226 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his manager, Doctor Love, he is a two-time former AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion of the World, Rick Shoooooooowboooooooaaaaaat! (The theme from "The Love Boat" plays loudly over the PA as Showboat makes his way toward the ring, to a HUGE round of boos from the crowd. He wears an black tuxedo, with a red rose sticking out of the pocket. Doctor Love is at his side, wearing a yellow suit.) LB: And Ricky looks SO ready tonight, not to mention cute. There's NO WAY he's gonna lose this one. MW: Well he's got a stiff challenge ahead of him in Randall Osbourne, so it's not going to be THAT easy. BD: For Osbourne it is. MW: Knock it off! Both of you. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 362 pounds, he is the current AEWA Television Champion, Raaaaaaaaandall "The Rebel of Rock" Ooooossssbooooooooooourne! (AC/DC's "It's a Long Way to The Top" plays loudly over the PA as Osbourne makes his entrance to a BIG pop from the crowd. He slowly drags the belt behind him, and seems to be almost ASHAMED of it.) LB: Osbourne wouldn't even BE champion if it wasn't for the Angels of Death, and Ricky's gonna show everybody WHY he doesn't deserve to be champion tonight! MW: He may have won the title under some bad circumstances, but that doesn't mean he's not worthy to wear it. He's also a former North American Champion, you know. BD: Yes, and that's why he's gonna mop the floor with Showboat tonight! LB: IS NOT! BD: IS SO! LB: IS NOT! MW: KNOCK IT OFF!! There is a MATCH about to happen, you know. And it looks like we're just about ready to get underway! Both men meet in the center of the ring, and we've got a lockup! Showboat loosens up, and pulls Osbourne in with a short clothesline! BD: Lucky move. MW: And now Osbourne gets back up to his feet, Showboat is working him over with a series of punches, EUROPEAN UPPERCUT--BUT OSBOURNE CATCHES HIS ARM, AND COUNTERS WITH A BACKSLIDE!! One..Two..Kickout! LB: He's not gonna catch MY man with a crappy little move like that! MW: He almost DID, Bertha! Both men up, but Showboat AGAIN catches Osbourne with a short clothesline! Showboat picks the champion up, VERTICAL SUPLEX! BD: (yelling to the ring) Don't let him do that to you, Ozzy! MW: Who's Ozzy? BD: Isn't that Ozzy Osbourne? MW: NO, it's RANDALL Osbourne. BD: Oh, same difference. MW: Whatever. And now Osbourne is down, and Showboat is REALLY working over his legs, getting him ready for the Corporate Buy-Out. Showboat DROPS an elbow onto the knee of Osbourne, AND IT LOOKS LIKE HE'S GONNA GO FOR THE BUY-OUT ALREADY! LB: Yes! It's gonna be over ALREADY! I told you this would be easy! MW: Showboat picks up the legs of Osbourne, and steps into it, BUT OSBOURNE REACHES UP AND PULLS SHOWBOAT DOWN, INTO A SMALL PACKAGE!! One..Two..Three!! (DING, DING) BD: (Laughing hysterically) That was GREAT!! Welcome to the Heavyweights, Showboat! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of one minute and forty seconds, here is your winner, and STILL AEWA Television Champion, RANDALL "THE REBEL OF ROCK" OSBOURNE!! MW: And Showboat is absolutely IRATE!! He is complaining about a fast count! LB: It WAS a fast count! His shoulders were barely on the mat for a ONE count! MW: It might have been a LITTLE fast, but arguing about it's not gonna do any good. The match is over, and the referee Bill Johnson's not gonna change it just because Showboat didn't like it. BD: Face it Ricky, YOU SUCK! MW: I wouldn't say that; he just went for his move WAY too early, and he got caught. But Showboat is STILL arguing with the referee Johnson! AND NOW SHOWBOAT JUST SHOVED HIM! Johnson was none too happy with that, and he SHOVES Showboat back! LB: Not a good idea! MW: Showboat looks STUNNED that the ref had enough guts to push him, AND HE LEVELS JOHNSON WITH A CLOTHESLINE! That's gonna cost him some money! And now Showboat picks up Johnson by the legs, and locks HIM in the Corporate Buy-Out! BD: Showboat has no right to do this! He lost, fair and square. He should be fined and suspended immediately! MW: I agree, but saying that is not gonna help Referee Johnson out there right now. Showboat's REALLY applying the pressure, and Johnson is in pain! I'm sure Osbourne would do something about it, but he already left long ago, and Showboat is left alone to torture the referee! LB: He DESERVES it, he cost Ricky this match! MW: He did NOT; if Showboat had enough sense to know not to apply his finisher less than 2 minutes into the match, then he would never have lost! Johnson needs help in there, but wait a minute, HERE COMES THE GRYPHON!! BD: Like that big moron'll do any good. MW: Gryphon hits the ring like a house of fire, and BOTH Showboat and Doctor Love dive for cover! They're escaping by exiting through the crowd! They don't want any part of the Gryphon, and this crowd LOVES it! LB: They're not scared of him, he's just not worth wasting their time on. MW: Sure, that's probably exactly what they're thinking-- NOT! The Gryphon is helping Referee Johnson back to the locker room now, and this crowd yells its approval! BD: Yeah, get him the hell outta here, we've still got more matches to go! MW: Speaking of which, up next is our main event, as Shane Matthews defends his World Title against American Ambush! LB: This should be a pretty good match. Shane's got a tough one ahead of him tonight! MW: He sure does. American Ambush means business, and Shane's got his work cut out for him tonight. BD: This'll be the first time we've seen Ambush wrestle anyone other than Jose Gomez in quite a while. Does he still know HOW to wrestle other guys? MW: I don't think that'll be a problem, Big Daddy. Especially with the World Title on the line. He's gonna do anything and everything it takes to win that title, and I'm sure Shane will do the same to try to hold onto it. LB: Definitely, that's why this one's gonna be so good. MW: It certainly looks good on paper, but the only way to find out is in the ring! RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is set for one fall and is for the AEWA World Heavyweight Championship! Introducing first, the challenger, weighing in at 243 pounds, American Aaaaaaammmmmmmbuuuuuuuuuuuussssssh! (American Ambush makes his way toward the ring to a BIG round of boos from the crowd. He is making belt strapping motions around his waist.) BD: Well he certainly looks confident! MW: He'd better not be too confident, or else he's gonna get beat! LB: And Shane Matthews ISN'T overconfident? MW: You may have a point there. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, he weighs in at 222 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his manger, Johnny Terror, He is the current AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, "Sudden Death" Shaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaane Maaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaattheeeeeewwwwws! (Anthrax's "Looking Down the Barrel of a Gun" cranks out over the sound system as Matthews, belt around his waist, makes his entrance to a HUGE round of boos from the crowd. Johnny Terror is at his side.) BD: And Shane looks ready for anything, as always. MW: He'd better be if he wants to come out of this one with his title in tact. LB: Oh, come on Wilson, let's get serious. Shane's a double World champion; he doesn't need to be ready, it just comes naturally to him. MW: Maybe so, but if he relies on that all the time, one time he's gonna get beat. Both men are in the ring now, and we've got a lockup! And Ambush whips Matthews to the ropes! Matthews comes back, and LEVELS Ambush with a clothesline! BD: That's why Shane's the man-- he can turn a match around just like that, and then BAM! Sudden Death. MW: He also had a LITTLE help becoming "the man" from Scorpion. BD: Details.... MW: Whatever. Matthews picks the Ambush up now, INVERTED ATOMIC DROP! Ambush is stunned, and Matthew just NAILS him with a dropkick! LB: Ambush can't let the champ jump out on top of him like this if he wants to win the belt. MW: No, he can't. And now Matthews grabs him by the head, SPINNING DDT! Matthews picks him back up, and nails him with ANOTHER spinning DDT!! BD: That's humiliating, having the same move done to you twice in a row. I LIKE IT! MW: You would. And now Matthews DROPS an elbow onto Ambush, and he's stepping to the outside! Matthews stands out on the apron, SLINGSHOT LEG DROP!! LB: The Ambush is in TROUBLE! MW: Yes he is. And now Matthews is picking him up, he lifts the Ambush into the air, JACK-KNIFE POWER BOMB! The cover! One..Two..Foot on the ropes! BD: Ambush mighta been gone there if he wasn't so close to the ropes. MW: Yes, but at least he still has enough wits about him to put his foot there. Now Matthews is just going right back to work. He picks the Ambush up off the mat, and hits ANOTHER Spinning DDT! LB: Matthews is really pouring it on now! MW: And now the champ picks him up, and tosses him to the outside! Shane charges to the ropes, AND EXECUTES A SLINGSHOT SOMERSAULT PLANCHA TO THE FLOOR! Ambush is REALLY hurting now! BD: Those kinda moves are where Shane Matthews is at his best. MW: They are indeed. And now Matthews picks the Ambush up, and RAMS him into the ringsteps! Matthews hops back up onto the apron now, AND TAKES AMBUSH DOWN WITH A FLYING HEADSCISSORS! LB: This just hasn't been Ambush's day. MW: THAT'S an understatement. Matthews picks Ambush now, and WHIPS him to the steel guardrail! Matthews charges at him again, AND TAKES HIM DOWN WITH A NECK SCISSORS! And Matthews heads back into the ring! BD: Is he just gonna take a count out here? MW: That's what it looks like! The ref is laying a count on Ambush, and Matthews is doing nothing to stop it. 1...2...3...4...5...6...7...8...9...And Ambush manages to CRAWL back in! LB: I think it might've been a better idea for him to stay out there. MW: You could be right. Ambush STRUGGLES to his feet, and Matthews picks him up, POWERSLAM!!! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Looks like Ambush DOES still have somethin' left! MW: He's hanging on. Matthews brings the Ambush to his feet now, and runs to the ropes! Matthews comes back, HIGH CROSS BODY--BUT AMBUSH CATCHES HIM, and brings him DOWN with a backbreaker! LB: Looks like Shane took one risk too many that time. MW: And Ambush made him pay. The Ambush picks Matthews up now, SOVIET SUPLEX! Matthews tries to get up, but Ambush takes him RIGHT back to the mat with a single-leg takedown! BD: Ambush is in control now, let's see what he can do with it. MW: He's doing good so far. The Ambush picks Matthews up now, and NAILS him with a Russian legsweep! And now Ambush tosses MATTHEWS out of the ring! LB: Looks like it's time for some payback! MW: Ambush follows him out, and he WHIPS the champion to the ringsteps! And now he picks Matthews right back up, and WHIPS him to the guardrail! If revenge is what he wanted, he's getting it! BD: And then some! MW: And now Ambush is quick to roll Matthews back into the ring, he wants to capitalize on this before it's too late! Ambush follows him back in, and goes right to work! (As the action continues in the ring, Mean Massimo quietly makes his way to ringside.) LB: And here comes trouble! MW: You've got THAT one right-- trouble in the form of Mean Massimo! He and the Ambush, former friends, have been on REALLY bad terms lately. BD: Well that's what usually happens when you join your enemies and beat your friends to a bloody pulp! MW: Good point. Ambush doesn't see him yet, and right now he NAILS Shane Matthews with a kneebreaker! Ambush picks Matthews up now, and whips him to the ropes! Matthews comes back, AND GETS CAUGHT IN A POWERSLAM!! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Shane's not ready to go down yet! MW: No, but it was darn close. And now the Ambush just spotted Massimo, and he is NOT happy! He's gone over to that side of the ring, and he's yelling some things at Massimo that I really can't repeat on the air! BD: Sure you can, wussie. He's saying "Fuc... MW: (interrupting) Uh, that's enough, Big Daddy. I think they get the point. And Ambush is really upset; the ref is trying to send Massimo away, but he wont go! AND SHANE MATTHEWS JUST NAILED AMBUSH IN THE BACK OF THE HEAD WITH THE TITLE BELT! Where'd he get that? LB: Johnny gave it to him while Ambush and the ref were busy with Massimo, stupid! MW: Well EXCUSE ME for paying attention to where the action is. Matthews tosses the belt away now, and the ref JUST turned his attention back to the ring from Massimo. BD: He got rid of that sucker just in time! MW: And now Matthews places the Ambush on the top rope-- I think we ALL know what's coming here! He climbs up there with the Ambush, and he JUMPS, AND NAILS AMBUSH WITH "THE END OF THE ROAD"!!! Here's the cover! One..Two..Three!! (DING, DING) LB: Well Ambush let his hatred for Massimo get the better of him, and it cost him his chance at the World Title tonight. RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of fourteen minutes and forty-four seconds, here is your winner, and STILL AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, "SUDDEN DEATH" SHANE MATTHEWS!!!! MW: And Matthews and Terror are quick to hightail it outta there with the belt before Ambush comes to. But Massimo is still waiting at ringside! BD: Looks like he's waiting for the perfect time to strike. MW: Yes, it does. The Ambush is struggling to his feet, and Massimo begins to scale the ringsteps! He has just climbed into the ring, and now he's reaching into his coat pocket! LB: He just pulled something out! It looks like a little wooden rod. MW: But Massimo just pushed some kinda button, AND IT'S A SWITCHBLADE!!! He's gonna STAB American Ambush! He moves in closer.... BD: BUT A SECURITY GUARD JUST TACKLED MASSIMO!!! Where did HE come from? MW: I don't know, but it's a good thing he was there, or else the Ambush might be dead now! And here are several other guards, and it looks like there's some actual POLICEMEN there too! LB: What's the big deal? He only tried to stab a guy! MW: Yeah, and that's called ATTEMPTED MURDER, Bertha. They're handcuffing Massimo, and it looks like the police are actually taking him away! To jail, I hope! BD: And the Ambush just saw what Massimo tried to do, and now HE has to be restrained by the security guards! They're both trying to get at each other, but they're BOTH being heavily restrained! There's no love lost here, that's for sure! MW: No there's not. When these two finally meet, it's gonna be a WAR! But they're not gonna get that chance tonight, as Massimo will be spending the night in a jail cell. Well fans, I'm sorry but that's all the time we have for this week! So for the Lovely Bertha and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt, this is Matt Wilson saying so long everybody!
