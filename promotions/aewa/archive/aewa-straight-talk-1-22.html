Straight Talk (1/22)

23 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera fades in on the AEWA Studios, the AEWA logo falls onto the screen, and the words "STRAIGHT TALK" slide in underneath it. The whole logo then tilts, and falls off the bottom of the screen. Behind the desk in studio are Chuck Carlson and Ken Wilson.) CC: Hello everyone and welcome once again to the AEWA's one and only interview show, Straight Talk. I'm your host, Chuck Carlson, and with me is my ever faithful sidekick, Ken Wilson. KW: Faithful sidekick? What am I now-- Tonto? CC: No, Tonto's smarter. KW: You know, Carlson, one of these days I'm gonna kick your butt right here on this show. And I mean it! CC: Sure you do. Anyway folks, we've got plenty of interviews coming your way tonight, so let's get started. Up first, we've got a newcomer to the AEWA. Here's Paul Smith. ---------- (Paul Smith is in the locker room with his bodyguard, Mister Klash. Smith is wearing the FWA Heavyweight Championship belt.) Smith: You know, I sometimes get the reputation of a mercenary-- willing to do anything for money. Well, that's not entirely false, but I'm driven by something else right now. There are more important things on my mind. I have won many titles in smaller leagues as a rulebreaker, and as a fan favorite. The one I have around my waist now, I won as a rulebreaker. However, I'm not going to downplay its significance. I also sometimes get the reputation of being a jerk just because I have a bodyguard. Well, I'm not Shawn Michaels! (points to Klash) He's there.... because he's a friend and I like having encouragement. Whether it's from the fans, or from Klash, I know I'm getting it. Sure, the fans are great to have, but I'm here to tell you one thing-- as much as I like to have them, I can live without them. I'm one guy who is going to do whatever it takes to win. However, I am still loyal to my friends outside the ring. I can separate between business and pleasure. I'm looking forward to my first opponent here in the AEWA, it's going to be a learning experience for the both of us.... I still have the element of surprise going for me. I've been scouting this league for months now, so watch out! I'm coming, AEWA! (fade to black) ---------- KW: I've heard lots of good things about this guy from my sources down in the FWA. He should be making a lot of noise here in the near future. CC: Who do YOU know in the FWA? KW: I can't tell you who my source is! CC: Why not? KW: Because.... uh.... because he asked to remain anonymous. Yeah, that's the ticket! CC: Yeah, or because he doesn't exist and you made the whole thing up. (Ken looks embarrassed) CC: Let's just check out these words from Stan Crawford. ---------- ("The Shotgun" Stan Crawford is standing on a busy street corner in New York City. He is wearing a black leather jacket, and blue jeans. His hair is pulled back into a ponytail, and he wears a pair of black sunglasses.) Crawford: Primal Fear, you want a rematch with ME?! I'll give it to you, but just know, I'll beat you just like I did a few weeks ago. You want ME to get my "fat ass" into the AEWA offices and sign a contract?! Well no problem! I'll do that! But I'm not the one with the "fat ass", buddy! If I remember correctly, it was YOUR fat ass that couldn't get back into the ring! I didn't like getting the countout victory, but it WAS a victory! I'll give you this rematch just to prove to you and everyone else here in the AEWA that my win was not a fluke! (Crawford rips off his sunglasses and throws them down.) Crawford: Beware, Primal Fear! You have made me very angry! You are just a stepping stone to my rise to the top of the AEWA. Sure, you are a great wrestler, but I'm better! I have waited too long and worked too hard for you to beat me! I've done it before, and I'll do it again. Primal Fear, you are going down! (Crawford casually walks backwards into the crowd on the street, as the camera slowly zooms in on the broken sunglasses on the sidewalk. The camera fades to black.) ---------- KW: Primal Fear just upset the WRONG guy. He could've gotten off nicely, with just a countout loss, which is practically meaningless, but he had to challenge Crawford to a rematch-- and he's not gonna be so lucky this time. CC: How do you know that? It was a close match last time, and Primal Fear didn't even know anything about his opponent. This time, he knows what he's up against, AND he has something to prove. What makes you think that Crawford would beat him so easily this time? KW: Because I said so, that's why. CC: BRILLIANT deduction, Sherlock. KW: Thanks, I knew you'd like it. CC: And now let's go to our North American Champion, Justin Trouble. ---------- (The camera opens on Justin Trouble, wearing a black sweatshirt, gray sweatpants and a "No Fear" black baseball cap. He appears to be on a mountain, and behind him is the morning skyline with a city of which is unclear.) Trouble: You know, a lot of people have been listening to a lot of other people talk. Bob Freeman, you came on and talked about how I wasn't there when you were power-bombed through a table and severely beaten up. Should I have been there? Yes. Could I have been there? No. And whether YOU like it or not, whether the fans like it or not, whether the wrestlers like it or not, they all, including you, know why I wasn't there-- and for you to take out your attack on me is totally unprofessional. If you want to whine, than you go right ahead and do it. But you won't see me doing that. I'm a man of action. You know it, and the fans know it, and my opponents know it. But despite all of the rotten things you've said about me, I'm going to help you, whether you like it or not. I've got a score to settle. Actually I've got a lot of scores to settle. And I'd like to address a few of those admirers real quickly. Deathbreath, you know how I feel about you. And buddy, this is the wrong time to pick a fight with me. But I'm no coward and I'll be more than happy to teach you a wrestling lesson or two. And as for Larkin and the Visigothic Whores, you'd better watch your backs, too. I don't care what the odds are, whether it's 2-on-1 or 10-on-1, I'm going to get even with you. Very shortly. It'll be at that moment when you're on cloud nine, and then it'll happen. I'll do to you what you did to me-- crush your dreams, and make one hell of a living nightmare for you punks. Change is a little bit like evolution. You're forced to go through it so you can survive life's obstacle course. And I HAVE changed. Better for me, worse for you. I'll be seeing you soon.. (Justin begins to jog off as the camera fades.) ---------- KW: What a crybaby! He gets fired for not doing his job, and all he does is complain about it. CC: It's not his fault! He COULDN'T do his job. He has a right to complain, but he did a lot more than that in this interview. KW: Like what? CC: Like respond to Deathwatch, like telling the Visigothic Horde that he's not done with them, and basically stating that he's not going anywhere, and he's gonna continue to take out the AEWA's trash. KW: Like anyone gives a crap about that. CC: Anyone who's a true wrestling fan should. KW: Well I don't, sue me. CC: Apparently I'm not gonna get anywhere with this, so why don't we just watch this interview with Ben Slocum. --------- (The camera opens on the "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum and his sister Sondra, both standing if front of an AEWA banner.) Ben: Heavy Metal! Heavy Metal! How many times have you heard that one before? How many people have this vicious individual taken out of action with his brutal assaults? Well, I don't know, but I DO know that he tried to take me out in that Combat Cage match. I guess I'm neither the first or the last, but when he tried it with me, he did it to the wrong man. That was the worst mistake of his life. Sondra: Well, you'll meet him again someday, and I'm pretty sure that the result will be very different then. He may be big and fat, but when it comes down to wrestling, he's not much to write home about, that's for sure. It's also rather interesting to note that Cardinal Crap and his bogus church have done everything within their power to stay away from you, and I think that speaks for itself-- but maybe they'll FINALLY come out of the closet and step into the ring. But they're cowards, so I doubt it. Ben: Yeah, still no words of wisdom from the frauds and the failures hiding in his sanctuary. No Cardinal coming out to defend his church, no Mister Crowley, not even Bishop Bedwetter or whatever his name is. Maybe, just maybe, they've finally gotten the message that there is a new breed of law and order in town-- the Knights of Deliverance. Then again, they might not have caught on quite yet; after all, they've always been kind of slow. Sondra: I think they've got it by now. When we speak of respect and integrity though, that's something they'll probably never understand. Those guys just haven't been respecting the rest of competition, the commissioners, or the audience in any decent way. The Cardinal is REALLY bad-- and he claims to be some kind of leader? Ben: (stares directly at the camera) Chaos! Above all, this is about honor and integrity. This ain't the place for a traveling circus, which is pretty much how your so-called "church" is run. Perhaps you should just ask yourself why I challenged you in the first place. I want to show you, I want to show the rest of the AEWA, or the rest of the WORLD for that matter what you're really all about. I intend to expose you for the fraud and cheater that you are. This is just to give you a wake-up call, Chaos. I'll be coming after you guys, and this time, I'm not alone. What're you gonna do about it? (fade to black) ---------- KW: (laughing) Slocum ACTUALLY thinks he can beat Cardinal Chaos? HA HA HA HA HA HA.... CC: What's so funny? He's done it before. If I remember correctly, Slocum beat Chaos some time ago for the TV Title. KW: FLUKE! And besides, Chaos won the match that counted. He won his second WORLD title from that chump, Slocum. CC: Yes, but NEITHER match was a cakewalk for either man, and it's been a LONG time since their last match. A lot of things have changed. KW: Yes, the Cardinal has gotten better, and Slocum has gotten worse. CC: Not exactly. This would be an outstanding matchup, and I think Slocum is MORE than capable of coming out on top. KW: If you say so. CC: I do. And now let's get a word with Heavy Metal. ---------- (The camera opens on a rock concert, with around 150 people dancing and headbanging around the mosh pit. White Zombie plays on stage. They sing the final verses of "More Human Than Human" and then they end the song and people start to cheer. Rob Zombie, the lead singer of the group, grabs the microphone out of the holster and walks halfway across the stage; then walks back to the center, giving everyone in the front row a smack of the hand. He begins to speak to the audience.) Rob Zombie: I hope you've all enjoyed the show, but before we wrap this sh*t up, I'd like to introduce you all to a personal friend of mine. How many of you here watch the AEWA Professional Wrestling promotion? (About half the crowd cheers wildly when he mentions the AEWA) Rob Zombie: Well, I got a special treat for all you wrestling-maniacs out there. From Atlanta, Georgia, he weighs in at over 450 pounds and stands about 7'4" tall, he's a great guy and long-time friend of mine.... here is HEAVY METAL!! (The crowd goes wild for the former-AEWA Heavyweight Champion, as he walks from behind the stage. He is wearing his usual black leather vest, black "White Zombie" T-shirt, black boots, black leather pants, a black glove on his right hand, and a black elbowpad on his left arm. His long brown hair hangs to the sides of his head, and he high-fives Rob Zombie as he hits center stage. The massive Heavy Metal is given the microphone from Zombie, and pyrotechnics go off along the front of the stage and from the ceiling. He raises his hand in the air with the typical rocker symbol, yells "ROCK ON" to the crowd, and they go absolutely nuts. As the pyrotechnics simmer down, he begins to speak...) Heavy Metal: Hey, all you wrestling fanatics! (crowd cheers once more) Heavy Metal: First off, I'd like to thank my old friend, Rob, for letting me come out here and do this. Thanks a lot, pal! (Rob nods to Metal and he continues) Heavy Metal: Alright, now you all should know the situation in the AEWA, right? I'm the former Heavyweight Champion and soon-to-be Heavyweight Champion yet again. I got screwed over in the past, and I'm not gonna get screwed over again! But, before I can get that Heavyweight Title, I have to wait to see who comes out on top in the Deranged-Reed match. This one is a no-brainer folks-- who's gonna win? (The crowd is silent for a second, and then one man blurts out....) Man #1: DOES IT REALLY MATTER!? Heavy Metal: Exactly! It doesn't matter worth sh*t who wins or who doesn't. Either way, when I get my title shot against either of those no-name losers, the outcome is gonna be-- say it, folks! Crowd: THEY'RE GONNA GET ROCKED!! Heavy Metal: I love this crowd! Seriously, you guys are great! Now obviously, I'm not gonna sit here talking about a match that ain't even happening yet, so I'm here to issue a challenge to someone.... just so I don't get any ring rust. (Crowd laughs) Heavy Metal: Yeah, I know what you're thinkin'.... "Heavy Metal? Get ring rust? You're kidding, right?" And to tell you the truth-- yes, I AM kidding! There's no way in hell I'd let myself lose touch with the sport, there's just no way. Now, on to the challenge. Who to challenge, who to challenge.... Audience? Any suggestions? Crowd: Os-bourne! Os-bourne! Os-bourne! Heavy Metal: Hey, exactly what I was thinkin'! Mister Rebel of Rock, you got yourself a problem. You recently had a title, I don't have one-- what's wrong with this picture? You guessed it. Now Randy-boy, I want a title, and I'm willing to crack ribs to get it. If those ribs just happen to be attached to your body, then so be it, dude. I figure that beating your sorry ass should leave NO DOUBT who deserves the next title shot. Not to mention your name.... You think you rock? I AM ROCK!! I am everything that you're not; the only thing that separates you from me is the fact that you've held a title more recently than I have. That's about to change, little man. Alright, now I'm taking questions from the crowd-- who's first? (A man in the audience raises his hand. He's wearing a plain-black T-shirt and has long brown hair, and looks as if he's been head-banging a little too long for some reason.) Man #2: Yeah... like.... I heard that you're gonna start a band or something. Is this true? Heavy Metal: (thinking) Well, I've considered it over my time as a professional wrestler, especially when things started to fall and everything seemed as if it wasn't worth it anymore. But the answer is, no.... I'm not gonna start a band anytime soon, but maybe when I'm through wrestling I'll call up MetalHead and we can jam. Until then, though, I'll stick with wrestling. Yeah, you in the plaid shirt.... (A man wearing a plaid flannel shirt and blue jeans begins to speak) Man #3: I was just wondering-- have you ever met Alex Adams? Heavy Metal: (sighs) Alex Adams is a pathetic little man who doesn't need to have his ass kicked by me to let him know that, but it would help. He isn't in this federation anyway, so next question-- you, front and center. (A man in the front row wearing an "Iron Maiden" T-shirt begins to speak) Man #4: Are you still in ICU? I heard that you and Deathwatch got into some kind of argument, and now you're both split apart from each other-- is that true? Also, what's up with him pushing you around like that? It's like he controls you or something. Heavy Metal: First of all, NO ONE CONTROLS HEAVY METAL!! And second of all, no.... Me and Deathwatch had some problems, but we've worked things out and are getting along just fine. Deathwatch likes to run the show, but not get involved in the action too much. He's a businessman with a flare for action, that's all. Next question-- you, with the pink mohawk, where do you think you are? A Sex Pistols concert? Man #5: Uh, no. What I wanted to ask is this-- are there any more people comin' into ICU? Or are you guys through recruiting people? Heavy Metal: Ah, yeah, we have our sights set on someone else. If you're watching Straight Talk next time, we might get to see his response. (Another man in a "Heavy Metal" T-shirt speaks up) Man #6: Uh.... yeah.... you're like my biggest fan or something.... uh.... Wait a second .... that didn't sound right, did it? Oh, anyway, I was just wondering if.... um.... if like ....uh.... you know.... if you could sign my shirt? Heavy Metal: I haven't waited for someone to spit out a sentence that long since I heard Leroy Brown talk his crap! (Crowd laughs) Heavy Metal: Sure, I'll sign your shirt, come on up here. (The man walks up on stage, and he trips on the last step and falls onto the stage. He stands back up, and smiles as Heavy Metal pulls a pen from his vest and signs his shirt. The guy smiles widely and then stands on his toes so he can whisper something into Metal's ear.) Heavy Metal: You want me to WHAT!? (Crowd is silent) Heavy Metal: Oh, alright, whatever floats your boat. Hey guys, check this out. This dude wants me to give him a Megadeth Power Bomb into the crowd-- what do you guys think? Crowd: YYYYEEEEAAAAAHHHHH!! Heavy Metal: Alright then.... Here goes.... (Heavy Metal sets the guy up in a gutwrench position, and lifts him into the air. He then tosses him into the crowd, and they all catch him and set him back down. A bunch of guys pat the guy on the back and rub his hair, but he looks a tad shaken up.) Heavy Metal: Randy-boy! That's what's gonna happen to you later this month! Only difference is that you'll be hitting the hard canvas instead of getting a smooth ride to the ground like that dude! That's all the time I got, thanks again to Rob for letting me come out here tonight, and thanks to all you guys for bein' so cool about it. Randy, I'm comin' for ya, whether you like it or not. AEWA, ROCK ON!! (Metallica's "Battery" begins to play as Heavy Metal walks off-stage and is met by Rob Zombie. Rob pats him on the back and they both go out of sight. Fade to black.) ---------- KW: Heavy Metal seems to really wanna get his hands on Osbourne for some reason, and when he does, it ain't gonna be pretty. CC: Indeed, this would undoubtedly be one of his greatest challenges ever, but Osbourne seems to be entangled in a feud with Shane Matthews right about now. I don't think he has time to worry about this. KW: Maybe not, but that'll make it all the more dangerous when Heavy Metal strikes-- Osbourne won't be ready for him. But Heavy Metal's not exactly concentrating solely on Osbourne either, he's got his eye on the world title. CC: Who doesn't? KW: Deranged. He's already got it. CC: Very funny. And now we've got an interview with our World Tag Team Champions, The Machines. ---------- (Paul Wong and Simon O'Neal are in their locker room. On their "Wall of Conquests", the names THE GRAVEYARD SHIFT, HEAVEN & HELL, and THE WEST COAST CONNECTION are added in green marker. Most of the damage has been cleared up, and only a few bruises remain from their four-way match. Simon is wearing his belt around his waist, while Paul is carrying his over his left shoulder.) Paul: A few quick comments. First of all, now that we've won the World Tag Team Title Belts, we intend to prove ourselves as worthy champions. That means that we plan on taking on all of the contenders-- The Assassins, The Shadow Warriors, Heaven & Hell, Graveyard Shift, even Graphic Violence. We don't intend to back down from anyone. Simon: Of course, it helps that we've beaten each and every team on that list. But one team stands out-- the West Coast Connection. Paul: You beat us in the LaValle Cup match, and then we outlasted you in the four-way brawl. Looks like we need a tie-breaker match to determine who is the better tag team. Simon: Well, we are, of course. Still, it might be easier if they would bother to leave their corner thug-- what's his name? Paul: Hood Lum. Simon: Of course it is. I'd suggest you leave Mr. Lum behind-- after all, you wouldn't want him to get you disqualified, would you? Paul: One other point. We heard Mr. Freeman's comments about Kevin Larkin. Now, while it is true that Kevin Larkin helped bring us to the AEWA, his actions have been horrible, as we volunteer to be Bob Freeman's tag team against the Visigothic Horde. We have one other advantage-- a win over the Visigothic Horde. It happened over a year ago in another league, but we have beaten them. (Looks over at Simon, who is shaking his head) And your thoughts? Simon: Hey, I like Kevin Larkin being in charge. We work well with him. Paul: Are you kidding? You had to get him drunk to get the National Tag Team Title Match. Simon: Exactly. A few cheap comments and some cheaper beer.... he's putty in our hands. The matches WE want, with the opponents WE choose, with the referees WE like. I say we stick with Kevin. Paul: You would want that? A corrupt vice-president, using low-down underhanded means to keep his power? (Simon looks at Paul strangely for a few seconds, then sticks out his right hand.) Simon: You obviously have no idea who I am. My name's Simon O'Neal... Paul: Nevermind. President Freeman, our offer stands. And West Coast Connection, or anyone else who wants a match for our belts, feel free to take your best shot-- we don't plan on letting go of these for a long time. (fade to black) ---------- KW: I predict that these guys will last exactly 2 days as Tag Team Champs. CC: They've already been champs for WELL over two days. KW: Really? Well, then I predict that they will lose the belts at their next defense. CC: To who? KW: To whoever they're fighting, it doesn't matter. These guys won the titles on PURE LUCK, and when they meet ANYONE who's worth a damn, they're gonna get the crap kicked out of them. CC: And why is that? Other than the National Title loss to Graphic Violence, they haven't lost a match here in the AEWA. KW: Well.... uh.... Then that's gonna change! CC: (sarcastically) Good reasoning. And now for our final interview, let's hear from the Television Champion, Sir Gallade. ---------- (The camera opens on the AEWA studios. Sir Gallade faces the camera and looks quite upset.) Gallade: There is a thin thread that holds me to the brink of morality. What Cardinal Chaos and the Church tried to do to me cut that thread, and now it is time for my counter-attack! You all may have had the upperhand at the end, but remember Bishop Bedlam, I kicked your bloody teeth down your throat! When it comes to the Church, there is no more Sir Gallade that abides by the rules, because Cardinal Chaos, when you brought that axe into the ring and attempted to behead me, the thread was cut and out comes the WARRIOR in Sir Gallade! Cardinal Chaos, (Sir Gallade draws his sword) I saw your weapon, now see mine. Say hello to Excalibur. I guess I will have to keep my sword by my side at all times now! Cardinal Chaos, I challenge you to a Combat Cage Match where we will both have our weapon of choice, and you will then see that I am the greatest swordsman in the world! It is time for justice and Cardinal Chaos and the Church, I am your judge, jury, and executioner! You will all feel the wrath of my Guillotine! (fade to black) ---------- KW: This guy wants to have a Combat Cage match, with a SWORD as his weapon? Is he NUTS? You could kill someone! CC: Which is EXACTLY what Cardinal Chaos tried to do to HIM last week with that axe! Now while I don't condone the idea either, I know where he's coming from, and he's out for revenge. KW: Well weapons or no weapons, Cardinal Chaos would wipe the mat with that pathetic loser, and that's a fact. CC: Is it? KW: Yes. CC: I don't think so. And fans, that's all the time we have for this week, so for my broadcast partner, Ken Wilson, this is Chuck Carlson saying see ya next week for MORE Straight Talk!
