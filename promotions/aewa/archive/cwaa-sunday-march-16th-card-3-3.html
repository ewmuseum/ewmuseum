AEWA — Card Archive

Sunday, March 16th, Card (3/3)

16 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Time Life Video Presents: The World of Animals, a six-part video series that shows you up close and personal the life of all creatures that walk the earth, fly the skies and swim the seas. Your first video, "Nematodes, Annelids and True Worms" takes you into the world of the tapeworm, the earthworm and more. See hermaphroditism in action. See what happens when you cut an earthworm in half. Then, Time Life Video will send Volume II: "Bugs and Icky Things". Try it for 30 days. If you aren't satisfied, send it back and cancel your account and we'll refund your money. But keep "Nematodes, Annelids and True Worms" as our gift to you...) EL: Before we get to Joey Haven and Marc Thomas, let's hear from the man they are fighting for the chance to meet- AEWA World Light Heavyweight Champion, the Cardiac Kid, Joey Valachi. ************************************************************** (The scene: A vacant stage in the AEWA studios. Offstage, we recognize the voice of AEWA Light-Heavyweight Champion "Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi) Valachi: All right, you win. The contract DOES say I have to wrestle the CWAA loser. But why do I have to waste my time here in the studio? Director: Because your contract ALSO says you have to do your part to promote your matches, and the AEWA in general. Speaking of which, let me see what shirt you're wearing. (pause) No way, Joey. We're not putting that shirt on the air again. Valachi: It's true. Director: No it isn't, and even if it was it doesn't matter. You don't get out of here until this is done, and this ain't getting done till you take that shirt off. Understand? Valachi: Fine, fine. (mutters) You'd think a franchise player like me would be a little more respect. (full voice) How's that? Can we get this over with now? Director: Please. ("Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi steps into view. He is about 6 feet, 212 pounds, wearing jeans and a leather jacket, but no shirt. His AEWA Light-Heavyweight Title is draped over his shoulder, and he is wearing his customary sneer.) Valachi: CWAA fans, I'm going to make your day. One week from tonight, the powers that be have decreed that I have to face either Joey Haven or Marc Thomas for the AEWA World Light-Heavyweight Title, a belt that only has meaning because I wear it. Fine. I just hope you weren't expecting any competition, because I'm already light-years above the best in the AEWA. I shudder to think how far short they'll fall in the minors. Now, I don't know much about the two cretins fighting for the right to be next on my list, but I know Haven thinks he's "Hardcore." Now, I'm just wondering, when you say Hardcore, do you mean the "I'm willing to dive through a table head first to cover up the fact I have no talent" definition of Hardcore? (nods) Thought so. And Thomas, I vaguely remember. Had some potential, I believe, but he seems to think he's entitled to call himself "The Iceman." The sport already has an Iceman, though, Thomas, and I'll bet he's better than you. The point is, none of this matters, because any organization that thinks Randy Sheridan is a star doesn't have the talent to make it worth my while. But the Idiot Patrol commonly known as the AEWA Board wants me down here, so here I come. Count yourselves lucky. (Valachi walks off, asking "Can I go now?" The camera fades as the director lectures Valachi about how one does and does not promote a match.) ************************************************************ EL: I know a lot of people up in the AEWA and down here in Texas would love to see that man get his comeuppance. Next week, we'll see what happens when he brings that attitude here to the world-famous Sportatorium. But first, we need to find out who he'll be meeting. Let's go up to ring announcer David Myers. ("Life in the Fast Lane" by the Eagles plays) DM: This next contest is to determine the CWAA's contender for the AEWA World Light Heavyweight Championship. It is set for one fall- EL: Wait a second, here come Randy Sheradin and Joshua Barnes! (Sheradin and Barnes hop in the ring and Barnes grabs the microphone. The music stops.) JB: Ro...ro...ROBERTSON! We have ha...ha...ha...HAD IT WITH YOU! (Sheridan takes the mike from Barnes) RS: Take it easy, big guy. What Josh is trying to say, Max is...well...we've had it with you. You want to be the bad guy, fine. We've dealt with bad guys before. But you want to play like you're a friend, then stab me in the back? I've had that happen one too many times to let it slide. (Barnes reclaims the mike) JB: You...tried to come between me and Randy. Well it didn't work. We're still here, and I'm going to watch his back when he wins the title tonight! (Barnes tries to start a chant of "Dreams come true!" but the crowd quickly settles into the tried and true "Go, Randy, go!" Eventually, Barnes gives up on trying to change the crowd's habits, and joins the chant as Sheridan gets on the mike one more time.) RS: I'm used to coming out of nowhere to pull off the win. I'm not going to pretend it's going to be easy, but I did beat you before. And I wasn't NEARLY as geared up for that match as I am for this one. (Barnes raises Sheridan's hand as the crowd resumes the chant of "Go, Randy, go!" The two men leave, shaking hands and trading hi-fives with the fans.) ("Life in the Fast Lane" resumes) DM: This next contest is to determine the CWAA's contender for the AEWA World Light Heavyweight Championship. It is set for one fall, with a thirty minute time limit. First, from Charlotte, NC, accompanied by Jak Hammer, weighing 245 pounds, Marc Thomas! (Big heel pop for both individuals) (Marc Thomas grabs the microphone as Jak Hammer, bandaged and taped up after the steel cage match, stations himself at ringside) DM: Marc Thomas, what are your feelings about this important match for a shot at the AEWA Light-Heavyweight Championship? MT: What are my feelings? What kind of question is that? I'm thrilled to be getting this chance to show that I am one of the best wrestlers not only in the CWAA, but also in the AEWA as well. President Freeman and the Executive Committee of the AEWA always had their champs avoid me! Why? I don't know. I guess they knew that I wasn't some corporate puppet who would sign matches with anyone they chose. I march to my own drummer and the execs in the AEWA were worried that if I got the belt then they would have to sign the matches I wanted instead of the ones they wanted. Look at the Light-Heavyweight scene in the AEWA. You've got Valachi, the Holy Ghost, and a bunch of nobodies. What kind of competition is that? They send me down here to wrestle and look what they replace me with. El Jobber? Things got so desperate that an announcer actually held the belt for short amount of time!!! Haven, I've watched you get beat from one end of the arena to the other by Jak Hammer. Do you really think you have a chance against someone who can actually wrestle? You call yourself "Hardcore." Well, you see what that reputation has done for the idiots of ECW. The fans might love that style of "wrestling" but no fan has ever gotten into a ring and won a match! After I beat you Haven, I'm going to show the execs of the AEWA the mistake they made by sending me down to the CWAA by beating Valachi for the belt. Then, I'm going to come back to the CWAA where I can get some real competition for the title! If the AEWA wants the belt back, then they are going to have to come and get it from me. DM: His opponent, from Philadelphia, PA, weighing 245 pounds, "Hardcore" Joey Haven! (AC/DC's "Highway to Hell" plays as Haven, also bandaged and taped after last week's match, enters to a huge pop.) DM: Your referee for this contest, Jeff Howard. EL: Well, this should be interesting, as Joey Haven has to walk by Jak Hammer to get to the ring. Jak Hammer stands back, but he and Hammer never take their eyes off one another as Haven backs into the ring- big mistake, as Marc Thomas is more than happy to start the action when Haven is not looking- went to the Major Mayhem school of ethics, I suppose. Marc Thomas with a double sledge. He picks Haven up- hip toss, into an arm bar. Good leverage advantage for Thomas as he is on his feet, Haven on the mat. Thomas has one big advantage in this encounter- Joey Haven was in a very, very grueling steel cage match up last week right here, plus he has to be looking at the AEWA Extreme matchup with Jak Hammer. Now Thomas wraps a leg around Haven's extended arm, and rolls back, applying more pressure. Marc Thomas has tasted gold before, as CCWF Canadien Champion- Joey Valachi has been the ACWF Canadien champ as well, quite coincidentally- but Thomas, along with Ron Stern, has also been a CCWF Tag Team Champion. As far as I know, next week, should he win tonight, would be Joey Haven's first real chance at a title of any kind. OK- Joey Haven hooks one arm around the middle rope, and Thomas has to release the hold. Both men circle one another warily, with the stakes this high, neither one wants to be too rash. (Jak Hammer walks around the ringside area, glaring at Haven the whole time) EL: Collar and elbow tie up, and Thomas takes him over in a fireman's carry, into another armbar. Joey Haven reverses it, and Marc Thomas with a rake of the eyes! That'll break the hold. Jeff Howard warning Marc Thomas- and Thomas just shrugs! Thomas now with a European uppercut. Now a punch- right on the laceration Haven suffered last week! He has stitches up their, for cripe's sake! Marc Thomas now with a legsweep... 1... 2... kickout! Thomas takes Haven back up- Irish whip- knee, right in the midsection. Now Thomas locks him in an abdominal stretch. He has the leg grapevined, the hold applied very expertly. (Jak Hammer starts taunting Haven, telling him to quit now and stop wasting everyone's time, etc.) EL: Well, I don't think "quit" is in Joey Haven's vocabulary. He's fighting this hold. He pulls and strains, and- yes! He hiptosses his way out of it! (Big pop- yes, the Dallas marks pop for a hiptoss on occasion!) EL: Joey Haven drop-kicks Thomas as he gets up- now an Irish whip- Hotshot! Hotshot by Joey Haven! The cover... 1... 2... kickout! Now Haven with a gutwrench suplex! Bounces off the ropes- kneedrop. The cover... 1... 2.... kickout by Marc Thomas. Big right hand by Joey Haven drops Thomas, and now he is going up top- what's coming next? Flying clothesline! And he stuck him with it! Haven pulls him back up, drags him to the ropes. Joey Haven up on the middle rope, and he lifts Thomas into the powerbomb position- Joey Bomb coming up! Jak Hammer up on the ring apron- and Joey Haven drops Thomas and heads across the ring toward Hammer- I don't like this. Haven with a big right hand, and Hammer staggers. Haven nails him again! He turns around- Icebreaker! Marc Thomas was waiting on him- and he nailed that Icebreaker! The cover... 1... 2... 3! He got him! Marc Thomas over "Hardcore" Joey Haven, and Jak Hammer is on his way to the lockers- I guess his job is done for the night. ("Life in the Fast Lane" plays again) DM: Here is you winner, Marc Thom- (Myers heads for the hills as Joey Haven drags a chair in the ring and chases Thomas, who slides out of the ring just before the chair strikes the ropes.) EL: Marc Thomas gets what he has wanted, even before he showed up here in the CWAA: a chance to wrestle for the AEWA World Light Heavyweight Championship. And with each passing week, it becomes more and more obvious that his Icebreaker can come at any time from any position, and Thomas has very quickly become a very dangerous man here in the CWAA. When we get back, we're going to see, speaking of dangerous men, Max "The Professional" Robertson and "The Dreamer" Randy Sheradin, wrestle for the CWAA Heavyweight Championship! (New, from the Franklin Mint, our series of plates commemorating the Presidents of the reconstruction era... Plates are not guaranteed to increase in value, but our series of Signers of the Treaties of Utrecht and Westphalia recently resold for $500...) DM: This bout is scheduled for one fall and television time remaining. It is for the CWAA Heavyweight Championship. First, from San Francisco, CA, weighing in at 200 pounds, here is "The Professional" Max Robertson! (As the shaved-headed Professional ambles down the aisle to Darth Vader's theme, the crowd boos, hisses, and makes other noises in their endeavors to convey a sense of disapproval. As he enters the ring, takes the microphone from David Myers' hand.) MR: Joshua Barnes! I have something to say to you, Joshua Barnes, and I want to say it one on one, man to man! I don't want to talk to your little friend, Mr. Sheridan. This is for you, and only you! So come on out! Unless you're afraid to face me by yourself! (Joshua Barnes stands in the aisleway) EL: Little Joshy , your ignorance astounds me! You said last week that you know I can outwrestle you, so you just want to beat me up. Well, son, you see wrestling is what we do here. . . That's why we call it _professional_ wrestling. I don't even know how you got into this federation. The whole thing seems to be a little beyond your comprehension. Why don't you go back to your little bar, and your hick town and your red-neck friends and save us all a lot of headaches. Besides, nobody cares about you anyway. Sheridan is afraid to stay in single action, so he needs a partner. One that's even stupider than he is. You're too stupid to even understand that it's Sheridan that I'm after. I couldn't care about you less! (A security guard emerges from the locker room and taps Barnes on the shoulder. Barnes turns around and follows him back.) EL: I understand we're going to see what is going on back there. (The camera man heads up the aisle and into the entrance to the locker area, where Lt. Lawson and Major Mayhem are seen fleeing, Randy Sheradin on the ground holding his right shoulder, and Joshua Barnes kicking lockers.) EL: OK- I understand we are going to take a quick break to give Randy time to get himself together. When we get back- the CWAA Heavyweight Title is on the line! (Would you like to make more money? Sure, we all would....) EL: OK, Max Robertson is still in the ring, waiting. I'm not sure what happened- it looked as thought Lt. Lawson and Major Mayhem had ambushed Randy Sheradin while Max Robertson was berating Joshua Barnes. Why they would do such a thing, I don't know. Jealousy for last week's result? Let's go back to David Myers for the rest of the introduction. (The Aerosmith/Philadelphia Philharmonic version of "Dream On" plays as Sheradin and Barnes come to the ring to an enormous pop) DM: Coming to the ring now, accompanied by Joshua Barnes, from Des Moines, Iowa, weighing 170 pounds "The Dreamer" Randy Sheradin! Your referee for this contest, Jeff Howard. EL: OK- this is what it has been about from the very beginning. When the CWAA debuted, these men faced off in the main event. Ever since, there has been reconciliation, betrayal and threats, all leading up to this one event: the finals of the CWAA Heavyweight Tournament. Kore Wrench, Joshua Barnes, and, now Lt. Lawson, have all been drawn into the conflict, but now it is down to two competitors. These two men have been inextricably bound together for the last two months, and familiarity has not bred contempt, it has bred hatred. At the end of the night, for one man, the past two months will have been a road leading to a dead end. For the other, posterity and gold beckon. There's the bell, and this one is underway. Lockup- Max Robertson with a side headlock on Sheradin, who slips out and into a hammerlock on the Professional. Robertson ducks and spins, and reverses the hammerlock, applying the pressure to the arm Sheradin was nursing in the locker room. The Dreamer drops to one knee, and Lawson must have done something to soften that arm up. Sheradin reaches up with his left hand- snap mares the Professional over and hooks him in a reverse chinlock, but Robertson grabs the bottom rope, and referee Jeff Howard calls for a break. Both men back up in midring. Lockup- Robertson whips Sheradin into the ropes- shoulderblock! Sheradin drops to the mat, and Robertson with an elbow drop- missed! Sheradin out of harm's way- comes back with a European uppercut with his left arm- Irish whip- leapfrog- Robertson off the ropes- into a savate kick! (Big pop as the Professional hits the mat) EL: Monkey flip by the Dreamer! Now a dropkick, and Robertson slides out of the ring for safety- and right in front of Joshua Barnes, who cocks a fist, as Robertson slips to the floor trying to change direction. Sheradin out on the floor waiting for him to get back up- savate kick! (Big pop again) EL: Things going very badly for the Professional right now, as Sheradin rolls him back into the ring. Randy Sheradin off the ropes- cartwheel kick! He covers... 1... 2... kickout! Sheradin with a headlock takedown now. Robertson rolls him back... 1... 2..., Sheradin leans forward again, still holding that headlock. Robertson rolls him back again... 1... Sheradin rolls forward again. Robertson back on his feet, but still caught in that headlock- Robertson stands up- back suplex! Now he's back up- stomping away at Sheradin! He pulls him up, stretches Sheradin's right arm over the bottom rope, and tugs down. He has definitely targeted the same arm as Lawson- I can only speculate, but they may well have been in cahoots. Robertson whips Sheradin into the corner- follows him in- handspring elbow! Sheradin drops to the mat, and Robertson pounces on him with a hammerlock, going back to that right arm. Jeff Howard checking for the submission. (Guess what: "Go, Randy, Go!") EL: Robertson stands Sheradin back up in that Hammerlock- picks him up- back suplex! Bridges... 1... 2... kickout. Robertson back up- waits on Sheradin- superkick! Lateral press... 1... 2... kickout! Robertson now with the Irish whip- spin kick! Now he hooks his arm around Sheradin's shoulders- legsweep! Cover... 1... 2... kickout! And here come Lt. Lawson and Major Mayhem down the aisle! Robertson tosses Sheradin out through the ropes to the floor and heads over to the side of the ring where Joshua Barnes is. I can't quite make it out from here, but he's barking at Barnes about something- and Lt. Lawson just slammed Sheradin's head onto the ring apron. Now he stands the Dreamer against the ring- open handed chop across the chest. Referee Jeff Howard is trying to keep Robertson in the ring and Barnes out- but he doesn't see what's going on between Lawson and Sheradin on the other side of the ring. Lawson takes that right arm and slams it against the ring post. Robertson and Lawson have obviously worked this out beforehand- Lawson rolls Sheradin back into the ring, and the Major calls to Robertson, who abandons his heckling of Joshua Barnes and stands Randy Sheradin up- double chickenwing submission! Robertson leans back to pull Sheradin off the floor- we've seen Randy in this hold before, and he couldn't shake free. Referee Jeff Howard checking for the submission, as Sheradin kicks his legs- he keeps kicking- he reaches the mat and yanks his whole body forward- Robertson is carried forward by the momentum- his head hits the middle turnbuckle! (pop) EL: The impact breaks the hold as Robertson staggers back! Sheradin on his feet, sweat pouring down his face- savate kicks flattens the groggy Professional! Sheradin climbing to the top rope- and Lt. Lawson up on the apron- Barnes pulls him back down and slams a big meat hook right hand in the mush, and Lawson is laid out with one blow! Randy Sheradin off the top rope- the Dream Come True! (i.e., Shooting Star Press) EL: The cover- hooks the legs- 1... 2... 3! He got him! He got it! (Pop unlike any heard in the first two months of CWAA action) ("Dream On" plays again as Joshua Barnes grabs the title belt and rolls into the ring to present it to Randy Sheradin) DM: Your winner, and NEW CWAA HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, "The Dreamer" Randy Sheradin! (Popping continues) EL: Randy Sheradin is the first-ever CWAA Heavyweight Champion, with a stunning win over Max "The Professional" Robertson in this grueling contest. Joshua Barnes presents the belt to his partner- starting next week, they go for the tag team titles, too. And- hey! Max Robertson with a running knee from behind on Joshua Barnes and tosses him to the floor. Now Robertson with a punch for the stunned Sheradin- Irish whip- spin kick! Max should change his name to "The Unprofessional" for this! Lt. Lawson back on his feet and he holds back Joshua Barnes while Robertson slaps the chickenwing submission on Sheradin! The bell is ringing, Sheradin stuck in that chickenwing, Lawson tying up Barnes- and here come the Preacher, Twister and Stan Stevens, and Robertson beats it out of the ring, and Lawson follows. Now Robertson raising his arms as a gesture of victory, but the record book tells a different story. Randy Sheradin is your new CWAA Heavyweight Champion, and next week, we'll have the first round of the CWAA Tag Team Title Tournament, as the New Frontier World Champions, the Trailer Park Assassins, take on McNultey and Anderson, Joshua Barnes and Randy Sheradin take on Double Jeopardy, Twister squares off against Lethal Injection, and Midnight Rampage meets the High School Toughies. And if that weren't enough, our special feature match has AEWA World Light Heavyweight Champion "Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi, defending his title against Marc Thomas. We'll see you next weeke, everyone! -kl
