Sunday, April 20th, Card (2/3)

20 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Monday Night at 11, here on WYXZ TV 39, join the Fonz and all the "Happy Days" gang as they visit a dude ranch. Are Richie and Fonzie competing for the same girl? Find out Monday at 11, right here on WYXZ TV 39!) EL: OK- let's go to the ring where David Myers will introduce our new CWAA Heavyweight Champion. David Myers: Ladies and gentlemen, my guest at this time is the new CWAA Heavyweight Champion, "The Professional" Max Robertson! (The stunned crowd unleashes a torrent of boos as the "Imperial March" of Darth Vader plays and the 5'11"-200 lb. San Francisco native with his clean-shaven head, CWAA Title belt, and street clothes enters the ring.) DM: Max Robertson, you have the unique opportunity tonight of leaving the Sportatorium as both CWAA Heavyweight Champion and CWAA Television Champion- Max Robertson: No. No, I don't. DM: What do you mean? You are signed to meet Lt. Lawson for the CWAA Television Championship this evening, aren't you? MR: Not any more. You see, the Television Title is small change. As CWAA Heavyweight Champion, I don't need the added prestige of a lesser title. There's no need for me to compete for it now. <shrugs> But I understand that next week, the AEWA World Heavyweight Champion is going to be here. DM: Well, now it's Heavy Metal, not Crimson Joe Reed. MR: Metal, Reed, Slocum, Crowley- it doesn't matter. The AEWA World Champion has to come here, and as CWAA Heavyweight Champion, the right to contend for that title falls to me. I see no reason to endanger my chance to become World Champion by contending for the television, nice little belt though it is. Looking after the big picture- that's the Professional way. So you can go in the back and find whomever you want to face Lawson- I don't know that anyone in this arena except me could defeat him anyway. I'd love to stay and chat, but I have to go meet with some very important people from the AEWA just now. (Exit MR to another round of boos.) EL: Before we get to our first contest, let's take a look at this video of "The Preacher" Kore Wrench. *************************************** (Cut to black - the only thing visible is the white glowing cross of the Preacher's Parish. The lights go up to reveal "The Preacher" Kore Wrench standing in front of the cross in his original, standard preacher's outfit.) KW: Marc Thomas! Was that your idea of a joke? To attack me and to interrupt my title match with Lt. Lawson? Well, I don't like jokes very much. You will pay for your interference when next we meet. And Jak Hammer! You are equally responsible for my failure to win the Television Title. But I will give the option: Either apologize, or face the wrath of The Preacher. The choice is yours. I suggest you make it wisely. *************************************************************** ( "Life in the Fast Lane" plays and the 6'0"-240 lb. black-leather-clad Marc Thomas, followed by the 6'10"-310 lb. similarly-dressed Jak Hammer, and the booing resumes.) EL: All right- I see Marc Thomas, again accompanied by Jak Hammer, as we get set for our first contest, and this one could be a main event in any arena in the country- Marc Thomas and "Preacher" Kore Wrench! And, well, it looks like they want a few minutes of airtime first. Marc Thomas, how do you view this matchup with Kore Wrench? Marc Thomas: Just like any other match I have. Kore is a big man with some quickness, but that doesn't mean he can't be beat. I've faced him before and he is a tough opponent, but I think the better wrestler will win tonight....and that's me. Wrench, you and your goody-goody actions don't mean squat to me. They may appeal to some of the fans, but being popular only wins high school elections, not wrestling matches. You better pray to all of your gods tonight that they will have mercy on you because once you step into the ring I WON'T!!!! EL: OK, Mr. Hammer, what do you know about your opponent tonight, Little Yvette? JH: Sounds like a young girl to me. I don't really give a d*** who this person or thing is, I get paid to go into the ring and beat the living crap out of my opponent. That is how I earn my living! Not being friendly with the fans or talking nice about my opponent. I am a serious man engaged in a serious business. I don't know what this person was thinking when they signed a match with me, but I know that thinking will be the least of their worries after our match is over. Yvette, go ask Joey Haven what I am capable of doing. (MT hops onto the ring apron and Jak Hammer stands by the ring steps with his arms crossed across his body) EL: Let's go to David Myers for the introductions. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this first contest is set for one fall with a fifteen-minute time limit. Introducing first, from Charlotte, North Carolina, at 240 lb., Marc Thomas! (The crowd reaction is easily predicted) DM: And his opponent, from New York, New York- (Gregorian Chant plays while the 6'4"-275 lb. priestly-garbed Wrench appears) DM: - weighing in at 275 lb.- "The Preacher" Kore Wrench! (The crowd pops just as loudly as they had been booing a minute ago) DM: Your referee, Jeff Howard. EL: OK, everyone, Senior Official Jeff Howard should have his hands full with this one, with Jak Hammer apparently determined to stay here at ringside for this one, as two of the top stars in the CWAA hook up, and- well, now where is he going? (As soon as Wrench sees Hammer, he turns around and walks back up the aisle) EL: Kore Wrench has just returned to the locker room- I don't know what it is that he has planned- (The crowd pops as he returns, accompanied by a never-before-seen-here youngster of the same dimensions as Thomas, with clearly defined musculature in blue and white tights bearing the name "Little Yvette" and the image of an eagle in flight.) EL: Well, the Preacher has found himself an equalizer- Little Yvette, who makes his debut against Jak Hammer a few minutes after this contest, out here to even the odds for the Preacher, and Marc Thomas and Jak Hammer are not happy about this- not one bit. Thomas reading the riot act to senior official Jeff Howard, who's heard it all before, I'm sure- and the Preacher wastes no time in rolling Thomas up from behind! 1... 2... kickout! Jak Hammer made a move for the ring, but Little Yvette has parked himself right in front of the big motor-city madman. OK- Thomas and Wrench in there right now, and Thomas throws a big right hand- Wrench ducks and hits him with an uppercut on the chin- and a second- now a right to the body- and a left- and a right! (Big pops) EL: And Marc Thomas goes for a walk on the outside, and Wrench right out after him! The chase is on- and Thomas rolls back in- and he stomps the Preacher as he follows him back into the ring. Thomas with an elbow drop now, and now he sets a half-Boston crab, pulling on the Preacher's leg. Little Yvette pounding the ring apron to rally the Preacher. OK, and Thomas releases the hold, and stomps once more on that thigh area. Stands him up- snap mare takedown. Now an elbow smash on top of the head, and Marc Thomas in clear control here early on. You know, there are some who suggest that Marc Thomas may well be the best all-around wrestler, top to bottom, in the CWAA right now- it was in the first round of the title tourney that eventual champion Randy Sheridan managed to squeak by him, and a few men like Sheridan may be better high flyers, and Lawson and Robertson may be better on the mat, but a lot of people think Thomas is more the complete wrestler. We'll see how he handles the powerful Preacher. Thomas with an Irish whip- and smacks a knee into the midsection, and Kore Wrench goes back down. Thomas with the cover...1 ... kickout. You're not going to beat Kore Wrench that easily. (Little Yvette starts pounding on the ring apron again, and Jak Hammer taps on the shoulder and warns him to keep quiet) EL: Marc Thomas backs Kore Wrench into the corner now- and a forearm uppercut by the Preacher! Wrench spins him around- big open hand shot across the chest! (Pop) EL: And Thomas now goes to the eyes- I don't care how big and strong you are, everyone is vulnerable there. Now Thomas slams his head onto the turnbuckle. Thomas choking him against that top rope- a blatant breaking of the rules, and Jeff Howard giving him a 5-count. Thomas stands Wrench up in the corner- spin kick. Now he tosses Wrench to the mat- lateral press- 1... 2... kickout. Thomas back up- fistdrop- and he connected right between the eyes on that one. Now he's stomping away at those legs again- stands him up- dropkick to the knees! And the Preacher is felled like an oak. And Marc Thomas taunting Little Yvette- looks like he said "You're next," or something to that effect. And Jak Hammer grinning ear to ear on the outside. Now Thomas stands Wrench back up- sets him up- swinging neckbr- no! Small package! 1... 2... kickout! Kore Wrench nearly pulled this one right out of a hat! Thomas swings- blocked! Kore Wrench with a big right hand- Irish whip- clothesline! (Marc Thomas flips all the way around and lands on his back as the crows pops) EL: Irish whip again- he caught him- Kore Wrench with a powerslam! 1... 2... kickout! Jak Hammer shoves Little Yvette aside and hops up on the ring apron- Kore Wrench heads his way, senior official Jeff Howard gets between them- and here comes Marc Thomas- he leaps- Wrench ducks! Marc Thomas just nailed Jak Hammer! Hammer slumps the rign apron- Thomas falls back into mid ring- I think they knocked heads! Kore Wrench picks him up- Crucifix Slam! (Razor's Edge) He hit it- Jak Hammer trying to get in the ring- Little Yvette has him by the tights- 1... 2... 3! Kore Wrench got him! (Gregorian Chant plays again while Marc Thomas tries to shake the pain out his head and Jak Hammer protests the decision) DM: Here is your winner, "The Preacher" Kore Wrench! (Big time popping going on here, folks) EL: We're going to take a break now, folks, and when we get back, Jak Hammer and Little Yvette step into that ring- don't touch that dial! (If you've been in an accident, how can you be sure you're getting all the compensation that you deserve? Call Shylock and Shylock, Attorneys at Law, for information on how you can make sure that you get what's coming to you. Shylock and Shylock, [detail withheld], providing quality legal service for over 3 months, never indicted.) EL: Well, we're back, and the participants for this match have already been out at ringside for a good ten minutes, now. While we have a second, I'd like to get a few words with Little Yvette... (Little Yvette heads over to Eric Lane) Little Yvette: Thanks, well I just wanted to introduce myself to all you great CWAA fans out there. My name's Little Yvette, I may be small but folks I give it my all every time I step into that ring and I hope that during all my matches no matter what the outcome is you folks are entertained. This is my pro-wrestling debut, and I'm hopefully here to stay. I'm ready for my first test, my father has taught me many things and tonight I'm going to see if I can cut it in the big time. I am confident I can do it and I'll give it my best shot. So bring out this Jak Hammer and we'll hopefully wrestle a good clean match. Thanks again. (He heads back to the ring) EL: Let's go to David Myers for the introductions. DM: This match is set for one fall with a fifteen-minute time limit. In the ring to my left, from Detroit, Michigan, at 310 lb., Jak Hammer! (Many, many boos, as Hammer glowers at the crowd) DM: And his opponent, hailing from Seattle, Washington, at 235 lb., Little Yvette! (The fans cheer as Yvette raises his hand and Wrench stands behind him, applauding) DM: Your referee, Chris Herman. EL: OK, a second generation wrestler in there now, Little Yvette, son of Canadien legend Ron Yvette, making his debut against the big bull, Jak Hammer. Well, it looks like we're ready to start, but Chris Herman is holding things up. Now he's asking for David Myers to return to the ring. Let's listen to what's going on. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, referee Chris Herman has informed me that if Marc Thomas and Kore Wrench remain at ringside, this bout will be declared a double disqualification! (A lot of crowd noise, some positive, some negative. Kore Wrench nods, and offers Little Yvette a slap on the back as he hops to the floor. Marc Thomas shows more reluctance, and Jak Hammer's treatment of Chris Herman is more or less the same that Thomas gave to Jeff Howard.) EL: Senior official Jeff Howard out there to lend a hand, and it looks like he'll escort Thomas and Wrench back to the locker area. Hammer and Thomas putting up a big stunk about it, as Wrench leaves. Jeff Howard and Chris Herman arguing with Thomas and Hammer about it. Chris Herman now takes Little Yvette by the wrist- he's threatening to raise his hand right now. (Jak Hammer immediately stops barking and goes into contrition-mode, pleading with the officials to let the match go on, and telling Thomas to go ahead and leave. As a reluctant Thomas acquiesces, the crowd starts chanting the "nah-nah-nah-nah" chorus to "Kiss Him Goodbye" as Thomas leaves- yes, the Sportatorium crowd has learned a second chant!) EL: OK- we've cut it down to Little Yvette and Jak Hammer in there now. Jak Hammer now, with about a one-foot height advantage and almost 75 lb. on his foe, calling for a test of strength. Yvette would be foolish to try him- Hammer holds both hands up for Little Yvette to grip- he can't reach those! And Little Yvette greets him with a dropkick in the mush! (Big pop as Jak Hammer flies backward into the corner) EL: Yvette catches him out of the corner- cross corner hip- in after him- monkey flip back to midring! Now Little Yvette up on his shoulders- rolls down- Victory roll! 1... 2... kickout! (Crowd groans) EL: Both men back up, and Jak Hammer brings a big forearm down on his smaller foe. Now a double sledge in the back, and he is beating Little Yvette down to the mat, it looks like. Whips him into the corner, and now Jak Hammer wraps those big hands around Little Yvette's throat, Chris Herman giving him a 5-count to break the hold- and Jak Hammer goes right back to the choke! Now Hammer turns him around in the corner, and another double sledge into the back. Jak Hammer with an Irish whip- boot to the midsection. He is just manhandling the rookie, who had come out of the gate pretty quickly. Hammer now grabs Little Yvette and just flings out through the ropes, and now he's out there after him- familiar ground for Jak Hammer, who never met a rule he didn't break. He slams Little Yvette's head onto the ring apron. Welcome to pro-wrestling, Little Yvette! Now Hammer has him in a sort of bearhug- and he runs him right into the side of the ring! He rolls Yvette under the bottom rope, (Big heel pop as Hammer pauses to raise his arms in triumph) EL: Jak Hammer taking his time getting back in there now. As he comes through the ropes, it's Little Yvette with a running kneelift! Irish whip by Yvette- leaping clothesline takes the big man down! Little Yvette now up to the top rope- he stands there on that top turnbuckle- Jak Hammer on his feet- he has no idea where Little Yvette is now- he turns around, and like an eagle descending on a field mouse, Little Yvette with a flying clothesline! (Big pop- top rope moves are very, very rare around here, despite the proximity to the Mexican border) EL: Little Yvette in control here against the much bigger foe, and he looks very good for a newcomer- it must be in the genes. Yvette now with a camel clutch, slowing things down, maybe getting his breath back while weakening his much more powerful opponent. Well, last week, we saw Jak Hammer out here lobbying for a TV title match, and then he and Marc Thomas tried to force the issue- Little Yvette releases the hold and goes to an armbar- and now Lt. Lawson is supposed to defend that title against Max Robertson this week. In case you just joined us, Max Robertson is the new CWAA Heavyweight Champion, and he came out here this evening and basically told us he had better things to do than worry about the TV Title now that he was cock-of-the-walk around here. Jak Hammer now with a handful of ropes, and Chris Herman calling for the break. Jak Hammer now with perhaps a new-found respect for the skills of one Little Yvette, and he comes at Yvette- armdrag takedown, and Little Yvette goes right back to that armbar, twisting that arm. Hammer on his feet, but in obvious pain. He did not expect this kind of competition from the newcomer. Hammer reverses the armbar- re-reversed by Yvette! Now Little Yvette with the Irish whip- Hammer drops him with a shoulderblock. Hammer off the ropes again- another shoulderbock, and he's using the size advantage to regain momentum. We have to take a break- we'll have the conclusion of this contest when we get back- stay tuned!
