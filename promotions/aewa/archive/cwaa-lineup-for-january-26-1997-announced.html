Lineup for January 26, 1997 Announced

22 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This Sunday, the Championship Wrestling Association of America (a regional federation affilaited with the AEWA) debuts from the world-famous Sportatorium in Dallas, TX. The lineup is as follows: Joshua Barnes versus Healer's Havoc "The Preacher" Kore Wrench versus Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson Twister (Paul "Tornado" Langford and Travis Conley) versus (Well, I don't know whom they will face just yet, but they'll be there!) "Hardcore" Joey Haven versus Jak Hammer and in our feature match "The Dreamer" Randy Sheridan versus "The Professional" Max Roberston The CWAA is a small federation whose roster teems with rookies and other wrestlers of limited experience hoping to translate success in the CWAA into a career in the much bigger AEWA. The roster is kept very small to foster a regional or indy-fed feel, and the CWAA makes no pretensions about having national exposure, world title status, or a viable pay per view audience. It is a fed for experienced players to experiment with new character ideas or to try an build some momentum for existing characters who have flourished in other small, indy-style feds or who have found it difficult to leave their mark on the larger, high-profile organizations. For the present, enrollment is limited to handlers who are members of the AEWA. The CWAA will post no advertisements on the usenet until it has demonstrated an ability to produce a quality program on a timely basis. It incorporates some of the ideas and advice offered from a number of experienced players and from posters like Jacob Kane and Anthony Aidan Palmer (I hope I got that last name right!), and has been planned for months before beginning this weekend. It is my hope that the debut show and all following programs will live up to and even exceed the standards that it's players demand. -kl
