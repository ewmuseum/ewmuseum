AEWA — Card Archive

December Doom (12/15, part one)

22 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen, set on fire. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Rock music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... David Graves TAKING SOME GUY'S HEAD OFF with the Gravestone, Penance PLANTING some guy with the Final Judgment, The West Coast Connection DRIVING some guy's head into the mat with the West Coast Killa, Cardinal Chaos STICKING some poor soul with the Inverted Crucifix, Shane Matthews BRUTALLY hitting the End of the Road on some hapless jobber, and Heavy Metal notching yet ANOTHER casualty courtesy of the Megadeth Power Bomb. The camera then shifts to Matt Wilson, The Lovely Bertha, and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt in the PACKED San Jose Arena in California, the site of AEWA DECEMBER DOOM!) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to the first annual December Doom extravaganza! We have a HUGE card for you tonight, and in the main event, Shane Matthews will be defending his World Heavyweight title against the "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum! BD: This is going to be a GREAT show, folks. We've got plenty of awesome matches scheduled, and I'm ready to rumble! LB: Personally, I really want to see Shane kick Slocum's ass tonight. Those two guys have NEVER liked each other-- now they're FINALLY going to settle the score. MW: Don't get your hopes up, Bertha. Ben Slocum has held the World title before, and he plans on recapturing it tonight. It should be an INCREDIBLE bout. BD: But what's first? MW: It's now time for a special edition of the Terrordome! Johnny Terror is about to interview Miss DeMeanor and Graphic Violence.... BD: Johnny has been quite the playboy lately, hasn't he? Did you see him flirting with DeMeanor earlier today on the Countdown show? LB: (chuckles) Yeah, I saw that. They make a cute couple. MW: But just IMAGINE what their kids would look like. (shudders) Now that would put the "Terror" back in the Terrordome. BD: Quit your babbling, Wilson; we've got a SHOW to run here! I can hear Johnny's music playing, so let's just sit back and watch.... (Public Enemy's "Welcome to the Terrordome" plays loudly throughout the arena as the camera shifts to a familiar set near the back of the arena. Hanging over this set is a large half-dome. Underneath the dome, there is a very dark, dank room FILLED with various gruesome looking torture devices. AEWA manager Johnny Terror emerges from behind the curtain, and proceeds toward the Terrordome set, all the while being booed heavily. Johnny yells back some inaudible, but definitely crude, comments. He gets to the set and picks up the microphone.) Terror: Hello, and welcome once again to the happiest place on earth, THE TERRORDOME! I am the manager of the AEWA World Heavyweight Champion "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews, and I'm also the star of the Terrordome, Johnny Terror! (LOUD BOOS) Shaddup! You know, there's a lot of BIG matches coming up tonight, and my guests tonight are part of one of them. They have been terrorizing the Machines for WEEKS, and tonight they will take them on in a Street Fight for the National Tag Team Titles! And they're gonna win, too. So without any further ado, here they are, along with my favorite gal in the world, the BEAUTIFUL Miss DeMeanor, Booze Murphy and Leech Rogers, the team of GRAPHIC VIOLENCE!! ("Here Comes the Hotstepper" by Ini Kamoze is played as Miss DeMeanor leads Graphic Violence to the stage. They are wearing football jerseys with their names on the back.) Miss DeMeanor: Johnny, it's nice to see you once again. Booze: Yo, what's up, Johnny Boy! You forgot to mention that we's in the LaValle Cup, too! Leech: Yeah, and we's about to whoop some good ole ASS!! Miss DeMeanor: Boys, I don't think Johnny wants you cussing on his show. It's a show with class. Terror: (Johnny kisses Miss DeMeanor on the hand to greet her, as always) Don't worry about me darlin', I'm used to it from hangin' around Shane all the time. Now I'm sure you boys will do a great job in the LaValle Cup tournament, but let's talk about what's going down tonight. We're here to talk about the AEWA, and specifically, the Machines. Now you have been feuding with them for quite some time, and you even lit Paul Wong's CAR on fire, which I thought was GREAT, by the way, but back to the point. Why do you guys hate them so much? Booze: Well it's all about dis-- they have da titles and we wants them! Plain and simple, homeboy. Leech: We saw how WONG loves his car and, you know, it just came to our mind; burn the stupid mudder f*dder! Booze: It's not like it was the first time we have done that! Terror: So you just burn somebody's car to get their attention and get a title shot? VERY sneaky, I like it. But anyway, let's talk about the matchup, you and the Machines have COMPLETELY different styles of wrestling. How do you see yourselves matching up against them? Booze: You know, in dis match, styles don't mean sh*t 'cause it's an all-out war! Miss DeMeanor: That's right, there aren't any disqualifications, and that's like giving OJ Simpson a sharp object and looking the other way. Graphic Violence are going to ANNIHILATE the Machines! Leech: Wong and Simon.... that sounds like a bad rock 'n' roll band. Listen up, we ain't coming to play games-- we're coming to hurt you, make you bleed, break some bones, and take your titles! Terror: They're probably cowering in fear in the locker room right now. But recently, Paul Wong has decided to throw out the rulebook when it comes to you guys. You've really gotten under his skin. And we all know what Simon's capable of. Add that to the fact that if THEY win, they've said they're going to torch YOUR car, Miss D. That could add up to a pretty dangerous situation. What are your thoughts regarding that? Miss DeMeanor: You forgot something, Johnny. It's not MY car, it's Primal Fear's car! HA HA HA!! Also, if the car is ruined, then I'll just go buy another one with more of Primal's money! Leech: Don't worry, Miss D, Booze and I ain't going to lose to those two wannabe hardcore wrestlers! Terror: I'm sure you're not, but I gotta bring up the possibility, or I wouldn't be such a great broadcast journalist. So now, what are your plans beyond tonight's match? The Machines are in the LaValle Cup Tournament, too, ya know, so you may even have to go through them AGAIN. Comments? Booze: Well, we already beat our first round opponents and The Machines are in a different pool then we are, but if it comes to us two teams in da final, the LaValle Cup officials better have extra security on hand! Terror: Yes, I've already seen the tape, you boys handled Canadian Carnage rather easily. I'm sure you'll be in the finals, but I doubt that the Machines will make it there with you. I don't even see them getting past the Dream Police in the first round, they're pretty much toast. Hell, they might not even make it there after tonight, am I right guys? Booze: Exactly Johnny! You see, Graphic Violence is one of da most underrated tag teams in the league, but when it's time for an ass kicking, you'll be sure that we are the ones handing them out! Leech: That's right, just like growin' up in da hood!! Ass kickings are a part of everyday life! Machines, boys, you'd better be ready for a fight because there is going to be a WAR tonight! Terror: That's for sure. And there's gonna be a BIG victory party afterwards, celebrating your big victory over the Machines, and Shane's victory over Ben Choke'um. It's gonna be a WILD bash. Speaking of that party, are we still on for....uh..... you know what, after the party, Miss D? (Miss DeMeanor gives Johnny a kiss on the cheek) Miss DeMeanor: You bet, big boy! Terror: (Johnny looks like he's just about to faint.) That was nice, REAL nice. I can't WAIT 'till later! But then again, they always say that the best things come to those who wait, and I'm SURE that will be the case tonight. Well, that's our show for this week, folks. The Machines had better watch out because these guys are coming for those National tag titles! Don't miss my next show, when my guests will be The Shadow Warriors! But for now, I'm outta here. Later, morons! (Johnny grabs Miss DeMeanor by the arm and escorts her back to the locker room as Graphic Violence follows. The camera returns to the broadcast booth.) MW: Later tonight, they'll finally have to back up their words. Personally, I'm getting REALLY tired of listening to those idiots. BD: What? You don't like Graphic Violence? That's BLASPHEMY! They're going to become the National tag team champions tonight; maybe THAT will change your tune. MW: Or maybe not. LB: Well anyway, is it time for the first match yet? Enough of this talking, I want to see some damn ACTION! MW: Well, Penance is about to defend his Light Heavyweight championship against "Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi! I've really been looking forward to this match.... BD: The ICU only has one championship right now, and Penance is about to lose it. He just doesn't have enough experience for a big match like this. LB: Valachi is going to absolutely DESTROY Penance tonight. It'll be a complete massacre; mark my words. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall, and is for the AEWA Light Heavyweight title! Introducing first, the challenger, weighing in at 202 pounds, here is "THE CARDIAC KID" JOEY VALACHI! (Pat Benatar's "Heartbreaker" plays loudly as Valachi makes his way to the ring. He has a determined look on his face.) MW: "The Cardiac Kid" looks good! I don't like him very much, but I hope he beats Penance and those ICU thugs! LB: He's gonna ANNIHILATE Penance! That Light Heavyweight strap is finally coming back to the AEWA. BD: And Penance is on his way down the aisle now.... RING ANNOUNCER: Next, coming down the aisle, at 223 pounds, the AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion of the World, PENANCE! ("My Enemy" by Skid Row plays as Penance makes his way toward the ring. He is dressed totally in black, and holds the title high above his head. The audience showers him with boos.) MW: Penance looks really determined, folks. LB: Yes, he does, but I smell the end of the ICU's possession of the Light Heavyweight Belt! Valachi's gonna get the belt back tonight! MW: There's the bell, and we're underway. Penance quickly dives at Valachi, taking his legs out from under him! Valachi goes down hard! LB: No problem; Valachi is just getting warmed up. MW: Penance goes for a figure-four leglock, but Valachi counters with a kick! PENANCE FALLS OUT OF THE RING! BD: Penance went for that hold WAY too early! Valachi just kicked him right out of the ring! MW: Now Valachi runs, slides under the bottom rope, and NAILS Penance with a kick to the head! That had a LOT of impact. BD: Penance looks hurt.... MW: Valachi climbs out of the ring. He picks Penance up, AND DROPS HIM THROAT FIRST ON THE GUARDRAIL! BD: That could have broken Penance's neck! The referee is trying desperately to get some control now, but it might be too late. MW: Joey Valachi rolls Penance back into the ring now. He picks him up and executes a DEVASTATING sidewalk slam! Here's the cover.... One.......Two....Kickout! MW: Valachi kicks Penance in the stomach, and follows that up with a kick to the throat! Penance goes down hard! LB: Jeez, Penance's neck is taking a MAJOR beating! The ICU chiropractor is going to have some work to do. MW: Yeah, he sure will. Now Valachi whips Penance into the ropes, and hits him with a BIG backdrop! BD: He follows that up with an elbowdrop! MW: Penance is whipped into the corner, and Valachi lifts him up on the turnbuckle! He follows him up for a superplex, but Penance hits him with a HUGE elbow! LB: And Valachi flew off the top rope HARD! He landed right on the back of his head! MW: But Penance remains on the top rope! He leaps off, and NAILS Valachi with a moonsault! The cover.... One....Kickout! LB: Joey won't be pinned quite that easily. MW: Now Penance hits Valachi with a standing dropkick! He gets back up, and drops a leg on him.... BD: The match has really turned around! Penance is in COMPLETE control! MW: And Penance hits Valachi with a side suplex! He picks him up again, and NAILS him with a big uppercut! LB: Now Penance just SPITS on Valachi, trying to humiliate him even more. Jeez, this is really uncalled for. MW: But the ICU is trying to send a message, Bertha. Valachi would probably have done the same thing to Penance. LB: Well, THEN it would be okay. MW: You seem to be rather biased, but I don't feel like arguing. Now Penance takes Valachi over with a snap suplex, and he goes for another cover! One......Two...Kickout! BD: How does Valachi keep finding the strength to kick out? He's such a fighter! MW: Now Penance whips Valachi into the ropes-- Valachi comes back, AND GETS NAILED WITH A FRANKENSTEINER! LB: What a beautiful move by Penance! I hate to admit it, but he's looking GOOD! That was incredible! MW: And now Penance lifts Valachi over his head, AND THROWS HIM RIGHT OUT OF THE RING! BD: (yelling) Get up, Joey! This just isn't fair! MW: Don't get excited, Big Daddy. Penance quickly runs to the ropes, and jumps right out of the ring onto Valachi! He really HAMMERED him there! LB: Penance is a MASTER of aerial maneuvers like that. MW: Good point. Now Penance picks up the staggering Valachi, AND HITS HIM WITH A DDT ON THE CONCRETE FLOOR! LB: And Valachi is out cold! MW: Penance throws Valachi back into the ring, and follows him under the bottom rope. He gets back to his feet, and NAILS Valachi with a vertical suplex! Here's the cover.... One......Two.....Kickout! LB: Valachi still has SOME strength left in him; maybe he can pull this win off. He seems to be regaining his senses.... MW: Penance whips Valachi to the ropes, and as Valachi comes back, Penance hits him with a BIG clothesline! That was a DEVASTATING blow! LB: Joey flipped upside-down and landed right on his head! MW: AND PENANCE HITS VALACHI WITH THE "FINAL JUDGMENT" TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER RIGHT IN THE MIDDLE OF THE RING! It's over! He goes for the cover.... One.......Two........Three! (DING DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner, and still AEWA Light-Heavyweight Champion, PENANCE! MW: Look at this; Penance is still stomping away on Valachi! He just slapped him, and throws him right out of the ring! BD: Jeez, can ANYBODY stop the ICU? Penance seems to be the most dominant member of that entire group right now. LB: I don't like him much, but that "Final Judgment" piledriver is the best move in the business. Nobody has EVER gotten up from it. BD: So what's next, Wilson? MW: Red Dog is about to square off against Primal Fear, in what promises to be an action-packed match. LB: Red Dog has been hanging around with the Angels of Death lately, so that should give him the advantage. This should be an EASY match for him. MW: Don't be so sure, Bertha. He hasn't exactly been on a winning streak, and Primal Fear should NEVER be taken lightly. BD: That's for sure. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following bout is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, weighing in at 245 pounds, representing the Angels of Death, here is RED DOG! (Red Dog walks to the ring in an "Angels of Death" T-shirt. The fans boo him loudly, and a few even throw things at him. He doesn't seem to notice, though, and is literally foaming at the mouth.) LB: I love it! Look at Red Dog, he's covered in soda! MW: Red Dog has really felt the wrath of these fans here tonight. He hasn't been very popular since joining the Angels of Death. BD: These San Jose fans are just plain RUDE, Wilson. There's no need to throw things at a wrestler, no matter how much you hate him. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, at a weight of 360 pounds, here is PRIMAL FEAR! (Primal Fear walks down the aisle. He is dressed in a black vest, wearing brown tights. He has a determined look on his face, not even noticing the cheering crowd.) MW: Primal Fear has looked VERY good lately! He has sent quite a few opponents to the hospital with that Bump Splash of his! LB: Let's hope he doesn't do the same to Red Dog! MW: Red Dog isn't paying attention, and Primal Fear just took his head off with a BIG clothesline! Fear whips him to the ropes, and nails him with ANOTHER clothesline! BD: And Red Dog is ALREADY out cold! MW: Primal Fear gives the sign for the Bump Splash! AND HE NAILS RED DOG WITH IT! Here's the quick cover.... One.....Two.....Three! (DING DING) MW: What a short match, that lasted only 45 SECONDS! Jeez, can ANYONE survive the Bump Splash? What a DEVASTATING maneuver! RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner, PRIMAL FEAR! BD: We have some paramedics at ringside now, and Red Dog is being carried away on a stretcher. He appears to have a few cracked ribs-- this is really unfortunate. Primal Fear should be banned from the AEWA, because that damn Bump Splash is just TOO dangerous. LB: Red Dog appears to be in a LOT of pain right now. After getting attacked so quickly, he probably wasn't even READY. MW: (chuckles) Quit making excuses for him; he got his butt kicked. BD: Watch your mouth, Wilson.
