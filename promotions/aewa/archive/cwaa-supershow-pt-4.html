CWAA Supershow pt 4

3 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EL: OK, everyone, we are all set for the big $25,000 battle royale- 20 top stars, all in one ring, and the last man standing walks home $25,000 richer. As soon as this one is done, we'll get the six-man tag team championship decided as The Preacher and Joshua Barnes and Randy Sheradin- the Dream Team some of the fans have dubbed them- take on Max Robertson and the ACWF Tag Champions, Sgt. Sherrick and Lt. Lawson- should be a very exciting event. Then we'll be back tomorrow for more exciting championship wrestling- four titles on the line, as the ACWF Tag titles are up for grabs: Lawson and Sherrick versus Barnes and Sheradin. The war between Twister and Byron McNultey and Michael Anderson continues, and the CWAA Tag belts will be on the line. TV Champion Max Robertson defends against arch-rival Joshua Barnes. We'll also see the Freak here again taking on Elektro- what a matchup that one should be- then the biggest of them all, as AEWA World Heavyweight Champion Crimson Joe Reed and CWAA Heavyweight Champion Marc Thomas go best 2 out of 3, and after what happened last week, we know Reed is just itching to get that one started. OK, here comes the combatants for the big battle royale- let's take a quick listen to what Sgt. Sherrick, Marco Moura, and the Freak had to say about this event earlier today. *********************************************************** (the camera fades to the backstage area, and shown is Mark Sherrick, without Lawson or Mayhem. He is stretching out and preparing to wrestle. He spots the camera.) Mark Sherrick: Hey, maggot, over here. Yeah, I'm talkin' to you. This battle royal I am in, is just a formality. I'm gonna win the whole thing, and there ain't nothin' all the maggot-brained, slime-sucking civilian wrestlers out there can do about it. The only one that presents a challenge to myself is my own tag team partner! In simple words, I will win this battle royal, with no problem. Now get out of my face, you idiotic puke! (Sherrick grabs the cameraman and throws him out of the room) *************************************************************************** {The scene fades quickly to a darkened where a silhouette of a man is seen. His voice is unknown to the CWAA's fans, but it's full of sarcasm and madness...} Marco Moura: HHHUUUUMMMM!!!! CWAA, CWAA... Time to play has come... The time has come... HAHAHAHAHAHA!!! Maybe... I'll pay you a visit... ARRRRGGGGHHHH!!!! Hey, guys!! Bring me some lemons!! LEMON!! LEMONS!!! {We can see that these man grabs some lemons and start to eat them...} MM: Fose fings are deliciuz... *munch, munch* Do iu want zome? *gulp* Ahh... CWAA... The lemon are coming... Beware and bewarned... Cause I'm coming...with a Lemon-head... AHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! ******************************************************************** (the camera fades to Dave "The Freak" Johnson clad in his blue tights, and the ever familiar blue hair waving behind him. He notices the camera, and makes the cameraman come over, taunting him with something about being an alien lover) Dave Johnson: Hey, hey, looks like its time for the Freak to win yet again this weekend, and beat a whole bunch of people who don't know what's going on. We have a few people in that match that do know what's going on, and I will take care of those alien-haters first, and then I will take care of the coverup artists, and then the non-believers, and then I will end up winning the whole thing, and there isn't a damned thing you can do about it!! Now get outta my face, or I'll make sure you get close to knowing whatever is out there on Mars! *************************************************************************** EL: All right, the only way to be eliminated is to be thrown over the top rope to the floor- let's just run down the lineup: Brad Gorman, "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey, Casey Spahn, Cooter Lee Roy of the Trailer Park Assassins, Dave "the Freak" Johnson, Eddie Intellecta, Elektro, "Hotshot" Gary Jackson, Jimmy Dangerous, Joshua Barnes, "The Samurai" Kensei Tokugawa, "The Preacher" Kore Wrench, Little Yvette, Lt. Lawson, "Maniac" Marco Moura, "Marvelous" Michael Anderson, Orbit, Twister's "Tornado" Paul Langford, "The Dreamer" Randy Sheradin, and Sgt. Sherrick. OK- we're underway! Senior official Jeff Howard in the ring, and Chris Herman on the outside. (Some people appear to have immediate agendas, as Dangerous heads right for Tokugawa, who had assaulted him with a kendo stick last week, the Preacher went for Lawson, and the tag champs were busy trying avoid the Forces of Nature) EL: So much going on at once- Double Jeopardy double teaming Brad Gorman in one corner; Elektro grapples with Orbit; Sherrick nails the Preacher from behind as he had Lawson on the mat; Marco Moura, a newcomer, raking the eyes of the Dreamer- and now Barnes spins the Maniac around and pops him one; Casey Spahn smacks McNultey's head into the top turnbuckle; look out! Anderson just holding on as Langofrd nearly dropkicked him right out of there; the Freak has Cooter in a headlock. No managers at ringside for this one, all of these men have to fend for themselves. Brad Gorman has some good news and some bad news. The good news is he's in no immediate danger of being eliminated- the bad is that Hotshot Jackson and Eddie Intellecta are stomping away and choking him with those boots. (Sherrick knocks the Preacher to his knees with an elbowsmash on top of the head) EL: Joshua Barnes has the Maniac in trouble, but the Freak kicks Barnes in the back. Twister giving the champs a taste of what's to come tomorrow, and the Samurai has taken control against Jimmy Dangerous with a vicious chop. Now a thrust kick- he ducked! And the Samurai just kicked Michael Anderson, who was taking aim at Yvette, in the back of the head by accident, and the Marvelous One is gone! (pop) EL: Sherrick and Lawson double teaming the Preacher- and his new partner, the Dreamer to his rescue with a dropkick on the Sergeant! Keep an eye on Marco Moura- I don't know what just happened, but Barnes is on the mat, and Moura is sneaking up behind Orbit- and now he's trying to push him over- got him by the legs- but Cooter with a double axe handle to help his partner. The Freak picking up where Moura left off, chokes Barnes on that bottom rope. Joshua Barnes is going to need to be a real iron man, as he has three matches today and 2 tomorrow, off to a rough start. Speaking of rough starts, the Pretty Boy in danger of joining his partner, as Twister has him hanging on that top rope- and the Samurai with a kick in Langford's kidney- and now Jimmy Dangerous with a headscissor takedown on the Samurai- he hasn't forgotten what happened last week! And Yvette there to stomp the Samurai. (McNultey falls over the top rope, but lands on the apron and scurries back under the bottom rope) EL: Now Sheradin holds Lawson a back- and a big right jab by the Preacher! And another! (pop) EL: Kore Wrench and Randy Sheradin taking it to the former TV champion! He'll see him in a few minutes again for the six man tag title match. And Joshua Barnes now pounding away at Sherrick in the corner- and there goes Brad Gorman! Double Jeopardy knock him to the floor- and a little high-five, and- (pop) EL: And Orbit smacks their heads together- they didn't see him coming! The Pretty Boy with a few kicks in the midsection for Spahn as Paul Langford grapples in the corner with the Maniac. And Jimmy Dangerous has the Samurai over the top rope- one leg, anyway! Standing dropkick, and Kensei Tokugawa over- no! He hangs on to the middle rope and slides back in! Almost had him. Barnes and Wrench taking turns punching Sgt. Sherrick, as Lawson chops away at the Dreamer in the corner- Sheradin spins him around and fires back with chops of his own! And the Samurai with a chop on Sheradin from behind! Lawson back on his feet in the corner- and the Samurai with a thrust kick on Lawson- and out he goes! (pop) EL: And the crowd is on its feet, as one of the favorites, Lt. Lawson, is one of the first to go! And Kensei Tokugawa has eliminated 2 of the 3 men so far! Double Jeopardy going to work on Randy Sheradin now. And Jimmy Dangerous tied up with Marco Moura and Paul Langford. Look at the Pretty Boy, as Casey Spahn put the boots to him and he slides out under the bottom rope for a breather. 3 down, 16 to go, and Lawson, Gorman and Anderson will not walk out of here $25,000 in the black. We have to take another break, and when we get back, we'll have more of this exciting Battle Royal action for you! Stay tuned! (Sunday, August 24th, in Reunion Arena, Crimson Joe Reed faces Marc Thomas- champions collide with a 90-minute time limit- and pinning your man once is not enough! Two out of three falls, champion versus champion, right here on WYXZ-TV Channel 39!) EL: OK- we're back, and so is the Pretty Boy, who's done taking his walk. A quick look around the ring now- Eddie Intellecta and Gary Jackson stomping the Dreamer, Barnes and Wrench working over Sherrick, Spahn now getting his face raked by the Pretty Boy, the Maniac on Jimmy Dangerous, the TPAs taking it to Paul Langford, and Elektro and Yvette have the Freak up against the ropes, while Tokugawa standing off on his own. All right- Byron McNultey with an armful of Casey Spahn's leg-he has him halfway there- and Spahn jabs him with an elbow. Now Spahn with a clothesline- McNultey ducks, and back drops Casey Spahn over the top rope! (pop, as....) EL: And Paul Langford dropkicks the Pretty Boy right between the shoulder blades, and out he goes! And Casey Spahn there waiting for him- big forearm uppercut fells the Pretty Boy! Here comes Michael Anderson- and Spahn meets him with a clothesline! (pop) EL: Casey Spahn taking on both tag champs on the floor now- back in the ring, Kore Wrench and Randy Sheradin and Joshua Barnes are all trying to eliminate Sgt. Sherrick, they've got him hanging- he has one foot wrapped around that top rope for survival- about to join his partner- hey! (The Samurai with a well-placed thrust kick in the back of Barnes' head sends he and Sherrick over the top, as the Dreamer and Preacher hang on.) EL: And the Samurai on a tear- that's- what, four?- four men he's eliminated! And now the Dreamer and Preacher double-teaming the Samurai. Jimmy Dangerous tangles with Little Yvette, and Marco Moura running Langford's face across the top rope, and Double Jeopardy in a four-man battle with the Trailer Park Assassins. Elektro and the Freak, perhaps in a preview of tomorrow's action, going at it- kneelift by Elektro, and now Yvette breaks free and joins him in the attack on the Freak, now Dangerous joins in the attack on the Samurai. Sheradin and Barnes hold the Samurai down, and Yvette- I mean, Dangerous- off the top rope with a kneedrop across the head. Kensei Tokugawa reaping what he's sown- and now Eddie Intellecta with a rabbit punch on the Dreamer. And Little Yvette and Elektro have just dumped the Freak to the floor! (pop) EL: And now Elektro with a kick in Yvette's mid section! Paul Langford and Marco Moura up against the ropes- and now Gary Jackson tries pushing both over, and- (pop) EL: Langford and Moura each pop him one right in the head! Now they turn around and resume their attack on each other- as Eddie Intellecta in real trouble, the TPAs almost have him over- and Gary Jackson with a double sledge in Cooter's back bails Eddie out. And Elektro has just eliminated Little Yvette. Barnes and Sheradin hold the Samurai in the corner- here comes Dangerous across the ring- and Marco Moura just collided with him! Paul Langford with an Irish whip, and he threw Moura right into Dangerous! Now Dangerous up on spaghetti legs- and Langford with a dropkick- there he goes! Jimy Dangerous has been eliminated! (pop) EL: Elektro now tussles with Joshua Barnes- and a Barnes right hand sends Elektro to the ropes- now an uppercut, and there goes the popular English star! Joshua Barnes has just eliminated Elektro! Let's see now, Marco Moura trying to shake the cobwebs, Sheradin and Tokugawa in the corner, the TPAs and Double Jeopardy paired off, and Barnes and Langford lock up. Now 3M "Maniac" Marco Moura with an elbow jab on Orbit, and then he turns and kicks Eddie Intellecta- attacking two men at once. The Samurai choking Randy Sheradin with that tag rope now. Barnes sends Langford for the ride- ducks- and a kneelift from the Tornado- and Langford with a dropkick and Barnes stumbles to the floor! (groans) EL: The Tornado has just eliminated Joshua Barnes. All right- Gary Jackson nails the Dreamer, and the Samurai with a kick on Jackson. Cooter and Orbit with a double headbutt on Eddie Intellecta, Moura to Eddie's aid. Langford now after Jackson, and Randy Sheradin sends the Samurai for the ride- monkey flip takes him over. Double front layout suplex by the Trailer Park Assassins on Intellecta. Sheradin now with a headscissor takedown- no! Marco Moura dropkicks the Dreamer off of Tokugawa's shoulders to the floor! We're down to Cooter, Orbit, Eddie, Jackson, Langford, Moura and Tokugawa, and Randy Sheradin exits to the cheers of the crowd. Getting down to the nitty-gritty here, as Tokugawa rewards Moura with a chop. And Gary Jackson takes off his ankle brace and clanks the Tornado on the head with it, and knocks him to the floor! Paul Langford has been eliminated! Kensei Tokugawa now nails Maniac Marco Moura with that heart punch- sets him up- hangman! This is what he does to pin his man, usually, but a pin doesn't help him here. Moura is out of it- and Tokugawa pulls him up- and just throws him over the top rope! 15 down, 4 to go until we have one winner. A tag team bout breaks out between the TPAs and Double Jeopardy as Kensei Tokugawa gets his breath back. And Cooter with a right, and a left, and a right- and Jackson to the floor! (pop) EL: They're dropping like flies now- a strong performance by Double Jeopardy though, as both men among the last 5, and Eddie Intellecta still in there, with Orbit of all people- average them out and you have an average IQ, Eddie would tell us. Tokugawa with a kick on Cooter now, scoops him up- big slam. Off the ropes- elbow drop. The Samurai making a very impressive showing- undefeated in hid short stay so farm and going down to the wire in this special $25,000 battle royal. I know one man who'd like to get his hands on him, and that's the TV champion, the Professional Max Robertson, as it was the Samurai who smacked him with that kendo stick last week-.Cooter with a big right hand, and down goes Tokugawa. Now the TPAs after Eddie Intellecta- Irish whip- double back body drop- and there he goes! Eddie Intellecta is out! (pop) EL: We are down to the Trailer Park Assassins and the Samurai, Kensei Tokugawa, and the odds are about to catch up with the Samurai. The TPAs cornering Tokugawa, and he lashes out- thrust kicks- hits Orbit- Cooter ducks, and he hits referee Jeff Howard! Chris Herman now checking on his colleague, who's really caught that thrust kick, right under the jaw. Tokugawa now with a handful of salt, some kind of powder, in Cooter's eyes! Orbit down, Cooter down, and Tokugawa now slips to the outside and grabs that kendo stick- and here comes Jimmy Dangerous! (pop) EL: Tokugawa readies to swing- and Dangerous up behind him, yanks it right out of his hand! The Samurai spins- and Dangerous on the apron nails him right in the head with his own kendo stick! (pop) EL: Chris Herman helping up Jeff Howard- neither sees what's going on behind them- Tokugawa stunned by his own kendo stick- staggers back and Orbit catches him now- atomic knee drop- and Tokugawa bounces off of his knee and over the top rope! (pop) EL: The Samurai victimized by his own kendo stick, and it's just the Trailer Park Assassins in the ring! But what now? Will we see Cooter and Orbit throw friendship aside for $25,000? We've seen Langford and Barnes, Yvette and Elektro- once that bell rings, there are no friends. Does Orbit have the brain power to see past it if Cooter smacks him with of those big soupbone fists? Orbit and Cooter both up- and Cooter motions for Chris Herman to ring the bell. (Jeff Howard is in no shape to interpret such gestures) EL: What's going on? There's the bell, and let's get the official word from David Myers. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your co-winners of the Battle Royal and the $25,000- Orbit and Cooter Lee Roy- the Trailer Park Assassins! (frenetic pop, and Dangerous cheers along as he leaves) DM: And now, to present the check for $25,000, here is the AEWA's own Lovely Bertha! (the crowd is underwhelmed) (Bertha arrives in the ring carrying one of those giant cardboard novelty checks, made out for $25,00, and has CWAA Execs hold it up while she writes out "Trailer Park Assassins" in a black magic marker. As she finishes, Orbit sniffs the marker.) EL: Well, we ought to keep an eye on Bertha and make sure she doesn't post-date the check. LB: Goober, Elmer, it is my privilege- (the way she rolls her eyes and spits out the word "privilege" betray more than a modicum of insincerity) LB: -to present you rejects from Hee Haw stunt-man school with this check for $25,000, and- what in blazes are you doing, you Freak? (Cooter is trying to endorse the 6' long check on the back, and asks Orbit if he knows their account number. Orbit smiles, nods, and holds up 3 fingers. Cooter nods knowingly.) LB: This isn't the real check, you know. Get the weevils out of your brains! Duh! (Cooter and Orbit look confused) LB: It's cardboard! (Orbit is beginning to feel he's been had) Cooter Lee Roy: Um- begging your pardon, ma'am, but I believe we is supposed to be gettin' real money. No one said nothing about comin' all the way down here and fightin' for no cardboard. (Orbit nods indignantly) LB: This is for the cameras, you walking parasite colony! CLR: We may not be no Nobel Prize winners, but even we know that camera don't eat no cardboard. (Orbit chuckles at Bertha's ignorance of modern science and photographic techniques) LB: No, no! It's for the people who want to take pictures of you guys getting the check! CLR: (his voice raising in pitch as well as volume) Well what're you givin' them the money for! LB: Hold on, hold on- listen real carefully, OK. You guys did win the money, and will get it in a second. We only brought this thing out here because when people take pictures of you getting your prize, for like the Enquirer or National Geographic or Neanderthal's Digest or whatever, the check is too small to be se- CLR: Wait just a cotton picking minute! Too small! It's supposed to be $25,000, and- LB: Oh, here! (pulls the actual check and hands it over, then starts to walk away) CLR: Uh- there's two of us, and only one of these checks. (LB spins on her heel, takes the check, and rips it in not-quite-in-half. She hands the two pieces to Cooter.) LB: Satisfied? CLR: (nods) We only wanted what was comin' to us. Hey, Orbit, you can have this one. (hands him a piece of the check) EL: Well, when we get back, we'll crown our six man tag team champions! Stay tuned! (fade as Orbit and Cooter argue over who gets the bigger piece)
