Sunday, March 2nd, Card (1/3)

2 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last week, on Championship Wrestling Association of America: EL: I wouldn't do that if I were him. Then again, if I were the Major, there are a lot of things I wouldn't do. Go on living, for starters. (MVM continues sputtering, and Stevens looks to the crowd, who egg him on, and he grabs the scrawny major by the throat and lifts him two feet of the ground, as the crowds erupts in a frenzy of approval.) EL: The major is finding out just how "suitable" Stan Ste- look out! Lt. Lawson just came out at a dead sprint and clipped the legs of the Powerhouse. I smell a set-up... ****************************************************************** Eric Lane: Welcome to another edition of Championship Wrestling Association of America, here in the world-famous Sportatorium in beautiful Dallas, Texas. We've got a full slate this weekend, so let's get right to the action- "Hotshot" Gary Jackson is already in the ring, waiting for the second round of the CWAA Heavyweight Title tournament. (Darth Vader's customary Imperial March strikes up. A smattering of cheers greets "The Professional" Max Robertson, the 5'11"-200 lb. San Francisco native with the bald head and the red/black tights. His opponent, in contrast, has a beer belly hanging over his blue jeans and under his spotty undershirt, and stands 5'11"-250.) EL: Let me see if I can get a word from Max Robertson before he gets to the ring... Mr. Robertson, you seem a little preoccupied tonight, are you concerned about your forthcoming match with 'The Hotshot' Gary Jackson? MR: No comment. No interviews today. EL: OK- Gary Jackson and Max Robertson, and we're going to find out who moves on to the semifinals. Keep an eye on Henry King at ringside, and we know Eddie Intellecta can't be too far away. Lockup- Robertson into hammerlock, and Jackson trying to scramble free, but Max locks it on, and all that happens is Gary Jackson is stuck running in a circle around the Professional, who has one of the Hotshot's arms pinned behind his back. Jackson reaching for the ropes- Robertson grabs a handful of tights and pulls him back- back suplex. Robertson back up now, he whips Gary Jackson into the ropes- legdrag takedown. As Jackson gets back up- Robertson was waiting for him with a savate kick! Henry King now reaches in and pulls his man out under the bottom rope- and Gary Jackson out on the floor swinging wildly- and he falls flat on his face on the floor! While we wait for Jackson to recover his wits, let me tell you what else is coming up this week here on Championship Wrestling Association of America: more second round action as Healer's Havoc squares off against Mark Sherrick. The Preacher Kore Wrench- Jackson back up on the ring apron now- Max Robertson off the ropes- handspring elbow- right into Jackson's chest- and down goes the Hotshot on the floor again! Kore Wrench will show us some footage of a recent visit he made to the children's ward of an area hospital, then he'll take on "The Dreamer" Randy Sheradin. An ACWF official has also sent us a statement we'll play for all of you about the allegations regarding Randy Sheradin's past. OK- Gary Jackson back in the ring now, and he and Robertson lock up again- hip toss by the Professional, and now he locks on an arm bar. The second round of the CWAA Heavyweight Title tourney will end with Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson taking on big Travis Conley, one half of Twister. And in our special feature bout, Conley's partner, Paul Langford steps in the ring one more time with Double Jeopardy's Eddie Intellecta. We'll have some special announcements from the AEWA, as well, today. Gary Jackson now up on his feet, and he reverses the arm bar on Robertson, and now it's Jackson with an Irish whip- clothesline. He covers ... 1... Robertson kicks out- we have to take a break now- we'll bring more of this exciting matchup when we return. (Monday Night at 10:30 PM, WYXZ Channel 39 presents "Happy Days"- this week, Fonzie, Pinky Tuscadero and the Malachi Brothers enter a demolition derby! Then, at 11:00 PM, on "The Dukes of Hazzard", Roscoe chases the Duke boys while Flash sits in the back seat, and Boss Hogg sputters a lot.) EL: OK- we're back, and Gary Jackson has Max Robertson trapped in the corner, and is driving his shoulder into Robertson's midsection. Cross-corner whip now, Robertson stops in the corner- hops up onto the second turnbuckle- and spins around into a cross body press... 1... 2... kickout. Both men back up now- and Robertson goes for the hip toss- Jackson blocks it- big right hand by the Hotshot! Now a left, and Robertson drops to the mat. Kneedrop by Jackson, now he's flailing away at the Professional, straddling his chest! A flurry of punches, and the referee is warning him- now the ref stops his punch- Jackson looks up incredulously- and there's Max Robertson with a double leg cradle rolling Jackson back! (Crowd pops in anticipation...) EL: 1... 2... kickout! (Crowd groans) EL: Jackson is beside himself- whips Robertson into the ropes- and drives a knee into his stomach. Now Jackson, still hot at the referee, turns around to bark at him some more. Robertson comes up behind him- legsweep! Robertson now with an elbow-drop- lateral press...1 ...2... no- kickout. Robertson rolls him over- stretches Jackson's arm- stomps on the exposed elbow! Now he gets down on the mat- hammerlock submission hold. He's really goit that one locked in, and Jackson is in very obvious pain. Earlier this week, Gary Jackson was one of the men who joined Kore Wrench on his trip to the children's ward of a local hospital- we'll see footage of that shortly- he and Eddie Intellecta seem like two of the least likely people to be involved in such an enterprise, but we'll see how they behaved. And there's Henry King reaching into the ring again, pushing the rope into Jackson's reach to break the hold. Robertson now whips Jackson into the corner- Jackson bounces back out backwards- and Robertson up behind him with that double chickenwing- he's got it locked in and there's nowhere for Jackson to go- there's the bell, and Max Robertson is heading for the semifinals next week- an impressive submission victory for the Professional. (Big pop as MR has his hand raised and the Imperial March plays while Jackson is led away by King.) (Marilyn Manson's "Beautiful People" plays and hometown-villain Mark Sherrick, with his Steiner-esque black apparel, his black glove, and the "Never Forgotten" t-shirt adorning his 6'4"-275 frame. And there is a lone scar running along his left shoulder.) EL: Here comes Mark Sherrick, who continues to insist that his father was killed right here in this ring some years ago, and that it is his filial duty to exact revenge against all modern-wrestlers. Our research people still have not turned up anything that would fit his story, but that doesn't seem to affect his attitude. And speaking of attitudes, he's getting one from the fans here tonight. (A warmer reception is afforded his opponent, Tucson's own Healer's Havoc, as he, Tip Smith and BD Brown arrive, to the music of Three Dog Night. As always, the toothless one wears a dog collar, and stands 6'6"-260.) [The music of "Three Dog Night" begins to play in the background as Healer's Havoc, BD, and Tip Smith enter the arena] EL: Let me see if I can get a word with the team of Healer's Havoc and BD Brown, Midnight Rampage.............! HH: I'd first like to thank everyone for the cards after last week's easy win over that gimp, Eddie 'what'? Now, tonight I'm going to be fighting against Mark Sherrick. Oh, he really scares me, how about ya', BD? BD: You know he cheated two weeks ago when he defeated me, don't ya' know, Havoc? HH: He did, I must have been watching the beautiful women sitting in the front row, then. BUT, this week is going to be different! No women, just you and me and the "BIG DOG!" BD: Yep, and for protection he recently brought a brand new Haliburton case with your name of it! HH: Get Ready, Mark! Cause watch ya' gonna do, when the Big Dog and the Real Deal run wild on YOU! [HH, BD and Tip leave the interview area for the ring.] EL: Ok, Healer's Havoc set to face Mark Sherrick- Sherrick won't even let him in the ring- as he attacks Havoc coming through the ropes- a big kick, and now a double axe handle. Sherrick runs his face along that top rope now, which has to sting. Now Sherrick rams his face on to the top turnbuckle. Havoc slumped on that ring apron, and Mark Sherrick backs up- running clothesline- Havoc bails out, and Sherrick slams into the corner! (The crowd engages in a spontaneous act of mass approval. In short, they pop.) EL: Healer's Havoc grabs him by the ankle and pulls him out of the ring now- big right hand by Havoc. Now he slams Sherrick's head onto the ring apron. Headbutt now by Healer's Havoc- and Mark Sherrick tries heading around the corner of the ring to walk it off, but Healer's Havoc is right behind him-Havoc with a handful of hair, as he sends Sherrick to the ringpost- no- reversed, and Havoc goes right into that post. Sherrick goes for a chair now- the wind up-the swing- again Havoc bails out in the nick of time, and that chair smacks the ring post. Big Dog Brown now with that, it looks like some metal-suitcase, Haliburton he called it, I think- and Sherrick turns around holding the chair as Brown stands with that suitcase raised. The bell is ringing- I'm guessing a count-out of some sort or no contest, and Sherrick and Brown are staring each other down with those weapons poised- Havoc back int he middle of things as he tackles Sherrick. The bell is still ringing and Smith and Brown are trying to separate Havoc from Sherrick, and here comes Kore Wrench and our second referee to help sort this all out. OK- Smith and Brown are holding back Havoc, and Wrench and the referees are standing between Sherrick and Midnight Rampage. Well, it looks like a double countout has occured, which must make the Professional one happy individual. When we get back, we'll have more exciting second round action for you, coming up next!
