Straight Talk (9/20)

22 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera fades in on the AEWA Studios, the AEWA logo falls onto the screen, and the words "STRAIGHT TALK" slide in underneath it. The whole logo then tilts, and falls off the bottom of the screen. Behind the desk in studio are Chuck Carlson and Ken Wilson.) CC: Hello fans and welcome to yet another edition of AEWA Straight Talk! I am of course your host, Chuck Carlson, and this bitter young man is my co-host, Ken Wilson. KW: Always a pleasure, Chuck. (under his breath) A pleasure to get the hell away from you at the end of the show. CC: Likewise, I'm sure. By the way, do you ACTUALLY think that people can't hear you when you talk like that? Because when you tilt your head down like that, you're actually talking more directly into your microphone. KW: I'm wearing a microphone? CC: Yes, it's clipped onto your collar. KW: (looks down and sees the microphone) Wow, cool! CC: It's amazing what amuses some people. Let's just watch this interview by a newcomer who calls himself Apollo. ---------- (A man is sitting in a director's chair in a darkened studio, with a blue cloth backdrop. Slowly a single spotlight begins to glow; softly at first, then brilliantly illuminating the man who is dressed in all white. He looks to be about 5'11, around 235 pounds. He begins to speak.) Apollo: December 27, 1991, a day that will live forever.... (We cut to video clip. The screen shows footage from another wrestling organization, and two wrestlers are going at it in the ring) Announcer 1: Apollo has gone nuts! I can't believe the beating he is handing to the Terminator! We need some officials in here! Announcer 2: Apollo ties him UPSIDE-DOWN in the ropes! What the hell is he doing? Apollo now climbing to the top of the cage, he's going to climb out.... Announcer 1: NO! Apollo jumps onto Louie's legs-- he just single-handedly spike-piledrove him from the top of the cage! He is all over the Terminator.... We need some help in here. (Faces and heels alike storm the cage to stop the carnage. The other wrestlers, despite being much larger, take several minutes to subdue the crazed Apollo, and many of them get knocked out in the process) Ring Announcer: Here is your winner, by referee's decision, and the FIRST EVER CSWF WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, APOLLO! (As the crowd goes wild, the footage ends. We see the same man again, still sitting in the director's chair.) Apollo: Well, those were the early days. (chuckle) And I have mellowed down a little bit. Now I use my technical skills on the big guys.... (We now see more footage. This time, two wrestlers are going at it in the center of the ring.) Announcer: What's that? MANDIBLE CLAW! The Colossan is in the center of the ring! He has dominated this match, but he is helpless under Apollo's clawhold! 1...2....3! (This footage ends) Apollo: I have a lot of ways to get the job done. I like keeping the fans in suspense a little, keeping them wondering what finisher I will use. But, hell, you know I have a soft spot for those kids out there, so quite often I will use all of my finishers. (We see more footage, from another wrestling organization. Some commentators are calling a match) Announcer 1: Apollo has Jandarin dead to rights. Just NAILED him with his own finisher. Apollo puts his hands in the time out sign.... It's time for a little cup of "T"!!! Announcer 2: Apollo sends Jandarin off the ropes, catches him-- Spinebuster slam into a backward bridge, the TITANPLEX! 1..2..3.... (The footage ends. The camera zooms in on Apollo, and he begins to speak again) Apollo: It is time for me to conquer new territories. I am the DWO champion. I already am dominating in the CSWF. With the fans behind me, I cannot be stopped. Welcome to the new beginning of the AEWA, my friends. (The lights dim, and the scene fades) ---------- KW: What does CSWF stand for anyway? The Crappy, Sucky Wrestling Federation? That's the only type of fed I can think of where THIS guy'd be able to win a title. CC: Be nice Ken, you haven't even seen the guy wrestle in an AEWA ring yet, and he certainly looked great in that footage we just saw. KW: That footage was probably doctored. They just put Apollo in, in place of the guy who was REALLY winning. And the commentary was just dubbed over. CC: You really do have a very warped mind. KW: Thank you. CC: Now let's get a word from another newcomer, Darren Lee. ---------- (We see "Downtown" Darren Lee in the locker room, looking very angry. He stares directly at the camera, and begins yelling) "Downtown" Darren Lee: I'm on my way to the AEWA for one reason, to inflict as much pain as possible. I've been hurting punks on the streets of Philly my whole life and if anyone thinks that's gonna change when I step into the ring, they are dead wrong! Because if you take a trip downtown you ain't comin' back, except in a box! (fade to black) ---------- KW: Now THAT'S a rookie with some potential. CC: Those are just words, anyone can SAY they're going to kick some butt, but doing it is another thing. At least Apollo showed us what he was capable of, instead of just blowing off his mouth about how good he is. KW: I already told you what I thought about Apollo's "footage", but that's another story. This guy's from the inner city, and you gotta be tough to survive in the 'hood. CC: You being an expert on the "'hood", right? KW: Of course. CC: Whatever, let's just get a word from Rage. ---------- (Rage is standing with Johnny Switchblade in a dark alley. Switchblade stands in front of Rage, who has his greasy black hair pulled down over his face. Switchblade holds his baseball bat, with the word "RAGE" in blood red letters.) Switchblade: Everyone keeps asking me about Rage. "When is he wrestling?" they keep asking me. The answer is soon, very soon. The reason he hasn't been in the ring has been because the big wigs in the AEWA won't schedule any matches for him. They fear him and what he will do to all their precious wrestlers. Well, I can understand where they are coming from. Rage will destroy you if you get in the ring with him! I want a match for him! Schedule it, board of directors. I don't care who it is! And if he doesn't get a match soon, he'll just have to take matters into his own hands and destroy the wimps here outside of the ring and out in the street! Rage: Board of directors, I want a match. The time has come for me to take over the AEWA. No wrestler can stop me. I will make the man who tries to stop me pay for his stupidity. Johnny and I will rule supreme as soon as I step into the ring and prove to everyone that I am the king of wrestling. I will make you stop and ask yourself "Who opened the Gates of Hell and let this man out?!" (Rage bows his head and the camera fades to Johnny laughing menacingly.) ---------- KW: Another week has gone by, and still no match for Rage. This whole freaking federation is scared of him! CC: I SERIOUSLY doubt that, it's just with all the things that have been going on around here recently, there hasn't been room to schedule him a match. KW: (sarcastically) Sure there hasn't. I know you're in on the conspiracy to keep Rage out of the ring, Carlson. You, along with my brother, and everyone else with no guts in the AEWA, so don't try to hide it. CC: I have NO IDEA what you're talking about! KW: (winks) Sure you don't. I gotcha. CC: That conspiracy exists only within the confines of your mind. Now let's get off this silly subject and watch this interview with our TV Champion, Marcus McTeague. ---------- (Marcus McTeague is lounging around after his first title defense. He looks pleased and relaxed.) McTeague: Well, Curate Kill, you were an interesting opponent but it looks like you just can't handle me. Onto the next challenge. Raekwon, I believe you were the individual that slapped me. If you think that's enough to goad me into offering you a title shot, you're sadly mistaken. Once you earn the shot, you'll get it. I'm not a stupid man and I don't intend to jeopardize my title by giving into anger. I think one of these days I might have to take a shot at Heavy Metal.... It looks like nobody around here can beat the guy. Well, I think I can, and pretty soon I will prove it. (fade to black) ---------- KW: Well everybody wants a piece of Heavy Metal. There's only one problem with that-- the man's unstoppable. CC: No one's unstoppable. Anyone in the AEWA could be beaten by anyone else on a given night. KW: Not Heavy Metal, he's gonna retire with that belt. CC: We'll see. And you'll see that I'm right. And the day that he does lose it, I'm not gonna forget this "unstoppable" business. You're gonna hear about it. KW: In your dreams, buddy. CC: Just wait. And now let's get a word from the Gryphon. ---------- (Gryphon is sitting in a locker room. He looks slightly peeved, but begins to speak) Gryphon: Hello there, AEWA. I'm sure you're wondering why you haven't heard from me in awhile. Well, ever since I lost to Shepherd Chaos, I've been revising my style. Now I'm back and prepared to kick someone's ass. If I had my pick, the first one receiving the ass kicking would be that scumbag Deathwatch. Deathwatch, you went too far. First burning the AEWA logo, and then attacking President Smith. Then you go and smear two light heavyweights! Pick on someone your own size, jerk-off! Try me, for example. You way outweigh me, but I'll knock you so far it'll cost you 14 dollars to sent me a postcard. There are only about three other people around here that I truly despise. Twisted Steel, Ricky Roberts, and Grim Reaper. You guys are lower than pond scum. Especially you, Roberts. You were too damn chicken to defend your woman when the Reaper attacked her, so you joined in. You make me want to puke, Roberts. Grim Reaper, you're the one who attacked her in the first place. What the hell is wrong with you? Did you get so tired of losing in the ring that you decided to start attacking women? Granted, not all women are as defenseless as Vicki.... I'm sure that the Lovely Bertha would have kicked your sorry ass from here to next week-- but that's not the point. Twisted Steel, you turned your back on the fans and the whole federation. I don't know what your problem is, but I'm here to resolve it, son. Now, I've heard that Ben Slocum and Sir Gallade are looking for a partner to take these three lowlifes on. Well, I'd just like to throw my name into that hat. I'd love a chance to beat any one of these guys to a bloody pulp. AEWA, I'm back, and I'm better than ever before. I've been to other federations, and this one's the best. I'll be damned before I let people like Deathwatch, Twisted Steel, Roberts, and the Reaper ruin it for everyone else. (fade to black) ---------- KW: Does this guy have something wrong in his brain or something? Challenging Deathwatch AND the ENTIRE Angels of Death stable in a single interview? He's just BEGGING for a pounding. CC: At least he's got the guts to stand up and fight for the AEWA; we could use a hundred more like him. We need to rid this federation of the filth that infests it. KW: I think it's doing just great the way it is. CC: I know what YOU think, and quite frankly, I don't care. KW: I feel the same way about you, Chuck. CC: How nice, let's just watch this interview from the Graveyard Shift. ---------- (The camera opens on the locker room, and we see "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt standing with the Graveyard Shift. He is holding a microphone, ready to interview them) Big Daddy: (laughs) Boys, you got your butts kicked tonight! Coffin: Yeah, keep right on laughing, jerk-off! But yes, we did get beat by Graphic Violence tonight and we aren't a team that makes excuses. When Grave Dweller here got put in that Scorpion Deathlock, he only had one thing in mind and that was getting at the Horde! And to tell you the truth, shit happens. Graphic Violence! We won't forget you, boys! But right now, we have MORE IMPORTANT things to take care of! Tell 'em, Mortician.... Mortician: You got that right, brotha. Horde, you poor excuse for men. We have challenged you to a "suspended ring" match, and we want it. You can run and you can keep taking cheap shots, but sooner or later you're gonna have to act like men and accept the challenge. Big Daddy: They would be nuts to accept that challenge. I have been in the "suspended ring", and let me tell you boys something-- it hurts like hell when you fall way down to a concrete floor. (he grabs his neck) Coffin: Yeah, but we ain't planning on getting our asses kicked like you did by the Caretaker and Raekwon. HORDE, WE WANT YOU, AND WE WANT YOU NOW! (he's so excited that spit flies everywhere) Big Daddy: There you have it, fans. The Shift isn't backing off of this challenge, and I think they need to have their heads examined! (camera fades to black) ---------- KW: I agree with Big Daddy. The Shift's in WAY over their heads. CC: I don't think so. They've had just about enough of this crap from the Visigothic Horde, and they're not gonna sit and take it anymore. They want a match that's gonna END it. And a suspended ring match would certainly do that! KW: It won't just end the feud, it'll end the CAREERS of the Mortician and the Grave Dweller. CC: That's a possibility, but not if they win. The same fate could await the Horde, you know. KW: No it couldn't. CC: Yes it COULD. And now I understand that we have some footage of Primal Fear in the hospital after he was BRUTALLY attacked last week by the Twilight Strangler, so let's go to that. ---------- (We see Primal Fear in a hospital bed, looking very weak. He stares at the camera, and begins to speak) Primal Fear: My kids....my wife....the fans....they all saw....what happened to me........ (A machine next to Primal Fear's bed starts beeping, and doctors rush in to help him. Doctor Fritz Gunolven moves in front of the camera) Gunolven: Get that camera out of here! Someone get me some help in here, this man is going fast, hurry the hell up! (fade to black) ---------- KW: (laughing uncontrollably) That was great! CC: You're DISGUSTING! Apparently folks, Primal Fear is in a VERY critical condition right now, and I just hope that he can pull through. KW: Yeah, so that he can crawl back into the ring with the Strangler and get his ass whipped again! CC: It wasn't IN the ring last time. It was a cowardly sneak attack on the interview platform with a baseball bat! KW: Same difference. CC: Hardly. And now I understand we've got an interview with the man who put Primal Fear into his current condition, The Twilight Strangler. ---------- (We see Miss DeMeanor sitting in her office, and she looks very happy) DeMeanor: You know something, when I make a promise, I keep it! Primal honey, are you a little choked up? Ha ha ha! Boy AEWA, I think my new man shocked you, didn't he? I think he scared the hell out of you and just wait till he gets in the ring and takes the gold! (Twilight Strangler walks into the office) Strangler: Primal Fear, I saw the horror in your eyes as you were going under, I saw the kids cry as the doctors attended you, and the only thing I feel sorry about is that I didn't finish the job. You're not dead, but you will be! Boy, I wish Uncle Jeffrey Dahmer was still around, he would have loved this! DeMeanor: Now before anybody asks me how the Strangler got out of jail, I will tell you! The bail was set at $950,000, and since Primal Fear is still under contract with me, I just had it taken out of HIS salary! So if you look at it, Primal Fear and the AEWA helped released a criminal! Strangler: AEWA, look into my eyes and you will see nothing-- I fear nothing, and I will do ANYTHING to hurt you! DeMeanor: He's kinda cute when he's mad. AEWA, I am warning you now, don't sign a contract with this man! (fade to black) ---------- KW: Boy, I'd hate to be the next guy who has to mess with this monster! CC: I don't know, we haven't seen what he can do in an actual match, when he doesn't have a baseball bat in his hand. KW: You will soon enough, and you'll be sorry that you asked. CC: Somehow I've got a feeling that you're right. KW: I'm ALWAYS right. CC: Not really. And now let's get some comments from Sewerdog. ---------- (The camera is panning around a junk yard filled with old cars and other garbage and debris. Sewerdog then seems to appear from out of nowhere.) Sewerdog: Heavy Metal, take a long look around, this is where you belong, with the scrap and garbage that you probably call family. Unfortunately, for you, you'll never be able to defend that belt against me 'cause you're such a bitch-assed wuss. It's really pathetic. Why won't you give me a shot, we can settle our differences and everything. (chuckles) Oh, wait, you're a crybaby loser. Kicking your ass would be as easy as this.... (He picks up a can and boots it over the junkyard, then walks away out of the camera's sight. Fade to black) ---------- KW: Sewerdog sure is confident, but that'll mean squat when he's in the ring with Heavy Metal. I can't believe that he's taking the champ so lightly! CC: Neither can I. This Sewerdog just seems to have no respect whatsoever for his opponents! KW: I know, that's why I like him, but against Heavy Metal, I think that that could be a BIG mistake. CC: It certainly could. And now let's get a word from Sir Gallade. ---------- (Sir Gallade, with his neck in a brace, kneels with his sword in hand. Lady Guinevere stands beside him) Sir Gallade: I swear by the sword of my king that Twisted Steel, Ricky Roberts, and Grim Reaper will pay the ultimate price for insulting my honor. (He stands, but points the sword toward the camera) It is one thing to attack someone when you are face to face, but it is an act of a cowardice to hit them from behind. Yes, go off and laugh about what you did to Ben Slocum and myself now, because when it is over, you three will be the ones going down. They say that war has been declared, well bring it at me because I am ready. The one that hit me from behind, the one called Grim Reaper, I do not care how scary you think you are, or how you may intimidate other wrestlers, you do not scare me or intimidate me. You see, where I came from, a man can not be afraid of anything. Let us see how well you can match up to the First Knight. I hereby challenge you to a match with no disqualifications, so that there must be a winner no matter what happens! Lady Guinevere: So Grim Reaper, will you accept or not? (Sir Gallade turns and walks away with Guinevere) ---------- KW: This guy wants the Reaper in a no DQ match? Is he mental or something? CC: No, but you are. What's wrong with that challenge? KW: I'll tell ya what's wrong with that. Not only is the Reaper a complete psycho, but you know he's gonna have the rest of the "Angels of Death" there with him, and they could interfere all they want and it would all be perfectly legal! This is a match that Sir Gallade CAN'T win! CC: I'm not so sure about that, maybe we could get someone to keep an eye on the Angels, and that would even up the odds quite a bit. KW: Yeah, like there's actually anyone who could keep the Angels of Death away from the ring. CC: We'll see. But now let's get a word from Cool Carlson and Groovy Gonzalez, the Crew. ---------- (We see Groovy Gonzalez and Cool Carlson, the Crew, standing in their dressing room. They both look very angry) Gonzalez: What has HAPPENED to the AEWA? We left for a few months, on a nice little tour of Alaska, and now this place has totally changed. The inmates are running the asylum! It's pure anarchy around here anymore, buddy. Carlson: I'm really pissed off at the Machines right now. Those bastards caused us to lose a match, and it wasn't fair. Gonzalez: (in a very insincere voice) And that's what we came here for-- to enforce the rules! The Machines can't win a match without cheating, and it makes me sick! Carlson: And I'd like to warn Submission-- we haven't forgotten about them. They're going to PAY, buddy. Gonzalez: (in a "Macho Man" voice) Oh yeah! (fade to black) ---------- KW: I'd like to make a point here. The Machines didn't trick the Crew, Simon did. Paul's too much of a wuss to cheat. CC: Playing by the rules does not make him a wuss, but you do have a point there. The treachery was on the part of Simon, and not Paul. KW: Of course I have a point there, I'm a friggin' genius! CC: If you say so. KW: I do. CC: Whatever. Now we've got an interview with the man that you just called a wuss, Paul Wong of the Machines. ---------- (Paul Wong is watching films of the Shadow Warriors on a VCR. "The Crew" is written on The Machines' WALL OF CONQUESTS. Hank Thompson starts the interview.) Thompson: Paul, thanks for the interview. Where's your partner, Simon O'Neal? Paul: Simon went to speak to Kevin Larkin about signing us a match against the Shadow Warriors for the AEWA National Tag Team Titles. I've been studying their matches, working on strategies for us to beat them and win the titles. Thompson: Are you certain that's a good idea? We've heard you two had some arguments.... Paul: Hold on.... Now, I'll be the first to admit that Simon cuts a few corners, and I don't agree with his philosophy on a lot of things. But Simon does keep his word. He promised no bribes, he won't attempt any. I'm certain that when Kevin Larkin sees our record, he'll be glad to give us a title shot. We are more than capable of beating the Shadow Warriors, and winning the belts. (fade to black) ---------- KW: They WOULD be more than capable of winning the belts, if Paul would just forget about that little thing called the rulebook. Simon apparently has. CC: I think that they are a very capable team and could win the belts WITHOUT cheating, but for some reason, Simon feels the need to break a rule here and there to get the win. KW: And that's why he's the driving force behind the Machines. CC: Not in my opinion. KW: I don't care about your opinion, and neither does anyone else. CC: How nice. And now for our final interview, let's hear from Scorpion. ---------- (The camera opens on the AEWA locker room, focused on the man called Scorpion.) Scorpion: Well, the Scorpion has made his debut, but it appears that I've got a little work to do before I can get to that upper echelon of competition here in the AEWA. Let's break down my matches here in the AEWA so far. First, I had a double countout against "Superman" Mike Stewart. Now Stewart's a great wrestler and I really respect him, but I know that I can personally do better, and I'll be more careful to watch that count from now on. Next, I was in that seventeen man battle royal for the North American Title. Now I did a little better in this one, I was in there for quite a while and I got to showcase some of my best moves, but I didn't win, and that is always my ultimate goal. So I was, of course, disappointed with my performance again. Now you may be asking, "What's next for the Scorpion?" To tell you the truth, I really don't know the answer to that one, I'm just gonna wrestle whoever they put in the ring with me, and show the world what the Scorpion is made of. If you like what you've seen from me so far, you ain't seen nothin' yet, and that's a promise! (fade to black.) ---------- KW: Gimme a break. This guy couldn't fight his way out of a wet paper bag. CC: Give the guy a LITTLE credit, Ken. He's only had two matches thus far in the AEWA. I think he looked fairly impressive, and I'm sure that the best is yet to come. KW: Whatever. CC: And that's our show for this week fans! Unfortunately, we're being forced to leave you with "the Kevin Larkin Show" again, but there's nothing I can do. I guess you just have to sit through it. But as for us, we're out of here. See you next week for more Straight Talk! ---------- (Kevin Larkin stumbles into Studio K. He is carrying a can of beer in one hand, and an empty case of beer in the other. Simon O'Neal of the Machines walks right behind him. He is also carrying a can of beer, but his seems to be untouched. Kevin almost trips over his feet as he walks to the desk) Larkin: Do ya really think that waitress liked me? Simon: Absolutely. That game where you kept grabbing her hat and wearing it-- you two were flirting like crazy. Larkin: Yeah.... I'm certain that she was just kidding about calling the police. Simon: Definitely. I even gave her your phone number. She'll probably give you a call tonight. Larkin: All Right! Well, it's time for the Kevin Larkin Show now. Simon: You got it. I'll be leaving now (starts to leave, then turns around). Oh, I almost forgot-- Kevin? (Kevin nods) Paul and I were wondering if you could give us a title shot against the Shadow Warriors? Larkin: Um.... Simon: (hands him the last beer) Have another one. Larkin: Sounds great! Simon: Great (pulls out a title contract that he just happened to be carrying). If you could just sign this, I'll be going. Larkin: All right. (signs the contract, which Simon puts back in his jacket pocket) Thanks for the drinks. (Simon nods and winks at the camera as he leaves, while Kevin tries to start the show. Larkin stumbles trying to sit in the chair behind his desk, and the beer splatters. His feet can be seen protruding from behind the desk, and he mumbles plaintively, "I don't feel sho good." After a few discreet moments, the credits begin to roll.) \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- American E-Wrestling Association!
