Straight Talk (1/15, part one)

17 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera fades in on the AEWA Studios, the AEWA logo falls onto the screen, and the words "STRAIGHT TALK" slide in underneath it. The whole logo then tilts, and falls off the bottom of the screen. Behind the desk in studio are Chuck Carlson and Ken Wilson.) CC: Hello fans and welcome yet again to AEWA Straight Talk. I'm your host, Chuck Carlson, and with me as always is my co-host, the ever ignorant, Ken Wilson. KW: HEY! Was that an insult? CC: I guess you'll never know. We've got a TON of interviews for you this week, and I'm sure it's gonna be one heck of a show. KW: Says you. This show'll probably suck, just like every other episode we've ever done, except for that one where you weren't here. That was cool. CC: Yes, there was no one here to point out how stupid and irrational you were being. KW: That's right! Hey, wait a minute.... CC: While Ken is trying to figure out what I just said, why don't you take a look at our first interview. Hank Thompson recently caught up with the doctor of former AEWA World Champion, "Superman" Mike Stewart. This should be VERY interesting, so let's hear what he had to say. ---------- (The camera opens on Hank Thompson, walking down a hallway in an Indianapolis hospital. Thompson walks to the reception desk and asks for the room of "Superman" Mike Stewart. The woman at the desk tells Thompson that the room is right behind him, and as Thompson turns around, Stewart's doctor comes out of the room.) Thompson: Excuse me, doctor, but we're looking for an update on the condition of Mike Stewart. Doctor: Well, I've been told not to discuss the specifics of Mr. Stewart's injury to the press, but I will tell you that his neck has been seriously injured, and there is a good chance that Mr. Stewart may never walk again. Thompson: Then his career in the AEWA is over? Doctor: Unless a miracle happens, yes it is. Thompson: I'm sorry to hear that. (pause) May we speak with Mike? Doctor: Mr. Stewart has requested that no press be allowed to speak with him, as he is having a hard time dealing with his new condition. He did ask me, however, to pass along a request. Thompson: (looking interested) What kind of request? Doctor: Mr. Stewart asked that I pass along a request for him to have a face-to-face interview with the man who was partially responsible for his condition, the professional wrestler known as Heavy Metal. Thompson: (surprised) He wants to be face-to-face with Heavy Metal? Doctor: That's what he told me. Now if you'll excuse me.... Thompson: Of course. (turns to the camera) Well, there you have it, fans. "Superman" Mike Stewart has requested a face-to-face interview with the man who apparently partially paralyzed him, Heavy Metal. I guess our next turn is to get a response from Heavy Metal. From Indianapolis, Indiana, I'm Hank Thompson. (Fade to black.) ---------- KW: Stewart wants to be face-to-face with Heavy Metal, and he can't even WALK? I'm no expert, but that DEFINITELY doesn't sound like a good place for him to be in that condition. CC: You could be right for once, Ken. Mike Stewart may never be able to walk again, and Heavy Metal is ALWAYS unpredictable; it could end up being a very dangerous situation. But I suppose Stewart needs to do this, for himself, so that he is able to deal with his tragic future. KW: I don't care what he THINKS he needs to do, the fact remains that if he gets face to face with Heavy Metal, bad things could happen. That's the bottom line. CC: Ken, I'm surprised at you. Are you actually CONCERNED for the condition of Mr. Stewart? KW: (very unconvincingly) Uh.... NO! CC: Sure you're not. In any case, this situation is VERY interesting, and I'm sure we'll get a response from Heavy Metal regarding that VERY soon. But right now, let's take a look at this recent interview with the always intimidating tag team of Heaven and Hell. ---------- (The camera opens on a cross standing in the middle of an empty field. A fire burns brightly in a trash can in the distance. Two men are walking towards the camera, one from the cross and one from the fire) Cardinal Chaos: Kevin Larkin, how dare you take my name in vain! I am the past, present and future of the AEWA. I am he who strikes fear in the hearts of mortals. I am he who brings truth to the mouths of the ignorant. I am the way, the truth, the light. You are but a sheep in the pasture of souls and I am your shepherd. You have wronged the master and ye shall burn at the stake of divine judgment. (pointing to Mister Crowley) My brother, tell our dear Mr. Larkin what is in store for him. Mister Crowley: Darkness, death, and eternal pain. Cardinal Chaos: Larkin, don't be fooled by the temporary security of the Horde. Heaven and Hell shall convert their souls before your very eyes. They shall feel the verdict of judgment day and be sentenced for all the world to see. The conversion of their souls is but a stepping stone in our quest for the Lord. We shall-- Mister Crowley: (interrupting him) Enough talk of the Lord, Chaos. The time for talking is over. Larkin, your days are growing shorter. Watch yourself very carefully. When you wake in the morning, look under your bed. When you get in your car, check the backseat. When you get to your little office, check your closets. We'll be there. When you least expect it, we'll be there waiting for you. Cardinal Chaos: (laughing) A man of few words, yet when he talks you should listen. We'll be there, Larkin. The followers of the light shall be our eyes and ears. You shall never be alone again. After the Horde has seen the light, we shall take care of what little trash is left in the AEWA. (pointing to the trash-can in the distance) The fire of damnation shall burn brightly until the day of atonement. On this day, your body and soul shall be quenched by the flame and you shall be reborn unto the Lord. Mister Crowley: Rest in peace.... (The camera fades to black.) ---------- KW: Boy I'd hate to be in Kevin Larkin's shoes right about now. CC: Tell me about it. Chaos and Crowley are two guys who you definitely do NOT want to be on their bad sides. Larkin's got his Visigothic Horde to do the fighting for him, but will they be enough? KW: If they're not, then Larkin's in some deep doo-doo. CC: That's an understatement. They want to tear Larkin apart, and frankly, who can blame them? Larkin's done nothing but cause trouble in the tag team division for MONTHS, and he's supposed to be the one in charge of it. He messed with the wrong guys this time, though. KW: He sure did. There's SIX World titles between the two of them, and they're always ready to pick up more. CC: They're gonna have to go through Larkin and his Horde first though, and it should be quite a war. And now let's get a word with another member of Chaos' Church, the wrestler formerly known as Curate Kill, Bishop Bedlam. ---------- (The camera opens on an unfamiliar site. Bishop Bedlam is standing behind the altar of the Church; his eyes seem to be glowing. Cardinal Chaos is nowhere to be found.) Bishop Bedlam: (chanting) I am the power. I am the glory. I am the instrument of destruction. I am the power. I am the glory. I am the instrument of destruction. I am the power. I am the glory. I am the instrument of destruction. I am the power. I am the glory. I am the instrument of destruction. (The Holy Ghost enters the room.) Holy Ghost: Brother Kill, what seems to be-- Bedlam: I am no longer he who is called Kill. You will refer to me by my newest title, Bishop Bedlam, or thou shall feel the wrath of my power. Ghost: I am sorry, Master Bedlam. Please forgive me. Bedlam: You are forgiven this time, Ghost. (turning towards the camera) Angels of Death, open thine ears and hear my prophecy. Your reign in the AEWA is quickly coming to a close. I am the power. I am the glory. I am the instrument of destruction. You shall fall at my feet and bow to the one true Lord. Ghost: Master Bedlam, may I assist you on your quest to rid the AEWA of the Angels of Death? Bedlam: That is a commendable offer, my son, but I alone have been given the task. One by one, I shall bring down the Angels. They will be converted or they will suffer the wrath of the newest member of the Church of the Pounding Light! Ghost: (bowing his head in reverence) From your lips to my ears, my master. Bedlam: Angels, God help you all. (The camera fades to black.) ---------- KW: And there's ANOTHER hot feud involving the Church. They've still got a score to settle with the Angels of Death, and it looks like Bishop Bedlam is prepared to take care of it. But can he take them ALL by himself? CC: Oh come on, Ken. You know that no member of the Angels OR the Church is EVER by himself. KW: That's true, and Bedlam looks MUCH more confident than he was as Curate Kill, but will that make him a better wrestler? CC: I guess we'll have to wait and see, but now let's get a word with the former AEWA TV Champion, Randall Osbourne. ---------- (The camera opens on a familiar Kansas City biker bar. The usual festivities are going on amidst the patrons and the camera sweeps over to the far booth in the corner. Sitting in the booth, nursing a glass of water, is former Television Champion of the AEWA, Randall Osbourne.) Osbourne: This place is a home away from home. Every time I pass through my hometown of KC, whether it be on tour or just for a little R and R, I come here. Some of my longtime friends and now fans visit here all the time. And it gives me that feeling of closure. Not to say that I'm some expert on psychology or the workings of the human brain, but for awhile, I've felt that I have had no closure. I headed into that Television title match against Deranged all hyped up and ready to go. I wanted to win, but not in the way that I did. I didn't need the Angels of Death to help me. They might not CALL it help, but it really was. Some asked me why I was ashamed of that title, well folks, I think the answer is as plain as night and day. What everybody saw was the Television Title of the AEWA. But I saw something VERY different. I saw a scar. I saw an open wound that would not heal. I saw a tarnishing mark on me and the things I hold true. I saw a darkness on the Knights of Deliverance and on the AEWA. At first I dreaded having to face my good friend Sir Gallade, but the match did something for me that nothing else did. I felt whole again. Yes, I lost, but I was able to take away that scar. I did lose, but I lost with PRIDE. I lost fair and square. Sir Gallade will have something that I didn't. He knew that he won that title fairly. He can hold his head high. And now that I have that mark taken away, I can hold my head high too. And I have the support of the Knights of Deliverance behind me. Gallade, Ben, and the others were there when I needed them. I feel that the ICU and the Angels of Death don't have that. They aren't a united front and that will lead to their end. My congratulations go out to Justin Trouble for winning the North American Championship. I wish you the best of luck, man. The Knights will be watching your back. And now, there is the little situation with Matthews. If Matthews is going to attack me and beat me like a dog, then he can sure as hell expect to get bitten. Those DDT's were only the icing on the cake. Screw the title, I just want revenge. Yet again, it's time to hit overdrive on the highway of life. We all gotta put the pedal to the metal, 'cuz its a long way to the top, if ya want to rock 'n' roll. (fade to black.) ---------- KW: What the hell is wrong with this guy? He's HAPPY that he lost the TV title? CC: He's just glad that he doesn't have to carry around the burden of something that he didn't earn anymore. I'm sure he's glad to see it fall into the hands of Sir Gallade, as opposed to someone from ICU or something. KW: So what? He still lost the title, and he should be upset. He's just stupid or something. And you know for SURE that he's stupid, since now he wants a piece of Shane Matthews. This boy is just BEGGING for a pounding. CC: I don't think so. Osbourne is more than capable of defeating Matthews, and with the added motivation of Shane's attack on him a while ago, I think his chances are VERY good. KW: Then you're stupid too. CC: Whatever. Now hear from Primal Fear. ---------- (The camera opens on Primal Fear, sitting at the local bar with other AEWA wrestlers after his last match.) Primal Fear: Okay, I have NOT been having a good month. As you all saw, I lost my recent match against the Twilight Strangler. That was bad. Unfortunately, I'm even MORE pissed off at ANOTHER wrestler. Two weeks ago, some young punk named "The Shotgun" Stan Crawford managed to beat me by countout. Crawford, it's not over between us, rookie. I'm making a promise; you have a one-way ticket to the hospital! Crawford, get your fat ASS to the AEWA offices and sign a contract against me! Now, if I seem a little ticked, it's because I AM! I mean, I lost to some chump newcomer, and that won't EVER happen again! I'll see you in the ring, Crawford-- if not there, I'll see ya in an alley somewhere! Watch your back, punk! (fade to black) ---------- KW: Boy, he sure is pissed about losing to Crawford. How pissed do you think he's gonna be after he loses AGAIN? (Ken begins to laugh.) CC: I wouldn't be too sure about that, Ken. Primal Fear is one of the toughest competitors in the HISTORY of the AEWA. He may have been caught off guard by the newcomer this time, but I can guarantee that if they do meet again, Primal Fear will be ready. KW: Ready to get SPANKED again? CC: Not likely. And now we've got an exclusive interview with the brand new AEWA National Tag Team Champions, Graphic Violence! ---------- (The camera opens on Booze Murphy and Leech Rogers, riding in the back of a limousine. They have the National tag team titles with them, next to a bottle of champagne.) Booze: WHOOOO!! We've never ridden in the back of a limo before, but there's a first time for everything! Leech: We did it, punks! No one thought that Graphic Violence could come away with da victory at December Doom, but lookie here-- we got the titles! Booze: Wong and O'Neal, we can give you a little credit. I mean, it was a good match, but just remember-- Graphic Violence is the team to beat now! Leech: You guys came out later in the night and won the WORLD tag team titles, so I think you should thank Booze and I. If it wasn't for us, you wouldn't have gotten that chance, boys! Booze: (lifts up one of the belts) Leech, you think we should go pawn these things? Leech: We could get some major money, but we'd better not. I think the AEWA would get a little mad. Booze: Yeah, anyway, we're outta here! (fade to black) ---------- KW: What great champions. Now THERE'S someone that all the little kiddies can look up to. CC: Oh yeah, they're GREAT rolemodels. Right up there with Michael Irvin and OJ Simpson. KW: My point exactly. And they're gonna be the champs for a LONG time to come so you'd better get used to it. CC: I don't know. With all the competition that the AEWA has to offer, that'll be a tough task. KW: Don't you worry. They can handle it. CC: Time will tell. And now let's get a word with the Twilight Strangler. ---------- (The camera opens on the Twilight Strangler and Miss DeMeanor, waiting for Shane Matthews to arrive in the gym.) DeMeanor: Well, it's almost time for your first training lesson from the man himself-- Shane Matthews! Strangler: Yeah, with this great training, I will become the most dangerous man here in the AEWA. Technical skills combined with brawling-- that's a lethal combination! DeMeanor: It certainly is, and that's why Johnny and I thought of it. (smirks) I mean, after Shane gets done training you, you'll be the best wrestler in this entire federation! (Shane Matthews and Johnny Terror suddenly walk in. They are both carrying towels, and appear to be there for a work-out session.) DeMeanor: Okay, now let's get this damn camera outta here! We've got some training to do, boys! (Fade to black) ---------- KW: With Shane Matthews training the Twilight Strangler, I think it's only a matter of time before we see some gold around his waist. CC: You REALLY think that the Strangler can develop a technical game? KW: With Matthews as his teacher, why not? Matthews has got it all, and if just a little bit of that can rub off on the Strangler, he's gonna be VERY tough to beat. CC: Can't argue with that. We'll have to wait and see what happens with this. It could be VERY interesting. But now let's look at this interview with Brian Rice. ---------- (We see two limousines parked outside the AEWA offices. From one of them, Brian Rice and Jim Williams come out.) Rice: Well, well, well.... Boy, do we have a surprise for you people. I usually have a lot to say, but today, I'm going to let my manager, Jim Williams, do the talking. Williams: Thank you, Brian. Now let me get something off my chest. A little while ago, at the Countdown To Doom show, we all saw Miss DeMeanor hit me with a chair. (he stops for a few seconds and then begins yelling) Miss DeMeanor, you stupid b*tch, you just got yourself in trouble! You'd better watch your back for the next few weeks, whore. You can consider this a threat. That's really all I can say right now. Rice: Thanks, Jim. We're going to rip that girl apart. Miss DeMeanor, look both ways before you cross the street. You can get your punks to protect you, it really doesn't matter. Nothing is going to stop our plan-- an "accident" just might happen to you soon. If any of your goons try to save you, we have something planned for them, too. Bring it on. Williams: We're coming for you, girl. (They both begin laughing, and get back into the limo. As the scene fades to black, both limousines drive away.) ---------- KW: It looks like Brian Rice and Jim Williams aren't too happy with the outcome of Rice's match against the Strangler on the Countdown show, and they're out for revenge. CC: Well Rice DID win the match via disqualification, but apparently that's not good enough for him. And Jim Williams is NOT happy with Miss DeMeanor. KW: Would you be if she cracked a chair over YOUR head? CC: I suppose not. KW: Well that's why he's so mad. I wonder what they've got planned. CC: I don't know, but I've got a feeling that it's NOT gonna be pretty. But now let's get some comments from Joe Reed. ----------- ("Crimson" Joe Reed and the Lady in Red are watching December Doom on Reed's large-screen TV in his basement.) Reed: Damn.... I wish I had gotten a match for this. I would have gone down to San Jose to watch in person, but I was afraid I'd end up getting involved. Lady In Red: Not to worry-- we'll try to get signed up for the next pay per view. I've sent an open contract to Freeman's office, and hopefully we'll get a response soon. Reed: (sighs) I suppose this is the point where I insult some of the wrestlers in the AEWA, or make some ridiculous claims about how badly I'm going to kick everyone's ass. Let's be honest, folks. I wouldn't be wrestling if I didn't think I was as good enough to be among the best. But instead of me yapping about it to the cameras, let's get a match or two to show everyone what I've learned in my ten years of wrestling. Lady In Red: The great thing about the wrestling ring is that it separates the men from the boys-- and he (points to Reed) is one of the men. (fade to black) ---------- KW: You know, this Reed is annoying. CC: Why do you think that? I can think of at least twenty guys in the AEWA who are annoying, and Reed isn't among them. KW: Well he's always talking about how he's gonna rise to the top and stuff, but he's really gonna get his head handed to him once he gets into the heart of the roster. CC: Well that remains to be seen. He has looked VERY impressive in his matches thus far, and I think he's going places in the AEWA. KW: Then you're retarded. CC: The intellectual level of your arguments never ceases to amaze me. KW: Thank you. CC: It WASN'T a compliment. And now let's take a look at this recent interview with the head of ICU, Deathwatch. ---------- (The camera opens on Deathwatch, sitting behind his desk at ICU headquarters. There is a glistening light that is almost blinding coming from the side, but not one piece of that light hits Deathwatch. His long red hair is tied back, and his dark red eyes gaze into the camera. He holds a sinister grin on his face, as always. He wears a gray suit, with a red tie and a white shirt underneath. He begins to speak in his usual deep, yet sophisticated accent.) Deathwatch: My, my, my. What a tangled web we weave. (grins) Today we will be doing an interview with yours truly, and to do so, I shall bring in a man that I have hired since he joined WWEW's broadcasting team. (Deathwatch presses a button underneath the desk and then speaks into the air, never letting his eyes off the camera) Deathwatch: Send in Mr. Derringer, would you? Woman's Voice: Right away, sir. (A man walks out from the elevator, and he walks toward Deathwatch. He has an arrogant smile on his face and has short black hair, brown eyes, and a medium build. He wears a black suit and a white shirt underneath, along with black pinstripe slacks and black dress shoes. The man leans against Deathwatch's desk and relaxes himself there.) Deathwatch: AEWA, meet Zack Derringer, my personal interviewer. Don't you wish you had one too? (grins) Mr. Derringer, if you will. Zack Derringer: No problem, boss. Hey, losers of the AE freakin' WA! My name's Zack Derringer-- and dammit, you were already supposed to know that. Pay attention next time! Anyway, let's get straight on with the interview. Deathwatch, you have much to discuss, correct? (Deathwatch simply nods his head with a stern look upon his face.) Zack Derringer: First off, your comments on December Doom.... One hell of a card if I must say so myself, but one of the main things at that event was where you got into the ring and helped your stablemate-- Deathwatch: (interrupting, clears throat) Sorry for interrupting, but please refer to him as an "associate". This is a corporation, not a wrestling mini-alliance here. Zack Derringer: Terribly sorry. Anyway, you got into the ring and actually helped your associate lose the match, but do you feel as if you gained something in knowing that the Masked Yorel is really Leroy Brown? Deathwatch: I hadn't a care in the world whether or not that was Leroy Brown or whatnot, but Exodus had given me the signal. I just told him before the match that if he wanted to, he could give me a signal and I would help him with the match. It just so happens that he gave me the call and I responded. Zack Derringer: I see. Now, I hear you are looking for more members in ICU. Are there any "prime-cuts" that you have your eye on? Deathwatch: I am always scouting new hopefuls for ICU, of course, but as of right now, I do wish to make a proposal to three men of the AEWA. First off, Kevin Larkin. Mr. Larkin, it has come to my attention that President Bob Freeman is getting on your raw side. Allow me to help you with that problem-- if you will join with me. Because it doesn't just take brute strength to run an operation such as this; we need brain power as well, and you are just the addition that I have been looking for. (Deathwatch reaches under his desk and then presses the button under the desk again) Deathwatch: Ms. Young, please come into the office and take a note for me. Woman's Voice: Yes, sir. (After a few seconds, the elevator door opens. Deathwatch's gorgeous personal secretary, Breanne Young, steps out. She wears a blue skirt and a white shirt; her long brown hair flows behind her as she walks. She takes a seat in front of Deathwatch's desk, and all Zack Derringer can do is stare wide-eyed at her.) Breanne Young: Ready when you are, boss. Deathwatch: Dear Mr. Larkin. Stop. I would like to address you in aligning yourself with International Contracts Unlimited. Stop. There is plenty of pay in it for you, and rest assured, you will get all accommodations you wish. Stop. All that I ask in return is that you bring along your Visigothic Horde; ICU can always use more teams. Stop. Thank you for your time, and I hope you consider this thoroughly. Stop. Sincerely.... (looks to the camera and grins) Deathwatch. Stop. Breanne Young: And where do you want this to be sent to? Deathwatch: To the AEWA's main offices. Address it to Vice President, Kevin Larkin. Breanne Young: Right away, sir. (Young climbs into the elevator again and leaves the camera's view. Derringer shakes himself out of the "trance", then addresses Deathwatch again.) Zack Derringer: Good lord! You have a woman like that working for you as secretary, and all you do is treat her as another "ASSOCIATE"!? Deathwatch: Of course, you couldn't possibly think that I would get personal on a work basis. I am always working; twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week. I don't have time to be getting into any relationships. Strictly business. Zack Derringer: Alright, if you say so. Now, why the Visigothic Horde? I mean, there are plenty of talented teams out there and you picked the Horde-- the only team that gets in trouble at least once a card, and attacked the President of this federation.... Wait a second.... You attacked the President as well.... So I guess it does kind of work out even, doesn't it? Deathwatch: I would answer, but you answered your own question for me. (grins) I will, however, explain to you why I chose the Visigothic Horde instead of another team. Quite simply, they have the talent, the skill, and the endurance to keep themselves as steady threats. They DESERVE a shot in this corporation, don't you think? Zack Derringer: Do I have a choice? (smiles) Just kidding, boss. Now, let's talk about Heavy Metal. It seems all of his title shots are being thrown away due to interference, and his last match with Scorpion was a complete squash. It didn't even take him but two minutes to get rid of the man, and he has not yet received that promised title shot. Your comments.... Deathwatch: Heavy Metal is his own man. He may not have won his last few title shots, but that wasn't his fault, now was it? No, it was Scorpion's fault, and Heavy Metal has dealt with Scorpion and now looks to a brighter future. Whether or not he obtains the World title is none of my business, but I wish him the best of luck. Now let's talk about me.... I haven't gotten a match at all in a long while, and quite frankly, I think I'm due. Now, what better a person to choose than Justin Trouble. You wanted me so bad? Now you have me. Zack Derringer: Do you have any doubts about that match with Trouble? I mean, I know that he doesn't stand a chance, but how do you feel about him? Deathwatch: I think he is what his name says he is-- trouble. He's trouble for me and my business; but other than that, he is a man who wants nothing more than to make his mark on this federation. Now Trouble has himself the North American title, and I congratulate him on that victory, but unfortunately for him.... I want his head on a silver platter. The title means nothing to me; I just want to prove my point. ICU is the ONLY thing, and any man who opposes it shall pay the price. Zack Derringer: How about the newest wrestler in the AEWA, who also happens to be from WWEW, Damian "The Predator" Steele? Do you have any plans for him? Deathwatch: Funny you should mention him. Mr. Steele has had an offer for his services in ICU; all he has to do is accept. Mr. Steele, does $50,000 a month suit you? How about $500,000? The price is no matter to me; I have enough money in stocks to make Ross Perot cry. (grins) Zack Derringer: You would be willing to pay him THAT much, just for his services in ICU? I'm putting a lot of trust in this one, boss. When you know, I guess you just know, huh? Deathwatch: You could say that. Well, I haven't enough time left in my schedule for this interview, but it has been great to see you again, Mr. Derringer. 'Till next time then.... Zack Derringer: Yeah, thanks for your time.... Actually, you PAY me to do this.... Nevermind. Hey, one more thing. (whispering) Ya think I could get that chick's phone number? Deathwatch: (chuckling) I don't see any harm in it. (rips out a piece of paper from an address roller sitting on his desk, and hands it to Derringer) Just tell her-- "Deathwatch sent me." (laughs and grins) AEWA, ICU is the only way and you all shall realize that. Justin Trouble, you will be the next victim. Prepare to lose it all-- to the power of International Contracts Unlimited! (fade to black) ---------- KW: It looks like Deathwatch has his eye on some new members for ICU, and if he can acquire them, the AEWA is in deep trouble! CC: And among them is AEWA Vice-President Kevin Larkin. How can HE join the ICU when he's an AEWA official? KW: Eric Bischoff joined the nWo, didn't he? CC: I suppose he did, and you're right-- if Deathwatch gets what he's seeking, the entire AEWA is in trouble. KW: Of course I'm right. I'm always right. CC: If you say so.
