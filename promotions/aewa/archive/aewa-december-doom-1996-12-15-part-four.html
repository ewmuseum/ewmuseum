AEWA — Card Archive

December Doom (12/15, part four)

22 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
********************************************************* CONTINUED FROM PART THREE ********************************************************* MW: I've got to agree with you there, Bertha. Up next, it's time for that big four-way tag team match. The West Coast Connection will be defending their World tag team titles against the Graveyard Shift and Heaven & Hell and the Visigothic Horde. Now, before the match begins, let me remind you all of something-- we've never had a match like this before. I have no idea what to expect. BD: Neither do I, Wilson. With that many guys in a match at one time, we might have some SERIOUS security problems. It's going to be GREAT. LB: And one thing's for sure-- ALL the teams will be targeting the West Coast Connection. When the match is over, I think we'll be seeing new tag team champions. MW: That's a distinct possibility. As always, the AEWA World Tag Team Championship situation is a complete and utter mess. In the last few months, the Graveyard Shift and West Coast Connection have traded the titles back and forth; while Heaven & Hell forced Bob Freeman into putting them into title contention, under their own terms, without having ever fought together as a team. It all came to a head in a triangle match a few weeks ago, when returning vice president Kevin Larkin stuck his nose in the whole affair, and his team launched an attack on two of the teams in that matchup. So now we're throwing every team involved into one ring and we're going to let all four battle it out for the AEWA World Tag Team Championship. BD: And if I were the West Coast Connection, I'd start wondering why I have to defend my titles against three teams at the same time. You know what the answer is, Wilson? MW: Because there is obviously no order or control in the championship committee right now. BD: No, the West Coast Connection's problems stem from the way they dismissed their managers, the men who sacrificed everything to get them where they are today. They no longer have anyone looking out for their interests and fighting for them administratively. MW: Sacrificed everything? Last I heard, Grohl and Muggs were taking 30% of everything the WCC got, and their interference was costing their team during every match. BD: Let's just get this match started.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is a four-way match for the AEWA World tag team titles! Introducing first, at a combined weight of 641 pounds, HEAVEN & HELL! ("Sympathy For The Devil" begins playing loudly, as Cardinal Chaos and Mister Crowley make their entrance. They get a HUGE round of boos from the capacity crowd.) MW: There they are, the two men who made the AEWA what it is today with their incredible feud earlier this year. Now they have joined forces, and I don't know if anyone can stop them as a team. Lord knows no one stopped them when they were alone. LB: And here come those Shift idiots.... RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing next, at a combined weight of 696 pounds, please welcome the GRAVEYARD SHIFT! (The lights dim, the opening chords of "Thriller" resonate throughout the electrified arena, and The Grave Dweller and Mortician are already in the ring when the lights are restored. Their manager, Coffin, is standing at ringside.) MW: And here are the most prolific tag team in AEWA history, the four-time world champions, the Graveyard Shift! These guys look great tonight. BD: They have always been a dominant force around here, but in this four-way match, they really don't have a CHANCE. Let's get serious, Wilson. MW: We'll see about that, Big Daddy. The Visigothic Horde should be coming out momentarily.... (There is a rather lengthy pause, but the Horde doesn't appear.) MW: Um.... we are waiting for the arrival of the Visigothic Horde, Fritigern and Athanaric. This is their first crack at AEWA gold, and we don't know why they aren't here. Can we switch to Hank Thompson backstage? BD: We just got a camera back there, so let's try to find out what the hell is going on.... ------------- (The camera switches to Hank Thompson, holding a microphone in the backstage area. There is an official-looking man standing next to him.) Thompson: Matt, I'm here with Michael Voigt, a representative of the AEWA Board of Directors, and he has just been meeting with Kevin Larkin and Bob Freeman, and he has an announcement to make. Voigt: That's right, Hank. The AEWA Board of Directors met earlier today and have agreed to accept a proposal submitted by President Freeman. It revokes the Visigothic Horde's right to participate in a championship match in the tag team division due to the potential conflict of interest, as their manager is also the vice president responsible for overseeing the tag team division. Thompson: And this decision is effective immediately? Voigt: That is correct. Thompson: As Vice President of the tag team division, is Mister Larkin in a position to offer his own team a title opportunity anyway? Voigt: While the Vice Presidents of the AEWA are permitted to exercise considerable authority over their divisions, they are answerable to two parties-- the Commissioner's Office and the Board of Directors. If Mister Larkin were to attempt to involve his team in this matchup, I'm sure he would jeopardize his continued employment with the AEWA. Thompson: So what will happen with the upcoming four-way match? Voigt: President Freeman is busy securing a fourth team to participate in the matchup. As he gets an additional team ready, he wants the West Coast Connection to be announced. Thompson: Okay, thank you, Mister Voigt. Let's go back to ringside.... (The camera quickly returns to our broadcast team, but they seem to be even MORE confused.) ------------- MW: Okay, so the Visigothic Horde won't be competing in this match? But we still don't know who's going to take their place. BD: So when will we find out? MW: Well, here come the tag team champions. After they get to the ring, maybe we'll find out who will be taking the Horde's place. RING ANNOUNCER: Here are the AEWA World tag team champions, at a combined weight of 610 pounds, being led to the ring by their bodyguard, Hood Lum, THE WEST COAST CONNECTION! (As "Tonight, Tonight" begins playing, Hood Lum follows MC Read and Axl Sharkman to the ring. They both have on their traditional black & white flannel jackets and wraparound shades.) MW: It is customary for the champions to be introduced last, but it's also customary to have challengers signed to a contract before the announcements begin! Here are the three-time AEWA World Tag Team Champions, along with their-- BD: (interrupting) "Associate". MW: --right, their "associate", Hood Lum. And they look a little confused as to why there are only two out of three teams waiting for them. Wait one second.... I understand we're going back to Hank again. Hank, are you there? Thompson: (we can suddenly hear his voice) All right, Matt. I've just been informed that we have a fourth team and they will be making their entrance any second now. MW: Well, who is it? Thompson: I haven't been told yet, Matt. But we should all find out in a few seconds. RING ANNOUNCER: And finally, the fourth team, at a combined weight of 492 pounds, please welcome.... THE MACHINES! ("Welcome to the Machine" plays as Simon O'Neal and Paul Wong arrive without the National belts. Both men seem a little dazed and in more than a little agony. The fedoras and sunglasses have been replaced by bandages and swellings) MW: YOU HAVE GOT TO BE KIDDING ME! These guys can barely walk, and have taken a brutal beating at the hands of the AEWA's preeminent thugs less than an hour ago, and you're going to throw them in there with those six? BD: I bet it was Paul's fault. Simon was always the bright one in that pair, and he must have advocated waiting until a better opportunity arose. But you take someone like Paul and start slapping him in the head and all that, you see, you do that and.... what were we talking about again? MW: I believe you were saying good night and heading home early to catch the encore presentation on TV. BD: Oh, right, I almost forgot. (stands up) Ladies and gentlemen, Big Daddy is leaving the building! (he walks past the curtain and leaves) MW: I can't believe that worked. Maybe we can FINALLY get some peace and quiet around here. LB: (chuckles) Good thinking, Matt. MW: Anyway, we've flipped the coins, and it looks like the West Coast Connection are starting it off against the Graveyard Shift. LB: We've got two referees for this one-- Roger Peterson in the ring and David Mitchell on the outside, and we hope that this will allow for a greater sense of control. We're off, and MC Read is starting off with the Mortician. MW: These guys have seen a lot of each other during the past few months. And now we see why the West Coast Connection are the champions- they are some of the most street-smart guys to have ever come through here. As the Mortician waits to lock up, Read tags Simon O'Neal, who at this point is already in a daze. Simon staggers into the ropes, AND THE MORTICIAN NAILS HIM WITH A RUNNING CLOTHESLINE! The cover.... One...Two...Kickout LB: And Simon stuck his foot on the bottom rope, and the outside ref, Mitchell, called Peterson's attention to it. Now the Mortician pulls Simon up, and tags in Mister Crowley. Well, not so much tags, as punches in the mush. MW: Crowley follows the Mortician across the ring, then decides to take advantage of a still-dazed Simon O'Neal. He pulls him back up by the hair and blasts him in the face with a right hand. (Big Daddy suddenly walks back out to the broadcast booth, with a confused expression on his face, and taps Wilson on the shoulder.) BD: Wait, why was I going home in the middle of the match? MW: You wanted to see it on TV. BD: I did? MW: You were pretty insistent on it. BD: I was? MW: Yep. BD: Um.... Okay. (he leaves again) MW: I should have thought of this MONTHS ago. LB: Yeah, it was a pretty damn good idea, Wilson. Anyway, the action is getting pretty intense back in the ring.... MW: Well, while I was interrupted, Simon reached in and pulled out a little something-- I know what you're thinking, but this time I'm talking about guts, not a chain or anything like that. He even managed to get a couple of two-counts on Crowley. LB: And Simon has the presence of mind to take advantage of the situation and get out. He staggers to the Graveyard Shift's corner, but they refuse to tag. They know the more they save their energy, the better off they are. MW: Simon falls into the WCC corner and tags in Axl Sharkman. And Axl goes to work on Crowley. He remembers what happened in that triangle match. LB: Sharkman has Crowley in the WCC corner, and Read makes sure he gets out of the way before he is inadvertently tagged in to face his partner. Meanwhile Hood Lum has been taking O'Neal to task for his tag of Axl, AND JUST SLAMMED SIMON TO THE FLOOR! MW: Paul Wong is over to help his partner out, and David Mitchell is shooing Hood Lum back to his team's corner. While Peterson looks to see what the commotion is, Read is back in and he and Sharkman NAIL Crowley with a double DDT! LB: Read is back out, and Sharkman goes up top-- FLYING CLOTHESLINE ON CROWLEY! That had a LOT of impact! MW: Instead of covering the man, he goes for a blatant chokehold. And these are the guys I just described as street-smart. (notices that Big Daddy has once again returned to the broadcast booth) I thought you were gone. BD: Uh, do you remember where I parked? MW: Didn't you take a cab? BD: Oh, right. Do you remember the number of that company? MW: Here it is. (writes it down) Now, if you don't mind, I have a match to call.... BD: (looks into the ring) Man, I better hurry. (he walks past the curtain and disappears into the locker room again) MW: Once again, he distracted me from the action. Now Mister Crowley is throwing Axl Sharkman around like he were a rag doll-- HE NAILS HIM WITH A SCOOP SLAM! LB: Crowley with a whip, and Sharkman goes into the Shift's corner and makes the quick tag to the Grave Dweller. We still haven't seen Paul Wong or Cardinal Chaos in the ring yet, and no one has been eliminated. MW: It has really just been Simon O'Neal and Mister Crowley who have been suffering, but Crowley seems to not be affected by it anymore. The Dweller FLATTENS Crowley with a hard right hand, and goes for the elbow drop.... BUT CROWLEY ROLLS OUT OF THE WAY! LB: Crowley is up, he waits for the Dweller to get back to his feet-- running clothesline, the Dweller ducked! And Crowley runs right into his own corner and collides with Cardinal Chaos, who hits the floor. MW: Referee Peterson is counting Crowley out, but Mitchell is waving Chaos in. He signals that there was a tag, and Peterson accepts it. (chuckles) For the first time this match, someone has tagged his own partner. LB: Cardinal Chaos tries to shake the cobwebs out, and crawls into the ring to face the Grave Dweller. Chaos and the Dweller tie up, and Chaos connects with a knee in the groin. Simple, but it works. Chaos now with a kick in the solar plexus, and he hoists him up-- he's going for the Inverted Crucifix! HE STUCK HIM! The cover.... One...Two...Three! MW: We have our first elimination, as the Graveyard Shift, the team that has dominated this inaugural year of AEWA tag team action, is being sent back to the locker area. One down, three to go! LB: And the referees are ordering the West Coast Connection into the ring! This isn't fair! MW: A match like this comes with a hundred logistical problems to solve, and it was decided that when a team was eliminated, the team on their right had to enter. If that team was already in the ring, then the two wrestlers from that team had to face one another. In comes MC Read, and here comes Big Daddy to the booth again! BD: (whispering) Hey, am I gonna get in trouble for this? I mean, I can just leave in the middle of a pay-per-view? MW: Don't worry, I'll take care of it. BD: You're sure? MW: Don't worry. Just hurry out of here. BD: You know, you're all right, Williams. MW: Wilson. BD: Whatever. You're all right. Makes me sorry I-- um, nevermind. (he walks back to the locker room) MW: Sorry you what? Well, I hope he's really gone this time. Anyway, Chaos and Read are in there now, and it is all Chaos. Eliminating the Shift like that must have set off a surge of confidence, and he is demonstrating the brawling ability that took him to the top. He connects with a BIG suplex, and goes for the cover.... One...Two...Kickout LB: MC Read didn't win three tag team titles by getting pinned so easily. NOW CHAOS IS GOING FOR ANOTHER INVERTED CRUCIFIX-- but he can't quite get him up there. MW: Read is kicking his legs, trying to upset Chaos' attempt to get balanced. Chaos can't hold him, and Read is reversing it into a back body drop. Over he goes, and Read heads for the Machines' corner and tags Simon O'Neal back in! Now, I can't agree with this strategy. The Machines are in such a bad state right now, the other teams should concentrate on eliminating one another, then quickly eliminate the Machines last. LB: No, they need to get the Machines out of there NOW. Simon and Chaos lock up; not a smart move by Simon, far and away the smallest man in this matchup. Simon with a single leg takedown, into a spinning toe hold, now he reaches out and tags Paul Wong. MW: In a match like this, the ring apron is where you want to be, but I don't think the Machines are thinking very clearly right now. Paul with an elbow drop on the prone Chaos, and he tags Simon right back. If there weren't a third team resting on the ring apron, this would be terrific strategy. It's obvious that they were in no shape for this match. Simon and Chaos lock up again, and Chaos has been in there for a long time. LB: His opponents have tagged in and out almost a half dozen times since he's been in there, and his partner was in for a long time before that. But he's eliminated one team already tonight, the Graveyard Shift, and that's quite impressive. Now Chaos with a hiptoss, O'Neal hits the mat and grabs Chaos by the legs, and rolls him back.... One...Two...Kickout MW: Back on their feet, and Chaos with a right hand-- BLOCKED! A left-- BLOCKED AGAIN! Simon kicks him in the groin, grabs a handful of tights and rolls him up.... One...Two...Kickout LB: Simon heads for the empty corner, and goes up top. Chaos catches up to him, and drives a fist into Simon's face. His left eye is swelling up really badly now, and I think his nightlife is going to need a break this week. He is just completely black and blue, and is starting to look a little like a raccoon. But he's fighting back, and is returning Chaos' blows, but Chaos is more powerful, and his punches carry more force. MW: Now he's going up, Simon is still seated on that top rope, and up he goes-- SUPERPLEX! No, it was a Fisherman's superplex; he had a hold of the leg.... One...Two...Kickout LB: Now the frustration is getting to Chaos. He is stomping away at his prone foe, kneels down and connects with a flurry of fists to Simon's face. ELBOW DROP BY CHAOS! He picks Simon back up, and Simon's legs are like jelly-- he can hardly stand on his own. MW: Chaos whips him into the ropes, and Simon ducks the clothesline and comes back for the flying body press-- CHAOS CAUGHT HIM! He just caught him in mid air, and dumps him out over the top rope, and right in front of Hood Lum-- the WCC's "associate"! LB: Hood Lum makes his move, but David Mitchell is right there, and he tells Hood Lum that anything he may do here could cost his team the title-- if you get eliminated in any way, you're out, and the other two teams fight for the belts. I wonder if Chaos dropped O'Neal out there just to goad Hood Lum into getting his team disqualified.... MW: Chaos goes out there now, and slams O'Neal into the ringpost. O'Neal slumps to the floor, and Chaos tosses him back in and slides in under the bottom rope after him. Simon reaches up and tags Axl Sharkman! The West Coast Connection has spent about 15 minutes just watching their competition fight it out! Now they're back in. LB: Sharkman hooks up with Chaos, and spins for a backslide. Sharkman is pulling him over.... Chaos is straining to resist..... Sharkman is well-rested, so he has a real strength advantage right now.... He still can't quite get him.... Chaos starts pulling Sharkman-- it's like a human teeter-totter. Sharkman finds the strength, but Hood Lum is up on the apron and Mitchell is trying to get him out of there. MW: Sharkman gets Chaos' shoulders down, while Peterson gets in position and Mitchell jaws with Hood Lum. Read reaches over the top rope and has a hold of Chaos' ankles! One...Two.......Three! LB: That's it, Heaven & Hell are outta here! Chaos gave it one hell of a run, and the tag team belts could possibly be in his future-- but not tonight! Heaven & Hell are very upset, and they're ushered out of here. MW: We are down to the West Coast Connection and the Machines, folks. Paul Wong is now the man in the best shape on his team, and he is in the ring to face Axl Sharkman. LB: Sharkman executes a fireman's carry and goes for the legdrop-- he missed! Paul is up for an elbow drop- HE MISSED AS WELL! BD: (walks up to the broadcast booth again) Pssst.... MW: What is it now? I thought you were going to be home by now! BD: You won't forget to punch my time card for me, will you? MW: You don't use a time card. BD: Sure I do. MW: Since when? BD: I always have. Bertha does, too. Don't you, Wilson? MW: Of course not. Why do you two need time cards? BD: Why don't you? MW: Perhaps because the AEWA trusts me to show up on time and to not leave early. BD: Whatever, just punch mine when you leave, okay? MW: Listen, I'm trying to call a match here. Paul with a running clothesline, and down goes Read! How did he get in there? (turns back to Big Daddy) Now you've made me miss what happened! BD: Are you going to do it or not? MW: Do you mind? Read connects with a single leg takedown, and a double leg cradle.... One...Two...Kickout BD: Well, if you aren't going to-- MW: Kickout! Either sit down and shut up or go already, Big Daddy! We almost had a finish at last, and you're standing here talking about your time card! BD: Stop interrupting! I was tryin' to say that if you won't do it, I'm not going, so move over, I need to sit back down. Now who's fighting in this one? MW: Axl Sharkman tags in. Axl Sharkman and Paul Wong in there, and this has gone on for over an hour now, I believe. Axl and Paul are just trading punches and kicks, now. Paul with the slight advantage, scoops him up, BODYSLAM! But not a particularly hard one. Axl rolls out to the floor, and confers with Read and Hood Lum. Wong is following him. BD: This guy's crazy. He's walking into a one-on-three! LB: Paul Wong hasn't exactly been known for his intelligence lately, Big Daddy. He's actually rather insane. MW: He grabs Axl, as Read and Hood Lum scatter while Mitchell tries to head off any trouble. Paul drags him back into the ring, and they lock up in the Machines' corner. Simon has the presence of mind to grab a hold of Sharkman's arms and Paul works over the exposed midsection. Sharkman wriggles free. Axl and Paul circle each other; neither man expected it to last this long. LB: This has been GREAT, and it's not even over yet! MW: Sharkman is looking for the test of strength. Paul looks wary-- but he goes for it. And Sharkman suckers him into a small package.... One...Two...Simon breaks the pin! LB: Simon breaks the pin just in time with a running elbow drop. That brings in Read, and Hood Lum is on his way to the ring. Mitchell has a hold of Hood Lum's jacket, and Peterson is alone to restore order in the ring, where all four men are still at it. MW: Read drops O'Neal with a right hand, while Paul sends Sharkman into the ropes. Peterson sends Read back to his corner, and Hood Lum is threatening Mitchell. LB: Jeez, these referees seem to be CONTRIBUTING to the anarchy in there. MW: Sharkman tosses down Paul with a sunset flip! Paul really hit the canvas HARD after that maneuver, folks. LB: But wait a minute-- look at Simon! He's pulling something out of his boot! Is that what I think it is? BD: HE'S GOT THAT CHAIN! MW: Sharkman is going for a cover on Wong, but O'Neal SLUGS him with that chain before he can! OUCH! The referees didn't see it! LB: Wong rolls Sharkman back with a double leg cradle, and Peterson runs over to make the count.... One...Two....Three! (DING DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here are your winners, and NEW AEWA World tag team champions, THE MACHINES! MW: That was AWESOME! The Machines have upgraded their gold tonight, reaching down deep to pull out the biggest win of their careers! BD: And the West Coast Connection are livid.... MW: The crowd is stunned. There were an awful lot of fans of both teams out there tonight, and after what we saw earlier, I don't think anyone foresaw that! Hell, they weren't even in the match when the first teams were announced! And in one night, the balance of power in the AEWA Tag Team division has shifted. National Champions-- Graphic Violence. World Champions-- the Machines. On the outside looking in, the Visigothic Horde. But that's another story, folks. LB: The Horde must be EXTREMELY angry that they didn't get a shot in this match. Frankly, that just wasn't fair. MW: I thought it was MORE than fair, Bertha. LB: Well, that doesn't surprise me. So what's coming up now, Wilson? *********************************************************************** TO BE CONTINUED IN PART FIVE *********************************************************************** \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- American E-Wrestling Association The AEWA-- We Deliver!
