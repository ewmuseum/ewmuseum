Sunday, June 22nd, Card (3/3)

22 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Another promo for "Night of the Lepus". They really want you to tune in for this one. Killer rabbits, wolf-sized, people. A television event of a lifetime.) EL: OK- let's talk to the team of Lt. Lawson and Max Robertson! {Max Robertson, Lt. Lawson, and Major Mayhem head down to ringside, ignoring the loud boos from the audience. They approach the announcer's desk: EL: Well, what... MVM: {Grabbing the Microphone from Lane.} That's enough, Private. This was a successful military operation, carried out by the finest warriors in this sport! DL: Hammer, Thomas... What a pathetic, cowardly attack by you two. How typical. They can't beat me by themselves, and they can't beat Robertson by themselves. So they cook up this little parlor game, like a couple of little girls, and they steal Mr. Robertson's belt. We shall recover the property you confiscated, and you will be punished. MR: The two of you have made a very, very big mistake. Once more, the people in this federation don't seem to understand who they are dealing with. Lawson and I are the best at what we do because this is all we do. He's a soldier by trade; a professional fighter. I wrestle; that's all I do. When you crossed us, you crossed the best. Lawson's proven that against you time and time again. I've proven it against Snoozer and the Idiot over and over. But just wait, 'cause you ain't seen nothing yet. I _will_ reclaim my title. We _will_ have our vengeance. That's the Professional way. That's the only way. (Exeunt Lawson, Robertson, and Mayhem) EL: Well, they were telling me in the back that they think this Masked Man is either Marc Thomas or someone aligned with Thomas and Hammer, and that they think not just Hammer, but Thomas, too, was involved in that controversial title match last week. Of course, the Masked Man has attacked Marc Thomas in the past, and has already wrestled earlier tonight, even thought Marc Thomas is scheduled for this match next. We'll see what happens. Let's get the introductions. DM: Ladies and gentlemen- this next contest is set for one fall with a fifteen-minute time limit. Introducing first, in the corner to my left, accompanied by Major Mayhem, at a combined weight of 419 lb., here are the former CWAA Heavyweight Champion, Max "the Professional" Robertson, and CWAA Television Champion, Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson! (Mixed reaction, given their opposition for the night) DM: And their opponents- ("Bad to the Bone" plays, and the crowd against is mixed in its reaction) DM: -at a combined weight of 555 lb., here are Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas! Your referee Chris- EL: Chris Herman has his hands full as all four men in the ring now going at it- Jak Hammer lugging it out with Lt. Lawson- Hammer sends him for the ride- clothesline! In the meantime, Max with a poke in the eye on Thomas and goes right to work on the arm. We've got total mayhem in the ring, the Major barking orders, Jak Hammer choking the TV Champ on the top rope- Max with a hammerlock on the mat on Thomas. Now Jak Hammer abandons the lt.- elbow drop on Max. He stands Max up- big right to the jaw. Chris Herman trying to restore order- and Hammer just shoves him to the mat. The Major passes that flagpole to Lawson- Chris Herman warning him- and Lawson tosses him to the mat! Chris Herman being thrown around by both teams. Marc Thomas holding Max back as Jak Hammer kicks him in the ribs- and look out! (Lawson, flagpole, figure it out.) EL: He swung that thing like a baseball bat at Jak Hammer's back! Chris Herman back up now- calling for the bell- the Major in there now. Lawson clubbing Hammer in the legs with that flagpole. Thomas tosses Robertson to the floor now- now he grabs Lawson- Icebreaker! Jeff Howard out here now to help restore order- he checks on Max- and Max just punched our Senior Official! He belted him right in the mush! Both referees getting abused in this one- here comes the Preacher now, as the Major kicking at the fallen Chris Herman! And Kore Wrench spins the Major around and just popped him one! (Pop) EL: The Lt. and Jak Hammer laid out in the ring, and Marc Thomas pulls off one of his partner's boots- what is he doing? He raises it- going to club the Preacher- Max grabs it! Max up behind Thomas- we have just absolute mayhem in there now- Max with a kick in the groin. Jeff Howard and Chris Herman up now- the Major trying to attend to Lawson- Lawson up on all fours- Hammer clutching his knee. Our two referees trying to separate Marc Thomas and Max Robertson- and each referee gets a punch in the mouth for his troubles. Kore Wrench in the ring now- and here come Sheridan and Barnes. None of these men likely to abide by the abuse of CWAA officials like this. Sheridan and Barnes after Max- who dives for the floor. Kore Wrench scoops Thomas up- body slam! Thomas slides under the bottom rope. Lawson pauses for one more kick on Hammer- Joshua Barnes with a steel chair in his hand leans over the rope and swipes at Max- just missed by a hair. Lawson out of there, and Marc Thomas pulled to safety! Wrench, Sheradin and Barnes standing alone in the ring now- both referees are down, and the four men wrestling are all on the floor on opposite sides of the ring. OK- Hammer and Thomas heading back up the aisleway to the lockers. Max shouting at Barnes about something- Wrench and Sheradin holding Joshua back, and the Major now leading his man and the Professional away from the ring. we have to take another break, and when we get back, we'll have our main event- if we can find a referee! (Monday nights on WYXZ-TV Channel 39, at 11 PM, back-to-back, it's "Three's Company" and "The Ropers"! That wacky Mr. Furley is now the landlord for Jack, Janet and Chrissy, but you can still follow the zany adventures of Helen and Stanley Roper at 11:30! This week, Chrissy thinks Jack and Janet are having an affair! Then, Helen complains that Stanley has let the romance go out of their marriage. Right here on WYXZ-TV Ch. 39!) EL: OK- we're back and just about ready for our special feature match- the tag titles are going to be on the line! Looks like Jeff Howard is back up and ready to officiate this contest- I think a few top stars are about to take a big hit to their wallets for what we just saw, as Jak Hammer, Lt. Lawson- all of them putting their hands on our referees, which is a very serious no-no. Before we get started, let me tell you about what's coming up this next week. At AEWA Battle Klash II, you'll see Sheradin and Barnes tackle the duo of Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas- maybe for the tag belts! Then Lt. Lawson defends the TV Title against English light-heavyweight sensation, Fuse! Then here, next week, in the world-famous Sportatorium, Randy Sheradin meets Mark Sherrick- what a match that will be. Jimmy Dangerous and Little Yvette team up to face the team of Double Jeopardy. Then, three big matches- all of them could be a main event in any arena in the country. Lt. Lawson meets Jak Hammer- inside a steel cage! Then, the match Joshua Barnes has wanted for months: Barnes and Max Robertson in a lumberjack match! Then, in our special feature match- it has been signed- Michael Anderson and Byron McNultey take on Twister in Texas-Tornado Penalty-Box match- if Twister loses, they'll never team again! And if the Pretty Boy and Marvelous Michael can get past Sheradin and Barnes tonight, that last match will be for the tag titles! (Wrench offers a good-luck-handshake to fellow do-gooders Sheradin and Barnes before leaving) EL: OK- let's get the introductions. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is set for one-fall with television time remaining, and is for the CWAA Tag Team championship! Introducing first, in the corner to my left, the challengers! At a combined weight of 462 lb. they are Joshua Barnes and "the Dreamer" Randy Sheradin! (Big, big pop) DM: And their opponents- ("Sharp Dressed Man" starts playing, accompanied by booing) DM: -from parts unknown, at a combined weight of 497 lb., they are the CWAA Tag Team Champions, "Marvelous" Michael Anderson and "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey! Your referee, Jeff Howard. (Anderson and McNultey are again clad in sequined robes and do their little strut and dance routine) EL: OK- the tag titles are on the line, as the CWAA's first Heavyweight Champ tries to become our first two-time champion. Randy Sheradin starting off for his team- Anderson and McNultey can't seem to decide. Let's see- wait, they're asking for the house mike. BM: You know, Mikey, we've got the big feature match here, our big moment in the spotlight again, showing off our stuff for all the lovely ladies out there. Now I know that if I said, "Hey, Mikey. Let me start off against this reject from the midget wrestling school, and we'll wrap this one up nice and quick," but then you wouldn't get a chance to show your stuff, since, being the greatest partner anyone could have, you'd let me wrestle circles against this clown all by myself. Now- would you all please shut up for two seconds- we're letting you listen in on how champions strategize here! Now, Mikey, to show you how much I appreciate you as a partner, I'm going to stand aside and offer you the opportunity to take the spotlight and give this half-pint the beating he deserves, and- (Pop) EL: Look out! Randy Sheradin has heard enough, and just gave the champs a double-noggin-knocker! A meeting of the minds, as it were. And Anderson and McNultey are knocked silly. Sheradin sends the Pretty Boy for the ride- dropkick! Now he takes Marvelous Michael Anderson- monkey flip- now he dropkicks him- right out of his robe! Now Randy Sheradin takes the robe as the champs roll out to the floor. And Randy Sheradin puts on Anderson's robe and does a little strutting of his own! (The crowd apparently much prefers his strutting to the champs') EL: Now Sheradin takes the robe off and hands it to his partner. (Big pop as Barnes blows his nose into it) EL: The champs are having a conniption fit on the floor now! Michael Anderson in the ring now- charges at the Dreamer- catches him in an arm drag takedown! And again! Randy Sheradin throwing the champion around! Tags in Joshua Barnes. Anderson charges, and- (chuckle) (Anderson sees Barnes' cocked fists and slams on the break, and slips on his back) EL: Barnes covers- 1... 2... kickout! Now he pulls Anderson up- body slam. The Pretty Boy in the ring now, too- clothesline by Joshua Barnes. Barnes now takes McNultey into the neutral corner- starts punching away- 2... 3... 4... 5... look out! Anderson with a knee in the back, and Barnes collapses to the mat. Randy Sheradin shouting encouragement to his partner. Anderson stomping away now. McNultey rolls to the floor- stands up and swings wildly at nothing. (Pop as the Pretty Boy falls flat on his face on the arena floor) EL: Michael Anderson now choking Barnes on that bottom rope- Jeff Howard starts his count- and Anderson breaks on 4. But right back to the choke! Byron McNultey back to his corner. Anderson now to a camel clutch- way down on Barnes' back. McNultey asking for the tag now, and Anderson obliges. The Pretty Boy in there now, drops an elbow on Joshua Barnes. Snap mares him over now, and a kick in the back. Sends him for the ride- knee to the midsection. Now the Pretty Boy to a Russian legsweep. Covers 1... 2... kickout. McNultey takes Barnes by the hair, tags in Anderson. Double whip- double elbow smashes. Anderson now tosses Barnes into the neutral corner, and smashes his head against the turnbuckle. Anderson tags back out- the Pretty Boy with a series of kicks for Joshua Barnes. Snap mares him over- the cover- 1... 2.... kickout. Tag to Anderson. As McNultey holds Barnes' legs down, Anderson off the ropes with an elbow drop. 1... 2... kickout. Tag to the Pretty Boy- double body slam. 1... 2... and the Dreamer breaks the pin! (Pop) EL: Jeff Howard ordering Sheradin out of the ring- and the champs whip Joshua into the ropes- double elbows again. The Pretty Boy out, Anderson covers- 1... 2... kickout again. They just can't seem to put him away, and the frustration showing clearly on the champs' faces. The Pretty Boy tags back in- they sends Barnes for the ride- double back drop- no! (Pop) EL: Joshua Barnes grabs each man by the hair and drives their heads into the mat! All three men down, and Barnes needs to tag out very badly. Sheradin straining for the tag, Barnes down just out of reach, and the champs look just about out of it. The Pretty Buy pulling himself up by the ropes. Barnes rolling over to reach to his partner- we're running short on time, here- if we run out of time, the match ends, this being a television-time matchup. Sheradin needs to tag in. Both champs up on spaghetti legs now- the Pretty Boy leaning on the ropes for support. (Huge pop) EL: Barnes makes the tag! The Dreamer in there now- dropkick for Anderson- now he charges at the Pretty Boy- clotheslines him right over the top rope! The Pretty Boy lading right in front of my table here. Sheradin now grabs Anderson- swinging neckbreaker! The cover- 1... 2... no! Had his foot on the bottom rope! Sheradin with the whip- McNultey grabs a title belt- where's he going? Randy Sheradin with a back suplex- lays Anderson out in the middle of the ring- he's going up top- Dream Come true coming up- Joshua Barnes yelling for him to turn around- look out! The Pretty Boy just reached up and blasted the Dreamer in the head with the title belt! Sheradin falls to the mat- Jeff Howard calling for the bell- and here comes Joshua Barnes! He grabs the Pretty Boy the throat and lifts him off the ground! Pulls him over the top rope- sends him for the ride- spinebuster slam! Joshua Barnes now slams Anderson right next to his partner- Barnes covers both men- Jeff Howard calls for the bell again- Joshua Barnes counting the pin himself- 1... 2... 3. well, it won't count, but Joshua Barnes has just pinned the CWAA Tag Team Champions all by himself. Randy Sheradin back on his feet grabs the title belts and holds them aloft- (Pop) EL: -but they are not the new tag team champions. Disqualification victory, and we have to go- don't forget the exciting action next week- Lawson and Hammer in the cage, an update on the vacant title, the Penalty Box match, and the lumberjack match! See you then!
