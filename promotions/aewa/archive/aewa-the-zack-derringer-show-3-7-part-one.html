The Zack Derringer Show (3/7, part one)

11 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * THE ZACK DERRINGER SHOW * * * * Live, from the ICU Studios ("King Nothing" by Metallica begins to play loudly, as the camera opens on Zack Derringer and Hank Thompson sitting behind a desk. Derringer wears a black suit with a blue shirt underneath, he has a gold clip holding his tie to his shirt and his long, black hair hangs to the sides of his head. Hank Thompson wears a blue suit with a white shirt underneath, a black tie, and he sits up straight with a smile on his face. Derringer picks up a pencil and he looks at it for a second and then he flicks it at the camera and begins to talk.) Zack Derringer (ZD): You know, everyone sucks so bad at commentating that I had to go out and get my own damn show and here it is! The Zack Derringer Show, folks.... the best it can get! And this bald freak next to me is Hank Thompson-- you all know this loser, right? (Hank smiles and waves to the camera) ZD: Yeah, so anyway.... we're gonna start this thing off by showing you an interview from the master of International Contracts Unlimited, Deathwatch! He's my personal hero, you know that, Hank? Hank Thompson (HT): He's a mean guy and I don't see how anyone can like him, although the treatment I got when I interviewed him at the ICU building was rather intriguing. ZD: Yeah, whatever, Honk. HT: It's Hank. ZD: Whatever, let's just roll the interview. ------------ (The camera opens on Deathwatch sitting behind his desk at his office in the ICU building. He has his hands folded on top of his desk, and a dark grin is on his face. He wears a gray pinstripe suit with a black tie and a white shirt underneath. His long, red hair is tied back into a pony tail and his cold, red eyes stare into the camera with nothing but seriousness. He begins to speak with his dark, yet sophisticated voice.) Deathwatch: It sure is good to be recognized again. Trouble, I told you what I would do to you, but you took it for granted. I told you that coming out of that match you would be minus a title and was I right? Of course I was, Trouble, of course I was. (Deathwatch picks up a piece of paper and reads it over.) Deathwatch: Now.... it says here that I have to face Mike Stewart at the pay-per-view, AEWA Extreme. Now how can I face Mike Stewart, when he seems to be retiring again? I can't, it's that simple. It also appears that Mr. Stewart has got himself a replacement that he is "designating" to this match for my North American championship. (Deathwatch pulls out the North American title and sets it standing up on his desk.) Deathwatch: Mikey, I don't think so. You are in NO position to designate ANYTHING! Since you've left the AEWA again, you aren't even in the picture anymore.... which leads me to the match at AEWA Extreme. I definitely have my own little way to determine just who exactly I shall be facing at the Pay-Per-View. I am issuing a challenge to ANYONE in AEWA that has the gall to try me. (Deathwatch pulls out a sheet of paper from his desk drawer and holds it up to the camera.) Deathwatch: This, morons, is a contract. You see, I'm NOT the bad guy here, I'm not even trying to give you that impression. One thing I _am_ though.... is fair. (Deathwatch grins at the camera in a manner that says "Uh huh, sure.") Deathwatch: Now, this contract states that five people have the opportunity to face me for the title. I know this has been done before by some other small-time federation out there, but I liked the idea so I am going to use it. Five men can face me, I'll decide who is first.... and I'll decide who is last. The five men will come out individually in a row. The first man that can beat me in any way, shape, or form will be the one that gets to face me for the title. (Deathwatch puts his fingers around his chin and grins to the camera, while leaning his elbow on the desk.) Deathwatch: But if you LOSE, however, you get nothing.... not even the chance for a title shot in the near future, that is, unless I say otherwise. Here are the five men that I have chosen, you can either accept or deny the challenge, but only an idiot would deny it. (Deathwatch pulls out a small piece of paper and reads off the names.) Deathwatch: First off, Super-Blonde. As I know, you might want a good shot at me for finding your cousin attractive and extremely fascinating, but I can't help it.... it's the romantic part of me. (grins) (Deathwatch pauses) Deathwatch: Next is Justin Trouble. I know you have a match already at the pay-per-view, or at least I thought you did, but I am giving you a good opportunity to reclaim this title that I took away from you. If you have what it takes, you'll be able to take me down where you failed the last time. (Deathwatch pauses) Deathwatch: Nightshade.... Yes, Nightshade, I am actually acknowledging your presence. You see, you might have your little Horsemen, but I have my big, bad corporation. (grins) I chose you because you've been agitating me for far too long. I bet you'd love to get ahold of my title, wouldn't you? (Deathwatch holds up three fingers and then flips up a fourth) Deathwatch: Three and now four.... the next choice that I have, oh and this is good, Primal Fear. I don't know that you will have a match at the pay-per-view or not, but I still include you. Besides.... you want my title too, right? (Deathwatch pauses and holds the small piece of paper up again. He puts it down onto the desk and reads the last name off) Deathwatch: And finally.... yes, The Caretaker. You've been a thorn in my side for far too long for me to just sit back and let it slide. We'll see how much bravery you have come time for this match. What do you want more? This title? Or just a good, solid shot at me? We'll find out. Well, that's all I have to say.... for now. Oh, and one more thing.... Kismet, I'll be watching you. (Deathwatch chuckles and the camera fades to black.) ----------------- ZD: What a generous guy that Deathwatch is, giving five whole people the opportunity to get a match for the title. How nice can you GET!? HT: I don't trust him one bit, Zack. He's still the same old Deathwatch in my book. ZD: Yeah? Well your book is three pages long and is full of sh*t, so why don't you just shut the hell up!? HT: Um.... ooo-kay. ZD: Besides, it's your pleasure even to be ON this show. I needed a co-host BAD and you were the first loser that I could think of, so consider yourself lucky. HT: Well in any case, let's go to our next flash, from none other than Heavy Metal. This should be good. I bet he has a lot on his mind right now. ZD: He always has a lot on his mind, but seeing that he gets to have his World title again, there's probably a lot MORE on his mind. ----------- (The camera opens on Heavy Metal, standing in front of a black back drop with his arms folded. He's wearing his usual black leather pants, black boots, black shirt, black leather vest, black leather glove on his right hand, and a pair of shades. He has long brown hair and a brown goatee, his hair hangs to the sides of his head and he grins as he begins to speak.) Heavy Metal: Hey, check it out-- the AEWA Board finally decided to give me another shot at that title. Well, Reed, looks like you got some competition 'cause I ain't gonna even THINK of lettin' up on your little body. No matter HOW much you scream, no matter HOW much you beg, I'm going in there for that title and that title ONLY! I've taken a lot of bullsh*t over the past few months.... Mike Stewart was one of those things that I've taken in. Stewart took something that was mine. Stewart should never even have HAD that match! Flukes happen and that was the BIGGEST mistake that EVER happened. (Heavy Metal takes off his shades and clips them onto his leather vest.) Heavy Metal: But now.... now I get another shot at the thing I lost MONTHS ago, but I never SHOULD have lost it! You know why they never wanted me to get that shot again? Do you have ANY idea what they were thinking all this time? Well, I'll tell ya. They were thinking, "Well, we obviously can't give Heavy Metal a title shot. Remember what happened last time he held it? It took three months before he lost it! Who KNOWS how long he'll keep it for if we let him have it again!" (Heavy Metal chuckles and then shakes his head.) Heavy Metal: It's a shame when I see losers like Deranged and Joe Reed holding that title. I mean, when are people going to realize that I am the rightful holder of that belt? I am and always will be the best thing running in this federation and you ALL know it. I can't wait until AEWA Extreme to reclaim what never should have left me. Joe Reed, you may be having a lucky title run right now, but I'm the man that's gonna bring your train to a screeching halt! (Heavy Metal gets a cold stare on his face and glares into the camera.) Heavy Metal: And once I grab that title and wrap it around my waist, it's never.... EVER.... leaving. Everyone signed their death warrants when they signed the contract for this match, and I'm going to claim their bodies. It's gonna be real fun handin' 'em over to the Caretaker, maybe he can find some use for 'em. (laughs) Like the great Ozzy Osbourne said.... "I'm runnin' off the rails of a crazy train!" and that's exactly what I'm doin'. I'm gonna run right through YOU! One more thing.... AEWA.... ROCK ON! (fade to black) --------------- ZD: Ah, how can you NOT like that guy? HT: He's a monster of a man at seven feet four inches, not to mention weighing four hundred and fifty pounds. I think it's kind of easy to not like him, especially if you've made him mad. ZD: Well.... no one is stupid enough to make him mad. HT: What was he in that interview right there? ZD: Being a bad-ass, what else? HT: Let's just go to the next interview from.... well.... it looks like we have something by Metal again. ZD: Really? Let's see that! (Derringer grabs the paper from in front of Thompson) ZD: Awesome.... I guess Metal did a music video and we get the first look! Roll it, Billy-Bob! ----------- (The camera pans in from a back view of the massive Heavy Metal, it is dark in the entryway where the wrestlers wait before entering the arena. He wears his black leather vest and his black pants, boots, and shirt. He has his black leather glove on his right hand and he clenches it in his fist. You can hear the ring announcer in the background of the dark setting) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen, From Atlanta, Georgia, weighing in at 450 pounds, he stands over seven feet tall and is the former AEWA World Heavyweight Champion, Here is.... Heavy.... METAL!! (The crowd erupts as Heavy Metal Makes his entrance down to ringside. "Metal Militia" by Metallica begins to blare, and then the scene starts to flicker with flashes of light. Heavy Metal turns toward the camera and grins as he throws his fist into the lens, and then the music picks up even faster. The scene fades to black and then shows Heavy Metal in the ring as the music plays....) [....Thunder and lightning, the gods take revenge....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal powering someone down with a running clothesline.) [....Senseless destruction, victims of fury are cowardly now....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal driving Deranged's head into the canvas with a DDT.) [....Running for safety, stabbing the harlot to pay for her sins....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal raking Volcano Jackson's face across a steel cage, and shows blood pouring from his own.) [....Leaving the virgin, suicide running as if it were free, ripping and tearing....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal and "Superman" Mike Stewart falling from the scaffold, and landing as hard as a rock.) [....On through the mist and the madness, We are trying to get the message to you....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal setting someone up for The Megadeth powerbomb.) [....METAL MILITIA!....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal planting Deranged with the Megadeth powerbomb.) [....METAL MILITIA!....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal planting "Superman" Mike Stewart with the Megadeth Powerbomb) [....METAL MILITIA!....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal planting Scorpion with the Megadeth powerbomb.) [....Chained and shadowed to be left behind, nine and one thousand....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal standing in the ring, and then fireworks BURST from behind him with a shower of fire.) [....Metal Militia for your sacrifice, Iron clad soldiers....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal drilling a steel chair down onto someone's back.) [....Join or be conquered, the law of the land, what will befall you....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal covering someone for the one, two, three.) [....The metalization of your inner soul, Twisting and turning....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal slamming an aluminum bat across the back of the Gryphon.) [....On through the mist and the madness, we are trying to get the message to you....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal raising his hand with the "Rock On" symbol and the crowd cheering in response.) [...METAL MILITIA!....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal planting Ben Slocum with the Megadeth Powerbomb.) [....METAL MILITIA!....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal nailing Twisted Steel with the Megadeth Powerbomb.) [....METAL MILITIA!....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal crushing the Gryphon with the Megadeth Powerbomb.) [....We are as one as we are all the same, fighting for one cause....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal and the rest of ICU battling it out with the Knights.) [....Leather and metal are our uniforms, protecting what we are....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal driving his knee into Sewerdog's stomach.) [....Joining together to take on the world, with our Heavy Metal....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal and ICU taking over the AEWA Hall of Fame show.) [....Spreading the message to everyone here, come let yourself go....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal battering Ben Slocum with an aluminum bat) [....On through the mist and the madness, we are trying to get the message to you....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal raging after losing the AEWA World Heavyweight title.) [....METAL MILITIA!....] (The screen shows Heavy Metal planting Sewerdog with the Megadeth Powerbomb.) [....METAL MILITIA!....] (The screen shows a bloody Heavy Metal planting The Extremist with the Megadeth Powerbomb onto a fallen steel cage from PCW) [...METAL MILITIA!...] (As the music fades out, so does the screen. The last shot is of Heavy Metal riding away on his Harley Davidson with his shades over his eyes and a cigarette in his mouth, and then the camera fades to black.) -------------- ZD: Holy sh*t, that was cool! Metal, if you're listenin' pal, I think you've outdone yourself here. What did you think, Thompson? HT: Well, the music was a little loud, but.... ZD: Bah, what'd you know! Let's just keep this thing goin'-- who do we got up next? HT: It seems that we have.... *RING* ZD: What the fu-- *RING* ZD: Get that thing! (Hank Thompson clicks the speaker phone.) HT: Hello? [Voice]: (laughs) Ah, how great it is to know that one single force can bring so many to fear. ZD: Who is this!? [Voice]: Oh, you don't recognize my voice? I'm appalled. Let me give you a few.... shall we say.... hints. Let's see, I LOVE the dark, but then again-- who doesn't? I'm "new" here to the AEWA.... what else? Need another hint, morons? HT: I think it's Bl-- ZD: I _know_ who it is, Hank, Shut up. Blackout, right? What the hell do you want? [Voice of Blackout]: It's not so much what I want, rather, what I want to say. You all witnessed what we can do at Kickoff '97, I don't think that there is a doubt in any of your minds that we mean what we say. Your apocalypse has come whether you like it or you hate it.... and your apocalypse will come at the hands of the 4HotA. HT: Mind if I ask a quick question? [Voice of Blackout]: Oh, by all means.... ask away. HT: Yeah.... well, what is this quarrel you seem to have with ICU? [Voice of Blackout]: That old spat? (chuckles) ICU crossed the line in WWEW and we are simply here to pick up where we left off. It's really nothing more than taking care of business, but after we are through with ICU, we move on to the entire AEWA. Isn't it grand to know that the entire federation can be trampled over by four men? (laughs) ZD: Whatever, Blackout. I see nothing changes with you. [Voice of Blackout]: No one EVER changes, Derringer. You keep that in mind! As they say.... a leopard can't change his spots and I don't think he'd want to. Anyway, I'd love to continue this little chat, but I really have other things to attend to, so if I may be so bold.... *CLICK* ZD: Well, that was one way to keep things interesting here. Now.... who do we have up next? HT: It looks like we have a flash from Exodus, a man who's been silent for quite awhile here in AEWA. ZD: Unless you count the Terrordome. Roll it. ------------- (The camera opens on Exodus, laying on a sunny beach in Miami, Florida. He is wearing black trunks and a pair of shades over his eyes. His body glistens in the sun with sun-tan lotion, as he lies on a black beach towel with his Exodus Championship Title laying beside him. He looks up and sees the camera, lowering his sunglasses just to make sure. He sighs and then sits up, resting his elbows on the towel, and then pushes his shades back up onto his face as he begins to speak.) Exodus: Must you people come here at such awkward moments? Bah, oh well, I have to get some things off my chest anyway. Let's see, no matches coming up anytime soon, but I have a few words for one Mr. Gryphon. Gryphon, what are you? Some kind of extra large mosquito? You poke your nose around in my business FAR too much and I think it's high time I take care of you. (Exodus holds his Exodus Championship Title in his lap and grins.) Exodus: I bet you want this, don't you? So here's the challenge.... I challenge you to a match at AEWA Extreme! You might be facing Damian Steele right NOW, but I have a feeling that the fed heads might not mind changing this around a bit-- will you Mr. Freeman? I thought not. (Exodus grins and looks down at the title.) Exodus: Oh, that isn't all of it either! This will still be a scaffold match, of course, and not only am I going to fight you just for the pure satisfaction of beating the snot out of you, but I'm ALSO going to put the title on the line. You see, I want a little bit of a challenge here, not just some random beating, I can do that any time of the day. I figure if I put something up in this that maybe you'll try just a TAD bit harder. (Exodus sets the title aside again.) Exodus: So, it all rests upon your shoulders now, Gryphon. Do you accept my challenge? Or do I have to keep making you WANT to fight me? I can do both, no problem.... but I really.... REALLY want to use you as a human catapult off of a scaffold-- wouldn't that be FUN!? Anyway, you people are ruining my tan, now get lost! (Exodus lies back down with his hands behind his head, and the camera fades to black.) --------------- HT: Wow! Exodus wants Gryphon at AEWA Extreme AND he's going to put up the title!? This could turn out better than I expected! ZD: Is there an echo in here? Am I just hearing things? In any case, it should be a good match, but we ALL know that Gryphon can't even hold a candle to Exodus-- it's just one of those facts. HT: They're both around the same height and I.... ZD: Are you kidding me? Did you SEE the build on that guy in that interview!? He's like a body builder, only not as disgusting. That man is practically a living sculpture. HT: Yeah, well.... let's move on, shall we? ZD: This next interview is from-- HEY! Look, it's from the new Vice President, Matt Furnish! I also guess that this is actually LIVE via satellite, isn't that great? Let's see what good things he has to say about ICU.... * * * * TO BE CONTINUED IN PART TWO * * * * \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- "A New Beginning" American E-Wrestling Association The Official E-Wrestling Federation of Wrestle World and TNM The Best E-Wrestling Fed of the Year 1996 in the TNM Year-End Awards Be sure to visit our Event Center at: http://www.california.com/~bullet/index.html QUOTES OF THE DAY: "That's just the way it is." --(Blackout) "Glad to have you aboard, Sewerdog." --(Damian Steele)
