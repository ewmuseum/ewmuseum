Straight Talk (9/13, part two)

17 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
****CONTINUED FROM PART ONE**** CC: My point exactly. Let's get some comments from American Ambush. ---------- (We see Hank Thompson in the locker room, ready to interview American Ambush) Thompson: American Ambush... what exactly were you doing interfering in the match between Jose Gomez and Mean Massimo ? If my memory serves me correctly, you lost a Loser-Leaves-Town Match in the recent past. You are not allowed to wrestle in the AEWA! Ambush: Well, just in case you need a reminder, I did not wrestle anyone at the Championship Challenge. Gomez thought he had Massimo just where he wanted him, he thought he had him figured out-- NOT! Greedy Giovanni told him you never know what goes down in the ring (breaking out in a sick laugh)... Looks to me like I surprised everyone. Ambush: I guess you're right on that one, but it still doesn't answer my question. Ambush: (producing a copy of the "loser-leaves-town" match contract from May 11's Saturday Night Slam) Take a close look at the fine print.... if my reading skills serve me correctly, the stipulation said I couldn't wrestle in the AEWA for three months, and that means I was reinstated effective August 12.... when did the Championship Challenge take place? Thompson: August 18, but.... Ambush: Don't argue-- I'm back and back for good, and you can't do anything about it ! Thompson: Maybe *I* can't, but Jose Gomez is a different story. Ambush: So he wants to get his butt kicked again courtesy of Asskickers Inc.? That's fine with me. Let him name the place and the time. I'll be there. Let me just ask one simple question: why hasn't Gomez had the balls to come out here like Massimo and myself and speak his mind? He seems to have turned mute since Massimo's return. Coincidence? (turns to the camera) HELLOOOO....CHICKEN....I'M TALKING TO YOU, GOMER! DO YOU WANT TO PROVE YOUR YELLOW STREAK TO THE FANS? WE CAN'T HEAR YOU! Maybe I should just shave his hair off and spray him yellow... at least then his appearance would match his guts. Thompson: You're a sick man. I'm appalled. Ambush: Well, thank YOU ! Thompson: Back to you, guys. (The image fades to black, and we return to our studios.) ---------- KW: Boy, American Ambush has become MUCH cooler since he's been gone from the AEWA. CC: You would think so, you're as sick as he is. KW: I resent that remark, I'm MUCH sicker. CC: I know, I just didn't think you would so readily admit it. KW: You don't know me very well, do you? CC: How can I? Everything you say contradicts the last thing you said. KW: I know, that's because I'm cool. CC: Uh, no, it's not. Now here's some comments from Mike Stewart. ---------- (The camera opens on "Superman" Mike Stewart and his manager/girlfriend, Michelle Anderson, in their hotel room. Anderson is sitting on the bed, reading a magazine.) Stewart: I've heard a lot of people talking. They say that the champ can't be beat. Heavy Metal may be a big man, but you're looking at the man who's going to take him down. I know what you're all thinking-- a rookie comes into the AEWA and says he's going to defeat the champ? That's right. I'm in the AEWA not only because it's the big league of wrestling, but because it's where the best wrestlers are. Heavy Metal is the champ, and that's what I'm aiming for. So if I have to go through wrestlers like Primal Fear, Sewerdog, and Cardinal Chaos to do it, I will. Just remember that I will be on top, sooner or later. (The image fades to black, and we return to our studios.) ---------- KW: The rookie claims that he can beat Heavy Metal now, but if you put him in the ring with Metal he'd probably run away screaming. CC: Heavy Metal may be a massive man and an awesome force, but he is still a man, and he can be beaten. KW: Not by a moron like Stewart. CC: What do you really know about Mike Stewart? He's barely wrestled at all yet. KW: I know all I need to know. Anybody named after a comic book character automatically sucks. CC: There's a comic book character called Exodus, you know. KW: Yeah, but that's, uh, different. CC: Sure it is. Let's just watch this interview with U.S. Law. ---------- (The camera opens on a crowd of people standing in front of a gym. They all look very excited.) Man #1: I heard that a wrestler from the AEWA is in there. He might be coming out soon, and I'd like to get his autograph.... Teen #1: Really? Who? Man #2: I heard it was U.S. Law. Lady #1: Seriously? He hasn't been wrestling much lately.... (The door opens, and U.S. Law steps out) Law: Hey, guys and gals. You people are pretty lucky, seeing how I have some news for the AEWA. Man #2: What are you talking about? Law: I have returned, and I am sending in a challenge in about 5 minutes. A camera crew will be here to tape it all..... Teen #2: Who will be your first opponent? Law: I don't know. I guess who ever has the balls to step in the ring. (An AEWA van pulls up, and interviewer Hank Thompson steps out. He approaches Law, and begins to speak) Thompson: We'll be set up for the flash in about 3 minutes, Mister Law. Just a second.... Law: (nods, and turns to the crowd) Well, I have to go tape it now. I'll see you all later. (He walks into the gym with Hank Thompson, and the camera crew follows them. They begin taping) Law: Well, if it isn't the boys from the AEWA, come to find out who I am practicing for. That's a hard question, since no match has been booked for me. (stares at the camera) President Smith, I am asking for a match on the next live show. Set me up with a match, I think that this career better start. And John Timber, I know it has been a while, but I am back. I'm looking forward to teaming with you, buddy. (We return to our studios.) ---------- KW: What's this moron doing back? CC: He's here to compete in the AEWA and bring some semblance order to it. KW: Who says we need any? CC: I do, and so do a lot of other people. KW: Like who? Your mom and your invisible friend? CC: NO, I mean the fans and wrestlers who actually think there should be something that RESEMBLES wrestling, not barroom brawls, in that ring. Now let's get a word from a newcomer by the name of Rage. ---------- (Rage is standing with Johnny Switchblade, his badly dressed manager. Johnny holds a black baseball bat with the word "RAGE" in blood Red lettering across the side. Rage stands in black tights. His greasy black hair falls down in his face. An ominous figure over six feet tall, Rage has an evil look in his eyes as Johnny stands next to him, smiling and laughing.) Switchblade: It is time for everyone to bow! Bow down to the new master of wrestling! Say your prayers, "superstars" of the AEWA! For Rage is here, and he is VERY angry! Rage: Wrestlers of the AEWA, it is now time to quiver in fear. I am now here, and I am ready to destroy all of you pretty boys. It doesn't matter who it is I wrestle, I will dominate you. I live with no rules, and will use any tactic to beat you. Anything is fair game when you are in the ring with me, so expect a good ass whipping. I am your worst nightmare, a living hell. It's so nice to meet you. (Rage bows his head down, covering his face with his hair.) Switchblade: Before we go I want to ask President Smith something. Why the HELL wasn't Rage in the North American title battle royal?!? He deserves that title shot more than any wrestler does in that whole arena. Oh well, maybe Rage will make a special "guest" appearance at the arena during a defense. Yeah, maybe he'll just do that.... (Johnny Switchblade laughs sarcastically as the camera fades to black. Rage growls softly to the camera, never lifting his head. We return to our studios.) ---------- KW: Yeah, why the hell WASN'T Rage in that battle royal? CC: Because there were only so many spots in the battle royal, and the newcomers who were in it were chosen at random. KW: Sure they were. I bet that Rage was kept out of it JUST to keep him from winning. CC: Maybe he should just win some matches and prove that he DOES deserve a shot instead of just running off his mouth without actually wrestling. KW: Because everybody's afraid to wrestle him. CC: I don't think so. Now let's get a word from Ronnie Hoffa. ---------- (We see Hank Thompson, holding a microphone. Ronnie Hoffa is there with him, ready for the interview) Thompson: I'm back stage here with Ronnie Hoffa. He's been a busy man lately, leading his team into a double count out with the Icemen and then dominated the amateur team on Wednesday Night Rumble. What about it, Ronnie? Hoffa: Well, our match with the Icemen was vicious brawling, I loved every minute of it. I have to say I was disappointed with the outcome though, we had those two punks right where we wanted them. As for the amateurs, they just got a taste of our frustration that Organized Crime and The ILCA are both still hanging around Ronnie Hoffa's territory. Everybody should watch their backs. Thompson: It's true that you have had problems with those two tag teams, but what do you intend on doing about it? Hoffa: Do what I always do, have them taken care of. If not in the ring, out of the ring. Although I doubt they would have the guts to step in and face my boys. Thompson: Ronnie.... (Hoffa grabs him by the collar) Hoffa: That's Mister Hoffa! Thompson: I apologize, Mister Hoffa. Your wrestlers are single wrestlers as well, correct? Hoffa: They certainly are, but it's hard to get singles matches when people like Shane Matthews talk big but never sign the dotted line. Birdman wants a piece of him, and he doesn't need a size advantage. As for Icebreaker, he'll fight anyone, this guy has got an iceberg on his shoulder because of a hard life in the Arctic and he wants to take his anger out on some poor slob. Maybe that guy who thinks he stole his name or something. Thompson: I noticed you are here unprotected, isn't that odd for a crime lord? Hoffa: I'm a business man, Thompson, get it straight. You screw up almost as much as that announcer who called me a criminal, Matt Wilson, and I think both of you should be watching your backs.... as well as Organized Crime, ILCA, Icemen, and whoever else gets in my way. Oh, one last thing. Ronnie Hoffa is never without protection.... (two huge guys walk onto the screen) Word to the wise--get out of AEWA or feel our wrath. (The image fades out, and we return to our studios.) ---------- KW: Boy, Hank Thompson's been a busy guy tonight. CC: You can say that again, he's been all over. KW: Yup, but what about this Hoffa guy? CC: I think he's a disgusting individual. KW: Yeah, me too. That's why I like him. CC: And, wait a minute fans! I understand that AEWA Vice-President Bob Freeman has something to say about President Smith's current situation, so right now, let's go to him. ---------- (The camera switches to Bob Freeman, sitting in his office. He looks very serious) Freeman: I have some important news, ladies and gentlemen. As you all know, Deathwatch brutally attacked President Smith on a recent episode of Saturday Night Slam. It was an absolutely disgusting incident. Deathwatch is being fined $50,000 for that, and he has been suspended from the AEWA for one week. This violence will NOT be tolerated. Now, many people are wondering about the condition of President Smith. He is currently resting, with severe damage to his neck. Our prayers go out to him. Until President Smith is physically able to return, I will be taking his place. Henry Gehringer, one of our top executives, is going to serve as our "Commissioner". Together, we plan to take out the trash around here. The rules WILL be enforced. (The image fades to black as we return to the studio.) ---------- KW: WOW! President Smith is gonna be out of action THAT long? It's party time! NO RULES!!! CC: Were you even listening? Bob Freeman is taking over as active President and Henry Gehringer is being promoted to Commissioner, and they are going to enforce the rules. KW: When did he say that? CC: Just now. KW: Really? CC: YES REALLY!! KW: You don't have to yell, I'm not blind. CC: HUH? Let's just go to these comments from Ricky Roberts. ---------- (We see "Texas Wild Fire" Ricky Roberts, sitting in the locker room. His manager, Bubba Baxter, is with him. He looks very happy) Roberts: Now that I'm teaming up with Twisted Steel and the Grim Reaper, things are REALLY starting to get exciting. We're calling ourselves the "Angels of Death" so watch out. This will be the BIGGEST stable in AEWA history, folks. Baxter: Ben Slocum had better shut his freaking mouth. That little bastard is NOTHING, and he doesn't even deserve to wrestle in this great federation. Roberts: The Angels will be unstoppable. After Twisted Steel gets through KICKING Slocum's ass on Saturday Night Slam, I'll climb into that cage myself. You see, we NEVER fight alone. Baxter: It's only a matter of time. (The image fades to black, as we return to our studios.) ---------- KW: Wow. These guys are forming a PERMANENT stable? That's gonna be awesome! The "Angels of Death" rule!! CC: Not as long as there's people like Ben Slocum to do something about it. KW: They'll take care of him soon enough, then it's on to the rest of the AEWA! They will rule supreme! CC: Let's just wait a little longer before we declare them the greatest stable of all time, huh? Now let's hear from Sewerdog. ---------- (The room is dark, and a single white candle burns next to Sewerdog. He stands there looking at the camera with a smirk on his face.) Sewerdog: Heavy Metal, everyone's been asking me why did I attack you. I think the answer's pretty simple-- you're an idiot. You become the first man to win two titles at the same time, and you insult the AEWA by calling one a piece of crap? I'd give anything to have a title and you have to act like a stubborn ass. And people wonder why I helped "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews. You ain't Heavy Metal, you're a piece of Scrap Metal, and anything that's scrap gets dumped in the trash, right where you belong. I ain't scared of you or anything you throw at me. I lose, I learn, I win, I learn, but no matter what you do, you can't stop me. I've been the underdog for so long now, I don't care. (The image fades to black as we return to our studios) ---------- KW: See, I told you earlier that he doesn't care. This is gonna be one hell of a match if these two ever get in the ring together. CC: It sure is, it'll be interesting to see if how Sewerdog can match up against Heavy Metal. KW: It'll be the toughest battle of his life, that's for sure. CC: That's the ONLY thing that's for sure. Now let's hear from Brad Gunloc. ---------- (We see Brad Gunloc, standing in the locker room. He looks very excited) Brad Gunloc: Since I have just arrived in the AEWA, I obviously have no problem with anyone in the AEWA. So, can you get me a match? I am eager to wrestle, and I'm really talented-- so let's do it! If you're up to the challenge, then meet me in the ring! Also, watch out for my deadly leg lock. It's a very effective hold, and I hope to win some titles with it! AEWA, here I come! (The image fades to black as we return to our studios.) ---------- KW: Where did they find this guy? Here's a hint, man-- BUY a personality. CC: That's not very nice, Ken. KW: It wasn't supposed to be. CC: Well that's only his first interview, and I'm sure we'll be seeing a lot more of him. You can't really tell much from that. KW: I can. CC: Sure you can. And now let's get a word from "Ultimate" Jose Gomez. ---------- (We see "Ultimate" Jose Gomez in the locker room, and he looks very angry. His manager, El Diablo, is with him) Gomez: Hello everyone, I wanted to talk about a few things. My career needs some motivation, because I'm going nowhere. Every time I wrestle anymore, bad things happen. It has become increasingly difficult for me to win matches. That's why I have a new game plan in life-- seek and destroy. I will NOT stop until American Ambush has been eliminated from this sport. Diablo: It's going to be total devastation. When Gomez gets into the ring with Ambush, all hell will break loose. (The image fades to black as we return to our studio.) ---------- KW: Both of these guys think that they are going to destroy each other? CC: Yes, but only one of them can be right. KW: I wonder which one it is. CC: We'll just have to wait and find out, and now, for our final interview, I believe that Miss DeMeanor wants to say something else. Let's go find out what it is. ---------- (We see Miss DeMeanor, standing in the parking lot. She looks very excited, and her hair is totally messed up.) DeMeanor: For one week, I haven't slept at all. Since Primal Fear left me, I have been searching for a new wrestler. Someone to DESTROY Primal Fear. Well, folks, the search is over. I recently was reading about a small town in California, where a lunatic has been terrorizing the locals. This guy is a complete psycho. Anyway, he had finally been arrested, so I decided to bail him out of jail. Now, the man is working for ME. Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to introduce-- THE TWILIGHT STRANGLER! (A large man approaches the camera. He is wearing ripped up jeans, with a blood-stained shirt.) Strangler: I have come for you, Primal Fear. DeMeanor: The Twilight Strangler is on a mission, and he will NOT stop until it's complete. Primal Fear WILL go down. (The image fades to black as we return to our studios.) ---------- KW: Oh BOY, is Primal Fear in trouble now! CC: How do you know? KW: Haven't you read the newspaper articles on this guy? He's INSANE! Even more so than Primal Fear USED to be! CC: Maybe so, but he has no experience in a wrestling ring, and that give Primal Fear a distinct advantage. KW: Yeah, like Primal Fear actually WRESTLES. CC: He's GOTTA know more than this "Strangler" character. KW: Maybe so, but that'll mean jack when they get in the ring together. CC: You just might be right, it would be a WILD brawl. It'd be fun to watch though. And that's our show for this week, but again, you're being forced to watch the Kevin Larkin Show immediately following this episode. Sorry about that-- contractual obligations and stuff. But we're outta here fans, so see ya next week for more Straight Talk! (The camera enters Studio K, where the table this week is surrounded by Wesley Cantrell, Superfly Aaron Ladd, Reuben Thunder Slade, Kevin Larkin, Master Wu-Tang, Skull, and Crossbones.) Larkin: Welcome to my show again. You may have noticed that the show was....um....we weren't on last week. There were delays in getting both teams ready for this, and we were all set to go on Saturday, but I had a minor "setback". Anyway, tonight we have Mr. Wesley Cantrell, CEO of the Cantrell Corporation and manager of AEWA National Tag Team Champions, the Shadow Warriors, who are here themselves. Last week Mr. Cantrell suggested that his team deserved a shot at the world belts, and I suggested he put the question straight to the men themselves. So now we also have Master Wu-Tang, manager of AEWA World Tag Team Champions Skull & Crossbones, both of whom are here as well. So....um.... Mr. Cantrell, why don't you....um....start things off. Cantrell: I'd like to keep this nice and brief, and I have other, more pressing matters which require my attention. There can be no disputing that the Shadow Warriors are the rightful number one contenders for the World Titles. As National Champions for the past month, they have proven themselves superior to the competition. It has been at least two months since another team has defeated them. And they remain the one marquee team in the AEWA to not receive its fair opportunity. Wu-Tang: We have never been all that concerned with whom our victims-- I mean, worthy opponents-- happen to be. We've been through Submission and the West Coast Connection in one night, and don't see any reason why we should be all that worried about facing you two. But.... I hear you asking for a chance at our gold, yet I hear nothing about your gold. If you want us to put the World Titles on the line, we want you to put those other belts on the line as well. Larkin: Well, what do you say, Mister Cantrell? (Larkin slides a contract and pen over to Cantrell) Cantrell: Well.... (Cantrell waits a moment, and without breaking eye contact with Master Wu-Tang, signs the dotted line. He slides them across the table to Master Wu-Tang. As Larkin backs his chair away from the table, Ladd and Slade flip the table over onto Skull, Crossbones and Master Wu Tang, and the expected result ensues. The standard punch-and-stomp brawl follows, and the element of surprise serves the Shadow Warriors well. As the two teams, and their managers, brawls across the set, Skull & Crossbones begin to gain a little momentum and start fending off the Shadow Warrior attack. Just as things begin to go in their favor, the Visigothic Horde bursts into the room and blindsides Skull & Crossbones with double-sledges and kicks in the back. After the Shadow Warriors and the Horde work over the World champs for a few minutes, Wesley Cantrell calls off his men, and apologizes to Larkin for having to leave just as things were getting interesting. Larkin nods and thanks him for being here as the Horde-Skull/Crossbones brawl proceeds. Before leaving, Cantrell tips Larkin $5 and asks him to please clean up this mess. As the credits roll, Larkin mutters "Damn, I lost count" and resumes counting money, and Athanaric and Fritigern continue pounding on the World champions.) \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- American E-Wrestling Association!
