Wednesday Night Rumble (12/11)

14 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power Bomb, and a look at Mister Crowley DRIVING the head of some guy into the concrete with the Premature Burial! Then the camera switches to Hank Thompson and Roy Stanley in a packed arena) HT: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Wednesday Night Rumble! We're coming to you LIVE from Minneapolis, and there are plenty of GREAT matches scheduled for tonight. RS: That's right, Hank. In the main event, "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews will be defending his World Heavyweight title against Deranged! HT: That should be an EXCELLENT bout. I might not be Deranged's biggest fan, but I'd like to see him kick Shane's butt. RS: Don't hold your breath, buddy. Anyway, let's get to our first match! HT: First up, we're going to be seeing a team who thinks there is a conspiracy against them these days, former AEWA National Tag Team Champions, the Shadow Warriors. RS: Those guys are extremely talented, and they haven't been getting their due respect. Wesley Cantrell just wanted to get some RESPECT for his team. HT: Here we go.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is scheduled for one fall! Being led to the ring by Wesley Cantrell and Lianne, they are former National tag team champions, Aaron Ladd and Reuben Slade, THE SHADOW WARRIORS! (The Shadow Warriors make their way toward the ring, to a loud round of boos from the crowd. Wesley Cantrell walks closely behind them, escorting the lovely Lianne.) RS: They're looking good tonight! HT: They're set to take on "Jumping" Joey Wrangler and David Graves, a couple of youngsters who look like real up-and-comers in the AEWA. Those guys are already in the ring, so we're ready to start things off. RS: Well, we've seen Graves before, but I don't know much about this Wrangler guy. I can't see these two men handling the likes of Reuben Slade and Aaron Ladd. HT: Well, we'll find out soon enough. Graves spends a lot of time with the current National Champions, the Machines, and I'm sure they gave him some pointers on tag team competition. The bell has rung, and we're off. Looks like Wrangler and Slade to start off. RS: (starts giggling) "Jumping" Joey Wrangler, who must check in at about 215 pounds, wants a test of strength with Reuben Slade?! HT: Well, Slade agrees, they lock up, and-- DROPKICK RIGHT IN THE MIDDLE OF THE CHEST BY WRANGLER! He had the big guy fooled on that one! RS: Nice bit of strategy by the rookie. Slade is fuming as he gets back on his feet, and Ladd asks to be tagged in. Here he comes.... HT: He's saying something to Wrangler-- I can't make out what it is. Now he's getting down on his hands and knees. What is he trying to do? He's down, and Wrangler is hooking him; it looks like Ladd offered to fight amateur style. RS: Yeah, that's EXACTLY what he did! Now he's trying to wriggle out, but Wrangler's got a good hold on him. Ladd scurries, but can't break free. He's got a hold of the rope- they're going to have to break the hold. HT: Graves seems to think this is a riot, but the Shadow Warriors are just getting angrier and angrier. RS: They didn't expect this kind of competition from such a rookie. Now Ladd and Wrangler are talking again, and now it's Wrangler's turn to get on all fours in referee's position. HT: Ladd gets into position, but Slade wants back in. There's the tag. Wrangler is still waiting on all fours for Slade. Slade approaches and-- OUCH! RS: An amateur style kick in the gut! Now he's stomping Wrangler into the mat. Looks like he's trying to pound him all the way though to China.... HT: What a disgusting display of sportsmanship! RS: What a disgusting display of idiocy! HT: Slade yanks Wrangler up by the hair and throws him into his own corner. It looks like Slade really wants to match up with Graves; the two men with the power edge in this match. Graves is in, and he and Slade lock up. RS: Graves with an armdrag takedown, and Slade is back up again and runs right into a scoop bodyslam! This is not going as Slade planned.... Graves sends him into the ropes, and a big clothesline! HT: In comes Ladd, and he gets nailed with a clothesline, too! Ladd gets knocked through the ropes, and Graves goes back after Slade-- TILT-A-WHIRL SUPLEX! RS: Graves goes to the top, and a flying clothesline on Slade! I believe he calls that "the Gravestone". He should go for the cover, but he goes to tag Wrangler back in. If you've got both of your opponents on the run, why tag the smallest guy back in? HT: Well, Wrangler didn't do too badly himself last time out. Wrangler picks Slade up, and whips him into the ropes, flying bodypress! Here's the cover.... One...Two...Kickout RS: Now Wrangler grabs Slade again and whips into a neutral corner, and follows in with a running splash. HT: Wrangler mounts the second rope and begins POUNDING Slade in the head! The crowd is counting along, and-- uh oh. SLADE GRABS WRANGLER AROUND THE WAIST AND NAILS AN INVERTED ATOMIC DROP! RS: Wrangler is in obvious pain, and Slade flattens him with a kick to the head. Slade tags Ladd, and then takes Wrangler up top while Ladd goes up in his own corner. Superplex by Slade, and he rolls out of the way as Ladd connects on a somersault leg drop. The cover.... One..Two..Slade grabs a hold of Graves' leg as he goes to break the pin....Three! (DING DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here are your winners, THE SHADOW WARRIORS! HT: Well, Graves and Wrangler gave it a good shot, but experience won out in the end. RS: Notice how the Shadow Warriors seemed to drop out of title contention without ever getting a rematch when they lost the National titles? Maybe they're right. It has been, what, 2 months now? HT: Well, they practically BOUGHT the titles in the first place. Maybe Larkin is waiting for them to pay up for the second try! (Lee Aaron's "We Will Be Rockin''" begins to play.) RS: Why does that sound familiar? HT: We used to hear it all the time here in the AEWA. It is the preferred entry song for the two-time World Tag Team Champions, the NATO Powers! RS: I thought they were gone. HT: You thought wrong. And tonight they are matched up against another pair of former title holders. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, being accompanied by their manager, Wally Henderson, they are former AEWA World tag team champions, THE NATO POWERS! ("NATO" Wally Henderson leads the British Destroyer and Russian Tank to the ring. They all look very angry.) RS: I hate to admit it, but these guys are looking pretty good. Are they going to be competing full time again? HT: Yeah. RS: I was afraid of that. HT: Let's just watch.... RING ANNOUNCER: And their opponents, at a combined weight of 525 pounds, here are THE ASSASSINS! (Banshee and Shockwave make their way toward the ring, to a BIG pop from the crowd. They slap hands with the fans, and quickly climb into the ring.) RS: Who are those guys again? HT: The Assassins. RS: Didn't they wear goofy masks? HT: Wrong assassins. These are the two men who beat the Graveyard Shift way back in March for the tag titles, then lost them back 2 weeks later. RS: NATO Powers, Assassins-- what is this? Old-timers day? Are we going to blow the dust off of every AEWA has-been? Where's Raekwon? Black Hawk? HT: The NATO Powers must be very interested in the 4-way tag team title match coming up this weekend, to which they have not been invited. If you have been watching the AEWA since it's inception, you'll remember that for the first three months of our history, the NATO Powers and West Coast Connection fought a series of title matches, and the WCC was never able to walk away with the gold. RS: Then the NATOs launched that unprovoked attack on the West Coast Connection, and were rightfully suspended. The WCC exacted vengeance at Battle Klash and won the right to a tag title match. HT: Well, the attack was retribution for one which caused the NATOs to drop their titles to Organized Crime. The suspension was probably the first sign of psychological trouble in Larkin, and the WCC's partner, that country-singer guy, beat the Crazy Canuck in the six-man match at Battle Klash. Otherwise, you had it almost half-right. RS: Well, while you've been yapping all this while, the NATO Powers have been breaking every rule in the book against that bigger Assassin.... HT: Actually, folks, the NATO Powers have kept Shockwave in their own corner and used frequent tags to keep fresh while they take turns working him over. I don't know where Roy got that "breaking every rule in the book" stuff from. RS: I saw at least one closed fist punch. HT: Now The British Destroyer sets him up for a Doctor Bomb-- HE NAILS HIM! The cover.... One..Two..Kickout RS: Banshee hops into the ring, and flips the Destroyer over with a thrust kick! Shockwave rolls with him, AND EXECUTES A DOUBLE LEG CRADLE ON THE DESTROYER! One..Two..Russian Tank makes the save HT: All four men are in the ring now. Shockwave and the Russian Tank, all 700 pounds of them or whatever they weigh, are tied up in one corner, and Banshee hooks the British Destroyer in a German suplex One..Two..Three! (DING DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here are your winners, THE ASSASSINS! RS: That was fast! HT: Banshee got him! He wasn't the legal man! RS: You know, you use that phrase "legal man" all the time but no one ever explained to me what it means. HT: I think there are a lot of things no one ever explained to you-- rules, fairness, sportsmanship, hygiene.. RS: High jeans? What the hell are you talking about? This is a wrestling program, stick to the subject. HT: What was the subject? RS: We were talking about those guys, the Ninja ones-- HT: The Assassins, former AEWA World Tag Team Champions. RS: --right, those ones, just beat the other guys. HT: You mean, the NATO Powers, two-time AEWA World Tag Team Champions. RS: Um, yeah, I guess so. HT: So that's the subject. RS: Right. HT: Well, you seemed so eager to stay on that subject, what did you want to say? RS: Oh....um....those guys won. HT: Anything else? RS: It was.... a pretty good match? HT: As always, Roy, thanks for the invaluable insights you and your years of experience bring us every week. Anyway, it's now time for the big main event, as Shane Matthews defends his World Heavyweight title against Deranged! RS: I've really been looking forward to this match, folks. Deranged is finally starting to get some fan support again, so I'm rooting for Shane. HT: I'm not surprised. Let's just watch.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall, and is for the AEWA World Heavyweight title! Introducing first, the challenger, he weighs in at 260 pounds and is accompanied by his manager, Angel, He is a three-time former AEWA Television Champion, Deeeeeeeeeeeeerrrrrranged! (The Theme from "Halloween" plays loudly over the PA as Deranged makes his way toward the ring to a mixed reaction. Angel is following closely behind him.) HT: I, for one, was very surprised at the actions of President Freeman, giving Deranged a title shot like this. RS: I agree! Deranged lost his last match, and he STILL gets this title shot. Furthermore, he expects the fans to be behind him! He turned his back on all the fans and the AEWA! HT: I don't think all the fans have forgiven him yet. I don't know who would be a worse champion, Deranged or Shane Matthews! RS: Clearly Shane is the better of the two. He is going to keep the title for a long, long time! HT: Here comes Shane now! RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, he weighs in at 222 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his manger, Johnny Terror, He is the current AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, "Sudden Death" Shaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaane Maaaaaaaaaaaaaaattheeeeeewwwwws! (Anthrax's "Looking Down the Barrel of a Gun" blares loudly over the PA as Matthews makes his entrance to a HUGE chorus of boos from the crowd. Johnny Terror is at his side, as always. Shane wears the belt around his waist.) RS: Man, he looks good with that belt! There is NO way Deranged will get that belt from him! HT: Well there's the bell, and we're underway! Shane comes right at Deranged with a BIG clothesline! He took his head right off! RS: YES! That's the beginning of the end for Deranged! HT: Don't speak too soon! Deranged gets to his feet, but Matthews knocks him right down with a standing dropkick! He follows that up with an elbowdrop to the back of the head! The Cover! One....Kickout! RS: (yelling to the ring) Come on, Shane, finish him off! HT: Shane drags Deranged to his feet, and NAILS him with a big right hand! Deranged falls back onto the ropes! RS: Deranged is out on his feet! HT: Matthews comes off the ropes--BIG clothesline on Deranged! And they both go to the outside! RS: Deranged is in trouble now! Matthews is dangerous inside the ring, but I'd hate to face him out of the ring even more! HT: Matthews levels Deranged with right hands! He leans him on the guardrail and enters the ring! RS: What the hell is he doing?! HT: Matthews comes off the ropes, and dives through the second and third ropes, RIGHT ONTO DERANGED! RS: WOW! Deranged was almost cut in half on the guardrail! HT: Matthews drags Deranged back into the ring, and plants him on the mat with a Sidewalk Slam! RS: Deranged is out cold! Matthews really took it to him outside of the ring! HT: Shane Matthews climbs to the top rope! He goes for a BIG Splash on Deranged, but Deranged moves right out of the way! RS: NO! Get up, Shane! HT: Deranged gets to his feet first and hits him with a lariat! He jumps on Matthews and hits him with punishing lefts and rights! RS: Come on, ref! Get some control! HT: Deranged hasn't done anything wrong, Roy. RS: Oh, sure! Take HIS side! HT: Just watch the match. Deranged picks up Matthews, and hits him with a back suplex! RS: Boy, this match really turned around! Matthews has totally lost control! HT: Deranged drops a leg on Matthews, and goes for the quick cover! One......Two..Kickout! HT: Matthews gets to his feet, but Deranged takes him down with a bulldog! He locks in an armbar submission! RS: Matthews is really squirming in pain! He is slowly inching his way to the ropes, though! (screaming) Reach, Shane! HT: Matthews reaches the ropes, and Deranged is forced to break the hold! He picks him up again, and ROCKS him with a shoulderbreaker! RS: Man, he's really working on that arm! HT: Deranged hits him with a Snap Suplex! He picks him up again, and NAILS him with a DDT! The cover! One.....Two....Kickout! HT: Deranged whips Matthews into the ropes. Matthews comes back, and Deranged hits him with a Fireman's Carry Takedown! He quickly drops a leg! He hooks the leg again! One....Kickout! RS: Only a one count that time! Shane Matthews is making a comeback! HT: I'm not too sure about that! Deranged scoops Matthews up, and throws him right over the top rope! RS: Deranged runs full speed, slides under the bottom rope, and NAILS Matthews with a kick to the head! Man, I haven't seen Matthews get hurt like this in weeks! HT: Deranged rolls Matthews back into the ring! HE HITS HIM WITH A POWER BOMB! The Cover! One.....Two..Kickout! HT: Deranged whips Matthews into the corner! He places him on the turnbuckle, AND HITS HIM WITH A SUPERPLEX! RS: Matthews is really taking a beating! I can't believe this! HT: Deranged is standing right over Matthews! He spits on him, and starts to scream right in his face! RS: He better hurry up and pin him! This is only going to make Shane madder! (As the action continues in the ring, two men slowly make their way to ringside. The camera zooms closer, and we see that it is Fritigern and Athanaric, better known as the Visigothic Horde.) HT: Wait! The Visigothic Horde are coming down there, and they're carrying CHAIRS! This could be disastrous! RS: What the hell's going on? Why are THEY here? HT: They both enter the ring and NAIL Deranged in the back with the chairs! This is getting absolutely out of control! RS: I don't know why they're here, but I love it! They are going to DESTROY Deranged! HT: The referee has called for the bell! (DING DING) Now Fritigern pummels Deranged in the face with right hands! He whips him into the ropes, and Deranged gets a faceful of chair! RS: I love it! Deranged is finally getting what he deserves! Some of the fans are even cheering! HT: I don't like Deranged very much either, but he does NOT deserve this punishment! RS: Yes he does, Thompson! It's Judgment Day here in Minneapolis! HT: I wonder if the stipulations Deranged proposed to President Freeman are in place here. Deranged asked for anyone who interferes in the match to be permanently banned from the AEWA forever! RS: I'm sure those stipulations aren't in place! Actually, I HOPE they're not in place. Why would the Visigothic Horde be here if they were?! HT: AND FRITIGERN JUST POWER BOMBED DERANGED ON THE METAL CHAIR! Deranged is out cold! RS: YES! Well, their work is done, and they can leave! HT: Don't be too sure, Roy! Matthews is getting up, BUT ATHANARIC JUST PUNCHED HIM RIGHT IN THE FACE! Deranged falls to the outside of the ring, while The Visigothic Horde beat on Matthews with kicks and punches! RS: WHAT THE HELL?! Now I have NO idea why these two came out! HT: I think your answer is coming down the aisle now! Kevin Larkin is coming down to the ring, and he looks VERY happy! Look at that smile on Larkin's face! He is a SICK man. RS: And now Matthews is fighting back! He nails Athanaric with a BIG right hand, but Fritigern clotheslines him down to the mat! That didn't last long. HT: Man, both Matthews and Deranged are out! And now Larkin is calling for the microphone! LARKIN: You see this? This is your champion, Freeman! This is the man who makes you what you are! (the Horde continue to stomp Matthews) This is the flagship of your fleet, the AEWA World Heavyweight Champion. See that guy over there? (points to a dazed Deranged) He could have been the next one. But you know what? No one YOU could sign measures up to my two men. (points to the Horde). Maybe you thought it was real cute to start setting up title matches in my division. Maybe you thought it would be funny to take two guys from the heavyweight division and give them my tag team belts. Maybe you thought, "Hey, Chaos and Crowley used to be singles champs, why don't I give them Larkin's belts." Well, Freeman, I was here long before anyone had ever HEARD of you. I was making and breaking careers back when you still needed a ticket to get in this arena. And you think YOU'RE gonna walk in here and start ordering me around? You think YOU'RE gonna start telling the guys who work for me what they're gonna do? You stuck your nose in my business once too often. Now it's my turn. Watch this! (Fritigern drags Deranged back into the ring, and he and Athanaric hold him up while Larkin slaps him in the face) LARKIN: See that, Freeman? You messed in my business, now here I am slapping around your favorite wrestler. See this, Freeman? I call the shots for the tag teams around here. You stay the hell out of my business. Let's go, boys. My Horde here has a tag title match to get ready for this weekend, Freeman, so we don't have time to play with your losers anymore tonight. We'll see you at December Doom! (Larkin tosses down the microphone, and leads the Visigothic Horde back to the locker room. The entire arena is in silence.) RS: That was GREAT! HT: I'm absolutely disgusted, Roy. Kevin Larkin can't just waltz into a match and cause trouble like that. He should be permanently banned from AEWA shows-- FOREVER. RS: Are you kidding me? He's a GENIUS! HT: But he's RUINING this great organization! We need to keep him OUT of the arenas, because these outbursts are just getting crazy. By the way, folks, that match was declared a no contest, and Matthews retains that title. RS: But will Shane be in any condition to wrestle at December Doom? HT: That's a good question. Well fans, I'm sorry but that's all the time we have for this week! So for "Rambunctious" Roy Stanley, this is Hank Thompson saying so long everybody!
