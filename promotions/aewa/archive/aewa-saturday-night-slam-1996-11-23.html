Saturday Night Slam (11/23)

26 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power Bomb, and a look at Mister Crowley DRIVING the head of some guy into the concrete with the Premature Burial! Then the camera switches to Hank Thompson and Roy Stanley in a packed arena) HT: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Saturday Night Slam! I'm Hank Thompson, and with me is the AEWA's newest commentator, Roy Stanley! RS: Look, can we just get this show on the road? I'm really excited to be here in the AEWA, but you guys talk WAY too much. HT: Actually, I don't think we talk ENOUGH. Ladies and gentlemen, in tonight's main event, the Graveyard Shift will be defending their World tag team titles in a triangle match against Heaven & Hell and the West Coast Connection! RS: That's great, but what's first? HT: Well, we're going to watch a special interview with Cardinal Chaos and Big Ron Raider. They had an interesting conversation recently, and we have the footage. RS: Really? That should be cool. HT: Let's watch.... -------------- (The camera fades in on the Church rectory. Big Ron Raider is sitting with the Cardinal.) Cardinal Chaos: My son, you have impressed me greatly. Your latest title defense was nothing short of spectacular. You have the ability to bring our word to the poor in spirit. Curate Kill was wise to try to induct you so many months ago. Raider: Curate Kill is a good guy. We go back pretty far. I don't get it though. Why are you always giving him such crap? I mean, come on, the guy bends over backwards to make you happy. Chaos: Your frankness amuses me, my child. Curate Kill is a special case. I have definitive plans for him, plans which are soon to come to fruition. But Curate Kill is not the reason I called you here. I have asked you hear to make a promise to you. Hear me well, Mister Raider, the belt that is currently around your waist shall remain there for quite sometime. The Church looks out for its members and you shall be no exception. You may not always see us, but believe me, we WILL always be there. (The scene fades to black, and the camera returns to our broadcast team.) ---------------- RS: That was rather strange, wasn't it? What do you think Cardinal Chaos has planned for Curate Kill? HT: We'll just have to wait and find out, Roy. Chaos is a VERY unpredictable man, so there's really no way to make a prediction. RS: That's for sure. What's next? HT: The Machines are about to defend their National tag team titles against Graphic Violence. This should be good. RS: Are you kidding me? It's gonna be GREAT! Those are the two best teams in the AEWA, if you ask me, and they're FINALLY going to settle the score! HT: But I DIDN'T ask you, Roy. RS: Hey, here's a newsflash for you-- I don't CARE. They thought they were striking a blow at Simon O'Neal, which I myself wouldn't necessarily mind seeing, but the car they torched actually belonged to the normally mild-mannered Paul Wong. Let's see if Paul's anger causes him to focus too much on revenge and not enough on defending his titles, or if his anger catapults the Machines over Graphic Violence. HT: You would, wouldn't you. RING ANNOUNCER: And their opponents, at a combined weight of 514 pounds, they are the National tag team champions, THE MACHINES!
