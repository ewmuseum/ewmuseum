Sunday, April 6th, Card (3/3)

6 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(As Jeff Howard checks the boots of both competitors and gives each a quick frisk...) EL: Well, so far in singles competition, we've seen Sheradin come out on top both times he's met the Professional, including in the match where we crowned our first CWAA Champion, but it was just a few weeks ago, in a special tag team contest put on with the cooperation of the FWA, that Max Robertson countered the Dream Come True and rolled the champ into a half nelson for the win. After all of the out-of-the-ring encounters, and the two closely-contested matches, has the Professional figured out the champ? We'll find out tonight. (The bell rings.) EL: Both men circling warily. Sheradin feigns for the leg- backs up. Collar and elbow tie up- Robertson disengages and backs up. Both men feeling each other out, waiting for an opening. Senior Official Jeff Howard trying to get both men to get this match really started. Robertson gesturing for Sheradin to come to him. While we're waiting for one or the other to commit for the first move, I can tell you some of the matches already signed for next week. Jak Hammer, we'll have footage from his match at AEWA Extreme with Joey Haven, tackles Stan Stevens. And the CWAA Television Championship will be on the line as Lt. Lawson defends his belt against "The Preacher" Kore Wrench. OK- both men back in midring- Sheradin with an arm bar, Robertson reverses. Sheradin quickly slips behind him, hammerlock- Robertson to the ropes, and Jeff Howard calls for a clean break. Robertson now complaining that his tights were pulled, but if Howard didn't see anything- there was no infraction to see, anyway- he can't reprimand or threaten to disqualify Sheradin over it. Lockup again- Sheradin with an arm drag take down, and Robertson scuttles under the bottom rope and out to the floor. (The crowd boos his delaying tactic as Sheradin stands in the ring poised to strike, fists clenched.) EL: Referee Jeff Howard begins his count. Max taking his time as he walks around the ring. We're up to 8 now- and Max back on the ring apron. OK- he's back in. Lock up- Sheradin with a side headlock. Max reaches toward the rope- and Sheridan pulls him back to midring. Max struggling to break free. He grabs a handful of tights- picks Sheradin up off the ropes- atomic kneedrop! But Sheradin still has that headlock clamped on! Max still struggling to get free- pushes to the ropes. Jeff Howard calls for the break- and Max whips Sheradin across the ring. Max drops down- the Dreamer hurdles over him- Randy Sheradin with a cartwheel kick! (pop) EL: Sheradin off the ropes now- cross body press- he hit too high as Robertson rolls him over... 1... 2... Sheradin kicks out. Sheradin immediately goes for a hammerlock from behind. Robertson reverses. Now Robertson with an arm around the shoulder- legsweep! Max follows with an elbow drop, now an arm bar down on the mat. Max's finisher is the double chickenwing submission, so I suppose he'll be looking to work on the arms and upper back in this one to soften up the champ. (As Sheradin strains to get free, chants of "Go, Randy, Go!" start.) EL: I told you earlier about some of the matches signed for next week, Jak Hammer and Stan Stevens, Lt. Lawson and Kore Wrench. I've just been informed that Joey Haven, also coming off of the AEWA Extreme match- which we hope to show in its entirety next week- will get in the ring with Double Jeopardy's "Hotshot" Gary Jackson. Plus, CWAA Tag Team Champions, Twister, will be in singles competition- Sheradin on his feet- Max pulls him back down with a heaping handful of hair, and back into an arm bar down on the mat. Like I was saying, Travis Conley, co-holder of the CWAA Tag Team title, will square off against Marc Thomas, who has been making some very big waves in the CWAA lately. Conley's partner, Paul "Tornado" Langford, will step in the ring with "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey. (The chant devolves into a simple rhythmic clap.) EL: The crowd trying to rally "Dreamer" Randy Sheradin, as he fights his way back to his feet- whips- reversed! Max Robertson whips the Dreamer into the corner. Robertson in after him- spin kick! Now the Professional drapes Sheridan's right arm over that top rope, and pulls on it, pulling the arm and shoulder against the rope. Referee Jeff Howard in there counting- Max releases on the 4 count. He goes right back to it though, and Jeff Howard warning him that he is really on thin ice now. Now Max with an elbow jab to the head, and he takes Sheridan out to the ring- Irish whip- legdrag takedown by Robertson. Max back into a hammerlock down on the mat again. So far Max is playing to his strengths rather than trying to neutralize Sheridan's. Max works bet on the mat, and he is obviously looking to wear down Sheradin's arm strength to maximize the impact of a double chickenwing later in this match. Another wrestler might want to work on the Dreamer's legs to take away the champ's aerial game, but I guess as long as Max keeps him down on the mat in a hammerlock, then Sheridan's aerial strategy is not an issue anyway. (Sheridan strains to break free, and reached out with his left foot to reach the bottom rope.) EL: Senior official Jeff Howard calling for a break now- Robertson gives an elbow drop to the champ instead! So far, neither man has gone for a real high-impact weapon, opting instead to grind the opponent down. In a way that will work to Sheradin's advantage- Max picks him back up- Irish whip- spinebuster slam! 1... 2... kickout! In a way, it is to Sheradin's advantage that the pace remain slow- he has proven to have more staying power than Max, who cannot match Sheradin's ability to wrestler a longer match. Now Max with a whip- dropkick- Sheradin slammed on the brakes, and Max just kicked the air. (Pop) EL: Now Sheradin with a frog splash- 1... kickout. The Dreamer picks him back up- European uppercut. Sends Max for the ride- monkey flips him over. Max back up- charges- hip toss by Sheradin- the Dreamer spins- savate kick by the champ- and Max slides back out to the floor, Again, at the first sign of an advantage for Randy, Robertson takes a walk, to break the momentum and get his breath back. You can tell Max is a little winded, the way he rests with his hands on his knees like that. The count starts. And there goes the Dreamer out after Max- he's going to take him back in. Sheradin up behind Robertson, spins him around- European uppercut on the floor! (pop) EL: Now he slams Max's head on the ring apron and stuffs him under the bottom rope. Sheradin now slingshots himself in with a splash on Max... 1... 2... kickout! Sheraton picks him back up- standing side headlock- no! Running bulldog- and he drills Max's head into the mat! (pop) EL: Sheradin going up top now. (the crowd noise builds to a crescendo as Sheradin prepares for his Dream Come True finisher) EL: And he hops back down as Maxis getting back to his feet. Chop by Sheradin. Now he turns Max around- backslide...1 ... kickout. Sheradin with a kick in the midsection- now he bounces off the ropes- sunset flip- 1... 2... kickout! Sheradin with the whip- cross by press- and he was again too high- now too close to the ropes- and his momentum carries him right over the top to the floor as Max hangs on in the ring. (collective groan as Sheradin crashes to the floor) EL: Referee Jeff Howard starting his count, as Max goes out to the floor. Max stands the champ up now- slams his head on the ring steps. Those steps are made out of steel, folks, and steel does not give when your head hits it. Now Max stands him up against the ring- chop across the chest- now a spin kick! Max rolls into the ring- breaks the count- back out again. He's reaches around behind Sheradin's back, pulling the Dreamer's right arm behind him- and slams him into the side of the ring! so he has the pressure on the arm from being pulled back like that, plus the impact as it hits the ring. Now Max shoves the Dreamer into the ring and follows him in himself. Max sends him into the corner- follows him in- handspring elbow! And the champ collapses in a pile in the corner. Max takes a second to get his breath back- longer matches are not his forte. He picks the champ back up- waistlock- pulls him over in a Northern Lights Suplex! Lateral press... 1... 2... kickout! (Lt. Lawson and Major Mayhem come down the aisle, with the Major carrying the flagpole and wearing Lawson's title belt over his shoulder as the crowd boos.) EL: Now what do they want? This match is none of their business. (Robertson and Lawson make eye contact, and Robertson nods.) EL: Now Robertson with a handful of tights- and he tosses the champ out through the ropes. Now Max over to senior official Jeff Howard complaining about something. Now Lawson on the floor, in Howard's blind spot, putting the boots to the champ- slams his head onto the ring apron, wait a second! (wild popping ensues as Joshua Barnes sprints down the aisle. The Major shows amazing speed for a septuagenarian as he takes off for the other side of the ring.) EL: There's Joshua Barnes firing away a series of fists on Lt. Lawson, who quickly disengages and retreats to the other side of the ring. Barnes helping up his partner and rolls him back into the ring. And now Robertson pointing to Barnes and Jeff Howard over with words for Barnes, who points at Lawson and the Major. Max Robertson now sets Sheradin up- side suplex. Hooks the leg... 1... 2... kickout! (JB tries to lead the crowd in a chant of "Let's Go Randy!", but instead they revert to their usual "Go, Randy, Go!" The Major tries to get a chant of "Commies Go Home!", but to no avail. Well, actually he has some success, as Lawson picks it up, but that is about it.) EL: Max whips Sheradin into the ropes- hey! Lt. Lawson reaches in and trips Sheradin, who stumbles to mid ring where Max wraps him in a small package... 1... 2... kickout. Max now sends Sheradin into the corner, and walks in after him. Max's tank is just about empty- he's running on fumes now, and Sheradin can't have much left in hi, either. Again Max drapes the arm over the top rope, and now he pulls on the arm with one hand and pushes on the shoulder with his boot. Referee Jeff Howard counts- Max breaks it on 4. Now a cross corner whip- dropkick by the Professional. He circles around behind Sheridan as he gets up- he's going for the double chickenwing! He's got one arm, but Randy Sheradin trying to keep his left arm free. Max has the right arm secure- Barnes encouraging his partner- and Randy grabs the top rope. Jeff Howard calls for the break- and Max know over kicks at Barnes, but Joshua backs up. Joshua Barnes up onto the ring apron now- and Jeff Howard orders him back down, threatening to disqualify Sheradin if Barnes gets involved. Max with an Irish whip- up on the shoulders- rana- cradles the legs... 1... 2... kickout again! Max with a rake of the eyes now, and as Sheradin leans on the ropes rubbing at his eyes, Max again trying to catch his breath. I think Max had hoped to wrap this up much earlier than this- he is sucking air at this point. (Lawson and the Major try to offer encouragement to Robertson, and Barnes circles the ring to try to scare them off. Barnes has no success, but Kore Wrench, his left eye covered by a patch, does manage to chase Lawson away as he comes tearing down the ring aisle to a big pop. Lawson beats a hasty retreat with Wrench hurtling the ringsteps behind him while an unwary Major continues to bark at Barnes.) EL: There goes Lt. Lawson, running for the hills, with "Preacher" Kore Wrench in hot pursuit. I don't know if he can catch the Lt. now, but he'll get a crack at him next week in our special feature match. While we have a quick break, I also understand that two weeks from tonight, in accordance with the verbal agreement made here last week, Twister is offering the team of Randy Sheradin and Joshua Barnes a chance at the CWAA Tag Team Championship. I can't wait to see that one, but first Randy Sheradin has to get past Max Robertson. (The Major looks behind him for support and realizes that Lawson has deserted him for the moment, and turns back to look at Barnes, who grins menacingly. The Major points to the ceiling, and when Barnes looks, runs as fast as a 72-year old manager carrying a title belt and flagpole can.) EL: Max Robertson back on Randy Sheradin now- suplexes him over. The cover...1 ...kickout. Max did not even hook the leg this time. He whips the champ into the corner- and Sheradin is slammed right into those turnbuckles. Max sets in the opposite corner- a slow jog across the ring- handspring elbow- no one home! He was too slow, and the Professional flings himself into the corner. Sheradin grabs him in a waistlock from behind as he bounces out- pulls him back- bridging back- German suplex! 1... 2... 3! He got it! (Enormous pop as "Dream On" plays again and Joshua Barnes grabs the CWAA Title belt to present it again to his partner) DM: Your winner, and still CWAA Heavyweight Champion, "Dreamer" Randy Sheradin! (More popping) EL: Randy Sheradin pulls it out, and Max Robertson is beside himself. For a while it looked like he was going to pull it off, but he just ran out of gas at the end. It looks like the Dreamer is running low now, too, but he'll walk out as he walked in, CWAA Heavyweight Champion. (A disgruntled Robertson grabs a folding chair and flings it into the ring as he leaves. It lands harmlessly a few feet away from Barnes and Sheradin.) EL: Don't forget, next week, footage from AEWA Extreme, Jak Hammer and Stan Stevens, Joey Haven and Gary Jackson, Marc Thomas and Travis Conley, Paul Langford and the Pretty Boy, and the CWAA TV Title match, Lt. Lawson and Kore Wrench, if Wrench doesn't catch him between now and then. Plus, we'll hear about negotiations with the AEWA for another match between an AEWA Champion and a CWAA challenger right here in the world-famous Sportatorium! See you then, folks!
