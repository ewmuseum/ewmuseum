Hall of Fame Show (10/6)

19 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opens on a small auditorium, with hundreds of well-dressed wrestlers and businessmen seated in front of a stage. Chuck Carlson and Hank Thompson are standing at the podium, on-stage.) Carlson: Hello everyone, and welcome to the AEWA's very first Hall Of Fame ceremony! Thompson: Tonight, we will be inducting three different legends into the Hall Of Fame. This auditorium is PACKED with heroes, so let's get the show started.... Carlson: First up, we would like to talk a little bit about the upcoming pay-per-view event, Mega Combat. It's going to be a SPECTACULAR show, so call your local cable company today! Thompson: If you don't know how to order pay-per-view, let me give you some brief instructions. Just pick up the phone, and tell 'em that you want Mega Combat.... Carlson: Don't wait until the last minute-- call now! It's going to start immediately after this Hall Of Fame ceremony, so hurry up and order! (Deathwatch suddenly walks out onto the stage, and the audience ROARS with boos and hollers. Deathwatch pushes Hank Thompson and Chuck Carlson away, and begins to speak) Deathwatch: Get off the stage! I am here to make an announcement, and all you are doing is getting in MY way. So go on-- Get out of here, and quit your shameless advertising! Scurry off and calm down this audience as I speak! The AEWA Hall of Fame awards ceremonies.... This is a great moment in wrestling for you-- but a piece of crap to me! Now, I have waited TOO long. This is what I get for stalling.... And now, the announcement you all have NOT been expecting. The announcement that will SHAKE this very federation. You morons couldn't handle this on a tape, so I'm doing it LIVE! Right here, RIGHT now-- on LIVE television. This is it, this is the Armageddon for AEWA. This will rock the federation until the federation is NO MORE! The AEWA is overrun with losers, and it makes me want to vomit! I am here now to fix all of this.... You wanted the AEWA to unionize to take me out!? THINK AGAIN! I am going FULL force on AEWA now-- this has pushed me TO THE LIMIT! Allow me to introduce to you, the first two men that are willing to join me on my quest to DESTROY this crappy little federation, two of the BEST athletes in the world today and two men that will separate the men from the BOYS. The boys obviously being men like Marcus McTeague! He is on the list of men to be executed and taken out of action first! Now for MY announcement, the men who will take this federation apart piece-by-piece.... (HEAVY METAL and EXODUS walk out and stand beside Deathwatch. The audience is completely silent, in total shock. Exodus smiles in arrogance, and Heavy Metal has his hair pulled back and is smiling as he wears his dark shades) Deathwatch: These are just two of the men that are going to assist me in this quest. We may look like total opposites, but we all have ONE thing in common-- we all DESPISE the AEWA! Now to make sure we have everything covered, I'm bringing out my next member of International Contracts Unlimited! (A man walks out, wearing dark blue and black tights. He has long black hair that hangs to the sides of his head, he also has a scar that runs from ear-to-ear on his neck. The man has a stiff and cold look on his face and shows no emotion at all) Deathwatch: This is Exodus' tag team partner, you will all find out who this man is later on. So what are you saying? We STILL don't have the Light Heavyweight division covered? Think again! (Penance comes out wearing a white shirt and a black leather vest with black vinyl pants on. As he walks out, the crowd is yet again in shock) Deathwatch: Penance hasn't been around lately! Isn't that right? (Deathwatch steps aside to allow Penance to speak, who then steps up to the podium) Penance: When I came to this sorry excuse for a wrestling organization, I was the "good guy", trying to help everyone.... trying to beat on those who would "wrong the federation". Well my old friend Pestilence, which some of you may not remember, led me to a NEW direction. I joined the Church, and I was on top of the world! Then I realized how poorly the Church was organized, so I QUIT! I took a nice vacation, but just a few days ago, I'm sitting at home and the telephone rings. I pick it up and Deathwatch is on the other line.... and I think here, the last time I saw him he was goody-two-shoes running around with the Caretaker and getting no recognition.... but I listened to what he had to offer, and what he offered was far greater than any AEWA contract! So, I am not here to be the "good guy" now. I'm here to DESTROY this federation with Deathwatch. I like his purpose and I hate the AEWA-- so I guess it all works perfectly! (Penance steps aside, and Heavy Metal walks up and grabs the microphone. His AEWA World Title is draped over his right shoulder.) Heavy Metal: Deathwatch is a man I would NEVER have thought of to align with.... until he made his proposition. I LOVED the sound of it. This title isn't the AEWA's anymore, it's the ICU World Title and I think I'm gonna take it down to the shop and melt it down.... make some other form of shape for it. All I know is it looks stupid right now and I gotta fix it! Deathwatch: And let's hear from my crowning achievement, the ONLY man to step forward without me offering a THING.... the man that has no appreciation for ANYONE-- EXODUS! (Exodus is wearing his flashy costume and his long white silk cape. He pulls his hair back and lets it drop, and he talks into the microphone) Exodus: Actually, I do appreciate one man-- ME! You know what? I came from another federation, and I'm the champion over there... and that is, I assure you, NO mishap whatsoever. McTeague, you may have gotten lucky on me, but don't expect it to happen again! I'll get my rematch with you, and then it will be just ME and YOU.... nobody to tag out to, no help. Anyway, that isn't the point. I came here to this sh*tty federation, and now I am going to help Deathwatch break it down-- until all that is left are memories of what once was here! (points to the other guy) And as far as my partner goes, you can call him LEGACY! His name fits so perfectly, since he is the legacy of his ancestors tradition. He fights for one side-- MINE! I control this operation JUST as much as Deathwatch does.... and Legacy is here to help us. Isn't that right, Legacy? (Legacy stands perfectly still, saying nothing) Exodus: Well, you get the picture. From this day forth, there is a new law in the AEWA, and that law goes by the name of International Contracts Unlimited! Deathwatch: And what's with this? (points to the audience) All the little lambs gather at the blow of whistle and come to this arena for these "award ceremonies". So you came here to see the AEWA wrestlers get an award? For WHAT? Mister Crowley was once the leader against the man Cardinal Chaos and all of his "terror". He's held the World title FOUR times-- BIG deal! It isn't how many TIMES you hold it, it's how LONG you can hold it for! (points to Heavy Metal) Heavy Metal is appropriately named, because he goes through every opponent like a steamroller. Maybe this man is made of Metal because NO MAN has been able to defeat him-- not ONCE! (Heavy Metal whispers into Deathwatch's ear) Deathwatch: Okay, so maybe ONCE, but that fool doesn't count anyway! And then there's Cardinal Chaos, the religious fanatic. Oh NO! Be afraid, he has his precious church behind him! HA HA HA HA! Church of the Pounding Light, is that what he calls his group? Yes, well, it's more like the Church of the Pathetic Losers! Oh, who else is an award nominee tonight? Big Ron Raider? Raider, a fat slob to say the least, wouldn't get a single vote, unless he flooded the ballot with his, so he is not even a concern here. Everyone knows who the "winners" are, but right now I am declaring both of them losers with a poor cause.... After all this time fighting against each other, now for some odd reason, they align. What do I have to say about this? Absolutely NOTHING! They can be screw ups with each other for as long as they want for all I care! Right now, all I care about is the DESTRUCTION and the total CARNAGE about to be unleashed upon the AEWA! Now, as you all may think I'm full of empty threats, it's time to PROVE my statements!! (Deathwatch snaps his fingers together, and Heavy Metal KICKS the podium over. Legacy tears the microphone out of the podium as Deathwatch goes into the crowd. Deathwatch slams his fist through a table, and pushes a security guard out of the way! Penance and Exodus go to work picking tables up and smashing them onto the floor breaking them instantaneously! More security rushes out there, but they can't seem to get things under control. Then El Aviadore comes in and grabs Penance by the arms. Legacy comes from behind and pounds his fist into Aviadore's back, and then proceeds to viciously kick him down. More security rushes in and they attempt to restrain them, but again to no success. Deathwatch snaps his fingers again and they all suddenly stop.) Deathwatch: We've proved our point. Let us go, there's nothing left here but a broken room. Soon this is what the AEWA will become-- BROKEN! Be prepared for the destruction of the AEWA! (Heavy Metal pushes people down as they try to get back up, and Legacy just stands there with a stone cold look in his eyes. Deathwatch laughs sadistically as they all leave. Paramedics come out and attend to El Aviadore, who is laying there unconscious from the attack. Chuck Carlson and Hank Thompson slowly walk back up to the stage, and pick the microphone up off the ground.) Carlson: Ladies and gentlemen, I apologize for that horrible interruption. We're just trying to have a nice little award show, and those jerks completely ruined everything. Thompson: So what was that all about? Deathwatch is forming his own STABLE? Don't we already have enough anarchy in the AEWA? Carlson: Even Heavy Metal, our own World champion, was with him. This is an absolute disgrace. Thompson: Anyway, let's move on. It's now time to induct the first legend into our Hall Of Fame! Carlson: Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to tell you a little bit about this man. When the AEWA was created earlier this year, Tommy Smith was the head honcho. He was a fearless president, not afraid to personally confront the wrestlers when they got out of control. Smith wasn't always the most popular president because of this, but he was ALWAYS the most respected. On numerous occasions, he put his body on the line-- just to enforce the rules. Thompson: Tommy Smith has always been the "Captain Kirk" of professional wrestling. He has ALWAYS been the guiding light of the AEWA, and we would like to honor him for that tonight. Carlson: Ladies and gentlemen, here is the first inductee to the AEWA Hall Of Fame.... Tommy Smith! (As the band plays softly in the background, Smith walks up to the stage. He is wearing a nice tuxedo, and smiles to the crowd) Tommy Smith: Thank you, thank you. As you all know, I have busted my tail since being here in the AEWA, and I have not always made popular decisions, but it does go with the job. (polite applause) I'm not going to waste your time. I just want to say thank you to everybody, and I hope that I can continue doing the job that you all deserve! (As the crowd cheers, Smith walks back to his seat in the audience) Thompson: That was great. Up next, we would like to talk about a man who is an ICON of the American E-Wrestling Association-- Mister Crowley. Carlson: This guy has been in the AEWA since day-one, and his wars with Cardinal Chaos are legendary. Crowley is a four-time former World champion, so he definitely deserves a spot here in the award show. Thompson: Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome the next inductee to our Hall Of Fame.... Mister Crowley! (The room is suddenly emerged in a pool of blackness. The sounds of the ominous "bong" can be heard in the distance. The temperature in the room rises to an eerily high degree.) Carlson: (wiping the sweat from his forehead) What the hell? I don't get paid enough for this! Thompson: This is rather strange.... (Light returns to the room as quickly as it disappeared. Hank Thompson and Chuck Carlson are still standing on-stage, alone.) Carlson: Where is Crowley? That was weird. Thompson: I guess he couldn't make it. Let's just announce the next winner and get the heck out of here. I'm suddenly thinking this award ceremony was a bad idea. Carlson: Yeah, it's starting to look that way. Anyway, our next inductee was the first ever AEWA World champion. He is leader of the "Church Of The Pounding Light" around here, and one of the most feared men in professional wrestling history. Thompson: Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome the next inductee into our Hall Of Fame.... Cardinal Chaos! (A gust of wind rips through the auditorium. Suddenly, every light in the building starts to glow at an unbelievable rate. Within seconds, the room is filled with a blinding light.) Carlson: I definitely don't need this! What the hell is happening in here? Thompson: I have NO idea.... (The lights begin to return to normal. The crowd is dazed and slightly disoriented. Behind the podium stands Cardinal Chaos, and at his side is Mister Crowley.) Carlson: What's going on here? Cardinal Chaos (pushing Carlson aside): I shall talk now, little man. Now is not the time for your puny ramblings and insidious remarks. The AEWA held a vote for the best man in the world, well, it seems that the followers of the light have spoken. (pointing to Mister Crowley) They have chosen this man! Thompson: But they chose you also... Cardinal Chaos (pushing Thompson aside): Yes, they did. But that was inevitable, my son. For thirteen days I have been behind closed doors with this man. I have fought with him, ate with him, meditated with him, beat him, and been beaten by him. I have been to the top of the mountain with him and down to the pits of fire below. I have seen the world through his dark eyes and shown him the view through mine. I have accepted this man as my brother, and he has accepted me. Carlson: What are you saying? Cardinal Chaos: DO NOT interrupt me again, pion. What I'm saying is this-- The world of the AEWA is about to undergo a major transformation. A transformation of biblical proportions. The two most powerful men in the history of this fine sport have worked out their differences. This can only mean one thing for the AEWA. Brother Crowley, would you do me the honors of telling the flock what is in store for the AEWA? Mister Crowley: Death. Cardinal Chaos: Ladies and gentlemen, I give you the most awesome force in the AEWA.... The future of the federation.... The end of civilized wrestling.... We are the newest tag-team in the AEWA, Heaven and Hell! (Mister Crowley suddenly picks up Chuck Carlson, and executes the Premature Burial-- right through the already-broken podium.) Thompson: Somebody call security! NOW! (Cardinal Chaos picks up Thompson and shows him the power of the Inverted Crucifix. Security storms the stage, trying desperately to stop the carnage that is quickly ensuing. One by one, they are knocked down. The crowd is frantically rushing to the exits. Mister Crowley grabs the microphone and yells out to the crowd.) Mister Crowley: I don't think so. We're not done yet. (The exit doors suddenly lock and the room is once again submerged in darkness. The sounds of screams rise up from the crowd. When the lights come back on again, we see that many of the audience members have fainted. The stage is covered with the unconscious bodies of what was once AEWA's finest security guards. In the center of the stage we see President Freeman tied to a metal folding chair. To his right is Crowley, to his left is Chaos.) President Freeman: Please don't. I always liked you two. You were my favorite wrestlers in the federation. Cardinal Chaos: Enough! We have suffered through your banal diatribes far too long, Freeman. The time has come to convert your pitiful soul. President Freeman: (screaming) NO, DEAR GOD, NO! I'll do anything you say, just don't KILL me! PLEASE! Mister Crowley: Anything? President Freeman: Anything! Just let me live! (Mister Crowley and Cardinal Chaos briefly whisper to each other. A smile comes to both of their faces.) Cardinal Chaos: Alright Freeman, this is what you shall do. At the next AEWA live event, Heaven and Hell shall receive a title shot at the AEWA tag team belts. Curate Kill shall be the referee assigned to the match. It will be a "no disqualification match", and YOU will personally award the title belts to the winners. President Freeman: You're crazy. I can't do that. You guys are out of your minds! (Mister Crowley grabs Freeman's left hand and crushes it. The sounds of bones breaking echo through the auditorium.) President Freeman: (screams in pain) OWWW! Jesus Christ, STOP IT! Cardinal Chaos (laughing): It's a good thing that my new partner didn't crush your right hand. You're going to need it, Freeman. (he pulls a legal contract from his tights.) SIGN IT! President Freeman: (whispering) You'll have to kill me first, you crazy bastards.... Mister Crowley: (grabbing Freeman around the neck) So be it. President Freeman: (gasping for air) Wait.... wait.... I'll sign it.... Please.... (Chaos hands the contract to Freeman who awkwardly signs it. With the signed contract in their hands, Heaven and Hell turn to leave the auditorium.) President Freeman: I'll give you the match, you bastards, after the police are done with you! Look at what you've done here. You'll both be fried for this. I'm President Freeman, dammit, NOBODY pulls one over on me. Ha! Ha! (Cardinal Chaos returns to the stage and grabs the microphone.) Cardinal Chaos: You really should read contracts before you sign them, Freeman. Look at your copy and check out the small print. Paragraph 19.3 clearly states that the AEWA CAN NOT reprimand us for the events of this night. It further states that the events of this night are solely the responsibility of the AEWA and that any bodily injury imposed on a spectator or official are the sole responsibility of the AEWA. It looks like this night was your fault, Freeman. Who's laughing now? Mister Crowley: Don't worry, Freeman. With time and therapy, everyone here will be just fine. Cardinal Chaos: Yeah, Freeman. We're going to be the new AEWA Tag Team champions, but we're NOT monsters. (Chaos drops the microphone at Presidents Freeman's feet, and he and Crowley walk out laughing.
