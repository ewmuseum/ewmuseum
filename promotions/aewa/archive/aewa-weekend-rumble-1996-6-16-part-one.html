Weekend Rumble (6/16, part one)

20 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen, set on fire. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power Bomb, and a look at Mister Crowley DRIVING the head of Volcano Jackson into the concrete with the Premature Burial! Then the camera switches to "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt, the Lovely Bertha, and Matt Wilson in a packed arena) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to a very special show! We are here in beautiful San Francisco, for the AEWA's first Weekend Rumble! LB: That's right, buddy. Most wrestling organizations would offer something like this on pay-per-view only.... but we're giving it to you for FREE! BD: So what matches do we have scheduled for tonight? MW: Mister Crowley is going to defend his World title against Volcano Jackson in a Philadelphia Street Fight! That should be incredible.... LB: And Cardinal Chaos will finally put an end to "the Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum-- I can hardly wait! Plus, the first round of our tag team tournament will finally conclude tonight! BD: So what's going to happen first? MW: After weeks of absense, "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews is finally BRAVE ENOUGH to give Butch Taylor a title shot. But it won't be any ordinary match-- the loser will NEVER be able to wrestle again! LB: Is that really necessary? Do we always need to have these stupid rules? BD: I totally agree with you, Bertha. A "retirement match" is WAY too risky. MW: Shane Matthews has been running from Butch Taylor for a long time now, and it's ALL going to end tonight. This should be great.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen, this is a special "retirement match" for the AEWA Light Heavyweight title! ("Fortunate Son" begins playing loudly, and Butch Taylor runs out. He is wearing white camouflage) LB: This guy is a total maniac, and he should never be allowed in an AEWA ring. BD: Why do they let him run WILD like that? MW: What are you guys talking about? Butch Taylor graduated from an expensive wrestling school in Nevada, so he shouldn't be taken lightly. ("Looking Down The Barrel Of A Gun" can be heard playing on the speakers, and Shane Matthews slowly struts to ringside. He is escorted by several beautiful women, with Johnny Terror following closely behind. Matthews hands his title belt to them, then immediately enters the ring and attacks Taylor) LB: This match is already underway-- it looks like a street fight in there! BD: Matthews slaps Taylor pretty hard, and follows it with a stunning piledriver! He's trying to HUMILIATE that idiot.... I love it! MW: "Sudden Death" is wasting no time right now. He's locking him into an abdominal stretch! LB: Butch Taylor screams out in pain! What a little crybaby! He DEMANDED this match, but now he can't HANDLE it! The action will continue, so stay tuned.... (We go to commercials, and see an advertisement for the new TNM Wrestling Game. After several minutes, the show comes back on) MW: Taylor is STILL trapped in that abdominal stretch! More than four minutes have passed, but he WON'T submit! BD: Matthews just released the hold, and begins to nail him with some jumping kicks. Many people don't realize this, but "Sudden Death" is actually quite skilled in the martial arts. MW: I haven't heard anything about that, Big Daddy. Where do you get your information? BD: Call my hotline and find out! It's only $3.99 per minute (looks at the camera) but kids, get your parent's permission first! LB: Wait a minute! Butch Taylor just jumped up, and he NAILS Matthews with a crushing uppercut! BD: Where the hell did THAT come from? Is this man on DRUGS or something? MW: Taylor is on top of Matthews now, and he won't stop punching him.... "Sudden Death" is starting to bleed! LB: This is totally disgusting! BD: (yelling) Now Taylor climbs the turnbuckle.... He goes for a flying dropkick, but Matthews grabs his legs on the way down-- and reverses it into an incredible Boston crab! MW: For once in my life, I'm actually speechless! We're seeing some GREAT wrestling in there tonight! LB: Shane Matthews is really applying the pressure, and Butch Taylor begs for mercy. It's almost over! BD: "Sudden Death" has that move perfectly applied.... MW: And Taylor just submitted! (ding ding) After 5 minutes of incredible action, this match is history. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner-- and still AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion-- "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews! LB: So do you realize what this means? Butch Taylor will NEVER be able to wrestle ANYWHERE again! BD: (laughing) That stupid moron is FINALLY gone! MW: Come on, guys.... He was a great competitor, so show him some respect! Maybe we should give him a "going away" party. LB: Are you kidding me? Who would actually come? BD: Shane Matthews has just won another thrilling match, and it doesn't surprise me a bit. That kid is amazing.... MW: We are about to be joined by Skim Hoss, who has some big news for us. This should be really interesting.... LB: Why does President Smith even let this guy on TV? (Skim Hoss arrives at the booth, and sits down. He puts on a headset, and begins to speak) HOSS: Ladies and gentlemen, I have some very important news. As you already know, Joey Valachi has been getting into all kinds of trouble lately. Even in his recent matches, it seemed like he was actually TRYING to lose! And those strange "mystery men" keep arriving after his losses, giving him more money each time. This strange behavior reached a climax recently, when he tried to attacked President Smith in a hospital room. Well, I sent out my "investigative team" to do a little research on this situation.... BD: So what? Joey Valachi has never done ANYTHING illegal in his life! What could your friends POSSIBLY find out? HOSS: It's very shocking, Big Daddy. We found out that the "mystery men" have been paying Valachi to LOSE! BD: (yelling) Yeah right! Why would he ever do that? HOSS: Because these "mystery men" are betting AGAINST him in Las Vegas, and making a fortune because of it! Every time Valachi loses a match, they make more money.... BD: So what are you telling me? Valachi has been THROWING his matches lately? HOSS: It sure appears that way! And here's some more shocking information.... Valachi is scheduled to wrestle Shane Matthews for the Light Heavyweight title very soon. BD: So is he going to lose THAT on purpose? One more loss could RUIN his career! HOSS: Those "mystery men" have already bet millions of dollars against Valachi, so it's very likely. (stands up) I'm a busy man, so this meeting will have to end now. BD: See you later, fat boy.... (Hoss leaves the booth, and walks away) MW: I am totally dumbfounded right now. Could that possibly be true? Has Joey Valachi been losing his matches ON PURPOSE? LB: There doesn't seem to be any other explanation, Wilson. Those "mystery men" must be giving him a LOT of money for this! BD: I will be interested to see what happens during Valachi's upcoming encounter with Shane Matthews. He only has two options-- lose the match, and make millions of dollars-- or win, and take home the title! MW: (looks shocked) I still can't believe this situation! Valachi is actually involved with the MAFIA? He's always been an idiot, but I never expected anything THAT stupid! LB: Leave him alone, Wilson! BD: This has been a very exciting card so far.... What match is happening next? Isn't it time for some tag team action yet? MW: Well, the first round of the AEWA World Tag Team Title Tournament ends tonight, and the second begins, assuming all participants show up. (The Maloney Brothers enter the ring to "Teenage Kicks" by the Undertones) MW: Former AEWA National tag champs, the Maloney Brothers have amassed more victories in the AEWA than just about any team. BD: And had their asses kicked by more teams than anyone else. The Icemen, Heavy Metal Crew, Submission... LB: And don't forget Mayhem, Inc. MW: No one ever forgets Mayhem, Inc. And it's funny you should mention them, since... (The standard entrance music Ric Flair uses plays over the PA system, as Josh Brown leads the current National Title holders to the ring.) MW: History has a funny way of repeating itself- in the two previous tag tourneys in the AEWA, these teams met. In the initial world tag title tournament, Mayhem beat the Maloneys in the first round. In the National Tag Team tournament, the Maloneys defeated Mayhem, Inc. in the finals. Our third tag team tournament brings these teams together a third time, and we're underway! AEWA World Tag Team Championship Tournament First Round Match AEWA National Tag Team Champions MAYHEM, INC versus THE MALONEY BROTHERS Mayhem II and Brian Maloney started for their teams. Mayhem, Inc, dominated the early action with a series of quick tags, brutal brawling tactics, a considerable size advantage, and the confusion that their similar builds and outfits provide. A powerbomb by Mayhem I (who was not the legal man, by the way) brought about a 2-count at the 4 minute mark. When Mayhem I missed his patented Avalanche, Brian dove into his corner and tagged in Kelly. Kelly immediately went to the top rope, but Josh Brown pulled on the ropes until Kelly fell to the floor. The 300 pound Mayhem I then performed an over-the-top-rope plancha, flattening the 212-pound Kelly. He dragged Kelly back in, covered, and picked up the three-count at 5:57, completing what was essentially a squash. BD: That was quick. MW: Well, Mayhem's manager had something to do with it. LB: Right, he scouted the Maloneys for works, ran the strategy sessions.... MW: Interfered in the match.... BD: Now wait a second- that match lasted 357 seconds. For three seconds, Brown interfered. That's a ratio of 119 seconds to 1, meaning his interference accounted for less than 1% of the action. I think he ought to be commended for his restraint. MW: I think you could use some constraints. LB: Can we start the next match? I've been dying to see this! MW: One of the quirks of these random matchups in the tournament is that sometimes friends fight against friends. In this instance, both teams are part of the same stable. The Blood Angels and the Tidal Waves, up next. (Monsoon Mason and Tsunami Jones hit the ring to "Another One Bites the Dust", but without their manager. Dark Horse and Eradicator also were without their manager as they entered the ring.) AEWA World Tag Team Championship Tournament First Round Match THE TIDAL WAVES versus THE BLOOD ANGELS Mason and Horse represented their teams at first, and it was pretty obvious that friendship was out the window as both teams smelled gold. It isn't often that the 6'8 300 pound Dark Horse is at a size disadvantage, but Monsoon Mason at 7'6 450 towered over him. Horse went for a running shoulderblock, and Mason didn't budge (although Horse bounced backward and landed on his head). Horse altered his tactics, and tried a forearm smash in the chest. Mason responded with one across the back of the neck, and Dark Horse crumpled to the mat. Mason then delivered a falling splash and covered, but Eradicator made the save with an elbow drop in the back of Mason's head. This brought Jones into the ring, and we had four men totaling 1,499 pounds all brawling at once. Jones was pounding on the Eradicator in one corner, while Mason choked away at Dark Horse. Jones and Mason tried a double Irish whip to knock their opponents into each other, but Eradicator reversed the move, and whipped Jones into the middle, where he collided with Dark Horse. As Mason was able to whip Dark Horse in with greater force than Eradicator could with Jones, it was Tsunami Jones who toppled backward, and Dark Horse covered for a one count. As Dark Horse tried to maintain his advantage over Jones, Mason and Eradicator dug into one another, with Mason getting the upperhand. After a few more minutes of this brawling, Jones grabbed a handful of Dark Horse's tights, and using the leverage advantage this offered, dumped Horse out of the ring. The Tidal Waves were left alone in the ring with the Eradicator. Mason climbed to the second turnbuckle, and Jones helped him position Eradicator for a second rope piledriver. Mason covered, got the three, and the Tidal Waves were on their way to a second round matchup with Mayhem, Inc., at 12:16. MW: I always knew I should have trained to become a chiropractor. Did you see how the Eradicator's spine seemed to bend as he was piledriven from the second turnbuckle into the mat? And these guys are supposed to be friends. BD: Once that bell rings, you have no friends. In fact, sometimes fighting a friend will inspire you to greater violence, because every time he punches or kicks you, you think about the time you drove him home from the bar. Every time he gouges your eyes, you think of the food he's eaten in your home. And every time you slap him around, you think of all the bragging rights this gives you. Personally, I always liked it. MW: You had friends when you wrestled. BD: For some reason it never lasted long. MW: I wonder why. BD: I could never figure it out. MW: Easter Island, Stonehenge, and Big Daddy's social life. I guess some things just aren't meant to be understood. Now that the first round is over, let's take a look at who's left. The Trailer Park Assassins > The Masters of Destruction > Submission > Submission > (bye) Graveyard Shift Mayhem, Inc. > The Tidal Waves > > Suicide Squad > Skull and Crossbones MW: We were going to start the second round tonight, but since Cooter Lee Roy still hasn't appeared, and since we'd only have one second round match on Wednesday, since neither the Shadow Warriors nor Sky Walker and Red Dog advanced after their first round match, Cooter is being given three more days to come to his partner's side. Otherwise, Orbit will stand in the ring alone with Raekwon and Old Dirty Bastard salivating at the chance for a handicap match. If another non-result occurs for the duration of the tournament, we'll fill the time with a NATO Powers-Judicial Powers matchup, but for now all tag matches this month are part of the tag title tourney, so we may have to wait a few more weeks before seeing those teams meet for the first time. BD: You talk too much, Wilson. (stands up) I hate to leave you two alone again, but President Smith is going golfing with me. LB: Really? You hang around with him? BD: Of course, Bertha! President Smith even lets me play on his computer and stuff! We're actually pretty good buddies! I'd love to stay and chat, but it's almost time.... (Big Daddy takes off his headset, and walks away) MW: Don't be fooled by him.... President Smith would NEVER hang around with a loser like that. In case you haven't already noticed, Big Daddy has a slight problem with lying. LB: I'm sure he wouldn't lie to me! MW: Bertha, you really can't afford to be that naive. Big Daddy is nothing but a con artist.... I don't understand why you even like him! (They look at each other, and there is an awkward silence) LB: Anyway.... What match is next? MW: Our next match is a contest between the last two men to get world title shots at Mister Crowley, The Crazy Canuck and Primal Fear. This should be a very interesting matchup. LB: Interesting my butt. Primal Fear gave Crowley all he could handle before Crowley took a blatant DQ, but the Canuck got squashed by Crowley. Those two matches were NOTHING alike. MW: Maybe Crowley just caught the Canuck on an off night. LB: (sarcastically) Whatever you say, all knowing one. MW: Let's just go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from the Badlands, Sudan, he weighs in at 360 pounds and is accompanied by his manager, "The Beautiful" Miss DeMeanor, Primal Feeeeeeeeeeeeeeeear! (Primal Fear makes his entrance, gnawing on a raw slab of meat, to a large chorus of boos. Miss DeMeanor follows close behind.) LB: That's my main man there! He would of won both singles titles by now, but he was cheated out of them both. You know, I bet that loser Smith paid that referee to disqualify Crowley JUST so Primal Fear couldn't win the title. MW: Come on, Bertha, what other choice did the ref have? Would you rather have had Crowley choke your "main man" to death? LB: Primal Fear CANNOT die, he is invincible. MW: If that's what you want to believe. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 253 pounds and accompanied by his manager, NATO, The Crazy Canuuuuuuuuuuuuuck! (Pat Benatar's "Hit Me With Your Best Shot" begins playing loudly over the PA, as The Crazy Canuck makes his entrance to a large crowd pop. NATO is at his side.) LB: And now here's the Dead Duck. MW: That's Canuck. LB: Same difference. MW: And the Canuck has made it to the ring, and we're ready to go! He steps through the ropes, and gets NAILED by a running elbowsmash from Primal Fear! LB: He's not wasting any time. MW: He sure isn't. Primal Fear picks up the stunned Canuck, BACK SUPLEX! The Canuck looks hurt already! The Canuck makes it to his feet, and gets DOUBLED OVER with a fist in the midsection! LB: My man is out with a vengeance tonight! MW: Yeah, but his vengeance should be against Mister Crowley, not the Canuck. LB: Crowley isn't wrestling now, the Canuck is. MW: I see. Back in the ring, Primal Fear LEVELS the Crazy Canuck with a lariat! The Canuck slowly gets back to his feet, and just as he does, Primal Fear kicks his legs out from under him! LB: No Mercy! MW: Primal Fear picks the Canuck up, INVERTED ATOMIC DROP! But Primal Fear does not let the Canuck fall! He grabs him around the waist, AND TAKES HIM OVER WITH A BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One..Kickout! LB: ALL THAT for a freaking one count? MW: I guess so, but Primal Fear's not happy about it. He picks the Canuck up, and NAILS him with a BRUTAL kneelift! Another cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: Much better. Now go in for the kill! MW: It looks like that's what he's doing! He brings the Canuck to his feet, grabs him by the neck, AND ELEVATES HIM, BRUTAL CHOKESLAM! And he's not through yet! He picks the Canuck up again, SIDE SUPLEX! LB: He's signaling for the Bump Splash! It's all over! MW: Yes, he appears to be setting up for it, he's in position, BUT THE CANUCK GRABS HIM BY THE LEGS, AND TAKES HIM OVER WITH A SUNSET FLIP! One..Two..Kickout! LB: What a LUCKY move by the Canuck! MW: Lucky nothing, Primal Fear took way too long setting up for that move, and on top of that, he told everyone in the arena that it was coming. That was some good quick thinking by the Canuck! LB: Whatever. MW: Primal Fear is up, and he is UPSET at being caught in that maneuver! He gets up, and kicks the Canuck in the midsection! Now he picks him up, runs, POWERSLAM! The cover! One..Two..Thr..Kickout! LB: He's got him now! It won't be much longer. MW: Not if it continues like this. Primal Fear picks up the Canuck, and TOSSES him over the top rope like a rag doll! Primal Fear goes out onto the apron, and DIVES to the outside! BUT THE CANUCK MOVES! And Primal Fear SMACKS against the concrete! LB: OUCH! That even hurt ME! He REALLY hit the ground hard! MW: And now the Canuck is up and ready to take advantage! Primal Fear gets to his feet, and the Canuck RAMS him into the guardrail! And now he grabs Primal Fear, ELBOW SMASH! LB: How is this puny little Canuck beating Primal Fear on the floor? That's where he's at his best! MW: Two reasons, tenacity, and that little spill that Primal Fear just took, probably a little more of the latter. Anyway, they're still on the outside, and the Canuck runs Primal Fear into the ringpost! He sure isn't pulling any stops! LB: Yeah, why isn't he? He's supposed to be a goody goody! MW: I think it's because he knows that, if given the opportunity, Primal Fear would do the EXACT same thing to him. Canuck the stunned Primal Fear, grabs a double underhook, and DRIVES the head of Primal Fear into the concrete! What a DEVASTATING double underhook piledriver on the floor! LB: Isn't the ref supposed to be counting or something? MW: Yes, but I don't think anyone wants to see this one end in a double countout, except maybe Primal Fear right about now. Now the Canuck grabs him again, BRUTAL snap mare! And the Canuck FINALLY heads back to the ring. LB: It's about time! Look at all the damage the ref let him do on the floor! MW: Shut up, Bertha. You wouldn't be complaining if it was Primal Fear doing the damage on the floor. LB: Well that's different. MW: That's what I figured. Primal Fear finally makes it back to the ring, for a second there I didn't think he had enough left to beat the count. LB: What count? He's been out there at least a few minutes. MW: The count that started when Canuck got back in the ring. LB: It figures that that's when this biased piece of crap would start counting! I bet Smith paid this ref off too! MW: Highly unlikely. Primal Fear staggers to his feet, AND GETS LEVELED WITH A SHORT LARIAT FROM THE CANUCK! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Primal Fear cannot be pinned, especially not with a move as crappy as that. MW: It was a great move, And Primal Fear was REALLY in trouble. It was a great pinning opportunity. LB: Stop kissing the Canuck's butt! MW: If ANYONE'S doing any butt kissing around here, it's you kissing Primal Fear's. LB: I'm just telling it like it is! MW: You mean like you WISH it is. Back in the ring, the Canuck takes Primal Fear over with a BRUTAL neck snap! And now another short lariat! And the cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: And another WEAK pin attempt by the Canuck. MW: Why don't you just shut up until you know what the HELL you're talking about? Primal Fear slowly gets to his feet, and the Canuck quickly runs to the ropes! On the comeback, PRIMAL FEAR CATCHES HIM WITH A BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: Now THAT'S a pin attempt! MW: It wasn't any better than any of the Canuck's. LB: Sure it was! MW: How so? LB: It was Primal Fear, that's how! MW: That's what I figured you'd say. Why don't you try the concept of logic some time, you might like it. LB: Shut up, Wilson! MW: The Canuck gets to his feet, and staggers to the corner. Primal Fear runs at him, AVALANCHE--BUT THE CANUCK MOVES! And Primal Fear hits the buckles hard! Primal Fear staggers out of the corner, and gets NAILED with ANOTHER short lariat! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: How can a little wimp like the Canuck be doing this to Primal Fear? MW: Because no matter what you believe, the Canuck is not, by any means, a little wimp. He is an excellent wrestler and he deserves to be treated as such. The Canuck now, whips Primal Fear to the ropes, AND INTO THE REFEREE! The ref is out COLD! LB: Ahhhh! Here's the opportunity we've been waiting for! MW: You mean YOU'VE been waiting for. Primal Fear is down, and Miss DeMeanor reaches into her purse for something, IT'S A PAIR OF BRASS KNUCKLES! She hands them to Primal Fear! And the Canuck doesn't see it, he's busy attending to the ref! LB: Aren't ref bumps wonderful? MW: Not really. And now Miss DeMeanor jumps up on the apron! LB: And the stupid Canuck falls for it! MW: The Canuck goes over to confront her, and he gets CLOCKED from behind with those brass knuckles! The Canuck is out! Miss DeMeanor is going into her purse again, and pulls of a bottle of some kind! What is it? LB: Looks like lighter fluid. MW: YES, it is! AND SHE'S SPRAYING IT ALL OVER THE BACK OF THE CANUCK! LB: Canuck roast, anyone? MW: This is not funny! Now Primal Fear reaches into his pocket, and pulls out a book of matches! He lights one, AND TOSSES IT ONTO THE BACK OF THE CANUCK! THE CRAZY CANUCK HAS BEEN SET ON FIRE BY PRIMAL FEAR! (The image of the flaming Canuck is blurred by the censors.) MW: MY GOD! SOMEONE MUST CONTROL THIS MAN! THIS KIND OF THING HAS NO PLACE IN WRESTLING! LB: So, do you like your Canuck extra crispy or original recipe? MW: WILL YOU STOP MAKING JOKES, THIS IS NOT FUNNY! LB: Extra crispy then? Me too. MW: WHERE THE HELL IS SECURITY? The Canuck is kicking and screaming, SOMEONE MUST STOP THIS! LB: And Primal Fear does, with a big splash! MW: Yes, Primal Fear puts the fire out with his own body, smothering it by splashing onto the flaming back of the Crazy Canuck! At least it's out, but that was TOTALLY UNCALLED FOR, PRIMAL FEAR SHOULD BE BANNED FROM THE AEWA FOR LIFE! LB: The barbecue's over and now it's time to eat! MW: YES, PRIMAL FEAR HAS BEGUN TO SINK HIS TEETH INTO THAT BURNT BACK OF THE CANUCK! AND BLOOD POURS OUT EVERYWHERE! THIS IS DISGUSTING! (The image becomes even more blurred.) LB: Don't you know? This is his favorite dish, Fillet O' Canuck! MW: LOOK AT THAT INSANE LOOK IN THE EYES OF PRIMAL FEAR! HE LOVES THIS! WHAT A SICK, SICK MAN! And now he stops eating, picks the Canuck up, AND POWERSLAMS HIM ON THAT SEVERELY BURNT BACK! The Cover? Is this match still going on? LB: I didn't hear no bell, and the ref is still out! Miss DeMeanor is getting him up! MW: The ref is getting his senses, and Miss DeMeanor is pointing him toward the pin attempt! He sees it and begins to crawl over! NO, it can't end like THIS! He makes it there, and slowly begins to count! One........................................................................... Two............................................................................ Three!!! (DING, DING) MW: They CAN'T be allowing this! RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner.... PRIMAL FEAR! LB: They just did! Ain't it great? MW: No, it's not great! Since WHEN are you allowed to light your opponent on fire? LB: Since anything that the ref doesn't see happen is perfectly legal. MW: Okay, but FIRE? Come on, that kind of thing could take a man's life if you make a mistake! LB: That's the risk you take everytime you step in the ring! MW: NO! You shouldn't be at risk of being burned at the stake everytime you step into the ring! This kind of violence has been running rampant in the AEWA for WEEKS now, it has to stop! President Smith, or SOMEONE, has to take a stand and do something about it! LB: I don't know, I kinda like it! MW: You would. You're just as sick as Primal Fear! LB: Thank you. MW: That wasn't a compliment. Primal Fear has left ringside long ago with his "victory" if you can call it that, and now paramedics are attending to the Canuck. They're wheeling him out on a stretcher. I hope to God he's okay. LB: I don't we need to rid the world of people like him! MW: How can you say things like that and live with yourself? LB: I'm special. MW: That's for sure. This is a very sad occurrence, but we still have other matches, and we have to move on. We'll give you updates on the condition of the Canuck as soon as we receive them.
