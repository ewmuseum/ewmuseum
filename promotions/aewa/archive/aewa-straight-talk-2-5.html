Straight Talk (2/5)

6 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera fades in on the AEWA Studios, the AEWA logo falls onto the screen, and the words "STRAIGHT TALK" slide in underneath it. The whole logo then tilts, and falls off the bottom of the screen. Behind the desk in studio are Chuck Carlson and Ken Wilson.) CC: Hello fans, and welcome to this special pre-Kickoff '97 edition of AEWA Straight Talk! I'm your host, Chuck Carlson, and with me, as always is Garth! KW: Party on, Wayne.... Hey, wait a minute! That's not my name! What do you think this is, Wayne's World? CC: Just wanted to see if you were paying attention, and apparently you weren't. KW: Only because everything you say is so damn boring. CC: I don't know HOW your brother Matt puts up with you. KW: I have him trained. CC: Apparently so, but let's get to some interviews. Up first is an exciting newcomer who, after months of teasing, is FINALLY coming to the AEWA. I'm talking, of course, about the Extremist. ---------- (The scene fades in from black to a shot of The Extremist. He sits at a table inside of his Detroit home. On the wooden table are a series of magazines, a good 10 to 20 of them. They are all pro-wrestling related. The Extremist sits on a little folding chair, he wears a black "This is Extreme" T-shirt, dark blue jeans, and a pair of black boots. His black hair hangs in his face as he digs through the pile. As the camera moves in on his face, he looks up, and meets the camera with an ear-to-ear smile. Not a smile of happiness, but a complete sh*t-eating grin. He leans back into the chair and takes a deep breath before he begins to speak.) The Extremist: I know this scene may be a bit misleading. No, I am not a jackass that goes out and buys all of these magazines on a regular basis, but I keep the ones that have things pertaining to me in them. In fact, one of these has broken my AEWA silence, and is now going to lead me into the limelight. I think this man has forgotten this incident in which he chose to run his mouth. But allow me to stir the sh*t, just like it was Kool-Aid-- I am bringing this back to life. (The Extremist leans forwards and picks up the magazine in front of him. He flips through a couples of pages and slams the magazine down.) The Extremist: I competed in the ACEW about 5 months ago; yes, the ACEW, run by that stupid jackass of a boy. But I don't feel like talking about that loser, my beef is with someone else. The self proclaimed "Big and Bad" one, Leroy Brown had some choice words for me as I walked out the door. At first, slanders from a piece of sh*t would be taken with a grain of salt, but I am not the calm Extremist I once was. This is all out, Leroy Brown, I am calling you out. Now personally, I could give a rat's ass what you have done here, ACEW, ABCDEFG, or anywhere, it doesn't mean sh*t to me. What matters to me is that somebody of your skill level has taken it upon themselves to run their mouth. Now, I am not an ordinary guy, I am NOT going to sit back and listen to some little bitch run around talking sh*t as he pleases. (The Extremist stands up....) The Extremist: I am not here to play games with any of you, this is 0 percent business as well. It is ALL about me, all about fighting, all about kicking ass. Where the chips fall are where the chips fall, I will fight whoever the hell you put in front of me. I am not in the quick loss club, when I get into the ring, I go out there to bang heads. I have had my ass handed to me on a plate before, but not in a loss. I fought Gunnar Gaines, a man that was being proclaimed unbeatable, and I took Gunnar Gaines out. I had my ass kicked in that fight, but I dealt Gunnar one better. In fact, I dealt the entire EWA their cards, and I came up all aces. Throughout the PCW and EWA I have not been pinned. My one loss comes by disqualification after slamming some poor little bastard around the ring without mercy. (The Extremist walks toward the camera.) The Extremist: The past doesn't matter, and I could care less if you care about mine, but the bottom line is, you haven't accomplished anything close to defeating Gunnar Gaines, who is actually probably the star of your wet dreams. Leroy Brown, the book has been reopened, and I don't like to read. I like to skip to the last page, and the ending of this story does not look good for you. (The Extremist walks out of the room. The camera moves in on the open magazine on the table, it shows something about the ACEW, but shortly after the scene fades to black....) ---------- KW: So the Extremist is back in town, and he looks good and ready to get in there and kick some ass. Especially against that LOSER, Leroy Brown. I can't wait. Let the slaughter begin! CC: It would be FAR from a slaughter. The Extremist has picked himself one hell of a task by coming right in and challenging Leroy Brown. And we have yet to see how this Extremist fares against AEWA competition. Don't count your chickens before they're hatched, Ken. KW: What the hell is that supposed to mean? Do you think I'm a farmer or something? I don't have any chickens! CC: Now you can't tell me you've never heard that phrase before! It's a metaphor, Ken. KW: A WHAT-a-four? CC: Oh, nevermind! Let's just watch this interview with the man who will get his shot at Deranged for the AEWA World Heavyweight Title at Kickoff '97. This is "Crimson" Joe Reed. ---------- (The camera opens to a basement with a wrestling ring built in the middle. "Crimson" Joe Reed walks down the stairs, enters the ring and sits on the top turnbuckle. He is wearing a black workout outfit, and is carrying two belts with him. He starts speaking in a calm, steady voice.) Reed: A lot of people were surprised when I received the title shot at Kickoff '97. To be honest, even I was a little surprised when I received the match this soon. This is just one more reason why I think my manager, the Lady in Red, is the best in the business. I know a lot of people in the AEWA are wondering just who the hell I am to be getting the shot. Well, I'm not a pretty boy, and I'm not claiming to be the devil, or an angel, or a dentist, or any of those things. I'm just a wrestler-- but I'm a damn good one. (Holds up the two belts) In the eleven years I've been wrestling, I've won two World Titles; in the PWF and the FWL. I never lost either belt-- you know how the business is; leagues start up and close down all the time. But I defended these titles with honor and respect, and plan on doing that with the AEWA title also. Deranged, I've watched your matches. You're good-- very good, and you've earned my respect. But I think I'm as good as they come, and I plan on beating you for the title. At Kickoff '97, prepare for the toughest match of your life.... because I will. (fade to black) ---------- KW: Boy, is this guy sappy or what? All that talk about honor and respect, I mean who really gives a crap? CC: I, for one, do, and I'm sure that many of our fans will agree. Not every wrestler carries himself with honor, but Joe Reed is one man who any fan would be proud to call champion. KW: Don't make me puke. What did Reed do to deserve this shot anyway? I betcha he paid off Freeman! CC: I think not, Joe Reed would never stoop to that level, unlike some of your favorites. He has looked impressive in what we've seen of him so far, and I, personally, am very much looking forward to this matchup with Deranged. KW: You gotta be kidding! That's the WORST match on the card! They both suck, and I don't really give horse's ass about who wins. CC: What a lovely outlook you have there. And now we've got some comments from.... well...I really don't know. ---------- (Static fades to a black screen. A deep voice resonates from the speakers, but we can't see anything.) Voice: It will be good to finally get into action after the brief tour I had through that inter-federation league. There was some talent there. But now I get back to what I love the most, beating up on all the wrestlers here in the AEWA. Some may remember me, but I see some new faces here as well. I am hungry for fresh meat! (A green glow begins to silhouette a muscular figure on the screen.) However, I will keep my identity a secret until the time I see fit to reveal myself, which may be sooner than some will like-- too bad. I'm back, and it's going to take a lot more to get me away from the AEWA this time.... a whole lot! I may not have been in the best of shape when I was here before, but believe me, I'm a changed man. However, my strongest ability in wrestling is still very sharp. If you're not ready for me, I'll leave you broken and beaten. To those that still stand, watch your back. (A green explosive is set off in front of the camera, and the screen is absolutely blinding. Static resumes; fade to black.) ---------- KW: I know who that is! CC: Who? KW: It's the Green Lantern! CC: The Green Lantern? You mean the "Justice League of America" Green Lantern? KW: The one and only-- how else do you explain that green glow? CC: Since when has the Green Lantern ever wrestled in the AEWA before? KW: Well.... I haven't exactly figured that part out yet.... CC: But you'll make it up soon enough, right? Don't bother, let's just get some words with the men who will challenge Graphic Violence for the AEWA National Tag Team Titles at Kickoff '97. Here are the Assassins. ---------- (Banshee and Shockwave are both standing in front of the Fresno Convention Center, the site of last week's Saturday Night Slam. The complex is dark, and the Assassins are standing under a street light. Shockwave has a bag of ice on his arm, but both men are smiling.) Banshee: It was just hours ago that we beat the Shadow Warriors for a chance to face Graphic Violence at Kickoff '97. It was a great win for us, one of our fastest ever! Shockwave: Shadow Warriors, you two tried to cripple me! (holds up his injured arm) I'm sorry to say, however, that you did nothing a little ice and an aspirin or two can't cure! We beat you in three minutes! One kick and a Moonsault finished you two off, and now we are off to fight for the National Tag Team Titles! Banshee: Graphic Violence, you two are going down to the Assassins! We want your National Tag Team Titles! At Kickoff '97, Shockwave and I will destroy you just like we did the Shadow Warriors! Shockwave: The road to the National Tag Team Titles has been a long and tough one. Graphic Violence, you are perhaps the toughest tag team we will face on this road. We know that you two are good wrestlers, but we also know that you two are good cheaters! Do NOT try anything with us, or you will be VERY sorry! If you pick up a chair, we'll pick up one, too. Banshee: We also have a few other surprises for everyone here in the AEWA planned for Kickoff '97. We can't tell you what it is, but you'll be stunned. Shockwave: Graphic Violence, I hope you two are prepared to lose those belts of yours! We WILL beat you at Kickoff '97! (The street light suddenly goes out, and the Assassins seem to disappear into the shadows. The camera slowly fades to black.) ---------- KW: Just because these guys got lucky as hell one night and beat the Shadow Warriors doesn't mean that they stand a chance against Graphic Violence at Kickoff '97. Booze and Leech are gonna go Old School on their sorry little butts! You can count on that one. CC: Sorry, but I see this one from COMPLETELY the opposite viewpoint. The Assassins are former World Tag Team champions, and they pose a serious threat to the National Tag Titles of Graphic Violence. If the two hoodies don't come prepared, they're gonna get beat. KW: That's funny, Chuck! (Ken begins to laugh hysterically) CC: We'll see who's laughing come this weekend. But now let's hear from the man who, until recently, held the AEWA World Light Heavyweight Title. This is Penance. ---------- (The camera opens on Penance, walking through a dark graveyard. Mist sets on the ground, and a cool breeze brushes past Penance. His long, black hair sways slightly in the wind. He wears a black leather vest, black shirt, black pants, black boots, and black gloves to top it all off. His pale-white face reveals a look of depression, and yet, a cynical attitude to it. Penance trudges through the mud and the soft ground in the graveyard; he stops at a headstone and climbs onto it and sits down. He then folds his hands and stares with his cold, black eyes into the camera.) Penance: The Holy Ghost is the champion now.... and do I care? No, I do not. Do you know why? Because I have plans for my future and that title was not part of it, nor was ICU. That's another story altogether, though. You see, after reigning as champion for so long, you get bored with the same old thing. It is time for a change and this change will be for the better, but it shall not happen yet. The presence of the reckoning day has yet to come, but soon, it will be much more than the reckoning day.... it will be the apocalypse. (Penance reaches behind the headstone with his right arm, and pulls up a symbol made of something that looks as if it might be nickel. The symbol is a circle with the lettering of "4HotA" in it. Penance grins menacingly to the camera.) Penance: What does this little symbol mean, do you ask? Well, ask and ye shall receive, and I am hoping that Mr. Terror, how fitting of a name, will ask the right questions on the next edition of his Terrordome. You see, symbolism is one of the ancient ways of communication.... cavemen used it, Egyptians used it, native Americans used it...and now in modern society, I am using it. What does it mean? You shall see, you shall see. But for now, I would like to congratulate the Holy Ghost on winning my title. It was overdue to be taken from my possession, and now you have done it. Good luck to you in the future, something tells me that you shall be needing it. (Penance hops off of the headstone, and begins walking through the graveyard again. The night's chill can be seen from the breath coming from his mouth. He approaches a small plant and then kneels down beside it. He pulls it out with his gloved hands and then sniffs it for a brief second.) Penance: Ah, deadly nightshade.... to a human of normal standards it is enough to kill one man. But at a higher concentration, it will do much more damage than you can even imagine. One must ask themselves-- would I rather die a slow death, or suffer a pestilence far worse than the black plaque? It's your call. Then again, how would it feel to be destroyed in the dark? Have you ever had the lights turned out on your soul? Have you ever felt what a pure blackout is like? No, I didn't think so, but you will, oh yes, you will. (Penance stops and rests his arm against a headstone, which happens to have the same "4HotA" symbol engraved in it.) Penance: Now, I'd really love to stay and chat, but unfortunately I have business to attend to back at the corporation. So, if you will excuse me, until next time.... Lights out. (Penance chuckles under his breath as the camera slowly fades to black.) ---------- KW: That was weird. CC: Unquestionably, but what do you think Penance's agenda is? And what does "4HotA" stand for? Personally, I haven't got a clue. KW: "4HotA", huh? How about "4 Hoes on the Avenue"? CC: What could that POSSIBLY have to do with wrestling? KW: Nothing, but it sounds like a good thing to me. CC: I think we've heard just about enough of this. Let's get a word from one of the men who will be involved in the Triangle Match for the AEWA North American Title at Kickoff '97. Here's "Big Bad" Leroy Brown. ---------- (Camera opens on Leroy Brown and Street King in a gym. They are sitting on a bench, with some people wrestling behind them) Brown: AEWA, the time has come, the time for Leroy Brown to get some gold. At Kickoff '97, in a triangle match, I am gonna take Deathwatch and Justin Trouble for the AEWA North American title. Fans, I am asking you one thing. Give me all your support. At one of the past cards, I got a mixed reaction from the crowd, because of that whole "Masked Yorel" thing. That was then, a way for me to get a feel for the AEWA. This is now, the Leroy Brown and Street King era. Fans that are already behind me, show the other fans what a great worker I am. I can't win the North American Title without you! Street King: Yes, fans, when you cheer for Leroy Brown, everyone feels better and has fun at a match, and if you can't have fun when you go to an AEWA event, what is there? Brown: That's right, and at Kickoff '97, when you are havin' fun cheering for me, you will have an even better time when I beat Deathwatch and Justin Trouble for the North American gold!! (fade to black) ---------- KW: Okay, I can see him beating Justin Trouble, but Deathwatch? It just ain't gonna happen. CC: I don't know, Leroy is a.... KW: Two time ACEW champion. I know, you tell me that EVERY time we see him-- ya think I got attention deficit syndrome or something? CC: Actually, I did. That's the first time I've ever seen you remember something. KW: Remember what? CC: Nevermind. Let's just get a word with the Pastor of the Church, Cardinal Chaos. ---------- (The camera opens on a dark alley. From somewhere in the darkness comes the voice of Cardinal Chaos.) Cardinal Chaos: President Bob Freeman. President Freeman. President Bob. (laughing) Not one of them sounds right. How do the words "President Larkin" sound, Bob? I'll tell you how they sound. (looking up to the sky) Forgive me, father, but they sound like sh*t! Hear my words, President Freeman, if you lose this match with Mister Larkin, the darkness of this alley will not be enough to hide your pitiful soul. Heaven & Hell will hound you until the good Lord sees fit to end your sorrowful life. I make this promise to you now. Should your tag team lose this match, my followers and I will leave the AEWA and make it our mission in life to prolong your agony and suffering. (The Cardinal pauses, and a small chuckle can be heard from the darkness.) Freeman, I have a proposition for you. We've had our differences in the past. Crowley and I have physically hurt you and have mentally crushed you. We've run presidents out of this federation and we've sent wrestlers to their eternal resting place. But today, all of that changes. Today I make a promise to you.... Let Heaven & Hell be your tag team, and we WILL destroy the Horde. We will save your federation, your job, your very soul. All that we ask in return is five minutes with Kevin Larkin. But hear me well, Freeman, we don't want Larkin in the ring. We want him hand-delivered to the Church. Drop him at our door and let the Lord have mercy on his soul. It's your choice, Freeman. Will it be the light of salvation? Or will you wallow away in the darkness of eternal damnation? It's your choice.... (The camera fades to black.) ---------- KW: CARDINAL CHAOS and MISTER CROWLEY want to be Freeman's tag team? But only a few months ago, they beat him senseless until he would sign them a tag team title match! CC: I guess that they just see Freeman as the lesser of two evils. They've never much liked Freeman, but they've got some BIG problems with Larkin. KW: Still, I just can't picture Heaven & Hell teaming with President Freeman, there's WAY too much bad blood there. CC: Well Freeman hasn't accepted the offer yet, but it does pose a VERY interesting possibility. That would be one hell of a match. KW: It sure would-- EVERYONE in this match hates each other! CC: It wouldn't be pretty, that's for sure, but we'll just have to wait and see if Freeman accepts. For now, though, let's watch yet another interview from a man who may be returning to the AEWA in the near future. "Jawbreaker" Jeff Jannetty. ---------- (The camera opens on "Jawbreaker" Jeff Jannetty, sitting alone in his dressing room. He smiles, and begins to speak.) Jeff Jannetty: Yo AEWA! The time has almost come; time for the return of "the Jawbreaker" Jeff Jannetty! In the past, not much impact was ever made by me, but that will change in the VERY near future! I'm coming after ALL of you. When I get through with this federation, EVERY title will be in my possession. Remember my words, AEWA. (fade to black) ---------- KW: So this guy's back for more huh? Actually, I don't even remember the last time this guy was here.... CC: That's because YOU weren't here. KW: That's besides the point, and DON'T interrupt me. Anyway, as I was saying, I'm sure that THIS time around he'll be beaten so bad that he has to change his name to "Punching Bag" Jeff Jannetty. CC: I doubt that seriously. The Jawbreaker was always a competitor to be reckoned with in his last stint, and I'm sure he'll be even better this time around. KW: In your dreams, Chuckie. CC: We'll see. And now, for our final interview, here's the other man who will be challenging Justin Trouble for the North American Title at Kickoff '97. This is the one and only Deathwatch. ---------- (The camera opens on the ICU building's main office, which also happens to be Deathwatch's penthouse. He sits in the dimly lit room, with his elbows resting on the desk in front of him. His right hand is clenched in his left, and a characteristic half-smile comes across his face. As always, his long, red hair is tied back. He wears a gray pinstripe suit and a white shirt underneath. Zack Derringer walks into the office and then takes a seat in front of his desk. He wears a black suit, white shirt underneath, and his long black hair hangs to the sides of his head. He extends his hand to Deathwatch who, in turn, shakes it and then Derringer begins to speak.) Zack Derringer: Great to see ya again, boss. I guess it's that time again, eh? Deathwatch: I suppose so, I have much to say as well. Zack Derringer: Well then, I guess we have ourselves an interview ahead of us, don't we? (grins) Now, Deathwatch, you have a triangle match coming up at the pay-per-view against Justin Trouble and some other loser-- what's his name again? Deathwatch: It doesn't matter at this point. The only person that matters to me is Justin Trouble. Zack Derringer: Then I'll get on with it and ask you this-- what do you think of Trouble? Deathwatch: I believe him to be a worthy adversary and a great competitor, but that isn't going to stop me from taking away his privilege of walking. You see, Trouble is a man who doesn't know what he wants. First he wants to set an example and be the good guy of the federation, now he wishes to be a cocky individual that doesn't care about his job protecting the so-called president, Bob Freeman. Which is it, Trouble? You come out saying you still want to protect the man, but you have yet to learn how to make up your mind. Zack Derringer: This is true. I still think Trouble is a lousy punk just trying to make a name for myself, but that's just me. Considering the facts, he has always been in this for himself. He won his NA Title and it seems as if he's lost all respect for his duties as Freeman's bodyguard, not that I can blame him. Now, this is obviously an advantage for you in the match and a disadvantage for him-- how do you plan to capitalize? Deathwatch: Simple, really. I plan to go in there and do what I do best-- break a misguided man's hopes and dreams to continue on as a champion. It all boils down to who has the most experience, which is me. Who has the most skill; me again. And who has the better side with the fans.... okay, so he outdoes me in one spot, but it's no matter. I want what's rightfully mine and I'll do anything to get it. I've been in this federation for far too long to not have ever held a title, and it's beginning to get on my nerves. Popularity is getting the better of me, and I have asked the rest of my corporation to take a step back in getting a world title shot, which they are glad to do, but do I want it yet? No, I want it at the NEXT pay-per-view and I want it guaranteed. Zack Derringer: How does the NA Title fit into this, then? If you want the World Title so badly, why not just go up against the champ, Deranged, instead of wasting your time on Trouble? Deathwatch: I have something to prove to Justin, and it's a simple fact that nobody can be perfect. Except for me. You've crossed a line, Trouble, and you can never step back over without losing an arm and a leg.... literally. Justin, when you and I step into that ring together, we'll be confronting each other with all we have, and the man that is going to win the title.... it will be me, rest assured. Zack Derringer: Speaking of titles, Penance recently lost his Light Heavyweight Title to the Holy Ghost. What do you have to say about that? Deathwatch: I do not care who holds and who doesn't hold a title within ICU. If he loses, it's his problem, not mine. ICU isn't here to take titles-- ICU is here to dominate, and we will accomplish that when it's all said and done, count on that. Just because a man loses a match, it doesn't mean the whole deck of cards has fallen out of place. No, it just means that we have slacked off and need to improve, and there is ALWAYS room for improvement. Zack Derringer: Now, on another note, what about Damian Steele? He seems to have changed remarkably from his time spent in WWEW. You offered him a place in ICU, but it's obvious that he's not gonna bite hook, line, and sinker as you had planned. In fact, it seems that he wants to get into the team that opposes ICU-- the Knights of Deliverance. Your comments? Deathwatch: Certainly. Damian, you made a mistake going to the wrong side of the tracks. You want in the Knights; we'll see how well they can trust a man such as yourself. I know I wouldn't trust you if I were them. You want to start a war with me? So be it. We shall see who the better man is, Steele, and when we do, you'll be looking just like Justin Trouble-- crippled and emotionally distraught. Your time to serve Death's Vengeance is coming sooner than you think, my friend.... we'll see how well you do against my experience. Zack Derringer: Well, that's all the time we have for now, but we'll be sure to be back with another episode of "ICU - The Takeover". (laughs) (Derringer stands up along with Deathwatch, and they shake hands once more. Derringer leaves the office.) Deathwatch: Good luck to you, Trouble. And Steele, we aren't finished. Larkin.... get back to me. (grins) (Deathwatch sits back down and then the camera fades to black.) ---------- KW: I told you. Deathwatch is gonna tear that pathetic North American Champion apart this weekend. And then we must all bow down and pay homage to the almighty Deathwatch, ruler of all he surveys! CC: Are you sure you're all right? KW: I'm just fine-- can't a man just worship a wrestler in peace? CC: Not on this show. Anyway, it's certainly gonna be quite a three way brawl, and any of the wrestlers could come out of this one with the title. KW: Nope, Deathwatch, period. CC: Whatever. And that's all the time we have for this week, fans. We'll see you next week, and don't miss AEWA Kickoff '97 live on pay-per-view this weekend! So for my broadcast partner, Ken Wilson, this is Chuck Carlson saying see ya next week for more Straight Talk!
