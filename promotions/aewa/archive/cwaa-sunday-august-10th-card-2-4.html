Sunday, August 10th, Card (2/4)

11 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EL: We're back, and it's time to see the former CWAA Heavyweight Champion, Max "the Professional" Robertson, as he tackles young Ed Farmer. Let's go to David Myers in the ring. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next bout is set for one fall and a fifteen-minute time limit. In the ring, to my left, from San Antonio, Texas, at 225 pounds, here is Ed Farmer! (Ed greets the lukewarm response with a raised hand. He wears a non-descript black singlet, with an unsightly profusion of sandy-blond chest and back hair clearly visible. He also has a rat's nest of similarly-colored hair atop his droopy head.) DM: And his opponent- (The boos begin as the Darth Vader Imperial March strikes up) DM: -from San Francisco, California, weighing in at an even 200 pounds, he is the former CWAA Heavyweight Champion, Max "the Professional" Robertson! Your referee, Chris Herman. (Max is as always completely bald and dressed in red-and-black tights. At 5'11", he matches Farmer inch for inch) EL: OK- there's the bell, and Max with a forearm uppercut on the chin! Sends him for the ride- knee to the midsection. Farmer staggers, and- look out! Max to the double chickenwing- nowhere for Farmer to go- that's it! He got him! Record time as Max Robertson cuts a swathe through Ed Farmer! DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, "The Professional" Max Robertson! (boos as Farmer rolls out to the floor and Max heads over to David Myers) DM: I hate to admit it, but that was truly a professional performance. MR: Of course it was. I don't have time to play with idiots like that. I'm still in the best shape of my career, and we will reclaim my gold. DM: 'We?' Who do you mean when you say 'we?' MR: Look. Wrestling is not a team sport. When you're in the ring it's you against your opponent. But it's no secret that Lawson and I have been friends for several months now. And the Major has been around a long time... those are straight up guys. Real professionals. DM: What about Sherrick, or should I say, Sergeant Sherrick? (Robertson looks at the camera and almost smiles) MR: I've been watching Mark Sherrick's career for some time. He's always been intense, and powerful, but something was missing. But now that the Major has taken him under his wing, I've noticed some serious improvement. He was always good, but now he's got focus. He always could win. Now he will. DM: Do you have a rank within the Major's little organization? (for a second time Max almost, but not quite, smiles) MR: No. Let's make this clear right up front. I don't do uniforms, and I don't take orders. Sherrick and Lawson work for the Major. I work with all three of them. I listen to the Major because I respect his experience, not because 'he's my superior officer' because he ain't. And he's cool with that. DM: What are your plans for the immediate future? MR: I've already proven that I'm the best. I've been the Heavyweight Champion. But that got taken away by the current CWAA Television Champion. So that's where I'm headed next. I'm going to take that belt, and I'm going to keep it. And all the other little crapola wrestlers in this league, yes I mean you Idiot, yes, I mean you Snoozer. Can line up to try and take it... but you'll fail. All of you- because that's the Professional way. That's the only way. (Robertson turns and proceeds to the locker room without so much as a good-bye to David Myers.) EL: A very confident Max Robertson in that ring, and it sounds like Jak Hammer is on his hit-list, could be a classic match, with a real difference in styles if those two should meet again. And we'll see the TV Champ later tonight as he defends his prestigious title against Joshua Barnes- the first-ever meeting of those two rising stars, I believe. And congratulations to Max Robertson and Jak Hammer, who both cracked the AEWA's Top contenders list, and I'm sure AEWA World Heavyweight Champion Crimson Joe Reed has been keeping an eye on the competition down here in the world-famous Sportatorium, and I was just informed during our last break that the World Champ is here in the arena tonight, and we hope to get a few words from him about his big upcoming match with new CWAA Champion, Marc Thomas. 2 out of 3 falls in Reunion Arena- should be one of the biggest events of the year- Sunday, August 24th, mark that one down on your calendars, folks. OK, we have a special attraction for everyone, as the ACWF Tag Team Champions, the New Sensations, are here tonight, and we'll see them in non-title action against Double Jeopardy, in just a few minutes, but first we're going to get the Heavyweight and Television Champions out here to talk about- well, everything that has been going on around here for this summer. Let's go to David Myers in the ring. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, my guests at this time, CWAA Heavyweight Champion, Marc Thomas, and CWAA Television Champion, Jak Hammer! (Tremendous booing greets the 2 champs as they head to the ring to "Life in the Fast Lane") DM: Marc, you recently won the CWAA Heavyweight Ti- MT: (grabbing the mike away from Myers) Myers, lets get to the real reason you wanted me out here! You and the rest of these idiots want to know why I became the Masked Man. Its simple. Ever since I joined the AEWA, I've been routinely dodged by the belt holders in that organization. In my six months of service, I had one- count that- one title shot. As you and the rednecks in this building know- (boos) MT: -I am one of the finest wrestlers not only in the CWAA, but also in the world and somehow, I only got one title shot. Then Freeman comes to me and tells me I need to go to the CWAA to work on my skills. He said I wasn't "championship material" and that wrestling down here would do me good. I come down here and what happens when I finally win a title... Freeman takes it away from me!! He can't stand the fact that I was able to score a victory over someone the caliber of Max Robertson. So he strips me of the title and organizes this tournament, which, amazingly, I win! (boos) MT: I bet he's having some sleepless nights now knowing that in a few weeks his Heavyweight champion is going to leave Reunion Arena a sad man. I've won 2 out of 3 fall matches before for titles and I've beaten better men in doing so. (The camera pans to Crimson Joe Reed and the Lady in Red, in a ringside seat, with very serious looks on their faces) EL: Myers, you got anymore questions? DM Well, uhh-who was under the mask when you faced Hammer the other week and lost? MT: It was me! No, it was Ron Stern, my old tag partner from the CCWF. We've been helping each other out over the last few weeks and he agreed to help me in my quest to win the CWAA Heavyweight Title. We've talked about reforming the Icemen, but right now he has some other issues to take care of before we can do that. However, he did say he has worked with a wrestler that would be interested in joining the CWAA, but for right now, Hammer and I have decided to continue to wrestle together as a tag team. In fact after the supercard at Reunion Arena, Jak and I challenge the tag team champs to a match for the CWAA Tag Team belts. I've said enough, Jak has a match tonight against that brain surgeon Joshua Barnes, (pop) EL: -ask him some questions. (Before Myers can say a word, Hammer grabs the mike.) Jak Hammer: Barnes, your might is unable to compensate for your lack of brain power. (boos) JH: Unfortunately for you, I've got both power and smarts. I have worked long and hard to win this CWAA TV Title and I refuse to let a moron like you beat me for the belt. And don't think that Sheridan is going to be of any assistance, because Marc will be at ringside to watch my back just like Ill be at ringside during his matches with Joe Reed. Prepare to get HAMMERED!!!! (Thomas and Hammer leave the ring to boos.) DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is set for one fall with a twenty-minute time limit. Introducing first- ("Sweet Home, Alabama" plays and the boos start as Eddie Intellecta leads the Hotshot, Gary Jackson, to the ring. Eddie has his "AEWA Rocks" t-shirt, yellow trunks, and blue mask on, and Jackson is still a slob. Both weigh 250, and Intellecta stands 6'7"- more than 1/2 a foot taller than the ex-mechanic, who is missing his trademarked ankle brace this week.) DM: -at a combined weight of 500 pounds, from Houston, Texas, here are Eddie Intellecta and "Hotshot" Gary Jackson- the team of Double Jeopardy! (Gary Jackson and Eddie Intellecta walk down to the ringside table, with especially dirty looks on their faces. Eddie snatches the TV microphone away from Eric Lane, and begins to speak while Myers waits in the ring.) Intellecta: First of all, I want to make a statement to the New Sensations. Those gentlemen might be the tag team champions over in that ACWF promotion, but their belts don't mean ANYTHING around here. After tonight's match, Gary and I will finally prove that we are the best team in the world today. EL: Eddie, before you go any further, I must remind you that the New Sensations are more than just a popular INXS song. They're also one of the most feared-- Intellecta: (interrupting him) Eric, you have failed to phrase your answer in the form of a question, and that's going to cost you. Allow me to continue, you pretentious moron. I might not be the biggest fan of Texas, but this _is_ my home, so I don't plan on losing to those Ñ "invaders." When the bell sounds tonight, and the smoke clears, there will be one team left standing.... Double Jeopardy. (Gary Jackson grabs the mike from his partner.) Jackson: I hate to interrupt ya, buddy, but I've got a few things to say about these Sensational fellas. I don't give a damn what they might have accomplished in that CANADIAN league, Ñcause it don't mean <Expletive deleted> over here. We've been on a losing streak for a long time, but that's going to change tonight. (motions toward the ring) Let's go get our business done, buddy. EL: Double Jeopardy expecting to give the popular newcomers a real Texas welcome, it sounds. DM: And their opponents- (Pop as INXS' "New Sensation" plays) DM: -at 515 pounds, they are the ACWF Tag Team Champions, James Thompson, Alex Peters- the New Sensations! Your referee, Jeff Howard. (Thompson and Peters are similarly built at 5'11"/6'0" and 250-260 pounds, and dressed in red and white trunks) EL: And we're underway- James Thompson and Eddie Intellecta to kick things off. Single led takedown by Thompson- and Intellecta with a double leg cradle- 1... kickout. Both men up now and a lock up- Intellecta into a front facelock. Thompson with an elbow jab to the midsection- and another- and that breaks the hold,. A powerful youngster, that Thompson. Could go far. Intellecta with a double sledge now, and Thompson tags in young Alex Peters. Peters to a standing wristlock. Turns it again- and now a standing dropkick! Very impressive, and Intellecta talks it over with Gary Jackson. All right, both men in the middle of the ring- and I understand we will be talking to the World Heavyweight champion as soon as this one is over. (The camera pans the crowd, where we find the Lady in Red and Crimson Joe Reed in a ringside seat) EL: And there they are, taking in this tag team action, perhaps scouting future contenders or just enjoying a night out here in the world-famous Sportatorium. A test of strength in the ring now, Intellecta and Peters- and Alex Peters with a double kneelift. And Intellecta back to his corner, conferring with his partner. Big main event tonight, as Fuse and the Preacher and a mystery-guest- perhaps that is why Reed's here tonight- take on Sherrick and Lawson, and- get this, folks- Major Mayhem! In a steel cage! I know a lot of people will have their eyes glued to the set for that one. Another test of strength. And Intellecta with a kick- and Peters caught him! He has Eddie on the mat- and rolls him- stretching those hamstring. Tags in Thompson- double whip- Peters ducks- Intellecta hurdles- and Thompson with a picture-perfect dropkick! (pop) EL: Textbook tag wrestling! And Intellecta falls into his corner and tags the Hotshot! Gary Jackson charges- right into a Thompson forearm blow. Now a snap mare takedown. Off the ropes- elbow drop- 1... 2.... no- couldn't hold him down. Thompson tags Alex Peters. Now Thompson with a bearhug on Jackson- Peters to the top- flying clothesline into a modified spinebuster! Thompson with the cover- Peters plasters Intellecta with a dropkick as he tries to break it up- that'sÊit! Just like that, it's over! DM: Ladies and gentlemen, here are your winners, the ACWF Tag Team Champions, the New Sensations! (Intellecta leads a shamefaced Jackson away from the ring as the crowd pops, and Myers heads toward the ACWF champs) Alex Peters: I just want to say to everyone here in Dallas, thanks for the warm welcome. (pop) James Thompson: That's right, and we're here for one reason and one reason- to do what you just saw to a certain Lt. "Lethal" Lawson and Mr. Mark Sherrick! (pop) AP: You know it- and we want them in the ring so badly that we're willing to put these, our ACWF Tag Team championship belts on the line, right here next week, in front of you great Texas fans, against those two Beetle Bailey rejects! Fuse, Preacher, save a little for us! (pop as "New Sensation" plays and the ACWF champs exit) EL: OK- as soon as we get back from this message, we'll talk with the man himself- the World Heavyweight champion, Crimson Joe Reed! (Your favorite stars in your favorite films- the WYXZ-TV Channel 39 Movie of the Week- each and every Wednesday night, and Thursdays at midnight. This week, is a serial bomber plotting to destroy America's amusement parks? Henry Fonda and George Segal star in "Roller Coaster"- only on WYXZ-TV Channel 39) EL: Well, we all saw the World Heavyweight Champion here at ringside, I'm going to get his views on his upcoming match with CWAA Heavyweight Champion, Marc Thomas. "Crimson" Joe, Ms. Red, welcome to the CWAA. CJR: We're honored to be here. We've been walking around town for the last few hours, and I'd forgotten how great the Southern Hospitality is in Texas. {His Kentucky accent is a little bit thicker than normal today. The partisan crowd in his area of the audience cheers in response.} CJR: Now, I tend to claim that the AEWA Title is the greatest title in the sport, and that it should be defended against the finest competition in wrestling. So I jumped at the chance to defend the belt against the current CWAA champion. There are some great wrestlers here; Robertson, Hammer, Sheridan, and many others. I even asked my manager here to personally scout out my opponent. EL: What did you discover when you watched the match between Max Robertson and the Mystery Wrestler who was Marc Thomas, Ms. Red? LiR: Marc, you lost a huge advantage when your "Mystery Wrestler" was exposed. Instead of defending against a complete unknown, Joe now has a full scouting report on you. CJR: Marc, I'll give you credit. It took me five years to make the jump from a tag team wrestler to a single's champion. It didn't even take you one year. But I've had seven more years since then to improve my game. LiR: You are a very good wrestler. But Joe's better. And he'll beat you in your match tonight. Two out of three falls in a ninety-minute match is a tough call. But Joe wouldn't have the belt if he couldn't handle the tough ones. EL: Well, Mr. Reed, ms. Red, thank you very much for your time, and best of luck at the supershow in Reunion Arena. CJR: Thank you, Eric. (Lane heads back to his table) EL: All right, we are just about ready for our Television Title matchup, as Jak Hammer takes on Joshua Barnes. Let's go to ring announcer Dave Myers. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next bout is set for one fall with a twenty-minute time limit, and is for the CWAA Television Championship. Introducing first, the challenger- ("Tom Sawyer" by Rush plays, and Joshua Barnes heads to the ring to a frenetic pop) DM: -from Brownsburg, Indiana, weighing in at 292 pounds, Joshua Barnes! And his opponent- ("Bad to the Bone" plays and the crowd's mood turns dark) DM: -from Detroit, Michigan, weighing in at 315 pounds, accompanied buy his printer, CWAA Heavyweight Champion, Marc Thomas, here is the CWAA Television Champion, Jak Hammer! Your referee, Chris Herman. EL: OK- Joshua Barnes and Jak Hammer, one-on-one on the ring, and the gold is on the line! Barnes has a warning for Thomas, at ringside, and Jak Hammer takes advantage of the opportunity and nails him from behind with a double axe-handle! We're officially underway- as soon as this one is done, we'll have that big six-man main event in the steel cage- with Major Mayhem donning the tights! Hammer pounding the challenger with forearm blows, now he smashes Barnes' face into the top turnbuckle. Choking him with the tag rope- 3... 4... and he breaks the hold just in time to avoid a disqualification- Jeff Howard not one to take any guff from any of the CWAA's stars, no matter who they are. Jak now kicks Barnes in the ribs and tosses him to the floor. (Of course, as Hammer gives Howard the guff he presumably does not take, Thomas rams Barnes' forehead onto the ring apron) EL: Marc Thomas working him over on the outside- now he rolls the challenger under the bottom rope. Jak Hammer picks Barnes up and sends him for the ride- clothesline! And Joshua Barnes in real trouble in the early going. Barnes up on his knees now- a fist right in Hammer's midsection! And Jak Hammer with a rake of the eyes, right in front of Senior Official Jeff Howard. (Camera cuts to a shot of Marc Thomas at ringside in front of Joe Reed and the Lady in Red) EL: Jak Hammer with the scoop slam- covers- 1... 2... kickout! (small pop) EL: Jak Hammer to an arm bar now, neutralizing Barnes' tremendous upper-arm strength. Thomas tempting fate with his showboating in front of the master of the Kentucky Backsmasher. You know, when the CWAA goes to Reunion Arena, Joshua Barnes will be there in action, as he teams up with the Dreamer, Randy Sheradin, to take on the twosome of Lt. Lawson and Mark Sherrick. Think about the power of Barnes and Sherrick and the mat-grappling of Sheridan and Lawson- should be a good one, and I know that the press will be hear, making sure they cover all of the action in that two-day all-star lineup. Jak Hammer, in the meantime, takes on the Preacher, Kore Wrench- two of the most powerful men in Texas set for a head-on collision- action you won't find anyplace else! OK- Jak Hammer with that arm bar, Barnes on the mat, and Thomas heaping abuse on the kid from Brownsburg. (Reed continues to eye Thomas menacingly) EL: OK- Thomas turning to the AEWA's champion now, telling him "Two more weeks!" Joshua Barnes up on his knees now, as Hammer still has that arm immobilized. Barnes now grabs the calf- single leg takedown- and Barnes to a headlock on Hammer down on the mat! (pop for the reversal) EL: Joshua Barnes now grinding Hammer's head beneath that enormous bicep. And look at this! Marc Thomas pushing the bottom rope toward Jak Hammer so he can reach it! And Joe Reed is stepping out of the audience! (pop, and a chant of "Joe! Joe! Joe!") EL: Jeff Howard breaks the hold- and Reed taps Thomas on the shoulder! Look at the fear in Thomas' eyes as he stands face to face with the legend, the hall of famer, the World Heavyweight Champion of both the AEWA and the UWF! Reed jabbing a finger in Thomas's chest, and Thomas is backed against the ring! In the ring, Joshua Barnes and Jak Hammer in a test of strength, and here comes security! Barnes with the power advantage, driving Jak Hammer down to the mat- Hammer straining to resist. And it looks like security is ordering Crimson Joe Reed to leave! (They are. See, he's a paying member of the audience, and therefore is not supposed to cross that single piece of string separating the first row from the ringside area.) EL: Jak Hammer with a kick in the midsection, and the air has just been slammed from Barnes' lungs. And Jak Hammer with an elbowsmash to the head. Snap suplex takes him over- and Crimson Joe Reed is being taken from the arena! Security is ejecting the World Heavyweight Champion, and Marc Thomas can breathe easily. In the meantime, Jak Hammer has Joshua Barnes in a full nelson. (to be continued)
