Saturday Night Slam (3/8, part two)

11 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * CONTINUED FROM PART ONE * * * * BD: And it's going to be a hell of a fight! Randall Osbourne was actually stupid enough to challenge both Shane Matthews AND Sir Gallade in a handicap match. What a moron! LB: Yeah, the guy can't beat either man, and he's challenging them both! This is ridiculous. MW: Osbourne has plenty of reason to be mad at both of these men. His feud with Matthews goes back a long ways, and Gallade betrayed the Knights of Deliverance-- and cost Osbourne his last match with Matthews at Kickoff '97. Let's get to the ring! RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is a special handicap match! Introducing first, weighing in at 362 pounds, Raaaaandaaalllll "Rebel of Rock" Oooooooooosssbooooooooooourrrrrne! (AC/DC's "It's a Long Way to the Top" begins playing loudly over the PA, and Osbourne makes his way to the ring. The crowd goes absolutely NUTS, but Osbourne seems too determined to notice.) MW: Osbourne's got murder in his eyes. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponents.... First, from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, he weighs in at 222 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his manager, Johnny Terror, "Suuuuuudddden Death" Shaaaaaaaaaaane Maaaaaaaaaaatttttheeeeeeeews! And next, from Hackney, England, he weighs in at 251 pounds and is the reigning AEWA Television Champion, Sir Gaaaaaaaaaaalllllaaaaaaade! (Anthrax's "Looking Down the Barrel of a Gun" blares out over the PA as Matthews and Gallade make their way to the ring, to a BIG round of boos. Someone in the audience tries to throw a soda can at Johnny Terror, but he simply picks it up and throws it back.) MW: Matthews and Gallade aren't entering the ring yet.... they're circling around, getting on opposite sides of the ring. BD: Good move. Osbourne can't go after both men at once. MW: Gallade hops up the ring apron-- Osbourne charges after him, and Gallade drops back down again! LB: And while Osbourne's occupied with Gallade, Shane Matthews charges into the ring and clotheslines Osbourne! MW: ....but Osbourne doesn't even move! Shane rears back, and hits Randall with a right fist-- again, Osbourne doesn't flinch! Shane tries again, and it's BLOCKED by Osbourne! Osbourne is hitting Matthews with a series of punches. He picks Matthews up.... A THUNDEROUS POWERSLAM TO THE MAT! BD: But Sir Gallade comes off the top ropes, and nails Osbourne with an elbow to his skull! MW: Gallade's kicking away Osbourne, and giving Matthews a chance to recover. Now they both pick up the big man, and drop him with a double vertical suplex. Finally the referee gets Matthews out of the ring and into his corner. BD: Yeah, but that won't last long. Gallade whips Osbourne right into Matthews' corner, and starts arguing with the referee! LB: And with the referee occupied with Gallade, Matthews is choking Randall Osbourne with the tag rope! What was Osbourne thinking when he agreed to this match? BD: He wasn't thinking at all. MW: That's enough, you two. Matthews is holding Osbourne in the corner as Gallade charges, and hits a spinning elbow onto Osbourne! They're showing very good teamwork, and are taking advantage of Osbourne's handicap. Gallade tags in Matthews, and they whip Osbourne into the ropes.... a double-dropkick by Matthews and Gallade! BD: Osbourne's getting slaughtered! Here's the cover by Gallade. One.. Two.. Kickout! MW: Not yet. They whip Osbourne into the ropes again. They're setting him up for a double clothesline... Osbourne ducks. He bounces off the opposite ropes, and hits both of them with a clothesline! He lifts Matthews up in a gorilla press.... and throw him over the top rope! LB: Disqualify him, ref! MW: The referee's being lenient, as he's allowing the match to continue. Gallade's in serious trouble now, as he's alone in the ring with a furious Osbourne.... Osbourne picks up Gallade, and piledrives him to the mat. Johnny Terror gets up on the apron, but Osbourne shoves him off the apron onto the guardrail! LB: Terror is down, and looks out of it. MW: Gallade gets up, but Osbourne greets Gallade with a fist to the face. AND ANOTHER! Gallade is on his knees, pleading for his life.... BD: But a thumb to the eyes by Gallade blinds Osbourne! Osbourne stumbles around, and look at the grin on Sir Gallade's face. MW: Gallade has certainly turned into a sadist since his "conversion" to the Church. He bounces off the ropes, and bulldogs Osbourne. Matthews enters the ring, and he and Gallade are having a quick conference. Matthews climbs up the turnbuckle, while Gallade picks up Osbourne.... A SPIKED PILEDRIVER! LB: They got him! Here's the cover! One.. Two.. Kickout! (The crowd starts chanting "Rebel of Rock! Rebel of Rock!") BD: Can somebody get these morons to be quiet? MW: You're welcome to give it a try! BD: No problem! (picks up the audience microphone) Will everyone just shut up and let these two great wrestlers beat up on this idiot! (shuts off the microphone) There, that should do it. (Naturally, the crowd is chanting even louder-- except that their chant is now "Big Daddy Sucks!") MW: Nice job, Big Daddy. Meanwhile, Osbourne is in serious trouble as both men pick him up for a double belly-to-back suplex. He goes SMASHING to the canvas! Gallade exits the ring as Shane Matthews picks up Osbourne's legs, and puts on a Scorpion Deathlock. LB: The match is over. Osbourne's going to give up! MW: He's still fighting the hold, but he's near the center of the ring. (The crowd goes back to chanting for Osbourne). The referee's asking him if he wants to give up, but I can hear him scream "NO!" from here. He's struggling.... trying to move to the ropes. He got them! BD: But Gallade's entered the ring, and the referee's too busy getting him out to check on Osbourne. Shane Matthews is pulling him back to the center of the ring. LB: He's been in the Scorpion Deathlock for almost a minute. MW: The referee's checking on him. I think Osbourne may be unconscious! No, I can see Osbourne still shaking his head. But how much longer can he last? BD: Look, he's trying to power out again.... WILL THESE MORONS STOP SHOUTING?! I'M GETTING A HEADACHE! MW: Sorry, Big Daddy.... maybe you should leave and lie down for a while. The crowd keeps trying to help out Osbourne, and he seems to be drawing strength. He's pushing up on his hands, lessening the pressure to his back.... He flipped Matthews over! He just powered out of that Scorpion Deathlock! AMAZING! LB: For all the good it did him. Sir Gallade's entered the ring, and whips Randall Osbourne to the ropes. MW: Osbourne reverses it, and clotheslines Gallade! Now he charges and clotheslines Matthews, knocking Matthews out of the ring! BD: Why doesn't the referee disqualify Osbourne? Look at all the closed fist punches he keeps hitting Gallade with. MW: Now he doubles over Gallade, picks him up, POWERBOMB! A cover by Osbourne.... One.. Two.. Matthews breaks the count! BD: Osbourne grabs Matthews' throat, and chokeslams the former World Champion to the ground. He picks him up again, and tosses him out of the ring! LB: It looks like Osbourne is concentrating on Gallade, which actually makes sense. Work on just one of your two opponents. Does this mean that Osbourne actually has a brain inside his head? MW: Osbourne picks Gallade up over his shoulder, and performs an airplane spin! Now he Samoan Drops him to the mat, with PLENTY of force! He picks him up, and puts Gallade in an over-the-shoulder backbreaker! Listen to Gallade scream! BD: Shane Matthews comes back in, and kicks Osbourne, causing Osbourne to drop Gallade. MW: Osbourne again throws Matthews out of the ring, and goes back to attacking Gallade. He picks him up for a suplex, and crashes him to the mat. Here's another cover.... LB: But the referee is occupied by Matthews, who is back in the ring. This is great! MW: The referee is trying to get Matthews out of the ring, and Osbourne goes over to help. I guess that right fist will help send a message to Matthews. LB: But he's giving the TV champ time to recover. Osbourne goes back to Gallade.... MW: ....who is getting something from his tights! Gallade hits Osbourne in the head! His fist was loaded-- LOOK AT THE BRASS KNUCKLES IN HIS RIGHT HAND! BD: I didn't see anything-- Bertha, did you? LB: Nope. Not a thing. MW: How could you miss it? (shakes his head) I give up on you two. Here comes the cover.... One.. Two.. Three! (DING, DING) BD: I told you Osbourne didn't have a prayer against Matthews and Gallade. MW: They had him in a two-on-one match, and still needed a foreign object to win. Let's go to the official verdict. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here are your winners, "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews and Siiiirrrrr Gaaaalllllllaaaaaade!!!! (The crowd boos like crazy) LB: And they're not finished yet! They're putting Randall Osbourne on the top turnbuckle, and Matthews is signaling for a superplex! MW: But here come the rest of the Knights of Deliverance! Slocum, Gryphon, the Assassins, and the others are coming in to save their friend. BD: Too bad-- it might have been the end of Randall Osbourne. MW: Gallade, Matthews, and Terror flee the ring, and walk out with a cheap victory. The Knights check up on Osbourne, who looks like he'll be all right. A tough match for the Rebel of Rock, who is still looking for revenge against Matthews and Gallade. LB: Yeah, I might not like the guy, but I have to admit-- he put up a good fight tonight. There's only one problem.... HE STILL LOST! MW: (rolls his eyes) You're so thoughtful, Bertha. Anyway, it's now time for our next match, as-- (Suddenly boos can be heard from the audience, and that means only one thing.... Zack Derringer comes down the aisle dressed in a nice black suit, and he mouths off to the fans as he goes. He reaches the announcer's booth and takes a seat next to Wilson.) ZD: Hey guys, how's it goin'? (snickers) BD: It WAS going fine until you showed up. ZD: I love a warm welcome, nice to see you too, Big Dorky. LB: Oh no, not again. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, the challenger, from Los Angeles, California, and weighing in at 265 pounds, Damian "The Preeeeeedator" Steeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeleeeee! (Tool's "Swamp Song" plays loudly over the PA as Steele makes his entrance to a chorus of boos from the crowd. He is wearing a black vest with a red skull on the front and "The Predator" written on the back of it. He comes to the ring with a cocky smirk on his face.) MW: Steele looks.... well, confident, to say the least. ZD: Steele is ALWAYS confident, because no one is better than him and he knows it. BD: I'll admit that Damian has a lot of skill, and I'm also glad that he isn't one of those babyfaces that seem to be all too common around here anymore. LB: Yeah, but unfortunately he picked a stupid stable to join. ZD: Hey, he didn't pick it.... Deathwatch offered him a job with ICU and he took it. It's not like he was being begged by any other stables. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 310 pounds, from parts unknown, accompanied to the ring by his manager, Laura, here is THE GRYPHON! (The Gryphon and Laura make their way toward the ring, without his black cape. He receives a BIG pop from the crowd, but stares directly at Steele. He grabs an "ICU" sign from a fan at ringside, and rips it to shreds, to a HUGE pop from the rest of the fans.) ZD: Look at this loser, he thinks he's cool cause he can rip up an ICU sign? We'll see how special he feels once ICU rips HIM in half. And what is this? Does he think that if he brings his girlfriend, or whatever that dog is at ringside, with him that he'll have a better chance of winning? LB: Maybe he does, Zack. I know one thing for sure.... you talk too much. MW: In any case, this should be a great matchup here. (DING, DING) MW: Steele is measuring Gryphon up. I don't think he wants to rush into this, seeing as how Gryphon is around seven feet tall. Actually, I don't think ANYONE would want to get too close, too soon. ZD: Yeah, Steele knows how to handle the bigger guys, that's for sure. BD: Steele makes the first move.... lock up, NO! Steele takes out Gryphon's leg and is POUNDING away on Gryphon! Steele bounces off the ropes, elbowdrop-- MISSED! MW: Now the Gryphon picks Steele up, and he SLAMS him into the corner! Gryphon runs in for an avalanche.... NOBODY HOME! Steele rolls Gryphon up for the count.... One.. Kickout! LB: Steele was only able to get a one count, and both men are right back on their feet! ZD: Ouch! Nice dropkick by Steele there, and it looks like Gryphon isn't gonna be winning anything here tonight. BD: You never know, Zack. Your ICU boy could lose any time. ZD: You wish. BD: We'll see, Zack. Steele grabs Gryphon by the hair, but Gryphon RAMS his shoulder into Steele's stomach! The Gryphon picks him up.... POWERSLAM!! Gryphon wraps Steele up for the cover-- One.. Two.. Thr.. Kickout! MW: Steele was able to kick out at the last second, that was close! The Predator is back up on his feet and Gryphon scoops him up.... and slams him down! Gryphon bounces off the ropes, KNEEDROP! He hits it! The Gryphon brings Steele up again and he's setting him up for a piledriver, he picks him up-- NO! Blocked by Steele! He goes for it again.... NO! Steele able to block it, and Gryphon tries again.... BACKDROP BY STEELE!! BD: Smart thinking by Steele, folks. If Gryphon had hit that move, it might be over. LB: You're probably right. ZD: You gotta be kiddin' me. Steele wouldn't lose via piledriver, there's no way in hell. BD: Never know. Steele picks Gryphon up, and he CLOTHESLINES him over the top rope and to the floor! Steele follows him out and he grabs him by the arm.... and whips him right into the steel ringpost! Steele charges in-- Gryphon moves, but Steele stops as well. MW: Steele shakes his head at the Gryphon, and the Gryphon charges at him with a clothesline-- ducked by Steele! And now Steele slams Gryphon's head into the ring apron, and rolls him back into the ring! Steele is climbing back into the ring now, and Gryphon is back on his feet. LB: Steele runs right into a BIG boot by Gryphon, and here's the cover.... One.. Two.. Shoulder Up! ZD: Gryphon isn't going to win, so he should just stop trying right now. It's just a waste of time for him to keep putting up any kind of fight. BD: Like I said, we'll see about that. MW: The Gryphon picks Steele up again and.... CHOKESLAM!! He goes for the cover AGAIN! One.. Two.. Kickout! BD: Uh oh, here comes Exodus to ringside! Man, have these two started heating up lately! ZD: You've got that right, and Exodus sure as hell ain't coming out for a handshake! (laughs) MW: Exodus has a chair now, and Laura has climbed up onto the ring apron. She's trying to get the referee's attention, but Damian Steele is currently distracting him. Exodus comes in behind the ref's back.... He comes up to the Gryphon and takes a swing.... LB: OH MY GOD!! BD: Gryphon just ducked out of the way, and Exodus NAILED Laura! She's on the ground now and I think she's bleeding! Both Exodus and Gryphon are in shock-- I don't think Exodus meant to do that, nor did he want to. ZD: That was.... that was.... hell, that was cool! LB: You're sick, Derringer. ZD: Yeah? So? MW: Exodus rolls out of the ring, and he's walking away from it. Gryphon is yelling at Exodus from the ring, and Steele rolls him up from behind!! One.. Two.. Three!! (DING, DING) ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner, as a result of a pinfall, Damian "The Preeeeeedator" Steeeeeeeeeeeeeeeellllleeeee! (Tool's "Swamp Song" begins to play as Steele leaves ringside, with a grin of victory on his face.) MW: The Gryphon is really upset, and he's tending to Laura, who has a bloody nose and possibly a VERY sore face. Medics have come out and attend to Laura now, and Gryphon is going nuts! He runs back to the locker rooms, apparently to find Exodus, but I wouldn't be surprised if he just took off altogether. BD: That was a sick thing even for Exodus to do, but I doubt it was on purpose. ZD: Well, as always.... I'd love to stick around, but I gotta fly. Catch ya later, losers. LB: Thank god. (Zack stands up from the booth, and walks away.) MW: I'm glad he's gone-- that guy gets more annoying every time I see him. Why the hell does he come out here, anyway? BD: To piss us off. MW: That's what I thought. Anyway, it's now time for our main event! We have the newly crowned AEWA World Heavyweight champion, "Crimson" Joe Reed, preparing to defend his belt against Primal Fear. On a different note, I'd just like to congratulate Joe Reed on his recent victory over Mark Durman in a brutal steel cage match in the UWF. I was sitting in the audience for that bout, and it was INCREDIBLE. BD: Well, Wilson, forget about his past matches, because tonight he's gonna have to survive a bout against Primal Fear. MW: True enough. Let's get to it, folks! RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall, and is for the AEWA World Heavyweight championship! Introducing first, the challenger, weighing in at 360 pounds, he is the AEWA's most extreme wrestler, here is PRIMAL FEAR! (Primal Fear walks down the aisle, getting a HUGE pop from the crowd. He's wearing his black vest and brown tights, and he has a rather crazed expression on his face. He rolls into the ring to await his opponent.) RING ANNOUNCER: His opponent, from Lexington, Kentucky, he weighs in at 257 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his manager, the Lady in Red, he is the AEWA Heavyweight champion of the world, "Crrrrrrrrrrrrrriiiiiiiiiiiimson" Joe Reeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeed! (Bad Company's "Bad Company" plays loudly over the PA as Reed makes his way down to the ring, the Lady in Red by his side. The crowd pop BIG TIME. Reed slides into the ring, and hands the belt over to the referee.) MW: The bell has sounded -- oh my, Primal Fear is going right to work on Reed! Fear is laying into Reed with rights and lefts, and Reed is stunned! LB: Whoa, Primal Fear is sure ready to go tonight! He's pounding the CRAP out of Reed, and now Reed is slumping against the ropes! The referee is trying to make Primal Fear back off, but he won't quit! MW: The referee finally gets Primal Fear off of Reed, and Reed is just standing there, trying to recover from that onslaught by Primal Fear. Now Fear is charging Reed again -- but Reed moves, and now Reed is laying into Primal Fear! Reed with a series of rights and lefts! He whips Fear into the ropes -- huge clothesline, but Fear doesn't go down! BD: Reed runs into the far side ropes and nails Fear with another clothesline, but the big man still won't go down! Now Reed runs into the ropes, he's going for another clothesline -- but Fear ducks, and nails Reed with a HUGE lariat! Reed is down on the canvas! Uh oh, where's Fear going? LB: Primal Fear is signaling for the Bump Splash! If he hits this, it's all over! Fear bounces off the ropes, BUMP SPLASH -- but no, Reed rolls out of the way, and Primal Fear lands right on his butt! Boy, Reed sure got lucky there. MW: I'm surprised he had the presence of mind to get out of the way after that huge lariat, but now Reed's to his feet. He comes off the ropes-- and drops an elbow on Primal Fear! Off the ropes again -- Big leg drop! Here's the cover! One.. Kickout! BD: There's no way you'll pin Primal Fear with that. Get a clue, Reed! MW: Reed picks Primal Fear up, whips him into the ropes -- he's going for a powerslam, but he can't get Primal Fear over, and he falls down with Fear on top of him! One.. Two.. Kickout! LB: Reed made a mistake there, and it nearly cost him the World Championship. What was he thinking, trying to powerslam a 360 pound man? Now Primal Fear is on his feet, he whips Reed into the ropes -- and nails him with a powerslam of his own.... but he doesn't go for the cover! MW: I don't like that look on his face one bit. Primal Fear picks Reed up-- and tosses him outside the ring! Fear rolls outside the ring, picks Reed up, and whips him into the ringpost! And now Primal Fear has a chair! BD: This isn't looking good for Reed! Fear has the chair up, he charges at Reed -- but Reed moves, and Primal Fear CRASHES into the ringpost with the chair, and the chair hits him right in the forehead! Primal Fear is busted wide open! LB: I thought "Crimson" was REED's nickname, not Primal Fear's! MW: "Crimson" may not be Fear's nickname, but it certainly describes him at the moment. Reed rolls Primal Fear back in, who looks REALLY dazed after getting that chair plowed into his forehead. BD: Duh. LB: Yeah, no kidding, Wilson. Reed stands Primal Fear up, and floors him with a right hand! Primal Fear goes down hard! Now Reed flips Fear over, and puts him in a Boston Crab! Primal Fear is trying desperately to reach the ropes! MW: If he doesn't get there soon, this one's over -- ah, there he goes. Fear hooks the ropes with one arm, and the hold is broken. Now Reed is just stomping Primal Fear's back over and over! Reed drags him out to the center of the ring, and now he's going up top! BD: What's he doing? Reed jumps off the top -- yow! He just NAILED Primal Fear with a flying kneedrop right in the BACK! Now Reed is signaling for the Kentucky backsmasher! There's no WAY he can get Primal Fear up! MW: He lifts Fear to his feet.... he's having trouble getting him up! OH MY GOD! JOE REED LIFTS PRIMAL FEAR UP TO THE VERTICAL SUPLEX POSITION -- AND NAILS THE KENTUCKY BACKSMASHER! This match is OVER! Here's the count! One.. Two.. Three!! (DING, DING) LB: I can't believe he got him up! I don't like Reed, but I have to admit, that was a great move! BD: You said it, Bertha! I didn't think there was ANY way Reed would be able to pick up the 360 pounder! RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner, and STILL AEWA World Heavyweight champion, "Crimson" Joe Reed!! MW: That was a great match, and I still can't believe Reed was able to execute the Kentucky Backsmasher. He's an amazing wrestler, that's for sure. BD: Primal Fear doesn't look very happy, but he just got back to his feet and walked up to Reed.... and they shake hands! MW: This is a great show of sportsmanship, folks, which is something we don't see much of anymore. LB: I think it's disgusting. MW: That doesn't surprise me, Bertha. Anyway, we're completely out of time, so for "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt and the Lovely Bertha, this is Matt Wilson saying so long everybody!
