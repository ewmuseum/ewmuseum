Kickoff '97 (2/23, part eight)

26 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * CONTINUED FROM PART SEVEN * * * * MW: Well the next matchup is a VERY big match indeed. In fact, the Presidency of the AEWA hangs in the balance! Vice-President Kevin Larkin and his Visigothic Horde will be taking on President Bob Freeman and his still-unnamed team. The winner takes over complete control of the AEWA, and the loser is gone forever! BD: I don't think Freeman even HAS a team. I don't think there are any teams that would have him. MW: That's just not true. Mr. Freeman received SEVERAL offers from wrestlers to be his team, he just wants to maintain the element of surprise. LB: Well I'll be surprised with just about anything he brings out here, because I don't think any decent wrestler would agree to team with him. MW: Well, we're just about to find out exactly who his team is, so let's go to the ring and find out! RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is a special six-man tag match and is scheduled for one fall! The captain of the winning team will take COMPLETE control of the AEWA, and the captain of the losing team will be barred from the AEWA FOREVER!! Introducing first, at a total combined weight of 650 pounds, accompanied to the ring by their manager, Alaric, here are Fritigern and Athanaric, The Visigoooooothiiiiic Hooooooooooooooorde! Their tag team partner, and team captain is AEWA Vice-President Keeeeeeviiiiiiin Laaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaarkiiiiiiiin! (The boos begin to rain down as the Visigoths and Larkin make their way to the ring, accompanied to the ring by Alaric. The Visigoths wear their normal wrestling attire, and Larkin is dressed in a pair of EXTREMELY dorky, pink tights.) BD: The Visigoths aren't gonna let Larkin down. In just a little while, he'll be running this federation. MW: We'll just see about that. You don't even know who Freeman's team is. BD: I don't need to know. The Horde will kill them, no matter WHO it is! MW: We'll see. RING ANNOUNCER: And their opponents, First, the team captain, AEWAPresident Bob Freeman! (President Freeman emerges from the locker room wearing an even MORE dorky pair of yellow tights. He is alone, and gets a good sized pop from the crowd.) LB: I knew it! He doesn't have a team! MW: You don't know that! They're not out here yet! BD: And they're not coming, either, because they don't exist! HA HA HA HA! (President Freeman steps into the ring, and asks for the microphone.) FREEMAN: Now I've thought long and hard about this decision, and I DO have a team to come out and wrestle with me. I'm going to introduce them to you now. Now this team and I have had our differences in the past, but I couldn't ask for a more accomplished pair of guys to help me defend my right to run the AEWA. Together, they have held a combined FIVE AEWA World Heavyweight Titles, now here are Mister Crowley, and Cardinal Chaos, THE TEAM OF HEAVEN AND HELL!!!! (The Rolling Stones' "Sympathy for the Devil" begins to play loudly over the PA as the two former World Champions make their way toward the ring, to a mixture of cheers and shocked gasps from the crowd.) MW: Well would you look at that! LB: I'm looking at it, and I STILL don't believe it. These two guys almost KILLED President Freeman only a few months ago, and now he's gonna TEAM with them? MW: Stranger things have happened. LB: Not many. MW: Heaven and Hell hit the ring, AND THE RING EXPLODES INTO A WILD SIX-WAY BRAWL! The Horde and Heaven & Hell are tearing into each other, and Larkin just hit Freeman with the ring bell!! BD: That'll ring your bell, hey Wilson? MW: That it will, Big Daddy. And now Larkin is getting out of the ring, leaving his Horde to do the dirty work! Kevin Larkin is actually hiding under the ring now! How cowardly can you get? LB: Oh, he can get MUCH more cowardly than this, trust me.... MW: Well we've got chaos going on in that ring there, and I'm not referring to the Cardinal. It doesn't look like there's gonna be any tags in this one. Just an out-and-out brawl! BD: Good, that's my favorite. MW: Fritigern appears to have knocked out Chaos with a chair now, and he's working over Freeman! He picks the President up, and NAILS him with an earringer! LB: Yeah! Kill Freeman! Woo hoo! MW: Aren't we a little excitable? Chaos is back up now, and Fritigern deals HIM an earringer as well! And Fritigern goes back to work over Freeman! BD: I think their strategy is to beat the crap out of Freeman at all costs. Sounds good to me. MW: Yeah, I kinda figured that. And now Cardinal Chaos is up again, and he's climbing to the top rope! He's up there, and he calls to Fritigern, who turns to face him, AND GETS LEVELED WITH A FLYING CLOTHESLINE! And now Athanaric runs over, AND LEVELS CHAOS WITH A CLOTHESLINE! LB: It's clothesline city! MW: And now Athanaric picks up Freeman, and tosses him out of the ring! Athanaric DIVES out after him, and SPLASHES him to the concrete! He picks up Freeman now, and whips him to the guardrail! He picks him up, and whips him AGAIN to the guardrail! BD: The Horde is really going all out! MW: They have to, their partner abandoned them. BD: Like he would've been any help anyway. MW: True. Athanaric rolls Freeman back into the ring now, and follows him in! Freeman tries to get up, standing next to Chaos, and Athanaric levels them BOTH with a double clothesline! Freeman tries to get up again, but Athanaric dropkicks his knee! LB: I didn't know he knew that move! MW: I didn't know the Horde knew ANY moves. And now Athanaric tosses a severely beaten and bruise Freeman outside, and goes to battle Chaos! Mister Crowley has gotten the best of Fritigern on the other side of the ring, and now he's on the top rope! He jumps, and LEVELS Athanaric with a flying dropkick! BD: Is the ref gonna just let them ALL stay in there, ALL the time? MW: I don't think he has much of a choice. He can't break this up himself. Crowley picks up Athanaric now, SOVIET SUPLEX--BLOCKED BY THE VISIGOTH! Athanaric grabs Crowley, AND HITS HIM WITH A FACESLAM! LB: This is quite a brawl! MW: It couldn't be anything else with these two teams. And now Fritigern enters the fray! He picks up Crowley, AND HITS HIM WITH THE POWER BOMB! And Athanaric drops a fist! THAT'S THE SACK AND PILLAGE! Cover by Athanaric! One.. Two.. Thr.. Kickout! BD: How on earth did he get out of that one! MW: We've seen Crowley kick out of just about every move in the world, and then sit up! So, don't act so surprised, Big Daddy. Fritigern and Athanaric are teaming up on Crowley some more now! They whip him to the ropes! Crowley comes back, and is DECAPITATED WITH A DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE! LB: They really LEVELED him! MW: Athanaric is going back to work over Chaos now, and Fritigern executes a falling splash on Crowley--BUT HE PULLS HIS KNEES UP! And Fritigern crashes right into them! Crowley gets up, as does Fritigern! Crowley whips him to the ropes! Fritigern comes back, and gets LEVELED with a clothesline! BD: There really have been a LOT of clotheslines in this match. MW: You're telling ME? I'm the one who has to call them all! Fritigern gets up, and Crowley hits him with ANOTHER clothesline, and now an elbowsmash! Crowley lifts Fritigern up now, GORILLA PRESS--COUNTERED WITH A ROLLING REVERSE CRADLE BY FRITIGERN! One.. Two.. Kickout! LB: Wow! I would've NEVER expected that from either of the Horde! MW: Neither did Crowley. Both men are up, and Crowley tries the Gorilla Press again--COUNTERED WITH THE CRADLE AGAIN! One.. Two.. Reversal! BD: And Crowley reverses the cradle this time! One.. Two.. Kickout! MW: That was close! Both men are quickly back up, and Fritigern grabs Crowley, GERMAN SUPLEX! One.. Two.. Shoulder Up! LB: Boy, these Visigoths are FULL of surprises today. MW: And they need every last one of them if they want to put Kevin Larkin in charge. Fritigern picks up Crowley now, GORILLA PRESS! And Fritigern drops him like a bad habit! Crowley tries to get back up, and gets NAILED with a diving shoulderblock, and here's a cover by Fritigern! One.. Two.. Kickout! BD: I got this funny feeling that at the end of this match, we're going to be calling Kevin Larkin "AEWA President Larkin". MW: I sure hope not. But on the other side of the ring, it's quite a different story, as Chaos is really taking it to Athanaric-- now he picks him up, VERTICAL SUPLEX! Chaos picks the Visigoth up now, and tosses him over the top rope--WHERE HE LANDS ON FREEMAN, WHO'S STILL OUTSIDE THE RING! LB: (laughing hysterically) Boy, even Freeman's own team is hurting him! Great selection, Bobby! MW: I'm sure that was an accident, but Freeman really is SEVERELY hurt. Chaos now battles Athanaric on the outside, and back in the ring, Fritigern is still taking it to Crowley-- he's going for a Power Bomb here! BD: This might be it! MW: Here goes Fritigern, BUT CROWLEY COUNTERS WITH A BACKDROP THAT SENDS HIM FLYING TO THE OUTSIDE! Now back on the outside, Chaos WHIPS Athanaric to the ring post! Athanaric is in trouble! LB: No he's not, he's just taking a break. MW: Some break! He's getting the crap kicked out of him! And now Chaos is going over to the timekeeper's table, AND HE PICKS UP THE RING BELL! And now he's going back to Athanaric, AND HE JUST CLOCKS HIM OVER THE HEAD WITH IT! BD: Boy! Did you hear that? The bell actually RUNG! Athanaric must have one HARD head! MW: And.... WAIT A MINUTE.... Kevin Larkin just came out from under the ring! He must've heard the bell, and thought the match was over! He's asking Alaric who won! Alaric tells him it's not over, AND HE SCRABBLES TO GET BACK UNDER THE RING--BUT CHAOS CATCHES HIM BEFORE HE CAN! The Cardinal grabs Larkin by the neck, and he smiles an EVIL grin! LB: I don't like this! MW: Chaos lifts Larkin up, AND CHOKESLAMS HIM ON THE CONCRETE! Now Chaos picks him up, and THROWS him back into the ring, WHERE CROWLEY IS ALL ALONE WITH HIM! Crowley DROPS a leg on him, and here's the cover! One.. Two.. Three!!!! (DING, DING) BD: This isn't fair, they tricked Vice-President Larkin! MW: You mean EX-Vice-President Larkin! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of thirty three-minutes and forty-four seconds, here are your winners.... HEAVEN & HELL AND PRESIDENT BOB FREEMAN!! As a result of this victory, Kevin Larkin is now BANNED from the AEWA! LB: I just don't believe this! Kevin Larkin, GONE? MW: That's right. That's what Larkin gets for trying to hide and let his men do all the work. It was working for a while, but communication just broke down between the Horde, causing Heaven and Hell to take control of the match, and led to the events that brought Larkin out from under the ring. BD: Communication? Between the Horde? How are you supposed to communicate when you have the combined IQ of a doorknob? MW: Good point. In any case, Freeman's team has won this match, and now he won't ever have to worry about Larkin's RIDICULOUS rulings ever again. LB: Hey! I liked Larkin's rulings! MW: You would. The man had the competency of a monkey, or even less so. He was totally unprofessional and completely corrupt. In other words, just your kind of guy. LB: Exactly what I was thinking. So now that we know that the AEWA is in for more lunacy under President Dorkman, what do we have left to look forward to? MW: Well we've still got that World Title match coming up between Deranged and "Crimson" Joe Reed. BD: (obviously disappointed) Yippee. So then do you mind if I just take a nap? MW: Personally, I, myself, wouldn't, but I think the AEWA officials who PAY you to commentate might have a problem with that. BD: Spoil sport. LB: Why is JOE REED getting a World Title shot anyway? Hasn't he wrestled like ONE MATCH in the AEWA? MW: Well, yes, but he was VERY impressive in that outing, and he had such an excellent record coming into the AEWA that the AEWA Board of Directors thought that he deserved this shot. BD: That AEWA Board wouldn't know a contender if he bit them in the ass. They gave Deranged a shot, didn't they? MW: Yes, and he won the title. LB: Not without help. MW: Without that help, Shane Matthews would've beat him senseless with a chair! BD: Which is EXACTLY what he deserved. MW: Gee, you're a compassionate guy, Big Daddy. BD: I know, now can we just get on with this so I can go home? MW: Alright. This should be an outstanding matchup, fans, so let's go to the ring! RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is for the AEWA World Heavyweight Title, and is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, the challenger, >From Lexington, Kentucky, he weighs in at 257 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his manager, The Lady in Red, here is "Crrrrrrriiiiiimson" Joe Reeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeed! (Bad Company's "Bad Company" plays loudly over the PA as Reed appears from behind the curtain, and receives a BIG crowd pop. He makes his way to the ring, with The Lady in Red at his side.) LB: I just can't STAND that Lady in Red. MW: Why? Are you intimidated by the fact that some people think she's more beautiful than you? LB: WHO said that? I'll show them who the most beautiful woman in the AEWA is! That woman is NOTHING compared to me! BD: Ya musta hit it pretty close to the mark to get her THAT angry, huh Wilson? LB: You just shut up, Big Daddy, or I'll have to kick your ass too! MW: That should be fun to watch. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 260 pounds and accompanied to the ring by his manager, Angel, he is the reigning AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, please welcome Deeeeeeeeeeeerrrrrrrrraaaaanged! (The theme from "Halloween" plays loudly over the PA as Deranged makes his way to the ring, with Angel at his side, and the belt around his waist. The crowd gives a big pop as the duo hits the ring.) BD: I don't particularly like Deranged, but he should dispatch this rookie with no problem. MW: Don't be too sure about that, Big Daddy. Joe Reed may be a rookie here in the AEWA, but he's one of the most experienced veterans in our sport. This should be quite a match here tonight. LB: If you say so. MW: I do. And now we've got a handshake between the two gentlemen, and here's the lockup! Deranged gets the initial advantage, and whips Reed to the ropes! Reed comes back, and Deranged LEVELS him with a shoulderblock! BD: I told ya. This'll be over in no time flat. MW: I don't think so. Deranged picks Reed up now, and NAILS him with a HARD right cross! He grabs Reed now CRADLE SUPLEX! One.. Two.. Kickout! LB: Yep, Reed sure looks impressive. MW: Please don't mock me. LB: I'm sorry, it's just so easy. MW: Uh, thanks, I think. And now Deranged brings the challenger back up to his feet, VERTICAL SUPLEX--BLOCKED BY REED! And countered with a vertical suplex of his own! BD: Okay, now we'll see what this "Crimson" fella can do. MW: We sure will. Joe Reed DROPS a knee right across the throat of the champion, and now he picks him up, BODYSLAM! LB: Am I supposed to be impressed yet? MW: Just wait. Reed picks Deranged up to his feet again, and nails him with a kick to the midsection! He WHIPS the champion to the ropes, and catches him with a POWERSLAM on the comeback! Here's the cover! One.. Two.. Kickout! BD: Okay, Reed looks like he might be okay, but I doubt he can maintain this kind of pressure. MW: Reed picks the champion up AGAIN, and nails him with a HARD forearm! He whips the champion the ropes, Deranged comes back, AND GETS CAUGHT WITH A LOU THESZ PRESS FROM REED! One.. Two.. Thr.. Kickout! LB: You don't see that one all that often anymore. MW: No you don't, but Reed is from the old school, and I must say it works quite well for him. BD: Kiss butt. MW: Please ignore the ramblings of the poor, misinformed man beside me, fans. BD: Are you talking about me? MW: Now what on earth would give you that idea? And now Reed scales to the top rope! He waits for Deranged to get to his feet, and jumps, FLYING AXEHANDLE--BUT DERANGED NAILS HIM WITH A PUNCH TO THE MIDSECTION BEFORE HE CAN CONNECT! LB: Reed took a risk, and it cost him. MW: In this sport, you've gotta take risks, and risks sometimes have their consequences. Deranged grabs the head of Reed now, ROLLING NECKBREAKER! That dropped Reed pretty good! And now Deranged picks up the legs of the challenger, and turns him over, BOSTON CRAB!! BD: Say goodnight, Reed. You're done! MW: Reed is visibly in a LOT of pain, but he's not gonna give up. Not yet, anyway. Reed is fighting the pain, but you can see it in his face. He can't stand this much longer! LB: Give it up, ya loser! And go back to wherever the hell you came from! MW: Reed is scratching and crawling to reach the ropes now, he's pretty far away, but that's not stopping him. He's pulling for ALL he's worth, and Deranged has to backpedal to hold on! Reed tries one last grasp, AND HE'S GOT IT! The ref is breaking the hold! BD: It doesn't matter. The damage has been done. MW: It certainly has. Reed is hurt pretty badly, and Deranged isn't letting up at all. He obviously sees this as his big chance. Deranged picks up the challenger, INVERTED DDT! LB: That'll ring your bell pretty good. MW: Almost as good as Chaos did to Athanaric earlier, huh? And now Deranged continues to work over his injured opponent. And he begins to climb up to the top rope! BD: This ain't exactly his territory. It might not be wise. MW: But Deranged's never been known to be wise, now has he? He dives, and connects with a PERFECT flying legdrop to the face of Reed! Here's the cover! One.. Two.. Thr.... KICKOUT! BD: BOY! Reed kicked out a NANOSECOND before the three count! MW: He's just barely hanging on now! Deranged is getting a bit upset, and he tosses Reed out to the floor! He could REALLY do some damage out there! Deranged follows Reed to the outside, and he gets ready to go to work! LB: This should be fun to watch. MW: Deranged picks up the challenger now, and whips him into the ringsteps! Reed is in serious pain now! And now Deranged picks Reed up, and he's just BASHING his head, over and over, into the ringpost! BD: What a wuss. That wouldn't hurt. MW: Okay, then how 'bout I do it to you? (Big Daddy says nothing) That's what I thought. And Deranged is still POUNDING Reed into the post, but the Lady in Red is trying to stop him! She jumps onto the back of Deranged, ATTACKING HIM! LB: Throw her off and beat her ugly ass, Deranged! MW: He's trying, but he can't shake her, and Angel is over there now! She YANKS the Lady in Red off of the back of Deranged, and attacks HER! The two women are brawling on the floor! BD: ALL RIGHT! Chick fight! Come on! Rip each other's clothes off! MW: How mature. The two women are REALLY tearing into each other, and for the moment, it looks like the Lady in Red is getting the best of it. She's a former wrestler herself, you know. LB: Yes, and I don't CARE either. MW: But now Deranged has gone back to working over Reed! He picks him up, AND WHIPS HIM TO THE RINGPOST--NO! Reversal by Reed! Deranged just CRASHES into the post, and bounces off, dizzy beyond belief! BD: Where'd he find the energy to do that. MW: Somewhere deep inside himself, that's for sure. Deranged now, stumbles towards Reed, who doubles him over with a kick to the midsection! Reed picks Deranged up now, AND POWER BOMBS HIM THROUGH OUR ANNOUNCERS TABLE! Our monitors are everywhere! LB: Now this is what I call a good seat! MW: Well that depends on how intense your fear of bodily injury is. Reed picks up Deranged from the rubble, and tosses him back into the ring! BD: Why is the ref allowing all this? They both should've been counted out AND disqualified by now. MW: Nobody, even the ref, wants it to end that way, Big Daddy. Reed picks the champion up now, and grabs him by the head, VERTICAL SUPLEX--NO! IT'S THE KENTUCKY BACKSMASHER!!! He NAILED it! Here's the cover! One.. Two.. Three!!!! (DING, DING) LB: Well I'll be! The newcomer really DOES live up to the hype! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of ten minutes and twenty-four seconds, here is your winner, and NEEEEEEEEEEEEEW AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World.... "CRIMSON" JOE REED! MW: And it appears as if the cat fight on the outside has broken up, and now the Lady in Red is in the ring, congratulating her man. Reed is handed the title by the referee, and he raises it high in the air, as this crowd goes absolutely NUTS! BD: Reed may have been the better man than Deranged tonight, but he's NOT the best wrestler in the AEWA, by FAR. Deranged was the luckiest bastard on the face of the earth to win that title, he was just in the right place at the right time. Now let's see what happens when Reed defends his title against some REAL competition. MW: Deranged was a FINE champion, Big Daddy, and I'm sure Reed will wear the belt with just as much pride and honor, if not more. He won't back down from any challenges, and I don't think there are too many challengers out there who have what it takes to bring this man down. LB: Oh, we'll see. Just watch him get MUTILATED in his first defense. MW: I'll be watching all right. I'll be watching Reed prove the BOTH of you wrong, as he defends his title successfully, and gracefully. BD: GIVE ME A BREAK! MW: Well, you can not only take a break, but you can take the whole rest of the day off, because we're out of time. This has been perhaps the AEWA's most SPECTACULAR pay-per-view yet, fans, and if you thought this was good, I've got a feeling that the year ahead is only gonna get better. So for now, for my broadcast colleagues Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt and the Lovely Bertha, this is Matt Wilson saying so long everybody! (A slow-motion replay of Reed POWER BOMBING Deranged through the announcer's table comes on the screen, followed by clips of him hitting the Kentucky Backsmasher for the three count, and Reed proudly raising his new title belt high overhead for all the world to see. Fade to black.)
