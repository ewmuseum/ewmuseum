Straight Talk (9/13, part one)

17 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera fades in on the AEWA Studios, the AEWA logo falls onto the screen, and the words "STRAIGHT TALK" slide in underneath it. The whole logo then tilts, and falls off the bottom of the screen. Behind the desk in studio are the two newest AEWA broadcasters, Chuck Carlson and Ken Wilson.) CC: Hello fans, and welcome again to the AEWA's weekend interview show-- STRAIGHT TALK! KW: Jeez, you make it sound like the Super Bowl or somethin'. It's just an interview show, Chuck. CC: I know, but it doesn't hurt to give things a little extra pep. KW: In your case, yes, it does. CC: We can get your brother Matt in here to do the show with you if you want. KW: (nervously) Did I mention how much I liked that show intro you did? CC: That's what I thought. But we've got a LOT of interviews to get to tonight, so we'd better get moving. Up first, let's hear from Twisted Steel! ---------- (We see Twisted Steel in the locker room. He is laughing uncontrollably, and looks very happy. Hank Thompson is there, ready to interview him.) Thompson: Sir, thanks for agreeing to this interview. I have several important questions for you. First of all, why did you recently attack Ben Slocum? Steel: "The Modern Day Warrior" is a pathetic fool. He really makes me sick. For months, I have wanted to eliminate him from the AEWA-- now the time has come. Thompson: But why did you team up with Ricky Roberts and the Grim Reaper? Those men used to be your WORST enemies, but now you're friends with them! Steel: In the past, I have feuded violently with them both. But they offered me a lot of money, and I just couldn't refuse. You see, after losing the World title, I was slowly slipping in the rankings. Now, however, Ricky and the Reaper have made me famous again. Because of those guys, my career is finally back on track. In order to win, I was willing to sleep with the devil.... Thompson: That's a very shallow thing to say, sir. You have betrayed all of your fans, and it makes me sick. Steel: I don't care about the fans, and I never really did. They can go to hell. (yelling) LOOK AT ME! I am the greatest wrestler of all time! I'm the man who chased Volcano Jackson out of the AEWA, for crying out loud. Thompson: What are you TALKING about? Volcano Jackson left because he wanted to wrestle in other leagues. It had NOTHING to do with you! Steel: Well, he's gone, and that's good. The legend of Volcano Jackson is finally dead. Now, I have another mission. I want to chase Ben Slocum out of the AEWA. I beat him last week, and now he wants to fight me in a barbed wire cage match? Whatever. I'll be there. Thompson: Thank you for your time, sir. (The image fades to black, and we return to our studios.) ---------- KW: Boy, is Slocum in trouble now or what!? I love this! CC: Why would Slocum be in trouble? KW: Well DUH-- A BARBED WIRE CAGE MATCH, with TWISTED STEEL? Twisted Steel's a nutcase psychopath, HE'LL TEAR HIM APART! CC: There is more to wrestling than brute force and sadistic cruelty. KW: Not in a barbed wire cage match there's not. CC: You may have a point, but I'm sure Slocum can handle himself, otherwise he wouldn't have issued the challenge. But we've discussed this issue long enough. Up next, we've got an interview with the Graveyard Shift that was taped directly after their match with the Visigothic Horde last week! Let's roll the footage. ---------- (The camera opens in the locker room. Coffin enters, followed by the Mortician and Grave Dweller) Coffin: We got that son of a b*tch tonight! Dweller: Yeah Mortician, that chokeslam was awesome! Mortician: I wanna do it again, damn it! (Coffin walks to the camera and looks directly in it) Coffin: Larkin, you lousy piece of trash, we are gonna finish you and your boys off once and for all! SMITH, I am begging you to give us a match that no one will ever forget! It has only happened once before, and that was here in the AEWA! Dweller: Yeah, we want Larkin and his bozos in a "suspended ring" match! Coffin: Smith, sign this match-- AND MAY THE BEST TEAM WIN! (Camera fades to black, and we return to the AEWA Studios.) ---------- KW: Why do these guys always have to pick on poor Vice-President Larkin? CC: Because he started it! The man is insane, and he has no business running a federation! I hope that the Graveyard Shift DO get him in a suspended ring match, maybe they'll knock some sense into him! KW: But those matches are so DANGEROUS! And Larkin's not even a trained wrestler! He could be killed! CC: That's a risk I'm willing to take. KW: You're sick. CC: And you're more so. But up next, let's hear from our World Champion-- Heavy Metal. ---------- (camera opens in on a dark room. Heavy Metal steps out of the shadows in front of the camera. He has his long hair hanging to the sides of his head, and has an extremely pissed off look on his face. The AEWA World title is draped over his shoulder) Heavy Metal: Sewerdog, I respected you at one time. Hell, I even liked you.... but not anymore. You lost all chances of having any future relationship with me. I may have helped you, I may have even given you a shot at this belt.... but not anymore. No, you went and attacked me after I beat the face in of your little punk friend Matthews. Yeah, you heard me right-- your LITTLE PUNK FRIEND! (points to the World title) You want this? YOU want this!? Come and get it, f*cker! Yeah that's right. I've had it up to....hell....(raises his hand to about half his body size) HERE with you guys. This is about how big Matthews is, right? You losers are going to regret having EVER messed with the Metal! I'm going to introduce you to my friend the next time I see you.... and that friend is Mr. Baseball Bat! BATTER UP, PAL! HA HA HA! AEWA, rock on! (The image fades to black with the scene of the huge man laughing. We return to our studios.) ---------- KW: I like Matthews and Sewerdog, but they sure PISSED off Heavy Metal, and that's NOT a good place to be. CC: Tell me about it. Those guys aren't very big at all, and they want HEAVY METAL on their tail? Are they nuts? KW: Yes, they are, but that could work in their favor. CC: I suppose you're right. Anyone under 300 pounds who steps in the ring with Heavy Metal would HAVE to be nuts, and the fact that they don't care could be just what you need in a match against Heavy Metal. KW: Exactly, and that's why I can't wait for this one to get started, because a LOT of crazy things are gonna happen. CC: That's for sure. And now let's hear from Exodus. ---------- (We see Exodus sitting in a leather chair inside of his huge mansion. He is kicked back, and even as he sleeps, he has an arrogant smile on his face. His long black hair lays to the sides and his huge body fills the chair, as the doorbell rings) Exodus: Huh? Oh....damn....he's here already? THE DOOR'S OPEN! LET YOUR OWN ASS IN! (a man walks through the door and shuts it behind him. As he steps into the light, we see that it is Hank Thompson) Thompson: Hello, Exodus. Exodus: Yeah, whatever. Cut the crap, let's just get this interview over with, okay? Thompson: Fine with me. My first question is, how do you feel about losing the match against Marcus McTeague? Exodus: How should I feel? (slightly grimaces at the thought) He pinned me in the ring. I'm not here for titles.... although the gold DOES give my outfit that extra push of greatness, don't you think? Thompson: Yeah, sure it does. On to the next question.... During the triangle match, Sky Walker attempted to tag out to you, but instead you nailed him square in the face. What were you THINKING!? Exodus: Hey, pal. I think what I want, WHEN I want. NO ONE tells me what to do. Sky Walker tried to tag out to me-- but he doesn't have enough skill to TRY and tag out to a man such as myself. Thompson: I see. Now with the fact that you are here.... why exactly did you want to come here? Is there any particular reason? Exodus: Yes, there is a reason.... that reason is that I am the GREATEST WRESTLER ON THE FACE OF THIS PLANET, YOU HEAR ME? All these poor excuses for wrestlers out there can kneel down and kiss my boot! If I step in sh*t, you lick it off! Thompson: Well um....okay....anyway.... let's move right along. After holding titles in other federations, do you have expectations to win them here as well? Exodus: Of course. No one here has enough talent to defeat me. What happened last week was not a show of skill, it was a show of cowardice. Marcus McTeague chose to take that path, and now he has the title. But you know, titles don't really MATTER to me-- just another addition to my collection of trophies and accomplishments. Thompson: Now how about Heavy Metal, the World champion? What do you have to say about him? Exodus: I have nothing to say to him....except....watch your back in the future. I know I will. Thompson: In any case, people here see you as a threat. Do you view yourself this way as well, or do you think it is a separate feeling? Exodus: I KNOW they are afraid of me-- wouldn't you be? Anyway, AEWA is a place where losers are born. It's a place where deadbeats hang out. Think about what you guys have here.... You allow people like Deathwatch to run wild here and burn banners, you let a rocker hold the World title, you have an Italian Loan Collection Agency going after some wrestler. If this were any kind of organization, security would have those Italian lasagna-eaters out of the building within SECONDS! How much do you pay these security guards anyway? It took them HOW long to get down there and help President Smith? Offer them a donut for every time they do something worthwhile, maybe that'll get 'em off their asses! Thompson: I hate to admit it, but I have also noticed the lack of security here in AEWA. Smith needs to take care of this. As of right now, we still don't know what kind of condition he is in. Exodus: I mean, I might not like what Deathwatch did to Smith, but it sure was fun to watch! Thompson: Well I'm almost out of time and tape for this interview. Do you have any closing statements before we leave? Exodus: Yeah, I do. McTeague, you beat me. I don't like that. So, I challenge you to a match next Saturday! I will kick your ass come hell or high water, and then you'll know what it feels like to be punished-- BY THE ARMAGEDDON! Ha Ha Ha! (The image fades to black, and we return to our studios.) ---------- KW: Exodus sure had a lot to say, and I loved every word of it! CC: You would. How can a man be so ARROGANT when he has wrestled ONE AEWA match, and lost? KW: You heard him, it was an act of cowardice and stuff! If McTeague has the GUTS to step into the ring with him again, and not cheat this time, Exodus will rip him to pieces! CC: Marcus McTeague didn't cheat the last time, and he won't be cheating should they meet again. KW: Liar. He cheats constantly. CC: Since WHEN? KW: Since.... um......uh.....since I said. CC: What an intelligent response. KW: Thank you. CC: Now let's go and get a word from AEWA's resident turncoat-- Deathwatch. ---------- (camera opens in on Deathwatch sitting at his head office in New York. He is reviewing the video tape of Saturday Night Slam and pausing between segments, then rewinding to watch it all over again) Deathwatch: Smith, what I did to you on Saturday was not EVEN the beginning. You DARE fine me? You want your $25,000? (pulls out a wad of cash from a drawer and throws it at the camera) HERE! Come get it! I don't care how much you try and fine me. I could cover a million dollar fine, and I'd still be set for life! You see, here at ICU, we don't get paid hourly wages. We get paid BUNDLES of money for our services, and last week I was fulfilling a client's service. He paid me (pulls out another wad of cash) 2 MILLION dollars to do that job! Another client paid me $500,000 just to burn your stupid AEWA banner.... but to tell you the truth, I would have done that anyway! See this folder?? (pulls out a massive folder that is thicker than an encyclopedia) LOOK! This is only this year's accomplishments. I have four more of these-- three times as thick! We do our work confidentially, so the clients who paid me to do these things....they could be anyone, it could be any wrestler or any commentator, or just some bum off the street that wants to see you hurt. Quite frankly, I don't care which one it is! AEWA.... this federation is sinking down the drain by the day, and I am going to be known as one of the few men who get the HONOR of putting it under! (fade to black) ---------- KW: Deathwatch is totally out of control, and the rest of the AEWA is in BIG trouble. CC: How could he POSSIBLY take out the ENTIRE federation by himself? KW: Maybe he's not alone. CC: That IS a scary thought. KW: I know, and I thought of it all by myself, too! CC: What do you want, a cookie? You're SUPPOSED to think of things, that's what your brain is for. KW: Really? CC: Yes REALLY. And now let's get a word from the last man eliminated in the North American Title battle royal-- "Slim" Jim Coolridge. ---------- (We see "Slim" Jim Coolridge in the locker room. His manager, Flashy Freddy, is standing with him) Flashy Freddy: Jim, you wrestled a good match. You had to go through 14 guys tonight and fell a little short. I'm proud of you anyway. You WILL be the next AEWA Champion! "Slim" Jim Coolridge: Osbourne, you had 15 other guys help you win. One day it's going to be me and you to go one-on-one. But what I want is a shot at Marcus McTeague! I was promised a TV title shot months ago and never got it. Marcus is a fluke. He SUCKS. I'm the stud of this league and everybody knows it. Marcus get in the ring before I hunt you down and if that happens you'll regret it for the rest of your career. (The image fades to black, and we return to the studio.) ---------- KW: Slim Jim's right. Marcus McTeague DOES suck, but 'ol Jim there sucks even more! CC: That's not very nice. Jim Coolridge and Marcus McTeague are both EXCELLENT wrestlers. KW: No they're not. CC: Then explain to me why McTeague is the TV champion. KW: Because I already told you, he cheated. CC: Oh yes, how could I forget such a well stated argument like that. KW: It's quite all right. CC: I was being sarcastic. KW: Sar-what-stick? CC: Nevermind. Let's just watch this interview with the Machines. ---------- (Paul Wong is finishing a set of push-ups, as Simon O'Neal of the Machines is setting up a chalkboard on the wall of their gym. The title of the board reads WALL OF CONQUESTS and Simon writes the name "Jobby Boys" underneath.) Simon: Just the first AEWA team to be conquered. Now, if I only had a partner who TRUSTED ME.... Paul: Simon, that punch looked loaded, and I still don't like your glove. Remember that mask you wore, that "Just Happened" to always have a piece of metal in it? Simon: Past history. For the last time, the glove ain't loaded. Would I lie? Paul: Let's see. I could ask the referees in our past matches, the IRS, your three ex-wives.... Simon: (interrupting) Let's move on. Who should be our next victims? Personally, I wouldn't mind facing someone easy, like the Assassins or Graveyard Shift. Paul: Nope. We want some gold. I say we challenge The Shadow Warriors for a shot at their National Tag Team Titles. I just hope we don't have any problems petitioning for a shot. Simon: I still say we call up Kevin Larkin and.... Paul: Bribery is NOT an option! Understand? Simon: (Rolling his eyes) Fine. The Shadow Warriors are good, but we're better. Now we just need a chance to prove it. (Mutters to himself) Friggin' Boy Scout of a partner.... (fade to black) ---------- KW: You know somethin'? Simon should drop this loser, Wong-tong-fooey, or whatever his name is. CC: His name is Paul Wong, and he is NOT a loser. KW: Sure he is, he doesn't want to do any of the essentials of wrestling. CC: Using foreign objects, tricking referees, bribing officials-- are THESE your essentials? KW: Are there any others? CC: Not in your mind, apparently, there's not ROOM for anything else. Now let's go and hear from our new North American Champion, Randall Osbourne. ---------- (We see a biker bar outside of Kansas City. From the noise inside, you can tell that there is obviously a celebration going on inside. The door opens and you see the new AEWA North American Champion, Randall Osbourne) Osbourne: The wild ride of Rock n' Roll just made another stop for the "Rebel of Rock". First ever North American Champion. I'm proud of the belt, and proud of my fans. It's all for the fans. This belt goes to the fans. (Bikers in the back ground start yelling "ROCK ON") I won't keep the fans in rut of inaction. I will defend this title against everybody with the same fire that helped me win the battle royal. And I want all the fans and the AEWA to know that the wild ride of the rebel is long from over. I've got more people to face, more titles to win and more fans to make happy. It will never end, because it's a long way to the top if you want to Rock n' Roll. (Osbourne gets onto his Harley, and speeds off into the darkness. Our camera returns to the studio.) ---------- KW: I can't believe that THIS guy won the North American Title. He makes me sick! "This is for the fans", what a load of crap. CC: What's wrong with caring about the fans? KW: Hmmm, let's see.... EVERYTHING! CC: Well that's a very cynical attitude. Personally, I think it's great that the fans finally have a champion they can look up to. KW: That's because you're a retard. CC: Do you have any comebacks that are beyond third-grade level reasoning? (silence) CC: Didn't think so. Let's get a word from Joey Valachi. ---------- (Joey "the Cardiac Kid" Valachi is at an undisclosed location, in what he thinks is the Federal Witness Protection Program. Hopefully he never watches any of those "TV's Practical Joke Shows", as learning that his whereabouts are being broadcast all over the world would probably alarm him a little, since he's running from the Italian Loan Collection Agency.) Valachi: Yeah, Lopez, I heard your challenge, from my hiding place here in Chicago. (to cameraman) Hey, I didn't mean to say Chicago. Can you edit that out? Cameraman: Sure, Joey. Valachi: All right. First of all, how do I know you're not in league with those Italians? I remember from school. Mexico's like the next country over from Italy, right next to Vietnam. So this is probably a setup, and all the Lumberjacks are hired thugs of the ILCA. But I'll take your challenge, if I can name some of the Lumberjacks. Let's see.... I'd want guys like, the Shadow Warriors, they seem like trustworthy guys, and Red Dog, he'd be good. Oh, speaking of dogs, that Sewerdog looks like somebody I can trust. Also Shane Matthews. I don't like him, but I respect him. And those guys who hang with that Hoffa guy, you know who I'm talking about? Cameraman: Of course not. You're babbling again, Joey. Valachi: You shut up. Give me lumberjacks like that, and I'll take your deal, Lopez. And I'll wipe the floor with you, too. Just like I always have. (The image fades to black as we return to the studio.) ---------- KW: Smart move by Valachi. If he had just accepted, without asking to pick lumberjacks, who knows what kind would be assigned by the AEWA. CC: Oh, I don't know-- fair, unbiased ones perhaps? KW: That's exactly what I'm talking about, we can't have that. CC: (sarcastically) Yes, what a tragedy THAT would be. KW: I know. I'm glad you agree. CC: Actually, I don't, I'm just trying to see how deeply messed up your logic is, and apparently, there's no limit to it, so let's just watch these comments from Graphic Violence. ---------- (We see Booze Murphy, Leech Rogers, and Miss DeMeanor in a limo. They all appear to be very happy) DeMeanor: Well AEWA, these are the NEXT AEWA Tag Team Champions, and with my leadership and managerial skills, it will happen! Booze: As you can see, we got Miss DeMeanor helping us now. She was there before, but now she can pay attention to people who have capabilities! Leech: Yo Booze, these AEWA jobbers are pathetic. My Gramps and Grams can fight better than that. We're SICK of wrestling amateurs! We want the titles! Booze: It's just a matter of time! DeMeanor: That's right, we will get the titles soon-- very soon! Oh....and Primal Fear, I have some surprises for you! (The image fades to black, and we return to the AEWA studios.) ---------- KW: All right! Graphic Violence has been overlooked in the AEWA for long enough, the championships are coming home now! CC: Home? When did they ever have the titles in the first place? And would you relax, for crying out loud. They just won a prelim match, that's a far cry from championship quality opposition. KW: You're just jealous that Graphic Violence is SO much better than anyone you like. CC: I beg to differ, my friend. When they start consistently beating teams like Submission and the Graveyard Shift, then we can talk about that. Until then, keep your mouth shut. KW: Don't you worry. They'll eventually get in there with the top teams, and they'll tear them apart too! CC: That remains to be seen. Now let's get some comments from the man who recently had that nasty split with Miss DeMeanor-- Primal Fear. ---------- (We see Primal Fear in his house, watching the replay of Saturday Night Slam) Fear: You know, I am not even mad that I didn't come out of the battle royal with a title. I tried and I tried, maybe I wasn't as brutal as I should be.... but then again, maybe I was. (he fast forwards it to the Deathwatch scene) This is what disgusts me! Beating up a 130-pound man? A man who has run the greatest league in the world? Deathwatch, you proved nothing on Saturday Night Slam! You wanna prove something, get in the ring with me, and I will give you the ass kicking of your life! You think that the AEWA is pathetic? YEAH RIGHT! Deathwatch, I want you in the ring. Hopefully someone can sign one of the BLOODIEST types of matches you can get, because guess what, fans-- I am gonna be BRUTAL! (The image fades to black and we return to our studios.) ---------- KW: Well somebody had to stand up for the AEWA, too bad it's Primal Fear. CC: What's THAT supposed to mean? KW: You KNOW what it's supposed to mean. Primal Fear just hasn't been the same since the split, and I don't think he can handle those types of matches anymore. He's too civilized. CC: Primal Fear can still be uncivilized and vicious in the ring, he just chooses to portray himself differently outside of it. KW: You ACTUALLY think he can still be as wild as he was before? CC: Yes. KW: Well that's a load of bull. Next time he sets somebody on fire, we can talk. CC: With what Deathwatch has been doing lately, he just might do something like that. But now let's hear from a newcomer who recently made his debut in the North American Title battle royal. ---------- (Sir Gallade, decked out in full armor dress, and Lady Guinevere, as beautiful as ever, sit at King Arthur's Round Table) Sir Gallade: Feast your eyes on the pride of England, and not to mention, the king's First Knight. We must say that we are honored to be a part of the AEWA. My Lady.... Lady Guinevere: Now, feast your eyes on the only woman beautiful enough to satisfy our king and his First Knight. Sir Gallade: Enough with the pleasantries, I must say that we are not here to play, but we are here because we were chosen by our king to come to the AEWA and be victorious in winning championship gold. Lady Guinevere: We were given direct orders to win gold and that is what Sir Gallade will do. Sir Gallade: Anyone who gets in the way of us accomplishing our task will fall in defeat. So I guess it is time to make a challenge. Lady Guinevere: Yes, and who shall it be? Sir Gallade: I will leave that open for now, but what caught my eye was the cowardly attack on Mr. Slocum. I am offering my help to him if he wants it. I will be the one to step up and help to rid us of the scum in the AEWA. (The image fades to black as we return to our studios.) ---------- KW: Where the hell is this guy from? Doesn't he know that the knights and stuff died out like thousands of years ago or something? CC: Sir Gallade embodies all the good things that are missing from today's society. What ever happened to loyalty, honor, and justice? KW: We dumped them in favor of lying, cheating, and stealing. I think it was a good trade. CC: You also thing that "fair play" is when you give both guys baseball bats. KW: What's wrong with that? ****TO BE CONTINUED IN PART TWO**** \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- American E-Wrestling Association!
