Saturday Night Slam (9/22, part one)

24 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen, set on fire. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power Bomb, and a look at Mister Crowley DRIVING the some guy's head into the concrete with the Premature Burial! Then the camera switches to the Lovely Bertha, Matt Wilson, and "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt in a packed arena) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Saturday Night Slam! We have some great matches scheduled for tonight! BD: In the main event, Heavy Metal will be defending his World Heavyweight title against Mister Crowley! LB: Okay, but what's first? MW: It's time for some tag action! The Time Angels had an impressive debut last week, and this week, they'll be facing the Crew. In a few moments, we'll see which team will be moving up in the ranks. (Groovy Gonzalez and Cool Carlson race to the ring, fists pumping, trying to slap the hands of fans who really couldn't care less.) BD: These guys look ready for action! They're going to kick some butt tonight, and I'm excited. LB: Me too. ("Time Is On My Side" plays as Jason White leads Chrono and "The Great" Jack Jerovah, to a friendly reaction from the crowd. Chrono is 6'7", 250 pounds, short brown hair, blue eyes, silver trunks and a cloak; Jerovah stands 6'11", 327 pounds, with long brown hair/hazel eyes, wearing gold trunks) MW: Okay, the referee has finished checking both teams, and we're ready to start things off. BD: Here we go.... The Crew (Groovy Gonzalez and Cool Carlson) versus The Time Angels (Chrono and "The Great" Jack Jerovah) The Time Angels successfully bullied their smaller foes around the ring, as the fans exploded into a paroxysm of glee. The high flying tactics of the Crew were countered by Jerovah's size advantage and Chrono's concentrated attack on the Crew's legs. Chrono used his hybrid scientific-brawling style to dominate the opening moments, and at one point he had Gonzalez locked in a scorpion death lock-- but Carlson entered the ring and connected with a thrust kick. As the huge Jerovah tried to enter the ring, he was cut off by the referee, permitting the Crew to double team Chrono for a moment or so. They executed their "Electric Pin" maneuver (a thrust kick followed by a flying elbowdrop), and as Gonzalez made the cover, Carlson grabbed a hold of Jerovah's ankles to prevent him from breaking the pin. The referee counted, and the Crew had their first victory since their return at 11:07. MW: The Crew looked good for the first time in months, but I really liked what I saw out of the Time Angels. I think we have just seen a team that could have a long, successful career. BD: I think we saw a pair of mongoloids who were outwitted by the Crew, of all people. LB: What's up next? MW: The Machines are about to square off against the Assassins. This should be a VERY interesting match. BD: Are you kidding me? It won't even be fair-- the Assassins don't stand a CHANCE! I'm really going to enjoy this.... ("Welcome to the Machine" by Pink Floyd heralds the arrival of the Machines, Paul Wong and Simon O'Neal, to a reasonably warm reception. As Paul Wong exuberantly shook hands with the fans on his way down, Simon seemed genuinely annoyed when someone slapped him on the back.) MW: The Machines have been having their differences lately, folks. They don't seem to be getting along. LB: Quit trying to cause trouble, Wilson. Paul Wong and Simon O'Neal are doing just fine, so shut up. (Shockwave and Banshee, in their full ninja regalia, came to the ring. The crowd went wild.) MW: These guys are looking really good tonight. They want to get noticed by our championship committee. BD: Here we go.... The Machines (Paul Wong and Simon O'Neal) versus The Assassins (Shockwave and Banshee) The ever-wary Assassins seemed worried at first, and they asked the referee to check Simon O'Neal's glove. At first Simon tried to resist the search, but he finally allowed the referee to check. The search revealed absolutely nothing. The Assassins have seen the Machines before, and also insisted that Simon's boots be checked. As Paul argued, pointing out that the glove check had vindicated his partner and that enough time had been wasted already, tensions began to be stretched a little too far. As the referee tried to prevent a pre-match fracas between Paul and Shockwave, Simon removed a small chain from his left boot and placed it in his glove. When things settled down, the ref was finally free to check Simon's boots, and he was much more cooperative this time. The search again found nothing out of the ordinary, and when Banshee protested that he had seen Simon place a chain in his glove, the referee announced that enough was enough and it was time to finally start this match. Simon shrugged to his partner and sighed, and Banshee explained to Shockwave what he had seen. As the match began, the two larger men started off: Paul and Shockwave. The match was evenly-fought, with several two-counts scored by both teams, and with Shockwave's martial arts attack taking its toll on Paul. Each team seemed to adopt a strategy of matching the larger men against each other and the smaller men against each other, so that the pace alternated between lightning quick and incredibly slow. At one point, Simon broke the action to request that the referee inspect the Assassins' masks for foreign objects, but the ref was no longer interested in conducting any further searches. When Banshee hooked Paul into a dragon suplex, Simon punched Banshee square in the left temple with the chain-wielding, glove-concealed hand, effectively breaking the pin. As Shockwave entered the ring to protest the use of a foreign object, the referee intercepted him. This gave Simon the opportunity to toss the chain to the floor, and he then held up the empty glove for the ref to inspect. Making a show of indignation at having his integrity impugned, he also tossed the glove aside. As the referee ordered Shockwave back to his corner, the match resumed, and the groggy Banshee was unable to keep pace with Paul. The match didn't last long after that. Paul finished him off with a top rope powerslam moments later, for the win at 22:13. As the Assassins argued with the referee, and the crowd gave a mixed response, Paul found the discarded chain and glove, and looked inquiringly at his partner. Simon defended himself by pointing out that the chain couldn't possibly be his, as his boots were thoroughly searched and his glove had been examined twice. Clearly, he appeared to say, the chain had actually been brought to the ring by the Assassins. Paul, enraged that his opponents should use such tactics, tried to reenter the ring, but Simon pulled him back and reassured him that truth had won the day, since they had successfully overcome these cheating tactics. After the Machines had gone, the Assassins continued to argue the decision, and went out to the floor and found the glove and the chain. They presented it all to the official, who maintained that he had made three searches without uncovering any foreign object. MW: That was a complete miscarriage of justice. The Assassins were cheated out of a victory, and it's all because of Simon O'Neal. BD: The Machines won tonight, and nothing else matters. Let's just hurry up and move on.... LB: So what's next? MW: American Ambush is taking on Jose Gomez in a haircut match. This one has more than a little bit of revenge wrapped up in it too. BD: It sure does. At the Championship Challenge, I guess you could say that Gomez was "Ambushed" during his match with Mean Massimo. MW: Yes he was. It was a no-disqualification match, and Ambush took full advantage of that, and just came out and teamed up with Massimo to DESTROY Gomez. Gomez is looking for revenge, and the loser of this one's gonna find himself bald! LB: I wonder who it's gonna be? MW: Let's go to the ring and find out! RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is a special "haircut match" and is scheduled for one fall, with the loser to be shaved BALD! Introducing first, weighing in at 243 pounds, American Ambuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuush! (American Ambush makes his way to the ring to a large chorus of boos. He just seems to laugh it off.) BD: You know, this guy's had a whole new attitude since returning to the AEWA, and I LIKE it! MW: I know you do, which is one of the reasons that I DON'T like it. He doesn't care about anyone or anything anymore, and he was never like that before. LB: Well that's because this is the "new and improved" American Ambush. MW: Improved is not the word I would've used. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 247 pounds and accompanied by his manager, El Diablo, here is "Ultimate" Jose Goooooooooooooomez! (Gomez makes his way to the ring to an accompaniment of boos, but also gets some scattered cheers from fans who are VERY upset with Ambush right now.) BD: What're these morons cheering for? This is a member of the Church right here. Have they finally developed some sense of taste? MW: I think it's just that they're still upset with Ambush right now for turning his back on them, and right about now they'd cheer for anyone who's facing him. LB: Even the Twilight Strangler? MW: To every rule, there are exceptions, and I'd say that the Strangler, and a few select others, might fall into that category. LB: Darn. MW: Gomez steps into the ring, only to get CLOBBERED by Ambush! Ambush is really pounding away on him, and now he hooks both arms, he's going for a double underhook suplex--NO!! Countered with a backdrop by Gomez--But Ambush counters THAT with a sunset flip! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Gomez just couldn't throw Ambush off there! MW: No he couldn't. And now both men reach their feet, and the Ambush whips Gomez to the ropes! Gomez comes back, AND NAILS THE AMBUSH WITH A CLOTHESLINE, WHICH SENDS HIM FALLING OUT OF THE RING! LB: Gomez really came in HARD that time. MW: He certainly did. Ambush makes his way back into the ring, and Gomez LEVELS him with a rolling kick! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! BD: You're not going to pin American Ambush with THAT. MW: Maybe not, but he's wearing him down. And now Gomez brings the Ambush back to his feet, and nails him with ANOTHER rolling kick! But the Ambush doesn't fall! Gomez takes a step back, and charges in with a clothesline, which LEVELS the Ambush! LB: The Ambush better do something FAST if he wants to turn this one around. MW: Jose Gomez brings the Ambush to his feet, and NAILS him with a fist to the midsection, AND ANOTHER! Ambush is stooped over in pain now, and Gomez grabs him, REVERSE CRADLE! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Gomez is really taking the fight to him! MW: And Gomez is looking to end it! He's going up top, AND HE'S GIVING THE SIGNAL FOR THE LATIN LEAP! He waits for Ambush to get to his feet, AND DIVES--BUT GETS CAUGHT!!! Ambush catches him in mid-air, and now he turns Gomez upside-down, TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER! LB: WHAT A COUNTER! MW: It sure was! And now the Ambush picks Gomez up off the mat, and he hooks both arms again, DOUBLE UNDERHOOK SUPLEX!! He NAILED it this time! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Gomez is in trouble now! MW: The Ambush brings Gomez back to his feet, and NAILS him with a Russian Legsweep! The Ambush picks Gomez up now, AND GORILLA PRESSES HIM! He's got Gomez WAY up there, and he DROPS him neck first across the top rope! LB: Now that was just mean and sadistic-- I like it! MW: I know you do. Gomez is down now, and Ambush NAILS him with a falling headbutt! And now Ambush is picking Gomez back up, he lifts him onto his shoulder, POWERSLAM! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder up! BD: Ambush almost had him that time! MW: ALMOST, but not quite. And now the American Ambush brings Gomez back to his feet, and sinks in an octopus hold--BUT GOMEZ COUNTERS IT WITH A HIPTOSS!! LB: I didn't think he had enough left in him for that! MW: To tell you the truth, neither did I. The Ambush quickly gets back to his feet, but Gomez catches him with a single leg takedown! Ambush stands back up, and gets NAILED with a clothesline! BD: Somebody lit a fire under Gomez! MW: They sure did! And now he's fighting for all he's worth! Ambush rises again, and Gomez takes him down with a football tackle! Gomez picks the Ambush up now, BACKBREAKER!! LB: Ambush is DANGEROUSLY close to a haircut right now! MW: That's for sure. Gomez has the Ambush again, AND HE NAILS HIM WITH A SENTON! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: He's still hangin' in there though. MW: And now Gomez is starting to REALLY pour it on. He picks the Ambush up off the mat, SOVIET SUPLEX! The Ambush is in trouble now. Gomez picks him up again, grabs him by the hair, BACKSPIN DDT!! LB: That's enough to put your lights out, but the Ambush is one tough cookie. MW: You can say that again. And now Gomez gets up, AND EXECUTES ANOTHER SENTON! The cover! One..Shoulder Up! BD: After all THAT he gets up at one? MW: Yes he did, and Gomez doesn't like it! He's complaining to the ref about a slow count, but he's not getting any sympathy. Now Gomez turns back to the Ambush, who is starting to get up, AND GETS CAUGHT WITH AN INSIDE CRADLE FROM THE AMBUSH! One..Two..Kickout! LB: I guess you could say he "Ambushed" him on that one, huh? (laughs) MW: You could, but that wouldn't make it funny. And now the Ambush gets up, and NAILS Gomez with a kick to the head! The Ambush picks Gomez up now, and whips him to the ropes! Gomez comes back, AND IS ALMOST DECAPITATED WITH A CLOTHESLINE! BD: Gomez did a 360 on that one! MW: Tell me about it! And now the Ambush steps to the outside of the apron, what's he trying to do here? He jumps up, SLINGSHOT SOMERSAULT SPLASH--BUT GOMEZ ROLLS AWAY! And Ambush hits HARD! LB: That's the price you pay when attempting those high risk maneuvers. MW: Gomez is now pulling himself to his feet, and he's scaling the ropes, while at the same time, the Ambush is slowly getting back up. Gomez is on the top rope, and Ambush is up! Ambush turns to face him, and Gomez dives, AND NAILS THE LATIN LEAP!!! The cover! One..Two..Three!!! (DING, DING) BD: And it looks like we're gonna have a hairless Ambush! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of twelve minutes and forty-one seconds, here is your winner... "ULTIMATE" JOSE GOMEZ! MW: So Gomez gets his revenge, and he RUSHES to the outside to grab those clippers! And now he's coming back into the ring. LB: And the Ambush is out COLD! MW: That makes these haircuts go much nicer. Gomez is making an initial trim with those scissors, and now he's got the electric shaver! BD: It looks like the Ambush is waking up! MW: BUT EL DIABLO KNOCKS HIM BACK OUT WITH A FRYING PAN! What's he doing carrying around a frying pan, anyway? LB: Maybe he was planning on making eggs? MW: Doubtful. And Gomez is really doing a hack job in there, and it appears as if he's done! BD: Somehow I have the feeling that Ambush is not gonna be happy with his haircut when he wakes up. MW: Well would YOU like someone shaving off your hair? BD: I guess not. MW: That's right. Gomez appears very happy with his work, and now he's leaving. LB: Revenge is sweet. MW: It certainly can be. But right now I'd like to take a time out and announce the AEWA's next pay-per-view! It's not very far away, so on October 6th, get ready for AEWA Mega Combat: The Rising Sun. It will be held, appropriately, in the land of the rising sun-- Tokyo, Japan! BD: But I don't speak Chinese! MW: Neither do the people in Japan, Big Daddy, they speak Japanese. They're two different countries you know. BD: Really? MW: Yes, Really. And you won't have to speak Japanese either, because we'll still be broadcasting in English. BD: Oh, good. LB: So what are the matches on this upcoming pay-per-view? MW: Well, they're yet to be announced, but I'm sure that we can expect some big ones, as always. BD: That's pretty cool, but what do we have NOW, as in the next match? ****TO BE CONTINUED IN PART TWO**** \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- American E-Wrestling Association!
