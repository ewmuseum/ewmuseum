Kickoff '97 (2/13, part seven)

26 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * CONTINUED FROM PART SIX * * * * MW: Well, up next is that much anticipated "Chain of Pain" match between Shane Matthews and Randall Osbourne, and there is so much bad blood here that I don't even know where to begin. Matthews originally BRUTALLY attacked Osbourne after a match, beating him with a billy club, and handcuffing him to a guardrail. Then, at December Doom, Osbourne returned the favor, brutally attacking Matthews after his World title defense against Ben Slocum. BD: Yeah, Osbourne attacked him for no good reason! MW: He had PLENTY of good reasons, but even that wasn't the final straw. That came when Matthews was defending his World Title again, several weeks later, this time against Deranged. Matthews was breaking every rule in the book, and Osbourne decided to do something about it. He snatched the chair Matthews was using on Deranged away from him. That REALLY upset Matthews, who attacked Randall, who in turn, hit MATTHEWS with the chair, and allowed Deranged to win the title. We haven't seen much of either man since then, but things are bound to explode tonight. LB: Yeah, I can't wait for this one. MW: Well, you're gonna have to wait at least a little longer, as Hank Thompson is standing by with Shane Matthews for an interview right now. Take it away, Hank! (The camera shifts to a view of the locker room, where AEWA broadcaster Hank Thompson stands, alongside Shane Matthews, who wears a sleeveless leather jacket and sunglasses, and his manager, Johnny Terror.) Thompson: Thanks, Matt. I'm standing here now with the former AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews. Now Shane, there's a lot of bad blood going into this match with Osbourne. Would you care to comment on it? Matthews: Well I'm not gonna go through the whole history of me and Randall, as I'm sure that putz, Wilson already did, but I do got something to say about Randall anyway. Now Osbourne, you made the biggest mistake of your career when you cost me that World title. That piece of <BLEEP>, Deranged, isn't even fit to lick my boots, let alone wear my title. And the reason he's got that title is all because of you, Randall. Now you might think that's funny, but this is no joke to Shane Matthews. You caused me to lose my most prized possession in the world. And that's not something I take lying down. You're gonna pay for that, 'cause I'm gonna take it outta your ass. Thompson: Um.... Interesting analogy, but what is your strategy going into this match? Terror: I'll tell you what his strategy is, baby. He's going all out. There will be Death tonight, but it won't be Sudden. Randall is in for the longest, most painful night of his career. Am I right, Shane? Matthews: You know it, Johnny. Osbourne, your fate tonight is gonna be WORSE than death. You'll be WISHING you were dead, just to be put out of your misery. I'm gonna whip you, and beat you, and whip you some more, until you're crying for your mamma, boy. Your first mistake was pissing off Sudden Death, and you won't be around to make second mistake. You've hit the end of the road, buddy, and it's a DEAD END. (With that, Matthews just walks off, with Johnny following, apparently headed for the ring.) Thompson: Thank you Mr. Matthews, Mr. Terror. Back to you, guys. (The camera shifts back to the capacity filled arena.) BD: Boy, Shane sounds like he means BUSINESS. I'd hate to be in his shoes right now. MW: I'd hate to be involved in this match at all, it's gonna be a bloodbath! But it's gonna be one hell of an exciting bloodbath, so with that in mind, let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is set for one fall, it is a special "Chain of Pain" match. The rules for this match are as follows. Both men will be chained to each other by the wrists, by way of a ten foot chain! There will be NO countout, NO disqualification, NO submissions, and NO time limit, the only way to win is by pinfall, and there MUST be a winner! Introducing first, From Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, he weighs in at 222 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his manager, Johnny Terror, "Suuuuuudddden Death" Shaaaaaaaaaaane Maaaaaaaaaaatttttheeeeeeeews! (Anthrax's "Looking Down the Barrel of a Gun" blares out over the PA as Matthews makes his way to the ring, and the boos become deafening. Matthews taunts several members of the audience on his way to the ring, and the boos only get louder.) LB: These people REALLY hate Matthews. MW: Of course they hate him. He's a cheating, stealing, lying, self-centered, arrogant jerk. LB: Yeah, I think that's why I like him so much. MW: Whatever. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 362 pounds, Raaaaandaaalllll "Rebel of Rock" Oooooooooosssbooooooooooourrrrrne! (AC/DC's "It's a Long Way to the Top" blasts out over the sound system as Osbourne makes his way to the ring, and the crowd goes NUTS.) BD: I've never understood why people cheer this moron, and I guess I never will. MW: No, you never will, because you can't grasp the concept of skill and fair play. LB: Fair play? What about when Osbourne interfered in Matthews' match and cost him the title? MW: He was only trying to stop Matthews from cheating, and then Matthews provoked him. LB: Excuses, excuses. MW: And now the two competitors are being chained together, and we're just about ready to get underway! Both men are hooked up to the chain, and they charge in at each other! We've got a lockup! Osbourne gets the advantage, and he picks Matthews up, INVERTED ATOMIC DROP! BD: You know, I've always wondered why they call that an atomic drop, because really, what does it have to do, AT ALL, with atomic weapons? It doesn't even hurt that much. So why.... MW: Oh just shut up and pay attention to the match, will ya Big Daddy? Matthews is stunned, and Osbourne NAILS him with a back heel kick! And now Osbourne wraps that chain around the neck of Matthews, AND HE'S CHOKING HIM WITH IT! LB: Disqualify him, ref! He could get killed! MW: Sorry, Bertha, but there's no DQ's in this one, and that means anything goes! Matthews can barely breathe, but Osbourne finally releases the chain! He picks Matthews up now, CROTCH SLAM! And now Osbourne is wrapping a length of that chain around his wrist, and he's POUNDING away on the forehead of Matthew with it! Matthews is busted open! BD: And it's a gusher! MW: It sure is, there's blood EVERYWHERE! And Osbourne picks Matthews up now, GUTWRENCH SUPLEX! And now Osbourne's just WHIPPING him with that chain! That's gonna leave a BIG welt on the back of Shane Matthews! LB: It's also gonna hurt a whole hell of a lot too! MW: No kidding! Osbourne picks up Matthews now, and whips him to the turnbuckle.... and right into our referee! The ref is out COLD! Osbourne scoops up Matthews now, RUNNING POWERSLAM! The cover! BD: But there ain't no one to count! This is great! MW: Osbourne doesn't seem to think so. Apparently, he thinks he has Matthews beat already, but there's no one there to count the pinfall! Osbourne is trying to revive the official, but he's not having any luck. So now he just is going back to work on Matthews. Osbourne picks Matthews up, and whips him to the ropes! Matthews comes back, and Osbourne LEVELS him with a VICIOUS clothesline! Here's the cover! LB: And the ref is STILL out! HA HA HA.... MW: Stop laughing, this isn't funny! Why isn't there another official out here? Osbourne is once again trying to help the official, and he's STILL having no luck! Osbourne gives up again, and goes back to Matthews! He NAILS Matthews with a falling headbutt, and now he's POUNDING on him with that chain again! BD: And Matthews is losing a LOT of blood! MW: He sure is. Osbourne scoops Matthews up again now, and puts him over his shoulder. He runs, AND PLANTS MATTHEWS WITH ANOTHER RUNNING POWERSLAM! The ref STILL hasn't recovered, but he appears to be crawling over to make the count! He's ALMOST there, and now he's in position! One.. Two.. Thr.. Kickout! LB: Woah! Matthews almost lost it right there! MW: And he WOULD'VE lost too, if the ref had been healthy and in position. That was an EXTREMELY slow count! BD: The man's hurt. What do you want him to do? MW: There's nothing he CAN do. I'm just saying it should be over. LB: Oh quit your whining and just call the match. MW: Fine. Osbourne picks Matthews up now, and NAILS him with an earringer! He grabs Matthews by the head, and DRIVES him to the mat with a bulldog! And here's the cover! One.. Two.. Thr.. Kickout! BD: And ANOTHER close shave for Matthews. MW: I'm surprised he got out at all! The man's a bloody mess! Osbourne picks Matthews up now, ANOTHER EARRINGER--BUT MATTHEWS COUNTERS WITH A BACKDROP! LB: Don't be so surprised, Wilson. Shane's still got plenty of tricks up his sleeve. MW: I don't doubt it. Matthews takes a few seconds to recover, and now he wraps the chain around the neck of Osbourne! And he's squeezing TIGHT! He's trying to do some PERMANENT damage! Matthews lifts him up by the chain around his neck now, AND USES IT TO SLAM HIM BACK DOWN TO THE MAT! Matthews is moving in now, he NAILS Osbourne with a senton, and here's the cover! One.. Two.. Kickout! BD: That was three! I saw it! MW: It was not, so clam up! Matthews gets back up now, and he NAILS Osbourne with a diving headbutt! He pulls himself up slightly, AND NAILS OSBOURNE AGAIN WITH A KNEELING HEADBUTT! And now here's a cover! One.. Two.. Shoulder Up! LB: And now it's Osbourne who's in trouble. I told you Shane would come back! MW: You did indeed. And now Matthews is going up top! He perches himself on that top rope, and dives! FLYING KNEEDROP--BUT OSBOURNE ROLLS AWAY, AND MATTHEWS HITS HIS KNEE RIGHT ON THAT CHAIN! BD: OUCH! MW: You can say that again. Osbourne slowly makes his way to his feet, as does Matthews! Both men have gotten up, and Matthews charges in, BUT OSBOURNE CATCHES HIM--HOTSHOT! And now Osbourne is ready to follow it up! He picks up Matthews, INVERTED POWER BOMB! LB: Matthews is in VERY big trouble now! MW: Osbourne begins to POUND on Matthews with that chain again, and the blood starts to flow even MORE from the head of Matthews! Osbourne picks him up now, POWERSLAM! And the cover! One.. Two.. Foot on the ropes! BD: Can you believe it? After all that, Matthews still knows EXACTLY where he is! MW: That's why he's one of the best. Osbourne picks Matthews back up now, and places him on the top rope! Osbourne climbs up there himself now, AND EXECUTES A BELLY-TO-BACK SUPERPLEX! Osbourne now DROPS a knee on Matthews, and here's the cover! One.. Two.. Foot on the Ropes! LB: You'd think Osbourne woulda been watching for that after the last pinfall attempt, but I guess he's just too stupid. MW: Bertha! LB: Hey, I call it like I see it. MW: You certainly do that. Osbourne is REALLY working over Matthews with that chain now, and wait a minute, JOHNNY TERROR'S COMING IN THE RING! LB: And he's got a chair, too! MW: That's not good news for Osbourne, who doesn't even see him! Johnny comes up behind him, and just CLOBBERS him over the back of the head with the brunt of that chair! This is insane! BD: And all perfectly legal, because there are no disqualifications! HA HA HA HA HA! MW: Unfortunately, you're right. And now Terror picks up Matthews' arm, AND DRAPES IT OVER OSBOURNE! I can't BELIEVE that the ref is counting this! One.. Two.. Foot on the ropes! LB: What the hell? MW: And Osbourne knew where he was that time! Don't ask me how, but he did! I sure am glad it didn't end that way, though. BD: Speak for yourself, peabrain! MW: That's exactly what I'm doing. Matthews is coming to his senses now, and he gets up, and NAILS Osbourne with a headbutt! And now he get to his feet, and pulls Osbourne up too! He grabs him, NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX! One.. Two.. Thr.. Kickout! LB: That was THREE, DAMN IT! I swear! MW: Would you be willing to take a polygraph, then? LB: What are you talking about? I don't have false teeth, and I don't need polygrip! MW: (sighs) Nevermind. Matthews brings Osbourne to his feet now, JUMPING SIDE KICK--DUCKED BY OSBOURNE!! Matthews goes down, AND OSBOURNE WHIPS HIM WITH THAT CHAIN! BD: Shane really is getting the worse end of this chain deal. MW: That's the truth. Osbourne isn't even busted open yet, Shane has bled enough to fill a river! And now Osbourne whips him AGAIN with that chain! Matthews is REALLY hurting! Osbourne picks him up now, INVERTED ATOMIC DROP! LB: Now Big Daddy raised an interesting question about that move earlier.... MW: Oh, just shut up about that already! And now Osbourne grabs ahold of Matthews, INVERTED DDT!! And the cover! One.. Two.. Foot on the ropes! BD: Geez, doesn't this guy learn ANYTHING? LB: Apparently not. MW: What am I gonna do with you guys? BD: Well personally, I'd like to be.... MW: It was a rhetorical question, Big Daddy. BD: A what? MW: Nevermind. Osbourne is really taking it to Matthews now. He whips him of the ropes! Matthews comes back, AND GETS CLOTHESLINED WITH A SECTION OF THE CHAIN! Osbourne DROPS a knee! LB: And look who decided to join the party! MW: It's Sir Gallade! Osbourne's former stablemate, and he's carrying that cane that he and Cardinal Chaos used to beat up Damian Steele earlier today! BD: And I don't think he's planning on using it to help him walk, either! MW: I'd say that's a safe bet. Back in the ring though, Osbourne lifts Matthews up, and NAILS him with a fist to the midsection! Now he grabs Matthews by the head, AND NAILS THE BACKSPIN DDT!! IT'S OVER! Here's the cover! One.. Two.. And Sir Gallade CRACKS the Singapore Cane over Osbourne's head, breaking the count! LB: You know, I'm beginning to REALLY like Sir Gallade. MW: You would. This is disgusting! And the match is continuing! BD: As well it should, the rules say no disqualification, don't they? MW: Well yes, but this is carrying it a bit far! And Gallade CONTINUES to beat Osbourne with that cane! Osbourne's finally busted open! LB: And Matthews is back up, and he's joining the fun! MW: Yes, Matthews has joined right in with Sir Gallade, whipping Osbourne with that chain! And now Matthews is telling Gallade to stop, and go outside the ring? What's going on? BD: I don't know, but I think it's gonna be something VERY cool! MW: Matthews is wrapping that chain around the throat of Osbourne, AND HE HANGS HIM OVER THE TOP ROPE WITH IT!! That could KILL the man! And now Gallade is BEATING him with that cane, in ADDITION to the hanging! This is madness! LB: Now THIS is entertainment! MW They're ruthlessly beating him senseless! This has got to stop! And now Matthews releases Osbourne, and pulls him back into the ring! The man is COMPLETELY unconscious, lying on the mat! And now Matthews covers him, by placing a single foot on his chest, while standing sadistically over him! This is despicable! One.. Two.. Three!!! (DING, DING) BD: I knew Matthews could pull this one out. What a great technical matchup! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of thirty-two minutes and fourteen seconds, here is your winner.... "SUDDEN DEATH" SHANE MATTHEWS!!!! MW: This match was a disgrace to the AEWA! And now look at Sir Gallade, raising Matthews' arm! And now he's standing over Osbourne, laughing! And Gallade's calling for a microphone! What could he possibly have to say now? GALLADE: You always were a worthless loser, Randall. You make me sick! The Knights of Deliverance can kiss my bloody ass! MW: And now Gallade just THREW down the microphone, and he's AGAIN pounding on Osbourne's prone body with that cane! This is TOTALLY uncalled for. LB: Maybe so, but it's fun to watch. MW: You're sick. And now Gallade and Matthews are leaving, smiling and laughing about what they've done! This is appalling! And now the paramedics are out there attending to Osbourne, and we can only hope that no serious damage was done. BD: Don't be such a crybaby, Wilson. Osbourne knew the rules when he came in here. MW: I think what happened tonight went a LITTLE beyond the rules. LB: Maybe so, but there's nothing you can do about it, so let's just get on with the card.
