AEWA — Card Archive

December Doom (12/15, part six)

22 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
********************************************************** CONTINUED FROM PART FIVE ********************************************************** MW: Randall Osbourne is defending his TV title against fellow member of the Knights of Deliverance, Sir Gallade. BD: In other words, nap time. Wake me when it's over, Wilson. LB: Yeah, and you people watching at home might want to get up and go to the bathroom during this match, maybe make yourselves a sandwich. That's what I'm gonna do. MW: Neither of you are going anywhere. We're going to call this match, and it's gonna be a great one. BD: Yeah, sure. LB: Whatever. MW: Let's just go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA Television Title! Introducing first, the challenger, From Hackney, England, and weighing in at 315 pounds, Siiiiiiiiiiiiir Gaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaalllllllllaaaaaade! (The British National Anthem begins to play loudly over the PA as Sir Gallade makes his way toward the ring, to a big pop from the crowd.) BD: This guy thinks he's a medieval knight or something. Is he in some kinda warped reality, or is he just retarded? MW: Don't talk about him that way, Big Daddy. I'm sure he doesn't actually THINK it's medieval times. He just embodies all that was good and pure about those times, and he's trying to help bring a little of that spirit into our times. God knows we could all use it. LB: I think he's just some wacko, like that Don Quixote guy. MW: I'm surprised you even know who Don Quixote is. LB: I read about him one time in a book once, I don't remember the name of it, though. MW: Could it be "Don Quixote"? LB: Yeah, that's it. Thanks Wilson. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, he weighs in at 362 pounds and is the current AEWA Television Champion, Randaaaaaaall "The Rebel of Rock" Ooooooooooossssssssbooooooooooooooouuurne! (AC/DC's "It's a Long Way to the Top" cranks out over the PA as Osbourne makes his entrance, and the crowd goes NUTS. Osbourne drags the title behind him like it's something he's ashamed of.) BD: Why's Osbourne always draggin' that title around? MW: Would you be happy if you won a title due to someone else's interference that you didn't know about? BD: You're damn right I would be. In fact, I'd probably find the guy who interfered and personally thank him. MW: Sorry, I forgot who I was asking. Well any person with any kind of morals would NOT be happy with that kind of victory. LB: Morals suck. BD: Yeah, who needs 'em. MW: Apparently not you two. Both men are in the ring now, and we've got a friendly handshake between them. And now the bell rings, and we've got a lock up! LB: And now, the boredom begins! MW: Be quiet and watch the match. Osbourne now whips Gallade to the ropes! He comes back, and gets CAUGHT with a dropkick to the knee! Osbourne picks Gallade up now, SNAP SUPLEX! BD: He's not wrestling like he's ashamed. MW: Maybe he's not thinking about the belt, just wrestling. And now Osbourne picks Gallade up, and NAILS him with a Hotshot! Gallade is reeling, and Osbourne picks him up again, and places him on the top turnbuckle! He climbs up himself, and NAILS Gallade with a belly-to-back superplex! LB: Is it over yet? MW: No. Osbourne picks up Gallade now, and places him in the corner! Osbourne backs up, and charges in, AVALANCHE! Gallade is in trouble! And now Osbourne picks him up, and nails him with ANOTHER Hotshot! BD: He's not wrestling like they're friends, either. MW: You can't afford to in a match with this much at stake, you've gotta lay it all on the line. Osbourne puts on an earringer now, BUT GALLADE COUNTERS WITH A BACKDROP! AND OSBOURNE RE-COUNTERS WITH A SUNSET FLIP! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: Darn, it didn't end. MW: No, and it's probably not going to anytime soon, so get used to it. Both men are up now, and Osbourne whips Gallade to the ropes, but Gallade reverses! Osbourne comes back, AND GETS CAUGHT IN A BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! BD: And Gallade takes control just like that. Yippee. MW: You seem so thrilled, Big Daddy. Gallade picks Osbourne up now, AND NAILS HIM WITH A CHOKESLAM!! That'll take something outta ya. And now Gallade picks up Osbourne again, BODYSLAM--BUT OSBOURNE COUNTERS WITH A SMALL PACKAGE! One..Two..Kickout! LB: That REALLY caught Gallade by surprise. MW: It caught ME by surprise too. But he's gonna try again. Gallade picks him up for a bodyslam, AND AGAIN gets caught with a small package! One..Two..Kickout! BD: I can't believe he fell for that TWICE IN A ROW! What a moron! MW: Now come on, Big Daddy, he's just trying to do all he can. Gallade is still better for wares of the two, and he picks Osbourne up, BACKSPIN DDT--COUNTERED WITH A BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX BY OSBOURNE! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Well Gallade just tried to do Osbourne's OWN finisher to him, and you can be sure that Osbourne knows all the counters for it, because he's gotta watch out for them himself. MW: That's for sure. Both men are up now, and Osbourne whips Gallade to the ropes! He comes back, and LEVELS Osbourne with a clothesline! Osbourne tries to get up, but Gallade takes him back down with a small package! One..Kickout! LB: This match has more swings than a pendulum! MW: You could say that. And now Gallade picks up the champion, and NAILS him with a belly-to-back suplex! Osbourne tries to get up again, AND IS SMALL PACKAGED AGAIN! One..Two..Kickout! BD: HE fell for the same move twice TOO! BOTH of these guys are morons! MW: On second thought, maybe you SHOULD take a nap, Big Daddy. Osbourne gets up now, and CHARGES at Gallade, who promptly BACKDROPS him! And now Gallade runs to the ropes. Osbourne just reached his feet, and Gallade LEVELS him with a clothesline! LB: Maybe Osbourne IS letting his shame affect him. MW: That's very possible. Osbourne tries to get up, and Gallade catches him with ANOTHER small package--BUT OSBOURNE REVERSES IT! One..Two..Kickout! BD: What do ya know? He actually caught on by the third time. MW: Yes he did, and now both men are to their feet, and Osbourne catches Gallade with a snap suplex! Gallade tries to get back to his feet, and Osbourne CHOPS him down! LB: Is this gonna end soon? MW: Just watch it, okay? And now Osbourne picks Gallade up, and NAILS him with another Hotshot! He props Gallade into the corner, and hits ANOTHER avalanche! Gallade STAGGERS out, and gets his legs swept right out from under him! BD: Maybe that'll knock him into the correct century. MW: And now Osbourne picks Gallade up, and NAILS him with a vertical suplex! Osbourne DROPS a headbutt onto the challenger. And now he picks Gallade up, and places him on the top turnbuckle! Osbourne climbs up himself, BELLY-TO-BACK SUPERPLEX! LB: That looked like it hurt a little. MW: Probably. And now Osbourne picks Gallade up, and he's calling for the end! He grabs Gallade by the head, AND NAILS THE BACKSPIN DDT!! The cover! One..Two..Thr..KICKOUT! BD: Looks like the Knight came here to fight. MW: He sure did, but technically, they're both Knights-- of Deliverance, that is. Osbourne can't believe it, but he picks up Gallade now, and hits an earringer; BUT GALLADE COUNTERS WITH A BACKDROP! Osbourne tries to get back up, but Gallade catches him by the neck, CHOKESLAM! LB: If this is what he does to his friends, I'm surprised he has any. MW: He's gotta pull all the stops in this one. It doesn't matter who he's fighting, he wants the gold. And now Gallade picks Osbourne up, and nails HIM with a backspin DDT! Osbourne slowly gets up, but Gallade sends him back DOWN with a clothesline! BD: He used Osbourne's finisher AGAIN! MW: Only this time, he hit it. No cover though; strange. And now Gallade NAILS Osbourne with an elbowsmash! Gallade picks him up, and hits a FACESLAM! LB: Just pin him already! MW: And now Gallade picks Osbourne up again, and he looks to be setting him up for a double underhook piledriver--BUT OSBOURNE COUNTERS WITH A BACKDROP--AND GALLADE COUNTERS THAT WITH A SUNSET FLIP! One..Two..Kickout! BD: He almost got him that time. MW: But Osbourne escaped, just in the nick of time. Gallade gets back up now, and Osbourne reaches his feet as well! Gallade runs to the rope, AND GETS CAUGHT SQUARE ON THE JAW WITH A SPINNING MULE KICK! LB: And the champion takes back control! MW: Gallade is really stunned from that blow! Osbourne picks him up now, POWERSLAM!! The cover! One..Two..Thr..Kickout! BD: They don't come any closer than that one. MW: No they don't. Osbourne picks him up now, and NAILS him with a kneelift! Gallade struggles to get back up, and Osbourne NAILS him with a back brain kick! LB: I didn't even know he knew that move! MW: Neither did I. Gallade tries to get up again, and gets FLOORED with a clothesline! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! BD: Another close call for Sir Gallahad. MW: That's Sir GALLADE. Osbourne picks Gallade up now, and whips him to the ropes--BUT GALLADE REVERSES! Osbourne comes back, and gets FLOORED with a clothesline! LB: But Sir Gall Bladder comes right back into it! MW: SIR GALLADE! Gallade picks Osbourne up now, and whips him to the ropes! Osbourne comes back, and Gallade catches him with a backdrop! Gallade picks him up off the mat, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX!! The cover! One..Kickout! BD: And Sir Gatorade's assault is only good enough for a one count! MW: THAT'S SIR GALLADE, MORONS! LB: Don't ya just love pissing him off? BD: Yeah, it's the best. MW: Now Gallade NAILS Osbourne with an elbowsmash! Gallade picks him up off the mat, BELLY-TO-BACK SUPLEX! Gallade picks Osbourne up again, AND ROLLS HIM INTO A SMALL PACKAGE! One..Two..Kickout! LB: We sure have seen an awful lot of small packages today. MW: Can't argue there. Gallade lifts Osbourne to his feet, and runs to the ropes! Gallade comes back, FLYING FOREARM--DUCKED BY OSBOURNE! And Gallade hits the mat hard! BD: It looked more like Osbourne collapsed than ducked to me. MW: That's quite possible. Osbourne slowly makes it to his feet, and now he picks up Sir Gallade, EARRINGER! That left Gallade hurting, and now Osbourne picks up Gallade again, AND NAILS THE BACKSPIN DDT!! The cover! One..Two..Foot on the Ropes! LB: And Gallade escapes the Backspin DDT for the SECOND time tonight! MW: Just barely. Without those ropes, he's a goner. Osbourne looks upset. He picks up Gallade now, and whips him to the ropes. Gallade comes back, and Osbourne puts his head down for a backdrop, BUT GALLADE PUTS ON THE BREAKS!! He grabs Osbourne's head, DDT!! BD: Great quick thinking! MW: That's what you've gotta have to be a champion in the AEWA. And now Gallade is going up top! He stands there and waits for Osbourne to get to his feet, AND HE ALMOST DECAPITATES OSBOURNE WITH THE GUILLOTINE!! LB: That's what Guillotines usually do. MW: The cover! One..Two..Three!!!! (DING DING) BD: I would be excited, but I really don't give a crap about either of these guys. RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of seventeen minutes and five seconds, here is your winner, and NEEEEEEEEEW AEWA Television Champion... SIR GALLADE!!!! MW: Boy, these titles are just changing hands all over the place! And Osbourne's up now, and he's congratulating the new champion! They share a big embrace, and this place just ERUPTS! LB: I think I'm gonna be sick. MW: Yet another FANTASTIC title matchup! This night just keeps getting better and better. BD: If you say so, I think it sucks. MW: Who asked ya? LB: So what's coming up now, oh overexcited one? MW: Hank Thompson is going to conduct an interview with President Freeman. BD: That should be thoroughly mind-numbing. MW: Just watch it, alright? BD: If I HAVE to. (The camera switches to Hank Thompson, waiting on the interview podium. He's holding his trusty microphone, and President Freeman is standing next to him.) Thompson: Ladies and gentlemen, I have a BIG interview for you tonight! Bob Freeman, I have some very important questions for you. First of all, what is going on between you and Kevin Larkin? Freeman: That situation has gotten totally out of control. Kevin seems to feel that I am interfering in his personal business-- I'm not, of course. Over the past few weeks, he has grown INCREASINGLY paranoid, and he's impossible to work with anymore. I'm currently talking with my fellow executives about getting him PERMANENTLY fired from the AEWA. It's finally come to that point. Thompson: Personally, I think you should have fired him a LONG time ago. He's become an embarrassment to this league. Freeman: I totally agree with you, Hank. He recently interfered during a World title match, and that's where I draw the line. A lot of the wrestlers are starting to get sick of Larkin, too. Thompson: How do you feel about the Visigothic Horde? Freeman: They're a couple of thugs, plain and simple. If it's up to me, they will NEVER be contenders to any AEWA titles-- ever. Fritigern and Athanaric are exactly what I DON'T want in the AEWA. Thompson: Is Shane Matthews doing okay? He was brutally attacked by the Horde on the last edition of Wednesday Night Rumble. Freeman: He looked okay on the Countdown show, so I think he'll be able to wrestle. If Kevin Larkin thought that little attack would stop Shane Matthews, he was wrong. I might not like Matthews very much, but he's a fighter. Thompson: Any final comments? Freeman: Yeah, I'd just like to make one thing clear-- _I_ run the AEWA. Kevin Larkin does not have ANY power at this point. If he EVER sends his goons after another AEWA wrestler, it's OVER. (Suddenly, two heads pop up from behind the interview platform. They are the heads of Fritigern and Athanaric, The Visigothic Horde! Fritigern grabs Freeman by the leg, and YANKS him down to the floor. Hank Thompson runs for cover.) MW: This is TOTALLY uncalled for! Those two barbarians are just POUNDING the living daylights out of President Freeman! I have no doubt that Kevin Larkin is behind this! LB: (laughing) Larkin would NEVER do a thing like this! MW: Like hell he wouldn't, I'll bet he's somewhere right now enjoying this carnage. Athanaric just picked up Freeman, AND THROWS HIM LIKE A JAVELIN INTO THE SIDE OF THE INTERVIEW PLATFORM! Someone has GOT to stop this! Freeman could be PERMANENTLY injured! BD: I think that's the idea. MW: Fritigern's got a chair now, and he is just REPEATEDLY BASHING it over the head of President Freeman! This is utter chaos! And NOW what are they doing? LB: It looks like Athanaric just went to get a table! MW: Yes he's got one, and he's bringing it back! Meanwhile, Fritigern has just TOSSED Freeman back onto the platform! Athanaric is now setting up the table, right next to the platform! BD: I think I know what they've got in mind, and it ain't gonna be pretty! MW: Now Fritigern is up on the platform with Freeman. He picks him up, and this DOESN'T look good! He lifts Freeman into the air, AND POWER BOMBS HIM DOWN THROUGH THE TABLE AND TO THE CONCRETE!! THESE MEN HAVE TO BE STOPPED! LB: Security's on the way, but the Horde hightail it outta here before they get caught! MW: But the damage has been done. They should be PERMANENTLY BANNED from the AEWA. This kind of stuff has NO place in wrestling. BD: I don't know. I kinda like it. MW: I know, but you're beyond help. And now the paramedics are tending to President Freeman, and things don't look good, fans. LB: Maybe that power bomb part was a bit much. MW: I'll say it was! Freeman could be VERY seriously injured! This is no laughing matter. Look how careful the paramedics are being! I've got a really bad feeling about this. And now they're taking him away, and we can only hope that he's alright. BD: That probably was excessive, but they made their point. MW: Which is? BD: Freeman CAN'T order Larkin around. Larkin was here before HE was, for cryin' out loud. MW: If this is how Larkin deals with his problems, then he is in no condition to be running a federation. I'm sorry we've gotta put this situation on hold for now, but there's really nothing we can do right now, and we've gotta go on with the show. LB: Where was Justin Trouble, anyway? Isn't he supposed to be Freeman's "bodyguard"? MW: I really have no clue where Justin was. Maybe he was still celebrating his victory earlier tonight, and didn't realize what's going on. In any case, we've got to move on. BD: So what's next then? ***************************************************************** TO BE CONTINUED IN PART SEVEN ***************************************************************** \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- American E-Wrestling Association The AEWA-- We Deliver!
