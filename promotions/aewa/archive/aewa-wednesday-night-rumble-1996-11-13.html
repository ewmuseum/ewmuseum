Wednesday Night Rumble (11/13)

15 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power Bomb, and a look at Mister Crowley DRIVING the head of some guy into the concrete with the Premature Burial! Then the camera switches to the Lovely Bertha and Hank Thompson in a packed arena) HT: Hello wrestling fans, and welcome to another edition of Wednesday Night Rumble! I'm Hank Thompson, and with me is my broadcast colleague The Lovely Bertha. LB: Well Hank, we've got some great action tonight. We're gonna see that slimeball "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt defend the Light Heavyweight title against Marc Thomas! Of course, Big Daddy STOLE the title from Rick Showboat, so this match shouldn't even be happening. HT: Well, we will have that match, and we'll also have a match for the World tag team championship between the former THREE-TIME AEWA World tag team champions The Graveyard Shift and the current champs The West Coast Connection. LB: (not even listening) You KNOW Hurt stole the belt from Showboat, don't you? It was as plain as day. He hit him with those brass knuckles. HT: I did see the match, but let's go now to the ring announcer for our first match here on Wednesday Night Rumble! RING ANNOUNCER: This contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA Light Heavyweight championship of the World! Introducing first, the challenger, weighing in at 240 pounds, here is MARC THOMAS! (Thomas makes his way toward the ring to a mixed reaction from the crowd. He is wearing a black sleeveless leather jacket.) HT: Thomas looks very determined tonight. LB: I really hope Thomas beats Big Daddy. "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt shouldn't be wearing that gold in the first place. He stole it! HT: You've mentioned that before. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 239 pounds, he is the AEWA Light Heavyweight champion of the World, here is "BIG DADDY" MIKE HURT! (Johnny Cash's "Ring Of Fire" plays loudly over the PA, and Mike Hurt makes his entrance to a small pop from the crowd. He has the belt strapped around his waist.) HT: Big Daddy looks pretty cocky tonight. I hope he's not taking Thomas too lightly here. LB: I think he is, and I hope Marc Thomas wins that title from him. Of course, Rick Showboat will win it back eventually. HT: It would be a tremendous win for Marc Thomas if he were to take the title tonight. Both men are in the ring now as the bell sounds (DING DING) Hurt's walking over to Thomas, and he's sticking a finger right in Thomas' face, just taunting him! LB: That's just the kind of punk that Mike Hurt is. He thinks he's the world's best wrestler just because he stole that belt from Rick Showboat. HT: Well, it looks like Thomas isn't going to take it, as he SHOVES Hurt back! And now Hurt SHOVES Thomas, and now takes him in a side headlock! Hurt backs Thomas into the corner, and makes a clean break! LB: He's just trying to lure Thomas into a false sense of security. Big Daddy will always cheat if he can. HT: Both men lock up again, and Hurt takes Thomas down with a BEAUTIFUL armdrag! LB: As much as I don't like Big Daddy, sometimes he can come up with a good move, but an armdrag's not gonna beat Thomas. HT: No it won't. Both men now lock up again, and Hurt takes Thomas down with a HIPTOSS! And now Hurt's mocking Thomas! Look at this! LB: See, what did I tell you, Big Daddy is nothing but scum and just wants to rub whatever he does in other people's faces. HT: I thought you liked those qualities. LB: Just not in him. HT: Well, no one seems to be able to get an upperhand here, so both men lock up again, and Hurt takes down Thomas with a fireman's carry! Again, Hurt takes down Thomas and mocks him! But Thomas gets up and NAILS Hurt with a BIG right hand! LB: That's what I want to see out of Thomas. HT: Now Thomas is taunting Hurt! Hurt, though, puts a knee into Thomas' midsection and retakes control of the match! Hurt puts Thomas back into another side headlock, but Thomas takes Hurt and WHIPS him to the ropes! Thomas goes down, now up with a leapfrog, now down again.... LB: C'mon, SOMEBODY do a move in there! HT: As Thomas gets back to his feet, Hurt stops and picks him up with a BODYSLAM! Hurt now going for another bodyslam, but Thomas goes over the top and does a bodyslam of his own---BUT HIS KNEES BUCKLED UNDERNEATH HIM! Hurt goes for the cover! One...Two...Kickout! LB: Thomas was lucky to get out of that one, but I'm sure he'll still be able to beat Big Daddy. HT: Now Hurt brings Thomas back up, and nails him with a HUGE chop! Thomas falls down to the mat and Hurt LAYS in a couple of kicks to Thomas' head! LB: Where's Rick Showboat? We need a better challenger in there for Hurt. HT: Hurt seems to be having his way with Thomas right now. Hurt picks Thomas up and delivers a STANDING DROPKICK to Thomas! Thomas now is rolling out of the ring. LB: Maybe he can regroup on the outside, come back in, and whoop Hurt's tail. HT: Thomas now coming back into the ring---and gets met by two FOREARMS to the back! Thomas staggers back into the corner, but he KICKS Hurt right in the midsection! LB: Now we're gonna see some offense out of Thomas. HT: I don't know about that. Hurt comes back with a RIGHT HAND to Thomas, but Thomas comes right back with one of his own! Thomas is trying to turn it around, and rakes the eyes of "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt! LB: That's what Thomas has to do-- give Big Daddy a taste of his own medicine! HT: Thomas whips Hurt into the ropes, and delivers a BRUTAL clothesline! Thomas connects with an ELBOWDROP to Hurt, and follows it up with ANOTHER elbowdrop! LB: Marc Thomas is on a roll now! He looks like he's going for a DDT! HT: He is! He's hooking it---but Hurt takes him down, and the referee is knocked down in the process! Both men are slugging it out now on the mat, but Thomas seems to be getting the BETTER of it! Thomas picks Hurt up, and DROPS HIM WITH "THE ICEBREAKER" MOVE! Thomas has got the cover---but the ref's still out! LB: C'mon ref, wake up! HT: Thomas is now going over to the ref and---OH NO! Hurt just pulled out those brass knuckles that helped him win the Light Heavyweight championship! LB: No, Marc, don't turn around! HT: Thomas is going back to Hurt, and GETS NAILED WITH THOSE BRASS KNUCKLES! LB: No, this isn't fair! Thomas was on such a roll, and he even had "the Icebreaker" on Hurt! HT: The ref's waking up now, and Big Daddy is going for the cover.... One...Two...Three! (DING DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner and STILL AEWA Light Heavyweight champion, "BIG DADDY" MIKE HURT! HT: Hurt conveniently put those brass knuckles back in his tights before the ref woke up. LB: Somebody's gonna end "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt's title reign, and that somebody's gonna be Rick Showboat. HT: I'm sure we'll be seeing that match down the road soon, and it may well end up with the result that Bertha just predicted. LB: I know it will. HT: I think we're about ready for our next match here on Rumble, as the West Coast Connection put their World tag team titles on the line against the Graveyard Shift! LB: There's no doubt in my mind that The West Coast Connection will walk out of here with those tag belts still strapped around their waists. HT: Why's that? LB: Because they'll do whatever they have to do to win. HT: (half smiling) That's the Bertha we all know and love. Let's go to the ring! RING ANNOUNCER: This contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA World tag team championship! Introducing first, the challengers, at a combined weight of 731 pounds, they are former three-time World tag team champions, here are THE GRAVEYARD SHIFT! (The Mortician and Grave Dweller walk toward the ring to a BIG pop from the crowd. Their manager, Coffin, follows closely behind.) HT: The fans LOVE these guys. They could very easily walk out of this building as World tag team champions for an unprecedented fourth time. LB: I highly doubt that. The West Coast Connection is one of the best teams to come along in the AEWA's history. They'll beat these chumps. No problem. HT: We'll see about that. Now let's hear the intro for the World tag team champions. RING ANNOUNCER: And their opponents, at a combined weight of 524 pounds, here are the reigning AEWA World tag team champions, THE WEST COAST CONNECTION! ("Tonight, Tonight" begins playing loudly, as Axl Sharkman and MC Read make their entrance. They are proudly waving the championship belts around, and the crowd boos loudly.) HT: I don't know who looks more ready, the Shift or the Connection. LB: The West Coast Connection does. HT: What are your reasons? Actually, I'm really not in the mood for a lame explanation, so let's just watch this match. Both teams are deciding who should start out this contest, and it's looks like it will be The Mortician against MC Read. Both men lock up, and Read puts The Mortician in a side headlock! The Mortician turns it around and WHIPS Read into the ropes, though, and ARMDRAGS Read as he comes off the ropes! And now ANOTHER armdrag to Read! Read gets back up and gets put into an armbar! LB: The Graveyard Shift appears to be getting off to a fast start in this one, but I have it on authority that The Connection were out late last night partying, so they've got an excuse. HT: They won't have an excuse if they lose those titles. The Mortician drops a BIG forearm to Read in the armbar! Read, though, kicks The Mortician right in the midsection! Read now makes the tag to Sharkman! LB: Now we'll see what the champs are gonna do to The Graveyard Shift. I say they annihilate them. HT: Well, The Mortician just kicked Sharkman right in the midsection, so annihilation doesn't look like it's on the horizon. The Mortician takes Sharkman into a hammerlock, and makes the tag to Grave Dweller! Grave Dweller comes in and drops a BIG double axehandle onto the arm of Sharkman! Dweller takes up on the armbar now. LB: The Graveyard Shift seem to be working on the arms of The West Coast Connection. I wonder if they actually thought this out before the match. HT: Why wouldn't they? LB: Well, these two aren't exactly rocket scientists. HT: Back in the ring now, Grave Dweller whips Sharkman into the ropes, and goes for a BACKDROP---but Sharkman reverses it into a DDT! LB: Nice move by Sharkman. That's why these guys are the champs. HT: It certainly was a nice move, and Sharkman seems to know it as he's going around and gesturing to all of the fans. Grave Dweller meanwhile has gotten up and is now BEHIND Sharkman! Grave Dweller picks up Sharkman by his neck in a chokehold and is CARRYING him around the ring like a rag doll! Sharkman can't seem to get out of it---but there he did with a KICK TO THE GROIN! LB: A very smart move by Sharkman. Win if you can, lose if you must, but always cheat. HT: Wow, you're really original. Sharkman goes over and makes the tag to Read, who comes in and DROPKICKS Grave Dweller! An impressive move on a man of Grave Dweller's size! LB: You seem to act like The West Coast Connection doesn't know how to wrestle. How do you think they won those World tag team titles then? HT: I know how they won those belts, but some of their means are unnecessary. LB: They get the job done. HT: I guess so. Read is in the ring now with Grave Dweller, and Read BODYSLAMS him! That had plenty of impact. LB: Another impressive move. HT: Read now is going to the top rope! He comes off and NAILS A FLYING HEADBUTT! He's going for the cover! One...Two...Kickout! HT: There was still a little too much fight left in Grave Dweller there. Read knows it and he goes over to tag Sharkman---but he forgot about Grave Dweller, who gets to his corner and makes the tag to The Mortician! The Mortician comes into the ring and goes to town on both Sharkman and Read, UNLOADING with lefts and rights to both of their heads! The Mortician whips Sharkman, the legal man, to the ropes and POWERSLAMS him! He's going for the cover! One...Two...Kickout! LB: Both teams want to get out of this ring with those World tag team title belts strapped around their waists. HT: They sure do, but only one team can have them. The Mortician comes off the ropes, and goes for a JUMPING CLOTHESLINE---but Sharkman ducks and The Mortician falls to the canvas! LB: Brains and ability of The West Coast Connection. That's what go them to the top. They don't need any managers. HT: They're certainly trying to prove that tonight. Sharkman is going to the top turnbuckle, but I don't think he got The Mortician groggy enough to do that. The Mortician's up! He hits Sharkman with a VICIOUS right hand to the midsection while Sharkman's on that top turnbuckle, and ANOTHER one! LB: Luckily for Sharkman, and his date after the match, he didn't injure any "vital" organs on that top turnbuckle. HT: No he didn't, but he's in trouble anyway. The Mortician climbs those ropes to the top turnbuckle, and now he appears to be setting Sharkman up for something! It looks like a belly-to-belly superplex! HE HIT IT! HE HIT THE BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPERPLEX! Here's the COVER! One...Two...Three! (DING DING) LB: I can't believe this! A belly-to-belly superplex and now The Graveyard Shift have won the World tag belts for the FOURTH time! RING ANNOUNCER: Here are your winners and NEW AEWA World tag team champions, THE GRAVEYARD SHIFT! HT: This crowd is going nuts as the new champs raise their belts above their heads! LB: This is the kind of thing that makes me sick. HT: Hopefully it's not "Crazy Canuck"-kind of sick. LB: I hope not too. HT: Well, we've had a GREAT show tonight, but it's over. We're completely out of time. So this is Hank Thompson, saying good night and we'll see you next time! LB: Adios, folks.
