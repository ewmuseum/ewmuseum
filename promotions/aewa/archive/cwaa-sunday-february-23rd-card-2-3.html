Sunday February 23rd Card (2/3)

23 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EL: Earlier this week, some of you may have seen CWAA star "Hardcore" Joey Haven here on WYXZ Channel 39's own "Talkin' Texan with Sky Whatley", a popular program airing Wednesday evenings. Let's take a quick look at how Joey Haven's visit went. ********************************************************** ["Talkin' Texan with Sky Whatley", jip] Whatley: All right, we're back here with Talkin' Texan. My next guest beats up people for a livin'. Seriously, he is a current star in the CWAA, folks, help me in welcoming, "Hardcore" Joey Haven!!! [Haven walks out as the crowd applauds. He is wearing jeans, a CWAA T-Shirt and a black leather jacket with the word "Hardcore" on the back] Whatley: Joey, welcome to Talkin Texan! Haven: Its great to be here; I am kind of nervous, though. Whatley: Don't worry about it. First let me say, I hear you are from Philly, how is it converting from Philly to here in Texas? Haven: Its funny, people here don't feel they have an accent, but ya' do. It's hard, because I am used to cold winters, warm summers. Its pretty warm here all the time. I came here looking for a role model- guess I won't be going to any Cowboys games. [Crowd Laughs] Haven: The people here are great and I enjoy being a Texan for now. Whatley: What makes you want to wrestle? Haven: I don't know, its the challenge of outsmarting someone, but also being tougher. I mean, if you show someone that they can't beat you by using your head and they are bigger then you, that says a lot. Wrestling to me is fun, and I enjoy it immensely. There is no greater high then smashing a guy over the head with a chair! (Crowd laughs, but a little nervously) Whatley: Now, I see you will be facing "Preacher" Kore Wrench this Sunday? Haven: I have no problem with him personally, but like someone once said, Haven 3:16 says I am going to whoop your <beep> and come this Sunday, I will show you, Jak Hammer, and the whole CWAA, why they call me....Hardcore!!! Whatley: Joey, thanks for joining us. Haven: No problem! (Exit Haven) Whatley: OK- up next Slim Bolton is gonna show us his Amazin' Three Ring Flea Circus, as those mighty mites perform death-defyin' feats for all of us, right after this! ******************************************************************** ("Highway to Hell" starts up and the crowd pops vociferously for "Hardcore" Joey Haven, the 5'10"-245 Philadelphia native with a small bandage on his forehead from the previous week's exertions, who provides the gratuitous hand-slaps that so delight those fans who happen to be stationed near the aisle.) EL: Joey Haven, who is coming off a contest unlike any seen here in the world-famous Sportatorium in years, makes his way to the ring. It may not have been a good idea to agree to that no-disqualification encounter just a week before taking his first crack at a title opportunity. It should be a different type of match up this week, as he squares off against "The Preacher", Kore Wrench. (Right on cue, Gregorian Chant begins to pipe in over the pa system, and the crowd again applauds as the Preacher's 6'5"-275 lb. frame appears at the head of the aisle. On his way to the ring, he pauses to bless a small first-row child.) EL: We've seen the Preacher intercede successfully between Max Robertson and Randy Sheradin- maybe we could get him to sit down with Joey Haven and Jak Hammer. Just so long as I don't have to be present, of course. Joey looks to be itching to start, but the Preacher has all of that religious paraphernalia to remove first. OK- he looks to be just about done- there's the bell. A quick handshake between the two, and off we go. They lock up- fireman's carry takedown by Wrench, into a headlock. He stands back up, still with the headlock on Haven. You know, the Preacher has some very strong statements to make about the AEWA's Church of the Pounding Light recently. Joey Haven tries to push off, but Wrench just tightens the hold. Well, the Church has been an important part of the AEWA World Light Heavyweight Title scene- Joey Haven slips out of the headlock and puts the Preacher in a hammerlock- but it's the Preacher reversing it right back into a side headlock. The Holy Ghost, specifically, has worn that belt on two occasions now, and, it is official, the AEWA World Light Heavyweight Title will be defended here in the Sportatorium exactly one month from today. If the Holy Ghost walks in here wearing the gold and surrounded by his Church co-horts, you can bet that Kore Wrench will be around to make sure the Church doesn't try anything. Joey Haven now pushes off, and the Preacher is sent to the ropes- shoulderblock by Wrench knocks Haven over. Haven back up as the Preacher comes off the ropes again- leapfrog by Haven- Haven with a hip toss- now a dropkick. Nice elevation by Joey Haven as he puts the Preacher back on the mat. As Wrench comes back up, there's Joey Haven with a full nelson. He has the fingers locked, pushing forward on Wrench's neck. We have to take another break right now, but when we come back, we'll bring you the conclusion of this exciting matchup. (Pizza Pizza.) EL: OK- right now you see Kore Wrench has Joey Haven on the mat in an armbar- here's what you missed while away. ***************************** (Haven has Wrench in a full nelson, and Wrench, one of the most powerful men in the CWAA, simply muscles his way out of the hold, jabs an elbow in Haven's midsection and hiptosses him over, locking him in an armbar, which brings us back to the present. All while a little guy in a toga and garland tries to get you to buy crazybread.) *************************** EL: Wrench takes Haven back up onto his feet, twists that arm again- short clothesline! The Preacher nearly smacked Joey Haven right out of his boots with that one! Lateral press... 1... 2... kickout. Wrench picks him back up, Irish whip- kick in the midsection, and Haven is doubled over. Kore Wrench now sets him up- powerbomb coming up- he planted him. Kore Wrench signifying that the Crucifix slam is coming up. And- hey! Jak Hammer on his way to the ring, now. Wrench has Haven up- no, he just dropped him and is on his way to intercept Hammer. Jak Hammer takes a swing at the Preacher, and Wrench staggers back. The referee is calling for the bell- and Jak Hammer with a kick now on Wrench, and he dumps him out of the ring. Joey Haven is back on his feet- he has no idea what's going on. Hammer grabs him, sends him for the ride- he caught him- he has him in that Rebel Rack backbreaker! The bell continues to ring- and the Preacher is back in the ring, and he has a boot for Hammer's midsection, right in those ribs that were taped up after that incident last week, and he drops Joey Haven. Kore Wrench now with a flurry of punches driving Jak Hammer back into the corner. Hammer wanted a piece of "Hardcore" Joey Haven, but he's let himself in for some fire and brimstone instead! Joey Haven in there now- he and the Preacher whip Jak Hammer into the ropes- double clothesline! Hammer flips over the top rope, and- ow! Lands right on those ring steps which he had come into such harsh contact with last week! And he hits the ground running, with Joey Haven and Kore Wrench after him! In the meantime, we've just got out official decision, and it is Kore Wrench by disqualification, since it was he whom Jak Hammer initially hit. I guess Jak Hammer was looking for Joey Haven, but Kore Wrench got in the way, so Joey Haven not only gets trapped in that Rebel Rack, but he loses the first round match, to boot! It will be Kore Wrench and Randy Sheradin now in the second round, as the Dreamer faces the man who reconciled him with Max Robertson. Next- oh, no. (Enter, stage left, Major Victor Augustus Mayhem) EL: (exasperated) Can I help? MVM: (after examining the ring steps and measuring by foot the distance from the end of the aisle to the ring) Sssh! EL: Major, exactly what is it you're doing? MVM: Reconnaissance mission, my boy. Reconnaissance mission. EL: I... see. (X's "You Got the Power" plays, and applause greets San Jose's favorite son, Stan "The Powerhouse" Stevens, whose 6'7"-335 lb. blue-spandexed power lifting body ambles down the aisle.) EL: Stan Stevens on his way down, now, and Major Mayhem is in the wrong place at the wrong time... (MVM, standing in the aisleway, gets in Stevens' face, prodding him in the middle of the chest with an index finger to punctuate his points, which appear to about Stevens being unsuitable for Mayhem's old crack hand-to-hand combat team, but the mike can't pick things up too clearly from the aisle.) EL: I wouldn't do that if I were him. Then again, if I were the Major, there are a lot of things I wouldn't do. Go on living, for starters. (MVM continues sputtering, and Stevens looks to the crowd, who egg him on, and he grabs the scrawny major by the throat and lifts him two feet of the ground, as the crowds erupts in a frenzy of approval.) EL: The major is finding out just how "suitable" Stan Ste- look out! Lt. Lawson just came out at a dead sprint and clipped the legs of the Powerhouse. I smell a set-up, and now Lawson is stomping away at the fallen Stevens there in the aisle, and the Major is busy trying to get his breath back. Now Lawson checks on his commanding officer, and they both are headed for the ring. Lawson now berating the official- I think he wants him to start the match, but Stan Stevens is back in the aisleway clutching at his leg. And, well, here comes the Major back to join me again. Major, are you all right? MVM: (clutching at his throat, whispers) Achh.. I can't.. achh. EL: Such a pity, Major. Well, the referee and some members of the medical crew are looking over Stan Stevens. Lawson waits in the ring; Major, what do you have to say for yourself? MVM: He... (points to his own throat)... unfair... EL: Well, that was just about the mo- wait, the referee is informing us that the match will not be started just yet, but that it will be postponed until the end of tonight's card, to give Stevens a chance to recover. (The crew escort the limping Stevens to the locker area). (Lawson storms around the ring, kicking the ropes, yelling at the crowd to shut up, etc.) MVM: Achh... achh. (Exeunt Major and Lt.) EL: The sad thing is that the delay is likely to give the Major a chance to recover his voice, as well. In the meantime, we are moving on to Jak Hammer and Travis Conley, in what was scheduled to be the last first round match-up. (The "Twister" theme plays as Conley and Mother Nature enter, sans Paul Langford. Travis is a young ex-Husker lineman of the 6'8"-315 variety, and, as always, sports the Twister logo on his brown trunks. The blue-eyed boy of the CWAA seems genuinely excited about the positive response he receives. As Travis waits in the ring, the heavily-taped-up-in-the-midsection Jak Hammer emerges, accompanied by the opening chords of "Bad to the Bone". At 6'10"-310, Hammer offers Conley an even match, physically, but the response he elicits from the crowd is very, very different. As others have done before and no doubt will do again, Hammer shows more interest in talking than in starting the match, and heads over to Eric Lane.) EL: Jak, how do you feel about tonight's match against Travis Conley? JH: Travis is a big man, but he can't expect to use that as an advantage, since I'm even bigger than he is. Also, he's used to having a partner to bail his sorry butt out when he gets in trouble. There aren't any partners in singles wrestling and if he thinks his partner is going to help him, then he's got another thing coming. I'm an equal opportunity pain inflictor! Your partner interferes, then he gets whooped, too! Haven, yeah, you, ya big idiot, you. (The crowd reacts to the mention of his name with a nice little pop, as though he had arrived on the scene.) JH: I've already humiliated you twice and now I've taken away your shot at a title. So go back home to Philly and cry about it to someone who cares. If wrestling was for crybaby losers like yourself, then Deion Sanders would be the World Champion. Wrestling ain't for wimps!!! Watch this match I have with Conley to see how a real man wrestles. You call yourself "Hardcore." The only hard thing I've seen is that abnormal growth on your neck. You just don't seem to get it, do ya! I'm toying with you!! I could have ended your career in our first match, but I thought you might actually be good competition. What a mistake that was!! Now I've got an a** to kick and if you decide to show your ugly mug tonight, then the fans might get two whoopings for the price of one! (Jak makes his way to the ring.) EL: OK- we have to take another break- back in a sec! (This week on "Talkin' Texan with Shy Whatley", the physical and mental rigors of a day in the life of a rodeo clown school student, and exciting new recipes for raspberry jam.) EL: We're back, and underway! Conley offers a test of strength- this would be an interesting encounter Jak Hammer with a boot in the gut, instead! Now a double sledge- and again, and again! Jak know with a right hand to the head, and Conley is on his knees. A bite on the forehead by Jak Hammer- that was disgusting! Jak Hammer now- snap suplex brings Conley right back over. Jak Hammer now with an elbow drop. Now he locks Conley in a hammerlock, and rests all of his weight on one knee on the back of Conley's neck. Joey Haven has already been eliminated thanks to Hammer's interference- he must be pulling hard for Conley in this one. And Hammer is pulling hard on that hammerlock. (Your typical "Go, Travis, Go!" chant picks up steam) EL: Hammer releases it, rolls Conley over- off the ropes- kneedrop, flush in the forehead. The cover... 1... 2... kickout. Hammer now whips him into the corner, and Conley's massive frame slams into the turnbuckles. Hammer adjusting that fingerless glove, and now he slaps Travis Conley, right in the face. And again! Conley's fuse is a short one, and I think it's just been lit... big right hand by Travis! And another, and Jak Hammer is forced to retreat! Conley now backs him into the corner, and now he's going to work on those ribs. Last week, we saw Joey Haven drop Jak Hammer onto the ring steps, gut first, and Jak Hammer looks real, real sensitive right there. Cross corner whip- and Jak Hammer staggers out of the corner- Travis Conley with a huge clotheslines; you know the impact is big when the man throwing it leaves his feet to do so! (Crowd pops appreciatively) EL: Conley picks him back up- pushes him into the corner- shoulderblock. He drives his shoulder right into those sore ribs- and again! Jak Hammer is obvious pain this week, as the rigors of last week's no-disqualification match have taken their toll on both men this week. Now Conley going for the big finish- his powerbomb! He can't quite get him up though- Jak Hammer kicking away furiously to halt the move. Now it's Jak Hammer extending- back body drop. Hammer off the ropes as Conley gets up- clothesline! Hammer now choking him on the top rope, and the ref starts counting. 4 and he breaks it off. A rake of the eyes now by Hammer, and mother Nature is up on the apron hollering to the referee about it. Irish whip - he catches Conley in a powerslam. How did he manage that with that rib injury? Now Jak Hammer leaves Conley and heads over to Mother Nature who's still up on the apron urging her charge on. Hammer seems to be threatening her in some way- he can't seriously intend to assault a woman less than half his size! If he is, she's not afraid- she's right up in his face arguing right back. He raises his hand as if to show he's not too much of a man to strike her- and she slaps him right in the face! Now he grabs a handful of her hair- and she shoves him with both hands! He staggers back- and Conley rolls him up as he falls! 1... 2... 3! (Crowd pops deliriously as the "Twister" theme kicks in again) EL: Travis Conley has just pinned Jak Hammer, and credit the assist to Mother Nature! That's what you get for messing with Mother Nature, I suppose. Jak Hammer is just beside himself as Travis heads up the aisle soaking in the cheers. When we come back, our special feature match between Double Jeopardy, two men very family with Mr. Conley, and the team of Healer's Havoc and Big Dog Brown.
