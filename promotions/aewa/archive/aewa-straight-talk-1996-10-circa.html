Straight Talk (circa October)

21 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera fades in on the AEWA Studios, the AEWA logo falls onto the screen, and the words "STRAIGHT TALK" slide in underneath it. The whole logo then tilts, and falls off the bottom of the screen. Behind the desk in studio are Ken Wilson and.... Johnny Terror?!) KW: Hello peons! It is now your pleasure to be watching me, Ken Wilson, the new HOST of Straight Talk! And here is my new co-host, one of the greatest managers in wrestling today, Johnny Terror! JT: It's about time that they got rid of that IDIOT, Chuck Carlson, isn't it Ken? KW: It sure is. Now all they need to do is get rid of my brother Matt, Skim Hoss, and Hank Thompson, and we'll have a perfect broadcast team! VOICE OFF CAMERA: Uh, guys? KW: That's just our producer. (to producer) What do ya want? We're trying to conduct a show here! PRODUCER: I just thought I'd tell you that Chuck's not gone. JT: He's NOT?? PRODUCER: No, he just can't do the show this week because he's still hurt from being attacked on the Hall of Fame show. KW: Does that mean I'm NOT the new host? JT: And I'm NOT the new co-host? PRODUCER: Yep. KW: Well that sucks. JT: It sure does. KW: While we get over this bit of news, why don't you morons just watch this interview with our *shudder* World Champion, Mike Stewart. ---------- (The camera opens on "Superman" Mike Stewart and Michelle Anderson walking into the dressing room after Mega Combat. "Eye Of The Tiger" is still playing in the background and in the locker room are friends of Stewart, including Kyle Hatcher, The New Gods, and Lance Stevens. They start to chant "Superman! Superman!" as Stewart walks in and also start to pour champagne all over the place. Stewart and Anderson both have huge smiles on their faces and the AEWA World title is around Stewart's waist) Stewart: All right, guys. Thanks for the little party back here. I'd first of all like to thank the AEWA for giving me the shot at the World title tonight, and I'm glad I made the most of it. (everyone cheers) There are some people I'd like to thank. First off, I'd like to thank the fans everywhere around the world for their support. Secondly, I'd like to thank the woman who gave me the desire and inspiration to win this title, my beautiful manager and partner in life, Michelle. (Stewart and Anderson kiss and then Stewart grabs Anderson and hoists her up on his shoulder) This title belongs to you as much as it does to me, hon. (Stewart hands Anderson the World title) Now I know there are going to be a lot of people who are going to stay that I didn't deserve this shot. Well, if those guys want to take me on, I'm not going anywhere. I'll defend the title against anyone anywhere. I've also got something to say to Deathwatch, his buddy Heavy Metal, and their friends. Boys, your little takeover attempt of the AEWA ended before it got started. Now that I've got the title, none of you will disgrace the AEWA. Count on it. Now if you'll excuse me; the guys, myself, and my lovely girlfriend have a little partying to do. (Everyone cheers as Stewart lets Anderson down, gives her another kiss, then hoists her back up on his shoulder again as everyone continues to pop champagne and party.) ---------- JT: What a DISGUSTING scene. KW: I know. Everyone was just all happy, and cheery, and then you have Stewart thanking his girlfriend and his fans-- it just makes me sick. JT: Don't worry, Heavy Metal's gonna come looking for his title VERY soon, and this time Stewart WON'T catch him by surprise. KW: I couldn't agree more. Stewart won't be able to HANDLE having that title; he'll lose it in NO time. JT: That's for sure, he's gonna have everybody in the AEWA gunning for him. Frankly, I don't think he can take the pressure. KW: I know he can't. And now for some comments from a true champion that we can all look up to, let's hear from our North American Champion, The Grim Reaper! ---------- (The Grim Reaper stands next to Rick Verrano on a foggy "River Styx"-like setting. His scythe is over one shoulder, his North American title is around the other) Rick Verrano: The Grim Reaper is still the AEWA North American Champion. No surprise there, Raekwon should be called RaeTARD after that shoddy display in Japan. The Angels of Death are the most feared, and respected stable in the history of this federation. Soon, we will have more gold and then we will have our place in the annals of all wrestling federations. Big Ron Raider, it's too bad you have to face the Reaper this week, but that's death, right? (chuckles) As for the alliance between Sir Gallade and Slocum, come on fellas, where's my Six man Tag match? The Angels want to brush away you flies as soon as possible, and all you got to do is sign. Or have the pains of fear scared you off? (fade to black) ---------- JT: Yeah, where the hell is that six man tag match? KW: You know what happened-- Ben Suckum and Sir Gatorade chickened out. JT: Jeez, it's just like those wussie little fan favorites to do something like that. KW: It sure is. They should just all be banned from the AEWA, then we would only have real wrestlers. JT: What a great idea, Ken! You and me should be on the AEWA Board of Directors. KW: Nah, I think that one of the requirements of being on the board is that you gotta be like totally biased towards all the goodie-goodies. This job is much better. Now let's just watch this interview with Wild Thing. ---------- (We see Wild Thing in the locker room, and he looks VERY upset. His entire body is covered in scabs and scars. He is holding a bloody razor blade, and he begins to slowly cut a chunk out of his injured arm.) Wild Thing: It feels so good. Heavy Metal, you tried to kill me at Mega Combat, but you didn't finish the job. My arm may be broken, but it really doesn't matter. I don't feel any pain, you son of a bitch. The drugs have numbed my body, so NOTHING can hurt me. Now listen to me, Heavy Metal. When I wrestle you, I will FINALLY end our feud. The end is near for you, boy-- and payback's a BITCH. (Fade to black) ---------- JT: That Wild Thing is one sick bastard. KW: He sure is, that's why he's so cool! JT: I know. It's gonna be NUTS when him and Heavy Metal finally get in the ring together. KW: If he makes it to the ring this time. JT: Good point. KW: And now let's hear from Joey Valachi. ---------- (We see "Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi in his dressing room, holding his toy WWF belt. He is wearing a fake beard and glasses.) Valachi: If there's one thing they don't teach you at the Harvard Business School, it's who you shouldn't do business with. Italian Loan Collection Agency, you made it clear there's nothing I can say or do to get you off my back. So I'm not running anymore. (takes off fake beard and glasses.) I just realized you guys never actually wrestle. Makes me think you can't. Whatever people say about me, I DO get in the ring, and I DO win. So I'm gonna get me a partner, who ACTUALLY wrestles, and we're gonna kick your butts. Now comes the part I can't believe I'm doing. Juan Lopez, you came to the AEWA trying to make your rep off of me. Talking like you were somehow worth my time. We've been back and forth more times than I can count. But me and the Flying Liberian have one thing in common. We HATE those freaking loansharks. So Lopez, what I want is this-- One time, and one time only, you and me take those punks on in the ring. After that, I go right back to kicking your butt, just like I always do, but first, you and I take care of our mutual problem. What do you say, Lopez? I'm asking you nice and everything. (Valachi walks off, muttering "I can't believe I just did that.") ---------- JT: I can't believe he just did that either! Valachi and LOPEZ against my Italian Loan Collection Agency? He's gotta be outta his freakin' mind! KW: I know. I can't believe that he would actually stoop so low as to tag with that stupid illegal immigrant. So do your boys accept, Johnny? JT: You're damn right we accept, and Valachi, this is the biggest mistake you EVER made! I just hope the ring attendants can manage to scoop up what's left of you two. KW: Well, that should be interesting-- Valachi and Lopez against the ILCA. When would you want the match to happen, Johnny. JT: I don't know, and I don't CARE. All I know is that Valachi and Lopez are in for the beating of their lives! KW: Well put. Now let's hear from Deathwatch's newly formed stable, International Contracts Unlimited! ---------- (The camera opens on Heavy Metal in the locker room directly after his match at Mega Combat. He has an EXTREMELY upset look on his face and is lacking his world title. Deathwatch is behind him as Metal sits on the bench, and Deathwatch pats him on the back and rubs his shoulders. Penance sits in a chair off to the side and has a worried look on his face. Heavy Metal's hair is hanging in the front of his face and he lowers his head; he talks with an extremely deep voice that sounds rough and scratchy) Heavy Metal: (takes in a deep breath) Let.... me.... tell you SOMETHING! (he screams viciously and stands up, so Deathwatch backs off) STEWART! (closes his eyes for a second) You should NOT have even BEEN here tonight! You STOLE what was rightfully mine, and you DESERVE another ass kicking. There won't be any rocking this time. Oh no, you have pissed me off BEYOND that point. I have NEVER been this upset in my ENTIRE career! (Heavy Metal lets out a violent scream again and SLAMS his fist through a locker door. It dents in and you can see the hand print left in it. Penance falls out of his chair at the sight and scurries over near Deathwatch) Heavy Metal: This will NOT be forgotten without a fitting punishment! ARGHHHH! (Deathwatch walks over to Heavy Metal, and puts his gloved hand on Metal's shoulder. He sits him down on the bench again and begins to speak) Deathwatch: Let us not get ahead of ourselves, Metal. I have my end to share, and this is not to Mike Stewart, this is to the AEWA BOARD! (Deathwatch thinks to himself for a brief moment and laughs silently) AEWA, I am not allowing this to go unpunished, you see.... I have Heavy Metal's contract, RIGHT here. Gehringer comes out there, and states that Metal HAS to fight Mike Stewart, and that's not fair. Well Gehringer, you are sadly mistaken, I am bringing the law into this one, and I can assure you.... I AM THE LAW! I have taken schooling in every kind of law known to man, and there will be justice brought upon this blatant injustice act! Now, here is my proposition.... No, this is a DEMAND! You will IMMEDIATELY re-instate Heavy Metal of his title or I am SUING the AEWA and reprimanding ALL of it's board. Mister Freeman, you will do as I say. I am not as polite as Cardinal Chaos was the other night, I am not afraid to kill you and I will do so if these actions are not taken immediately. The fine for this is more than anything you will ever see.... EVER. I am suing for twenty MILLION dollars if these actions are not placed under immediate circumstances. Penance: I haven't been here long since my return, in fact, it was ONE FREAKING DAY! And I see the same exact CRAP that I saw when I left. I don't want titles, that's not why I am here, but when I see something wrong, I take ACTION! International Contracts Unlimited is not going to just stand by and watch things such as this happen anymore. That is why we are here, to teach everyone JUSTICE! Deathwatch: And do think I am STILL making empty threats? Well I'm not, so you had all better realize just what I am here to do! (Deathwatch RIPS his mask off and stares into the camera with his blood red eyes and his sadistic smile) Deathwatch: Gehringer.... Freeman.... Larkin.... You are my targets now. No more cheap thrills for you, nothing, not a DAMN single thing. Cardinal Chaos is NOTHING compared to the power that I hold. NOT A THING! When I am through with all of you, this federation will be just as I said-- BROKEN! (Heavy Metal stands up from the bench; calmed down. He gently pushes Deathwatch aside and looks into the camera) Heavy Metal: (takes in another deep breath) This is only the beginning. I joined with Deathwatch because I KNEW he hated the AEWA, and guess what? So do I! I have never liked the AEWA, and I NEVER will. Cheap matches, CHEAP results; this damn federation is just a cheap piece of CRAP! (Heavy Metal walks over to a locker) See this? (He TEARS it off and holds it up) CRAP! (picks the chair that Penance was sitting in up and shows it to the camera and smiles) And this? (he SLAMS it over his knee and it breaks in half) CRAP! But the AEWA is WORSE than CRAP-- It's TOTAL SHIT! (Exodus storms into the locker room and throws a piece of paper in front of Deathwatch's face) Exodus: (out of breath) Here ya go, Deathwatch.... *huff* *puff* They wanted me to deliver this notice to you.... *huff* *puff* (Deathwatch takes the sheet of paper and reads it over. He then gets an angry look on his face, and an evil glare in his eyes) Deathwatch: Oh.... You are not going to like this, Metal, my friend. It says that you will be facing Wild Thing. Heavy Metal: (raises an eyebrow and smiles) Heh Heh Heh.... so what's the BAD part? Deathwatch: It also says that Mike Stewart will be facing some other loser, BUT, it reads that he will be defending.... YOUR title! (Heavy Metal's eyes widen with anger as he SHOOTS up from the bench) Heavy Metal: WHAT!? (All of them back away from Metal) How DARE they let him keep the title!!! (Heavy Metal screams out in rage. He SLAMS his fist through the bench and it breaks it half) Deathwatch: Wait!! Settle down! I have a plan. Heavy Metal: So far all your plans have led me to is the loss of MY title! Deathwatch: Hear me out.... Actually, AEWA Board, YOU hear me out! "Superman" Mike Stewart will unfortunately NOT be able to make it to his match on the upcoming card, you see. He is going to court with the AEWA! Oh yes, the title had better be vacated or given back to it's rightful owner, Heavy Metal. Now I am through here, members-- Come, we have a plane to catch. (All of them begin to leave. Metal punches a locker again, denting it. He lets out a bellow and slams the door behind him, making a mirror fall off the wall shattering across the ground. They leave the entire locker room a disaster area) ---------- JT: BOY! ICU's REALLY pissed! KW: They have every reason to be. Commissioner Gehringer just throws any moron in the building into the ring and gives them title shots at a moment's notice. There was never any title contract between Stewart and Heavy Metal. JT: I know. I can't believe some of the guys that they let run a federation. Heavy Metal will HAVE to get a rematch though, and when he does, there's gonna be hell to pay. KW: That's for sure. And now let's hear from Submission. ---------- (We see the Phantom and Lord Of Midnight in their dressing room. They're both wearing "AEWA Sucks" T-shirts. The Phantom is holding a large crowbar, and he turns to the camera) The Phantom: (screaming) Enough is enough! We can't stand the AEWA anymore. We did our best to keep giving good performances to entertain the fans, but it seems that the AEWA doesn't care about the fans. They just ignored us. I understand now why people like Heavy Metal want to destroy the AEWA.... But, at least Kevin Larkin cares a little bit about the wrestlers. Bob Freeman has ignored us much too long. Now we'll change our way of doing things, we'll change our attitudes, we'll change our wrestling style.... AND IF YOU DON'T LIKE IT, THE HELL IF I CARE! (The Phantom smashes the crowbar into the camera. Fade to black) ---------- JT: Ah, there's another set of wrestlers to finally see the light! Welcome to the other side, Submission. I think you'll like it here. KW: Yeah, we need to get every wrestler to come over to the rulebreaker side, then every match would be cool. The best part would be that since there are no fan favorites, there would be no fans! JT: We can always dream, Ken. But I really am glad that Submission is finally fed up with the way things are going on around here. I wonder if they're looking for a manager. KW: They could certainly use one. Now let's watch this other moron, what's his name? Oh yeah, the Gryphon. ---------- (Gryphon is standing in the AEWA Hall of Fame. He looks at the names on the wall, and shakes his head in disgust. He looks toward the camera, and you notice a flame within his eyes that has previously not been seen.) Gryphon: How the mighty have fallen.... Crowley, you were once respected and feared by the whole federation. Now, you're nothing but another of Cardinal Chaos's sheep. How does it feel to be in the shadow of another, dark one? Does the 'Pounding Light' give you a headache? (Gryphon snorts) You two disgust me. You think it takes a lot of courage to threaten a small guy like President Freeman? Yeah right-- about as much courage it took for Ricky Roberts to attack Vicki. It sure seems that there are a lot of scumbags in this federation. You have Cardinal Chaos and his group of misguided sheep. Now, we have Deathwatch, and the bunch of thugs he calls "International Contracts Unfulfilled" or something like that. Heavy Metal-- karma caught up with you, big man. You stand at Deathwatch's side and, suddenly, you drop the World Title when you seemed unstoppable. Coincidence? I think not. Exodus, Penance, Legacy.... never in my life have I seen more pathetic comic book character clones. (Gryphon laughs) So, Exodus, your career was so pathetic you hauled your fat ass over to Deathwatch's place for a boost, eh? Good idea.... If Deathwatch weren't the most marked man in the AEWA, at least. First, he burns the AEWA banner. Second, he tries to kill President Smith. Finally, he copies one of the most drawn-out gimmicks in wrestling today! Let's not forget the Grim Weeper and his band of cronies, Twisted Sister and Ricky Roberts. Well, Weeper, someday I hope to get you in the ring, but I've got more important things to think about right now. ICU, your asses are all mine, boys. Don't think I'll come after you alone.
