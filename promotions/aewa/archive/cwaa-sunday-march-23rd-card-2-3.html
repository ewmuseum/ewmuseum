AEWA — Card Archive

Sunday, March 23rd, Card (2/3)

23 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Catch the news every weeknight at 6:00 from the award-winning WYXZ TV News Team. Anchorman Mark Powell brings you the news, Dave Clark with sports, and weather-bunny Tai West brings a little sunshine into all of our lives, evenings at 6:00, here on WYXZ TV 39.) {As "Dream On" plays, Randy Sheridan and Joshua Barnes walk down the aisle to a huge pop.) EL: And here they come... DM: This next contest is also a first round match in the CWAA Tag Team Title Tournament, set for one fall with a 15-minute time limit. On their way to the ring now, at a combined weight of 462 lb.., Joshua Barnes and CWAA Heavyweight Champion, "The Dreamer" Randy Sheradin! (Randy is wearing the CWAA title around his waist, and they are both enthusiastically slapping hands with the fans. As they approach ringside, Randy hops up on the top turnbuckle, faces the crowd, and does a backflip into the ring (to a thunderous cheer). Joshua hands the ring attendant his glasses (perceptive people might notice that he always gives his glasses to the same blond-haired ring girl, but that's probably just a coincidence), and enters the ring through the ropes. Dave Myers goes over to speak to the wrestlers.} DM: Randy, congratulations on winning the CWAA title last week. RS: Dave, when I first said I was going to be a professional wrestler, people said I was dreaming. I was too small, too short, out of my league. Well {takes the CWAA title, and holds it over his head}, last week I proved that Dreams can come true. And tonight, the big guy and myself are going to try and win the Tag Team titles. I know most people are saying that we're the underdogs. That's the way we like it. {Dave heads over to Joshua Barnes} DM: Although you and Randy are allies, tonight's the first time you two have teamed up. And Double Jeopardy has teamed together since the beginning of the CWAA. Can you two work as well together? (EL: Actually, they teamed last night for the first time...) JB: {Takes a deep breath before starting} I think so. Randy's a former tag team champion, and he's been giving me tips. Also, with his strength and my speed, we should make a good combination. {Dave Myers looks at him strangely.} JB: I mean, with his speed and my spee... with his speed and his stren... {takes another deep breath} with his speed and MY strength, we should make a good combo. {laughs nervously} I guess you don't have to worry about me taking your job, Dave. But Double Jeopardy should worry, because we plan on beating them tonight. ("Sweet Home Alabama" starts, and "Hardcore" Henry King leads Double Jeopardy to the ring.) DM: Their opponents hail from Houston, Texas, at a combined weight of 500 lb., "Hotshot" Gary Jackson, Eddie Intellecta, the team of Double Jeopardy! Your referee, Chris Herman. (Many boos for one of the most hated duos in Texas.) EL: OK- one of the favorites in this tournament, a team that has been in the ring perhaps more than any other team in the CWAA today, takes on Joshua Barnes and the CWAA Champion. There's the bell, and Sheradin will be starting with Gary Jackson. Jackson charges- armdrag takedown by the champ. Jackson back up- Sheradin with a dropkick- and another! Jackson staggers into the wrong corner for a tag, and Joshua Barnes tags him all right! (Big pop as Barnes headbutts the Hotshot) EL: Jackson on spaghetti-legs now, Sheradin up on his shoulders- victory roll... 1... 2... kickout! Sheradin tags in Barnes, and the Dreamer with the Irish whip- drops down- Jackson leapfrogs- and Barnes catches him- Hotshot! He dropped him right across the top rope! Cover... 1... 2... kickout! Barnes with a standing arm bar now on the ex-auto mechanic. And as Jackson came to the ring, I could swear that t-shirt of his smelt of beer. Now Barnes hoists him onto his shoulders- airplane spin! Intellecta hollering for Jackson to cover his eyes, but Jackson pays no attention. Barnes drops the woozy hotshot and tags in the Dreamer, who heads up top. Jackson back up on his feet- Sheradin with a flying sunset flip! 1... 2... Intellecta breaks the pin with a boot to the head. (Crowd groans) EL: As referee Chris Herman tries to get Intellecta out of there, Eddie shoves Jackson toward the Double Jeopardy corner. Intellecta clearly the brains of the organization- you saw him advising Jackson to cover his eyes in that airplane spin before to counter the dizzying effect of seeing the arena seemingly spin in front of you, now he puts his partner in a better position to tag out after taking a beating. And Jackson does make the tag, and Eddie Intellecta in there now with Randy Sheradin. Collar and elbow tie up, and Intellecta with a handful of hair pulls Sheradin back into the Double Jeopardy corner- Jackson holds back the Dreamer's arms- Intellecta swings- and he nails his own partner! (Big pop) EL: The Dreamer ducks just in time, and Intellecta hit Jackson right in the mush. I see The Professional on his way down the aisle now- at least he's heading here instead of the ring, as Intellecta slides Sheradin into a side headlock. Max Robertson, what possible business could you have here right now? MR: Oh, I don't know, I just thought I'd come down and watch the match. Look at Barnes, that drooling idiot! I think he's afraid of me. EL: What could possibly make you say that? Sheradin reverses the headlock into a hammerlock, but Intellecta reverses into a standing hammerlock of his own. MR: This past week, he challenged me to a bunkhouse match! Can you imagine that?! That kid is so stupid I'm surprised he can remember how to breathe. EL: It was not done out here in public, but Joshua Barnes to apply to the CWAA offices for a bunkhouse match with Max Robertson. I take it that you didn't accept his challenge, then? MR: Listen, I've explained this before, but either Mr. Joshua Barnes wasn't listening, or he forgot: First of all, that gibbering fool is not my present concern. I'm concerned with my upcoming rematch with Randy (he turns and spits on the floor) Sheridan. I don't care about who can beat up who. I care about getting my due. I was robbed of that belt last week, and I don't intend to let that happen again. EL: So could anything persuade you to wrestle Barnes in the near future? After your rematch with Sheridan, perhaps? Sheridan hooks a boot over the bottom rope, and Intellecta has to break the hold. MR: Look, I'll wrestle him, but I'm no fool. It'll be on my terms. If he wants a gimmick match, I'll give him a gimmick match. If he wants a cage match, I'll give him one. If he wants a lumberjack match, I'll give him that too. But I don't do bunkhouse matches. (Robertson gets up and walks around to the other side of the ring past the DJ corner, ignoring the questioning glance he gets from Henry King as he passes.) EL: Chris Herman calls for a clean break, and Intellecta clotheslines Randy Sheradin right over the top rope. But the Dreamer lands on his feet and connects on a shoulderblock through the middle ropes, doubling over Intellecta- sunset flip...1 ...2 ...kickout. Sheradin with an arm bar now, and he tags in Joshua Barnes. Barnes with a kick to the exposed ribs of Eddie Intellecta. Barnes with an Irish whip- reversed- and Gary Jackson with a knee in the back! Barnes drops to one knee- and Eddie Intellecta with a pop in the face for him. Intellecta hooks him- vertical suplex- no! Brainbuster! He just dropped Barnes on the top of his head. The cover... 1... 2... kickout. Intellecta whips him into the Double Jeopardy corner- tags in Jackson, who hip tosses Barnes out of the corner. Now he slaps on a camel clutch. Jackson leans back- and as he pulls Barnes' head up, his eyes meet those of Max Robertson, who had been glaring at Sheradin. Barnes' face grows brick-red as he stares back at the Professional, but he has bigger problems right now, being stuck in the camel clutch. Barnes tries to power his way out, but can't. Referee Chris Herman checks for a submission, but the Indiana native hangs in there. Jackson releases the hold, and pulls Barnes up. Swinging neckbreaker- the cover...1 ...2... kickout. And Barnes sits up and stares at Robertson, and Jackson with a kick in the back. He pulls Barnes back up- Irish whip- clothesline. Gary Jackson off the ropes- frog splash- and Barnes pulls up his knees! (Big pop) EL: Jackson lands right across the knees! Jackson on the mat in obvious pain, and Barnes on his feet. Sheradin reaching into the ring calling for the tag, but Barnes' attention is fixed on Max Robertson. Barnes turns around as Jackson regains his footing- double sledge right in the chest. Barnes turns back to glare at Robertson again- Sheradin still calling for the tag, but the ex-bouncer is oblivious. Barnes picks up Gary Jackson- backbreaker! Last night, when Barnes finally did catch up to Robertson, that was one of the first things Barnes did to him. Now Barnes turns once more to glare at the Professional, who assumes an expression of complete contempt for Barnes' efforts. Joshua Barnes whips Gary Jackson into the ropes- spinebuster slam! The cover... 1... Sheradin intercepts Intellecta with a dropkick- he got it! (Enormous pop as "Dream On" plays again) DM: Here are your winners, "Dreamer" Randy Sheradin and Joshua Barnes! EL: Joshua Barnes and Randy Sheradin advance to meet McNultey and Anderson next week, and as Sheradin exults, Barnes stares after Max Robertson who slowly makes his way back to the locker area. We're going to take another break, and when we get back, we'll meet the FWA's own Lethal Weapons. (Wednesday, the WYXZ TV 39, it's the movie of the week. This week, Patrick Swayze is a philosophical bouncer in "Roadhouse".) DM: This next contest is also a first round match in the CWAA Tag Team Title tournament, set for one fall and a 15-minute time limit. First, led to the ring by Mother Nature, at a combined weight of 559 lb., Travis Conley, Paul "The Tornado" Langford, they are Twister! (Pop as the trio arrive to the accompaniment of the Twister theme song.) DM: And their opponents, representing the FWA and the Too Hot Crew, led my their manager, Too Hot, Murder, Homicide- the Lethal Weapons! (Default heel heat for being the opposition for the popular Twister greets the FWAers.) DM: Your referee for thi- Too Hot: (grabs the microphone) Well I guess The Lethal Weapons are about to show these so called rising stars what tag team wrestling is all about! To all these CWAA tag teams who don't know what they are getting in to WELCOME TO THE BIG TIME!!! Murder: I see that we are taking on some future superstars, HA HA! You guys are gonna be able to tell your grandkids that you lost to the best tag team in wrestling, THE LETHAL WEAPONS!!! We are here in CWAA representing the FWA and we are planning on showing the CWAA what we can do. I do want to wish the team of Twister good luck! GET READY BOYS!!! ITS TIME FOR THE LETHAL INJECTION! Homicide: WE ARE THE TAG TEAM OF THE FUTURE AND THE FUTURE IS NOW!!!!!!!!!! (Drops the microphone to the mat) DM: Your referee for this contest is Jeff Howard. EL: OK- our first look at the Lethal Weapons as they square off against Twister. Paul Langford and Homicide start it off as the bell rings, and Homicide greets him with a double sledge, and again, and again, beating him down to the mat. Stomps away, and now a tag to Murder. Double Irish whip into a double clothesline! (Murder and Homicide mount the bottom rope to be in a better position to inform the crowd of what they have been witnessing.) EL: Impressive start for the Lethal Weapons so far. It looks like Homicide staying in the ring- I forget which one the legal man is. Irish whip- and a flying shoulderblock levels the Tornado. Cover... 1... 2... kickout. Homicide now picks him back up -whip into a neutral corner- and Langford bounces out of the corner with a clothesline for Homicide. (pop) EL: Paul Langford now with a scoop slam, and tags in Travis Conley. Conley with an elbow drop. Now he lifts Homicide and slaps on a full nelson. He does not quite have his fingers locked, though, and until he does, he won't get maximum effect. Homicide trying to power out of it, but Travis Conley sent several years pushing linebackers around- he is not likely to be overpowered. Murder in there now to break it up with a forearm in the back. As referee Jeff Howard sends him back to his ring, it's Homicide with a low blow that stuns the young Conley. Murder tags in now, and a double Irish whip into a double clothesline. And Homicide is heading to the wrong corner- he swings at Paul Langford, and the Tornado into the ring now, and he's cut off by Senior Official Jeff Howard. This leaves Travis Conley at the mercy of both Lethal Weapons- spiked piledriver coming up! And they stuck him. Murder covers, but now Jeff Howard sending him to the corner instead- I think he is the legal man, though. In any event, it has not taken long for Jeff Howard to lose control of this one. Both Too Hot and Mother Nature starting to give him a hard time about this match, and Murder, in particular, is livid about that decision. Homicide in there now, and he tosses Conley to the outside, and follows. Paul Langford out there to help his partner, and Murder just shoved Jeff Howard! (The crowd starts to get ugly. Well, they probably already were, but i'm speaking figuratively.) EL: I don't know if that kind of thing is acceptable in the FWA, but here in the CWAA, our referees don't tolerate that sort of thing, and Jeff Howard is calling for the bell. Paul Langford and Homicide are exchanging blows on the floor, and now Murder hops to the floor and grabs a chair. It looks as though Twister will get the disqualification victory, but the Lethal Weapons apparently have decided that they are not leaving on anyone's terms but their own. Now Murder slams that chair in Travis Conley's back, and Paul Langford rams Homicide's head onto the ring steps. Now Langford after Murder, who smacks him with the chair, too. The bell is still ringing, and here come Randy Sheradin, Joshua Barnes, and the team of Midnight Rampage, and the Lethal Weapons and Too Hot apparently have decided that they accomplished what they came to do. (Boos and our first ever shower of debris greet our visitors as they depart.) EL: OK- Tip Smith and his men and Barnes and the champ check on the condition of Twister- let's get the official word from David Myers. DM: Your winners, as a result of a disqualification, Twister! (The Twister theme plays and there is moderate popping for them, until they both struggle to their feet to exit, at which point the crowd noise picks up.) EL: So Twister advance via disqualification, but not without suffering some real pain at the hands of the FWA's Lethal Weapons. When we get back, the final first round matchup, and the AEWA World Light Heavyweight Championship! (Wednesday, the WYXZ TV 39, it's the movie of the week. This week, Patrick Swayze is a philosophical bouncer in "Roadhouse".) EL: Before we go back to the ring, let's go to the ring for a talk to the man who wears the AEWA World Light Heavyweight Championship belt, the Cardiac Kid, Joey Valachi. DM: And please welcome my guest at this time, who will be facing Marc Thomas later in the evening, the AEWA Light-Heavyweight Champion of the World, "Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi! DM: Ladies and gentlemen, my guest at this time is the reigning AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion of the World, Joey Valachi! ("Heartbreaker" by Pat Benatar starts up as Valachi enters to a chorus of boos. He practices his usual charm on the fans, in the process using language that makes Bret Hart's recent RAW diatribe resemble a Disney flick. As soon as he passes the camera, he opens his jacket to reveal his "AEWA SUCKS" T-shirt. A few smarts chant "UWF" which causes Joey to flash a trucker's salute before turning his attention to David Myers) DM: Welcome, Joey...(He is interrupted by Valachi, who grabs the microphone.) JV: Let's just get this over with. I'm Joey Valachi, you can call me the Cardiac Kid, I'm gonna kick Marc Thomas' butt, bye now. (He throws down the mike and starts to walk away, but Myers grabs the mike and stops Valachi.) DM: Hold on, Joey. You can't tell me that's all you want to say. JV: No it isn't, but why should I waste time telling you, your inbred fans, and the three people watching on TV? I'm down here against my will, and I just want to waste this loser and get back to the AEWA, where I waste losers too, but at least there I do it for more money. DM: You're not much of an ambassador for the AEWA, you know. JV: I don't have to be. Everyone up at that remedial wrestling factory is out to get me. You know it, I know it, they know it, and the fans know it. Did you see the AEWA report last week? "We wish Valachi the best of luck?" They wish me crap and they know it. I tell it like it is. The AEWA has gone to pot. Think about it. I tell the world the AEWA has no competition, and what do they do? Does someone step forward to tell me I'm wrong? Does some hero step up to defend the honor of the AEWA? Hell, no. They send me down to the minors to face some nobody. That's as close to an admission as those cretins will come. DM: Now just a second. There's some great competition down here, and Marc Thomas is a fine example of it. If you're not ready for this, he'll beat you. JV: Yeah, sure. And I'm the Queen of Canada. I mean, come on, who's you guys' champion. DM: Randy Sheridan. He won a great match for the title last week. JV: Randy Sheridan? "The Dreamer" Randy Sheridan? HE'S the best you have? (Laughs, then to himself) I'll be out of here quicker than I thought. (Into the mike again) Marc Thomas, I've been around enough to know if I offered you a chance to back out, you wouldn't take it. Still, I think everybody'd understand if your music played tonight, and nobody came out of the locker room. (Boos. No they wouldn't.) So, all I'm going to say is, after I beat you, don't let it get you down. I beat great wrestlers every day. A scrub like you losing to me's nothing to be ashamed of. Now, if you'll excuse me, I AM leaving, and you're NOT stopping me. Even though I don't need to, I'm actually going to do you losers the honor of getting dressed up for this match. So excuse me. (Valachi walks back to the locker room, ignoring the abuse fans heap on his head.) DM: Well, he'll never win any popularity contests, but I'll bet most of the people watching tonight will be pulling for Marc Thomas. I don't know if he'd be any better, but I doubt he could be any worse.
