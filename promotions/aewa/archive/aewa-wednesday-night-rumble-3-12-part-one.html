AEWA — Card Archive

Wednesday Night Rumble (3/12, part one)

15 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * A E W A W E D N E S D A Y N I G H T R U M B L E * * * * LIVE!, from inside the Mall Of America! (We see the AEWA logo on the screen, set on fire. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Rock music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... David Graves taking some guy's head off with the Gravestone, Penance PLANTING some guy with the Final Judgment, The West Coast Connection DRIVING some guy's head into the mat with the West Coast Killa, Cardinal Chaos STICKING some poor soul with the Inverted Crucifix, Shane Matthews BRUTALLY hitting the End of the Road on some hapless jobber, and Heavy Metal notching yet ANOTHER casualty courtesy of the Megadeth Power Bomb. The camera then shifts to Matt Wilson, The Lovely Bertha, and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt in the Mall of America, the site of AEWA Wednesday Night Rumble! Their booth is set up in the middle of the mall, with a large crowd of onlookers eagerly waiting for the event to begin.) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to the Mall Of America, right here in beautiful Minnesota! We're about to kick off this VERY special episode of Wednesday Night Rumble, and in tonight's main event, Primal Fear will be squaring off against "the Shotgun" Stan Crawford in a falls-count anywhere match! That should be great, folks. BD: Yeah, they're going to tear up this whole damn mall. I can make one guarantee-- there WILL be blood shed tonight. No doubt about it. LB: I'd have to agree, Big Daddy. When Crawford finally locks up with Fear, all HELL is gonna break loose. I can hardly wait to watch those two idiots destroy each other. BD: Anyway, is it time for the first bout yet, Wilson? These fans seem to be getting a little bit impatient. MW: The fist match here at the Mall of America is set up to be a good one. Big Ron Raider takes on Bishop Bedlam. The last time they met, Bedlam won a controversial victory, using some brass knuckles to beat the former World Champion. LB: This is weird. Both men are members of the Church, yet they're going after each other like mortal enemies. MW: Maybe the Cardinal doesn't have the control over his men he once had. BD: Or maybe he wants them to fight it out, as a way of proving themselves. MW: Maybe. You can never be too sure what's going on regarding the Church. Let's get to the ring... RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from the Church of the Pounding Light, weighing in at 275 pounds, Biiiiiishooooop Beeeeeeeeeeedlaaaaaaaaaaaaam! (Bishop Bedlam makes his entrance to a HUGE round of boos, and he appears to be in some kind of a trance. He is wearing a long red robe, as always, and stares directly at Raider.) BD: Bedlam looks determined. LB: He has an advantage, winning their previous match. MW: Even though he used an illegal weapon to win? LB: A win is a win is a win is a.... BD: Win? LB: Exactly. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, From the Rocky Mountains, also a member of the Church, he weighs in at 416 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his manager, Million Dollar Mark, here is Big.... Ron.... Raaaaaaaaaaaaaaaiiiiiiiiideeeeeeeeeeer! (Lynyrd Skynyrd's "Saturday Night Special" cranks over the PA as Raider makes his entrance to a HUGE chorus of boos. He spits at several ringside fans, and enters the ring. Million Dollar Mark is at his side.) MW: And he charges into the ring and attacks Bedlam! I don't think Bedlam was expecting this onslaught from Raider. BD: He should have been. You don't beat Ron Raider and expect him to be in a good mood for the rematch. MW: Raider sends Bedlam into the ropes, and nails him with a clothesline! Raiders picks him up, and powerslams him to the ground. Another pickup, and a shoulderbreaker this time. A third pickup.... Tombstone Piledriver! BD: Bedlam is getting slaughtered! MW: Here's the cover.... One.. Two.. Kickout! MW: Raider whips Bedlam into the turnbuckle, and now Bedlam is just slumping down against the corner. Raider charges.... CAUGHT! A big boot by Bedlam knocks Raider to the ground! LB: He should have stayed with the power moves. A man can only take so many powerslams before losing. MW: Bedlam's still leaning against the turnbuckle, recovering from Raider's assault. Raider sits up, and starts to stand.... BD: Bedlam runs over, and hits a swinging neckbreaker on Raider! Now he grabs Raider's head, and twists the neck. MW: The referee is asking for a submission, but Raider refuses. This is a good move on Bedlam's part. He's still hurt, so he can recover and attack Raider's neck simultaneously. BD: Simul.... what? LB: He means "at the same time." Try not to use those fancy words, Wilson. MW: Fine. Nothing more than three syllables, I promise. Raider is powering out, but Bedlam kicks Raider, then snapmares him back to their original position. BD: The big man doesn't even pause, he just powers up to a standing position. Bedlam's still behind him, but Raider shoves him into the ropes, and sets up for a clothesline.... LB: Nope. Bedlam caught himself on the ropes, and gives an obscene finger gesture to Raider. MW: I don't think that's the Church's sign for peace, and I hope our camera's didn't catch that. Bedlam ducks under the ropes and walks outside for a break. BD: Bad move-- here comes Raider charging after him! LB: Smart move- he just threw a drink into Raider's eyes! Big Ron is blinded! BD: And that was one of those 96-ounce suckers that the Mall rips people off for. Bedlam whips Raider into the outer turnbuckle, and Ron hits it with full force! MW: With the damage done, Bedlam throws Raider back into the ring. Raider is still unable to see. Bedlam climbs up the apron... climbs up to the top turnbuckle.... AND NAILS RAIDER WITH AN ELBOW! The cover! One.. Two.. Kickout! MW: Bedlam drags Raider over to the ropes.... it looks like he's tying Raider into the ropes! LB: He's using the ring ropes to tie up Raider's head! Now he flips Raider over, so that Big Ron is hanging over the ring by his neck! MW: The referee is trying to untie Raider, but Bedlam keeps hitting Raider with forearm shots. Raider's starting to turn blue-- I don't think he can breathe! BD: The referee is pushing Bedlam away, and Million Dollar Mark is lifting the Raider's legs, trying to flip him back into the ring and get him out of the ropes. MW: With the referee keeping Bedlam from interfering, Million Dollar Mark helps Raider out of that situation. But Raider suffered a tremendous amount of damage. LB: Bedlam's going for a lazy cover! One.. Two.. Foot on the ropes! BD: If he had hooked the leg, he would have gotten him. Bedlam now rolls Raider over, and is trying to put a camel clutch on Raider. MW: With so much damage to Raider's neck, this camel clutch might put him away. Bedlam doesn't have it on very well-- Raider's left arm is free-- but you can tell that Big Ron is in agony. The referee is checking for a submission, but Raider refuses. LB: He's using that left arm to push up both himself and Bedlam, to relieve the pressure form the camel clutch. Bishop is trying to push Raider back down, but Raider's too powerful. MW: He's lifting.... he's on his knees.... He's standing up! He's carrying Bishop Bedlam around, piggyback style! BD: Bedlam rakes the eyes! Raider has to drop the Bishop, who was in a bad situation. MW: Bedlam kicks Raider, bending him over. Now he picks him up for a piledriver.... LB: With all the damage to Raider's neck, a piledriver could put him away for good. MW: Bedlam's struggling to keep Raider in position... he's lowering Raider back down.... RAIDER BACKDROPS BEDLAM! Now Raider picks him up, and slams Bedlam into the mat. BD: Raider is back in his ass-kicking mode. Bedlam's in trouble. MW: He certainly is. Raider whips Bedlam into the ropes, and delivers a spinebuster! The cover.... One.. Two.. Foot on the ropes! LB: Too close to the ropes for Raider. The referee's pushing him away. MW: Bedlam's reaching into his tights! He's pulling out the brass knuckles again! As Raider approaches, he swings.... BD: ....and is caught by Raider! Raider catches Bedlam's fist with one hand! LB: Million Dollar Mark warned Raider what Bedlam was up to, and Raider managed to stop Bedlam from nailing him with the brass knuckles. MW: Raiders kicks Bedlam once.... twice.... three times! He lifts him up, AND EXECUTES THE RAIDER BOMB! Here's the cover! One.. Two.. Three!! (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of this match, in fourteen minutes and twenty seconds, Big Ron Raiiiiiidddddeeeerrrrrrr!! MW: An excellent match. Big Ron Raider beats his fellow Church member Bishop Bedlam here in the Mall of America. And.... I guess he's not done yet. BD: He's getting a chair from the crowd, and tossing it back into the ring! Now what the hell's gonna happen? LB: Bedlam's in trouble. Raider picks up the chair.... [CRACK! CRACK! CRACK!] MW: Raider nails Bedlam three times in the head with the chair, just as Bedlam was getting up! BD: The Church is in trouble. Using brass knuckles during a match is just doing what it takes to win, but using a chair AFTER the match.... that's a different story. LB: These two are going to have serious trouble being in the same stable. What's Chaos going to do with Bedlam and Raider? MW: Raider puts the folded chair on the mat, and grabs Bedlam. He picks him up.... RAIDER BOMB RIGHT ONTO THE CHAIR! Bedlam is out, and that last move could cripple him. BD: I guess if Bedlam can't wrestle anymore, that would take care of any problems. MW: Raider and Million Dollar Mark walk out of the ring, apparently satisfied with their revenge. The Mall of America Emergency Crew is coming to the ring with a stretcher.... Bedlam is still out. Hopefully he'll be all right, and I'd love to hear the Cardinal's spin on this match. BD: Well, I hate to change the subject at such an important time, but isn't it time for the next match? MW: Yes, it's time now for the Light-Heavyweight Championship title match as Joey Valachi takes on El Aviadore. Wait a minute-- somebody appears to be coming down to our booth. (A huge round of boos choruses through the ringside area of the Mall, as Zack Derringer comes down to the broadcast table. He takes a seat next to Bertha, puts on a headset, and begins to talk.) ZD: Great to be here again, as always. BD: Dammit, how in the hell do you get passage to all of these events? The AEWA can't possibly be paying you that much. ZD: You're right, that's why I'm on salary by Deathwatch. LB: Why would Deathwatch pay a scumbag like you? ZD: Hey, I'm a quality commentator and I handle all of his business affairs. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, this bout is for the AEWA Light Heavyweight championship! It has been set for one fall, with no time limit. Introducing first, the challenger, from Mexico City, weighing in at 180 pounds, here is EL AVIADORE! ("La Bamba" begins playing loudly over the PA, and El Aviadore makes his entrance. He is wearing a blue mask, and waves at the adoring crowd.) MW: Aviadore hasn't been around a lot as of late, but he's hoping to win himself a title here tonight. ZD: Are you kidding me? He won't win jacksh*t in this match-- Valachi is the better everything. BD: For once, I agree with the jerk. Valachi has what it takes to be the champ, and he didn't win it by slouching. LB: He'll make a great champion. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 202 pounds, he is the AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion of the World, "the Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi! (Pat Benatar's "Gettin' Paid" begins playing loudly, and Joey Valachi makes his entrance to a HUGE round of boos from the crowd. He's wearing an "AEWA Sucks" T-shirt, and appears to be in a VERY bad mood.) ZD: That's one reason why I like this guy, he hates the same things I do. Maybe I can talk with him about a contract with ICU. LB: I sure as hell hope not. MW: Well, both men are in the ring now.... (DING, DING) MW: ....and there's the bell! Valachi takes it to Aviadore with a standing dropkick! He follows it up with an armdrag takedown as Aviadore runs at him! Valachi locks an armlock on Aviadore, but Aviadore shocks him with a fancy kickout and reverses it! BD: Quick thinking by Aviadore, and Valachi grabs him by the waist.... SIDE SUPLEX!! Valachi goes for an early cover.... One.. Kickout! LB: Valachi is good, but you aren't going to get Aviadore like that. ZD: That's one thing that I don't like about you-- your mind isn't in the right place, not enough ambition. LB: Good, the less you like about me the better. MW: Back to the action, folks. Valachi picks Aviadore up to his feet, and here's an irish whip.... Aviadore counters with a sunset flip! Valachi goes over.... One.. Two.. Thr.. Kickout! BD: That was a close call. Now Aviadore climbs up to the top rope, but Valachi is quick to stop him before he jumps! Valachi pounds him down, and Aviadore lands spread eagle on the top rope! Valachi climbs up to the top, and here's the set up.... SUPERPLEX!! Valachi is climbing back up top, and Aviadore gets to his feet.... FLYING DROPKICK! LB: Valachi shows off a lot of skill, and it's those quick evasive maneuvers that let him pull it off. ZD: (notices something at ringside) Oh goodie, look who's coming down the aisle! MW: Deathwatch is coming down the aisle, and he's with KISMET! I still don't believe it! Kismet has her arm wrapped around his, and Deathwatch stops at ringside-- it looks as if he is just observing this match. BD: Who would think Kismet would go with Deathwatch? This is just too freaky. Valachi and Aviadore haven't noticed him yet, but if anyone has a past with Deathwatch, it's Aviadore. Valachi whips Aviadore into the ropes, and Valachi catches him with a drop toe hold! ZD: Don't Deathwatch and Kismet make such a nice looking couple? (snickers) LB: No, they don't. It's a total mismatch, but from the looks of things, Deathwatch is training Kismet to be more and more like him every day. MW: Valachi has noticed Deathwatch now, and he's turned the pace down a notch to avoid any confrontation with him. Valachi is climbing up onto the top rope, but Aviadore rams himself into the corner-- Valachi drops down on the top turnbuckle! Aviadore climbs up to the top.... FRANKENSTEINER! BD: Vicious move by Aviadore, and he goes for the cover.... One.. Two.. Thr.. Kickout! LB: That was a close one, but Valachi was still able to kickout. ZD: No matter how long it takes, Valachi will still win. He's just that good, and he's not even in ICU.... yet. BD: He would never go to ICU, Derringer. ZD: That's what you think. Anyone would go to ICU, they just need to be offered the opportunity. If they don't, they either already have their security or they're morons. MW: Valachi is back on his feet, and Aviadore runs off the ropes.... Aviadore with a cross body block, and Valachi CATCHES him with a backbreaker! Valachi picks Aviadore up, and he whips him into the ropes-- REVERSED BY AVIADORE! Now Aviadore runs off the opposite side, and gets TRIPPED by Deathwatch! The ref didn't see it, and Valachi NAILS Aviadore with a kneedrop to the back of the head!! BD: Valachi picks Aviadore up, and he slams him down in the center of the ring! And now Valachi is climbing up to the top rope.... he jumps off, and NAILS the Heartstopper! This one is over! One.. Two.. Three!! (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner, as the result of a pinfall.... "The Cardiac Kid" Joooeeeeeyyyy Valaaaaaaaaaaachi! (Pat Benatar's "Gettin' Paid" begins to play, and Deathwatch and Kismet both leave together, smiling. Valachi takes his title and catches up to Deathwatch-- they seem to discuss something as they walk behind the curtains.) ZD: Well, I gotta go supervise my boss' business, so.... later! MW: Maybe you could get us some inside information from Deathwatch, Zack? Would that be possible? ZD: Not a chance. (Zack gets up from the broadcast booth, puts his headset down, and walks back behind the curtain.) BD: It seems like he's our personal angel of death, taking away something every time. LB: I hate him too, Big Daddy. So, what's up next, Wilson? * * * * TO BE CONTINUED IN PART TWO * * * * \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- "A New Beginning" American E-Wrestling Association The Official E-Wrestling Federation of Wrestle World and TNM The Best E-Wrestling Fed of the Year 1996 in the TNM Year-End Awards Be sure to visit our Event Center at: http://www.california.com/~bullet/index.html
