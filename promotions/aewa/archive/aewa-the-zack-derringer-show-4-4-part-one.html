The Zack Derringer Show (4/4, part one)

5 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * T H E Z A C K D E R R I N G E R S H O W * * * * Live, from the ICU Studios in New York City (A screen appears with a black background and red text that reads: "WARNING! The following program has language and material of an adult nature. If you are under 14 years of age, please change the channel now. This is definitely not for your viewing. Thank you.") ("King Nothing" by Metallica begins to play slowly over a black screen, as the guitars and the bass kick in, and then the drums hit and the entire screen BURSTS with a white blast. "International" shows up on the screen in the back, really small, and then it gets bigger and flashes past, as the screen shows Sewerdog driving someone's head into the mat with a DDT. "Contracts" flashes past the screen, and then it shows Damian Steele drilling someone back with The Shining. "Unlimited" flashes past the screen, and it shows Heavy Metal SLAMMING someone down onto the mat with The Megadeth power bomb. Finally, it shows Deathwatch DRIVING someone's head into the mat with the Death's Vengeance. "The Zack Derringer Show" comes up onto the screen, and then drips away like blood, turning red as it goes. The last thing you can hear is Deathwatch's sadistic laughter, as the camera fades in on the ICU Studios in New York City. Zack Derringer and Hank Thompson are sitting behind the desk. Zack has his feet kicked up on the desk and is wearing black jeans and a white shirt. His long, black hair hangs to the sides of his head. Hank is wearing a sharp looking blue suit with a black tie, and he smiles at the camera. Derringer begins to talk.) Derringer: Yet again, I come here to entertain and humiliate all the monkeys of the AEWA. Now.... this show has been delayed slightly, so don't be surprised if you see an interview or two focused on old matches. We've also got plenty of important information on AEWA Extreme, so stay tuned. Thompson: This should be a good show tonight. Let's start it off by taking a look at one of those older flashes we were talking about-- here are the Machines. Derringer: Yippie. ------------------- (Paul Wong and Simon O'Neal storm into the locker room. Paul, being from Denver, is wearing a "Colorado University" sweatshirt. Simon is wearing an "Indiana University" T-shirt. Paul looks thrilled, while Simon looks pissed.) Paul: (acting very smug, for him) Remember, you have to wash my car for the next year. I still can't believe you accepted that bet with me. Simon: If I had known the referees were that blind, I wouldn't have! They were as bad as the ones in the AEWA. Those bums were letting Colorado get away with hits that we couldn't get away with! Paul: Hey, what's the camera.... damn! I forgot that we had to do an interview for Extreme, and.... where are you going? Simon: (walking out the door) I'm washing that German piece of junk you drive! You handle this interview yourself! (he slams the door as he leaves) Paul: He's just upset that he lost.... To begin with, congrats to Colorado. Hopefully we can celebrate your NCAA Championship along with our victory. I'm sure Simon would like that. (grins) Anyway, on to Extreme, and the Assassins. Guys, I like you. I really respect you, and I'd much rather take you guys on than, say, the West Coast Connection. But let's correct a few things. First of all, I was watching your match against Heaven & Hell. Simon didn't do a damn thing in that match other than watch it. He didn't interfere, he didn't touch you, he didn't help Chaos or Crowley. He can be very annoying and distracting-- TRUST ME on that one-- but don't blame him because Crowley hit you in the head with a chair. Secondly-- about his deal. I have no idea what he's planning. Personally, I'd rather just fight you in a clean match; I'm convinced we can beat you. But Simon always has to pull these stunts. If you can catch him, go for it. I couldn't help you if I wanted to. And, whatever else, he is my partner. So I wouldn't help you if I could. I intend to fight a clean match at Extreme, but I also intend to win. The Machines have worked very hard to win the titles. We're not losing them now. (Paul starts to walk away, chuckling to himself) .... and Simon actually spotted me two points. (fade to black) --------------- Derringer: Another stupid interview from another stupid team. Anyway, what's next? Thompson: Next up we have a rather interesting flash from "Crimson" Joe Reed, so let's take a look. Derringer: Can we NOT take a look and pretend like we did? (Thompson raises an eyebrow) Derringer: Bah, you're no fun. Roll it. ------------------- (Inside a wrestling ring in an otherwise empty gym, "Crimson" Joe Reed and a sparring partner are fighting. Reed has an armbar on the wrestler. Outside the ring, in different corners, are two other sparring partners. And outside the ring, with a stopwatch in one hand and a notebook in the other, is the Lady in Red. She walks toward one of the other wrestlers.) Lady In Red: Go in and attack Joe, break the armbar. (The wrestler nods, then jumps into the ring and helps double-team Reed. As he fights off both wrestlers, the Lady in Red faces the camera.) Lady In Red: We're just preparing for the four-way match at AEWA Extreme. Joe's been in the ring for.... (checks the watch) over 27 minutes now, against one of the three men. We're training for the worse-case scenario, where Reed can't tag out and has to take the brunt of the attack. (Looks at the watch again) Okay-- NOW! (At her command, all three men enter the ring, and triple-team the champion. As Reed fights the men, she continues....) Lady In Red: Unfortunately, the biggest man we could find is just over 300 pounds, so we couldn't find a good comparison for Heavy Metal. I'm not that worried, though. Heavy Metal is 7 foot, 4 inches, and weighs over 450 pounds. I've got news for you, big fellow-- Joe's fought bigger men before. Fought them, and won. (She looks in the ring. Crimson Joe has throw out one of the wrestlers, and is whipping the other one into the turnbuckle. The third wrestler catches him in a bulldog, but Reed powers out, and suplexes him. The Lady in Red looks at the watch again.) Lady In Red: Okay-- Time! Thirty minutes. Good job. Joe, any problems? (Reed, still winded from the training session, shakes his head no. He thanks the wrestlers, then gets a drink while the Lady in Red continues.) Lady In Red: I guess if the big guy is Heavy Metal, then Joe could be considered Classic Rock. Funny thing about Classic Rock. Heavy Metal is louder, and gets more attention. But Classic Rock has a way of staying on top forever. ("Crimson" Joe Reed joins the Lady in Red. He's still breathing heavily from his match.) Reed: Slocum, Crowley, and Heavy Metal.... Someday, someone is going to take my title from me. They'll probably have to kill me to do it, but it someday will happen. However.... it won't be soon, and it won't be any of you. I'm walking in to the match with the AEWA World Title, and I'm walking out with it. (Reed and the Lady In Red walk away, discussing the notes from the match as the camera fades.) ------------------- Derringer: You know what, Joe? I know you're a "big name" from some other federations, but right now you're messing with Heavy Metal, one of ICU's premier stars, and I got news for you.... it isn't going to be that easy. In fact, next up we have a flash from Heavy Metal. Thompson: Metal is a great wrestler, former champion, and and all-around big wrestler. If Reed wants to walk out of that ring with the title, he's going to have to get through him to do it. Not to take anything away from Crowley or Slocum, but as Reed said.... Metal is the main threat right now. Derringer: Let's take a look at what Metal has to say. Roll it. ------------------- (The camera opens on Heavy Metal laying down on a bench, pumping up and down what looks to be over 500 pounds of weights. He continues for about twenty more presses, and then racks it on the bench. Heavy Metal is wearing a pair of black pants, black boots, and a white T-shirt that says "Heavy Metal - Forcing away the competition" and has a picture of himself on it. Heavy Metal sits up on the bench and looks into the camera, sweat pouring down his face. His long, brown hair hangs to the sides of his head and he has the look of intensity in his eyes. He begins to speak with a semi out-of-breath tone.) Heavy Metal: Joe Reed. You have a mighty large task ahead of yourself, and I mean that in the mental and physical sense. I'm not one to go down easy, and while you may think that I'm not a competitor up to your standards of athleticism, you're wrong. Let's take a look at my history, shall we? May, 1996-- I make my debut in ACEW while it's still the regional federation of the AEWA, and soon after, I win the Television championship. That meant nothing to me, but it was fun to throw around some losers from a pathetic fed. July, 1996-- I leave ACEW for better things with a record of 15-1. I join WWEW, signing a contract. I take my abilities to the max, overpowering every competitor that stood in my way in the singles ranks. Later that month, I decide that singles wrestling is too easy in that promotion, and I bring in my brother, MetalHead, who is currently training to someday come back into the sport. We form one of the greatest tag teams in the history of wrestling, the Metal Militia. August, 1996-- I sign a contract with PCW and dominate matches, but unfortunately don't win, against the Northwest Mafia. All by myself I took on an entire stable and nearly defeated them, it was fun, but I didn't have enough power all by myself to complete the task. I gave up on trying to overthrow the Mafia and stuck with wrestling. I won a few, I lost a few, and then I decided to try some other feds out instead. I leave PCW with a record of 2-4. September, 1996-- I join the AEWA while still under contract with PCW, wearing a mask, making my debut against Volcano Jackson. I beat the crap out of him to prove that I'll be a threat in the federation. Jackson beat me the next week in a steel cage match, by a lucky break of walking out of the cage. I continued to dominate the AEWA, overthrowing many of the top superstars. I gain the AEWA World Title in the same month as my debut. October, 1996-- My brother and I decide to test our skills in a top name federation called the CWF. We dominated the organization for awhile, and before long, we decided it wasn't for us, leaving with a 3-2 record. We continued on in WWEW, facing some excellent tag teams such as the _real_ Heaven & Hell, the Deadly Duo, and the Master Blasters. Later that month, we join EEWF just in time for the tag title tournament. We plow through the competition and easily take the belts. November, 1996-- After a long and illustrious title reign in AEWA, along comes Mike Stewart, unexpectedly, and he takes my title and ends my reign. I tried regaining it on a few occasions, but got screwed out of them on each. I never thought much of titles anyway. That same month I join up with a new federation called UEWA, bringing in the force of the Metal Militia. Soon after, we are named the number one tag team in the federation. At the end of this month, we vacate the EEWF tag team titles and leave the EEWF with a record of 6-1. December, 1996-- Metal Militia finally win the UEWA Tag Titles after defeating the Deadly Duo. I win yet another match in the AEWA at December Doom. The CWF, WWEW, and NWF merge into one federation and keep the name CWF. Metal Militia makes it's "return" to the CWF and defeat a couple of teams, and then the merger gets called off. WWEW closes as a result, along with Metal Militia's career in that fed. January, 1997-- We end our reign in UEWA by vacating the titles. We leave with a record of 5-1. I continue to prove my worth in the AEWA. February, 1997-- I hear about the fact that I am going to get another title shot in the AEWA at AEWA Extreme in a four-way match. Which leads me up to now. (Heavy Metal stands up and grabs his leather jacket and slings it over his shoulder.) Heavy Metal: Now, if that isn't a good resume, then I don't know what is. I'd like to hear yours sometime, Reed. (grins) I still have a lot of training and preparation that I need to do before AEWA Extreme, but right now it's time to rest up. Can't wait to hear from ya, Reed. (Heavy Metal smirks to the camera and walks out the gym door, and then the camera fades to black.) ------------------- Thompson: That certainly is an impressive resume, but the question isn't what you have done, it's what you can still do. Derringer: Don't worry about that, Metal can still do a hell of a lot. He'll be doing even more after he takes that title from Reed at AEWA Extreme, though. Thompson: We'll see about that, but Reed shouldn't be underestimated. Next up, we have a flash from Randall Osbourne. Let's take a look. Derringer: Goodie, a Knight. He should deliver himself on outta the AEWA for all I care. ------------------- (The camera opens inside the "Road To Nowhere" bar on the outskirts of Kansas City. The band is playing and in the corner booth sits Randall Osbourne. The camera zooms in as the waitress leaves with his order.) Osbourne: Once again, it has been proven that Matthews can't beat me without any sort of interference, whether it be Johnny Terror, Gallade or a foreign object. I find it shocking to see the degree of lowliness Gallade has reached. He's gone from a respectable member of the Knights to a lowly cheating underhanded member of the Church. Neither of you can defeat me fairly. Cheating might take you places, but eventually it will take you into your nightmare. The best thing about this whole situation is that I get another shot at you, Gallade. This time in a steel cage. It will be somewhat more difficult for you to get any help, but there are still other matters to attend, too. So Gallade, bring with you the brass knuckles. Bring with you the baseball bats or the lead pipes, because I'll be bringing along the steel chairs and whatever else I need for equal measure. If it's a war you want, then its a war you're going to get. I'd like to move on to better things. The Knights have a problem with both the Church and the ICU. I'd like to take care of these as do the other Knights. I'd like to take on some of the big guns of the league like Crowley or Big Ron Raider. Not too long ago, Heavy Metal issued a challenge to me. I'd like to take him up on that, but people like you prevent me from doing so. I plan to end this, Gallade. The name of the game is pain, and you will be Delivered. (fade to black) --------------- Derringer: Neat. He wants to fight Heavy Metal. Isn't that just wonderful? Thompson: Um, I guess so? Derringer: No! You don't GUESS so, you gotta KNOW so! Do you know WHY it's wonderful and neat and nifty and all that other stupid sh*t? It's because Metal is the only _true_ rocker in the AEWA, and Metal would kick his ass into the next millennia! Thompson: Okay, if you say so. Derringer: You're impossible. Roll the next flash from some more losers, the Battalion. ------------------- (The camera opens on a military base. Two men covered in mud are running with enormous bags of equipment on their backs. They are finishing up on an obstacle course run. The smaller of the two men wins the race by only inches....) Lieutenant Lightning: My name is Lightning for a reason, you know. Captain Crusher: Yeah, but my name is Crusher for a reason too. Want me to show ya why? (Crusher drops his gear, and starts to stomp toward Lightning....) Doctor I.Q.: Gentlemen, please! Save your energy for your match against one of the many underhanded, corrupt athletes in the AEWA! You want to mortify your opponent, don't you? Captain Crusher: Well, I don't know what mortify means. But I do know that I want to teach them some military discipline. And if that means crushing their skulls, then so be it! Lieutenant Lightning: I think mortify means to humiliate, and I wouldn't mind doing that! I want to inflict so much pain on these weirdoes that they run home crying for their mamas! When are we going to wrestle?? Captain Crusher: Yeah, Doc, when are we going to put this training to use, and put these NATO Powers into the hospital? The fact that they are named after one of our countries greatest alliances makes me just want to take their little heads, and pop them like zits! Doctor I.Q.: Hmmm.... That was a nice little analogy there, Captain. I have good news, men. I talked to some powerful people in the AEWA about your debut, and after talking about it for a while, it seems that a match will be signed soon. Just follow the itinerary I have given to you, and you will be ready to wrestle Goliath if needed. The Battalion will soon show that they are serious in their mission. Now start on the wrestling techniques that I showed you earlier, and leave the politics to me. (Both men stand at attention, and salute Doctor Quimbley.) Crusher & Lightning: YES, SIR!! (Doctor I.Q. stands by and watches the two men practice their moves on three other Marines. Captain Crusher picks up a fallen Marine, and does a suplex to him. The Marine's body crunches as it hits the rocky ground. Lieutenant Lightning takes another Marine and does a DDT. After the DDT, the Marine lies on the ground totally motionless. Then both of the Battalion do a double clothesline to the third Marine. The last Marine's neck snaps back as spit shoots out of his mouth. A medical team rushes to the practice area. Doctor I.Q. turns back toward the camera.) Doctor I.Q.: I pity the team that has to face the Battalion in their debut.... (The camera fades to black.) -------------- Derringer: Well, they don't look _too_ bad, but I don't know if they're anywhere near ICU material. Let's just move on. Thompson: Alright, our next flash is from Sir Gallade. This is one of those older ones, so.... Derringer: Yeah, you know the drill.
