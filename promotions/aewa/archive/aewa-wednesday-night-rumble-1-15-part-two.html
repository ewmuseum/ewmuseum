Wednesday Night Rumble (1/15, part two)

17 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~~ CONTINUED FROM PART ONE ~~~~ LB: They're setting up the cage! It's time for the Combat Cage Match! MW: That's right, in a very special match, Exodus and Heavy Metal will face the team of Ben Slocum and the Gryphon in a Combat Cage Match. BD: What the hell is a "Combat Cage Match"? MW: Well, I was just about to explain the rules. You see, all four men will be locked inside the steel cage, with no escape. Each team is allowed to bring one weapon, of their choosing, into the cage with them. And it's under elimination rules-- you have to pin BOTH members of the opposing team in order to win. BD: That sounds pretty damn cool. MW: It is, and these four men hate each other, so that should make it all the more interesting. Slocum and Gryphon are members of the Knights of Deliverance, while Heavy Metal and Exodus are members of International Contracts Unlimited, so there's a lot of bad blood here. LB: You could say that. MW: And it looks as if the cage assembly is just about complete, so without further ado, let's go to the ring! RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is the special "Combat Cage" matchup and is scheduled to go until BOTH members of one team have been eliminated! Introducing first, From Los Angeles, California, weighing in at 346 pounds, Exxxxxxxxxxxxooooduuuuuuuuus! His partner, weighing in at 450 pounds, Heeeeeeeeaaavy Meeeeeeeeeeetaaaaaaaaaaaal! (Anthrax's "1,000 Points of Hate" plays loudly over the PA as the hated duo from ICU make their way to the ring. Heavy Metal carries a baseball bat to the ring with him.) BD: So it looks like ICU's weapon of choice is the baseball bat. These two maniacs are gonna be tough to beat, especially in a cage match, with weapons inside. MW: Yup, they're right in their environment. This is gonna be a brutal match no matter how you look at it. LB: And that's why I'm so looking forward to it. RING ANNOUNCER: And their opponents, first, weighing in at 310 pounds, The Grrrryyyyphooooooooooooooooon! His partner, from Austin, Texas, he weighs in at 302 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his sister, Sondra, "The Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slooooooooooooooooooooocuuuuuuuum! (Laura Branigan's "Self Control" plays loudly over the PA as the two Knights make their way to the ring, along with Sondra. The crowd goes NUTS. They all slap hands with the fans, and Gryphon holds a lead pipe in his right hand.) BD: And it looks like the Knights chose the lead pipe for their weapon. Think they borrowed it from the Street King? MW: Who knows? They might have. In any case, we're just about to get underway with an all-out war! We've got all four men in the cage, AND PANDEMONIUM has just broken loose! The men appear to have paired off, The Gryphon against Exodus and Ben Slocum against Heavy Metal. LB: The Knights are at a SERIOUS weight disadvantage in this one, Wilson. They're outweighed by a combined 184 pounds! MW: Most of that is Heavy Metal, but you DO have a point. Let's just get to the action! Gryphon whips Exodus to the ropes, Exodus comes back, with a CLOTHESLINE--DUCKED BY GRYPHON! Exodus comes back off the opposite ropes, and gets NAILED with a lead pipe clothesline from the Gryphon! BD: Ouch. MW: Meanwhile, Heavy Metal is just POUNDING Slocum into the ground with that baseball bat. Slocum's already busted open! And now the Gryphon whips Exodus to the ropes, Exodus comes back, HOTSHOT--NO! Countered with a lariat by Exodus! LB: Slocum is a bloody MESS! MW: Yes, he's bleeding profusely after that beating with the baseball bat, and now Heavy Metal appears to be coming over to help Exodus! Exodus NAILS The Gryphon with a dropkick, and that knocked the lead pipe from his hand! Exodus has it! BD: That's VERY bad news for the Knights of Deliverance. MW: It certainly is. And now Heavy Metal and Exodus are just beating the CRAP out of Gryphon with BOTH foreign objects! And now Gryphon is busted open too! He can't stand much more of this. Now they lift him to his feet, and whip him to the ropes! He comes back, and they're holding the bat between them, AND THEY CLOTHESLINE GRYPHON WITH IT! LB: So far, ICU is just beating the hell out of the Knights. MW: Sadly, yes, they are. And now Exodus is going to go work over Slocum, and the Gryphon is left to fight Heavy Metal. Heavy Metal sets the Gryphon up, it looks like a Doctor Bomb--BUT GRYPHON COUNTERS WITH A BACKDROP, WHICH PROPELS HEAVY METAL INTO THE CAGE! BD: I'm surprised he could backdrop such a big man! MW: You'd be surprised how much strength you have when you think about the other option-- being power bombed BRUTALLY to the mat. Heavy Metal tries to stand up, and Gryphon nails him with a football tackle! And now Gryphon has got the bat! LB: This isn't good! MW: For ICU it's not. And now Gryphon is just going to town with that bat on Heavy Metal! Heavy Metal is now busted open! That ring is just COVERED in blood! Gryphon had POUNDED Heavy Metal to the ground with that bat, and now here's the cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Wow! That's AMAZING! How the hell did he kick out? MW: I really DON'T know. And now Gryphon brings Heavy Metal to his feet, but Exodus comes up from behind him, and CRACKS Gryphon on the back of the head with the lead pipe! That dropped him like a rock! And now Heavy Metal and Exodus are going over to double team Ben Slocum! LB: If I were Slocum or Gryphon right now, I'd just shoot myself just for being dumb enough to sign this match. MW: I don't think they're considering that. Heavy Metal and Exodus now pick up Slocum, and RAM him head-first like a spear into that steel cage! And now Heavy Metal is going back to work over the Gryphon. Exodus picks up Slocum, and hits a Russian Legsweep! BD: Who would've thought that we'd see a WRESTLING move in THIS match. MW: Yeah, hard to believe, isn't it? Exodus picks up Slocum now, and whips him to the turnbuckle! He charges in behind him, AVALANCHE--BUT SLOCUM LIFTS A LEG, AND IT CATCHES EXODUS SQUARE IN THE FACE! LB: Exodus is stunned! MW: And now Slocum goes up to Exodus, and throws in a front facelock, BUT EXODUS COUNTERS WITH A BACKDROP! And he takes back control, but here's Gryphon with the bat! He NAILS Exodus over the back with it, and beats HIM to the ground, and Exodus has been busted open as well! BD: That makes all four of them. This one sure is messy. Where did the Gryphon come from, anyway? MW: It appears he got the best of Heavy Metal for now, and he came to his partner's rescue. And now Slocum picks up the lead pipe, and they're BOTH working over Heavy Metal! LB: All these guys are gonna have a LOT of bruises when this one's over. MW: That's for sure. And now Gryphon goes back to work over Exodus, and Slocum is gonna take Heavy Metal. Slocum RAKES Heavy Metal's face across the cage, spilling even MORE blood, and now he throws Heavy Metal, who can't even stand up under his own weight, back to the ground. BD: These guys aren't gonna have any blood LEFT when this is over. MW: At the rate they're going, I wouldn't doubt it. And now Slocum has the lead pipe, and he falls down onto Heavy Metal with it, BUT HEAVY METAL MOVES! And Slocum ends up crashing down on top of the pipe himself! Heavy Metal slowly gets up, and begins to work over Slocum with the pipe! LB: And yet MORE blood spills! MW: Now Heavy Metal picks up Slocum, DOCTOR BOMB, HE NAILED IT! The cover! One..Two..Thr..Kickout! BD: BOY, that was close! MW: And Heavy Metal isn't satisfied with close! He picks Slocum back up, and BRUTALLY clubs him over the head with the pipe a few more times, and now he's setting him up for the grand finale! Heavy Metal lifts Slocum up, AND MEGADETH POWER BOMBS HIM ONTO THE LEAD PIPE! The cover! One..Two..Three!!!! LB: That's it for Slocum. Maybe he should consider himself lucky. He gets to leave this crazy match. RING ANNOUNCER: "The Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum has been ELIMINATED! MW: And the paramedics are already at ringside to pull Slocum out of the cage and get him to IMMEDIATE medical attention. He's lost a lot of blood. But now Gryphon is left all by himself to face Exodus AND Heavy Metal! Not an enviable position. BD: He should just give up now, and save himself some pain. MW: The Gryphon doesn't know the MEANING of the word quit, Big Daddy. Gryphon had just gotten the best of Exodus with that bat as Heavy Metal was pinning Slocum, but now Heavy Metal comes up behind him, and NAILS him in the back with that lead pipe! LB: That'll get your attention. MW: And now Heavy Metal attempts a kneeling headbutt, BUT GRYPHON BLOCKS IT WITH THE BAT! That's one way of doing it! Heavy Metal just headbutted that aluminum bat, and I guarantee that he's not feeling too good right now. BD: Gee, what would make you think that? MW: Wiseguy. And now The Gryphon picks Heavy Metal up, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Gryphon almost pulled off a HUGE upset there. MW: That's for sure. But now the Gryphon picks up the bat again, and he goes back and nails Exodus a few times to make sure that he stays put! I can't believe that the Gryphon is DOMINATING two men like this! LB: You're not alone. MW: Heavy Metal is getting up now, but Gryphon CLIPS his leg from behind with that bat! And Heavy Metal falls like a giant redwood tree! And now Gryphon is just POUNDING Heavy Metal to a pulp with that bat! BD: Heavy Metal is just a sea of crimson right now! MW: And now Gryphon climbs to the top rope! He jumps, AND BRINGS THE BAT CRASHING DOWN ONTO THE NECK OF HEAVY METAL! The cover! One..Two..Three!!!! LB: HOLY SH*T! HE JUST PINNED HEAVY METAL! RING ANNOUNCER: Heavy Metal has been ELIMINATED! MW: And surprisingly, the Gryphon pins Heavy Metal, to even the odds once again, and now it's down to one-on-one. And Heavy Metal is now being removed from the cage in the same manner as Slocum was earlier, although it takes SEVERAL more men to move his massive frame. Nobody expected these to be the final two men, but they're the ones who are here. And one of these two men is going to win it. LB: Yeah, Slocum and Metal HAD to be the favorites, but they're gone, and Gryphon and Exodus are still here. MW: Gryphon picks up Exodus now, and RAKES his face across the cage! Practically everything in the ring is now red. I'd hate to have to clean this up. And now Gryphon whips Exodus to the ropes! Exodus comes back, AND IS MET WITH A POWERSLAM! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Exodus AMAZINGLY still has the strength to kick out! MW: Yes he did, and but not by much. Gryphon POUNDS on him with the bat, and now he's going up top with the bat again! He's gonna try to finish Exodus the same way he just finished Heavy Metal! LB: If he hits this, it's over! MW: Gryphon DIVES, but Exodus rolls slightly to the side, and manages to pick up the lead pipe-- AND HIT GRYPHON IN THE GROIN WITH IT ON THE WAY DOWN!! BD: I don't even wanna THINK about how that felt! MW: And now the Gryphon is doubled over in pain! And Exodus NAILS him in the back of the head with the lead pipe! And now he picks up the bat, and just TEES OFF on him with it a few times! LB: He can't take much more of this. MW: Exodus hits him with the bat one more time for good measure, and now he picks him up, AND EXECUTES THE ARMAGEDDON!! The cover! One..Two..Three!!!!! (DING, DING) BD: WHAT a bloodbath! And Exodus pulls it off! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of fourteen minutes and thirty-six seconds, here is your winner, and sole survivor.... EXODUS!!!! MW: Exodus manages to pull off a victory for the ICU, but that won't be the last time they run into the Knights of Deliverance. This was such a wild match, really anyone could've won, it was just a question of who hit who, where and when. LB: Oh come on, the Knights never had a chance. MW: If that's what you choose to believe, but actually watching the match would show otherwise. It just happened that Exodus was lucky enough to get the final pin, and that's what decided this, but I don't think this little feud is over-- not by a longshot. BD: That ICU victory is a big step, though. Soon, they'll be running things around here. MW: Why don't you just take things one at a time, okay? BD: Okay, fine. Then at least tell me what's next. MW: They're taking down the cage to set up for it right now, so we might as well start talking about it. Up next is our main event, Shane Matthews is defending the World Heavyweight Title against Deranged. It should be quite a classic. LB: No it won't. Matthews will kick his pathetic little butt, like always. MW: I don't know, Deranged is a three-time former TV Champion, but he's always had his eye on the World Title. I think he has a very good chance of beating Matthews for the title tonight; he did beat him once before. BD: Yeah, but that was a fluke, and besides-- Matthews has improved since then, Deranged has only gotten worse. MW: We'll see, my friend. And with that in mind, let's go to the ring! RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is for the AEWA World Heavyweight Title and is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, the challenger, weighing in at 260 pounds and accompanied to the ring by his manager, Angel, he is a THREE-time former AEWA Television Champion, Deeeeeeeeeeeerrrrrrrrraaaaanged! (The theme from "Halloween" plays loudly over the PA as Deranged makes his way to the ring, with Angel at his side. The crowd pops slightly, but still some are booing. The cheers do seem to be a little louder than the boos, though.) LB: It looks like this crowd might finally be warming back up to Deranged. Those morons. MW: They haven't accepted him back yet, there were still a lot of boos out there, but slowly, perhaps one by one, Deranged DOES seem to be winning back the fans. But he can't concentrate on that tonight. Tonight, it's all on the line, and he's just got to do what he does best. BD: And realize that it's not enough. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, From Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, he weighs in at 222 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his manager, Johnny Terror, He is the current AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, "Sudden Death" Shaaaaaaaaaaaaaane Maaaaaaaaaaaaaaattheeeeeewwws! (Anthrax's "Looking Down the Barrel of a Gun" plays loudly over the PA as Matthews, cocky as ever, struts to the ring. He has the belt around his waist, and Johnny Terror at his side. The crowd BOOS like crazy.) MW: And perhaps the crowd's hatred for Matthews is what is fueling the cheers for Deranged. LB: Who cares? The fans don't do anything, they don't even matter. All that matters is winning or losing. MW: But if you let them, the fans can be a GREAT motivation, inspiring you to do things even YOU never thought possible. BD: That's bull. I never needed the fans, and I never will. MW: And that's why your Light Heavyweight title reign lasted as long as it did. BD: Shut up, Wilson! MW: Let's just watch the match, it's about to get underway. We've got a lock up, and Matthews gets the first upper hand, taking Deranged down with a leg lariat! Matthews picks him back up, and NAILS him with a legsweep! LB: Look at him take the fight to your pathetic three-time TV champion. Face it; Deranged is a has-been, and Shane Matthews is the future. MW: The match is only two moves old, Bertha. Deranged is up now, and Matthews FLOORS him with a shoulderblock! Deranged gets up again, and Matthews NAILS him with a dropkick! And now Matthews picks him up, JACK-KNIFE POWER BOMB! The cover! One..Two..Foot on the Ropes! BD: He's almost done already! This'll be the shortest title defense ever. MW: Don't count your chickens before they're hatched, Big Daddy. BD: Uh.... Okay, but what does that have to do with wrestling? MW: Nevermind. Matthews picks Deranged up now, and just SLAPS him! He whips Deranged to the ropes! Deranged comes back, and gets caught in a Frankensteiner! The cover! One..Two..Thr..Shoulder Up! LB: He ALMOST got him! MW: "Almost" doesn't get you anything. Matthews brings Deranged to his feet now, and charges at him, HANDSPRING BODYBLOCK--BUT DERANGED SIDE STEPS! AND MATTHEWS HITS NOTHING BUT THE TURNBUCKLE! BD: Deranged just managed to get out of the way. MW: That he did, and now Matthews pays the price for his high risk arsenal. Deranged picks up Matthews now, and NAILS him with a backdrop driver! And he follows it up with a kick to the prone Matthews. LB: And now Deranged has got control. Let's see if he can actually do anything. MW: Deranged picks up Matthews now, FISHERMAN SUPLEX! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Deranged may be winning now, but Matthews is still ahead on points. It's 138000 to 4, as far as I'm concerned. MW: Well that's nice, but first of all, the AEWA doesn't use a point system. And second of all, even if we did, YOU wouldn't be the one who keeps track of it. BD: Darn. MW: Deranged grabs Matthews now, and NAILS him with a jumping neck snap! And now Deranged returns the SLAP that Matthews gave him earlier! Deranged picks him up, and NAILS the champion with ANOTHER backdrop driver. He's taking the fight to the champ. LB: Yeah, but just until the champ regains control, and kicks his ass. MW: And now Deranged picks Matthew up again, and hits a legsweep faceslam! Deranged picks him up again, and PLANTS him with a fallaway slam! BD: (yelling) Come on, Shane, quit screwing around with this guy and beat him! MW: I don't think that's an option at this point. And now Deranged nails the champion with a fist to the midsection! And he picks Matthews up yet again, and hits ANOTHER fallaway slam! Deranged DROPS a forearm onto him now, and we've got a cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: It'll take more than that to beat Shane Matthews. MW: Yes, it will. Deranged picks Matthews up now, and whips him to the turnbuckle! Deranged follows him in, but Matthews lifts his knee, and Deranged runs RIGHT into it! BD: And just like that, the champ regains control. MW: Matthews picks Deranged up now, INVERTED ATOMIC DROP! Matthews now hits him with a jumping side kick, and a headbutt, and ANOTHER jumping sided kick! LB: I think he's just using Deranged for target practice. MW: Well he'd better not do that for too long, because Deranged won't stand there forever. Matthews charges at Deranged now, and FLOORS him with an elbowsmash! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Deranged's goose is just about cooked. MW: Matthews picks Deranged up again, and takes him back down with a legsweep! Deranged slowly gets up, but Matthews takes him down with a flying headscissiors! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Deranged is just being taken to school! MW: But that's dangerous, he might learn something. Matthews picks him up now, SPINNING DDT! Matthews lifts him to his feet again, and NAILS him with a slap! And now Matthews picks him up, PILEDRIVER! The cover! One..Two..Thr..Kickout! BD: THAT WAS THREE! MW: It was not! Matthews picks Deranged up again now, and nails him with another spinning DDT! Deranged tries to get up, but Matthews nails him with a legsweep! And now Matthews picks up Deranged once again, NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Deranged just can't get any offense in. MW: Matthews isn't letting him. And now Matthews sets Deranged up, SENTON--BUT DERANGED ROLLS AWAY, and Matthews hits hard! Deranged gets up, and manages to drop a fist onto Matthews. BD: Why does Deranged PERSIST in coming back again and again? He only ends up making a stupid mistake, and getting his butt kicked each time. MW: I'm not even going to dignify that stupid question with a response. Deranged now, NAILS Matthews with a forearm smash! Deranged picks Matthews up now, FALLAWAY SLAM! And now Deranged scoops him off the canvas once again. He lifts him up, JACK-KNIFE POWER BOMB! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Do you believe the nerve of that guy? Power bombing Matthews? He's gonna pay. MW: If Matthews can ever get back up. Deranged picks the champion up again, and NAILS him with a brain buster! Deranged SLAPS the champion, and now runs to the ropes! He comes back, and LEVELS Matthews with a high cross body! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Get up, Shane! MW: Starting to get a little worried, Big Daddy? Deranged NAILS Matthews with a forearm smash! And now he picks Matthews up, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Shane won't go down to some piece of crap move like that. MW: And now Deranged picks Matthews back up, and whips him-- INTO THE REFEREE! The ref goes down! And now Deranged is trying to attend to him.... BD: Why's the idiot trying to help the ref when he should be trying to finish Matthews? MW: You can't win the match without the ref. Deranged is still over him, AND MATTHEWS NAILS HIM FROM BEHIND WITH AN INVERTED DDT!! LB: That's what he gets for caring about the ref's well being. MW: And now Matthews is going to the outside, but what's he gonna do? BD: Johnny Terror is giving him a chair, and he's heading back into the ring! It's party time, folks! MW: But wait a minute! Here comes Randall Osbourne down the aisle! LB: What does that moron want? MW: Matthews winds up the chair, AND OSBOURNE YANKS IT OUT OF HIS HANDS FROM BEHIND! Matthews doesn't know where his chair went! He turns around, and is SHOCKED to see Osbourne holding it! BD: What business of his is this? MW: He's just making sure that Matthews doesn't use unfair tactics to win. And now Matthews is demanding that Osbourne give the chair back! But he won't! Matthews is getting angry, AND HE SLUGS OSBOURNE! That PISSED OFF Osbourne, and Shane is still demanding the chair, SO OSBOURNE GIVES IT TO HIM--RIGHT UPSIDE THE HEAD! LB: What kind of illegal tactic prevention is that? MW: Matthews was just knocked COLD by that chairshot from Osbourne! Deranged is still down, but he sees Matthews next to him, and drapes his arm over him! And the ref is coming to! Here's the count! One.............Two.............Three!!!! (DING, DING) BD: This is the biggest rip-off I have ever seen in my life! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of fourteen minutes and twenty-five seconds, here is your winner, and NEEEEEEEEEEW AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World.... DERANGED!!!! MW: And how ironic is this? Not too long ago, Osbourne won a title from Deranged that he didn't earn, because of a sneak attack. And today, Deranged wins a title that he didn't earn because OSBOURNE interfered. LB: Who cares about that? All I know is that Matthews was robbed blind tonight, and the next time that Matthews gets his hands on him, Osbourne is gonna pay! MW: But as for right now, we have a NEW World Heavyweight champion! And this crowd actually appears to be cheering him! And now he has been handed the belt, and he gets down on his knees and kisses it! What a great moment for him. He's certainly paid his dues here in the AEWA, and he really deserves it. And I'm afraid that's all the time we have for this week, so for the Lovely Bertha and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt, this is Matt Wilson saying so long everybody!
