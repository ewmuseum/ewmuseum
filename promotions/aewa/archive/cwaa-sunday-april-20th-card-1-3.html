Sunday, April 20th, Card (1/3)

20 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last Week on Championship Wrestling Association of America Eric Lane: Elbow to the midsection by Wrench- and another- and a third- and now the hold is broken! Kore Wrench off the ropes- clothesline! And- hey! What are THEY doing here? (The crowd starts to get a little nervously confused as Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas head to the ring, and stop at the foot of the aisle) EL: I know that there has been trouble between Thomas and Wrench before, but Hammer wants the TV title for himself- are Hammer and Thomas here for different reason? Kore Wrench hooks the Lt. in a bearhug, and Hammer and Thomas are whispering to each other about something. Maybe they're just scouting future opponents? Wrench squeezing the air out of Lawson- the Preacher has incredible power, and he has that hold almost perfectly applied- Lawson's feet are off the ground, Wrench's arms are low enough to get to the lower ribs. The Major looks a little groggy still out on the floor. Wait a minute now- Thomas just nodded and here he and Hammer come to the ring. Marc Thomas in there now, Hammer behind him- I smell a rat- two rats, more precisely! And Marc Thomas with a double sledge on Kore Wrench! ******************************************************************** Eric Lane: Hello, everyone, and welcome back for another action-packed edition of Championship Wrestling Association of America, coming to you from the world-famous Sportatorium here in beautiful Dallas, Texas. We've got a full slate tonight- Lt. Lawson is here to defend his Television Title, we'll see Marc Thomas and Jak Hammer, a tag team title match, plus the debut of second-generation rookie Little Yvette. But before we get to the action, it has been a busy week for Max Robertson, who steps in the ring with TV Champ Lt. Lawson tonight. Monday night he was over in Manchester, UK to face Joshua Barnes as part of the ACWF Rule Britannia! event, then Wednesday night he was in Las Vegas, NV, gunning once more for the CWAA Title held by Randy Sheridan on AEWA Wednesday Night Rumble. Let's take a look at both of these exciting matches. (A clip runs showing Barnes attacking Robertson as he enters the ring, and flipping him 360 degrees with a clothesline while Lane narrates) EL: Joshua Barnes wasted no time in taking it to the Professional. (We jump to footage of Robertson backed into the corner fighting his way out with a low blow and an eyerake) EL: Robertson again found it necessary to circumvent the rules in order to keep up with the Brownsburg native. (Robertson is shown applying a camel clutch) EL: Once he had the advantage, Max took the action down to the mat and went to his repertoire of submission holds to wear down the big ex-bouncer. (With a hammerlock applied, Max suplexes Barnes) EL: Before long, Max had targeted Barnes' arms, with a double chickenwing submission hold in mind? (Robertson goes for a slingshot somersault splash, but Barnes lifts his knees in time) EL: A critical tactical mistake by the usually meticulous Professional gave Barnes the opening he would need. (Barnes whips Max into the ropes and executes the spinebuster slam for the three-count) EL: Revenge at last for Joshua Barnes, whom for two months Max Robertson had played for a fool, had attacked from behind, and had ducked in the ring. (Barnes and CWAA Champion Randy Sheradin celebrate in the ring as Robertson leaves, clutching his lower back) EL: Joshua Barnes and Randy Sheradin scored a big win in their ongoing feud with the Professional- a feud that has gone from Dallas- (File footage of Max cinching a double chickenwing on Barnes in the Sportatorium) EL: -to Los Angeles- (Footage from the FWA Grudgewars event of Barnes punching Robertson during the Barnes/Sheridan-Robertson/Lawson match) EL: -to England- (Footage of Robertson watching from the aisleway as Randy Sheradin fought his I-Quit match at Rule Britannia!) EL: -to Las Vegas! This past week, on AEWA Wednesday Night Rumble in Vegas, the CWAA Title was on the line as Sheridan squared off against Robertson. Complete and uninterrupted, here is the CWAA Heavyweight Championship match that aired on AEWA Wednesday Night Rumble. (While the TV audience sees this footage, the live crowd watches a midgets tag team match which quite coincidentally will last precisely as long as the following tape...) *********************************************************************** Matt Wilson: OK- up next, we bring back some more young up-and-comers from Texas, as "Dreamer" Randy Sheradin defends the CWAA Heavyweight Title against Max "the Professional" Robertson. Big Daddy Michael Hurt: The what title? MW: CWAA Heavyweight. The Lovely Bertha: Are we on the right program? MW: You're never with the right program, Bertha. Don't you guys remember, at AEWA Extreme we had some guys from the CWAA, Jak Hammer and Joey Haven? Falls-count-anywhere? BD: I remember you stiffed me $25, Williams. MW: It's Wilson, and Bertha was the one you bet with. LB: Yeah, but it was your money we were betting. I can't believe you haven't paid him yet. Welcher. BD: That's OK. I've had Johnny Terror, uh... <ahem>... put in a call to a few people, if you know what I mean. MW: Johnny Terror couldn't call the operator, Big Daddy. (Darth Vader's "Imperial March" begins- no, Darth Vader doesn't actually perform it-it's just the music that often accompanies his entrance. A 5'11"-200 lb. individual in red and black tights with a shaved head and a natural sneer walks to the ring. The Nevadians who are present and who pick up a certain local Dallas station on their satellites/cable companies, or who follow the mark mags, boo.) BD: Look at this guy- he can't be more than 175 lb. soaking wet! MW: 200 is what the program says. LB: (reading her program) Hey, Big Daddy- tell you what. I'll take this guy, and we'll go double or nothing this time. BD: You mean, you'll take the flyweight here, and if he wins, Wilson doesn't owe anyone anything, but if this guy can't hang with the heavyweight champ, Wilson owes me (stops to do the math) $40 now? Done. (spits in his hand and holds it out for her to shake) LB: (to MW) He's doing it again. MW: Just ignore him and he'll go away. And now coming to the ring, CWAA Heavyweight Champion- "Dreamer" Randy Sheridan! (Fans pop as "Dream On' by Aerosmith plays and the 5'8"-170 lb. CWAA champ arrives. As BD realizes he is now betting on a man smaller than Wilson, a strange strangled cry escapes his throat.) MW: These two have been feuding for months, and the challenger, Robertson, has targeted Sheridan in the ring, on the floor, and in his head, trying to drive a wedge between Sheridan and his allies. He even posed as a friend of Sheridan's several weeks to try to sabotage Sheridan's title chances. LB: An important lesson I learned a long time ago: to stab someone in the back, it's important that you first get behind them. BD: Or have long arms. LB: Well, duh! That goes without saying. MW: I wonder if the CWAA is in the market for a pair of color commentators. BD: What does that have to do with anything? MW: Never mind. We're set to go, now. Sheridan and Robertson lock up- Robertson with a fireman's carry takedown into an arm bar, and Sheridan hops right up onto his feet- monkey flip takes him over- but Robertson still has him in that arm bar. BD: So these guys are like 150 lb. right? Are you sure this is a Heavyweight title? LB: I think he said it was the midget TV title. MW: That's enough, you too. Robertson cranks that armbar once more- now behind the Dreamer- waistlock takedown, and now he maneuvers into a hammerlock down on the mat. Sheridan pounds a fist on the mat- Robertson really applying the pressure. Now Sheridan wriggles free and hooks the challenger in a headlock. Now Robertson and Sheridan both up on their feet, Sheridan still with a side headlock. BD: You know, I've seen these guys on TV, now that I think about it. They're smaller in person than they are in real life. You notice? MW: Thanks for the insights, Big Daddy. Get hit with a lot of brainbusters in your career? Head butts? BD: You know, sometimes, I just really don't remember. Whole years in my career, just wiped from my memory. LB: Wish they were wiped from mine... MW: Robertson looks like he's found a way out of the headlock, as he lifts Sheridan-back suplex- no! Sheridan flips over- lands on his feet behind Max- runs him into the ropes and rolls back- cradle- 1... 2... kickout! Both men up now, and it's Sheridan with the arm drag takedown- and another! Now the champ cinches on an armbar. BD: Tell me again which one is Sky Low Low? And where's the Haiti Kid? LB: Are they going to do the thing where one guys kicks out, and the ref catches the other one? That's always a hoot. MW: For the last time, these are not midgets. One weighs 170 lb., the other 200 lb. Both are nearly 6'0", one is the heavyweight champion, and the other is the number one contender. Sheridan now with the Irish whip- dropkick- no! Robertson hung onto the ropes- no he follows with an elbow drop on the fallen champion. Back up- now a fistdrop. Robertson pulls him back up- Irish whip- legdrag takedown. Now he stomps away at the legs. Sheridan in the ropes, and the referee calls for a break. LB: Yeah, break his ankle! These refs get better all the time. BD: You said it. MW: He means "break the hold"- i.e., cease and desist, and back up. LB: The quality of officiating in this place started bad and went downhill from there. BD: Amen. MW: Robertson back up- and as Sheridan stands up, it's Robertson with a picture-perfect spinkick- and the Dreamer tumbles to the floor! (Crowd groans) MW: Robertson out after him- lifts Sheridan- drapes the Dreamer's leg over the guardrail and kicks away! Rolls back in to break the count, back out, and a stinging open hand chop across he chest. He rolls Sheridan back in- leans back on the apron- slingshot somersault spla- Sheridan pulls his knees up! (Big pop) MW: Robertson on the mat, clutching his ribs- Sheridan off the ropes- kneedrop across the forehead. Now Sheridan picks him back up- German suplex- this is the move he used to put away Robertson last time- 1... 2... kickout. Sheridan stands him up- kick in the midsection doubles the Professional over- swinging neckbreaker,... yes, he hit it. Lateral press... 1... 2.... kickout. Now Sheridan waits for his prey to get back up- savate kick knocks Robertson into the corner- Sheridan in after him- monkey flip to the middle of the ring! Sheridan up on his shoulders- franken- no- he spins- rolls forward- Victory Roll- 1... 2... kickout again! The challenger hanging in there, but the champ has put him on the mat three times now in these last few minutes. Sheridan with an Irish whip- Robertson leapfrogs- Robertson now with a dropkick right under the chin. Robertson with a stomp in the back now, and now he hooks on a Boston Crab. He's sitting way up on Sheridan's back- he practically has the champ's chin on the mat as he pulls the legs. Pretty clear so far that Robertson has been targeting the legs, perhaps to take away the Dreamer's aerial advantage. BD: Or maybe just because it's fun to hear someone's leg snap. MW: Robertson pulling hard on the legs, and we can see the veins sticking out on Sheridan's forehead as he strains against the pain, reaching for the bottom rope. Robertson pulls harder now, perspiration streaming down Sheridan's face- he's still reaching for that rope- look at the concentration on Robertson's face. Reaching- reaching- he got it! (Pop) MW: The referee calls for the break- and Robertson picks Sheridan up by the hair- European uppercut stuns him- Irish whip- and the Professional with a dropkick to the knee. Now Robertson picks him back up- small package! Randy Sheradin wraps him up- 1... reversed... 1... 2... re-reversed- and they roll right into the ropes. The referee tries to untangle them- Sheridan out to the ring apron, starting to favor his right leg. Robertson grabs at him- Sheridan drives a shoulder into the midsection- again- now the Dreamer with a sunset flip- he's got him over... 1... 2... kickout! Both men back up now- Sheridan whips him into the corner- follows him in- ow! (Crowd groans) MW: Looks like he was going for a monkey flip there- but no on was home and he landed spread eagle in the region of that second turnbuckle! LB: Do you guys really think that hurts so bad? BD: You couldn't begin to imagine. MW: He seems to have been slowed by all the work done to that right leg. Robertson now drapes it around the second rope- and stomps away at the leg again- still going after the lower limbs. Irish whip by Robertson- elbow smash in the upper chest. LB: But why would it hurt so much? BD: It just does, OK? LB: Let me see. (Drops the ring bell in BD's lap. BD emits a sound like a stricken animal and falls to the floor.) LB: That was cool! He's purple! MW: Settle down, Beavis. Robertson now with a whip- back body drop- no! Sheridan leapfrogs- turns- bulldogs Robertson right into the mat! The cover... 1... kickout! Sheridan now with the whip- catches him - belly to bel- Robertson spins him around in that waistlock- up and over- Northern Lights Suplex! (Crowd groans again) MW: He just dumped the champ right on his head! Now Robertson takes a leg- figure four coming up! And he's got it on right in the middle of the ring! He's got it hooked- and he has it on tight, too. LB: I guess he wasn't worried about Sheridan's high flying skills- he was softening him up for this. MW: Sheridan struggling against the pain. He reaches for the ropes- he's a good foot away, though. He drops to the mat... 1... 2... back up, but he still is caught in that figure four. (Chant of "Dreamer" picks up- he's more accustomed to the traditional Dallas Sportatorium chant of "Go, Randy, Go!") MW: Sheridan trying to roll over now- he can't get to the ropes, so this is his only chance to get out. He's turning- turning- he has him a quarter of the way over- Robertson straining to resist- the pain is too much, and Sheridan's shoulders drop back to the mar again- 1... 2... back up. The referee is checking for a submission, but "quit" is not in Sheridan's dictionary. LB: There are a lot of words that aren't in hi- MW: The crowd still chants, and Sheridan pumps a fist in time with the chant- but Robertson has been working on that leg all night- Sheridan drops back to the mat- 1.. 2... 3! He couldn't get up in time! (The "Imperial March" starts again as the crowd boos) MW: We have a new CWAA Heavyweight Champion- Max "The Professional" Robertson! He got the clean pin in the middle of the ring- I think it was that last leap into the corner that did the final damage to Sheridan's legs, and he couldn't recover. Max Robertson, new CWAA Champion! ************************************************************************** EL: Yes, we have a new CWAA Heavyweight Champion, as Max "the Professional" Robertson strikes another blow in the situation between he and Randy Sheridan. We're going to talk to the new CWAA Heavyweight Champion, right after this!
