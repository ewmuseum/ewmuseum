AEWA — Card Archive

Kickoff '97 (2/23, part two)

26 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * CONTINUED FROM PART ONE * * * * BD: These two guys really can't STAND each other, so the referee is going to have a hard time keeping order in there. MW: Unfortunately, you're probably right. This is going to be an EXCELLENT match, and... why are you two grinning like that? LB: Two guys that I don't like are getting ready to pound each other? I love it! MW: Primal Fear was on a huge tear here in the AEWA when he went up against newcomer "The Shotgun" Stan Crawford. As we all know, Crawford, in a huge upset, beat Primal Fear by countout. Since then, Fear has had several problems with the Twilight Strangler. Now he's going to face Crawford again in a rematch, and maybe beating him will put him on a winning streak again. Let's get to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, at a weight of 320 pounds, from New York City, here is "THE SHOTGUN" STAAAAAAAAAAN CRAWFORD! (Filter's "Hey Man Nice Shot" plays loudly as Crawford makes his way toward the ring, to a BIG pop from the crowd. He is dressed in a black leather jacket, and wears black tights.) LB: He looks ready to fight. BD: But is he ready for an angry Primal Fear? RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 360 pounds, he is the most extreme wrestler in the AEWA, PRIMAL FEAR! (Primal Fear makes his entrance to a HUGE pop from the crowd. He is wearing a black vest and brown tights. He has a determined look on his face, and quickly climbs into the ring.) MW: They stand face-to-face with each other. Each one is saying something, but I can't make it out from here. Now Crawford is pointing his finger into Primal Fear's face! BD: Fear knocks the hand away, and Crawford responds with a fist to the face. Here we go! MW: Fear and the Shotgun are exchanging blows, making this more of a boxing match than a wrestling match. Fear knocks "Shotgun" Crawford to the ground with an uppercut. Shotgun gets right back up, and is put into a side headlock by Fear. LB: Look at the expression on Primal Fear's face. He looks like he's trying to tear Crawford's head right off. BD: Now THAT would make the pay-per-view! Seeing someone decapitated... I'd pay to see it! MW: I bet you would.... Now the Shotgun pushes Primal Fear into the ropes, and shoulderblocks him to the ground. He leaps up with an elbow, BUT MISSES! BD: Way too soon to try that move. He should have stuck to a ground game. MW: I agree. Fear stomps away at Crawford, concentrating on the ribs. He picks him up, and drops him across his knee! Crawford's holding his side. Now Primal Fear goes back to kicking Shotgun.... LB: He's not even thinking about pinning him. He's on a mission for revenge. BD: Well, he knows he can't handle the Twilight Strangler now, so I guess he's going after lesser game. MW: I wouldn't call "Shotgun" Stan Crawford lesser game to anyone, let alone the Twilight Strangler. Crawford's standing up now, and Primal Fear sends him to the ropes. He bends down for a backdrop, but Crawford greets him with a DDT! BD: Bad move-- you're not going to hurt Primal Fear by hitting his head. There's nothing to damage. MW: Well, it seems to have worked. Fear's stunned, and now it's Crawford's turn to return the favor for his previous beatings. He picks Primal Fear up and powerslams him into the mat! Now he runs off the ropes, and drops a leg across Fear's throat! Here comes a cover... One.. Kickout! MW: Listen to the fans roar their approval, folks. LB: Who cares about a bunch of mindless fools? Both of these guys could be great if they would just forget about trying to win everyone's approval. MW: I'm not even going to dignify that. Crawford's still in control of the match. He picks Fear up, and throw him outside the ring. Now he's following him.... AND FEAR GREETS HIM WITH A RIGHT! BD: Now Primal Fear whips "Shotgun" Stan into the guardrail. Stan hit the guardrail, and flipped over into the crowd! LB: Fear's following him into the audience. Maybe they'll beat up a fan or two outside the ring! MW: Let's hope not. Fear grabs Crawford, but Crawford picks Primal Fear up, and slams him over the guardrail onto the concrete! LB: Now that's a quality wrestling move. MW: Crawford climbs over the guardrail, and grabs a chair! BD: But look-- Primal Fear has grabbed a chair also. MW: They both swing, and hit each other at the same time! They both fall to the ground. LB: Why doesn't the referee disqualify them? MW: I guess he figures that since they both used weapons, he'll just let them go. But I notice that he's outside the ring, and that he's pulled both chairs away from the wrestlers. And he's too busy with that to count out both wrestlers. BD: It looks like Primal Fear took the worse of that blow. "Shotgun" Stan Crawford is getting up first. LB: Yeah, but he's bleeding over his forehead where the chair hit him. MW: Crawford grabs Primal Fear, and throws him into the ring. Now he's on the apron. He leaps over the ropes and splashes Primal Fear! BD: Amazing that a man who weighs well over 300-pounds can leap over like that. MW: Here comes the cover! One.. Two.. Kickout! BD: Can't these damn fans just shut up? I'm getting a headache. MW: (suddenly inspired) Here's a twenty-- why don't you get a coke, or a hotdog, or something. Bring Bertha along. BD: (taking the twenty) Eh, maybe later, Williams. LB: Nice try, Wilcox. We're not going away that easily. MW: Well, can I at least have my money back? BD: Money? What money? LB: I didn't see any money. MW: I give up. Crawford's gone back to kicking Primal Fear, but Fear's trying to get up. Crawford sends Fear into the ropes.... a reversal by Fear.... he picks up Crawford over his shoulders, and Samoan Drops him onto the mat. And now he's hitting Crawford repeatedly on the head, right where he's bleeding! BD: It's working-- Crawford's cut is opening up. Now he's gushing blood all over the place. MW: Primal Fear picks up Crawford, and starts slamming his head into the turnbuckle! This is WAY too vicious.... (The crowd starts a count, and Fear keeps slamming Crawford's head into the turnbuckle for a 20 count, to the delight of the fans.) MW: The referee's having a conniption fit. He can't control either of these men, and it's REALLY starting to bother him. BD: He should have disqualified them earlier. Primal Fear bodyslams Crawford, and now he's going to the top rope! MW: He's going to execute the Bump Splash from the top rope! This could KILL Crawford, folks. He jumps.... AND MISSES! "Shotgun" Stan Crawford somehow managed to roll out of the way. I have no idea how he did it. LB: Yuck! Crawford's making a mess; look at all that blood on the mat. MW: I don't think he cares, Bertha. And Crawford goes for a cover... One.. Two.. Kickout! MW: Primal Fear's kicked out, and is now on his feet. BD: They're exchanging blows in the middle of the ring again. Primal Fear bounces off the ropes, and tackles Crawford. MW: And now he's biting Crawford on the forehead, right where he's bleeding. Crawford's in serious trouble here-- somehow, that cut is even more open than before. He's losing blood rapidly. LB: That's not my idea of a snack. But I've always said that Primal Fear wasn't human. MW: The referee finally pulls them apart.... wait! He's checking Crawford.... and he's calling for the bell! (DING, DING) Crawford stands there, shocked, while the referee raises Primal Fear's arm! RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of this match, as a result of the referee's decision to stop the match.... PRIMAL FEAR! MW: Crawford is screaming at the referee! He wants to continue. He starts to move toward Primal Fear.... but he stumbles from a loss of blood... he'd better get checked out by a doctor right away. BD: Fear, who has the victory, is out slapping the hands of the fans at ringside. It looks like he got his revenge. MW: It's a controversial victory, but I agree with the referee. "Shotgun" Stan Crawford has lost a lot of blood, and the physician is in here now, checking him out. He's letting Crawford walk to the locker area; Crawford's still upset about the decision. BD: I doubt that this is this last we've heard from these two. LB: Well, that was a rather violent match, but what's next? Is it time for that big main event yet? MW: We still have a long way to go, Bertha. Up next, Hank Thompson is about to interview ALL the Knights of Deliverance! These guys are EXCEPTIONAL atheletes, so I'm really interested to hear what they have to say. BD: But why do we have to watch this, Wilson? It's just another boring infomercial for those babyfaces, so can't we just SKIP it? MW: Not a chance. Take it away, Hank! (The camera shifts to Hank Thompson, who is standing in the ring, about to start his interview.) Thompson: Ladies and Gentlemen, I introduce to you at this time, the most popular fighting force in the AEWA today, the Knights Of Deliverance!! (The crowd EXPLODES with cheers as Randall Osbourne, Ben Slocum, Sondra Slocum, Sir Gallade, Caretaker, and the Gryphon make their way down the aisle. They all slap the hands of the fans as they make their way to the ring.) Thompson: Welcome, and thank you for joining us. Osbourne: No problem. Thompson: I understand that you have an important announcement for us tonight, right here at Kickoff '97. Osbourne: That's correct. We have all agreed that the AEWA is the greatest federation out there, but there are a few things lacking. For one, the AEWA lacks control. Madmen like Deathwatch and Kevin Larkin are roaming around, causing wanton havoc and chaos with not so much as a slap on the wrist. That is why the Knights were founded-- to give back some control to the federation. Thompson: So what's next on your agenda? Are you guys planning on doing something about the Church or ICU? Caretaker: Deathwatch and the rest of the ICU will be dealt with, right along with the Church, the Angels, and Larkin's group of maniacs. We don't have an exact list, but we do have an idea of who and what shall be taken care of. Sir Gallade: With Church members bringing deadly weapons to the ring with intent to kill, we can never know what to expect. Gryphon: But we WILL get some control around here. Thompson: You said there were several things that the AEWA lacked in. Could you possibly discuss that subject further? Osbourne: Yes. Respect. Thompson: Respect?. Osbourne: You respect this man (points to Ben Slocum) because of his ability to wrestle and because of his loyalty to the fans. Every title he has held around his waist has been for the fans. Every time the music plays, he enters the ring determined to take care of what needs to be done, and he does that with the greatest of work ethics. He plays fair. You respect this man, the Caretaker, because he deals with so much. This man lives with the fact that his brother is the epitome of corruption and immorality. He does not let that weaken him. He takes this opposition and it makes him STRONGER. The seeds of his family have been poisoned but this man will not be corrupted. He is determined to seek out vengeance. He lives for justice. You respect this man, the Gryphon, because he has done what many have not. He stands up for the smaller man. When Rick Showboat went nuts and attacked the official, who was the one to make the save? It was the Gryphon. This is a man you want to watch your back. This is a man you want to be on your side in a fight. He has faced many the big man. Not too long ago, he pinned Heavy Metal. You respect this man for his integrity and his willingness to help. You respect this man, Sir Gallade, because he, like Ben, has an incredible work ethic. Like the knights of old, he plays fair and fights for what is right. He is truly a better man than I. He once beat me for a Television title that I did not earn, and I respect him for that. I respect all these men, as does everyone in this arena. And they respect me. That is what the Knights are about. Respect. And that's what we are planning to bring back to the AEWA. Thompson: I see your point, and I wish you the best of luck on your mission, but there is still the fact that there are only five of you as opposed to the many members of the Church, ICU and the Angels of Death. Gryphon: We've taken that fact into consideration. All of us have looked through the roster of the AEWA and sought new Knights to aid in our cause and tonight, we shall introduce them. (Crowd ERUPTS) Ben: This first guy may not come as a surprise, but he's still a great addition and holds the same goals as we do. Ladies and gentlemen, I introduce to you, JUSTIN TROUBLE!! (The crowd explodes again as Trouble enters the arena. He climbs into the ring, and shakes hands with all his new partners.) Trouble: I say it's miller-time! (pauses as the crowd goes crazy) You know, lately here in the AEWA, it's been so darn hard to know who to trust. But you know what? I found a group of men who I know I can trust. A group of men who still have morals and still know what a kid's role model should act like. In a crazy world, sometimes you have to do crazy things to survive. And the Knights did that crazy thing by bringing me in, baby! I'll tell you like it is, with me at the Knight's side, we're going to put the pieces of that broken puzzle called "the AEWA" back together. So Larkin, Visigothic Horde, ICU, Church, Lions and Tigers and bears, oh my! I don't care, you'd better bring everything you got because you're gonna need it when you face Justin Trouble and the Knights! (The crowd goes nuts, and Trouble hands the microphone over to the Caretaker.) Caretaker: We are well aware of the current tag team situation. A wide range of rampart destruction and anarchy. But, we have found a pair of wrestlers that are willing to step up to the challenge and clean up their part of the AEWA. They are the brand-new AEWA National tag team champions, they are.... THE ASSASSINS!! (The crowd erupts yet again) Banshee: That's right, folks! The Assassins are now officially in the Knights of Deliverance! We are going to clean up the AEWA tag team ranks. Kevin Larkin's Visigothic Horde, watch out! Heaven & Hell of the Church, you'd better watch out! And EVERYONE in the ICU, you're number ONE on our hitlist! Shockwave: It's just like Randall Osbourne was saying-- this league lacks respect! And, ever since our return here to the AEWA, that's what the Assassins have been based on. Banshee: And, now that we are part of the Knights, we can bring this respect back to the AEWA! As long as we are here, there will be NO more attacks on President Freeman, there will be NO more attacks on members of the AEWA by ICU thugs! The Assassins want to take the tag team belts back where they belong! Shockwave: We want to get the belts back to a team with some respect! That team is us! Right now, two rulebreakers here in the AEWA, The Machines have the World Tag Team belts! They cheated their way to the top of the AEWA, and we don't like it at all! Banshee: It seems that now in the AEWA, you have to be devious and a cheater in order to get anywhere! Well, we are going to change that! Starting now, The Assassins are going to bring respect back into the AEWA, even if we have to beat it into everyone who lacks it! Slocum: We started off with talking about the lack of control in the league, and that's really the reason the Knights formed in the first place. We know it's a huge task to bring back the control to where it should be in the first place, but with these new additions, we're on the right track. We now have the necessary means to achieve what we strive after. As we said earlier, the madmen running wild on everyone will come to a stop, the attacks on league officials will come to a stop, the chaos is about to end, and most of all, order is about to be restored. Sir Gallade: Guys, I also have a surprise for you later tonight. Just watch what happens in my match against Damian Steele. (smiles) It's going to be GREAT. Thompson: Well, unless you guys have anything else to add, I need to wrap up this interview now. Thanks for your time, Knights, and now let's back to some more action! (The Knights begin walking back to the locker area, and the camera returns to our broadcast team.) BD: So the Assassins and Justin Trouble have joined the Knights? It really doesn't matter, folks, because the Church STILL has more members. MW: But the Knights are consistently recruiting new guys, so they're quickly becoming the fastest growing stable in AEWA history. EVERYBODY wants to join them, and I think it's great! LB: Here's a news flash for ya, Matt-- we don't CARE what you think. Anyway, is it time for the next match yet? * * * * TO BE CONTINUED IN PART THREE * * * * \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- "A New Beginning" American E-Wrestling Association The Official E-Wrestling Federation of Wrestle World and TNM The Best E-Wrestling Fed of the Year 1996 in the TNM Year-End Awards Be sure to visit our Event Center at: http://www.california.com/~bullet/index.html
