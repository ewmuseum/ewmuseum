Mega Combat: The Rising Sun (10/6, part one)

19 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power Bomb, and a look at Mister Crowley DRIVING the head of some guy into the concrete with the Premature Burial! Then the camera switches to the Lovely Bertha, Matt Wilson, and "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt in a packed arena) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to "Mega Combat: The Rising Sun"! We are coming to you LIVE from Tokyo, Japan.... BD: Jeez, things are a LOT different here in Asia. I still haven't figured out how to use those damn chopsticks. Anyway, we have plenty of action scheduled, so let's get it started! LB: So what's first? MW: The Shadow Warriors are about to defend their National tag team titles against The Machines. This should be an EXCELLENT match! BD: But these two teams are BOTH great! This is going to be rather confusing; who will I root for? MW: (sarcastically) Here're a great idea-- do your job, and be an UNBIASED journalist. Anyway, let's get down to ringside.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall, and is for the National tag team titles! Introducing first, at a combined weight of 514 pounds, here are THE MACHINES! (Simon O'Neal and Paul Wong make their entrance, and the fans give them a mixed reaction.) BD: These guys are complete opposites, folks. They can NEVER seem to agree on anything, and that could be their downfall. LB: Simon's pretty cool, but Paul really gets on my nerves. He's SUCH a babyface; it makes me sick. RING ANNOUNCER: And their opponents, at a combined weight of 532 pounds, they are the National tag team champions, THE SHADOW WARRIORS! MW: The referee has checked both teams, and we're ready for action now. Aaron Ladd is starting off against Paul Wong.... BD: They're locking up now, and Ladd just slapped him in the face! Jeez, he's starting this match off with a BANG! LB: Paul Wong didn't like that, and he takes a wild swing-- but Ladd ducks under it, AND NAILS HIM WITH A ROUNDHOUSE RIGHT! MW: Now we have a complete brawl breaking out in there! These two guys are really going at it! BD: Ladd seems to have the upperhand, and he's NAILING him with a series of punches! Simon seems to be pulling a chain out of his tights, folks. MW: (yelling) And he just NAILED Ladd with it! Now he turns, and does the same thing to Slade! Jeez, Simon has knocked them BOTH out-- all by himself! BD: The referee didn't see that happen, so we won't be having a disqualification. Paul Wong would have NEVER wanted Simon to cheat. They could have won the match FAIR and SQUARE, but Simon just HAD to use that chain! I think that he's still hurt, and a comeback this early is a BIG mistake. MW: You could be right, but he's not the kinda guy who'll let that stop him. The following contest is a special "Bloodbath match"! No countouts, no disqualifications, Falls-Count-Anywhere, and there MUST be a winner! Introducing first, weighing in at 304 pounds and accompanied by his manager "The Beautiful" Miss DeMeanor, here is The Twilight Strangleeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeer! MW: Probably, but don't give her any ideas. Primal Fear pulls the Strangler off the guardrail, and whips him the other way, RIGHT INTO THE RING STEPS! But Primal Fear doesn't cringe at all! He just turns around, and he sees Miss DeMeanor with the bat! BD: I'd say Miss D better run, while she still can. LB: Why does he have to go and do something like that? MW: Because payback's a bitch, and he wants to make sure the Strangler knows it. The cover! One..Two..Thr..Shoulder Up! BD: He STILL won't go down though. MW: And you have to give him credit for that, but Primal Fear's not giving him ANYTHING, other than a good beating. I wonder what he has to say about his World Title match tonight? MW: Let's find out. Hank, take it away.... (Hank Thompson is standing on the interview area, holding a microphone. He is wearing a nice tuxedo, as always, and begins to speak. There is another guy translating everything said into Japanese for the crowd.) Thompson: Thank you, Matt. Ladies and gentlemen, on the most recent edition of Wednesday Night Rumble, Wild Thing got into an altercation with Heavy Metal. It was eventually broken up, but the problem remains. Wild Thing is TERRIBLY angry with Heavy Metal, and they'll be wrestling for the world title tonight. So now, please welcome my special guest-- WILD THING!
