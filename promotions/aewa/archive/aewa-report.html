--------------------- ************** AEWA REPORT --------------------- ************** Welcome to the twenty-sixth official edition of the AEWA Report! We have plenty of big news to tell you about, so read on.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ RECENT NEWS ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ --JOEY VALACHI PINS THE HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION! In one of the most scandalous matches in AEWA history, Joey Valachi was actually able to pin “Crimson” Joe Reed in a non-title match. Not surprisingly, he didn’t do it without outside assistance. Near the end of the match, as Reed was attempting to get back in the ring, a cameraman hit him in the head with a video camera. The mysterious man then rolled Reed back into the ring, where Valachi pinned the champ. A few seconds later, we all found out who the cameraman actually was.... Randall Osbourne. Does this mean Osbourne has turned his back on the AEWA for good? He wasn’t available for comment. --SCOTT DANIELS VERSUS JOE REED! “Hotspot” Scott Daniels is set to face “Crimson” Joe Reed for the World Heavyweight title in the main event of the Championship Challenge. President Freeman made this surprising announcement on the latest episode of Wednesday Night Rumble, but we still don’t have any confirmation from Daniels himself. According to one rumor, which is currently circulating through some of the wrestling newsgroups (rec.sport.pro-wrestling), Daniels didn’t know _anything_ about this match. We hope to get an exclusive interview with him soon, but the pay-per-view is coming up later this month. Will Daniels actually be at the Championship Challenge? Order it and find out! --SIMON O’NEAL ATTACKS SONBY AND GETS FIRED! During an interview on Wednesday Night Rumble, Commissioner Sonby suspended Simon O’Neal because of a recent series of no-shows. O’Neal didn’t appreciate this, of course, and stormed down to the ring.... bad career move. As the Commissioner was leaving, O’Neal lost his temper and punched Sonby in the face. Several seconds and a bloody nose later, O’Neal was officially fired from the AEWA. We have to wonder: where does this leave Paul Wong, Simon’s partner in the Machines? Our reporters weren’t able to contact Wong, but we’re attempting to get an interview with him for a future report. ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ INJURY REPORT ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ TWISTED STEEL pulled a muscle in his leg during a recent house show in Washington DC, after executing a plancha onto Tucker Thomas.
