Kickoff '97 (2/23, part six)

26 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * CONTINUED FROM PART FIVE * * * * MW: Thanks, I guess. Anyway, coming our way next is the second of our three Heavyweight Title defenses, as Justin Trouble defends his North American Title, and perhaps his right to wrestle here in the AEWA, against the monsters known as Deathwatch and Leroy Brown in a deadly triangle match! BD: I can't wait to see this one. There's a lot of history between these three guys. MW: There certainly is, Deathwatch and Trouble have been having a war of words ever since Justin arrived on the scene here in the AEWA. This stems mostly from Trouble's dislike of Deathwatch's corporation, ICU, which apparently has no regard for the AEWA, or any of its rules. And as much history as there is here, I don't think these men have ever met each other face to face before tonight. LB: Yeah, but they sure met just a little while ago, in that wild brawl that just ended. MW: And I'm sure we can expect to see more of the same. And both of these men also have a history with Leroy Brown. He was Justin's last title defense, and Leroy was not AT ALL happy with the outcome, hence the rematch tonight. Deathwatch, on the other hand, is the man who unmasked Brown, when he was masquerading under the name of "The Masked Yorel". BD: Yep, that's quite a past, but do you think Leroy will have the advantage in this one, considering that brawl that Deathwatch and Justin were just involved in? MW: Not necessarily. It's possible, of course, but I don't think either man was too roughed up in that confrontation, and it may have served as just a warm up for tonight's match. These guys are ready for action, and they want each other in the worst way. LB: And speaking of that, someone who USED to want Justin Trouble, in a different way, is sitting at ringside. Check it out, boys. MW: That's Tracy again! Justin's former manager showed up at the end of his last match, and everyone expected a happy reunion, but that's far from what actually transpired. Just as Justin was about to embrace her, Tracy just slapped Justin, for no apparent reason, and ran away. Her presence here is interesting, to say the least. BD: What could she want now? MW: Only time can tell, Big Daddy, and right now, time's a wastin'. Let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is a special triangle match, and is for the AEWA North American Title! Introducing first, weighing in at 370 pounds, and accompanied to the ring by his manager, The Street King, "Big, Baaaad" Leeeeeeeeeeerrrrooooooy Brrrrooooooooooooooooooooown! (The Smashing Pumpkins' "Disarm" begins playing loudly over the PA as Brown makes his way to the ring, slapping hands with the fans along the way, to a good sized crowd pop. The Street King is at his side.) LB: I still don't like how this guy tricked and deceived us into thinking he was evil, and then, when unmasked, he turns out to be just another goody-goody. MW: The Masked Yorel was only a device that Leroy used to scout the AEWA before he let his true self be known, Bertha. Actually, I think it was a pretty good idea. He got the drop on a lot of AEWA wrestlers that way. BD: Yeah, and that makes him a real friggin' hero, huh? MW: I didn't say it was heroic, just effective. RING ANNOUNCER: And next, from New York City, and weighing in at 315 pounds, the leader of ICU, here is Deaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaatthwaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaatchh! (Metallica's "Master of Puppets" blasts over the sound system as Deathwatch appears from behind the curtain, and the crowd erupts into a literal downpour of boos. Deathwatch simply smiles at the reaction, and continues on his course for the ring.) LB: Well, ICU's got its chance to take a significant piece of the AEWA title picture tonight, and they couldn't have a better man going for it. MW: Yes, Deathwatch is ICU's head figure, but as of yet he has ironically never held a title in the AEWA. Justin Trouble will be looking to keep it that way tonight. BD: I just don't think Justin can handle him though. He's been barking up the wrong tree for quite a while, and tonight, I think he just might have to pay for that mistake. MW: Anything's possible here in the AEWA, and in no match is that more true than a Triangle match. Usually the guy who gets the least ring time wins these matches, just due to the physically demanding nature of the match. This particular one's gonna be VERY interesting, though, I can tell you that much. RING ANNOUNCER: And finally, from Atlanta, Georgia. He weighs in at 238 pounds and is the reigning AEWA North American Champion, Juuuuuuuustiiiiiinnn Trrrrrrroooooooooooooublllllle! (Tom Petty's "Makin' Some Noise" begins to play loudly over the PA system as Justin Trouble, belt around his waist, makes his way to the ring to a HUGE crowd pop.) LB: And what's gonna happen when he sees Tracy? MW: I don't know, but I don't think it's gonna be pretty. BD: Well of course not, Tracy's a dog in the first place, and when she's angry, she even looks ugly for a dog. MW: Thank's for that insight, Big Daddy. BD: Any time, Wilson. MW: Well Justin just stepped into the ring, and I think he sees her, but he's not making any motion to go towards her. He's obviously a little apprehensive after their last confrontation. LB: If you got your butt kicked by a girl, and I'd be happy to volunteer to do so, by the way, wouldn't you be a little apprehensive the next time you saw her? MW: He didn't get his butt kicked. It was just a slap, but yes, I do think that the nature of that meeting would make any man apprehensive. BD: It wouldn't make me apprehensive, I'd go down there right now and slap her right back. MW: And that was evidenced to be true by all the times you have cowered in fear from Bertha, right Big Daddy? BD: Hey, that has nothing to do with this! MW: Sure it doesn't. They have apparently flipped a coin in there now, and it looks like Justin Trouble is gonna start it off with Leroy Brown. LB: This should be good. MW: The two men lock up, and Trouble makes the first strike, taking Leroy down with an armdrag! Justin DROPS a knee to the back of Brown, and he's really laying into him! BD: Maybe Justin's got a little extra motivation tonight. If he loses that title, he may not ever be allowed to wrestle in the AEWA again. He's only allowed to defend his title now, remember? MW: I do indeed, and that has gotta be bearing down the back of Justin Trouble at all times. Leroy tries to get up, but Justin takes him back down with another armdrag! He climbs the top rope now, and he waits on Brown. Brown reaches his feet, AND JUSTIN NEARLY DECAPITATES HIM WITH A FLYING CLOTHESLINE! Leroy is knocked FLAT, and now Justin is applying the Trouble Maker! LB: It could be over for Leroy already. BD: The sooner, the better. MW: Justin's got that locked in tight now, and Leroy is SCREAMING, but he manages to reach out and grab the ropes, forcing Justin to break the hold. And now Justin tags out to Deathwatch! LB: I'm surprised Deathwatch even accepted that tag from Justin. Why would he want to allow Justin out of the ring? MW: I'm even more surprised the Justin was so quick to tag his arch-rival like that, but I guess that's how it goes in these matches. Deathwatch now helps Leroy to his feet, AND GETS A CHOP IN THE CHEST FOR HIS TROUBLE! BD: I think Leroy might be a little guilty of playing possum there. MW: I'd have to agree. Deathwatch is upset by that, and he whips Brown to the buckle--REVERSED BY BROWN! Deathwatch crashes into the corner, and Leroy comes right in behind him, AVALANCHE!! And Deathwatch CRUMBLES to the mat! LB: Boy, has the momentum shifted here or what? MW: You can say that again. And now Brown is climbing up to the top rope! He waits for Deathwatch to get to his feet, AND LEVELS HIM WITH A FLYING CLOTHESLINE! And now it looks like he's gonna go for the Power Bomb! BD: Already? MW: Justin Trouble already used HIS finisher, so why not? Leroy LIFTS Deathwatch up, BUT DEATHWATCH USES HIS MOMENTUM TO CARRY HIMSELF OVER--AND COUNTERS WITH A BACKSLIDE! One.. Kickout! BD: THAT'S why not, Wilson. MW: Point taken. Deathwatch rises first, and LEVELS a charging Brown with a big boot! And now Deathwatch goes up top, HE'S GOING FOR THE DEATH'S VENGEANCE! He waits for Leroy to get up, and dives! BUT LEROY TURNS TO FACE HIM--AND CATCHES HIM IN A POWERSLAM!! One.. Two.. Shoulder Up! LB: You can't accuse anyone of wrestling smart tonight. Every one of them has gone for their finisher in the fist three minutes of this match! MW: What can I say? They're just anxious to win. Deathwatch gets up now, and manages to stumble over and tag out to Justin Trouble. Justin comes in, AND IMMEDIATELY TAKES LEROY DOWN WITH A SHORT LARIAT! The cover! One.. Two.. Kickout! BD: Justin's just not giving him any breathing time tonight. He has been operating VERY fast tonight. MW: Yes he has, and still is, as he quickly scales the top rope! Leroy is still down, and Justin jumps, AND NAILS A FROG SPLASH! The cover!! One.. Two.. Shoulder Up! LB: Leroy's hanging in there though. MW: Barely. Justin waits for Brown to rise to his feet now, and he takes him right back down with a legsweep! Leroy tries to get up again, but AGAIN gets his legs swept out from under him! BD: Maybe he should just stay down. MW: That might not be such a bad idea. Justin is still not letting up though, and he NAILS Brown with BIG senton! And the cover! One.. Two.. Kickout! LB: Maybe Leroy should tag out sometime tonight. MW: Might be a good idea. Leroy gets back up to his feet, BUT JUSTIN LEVELS HIM WITH ANOTHER SHORT LARIAT! The cover! One.. Two.. Kickout! BD: This is getting rather ridiculous. MW: It's just near-fall after near-fall, Big Daddy. I think it's exciting. BD: Then you're a moron. MW: Gee, thanks. Justin picks up Leroy now, and whips him to the ropes, WHERE HE TAGS DEATHWATCH! I don't think Justin saw it! Leroy comes back off the ropes, AND GETS BACKDROPPED!! And now Deathwatch comes charging in next, BUT JUSTIN CATCHES HIM IN A POWERSLAM! The cover! One.. Two.. Shoulder Up! LB: Justin made a quick adjustment, and he REALLY caught Deathwatch with that one. He surprised even me! MW: That's not hard. But this is the matchup everyone's been waiting to see. Justin Trouble and Deathwatch, one on one. Justin picks Deathwatch up to his feet, and runs to the ropes! He comes back, BUT GETS LEVELED WITH A CLOTHESLINE FROM DEATHWATCH! BD: He just did a 360 on that move! MW: Yes he did, and now Deathwatch picks Trouble up off the canvas, and he whips him into the turnbuckle, where Justin tags out to Leroy Brown, and rolls out of the ring! LB: What a chicken! Deathwatch hits him with one move, and he bails? MW: He did what was best for him at the time, Bertha, and I don't blame him. Leroy Brown comes running into the ring, but Deathwatch meets him with an elbow! Deathwatch picks Brown up now, BACK SUPLEX--COUNTERED WITH A CROSS BODY BLOCK FROM BROWN! The cover! One.. Two.. Shoulder Up! BD: Leroy sure turned the tables on him that time! MW: Brown picks Deathwatch up off the mat now, AND CRUSHES HIM WITH A SIDE SUPLEX! The cover! One.. Two.. Kickout! LB: Deathwatch needs to do something here, or Leroy's gonna squash him soon. MW: Leroy brings Deathwatch to his feet now, but Deathwatch hits him with a chop! I guess he's not as hurt as you thought! And now he lifts Leroy up, BODYSLAM--BUT LEROY COUNTERS WITH A SMALL PACKAGE! One.. Kickout! BD: That didn't do anything but upset Deathwatch. MW: It took him off his game for a second, too, and Leroy manages to catch him with a jawbreaker! Brown grabs him by the head now, and drags him to the rope, ROPEBURN! LB: That stings, but unless he follows it up with something, it's not a very useful move. MW: Leroy grabs Deathwatch around the waist now, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One.. Two.. Shoulder Up! BD: I can't believe the way he's manhandling Deathwatch! MW: And now Deathwatch uses the ropes to pull himself up, AND MAKES THE TAG TO JUSTIN TROUBLE! LB: That's the smart thing to do. Deathwatch just needs to regroup right now. Let the other two guys beat the crap out of each other. MW: I think that's what he had in mind. Justin Trouble again comes in like a house of fire! He floors Brown with a legsweep! Leroy tries to get back up, but Justin NAILS him with a short lariat! The cover! One.. Two.. Kickout! BD: Justin hasn't let up ONCE tonight, but that might catch up with him later. MW: Good point, for a change, Big Daddy. Justin picks Brown back up to his feet now, VERTICAL SUPLEX--BLOCKED! Leroy grabs Justin around the waist, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX!! The cover! One.. Two.. Kickout! LB: Finally someone gets something done against Justin! MW: And Brown continues his assault. He picks Trouble up, and NAILS him with a jawbreaker! Justin tries to get up, but Brown grabs him by the head, BULLDOG! BD: And Justin don't look too good now. MW: No he doesn't, and he continues to look worse by the minute. Leroy picks him up to his feet now, and NAILS him with a chop! That stunned the champ, and now Brown is choking him! LB: Yeah, squeeze the life outta the punk! You know, maybe this Brown's not so bad after all. MW: That's not funny. Brown breaks the hold at the count of three, and now he picks Justin up, PILEDRIVER! Boy, did his head hit the canvas hard! And the cover! One.. Two.. Kickout! BD: How'd he get out of that one? MW: Endurance, Big Daddy. Justin's got an awful lot of that. He's proven it time and time again. Brown waits for Justin to reach his feet now, and he locks on a sleeperhold--BUT JUSTIN COUNTERS WITH A JAWBREAKER! LB: That sent the big guy reeling! MW: Yes it did, and Justin is quick to capitalize! He LEVELS the big man with a clothesline, and now he picks up Brown's legs--AND LOCKS IN THE TROUBLE MAKER! BD: Brown's got nowhere to go, that's right in the center of the ring! MW: And the big guy is just SCREAMING in pain, but he won't give in! Justin applies even MORE pressure, and Brown's fighting it, but it's just too much pain--AND BROWN SUBMITS!! LB: Hit the showers, Brown. RING ANNOUNCER: Leroy Brown has been ELIMINATED! MW: And then there were two, and would you just look at these two? They absolutely HATE each other. This is going to be an all-out war! LB: But Deathwatch has been resting while Justin was getting the crap kicked out of him by Leroy Brown. MW: True, so Deathwatch MAY have an advantage, but I can guarantee you that Justin's not going down without a fight. Deathwatch charges right in, and NAILS Justin with a forearm! And now Deathwatch grabs him by the head, REVERSE NECKBREAKER! BD: And he almost DID break Justin's neck with that one. MW: They're not wrestling for friendly competition, that's for sure. Deathwatch picks Justin up to his feet now, and he lifts the champion, ATOMIC DROP! Justin is stunned, and Deathwatch grabs him by the head, AND LOCKS ON THE SOUL STEALER!! LB: There's NO WAY Justin's getting out of this one! I bet Deathwatch would just LOVE to hear him submit! MW: That hold is EXCRUCIATINGLY painful, but Justin's fighting it, and he begins to kick Deathwatch in the midsection! Deathwatch tries to hang on, but Justin kicks him a few more times, AND HE BREAKS THE HOLD! BD: Justin was lucky as all hell that time. MW: Deathwatch takes a moment to regain his composure, but Justin won't let him! He hits the bigger man with a jawbreaker! And now he goes up top! Deathwatch turns to face him, and Justin dives--AND FLOORS DEATHWATCH WITH A FLYING CLOTHESLINE! LB: Where is all this energy coming from? MW: Deep down inside, Bertha. VERY deep down inside. And now Justin picks Deathwatch up, and NAILS him with a short lariat! The cover! One.. Two.. Kickout! BD: Deathwatch has gotta take back control, and fast! MW: Justin won't let him. He goes up top again, and comes off, NAILING Deathwatch with a flying kneedrop! Deathwatch slowly rises to his feet, but Justin just LEVELS him with a standing dropkick! Here's the cover! One.. Two.. Shoulder Up! LB: But Deathwatch escapes again! MW: And now Justin picks Deathwatch up, and just TOSSES him over the top rope like a rag doll! BD: And he's right out there in front of Tracy! MW: Yes he is. Deathwatch makes it back up to his feet, and he sees Tracy too! He's going after her! LB: And that REALLY upset Justin! He's coming after him! MW: Deathwatch grabs Tracy by the wrists, but he's SHOVED away by Trouble, who obviously still cares deeply for Tracy. Justin stops to ask Tracy if she's alright, AND SHE SLUGS HIM!! Justin staggers back, AND DEATHWATCH RAMS HIM HEAD FIRST INTO THE RINGSTEPS! I don't believe this! All Justin did was help Tracy, and she punches him to repay him? BD: Maybe she didn't want HIS help. MW: I don't know, but Deathwatch is REALLY pounding Justin into the ringpost now! He rolls Justin back into the ring now, and he follows him in. Justin is struggling to regain his footing, and Deathwatch goes up top! Justin finally reaches his feet, and Deathwatch dives, AND NAILS THE DEATH'S VENGEANCE!!! Here's the cover! One.. Two.. Three!! (DING, DING) LB: We've got a new champion! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of twenty-one minutes and thirty seconds, here is your winner, and NEEEEEEEEEW AEWA North American Champion.... DEATHWATCH!!!! MW: I still don't believe that Tracy just did that. What did Justin do to deserve that? BD: How about being born? MW: Very funny, but I'm serious. BD: Well how the hell should I know? All I know is that Justin Trouble doesn't have the North American Title anymore, so I'm happy. MW: That's good to know. Well fans, this is all very confusing, but the one thing for sure is that we DO have a new North American Champion, and it's Deathwatch. LB: That's two things. MW: Oh, shut up.
