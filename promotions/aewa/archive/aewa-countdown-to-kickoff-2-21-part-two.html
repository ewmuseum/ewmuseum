Countdown to Kickoff (2/21, part two)

25 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * CONTINUED FROM PART ONE * * * * ------------- (The camera opens on Sir Gallade, sitting in the casino at the MGM Grand. He is watching some people gamble at the slot machines, with great curiosity. As the camera approaches, Gallade turns and smiles. He begins to speak.) Gallade: Your chance at my Television title is right around the corner, Damian Steele. The question is, are you ready? I sure am ready to kick your butt all over the ring. I'll do anything I can to keep this belt around my waist! I hear you have been a big shot in other organizations, well you're in the AEWA now, and I'll let you know it! Get ready, boy, because if you want this title, you'll have to be prepared to do anything to get it, just like me! On to some other business that I wont leave ignored. Cardinal Chaos, you took the morality right out of me, and you are going to be one of the first to notice it! President Freeman, I want a match with this psycho-- better yet, put it in a steel cage, so I can really hurt him. Oh, and one more thing, at Kickoff '97, we're all in for a big surprise from Sir Gallade.... a VERY big surprise. (Sir Gallade gives a wicked smirk into the camera as it fades to black) ------------ KW: I know what his big surprise at Kickoff '97 is, Chuck. I've got some sources in the AEWA executive offices, and they gave me a little inside information. CC: Well, what is it? KW: Unfortunately for you, my top secret sources have told me to not tell anyone what it is. CC: Somehow, that doesn't surprise me. KW: What's that supposed to mean? CC: Most of your "top secret sources" are inanimate objects that talk to you after a few drinks, Ken. KW: Hey! My shoes have never been wrong about anything.... CC: I'm not going to argue with you today, so let's just watch this interview with Jawbreaker. ------------- (The camera opens on "Jawbreaker" Jeff Jannetty, sitting alone in his hotel room at the MGM Grand. He is wearing a white polo shirt, with black sunglasses. He chuckles slightly, and begins to speak.) Jeff Jannetty: Kickoff '97 is almost here, and although I'm not going to be wrestling on the card, I WILL be watching. I want to make my presence known soon enough, and the AEWA will never forget it! I'm going to kick some butt, buddy, and when I finally get my first title shot, I will OWN this federation! Don't ever take me lightly, folks. I EARNED this nickname, and I'm going to go CRAZY on everybody around here. The Jawbreaker is coming back, AEWA-- you can run, but you can't hide. (Fade to black) ----------- KW: What a GREAT name-- the Jawbreaker. Why couldn't my mother have named me that? CC: Because it's not a name, its a nickname. KW: So what? CC: Never mind. Randall Osbourne has a few things to say about his "chain of pain" match at Kickoff '97. Here he is.... ---------- (The camera opens in front of a familiar Kansas City biker bar, with Randall Osbourne swinging a chain and standing amidst a group of Harleys.) Osbourne: I'm waiting, Matthews. Where is the pain you promised me? I haven't gotten anything from you since the night I won the TV title. Granted, it was enough to scare me, mentally and physically-- but it seems I'm the only one that has been putting out the pain, and all you've done is take it. First I DDT'd you into the concrete a few times, and then to top it all off, I cost you the most sacred thing you had, the World Title. Don't get me wrong, Matthews-- costing you the title was not a thing I had planned, just an added bonus. You see, I have a debt I have yet to pay to Deranged. In my match with him for the TV title, he was cost the title and I had it given to me. That's not how I play. And when I saw you with the chair about to rob Deranged of another title, it was time for me to pay back some of what I owed. You have to understand, you only brought that upon yourself. I only took the chair to prevent you from beating Deranged with it. You decided to hit me because of that and, in turn, I beat you upside the head as payback to you. And it all comes down to the "chain of pain". Come Kickoff '97, we shall see who takes the pain the best. You're nothing, Matthews. You're another passerby on the lost highway, while many of us have a long way to go, 'cause it's a long way to the top if you want to rock 'n' roll. (fade to black) ----------- KW: Matthews is gonna destroy Osbourne later today, folks. I've NEVER been wrong with a prediction, so trust me on this. CC: I don't know, Ken. Osbourne HAS been on a roll lately, and he is VERY angry. KW: Up next, we have an interview with Ben Stroke-Um. (he starts to laugh hysterically yet again) Jeez, I really crack myself up.... CC: Just roll the footage.... ------------ (The camera opens on the home of the Modern Day Warrior and the rest of the Slocum family. It's a ranch in the outskirts of Austin, Texas.) Ben: It doesn't really matter what that creep the Twilight Strangler tries, I'll get the win at the Kickoff '97 pay-per-view anyway. I know I'm the better wrestler, and in the end that's what counts. Right now there is a lot of action going on here in the AEWA, and the Modern Day Warrior is right in the middle of it. Sondra: And that's just the way we want it to be. It may be Kickoff '97, Saturday Night Slam, Wednesday Night Rumble or whatever, you can all count on the Slocums showing up. We'll show you fans what us Texans and especially my brother is all about. Ben: It's been kind of a rough time for me lately, but that's about to change soon, as you will all see. The Knights of Deliverance stand by each other through good times as well as bad times, unlike most other stables I've seen, and that's really all that matters. The cheaters can bring their managers, foreign objects or whatever they want. I don't really care 'cause I truly believe that in the end, the best wrestler will prevail, and we all know who that is, don't we? Sondra: You, of course, Ben! Anyway, it's time for me to concentrate more on signing the matches you really want, and not accept challenges from every punk who steps through the AEWA office doors hoping to make a name for themselves. I think we have this fed pretty well scouted already, and we know just who to go after and when, but for now we're keeping it to ourselves. As for the pay-per-view and that sleazy Miss DeMeanor, don't you worry about her, 'cause I'll be there to make sure she doesn't try anything. Ben: Whatever you say, and as for the Strangler, well he's been running around bragging like he's some kind of champion-- but hasn't really shown anything yet, so no worries there either. (yelling) STRANGLER!! It's time to find out if you've got anything to fight with or if you're just hiding behind that worm of a manager of yours. My guess is it's the latter, but perhaps you'll prove me wrong. Maybe, just maybe, you're not a totally spineless coward after all, and you'll fight this fight in a fair way, but I doubt it. Either way, it's time to stop talking and start wrestling, so what do you say? (fade to black) ----------- KW: (still laughing) Boy, we just had a good laugh, right Carlson? I TRY to listen to what he's saying, but it's impossible-- his name is Stroke-Um, for crying out loud! CC: His name is SLOCUM, Ken, so PLEASE get it right. I really find no humor in making fun of other people's names. KW: Oh shut up. We now have an interview with "Wild Card" Jeff Hawkins, who, as we already said, will be squaring off against Paul Smith tonight. CC: Let's watch.... ------------- (The camera opens on the Hustlers, "Wild Card" Jeff Hawkins and 8-Ball. Hawkins is dressed in blue jeans and cowboy boots, with taped fists.) Jeff Hawkins: You have to be saying to yourselves "How can somebody so BAD be so GOOD." That's right kiddies, the Hustlers, the masters of dirty pool, are here in the AEWA. Now here I was, ready to make my grand entrance into the ranks of the AEWA, when Paul Smith had to do something stupid and bring his bodyguard out in the middle of me kicking his butt. Well Smith, you might have noticed I have a tag team partner of my own. 8-Ball, say hello. 8 Ball: Greetings and salutations. Hawkins: Then, Smith, that low roller, decides "I know what I'll do, I'll get in the ring in a no-holds-barred match, with a man who's already ticked at me." Good thinking, bright boy. You see Paul, they say you can win by losing, and sometimes you can lose by winning. I believe this is true. If you just lay down and let me pin you, you get to keep some form of career in this sport. Should you decide to fight, I'll break your arm-- maybe a leg, hell, maybe I'll get a little kooky and break your neck. Let's just say you won't be getting in the ring anytime soon if you show up, since there's only one way to prepare for this kind of match with me-- get acquainted with every chair, every bathroom stall, every table, every stair in that arena. Because come this weekend, you're going to meeting them again. Should that big trained orangutan of yours, Mister Klash, decide to want a piece, I'm sure 8-Ball wouldn't mind making it a Texas Tornado match and rolling his tail all around the arena. 8 Ball: I'll rock his world.... Hawkins: No doubt about it. Then after I'm through with Smith, it's the pitiful tag team ranks of the AEWA. Then the crown jewel, the Wild Card gets the Light Heavyweight belt. Holy Ghost, you and the Church of the Poisoned Mind or whatever get that belt shined up, because the Wild Card's holding a Royal Flush, and NOTHING beats a Royal Flush in this card game. Then you'll be saying "How can somebody so BAD be so GOOD" 8-Ball: Rack 'em up. (Hawkins points a remote at the camera, as it turns off. Fade to black.) ----------- KW: That should be a VERY interesting match. CC: Yes it should. Now let's hear from some newcomers to the AEWA, The Battalion. KW: I've heard quite a bit about these guys-- they're two VERY tough customers. Roll the footage.... ------------- (The camera opens in a General's office. Two officers enter the room, and salute their commanding officer.) General: Welcome, gentlemen. Lieutenant Lightning, Captain Crusher, please have a seat. It has been brought to my attention that you two have been complaining about lack of action. Lightning: Yes, sir. Of course, we don't hope that our great nation enters into a foreign conflict. But we, to be blunt, are getting bored. Crusher: Sir, I think what he is trying to say is-- we want to bash some heads in. General: Well, I have very good news for you. I have a mission that will be both interesting, and at the same time, will allow you to.... um.... bash some heads in. Lightning: That would be great, sir. Thank you for choosing us. Crusher: What mission? General: You see men, our Commander-in-Chief has done it again. He has decided that American TV, especially wrestling shows, are a terrible influence on our nations citizens. At the same time, he feels that our military could use a reputation facelift. Lightning: That is all well and good sir, but where do we fit in? General: I have direct orders from the White House to send two military personnel, and a specialist to a wrestling federation called the AEWA. It would seem that there is an over abundance of bad influences in that one federation. I have personally picked you two for the job. Your mission is simple, go to this AEWA and attempt to eliminate these bad influences. I have already picked the specialist to help you in your mission. You are to listen to him, and do what he says. He will be your manager. Crusher: Who is this specialist? General: His name is Dr. Ian Quimbley. He is a Doctor of Philosophy for Mathematics, Physics, Quantum Physics, Economics, Biology, and more that I can't even remember. Crusher: What does that mean exactly, sir? General: It means that he ain't no dummy, and he is your Commanding Officer now. Dr. Quimbley has informed me that your tag team will be called "The Battalion". He has also told me that he will meet you at your first match. He has been attempting to arrange a match for you with a tag team called "The NATO Powers". It would seem that this tag team lies, cheats, is dishonorable, and tarnishes the name of one of our greatest alliances. Dr. Quimbley will have a report ready for you, and will train you when you get there. Now go! Your chopper is waiting outside. And good luck, men! Crusher and Lightning: (simultaneously) YES SIR, THANK YOU SIR! (Both officers rise, and salute the general. They then jog out of the office, and head for the chopper.) ---------- KW: Boy, look at those two military stiffs. Do they need permission to take a crap? CC: Be quiet! They appear to be a very competent tag team, and the NATO Powers should be worthy adversaries to them. Next, we have some comments from "Ultimate" Jose Gomez. KW: He's been VERY quiet lately, so I wonder what's on his mind. How has the Church been treatin' him? CC: We're about to find out. This interview was recorded from a few days ago, by the way.... ------------- (The camera opens on "Ultimate" Jose Gomez, sitting in the empty MGM Grand Garden. The arena is completely silent, except for a few workmen setting up for Kickoff '97. The ring hasn't been completely put together yet, but Gomez climbs into it, and leans against the turnbuckle post. He is smoking a cigarette, slowing exhaling black clouds of nicotine. He turns to the camera, flashes a decaying grin, and begins to speak slowly.) Gomez: Well, they're almost ready for the big event. Kickoff '97 is going to be GREAT, but unfortunately, I wasn't invited. President Freeman didn't think I was MARKETABLE enough for his damn pay-per-view. (yelling) What the hell is his freakin' problem? I'm the most marketable S.O.B. in this entire federation, it's time I get some respect! But of course, it's not really Freeman's fault..... It's the Church. Ya know, I've been very disappointed with the Church lately. When I joined up with Cardinal Chaos last year, he promised to get me some title shots. Hell, when was the last time I even WRESTLED? I haven't gotten a title shot, not to mention a REGULAR match, in over four months. Quite frankly, it's really starting to piss me off. Now, this is a hard decision for me to make, but I really have no other choice. (clears his throat) I QUIT the Church of the Pounding Light! Those religious freaks have never done ANYTHING for me, so I quit! Cardinal Chaos, you can take that damn group and shove it up your ASS! I never needed you idiots, and I never will! It's time for me to move on. First of all, I am DEMANDING a shot at the North American title. I'll be the first Mexican champion in the history of the AEWA, buddy. Now that I don't have the Church weighing me down, what can stand in my way? NOTHING! People have underestimated me for a long time, but that's about to change. Mark my freakin' words-- that IS about to change. Cardinal Chaos, I want to end this interview with a warning to you-- don't you EVER try to give me orders again. I've spent so many hours in that Church, but you can't push me around anymore. Hell, look at the problems between Ron Raider and Bishop Bedlam, for instance. If you had any control in your Church, Chaos, there wouldn't BE any problems like that. But that doesn't matter to me anymore, because like I already said-- I quit. Well, that's really all I have to say. I'm going to be in the audience at Kickoff, watching everything that goes on. But watch your back, AEWA, 'cause you never know when I'll strike. (fade to black) ----------- KW: He QUIT the Church? That could be a BIG mistake, folks, and quite frankly, I can't believe what I just heard. CC: Neither can I. It should be interesting to see how Cardinal Chaos deals with this loss. KW: Yeah, but isn't it time for the match yet? We're running out of time, so shouldn't we get started? CC: Well, before the match begins, we have some pre-recorded comments from the Extremist, who's going to be facing Sewerdog. KW: Let's watch.... ---------- (The scene fades in from black and we find ourselves in the living room of The Extremist, which is fairly well kept. There is a 20-inch television at the front of the room, a black leather couch against the wall across from the television, a matching reclining chair is to the right of the couch, and there is a two-seated couch on the other side of the big couch. There is a small table to the left of the couch which faces the camera, it is primarily used as a beer holder. The Extremist enters the room from the kitchen, a bottle of beer in his hand, and he has a seat on the couch. After looking for the remote for a few seconds, The Extremist finds it, and turns to the camera. He smiles and takes a deep breath as he begins to speak...) The Extremist- I have a joke for you.... check this out. (He presses play.) TELEVISION: (AEWA promo) Be sure to watch Countdown to Kickoff, when the Extremist will be making his AEWA debut against Sewerdog! Plus, we'll have plenty of exclusive pre-match interviews, so-- (The Extremist stops the tape, laughing....) The Extremist- Wasn't that the funniest damn thing you've ever heard? That's right, ladies and gentlemen, Sewerdog will be my first victim. Well, all names are given for a reason, this guy obviously went down on his mother a few times. (He takes a drink from the bottle.) The Extremist- I am not in the mood to sh*t around, but I am in the mood to comment. Sewerdog, you're quite the actor. Yes, you pretend to be an actor. When The Extremist opened his mouth, you ACTED like you could kick my ass! I am going to prove you to be just another fake imitation. (The Extremist takes another drink.) The Extremist- What do I mean by that? Well, when the Power Rangers came, the VR Troopers followed, when the Transformers were the top thing, the Go-Bots came from the woodwork, and now when The Extremist is here, EVERYBODY is an actor. They ALL pretend that they can take what I have to offer. Well boys, I would step over my own grandmother for a beer, and I will step over ANY of you for a paycheck. Don't think I am joking, Sewerdog obviously does, and I am going to show him that I suck as a comedian, but as an ass-stomping son of a bitch, there is none higher than me. (The Extremist finishes off most of the beer in the bottle.) The Extremist- Follow my example, Sewerdog, drink. Drink a LOT. Find all of the Jack Daniels, the Moonshine, the Aftershock, and drink it ALL. Because you're going to need something to numb the pain. I think morphine would be more in order, so if you can bum some from your dad, I think we're in business. I don't plan to show any mercy, son, I plan to do my job. You're just a task, and you will be done away with... ...I PROMISE. (The Extremist stares into the camera as the scene fades to black.) ---------- CC: It's now time for the match, and this is going to be a VERY tough challenge for Sewerdog. I've seen the Extremist in action before, and believe me, he's on the most awesome wrestlers in the sport today. KW: But Sewerdog isn't any spring chicken either, Chuck. They're both excellent brawlers, so this should be a great bout. CC: To tell you the truth, I really don't like EITHER of these men. And the fans absolutely HATE the Extremist, from what I've seen. KW: That's MISTER Extremist to you, Chuck. CC: And what do you get to call him, Ken? KW: We're on a first name basis. I call him Ex. CC: And with that witty comment, let's get to the ring.... RING ANNOUNCER: The first match of the evening is scheduled for one fall, with a thirty minute time limit. Introducing first, from Detroit, Michigan, weighing in at 244 pounds, The Extreeeeeemist! ("Goddamn Devil" by Ugly Kid Joe bellows over the speaker system as The Extremist makes his way down to the ring. Most of the fans give him a mixed reaction, and he doesn't seem to respond to the fans. He rolls into the ring and stares towards the locker room, waiting for his opponent.) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, From the Sewers of New York City, and weighing in at 275 pounds, Seeeeeeeeeeeeeeewerdoooooooooog! (White Zombie's "I Am Hell" plays loudly over the speaker system as the Sewerdog makes his entrance to a round of boos. He flips off the crowd, yells some obscenities out, and enters the ring.) CC: And the Extremist is there to greet him. He pounds Sewerdog with a double-hand sledgehammer to the back, and sends him to the ropes. Sewerdog ducks a clothesline on the rebound, BUT NOT A SECOND ONE!! Sewerdog almost flips over completely as he hits the mat. KW: I told you Ex is great. Now he picks him up for a bodyslam, and crashes him to the mat. Look at him kicking Sewerdog! CC: He certainly is taking the fight to the Sewerdog. He picks Sewerdog up and whips him to the turnbuckle. He charges in after him, and is met with a foot to the face. Sewerdog hops up on the first turnbuckle, and drops an elbow onto the Extremist. KW: Now Sewerdog picks up Ex, and slaps on a side headlock. Ex lifts him up and suplexes him back. He's going to destroy Sewerdog! CC: I'll be sure to tell Sewerdog you thought so. (Ken Wilson turns an ugly shade of white.) KW: You wouldn't.... he'd KILL me.... Chuck, after all we've been through! CC: Be quiet, Ken. The Extremist has been working on the legs of Sewerdog, and is putting a spinning toehold on the man. Why doesn't he try to lock the legs in a figure-four? KW: (still babbling) Sewerdog.... you know I'm just joking.... I'm your biggest fan.... it was.... CC: Nevermind. (grabs a twenty dollar bill from his wallet) Ken, go get yourself a soda. You've got to calm down and call the match! (Ken's eyes light up, and he grabs the bill.) KW: Can I get a hot dog also? CC: (rolls his eyes) Fine. Get a hot dog. (Wilson leaves} That's the last I'll ever see of my money, but at least he's gone. The Extremist has been working over Sewerdog's right leg the entire time with a combination of leglocks and sheer brutality. His strategy makes sense- take out a leg, and the bigger man's vulnerable. Now he drops the leg, and bounces off the rope for a spinning elbowdrop! He covers.... 1.... 2... SEWERDOG KICKS OUT! CC: You may not like him, but he's not going down that easily. The Extremist bounces off the ropes again for another elbowdrop, but Sewerdog moved out of the way! The Extremist hit nothing but canvas! Now it's Sewerdog picking The Extremist up in a suplex.... he's holding him up, and he drops him on the ground! A cover! 1.... THE EXTREMIST EASILY KICKS OUT! CC: The Extremist kicks out easily. Sewerdog grabs the Extremist's head, but the Extremist hits him with a right hand. Sewerdog's stunned, and the Extremist bends him over with a kick to the gut. A pickup.... Power bomb! But rather than cover him, he picks him up for another Power bomb. This should be it.... 1.... 2... KICKOUT! CC: Now the Extremist picks him up! That could be very risky. He sends him to the ropes, and stun-guns him on the tope rope! Sewerdog is just about out on his feet. (Ken Wilson comes back with a hotdog and a coke, grinning wildly. He sits back down at the booth.) CC: Ken, you're back to your old self. Where's my change? KW: Change? (looks in the ring) I told you Ex would clean Sewerdog's clock! CC: (rolling his eyes) Back to the match. The Extremist puts Sewerdog on the top turnbuckle, but he's facing out towards the audience. Now the Extremist backs up to the opposite corner. KW: Here comes the Extermination maneuver.... CC: The Extremist runs, and-- GOOD GOD! He leapt over the ropes, grabbed Sewerdog's head, and rammed it to the steel ringpost! Sewerdog just collapses! The Extremist rolls back into the ring, and makes a cover that should be academic.... 1.... 2... 3!! (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen, at nine minutes and twenty-four seconds, here is your winner.... THE EXTREEEEEMIST!! CC: An amazing finishing move by the Extremist. He's going to be a force in the AEWA. KW: Yeah, mark my words-- Ex is going to be holding all the titles soon. He's a genuine superstar. CC: Well, that's all the time we have for this Countdown program, so it's time for us to sign off. In a few minutes, the actual Kickoff '97 pay-per-view will begin, so folks, call your cable company and order it NOW! You only have fifteen minutes left! KW: Ladies and gentlemen, let me put it more eloquently-- if you don't order Kickoff '97, I'll personally come to your trailer park and kick your ASS! Don't waste time, pick up that phone right now! CC: See ya there, folks.... (fade to black) \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- "Special thanks to Mike Sonby!"
