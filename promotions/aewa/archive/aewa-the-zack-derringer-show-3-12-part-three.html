The Zack Derringer Show (3/12, part three)

15 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * CONTINUED FROM PART TWO * * * * ----------- (The camera opens on Exodus, standing in front of a black backdrop with his arms folded. He is wearing his usual black all-over suit, with the electric blue streaks going across the sides. His long black hair hangs to the sides of his head, and he glares into the camera with a cynical smile. He has the Exodus Championship title over his right shoulder, and he pats it as he begins to speak.) Exodus: Gryphon, it looks like my match got signed, and I guarantee, I don't want to be the one that goes over the top of the scaffold. I _also_ intend to KEEP my Exodus Championship title on that night, no matter _how_ hard you try. This match is going to be all mine for the taking and you are just going to be the fly in my soup, bugging me until I get rid of you. (Exodus tilts his head to the side and thinks for a second.) Exodus: I've gone from federation to federation beating the crap out of people and smashing their hopes of becoming anything, and you are going to be no different, my friend. I didn't mean to hit your little girlfriend and I wish I had hit you, because I'm not the kind of person that intentionally hurts women, not that the broken nose will hurt her looks or anything-- she's already dog ugly. (Exodus grins and points his finger at the camera.) Exodus: You are going to find out why my name is Exodus soon enough. At AEWA Extreme, I will be your pain and suffering, I will be the hatred that takes you apart, I will be the man that forces you to realize your destiny is to suffer, and at the pay-per-view.... I will be the one walking away the victor. Count yourself out, because you have no future. (Exodus laughs and the camera fades to black.) ---------- ZD: Man, he's so awesome. Gryphon doesn't even stand a chance in that match! HT: We'll see, Zack. We have an interview from Super Blond up next, let's take a look. ZD: Oh goodie, let's see what Mr. Stupid-Blonde has to say today! ---------- (The scene opens to the same gym as previously in Vancouver, BC. Super Blonde is sparring against a local pro, and Kismet is taking notes at the timekeeper's table....) (Super-Blonde executes a tilt-a-whirl suplex on the pro, and he goes for the cover.... one.... two.... foot on the ropes!) Kismet: Now that was a sloppy cover if I've ever seen one. (Super-Blonde sets the pro on the top turnbuckle, and executes a top-rope DDT. Super-Blonde with the cover.... one... kickout!) Kismet: You're never gonna beat Deathwatch like that. Stop with the semi-power moves and work on your athletics. Blonde: Yeah.... so he can grab me and put me through the mat. Good idea. Kismet: Fine, you think you're gonna get him on the top rope, you're crazy. Blonde: This is for a title shot. I don't wanna make one mistake and mess it up. I'd rather stick to what won't cost me the match. Kismet: Fine. Whatever. Not like you're gonna win anyway. (Super-Blonde stops and signals a timeout to his training partner, and turns back to Kismet.) Blonde: And what the hell does that mean? Kismet: There's a reason he's the champ and you've never held a belt to save your life. Blonde: Thanks for the vote of confidence there. Well, I'm just hoping I can catch him on a bad break.... Kismet: Need more than that. Speaking of that, he's picking me up in half an hour.... Need the car.... Bye! (She grabs the keys and runs out the door. Super Blonde hangs his head and hears Vancouver's distinctive storm outside. Fade to black) ---------------------------------------- ZD: And next time on "This Dysfunctional Family...." HA! HT: That isn't very nice, I think it's sad what's happening to them all because of the sadistic Deathwatch. ZD: He's not sadistic, dammit, he's just misunderstood. We've been over that. HT: Yeah, right. Let's go to our next flash from the Twilight Strangler. ------------- (The camera opens on the Twilight Strangler and Miss DeMeanor, sitting in a office room. There seems to be some tension in the room, and Miss DeMeanor is talking into a telephone receiver. The Strangler sits on the edge of his seat, eagerly awaiting the important news.) DeMeanor: Okay, thanks.... this is great! (She hangs up the phone.) DeMeanor: Your brother.... Strangler: What about him? DeMeanor: I got him out of jail! I got him out and now he can come wrestle with you here in the AEWA! Isn't that great? Strangler: You did WHAT? No.... my brother and I don't get along. How could you do this? How COULD you? Dammit, I can't BELIEVE you did this! This really complicates everything. DeMeanor: We talked about it! We said he could help you here, remember? We thought that you and him could show the world what you are all about. What's wrong with that? Strangler: Look, I have got to make sure he doesn't get out. I.... I.... I gotta get over there! (The Strangler jumps up from his seat and runs out of the room, with a VERY upset expression on his face.) DeMeanor: Tommy.... get back here! Damn it, why does he always do this to me? What the hell is going on? (fade to black) ---------------- ZD: Hmm, I wonder who his "brother" is and why is he coming here? HT: We've seen what AEWA's history of "brothers" are. The Caretaker and Deathwatch, Justin and Maxx Trouble, now the Twilight Strangler and this guy who's coming here. ZD: Wonder if his brother is going to kick his ass like Maxx did Justin. HT: All will be solved in due time, so let's go to our next flash from a newcomer to AEWA. -------------- (The camera opens on a man sitting on the bench of a gym. He is wearing flip-up sunglasses with one lens up and one down. He has a bandanna tied around his head, disguising his hair and is wearing jeans. He isn't wearing a top, so his muscular frame is visible. He picks up a bottle of Energade and slurps from it. He then turns to the camera and begins to speak, whipping the Energade from his lips as he does so.) Man: Hello, all you great people out there. (He pauses and looks at his shoes before continuing.) Man: Some of you may recognize me, but only fans of the CWAA, I may look like little brother over there but don't mix us up. (He pauses again to emphasize what he said.) Man: I am Rob Stevens, the party animal, the walk on the wildside, but whatever you want to call me.... you say it nicely or I'll just walk right up to you and kick your butt from here to Ethiopia. Some of you in the AEWA don't act nice. I'm going to have to correct that quickly before you get out of hand. But.... listen, what I want to say is that this wrestling league simply isn't right without justice and well behaved people. (He flings a towel across his shoulder and takes another slurp from the Energade.) Man: I may have been bad before in my life.... But then I put myself back on track. I righted the wrongs, and people who can't understand that had better just start to, or else the walk on the wildside will be a trip you won't forget. And DON'T you forget it. (He stands up and begins to walk out of the door, he pauses and turns back, looking at the camera and pointing his finger casually.) Man: I challenge anyone who can step up to the plate with the San Jose Sidewinder. You're all gonna be gone by the time I'm through with ya. (The camera fades to black as Rob walks out of the gym. Above the doors, the sign reads: "Steven's Gymnasium And Sparring Room.") ------------- HT: Looks good to me, what do you think? ZD: As always, another do-gooder. God, these morons are really getting to me. HT: Maybe if you weren't a do-badder you would think higher of him. ZD: Nah. Next flash is from "The Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi, who is back in the AEWA, lookin' pretty impressive, too. -------------- (The camera opens on Matt Wilson, standing ringside at a sold-out AEWA event. He is holding a microphone, ready to begin the interview.) Wilson: Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome a man who made his return to the AEWA in a big way. He's the four-time AEWA World Light Heavyweight Champion of the World.... "Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi! (A crescendo of boos greets the Cardiac Kid as he struts to ringside, with the AEWA belt draped over his shoulder. He's dressed in black jeans, a leather jacket, and a T-shirt with a similar design to the popular "AEWA ROCKS!" T-shirt, but instead says "AEWA SUCKS!" Valachi displays the shirt, to universal disdain.) Wilson: For Pete's sake, Joey, would it kill you not to insult the organization that pays your salary and made you a star on national television? Especially OUR national television show? Valachi: Truth hurts, Matthew. Deal with it. Wilson: Some things never change. So, Joey, why'd you leave, and even more importantly, why'd you come back? Valachi: I left because the AEWA sucks. I came back for the same reason. You see, this title (pats the belt) is mine. Always has been. I own the Light Heavyweight division around here, and you can't deny it. (Wilson regretfully nods his head) Oh, and by the way, it's FIVE times I've held this belt, pinhead. Wilson: That still doesn't answer my question. Why did you.... Valachi: (putting a hand over Wilson's mouth.) Don't interrupt while I'm working. Do I go to the brothel and pull your wife off the sailors? (Wilson turns an interesting shade of red.) I got bored around here. I waited and waited for you losers to find me a decent light-heavyweight, and the best you could do was the Flying Nicaraguan. I even watched your crummy TV show while I was gone. You still don't have any good light-heavys. So I thought, hey, free belt. (pats title again.) Wilson: That's not true. We've got plenty of good light-heavyweights in the AEWA. Valachi: Say that again and the FCC will pull you off the air. There are laws about popping B.S. like that. Wilson: What about El Aviadore? You'll face him for the title next week. Valachi: You have GOT to be kidding me. This (points to the "AEWA SUCKS" logo on his shirt) is the only reason that loser's getting a title shot. The only reason I'm even wasting my time on that overmatched putz is it's been a while since the AEWA got a look at what I can do in the ring. Never let it be said the Cardiac Kid didn't care about his fans. Wilson: Yeah, all three of them. Well, your personal charm hasn't picked up, but unfortunately, you're the AEWA Light-Heavyweight Champion of the world. Though in my opinion, you looked better with the mustache and beard. Valachi: Oh, you're funny. Aviadork, bask in reflected glory. You get to add your name to an impressive list of nobodies made famous by getting beat by the Cardiac Kid. That's a fact. Just like this is. (points again to the "AEWA SUCKS" logo, then walks off to a rousing chorus of boos.) Wilson: I think we need to have this place fumigated. Back to you, Hank and Zack. (fade to black) --------------- ZD: Joey, you have my utmost respect as a professional wrestler, we could use someone like you in ICU, but it's your call. ICU isn't a double crossing group of thugs like some OTHER organizations. We watch out for each other and we all have one common goal.... to take the AEWA for our own. Think about it. HT: Trying to recruit more members, eh? ZD: Damn straight. HT: Well, while you're recruiting people, let's watch "The Shotgun" Stan Crawford as he prepares for his match with Primal Fear. --------------- ("The Shotgun" Stan Crawford stands in front of the Mall of America. He is wearing black jeans and a white "AEWA" T-shirt. His hair is hidden under a backwards "AEWA" baseball cap, and he wears black sunglasses.) Crawford: Primal Fear, in just a few days, I will face you in a falls-count-anywhere match here at the Mall of America. I will also be facing you at the pay-per-view, but I can extract my revenge on you right here first. There will be no escape from me. You call yourself the most extreme wrestler in the AEWA today, but you will find out that I can be just as extreme as you. I can swing a chair, I can smash through tables, I can do everything you can do, and I can do it BETTER! (He turns and starts to walk towards the Mall. He motions for the camera to follow.) Crawford: In fact, starting right now, I'm going to go around to some of the shops here in the mall and warn the owners about our match. You never know where I'm going to pin you, Primal Fear. Will it be on a table at a restaurant? In the electronics department at a department store? Next to the Power Rangers in a toy store? I hope the referee can keep up with us, because I'm planning to go all out to beat you! (As he reaches the front doors of the mall, he stops.) Crawford: Primal Fear, notify your next of kin, because "The Shotgun" is going to blow you away! (He opens the doors to the mall, and walks in. The camera slowly fades as the doors slowly close.) ---------------------------------------- ZD: God, some of the morons the AEWA lets into the federation nowadays. HT: I think he's going to be ready for Fear in their match, I don't see what your problem with him is. ZD: It's just principle. We have one more flash to get to before closing this thing up and going to our guest of the day, so here they are, the Four Turds of the A-CRAP-alypse. ------------ (The camera fades to black and creepy organ music begins to play as "4HotA" slowly appears inside a circle. The entire symbol is purple with a few black areas to blend it in with the background. Then "Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse" appears behind the symbol in dark red. Soon after, the music fades and the camera fades into a grassy area behind Nightshade and Pestilence's Satanic church. Nightshade wears his normal black robe with the hood down and his long black hair rests on his shoulders. Pestilence sits on the ground in his normal attire and his hair also rests on his shoulders. Blackout wears a black leather trenchcoat, black pants, black boots, and a white shirt. His long white hair hangs behind his head, and there are two small braids in the front corners that are held together by two small metal clips. Penance wears a black trenchcoat, black shirt, black boots, black pants, and a black glove on his right hand. His long black hair hangs to the sides of his head, and he grins at the camera. Nightshade then smiles at the camera and begins to speak.) Nightshade: Deathwatch, you have made the biggest mistake in your career, my friend. I suppose you are expecting me to say that your mistake was letting me wrestle you for your North American Title; no such luck. You see, Deathwatch, the mistake you made was not giving the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse the shot at the title. We are the four best people to wrestle you for it, so why don't you give all of us a shot, hmm? You seem to think you're so much better-- prove it. Blackout: He intends to, Nightshade, only he doesn't realize that his intentions are a false one. You see, Deathwatch thinks he can waltz around in his own glory, when in fact.... darkness is the thing that awaits his future. I intend to put the lights out on the situation, if you know what I mean. Pestilence: The 4HotA are just getting started here in the AEWA. You can expect a very big surprise from my brother and I. (smirks) What is this surprise? Well, if I told you, it wouldn't be a surprise, now would it? No, all of you will have to wait. Penance: Surprise, surprise is what's on my mind. Taking the things of life and balling them up into something so minuscule that you can hold it in the palm of your hand. I take my pride in taking things away from others and I've taken something away from ICU already. I took away talent.... I took away security.... and I took away my presence. Something that they shall never hold again. Pestilence: Oh, and Damian Steele.... regarding your comments about Penance kissing my ass, it just goes to show how clueless you really are. You are nothing but a fool, Steele. Penance was _NEVER_ a part of ICU, he has always been a Horseman. Back in WWEW when the puppet Drake was a Horseman, did you think he was really one of us? Of course not, he was a puppet, and we used him for all that he was worth, which wasn't much, and then we ditched him and made way for the real fourth member. Do not think that lightning cannot strike twice in the same place, because for Penance, it has, and he used ICU for all it was worth to him. Nightshade: Perhaps you are just jealous that Penance is in the company of intelligent and powerful people. (shrugs) Blackout: And who is to say what true power is? Well, we are. We are the definition of power in unison and the world will be figuring out that to take the horsemen off of their steeds they must first disable the steed that they ride. We ride a much higher power than gods and religion and what have you. We hold all of our power within ourselves unlike most of you. True power is something that doesn't come often, but when it does come, it enhances a person's ability ten fold. Penance: Welcome to your Apocalypse in its early stages. Watch as we dismantle your pride and take apart your character until all that is left is the fleshless bone that once was your pathetic little carcasses. We hold in our hands and our minds the power to destroy all that you hold dear and then some. It will be enjoyable to watch how you all cower in fear. Something we love to have.... is fun. Pestilence: Until next time, maggots. Avoid The Plague. (They all laugh and the screen fades to black.) ---------- ZD: Well, as always, it's time to get to the guest for this week. Man, this one's gonna be great. I'll be seein' ya, Hank. HT: Adios, Zack.
