Mega Combat Countdown (10/4, part one)

5 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power Bomb, and a look at Mister Crowley DRIVING the head of Volcano Jackson into the concrete with the Premature Burial! Then the camera switches to the Lovely Bertha, Matt Wilson, and "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt in a packed arena) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to this very special Countdown Show! We are only a few days away from Mega Combat, but we have plenty of matches scheduled for tonight. BD: In the main event, Heavy Metal will put his World Heavyweight title on the line against Sewerdog-- in the first ever "revenge match"! LB: So what's up first? MW: Randall Osbourne is about to defend his North American Title against the Grim Reaper! BD: It's gonna be really tough for Osbourne, because when you face one member of the Angels of Death, the others are never too far away. MW: With the way things have been recently, we know that all too well. LB: Just like we know that Osbourne is going to be pounded helplessly into the ground. I can SMELL a title change tonight, or is that just you, Matt? MW: I don't think so. Let's just go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA North American Title! Introducing first, the challenger, weighing in at 435 pounds and accompanied by his manager, "Cutie" Rick Verrano, he is a former AEWA Television Champion, The Grim Reeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeaper! ("The Funeral March" plays loudly over the PA, as the Reaper makes his way toward the ring to a HUGE round of boos. Rick Verrano is at his side.) BD: Here comes the challenger, and he looks ready. MW: How can you tell that? He's wearing a mask. BD: He just does, okay? MW: Whatever. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, he weighs in at 362 pounds and he is the AEWA North American Champion, Randall "The Rebel of Rock" Osboooooooooooooourne! (AC/DC's "It's a Long Way to the Top" plays loudly over the PA as Osbourne makes his entrance to a BIG crowd pop.) LB: It'll be interesting to see how these two match up. Osbourne's never faced anyone as big as the Reaper before. MW: No he hasn't, but size isn't everything. BD: Did you ever notice that only small guys ever say that? LB: Good point, Big Daddy. BD: Thank you. MW: All right, enough of that, we're just about ready to get started here. Osbourne is in the ring, and the Reaper charges in at him, BUT GETS CAUGHT WITH A SPINNING MULE KICK! LB: I guess the Reaper was just a little too impatient to get this one started. MW: You can say that again, and he got caught. The Reaper is stunned now, and Osbourne is pounding away furiously with lefts and rights! He nails the big man with a chop, and now he quickly grabs him, AND ROLLS HIM UP, INSIDE CRADLE! One..Kickout! BD: You're not gonna get the Reaper like that. MW: Not this early in the match. Both men quickly up, and Osbourne whips the Reaper to the buckle! He follows in behind him, AVALANCHE!! And now Reaper staggers out of the corner, and Osbourne LEVELS him with a shoulderblock! LB: This is what Osbourne's gotta do if he wants to have any hope of winning this match. Don't give the big guy any breaks. MW: Indeed. The Reaper makes it to his feet, and Osbourne now stuns him with a kneelift! Reaper staggers back into the corner, and Osbourne CRUSHES him with ANOTHER avalanche! BD: (yelling) Come on, Reaper! What's wrong? MW: He's not getting any time to fight back, that's what's wrong. Osbourne waits for the Reaper now, who staggers out of the corner, and gets NAILED with a fist to the midsection! The Reaper is dazed, and Osbourne LEVELS him with a superkick! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: The Reaper is taking a pounding, but he's hanging in there, and if Osbourne lets up for one second, the Reaper's gonna make him pay. MW: That's very possible. The Reaper's still down now, and Osbourne's going up top! He's on the top rope now, and he dives! FLYING KNEEDROP, RIGHT ACROSS THE THROAT OF THE REAPER! What a painful maneuver! BD: Painful for the ordinary man, maybe, but the Reaper is no ordinary man. MW: He's tough as nails, that's for sure. And now The Reaper is slowly getting to his feet, but Osbourne takes him back down with a legsweep! The Reaper gets up again, and gets nailed with a chop! LB: Come on Reaper, you GOTTA be able to do SOMETHING. MW: Not at the moment he can't. Osbourne lifts up the Reaper now, INVERTED ATOMIC DROP! The Reaper's still standing, but Osbourne LEVELS him with a clothesline! BD: Osbourne has just dominated in every way so far, I'm shocked! MW: Don't be. He's a great competitor, that's why he's the North American Champion. And now Osbourne brings the Reaper to his feet, and nails him with an earringer! Reaper is staggering now, and Osbourne catches him in ANOTHER inside cradle! One..Kickout! LB: The Reaper's still not ready to be fooled by that one. MW: No he's not, but it isn't because Osbourne's not trying. Osbourne waits for the Reaper to get up, and now he runs to the ropes! He comes back, and NAILS the Reaper with a clothesline! It didn't knock him over though! Osbourne takes advantage of the wobbly step now, and tries ANOTHER inside cradle! One..Two..Kickout! BD: A little closer that time, but no cigar. MW: The Reaper is in BIG trouble, though. LB: (notices someone coming down to ringside) But here comes Ricky Roberts to the rescue! MW: Uh-oh, you know what that means. BD: That Ricky Roberts wants a closer view of the match? MW: Not exactly what I was thinking, but let's turn our attention back to the match. Rick Verrano just jumped up on the apron! And the referee is going to send him down! Oh, this reeks of deception. LB: No, I think it's just you that reeks. MW: Now Verrano is arguing with the ref, and Osbourne is lifting the Reaper to his feet back in the ring! Osbourne runs to the ropes, AND GETS A CHAIR CRACKED OVER HIS BACK, COURTESY OF RICKY ROBERTS! BD: HA! That was great! Now Roberts is hightailing it outta here! MW: And Verrano CONVENIENTLY finishes his discussion with the ref now. The Reaper still on his feet, but he is just now realizing that Osbourne is down! He goes over to him now, and begins to work him over with several boots to the midsection! LB: See, I told you that the second Osbourne stopped, the Reaper would take control. MW: He didn't actually have a choice about stopping that time, Bertha. And now the Reaper picks Osbourne up off the mat, and nails him with an earringer! Osbourne is hunched over now, and the Reaper goes and picks him up, AND DRIVES HIM HEAD FIRST INTO THE CANVAS WITH A PILEDRIVER! BD: Now it's Osbourne who's in big trouble. MW: He sure is. Now what's the Reaper doing? HE'S SETTING HIM UP FOR ANOTHER PILEDRIVER! He lifts him up again, AND NAILS IT AGAIN! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: What the Hell? MW: Osbourne's a fighter, he'll kickout till his last breath. BD: Which is coming right up, 'cause the Reaper's setting him up for his finisher-- the Power Bomb! MW: He is! He's got Osbourne in position! He lifts him up, AND PLANTS HIM! The impact was UNBELIEVABLE! We've got a cover! One..Two..Three!!!!! (DING, DING) LB: Finally, a champion I can respect! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of eight minutes and fourteen seconds, here is you winner, and NEEEEEEEEEW AEWA North American Champion.... THE GRIM REAPER! MW: This is disgusting. Why did Ricky Roberts NEED to interfere in a perfectly good matchup? The Reaper can handle himself. BD: He was just making things a little easier for a friend. That's what friends do for each other. You wouldn't know, because you have no friends. MW: I do have friends, and I don't ask them to cheat for me. BD: Then you should try it, you don't know what you're missing. LB: I hate to interrupt your silly little argument, guys, but Raekwon is coming down to ringside. MW: Raekwon? What does he want? BD: I have no idea. MW: He's calling for a microphone. RAEKWON: (Standing face to face with the Reaper) Now I know you just won that belt and all, but listen to what I gotta say. I've been waiting around for a title shot too damn long! Yet, I have beaten every sorry-ass wrestler that they have put in front of me for MONTHS, and I WANT my shot at the gold. So right here, right now, I am DEMANDING a shot at what you got in your hand right there, Reaper, the North American Title. This weekend, at Mega Combat. What do you got ta say? (Raekwon holds the microphone up to the Reaper, but he says nothing.) RAEKWON: Oh, the quiet type, eh? That's okay, I ain't afraid to beat it out of you. (Raekwon suddenly nails the Reaper over the head with the microphone, and begins to go to work.) LB: This is nuts! I like it. MW: And now the Reaper and Raekwon are brawling around on the ground! They're really beating the heck out of each other! Raekwon appears to have the upper hand, but the Reaper is getting some shots in too! BD: And here comes the jobber brigade to break it up! MW: Yes, several preliminary wrestlers have come out here, and are attempting to separate these two superstars, but they're not having an easy time of it. LB: No they're not, both of these guys are acting as if they're out for blood! MW: And now they're finally being pulled apart, and dragged back to the locker room. Boy, what a volatile situation this has become! BD: And these guys barely knew each other moments ago, now they hate each other's guts! MW: Well, attacking someone right after a match can kinda cause that. We all know what Raekwon wanted, I guess the question now is, will he get it? LB: I hope so, I'd PAY to see that one. MW: It'd be an interesting matchup, that's for sure. BD: So what's up next, Wilson? MW: It's time for another episode of the "Terrordome", and I'm really looking forward to this one. Johnny Terror is going to interview Ben Slocum! BD: Are you kidding me? Ben Slocum isn't smart enough to hold an intelligent conversation with Johnny. LB: Let's just watch.... (Public Enemy's "Welcome to the Terrordome" plays loudly throughout the arena as the camera shifts to a familiar set near the back of the arena. Hanging over this set is a large half-dome. Underneath the dome, there is a very dark, dank room FILLED with various gruesome looking torture devices. AEWA manager Johnny Terror emerges from the locker room and the boos begin to rain down. Johnny just smiles at the reaction, and he just laughs at some fat guy standing up in the front row giving him the finger. Johnny looks into the camera and mouths the words "They love me". He gets to the set, and picks up a microphone.) Terror: Ah, my adoring public. It's times like this when I just wanna take each and every one of you, AND SMACK YOU UPSIDE THE HEAD! Show some respect for the greatest manager alive, will ya? I've actually gotta interview someone that you morons LIKE for a change this week, SO SHUT UP! Thank you, and welcome to yet another edition of the Terrordome, starring yours truly, the one and only, Johnny Terror!!! (LOUD BOOS) You people want me to hafta sick the Italian Loan Collection Agency on ya? (quiets down) I didn't think so. And now it's time to get to my guest. He SOMEHOW managed to win the TV Title twice, and even the World title on one occasion. But more recently, you'd recognize him as the man beaten to a bloody pulp by Twisted Steel and the rest of the Angels of Death in last week's barbed wire steel cage match. And now here he is, along with his sister, Sondra, here is the man who calls himself the "Modern Day Warrior", KERRY VON ER.... Oops, sorry, wrong Modern Day Warrior. What I meant to say was, BEN SLOCUM!!! (A bruised and battered, but very determined Ben Slocum makes his way toward Terror to a huge crowd pop. Sondra is following in his footsteps, as usual.) Ben: Well, I would like to say that it's a nice place you got here, but it really isn't. As for Twisted Steel and his recent actions, it's not over yet. Sondra: Not by a long shot! Ben may have to change his attitude a little to get the revenge we want, but the so called "Angels of Death" shouldn't feel safe in any way. Terror: I'm sure they're quaking in their boots right now. (Johnny laughs) But seriously, how can you hope to stand up to these guys? I mean there's three of them, and possibly others on the way, as opposed to just one of you. And two of them are just MONSTERS. I know that the AEWA's newest twit, Sir Gallade is trying to help you out, but he doesn't count-- he couldn't fight his way out of a paper bag. I guess what I'm really asking is, what the hell are you gonna do to fight these guys? Sondra: Ah, but there are more people out there interested in doing something about these Monsters, as you call them. Ben: Yes, there sure are, and these people have agreed to join my efforts to clean up the federation from the kind of ridiculous nonsense going on in way too many matches. We're gonna take care of business by going after cheap trash like the Angels of Death. You should also remember that there aren't any real monsters, only bad wrestlers who can't win without cheating. That's also why the Angels will lose eventually. Terror: (sarcastically) Sure it is. And what do you think of this Sir Gallade character that has come to rescue you from the big bad meanies? He's a newcomer, do you think that HE'S got what it takes to help you with the Angels? You already know what I think. Sondra: You sure do think a lot, don't you? Well, he may be a newcomer to the AEWA, but he has already proven himself as a courageous and honorable man, which is something you will never be. Ben: We also said that there are others interested in cleaning up this federation, and by that I mean that a new alliance has been formed to help enforce the rules against petty rulebreakers such as yourself, the Grim Reaper, Roberts or Twisted Steel or anyone else for that matter. Terror: (In his best Church Lady imitation.) Now isn't that special. (back to his normal voice) By the way, I'm not a "petty" rulebreaker, I'm a top-notch, first class rulebreaker, but that's not what we came here to discuss. Now last time on my show, Rick Verrano challenged you and Sir Gatorade to a six man tag match, and recently the Gryphon offered his help to you two, God knows why. But the point is, now that you have a third man who is willing to fight beside you, what do you have to say to the Angels' challenge? Ben: I am not a violent man by nature, but when I'm attacked I will fight back with all my might. To the Angels' challenge, all I have to say is that you've got yourselves a match. For your sake, I hope you come prepared, because we sure as hell will be. Sondra: And if they even think of trying another cheapshot like they have in the past, then we have a surprise or two for them. Terror: It's one of those "surprises" like the Gobbledy-Gooker, is it? (Sondra just stares at Johnny as if to say "don't ask stupid questions.") Terror: Didn't think so, just checking. It better be a cool surprise though or I'm gonna be pissed. So Ben, beyond this feud, what goals do you have left that you want to accomplish in the AEWA? Got your eye on some title, or somebody? Ben: I have held titles in the past, and I intend to do so again, but to achieve the task of capturing and holding onto a title takes a lot out of you. Most wrestlers are way too impatient, and instead of working their way up to the title shots the right way, they simply choose to take the easy way out. I will come looking for a title myself again pretty soon, but I won't say which one at the moment. As for getting title shots, I am patient, because I know they will come eventually. I won't make the same mistake so many others have done, and waste them when the opportunity arrives. Right now, however, my primary concern are the Angels of Death. Terror: How well put. (whispering) What a load of crap. (back to normal voice) And now my producers are telling me that we're out of time, so it's time for you get the hell out of here. Did I say that out loud? What I meant to say was, it's time for us to conclude our friendly little interview. Ben: I know what you meant, but don't worry about it. I've been here a little too long for my liking as well. I'll see ya Johnny, but I won't miss ya. (Ben and Sondra walk back to the locker room.) Terror: Well that's our show fans. I hope you liked it. Actually, I don't really care if you liked it or not, because it's MY show, and I'll do whatever I want on it, whether you like it or not. As you know I always like to thank my guests at the end of the show, but I think this week I'll make an exception. Don't miss next week's show, when my guest will be Sewerdog! Until then, I'm outta here. See ya, morons. (Johnny drops the microphone and walks off as the camera returns to our broadcasting team) MW: Ben Slocum seemed VERY angry there, so the Angels of Death should watch out. BD: He was absolutely TERRIFIED! Did you see the look in his eyes? I saw fear, folks. LB: So did I.
