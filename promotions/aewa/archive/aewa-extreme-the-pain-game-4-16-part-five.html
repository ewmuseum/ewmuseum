AEWA Extreme: The Pain Game (4/16, part five)

18 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * CONTINUED FROM PART FOUR * * * * MW: Can't you tell, just by looking at the ring? BD: It looks like they're building a scaffold, So? MW: SO?? It's not like there's multiple SCAFFOLD matches on this card, buddy! LB: What're you trying to say? BD: I didn't even know there WAS a scaffold match on this card. MW: Well what ROCK have you been living under? The ONLY scaffold match on the card is Gryphon versus Exodus, and the match was announced over a MONTH ago. LB: I really don't keep up with what matches are scheduled. BD: Me neither, that's why you're here. It's your job. MW: Yeah, and it's both of YOUR jobs to be color commentators. The AEWA PAYS you to talk about the matches. You'd think you'd at least have some idea what they are. LB: Nope. BD: Not a clue. MW: Why do I bother? It looks like they're just about ready now, so without further ado, let's go to the ring! RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is a special scaffold match! The two competitors will fight it out on the scaffold that is now assembled twenty feet above this ring. The first man to fall off the scaffold and down to the floor loses! And now the competitors, Introducing first, representing International Contracts Unlimited, he is from Los Angeles, California, and weighs in at 346 pounds, Here is Exxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxoooooooodddduuuuuussssss! (Anthrax's "1,000 Points of Hate" plays loudly over the PA as the sadistic man known as Exodus makes his way to the ring to a HUGE round of boos. He seems to enjoy the reaction, and just continues on his path towards the ring.) BD: Exodus sure looks focused tonight. I don't think there's any way the Gryphon can take him tonight. MW: Looking focused and BEING focused are two ENTIRELY different things, Big Daddy. And besides, the more focused wrestler doesn't always win, especially in a match as wild as this one. LB: Maybe Big Daddy didn't make it clear enough for you. I'll clarify. Exodus won't lose, bottom line, because Gryphon sucks. Understand now? MW: I guess you guys really are hopeless. BD: Yeah, great, ain't it? RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Parts Unknown, he weighs in at 310 pounds, and is accompanied to the ring by his manager, Laura, here is The Grrrrrrrryyyyyyyyphhooooooooooooooooooonn! (Metallica's "The Unforgiven" takes over the sound system as the crowd ERUPTS with approval. The Gryphon and Laura appear from behind the curtain, and slowly begin to make their way toward the ring. They slap hands with the fans, but it's obvious that Gryphon's mind is on the task at hand.) LB: What do the fans see in this guy? MW: Let's see.... He's brave, courageous, a fine role model, an excellent competitor, he plays by the rules, he never gives up, he never backs down-- should I go on? BD: Not unless you want me to throw up right here in the broadcast booth. MW: I think I'll pass. LB: Thank God. MW: Gryphon is scaling the ladder to get up to the scaffold now, and Exodus awaits him at the top. BD: This isn't even gonna be funny. MW: We'll see. The Gryphon has reached the top now, and it looks like we're ready to begin. The ref calls for the bell (DING, DING) and we're underway! The two men lock up at the center of the scaffold, and they're now jockeying for position. Gryphon gets the upper hand, and SLAMS Exodus to the mat. LB: Lucky first move. MW: Gryphon now DROPS an elbow on his opponent, and is quick to follow that up with a headlock on the canvas. BD: Boy I love rest holds. They're almost as fun as watching paint dry. MW: Very funny. Exodus is working for an escape now, and he manages to get to his feet, with Gryphon still holding on to that headlock. From that position, Exodus grabs Gryphon from around the waist, and lifts, BELLY-TO-BACK SUPLEX! LB: Now Exodus is gonna go to town! MW: He's gonna try. Exodus quickly regains his footing, and lifts the Gryphon to his feet! He WHIPS Gryphon towards the edge! But Gryphon manages to put on the breaks just in time! He turns back towards his opponent, AND GETS LEVELED WITH A CLOTHESLINE FROM EXODUS! BD: Now that's smart thinking. He didn't simply try to throw Gryphon off, and assume that it would work. He had a backup plan, and it worked to perfection. MW: Can't argue with that. Exodus tries to roll Gryphon off the edge, but he won't budge, so Exodus just applies an armbar on the mat! Why's he doing that? There's no submissions here. LB: No, but that doesn't mean you can't torture your opponents with them if you want, does it? MW: I suppose not.... LB: Then shut up, and commentate on the match. MW: Like YOU'RE doing anything constructive. Anyway, Exodus is really yanking on that armbar, and Gryphon is SCREAMING in pain. BD: Of course he is-- that hold, when applied right, can break your arm off. MW: It certainly could. And now Exodus simply lets go of the hold, obviously happy with the pain he's caused. He lets the Gryphon get to his feet, and sets him up for a vertical suplex! LB: Look where he's standing, he's gonna suplex the poor guy right off the scaffold! I love it! MW: Exodus now lifts on the suplex, trying to finish this match, BUT GRYPHON MANAGES TO BLOCK IT! Exodus lifts once more, AND IS BLOCKED AGAIN! He tries a third time, AND GRYPHON COUNTERS WITH A VERTICAL SUPLEX OF HIS OWN! BD: Stop yelling, damnit! I'm sitting right here. MW: I'm sorry, but I get a little excited sometimes. LB: That's the understatement of the year. MW: Gryphon slowly gets back to his feet now, as does Exodus. They stagger towards each other, and Gryphon NAILS his opponent with a HARD right hand. Exodus counters with a left, BLOCKED, and Gryphon lands another right! BD: This is degenerating into a slugfest. MW: And it looks like Gryphon's winning in that department. Exodus is on rubber legs, and Gryphon FLOORS him with a standing dropkick! LB: Oh man, and I thought we were going to get through an entire ICU match without seeing HIS ugly face. MW: WHO'S ugly face? (Matt Wilson turns around, to find that Zack Derringer is standing behind them at the broadcast booth. Zack sits down and takes a headset.) ZD: You didn't think I was gonna let you guys do this match without me, did ya? MW: We were HOPING to. ZD: Well, I'm back, so just shut the hell up. Any time ICU's involved, you can bet I'm gonna be here. LB: How lucky for us. MW: Let's just get back to the match. Gryphon sure seems to be taking it to your boy Exodus right about now, Derringer. ZD: That's only temporary, trust me. BD: I wouldn't trust you as far as I could throw you. MW: That goes for me too. And now Gryphon picks up Exodus, and NAILS him with a reverse neckbreaker! He picks him up again, and lifts Exodus into a bodyslam position. He tries to toss him off the scaffold, but Exodus won't let go of Gryphon's arms! He's grabbed one of them with his arms and the other with his legs, and is clinging to Gryphon for dear life! ZD: What a BRILLIANT move on the part of Exodus. He knew EXACTLY what was going on, and refused to be thrown off. That's why he's in ICU! MW: So Gryphon just turns back towards the center of the scaffold and falls forward, effectively POWERSLAMMING Exodus! LB: Yeah, Derringer, that was REAL brilliant, huh? ZD: Just shut up. MW: Woah, "the Great" Zack Derringer can dish it out but he can't take it? ZD: For the sake of being polite, I'll take it from a woman, but if you even TRY starting something with me, Wilson, I'll kick your ass so fast, you won't even know what hit ya. MW: Whatever. Gryphon picks up Exodus now, and he NAILS him with DDT! And now he's calling for his finisher, THE POWER BOMB! LB: If he hits this, he'll be able to roll Exodus out of the ring EASY. ZD: Excuse me for a minute.... MW: Where are you going? BD: He's climbing up the ladder! HE'S gonna get on the scaffold? Is he NUTS? MW: Apparently. Gryphon now picks Exodus up, and he's got him in position! Derringer is up on the scaffold now, and Gryphon starts to lift, BUT HE SEES DERRINGER, and stops! He throws Exodus down, and he's going after Zack! LB: YEAH! Squash the punk! You know, maybe I COULD actually get to like the Gryphon! MW: Derringer tries to back off, but he's got nowhere to go. He makes a mad dash for the ladder, BUT GRYPHON CATCHES HIM! He grabs Derringer by the neck, and he's got a delighted grin on his face! He's gonna chokeslam him off the scaffold! BD: I can't wait to see this! MW: Gryphon lifts Derringer into the air, AND GETS SHOVED FROM BEHIND BY EXODUS, WHO HAS REACHED HIS FEET! Gryphon falls off the platform, BUT DERRINGER GOES WITH HIM! Derringer lands on the concrete, AND GRYPHON LANDS RIGHT ON TOP OF HIM! The ref is calling for the bell! (DING, DING) LB: That was beautiful! Not only did Derringer fall off there, but Gryphon landed right on top of him, SQUASHING him into the concrete! I think justice has been served. Gryphon didn't win, and that annoying little Derringer got crushed. BD: Couldn't have said it better myself. MW: Let's get the official word. RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of nine minutes and forty-three seconds, here is your winner.... EXODUS!!!! LB: And just look at Derringer, lying there all helpless. That's what he gets for being such a jerk. BD: Yeah. MW: Look who's talking. LB: We may be jerks, but at least we're likable jerks. MW: Whatever that means. Anyway, it looks like the Gryphon is almost completely unharmed. He's getting up and heading back to the locker room. BD: Of course he's unharmed, Derringer broke his fall. Wasn't that nice of him? MW: Oh yeah. And Exodus also showed how nice he is, by pushing his associated off the scaffold with Gryphon to get the win. LB: Hey, you gotta do what you gotta do. MW: I suppose so, and now Exodus is carrying Derringer back to the locker room, and we should be getting ready for our next match as they disassemble the scaffold. BD: Well, after that last match, I think-- ("Sympathy For the Devil" suddenly begins to play over the public address system, interrupting the commentators. Recognizing the music, a good majority of the audience begins to boo.) MW: Folks, I have absolutely no idea what's going on right now, but that's Cardinal Chaos' theme music. LB: We've had plenty of interruptions tonight, so I wouldn't be surprised if we're about to see another.... but the Cardinal is ALWAYS welcome out here. BD: This should be interesting.... (Without any warning whatsoever, Cardinal Chaos storms out from behind the curtain and begins making his way toward the ring. He's not alone, either. The entire Church is following behind him, as Sir Gallade, the Visigothic Horde, Bishop Bedlam, Mister Crowley, the Holy Ghost, and Big Ron Raider follow their leader. The crowd instantly ERUPTS with boos for these men, but Chaos simply ignores it, and continues his march. He finally gets to ringside, and violently pushes the ring announcer down to the concrete. Chaos quickly grabs the microphone from the table, and climbs into the ring with a dirty look on his face. The other Church members also enter, and the booing from the crowd only seems to get louder. Chaos leans against the turnbuckle, obviously still tired for his match earlier in the evening. He begins to speak.) CHAOS: I have an important announcement to make, sinners, so I wish you would all just quiet down. What you are about to hear will change the AEWA forever. Please, for once in your evil lives, do not judge me or my followers.... just listen. As you all know, I have been in this promotion for over a year. It wasn't very long ago that I was inducted into the AEWA Hall Of Fame. I might not have shown my feelings at the time, but the angels were dancing in Heaven when I received that honor. It almost made me happy. Unfortunately, the good times are over for me. I have watched in dismay as the AEWA, and the entire world, became a haven for sinners. I've watched as Wild Thing bastardized the innocent youth on national television, and as Joey Valachi continued to make a mockery of the very organization that signs his paychecks. I even watched as thirty-nine members of the Heaven's Gate cult, my old friends, were found dead. I almost wish I had joined them on their trip to Heaven. The world is a bitter, cold place, and there are very few places of holiness anymore. It saddens me. Now, I have finally made my decision. I am taking my original members of the Church, Bishop Bedlam and the Holy Ghost, and I am going on a mission throughout the World. I will not stop until EVERYONE has converted to the Church. Unfortunately, this means.... I am leaving the AEWA. I have thought long and hard about this decision, but it needed to be done. I cannot stand by and watch as the world slowly falls into the pits of hell. I must do something about it. God has cast a shadow down upon the AEWA, and I'm not sure if it can ever be removed. Brother Bedlam, would you like to say a few words? (Chaos hands the microphone to Bishop Bedlam, who begins to speak in his usual low voice.) BEDLAM: This has been my home for so long, sinners, I am almost reluctant to leave. It was right here, in the AEWA, that I made the long journey from boy to man. The AEWA will always hold a special place in my.... cold heart. (Bedlam wipes his eyes, and although we can't be sure, it looks like he is wiping away a tear. The Holy Ghost gently takes the microphone from him, and begins to speak.) GHOST: I never thought I would leave this place, the AEWA. When I first began in this organization, I set off to teach fools like Joey Valachi a lesson. I did. Although I am not quite as emotional as Brother Bedlam, the AEWA does have special meaning for me as well. Because of the Church, I am now a two-time former Light Heavyweight champion. To win the belt the first time, I easily disposed of that underhanded sinner, Joey Valachi. The second time, I put Penance to rest in one of the best matches of my life. The point is this-- through good times and bad, the Church has always prevailed against evil. I will miss you, AEWA, and I hope to return someday in the distant future. I am not sure when that will be, if ever, since that is for the good Cardinal to decide. Goodbye.... (He hands the microphone to Mister Crowley, who stares at the Cardinal with his cold, black eyes. He is silent for several seconds, then finally begins to speak.) CROWLEY: I will miss you, Chaos. (And with those five words, he drops the microphone. The Cardinal appears to be somewhat touched by the comments, and picks it up.) CHAOS: Brother Crowley, throughout the past year, you have been both my enemy and my friend. We have fought against each other and fought together, but one thing never changed-- we were always here. Now, as I have been called upon by my God to journey to faraway lands, we must finally part ways. I have always thought of you as my brother, Crowley, and I will never forget what you have done. I feel almost sentimental as I say this, but it must be heard-- thank you for being my friend. (He suddenly steps forward and hugs Crowley, which is a sight most people never expected to see. A good portion of the crowd actually begins to cheer. Finally, Bishop Bedlam takes the microphone from Chaos, and turns to Big Ron Raider.) BEDLAM: I have something to say to you, Brother Raider. Just like Chaos and Crowley, you and I have had our difficulties in the past. In fact, we almost have come close to KILLING each other on several occasions. Although we have always been teammates in the Church, there has always seemed to be a barrier between us. I feel that I still don't really know who you are. I just wanted to say.... I'm sorry. When I someday return to this organization, I hope to patch up my differences with you. Our brawls could have easily been prevented, and I admit, it was all my fault. Could you possibly forgive me before I go? (Raider appears somewhat confused by the sudden apology, but cautiously takes the microphone from him.) RAIDER: Well.... Apology accepted. (Raider shakes hands with Bedlam, and some of the audience begins to cheer again. Chaos takes the microphone back.) CHAOS: I am glad that you two have finally settled your many differences. Now, it would not be right for me to leave the AEWA without putting someone else in control. I have thought long and hard about who would make a proper leader in my absence, and I have come to a decision. I am leaving Sir Gallade as the new leader of the Church, and he has agreed to take over my duties in this federation. I trust this man with my Church, and I realize that he will do an adequate job for me. It will not be an easy task, however, since the AEWA has many sinners. Gallade, give them your first speech as the new leader of my Church.... (Gallade steps forward to the microphone, and the crowd instantly explodes with boos. He simply grins at the fans, absorbing their hatred. Finally, after a few seconds of working the crowd, Gallade begins to yell quite loudly.) GALLADE: Quiet down, you sinners, before I go outside and kick all your bloody ASSES! Didn't you hear what the Cardinal said? I am the new leader of the greatest stable in the history of professional wrestling-- so you must give me some RESPECT! I will fight with honor for the Church, that's for sure. Nothing else in this sinful world means ANYTHING to me.... (points to his belt) Not this title, not the fans; there's only one thing I care about, and that's my beloved Church. Thank you for bestowing this great honor upon me, Chaos. I will not let you down. (The Cardinal leans forward and begins to speak into the microphone, as Gallade holds it.) CHAOS: You deserve it, young warrior. But you must never forget about your newfound responsibilities. (points to the Visigothic Horde) These two sinners are also under your control now, so you must continue to teach them. They are not yet ready to accept our God, but with the proper guidance, you can convert them to the Light. GALLADE: They're a tad bit wild, but I should be able to do it. That's what the Church is all about-- saving souls is our business, and business is BOOMING. CHAOS: Now, it is time for me to go. It has been a memorable ride in the AEWA, and after I accomplish my mission, I'll be back. I'm not one for long farewells, so just let me leave you all with this.... You must always fear the Light. (With that final thought, Cardinal Chaos leads Bishop Bedlam and the Holy Ghost out of the ring. They walk slowly back to the locker room, and just before they walk through the curtain, Chaos turns back to the audience. He stands there in silence, apparently in deep thought. Finally, he waves goodbye to the crowd and exits into the darkness.) GALLADE: Well, I can't believe he's really gone now. That was the only man I've ever respected in my life, and I'll never forget his teachings. He truly changed my life. I shall count the days until he returns, for we will be lost without him. Now here we are in the ring, absolutely alone. As the new leader of the Church, however, I think it's only appropriate that I make an announcement..... I am going to rule this place with an IRON FIST! If anybody doesn't like the decisions that I make, they can get down on their knees and kiss my bloody ass, because-- (Ron Raider suddenly grabs Gallade by the collar of his tights, and raises his fist in anger. He takes the microphone from him.) Raider: Look, I didn't want to cause any trouble in front of the Cardinal, but I've got somethin' to get off my chest. (yelling) Why the hell are YOU the new leader? I've been in the Church for almost a year now, and damn it, _I_ deserve to be the leader right now! You can make whatever orders you wanna make, but that doesn't mean I'm gonna do what you say. I respected Cardinal Chaos, but Gallade, I DON'T respect you! (The crowd begins to cheer in approval. Gallade breaks away from Raider's grip, taking the microphone with him.) GALLADE: Ya know, Ron, my hearing must be failing me, because I thought I just heard you disobey me. (chuckles) Do you know who I am, buddy? I'm your friggin' BOSS! You don't want to follow my orders? Well, that's actually rather funny, because you don't have a CHOICE! The Cardinal put ME in charge a few minutes ago, and you CAN'T argue with that. There's a very good reason why he didn't put _YOU_ in charge.... Because you SUCK! When was the last time you did ANYTHING around here, Ron? I can't even remember anymore. You're the dead weight of the Church, and because of what you just said, I might even consider firing you. You've got a serious attitude problem, and-- (Mister Crowley suddenly pushes Sir Gallade into the corner, causing him to drop the microphone. The crowd begins to cheer once again. Crowley picks up the mic and begins to speak.) CROWLEY: The Cardinal would not want us to fight amongst ourselves, Brother Gallade. Silence. (Gallade appears to be rather shaken, and he backs off. It's obvious that he doesn't want to mess with Crowley, who looks VERY angry at this point. They all stand there for awhile, trying to decide what to do next. (Crowley finally leaves the ring with an angry scowl on his face, as Raider, Gallade, and the Horde follow behind him. They all walk straight to the locker room, together as a team, but the dissension among them is obvious. The camera switches back to our broadcast team.) Mr. Goody Two Shoes? Why would he have a weapon? MW: I don't know, but the Assassins want him checked, I guess so that we can know that everyone is clean.
