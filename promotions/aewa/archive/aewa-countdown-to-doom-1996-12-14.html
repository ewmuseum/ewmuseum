Countdown To Doom (12/14)

19 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power Bomb, and a look at Mister Crowley DRIVING the head of some guy into the concrete with the Premature Burial! Then the camera switches to Ken Wilson and Chuck Carlson in a PACKED arena) CC: Hello everyone, and welcome to the AEWA Countdown To Doom show! We are just HOURS away from December Doom, and the clock is ticking! There's still time for you to call your local cable company, and order the biggest pay-per-view extravaganza of the year! KW: We're only going to be broadcasting this Countdown show for 30 minutes, and after that, you'll have to pay. Don't waste time, ORDER NOW! CC: Sounds like a good idea to me. KW: So what's up first, Chuckster? CC: It's time for a special edition of the Terrordome, and Johnny Terror is going to be interviewing Miss DeMeanor and the Twilight Strangler. This should be interesting. KW: Yeah, and later tonight, the Strangler will be squaring off against Brian Rice. I'm interested to see what he has to say. CC: Instead of talking about it, let's just watch.... (Public Enemy's "Welcome to the Terrordome" plays loudly throughout the arena as the camera shifts to a familiar set near the back of the arena. Hanging over this set is a large half-dome. Underneath the dome, there is a very dark, dank room FILLED with various gruesome looking torture devices. AEWA manager Johnny Terror emerges from behind the curtain, to his usual positive reception from the crowd. (YEAH, RIGHT!) Johnny accepts them gracefully as always-- Translation: he flips them the bird. Johnny gets to the set, and picks up a microphone.) Terror: Hello again everyone! How nice it is to see you all again! Now shut the <BLEEP> up so I can run this damn show. You are watching the highest rated interview show in the history of the AEWA.... Okay, so it's the ONLY interview show in the history of the AEWA, but that's besides the point. You're watching the wonderful, the stupendous, the MAGNIFICENT... Terrordome, starring yours truly, Johnny Terror! (LOUD BOOS) You people are TOO kind. Anyway, it's time to get to my guest. You probably remember him from that time when he beat the crap out of Primal Fear with a baseball bat and put him up in the hospital. (Crowd BOOS). See, I knew you did. Great memories, huh? In the ring, he is an absolute madman, and has been crippling his opposition left and right. In fact, you could say that he has had a "strangle hold" on the competition. So here he is folks, along with his manager, the BEAUTIFUL Miss DeMeanor.... THE TWILIGHT STRANGLER!! (The Eagles' "Tequila Sunrise" starts blaring over the PA and the crowd begins to boo loudly. Miss DeMeanor leads Strangler in. She is wearing a red leather dress. Twilight Strangler is carrying a baseball bat and has blood stained clothes on. On his shirt it says, "If OJ can get away with it, then so can I") Terror: Hello Miss D, you are looking INCREDIBLE, as always. (Johnny reaches out and grabs her by the hand and kisses it.) But unfortunately, we're not here to talk about how stunning you look. We're here to talk about this guy, right here, The Strangler. Where did you manage to find such a monster? He's ten times better than Primal Fear ever was! Miss DeMeanor Well Johnny, when I need a man, I can always find one. You see, I was watching Hard Copy and they were talking about this man in L.A. who would just walk through the town and hit people and beat them up, just for looking at him the wrong way. They also said how, one time, a local gang tried to car-jack him, but he got out and beat the living crap out of THEM. So I thought, Hell, if he can do that to people out there, I am sure he can do that to people here in the AEWA. And so far, he has done just that; the AEWA won't even let him wrestle! Terror: That's terrible. The AEWA Board wouldn't know talent if it jumped up and bit them in the ass! Why won't they let ya wrestle, big guy? Could it be they're afraid of ya? Strangler: Ever since I put Primal Fear in the hospital, everyone has been worried about what else I could do. Primal Fear was supposedly the most dangerous man in the AEWA and then all of the sudden he was on the verge of death, so, of course, they are worried about what I might do to all the other "AEWA Superstars". Terror: I get the idea, I used to have the same trouble with Shane, until he won the World Title. Now they HAVE to give him matches. And speaking of the World Title, you were involved in that 26 man battle royal that my man, Shane Matthews, won the World Title in recently. Would either of you care to comment on your performance there? Strangler: I did pretty damn shitty. Miss DeMeanor: Hon, it's all a part of the plan. Johnny, you and I both know that no title is safe with this man around. Right now, I am getting him ready for matches against some kid named Brian Rice and some other people and when I feel he is ready, then I will try and sign a title match for him. Terror: And what of your arch-rival? I am, of course, talking about the one and only Primal Fear? Are you done with him yet, or is there still more pain in store for him? You know he's never been quite the same since he left you, Miss D. Now he's wasting himself in another worthless feud with the AEWA's resident punching bag, The Crazy Canuck. Do you have anything to say about him? Miss DeMeanor: You know it's funny, I left Primal Fear 'cause he could never get the job done and it appears he still has the same problem. I mean, he can't even get rid of that chump Crazy Canuck. We aren't exactly done with Primal Fear, but at this time, he is not our main concern. Terror: Then what exactly IS your main concern at this point in time? Miss DeMeanor: The main concern is to get the Strangler here some more training, so he gets a little more technical skills and then maybe to go on and get a title. Terror: If this man had ANY technical skill then he would be a SURE champion! He's already probably the best brawler in the entire fed. Adding a technical repertoire would REALLY take some people by surprise. I could get Shane to help train him if you wanted, ya know. (Johnny winks at Miss D) Sound like a good idea? Miss DeMeanor: What exactly would you want in return? Terror: What would make you think that there's strings attached? I'm offended that you would think that of me, darlin'. I'm offering to do this out of the goodness of my heart! We're friends, right? That's what friends do for each other. Of course, if you wanted to be more than friends.... Miss DeMeanor: Well, we can work out the "KINKS" in the back, big boy.... Terror: Sounds GREAT to me! Let's get started!! Do either of you have anything else to say before we get back there and get started with those "kinks"? (Johnny winks again) Miss DeMeanor: One more thing. Folks, with the AEWA World Heavyweight Champion training my man, you'd better watch out! Terror: That's for DAMN sure. Let's get back there and get busy! Wait a minute, that didn't come out right. What I meant to say was.... Lets get busy.... uh.... with the training and stuff. Yeah, that's the ticket! Anyway, I'd like to thank the Twilight Strangler and the BEAUTIFUL Miss DeMeanor for appearing here today, and don't miss our next show when this LOVELY lady will be here once again, this time with her tag team, Graphic Violence. Later, morons, I'm outta here! (Johnny and Miss DeMeanor walk arm-in-arm back to the dressing room, and the Twilight Strangler follows behind them. The camera returns to our broadcast team.) KW: So Shane Matthews is going to train the Twilight Strangler! What a GREAT turn of events! I NEVER could have predicted that! CC: That spells trouble for a lot of our AEWA superstars, folks. With Johnny Terror working together with Miss DeMeanor, ANYTHING could happen. Personally, I don't like it. KW: I didn't ask. CC: Good point. Anyway, it's time to move on. I'd like to talk a little bit about the upcoming TV title match between Randall Osbourne and Sir Gallade. These two guys are TEAMMATES in the Knights Of Deliverance, and now they have to wrestle. It should be an extremely awkward match for both of them. KW: I want to see those two losers rip each other APART! It's going to be absolutely BEAUTIFUL. CC: Well, we have a pre-taped interview with Sir Gallade now. He was recently back in England, preparing for this big match. KW: Roll it.... -------------- (The scene opens with Sir Gallade standing on a hillside, overlooking his home, Hackney, England. He has a very concerned and confused look on his face) Gallade: It is shocking how one friend can be pitted against another, isn't it? For the TV Title no less. You know, when I get into a ring, it doesn't matter who it is I am in there with, my intent is to win. This weekend, it will be Randall Osbourne and myself for the TV Championship. Ever since I came up short at the Battle Royal for the World title, I have been waiting for a chance like this, and now it has come. You see, my goal here in the AEWA was and always will be to win the World title, and winning the TV Title will put me one step closer to accomplishing my goal. We are both members of the Knights of Deliverance (he says it as though he is not happy) but this is a chance that I will not pass up. Randall, come prepared, because I will, and I plan on riding home with the TV title around my waist, and then on to the World Title! (fade to black) --------------- CC: Outside of the ring, Osbourne and Gallade are good friends. But what's going to happen when they finally have to WRESTLE each other? That should be a CLASSIC match. KW: Personally, I think it'll be rather boring. In fact, I'll probably fall asleep during that match. CC: Up next, we have some comments from "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum. He's going to be in the BIG main event, squaring off against Shane Matthews for the World Heavyweight title. KW: Let's watch.... -------------- (We join the "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum and his sister Sondra at their ranch outside Austin, Texas) Sondra: I think we're about to prove why you shouldn't mess with the Knights Of Deliverance. Shane Matthews may think he's gotten away with it, but he's about to find out that he's all wrong. Ben: That's right! It doesn't matter how or why you happened to get the AEWA World Title into your possession, because once we are in the ring, it's one on one-- and I can assure you, that's just the way I like it. When you've been to the top, there is nothing sweeter, and that's why I'm headed there again. Sondra: There are still all kinds of outside interference and attacks to consider, but it won't matter because my brother has every intention of taking the title away from you, Matthews. So enjoy your reign while it lasts, because it's coming to an end soon. Ben: It's been a while since I held gold here in the AEWA, but I've always known that there would come other opportunities in the future. I would advise you all not to count out the Modern Day Warrior just yet. As for Shane Matthews, it's about time someone expose you as the coward you really are. Everyone knows you're undeserving of the title, and that you stole it, but now you'll learn the meaning of "easy come, easy go". Sondra: You bet we haven't forgotten about our other commitments either, and we'll be coming after some people no matter what happens in the World Title match. There is also some unfinished business with certain members of the Angels of Death to consider. Ben: There are some people I would like to meet in the ring in the near future, but we'll deal with them when the time comes though. Now, a few words concerning my fellow Knights battling it out for the TV Title.... Randall Osbourne wasn't too happy about the way he obtained his title, but since then he's proven himself to be a worthy champion. I'm certain that Gallade would also make a fine champ, so I can only wish both of you luck, and may the best man win. Now count the minutes, Matthews, cause at AEWA DECEMBER DOOM, later today, I'm coming to take the World Title away from you. (fade to black) --------------- CC: In my opinion, Ben Slocum is the next World champion. After tonight, that championship belt will finally be back around his waist. KW: You've got to be KIDDING me, Chuck. Shane Matthews has pinned him before, and he'll do it again. Mark my words. CC: We'll see about that. Coming up next, we have a special interview with "Slim" Jim Coolridge. He's been challenging Matthews lately, so he should be VERY interested in what happens at December Doom. KW: Coolridge is one of the most talented wrestlers in this federation, but he has a REALLY big mouth. CC: Let's see.... ------------- (The camera opens on "Slim" Jim Coolridge, standing in the locker room. His manager, Flashy Freddy, is seated behind him.) Flashy Freddy: Matthews, put your money where your mouth is. Myself and my man have decided to have a new stipulation in the contract-- LOSER LEAVES TOWN! If you can beat my man, we'll leave the AEWA. Jim Coolridge: I've been waiting for months to wrestle you, Matthews. You kept ignoring and ignoring me. Well, the man has stepped up. I've been feared by everybody in this league since I entered. Everybody in every league wants the "Slim" Jim. Well, maybe my mission since I first entered the AEWA has been to get my hands on you, Shane Matthews. Your championship reign is just a flash in the pan. It'll probably be over after your match with Ben Slocum at December Doom. The clock has struck midnight, Cinderella.... (fade to black) -------------- KW: Is Jim Coolridge a complete IDIOT or something? What's his freaking problem? He could NEVER beat Shane Matthews, so why does he keep challenging him? CC: You ask too many questions, Ken. KW: Let's just move on.... CC: Good idea. Unfortunately, our next interview is with Cardinal Chaos and one of his Church members. I REALLY don't like that guy. KW: Let's just watch. -------------- (The camera opens on a familiar site. Cardinal Chaos is standing in front of the altar. Kneeling in front of him is a figure dressed in a black robe.) Cardinal Chaos: Oh great God of power and wisdom, look down on your humble servant and grant unto him the power to go forth and spread your word to the masses. He has served me well over the years, but the time has come for him to move on. I ask you now, my son, to profess your unwavering acceptance of the supreme one. (The figure removes his hood to reveal none other than Curate Kill.) Curate Kill: Father, I have given my life to you. You are the lifeblood which gives me guidance. I swear to you now that I am your most humblest of servants from this day forward until the end of time. Cardinal Chaos: It is understood, my son. You have proven your devotion on more than one occasion. For this you shall be rewarded in the eyes of the Lord. Are you prepared to accept this new responsibility, my child? Curate Kill: I am, my master. Cardinal Chaos: Then hear me well. From this day forward, you shall no longer be addressed as Curate Kill. As of this day, you shall be known as Bishop Bedlam. You shall begin a new reign of terror in the AEWA. There are many non-believers there, none more so than these Angels of Death. As your first duty, I ask of you this-- make believers of them. If they do not see the light, then destroy them. Now go, my son. Go unto the flock and bring the light of salvation to the non-believers. (camera fades to black) ---------- CC: So Curate Kill wants to be called Bishop Bedlam from now on? What's the point of that? KW: Don't you UNDERSTAND, Chuck? He's finally been promoted by Cardinal Chaos, and that's a BIG honor. I'm really proud of him. CC: But it really doesn't matter WHAT you call him. Curate Kill wasn't a very good wrestler, and as Bishop Bedlam, he won't be ANY better. KW: I disagree. CC: You always do. Anyway, up next, we have a special interview with Super Blonde. We haven't seen the Blonde much lately, so this should be VERY interesting. KW: Roll the footage.... ------------- (The scene opens to a living room; there's a couch in the middle facing a TV with the Philadelphia Eagles / Indianapolis Colts game on. Blonde hair flows over the back of the couch.) Voice: Guess who's back.... (The mass of blonde hair rustles a bit and turns to show the face of Super Blonde.) Blonde: I know I've been missing for some time, but I was taking care of some domestic business. Frankly, the idea of gang warfare in the AEWA is quite disturbing, and after losing my title to Deranged, I decided to take a bit of a siesta. So, after taking a minor excursion to Martinique, I went back to Vancouver and.... (raises up his left hand, with a ring on) ....got married. (calling into the back) Hon! Come here for a sec! Guess who else is back? (smiles) (From the back doorway comes out Jenny Babbs, who slumps over the head of the couch also.) Jenny: Mr. Freeman, Mr. Gehringer-- is there still a place there for little ol' me? Just got back in town after covering the Olympics! Blonde: As you can see, I'm back for good and in the mood for curbing some young 'uns. Seems that we've got an influx of newbies kicking around, demanding title shots. Guess I'll have to burst a few of their bubbles before I'm championship material again. Speaking of titles, how'd Mr. Matthews get himself that title?!? I know I was in that battle royale, but only for contractual obligations. Shane, I'm after your title now. (smiles again) Remember last time? Also, what's this about a women's division? (camera nods) Hey Kis!! Got some good news! (Kismet comes in from the back doorway, towel over her head.) Kismet: What? I'm doing my hair! Blonde: Looks like they're instituting a womens division! Kismet: You're kidding? That's great. I should go get shaped up.... Take my suit to the tailors.... Call back my trainer.... (walks out) Blonde: Last thing-- these groups. What a waste of human wreckage. Be it the Church, ICU, Angels of Death, Knights; I don't care. You little boys go play somewhere, go beat up someone for their milk money. I'm back, and possibly even more beautiful.... (turns away from the camera for a second and turns around again) Speaking of the Church-- Cardinal Chaos, for all the time we've been here, we've never faced each other. I've always respected your talents, and would love to get you in the ring. Talk about a possible "Match of the Year". I gotta shut up now; Philly's got the ball again! See ya! (Jenny and him turn back to the TV as the camera fades.) ----------- CC: That's GREAT! Super Blonde and Jenny Babbs have finally gotten married, after all these months! What a cute couple! KW: (uninterested) Big deal. CC: Jeez, Ken, thanks for the enthusiasm. You have to admit, though-- if Super Blonde and Cardinal Chaos ever get into the ring, it'll be an INCREDIBLE match. KW: No, it'll be a SHORT match. Chaos will kick the Blonde's ass in less than two minutes, and I can personally guarantee that. CC: Let's just move on. Up next, we're going to be watching a pre-recorded interview that Hank Thompson conducted with the Gryphon. This should be good.... ------------- (Hank Thompson is standing outside the AEWA arena. The Gryphon walks out of the arena, and towards Hank Thompson.) Thompson: Hello, Gryphon. Can I have a few moments of your time? Gryphon: Sure, Hank. What do you need? Thompson: President Freeman has just released the lineup for the December Doom pay per view. Have you seen it yet? Gryphon: No, no I haven't. What's on it? (Thompson removes a program from his pocket and hands it to Gryphon.) Thompson: Take a look. (Gryphon examines the program. He then smiles.) Gryphon: There is justice in this world! President Freeman must have watched Saturday Night's card and saw what that low-life, Rick Showboat, did to that poor referee. Thompson: That brings me to my first question. Why did you save Referee Johnson? Gryphon: What kind of question is that? The man was in a great deal of pain, and he didn't deserve to be. Showboat had no business blaming his loss on that referee. I just did what I thought was right, Hank. Randall would've done it himself, but he's.... well, he's been kind of upset lately. Thompson: Why is that? Gryphon: You've heard the interviews. You saw the way he won the Television title. Winning a title because somebody else did something is very, very unsatisfactory. And when you're a man that has as much honor as Randall Osbourne, it's downright upsetting. Listen, Hank, if you want to know how Randall feels, you should just ask him, okay? I've already said a little too much for him. Thompson: Okay. How do you plan on wrestling Showboat at December Doom? Gryphon: Well, Showboat is a former Light Heavyweight champion, Hank. This means two things -- number one, he's got what it takes to be a champion. Number two; he's at a serious weight disadvantage. Don't get me wrong, Hank. I know that just because a guy is smaller than you, doesn't mean he's any less skilled. I know this very well. However, being at a weight disadvantage can do a lot of things. For one, looking up at a man who is half a foot taller and seventy pounds or so heavier has got to be intimidating. Thompson: Yes.... Yes, it is. Gryphon: (chuckles) Anyway, I've also got the advantage of power. Showboat's no weakling -- but, well, how many people have been able to walk into the ring with the Twilight Strangler and been able to WALK out? Only one other man; Primal Fear. He's the only guy I've ever seen who has more power than I. Power, though, means nothing if it is not tempered by experience.... and I have experience. Showboat might have more, he might not. Thompson: What strategy do you think he will attempt to use on you? Gryphon: Well, when you're facing a bigger man, there are two obvious choices. Left knee, right knee.... it's that simple. Showboat's finisher, the Corporate Buy-out, is a perfect example. When you stand in front of a man who you know you can't beat with power, you use speed. Showboat will probably use a 'stick and move' strategy to try to take me down, then he'll go to work on the legs. The problem with this strategy is, well, I know he's going to try to do it, and I'll do everything in my power to stop him from doing it. Showboat can run all he wants -- eventually, he'll make a mistake. No matter how small this mistake it, from that point on, he loses. Mistakes make people, or they break them. Showboat, eventually, you'll make a mistake.... actually, you've made a mistake already. You've gotten me angry, Showboat. I don't think you'll need to make another mistake. That one is enough to put you away--for a long time. Showboat, your time is coming. I'll see you at December Doom. Thompson: Thank you for your time, Gryphon, and good luck. (Fade to black) --------------- KW: Well, the Gryphon is going to finally square off against Rick Showboat at December Doom tonight. I'm really looking forward to that bout. CC: After what Showboat did to poor Referee Johnson last week, I hope the Gryphon teaches him a lesson. KW: That'll NEVER happen. The Gryphon couldn't wrestle his way out of a wet paper bag. CC: We'll see. Anyway, it's now time for an exclusive interview with Justin Trouble. He's going to be squaring off against Big Ron Raider tonight, so let's hear what he plans on doing.... ------------- (The camera opens to Justin Trouble, sitting with Hank Thompson, in the living room of Trouble's ranch in Canada. Trouble has his hair tied back in a pony tail and is wearing an "AEWA Rocks" T-shirt, black jeans and military boots) Thompson: First off, Justin, I've got to say, this is a helluva' place. Trouble: Thanks, Hank. So what brings you to Canada? Thompson: Well as you know, the ink is still fresh on the contract you signed to face Big Ron Raider at December Doom, in none other than the San Jose Arena. How did this opportunity come about? Trouble: Well Bob called me into his office a week ago, almost immediately after my interview was aired on "Straight Talk". I had an open contract to face anyone, and the ICU wasn't stepping up to the plate. Big Ron Raider had an open contract also, and after a few negotiations, the match was set for December Doom. Thompson: And what a match that will be. Do you still have your sights set on the ICU now that you have this world caliber match at hand? Trouble: Definitely. Don't get me wrong. I'm not looking past Big Ron Raider in any way. Big Ron Raider is one of the most, if not the MOST talented big man I've yet to see in the ring. Look at his record of defending the North American title, the guy's a monster. But as for the ICU, well, let's face it, they're not the threat they used to be. The AEWA is definitely winning the war. I mean, let's look at the statistics, Hank. ICU comes in, they take a few titles. But the AEWA has turned the tide by regaining those titles. Who's the World champion? Shane Matthews. You may not like him, I may not like him or what he's done, but the fact is, the guy's 100% AEWA. Then there's Osbourne; he's the TV champ, a guy I respect, and he's 100% AEWA. The only thing the ICU's got is the Light-Heavyweight championship, and come December Doom, you may not like him, I may not like him, but we have to respect him, because Joey Valachi is going to take that title. And once that has been done, then it'll be time for the icing on the cake, because I have a little surprise for the ICU. And once that surprise is revealed, the ICU will be nothing but a distant memory. Thompson: And what's that surprise? Trouble: Now, Hank, it wouldn't be a surprise if I told you.... Thompson: Okay, okay. I guess I'm not gonna get anything out of you there, so can you at least tell me what's going on with you and your manager, Traci? Trouble: I wish I could, Hank, but I honestly don't know. I've called her home and she doesn't return my calls. But the strangest thing about the whole incident is that I called her offices at the wrestling organization that she was the President of, and apparently she handed in her resignation over a month ago. Thompson: Very strange indeed. What do you make of it? Trouble: Well, all I know is, she was not only the first female president of any wrestling organization, but she was also getting paid a lot more than I was paying her, so I don't think her being at the house show to see my interview has anything to do with her wanting her managerial duties back. So to answer your question, I don't know what to make of it. Big Ron Raider, you're a great wrestler, and you've had some great title defenses as of late, but it's not gonna be any picnic for you during our match at the San Jose Arena. You've got something I want, and Raider, I'm going to do my best to take it. At December Doom it's not going to be RAIDER-TIME, but it will be TROUBLE-Time for you, pal. See you at December Doom, big man! Thompson: That's it folks, and don't forget to order December Doom, the pay-per-view event of the year! (fade to black) -------------- KW: Trouble and Raider are both big men, so that should be an AWESOME match. Of course, Raider will power bomb him through the freaking ground. CC: (sarcastically) You're just SO unbiased, Ken. KW: Hey, let's get SERIOUS here-- Justin Trouble doesn't have a snowball's chance in hell. He's going to get his ASS kicked tonight at December Doom. CC: I disagree with you again, but there's no time to argue. Let's go to our next interview; this one is with Paul Wong of the Machines. KW: Yeah, the Machines are going to be defending their National tag team titles against Graphic Violence tonight. Let's hear what Paul has to say.... ---------------- (It's the same room that Simon O'Neal was interviewed in last week; even the folding chair is still there. Pacing back and forth behind the chair is Paul Wong. He continues pacing as he rants in front of the camera.) Paul: First of all, I want to apologize to everyone, especially the fans. I know I have a temper, and that certain things can set me off. But I saved up for three years for that car; I've been wanting a BMW since I was thirteen. For Graphic Violence to destroy it.... You know, when we won the titles, I knew lots of people would be after us, and that they'd try anything to win the belts. But I always figured it should be left behind the ring. You don't go after a man's personal life. Well, Graphic Violence crossed the line, and I smashed one of them with a chair. I don't even remember which one I hit. I know it was wrong, but it felt damn good. Most of the fans seemed to approve, and Simon... well, you know him; he was more upset that I did it right in front of the ref. Now we have a Street Fight Match coming up- no rules, no DQ. We've set it up so that it's us and them, no interference. Great. I'm going to get my hands around their necks, and squeeze.... GRAPHIC VIOLENCE, YOU'RE GOING TO PAY! For destroying my car, for your stupid comments, and for being a waste of humanity! WE'RE GOING TO END YOUR CAREERS!! (Paul picks up the folding chair, and throws it against the wall at full force, denting the chair. Paul looks at the chair, shakes his head, and walks away from the camera without saying another word.) ------------- CC: Paul Wong has gone absolutely insane. I hate to insult him, but it's true-- he's a complete MADMAN. KW: If it wasn't for Graphic Violence, he wouldn't be acting this way. (chuckles) It's actually rather funny. Wong has been preaching to us for MONTHS about not breaking the rules, but then somebody wrecks his car; and he totally loses control. What a moron. CC: But the Machines are just SO determined, I don't see how they can lose the titles tonight. KW: You'll be surprised, Chuck. CC: It's now time for our final interview of the show; "Crimson" Joe Reed and the Lady In Red have some comments for us. Let's watch.... ------------ (The Wickliffe Bar 'n' Grill is crowded with several retired and older wrestlers from past leagues. At one table sits several old IWA stars, including "Crimson" Joe Reed. Sitting next to him is the Lady in Red. Reed is drinking his beer as he tells his story....) Joe Reed: ....So anyway, I finished the dark match, cleaned up, dressed, and went to the concession area for a burger.
