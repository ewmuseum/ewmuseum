Sunday, July 20th, Card (2/3)

20 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EL: We have to take a quick break, now, and, when we get back, we'll talk with Joshua Barnes- you won't want to mis it! (Your favorite stars in your favorite films- it's the WYXZ-TV 39 Movie of the Week, every Wednesday at 8 PM- and now Thursday nights at midnight! This week, Tony Danza inherits $5 million- and 3 orang utans! It's "Going Ape!" on the WYXZ-TV 39 Movie of the Week!) EL: OKJ, everyone, we're back in the world-famous Sportatorium. You'll want to be sure to catch that film, "Going Ape!" Sounds like a great film for the whole family to enjoy. Let's go up to ring announcer, David Myers, and he'll be talking with Joshua Barnes. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, my guest at this time, Joshua Barnes! ("Tom Sawyer" by Rush plays as Joshua Barnes shows unexpected historical savvy. The crowd pops as much for him as for the memories it brings. Over the past few weeks, his hair has grown out, and is now over his shoulders, making his look like a quieter Howard Stern (if such an animal is possible). He looks up and gives a small smile to the camera.} JB: Ummm... Hi. This week, I'm teaming up with the best tag team in the CWAA, Twister. We've got Maxie and those two ... hell, I don't know WHAT you'd call them. McNultey and Anderson. They're all high on my list of people I'd like to kick the s...um... just people I plan on getting back. When I was a bouncer, the bartender at the place gave me a piece of advice. He told me that whatever happens, I have to gain everyone's respect. Otherwise, they'd just ignore me. Well, I don't have the respect of those three. So I have to get it- even if it means I have to beat it out of them. (Barnes quickly leaves, perhaps not aware that Myers may have had questions for him. Oh, well.) EL: OK- Joshua Barnes and Twister- we'll see them together later this week to take on Max Robertson and the tag team champions- it was the PrettyBoy, Byron McNultey, who scored the assist for Max's win in that big lumberjack match, and everyone on that other side has a few scores to settle in this one. Speaking of that lumberjack match, we were supposed to bring you Mark Sherrick and Lt. Lawson- trying to double-team Barnes that evening out on the floor they ended up getting in a little scrape with each other, but as we heard earlier, Mark Sherrick wants to put it off a week. We're going to see if we can get the Lt. out here and maybe get him a stand-by opponent. {The Major and Lt. Lawson enter to their music, "When Johnny Comes Marching Home...". To the great joy and delight of Eric Lane, The Major decides to stop by his announcer's desk and entertain us with his raspy voice and youthful vigor.} MVM: Once again, the force of our opposition combine to attack us. Hammer- and-Sickle, you may have won a battle in our skirmishes. But you will certainly lose the war. I've forgotten many more great battle strategies than you will ever know. Your underhanded and illegal tactics worked once, but will crumble when opposed by the greatest wrestler in the history of the CWAA, Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson. {He pauses. Eric grins, thinking that it's over} MVM: Now then. {Eric's face falls} Soldier, do you know why we were victorious at Battle Klash II? EL: Well, you used a flagpole to chea... MVM: Wrong! We won because our opponent was an English Simpleton who couldn't wrestle his way out of a paperbag! This just proves that once again, when the going gets tough, the English run like the yellow cowards they are. And one other- (David Myers bails) EL: He's done it now! (A gentlemen of 5'8"-250 pounds in a blue mask and recognized by those in the audience who follow the AEWA, WWEW, and other federations as the Light Heavyweight sensation Fuse, runs down the aisle and sldies under the bottom rope) EL: He's got the Major by the ears- headbutt! (Enormous pop as the Major sells it like a gunshot) EL: Lt. Lawson now with a double sledge- and Fuse spins around and pops him right in the mouth! (More popping) EL: Fuse sends him for the ride0 clothesline! (As Lawson flips in mid air his military cap flies off) EL: Sends him to the corner- Fuse up on the second turbuckle- (Cliche alert: Fuse straddles his foe and begins punching to the head as the crowd coutns along) EL: 4- 5- 6- Fuse really giving the Lt. what for-9 -10! (Lawson flops face first and the fans scream with delight) EL: He's going to regret that! (The Major has his fists cocked and ready. Fuse proves to be not unwilling to defend himself against a septuagenarian.) EL: The Major has blown it here- he wants to mix it up with Fuse. The Major swings- nothing but air! (As the Major swings and misses, the force spins him around, and Fuse catches him in an atomic kneedrop. At this point the crowd considers that the rest of the show could be canceeled and they have still gotten more than their money's worth out of these tickets. The Major now sells like Ricky Steamboat, which is more than selling like you've been shot, for those of you too young to know.) EL: The Lt. now rakes his nails acorss Fuse's back. Irish whip- reversed! Fuse catches him- Northern Lights Suplex! He just dropped the Lt. right on his head! (Pop pop pop.) EL: The Major reaching for that flagpole now- keep an eye on him! Fuse pounding away at Lawson. (The crowd pops as Mark Sherrick lumers down the aisle from behind the Major who is trying to steady himself to take a swipe with the flagpole at Fuse) EL: The Major and Lt. have really stepped in it now- look out! (Sherrick yanks the flagpole out of the Major's hands and climbs into the ring) (Sherrick jabs the flagpole into the small of Fuse's back. The crowd boos) EL: What in the world?!? Mark Sherrick attacking Fuse now! Sherrick stomping and kicking and now he tosses that flagpole down and starts choking him. Lawson back on his feet, now he's got the flagpole. (The booing is getting ugly. Debris is thrown into the ring.) EL: Sherrick rolls Fuse over- we need some help in there- what are Sherrick and Lawson doing together like this? Sherrick holding him down by the legs- oh no! Lawson just swung down with that flagpole on Fuse's back! Again! This is reallu un- here we go! (The Major waves frantically for a retreat as the crowd erupts at the sight of the Preacher, Kore Wrench, sprinting to the ring and slidong under the bottom rope, chair in hand.) EL: Lawson and Sherrick dive for safety- the Preacher nearly took off Lawson's head with that swing. Well, Ok. These guys look real proud of themselves, look at the Major grinning. Pretty frightening with the shape his teeth are in, too. Yes, yes, we saw you. (To the three perpretrators, gloating to Lane from 10 feet away as the get ready to leave.) Real big men the three of you are, too, ganging up with flagpoles and what-not to take on one guy from behind. Go on. (Lawson, the Major and Sherrick leave to some serious booing as Wrench, Jeff Howard, Chris Herman, and security attend to Fuse) EL: Well, it looks like this is going to take a few minutes. We were supposed to have Lawson and Sherrick in the ring tonight, but not like this. I don't know why they suddenly became all buddy-buddy like that- Fuse obviously in tremendous pain, that flag pole's solid steel. Last time we saw Sherroick and Lawson they were at each other's throats. I don't know what Sherrick'sm problem, with Fuse can be. Well, we're going to take another break while they take care of Fuse. I'm sure we haven't seen the last of this. Six man action, when we get back. This is just awful. (Another ad for the Movie of the Week. Inc ase you missed the comercail earlier, it's "Going Ape!" with Tony Danza. Really.) EL: OK, we're back. Fuse was able to leave the ring under his own power. We're going to get ready for six-man tag team action now- let's go right to the ring. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is a special six-man tag team match, set for one fall with a twenty-minute time limit. Introducing first- (Darth Vader's normal accompaniment, the "Imperial MA=arch" plays) DM: -at a combined weight of 697 puinds, former CWAA Heavyweight Champion, Max "the Professional" Robertson, and the CWAA Tag Team Champions, "Marvelous" Michael Anderson and "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey! (The bald 200-pound Professional walks in front oif the sequined strutting duo- strutting to the Imperial March?) DM: What do you think of the various proposals which have been made for filling the CWAA Heavyweight Championship? Max Robertson: I am wholly dissatisfied with all of them. I want it made clear that I do not appreciate this legal bull*&$% one bit. If the AEWA had a problem with my choice of challengers, then they should have made their opinions clear before my title defense. If I had known that my title was going to be stripped, then of course I would have given Thomas his shot. I'm no fool. But, it's too late to change things now. My match with the Masked Man was unsanctioned by the AEWA or by the CWAA. So be it then. It doesn't matter to me because I can read rules and regulations as well as any lawyer, and the rules clearly state that the former Heavyweight Champion is first in line for shots at the title. It doesn't matter how I became the former Champion. In reality, I lost the match because of Hammer's betrayal. According to the CWAA and the AEWA, that match never happened, the Masked Man is still a virtually unranked non-contender, and I still deserve the next title shot. (The tag champs nod gravely hile Max speaks) DM: But according to the most recent rankings, you are far from being the number one contender... MR: Listen! That belt is mine, just like the Television Belt is Lawson's! And I will reclaim my property! I don't care what it takes! (In the background, McNUltey can be heard to say "Testify!") DM: Rumor has it that there will be another tournament for the Heavyweight Title. Would you be willing to participate in such an event? MR: I'd be willing to wrestle the winner of such a tournament for the title. That's fair. That's the Professional Way; that's the only way, and this interview is over. (Our tag team champions, making sure the cameras and fans notice the titles, head over to David Myers , in order to address the fans. "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey is dressed in a Hawaiian shirt, and yellow pants, while "Marvelous" Michael Anderson sports his tuxedo that he wore in his match against the "Dreamer" not too long ago.) BM: Why don't all of you people be quiet and admire the beauty that sits in this ring? (The crowd continues their booing, but slowly die down.) Anyway, Mikey, it's been a while since we last gave all these ladies in the crowd something to dream about. Apparently, after seeing us one hour a week, all those hogs they call husbands demanded that we be taken off the air. MA: Can you blame them? I mean, we are THAT great looking. (Barely noticing his partners' antics, Max is stretching in a corner) BM: Now you see Dok down there (pointing to Lane, who is shaking his head), he is the biggest offender. (The crowd likes Lane, and the jeering grows louder.) MA: Really. Always telling people that we cheat to win, and are afraid of those second-rate opponents they throw at us every week. BM: Second-rate? I would've thought fourth-rate at best. Hell, my ole granny wrestles better than those goofs they put us in the ring with. (Insulting fan favorites like Twister and the rest of the "cavalry" doesn't help quiet the crowd down, but it doesn't phase our heroes in the ring.) MA: Ya know, I hear that those two are in some other league, a fancy one with lights that work, and air-conditioned lockers, and get this. Their fans almost have all of their teeth. (Insulting the crowd won't quiet them down any quicker as well.) BM: I haven't met someone with all their teeth in a long time. With the obvious exception of us, I thought people like that didn't exist anymore. MA: Now, let us get to the point for why we're out here. BM: Wasn't it to show the ladies what real men look like, after they've gone without us for so long? MA: Yea, but there's a second thing. Let's see, we danced, we showed off our titles-that's it. Out titles. Now, ever since we won these fancy belts, fair and square (the crowd begins to let them know that they disagree), people have falsely claimed that we need to cheat to win. BM: Well, ya wrong. We don't need no cheatin'. In fact, we don't have a cheatin' bone in either of out beautiful bodies. (The crowd continues to boo) Oh, shut up you buck-toothed inbreds! MA: So-shut up!- when we come back out here tonight to wrestle with the third-best wrestler in this leauge (Max's ears prick up at this) MA: -um...you know who I'm talking about-we will show all of you heifers just what pure wrestling is like. EL: That was just a tasteless display put forth by our current Tag Team champions. Fans, I apologize... DM: Their opponents- (The "Twister" theme plays) DM: -at a combined weight of 831 lb., Paul "the Tornado" Langford, Travis Conley, and Joshua Barnes! (pop pop pop. Hop on pop...) DM: Your referee, Chris Herman.
