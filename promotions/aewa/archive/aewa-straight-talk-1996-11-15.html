Straight Talk (11/15)

19 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera fades in on the AEWA Studios, the AEWA logo falls onto the screen, and the words "STRAIGHT TALK" slide in underneath it. The whole logo then tilts, and falls off the bottom of the screen. Behind the desk in studio are Chuck Carlson and Ken Wilson.) CC: Hello everyone, and welcome to yet another edition of AEWA Straight Talk. I'm your host, Chuck Carlson, along with my co-host, Ken Wilson. KW: I've got a migraine headache, so PLEASE don't talk so damn loud. Let's just get to the freaking interviews. CC: Uh, Ken, maybe you should have called in sick. KW: Maybe you should shut your damn mouth. I felt fine before I got HERE, and then YOU started talking. Can we just hurry up and get this over with? CC: Of course. Up first, let's watch this interview with "Slim" Jim Coolridge.... ---------- (The camera opens on "Slim" Jim Coolridge, sitting alone in the locker room. He appears to be in a VERY bad mood.) Coolridge: Shane Matthews, I've waited and waited and waited. Time's up. One way or another, you're going to HAVE to face me. Even if that means I have to hunt you down.... If you want to lessen your chance of injury, get in the ring. Otherwise, you're a marked man! (fade to black) ------------- KW: What does he have against Shane Matthews? Coolridge is barking up the wrong tree, folks, because Shane could DESTROY him. CC: I don't agree, Ken. KW: You never do. CC: That's a good point. Anyway, let's move on to our next interview, from the Japanese Dragons. These guys were in the AEWA several months ago, and they'll be making their return soon. ------------- (We see Super White Samurai and Blue Ninja, the Japanese Dragons, standing in front of the AEWA banner. Their manager, "Dangerous" David Jones, is right behind them.) David Jones: WE'RE BACK! I'm back in the AEWA with my tag team The Japanese Dragons. Our contract was examined and we got back in the AEWA! Now, we are ready to take care of some business. Here's Super White Samurai! Super White Samurai: I'm back on the AEWA scene. I'm ready to face Light-Heavyweights and Tag teams with my partner Super Blue Ninja. This federation is the place to be! Baby, I see that "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt has been on a hot streak recently defending his AEWA Light-Heavyweight title. Well, I'm no pushover, and I'm ready to get a shot at that belt. Here's Super Blue Ninja.... Blue Ninja: 265 pounds of pure muscle, that's what makes me Super Blue Ninja. I see Deranged added another title to his list of belts. Well, I'm looking for that AEWA Television Title because I'm one of the best-- I'm on a quest for gold, baby. Me and my partner, Super White Samurai, formed the Japanese Dragons a year ago. We have held tag titles in other places, but the AEWA is our next stop. We see the Machines are the National tag team champions and we're gonna flame broil them first. So Machines, get ready to face me and my partner, because The Japanese Dragons are on fire! David Jones: AEWA, get ready to feel the heat, baby! (fade to black) ------------ KW: Those guys are in for a BIG surprise. The AEWA has changed a LOT since they last wrestled here, and they're going to learn that-- the hard way. CC: The Japanese Dragons should NOT be taken lightly, Ken. They're going to be wrestling soon, so we'll have to wait and see. KW: So what's next? CC: We have an interview with the Masked Yorel. This guy looks VERY familiar to me, but I'm not sure why. Let's just watch.... ---------- (The camera shows a dark shadow of a very, very big man. He then steps into the light a little bit, and says....) Masked Yorel: AEWA, if I were you, I'd be very afraid.... afraid of what I might do to all the so-called superstars, afraid of how I will turn the AEWA upside down, afraid of what I will do to win the Battle Royal, afraid of what The Masked Yorel might do to the wrestling world! Sooner or later, you will have to face the force that is The Masked Yorel, because I am now in the AEWA. I will beat all these goody two-shoes superstars up; it's going to be BEAUTIFUL. You won't even know what hit ya after the Masked Yorel runs OVER you! (fade to black) -------------- KW: Who IS that guy? I agree with you, Chuck, he REALLY looks familiar. I think he might have wrestled in another organization or something. CC: Maybe we should do some investigative reporting.... KW: (laughing) Spend extra time with YOU? You've got to be KIDDING me, buddy. My headache is bad enough right now, there's no need to make it worse. CC: Let's just listen to these comments from Sir Gallade, a man who will be competing in that upcoming battle royal.... --------- (The scene opens with Sir Gallade standing in the AEWA studios. He smiles at the camera, and begins to speak) Sir Gallade: Now, I am usually not a selfish guy, but I am taking the upcoming Battle Royal very seriously. I am planning on winning the Battle Royal any way I can, because to win the World Title you have to bite and scratch your way to the top. I do not care who it is I have to throw out, because I, Sir Gallade, want to represent the AEWA by being it's World Champion. I will see you all at the Battle Royal. (fade to black) ------------- KW: Jeez, he is SO annoying. The Middle Ages ended thousands of years ago, but nobody has taken the time to inform that loser. Who does he think he is? King Arthur? CC: No, but he IS an extremely talented wrestler. Personally, I think he'll win that battle royal and become the AEWA World Heavyweight champion. KW: In your dreams, Chuck. CC: Up next, we have some comments from the Crazy Canuck. That guy is a VERY strange character, isn't he? Let's watch. ----------- ( We see the Crazy Canuck in a hospital bed. His manager, "NATO" Wally Henderson, is at his side. The British Destroyer and the Russian Tank, better known as the NATO Powers, are sitting down next to him.) Henderson: Primal Fear, you really ticked us off with your repeated attacks on the Canuck. I hope you're ready for what's coming your way. Canuck: PRIMAL FEAR, YOU'RE MINE. This is the second time you've attacked me and this time you will suffer the consequences of your actions. Setting me on fire was the first step in my rebirth, trying to kill me with that baseball bat of yours completed the process. Are you ready to face the monster you've created? I think you're scared of me but if you're dumber than you look, sign this contract. It's simple, I want to face you in a STEEL CAGE STRETCHER MATCH. Are you brave enough to face me or is your name, Primal Fear, a reflection of your soul when you think of me? Are you afraid of the CRAZY ONE? Fear, your end is coming soon. Henderson: Primal Fear, I hope you're ready for what you've unleashed. Tank: Fear, the Canuck will destroy you. Now as for the Powers, we have an open contract to face any tag team out there brave enough to wrestle us. So sign on the dotted line, IF YOU DARE. Destroyer: We will rise once again. (screen fades to black) ------------ KW: Jeez, the Crazy Canuck looked absolutely INSANE in that interview! He's going to DESTROY Primal Fear when they finally go at it! CC: And what about the NATO Powers? They claimed to have an open contract now, so we might be seeing them back in action soon. KW: It's definitely a possibility. CC: Now here are some comments from Justin Trouble, another participant in that upcoming battle royal.... ------------- (The scene opens on Justin Trouble, running down a quiet trail. He is wearing a sweater, black jeans, military boots, and sporting a rugged looking beard. Trouble stops running and faces the camera) Trouble: You know, if a man can overtake and beat the elements of Canada, it says a lot for his will power as well as his physical power.. And that's what I'm doing. I'm staying here, in Calgary until the battle royal for the world's championship. Every morning, I get up at 5 am, and leave by 5:30 (am) so that I can run through the snowy mountains of Canada. I'm preparing for an enemy that's every bit as cold as the weather here in Canada. I'm preparing to wage war with the ICU. The battlegrounds are marked. The battle royal. Heavy Metal, you're a big guy. Towering over 7 feet, weighing at over 400 pounds. You're dangerous; your body is a lethal weapon. But don't underestimate the powers of the mind. A great average-sized man can beat a good big man anytime. As for the battle royal, I've said it before and I'll say it again-- I may walk out of there still a contender, I may walk out champion, but, I'll walk out happy either way knowing that the ICU didn't get their grubby hands on the gold. So here it is.... ICU, you KNOW I'm gunning for you. But as for the wrestlers of the AEWA, don't think once I've kicked the asses of the ICU that I'll stop at them. Pure and simple, there's a lot at stake here. And I'm willing to do what ever it takes to walk away with the most coveted title in the sport today. As for "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt, let's get something straight. I DON'T like you; I never have, and probably never will. But you have a big match-up with Penance coming up.... And Mike Hurt, if you need me, I WILL be in your corner. As much as I dislike you. I'd rather help you then see the ICU walk away with the AEWA Light Heavyweight belt. And on a side note-- Mike Stewart, if you're out there.... my best wishes and prayers for your quick recovery go out to you. FANS, never give up faith, because without it, we have nothing. (The camera fades to black, as Trouble starts running again) ----------- KW: Why does he CONSTANTLY challenge the ICU like that? Is he TRYING to get his ass kicked? CC: Justin Trouble has a bright future ahead of him, Ken, and he just might win that battle royal. Don't underestimate him. KW: I can underestimate whoever the hell I want. CC: Let's just move on to this interview with Primal Fear. He recently attended an NBA basketball game, and we have some footage.... ------------- (The camera opens on Primal Fear at a basketball game, standing in the locker room tunnel. He's wearing an NBA T-shirt.) Primal Fear: You know, watching these guys out here play gracefully and slam dunking with ease, reminds me how I am gonna slam dunk all the competition in the battle royal. I have never held a title here in the AEWA and this is the time where I plan on getting one. Whoever gets in my way is gonna get tossed out and better be prepared to hit the cement hard. Twilight Strangler, you better watch out 'cause your ass is gonna get eliminated early! (As Primal Fear continues to talk, Jack Nicholson happens to walk by. He is obviously going to watch the rest of the game.) Primal Fear: (doing his best impersonation) YOU CAN'T HANDLE THE TRUTH! (Nicholson smiles and just keeps walking) Primal Fear: Hmmm.... My impression wasn't THAT bad. Anyway, everyone in that battle royal had better watch out, 'cause I am not wasting any time. Oh and Crazy Canuck, I have a big surprise for you.... (fade to black) ---------- KW: Primal Fear actually got to meet Jack Nicholson, and he acted like a complete dork. Jeez, I'm not surprised. CC: You have to admit, Ken, it WAS a good impression. KW: Uh, I hate to burst your bubble, but it SUCKED. Anyway, we don't have time to talk about Primal Fear's stupid little comedy routine.... What's next? CC: Let's watch this interview with the Twilight Strangler and Miss DeMeanor. These two are ALWAYS interesting. ------------ (The camera opens on Miss DeMeanor and the Twilight Strangler in a training gym. They both look very angry.) Miss DeMeanor: AEWA, are you nuts? You are letting the inmate run the asylum! You have released the MAD DOG, and I hate to scare everybody, but this man is CRAZY! Strangler: AEWA, I have warned you many times, but you just haven't listened, have you? Now I'm going to do what I do best-- and that is just breaking bones, cutting people open, and kickin' ass! Miss DeMeanor: You know, Strangler, people say that you can't wrestle worth sh*t. How does it make you feel when people insult you? (The Twilight Strangler grabs a 50 pound weight and throws it across the gym. It crumbles against the wall) Miss DeMeanor: Oh, he's mad now! Watch out, AEWA! (fade to black) ------------ KW: That guy is absolutely insane, and he's going to DOMINATE in the battle royal. In fact, I think he'll win the title. CC: Then you're even crazier than HE is! The Strangler has a LOT to learn here in the AEWA, and Primal Fear still wants to kick his butt. KW: What's next? CC: We have an interview with a newcomer to the AEWA, "Crimson" Joe Reed. That guy has been VERY successful in other leagues, and we're all very excited about his arrival. Let's watch.... ------------- (Hank Thompson stands outside the AEWA offices. The door opens, and two figures exit. The man is tall and lanky, with short red hair and a calm look in his eyes. The woman could best be described as an Amazon; tall, in excellent physical condition, and with a fire in her eyes to match her own hair.) Thompson: Folks, I'm standing here with wrestling superstar "Crimson" Joe Reed and his manager, The Lady in Red. We've heard rumors that you might be joining the AEWA, and now here you are outside Bob Freeman's office. Can you confirm these rumors? Lady in Red: We have a few more details to work out, but Joe and myself will be joining the AEWA once those are ironed out. We were hoping to join in time for the World Title Battle Royal, but some other commitments kept us out. Thompson: What are your goals in the AEWA, Joe? Reed: I've been wrestling for over ten years now, Hank, and I've held two World Titles. Twice I've been King of the Hill, and it's the most powerful feeling I've ever experienced, more addictive than any drug. I want to be there again. Now I know I can't just waltz in and demand a title shot. I've climbed up from the bottom before, and I'm willing to do it again. I have an open contract with any wrestler in the AEWA. I'm not out for blood or revenge; I just intend to climb my way up the ladder. Lady In Red: Also, we've heard that the AEWA might be starting a ladies' division soon. I haven't wrestled in several years.... but it's like riding a bicycle; once you have it, you never lose it. I might be in the ring myself soon. Thompson: There you have it. Back to Chuck and Ken.... (fade to black) ------------- KW: He seems like a pretty cool guy, but I'm withholding my judgment until I see him wrestle. CC: This may come as a surprise to you, Ken, but no one really CARES about what you think. Anyway, Joe Reed should be making his debut sometime in the next week. KW: (with little enthusiasm) I can hardly wait. CC: Up next, let's watch these comments from "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum and Sondra. This should be interesting.... ---------- (The scene opens outside the locker room, where we join the "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum and Sondra Slocum.) Ben Slocum: It seems to me as if all of the heel groups may have actually bitten off more than they could chew. The fact is that they're practically falling apart as we speak, while the Knights of Deliverance only keep getting stronger. It may be Roberts, the Reaper, Deathwatch, Crowley or Cardinal Chaos ahead for me. Right now, I really couldn't care less. They're all scum, and they'll all eventually have to answer to me or someone like me. There are just too many wrestlers who think they can switch from day-to-day without anyone paying attention, but that's not how it works. Sondra: The battle lines have been drawn, and now it's time to put up or shut up. More of that later, but let's not forget that there is that huge Battle Royal for the World Title coming up, and my brother has been quite successful in a few of those earlier on in his career. I'm sure this one won't be that different. Ben: Although you can't help but feel sorry for the way Stewart lost the title, this is still a great opportunity for me to once again reach the top and get to wear the World Title around my waist again. With well over twenty opponents in that ring, the name of the game is survival, and that's exactly what I'm about-- SURVIVAL. Sondra: The AEWA really had some problems until now, and it was getting out of control. It was obvious that the AEWA needed a strong group to assemble and speak up for all of the GREAT FANS out there. That's why the Knights of Deliverance were gathered, and that's why we'll win eventually. I believe Ben has a challenge for all of you rulebreakers.... Ben. That's right! Although I haven't spoken to my partners about this, I'm pretty sure they won't hesitate to accept.... It's up to anyone of you heel stables to find the courage to sign on the dotted line, 'cause on behalf of the Knights of Deliverance, I'm challenging you to a Wargames match. You'd better prepare well, though, because we'll sure as hell be ready for a war. (fade to black) -------------- KW: Ben Slocum has a really big mouth, but he never ACTS on any of his challenges. If the Knights of Deliverance want a match with another stable, it WILL happen-- but Slocum probably wouldn't even have the courage to show up. CC: You've got to be kidding me, Ken. He's a former World champion, give him SOME credit. KW: No thanks. CC: Anyway, moving right along, our next interview is with the Shadow Warriors. These guys are looking for some gold.... --------------- (The camera opens on Aaron Ladd and Reuben "Thunder" Slade, the Shadow Warriors, sitting in the Cantrell Centre. Wesley Cantrell and Lianne are standing next to them. They're all watching tapes of other AEWA tag teams in action.) Ladd: There is obviously something wrong when the two tag team titles in the AEWA are held by two of the worst teams to ever step into the squared circle-- the Graveyard Shift and the Machines. I'm not sure how it happened in the first place, but if the fed doesn't want to lose whatever is left of it's already weak credibility, then they had better snatch the belts away from that bunch of freaks and give them to someone who deserves 'em-- like us. Slade: But then WE wouldn't get to wipe the mat with those sewer rats, Aaron. There really is nothing that helps better to cheer you up than destroying some of these jokes, and completely annihilating their pathetic careers. Don't you agree there, Mr C? Cantrell: Perhaps there is one thing, and that would be to turn the imbecile simians that gave them birth into real trashy bagladies. That's the reason why money is so great. You can use it to further humiliate those who have had a rough time in life, and then go ahead and laugh as the jackasses make fools out of themselves. Life is just so great when you have money. Lianne: Perhaps the easiest way to get a win here in the tag-team division would be to drop a dollar in the ring, and then watch the morons fight over it before finishing them off and pinning them. Cantrell: There's an idea we haven't tried yet. Perhaps they don't realize what kind of amounts I'm ready to pay to get my hands on the titles again. Therefore we should probably lower our offers until we reach numbers that are comprehensible to the drooling little hillbillies. Ladd: Anyway, there is no way that the Shadow Warriors will remain idle for long while others are dominating. The tag-team division is a big joke, and we'll go to any lengths to get our hands on the gold again. Slade: (stares at the camera) Just don't complain if you happen to wake up without an arm or a leg, 'cause you all brought it upon yourselves by refusing to give us title matches. We DESERVE a match. Cantrell: As if that wasn't enough, they have also tried to censor us and limit our airtime, to keep all of those peon fans happy. Therefore from now on, we stop being nice guys. The Cantrell Corporation is turning violent. Remember that you only have yourselves to blame though. (fade to black) --------------- KW: The Shadow Warriors seemed VERY angry with the AEWA Board Of Directors. There does appear to be a CONSPIRACY against them. CC: Jeez, you get stupider every week. The Board has NOTHING against Wesley Cantrell or his men, but the Shadow Warriors haven't EARNED a title shot. I mean, when was the last time they wrestled? Back at Mega Combat, when they lost to the Machines. That doesn't seem like a winning streak to me, Ken. KW: I didn't ask you. CC: Up next, we have an interview with another newcomer to the AEWA, a guy by the name of Brian Rice. He's going to be competing in that battle royal also, so this should be interesting.... -------------- (We see Brian Rice in the AEWA locker room. He turns to the camera, smiles, and begins to speak loudly.) Brian Rice: I'm getting really tired of sitting here, listening to the Twilight Strangler talk about how no one can beat him. That's a load of crap. Well, I've got a challenge for you, Strangler. Why don't we make a match for Wednesday Night Rumble, and we'll find out who the REAL sissy is! Are you ready, chump? (fade to black) --------------- KW: Jeez, he's ALREADY challenging a big guy like the Twilight Strangler? Is he a retard or something? CC: No, he's a talented young athlete, and he's going to surprise a lot of people. In fact, he just might win that battle royal. KW: (sarcastically) Yeah right. CC: Next, let's listen to these comments from the Machines. After what happened last week with Graphic Violence, I'm very interested. KW: Roll it.... ------------- (Paul Wong is studying videotapes of various AEWA wrestlers as Simon O'Neal walks in. For once, Simon actually has a worried look on his face. He is carrying a videotape in his hands.) Simon: Uh.... Paul, we need to talk. Remember how I borrowed your car last week? Paul: Yeah, the VW Rabbit you drive is being fixed, and you claim your ex-wives will take anything else you drive. (suddenly looking worried) You didn't cause MY car to get towed again, did you? Simon: This isn't my fault! Here, watch this.... (He puts in the videotape of the latest Straight Talk. We see the footage of Leech Rogers and Booze Murphy, better known as Graphic Violence, drenching the car in gasoline. Leech throws a lit cigarette on the car, and it BURSTS into flames. As the footage ends, there is an awkward silence. Paul continues to stare into the television with an odd look on his face.) Simon: Paul? Look man, they were trying to burn MY car. I'm sorry that they got yours by mistake.... Paul: (speaking barely above a whisper) First, I'm going to call my insurance, and take care of this. Second, we're going to offer them a rematch. They obviously want us in the ring very badly. Thirdly.... I'm going to end their careers. And you're going to help me. Simon: End their careers? What about sportsmanship, fair play, giving everyone a second chance.... Paul: Be quiet, Simon. (still speaking in that odd tone) All I wanted was to wrestle without any of that unnecessary garbage. Graphic Violence crossed the line. I'm going to make sure they don't do it again. (Stands up and walks away) Simon: (shakes his head, then faces the camera) You know, for all the fights I get into with Paul, I don't dare cross certain lines. He's one of the only people I've met that actually can scare me. Graphic Violence, Booze, Leech-- I actually feel sorry for you. Because the only other time I ever saw him like this.... (also walks away) (fade to black) -------------- CC: Jeez, Paul looked SCARY during that interview. What's he planning to do? That was rather weird. KW: I have no idea, but he had better stay FAR away from Graphic Violence. When those teams finally get into the ring again, all HELL is gonna break loose. I can hardly wait. CC: It's now time for our final interview. Hank Thompson recently traveled to the ICU Headquarters, and we have the EXCLUSIVE footage for you. This should be good. KW: Let's watch.... ------------- (The camera opens on Hank Thompson standing outside of a huge black building. There are double doors in front and there is a metal sign that is latched onto the door and reads "International Contracts Unlimited" on it. Hank Thompson straightens his tie and clears his throat before walking in) Hank Thompson: Hello AEWA fans, I'm here at this building to conduct an interview with one of the stables that has been causing a lot of noise lately in the AEWA-- and that stable is none other than International Contracts Unlimited, or as some may call it, ICU. Let's go in, shall we? (Hank Thompson walks through the door and the camera opens in to a huge lobby room. There is red carpet on the floor; the ceiling is about 20 feet off the ground and is lit up with chandeliers. There is a desk with a secretary behind it to the left of Thompson. She is a young woman with curly brown hair and she wears a lot of make-up. Thompson walks up to the lady) Hank Thompson: Excuse me, would you happen to know what floor I can find a Mister Deathwatch at? Woman: Penthouse. Hank Thompson: Thanks. (Hank Thompson walks over to the elevator and waits for about 1 minute. The elevator door is made of steel and is a dark gray; the two doors split apart and open in to an elevator with white carpeting and wood-covered walls. Hank Thompson and the cameraman step inside, and Thompson presses the button for the penthouse. They sit in the elevator for what seems like forever, but in actuality only lasted about 3 minutes, until the doors of the elevator open up. Thompson and the cameraman step out.) Deathwatch: Come.... sit. We have much to discuss. (The penthouse is a HUGE room with wood-frame walls, a black carpet, and a huge window behind the oak desk that Deathwatch stands at. Deathwatch is wearing a gray suit, white shirt, gray slacks, and a red tie. His long red hair is pulled back and he has a grin on his face. As Deathwatch sits down in his black leather chair, Thompson takes a seat in one of the leather chairs in front of the desk. The desk is cleared off, and it looks like Deathwatch was prepared for the company of Thompson) Hank Thompson: Thanks, where is the rest of your group? Deathwatch: They will be here, don't worry about them for right now. We can start without them. Hank Thompson: Okay then. For my first que-- Deathwatch: (interrupting) Must you be so formal? Would you care for anything to drink? Hank Thompson: Uh, yeah, I guess.... I think I'll just take a glass of ice water. Deathwatch: (holding his finger under the desk) Breanne, please bring up one glass of ice water, would you? Breanne: (from the other end of an intercom that is nowhere to be seen) Right away, boss. Deathwatch: Now, you were saying? Hank Thompson: Yes, well, my first question is.... What are your comments on the latest addition to the AEWA and President Freeman's personal "bodyguard", Justin Trouble? Deathwatch: Trouble.... Trouble.... He is a man that I have respect for. Then again, I have respect for almost anyone as long as they don't get in my way, if you catch my drift. Trouble is a man that wants to be the hero, he wants to stick up for this federation because he wants to be on the President's good side. I don't see him as much more than a brown noser, but I still have some respect for the man because he has not yet stood in my way. Maybe when that time comes, I will show him why my name is Deathwatch. Hank Thompson: He sure sounds like he WANTS to stand in your way. He has challenged you to a match for Saturday Night Slam-- your comments on that? Deathwatch: Of course, the young newcomer wants to go after one of the men that has been here the longest. Trouble, if you truly want to get in my way come this Saturday, then so be it. I have no quarrel with you, nor do I wish to start one, but if this is how it must be.... then good luck to you, because you will be needing it! Hank Thompson: Don't you find this man as the slightest threat to you and your stable? I mean, under authorization from the AEWA, he has almost as much power as you do-- if not equal. Deathwatch: I find no man as a threat to me, my lifestyle, my endeavors, or my organization. Justin Trouble is just what I have stated before.... a lackey. He's there to do Bob Freeman's bidding, nothing more. Sure, he may have offered his services to the President, but he could have done the same to me and he would have been nothing more than what he is right now-- a lap dog. (A beautiful young woman walks into the room with a glass of water. She wears a tight blue dress and has long blonde hair and blue eyes. She walks up to the desk and hands Thompson his water, leaning over enough to allow him a good look and then she walks away. Hank Thompson appears to be a little shaken) Hank Thompson: (gulps) Ahem, sorry.... So, is that what you consider your stablemates? Heavy Metal doesn't seem like the kind of guy that would willingly be someone's "lap dog", as you put it. Deathwatch: You have a bit of.... (touches himself on the cheek and Thompson quickly wipes away at his mouth. Deathwatch grins) But no, the people that I ask to join my force are their own men. They do what they want, when they want. They do as they please and go where they want to, I have nothing against that. The people that become lap dogs are men like Justin Trouble-- he asks to join with them and then does all the dirty work. Trouble, you do know that there are people who can do that for them, it's called SECURITY.... and if you knew what that was, take a look in a mirror sometime; it's what you have become. Hank Thompson: (drinks water) Fair enough, now what do you have to say about the Battle Royal coming up? You have three of your men in there, what do you think your chances are? Deathwatch: Our chances are as good as anyone else's. Battle Royals are very unpredictable; you could have your back turned one moment and the next, you will be laying on the floor, eliminated. It's always hard to tell who will win. Some may shoot for the bigger men, some may choose the smaller and quicker, but there is only one way to determine who will win it, and that is by sheer skill. I wish the best of luck to my teammates, but there is only one man worthy to walk out of that ring the champion-- and that is me. I have been here far longer than most going into that ring, and I have been overlooked throughout my stay here. This is my chance to prove to the world where the ultimate power lies.... and it lies within me. Hank Thompson: How about The Caretaker? He is back in the AEWA, he is your blood brother, and he absolutely HATES you. Deathwatch: Yes, regretfully The Caretaker is my blood brother, and as he denies me, I also deny him. He is not even related to me as far as I am concerned. Hey 'Taker, your attitude has changed a bit, hasn't it? You used to be so dark, so unusual, so.... repulsive. Was it that whack over the head with the shovel that did it? Maybe I knocked a little bit of sense into you to make you act like a human being instead of a dead drone. Sure, you got a new look, a new attitude, and a new group of losers to hang around with, but you will always be the same old Caretaker. Hank Thompson: And how about his "group of people"? They call themselves the Knights of Deliverance-- your comments? Deathwatch: You must look at their name and think of whether they are for real; or if it is just a fantasy in all of their lost hopes. They will never be knights, and they shall never serve their deliverance. As you look to most "groups", you have to think-- are any based upon reality or are they all just fictional dreams? I have named mine true-to-life, based upon the company in which I am the CEO of. You cannot deny the fact that it is based on reality. To the Knights of Deliverance, I say this.... Keep dreaming. Hank Thompson: I would like to get some comments from your other members, do you think it's time yet? Deathwatch: But of course. (holds hand under the desk again) Breanne, send in Heavy Metal. (About one minute passes and Heavy Metal steps off the elevator. His long brown hair hangs to the sides of his head. He wears black leather pants, a black leather vest, a black "Metallica" shirt with blood dripping from the letters, a black leather glove, and black boots. He takes a seat right next to Thompson) Heavy Metal: Hey dudes, what's up? Deathwatch: This is Mister Thompson, from the AEWA, he would like to conduct an interview with you-- but try not to take too long, we are limited on time and still have to fit everyone else in as well. Heavy Metal: Yeah, do I EVER spend too much time with people in suits!? Oh....well....besides you, of course. Deathwatch: Note taken. Deathwatch: It's a little thing called mind manipulation. What are your goals and comments on the situation? Heavy Metal: Dude, you could put ALL my stablemates in there and it will be the same result-- I am gonna go on in there, kick everyone's asses and throw each and every one of those losers over the top rope. Whether it's Exodus OR Deathwatch, I really couldn't care less. I want my gold back and that is how it's going to happen! Hank Thompson: (drinks water) So, you don't even CARE about eliminating your stablemates? Heavy Metal: What? Are you deaf? Is that not what I just told you?? Listen, dude. I don't care WHO it is, if they are in MY way, you can consider 'em history! You get me, little man? Hank Thompson: Understood. How about you, Deathwatch? Your comments on what Heavy Metal just stated? Deathwatch: I told you before-- they do what they want, when they want. I'm not here to control them, just to guide them through, and to back them up. WHAT they do concerns me, but HOW they do it doesn't. Hank Thompson: So, this group isn't a one-for-all relationship then? Heavy Metal: HELL no, we're one-for-ourselves. Deathwatch is a friend, not much more, and if he ever double crossed me at any point in time, he could kiss my ass goodbye then. Hank Thompson: Interesting. So, Metal, what do you think about the Angels of Death? Heavy Metal: What'd ya mean "What do you think"? They're a group of idiots, each and every one of them. Twisted Steel is a moron in his own right, and just because he quit the Angels of Death, doesn't mean he's off the hook. Let me just tell you this right now, little man.... EVERYONE that stands up against us, plots to do our job, or annoys us in any way, they will not only be marked up on our "To do" list, they'll be destroyed along with all the other punks! Hank Thompson: I see, so how about Justin Trouble? Any thoughts? Heavy Metal: Yeah, I got some thoughts. I THINK he's a moron, I THINK he's stupid for even challenging us, I THINK he's in for a real ass kicking! Would ya like some more thoughts or does that sum it up for ya? Hank Thompson: I think I get the picture. Now how about Mike Stewart, he had the worst out of the fall during that scaffold match. (drinks some water) What do you think about that situation? Heavy Metal: What happened to Stewart was his own fault. If he had let me do what I was gonna do anyway, and that's give him a beating, it wouldn't have been AS BAD, but I assure ya.... it would have been enough to put him out for awhile anyway. But what he tried to pull, now THAT put him out forever. Hasta lavista, Stewart. You won't be missed! (laughs) Hank Thompson: You sure don't show much compassion towards anyone, do you? Heavy Metal: Nope. Hank Thompson: Well, do you have any closing statements? Heavy Metal: Nope. Hank Thompson: Then I'll move along. Heavy Metal: Nice to get things straightened out, ya know? (grins) See ya later, dudes. (Heavy Metal gets up and walks into the elevator and the doors shut. Deathwatch puts his hand under the desk and holds it there again) Deathwatch: Breanne, send up Penance. Breanne: He's already up there, didn't you know? I tried to stop him, but he insisted-- Deathwatch: It's fine, Breanne. (brings his hand up) You can come out now, Penance. (A man walks out from the corner of the room where the shadows fill it. He hangs his head low, with his long black hair hanging in front of his face. He wears a long black trenchcoat, black shirt, black pants, and black boots. He walks over to the seat where Heavy Metal was sitting and slowly takes a seat; he gives Hank Thompson a cold stare and then a dark smile) Deathwatch: Well, there he is. Conduct your interview, Mister Thompson. Hank Thompson: Oh....well I....uh.... (takes a drink of water) It's just that.... I mean.... you know, like.... Penance: (talking in a soft, dark voice) Hush. You talk too much. You wear out your jaw, speak clearly now and make what you have to say be known. I am not here to listen to your babble. Penance: I have no goals. I am a man that allows himself to be taken with the wind-- and wherever that wind leads, you will find Penance there. Don't trust the darkness, it can play around with your mind. Hank Thompson: There's some food for thought. How about the Light Heavyweight championship? Deathwatch: Thank you, you truly are a sweetheart. Breanne: Thanks, boss! Exodus: It's the title formerly known as the ACEW Championship. It is now the EXODUS Championship Title! If anyone has a problem with that, PLEASE report to me, thank you. Exodus: I will go back to that loser federation, but only if the demands set are met, and so far.... it's not looking too good. Hank Thompson: What were the demands?
