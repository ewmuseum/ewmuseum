Straight Talk (11/22)

25 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera fades in on the AEWA Studios, the AEWA logo falls onto the screen, and the words "STRAIGHT TALK" slide in underneath it. The whole logo then tilts, and falls off the bottom of the screen. Behind the desk in studio are Chuck Carlson and Ken Wilson.) CC: Hello everyone, and welcome to another informative edition of AEWA Straight Talk. I'm your host, Chuck Carlson, along with my co-host, "Crazy" Ken Wilson. KW: My headache is finally gone, but you're STILL annoying. Can we PLEASE just skip the crap and get the show started? CC: Let's just take our time, Ken. I'm getting paid by the hour. (chuckles) Our first interview comes to us from Primal Fear. That guy has been VERY angry with the Crazy Canuck lately, so this should be interesting. KW: Roll the footage.... ------------- (The camera opens on Primal Fear at a Kansas City Chiefs football game. He appears to be in a bad mood.) Fear: Boy, being in the AEWA does have its perks, but it also has its irks! Crazy Canuck, you have the audacity to challenge me to a Stretcher Cage Match? Son, I don't think you have the POWER to say what kind of match it will be! This is gonna be a Primal Cage match, son! What's a Primal Cage match, you ask? It's a cage, with a top on it, and it is wrapped in barbed wire! The only way you can win is by making your opponent submit! Your opponent has to GIVE UP! Canuck, after I get down with you tonight in a "regular" match, I want this PRIMAL CAGE match to happen! Ya got the guts, Bird Man? (fade to black) -------------- CC: Well, Primal Fear has officially challenged the Crazy Canuck to a steel cage match! If that bout ever takes place, there could be some SERIOUS injuries. KW: Personally, I think it's a little TOO dangerous. I mean, somebody could get KILLED in a match like that! CC: I agree. Up next, let's watch this interview from The Twilight Strangler and Miss DeMeanor. These two have just been absolutely OBSESSED lately. KW: Roll it.... --------------- (We see Miss DeMeanor and the Twilight Strangler in the penitentiary. They just finished talking to the warden about the Strangler's brother, and they're all alone in the waiting room) Miss DeMeanor: Don't worry, babe! We'll get him out of there sooner or later, but for now, we have other things to worry about! Strangler: Oh yeah, some chump rookie sh*tface has challenged me! Does he have a deathwish? Miss DeMeanor: I don't know, babe! I think his mom must have smoked crack! Strangler: If that little punk wants to get into the ring with me, I am not responsible for what happens to him! Brian Rice is going to PAY! Miss DeMeanor: (smiles at the camera) Kid, buy some life insurance and make your family happy after you die! Strangler: Also, I have a message for the Machines. If those bastards mess around with Graphic Violence, maybe I'LL have to get involved. Miss DeMeanor: (laughs) We'll just have to wait and see. (fade to black) -------------- CC: Jeez, the Twilight Strangler has been a busy man lately. He really wants to get in the ring with Brian Rice now; that would be a GREAT match. KW: Yeah, but it wouldn't last long. The Strangler shouldn't worry about stupid rookies like Rice-- he needs to concentrate on titles! CC: But he doesn't DESERVE any titles shots yet. If he manages to get a victory over a popular new wrestler like Brian Rice, his career might get a jump-start. KW: And he WILL get a victory. CC: That remains to be seen. Up next, we have an interview with Booze Murphy and Leech Rogers-- better known as Graphic Violence. -------------- (The camera opens on Booze Murphy and Leech Rogers, leaving a rap concert. They both look very angry.) Booze: (singing) Yo, come into our world and you can see that we are more than thugs, more than thugs, more than thugs.......... Leech: Hey, I got a message for Simon O'Neal. I hear you're mad about your friend's car getting a little fire job! Booze: (yelling at the camera) Aw, poor little rich kid, can't your mommy or daddy buy you a new one? Leech: You little piss ant, you want to try and threaten us? Well come on, bitch! Let's see what you got! Booze: As far as I am concerned, you are no threat to us! Simon, you're just some little chump in a suit! Leech: Poor little church boy can't handle a joke! All we're saying is this-- we want a rematch. If we get one and you beat us, hell, we will buy you a NEW car. Booze: Well, actually, we'll STEAL you a new car. It's the same thing, though, so who gives a sh*t? Leech: What's the big deal, anyway? Paul Wong has insurance, so what the hell is he crying about? Anyway, we will WASH your car or something, but chump, don't make threats that you can't back up! Booze: And as a good friend once said, "You are all bluff and no stuff!" Leech: See ya in da hood, corn nuts! (fade to black) -------------- KW: Jeez, they just put some strange stipulations on that upcoming match with the Machines. Do you think they're serious? CC: Of course not, Ken. After burning that car, Booze Murphy and Leech Rogers should have been permanently banned from the AEWA. Unfortunately, they're still here, and that match will be happening sometime soon. KW: I can hardly wait. Those are two GREAT teams, and I'm excited to see what happens. CC: Let's just watch this interview with Randall Osbourne, the new TV champion. He recently defeated Deranged to win that title, so this should be interesting.... -------------- (The camera opens on Randall Osbourne, standing in front of a biker bar in Kansas City. He is holding the TV title, but doesn't seem especially proud of it.) Osbourne: This title is dirty. Of all the ways to win the TV title, this is not the way I wanted. I more than likely would have lost that match to Deranged but the Angels of Death interfered somewhat on my behalf. And that is what disgusts me. This TV belt is not really mine unless I can beat Deranged on my own, so I AM DEMANDING that I have a rematch against Deranged. The TV title, which is technically mine, will be on the line against the man that should still be champion. And we have this little situation with Shane Matthews. I would congratulate you on your victory, but I will not. You're nothing but a scum-sucking bottom feeder. Attacking me and other people will just not do. You had to handcuff me, you coward. DELIVERANCE is coming, my friend. After these wounds heal and after I take care of this whole Deranged situation, I'm coming after you. And if by some chance you haven't been stripped of that title, I'm going to take that belt from you and wipe that wretched smile off your face. It's time for you to become roadkill, Mathews. You're just another blurry object on the highway of life. I'll pass you by in an instant and you'll realize that its a long way to the top if you want to rock and roll. (fade to black) --------------- KW: That was rather strange, wasn't it? He just won the TV title after a hard-fought match, but he's not HAPPY? CC: He wouldn't have won it if the Angels of Death hadn't interfered, so he's not exactly proud. Randall Osbourne is obviously a respectable guy, and he wants to give Deranged another chance. KW: No, he's an IDIOT. CC: You never cease to amaze me, Ken. Up next, we have an exclusive interview with President Freeman! KW: (with little enthusiasm) Oh joy. ---------------- (The camera opens on President Bob Freeman, sitting alone in his office. He nods his head politely, smiles, and begins to speak.) Freeman: I just wanted to make a little announcement. After reviewing the tag team situation recently, I have decided to sign a special match. This week on Saturday Night Slam, the Graveyard Shift will be defending their World tag team titles against the West Coast Connection and Heaven & Hell in a triangle match! I've talked to the members of each team, and they have all agreed to that match tonight. Anyway, it should be a great bout, and I look forward to seeing everyone there! (He goes back to his paperwork, and we fade to black.) --------------- CC: Wow, President Freeman has scheduled another great match! It's nice to see that he truly cares about the fans. KW: Chuck, could you possibly kiss any MORE ass? Freeman is in this sport for the MONEY, and that little "interview" was really nothing more than a commercial. Gimme a break. CC: I don't want to argue with you, so let's just move on. Up next, we have an interview with newcomer "Classic" Andrew Archer! KW: Whatever.... -------------- (The camera opens on a huge field; we can see a tree in the distance. "Classic" Andrew Archer is leaning against the tree, relaxing in the shade. Hank Thompson suddenly comes into view, puffing and panting. It's obvious that he has been running, as he is covered in sweat.) Thompson: My God...."Classic".... You're a hard man to find.... Whew.... I had to hike for MILES just to get here.... Could I possibly do an interview with you? Archer: God, can't you EVER leave me alone? I'm trying to spend some peaceful time in my beautiful home county, before I have to go back to the overcrowded, polluted, hell-hole you call home. Thompson: I've been sent here by our network officials, to try and find out what's going on with you. You first appeared in September, made some noise, and disappeared. What's going on? Archer: Where have I been? I've been in the President's office nearly every day. He had me wrangled up in so much red tape, I looked like a Christmas present. The problems however have been sorted out, and I am ready to MAKE MY DEBUT! Thompson: Anybody in mind? Archer: Yes. The so-called Medieval Knight that's parading around the AEWA at the moment. I'm ashamed to call him English-- Sir Gaylord. Thompson: You mean Sir Gallade? Archer: (sarcastically) Of COURSE I mean Sir Gallade. Gallade, get yourself in the ring, and let me show you what it means to be Classic! Thompson: Any last words? Archer: Gallade, you will go down.... IN CLASSIC STYLE! (fade to black) -------------- CC: When he finally makes his AEWA debut, he's going to impress a lot of people. I personally don't like Archer, but he has looked VERY good in some other federations. KW: And now he wants a piece of Sir Gallade. Can you imagine what that match would be like? CC: It'd be a ROYAL ass kicking. KW: (looks shocked) Chuck? Did you just say the word "ass" on national TV? That's MY job, not yours! CC: (laughs) I'm a multidimensional man, Ken. Let's just move on to our next interview-- this one is from Paul Wong and Simon O'Neal, the Machines. KW: Here we go.... ------------- (Inside the Machines' gym, Paul Wong and Simon O'Neal are wrestling with sparring partners. Simon is in the ring with the larger of their opponents. He keeps taunting his opponent, slapping him across the face, pulling the ropes at every opportunity, and generally mocking his opponents. Finally he tags to Paul, who enters the ring and locks up with his opponent as Simon steps away from the ring and faces the camera.) Simon: Welcome to one of our workout sessions. We picked up these two scrubs from the gym and offered them some money to spar with us for an hour. Judging by the way they wrestle, they've never sparred before. And judging by the way they look, I'd say they never have been paid before.... (As Simon keeps cracking jokes about their opponents, Paul is growing increasingly more violent in his workout. When he started, he softened his slams and suplexes to not hurt his opponents. By this time, he is going full force, and locks one wrestler in a bear hug. He refuses to let go, even when the other wrestler tries to interfere. Finally, Simon notices the problem, and enters the ring. He manages to get Paul to drop his now-unconscious opponent, and walks over to a far corner, holding his head in his hands.) Simon: (to the other sparring wrestler) Look, take your partner and get out of here. We'll take care of payment later. (As the opponents leave, Simon turns to Paul). Paul, what the HELL happened? You could have killed him! Paul: Sorry.... I just lost it. I was just wrestling, and then I saw Graphic Violence in my mind.... and I just lost it.... (gets out of the ring, and walks away) Simon: Okay, I'm going to settle Paul down.... And Freeman, listen here-- We didn't have Graphic Violence arrested, because we knew you wanted the rematch. Don't give us any flack for what we do to them at the rematch. I don't think I can control Paul, and quite frankly, I don't think I want to. (shakes his head) I've never seen him this upset before.... (fade to black) ------------ CC: Jeez, Paul Wong is really starting to SCARE me. He's eventually going to permanently injure someone, so this situation needs to be stopped. Personally, I put all the blame on Graphic Violence. KW: WHAT? Paul Wong has always been the weak link of The Machines, and now we're seeing that he's also INSANE. His car got vandalized, and he's really overreacting. Simon needs to get him under control. CC: I disagree with most of what you said, but one thing's true-- Paul DOES need to calm down. KW: So what's next? CC: We have an interview with Big Ron Raider, the current North American champion. I might not like him, but he's been a DAMN good champion. Let's watch.... ------------- (The camera opens on Raider in the locker room, shortly after defeating Caretaker to retain the North American Title.) Raider: Another win for Raider! Is anyone surprised? Since my return to the AEWA, I've won this title and defended it successfully three times and yet, I get no invitation to participate in the Rumble for the World Title. What's up with that? Right now, I'm on the biggest roll here in the AEWA and anyone who steps into the ring can expect to lose to the MAN! President Freeman, I want a match with the new champion Shane Matthews. If you want to make it more interesting, put Randall Osbourne in the match and we'll have a three way dance with all of the belts on the line. If I pin Matthews, then I win the AEWA World Title, but if Matthews pins Osbourne he wins the TV Title. Thus, whoever gets pinned or submits loses their title. Notice I said pin or submit! That means No Disqualification and No Countout. Put it in a cage if you want.... I don't care. I want and deserve a chance at that World Title and you know it as well as I do. All of these other wrestlers are looking to have their 15 minutes of fame by challenging the title holders to matches. You idiots haven't proved anything yet. This is my fifth title here in the AEWA! Some of you think you can just come into the AEWA and get a title shot. YOU HAVE TO PROVE YOURSELF FIRST!!! I won a battle royal to obtain my first title and that was after two months of being in the AEWA. Like I said, sign the three-way dance for the next pay-per-view. Now, moving on.... Caretaker, you and your Knights of Deliverance couldn't deliver a pizza! You think your group of wimps can "clean up" the AEWA. Get some overalls and a mop! Like all of the other stables here in the AEWA, you aspire to imitate the Church. We are the strongest stable here in the AEWA and we will continue to dominate the AEWA! The Angels of Death can't kill an ant. They can't even get along with each other. The ICU? They talk big, but they haven't won anything. Matthews, Osbourne, prepare to feel the wrath of Raider! IT'S RAIDER TIME!! (fade to black) -------------- KW: Did you HEAR that challenge? What a good idea! Put all three heavyweight title holders in a triangle match, and let them battle it out! We've never had anything like that before, have we? CC: No, but I'm not sure it's such a good idea. We'll just have to wait and see how the other champions respond. KW: Of course. CC: Up next, we have a special interview with Justin Trouble. He recently competed in that battle royal, so this should be interesting. -------------- (The camera opens on a wet, shirtless Justin Trouble. He has a towel draped over his shoulder.) Trouble: What a night!!! In one given night, I took it to the AEWA's top superstars! I told my fans a week ago that I would be happy with the outcome of the battle royal as long as the ICU didn't walk away with the gold.... And they didn't.... Nor did I, but I'll get my chance at some later time. My fans know it; I know it! Still, I have current business to attend to. Namely, you, Deathwatch! I see you accepted my challenge for Saturday Night Slam. Well I'm warning you now, I WILL have a special guest at ringside to make sure none of your ICU boys interfere. Oh, by the way, Death-breath... I've got one more challenge for you. At the next pay-per-view, I challenge you and your crew to a tag team elimination match. We'll see who's the best then. But as I said before-- Saturday night, Death-Breath, the only ICU I'm gonna care about is the Intensive Care Unit that I'll put you in! See you Saturday! (fade to black) ------------ KW: Justin Trouble STILL wants a piece of Deathwatch? Is he STUPID or something? CC: Of course not, Ken. He just wants to make a name for himself in the AEWA, and there's only one way to do that-- challenge the stars. KW: Or pay off the Board Of Directors. CC: Uh, now we all know how YOU got this job. Anyway, let's watch this interview with Heavy Metal. ------------- (The camera opens on Heavy Metal standing in front of a black background. He has his dark shades on, a black leather glove on his right hand, black leather vest, black leather pants, and a black shirt. His long brown hair hangs to the sides of his head, and he cuffs his right fist in his left hand in anger. He begins to speak with his usual deep voice) Heavy Metal: More times than any, I've been screwed over in this federation. Well, not anymore. Ya see, there's gonna to be a new order around here, and that order will be ICU.... or it'll be nothin' at all. I'm going to make sure it happens that way. Scorpion, you've made one HELL of a mistake by cheating me out of the World title. And now you're gonna pay for it. I want you in the ring-- you and me-- in the ring, by ourselves.... and we're doin' it METAL STYLE! You might be askin' yourself, "What the hell is this 'Metal Style'?" Well, I'll tell ya since your so DENSE that you probably couldn't figure it out anyway. It's a steel cage match; not just any steel cage match, but a steel cage match with a roof over it. No escapin', no runnin', no nothin'. If you accept.... well, let's just say that you had better accept or bad things can happen to a little man named Scorpion. I don't put up with bullshit, and since that's exactly what you are, you can damn well be sure that I ain't gonna put up with you. You cost me the title, you made it so ICU didn't get back what is rightfully theirs, you made it so your life is about to end.... and it ends when you step into the ring. This big man doesn't like a lot of crap. Just leave me alone, and I usually won't kick your ass-- but you've already crossed that line, boy. You've made it so I gotta pound you into pulp and make an example outta ya. Well, I got no problem with doin' it. Meet me in the ring; Saturday Night Slam. Except the only thing that's gonna be slammed is your sorry ass all over the ring. I'm gonna put so much blood on that ring with your face that they won't be able to tell WHAT color it was before. Scorpion, see you in the ring.... little man. (fade to black) ----------- KW: Heavy Metal means BUSINESS now, so Scorpion had better start taking him seriously. If that match ever happens, bad things COULD happen. CC: Definitely. KW: Heavy Metal hasn't had much luck lately, but he's still only lost ONE match in his entire career. That's pretty good. CC: It sure is. (checks his watch) I hate to change the subject, but the show is about to end. KW: Really? Another week of freedom for me? CC: Of course. That's all the time we have for this week, fans! So for my broadcast partner, Ken Wilson, this is Chuck Carlson saying see ya next week for more Straight Talk! (The Straight Talk theme song plays as the image fades to black.) \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- QUOTE OF THE DAY: "Let's just take our time, Ken. I'm getting paid by the hour." --(Chuck Carlson, after Ken Wilson tried to hurry the show along.) American E-Wrestling Association The AEWA-- We Deliver!
