Saturday Night Slam (3/29, part two)

1 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * CONTINUED FROM PART ONE * * * * (Camera pans up to the aisleway to see what the crowd is popping about. The camera zooms closer on a figure walking down the aisleway, and it's revealed to be-- "Superman" Mike Stewart! By this time, Stewart has come into sight of all of the fans, and they are popping like crazy! Stewart is wearing blue jeans, a black sportsjacket, black cowboy boots, and a green tee-shirt. He slaps hands with the fans on the way to the ring. He then climbs the stairs and hops over the top rope, putting his arms in the air as the crowd pops even louder. He signals to the ring announcer, who hands him the microphone. The music dies and the crowd begins to quiet.) Stewart: Well, it seems like the fans of the AEWA haven't forgotten me! (Big crowd pop) Seriously though, I thank all of you for that tremendous ovation, and I'd also like to thank all of you for the cards and letters that were sent to me during my recent troubles. (Crowd pop) The reason I came out here tonight is to clear something up. A few weeks ago, I was diagnosed with a neck problem that would end my career if I continued to wrestle. I thought long and hard about the decision I made to retire, and now, I've found out a little something. And that is this -- I am all better! (Huge crowd pop) It seems like my neck injury wasn't really as bad as everyone thought, and now, "Superman" Mike Stewart is back in the AEWA! (The crowd pops thunderously, as Stewart stands back and smiles. The ovation lasts close to a minute, and Stewart is smiling the whole time.) Now, I know that may not make Deathwatch, the ICU, or any of the other scum here in the AEWA really happy, but to be perfectly honest, I don't give a damn about them! (Big crowd pop) Right now though, I've got one mission here in the AEWA, and that's to knock out this disgusting growth known as International Contracts Unlimited. So to do this, I'd like to bring out two men who will be fighting with me in my cause. Ladies and gentlemen, let me introduce Mark Washington and David Kennedy, better known as American Thunder! (The crowd gives a medium pop to the relatively unknown Washington and Kennedy, who slaps a couple of hands on the way to the ring. Both men are dressed in their ring outfits.) Stewart: Together with Mark and David, we plan on putting an end to the ICU, and to start off, we've got a little challenge. Tell 'em, David. (Stewart hands Kennedy the microphone.) Kennedy: The AEWA has a pay-per-view coming up called Extreme. We're gonna challenge The Visigothic Horde, The Shadow Warriors, and Graphic Violence to a four-way match, with the winner getting a shot at the AEWA World tag belts on the next regular card. (The crowd pops as Kennedy hands the mic to Washington.) Washington: We're getting a little tired of all of the teams in the AEWA who don't treat the fans with a little bit of respect. We think the fans deserve that, and we're gonna give them the AEWA World tag team champions they deserve! (The crowd pops again as Washington hands the mic to Stewart.) Stewart: I've also got a little challenge to make, and it has to do with the same fashion as Thunder's challenge. When I left the AEWA with my neck injury, I sent a man in here to take my place. Well, that man hasn't been properly received here by the AEWA brass, so right now I'd like to introduce Kyle Hatcher! (The crowd pops once again as Kyle Hatcher makes his way to the ring. He is wearing blue jeans, a white tee-shirt, black sportsjacket, and black cowboy boots. He hops over the top rope and walks over to shake hands with Washington, Kennedy, and finally Stewart.) Stewart: The challenge I'd like to make is that myself, Kyle, and The Battalion go at it in a four-way match with the winner to get a shot at the World heavyweight title! Now, I know Kyle wants the belt, and I know Joe Reed is a good champ, but this is a business, and that belt means a lot to me. I never really lost it, and I'd like to get a chance to regain it, but I'm willing to wrestle Kyle and The Battalion to get that chance. (Stewart hands the mic to Hatcher.) Hatcher: I've known Mike for a long time, and I know he'll fight for what's right in the AEWA. He'll go out and give it all he's got. When it comes right down to it, I feel I deserve a shot at a belt here too. I've been shafted since I got here, so I think that our challenge stands some ground. (Hatcher hands the mic back to Stewart.) Stewart: So to the rest of the AEWA, watch out because "Superman" Mike Stewart, American Thunder, and Kyle Hatcher are here, and we're gonna make some noise! ("Eye Of The Tiger" blares over the PA again as the crowd gives a big pop and all four men exit the ring to walk down the aisle to slap hands with the fans.) MW: Did you HEAR that, folks? Mike Stewart has finally returned! This is a great day for the AEWA, and personally, I couldn't be happier. BD: Shut the hell up, Wilson. That jerk has been on-and-off in this federation for MONTHS now, so why should we take him seriously this time? He's just gonna quit again, right? LB: I'd have to agree with you there, Big Daddy. Stewart's just a high-paid loser, and quite frankly, I wish he had just STAYED retired. MW: Listen, I'm good friends with this man, and I can tell you-- he's back for good. I could see it in his eyes. BD: Whatever you say, Wilson. Can we just get to the next match now? MW: Sure. It's now time for "Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi to square off against Sir Gallade; I've been waiting for this bout. BD: Sir Gallade is putting the Television title up for grabs against Joey Valachi.... LB: Sorry, Big Daddy. The TV Title is not on the line tonight. BD: Fine. Valachi's putting the AEWA Light Heavywei-- MW: Wrong again, Big Daddy. BD: So what title is up in this match? MW: Neither. Gallade wouldn't make it a title match unless the Church got some future Light Heavyweight title shots, and Valachi responded by.... well, his response can't be repeated on this program. So tonight is a non-title match. Let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following non-title match is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, weighing in at 202 pounds, he is the AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion of the World, "the Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi! (Pat Benatar's "Gettin' Paid" begins playing over the PA, and Joey Valachi makes his entrance, to a HUGE round of boos from the crowd. He's wearing his usual "AEWA Sucks" T-shirt, and appears to be in a VERY bad mood.) LB: He's upset that this isn't a title match. BD: Tough on him. I don't like anyone who thinks that the AEWA sucks. MW: For once, I agree with you. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Hackney, England, he weighs in at 251 pounds and is the reigning AEWA Television Champion, Sir Gaaaaaaaaaaalllllaaaaaaade! (The British National Anthem blares out over the PA as Gallade makes his way to the ring, to a BIG round of boos. He appears to be in a VERY bad mood, and waves his middle finger at someone in the front row.) LB: Apparently, neither man is happy about the situation. MW: It's funny, I think we are all rooting for Gallade. Whatever our differences with the man, he at least has shown some loyalty to the AEWA. But from the sounds of the fans, I think most of them would prefer that Valachi win. BD: That's because Valachi hasn't hurt their precious Randall Osbourne lately. Most of these idiots put Osbourne and his loser Knights of Deliverance friends on some sort of pedestal. MW: The bell rings, and Valachi bounces off the ropes, and nails Gallade with a dropkick! A quick cover.... One.. Kickout! MW: Valachi is going for the quick strike. He bounces off the ropes again, and hits Gallade with a leg whip. A third time off the ropes, he's going for a Thesz press.... but Gallade catches him with a Hot Shot! LB: Gallade needs to slow down the match NOW! He can't handle Valachi's speed. MW: He must have heard you, because he's picking up Valachi's leg, and kicking the back of the leg. That will strain the hamstring muscles, and make it difficult just to stand, let alone using aerial moves. He drag's Valachi over to the ropes, lays Valachi's leg over the ropes, and drops all of his weight onto it! Valachi's trying to call a time out, but he's not getting anywhere. BD: Sorry, Joey, not in the AEWA! MW: Sir Gallade picks up his legs, and turns him over for a half-boston crab. He's putting a lot of pressure onto Valachi's leg. The referee's asking for a submission, but he's getting a negative answer. LB: Valachi's is trying to grabs the ropes, but he's a little too far away. He's in trouble. BD: He's biting Sir Gallade's leg, forcing Gallade to let go! I guess that's one way to get out of the crab. MW: Gallade comes over to continue the assault, but Valachi nails him with a right fist. And another, and another! He hops up on the second turnbuckle, and hits Gallade with a dropkick! Gallade's on the mat, and Valachi makes a cover. One.. Two.. Thr.. Kickout! BD: Gallade kicks out. Valachi stands over him, slapping his face and taunting him. Gallade responds with a left fist of his own! He whips Valachi into the ropes, and catches him with a spinebuster slam! He makes a cover.... One.. Two.. Kickout! MW: Valachi with a kickout. Gallade picks him up again, and whips him into the ropes.... Valachi catches him with a flying bulldog! Valachi climbs the rope quickly-- he's going for an elbowdrop-- he misses! Another cover by Gallade.... One.. Two.. Kickout! BD: Gallade should have hit him with a move before covering. MW: Too late now. Gallade picks him up, and whips him into the ropes.... Sleeperhold! The referee checks, and it's a legal sleeper. Gallade is leaning all of his weight onto Valachi, making this a very difficult move for Valachi to get out of. LB: But he's trying to get up. I'll say this for the S.O.B. -- he's annoying, no one I know likes him, but he's a tough little guy. MW: He's trying to stand, to relieve the pressure.... he drops down, and hits Gallade with a jawbreaker! Gallade releases the hold. BD: The two men are standing up, grappling in the corner and trying to get a better position. MW: Gallade whips Valachi.... reversed.... AND GALLADE COLLIDED WITH THE REFEREE! And as Gallade bounces back, Valachi nails him with a superkick! He quickly scales the ropes.... A HEARTSTOPPER! He nailed his finishing hold on Gallade! But the referee's unconscious, and can't make the count. LB: And look who's entering the ring. MW: "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews! Valachi's old rival enteres the ring, and he nails Valachi with a loaded sock! Valachi has been knocked completely unconscious, folks. Matthews picks up Gallade, and puts Gallade over Valachi. Now he rolls outside the ring, and waits for the referee to recover. BD: Good.... it's nice to see the AEWA working together to teach Valachi a lesson. MW: Hold on.... something's going on in the audience.... It looks like a fan is attacking Matthews. LB: That's not a fan! THAT'S RANDALL OSBOURNE! And he's got something in his fist. MW: He's got a pair of brass knuckles, and he's just knocked out Shane Matthews! Now he jumps into the ring, and puts Valachi back on top of Gallade. The referee's finally waking up to make a cover.... One.. Two.. THREE!! (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here is the winner of the match.... "Cardiac Kid" Joey Valllaaaccchhhhiiii!!!!! (Boos from the audience.) MW: Well, the referee is holding his head-- I guess he hit it hard when Gallade collided with him, and now he raises Valachi's arm. Valachi is totally confused by what happened, but I think he's starting to get the idea. He rolls out, and grabs BOTH belts! BD: I think he still wants to claim the TV title. MW: But look who just grabbed the belt from behind Valachi-- Randall Osbourne! Valachi tries to make a grab for the belt, but I don't think he wants to tangle with the "Rebel of Rock" right now. LB: Valachi leaves with a few empty threats and the AEWA Light Heavyweight title. MW: Meanwhile, Osbourne is holding the AEWA TV title over his head, and has one foot on Gallade's prone body. I guess he's getting a small measure of revenge on Gallade. BD: The referee is ordering him to return the TV Title to Gallade.... MW: Which Randall is doing, although I don't think the referee was expecting him to throw it down so hard. LB: Nor was he expecting Osbourne to spit on Gallade! That's disgusting! He should be fined! MW: Well, Osbourne leaves to thunderous cheers, as Matthews and Gallade are finally beginning to stir. This war is not over by any means. BD: So what's next? MW: It's now time for our final match of the evening-- (notices something) Wait a minute, there appears to be some kind of a distraction in the audience. I'm not sure what's going on down there.... LB: It looks like somebody is climbing over the steel guardrail! We've had quite a few of these disturbances tonight, so I wouldn't be surprised if he wants the microphone. MW: And that's exactly what he wants, Bertha. I can't tell who this guy is yet, but maybe we're about to find out. * * * * TO BE CONTINUED IN PART THREE * * * * \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- "A New Beginning" American E-Wrestling Association The Official E-Wrestling Federation of Wrestle World and TNM The Best E-Wrestling Fed of the Year 1996 in the TNM Year-End Awards Be sure to visit our Event Center at: http://www.california.com/~bullet/index.html
