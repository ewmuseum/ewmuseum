AEWA — Card Archive

Kickoff '97 (2/23, part one)

26 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * AEWA KICKOFF '97: THE NEW BEGINNING * * * * (We see the AEWA logo on the screen, set on fire. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Rock music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... David Graves TAKING SOME GUY'S HEAD OFF with the Gravestone, Penance PLANTING some guy with the Final Judgment, The West Coast Connection DRIVING some guy's head into the mat with the West Coast Killa, Cardinal Chaos STICKING some poor soul with the Inverted Crucifix, Shane Matthews BRUTALLY hitting the End of the Road on some hapless jobber, and Heavy Metal notching yet ANOTHER casualty courtesy of the Megadeth Power Bomb. The camera then shifts to Matt Wilson, The Lovely Bertha, and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt in the PACKED MGM Grand Garden, the site of AEWA Kickoff '97!) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to the New Beginning of the AEWA! We have a capacity crowd of 15,000 here, and this place is about to EXPLODE! Ladies and gentlemen, we're about to witness the BIGGEST night in professional wrestling history.... BD: In the main event, Deranged will be defending his World Heavyweight title against "Crimson" Joe Reed! That's going to be an interesting bout, folks, and I can hardly wait. LB: And have you heard the news? There's a rumor going around right now-- President Freeman might not even HAVE a team for his match! That could dramatically affect the future of this federation, so we'll try to get an update on the situation. MW: But before we get to that, it's time for our first match of the pay-per-view! Graphic Violence are about to put their National tag team titles on the line, when they step into the ring against the Assassins! BD: Ya know, this is a VERY historic night for the AEWA, so it's really only appropriate that the Assassins be here, right? MW: Without a doubt, Big Daddy. Perhaps the most unlikely World Champs this side of Organized Crime, they burst on the scene a year ago or so, and within three weeks were wearing the world title belts. A few weeks later, they appeared to be gone for good, resurfacing only in recent months. A win over the Shadow Warriors a few weeks ago now puts them in position to make history, as only the second team to win both the National and World Titles. LB: Actually, they're in position to lose in matches for both titles. These two can't go blow-for-blow with Graphic Violence. BD: I'd have to agree with you there, Bertha. Anyway, let's go down to ringside for the official introductions. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team match is scheduled for one fall, and is for the AEWA National tag team titles! Introducing first, the challengers, Shockwave and Banshee, the ASSASSINS! (Shockwave and Banshee make their entrance, to a HUGE pop from the capacity crowd. They bow their heads in respect, enter the ring, and begin to do some pre-match stretches.) MW: These guys are great role models, folks. I wish there were more teams around here with the respectability of the Assassins. BD: Are you KIDDING me, Wilson? They're just babyfaces-- and don't we have ENOUGH of those around here? MW: Well, that's questionable. Graphic Violence, on the other hand, are exactly what we need LESS of. They personify everything that is wrong with our society, as far as I'm concerned. LB: Speaking of which, here they are, Booze Murphy and Leech Rogers, the National champs! RING ANNOUNCER: And their opponents, weighing in at 533 pounds, being led to the ring by Miss DeMeanor, here are the National Tag Team Champions, GRAPHIC VIOLENCE! ("Here Comes the Highstepper" begins to play over the PA, as Leech Rogers and Booze Murphy make their way to ringside. Miss DeMeanor follows closely behind, clutching her ever-present purse.) BD: And remember, they already hold one clean pinfall win over the so-called "World Champions", the Machines. MW: Well, that is true, and we'll be seeing them later this evening, but first it is Graphic Violence and the Assassins. Leech Rogers is starting off against the big 350-pound Shockwave, and Leech charges right at him-- thrust kick by Shockwave! LB: Leech staggers back, and Shockwave with an open-hand chop on the head, and down goes Leech! Shockwave with an Irish whip, and a big elbow smash knocks Leech to the floor. And he grabs the belts, AND HE'S LEAVING! Booze follows him up the aisle.... BD: Sound thinking. If it isn't working tonight, take it out of here and deal with it later. LB: If at first you don't succeed, forget about it. MW: Inspiring words from you both. The referee is up to the six count, and they turn around and come back. I guess they've decided to stick it out after all. Leech is back in the ring, and Shockwave goes to lock up, but Leech backs up to the Graphic Violence corner. BD: He's motioning that he wants Banshee in the ring now. Not a bad idea; Shockwave has manhandled him so far, where as Banshee is at the decided size disadvantage. Shockwave looks to his partner, then to the crowd-- LB: What, does he now have 15,000 managers? BD: --and tags in Banshee. Banshee and Leech lock up, and there's a knee lift by Leech. Now he grabs him in an armbar, but Banshee catches him in a drop toe hold. And he follows up with a jumping elbow drop! MW: He whips Leech into the ropes, spin kick to the head, and Leech drops to the mat again. Leech is back on his feet-- flying head scissors by Banshee-- he spins, and.... WOW! Booze Murphy just reached up and pulled him down over the top rope in a modified Hot Shot! LB: That was INCREDIBLE! Now Banshee is on the mat in obvious pain, as Leech tags in. It's party time, folks.... MW: Booze Murphy is putting the boot to Banshee in the GV corner, and now he kneels down to choke the man out! Blatant illegal tactics, right in front of the referee. BD: As long as he breaks by the five-count, it's all legal. LB: Do you suppose you could use a flame thrower, as long as you switched it off on the five-count? MW: Let's nip this one in the bud; bring in a flame thrower and there will be serious legal ramifications. LB: This league just isn't fun anymore. MW: Booze tags out again, and holds Banshee in an armbar, and Leech with a kick in the exposed ribs. Now Leech catches him with an irish whip, AND THERE'S A SPINEBUSTER SLAM! He goes for a cover.... One.. Kickout! LB: This is an amateur rules match, right? Shoulders on the mat for one count and it's over? MW: "Amateur rules"? But you wanted them to bring in a flame-thrower! BD: Let me take this one for ya. You said they couldn't, right? MW: Right. BD: And you can't use one in an amateur match, right? MW: Right. BD: So if you can't use one in an amateur match, and you can't use one here, than this is an amateur match. MW: Let me illustrate the fallacy-- LB: I don't want to see your fallacy. MW: His fallacy-- LB: I don't want to see that, either. MW: His logical fallacy! By your reasoning, the following would hold true-- a cat has four legs, my dog has four legs, therefore my dog is a cat. BD: Your dog is a cat? MW: No, it is NOT. LB: You just said it was. MW: Using his logic! BD: Just call the match, Williams. MW: For the last, time, it is "Wilson", not "Williams"! LB: I thought that was his dog's name. BD: No, no.... His dog is a cat, remember. LB: Oh, yeah. MW: (rolls his eyes) I'm WAY too confused. Anyway, let's just get back to the match. Booze Murphy picks Banshee up for a vertical suplex, and he holds him up there for an extended period to let the blood rush to his head. BD: He's wearing a mask, how could you tell? LB: No, no. I think he means it is running to Leech's head. MW: Running to-- Leech? I thought Booze was in the ring. BD: He tagged out while you were boring us with stories about your dog. MW: I don't even have a dog. LB: Then why did you tell us your dog was a cat? MW: It was a hypothetical dog. LB: What's that, like some exotic breed? MW: Will you PLEASE shut up? Leech rams Banshee's head into the top turnbuckle, with PLENTY of force. Cross corner whip, and Leech meets Banshee with an elbow smash! Leech tags in Booze, and now it's Booze with a front facelock on Banshee. BD: This match has been rather fast-paced, but Graphic Violence seem to be slowing it down. They want to keep their advantage. LB: Good observation, Big Daddy. Banshee is trying to drive Booze back to the Assassins' corner.... Leech is in there now, and the referee is going to put a stop to it. MW: Banshee makes the tag, and in comes Shockwave with a kick in the back-- but the ref is sending him right back out again! BD: If he doesn't see the tag, it didn't happen. LB: So if he doesn't see the flame-thrower-- MW: Would you please shut up about the flame-thrower?! Banshee has to stay in the ring now, with Leech Rogers. Leech locks him in an abdominal stretch, and he's really applying the pressure. BD: He has that leg grapevined, which just ADDS to the pressure. MW: Would you care to draw on your years of in-ring experience and explain how? BD: Um.... well, I heard Gorilla Monsoon say that it does. LB: You believed him? MW: Leech still has the abdominal stretch hooked on.... and he reaches back and grasps Booze's hand for additional leverage! Shockwave steps in, and the referee cuts him off. BD: If he doesn't knock it off, he'll get disqualified and blow his chances. Shockwave has REALLY been bending the rules. MW: But he's trying to COUNTER the illegal tactics of Graphic Violence, Big Daddy. BD: Read the fine print-- the champions are always allowed to break a few more rules than the challenger. MW: Where did you get that from? BD: My vast experience. MW: More like your vast posterior! LB: Good one, Williams. MW: It's "Wilson". BD: Williams is the half-dog, half-hypothetical cat, remember? LB: Oh, yeah. MW: Do you two EVER stop? Now Leech really has that abdominal stretch cinched in, and the referee checks for a submissions-- he caught them! He sees Leech and Booze linking hands for leverage, and he kicks to break their hold! BD: The ref shouldn't be getting physically involved. Leech and Booze are livid now, and they turn their attention to the referee-- as Banshee tags out! LB: But Leech shoves the official! And he shoves right back! Leech is knocked backwards-- AND SHOCKWAVE CATCHES HIM WITH A KICK TO THE HEAD! Leech goes down! This isn't fair.... MW: It's totally legal, Bertha. BD: Banshee is brawling with Booze Murphy right now, so there's NO ONE to save Leech! Shockwave goes to the top rope-- AND NAILS LEECH ROGERS WITH A FLYING MOONSAULT! Here's the cover! One.. Two.. Three!!!! (DING, DING) MW: They did it! They did it! The Assassins, former AEWA World Champions, are now the National Champions, as well! BD: That was the most biased officiating that I've EVER seen, folks. The stupid referee should NEVER have gotten involved-- he ruined the whole damn match! RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here are your winners, and NEW AEWA National tag team champions, the ASSASSINS! MW: The Assassins take their new belts back to the locker room, and the referee leaves, and Graphic Violence are left alone in the ring, watching their title reign come to an ignominious close right here at Kickoff '97! This is great.... BD: You've really got a big mouth, Wilson. As far as I'm concered, that referee should be FIRED because of this match. LB: I agree. MW: Quite frankly, I really don't care WHAT you think. That was a great match, and the Assassins are going to make excellent National champions. Anyway, it's now time for another edition of the Terrordome. Johnny is about to interview the entire ICU, so this should be VERY interesting. BD: I'm a little bit worried about this, to tell you the truth. Remember the last time Deathwatch was a guest on the Terrordome? He set the AEWA logo on fire, right? MW: Yes.... it was a HORRIBLE incident. BD: Well, things have changed a lot since then, and Deathwatch is even MORE powerful now. If things get out of control, he could literally destroy the entire arena. MW: I sure hope not. Well, I can hear the music playing, so it's time for Johnny Terror to take over the show for a little bit. Let's watch.... (Public Enemy's "Welcome to the Terrordome" plays loudly throughout the arena as the camera shifts to a familiar set near the back of the arena. Hanging over this set is a large half-dome. Underneath the dome, there is a very dark, dank room FILLED with various gruesome looking torture devices. AEWA manager Johnny Terror emerges from behind the curtain, and begins to make his way toward the set as he is booed tremendously. Johnny simply smiles ear-to-ear at this reception, and continues on his way. He gets to the set, and picks up a microphone.) Terror: Hello peons, nice to see ya again. I just love the feeling of how angry you guys get each and every time I come out here. It gives me goosebumps. Now on to business, this is the Terrordome, by the way; if you didn't already know that, seek mental help. And I am the greatest talk show host since Geraldo, the one and only Johnny Terror! (LOUD BOOS!) Thanks again. Now it's time to introduce my guests for this week. They are an organization that has been wreaking havoc for MONTHS in the AEWA. In their time together, they have captured the AEWA World Heavyweight, and Light Heavyweight titles, and have also managed to attack the President, and just about everyone else in the league, for that matter. And so without further ado, here they are, Penance, Heavy Metal, Exodus, Legacy, and their CEO, Deathwatch. These are the men collectively known as International Contracts Unlimited, or ICU!!!! ("Master of Puppets" by Metallica begins to blare throughout the arena as Heavy Metal, Exodus, Penance, and Legacy all walk out to a HUGE round of boos from the crowd. Deathwatch leads the way with a sinister smile on his face that shows he enjoys the crowd's reaction. They all wear their usual attire. Exodus carries his "Exodus Championship Title" over his right shoulder and his UEWA Heavyweight Title over his left. Heavy Metal smacks fists with the few people that actually cheer him on. As Deathwatch steps into the Terrordome, he grabs a microphone and begins to speak.) Deathwatch: Ah, great to be here again, Mr. Terror. Don't you just love the reaction you get from the crowd every time? I know I do. (grins) Terror: Yep, nothing warms my heart more than hearing that downpour of hatred every time I walk out here. It just makes life worthwhile, you know what I mean? Well anyway, why don't we get started with this here interview. I guess my first question would have to be regarding the members of your corporation. Now I know that there are a lot of individuals here, with individual goals that often conflict with one another. How does that affect the goals you wish to achieve as a group? Anybody wanna start? (Heavy Metal steps forward and grabs the microphone from Deathwatch, who steps aside with a mere grin on his face as usual. Metal looks down to Terror and smiles casually.) Heavy Metal: You know, one of the main reasons I joined up with ICU was because they treated me as an equal. It wasn't just another "join up and get ordered around" kinda thing. We all do our separate things aside of the corporation, but we work as a single mind.... linked in the sense that everything we do, we can count on the fact that everyone else in ICU is watching. Now to answer your question, dude, our goals are personal, nothing more. We don't take personal matters into the group. If we do, when it means it's serious and needs the full attention. (Heavy Metal hands Deathwatch the microphone again.) Deathwatch: I couldn't have said it better myself. I'll give you an example here as well-- say one of us wants a title shot.... easier done than said. But whoever gets first shot, gets first shot-- plain and simple. We aren't going to hold off just because one wants a title shot .... the only way we're going to succeed is to work as a team, and we can't have titles causing conflicts within the corporation, there just isn't room for it. Terror: Well you fellows certainly seem to have it together on that issue, but I guess the real test won't come until your goals DO conflict, but ICU's a strong company. I think it can survive such a test. Since you've stated that you DO all have individual goals, what are they? Just wondering what each of you has planned for your near future here in the AEWA. Deathwatch, you're obviously after Justin Trouble, but what about everyone else? (Exodus steps forward and nods to the rest of the members of ICU. He takes the microphone and begins to speak) Exodus: I'll start out by saying.... I don't really have any goals, so to speak, I'm mainly here to take away people's hopes of becoming a new champion or even becoming a new hot rookie by giving them hell in their first match. I already hold two titles, one of which is the Exodus Championship Title; I don't need anything else to prove that I'm the best there is to offer. With the exception of my comrades, that is. (Heavy Metal takes his stand in front of Exodus, who hands him the microphone. They look at each other at an almost eye-to-eye level, and then Exodus grins and steps back.) Heavy Metal: I've already accomplished most of what I want to do here in AEWA. I've held the World title, I've kicked everyone's asses, and I've proven to all the doubters that you just can't kill rock n' roll.... or in my case-- heavy metal. (chuckles) The only thing I have left to do is to keep showing everyone just how it feels to get.... ROCKED! (Heavy Metal hands Penance the microphone and steps out of the way, while patting him on the back. Penance, who is completely decked out in black, takes the mic in his hand and then begins to speak.) Penance: Mr. Terror, my goals are quite simple actually. But rather than babble about them like some kind of idiot, I'll show you what my goals are.... but not yet. Ask me again at the end of this interview. (grins) (Deathwatch takes the microphone and holds up his palm to Penance, who backs off as the rest have.) Deathwatch: Now allow me to correct something really quick. You said that I am "after Justin Trouble", which is not entirely true. Trouble is after ME, and I am just taking my stand to show him that no matter how hard he tries, and no matter how bad he wants to.... he can't win. It's just not going to happen. Anyway, continue. Terror: But you sure wouldn't mind kicking the crap out of him and taking his title tonight, now would ya? Just the fact of taking something away from him that he holds so dear has got do be a WONDERFUL feeling, don't ya think? And ya get to beat up that moron, Leroy Brown, as an added bonus. It's like a dream come true. Deathwatch: Oh, of course. I can't wait to get my hands on him, it's just that titles aren't everything in the world. He will lose the title and just about everything that he holds dear, but I plan on taking something else away from him as well-- not titles, not pride, but walking ability. After I show Trouble exactly what ICU does to no-name punks like him.... he won't be so cocky, this I guarantee. (Deathwatch pauses for a brief moment, and then resumes.) Deathwatch: You see, and I have said this before, Trouble is the kind of kid that doesn't give up without a fight. If it is a fight that he wishes, it's a fight that I shall grant him. I love it when people ask to be injured, it makes it all the easier. Terror: It sure does. And now you've all gone over your personal goals, well, all of you except Legacy, obviously. But anyway, now let's talk about where you're headed as a group. First off the bat, are there any plans to expand ICU or add new members anytime in the near future, or are you happy with your current size? Deathwatch: I'm so glad you asked that, because at this time I would like to introduce you all to the newest member of International Contracts Unlimited. You all know him well, but you don't know him like this. Here is.... SEWERDOG! (Sewerdog comes out to a huge round of boos, wearing a black jacket instead of his usual brown one. He turns around and shows off "ICU" written on it and then walks up onto the set. He smacks hands with everyone, but when he gets to Deathwatch, he goes to give him a high-five, and Deathwatch just stands there. Finally, Deathwatch extends a hand and they both shake hands. Sewerdog turns to Heavy Metal, who has his arms folded, and they both give each other a glare-- and then they grin as they both high five each other.) Sewerdog: It took me a while to realize it, but this is the dominant force in the AEWA. I know that me and the rest of the boys have had our disputes, but hey, that just knocked some sense into me. I realize that if I want to get anywhere, then these are the boys that are going to help me. And now under the tutelage of four of the greatest wrestlers in the AEWA, it's time for nothing more than a bloodthirsty rampage. Terror: Well this is certainly an interesting turn of events. Just a few months ago, Sewerdog, you were right here on this show demanding to face Heavy Metal again, and now you're JOINING him? I guess ya gotta do what ya gotta do to get places in this business, huh buddy? (Sewerdog simply nods in response.) Terror: I understand, it was the same deal when Scorpion came to me. Unless you belong to one of these big groups, nobody takes you seriously. (Johnny looks off camera as if someone called him.) Okay now, the producer is telling me that we're out of time-- like there are actually other things on this pay-per-view that are more important than MY show, yeah right. Anyway, Penance, you told me to ask you again about your goals at the end of the interview.... well this looks like it, pal. (Penance grins widely at this and then steps off of the stage. He stands at the foot of the set, then raises one of his hands to about face level, and grins widely again. He snaps his fingers and then three other men join at his side. He grabs the microphone and begins to talk into it.) Penance: HA HA HA!! It's sure great to see the look on everyone's faces right now, especially YOURS, Deathwatch, sir. HA HA HA!! I would like to introduce the WORLD to its apocalypse! To my right are two men who are extremely talented, extremely dark, and most importantly.... SATANIC AS HELL! (The camera shows two of the men, who are wearing black robes with hoods that rest behind their heads. They stand at about the same height, they have long black hair, and dark eyes that could penetrate any man's soul.) Penance: These men's names are Nightshade and Pestilence; I'm SURE you all remember Pestilence though, don't you? To my left is a good friend of mine, and a man that can make the lights go out ANYTIME.... I'd like to introduce you all to Blackout! (The camera shows the man to his left, who is grinning a dark, evil grin all his own. He wears a long brown trenchcoat, black shirt, black boots, brown pants, and a black glove on his right hand. He has long white hair that is braided in the front corners, and clipped together with small metal clips. He grins, showing off his sharp, canine teeth.) Penance: And together we wreak havoc.... together we cause anguish.... together we are the only force that you will be seeing in your future.... because the Apocalypse has come, oh yes, it has. It has come for everyone here, and our primary target is to take out the main threat-- to take out ICU. We've been here before, haven't we, Deathbreath? I assure you, you aren't going into that match tonight a whole man! WE ARE THE FOUR HORSEMEN OF THE APOCALYPSE!! (Nightshade and Pestilence tear off their robes, and Blackout and Penance do the same with their trenchcoats, and they all charge the stage. Blackout sinks his teeth into Sewerdog's wrist, and then is knocked off by Heavy Metal. Nightshade and Pestilence double team Legacy and Exodus, as Penance charges straight for Deathwatch. Penance crushes Deathwatch into the cage of the Terrordome, and then Deathwatch throws him to the ground, but Penance only gets back up and grabs a metal object from the floor. He NAILS him upside the head with it, but Heavy Metal and Sewerdog clothesline Penance down. Legacy and Exodus continue to brawl it out with Nightshade and Pestilence, as Blackout jumps onto Heavy Metal from behind and claws him in the neck with his fingernails. Deathwatch gets back up and grabs Pestilence by the head, ramming him face-first into the cage. Nightshade kicks Sewerdog in the head with a dropkick, and then Deathwatch signals for everyone to flee-- and they all retreat from the Horsemen. Blackout grabs the microphone off the ground and begins to speak.) Blackout: Oh, this isn't over by far. We will be victorious as we were here tonight, and this is only the first step. (Blackout hands Penance the microphone.) Penance: As for this precious interview.... it's over! (Pestilence takes the mic and grins.) Pestilence: AEWA, say hello.... (Nightshade is handed the microphone.) Nightshade: TO YOUR APOCALYPSE! (After grabbing their trenchcoats and robes, all four men leave the area, leaving Terror shocked. Johnny's jaw hangs open for a few seconds, and then he realizes he's still on the air.) Terror: Woah! That was the coolest thing I've ever seen! And it happened on MY show, now THAT'S entertainment. Beat that, Letterman! Anyway, since all my guests, and their attackers, for that matter, are gone, I guess that'll be it for this week. Tune in next time when my guest will be The Gryphon. Until then, see ya, morons! (Johnny drops the microphone, and begins to walk off. He stops to check out the damage done to his cage, and smiles at the sight of blood on it. He then proceeds to walk back to the locker room area. The camera returns to our broadcast team.) MW: That was INCREDIBLE! Not only has Penance quit ICU, he also just introduced the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse! I've seen those guys from their days in WWEW, and believe me, they mean BUSINESS. BD: After what they just did to the ICU, I don't doubt it. I've never seen Deathwatch run from anyone, but he was HORRIFIED of those guys! And he still has a match for the North American title tonight! LB: Good point, Big Daddy. After that brutal attack, I'm not sure if he'll even be physically able to compete. MW: Well, I'm looking forward to that match, so I HOPE he will. Anyway, we're ready for the next match, as Primal Fear is about to square off against "the Shotgun" Stan Crawford. * * * * TO BE CONTINUED IN PART TWO * * * * \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- "A New Beginning" American E-Wrestling Association The Official E-Wrestling Federation of Wrestle World and TNM The Best E-Wrestling Fed of the Year 1996 in the TNM Year-End Awards Be sure to visit our Event Center at: http://www.california.com/~bullet/index.html
