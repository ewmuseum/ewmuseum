Sunday, June 8th, Card, (1/3)

8 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last Week on Championship Wrestling Association of America Max Robertson: I'm tell you if you'll SHUT UP! During last week, I thought, and I thought - how can we decide this? After all, I only have the needs of the federation at heart (he sneers), and then it came to me. We're going to have a little fun. Two matches. The first, a tag-team match - these guys are all tag-team partners, so that shouldn't be a problem - but, instead of their normal partners, everyone is going to switch: Sheridan and Hammer on one side, and Thomas and Barnes on the other. Then, after Sheridan and Hammer beat Thomas and Barnes - no disrespect of Thomas intended, but any team with that other idiot is bound to lose - then Sheridan and Hammer wrestle, and the winner has the right to get beat up by me during a CWAA Heavyweight title match. David Myers: Now wait a minute, what makes you think these people will accept... MR: And if they refuse my terms, I'm going to give the next title shot to the Masked Man, and they'll have to wait another four weeks for another chance. ********************************************************************** Eric Lane: Hello, everyone, and welcome to another exciting week of CWAA action, here in the world-famous Sportatorium in the heart of beautiful downtown Dallas, Texas. As you just saw, CWAA Heavyweight Champion Max "the Professional" Robertson has announced that he will defend his title only against the winner of a tag team match which would see enemies aligned against friends. By the end of tonight, we hope to hear from the parties involved as to whether or not they'll accept this extraordinary challenge. In the meantime, the CWAA and the AEWA continue to insist that Robertson must face Marc Thomas, and that the match must take place right here, next week, for the CWAA Title. We are just seven short days away from that confrontation, and Robertson adamantly insists that if he doesn't get his way, he'll be here all right- to take on the mysterious Masked Man who has terrorized the CWAA for this past month. But that's next week- this week, have we got a full slate of action for you! Tag teams galore, as Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas tangle with the Trailer Park Assassins- Cooter and Orbit back here in the world-famous Sportatorium- always a favorite venue of theirs- as they tour the country. Double Jeopardy is here and have they got their hands full with Joshua Barnes and "the Dreamer" Randy Sheradin! Little Yvette gets perhaps the chance of a lifetime, a golden opportunity, as he gets a non-title match with the CWAA Champion himself. And two big title matches on deck, as well. Lt. Lawson, fresh off that impressive win over Paul Langford, goes one more time with "the Preacher" Kore Wrench. And in our special feature match, we have the CWAA Tag Team Champions, Twister, taking on their hated rivals, "Marvelous" Michael Anderson and "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey- inside a steel cage! All this, and some big announcements about the upcoming King of E-Wrestling Tournament, the next big AEWA Pay Per View extravaganza, Battle Klash II, and, of course, the situation surrounding the CWAA. Stay tuned, because when we get back, it'll be Little Yvette and the Heavyweight Champion! (Monday nights at 11:00 PM, right here on WYXZ-TV Ch. 39, it's more of those zany adventures with Jack, Janet, Chrissy and the whole gang on "Three's Company." Then, starting this Monday, stick around for another half-hour with Stanley and Helen- "Three's Company" and "The Ropers"- back to back, Monday nights on WYXZ-TV Ch. 39!) EL: OK- we've got a real barnburner coming up for you as we kick things off with the CWAA Heavyweight Champion in non-title action against Little Yvette! But first, let's get a quick word from the Trailer Park Assassins, taped earlier this evening. [The Trailer Park Assassins are backstage in the locker room. Cooter is holding a microphone, while Orbit looks determined for a moment, before his natural tendency takes over, and he gives a stupid grin and a wave.] Cooter: Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas. You can't spend too much time in the CWAA without hearing about them. You got a big tough guy, and a little guy with a lot of wrestling moves. That's a good combination, don't you think, Orbit? [The 245-pound Orbit looks up at his partner, bigger by 5 inches and 105 pounds, and nods.] Cooter: But you see, Thomas, that part of your brain that holds wrestling moves? That part of Orbit's brain is full of wrestling moves too. And that part of your brain that knows not to stick a penny into a light socket to see if the electricity's on? That part of Orbit's brain is full of wrestling moves. [It is at this point we notice that Orbit's beard stubble is a little singed.] Cooter: And Hammer? I'm bigger than you, I'm tougher than you, and you haven't had a hit since "You Can't Touch This," and I hated that record. So it looks like we match up pretty well. We're gonna have fun tonight. Tell 'em Orbit. [Orbit smiles and spits, before waving to the camera.] EL: Cooter and Orbit tackling the duo of Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas- you're not going to want to miss that. But first, we've got Little Yvette and the Professional. Let's go to David Myers for the introductions. David Myers: Ladies and gentlemen, this first contest is set for one-fall with a fifteen minute time limit. Introducing first, from Seattle, Washington- (Soundgarden's "My Wave" plays to an enthusiastic response) DM: -weighing in at 250 lb., here is Little Yvette! (Little Yvette, standing 5'10" and a muscular 250 lb., heads to the ring in his blue singlet decorated with an eagle in flight and his name.) DM: And his opponent- (The familiar chords of the Imperial March are greeted with the usual boos and jeers) DM: -at 200 lb., from San Francisco, California, he is the CWAA Heavyweight Champion, "The Professional" Max Robertson! Your referee, Jeff Howard. (Max is, as always, sporting a cleanly-shaved head and black and red tights.) EL: OK- non-title action here, but what an opportunity for Little Yvette. Max with a single leg takedown, stomping away now at the midsection as he holds Yvette by the ankles. Max going right to the attack on this one. Now, a legdrop right between the spread legs. Max stands him up- snap mare takeover. It looks as though the champ is in the best shape of his life, taking the action right to Little Yvette, perhaps fired up by all the commotion he's caused this week with all of the top contenders and his threat to face the Masked Man next week instead of Marc Thomas. Max to a chinlock from behind. No offense yet from Little Yvette. (Max hounds the rookie, urging Jeff Howard to "Ask him! Ask him!") EL: The CWAA and its governing body, the AEWA, weighed all the pros and cons and declared Marc Thomas, a very dangerous foe, the number-one contender, and the Randy Sheradins and Joshua Barnes and Lt. Lawsons all were given serious consideration. But Max insists that it is his choice to make, and of the top 4 contenders don't wrestler each other under his terms- get this, Sheradin and Hammer against the team of Barnes and Thomas- he's going to leave signed contract in the CWAA offices for the mysterious Masked Man to sign- and he isn't even a member of this organization! Little Yvette with a snap mare takeover now- but Max back up with a European uppercut. To the ropes- leapfrog by Yvette- (Big pop) EL: Yvette from behind with a back suplex! And Max wants to take a timeout on the floor- and here comes Little Yvette after him. And the champ greets him with a poke right in the eyes. And- ooh! He just smacked the rookie's head right into that steel ring post. Now a smash on the ring apron. Little Yvette climbs back in, Max right after him. We'll see both Hammer and Thomas and Sheradin and Barnes in tag team matches later this evening and see what they have to say about Max's challenge. The title match, one way or another, takes place right here next week- you won't want to miss that. Max sends him for the ride- flying hangman! Dropped him neck-first right on that top rope. Little Yvette has been in trouble right from the get-go in this one. Setting him for a legsweep- no! Legsweep faceslam, and Max just drilled him right into the mat with that one. Very crisp execution tonight by the champ. Later tonight, we've also got the TV and tag champs in action-Max stomping away- as Lt. Lawson meets the Preacher, Kore Wrench, once more, and then the duo of Anderson and McNultey get locked in a steel cage with Twister. Max to a Boston crab now, tremendous pressure to the legs and back area. Senior official Jeff Howard in there checking for the submission. ("Go, Yvette, Go!") EL: The crowd urging Little Yvette to hang in there, and he's reaching for the bottom rope to break this hold. Max again barking at Jeff Howard to ask Yvette if he gives up yet. Little Yvette has absorbed a real beating so far. (Minor pop) EL: Little Yvette gets his hand on that rope, Jeff Howard calls for the break. Very unlike the champ to have not taken his man to the middle of the ring for that maneuver. Perhaps Max was not looking for the win just yet, but was merely softening him up. Sends him for the ride- clothesline. Cover... 1... 2... Yvette kicks out. Back into the ropes- the champ with a well-placed dropkick. Right under the chin with that one. We can be sure that Marc Thomas, Randy Sheradin, and all of the top contenders are keeping a close eye on this one. Max whips him to the ropes again- drop- no! (Pop) EL: Max misses the dropkick- and Yvette pounces- 1... kickout. Little Yvette with a near fall on the Heavyweight champ- had he scored it he would have been hearing from promoters all over the world. back up- Max to a hiptoss- Yvette catches him in a backslide again- 1... 2... oh! He almost had him there! So close, so close. And we must be close to the time limit in this one- Yvette with a dropkick- what elevation. Picks him up- atomic knee drop. Lateral press- 1... kickout again. Little Yvette coming on strong now- forearm uppercut stuns the champ. Setting him up- suplex perhaps- front layout suplex! Dropped him right on the face! Little Yvette signaling that he's going to fly- going up top- waiting- (Anticipatory pop) EL: -Max on his feet, but a little woozy- Yvette nails him! Clothesline from the top rope- cover him! Cover him! 1... 2.... no! Oh- he was too close to the edge of the ring- the champ gets his foot in the ropes just in the nick of time. Yvette hooks him around the waist- Northern Li- Max grabs a hold of the top rope- falls on top of Yvette- he has him by the tights! 1... 2... 3! No! (Heel pop) EL: Max Robertson, the Heavyweight Champion, got all he wanted and then some out of Little Yvette, and still needed to use the tights to get a non-title win. Let's get the official word... (...as Max grabs his belt and legs it for the locker area while Yvette protests to Jeff Howard) DM: Here is your winner, the CWAA Heavyweight Champion, Max "the Professional" Robertson! (The crowd registers its disapproval of the decision) EL: Well, Little Yvette took a licking and kept on ticking, and really had the champ on the run in those closing minutes. I'm sure we'll see him in there against CWAA champions again in the near future. All right, when we get back, we'll see the team of Randy Sheradin and Joshua Barnes against Double Jeopardy. Stay tuned! (Coming soon, available only on Pay Per View, AEWA Battle Klash II: Justin Trouble faces Superman Mike Stewart in Trouble's retirement match. Heavy Metal, Sir Gallade, Leroy Brown, Joe Reed and all the top AEWA stars making history one more time- contact your local cable operator and tell them you want AEWA Battle Klash II- only on pay per view) EL: OK- we're back, and we've got some big tag team action coming up next. Let's head right to the ring. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next bout is set for one fall with a fifteen-minute time limit. Introducing first- ("Sweet Home Alabama" plays and the crowd boos) DM: -weighing in at an even 500 lb., Eddie Intellecta, "Hotshot" Gary Jackson- the team of Double Jeopardy! (Jackson is again dressed in old blue jeans and a beer-stained undershirt on his 5'11"-250 lb., body, wearing the ankle brace yet again. Intellecta is 6'7"-250 lb. and wears his blue trunks and trademark blue mask.) DM: And their opponents- (Frenzied pop as "Dream On" by Aerosmith and the Philadelphia Philharmonic plays) DM: -weighing in at 462 lb., former CWAA Heavyweight Champion "the Dreamer" Randy Sheradin and Joshua Barnes! (Sheradin stands a mere 5'8"-170 lb. in his long purple tights which bear his nickname. Barnes is 6'5"-292 and dresses in long black tights. As he hands both his wire-rimed glasses and his emerald green jacket to the ring attendants, an "IU" tattoo is revealed on his right arm) DM: Your refer- (DM bails out of the ring) EL: Look out! Double Jeopardy attacking as Sheradin and Barnes hand over their jackets, jumping them from behind before the bell rings. Stomping and kicking away, and now Eddie Intellecta tosses the Dreamer out to the floor, and he hits the floor hard. Double team action on Joshua Barnes now. They send him for the ride- double clothesline. (As Randy Sheradin gets back on his feet and heads for the ring apron, Intellecta kicks him in the head) EL: Now they set Barnes up- Chris Herman yet to get any kind of control in this one. Double suplex. They send him for the ride- (Big pop) EL: -and Joshua Barnes with a clothesline for both men! Both halves of Double Jeopardy just steamrolled by the Indiana native on that one! Now he headbutts the Hotshot- spins around and pops Intellecta! Down in a three-point stance- tackles Jackson. Back down- across the ring- nails Intellecta. Learned that one watching the Hoosiers football squad back home in Indiana- Randy Sheradin back in the ring now- big dropkick on Eddie Intellecta- and Barnes with a spinebuster slam on the Hotshot! 1... 2... 3! It's over! (Pop) EL: Joshua Barnes is one man you do not want riled up. As gentle a man as you'll find out of the ring, but in the past few times we've seen him he has grown more and more- well, almost vicious- when angered. Let's get the official word. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winners, the team of Randy Sheradin and Joshua Barnes! (Pop as "Dream On" plays) EL: OK- right after- wait a second! (Sheradin asks for the house mike- Barnes, already on the floor, turns to listen to his partner)
