CWAA Supershow pt 9

3 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EL: OK- we are back and ready for the big Television title match, as the war between Joshua Barnes and Max Robertson just refuses to die down. For over half a year now these two have fought tooth and nail, and Max Robertson stepped things up last week with his antics with that joy buzzer and by taking away the TV belt. Tonight, they do battle once more, here in Reunion Arena. Let's go to Ring Announcer David Myers for the official introductions. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is set for one fall and a twenty-minute time limit and is for the CWAA Television Championship! (pop) DM: Introducing first, from Brownsburg, Indiana- (pop as "Tom Sawyer" by Rush plays and Joshua Barnes emerges from the locker area for the fifth time in the past two days) DM: -weighing in at 292 pounds, here is the former CWAA Television champion and current co-holder of the CWAA Six Man Tag Team championship, Joshua Barnes! And his opponent- (boos as Darth Vader's "Imperial March" plays and the Lawson-Sherrick team with Major Mayhem join the TV champ) DM: -from San Francisco, California, at 200 pounds, he is the former CWAA Heavyweight Champion and current CWAA Television Champion, Max "the Professional" Robertson! Your referee, Chris Herman. EL: OK- Max Robertson has brought along some reinforcements for this one, and he's taking his sweet old time getting into that ring, as he hands the TV title belt over to the ring attendants. OK, Chris Herman telling the champ to get a move on. OK- Max finally on the ring steps, and Barnes lunges for him- Max arguing that the challenger has to let him enter the ring unmolested. (the crowd continues to buzz even though nothing has happened, the champion is still o the ring steps, and referee Chris Herman is trying to stand between the two combatants) EL: All right, looks like we're finally about to get underway- Joshua Barnes and Max Robertson. OK, the champ still stalling, and now Chris Herman orders Joshua Barnes to the other side of the ring to allow Max a free path into the ring. All right, here we go. Max in the- look out! (Barnes dashes back across the ring again to smack a double ax handle on Max as he comes through the ropes) EL: Joshua Barnes with a kneelift now- sends him for the ride- big clothesline! (pop) EL: Now Barnes with a snap mare takedown- off the ropes- elbow drop. Joshua Barnes just a house on fire in this one so far, and Max Robertson heads for the floor and a quick timeout. Really needs to regroup as he has had no chance to get any kind of offense started. Max Robertson a master of stalling tactics- but Barnes won't wait! (pop as Barnes follows him to the floor and chases him around the ring, shoving Sherrick to the floor as he tries to run interference) EL: Max dives back into the ring, and stomps away as the challenger follows. Really putting the boots to the big rookie. And now he's using that bottom rope to choke him! Right in front of referee Chris Herman who issues a stern warning. Robertson now to a hammerlock down on the mat. Leaning forward so as to press Barnes' face down into the mat, and see the ACWF tag champs getting right up in his face, pounding the canvas, trying to rattle the youngster. It'll take all of his concentration to get free from this hold, and to have this kind of racquet going on right in front of him has to be a distraction. Max a master manipulator, using every physical and psychological weapon he can muster. Has really seemed to play his opponents like an instrument at times. Joshua a real powerful presence in the ring, but lacks a certain savvy, and Robertson has plagued him for months by playing games like this, using hit and run strikes, working on Barnes emotionally as much as physically, trying to rattle him up, trying to get him out of his game plan. Still putting on the pressure with that hammerlock. We all thought Barnes really had him once or twice- when Barnes does get his mitts on the Professional, it's never pretty, and Max has taken his lumps, but right now, Max has been able to really rile the kid up and then use his anger against him. (Sherrick and Lawson exchange words with Chris Herman over their antics at ringside) EL: All right, Robertson spins around and moves into a chinlock. His strategy pretty clear- keeping the bigger man on the mat and wearing down. Pulling back on his neck- now he hauls off and unloads a right hand right between Barnes' eyes! Pulls him up- sends him for the ride- stomach claw! Max just clamps a handful of the challenger's midsection, and you can see the agony in Barnes' face. A tight, viselike grip on that midsection, and Joshua Barnes in trouble here now. ("Go, Joshua, Go!", with Sherrick hollering at the crowd to shut up) EL: And Sgt. Sherrick not happy about the reaction of the great Reunion Arena fans, cheering on one of their favorites. Joshua Barnes a very popular figure here in the CWAA, but Max Robertson has taken the match to him in these last few minutes. Joshua trying to rally his strength- and he claps two forearms around Max's head! (pop) EL: Now a big right hand! Now a headbutt, and Joshua Barnes is on the warpath! Irish whip- back body dr- leapfrog by Max- and he catches the Indiana native in a Russian legsweep! And now he rolls him over on the mat again- back to that hammerlock. A flurry of offense by Barnes, and Max right back to basic mat moves, and back in control of this pivotal TV title match, in front of a record crowd for the CWAA. The standing room only signs were out, and we have at least 4,000 people on hand here this weekend to see Championship Wrestling, and they're being treated to some of that right now as Max Robertson tangles with Joshua Barnes. Barnes back up on his feet now, and- ooh! (Max drapes Barnes' arm over the top rope and tugs the writ between the top two ropes) EL: Snaps that arm- now he runs Barnes' face along that top rope- and that burns like all heck. Sends him for the ride, and- (pop) EL: Joshua Barnes with the lariat! Flattens the champion! Cover- 1... 2... no! Almost had him there. One tough son of a gun, that Max Robertson. Scoop sl- and the champ with a small package- 2.... kickout. Near falls there for both competitors, and this one is just starting to heat up. Max hip tosses hi over- off the ropes- and he catches the ex-bouncer with a superkick! Joshua Barnes takes a nasty spill on the arena floor- and keep an eye on Sherrick and Lawson. Those two are usually up to no good- Max to the floor now, and just pounds Barnes' head into the ring apron. Later on tonight we have that World Title bout still to come- 2 of 3 falls. Right now Max pins Joshua's hand behind his back- and rams him shoulderfirst right into that ring post- that's solid iron, folks- it has absolutely no give. Softening him up for the double chickenwing submission hold, perhaps. Max Robertson grabs a chair now, and Chris Herman getting on hi case about this. Lays Barnes' arm out on the ring apron- and smashes down with that chair! Risking an immediate disqualification and the short end of the purse on that one. Chris Herman taking a firm stance on this, but he knows that a disqualification here gives Max the result he wants, lets him keep that title belt. Max rolls into the ring- and back out- just in long enough to break the count, I suppose. And he hooks the double chickenwing! (screams for Barnes to recover) EL: Joshua Barnes in a whole pile of trouble, Max has his patented double chickenwing submission hold on him out on the arena floor- Chris Herman counting both men out- and Max rolls back in the ring again, and right back out- broke that count at about 9 1/2. Rolls Barnes back in, and this match has gone Max Robertson's way almost from the get-go. Puts the boot to him- sends him for the ride- what a maneuver! Max Robertson, just such a skilled technician- Northern Lights suplex- just suplexed the man onto his head, and this has to be it. No cover- Max measures him up- drops a knee right across the forehead. Stands him up again- and back to the double chickenwing- Max trying to end this one right here. The key to his hold is that he gets the man off of the mat, so his arms are immobilized and he can't use his legs- dangling in mid-air. Joshua Barnes kicking, trying to get free, trying to reach the ropes with his feet, and Max holding him up in that chickenwing hold. The Major and his team telling Barnes to give up. And look at this! (pop as Barnes pushes off the middle turnbuckle with his feet so that he and Max fall backward, Barnes still trapped, but on top of Max who has his back to the mat) EL: 1... 2... kickout! And Max had to release the hold to get free and save his title. Max with a slap in the face now- frustration setting in. And Barnes with a right! (pop) EL: And a left! (pop) EL: Sends him for the ride- big clothesline! (pop) EL: Joshua Barnes back in the saddle! Max almost flipped over that top rope, and now he's all tied up- caught between the two ropes! Chris Herman trying to get him free, Barnes off the ropes- running punch to the head! (pop) EL: And another big right hand! Joshua Barnes just pounding away as Max Robertson is stuck in those ropes. Chris Herman trying to pull Barnes back as he goes to work on Max, who's just trapped- and Joshua Barnes just nailed Chris Herman! He hits the referee and goes right back to work on Max! This is going to be trouble! Watch out! (Lawson and Sherrick scramble into the ring as the bell rings and Barnes continues to hammer Max) EL: Lawson and Sherrick to the aid of their partner, taking him down from behind. (pop) EL: And here comes help! (The Preacher and the Dreamer, AKA Kore Wrench and Randy Sheradin,. sprint to ringside) EL: All right- Barnes and Sherrick trading blows- Lawson trying to hold Barnes back for his ally- and Sheradin and Wrench explode into the ring! (Lawson and Sherrick scatter, and Max, whom the Major set free, rolls to the floor and grabs his title before staggering away) EL: Joshua Barnes just went berserk and punched Chris Herman- there's going to have be some kind of discipline- laying hands on an official just cannot be condoned. (Sheradin and Wrench hold their partner back as he lunges to follow the exiting foes) EL: OK- let's get the word from David Myers. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner as the result of a disqualification, and still CWAA Television Champion, Max "the Professional" Robertson! (boos) EL: Well, not a popular decision, but the only one that could be made. Joshua Barnes was just out of control, and there is just no way to justify hitting an official like that. OK- we'll be right back with the AEWA World Heavyweight Championship match- 2 of 3 falls, Joe Reed and Marc Thomas! Stay tuned!
