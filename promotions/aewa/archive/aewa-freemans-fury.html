Freeman's Fury?

9 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~ Here is the transcript of the interview that Hank Thompson conducted with President Freeman on AEWA Saturday Night Slam. ~~ (The camera switches to Hank Thompson, standing with President Freeman in the ring. He is holding a microphone, as usual, and begins the interview.) Thompson: Hello everyone, I'm here with President Freeman, and I have some very important questions to ask him. (turns to Freeman) Sir, I wanted to ask you about the current situation with Justin Trouble. You recently fired him, however, many people thought you were too harsh about it. Any thoughts on the matter? Freeman: I really don't understand what all the controversy is about. Justin was hired to work as my bodyguard, but he never even showed up when I was getting attacked by the Visigothic Horde. Naturally, like any reasonable employer, I fired him. What's wrong with that, Hank? Thompson: Well, according to Justin, it wasn't his fault. He claims that Kevin Larkin locked him in a room, so he COULDN'T have been there. He even has the padlock to prove it! Freeman: Frankly, I don't give a damn what he says. The point is, he wasn't doing his job, and now he's making lame excuses about it. He can't even take his punishment like a man, I guess. Thompson: I've become good friends with Justin over the past few months, sir, and you're being way too hard on him. I really wish you'd stop. Freeman: When I want your opinion, Hank, I'll ASK for it. This thing is between me and Justin, so please-- mind your own business. Thompson: (looks slightly hurt) I'm sorry for offending you. Anyway, as you may have heard, he recently offered to join your tag team at Kickoff '97, with some special stipulations. What's your answer? Freeman: When I first heard him say that, I thought it was a joke. After all he's done wrong, he wants me to give him ANOTHER job? (shakes his head) Not a chance in hell, buddy. Justin Trouble will never work for the AEWA again. Thompson: I would comment on what you just said, but I'm afraid of losing MY job. You sure have developed an attitude lately, sir. Freeman: Hank, I do NOT need to listen to your criticism. I've just been under a lot of stress lately, because of all this stuff with Kevin. (puts his hand on Hank's shoulder) I apologize for being so rude. Thompson: It's okay, Bob, I understand. Since you mentioned him, though, I was wondering if I could get your comments on Kevin Larkin. Have you picked your team for Kickoff '97 yet? Freeman: Yes, I have. I'm not going to reveal WHICH team, however, because I want to maintain an element of surprise. Thompson: Can't you tell us ANYTHING about the team? Freeman: No, I can't. Thompson: Well, I really don't have any other questions for you, so unless you have any other comments-- Freeman: Yes, I want to say one other thing. (turns to the camera) Kevin Larkin, it's almost time for your judgment day. After Kickoff '97, there will only be ONE law in the AEWA-- me. Bring your damn Visigothic Horde, it doesn't matter, because my team WILL emerge victorious. I'm ready.... are you? Thompson: I'm sure he is, sir. Thank you for your time, and folks, be sure to order Kickoff '97 on pay-per-view! (Both men leave the ring, and the camera returns to our broadcast team.)
