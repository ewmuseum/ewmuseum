AEWA — Rules & Regulations

Sunday, June 22nd, Card (1/3)

22 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last Week on Championship Wrestling Association of America Eric Lane: Rolls him up- no referee- 1... 2... 3... 4... 5... still no referee- here comes Jak Hammer to the ring as the Masked Man kicks out of the small package. The Masked Man sees Jak coming- everyone in the world-famous Sportatorium has wanted to see this man get his comeuppance- in the ring now. Jak Hammer standing toe to toe with the Masked Man- grabs him by the throat- and kicks back at the champ! Jak Hammer has just kicked Max Robertson! ********************************************************************** EL: Hello, everyone, and welcome to another exciting week of Championship Wrestling, here in the world-famous Sportatorium in beautiful downtown Dallas, Texas. As you just saw, last week Max Robertson defended his CWAA Heavyweight Title against the mysterious Masked Man, and Jak Hammer interjected himself into the action. Now among the many top stars here in the CWAA whom the Masked Man has provoked has been Marc Thomas, Jak Hammer's primary ally here in the CWAA. Is Jak Hammer with Marc Thomas? Is he with the Masked Man? Or is he playing his own mind games, if you will, with the top contenders? I've been speculating for weeks now about the Masked Man and how he came to arrive in the CWAA- the one man he has not involved himself with has been Lt. Lawson, so I speculated that the Masked Man may be an operative of the TV Champ and the Major- but now he appears to be aligned with Jak Hammer- perhaps Lawson's main threat. It just gets more complicated every week! In the meantime, Jak Hammer has just helped the Masked Man to defeat the CWAA Heavyweight Champion for the title belt. Let's hear some pre-taped words, recorded earlier today, from the Masked Man. ********************************************************************** (MM stands in front of the camera with the CWAA Championship belt over his right shoulder.) MM: As everyone can see, I am the CWAA Champion. No one in this fed or anywhere else believed that I could become a champion. Well, what do you idiots think now? Not only did I win the belt, but I beat one of the best wrestlers in the world to do it. Robertson, you were a good champion, but your best day can hardly compare to my worst day. I am sure that everyone here in the CWAA wants to know who I am and what my purpose is. You can call me Mr. X or whatever, but you've got to call me "the champion." Well, I have already achieved one of my goals by winning the CWAA title but I have other plans which I choose not to disclose at this time. As far as Robertson goes, you can get in line behind Marc Thomas and all the rest of the CWAA wannabes. Tonight, the CWAA officials say I have to face Jimmy Dangerous or what ever his name is. The only thing he is a danger to is himself! He couldn't hurt a fly. He got lucky a few weeks ago in his debut, but luck will play no part in our match tonight. Hammer, I don't know why you interfered in the match last week, but stay out of my way or I will drop your (bleep) like everyone else! *************************************************************************** EL: Well, since then- wait a second. Here come Marc Thomas and Jak Hammer-what do they want? (Thomas and Hammer approach Lane) EL: You men have an important match tonight against...... MT: Look, I don't care who we're wrestling tonight....I've got to talk to Jak about what happened last week. Now, as everyone knows, I was in Canada helping my friend Ron Stern with a match. I taped the show and I could not believe what I saw. You (pointing at Jak) came down to the ring and helped that Masked Man or Mr. X or whatever win the belt from Robertson. Don't me wrong, Robertson is no friend of mine, but if he had kept the belt then I would have been in line for a title shot. What were..... JH: I want to get back at that Masked Man for what he has done to you, but I also what is due me.... and that is a shot at the CWAA Title. There is no one else I would rather beat for that title than the Masked Man.... EL: Well, if you two would let me- MT: So you think you have a better chance at beating him than I do? JH: As a matter of fact.... EL: Like I'm trying- MT: (Standing toe to toe with the taller Hammer)If you want to be able to walk out of here you won't answer that question. I'll tell you what, if you beat Lawson in that steel cage match next week, I'll let you have my title shot. Not only do you win the TV title, but you get a shot at the CWAA Championship. What do you think about that? EL: Listen, guys- JH: You got a deal...but I don't want this situation to effect our match tonight. MT: We're both professionals, Jak......I can handle it if you can. (MT walks off with Jak eventually following behind him.) EL: Well, if those guys would have listened to me for just a few seconds, well, earlier today, the AEWA Commissioner, Bob Freeman issued this statement. (EL produces a paper from an envelope in his jacket inside-pocket) EL: "The AEWA maintains a high standard for the defense of its championships, and demands full compliance with the rules and regulations surrounding the defense of AEWA-sanctioned championships in AEWA-sanctioned matches. The AEWA as a very clearly-defined set of rules and regulations surrounding the defense of the CWAA Heavyweight Championship. At the conclusion of each calendar month, the AEWA announces a date and challenger for a mandatory CWAA Heavyweight Championship defense. Any additional CWAA Heavyweight Championship contests are at the incumbent champion's discretion, but are subject to the rules and regulations regarding the AEWA's sanctioning procedures. The AEWA had announced, in accordance with its policies, that on Sunday, June 15th, 1997, Max Robertson would face Marc Thomas for the CWAA Heavyweight Championship. Max Robertson chose not to face Marc Thomas, and Marc Thomas failed to appear on hearing that Max Robertson would not meet him. A substitute opponent was procured without sufficient notice by the AEWA, and his agreement to participate in the match was not arranged within the bounds of the AEWA's policies. Hence, the AEWA takes the following actions, effective immediately: 1) the unnamed opponent who defeated Max Robertson on June 15th was not recognized by the AEWA as a challenger for the CWAA Heavyweight Title, and was therefore ineligible to obtain the CWAA Heavyweight Championship 2) the contract signed by the unnamed opponent who defeated Max Robertson on June 15th did not meet the standards of the AEWA. The opponent did not sign his own name, and therefore the contract was invalid, and therefore the match was not sanctioned by the AEWA and the subsequent title change is null and void 3) Max Robertson failed to meet the requirements of the AEWA's champions by failing to defend the title against the officially-designated challenger named by the AEWA, and therefore has disqualified himself as CWAA Heavyweight Champion 4) Marc Thomas has failed to fulfill his duties as AEWA-designated challenger for the CWAA Heavyweight Championship, and therefore has disqualified himself as CWAA Heavyweight Champion. In summary: The match between the so-called Masked Man and Max Robertson on June 15th, 1997, was not officially sanctioned by the AEWA, and the CWAA Heavyweight Title did not change hands. Max Robertson has forfeited his rights as CWAA Heavyweight Champion. Marc Thomas does not win the forfeited title, as he has forfeited his rights as number one contender. Therefore, the CWAA Heavyweight Championship has been declared vacant, and a procedure for declaring a new CWAA Heavyweight Champion will be forthcoming." (EL returns the envelope to his jacket pocket) EL: So we now have no CWAA Heavyweight Champion, and the Masked Man, who is set to participate in our first contest here tonight, and Max Robertson, who will be on hand later this evening, are being informed of the decision in the locker areas now. When we get back from this message, we'll see the Masked Man in action against Jimmy Dangerous! Don't touch that dial! (Coming this week, available only on pay-per-view: AEWA Battle Klash II! Heavy Metal, Joe Reed, Randy Acorn, Justin Trouble and all the top stars of the Major League of Professional Wrestling, the AEWA, all in one arena on one night: Battle Klash II: Order now!) EL: OK- let's get right to it- David Myers with the ring introductions. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next bout is set for one fall with a 10-minute time limit. Introducing first, from Edmonton, Alberta- ("Danger Zone" by Kenny Loggins plays, and the crowd pops) DM: -weighing 215 lb., here is Jimmy Dangerous! (Jimmy wears a white tank style top with the words "About to enter the Danger Zone" across the front in blue lettering. He wears regular white tights with "JD is OK" down the side in blue. Jimmy does a quick walk around the ring to shake a few hands, sign a few autographs, and to consider getting a shorter name or to start just initialing rather than signing, before hopping into the ring) DM: And his opponent- ("Siris" by the Alan Parsons Project, best known for being played every Sunday on NBC during the introductions of the Chicago Bulls, plays) DM: -from parts unknown, weight unknown, the Masked Man! (The booing starts as the almost-6'-tall 240-250 pounder dressed entirely in black charges to the ring, and the CWAA Heavyweight Title belt is nowhere to be found) DM: Your referee, Jeff Howard. EL: OK- the man who thought he was the new champ- look out! The Masked Man wastes no time- a kick for Jimmy Dangerous. Sends him for the ride- drives a vicious knee into the midsection, doubling the youngster over. Now he starts choking him- blatantly breaking the rules! It looks like the announcement made by Bob Freeman has really gotten under the Masked Man's skin. Now he picks Jimmy up- the kid doesn't even know what's hit him yet- full nelson. A full nelson into a suplex- dragon suplex- and the Masked Man taking out all of his frustration on the kid from Edmonton. The Masked Man off the ropes- elbow drop. Back up- back down again- another elbow drop. All Masked Man early on. ("Go, Jimmy, Go!") EL: Now the Masked Man sends him for the ride- into the turnbuckles. Jimmy Dangerous hit the corner hard- hasn't gotten any offense- he bounces out- right into an elbow smash from the Masked Man. Now the Masked Man sets him up- ddt! This one's over. Covers... 1... 2... hey! (Boos) EL: The Masked Man just pulled him up by the hair- he had the man pinned, but wants to beat on him some more! Sends him for the ride- chop across the chest. Very unprofessional attitude displayed by this individual, whoever he is. Sends him to the ropes again- back body- (Pop) EL: No! Sunset flip- Jimmy Dangerous takes him over- 1... kickout. The kid caught him napping. And the Masked Man with a big right hand right under the chin. Now he grabs Jimmy Dangerous- tosses him out to that concrete floor! And Jimmy Dangerous hits the floor hard. (Boos) EL: Now the Masked Man coming out after him- this could be bad. Grabs the Canadian rookie- and smashes his head right onto the ring apron. Really rang his bell with that one. Spins him around- another chop right across the chest. Now he whips- reversed! (Pop) EL: Jimmy Dangerous just whipped him right into that ring post! The Masked Man ran right into the post! Kick to the midsection by Jimmy- and Jimmy Dangerous scoops him up- body slam , out on the concrete floor! (Frenetic pop) EL: And Jimmy Dangerous in under the bottom rope to beat the count- soaking in the reaction from the great fans in the world-famous Sportatorium- they've really taken to this youngster. The Masked Man back in there now, too. Jimmy Dangerous with a dropkick. Sends the Masked Man for the ride- big clothesline. Jimmy Dangerous has turned the tables on the Masked Man. Now Dangerous with a Monkey flip- and another! The Masked Man has no idea which end is up, as Jimmy Dangerous is tossing him all around the ring. Dangerous now with the whip- back body dr- no! The Masked Man with a ddt! (The crowd groans) EL: Hooks the leg- 1... 2... 3. He got it. Didn't let him up this time, as the Masked Man gets the win. Let's get the official word from David Myers. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, the mysterious Masked Man! ("Siris" plays again, and Jimmy shakes the cobwebs loose as the Masked Man climbs out of the ring) EL: OK, when we get back- wait a second. (The Masked Man throws a chair into the ring and starts shouting at Eric Lane, presumably about the Freeman dictate- notice how wrestlers always try too blame the announcers when these things happen?) EL: All right, don't get me involved in this. I have nothing to do with any of it. You take it up with the AEWA and the CWAA. OK, looks like he's going. Like I was saying, when we get back from this break, we have a big rematch from last week, as the Preacher tries to get his revenge against Mark Sherrick! And- look out! (Robertson and Lawson race down the aisleway toward the Masked Man) EL: The TV Champ and Max after the Masked Man! They've got him cornered against the ring- and Lawson with a kick. Now they grab the Masked Man- smack his right arm and shoulder against the ring post! And again! He drops to the arena floor- Max and Lawson stomping him on the floor! (Frenzied pop) EL: The fans have waited for something like this for some time, now. Jimmy Dangerous still in the ring- Lawson and Max working the Masked Man over. OK- Lawson into the ring now- he's grabbed that chair- and look out! Jimmy Dangerous with a thrust kick on Lawson- he must have thought the Lt. was after him! Lawson drops to the mat- he didn't see that coming! (Big pop) EL: Max tugging on that mask now. The locker are clearing- officials and security breaking this melee up. The Masked Man trying to crawl to safety under that ring- Max still going for the mask. All Jimmy Dangerous sees is Max Robertson on top of someone and security trying to pull Max off- Jimmy Dangerous trying to pry the Professional away- pulls him up- right hand to the jaw by Jimmy! He grabs a chair now and stands over the security guards and the Masked Man, holding Max and Lawson at bay. Max and Lawson backing up now- the Major out here now pointing a threatening finger at Jimmy- we have to take a break now- we'll be right back. (Silvertone Records presents the music event of the 70s- Engelbert Humperdink performs all your favorite hits of the 70s: "The Night Chicago Died", "Convoy", "Seasons in the Sun", "Rock the Boat", "Rubberband Man", "Afternoon Delight"- all your favorites! Order now, and get, absolutely free, a special recording of Tom Jones Goes Metal: "We're Not Gonna Take It", "Cum' On Feel the Noize", "Round and Round", "Shout at the Devil" and many more! Act now, while supplies last!) EL: OK- last week we saw Mark Sherrick, well, cheat his way to an ill-gotten win over the Preacher, Kore Wrench. Well, Kore has seen the tape of what happened, and went to the CWAA offices and demanded a rematch, and, in a few minutes, he'll get it. But just to let you know what has gone on here- Jimmy Dangerous used that chair to chase off Lawson and Max- he had no idea what was really going on, though- didn't realize that the guy he saw on the floor was that Masked Man. Jimmy still a little foggy in the head, I think, after his matchup. Anyway, as security was trying to keep between Jimmy and the duo of Robertson and Lawson, the Masked Man bided his time, and when Max and Lawson were gone, he thanked Jimmy Dangerous with a knee in the back, then banged Jimmy's head right into that ring post and left him lying in the floor. How's that for gratitude! Let's go to David Myers. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next bout is set for one fall with a fifteen-minute time limit. Introducing first, from New York, New York- (Gregorian Chant plays and the crowd pops) DM: -weighing in at 275 lb., he is "the Preacher" Kore Wrench! (the long-haired Preacher foregoes the blessing ritual to head straight for David Myers and his house mike) Kore Wrench: Mark Sherrick, you made a very serious mistake last week: you made me angry. Your little fits have been tolerated in this league for several months, but now, you've gotten a little out of hand. Normally, I would be too busy to deal with the likes of you; my war with Thomas and Hammer, Lawson and Mayhem require much of my effort. But this week, those enemies are fighting each other. This week, I have the luxury of vengeance. Prepare yourself, Sherrick. Prepare yourself for the Wrath of the Preacher. (The crowd pops. Myers and Wrench wait a moment for them to quiet down.) DM: Are you planing on avoiding any confrontations with your other enemies today? KW: No. I will avoid nothing. If any of them cross that line, I'll be there. You can count on that. Somehow, though, I think they'll all be too busy fighting amongst themselves to cause anyone else much trouble. EL: OK- strong words from the Preacher... DM: And his opponent- (Marilyn Manson's "Beautiful People" plays as the crowd boos) DM: -from Dallas, Texas, weighing 275 lb., Mark Sherrick! Your referee, Chris Herman. (The mohawked Sherrick's tights are a black and purple version of the Bret Hart tights, with his initials on the back of the tank top part. He also wears a black sleeveless trenchcoat on his 6'4"-275 lb. body) EL: The Preacher not even letting Sherrick into the ring- greets him on the apron with a big right hand! Sherrick answers back. Trading blows over the ropes now, Wrench in the ring, Sherrick out- and Sherrick with a knee in the midsection. Now- what's he doing? He has the Preacher doubled over- setting him up- he wants to suplex him all the way to the floor- you could cripple a man doing that! He has him up- no- he can't get him- the Preacher reaches over- scoops him- powerslams him into the ring! (Huge pop) EL: He powerslammed the man over the top rope into the ring- 300 lb.! 1.... 2... no! Sherrick kicked out. (to be continued)
