Sunday, May 4th, 1997, Card (2/3)

4 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Be sure to catch the encore performance of AEWA Extreme: The Pain Game, Tuesday at 8 and again at 11 on ppv: Crimson Joe Reed, Heavy metal, the Assassins, the Machines- all the top stars in the AEWA made history- and you can see them make it again, Tuesday night on the encore presentation of AEWA Extreme: The Pain Game.) EL: OK- we're back and Jak Hammer is finally back up on the ring apron- something seems wrong, and I just figured out what it is- Jak Hammer normally would be out conferring with Marc Thomas at a time like this, but Thomas is nowhere to be seen tonight, as Jak Hammer, flying solo as it were, out there against Kore Wrench. OK- the Preacher has done all the waiting he's going to do- shoulderblock on Jak Hammer while Hammer is still on the apron. Now he hooks him- he's going to bring him in the ring the hard way- scoops him up- carried him into the ring- powerslam by Wrench! But he doesn't go for the cover- instead slips a side headlock on the big Dallas native. Kore Wrench applying the pressure to Hammer's head. Both men are going to need to be careful and pace themselves, since Lt. Lawson awaits the winner later tonight, and Lawson didn't get the Television Title without being a skilled grappler. Watch it- Jak Hammer rolls back, he's got the Preacher's shoulders down- 1... Wrench rolls forward again, still has that headlock on again. The Preacher's got those huge biceps, Hammer must feel like someone has stuck his head in a vice. Hammer rolls him back again- 1... kickout again. Wrench still has his arm around Hammer's head, and Jak Hammer is obviously not going to beat him this way. Jak Hammer in obvious pain- now he works his way back to his knee- up on his feet- pushes Wrench to the ropes- Irish whip- shoulderblock by the 310-lb. Hammer! Hammer off the ropes- steamrolls the Preacher with another big shoulderblock! (The crowd boos as Hammer calls for applause) EL: Jak Hammer now pull the Preacher back up- whips him into the corner- and- look out! 310 lb. of Jak Hammer just plowed into the Preacher as he stood in that corner. Big avalanche, and it looks like Jak Hammer's mode of operations is to use his size advantage. Now Hammer grabs the Preacher, and a big smash into the turnbuckle. They may be padded, but that's real steel underneath, and when your forehead hits, you realize just how thin that padding really is. Hey- Jak Hammer now blatantly choking him- both hands wrapped around the throat! Referee Chris Herman is not going to stand for this! He's giving the 5-count- Hammer releases the choke. Hammer with a body punch, now a kick right in the ribs. Hammer hip tosses him out to the middle of the ring, and the Preacher really needs a chance to start getting some air back in his lungs if he's going to have any chance of winning this match. Hammer is all over him, though- running clothesline puts the Preacher back on the mat. Jak Hammer now hoists him into a bearhug. Squeezing the air out of him. He's got those huge arms wrapped around the rib cage, just cutting off the air like a huge python, or- what's that movie called, the one all the kids are seeing?- I forget, but you get the point he's just squeezing all the air out of the Preacher. (We have the night's first chant of "Go, Kore, Go!") EL: The fans trying to rally behind the Preacher, but Jak Hammer really has that bearhug on tight. Referee Chris Herman checking for a submission- drops the Preacher's arm once- twice- thr- no! (Crowd starts to pop) EL: Kore Wrench fighting out of it- smacks Hammer around the ears with tow big forearms! Again! And a third time- that broke it! Now Kore Wrench grabs Jak Hammer by the ears- head butt! Jak Hammer is reeling- the Preacher off the ropes- clothesline! Down goes Jak Hammer. Kore Wrench picks him up now- he's trying to press him over his head- he can't be serious, Jak Hammer must be almost 400 lb.- he's got it! He's got it! He's got Jak Hammer all the way up over his head- Gorilla press- and a slam! There was only one way to go from there! (Big pop) EL: The cover... 1... 2... kickout! I can't believe Jak Hammer kicked out of that. Probably the only time in his life he's been thrown to earth from that height. Kore Wrench now grabs his arm- arm bar on the mat, and he's twisting it now, obviously trying to negate Hammer's main strength- upper body power. Jak Hammer in real trouble, but a game competitor, no matter what else you think of him- and who doesn't- Hammer up to his feet- Irish whip- reversed- the Preacher catches him in another powerslam! 1... 2... kickout! The Preacher back on his feet, waiting for Jak Hammer to get back up- he hooks him- up he goes- atomic kneedrop, and you can see the pain fly right up Jak Hammer's spine. Now Kore Wrench off the ropes- bulldog- he just drove Jak Hammer's face into the mat. And Jak Hammer rolls to the floor. He's down on one knee, calling for referee Chris Herman- what is he doing? He's pointing to his left eye. What's wrong? Chris Herman motioning Kore Wrench to hold off for a second- he's taking a closer look at Jak Hammer's eye. (Crowd shrieks as a masked man enters the ring behind Wrench) EL: And- hey! There's someone behind Wrench in a black mask- Kore Wrench is watching what's going on out on the floor- hey! He just spun the Preacher around and kicked him right in the gut! DDT! This masked man just smacked Kore Wrench's head right into the mat! Now he stands the Preacher up against the ropes and rolls out of the ring. Jak Hammer now back on his feet, heading to the ring, referee Chris Herman follows. Jak Hammer- now it's his right eye that he keeps blinking- I though it was his left he was complaining about! I smell a rat here, folks, and it just walked right past me here on his way to the locker area. Hammer now takes Kore Wrench, who is just about out on his feet after that DDT- he just shoves him onto the mat- 1... 2... 3! He got it! I cannot believe this- Jak Hammer feigns an eye injury to distract the referee so this other character- Anaconda- that's the film I was thinking about before. DM: Here is your winner, and number one contender for the CWAA Television Championship, Jak Hammer! ("Bad to the Bone" plays and the booing just keeps going and going and going...) EL: OK- I was trying to say, he fakes that eye injury, obviously- he himself couldn't even remember which eye was hurt, as he keeps rubbing that right eye now- and this other individual shows up, just DDTs the Preacher, out of the blue, and now Jak Hammer gets his crack at the TV title later tonight. Well, one thing I think we can be sure of is that we have not seen the last of the Preacher and Jak Hammer. I can only speculate that the other person was Marc Thomas, who was not here to be in Jak Hammer's corner tonight, but again, he had that mask on. Well, we're going to take another break- OK, I can see the Preacher getting back to his feet- we're going to take a quick break, and when we get back, Little Yvette is back to meet Mark Sherrick- should be an outstanding matchup, folks, and you won't want to miss it- stay tuned! (Would You Like to Make Moe Money? Sure, we all would...) EL: Ok- we're back with more exciting CWAA action. Last week we saw the debut of a youngster I think you should all keep an eye on, Little Yvette, the son of, well, one of the big legends from Canada, Ron Yvette. Last week Little Yvette came out here and put up a Hell of a fight before falling to Jak Hammer, who is on one serious roll right now. He's going to step in the ring with another second generation star, Mark Sherrick, whose father Kenny "the Destroyer" Sherrick may be well known to some of you. So far, Sherrick has been very, very impressive, and seems to have some kind of relationship with the West Coast Connection, and we've seen them out here doing his dirty work before. Let's get the introductions from David Myers. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is set for one fall, with a fifteen-minute time limit. Introducing first, from Seattle, Washington- ("My Wave" by Soundgarden plays and a fair amount of popping starts) DM: -weighing in at 240 lb., here is Little Yvette! (Little Yvette stands a little under 6'0", and his well-built frame is encased in a blue and white singlet bearing his name in a cursive script and the image of an eagle in flight.) DM: And his opponent, from Dallas, Texas- (Marilyn Manson's "Beautiful People" plays and the booing begins) DM:- he weighs 275 lb., Mark Sherrick! (Sherrick wears his black hair long on top and shaved on the sides, and there is a long scar on his left shoulder. His blue eyes glare coldly, and his black tights bear silver streaks. And the 6'4"-275 lber dons a single black glove.) DM: Your referee, Jeff Howard. EL: OK- Senior Official Jeff Howard to officiate this contest, and these rookies will learn quickly that Jeff Howard takes no guff. There's the bell. The two circle each other- both so new to the sport that each must be a closed book to the other, we'll see who risks the first offense. Collar and elbow tie-up, and Sherrick with a knee lift. Now a closed fist punch, and Jeff Howard is warning him about using that glove. Another kneelift by Sherrick, and he has Little Yvette in trouble early. Sherrick now goes to the throat- blatant chokehold, and Jeff Howard giving him that 5 count- and Sherrick starts barking back at the referee. One day, Mark Sherrick will learn just how big a mistake that can be. He turns now- Irish whip- back body drop- nice elevation. We know Little Yvette likes to fly- he has that beautiful eagle image on his tights, but this isn't what he had in mind. Now Sherrick grabs him by the tights, and - hey! He just threw him right through the ropes. I don't know what his problem is- you aren't going to pin a man if he's out on the floor and you're in the ring. Jeff Howard starting his count- not as quick as Sherrick's is, though. All right, Little Yvette shakes the cobwebs loose, and back up onto the ring apron. Oooh- a big right hand by Sherrick, and Yvette hangs on to the rope to keep from falling back to the floor. Jeff Howard with another warning for Sherrick. Sherrick turns his attention to his opponent- shoulderblock by Yvette- now he leans back- catapult himself in- slingshot body block! (Big pop. We don't get many such moves here in the CWAA.) EL: Lateral press- 1... 2... kickout. When his dad sees this tape, he'll tell him about hooking the leg, I wager. Yvette back up- monkey flip takes Sherrick down. Now Yvette up- big dropkick, right in the mush. Now where's he going? We told you this kid likes to fly- up top- flying clothesline, and he was right on target with. Like a smart bomb, almost. The cover- 1... 2... Sherrick gets a foot over the bottom rope. That's twice Little Yvette almost pulled off his first victory here in the Sportatorium. Little Yvette with a corner whip now- he follows him in- monkey fl- no! Sherrick caught him! Spinebuster slam! Sherrick covers- 1... 2... and Jeff Howard catches him with his feet on the second ropes for leverage. Jeff Howard did not just fall of the turnip truck- you have to get up pretty early to pull one over on him. Sherrick slaps on a facelock now- he needs to slow this one down. He can out-muscle Little Yvette, but he can't keep up with the aerial attack or Little Yvette's quickness. Mark Sherrick using his power to drain Yvette's energy. ("Go, Yvette, Go!") EL: Still two big matches later tonight, as we're going to see Lt. Lawson put his title on the line against Jak Hammer- he came out here last week looking for a title shot and now he's going to get it. And, in case you've just turned in and haven't heard yet- forearm uppercut by Sherrick now as he releases the hold- the AEWA World Heavyweight Champion, Heavy Metal is here in the arena tonight, and we are going to see him out that World title on the line against CWAA Heavyweight Champ Max "the Professional" Robertson, and I know Randy Sheradin must be beside himself- just two weeks ago that would have been his title shot. OK, Sherrick with a right hand to the chin. Now he sets up- oooh- DDT. Roll him over- 1... 2... kickout. This is what happens when you throw these youngsters against each other- both men forgetting to hook that leg. A small leverage advantage which could be crucial- if one of them were in there with a Marc Thomas, that kind of mistake could be the difference between winning and losing. Sherrick now whips him to the corner, and- oh, come on! Choking him out, with that size 15 boot on Yvette's Adam's apple, and Jeff Howard starting the count- he's got to be losing his patience with Sherrick. Kick by Sherrick, now another closed fist. He's really taken it to Little Yvette here tonight. Whips him across the ring- and a running splash across the ring! Sherrick just took off, and about halfway over leapt and came crashing down on Yvette in that corner. Tosses him to the mat- 1... 2... kickout. Now Sherrick giving Jeff Howard, our senior official, some grief about a slow count- Jeff Howard has been giving these counts for years, there was nothing wrong with it- and Mark Sherrick pays the price for not paying attention, as Little Yvette just knocked him out of his boots with a dropkick! (Big pop) EL: Yvette off the ropes- cross bod- Sherrick catches him- positions him over the shoulder- powerslam! 1... 2... kickout again! Sherrick up now- elbow drop. Now back up- fistdrop! Right between the eyes. Stands him up- grasps around the waist- gutwrench suplex. Cover- 1... 2... kickout. Sherrick starting to lose his temper it looks like, as he is really giving it to Jeff Howard about the two counts, but it isn't the ref's fault if you can't put your guy away. Sherrick whips Yvette to the corner. Now he smashes his head onto that top turnbuckle. Whips him into the ropes now- big elbowsmash, catches him right in the sternum. Mark Sherrick in complete control in this one. He picks Yvette up off the mat- small package! Little Yvette- 1... 2... 3! He got it! He wrapped him right up! ("My Wave" resumes playing) DM: Here is your winner, Little Yvette! (Pops as Sherrick storms off and Yvette raises both hands, basking in the cheers.) EL: Well, Mark Sherrick looked to have this one all wrapped up and ready to take home a couple of times, but Little Yvette guts it out and pulls one out of his hat in the end- the first CWAA victory for Little Yvette, and it looks like it won't be the last by far. We've got the TV Title match up next, let's go to the ring for the introductions. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is set for one fall with a 30-minute time limit, and it is for the CWAA Television Championship! Introducing first, the challenger, he hails from Detroit, Michigan- ("Bad to the Bone" plays, and has that song ever elicited cheers in this arena?) DM:- and weighs in at 310 lb. accompanied by Marc Thomas, here is Jak Hammer! ("When Johnny Comes Marching Home" plays. Hurrah, hurrah.) DM: And his opponent, accompanied by Major Victor Augustus Mayhem, from Poughkeepsie, New York, at 219 lb. he is the CWAA Television Champion, Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson! (The boos continue as the Major and Lt. arrive. The Lt. wears his khakis and sports a crew cut. You already know what Jak Hammer looks like, if you've been paying attention.) DM: Your referee, Chris Herman. EL: OK- looks like a break for me, as the Major appears to feel it necessary to counter Marc Thomas' presence by remaining at ringside. And Jak Hammer wasting no time- Lawson was just still getting in the ring as Hammer nails him with a kneelift. Now he drives him to the mat with those double sledges, just like a jack hammer. Now Lawson on all fours- and a kick in the ribs! If Lawson were a football, he'd be a 50-yard field goal. Now Hammer pulls him up- scoop slam. and Hammer, having fought one match already tonight, looks fresh, but it is Lt. Lawson who looks like he's already been beaten on. Lawson rolls out to the floor, and Chris Herman tries to stop Jak Hammer from following. Look out! Marc Thomas with a spin kick on the floor, and Lt. Lawson should perhaps have kept his mouth shut- this match is not going as he would like. Now Marc Thomas smacks Lawson's head onto the ring apron and rolls him back under the bottom rope. The Major waving that flagpole menacingly, and Thomas cocks his fist as the Major beats a hasty retreat. Jak Hammer with a forearm in the back- Irish whip- shoulderblock puts the champ back on the mat. (A small group of fans actually chants "Go, Lawson, Go!" but the majority would prefer to see these men beat each other into damp spots on the canvas) EL: And Lawson reaches up with a low blow on Hammer- that'll stop anyone's momentum. Chris Herman threatens Lawson with a disqualification, but that won't undo the blow. Lawson now goes to the eyes- and gets another warning. Perhaps if Chris Herman were to threaten to have him court-martialled. Now Lawson up- legsweep takedown- I've been warned not to call it a Russian legsweep- yes, I know you can hear me from there (to the Major). Now Lawson to an armbar- he's keeping Hammer on the mat, which he needs to be concerned with, and to counter the power advantage. Really twisting it on there. Somewhere in the back, Kore Wrench must be taking a little Excedrin and watching this match. He can't have any real love for either man in that ring, and I know he's just itching for a chance at the winner of this one. Probably at the loser, and the two on the outside, as well.ĘOK- Lawson lets go, and now he stomps away at Jak Hammer's legs, and now goes to an armlock-leglock submission hold. Again, keeping Hammer on the mat and reducing the power advantage by attacking those muscular limbs. Lawson lets go again, back up and stomping away again. (Another small group starts "Go, Hammer, Go!") EL: Lt. Lawson now stands him up- fisherman suplex- 1... 2... kickout. Elbow drop by Lawson, and now he starts going back to work on those legs. Stomping on the knees. I can hear Thomas jawing at the Major about something. Lawson now whips his challenger into the corner. It looks like the extra match this evening is coming back to haunt Jak Hammer- he is running on empty in there- ow! Hard chop across the chest, like a cat-o'-nine-tails, by the champ. Now Lawson biting the forehead- he doesn't know where that's been! Referee Chris Herman now warning Lt. Lawson about a disqualification. Lawson with an Irish whip- sleeperhold! He caught him! Lt. Lawson cutting off the carotid artery, and Jak Hammer sinks to one knee- Lawson really has it on tight- Hammer sinking fast- no! Jawbreaker by Hammer- he sinks so fast that Lawson's chin crashes on the top of his head, and I hope the Major's unit has a good dentist! Lawson in serious pain, and Jak Hammer taking the opportunity to get a little rest. Hammer up now- vicious forearm shiver in the back. Hammer now sets him up- brainbuster suplex- he just dropped the champ right on his head! And the Major up on the apron, flagpole at the ready! Hammer over- grabs the Major by the throat! (Enormous pop) EL: Jak Hammer choking out the Major, who has that flagpole- Lt. Lawson coming to his manger's aid- a knee in Jak Hammer's back- Marc Thomas involving himself now- the Major swings the pole- Thomas grabs it- tug of war on the apron- Thomas yanks it free- and it smacks the champ right in the head! Lawson collapses- he was just stomping away at Jak Hammer, and now he gets a headful of flagpole! Referee Chris Herman calling for the bell- Jak Hammer and Lt. Lawson both on the mat in clear pain. The Major gasping for air seated on the floor- here's David Myers with the official word. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, as the result of a disqualification, and still CWAA Television Champion- Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson! (Mixed pop, as Marc Thomas pleads with the referee for understanding the accident that just transpired and "Johnny Comes Marching Home Again" plays.) EL: Lawson gets the win, but he doesn't look like someone who just won. Thomas gives up on Herman now, and he helps his partner up- and they start stomping away at Lawson- Thomas stands him up now- Icebreaker! He just hit the Icebreaker on the TV champ- this match is already over- they aren't proving anything, here. Oh- now Jak Hammer covers, and Marc Thomas making the cover- he counts to three, and hands Hammer the TV Title- well, he isn't the champion, by any stretch of the imagination. Chris Herman trying to tug the title belt back. (Jak Hammer lets go, and Chris Herman falls on the proverbial keester with the title belt as Hammer and Thomas exit) EL: We need to take one more break, and then we are going to see none other than AEWA World Heavyweight Champion, Heavy metal, in the ring with CWAA Heavyweight Champion, Max Robertson!
