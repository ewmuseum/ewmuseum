Sunday February 16th Card (3/3)

16 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(As we return from yet another commercial break....) EL: Before we head to our feature match, here are some words from the man who affected the reconciliation between Max Robertson and Randy Sheradin. ***************** (cut to black. The room's lights slowly rise and reveal The Preacher, The Preacher's Parish standing in front of his altar. He turns toward the camera.) 'The Preacher' Kore Wrench: Good evening. Since I am not scheduled to wrestle this week, I will be spending this Sunday attending to my duties as pastor of this fine chapel. I would, however, like to take this opportunity to clarify something about myself. First of all, I am in no way affiliated with any group or organization aside from membership in the CWAA and my ordination as a member of the Catholic Church. It recently has been brought to my attention that there might arise some confusion because of the group of pseudo-religious wrestlers that are currently running amok in our parent organization, the AEWA. I have never even met these people, and I my interests are exactly the opposite of theirs. I would, someday like to teach these heathens what a true man of God, but that is a matter for another time and another place. For the foreseeable future my mission will be to continue smashing evil here in the CWAA. I have come to realize that the best way for me to fight the righteous fight is to become the first World Champion of the CWAA. The other wrestlers of the CWAA better watch out next week because I won't be there to settle disputes, I'll be there to wrestle, and to pursue the CWAA Championship. Thank you, and God bless. (The lights dim, but the cross continues to glow - cut back to Eric Lane.) ************************** EL: Well, the Church of Pounding Light, as they are known in the AEWA, has been very active in recent months, and one issue they've have been an important factor in has been the AEWA Light Heavyweight Championship since it debuted last year. In the coming weeks, we'll have some very big news concerning that, but first, we have a feature match to present. ("Bad to the Bone", and I have no Gino allusions to make this week. Although I suppose that was one... Jak Hammer arrives to a seriously unfavorable reaction from the many-headed beast in the seats. At 6'10"-310, Hammer cuts an imposing figure, with bulging muscles and black leather attire.) EL: Well, apparently he wants to speak to us all- Mr. Hammer? Hammer: All I've got to say is that, Joey is going to be in for a real beating tonight! This No DQ, No Countout match is right up my alley. He thinks I've been rough on him before, well he ain't seen nothing yet. Once I take care of him tonight, then I'm going to focus my energies on winning the CWAA Championship. Just remember Joey and all the rest of the "tough" guys here in the CWAA, its not always whether you win or lose, its how many people you send to the hospital that counts! Joey, prepare to get Hammered!! EL: Let's take a look at some of the events leading up to tonight's match, as Jak Hammer heads into the ring. **************************** (CWAA File Footage: 01-26-97) Samoan Drop! The 1..2...kickout again. Hammer stomps the head, now he's barking at the referee. He should be focusing more on finishing the match than in intimidating the official. Hammer sends Haven into the ropes-clothesline, and Haven is on his back again. 1..2... and another 2 count for Jak Hammer, and the frustration is showing on his face, and now he takes it out on Joey Haven. He tosses the youngster out of the ring, and Haven lands with a crash out by the front row. (Note to the readers- there is no guardrail in the Sportatorium, just a piece of string running along in front of the front-row marks.) EL: Hammer follows him now, and this one is going to be battled out on the floor, Uh-oh! Hammer's got himself a chair, but our referee'll have none of this. Haven is still trying to collect himself on the floor, Hammer is standing about 20 feet away with a folding chair in his hand, and he's glaring at the referee, who is giving him a 5-count to drop the chair or risk the DQ. 1..2...the crowd counts along...4...Hammer tosses the chair down again. He heads over to Haven, and the referee begins his 10-count- why the count doesn't coincide with the count he gave Hammer to drop the chair, I don't know. Hammer pulls Haven up but the hair- and Joey Haven with a right hand that caught the big man by surprise, but Hammer comes right back with a kick in the midsection, and he drilled Haven's face into the floor! DDT, right on the floor! The crowd is stunned. Now Hammer is hurrying back to the ring to beat the 10-count! And there's the bell! I don't know if he beat it in or not; we've either got a double count-out or a victory for Jak Hammer here. OK, I see the referee raising Hammer's hand. Jak Hammer defeats Hardcore Joey- wait a second! ("Bad to the Bone" plays again) EL: Haven is back on the apron, chair in hand, and his hair is matted with blood, adhering to his forehead. He tosses the chair, it sails over Jak's head, lands innocently- if he was aiming for Hammer's head, he missed by a mile. Hammer turns around as he hears the crash, and Haven is running at him- a dropkick knocks Jak hammer off of his feet, and as he gets back up Haven is running at him again- Haven grabs him in a side headlock and bulldogs him right onto the chair! This match ended a minute or two ago, folks, and the referee is trying to get both men out of there. Haven picks up the stunned giant and whips him into the ropes- the referee grabs the chair and pulls it out of the ring, where it won't cause any harm, and Haven catches Hammer- looks like an airplane spin or Samoan drop- no! He drops Hammer right on his head, and right where that chair was a few seconds ago, and the fans are on their feet! Hardcore Joey Haven has been ddted into the ground, and just came back and nearly Death Valley Drove Jak Hammer into that chair, and Hammer rolls under the bottom rope and staggers to the aisleway. He wants no more of this, but he's the one who gets credit for the win, as it was Jak Hammer over Joey Haven via count-out. **************************** (CWAA File Footage: 02-03-97) EL: Last week they didn't care that the match had stopped, now they don't care that it hasn't started! Jak Hammer with a tremendous cheap shot, and now he's kneeling on the floor punching away at Haven's head. Has the bell even rung for this one? Hammer's got that chair again, and he lays it on the floor. What is he doing? He drapes Haven over his shoulders- oh, sweet Jesus- he Samoan Dropped him onto the chair, and you could hear that one all the way back in Haven's hometown of Philadelphia! Now he drapes Haven over his shoulder again- is he going to drop on that chair once more? No- horizontal backbreaker! He's got him in a horizontal backbreaker after that back has had two collisions with a chair already. Will someone please do something about this? The bell is ringing like crazy, there's the cue for help to come out here, and here comes Travis Conley and Paul Langford, and Hammer drops Haven to the floor... ******************************** EL: And- wait a second- here comes Joshua Barnes- what is he doing out here? Joshua? (Joshua reappears, holding a manila envelope) JB: Umm.. have you seen... Randy? EL: Randy Sheradin? Not since you left with him and Robertson? Is something wrong? JB: I... umm..well, I went back to... you know... my locker? And this (holding up the envelope) was in it. There are these... pictures...and in them... there's Randy... these pictures are of Randy and a referee, and it looks like... Randy handing the referee... some.. ummm.. money. A lot, actually. EL: I'm sure there's an explanation. JB: Umm.. yeah. And there's a note, and I don't know who it's... I dont't know who wrote it. It says he just wants me... the guy who wrote it- he wants me to know how Randy became a champion, because... you know... he was a champion somewhere else. EL: Who is it from? JB: It's says "an ally". I have to find Randy. (Exit Joshua Barnes, which is a good thing because Jak Hammer was growing visibly impatient) ("Highway to Hell" plays and the crowd erupts in response to the presence of the 5'10"-245 lb. Philadelphia boy.) EL: I don;t have time to speculate about that, as once again, they aren't waiting for the bell- Haven races into the ring, and Hammer meets him with a kneelift as he goes through the ropes. Hammer know with a double sledge, and another, and another- he is just Hammering Haven into the mat, if you'll forgive the pun. Hammer now removes that sleeveless top he wears- and he is choking Haven with it now! The ref starts to count- but he realizes thee are no disqualifications- a very dangerous type of match, indeed. Hammer know releases the chokehold and whips Haven into the ropes- Haven comes back with a cross body press- he has Hammer on his back, but rather than cover, he unleashes a flurry of punches on Jak Hammer's head, and the fans here in the world-famous Sportatorium are solidly behind this young man. Joey Haven pulls Hammer up now- Irish whip- he catches him- Hotshot! He just dropped Jak Hammer's throat across that top rope! Now he clotheslines him, and both men are carried over the top rope. Jak Hammer lands on the floor, but Haven keeps his balance out on the apron. Now it's Joey Haven climbing the ropes while Jak Hammer tries regain his senses out on the floor. Haven stands poised on the top rope like some hungry bird of prey while Jak Hammer makes his way to his feet- flying clothesline! On the floor, a top rope clothesline by Joey Haven, and know he grabs Hammer by the ears and is ramming the back of his head onto the floor! He gets up and stands with his hand raised, and the crowd shows their support for the plucky youngster. Now he's going after the ringsteps- kicks them over. What is planning now? He's lying them on their side next to Jak Hammer. He picks Hammer up- is he suplexing him on the floor? Ow! Front layout suplex, right across the overturned ring steps! Joey Haven, now, grabs a chair, and he drags it into the ring. He unfolds it, and, well- go ahead, take a seat. You've worked hard so far, you've earned it. Joey Haven, seated in the middle of the ring waiting for his opponent to rejoin him. While Jak Hammer gets his breath back, let's take another look at that suplex. (Screen splits to show Haven, in real time, sitting in his chair and also to show him executing the front layout suplex across the ring steps.) EL: Next week, we'll be seeing both of these men in their first round matches for the CWAA Heavyweight Title tournament, as Joey Haven faces The Preacher, Kore Wrench- Jak Hammer standing out on the floor, resting with his hands on his knees. (The crowd starts humming the Jeopardy theme song.) Twister's Travis Conley must be enjoying what he's seen so far- he drew Jak Hammer as an opponent next week. I thought we were going to see those two butt heads a few weeks ago when Twister came to Joey Haven's aid. OK- it looks like this match is about to resume, Jak Hammer gingerly heading back into the ring. Haven up to meet him, and here we go- wait a second! Jak Hammer just reached into his tights and pulled out some kind of white powder and just flipped it in Haven's face! There's a cloud of some kind of dust, still swirling in the air, and Joey Haven has been effectively blinded. I don;t know what that was- chalk, maybe? Whatever it was, it;s worked. Jak Hammer now with a series of jabs- Haven drops to one knee, bounces up and starts swinging wildly, but he can't see his opponent! Hammer know takes that chair that Joey Haven was so cavalierly resting in a second ago, and- no! He just smacked it over Joey's head! Hammer know discards the chair, and backs Haven into the corner. Now he;s using that big foot of his to choke Joey out, and Joey is kicking wildly as he struggles to get air to his lungs. Now Hammer stops, and he's going back for that chair. He's unfolding it- is he going to take a break now, too? No- he places it around Haven's neck, like a life saver, or a wreath or a harness. Joey Haven wearing a dented metal chair like it were some metallic Elizabethan ruff. And- oh, no. Jak Hammer ddts him, with the chair around his neck! Haven;s Adam;s apple just met the chair on his way down, and he' struggling terribly to get it off, as Jak Hammer stomps away on his back. Haven rolls out to the floor, and finally gets that chair off, but he still has his face caked in white powder. It looks like he can see again, but he;s been taking some very serious abuse, and it looks better and better for Kore Wrench next week, too. Jak Hammer now out there after him. He spins Haven around and floors him with a punch in the head. Now he picks Haven up- military press- he;s got him way up over his head- Joey Haven, perched tenuously seven feet-plus over the floor, and Hammer tosses him to the ground- a gorilla press, out on the floor. He's got that chair again, and he lays it down on the floor, folded up. He's done this twice, now- a ddt the first time they met and Samoan drop the second. Jak Hammer now sets him up for a second ddt- and he just drove Joey Haven face first into that chair! Blood now mixing in with that white powder still all over his face, and Joey Haven now looks like some nightmarish clown. Jak Hammer the ringmaster in this circus, as he drags Joey back to the ring- time for his endgame perhaps- there are no disqualifications and no countout- but you still need to beat the man in the ring to win this one. Irish whip by Hammer, and he meets Haven with a big boot in the stomach. Now he picks him, drapes him across the shoulders- it's the Rebel rack! His horizontal backbreaker, and he bounces while applying it, adding the extra pressure. The referee checks for a submission- Haven's arm drops limply once... twice...thr- no! Haven kept that arm up, and now he reaches over and slaps a clawhold on Jak Hammer! Hammer leans back and falls for a Samoan drop, and both the rebel rack and clawhold are broken. Both men back up, and Joey Haven with a drop kick that knocks Hammer back into the ropes- and Hammer is tied up in those ropes! Nowhere to go! Joey Haven looks to the crowd, and they egg him on- he rushes up to where Jak Hammer is caught helpless, and starts flailing away with fists to the face. The referee tries to get between the two so he can get Hammer out, but Joey Haven just shoves him aside! Now Haven stops and parades around the ring with his arms in the air as the crowd cheers. The referee is buy freeing Jak Hammer, and we're ready to continue, or we will be after this break! If a finish occurs while we're gone, we'll show it to you when we get back! Don't change that channel! (Monday night, on Happy Days, Joanie has a chance for stardom as Leather Tuscadero offers her a spot in her concert tour, at 10:30 here on WYXZ 39. Then, at 11:00, Ponch enters the police boxing tournament on CHiPs.) EL: OK- while we were gone, both men stood toe to toe exchanging blows, and it was the much bigger Jak Hammer getting the better of it, but Joey Haven reversed a suplex and stuck him with a brainbuster. Now it's Joey Haven in control, as he sends Jak Hammer into the corner, and charges- no one home, as Jak Hammer bails out, and Joey Haven runs right into the turnbuckles. Jak Hammer back into those tights- how many foreign objects does he keep on there, anyway- and out comes a set of brass knuckles! He puts them on, and bam! He slugged Joey Haven with the brass knuckles, and Joey collapses in a heap in the corner. Hammer with the cover, and he grabs a handful of tights, AND he drapes his own feet over the second rope for added leverage... 1... 2... 3! He got it! (The crowd's groans turn into angry and shocked shouts.) With a foreign object, a handful of tights and using the ropes for leverage, Jak Hammer has beaten Joey Haven. ("Bad to the Bone" plays again, and Jak Hammer gives several hundred people (we are a small group, after all) a loom of "I-told-you-so.") EL: Wait a second! Joey Haven is getting back to his feet- his face and shirt covered in that hideous combination of blood and that white powder. Joey Haven leaps on Hammer's back, and takes him down! A series of punches, and this match be finished, but Joey Haven isn't. Joey Haven now with an Irish whip, he catches Jak Hammer- Death Valley Driver! He stuck him headfirst onto the mat! Now he drags Jak Hammer back over the ropes- Joey Haven up on the second rope- he turns Hammer over- powerbomb coming up- he leaps off of the rope and plants Jak Hammer in the mat- the Joeybomb! Joey Haven gives his own count... 1.... 2... 3! Well; he got a three count, but this one didn't count! Of he goes, and, folks, we've got to go, too! We'll be back next week with more tournament action, plus a special feature match! See you then!
