Saturday Night Slam (11/2, part two)

4 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
****CONTINUED FROM PART ONE**** MW: We've got a BIG grudge match, as former Angel of Death Twisted Steel takes on the leader of the Angels, The Grim Reaper! They got into an argument over who was the leader last week on Straight Talk, which ended up in a wild brawl. Now they're going to face off to settle their differences, it should be a wild one! BD: And I just noticed something! Look who's sitting at ringside! MW: (Stops and looks) OH MY! It's Vicki, Twisted Steel's estranged wife who left him long ago for Ricky Roberts. Roberts has since dumped her, and now she claims to want Twisted Steel back. That could lead to a very volatile situation at ringside! LB: It sure could! Vicki sure seemed sincerely sorry on Straight Talk when she was begging Twisted Steel to forgive her. Will his pride allow him to accept her back? MW: Only time will tell. This one has the makings of a classic, so let's not waste anymore time, let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is a special grudge match and is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, he weighs in at 435 pounds and is accompanied by his manager, "Cutie" Ric Verrano, The Grim Reeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeaper! ("The Funeral March" plays eerily throughout the arena as the Reaper slowly and methodically makes his entrance to a HUGE downpour of boos. Ric Verrano is at his side.) BD: That man certainly is intimidating. MW: You've got that right, and he can back it up too! Former TV Champion, former North American Champion, this guy is one person you don't wanna mess with. LB: Apparently, Twisted Steel does. MW: Well he's another one of those guys you don't wanna mess with, that's why I can't wait for this one to start. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 425 pounds and accompanied by his manager, Karma, Twisted Steeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeel! (The sounds of truck engines resonate throughout the arena, as Twisted Steel makes his way to the ring to a mixed reaction.) BD: Twisted Steel's gonna have one tough task on his hands though, because you know the other Angels are in the building. MW: They sure are, and we're likely to see them here tonight. Twisted Steel had better watch his back. LB: I'm sure he already knows that. He was one of them, remember? MW: Of course I do, he only quit a couple weeks ago. Twisted Steel steps into the ring, and immediately attacks the Reaper! BD: We're not gonna see much technical wrestling here. MW: That's for sure. Twisted Steel is really pounding on the Reaper now, and he doubles him over with a fist to the midsection! HE'S GOING FOR THE PAIN BOMB ALREADY!! LB: There's NO WAY that he'll get him up. MW: We're about to find out! Twisted Steel LIFTS, AND HE GOT HIM UP--BUT THE REAPER GOES ALL THE WAY OVER, AND COUNTERS WITH A BACKSLIDE!! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Twisted Steel just couldn't hold him up. MW: No, the weight was just too much. Both men up, and Twisted Steel's gonna go for the Pain Bomb again! But the Reaper counters with a backdrop! LB: Come on Steel, you're not gonna win like that and you know it, so cut it out! MW: Actually, that's sound advice. And now the Reaper drops a fist onto the head of Twisted Steel! The Reaper picks him up off the mat, and grabs him by the head, DDT!!! BD: And now the Reaper is in control, MW: For now. The Reaper brings Twisted Steel back to his feet, and NAILS him with a kick to the midsection! And now the Reaper's setting Twisted Steel up for HIS Power Bomb finisher! LB: Oh come on, Reaper! He's just as big as you are! MW: The Reaper LIFTS, and he gets Twisted Steel up! But now STEEL counters with a backslide! One..Two..And Ric Verrano jumps up on the apron, distracting the ref! BD: Boy! Will this managerial brilliance ever cease? MW: It's not brilliance, it's just plain cheating! Twisted Steel should've won right there! Twisted Steel is upset, but goes back to work; Verrano is STILL arguing with the ref though! He's poking at the ref with a cane! LB: He's gotta do something to keep the referees in line. MW: That's not his job. Now Twisted Steel picks up the Reaper, and whips him to the ropes, RIGHT WHERE VERRANO IS STANDING! The ref dodges for cover, AND THE REAPER COLLIDES WITH VERRANO, AND HE RAN RIGHT INTO THAT CANE! BD: This isn't fair! MW: Twisted Steel didn't do anything, Verrano shouldn't have been on the apron. The Reaper staggers back from his collision, AND TWISTED STEEL CATCHES HIM WITH A POWERSLAM!!! The cover! One..Two..Three!!!! (DING, DING) LB: It's over ALREADY? RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of two minutes and thirty five seconds, here is your winner.... TWISTED STEEL!!!!! BD: What a cheap win! Where the hell were the Angels? MW: I don't know, but they weren't here, and Twisted Steel managed to pull a fast one on the Reaper. Maybe they were still celebrating Deranged's title victory, I mean it WAS less than five minutes ago. LB: Who knows where they are, all I know is, they should've been here! MW: Well they weren't, and The Reaper lost, get over it. Twisted Steel is about to leave, BUT HE JUST SPOTTED VICKI AT RINGSIDE, and look at the look on her face! She is obviously VERY sorry. BD: Will he take her back? MW: We're about to find out. Twisted Steel steps out of the ring, AND VICKI HOPS THE GUARD RAIL! She's holding out her arms, and BEGGING Twisted Steel to take her back! LB: I wonder what he's gonna do. MW: Twisted Steel looks Vicki straight in the eye, AND PICKS HER UP IN A BIG HUG! How beautiful! BD: Wow! I didn't think he'd do it! MW: I wasn't sure but.... NO!!!! I CAN'T BELIEVE THIS!! Twisted Steel just used that hug, and took Vicki over in a belly-to-belly suplex! This is sickening! What's wrong with this man! LB: I don't know, but this guy's got a screw loose, that's for sure. I can't believe he's doing this to the woman he was MARRIED to! MW: And now Twisted Steel is just stomping away on her! She's helpless! This is disgusting! BD: You've got that right, I mean I knew Twisted Steel was mean, but I NEVER thought he'd stoop so low as to assault the only woman he's ever loved like this! MW: You took the words right out of my mouth, Big Daddy! Twisted Steel is picking Vicki up now, and he lifts her into the air, AND PAIN BOMBS HER ON THE CONCRETE!!!! Someone has got to stop this! LB: This is beyond bad taste, and now Twisted Steel is grabbing a microphone! TWISTED STEEL: You had your chance, b*tch! But you chose to leave me for Roberts, that puss bag. You've made your choice, now LIVE WITH IT! (Twisted Steel THROWS down the microphone and SPITS onto Vicki, and then just walks away.) MW: I can't believe this! I don't think this man cares about ANYONE anymore. He's not out for anyone but himself! BD: Vicki isn't moving! That man is just SICK. MW: And now the paramedics are attending to her, and we can only hope and pray that she's alright. Unfortunately, we still have to move on. LB: And I see what next, they're setting up the scaffold, you know what that means! MW: I sure do! Mike Stewart is about to defend his World Title against the former champion, Heavy Metal in one of the most dangerous matches in all of wrestling, a scaffold match! BD: These things are nuts! MW: You can say that again. In case any of you don't know what a scaffold match is, here are the rules. The two wrestlers will wrestle on a platform, with NO ROPES, which is suspended 15 feet above the ring! The only way to win is to knock your opponent off of it, and down to the floor. The falls can be DEVASTATING! LB: They sure can, and that's why Stewart is gonna be leaving this place in an ambulance tonight. MW: Don't be so sure. This is one of those kinds of matches that anyone can win, because a spill can happen at any time. This should be a hot one folks, so let's quit talking, and let's go to the ring! RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is a SCAFFOLD MATCH for the AEWA World Heavyweight Title! The first man to knock his opponent off of the platform and to the floor will be declared the winner! Introducing first, the challenger, he weighs in at 450 pounds, he is the former AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, Heavy Meeeeetaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaal! (Metallica's "Battery" cranks over the PA as the massive former champion makes his way to the ring to a HUGE round of boos.) BD: And he looks primed and ready to take the title tonight. MW: Heavy Metal is ALWAYS ready, that's why he was champ for so long, but he's got a tough one ahead of him tonight. LB: No he doesn't, probably climbing up that ladder to get to the scaffold is the toughest challenge he'll have all night. MW: I HIGHLY doubt that. Stewart's been a great champion, and this should be one heck of a match! RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 250 pounds and accompanied by his manager, Michelle Anderson, he IS the AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, "Superman" Mike Steeeeeeeeeeeeeeeewwwwwarrrrt! (Survivor's "Eye of the Tiger" begins to play as Stewart, belt around his waist, makes his way to the ring, and the crowd EXPLODES. Michelle Anderson is at his side.) BD: Just look at him! How can a skinny wuss like this be our champion? MW: He is not a wuss, and he's champion because he defeated Heavy Metal. What more credentials could you possibly need? LB: A lot. MW: I don't think so. Stewart is climbing up the ladder now, and we're just about ready to get underway. BD: This should be fun. Stewart's gonna get squashed like a grape! MW: We'll see. Stewart is up on the platform now, and Heavy Metal immediately charges at him, AND LEVELS HIM WITH A CLOTHESLINE! Stewart is down, and Heavy Metal CRUSHES him with a big splash! LB: What a joke! It's gonna be over already! MW: Heavy Metal is trying to roll Stewart off of the platform now, but Stewart hangs on for dear life. Heavy Metal can't get him, and he goes back to work. Heavy Metal lets Stewart get to his feet, STANDING DROPKICK--SIDESTEPPED BY STEWART! And Heavy Metal falls hard, but not off the platform. BD: Big deal. Like Stewart could actually win. MW: I'll assure you, he can. Stewart picks up Heavy Metal, and takes him DOWN with a jawbreaker! Heavy Metal is down, and Stewart executes a somersault splash onto him! LB: Come on, Metal! You're MUCH better than this! MW: Right now he's not! Stewart lifts Heavy Metal up again, AND NAILS HIM WITH A JUMPING NECK SNAP! Heavy Metal falls forward, and ALMOST falls off the edge, but manages to stop a step early. BD: That was a close one. MW: Yes it was. Stewart grabs Heavy Metal now, and he's going for a vertical suplex! BUT HE CAN'T LIFT HIM UP! And now Heavy Metal just picks up Stewart, and DROPS him into a side suplex! LB: And just like that, the big man takes control. MW: He does indeed. Heavy Metal picks Stewart up now, GUTWRENCH SUPLEX! Stewart tries to get up, but Heavy Metal CHOPS him back down! BD: I think Stewart's about to take a fall! MW: We'll see. Heavy Metal picks up the champ, AND NAILS HIM WITH A POWERSLAM! He sees his chance! He rolls Stewart to the edge, and drops him off! BUT STEWART HANGS ON TO THE EDGE, AND HEAVY METAL DOESN'T SEE HIM! He thinks he's won! LB: Turn around, Heavy Metal! MW: You know he can't hear you, right? Stewart manages to pull himself back up, but Heavy Metal has his arms raised, and is facing the other way! Stewart charges, AND LEVELS HIM WITH A CLOTHESLINE FROM BEHIND! BD: Next time, make SURE he falls! MW: I'm sure he will. Stewart grabs Heavy Metal now, and NAILS him with another jawbreaker! Heavy Metal gets back to his feet, but Stewart NAILS him with a lariat! Heavy Metal staggers, and Stewart hits him with an elbow! He's teetering near the edge, but he won't fall! LB: Move toward the center! MW: And now Stewart charges at him, FLYING CROSS BODY PRESS! BUT HEAVY METAL SIDE STEPS, AND STEWART GOES OVER THE EDGE!!!! BUT HE MANAGED TO GRAB ONTO THE LADDER, AND HE CLIMBS BACK UP!!!! BD: I don't BELIEVE that! MW: Heavy Metal's not gonna let him climb up though! He's trying to shake him off the ladder! Stewart is gonna fall! BUT HE MANAGES TO THUMB HEAVY METAL IN THE EYE, AND BUY HIMSELF TIME TO SCRAMBLE BACK UP TO THE PLATFORM! LB: How the HELL did he hang on? MW: Quite frankly, I don't know. Stewart is back up on the platform, and he NAILS Heavy Metal with a shoulderblock! Heavy Metal teeters towards the edge, and Stewart charges in to finish him off, BUT HEAVY METAL MOVES! And Stewart ALMOST charges right off the edge, just BARELY holding up in time. LB: These guys are showing us quite a balancing act. MW: That's for sure. Heavy Metal NAILS Stewart with a dropkick, and he ALMOST FALLS again, but he's teetering! Heavy Metal hits Stewart with a kick to the thigh, AND NOW HE'S SETTING HIM UP FOR THE MEGADETH POWER BOMB! BD: HE'S GONNA POWER BOMB HIM OFF THE SCAFFOLD, DOWN TO THE CONCRETE!!! MW: YOU'RE RIGHT! If he hits this, Stewart's CAREER may be over! Heavy Metal now lifts Stewart up, AND BRINGS HIM.... NO! STEWART COUNTERS WITH A FRANKENSTEINER!! And BOTH men go FLYING from the Platform! AND THEY BOTH HIT THE GROUND!!! (DING, DING) LB: Huh? What do we call this?? MW: I don't know. BOTH men came down, and I believe they hit the ground at the EXACT same time, and Stewart landed on his HEAD! He's gotta be really hurt! Well let's get the official word. RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of seven minutes and twenty-three seconds, here is your winner...... uuuuuhhhhhh..... BD: Well that was helpful. MW: Not really. LB: What do we do now? MW: I don't know.... Wait a minute, here comes President Freeman, perhaps he can shed some light on this situation. He's grabbing a microphone. PRESIDENT FREEMAN: Now we just witnessed a CLASSIC AEWA Title match, but BOTH men fell and hit the ground at the EXACT same time, and since the rules state that the first one to hit the ground loses, technically, they both lost. Now this is the toughest decision I've ever had to make, but because of this situation, as of right now, I am declaring the AEWA World Heavyweight title VACANT, with the new champion to be determined at a future date. I know you may not agree with this decision, but in my position, it was the only one I could make. If they are able to compete, both Heavy Metal and Mike Stewart WILL be involved in whatever format I decide to use to determine the new champion. Thank you. (Freeman puts down the microphone, and walks back to the locker room.) MW: Well, there you have it folks! The AEWA Title is officially vacant! I wonder how they'll go about determining the new champ. BD: Only Freeman knows that. Heavy Metal is getting up and walking away now, but I think Stewart is hurt BAD. He hasn't moved since he fell. MW: Yes, the paramedics are attending to him now, and they're being very careful-- it doesn't look good. LB: No it really doesn't. He may even have a broken neck. MW: If he is indeed seriously injured, then it's an unfortunate situation for the entire AEWA. And I'd like to stay with this, but unfortunately we're out of time, so for now, for The Lovely Bertha and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt, this is Matt Wilson saying so long everybody! (A slow motion replay is shown of Mike Stewart nailing Heavy Metal with the frankensteiner off the platform, and coming down with him. They both CLEARLY hit the ground at the same time, and Stewart lands very awkwardly on his head.
