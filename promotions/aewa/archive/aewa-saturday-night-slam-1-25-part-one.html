Saturday Night Slam (1/25, part one)

27 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen, set on fire. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Rock music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... David Graves taking some guy's head off with the Gravestone, Penance PLANTING some guy with the Final Judgment, The West Coast Connection DRIVING some guy's head into the mat with the West Coast Killa, Cardinal Chaos STICKING some poor soul with the Inverted Crucifix, Shane Matthews BRUTALLY hitting the End of the Road on some hapless jobber, and Heavy Metal notching yet ANOTHER casualty courtesy of the Megadeth Power Bomb. The camera then shifts to Matt Wilson, The Lovely Bertha, and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt in the Fresno Convention Center, the site of AEWA Saturday Night Slam!) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Saturday Night Slam! We have some GREAT matches scheduled for you tonight, folks! BD: Yeah, but I'm really looking forward to the main event. Penance is going to defend his Light Heavyweight title against the Holy Ghost, and that should be an EXCELLENT bout. LB: They're both superb technical wrestlers, so ANYTHING could happen. Personally, though, I'm more excited about the special interview with Kevin Larkin. MW: He has some VERY strong comments for President Freeman, folks. Those two men are in the middle of a BRUTAL corporate war, and frankly, I think it's gotten WAY out of control. BD: Do we have the footage ready? MW: Yeah, let's roll it.... ------------ (The camera opens on the vice president of the AEWA, Kevin Larkin. He is standing in his office, wearing a loud Hawaiian shirt and shorts. His hair is totally messed up, as if he hasn't slept in several days. He begins to speak....) Larkin: Freeman, I am Vice President of the AEWA. I have been here from the beginning. I was here when Black Hawk and Organized Crime were big names, when Cardinal Chaos plotted against Mister Crowley, and when the AEWA flourished under my beneficent leadership. Then you came along, and my powers were curbed. When I attempted to retaliate against wrestlers who had insulted my person and threatened me with bodily harm, you suspended me and cut off my funds. You cut off all funding of my show until you were able to cancel it under the pretext of poor ratings. You suspended me a second time and surrounded yourself with your cronies in the boardroom to contain my influence. You tampered with the contender rankings in my division to get Chaos and Crowley off of your back. You banned my team from its rightful place in the World Tag Team Championship match. After you have done all of this, you have the temerity, the audacity, the unmitigated gall to be upset at ME? You interfered with the Horde's attempt to earn a living, so of COURSE they reacted as they did. The punishment they meted out was just. And very, very funny, I might add. You think you can settle this by offering me "control" of the AEWA if we beat you in a match? How can you offer me what I already have? I OWN the AEWA, and if you think I don't already have control, ask yourself where all of your supporters were when Athanaric and Fritigern were driving your miserable little body into the floor. But, for fun, I accept your "generous" offer. At Kickoff '97, Freeman, find yourself two people in this organization who would rather work for you than for me, and bring them along. You and your cronies versus me and my Horde. Loser walks away from the AEWA-- forever. Winner has sole control of the AEWA-- forever. It is that simple, Freeman. (The scene fades to black, and the camera returns to our broadcast team.) ------------ MW: Well, Kevin Larkin has accepted President Freeman's challenge for Kickoff '97, folks. BD: That's going to be a VERY interesting match. But Freeman still hasn't selected a team, right? What's taking him so long? LB: He's just afraid of Larkin, that's all. MW: No, he's just trying to pick the BEST team. His entire business is depending on this decision, so of COURSE he's not rushing it. BD: I think he's chicken. MW: This might come as a surprise to you, Big Daddy, but nobody CARES what you think. Let's just get started with the first match of the evening. Heaven & Hell are about to square off against two preliminary competitors, Peter Penthouse and Sammy McSlam. LB: Well, McSlam and Penthouse are just a bunch of amateurs, and they're NOT going to win tonight. I mean, let's get SERIOUS. MW: I think you may be right, Bertha. BD: I've heard those words before, but never together like that. "I think you may be right, Bertha." Wow. Interesting concept. (Bertha smacks Big Daddy in the back of the head, knocking his headphones off. Being too lazy to pick them up, he instead takes Wilson's. We hear a quick cry of protest from Wilson, who can't reach the other pair.) BD: Well, it looks like I'M running the show now. For the first time in the history of this crappy little program, we're going to have some QUALITY play-by-play. LB: Wilson, are you gonna let Big Daddy push you around like that? (Wilson can be heard in the background, but the absence of a microphone means his voice is not sufficiently amplified.) BD: I'd couldn't hear ANYTHING you just said, buddy. (grins) Could you possibly speak up? LB: Until Wilson can get another pair of headphones, I don't think we'll be hearing much from him. BD: Jeez, this is my dream come true. LB: Mine too. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team match is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, at a combined weight of 641 pounds, Cardinal Chaos and Mister Crowley, HEAVEN & HELL! (As "Sympathy for the Devil" begins playing loudly, Mister Crowley and Cardinal Chaos make their entrance. They get a HUGE round of boos from the crowd.) BD: These guys are looking GREAT tonight, folks. They're both members of the AEWA Hall Of Fame, of course. LB: Well, after they get through with these damn rookies, Heaven & Hell will probably be demanding a title shot. They haven't seen much action since that four-way match at December Doom. RING ANNOUNCER: And their opponents, already in the ring, here are Sammy McSlam and Peter Penthouse.... BD: Well, this match is about to begin, so I guess it's time for some of my EXPERT analysis. LB: Uh, isn't that MY job? Who died and made you play-by-play announcer? BD: Don't tempt me. McSlam has thrown a series of punches at Crowley's stomach, but Crowley closes a fist around his Adam's apple, and-- (notices Wilson, who is trying desperately to pick up the other headphones) Wilson, sit back down and be quiet. I don't want to have to reprimand you again. LB: Really, it's very difficult to concentrate with you squirming and whining like that. BD: While we were RUDELY INTERRUPTED, both teams have tagged and Chaos is in there in Penthouse. LB: Cardinal Chaos was in Penthouse magazine? BD: Maybe I should have taken YOUR headphones instead. Penthouse comes off the ropes, cross body press, OH MY GOD, HE'S GOT HIM! One..Two....Referee breaks the count! LB: Penthouse used the ropes for leverage, and the ref forced the break. Jeez, what an IDIOT-- he can't even cheat without getting caught. BD: I couldn't agree more. Both men are back up and Penthouse is connecting with a series of European-style uppercuts. LB: What is this guy-- a Communist? Somebody should tell him that he's in the USA now, and we throw AMERICAN uppercuts around here. BD: Uh, yeah, whatever. Now Penthouse hooks Chaos, he's going for a suplex-- HE HAS HIM UP! This is the highlight of this man's sad little career, suplexing Cardinal Chaos-- but wait, Crowley just came in and kicked him in the gut, and he crumples to the mat with Chaos on top of him. LB: McSlam runs in and charges at Crowley, who catches him and dumps him over the top rope! Chaos covers Penthouse.... One..Two..Chaos pulls him back up! LB: He wasn't "ready" to finish him off yet, so he just decided to add insult to injury. I love when he does that! BD: He holds him up, and whips Penthouse into Crowley, who catches him with a boot to the gut! LB: Penthouse is doubled over, and he gets NAILED with a piledriver! Crowley just DRILLED him into the mat, folks. The cover! One..Two..Three!! (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here are your winners, Mister Crowley and Cardinal Chaos, HEAVEN & HELL! BD: And Heaven & Hell have emerged victorious once again. This match didn't take 'em long, did it? LB: Just like I predicted. BD: Actually, that was MY prediction, wasn't it? LB: Think again, Big Daddy. Anyway, let's get on with the next match. (checks her papers) According to this schedule, the Visigothic Horde are about to square off against "Jumping" Joey Wrangler and Parker Brown. BD: This should be another squash, folks. The Horde are preparing for their big match at Kickoff '97, so NOTHING could stop them. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, already in the ring, here are Parker Brown and "Jumping" Joey Wrangler! LB: These guys look ready to get their teeth kicked in, so at least they did SOMETHING right. They're about to be ripped apart. BD: But who's Wrangler and who's Brown? LB: Well, they're both kind of tanned-- (Before she can continue, Matt Wilson snatches her headset away. He quickly puts it on, before she can protest.) MW: I just couldn't listen to anymore of that nonsense, folks. We REALLY need to get a few more headsets out here. BD: Why? I'm not in any hurry to listen to Bertha's voice again, are you? Of course, I'd rather not hear YOUR voice, either. MW: Thanks for your words of support, Mike. BD: Anytime, buddy. RING ANNOUNCER: And their opponents, from parts unknown, Athanaric and Fritigern, THE VISIGOTHIC HORDE! (Athanaric and Fritigern storm down the aisle, without Kevin Larkin. They appear to be VERY angry, and immediately attack their opponents.) MW: I understand that the ICU are very interested in taking them and Larkin into their stable. Can you imagine if one of the most feared stables in the AEWA were actually joined by a Vice President who is in position to win complete control of the AEWA? BD: Yeah, this may be Deathwatch's way of guaranteeing himself a World title. He's sneaky enough to pull something like that off. MW: Unfortunately, yes, he is. BD: For my own self confidence, Wilson, please don't EVER agree with me again. (Bertha gets out of her seat and walks around the table to pick up the headphones she had knocked off of Big Daddy. She snaps them on and walks back to her seat and sits back down, but the cord, intended only to reach to Big Daddy's chair, stretches across Wilson's face, pushing his glasses off balance.) MW: Bertha! I can't see what's going on! What's happening? BD: Fritigern is biting Parker Brown's forehead, and the Jumping guy and Athanaric are trading punches in the corner. LB: And Athanaric has the upper hand, and he tosses Wrangler out to the floor! What power! (Wilson tries to remove the cord stretched across his face, but it slides down under his chin and presses on his throat) MW: Acccchhhhhhhhhhhhhhh.... Bertha.... I can't breathe.... BD: Will you please not eat while you're announcing? Hurry up and swallow, or just shut up. MW: I'm...cho....king..... BD: I don't care what you're joking about, just try to show a little consideration. LB: Fritigern has Wrangler on the outside and rams his head into the ring post-- NOW HE SLAMS HIM ONTO THE FLOOR! Athanaric is on the outside, now, too. Brown is down, I don't know what happened to him-- BD: This is MY job! Athanaric is sliding Brown back into the ring under the bottom rope, and he's following him in. Fritigern is coming this way, though! LB: What does he want? Fritigern: (leaning into Bertha's face and shouting into her microphone) ARGHH! BD: What is he saying? MW: (still choking) Acchhhhhhhh.... BD: Stop that, Wilson; I'm listening to Fritigern here! (Fritigern heads over to Big Daddy and sits on the table in front of him, and barks into his face) Fritigern: ARGH! (points to the ring) LB: Here comes the other one; he's grabbing his partner and pointing back in the ring. Good, get him out of here. I don't know what he was eating before, but his breath stinks like Satan's unwashed armpit. BD: It looks like Athanaric is trying to get Fritigern to go back in the ring, but he won't budge-- and the ref is counting. 1...2...3...4...5...6... LB: And they're STILL arguing! 7...8...9...10! (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here are you winners, the team of PARKER BROWN AND JOEY WRANGLER! BD: WHAT THE HELL? The Horde are still arguing out there, and they're not even paying attention. I don't think either of them even understands that they were in a match here tonight. This NEVER would have happened if Kevin Larkin were around. Where is he anyway? LB: Someone said he was in a special training session to get ready for Kickoff '97. BD: What? He went to McDonalds and didn't ask if we wanted anything? LB: He was getting me some of those McDonaldland cookies. BD: Damn, I love those. Anyway, "Jumping" Joey Wrangler and Parker Brown SOMEHOW managed to beat the Horde by countout, who are finally leaving. And-- hey! He's choking! LB: Who? BD: Wilson! LB: Oh, yeah. I knew that. BD: And you didn't do anything to save him? He owes me $5! LB: Well, I was busy calling the match! I'm too well-paid for my work here to just drop it every time someone starts choking death. BD: Give him the Heimlich! LB: I'm not licking anything of his! BD: Well, I'm not either! LB: Fine, then he can just die and it'll be all your fault. BD: Fine! See if I care! LB: Fine! If you're going to get this upset every time someone starts turning blue, I'm going to leave. I don't need to sit here and listen to you scream at me while people are dying next to me. I don't need this kind of stress. (Bertha gets up and walks away. As she leaves, she removes her headphone, and the cord goes slack.) MW: (panting and wheezing) Finally.... I can.... breathe.... BD: Oh, cut that out. MW: I really couldn't breathe! BD: Only for a few minutes. Don't be such a baby about it. MW: Without air, you can't get oxygen.... to the brain.... so brain cells.... start to die... it only takes.... a few.... seconds. BD: Happens to me all the time, and look at me! MW: Oh, dear God, no! It's time for next match, anyway. Penance is about to defend his Light Heavyweight title against the Holy Ghost.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is scheduled for one fall, and is for the AEWA Light Heavyweight title! Introducing first, the challenger, weighing in at 220 pounds, please welcome the HOLY GHOST! ("Amazing Grace" begins playing loudly, and the Holy Ghost makes his entrance to a BIG round of boos. He is wearing his traditional white mask, and looks VERY angry.) MW: He's in great condition tonight, but could he POSSIBLY be ready for the "Final Judgment" piledriver? BD: Unfortunately, I don't think so. No one has EVER gotten up after that move, so the Holy Ghost probably won't be any different. . MW: Well, here comes the champ! RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, coming down the aisle, at 223 pounds, the AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion of the World, PENANCE! (Skid Row's "My Enemy" plays as Penance walks down the aisle. He is dressed totally in black, with the championship belt strapped around his waist. The audience boos him heavily.) BD: Penance has been involved in some GREAT matches lately, folks. It's really too bad that he's with the ICU. MW: And there's the bell! Penance goes right to work on the Holy Ghost, leveling him with a roundhouse right! He's not wasting any time in there, Big Daddy. BD: Penance is one tough customer, that's for sure. He whips the Holy Ghost into the corner, and NAILS him with a running clothesline! For such a little guy, Penance is one of the most powerful wrestlers in the world today. MW: I agree. Now Penance lifts The Holy Ghost onto the turnbuckle-- it looks like he's setting him up for a superplex! BD: But the Holy Ghost stuns him with a BIG uppercut, and Penance falls backwards off the top rope! That came out of NOWHERE! MW: And now the Ghost come on him with a BIG splash! He pulls the tights, and goes for the cover! One......Two...Kickout! BD: That a close one, folks. The Holy Ghost ALMOST pulled off an easy victory there, but he just wasn't able to get the pinfall. MW: And now he lifts Penance onto his shoulder, and NAILS him with a running powerslam! That had a lot of impact, folks. Here's another cover! One....Two....Kickout! BD: Jeez, Penance isn't doing too well tonight, is he? The Ghost just got ANOTHER close cover on him-- I'm shocked! MW: So am I, Big Daddy. And now the Holy Ghost scoops Penance up, and SLAMS him down HARD to the mat! He drops a BIG elbow on Penance, and SLAPS him in the face! This is great! BD: I changed my mind-- we're going to see a new Light Heavyweight champion crowned tonight. The ICU is going down! MW: I'd like to see it too, Big Daddy, but don't get too excited. Penance has come from behind in many of his matches, so don't count him out yet. Now the Ghost has just locked him in a Surfboard Submission, and Penance is screaming in pain! BD: Man, that looks like it hurts. MW: Did you JUST figure that out, Big Daddy? The Holy Ghost lets go of the hold, and pulls Penance to his feet. He whips him into the ropes, and takes him down with a back body drop! BD: Jeez, Penance flew high in the air, and hit HARD on the mat. He's gonna get some frequent flyer miles for that. MW: The Holy Ghost pulls Penance to his feet-- AND HITS HIM WITH A BIG NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX! One.....Two.....Kickout! BD: VERY close. MW: The Holy Ghost whips Penance to the ropes-- but he ducks his head too early, and Penance ROCKS him with a kick to the head! AND NOW PENANCE TAKES HIM DOWN WITH A DDT! BD: Oh no; it looks like the beginning of the end for the Holy Ghost! We could be seeing the Final Judgment piledriver soon, folks. MW: I sure hope not. Penance takes him over with a BIG snap suplex, and he hooks the leg! Here's the cover! One....Two...Kickout! MW: Penance appears to be frustrated, folks. He picks the Holy Ghost back up, and SLAMS him down with a Soviet Suplex! SLAMS the Holy Ghost down with a Soviet Suplex! And now he's going to the top turnbuckle! BD: Penance is a MASTER of aerial moves; this is where he's at his best. This could be "lights out" for the Ghost, folks. MW: And Penance comes down HARD on the Holy Ghost with a BIG Moonsault! That was absolutely INCREDIBLE-- here's the cover! One....Kickout! BD: The Holy Ghost appears to be making a comeback; he got up after only a one count! MW: Penance goes for a back suplex, but the Holy Ghost counters with a cross body! Here's the cover.... One....Two..Kickout! MW: And Penance is first to his feet. He picks up the Holy Ghost, slaps him, AND THROWS HIM OUT OF THE RING! BD: That's NOT a safe place to be, folks. MW: You can say that again, Big Daddy! Penance jumps over the ropes, and comes down hard on the Holy Ghost with a somersault plancha to the concrete! That did PLENTY of damage. BD: Well, the Ghost appears to be unconscious. He hit his head on the steel railing on the way down, so this match might end right here. MW: And now Penance quickly rolls the Ghost back into the ring. He SPITS on him, and drops a BIG elbow! But why isn't he going for the cover? BD: The match could be over right here, but Penance doesn't seem satisfied. He's really just adding insult to injury. MW: AND PENANCE GIVES THE SIGN FOR THE FINAL JUDGMENT PILEDRIVER! It doesn't look good for the Holy Ghost, folks. Penance scoops him up! BD: It's over-- NO ONE has ever kicked out of this move! MW: AND HE HITS IT! The Holy Ghost is totally out, and Penance goes for the cover. (sighs) Well, chalk another victory up the ICU.... One.....Two....Thr..Kickout! BD: WHAT THE HELL? He kicked out!? MW: HE KICKED OUT-- it's a miracle! I can't believe what just happened, and neither can Penance! BD: He's throwing a FIT in that ring, folks. This is GREAT! MW: Yeah, he's completely shocked! Now he pulls the Ghost to his feet, and goes for a right hand-- BUT IT'S BLOCKED! The Holy Ghost fires back with a right hand, and another, AND ANOTHER! BD: And he nails Penance with a dropkick! Where the hell did he find enough strength for a comeback like this? I'm amazed that the Holy Ghost even managed to kick out of that move, but now he actually might WIN! MW: He picks Penance up, and SLAMS him down with an atomic drop! He's moving in for the kill, folks. BD: He has COMPLETELY turned the tables on Penance; I really can't BELIEVE this! MW: And now the Holy Ghost takes Penance down again with a running clothesline! He's giving the sign for The Confirmation axe-handle, his finisher! BD: And he's going up top! Penance is getting back to his feet, but the Ghost dives at him-- AND CONNECTS WITH THE CONFIRMATION! MW: Penance is down and out, and the Holy Ghost is going for the cover! One.....Two.....Three!! (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner, and NEEEEW AEWA Light-Heavyweight Champion of the World, THE HOLY GHOST!!!! BD: After weeks of ICU domination, we FINALLY have a new Light Heavyweight champion! This is incredible! MW: The Holy Ghost actually appears to be smiling, folks, and I've NEVER seen him do that before. BD: Well, Penance is walking back to the locker room in defeat. He doesn't seem TOO disappointed, though-- I don't think Penance really CARES about titles.
