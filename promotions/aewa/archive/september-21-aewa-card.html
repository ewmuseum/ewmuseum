September 21 AEWA Card

2 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
September 21 AEWA Card by [name withheld] The AEWA showed up last night in Gund Arena in Cleveland, Ohio. My three friends (Brian, David, and Kevin) all took off and headed down to the show. Some traffic problems almost caused us to be late, but we arrived just as the first match started..... Match 1: Jeff "Wild Card" Hawkins (w/ 8-Ball) vs. Ben Slocum This was a great opening match-- except that I sat behind four LOUD "Wild Card" fans. I swear, if I hear, "How can someone so BAD be so damn GOOD!" one more time.... Slocum dominated the early part, until 8-Ball distracted the referee, giving Hawkins a chance to deliver a low blow. Hawkins then took over, and often threw Slocum out of the ring for 8-Ball to get in a few shots. Slocum recovered, but Hawkins shocked me by rolling Slocum up, without pulling the tights or the ropes. WINNER: Jeff "Wild Card" Hawkins by pinfall in 12:13 Match 2: The Machines vs. The Battalion (w/ Dr. IQ) The announcer came out; Simon O"Neal was unable to compete in the card, so a replacement would have to be found. Some of the other fans that Kevin talked to said that O'Neal was really ticked off at the AEWA brass, and hadn't shown up for several cards. I'm ticked becasue Simon O'Neal is a fellow Clevelander, and I wanted to see him live. So "Shotgun" Stan Crawford teamed with Paul Wong to take on the Battalion. Paul did not look happy about this situation. They did not work well together, and the Battallion dominated this match with effective double-teaming (including a double clothesline on Crawford that hurt MY neck). Despite being the smallest man in the ring by far, Lt. Lightning put on a hell of an air show, doing three somersault planchas in about a minute. Crawford and Wong battled back, but the Battlaion pulled it out with a top-rope sunset flip by Lightning onto Wong. WINNER: The Battallion by pinfall in 19:22 Match 3: Ryan "The Lion" Peterson (w/ Bootknife Kelley) vs. December Grey This was billed as a grudge match, after a botch tag team against the Fabulous Ones on TV. Both men went after each other from the bell, and spent more time outside the ring than inside it. At one point, Grey December put about eight different forms of suplexes on Peterson (David counted). Peterson fought back, and put a devastating bulldog on December onto the concrete floor. It looked like Grey won the match with a running powerbomb, but Peterson was too close to the ropes, and Bootknife pulled him out of the ring. Grey followed, and both men were counted out. WINNER: Draw (Double-Countout) at 9:50 Match 4: TV Title Defense: "Bullywug" Glenn Hudson vs. Kelly Maloney The announcer came out and announced that Kelly Maloney recently retired. The Maloneys have been a long-time favorite of mine, so I hated hearing that. Bullywug entered to a friendly response and was being interviewed by WKNR Sports Talk Show Host Greg Brenda when Jeff "Wild Card" Hudson came out and demanded a Television Title Shot. He started insulting Kelly Maloney, Bullywug, even Australia. Finally, Hudson had enough, and accepted the challenge. Hawkins entered thering, and both men immediately began brawling. Hawkins was still winded from his earlier match, but 8-Ball came down and helped out Hawkins when the ref wasn't looking. It was a very good match, but I had to suffer through the "Wild Card" fans again. Near the end, Hawkins missed a splash, and Hudson climbed up to the top turnbuckle. 8-Ball gave a cheap shot and stopped Hudson, giving Hawkins a chance to climb up and set up for a superplex. Ben Slocum came down and started attacking 8-Ball, Hudson pushed Hawkins off the turnbuckles, then leaped off and delivered a "No Hard Feelings" DDT. A three count, and Bullywug retained the title. WINNER: "Bullywug" Glenn Hudson by pinfall in 16:47 Match 5: Unified Tag Team Title Defense: The West Coast Connection (w/ Hood Lum) vs. The Assassins Both teams came out to huge cheers from the fans. The WCC kept trying to make it into a brawl, while the Assassins kept pulling back into a scientific match. At about the ten minute point, a masked wrestler came out and jumped Hood Lum, using a pair of brass knuckles to knock him out. The WCC left the ring and chased him away. At the end of the aisle, the man removed his mask.... and was Simon O'Neal! I guess he wasn't sick after all. Hood Lum was taken to the back, and the match continued. The Assassins spent the entire match working over Axl Sharkman's leg, and nearly had him when Banshee slapped on a figure-four leglock (I SWEAR the referee counted three, but I'm the only one). Axl finally made the tag, and MC Read just started hammering on the smaller Banshee. I think Banshee was busted open under his mask. Finally, Banshee regained control, and tried to go for a standing moonsault, but MC Read caught him for a powerbomb and the win. WINNER: The West Coast Connection in 24:08 Match 6: Twisted Steel vs. Mister Crowley A short, vicious brawl by both men. A bunch of UWF fans on the other side kept jeering Steel, who offered to take them all on at once. (Security prevented that). Steel and Crowley just went after each other before, during, and after the bell rang. I saw nothing but a flurry of punches, kicks, and elbows as both men brawled everywhere. Once they got outside the ring, a chair was introduced, and both men slammed the chair into each other's head. Mister Crowley lost his sunglasses at one point (I think they broke from the shot in the face by the chair). The referee never had control of this one, and the one time he tried to stop the brawl (Crowley was choking Twisted Steel under the ropes), Mister Crowley just stood up and blasted the referee, earning a disqualification. This was a short, brutal match. WINNER: Twisted Steel by Disqualifcation in 7:33 Match 7: Light Heavyweight Title match: Fuse vs. "Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi Valachi came out first, and started blasting Cleveland, the fans, the Indians.... about the time he started praising Art Modell, I was ready to start lynching the little punk. Fuse snuck up behing him, and brained him with the belt, causing the largest cheer from the crowd, although the UWF fans from the other side started a "Pride" chant at one point in the match. Fuse really came on strong during the early part, with a series of slams and hiptosses. Both men then started going outside the ring, and were fighting on the ring apron. Valachi grabbed Fuse's head, and dropped him with a Diamond Cutter to the floor. Valachi took control for a while after that, nailing him with frankensteiners, planchas, and a shooting star press. A missed Heartstopper gave Fuse a chance to recover. The referee reached seven before Valachi moved.... right into a small package for a three count. Fuse retained the belt. Afterwards, Valachi pulled something out of his trunks, and started pounding Fuse with it. Then he nailed two Heartstopper flying kneedrops before "Bullywug" Glenn Hudson and "Crimson" Joe Reed arrived to help out. Fuse was bleeding badly, and needed help walking back to the locker room. Match 8: Main Event, for the AEWA World's Heavyweight Title: "Crimson" Joe Reed vs. Wild Thing Cleveland Cavaliers Coach Mike Fratello was the special guest ring announcer, and he was really into the job (he announced Wild Thing as "The man who inspires Dennis Rodman" and said that Crimson Joe would be the Cav's new Power Forward). Wild Thing came out, to a huge chorus of boos. Then Crimson Joe came out to thunderous applause (although I was applauding the Lady in Red more than him). This match barely got underway (not even three minutes) when both men were outside the ring, struggling over a chair. The referee came down to grab the chair from both men, and was accidentally hit. He called for the bell and a double-disqualification. Everyone in the audience was PI$$ED that the main event was so short.... until Wild Thing took the microphone. He called Reed a coward, the AEWA a bunch of cowards, and said that he wasn't done yet and that Joe should keep fighting. Reed took the microhpone, and offered to make it a no-DQ match. Wild Thing accepted, and the match was restarted under no-DQ Rules. This time they lasted almost one minute before spilling out into the audience. Wild Thing look like he took three nasty shots in the face from the fans, before security could pull them away from the audience. Wild Thing then managed to launch himself right off the guardrail into Reed. He then took a chair, and DDT'd Reed onto it. Wild Thing then entered the ring, flew off the ropes, and dived right onto Reed. He probably would have gotten a three count, except that the ref told him he could only pin Reed in the ring. Thing then rolled him into the ring for a 2.99 count. After beating on Reed some more, Wild Thing brought a table into the ring. He lift positioned Reed to piledrive him on the table, but Reed countered with a backdrop that took Wild Thing off the table onto the mat; he must have fallen seven or eight feet! Reed then powerslammed Wild Thing onto the table, climbed the turnbuckles, and delivered a legdrop onto Wild Thing, almost breaking the table. Tucker Thomas showed up, but Mike Fratello stood up, and they started arguing (I loved this). While Fratello and Thomas were arguing, the Lady in Red grabbed another chair and waffled Thomas with it, sending him to dreamland. Meanwhile, Reed suplexed Wild Thing off the table, and went for a 2.99 count of his own. A missed splash put Wild Thing back in control. The match went back and forth for almost thrity minutes. Finally, Wild Thing hit a Party Time Splash that went for a 2.9999 count, but Reed lifted his shoulder at the last second. Still, Wild Thing thought he had won the title, and started showing off to the crowd. By the time he realized that the bell hadn't rung, Reed had gotten a steel chair from his manager, and blasted Wild Thing with it. Then he choked Wild Thing with the chair until he was unconscious, and went for the three-count. WINNER: "Crimson" Joe Reed by pinfall in 34:02 Overall, I liked the card. I hated the no-shows, as Kelly Maloney is one of my favorites. I heard the AEWA may be back in the Cleveland area very soon, and plan on going to that show. Thank you for paying attention. [name withheld]
