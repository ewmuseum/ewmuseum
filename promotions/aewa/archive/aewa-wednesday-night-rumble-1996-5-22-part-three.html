Wednesday Night Rumble (5/22, part three)

25 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SMITH: I know that you two are anxious to get this one underway, but I've got a little something I'd like to say first. Now you two are a couple of the most vile, vicious rulebreakers that we have here in the AEWA, and tonight you shall clash for the coveted TV Title. I can't just stand by and watch this happen, and I'm gonna do something about it! LB: What's he gonna do, strip the shot from Primal Fear and give it to one of his personal favorites? That's just like President Smith! What a moron he is! Primal Fear EARNED this shot! SMITH: Now I'm gonna let ya both fight for this belt, but I've got a special stipulation to add. It will be..... A CASKET MATCH! (The lights dim, and bells ring, as Caretaker emerges from the back, wheeling a casket to ringside. He puts it next to the ring, and leaves it there, as he goes back to the dressing room and the lights come back on. During this whole sequence, both the Punisher and Primal Fear are cowering in terror.) LB: He can't do that! MW: Well he just did, and now we've got a casket match for the TV title! Remember fans, to win this match, you have to place your opponent into the casket, and close it. The match will not end until someone is closed inside that casket the Caretaker was kind enough to provide. LB: Kind enough? It's a casket for crying out loud! MW: But it's his, and he's letting Punisher and Primal Fear use it. LB: They don't seem to appreciate his "kindness". I wonder why? MW: Couldn't tell ya, but we're underway. Punisher charges at Primal Fear, and Primal Fear catches him with a hiptoss right out of the ring! And now Primal Fear steps through the ropes, and DIVES at Punisher on the outside--BUT PUNISHER MOVES! LB: And the big man went SPLAT on the concrete! MW: The Punisher picks him up, and WHIPS him to the steel guardrail! And Punisher grabs Primal Fear by the hair, and he's trying to ram his head into the guardrail--BUT PRIMAL FEAR BLOCKS IT, and rams PUNISHER'S head into that guardrail! And now Primal Fear grabs him, and whips him INTO THE SIDE OF THE CASKET! LB: Punisher does not want to be there! MW: Probably not. He lifts his head, sees where he is, and LOOK AT THAT LOOK OF TERROR ON HIS FACE! He QUICKLY scrambles into the ring! LB: Quick thinking there by the Punisher. MW: More like frightened to death thinking! Primal Fear follows him into the ring, AND IS MET WITH A CLOTHESLINE! Now Punisher whips him to the ropes, on the comeback, BACKDROP OVER THE TOP ROPE AND INTO THE CASKET! LB: Primal Fear sees where he is, and he BOLTS outta there before Punisher can close the lid! I've NEVER seen the big guy move that fast! MW: Me neither! Primal Fear scrambles back into the ring, and LEVELS Punisher with a clothesline! Primal Fear whips him to the ropes, AND MISS DEMEANOR PULLS THEM DOWN! Punisher falls out and into the casket! LB: Now it's the Punisher inside there, Man that's gotta be scary! MW: Primal Fear runs over, and tries to close the lid, BUT BOOTKNIFE KELLY BRACES IT OPEN WITH A CHAIR! And Punisher is able to escape! Primal Fear is upset! LB: And he has every right to be, but that's too damn bad. MW: Now Punisher is consulting with Kelly. Kelly reaches into his jacket, and hands a brown paper bag to Punisher. What's in there? LB: His lunch? MW: Somehow, I doubt it. Punisher climbs back into that ring, and he reaches into that bag, and pulls out a BIG, JUICY, BLOODY, RAW STEAK! Primal Fear is watering at the mouth! His eyes are completely focused on that steak! LB: It's like he doesn't see ANYTHING else! MW: Like a bull, he charges at it! AND PUNISHER TOSSES IT INTO THE CASKET! LB: AND PRIMAL FEAR JUMPS IN AFTER IT! MW: And Punisher SLAMS that lid shut! It's over! (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner, and STILL AEWA Television Champion... THE PUNISHER!!!!!!!!! LB: What a GREAT idea! Why didn't I think of that? MW: That's because even you aren't as devious as Bootknife Kelly. Even though he did nothing illegal, because there are no DQ's or Countouts, that was still pretty sneaky. LB: It got the job done didn't it? MW: I suppose it did, but... LB: But nothing, he's brilliant I tell ya! MW: Whatever, let's just move on. LB: What do we got now? MW: Skim Hoss is about to interview Mister Crowley AND President Smith, at the same time.... Buckle your seat belt, Bertha! LB: (laughing) This should be a wild ride-- those two have had PLENTY of problems before! MW: Yeah.... I hope there isn't any trouble tonight. Crowley stormed into the AEWA Headquarters this morning, making some BIG demands. Maybe we'll hear more about that? LB: Who cares? Give me blood! MW: Quit trying to start trouble, Bertha. We don't need any more of it around here! (Skim Hoss is standing in the interview area with President Smith) Hoss: Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome at this time President Smith! (polite applause) And my other guest at this time - from the Dark Side, Mister Crowley! (Lights instantly go off and a few bongs are heard before the eerie organ intro of "Mister Crowley" by Ozzy Osbourne plays) LB: Yay! Here's my guy! MW: One word , Bertha - sick. LB: Hmmf. (Crowd is booing like hell, except for the hardcore fans who are chanting "Rest In Peace, Smith, Rest In Peace!". Crowley finally reaches the podium and raises the lights) Hoss: (clearly a bit unnerved by the enormous Crowley, who is glaring at President Smith) Uh... yeah. Gentlemen, thank you for being here. Smith: Okay, Crowley, you wanted me out here, I'm here. Say what you have to say. Crowley: (speaks not so much into the microphone, but his voice still seems to come from everywhere at once) Many are the sins of man. I am sprung from the darkest depths of man's soul, the birthing ground of sin. I am he who takes the souls of the damned to their just retribution. Smith: Yeah, yeah. Your point being? Crowley: I shall be brief, then. I have not yet received a rematch for my World title. Others have come before me... even he who I saved you from-- Cardinal Chaos. (pauses) In just a few moments, Phil Grand will also be getting a chance at the gold. Has the AEWA forgotten about me? If I do not receive a rematch -- tonight -- I shall leave the AEWA forever...... Smith: (Crowd is half booing, half cheering) Well then, by all means, let me be the first to tell you -- YOU'LL NEVER GET A F*CKING REMATCH NOW! (Crowd goes ballistic. Crowley raises his right hand, as if to strike President Smith - but then simply draws his thumb across his throat - and the lights go out with an accompanying "BONG". When the lights come back up, Hoss and Smith are alone on the podium) LB: Did I just hear President Smith say "the f-word" on national television? I hope we have some censors working tonight! MW: That was totally out of control.... Why did Crowley even EXPECT to get a title shot tonight? Phil Grand is about to wrestle! LB: With all due respect to "the Specialist", he really doesn't deserve this match. MW: But neither does Mister Crowley! (yelling) What has HE done lately? Nothing! LB: (yelling) Are you kidding me? He's held that stupid belt over THREE times, and now everyone is ignoring him! MW: Crowley will probably eventually get a title shot, despite what President Smith just said. But it WON'T be happening tonight! LB: So is it time for the main event yet? MW: Yeah.... As you know, Volcano Jackson is defending his World title for the first time against "The Specialist" Phil Grand. LB: And he's gonna lose it too! Grand hasn't forgotten when Jackson beat him in that Philadelphia Street Fight quite a while back. MW: Yes, this feud has been on the back burner for quite a while, but I don't think there's any love lost here. These two HATE each other, and that should make for a VERY interesting match. Let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA Heavyweight Championship of the World. Introducing first, the challenger, weighing in at 275 pounds, "The Specialist" Phil Graaaaaaaaaaaaaand! (Judas Priest's "Turbo" plays as the Specialist makes his entrance to a lot of boos from the crowd. He is wearing a blue robe with "The Specialist" written on the back.) MW: Here comes the challenger. LB: Soon to be champion. MW: Maybe. LB: Count on it! RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 290 pounds and accompanied by his manager, Aaron Proctor, He is the AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, Volcanoooooooooooooooo Jackson! (Queen's "Another One Bites the Dust" plays over the PA as the champ makes his entrance, and the crowd goes WILD. He proudly holds the World Title high over his head as he makes his way to the ring. Aaron Proctor is at his side.) MW: My does it look good to see that man with the belt. He REALLY deserves it. LB: No he doesn't, he beat a guy with a submission hold, but the guy didn't submit! Chaos should still be the champ! MW: Please tell me that we're not gonna get into that again. LB: We'll get into whatever the heck I wanna talk about! MW: Okay Bertha, chill out. We're about to get started anyway. They lock up, and Grand whips Jackson to the turnbuckle, AND INTO THE REFEREE! Grand charges into the corner, AND JACKSON MOVES! Grand hits the turnbuckle hard! He comes back out, AND JACKSON CATCHES HIM IN A GERMAN SUPLEX! LB: But the ref is still out, there's no count! I love this! FINALLY it happens to a guy I don't like, instead of the other way around! MW: Jackson gets up, and delivers a headbutt to Grand! And our ref is back to his senses! Jackson grabs Grand with a CHOKEHOLD! I told you these guys hated each other! LB: No fair, he's cheating. MW: And the ref is stopping him, so get over it. Jackson grabs him, he's going for ANOTHER German Suplex--BUT GRAND BREAKS OUT WITH AN ELBOWSMASH! He jumps over Jackson, AND TAKES HIM DOWN WITH A SUNSET FLIP! One..Two..Kickout! LB: This is where Grand starts to take it! MW: Now Grand is going for a belly-to-back suplex--BUT NOW JACKSON BREAKS OUT WITH AN ELBOWSMASH! He takes Grand down with a short lariat! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! MW: Now Jackson takes Grand in ANOTHER chokehold! LB: Come on ref! 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. MW: What are you doing? LB: I'm showing you, the ref could have broken that hold, but he's intimidated by this big stupid dummy. MW: Well, why don't you go down there and referee? LB: I'm needed here. MW: That's what I thought. Jackson releases the choke, and CHUCKS Grand over the top ropes! He waits for him to get back in the ring, AND KICKS HIM IN THE HEAD--NO!!! GRAND DUCKS! Grand runs, off the ropes, FLYING FOREARM! IT CONNECTS! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: He's almost there, I can FEEL it! MW: Grand grabs him, POWER BOMB!--BUT JACKSON TURNS IT INTO A BACKDROP-- AND GRAND TURNS IT INTO A SUNSET FLIP! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: Told you, Jackson couldn't take control if it tried, Grand is just that good! MW: Grand gets up, and takes Jackson over with a fireman's carry! He waits for Jackson to get to his feet, grabs him, GERMAN SUPLEX! The count! One..Kickout! LB: How did he kick out at one? MW: I don't know, Jackson appears to be having a resurgence here, and he whips Grand to the ropes! Grand comes back, jumps up on the shoulders of Jackson, AND TAKES HIM DOWN WITH A CRUCIFIX! One..Two..Kickout! MW: Grand gets up, and quickly whips Jackson to the ropes, FLYING FOREARM! ANOTHER COVER! One..Kickout! LB: He kicked out at one again! MW: Yes, and that REALLY upset Grand! He gets up, SPINNING ELBOWDROP! And now he picks him up, DRAGON SUPLEX! That's his finisher! The cover! One..Two..Thr...Kickout! LB: That was three! MW: No it wasn't, Jackson kicked out! Grand looks ready to KILL Jackson now, he picks him up, DOUBLE UNDERHOOK SUPLEX--NO! JACKSON BLOCKS IT! He runs to the ropes, and takes Grand down with shoulderblock! He picks him up, GERMAN SUPLEX! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: No this match is over, Grand had him last time, Proctor paid off the ref! MW: Will you just give it a rest? Jackson grabs him in a front facelock--BUT GRAND TURNS IT INTO A BACKDROP! Grand runs, and takes Jackson OUT with ANOTHER flying forearm! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: How does he keep doing that? MW: It's called stamina, perhaps you've heard of it? Grand whips Jackson to the ropes, but Jackson reverses it! On the comeback, MONSTER CLOTHESLINE BY JACKSON! The cover! One..Kickout! LB: HA! Now Jackson's got Grand kicking out of his stuff at one! MW: Yes, and Jackson is getting upset too! Grand gets to his feet, AND GETS NAILED WITH A SPINNING MULE KICK! JACKSON COVERS HIM! One..Kickout! LB: Again! I love it! MW: Now Jackson looks REALLY mad! He picks Grand up, PILEDRIVER! Jackson picks him up, and places him in the corner. Now Jackson charges in, AVALANCHE! Jackson whips him to the opposite corner--BUT GRAND REVERSES IT! LB: Just Look at how pitiful Jackson is! MW: Jackson to the buckle, Grand follows him in, BUT JACKSON MOVES, AND GRAND CRASHES INTO THE CORNER! Now Jackson charges in for an avalanche--BUT GRAND LIFTS HIS LEG, AND JACKSON RUNS RIGHT INTO IT! Grand grabs him, DRAGON SUPLEX AGAIN! The cover! One..Two..Foot on the Ropes! LB: Aaron Proctor put it there! I saw it! MW: He did NOT! But Grand is UPSET! He's going for the Dragon Suplex AGAIN--BUT JACKSON STARTS PUNCHING HIM! AND HE PUNCHES HIS WAY OUT OF IT! LB: What is this? You can't use closed fists! MW: Well the ref is allowing it, and again, you're not the ref. Grand grabs him, POWER BOMB--NO!! JACKSON REVERSED IT INTO A BACKDROP! He turns around, grabs Grand, picks him up, PILEDRIVER! HE NAILED IT! He picks him up again, ERUPTION POWER BOMB! The cover! One..Two..Three!!!!! MW: He did it! RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner, and STILL AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, VOLCANO JACKSON!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Volcano Jackson poses for the screaming fans, and hurries off towards the locker room) LB: I can't believe that! He lost this match! Grand had him a million times! And that was a fast count! This is a rip-off! MW: Just shut up, Bertha. Man that felt good! No wonder you say it all the time. LB: Shut up Wilson. MW: So Volcano Jackson is successful in his first defense, and it couldn't have been better, what a great competitive matchup! LB: Except for the fact that Grand won! MW: He did NOT! Will you stop that? LB: No. MW: Okay fine, we're out of time anyway, so until next time this is Matt Wilson for the Lovely Bertha saying so long everybody! LB: (reads a note) Wait a minute! I just received some incredible news.... As Volcano Jackson was entering the locker room after his match, Mister Crowley attacked him! We're attempting to get some footage of the incident. MW: (yelling) Can we get a camera in the back-- this instant? What's going on? LB: Don't ask me, Wilson! How would I know? It appears that we have a crew back there now, so here we go.... This is taped footage of the incident that just occured several minutes ago. (We see Mister Crowley nailing Volcano Jackson with the Premature Burial) MW: Oh my gosh-- all hell has broken lose in that locker room! LB: Jackson will NEVER be able to wrestle again after that! He's gone forever.... MW: Don't bet the house on it, Bertha. (looks nervous) But it doesn't look very good right now. Some paramedics are currently trying to help him. LB: Jackson MIGHT not even be able to wrestle on Saturday! This is great! He has been ignoring Mister Crowley lately, so this incident HAD to happen! MW: After what just occurred, Crowley is almost guaranteed to get a title shot. (yelling) What a jerk! LB: Volcano Jackson is being placed on a stretcher right now, and they're taking him to the hospital. This scene seems to have calmed down a bit. MW: I hope he's okay....After all that damage, the situation is looking pretty grim. LB: (yelling) Oh my gosh! Mister Crowley just dumped Jackson off the stretcher, and he's attacking him again! I love it! MW: We don't have anymore TV time, so see you on Saturday everyone! (fade to black)
