AEWA One Year Anniversary Awards

10 December 1996

The AEWA One Year Anniversary Award Special is Coming!
*************************************************************************

The end of January will mark one year of AEWA action. Tommy Smith and [name withheld] met in the PEWA, one of countless feds which lasted less than 2 weeks. When Kevin offered to help assume match-writing responsibilities just before the PEWA folded, Tommy told him about an idea he had for a fed. The AEWA opened with a brief warm-up card, followed by the World and Tag Team Title tournaments, won by Mister Crowley and the NATO Powers respectively. Since then some things have changed, and some have stayed the same. As the roster swelled, new titles were added. A new president, Bob Freeman, took over, and new division heads joined in the summer and fall-- Henry Gehringer and Roy Stanley. We've seen scaffold matches and suspended ring matches, a Crowley-Chaos feud and a Crowley-Chaos tag team, World Champions win the TV Title and Light Heavyweight Champions win the Heavyweight Title. This is your chance to reward your peers (or yourselves) for work and accomplishments here in the first year of the AEWA, as the first-ever AEWA Achievement Awards nominating process begins.

Below, you will find a list of categories for which we would like to reward our best players and wrestlers. Everyone has 2 weeks to return to [name withheld] ([detail withheld]) as many nominees as they would like for each category.

To make Kevin's life just a little easier, please put "AEWA Awards" in the subject header of your nomination list. This will help him keep the nomination messages separate from the rest of his mail, making vote-tabulating just a tad easier.

Okay, here are the categories:
Wrestler of the Year
Tag Team of the Year
Manager of the Year
Match of the Year
Feud of the Year
Pay Per View of the Year
Best AEWA Program
Most Improved Wrestler (or Tag Team)
Best-Roleplayed Character
Biggest Surprise

Don't forget- you have 2 weeks to get your nominees in. Thanks, and good luck!
