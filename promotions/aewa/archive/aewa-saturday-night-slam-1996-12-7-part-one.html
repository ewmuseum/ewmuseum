Saturday Night Slam (12/7, part one)

8 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen, set on fire. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power Bomb, and a look at Mister Crowley DRIVING the head of some guy into the concrete with the Premature Burial! Then the camera switches to the Lovely Bertha, Matt Wilson, and "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt in a packed arena) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Saturday Night Slam! We have plenty of GREAT matches scheduled for tonight, and-- BD: (interrupting him) Quit your damn babbling, Wilson. There's only one big thrill in tonight's card, and you've already seen it.... I'M BACK! That's right, folks, after several weeks away from the booth, I've finally returned. LB: I'm not exactly thrilled to see you, Big Daddy. BD: Frankly, dear, I don't give a damn. MW: Well Big Daddy, it's nice to have you back. I might not always agree with you, but things were getting pretty boring around here during your absence. I just have one question-- are you going to wrestle again? BD: My arm got hurt pretty badly during that last match, so I doubt it. I think it's FINALLY time for me to hang up the tights.... LB: Don't expect ME to shed any tears. MW: Hey, can you two PLEASE not argue? Let's just hurry up and get this show on the road! Up first, we have a very special interview with "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews, the World Heavyweight champion. BD: I'm really proud of that guy. He's the most exciting wrestler in the AEWA today, and he has the World title to prove it. MW: Well, he WOULDN'T be champion if Scorpion hadn't been at ringside during the battle royal. That match was a travesty of justice. LB: Were you watching a different show, Wilson? Why can't you just tell the TRUTH? Shane Matthews didn't cheat ONCE. MW: (rolls his eyes) Yeah right. Anyway, let's just watch the interview. This was taped several days ago.... --------------- (The scene opens on the AEWA Locker room. Standing there is AEWA manager Johnny Terror. He has a HUGE grin on his face. He begins to speak.) Terror: Well, it seems my men have been having a bit of luck lately. They finally got some freaking matches, and just like I knew they would, they REALLY kicked some ass! (Johnny's grin gets even wider.) Let's start with my tag team, The Italian Loan Collection Agency. (As Johnny says this, "Machine" Gun Ciraulo and "Big" Antonio Sabatini walk in and stand behind him. They are wearing their standard black suit, white shirt, white tie, sunglasses, and black hat.) Terror: Now these guys FINALLY got their chance to wrestle after MONTHS of being in the AEWA, and they sure didn't let me down! Ciraulo: We ALWAYS do our job, Johnny. Valachi finally got what was coming to him, and we finished off Lopez too. They were just pathetic. Sabatini: They sure were. I didn't even break a sweat! Now this is TOO easy! I'm TIRED of beatin' the livin' daylights outta Valachi! I want some REAL competition! Ciraulo: Same here, big man. Valachi, we're sick of you, so just get out of our face now, and we'll just forget about when you double-crossed us a while ago. As of right now, you're paid in full. But the rest of the AEWA had better watch out, because now that we're done with Valachi, we're coming after YOU! Sabatini: Yes, we've wasted too much time torturing one man, now we're ready to unleash our tactics on the ENTIRE AEWA, and it sure ain't gonna be pretty! Terror: That's for damn sure! The tag team division is about to experience a whole new kinda pain. A pain by the name of The Italian Loan Collection Agency! But enough about them, we've got other matters to discuss here tonight; the AEWA World Heavyweight Title falling into the Realm of Terror! That's right everybody, here's your NEW AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, The man they call "Sudden Death", Shane Matthews! (Matthews walks into view, and stands next to Johnny. The ILCA still stand silently behind them. Shane is wearing a black leather jacket, and has the AEWA World Heavyweight Title draped over his shoulder.) Matthews: Hey everybody! Lookie what I got! (Shane points to the strap) Yeah, that's right, the World HEAVYWEIGHT Title! Nobody thought I could do it, but I did, so now you can all just kiss my rosy red ass! They were saying, "Oh, but Shane, you don't weigh enough! You'll get killed!" Well, BULL F*CKING SH*T! I've been telling ya since day ONE that it was only a matter of time before the ENTIRE AEWA fell into a state of Sudden Death. Well guess what? That day has ARRIVED! I went through TWENTY-FIVE other guys--who quite frankly, all sucked--and I came out on top! That's right, little 'ole Shane was the only one left standing! Can you believe that? You'd BETTER, or else I might have to kick YOUR ass too! Terror: And now that Shane has the title, EVERYONE is on our hit list! We're just gonna let the Championship Committee set them up, and Shane's gonna knock 'em down! It's just that simple! Matthews: That's right. Anybody wants a shot at this puppy, you know where to find me. Just step right up, and get ready to receive the worst ass kicking of your life! Hell, they're ALREADY lining up to take a shot at me. It seems I got Randall Osbourne a little pissed at me. Well too F*CKING bad! I was in the mood to do some ass kicking, and you just happened to be the one in the ring. It's not MY fault that you fight like a little girl. If you want a piece of me, just get in line, and when it's your turn to face me, I'll show you what a REAL beating is like! Another guy who apparently wants at me is "Snap into a Slim" Jim Coolridge! Well, boy, I don't see what you've ever done to deserve a shot at this, but if you're really that anxious to get your head handed to you, I'm not going anywhere, so step right up-- and experience Sudden Death first hand. I can guarantee you'll be cryin' for your momma before the night's over. Terror: And now Raider's challenging you to a match too! Matthews: He is? Gee, EVERYBODY wants a shot at Sudden Death! Well Raider, ya fat slob, if you're not too busy defending that piece of tin foil that YOU got, feel free to get in line with everybody else! I don't make the matches, President Freeman does, and if you can get him to put us in a ring together, I can PROMISE you that you'll be in for the ass whooping of your life! It's gonna be the "End of the Road" for Raider Time! Terror: Not even the Church could save him! Shane Matthews is gonna be YOUR AEWA Heavyweight Champion for quite some time, so you'd better get used to it! But we're not done! A fourth piece has been added to the the puzzle; you could call us a genuine stable now! Now meet the newest member of my "Ring of Terror", SCORPION! (Scorpion walks into the room and takes his place at the other side of Johnny Terror. He wears his mask and traditional wrestling attire. He shakes hands with all four men, and begins to speak.) Scorpion: So now the mystery is solved! I have joined with Johnny Terror, and helped Shane Matthews win the AEWA World Title! Now all the little kiddies are probably asking "Why, Scorpion, Why??" You wanna know why? BECAUSE I FELT LIKE IT! Now ever since I came here to the AEWA, I've preached about how much cheating and violence there was here, and how I was going to clean it up. Well now I realize WHY there is so much cheating and violence.... BECAUSE IT'S THE ONLY WAY YOU GET ANYWHERE IN THIS DAMN FED! I was here for MONTHS, and I never broke a rule, and guess how many matches they gave me? TWO! TWO FREAKING MATCHES!! I wrestled to a draw with a man who went on to become world champion, and did I get any recognition for it? Did I get any upper echelon matches for it? Did I even get a re-match out of it? HELL NO I didn't!!! And all the while, guys like Mister Crowley and Deathwatch are getting title matches for attacking officials! Now does that make ANY sense at ALL? Now you might call me a traitor or a hypocrite, but the fact remains that NO ONE gets ANYWHERE in this federation by being nice! Believe me, I've tried. Now I've got big name stars like Heavy Metal practically BEGGING to fight me! THAT'S why I took Johnny's offer, because that's what was best for my career. If you got a problem with it, and apparently Heavy Metal does, then take it up with me in the ring, and prepare to be Stung! Terror: You said it all, my man. The AEWA will NOT look this man over any longer! The Scorpion will rise to the top; he is the final piece to the puzzle that forms the Ring of Terror! We shall wreak havoc throughout the AEWA. Why? Because nobody can stop us! Matthews: Not even federation barriers can stop us! Check this out! (Matthews opens his jacket to reveal a SECOND gold belt, which is strapped around his waist. It reads "TOWL World Heavyweight Champion".) I picked this baby up the other night, in a 32-man tourney to crown the very first TOWL Champion, and Shane Matthews doesn't settle for second place! Terror: No he doesn't! I was there, and I saw Perfect tell his second to throw in the towel when you locked him in that surfboard! He was practically BEGGING for mercy! Matthews: You're damn right he was, and just like that, Shane Matthews is the holder of TWO World Titles at the SAME TIME! And I ain't planning on giving EITHER of them up. Especially not to no pansy like American Ambush, who I gotta defend the AEWA Title against next. American Ambush will just be the next page in the LEGEND of "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews. You'd better kiss all your hopes and dreams goodbye, Ambush, and just worry about getting out of this one ALIVE! Terror: So anybody who's thinking about messing with Shane Matthews, or any of the rest of the Ring of Terror, you'd better think again-- because if you don't, it'll be the last mistake you ever make! (The scene fades to black, and the camera returns to our broadcast team.) --------------- BD: What a GREAT champion! Shane Matthews is truly an inspiration to us all, and I'm really happy to see him with the belt. LB: Me too. MW: He's a DISGRACE to this federation. He made a mockery of the battle royal, and now he's doing the same thing over in another league. I can't STAND him. BD: But you've NEVER liked him. I think it might have something to do with that time he kicked your butt. MW: (shrugs) Don't remind me.... LB: So what's up next, Wilson? MW: We've got a battle of newcomers, as Brian Rice takes on "Crimson" Joe Reed! BD: We saw Rice in that World Title Battle Royal, folks. He survived 'till the very end, and was the last guy eliminated by Shane Matthews. He's gotta be a really great wrestler to have done that in his first AEWA match. MW: He sure does, but Reed's no slouch himself, and has won several titles in other organizations. This should be a really great match-up. LB: It should, but I think Rice has got the advantage, especially with Jim Williams and his trusty crowbar on the outside. MW: Well Reed's got the Lady in Red, and she'll be looking to prevent any interference from Williams. She's no stranger to fighting herself, you know. BD: Maybe so, but she only has to take her eyes off him for one second for him to interfere. MW: Very true, so who knows WHAT'S gonna happen? The only way to find out is to go to the ring! RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, From Los Angeles, California, he weighs in at 345 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his manager, Jim Williams, here is Brrrriiiiiiiiiaaaan Riiiiiiiiiiiiiiice! (Coolio's "Gangsta's Paradise" booms out over the speaker as Rice makes his way toward the ring, to a mixed reaction from the crowd. He has brown hair and wears baggy jeans, no shirt, and blue boots. Jim Williams is at his side, wearing a white tuxedo and carrying a crowbar.) MW: Why is Williams allowed to carry that crowbar to the ring anyway? BD: Because he needs it! MW: For WHAT? BD: To scrap the competition off the mat after Rice is done with them. MW: I see. In that case, I don't think he'll be needing it tonight. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, From Lexington, Kentucky, he weighs in at 257 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his manager, The Lady in Red, "Crimson" Joe, Reeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeed! (Bad Company's (suprise) "Bad Company" plays loudly over the PA as Reed appears from behind the curtain, and receives a good sized pop from fans who recognize him from other federations. He has short red hair, and wears black shorts and a red jacket with the word "Crimson" written in script on the back. The Lady in Red is at his side; she is a tall, red-headed knockout, and she wears a long red dress.) BD: Would you LOOK at the Lady in Red! (You hear Big Daddy's tongue hitting the floor.) LB: Why would you wanna look at HER when I'M sitting right next to you? BD: Because she's MUCH hotter than you.... OWWW! What'd ya hit me for! We're not going out anymore! LB: That STILL doesn't give you the right to drool over other women in front of me! MW: BOTH of you just calm down! Big Daddy, you don't have a chance in hell with the Lady in Red, and Bertha, you know it! LB: Good point. BD: Just crush my ego, why don't ya? MW: It's too late for that. But we should start paying attention to what's in the ring, because we're just about to get underway. And we've got a lockup! Rice gets the upper hand, and whips Reed to the ropes! LB: Rice looks a lot stronger, he just POWERED his way through that lockup! MW: Yes he did, and now Reed comes off the ropes, WITH AN ELBOW--DUCKED BY RICE!! Reed comes back, CLOTHESLINE FROM RICE--DUCKED BY REED!! Reed comes back again WITH A CLOTHESLINE OF HIS OWN--DUCKED AGAIN BY RICE!! BD: Are they playing "Duck, Duck, Goose"? MW: Doubtful. Reed comes back again, AND GETS CAUGHT IN A SLEEPER BY RICE! LB: His lights are going out! MW: Not yet they're not; Reed just grabbed the ropes, and the ref is forcing the break, and Rice releases the hold. Reed turns to face him, and Rice catches him in an inside cradle! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Sneaky..... I like it. MW: It almost worked too, but Reed kicked out in time. Rice quickly goes up top, and Reed is struggling to his feet! Rice dives, FLYING BULLDOG!! And Rice is going for the cover! One..Two..Thr..Shoulder Up! LB: Reed is in trouble, big time! He almost lost already! MW: The key word is ALMOST. And now Rice is taking a time-out to discuss things with Williams. BD: Smart move, why not take a break when your opponent's unconscious? MW: Because you COULD be finishing him off. Rice comes back from his little conference, and lifts Reed to his feet! Rice picks him up now, BACK SUPLEX--NO!! Countered with a cross-body block from Reed! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Wow, you were actually right for once, Wilson! MW: Thanks, I think. Both men up now, and Reed grabs Rice by the arm, SHORT CLOTHESLINE--DUCKED BY RICE!! Reed turns around, and gets CAUGHT with a roundhouse right from Rice! And Rice follows it up with a forearm smash! BD: Rice got out there because he's still MUCH fresher than Reed. MW: Can't argue with that. Rice picks him up again now, GUTWRENCH SUPLEX! Reed tries desperately to get up, AND RICE TAKES HIM DOWN WITH AN INSIDE CRADLE! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Rice is REALLY in control now! MW: Reed just hasn't had time to recover, and Rice doesn't look like he's gonna let him. Rice picks Reed up, and whips him to the ropes--NO! REVERSAL BY REED! Rice comes back, and gets LEVELED with a clothesline! BD: I didn't think Reed still had that in him! MW: I've seen him wrestle before, you'd be surprised how much he's got in him. Rice tries to get up, and Reed NAILS him with a kick to the midsection! Reed lifts him up now, and hit a BIG kneebreaker! LB: This Reed's actually not half bad. It's a shame he's with that bimbo, or I might actually like him. MW: The Lady in Red is NOT a bimbo. Rice tries to get to his feet now, and Reed runs to the ropes! He comes back, and takes Rice's head off with a clothesline! Rice gets up again, and Reed nails him with a fist to the midsection! BD: Rice is in trouble; I think it's time for the crowbar. (yelling) Oh, Jim..... MW: Don't even THINK about it, Big Daddy. Reed RAKES the face of Rice now, and he picks him up, BRIDGING BACK SUPLEX!! One..Kickout! LB: Rice is far from done. MW: It would appear that way. Reed picks him up now, and NAILS him with a Russian legsweep! Rice is getting up now, Reed grabs him, SIDE SUPLEX--BLOCKED BY RICE! Rice picks REED up now, BACK SUPLEX--NO! Countered AGAIN with a cross-body block by Reed! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Reed just won't give up control. MW: Not if he can help it. Reed picks him up now, and NAILS him with a short clothesline! Reed is by the ropes now, and it looks like he's setting him up for the Kentucky Backsmasher! He lifts-BUT RICE BLOCKS IT, AND COUNTERS WITH A KICK TO THE THIGH! LB: Almost curtains there for Rice, but he found a way out! MW: Yes he did, but he hasn't quite recovered yet. Reed grabs Rice by the arm, and nails him with ANOTHER short clothesline! And Reed is setting him up for the Kentucky Backsmasher again! He's got him in position, BUT RICE COUNTERS WITH A VERTICAL SUPLEX! BD: Didn't the dummy learn the first time? MW: He thought he'd try his luck again, I guess. And it failed again. Reed is trying to get to his feet, and Rice quickly grabs him with a BACKSLIDE!! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Rice has gotta do something big to stay in control here! MW: Rice waits for Reed to get to his feet now, BACK HEEL KICK--BUT REED SIDE STEPS, and LEVELS Rice with a short clothesline! And Reed TAKES back control, he picks up Rice now, DDT--COUNTERED WITH A BACKDROP BY RICE--AND RE-COUNTERED WITH A SUNSET FLIP BY REED! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Rice just can't catch a break! MW: Not at the moment. Both men up, and Reed CHOPS away at Rice! He grabs Rice, and WHIPS him to the turnbuckle--REVERSED BY RICE! Rice follows him in, and AVALANCHES him into the corner! Reed STAGGERS out, and Rice catches him with and inside cradle! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Do you think he knows any OTHER pinning combinations? MW: Most likely; we already saw a backslide, but he's just had good chances to throw in the cradle, and he took them. Rice is arguing with the ref about the count now, but it's not doing any good, and he goes back to work on Reed.... He tries to put in a armlock leglock submission, BUT REED BLOCKS IT, and NAILS Rice with a fist to the midsection! BD: Now it looks like NEITHER guy can maintain control! MW: It sure does. Reed grabs Rice now, and NAILS him with a jumping DDT! Rice is in trouble! Reed picks him up again, DRAGON SUPLEX! One..Kickout! LB: BOY! Brian Rice must be made out of STONE! MW: He's giving that impression, but Reed's not letting up, either. Reed picks him up, and LEVELS him with a short clothesline! He's been using that to set up the Kentucky Backsmasher, AND HE'S GOING FOR IT AGAIN! He lifts Rice up, slingshots him off the ropes, and brings him CRASHING down!!! He NAILED it! The cover! One..Two..Thr..Shoulder Up! BD: How the hell did Rice kick out? This guy is AMAZING! MW: Well, he absorbed a lot of punishment the last time we saw him, and now he's doing it again! And Reed can't BELIEVE it, but he's going to work again! Reed lifts him to his feet, and NAILS him with a chop! And now Reed lifts the bigger man up, and PLANTS him with a piledriver! LB: Rice doesn't look like he can stand much more of this! MW: No, he doesn't. Reed lifts Rice to his feet again, and once again CHOPS away! Reed grabs him by the head now, JUMPING DDT!! BD: Rice is still hanging in there, though! MW: And now Reed lifts him up again, BEAUTIFUL DRAGON SUPLEX! One..Kickout! LB: What the HELL?? He's getting STRONGER? MW: I don't know, but Reed looks TOTALLY shocked! Rice tries to get up, but Reed nails him with a Russian Legsweep! Rice gets up again, and Reed DOUBLES HIM OVER with a fist to the midsection, and now grabs his head, JUMPING DDT! BD: I just can't believe this kid! MW: But he STILL hasn't escaped Reed's attack! Reed DROPS an elbow onto Rice, and punches him again in the midsection! And now Reed picks his opponent up, FALLAWAY SLAM! LB: He may be resisting defeat for all he's worth, but he's not gonna win like this! MW: That's for sure. Reed DROPS another elbow, and now he NAILS Rice with a kneebreaker! Reed lifts him up off the canvas now, and STUNS him with a jawbreaker! Reed gets up, AND HE'S SETTING HIM UP FOR THE KENTUCKY BACKSMASHER AGAIN! BD: Let's see if the fourth time's a charm. MW: He lifts Rice up, slingshot off the ropes; Rice comes back, and gets PLANTED with the Backsmasher! The cover! One..Two..And Jim Williams puts Rice's foot on the ropes! LB: And he STILL MANAGES to get his foot on the ropes! MW: He did NOT, Williams put it there! LB: When? My monitor went out. MW: Sure it did. And Reed is FURIOUS! He's trying to go after Williams but the referee is restraining him! It looks like the ref is managing to calm him down, and he's FINALLY turning back towards the ring! BD: And while he wasted all that time going after Williams, Rice has made it to his feet! MW: But he's not completely up, and Reed grabs him, and sets him up for a piledriver--COUNTERED WITH A BACKDROP BY RICE! And now Rice is quick to capitalize! He picks Reed up, and places him on the top rope! He's climbing up there with him now, BELLY-TO-BACK SUPERPLEX!!! LB: And now Rice is firmly in control! This is unbelievable! MW: You can say that again! And now Rice picks Reed up, AND HE'S SETTING HIM UP FOR THE POWER CHOKESLAM! He lifts Reed up, AND PLANTS HIM!!! The cover! One..Two..Thr..Shoulder Up! BD: And REED kicks out! BOTH of these guys are amazing! MW: They're putting on quite a show tonight! And Rice is arguing the count with the ref! It was VERY close, but Reed DID get out in time and Rice needs to go back to work. The ref isn't giving him any sympathy, and finally he turns to go back to work. Reed has reached his feet, and Rice hits him with an armdrag--NO! Countered with a LARIAT by Reed! LB: Where did that come from? MW: As far as I can tell, nowhere! Rice is down, and Reed quickly sinks in an armlock leglock submission! He's right in the center of the ring! This is going to be TOUGH to get out of! Rice is SCREAMING! BD: Come on Brian, FIGHT IT!! MW: Rice is resisting it for all he's worth, but there's no way he can stand much more of this, or he'll get broken in half! Reed CRANKS up the pressure, AND RICE SUBMITS!!! The referee is calling for the bell! (DING, DING) He EARNED that one. LB: He sure did, Rice put up a GREAT fight. RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of seventeen minutes and ten seconds, here is your winner.... "CRIMSON" JOE REED!!! MW: What a battle that was! You've got to give a lot of credit to both men for that one. BD: Okay, but give ME cash. MW: Very funny. But really, that was a FANTASTIC match, it's really a shame someone had to lose. Both of these guys have great futures ahead of them in the AEWA. LB: And this is only the undercard! What's next, Wilson? ******************************************************************** TO BE CONTINUED IN PART TWO ******************************************************************** \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- American E-Wrestling Association The AEWA-- We Deliver!
