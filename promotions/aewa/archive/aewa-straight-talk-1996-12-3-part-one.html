Straight Talk (12/3, part one)

6 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera fades in on the AEWA Studios, the AEWA logo falls onto the screen, and the words "STRAIGHT TALK" slide in underneath it. The whole logo then tilts, and falls off the bottom of the screen. Behind the desk in studio are Chuck Carlson and Ken Wilson.) CC: Hello folks, and welcome to another episode of Straight Talk! I'm your host, Chuck Carlson, along with my co-host, Ken Wilson. KW: Shut up, Carlson! CC: What's you problem?! KW: You. CC: Ken, I can't keep up with your rapid mood swings. You aren't still drinking moonshine before every show, are you? KW: No comment. Shut up and show an interview. CC: Okay, calm down. Up first, we have an interview conducted by Hank Thompson-- he recently had a conversation with Sir Gallade. Let's watch.... -------------- (The scene opens with Hank Thompson trying to keep up with Sir Gallade in the weight room) Thompson: Sir Gallade, please slow down, I need to ask you a few questions about the newcomer, "Classic" Andrew Archer. (Gallade throws his weights down and glares at Hank) Sir Gallade: Oh, my old friend, "Classic Andrew Archer.... You see Hank, Archer and I have run into each other before in other federations, and now he has come to my home, the AEWA to try and prove himself to the world. Well, Archer, you will have to go through me and my friends, The Knights of Deliverance, to ever make a name for yourself here in the AEWA-- and that will NOT happen! Thompson: He recently challenged you to a match. What will be your reply to his challenge? Sir Gallade: My reply is, BRING IT ON!!! You dare call me a phony Englishman? You see Archer, I have more allegiance to England than you will ever have! (Hank Thompson starts to talk, but Sir Gallade interrupts him) Sir Gallade: I have one more thing to say to Archer.... I can not wait to shut your mouth, because I am tired of hearing you insult this beautiful country, the USA. When we get into that ring, I am going to give you a bloody beating, my friend! (Sir Gallade goes back to weightlifting) Thompson: Thank you Sir Gallade, that should be a great bout. (fade to black) ---------------- CC: Boy, Sir Gallade and "Classic" Andrew Archer seem to have had some bad blood in the past. KW: Well, you know what I heard? CC: (sighs) No, what did YOU hear? KW: The reason Sir Gallade came to the AEWA was so he could get away from "Classic" Andrew Archer. He's afraid. CC: So why did Sir Gallade accept his challenge? KW: Well, that's kinda complicated, and I'd rather not get into it. CC: I bet. Now let's hear some comments from Simon O'Neal, from The Machines. --------------- (Simon O'Neal pulls up a folding chair, sits downs, and faces the camera. For a few moments he just stares into the camera. Finally he speaks.) Simon: If it were just up to me, I'd like to move on from Graphic Violence. They had two shots at the title, and couldn't get the job done. But Paul's still pissed at them-- hell, he was talking about going down to their homes and finishing the fight yesterday. I managed to talk him out of it for now, but.... Anyway, if Graphic Violence wants another shot at the belts, they have to play by our rules. If they don't agree to these stipulations, we won't sign a title match. We already have a victory over them, so by all rights they shouldn't have received a second shot. And as for our last match, I made a mistake in holding back my partner; I won't do that again. Here's our stipulations: (Starts counting off on his fingers) One, both Miss DeMeanor and her thug the Twilight Strangler will be caged at ringside. We want to be able to see them, and know they won't interfere in the match. Two, the match will be under Street Fight Rules- no DQ, no countout, use anything you want. Three, if Graphic Violence wins, they get the belts. But WHEN The Machines win, we get to destroy Miss DeMeanor's car. Why her car and not her wrestlers'? Because I don't think they're smart enough to drive, let alone own a decent car. Before I thought it would be better to stop Paul before he did something he might regret; now I think that smashing their heads into the ground would be very therapeutic for him. And as for myself (tightens his glove), well, whenever I enter the ring, everyone knows I cheat. The fans know it, the opponents know it, the refs know it. And I still get away with it. What do you think I'm capable of when I don't have to hide anything? (Smirks at the camera, stands up, and leaves. Fade to black.) -------------- KW: Well, I think he's a little pissed off. CC: The Machines have been very hard to control lately, and Graphic Violence is a little messed up, so if they do have this Street Fight match, I plan to be far away from the ring when these teams are let loose. KW: And Miss DeMeanor's car gets destroyed? I'm not gonna park anywhere near her. CC: Either way, this should be a great match to watch, although Graphic Violence may not like the stipulations. Both their manager AND The Twilight Strangler in cages? I can see the Strangler in a cage-- hell, he probably sleeps in one-- but Miss DeMeanor? I just don't see it. KW: What's next, Carlson? CC: "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum has a few comments regarding the ICU. This should be interesting.... --------------- (The "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum and Sondra Slocum enter the interview area. They both look very angry.) Ben Slocum: You know, it wasn't that long ago that I challenged any rulebreaking group to a War Games match. You would've thought that they would at least have had the courage to admit that they're all afraid; afraid of the embarrassment we'll put them through when we humiliate their pathetic asses once and for all.... But no, they all seem to have turned into mimes. Sondra: Well, the challenge is still there for anyone who has the guts to accept the challenge. There has also been a lot of turbulence concerning the titles lately. It seems as if we have two champions that we all despise, in the big crybaby Raider and the ultimate joke Shane Matthews. At least we have ONE champion to respect and be proud of-- Randall Osbourne. Ben: His victory was somewhat spoiled though by the interference of the Angels Of Death, who just can't seem to understand that they're not wanted. Some time has now gone by since the first encounter I had with them, but my opinion of them hasn't changed one bit during this time. They're still the same scum they always were. That's why at this time I'd like to challenge Ricky Roberts to a Texas Death Match with all members of our alliances banned from the building. Knowing him, I' bet he won't accept, because we both know that he's a no-talent bum. With or without the Angels of Death in his corner, Roberts is ALWAYS a loser. (fade to black) -------------- CC: Ben Slocum wants Ricky Roberts in a Texas Death match? That'll be a real slobber knocker! KW: Who are you now-- Dusty Rhodes? CC: I was just saying.... KW: I KNOW what you were saying. You Texans are all a bunch of crazy hillbillies, Chuck. CC: (laughs) Actually, I'm from New Jersey, so your point is kind of ruined. Just be quiet and let's hear from the Shadow Warriors.... -------------- (The camera opens on Wesley Cantrell and the Shadow Warriors. They all look rather angry.) Cantrell: You really have to hand it to that Larkin guy. After all, despite constantly being cheated out of what is rightfully his, he still manages to come up with a plan to get it back no matter what the cost is. It's leaders like him that make me believe there is a future for this federation, despite the crap that's been going on here recently. Ladd: You can't help but wonder how two teams like the Machines and the West Coast Connection, whose combined wrestling skills are less than those of Dodo the Clown, ever happened to become champions in the first place. I mean, anytime you have to consider a team like Heaven & Hell a step upwards, you're really on thin ice. Slade: The AEWA Board of Directors still continues to overlook the best tag team to ever set foot in the ring, the Shadow Warriors. I don't know how long this will go on, but I fear our patience is about to run out. We've been pushed to the limit, and we're ready to take action. That's fine with us, but just remember who caused the bloodshed in the first place. Cantrell: Others have begun to notice what we in the Cantrell Corporation have known for some time now-- there is a conspiracy against us. We demand an immediate investigation concerning this whole matter, and there's no doubt in my mind that such an investigation will clearly support our statements. We want everyone to see the blatant incompetence and corruption among the Board of the AEWA. Ladd: Everyone knows that we're the best there is, so we're used to the constant jealousy among the other wrestlers, but now it has also spread to the management of the federation. We won't just stand by and watch while they hand OUR titles away to some inbreeds. We want the belts back, and we won't stop until we them! (fade to black) -------------- KW: It's about time that the Shadow Warriors demand an investigation of the AEWA. I was just thinking to myself the other day, "Why haven't I seen the Shadow Warriors around lately? Maybe it's a conspiracy against them." CC: Oh, please! KW: What?! CC: The Shadow Warriors are a great tag team, but those accusations were ridiculous. Stop kissing their butts. KW: How DARE you accuse me of that! CC: Well, we have to interrupt this insanity to go to an interview with an even crazier group.... the ICU. -------------- (The camera opens on Deathwatch and Penance in the locker room, after Penance has just finished defending his Light Heavyweight title. Penance has the title draped over his shoulder, and his hair is wet and the black of it shines as if it had gel in it, but obviously it doesn't. He wears his usual ring attire. Deathwatch stands behind him, rubbing his shoulders. Deathwatch grins sadistically toward the camera and begins to speak) Deathwatch: That was an EXCELLENT match. Penance, you've done it, my boy. That Light Heavyweight strap is going to be yours for a VERY long time. Penance: I have not much more to accomplish, except wreak a little or a lot more havoc. Mike Hurt, you crossed the path of ICU, now how do you feel? Probably a lot worse than you used to. ICU has the Light Heavyweight title, and now it looks as if Heavy Metal is going to gain the North American title for this great corporation. Metal, best of luck to you, I know you'll make ICU proud. Deathwatch: Someone's in Trouble and his name fits the bill. Justin Trouble, what is your problem, dear boy? You have a lack of respect for everything I do? You want me in the ring? Well now, I've been here for quite awhile and I've fought it out with the best of them, but you.... you seem to be more of an annoyance than all the rest. You wish to make a name for yourself, and by doing that you wish to take on the strongest stable in AEWA? You want yourself to be recognized as the man that took apart ICU? Well, I'm sorry to say, but ICU has been around for a long time and no man is going to corrupt it, EVER. Trouble, you might be able to bring some people down, but ICU isn't going down with them. ICU will always be standing and looking down upon you with its fury, child. Stay out of the way Trouble. The match at Saturday Night Slam that you have requested, I accept it. I accept anything you throw at me; I am no coward and I don't expect you to be either. Penance: Joey Valachi, it looks to me as though you have yourself a title shot. You want this? (points to the Light Heavyweight title) Well, that's just too bad, now isn't it? Valachi, you are nothing more than a step ladder-- all you can do for me is boost my career. If you want this title, come and get it. It's right here; right on my shoulder, right here for the taking, but one thing it isn't right for.... It's NOT right for YOU. I've wanted this more than any man has wanted anything in this federation before, and I have obtained it-- what are YOU going to do about it? Absolutely nothing. This title is staying where it belongs, and that's with ICU. Deathwatch: Trouble, at Saturday Night Slam, there will be more in store for you than an attack by ICU-- there will be a severe beating at my hand! I care not who you bring out to ringside with you, you could have any man you want.... but rest assured, I'm bringing someone to my aid as well, just in case the odds start to lean in your favor due to your "escort". It's only fair, now isn't it? After this match with Trouble, I want my LONG awaited World Title shot. The last shot I had was at The Caretaker, but that wasn't even a match, that turned out to be something much more different-- it turned out to be The Caretaker's demise. Well, it looks as though he has emerged from his dark pit of ignorance and has come to face me once more. I'll tell you what Caretaker, when I get my title shot, THEN I'll face you. I have beenoverlooked far too many times to allow this to continue, and if I win the title, rest assured I will give you the shot that you.... deserve? No, you don't deserve it. What you deserve is a proper BURIAL-- six feet under-- then someone needs to cover the grave with brick so you DON'T come back. Penance: Valachi, this Wednesday, you are in for one of the most invigorating confrontations that you will ever encounter. I assure you, the Light Heavyweight title is going nowhere. Await your Final Judgment, and prepare to suffer defeat. (Fade to black as Deathwatch grins menacingly into the camera) --------------- CC: The only good thing about that interview is that it is now over. KW: For once, we agree. The ICU is becoming more and more of a nuisance. Unfortunately, they are also becoming more and more powerful. CC: Penance has the Light Heavyweight Title, and Heavy Metal has a chance to win the North American Title. It's getting a little frightening. KW: Well, it seems that the AEWA is just starting to really bond and take up arms against the ICU. Maybe we can fight them off. CC: Let's hope so. Up next, El Aviadore has some comments for us. He's been doing really well lately.... ------------ (The camera opens on El Aviadore, sitting alone in his apartment. He is watching some of his old AAA tapes.) Aviadore: You know something, AEWA, what have I really accomplished? Pretty much nothing, except for beating the crap out of Deathwatch. Right now, I am going to stop concentrating on just getting the title, I'm going to go after other wrestlers and get a feel for what the AEWA is like. As of this time, the El Aviadore has an open contract..... Keep looking to the sky, AEWA! (Fade to black) --------------- KW: I've said it once and I'll say it again, we don't need any more illegal immigrants here in the AEWA. CC: I don't think he'd be wrestling here if he didn't have all of his papers, Ken. KW: Well.... I STILL don't like him. CC: Well, here's someone who you may like, Ken. Our next interview is with The Twilight Strangler! -------------- (We see the Twilight Strangler and Miss DeMeanor in the locker room. They both look VERY angry.) Miss DeMeanor: After spending a night in the hospital, the Twilight Strangler is back in action! That match with Primal Fear didn't hurt him at ALL.... Strangler: Well, maybe a LITTLE.... Miss DeMeanor: (annoyed) No, not at ALL. Anyway, Brian Rice, are you still there, boy? Do you still want to wrestle the most dangerous man in wrestling history? Strangler: Brian Rice, my SH*T is bigger than you. Jeez, my MOM could probably kick your ass! You won't stand a CHANCE against me! Miss DeMeanor: Brian Rice, this is just a warning-- if you want to back out of the match, do it NOW! Strangler: See ya in the ring, son, because you won't be walking out! (fade to black) -------------- KW: Now THERE'S the kind of interview I like to see! CC: Well, it looks as if The Twilight Strangler has recovered from his injuries that Primal Fear inflicted upon him a week ago. KW: Like he said, it really didn't hurt much. He's a tough man. CC: Speaking of which, here are some comments from Primal Fear. Let's see what he has to say.... ------------- (The camera opens on Primal Fear, walking to his limousine after Saturday Night Slam. He is carrying a gym bag, and looks extremely tired. He turns to the camera and begins to speak.) Fear: I'm on a roll, baby! I sent the Crazy Canuck to the hospital after my Bump Splash last week, and then THIS week, I did the same thing to the Twilight Strangler! Can ANYONE stop me? (He opens the door to the limo, throws his bag in, and turns back to the camera.) Fear: Crazy Canuck, you may NEVER learn not to mess with me. I have burned you, put you in the hospital and humiliated you-- GET THE MESSAGE! Canuck, for your own good, stay out of my way! I hope to get a match at December Doom, and if you show your face there, WE'RE GONNA HAVE PROBLEMS! (fade to black) -------------- CC: Boy, Primal Fear is really angry. KW: But you have to give The Crazy Canuck SOME credit. He did have all those things done to him, but he keeps jumping right back up and fighting. CC: I guess. Now let's hear from Graphic Violence. ------------- (The camera opens on Booze Murphy and Leech Rogers, roaming the hallways after Saturday Night Slam.) Booze: The Machines are complete IDIOTS! Those two guys are a couple of little gay lovers! Leech: They got themselves disqualified on purpose, 'cause they were afraid of losing the titles to us. When we meet them again, there will be NO escape. Booze: Wong, you want to hit us in the head with a chair? Fine, but we've taken MUCH worse chair shots than that before! Bring it on, you little BITCHES! Leech: Machines, you sick mother f*ckers, you thought what we did to your car was bad..... We're gonna KILL you! Booze: Right now, I want to send a little "shout out" to President Freeman. Buddy, don't you think the third time could be the charm for us? Give us a match with the Machines at December Doom-- OR ELSE! Leech: See ya later, fools! (fade to black) -------------- CC: Well, we heard some interesting new insults from Graphic Violence there. Personally, I thought they were rather offensive. KW: Yeah, ain't it great? CC: Well, it seems that they are going to get their wish, as long as they agree to the stipulations that The Machines put forth. KW: They will. CC: Don't be too sure. Up next, we have an interview with Rick Showboat. Let's roll the footage.... -------------- (The camera opens on Rick Showboat, standing at the bar in an empty nightclub. He is wearing a suit, shades, and his hair is brushed lightly back to the right.) Showboat: AEWA, I'm getting my first shot at a heavyweight title soon! Man, I feel really good about this! But you know what? I hear no one expects me to win-- they expect that cool cat Rick Showboat to lose and go back to the Light Heavyweight division! THINK AGAIN! That's not how my story is gonna be told! (He takes a drink of his martini.) Showboat: I came waltzing into the AEWA, picked up a title, then I lost to some color commentator. I lost on PURPOSE, because I was BORED with the Light Heavyweight division. It's common knowledge! And I am gonna prove that Rick Showboat is the best wrestler in the history of the AEWA! BET ON IT! (Showboat wipes the empty counter with his hand.) Showboat: You know, I just bought this little place, and after I win the TV title, the victory party is right here! I have already invited some people, so I ain't letting 'em down! NO WAY! I will guarantee you this one thing-- Rick Showboat will become the AEWA TV champion, whether you like it or not! You'd BETTER like it, 'cause you've got no choice!! (Fade to black) -------------- KW: Rick Showboat has a nightclub now? CC: So it seems, or maybe his drink had some effect on him. KW: (chuckles) He did seem a bit tipsy, didn't he? CC: Yeah, I think he might have been hitting the Jack Daniels a little bit too much. Now here are some bizarre comments from Sewerdog, who we haven't seen in the ring for quite some time ---------------- (We see nothing but pure darkness, then Sewerdog's face just seems to appear in front of the camera. He seems calm and rational for the first time in his life.) Sewerdog: It has come to my attention that I haven't gotten many matches lately. And I'm not upset about it, I'm damned pissed off. But I now know the reason. After my last one-on-one match with Heavy Metal, President Freeman is afraid that I may accidentally paralyze, if not kill, one of his precious little wrestlers. (He starts to speak louder.) When I finally go after another title, someone's gonna get really hurt and it aint' gonna be me. I mean, even Heavy Metal and his precious Idiotic Cartel of Ugliness are afraid to face me and my mystery pal. Who IS this mystery man, you're probably wondering? His name is Sewaside, but that's all I can tell you. I'm waiting for anyone, and I may be waiting behind the curtains, under the ring--or I might just grab a seat and get you outside the ring. Only a precious few of my associates are safe. (His head just disappears in the darkness and the camera returns to the studio.) -------------- KW: What a NICE guy! CC: Yeah right. You know, I've noticed a disturbing trend here. KW: What's that? CC: It seems like everybody is using President Freeman as a scapegoat for their OWN problems. That's really not fair. KW: No, it's PERFECTLY fair. Freeman is an idiot, and he should NEVER have been given control of the AEWA. He's too damn biased. CC: I'm not too sure about that. Now, lets hear some comments from a newcomer to the AEWA, "Crimson" Joe Reed. Let's watch.... ------------ ("Crimson" Joe Reed and the Lady in Red are in Reed's basement gym. They are watching a video of the recent battle royal for the AEWA World Title.) Reed: Brian Rice. I'm watching your performance in the battle royal. You wrestled over ninety minutes before being eliminated, and outlasted twenty-eight other wrestlers. You sure as hell impressed me. Now you're facing me in an upcoming match. Well, I've always welcomed a challenge, and you look like you're presenting a good one. Lady In Red: I'm worried about his manager, Jim Williams. I saw some.... "questionable" tactics during the battle royal, like handing Rice a crowbar. Reed: Well, it was for a World Title, and we both know what a person's willing to do to win one. Brian, I'm looking forward to this match. If you want to wrestle, I can wrestle. If you want to mix it up, I'm more than willing to go that route also. And if you feel the need to bend the rules.... well, I've been in this sport for over ten years. I know how to handle that situation, and it isn't pleasant. Two men will enter that ring soon, and the better wrestler will walk away with the victory. I intend to be that man, Brian. I've come too far, trained too hard, and sweated too much to stumble now. (fade to black) -------------- CC: He seems ready for that match, huh? KW: Whatever. It's useless, however. He won't beat Brian Rice. And, by the way, I didn't see any "questionable" tactics by Jim Williams. All I saw was good managing. CC: Do you need glasses? He clearly cheated. KW: All I saw was that someone left a crowbar in the ring, and Jim Williams handed it to Brian Rice so no wrestler would pick it up and use it in an illegal fashion. CC: And what about the wrestlers he hit with the crowbar? KW: Innocent bystanders. Brian Rice swung it out of the reach of the other wrestlers' hands and it happened to connect with a head or two. CC: I'm always amazed by your perception of things here in the AEWA. KW: (bowing) Thank you very much! CC: Only YOU would think of it as a compliment. KW: It wasn't? CC: Uh, not exactly. But anyway, let's just watch these comments from Deranged.... ******************************************************************************** TO BE CONTINUED IN PART TWO! ******************************************************************************** \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- American E-Wrestling Association The AEWA-- We Deliver!
