CWAA Supershow Aug 23/24 pt 5

3 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EL: OK- what a shocker, as the Trailer Park Assassins, Cooter and Orbit, split that $25,000 check-literal- presented by the AEWA's own Lovely Bertha. But now, in tonight's special feature match, we're going to crown our first-ever CWAA Six Man Tag Team Champions, as the ACWF Tag Champs, Lawson and Sherrick, hook up with the TV Champ, Max Robertson, to take on the trio of Ramdy Sheradin, Kore Wrench and Joshua Barnes! Let's go to the ring! DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is set for one fall with a 30-minute time limit- and is for the CWAA Six Man Tag Team Championship! (pop) DM: Introducing first- (Darth Vader's customary "Imperial March" plays to its customary boos) DM: -accompanied by Major Victor Augustus Mayhem, at a combined weight of 694 pounds, here are CWAA Television Champion Max "the Professional" Robertson and his partners, the Atlantic Coast Tag Team Champions, Lt.' Daniel "Lethal" Lawson and Sgt. Mark Sherrick! And their opponents- (pop as generic Gregorian Chant plays) DM: -at a combined weight of 737 pounds, here are former CWAA Heavyweight Champion, "the Dreamer" Randy Sheradin. former CWAA Television Champion, Joshua Barnes, and "the Preacher" Kore Wrench! Your referee, Jeff Howard. EL: OK- Jeff Howard to call this historic contest. When this one is over, three men will walk away with new gold belts- one team already has three gold belts, and Sheradin and Barnes have worn some before. In fact, on the other side of the ring is the man who ended both their reigns. OK- there's the bell and we are underway. The Major fortunately taking a place near his team's corner for this very important encounter. Sgt. Sherrick, the workhouse of Major Mayhem's crack fighting unit, starts with Joshua Barnes. A couple of real wild bulls in that ring. And Sherrick challenging Barnes to a test of strength. Not the wisest of moves, but let's face it, Sherrick is not a man to doubt his own abilities. OK- they lock fingers. both hands now- and Barnes -proving the stronger of the two! (pop as Sherrick sinks to his knees) EL: Not too many men in the state of Texas who could match power with the ex-bouncer from Brownsburg, Indiana- one of his partners could, the big Preacher, perhaps, but I can't think of anyone else. Well, Sherrick finds a way to counter. (by jamming his forehead into Barnes' solar plexus) EL: Now Sherrick with a forearm smash to the jaw. Big closed fist, and Barnes replies in kind. The two men trading punches in mid-ring now. Blow for blow- and Sherrick getting the worst of it- he swings wildly and misses- spins, and Barnes with a hip toss. Here comes trouble- (pop as Lawson, then Robertson, run head on into Barnes' right fist) EL: Now a clothesline takes down the Sgt.- now the Lt. and the Professional! (pop) EL: Joshua Barnes clearing the ring, taking on all three at once, and listen to the crowd roar! They love this kid! Sherrick on his feet- Barnes scoops him up- body slam! Lateral press- 2... kickoff! Now Barnes tags in the Preacher. (pop) EL: Barnes with a wristlock, holding Sherrick up- Kore Wrench off the ropes with a clothesline! Sgt. Sherrick on the mat as the Preacher clamps on an arm bar. You know, the Preacher is the one man in this bout to have never won a championship, and he supposed to have a crack at Jak Hammer for the TV championship tomorrow, but now that match is between Joshua Barnes and Max Robertson for that very same title- now held by the Professional. The Preacher moves into a hammerlock now, now he tags in the Dreamer, Randy Sheradin! (pop) EL: Wrench whips him into the ropes- ducks down- and Sheradin with a dropkick as Sherrick hurdles the Preacher! (pop) EL: Sheradin covers- 1... 2... kickout again! Excellent teamwork demonstrated by the Dreamer and his partners. Sheradin with a snap mare takedown, and now he tags in Barnes- quick tags the hallmark of a sound team- and Sheradin and Barnes gun for the ACWF Tag Team titles tomorrow in this same ring against Sherrick and Lawson. (as Barnes connects with an elbow drop) EL: Hooks the leg- 1... 2... kickout again. Sherrick holding up well under all of this punishment. Joshua with the Irish whip- bounces off the ropes- and the Professional jabs him in the back with a knee- right in the kidney area! Sherrick now, angered at how this match has progressed, taking it out on the stunned Barnes- closed fist punches. And now he sets- belly to belly suplex! Up and he tags the Professional in- a preview perhaps of tomorrow night's Title matchup! (boos) EL: Max with a kick in the ribs. Now he stomps on his fallen foe. Sets him up- abdominal strecth! Max Robertson and his sometimes partner, the Lt., real masters of the mat, connoisseurs of the canvas. No one in the CWAA is more knowledgeable of submission holds or as skilled in the application of these holds. Max has that leg grapevined, the hold executed just perfectly- a textbook example. But he reaches back and grabs Sgt. Sherrick's hand for greater leverage! (boos) EL: Referee Jeff Howard checking for a submission, and Wrench and Sheradin yelling to him about Sherrick. Max lets go, and Jeff Howard checks and finds nothing wrong. He checks Barnes for a submission again, and now Robertson and Lawson have linked hands! (boos) EL: Sheradin and Wrench hollering again, as Lawson and Robertson gain the illegal leverage advantage. Referee Jeff Howard checks and they let go just in time. All the while, Joshua Barnes suffering terrible pain at the hands of his arch-nemesis, the man who adds insult to injury and injury to insult and has taken his TV title belt away! See the beads of sweat streaming down his strained face! Jeff Howard checks for a submission again- and Sherrick aiding the Professional yet again- and now they've been caught! (pop) EL: Jeff Howard kicks the joined hands! (pop) EL: And Joshua Barnes hip tosses Max Robertson over! (pop) EL: Barnes now takes him down with a football tackle! Max Robertson rolls to the floor for a time-out. When the going gets tough, the Professional gets going- we've seen it time and again. OK- Robertson rebuffs the Major's attempts to advise him, and rolls back in. He tags the Lt., and Barnes tags Wrench. Terriffic enmity between these two men all the way back to the beginning, back in January of this year- 7 long months ago. Lawson greets him with a knelt. Now a closed-fist punch- the promise of gold bringing out the most vicious side in many men, the Lt. among them. He's looking to become, along with the Professional, the first man to wear three different title belts here in the CWAA- a former CWAA TV Champion and current ACWF Tag champion. Another punch- sends him for the ride- reversed! And the Preacher catches him in a big bearhug! (pop) EL: Squeezing like a boa constrictor- you can almost see the Lt.'s eyes bulging out of his head! The Major shouting some sort of gibberish at the Lt. as the Preacher squeezes harder and harder, pushing the air out of the Lt.'s body. And now Lawson reaches up and scratches the eyes- that'll break any hold! Senior Official Jeff Howard warning Lawson, who now tags Sgt. Sherrick back in. Double Irish whip- and a double elbow jab to the sternum area. Lawson exits, and Sherrick to a standing arm bar. And the Preacher turns and flattens him with a short clothesline! (pop) EL: The Preacher sets him up with a full nelson- look at those biceps. He throws him over- Dragon Suplex! This could be it! 1... 2... no! Another kickout! The Preacher spins him over- backslide- 1... 2... kickout! The Preacher sends him for the ride- an Iron claw! (been a long time since anyone's used that here, I'll wager) EL: The Preacher clamps that claw on Sgt. Sherrick's head, and Sherrick in a mess of trouble. The Preacher has such powerful hands. And now Max Robertson with a double sledge that breaks the hold. Sherrick free, but still in obvious pain. He reaches over and tags Lawson, and the Preacher tags in the Dreamer. Good matchup here of two top-light pure wrestlers. Fireman's carry takeover by the Dreamer, into an arm bar. Lawson gets his feet underneath him as Sheradin twists that arm again. Now the ex-TV champ reverses it and applies an arm bar of his own. Sheradin now feels the pressure. He sets- backflips over and uses the momentum for another reversal! (pop) EL: Another fireman's carry takeover, and now he slips on a headlock down on the mat. Now this is real championship wrestling, men matching move for move on the canvas. Human chess. Hold and counterhold, in front of a capacity crowd of great CWAA fans, here in Reunion Arena, the site of so many historic battles. Ric Flair, Bruiser Brody, the von Erichs, the Freebirds, the Fantastics, so many top stars have grappled here before. Now Lawson with a handful of tights rolls Sheradin back- 1... 2... Sheradin rolls forward again. Now Sheradin slips into a hammerlock. Now a waistlock- German suplex! 1... 2... kickout! Sheradin with an Irish whip- dropkick! What elevation! Sets him up- 1... 2... swinging neckbreaker! This could be it! Cover him, Randy, cover him! (OK- so Eric lapses into David Crockett mode just this once) EL: And now he tags in Joshua Barnes. He should have gone for the cover right then and there. Barneswith a scoop slam. Covers- 1... 2... and Robertson breaks the pin! Barnes now grabs at Robertson- and Lawson clips his legs from behind! Now Lawson tags Max, and Max goes to right to work on the legs- kicking and stomping at the ankles and knees- softening up the joints. Now Max wraps his leg around the bottom rope and pulls on the ankle- straining those joints and hamstrings some more! Max like a shark when he scents blood. Now where's he going? (to the floor) EL: Drags Barnes' leg- and slams the knee against the ring post! He isn't just aiming for the six man belts- he's weakening Barnes for the big TV title match tomorrow night! Now Barnes on the apron- slinsgshot somersault splash! 1... 2.. no! Barnes kicks out! (pop) EL: Max sends him for the ride- catches him- hangman! Dropped him throatfirst right on the top rope! Now he tags in Sgt. Sherrick, and he goes right to the cobra clutch! Clamps it right on, cutting off the carotid artery! Barnes sinks to the mat. ("Go, Joshua, Go!", as Sheradin and Wrench stamp their feet and clap in time) EL: This whole arena trying to rally the ex-Brownsburg bouncer! And Barnes, his face in the canavs, Sherrick's forearm cutting off the blood to his brain, reaches weakly, and grabs the bottom rope! (pop) EL: And Jeff Howard commands Sherrick to break the hold! The Major is irate- he thought it was over- I thought it was over- but this kid, this rookie who came all the from Indiana to capture the hearts of the whole state of Texas, has so much fight in him- a champion's spirit! (OK, Eric is clearly getting too into this whole card. Now he thinks he's Lord Alfred Hayes, or, even worse, calling a midcard match at the Showboat Arena for the AWA) EL: Sherrick stomps away, really going to work on Barnes' back. Barnes has taken so much abuse, so much punishment in this one. A double sledge right in the small of the back. Sends him for the ride- knee to the midsection. They are just taking Barnes apart as his partners watch helplessly, with so much at stake. The six man belts and mometnum going into tomorrow's title matches. AEWA title shots could be at stake- UWF title shots, with the ACWF belts on the line! Sherrick nails him with a ddt! Sgt. Sherrick has come so far since attaching himself to Major Mayhem and Lt. Lawson. He covers- 1... 2... no! (pop) EL: I can't believe it! Barnes kicks out again! These six men giving it all they've got! Giving 110%! Lawson tags in now- and a vicious chop- a Lawson specialty. Cross corner whip- and Barnes runs right into a knee jab to the midsection. Lawson now sets him up- fsherman's suplex- a cradle suplex- 1... 2... kickout again! Lawson growing frustrated now- hooks him- Russian legseep! Don't tell the Major I said that. The Major, speak of the devil, pointing to the top turnbuckle, and Lawson nods. He sets Barnes for a suplex- no! He sits him on the top turnbuckle! Superplex coming up- all 4 men on the outside watching intently- this could be it- this could be the turning point! Lawson balances himself on the second turnbuckle- hooks him- and- (pop) EL: Joshua Barnes with a big righthand to the head! And another! Lawson on rubbery legs now! Barnes shoves him to the mat, and collapses! Both men down- Jeff Howard begins his double ten count! ("Go, Joshua, Go!", with, again, the accomaniment of Wrench and Sheradin) EL: Lawson flat on his back as Barnes crawls to the corner- reaches- yes! (pop) EL: The Preacher! The Preacher tags in! He pounces- the cover- 1... 2... kickout! Kore Wrench with the Irish whip- powerslam! 1... 2.. no! So many 2-counts in this one! Gets him up- Gorilla Press- 240 pounds over his head- and he tosses him over the top rope on top of Major Mayhem! (pop) EL: A difficult night for the Major- he's been smacked silly! Lawson rolls back in under the bottom rope- tags in Sgt. Sherrick. Sherrick now gesturing for a test of strength. He's alreayd lost one to Barnes tonight, now he wants to take his chances with the Preacher, the one man in the CWAA who may be more powerful than Barnes. They lock up- and Sherrick kicks him in the guts! He learned his lesson- and look at that smirk. He's real proud of himself. Side headlock- the Preacher pushes him off- Sherrick off the ropes- shoulderblock by the Preacher! Sherrick grabs for an ankle- single leg takedown. Tags the Professional- Max Robertson in there now. Kicks the ankle. Stomping away at the Preacher's ankle- mow he sets- figure four leglock! He's got it! He has the Preacher in the figure four, and- (pop) EL: Barnes nails him with an elbow drop! Max was lying exposed, and Barnes just punded him into the mat! Senior Official Jeff Howard now sending Barnes back to his corner- and here comes Lt. Lawson! Double Irish whip- double cl- no! (pop) EL: Kore Wrench just clotheslined the Professional and Lt. Lawson simultaneously! Everyone in the ring now! (Barnes throws Robertson to the floor, and Sheradin dropkicks Sherrick) EL: Kore Wrench grabs Lt. Lawson- sets him up- Crucifix Slam! (Razor's Edge) EL: He hit it! (Max is on the floor trying to elude Barnes, and Sheradin clothesline's Sherrick over the top rope) EL: 1... 2... 3! He got it! (roof-lifting pop) EL: He got him! It's over! It's finally over! Let's get the official word! DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winners, and new CWA Six- (The rest is inaudible over the roar of the crowd) EL: Kore Wrench, Randy Sheradin and Joshua Barnes- Six Man Tag Team Champions! What a night of championship wrestling- the six man champs, the Trailer Park Assassins, and let's not forget the Freak- still undefeated! We'll see you back here tomorrow night, for 4 championship bouts, Elektro and the Freak, and the AEWA World Heavyweight Champion! Good night everyone!
