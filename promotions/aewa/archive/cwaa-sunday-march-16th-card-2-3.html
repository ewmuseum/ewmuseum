Sunday, March 16th, Card (2/3)

16 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Wednesday, the WYXZ TV 39, it's the movie of the week. This week, Gary Coleman and Robert Guillaume star in the story of a boy who teaches a bitter retired baseball player a lesson in sportsmanship and just might inspire a team into the world series in the process. it's "The Kid from Left Field", this Wednesday at 8:00, here on WYXZ TV, Channel 39.) EL: OK, we're back. Let's go to the ring and David Myers. ("When Johnny Comes Marching Home Again" plays, and the booing starts) DM: This next contest is for one fall, with a fifteen-minute time limit. First, on his way to the ring accompanied by his manager, Major Victor Augustus Mayhem, at 219 pounds, from Poughkeepsie, NY, Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson! (The booing grows louder and louder) EL: Well, it looks like the Major has left the flagpole home tonight. (A disgruntled-looking Major takes a seat next to Lane) EL: Major, where's your flag? MVM: Clearly some commie punk, perhaps on the take from the Iraqi elite Republican Guard or the Democratic Party, I'm not sure which, stole our flag. Can you believe it? This is the problem with urban America today, where you can't even leave a flag in the locker room. (Gregorian Chant fills the arena) DM: And his opponent, from New York, NY, at 275 pounds, he is "The Preacher", Kore Wrench! (The crowd erupts as the priestly-garbed one arrives, carrying a neatly folded American flag and a busted flagpole.) DM: Your referee for this contest, Chris Herman. (Wrench asks for the microphone from Myers, as Lawson watches incredulously.) Kore Wrench: Since I've come to this federation, I've been stabbed, lied to, and my relationship with my fans has been taken advantage of. All of these things show me that now, more than ever, my work here is cut out for me. I have permitted evil to run rampant for too long already. Lawson, the last time I faced you, you were spared from my Crucifix Slam by an intervention from the Major, and this. (Holds up the broken flagpole) I may have gotten the win that night, but tonight, I want more. Tonight, I'll get the pin. (Tossing the broken flagpole at Lawson's feet). I know something about preemptive strikes myself. (He hands the mike back to Myers and tosses the flag into the Major's lap, and Lawson strikes him with a double sledge in the back.) EL: We're off, and it was Lethal Lawson with the cheap shot to start. Major, whatever happened to "don't fire until you see the whites of their eyes"? MVM: Don't fir until you see the backs of their... backs. EL: That doesn't sound very inspirational. MVM: When you win, you don't need inspiration. EL: Lt. Lawson now choking the Preacher against that bottom rope. Referee Chris Herman warning Lawson about that choke. MVM: The Lieutenant already knows all he needs to know about choking someone; the referee's comments are superfluous. EL: Lawson now with a European uppercut. Irish whip- back body drop by Lt. Lawson. He follows with an elbow drop. The lateral press... 1... 2... kickout. Wrench sits up as Lawson locks on a reverse chinlock. Chris Herman checks to make sure this doesn't turn into an illegal chokehold. MVM: The Lieutenant only applies legal chokeholds- you should know that. EL: Lawson applies the most basic holds very expertly, as he forces Wrench to endure both the pain of the hold and to support Lawson's weight as he leans down on his foe. You know, Major, these two men have met before. And as the Preacher had Lawson up in that Crucifix Slam, you jabbed him with that flagpole, which appears to not be an option tonight. Have you and Lt. Lawson revised your strategies as a result? MVM: Of course, nothing would make me happier than to come out here and spout off all of our plans in front of rolling cameras and a live audience. EL: OK. Lawson releases the chinlock now, and slams an elbow onto the top of Wrench's head. Now he spins the Preacher around and clamps on an iron claw. And Wrench responds with one of his own! Both men now with clawholds on each other. Wrench's hands are much more powerful, and is driving Lawson all the way down to his knees- Lawson is forced to release his hold. Lawson on his knees, his arms hanging limp, and Wrench squeezing that clawhold on. Chris Herman checking for the submission. Now Wrench rears back and drives a big boot right into Lawson's chest! (Pop) EL: Picks him up- Irish whip- a knee right in the midsection! Lawson, doubled over- MVM: It was a low blow- this is grounds for an immediate disqualification. EL: - it was in the solar plexus- Lawson doubles over, and Wrench drives his head right into the mat...bulldog, with authority. He rolls him over, hooks the leg...1 ...no- kickout. Wrench rolls him over- half Boston crab, as he pulls back on that left leg. I notice you're getting a little fidgety, Major. Is this where you would normally start looking for that flagpole? MVM: It is true that the Lieutenant derives a certain amount of inspiration from seeing the flag waving grandly. EL: I thought you said winners didn't need inspiration. MVM: I think Wrench is pulling the tights on this half Boston thing. EL: You can't pull tights in a half Boston crab. Can you not just accept even once that someone is successfully executing a maneuver on your charge? MVM: And I think he grabbed some hair, too. EL: (sighs) Kore Wrench in control, here, against Lt. Lawson. Kore Wrench releases the half Boston now, and pulls Lawson back up- he's setting him up now for a piledriver- MVM: Excuse me. (heads to the ring) EL: -he connects! The cover... 1... the Major drapes Lawson's foot over the bottom rope- just a two-count. Wrench now rolls his man over- arm bar. I appear to have the announcing duties to myself again, as things are looking difficult enough for Lawson that the Major has to position himself in a more strategic area. Kore Wrench cinching that arm bar on Lt. Lawson. You know, the Lieutenant, at a mere 219 pounds, could qualify as a contender for the Light Heavyweight Title of the AEWA, but when the match was signed last week, Lawson was still in the hunt for the heavyweight title, so it is just down to Marc Thomas and Joey Haven, who just get in under the weight limit at about 240-245 pounds each. Now Wrench, still with that arm bar on, stands his ma back up- short clothesline, and he nearly smacked Lawson all the way back to Poughkeepsie with that one. (Big pop) EL: Now Wrench setting him up for the Crucifix Slam- this could be it- he has him up there- and the Major on the apron, pulls Lawson down to safety. Rabbit punch by the Lieutenant now- he saw the back of his back, I guess. Referee Chris Herman is asking the Major if he interfered in any way- I wonder what kind of answer he was expecting! The Lieutenant now with a legsweep on the Preacher, and the cover... 1... 2... kickout. Lawson picks him back up- takes him right back down- belly to belly suplex. 1... 2... kickout, again. Lawson whips Wrench into the corner. Wrench bounces out- and right into an elbowsmash. Scoop slam by Lawson, who bounces off the ropes- kneedrop- no one home! Lawson hops back up, favoring that leg- and Kore Wrench comes up- ddt! Kore Wrench stuck him! The Major back up on the ring apron- and the Preacher with a big elbow in the chest for Major Mayhem! (Enormous pop) EL: Wrench turns back to Lawson- sets him up- Crucifix Slam! (Razor's Edge) 1... 2... 3! He got it! (Gregorian chant resumes playing.) (More popping from our popping-fresh crowd) DM: Here is your winner, "The Preacher", Kore Wrench! EL: Kore Wrench hands the Lieutenant a loss and the Major a chestache. When we come back, we'll have a rematch from last week, as Twister tries to avenge their loss to Michael Anderson and Byron McNultey. (...if you're a veteran, you can not be turned down. No medical exam, no embarrassing questions. Just piece of mind and guaranteed coverage....) EL: Last week we got our first look at "Marvelous" Mikey Anderson and his partner, "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey, as they defeated Twister by some underhanded means. Let's take a look. ************************************************************* (CWAA File Footage- 03/09/97) EL: And he stumbles backward, and Conley rolls him up... 1... 2... kickout! Travis Conley up again- and he tags in Paul Langford- who runs in and drops an elbow on Byron, and now locks him in an armbar down on the mat. And Michael Anderson starts heading down the ring apron to where Mother Nature is stationed- and he just blew her a kiss! He's winking at her, and now she's on her way to his corner. He's pointing to his cheek, expecting a kiss, I guess- and she slaps him, right where we has pointing! (big pop) EL: Anderson is stunned, and now he takes a swing at her- she ducks and runs. Langford saw none of this but sees her heading for the safety of her team's corner, and he releases the hold, and turns to see what was wrong. Uh-oh- Byron McNultey now up behind him- full nelson- no! He suplexes him back- Dragon Suplex! The ref starts his count- and Michael Anderson leans over the ropes again and pulls back on Langford's feet! He can't kick out because Anderson has him by the feet! That's it- a three count! *************************************************************** EL: Tonight Twister looks for their revenge. Let's go back to the ring and David Myers. DM: This next match is a tag team contest, scheduled for one fall and a twenty-minute time limit. First, accompanied by Mother Nature, at a combined weight of 559 pounds, here are Paul Langford, Travis Conley- Twister! (There is a big pop as the "Twister" theme plays and Mother Nature leads the two men to the ring.) DM: And their opponents, at a combined weight of 497 pounds, "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey and "Marvelous" Michael Anderson! ("Sharp Dressed Man" by ZZ Top plays as the two strut to the ring to a chorus of boos.) DM: Your referee for this ma- (Myers bails out as Twister decides to skip the introduction of referee Jeff Howard) EL: We're off and running, as Paul Langford goes to work on the Pretty Boy, with a series of bodypunches in the corner- McNultey still has that red robe on. In the meantime, Travis Conley is slamming Marvelous Mikey, who rolls under the bottom rope to safety. Now Langford and Convey whip McNultey into the ropes- double back body drop. Now Conley pulls him and (Big pop as McNultey flattens Anderson on the floor with an involuntary cross body press) EL: Referee Jeff Howard trying to get some sense of order in the ring. OK- Travis Conley heads to the corner, and the Tornado waits in midring for Anderson and McNultey to sort themselves out. OK- it looks like the Pretty Boy to start for his team- well, he's telling referee Jeff Howard to make sure Langford gives him plenty of room to get in. Langford backs up, and Byron enters through the ropes- and Langford meets him with a running kneelift! Irish whip- sunset flip! 1... 2... McNultey with a double leg cradle rolls him back... 1... 2... kickout! Both men up now, and Langford with a dropkick that knocks McNultey back into his corner. Anderson tags in, and Langford greets him with an arm drag takedown, and another, into an armbar. Anderson on the mat, and Langford twisting his arm- Langford reaches up and tags Conley with his free hand- and Conley puts the boot to Marvelous Mikey. Now Conley picks him up, and drops him across the knee- backbreaker by the ex-Husker lineman. He's setting for a power bomb- but McNultey breaks it with an elbow in the back. That'll bring Langford back in, and he lunges at McNultey, Jeff Howard between the two, and he's trying to send Langford back to the Twister corner. Anderson now hits Conley with a forearm in the back, and he and McNultey set him up- double snap suplex! Anderson sips back into the corner, and Jeff Howard asks if there was a tag. What a surprise, Anderson and McNultey say there was one indeed. Byron McNultey with a legdrop on Travis Conley now. We have to take another break, but when we get back, we'll bring you the rest of this exciting tag team matchup. (In a few weeks, the superstars of the ACWF bring you Rule Britannia! See Joey Valachi, Ricky Reno, Ian Mantle, the Scholar, Randy Sheradin, David Graves, Kyle Spartan and "Iceman" Greg Smith as the ACWF takes its exciting action across the pond. Contact your local cable operator for details.) EL: OK- we're back, and Byron McNultey has Conley in the corner, and it looks from here like Anderson has a handful of Conley's tights, too, as McNultey throws a series of open-handed chops across Conley's chest. McNultey now with the tag, and Anderson comes in. Irish whip- and Mikey Anderson with a Hotshot on Travis Conley. The cover... 1... 2... kickout! Anderson tags back out, and Byron McNultey in with a fist drop. ("Go, Travis, Go!" etc.) EL: Now the Pretty Boy stands him up. Hooks him around the waist- Northern Lights Suplex! Lateral press... 1... 2... another kickout! McNultey getting a little frustrated, now, and he heads over to the Twister corner- and slaps Paul Langford, right in the face! Here comes the Tornado, and here comes referee Jeff Howard to send him back to the corner, and Michael Anderson in the ring now- double clothesline on Travis Conley! McNultey heads to the corner- Michael Anderson in there with Conley- again, there was no tag! Anderson hoists Conley over his shoulders- airplane spin! He drops him, the cover- 1... 2... kickout! Anderson with the Irish whip- clothesline- no! Conley ducks and comes back with one of his own! (Big pop) EL: Conley crawling to his corner- Langford stretches- he makes the tag! (Another big pop) EL: In comes Langford- drop-kicks Anderson! Here comes McNultey- and Langford with a dropkick for him, too! Paul Langford with an airplane spin on Marvelous Mikey, and he drops him to the mat! Swinging neckbreaker- the Tornado drop! He hit it! 1... 2... McNultey tried to break it with a legdrop, but Langford rolled out of the way! (Yet another pop) McNultey leg drops his own man- and in comes Conley with a shoulderblock that knocks McNultey to the floor! Langford covers Anderson again... 1... 2... 3! He got him! (Last pop of the match) (The "Twister" theme plays as Twister exults and McNultey and Anderson trudge back to the lockers) DM: Here are your winners, Travis Conley, Paul "Tornado" Langford- Twister! EL: We'll have Joey Haven and Marc Thomas, right after this break!
