Straight Talk (10/25)

1 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera fades in on the AEWA Studios, the AEWA logo falls onto the screen, and the words "STRAIGHT TALK" slide in underneath it. The whole logo then tilts, and falls off the bottom of the screen. Behind the desk in studio are Chuck Carlson and Ken Wilson.) CC: Hello everyone, welcome to another jam-packed edition of Straight Talk. I'm Chuck Carlson, and alongside me is my tag-team partner Ken Wilson. KW: Since when am I your tag team partner? And who are we wrestling? CC: No, I meant to say "tag team partner in the broadcast booth". (Wilson gives him a confused stare.) Never mind. Let's just get to our first interview, from Banshee and Shockwave, The Assassins. -------------- (Banshee and Shockwave are standing in front of an AEWA banner. They each are wearing headbands with the letters "AEWA" on them.) Banshee: (looking angry) We have two issues to talk about today. The first one is the National Tag Team Titles. Machines, we haven't forgotten what you did to us. It is time for payback. We want your titles! If you are beat before we get to you, we will take down whoever beat you and then we will come after you! Do NOT underestimate us! We will get our revenge, in the ring or out! Shockwave: The next issue involves the state of the AEWA. Lately, a few wrestlers have shown the desire to destroy the AEWA. Well, we just want to tell everyone that the Assassins will do everything in our power to stop them. We are loyal to the AEWA! Nothing could ever make us turn against this great federation. (fade to black) ----------- CC: There's another team willing to defend the AEWA against this new crop of wrestlers who seem to want to destroy it. KW: (oblivious to the last comment) When did we become a tag team? CC: (rolls his eyes) I hope this doesn't go on all night long. Now let's go to comments from Vicki, the former wife of Twisted Steel. ----------- (The camera opens on Vicki, sitting alone in her apartment. She's wearing an inexpensive blue dress, with a lit cigarette in her mouth. She turns to the camera, smiles, and begins to speak) Vicki: Twisted Steel, you and I have been apart for a long time now. I've had time to think about my mistakes, and I've realized something very important-- I was wrong. You treated me like a queen, and I was too immature and greedy to appreciate you. Now, after several months of reality, I've finally come to my senses. Baby, I'm begging you; PLEASE forgive me. I'm going to be in the front row at the next show, and I hope that you'll come over to see me. I made some horrible mistakes in my past, but PLEASE take me back. Without you, I'm completely alone. Twisted Steel, since we got divorced, my life has been a mess. My life doesn't even seem to matter anymore-- everything is meaningless without you. I want our love back, baby.... Please. (fade to black) ----------- CC: WOW! Vicki is asking Twisted Steel to take her back! This is big news! KW: (STILL not listening) Wait.... who are we wrestling? CC: (angrily) Will you snap out of it!? Vicki just asked Twisted Steel to take her back! KW: She what? CC: Vicki just asked Twisted Steel to take her back! KW: When? CC: Oh, never mind. I'm sure there'll be more discussion about this later. Let's go to a recent house show interview done by Hank Thompson with Justin Trouble. ------------- (The camera opens on Hank Thompson at an AEWA house event. He is holding a microphone, and begins to speak.) Hank: Hello, fans of the AEWA! Folks, today, I bring you a very special guest. A guest who is a relative newcomer to the AEWA, but has decided to take a stand against the ICU. Are you ready to bring him out?! (the fans go nuts) Well then, without further ado, please help me welcome President Freeman's new bodyguard, and newcomer to the ranks of the AEWA, JUSTIN TROUBLE! (Justin comes out to Tom Petty's "Makin' Some Noise" to a huge pop from the fans. He's wearing jeans, boots, an "AEWA Rocks" T-shirt, and a biker jacket. Trouble acknowledges the fans, and slaps hands with them. A couple of beautiful women jump over the barricade and hug Trouble, but security escorts them out. Trouble finally makes it to the ring) Hank: Good to finally meet you, Justin! (shakes Trouble's hand) Trouble: Pleasure's all mine! Hank: You made some comments about the ICU after the award banquet, and now we find out that you're Freeman's bodyguard. Could you elaborate on the subject? Trouble: What's to talk about? The ICU is nothing but a bunch of THUGS, and they need to be stopped. I'm just the man for that job, Hank. When I get through with the ICU, there'll be some LAW around here again. Hank: Could you tell me a little bit about your past? I heard that you lived in Hollywood for some time. Trouble: Oh yeah, that was a great time in my life. I worked as a bodyguard to several Hollywood celebrities, and I got quite a bit of money from it. Hank: So people should take you seriously, huh? Trouble: You'd better believe it. As Bob Freeman's new bodyguard, I'm going to defend the AEWA from the ICU. Heavy Metal had better watch his back, because the LAW has arrived. (fade to black) ------------- CC: Well, Justin Trouble is making a stand against the ICU and particularly Heavy Metal. It's good to see everyone standing up for the AEWA against all of these, as Justin put it, thugs. KW: Well, Trouble comes in here, and he thinks he's gonna stand up to the ICU and be the hero savior to the AEWA. He doesn't understand who he's dealing with. Heavy Metal is a former World champion. He'll eat Trouble for lunch. CC: Speaking of Heavy Metal, let's go now to comments from the man who defeated him for the World heavyweight championship at Mega Combat, "Superman" Mike Stewart. ------------- (The camera opens on "Superman" Mike Stewart sitting on his couch in his Indianapolis home. He is wearing a light brown sport shirt, blue jeans, and brown hiking boots. Michelle Anderson then walks in with two cups of coffee and hands one to him. Mike flips off the television and looks to the camera as he thanks Michelle. The AEWA World title belt is lying on the coffee table in front of the couch. The date is two days after this past Saturday Night Slam.) Stewart: Well, I got one hell of a match with Primal Fear, and I was happy to get out of that with the World title still around my waist, and then all of the sudden this idiot comes running into the ring wielding a chair. First he hauls off on Fear, then he decided to take me down and then proceeds to pound on me. Well, I've got a little bit of advice for this Canuck guy. You're out of your league. (leans forward towards to camera) You may have been in the AEWA for some time, but what have you done? I don't see your name on the list of champions. I don't see your name in the top twenty rankings. What's the deal? Are you looking for a little spotlight? Listen pal, in the AEWA, you've got to fight for your title shots. A lot of people say I fell into this title. They might be right, but I am the AEWA World champion and I plan on staying that way. So, Canuck, you want a World title match? Why don't you prove that you're worth it by beating some of the other guys in this fed before you jump the champ. Now as for the ICU.... (sits back on the couch, puts his arm around Anderson, and smiles.) It looks like your little lawsuit didn't go exactly as planned. I've still got the belt.... (nods to the World title belt) ...and Heavy Metal's still whining and crying. Metal, you had you're chance against me, but if your buddy Deathwatch wants a piece of me, I think he'll remember what happened when we met the first time. I believe I kicked your butt out of the ring, didn't I? If you want a match, you're in for the fight of your life, pal. But, bring it on because I'm not ducking anyone. (Stewart flips the television back on and takes a drink of coffee as the screen fades to black.) ------------- KW: Who does this loser think he is? First he gets a fluke win over Heavy Metal and now he thinks he's the ruler of the world. CC: Well, in a way he is. He is the World heavyweight champion. KW: But Heavy Metal deserves to wear that belt. CC: That may be your opinion, but Stewart won it fair and square in the middle of the ring. KW: He'll have to deal with Heavy Metal down the road, and next time he won't be so lucky. CC: We'll see. Now let's go to a new team in the AEWA, The New Gods. ------------- (Camera opens on Thor standing on a hilltop. There is lots of green grass on the ground and a blue sky. He seems to be looking to the stars.) Thor: For a few weeks, Superior and myself have been challenging any team who feels they are worthy of facing ourselves in a contest. We have yet to hear from anyone. I am beginning to believe the competition in the AEWA is not worth the time of myself and Superior. We have two men as World tag team champions who do not respect the worthiness of the fans' cheers and have turned their backs on their managers. We are truly for the fans, West Coast Connection. (Thor looks at the camera, as Superior comes into view. He's walking into the background behind Thor.) Thor: We're not challenging The West Coast Connection. Superior and myself are challenging the World tag team champions, whether they be the team of Heaven & Hell, The Machines, or any other team who happens to hold those prestigious belts. (Superior comes closer into camera view.) Superior: Mike, Kyle, and Lance are ready for the training session. We've got to go. (Superior walks back in the direction that he has just come.) Thor: Our friends and training partners are in need of our presence, but we will be back for those tag team belts. (The camera fades to black, as the sound of thunder fills the clear blue sky.) -------------- CC: The New Gods seem anxious to win those World tag belts, and have already put out the challenge to the World tag team champions. KW: All of these rookies come into the AEWA and immediately think they're gonna be World champions. Stewart got lucky and now these guys think they're gonna be just as lucky. I don't think so. CC: Time will tell. Next, we have comments from yet another AEWA newcomer, Lance Stevens. ------------- (The camera opens on Lance Stevens in a wrestling ring sparring with a partner. Stevens leapfrogs over the other man and then whips him with an armdrag and then hits him with a dropkick. The man falls to the ground and then gets up. Stevens and the man shake hands and Stevens comes outside the ring and faces the camera. He grabs a waterbottle from the ring apron and takes a drink.) Stevens: Well, now that I'm in the AEWA, I'm guessing that everyone wants to see what I can do in the ring. I'm looking forward to showing the fans the action they would expect from me. The first opponent I'd like to face is The Holy Ghost. He's a former light heavyweight champion, so I'm sure he'll be a tough challenge, but I feel I'm up to it. And Ghost, if you've got any plans of bringing your buddies from The Church to ringside, I've got a couple of my own guys to watch my back. (Stevens smiles to the camera, picks up a towel from the ring apron, and throws it on the camera. The towel covers the camera as it fades to black.) -------------- KW: Doesn't this idiot realize that The Church is the most destructive force in the AEWA? CC: More destructive than the ICU or the Angels Of Death? KW: Well.... maybe not THE most destructive, but pretty destructive. CC: Ah ha. Well, we'll let Mr. Wilson get out of this predicament, and move on to comments from Brian Montgomery. ------------- (We see footage of Brian Montgomery defeating a wrestler in the GIWA. Then the camera switches to Montgomery in his hotel room, sitting next to Candice Rochon. They both appear to be very happy, and Brian begins to speak) Brian Montgomery: You see that match, AEWA, was just a little example of what I can do. Sure, that guy was a loser, but I looked good, right? Candice Rochon: And we want to prove it here, in the AEWA. So if you want to challenge Brian.... Brian: Then we challenge you! I'm really looking forward to this, and I hope my first victory, which will also be my first match, is VERY IMPRESSIVE. Candice: Ciao........ (fade to black) --------------- CC: Montgomery seems very poised to do some damage in the singles ranks of the AEWA. KW: He may be able to beat some loser in the GIWA, but I'll bet he can't stand up to the competition here in the AEWA. Let's see how he does when he goes up against guys like Heavy Metal or Deathwatch. CC: You think he can't beat Deathwatch? KW: Didn't I just say that? CC: Well, let's now go to some comments from the aforementioned Deathwatch. -------------- (The camera opens in on a the dark office of Deathwatch. The desk sits in front of the huge window as usual, and there are papers and folders lying on the desk. Deathwatch sits in the black leather chair. Exodus stands to the side of him with his long white cape spread across the floor. His long black hair hangs down to the side of his head and he wears his flashy outfit; he smiles egotistically at the camera. Heavy Metal stands to the other side of Deathwatch with his arms folded; he wears a black shirt, a black leather vest and pants that say "Heavy" on the left and "Metal" on the right. He has a black leather glove on his right hand. Penance sits on the floor fidgeting with a knife in-hand, he wears an all-black outfit and grins as he looks at the camera. Legacy stands behind Deathwatch, standing perfectly still with his arms folded and not saying a word. He wears his usual black outfit with the blue streaks on it, his long brown hair hangs to the sides of his head, but almost in front so it shadows his face. Deathwatch wears his usual gray business suit and a red tie, with a white shirt underneath the suit. He looks at the camera with a stern look on his face. His voice is deep and yet a little scratchy as he speaks) Deathwatch: This is not to my liking, not one bit. Freeman, are you trying to DODGE me? Is that it? You have yourself a bodyguard now, you think that is going to stop me? Hell no. I am willing to wager that MY bodyguards are able to beat the one you have senseless-- and they are all eager to prove this. Heavy Metal is getting a rematch for the world title. It is time for him to become the two-time AEWA champion.... Actually, two-time ICU champion! (Heavy Metal raises his head a bit and then lowers it down. He has the look of a madman on his face and speaks with his usual deep voice) Heavy Metal: Mike Stewart, you've held that title for too long already. Come the scaffold match, I'm gonna show you the Megadeth Power Bomb twenty feet down to the concrete. There'll be nothin' left for ya. You'll be a broken man with broken bones. Your career ended as soon as you defeated me for my title. It's time to show the world why they call me Heavy Metal. As for the dude that said he's disappointed in me-- SCREW YOU, PAL! I never ONCE said I liked this piece of sh*t for a federation! Not ONCE did I say the AEWA was my home, and not ONCE did I commit to being a top AEWA superstar. My home always is and always will be with International Contracts Unlimited! Mike Stewart, you have enjoyed your last moment of glory. I will not be deprived of my revenge and I am going to make you SUFFER! That title will go around my waist, I guarantee ya this. Deathwatch: Heavy Metal has his agenda set, how about Exodus? He can speak for himself.... (Exodus cracks a half-smile and looks into the camera. He has his ACEW Championship around his waist and he pats it with his right hand) Exodus: I have my gold, so I am not looking for any of yours, but if I do happen to run into some along the way-- Hell, I can melt it down with the rest of this crap federation! Those of you who know me; fear me. Those of you who fear me; acknowledge my power. And those of you who have no f*cking clue.... should rot in hell for their lack of respect! I didn't come to this hell hole to find myself lost in a world of idiots, but guess f*cking what? That's exactly where I ended up! Piece of sh*t federation, piece of sh*t wrestlers! (Exodus breathes in and out for a minute and then closes his eyes. He tilts his head toward the camera and slowly opens his eyes.) Exodus: Every single one of you will give me the respect I deserve, no matter what the limits. No matter how much pain I have to give you worthless excuses for wrestlers. You will all realize my power, and right now, I have to issue a challenge.... Heaven & Hell, you think you are the true masters of reality-- why don't you come out and face reality square in the eyes, and get in the ring with us? We'll send Crowley down to hell where he belongs, and Chaos can dream about being in heaven while his master Satan preaches on! Cardinal Chaos and his church.... The Church of the Pounding Light.... The original in the AEWA, but now he must face up to the new era and stand with me and Legacy. We shall truly see who's ground falls through from under them. This will be no ordinary match; this will be a match with.... (thinks about it) Yeah, a steel cage. This will be a match with.... That's right, barbed f*cking wire. This will be a match where the only way to win is to get over the top, and I'm making that a special way of escape. You see, the top of the cage will be lined with oil, so if you can make it that far-- Happy landings is the best I can say! HA HA HA! Deathwatch: And now, pound for pound the best in his division, the man of darkness, the man that cannot be manipulated, the man that serves his own purpose; and let us not forget, the man that extracts pain on himself to prove a point.... You are looking at your next ICU Light Heavyweight champion-- Penance! (Penance stands up, with his black hair hanging in front of his face. He walks up to the camera with an evil smile on his face, and twirls the blade he was holding in between his fingers, cutting himself each time it switches from finger to finger. The blood covers his hand and runs down his arm as he begins to speak) Penance: For a long time, I have been out of action. Most people didn't even know that I was gone-- most didn't care. But now I am going to give them reason to care. I am going to teach the men that once mocked Penance's presence. I have my list, and I'm checking it twice, but no, I am not Santa Claus. I'm the reaper coming to take each and every one of your lives, and I have the first morbid soul to push my vengeance on.... And that is Super Blonde. Non-title or title, I do not care, your punishment will come either way. You have something that I want, and that is YOUR LIFE! Each time you meet up with me will be part of your life taken from you. Piece after piece after piece until there is no more left to take-- and you will be left with nothing. Left with a shattered dream. I remember when I first came here.... You have come quite a long way, but you will always be the little man that pulled off big feats to me. Nothing will soothe my pleasure more than to throw you around and teach you what a true beating feels like. Then when it is all said and done.... I wouldn't be surprised to see you lying in the gutter with no hopes of greatness-- ever again. Deathwatch: Sewerdog.... Oh, how you underestimate the corporation. A tag team match, you say? With your own mystery partner? Well, I think that sounds more than fair. Now you want Heavy Metal? I'm sure he is more than willing to oblige you-- how about it, Metal? Heavy Metal: There's nothin' I'd want more than to kick the sh*t out of Sewerpunk again. I think this dog has bitten off a lil' more than he can chew. Sewerdog, you want another shot at me? Come and get it, dude! You know where to find me! Deathwatch: That he does indeed. Now, as I was so rudely edited the last time, I have some legal comments to make. Heaven & Hell, the president of the World Wide E-Wrestling organization has told me to tell you something. Your name is going over as copyright infringement to one of my clients' wrestlers. Yes, it seems there has been a Heaven & Hell in WWEW for quite some time now. It's time for you to either A) Pick a new name and my client will not take action. Or B) Keep the name and allow ME to take the action.... and I seriously hope you choose choice "B", because I have waited a long time to show you the falsities of your religion. I will show Crowley what it truly means to be "buried alive". Maybe a deep sea burial is called for-- cement shoes fit nicely on men that are already dead! HA HA HA! (Deathwatch looks into the camera with a sadistic grin) Gryphon, what's this? You don't like my "attitude toward the federation"? Well, guess what poor Gryphon--too damn BAD! I'm here whether you like it or not, and don't think that I will not destroy anyone that opposes me in a heartbeat. And with you trying to pose as some sort of threat and be the hero to the world, then I have news for you.... Good luck. You will definitely be needing it! (fade to black) ------------------ CC: Quite a statement being made there by the ICU. KW: I can't wait for them to do what they just said they'll do. CC: They certainly do look determined. Penance seems especially-- intense. KW: Intense? He's crazy, and that's just the way the ICU wants him. CC: He certainly is more than enough of that. Well fans, here are some comments from the National tag team champions, The Machines. ------------- (Simon O'Neal is working out on the treadmill as Paul Wong enters the room.) Paul: I just receive the word; our first title defense will be against Graphic Violence. Simon: (Gets off the treadmill and rolls his eyes) Great. I wanted an easy match, but come on.... Paul: They're a pretty tough team; we saw them beat the Graveyard Shift, and they're the #2 contenders right now. Simon: Maybe. But somehow a team with wrestlers named "Leech" and "Booze" doesn't put the fear of God in me. How the hell did they get a title shot? Paul: Maybe they got a Vice-President drunk and tricked him. Simon: Yeah, maybe they.... (turns and stares at Paul) When did you become such a smart ass? Paul: Must be the company I keep. Graphic Violence is better than you claim, but we can beat them. Simon: And when we beat these jokers maybe they'll give us some real competition, like the West Coast Connection-- I know they keep calling us jokers. Give us a chance, and we'll laugh all the way to owning both tag team titles. Until then, we'll have to hold our noses and fight Booze and Leech. I mean that literally. Have you ever been up close to those two? (Paul and Simon continue their discussion as the camera fades) ------------- CC: Well, it seems The Machines are still having their problems, but not as much as they were. They now seem to be looking forward to facing Graphic Violence. KW: Yeah, and they also challenged The West Coast Connection. I mean, how stupid are they? CC: What do you mean? KW: What I mean is that The West Coast Connection will destroy them if they ever meet in the ring. CC: We'll have to wait and see. Here now are some comments from Sir Gallade. --------------- (The camera opens on Sir Gallade, sitting alone in his courtyard. He turns to the camera, and smiles.) Gallade: As you can see, Lady Guinevere is not by my side anymore. That is because she has been called back to England by our king. I guess he wants to rekindle his sex life or something. I am now without a manager and I will be able to concentrate on my matches and challenges better. Now, down to business, The Angels of Death have brought in Deranged. Oh, now I am scared.... YEAH RIGHT!!! Just one more rulebreaker to be taught a lesson. I have challenged Grim Reaper to a match and there was no response, then I challenged Ricky Roberts to a match and once again there was no response. Just a couple of cowards I guess. Now, once again I am challenging Ricky Roberts to a match at an upcoming Saturday night card or Wednesday night card, it does not matter to me, I just want him in the ring! You see Angels, there is a group of guys here in the AEWA that are not afraid of you all, and you are in for a big surprise in the weeks to come. That goes for all of the scum around here. Hope to see you soon, Roberts. (fade to black) --------------- KW: What the hell is this guy's problem? Is he stuck in the medieval ages? Lady Guinevere is going to back to the King of England? Last time I checked there was no King of England, only Queen Elizabeth. CC: Well, Gallade seems bent on defeating the Angels Of Death. He hasn't gotten a response to his last couple of challenges, but he's still challenging Ricky Roberts. KW: No contest. Roberts all the way. CC: I'll have to disagree with you on that. Let's hear now from the new AEWA North American champion and first-ever Triple Crown champion, Big Ron Raider. ------------- (The camera opens with Raider in a locker room.) Raider: Everyone in the AEWA is talking about me winning the AEWA North American Title and they should be! No one else in the history of the AEWA has achieved this amount of success. Not Heavy Metal, not Deranged, not even my new associate Cardinal Chaos has managed to win the AEWA Triple Crown. Now Crazy Cook or whatever his name is gets a shot at the NA title. Well crazy boy, it ain't going to be as easy as you think. I've been away for awhile and the taste of gold has rekindled my competitive fire. Also, what's up with the ICU? Is that where they want to be placed, in the Intensive Care Unit? Crazy man.... its RAIDER TIME! (Camera fades to black.) ---------------- CC: The North American champion Big Ron Raider is sending out a message to everyone here in the AEWA. KW: Well, this man is big and strong. He's got all of the tools, and he's shown on several occasions that he's championship material. He's gonna be tough to beat. CC: Well, somebody's got to be able to stop him. KW: Who? Marcus McTeague? Randall Osbourne? Please. Raider could beat both of them in one match by himself-- with one arm tied behind his back. CC: I highly doubt that. Now let's hear from AEWA newcomer Kyle Hatcher. --------------- (The camera opens on Kyle Hatcher with his feet up on the couch in the living room of his Seattle home. He is reading "Executive Orders" by Tom Clancy. He then puts down the book and looks to the camera.) Hatcher: I've been waiting around for awhile for one guy here in the AEWA. That guy is Shane Matthews. This guy has no respect for anyone and he honestly believes that his "End of the Road" top-rope Frankensteiner is the best in the business. Well, let's see you put it to the test, pal. (Hatcher takes a glass of water from the coffee table in front of his couch and takes a drink. He then sets the glass back down.) I've challenged you before, but I'm gonna do it one more time. This time with a little stipulation-- the only way that there is to be a winner in our match is with the top-rope Frankensteiner. That way we'll finally know who has the best one in the AEWA. Of course, I already know that I do, but I'm sure with your big ego, you should be willing to put yours to the test. So, President Freeman, I'd like you to sign this match. That is if Matthews has the guts. (The camera fades to black, as Hatcher goes back to reading the book.) -------------- KW: Jeez, Hatcher is REALLY asking for it. When he finally gets in the ring with Shane Matthews, I'll be there to say "I told ya so." CC: Don't fool yourself, Ken-- he's perfectly capable of defeating Matthews. In fact, I can GUARANTEE that he will. KW: Want to bet on it? CC: Sorry, I'm not a gambling man. And now, for our final interview, Skim Hoss is gonna conduct another LIVE, in-studio interview with Twisted Steel. We did this last week, and we're doing it again tonight. KW: This should be cool. CC: Okay, Hoss is ready for the interview, so let's watch.... (The camera switches to Skim Hoss, standing on the interview platform. He takes the microphone, smiles at the camera, and begins to speak.) Hoss: Ladies and gentlemen, I have a big interview for you this week! Last time, there seemed to be some problems between Twisted Steel and his fellow "Angels Of Death". Tonight, I'm going to talk with him again, and try to find out if anything was settled. So now, please welcome, the former World champion, TWISTED STEEL! (Twisted Steel walks out from behind the curtain, wearing a black parka. He steps up to the platform, and shakes hands with Hoss.) Steel: Hey, how ya doing? Hoss: I'm fine, sir, but let's get right down to business. First of all, are you getting along with the other Angels Of Death? After last week's interview, there seemed to be some problems. Can you explain that situation to us? Steel: Well, I had a long talk with Ricky Roberts that night. You see, folks, I REALLY don't like the current situation with the Angels. The Grim Reaper is nothing more than my PARTNER, but he pretends to be the LEADER. Let me get one thing straight-- I have no leader. The Grim Reaper isn't anything special, so why should I run around like his sidekick? HE should be shining MY shoes. That's the only problem I have with the Angels Of Death right now; I don't get enough respect. Hoss: But I'm not sure what you mean. What has the Grim Reaper done to make you so angry? He doesn't talk much, so how could he possibly boss you around? Steel: It's not what he SAYS, it's just the way he acts. For instance, remember when we all appeared on the Terrordome show? Hoss: Of course. Steel: Well, the Grim Reaper came out first. In fact, he ALWAYS comes out first. I'm just getting sick and tired of being the follower-- it's time for me to be the leader. (Without any warning, the Grim Reaper and Rick Verrano storm out to the stage. The Reaper violently pushes Hoss off the stage, as Verrano grabs the microphone from Twisted Steel. They both look rather angry.) Verrano: What's going on, Steel? We heard you talking about us, and we DIDN'T like it. Now we want an answer-- are you with us, or AGAINST us? Steel: I really don't know anymore. (points to the Grim Reaper) This guy seems to think that he's better than me.... Verrano: (pushes Steel's finger back) It's not polite to point, buddy. Now let me make one thing clear-- the Grim Reaper IS better than you, and he always will be! In the Angels Of Death, HE is your boss. If you can't handle that, get the hell out! Steel: What the hell are you talking about? When I joined this alliance, we were all EQUALS. Now you're telling me that HE'S the boss. What changed? Verrano: YOU changed, buddy. Steel: (yelling) In that case, I QUIT! If the Angels Of Death are going to be run like this, THEN YOU CAN KISS MY ASS! (Twisted Steel suddenly reaches out and SLAPS Verrano. The force causes Verrano to fall off the stage, and he lands hard on the concrete. Twisted Steel turns to the Grim Reaper, and NAILS him with an uppercut.)
