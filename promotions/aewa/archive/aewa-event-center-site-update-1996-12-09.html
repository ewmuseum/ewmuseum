AEWA Event Center (site update, 12/9)

9 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ladies and gentlemen, I have several big announcements! First off, the AEWA web site now has frames! Just go to our regular address ( http://www.california.com/~bullet/index.html ) and decide whether you want the "frames version" or "non frames". Then, check out the new look! Absolutely EVERYTHING has been updated! Plus, today is the grand opening of AEWA Wrestling Chat! We're going to have some BIG guests making appearances in there soon, so be sure to visit it daily! We'll send out a notice whenever a big guest is going to be in the chat room. Also, as if all this wasn't enough, we just introduced the AEWA News Network today! It's ONLY available in the frames version, at the bottom of the main page. Be sure to check this little thing out-- it ALWAYS has up-to-date AEWA news, scrolling 24 hours a day! We're going to be updating it a LOT, so check it out! Well folks, I hope you like the big changes. I'd really like to thank Aaron (from the Extreme Wrestling Association) for allowing me to use his frame design. Anyway, check out the AEWA Event Center's new look! Once again, the address is the same ( http://www.california.com/~bullet/index.html ), so we'll see you there! Sincerely, President Freeman
