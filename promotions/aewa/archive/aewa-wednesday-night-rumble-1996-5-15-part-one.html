(5-6) Double T Playerz (0-3) Mayhem II jumped Thompson as he was removing his jacket, connecting a series of double sledges. He dragged Thompson into the Mayhem corner, and the Nat'l champs lived up to their name. Among the maneuvers demonstrating Mayhem Inc.'s technical expertise and finesse: kicks in the ribs, an eye gouge, the occasional choke, and Mayhem I holding a handful of Thompson's hair, pulling back his head in the process and exposing the adam's apple to the pressure applied by Mayhem II's boot. This sort of thing went on for some time, as Mayhem, Inc. complimented frequent tags with frequent double-teaming (obviating the necessity for tags, actually). Meanwhile Terrell Thomas languished on the ring apron, awaiting the tag. After some 5-6 minutes of this action being repeated, Mayhem I whipped Thomas into an neutral corner, then whipped Mayhem II after him, adding greater force to their avalanche finisher. Hardball had snuck up on the ring apron with a chair, and held it up in front of Thompson, and Mayhem II ran headlong into it. Somehow both the sight of the chair-laden manager, and the resounding "clank", both escaped the attention of our ref, or he just chose to ignore them, and the match continued. Terrell Thomas decided that a tag was no longer needed to introduce him to the ring, and he scampered to the top rope and hit Mayhem I with a flying double axe-handle. In the meantime, Thompson covered II for a 2-count. Josh Brown decided to join the fine, despite not being explicitly invited, and Hardball felt there was no reason who should be the only man left out. While the six men bralwed in mid-ring (for those interested, Thompson was on top of II, with a hand on each side of II's head, banging it into the mat; Mayhem I had gained control over Thomas at this point and was choking him with one of the tag ropes; Josh Brown was stomping away at Hardball, who quite conincidentally had fallen face first to the mat when Brown hit him in the back of the head witha title belt).
