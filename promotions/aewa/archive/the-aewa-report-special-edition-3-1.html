The AEWA Report, Special Edition (3/1)

2 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
------------------------- ***************** AEWA REPORT: SPECIAL EDITION ------------------------- ***************** Welcome to the eleventh official edition of the AEWA Report! This is a special edition, and it's absolutely PACKED with information. We have plenty of big news to tell you about, so read on.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ RECENT NEWS ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ --JOE REED WINS THE WORLD TITLE! During one of the most exciting matches from Kickoff '97, "Crimson" Joe Reed pulled off a victory against Deranged, becoming the new AEWA World Heavyweight Champion. He had the Lady In Red in his corner, and the capacity crowd seemed to support him. At one point, Reed even managed to power bomb Deranged through the broadcast table! Reed is going to be interviewed by Hank Thompson on the next episode of Saturday Night Slam, so check it out. --FREEMAN GAINS CONTROL! President Freeman unveiled Heaven & Hell as his mystery team at Kickoff '97, and they were able to defeat Kevin Larkin and the Visigothic Horde in an exciting match. The victory came after Crowley shocked Larkin with a powerful legdrop, and scored the winning pinfall. Oddly enough, immediately following the bout, a tearful Larkin sold the Horde to the ICU. Before he could even finish the interview, though, Mister Crowley and Cardinal Chaos broke into the room. Chaos offered the Horde their "salvation", and they left with him. This creates an interesting dilemma-- now that Larkin is gone, which stable will the Horde stay with? The Church or ICU? Our reporters have been trying to reach Deathwatch for an interview, but he refused to comment. --VALACHI IS BACK IN BUSINESS! In another shocking moment at Kickoff '97, the Holy Ghost's mystery opponent was revealed to be none other than Joey Valachi. He went on to defeat the Ghost in a controversial manner, making him the AEWA's first four-time Light Heavyweight champion. We wish Valachi the best of luck as champ. -- SUPERMAN HAS RETURNED TO THE AEWA! We're very happy to announce that "Superman" Mike Stewart made his return at Kickoff '97, and he's looking better than ever. He also had some rather shocking allegations, however, claiming that President Freeman was trying to keep him out of action. The crowd seemed to be 100% behind Stewart, as always. He will be holding a special press conference tomorrow afternoon, and we'll show you the footage on Saturday Night Slam. --SEWERDOG JOINS ICU! During a shocking episode of the Terrordome at Kickoff '97, Sewerdog announced his membership with ICU. After losing to the Extremist on the Countdown to Kickoff program, Sewerdog was desperately in need of some guidance-- and Deathwatch was more than happy to supply it. Our reporters had a chance to talk with Sewerdog after the event, and he warned us that "the AEWA is about to go through a state of Sewacide." --PENANCE INTRODUCES THE APOCALYPSE! Shortly after Sewerdog's announcement on the Terrordome, Penance gave us a surprise of his own, by quitting ICU. He then introduced Blackout, Nightshade, and Pestilence, his new partners, forming the AEWA'S newest stable-- the Apocalypse. Not surprisingly, a big brawl ensued with the ICU. This is a HUGE story, and we still don't have all the details, but be sure to watch Penance square off against Heavy Metal on the next episode of Saturday Night Slam. --DID LEGACY BREAK HIS NECK? After the Gryphon super bombed him onto the hard concrete at Kickoff '97, Legacy's future is uncertain. According to backstage doctors, his condition is "severe" and he might be forced to retire from action. This is especially unfortunate, since Legacy never even had a chance to make his professional debut. We hope to being you an update on his condition soon. --CHAOS LEADS GALLADE INTO THE CHURCH! Without a doubt, there will be one moment from Kickoff '97 that we'll never forget-- Sir Gallade betraying the Knights of Deliverance, and joining the Church. It was, in our opinion, one of the most despicable acts in the history of wrestling. As you all know, Chaos and Gallade have a long history, and until last night, we thought that they hated each other. But during the Television title defense, Gallade and Cardinal Chaos teamed up to attack Damian Steele with a Singapore cane. Gallade refused to speak with our reporters, but we plan on bringing you an interview soon. --THE ASSASSINS WIN THE NATIONAL TAG BELTS! Graphic Violence were unable to hold onto their National tag team titles at Kickoff '97, when the Assassins pinned them after an incredibly fast-paced match. The victory came after Leech Rogers got into an altercation with the referee, not paying attention to Shockwave and Banshee. It was definitely a mistake, because the Assassins are now wearing the belts. Congratulations, guys! --PRIMAL FEAR GOES EXTREME, CRAWFORD LOSES BLOOD! Stan Crawford and Primal Fear got into a wild brawl during their match at Kickoff '97, and it got WAY out of control. The referee was forced to stop the match, after deciding that Crawford was losing too much blood. We have learned that Crawford is demanding a "first blood" match against Primal Fear now, and that match has been signed for the next pay-per-view. --DAMIAN STEELE JOINS ICU! After much speculation about his intentions in the AEWA, Damian Steele finally showed his true colors. At Kickoff '97, Steele and the ICU brutally attacked the Gryphon, shocking EVERYONE in the packed MGM Grand. According to our sources, Deathwatch and Steele have some sort of a friendship that can be traced all the way back to their days in WWEW. We hope to get an interview with them both soon, so stay tuned. --DEATHWATCH WINS THE NORTH AMERICAN BELT! In an incredible triangle match, Deathwatch managed to pin Justin Trouble for the North American title. Of course, he didn't do it alone. Shortly after Trouble had eliminated Leroy Brown, Deathwatch left the ring and went down to ringside, where he began to taunt Traci. Naturally, Justin came to her defense, but it was all a trick-- and she punched him in the nose! Deathwatch capitalized on this distraction, and got the victory. --JOSE MAKES A RUN FOR THE BORDER! After several months of inactivity, "Ultimate" Jose Gomez finally decided to leave the Church. He made the announcement on the Countdown to Kickoff program, and had some VERY strong comments for Cardinal Chaos. We got an exclusive interview with Gomez, and here's a portion of what he said: "Chaos can take that damn group and shove it up his ASS! I never needed those idiots, and I never will," he told us. --THE ZACK DERRINGER SHOW IS COMING! Folks, we have some interesting news for you. The ICU's own commentator, Zack Derringer, has decided to start his OWN interview show. He was angry over the way werepresented the ICU on Straight Talk, so now he has decided to start the Zack Derringer Show, which will be premiering sometime soon. If you're interested in appearing on it, write to Zack at [detail withheld] with your request. ------------------------------------- AEWA TRIVIA QUESTION ------------------------------------- Question: Who won the first match between Heavy Metal and Volcano Jackson? Look for the answer later in this issue! ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ INJURY REPORT ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ LEGACY is currently in critical condition, after being super bombed onto the concrete by the Gryphon. He is having some major spinal problems as a result of this incident, and according to doctors, he may be forced to retire from wrestling. Unfortunately, this will probably be the end of Legacy's career in the AEWA. STAN CRAWFORD was taken to the hospital during Kickoff '97, as a result of his vicious brawl with Primal Fear. He required several stitches in his head, but other than that, he's doing quite well. Crawford isn't expected to miss any matches because of this injury. ******************* CHAMPION LIST ******************** AEWA World Heavyweight Champion-- "Crimson" Joe Reed AEWA North American Champion-- Deathwatch AEWA Television Champion-- Sir Gallade AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion-- Joey Valachi AEWA World Tag Team Champions-- The Machines AEWA National Tag Team Champions-- The Assassins ******************* THE TOP 10 (SINGLES)**************** 1. This week, we have two VERY interesting editorials.... ~~~~ I CHANGED MY MIND ~~~~ By: Ricky Roberts Status: AEWA Wrestler Well, for the first time in my life, I was wrong. Several weeks ago, I wrote an article about "Crimson" Joe Reed, saying that he never should have been given a shot at the World title. Well, I have changed my mind about that guy. After watching Kickoff '97, I realized that Reed is actually quite talented. He was able to power bomb Deranged through a wooden table, which is NOT easy to do. As soon as I saw that, Joe Reed earned my respect. Although I didn't like him at first, Reed could actually be a positive for the AEWA. He combines the old amateur type of wrestling with the new "extreme" style, and I have to admit-- it's entertaining. Now, I'm a totally different kind of guy than Joe Reed, and if I ever meet him, I'll probably hate his guts.... but he deserves some respect. This guy has been around the wrestling world for twelve years, which is more than most people can say. He started out in the old IWA, as part of the popular "Crimson Kids" tag team, and quickly rose to stardom. Now, after over a decade of competing, Reed has finally achieved the pinnacle of success-- the AEWA World Heavyweight. I might not like the guy's personality, but he deserves that belt. Anyway, I just wanted to write this article as an "apology" to Reed. He made me eat my words, and that doesn't happen very often. I wish him the best of luck in that upcoming four-way match at AEWA Extreme. ~~~~ FOUR-WAY FURY! ~~~~ By: Chuck Carlson Status: Host of AEWA Straight Talk I'm really excited about that four-way match at AEWA Extreme, folks. "Crimson" Joe Reed is up for the biggest challenge of his life, as he puts the World Heavyweight title on the line against Heavy Metal, Ben Slocum, and Mister Crowley. This match has some special meaning for AEWA fans, too, and here's why.... When the AEWA was first formed in early 1996, Mister Crowley dominated the ranks. He was, without a doubt, the biggest superstar in the wrestling world at that time. After winning the belt, Crowley defended it against everyone-- and he did it successfully. Since those days, however, Crowley has had to share the spotlight with other stars-- Like Ben Slocum, for instance. After Slocum defeated Crowley for the World title during one of the reigns, we had a new icon in the AEWA. Slocum totally dominated the ranks, disposing of everyone who got in his way. With his family backing him up, there didn't seem to be ANYONE who could stop him. But several months later, there was ANOTHER new leader in the AEWA, calling himself Heavy Metal. Standing over seven feet tall, this guy manhandled every foe that challenged him. Metal held the title longer than anyone else, and his Megadeth Power Bomb was one of wrestling's most feared maneuvers. But now, we have yet another new force in the AEWA-- "Crimson" Joe Reed. This guy is the federation's latest franchise player, and he wants to etch his place in history. Unfortunately, this is going to be quite a challenge, because he has to get past three of the deadliest wrestlers in the world. Can Reed do it? Will he be able to defy the odds? One thing is for sure, ladies and gentlemen-- the winner of this match will be the only TRUE icon in the AEWA. ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ KEEP IN CONTACT ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ If you have an angle idea, interview, or anything else, send it over to President Freeman.
