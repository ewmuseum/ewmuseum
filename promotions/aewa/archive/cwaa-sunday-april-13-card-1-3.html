Sunday, April 13, Card (1/3)

13 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last week, on Championship Wrestling Association of America (Split screen, pretty hi-tech for the CWAA, with the battle royale action (shown below) in one half and a replay of this in the other: As he turns to leave, an old gentleman in a trenchcoat and hunched over a walker makes his way painfully slowly down the row to the aisle. Kore Wrench begins to offer a blessing for the gentleman, who in turn offers a quick spray of an unidentified substance in the face. As Wrench rolls on the arena floor clutching at his eyes, Lt. Lawson sprints from the ring and starts putting the boots to him, before dragging Wrench to the ring as the Major cackles.) ************************************************************t***************** EL: Hi, everyone, and welcome to another edition of Championship Wrestling Association of America, here in the world-famous Sportatorium in Dallas, Texas. I'm your host, Eric Lane. As you saw last week, it was Lt. Lawson and Major Mayhem, almost literally using everything but the kitchen sink to eliminate "Preacher" Kore Wrench and win the battle royal to become our first-ever CWAA Television Champion- and they succeeded. Kore held on for a long time- he was the last man eliminated- but it took the additional interference of one Max Robertson. While the Lt. celebrated in his barracks, we saw Twister dispatch the Pretty Boy and Marvelous Michael to win the CWAA Tag Team Title tournament- and then Randy Sheradin and Max Robertson had one more go-around for the CWAA Heavyweight Title, and after a long, grueling encounter, it was the Dreamer who put the Professional's shoulders to the mat yet again. We've got more exciting championship news, but first, let's go to David Myers for the introductions to this week's first match. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is set for- (Suddenly every light within the Sportatorium is extinguished) EL: Well, we seem to be having some technical difficulties; I'm not sure exactly what's going on, but undoubtedly our mainten. . . (The Gregorian Chants begin to play from throughout the Sportatorium) EL (loudly, over the cheering crowd): Perhaps the Preacher will be making an appearance, but I don't. . . No, there! I see something! It looks like a floating cross! (indeed, a floating cross has appeared in the darkness near one of the entrances.) And it's moving to. . ...... (Eric hardly be heard over the crowd noise. The lights of the Sportatorium slowly are raised. "The Preacher" Kore Wrench is wearing his new costume, the chest of which is covered by the white, glowing cross. He makes his way toward the ring, stopping to shake hands with as many fans as possible on the way. He takes the microphone from DM and proceeds to mid-ring) KW: Thank you (he can barely be heard over the roaring crowd) Thank you. (He raises his hands and the exuberant crowd obediently hushes.) As everyone here knows, The Preacher had a rough day right here last week. I'm not going to mince words. I'm not going to lie. I'm not going to evade responsibility. The truth is, I failed last week. I failed myself. I failed God, and I failed all of you (he points out toward his fans in the Sportatorium). The forces of evil were too much for me last week. But I want all of you to remember, that I didn't give up. Not after I had been blinded by Lawson, not when I was outnumbered and outmanned, not even when Lawson had me in that sleeper hold. The spirit was willing, but the flesh is weak. Now, I don't want that to happen again. To prevent that from happening again, I'm going to need a little help from all of you out there. The next time that happens, I want each and every one of you out there to scream your hearts out. I want you to scream so loud, the roof shakes. I want you to make enough noise to wake the dead! That's what I need from you. There's one more think I want to make clear. I have nothing but respect for the American Flag. What I destroyed was Mayhem's flagPOLE. You see, those two lunatics want you to think that this is about Church battling State, but it's not. That's just one more lie. This is about good against evil. (he pauses for a moment as the crowd pops once again.) And I don't care what costume evil wears. . . if it's dressed like a man of the cloth, like that cult of lunatics over in the AEWA, or in a military uniform. Evil is evil, and I will not tolerate it!! (he pauses as the crowd pops) So what are you going to do, if Lawson hits me with another sleeper tonight?! (The crowd pops) What? I can't here you!!! (he holds the microphone out toward the crowd which roars even louder) That's it! That's exactly right. Thank you, and God bless. (The Preacher then gives the microphone away and exits the ring area.) DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this first contest is set for one fall with a 15-minute time limit. ("Bad to the Bone" plays, to the expected round of boos.) Introducing first, from Detroit, Michigan, checking in at 310 lb., accompanied by Marc Thomas, here is Jak Hammer! (The 6'10"-310lb. Hammer, in his black leather pants, boots and sleeveless top, plus the fingerless gloves (perhaps the fingers are with the sleeves?), with long brown hair and brown eyes is followed by his ally. Thomas, at 6'0"-240 is almost completely eclipsed by Hammer. He has short brown hair, brown eyes, and a chiseled body (so does Hammer, for that matter). Thomas and Hammer appear to shop together, as well, since Thomas is clad in a sleeveless leather jacket with black boots and trunks. Hammer head straight for Myers and yanks the mike from his hand.) Jak Hammer: Tonight, I am facing a young strongman named Stan Stevens. Some people have said that he is the strongest man in the CWAA. Well Stevens, they don't come any stronger than me. You're a big kid with a lot of power, but that doesn't mean you intimidate me. You'll find out exactly what power is when you step into the ring with me. Now, there is someone else I need to address. Lt. Lawson, that TV title does not belong around your waist. I was never disqualified from the Rumble. If you have any guts at all, you'll do the honorable thing and give me a shot at the belt. If you want to do this the hard way, I can come and take the belt from you! I'm going to be a thorn in your side until I get my chance to prove who deserves to wear that TV belt. (JH tosses the mike over his shoulder to Thomas, who deftly catches it.) Marc Thomas: This week, I've got to face Travis Conley, one of the best tag team wrestlers in the CWAA. What Conley doesn't realize is that this won't be a tag team match! I've beaten bigger and stronger men than Conley before and this match will be no different. I may not be the most popular wrestler here in the CWAA, but when the time comes I prove that I am the best. Sheridan, I want a shot at the CWAA World Title. You beat me once on your way to winning that belt and you and I both know that I deserve another chance to beat you. However, after you watch what I do to Conley, you may decide to just give me the belt instead of actually getting hurt and losing it. (Thomas hands the mike back to David Myers and takes his place on the floor outside the ring.) DM: And his opponent- ("You Got the Power" by X plays as "Powerhouse" Stan Stevens arrives to a decent face pop) DM: -from San Jose, California, at 335 lb., here is Stan "the Powerhouse" Steven! (Stevens stands 6'7"-335, with a power-lifter's build and short chestnut hair. He is garbed in blue spandex tight and gold lining, while his blue boots bear the inscription "Stan the Man".) DM: Your referee for this contest, Jeff Howard. EL: OK, almost 700 lb. of humanity in that ring as Stevens and Hammer square off, and if Hammer is thinking too much about Lt. Lawson, he'll soon get a reminder of who's in there with him now in a hurry. Staredown in the ring, each man trying to intimidate the other to start things off. Hammer with a two-handed shove that knocks Stevens back a step or two- Stevens replies in kind- and there goes Jak Hammer, tossed into the corner. (Big pop as Stevens flexes while Hammer glares.) EL: Now Hammer showing he wants a test of strength- a game of mercy, I think kids call it. Stevens obliges and reaches up- and Hammer smacks a knee into his exposed midsection! Jak Hammer now with a double sledge as Stevens doubles over- Irish whip- clothesline- Stevens ducks! Stevens off the ropes now- back heel kick! Stevens is not the quickest man in the ring, but his execution is always spot-on. Jak Hammer heads to the floor to discuss strategy with Marc Thomas now. Interesting to see the two out here together like this. We've seen them work in tandem before, when Jak Hammer faced Joey Haven in the steel cage match, just a week before Thomas was to face Haven to see who would get the shot at Joey Valachi's AEWA Title the next week. OK, Jak Hammer heading back into the ring. Collar and elbow tie-up, both men jostling for position- Stevens gets the leverage edge though as he drives Jak Hammer back to the corner again- Hammer mentioned the power game before, but so far Stevens has demonstrated a pretty clear edge. OK- Senior Official Jeff Howard calls for the clean break. (Cliche alert) EL: Stevens backs up- and Hammer with a kick in the midsection. Now he spins Stevens around into the corner- a shot to the abdomen, and now an elbow to the head, cross corner whip, and Stevens hits the turnbuckles hard. Hammer follows him in- no one home! (Pop as Hammer runs into the corner) EL: Stevens catches him as he bounces out- belly to back suplex. Now the Powerhouse with a big fistdrop. Stevens stands him up now- short clothesline! Now Thomas reaches in and starts dragging Hammer out of the ring. Stevens getting on Senior Official Jeff Howard's case about it, and rightfully so, but he should have just pulled Hammer back- he's got about 100 lb. on Thomas, as has to be at least twice as strong. But Jak Hammer out on the floor with Marc Thomas now, and Stan Stevens in the ring. Well, we're going to take a quick break, and when we get back, we'll have the conclusion of this match. (Join the WYXZ-TV 39 news team weekdays at 6 for all the breaking news, plus Chip James with sports and Tai West with the weather, and our award-winning agricultural reports. Right here at WYXZ-TV 39, weekdays at 6.) EL: OK- Jak Hammer back in the ring now with Stan Stevens. It's been all Stevens so far, and I have to wonder if the rigors of the AEWA Extreme Match with Joey Haven has had lingering effects on Jak Hammer- we'll show some footage from that match later in this broadcast, by the way. And speaking of the AEWA, we'll be talking about negotiations for more AEWA title chances here on Championship Wrestling. OK- Stan Stevens off the ropes- hey! Marc Thomas just reached in and grabbed at his ankle! Stevens turns to swipe at Thomas- and Jak Hammer with a running knee right in the back. Now Hammer with an Irish whip- spinebuster slam! He's going to try and get Stevens off the mat now, and an impressive display of power as Jak Hammer drops Stevens across his knee- back breaker. You can here Thomas telling him to "do it now", and it looks like he's going for the Rebel Rack backbreaker now that he's softened up that back- he's got him up- that's almost 350 lb., folks. He's got him up- and that's it. Jeff Howard calls for the bell, and it's going to be Jak Hammer with the win by submission over Stan Stevens. Well, Marc Thomas won't have far to go- he'll be in our next matchup, and it looks like he and Jak Hammer are just going to trade places. You know, Marc Thomas is just skyrocketing up the rankings here in the CWAA, and so far he has been the only CWAA star to get to compete for some AEWA gold. I'm sure the shots at CWAA titles are just around the corner for this budding star, but he's about to step in that ring with someone who already has a CWAA title belt. Let's go back to Dave Myers for the introductions for this one. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next bout is set for one fall with a 15-minute time limit. In the ring to my right, from Charlotte, North Carolina, he weighs 240 lb. here is Marc Thomas! (The crowd boos as Thomas raises his arm in acknowledgement of the "cheers".) DM: And his opponent- (Pops galore as the "Twister" theme plays and Travis Conley and Mother Nature walk out.) DM: - from Lincoln, Nebraska, weighing in at 315 lb., one-half of the CWAA Tag Team Champions, here is Travis Conley! (Conley is 6'8"-315, with short blond hair and blue eyes, and dark brown trunks with a little tornado logo on the part the French would call the derriere.) DM: Your referee, Chris Herman. EL: OK- CWAA Tag Team Champion Travis Conley in there now with Marc Thomas, and Jak Hammer hasn't gone anywhere yet. Hey! As Travis Conley was handing his title belt to the ring attendant, Thomas clipped his legs, diving into the back of this knees! The bell hasn't even rung yet, and Marc Thomas stomping away on the leg and knee. Now he rolls Conley onto his stomach, and hangs the leg out of the ring- now he pulls back on the ankle, and he's pulling that leg against the bottom rope! Chris Herman with the five count, and Thomas breaks the hold. Thomas barking with Herman-and now Jak Hammer over there, pulling Conley over the ring apron and smashing an elbow onto his upper chest. Thomas back to work now, and Mother Nature is off and running. Where's she going? Marc Thomas now with a Russian legsweep, and now he goes to a half-Boston crab in the middle of the ring. Jak Hammer pounding on the ring apron hollering at Conley to submit. (Big pop) EL: And here comes Mother Nature back, and she's got the Tornado with her! ("Tornado" Paul Langford is still dressed in street cloths- flannel shirt and blue jeans with workboots, and he doesn't bring his title belt with him. He's 6'3"-244, with short brown hair and green eyes.) EL: Back in the ring, Marc Thomas is not happy with this development, and he wants referee Chris Herman to do something about it. Conley does something about it- he just rolled Thomas up from behind... 1... 2... kickout! And Langford has just dragged over two metal folding chairs- he shoves Hammer into one and sits right down next to him! Conley now with a forearm uppercut- Irish whip- back body drop. Lateral press...1... 2... kickout! Conley picks him back up- Irish whip- back drop again- no! DDT by Marc Thomas- he just drilled Conley right into that mat. You know, of all the up and coming stars in the CWAA, Thomas has been around the block more than anyone, and you can't go for the same move like that twice in a row on him like that. He knew what as coming as soon as he hit that rope, and was able to make the adjustment. That and the Icebreaker finisher, which he sometimes seems to just pull out of nowhere, is what makes him such a dangerous foe. Thomas now with a side suplex, and look at Jak Hammer gloating to Paul Langford, as Langford shouts encouragement to his partner. Thomas now with an Irish whip- dropkick. And he goes right back to that half-Boston crab. (Chants of "Go, Travis, Go!" fill the arena) EL: Thomas releases the hold, stomps away at that knee and those hamstrings up on the upper leg again. Now he stands him up, side headlock. Referee Chris Herman checking to make sure that he doesn't slip an arm below the chin for a choke- now he checks for a submission. Conley pushes off- Thomas into the ropes- Conley to the mat- Thomas hurdles over him- Conley puts him down with a shoulderblock. Now Conley off the ropes- clotheslines- Thomas ducks- Conley off the opposite ropes- and Thomas catches him in that Icebreaker (Diamond Cutter) Hooks the leg- 1... 2... 3! He got it! ("Life in the Fast Lane" plays as Langford and Mother Nature accompany Conley to the back, CWAA Tag Title belt slung over his shoulder, while Hammer and Thomas celebrate the not-entirely-clean sweep to the accompaniment of a steady stream, of boos.) DM: Here is your winner, Marc Thomas! EL: When we get back from this break, we'll take a look at some of the action from Joey Haven and Jak Hammer at AEWA Extreme!
