Kickoff '97 (2/23, part four)

26 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * CONTINUED FROM PART THREE * * * * MW: Well, earlier tonight, we saw the Assassins become the second team to win both the National and World Tag Team Titles. In a second, we'll see the first. The Machines are about to defend their World tag team titles against the West Coast Connection! LB: These two teams are actually quite similar, which should make the match even MORE interesting. I'm really looking forward to this. MW: Before the match begins, we have a special pre-recorded interview with the Machines. They were signing autographs for the fans earlier today, and our camerman caught up with them. BD: Roll it.... -------------- (Outside the MGM Grand Theater, a crowd of autograph seekers have gathered around the back doors. A new BMW pulls up in the parking lot, and Paul Wong and Simon O'Neal get out of the car. The crowd surrounds The Machines, and Paul starts signing autographs while the crowd shouts some questions and comments.) Fan #1: Are you guys going to take care of the West Coast Connection tonight? Simon: (sarcasm dripping from his voice) No, we plan on meeting them in the ring and discussing gardening techniques. I think it's far too early for planting roses, while.... Paul: Yeah. The West Coast Connection are good-- probably one of the toughest teams we've ever faced. But we didn't win the belts just to lose them this soon, and we're capable of beating them in the ring. Fan #2: What about Hood Lum? Simon: Let's be honest-- if the West Coast Connection thought they were as good as us, they wouldn't be bringing Mr. Lum to the ring. Obviously they're scared of us. (Just then, a woman approaches The Machines. She's an attractive brunette in her late twenties, wearing a power suit. She walks up to Simon, slaps him hard across the face to the ground, and starts to walk away. Then she stops, walks over to Paul, and gives him a hug.) Woman: Paul! It's been years since I've seen you. Give me a call at the office tomorrow, and we can have lunch. Just leave the garbage (points to Simon) behind. (she leaves) Paul: Wow-- I didn't even know Rachel had moved down here. (helps Simon stand up) Tell me again why you two got divorced? Simon: Shut up. Just.... don't say anything. We've got a match to win, and you're making social plans for tomorrow! (Simon stomps away into the MGM. Paul shrugs, signs a few more autographs, and enters the arena a few minutes later. Fade to black.) ------------ MW: Jeez, Simon ALWAYS seems to be having trouble with the opposite sex. How many ex-wives does he HAVE? LB: I wouldn't know. MW: He really needs to work on his social skills. (chuckles) But at least Paul can find something positive in the situation. BD: I don't CARE about Simon's love life, buddy, so can we just get the match started? I'm anxious to see the West Coast Connection win their belts back! MW: I wouldn't be so sure about that. BD: I don't think the Machines can handle this challenge. I have to admit, though, they've been GREAT champions-- but it's over. MW: That remains to be seen, Big Daddy. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is scheduled for one fall, and is for the AEWA World tag team titles! Introducing first, at a combined weight of 610 pounds, being led to the ring by their bodyguard, Hood Lum, THE WEST COAST CONNECTION! (As "Tonight, Tonight" begins playing, Hood Lum follows MC Read and Axl Sharkman to the ring. They both have on their traditional flannel jackets and wraparound shades.) BD: These guys look prepared, folks, and they're in better shape than ever before. I expect the West Coast Connection to leave with the belts. MW: Don't ever underestimate their opponents, Big Daddy. The Machines have NEVER been pinned-- LB: But there's a first time for everything. RING ANNOUNCER: And their opponents, at a combined weight of 492 pounds, the World tag team champions, THE MACHINES! (Pink Floyd's "Welcome to the Machine" begins playing loudly over the PA, as Paul Wong and Simon O'Neal make their way to the ring. They are both wearing gray fedora hats and sunglasses, as always. Simon also dons his trademark glove.) MW: And here they come now, Paul Wong and Simon O'Neal, perhaps the most popular team in the AEWA today. BD: In Simon's case I can see why, but I just don't get Paul. What about you, Bertha, do you get them? LB: No, but Simon got m-- MW: (interrupting) We all saw the video; could we please concentrate on the match? It looks like both teams are content to regard this as a Texas Tornado match-- the bell hasn't rung yet, and the Machines are both still wearing the belts, and all four men are brawling in the middle of the ring. BD: That was a bad move by Axl, punching Paul right in the belt! Right now it is Paul Wong pounding away on Axl Sharkman now, and MC Read and Simon O'Neal grapple against the ropes. Hood Lum is stalking around the ring, just itching for a chance to get involved. BD: I won't hear a word said against him. Hood Lum has never involved himself without provocation. MW: And it is considered "provocation" when the opponents attempt to execute a maneuver against Sharkman or Read in the course of a match? BD: Precisely. LB: Goes without saying. MW: Well, now Paul and Axl have taken it outside the ring, and it is Simon straddled across Read's chest-- BD: Steady, Bertha. MW: --punching away at his face. The referee is separating Paul and Axl on the outside, and both men head for their team's corner. Irish whip by Simon, he leapfrogs Read, and Read ducks a clotheslines, off the ropes again, and Simon drops down-- Read slams on the brakes-- elbow drop in the back! BD: This has been a high-impact match so far, folks. Now it's Read stomping away, and he drags Simon to the WCC corner, and tags in Sharkman. Sharkman with a big fist right between the eyes. He stands Simon up in the corner, and chokes him out with a big boot up against the Adam's apple. LB: Jeez, shouldn't Axl be disqualified for that? I mean, I like the West Coast Connection, but Simon didn't deserved to be choked. MW: Now Sharkman slaps him in the face, and that'll grab Simon's attention! Another slap, and Simon comes back with a punch that rocks Axl back on his heels. Axl connects with a weak punch, and Simon back with another! BD: Well, you've gotta admit, Simon has a serious power and size disadvantage in this one. LB: That's not what I-- MW: (interrupting) Let's NOT go there, Bertha. Now Simon connects with another right hand and Sharkman hits the deck! Read takes a shot at Simon from the apron, and he turns around. Read grabs Simon as they tangle by the ropes, and with Simon so occupied, Sharkman has time to recover and delivers a well-placed kick in the lower back-kidney area. That'll bring in Paul-- BD: And an instant disqualification. MW: -- but he has a five-count, remember? LB: I thought that only applied to flame-throwers. MW: (pointedly ignoring Bertha) The referee is ushering Paul out, and the West Coast Connection dump Simon to the outside. Now they head over to the Machine corner for a meeting with the referee and Paul, and Simon is on the floor at Hood Lum's feet.... BD: That's NOT a good place for O'Neal to be. MW: Hood Lum scoops him up and rams his head onto the steel railing! Now he takes the ring steps, sets them up against the railing-- AND IRISH WHIPS SIMON RIGHT INTO THEM! BD: He rolls him back in the ring, and Axl and Read allow themselves to be cleared out of the Machines' corner. Simon is back up on his knees, and Sharkman locks on a reverse chinlock.The referee checks for a submission, but Simon, straining with every nerve, refuses to give in. Sharkman releases the hold, and-- EXECUTES A SNAP SUPLEX! LB: He tags in MC Read, who now sits on Simon's back for a Boston crab. He's really applying the pressure, folks. MW: The referee is again checking for the submission, as Read leans way back, and Sharkman leans in and pushes his partner's shoulders back, adding force to the hold. Paul sees this illegal tactic, and lunges at Sharkman, but doesn't connect with full force. That breaks the hold, and Paul and Sharkman are tied up in the corner, while Read abandons his hold on Simon and follows them. BD: The West Coast Connection now with a double Irish whip on Paul-- double clothesline-- NO, HE DUCKS! Paul bounces off the opposite ropes, and connects with a double clothesline of his own, and Sharkman and Read both drop to the mat! BD: Disqualify him right there! He hit the illegal man! MW: Simon is back up now, and he staggers to the corner, goes up top-- Paul picks up Read as Sharkman staggers to his feet, AND SIMON COMES DOWN WITH A CROSS BODY PRESS ON BOTH MEN! The cover! One.. Two.. They both kick out! LB: You can't pin both men like that! BD: Well, if you have a five count to do anything, and it only takes a three-count to win, I suppose that to pin two men you need a six-count, and that's more than five-- so you can't pin two men at once because you have one illegal second. MW: The only illegal second around here is Hood Lum. Everyone is back up again and in the middle of the ring, as Paul and Read trade shots, while Simon dropkicks Sharkman. The referee has no control in this one now. BD: Paul executes a powerslam, but there's no count-- the referee is busy trying to separate Simon and Read! Hood Lum is up there now, and he goes for an elbow drop-- PAUL ROLLS OUT OF THE WAY AND HOOD LUM NAILS SHARKMAN! LB: That wasn't fair.... MW: Now Paul connects with a clothesline that sends Hood Lum to the floor-- he covers Sharkman again! Here's the referee to make the count.... One.. Two.. Kickout! MW: That was a close one, folks. Axl Sharkman is probably going to be VERY upset with Hood Lum after this match is over. BD: Simon goes for a Thesz press, but Read turns him around-- a Hot Shot! He goes for a quick cover! One.. Two.. Kickout! LB: Now Paul is going after Read, and he backs him up into the corner.... and NAILS him with a European uppercut! MW: Sharkman is on Simon now, using his right arm for a side-headlock as he punches away with the left. Paul sets up for a powerbomb on MC Read-- THIS ONE COULD BE ENDING RIGHT HERE-- but Sharkman backs into Paul, who falls backward with Read on his chest.... BD: And Read is going for the cover! One.. Kickout! MW: Now Read and Sharkman are stomping away on Paul-- BUT SIMON SCHOOLBOYS SHARKMAN.... and the referee didn't get down for the count in time. Read breaks it with a kick to Simon's gut! BD: Read drags Simon to his feet, and SLAMS him with a Northern Lights Suplex! He's about to go for a cover, but Paul breaks it with an elbow drop, and now pulls Read up by the tights-- AND CLOTHESLINES HIM OVER THE TOP ROPE TO THE FLOOR! LB: Simon gets back to his feet, and attacks Sharkman from behind. He NAILS him with a back suplex, but doesn't go for the pin. Simon may have taken too many shots in the head-- he obviously isn't thinking straight. MW: I agree, Bertha. Paul follows Read to the floor, and RAMS his head onto the ring apron. Hood Lum is coming to his aid, but Simon slides under the bottom rope and into Hood, driving him into the steel railing! BD: Simon now jumps from the ring apron, POUNDING Read with a double axe handle! Sharkman is coming around from the other side, and spins Paul around, then a right cross. And he whips Paul into the ringpost! LB: This has been an action-packed match so far, but everybody seems to be tiring out. Sharkman sets Paul for a piledriver-- he can't quite get him up-- AND PAUL REVERSES IT INTO A BACK BODY DROP ONTO THE FLOOR! MW: MC Read is halfway up the ring aisle, with Simon in close pursuit. The match isn't over yet, buddy! Simon takes Read and chokes him against the railing. Read, trying to squirm free, slips over the railing and he's in the crowd now, and Simon follows. LB: This is CRAZY! MW: Security is on its way, and I think some fines are going to have to come down for this-- we can't be endangering the safety of the fans like this. BD: Oh, anyone who gets caught in the crossfire should just take it like a man. LB: Right. If you can't take a punch from a 275-pound psychiatric outpatient, you don't belong in the crowd. MW: Have you heard a bell yet? We must have had a double countout by now.... Simon is following Read back in the audience, and Paul is now being choked down by Hood Lum. Meanwhile, Sharkman is just lying on the floor, not stirring at all. Here comes more security to separate the parties involved. BD: Simon just stole a metal case from a concession stand-- the kind the vendors use to keep the hot dogs warm-- and he just SLAMMED it across Read's back, and hot dogs and condiments have just spilled out all over the place. Some kid just picked one up and ate it! LB: EWWW! MW: Simon now pulls a T-shirt from the merchandise stand-- it looks like a Graphic Violence T-shirt, of all things-- AND HE'S CHOKING READ WITH IT! The vendor is beside himself! BD: I didn't see Simon pay for it. LB: Someone should check him for a receipt later. MW: Back near the ring, security has just pulled Hood Lum off of Paul Wong, and both he and Axl Sharkman look like they'll need some attention. BD: Out near the concession stands, Read has just kicked at Simon's groin-area. Now he slams his head on the counter, and shoves Simon over it, back into the hot dog and soda vendor's work area. Read climbs up on the counter-- FLYING AXE HANDLE DOWN ON THE FLOOR! MW: Now he takes Simon's head and SLAMS the refrigerator door on it, and juices and other goods spill out on to the floor.... BD: Someone's going to have to pay for a lot of ruined food when this is all finished. MW: That's for sure, Big Daddy. Now Read has a brown paper bag from behind the counter, and puts it over Simon's head-- Simon can't see. And Read picks up a cash register, AND BLASTS SIMON IN THE HEAD WITH IT! Money is flying everywhere! BD: I think I need a root beer, maybe a pretzel-- see ya! (Big Daddy stands up from the broadcast booth, and goes running toward the money) LB: Wait for me! (Bertha follows him) MW: All hell is breaking loose over there, folks! Read now drags Simon back to his feet, and-- oh, my God-- HE'S GOING TO TRY AND PRESS SIMON'S FACE ONTO THE HOT DOG GRILL! And how did Big Daddy get back there so quickly? BD: Watch the hot dogs, man! (Big Daddy begins pulling them off the grill, stuffing two into his mouth, and others into his pocket for later. Bertha arrives soon after and starts scooping up some of the money that spilled to the floor.) MW: Simon elbows Read in the midsection to avoid being burnt! Now Simon grabs a roll of quarters, it appears, and it looks like he is going to use them to punch-- no, Bertha is trying to pry them out from between his fingers! She's got them, and Simon watches incredulously as she deposits them in a "safe place". Simon and Read have their hands around each other's throats on the floor behind the counter, and security is on the scene now, pulling them apart. (As one member of the security force treads on a wayward hot dog, Big Daddy utters a stricken cry.) MW: It looks like this one is finally over, but I doubt very much that this is the last we'll see of this war. And, as Big Daddy pointed out, someone is going to have to pay for all of this damage. If I had any say in the matter, I wouldn't let these teams at each other again without 10 feet of chicken wire on each side of the ring. Well, as both teams are escorted back to the locker room, let's try to get the official decision of this match.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, this match has been declared a no contest! The Machines will RETAIN their World tag team titles. MW: I still can't believe how VIOLENT that match was. The West Coast Connection might not like the Machines, but I NEVER expected to see anything like that. (Big Daddy and Bertha both arrive back at the broadcast booth, looking greedier than ever.) BD: Well, that was definitely my favorite match of the evening so far. There were hot dogs all over the place, absolutely free. God bless America. LB: I collected about fifty bucks over there, thanks to the West Coast Connection. If those damn security guards hadn't ruined the fun, I would've gotten even more. MW: You guys REALLY need to calm down. Anyway, it's now time for a special interview, as Hank Thompson will be talking with "Superman" Mike Stewart, and I'm sure we're going to find out some VERY interesting information. As you all know, NO ONE has seen Stewart since his unfortunate accident several months ago. BD: Personally, I think he's going to officially retire tonight. It's been WAY too long since we've seen him, so the news could only be bad. MW: Let's just watch.... (The camera pans up to Hank Thompson standing in the ring. One side of the ring ropes has been lowered and a ramp has been inserted to allow the interviewee to enter the ring in his wheelchair.) Thompson: Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome at this time, the former AEWA World Heavyweight champion, "SUPERMAN" MIKE STEWART! ("Eye Of The Tiger" by Survivor begins to play over the PA as Stewart makes his way through the curtain to a THUNDEROUS round of cheers. He is wearing a black suit, and slaps hands with a few of the fans on his way to the ring, while wheeling his wheelchair. He makes his way up the ramp in the chair and pulls up next to Thompson, who waits for the crowd to quiet before talking.) Thompson: Mike, the fans certainly have welcomed you back here to the AEWA. I must admit, it's great to see you back, and I'm sure everyone will look forward to any role you might have in the future in the AEWA. Stewart: Thanks Hank, it's good to be back! (Big crowd pop) Stewart: (turning somewhat serious) I've got something I need to say though, and I need to direct this towards Bob Freeman. Bob, I will tell you that I appreciate everything you and Commissioner Gehringer did to help me out, and giving me the only shot I needed to win the AEWA World belt. What I don't understand though, is why you've kept the fans in the dark for so long. Thompson (looking puzzled) What do you mean Mike? In the dark about what? (Stewart looks up at Thompson) Stewart: About this.... (All of a sudden, Stewart begins to move.... and he STANDS UP! Thompson looks totally stunned, and the crowd is not sure whether to gasp in awe, or to give Stewart a standing ovation.) Stewart: I don't understand why the fans didn't know that I can walk. I don't understand why they were never told. And I don't understand why I was never given a shot to regain the World title that I never really lost. (Thompson still looks partially stunned and can't speak.) Stewart: While it's true I was injured for a short time, I would have been able to come back and participate in the AEWA at any time. I was never given the chance. Quite frankly, I've been screwed by President Freeman! Now, in some people's minds, I may not have earned the World title shot in the first place, but the fact of the matter is, I'm the only man who's ever pinned Heavy Metal and won a match over him. Deranged has pinned him, but he lost the match. The Gryphon pinned him, but that was a tag team match. And then, I faced Metal again and still was not beaten by him. So, what I'm asking for right now is a shot at the AEWA World heavyweight title that is rightfully mine. I may not deserve the belt. I rightfully lost the championship title from my name, but what I haven't gotten is the right to defend my honor and fight for my title once again, and that's what I'm asking for. I know Freeman has made some mistakes in the past, but if he's any type of President or man, he'll give me the shot that's rightfully mine. Until then, I'll see you around. (Stewart turns around and jumps over the top rope of the ring to the ringside floor. He then climbs over the guardrail and exits through the crowd. The camera returns to our broadcast team.) BD: I can't believe what I just heard! Mike Stewart is back in the AEWA, and he wants to WRESTLE again? And after months of absence, he's been perfectly fine all along? LB: What was President Freeman trying to hide? MW: This is a VERY odd situation, folks. I'm really not sure what to think about that whole interview-- it's a little too much to register at once. BD: There seem to be a few skeletons in President Freeman's closet, huh? Well, I'd love to keep talking about it, but isn't it time for our next match? * * * * TO BE CONTINUED IN PART FIVE * * * * \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- "A New Beginning" American E-Wrestling Association The Official E-Wrestling Federation of Wrestle World and TNM The Best E-Wrestling Fed of the Year 1996 in the TNM Year-End Awards Be sure to visit our Event Center at: http://www.california.com/~bullet/index.html
