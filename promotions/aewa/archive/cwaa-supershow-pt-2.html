CWAA Supershow pt 2

3 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EL: OK- we know that the trio of Lt. Lawson, Sgt. Sherrick and Max Robertson- all of whom already hold titles- will go to the finals of this, the CWAA Six Man Tag Team Championship tournament. In a few minutes, we'll find out who they'll meet later tonight. Let's go to ring announcer, David Myers. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is set for one fall with a fifteen-minute time limit, and is a first round match for in the CWAA Six Man Tag Team Championship tournament. Introducing first, at a combined weight of 754 pounds, here are Red Dog and the team of Double Jeopardy- Eddie Intellecta and "Hotshot" Gary Jackson! (boos as "Sweet Home Alabama" plays and the blue-masked Intellecta and Hotslob Gary Jackson and their AEWA pal Red Dog hit the ring) DM: And their opponents- ("Dream On" plays to frenzied pop) DM: -at a combined weight 737 pounds, Joshua Barnes, "the Preacher" Kore Wrench and "the Dreamer" Randy Sheraton! (The DJ-RD team clear the ring as Barnes, whom Max Robertson has riled to a feverish state, and Wrench, always excitable, and Sheraton, who didn't want to be the last one into the ring, explode up the aisle) DM: Your referee, Chris Herman. EL: OK, we're underway. Joshua Barnes and Randy Sheraton have already tasted CWAA gold, but the Preacher was foiled in his attempts to become TV champion a few months ago. Double Jeopardy have had a rough summer, and it's our first look at the Red Dog. Looks like Gary Jackson and Randy Sheraton will start this one. Jackson with about a 75-pound weight advantage. They lock up, and it's the Dreamer with a hip toss. Jackson up, and Sheraton with a deep arm drag. Off the ropes- Jackson ducks, off the ropes again- criss-crossing- and Jackson with a hip to- blocked, and the Dreamer hip tosses him over! (pop) EL: Sheraton tags out to the Preacher- double Irish whip- double elbow smash! The cover- 1... 2... kickout. Now the Preacher with a standing wristlock, using that awesome power to wear his foe down. It was the Preacher two week ago who stood in the ring with Little Yvette, Fuse and Elektro and called on Randy Sheraton and Joshua Barnes to join them all in cleaning up this town, what with Marc Thomas and Max Robertson and Lt. Lawson and all the rest running rampant. Kore Wrench twists again- wristlock on the Hotshot. And Jackson to an eye rake, that breaks the hold. A double axe handle on the Preacher- Irish whip- and the Preacher nails him with a clothesline! (pop) EL: Kore Wrench follows with an atomic knee drop. The Preacher in control of this one early on. And Jackson tags in Red Dog. Red Dog and Kore Wrench now. Collar and elbow tie-up. Red Dog with a knee lift. Double sledge, and he takes the Preacher into the Double Jeopardy corner- a bad part of town. Eddie Intellecta tags in, and he and Red Dog going to town, kicking and punching the Preacher in the corner- and Barnes and Sheraton in to even the odds, as Chris Herman tries to break it up. Barnes pops the Dog one! (pop) EL: Chris Herman trying to get Sheraton and Barnes out of there- and now Double Jeopardy sends Wrench for the ride- double back body drop! Double team action, and Kore Wrench in trouble now. Intellecta stomps away, and again- now he tags Red Dog back in. Sends him for the ride- Wrench hurdles over Intellecta, and Red Dog goes downstairs with the right hand. Red Dog now takes him to a neutral corner. Right into the top turnbuckle, and that'll smart. Everyone but Red Dog will be back in this ring at least one more time tonight- Red Dog now with a kick to the midsection- as we have that big over-the-top-rope battle royal, and the winner takes home $25,000! Red Dog now runs Wrench's face across that top rope- the Preacher has spent too much time in the wrong half of the ring, Red Dog with a right jab- and the Preacher nails him right back! (pop) EL: And Gary Jackson tags in now and goes right to the eyes- and again it is a blatant breach of the rules that gets the Double Jeopardy trio the advantage. Jackson sends him for the ride- knee to the midsection. Snap mare takedown, and he goes to the reverse chinlock. Gary Jackson putting on the pressure. He and Eddie Intellecta have had their ups and downs this year here in the CWAA, and maybe the addition of a wild card, an unknown, like the Red Dog can jumpstart this team. Kore Wrench fighting the chinlock while Barnes and Sheraton wait on the outside. Earlier. we saw and heard footage of the Sheradin-Wrench-Barnes team talking about the Lawson-Sherrick-Robertson team- perhaps they have taken their first-round opponents lightly, but it is "Hotshot" Gary Jackson in control as he has the Preacher stuck in that chinlock. ("Go, Kore, Go!" with Sheraton stamping his right foot and clapping in time, and Barnes attempting to do the same) EL: The crowd and his teammates trying to rally the Preacher. He's been a big crowd favorite since we first opened business here in the CWAA, always around the Sportatorium early to sign some autographs and pose for pictures, and the fans have really taken to the big youngster. He's got one leg underneath now, up on one foot, and Jackson starting to look concerned. And there's a handful of hair, as he pulls the Preacher back down to the mat and into that chinlock! (boos, as Jackson yells, "Shut up!", after which the boos of course increase in volume) EL: Gary Jackson and his teammates, Eddie Intellecta and Red Dog, off to an impressive start here in this CWAA Six Man Tag Team championship tournament. (the chanting and foot-stamping and clapping gets louder) EL: And the crowd really lending its support to the Preacher, one of their big heroes, urging him and the rest of this so-called Dream Team to rally and pull out the victory- Kore Wrench back on his feet- elbow smash to the midsection- and again- and that breaks the hold! (pop) EL: Jackson turns back and tags in Red Dog, now with a drop toehold to keep the Preacher in his half of the ring- Red Dog off the ropes- elbow drop. And now Red Dog to a hammerlock down on the mat. An uphill battle for the Preacher, as he not only needs to break out of the hold, as he did a minute ago with the Hotshot, but then he has to go all the way across the ring to make the tag. Red Dog with that hammerlock on- and he rubs Wrench's face into the mat- adding insult to injury! Red Dog up- kneedrop onto the back. Sends him for the ride- clothesline! Red Dog off the ropes now- elbow drop! Covers- 1... 2... and the Preacher kicks out! (pop) EL: Red Dog now whips him into the corner- elbow smash as the Preacher bounces out of the corner! Red Dog putting the boots to him, and now he tags in Eddie Intellecta. Great teamwork by Double Jeopardy and Red Dog, while the Preacher has been in the ring for the entire match. Eddie and the Red Dog- Irish whip- double clothesline. Intellecta covers- 1... 2... kickout. Eddie off the ropes now- measures him up- kneedrop. Scoops him up- body slam. Intellecta now sends him for the ride- reversed! And Kore Wrench with the clothesline! (pop) EL: He nearly knocked that mask right off his face! Eddie Intellecta smacked right on his back by the Preacher, and the Preacher, who has absorbed so much punishment, is down as well! Referee Chris Herman begins his double 10-count, and if neither man can answer the call, Major Mayhem and his friends will take home the title belts! 4... 5... both men starting to stir- the Preacher on his knees, but Intellecta is closer to a tag- all four men on the outside straining to get in there. Both men crawling to their corners- and the Hotshot tags in- and so does Joshua Barnes! (pop) EL: Joshua Barnes in there now- Jackson swings- Barnes blocks it- 1... 2... 3 jabs by the former TV champion! (pop) EL: Jackson swings- missed- and Barnes with an atomic knee drop- here comes the Red Dog- and Barnes catches him in a scoop slam! Eddie Intellecta now- and the same fate befalls him! Barnes taking care of business- all three opponents in there with him, and now the Preacher and the Dreamer in there to even the odds. Wrench takes Intellecta to one corner and introduces him to Mr. Turnbuckle- Barnes kicking Jackson in the midsection in the Double Jeopardy corner, and Sheraton monkey flips the Red Dog into another corner! And now- (pop) EL: All three men whipped into the corner! Red Dog, Intellecta and Jackson collide in mid-ring! Referee Chris Herman cleaning things up. OK- Joshua Barnes and Gary Jackson in there now. Barnes scoops him up- body slam. Covers- 1... 2... kickout. Now he sends him for the ride- spinebuster slam! He hit it! (pop) EL: And rather than make the cover, he tags in the Dreamer! (pop) EL: Randy Sheraton, going up top- sets for the Dream Come True- (Shooting Star Press) EL: He nailed it! 1... 2... 3! (as Barnes and Wrench clothesline Intellecta and Red Dog as they try to break the cover) EL: Randy Sheraton scores the pin, and the Preacher, the Dreamer and Joshua Barnes will face Lawson, Sherrick and Robertson later tonight for the Six-Man gold! And what a performance by the Preacher, as he took everything his opponents threw at him, and his partners stayed nice and fresh to pick up the pinfall when it was all over. Let's get the official word from David Myers. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winners, the team of Joshua Barnes, "The Preacher" Kore Wrench, and "The Dreamer" Randy Sheraton! (pop as Gregorian Chant plays and the victors exit) EL: OK- when we get back from this next break, we will see a match we've waited weeks to sign- Little Yvette and Dave "the Freak" Johnson finally get into the ring, face to face! Stay tuned!
