Monday Night Live (9/30)

1 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see Hank Thompson and Bootknife Kelly in the studio, sitting at the metal desk. They are both wearing "AEWA Rocks" T-shirts.) Thompson: Hello everyone, and welcome to the Event Center! We have plenty of interviews for tonight's impromptu show, so this should be great! I'm here with my special guest, making his return to the AEWA-- Bootknife Kelly! Kelly: Quit your damn babbling, Hank. I'm only here because President Freeman offered me a big check, so shut the hell up. Thompson: (laughing) Actually, Bootknife, you were desperately in need of ANY check. We found you in New York with a sign that read "I will work for food". Kelly: Quit spreading these vicious rumors! I've got PLENTY of dough, and this little AEWA job is just a part-time thing. Thompson: Whatever. We have an interview with Submission up first, so let's see what they have to say.... ---------- (The Phantom of the Opera is in his dressing room, watching the tape of Wednesday Night Rumble. He watches the footage of El Aviador taking on the Holy Ghost.) Phantom: Could someone tell me why the Light Heavyweight title attracts so many cheaters? Look at the history of that title-- Joey Valachi, Hurricane Henson, Matt Master, Penance, Shane Matthews and now Cardinal Chaos' sheep, the Holy Ghost. That title is just a joke! The AEWA need a true champion to make this title's name shine like the gold it's made of. And who is the best Light Heavyweight wrestler in the AEWA? I've just won the World Tag Team Titles twice, so I do believe I've earned my title shot. I WANT A SHOT AT THE HOLY GHOST! And I hope that chipmunk won't run away like his "master" Cardinal Chaos ran away from the Lord of Midnight. And speaking of this.... (The Lord of Midnight arrives in the dressing room.) Midnight: My patience has limits. And the AEWA just went over those limits. A month ago, I asked once, twice, THREE times for a match at Cardinal Chaos. And the AEWA did not give me my match because he was TV champion and, according to the championship committee, I wasn't a top contender. Now that Cardinal Chaos is nothing but the crap he has always been, I want a shot at him. And if the AEWA doesn't give me that shot, I'm going to take it anyway! If before next Saturday I'm not scheduled to wrestle Cardinal Chaos in a ring, I'll take on him wherever I find him. And my little buddy, The Phantom of the Opera, will act as the referee. Phantom: The church's reign is over! Midnight: Watch out, Chaos.... and watch out Holy Ghost. Because Submission is coming after you! And if together we are strong, apart we are even stronger. (We fade to black, and the camera switches back to the studio) ---------- Kelly: Jeez, those guys have BIG mouths. They need to shut the hell up, because The Church will NOT back down from a challenge. Thompson: I'd have to agree with you, Bootknife. Anyway, up next, we have an interview with a newcomer called "Kwon Akido". Kelly: (smiles) Jeez, I wonder if HE'S Asian. Thompson: Let's just watch this interview, Bootknife, before you offend any of our audience members. ---------- (We see Kwon Akido, wearing a white kimono, sitting alone in his greenhouse. He turns to the camera, looking very calm.) Akido: Hello, ladies and gentlemen. I have come here to the American E-Wrestling Association, and I am on a mission. This is going to be VERY easy. First, I want to strip Randall Osbourne of his North American title, so I can send a message to these idiots in the USA-- China is the most dominant force in the world. You see, this will be just like the Olympics. I have come to your country, in the name of China, to humiliate you all. (laughs) The Olympics were such a joke. You couldn't even hold a public event without a big explosion, could you? The USA is a lazy country, and that's why China kicked your ASSES at the Olympics. (screaming at the top of his lungs) Americans are all LAZY! China dominated in the Olympics, and I will dominate in the AEWA! Prepare to meet your doom! (The camera switches back to the studio) ---------- Kelly: Hey, I really LIKE that guy! Thompson: Are you KIDDING me? He's a complete idiot! Plus, what was all that crap about the Olympics? If my memory serves correctly, America did GREAT in the Olympics! Kelly: But China did better. I really don't feel like getting into an argument about patriotism, so can we just move on? Thompson: Here's an interview with President Bob Freeman.... ---------- (We see President Freeman in his office, wearing a nice tuxedo. He appears to be in a very good mood) Freeman: Ladies and gentlemen, I would like to inform you of some upcoming events. Later this week, we will be holding the very first "AEWA Hall Of Fame" awards ceremony. That should be a GREAT show, so please be sure to watch. Also, as you already know, "Mega Combat: The Rising Sun" will be held this weekend in Japan. It's available ONLY on pay-per-view, so call your local cable company today! Thank you, and see ya at ringside! (The camera switches back to the studio) ---------- Kelly: Okay, that should be GREAT! We're running out of time tonight, and I'm about to sign off, but we still have one more thing.... Thompson: (in a very unexcited voice) It's now time for the Kevin Larkin Show. As you know, we are legally required to show this, so please bear with us. Kelly: Hey, I'm looking forward to this! Here we go.... ---------- (An abashed looking Kevin Larkin sits behind the same old desk, but the phone is now gone, due to cutbacks. After last week, he's still a little woozy, so the etch-a-sketch gets the night off). Larkin: Last week, there were....um....things I was going to....uh....you know.... (clears throat) ....talk about. So, now, I will.... um....talk about those things. First is (squints at the teleprompter).... wait, it says.... Oh, I almost forgot. Skull & Crossbones and the AEWA World Tag Team Title Belts. Okay. I got it now. As you all know, a few weeks ago, they appeared on my show and the Shadow Warriors attacked them and then my team, the Visigothic Horde, attacked them, too. I just wanted to clear the air on this. I have no real problem with Skull & Crossbones, and it was by no means personal. We softened them up, Shadow Warriors still couldn't win the belts, end of story. We can put this all behind us, I'm sure. Speaking of the Shadow Warriors, they are the National Champs, as we all know, and this Wednesday they defend their titles against the Machines. I don't recall sanctioning this match, but they produced the contract as evidence, so I guess in the hurly-burly world of running this whole federation-- don't believe what Freeman tells you-- some of the administrative minutiae elude me. I wonder how the contract got so wet, though. Anyway, like I said, we appear to have new National Title #1 contenders. We don't as yet have #1 world tag title contenders, and the....um....application process, such as it is, is still open. (Clears throat) Lastly, my team has recently been challenged to a suspended ring match, of all things. Coffin, Grave Mortician, Dweller, listen closely. No way in God's heaven, his green earth, or in Hell will you ever get me and/or my team in a suspended ring match. We will meet again, but it will be on our terms. Don't call us, we'll call you. Collect. (The credits begin to roll) Larkin: WAIT- I wasn't finished yet. (Credits continue to roll.) Next week, my special guests will be former AEWA World Champions, the-- (he's not done talking, but the show ends) \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- American E-Wrestling Association!
