Saturday Night Slam (10/19, part two)

21 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
****CONTINUED FROM PART ONE**** MW: Next, The Grim Reaper is defending his North American Title against a former World Champion making his return to the AEWA, Big Ron Raider. BD: Now THAT should be a good match. MW: It should indeed, so let's not just sit around and talk about it, let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, From the Rocky Mountains, he weighs in at 416 pounds and accompanied by his manager, Million Dollar Mark, He is a former AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, Big....Ron....Raaaaaaiiiiiiiiiideeeeeer! (Lynyrd Skynyrd's "Saturday Night Special" cranks out over the PA as Raider, looking mean as ever, makes his entrance to a large round of boos. Million Dollar Mark walks closely behind him.) LB: Boy, I sure missed this guy. I'm glad he's back. MW: Maybe you are, but I don't think that any of his opponents are. BD: Yeah, but the Reaper doesn't care about anyone or anything. MW: That's true. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 435 pounds and accompanied by his manager "Cutie" Rick Verrano, He is the AEWA North American Champion, The Grim Reeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeaper! ("The Funeral March" plays loudly over the PA as the Grim Reaper makes his way toward the ring to a huge round of boos. Verrano walks in front of him and carries the belt.) LB: And the Reaper looks intimidating, as always. MW: He sure does-- wouldn't you hate to run into him in a dark alley? BD: I'd hate to run into him in a lit alley. MW: You've got a point there. The Reaper steps into the ring, Raider immediately charges in, AND AVALANCHES HIM INTO THE CORNER! Raider grabs him now, and whips him to the opposite turnbuckle! Raider charges in behind him, and CRUSHES him with a shoulder-first ram into the corner. LB: Raider's not wasting any time here today. MW: No he's not, he WANTS that title. The Reaper staggers out of the corner now, and Raider NAILS him with a roundhouse right! Raider grabs him by the arm, SHORT CLOTHESLINE! BD: The Reaper's REALLY gotta get some offense in. MW: If he wants to keep his title he does. The Reaper gets up, and Raider just CLAPS the Reaper's head between his arms! Raider grabs him now, SHORT LARIAT! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: The Reaper's far from done. MW: Not if it keeps up like this. Raider picks him up off the canvas now, lifts him up, AND POWERSLAMS HIM! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Raider's completely in control of this one. MW: Yes, we haven't seen ANY offense from the Reaper as of yet, but he's usually a slow starter. He'll get better as the match goes on, if he makes it that far. Raider picks the Reaper up now, and whips him to the turnbuckle! Raider comes in behind him, AVALANCHE! Reaper staggers out of the corner, and gets LEVELED with a lariat! LB: Raider's looked VERY impressive so far, he's just manhandling the Reaper. MW: He sure is. Raider picks the Reaper up now, and just NAILS him with a short clothesline! Raider is going up top now, and the Reaper's down! The big man is fairly agile, and he's on the top rope, MOONSAULT! What a move by the big man! The cover! One..Two..Thr..Shoulder Up! BD: And the Reaper just BARELY got out of that one. MW: That's for sure. Raider isn't wasting any time though. He lifts the Reaper back to his feet, and NAILS him with a swinging punch! Raider grabs him now, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: The Reaper better do something, NOW. MW: The Reaper slowly makes it back to his feet, and Raider charges at him LARIAT--DUCKED BY THE REAPER! Raider bounces off the ropes and comes back, but the Reaper catches him, POWERSLAM! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: I think that counts as something, Bertha. LB: Thanks. MW: Raider slowly gets up off the canvas, and the Reaper grabs him by the neck! He lifts him up, CHOKESLAM! The cover! One..Two..Thr..Shoulder Up! BD: WOW! Now it's Raider who's in trouble. MW: Yes, things can change fast and furious in there. And now what's happening? Verrano's up on the apron! LB: And the stupid ref is going over to talk to him. Isn't he brilliant? MW: Yeah, a real Einstein. And now the Reaper is going over to his corner, and he picks up his belt, BUT MILLION DOLLAR MARK JUST THREW SOME KIND OF POWDER INTO THE EYES OF THE REAPER.... HE CAN'T SEE! BD: Yet another backfire! We're not having much luck with interference today. MW: It looks like Mark is. Raider is up now, and the Reaper has NO CLUE where he is. Raider grabs the belt out of the hands of the Reaper, AND CLOCKS HIM WITH IT! The Reaper was knocked COLD, and Raider tosses the belt aside. And now Mark just pulled Verrano off the apron, turning the ref's attention back to the match. LB: And Raider's going up top! MW: Yes he is. Once again he's on to top rope, AND EXECUTES ANOTHER BEAUTIFUL MOONSAULT, RIGHT ON THE MONEY! The cover! One..Two..Three!! (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of twelve minutes and nine seconds, here is your winner, and NEEEEEEEEEW AEWA North American Champion.... BIG RON RAIDER!!!! BD: Hey, I just thought of something. Raider's the first guy to hold all three heavyweight belts, ain't he? MW: Yes, I guess he is. It's quite an amazing feat. LB: Well Raider's quite an amazing guy. MW: Yes he is, and I believe that he's got something to say now. He's calling for a microphone. RAIDER: I just want to say that I dedicate this victory to my savior, Cardinal Chaos, and effective right now, you're looking at the newest converted member of the Church! As the Reaper just found out, those who do not see the Light must be punished. So Reaper, you and your "Angels" had better not mess with the Church again, or you'll find more of the same. Your "Angels" have no place in our Church. The Church is without a doubt, the most dominant group in all of wrestling. And if anybody thinks differently, I dare ya to try and prove me wrong. See ya, suckers. Me and the Church have some celebrating to do. (Raider and Mark walk back to the locker room, holding the belt and with big smiles on their faces.) MW: Well can you believe THAT? BIG RON RAIDER wants to be one of Cardinal Chaos' flunkies? LB: They're not flunkies, they're distinguished church members. MW: Yeah, and I'm a monkey's uncle. BD: I wouldn't be surprised. MW: Well what about all these constantly growing groups in the AEWA? We've got the Angels of Death, the Church, and ICU all recruiting. They're bound to run into each other. LB: And when they do, it's gonna be one hell of a war. MW: It sure is. BD: Reaper's being helped back to the locker room by the other Angels now, so what's next? MW: It's time for another installment of the Terrordome, folks. LB: Oh yeah! Johnny Terror has had some interesting guests in the past, but I'm REALLY looking forward to this one. He's going to be talking to Sewerdog, the AEWA's resident "crazy canine". BD: Let's just watch.... (Public Enemy's "Welcome to the Terrordome" plays loudly throughout the arena as the camera shifts to a familiar set near the back of the arena. Hanging over this set is a large half-dome. Underneath the dome, there is a very dark, dank room FILLED with various gruesome-looking torture devices. AEWA manager Johnny Terror emerges from the locker room to a big round of boos. He stops for a minute, and cups his hand to his ear, mocking "you know who". He gets to the set, and picks up a microphone.) Terror: Hello again peons, and welcome to another exciting episode of (he begins to air guitar and sing; very badly) The Terrordome! The Terrordome! Party Time! Excellent! (he then begins to do a very bad impression of some guitar cords.) I am your Excellent host, Johnny Terror! (LOUD BOOS) With me as always is, well.... no one. But that's another show. Anyway, I think it's about time we got to my guest for this week, don't you? (The crowd responds with a resounding "NO!") WELL TOO DAMN BAD! So, as I was saying, it's time for my guest for this week. This week's guest is a very unusual man. He is out for no man but himself here in the AEWA. In the time since his debut, he has made that VERY clear, attacking everyone from Volcano Jackson to Heavy Metal. And just two weeks ago, he was mere INCHES away from winning the World Heavyweight title, in a GRUELING match with Heavy Metal. Ladies and Gentlemen, at this time I give you one of the most unpredictable men in the HISTORY of the AEWA.... THE SEWERDOG!! (White Zombie's "I Am Hell" starts to blare out of the speakers and the crowd begins to boo loudly. Sewerdog is wearing a brown leather jacket, ripped jeans, AEWA T-shirt and his black dog mask. He looks at the crowd and holds his arms out as if to embrace the audience and spins around as if dancing to the boos. He walks up to Johnny, smiles and begins to speak) Sewerdog: Man, this makes me feel right at home. Terror: Really? I didn't know that there were obnoxious crowds in the sewers. You learn somethin' new every day. So anyway Sewerdog, let's get this interview on the road. First off, I'd just like to ask about your background. How is it that you came to live in the SEWERS anyway? Isn't it kinda gross down there? You're not the Rat King, as Big Daddy once suggested, are you? Sewerdog: First of all, Big Daddy doesn't know shit. I didn't live in the sewers, I trained in the sewers. Any man that can survive down there as long as I did is nothing but a true fighter-- as Heavy Metal found out last week. I won't deny that I lost that match, but no one else to my knowledge has been pinned by that monster and gotten up to continue fighting as much as I have. Terror: No, I can't say that I've been witness to anyone else taking Heavy Metal's best shots and getting up to fight back like that either, except maybe for Shane, but that's not what we're here to talk about. But since you mentioned the loss to Heavy Metal, let's talk about that. What are your feelings regarding the outcome of that match? Any regrets or things you would've done differently? Sewerdog: Yeah, I do have one regret, I wish I took that bloody fire extinguisher and broke it over his god-damned head instead of just hitting it a few times and spraying him with it. I also wish I had had the help of someone like Shane Matthews in my corner. Terror: Oh yeah, Shane could've turned that match around in a hurry alright. They don't call him Sudden Death for nothing. You two were quite a combo the last time you ran into Heavy Metal together, ya really kicked his ass. Think of what might happen if you guys were on the same side on a more permanent basis.... but that's in the past. Let's talk about what's in the future for Sewerdog? You planning anything new now that you've had your World Title match? Sewerdog: Yeah, I have something big planned and right now, I want everyone here to know about it. I am challenging Stopwatch and Scrap Metal to a tag team match against me and my mystery partner. That's all. I'm sure they're terrified of me alone after what I did to Heavy Metal by myself, just think of what a partner of mine would have to be like. Terror: He'd have to be pretty darn tough to keep up with you, and nuts too. Sounds like my kinda guy. I don't suppose we could get you to give us a hint as to who it might be, could we? Sewerdog: All I'm gonna say is, it's gonna be Sewacide for ICU. That's all I have to say. See ya Johnny. (Sewerdog leaves the set and walks back to the locker room.) Terror: Well, there you have it. The challenge has been made, and now the rest is up to Heavy Metal and Deathwatch. I'd like to thank Sewerdog for appearing on my show this week, and don't miss next week's show, when my guest will be Bootknife Kelly! That's it for this week, morons. See ya, wouldn't wanna be ya! (Johnny drops the microphone and walks off as the camera shifts back to our announcing team.) MW: Has Sewerdog lost his MIND? He's challenging the entire ICU now? Isn't that just a LITTLE too much? BD: I'd have to agree with you, Wilson. But then again, he HAS come close to beating Heavy Metal in the past, so anything's possible. LB: What's next? MW: Up next is our main event, "Superman" Mike Stewart defends the World Heavyweight title against Primal Fear. LB: I don't really like Primal Fear much anymore, but I don't think there's any way he can lose to this Stewart wuss. MW: Heavy Metal did. LB: One word-- Fluke. MW: We'll find out, as Stewart is making his first defense right now, and I think that you'll find out you're wrong. LB: And I think you're a moron. MW: Let's just go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from the Badlands, Sudan, he weighs in at 360 pounds, Primal Feeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeear! (Primal Fear appears from behind the curtain, and he's eating a slab of raw meat! The crowd just EXPLODES.) LB: What? I thought he gave up eating raw meat? MW: I guess he just wanted to get in the mood to tear somebody apart, and this obviously helps. Look at him rip the meat off the bone. BD: You know, you can't take Primal Fear fishing with you. MW: Why's that? BD: He eats the bait. MW: Very funny. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 250 pounds and accompanied by his manager, Michelle Anderson, He is the AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, "Superman" Mike Steeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeewwwwwart! (Survivor's "Eye of the Tiger" takes over the PA system as Stewart, belt around his waist, makes his way toward the ring to a HUGE crowd pop. Michelle Anderson is on his arm.) LB: How can a little wimp like this be our champion? MW: Easy, he beat Heavy Metal. LB: Well tonight, he's gonna find out what it means to be champion, and have everyone gunning for you. MW: He most certainly will begin to feel that pressure, but that comes with being champion. If he can't handle it, he won't be champion for very long. BD: He can't handle it, trust me. MW: I trust you about as far as I can throw you. The champ is in the ring now, and in a good show of sportsmanship, he and Primal Fear shake hands. LB: That never fails to nauseate me. MW: I know. We've got a lock up between the two men now. Stewart gets the upper hand. He grabs Primal Fear, NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Not a bad start, but you're not gonna pin Primal Fear like that. MW: No, I have to agree, you won't. Stewart gets up now, and he drops a leg onto Primal Fear! And we've got a cover! One..Shoulder Up! LB: That was a completely USELESS cover. MW: I don't know, you never know when you can catch someone by surprise. Primal Fear is up now, and he catches the champ with a kneelift! Primal Fear nails him with a fist to the midsection now, and grabs him, BELLY-TO- BELLY SUPLEX!! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Stewart's dead now. Kiss that title goodbye, "Superman". MW: Primal Fear picks the champ up now, and nails him with a knee to the back! Primal Fear whips Stewart to the ropes, Stewart comes back, and gets LEVELED with a clothesline! Primal Fear picks him up again, and levels him with ANOTHER clothesline! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: Stewart should just hang up his jockey strap and go home. MW: I don't think so. Primal Fear waits for Stewart to get up now, and takes him down with a legsweep! Primal Fear picks him up now, PILEDRIVER--NO! Stewart counters with a backdrop! And Primal Fear counters THAT with a Sunset Flip! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Stewart can't even do a counter without having it re-countered, This is funny. MW: Primal Fear DROPS an elbow onto the champ now, and Stewart's in trouble! Stewart gets up, but Primal Fear nails him with a legsweep. LB: If Stewart's such a great champion like you say, then where the heck is his offense? MW: Noticeably absent at the moment. LB: My point EXACTLY. MW: Primal Fear picks the champ up now, INVERTED ATOMIC DROP! And Primal Fear NAILS him with an elbowsmash, followed by a big kick! BD: FINISH HIM! MW: Easy, Big Daddy, this isn't Mortal Kombat. Primal Fear picks him up now, SIDE SUPLEX! And Primal Fear is stetting him up for the Bump Splash! He's lined up, and he sits into it! BUT STEWART MOVES--AND TURNS IT INTO A SUNSET FLIP! One..Kickout! LB: Oh, so Stewart actually IS alive. I was beginning to wonder for a second there. MW: He's VERY MUCH alive. And Primal Fear is just stunned. And Stewart is quick to take advantage. Stewart runs to the ropes! He comes back, and LEVELS Primal Fear with a shoulderblock! Primal Fear is down, and Stewart steps to the outside apron. He grabs the ropes, SLINGSHOT SPLASH! The cover! One..Two..Three!!!! (DING, DING) BD: Again? How does this keep happening? RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of twelve minutes and thirty-four seconds, here is your winner, and STILL AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World... "SUPERMAN" MIKE STEWART!!! MW: What a great come-from-behind victory for our champion! He just waited until Primal Fear made a mistake, and he capitalized, much like he did against Heavy Metal. Stewart may not be the biggest guy or the strongest guy, or even the meanest guy, but he's got that will to win, and you can't deny that. LB: I can. I say that this was another fluke. And Primal Fear isn't that good anymore anyway. MW: Oh, be quiet. Just accept the fact that Stewart won, and leave it at that. BD: There's someone coming to ringside. I can't see, who is it? MW: It appears to be the Crazy Canuck. LB: What could he POSSIBLY want? MW: I don't know. Stewart is celebrating with the fans, and Primal Fear is just coming to. The Canuck picks up a chair now, and slides into the ring! BD: What is he DOING? MW: He's got that chair, and he just CRACKS it over the back of Primal Fear's head! Stewart is standing on the turnbuckle celebrating, and the Canuck nails HIM from behind with the chair! Stewart falls off the turnbuckle and to the mat! What would make the Crazy Canuck do something like this? LB: I'll tell ya what. Lack of money, Lack of Respect, and Lack of Matches. The Canuck hasn't been getting ANY exposure as of late, and he's pissed. This'll get him some exposure. I think it's great! MW: I don't. You don't sacrifice your principles just to get more matches. LB: Sure you do. MW: No, I'm sorry, you don't. And anyone who DOES resort to such tactics is not DESERVING of any respect. And the Canuck continues to work over both men. He's just POUNDING on them with that chair! BD: Nothing like a good butt-whooping to brighten up your day. MW: (sarcastically)Oh, yeah. It's the best. And now the Canuck sets down that chair, AND PILEDRIVES STEWART ON IT. And now what's he doing? He picks up Primal Fear, AND PILEDRIVES HIM ON TOP OF STEWART! This is disgusting! I can't believe that the Canuck is acting like this. LB: Believe it, buddy. MW: And now the Canuck picks up that chair again, and he's just pounding and pounding on them! They're both a bloody mess, and there's a HUGE dent in that chair, he's really hitting them HARD. BD: He sure as hell is. MW: And now finally, now that he has incapacitated both of his victims, the Canuck is heading back to the locker room, and smiling. This crowd is really giving it to him, he's the second one to turn on them tonight! LB: I know. Isn't it cool? MW: No, it's really not. Primal Fear and Mike Stewart are now being attended to by the paramedics, and I'm sorry fans, we're out of time and we've got to go. So sad though it is, this will have to be our closing note. So for now, for my broadcast colleagues The Lovely Bertha and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt, this is Matt Wilson saying so long everybody!
