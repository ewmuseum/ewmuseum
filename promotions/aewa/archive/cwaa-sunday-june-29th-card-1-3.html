And Kore Wrench spins the Major around and just popped him one! ****************************************************************************** EL: Hello, everyone, and welcome back to the world-famous Sportatorium in beautiful downtown Dallas, Texas for another exciting week of Championship Wrestling. We have three contests tonight that could be main events in any arena in the country, as Joshua Barnes finally gets "the Professional" Max Robertson right where he wants him- in a ring surrounded by lumberjacks! Nowhere to run, nowhere to hide, as the shy ex-bouncer battles the former Heavyweight Champion. The tag titles are on the line in a special Texas Tornado-Penaly Box match, as Twsiter tries to regain the titles that some say, rightfully, I have to say, that were stolen from them. Then, in or soecial feature match, Lt. Lawson defends the TV Title against Jak Hammer... inside a steel cage! So much action here tonight, I don't even know where to begin. We're going to talk to, what was his name?- Roger Gibson, that's it. He apparently is the legal represenative for the mysterious Masked Man, and we're going to talk to him about the Masked Man's reaction to the AEWA's decision to declare the CWAA Heavyweight Title vacant. (A non-descript individual in a business suit and carrying a briefcase arrives at ringside) EL: Mr. Gibson, let's cut right to the chase. Your, uh, client, I suppose, disagrees with the AEWA's decision? Roger Gibson: That is correct. The executove board of the American E-Wrestling Association has arrogated rights and responsibiliies pertaining to the local representatives and their incumbent champion. They have acted in contravention of all accepted industry practices and legal precedents, and we have no doubt that any judge in the country would agree with our demand that the decision be restored. EL: So you're threatening to sue the AEWA over the decision? RG: Not at this time. Instead, we have made a proposal that we feel will be acceptablle to all interested parties. In recognition of the costs and time that would be expended in a protracted legal battle, and in recognition of the claims that multiple parties would make to the CWAA Heavyweight Championship, we are prepared to propse that the title be awarded to the victor of a match held here four weeks from today between all of the individuals who have a claim on the championship, namely my client, Max Robertson, and Marc Thomas. Additionally, we must demand that my client's right to privacy be respected and that I, having power of attorney, be permitted to sign his contracts for him. I'm sure the AEWA will find this to be preferable to potentially expensive litigation. (Exit RG) EL: Well, there you have it. This Masked Man, or Mr. X says he won't sue the AEWA if he, Max Robertson and Marc Thomas can have some kind of three-way match for the held up title. Well, we have to take a quick break, but when we get back, we'll see a former CWAA Heavyweight champion in action, as "the Dreamer" Randy Sheradin takes on Mark Sherrick! Stay tuned! (commercial break) EL: OK- let's go to David Myers for the introductions! David Myers: Ladies and gentlemn, this next contest is set for one fall with a twenty-minute time limit. Introducing first, from Dallas, Texas, weighing in at 275 lb, here is Mark Sherrick! Lawson walked into AEWA Battle Klash II as the TV Champion of the CWAA, hooked up with English sensation Fuse, and he still has that title, but Fuse gave him all he could handle, and it was the Major's interference that prevented us from having a new champion. If Mark Sherrick- just blasts Sheradin's head into the turnbuckle- can pull this one off, what a feather in his cap. Now he chokes the Dreamer against that top rope- blatantly breaking the rules, right in front of Senior Official Jeff Howard!
