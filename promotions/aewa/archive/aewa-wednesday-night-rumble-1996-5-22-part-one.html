(The Shadow Warriors, Superfly Aaron Ladd and Rueben Thunder Slade, led by Wesley Cantrell stroll ringward, flashing $ and pointing at the Gladiators and laughing.) $25,000 Texas Tornado Challenge The Shadow Warriors vs. The Gladiators As the bell rang to begin the match, Ladd and Slade approached their opponents, the Gladiators looked at each other, nodded, and ripped off their own masks, while DJ Muggs X and Chris Grohl hit the ring area, and the faces behind the masks belonged to Matt MC Read and Axl Sharkman aka the West Coast Connection. Ladd and Slade stepped back, obviously concerned, and soon learned that he who hesitates is lost, as Sharkman and Read launched themselves at the Shadow Warriors, seemingly intent upon beating the $25K out of them. While Sharkman pounded Ladd into the mat, Read alternating brawling maneuvers with aerial assaults on Slade, until Thunder had no idea which end was up. While Cantrell screamed and paced around ringside, the WCC dismantled the Shadow Warriors piece by piece. At the 5 minute mark, Ladd managed to get back on his feet, but he soon discovered that it really wasn't worth the effort, as Sharkman grabbed him in a bearhug (holding him up a little higher than usual, a la Jim Neidhart setting up the Hart Attack) and Read ran across the ring and hopped onto the top rope, for a springboard clothesline. With Ladd dispatched thus, the WCC turned their attentions to Slade. Sharkman powerbombed Slade while Read went to the top turnbuckle, and then delivered a moonsault to his prone opponent. Read rolled Slade over onto his stomach and applied a Boston crab, while Sharkman went back after Ladd in the corner, choking him with the top rope. While the referee tried to separate Sharkman and Ladd, Slade submitted. While Grohl and Muggs X tried to get the referee's attention away from Sharkman and Ladd, Cantrell grabbed a chair, rolled under the bottom rope and slammed the chair into the back of Read's head. He then draped Ladd's arm across Read's body, and left the ring again. The referee turned around and made the three-count, awarding the match to the Shadow Warriors at 7:56. Cantrell dragged his men out of the ring (Ladd still attempting to submit, even though the match had ended and he had scored the winning fall). Neither Shadow Warrior appeared to realize that they had won, or that there were still on the planet earth, but Cantrell knew who had the $25,000. MW: I don't know where to begin. I can't believe that the Gladiators were the West Coast Connection. They'd been quiet for so long, ever since the loss to the Graveyard Shift at Battle Klash, that I'd thought they'd disappeared. I'm sure the Shadow Warriors wished they had! LB: But who won? MW: Well, we all heard and saw- LB: Yes or no question, Williams. MW: That's Wilson. LB: No, no. I'm positive it's Williams. (Ric Flair's entrance music announces the arrival of the AEWA National Tag team Champions, Mayhem, Inc., and their manager, The Legend, Josh Brown) Chicago Street Fight AEWA National Tag Team Champions Mayhem, Inc. vs. The Double T Playerz This match had no rules, and I was clear early on that this one fact of which the participants did not need to be reminded. The Double T Playerz had the early advantage, their use of foreign objects commonly found at ringside (chairs, cables, the timekeeper's bell, the railings) working to their benefit. At one point in the opening minutes, each DTP grabbed a member of the opposing team and we had dual Gun Shot DDTs, both for 2-counts. The action spilled out to the floor, with the DTP maintaining their advantage. Thomas cleared the announcer's table (upsetting Bertha, whose drink ended up in her lap) to make room for both members of Mayhem, Inc., who were stacked one on top of the other. Then the DTPs dragged the table so that it was right up against the ring apron. Then they brought Josh Brown into the ring. Thomas stood in the ring near the ropes on the side where the table bearing Mayhem, Inc.. was found, while Thompson whipped Brown into the ropes. Thomas back body dropped Brown over the top rope, down onto his team who were propelled through the table. The DTPs went back onto the floor, and Bertha screamed about the damage down to her outfit and to the table. Thompson made a gesture which will not be repeated here, and while Bertha fumed, the DTPs resumed their attack on the National champs. Thomas and Thompson each hit a Gun Shot DDT on the floor, then returned to the ring to await their opponent's revival. While the DTPs celebrated their unprecedented success and made belt-strapping-on gestures, Mayhem, Inc. struggled to their feet and crawled in under the bottom ropes. A four-way brawl resumed, with Thomas backing Mayhem I into one corner (where Mayhem I halted the DTPs momentum with a thumb to the eye) and Thompson brawling in mid-ring with Mayhem II. Bertha grabbed the timekeeper's bell and hopped onto the ring apron, concealing the bell behind her back She screamed again at Thompson, who dragged the near unconscious Mayhem II with him as he went to confront Bertha. At the last second she produced the bell and introduced it to Thompson's left temple. He dropped with all the force gravity could muster, while Mayhem I followed up on his eye-gouge-bought advantage, by whipping Thomas across the ring into the opposite corner, following himself for the trademark Mayhem avalanche. As Thomas hit the mat, Mayhem I picked up his partner and bodyslammed him on top of Thomas. Mayhem I then covered Mayhem II to help cover Thomas for the 3-count at 25:48.
