Sunday, August 10th, Card (1/4)

11 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last Week on Championship Wrestling Eric Lane: He has it! He has it! The Preacher has unmasked the CWAA Heavyweight Champion! (pop) EL: It's... it's... it's Marc Thomas! Marc Thomas is the Masked Man- the new CWAA Heavyweight Champion- and he's off and running! I can't believe it! ************************************************************** Eric Lane: Hello, everyone, and welcome back to the world-famous Sportatorium in beautiful downtown Dallas, Texas, for more Championship Wrestling. As you just saw, if you missed it last week, we do have a new CWAA Heavyweight Champion, as the mysterious Masked Man and his trusty brass knuckles came out on top against former champion "The Professional" Max Robertson, but then guest referee "The Preacher" Kore Wrench became the second man to unmask the Masked Man, and this time we got a good look at who it is- Marc Thomas! So Marc Thomas is the Heavyweight champion, heading for a showdown with AEWA World Champion Crimson Joe Reed at the supershow in Reunion Arena. And it looks as though he is building up a unit of reinforcements, as he appears to have renewed his alliance with Television Champion Jak Hammer as well as with former partner Ron Stern. We'll be taking to the new champion, as well as the Professional, later this evening, but first, the CWAA Tag Team Champions, "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey and "Marvelous" Michael Anderson, have something to show us, they say. So, without further ado, let's go to ring announcer David Myers and take a look at the champs. David Myers: Ladies and gentlemen, my guests at this time, they are the CWAA Heavyweight Tag Team Champions, Byron McNultey and Michael Anderson! (As "Sharp Dressed Man" the champs come out to their usual routine of strutting and dancing, and as usual, the fans don't like it. But, the champs don't let it stop them.) Byron McNultey: (waving to Eric Lane)How ya doing Lane-sy? That's good. Now, last week as you all know, "Marvelous" Mikey and I wrestled a grueling match for these here titles of ours. Now, we knew it was going to be a colossal encounter, so we set up the VCR to tape the show. And what do we find when we go home? Tell 'em Mikey. Michael Anderson: Well, "Pretty Boy," we discovered that our good pal Lane can't call a match like a professional. It was a pathetic display on his part to even attempt to call the bout. So, Dok, as a personal favor from us to you, and all of you watching at home-and we know you are, cause the best looking duo in Texas is on TV-we decided to announce the match- the right way. BM: That's right Mikey. So, if all of you fat slobs can take a few minutes of your pathetic lives and stop shovin' grits in your mouth, you'll do yourselves a favor and listen up to what we have to say about last week's greatest tag team matchup-ever. Don't forget to take notes, Lane-sy. Roll the tape! (The champs hop down to the floor to join Lane in watching on the monitor as we see a video replay of last week's tape with the champs' play-by-play and commentary dubbed over) ***************************** (The tape shows the balding Curtis Hansen and Dwayne Griffith (known for wearing the Sherman Helmsley hair style) standing in the ring with Jeff Howard and David Myers) MA: Okay. This should be great. The greatest wrestlers, the most handsome men, and now-we're the best announcers. And lucky for those rednecks in Texas, we bless them with our presence. BM: So true, Mikey. Let's prove what great announcers we are. As you can see, that lame-duck Myers is telling everyone that Mr. Jefferson and Mr. Clean are in the ring. And their opponents are...oh, would you listen to that! (On the tape "Sharp Dressed Men" by ZZ Top begins, and Anderson and McNultey come out to boos from the crowd.) MA: Those rednecks don't know a good thing, even when it hits them in the face. I must say, we're looking pretty damn good in that ring. BM: I must agree, wholeheartedly. Oh, wait, I think we're going to speak. (On the tape, Byron McNultey takes the house mike.) Byron McNultey: Hold on, hold on, hold on. Mikey and I know that what you people really love to see, what really gets your fat butts out of the la-z-boy and into the arena, isn't seeing the tag champs come out here in a non-title match. We know that what you want to see is a historic tag team title affair, and that is just what you're gonna get. Ain't that right, Mikey? (On the tape, he hands the house mike to his partner) Michael Anderson: You got it! I don't know who these guys are over here, but they have to be better than the stiffs we've been beating lately, so why don't you two come on over here-don't be shy-shake our hands and we'll let you fight us for these here gold belts. What do you say, boys? MA: (announcing) What a golden opportunity for Mr. Jefferson and Mr. Clean. BM: (On the tape) Come on. You- Mr. Jefferson! (To Griffith) Wouldn't you like the chance to wear this here shiny gold belt? Of course you would, of course you would. So let's go-that bell rings, and we have ourselves a tag team title match. Let's do it! BM: (announcing) A stunning development, but these two guys have shown what it takes to earn a chance at those precious belts of ours. Wouldn't you say so, Mikey? MA: What? I was a little dazzled at just how good we look. Especially compared to those other two guys in the ring. (On the tape, Anderson and Griffith hare a quick handshake, with Griffith looking around warily) BM: And there's a true mark of a sportsman as you and Mr. Jefferson start the match with a handshake. What was going through your mind at that time, Mikey? MA: Well, Byron, I was hoping he wouldn't be trying to con me. 'cause you know in this day and age, daddy, that so many people will say one thing, but do something completely different. BM: I can see you went for a power approach with that headlock, using your massive forearms to squeeze the blood out of his head. But, Mr. J was able to take you down by the waist. MA: Well, no one's absolutely perfect. Being that he was the first one to ever get out of my headlock, we really had no choice but to congratulate him. (On the tape, Anderson offers his hand again, and Griffith takes it again) BM: Okay. If at first you don't succeed, try try again. Correct? MA: Yes. So, I went to twist that arm of his off, but again, he proved himself to be a worthy opponent. (By reversing the hold. Anderson grabs the ropes, and there is a congratulatory shoulder-rub and back-slap by McNultey for Griffith)) BM: At this point, he said something to me like, "Ow. My shoulder hurts." Being the polite gentleman that I am, I gave him a quick shoulder rub, because we always want our opponents at 100%. (On the tape, Anderson motions for a high-five, and kicks Griffith in the gut as he responds) MA: Exactly. I went to show my good sportsmanship by giving him a high-five, but he said something very vulgar, that I won't repeat on the air. BM: I know, I know. I heard him. And after all we had done for him. I'm surprised you didn't take his head off. A very sound scientific move on your part (a punch), Mikey. (Anderson smashes Griffith's head onto McNultey's boot on the top turnbuckle) MA: Thank you. Right here, I picked him up and I saw you trying to tie your boot laces in the corner. That's why you had that boot of yours on the second rope, right? BM: Uh-hmm. MA: Well, I was just so shocked when he ran head first into it. I didn't understand it. BM: No matter what he did though, he is still twice the opponent that those other goofs are, led by that heifer. MA: Agreed. At this juncture, I thought "Hey, this guy has offended me and the 'Pretty Boy'. Why should I have all the fun?" So, I tagged you in. BM: And I appreciated that. Normally, I wouldn't slap a fellow wrestler like that, but there was some kind of bug on his cheek, and I tried to squash it. I missed, I think. Now, after I tossed him across to that part of the ring, I saw that fly again, and tried to use my elbow to get 'em. But, again I missed. MA: I saw that fly too. Right there, you showed your sound technical abilities, by nearly cracking that man's leg in half. And then ya tagged me back in. BM: You decided to make quite the impact on this man. A big elbow drop by the "Marvelous" one. MA: Now, last week Lane said that he kicked out at the two count. But, you can clearly see that I let the man get up, so we could bring in Mr. Clean. BM: A nice right hand. Very scientific. And, another quick tag. To be the best team in the world, you have to be able to make the quick tags. And we're giving the people at home some textbook examples. MA: You think these people ever opened up a textbook? BM: Do they have teeth? MA: Do you mean their own? BM: Yeah. MA: Then no. BM: There's your answer. MA: Thought so. Beautiful kick. And a well executed knee to the midsection by the "Pretty Boy." BM: I try. This is when I realized that if we didn't end this, we'd be late for our other appointment. So, time to end it I guessed. (As McNultey covers Hansen, Griffith's attempt to make the save is intercepted by Anderson) MA: Mr. Jefferson tried to attack you after the bell, like a coward. So, I had no other choice than to slam him to that mat, as you cleared the ring of the debris that was Mr. Clean. (Twister hits the ring) BM: And now those goofs come storming in. I'm really sick of those two always cheatin' and interfering. MA: So am I. But, save that 'til another day. On a scale of 1 to 10 for this match though, I give us a 100 daddy. ********************************************************** EL: Well, I'm pretty sure my job is safe. BM: You got a day job, JR? Smart move, smart move. MA: We have to go get ready. You know, tag champs always have to be ready for any challenge. You never know when another formidable duo like the one you just saw will pop up and give us another tough challenge like that. (Exeunt the champs- on the way out they pause to test the stability of the announcer's table) BM: You know, Mikey, I think we'd need a new desk. MA: I think champions deserve something more like mahogany. Something a little stronger. BM: Nah- a desk will be enough. MA: I guess you're right. (Finally, Exeunt long-winded champs) EL: Well, before we get to our first matchup of the night, let's talk to the man who could walk out of here with the CWAA TV Title belt strapped firmly around his waist, Joshua Barnes! DM: Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome my guest at this time, the number-one contedner for the CWAA TV Title, Joshua Barnes! {Joshua Barnes comes out without any music (he hasn't learned the trick of getting the PA personnel to play "Tom Sawyer" every time he comes out yet). He shakes hands with several of the fans and rolls into the ring, where Dave Myers greets him.} DM: Josh, tonight you have your first one-on-one title match against Jak Hammer for the Television Title. JB: Thanks. I'm thrilled to be facing Hak Jam... Jak Jammer ... Jak Hammer for the belt. But I probably won't make a great TB..V champion. I know that my.... {mutters to himself} what's that phrase? oh, yeah.... my "Interpersonal Communication Skills are Substandard." which means that I screw up when I try to talk. But the belt doesn't go to the guy who talks the best. It goes to the guy who beats his opponent in the ring. Jak, you're big, strong and tough. Well, so am I. Let's see who wins. Thanks, everyone. {Joshua leaves to shake some more hands and accidentally taking Dave's microphone with him. Dave quickly follows him out of the ring and asks for his microhpone back. A shamefaced Joshua Barnes returns the microphone and apologizes profusely.} EL: We're going to take a quick break here, and when we get back we'll see a top-flight newcomer from the Orient- the Samurai, Kensei Tokugawa. Stay tuned! (Monday nights on WYXZ-TV Channel 39, join the wacky crew at a Cincinnati radio station. But is the rock era over at WKRP? Andy Travis is under pressure to restore the old format and issue a public apology to Lawrence Welk! Then, on "Three's Company", is four a crowd? Has Chrissy's mother moved in with Jack? Only here on WYXZ-TV Channel 39!) EL: OK- let's get our first look at the Samurai! DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is set for one fall with a ten-minute time limit. Introducing first, in the ring to my left, from Roanoke, Virginia, at 265 pounds, here is Curtis Hansen! (Hansen and his fish-underbelly-pale skin and balding head and droopy sandy blond mustache do little to grasp the crowd's attention) DM: And his opponent- (Sammy Hagar's "Marching to Mar" plays, and the crowd cranes its collective necks to get an eyeful of the newcomer, dressed in his Samurai attire and carrying a kendo stick) DM: -from Nagoya, Japan, he weights 265 pounds, "The Samurai" Kensei Tokugawa! Your referee, Jeff Howard. EL: OK- we're underway- Curtis Hansen and Kensei Tokugawa. And it's Hansen to an arm bar, and the Samurai with a kick in the midsection to break the hold, Elbowsmash by Tokugawa. Irish whip- big chop, and Hansen goes down. Stomps away- now he picks him back up- kneelift by the Samurai. Looks a little like The Great Kabuki, a legend here in Texas. Another vicious chop- sends him for the ride- ooh! Savate kick, right under the jaw. Hansen has been on the defensive since the bell rang, as the Samurai looking very impressive here in his US debut. Clothesline now. Irish whip- wow! (pop) EL: And a tilt-a-whirl suplex by the Nagoya native. Now he pulls Hansen back to his feet- sort of a front hammer lock- no! He was just immobilizing the arm to deliver that heartpunch! A maneuver banned in 26 states! Now he sends Hansen for the ride- catches him- hangman! Just dropped him throat-first right on that top rope! Covers- 1... 2... 3! He got it! ("Marching to Mars" resumes playing) DM: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner, "the Samurai". Kensei Tokugawa! (the pops turn to boos as the Samurai takes his ceremonial kendo stick and flails poor Curtis with it.) EL: Oh. no! What on earth-? The Samurai has already beaten his opponent handily, no he's just trying to embarrass him! This is uncalled for! He cracks him with that kendo stick again- what is he trying to prove? Sends him for the ride- another savate kick, and Curtis Hansen in serious trouble here.ÊOK, the Samurai looks like he considers his point to have (Exit Tokugawa to a chorus of boos) EL: When we get back, we'll see former Heavyweight Champion Max "The Professional" Robertson in action- don't touch that dial!
