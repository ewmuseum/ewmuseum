Saturday Night Slam (9/14, part one)

17 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen, set on fire. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power Bomb, and a look at Mister Crowley DRIVING some guy's head into the concrete with the Premature Burial! Then the camera switches to the Lovely Bertha, Matt Wilson, and "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt in a packed arena) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Saturday Night Slam! We have plenty of GREAT matches scheduled for tonight! BD: In the main event, "Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum will be facing Twisted Steel in a barbed wire cage match! That should be VERY exciting.... LB: So what do we got first? MW: Marcus McTeague is defending his newly won TV Title against Curate Kill. It should be one heck of a match-up. BD: And the TV Title is coming back home to the Church, where it belongs! MW: That's arguable. Let's just go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA Television Title. Introducing first, the challenger, weighing in at 275 pounds, Cuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuurate Kill! (Curate Kill emerges from the locker room and heads towards the ring to a chorus of boos, he doesn't seem to notice.) LB: Alright, tonight Curate Kill is gonna show this idiot McTeague what a REAL wrestler is like, and he's gonna prove that McTeague's title win last week was a fluke! MW: Curate Kill certainly CAN win this match, but it's not gonna be easy, by any means. BD: Sure it is. MW: I don't think so. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Northern Ontario, and weighing in at 412 pounds, he is the AEWA Television Champion, Marcus "Natural Wonder" McTeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeague! (The Screaming Trees' "Sweet Oblivion" plays loudly over the PA as McTeague, belt around his waist, makes his way to the ring to a big crowd pop.) LB: Just look at this peon, and can you believe the fans actually LIKE him? MW: Yes I can. Marcus McTeague is a great wrestler AND a great person, and the fans appreciate him. BD: Do you think I CARE what the fans think? They're even dumber than McTeague. MW: (sarcastically) Ah, and no one can match intellect with YOU. BD: That's right. MW: McTeague steps into the ring and heads towards the center. Curate Kill charges in at him, BUT MCTEAGUE JUMPS AND CATCHES HIM WITH A FRANKENSTEINER--AND CURATE KILL COUNTERS THAT WITH A POWER BOMB! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Just look at that beautiful counter by Curate Kill. McTeague can't POSSIBLY match wits with him. MW: Curate Kill's not exactly the brightest guy in the world either. Curate Kill picks him up now, and grabs him by the head, DDT!! And Curate Kill DROPS an elbow onto McTeague now. BD: Just look, he's tearing McTeague apart! MW: Curate Kill picks McTeague up now, and whips him to the ropes! He comes back, AND CATCHES CURATE KILL WITH A CROSS BODY PRESS!!! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: That lucky lumberjack. MW: It was far more than luck. Both men up now, and McTeague tries a backslide, but Curate Kill gets the better of it and pulls MCTEAGUE over with it! One..Two..Kickout! BD: He reverses McTeague's move on him and takes control again! MW: We can see that, Big Daddy. Both men up, and Curate Kill throws a swinging right hand, DUCKED BY MCTEAGUE! McTeague catches him, ATOMIC DROP! LB: What a crappy move. MW: It's a setup move. Curate Kill is stunned, and McTeague is going up top! Curate Kill turns to face him, FLYING SUNSET FLIP! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Actually, I hate to admit it, but McTeague's pretty agile for a guy his size. MW: He sure is. Wait a minute, who's that coming to ringside? LB: It's Raekwon! MW: What's HE doing here? BD: How the heck should we know? MW: Well, he appears to be just watching, and the match continues in the ring. McTeague picks Curate Kill up, and BODYSLAMS him! He's picking him up again, and setting him up for the Piledriver!! BUT CURATE KILL COUNTERS WITH A HEADBUTT TO THE GROIN! LB: What a great technical wrestling counter! MW: Not, but it worked. Curate Kill grabs him by the head now, NECKBREAKER! McTeague pulls himself to the ropes, and he's trying to pull himself up! He's right in front of Raekwon, WHO SLAPS HIM! BD: (laughing hysterically) That was great! MW: Not really. It wasn't painful, just insulting. And now Raekwon is just walking away. LB: He got his message across. MW: That's for sure, and McTeague's gonna be none too happy with him. Curate Kill picks McTeague up now, and hits him with a piledriver of his own! He's really taking it to the champ! BD: That's not the only thing he's gonna be taking from the champ. MW: We'll see. Curate Kill appears to be getting awful cocky now. LB: He can afford to. MW: Curate Kill whips McTeague to the ropes! McTeague comes back, and LEVELS Curate Kill with a high cross body! That 412-pound frame came down right on top of Curate Kill! The cover! One..Two..Three!!!! (DING, DING) BD: That was a fast count, and McTeague pulled the tights! This is robbery! MW: Where do you come up with this stuff? RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of eleven minutes and thirty-five seconds, here is your winner, and STILL AEWA Television Champion... MARCUS "NATURAL WONDER" MCTEAGUE!! LB: What a rip-off! MW: Why must you guys make up an imaginary illegal tactic every time one of your favorites loses? BD: BECAUSE WE CAN'T DEAL WITH IT! Wait a minute, that's not what I meant. MW: No, but it was the truth, finally. LB: Just quit torturing us and tell us what's next. MW: Fine. Randall Osbourne is defending his newly won North American Title against newcomer Brian Montgomery. BD: What did Montgomery do for a shot? MW: The same thing Exodus did to deserve a shot at the TV Title last week-- he has been a great champion in other federations; an international superstar, if you will. LB: Well he's in the AEWA now, and he's going to become an international spot on the mat, because this is the big leagues! MW: I think he'll do just fine. Let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA North American Title! Introducing first, the challenger, from Houston, Texas, he weighs in at 225 pounds and is accompanied by his manager, "Hitwoman" Candice Rochon, here is Brian Montgoooooooooooooomery! ("An Ode to the Heartbreaker" plays loudly over the PA as Montgomery makes his way to the ring to a good-sized crowd pop. Candice Rochon is at his side.) BD: Well this match should be extremely boring. MW: It should not, it should be a great matchup between two outstanding wrestlers. LB: Says you. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 362 pounds, he is the AEWA North American Champion, Randall "The Rebel of Rock" Osbooooooooooooooourne! (AC/DC's "It's a Long Way to the Top" blasts over the PA as Osbourne, newly won belt in hand, makes his way to the ring to a big ovation.) MW: Osbourne looks ready to defend that belt against the world. BD: Well he doesn't have to worry about that tonight, he's only facing Brian Montgomery. MW: On the contrary, that's EXACTLY what he has to worry about. Brian Montgomery has wrestled, and won, in matches ALL OVER the world. LB: Whoop-de-doo-dah. MW: You'll see. Osbourne is in the ring now, and he and Montgomery shake hands in a nice show of sportsmanship. BD: Yuck. MW: And now they lock up. Osbourne gets the initial upper hand, and whips Montgomery to the ropes! Montgomery comes back, AND GETS LEVELED WITH A CLOTHESLINE! Montgomery gets up, and Osbourne grabs him, VERTICAL SUPLEX! LB: HA HA! Welcome to the AEWA, Brian Montgomery! MW: He's just feeling the ropes, he'll get it. And now Osbourne picks Montgomery up, INVERTED POWER BOMB! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! BD: Sure he'll get it, right after he gets planted for the three count. MW: Just stop that and give the boy a chance! Osbourne now, picks Montgomery up, and whips him to the ropes! Montgomery comes back, and Osbourne catches him, POWERSLAM!! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Osbourne must be having a ball knocking this rookie around! MW: And now Osbourne picks him up again, ANOTHER POWERSLAM!! One..Two..Kickout! BD: How'd he do that? That's the second consecutive powerslam he's absorbed! MW: I told you, the boy can wrestle, and he's no stranger to punishment either. Osbourne can't believe it either, but he goes back to work. He picks Montgomery up, SHORT CLOTHESLINE! He brings Montgomery to his feet again, grabs him, INSIDE CRADLE! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Okay, maybe he's tough, but he hasn't show us any offense yet. MW: He hasn't had the chance. Osbourne picks up Montgomery now, and BODYSLAMS him! And now he's going up top! Montgomery is still down, and Osbourne dives, FLYING ELBOWSMASH--BUT MONTGOMERY GETS THE LEG UP AND CATCHES OSBOURNE RIGHT IN THE JAW! BD: Okay, he's got his shot. Let's see what the rookie can do. MW: Montgomery gets to his feet, and LEVELS Osbourne with a clothesline! Osbourne gets up and Montgomery grabs him, DDT! LB: I guess he looks okay, but to tell you the truth, I don't think Osbourne's that great either. MW: You don't like anyone who doesn't cheat. And now Montgomery is going up top! He waits for Osbourne to get to his feet, FLYING SUNSET FLIP! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Not bad. What else does he got? MW: Just watch. Montgomery gets up now, and grabs Osbourne, he lifts him up-- BRAIN BUSTER! LB: I love that move. MW: I know. It's a devastating maneuver. Osbourne is slowly making it to his feet, and Montgomery is just waiting for him! Osbourne is up, and Montgomery nails him with a bulldog! Osbourne slowly gets up again, AND MONTGOMERY LOCKS IN "THE CLOUD", HIS VERSION OF THE SLEEPER! BD: It's over, Montgomery's gonna win! MW: Quite possibly. Osbourne is staggering, he's going out! Osbourne is DESPERATELY trying to escape! Osbourne reaches back, and grabs the head of Montgomery, he falls to the mat, JAWBREAKER! LB: What a great escape! MW: That it was indeed, we almost had a new champion there. Osbourne makes it to his feet, and Montgomery is still staggered from that jawbreaker. Osbourne quickly grabs him, AND HITS THE BACKSPIN DDT! The cover! One..Two..Three!! (DING, DING) BD: Well, it appears that Montgomery's not so bad afterall, but Osbourne still came out on top tonight. RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of nine minutes and thirty three seconds, here is your winner, and STILL AEWA North American Champion, RANDALL "THE REBEL OF ROCK" OSBOURNE! MW: Montgomery fought valiantly, but the champ still managed to pull out the victory tonight. I'm sure we'll be seeing a lot more of Montgomery in the future. LB: Well he wrestled pretty good, but I think he's still got a ways to go before he can seriously compete here in the AEWA. MW: Maybe so, but he's a VERY fast learner-- he'll adapt. BD: We'll see. LB: So what do we got now? MW: The Icemen, who fought to a bloody disqualification with Ronnie Hoffa's team very recently, will be facing off against the Assassins. BD: This should be a VERY interesting match. The Icemen are about to come out, so here we go.... (Ron Stern and Marc Thomas, displaying their trusty brass knuckles, hit the scene, with their customary expressionless faces and straight-ahead stare.) MW: Some of you may not know this, but you are looking at the Canadian Tag Team Champions, a title not affiliated with the AEWA, but which certainly testifies to their ring ability. LB: The Icemen are international stars, buddy. Now, here come their opponents.... (The masked Banshee and Shockwave make their entrance, to a warm crowd reaction. They are both wearing ninja suits.) MW: Okay, the referee is checking both teams, and we're about to start. These two groups are VERY talented, so this will be great. BD: Here we go.... The Icemen (Ron Stern and Marc Thomas) versus The Assassins (Banshee and Shockwave) These teams both are both desperate to get noticed by our championship commitee, so they wrestled a great match. The Icemen matched up well with the 230-pound Banshee, their no-nonsense technical styles countering his high risk approach, but the much larger Shockwave posed a problem for them. Also, his martial arts-oriented attack was different from anything else Stern and Thomas had encountered yet. The Icemen proved more adept at double-team tactics and at manipulating the ever-gullible referee, but were never able to able to sustain their advantage for very long. Banshee kicked out of several pin attempts, and Shockwave was too confusing an experience for them. On the other hand, Banshee found his aerial attack neutralized by their attacks on his legs, and Shockwave lacked the Icemen's conditioning. Momentum switched back and forth until the bell rang at the 30-minute mark, and the referee declared a time limit draw. MW: A rare time limit draw, as these two teams appeared to match up well against one another. BD: Well, I just checked my scorecard, and I had the Icemen ahead on points. MW: Let me see that-- (grabs a crumpled, stained piece of paper out of Big Daddy's hand). "Joy Luck Dragon Phoenix"? This is a Chinese menu! BD: Yeah, but if you look in the margins, you'll see where I kept score. MW: Oh yes.... it says "Icemen 12, Assassins 3". LB: That sounds about right. MW: I only have two problems with this.... First of all, we don't use a point system in the AEWA. Secondly, no one at any time appointed you the judge for this or any other match. BD: I appointed myself. LB: Yeah, it's called "initiative." MW: I think there are too many people around here who demonstrate their own brand of "initiative" already, and if rumors I hear about changes in the boardroom are correct, that may soon change. BD: Are they finally getting rid of that ugly carpet? LB: How come you got to see the boardroom's carpet? MW: Jeez, you two are pathetic.... ("Time is on My Side" begins to play) BD: What's that music playing for? Is it time for another match? MW: Interesting choice of words, Big Daddy. We have another debut this week, and it should be rather interesting. Chrono and "The Great" Jack Jerovah-- the TIME Angels. (The Time Angels, and their manager Jason White, make their first AEWA entrance. Chrono is 6'7" 250 pounds, clad in silver tights and a cloak. Jerovah is 6'11" 321 pounds, and wears gold.) BD: These guys are.... unique. MW: Before we start, I want you both to promise me one thing-- no Jehovah's Witness jokes! BD: I'll try my best. They're going to be wrestling some amateurs, so let's get this match underway.... The Time Angels (Chrono and "The Great" Jack Jerovah) versus The British Jobdogs (#1 and #2) The Time Angels' relative inexperience was not easily visible, and they wrestled very well. Their tags were quick, their double teams were effective, and their blend of technical wrestling and brawling demonstrated an ability to compete in different styles. The end came quickly when Jerovah nailed his Spinal Tap (a top-rope piledriver) on one of the Jobdogsat 5:16. As the referee raised their hands in victory, the Time Angels looked as though they hadn't even been in a match. The crowd seemed to appreciate their professional style. MW: That was an impressive and brief debut for the Time Angels, a team we're sure to see more of during the coming weeks. BD: I have to admit it-- they looked good. LB: What's next, Wilson? MW: It's now time for another episode of the Terrordome. This should be very entertaining, folks. BD: Johnny Terror always has some really interesting guests, and there are NO RULES on his show. I like that. MW: Let's watch.... ---------- (Public Enemy's "Welcome to the Terrordome" plays loudly throughout the arena as the camera shifts to a familiar set near the back of the arena. Hanging over this set is a large half-dome. Underneath the dome, there is a very dark, dank room FILLED with various gruesome looking torture devices. AEWA manager Johnny Terror emerges from behind the curtain, carrying an umbrella this week, due to last week's garbage throwing incident. The boos rain down and garbage pelts the umbrella, but Johnny just laughs. He gets to the set, puts down the umbrella, and picks up a microphone.) Terror: HA!! Did you people actually think you could outsmart ME? Think again, because Johnny Terror doesn't fall for the same thing twice, unlike some OTHER morons I know. Now let's get right down to business. By now you all know that this lovely place here is the Terrordome, and I am your WONDERFUL host, the STAR of the Terrordome, and MASTER of the universe-- Johnny Terror! (loud boos) Okay, maybe I was confusing myself with He-Man on that last part. I always do that, ya know, we look so much alike, except I'm MUCH handsomer. But enough of that, now it's time to get to my guest. He is one of the most mysterious AND most ruthless men in wrestling today. He is a former TV Champion and has torn apart EVERYONE he has gotten his hands on here in the AEWA, from The Punisher to even Vicki, which was done in a very tasteless and sadistic manner I might add, and I LOVED it! So at this time allow me to introduce this human machine of death and destruction, here he is, along with his manager, "Cutie" Rick Verrano.... THE GRIM REAPER! (The arena darkens...."The Funeral March" begins to play, and a single spotlight shines down on the Reaper. The only reflection comes from the scythe that the Reaper is holding. Rick Verrano is in front and is mouthing off inaudibly, and the aisle-side fans are giving it right back. The two enter the set with Terror, and the lights come up) Verrano: Finally, a show worthy of the Grim Reaper's greatness! It's a pleasure to be here, Johnny. We have big plans, my man, big plans! Terror: I'm sure that you do, otherwise you wouldn't be here, right? Big things ALWAYS come out on my show, because it's the best one around, and everyone KNOWS it. But let's get right down to business.... First off, I'd like to ask the big guy a question. What happened to you? Right after you lost the TV Title, you kinda disappeared from the AEWA for a while, only recently re-establishing yourself. Where ya been? Was it a disappearance by choice, or did the suits just kinda decide you didn't fit into their so-called "family entertainment"? (The Grim Reaper is silent and stands behind Verrano with his scythe on shoulder, as Verrano addresses the question) Verrano: Fantastic question as always, Johnny. I addressed this issue many times to President Smith and the rest of the so-called Think Tank of the AEWA on why Grim Reaper was being held back in competition. I was ignored on every one of my complaints. The truth is, never before in the history of this federation has a man created such fear. Officials are purposely preventing their precious "superstars" to even be in the same building as the Reaper. Nothing causes greater fear in a man than his own mortality, and the Grim Reaper is a symbol of that. Terror: He most certainly is. Guys probably wet their pants just standing across the ring from this man, I know I would. (loud laughter from the audience) YOU DIDN'T LET ME FINISH! What I meant to say was, ummm....."I know I would be.....uh...... intimidated", NOT wettting my pants. (whispering) Yeah, that's the ticket. So anyway, I've humiliated myself enough for one show, let's move on. Now in this recent resurgence of action you've had, you've allied yourself with Ricky Roberts, a guy that you FOUGHT at Championship Challenge. What's that all about? Verrano: The Reaper, you could say, took Robert's soul at Championship Challenge. Roberts got it back when he joined hands with the Reaper, in the torturing of Vicki's soul. And think about Twisted Steel, he is another example of souls here in the AEWA given another chance when they join hands with the Reaper. I want to use this world-wide television outlet on your show, Johnny, to announce the alliance of Twisted Steel, Ricky Roberts, and the Grim Reaper-- now known collectively as the "Angels of Death." If I could please have Ricky Roberts and Twisted Steel come out to stand before the world! (Ricky Roberts and Twisted Steel both walk out from behind the curtain. They are wearing "Angels of Death" T-shirts, and the crowd boos them loudly.) Terror: And here they are. So gentlemen, what do you have to say about this newly formed alliance? Roberts: It feels SO good to be out here, Johnny. We are declaring war on the AEWA, and EVERYBODY is on our hit list! Steel: Now that we all have formed the "Angels of Death", NO ONE can stop us! We are, without a doubt, the greatest stable in wrestling history. Terror: I couldn't think of a more imposing threesome if I tried. I'd hate to be Ben Slocum right now, or anyone else who crosses you guys for that matter. So what's next for the "Angels of Death"? Verrano: Well, as expected, the Angels are gunning for Ben Choke-em and his "friend" that limp-wristed pansy Sir Gallade. The Angels will be watching the Barbed Wire Cage match very closely, if you get my meaning, and hopefully Gallade will make an appearance. I would think those girls wouldn't mind taking on my "Angels of Death" in a three way tag match? In fact Slocum, you and Gallade pick a third member of this team....anyone....doesn't matter. The Angels are waiting. (Verrano throws the mike down) Come on, Angels! ("The Funeral March" blares and the Angels of Death leave in a single-file line) Terror: (Johnny picks the microphone up off the floor.) You coulda just handed it back to me, ya know. Well, anyway there you have it folks. War has been declared on the AEWA, starting with Ben Slocum. I'd like to thank my guests, Rick Verrano and The Grim Reaper, and my unexpected guests, Twisted Steel and Ricky Roberts, all now collectively known as the Angels of Death. Don't miss next week's show when my guest will be the very man that these men are all gunning for, Ben Slocum. So until then, later morons! (Johnny drops his microphone and begins to walk away. The camera shifts back to our announcing team.) ---------- MW: These so-called "Angels of Death" are really starting to get annoying. What's their problem? BD: They're the best team in AEWA history, Wilson. Think about that combination-- Twisted Steel, Ricky Roberts, and the Grim Reaper. Who could POSSIBLY beat them? MW: Ben Slocum and Sir Gallade. LB: (laughing) Yeah right! You MUST be joking. Don't even try to explain yourself, just tell us what's next.... MW: Up next, a team we haven't seen much of yet, the Hollywood Bodies, who are still trying to find their niche here in the AEWA. They have a tough task ahead of them tonight, as they face off against former world tag team champions, the West Coast Connection. BD: The Bodies will have a TOUGH TIME winning this match, because their opponents are skilled veterans. Speaking of which, here comes the West Coast Connection. (Cyprus Hill's "Throw Your Set in the Air" ushers the entrance of Axl Sharkman, manager Chris Grohl, MC Read, and manager DJ Muggs X. Sharkman appears to be explaining to Grohl that his in-ring help will not be needed tonight. Grohl looks doubtful.) MW: What's going on? There's a little bit of tension out here.... LB: Quit trying to cause trouble, Wilson. Chris Grohl just hasn't been having very good luck lately, and his team wants him to be careful. Now, here come the Hollywood Bodies.... (The Offspring's "Nitro" marks the arrival of "Extreme Superstar" Alex Austin and "Loose Cannon" Brian Michaels.) BD: The Hollywood Bodies look determined tonight, and they COULD pull off a shock victory. It's entirely possible. MW: Let's get down to ringside.... The West Coast Connection (625 pounds) versus The Hollywood Bodies (480 pounds) The Bodies used their speed advantage to get in the first blows, as Michaels went to work on Sharkman. The initial advantage was somewhat short-lived, as Michaels seemed to suffer from Attention Deficit Disorder. He just couldn't seem to stay concentrated on the match, and he was particularly interested in the crowd and the cameras. As Sharkman began to even the match up, Austin negated his attack with a discreet knee in the back from the apron. Grohl jumped to the ring apron to register a complaint, and Michaels bailed, so Sharkman ordered his manager back to the floor, then tagged in Read. After a moment's consultation, Austin tagged in for the Bodies. Read is much larger than Austin, but is probably the quickest of the AEWA's big men, so Austin did not enjoy the same advantage his partner had. Read pummeled the "Extreme Superstar" from pillar-to-post (and back again), and he and Sharkman displayed the double teaming techniques that had kept them in the title picture for so long. Order was eventually restored, but Michaels was unfocused, and Austin was clearly weakened by the double teaming tactics of the West Coast Connection. Despite a few flurries by the Bodies, the WCC pulled out the win at 12:17 when Read stuck Austin with a picture perfect Northern Lights Suplex. BD: Jeez, the Bodies got their BUTTS kicked. MW: This was yet another win for the West Coast Connection, who have to be considered a serious threat to regain the World belts. But the Hollywood Bodies seem to be going nowhere fast. LB: So what's next? ****TO BE CONTINUED IN PART TWO**** \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- American E-Wrestling Association!
