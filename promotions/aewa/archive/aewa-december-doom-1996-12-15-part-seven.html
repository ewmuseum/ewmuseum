December Doom (12/15, part seven)

22 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*********************************************************************** CONTINUED FROM PART SIX *********************************************************************** MW: Exodus will be taking on the mysterious Masked Yorel. It's really starting to bug me that we can't figure out who this guy is. I KNOW that he's someone familiar. LB: I know who the Masked Yorel is! MW: WHO?!? LB: He's the guy wearing the mask! MW: Gee, thanks for that brilliant insight, Bertha. LB: Anytime, Wilson. MW: Let's just go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, From Parts Unknown, and weighing in at 370 pounds, The Masked Yooooooooooooooooooooorrrrreeeeeeellll! (The Smashing Pumpkins' "Disarm" begins to play loudly over the PA, as the Yorel makes his way toward the ring to a BIG round of boos.) BD: The Yorel looks ready for action tonight MW: He sure does, but he's got quite a task ahead of him in the man called Exodus. LB: It'll be interesting to see how these two match up against each other. They're both REALLY big, and REALLY tough. It should quite a battle. MW: That's for sure. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Los Angeles, California, and weighing in at 346 pounds, Exxxxxxxxxxxxxooooooooduuuuuuus! (Anthrax's "1,000 Points of Hate" cranks out over the loudspeaker as Exodus makes his entrance to a HUGE round of boos.) BD: This is one guy you don't want to run into in a dark alley. Or a lit one, for that matter. MW: You sure don't. That's 7'1", 346 pounds of fury. This will likely be Yorel's biggest test to date here in the AEWA. LB: I think he'll pass. MW: We'll find out in just a moment. Both men are in the ring now, and we've got a lock up to get this thing underway! Yorel gets the early upper hand, and he grabs onto a headlock! He drags Exodus to the ropes, ROPEBURN! And now he turns him around with that headlock, BULLDOG! BD: It looks like the Yorel came to fight. MW: And you can bet Exodus did too. Exodus gets up, and the Yorel STUNS him with a roundhouse right! And he follows it right up with a chop! And now the Yorel is going up top! LB: He's an awful big guy to be coming off the top. MW: Yes he is, but he looks comfortable up there. Exodus rises to his feet, and the Yorel LEVELS him with a flying clothesline! And he looks like he's gonna end it right here! He's setting Exodus up for the Power Bomb! BD: It could be over already! MW: He's got him in position--BUT EXODUS COUNTERS WITH A BACKDROP! He was ready for that one. LB: Of course he was. Exodus does his homework. MW: Yes he does, and now he's got control of the matchup. Exodus picks the Yorel up, SNAP SUPLEX! The Yorel tries to get up, but Exodus grabs him, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: The Yorel's got too much juice left to go down to somethin' like that. MW: And now Exodus is going to the top himself! The Yorel slowly makes it up, and Exodus dives, FLYING BULLDOG! And the cover! One..Two..Thr..Shoulder Up! LB: What a move! That's why one false step can be your undoing against Exodus. MW: Without a doubt. Exodus is now taunting the crowd, and they're giving it right back to him. They really HATE ICU. And now Exodus turns his attention back to the Yorel. He picks him up, LEGSWEEP FACESLAM--BLOCKED BY THE YOREL! Yorel quickly counters, SNAP MARE! BD: This guy never ceases to amaze me. MW: And now the Yorel grabs Exodus by the head, and AGAIN nails him with a bulldog! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: He almost got him. MW: And now the Yorel lifts Exodus back to his feet, and whips him to the turnbuckle! Yorel follows him in, AVALANCHE--BUT NOBODY'S HOME! EXODUS MOVED JUST IN TIME! And the Yorel hit nothing but buckle! Yorel stumbles out of the corner, and gets nailed with a back brain kick! BD: Popular move today. MW: Yes, it has been. And now Exodus picks the masked man up, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: These guys don't show any signs of letting up. MW: No they don't. And now Exodus waits for Yorel to get to his feet, AND LEVELS HIM WITH A SHOULDERBLOCK! Exodus picks him up again, ANOTHER BELLY-TO-BELLY, AND ANOTHER COVER! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Exodus likes that belly-to-belly today, too. MW: Hey, why not, if it works. And now Exodus picks the Yorel up again. He grabs him, RUSSIAN LEGSWEEP! And now Exodus lifts him up, SIDE SUPLEX! LB: I don't think even the Yorel can take much more of this. MW: Exodus goes up top, and Yorel gets up. Exodus come off, and NAILS ANOTHER flying bulldog! Yorel tries to get up again, but Exodus is quick to sweep his legs and prevent it. BD: He's working him over slowly and methodically. That's exactly what he needs to do. MW: And now Yorel gets up again, only to be LEVELED with a clothesline from Exodus! Exodus picks his opponent up again, and NAILS him with a faceslam! LB: The Yorel had better start thinkin' about doing something if he wants to win. MW: Exodus picks up the Yorel again now, REVERSE NECKBREAKER! And here's the cover by Exodus! One..Kickout! BD: WOW! This Yorel's really amazing, but who the hell is he? MW: That's what everybody would like to know. But Exodus just goes right back to work! Exodus picks him up, FALLAWAY SLAM! And now Exodus is going up top again. He's perched up there, and Yorel rises to his feet, AND GETS NAILED WITH A FLYING LARIAT! LB: I STILL don't think he can take much more of this. MW: We'll see. Exodus picks the Yorel up now, SNAP SUPLEX! He's gonna pick him right back up now, SOVIET SUPLEX! BD: Maybe he wants to put on a suplex clinic in there. MW: Doubtful. And now Exodus picks up the Yorel, and NAILS him with an earringer! Yorel attempts to get back up, and Exodus takes him back DOWN with a Russian legsweep. LB: And here comes the master! MW: Deathwatch, the head of ICU, is now making his way to ringside! What does HE want? BD: Obviously, to see ICU triumph once again. MW: I think he wants more than that, but we're sure to find out soon. But right now, in the ring, Exodus FLOORS the Yorel with a shoulderblock! Yorel is down, and Exodus DROPS an elbow! And now Exodus is going up top! LB: Deathwatch apparently likes what he sees. MW: He has no reason not to right now. Exodus is on the top rope, and the Yorel is still down! He dives, FLYING HEADBUTT! Right on the mark! The cover! One..Two..Foot on the Ropes! BD: Close one there for the Yorel! MW: It sure was. And now Exodus again takes him down with a Russian Legsweep. And it looks like he's calling for the end here! He picks the Yorel up, AND HITS THE ARMAGEDDON! Here's the cover! One..Two..Thr..Shoulder Up! LB: WHO THE HELL IS THIS GUY?? MW: Apparently Exodus is wondering the same thing! He's trying to rip that mask off! But the Yorel won't let him! BD: It looks like Deathwatch is interested too, 'cause here he comes! MW: Deathwatch comes into the ring now, and he's got a chair! Exodus holds the Yorel up, AND DEATHWATCH CLOCKS HIM! And the referee doesn't like that. He's calling for the bell! (DING DING) LB: It was just a harmless little chairshot! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of fourteen minutes and fifty-eight seconds, here is your winner, as a result of a disqualification.... THE MASKED YOREL!!! MW: I don't think the Yorel's too happy about his victory right now though, as Deathwatch and Exodus are beating the CRAP out of him! And now they're going for the mask again! BD: It should be no problem now, he's out COLD! MW: Exodus RIPS that mask off, and OH MY GOD! IT'S LEROY BROWN, FROM ACEW! LB: He's held TWO World Titles over there! No wonder he was so damn good! MW: He was indeed a HUGE superstar, and Exodus recognizes him too! He's spent a great deal of time in ACEW himself, and he doesn't look happy with what he's found! He's STOMPING away on him, and Deathwatch is joining in! BD: And they look to be really enjoying this. MW: And now they both SPIT on the Yorel, and they are apparently satisfied with their work, as they smile and head back for the showers! At least we finally know who the Masked Yorel is. LB: But there's still one thing I don't get. What in the hell is a Yorel? MW: (looking down at his program) Oh jeez! It was right in front of our faces the whole time! BD & LB: (in unison) What was? MW: Yorel! Y-O-R-E-L BD: Huh? LB: I don't get it. MW: Reverse the letters! BD: Let's see. (thinks VERY hard) L.....uh....R........Y...uh....O....E? LB: What the hell's a Lryoe? MW: Learn to spell, morons. Yorel backwards is LEROY! BD: Really? LB: Are you sure? MW: Yes, I'm SURE. Now I know why YOU TWO never figured it out. BD: Quit insulting our intelligence and tell us what's next. MW: Up next is our MAIN EVENT. Shane Matthews defends his AEWA World Heavyweight Title against a former champion, Ben Slocum. And now we've got some pre-recorded comments from Matthews before the big match. Roll it guys. ---------- (The scene opens on the AEWA locker room. Shane Matthews stands there, with the AEWA title belt draped over his shoulder At his side is his manager, Johnny Terror. They don't look happy.) Matthews: What the hell was that on Wendesday Night Rumble, Johnny? Terror: Well the way I saw it, you were kicking some ass, as always, and then along came Larkin and his two goons and they ambushed ya. Matthews: Exactly, and you just don't sneak up and attack Shane Matthews. Shane Matthews attacks YOU. Larkin, Horde, you boys just made the biggest mistakes of your lives. Now I'm busy defending my world title belt, and neither of these guys is gonna EVER get a shot at it, because they're tag teamers, so we've got somethin' else in mind. Tell 'um Johnny. Terror: Well if there's one thing about the Ring of Terror, we stick together. The Italian Loan Collection Agency has been looking for their next victims, and I think we've just found them. Matthews: That's for sure. You'd better be watching your back, Horde, because NO ONE attacks Shane Matthews and gets away with it. But now let's move on to discussing what we came here to talk about. ME! It looks like at this Pay-Per-View I'm scheduled to take on Ben Slocum. Slocum is a former World champ, and he's got his eyes on a second one tonight. Lemme tell ya somethin' Slocum, AIN'T GONNA HAPPEN. I beat you once before, and tonight is gonna be no different. Terror: That's right. There's nobody here who can stop ya, Shane. Same deal in the TOWL. Matthews: Speaking of the TOWL, for you morons who didn't see the last show, take a peek in that locker over there. (The camera pans back to Shane's locker. Sitting inside it are not only the TOWL World Title, but the TOWL US Title as well!) Matthews: It seems the guy who was US champ thought he was good enough to take Sudden Death. He was wrong. And so is Slocum if he think's he's got a chance in hell at this puppy right here. He is going to get his ass SPANKED, and there ain't no way he's coming out of this one on his own power. Terror: Hey, maybe if we're lucky, Slocum's dad will be here! Matthews: Yeah, that's right, your Mom and Pop WERE here the last time you won the title, weren't they Benny? Well this time it's gonna be a different story. I ain't gonna go after them, like Crowley was stupid enough to do. I think watching their baby boy get his ass whipped in the ring is far better torture, don't you, Johnny? Terror: You know it, Shane. Matthews: So you be sure and give your mom a kiss before the match, Slocum. It just might be the last time she gets to see you, because this time, you're gonna experience Sudden (Shane draws his thumb across his throat) Death. (fades to black.) ---------- LB: Shane is right, when the night is done, Slocum'll STILL be a FORMER champion. MW: I don't know about that. Slocum's parents ARE at ringside tonight, and that's gonna give him some extra motivation for sure. BD: Big deal. Shane's got Johnny Terror. MW: And what kind of emotional advantage is he? BD: None, but I can bet ya he's got a few tricks up his sleeve. MW: Yeah, like a set of brass knuckles. LB: You wouldn't call that an advantage? MW: I suppose so, but you can't count on stuff like that all the time, and if that's Shane's lone offensive strategy tonight, he's gonna get beat. And with that in mind, let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is set for one fall and is for the AEWA World Heavyweight Championship! Introducing first, the challenger, >From Austin, Texas, he weighs in at 302 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his sister, Sondra. He is a former AEWA World Heavyweight Champion, "The Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slooooooooooooooooooooocuuuuum! (Laura Branigan's "Self Control" plays loudly over the PA as Slocum makes his way toward the ring to a HUGE FREAKING CROWD POP! He and Sondra slap hands with the fans all the way to the ring, and Ben DOES stop to kiss his mother and hug his father at ringside.) BD: What the hell does he care about those old bags for? MW: Those are his parents! BD: I repeat, what the hell does he care about those old bags for? MW: Jeez, Big Daddy, you just don't respect anything. BD: Hey, I respect my mom. Every Christmas I visit her house for at least two minutes. MW: How thoughtful of you. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, he weighs in at 222 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his manager, Johnny Terror. He is the current AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, "Sudden Death" Shaaaaaaaaaaaaaaane Maaaaaaaaaaaaaaattttttheeeeeeeews! (Anthrax's "Looking Down the Barrel of a Gun" cranks out over the sound system as Matthews, belt around his waist, makes his way to the ring to a HUGE round of boos from the crowd. Johnny Terror is at his side.) LB: Matthews looks ready to kick some butt tonight. MW: He always LOOKS ready, but IS he? BD: Of course he is. MW: We'll see. Matthews is in the ring now, and he is just TAUNTING Slocum. Slocum knows better than to bite though. That's EXACTLY what Shane wants him to do. Both men are in the center of the ring now, and we've got a lock up! Slocum gets the upper hand! He grabs Matthews, DDT! LB: Matthews let him do that to lull him into a false sense of security. MW: Sure he did. And now Slocum picks Matthews up, it looks like another DDT, with a twist, SPINNING DDT! Slocum pick him up again, another headlock, INTO A BULLDOG! BD: This isn't the way it's supposed to start. MW: Well that's the way it IS starting, so get used to it. Now Slocum picks Matthews up, and STUNS him with a kneelift! Shane drops his head, and Slocum grabs it, ANOTHER DDT! LB: Here a DDT, There a DDT, Everywhere a DDT! MW: Very funny. Now Slocum DROPS an elbow onto Matthews, and now he's picking him up off the canvas! Slocum appears to be setting Matthews up for something here. He lifts him into the air, POWER BOMB!!! The cover! One..Two..Thr..KICKOUT! BD: Almost a new champion there, but Shane just wouldn't lie down and take it. MW: No he wouldn't, and I've gotta at least admire that about him, if nothing else. Slocum picks Matthews up now, and whips him to the ropes--BUT MATTHEWS REVERSES! Slocum goes to the ropes, AND JOHNNY TERROR PULLS DOWN THE TOP ROPE, AND SLOCUM GOES FLYING OUT! LB: THAT'S the advantage that Johnny Terror brings to the table, and the ref didn't even see it! MW: No, but he's VERY suspicious about how Slocum SPONTANEOUSLY flew from the ring. BD: He can't prove anything. MW: No, I don't suppose he can. Matthews is on his feet now, and Slocum has just CRAWLED back into the ring! Matthews gets in a few kicks, and now he picks up Slocum, and places him on the top rope. And now he's climbing up there with him! LB: And I think we know what's coming now, it's the End of the Road for Slocum. MW: Matthews has reached the top rope, and he jumps, AND NAILS SLOCUM WITH THE END OF THE ROAD!! HE HIT IT!! Here's the cover! One..Two..Thr..KICKOUT! BD: WHAT??? MW: He kicked out, that's what. If Slocum's got one thing, it's heart. And Matthews is REALLY giving it to the ref about a slow count, but he's not getting anywhere with it. He FINALLY turns back to Slocum, and he picks him up off the mat, and whips him to the ropes! Matthews puts his head down, BUT SLOCUM RUNS AROUND IT!! He comes back off the opposite ropes, AND NAILS MATTHEWS WITH A BULLDOG! LB: He can't do that! MW: He just did. Slocum picks Matthews up now, and gives him a well deserved SLAP! And now Slocum lifts him into the air, and NAILS him with a Hotshot! BD: Come on, Shane! MW: And now Slocum picks Matthews up, and WHIPS him to the turnbuckle! He charges in behind him, AND CRUSHES HIM INTO THE CORNER! Slocum NAILS Matthews with a knee to the back, and now he picks him up, SPINNING POWER BOMB! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Shane's hangin' tough. MW: He's a survivor. And now Slocum picks him up, and nails him with another Hotshot! He picks Matthews up again, ANOTHER SPINNING POWER BOMB! The cover! One..Kickout! BD: WOAH! Shane's REALLY got something DEEP inside there. MW: He sure does! Slocum waits for Matthews to get back up, and he NAILS him with a spinning mule kick! Slocum grabs Matthews by the head, and nails him with another bulldog! LB: (yelling) Okay Shane, you can turn it around now! MW: I don't think that's HIS choice, Bertha. Slocum NAILS Matthews with a knee to the back, and now he picks him up again! Slocum grabs him, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Shane looks like he's getting tired. MW: Who WOULDN'T be? Slocum picks Matthews up now, AND LOCKS IN A SLEEPER HOLD! You said Matthew was tired, well Slocum's gonna put him to sleep! LB: Fight it, Shane! MW: Matthews' legs are getting wobbly, he's staggering, BUT HE COUNTERS WITH A JAWBREAKER! Nice escape there by Matthews. BD: I KNEW he'd get out. MW: Sure you did. And now Matthews NAILS Slocum with a headbutt! Slocum tries to get to his feet, and Matthews FLOORS him with a dropkick! And now Matthews is going up top! LB: Something cool's coming up here. MW: Maybe. Slocum has reached his feet again, and Matthews DIVES, FLYING DROPKICK! Slocum tries to get up again, but Matthews catches him with a single leg takedown! BD: This is actually pretty exciting. I never thought I could actually ENJOY watching Slocum wrestle. MW: (laughs) Ya learn something new everyday, right Big Daddy? Matthews STOMPS on Slocum again, and now he's going up top! He's perched up there, BEAUTIFUL MOONSAULT!! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: That's was THREE! I saw it! MW: Apparently Matthews thinks so, but he's going right back to work. He brings Slocum to his feet, and NAILS him with a handspring bodyblock! And now Matthews picks Slocum up, and places him on the top turnbuckle! Matthews climbs up there with him, AND NOW HE'S REACHING INTO HIS TIGHTS! He pulled something out of there, and HE CLOCKS SLOCUM WITH IT, and quickly tosses it away! BD: And Johnny Terror had the ref's attention when he did it! MW: Now Matthews jumps, AND NAILS SLOCUM WITH THE END OF THE ROAD!! The cover! One..Two..Three!!!! (DING DING) LB: I TOLD you Matthews would win. Slocum never had a chance! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of twelve minutes and four seconds, here is your winner, and STILL AEWA World Heavyweight Champion.... "SUDDEN DEATH" SHANE MATTHEWS!! MW: Well Matthews reaches into his bag of tricks, and he managed to pull out a cheap win here tonight. And Slocum's not gonna be very happy about this when he wakes up. BD: IF he wakes up. MW: But wait a minute, here comes Randall Osbourne down to ringside! Matthews is trying to leave, BUT GETS MET WITH A CLOTHESLINE BY OSBOURNE!! Osbourne is obviously still VERY upset about when Shane attacked HIM several weeks ago, and now he's returning the favor! LB: This isn't fair! Where's security? MW: I don't see them anywhere, to tell ya the truth. It's like they're looking the other way. Osbourne picks up a chair, and BASHES it over the head of Shane Matthews! AND ANOTHER ONE! BD: Somebody's gotta stop this! MW: Osbourne grabs Shane by the head now, BACKSPIN DDT ON THE CONCRETE! And now Osbourne is making belt strapping motions around his waist! And he sees the belt in Johnny Terror's hands, and he's going after it! LB: Don't let THAT moron touch the belt, Johnny! MW: Johnny bails out through the stands, and while Osbourne was distracted with Johnny, the Italian Loan Collection Agency just came out and took Shane Matthews back to the locker room! But the damage has been done! BD: When Shane gets his hands on that sneaky little Osbourne, there's gonna be hell to pay. MW: We'll see about that. But right now, Osbourne is getting the ovation of his life! He helps Ben Slocum to his feet, and Slocum raises HIS arm! The crowd is going NUTS! LB: Screw what the crowd thinks, Shane still has the belt. MW: Yes he does, but maybe not for much longer. And I'm sorry fans, but that's all the time we have. We've run right up until the LAST minute of Pay-Per-View time. So now, for The Lovely Bertha and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt, This is Matt Wilson saying so long everybody! (A clip is shown of Shane Matthews hitting Ben Slocum with a foreign object, and then the End of the Road for the 1, 2, 3. The clip then shifts to Osbourne attacking Matthews in the aisle, DDT'ing him on the concrete, and then fades to black on an image of Ben Slocum raising OSBOURNE'S arm in victory.) \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- I'd like to thank Henry Gehringer, Tommy Smith, Kevin Larkin, Roy Stanley and Matt Furnish (in no particular order) for making this pay-per-view come together.
