AEWA — Card Archive

December Doom (12/15, part three)

22 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
************************************************************* CONTINUED FROM PART TWO ************************************************************* MW: It's now time for the Italian Street Fight match, as Mean Massimo squares off against American Ambush! Buckle your seatbelts, folks.... BD: We had to wait until a pay-per-view to have this match, because it's going to be too violent for regular television. I can hardly wait! MW: Before the match begins, we have a special pre-recorded interview with American Ambush. This was conducted by Hank Thompson several days ago. LB: Let's watch.... ---------- (We see Hank Thompson in the locker room, holding a microphone. American Ambush walks into the room, looking very angry.) Thompson: Ambush, you didn't fare too well in the Battle Royal for the AEWA World Title.... and your alleged 'helpers' were nowhere to be seen. Ambush: My hatred for Mean Massimo got the better of me, and I paid for it, that much I give you. As far as my icy surprise is concerned, the timing isn't quite right at this point. I just want to let everyone know that someone's watching my back, and if the Church of the Pounding Light go for anything shady, I won't be on my own. Thompson: The two of you have been tag team partners almost forever. Why do you think Massimo double-crossed you ? Ambush: To tell you the truth, I have no idea. The only thing I recall is that Massimo got a phone-call the morning before he turned on me. He said it was a prank call, so I didn't pay any attention. Maybe Gomez put some lies in Massimo's ear. In any case, there is no excuse for what Massimo did, and I won't stop short of anything in my quest to send him packing for Milan ! (The scene fades to black, and the camera returns to our broadcast team.) ------------- MW: Ambush looked ready for action, folks, and now he's going to back up his words. Personally, I'm rooting for him all the way. BD: But why? He's every bit as sneaky as Massimo, so you're really just choosing the lesser of two evils. MW: I disagree, Big Daddy. Massimo recently tried to stab him on national television, and that's when the whole situation went too far. Even Ambush wouldn't have tried something like that. LB: These two guys are going to DESTROY each other (turns to the camera) If there are any small children watching, change the channel! This match is going to give you nightmares! MW: That's actually some good advice, Bertha. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is an Italian Street Fight, with falls counting anywhere in the building. Introducing first, at a weight of 243 pounds, this is AMERICAAAAAAAAAN AMBUSH! (American Ambush makes his way toward the ring to a BIG round of boos from the crowd. He has a VERY angry expression on his face.) MW: This Italian Street Fight should be a good one! Ambush has been obsessed with revenge for the past few weeks, and he hopes to finally settle the score tonight. LB: And falls account ANYWHERE, Wilson? MW: Absolutely anywhere. LB: So they can just freely roam throughout the building? That seems a little dangerous, doesn't it? MW: Of course, Bertha; that's the whole idea. LB: But what if they come over HERE? What if they spill blood on my new skirt? Now that would REALLY put me in a bad mood.... MW: Then you might want to leave, Bertha. Wait a minute, Massimo is coming down to the ring now.... RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 325 pounds and accompanied by his manager, Greedy Giovanni, MEAN MASSIMOOOOOOOOOO! (Guns 'N' Roses' "Welcome to the Jungle" plays loudly over the PA System as Massimo makes his way to the ring, to a HUGE round of boos. Greedy Giovanni is at his side.) LB: Massimo was raised in Milan, so he's probably accustomed to this style of fighting. MW: Good point. Now Massimo enters the ring, and hits American Ambush right away with a BIG right hand! He dives on Ambush, and punches away at him.... BD: I LOVE IT! You can just forget about any scientific wrestling-- this is going to be a BRAWL! MW: Massimo lifts Ambush up, and tosses him DOWN with a bodyslam! Now he follows that up with a BIG legdrop! LB: Ambush is getting destroyed pretty early in this match; he doesn't even stand a chance. MW: Massimo exits the ring, and picks up a chair! He throws it into the center of the ring.... This is all perfectly legal, folks. BD: What's he going to do with that?! MW: Massimo lifts American Ambush onto the top turnbuckle! AND HE NAILS HIM WITH A SUPERPLEX RIGHT ONTO THE CHAIR! That was absolutely incredible! LB: Ambush is completely unconscious right now, and Massimo quickly goes the cover.... One....Two...Kickout! MW: But it wasn't enough to get the pin! Massimo is clearly frustrated, and he picks Ambush back up. He whips him to the ropes, BUT AMBUSH SHOCKS MASSIMO WITH A CLOTHESLINE! Where did THAT come from? Now Ambush covers him! One....Two...Kickout! MW: Massimo grabs the chair, and swings at Ambush with it. Ambush moves out of the way, and grabs the chair from him! BD: I don't like the looks of this! MW: American Ambush SMASHES the chair right on Massimo's head! He really HAMMERED him with it! LB: Ambush places the chair right on Massimo's head! AND HE DROPS A BIG LEG! BD: Massimo may be out cold! MW: Now Ambush pulls Massimo to his feet, and he hits him in the kidneys with a well-placed left hand! That did a LOT of damage, folks. BD: Massimo is really getting his butt kicked. I've never seen him beaten like this! MW: And Ambush hits him with a thumb to the eye. Massimo is temporarily blinded, and he gets NAILED with a DDT! The cover! One.......Two....Kickout! MW: Ambush picks that chair up again! It's already bent in two from when it was smashes over Massimo's head, but he's going to use it again.... LB: Massimo ducks the swinging chair, AND TAKES OUT THE LEGS OF AMBUSH WITH A SHOULDERBLOCK! MW: And now Massimo picks up the chair, and hits Ambush in the back with it! That could have CRIPPLED him, for crying out loud! LB: Now that he's back in control, Massimo is going to do some REAL damage. Ambush is about to get his ass kicked.... MW: Massimo scoops him up, and PLANTS him with a belly-to-belly suplex! He goes for the cover and hooks the leg.... One.....Two..Kickout! BD: Massimo just can't seem to beat this guy, Wilson! It's not fair-- the referee has been making some really slow counts. MW: The ref is doing a GREAT job, Big Daddy. Now Massimo is absolutely ENRAGED, and he tosses Ambush right out of the ring. That was a move of desperation.... LB: It was all a part of Massimo's brilliant plan, Wilson. That guy NEVER gets desperate. MW: Whatever you say, Bertha. Now Massimo follows Ambush out, and hits him with a single-arm DDT on the concrete! He grabs another chair, AND CRACKS IT OVER THE SKULL OF AMBUSH! BD: Ouch.... MW: You can say that again, Big Daddy. Massimo tries to pick him up, but Ambush reverses it-- AND HE TOSSES MASSIMO INTO THAT STEEL GUARDRAIL! That had a CRUSHING impact! LB: This match has been going back and forth, folks. MW: Now Ambush scoops him up, AND POWER BOMBS HIM ON THE CONCRETE FLOOR! That could have KILLED Massimo! BD: And now Ambush is going for the Camouflage Clutch! He once defeated Cardinal Chaos with that move, if I remember correctly. MW: Yes, he did. He locks it on, right there in front of the fans at ringside! Massimo is screaming in pain! LB: But Massimo CAN'T give up; this match means too much to him! We need some HELP out here! MW: But he just submitted! The referee calls for the bell, and American Ambush has emerged victorious! (DING DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner of the Italian Street Fight, via submission, AMERICAN AMBUSH! BD: I have to admit, that was a GREAT bout. Mean Massimo fought with every bit of strength in his body, but that damn Camouflage Clutch is just impossible to escape. He wasn't prepared for it. MW: I don't blame him. LB: And now Ambush just SPITS on Massimo, adding insult to injury. Sadly enough, this match probably didn't solve ANYTHING. These guys are going to hate each other for the rest of their lives. BD: Good point. So what's next? MW: It's time for a big grudge match, as Heavy Metal is about to square off against Scorpion! I've been looking forward to this one, folks. But first, we've got some pre-recorded comments from Scorpion. Let's get to those. ---------- (The scene opens on an empty old gym. Empty, that is, except for the old wrestling ring in the center. The entire place is dark, except for the lone light that shines brightly on that ring. Inside the ring, an ENORMOUS oriental man stands. He looks to be well over five hundred pounds. Across the ring from him stands a much smaller man, wearing bright blue tights and a familiar looking mask. This man has no name. He is known only as Scorpion.) Scorpion: Hello AEWA, I'm glad you could make it. This is the Scorpion's Lair. This is where I train, and tonight, I'm training for a BIG one. December Doom, and the 7' 4", 450 pound monster known as Heavy Metal. It seems that he's a little upset at me for costing him a couple of shots at titles. Maybe he has every right to be, but you know what? I don't care. He is my ticket to the big time. I've been sitting around this federation doing absolutely nothing for the past few months, and I FINALLY got someone to notice me. They wouldn't even PUT me on a Pay-Per-View before. I don't regret this decision in the least. But now that I've drawn his ire, the task of beating him still remains. I may not be as big, or as strong as him, but I know for damn sure I'm a LOT faster than him, and a lot smarter than him. That will be his downfall. Preparing for a man of this size is no easy task, however. That's why Johnny hired this guy here, uh, what's your name again? Man: I am Hamamato, grand sumo champion of all Japan. Scorpion: Yeah, whatever. Anyway, Johnny paid this guy wrestle me to help me get ready for Heavy Metal. So are you ready to go, big guy? Hamamato: Of course, you will fall just like all the others. Scorpion: Gee, that's great. (yelling) Hey Johnny, we're ready! (Johnny Terror walks into the ring from out of the darkness. He is wearing a referee's shirt and eating a hamburger.) Terror: (with his mouth full) Already? But I just started eating! Scorpion: Don't worry, this won't take long. Terror: Okay, fine. (Johnny walks over to the ropes and sets his hamburger down on a table at ringside, he walks back to the center of the ring.) Okay, you ready? (both men nod.) Okay, ding! (The sumo champion takes the traditional sumo stance as he gets ready to do battle. Scorpion begins to move in. He locks up with the big man, who promptly SHOVES him to the ground. Hamamato moves in, and drops a big elbow, but there's no one there! Scorpion is quick to his feet, and drops a knee on the big man. Scorpion is quick to his feet and runs to the ropes. The big man struggles up to his feet, and Scorpion actually jumps ON to the ropes, and comes back with a springboard moonsault that FLOORS the sumo. Scorpion picks the big man up to his feet, and brings him back DOWN with a reverse neckbreaker. After doing this, he is quick to scale the ropes. Hamamato again struggles up to his feet, all the while Scorpion is poised and ready to strike. He finally makes it up, and Scorpion dives, and catches the sumo's head right between his legs, and connects with a PERFECT top-rope Frankensteiner, otherwise known as the Sting of Death. Scorpion makes the cover, and Johnny makes the count. One...Two...Three.) Johnny: Can I go eat now? Scorpion: Yeah, go ahead and do whatever the hell it is you were doing. Heavy Metal, what you just saw was only a sample of what I plan to do to you at December Doom, and just remember, the bigger they are, the harder they fall. At December Doom, you're going down! (fade to black.) ---------- BD: Their feud has been going on for a LONG time, and they hope to finally settle the score tonight. LB: Scorpion eliminated Metal during that recent battle royal, costing him the World title. Then, just a few weeks later, Scorpion distracted Metal during a triangle match for the TV championship. As you can see, there isn't any love lost in the air between these two guys. BD: That's for sure. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, weighing in at 250 pounds, here is SCORPION! (Bush's "Machinehead" plays loudly as Scorpion makes his entrance, to a BIG round of boos. He is wearing his usual blue and black mask, with green tights. Johnny Terror follows closely behind him.) MW: Johnny Terror is the main reason for Scorpion's sudden change of attitude, in my opinion. Johnny has plenty of money, and unfortunately, everybody has a price. LB: For once, we both agree. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 450 pounds, he is a former AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, HEAVY METAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAL! (Metallica's "Battery" cranks over the PA as the MASSIVE Heavy Metal runs to the ring, to a HUGE downpour of boos. He looks VERY angry, and immediately charges for Scorpion.) MW: Jeez, he's going absolutely CRAZY over there! If the referee doesn't get some control early in this match, it's going to be even worse than that Italian Street Fight. Heavy Metal wants to KILL Scorpion right now.... LB: And he plans on doing just that! MW: Heavy Metal storms the ring and NAILS Scorpion with a BIG clothesline! He hits him with a series of right hands, into the corner! BD: Wow, he's really taking it to him! MW: And Heavy Metal hits Scorpion with a HUGE bodyslam! He pulls him to his feet, and hits him again with a clothesline! LB: Scorpion is getting his ASS kicked right now, folks. It's still rather early in this match, but he'd better start fighting back soon. MW: Now Heavy Metal picks up Scorpion, and nails him with a reverse DDT! Jeez, he's just SLAUGHTERING him! LB: Metal hasn't given Scorpion any time to mount ANY offense! Has the match even officially started yet? This is INSANE! MW: Now Heavy Metal quickly picks him up again, AND NAILS HIM WITH THE MEGADETH POWER BOMB! No matter WHAT happens at this point, the match is OVER! Metal goes for the cover.... One...Two...Three! (DING DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner, after only two minutes, HEAVY METAL! BD: That wasn't even a match, it was a complete MASSACRE. Scorpion wasn't expecting the sudden attack, and it cost him in the end. LB: Heavy Metal should be getting some more title shots soon, because he just MANHANDLED Scorpion out there. ************************************************************* TO BE CONTINUED IN PART FOUR ************************************************************* \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- American E-Wrestling Association The AEWA-- We Deliver!
