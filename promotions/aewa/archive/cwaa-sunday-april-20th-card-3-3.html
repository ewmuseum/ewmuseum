Sunday, April 20th, Card (3/3)

20 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(This Wednesday night at 8, on the WYXZ TV 39 Movie of the Week, Shaft is one bad mother- Shut your mouth! We're just talking about Shaft. Can you dig it?) EL: OK, Jak Hammer right now with a big clothesline takes down Little Yvette. Scoops him up, body slam. Hammer sends him for the ride, and smacks a hard elbow into the upper chest. This one has gone back and forth all match. Now Yvette with a forearm uppercut, but Hammer responds with a huge double sledge. Hammer picks him back up, forearm blow to the head, and now he sends him to the ropes- high cross body press by Little Yvette! He's got him- no! Too high, and the momentum carried him over- Jak Hammer on top now- he's got his feet on that second rope now! 1... 2... 3! He got him! ("Bad to the Bone" strikes up, and 12 years after Gino's death that song still pisses the Sportatorium crowd off.) DM: Your winner, Jak Hammer! EL: A travesty of justice, as Little Yvette, really taking the fight to his bigger, more experienced opponent, takes the loss. I'm sure you could see it from just about any angle in the arena, as Jak Hammer had his feet on that rope for added leverage, and between that and his 310 lb. body, there was no way Little Yvette could kick out. But Little Yvette certainly gave a good acquitting of himself, and I'm sure we'll be seeing much more of him in the coming weeks. Well, next up, we have the team of Michael Anderson and Byron McNultey, they call themselves "Marvelous" Michael and the "Pretty Boy", and I'm pretty sure those are names they came up with themselves, are going to take on Tip Smith's duo of Midnight Rampage, and the good thing is that Michael Anderson will be too busy with "The Real Deal" Healer's Havoc and BD Brown to join me here at the announcer's table. ("Sharp Dressed Man" plays and the afore-mentioned duo bounce to the ring, pausing to talk with the clearly-reluctant Eric Lane.) EL: Well, it looks as if "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey and "Marvelous" Michael Anderson are on their way over here. Hello gentlemen? BM: Yes, the feeling's mutual Mr. Russell. EL: La- MA: -nce. EL: Why do I even bother? BM: I don't know. Why? Wait. That's too much about you. We're honoring you with our presence to make it known that our opponent, uh, who are they again? MA: I dunno. Does it matter? BM: You're right, it doesn't. Well, whoever it is, wait. Do they have the belts? EL: No, the- MA: This is an outrage. Why are we facing whoever they are, if they don't have the belts? We are the real champions, and every beautiful lady in this grand audience and those watching at home will attest to that. [some boos start to come from the audience] BM: Ooh, Mikey, they're calling for our great dancing abilities. Must be those ladies who can't get enough of us. Can't blame them, seeing as after seeing us, they look at their husbands, and what do they see? MA: Some toothless, balding, overweight monster that couldn't even define "fantasy", let alone fulfill their wives' fantasies. BM: Well, now that we have given the ratings a jump that no one else could give them, we should go show the ladies what they want, but they cannot touch. MA: That's right Byron. And that includes that Mother Nature gal. Did you see how she was all over me last week Lance? EL: Where were you last week? MA: I was aiding you in your commentating Lance. But, to all those disgustingly overweight clowns out here, don't hate us because we're beautiful. (exit the two to strut in the ring as they await for the arrival of their opponents) EL: Well, after that display, let's go to the ring and David Myers. DM: This next contest is set for one fall with a fifteen-minute time limit. In the ring to my right, at a combined weight of 497 lb., "Marvelous" Michael Anderson and "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey! (The crowd is unimpressed) DM: And their opponents, led to the ring by Tip Smith, at a combined weight of 510 lb., here are Big Dig Brown and the Real Deal, Healer's Havoc- Midnight Rampage! Your referee is Jeff Howard. (Anderson: 5'11"-254 in red tights bearing his nickname; McNultey: 5'10"-243 in blue full-length tights with his nickname, too; Havoc: toothless and dog-collared, 6'6"-250;, Brown: 6'3"-250, dressed in black) EL: OK- we are underway, and it looks like the Pretty Boy and Big Dog in the ring first. Brown signals for a lockup, but the Pretty Boy moonwalks and adjusts his hair instead. (The crowd jeers and Anderson claps) EL: And the Big Dog mussing the Pretty Boy's hair! (The crowd laughs while Anderson calls for a disqualification) EL: McNultey is mortified, and the Big Dog now with a shot to the body! The Pretty Boy covers his stomach, and Brown goes for he face! Byron covers his face, and BD back downstairs! (BD feigns a shot to the face, and the Pretty Boy covers up instinctively, but BD stays his hand. As Byron uncovers his eyes to see what was happening, BD pops him in the nose again, and the crowd laughs again) EL: Brown now with an Irish whip- big back body drop! Legsweep faceslam now, and Healer's Havoc tags in. Brown holds McNultey open for Havoc to kick him one right in the ribs. Havoc now with a snap mare takedown, and he goes for an elbow drop. Got it. The cover- 1... Anderson breaks it with a kick in the back. Havoc up now, and he slugs Anderson! (big pop) EL: Havoc now scoops Anderson- slams him right on top of his partner. Havoc covers both men, and Brown on top for added pressure- and the referee is not going to allow this. Brown and McNultey head for their respective corners, and- wait- McNultey's the legal man! Anderson in there with Healer's Havoc now. Collar and elbow tie-up- Havoc with a kneelift. Now an arm bar by the Real Deal on "Marvelous" Michael. Don't forget, right after this, Twister defends the tag titles against Randy Sheridan, the ex-heavyweight champion, and Joshua Barnes, in what should be a thrilling contest, and, well, Max Robertson is declining his chance for the TV title, I hear that we're trying to arrange a substitute challenger so that we can get the promised TV title match. Havoc with the whip- he sets for the backdrop- Anderson saw it coming- ddt! (Crowd groans) EL: Anderson rolls him over- lackadaisical cover- 1... 2... kickout. Anderson with a handful of hair takes Havoc to the corner, tags in the Pretty Boy. McNultey with a waistlock- into a Northern Lights Suplex. Very impressive move- you know, with all of their hijinks, it's easy to forget that Anderson and McNultey are very skilled competitors- they just seem more interested in their appearance than their execution. McNultey back ou- frequent tags to keep the fresh man in. Anderson with an airplane spin, and he drops Havoc. Now Anderson tags back out again, and McNultey with a legsweep, and tags Anderson again! Anderson now with a swinging neckbreaker, tags McNultey, and heads for the Midnight Rampage corner! He pops BD Brown- Brown in the ring after him, and Jeff Howard is not going to have any of this. Howard cuts off the Dog- and McNultey holds Havoc back- Michael Anderson just puled something from his tights! Brass knuckles? I don't know what that is, but Michael Anderson, taking advantage of Jeff Howard and BD Brown, and just plastered Healer's Havoc with something. (The crowd shrieks) EL: Now Byron McNultey slips on a sleeper- Havoc is out cold. Michael Anderson calling for the ref- Jeff Howard checks Havoc's arm- drops once, twice- I don't believe these guys are getting away with this again. Yep- he's out, and Jeff Howard calls for the bell. While Tip Smith and BD Brown check on Healer's Havoc, let's get the official word. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, here are your winners, "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey and "Marvelous" Michael Anderson! (The crowd boos vociferously as "Sharp Dressed Man" plays) EL: When we get back, the CWAA Tag Team Titles are on the line, as Joshua Barnes and "Dreamer" Randy Sheradin- who lost in the semi-finals of the tag team title tourney when Twister interfered against their opponents- get the chance that was denied them before! (...it cuts through this old tin can, and still slices bread paper-thin!) EL: OK- no word on a possible opponent for Lt. Lawson yet, so we are going with the Feature Match now. Let's take a look at some footage of the challengers, taped earlier this week in Manchester after ACWF Rule Britannia! ********************************************************************** {Randy Sheridan and Joshua Barnes are in the locker room. Barnes looks positively giddy (if a nearly 300 pound man can look giddy), while Randy seems happy. The CWAA Title is wrapped around "The Dreamer's" waist.} Joshua Barnes: Tonight was fatant... fantastic. Randy won his tough "I Quit" match at Rule Britannia, and I finally got my hands on Max Robertson. Randy Sheradin: And did you ever get your hands on him! (The two high five) For those of you who missed it, the good guys went two for two in jolly old England last week. We are officially ON A ROLL! JB: Now we are facing Twister for the Tag Team Titles. Twister are both good friends, and they are a great team. But Randy's been helping me on tag team wrestling, and we might surprise some people. It'll be great to have a clean match without any outside problems. RS: I hear you there, big guy. Our teamwork may not be as refined as Paul and Travis, but we're two good wrestlers who trust each other, and will give you everything we've got. And like you said, I'm looking forward to wrestling a match without having to worry about someone coming at us from behind. JB: By the way, that was a good prank you pulled, but April Fool's Day was several days ago. RS: What do you mean? JB: Having "Pat Patterson" call me and offer me a contract to go up to Connecticut. I almost thought he was real, until he told me his idea to make me a "wrestling librarian". I hung up on his face.. RS: Josh... I didn't pull any prank. JB: Yeah, and that was real... {looks at Randy} That WAS real? {looks embarrassed} Oh. Well, I like it here in Texas and the CWAA. Besides, the idea was dumb anyway. RS: (Laughs as he pats Joshua on the back) Don't worry, Josh. You'll make the big time one of these days, I promise. But first, it's time you found out what it's like to win a belt. Here we come, Twister. We're looking forward to it. ********************************************************************** EL: Well, obviously that was taped before Max Robertson won the Heavyweight Title in Las Vegas this week. Let's go to- wait a second. (Suddenly, there is a commotion at the entranceway to the ringside area. The crowd starts booing, when they realize that Mark Sherrick is making his way down to the ring. He makes no motions to acknowledge the fans. He reaches ringside, climbs into the ring, and motions to David Myers to hand him the microphone. Myers complies, and Mark Sherrick begins to pace about the ring.) EL: I don't know what he's doing here. We haven't seen Mark Sherrick for several weeks now, and he's not on the program tonight- is he the substitute for the TV title match? Mark Sherrick: Well, well, it looks like I'm back. The CWAA was trying to back out of my long term contract, because of some of the things I pulled a while back, like the video tape, and the whole controversy over my father, and then the tag team match with the West Coast Connection. I fixed up my contract, and I am now under contract for a very long time, so be prepared to deal with me. (Crowd boos) MS: Now, speaking of my father, I may be a second generation wrestler under him, but I decided I don't need his memory anymore. I don't need his fame to carry me around, and I don't need to pretend to avenge his death. Actually, I never even liked my dad, so forget him. It seems to me, that there is another second generation wrestler somewhere in the CWAA. Well, if you are here right now, an even if you are not, why don't you sign a contract, and take me on?? I'll show you what a real wrestler can do, second generation or not. As far as I'm concerned, I never had a father who wrestled. You can call me second generation if you want, but you will soon have to call me the champ. I will continue my winning ways, and continue destroying all of you so called CWAA superstars. (Crowd boos some more) MS: Shut up, I'm not done talking. (The crowd boos louder) EL: Mark Sherrick, winning friends and influencing people with David Myers... MS: As for those two pathetic wrestlers who I was wrestling before my contractual problems, as far as I'm concerned, you still suck, and I could beat you any time, anywhere. (Sherrick drops the microphone on the mat, and walks out of the ring, as the crowd gives another round of Bronx cheers, Texas-style.) EL: Well, if he doesn't object, maybe we could get to the tag title match, our Special Feature match this week! DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest, set for one fall and a fifteen-minute time limit, is for the CWAA Tag Team Championship! (Pop) DM: First, the challengers- (Delirious pop as "Dream On" starts) DM: - at a combined weight of 463 lb., Joshua Barnes and former CWAA Heavyweight Champion, "Dreamer" Randy Sheradin! And their opponents- (More popping as the "Twister" theme plays) DM: -led by Mother Nature, at a combined weight of 559 lb. they are the CWAA Tag Team Champions, Paul Langford, Travis Convey- Twister! Your referee, Chris Herman. (Sheridan: 5'8"-170, decent build, brown eyes/black hair, long purple tights; Barnes: 6'5"-292, black hair, needs glasses, "IU" tattoo, long black tights; Conley: 6'8"-315, short blond hair-blue eyes, brown trunks, offensive lineman's build; Langford: 6'3"-244, washboard stomach, brown trunks, short brown hair, green eyes) EL: Ok, we are underway- and it is going to be Barnes and Conley, the big bulls on these teams, to start. A quick handshake between these gentle giants- at least outside of the ring. Collar and elbow tie-up, both men pushing hard, and Conley drives Barnes back to the ropes- Chris Herman calls for the break- Conley backs off- a clean break! I don't think we've seen one here before. Lockup again, and Barnes with a side headlock, look at the size of those arms. Conley now pushes Barnes to the ropes- Irish whip- shoulderblock by the Husker, and Barnes bounces back up to his feet- off the ropes- shoulderblock of his own sends big Travis to the mat. Travis Conley on his knees in the Twister corner now tags in Paul Langford. Joshua Barnes tags out Randy Sheridan- we've had the superheavyweights against one another, now we go to the lightweights. And a quick handshake between Langford and Sheridan. And I am joined at this time by Mother Nature. Mother Nature: Hello, Eric. EL: Hello. Langford and Sheridan lock up- hip toss by the Dreamer. I notice that we had the two bigger men against one another and now the two smaller men in at the same time. Is this part of your strategy? MN: Well, we thought that in Barnes and Conley, we would have a slight power advantage, but that with Conley and Sheridan in there, we would be find that Travis couldn't keep up with him move for move. Right now, Conley-Barnes looks like our best matchup, and Conley-Sheridan our worst, so the double tag worked to our advantage, actually. EL: Randy Sheridan with an arm bar now, and the Tornado with a reversal. Sheridan now backflips, drags Sheridan over in an arm drag, and back to an arm bar. (Pop for the neat little sequence) EL: Paul Langford up to his feet now- Irish whip, dropkick on the ex-champ. Now Langford to the chinlock. MN: We think it is important that - especially with Sheridan in the ring - that Paul be the one who dictates the pace of the action, that he keeps things going at a speed that works to our advantage. This means picking thighs up with Barnes in the ring and slowing them down on Randy Sheridan. EL: Paul nor releases the chinlock, and tags in Travis Conley. Travis with a scoop slam on the ex-champ. Do you think the loss to Max Robertson will have maybe provide an extra incentive for Sheridan and Barnes? MN: I would imagine not- if he's in there thinking about the Heavyweight Title, he can't focus on the task at hand. Overlooking Travis Conley, who just clotheslined him there now, is never a wise move. EL: He's a difficult man to overlook- that's for sure. Travis Conley now picks Sheridan back up- and the Dreamer hooks him in a small package! 1... 2... kickout! Both men back up now, and Travis Conley with a hip toss- blocked! Sheridan flips over behind Conley- dropkick from behind! Now Sheradin tags in Joshua Barnes. And Joshua Barnes in there with a full nelson now. MN: Joshua is a tough competitor all right, but his execution is not always that crisp, which I think takes a lot out of the holds he does apply. That full nelson is really not applied as well as it could be, and he loses some of the affect by not locking his fingers together. This should not be a difficult move to counter. EL: I don't believe this. Do you see what I see? MN: What? EL: Back there, up the aisle, Michael Anderson and Byron McNultey. MN: They aren't going to sit over here again, are they? EL: Who knows what ever goes through their minds? Meanwhile, in the ring, Travis Conley has powered his way out of the full nelson, and he sends Barnes for the ride- double clotheslines! Each connected, and both men are down! Sheridan and Langford are both leaning over the top turnbuckles, shouting encouragement to their partners, yearning for a tag- and here come Anderson and McNultey down he aisle. MN: I've got to stop this! EL: Mother Nature running to warn Paul Langford- but Anderson and McNultey get their first! Barnes and Conley slowly getting up- Barnes reaching to the corner- they pulled Langford from the apron, and- oh, no! They just slammed his face right into that steel post, and blood is spewing everywhere! Mother Nature is calling to Travis Conley as Randy Sheridan runs across the ring- Joshua Barnes follows him. Langford is down face first on the arena floor as the Pretty Boy drops a knee in the back of his head! The referee has called for the bell, and Conley and Barnes have Anderson by the arms, pulling him off of Langford, and Randy Sheridan has just unloaded a dropkick on the Pretty Boy. A lowblow by Anderson on Barnes and he breaks free, and he and McNultey are off and running. This is just ridiculous. What a couple of - well, spoiled brats, I guess. They didn't get the title match they wanted, so they had to ruin it for everyone else. David Myers in the ring now, as Travis Conley and Mother Nature and the challengers attend to the Tornado. He must have hit that nose right on the post, as blood is streaming down it, and both eyes are blackened. I don't think he knows what hit him. Let's get the official decision. DM: Here are your winners, as a result of a disqualification, and still CWAA Tag Team Champions, Twister! (The Twister theme plays as the crowd applauds Langford while the rest walk him to the locker room, using David Myers handkerchief to staunch the vital flow.) EL: What a display of childish petulance and cowardice by Byron McNult- I don't even want to think about this. A clean, scientific match like that ruined, and- well, we're going to take a break. When we get back, we'll see, I don't know what we'll see- Max Robertson declined his TV title chance tonight, if you recall. We'll be right back. (...if you're a veteran, aged 55 or more, you can't be turned down, regardless of your health... and for the price of a cup of coffee a day, you, too, can sponsor a child and make Bonnie Franklin feel like she's provided a real public service...) EL: OK- we're going to talk to CWAA Television Champion, Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson. Unfortunately, this is likely to involve hearing from the Major, as well. {"When Johnny Comes Marching Home Again" starts blaring, and the CWAA TV Champion and the Major come down the aisle to a chorus of boos. For once, neither man pays anyone any attention. Lawson yanks the microphone from Dave Myers' hands, and rolls into the ring.} Daniel Lawson: Jak Hammer! No one attacks me from behind and lives to laugh about it! I am going to make you pay... {The Major grabs the microphone from Lawson.} Major Victor Mayhem: Lieutenant! That is enough! You are letting your emotions getting to you. DL: But... MVM: That does it! Lawson, give me fifty! Now! {Lawson, still looking angry, nods and starts a set of push-ups. While Lawson is getting a quick work-out in, the Major speaks.} MVM: Now, Hammer, you seem to think that all your muscles entitles you to some special treatment. You made several tactical errors. First, you got Lawson mad, and he is not known as "Lethal" Lawson because of his breath! Secondly, you dared to mock the greatest tactical mind of the second half of the twentieth century... ME!!! Thirdly... well, I don't remember what the third reason was, but it was just as important. Hammer, you will pay. Now, the Lieutenant wants to offer you that title shot to get his hands on you- just like you want. But we will not fall for that trap. So here is our counter-offensive. Jak Hammer, you and the chaplain will get into that ring. The last man standing will get the honor of facing Lt. Lawson for the CWAA Television Title. Hammer, if you want that TV Title shot so badly, you have to earn it. DIS-MISSED! EL: Well, there you hear it straight from the horse's a- mouth- horse's mouth. Next week, Lawson will offer the title shot to the winner of a Jak Hammer-Kore Wrench match, and- wait a second! (The crowd pops as the Lt. (who either does his push-ups REALLY quickly, or will finish later) and Major find their way out blocked by the Preacher. The Preacher backs them up into the ring again, and he grabs the house mike. KW: Lawson- I'm not finished with you. I- EL: Now hold on! (Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas walk to the ring area, and climb in under the bottom rope, and Wrench drops the mike) EL: Folks, we are out of time- next week, the AEWA World Champion, Heavy Metal is here, Lt. Lawson faces Jak Hammer or Kore Wrench, and we'll have an update on Paul Langford's condition! We'll see you next week! (The credits roll while Wrench, Lawson, Hammer and Thomas all start shoving one another around as the referees and security fill the arena.)
