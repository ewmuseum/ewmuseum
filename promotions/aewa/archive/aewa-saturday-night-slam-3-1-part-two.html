Saturday Night Slam (3/1, part two)

3 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * CONTINUED FROM PART ONE * * * * MW: Of course, Zack. It looks like Wild Thing is squaring off against Super-Blonde, this one should be good! ZD: I can't wait to see what happens if Wild Thing tries anything on Kismet again, but whatever happens, I'm SURE it'll be cool. MW: You'd think so, wouldn't you? Well.... here comes Wild Thing now! RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, accompanied by his manager, Bootknife Kelly, weighing in at 246 pounds, this is WIIIIILD THIIIIIIING! ("Hurt" by Nine Inch Nails begins playing loudly, as Wild Thing makes his way to ringside. Bootknife Kelly follows behind, in a red tuxedo. Wild Thing wears a black leather vest, and his chest is COVERED in scars.) BD: Wild Thing looks confident, but he also looks like a raving psychopath. I don't know how well I could trust this guy. MW: He's had his problems in the past, and still seems to have those same problems. Just take a look at his chest, you'd have to be crazy to do that to yourself. ZD: Wild Thing is a big, dumb, animal. Plain and simple. LB: YOU are a big, dumb, animal compared to Wild Thing. ZD: Sure, I'm just too much man for you, honey. LB: Give me a break. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 202 pounds and accompanied by his manager, Kismet, This.... Is.... Suuuuuuuuuuper-Bloooooooooonde! (Elastica's "Connection" BOOMS out over the loudspeaker, as the Blonde makes his entrance to a BIG crowd pop. Kismet is right behind him, looking slightly worried as she glances toward Wild Thing.) (DING, DING) ZD: Look at how Wild Thing is looking at Kismet. It's inevitable that he's gonna try to attack her. MW: We'll see, but right now.... WOAH! Super Blonde just NAILED Wild Thing with a dropkick while he was looking at Kismet! He's POUNDING away on Wild Thing! And now Blonde catches him with a hiptoss, and he's going up top! BD: The Blonde goes for a moonsault, and he hits it! Blonde crawls on top of Wild Thing and here's the count.... One... Two... Kickout!! LB: Still not enough to put Wild Thing away, and the Blonde is going right back up top! ZD: Wild Thing is up.... and he's.... DOWN! MW: Wild Thing just got nailed with a huge clothesline, and Kismet is really cheering her cousin on now. Blonde picks Wild Thing up, but Wild nails Blonde with a LOW blow! BD: That sure as hell changed the tides there! Wild Thing picks Blonde up onto his shoulder, POWERSLAM!! ZD: Buh-bye, Blondie! MW: Don't count him out just yet. Wild Thing picks Blonde up and he whips him to the ropes, and the Blonde is down! Bootknife Kelly just hit him in the back with a chair, but the ref didn't see it! LB: Oh no! Wild Thing has that crazed look in his eyes again-- I can't watch this! BD: Wild Thing has just climbed out of the ring, and the ref is seeing if Blonde is okay. I think he better turn his attention back to Wild Thing! MW: Wild Thing has Kismet trapped at ringside, and he just JUMPED on her! He's rubbing her up and down, and-- SICK! This is totally inappropriate! Someone get security out here.... BD: WAIT A SECOND! Deathwatch has just come from out of the audience, and he's PULLED Wild Thing off of her! ZD: See? Deathwatch isn't such a bad guy.... he's just misunderstood, that's all. (grins) LB: I don't believe that for a second. BD: Deathwatch has Wild Thing by the hair, and he's giving him an EXTREMELY cold stare! Deathwatch has Wild Thing by the neck.... CHOKESLAM!! Wild Thing is out, and the referee still isn't paying any attention to this! MW: He is now! Unfortunately, he didn't see any of what happened. Deathwatch is now looking at Kismet, who is down on the ground, and she's in tears at what Wild Thing attempted to do. Deathwatch is extending his hand to her, and he helps her up! This doesn't look good! ZD: What're you worried about? You think he's actually going to HURT her? Deathwatch isn't that kind of guy, man. LB: He's telling the truth.... Deathwatch just kissed Kismet on the back of the hand, and now he's leaving again. ZD: See? I tried to tell ya. BD: Kismet actually looks happy, I think she's.... I think she's.... this is hard for me to say, but I think she's in LOVE! LB: And the referee has just counted Wild Thing out! (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner, as the result of a countout, SUUUPEEERRRRR-BLOOONNNDDDEEE!! (Elastica's "Connection" begins to play, as Super-Blonde and Kismet leave ringside. We notice that Kismet has an enormously happy look on her face.) LB: I think you're right, Big Daddy. I think she's in love with.... with.... Deathwatch! How could anyone love that PSYCHO? ZD: He isn't psycho, he's just.... casual. BD: Casual my ass! ZD: Well, I'd love to stay here and argue, but I have prior engagements. See ya later, losers. (Derringer stands up and removes his headset, and he leaves the announcer's booth.) MW: Well, I'm glad he's finally gone. It's now time for our next match, as Penance steps into the ring against Heavy Metal. This is going to be an VERY interesting match, folks. BD: As everyone knows by now, Penance broke away from the ICU at Kickoff '97, and joined his NEW group of thugs-- the Apocalypse. LB: And Heavy Metal wants to settle the score tonight! There's really only one problem with that-- he outweighs Penance by over 200 pounds! He's going to SQUASH the little guy. MW: That remains to be seen. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is set for one fall! Introducing first, coming down the aisle, at 223 pounds, he is a former Light Heavyweight champion, here is Penance! (Skid Row's "My Enemy" begins to play loudly over the PA, and Penance walks down the aisle. He is dressed totally in black, with a VERY nasty expression on his face. Oddly enough, some members of the audience are actually cheering him.) MW: Penance sure looks ready for action tonight as he makes his way to the ring. I don't see how he has much of a chance, but anything's possible. BD: I don't know who to root for here. I love both of these guys. MW: Just try to be impartial. BD: What's that mean? MW: (rolls his eyes) Never mind. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 450 pounds, he is a former AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, here is Heavy Metal! (Metallica's "Battery" blares throughout the arena as the MASSIVE Heavy Metal runs to the ring, to a HUGE downpour of boos. He is carrying a baseball bat, but leaves it on a table at ringside. He looks VERY angry, and immediately charges for Penance.) MW: These two guys aren't gonna hold anything back here tonight! LB: They sure aren't! Metal and Penance are laying into each other with those big rights and lefts, but Metal seems to be winning! BD: Of COURSE he's winning, you idiot. He's 450 pounds! MW: Metal now whips Penance into the ropes, and delivers a HUGE clothesline to the new Apocalypse member! BD: I guess Metal hasn't forgotten that attack at Kickoff '97, when Penance and his new friends ambushed the ICU. MW: It doesn't look like that's the case. Metal now picks up Penance, and slams him down! You can see Penance holding his back from the force of that one. LB: Metal's going outside the ropes now. BD: What's he doing? MW: Well, he's going to the floor, and he's pulling Penance out with him! Metal and Penance are now brawling on the ringside floor! Again, though, Metal's getting the better of Penance. BD: Metal looks like he's up to something. He's pointing at the ringsteps! He whips Penance in, but Penance reverses it, and Metal's back hits HARD against those steel steps! LB: That'll take the big man down. MW: Penance now seemingly in control of the match. He rolls Metal back into the ring, and he's going to the top rope. Penance comes off, AND HITS METAL WITH A FLYING CLOTHESLINE! LB: Heavy Metal didn't fall down, though. MW: Penance sees that, and now he's coming off of the ropes, and catches Metal with a dropkick! BD: But the big man's still not down! This guy can't be stopped! MW: And now Penance is coming off the ropes again, but he gets caught with a sidewalk slam! That was a vicious move by Metal, folks. BD: This is great action! It's just too bad it's between these two classy competitors and not two losers like Ben Slocum and Justin Trouble. MW: Classy competitors? Okay, whatever you say. Metal now whips Penance into the ropes, and locks in a sleeper! LB: With the weight of Metal on his shoulders, there's no way Penance can get out of-- well, I guess he could do THAT! MW: Fans, we don't condone that low blow by Penance. BD: Hey, speak for yourself! Whatever you've gotta do to get the job done, right? MW: Not always, Big Daddy. Metal now seems to be hunched over in pain, and Penance goes over to him. He's hooking him up for a DDT, but Metal somehow manages to backdrop out of it! BD: This is great-- the big man's still got enough power to put on a show for his fans. MW: Yeah, all three of them. BD: Hey, I'll have you know Heavy Metal has lots of fans. MW: Show me some. BD: (looks around the audience) Well.... (can't see any fans in favor of Metal) ....I'm sure they're here somewhere. MW: Just as I thought. Now Metal kicks Penance in the midsection, with PLENTY of force. LB: Penance is doubled over.... MW: And Metal's giving the signal for the Megadeth Power Bomb! Turn out the lights, folks, the party's over. BD: If he can execute this maneuver, there won't be ANYTHING left of Penance. Actually, it could be enough to KILL him! MW: Metal lifts Penance up, but Penance reverses it into a sunset flip! He can't seem to pull Metal over, though! Metal drops down, but Penance is gone! METAL FALLS HARD RIGHT ON HIS BUTT! BD: Don't worry, he has a lot of padding. MW: Obviously. Penance now waits for him to get up.... Metal is back to his feet, and Penance flies off the ropes with a clothesline that sends BOTH men over the top rope to the ringside floor! LB: That had to hurt both of 'em, folks. MW: Luckily for Penance, Heavy Metal didn't land on top of him. Penance now gets up, and so does Metal. BD: But what's Metal reaching for? LB: Forget about that, I want to know what PENANCE is reaching for! He's going for something.... BD: Heavy Metal's got that BASEBALL BAT! LB: And Penance has grabbed a CHAIR! MW: Both men seem to have the same idea, and they both come right each other, and NAIL the other with their foreign object! And the referee saw it! (DING, DING) LB: Apparently so. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the referee has decided that both men are disqualified! BD: What kind of B.S. ruling is that? MW: Are you kidding me, Big Daddy? What would you do if both of the wrestlers had weapons in a-- HOLD ON! Both men are back up and they're beating each other up! LB: They're fighting down the aisleway, and the officials can't do anything about it! MW: And now they're up on the ramp, and they're fighting all the way into the dressing rooms! What a wild match! BD: Yeah, I loved it. What's next? MW: Hank Thompson is about to interview Traci, the woman who caused Justin Trouble to lose the North American title. Given her current situation, this should be VERY interesting. (The camera cuts to Hank Thompson and Traci, standing in the ring. Hank picks up the microphone, and begins the interview.) Thompson: Alright, folks. I'm standing here with the former manager of Justin Trouble, the always beautiful, TRACI! Traci: Thanks, Hank. But let's skip the small talk, 'cause I'm here for one reason and one reason only. Thompson: Yes, and we're all curious to hear your explanation for showing up at Trouble's matches and interviews, not to mention your actions at Kickoff that lead to him losing the North American title. So if I maybe so bold, what is it? Traci: I'll be more than glad to tell you, but first.... (The camera cuts to the runway, where Justin Trouble has appeared and is gazing at Traci in confusion. Security surrounds Trouble to try to usher him out of the arena.) Traci: Let him through, let him through! This is something I want to tell him face to face! (Trouble makes his way into the ring, and takes the microphone from Hank. He begins to speak.) Trouble: Well, first off, I'd like to ask, what the hell is your problem? You show up at my matches and interviews, and now you've cost me the North American title AND my career here in the AEWA. You owe me some explanations, so start talking. Traci: Hold on just a second, Justin. You know and I know that I didn't cost you the title. Deathwatch beat you fair and square. The only person you have to blame is yourself. Under my guidance you could've been the World Heavyweight Champion right now. (fans start to boo) And I'm sure you miss my.... well.... companionship. (Traci runs her finger down Trouble's chest, then gets close and kisses him. Trouble pushes her away.) Trouble: Not that I don't appreciate it when a beautiful women gets physical, but what in the hell are you talking about? I mean, you have slapped me, punched me, and now you want to kiss me?! Traci: Justin, you've got to understand.... (At about this time, there is a disturbance in the audience. A man is making his way through the crowd and has gotten over the guardrail. As the camera focuses closer, the man's size is almost unreal. The man grabs a chair and gets into the ring, while Trouble is distracted by Traci. The man CRACKS the chair over the back of Trouble's head, knocking him out cold. The man grabs the microphone.) Man: I can't believe they let wimps like him into this organization. I mean, isn't pro-wrestling a contact sport? Look at this guy, napping on the job! Just shows how bad our country's work ethic is. What a loser! (the crowd is booing loudly now) That's why I've decided to come into this federation. To show what kick-ass is REALLY all about. And I've got the best manager money can buy. (Traci locks arms with the man) And the most beautiful! (Traci takes the microphone) Traci: Ah, you're so sweet! I guess I should introduce you morons to the next AEWA World Heavyweight champion, MAXX TROUBLE! Thompson: Trouble?! You mean.... they're related? Man: That's right, I'm junior's big-- well, much bigger-- brother! Being seven foot one, 356 pounds has it's advantages. For instance, I can beat on morons like you all day long without repercussions. (Justin starts to get up....) Traci: Honey, he's up again. Man: Not for long. (Maxx picks Justin up and executes the Wake-Up Call, a modified inverted crucifix, onto the steel chair.) Man: That was easy enough. (Maxx suddenly looks angry, as he picks up the microphone) Justin! You had your shot at greatness, but you screwed it up, punk! Well, now I'm here to pick up the pieces! Just like when we were kids! That was YOUR wake-call, little brother. Now you're in MY world-- it's called reality! And the reality is that I'm going to be the next AEWA World champion. Whether you like it or you don't, you'd better learn to live with it, because that's the way it is! Traci: Let's get outta here, baby. (The huge Maxx lowers the ropes for Traci, and they both leave down the runway. Thompson signals for help, and security brings out a gurney. Justin is lowered out of the ring on the gurney, and rushed out of the arena. The crowd chants, "We Want Justin! We Want Justin!" as the camera shifts back to Big Daddy Mike Hurt, Bertha, and Matt Wilson.) MW: I'm absolutely appalled at what just happened. I really can't find any words to describe this situation.... BD: Ya know, I always said that no one likes Justin Trouble, but now even his own BROTHER has attacked him! This is hilarious, folks. MW: No, it's really not very funny, Big Daddy. Justin is being taken to the local hospital, and I hope he's okay. LB: That "Maxx Trouble" character looked pretty tough, didn't he? I can't wat to see HIS debut! BD: Hey, here's an idea-- maybe he'll wrestle his brother at AEWA Extreme: The Pain Game. That would make a VERY interesting match, if you ask me. MW: I didn't ask you, though. Anyway, it's time to move on. The next match features the newest member of the ICU, Damian Steele, as he goes against Red Dog, one of the Angels of Death. We haven't heard much from the Angels lately. BD: I'm sure they're just biding their time, waiting for the right moment to strike. LB: It's kind of depressing, though. MW: What do you mean, Bertha? LB: Why are the ICU and the Angels of Death fighting each other, when they could be joining forces and destroying the Knights of Deliverance? I mean, couldn't you see Steele and Red Dog teaming up against Gryphon and Caretaker? Wouldn't that be great? MW: I don't think they're interested in working together. Let's get to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following bout is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, from Los Angeles, California, and weighing in at 265 pounds, Damian "The Preeeeeedator" Steeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeleeeee! (Tool's "Swamp Song" plays loudly over the PA as Steele makes his entrance to a HUGE chorus of boos from the crowd. He is wearing an ICU jacket, and several members of the audience throw their drinks at him-- but they miss.) BD: Look at the smile on his face. He's happy to be here! MW: Well, he tried to fool everyone into thinking he was a fan favorite, but he showed his true colors at Kickoff '97. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 245 pounds, representing the Angels of Death, here is RED DOG! (Red Dog walks to the ring in an "Angels of Death" T-shirt. The fans boo him loudly, but he doesn't seem to care. He is foaming at the mouth, as always, and carries his big bone with him.) MW: Hold on.... Damian Steele has grabbed the microphone from the ring announcer... DAMIAN STEELE: On behalf of the entire ICU, I just want to thank all of the fans for their support. You fans mean SO MUCH to all of us. (Steele bursts out laughing hysterically). LB: Is Steele a kidder, or what? This guy should have been a comedian. MW: Apparently Red Dog doesn't think so. He's jumping Steele from behind! BD: Bad move on the Predator's part. You don't turn your back on Red Dog. MW: Right now, Red Dog is hitting Steele's head with a series of axe handles. Steele's on one knee now. Red Dog bounces off the ropes, and drop kicks Steele! He picks up Steele now, but Steele rolls him up in a small package! One.. Two.. Kickout! LB: That almost got a surprise victory for Steele. Red Dog looks absolutely furious! MW: And he's taking it out on Steele, with a series of fists. Steele's on the mat now, and Red Dog is choking him, right in front of the referee. The ref is making a five count, but Red Dog lets up on four. Lets up, and immediately chokes him again for a four count before letting go. This time, Steele's rolling out of the ring. BD: Smart move. He needs a break from Red Dog's assault. LB: But Red Dog is following him outside the ring. Fortunately for Steele, he sees Red Dog, and circles the ring before re-entering. MW: Red Dog tries to re-enter the ring, but Steele catches him with a boot to the face. Steele picks up Red Dog.... belly-to-back suplex! Red Dog hit his head hard on that one. Steele picks up Red Dog again, and they lock up. Red Dog gets the advantage, and whips the Predator into the turnbuckle. He charges.... and misses! Steele moves out of the way, and Red Dog hits the turnbuckle hard. BD: Steele's showing why he calls himself the Predator now.... look at him as he slams Red Dog's head against the turnbuckle, over and over. MW: He whips him to the opposite turnbuckle.... Red Dog reverses the whip and sends Steele into the corner-- AND CATCHES STEELE WITH A CLOTHESLINE! LB: Damian Steele almost flipped over from that clothesline. He's in serious trouble. MW: Red Dog is stomping away at Steele. He makes a cover... One... Two... Kickout! BD: Steele kicked out, but he's in trouble. MW: And now Red Dog is climbing the turnbuckle for an aerial assault. He's not too comfortable up there, though. LB: He's taking too long. Steele's up, and he nails Red Dog with a fist to the midsection! MW: Now Steele climbs up.... and he's hitting Red Dog with a few more fists. He lifts him up for a Superplex, and he GOT HIM!! Here's a cover.... One... Two... Foot on the ropes! BD: Lucky for Red Dog that he so close to the ropes. He's in a lot of pain right now, and Steele is the type of man to take advantage of that. MW: You're right-- look at Red Dog hold his back. Damian Steele picks him up for a vertical suplex.... he's holding him in the air.... and drops him to the mat! Another cover... One... Two... Kickout! LB: Don't ask me how Red Dog kicked out of that one-- but he's still in real trouble. MW: Steele is now dropping elbow after elbow right on the small of the back, and Red Dog is defenseless against the Predator's attack. Steele picks him up for another suplex.... BD: But Red Dog small packages him! One... Two... Kickout! LB: Red Dog returns the favor from earlier by almost pulling out a victory. BD: Too bad all it did was make Steele mad. Look at the anger in his eyes! MW: Steele goes back to elbowing the small of Red Dog's back-- I guess you go with what works-- and now he picks him up for a powerbomb! GOT HIM, right on the back. But instead of making a cover, he pulls him up again. BD: That's a mistake on his part. You should go for the win whenever you get a chance. MW: We agree there, Big Daddy. He picks him up for another powerbomb.... but this time Red Dog is struggling.... AND RED DOG BACKDROPS DAMIAN STEELE! BD: You see? He should have covered him. MW: Red Dog has a surge of energy, as the crowd actually seems to be cheering for him. I guess they consider Red Dog to be the lesser of two evils. Red Dog off the ropes, and clotheslines The Predator! A cover... One... Two... Kickout! LB: Not yet, Red Dog. MW: Red Dog runs off the ropes again, perhaps for another clothesline.... but Damian Steele catches him! He pivots, and powerslams Red Dog into the mat! A quick cover! One... Two... Three!! (DING, DING) BD: Red Dog kicks out, but not in time! MW: Too many blows on the back for Red Dog. The powerslam put him away. LB: And listen to the crowd booing Steele as the referee raises his hand. They really hate this guy. MW: Yeah, but look at him.... he's still acting as if he were the most popular man in the AEWA. BD: In my mind, he might be! MW: I agree, Big Daddy. In your mind, and ONLY in your mind, is Damian Steele popular. LB: As you would say-- that's questionable. MW: We're quickly running out of time, so let's hurry up and wrap this program up. Be sure to tune into Wednesday Night Rumble, when "Crimson" Joe Reed will be defending his World Heavyweight belt against Primal Fear! BD: That's going to be a VERY boring match, but don't forget about the National tag team title defense, as the Assassins square off against Heaven & Hell! MW: Well, that about does it here tonight, so for "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt and the Lovely Bertha, I'm "Maniac" Matt Wilson, saying so long everybody!
