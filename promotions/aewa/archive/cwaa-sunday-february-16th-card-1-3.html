Sunday February 16th Card (1/3)

16 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Fade to a completely blank white wall. In front of that wall is what appears to be an altar. perched upon the altar is a brass urn. leaning up against the altar is a badly dented, rusted, discolored metal folding chair. suddenly a large man wearing black sweat pants and a silver T-shirt with the words "Never Forget" emblazoned on the front. He kneels down in front of the altar, and begins speaking, almost praying.) Man: Father, I have entered the CWAA because they wrestle in the arena that you were unjustly killed in. I promise you that next week when I have my debut match I will start the way down the road to the CWAA title, and when I gain it, defend it by any means necessary, just like you did. I will become the champion, and your death will be avenged in my eyes, and my name will never be forgotten, and will go down in the history of the CWAA. (the man stands up, and turns around) Yeah, that's right, CWAA, I will debut this week. The Sportatorium holds special memories for me, and they aren't good ones. They have deadened me to violence, and I will win matches by any necessary means. I will destroy all of you, do whatever it takes, and become then CWAA World Champion. (video starts to get some interference) And you will know my name is.. (picture and sound fade out for two seconds)...When I lay my vengeance of my past upon you, the CWAA. (video fades out again. this time it stays off.) ******************************************* Eric Lane: Hello, everyone, and welcome to another exciting edition of Championship Wrestling Association of America. I am your host, Eric Lane, and, well, normally we try to open the program with a clip of the previous week's action, but somehow something got switched. That was the same man whom we saw several weeks ago rummaging through the supply room looking for a dented chair, I believe, and who said something about his father having died from injuries suffered in the ring. I know of only one such wrestler, Mike DiBiase, and I doubt very much that this is his son. I'll have more to say on this later, but first we have complete brackets for the CWAA Heavyweight Title tournament, with four matches this week and four next week to complete the first round. Paul "Tornado" Langford "The Professional" Max Robertson Joshua Barnes "Hotshot" Gary Jackson Healer's Havoc Eddie Intellecta BD "Big Dog" Brown Mark Sherrick "The Dreamer" Randy Sheradin Marc Thomas "Hardcore" Joey Haven "The Preacher" Kore Wrench Stan "The Powerhouse" Stevens Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson Travis Conley Jak Hammer EL: Here tonight, in addition to the first round tournament action, the feud between "Hardcore" Joey Haven and Jak Hammer revs it up one notch as we head to a no-disqualification, no-countout match tonight. Joey Haven is on his way here know to share with me his thoughts on the situation that has developed between he and Jak Hammer in recent weeks. Come on in here, Joey. Joey, can I get a word with you before you go get ready for your match with Jak Hammer? Haven: Go ahead, Eric. EL: Jak Hammer has done well against you, what makes you think he won't beat you tonight? JH: Its plain and simple Eric. I mean, he doesn't have these wondeful fans in Texas supporting him. (Fans who react as one would expect when so mentioned.) He doesn't have them shouting his name and backing him up, I do. I may lead a rebel life, but these fans know whats good for them. They know I can talk the talk and walk the walk. They know, that what I say I am going to do, I will do. EL: So, tonight, you going to be "Hardcore"? JH: Just wait and see...ha ha ha....now this is Hardcore!!! [Haven walks off] EL: We'll see about that later tonight, but first, Paul "the Tornado" Langford and Max "The Professional" Robertson will open up the first round of the CWAA Heavyweight Title Tournament, and you can bet that representatives from the AEWA will be here observing. (The theme from the major motion picture "Twister" plays, and Paul Langford and Mother Nature arrive on the scene to a nice pop. As always, the washboard-stomached Tornado has adorned his 6'3"-244 frame in brown trunks with a Twister logo on the part he sits on.) EL: A very popular individual here in the world-famous Sportatorium, Paul Langford, whose partner Travis Conley is in the second half of the bracket, so it would take quite an extraordinary turn of events to see the two partners fight it out for the Heavyweight Title. (The Imperial March is heard in Darth Vader's big scenes plays, and the crowd, for the first time, has a decent-sized face pop for the 5'11"-200 pound Max Robertson. The bald-by-choice Professional is, as always, clad in red and black tights.) EL: There's the bell, and a friendly handshake between the two men, and they're off. Collar and elbow tie up- there's Max with a drop toe hold and now he cinches on a hammerlock on Langford. Langford now wriggles out and has a headlock on Robertson on the mat. Robertson pulls him back... 1... 2.. Langford rolls forward again and back into the headlock. Robertson escapes with a head scissors, but Langford right back out again, and hooks on a hammer lock, with Robertson lying on the mat. Langford stands him up, still holding on that hammerlock, now into a backslide- 1... 2... kickout by Robertson. Both men back to their feet. Interesting coincidence in that the next few matches will see both halves of Double Jeopardy and Robertson's new friend Joshua Barnes in action- we could see Robertson and Barnes in a- Langford with a hip toss, blocked by Robertson, and now Robertson with a snap mare into a chinlock- we could see Robertson and Barnes in a rematch from just a few weeks ago, where you may remember seeing Robertson locking both Barnes and then Sheridan in that double chickenwing submission hold of his. Or maybe Paul Langford will get past the Professional on his way to meeting both Gary Jackson and Eddie Intellecta in the next two rounds. Robertson now with an elbow smash on the top of Langford's head, and he pulls the Tornado back up to his feet- Irish whip, dropkick by Max Robertson. Now an elbow drop, and Robertson heads for the corner, he's going out on to the ring apron. Where is he going now? Wait- he uses the top rope to catapult himself in again- slingshot somersault splash... hooks the leg... 1... 2... Langford kicks out! Robertson now stomps away at Langford's left knee. Now he rolls him over onto his stomach, and pulls back on that leg- a half Boston crab. We're going to have to take a break, but when we come back, we'll have the conclusion to this exciting contest- stay tuned. (WYXZ Channel 39 plugs it's weeknight lineup of syndicated programs. Tuesday night at 10:30, 8 is enough for love, and at 11:30 I'm living on the air in Cincinnati- Cincinnati, WKRP.) EL: We're back, and Max Robertson has followed the half-Boston with some more stomps on the lower leg, and now he's trying to cinch on a figure four-leglock on the Tornado. He's got it on! Paul Langford in very obvious pain, as the referee checks for a submission. This one has been all Max Robertson from the beginning. Langford slumps back onto the mat... 1... 2... he gets his shoulders back up. You can see the veins standing out in Langford's forehead and neck as he strains against the pressure. He slumps back again... 1... 2... he gets up just in time. He's trying to summon the strength to roll it over now, and to put the pressure back onto Robertson- Robertson fights it- he's got him turned halfway- he's got it rolled over, and the Professional is in agony! He's pounding at the mat with both fists as the referee checks for a submission. Now he's reaching for the bottom rope- he can't quite make it- he stretches- just out of reach... Now he's trying to drag himself and Langford over- he's got it- he's got the bottom rope, and the referee calls for the break. He'll have to help them get untangled, now. Both men took an awful lot of damage in that last sequence, and Langford is definitely hobbled. Robertson now with a European uppercut, and Langford falls back into the corner. Robertson with a chop across the chest- and Langford repays in kind! Robertson with another uppercut- Langford with a kick in the midsection! Langford now with the cross corner whip, and as Robertson bounces out of the corner- Langford with the clothesline! Robertson is flattened- Langford picks him up- Irish whip. He catches the Professional in an airplane spin- this is how he sets up his Tornado Drop! He starts spin- no- he just dropped to one knee- his leg was too badly hurt by that figure four to bear both his and Robertson's weight! Robertson slips down, big back suplex. Robertson back on his feet, and he waits as Langford staggers back up, now Robertson looks like he's going for that double chickenwing- he's got the Tornado locked in it, and he leans back, taking the Tornado off of the mat. Nowhere for Langford to go, the ref checks for a submission. Did he get it? There's the bell, and it looks like Langford has submitted, and Max Robertson is on his way to the second round of the tournament. Nice display of sportsmanship as Robertson shakes Langford's hand, and later tonight we'll see who the Professional will face in the second round- Joshua Barnes or Gary Jackson. {Joshua Barnes walks down the aisle as several of the fans stick out their hands to greet the wrestler. Gingerly, he slaps several of them as he heads for the ring. He starts to climb into the ring, but Eric Lane calls him over. With a slight grimace, he walks over towards Eric. But he goes out of his way to avoid touching the table in any way.} EL: Joshua, you're about ready for the first round in the tournament, and you're facing one-half of Double Jeopardy, "Hotshot" Gary Jackson. Are you ready for the match? JB: Well... {wipes the sweat from his brow} I've been watching Double Jeopardy in the ring, and he's very good. But... {breathes deeply} I'm hoping that because I'm a singles wrestler and you're... I mean he's a tag team wrestler, that I'll have an advantage. EL: Are you worried about the rest of Double Jeopardy interfering? Their past matches have indicated that they would. JB: I just have to prepare for the worst, and hope that the referee catches them. I can't worry about them outside of the ring, because I have enough problems inside with Jackson. EL: What about Max Robertson? He's seemed to have formed an alliance with Randy Sheridan. JB: Well, Randy... {At this point, a woman in her twenties climbs over the guardrail, goes up to Joshua, and gives him a full kiss on the lips. Then, before anyone can react, she jumps back over to her seat, and starts giggling with her girlfriends.} EL: Please, Madame, no crossing the guardrail! I know you're a great fan, but we can't have fans over here, it's too dangerous! Josh, I'm sorry about this... {Joshua's face has turned a bright shade of red, he is looking down at the ground, his hands are twitching rapidly.} JB: I...uh...thank...I'vegotamatchbye. {He rolls into the ring, and looks at the ground.} EL: Well, a very nervous Joshua Barnes in there as he gets ready to face the "Hotshot". ("Sexual Healing" starts to play as Gary Jackson struts to the ring. He manages to get halfway there before remembering that his ankle is still hurting and therefore needs to be favored. He stands 5'11"-250, and a slight beer belly under a greasy white t-shirt hangs over his blue jeans.) EL: A working-class match up as the Brownsburg, IN bouncer and the Houston, TX mechanic square off. And keep an eye on Henry King and Eddie Intellecta out there. There's the bell, and we're underway. Jackson with a double sledge on Barnes has he removes his jacket, and Barnes is knocked into the corner. Jackson with a kick in the midsection, and now a punch, and Barnes' glasses are knocked to the floor. That's got him riled up, and the ex-bouncer spins Jackson around in the corner and uncorks right after right. Jackson shields his face, so Barnes goes downstairs and pummels that beer belly. Jackson covers his midsection, and Barnes goes back to the face! Now Barnes with a hiptoss, and Jackson scrambles to get out of the ring, but Barnes grabs him by the belt loop on his jeans, and swings him back to mid ring. Barnes sets in a three-point stance in the corner- charges and tackles Jackson for a loss. Back down again in the opposite corner, and another big tackle by Barnes, and the crowd is really enjoying this as "Hotshot" Gary Jackson is getting a severe case of comeuppance. Barnes now locks on a full nelson, and he appears to have his fingers locked, so this is going to be a very difficult situation for Jackson, who does not have the power to just break out of this kind of hold. Jackson is trying to shake his way free but to no avail, as the powerful Indiana youngster just applies more and more pressure. Wait a second- there's Eddie Intellecta up on the ring apron- I said to keep an eye on him. Now it's Barnes releasing the hold to go after Intellecta- a poor decision, I fear. Intellecta cocks a fist and fires away but Barnes ducks, and comes back with a shot of his own that knocks Intellecta to the floor, and the crowd erupts! Here come Randy Sheradin and Max Robertson to even things up, but Joshua Barnes was too concerned with Intellecta and not enough with Jackson, who just drove a knee into Barnes' back. Now Jackson rakes Barnes' face along the top rope, and traps him in a side headlock. Intellecta is hiding behind Henry King, hurling taunts at the Dreamer and the Professional. In the meantime, Gary Jackson drives Barnes' face into the mat with a bulldog. He rolls him over and bounces off the ropes- frog splash... 1... 2... kickout! Jackson now with an armbar on the mat, trying perhaps to neutralize Barnes' superior upper-body strength. Barnes slowly getting up- he's on one knee now as Jackson continues to apply the pressure. Randy Sheradin and Max Robertson pounding on the ring apron in time as the crowd picks up the chant. (The chant being "Go, Josh, go!" Those Dallas marks!) Barnes up on both feet now, and powering his way around- he's reversed the arm bar, and is now applying the pressure to Jackson! He's backing Jackson into the corner, cross-corner whip, and Barnes with a big clothesline as Jackson comes off the ropes. Intellecta up on the apron again, and Sheradin follows him- this will grab the referees attention as the two begin shoving. Henry King is watching with an ominous look on his face, and Barnes again drops his opponent to get involved with goings-on on the apron! Gary Jackson now removing that ankle brace as everyone else tussles on the apron! He's got it off- here comes Max Robertson! Jackson is about to slam Barnes with it, but Max Robertson wrenches it out of his hands! Robertson swings it at Jackson, who ducks and runs across the ring! Jackson ducks another blow, and runs back! The referee turns around as Jackson brushes him, and he sees Robertson raise that ankle brace- he swings it again- a glancing blow on Gary Jackson! The referee is signaling for the bell, as Eddie Intellecta is hoofing it out of here, with the Dreamer not far behind him. Barnes is trying to get that brace from Robertson, as Jackson stumbles through the ropes and races away with Henry King. The referee is signaling for disqualification, and, from the safe distance of 40 feet or so, Hotshot Gary Jackson raises his arms triumphantly. Barnes can't believe what is happening, Robertson looks dejected as he tosses the brace to the floor, kicking the bottom rope. So it'll be Max Robertson and Gary Jackson in the second round. When we come back, Eddie Intellecta- someone is going to have get him to stop running from Randy Sheradin long enough to get back in the ring- takes on Healer's Havoc.
