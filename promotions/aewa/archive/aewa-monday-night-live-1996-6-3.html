(WE WILL WE WILL ROCK YOU!!!!(is playing) and Skim Hoss appears on the screen, he's shaking his head back and forth wildly and is jamming to the tune) HOSS: Hi fans and welcome to Monday Night live...We have a hell of a show in store for you tonight...There has been so much going on in the AEWA I can hardly keep up with all of it....The tag team situation here is totally out of control...With the AEWA Tag Team title Vacant right now everybody wants there shot at winning it...I have a very special treat for you tonight but before I get to it let me bring you a couple of taped interviews from some of our tag teams... ( We see the Powers arriving at the airport. They see the camera and stop. ) BD : WE"RE BACK. Yes, we're back from our Europeen Tour and ready to kick ass. We're happy to see so many new teams to destroy. Tank, who do you think we should start with now that we're back. RT : Well BD, I think we should start with the Judicial Powers. BD : Why start with them. RT : Two reason, First, they remind me of what the LAW was like in my native country before the fall of the curtain. They are corrupt and should be taken care off. Secondly, only one team can truly be called POWERS of any kind, Judicial or otherwise. WE will teach them who the real POWERS are in the AEWA. BD : Well Judicial Powers, you're in for the fight of your life. Run while you still got the chance because once we get our hands on you, it's all over. NATO : AEWA, the Alliance is back and meaner than ever. Warning to the Tag Team Champs, we want those belts and we're coming for them. ( Screen fades to black ) HOSS: I'm so glad to see these guys back in action here in the AEWA they are definately one of the top tag teams here in the AEWA....Now it's time for a special treat as we join Matt Wilson on tape from last night with a big 16 man elimination tag match...Fans you are gonna love this... MW: We're going to show you now a match taped last night, a 16-man elimination match. But first, a little background. A few weeks ago, then-AEWA National Tag Team Champions Brian and Kelly Maloney were attacked in the locker room by the team of Submission, then, moments later, lost a brutally one-sided steel cage match to Mayhem, Inc. During that match, they were jumped by the Heavy Metal Crew, who had previously interfered in a Maloneys-Submission match. Among the wrestlers joining security to break up the HMC attack were the Icemen, who, as sson as the rest of security departed, launched an attack of their own. One night, four beatings. The Maloneys then challenged all four of those teams to meet them in a 16-man elimination match. They advertised for three teams to join them. The first to accept were Skull and Crossbones, one of the last teams you'd expect to see do this. But they have title ambitions of their own, and see the opportunity present in opposing Mayhem, Inc. The Graveyard Shift also accepted, as did the NATO Powers. What kind of chemistry will these teams have together, who have traded the World Title back and forth in a series of matches that ran from February to April? And how will the Graveyard Shift get along with Skull and Crossbones, two of the central teams in the current world tag title situation? On the other team, Submission has been grumbling about how the HMC cost them the NAtional belts by interfering and casuing Submission to suffer a dq loss. How will that team work together with such antagonism between teammates? Let's find out. *************************************** (The following match was staped the previous night. As it requires between 7 and 15 pins/submissions/count-outs or dqs, the tape will jump ahead several minutes at a time once we get past the opening minutes.) Marc Thomas of the Icemen and the British Destroyer started for the teams, and Groovy Gonzales lent Thomas a hand. Marc Thomas dominated the opening minutes, picking up two counts following a frankensteiner, moonsault, a small package, and a splash. The British Destroyer reversed a hiptoss into a backslide for a near fall of his own, but Thomas' offense was too much for the BD, and Thomas connected on a belly to back superplex which took (for the time being) away the face team's advantage of a raucous, supportive crowd. In the near silent arena, Mayhem II tagged in. He and the BD managed to connect with simultaneous clotheslines, but Mayhem II's clothesline had such force behing it that he knocked the BD back into his home corner, where Crossbones tagged in. Mayhem I joined the fun, and for the first time we had members of the same team double team an opponent. Mayhem, Inc. pounded away at Crossbones, and the Grave Dweller decided to introduce himself, pausing long enough to clobber Ron Stern, who had ideas about a 3-on-2. The Dweller tossed Mayhem I back out, and he and the Dweller and Crossbones, unlikely allies, worked over Mayhem II. Finally, after a 1/2 Bostobn crab (Stern made the save), Crossbones left the ring, and it never struck the ref as odd the the Dweller was still in despite having never tagged. The Dweller gorilla pressed Mayhem II, grabbed a handful of tights with the cover, and scored a 2 count. Crossbones tagged back in, and he and his erstwhile and fuuture foe continued to wokr well together, double teaming Mayhem II, who had had better days. When Crossbones applied an octopus hold, Stern made the save again. (Five minutes have elapsed, and we now fast forward to the 14 minute mark, where Mayhem I and the Mortician are in the ring.) The Mortician pildrove Mayhem I, then hoisted him up for a chokelsam. He dragged Mayhem I back up again and hurled him into a neutral corner. Mortician backed up into the opposite corner and launched himself. Sadly for him, he connected with nothing but the turnbuckles. As the ref attended to the Mortician who supported his weight on the buckles, Mayhem II swapped places with the bwearied Mayhem I and hit his patented avalanche, followed by the 3-count. Mayhem I eliminates Mortician at 14:15. (We skip another 8 minutes, as Brian Maloney and Marc Thomas are in the ring.) Thomas monkey flipped Brian out of a neutral corner, then dropped an elbow on him. He dragged Brian up by the hair and ddted him. As Thomas showboated for an unappreciative crowd, Brian staggered to his feet and rolled Thomas up in a crucifix for a 2 count. Thomas whipped Brian into the ropes and went for a clothesline, but brian tumbled beneath Thomas outstretched arm, and hopped back to his feet, locking in an armbar submission. Ron Stern went for the save, but Brian and Thomas were too near the other corner, and the NATO Powers, Dweller, Kelly, and Skull and Crossbones held him and his cohorts at bay, while Thomas submitted at 22:27. (Four minutes later...Brian is still in the ring, along with HMCer Groovy Gonzalez) Brian with a Russinal legsweep and the lateral press for a very close 2-count. He rolled Bonzales over and cinched a Boston crab, but Mayhem II kicked brian in the back of the head to break the hold. Brian managed to get back to his feet before Gonzales, and applied his abdominal stretch on the mat for a pin at 26:33. The Phantom of the Opera and Lord of Midnight applauded from ther apron, and the Phantom of teh Opera and Cool Carlson exchanged shoves before their teammates separated them. (Three minutes later, and Brian still ahsn't tagged out. This time he's in with the Phantom of the Opera) Brian went for a vertical suplex, but the Phantom of the Opera blcoked it and countered with a slingshot suplex. The Phantom of the Opera then ran against the ropes (stopping to punch teammate Cool Carlson), then dropped an elbow on Brian. The Phantom set up for his Chandelier (perhaps the most innovative finsiher in this league) and the three count at 29:31. (Fast forward another three minutes, and Kelly is in with the Phantom) The corwd popped as Kelly turned the Phatnom around in the neutral corner and delivered a series of stinging reverse knife edges. As Kelly bounced off the ropes, the Phantom attempted a frankensteiner, but Kelly caught him in a powerbomb. As Kelly covered, Carlson ran in with a chair and smacked into the back of Kelly's head. As the ref signalled for the disqualification of the Phantom of the Opera at 32:38, the Phatnom and Carlson exchanged blows. Soon the entire heel team was in the ring breaking it up while Kelly caught a breather and tagged in the Russian Tank. (Five more minutes pass by, and Crossbones and the Lord of Midnight are fighting on the floor) Crossbones delievred a hangman on the guardrail to the LoM, then tossed him back in the ring. Crossbones slowly reenterd the ring and signalled for a ddt, receiving an uncustomary crowd pop. The LoM backdropped his way out of trouble and applied his Brain Smasher. After about half a minute, Crossbones gave in, at 37:00. (At the 40 minute mark, Skull and Cool Carlson represented their teams) Skull whipped Carlson into the ropes and stunned him with a flying clothesline. Skull then straddled his opponent and uncorked a succession of rights and lefts to the face. Carlson escaped with a shot to the "nether regions", and stomped away at Skull as he writhed on the floor. Master Wu-Tang, manager of Skull and Crossbones, hopped on to the apron demanding a dq. Carlson spat upon him, and Wu-Tang grabbed a chair and swung it at Carlson. Carlson's face assumed a goofy grin as he dropped to the mat, to the amusement of the Lord of Midnight and the chagrin of Skull, who found himself disqualified at 41:45. As Skull and Wu-Tang headed back to the locker area, fellow teammate the Grave Dweller made a mocking wave goodbye.The British Destroyer entered for his team and locked on a cibra clutch. Carlson immediately gave in at 42:01. The Lord of Midnight rushed into the ring and at the British Destroyer, who assumed a defnsive posture, but the LoM just wanted to raise his hand and congratulate him on his win. Then the LoM dumped Carlson's inert body over the top rope. While I'm sure the LoM was enjoying this immensely, he may have done well to pick a different time to get his small measure of revenge, as the BD punched him in the back odf the head, planted him in the mat with a Doctor Bomb, and scored the pin at 43:17. We were down to the NATO Powers, Kelly Maloney, and the Grave Dweller vs. Mayhem, Inc. and Ron Stern. Mayhem I entered the ring and snap suplexed the former world tag champ. He went for his avalanche, but missed. The BD whipped Mayhem I into the ropes and smacked him in the jaw with an elbow. As Mayhem I fell, he reached out and touched someone, tagging in Mayhem II. Mayhem II blocked a BD punch and gouged BD's eyes. He followed with a ddt,and another avalanche, for the 3-count at 44:01, evening the odds again. Kelly Maloney jumped into the ring, and Mayhem II whipped him into the corner. The ref was between Kelly and the corner, however, and proved to be rather fragile. Mayhem II charged in, and Kelly ducked, so we had three men down. Kelly regained his footing and smacked Mayhem II with a thrust kick, then went for a springboard rana (aka the Kelly Cradle), but Mayhem II caught him and tagged in Ron Stern, who delivered a flying clothesline to Kelly while Mayhem II still held him up. The Dweller decided he had seen just about enough of this and deposited Mayhem II on the opposite side of the ring ropes, then returned to his corner, satisfied with a job well done. Kelly went for a flying sunset flip, but the ref was still unaware of his whereabouts. Mayhem I returned to the ring and slammed a fist into Kelly's jaw instead. Stern ddted Kelly, and the ref returned in time to make the 3-count at 44:50. (Jump ahead three minutes to see Ron Stren and the Grave Dweller) The Dweller scoop slammed Stern and follwed with a fistdrop. As the Dweller made the belt-strap-on-motion for the crowd, Stern pulled out a set of brass knuckles and struck them in the back of the Grave Dweller's head. Cover and pin would have followed at 48:33 had Stern not been disqualified. (Jump ahead 2 more minutes, as the match is now the Russian Tank and Grave Dweller vs. Mayhem, Inc. The Dweller and Mayhem II were in the ring) The Grave Dweller rose back to his knees while Mayhem II continued to rain double sledges up his back and neck. The Dwller, from his knees, drove hos shoulder into Mayhem II's midsection, then floowed with an uppercut to the chin. As the crowd popped loudly, the Dweller lifted the National Tag champ and chokeslammed back down. Mayhem I ran into the ring and straight into the raised boot of the Dweller. The Dweller whipped Mayhem I into the corner, and then whipped Mayhem II in after him. As Mayhem II bounced (or staggered) out of the corner, the Dweller caught him with an iron claw. The Russian Tank entered the ring and held back Mayhem I while Mayhem II submitted at 50:58. Josh Brown saw that he had one man left in the ring against two men who have held the world tag belts 5 times between them and put an end to the match by entering the ring himslef and swinging the chair at both opponents, who fell like wheat before the scythe. The winners, at 51:01, the Russian Tank and Grave Dweller. (fade back to Hoss) Hoss: Can you believe that, that was one of the toughest matches to date for all of those men...and fans the action wasn't over yet let's go back to Matt Wilson for an incident that occured immediately following that match... MW: After the match was over, things heated up even more. Coo, Carlson approached Spirit, the HMC manager, in the locker area and accosted him for not accompanying his team to the ring. Groovy Gonzales took the side of his manager, and the two members of the HMC began scuffling. The Lord of Midngiht and Phantom of the Opera arrived on the scene and assualted both members of the HMC. Submission battered the HMC across the locker area, back out into the aisle, down to the ring. The HMC tried to escape racing back up the opposite aisle, forgetting their enmity toward one another in their common purpose while Submission kept after them. The fight proceeded back into another locker area. The HMC managed to slip into a room and lock the doors. While the Phantom of the Opera and the Lord of Midnight pounded at the door, Gonzales and Carlson grabbed their duffle bags and equipment, tossed them out a window, and climbed out after them. They ran across the parking lot to a parked charter bus, looking nervously behind them. While the driver hassled them about tickets, Submission emerged from the arena entrance and gave pursuit once more. They caught up to the HMC, who were locked out of the bus by a driver who'd run out of patience with their excuses. The Lord of Midnight piledrove Gonzalez on the concrete parking lot ground, and the Phantom of the Opera executed his Chandelier on Carlson. They knocked on the bus door, and the driver, a Submission opened it. After a moment's discussion, they emerged from the bus, and the driver opened the luggage compartment. Submission stuffed the HMC in, the driver locked it up, and went back into the bus to wait for the rest of the passangers. The Lord of Midnight kicked the luggage compartment once or twice to make sure it was tightly closed, and headed back to the arena.
