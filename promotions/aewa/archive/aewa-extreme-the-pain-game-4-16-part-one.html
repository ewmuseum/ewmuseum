AEWA Extreme: The Pain Game (4/16, part one)

18 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * A E W A E X T R E M E : T H E P A I N G A M E * * * * Live, only on pay-per-view, from the CoreStates Center in Philadelphia (We see the AEWA logo on the screen, set on fire. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Rock music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... David Graves TAKING SOME GUY'S HEAD OFF with the Gravestone, Penance PLANTING some guy with the Final Judgment, The West Coast Connection DRIVING some guy's head into the mat with the West Coast Killa, Cardinal Chaos STICKING some poor soul with the Inverted Crucifix, Shane Matthews BRUTALLY hitting the End of the Road on some hapless jobber, and Heavy Metal notching yet ANOTHER casualty courtesy of the Megadeth Power Bomb. The camera then shifts to Matt Wilson, The Lovely Bertha, and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt in the PACKED CoreStates Center, the site of AEWA Extreme: The Pain Game!) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to the haven of hardcore, Philadelphia, where we're about to kickoff the most dangerous pay-per-view in the history of professional wrestling, AEWA Extreme! We have a capacity crowd here in the CoreStates Center tonight, and they all have on thing on their minds-- the main event. It's going to be incredible, folks! BD: That's right, Wilson, "Crimson" Joe Reed has quite a challenge ahead of him tonight. He's going to be putting that World Heavyweight title on the line against three other men in a "four way" match, and to tell ya the truth, I don't think he stands a CHANCE! LB: The odds are definitely against him, buddy. Mister Crowley, Ben Slocum, and Heavy Metal are all going to be gunning for the championship belt, so I don't see how he could POSSIBLY walk out of there with the title. MW: Never underestimate the powers of Joe Reed, Bertha. Anyway, it's now time to get to our first match, as-- (notices something) Wait a minute, we seem to have some kind of a disturbance down at ringside.... BD: We don't have a camera over there yet, but it looks like somebody is trying to climb over the guardrail. We've had quite a bit of these disturbances lately, and I'm getting SICK of it! What's wrong with the damn security guards around here-- is it that hard to keep the fans under control? LB: Okay, I'm trying to figure out what's going on, but I still can't get a good look from here. (The camera switches to a view of the front row, and we see that a large black man is trying desperately to work his way through the crowd. As the camera scans closer, we can see that the man is none other than....) MW: VOLCANO JACKSON! Ladies and gentlemen, I have absolutely no idea what he's doing here at an AEWA show, but there's no doubt about it-- that's him! LB: I never thought that jerk would show his face around here again, but there he is.... I can't believe what I'm seeing! BD: We've had plenty of problems with Jackson in the past, folks. He quit the AEWA last year after a legal dispute with President Freeman, but it looks like he has something to say.... (Jackson finally climbs over the steel guardrail, stepping out of the audience, and he walks up to the ring announcer and grabs the microphone. The fans, who remember Jackson from his last stint in the AEWA, begins roaring with boos. He is wearing a white shirt that says "Atlantic Coast" in black writing, with black shades hiding his eyes. Jackson slides under the ropes and enters the ring, and immediately begins to yell angrily at the crowd.) VOLCANO JACKSON: Shut up! Shut the hell up! Yeah, that's right.... I'm back! You wanna know _why_ I'm back?! I need a break from kicking some guy from Neptune's ass down in ACEW, and I need a break from kicking losers around Tokyo over in EW*ING! I just need a rest, and that's why I'm back in AEWA-- the most NON-COMPETITIVE promotion in this sport! I won the damned AEWA World Heavyweight Title; I was the best damn AEWA Champion ever. And what else? I was a two-time, TWO-TIME AEWA World Television Champion.... yet all the suits up in the AEWA Towers don't even mention it. YEAH FREEMAN, I'm talkin' to you! (Volcano Jackson suddenly turns and points down at the broadcast booth, as the commentators look up at him. He points to Matt Wilson, and continues speaking.) VOLCANO JACKSON: Matt Wilson.... the man who single-handedly ran me out of the AEWA. YOU'RE A DEAD MAN! (Volcano Jackson throws down the microphone and charges out of the ring, toward the broadcast booth. Security guards and a group of preliminary wrestlers run over to hold him back. As they escort Jackson to the locker room, we can hear him yelling, "You're a dead man, Wilson!" The camera immediately switches back to our announcing team.) MW: I'm absolutely speechless right now, folks. BD: What the hell was he talking about, Wilson? I know that you've had your problems with Jackson in the past, but _you_ didn't have anything to do with his legal problems. That guy is a freakin' nutcase. LB: After what he just said, I don't think he'll ever be allowed in an AEWA show again. Matt, don't you have any thoughts about this? MW: I really don't know what to say.... I just come out here every week and try to do my job, but idiots like Jackson always seem to pick _me_ as their punching bag. What did I even do wrong? BD: (pats him on the back) You didn't do anything wrong, Wilson, so don't even worry about it. If Jackson ever wants to cause trouble out here again, he'll have to get past ME first. That's a promise. MW: Well, he's definitely not under a contract with the AEWA right now, so I doubt we'll have any more problems with him. I still have nightmares from the last time I came in contact with that maniac, so I'm sorry if I seem a little bit shaken by this whole thing.... I just can't believe what he said to me. LB: Don't worry about it, Matt. Jackson couldn't even kill a FLEA, so don't lose any sleep over this. MW: Well, anyway, it's now time for our first match, so I guess we should be moving on. Primal Fear is about to square off against Stan Crawford in a "first blood" match, and this one should be a classic. BD: Yeah, we've never had a match like this before in the AEWA, so I'm really looking forward to it. These two guys have been at each other's throats for over three months now. LB: And you know what that means.... BD: We're going to have CARNAGE! Heh heh.... MW: While Big Daddy comes down from his bloodlust, I'll explain. This is a first blood match, which means that whoever is cut open first in the match loses. These two men have had some tremendous battles, from their first match where Crawford shocked Primal Fear by a countout win through their battle at the Mall of America.-- LB: (interrupting) We're not allowed to go back there, I understand. MW: ....to their recent battle at the UWF Gold Rush pay-per-view event. RING ANNOUNCER: The following "first blood" match is scheduled for one fall. The first man to cause his opponent to bleed will be declared the winner. Fear and Crawford both crashed into the fence separating the ring from the fans, and I think they dented the metal fence. BD: They could both be bleeding from that blow! Primal Fear is insane, folks. LB: It's one thing when skinny lightweights do that move, but Fear and Crawford each weigh over 300 pounds. MW: The referee rushes over to check each man. MW: Fear's up, and he's picking Crawford up-- and slams his head against the railing! Look at the look of glee on Xavier's face. BD: Hey, he's a happy guy. Xavier's getting a little too close-- and Crawford has him! Stan Crawford looks around at the crowd while his hands are on Xavier's shirt. (The crowd pops like crazy.) MW: He lifts him up.... and presses Xavier above his head. And he sends him down HARD, right onto the concrete! I think Xavier is out. BD: But he's given Primal Fear time to recover! Fear just delivered a knee to Crawford's back. He lifts him up.... a belly to back suplex on the ground! And another one! LB: It's a good thing this is a first blood match, or both men would have been counted out a while ago. Primal Fear is moving an announcer's table over by the ring. I wonder what he's planning? MW: He picks up Crawford over his shoulder.... he's walking around, climbing up the ring steps.... Instead of going in the ring, he's walking around the ring apron. Here comes a powerslam-- BD: RIGHT ONTO THE TABLE! Crawford is just lying down on the table, barely moving. MW: Primal Fear isn't happy, though. He's backing up along the ring apron.... he's charging.... A BUMP SPLASH, right onto Crawford who is still on the table! <CRACK!> MW: That table broke in half! BD: Forget it.... Crawford's probably bleeding from a dozen places now. MW: The referee goes over to check.... Primal Fear is raising his hand, claiming victory-- but the referee is saying the match will continue! Stan Crawford isn't bleeding yet. LB: The man isn't human. He must have cracked several ribs, and probably has broken a couple. MW: Fear throws Crawford into the ring, and begins biting him. In one of their earlier encounters, Fear opened a gash in Crawford's head. Apparently he's trying to repeat that now. BD: Or he just might be going back for a second course. MW: You truly are a sick individual, Big Daddy. BD: Thank you. MW: Xavier is just now starting to get up, and Primal Fear seems to be in control of this match. He picks up Crawford, and throws him outside the ring, on the opposite side of the table. LB: Fear knows he can win this match at any time.... he's just enjoying himself. MW: Fear is waiting for Crawford to get to his feet. Crawford is holding his side-- you might be right about his ribs, Bertha. Now Fear runs off the opposite ropes.... a plancha through the ropes onto Crawford! And Crawford and Fear again crash into the guardrail! LB: "The Shotgun" is getting on intimate terms with the steel railing. He's just about unconscious. MW: Fear casually tosses Crawford into the ring again, and.... hold on, look at Fear's shoulder. BD: Uh, I don't see anything. MW: I do.... I see blood coming from Primal Fear's shoulder! LB: Crawford must be busted open, and some of the blood got on Primal Fear. MW: No, that shoulder scar was one of the wounds Primal Fear suffered at the Mall of America match. I think when he did that plancha onto Crawford, he hit his shoulder on the guardrail, and re-opened that scar! BD: But.... that doesn't count! Crawford has to open up a new wound! That doesn't count! LB: Maybe the referee won't see it. MW: Fear and Crawford are again in the ring, and Fear is biting Crawford's forehead again. The referee is calling for the bell. Crawford's bleeding! (DING, DING, DING!) BD: Primal Fear bit a large chunk out of Crawford's forehead. MW: But who is the referee calling the bell for? Crawford's forehead gash or Primal Fear's bleeding shoulder? RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match, in eleven minutes and fourteen seconds.... Stan "The Shotgun" Craaaawwwwffffooooorrrddd!! (The crowd pops) BD: No! Primal Fear had the match won! MW: Fear was in control, but one of his old wounds opened up, and the referee caught it before Fear caused Crawford to bleed. LB: Yeah, but Fear doesn't look like a loser, and Crawford certainly doesn't look like a winner. MW: Fear is arguing with the referee.... I don't even think he was aware of his shoulder until the referee informed him. Xavier's in the ring, pleading Fear's case, but it will go down in the record books as a win for Stan Crawford. BD: Primal Fear is leaving, and-- wait a second, he's climbing the turnbuckle! LB: He's on the top ropes.... launching himself.... A BUMP SPLASH RIGHT INTO CRAWFORD! MW: Crawford's already so weakened that these blows could be crippling to him. The referee is having a fit as he orders Fear and Xavier to leave, and they reluctantly comply this time. Well, even though it doesn't look like it, "Shotgun" Stan Crawford comes away with a victory. BD: That guy got his butt kicked tonight, but just like you already said, he won the match. LB: It wasn't fair, though. Anyway, Wilson, isn't it time for the next match yet? I'm ready for some more action.... (Without any warning, Wild Thing suddenly walks out from behind the curtain, making his way toward the ring. He appears to be in a very bad mood, and as soon as they catch sight of him, the crowd absolutely explodes with boos.) BD: Ya know, I'm getting sick and tired of all these damn interruptions that have been happening lately. Wild Thing has continued to embarrass the AEWA for over a week now-- why do they keep letting him come out here? MW: I have no idea, Big Daddy, and I'm really not looking forward to hearing what he has to say. This guy has gotten the AEWA in trouble with the FCC on two different occasions, so just IMAGINE what he's going to say tonight on pay-per-view. LB: I'd rather not, Wilson. (As Wild Thing proceeds toward the ring, and the fans continue to shower him with their boos. His trimmed brown hair has been slicked back with gel, and he is carrying a cordless microphone. He's wearing a white polo shirt and blue jeans, with expensive black dress shoes. As he gets to the ringside area, he stops at the steel guardrail and slaps the baseball cap off a fan in a front row seat. Wild Thing chuckles slightly, and then walks up the steel steps and enters the ring. He waits several seconds and finally begins to speak in his usual low voice.) WILD THING: If you thought I got bad on television, there's no limit to what I can say on pay-per-view! Let me make this short and sweet, people. I'm not interested in listening to your damn boos, so you might as well just shut up. I want to make some comments about a man who already made an appearance here tonight, a man who calls himself Volcano Jackson. Ya know, I saw that Volcano made his "long awaited" return tonight. Not to be rude or anything, because as you all know, I'd NEVER try to offend anyone, but I have to say it-- I really don't give a damn about Jackson. That guy might be a former AEWA World champion, but how long did he hold the title? Not very long. It's really too bad that he didn't STAY gone, because I'm sick and tired of watching the losers go back-and-forth around here. If you want to wrestle in this federation, that's fine, but if you quit once, NEVER come back. That's my way of thinking. Volcano sucked when he wrestled here the first time, and guess what-- he still sucks. Jackson, what the hell did you come back for? Do you think we missed you around here, man? I don't think so. These fans hate you almost as much as they hate me, and that's saying a lot. You're nothing more than a talentless goon, buddy, that's all you EVER were. I remember when President Freeman was force-feeding your merchandise to the fans, but that didn't last long, did it? Of course not, 'cause nobody in their right mind would have ever bought your crap. And hey, is Aaron Proctor comin' back with ya, Volcano? He was the only reason you ever won any matches, if I remember correctly. Or what about Hurricane Henson, your old friend? (sarcastically) That guy was a REAL winner. Volcano, you really should listen to my advice, pal. Go down to the nearest Costco, buy yourself an entire box of aspirin, and do the world a favor-- overdose. I hate to sound harsh, but that's just the way it is. I might not like this damn promotion either, Volcano, but I'm sure as hell not a crybaby about it. You came out here tonight and started threatening the commentators, but what the hell do they have to do with anything? Absolutely nothing. I know you had some kind of a homosexual fixation about little Matt Wilson, but buddy, keep that stuff private. You're a loser, Volcano Jackson, and you don't mean crap to the AEWA. Unfortunately, I wouldn't be a bit surprised if President Freeman gives you a contract tomorrow morning. That guy lets ANYBODY wrestle in this federation anymore. It makes me sick. Anyway, I'm really only out here to make one point. Volcano, don't take off your jacket, 'cause you won't be staying long. We don't want you around here, buddy. I might hate this place, but one thing's for sure-- _I'm_ the only one who make fun of the AEWA. This is MY stomping grounds, and you didn't have the RIGHT to interrupt the show tonight. You're gonna eat your words, big man. You need to get your ass outta Philadelphia before sunrise, because.... I'm gonna kick your damn ass! Ya know, I'm sorry if you people think that last comment was a little too much, but that's just the way it is. I don't want to wrestle in the same ring as that scumbag, and I actually feel a little dirty using the same microphone that he used. Volcano Jackson doesn't have any place in this federation, so why the hell did he come back? Wasn't he making enough money stealing car stereos in the ghetto? That makes me absolutely sick. I don't really have anything else to say right now, so I'll leave you with this thought.... SCREW YOU ALL TO HELL!
