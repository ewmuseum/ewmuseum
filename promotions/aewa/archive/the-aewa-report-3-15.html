The AEWA Report (3/15)

16 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
------------------------- ******************** AEWA REPORT ------------------------- ******************** Welcome to the twelfth official edition of the AEWA Report! We have plenty of big news to tell you about, so read on.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ RECENT NEWS ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ --THE AEWA WILL BE IN THE SUMMIT! President Freeman has announced that the AEWA will be competing in the upcoming Summit of Wrestling, which is a special five-day convention that highlights the BEST talent in the sport. The Gryphon will be squaring off against Damian Steele, Wild Thing is scheduled to wrestle Heavy Metal, and Deathwatch meets "Crimson" Joe Reed in a title-versus-title match. These men should be excellent representatives, and we're very excited about this event. --PRIMAL FEAR AND STAN CRAWFORD: HAVE THEY GONE TOO FAR? On the latest episode of Wednesday Night Rumble, which was held in the middle of the Mall Of America, all hell broke loose in the falls-count anywhere match between Primal Fear and Stan Crawford. They battled into the Macy's jewelry department, and Primal Fear ended up crashing through a display case. His body was COVERED with blood; look for a medical update later in this issue. --WE'RE ABOUT TO INVADE THE UWF! The AEWA is going to have a special match on the Universal Wrestling Federation's next pay-per-view, Gold Rush 1997. "The Shotgun" Stan Crawford will be squaring off against Primal Fear, to show the UWF fans what we're all about. Be sure to call your local cable company and order that event, because it's sure to be a classic. When the UWF and the AEWA get together, there's no telling what could happen. --LEGACY GOES INTO SURGERY! Doctor Skidmore made an exclusive phone call to the Zack Derringer Show recently, giving the fans an update on Legacy's condition. He announced that Legacy would be going into surgery this week, to help determine the extent of his spinal injuries. We hope to contact Skidmore for the next Report, to find out exactly how it went. --VALACHI WILL DEFEND HIS BELT IN THE CWAA! "Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi is scheduled to put his AEWA Light Heavyweight championship on the line in the CWAA, but his opponent hasn't been announced yet. This is going to be an extremely interesting match, because it's the first time an AEWA title has been defended in another federation. We wish Valachi the best of luck. ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ INJURY REPORT ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ PRIMAL FEAR required stitches all over his body after the recent falls count-anywhere match. He isn't expected to miss any matches, but this could directly affect his performance at UWF Gold Rush 1997. He's still in pretty bad condition, and we wish him a quick recovery. STAN CRAWFORD also required stitches because of that match on Wednesday Night Rumble, but he appeared to be in much better condition. Most of Crawford's gashes were on his forehead, after being thrown headfirst into the escalator. ******************* CHAMPION LIST ******************** AEWA World Heavyweight Champion-- "Crimson" Joe Reed AEWA North American Champion-- Deathwatch AEWA Television Champion-- Sir Gallade AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion-- Joey Valachi AEWA World Tag Team Champions-- The Machines AEWA National Tag Team Champions-- The Assassins ******************* THE TOP 10 (SINGLES)**************** 1.
