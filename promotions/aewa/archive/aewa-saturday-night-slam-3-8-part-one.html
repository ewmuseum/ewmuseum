Saturday Night Slam (3/8, part one)

11 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * AEWA SATURDAY NIGHT SLAM * * * * Live, from the Mid-South Coliseum (We see the AEWA logo on the screen, set on fire. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Rock music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... David Graves TAKING SOME GUY'S HEAD OFF with the Gravestone, Penance PLANTING some guy with the Final Judgment, The West Coast Connection DRIVING some guy's head into the mat with the West Coast Killa, Cardinal Chaos STICKING some poor soul with the Inverted Crucifix, Shane Matthews BRUTALLY hitting the End of the Road on some hapless jobber, and Heavy Metal notching yet ANOTHER casualty courtesy of the Megadeth Power Bomb. The camera then shifts to Matt Wilson, The Lovely Bertha, and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt in the PACKED Mid-South Coliseum, the site of AEWA Saturday Night Slam!) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Saturday Night Slam! We have plenty of great matches scheduled for tonight, and in the main event, "Crimson" Joe Reed will be defending his World Heavyweight title against Primal Fear! That should be an excellent match, folks. BD: Wilson, you never cease to amaze me. You CONSTANTLY overlook the good matches, and choose to focus on the CRAP. Nobody really cares about that World title match-- that's not why the great people of Memphis have paid their hard-earned money to get into this show! MW: So tell me, Big Daddy, what do they REALLY want to see? Actually, let me guess-- the handicap match, right? BD: How'd ya guess? Yes, that's definitely going to be the highlight of this program, as Sir Gallade and Shane Matthews team up against that worthless sack of hot air, Randall Osbourne. LB: And it's all perfectly legal. Handicap matches don't happen too often in this sport, but when they do, all hell usually breaks loose. I'm looking forward to a good, old fashioned, ass kicking. MW: Don't be so sure, Bertha. Randall Osbourne has proven himself many times before, so he should NEVER be underestimated. I think he'll teach Matthews and Gallade a lesson in respect. BD: Think again, buddy. I'd love to keep arguing with you, but isn't it time to get the show started? MW: Yes indeed. Up first, Hank Thompson is about to conduct an EXCLUSIVE interview with our new World champion, "Crimson" Joe Reed. LB: Why does Thompson always get to do the interviews? He's getting more annoying after every program. MW: I disagree with you, but it really isn't worth arguing over. Let's just get a camera down there, and see what Reed has to say.... (The camera shifts to Hank Thompson, who is standing in the ring, about to start the interview.) Thompson: At this time, would you please welcome our special guest, he is the NEW AEWA World Heavyweight Champion, "Crimson" Joe Reed! ("Bad Company" by Bad Company starts playing over the PA. A red spotlight shines at the entrance, and "Crimson" Joe Reed and the Lady in Red walk down the aisle. Reed is wearing black tights and a jacket, and the AEWA World Title is worn around his waist. By his side is the Lady in Red in-- guess what-- a long red dress. They walk toward ringside, then circle the ring before entering, shaking hands along the entire route. Finally, they climb the ring step, Reed holds the ropes for the Lady in Red to enter, then hops over the top rope. He sits on the top turnbuckle, and raises the belt over his head to the cheering crowd.) Thompson: Joe, congratulations on your victory over Deranged at Kickoff '97. It was a classic. Joe Reed: Thank you, Hank. I've been in this sport for almost twelve years. Twelve years of struggling and straining, fighting and enduring, taking on all comers, any time, any where. To climb from all of that and reach the pinnacle of the profession-- to be the WORLD CHAMPION-- this is a great moment, and I'm glad everyone is here to share it with me. (The crowd cheers) Thompson: That was a rough match you had with Deranged to win the title. I'm certain just about everyone has seen you powerbomb Deranged through the table. Reed: I'm not going to lie to you-- I am willing to push it to the limit to win and keep the title. But I heard the Knights of Deliverance talking about respect. Well, I've always believed in giving the respect that a person has earned. It's how I've lived my life, it's how I've wrestled my matches, and it's how I plan on defending this title. I've been through too much to do it any other way. Deranged, you gave me a hell of a battle, and I have the utmost respect for you. A rematch is yours for the taking. Thompson: Let's talk about some of the top contenders. Not only Deranged, but also your three opponents at the Four-Way Dance-- Mister Crowley, Ben Slocum, and Heavy Metal. Joe Reed: Before I even talk about any of them, I have to get by Primal Fear. Fear, you are a very tough man, and I've seen you put away many wrestlers with your Bump Splash. I am not going to make the mistake of taking you lightly-- but I intend to take you down. Crowley, Slocum, Metal.... how many times have any of you been the world champion? Five? Six? Seven? You've all been the champ before, and you all have experienced both the desire to become the champion and the thrill of being the champion. Taking all of you on is a tough job- but I'm not complaining. I asked for this (points to the belt), I worked for this, I earned this! I've heard some people call me an "Old School" wrestler. Mister Crowley, Ben Slocum, and Heavy Metal.... bring your notebooks. Lady In Red: Joe has gotten to this point by being willing to take on anyone, and he's not going to slow down now. Get in line for a shot at the belt.... but be prepared to fall. Joe Reed: Lots of men claim to be King of the Hill, but there's only one. You're looking at him, and I have no plans on stumbling any time soon. Thompson: Ladies and Gentlemen, "Crimson" Joe Reeeeeeeeeed!! (Joe Reed and the Lady in Red leave the ring to mingle with the crowd again, and the camera returns to our broadcast team.) MW: Well, he definitely seems confident about that upcoming Four-Way match. It's all going to be part of our next pay-per-view, AEWA Extreme. BD: And Reed is gonna get his butt get in an EXTREME way, folks. That challenge is just too much for him. LB: But don't forget, he still has to get past Primal Fear in their World title match tonight. Can he do it? MW: We'll have to wait and see, Bertha. Anyway, it's now time for our first match of the evening, as The Assassins are about to defend their newly won AEWA National Tag Team Titles against Heaven and Hell. BD: And we're going to have new champions tonight! LB: There's no way that the Assassins can hold a candle to Chaos and Crowley. MW: I agree that in singles competition Chaos or Crowley would probably beat Banshee or Shockwave. But tag team wrestling is another situation altogether, and I think the Assassins will prove why they won the belts. Let's get to the ring! RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team match is scheduled for one fall, and is for the AEWA National tag team titles! Introducing first, the challengers, Mister Crowley and Cardinal Chaos, HEAVEN & HELL! (The Rolling Stones' "Sympathy for the Devil" begins to play loudly over the PA as the two former World Champions make their way toward the ring, to a mixed reaction from the capacity crowd. It's hard to tell whether the people are behind them or not.) BD: And they aren't alone! (He points as the Holy Ghost, Sir Gallade, Bishop Bedlam, and Big Ron Raider walk down. Most of the group get a mixed reaction, except for Gallade, who draws tremendous boos from everyone. The members of the Church stand in the ring by Chaos and Crowley.) MW: This is ridiculous. They're supposed to face two men, not six! LB: Notice how Raider and Bedlam are standing far apart. I guess there's still bad blood between them. RING ANNOUNCER: And their opponents, the National tag team champions, Shockwave and Banshee, the ASSASSINS! (Shockwave and Banshee make their entrance, to a HUGE pop from the crowd. They slaps hands with the fans in the front row, and stop at ringside. They quickly discuss the situation, then head back to the locker room.) BD: I knew it! The Assassins are quitting! They saw what they were up against, and decided to just run away. MW: I don't think so, Big Daddy. (And neither does anyone else, as the Assassins return. But they aren't alone either. Ben Slocum, the Caretaker, Justin Trouble, Gryphon, and Randall Osbourne walk down with the Assassins. The crowd cheers like crazy as the Knights of Deliverance enter the ring.) LB: Hold on! This isn't fair! The Church is outnumbered seven to six! MW: But a six to two advantage for the Church was fair. LB: Yes, because.... well, just because! MW: Osbourne looks like he has to be physically restrained by the Caretaker from going after Gallade. The thirteen competitors are facing each other in a standoff, neither side making the first move. BD: Now the ref is talking with both the Assassins and Heaven & Hell. It looks like he wants everyone else to leave ringside before he'll start the match. What a spoilsport! MW: The rest of the Church starts backing up, and the Knights of Deliverance start to leave.... LB: Sir Gallade just smacked Randall Osbourne in the back of the head! Osbourne's trying to go after him, but the rest of the Knights are holding him back. MW: You can hear Slocum telling Randall to wait, that he'll get his chance later.... It looks like both sides are heading back. Hold on a moment.... (Matt leaves the table) BD: Why does the referee need to talk to Matt? LB: Maybe he needs help finding his cane and guide dog. (Matt Wilson comes back, and picks up the house microphone. He turns to the crowd, and begins to speak.) MW: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN.... I have just been informed by the referee that both the Knights of Deliverance and the Church have been BANNED FROM RINGSIDE! Any appearance by either member will result in an immediate disqualification loss for that side! BD: The referee can't do that, can he? MW: (sitting back down) Apparently, he can. And it appears to work-- both sides are away from the ring. Gallade's still taunting Osbourne, but Randall will get his chance later tonight. LB: The referee is finally signaling the timekeeper to ring the bell, and they're off! MW: Mister Crowley is in the ring with Shockwave, the bigger of the Assassins. They lock up, and Crowley quickly puts a headlock onto Shockwave. He sends him into the ropes, here's the rebound.... Shockwave knocks Crowley to the ground with a shoulderblock! Shockwave grabs Crowley in an armbar, pulls him to the corner, and tags Banshee. Banshee climbs to the top rope, and nails Crowley with an elbow! BD: No fair, ref! Crowley couldn't defend himself-- Shockwave was still holding on to Crowley's arm. MW: The Assassins have a five count to get out of the ring after a tag, Big Daddy. LB: But that only applies to Heaven & Hell in this match. MW: (rolling his eyes) Uh-huh. In the meantime, Banshee has been working over Crowley's left arm with a series of karate kicks while still applying that armlock. Now Banshee's running to the ropes.... he leaps over the ropes, and yanks Crowley's arm over the top rope as he falls to the floor! BD: That can dislocate a shoulder. Crowley needs to make a tag. LB: Yeah, but Crowley's a four-time champion. He's not going to let a little pain stop him. I think he's setting Banshee up. MW: Crowley's on the mat holding onto his left arm, in too much pain to make a tag. Banshee hops up, and climbs the neutral corner turnbuckle. He leaps.... CAUGHT by Crowley! Crowley just dropkicked Banshee in mid-air as Banshee was delivering a flying axehandle! LB: Ha! Told you that Crowley was just playing possum! MW: Apparently he was, Bertha. Now Crowley pulls Banshee over to his corner, and tags in Chaos. The two former World Champions are just pummeling the Assassin. BD: Banshee is so much smaller than either of his opponents, he can't possibly win a brawl. He'd better get out of there now. MW: I'm sure he'd agree, but he's not in a position to argue. Chaos picks him up and delivers a reverse atomic drop! Now Chaos bounces off the ropes and clotheslines Banshee! Here's the cover.... One.. Two.. Kickout! BD: Too soon, they need to beat on the little guy some more. Not that it should be any problem. MW: Chaos picks Banshee up, and slams him to the mat! Here comes a tag, and Crowley's back in. They whip Banshee into the ropes, but he catches them with a high cross body, knocking them both to the ground! LB: The little guy still has some strength left. MW: He rolls over, and makes the tag to Shockwave. Shockwave grabs both Chaos and Crowley's heads, and knocks them together. Banshee's still in the ring, and he delivers a dropkick to Crowley, knocking him out of the ring! Shockwave picks up Chaos, and Banshee runs off the ropes to deliver a Hart Attack clothesline! Here's the cover... LB: But the ref is waving them off. BD: The Cardinal's not the legal man! The ref is ordering Shockwave to stop covering him MW: Meanwhile, Crowley has pulled Chaos out of the ring, and they are walking around the ring back to their corner. LB: Excellent move. They need to slow down the pace, bring it back to their style of fight. MW: Apparently the Assassin's don't think so-- they're going out after Heaven & Hell! Both teams are on the outside of the ring, exchanging lefts and rights. BD: Alright, Chaos and Crowley, kill those puny little ninjas! MW: It looks like Crowley's back into the ring, stopping the referee's count. Banshee's following him back in. Shockwave and Chaos are still brawling outside.... Crowley kicks Banshee in the midsection, and now he's locking an abdominal stretch onto Banshee in the middle of the ring. BD: Banshee doesn't have anywhere to go.... (The crowd roars, and Big Daddy stops) What's going on? (Walking down the aisle to a loud but mixed response is....) MW: SIMON O'NEAL! What's he doing here? LB: I'll go find out. (She walks out of the announcer's booth, and heads over to Simon.) MW: Bertha, you forgot your microphone.... oh, nevermind. Meanwhile, Banshee managed to hiptoss his way out of the abdominal stretch, but Banshee's still in serious trouble. Crowley in working on Banshee's back, and picks him up for a belly-to-back suplex. BD: Shockwave and Chaos are still battling it out on the outside of the ring. Shockwave picks up Chaos, and drops him onto the guard rail! MW: Shockwave is heading back to the ring-- but he sees O'Neal! He's yelling at Simon, telling him to go back. BD: Yeah, but what can he do? It's not like O'Neal is a member of the Church. MW: Simon's pointing at his belt, and I can hear him call Shockwave a "second-rate wrestler" from here. Bertha could give us more information, as she's a little closer. Actually, very close-- Simon's arm is around her waist.... BD: And here come Chaos! (THUD!) MW: Cardinal Chaos just nailed Shockwave with a chair! Shockwave was too busy with Simon O'Neal to even notice Chaos, and the referee was too occupied with the battle inside the ring to see Chaos! Shockwave is on the ground. BD: Shockwave was stupid, folks. He spent too much time looking at O'Neal to even think about the guy he was fighting with, and he got caught. MW: Meanwhile, Banshee has managed to fight back against Crowley, and nails him with a dropkick! He comes a cover... One.. Two.. Chaos breaks the count! MW: The Cardinal is stomping away on Banshee, and giving Crowley a chance to recover. With Shockwave unconscious, Heaven & Hell have a two-on-one advantage over the Assassins. Crowley goes over to Chaos, and officially tags him in. Chaos picks up Banshee, and delivers a vicious powerslam! Here comes a cover... One.. Two.. Kickout! BD: Not yet. Banshee stands up, and Chaos just slaps him across the face! Chaos kicks him, and delivers a powerbomb! Another cover.... One.. Two.. Foot on the ropes! BD: Damn! MW: Sorry, Big Daddy. But Banshee is in serious trouble-- just look at him. Chaos pulls Banshee over, and tags Crowley. Chaos holds Banshee down while Crowley delivers a series of kicks, right to Banshee's ribs! Chaos exits the ring, only to be tagged right back in. BD: Excellent teamwork by Heaven & Hell. Chaos is the fresher man, so they're keeping him in. MW: Crowley holds down Banshee while Chaos climbs the ropes, and Chaos nails Banshee with an elbowdrop! BD: Look at that! Shockwave's finally beginning to stir. MW: Chaos makes a cover on Banshee after that elbowdrop.... One.. Two.. Thr.. Kickout! MW: NO! The referee is only holding up two fingers. Banshee just kicked out at the last second.... BD: Come ON, ref! Fortunately, Chaos and Crowley are veterans, they can handle this. MW: It looks like Chaos is setting Banshee up for the Inverted Crucifix.... if he hits this, the match is over. He sets him up, AND NAILS HIM! Here's the cover... BD: But Shockwave's in the ring, and the referee is too busy with him to make the count! MW: The referee's trying to get Shockwave out of the ring, but meanwhile Chaos has Banshee down for a three.... four.... five count! Both Crowley and Chaos are going over to the referee to plead their case! Shockwave's finally back in the ring. And Banshee hits a dropkick that nails Chaos right into Crowley! Crowley is knocked out of the ring, and Chaos is down. Banshee quickly climbs the ropes.... MOONSAULT! Here's the cover... One.. Two.. Three! (DING, DING!) BD: This is ridiculous-- Heaven and Hell had them beat! MW: The match was a close one, but Banshee and Shockwave pulled it out. Here's the official announcement.... RING ANNOUNCER: Here are your winners, and STILL the AEWA National Tag Team Champions, Banshee and Shockwave, THE ASSASSINS!! (The crowd goes wild.) MW: Amazing. After being brutalized by both Chaos and Crowley for several minutes, Banshee manages to pull out a moonsault and get the win for his team. Chaos and Crowley are storming out of the ring, while the jubilant but exhausted Assassins are celebrating at ringside. BD: They cheated.... (notices Bertha) and where have YOU been, young lady? (The Lovely Bertha sits back down with Matt and Big Daddy.) LB: I've been talking with Simon. MW: And what was his purpose at ringside? LB: (laughs) Well, why don't we find out? (She grabs the microphone and hands it to O'Neal, who rolls into the ring.) SIMON: Hey, Assassins! Congratulations on your win. With your luck, you should be playing the lottery. Now stay there and listen to me! (motions for Banshee and Shockwave to stay at ringside.) I was thinking over some of your recent comments. Now, you called Paul and myself rulebreakers and implied that we weren't champions worthy of respect. Let's correct a few of these statements. First of all, Paul's not a rulebreaker. That "Boy Scout" partner of mine couldn't break a rule if it fell on his head. Me-- (gives his trademark smirk) I'm different. I didn't write the book on cheating, but there's a few chapters dedicated to me. But at least I'm open about who or what I am. Now to the other part-- that we don't seem to be your idea of worthy champions. Well, boys, that's BULLSH*T! We don't have to fit your idea of anything, and we don't have to play by your rules, but Paul and I are better than any team in the AEWA. We beat you once, and we'll beat you again. (The crowd cheers as Paul Wong walks through the crowd and rolls into the ring. He motions for the microphone, but Simon holds him up and continues speaking.) However.... I'm willing to offer you a deal. You see, I'm looking at you and all your Knights of Deliverance friends, and I don't like what I see. I see us being outnumbered-- what, seven to two? No way. So, I'll make you a deal. You keep the rest of your friends in the locker room, away from the ring.... and I'll let the referee search me. Not their typical three-second searches. Hell, I can hide about twenty different weapons on me there. I'll let him search my glove, my boots, my hat, everything, until you're satisfied that I can't bring anything to the ring. (At this point, Paul grabs the microphone from Simon.) Paul: What are you doing? Simon: I'm giving them what they want. They claim that we can't win without cheating? Fine-- here's an opportunity to prove it. The match won't start until they're convinced that I don't have any of my normal tricks with me. Paul: You're setting up something-- I know you. Simon: (grinning from ear to ear) Maybe I am. (now points to the Assassins) But guys, I have just one question. After my partner and I destroy you and take your belts, what's your excuse going to be? (He drops the microphone and rolls out of the ring. Paul, shaking his head, joins his partner. They walk past the Assassins, Simon flaunting his World Title Belt and Paul pulling him away from their future opponents.) MW: Well, that was interesting. Simon O'Neal is apparently daring everyone-- including his partner-- to catch him breaking the rules. What's so funny, Bertha? LB: (laughing) Just something Simon said to me about the Assassins. MW: Would you care to share your information? LB: Not right now. MW: (sighing) Then let's get ready for our next match. * * * * TO BE CONTINUED IN PART TWO * * * * \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- "A New Beginning" American E-Wrestling Association The Official E-Wrestling Federation of Wrestle World and TNM The Best E-Wrestling Fed of the Year 1996 in the TNM Year-End Awards Be sure to visit our Event Center at: http://www.california.com/~bullet/index.html
