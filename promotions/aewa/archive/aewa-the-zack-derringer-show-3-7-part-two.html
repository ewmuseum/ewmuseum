The Zack Derringer Show (3/7, part two)

11 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * CONTINUED FROM PART ONE * * * * ---------- (The camera opens on Matt Furnish, sitting behind a desk at the AEWA offices. He has long, brown hair that is tied back and he has a grin on his face. He wears a pair of blue jeans, black boots, and a "Heavy Metal" T-shirt that has a picture of Heavy Metal on it and below him it has the words "ROCK ON!". He starts to speak.) Matt Furnish: It sure is great to be here, that's all I can say. I've watched the AEWA for over a year now, and ever since day one I thought it was one of the best federation out there. As most of you know, I'm the former president of World Wide E-Wrestling, or WWEW as most called it, and in my personal opinion, I did a damn good job. There are going to be quite a few changes around here starting today. I noticed that sometimes things are late and held off for long periods of time.... well, not anymore! I'm going to make it my personal duty to get things out on time, and to make sure it's the same quality product that the AEWA has provided for the past year or so. (Someone hands him a paper from offstage, and Furnish grabs it. He reads it over and then gets a shocked look on his face.) Matt Furnish: Now I didn't know about this-- it seems since Mike Stewart left, there is a man by the name of Kevin Hatcher? Is that it? Well, anyway.... this Hatcher guy is supposedly taking over the match for Stewart against Deathwatch. Now I'm not a real big fan of Deathwatch, but I'm also not a very big fan of early jumps in this career. People like Deathwatch, no matter how sick minded they are or what their opinions are, they still worked damn hard to get where they are. I don't know how much of a say Mr. Freeman is going to allow me to have here, but if it were up to me, this Hatcher guy would NOT get a title shot. I also read that Deathwatch feels this way as well and has issued a challenge to.... well, the wrestlers, and I suppose the Board of Directors. (Furnish reads it over again for a brief second.) Matt Furnish: He wants to take on five different people in a ROW!? Where have I heard this before? Anyway, Deathwatch, if you want to do this little one-man-tournament of sorts, I won't oppose it. But I think that it's really up to Mr. Freeman and Mr. Gehringer as to whether or not this comes to pass. It also sounds like he's picked five talented wrestlers, all deserving of a title shot, and whoever wins this gets that title shot at AEWA Extreme. That sounds like a good enough idea to me. These men are: Super-Blonde, The Caretaker, Nightshade, Primal Fear, and Justin Trouble. Whoa, this sounds like one hell of a competition, I sure hope you're up to it, Deathwatch. 'Cause these are five of the most dangerous men in the AEWA, and even though Nightshade is a rookie to this federation, he's still had more than enough experience to qualify from WWEW, I should know. (Furnish goes over a few other documents and then continues.) Matt Furnish: Oh yeah! I almost forgot that I have a couple match changes for the pay-per-view that I have to announce, so here they are. First off, the Gryphon versus Damian Steele match has been replaced with Gryphon taking on EXODUS in a scaffold match. This meets up to Exodus' challenge just a few moments ago, and most importantly.... YES, it will be for the Exodus Championship Title! Is this ground breaking news or what? (Furnish reads another sheet of paper and goes on.) Matt Furnish: And to make up for his match being canceled, Damian "The Predator" Steele will be taking on "Big, Bad" Leroy Brown! That match should be GREAT! Now, I don't think I have any other business, so I'll leave you with this.... AEWA, ROCK ON! Hey, I've _always_ wanted to say that. (grins) (fade to black) --------------- ZD: Well, there you have it right from the Vice President's mouth. Two matches signed for AEWA Extreme-- Gryphon and Exodus in a scaffold match for the Exodus Championship Title, AND Damian "The Predator" Steele will meet up against "Big, Bad" Leroy Brown. Both of those bouts involve ICU members. HT: That's right, Zack. I'm really excited about this! There are more and more surprises happening at every turn! ZD: Our next flash is from Super-Blonde, this should be good. ----------- (The scene opens in a local gym in Vancouver. Kismet is in the ring, sparring with a local pro, and Super Blonde sits on the outside with a towel around his neck, exhausted. Kismet executes a springboard dropkick. Without wasting any times, she applies a senton and goes for a cover-- one, two, kickout!) Blonde: You almost done yet, cuz? The Canucks game is on in 20 minutes.... we've been here since breakfast! (Kismet turns to Super Blonde, and the pro sneaks up on her from behind. She snaps around, catching him with a Superkick.) Kismet: Just a few more minutes, dammit! (She goes back to the pro, and straps on a bow-and-arrow submission, but he breaks out after ten seconds. The pro gets up and DDTs her to the canvas. Kismet jumps back up and latches on a clawhold on him, and he starts screaming in agony. She holds it for about twenty seconds before Super Blonde climbs into the ring and breaks it.) Blonde: What's gotten into you lately? Since when did you start using stuff like that? Kismet: (turning around) Deathwatch taught it to me. Blonde: You've been seeing that guy nonstop for a week now.... Don't you see that he's a bad seed? Kismet: (now pissed) How the hell should you know? You've just seen him at work! The guy's an absolute dream. Blonde: Jesus Christ, he just about killed someone! What's gotten into you, cuz? Kismet: (turning) Huh? You gonna be like that? FINE! Walk. (She storms out of the ring, grabs her car keys from the table, and storms out of the gym; leaving Super Blonde and the fallen pro in the ring. From outside, thunder is heard and the beating of rain on the roof reverberates. Blonde hangs his head and helps the pro up. Fade to black.) --------------- ZD: I almost feel sorry for Blondie.... (pauses) ....Nah! It's good to see that Kismet's come to her senses. They make a good couple, Deathwatch and her. HT: I can't believe it! After all this time, Kismet would turn her back on her own cousin. ZD: Believe it, buddy. Super-Blonde has less brains than a pet rock, I'm not the least bit surprised that she left his side. HT: Oh well, can't linger on this for too long, let's just go to our next flash from the Gryphon. ZD: Yes, let's. ------------- (The Gryphon is strolling the halls of the Mid-South Coliseum. He has a slightly pensive look on his face, but otherwise he looks relaxed. Gryphon reaches the portal leading into the aisle, pauses for a moment, then continues to stroll down, until he reaches the ring. Gryphon pulls himself up on the ring apron, and steps over the top rope. Gryphon sits on a top turnbuckle, and turns toward the camera.) Gryphon: AEWA, what you witnessed at Kickoff '97 was just a sample of what I hold in store for those who stand in my way. Legacy, I didn't really have anything against you, other than the fact that you were an ICU member. You were in the wrong place, at the wrong time, and you paid dearly for it. Now you'll likely be stuck in a wheelchair for the rest of your natural life. That's a damn shame, but you've nobody to blame but yourself. The business we're in is a dangerous one-- people get hurt all the time. You're just one of the unlucky ones, Legacy.... I don't feel the least bit sorry for you. You were a punk, Legacy. You deserved what you got, and more. Consider yourself lucky to just be left crippled, because if I'd had any more time with you, you would be in a much worse condition. (Gryphon steps off the turnbuckle, and paces the ring a little.) Gryphon: Tonight, however, I have a whole other problem to deal with. Damian "The Predator" Steele. Steele, I've heard of your exploits in WWEW.... am I supposed to be impressed? Well, I really don't give a rat's ass what titles you've held in any other federation-- this is the AEWA, and you haven't done a damn thing here to earn my respect. You've done something to earn my ire-- you betrayed me, and you betrayed the people who will be filling these seats (motions around the arena) not too long from now. I'm gonna make you pay, Steele, and I'll send a message to the rest of your ICU buddies that the Knights of Deliverance are not to be trifled with. (The Gryphon turns to leave, then turns back toward the camera, seemingly remembering something.) Gryphon: Oh, and Exodus.... at AEWA Extreme, I've heard it's gonna be you and me on that scaffold instead of me and Steele. If that's the case, I'll give you a lesson in flight, that you'll have a very, very small chance of passing. I'm gonna enjoy hearing that bone crunching thud when you hit the ground, Exodus. Damian Steele, tonight, you will face Deliverance! (Gryphon steps over the top rope and leaves the ring area. Fade to black.) ------------ HT: Gryphon really looks prepared for his match with Steele, but you have to wonder-- will Exodus get involved? ZD: You and I both know that Exodus doesn't CHEAT! What're you thinkin'? (grins) HT: In any case, we have one last flash to get to, and it's from The Apocalypse. ZD: They call themselves the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse, but it seems there were some contractual differences and so officially they couldn't use that name, but here they are.... the Four Losers of the Apocalypse. ----------------- (The camera fades to black and you can hear heavy winds in the background. Seconds later, creepy organ music begins to play and "4HotA" in a circle appears in dark purple, and it glows. After that, "Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse" appears behind the symbol in a banner. The camera then fades to black again and opens up in a cornfield, where all four men are standing. Nightshade and Pestilence stand next to each other, with the wind blowing through their black hair. They both wear their usual black robes with their hoods down. Blackout wears his long, brown trenchcoat, a black shirt, brown pants, and black boots. His long, white hair is pulled back and tied near the small of his back with two small braids, one on either side of his head held together by two small, metal clips. His eyes are shaded by his John Lennon style sunglasses, and he has a cynical smile on his face that reveals his two canine teeth on each side of his mouth. Penance wears his long, black trenchcoat, black shirt, black pants, black boots, and a single black glove on his left hand. His long, black hair hangs to the sides of his head, and he shows off a casual grin to the camera and stares at it with his black eyes. Pestilence grins at the camera sadistically and speaks first.) Pestilence: Well, well, well, Pestilence has made his return to the AEWA, and he's returned with some.... friends. (grins) Tell me, Deathwatch, how did it feel to lose the best member of ICU? Let us face it, old friend, Penance is more talented than even you, and you blew it. Penance: Yes, Pestilence and I have been together for a very long time, and in his absence from the AEWA, I had been setting up the plot for the Horsemen's appearance here. It all worked smoothly, if I do say so myself. Nightshade: You know, when we came into the AEWA, we were told that we could not use our name. Our REAL name. Security cannot stop us, the corporate bulls of the AEWA cannot stop us, and your worthless pathetic God cannot stop us, simply because.... we ARE the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse. Blackout: It's a shame that the world must fear what they do not understand, but unfortunately.... that's just the way it is. AEWA, you all know why we're here and once we've (chuckles) BITTEN the core of the matter, (grins) we can establish our primary goal. Penance: ICU is our "unfinished business", so to speak. Once we have shown them the way-- to hell-- then we can move on with "Plan B", which is to show you mortals to the same direction. Of course, we're all human (looks at the other three, they all chuckle), but that's beside the point. Pestilence: I will make it perfectly clear, right now, that we do not want any trouble from The Church. Cardinal Chaos, you are an old friend and stablemate, but as you can see, I have moved on to bigger and better things. We have our sights set on EVERYONE here, and as long as you stay out of our way, no harm will come to you, your pride, your body, or your soul. The same goes for the rest of your stable. Nightshade: It seems as if just anyone can join a stable these days. Sir Gallade is in The Church, Heavy Metal is in ICU, and who was that waste of human body fluids that is in the Angels of Death? Bah, who cares. We are here to make an impression, and impression, we shall make. Blackout: There won't be five of us.... there won't be seven.... there won't even be TEN of us. There will be only four. Four simple men destroying an entire organization. There's just something about that that you have to admire, don't you think? Penance: We aren't here to play games and we aren't here to fool around, we're here to get what we want and express it to the world. What can you do about it? Ab-so-lute-ly NOTHING! We are the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse.... and your Apocalypse.... has come. (All four of them laugh, and then they step backwards into the corn and disappear. The camera fades to black, and then to a dark purple "4HotA" logo. It flashes brightly and then fades to pure black.) --------------------------------------------- HT: Psychos! I don't see why anyone would let them IN to the AEWA! There should have been more problems than just a name change. ZD: I have something to say to the Horsemen RIGHT here and now! You punks had better realize who you're messing with! You can't waltz right in here and threaten us, do you have the FAINTEST idea as to how POWERFUL we are!? NO, you don't, and when Deathwatch and the ICU run you over like a Mac truck.... WHO, just who, is going to save you? Your precious god, Satan!? I don't THINK so-- (Derringer begins to hyperventilate) HT: Calm down! Your head is about to explode! (Derringer wipes the sweat off his brow, and takes a deep breath.) ZD: Alright, that's enough for these damn interviews. I'd like to introduce the first guest to the Zack Derringer show, so I'll be seein' ya, Hank. (Derringer gets up out from behind the desk, and walks over to another studio. He shuts the door, and then the camera switches to a view of inside the studio. Derringer stands with a microphone in-hand in front of a small studio audience. A downpour of boos come from the audience as they throw small pieces of wadded up paper at him, with "King Nothing" by Metallica BLASTING over the PA. Derringer stands in a pure black set that has another microphone laying on the ground. He begins to speak.) Zack Derringer (ZD): Heya morons, how's it goin'? (loud boos) ZD: Aw, isn't that special. Screw you and the horse you rode in on, I should know.... I rode her last night! (Zack laughs and gets a HUGE round of boos from the crowd.) ZD: Bah, SHUT THE HELL UP! (He calms down a bit, and so does the crowd.) ZD: That's better! I'm gonna introduce my first guest here tonight. He's one of the newest members of International Contracts Unlimited and he's held titles in a couple federations during his short career of just around four months. Here is.... Damian.... The Predator.... STEELE!! ("Swamp Song" by Tool begins to play as Damian walks through the door and flips his middle finger to the audience. He wears no shirt and a black vest with the letters "DS" on the left flap in red, blue jeans, and a black pair of combat boots. He has a cocky smile on his face as he runs his fingers through his dark blonde hair. The music eventually stops and Damian stands beside Zack and he picks up the microphone.) Steele: Hey, Zack. Thanks for having me here, bud. It's nice to know SOME people appreciate good talent, unlike these morons in the audience. (Loud boos from the crowd, but Derringer ignores it and continues.) ZD: No problem, man. So, let's get this show on the road. Let's take you back to Kickoff '97, your match with Sir Gatorade.... err.... Gallade, for the TV Title. You would have had him BEAT if not for that damn steel pad on his elbow! You got screwed in every sense of the word, and I don't think it's right, but let's hear YOUR comments on this. Steele: Well, any normal wrestler would be extremely pissed off right about now, but you know what? I don't care. Gallade can have his cheap win. He just knows that if not for that steel bar concealed under his elbow pad, needless to say, I would have kicked his goddamned teeth in. And I used to respect Cardinal Chaos and The Church before they accepted that loser into membership. Wins and losses aren't a big thing. I'm one of the baddest motherf*ckers IN the AEWA, and with ICU by my side, I'm going straight to the top! ZD: That's what I like to hear, man. Now, what is your opinion on Penance's turn on ICU? He was with us for quite awhile there, and all of a sudden-- BAM! He turns on us and brings back those damned Horsesh*ts. Your opinion? Steele: Sh*t, I had no problems with them in WWEW, or CWF, but now this is different. He pledged himself to ICU and he just goes and turns his back on us. Again, I don't really care. If Penance wants to be weak and kiss Pestilence's ass, he can go right ahead and do that, because I know where I stand, Deathwatch knows where I stand, and that's all there is to it. And if you have a problem with that, Penance, I've got another ass for you to kiss, punk. ZD: Hah! You gotta love this guy, folks. Now, it seems to me that you had a match this weekend against.... um.... whats-his-face, uh.... Fed Dog, or um.... Dirty Dog or something like that. Oh yeah.... Red Dog! But this wasn't the match that WAS going to be signed, no, not at all. It was going to be the Gryphon, but they decided that they should just hold it off until later. They don't want Steele destroying Gryphon TOO soon, eh? Steele: Oh man, speaking of kissing asses, here's the prime case of it. Gryphon saw that I hadn't gone soft, so he went into Mr. Freeman's office, dropped his pants and got nasty, until Freeman, full of ecstasy, signed a different match. You can't escape me THAT easily, Gryphon. Be a man and face me. Just because you're not getting any p*ssy doesn't mean you have to act like one. It's corrupted pieces of sperm like you that make me want to throw up, and when I'm done looking at you, it makes me wanna kick your ass. ZD: The Zack Derringer Show, folks.... does it get any better!? Hell no! Now, Steele, you commented on Gryphon, but it doesn't matter cause you'll kick his ass anyway. All the fans want to see nowadays is good ol' Damian kick some ass, right? Man, it sure is good to know that you're on OUR side. (grins) Now, as far as ICU goes, we lost Penance....but gained one Sewerdog. What do you think of him? Do you think he's a good stablemate or a poor choice by Deathwatch? I was skeptical at first.... but I've gotten used to him. How about you? Steele: Well, hey, he's no Damian Steele, but he's a pretty good choice gained by Deathwatch. After all, that's what separates good ol' DW from other businessmen; he only makes GOOD choices. Penance was a good choice for the time that he served ICU, but.... I didn't want to bring this up, but I might as well.... Deathwatch let him go. He just wasn't satisfied with Penance, but Penance got on his knees and begged Deathwatch to let him at LEAST stay for the Terrordome interview. Being the nice guy DW is, (pauses for boos from the crowd and grins) he agreed and let Penance stay, but I guess the little punk had his own agenda. No matter. Everyone knows the little wussy Horsemen aren't fit to peel potatoes, much less wrestle with or against ICU. Sewerdog puts Penance to shame. Glad to have you aboard, Sewerdog. ZD: Really now? That's interesting, Deathwatch hadn't told me that yet, but I'm sure it's true. You are definitely not the kind of person that would lie. Now I know every interviewer asks this question, but what do ya got planned for the future in the AEWA? Looking for any titles? I've heard from the grapevine that you might be contending for the world title here pretty soon, your comments? Steele: Yeah, well, I've been tossing the idea around. I'm basically saving it for when I get bored of all of the crappy competition here in the AEWA. I'm also considering taking these pictures I took of President Freeman and.... oh, oops. Sorry, I wasn't supposed to tell anyone about that. (smirks) Let's see.... what else. Oh yeah, I wouldn't mind kicking some Horseman ass, but we'll just have to see about that. Other than that, I make the rest up as I go along. ZD: Ah, the spontaneous type. ICU material indeed. Now, you say that you know Peter Steele of Type O Negative, is this true? If so, is he cool? I heard those New Yorkers really know how to party and kick ass. (Some cheers from the crowd who are obviously from New York.) Steele: Oh, sure, Peter and I are really good friends. He's no Damian, though. In fact, just last week, he said to me, "Damian, how do you do it? How do you manage to just kick ass all over the place?" and I said, "Well, Petie, you just gotta train hard, eat your vitamins, and say your prayers." (crowd is silent) JUST KIDDING! Actually, I said, "Hey, f*ck you, man, I kick ass because I'm Damian Steele!" and then, he knew. He knew. (smirks) ZD: Well, I gotta wrap this thing up. Unlike Larkin's old show, I don't have a time limit, but I'm running out of questions to ask. So, got anything you want to add before we close this thing? Steele: Yes. I'd just like to say to all of my dear and loving fans.... The Predator is here to stay, with ICU, of course, and if you don't like it, kiss my ass, and deal with it. Adios, assholes. (waves) ZD: Well, that's it for the Zack Derringer show.... we KNOW you enjoyed it. Be here next time when I'll have a very SPECIAL surprise guest. See ya later, losers. (Derringer and Steele both walk off the set, talking to each other as they go.
