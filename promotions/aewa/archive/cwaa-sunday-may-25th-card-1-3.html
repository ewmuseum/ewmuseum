Sunday, May 25th, Card (1/3)

25 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last Week on Championship Wrestling Association of America Eric Lane: Sheridan going back up top again- I see that Masked Man back in the aisle again- what is he doing out here now? Who is he? Who is he after? Sheridan with a flying dropkick! (Enormous pop as Max is knocked halfway across the ring by the maneuver) EL: That Masked Man just slammed a chair into Joshua Barnes' back! Is that his target? Is he here to go for Barnes? He's in the ring- he grabs Sheridan by the hair! (Crowd shrieks) EL: Get him out of there! A kick in the midsection- ddt! He just ddted the Dreamer again! And it's all legal! Joshua Barnes is back on his feet- we are running out of time, I'm being told. The Masked Man helping up Max- has he been brought here to the CWAA by Max? No! (Big pop- you'll see why in a second) EL: Short clothesline on the champ by the Masked Man! He just nailed Max! *********************************************************************** Eric Lane: Good evening, everyone, and welcome back to the world-famous Sportatorium here in beautiful downtown Dallas, Texas. We've got a full slate of exciting action for you again this week, highlighted by- get this- a Texas Tornado match for the CWAA Tag Team Championship. Yes, Travis Conley, Paul Langford, Michael Anderson, and Byron McNultey- all four men in one ring at the same time, and you'll see it right here, tonight. But first, as you just saw, the Heavyweight Title match last week was marred by another appearance from that mysterious masked man in black, as he assaulted BOTH challenger and champion. and to top it all off, he has been implicated in a locker room attack on Marc Thomas, as well. Well, earlier today we caught up from "Dreamer" Randy Sheradin, who has been attacked twice by this individual. Let's hear what he had to say. ************************************************************************** ("The Dreamer" Randy Sheridan is sitting in the locker room, holding a microphone. He's already wearing his wrestling boots, with his long purple trunks bearing "Dreamer" in gold writing up one side, and wearing a "Midwest Wrestling Academy: Class of 1996" t-shirt. He does not look happy.) RS: You know, you'd have to be blind not to know that Robertson and I have a few issues left to resolve. I'll admit I don't come to the ring alone, but Josh is just there to watch my back, and everyone knows he doesn't interfere unless someone tries to pull a cheap shot. If Robertson could find a friend, he could bring him. But what does bother me is this masked man stopping Max and I from settling things. Yeah, I want to take the belt back, but mostly I want to stop Max Robertson from holding the title, because he doesn't deserve it. And Masked Man, you got in the way. Maybe last Sunday wouldn't have been my night, but thanks to you, we'll never know. Tonight, I wrestle Marc Thomas. If our show was an hour and five minutes instead of an hour, Thomas would have beaten Joey Valachi a few weeks back, and he'd have won the AEWA World Light-Heavyweight title, one of the most prestigious belts around. So I know Thomas is a major player. But I also know that I can beat him, because I've done it. Marc, maybe you think it was a fluke, but I know that anything that's been done once can be done again. Heck, maybe the fluke was you doing so well against Valachi. And after Thomas, win, lose, or draw, I'm still entitled to another shot at Robertson. And if that masked man has a problem with me, Josh, or Max, come on and do what you have to do. But have the guts to let us know you're coming, and have the courage to let the match run its course. ********************************************************************* EL: We'll see the former CWAA Heavyweight champion again later this evening, as he squares off against Marc Thomas, and I would have to think that the winner of this contest would be in the driver's seat when it comes to getting a match signed with the current champion, "The Professional" Max Robertson. We're going to get the action started real soon, but first these messages. (Monday nights at 11 PM, here on WYXZ TV Channel 39, join Jack, Janet, Chrissy, and, of course, the Ropers, for more wacky hijinks. "Three's Company" here on WYXZ TV 39!) EL: Let's go to ring announcer David Myers, and hear from the man who'll challenge Lt. Lawson for that TV title belt here later tonight. David Myers: Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome my guest at this time- (Crowd pops as Soundgarden's "My Wave" strikes up) DM: -challenger for the CWAA TV Title, Little Yvette! (Yvette heads to the ring, shaking hands with fans on the way down the aisle. For those of you who have trouble recognizing the CWAA stars, Yvette is the 5'10"-240 pounder who wears the blue and white Scott Steiner-type tights with the eagle in flight on the back.) DM: Well Little Yvette, you certainly have had an eventful start to your wrestling career. LY: Sure have, and there is no one who is more responsible for it than the fans. (Sheep that they are, they pop at the mention of their own presence. Of course, I speak metaphorically. Sheep generally do not attend CWAA events, and most sheep I have encountered seemed too disinterested in anything to do any real popping. But enough about sheep...) EL: You guys have been behind me from the start and it really pushes me to give it all I've got to put on a show for you. You guys are great. There's nothing I've dreamed of more than to be appreciated like that. (Apparently they, too, dream of appreciation, and continue to pop for themselves.) DM: Tonight you have a very difficult task ahead of you. You have been granted a TV Title Shot against Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson. What is your strategy? LY: I think I have two big advantages. One, I have the fans behind me 100% and that gives me such a huge boost. Two, no one else in this fed has the aerial ability I do, not to brag or anything, it's just not common. I think I can surprise him with stuff he hasn't seem. I'll risk myself to put on a show and to get my hands on the gold. I didn't expect this, but I'd sure love it more than anything. DM: Are you worried about Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas showing up tonight? LY: It's in the back of my mind. I really hope they have the honour and decency to let this be decided in the ring. If so maybe they'll get their chances with the winner. If not, well dad always told me never to let other people take advantage of your good nature. I'm sure Kore will be around to help. Thanks a lot, I gotta go get ready... (Pops for Yvette as he leaves...) EL: Well, speak of the devils. (Marc Thomas and Jak Hammer saunter down the aisle. Thomas' body is roughly the same size as Yvette's, and he is dressed entirely in black leather, as is his partner in crime. Jak Hammer towers over both men at 6'10"-310.) EL: This could be trouble. These men just have not gotten along. (Hammer and Thomas edge sideways past Yvette, and he and they warily keep their eyes on each other as Hammer and Thomas head for their real destination, the ring, and Yvette keep moving to the back, never ceasing to look over his shoulder.) EL: Well, I was worried for a few seconds that we might have... that there might be an incident of sorts out here, but it looks like Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas are more interested in talking than anything else... (Jak Hammer sidles over to Eric Lane and demands the microphone. Eric, a man possessed of much common sense, hands it over, and Jak proceeds to talk into the camera.) JH: Tonight, I have to face that do-gooder preacher, Kore Wrench, who I have beaten before. Wrench, if you think that this encounter is going to be any different than last time, then you have not learned what Jak Hammer is all about. At least you got the guts to keep getting into the ring with me! It seems that no one else here in the CWAA wants to take me on in a fair fight. Lawson and the Major attack me from behind and then they take out Marc, here! EL: What do you mean, "took out Marc?" Marc was apparently ambushed last week by the..... MT: By the Masked Man, yeah how can I forget that! But we have figured out who the Masked Man is and we will expose him tonight for who he is...a COWARD!!! EL: How do you plan to do that? MT: Just think about what happened last week. I was on my way to the hospital to get my ribs x-rayed, but Hammer had to stay to have a match with Lawson. Needless to say, the Major interfered and cost Hammer the match. Now use that puny mind of yours, Lane, and ask yourself, "Why would someone attack me when I was not even scheduled to wrestle that night?" Why? Because they did not want me to be there to help Jak in his match with Lawson. EL: So you're saying... MT: If you would quit interrupting me, I'll continue to finish my story! EL: I'm sure I'm sorry. MT: No, you'll be sorry if you interrupt me one more time. Now think back to the title match between Sheridan and Robertson. When the Masked Man appeared, he dropped both Sheridan and Robertson. When the locker room emptied, who came out to chase the Masked Man? EL: Well, let's see, there was Hammer and Wrench and Yvette... MT: And that's it! Now if I had been healthy, I know I would have been able to help chase the Masked Man. Notice in both cases last week that Lawson and the Major either benefited from the Masked Man or were nowhere to be found when the Masked Man was around. Draw your own conclusions. As for match tonight with Sheridan, Randy, you're still dreaming if you think you can beat me twice. Fool me once, shame on you. Fool me twice, shame on me. I always learn from my mistakes, Randy, and tonight you are going to face the best wrestler in the CWAA, bar none! Since Sheridan has been getting all of the title shots lately, I think this should be a top contenders match where the winner gets a shot at Robertson for the title. Hammer, you got anything left to say. JH: Just that Lawson and the Major better watch their backs. Payback is a (bleep) and I am going to make sure that both of you get what you deserve. LAWSON, if you've got any guts then you'll put the TV title on the line one more time in a cage match! (Hammer hops into the ring and Thomas takes his position at one of the ring posts.) EL: Assuming there are no more interruptions, let's go to David Myers for the introductions for this, our first contest. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next bout is set for one fall with a 1-minute time limit. In the ring to my left, from Detroit, Michigan, weighing in at 310 lb., accompanied by his partner, Marc Thomas, here is Jak Hammer! (The crowd does not hold Hammer or Thomas in the same esteem as they hold themselves.) DM: And his opponent- (Generic Gregorian chant begins to play, and the fans pop) DM: -from New York, New York, he weighs 275 lb., "The Preacher" Kore Wrench! (A warm reception greets the 6'4" muscled priest as he pauses to perform his pre-match ritual blessing of a small child with a good seat) DM: Your referee for this contest, Jeff Howard. EL: OK- another meeting between Jak Hammer and Kore Wrench, two men who have been competing with each other and with TV Champion Lt. Lawson in recent weeks, and we can be sure the champ is in the back watching this one intently, perhaps preparing to face the winner. OK- the Preacher getting into the ring- and Jak Hammer right away with a double axe handle to the back. Harsh kick, looks like Jak takes maybe a size 14 boot. Sends him for the ride- the Preacher ducks the clothesline! And the Preacher with a big right hand! And another! And another! Jak Hammer a little woozy- the Preacher scoops him up- that's 350 lb. he's lifting- slams him right back down! (Big pop) EL: What amazing power! The Preacher stands alone now, as Jak Hammer rolls to the floor to talk things over with Marc Thomas. What a start to this one, as we're less than a minute into the match and already both men were going at it full speed. Jak Hammer up on the apron now- and here comes the Preacher. They lock up- Hammer still not in the ring- and Jak Hammer with a rake of the eyes. That'll slow anyone down. Now Jak Hammer with a shoulder block to the midsection, and Wrench doubles over. That'll knock the wind clean out of you. What's this now? Jak Hammer sets him up- he can't be serious- he's going to suplex the Preacher out of the ring- that's concrete on that floor- no! He can't get him over- the Preacher reverses now- and Jak Hammer back in the ring the hard way! (Pop) EL: The over- 1... 2... Hammer kicks out. Now the Preacher with a standing side headlock. Look at those biceps bulge, squeezing Hammer's head like a boa constrictor. You know, later tonight, we have two big title matches, as Lt. Lawson squares off against the impressive youngster, Little Yvette. This is the biggest night of Yvette's career, I'm sure. Plus that Texas Tornado match- Jak Hammer driving to the ropes, and Senior Official Jeff Howard calls for a break. The Texas Tornado Tag Team Title tilt- say that five times fast- as Twister takes on the team of "Marvelous" Michael Anderson and "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey. Jak Hammer with a punch now. Another- blocked! And the Preacher fires back! (Pop) EL: Now a hip toss, and Jak Hammer hits the canvas. The Preacher waits on him- running clothesline puts him right back down again. Now the Preacher to a front facelock. Wearing him down with these tiring power holds. Also coming up tonight, you know that Mark Sherrick has signed an open contract, says he'll take on anyone in the whole of the CWAA who wants to get in the ring with him- well, Joshua Barnes has taken up that challenge. And Marc Thomas meets Randy Sheridan, perhaps the two best all-around wrestlers in the state of Texas to not wear a title belt. Jak Hammer back to his feet now, Wrench still with that facelock- and Jak Hammer drives him to the corner. Tremendous power in both competitors. Kore Wrench now in the corner, and Jak Hammer with a double sledge to the chest. Shades of Polish Power Ivan Putski there. Now Jak Hammer sends him for the ride- and the Preacher hits the turnbuckles. (A chant of "Go, Kore, Go! starts, and Hammer requests that the paying crowd kindly shut the Hell up.) EL: Hammer now- splashes him into the corner. The Preacher staggers to mid-ring now- and a ddt by Jak Hammer. This could be it- the cover... 1... 2... kickout! (Pop) EL: Jak Hammer now blasts him with an elbow smash to the top of the head. Now he's setting up- powerbomb coming up- he can't get him up, though- and the Preacher now back body drops him over! (Another pop as Jak Hammer falls to the canvas) EL: The Preacher escapes what could have been the jaws of defeat there. The Preacher off the ropes now- tremendous clothesline! He literally knocks Jak Hammer right out of the ring, and nearly out of his boots! Jak Hammer down on the floor, and Senior Official Jeff Howard starts his count. And now Jak Hammer grabs the Preacher by the ankle and pulls him to the outside! Big right hand. Now they're exchanging blows out on the floor- bar the door, Katie, we've got a pier six brawl! (You knew no stereotypical southern announcer could call matches for this long without saying that one) EL: The Preacher with a headbutt, and now he rams Jak Hammer's head into the apron. You can see Marc Thomas just itching to get himself involved, but this bout too important for Jak Hammer to risk getting disqualified. The Preacher now stands him up- heart punch! Jak Hammer sinks to the mat, the Preacher heading back to the ring- Hammer's got a hold of his boot! Hammer's got a hold of his boot! There's the bell- I think Jeff Howard has counted both men out! Kore Wrench stomps Jak Hammer's head to get free- he's back in there now- let's get the word from David Myers. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, the referee has declared this match a double-count-out! (The crowd always boos such decisions) EL: OK, the Preacher looks sorely disappointed as he gets the word, and Marc Thomas helping up Jak Hammer. This grueling encounter between both men must have made Lt. Lawson a happy man. There go Thomas and Hammer- we'll see more of them later on tonight, I'm sure. We're going to try to get a quick word with the CWAA Tag Team Champions next, as they prepare for what should be a wild title defense, a Texas Tornado match, even. Here comes Mother Nature now. (Mother Nature comes down the aisle, but Langford and Conley are not with her.) MN: Eric, I'm sorry about this, but Travis and Paul are in no mood for an interview right now. But I will be more than happy to talk with you for a bit. EL: How does your team feel about tonight's match? MN: For months now, we have had to watch those two morons strut around the ring, trash talk, and break every rule in the book. And every time we wrestled them, we have yet to have a clean match. So, Paul, Travis and I figured, if they are going to break the rules anyway, why not make it legal, so we can give them a dose of their own medicine. EL: We have heard reports that Paul will not be wearing the catcher's mask again. Is he fully recovered, or is he taking a risk? MN: We feel that Paul is fully recovered and he was adament that the catcher's mask would not go on his face again. But I respect the CWAA officials, and they want some type of protection, so we have reached a compromise. Paul will wear a plastic nose gaurd, just like the style that is used in the NBA. His vision will be much better, and nothing like last week will happen again. EL: Thank you for the information, Mother Nature. Any final comments? MN: Anderson, McNultey, I believe you may have made the worst mistake of your life. You could not handle my men one at a time. What makes you think you can handle them both at the same time? By the time the night is through, your names will be Dorothy and Toto, because once Twister touches down, we will send you to Oz. Eric, fans, tonight you will see the first natural disaster in the ring. And Anderson and McNultey will be nothing but rubble in the end. (Exit MN) EL: OK, when we get back from this break, Joshua Barnes steps up to the take on Mark Sherrick and his open contract!
