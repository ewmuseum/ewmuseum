AEWA — Card Archive

December Doom (12/15, part five)

22 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
************************************************* CONTINUED FROM PART FOUR ************************************************* MW: Our next contest is gonna be a BIG one. Big Ron Raider is defending his North American Title against Justin Trouble. BD: And Justin Trouble is gonna get squashed like a bug! MW: I wouldn't say that. He's a fine young athlete, and if Raider's not careful, he's not gonna have the shiny gold belt anymore when he walks outta here. LB: Of course he will. If you think that Justin Trouble has ANY chance of beating Raider, then you've got a screw loose. MW: We'll sure find out tonight, and with that, let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA North American Championship! Introducing first, the challenger, from Atlanta, Georgia, and weighing in at 238 pounds, Justiiiiiiiiiiiiin Troooooooooooouuuuuuuuuuublllllle! (Tom Petty's "Makin' Some Noise" plays loudly over the PA as Justin Trouble makes his way toward the ring, to a sizable crowd pop.) BD: Yep, he looks just about ready to be sacrificed to the Church. MW: I don't think so. He'll surprise you. LB: Yeah, he'll surprise me if he doesn't just decide to quit two minutes into the match. MW: Just watch, okay? RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, From the Rocky Mountains, he weighs in at 416 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his manager, Million Dollar Mark. He is the current AEWA North American Champion, Big... Ron.... Raaaaaaaaaaaaaaaiiiiiiiiideeeeeeeeeeer! (Lynyrd Skynyrd's "Saturday Night Special" cranks over the PA as Raider makes his entrance to a HUGE chorus of boos. He wears the NA strap proudly over his shoulder, and repeatedly shouts "Who's the Man?" at the crowd. Million Dollar Mark is at his side.) BD: Yep, we've got the makings of a classic here; a classic ass kicking! LB: It's not "Classic" without Andrew Archer. BD: You're right, how silly of me. MW: Just shut up, you guys. We've got a match to call. The champion is in the ring now, and he's trying his best to psyche out Justin Trouble, but Justin stands tall in the face of the champion. LB: Liar, he's practically peeing his pants. MW: He is not! Where do you come up with this stuff? LB: I just call it like I see it. MW: Well you've got a pretty warped view of reality. LB: Thank you. MW: It WASN'T a compliment. Anyway, the bell has just rung and Justin Trouble IMMEDIATELY goes to work on the champion! He STUNS the big guy with a FLURRY of rights and lefts! BD: That's a closed fist! Disqualify him! MW: Like you never did it in YOUR career. Trouble's got Raider reeling, and now he's going up top! Raider turns to face him, and Trouble FLIES off of the top rope, and FLOORS Raider with a picture perfect flying clothesline!! The champion is down, and Justin's going for the Trouble Maker! He picks up the legs of Raider, and he's just about got it in, BUT RAIDER COUNTERS WITH A SMALL PACKAGE!! One..Two..Kickout! LB: That's what the moron gets for thinking he could beat Raider just like that. MW: But he made it out in time. Both men are quick to their feet, and Raider NAILS the challenger with a big swinging right hand! Raider picks up Trouble now, AND GORILLA PRESSES HIM HIGH OVER HEAD! He's got him WAY up there, and he DROPS him like a bad habit! BD: I think you know what time it is! MW: Let's just call the match. Raider picks Trouble back up off the mat, and whips him to the ropes! Justin comes back, and Raider catches him with a DEVASTATING powerslam! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: How'd he get out of that? MW: Justin Trouble's no pushover, I've been telling you that since the beginning of the match. And now Raider picks Trouble up again, SHORT LARIAT--DUCKED BY JUSTIN TROUBLE!!! Trouble turns around, and catches Raider with a JAWBREAKER! BD: Lucky move. MW: Sure it was. Raider was REALLY stunned by that maneuver, and Justin follows it right up with a SENTON! The cover! One..Kickout! LB: That's about as close as Mister Trouble's gonna get to that title belt tonight. MW: We'll see. Justin brings Raider back up to his feet, and FLOORS him with a short lariat! Another cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: Okay, so THAT'S as close as he's gonna get. MW: Whatever. Raider's attempting to make it to his feet now, and Justin moves in, BACK BRAIN KICK--DUCKED BY RAIDER! Justin gets back up, but Raider is quick to the attack, just using his bulk to PLOW Trouble down! BD: Those are the advantages of outweighing your opponent by 178 pounds. MW: Yes, I suppose they are. Justin makes it back to his feet now, and Raider just NAILS him with a swinging punch. Raider scoops the challenger off his feet, BODYSLAM! LB: This is just too easy for Raider. MW: He's doing well so far, I'll give him that. Raider picks Trouble up now, and nails HIM with a short lariat! The cover! One..Kickout! BD: Why doesn't he just lay there and take a three count? It'll save him a beating. MW: That's just not in his nature, Big Daddy. Raider is quick to follow up though, and he absolutely CRUSHES Trouble with a falling splash! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: That's it, just keep working him Raider, you've almost got him. MW: And now Raider picks him up again, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! ANOTHER cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: Raider's really beating the snot out of him. MW: You could say that. Raider gets back up, and just SQUASHES Justin Trouble with a splash! The cover! One..Two..Foot on the Ropes! LB: He's just about got him now! MW: It ain't over 'till it's over. BD: You know who said that? MW: Who? BD: Yogi the Bear. MW: (under his breath) What am I gonna do with you? Raider scoops the challenger up again, POWERSLAM!! And again, the cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: This is actually pretty funny, you know that? Trouble's getting the crap kicked out of him! MW: Yes, but he refuses to go down. Raider picks Justin up again, and connects with another swinging punch! Raider whips Justin Trouble to the ropes now, he comes back, and Raider LEVELS him with a big boot! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: WHY does he keep getting up? MW: Because he CAN. Raider seems a bit upset, but goes back to work, NAILING Justin with another swinging punch! AND HE FOLLOWS IT UP WITH A BRUTAL SHORT LARIAT! The cover! One..Two..Thr..Kickout! LB: What that Hell? MW: Believe it, this boy ain't giving in. Raider can't believe it either. He picks Justin up again, and BODYSLAMS him! He's quick to follow it up, BIG SPLASH! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: This boy musta been eating his Wheaties! MW: All that and more. Raider is complaining about a slow count, but our official gives him no sympathy. Raider goes back to work now, and he lifts the challenger to his feet again. Raider sets him up here, VERTICAL SUPLEX--BLOCKED BY TROUBLE, WHO COUNTERS WITH A SUPLEX OF HIS OWN! LB: Where did THAT come from? MW: From deep down inside, that's for sure. Raider tries to get back up to his feet, but Justin NAILS him with a back brain kick! Justin grabs Raider by the head now, and takes him DOWN with a jawbreaker! BD: (yelling to the ring) Come on Raider, the guy's just a punk! MW: A punk who can WRESTLE. Justin goes to work on Raider on the ground now, STOMPING him down and DROPPING a forearm to the back! Justin picks Raider up now, and NAILS him with a DDT! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: Raider's not done, not by a longshot. MW: No, but he's getting closer. Justin is taking it to the champ now, he comes in with a SENTON, BUT RAIDER ROLLS AWAY! And Trouble hits nothing but mat! BD: See, I knew he couldn't maintain an offense against Raider for more than a minute. MW: And now Raider is back in control! He gets up to his feet, and Justin is still down! Raider moves in, BIG SPLASH! The cover! One..Two..Foot on the Ropes! LB: Drag him to the center and pin him again! MW: Raider DROPS an elbow, and now it looks like that's what he's doing! He brings Justin to his feet, and drags him to the center! Raider winds up now and FLOORS Justin with a big right hand! The cover! One..Two..Thr..Kickout! BD: He only got out there because Raider didn't use something more devastating before the cover. MW: Then I'd call that Raider's loss. Raider picks Trouble up now, and places him on the top turnbuckle! Raider's going up there too, and he NAILS Trouble with a SUPERPLEX! LB: He's in "Trouble" now, wouldn't you say? MW: Yes, he is. Raider picks Justin up again, and FLOORS him with a short clothesline! Raider picks him up off the ground now, and props him up against the turnbuckles! Raider now backs off, AND CHARGES BACK IN, AVALANCHE! Justin just SLUMPS out of the corner and falls to the floor! The cover! One..Two..Foot on the Ropes! BD: DAMN THOSE ROPES! MW: I don't think swearing at the ropes is gonna help, Big Daddy. Raider looks fed up now, though! He's dragging Trouble to the center of the ring, and he's signaling for the Raider Bomb! LB: If he hits this, it's DEFINITELY over! MW: Raider's got him in position now. He lifts Justin WAY up in the air, AND PLANTS HIM!! The cover! One..Two..Thr..KICKOUT! BD: This is bullsh*t! MW: Calm down, Big Daddy, I can't believe it either, but there's nothing you can do about it. And Raider is in a COMPLETE state of shock. What does he have to do to beat this guy? Raider just picks up Justin now, and CHUCKS him out of the ring! LB: That's what he deserves for kicking out of the Raider Bomb! MW: And now Raider's looking to go after him! He steps out onto the apron, runs, AND DIVES OUT TO THE CONCRETE--BUT TROUBLE MOVES--AND RAIDER CRASHES HEADFIRST INTO THE GUARDRAIL! BD: NO! MW: Justin now DESPERATELY tries to drag himself back into the ring, and he makes it! He's regaining his footing, while Raider is still figuring out where he is! Mark helps Raider back into the ring, where he slowly gets to his feet, AND IS MET WITH A DROPKICK! Justin Trouble appears to be back in control. LB: Where did they GET this guy? MW: From Mister and Mrs. Trouble. LB: Gee, thanks. MW: No problem, it's nice to give you guys a taste of your own medicine every now and then. And now Justin DROPS a knee to the back of Raider! Justin DRAGS Raider to the center of the ring, and KICKS him again for good measure! And now he's going up top! BD: What's he gonna try here? MW: I really don't know. Justin scales those ropes, looks back one more time, AND FLIPS BACKWARD, BEAUTIFUL MOONSAULT!! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: (shouting) That's it Raider, this punk can't take you! MW: Trouble is quick to his feet now, and DROPS another knee onto Raider! He follows it up with a FLURRY of kicks, and now he picks Raider up, and nails another jawbreaker! BD: What's wrong with Raider? MW: It's not what's wrong with Raider, it's what's RIGHT with Justin Trouble. Justin kicks Raider a few more times, and NAILS another senton! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: That's it Raider, just hang in there, bide your time and wait for your chance. MW: At this rate, he might not GET a chance. And now Justin picks Raider up, and NAILS him with a short clothesline! Raider slowly gets to his feet, and gets taken back DOWN with a Russian Legsweep! BD: He's not Russian. MW: It's the NAME of the move, Big Daddy. (Big Daddy just sits there, puzzled) MW: Oh, nevermind! And now Justin picks Raider up, and he FLOORS him with a short lariat! The cover! One..Two..Foot on the Ropes! BD: Thank GOD for those ropes! MW: You were damning them just a little while ago. LB: Don't try to make sense out of what he says, Wilson, I never have. MW: I see. And now it looks like Trouble is looking to end it! He picks up Raider by the legs, and he's going for the Trouble Maker again! BD: And he won't get it again. MW: Justin turns over on the move, AND HE LOCKS IT IN! Raider is SCREAMING! He's in SERIOUS pain! LB: Come on Raider, fight it! MW: Raider reaches DESPERATELY for the ropes, AND MARK PUSHES THEM IN TOWARDS HIM!! Raider's got a hold of them now, and the ref is forcing the break! BD: Yes, he's forcing Justin Trouble to back off, but he doesn't see what Mark's doing. MW: You're right! Million Dollar Mark has just grabbed the BELL from the timekeeper's table, and he handed it to Raider! LB: Yep, and it's gonna go DING when it connects with that Trouble Maker's head! MW: The referee is still holding off Trouble, and Raider makes it back to his feet! He turns back toward the ring, and lifts that bell high overhead, BUT JUSTIN CATCHES HIM IN THE FACE WITH A DROPKICK! AND RAIDER DROPS THE BELL ON HIS OWN HEAD!!!! BD: He cheated! Ring the bell! MW: It just rang, on Raider's head! The bell falls to the mat, and the timekeeper is quick to grab it back, but Raider looks to be OUT on his feet! Justin moves in, AND CATCHES RAIDER IN A SMALL PACKAGE!! One..Two..Three!!!! (DING DING) LB: That did NOT just happen. RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of twenty-two minutes and twenty-seven seconds, here is your winner, and NEEEEEEEEEEEEW AEWA North American Champion.... JUSTIN TROUBLE!! MW: And Justin Trouble pulls off the impossible, beating the practically unbeatable Big Ron Raider. Raider had been breezing through the competition for some time now, but he ran into some trouble tonight, Justin Trouble. BD: Oh, quit the bull crap! Raider shoulda won this match and you know it! MW: I know nothing of the sort. He never pinned Justin, and he lost because he tried to bend the rules, and it backfired. When you mess with fire, sometimes you get burned. Let's not take anything away from this match though, it was a CLASSIC! Both men wrestled the matches of their lives, I'm almost sorry there had to be a loser. LB: No you're not. Admit it, you're glad that Raider doesn't have the belt anymore. MW: That may be true, but that's only because I disagree with his way of doing things, he is still a great wrestler and deserves to be commended for this match. BD: Yeah, whatever. Hey, is this guy gonna pose with the belt all night or something? We got other matches to watch. MW: He's just enjoying the moment, Big Daddy. And so are these fans, they love it! But now Justin is leaving to a HUGE, well-deserved ovation, and I think we're about ready for our next match. LB: Which is? ********************************************************************* TO BE CONTINUED IN PART SIX ********************************************************************* \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- American E-Wrestling Association The AEWA-- We Deliver!
