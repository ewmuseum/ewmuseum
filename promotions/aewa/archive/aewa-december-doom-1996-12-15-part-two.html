AEWA — Card Archive

December Doom (12/15, part two)

22 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
************************************************************* CONTINUED FROM PART ONE ************************************************************* MW: The Machines are about to defend their National tag team titles against Graphic Violence. These teams have a LOT of hatred for each other. LB: Jeez, they've been feuding for MONTHS. After tonight, though, Graphic Violence will have those National belts around their waists. MW: While we have a minute, let's go over the events leading up to our next match. The Machines were undefeated AEWA National Tag Team Champions, on a roll unlike any newly arriving team in the history of the AEWA. Graphic Violence had spent months and months nursing a record of mediocrity and fighting only sporadically. Suddenly, in the midst of the Visigothic Horde-Graveyard Shift feud, Graphic Violence was signed for what the Shift saw as a warm-up match. The Shift lost via submission, thanks to outside interference, to Graphic Violence, and suddenly we had a new player in the upper echelon of AEWA Tag Teams. Out of midcard status, and into a matchup with the Machines for the National titles. But Graphic Violence wasn't satisfied. They decided to intimidate the opposition with a parking-lot exercise in vandalism, destroying Simon O'Neal's car. Few people in the industry shed any tears for one of the most arrogant, smug, and under-handed cheaters in the game. According to many, he had this kind of thing coming. But our cameras caught Simon breaking the news to partner Paul Wong, as upstanding a professional as any I have met. And it was revealed that the car in question belonged to Paul Wong, not Simon O'Neal. What difference does it make? This much difference. (Plays a videotape of the ending of the last matchup between the two teams, as mild-mannered Paul Wong out-cheats his own partner and blasts Graphic Violence in their heads with a metal folding chair) MW: The Machines suffered the first loss of their AEWA careers when Paul lost his temper. And Simon must be thrilled to see a partner as vicious as he has been. The AEWA has mandated a rematch, here tonight, and with no disqualifications. Leech Rogers and Booze Murphy clearly were cut out for such a match. Simon O'Neal has built a career out of covert illegal acts in the ring-- with no rules to enforce, we may get to see just how far he would go. But what about Paul Wong? Is he really prepared for this type of action? And will his temper again cause him to make that one big mistake? (Replays the videotape of the last GV-Machines match ending with Paul swinging that chair) BD: All hell is about to break loose, folks. These two teams have so much hatred for each other, something has to give. LB: Here we go.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team match is scheduled for one fall, and is for the AEWA National tag team titles! Introducing first, at a combined weight of 533 pounds, being led to the ring by Miss DeMeanor, GRAPHIC VIOLENCE! ("Here Comes the Highstepper" plays as Leech Rogers and Booze Murphy make their way to ringside. Miss DeMeanor follows closely behind.) BD: These guys look ready for business. MW: Here they are, getting ready for the biggest match in their AEWA careers, a title opportunity on a pay per view extravaganza. Ladies and gentlemen, I think Graphic Violence have officially arrived. BD: Well, they lacked real direction earlier. They didn't have any kind of plan, and seemed more interested in a personal feud with members of the Proctor posse back in the spring than in establishing themselves as real title contenders. That has all changed. RING ANNOUNCER: And their opponents, at a combined weight of 492 pounds, the National tag team champions, THE MACHINES! ("Welcome to the Machine" by Pink Floyd plays as Paul Wong and Simon O'Neal arrive, gray fedora hats atop their heads and sunglasses on their faces. Simon is wearing his trademark glove, as always.) MW: And we are under way.... Looks like both teams are already breaking all the rules, and we have a Texas Tornado match of sorts on our hands. All four men are in the ring, and Simon is taking it easy as his partner vents his anger at both opponents. LB: Paul connects with a series of punches and kicks, backing both members of Graphic Violence into the corner. Paul has Leech backed against Booze in the corner, and now he's waving for Simon. BD: Simon heads for the opposite corner, and here he comes! He's gonna squash his own teammate! MW: No, Paul dives out of the way, and it's Simon with a running splash into the corner-- SLAMMING BOOZE AND LEECH INTO THE TURNBUCKLES! This isn't going well for the challengers at all. BD: The best thing Graphic Violence could do right now would be to get out of the ring, gather their wits and their breath, and maybe pull a weapon of some sort out. No disqualifications, after all. MW: Well, it looks like they're going to stay in there for now. Simon monkey flips Booze into mid ring! LB: And Paul has Leech down in the corner, and has his boot up against Leech's throat, and all of his weight is coming down on his opponent's windpipe. BD: Meanwhile, Booze tumbles to the floor, and takes a wild swing at, well, no one. Everyone else is in the ring, and Booze may as well be on Mars. Paul and Simon both have Leech in the middle of the ring now, and they heave him up for a double front layout suplex. Simon takes a look outside and sees Booze still staggering, and there he goes! (Simon completes a very nice-looking plancha. However, he hit it on the guard rail, since Booze had stumbled just out of harm's way without knowing what was happening.) LB: They call those "high-risk" maneuvers for a reason. Now we've got two guys out on the floor, and the sound of Simon hitting that rail must have yanked Booze back into the world of the living. MW: I think you're right. Booze has remembered where he is and why he's there, and is now working over Simon. Paul, meanwhile, is still exacting his revenge on Leech; and he's up for a press slam, AND DOWN HE GOES! He just hoisted Leech right over his head and literally THREW him. Meantime, Booze is heading back to the ring, and he has something in his hand. It looks like.... AN UMBRELLA? BD: I got it! He's going to open it up indoors and give the Machines seven years of bad luck! MW: I think he grabbed it out of the front row, actually, and he just slapped Paul in the back of the head with it. It looks like it stung, but it doesn't look like it'll cause Paul any serious problems. He turns around and locks up with Booze. Simon is back on his feet and on the ring apron, and Paul whips Booze into the ropes, and Booze and Simon just bumped heads. Booze slumps to the mat, but Simon is back on the floor again, and that really didn't sound good at all. LB: And Leech is back in the ring as Paul looks after his friend. Leech with a running start, AND HE JUST CLIPPED PAUL'S LEGS! MW: He just rolled right into them, and it looks like his shoulder drove into Paul's knees. Now he has Paul down and is working the knee over, with a few stomps, and an elbow drop. LB: Now Leech leaves Paul and starts shaking Booze. Booze is regaining his wits, and Leech goes back over to Paul and turns him-- what is he doing?-- A SCORPION DEATHLOCK! He's got Paul in a scorpion deathlock. BD: And here's why he went to get Booze back up-- Booze has a chair and SLAMS it right into Paul's head! And AGAIN! Paul has his legs trapped in the scorpion deathlock, and has no defense for the chair shots! The ref is calling for the bell! Did Paul just submit? MW: I didn't hear it, but it is hard to tell with all the noise from that chair. Leech is releasing the hold, and-- well, I think I see what happened. The referee is stopping the fight and awarding the match to Graphic Violence; a TKO of sorts, I suppose. Let's go to the ring for the official announcement. RING ANNOUNCER: At 15:16, here are your winners, by referee's decision, and NEW AEWA NATIONAL TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS, Booze Murphy and Leech Rogers, the team of GRAPHIC VIOLENCE! MW: Graphic Violence take the National Tag Team titles from the Machines, and I believe it is the first time the Machines have lost without being disqualified since joining the AEWA. Simon is back on his feet again, but Paul is injured in the ring, and it looks like he's going to need some kind of assistance getting out of there. BD: Simon's helping Paul back to the locker room now; he looks hurt. Graphic Violence, on the other hand, look absolutely FABULOUS! LB: They just grabbed the belts, and Miss DeMeanor is dancing all over the place. What a great victory for these guys. MW: It's a shallow victory, Bertha. The Machines STILL haven't ever been pinned, so Graphic Violence can't really feel TOO confident. BD: At this point, Graphic Violence can do whatever the hell they want to. MW: Well, it's time for our next bout. Rick Showboat is about to square off against The Gryphon, and this should be an EXCELLENT match. BD: During a recent program, Rick Showboat started complaining about a fast count at the conclusion of one of his matches-- and he BRUTALLY attacked the referee. The Gryphon ran to ringside and chased Showboat away; now they're finally going to settle the score. LB: Ricky was just having a little fun, but that big idiot had to come out and spoil the party. I really can't stand the Gryphon. BD: I don't like him either, but Showboat's even worse. I've been looking forward to this match, because I want to see Rick get his ASS kicked. This should be HILARIOUS. MW: Let's just watch.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, the challenger, From Palmdale, California, he weighs in at 226 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his manager, Doctor Love, he is a two-time former AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion of the World, RICK SHOOOOOOOOWBOOOOOAAAAAAT! (The theme from "The Love Boat" plays loudly over the PA as Showboat makes his way toward the ring, to a HUGE round of boos from the crowd. He wears an black tuxedo, with a red rose sticking out of the pocket. Doctor Love is at his side, wearing a gaudy yellow suit.) MW: Showboat hasn't had much luck in the Heavyweight ranks here in the AEWA lately. LB: He'll do just fine, Wilson. BD: Personally, I really enjoyed kicking the crap out of him! Showboat is VERY overrated. LB: You SHUT UP, Big Daddy! I'm not in the mood for your crap tonight, so just shut up. MW: (laughing) Please, children, stop fighting! Anyway, here comes The Gryphon now, and he looks ANGRY! RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 310 pounds, here is THE GRYPHON! (The Gryphon makes his way to the ring, wearing his long black cape. He receives a big pop from the crowd, but stares directly at Showboat.) MW: The Gryphon outweighs Showboat by a lot; that could be a major advantage for him. LB: But Rick has such wrestling ability, it will be NO problem. It's like the old saying goes-- the bigger they are, the harder they fall! BD: His "wrestling ability" was no match for me and MY skills, Bertha. I think Showboat is in for a BIG surprise tonight. LB: Let's get serious, Big Daddy. Your "skills" involved pulling out a pair of brass knuckles and hitting him with them! BD: Yeah, so what's your point?! MW: Once again, I must ask you two to stop fighting and pay attention to the match. It just started, and Showboat just NAILED The Gryphon with a standing dropkick! LB: See what I mean? Showboat is going to dominate the heavyweight ranks just like he dominated the light heavyweight ranks! MW: Now Showboat whips The Gryphon into the corner-- he charges in, and NAILS him with a clothesline! The Gryphon is staggering! BD: Showboat takes the Gryphon's legs out from under him with a leg sweep! He hooks the leg! One....Kickout! BD: Showboat is just too small! He might be able to get the big man down, but he'll NEVER be able to pin him. MW: Showboat runs to the ropes, he comes back at the Gryphon with a cross body block, BUT THE GRYPHON CATCHES HIM IN MID-AIR! He lifts him high over his head, and drops him down hard! LB: (yelling) NO! Get up, Rick! MW: The Gryphon hits Showboat with a swinging neckbreaker! That had a LOT of impact. Here's the cover.... One......Two....Kickout! MW: The Gryphon whips Showboat into the corner! He lifts him onto the turnbuckle, and goes for a superplex-- BUT SHOWBOAT COUNTERS WITH A CROSS BODY BLOCK! The Cover! One....Two....Kickout! BD: In order for Showboat to win, he MUST keep this match going fast and furious. He's MUCH faster than The Gryphon, and that's his only real advantage. MW: Showboat runs at The Gryphon and hits him with a flying clothesline! Now he drops a BIG elbow on The Gryphon's back! LB: There's my Rick! He's going to take over NOW! MW: Showboat hits The Gryphon with a Russian legsweep! He picks him up, and NAILS him with a DEVASTATING bulldog! The cover.... One....Two...Kickout! MW: Now Showboat whips him to the ropes, but The Gryphon reverses it! HE PLANTS SHOWBOAT WITH A SPINEBUSTER! The Cover! One......Two...Kickout! MW: The Gryphon grabs Showboat, and just SLAMS him down with a gutwrench suplex! Now he drops a BIG leg on him! BD: Look at Showboat, he's getting completely mauled. MW: And the Gryphon hits Showboat with a backbreaker! He pulls him up, and hits him with a backspin DDT! He goes for the cover.... One....Two...Kickout! LB: Rick still has some fight left in him; he's just getting warmed up. MW: Yeah right, Bertha. And now the Gryphon tosses Rick Showboat right out of the ring! He follows him out, AND HITS HIM WITH A VERTICAL SUPLEX ON THE CONCRETE FLOOR! He picks up Showboat, but Rick rakes his eyes! LB: Rick is finally going to take control! MW: It sure looks that way. Now Showboat hits the Gryphon with a series of right hands, and he rolls him back into the ring. Showboat lifts the Gryphon up, and hits him with a back suplex! LB: Rick is in TOTAL control right now. MW: Now Showboat's climbing to the top turnbuckle, an area that he's not too familiar with. He jumps, and NAILS the Gryphon with a flying fistdrop! LB: And he's giving the sign for the Corporate Buy-Out! If he can apply that move, it's all over. MW: AND HE LOCKS IT ON! Showboat is REALLY applying some pressure now, folks, and the Gryphon is SCREAMING in agony! LB: To my knowledge, NO ONE has ever broken out of this maneuver. The Gryphon might be big, but he's just in too pain.... BD: And the Gryphon just submitted! (DING DING) Jeez, I just HATE it when Showboat wins. LB: He earned this victory, Big Daddy. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner, due to a submission, RICK SHOWBOAT! MW: The Gryphon put up a good fight, but he just couldn't break out of the Corporate Buy-Out. That's a VERY dangerous move. BD: And look at Rick Showboat now; he's dancing around the ring like an IDIOT. (shakes his head in disgust) I really hate that moron. LB: So what's next, Wilson? *************************************************************************** TO BE CONTINUED IN PART THREE *************************************************************************** \ | | | / ( o o ) ------ooO-(_)-Ooo------- American E-Wrestling Association The AEWA-- We Deliver!
