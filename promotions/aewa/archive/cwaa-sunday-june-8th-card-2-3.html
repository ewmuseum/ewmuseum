Sunday, June 8th, Card (2/3)

8 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RS: Tonight, Josh and I had a match with a couple of old troublemakers. Everyone's favorite invalids, Double Jeopardy. Eddie, Gary, we've seen all your tricks, and we're in a really bad mood, so your odds...they just weren't good. But now I want to talk about the reason we're in a bad mood...Max Robertson's little ultimatum last week. Max, obviously you're so scared to face the top contenders you'd...what the hell? {Joshua Barnes suddenly picks up an empty chair and smashes it to the ground, shocking the audience. He hops into the ring and grabs the mic away from Sheridan.} JB: Max! I'm so samn-damn sick of this. You stole Randy's title, you've cheated both of us out of a bunch of matches, now you're trying to make us fight each other.... I'll be right back. EL: Where's he going? JB: I'm going to find that ba-bastard in the locker room and cripple him and we can get rid of him for good. {Crowd pop for that idea. Barnes starts to storm off, but Sheridan holds him back, which is pretty impressive considering the size difference.} RS: Josh, calm down. There's a solution to this. {Takes the mic away.} Marc Thomas, Jak Hammer, if you guys are listening, come out here. We need to talk. EL: Wow! Let's stay here for a few minutes and see what develops. Randy Sheradin and Joshua Barnes calling for Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas to come to the ring to settle this. Are we going to see Max's tag team match take place right here? (Many heads crane toward the direction of the locker area until Hammer and Thomas appear and slowly enter to a big heel pop. Barnes eyes the two men suspiciously. Jak and Thomas are again dresses in black leather. Hammer stands 6'10"-310 with a Sid-like physique, while his partner is built more along the lines of Chris Benoit at 6'0"-240. Both men stand on the ring apron and eye Sheradin and Barnes carefully.} EL: This could be trouble. RS: All right. You guys and Josh and I aren't going to agree on much, so I'll just keep this confined to our mutual problem. All four of us believe we're entitled to a shot at Robertson, but he refuses to face any of us until we either cripple each other, or tear our respective friendships apart. Well, we need to settle which one of us is next in line, and we need to do it without playing into Robertson's hands. [Hammer and Thomas glare in silence, and Sheradin and Barnes both look a little wary.] JB: Umm.. how about... maybe a coin flip? Whoever wins the coin toss wins the match, and those two can fight for the title shot. Does... does that sound like an idea? EL: Joshua Barnes offering to skip the tag match and just let two of the men fight foir the title shot. We've got the Trailer Park Assassins in the back waiting for a- hold on. (Hammer and Thomas head through the ropes- well. Hammer walks in over the top rope, actually- and join Sheradin and Barnes in mid-ring. As Barnes and Sheradin assume a defensive posture, Thomas nods.) MT: Go ahead. Heads, me and you (points to Sheradin). Tails, you two. (Points to Barnes and Hammer.) EL: OK- David Myers in the ring now- I wouldn't want to be in his shoes- he's got, what is that, a quarter? Maybe a nickel. OK- he shows all foru men- I think he said it was "tails". So Joshua Barnes and Jak Hammer will- hey! Jak Hammer just kicked Randy Sheradin right in the gut- he's almost 200 lb., bigger than the Dreamer! Marc Thomas smashed Joshua Barnes with the microphone! Jak Hammer now lifts Sheradin by the throat- choking him out, and Marc Thomas continues to use that microphone on Joshua Barnes. Someone get in there! Jak Hammer on the Dreamer- is there going to be a match after all? Thomas now choking Barnes with that microphone cord. Here comes the cavalry! (Pop as Cooter and Orbit race to the ring, chairs in hand, followed by Jimmy Dangerous, Kore Wrench and Travis Conley) EL: OK- Hammer and Thomas back out to the floor as the Trailer Park Assassins and friends come to the rescue- we need to take a quick break- we'll try to have things sorted out when we get back. (The WYXZ-TV Ch. 30 movie of the week comes to you Wednesday nights at 8- all the biggest stars in all the biggest films. This week, George Burns is you-know-who as he appears before John Denver in the wacky "Oh, God!" This Wednesday at 8, only on WYXZ-TV Ch. 39) EL: We're back. Randy Sheradin and Joshua Barnes have returned to the locker room- Barnes needed to be restrained from going back out after Hammer and Thomas, who wait in then ring with the Trailer Park Assassins- I don't know what's going to happen about the tag team challenge match- according to that coin flip, it would be a match between, I believe, Josh Barnes and Jak Hammer, but it looks like we're going with the scheduled tag match with the Assassins here. Hold on- Marc Thomas takes the mike back. MT: No one is going to tell me who I have to wrestle and when I have to wrestle them. I had enough of that crap in the AEWA where the Light Heavyweight champion always seemed to find some idiot to wrestle rather than me. Max, you can give the title shot to that coward the Masked Man, because I would rather beat his (bleep) than yours. Yeah, Hammer and I have had some problems, but we are as solid as any team in the CWAA and no one, not Max, not Sheridan, and not that (bleep) title are going to get between me and Jak. EL: He's got to remember that this is a family program- JH: Right, Marc. As for the Trailer Park Trash (Cooter appears to bristle at that remark, but Orbit just doesn't understand what's going on) we have to wrestle tonight, the only things you could kill are time and a buffet. Both of you morons are an insult to wrestling. Why don't you just go back home with your cousins and sit in front of a bug lamp with a six pack? You'll have more fun and you won't have to get your butts kicked by me and Marc. Lawson, I still want another shot at you and that title and Yvette, you lucky little freak, if you think that you can beat me again, then step into the ring with me one more time. We'll see who is the better man! DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next bout is set for one fall with a twenty-minute time limit. Introducing first, in the ring to my left, at a combined weight of 550 lb., the team of Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas! (Intense booing, Texas-mark style) DM: And their opponents, in the ring to my right, from Weasel, Arkansas, at 595 lb., Cooter Lee Roy and Orbit, the Trailer Park Assassins! (The TPAs have many a kindred spirit in the world-famous Sportatorium) DM: Your referee, Jeff Howard. EL: OK- we are underway, and I'm being told that there will be no unscheduled match between Jak Hammer and Joshua Barnes this week after all. We hope to talk to the champ before the end of the evening about what he has seen and heard here tonight. Jak Hammer and Cooter start it off- an exchange of blows- and Hammer with a kick to the midsection. And now he smacks Cooter into the top turnbuckle and tags in Marc Thomas. Hammer holds him open, and Thomas with a kick in the ribs. Thomas to an arm bar- you know, he is the one with the most to lose in all of this over the title shot, as he is the official number-one contender- open hand chop- and Cooter responds in kind! (Pop) EL: Thomas tags as Cooter sends him for the ride- Cooter setting for a backdrop-but Hammer the legal man now with a double axe handle. Now another. He sets the big man up- piledriver- no- poerbomb coming up- he got him. Cooter in trouble early- 1... 2... and Orbit makes the save. Referee Chris Herman ordering Orbit out of the ring, and Marc Thomas tags in. Double clothesline by Hammer and Thomas on Cooter lee Roy. They send him for the ride- (Pop) EL: -and Cooter with a double clothesline of his own! Now he scoops Marc Thomas up- big body slam! He turns to Hammer- he has him up- 350 lb.!- and a body slam! Awesome power displayed by Cooter. Thomas into the ropes- shoulderblock by Cooter. Hammer wincing in pain on the ring apron. And now Orbit is tagged in! Sets Thomas- vertical suplex. Now Orbit to an arm bar. Not the swiftest, that Orbit, but he does know his way around that ring. Very technically sound competitor. Thomas with a handful of tights- a braver man than me- and he uses the leverage advantage to pull Orbit to the ropes. Irish whip- and Orbit puts him on his back with a shoulderblock! Orbit off the ropes again- another shoulderblock! Elbow drop- 1... Thomas kicks out. Not going as Thomas and Hammer planned. Cooter tags back in- right to an arm bar. National stars, the Trailer Park Assassins, and this will likely be their last stop in the world-famous Sportatorium during this tour of the country, but I'm sure we'll be seeing them again soon. Wildly popular wherever they go, these two. Thomas with a handful of hair to drag Cooter to the ropes- Chris Herman calls for the break- and Thomas with an open handed chop. Sends Cooter to the corner- they heard that chop all the way back in Weasel, Arkansas. And Thomas with a devastating elbow smash to the chest as Cooter bounces from the corner. ("Go, Cooter, Go!") EL: The fans trying to rally the Trailer Park Assassins, but it won't be easy. Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas undefeated as a tag team. Marc Thomas drops the elbow. Thomas and Hammer have regained control of this one. Jak tags in again. Hammer stands him up- punch-blocked! Cooter with a right hand! (Pop) EL: Hammer with a right- blocked! Cooter with a left cross! Now Cooter off the ropes- clothesline! And Orbit tags back in! (Pop again) EL: Orbit with a headbutt on the giant from Detroit. And another! Here comes Marc Thomas- orbit biting Jak Hammer on the forehead- Cooter just flattened Thomas with a big soupbone right hand. Chris Herman getting between Cooter and Thomas- Orbit has Hammer in the corner- Orbit up on the middle rope pounding away- Chris Herman sending Cooter back to his corner. Look out! (Boos) EL: While Chris Herman was running Cooter out of there- Marc Thomas just yanked Orbit off the turnbuckle and nailed him with that Icebreaker of his! Hammer with the cover- 1... 2... 3! No! Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas get the win- Marc Thomas illegally hit him from behind with that deadly Icebreaker while Chris Herman's back was turned! Orbit never knew what hit him! (We'll let that last go unelaborated...) DM: Here are your winners, Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas! (As "Bad to the Bone" plays, the duo head back to the locker room. Cooter tries in vain to explain to Orbit what has happened) EL: when we get back from this message, we'll see the CWAA TV Title match, as Lt. Lawson and "the Preacher" Kore Wrench go at it one more time, and maybe we'll talk to the Heavyweight Champion about what happened. (The Battle Klash II promo runs again) EL: OK- we're ready for the first of our two title bouts- Lt. Lawson and the Preacher. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is set for one fall with a thirty-minute time limit and is for the CWAA Television Championship! Introducing first, the challenger- (Gregorian Chant starts to play to a big pop) DM: -from New York, New York, at 275 lb., here is "the Preacher" Kore Wrench! (The long-haired, muscled persist appears in his usual ornamental robe and priestly garb. He stands 6'4"-275, and asks to borrow Myers' house mike.) KW: Every week, Marc Thomas and Jak Hammer... every single week. This all began during my last match with Lawson, and I don't think it's over yet. I have business with Lawson to conduct, and I'd like to do it without interference. This doesn't concern you, Thomas and Hammer. But somehow, I doubt you'll be able to resist. So come on down! Interfere with my match! I dare you! I'll fight you all! Lawson, Thomas, Hammer, Mayhem... it doesn't matter. I have no fear. DM: His opponent- (When Johnny comes marching home again the crowd gets ugly) DM: -accompanied to the ring by his commanding officer, Major Victor Augustus Mayhem, weighing in at 219 lb., from Poughkeepsie, New York, here is the CWAA Television Champion, Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson! Your referee, Chris Herman. {The CWAA Television Champion, with his brown crew cut and military fatigues capping off the 5'11"-219 lb. frame, and his superior officer head down to ringside. Life is not good for Eric Lane, as the Major decides to visit him today.} MVM: Once again, the great Lt. Lawson is here to defend this country from the evil conspiracy led by dreaming peaceniks like Sheridan, Commies like Hammer-and-Sickle, Secret Iceland Spies like Marc Thomas, and Third-World Immigrants like Jimmy Dangerous! Last week, we had that Twisted fellow, and his little tramp ended up lying to me and handcuffing me. {Chuckles to himself} Reminded me of this geisha house I visited in '52... anyways, despite your feeble attempts to capture, interrogate, and torture me, the good Lt. came through and defeated your sick Twisted loser. Tonight, we have the chaplain... or we are SUPPOSED to have to face the chaplain. However, Subsection 3514, paragraph 3, line 2 of the official Switzerland Peace Accords, explicitly states that "Religious Officers are not allowed to be harmed during battle." Therefore, you cannot have this match tonight. DL: Kore Wrench... your occupation has saved you from a beating tonight. I'm glad- I certainly did not want to cripple a man of the cloth. I suggest that you do not get in my way in the future. (Lawson and the Major turn to leave, waving the military codebook at the ring as they go, as though that would explain their actions to Kore Wrench and Chris Herman.) EL: Well, the champ says there will be no match tonight, and there he goes. (Chris Herman takes the house mike as Lawson gets halfway up the aisle, and, for the first time in CWAA history, we hear the blonde-haired, blue-eyed referee) CH: Lawson, I'm a-gonna coun' tah ten, ahn if you ain't in the ring bah then, I'm a gonna' coun' you ou'! (The fans pop for Chris, perhaps for the first and last time in his career. Evanescent approval is never a good substitute for a series of elocution lessons.) EL: Well, Chris Herman laying down the law to the TV champ, and Lawson and the Major are just livid. The Major is beet red- I don't know how they could expect anything different. (Lawson continues to hold up his little codebook, still laboring under the delusion that a) Chris Herman has any idea what it is supposed to mean, and b) that if he did know, he would care.) EL: Chris Herman starting his count- and listen to the crowd counts along with him! (Herman gets to 8 before Lawson steps though the ropes, as the Major takes his seat next to Eric Lane.) MVM: When the General hears about this... EL: OK, we are underway, there's the bell. The two men, champion and challenger in the ring. Wrench and Lawson circle one another warily. Wrench to the - feigns a grab to the head. Collar and elbow tie up- and the Lt. pushing the Preacher to the corner. MVM: The chaplain has the power advantage, but the Lt. is smarter and has the leverage advantage- see how far down he lowered himself to get under the chaplain? EL: Chris Herman calling for the clean break... and he gets it! Lawson backs off. Collar and elbow tie up again- Lawson to a standing side headlock. Now a headlock takedown, and Lawson in control early on. MVM: Of course he's in control- he's a commissioned officer. This chaplain's an enlisted man, and not the best of them, either. EL: Two words, Major: "Section 8." MVM: Why? Who's sitting there? Is it that woman with the handcuffs? (The Major spits on his hands and begins arranging his two hairs.) EL: And Kore Wrench powers out into a hammerlock on the mat on Lawson. MVM: I don't see anyone. Which way is Section 8? EL: Lawson grabs the bottom rope. Chris Herman calls for the break- Wrench reluctant to release the hold. MVM: Break it, ref. Break it. You know, the quality of officiating in this regimen is at an all-time low. How can we ever hope to contain the Communist threat with officiating like this? EL: Lawson up now, and he has a few words for referee Chris Herman. Another tie up now, and the powerful Preacher to a side headlock. Look at those biceps. MVM: I agree- the Lt. is in the best shape of his career as he prepares for the upcoming offensive against that welfare-state punk. EL: Well, you've jumped the gun on me, Major- MVM: A good pre-emptive strike always catches the enemy with his guard down. EL: -I'm the enemy? (Lawson tries to send Wrench for an Irish whip, but Wrench keeps the headlock on, effectively dragging the TV champ across the ropes.) MVM: With enemies like you, who needs friends. EL: Anyway- as I was trying to say- (The same sequence of headlock- aborted Irish whip- headlock drag recurs) EL: - we have been informed that the lt., assuming he retains his title tonight, is on his way to AEWA Battle Klash II to defend the CWAA TV Title against English Light heavyweight sensation Fuse, a new arrival in the AEWA who may be, pound for pound, the toughest competitor to date in the AEWA. (Lawson turns Wrench's arm and converts the headlock into one of those old-style tests-of-strength with the forearms, as opposed to the mercy-game type) EL: Should the Preacher win the title here tonight, next week he'll go into his match, signed earlier tonight, against Mark Sherrick as TV Champion, and whoever emerges from that one with the title would go to Battle Klash II against Fuse. The Lt., in the meantime, faces Jak Hammer again next week. (Wrench powers his way back to a standing side headlock) MVM: The Hammer-and-Sickle- don't think he uses the name "Hammer" for no reason- and what kind of American spells his name J-A-K anyway- sounds almost Polish or Czech to me, and you know what that means. EL: Major, the cold war ended 8 years ago. MVM: That's what they WANT you to think. (The test-of-strength back into a headlock happens again) EL: We're going to take a break- when we get back, we'll have more TV Title action!
