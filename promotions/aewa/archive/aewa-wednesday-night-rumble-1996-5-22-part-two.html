Wednesday Night Rumble (5/22, part two)

25 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MW: Lopez nails Valachi with an elbow smash, and now he's climbing the turnbuckle.... LB: (screaming) He connects with a BIG flying dropkick! This is horrible! MW: Valachi has already been knocked unconscious-- and Lopez goes for the cover! One....Two....(kickout) LB: He ALMOST had him there! I hate to admit it, but "Flying Mexican" looks pretty damn good! MW: (laughing) For once, we both agree! LB: Lopez whips Valachi right into that cage wall! Can we gets some help out here? MW: Of course not! "The Cardiac Kid" doesn't have ANY friends in the AEWA anymore! LB: Lopez pounds him with a kick to the face, and Valachi is begging for mercy. MW: (laughing) I love it! LB: Now Lopez executes a stunning moonsault off that top turnbuckle-- and he's going for the cover....One....Two....Three! (ding ding) MW: That match was less than 3 minutes long! What exactly just happened? LB: (looks shocked) I have no idea! Joey Valachi didn't execute ONE freaking move! RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner-- "The Flying Mexican" Juan Lopez! MW: That was REALLY strange.... It was almost like Valachi TRIED to lose the match! Maybe he has some kind of a plan. LB: (gets an idea) Of course! He didn't even CARE about the match-- it was all an act! MW: But why would Valachi ever LET someone beat him? That doesn't make any sense! LB: We'll try to get an interview with him soon, and find out. But until then.... (Several large men begin walking to the cage, and they're all wearing black coats. They hand an envelope to Valachi, and immediately leave) MW: Wait a minute! Who were those guys? LB: I have no idea, but Valachi looks very happy right now. He's tearing open the envelope, and there's money inside! What's this all about? MW: Maybe he's making a deal with some sort of manager-- or even another wrestler! (shouting) But WHO WERE THOSE MEN? LB: I don't know.... Maybe Valachi will tell us about it on Friday! He sure seems happy right now. MW: "The Cardiac Kid' has pulled off some weird stunts in the past, but this one is just TOO weird! The cage is being taken down, and we're ready for another match. LB: So what's up next? MW: Well, Sgt. Mike "Destroyer" Williams will be taking on Colonel Fear in a Philadelphia Street Fight. And, of course, in a Philadelphia Street Fight, there are no countouts, no DQ's, and Falls-Count-Anywhere! LB: Yes, Colonel Fear has already retired one pathetic patriot, American Ambush, and now he's going to cripple another one! MW: I wouldn't say that. Sgt. Williams is an outstanding competitor, and if Colonel Fear intends to put him away, he's gonna have a VERY tough time. LB: No way! Our American competitors are pathetic compared to the great athletes that come out of Iran! MW: I think you'll find that the competition here is among the finest in the world. Our athletes can hold their own with the best of the best, and Colonel Fear is going to find that out tonight! Let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is a Philadelphia Street Fight and is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from Teheran, Iran, he weighs in at 262 pounds and is accompanied by his manager, Bootknife Kelly, Colonel Feeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeear! (The Iranian National Anthem plays as Colonel Fear makes his entrance, to a large round of boos. Bootknife Kelly is close behind him, waving the Iranian flag.) MW: Look at this despicable individual! LB: What's wrong with having pride in your country? MW: Nothing, but when you get to the point of saying that all other countries suck, you've gone too far. Sgt. Williams is gonna teach him some respect for America tonight! LB: In your dreams. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 310 pounds and accompanied by his manager, Aaron Proctor, and his valet, Bambi, Sgt. Mike "Destroyer" Wiiiiiiiiiiiiiiilliams! (Sgt. Williams makes his entrance and the crowd goes wild. Aaron Proctor and Bambi are close behind him.) MW: And Williams looks ready to take a piece out of Fear. He's running to the ring! He charges right into there, and NAILS Fear with a short lariat! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: That isn't fair! Colonel Fear wasn't even ready yet! He hasn't even taken off his robes! MW: Well that's too bad, cause this one's underway! Williams, on his knees, delivers a kneeling headbutt to Colonel Fear! Now Williams picks him up, BACK SUPLEX! Williams is just not letting up! He picks up Fear, BODYSLAM--NO!! Fear has turned it into a small package! One..Two..Reversal! MW: Now Williams has Fear in a small package of his own! One..Two..Kickout! LB: There's too much action to call! MW: That's for sure! Williams up, he runs to the ropes, on the comeback, THEY BOTH NAIL EACH OTHER WITH CLOTHESLINES! They're slowly getting to their feet, Fear now, takes Williams down with a legsweep! LB: Here's where Colonel Fear takes over! MW: Fear whips Williams to the ropes, Fear runs, AND EXECUTES A FLYING BODYPRESS! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Look at that agility by the Colonel! No pathetic American wrestler could ever beat him! MW: That's the STUPIDEST thing I've ever heard. Now Fear runs, and NAILS Williams with a flying headbutt! The cover! One..Kickout! LB: How'd he kick out at one? MW: Maybe he's not as "pathetic" as you thought, huh? LB: I guess not, but he's close. MW: (frustrated) Ugghhhhhh! Anyway, Fear up, he charges at Williams, AND NAILS HIM WITH A CLOTHESLINE THAT SENDS HIM OVER THE TOP ROPE! Colonel Fear runs, and dives over the top rope! BUT WILLIAMS MOVES! And Colonel Fear crashes to the cement! LB: Come on Colonel! Get up and finish this wimp! MW: Williams makes it to his feet! LB: But look who's behind him! MW: Yes, Bootknife Kelly is sneaking up behind him! He's just about there, BUT WILLIAMS TURNS AROUND! And Williams CLOCKS him with a BIG right hand! Williams turns back around, AND GETS NAILED BY COLONEL FEAR! LB: What brilliant strategy! Kelly sacrifices his own body to give his man the upper hand! MW: Now Fear WHIPS Williams to the guardrail! He is in pain! Now Colonel Fear picks him up, and throws him OVER the guardrail! LB: And the fun begins! MW: Colonel Fear follows Williams into the crowd! He picks him up, AND WHIPS HIM RIGHT INTO A ROW OF CHAIRS!!!! THE FANS SCATTER! LB: He should pick up one of those punk fans, and throw him at Williams. MW: Will you stop? They're brawling right up the aisle! These fans are really seeing this action up close and personal! Williams grabs a chair, and CLOBBERS Fear with it! LB: He can't do that! MW: Ummmm, yes, he can! They are really going at it now, folks. They have brawled to the back of the area, and now they are going up a flight of stairs! I believe that that staircase leads to the upper deck of this arena! LB: They wanna make sure that even the fans in the cheap seats get to see them up close, how nice. MW: They've reached the top of the staircase, and yes, they are on the front walkway of the upper deck! Williams charges, and NAILS Fear with an elbow smash that plants him right on the lap of a fan! LB: That must be the thrill of that stupid fan's life! He had the Colonel Fear fall into his lap. MW: Yeah, I'm sure he's honored. Williams picks Fear up, and GORILLA PRESSES him over his head! And now he's walking with him, and, OH MY GOD! He's gonna throw him off the balcony! LB: Do something Colonel Fear! MW: And he does! He just turned that Gorilla Press into a rolling reverse cradle! One..Two..Kickout! LB: And look at the amazing wrestling by Colonel Fear! Williams can't even throw him off the upper deck right! MW: Williams is upset! He gets up, and a fan just handed him a frying pan! LB: Where'd he get that? Did he plan on making some eggs later? MW: I don't know WHERE he got it, but Williams has it now, and he NAILS Colonel Fear with it. Colonel Fear COLLAPSES against the upper deck guardrail! LB: Now WHAT is Williams doing? MW: It appears he's climbing to the other side of that upper deck guardrail! He'd better be careful not to fall! He makes his way down the guardrail, to where Colonel Fear is lying, and grabs him in a bear hug! LB: What is he doing? He had to stand on the edge of the balcony to do a bear hug? MW: I don't think he's done yet! He LIFTS Fear, and, OH MY GOD! HE JUST EXECUTED A BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPERPLEX FROM THE UPPER DECK! AND COLONEL FEAR SMACKS AGAINST THE CONCRETE! WHAT IMPACT! And Williams landed right on top of him! LB: That was the most insane thing that I have ever SEEN! The ref is making his way down from the upper deck to count! MW: And he's there now, I don't think that Fear'll be getting up from that one. The count! One..Two..Three!!!!!! MW: Anyone surprised? RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner... SGT. MIKE "DESTROYER" WILLIAMS!!!!!!! MW: And Williams just "destroyed" Colonel Fear. Williams is slowly leaving under his own power, but I believe we're gonna have to get some paramedics for Colonel Fear. Can you believe that superplex from the upper deck? LB: I'm STILL in shock about that one. What would posses a man to do something like that? MW: Perhaps hatred for an opponent who berates America? LB: Nah, that couldn't be it. MW: Well I'll tell you one thing, if that match didn't teach Colonel Fear some respect for the good old U. S. of A., nothing will. But let's move on. LB: What's next? MW: We've got that exciting ten man battle royale for a TV Title shot. The winner of this match will challenge the winner of tonight's TV Title match on Saturday. LB: This one should be interesting. Anybody could come out with the shot, cause there's no pinning (no matter what WCW says), all you gotta do is toss your opponent over the top. MW: But this kind of match does favor the big men, it should be very interesting to see who wins this one, so without any further ado, let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is a ten man battle royal, introducing the competitors... Wiiiiiiiiiiild Thing, Mister Crowleeeeeeeeeey, Twisted Steeeeeeeeeeeeel, Desperadoooooooooooo, Heart Throoooooooooob, The Caretakeeeeeeeeeeeeeer, "Ultimate" Jose Gooooooooooomez, The Grim Reeeeeeeeeeeaper, The Crazy Canuuuuuuuuuuuuuck, and "The Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slooooooooooooocum! (The men come out of the locker room one by one and make their way to the ring, along with their respective managers and valets.) MW: This should be some battle royal, hey Bertha? I've got to go with the former World Champ, Ben Slocum. After all, the first time he won the TV Title was after winning a battle royal. Who's your pick? LB: I'm going with the THREE TIME former World Champ-- Mister Crowley. MW: Fair enough, we've got all our competitors in the ring now, and we're underway. It's a wild brawl in there, everyone for themselves! Ben Slocum's got The Crazy Canuck on his shoulders, he climbs the turnbuckle, DEATH DROP! LB: Awful early to be going to your finisher, don't ya think? MW: In this match, there's no such thing as too early. And Jose Gomez goes right on the attack on the Canuck after that! LB: That's it, you gotta learn to kick a man when he's down. MW: But while Gomez is busy attacking the Canuck, The Grim Reaper goes on the attack on HIM! This is nuts! LB: You can say that again! MW: And now on the other side of the ring, we've got the unusual alliance of Heart Throb and Mr. Crowley double teaming the Crazy Canuck! LB: You'll see alliances like that in these matches, you will also see those alliances broken. MW: The Canuck escapes the double team, only to get NAILED by Ben Slocum! And now we've got the Grim Reaper and Jose Gomez teaming up on Heart Throb! They whip him to the ropes, but he comes back, and CLOTHESLINES THEM BOTH! LB: That alliance backfired. MW: No kidding! And Wild Thing goes right to work on Gomez! He nails him with a double underhook suplex! And just as he hit's the ground, we've got Ben Slocum going to work on Gomez. LB: There are NO friends here! They all want the shot at the gold, and who can blame them? MW: And while he's working on Gomez, Heart Throb attacks Ben Slocum! Heart Throb takes Slocum DOWN with a side suplex! He gets up, AND GETS CLOTHESLINED OVER THE TOP ROPE BY MISTER CROWLEY! RING ANNOUNCER: Heart Throb has been ELIMINATED! LB: So, Heart Throb is our first casualty, I knew the pretty boy couldn't hack it in there! MW: And just as Mister Crowley eliminates Heart Throb, Caretaker goes to work on HIM. We've been waiting for this confrontation. I still don't know what happened to Crowley after that "Life or Death" match Monday night. LB: He disappeared, but now he's back, and he's mad! MW: And now Ben Slocum comes over to help Caretaker work over Crowley-- there's no love lost between those two either. They whip Crowley to the ropes, and NAIL him with a double kick to the midsection! LB: Do you honestly think that something like that can stop Mister Crowley? MW: I don't know, but Slocum has gone off to other things, and Caretaker takes Crowley, DOUBLE UNDERHOOK SUPLEX--NO! Crowley reversed it into a backdrop, a back drop over the top rope and to the floor! RING ANNOUNCER: The Caretaker has been ELIMINATED! LB: There's Crowley's revenge! And he has eliminated BOTH of the first two guys! I told you he's gonna win! MW: There's still 8 men left to go, Bertha! And now Crowley's got the Crazy Canuck! He picks him up, PREMATURE BURIAL! LB: Just look at him dominate! MW: It's far from over. And now we've got Jose Gomez in there helping Crowley double team the Canuck. Gomez seems to REALLY like this double teaming! LB: It sure looks like it! MW: And now on the other side of the ring we've got the Grim Reaper and Wild Thing double teaming Ben Slocum! And Jose Gomez attacks Grim Reaper! This is nuts! LB: You already said that. MW: And you said "You can say that again." So there. Gomez nails the Reaper with a flying forearm, and just as the Reaper gets up, the Crazy Canuck attacks him! The Canuck goes for a Lariat--DUCKED by the Reaper! Now the Reaper catches him, POWER BOMB! That's his finisher! LB: And it's also the THIRD finisher to be done to the Canuck, he's gotta be ripe for getting tossed! MW: He is, Desperado picks him up, AND DOES JUST THAT, TOSSES HIM OVER THE TOP! RING ANNOUNCER: The Crazy Canuck has been ELIMINATED! LB: Can I call 'um, or can I call 'um? MW: Yes you called it, but now we've got Slocum going at it with Desperado! And Twisted Steel breaks it up, AND ATTACKS SLOCUM! Slocum and Twisted Steel are going at it tooth and nail! Twisted Steel whips Slocum to the ropes, Slocum come back, and NAILS Twisted Steel with a shoulderblock! He falls back into the ropes, and now Slocum is trying to throw him out! LB: He'll never get that fat tub of lard out of there! MW: He's got him up, but the weight is too much and he drops him back in! LB: Told you. MW: Now Twisted Steel goes over and attacks Mister Crowley! He catches him in a sleeper! LB: But the Grim Reaper makes the save! MW: And now Desperado and Twisted Steel are double teaming Mister Crowley! They're really beating up on him! They try to throw him over the top, BUT MISTER CROWLEY GRABS THEM BOTH BY THE NECK! LB: AND THEY LET GO! Mister Crowley is awesome! MW: And on the other side of the ring, we've got Ben Slocum taking it to Wild Thing! Slocum's got him over his shoulders, he's going for the Death Drop!--BUT WILD THING TURNS IT INTO A CRUCIFIX! LB: Look at Wild Thing outsmart that big dummy! MW: And now Wild Thing's going for the Party Time Splash--BUT SLOCUM PULLS UP HIS KNEES! Now who's the big dummy? And we've got Jose Gomez going at it with the Grim Reaper! Gomez nails him with a flying axehandle! LB: He knocked into Desperado! MW: And the Reaper gets into a tussle with Desperado over that little incident! The Reaper takes him down with a Bulldog headlock! He gets up, and gets NAILED by Twisted Steel! LB: What is this? Everybody attack the Reaper? MW: I guess so! Reaper is doubled over, AND TWISTED STEEL'S GOING FOR THE PAIN BOMB! He tries to pick him up, but he can't! And the Reaper turns it into a backdrop! LB: Of course Twisted Steel couldn't do it, the Reaper ONLY weighs 435 pounds! MW: Now Slocum attacks the Reaper, but Jose Gomez comes to his aid, and they're double teaming Slocum! Slocum escapes the double team, and he's trying to pick up The Reaper for the DEATH DROP! But the Reaper just falls on top of him! LB: That idiot! He goes and makes the same mistake that Twisted Steel just did! MW: And now Mister Crowley has Gomez. He throws him over the top--but Gomez hangs on and slides back in under the bottom! He comes back in, and gets caught by Twisted Steel! Twisted Steel takes him, GORILLA PRESS! And he THROWS Gomez to the floor! RING ANNOUNCER: "Ultimate" Jose Gomez has been ELIMINATED! LB: Four down, Five to go. MW: And now Twisted Steel turns around, and grabs Mister Crowley! He doubles him over with a kick to the midsection! He picks him up, PAIN BOMB! He NAILED IT! LB: He can't do that to Mister Crowley! MW: He just did! And now he throws Crowley over the top, but Mister Crowley hangs on and pulls himself back in! And I see Desperado just got the Grim Reaper in his finisher, the Scorpion Deathlock! LB: But for a reason that I do not know, Twisted Steel makes a save! MW: Desperado slaps it on him again!--And Twisted Steel makes the save again! Now Ben Slocum's got Desperado! He picks him up on his shoulders, climbs the ropes, DEATH DROP! LB: He really planted him! MW: Slocum picks him up, and tries to throw him over the top, Desperado's hanging on, and he fights his way back into the ring! Ben Slocum comes back into the ring, and gets PAIN BOMBED by Twisted Steel! LB: Yeah, beat the little chump! MW: And as soon as Slocum hits the mat, Wild Thing crashes down onto him with the Party Time Splash! He picks Slocum up for a Soviet Suplex, But Slocum blocks it! Slocum tries to throw Wild Thing over the top, but he hangs on and slides back in! LB: This is amazing! MW: Slocum turns back to the ring, and gets NAILED by a clothesline from the Grim Reaper! The Reaper picks him up, and tosses him out like a rag doll! RING ANNOUNCER: "The Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slocum has been ELIMINATED! LB: I told you he wouldn't win! MW: Now the Reaper's got Desperado, POWER BOMB! And Wild Thing picks Desperado up, and throws him over the top, BUT DESPERADO HANGS ON! LB: I wish he would stop doing that. MW: And now, on the other side of the ring, Twisted Steel's going at it with Mister Crowley! Twisted Steel charges, and CLOTHESLINES Crowley over the top and to the floor! LB: NOOO!!! RING ANNOUNCER: Mister Crowley has been ELIMINATED! MW: HA HA! Your pick is gone too, and we're down to the final four: Desperado, Wild Thing, The Grim Reaper, and Twisted Steel! LB: Not the for I would've picked! MW: Maybe not, but they're here. Desperado is trying to get Twisted Steel over the top, but that weight is just too much! Wild Thing comes over to help, but they still can't do it! LB: We've got 2 really fat guys in there now, Twisted Steel and The Grim Reaper, It's gonna be hard eliminate them. MW: An intelligent observation from YOU? What's wrong with you? LB: Shut up, Wilson! I know what I'm talking about! MW: Sometimes. Now Twisted Steel is trying to throw out Desperado, but he's hanging for dear life! And he's made it back in! LB: I thought I told him to stop that! MW: Now Twisted Steel turns back towards the ring, and Wild Thing is charging at him! Twisted Steel puts his head down, and BACKDROPS Wild Thing out of the ring! RING ANNOUNCER: Wild Thing has been ELIMINATED! LB: And there goes ANOTHER one of my favorites! MW: Gee, that's too bad. Twisted Steel gets his footing back after that backdrop, AND GETS NAILED BY A SHOULDERBLOCK FROM THE GRIM REAPER! Twisted Steel falls back into the ropes, AND WILD THING PULLS DOWN THE TOP ROPE! TWISTED STEEL FELL OUT! RING ANNOUNCER: Twisted Steel has been ELIMINATED! LB: And Wild Thing gets his revenge! That's what Twisted Steel gets for eliminating him! MW: But Twisted Steel is upset! He's going after Wild Thing, and he's chasing him all the way back to the locker room! Boy, I wonder what he's gonna do if he gets a hold of him? LB: Get his butt kicked, that's what. MW: Not Likely, but now we're down to only two men, The Grim Reaper and Desperado! One of these men will get a TV Title shot on Saturday night, and the other will go home with nothing. If that's not motivation to win, I don't know what is! LB: Yes, but Grim Reaper outweighs Desperado by 175 pounds! There's no way Desperado can get him over the top! MW: You'd be surprised by what you can do with the right motivation. Now let's get back to the action in the ring that we've been ignoring. Desperado runs, and NAILS the Reaper with a bulldog! The Reaper's down, Desperado's got him, SCORPION DEATHLOCK! LB: Oh, that's real smart, ground the big man, that's a REAL good way do get him over the top! MW: Desperado is now realizing that he can't do much with this hold, and he breaks it. Desperado's trying to pick him up and get him over the top. LB: Yeah, GOOD LUCK! MW: He's not having much success with that, he's gonna try something else! He's got his noose! He puts it around the neck of the Reaper, and tosses the other end over the top rope! LB: What the HELL is he doing? MW: Now he slides under the bottom rope, and he's pulling down on that rope! He's got leverage now! IT'S WORKING! LB: He can't do that! MW: He's almost got the Reaper over, BUT "CUTIE" RICK VERRANO JUST NAILED HIM WITH A CHAIR! And Desperado just falls to the ground! This was COMPLETELY uncalled for! LB: It was VERY called for! He couldn't just stand there and watch his man be choked out like that! MW: He wasn't choking him, not a lot anyway. He was just pulling him over the top! And now The Reaper has gotten that noose off his neck, and he regains his composure! Verrano tosses Desperado back in the ring! LB: It's over now! MW: You could be right, The Reaper picks Desperado up off the mat! He lifts him over his head, AND POWER BOMBS HIM OVER THE TOP ROPE AND TO THE FLOOR! It IS over. (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner... THE GRIM REAPER!!!!! LB: And he sure earned that one! MW: No he didn't, without Verrano, he would've lost! LB: Who's getting the TV Title shot? MW: The Grim Reaper. LB: I rest my case! MW: You have a very sick outlook, do you know that? LB: Yes, and I'm damn proud of it. MW: Well, anyway, up next we've got The Punisher defending the TV Title against Primal Fear. Primal Fear still looks near invincible after all this time, just look at the way he beat Big Ron Raider on Monday! LB: He did look pretty darn good, and I'm sure he'll give the Punisher a run for his money! MW: Indeed he should, so let's go to the ring and find out! RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is for the AEWA Television Title and is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, the challenger, from The Badlands, Sudan, he weighs in at 360 pounds and is accompanied by his manager, "The Beautiful" Miss DeMeanor, Primal Feeeeeeeeeeeeeeeear! (Primal Fear makes his entrance, as always, gnawing on a raw slab of meat. He gets mostly boos, but some of the hardcore fans are cheering him. Miss DeMeanor follows close behind.) MW: And here comes the psycho! LB: Miss DeMeanor? Why would you call her psycho? MW: Not Miss DeMeanor, you twit. I was talking about Primal Fear. LB: Oh, sorry. MW: You should be. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 301 pounds and accompanied by his manager, Bootknife Kelly, he is the AEWA Television Champion, The Punisheeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeer! (The music from "Star Wars" plays, as the TV champ makes his entrance, waving the belt in the air to a shower of boos. Bootknife Kelly is at his side.) LB: And here comes the champ! MW: Perhaps his last day as champ. LB: What's that supposed to mean? MW: Nothing, it just means that it's very possible that he could lose the title tonight. LB: Why would you say that? MW: Because it's true. You were saying it yourself a minute ago. LB: Oh yeah, I forgot. MW: And they're ready to lock up, but the ref's not letting them! LB: Why not? MW: It appears we have another ring announcement. RING ANNOUNCER: And now, please allow me to introduce the President of the AEWA, President Smith! (President Smith makes his way to the ring, and takes the microphone.) LB: What's he doing here? MW: I believe we're about to find out! (continued in Part 3)
