Wednesday Night Rumble (5/29)

31 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(the camera comes on with the new opening for the AEWA... (The AEWA logo comes on the screen then the image BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power Bomb, and another look at Mister Crowley DRIVING the head of Volcano Jackson into the concrete with the Premature Burial!) MW: Hi and welcome to Wednesday Night Rumble...We have a match already getting ready to start...So let's get to the action...our first match has been mandated by President Smith...This past Saturday Night, in a last second Lumberjack match Between Deranged and The Outlander Dennis Windham, Red Wolf and The Phantom of the Opera came out of the locker room and posed as security guards and totally beat the hell out of both of them...and now they have both asked for rematches and they will get them here tonight... LB: I love it..They are gonna kick ass tonight...They are gonna teach these guys not to get into their business... MW: Bertha do you really think that they will get anything settled tonight?? LB: Of course..When Red Wolf and the Phantom of the Opera get their butts kicked they will run from the AEWA and probably never be heard from again... MW: Or it could be the other way around!! LB: No way it will never happen..you have to understand that Windham and Deranged have some pride...And I think that will be the difference of this match!!! MW: Time will tell and Deranged is already in the ring...Dang they outta feed him at that hospital..He is chomping on the corner turnbuckle pad again and there is padding everywhere... LB: Wait till Red Wolf hits that corner with the padding gone...Puppy Blood I love it!! MW: And here comes Red Wolf and the fans are going nuts!!!HEY WATCH OUT!!! Deranged has snuck into the crowd and as Red Wolf walks by he.. HELL IS IT... LB: That looks like that cheater the Lord of Midnight...Phantom's buddy and now he is mixing it up with Windham...Where the hell did Phantom and Deranged go?? MW: I think they are out front, but look at this Red Wolf is back on his feet!!! But security just rushed in and they have him down and they are putting him in handcuffs and he his defensless and now security grabs the Lord of Midnight and they are getting the cuffs on him...But, Windham is running thru the crowd and he's headed towards the front door...DO WE HAVE A CAMERA OUT FRONT!! (camera immediately swings and we can see Phantom of the Opera and Deranged battling it out right in front of the snack bar, but suddenly Dennis Windham appears and kicks Phantom of the Opera from behind and he goes down...) MW: Look at this Windham just jumped over the snack bar counter and is picking up that big old popcorn machine...NOOOO NOOOO HE CAN'T!!!! LB: He is up on top of the counter and he is gonna smash the hell out of the Phantom with that big machine...DO IT!! DO IT!!!!Yes here he goes and OHHH NOOOOO!!!! MW: Phantom rolled out of the way and that popcorn flew everywhere as he landed right on top of it and look blood is squirting everywhere the glass cut the hell out of him...And finally here comes security... LB: But, look, I can't believe it the bruised and bleeding Windham just ran out the front door and he's running down the street...and look the Phantom is chasing him...For his sake he better not catch him... MW: HANK!! HANK!! ARE YOU THERE HANK... (a camera is moving like somebody is running and we can hear Hank Thompson's voice as he is trying his best to talk but he is gasping for air as he's running down the street) HT: Windham just ran into a 7 eleven and as you can see fans Phantom of the Opera is right on his tail... (about 10 cop cars pull up with sirens blaring as we see thru the windows to the 7 eleven that Windham and Phantom are totally destroying the store as they are brawling thru the aisles) HT: As you can see the cops are now entering the store and they have pulled their pistols but it hasn't had any effect on these 2 men yet as they are still brawling...Look at this Windham has the Phantom of the Opera down and he just busted him wide open with some kind of foreign object and they are both bleeding badly now... (10 cops run up and knock Windham off of the Phantom and they cuff both men and take them out of the store) HT: Well there you have it folks both of these men appear to be headed to jail but probably via the hospital as both of them are bleeding profusely for several cuts and the Phantom of the Opera has a very nasty gash on his forehead...And if you can see over my shoulder folks they are having to tie both of these guys to stretchers to keep them away from each other...Back to you Wilson... (camera swings back to the arena) MW: JESUS....That was totally out of control...President Smith has his hands full with these guys... LB: He should suspend Phantom of the Opera, Lord of Midnight and Red Wolf for their actions, they are totally uncontrollable... MW: Why not Windham and Deranged??? They started this whole rucuss?? LB: NO NO NO!!! THEY WERE JUST DEFENDING THEMSELVES...Heck Deranged and Windham even offered food and beer as a peace offering and you saw what happened... MW: Yeah I saw exactly what happened!!! But, as usual you didn't!!! LB: Shut up Wilson, or i'll go get that popcorn popper and take care of ya!!! MW: Sure, whatever, yeah right, and all that crap Bertha....I think they have the arena cleaned up enough now to start our next match... LB: Yeah isn't the Super Blonde minus his slut cousin and his slut girlfriend gonna take on my favorite wrestler... MW: Every wrestler can't be your favorite Bertha!!! LB: Well, this guy is my bestest favorite!!and he's back in the AEWA...and you know what?? MW: I'm afraid to ask... LB: He's back for only one reason...and that is to take care of the Super Blonde forever!!! MW: Well, he's gonna have his hands full as the Super Blonde isn't in very good spirits lately...He is really upset about the problems that he's been having with Kismet...not to mention with Jenny Babbs!! We still have yet to hear anything about her whereabouts... LB: Oh from what I hear she's working in some fancy whorehouse in Vegas!! MW: Knock it off!! That ain't true... LB: I will prove it!!! MW: How?? LB: I don't know yet but I will!!! MW: Ok well look at this...the Super Blonde is walking to ringside and it is dead silent no entrance music or nothing and he's not doing his usual hand slapping with the fans...He looks very determined tonight!!! LB: Maybe he is finally waking up and realizing that the fans ain't worth a damn!! MW: You and I both know that ain't it Bertha!!! LB: Ohhh Shitttttt!!! the lights just went out and ohhh nooo look all I can see are those glowing eyes coming down the aisle (she stands up and cheers as the Toxic Twin walks down the aisle) MW: Ahhh, Bertha did you notice that you are the only one in the arena cheering?? LB: So what!!! I know wrestling and they don't! That's why I cheer for the winners all the time.. MW: Or the cheaters to be more exact... LB: Well like Big Daddy tells me...It's not whether you win or lose as long as you CHEAT!! CHEAT!! CHEAT!!! MW: Hey speaking of him, I heard he was due back tonight, where is he?? LB: I'll never tell!! Hey Toxic Twin is in the ring and he has already attacked the Blonde and is tossing him around like a rag doll in there!!! This should be one very short match... MW: It could be..a hiptoss takedown and now Toxic Twin into the ropes and he drops a big knee onto the Super Blonde...and now he drags him up by the hair of the head and wham a body slam...Now he's going out of the ring and he tosses a chair into the ring..The ref is warning him... LB: HIT HIM!! HIT HIM WITH THAT CHAIR!!!! MW: He tried but the Blonde kicks him in the knee and he drops it and the Blonde picks it up and listen to the fans they really want him to clock him with that chair...But, intead he tosses it out of the ring and now he's setting him up for a handstand Frankensteiner and yesss!!!! He got it....and now he climbs to the top turnbuckle and executes a moonsault and the cover ....One...two..(he tosses the Blonde clean out of the ring, as his eyes start to glow very bright!!!) LB: HE'S PISSED NOW...Now the Super Punk is gonna get what he deserves...Toxic Twin climbs to the top tope and goes for a full splash...he must be 15 feet up and ohh nooo... MW: The Super Blonde gets a boot up at the last second and now both men are down outside the ring and listen to this crowd they are going nuts...Super Blonde is back to his feet and slides back into the ring (ding ding) the bell sounds and let's go to the announcer... ANNOUNCER: The referee has ruled the Super Blonde the winner of the match via countout at the 3 minute and 9 second mark of the match... (Super Blonde slides out of the ring and immediately walks back to the locker room) LB: The only good thing was that he didn't stay in the ring and show off tonight!! MW: I think he has other things on his mind today Bertha... LB: HEY LOOK TOXIC TWIN IS RUNNING DOWN THE AISLE AND HE JUST HIT THE SUPER PUNK FROM BEHIND!!! God I love this guy!!! MW: Now he picks up the Super Blonde and Power Bombs him right in the middle of the aisle... LB: That will teach him to cheat won't it!!!And I love it Toxic Twin just walks away and leaves the trash lay right there in the middle of the aisle... MW: Well, it's pretty clear that the Super Blonde is out of it right now....The Super Blonde in the past would have never been caught from behind like that...he better get his private life straight soon or it's gonna cost him his career!!! LB: Good...I just wish that he would leave the AEWA for good...We don't need any more punks like him around...Volcano Jackson is enough to deal with!!! MW: Yup as usual Bertha you make no sense but I'm getting used to it...I still can't wait to get Big Daddy back in here... LB: I'm not leaving Wilson....Little to you know little man...(she reaches in her purse and pulls out a piece of paper) Read this and weep rat boy!!! (Wilson takes the paper and he looks totally pissed off) MW: This is a bunch of BLEEP!!! BLEEP BLEEP THAT SON OF A BLEEP!!! BLEEP, (I think you get the idea) (he calms down a little) How in the hell did you get a contract for twice as much as I make and for twice as long... LB: You know the way I do business...(winks and then laughs) MW: Yooouuuu slept with him didn't ya!!! LB: (Still laughing) That's none of your business and don't forget to look at line 18c-6-ac1(w) and if you would please read it aloud!!!! MW: Ahhhh oh here it is right after she can never be suspended for her involvement in matches...ok..The Lovely Bertha will act as the head announcer for all AEWA events and she may terminate any other announcers at her descreation....Oh Shit!!!This can't be happening... LB: And you know what that means don't ya Mister I'm too coool!!! MW: (ALMOST IN TEARS) I'm fired?? LB: Yup pack your junk and get the hell off my set...(suddenly) Uh ohhh! (Presiden Smith walks down to the table) SMITH: Hold on Wilson, this is a forgery, I never signed this and Bertha I never slept with you so let's get that straight!!! LB: (TURNING RED) BLEEP you Smith (she then slaps his face) SMITH: (Rubbing his face) You crossed the line this time young lady...(he then slaps her back right in the face!!!)(HUGE FREAKING CROWD POP!!!) LB: (SCREAMING) OK YOU SACK OF BLEEP YOU WANNA HIT A WOMEN COME ON COME ON!!! RIGHT HERE, RIGHT NOW, YOU AND ME, RIGHT UP IN THAT RING!!! (She takes off her his heel shoes and walks in the ring wearing just a short sundress) SMITH: (Laughing) she can't be serious!! I can't wrestle..I am the President. Presidents don't wrestle....(Suddenly, out of nowhere, Big Daddy appears and attacks Smith from behind and tosses him into the ring) MW: BIG DADDY BIG DADDY!!! (Big Daddy puts on a headset and sits down next to Wilson) MW: What in the hell is going on?? BD: She owes him Wilson...Did you know that he sexually harrased her?? MW: No way...Smith ain't that kinda guy... BD: Oh yes he is...She offered him her body in return for a permanent job and he turned her down...So that's sexual harrassment... MW: Ohhhh, sure. Yeah, right. Big Daddy, all that time in the hospital didn't help you a bit did it. Oh nooo! Bertha is really beating the Hell outta Smith there in the ring. She is trying to pick him up, but he is just a little too heavy and he falls on top of her. The ref counts one..two..(shoulder up)... BD: That was the slowest count I've ever seen...And now a superkick by Bertha, and look at that weak President of yours now fans, he's getting what he has deserved for months now... (the lights dim and as we see Bertha still beating the hell out of the Prez a spotlight shines on the aisle and Kismet comes running to the ring and following not too far behind her is RAEKWON) BD: Hey I gotta go (he jumps up and runs toward the ring) MW: KISMET just hit the ring and she is taking it to Bertha...this is something, Kismet with a super kick and now whe grabs Bertha by the hair and is swinging her around and now a powerslam...Meanwhile on the outside Big Daddy and RAEKWON are both tossing lefts and rights and finally Big Daddy gains the advantage with a kick to RAEKWON's groin...Now Big Daddy tosses him into the ring and as he enters Kismet catches him with a super kick...and somehow during all of this confusion President Smith has managed to slide out of the ring to the floor and is being attended to by EMT's. He really looks hurt down there laying on the floor...Now RAEKWON is back on his feet and believe it or not the crowd is cheering for him as he Power Bombs Big Daddy, and now he and Kismet clean house as both Bertha and Big Daddy run for the hills...and look at this they just high fived in the center of the ring..I can't believe this and the crowd is loving it (HUGE CROWD POP)....Well fans while all of this get's straightened out let's take you to a previously recorded message from Cardinal Chaos...... (camera fades in on a crowded church. Cardinal Chaos is at the microphone talking to his congregation.) Cardinal Chaos: ...and I tell you now, each and every one of you, NOW is the time to repent! NOW is the time to turn to the Lord! NOW is the time! Do you hear me, my children? The Lord has given a sign unto me. This past Monday night, a once bitter enemy of the church regained my AEWA World Championship belt. Should I cower? Should I envy this man of power? NO I tell you! The good book says that the worthy man shall hold the keys of the kingdom until the rightful owner makes his presence known. Mr. Crowley is that worthy man. His light has quietly started to shine in the eternal confessional of life. He shall hold the belt proudly for as long as I deem him worthy. This is the man you should look to for guidance in my absence. This is the man who shall reign at my right side. When you sleep tonight, pray not to me. Pray to the man they call Crowley. For when you pray to him, I shall hear. Now go in peace, my children. Go and spread my light for all to see. (the congregation quietly starts to exit the church while the lights fade to black.) MW: Ohhh Jesus, that man is wacko. I think he actually believes he's God....Well fans that is all we really have for you tonight...Because as promised we are trying to keep our Wednesday night time slot down to only about one hour...So for the Lovely Bertha, and the newly returned Big Daddy, Good evening and see you Saturday night... (camera fades to black)
