Sunday, March 30th, Card (2/3)

30 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(This Wednesday at 8:00, on the WYXZ TV-39 movie of the week, it's Barbara Eden in the "Harper Valley PTA", only here on WYXZ TV 39) EL: Let's go back to Dave Myers for the introductions to this next one. DM: This next contest is scheduled for one fall with a 20-minute time limit. Already in the ring to my left, weighing a combined 559 lb., Paul "the Tornado" Langford and Travis Conley, Twister! (Pops as the two Twisterians raise their hands in recognition of the crowd reaction.) DM: And their opponents, accompanied by their manager, Tip Smith, weighing in at 510 lb., Healer's Havoc, Big Dog Brown, Midnight Rampage! (Pops continue. For those of you playing at home, here is how you distinguish the players from one another: Paul Langford is 6'3"-244 with a washboard stomach, short brown hair, green eyes, and the brown trunks; Travis Conley is dressed like his partner, at 6'8"-315 with short blond hair and blue eyes; Healer's Havoc is 6'6"-260, black-haired, toothless and in a dog collar; BD Brown is 6'3"-250, with blue eyes and a nose that has been broken more times than is really healthy.) DM: Your referee for this contest, Chris Herman. EL: OK- there's the bell, and the Big Dog and Travis Conley start things off- quick handshake. Brown with an arm bar, twisting Conley's arm- Conley reverses- and Brown quickly re-reverses. Now Brown goes around behind-crucifix roll-up- 1... kickout. Brown goes for the big payoff early on. Conley back up and tags in Langford. In comes the Tornado- lockup- hiptoss by Brown- now Brown whips Langford into the ropes- Langford with the leapfrog- catches Brown from behind- belly to back suplex! Now Langford off the ropes- cross body block! The cover... 1... 2... kickout. Now Langford goes to a side headlock on Brown, really grinding that head. Havoc urging his partner on as Langford tightens the pressure. Next week, one of these teams will try to become the first-ever CWAA Tag Team Champions, but the team that falls tonight gets its chance at gold, too, as they'll enter the TV title battle royal. Brown gets Langford up- back suplex out of the headlock. Now BD up- Irish whip- monkey flip by the Dog. Now he goes up for a dropkick- Langford knocked into the corner- and Brown in after him- cobra clutch! Referee Chris Herman checking to make sure that arm does not drop below the chin into an illegal chokehold. ("Go, Paul, Go!") EL: Now Herman calls for the break as Langford was in the ropes- and Brown with a cross corner whip- Stinger splash flattens the Tornado! Now Brown drags him to the Midnight corner, and Havoc sticks out his foot- and Brown slams Langford's head onto Havoc's boot. There's the tag- and Healer's Havoc with a shoulderblock on Langford in the corner, and now an elbow jab to the head. Open hand chop across the chest, and now an Irish whip- clawhold by Healer's Havoc! And Langford drops to one knee as Healer's Havoc clamps that hand onto his head. Paul Langford in obvious pain as referee Chris Herman checks for the submission- and here comes Travis Conley with a double sledge to break the hold! As Conley is sent back to his corner, Havoc tags Brown back in. Brown sets him up- power bomb. 1... 2... kickout. Brown now stands him up- hammerlock. Langford slips behind him- rolls him up... 1.... 2... kickout! Near fall for Twister there. Brown up with a savate kick now- Irish whip- Langford tags as he hits the ropes- Langford leapfrogs Brown as Comely runs in as the legal man- Langford slides out- Conley clotheslines the unsuspecting Brown... 1... 2... kickout! Conley up now with a full nelson on the Dog. Conley does not have the fingers locked though, so he does not get the full effect of his prodigious power. But at 6'8" he does have a real leverage advantage. ("Go, Dog, Go!" Hey- one of my favorite children's books!) EL: Brown struggles to break fr- Healer's Havoc in now with a double sledge on Conley to break the hold. Referee Chris Herman chases Havoc out of there as Big Dog headbutts Conley, and now Brown tags out. In comes Havoc with an Irish whip- shoulderblock by Travis Conley, and now an elbow drop. Conley tags out, and Langford in there with a cross body press- Havoc catches him- powerslam! 1... 2... Langford gets a foot in the ropes. Havoc stands him up- hip to- no! Langford counters with a back slide... 1... 2... 3! He got it! (The theme to the major motion picture "Twister" plays.) DM: Here are your winners, "Tornado" Paul Langford, Travis Conley, Twister! (Big pop as Havoc and Brown accept the handshakes offered by the winners.) EL: So next week it will be Byron McNultey and Michael Anderson once more with Twister, this time for the CWAA Tag Team Championship. Healer's Havoc and BD Brown will be joining over a dozen others in the TV title battle royale. Well, later tonight we have another tag team matchup, as Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas meet Joey Haven and a mystery partner. (Boos as Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas walk to the ring.) EL: I see Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas in the ring now with David Myers, let's hear what they have to say. DM: Guys, this is the first time you have teamed together, how do you feel about your chances against Haven and his mystery partner? JH: We are both confident that no matter who Haven has as his partner, we'll still come out victorious. To tell the truth, I don't care if we win or not, as long as I get to bash on Joey's head some more. I know Marc is the best wrestler here in the CWAA and possibly in the world. He took that wimp Valachi to the bell without too much trouble and I know I can trust him. Marc, are you worried about Haven and his partner? MT: Not in the least. It doesn't matter who Haven wants to bring with him to the ring. I've beaten big people, small people, slow people, and quick people, so I don't care if he brings Andre the Giant or Andre the Seal, we'll kick both of their (expletive deleted). Valachi, yeah you little coward. I took you to the bell last week and you know as well as I do that if the match had continued that I would have won and that AEWA Light Heavyweight Belt would now be around my waist. Don't think for one second that this is over. You'll be seeing more of me in the weeks to come, I guarantee. If you think you had to hide to get away from the Italian Loan Collection Agency, then you better check into a witness protection agency to get away from me. Tonight will just be an example of what is going to happen to you the next time we meet. JH: Haven, this is just a warm-up for the next time we meet at the AEWA PPV....if you make it there! (Exit JH and MT) EL: All right, when we get back, we'll see Max Robertson in the ring with Stan "The Powerhouse" Stevens. (Coming soon, the AEWA goes Extreme! Crimson Joe Reed defends the AEWA World Title, the Machines and Assassins unify the tag titles, and Sir Gallade, Joey Valachi, Joey Haven and many more take it to the Extreme in the city that invented Hardcore: AEWA Extreme, live from the Core States Center in Philadelphia- call your local cable operator) EL: In a few moments, we'll see Stan Stevens for the first time in just about a month. Let's see what happened last time we saw him. ********************************************************** CWAA File Footage 02/23/97 EL: Stan Stevens on his way down, now, and Major Mayhem is in the wrong place at the wrong time... (MVM, standing in the aisleway, gets in Stevens' face, prodding him in the middle of the chest with an index finger to punctuate his points, which appear to about Stevens being unsuitable for Mayhem's old crack hand-to-hand combat team, but the mike can't pick things up too clearly from the aisle.) EL: I wouldn't do that if I were him. Then again, if I were the Major, there are a lot of things I wouldn't do. Go on living, for starters. (MVM continues sputtering, and Stevens looks to the crowd, who egg him on, and he grabs the scrawny major by the throat and lifts him two feet of the ground, as the crowds erupts in a frenzy of approval.) EL: The major is finding out just how "suitable" Stan Ste- look out! Lt. Lawson just came out at a dead sprint and clipped the legs of the Powerhouse. I smell a set-up, and now Lawson is stomping away at the fallen Stevens there in the aisle, and the Major is busy trying to get his breath back. ************************************************************************** ("You Got the Power" by X plays and the San Jose native arrives to a small pop) DM: This next contest is set for one fall with a 15-minute time limit. First, on his way to the ring now, weighing in at 335 lb., from San Jose, California, Stan "The Powerhouse" Stevens. (The Imperial March which announces Darth Vader's presence in the movies strikes up, and the crowd's reaction gets ugly.) DM: (Barely audible over the crowd noise) And his opponent, from San Francisco, California, weighing 200 lb., "The Professional" Max Robertson. Your referee for this contest is Chris Herman. EL: OK- Max Robertson, who had better not be looking ahead to next week's CWAA Title match when a man with Stevens' power stands in his way, has to be looking over his shoulder after the way we saw Joshua Barnes chasing him earlier tonight. There's the bell, and Robertson immediately clipping that leg which Lawson had injured just over a month ago. When you're down by almost 150 lb., on his back is the one place you want your opponent. Elbow drop by the Professional, who now turns his attention back to that right leg. He snaps back on it, stretching those hamstrings. Now he stomps at the thigh area, and goes for a figure-four leglock already! Stevens with a kick that launches Max back into the corner. Stevens up now as Max comes out of the corner- and Stevens goes downstairs with a shot to the solar plexus. Stevens grabs him by the throat now- chokeslam coming up? (The crowd groans in sympathy as the groin shot came more quickly than the chokeslam.) EL: Stevens writing on the mat, as Max takes advantage of the lull to get his breath back. Max goes right back to that leg, this time with a half-Boston crab, as he sits on the small of the Powerhouse's back and pulls back on that leg. You can see the veins on Steven's forehead as he strains to fight the pain. ("Go, Stan, Go!") EL: Stevens trying to power his way out of this one- almost like doing a one-legged push-up- he has his chest up off the mat- and look at the expression on Max's face as Stevens gets almost full extension of his arms with Max sitting on his back! Now Stevens reaches out and grabs the middle rope. Chris Herman calling for the clean break. Max hops off and looks warily at the Powerhouse, who stands almost a foot taller and 150 lb. heavier. Robertson off the ropes- cross body block- Stevens catches him and doesn't even back up one step! He lifts Max up above his head- over the shoulder stomachbreaker! (Big pop as Robertson rolls on the mat, the wind knocked out of him) EL: Now Stevens drags the Professional to the corner- slams his head on that top turnbuckle. He whips Max across the ring- big clothesline! Max slides out under the bottom rope for a breather, and trips over his own two feet as he tries to get away. (Crowd laughs at Max's misfortune) EL: Max back up on the apron now- and here comes Stevens- Robertson jabs a thumb right in the eye! Chris Herman warning him, but Max is too busy climbing to the top rope. Unfamiliar territory for the mat technician- and Stevens with a shot in the gut- and Max falls spread-eagle on the top turnbuckle! (Crowd pops/laughs again) EL: Now Stevens hooks Max's tights- superplex coming up- and down he goes! The cover... 1... 2... kickout! Stevens with an Irish whip- sets- leapfrog by Max- Max lands behind him- thrust kick in the head! Stevens falls to the mat- Robertson covers... 1... he has feet on the TOP rope for leverage!... 3! Max Robertson with a blatant use of the ropes to secure the pinfall as he slips out of the ring and out of harm's way. Let's go to David Myers for the official decision. DM: Here is your winner, Max "the Professional" Robertson! (Big-time booing as Max leaves with his hands in the air and Stevens argues his case with Chris Herman.) EL: When we get back, Marc Thomas and Jak Hammer meet Joey Haven and- well, we'll find out who, won't we?
