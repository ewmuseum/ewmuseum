"Lethal" Lawson and Max "The Professional" Robertson (4) 419 lb. Recaps: Once again, there is a new CWAA Heavyweight Champion. After the title was vacated by the AEWA for Max Robertson's refusal to make a mandatory defense against Marc Thomas, a four-man tournament was held among the top contenders. Max Robertson defeated Jak Hammer, and The Masked Man defeated Randy Sheradin- but not without losing his mask! The Masked Man managed to keep his face concealed as he escaped the Dreamer, and a week later he scored the victory over Robertson. Guest referee Kore "the Preacher" Wrench then unmasked the new champion, and revealed him as none other than Marc Thomas. Thomas has since revealed that his falling out with Jak Hammer was a ruse, and set his sights on AEWA World Heavyweight Champion. In mid-August, the AEWA announced that Reed would defend his AEWA World Title against the reigning CWAA champion (at the time undetermined still) on Sunday, August 24th, in a 2-of-3 falls encounter in Dallas, Texas' Reunion Arena. Thomas proved to be intimidated by the legendary Reed, and he and Hammer came back a week before the match to try and soften Reed up and score a psychological pre-emptive strike. The cowardly attack, beginning with a threat of violence against the Lady in Red, saw Thomas "pin" Reed twice, following Icebreakers. On August 24th, Reed and Thomas hooked up in a classic bout that went for nearly 70 minutes. After about 20 minutes of action, Reed scored the first fall by ducking a clothesline attempt and rebounding off the ropes into a cross body press. Thomas, who had been in control up until the first fall ended, quickly evened the odds by nailing the World Champion with an Icebreaker and recording the three-count. The third fall lasted over 50 minutes, and in the end it was Reed who came out on top, catching Thomas in a Kentucky Backsmasher to retain his championship belt. Although he left the ring without the fabled 10 pounds of gold, Thomas remained CWAA Heavyweight Champion, and any doubts about his ability to contend in the so-called big leagues have been dispelled. As Thomas exited the ring, and Reed celebrated, CWAA Television Champion Max "the Professional" Robertson, approached Reed. Flanked by his allies, Major Mayhem, Lt, Lawson and Sgt. Sherrick, the former CWAA Heavyweight Champion accused Reed of ducking him and reneging on an agreement to face Robertson in the spring (n.b.: Heavy Metal inherited the title defense obligations when he defeated Reed in the spring for the AEWA World Title, and defeated Robertson soundly). Robertson goaded Reed into accepting a title match on September 7th in the Sportatorium. Other title changes this month included Joshua Barnes' brief reign as TV Champ, as, one week after defeating Jak Hammer, Barnes fell to his archnemesis, Robertson. Robertson continues to try to humiliate Barnes and to force him into tactical mistakes with psychological ploys, and, while continuing to reign as champion, has taken some serious beatings from his much-bigger foe in the process. At the Reunion Arena show on August 24th, Barnes snapped and was so intent on hurting Robertson that he forewent at least one pin opportunity to continue his attack, and punched referee Chris Herman. Disciplinary measures against the embattled fan-favorite are expected to be announced soon. The first-ever CWAA Tag Team champions have had their ups and downs, as they fell in defeat to the Alliance of Power (Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas), and fell in the first round of the CWAA Six Man Tag Team Title tournament, but, with the aid of Cowboys All-Pro lineman Larry Allen, Paul Langford and Travis Conley regained the CWAA Tag Team Titles with a victory over Byron McNultey and Michael Anderson. Additionally, the CWAA Six Man Tag Team Title was created, and the winners of a four-team tournament were "The Preacher" Kore Wrench, "The Dreamer" Randy Sheradin and Joshua Barnes. Recently, then-reigning CWAA TV Champ Jak Hammer travelled to Dayton, Ohio to defend his title against former champion Lt. Lawson in front of an ACWF audience. Lawson and his partner, Mark Sherrick, and their manager, Major Mayhem, triple-teamed Hammer, until Randy Sheradin, who also wrestles for the ACWF, came to Hammer's aid. The trio dealt a beating to Sheradin as well, and then resumed the assault on Hammer in front of a horrified crowd. ACWF Tag Team Champions the New Sensations (Alex Peters and James Thompson) tried to halt the attack, but found themselves on the receiving end of a 3-on-2 assault for their troubles until a chair-wielding Sheradin chased the Mayhem Militia from the ring. Enraged, the ACWF Tag champs came to the Sportatoirum and put their titles on the line to coax Sherrick and Lawson into a match. Sherrick and Lawson scored the victory and won the titles under circumstances the UWF, the ACWF's governing body, has termed "dubious", and there is talk that the UWF refuses to recognize the new champions. Since then, the ACWF has ceased to operate, and Sherrick and Lawson have defended the ACWF Tag Team titles in defiance of the UWF. Roster Moves: There have been a number of arrivals this summer. Fuse may not have chosen to stay, but his long-time partner, Elektro, has joined the ranks of the CWAA full-time. "Maniac" Marco Moura, an eccentric green-haired newcomer, debuted this past month. But the hottest start belongs to "the Samurai" Kensei Tokugawa, who has won each of his first matches and lasted right down to the end of a 20-man over-the-top-rope battle royal before his attempts to use a kendo stick to illegally eliminate the Trailer Park Assassins were impeded by the interference of Jimmy Dangerous, whom Tokugawa had brazenly attacked with the same kendo stick just a week prior. Tokugawa has also made a name for himself by accosting the very unpopular TV champ, Max Robertson. Twister introduced a new partner in their Forces of Nature alliance, Casey Spahn. who has yet to make his solo debut but acquitted himself well in the team's opening round loss to Robertson, Sherrick and Lawson in the CWAA Six Man Tag Team Title tournament. Additionally, the Kabuki Kid is set to debut shortly. Injuries: No one is seriously hurt at present, but there are plenty of nicks and scrapes. Max Robertson has a sore neck after being jabbed in the Adam's Apple by Tokugawa's kendo stick. He also has sore ribs from his matches with Joshua Barnes, but is not expected to miss any ring time. After his exertion in the title match with Joe Reed, Marc Thomas suffered from dehydration, but recovered shortly thereafter. Who’s hot and Who’s not: Hot: Kensei Tokugawa. Three weeks into his CWAA career and already he is ranked among the title contenders and is close to signing a match with TV Champion Max Robertson. His performance in the battle royal at Reunion Arena was astonishing, and he has yet to be pinned. Not: Byron McNultey and Michael Anderson saw their rulebreaking and egos cost them the titles as Travis Conley's ex-teammate, Larry Allen, caught them in the act as Anderson tried to score an illegal pinfall. When Anderson got in Allen's face, Allen punched him, and Twister capitolized on the advantage to score the winning fall and regain the titles. The news for the ex-champs is worse- the loss, coupled by the recent win the Alliance of Power scored verse Twister, means that Hammer and Thomas are the new #1 contenders for the titles, and Anderson and McNultey will have to work their way back into a title match. Hot: Elektro has wasted no time in mixing with the top stars. He debuted in a six-man steel cage match with Fuse and Kore Wrench against top stars Sgt. Sherrick and Lt. Lawson and the mysterious El Diablo. A week later, he made his solo debut against Dave "the Freak" Johnson, scoring the pinfall (aided by Little Yvette). Not: Jak Hammer. A month ago he was the TV champ and part of the tournament for the CWAA Heavyweight Tournament after defeating his erstwhile partner Marc Thomas. Now, he was not even a participant in the Reunion Arena show, and has been relegated to watching Thomas' back. A TV Title match for the ex-champ should be coming soon, though. Mandatory Title Defenses: CWAA Heavyweight Title: Champion: Marc Thomas Challenger: Max Robertson Date: September 21st (n.b., assuming Robertson is still TV Champion at this time, this will be a title for title match) CWAA Tag Team Title: Champions: Twister Challengers: The Alliance of Power Date: September 28th
