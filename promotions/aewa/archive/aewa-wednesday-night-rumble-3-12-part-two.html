Wednesday Night Rumble (3/12, part two)

15 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* * * * CONTINUED FROM PART ONE * * * * MW: We've got what is perhaps the most interesting match of the evening. Primal Fear is once again gonna go at it with the "Shotgun" Stan Crawford. This time, it's falls count anywhere, and with all the possible places in the mall this one could end up, this should be really fun to watch. BD: Yeah, like what if they ended up in-- get this-- THE BATHROOM! Wouldn't that be interesting and new? LB: Yeah, that'd be something we've NEVER seen before. MW: Okay, you guys have had your fun, but seriously, what do you think of this matchup? BD: I think that Primal Fear's gonna kick the crap out of Crawford, once and for all, proving without a doubt who the most extreme wrestler in the AEWA is. This is his style of match, and frankly I don't see how he can lose. LB: Especially not with that new manager of his. Face it Wilson, the deck's stacked in Primal Fear's favor. With his new attitude, he's gonna be unstoppable again. MW: Well we're gonna find out tonight, that's for sure, and now we're just about ready to get underway, so let's go to the ring! RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is set for one fall. There will be no countout, no disqualification, and falls will count ANYWHERE in the building. Introducing first, from the Badlands, Sudan, he weighs in at 360 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his new manager, Xavier, here is the most extreme wrestler in the AEWA, Prrrrrrrrimmmaaaaallll Feeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeaaaaaar! (Primal Fear appears from behind the makeshift entrance curtain set up in the mall, and is followed closely by his new manager, Xavier. The crowd reaction is a mixture of boos and cheers, with neither side having a clear majority.) BD: The man has finally realized what made him a star in the first place, and gone back to his roots. We haven't seen this Primal Fear in quite some time. He looks ready to tear apart anyone who gets in his way, and he could do it too. MW: Yes, but at what price did Primal Fear obtain this new image? He has obviously lost a lot of fans because of it. LB: It's NOT a new image, it's the image of the REAL Primal Fear, the original Primal Fear. And the old Primal Fear I know couldn't care less what the fans thought. That is his opinion of the fans once again today, and I think with that outlook, he's finally gonna go places here in the AEWA. MW: I don't know, we'll see how it works out, but right now, we're ready to meet the opposition! RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent , From New York City, and weighing in at 320 pounds, "The Shotgun" Staaaaaaaaaaaaan Crrrrraaaaaaaaaaaaawwwwwfoooooooooooorrrrd! (Filter's "Hey Man Nice Shot" plays loudly as Crawford makes his way down the aisle, to a HUGE pop from the crowd. He looks ready for the fight of his life, and walks confidently toward the ring, eyeballing his opponent all the way.) BD: Crawford has no idea what he's in for tonight. MW: Oh, I think he does, and Primal Fear's in for much of the same. LB: Get serious, Wilson. Crawford doesn't have a shot in hell of winning this one. MW: I am serious, this won't be a cake walk for either man. BD: Cake? Where? Why didn't anybody give me a piece? I'm hungry, dammit. MW: There is no cake. BD: But you said there was! Ya damn liar. Why you have to get my hopes up like that and then crush them? MW: Just forget it, okay? We've got a match that just got underway. Crawford was just BLINDSIDED by Primal Fear as he stepped into the ring! LB: I told ya he wasn't gonna give Crawford a chance. MW: Please, Bertha. The match is two seconds old. LB: Yeah, but it's already over, just look at Primal Fear lay into him. He's not gonna let this one slip away, that's for sure. MW: Not if he has any choice in the matter. He HAMMERS away with a flurry of rights and lefts, and ALREADY he tosses Crawford to the outside! BD: And now we're in Primal Fear's domain. This is where he's at his best. MW: There's no doubting that. Primal Fear picks Crawford up now, and SLAMS him down to the concrete! Crawford is writhing in agony! And now what's Primal Fear doing? He's separating the guardrail! LB: It's easier to hit people with it when you break it into sections, you know. MW: I can imagine. Primal Fear's got the section of guardrail that he wants now, and he lifts it up, and DROPS it across the neck of Crawford! That do could permanent damage! BD: I think that's the idea. MW: Crawford's really in trouble now! Primal Fear picks him up now AND GORILLA PRESSES HIM! Crawford's got nowhere to go but down now, AND PRIMAL FEAR JUST TOSSES HIM OUT INTO THE CROWD, AND THE FANS SCATTER! LB: They'd better, unless they wanna become casualties of war. MW: Primal Fear follows him into the seating area, which is COMPLETELY filled with folding chairs, since this isn't an arena! Primal Fear finds a chair that he likes, and he winds up, and just CLOCKS Crawford, who was trying to get up, upside the head with it! And Crawford falls helplessly back to the floor! BD: This is a massacre. It's not even funny! MW: Primal Fear nails him with that chair once more for good measure, and now here's the cover! One.. Two.. Thr.. Kickout! LB: What the hell? Is this guy human? MW: He certainly is, but that was a superhuman effort to kick out right there. Crawford's got a lot of heart, that's for sure. BD: The only thing kicking out did for him is prolong his agony. Primal's not letting up in his assault. MW: No, he's not. He picks up Crawford now, and he's got him by the hair. Where're they going? LB: Well, Primal's obviously taking Crawford somewhere, but your guess is as good as mine. This is a huge ass mall. MW: That's an understatement. Primal Fear's still pulling Crawford along by the hair, and it appears as if he's heading for the escalator! Primal stops in front of the up escalator, and he rams Crawford, head first, RIGHT into the point on the end of those moving stairs! BD: And that busted Crawford open REAL good, and he's headed upstairs. MW: Not by any choice of his, he's lying on the escalator! And Primal Fear gets on with him, and CONTINUES to pound his head into those steps as they slowly ride to the top! LB: Face it, in this one, Crawford's done. MW: I'm not gonna count him out until all the chips have fallen, it's not over yet. BD: It will be soon, VERY soon. MW: They're nearing the top, and what's Primal Fear doing now? He's just holding Crawford's head down, between two steps. They reach the top, and, OH MY GOD! As that escalator closed up, Crawford's head was caught between the steps, and it ripped several HUGE gashes in his forehead. LB: If he doesn't get knocked out soon, he'll pass out from loss of blood. Either way, it doesn't look good for Stan Crawford. MW: No, it doesn't. Primal Fear picks Crawford up now, and they're heading for the stores now! And would you look at this? Half of the stores on this corridor are closing up, just because they see them coming! BD: These storekeepers knew this match was coming, and they don't wanna get their stores messed up. I think it was a good idea. MW: You're probably right. Primal stops in front of the Radio Shack, which is still open! There's a LOT of expensive electronic stuff to break in there, and Primal Fear knows it! The clerk inside looks VERY worried! LB: This should be cool! MW: Primal grabs Crawford and whips him towards the TV Display at the front of the store, but the clerk manages to pull down the front cage before he gets there! Crawford SMACKS right into it, and collapses onto the mall floor! BD: That might've been even MORE effective than Primal Fear intended! That clerk was only trying to protect himself and the store, but he ended up helping Primal Fear. MW: He sure did, and Primal Fear is just LAUGHING at what has just happened! Crawford is DOWN, and now Primal moves in! He delivers a BRUTAL falling splash, and now here's the cover! One.. Two.. Thr.. KICKOUT! LB: I have three words for you-- WHAT THE HELL? MW: My thoughts exactly! Crawford is just showing TREMENDOUS amounts of fortitude here, and I don't know WHERE he's getting it from. Primal Fear is incensed though, and he just continues the assault! BD: This is getting ugly. Crawford should've stayed down. MW: Maybe you're right. Primal Fear is just beating Crawford from pillar to post now, carrying him through the mall, and just RAMMING him into anything and everything that stands in the way!! LB: Just look at that! He's wrecking the whole mall! MW: Primal Fear picks up a steel garbage can now, and just DROPS it over the head of Crawford! And now he's marching into Macy's with him! BD: They should've closed while they had the chance. MW: I'll have to agree. Primal Fear WHIPS him into a clothing display, and T-shirts fly everywhere as Crawford CRASHES into the steel rack, knocking it over! Primal Fear picks him up now, and it appears he see something he likes, they're on the move. LB: They're heading over towards that bathroom! You know what that means. MW: I'm hoping that I don't. Primal Fear stops short of the bathroom, and now he's looking at the camera. PRIMAL FEAR: (pointing at the mens room) That's overdone.... but this isn't! MW: And Primal just RAMS Crawford, head first, into the drinking fountain! BD: See what a nice guy Primal Fear is? He lets his opponents have a drink of water to help refresh them in the middle of a match. MW: Yeah, along with bashing their bloody heads into the fountain. What a humanitarian. Primal picks up Crawford now, and he's heading towards the elevator! They reach it, and the customers waiting for it just SCRAMBLE! LB: Good move, I would. MW: As would I. And Primal grabs Crawford now, and whips him to the steel doors--BUT THEY OPEN--Crawford goes into the elevator--AND THE DOORS CLOSE BEHIND HIM!! Primal Fear is upset! He presses the elevator button, but it looks like it already went down! BD: Big deal. Primal'll just get him when the elevator comes back up. MW: Primal Fear is just BANGING on that elevator door now, but I don't see what good that's gonna do. He's just gonna have to wait. It looks like the elevator's coming back up now, and the match should resume shortly. LB: You mean the BEATING shall resume shortly. MW: The doors open, but there's no one inside! Crawford must've staggered out on the first floor! BD: He couldn't have gotten far in that condition. He's probably collapsed outside the door. MW: Well Primal Fear's getting into the elevator now, and our camera man and the referee are following him in. He's going down to the first floor, and Crawford SHOULD be down there. LB: Yeah, ready and waiting for more pain. MW: The doors open, but there appears to be no one outside them! Primal Fear steps out of the elevator AND GETS NAILED UPSIDE THE HEAD WITH A FIRE EXTINGUISHER! That's Crawford! He must've found that on the wall down here! BD: No fair! He's not allowed to surprise him like that. This isn't hide and seek, this is wrestling! MW: Actually almost NONE of what we've seen in this match has been actual WRESTLING. Nonetheless, it's been a WILD brawl, and Crawford is in control for the first time in the match! He continues to beat Primal Fear with that fire extinguisher, and now here's the cover! One.. Two.. Kickout! LB: Primal's barely softened up. He's not ready to be pinned yet. MW: Maybe not, but Crawford's doing everything he can to MAKE him ready. Crawford picks up Primal now and drags him out into the main area of the store. They're in the women's lingerie section! BD: My personal favorite. MW: Now Crawford grabs a bra, and he's strangling Primal Fear with it! LB: I never thought I'd see THAT in the AEWA. MW: He still holding Primal Fear with that bra, and he's using it to DRAG him around the store by the neck! The man could choke to death! BD: I don't think Crawford cares too much about that right now. MW: I'd be inclined to agree. He picks up Primal Fear, AND THEY'RE GOING INTO THE WOMEN'S FITTING ROOM! Crawford whips Primal Fear through the door, and into one of the compartments! And several half-clothed women are running for the door! BD: WOW, would you look at those ti-- MW: Big Daddy PLEASE, this is a family show. BD: Yeah, and after seeing THOSE, I'm thinking about starting a family right now. Care to help me out Bertha? I'm sure we can find a motel room somewhere.... LB: In your dreams, pal. MW: She sure told you, Bi-- BD: Just shut up, Wilson. MW: Fine. And now Crawford has followed Primal Fear into that compartment, and he picks the big man up, and RAMS him HEAD FIRST into that full length mirror on the wall! It shattered! And Primal Fear is busted open! LB: The blood sure has been flowing in this one. MW: That's for sure. And now Crawford grabs a fragment of the mirror off the wall, and JAMS it into the already bloody forehead of Primal Fear! He's ripping that cut WIDE open! BD: Just like Primal did to him. MW: That's right, this is sweet revenge for Crawford! He's ripping that cut open, and the blood is flowing like a river. And now Crawford gets up AND GETS NAILED OVER THE HEAD WITH AN ALUMINUM BASEBALL BAT!! LB: Where the hell did that come from! MW: It was Xavier! Primal Fear's new manager! And now he's continuing the beating! BD: Hey, you know, this guy was really a great find for Primal Fear. He really knows how to manage. MW: You mean he knows how to stick his nose in where it doesn't belong. He helps Primal Fear up now, and hands him the bat! But Primal's a little disoriented, he can barely stand! LB: Xavier's shouting orders at him though, and he's pulling himself together. MW: Fear's regaining his composure, and now HE'S beating Crawford with that bat! He puts the bat across the throat of Crawford now, AND HE STOMPS ON IT! That had to hurt! And now he's dragging Crawford back out into the main store area! BD: What will they think to do next? MW: Who knows, but I can guarantee it's gonna be violent. Primal Fear RAMS Crawford into a nearby pillar, and now something has caught his eye, and he smiles an evil grin! I don't know what it is, but he's dragging Crawford toward the jewelry section! LB: Maybe he sees some good necklaces to strangle people with. MW: Who knows. Primal Fear is now pulling Crawford toward a large glass display case, and he places him up lying down on top of it! What's he gonna do now? BD: It looks like Primal Fear is climbing up on top of the case himself! This doesn't look like it's gonna be pretty! MW: Primal Fear is now standing on top of that glass jewelry case, above the motionless body of Stan Crawford! AND HE GIVES THE SIGNAL FOR THE BUMP SPLASH! He jumps up for it, BUT CRAWFORD ROLLS OFF OF THE DISPLAY--AND PRIMAL FEAR COMES CRASHING DOWN THROUGH THE JEWELRY CASE ALL BY HIMSELF!! Glass flies everywhere, and Primal Fear just got himself SEVERELY lacerated ALL OVER HIS BODY! Stan Crawford manages to drape his arm over the now motionless Primal Fear! One.. Two.. Three!!!! (DING, DING) LB: Is it any surprise that he got the three after THAT? RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of eighteen minutes and twenty-two seconds, here is your winner.... "SHOTGUN" STAN CRAWFORD!!!! MW: What a WAR! In all my years in wrestling, I've NEVER seen a match quite like that one. BD: Same here, that was the CRAZIEST brawl I've ever seen, and I've seen a LOT of crazy brawls, and was involved in most of them. MW: All I know is that both men are gonna be going to the hospital after this one, and the AEWA is gonna get a HUGE damage bill from the Mall of America. LB: That's for sure, but what a match, eh? MW: Yep, that's gonna be one that we remember for a LONG time to come, that's for sure. With everything that happened tonight, it's gonna be hard to forget. BD: Our producer is signaling for us to sign off, so shouldn't we wrap this program up now? The Mall business owners seem to be pretty pissed off with our AEWA officials right now. MW: Yes, I'm afraid that's all the time we have for this week, so for the Lovely Bertha and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt, this is Matt Wilson saying so long everybody! We'll see you at Saturday Night Slam, folks.
