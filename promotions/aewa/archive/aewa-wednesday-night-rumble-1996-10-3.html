Wednesday Night Rumble (10/3)

3 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power Bomb, and a look at Mister Crowley DRIVING the head of Volcano Jackson into the concrete with the Premature Burial! Then the camera switches to the Lovely Bertha, Matt Wilson, and "Big Daddy" Mike Hurt in a packed arena) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Wednesday Night Rumble! We have PLENTY of great matches scheduled for tonight! BD: In the main event, the Holy Ghost will be defending his Light Heavyweight title against El Aviadore! That should be a VERY interesting match, especially since Deathwatch is in the building tonight. LB: But what's first? MW: The Visigothic Horde are about to face some amateurs, and this should be a VERY fast match. BD: Those barbarians haven't exactly been on a winning streak lately, buddy. In fact, they REALLY need a victory tonight. LB: They've been feuding with the Graveyard Shift for over a month now, and the Horde hasn't done well. But don't worry, they're going to DOMINATE in the near future. (Alaric leads Athanaric and Fritigern to the ring.) BD: Where the hell is Kevin Larkin? MW: Due to some recent incidents on the "Kevin Larkin Show", the AEWA Board Of Directors has temporarily suspended him. He isn't even allowed at ringside. BD: That's TOTALLY unfair. I don't feel like arguing with you, though, so let's just watch this match.... The Visigothic Horde (Athanaric and Fritigern) versus Sammy McSlam and Peter Penthouse This was not at all pretty. Three months in the AEWA have not been enough to convince the Horde of the necessity of tagging in and out, so McSlam and Penthouse were treated to their first ever Texas Tornado match. After about three minutes of one-sided brawling, Fritigern power bombed one of the hapless jobbers and Athanaric topped it off with a flying fistdrop for the three count. Before the three count was completed, Fritigern was halfway to the locker rooms. Alaric brought him back for some sort of in-ring celebration, but Fritigern seemed confused, so he began an attack on the opposition again and refused to leave until Athanaric had scored another three-count. LB: Do they get credit for two wins for this? MW: Of course not. After what the Shift has done to them, of course, they should be happy to have gotten any kind of win at all. It's certainly been long enough since the last one. BD: Shut up, Wilson. The West Coast Connection will be taking on the Crew in our next match, and I love BOTH of those teams, so this should be exciting. LB: These teams are looking to get noticed by our championship committee, so they'll all be "turning it up a notch" tonight. ("Throw Your Set in the Air" plays as Axl Sharkman and MC Read arrive, with Chris Grohl and DJ Muggs X in tow.) BD: Here come the former World tag team champions, and they look ready for business. MW: They've been on a losing streak lately, but things are probably going to change after tonight..... (As "YMCA" begins playing loudly, Cool Carlson and Groovy Gonzalez run to the ring. They are dressed rather uniquely tonight-- Carlson is wearing an Indian headdress, and Gonzalez is dressed in a police uniform. Their manager, Spirit, follows closely behind.) BD: Jeez, they REALLY know how to make an entrance. I haven't seen two guys get so excited about the song "YMCA" since.... well, never. MW: Let's just watch the match.... The Crew (Cool Carlson and Groovy Gonzalez) versus The West Coast Connection (Axl Sharkman and MC Read) The West Coast Connection pummeled their smaller opponents for the entire match. Axl Sharkman's submission holds neutralized the Crew's speed advantage, as he concentrated his attack on their lower limbs. Carlson seemed to spend an eternity in a figure four leglock, which was only broken by the referee's order, after Read grew tired of waiting for Carlson to submit and delivered a superfly splash on his prone opponent. Read stayed in the ring to continue the match while Sharkman headed for their corner, despite not having tagged out. Read, the most agile 300-pounder in the AEWA, took the action off of the mat and into the air. This did not bode well for the usually quick and high-flying Crew, as they'd had their wings clipped earlier in the match. After Read planted Carlson with a Northern Lights Suplex, two men appeared at ringside. It was Submission, who've had trouble with both teams before, and they were parading around the ring, waving the Alaska state flag, in the strangest veiled threat ever displayed in the AEWA. After all six men watched the Lord of Midnight and Phantom of the Opera come and go, the match resumed. The brief respite gave the Crew a much-needed chance to catch their breath, and when the match restarted, Gonzalez attacked Read with a dropkick and an arm drag takedown. As he celebrated his sequence of maneuvers, Muggs X jumped up on the ring apron, and Grohl handed him a chair. As Gonzalez lunged for the manager, Read regained his balance, and saw Gonzalez's back facing him. He came behind Gonzalez and started pushing him toward the ropes, gaining momentum to roll his opponent backward into a pinning situation. But, at the same moment, Read swung the chair and Gonzalez ducked. Read caught the impact on the flush on his back, between the shoulder blades, and dropped to one knee in pain. Carlson slipped into the ring and slapped a thrust kick on Read, knocking him from one knee to the mat. Then he blocked Sharkman, as Gonzalez covered Read for the three-count at 11:07. The Crew decided to bail out and do their celebrating from a discreet distance up the aisleway, while Sharkman and the two managers attended to Read. BD: Jeez, that was pretty damn shocking! The Crew actually won tonight, and frankly, it surprises me. LB: But why the hell did Submission come out there with the Alaska state flag? Was that some kind of THREAT to the Crew? MW: Of course. BD: The Crew will NOT back down from a challenge. Anyway, we're ready to move on-- so what's next, Wilson? MW: Well, the Holy Ghost is about to defend his Light Heavyweight title against El Aviadore! This should be a GREAT rematch! BD: These two men tried to wrestle once before, but that match was was interrupted by Deathwatch. He attacked both of them, and the bout was declared a "no contest". Hopefully, we won't have any more problems tonight. LB: I don't really like El Aviadore, but he REALLY didn't deserve to be attacked. It was a SICK incident. MW: Let's get down to ringside.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is for the AEWA Light Heavyweight title! (The Holy Ghost makes his entrance, as "Amazing Grace" plays loudly in the background. He wears a white mask, and looks ready for action.) BD: This guy was also hurt pretty badly, and he's still not feeling very good. MW: Deathwatch had absolutely NO reason to attack him, folks. I might not like the Holy Ghost, but that attack was completely unnecessary. (As "La Bamba" blares over the intercom, El Aviadore races to the ring. He gets a rather large pop from the crowd.) BD: Okay, both men are in the ring, and we're ready to start things off! MW: EL Aviadore just nailed him with a roundhouse right! He's not wasting any time in there, folks. LB: The Holy Ghost wasn't even finished with his pre-match prayer! (yelling) This isn't fair! BD: Yeah, he wasn't ready! MW: Aviadore whips the Holy Ghost to the ropes, and comes back with a BIG jumping clothesline! LB: Now El Aviadore gets back to his feet, and goes for a dropkick-- BUT THE GHOST MOVES OUT OF THE WAY, AND LOCKS HIM INTO A BOSTON CRAB! BD: That was incredible! MW: And now the Holy Ghost is REALLY applying pressure. El Aviadore is slowly crawling toward the ropes.... LB: AND HE MADE IT! The referee just forced the break, and now Aviadore is getting back to his feet. BD: But his legs must be hurting, so this match can't possibly last much longer. (As the match continues, Deathwatch slowly walks down to ringside. He sits down, and appears to be watching the match.) MW: Wait a minute! WHY THE HELL IS HE DOWN HERE? We need to get this guy escorted out of the ringside area-- before anything HORRIBLE happens! LB: Calm down, Wilson. Deathwatch COULD be here to watch the match, so we shouldn't get angry yet. Let's give him the benefit of the doubt. MW: Are you KIDDING me? He's not out here to WATCH the match, and that's pretty damn obvious. He has NO RIGHT to be at ringside! BD: Anyway, let's just concentrate on this bout. El Aviadore just nailed the Holy Ghost with a shoulderblock! MW: And now Aviadore goes up to the top rope-- AND EXECUTES A FLYING MOONSAULT! That was absolutely incredible! LB: Where did he EVER find the energy for that move? BD: Wait a minute.... El Aviadore has just noticed Deathwatch, and he's going out after him! We're about to have a freaking BLOODBATH over there! MW: Aviadore just NAILED him, and Deathwatch wasn't expecting that! Now they're brawling all over the ringside area-- ALL HELL IS BREAKING LOOSE! LB: The Holy Ghost is still down in the ring, so he's completely unaware of what's going on. The referee is DESPERATELY trying to get Deathwatch out of there, but it's not working! BD: Aviadore just SLAMMED Deathwatch into that steel guardrail, and now he's POUNDING away on him! MW: We need some security guards down here.... BD: Deathwatch wasn't doing anything wrong this time-- he was just SITTING there! What's wrong with THAT? (yelling) Aviadore has completely gone nuts, and he won't stop punching him! MW: This is sweet revenge, Big Daddy! Aviadore got sent to the hospital last week, and he's looking for justice tonight. LB: The Holy Ghost has finally gotten up, and now he's JOINING the brawl at ringside! He's teaming up with Aviadore, and they're BOTH attacking Deathwatch! Why doesn't anyone STOP this? BD: I can't believe my eyes.... MW: Aviadore and the Holy Ghost are actually putting aside their differences for tonight, and they're TEAMING up! Deathwatch can't handle this sudden attack, so he's running back to the locker room now. LB: We need to get some damn control around here, because that went WAY too far. Why didn't any security guards step in? MW: Because nobody LIKES Deathwatch! He has been attacking our AEWA wrestlers for TOO long, and he PLANNED to do it tonight, but the Holy Ghost and Aviadore decided to stop him. BD: It STILL wasn't fair. By the way, the referee has declared that match a "double countout". Anyway, we're quickly running out of time, so let's wrap this show up. MW: Good idea. (turns to the camera) This weekend, we're going to be holding "Mega Combat: The Rising Sun", so call your local cable company today! LB: See you there....
