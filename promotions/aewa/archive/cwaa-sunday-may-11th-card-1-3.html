sunday, May 11th, Card (1/3)

11 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last Week on Championship Wrestling Association of America Eric Lane: Hammer up now- vicious forearm shiver in the back. Hammer now sets him up- brainbuster suplex- he just dropped the champ right on his head! And the Major up on the apron, flagpole at the ready! Hammer over- grabs the Major by the throat! (Enormous pop) EL: Jak Hammer choking out the Major, who has that flagpole- Lt. Lawson coming to his manger's aid- a knee in Jak Hammer's back- Marc Thomas involving himself now- the Major swings the pole- Thomas grabs it- tug of war on the apron- Thomas yanks it free- and it smacks the champ right in the head! Lawson collapses- he was just stomping away at Jak Hammer, and now he gets a headful of flagpole! *************************************************************************** Eric Lane: Good evening, everyone, and welcome back for another exciting edition of Championship Wrestling, from here in the world-famous Sportatorium in lovely Dallas, Texas. As you just saw in that last clip, things got a little out of control here as we had some of the most devious individuals in, well, the whole state of Texas out here last week and threw them together. When the smoke cleared, Lt. Lawson was still the Television Champion, and then he and Max Robertson tried to do a number on AEWA World Heavyweight Champion, Heavy Metal, but came up on the short end of the stick, and a painful stick it was. We've got another full slate of action for you hear tonight. We'll see Joshua Barnes, a mainstay here in the Sportatorium whom we haven't seen in a few weeks. He'll be here to tackle "Hotshot" Gary Jackson. Also on tap, Mark Sherrick was set to square off against "Hardcore" Joey Haven, but last we heard Joey Haven had taken other employment elsewhere. I know a lot of the great fans here in the Sportatorium had taken a real shine to the Philadelphia native, but Joey Haven will not be joining us again any time soon, it appears. I know none of us will soon forget his titanic battles with Jak Hammer, some of the most vicious encounters this historic arena has seen since the days of Abdullah the Butcher and Bruiser Brody, and I'm sure I speak for everyone here tonight when I wish Joey the best of luck wherever he may go. Little Yvette is here tonight, and he's going to be teaming up with "The Preacher" Kore Wrench to take on Marc Thomas and Jak Hammer. Now both Yvette and Wrench have had their troubles with these two, well, I don't want to use the word "gentlemen", "thugs" is more like it. So we're going to get everyone in the same match together and let them sort it out themselves. Every time you see Marc Thomas or Jak Hammer, the other gets involved anyway, so we're just going to make it all legal and let the Preacher and the Seattle sensation do it right back. Co-tag team champion Travis Conley will go up against Byron McNultey, the self-professed "Pretty Boy", and the feud between those tow teams shows no signs of letting up. I know Paul Langford is still trying to get a doctor's clearance to get back in the ring with his partner, and, well, I may not think it fair myself, but if they can't get back in the ring and defend those titles real soon, they're going to have to give them up. But tonight it is one-on-one action, Travis Conley and the "Pretty Boy". Then, in our special feature match- and this one should be just terrific, folks- former CWAA Heavyweight Champion "Dreamer" Randy Sheradin goes for the TV Title, held by Lt. Daniel "Lethal" Lawson. These two have been on opposite sides more than once, and it's been a barn-burner each time. We're going to go to the ring right now and hear from a pair of very popular rising stars in the CWAA who we're going to see teaming up tonight for the first time- so let's go to the ring. David Myers: Ladies and gentlemen, at this time, please welcome my guests- ("My Wave" by Soundgarden plays and the crowd pops) DM: -Little Yvette and "Preacher" Kore Wrench! (Little Yvette shows up in his blue singlet with the now-familiar eagle design on the back, and the Preacher is dressed in his usual priestly garb. Yvette stands in the vicinity of 5'10"-240, while his partner extends to 6'4"-275. Both are quite muscular.) DM: Little Yvette, a word if I may- Little Yvette: I've got my first win under my belt, and it was a heck of a fight. I have never been so proud of myself in my life and from here on in it just gets better. Now the CWAA fans (sheep that they are, they pop at the mere mention of their presence- actually, I have never really seen sheep popping...) have seen what I can do and I bring lots of things they never see to the plate. I'm glad they're appreciating it and I'm not about to change. I'm a high flyin' kid who's ready to go out and give it his all to win your respect, and tonight I will be going out to do my best for Kore Wrench (points to the Preacher while the crowd reacts in the expected fashion as the Preacher nods and claps his partner's back), the man who has given me a chance, and I won't disappoint. Hammer, Thomas you guys are rotten and I can't wait to show the fans that the good wins over the bad. See you from over your head! (Yvette positions himself on the second turnbuckle, facing the crowd, to take in their adulation as Myers moves on...) DM: Preacher, last week, during your match with Jak Hammer, a masked man interfered and cost you the match. Who was he? Marc Thomas in disguise perhaps? Kore Wrench: I'd rather not speculate at this point. I suspect that things will become clearer with the passage of time. But regardless of Thomas's involvement in that situation, he is a dangerous and angry man who needs to be taught a lesson. DM: And you intend to teach him that lesson? KW: And I intend to teach him that lesson right here tonight with my friend Little Yvette. (More pops for the Preacher's idea) DM: Are there other tag-team plans for the two of you in the future? KW: Well, I don't want to rule anything out. If things go well, that's certainly a possibility. My battle against evil is ruthless, and it's nice to have an ally, but my mission is my own. First and foremost, I'll always be a singles wrestler, but Little Yvette is a noble ally whom I would like to work with in the future. (Wrench and Yvette exchange high fives and exit the ring area) EL: OK- when we get back from this break, we'll jump right into the action, as Gary Jackson meets Joshua Barnes! You won't want to miss this, so don't you dare touch that dial! (Wednesday night on the WYXZ TV Channel 39 movie of the week, a giant flying radioactive turtle threatens to destroy Tokyo- but one little boy understands him. Can Tokyo be saved? Find out, as WYXZ TV Channel 39 presents "Gamera," Wednesday night at 8:00) DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this first contest is set for one fall with a 10-minute time-limit. First, from Brownsburg, IN, weighing in at 292 lb., here is Joshua Barnes! ("Dream On" plays over the speakers, as Randy Sheridan and Joshua Barnes make their way down the aisle, shaking hands with all (well, several) of the fans along the way. They enter the ring, and shake hands with Dave Myers.) DM: So both of you have a match tonight, and Randy, you have a chance to win the Television Title from Lt. Lawson. Randy Sheridan: I'm looking forward to it. Josh and I have been concentrating on Robertson, for obvious reasons, but Lawson is probably just as big a disgrace. He wouldn't even have that belt if it weren't for the Major's interference, but with Josh watching my back, I'm confident he won't be a factor, and Lawson won't be able to win on his own. Joshua Barnes: Gary Jackson beat me in the tournament, thanks to Max Robertson. I plan on winning this time. Hopefully, someone will soon beat Max for that belt. But we also want to talk about NcMurley Anderson...McNultrey and Anderson. Not only did you cost us that match, you injured Paul Langford, who is a good man. Travis, if you want a partner, or just someone to watch your back, ask myself or Randy any time. (Wow- he actually got that out without stumbling. And of course the crowd pops for the offer.) JB: Um... Randy, I hope you mind... don't mind that I spoke for you. RS: That's not a problem. Paul and Travis are good guys. And Robertson, watch tonight very carefully. Because one of us will be relieving you of that belt very soon. DM: His opponent, from Houston, Texas, weighing in at 250 lb., here is "Hotshot" Gary Jackson! (The crowd boos the t-shirt-and-blue-jean-wearing ex-mechanic and his partner, Eddie Intellecta, and bodyguard, "Hardcore" Henry King. Jackson's ever-growing beer belly betrays his need to invest in a new t-shirt. And a belt.) DM: Your referee for this contest, Jeff Howard. EL: All right- a little crowded around the ring as both Barnes and Jackson have some reinforcements with them for this one. Joshua hands his glasses to the ring att- hey! Gary Jackson with a double sledge as Barnes had his back turned- he'll learn as he gets more experienced never to turn his back on an opponent, especially one as ruthless as the Hotshot. Now Jackson with a wicked kneelift. Irish whip- and Barnes with a shoulderblock that puts Jackson down on his keester. Now Barnes in a three-point stance- and he just bowls right over him- a football tackle- I wonder if he learned that one from Travis Conley. Just think about that, if say Travis Conley were to team up with a Joshua Barnes or even the ex-champ while Paul Langford gets healthy again. Conley and Barnes- think of the power in that duo. All right- Joshua using some of that power now as he goes to a standing side headlock. Of course, there is not a lot in Jackson's head to damage, if you understand me. Tough grappler in the ring, although he's getting the business end of a headlock right now, but Gary Jackson won't be winning any Nobel prizes anytime soon. Eddie Intellecta giving Jeff Howard a lot of lip about something or other, but Jeff has been a pro for some time now, and he takes no notice of Intellecta. Now Barnes with a headlock takedown, and Gary Jackson will, have quite an uphill battle to get out from under over 300 lb. of Joshua Barnes. (Intellecta exchanges words with Sheridan, who points to the ring and shows Intellecta who's winning right now.) EL: Randy Sheridan won't want to get too involved with Eddie Intellecta- Henry King is out there, too, and they've got him outnumbered. Watch out- Eddie Intellecta up on the ring apron, and Jeff Howard right on top of this situation, threatening the disqualification. Joshua Barnes now over to get his 2 cents in, and Eddie hops to the floor- he doesn't want to get in Barnes' face. Barnes turns- Jackson waiting for him- hip toss- and Joshua Barnes with the reversal! Now Barnes to an arm bar. Well, that little bit of chicanery came to naught. Big Joshua Barnes pretty much in control of this one from the get go. Well, now the Hotshot has a handful of hair and pulls Barnes back into the corner. Jeff Howard calling for a clean break- and Jackson with a forearm uppercut! He just stunned Barnes with that one- now he whips the Indiana native across the ring, and he hit that corner hard. Wow! Big clothesline by the Hotshot flattens Barnes, and, well, will you look at that. (Booing begins) EL: Gary Jackson trying to show off his physique to the crowd. He's on that Schlitz diet and training regimen, it appears. Off the ropes- frog splash by the Hotshot. (Sheridan starts leading a chant of "Go, Joshua, Go!" It's so much easier when their names only have 2 syllables...) EL: All right, the Dreamer leading the cheers, trying to rally behind his partner. Gary Jackson with a chop. Now Jackson goes downstairs with the kick- setting him up- yes- swinging neckbreaker, and Joshua Barnes in big trouble right now. Cover- 1... 2... kickout. Jackson stands his man up now- waistlock- German suplex. Very nicely executed, too- it's easy to forget sometimes just how accomplished a professional he is. Eddie Intellecta has a hold of Barnes' leg as Jackson goes for the pin- caught him red-handed! Jeff Howard cautioning Jackson and Intellecta about this blatant rulebreaking. They're just helping their foe by arguing it- Jackson should keep right on top of his man, keeping the offense going- now Barnes grabs him- airplane spin! Gary Jackson looking a little woozy- Samaon drop! 1... 2... no- kickout. Barnes nearly made him pay for that little bit of rulebreaking. Both men up- Jackson on spaghetti legs- short clothesline puts him right back down. Barnes off the ropes- elbow drop. (Sheridan applauds his partner's efforts) EL: Now he sends him into the corner. And Gary Jackson is introduced- up close and personal- to the turnbuckle. That's real steel beneath that padding- it can really smart. And- hey! Gary Jackson goes to the eyes- I don't care how big you are, your eyes are always vulnerable, and the smallest foe can do tremendous damage to the biggest giant with a quick blow like that. No Gary Jackson- still not quite completely in control of his faculties- slams Ba- no! Reversed, and Jackson goes head-first into the buckle again! (Pop as Jackson staggers to mid rign, teeters for a few seconds, then falls flat on his face) EL: Barnes sends him back to the corner- whip across the ring- catches him- spinebuster slam! This should do it! 1... 2... no! Barnes gets up and goes after Eddie Intellecta, back on the ring apron again! Keep an eye on the Hotshot, he's taking that ankle brace off- amazing how he still wears it- he hasn't favored either leg all match- he's got it up- and Randy Sheridan grabes it- yanks it right out of his hand! (Pop for the resourceful Dreamer) EL: Barnes pops Intellecta one- turns- sees Jackson arguing with Sheridan- rolls him up- 1... 2... 3! He got it! DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, Joshua Barnes! (Gary Jackson complains to the referee about his ankle brace while Intellecta kicks the bottom rope and Barnes and Sheridan exit, slapping hands with a few lucky fans on the way. As always, Henry King does nothing but stand there, arms crossed and expressionless.) EL: Joshua Barnes with the big win. Double Jeopardy tried more than once to cheat their way to victory, but Barnes pulls it out. We're going to take another break in a few minutes, but first, let's talk to the CWAA TV Champion. EL: Well, (sighs), we have an interview with the CWAA Television champion, and you know what that means... {"When Johnny Comes Marching Home Again" plays over the PA system as the Major and Lt. Lawson walk down the aisle. Lawson is wearing the CWAA TV Title strapped around his waist. Major Victor Mayhem walk over to Lane, and starts screaming. The microphone picks up halfway through his tirade.} MVM: ... and you salute when a superior officer approaches you! I am putting you on report, Private! EL: I'm not a private. MVM: You will be when I am through with you. This is insubordination on the highest levels! And just when we are in the middle of a major insurrection by the outlaw states. EL: Outlaw states? MVM: Do you have a hearing problem? I said outlaw states- in a covert conspiracy to deprive the good Lieutenant of his richly-deserved title. EL: But... MVM: Do not interrupt me! First, that Communist Tyrant Jak "Hammer-and-Sickle" and his ally Marc "Iceland" Thomas- I always knew the people from Iceland were troublemakers- attacked the Lieutenant and myself from behind, like the cowards they are. Then, after we brilliantly maneuver them into a secondary war with the chaplain, the chaplain decided to join their side, throw his match with Hammer-and-Sickle, and give him an easy match. EL: But... MVM: THEN... Hammer and Sickle and his Iceland partner stole OUR American Flag and used it against the good Lieutenant in his match, trying to permanently injure this fine soldier. Finally, after all the battles he has fought, the know-nothing bureaucrats in the CWAA offices put Lt. Lawson against that hippie dreamer from Des Moines, Randy Sheridan. (Pop for Sheridan's name from those near Eric Lane's table) MVM: Did I give anyone permission to speak?!? NO! It's obvious that there is a worldwide conspiracy to steal the TV Title from this man. This evil cabal of Hammer, Thomas, Wrench, Sheridan, and Barnes have joined forces to steal from this fine American. They even plan their scheme in UN Black Helicopters, and are sponsored by secret anti-American spy groups! They will go to any means, any method... {By now, the poor Major is having a conniption fit, and looks close to having a heart attack. His face is turning a fascinating shade of purple. Eric Lane decides that it's a good idea to hand the microphone to the champion.} Daniel Lawson: Sheridan... the referee gave you a break in the CWAA World Title Tournament. You will not have that luck this time. And all of allies- Jak Hammer, Marc Thomas, Kore Wrench, Joshua Barnes... none of them will prevent me from squashing you like the insignificant little bug you are. (The Major has apparently calmed down enough, and the two army men walk away.) EL: OK- we're going to take a quick break, and when we get BA- wait a second! (The crowd boos as the masked man from last week appears again. He stands in the vicinity of 6'0" and weighs somewhere around 250 lb., dressed in black leather). EL: What are you doing out he- MM: Wrench, you are only the first in a long line of people on my hit list. I dropped you on your head and cost you a chance at the title! You will not be the only person who finds themselves on the receiving end of a DDT. No one in the CWAA is safe, not even you Lane! (Thrusts a finger in Lane's chest and leaves just as quickly as he came) EL: I don't know what to make of this- we really have to take that break now. We'll be right back!
