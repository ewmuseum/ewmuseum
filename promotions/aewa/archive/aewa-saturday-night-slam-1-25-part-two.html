Saturday Night Slam (1/25, part two)

27 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
- - - - CONTINUED FROM PART ONE - - - - MW: It's now time for another episode of the Terrordome, folks. Johnny is going to be interviewing the Shadow Warriors. BD: Well, those guys have been complaining about a conspiracy lately, so this should be interesting, MW: Let's watch.... (Public Enemy's "Welcome to the Terrordome" plays loudly throughout the arena as the camera shifts to a familiar set near the back of the arena. Hanging over this set is a large half-dome. Underneath the dome, there is a very dark, dank room FILLED with various gruesome looking torture devices. AEWA manager Johnny Terror emerges from behind the curtain, and begins to be booed at previously unheard of sound levels. Johnny takes it all in stride, and heads for the stage with a big grin on his face. There, he picks up a microphone, and begins to speak.) Terror: It's always nice to know that you guys still care. (Johnny mockingly wipes a tear from his eyes, and then begins to laugh). So anyway, you people are once again privileged to be witnessing first hand the greatest interview show of all time.... Piper's Pit! (HUGE crowd pop) No, wait a minute, that's not right. WHO WROTE THAT ON MY CARD? Somebody's gonna get fired here! I mean it! (crowd laughs) Anyway, you're REALLY watching the Terrordome, starring me, your WONDERFUL host-- Johnny Terror! (LOUD BOOS!) That's more like it. And now for my guests this week. They are perhaps the greatest AEWA National Tag Team Champions EVER, and they are looking to get some more gold in the VERY near future. So right now please welcome, accompanied by their manager, Wesley Cantrell, and The Lovely Lianne, here are Reuben "Thunder" Slade and "Superfly" Aaron Ladd, THE SHADOW WARRIORS! (The Shadow Warriors come out to a chorus of boos. They're wearing shirts that say "The True World Champions" and have a couple of obviously fake belts stating "AEWA Tag Team Champions" around their waists. Cantrell and Lianne follow right after them, and Cantrell is carrying his usual briefcase. On their way to the ring, they stop and look at a large man, shaking their heads in disgust while laughing in his face.) Ladd: Alright! Feels great to finally be here. It's nice to know we have some unbiased interviewers around here, who aren't afraid to listen to our words of wisdom. It's a change from the usual sluts handling the interviews around here. Slade: Yeah, ever since the AEWA decided to limit our airtime and censor us, we've been waiting around patiently, 'cause we knew our opportunity would finally arrive. Cantrell: (while still looking at the large man in the crowd) I never cease to be amazed in this world, buddy. I must say, I really didn't know that men could become pregnant, but looking at a few of the ugly inbreed hicks here tonight has convinced me of the opposite. By the way, now that I have the opportunity to say so, didn't you just love the way President Freeman was manhandled at the December Doom pay-per-view? Terror: I can always enjoy a good beating, no matter WHO is on the receiving end of it, but I'd be lying if I said I didn't particularly enjoy seeing that chump, Freeman, finally get what was coming to him. But let's talk about you guys. That's what we're here for, after all. We haven't seen a whole lot of you around the AEWA in recent times. First of all, what's the deal here, and second of all, will that change in the near future? Slade: Well, the new commissioners around here didn't particularly appreciate what we did to their "pretty boy" tag teams, and they're trying to keep us as far away from them as possible. Because of this, our airtime has been severely limited. Ladd: They were obviously trying to keep us away from what was rightfully ours to begin with, the titles. Perhaps they wanted us to leave so their babyfaces could go play with each other and bore everyone to death, but that's not gonna happen. We don't appreciate being pushed around by a couple of jackasses in suits. We do whatever the hell we want, and whenever the hell we want to. You should get that straight. Lianne: Everyone knows who the only true tag team champions of the AEWA are, and it's these two standing right here beside me. There is a conspiracy going on against the Cantrell Corporation and the Shadow Warriors, and it's about time the world knew about it. Cantrell: Not that it really matters though, 'cause in the end, we'll get what we want. I've invested far too much money on feeding these imbecile AEWA officials to let it all go to waste. As far as I'm concerned, the titles have ALREADY been bought and paid for, so they should just hand them over to us, don't you agree? Terror: Of course, but the moron champions always INSIST on actually FIGHTING for their titles. (Johnny rolls his eyes) As if they actually had a chance of winning. (Johnny laughs) But you men OBVIOUSLY have gold on your mind. So I guess the big question is-- are you going to target Graphic Violence, and have another go at the National titles, or are you looking at the Machines, and their World Tag Team Titles? Or does it even matter? Cantrell: You'll find out in due time just what we're after, just like everyone else will, but we have no intention of revealing our future plans for right now. When the proper time arrives, the guilty will be punished, and that's a promise I WILL make. I think I can safely say that without a shadow of a doubt, the Shadow Warriors have already proven their superiority in the tag team division right here in the AEWA. Ladd: We don't really care who it is. It can be the Machines, Graphic Violence, the NATO Powers, the Assassins, or whoever dares to stand in our way. (clenches his fists) And speaking of the Assassins, hell yes we'll take you up on your challenge, but only with a few conditions attached to it. We'll choose the referee for the match, and then you're on. Lianne: And there's no such thing as a "Conspiracy Theory", it's already a proven fact, as any human being with half a brain can vouch for. There IS a conspiracy going on against the Shadow Warriors. Terror: Of course there is. If there wasn't a conspiracy, you men would be World Champions today, without a doubt. For example, at the recent AEWA December Doom pay-per-view. The tag title match was SCHEDULED to be The West Coast Connection vs. Heaven and Hell vs. The Graveyard Shift vs. The Visigothic Horde. But the Horde were unable to compete because of a suspension, and President Freeman picked THE MACHINES to replace them? This is a team that had already wrestled, and LOST, that same night. Meanwhile the Shadow Warriors were just sitting there, not even being given a match on the ENTIRE pay-per-view. I'm sure that had to upset you. Ladd: Now what kind of a stupid question is that? Of COURSE we were upset, Johnny. After all, we were practically guaranteed to walk away with the World Titles that night if we had only been allowed to wrestle. I mean, if a really crappy team like the Machines could win, despite having already been destroyed once that evening in a previous match, then there's no way in hell that WE'D ever lose. Cantrell: And here's where it gets real interesting, 'cause according to this contract (he hands a paper over to Terror) we were supposed to be the replacements if any team dropped out of that fight. You can see it's all in order. Interestingly enough though, it seems that somehow losing the National titles in such a humiliating fashion helped jump the Machines into OUR position. Slade: Which is the position as the number one tag team of the World, which the Machines will pay dearly for stealing from us. They should enjoy whatever little time they may have left, 'cause ya just never know, now do you? Accidents can happen so easily. Cantrell: I guess you can easily understand the tension between the teams when these things happen, and even the frustration my Warriors feel when they're clearly getting brushed aside for less qualified teams. I mean, you would think that after paying so much in "financial support" to the commissioners, as I have, you would be able to trust their word and always get fair treatment. Instead, they're promoting these paupers in favor of us. Terror: Shhhh! Wait a minute! (listening to his earplug.) I'm getting word from the back that the AEWA officials have been listening to your complaints just now. They just said that you DO deserve something, and so they're making a match! Wait! (listens again) They say that since you accepted the challenge of the Assassins earlier, you're going to get them, TONIGHT! And hold on, there's something else.... (listens once again) The winners will meet Graphic Violence for the National Tag Team Titles at the Kickoff '97 Pay-Per-View! What do you have to say to that? Cantrell: Just what I always say in situations like this-- he who has the money always wins! Now I'd like Graphic Violence to name their price, and then we can settle this in a much less violent fashion than would otherwise be necessary. So if you two imbeciles can just go ahead and find yourself someone with the ability to both read and write, I'm sure we can work it all out fine. Oh, and if that doesn't work, I've got something else in mind. (he turns towards Terror and lowers his voice) I wanted to ask you privately how much it would cost to acquire the services of your world-renowned mercenaries, to perform a special "job" I had in mind? Terror: (To Wesley) Well, the price depends on the size of the job, but I'm sure we can work something out. Just lemme wrap this show up, and we'll talk business. (Back to crowd) And with that, we'll end our show for this week. I'd like to thank my guests, Wesley Cantrell, the Lovely Lianne, and The Shadow Warriors for being on the show, and wish them luck in their quest for tag team gold. Look out, Assassins, these guys are coming for you! Don't miss my next show when my guests will be the ENTIRE stable of International Contracts Unlimited! Until then, Hasta la vista, baby! (Johnny drops the microphone where he stands and begins to walk back to the locker room, seemingly having a very intense conversation with Cantrell, who walks beside him. The Shadow Warriors and Lianne follow them back to the locker room. The camera then returns to our broadcast team.) MW: So the Shadow Warriors are going to be wrestling the Assassins tonight? That should be GREAT! BD: And don't forget-- the winners of that match will be going to Kickoff '97! This is a BIG opportunity for those guys, Wilson. MW: Without a doubt. Well, it's now time for our next match, as Primal Fear is about to square off against the Twilight Strangler! BD: This feud has been going on for MONTHS; is it EVER gonna stop? What is this-- the MILLIONTH time they've wrestled? MW: Well, the Strangler managed to pull off a convincing victory last week, and I was actually rather impressed. He's been training with Shane Matthews, and it seems to be paying off. BD: Yeah, Primal Fear's career will be HISTORY after tonight. He was on a winning streak for awhile there, but it's over. MW: I wouldn't be so sure about that, Big Daddy. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, at a weight of 360 pounds, PRIMAL FEAR! (Primal Fear walks down the aisle, to a BIG pop from the crowd. He's wearing a black vest, with brown tights. He looks angry, but slaps hands with the crowd.) BD: Primal Fear has been VERY upset lately, folks. Did you see his last interview on Straight Talk? MW: Yeah, he was complaining about his countout loss to Stan Crawford. He's still bitter about that bout, and now they have a rematch scheduled for Kickoff '97. BD: Primal Fear should be pretty damn embarrassed. He got humiliated by a ROOKIE! MW: Stan Crawford is an excellent wrestler, who has been on a roll lately. I, for one, think he is a GREAT addition to the AEWA. BD: Well, I, for one, think you should shut up! MW: Thanks for the intelligent response, as usual. I can always count on a mature conversation with you, Big Daddy. BD: Any time, buddy. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, coming down the aisle, accompanied by Miss DeMeanor and Johnny Terror, this is THE TWILIGHT STRANGLER! (The Twilight Strangler walks to the ring, with a big smile on his face. Miss DeMeanor and Johnny follow him, locked arm-in-arm.) MW: This guy has changed a lot since he started training with Shane Matthews-- he seems so much more confident anymore. BD: Yeah, and he has a right to be. The Strangler is a completely different wrestler than he used to be, and quite frankly, it's a great improvement. MW: I'd have to agree with you there, Big Daddy. But WAIT-- Primal Fear is already attacking him, and he just LEVELED him with a clothesline! They're not wasting any time, folks. BD: But I haven't even heard the bell yet; this is illegal! Why doesn't the referee get some damn control in there? MW: Now Primal Fear scoops the Strangler up, and SLAMS him down to the mat! This is a complete massacre! BD: (yelling to the ring) Come ON, Strangler! Kick his butt! MW: And Primal Fear executes a back suplex, and goes for the quick cover! This match could be over right here.... One....Two....Kickout! BD: That was close. Now Primal Fear tries to pull the Strangler to his feet, BUT HE GETS PUNCHED RIGHT IN THE MIDSECTION! The Strangler gets up and NAILS him with a headbutt! MW: We might be seeing another upset victory, folks. BD: I sure hope so. MW: The Strangler ROCKS him with a kick to the head! He whips him into the corner, and follows that up with a clothesline! Primal Fear just SLUMPS to the mat! BD: Well, the tables have definitely been turned. The Strangler is just wiping the floor with this loser! MW: Now he hits Primal Fear with a swinging neckbreaker, SNAPPING him back! That had a lot of impact, folks. BD: AND THE STRANGLER IS STANDING ON PRIMAL FEAR'S NECK! He's trying to completely cut off his circulation! (laughing) I love it! Crush his windpipe, buddy! MW: This is totally unnecessary-- the referee needs to get some damn CONTROL in there! The Strangler finally gets off Primal Fear's neck, and pulls him to his feet! He executes a jumping bulldog, DRIVING him into the canvas! Here's the cover.... One....Two..Kickout! BD: Primal Fear is just like a cockroach-- he doesn't die easily. MW: Very true, Big Daddy. Now the Strangler whips Primal Fear into the corner; he runs in and DRIVES A SHOULDER INTO HIM! The Strangler is climbing on top of him, and UNLOADS with a series of right hands to Primal Fear's face! BD: For the first time in his pathetic life, Primal Fear is completely helpless. He's being systematically DESTROYED in there, folks. MW: Unfortunately, it sure looks that way. Now the Twilight Strangler whips him to the ropes-- and he RAMS an elbow into his neck! He's going for another cover.... One....Two....Kickout! BD: The Strangler is VERY angry now, and he's signaling for the Choke! If he applies this hold on, it's ALL OVER. MW: He locks it on, and quickly begins to apply the pressure. Personally, I think this hold should be outlawed-- it's just a regular chokehold, for crying out loud! When did it suddenly become LEGAL to choke in a wrestling match? BD: When you get into the ring with the Twilight Strangler, EXPECT some illegal moves. This guy's a MADMAN, folks. MW: And Primal Fear has just submitted! It's over! (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner, due to a submission, THE TWILIGHT STRANGLER! BD: Well, I'm VERY happy with the outcome of that match. Now we finally know who the better man is, right? MW: No, Big Daddy, that did NOT settle their feud. The Strangler used a blatant chokehold to win the match; it wasn't fair. BD: You're SUCH a crybaby, Wilson. Primal Fear just got his ass kicked, and now you're trying to make excuses for him. MW: Well, I don't feel like arguing with you, so it's now time for our next match. The Assassins are about to square off against the Shadow Warriors, as a result of what happened tonight on the Terrordome! BD: This should be an interesting match, folks. The Warriors are going to dominate, of course, just like they always do. RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is scheduled for one fall! Currently making their way to the ring area, here are the SHADOW WARRIORS! (As "We Are the Champions" plays loudly throughout the arena, Wesley Cantrell leads out Reuben Slade and Aaron Ladd, who get a cool reception from the crowd) BD: Hey, by the way, why did you let Bertha walk out of here? The last time I tried that, I almost lost my job. MW: She looks better than you in a dress. BD: I've only worn a dress ONCE, and I didn't have time to properly apply any cosmetics, so cut me some slack. Plus, it was a bad hair day. MW: Actually, Big Daddy, I didn't know anything about that. That creates a DISGUSTING mental image, so let's both just shut up. BD: Good idea. RING ANNOUNCER: And their opponents, former World tag team champions, here they are, THE ASSASSINS! (Banshee and Shockwave make their entrance, to a BIG pop from the crowd. They slap hands with the fans in the front row, but never take their eyes off the Shadow Warriors.) MW: The winner of this one goes to Kickoff '97 to face the AEWA National Tag Team Champions, Graphic Violence. BD: Slade starts off against Shockwave, as each team opens with its bigger power specialists. Collar and elbow tie up, and Slade connects with a kneelift that staggers the big white ninja. MW: Now he cinches on an armbar and tags in Ladd. Ladd bounces off the ropes, leapfrogs his partner-- AND LANDS SPLIT-LEGGED ON SHOCKWAVE'S EXTENDED, EXPOSED ARM! BD: Shockwave is in some BAD pain right now. Ladd applies an armbar of his own, and he tags Slade back in, who comes down with an elbow smash on the same exposed arm! MW: They're trying to CRIPPLE him! BD: I guess they've done a little research, and have decided that Shockwave's arm strength was the best area to attack. MW: It looks that way. Slade with an armdrag now, and he holds on to that armbar, leaning way back on it. But he's too near the ropes, and Shockwave grabs the middle rope with his other hand, and Slade breaks the hold. BD: Both men are back up, and-- OUCH! Shockwave nails with a vicious kick to the head, and Slade hits the mat. Where did he find the strength for that? Shockwave tags in Banshee, and holds Slade down by the legs. Banshee is going up top.... MW: And he NAILS Slade with a flying moonsault! That was absolutely amazing, folks! Here's the cover! One..Two..Three!! (DING, DING) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, after only three minutes, here are you winners, Banshee and Shockwave, THE ASSASSINS! BD: I hate to admit it, but they EARNED that victory. I thought Shockwave was finished after what happened to his arm, but he was able to make a comeback. MW: There you have it! Former AEWA World Tag Team Champions, the Assassins, will meet Graphic Violence for the AEWA National Tag Team Championship! That's going to be an EXCELLENT match! BD: That's for sure. Well, I'd "love" to sit here and chat with you, but we're running out of TV time for tonight. Can we just hurry up and END this damn show? MW: It would be my PLEASURE, Big Daddy. I'm still having trouble breathing from that incident with microphone. BD: You deserved it. MW: (rolls his eyes) Whatever you say, buddy. Anyway, that's all the time we have for tonight, so for Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt, this is Matt Wilson saying so long everybody!
