AEWA — Card Archive

Weekend Rumble (6/16, part two)

20 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LB: Like anyone cares. What's next? MW: Twisted Steel against the Grim Reaper, in a Casket match! Twisted Steel will try to defend his wife, Vicki, after the Reaper brutally assaulted them both on our last card. LB: They asked for it. MW: Might I ask WHAT they ever said or did to provoke such a vicious attack? LB: Being born. MW: Why do I bother? Let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is a Casket Match, and will continue until one competitor stuffs the other inside the casket and closes the lid. Introducing first, weighing in at 435 pounds and accompanied by his manager, "Cutie" Rick Verrano, The Grim Reeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeaper! (The Reaper appears, and the boos POUR down from the rafters. He slowly and methodically makes his way down to the ring, pushing the casket with him. Rick Verrano is at his side.) MW: Look at this man. He has absolutely no regard for human life. He brutally sneak attacked a woman on our last card. Why on earth would he do something like that? LB: Because he can. MW: Yes, it seems like anyone can get away with anything nowadays in the AEWA. Why is he even here? Yes, he was fined, but shouldn't he be suspended or something? It doesn't seem like anyone can get suspended in this federation anymore, random violence is almost encouraged. LB: The more the better. MW: See what I mean? RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, weighing in at 425 pounds and accompanied by his wife, Vicki, Twisted Steeeeeeeeeeeeeel! (Large crowd pop. Twisted Steel appears, pushing Vicki in a wheelchair, but he has his eye on the Reaper, and his mind on revenge.) LB: Does Twisted Steel look more focused than he's ever been or what? MW: I think any man would be completely focused on beating the crap out of a man who attacked his wife. Wouldn't you think? LB: I guess. But that could also be a disadvantage, he could get overemotional, and it could cost him. MW: That's always a possibility. Twisted Steel places Vicki in her place at ringside, and slides into the ring. He stares directly across the ring at the Reaper. LB: There's no love lost here. MW: That's for sure. And Twisted Steel makes the first move. He charges across the ring, and begins to pound away at Reaper, and now he executes a brutal clothesline! He backs off, THEN CHARGES IN WITH ANOTHER! He REALLY wants this one. He's trying to stuff the Reaper in the casket already! LB: But the Reaper's hanging onto the ropes and won't let himself get shoved into there. MW: The Reaper climbs back into the ring, AND GETS NAILED WITH A SHORT LARIAT! He's down, and Twisted Steel drops a headbutt on him! But he's not done, he brings the Reaper to his feet, grabs him by the neck, CHOKESLAM! LB: Boy, Twisted Steel sure ain't messing around. MW: No he's not. He brings him back to his feet, grabs a front facelock, DDT! Twisted Steel backs off, and waits for the Reaper to get to his feet. He's slow, but he finally makes it up, AND GETS SENT RIGHT BACK DOWN WITH A CLOTHESLINE! LB: The Reaper's not really hurt you know, he's just pretending. MW: Well he's doing a pretty damn good job of it. Twisted Steel now begins to punch and kick the fallen Reaper, and he's gonna try to stick him into that casket again! He rolls the big Reaper into it, and is pulling down the lid... LB: But the Reaper's got his arm in there, he can't close the lid! MW: Yes, Reaper blocks that lid with his arm. Twisted Steel opens that lid back up. And the Reaper NAILS him in the face with a punch with his other hand, and he's STILL wearing those spiked gloves that he used to beat Vicki! That REALLY sent Twisted Steel reeling! LB: I would hope so, those spiked gloves aren't exactly intended for love taps. MW: The Reaper climbs back into the ring, and whips the still reeling Twisted Steel to the ropes, Twisted Steel comes back.... SHOULDER BLOCK-- But The Reaper side-steps, and NAILS him with a fist in the midsection! LB: Here is where the Reaper begins to take OVER! MW: The Reaper whips Twisted Steel into the turnbuckle, and follows him in, AVALANCHE! And now he grabs Twisted Steel by the neck, AND TAKES HIM DOWN WITH A JAWBREAKER! LB: Told you. MW: Yes, and what a difficult call that was. LB: Shut up, Wilson. MW: Twisted Steel gets to his feet, and gets nailed with a chop! The Reaper whip him to the ropes, WHERE HE GETS TRIPPED BY RICK VERRANO! LB: I didn't see that. Twisted Steel just fell, he's nothing but a big clumsy oaf. MW: Well, trust me, it happened, and Twisted Steel isn't happy about it. He quickly gets to his feet, BUT THE REAPER TAKES HIM RIGHT BACK DOWN WITH ANOTHER JAWBREAKER! And now he's biting Twisted Steel in the face! That has no place in wrestling! LB: Sure it does, it was a great technical maneuver, a Greco-Roman facebite. MW: Are you serious? How can you say stuff like that with a straight face? Anyway, Twisted Steel is down, and The Reaper is leaving the ring, Where's he going? LB: He's going to get Vicki again, stupid! How come I always know this stuff and you don't? Are you freaking blind? MW: No, but thanks for asking. Reaper is going after Vicki. This is disgusting! How low can you get? Attacking a WOMAN in a WHEELCHAIR? LB: Oh, he can get MUCH lower, trust me. MW: I'll take your word for it. The Reaper is stalking Vicki, and she's trying to wheel away as fast as she can! LB: But it's not fast enough! MW: Yes, she's backing away, but the Reaper charges in, and CLOTHESLINES HER RIGHT OUT OF THAT WHEELCHAIR! This man is sick! He turns around, and Twisted Steel dives from the ring and clotheslines HIM! LB: Wow! He caught EVERYONE by surprise! I wasn't even paying attention to him in the ring. MW: Me neither! Twisted Steel nails the Reaper with an elbowsmash! And now he grabs the Reaper, and whips him into the ringpost! Now he goes back into the ring, and waits for the Reaper! LB: And he's waiting rather impatiently, if I might add. MW: The Reaper climbs back into the ring, slowly gets to his feet, and gets DOUBLED OVER with a fist in the midsection! Twisted Steel grabs him, HE'S SETTING UP FOR THE PAIN BOMB! LB: What a stupid, stupid idiot! You can't do that to a 425 pound man! MW: He tries to pick the Reaper up for it, but he can't, and the Reaper turns the position into a backdrop out of the ring, AND INTO THE CASKET! And Rick Verrano is right there next to the casket, he closes the lid, and it's over! (DING, DING) LB: Well, the idiot tried to do the impossible, and it cost him. RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner... THE GRIM REAPER!!!!!! MW: Maybe it did cost him, but Rick Verrano helped a little too. LB: He did not! MW: He did so, Twisted Steel would've had more time to escape the casket if Verrano hadn't closed it the instant he fell in. LB: Well that's just too damn bad, ain't it? MW: Yes, it is. But let's move on. LB: Yeah, up next we have our savior, Cardinal Chaos, defending the TV title against Ben "The Chump" Slocum. MW: Yes, Slocum FINALLY gets his chance for revenge after Chaos stole the World Title from him, although the prize for this match is somewhat less than the one he lost. LB: So what? He's lucky to be getting a shot at ANY title with the way he's been wrestling lately. MW: He's just in something of a slump, he'll break out of it. LB: Yeah, sure. MW: Let's just go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is for the AEWA Television Title and is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, the challenger, from Austin, Texas, he weighs in at 302 pounds and is accompanied by his sister, Sondra, "The Modern Day Warrior" Ben Slooooooooooooooooooocum! (Laura Branigan's "Self Control" plays loudly on the PA as Slocum makes his entrance to a MEGA crowd pop. He and Sondra high five the fans all the way to the ring.) LB: You know, Slocum's music, that's my second favorite song. MW: Really? I'm almost afraid to ask, what's your favorite? LB: All the rest are tied. MW: It figures. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from the Church of the Pounding Light, he weighs in at 313 pounds and is the AEWA Television Champion, Cardinal Chaaasaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaos! (The Rolling Stones' "Symphony for the Devil" plays loudly as the Cardinal makes his entrance to a MEGA crowd boo. He holds his belt high as he is pelted with garbage.) MW: And this crowd does NOT like Cardinal Chaos. LB: Why not? He is nothing more than a simple religious man. MW: He is nothing more than a fraud, that's what he is. LB: He is not, and tonight, he's gonna make a believer out of Ben Slocum. MW: Doubtful. The Cardinal climbs into the ring, and is immediately attacked by Slocum. Slocum looks like he's been ready for this match ever since he lost the World Title. LB: This isn't fair! This is the second match in a row that the Cardinal hasn't even gotten to do his pre-match prayer! MW: Oh well. Slocum is not wasting any time. He pounds away on the Cardinal, and now he's placing him backwards on the top rope. He climbs up there as well, grabs the Cardinal, BELLY-TO-BACK SUPERPLEX! LB: That Slocum is nothing but a big cheater! MW: And the Cardinal isn't? LB: That's right. MW: (sarcastically) Whatever you say, Bertha. Slocum brings the Cardinal to his feet, and SLAPS him right across the face! And now he grabs Chaos, and whips him to the turnbuckle! He follows him in, and NAILS him with a shoulderblock into the buckles! LB: The Cardinal will come back, he always does. MW: We'll see.... Slocum now grabs Chaos, DDT-- BUT CHAOS TURNS IT INTO A BACKDROP-- AND SLOCUM COUNTERS THAT WITH A SUNSET FLIP! One..Kickout! LB: See that kickout at one? The Cardinal is far from done. MW: Yes, but Slocum's still in control. The Cardinal gets to his feet, and Slocum NAILS him with a spinning mule kick! Chaos is stunned, but UPSET! He charges at Slocum, who nails him with a backdrop! The Cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: Come on Chaos! You can't let this chump do this to you! MW: Slocum goes up top, and waits for the Cardinal to get to his feet! He does, and Slocum dives with a Cross Body Block, BUT CHAOS CATCHES HIM IN MID-AIR, AND SLAMS HIM DOWN! LB: That's it champ, I knew you had it in ya. MW: Slocum gets to his feet, Chaos grabs him, NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX! The count! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Slocum's pathetic! MW: Hardly. Chaos picks Slocum up, and whips him to the ropes, on the comeback, THEY NAIL EACH OTHER WITH A DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE! They are both down, but Slocum makes it to his feet first! LB: Darn it! MW: Chaos gets up, and Slocum grabs him, HEADLOCK TAKEDOWN! Chaos gets back to his feet, and gets NAILED with a spinning mule kick! And Slocum follows it up with an elbowsmash! Now he quickly jumps onto the back of the stunned Chaos, CRUCIFIX! One..Two..Kickout! LB: He can't do a Crucifix to the Cardinal! The Cardinal likes them inverted! MW: Well he just did, so that's too bad. Slocum, wasting no time, rakes the face of the Cardinal! And now he grabs him in a front facelock, DDT! But he's not ready to cover! He picks Chaos up, SPINNING POWER BOMB! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! (Barely) LB: That was close, but the Cardinal will never fall to someone as pathetic as Slocum. MW: You'd be surprised. Slocum quickly pick Chaos up, whips him to the ropes, on the comeback, Slocum catches him in a Belly-to-Belly Suplex! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: I haven't been surprised yet. MW: The key word is yet. Slocum whips Chaos to the ropes, on the comeback, CHAOS NAILS SLOCUM WITH A FLYING CLOTHESLINE! And now Chaos grabs him, and executes a Belly-to-Belly suplex of his own! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: That was a slow count! MW: Chaos is arguing that same point to the ref, to no avail. He finally give up, turns back to Slocum, who catches him off-guard with an inside cradle! One..Two..Kickout! LB: That wasn't fair, Chaos wasn't ready! MW: Well he should've been concentrating on his opponent, and not the ref. LB: Shut up, Wilson. MW: Both men up, Slocum whips Chaos to the ropes, Chaos comes back, and hits Slocum with a short lariat! The cover! One..Kickout! MW: Pretty good kickout for a "chump", eh Bertha? LB: I repeat, Shut up, Wilson. MW: Chaos picks Slocum up, HE'S GOING FOR THE INVERTED CRUCIFIX! BUT SLOCUM BLOCKS IT! He breaks free, and jumps onto Chaos with another regular crucifix! One..Two..Kickout! LB: AGAIN? This time blocking the inverted crucifix, THEN doing a crucifix? OHHHHHH, he's in trouble now. MW: He sure doesn't look like it. Slocum picks the Cardinal up, and SLAPS him again! And now Slocum hits the stunned Cardinal with another spinning mule kick! Chaos falls, and Slocum quickly drops a knee on his back! LB: What is wrong with the Cardinal today? He's just not himself. MW: Yeah, he's not cheating, and Slocum's kicking his ass. LB: You know what I'm gonna say, so just SHUT UP! MW: Getting you mad is SO much fun! Slocum now, on his knees, drops a headbutt on Chaos! He now waits for Chaos to get to his feet, and LEVELS him with a clothesline! Chaos up again, and Slocum takes him down with a bulldog! LB: THIS CAN'T BE HAPPENING! MW: It is. Slocum picks Chaos up, places him on the top rope, climbs up himself, AND EXECUTES ANOTHER BELLY-TO-BACK SUPERPLEX! He gets up, whips Chaos to the ropes, and catches him with a Belly-to-Belly suplex on the comeback! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: I'll admit, Slocum is kicking the crap out of Chaos, but Chaos will not go down! MW: Yes, you've gotta give him that. Chaos gets to his feet, and Slocum nails him with a chop! He grabs Chaos, and executes a facerake! Chaos still on his feet, BUT NOT ANYMORE, as Slocum FLOORS him with a clothesline! And he drops a spinning elbow! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: I told you, he CAN'T get him! MW: Well he's sure trying. He runs to the ropes, on the comeback, HE GETS CAUGHT IN A BACKDROP BY CHAOS! LB: FINALLY! MW: Slocum gets up, and gets NAILED with a shoulderblock from Chaos! Chaos goes up top, he waits for Slocum to get to his feet, he dives, FLYING CLOTHESLINE--BUT SLOCUM DUCKS! And Chaos hits the mat hard! LB: NO! MW: Chaos gets to his feet, but Slocum quickly picks him up, scales the ropes, DEATH DROP! HE NAILED IT! The Cover! One..Two..Three!!!!! (DING, DING) LB: No! How'd he do that? RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner, and NEEEEEEEEEEEEEW AEWA Television Champion, "THE MODERN DAY WARRIOR" BEN SLOCUM!!!!!!!! MW: Slocum did it! He got his revenge, and we have a new TV champ! He sure didn't look like a chump, did he Bertha? LB: No, he didn't, happy? MW: Yes. This crowd loves it! Slocum REALLY deserves this after all he's been through. Wait a minute, I just got an update from the locker room. The Crazy Canuck suffered only mild burns in that attack from Primal Fear earlier, and should be back in action in no time. LB: Darn. MW: Anyway, coming up now, Volcano Jackson is gonna get his rematch against Mister Crowley for the World Heavyweight Title, and it's gonna be in the match that Jackson wanted, a Philadelphia Street Fight! LB: Yeah, Jackson was complaining in his last interview that the last match was Crowley's type of match, and now he wanted HIS type of match. Now my question is, what the HELL is he talking about? Crowley beat him in a REGULAR match, no stipulations, fair and square in the middle of the ring. MW: That's true, but Crowley did sneak attack him a couple of nights before the match, and sent him to the hospital. Jackson was NOT 100 percent. LB: Yes he was, he only wanted this match, because he knows that he can't beat Crowley fair and square, so he wants a match where he can cheat. What a pathetic loser! MW: I'll tell him you said that. LB: That's quite all right, you REALLY don't have to do that. MW: Then let's just go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA World Heavyweight Title! Introducing first, the challenger, he weighs in at 290 pounds and is accompanied by his manager, Aaron Proctor, Volcanooooooooooooooooooooo Jackson! (Queen's "Another One Bites the Dust" plays loudly on the PA as Jackson makes his entrance, and the crowd ERUPTS. Aaron Proctor is at his side.) LB: And here comes the wuss who got pinned by Super-Blonde the other day. MW: How does that make him a wuss? Super-Blonde is an excellent wrestler. LB: Face it Wilson. Jackson, has been LUCKY all this time, and his luck has finally run out. Crowley is gonna prove that without a doubt tonight! MW: I think that Jackson still has a few tricks up his sleeve. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from the Darkside, he weighs in at 328 pounds and is the FOUR-time and current AEWA World Heavyweight Champion, Mister Crowleeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeey! (The lights go out, and the eerie organ intro of Ozzy Osbourne's song "Mister Crowley" begins to play as Mr. Crowley makes his entrance to some VERY loud boos. He gets to ringside, and raises the lights.) MW: Crowley steps into the ring, and is immediately attacked by Volcano Jackson! LB: This is the second match in a row that one of my favorite guys got attacked before the bell. What gives? MW: (sarcastically) Maybe it's because they're so popular. Jackson moves in, SHORT LARIAT--DUCKED BY MR. CROWLEY! Crowley grabs Jackson, DOUBLE UNDERHOOK PILEDRIVER! And now Crowley runs and WOW! The big man just delivered a somersault splash onto the prone Volcano Jackson! LB: Just look at Jackson. He's so pathetic. He can't even take control of a match after a sneak attack. MW: Crowley goes up top, he waits for Jackson to get to his feet, and NAILS him with a flying bulldog! Crowley picks Jackson up, and whips him to the ropes--REVERSED BY JACKSON! Crowley to the ropes, on the comeback, JACKSON NAILS HIM WITH A BACK HEEL KICK! LB: Lucky move. MW: And now Jackson tosses Crowley from the ring! Jackson goes up top, and DIVES to the outside, BUT CROWLEY MOVES! And Jackson crashes right into the steel guardrail! LB: See. MW: And now Crowley is reaching under the ring, and he pulls out a fire extinguisher! And he CLOBBERS Jackson with it! But he does not go down! Crowley NAILS Jackson with a jumping side kick! BUT JACKSON STILL DOESN'T GO DOWN! LB: He's out on his feet! MW: Maybe he is, but the fact that he is on his feet is STILL amazing! Crowley reaches into the crowd, and pulls a "Lefty McGee Souvenir Baseball Bat" away from a fan. AND HE HITS A HOME RUN WITH JACKSON'S HEAD! LB: Actually, I thought it looked more like a grand slam. MW: And now Crowley picks Jackson up, JACK-KNIFE POWER BOMB ON THE CONCRETE! The cover! One..Two..Thr..Kickout! LB: And he just BARELY got out of there, that's the move that Crowley beat him with last time too! MW: But he didn't beat him with it this time! Crowley picks Jackson up, and whips him to the ringpost--BUT JACKSON REVERSED IT, AND CROWLEY hits the post hard! LB: That looked like it hurt a little. MW: I would think so. Jackson picks Crowley up, and whips him to the steel guardrail! Crowley is just slumped against the guardrail! Jackson picks him up, and TOSSES him over the guardrail and into the crowd! LB: If I were those fans, I'd get out of their way. MW: That's probably a good idea. They're brawling down that aisle between the rows of seats! A fan gets off his chair, folds it up, and hands it to Volcano Jackson! LB: He can't do that! Where does he think we are, ECW Arena? MW: Jackson accepts the chair, and NAILS Crowley with it! But Crowley does not go down! Jackson winds up, and CLOBBERS Crowley again! This time he drops like a sack of rocks! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: Crowley's not done yet! MW: Yes, he just got up as if nothing's wrong! And now they're brawling down the aisle! They've gone through one of the entrances in the back, and now they're brawling into the arena concourse! LB: I wonder where they'll end up! MW: Crowley whips Jackson into a door, NO! He went THROUGH the door! It busted right open! Crowley follows him in, and they appear to be in some kind of arena utility room! LB: And it looks like there's lots of fun stuff in there! MW: Yes, and Crowley sees something now, there's a shovel leaning against the wall! Crowley picks it up, and CLOCKS Jackson with it! Jackson is wobbling, Crowley grabs him, and RAMS him into the wall! He begins to fall, but Crowley grabs him, AND JACK-KNIFE POWER BOMBS HIM ON THE CONCRETE AGAIN! The cover! One..Two..Thr..Kickout! LB: How the in the WORLD did he do that? That's the second power bomb on the concrete that he's kicked out of! MW: I don't know, but he did. They're still in that utility room and Jackson is still down. He reaches over to a nearby tool box, AND PULLS OUT A HAMMER! He nails Crowley in the leg with it! LB: Crowley didn't like that! MW: No he didn't, and he manages to grab the hammer away from Jackson, and sets it aside. He picks Jackson up, GUTWRENCH SUPLEX! He's not done! He picks Jackson up, PREMATURE BURIAL--But Jackson is kicking his legs! He falls backwards, onto his feet, and nails CROWLEY with the Premature Burial! LB: But he's not going for the cover, that's a mistake. MW: Maybe, Maybe not. Crowley on the ground, grabs the hammer, which he conveniently left within grabbing distance! He swings at Jackson's leg, but Jackson jumps over the swing, and quickly snatches the hammer away from Crowley! LB: Darn! MW: And now Jackson with the hammer, he brings Crowley to his feet, and NAILS him in the stomach with that hammer! He bring Crowley out of that utility room, and they're back in the concourse! LB: Jackson really like that hammer, cause he brought it out with him. MW: Jackson SLAPS Crowley right across in the face! Now he carries him by the hair, walks, and rams him head first into the arena wall! Crowley staggers away from the wall, and is LEVELED with a hammer in the head! LB: Crowley isn't hurt. MW: Yeah, you can tell that he isn't by that "Where am I?" look on his face. Crowley down, and Jackson pounds away on him some more with that hammer! Now he picks Crowley up, PILEDRIVER on the concrete! The cover! One..Two..Thr..Kickout! LB: I told you that he isn't hurt! MW: Oh, he's definitely hurt, but somehow he managed to find the energy to kick out! Jackson picks Crowley up, AND TOSSES HIM INTO A CONCESSION STAND! The Concession workers scatter! Jackson runs and DIVES into the middle of the concession stand! BUT CROWLEY MOVES, AND JACKSON FLEW INTO A POPCORN MACHINE! LB: And luckily, the machine sustained little damage. MW: Crowley staggers to his feet, and he's looking around for something among the concession stand's kitchen, HE'S FOUND A FRYING PAN! He waits for Jackson to get to his feet, winds up with that pan, and LEVELS him with a CRUSHING blow to the head! The cover! One..Two..Three! (DING, DING) LB: He did it! He beat Jackson at his own game! RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner, and STILL AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World... MISTER CROWLEY!!!!!! LB: You know, telling the result of one of these matches is like reading the answer to a game of Clue. Mister Crowley....In the Concession Stand....With the Frying Pan. MW: (sarcastically) What a brilliant observation. But you're right though, not about Clue, about Crowley beating Jackson at his own game. Some people thought that Crowley didn't deserve this belt, but tonight he went out there and proved that he did. LB: Wait a minute, Crowley just came back into the arena area, and he's dragging Jackson behind him! MW: Yes, he's coming towards the ring, what's going on? He's at the ringside area, and now he grabs the microphone! CROWLEY: (talking down to the prone Jackson) Do you think that I forgot about what you said in your little interview last week Jackson? When you challenged me to this Philadelphia Street Fight, you said that the loser would be put through three tables. Well guess what? You're the loser, and it's time to make good on your deal! (Crowley throws down the microphone and begins to set up the tables.) LB: Oh YES! I'll bet when Jackson said that, he never thought about it happening if he lost! But you know what they say, you shouldn't let your mouth write checks that your butt can't cash, and right now, Crowley's cashing his in! MW: Crowley's actually doing this! I remember Jackson saying it, but I never thought it would actually happen! This is insane! LB: And it was Jackson's idea. Don't make this out to be Crowley's doing, because this never would've happened if Jackson didn't say that. MW: I would be saying the same thing if Jackson was out here doing it to Crowley. LB: Liar. MW: And Crowley's got the tables set up now. Why do we have three EXTRA tables at ringside anyway? Do these tables serve any purpose, or are they just there for the wrestlers to put each other through? LB: I'll go with choice two. MW: He's got them set up outside the ring, but I don't see how he can get Jackson on top of them. LB: I don't think that he's going to. MW: And now he's going to get Jackson. He rolls Jackson into the ring, and goes in after him. He picks Jackson up in a Cross Body position, and scales the ropes. LB: THAT'S how he's gonna do it, he's gonna Powerslam him through the tables! I LOVE IT! MW: That appears to be what he's doing, he's up top, and he's still got Jackson! He dives, AND CRUSHES JACKSON THROUGH ONE, TWO, THREE TABLES, AND DOWN ONTO THE CONCRETE! This is nuts! LB: This is beautiful, and Jackson ASKED for it. You know what they say, be careful what you wish for, you just might get it. And Jackson just got his. MW: I suppose he did, but that STILL doesn't make it right. Crowley now grabs his belt and walks away, and the boos are deafening! LB: (whispers to a ring attendant) I have just received an important announcement. Hank Thompson is about to have a VERY special interview with some new guy.... MW: What? Nobody told me anything about an interview! LB: You aren't friends with "the powers that be", Wilson. Just shut up, and let's hear what this guy has to say.... (Hank Thompson walks up to the interview area, holding a microphone. He looks at the camera, and begins to speak) THOMPSON: Ladies and gentlemen, I have some important news. After witnessing what happened earlier tonight with Primal Fear, President Smith DEMANDED that we get some control here in the AEWA. So he has hired a lawman to do the job! For the first time ever, I would like to introduce.... "LAWMAN" JOHN TIMBER! (A very large man makes his way out, carrying a steel chair. He has a thick beard, and looks VERY angry) TIMBER: Justice has arrived, punks! These damn wrestlers have gone too far, and I'm going to enforce the rules! President Smith has hired me for only one reason-- to keep control. So the next time that somebody does ANYTHING wrong, I'll be there! THOMPSON: You're a very intimidating individual, sir. What are your plans for the future here? TIMBER: I'm gonna take out the trash.... (He pushes Thompson back, and starts to walk away) Justice is cruel! LB: That guy was HUGE! Smith should NEVER allow him around here.... He'll destroy the whole league! MW: Well fans, that's all we've got time for this week, so for the Lovely Bertha, this is Matt Wilson saying so long everybody! (fade to black)
