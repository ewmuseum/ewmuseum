Sunday, June 22nd, Card (2/3)

22 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EL: OK- the Preacher settles into a chinlock now. Serious power in the Preacher's arms. A lot of things are going to start buzzing around here, with that title vacant. We have no CWAA Heavyweight Champion, and I still don't know how the AEWA wants to crown a new champion. In a battle royal, I'd have to think that one of the two in the ring now, Kore Wrench and Mark Sherrick, would be among the favorites, both being so powerful, so large- maybe 300-325 lb. each. A Randy Sheradin, Lt. Lawson or a Marc Thomas- and hey, let's not forget the Professional- might be better suited for a longer tournament, where the ability to stay fresh makes the difference. Kore Wrench with the chin lock on Mark Sherrick- now punctuates it with an elbow smash to the top of the head. Now the Preacher back to that chinlock, using the power and the leverage advantage to wear the man down, to drain some energy out of Sherrick. Sherrick on his knees, the Preacher behind him applying the pressure. Sherrick now jabs an elbow into the midsection. The Preacher still hanging on- there's another elbow, and that'll break it. Sherrick with a snap mare now- and now he stomps and kicks at the Preacher! Puts the boot to him- pulls him up, sends him for the ride- right hand downstairs. Now Sherrick with a headbutt staggers the Preacher. Mark to standing wristlock now, trying to sap the power in Wrench's arms. ("Go, Kore, Go!") EL: Sherrick with that standing wristlock- and he throws in a kick in the ribs for good measure. And another! Kicks in the midsection as he applies that wristlock. We've got some big tag team matchups coming up after this. Twister is here, and we'll see Sheradin and Barnes go for the gold. Scoop slam by Sherrick. And the one everyone's waiting to see- Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas- thought I saw a little dissension in the ranks in that pair before- against Lt. Lawson and Max Robertson. Sherrick putting the boot to him again- bounces off the ropes- splashes- no! The Preacher has his knees up in time- Sherrick comes down right on those knees. (Pop) EL: Sherrick in serious pain now- the Preacher off the ropes- running clothesline! Sherrick just flipped in mid air! and the Preacher throws him into the corner- in after him- drives that shoulder into the midsection. Now a snap mare by the Preacher- off the ropes- leg drop- 1... kickout. Kore Wrench has taken over this contest. Sends him for the ride- elbow smash. Now a running knee lift, and Sherrick on the run. Kore Wrench signaling for the Crucifix slam- (Or Razor's Edge) EL: -he sets him up- he's got him up- plants him right in the mat. 1... 2... 3! He did it! He got him! (Pop as Gregorian Chant plays) EL: The Preacher, Kore Wrench gets his revenge for how Mark Sherrick cheated his way to victory last week. We have to take a break, and when we get back, we'll see Twister, the ex-tag champs, in action with Double Jeopardy- you won't want to miss this one, folks. (The WYXZ-TV Channel 39 movie of the week brings you your favorite stars in your favorite classic films, Wednesdays at 8 PM. This week, Janet Leigh, Deforest Kelley, and a cast of giant rabbits star in the horror classic, "Night of the Lepus.") EL: OK- let's get set for the introductions. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, this contest is set for one fall with a ten-minute time limit. Introducing first, at a combined weight of 500 lb.- ("Sweet Home Alabama" plays and the crowd reacts unfavorably) DM: -Eddie Intellecta, Gary Jackson- Double Jeopardy! (Eddie stands 6'7"-250 and wears his AEWA Rocks t-shirt and a blue mask and yellow tights. Jackson wears a beer-stained (probably Schlitz, too) undershirt and old blue jeans, and stands 5'11"-250) DM: And their opponents- (The "Twister" theme plays, and the pops start) DM: -from America's Heartland, at a combined weight of 559 lb., they are the former CWAA Tag Team Champions- Travis Conley, Paul Langford- Twister! Your referee, Jeff Howard. (Twister are of course led by Mother Nature. Conley has short blonde hair and blue eyes, and is 315 lb. Langford is the smaller of the two, and has longer brown hair and a washboard stomach. Both wear the trademarked Twister trunks.) EL: OK- Double Jeopardy and Twister underway- there's the bell. The Tornado locks up with the Hotshot- collar and elbow tie up. And Gary Jackson with a handful of hair pulls Langford to the ropes- Jeff Howard calls for a clean break- and the Hotshot with a forearm smash. Now he locks Langford into a standing side headlock. Keep and eye on Intellecta- he's got one foot on the ring- and while Jeff Howard warns him, Gary Jackson slips the arm down- from a headlock to a chokehold. Now he slides his arm back up into a headlock. Jeff Howard asking if there was a choke- Jackson shaking his head. He'd never admit it, Jeff, face it. Now the Hotshot tags in Eddie Intellecta. The self-professed genius of the CWAA in with the former tag team champions now. Double Jeopardy with the Irish whip- double clothesline. Jackson out of there. Eddie Intellecta to a wristlock now. Eddie Intellecta grinding the arm of Paul Langford. and I'm joined now by Mother Nature, manager of the team of Twister. How are you? MN: Fine, Eric, thanks. EL: Eddie Intellecta with a wristlock on Paul Langford. MN: Well, we know from prior experience that Eddie Intellecta knows his way around the ring, but he and Jackson always seem to make that one fatal mistake. Of course, you can't just wait for your opponent to slip up- you've got to force the error. We feel that we match up well with these two, and that a match liek this will help us get ready for another go-around those two- well, I can't say what I think of them here. EL: You mean the champs- MN: Well, my boys were never really beaten for those titles- you saw what happened. EL: I sure did. Paul Langford reverses the arm bar- and Intellecta with a MN: An Eddie Intellecta really doesn't need to wrestle in that style. I don't why he does it, but you know what? It only steels our resolve when people try things like that. EL: Gary Jackson tags in. So your resolve must be real steely where Anderson and McNultey are concerned. MN: Well, I'm betting that the next time we see them out here next week, they'll be ex-champs. I think Randy and Joshua are walking out of here with the gold tonight, and I look forward to seeing them in the ring with my boys, titles on the line. EL: The Hotshot to a hammerlock on Langford now. Gary Jackson with a standing hammerlock- and Langford with a snap mare takeover- and he tags Travis Conley! MN: There's no way these two can match power with Travis- watch what happens here now. EL: OK- Travis Conley grabs Jackson with a side headlock- look at those powerful arms. Eddie Intellecta now- look at this- pushing the top rope now within an arm's reach for Jackson. Jeff Howard calls for the break. OK- Gary Jackson tags in Eddie Intellecta. Collar and elbow tie up. Travis Conley- what power- just throws him right into a neutral corner. (Pop) MN: Eddie doesn't know what hit him. We really like the power advantage Travis gives up against just abut any team in the CWAA. EL: Well, they lock back up again- and again he throws Eddie back into the corner. (Pop) EL: Eddie can't believe what's happening to him in that ring. I can't believe it- he wants another lock up. MN: Well, Travis'll be more than happy to oblige. EL: OK- collar and elbow tie-up- and Eddie Intellecta with a knee to the groin! Now he tags in the Hotshot- double team action now as the both pound Travis with double sledges. Paul Langford just going nuts on the apron during this. They send him for the ride- double clo- no! Conley ducks and spins- clotheslines them both! He just knocked them both on their backs! Langford in now he dropkicks Intellecta right over that top rope- and Travis with an Irish whip on the Hotshot- powerslam! The cover... 1... 2... 3! Got him! (The Twister theme starts to play as Eddie pulls Jackson out under the bottom rope) EL: Well, Mother Nature, congratulations on another big win here. MN: Thanks, Eric. I'd love to stay and chat, but I've got to get in there and Paul, Travis and I have something to say that we want everyone to here. (MN heads to the ring) DM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winners, Paul Langford, Travis Conley- Twister! (Myers notices that the winners are endeavoring to get his attention) DM: Paul, Travis, we all saw what happened inside the steel cage two weeks ago. Those belts you wore around your waist so proudly are gone because of a set of brass knuckles. How do you feel? TC: (Enraged) Feel? How the (expletive deleted) do you think we feel? (Pauses, as the realization of what he said to the television audience sinks in.) Oh, man! I'm sorry, David! Sometimes my mouth moves faster than my brain. Fans, I apologize for what I just said. But I don't apologize for my feeling behind those words. Week after week, we proved ourselves to be one of the best, if not THE best, tag teams in the CWAA. And week after week, we beat those two loose-lipped, swivel-hipped pansies, no matter what they tried. (Pop) TC: And now, a week after they go as low as they can go, and beat us, do we get the first shot? NO! PL: Randy, Joshua, we don't mean to disrespect you, and if what Travis just said upset you, well, I'm sorry. We wish you the best of luck tonight, and we'll be in your corner to make sure no one does to you what they have done to us. As for you, Anderson and McNultey, we thought after the cage match that we would be through with you, having beaten you every way imaginable. But you have gotten our attention, and if Randy and Joshua don't take care of you this week, Travis and I will do it next week. MN: That's right, I'm making you a challenge. Next week, we will wrestle you two, with one added twist- another Texas Tornado match, and to make sure that you two are punished for each violation of the rules, we're going to have a little penalty box set up. Like in hockey, every time Jeff Howard catches someone breaking the rules, they'll get sent to the penalty box for 30 seconds- and his partner is stuck in the ring alone with two opponents. So every time you guys get caught breaking the rules, into the box you go, and Chris Herman locks you in and keeps the time. After thirty seconds he lets you back out. Now I know you two aren't going to like this, so how about this extra stipulation- if we lose, Travis and Paul will never challenge you again. That's right, if by some miracle you win, the team of Twister will never wrestle in the CWAA as a team again. Now I know that Lawson, and this Masked Man, will be rooting for us, because they don't want to have to face either one of these guys one on one. Sign your names to that contract, and we'll see you next week. (Exeunt Twister and MN to a big pop) EL: Well, you heard it right there- Twister offers to never team again here in the CWAA if Anderson and McNultey can beat them in a Texas Tornado-Penalty Box match for the CWAA Tag Team Championship belts! But right now, we have to take a break, and when- wait a second. Someone's coming out of the back- who is it now? It's Barnes and Sheridan, our challengers for the tag team belts later tonight. (No music plays, as their entrance was unexpected, but Barnes and Sheridan still receive a healthy crowd pop as they walk to the ring. Both men have grim looks on their faces, but Sheridan absent-mindedly slaps a few fans hands on the way. Instinct, we suppose. Once at ringside, Sheridan grabs a house mike, and the two partners enter the ring.) RS: You know, Robertson put all this effort into hiding from the top contenders, and it ended up costing him his belt. Any other time, that would be really funny. But I still have a score to settle with you, Robertson, I don't like you hiding behind the Masked Man, or Jak Hammer, or Marc Thomas, to try to avoid answering for everything you've done to me and Josh. So I'm putting you on notice, that champion or no champion, you're still a target as far as I'm concerned. JB: Max... I guess neither Randy or m-me can beat you for the belt now. We'll jo-just have to kick your ass for fun. {Crowd pop. The microphone picks up Barnes whispering "Can I say that on TV?" to Sheridan, who quickly nods his head before continuing.} RS: And tonight, we get a match with McNultey and Anderson, who are probably backstage hoping we'll get distracted with Robertson and all his buddies on the card tonight. Well, this is not the Barnes and Sheridan team of a few months ago. We've been together since the CWAA opened for business, and we're as good as any team in the CWAA, including you. JB: We're going to get back at you two for last yea-week, and pay you back. You call yourselves "Pretty Boy" and "Marvelous". Well, I think you're "Prissy Boy" and "Mar....ummm" {Pauses to think of a clever nickname} Ah, to hell with it. Let's just pound them into the mat. RS: And maybe when Josh and I have belts, and Robertson doesn't, maybe there'll start to be some justice around here. (Off they go) EL: OK- we're going to take a break now, and when we get back, Lawson and the Professional are in there with Jak Hammer and Marc Thomas. Stay tuned!
