"Lethal" Lawson and Max "The Professional" Robertson (1-0) Marc Thomas and Jak Hammer (1-0) "The Dreamer" Randy Sheradin and Joshua Barnes (1-2) "Hardcore" Joey Haven and "The Preacher" Kore Wrench (0-1) Recap of Major Events: The strained relationship between Randy Sheradin and Joshua Barnes with Max Robertson was revealed as a ploy by Robertson to sabotage both men's title aspirations and to weaken their alliance by sowing seeds of doubt in Barnes' mind about Sheradin's integrity. Right now, matters remain unresolved. Sheradin scored a big win over Robertson in the finals of the CWAA Heavyweight Title tournamentm, but turned right around and scored a clean pinfall over the champ a week later in a tag team match. Robertson's antics appear to be having an unexoected on Barnes' psyches, as the ex-bouncer used his anger at Robertson, who has belittled Barnes' role in the feud all along, to demolish Double Jeopardy in the first round of the CWAA Tag Team Title tournament. At present, 2 matches are signed which are relevant to this feud: on April 6th, Randy Sheradin makes his first title defense, against Robertson. On the 14th, all three men will be in the United Kingdom for the ACWF's Rule Britannia! pay per view event. Randy Sheradin is a full-time ACWF wrestler; Barnes and Robertson will be appearing in a special match to be agreed upon by the CWAA and ACWF. Working Relationships: As a member of the AEWA, the CWAA is entitled to call upon AEWA champions to defend their titles against CWAA stars on occasion, and has the opportunity to send stars to AEWA events for wider exposure. Thus, "Cardiac Kid" Joey Valachi brought his AEWA World Light Heavyweight Championship belt into the Sportatorium to face the most decorated wrestler in the CWAA today, Marc Thomas. Earkeir matches on the program and some lengthy interview segments, particularly by Thomas himself, restricted the available time for the match, and time expired just as Marc Thomas was executing a second Icebreaker on the champ in mid ring. Valachi held out as long as he could againt the challenger, kicking out of several pin attempts and escaping a loss by the dreaded Icebreaker at least once by draping his foot over the bottom rope as the referee was about to count three. The AEWA's next pay per view extravaganza will mark the first-ever appearance by CWAA wrestlers on an AEWA program, AEWA Extreme. Jak Hammer and "Hardcore" Joey Haven will take their feud to the Core States Center for a falls-count-anywhere-in-the-building contest. Negotiations are underway for continued CWAA involvement in AEWA events, as well as for future AEWA Title defenses on CWAA programming. Earlier in the month, three-time AEWA World Tag Team champions, the West Coast Connection, stunned the Sportatorium crowd with their unannounced appearance in support of Mark Sherrick against Midnight Rampage. Other organizations have been actively working with the CWAA of late, as well. The FWA sent top contenders the Lethal Weapons to the Sportatorium to face Twister in the first round of the CWAA Tag Team Title tournament, before eliminating themselves via disqualification for shoving senior official Jeff Howard. The Lethal Weapons took out their frustrations on Twister in a post-match altercation. The pervious evening had seen the duo of Max Robertson and Lt. Lawson score the win over Randy Sheradin and Joshua Barnes on the FWA's one year anniversary program. The ACWF, home to CWAA-familiar stars such as Randy Sheradin and Joey Valachi also sent a team for the CWAA Tag Team tourney, in the High School Toughies, who bowed out to Midnight Rampage in the first round. In one week, the CWAA returns the favor by sending Joshua Barnes and Max Robertson for (perhaps) two weeks worth of matches, culminating in a contest on the Rule Britannia! pay per view. Free agents the Trailer Park Assassins, current New Frontier World Champions, appeared in the CWAA Tag Team title tourney, dropping a controversial decision to Byron McNultey and Michael Anderson. Television Title Over a dozen CWAA stars will step into the ring together on Sunday, April 6th, with one goal: to become the first-ever CWAA TV Champion. The battle royal is not strictly an over-the-top-rope affair, as pinfalls and submissions will also count: referees Jeff Howard and Chris Herman can expect to have their work cut out for them. (That same night, the tag team titles will also be decided, and the heavyweight title will be defened.) Then, starting April 13th, the reigning TV champions will be obligated to defend the title once per week. Who's Hot, Who's Not: Hot: Marc Thomas- a hard-fought victory over Joey Haven and a thrilling encounter with Joey Valachi brought him closer than ever to AEWA gold and established him as one of the elite stars in the CWAA Not: Stan "The Powerhouse" Stevens suffered a leg injury at the hands of Lt. Lawson early on in the title tournament and has been little heard from since Hot: "The Preacher" Kore Wrench- Wrench has involved himself in many a rulebreaker's affairs, starting with Max Robertson. Since then he has come to the assistance of Twister, Joey Haven, and Randy Sheradin, and countered Major Mayhem's rulebreaking tactics by smashing his flagpole, all while inching his way up the rankings. Roster Changes: Expect one or two new faces to join the CWAA full-time in the next few weeks, while several other individuals appear to be phasing themselves out. -kl
