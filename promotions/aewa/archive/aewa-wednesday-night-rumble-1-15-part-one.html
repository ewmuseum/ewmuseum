Wednesday Night Rumble (1/15, part one)

17 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We see the AEWA logo on the screen, set on fire. The image then BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Rock music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... David Graves taking some guy's head off with the Gravestone, Penance PLANTING some guy with the Final Judgment, The West Coast Connection DRIVING some guy's head into the mat with the West Coast Killa, Cardinal Chaos STICKING some poor soul with the Inverted Crucifix, Shane Matthews BRUTALLY hitting the End of the Road on some hapless jobber, and Heavy Metal notching yet ANOTHER casualty courtesy of the Megadeth Power Bomb. The camera then shifts to Matt Wilson, The Lovely Bertha, and Mike "Big Daddy" Hurt in the Mecca, the site of AEWA Wednesday Night Rumble!) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Wednesday Night Rumble! We have a GREAT card for you tonight, and in the main event, "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews will be defending his World title against Deranged! BD: That's going to be an interesting match, but let's get serious-- Matthews won't have ANY trouble kicking that idiot's butt. LB: I'd have to agree with you there, Big Daddy. Deranged might be a good wrestler, but he'll NEVER be on the same level as "Sudden Death". MW: Let's just wait and see, folks. LB: Okay, but what's coming up first, Wilson? MW: Justin Trouble is just about to defend his NA Title against Leroy Brown, who will be wrestling for the first time under his true identity. BD: Yeah, too bad there wasn't anyone who was worth a crap under that mask. He looked like he had potential, but now he's siding with the fans. The moron. MW: Oh come on, Big Daddy, that's no way to talk about a man who has had as successful a career as Leroy Brown. You can't deny that he's an outstanding competitor, and will undoubtedly go far here in the AEWA. BD: I can, and I will. Leroy Brown has yet to experience very much AEWA competition, and I predict that the legend will crumble. MW: Unless he gives up on the fans and cheats? BD: Exactly. MW: Sometimes I just don't know what to make of you, Mike. Let's just go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA North American Title! Introducing first, the challenger, he weighs in at 370 pounds and is accompanied to the ring by his manager, The Street King, "Big Bad" Leeeeeeeeeeeeeeerooooooooooooy Broooooooooooooooooooooown! (The Smashing Pumpkins' "Disarm" begins to play loudly over the PA as Brown makes his way toward the ring. Street King stands at his side, carrying his characteristic lead pipe. They receive a mixed reaction from the crowd.) LB: This crowd sure doesn't seem to be warming up to Leroy too well. MW: They're probably still confused after the whole "Masked Yorel" fiasco. BD: I'M still confused over the whole "Masked Yorel" fiasco. MW: Yes, but that goes without saying. They're just probably having trouble with the transition from rulebreaker to fan favorite that occurred for no apparent reason. Not unlike Deranged's situation recently. LB: Yeah, but Deranged's is more severe, considering that as far as the fans are concerned, he turned his back on them. Leroy Brown, on the other hand, has never previously tried to endear himself to the AEWA fans. MW: You've got a point there, but it should be interesting to see how this whole scene develops. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Atlanta, Georgia, and weighing in at 238 pounds. He is the Current AEWA North American Champion, Juuuuuuuuustiiiiiiin Trooooooooooooooooooooouble! (Tom Petty's "Makin' Some Noise" begins to play loudly throughout the arena, and the crowd goes NUTS! Justin Trouble appears from behind the curtain and looks very distraught. He makes his way to the ring and slaps hands with the fans, but just doesn't seem to have his usual enthusiasm.) LB: What's up his butt? MW: Well he was kinda fired recently. He's being forced to defend his title, and he's not getting paid for it-- wouldn't YOU be a little confused and upset too? I mean, it wasn't even his fault, he was locked in his dressing room at the time President Freeman got attacked. He COULDN'T do anything. BD: If he was any kind of bodyguard, he wouldn't have BEEN in the dressing room in the first place while Freeman was out there. He should have been right there, at his side, body-guarding. He was celebrating in his dressing room, and not doing his job. I'd say that's grounds for firing. MW: Don't you think there were extenuating circumstances? BD: I have NO idea what you just said. MW: I'm not surprised. Anyway, it looks as if this one is just about to get underway. Brown starts it off in a hurry, attacking Trouble right off the bat! He takes Justin and whips him to the ropes, Justin comes back, and LEVELS Brown with a lariat! LB: That was quick thinking. MW: Yes indeed. And now Trouble has Brown up to his feet, and he NAILS him with a forearm to the back! The big man is still standing, and Justin grabs him by the arm, SHORT CLOTHESLINE! That dropped him! BD: Temporarily. If Leroy ever gets in control of this match, I really pity Trouble, because I don't think he can handle him, especially in the mental state he's gotta be in right now. MW: That's a good point, Big Daddy. Leroy is back up to his feet by now, and Justin STUNS him with a dropkick! That staggered him! Trouble gets back up to his feet, and FLOORS Brown with a short lariat! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: That was a close one, but Leroy still had the presence of mind to get that shoulder up at the last second. MW: And Justin goes back to work. Leroy is regaining his footing, and Trouble knocks him right back down to his back with a running dropkick! Justin's going up top now, he perches himself up there, and leaps, FROG SPLASH! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! BD: Leroy may have gone soft as far as his attitude with the fans, but he looks as tough as ever in that ring. MW: You're right about that; he just won't be beaten. Justin goes back up top now, with Brown still down, and NAILS a flying kneedrop! That had to take a lot out of the big man. Justin KICKS the challenger in the head, and now he picks him up off the mat, JAWBREAKER! LB: I gotta hand it to Justin-- for once, he's really taking the fight to Leroy Brown. MW: Which is surprising, considering his current situation with President Freeman, but perhaps the adversity has made him just that much stronger. Justin goes back up top now, and he NAILS Brown with a flying fistdrop, and we've got another cover! One..Kickout! BD: He had some authority on that one. This one's not even CLOSE to being over. MW: You can say that again. Now Brown is attempting to make it to his feet, but Trouble just NAILS him with a forearm to the gut! And now Justin takes him down with a senton! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: And Leroy escapes yet again! MW: Justin is quick to get back on the attack, and hits Brown with ANOTHER senton! And another cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: And another kickout! Just thought I'd beat ya to that one, Wilson. MW: Thanks. And in an uncharacteristic move, Justin Trouble is complaining about a slow count! Perhaps his "firing" IS really getting to him. LB: Either that, or he's finally found the correct way of treating referees-- like they're scum. MW: I doubt that. The ref isn't giving in to Justin's argument though, and he is forced to turn his attention back to the match. Trouble's going back up to the top now, and I wonder what's in store now? He leaps, ANOTHER Frog Splash, BUT BROWN ROLLS AWAY, and Trouble SMACKS down to the canvas! BD: What? I didn't see Trouble smack anybody. MW: It's a figure of speech. BD: Huh? MW: Nevermind. Brown picks the champion up now, and nails him with a jawbreaker! But he doesn't let go of the head! He stands right back up, and executes ANOTHER jawbreaker on Trouble. LB: And Justin's in Trouble now! (laughs) MW: Pun or no pun, yes he is. Brown picks him up off the mat now, and scoops him up, POWERSLAM! But he doesn't cover! He picks Justin right back up, and PLANTS him with ANOTHER Powerslam! And NOW we've got a cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: He's just barely hanging on. It won't be long now. MW: We'll see. Brown picks Justin up off the mat now, and whips him to the turnbuckle! Leroy charges in shoulder-first behind him, and just CRUSHES him into the corner! Justin STAGGERS out of the corner, only to be leveled by a dropkick from the big man! LB: Did you see how high he got up? That's AMAZING for a man of his size. MW: Indeed it is. Brown brings Trouble back up to his feet, and CHOPS away at him! And now he grabs Justin by the head, BULLDOG! That'll take the wind out of your sails! BD: What? Did a sailing competition break out while I wasn't looking? MW: No, it's another figure of.... oh, forget it. Justin is now struggling to get to his feet, but Leroy takes him down with an inside cradle! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Big Daddy was right earlier when he said that Leroy would win once he got control. Just look at him DOMINATE. MW: Yes, but Trouble dominated earlier, and look where it got him. This one's still up for grabs, trust me. Brown brings Trouble back up to his feet, and NAILS him with a roundhouse right! That rocked him, and Leroy sends him ALL the way down with ANOTHER standing dropkick! BD: I'm calling it right here-- there'll be a new champion tonight, folks! MW: It's not over yet, Big Daddy. Brown picks Trouble up off the mat now, and whips him to the ropes! Justin comes back, and is BRUTALLY powerslammed to the mat! And the cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: How on EARTH did he get out of there? MW: He's not going down as long as he can help it, and right now he's fighting it for all he's worth. Leroy is upset that he didn't get the duke right there, and he's letting the ref know it. He picks Justin back up now, and whips him to the ropes--NO! Reversal by Trouble! Brown comes back, and gets LEVELED with a superkick! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! BD: That was the LUCKIEST move I've ever seen in my life. MW: It worked, didn't it? Justin is slow to get back up after that, and rightfully so after all he's been through. Brown gets up as well, and Justin throws in an armdrag--BUT BROWN COUNTERS WITH A LARIAT! LB: (laughing) Boy, that was a pretty good offensive series by Trouble, huh? MW: That's all he could muster after the beating he's just taken. I don't blame him. And now Brown picks the champion back up, PILEDRIVER! And the cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! BD: The end is near for Justin Trouble's North American Championship reign! MW: And now Brown locks on a choke hold! The ref is counting, and Brown breaks at four, but goes right back to it! This isn't very consistent with his supposed "new attitude". This is absolutely unfair! LB: So's life, Wilson. Deal with it. MW: AGAIN Brown lets go at four, and he AGAIN goes right back to the choke! The referee is threatening disqualification, and this time Brown lets go for real. Justin is on the mat after all that choking, and now Leroy DROPS an elbow on him! BD: And Justin was just SQUASHED by all that weight. MW: Yes he was. And now Brown picks Justin up, and PLANTS him with a side suplex! Brown lifts Trouble up off the mat again, and TOSSES him to the outside, where he's quick to follow! The referee is laying a count on both men. BD: This is a BAD place for Justin to be right about now. MW: No kidding. And now Brown picks the champion up, and WHIPS him to the guardrail! Street King is making his way over to them, and I don't like the looks of this. LB: He looks like he's getting ready to put a notch in that lead pipe! MW: Brown is holding up Justin by the arms, and the Street King winds up like he's holding a baseball bat! He SWINGS--BUT Justin moves out of the way, and the Street King knocks his own man COLD! Justin rolls back into the ring, and the ref's calling for the bell! (DING, DING) He must've reached ten, but did Justin make it back in time? RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of nineteen minutes and thirty-seven seconds, here is your winner, as a result of a countout, and STILL AEWA North American Champion.... JUSTIN TROUBLE!!!! LB: It looks like Street King's little plan backfired there, huh? MW: It certainly did; it caused his man to get counted out, and Justin Trouble was able to retain the title. What kind of conduct is that if these guys are supposed to be fan favorites, anyway? BD: Hey, the fans should respect that kinda thing. When it works, it gets wins, doesn't it? MW: Yes, but it gets you victories that you didn't earn. BD: There you go bringing morality into it again. This is wrestling, Wilson. Winning is winning; bottom line. MW: But in this case, cheating didn't work for Leroy Brown. LB: Justin Trouble just got lucky, buddy. And since when did you know anything about wrestling in the first place? MW: I'd like to think I know a lot about it-- it's my job, you know. Your job is just to sit there and make dumb remarks. And..... Wait a minute, there's someone coming to ringside now! It's Traci, Justin's former manager! BD: What the hell does SHE want? I thought she left him to run some federation or something. MW: She did, but it appears that she's back, for some reason. Justin WAS still in the ring, but now he's coming down the aisle to meet her! They're about to meet halfway, and Justin opens his arms for a hug, BUT TRACI JUST SLAPS HIM! And now she's running back to the locker room! LB: What was THAT all about? MW: No clue. And it looks as if Justin doesn't understand what just happened either. I'm not aware of any problems that they had before going their separate ways, but Traci seemed AWFULLY mad about SOMETHING. BD: Maybe he left the toilet seat down. MW: I think it's something a LITTLE more serious than that. Justin has now overcome his initial state of shock and confusion, and he's chasing back to the locker room after her! LB: That was WEIRD. So what's next, anyway? MW: Let's see.... Up next, Sir Gallade is defending the TV Title against Bishop Bedlam, it should be interesting to see if the Bishop wrestles differently under his new persona. For those of you who don't know, he is the man formerly known as Curate Kill. BD: He really seems a lot more confident now that Cardinal Chaos has dubbed him "Bishop Bedlam" and at least SOME of that has gotta rub off on his in-ring performance. MW: Can't argue with that. Sir Gallade might not know what to expect with this new attitude, and that can only work to Bedlam's advantage. So with that in mind, let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AEWA Television Champion. Introducing first, the challenger, weighing in at 275 pounds, Biiiiiishooooop Beeeeeeeeeeedlaaaaaaaaaaaaam! (Bishop Bedlam, wearing a red robe, makes his entrance to a HUGE round of boos. He doesn't even acknowledge the presence of the fans, and looks straight toward the ring.) LB: This is the best career move Curate Kill has ever made. He demands respect now, and if he doesn't get it, he's gonna MAKE people respect him, by any means necessary. MW: I definitely respect his ability, but it's his "other" tactics that cause a low opinion of him in general. The whole Church is nothing but a a gang of thugs who have no respect for the rules, or anything else. BD: BLASPHEMER! You know that you're gonna go to hell for saying that, right? MW: I'll take my chances. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Hackney, England, he weighs in at 315 pounds and is the current AEWA Television champion, Siiiiiir Gaaaaaaaaaaaallaaaaaaade! (The British National Anthem plays loudly over the PA as Gallade makes his way to the ring, belt around his waist, and the crowd EXPLODES!) LB: Why do these people like this guy so much? He thinks he's a Knight of the Round Table, for God's sake. MW: He's just a great person, and a great champion. He might not be an actual Knight of the Round Table, but he's as close as you're gonna get in this day and age. BD: Whoop dee friggin' doo dah. Like anyone cares. MW: Well YOU don't, but that doesn't speak for the rest of us. And this one is just about to start, and there goes the bell! We've got a lock up, and the Champion strikes first with a knee to the back! LB: And that really caught the Bishop off guard. MW: Yes it did. And now Gallade goes to work. He puts Bedlam's head between his legs, and hooks both arms, DOUBLE UNDERHOOK PILEDRIVER! And as if that wasn't enough, Gallade picks him back up, BACKSPIN DDT! BD: The Bishop must not be praying hard enough. MW: Very funny. And now Gallade picks him up again, REVERSE NECKBREAKER! And the champion is heading for the top! He perches himself up there, and DIVES, FLYING HEADBUTT! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: The Lord has given him the strength to keep going. MW: Uh, sure he has. Gallade gets up now, and executes a senton--NO! Bedlam rolls away, and Gallade comes down HARD! Both men struggle to their feet, and Bedlam runs to the ropes! He comes back, and LEVELS Gallade with a shoulderblock! BD: See, NOW he's praying hard enough. MW: I guess so. And now Bedlam NAILS the champion with a forearm smash! Bedlam picks him up now, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: That was close! MW: And he's right back at it. He picks Gallade back up, GERMAN SUPLEX! One..Two..Thr..Kickout! BD: And THAT was even closer! Gallade's DONE! MW: It ain't over 'till the fat lady sings. Bedlam picks up the champion now, and whips him to the ropes! Gallade comes back, AND LEVELS THE CHALLENGER WITH A FLYING FOREARM! The cover! One..Kickout! LB: That was a lucky move, and nothing else. There's NO WAY he was getting the pin there. MW: Maybe not, but it bought him some time. Gallade makes it to his feet, and he's going up top! BD: There's no way this'll work, the Bishop is almost up already! MW: He is up, but Gallade is on the top rope, and he jumps, FLYING DROPKICK--HE CONNECTED! And now Gallade is quick to DROP an elbow and keep the pressure on. LB: He's done a nice job of mounting a comeback, but can he maintain it? MW: We'll certainly see. Gallade picks Bedlam up now, and whips him to the turnbuckle! He comes RIGHT back out--AND RUNS RIGHT INTO AN ELBOW FROM GALLADE! That knocked him backwards, and Gallade FLOORS him with a BRUTAL Clothesline! BD: It wasn't brutal, it just looked that way because Bishop Bedlam was off balance. MW: Sure it did. And now Gallade climbs to the second rope, and NAILS Bedlam with a double axehandle, JUST as he was getting up! And now Gallade picks him up, BACKSPIN DDT--BUT BEDLAM COUNTERS WITH A BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover!! One..Two..Kickout! LB: That's it, now the Bishop's back in control. MW: For now. Bedlam picks Gallade up now, and tosses him out of the ring! And now Bedlam runs to the ropes, and DIVES out after him with a plancha--BUT GALLADE MOVES, and Bedlam flies RIGHT into the steel guardrail! BD: That looks like it might hurt. MW: It WOULD. And now Gallade gets back into the ring, and he waits for Bedlam to do the same. The Bishop SLOWLY crawls in, and now Gallade's waiting for him to get to his feet! He does, and Gallade charges in, FLYING FOREARM! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: That wasn't even close. MW: No, but it's getting closer. But look out now.... Here comes The Holy Ghost to ringside! Gallade is climbing the top rope, and it looks like the Ghost is gonna try and stop him! The Ghost has made it to the ring, and he hits the top rope--BUT HE'S TOO LATE! GALLADE NAILS BEDLAM WITH A FLYING BODYPRESS! The cover! One..Two..Three!! (DING, DING) LB: He pulled the tights! MW: He did no such thing. RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of eleven minutes and thirty-six seconds, here is your winner, and STILL AEWA Television Champion.... SIR GALLADE! BD: Luck; PURE luck! MW: Gallade raises his arms in victory, BUT GETS LEVELED FROM BEHIND WITH A CHAIR! LB: The Ghost ended that party pretty quick, huh Wilson? MW: You could say that. And now Bedlam is back up and they're BOTH beating the crap out of Sir Gallade, and it looks as if they're calling someone from the locker room! BD: You're right! Check it out-- here comes the LEADER of the church, Cardinal Chaos! And just LOOK at what he's got! MW: An AXE? Is he CRAZY? What's he gonna do with that? LB: You need to ASK about that? MW: No, I suppose I don't, I just don't BELIEVE it. And now Chaos is in the ring, and he's standing over the still-down Gallade. And now he's lifting up that axe, RIGHT ABOVE THE NECK OF SIR GALLADE! BD: HE'S GONNA CHOP HIS HEAD OFF! MW: This is LUNACY! Chaos brings the axe CRASHING down, BUT GALLADE ROLLS AWAY JUST IN TIME! If he would've waited a half-second longer, he'd be DEAD right now, and that's no figure of speech! LB: For once, I gotta agree with you, Wilson-- this is out of control! There's no place for this in wrestling! MW: That's for sure. And Gallade just saw that axe for the first time, and he's SHOCKED! He had no idea what he was rolling away from! The axe is stuck in the mat, right where his head used to be! And Chaos is trying to pull it out of the mat for another go! BD: And his buddies are helping him! MW: Gallade is bailing out, and heading for the lockers as quickly as possible! And Chaos, Bedlam, and the Ghost have managed to get that axe out of the mat, and they're chasing after him! LB: And now they're GONE into the locker room! Don't we have a camera back there? MW: I'm afraid we don't, but I'm told security is protecting Sir Gallade, and they are armed with guns, in the event that Chaos should show up with his axe-- so at least that's good news. LB: So we're not gonna hear ANY more on this? MW: They're telling me that they've got the situation under control, so really, there's nothing we can do, but go on with the show. BD: Then what's next? MW: Well after much hype over the past month or so, WWEW star Damian Steele is FINALLY going to make his AEWA debut, against AEWA veteran Sewerdog. LB: Well I liked Steele's attitude in WWEW, but now he seems to want to cater to the fans, and I just don't understand that. Why mess with success? MW: I don't know, but myself, I'm not ready to believe that Steele really DOES want to come clean. He's been a bad apple for too long, and he hasn't shown me any reasons for why we should trust him. BD: Well he's gonna have a tough task ahead of him in the Sewerdog. It'll be interesting to see how he fares, even if none of us like him. MW: That's very true. He does have plenty of skill, and it'll be interesting to see how that skill is tested tonight. And so, without further ado, let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, From Los Angeles, California, and weighing in at 265 pounds, Daaaaaaaaaamiiiiian "The Preeeeeedator" Steeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeleee! (Tool's "Swamp Song" begins to play loudly over the PA as Steele makes his way toward the ring to a HUGE round of boos from the fans that recognize him from WWEW. He has dark blonde hair, and he wears a black vest with a red skull on the left front flap, and a larger version of the same skull on the back of the vest. It reads "The Predator" in red that looks like dripping blood on the back above the skull. He also wears black tights, with midnight blue streaks down each leg, and black boots.) LB: The fans don't seem to like him either. MW: I think they require a little time, as do I, for him to prove himself before they're gonna go and give him their support. It's only fair with this guy's past. BD: Ah yes, the good old days. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, From the Sewers of New York City, and weighing in at 275 pounds, Seeeeeeeeeeeeeeewerdoooooooooog! (White Zombie's "I Am Hell" plays loudly over the speaker system as the Sewerdog makes his entrance to a round of boos, but not nearly as loud as for Steele.) LB: It looks like the fans may actually be behind Sewerdog in this one, and they usually hate him. MW: I suppose their hatred for Steele outweighs their hatred for Sewerdog though. The lesser of two evils. BD: Evils? Steele claims to be a good guy. MW: He has yet to prove that he is serious about this, and until he does, I'm not gonna support him, and neither are these fans. BD: Well if that's the way ya feel. MW: It is. But right now, it looks like things are about to get started; Sewerdog is locked in a staredown with the newcomer, and neither flinches an INCH. They both step to the center of the ring, and here's the lockup! LB: Both of them are jockeying for position. MW: Steele's got the upperhand, and he rolls the Sewerdog up with a small package! BUT SEWERDOG REVERSES IT! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Good counter there by Sewerdog. MW: Yes, it was. And it looks like Steele agrees! He's offering to shake Sewerdog's hand! Sewerdog doesn't know what to make of this, but he reaches out, and shakes it! And they let go, a completely clean handshake! BD: I can't believe we got THAT out of these two. MW: To tell you the truth, neither can I. And now Steele runs to the ropes! He comes back, and Sewerdog catches him, SPINEBUSTER! And now Sewerdog drops a leg on him for good measure! LB: He doesn't look that tough-- Sewerdog seems to be handling him pretty well. MW: Yes, but you never know what to expect next with Steele. Sewerdog picks Steele up now, and BODYSLAMS him! And now he drops a knee to the chest! BD: Come on Sewerdog, kick the traitor's butt! MW: And now Sewerdog picks Steele up, and whips him to the ropes! Steele comes back, and gets caught with a Frankensteiner! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Steele's hanging on, though. MW: That he is, but Sewerdog isn't letting up. He picks Steele up, VERTICAL SUPLEX! And Steele drops down HARD! And now Sewerdog is going up top! He climbs ALL the way up, MOONSAULT, he NAILED it!! The cover! One..Two..Thr..Kickout! BD: That was SOOOOOO close! MW: But not close enough. Sewerdog appears upset with the speed of the count, but arguing with the ref isn't helping, and he goes back to work. He picks Steele, and whips him to the turnbuckle! He charges in shoulder-first behind him, BUT STEELE MOVES! And Sewerdog went RIGHT through those middle ropes, and hit his head on the ringpost! LB: And with the momentum he had, that CAN'T be a pleasant feeling. MW: You can say that again. Sewerdog comes crawling out of the corner, and Steele grabs him by the head, DDT! THAT couldn't have helped his aching head. And now Steele picks him up again, VERTICAL SUPLEX--NO, IT'S A BRAIN BUSTER! THAT'S HIS FINISHER, CALLED THE SHINING! AND HERE'S THE COVER! One..Two..Three!!!! (DING, DING) BD: Wow, this guy doesn't waste any time! RING ANNOUNCER: At a time of five minutes and forty seconds, here is your winner.... DAMIAN "THE PREDATOR" STEELE!!! MW: Well, Sewerdog was taking it to Steele, but he made one CRUCIAL mistake, and Steel was VERY quick to capitalize. I guess that's why they call him the Predator, huh? LB: That's right. He may have been aided by that post, but Steele saw his chance, and he took it. That's his specialty. And he didn't even break a single rule. MW: I know, and that in itself is hard to believe. Perhaps he IS serious about this turn for the better, but only time will tell. BD: And while we're waiting for time to tell, why don't you tell us what's next. MW: Fair enough.
