Saturday Night Slam (6/1)

4 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The AEWA logo comes on the screen, then the image BURSTS into flames and EXPLODES! Heavy metal music begins playing loudly. We see images of wrestlers.... Super Blonde taking some guy's head off with his Death By Blonde move, Ben Slocum NAILING the Death Drop, Primal Fear SQUASHING an opponent with the Bump Splash, Punisher PLANTING some guy with the Power Bomb, and a look at Mister Crowley DRIVING the head of Volcano Jackson into the concrete with the Premature Burial!) MW: Hello everyone, and welcome to another jam-packed episode of Saturday Night Slam! LB: We have some great action scheduled for you tonight, folks. Mister Crowley is even going to defend his new World title! MW: But before any of the matches begin, let's take a look at this taped interview with Primal Fear.... (We see Miss DeMeanor sitting with her lawyer, and Primal Fear is in a cage gnawing on a bone) DEMEANOR: Well President Smith, you couldn't stop Primal Fear this time and there is nothing you can do-- because Primal Fear has a shot at the World Champion on the next show! Now my lawyer has something to say. THOMAS VINCENT: Well I would like to thank Mr. Smith for giving Primal Fear the opportunity and for paying attention to my warning. Second off, I would like to say that if you plan on making any stipulations in the World Title match, that Primal Fear and Miss DeMeanor should know beforehand! DEMEANOR: Again I will say this.. the AEWA Heavyweight Title will soon be in my hands!!!!! (We return to the broadcasting booth, where Bertha looks very happy) LB: Primal Fear is SUCH a great wrestler-- he really makes me proud. MW: I'm not even going to argue with you this time.... That man is a complete psychotic! LB: Shut up, Wilson! MW: Isn't it time for your BIG interview yet? I thought you were going to interview The Grim Reaper tonight! LB: Oh yeah! How could I ever forget? (Bertha stands up, and walks towards the interview area. She looks VERY apprehensive about the upcoming interview) LB: Tonight it is my pleasure to introduce to you, the current AEWA TV Champion, the menacing GRIM REAPER, accompanied by his very proud manager, some say "handler", "Cutie" Ric Verrano. (The arena darkens.... "The Funeral March" starts to play.... A single spotlight shines on the immense Reaper.... He has a scythe over his right shoulder and the AEWA TV Belt over his left.... Verrano is in front, mouthing off to the crowd. Manager and wrestler get to the interview station and the lights come up) LB: First of all, congratulations Ric-- I knew it all along, when you brought this monster into the AEWA, you would achieve such greatness. RV: Thank you so much, it is so nice for a change that the AEWA gave my man a second look, and what a look it was. Reaper went into the battle royal, cleaned up house and then went on to mop up the ring with that pukin' Punisher, baybee! LB: How do you respond to the vicious allegations by Aaron Proctor and Desperado that if it wasn't for some alleged "outside interference", Reaper wouldn't even have received the win in the Battle Royal and the ensuing shot for the title?? RV: All I have to say about that is-- Desperado, you have a problem with my man or myself? Come into the ring, take what you so obviously think is yours (laughs) Grim Reaper could use another soul for his collection. Proctor, I think when you are finished crying in your beer you will figure out who the best man is. He is the AEWA Television Champion, and if you want a shot you got your shot. LB: Punisher, being the former champion, wants a rematch.... What do you say about that? RV: FORMER champion? More like PAPER champion! He presents no problem, the sooner he is out of the way the better. We want Proctor and his flunkie Desperado. Interview's over! Come on, Champ, let's go back to the locker room and prepare for tonight's match! (They storm off, and Bertha sits back down with Matt Wilson) LB: There you have it folks! The new AEWA TV title holder. This belt has not had a history of long reigns.... But in this reporter's opinion, The Grim Reaper might be around for a while. MW: But Bertha, he STILL has to defend his title tonight! LB: Yeah, but that won't be any problem! The Reaper can NEVER be defeated.... MW: (laughing) Yeah right. We have plenty of action about to happen, so let's go down to ringside. LB: What's next? MW: We've got a rematch of sorts. Two of the participants from the Memorial Day Madness triangle match, Ben Slocum and Twisted Steel.... LB: The two that didn't win. MW: Yes, but they are great competitors nonetheless. They will be going at it tonight, and it should be quite a match. LB: Who cares? Primal Fear kicked BOTH of their butts. MW: He did not, he won the match, but it was FAR from a butt kicking. Let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, weighing in at 425 pounds and accompanied by his manager, Karma, here is Twisted Steeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeel! (Twisted Steel makes his entrance, to a large crowd pop. Karma is at his side.) MW: Twisted Steel looks ready, and he better be. He's about to step in there with a former World Champion. LB: That was a fluke. He caught Mister Crowley on an off night. MW: He did not. Ben Slocum was a GREAT champ. The only reason that he's not still champ is because of the treachery of Cardinal Chaos. LB: Don't go dragging our savior's good name into this. MW: What? RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Austin, Texas, he weighs in at 302 pounds and is accompanied by his sister, Sondra, He is "The Modern Day Warrior" Ben Sloooooooooooooooocum. (Laura Branigan's "Self Control" plays loudly over the PA as Slocum makes his entrance and the crowd goes NUTS. He and Sondra high five the fans all the way to the ring.) MW: And just listen to this crowd reaction for Slocum! LB: Yeah, scary isn't it? MW: What's so scary? LB: That people actually like this guy. But that's not even the half of it. Do you wanna know the two things that scare me most about wrestling fans? MW: What? LB: That they're allowed to vote, and allowed to reproduce. MW: That's not very nice! LB: I never said that it was. MW: Anyway, we're just about ready to get started here. Both men are in the ring, and in a good show of sportsmanship, they shake hands before the bell. That's always a nice thing to see. LB: (sarcastically) Yeah, real heartwarming. MW: The bell rings, and we're underway. They lock up, Slocum whips Twisted Steel to the ropes, on the comeback, CATCHES HIM IN A SLEEPERHOLD! Slocum's wasting no time in there going for the finishes! LB: That's how he's got to do it. How long do you think he would last an extended combat with a man the size of Steel? MW: That all depends, but right now Twisted Steel is struggling! And he reaches the ropes! Slocum breaks the hold, runs to the ropes, on the comeback, Twisted Steel catches HIM in a sleeperhold! LB: How do you like it NOW Slocum? You can dish it out, but can you take it? MW: I think he can. Slocum falls to the mat, JAWBREAKER! That's one way to get out of there. Slocum gets up, and immediately jumps on Twisted Steel's back with ANOTHER sleeper! LB: It's a sleeper-a-thon! MW: But Twisted Steel is too close to the ropes and he immediately grabs them. Slocum jumps off, and NAILS Twisted Steel with a chop! LB: Looks like it only made the big guy mad! MW: It sure does! And now Twisted Steel returns the favor with a swinging punch that sends Slocum reeling! And now Twisted Steel grabs him in a Bear Hug! He's got that locked on there pretty good! Slocum's GOTTA be having trouble breathing. LB: But they're right by the ropes. MW: And Slocum grabs them! Twisted Steel breaks the hold, only to immediately deliver a BIG headbutt! He whips Slocum to the ropes, on the comeback, SLOCUM WITH A CLOTHESLINE--DUCKED BY TWISTED STEEL! Slocum comes back again, AND IS MET WITH A BIG BOOT IN THE FACE! LB: Look how pathetic Slocum is in there. I'm tellin' ya, it was a fluke! MW: And now Twisted Steel goes and picks Slocum up, runs, POWERSLAM! The cover! One....Two....Thr....Kickout! LB: Did you see how close that was? MW: Yes, and so did Twisted Steel, and he's not happy about it! Now he's shoving the referee! AND THE REF SHOVES HIM RIGHT BACK! Amazingly, he's not intimidated by this massive man. LB: And now Twisted Steel turns back to Slocum. MW: Yes, he jumps onto him, and he's pounding away on his head with closed fists! He can't do that! LB: Well, he's doing it. MW: Yes, he seems really upset too! He's not letting up. And finally the ref makes him stop. Twisted Steel picks him up now, GORILLA PRESS! And he just TOSSES him out of the ring and OUCH! His neck landed right across that steel guard rail! LB: And Slocum is getting the crap kicked out of him. I love it. MW: Slocum is hurt badly, but somehow he makes it back to the ring. Twisted Steel whips him the ropes, CLOTHESLINE--DUCKED BY SLOCUM! On the second bounce, SLOCUM CATCHES HIM IN A BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Lucky move. It won't happen again. MW: Why are you acting like this anyway? I thought you don't like Twisted Steel. LB: Yeah, but I HATE Slocum. MW: I see, Slocum is up, and gets NAILED with a shot to the groin! Twisted Steel's not pulling any shots, is he? LB: NO WAY, and Slocum doesn't deserve any shots to be pulled. MW: Twisted Steel grabs the doubled over Slocum, DDT! He picks him right back up, PILEDRIVER! He is just PUNISHING Slocum! He brings Slocum to his feet, and nails him with a fist to the midsection. Now he picks Slocum up, PAIN BOMB. He PLANTED him! The cover! One..Two..Three! (DING, DING) LB: YES! Slocum got CRUSHED! RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner.. TWISTED STEEL!!!!!! MW: Can you believe that one? Twisted Steel just pinned a former World champ in only three minutes and twenty-nine seconds! LB: It's not that big a deal. Slocum's a wuss. How many times do I have to tell you that his championship reign was a fluke? MW: Then how do you explain the fact that he is ALSO a former TV Champ? LB: (thinks about it) Ummmmm, Uhhhhhhh.... MW: That's what I figured. Let's just move on. LB: What's next? MW: We've got a very interesting confrontation. Desperado is taking on Cardinal Chaos in a match for a TV Title shot against the Grim Reaper TONIGHT! LB: Tonight? MW: Yes, the winner of this match will have no rest period, he will take on the Grim Reaper for the TV Title, IMMEDIATELY following this match! LB: That's not exactly fair. MW: No it's not, but these guys want that title, and they're not gonna pass up this opportunity. So without any further ado, let's go to the ring. RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, From the Church of the Pounding Light, and weighing in at 313 pounds, Cardinal Chaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaos! (The Rolling Stones' "Sympathy for the Devil" plays loudly as the Cardinal makes his entrance to a large showering of boos.) LB: And here comes our savior! MW: Not exactly. LB: Are you telling me that you're a non-believer? MW: In Cardinal Chaos, yes. The guy's a complete wack-job. LB: You shall pay for those remarks. All non-believers shall suffer at the hands of Cardinal Chaos! MW: Are you suggesting that he's going to attack me? Don't you remember what happened to the last guy who tried that? LB: Yeah, he's the Light Heavyweight Champion. MW: Oh yeah. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Mexico, he weighs in at 260 pounds and is accompanied by his manager, Aaron Proctor, Desperadoooooooooooooo! (Some Latino Music plays as Desperado makes his entrance, to a large crowd pop. He carries his trusty noose, and Aaron Proctor is at his side.) LB: And here comes that little Mexican puke again. MW: What do you have against Desperado anyway? LB: Well for starters, he's a goody-goody. And on top of that, he's an illegal immigrant. He should be sent back to Mexico, or at least sent to work at Taco Bell. MW: Where do you come up with this stuff? LB: It's the truth! MW: How do YOU know that? LB: I have my sources. MW: Yeah, probably Bootknife Kelly or Big Daddy or someone. LB: How did you know? MW: Lucky guess. Anyway, Chaos has finished his pre-match prayer, and we're underway. They lock up, and Chaos NAILS Desperado with a knee to the back, AND ANOTHER! LB: And look at the Cardinal destroying this pathetic non-believer. MW: The match is barely 10 seconds old, Bertha. And now Chaos is going up top, not usually his territory, Desperado rises to is feet, and gets LEVELED by a flying clothesline! And now Chaos picks him up, INVERTED CRUCIFIX! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: How'd he kick out of that? That's supposed to be the end! MW: Well, it's not. This upsets Chaos to no end. He picks up Desperado, AND CHOKES HIM AGAINST THE ROPES! LB: That's it. He deserves that for kicking out of the Inverted Crucifix. MW: And now the ref FINALLY orders the break, and Chaos reluctantly complies. Now Chaos runs to the ropes, Desperado is up, and he runs at Chaos as well. They meet in the center of the ring, and FLOOR each other with a double clothesline! LB: They really took each other's heads off! MW: Both men slowly make it to their feet, and Chaos whips Desperado to the ropes--but Desperado puts on the breaks! He quickly goes behind Chaos, BACKSLIDE! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Lucky move by Desperado, it won't happen again. MW: Oh yeah? He's still on the attack. He whips Chaos to the ropes, on the comeback, NAILS HIM WITH A SHOULDERBLOCK! Chaos makes it to his feet, and gets sent right back down with and elbowsmash! And wait, someone's coming to ringside, I can't quite see him yet, WAIT A MINUTE, THAT'S CURATE KILL! What's HE doing here? LB: He's a devoted member of the Church, he's here to support our savior. MW: Yeah, well I don't like it. LB: He's only watching and yelling words of encouragement to the Cardinal. MW: Right now he is, but who knows WHAT he's up to. Anyway, back in the ring, Desperado drops an elbow onto Chaos. Desperado whips Chaos into the turnbuckle, follows him in, and NAILS him with a shoulder. Now he grabs Chaos around the waist, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Chaos is just toying with him. He'll come back. MW: Uh huh, sure. Desperado brings Chaos to his feet, and takes him right back down with a legsweep faceslam! Desperado waits for Chaos to get to his feet, he runs, AND TAKES THE CARDINAL DOWN WITH A FLYING HEADSCISSORS! LB: Curate Kill's yelling spiritual advice to the Cardinal. MW: That's funny, "Get your ass in gear, Chaos" doesn't sound like spiritual advice to me. LB: Shut up, Wilson. MW: Chaos to his feet, Desperado runs, AND TAKES HIM DOWN WITH A HIGH CROSS BODY! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! MW: Desperado is wasting no time, he gets right up, and drops an elbow on Chaos. He brings Chaos to his feet, and tries to take him over with a backslide, BUT CHAOS WON'T GO OVER! And now Chaos takes DESPERADO over with that backslide! One..Two..Kickout! LB: Look at the presence of mind by Cardinal Chaos. He was totally getting his butt kicked, and he was STILL able to reverse that backslide. MW: And now both men get up, Desperado whips Chaos to the turnbuckle, BUT CHAOS REVERSES IT! Desperado to the turnbuckle, and Chaos comes RIGHT in behind him with a BIG shoulder! And now Chaos grabs him, GERMAN SUPLEX! The count! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: That was three! MW: Not really. Chaos picks up Desperado, AND DELIVERS A SHORT LARIAT--BLOCKED BY DESPERADO! And now Desperado grabs him, BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One..Shoulder Up! LB: And our savior finds the strength to kick out at one! MW: (sarcastically) Yippee. Desperado picks Chaos up, and whips him to the ropes, Chaos comes back, and NAILS Desperado in the face with a big boot! And now Chaos runs, AND NAILS DESPERADO WITH A JUMPING DDT! LB: And the supreme skill of Cardinal Chaos is just shining through! MW: Whatever. Chaos picks Desperado up now, GORILLA PRESS! And he just tosses Desperado outside the ring right next to Curate Kill! Desperado hit the ground HARD. The ref is laying a count on him! One..Two..Three..And Cardinal Chaos breaks the count! MW: Cardinal Chaos has just pulled the ref away from his count, and is now arguing with him. Meanwhile, Curate Kill's got Desperado on the outside. He picks him up, AND POWER BOMBS HIM ON THE CONCRETE! DESPERADO IS OUT COLD! And now Chaos let's the ref resume his count! One....Two....Three....Four....Five....Six....Seven.... Eight.... Nine....Ten! (DING, DING) MW: What a CHEAP way to get a title shot! LB: It worked, didn't it? RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner, as a result of a countout.... CARDINAL CHAOS! MW: Cardinal Chaos will get the TV Title shot in the next match, but he sure as hell didn't earn it. LB: Yes he did, he won the match. MW: No he didn't, Curate Kill won the match for him. And now we've got paramedics attending to Desperado, BUT WAIT! Here comes The Grim Reaper and Rick Verrano running down the aisle! LB: Boy, when they said no break between matches, they meant it! MW: The Reaper jumps into the ring, and goes to the attack on Cardinal Chaos, and I guess that this one is already underway! The Reaper picks Chaos up, BODYSLAM! Reaper goes for a BIG SPLASH--BUT CHAOS MOVES! And the Reaper hits the mat hard! LB: That took a lot out of the big man. MW: No kidding! Chaos brings the Reaper to his feet, AND TAKES HIM OVER WITH A BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: That was a close one. Chaos almost took the title in less than a minute! MW: Chaos brings the Reaper to his feet, and delivers a BRUTAL knee to the back! And now Chaos takes him DOWN with a beautiful legsweep! He goes to the outside, AND SLINGSHOTS HIMSELF ONTO THE REAPER! LB: Chaos is just not letting up! MW: And now Chaos drops a SPINNING ELBOW onto the Reaper. He wants this one BAD, folks! Chaos brings the Reaper to his feet, and takes him over with ANOTHER belly-to-belly suplex! The cover! One..Two..Shoulder Up! LB: But the champ's not ready to go down yet! MW: I thought you were rooting for Cardinal Chaos. LB: I'll root for whoever I want to, whenever I want to! MW: Okay. Anyway, back in the ring, Chaos brings the Reaper to his feet, and whips him to the ropes--THE CHAMP REVERSES IT! Chaos, off the ropes, caught by the Reaper, BODYSLAM--NO! Chaos turned it into a small package! One..Two..Reversal! MW: But the Reaper reverses it into a small package of his own! One..Kickout! LB: I don't believe that the Reaper has much experience with that small package. MW: Well, it's not a move you see everyday from a 435 pounder. Both men up, and Chaos NAILS the Reaper with a knee in the back! Chaos grabs him, INVERTED CRUCIFIX--BLOCKED BY THE REAPER! LB: He saw that one coming a mile away. MW: Yup, and he did something about it. But Chaos is not phased by that counter, and he switches to that belly-to-belly suplex again. AND HE NAILS IT! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: The Cardinal's really taking it to the champ, and he's already wrestled another match! MW: Yes, that is quite remarkable. Chaos now, brings the Reaper to his feet, and takes him back DOWN with a legsweep! The Reaper slowly gets up, AND GETS NAILED WITH A JUMPING DDT FROM CHAOS! He's going for the cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: The champ is getting in like NO offense at all! MW: Chaos now, tries to apply a kneelock submission, AND GETS A KICK IN THE HEAD FOR HIS TROUBLE! Reaper now, showing signs of life, gets up, and DROPS a BIG headbutt onto the Cardinal! LB: Finally! I thought he was dead or something. MW: And now Reaper is putting the boots to Cardinal Chaos. He brings the Cardinal to his feet, and takes him DOWN with a bulldog headlock! And now he DROPS a forearm into the back of Cardinal Chaos. He helps the Cardinal up, grabs him by the neck, CHOKESLAM! LB: WOW! Now the Reaper's really kicking the crap out of Chaos! MW: Reaper's not done yet! He picks Chaos up, and whips him to the ropes, WHERE CHAOS GETS TRIPPED BY RICK VERRANO! LB: Some fine managing in there. MW: Yeah, right. And the Reaper quickly takes advantage of the situation, jumping onto Chaos with a forearm into the back! And now the Reaper repositions himself, AND BEGINS TO BITE INTO THE HEAD OF CARDINAL CHAOS! LB: He's getting VICIOUS! I like it! MW: The Reaper now, gets up, and he brings Chaos to his feet as well. He picks Chaos up, and NAILS him with a stomach-breaker! But he's still not done! He lifts Chaos to his feet again, grabs him by the neck, ANOTHER CHOKESLAM! WHAT IMPACT! And now the Reaper is going for the pin! One..Two..Thr..Shoulder Up! LB: And the Cardinal just BARELY got out of that one. The Reaper has just been ON FIRE! MW: The Reaper gets up, and starts to yell at the ref. He really thought that was three. But wait a minute, here comes Chaos from behind, AND HE QUICKLY ROLLS UP THE GRIM REAPER! One..Two..Kickout! LB: The Cardinal's got a lot more juice left than I thought after that attack by the Reaper. MW: The Reaper gets up, and he sure looks mad, but Chaos is up too, and he quickly NAILS the champ with a short lariat! The cover! One..Shoulder Up! LB: The Reaper's not too happy about that one! MW: No, but he hasn't had time to do anything about it. They both get up, and Chaos whips the big man off the ropes, The Reaper comes back, AND GETS NAILED WITH A POWERSLAM FROM CHAOS! The cover! One..Two..Kickout! LB: And now look at this display from Chaos! MW: And he's still going to work. He goes up top, FLYING ELBOWDROP--BUT THE REAPER MOVES! And Chaos hits the mat hard! The Reaper slowly makes it to his feet, as does Chaos. LB: Both of them look like they're out on their feet. MW: Reaper looks to have the advantage, and he NAILS Cardinal Chaos with a chop! He kicks Chaos in the knee, and now a kick to the midsection, Chaos is doubled over! The Reaper picks him up, POWER BOMB! HE NAILED IT! The cover! One..Two..Thr..Foot on the ropes! LB: They don't get any closer than that one! MW: No they don't! The Grim Reaper is UPSET! What's he doing? He's going up top! A 435 pound man on the top rope, he dives, FLYING SPLASH--BUT CHAOS MOVES! LB: Chaos still had the presence of mind to roll out of that one. The Grim Reaper is in major pain! MW: Chaos manages to get up. He goes to the outside on the apron, AND SLINGSHOTS HIMSELF ONTO THE STILL PRONE REAPER! The Reaper is HURT! And now Chaos is going for the cover! One..Two..Three!!!! (DING, DING) LB: He did it! I told you he'd do it! He had to go through two men, but he did. What a glorious day for the Church! RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner, and NEEEEEEEEEW AEWA Television Champion... CARDINAL CHAOS! MW: You're right. He won the title fair and square, but the way he got the shot was VERY questionable. LB: So what? He's the TV champ, ain't he? MW: Yes, but I believe that Desperado might have something to say about that. LB: Who cares? That little wimp probably can't even wrestle anymore after Curate Kill power bombed him on the concrete. And even if he can, Cardinal Chaos will just kick his butt again. The guy's hopeless. MW: Hardly, but let's move on to our next match. LB: What do we got? MW: Mister Crowley is defending the AEWA World title against the Crazy Canuck... LB: Excuse me, did you just say the Crazy Canuck? MW: Yes, why? LB: What the hell did he do to earn a title shot? MW: I don't know, you'd have to ask the AEWA Championship Committee. LB: Oh yeah, those idiots. It doesn't matter anyway, because Mister Crowley will just kick his little Canadian butt. MW: Are you finished? LB: Yes. MW: Good, because as I was saying, Mister Crowley is defending the World Title against the Crazy Canuck in a Casket Match! LB: A Casket match? That's Crowley's specialty! Now the Canuck REALLY doesn't stand a chance! MW: We'll just see about that, let's go to the ring! RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is a Casket Match for the AEWA World Heavyweight Title, it will continue until one competitor places the other inside the casket and closes the lid! Introducing first, the challenger, weighing in at 253 pounds and accompanied by his manager, NATO, The Crazy Canuuuuuuuuuuuuuck! ("Hit me with your Best Shot" plays loudly on the PA as the Crazy Canuck makes his entrance to a sizable crowd pop. NATO is at his side.) LB: Here comes the sacrificial lamb. MW: Will you stop that? The Canuck is a fine competitor. LB: Yeah, and Primal Fear is a technically gifted athlete. RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from the Darkside, weighing in at 328 pounds, he is the four time and current AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World, Mister Crowleeeeeeeeeeeeeeey! (The lights go out, and the eerie organ intro of "Mister Crowley" by Ozzy Osbourne begins to play. Crowley makes his entrance, pushing the casket, to a large chorus of boos. He wears the AEWA World Title Belt around his waist. He gets to ringside, and raises the lights.) MW: That man REALLY gives me the creeps. LB: That's the idea. MW: Mr. Crowley steps into the ring, and is immediately attacked by the Crazy Canuck! LB: That's what the Canuck's gotta do if he wants to have ANY hope against Mister Crowley! MW: Canuck on the attack, takes Mister Crowley down with snap mare! Now he quickly goes up top, AND NAILS A DIVING HEADBUTT! The Canuck picks Crowley up, and whips him to the turnbuckle! He charges in behind Crowley, BUT CROWLEY MOVES! And the Canuck hits the buckles hard! LB: Just look at this pathetic attempt to take it to Mister Crowley! MW: Canuck staggers out of the corner, AND MISTER CROWLEY NAILS HIM WITH A JUMPING SIDE KICK! He whips the Canuck to the ropes, on the comeback, THE CANUCK NAILS HIM WITH A FLYING FOREARM! LB: Don't worry, Crowley's got things under control. MW: It sure doesn't look like it! The Canuck quickly jumps to the top, AND JUMPS BACK, SPRINGBOARD LEGDROP! Mister Crowley's really gotta be hurt now! LB: BUT HE SITS UP! MW: Yes, Mister Crowley just sat up from that BRUTAL springboard legdrop! He gets up, and picks the Canuck up, GORILLA PRESS! And he DROPS the Canuck to the canvas! He helps the Canuck to his feet, picks him up, JACK-KNIFE POWER BOMB! That's the same move he beat Volcano Jackson with! He stuffs the Canuck into the Casket, and closes the lid! (DING, DING) LB: And just like that, it's over! I told you he'd destroy him! RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner, and STILL AEWA Heavyweight Champion of the World... MISTER CROWLEY!!!!!!!!!! MW: And what an impressive show by Mr. Crowley! The official time on that match, one minute and fifteen seconds! Wait a minute, what's this? Cardinal Chaos is running to ringside! LB: These guys had a HEATED feud a while back, maybe Chaos wants to re-start it! MW: Chaos runs to the timekeeper's table, and he grabs the World title belt! What's he gonna do? He slides into the ring, and confronts Mr. Crowley! These two are LOCKED in a stare down! LB: Chaos makes the first move! MW: He steps forward, AND EXTENDS HIS HAND TO MISTER CROWLEY! AND CROWLEY SHAKES IT! And now Chaos hands Mr. Crowley the belt, AND RAISES HIS HAND! This is NOT what we expected! Talk about your unholy alliances! LB: It's a GREAT alliance! Two of the most evil men in the AEWA, one the World champ, the other the TV Champ, together on the SAME SIDE! Together they can rule the AEWA! MW: That's not a very pleasant thought. LB: To me it is. MW: Well, judging by the reaction of these fans, they're not going to be very popular "rulers." LB: Who cares? What do the fans know anyway? MW: Chaos and Crowley are now walking out together, being booed loudly and pelted with garbage from this audience. Well fans, that's all the time we have for this week, so for the Lovely Bertha, this is Matt Wilson saying so long everybody! (fade to black)
