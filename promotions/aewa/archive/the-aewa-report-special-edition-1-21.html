The AEWA Report (special edition, 1/21)

22 January 1997

------------------------- ***************** AEWA REPORT: SPECIAL EDITION ------------------------- ***************** Welcome to the eighth official edition of the AEWA Report! This is a special edition, and it's absolutely PACKED with information. We have plenty of big news to tell you about, so read on.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ RECENT NEWS ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ --DERANGED WINS THE WORLD TITLE! After fourteen minutes of intense action, Deranged managed to pin Shane Matthews for the World Heavyweight title. Ironically enough, he didn't do it alone. Randall Osbourne came to the ring and attacked Matthews with a steel chair, while the referee was unconscious. Deranged went for the cover on "Sudden Death", the referee woke up, and we had a new champion. "This title will always be around my waist. It's mine forever," Deranged told our reporters. --CARDINAL CHAOS TRIES TO DECAPITATE SIR GALLADE! In a disgusting incident from Wednesday Night Rumble, Cardinal Chaos attempted to decapitate Sir Gallade with an axe. Fortunately, Gallade was able to roll away in time, narrowly escaping death. Chaos chased him back to the locker room, in front of a shocked crowd. Luckily for Sir Gallade, police were waiting in the locker room, and Chaos was quickly restrained. The situation got WAY out of control, and we were BOMBARDED with calls from upset viewers. --KICKOFF '97: THE NEW BEGINNING Later in this report, we're going to bring you the official lineup for Kickoff '97! The card will be held live, only on pay-per-view, from the MGM Grand in Las Vegas. It's going to be GREAT, so order today! This will be, without a doubt, the biggest show in the history of the AEWA. ------------------------------------- AEWA TRIVIA QUESTION ------------------------------------- Question: Only three men have ever pinned Heavy Metal. Who are they? Look for the answer later in this issue! ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ INJURY REPORT ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ BEN SLOCUM was immediately rushed to a Milwaukee hospital on January 15, after being brutally beaten during the "Combat Cage" match on Wednesday Night Rumble. For those of you who missed it, Heavy Metal power bombed him on top of a lead pipe. Slocum stayed in the hospital to recuperate that night, but he's already back home. HEAVY METAL required medical attention in the locker room, following last week's ultra-violent cage match. He left the arena wearing a neckbrace, since the Gryphon had hit him in the neck with an aluminum baseball bat. Metal isn't going to miss any action, however. ******************* CHAMPION LIST******************** AEWA World Heavyweight Champion-- Deranged AEWA North American Champion-- Justin Trouble AEWA Television Champion-- Sir Gallade AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion-- Penance AEWA World Tag Team Champions-- The Machines AEWA National Tag Team Champions-- Graphic Violence *******************THE TOP 10 (SINGLES)**************** 1. This week, we have two VERY interesting editorials.... ~~~~ A TRAVESTY OF JUSTICE?~~~~ By: Ricky Roberts Status: AEWA Wrestler The AEWA has done some stupid things in the past, but this has to be the worst. They weren't sure who to put in the main event at Kickoff '97, so they picked "Crimson" Joe Reed. What's that-- a sick joke or something? Joe Reed has done NOTHING in this federation, and now he's getting a shot at the most prestigious title in the World. That's a complete travesty of justice! As far as I have heard, he's only wrestled ONCE in his entire AEWA career. He managed to beat Brian Rice, and that's impressive, but it did NOT warrant a World title shot. I mean, this reminds of me of what happened with "Superman" Mike Stewart, when he got to wrestle in the main event at Mega Combat. The AEWA Board Of Directors has been making some big mistakes lately, and it HAS to stop. If Kevin Larkin had more control, there wouldn't BE any problems like this. Losers like Joe Reed wouldn't EVER get title shots, no matter WHAT they did. After Larkin and the Visigothic Horde humiliate President Freeman's team (if he can find one) at Kickoff '97, things are going to be a lot different around here. I can hardly wait, folks. Well, I hope you've enjoyed my new column, but frankly, I really don't give a crap what you people think. Until Joe Reed gets that title shot taken away, a dark cloud is hanging over the AEWA. ~~~~RANDALL'S REVENGE~~~~ By: Matt Wilson Status: AEWA Commentator After weeks of torment from "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews, Randall Osbourne is finally going to get his revenge in a "chain of pain" match. These two guys need to settle the score, and it's finally going to happen-- at Kickoff '97. As you know, Matthews attacked Osbourne several weeks ago, handcuffing him to the steel guardrail. It was a completely unprovoked attack, and personally, it made me SICK. Then, at December Doom, Osbourne got a measure of revenge by DDT'ing Matthews on the concrete. It didn't stop there, of course. Just recently, on Wednesday Night Rumble, Osbourne interfered in the World title match, causing Matthews to lose the belt. Has this feud gone too far? Personally, I don't it has gone far ENOUGH. Until Matthews leaves the AEWA forever, their war will NEVER be over. Now, after this upcoming "chain of pain" match, Osbourne might FINALLY get rid of Matthews. It's going to be a bloody battle, but when the smoke clears, there WILL be a winner. As far as I'm concerned, there's no doubt about it-- Osbourne will emerge victorious.
