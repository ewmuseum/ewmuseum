Sunday May 4th, 1997, Card (1/3)

4 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last Week on Championship Wrestling Association of America Eric Lane: Mother Nature running to warn Paul Langford- but Anderson and McNultey get their first! Barnes and Conley slowly getting up- Barnes reaching to the corner- they pulled Langford from the apron, and- oh, no! They just slammed his face right into that steel post... *********************************************************************** Eric Lane: Hello, everyone, and welcome to another week of Championship Wrestling. This week, we'll hear from Paul "the Tornado" Langford about the... the just outrageous incident that you just saw a few seconds ago. And his partner, co-holder of the CWAA Tag belts, Travis Conly,y steps into the ring one-on-one with Michael Anderson- Conley wants both Anderson and McNultey in the ring, but in Paul's absence he'll have to do it one at a time. Jak Hammer and "The Preacher" Kore Wrench step into the ring, this time face to face, and Lt. Lawson has offered a CWAA Television Championship match later this evening to the winner. We'll get another look at two of the top young up-and-coming stars in the sport today- a battle of second-generation stars, if you will, as Mark Sherrick and Little Yvette face off. And in our special feature match this week- you are simply not going to believe this- CWAA Champion Max "the Professional" Robertson climbs into the ring with almost 500 lb. of AEWA World Heavyweight Champion Heavy Metal! I hope the crew has re-inforced the ring tonight, because it's no normal ring that can hold Heavy Metal. When we get back from these messages, we'll pay a visit to the Tornado. Don't touch that dial! (This Monday at 11:00, here on WYXZ TV 39, join Richie, Ralph Malph, and, of course, the Fonz, for more of those zany "Happy Days" hijinks!) EL: Let's listen to some words from Paul Langford, convalescing back home in Omaha, Nebraska. ******************************************************************* (The camera opens up into a living room. The decor is very tasteful, and several wrestling trophies are seen on the mantle in the background. Paul "The Tornado" Langford is seen sitting in a recliner chair, dressed in street clothes. His nose is taped up, and both his eyes are blackened.) Paul Langford: As you can see, I'm not in Dallas this week. The doctor's up here in Omaha have set my nose, and they suggested I take a couple of weeks off to let it heal. I've been hurt before, and in this sport, I'm sure I'll be hurt again, so the pain does not concern me. What does concern me is not being there in Dallas with all of the fans, and of course, my partner, defending the tag belts. And I have two people to take responsibility for that. Anderson, McNultey, what you two did has lit a fire in both Travis and I. He will take care of things down there for the next couple of weeks, but when I come back, you will feel what it is like to feel pain. The match has already been signed, so I am eagerly anticipating my return. And when I do return, you won't be able to hide from me. You want those belts, come and try to take them. And Randy and Joshua, again you did not get a clean chance for those belts. Talk with Mother Nature, and Travis and I will be more than happy to give you another shot. One more thing. Lawson, Robertson, just because we have the tag belts doesn't mean we don't care about the other belts. Travis or myself would be glad to show you what wrestling is all about! ************************************************************************* EL: Well, it certainly sounds as though that brutal attack by Anderson and McNultey may have had the opposite effect- rather than weakening Twister's resolve, they may be more determined than ever to get rid of those two cowards. ("Sharp Dressed Man" by ZZ Top plays, and the first boos of the night are awarded to the duo of "Marvelous" Michael Anderson, attired as always in his sequined green robe and red full-length tights, his 5'11"-254 lb. frame moving not quite in time to the music. His dons a black sports jacket over a particularly optical-nerve-offending Hawaiian shirt and gray slacks, his visage partially covered by inexpensive sunglasses.) David Myers: Ladies and gentlemen, this contest is set for one fall and a fifteen-minute time limit. Introducing first, in the ring to my left, accompanied to the ring by his partner, "Pretty Boy" Byron McNultey, from parts unknown, weighing in at 254 lb., here is "Marvelous" Michael Anderson! (As Anderson and McNultey swish about in the ring, McNultey informing the crowd that they would be autographing no lingerie tonight, sad to say.) DM: And his opponent, led by Mother Nature, hailing from Lincoln, Nebraska, at 315 lb. he is one-half of the CWAA Tag Team Champions, Travis Conley! (The "Twister" theme plays, and Mother Nature leads the massive (read, "6'8"-315) ex-Husker Travis Conley, and the golden-haired, blue-eyed boy soaks in the cheers of the crowd.) DM: Your referee for this contest, Jeff Howard. EL: OK- Jeff Howard is going to have his hands full with this one, as Byron McNultey- where do these two get these awful outfits?- is still in that ring with his partner and with Conley, who looks like he wants both men together. OK, McNultey and Mother Nature, finally, getting out of the ring, and- of course. Here both come now. MN: Good evening, Eric. EL: Hello, Mother Nature. BM: Scootch over, Doc. No call for hogging the whole table, you know. EL: Well, it is the announcer's table, and I am the announcer. BM: And you get a whole table, huh? The wife and kids must be so proud. EL: OK- there's the bell- BM: Thanks for pointing that out for everyone, Hendrix. I think the people sitting at home eating their tv dinners, waiting to see Marvelous Mikey in action have probably learned what a bell is by now. I mean, if Pavarotti's dog could do it... EL: That's enough now. Michael Anderson pointing to Conley's boots, and he's insisting that senior official Jeff Howard examine it more clearly. MN: Well, Anderson'd be an expert on the concealment of foreign objects, wouldn't he? BM: No, no. Did you smell that before? I'm sure that it's more like that goober stepped in something in the cow field on his way down here, and being ecologically minded he didn't want that stuff getting in the ring. EL: Referee Jeff Howard checking the boots for foreign objects, and- hey! Michael Anderson with a double sledge in the chest while Conley was having his boots examined! MN: Well, now we know what that was all about. BM: Hey, babe. That bell rang. Or didn't you hear Doc here announce it a minute ago. EL: Now Anderson raking his fingernails across Conley's back. Backs him into the corner now, and an open hand chop across the chest. And another, and Paul Langford may have heard that one all the way back home in Nebraska. MN: I wonder how Anderson would fare in a match where he didn't have to sneak attack his opponent before-hand... BM: You're seeing it now, sweetheart. You're seeing it now. EL: Hip toss by Mich- reversed! Travis Conley just threw Anderson into the middle of the ring! Now a running clothesline puts Anderson on the mat! Now another! (Pops each time) BM: He went for the throat! Where's that bell-guy? Why isn't he signaling that automatic disqualification? EL: Now Travis Conley with a big kick right in the ribs. Backs up, big kneelift. MN: You know, Travis was not just a lineman, but also the backup place-kicker at Nebraska, and- BM: Yeah, yeah. A gripping story full of suspense. Set it to music and make a song out of it, darlin'. But I'm watching some hick break every rule in the book, here, and Hendrix over here is dumb as a post. EL: Snap mare by Conley. I sometimes wonder if even you believe your own lies. Chin lock by Travis Conley. BM: Hey, ref. Check that chokehold. We can't have that kind of thing around here. MN: We can't have that kind of shirt around here. You look like Toucan Sam exploded. Where do you get those clothes? BM: If all the lovely ladies out there, not to mention those sweat hogs sitting at home in the trailer park with the coat-hanger attenas, found out where the Marvelous one and myself were getting in and out of clothes, there'd be a stampede the likes of which no one has ever seen since Dusty Rhodes heard about the early-bird special at Denny's, you know what I mean? EL: Elbow smash to the top of the cranium. Now Travis with an Irish whip- back body dro- no- kneelift by Anderson. BM: Great counter-wrestling here by the uncrowned co-uncrowned champion of the CWAA here. They don't make them like us anymore, do they? MN: I hope not. EL: European uppercut by Travis Conley- BM: More like a Wyoming illegal closed fist, or wherever this stable hand comes from. EL: Now an arm bar by Conley. He's really grinding it, there. And- ow! Low blow by Michael Anderson puts an end to that. BM: No, no. That's what we call- what is it?- oh, yeah: a Swedish forearm to the thigh muscles. EL: Are you making this up now, or do you work on your lies ahead of time? That was an honest-to-God low blow. And Jeff Howard admonishing Michael Anderson. Now Anderson rakes Conley's face on his bootlaces. BM: Just showing him the logo. EL: Cross corner whip, and Anderson follows him in- shoulderblock in the midsection. Now a rake of the eyes. MN: Or was that a Swedish orbital socket clawhold this time? BM: He's so quick, sometimes I can't even see the dazzling moves he's executing in there. For all we know he was up on the top rope, leapt down on top of the guy, rolled him over, slapped a figure four on him, let it go, and- where was I? EL: Anderson now kicking and stomping his foe after a snap mare takedown. Kneedrop, right on the forehead. BM: Oh, man. I told him- don't go for the head- no damage there. EL: Now a fistdrop by Anderson. Picks him up now, snap suplex. The cover- 1... 2- MN: Come on, Travis- for Paul! EL: Kickout! BM: Hey, she can't do that! Interference! Interference! EL: Now Anderson choking him out on those ropes. MN: I can't shout encouragement, but he can do that? BM: It's actually a kind of modified sleeper. Perfectly legal, above the board. Frank Gotch taught him that one. EL: Frank Gotch has been dead for years. BM: Are you sure? EL: Positive. BM: I'm pretty sure it was him. MN: That has to be wrong. BM: Not likely. You know, I thought I was wrong once, but it turned out I was mistaken. EL: OK, enough of that. While you've been going on with all of this nonsense, Anderson had him in a camel clutch, but Travis Conley made it to the ropes and he had to break that hold. Whips him, into the corner, and Michael Anderson with a big right hand. BM: an open-hand Karate- EL: Now Conley fires back! (Pop) EL: Anderson- blocked! And Conley slugs him again! Anderson- blocked again! And Conley uncorks another right hand! MN: Anderson threw the first punches! BM: Don't hit, don't count. EL: Conley ducks a right hand- spins him- atomic knee drop! BM: I have to go lodge a formal protest about this one! (Exit BM) MN: No you don't! (Exit MN) EL: OK- Mother Nature and the Pretty Boy heading to the ring- powerslam by Travis Conley- the cover... 1.. no! He got up and is heading for Byron McNultey- worry about the one in the ring, Travis! He just swiped at McNultey, and those cheap sunglasses go flying- Anderson grabs him from behind- rolls him up- 1... 2... he's got the tights! He got it! Michael Anderson, with an assist from his partner and a handful of tights, gets the duke! DM: Here is your winner, "Marvelous" Michael Anderson! (Crowd boos as "Sharp Dressed Man" plays) EL: Hey- look out! Travis Conley was just showing Jeff Howard how his tights were pulled, and Anderson and McNultey just hit him with double sledges from behind! Now Michael Anderson had Mother Nature and Jeff Howard cut off as Byron McNultey connects a series if right hands. Now he's stomping away on Conley- here comes help! (Pop as "Preacher" Kore Wrench, Little Yvette and Joshua Barnes run to the ring and Anderson and McNultey scoot away) EL: It looks like Travis Conley is all right, but while the cat's away, the mice have been playing. We're going to take a break now- OK, I see Conley back up, and what an angry expression on that face!- when we get back, Jak Hammer and Kore Wrench meet to decide who faces Lt. Lawson later in this broadcast! (A special encore performance this week on the WYXZ TV 39 movie of the week, Wednesday night at 8- "Shaft"!) EL: OK- let's go to the ring, and get a few words from a real up and coming star around here- keep an eye on this kid, folks. DM: Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome Little Yvette! ("My Wave" by Soundgarden plays as the 5'10"-240 pounder in his blue and white singlet decorated with an eagle in flight on the back arrives to a nice pop) David Myers: Well Mr. Yvette it didn't take you very long to start to make a bit of an impact in the CWAA. How did you feel after your first pro-card? Little Yvette: David, I'll tell ya', I've been on cloud nine ever since I signed on and it just keeps getting better and better. I really appreciated how the fans got behind me out there and I was really excited the whole night. This is great. DM: Any comments on the match? LY: It's simple really Dave, Hammer got the best of me using illegal tactics. It's a part of wrestling that I'm not found of, but I have to get used to it because it's going to happen from time to time. He knows he snuck away with that one and so does everyone who was watching the match. As long as I get respect and the people know I'm giving it 110% in there than that's all I need. DM: You seem to bring some really exciting high risk moves to the CWAA. That's something we don't see here much. LY: Dave, I'm not a big guy, so I figure I have one choice in there. It's either risk my body or find another career, and I love wrestling too much. It almost worked last week. DM: What happened back stage with Kore Wrench? LY: It's obvious those two goons were just trying to take advantage of Kore being a man with honour who wouldn't stoop to their levels, so he needed someone to make sure that didn't happen and I did. I would never stand back and watch a couple thugs steal a match against anyone, especially a class act like Kore. I really appreciated the support Kore gave me throughout the night, it's nice to know there are some good people out there. It seems like now a days the big thing is being rude and violent, that's not what wrestling is about and I'm glad sportsmanship, and fine wrestling skills have not been totally abandoned. DM: You have a match tonight with Mark Sherrick. Any thoughts? LY: He seems to have a problem with family. I personally would love to make a name for myself and that is one of my primary goals, but I am glad that my name signifies greatness and I'm proud of my heritage. This guy wants to take one path and I'm going down another, it should be an interesting match. DM: There you have it, Little Yvette ladies and gentlemen! (Exit LY to another pop) DM: This next contest is set for one fall with a 15-minute time limit, with the winner facing Lt. Lawson later tonight for the CWAA Television Championship. Introducing first, from Dallas, TX- ("Bad to the Bone" plays, and the boos start already) DM:- at 310 lb., here is Jak Hammer! (Jak in his all black leather outfit, arrives in the aisle. The long-haired muscle machine scowls as his 6'10" body strides to the ring.) EL: Well, it looks like Jak Hammer is making a detour in my direction yet again- let's see if I can't get a few words from him about his chance to contend for some gold tonight. Jak, this is a big chance for you. If you beat Kore Wrench, then you will get a shot at the CWAA Television Title against Lt. Lawson. JH: What do you mean IF I beat Wrench? That preacher is using nothing but smoke and mirrors to win his matches. He got lucky last week in his match against Marc. Now he thinks he is a top contender for the TV Title. No one beat me in the Rumble for the belt. No one through me out of the ring! Now I have to beat Wrench for a chance at the title. What kind of crap is this? Everyone in this building, including you Eric, knows that I should have that TV Title around my waist. I'll show all of you who deserves to be a champion here in the CWAA. I'm going to beat Wrench and then I gonna beat Lawson. Then, all of you idiots will see who deserves that belt. And just to make sure that there aren't any problems, Marc Thomas agreed to stay at home. Thus Wrench, its you and me. Goliath versus David. Too bad you don't have a sling now, because you'll have one after the match is over....along with a couple of broken bones! (JH hops into the ring.) DM: And his opponent- (Big pop as Gregorian Chant fills the arena) DM: - from New York, New York, at 275 lb., here is "the Preacher" Kore Wrench! Your referee, Chris Herman. (Back in his more traditionally priestly garb, the square-jawed black-haired well-built Preacher emerges to a raucous cheer. Like his opponent, Wrench takes the scenic route, via Eric Lane's general vicinity on his way to the ring.) EL: What do you think about your lineup tonight? First a match with Jak Hammer, and then, perhaps a re-match with Lt. Lawson for the CWAA Television Title. Kore Wrench: Well, Eric, I'll admit that the odds of my winning both matches are long. But that's nothing out of the ordinary. Everyday good men are outnumbered in their battle against evil, but that doesn't mean that we can just quit. I've had my eye on Jak Hammer for some time now - and tonight he'll get his due. And then I'll come for Lawson. He thinks he's pretty smart. He thinks he's guaranteed a win tonight by forcing his challenger to wrestle twice in one evening. I suppose that is pretty smart - but this goes beyond intelligence. His schemes are of no consequence when compared to my will and faith. EL: (To Jak Hammer who is trying to intimidate the Chris Herman into counting Wrench out) Keep your pants on, he'll be there. (KW hops to the ring apron and in he goes) EL: OK- I think this is the first time these two have actually met in a solo match, although we saw them just last week in the ring at the end of a match. Collar and elbow tie-up -these may be the two most powerful wrestlers in the CWAA today- and Jak Hammer just tosses Kore Wrench back into the corner! (Big negative pop as Hammer flexes for the benefit of an unappreciative audience) EL: Well, at least one person in the arena is impressed by Jak Hammer. There is no taking away from his skills, but it's how he carries himself, what he does with his talents that rubs so many of us the wrong way. OK- he's gesturing for the Preacher to try again. Collar and elbow tie-up again- and once again Jak Hammer just tosses the 275-lb. Preacher like a rag doll into that corner, and look at him now, kneeling in the middle of the ring, flexing his muscles, I'm sure he can hear how loudly people are booing, but he behaves just as though he's giving everyone some special treat. He wants the Preacher to try his luck once. The Preacher looking to the crowd- he's really become one of the big crowd favorites here in the Sportatorium, as the fans have really taken to this young man. I guess he's heard the answer he needed- he and Jak Hammer lock up once more- hip toss by Kore Wrench! Jak Hammer could not have really thought the Preacher was going to fall for the same move three times in a row. Jak Hammer back up, enraged, charges- Wrench caught him, scoops him up- big slam- that's almost 350-lb. he just slammed like it were nothing! (Big pop) EL: Kore Wrench now follows up with a big elbow drop. Cover- 1... kickout. Jak Hammer now rolls out of the ring, he needs to take a quick break, I guess. Some of the ringside fans getting on his case. I don't know what he thinks he proves by yelling back at them. He should worry more about who's in the ring, not the second row. We're going to take a quick break, but we'll be right back for more of this exciting action!
