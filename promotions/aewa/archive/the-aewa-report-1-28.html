------------------------- ******************** AEWA REPORT ------------------------- ******************** Welcome to the ninth official edition of the AEWA Report! We have plenty of big news to tell you about, so read on.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ RECENT NEWS ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ --THE CWAA IS HERE! The AEWA has opened a brand-new regional league, the Championship Wrestling Association of America! The CWAA held its first card on January 26th, and it was a huge success. Randy Sheridan squared off against "The Professional" Max Robertson in the main event, giving us a look at the incredible new talent that the CWAA has to offer. If you're interested in joining this great organization, send a request to [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ). --HOLY GHOST WINS THE LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE! During a recent episode of Saturday Night Slam, the Holy Ghost pinned Penance for the Light Heavyweight title. It was definitely an upset victory, though. He was able to withstand the Final Judgment piledriver, and managed to make a comeback for the win. "I have given this title to my beloved Church," the Ghost told us in an exclusive interview. ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ INJURY REPORT ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ PRIMAL FEAR required medical attention in the locker room, immediately following his match against the Twilight Strangler on Saturday Night Slam. Fortunately, the injury was not serious. After being locked in "the Choke" for quite some time, Primal Fear was simply having some trouble breathing. ******************* CHAMPION LIST******************** AEWA World Heavyweight Champion-- Deranged AEWA North American Champion-- Justin Trouble AEWA Television Champion-- Sir Gallade AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion-- The Holy Ghost AEWA World Tag Team Champions-- The Machines AEWA National Tag Team Champions-- Graphic Violence *******************THE TOP 10 (SINGLES)**************** 1.
