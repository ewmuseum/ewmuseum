The AEWA Report (10/22)

24 October 1996

--------------------- ************** AEWA REPORT --------------------- ************** Welcome to the second official edition of the AEWA Report! We have plenty of big news to tell you about, so read on.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ RECENT NEWS ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ --DERANGED JOINS THE ANGELS OF DEATH! On the latest edition of Saturday Night Slam, Deranged shocked the world by announcing his membership with the Angels Of Death. He completely betrayed all of his longtime fans, saying that they never meant "jack sh*t" to him. Ironically, Deranged went on to lose a match that night when the Angels' interference backfired. What does he have planned for the future? His spokesman declined to comment. --JUSTIN TROUBLE BECOMES A BODYGUARD! Justin Trouble was recently hired by Bob Freeman, and he will now be serving as the official bodyguard for the AEWA Board Of Directors. Trouble has been very angry with the ICU lately, mostly because of their recent actions at the Hall Of Fame show. He plans to bring some "law and order" to the AEWA. --BIG RON RAIDER WINS THE NORTH AMERICAN TITLE! Raider made his triumphant return to the AEWA on Saturday Night Slam, defeating The Grim Reaper for the North American championship. He then grabbed a microphone, and announced that he was the newest member of the Church. When we asked Cardinal Chaos what he thought about Raider, he had this to say-- "Ron will be a great addition to our sanctuary." --THE CRAZY CANUCK GOES INSANE! After months of being ignored by the championship committee, the Crazy Canuck has finally snapped. He attacked BOTH Primal Fear and "Superman" Mike Stewart at the end of Saturday Night Slam, ambushing them with a steel chair. The entire audience was shocked, and some of the youngsters in the front row were even crying. One thing is for sure-- the fans are disappointed in him. ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ INJURY REPORT ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ WILD THING was rushed to a local hospital after his match on Saturday Night Slam. Heavy Metal had done even MORE damage to Wild Thing's already-broken arm, and it didn't look good. Wild Thing has already returned home from the hospital, but he isn't answering our phone calls. ******************* CHAMPION LIST******************** AEWA World Heavyweight Champion-- "Superman" Mike Stewart AEWA North American Champion-- Big Ron Raider AEWA Television Champion-- Super Blonde AEWA Light Heavyweight Champion-- Rick Showboat AEWA World Tag Team Champions-- The West Coast Connection AEWA National Tag Team Champions-- The Machines *******************THE TOP 10 (SINGLES)**************** 1.
