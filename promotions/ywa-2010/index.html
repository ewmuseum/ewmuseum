Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Yahoo Wrestling Association, YWA, lived on a Spanish-language forum under the line "La empresa que se quedo sin lemas", the company that ran out of slogans. Its general manager posted a card for each show and then wrote the results out move by move, as a television broadcast with commentators, a Titantron and promos, in weekly shows under the AWZ banner, which the board expands as Allianze Wrestling Zone, and in pay-per-views named AMANECER, SOBREVIVE, YAHOOMANIA and DESTRUCCION. Each card ended with questions on wrestling history, true-or-false items and open opinion or imagination prompts, with a deadline, and in 2010 the answers went by private message through Yahoo Respuestas.

AMANECER 2010, the second edition of that pay-per-view, was staged on 22 January in Ha Long, Vietnam, before 10,010 fans, and there the general manager announced the birth of four brands. The first AWZ show a week later set the Heavyweight Champion, RateDX, against the chairman, ANTI BATISTA, for SOBREVIVE; a six-man battle royal on 6 February made Campero LWO champion, and X-treme Angel won the Royal Rumble at SOBREVIVE. YAHOOMANIA II never took place: on 9 June 2010 the chairman told the crowd that the promotion had been shut down after a wrestler was caught trafficking narcotics from its premises, and the reopening show ended with The Xtreme Tiger Checo winning a scramble match for the vacant title. By 23 June The Legend Killer Lashley had made himself the challenger, and TWO, Tijuana Forever and El Aire de Tijuana, held the Alliance Tag Team Championships.

On 16 March 2015, after a last cycle of a little more than eight months, its general manager announced that YWA was over. He wrote that his own lack of interest, and the weight of narrating every week, had outgrown the fun of it, that volunteers would help for a few weeks at most, and that the decision was final, though another season might come one day. The board's members answered with thanks, and he pointed newcomers to another board of the same concept with nearly the same users.
