Message-ID: <1992Apr13.233140.15385@CS.ORST.EDU> Date: 13 Apr 92 23:31:40 GMT Sender: usenet@CS.ORST.EDU Organization: Oregon State University Lines: 85 Nntp-Posting-Host: xanth.cs.orst.edu Greetings to all those who were wondering if I'd forgotten about Dreamcard in the last week or so since I last posted. The answer is, no. I was simply putting it on a back burner until I could arrange something that I think will be quite monumental. The lineup . . . RSPWF World Heavyweight Championship: "NATURE BOY" RIC FLAIR vs. JUSHIN "THUNDER" LIGER (champion) (challenger) Ric Flair was beaten only for the WWF Championship at WrestleMania; he still retains the RSPWF World Championship . . . (dare I say it?) the REAL World Championship. :-) RSPWF World Jobber Championship: "RUGGED" RONNIE GARVIN vs. "YOUNG PISTOL" STEVE ARMSTRONG (champion) (challenger) Mega-face match: HULK HOGAN vs. STING Newly returned to American wrestling! THE ULTIMATE WARRIOR vs. THE GREAT MUTA Newly returned exclusively to Dreamcard! "TOTAL PACKAGE" LEX LUGER vs. "MR. PERFECT" CURT HENNIG Squeegie match! SID JUSTICE vs. BRIAN PILLMAN For the RSPWF World Bandwidth Championship: "JOKER" [name withheld] vs. "FLAMMABLE" [name withheld] (champion) (challenger) RSPWF North American Bandwidth Championship: "TIDBIT" [name withheld] vs. "TEASER" [name withheld] (champion) (challenger) And, just released by the Fictional Wrestling Federation and the Electronic Mail Wrestling Alliance: The EMWA World Heavyweight Champion and the FWF World Heavyweight Champion will be engaging in a title-for-title matchup! The winner of the match between Titan Morgan and Doctor Death will represent the FWF against the EMWA World Champion, John Girder! Who is the REAL World Champion in fantasy wrestling? It will be decided at Dreamcard V: The Rising Sun in the Tokyo EggDome! Thus, the final matchup to round out the lineup for Dreamcard V: JOHN GIRDER vs. THE INVINCIBLE TITAN MORGAN or DOCTOR DEATH (EMWA World Champion) (FWF World Champion) It will all come your way no later than April 25th only on rec.sport.pro- wrestling!
