Message-ID: <1992Apr16.024717.3807@CS.ORST.EDU> Date: 16 Apr 92 02:47:17 GMT Sender: usenet@CS.ORST.EDU Organization: Oregon State University Lines: 74 Nntp-Posting-Host: xanth.cs.orst.edu The following is the final lineup for Dreamcard V: the Rising Sun, coming to you in the next week or so from the Tokyo EggDome in Japan. I will post this article one last time the day I actually put the results out. The format will be as a two-parter. The first part has the bulk of the matches, and the second part will be the FWF part (the bulk of the bandwidth, if you will. :-). The lineup goes as follows . . . RSPWF World Heavyweight Championship (the REAL World Championship :-) "NATURE BOY" RIC FLAIR vs. JUSHIN "THUNDER" LIGER (champion) (challenger) RSPWF World Jobber Championship "RUGGED" RONNIE GARVIN vs. "YOUNG PISTOL" STEVE ARMSTRONG (champion) (challenger) Mega-face vs. mega-face HULK HOGAN vs. STING Newly returned to American wrestling . . . THE ULTIMATE WARRIOR vs. THE GREAT MUTA Exclusively on Dreamcard! "TOTAL PACKAGE" LEX LUGER vs. "MR. PERFECT" CURT HENNIG Squeegie match--the two men will be tied at the wrists with a squeegie suspended from the midpoint of the rope, for use as a weapon by either man. SID JUSTICE vs. BRIAN PILLMAN Fastest squash match. The two men will each be placed against the Iron Mustafa and the "Mighty" Hercules. The man who can squash the two opponents in the quickest amount of total time wins the match. THE UNDERTAKER vs. BIG VAN VADER For the RSPWF North American Bandwidth Championship: "TIDBIT" [name withheld] vs. "BRAIN TEASER" [name withheld] (champion) (challenger) And in the main event for Part I, the RSPWF World Bandwidth Championship, laughing face vs. monster heel, in what likely will be a bloody war: "JOKER" [name withheld] vs. "FLAMMABLE" [name withheld] (champion) (challenger) And, for Part II . . . World Champion vs. World Champion! Title for title! JOHN GIRDER {with the Magician} vs. DOCTOR DEATH (EMWA World Heavyweight Champion) (FWF World Heavyweight Champion) There is the final lineup for DREAMCARD V: THE RISING SUN, coming to you on APRIL 22nd from the Tokyo EggDome, exclusively on rec.sport.pro-wrestling. __________________________________________________________________________ |The Stilt Man [detail withheld]@xanth.cs.orst.edu | | --Never ask a mortician to go out and bring you back a cold one. | |__________________________________________________________________________| |[detail withheld]@mundania.cs.orst.edu | The opinions expressed here are not of OSU | |[detail withheld]@mist.cs.orst.edu | or its departments.
