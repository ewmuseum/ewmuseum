"Get In The Ring" — ICPW Hands Out The Hardware

20 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~ ICPW Hands Out The Hardware ~ By: Chris Kitching ( [detail withheld] ) Monday, December 20, 1999 Archives: http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Stadium/8095 ICPW: http://www.netset.com/~merth/icpw The annual North American Championship Wrestling/International Championship
Professional Wrestling year-end awards were announced on Wednesday, December
15th. ICPW president, Justin Russell, handed out nine awards to honor the achievements of various wrestlers who wrestled for the ICPW and now defunct
NACW, which closed during the summer of 1999.
The night's big winner was ICPW world champion, "The Annihilator" Alan
Soriano, who walked away with four awards, two of which he was a co-winner with other wrestlers. Soriano won the "Most Popular Wrestler" award with thirty-six votes, eight ahead of former NACW Toughman titleholder, The
Flying Scotsman. The award for the "Feud Of The Year" was given to the classic Alan Soriano/Brian J. Blottie feud which occured in NACW and spilled into the ICPW's early days. The Chris Kitching/Soriano feud which led up to
Soriano's ICPW title victory came in second place, while the Soriano/Bob
Booreng feud finished in a distant third. Earlier this month at Bad and
Nationwide II, ICPW fans heralded its main event between Kitching and
Soriano as one of the best ever - the critics agreed, as the ICPW world title match (a best two out of three falls match) between the two was named the "Match Of The Year." And in the biggest category of them all, Alan
Soriano was named the NACW/ICPW "Wrestler Of The Year." Soriano was voted twenty-seven votes ahead of "The Deal" Chris Kitching, who came in at a distant second, and "The True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie, who finished with twenty-one votes.
In the "Announcer Of The Year" category, everybody's favorite insult victim,
Dan Mazin, won the award as he inched past Gary Kulash, everyone's favorite
Dan Mazin basher, by one vote. Play-by-play announcer, Steve White, came in third, three votes behind Mazin.
In every sport or game, there has to be a guy that nobody can get along with, no matter what the circumstances. This proved to be true this year as
"The Deal" Chris Kitching was voted the "Most Hated Wrestler Of The Year,"
surpassing Brian J. Blotie by sixteen votes. Kitching, who was forced to retire from the ICPW following Bad and Nationwide II, came into the NACW with no friends and left the ICPW with no friends, just enemies.
Brian J. Blottie, who is currently recovering from a vicious attack which occured at Bad and Nationwide II, won the "Entrance Music Of The Year"
award, with his entrance music, "Head Like A Hole" by Nine Inch Nails. Soriano finished second in voting with his entrance theme, Metallica's "For
Whom The Bell Tolls."
In wrestling, there is always a move that captures the attention of the audience and makes them rise out of their seats in a frenzy. This year, that move was Justin Russell's "Suicide Solution" (explanation from Russell:
"Russell lifts the opponent up, and lands him crotch-first on the top turnbuckle so the opponent is facing the crowd. Russell then goes for what looks like an inverted superplex, but turns himself as well as the opponent in mid-air and powerslams him. Inverted jackhammer").
Former NACW tag team champions, The Blood Brothers (Jeremy Rhodes/Flying
Scotsman), were awarded the "Tag Team Of The Year" award. The duo outshined longtime rivals, The Revengers (Revenger/Blood Boy), who finished second in voting behind The Blood Brothers.
K-5 News: The ICPW's next event will be held on New Year's Eve! The ICPW will close the century with a bang at Millenium Massacre...Chris "The Deal"
Kitching, who was recently forced to retire from the ICPW, has found a new home in Texas with Gulf Coast Wrestling Alignment, minus the services of
Vanessa...ACW champion, Mike Stryker, has signed a long-term deal with the
ICPW and will face Alan Soriano at Millenium Massacre...Crushin' Russian's attitude is rumored to be having some backstage problems with ICPW staff.
