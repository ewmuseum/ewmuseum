ICPW Sunday Night Slam (Part 3)

22 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SLAM CONTINUES... WORLD TITLE TOURNAMENT: QUALIFYING MATCH KENNY DRAGON v. PARANOIA Written (rather shoddily) by: ~Justin Russell~ Kulash: This should be a crappy match, Danny. Think about it; Dragon and
Paranoia don't want to fight each other.
Mazin: No kidding. They... Kulash: Yeah, yeah, we know. They suck. Mazin: I wasn't going to say that... Kulash: Sure you weren't... ("Down" by STONE TEMPLE PILOTS begins to play) Mazin: What the... Kulash: Go ahead, Danny...say <BLEEP>! Mazin: You can't say <BLEEP> on the air! Kulash: Hey, limp dick, I just did... Fortesque: Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome the president of the ICPW...JUSTIN RUSSELL! (The president of the company walks out, dressed in a Richmond Renegades road jersey [hockey...they play in the ECHL] and black jeans. He walks down to the commentator's table and sits down, as his music stops)
Justin Russell: You two seemed to be having so much fun out here that I decided to join you. Don't you two feel special?
Kulash: Not really... Russell: Ah, Gary, the wit and wisdom I remember so well. Mazin: Why are you out here? Russell: Simple; Kenny Dragon's going to be having Bob Carroll out here, and since Carroll thinks he can badmouth me, I'm making sure he doesn't interfere.
("Fist of Rage" by KID ROCK begins to play over the PA system) Fortesque: Ladies and gentlemen, this following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 15-minute time limit, and it is a World title tournament qualifying match. Your referee is Jimmy Bravado.
Kulash: Mazin, don't you like the first three letters of the ref's last name? Mazin: Bra? What? Kulash: You really are dense, aren't you? Fortesque: Coming to the ring at this time, weighing in at 275 pounds and hailing from Roswell, New Mexico...PARANOIA!
(Paranoia starts his walk to the ring, but stops and starts cowering in the center of the aisle. Jimmy Bravado exits the ring and helps Paranoia up, but he pushes Bravado and sprints into the ring. Bravado gets into the ring and tells Paranoia that if he pushes him again, he's disqualified. The music stops, and "Champion" by GRINSPOON begins to play)
Fortesque: And his opponent, from Robinson, Texas, weighing in at 220 pounds...accompanied to the ring by Bob Carroll...KENNY DRAGON!
(Dragon slowly walks down to the ring, and the massive Carroll follows him. Carroll walks right in front of Russell and gives him a snide look)
Russell: Keep walking, you oversized cocksucker. Mazin: Mr. Russell, that's hardly impartial... Russell: I don't have to be impartial, that's Tony Davis' job. If this guy is going to talk down to me, I can just as easily fire him as I can pay him.
(The music cuts, and the referee checks both men) DING! DING! DING! Mazin: These two are looking hesistant from the start. Russell: They had better fight, or I'll put them in a "Boy Band match" and we'll see who's hesistant to fight. Kulash: What would a "Boy Band match" be? Russell: We lock these two inside of a sound-proof booth, and we pump extremely loud boy band music at them at about 110 decibels until one of them submits.
Kulash: Sounds brutal. Mazin: That also sounds like their fate, as they are refusing to wrestle. These two are standing in the ring. Russell: Turn me to house microphone...NOW! (Russell can now be heard over the house microphone) Russell: Okay, you two...you are refusing to wrestle, I'm turning this match into a "Boy Band match." The rules? You two will be locked inside of a sound-proof booth (mainly so we won't be subjected to it) and be forced to listen to boy band music as loud as we can play it, and the first person to submit will be the loser. The loser will be forced to enter to boy band music of my choice for 45 days. Get inside of the booth!
(Russell switches back to regular microphone) Russell: That'll show them what-for. Mazin: Here comes Tony Davis, along with a load of cops, and they are aiming their stun guns at Dragon and Paranoia...they are forcing these two into that booth backstage! This booth is about eight feet by eight feet wide, sound-proof on the outside, but eardrum-blasting on the inside.
Kulash: You're making it sound like a candy bar. Stop that. Mazin: Sorry. Russell: This should be good... (Jimmy Bravado, who has had a microphone added to him, is ready to announce the first song)
Jimmy Bravado: The first song out combatants will be forced to listen to is..."Tearin' Up My Heart" by 'N Sync.
Kulash: Just Bravado announcing the song makes me sick... Mazin: Bob Carroll is at the of the booth, trying to encourage Dragon to hang in there. The song has started, as you can tell by both men holding their ears in disgust.
Russell: Well, I've made my business done here. You two can handle this. (Russell takes off his headset and returns the the back. You can see him walking up to the booth and turning up the volume on the booth)
Bravado: We're changing the song to "Everybody (Backstreet's Back)" by the Backstreet Boys.
Kulash: My god, no... Mazin: Think of the pain these two must be going through, not only by having to listen to this, but with the pure...uh...loudness of this.
Paranoia is signalling! He's given up! Kulash: Who can blame him? DING! DING! DING! Fortesque: Your winner of this match, and advancing to the tournament...KENNY DRAGON! Kulash: Well, that's sucked. Mazin: Before our next match we will be punished with the humorless and untalented moron is sports internetment.
Kulash: Guapo Miguel is going to give an interview? I can't wait. Mazin: Bob Booreng. Kulash: How can you bash Bob? After all, it has been a 'hard days night' and he has been working like dog.
Mazin: (Fake laughter.) That was sooo funny. Kulash: Drew Fortesque, subbing for Ross Howard, is ready. So, let's go to the ring. Has he put on some weight? He looks chubby.
Mazin: (under his breath) Rotten bastard. (The camera pans slowly around the arena. Thousands of fans are on their feet waving to the camera. The camera zooms in on a sign that says,
"SLAM! Is Booreng".)
Fortesque: Good evening ladies and gentlemen! (The fans respond with a moderate, but short pop.) Fortesque: As you all know, the World Title Tournament is scheduled to officially get under way next Saturday.
(Once again there is a short and moderate pop from the crowd.) Fortesque: I would like you all to welcome one of the men who is scheduled to participate in that tournament...
(The crowd is beginning to hum with anticipation. All lighting in the facility disappears. A single spotlight focuses on the entrance ramp.
The video wall flickers with static) Fortesque: Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome to the ring "the most galvanizing guy in sports internetment"... BOB BOORENG!
(The video wall begins to show clips and still of Bob Booreng as a "Hard
Days Night" by the Beatles plays throughout the arena. The crowd erupts into an absolute frenzy. A large mass of people rush the entrance way barriers for a chance to slap hands with Bob. After a long pause Bob walks through the curtain. Bob looks out at the crowd and smiles. Bob is dressed in a pair of khaki cargo pants, brown hiking boots, and a white ICPW polo shirt. Bob slowly makes his way to the ring as two weak pyros explode behind him. He takes the time to slap hand with fans and even sign an autograph. Bob hops up on the ring apron and waves to the fans before entering the ring.)
Kulash: It has been a while since I have seen Bob. His pyros are just as lame now as they always have been. At least now he has two weak pyros instead of just one.
Mazin: He is a passing fad. Nothing more. Fortesque: Hello Bob... Booreng: Hey Drew, how are you? Fortesque: Good. Bob, the fans love you. (The crowd pops.) Booreng: Thank you! It is great to be here at Slam! Fortesque: I need to ask you about Chameleon. Booreng: What about him? Fortesque: What is your relationship with Chameleon? He led you to the ring last week in your battle against Adam Alexander. You were out here earlier with him when he unleashed his big surprise. What is the deal?
Booreng: Chameleon and I both live in Ohio. He is from Cleveland. I am from Columbus. Fortesque: Is that it? Booreng: No. Chameleon is the strangest of the strange. He, to quote
Garbage, is the 'queer-est of the queer'. Now, before you get the wrong idea let me explain... I am not attempting to insult or put down
Chameleon. He has made a good life for himself doing things his way. He has made a good life for himself by being his own man. He doesn't worry about what other people will say. He is a good and decent person. I am proud to say that I consider him a friend. Chameleon and I both despise the SyndicatE. He and I both would love to completely rid ICPW of those low life's.
Fortesque: Johnny Coast and John Blaze were taken to the hospital tonight. Did you and Chameleon play a part in that?
(Bob has a stunned expression on his face.) Booreng: Excuse me? Fortesque: Are you and Chameleon responsible for the attack on Coast and
Blaze? (Bob looks angrily at Drew.) Booreng: Next question. Fortesque: Alan and Alexa were found heavily beaten backstage. Do you know anything about that?
(Bob is very annoyed at this point.) Booreng: Why don't you ask Adam Alexander? Huh? Why don't you ask
Thunder? Where were those guys?
Fortesque: Sorry. Booreng: I would love nothing more than to see the SyndicatE crumble. It is no secret... I am not bashful about blasting the least elite faction in wrestling today. The SyndicatE sucks... end of story.
Fortesque: Don't you feel bad seeing Coast, Blaze, Alan, and Alexa all getting injured and beaten this evening?
Booreng: Of course it saddens me to see people suffer. But, let's not pretend that Coast, Blaze, and the Soriano family are good and innocent people. Alan turned his back on the fans. The fans that supported him from day one... The fans that he claimed he would never desert.
Fortesque: What about the world title tournament? Booreng: I get down on my hands and knees every night and thank God that
NACW closed its doors. If NACW had not closed its doors, I wouldn't be standing in this ring right now. I would have gotten my butt kicked in the second match of the evening... packed my duffel bag... and left the
arena unnoticed. After NACW closed, I took some time off... I had to do a lot of soul searching. I had to decide what I want to accomplish with my life. I had to decide what I wanted from my career. I decided to take my career seriously... I used to joke and cut comical promos everyday... Not anymore. That didn't work. I knew if I wanted to be successful... I had to work out, hone my skills and become a world-class-bad-ass. When I joined NWF, that is exactly what I did.
Fortesque: Bob, what is the point of all of this? Booreng: The point is... When I was in NACW... I was the loveable loser... The fans cheered for me... My opponents mocked me... and management laughed in my face. Times have changed. President Justin
Russell has given me a chance. I want to thank Justin Russell and Tony
Davis for giving me a chance to prove myself.
Fortesque: Why are you mentioning this now? Booreng: Why now? Fortesque: Yes, why now? Booreng: I am in the world title tournament. How did I get there? I worked my damn ass off. I gave my blood, my sweat, and my body for just one chance to be in this tournament. If anyone thinks for one second...
I am going to rest on my laurels. If anyone thinks for one second I have peaked... If anyone thinks I have hit a plateau... THINK AGAIN! I AM
JUST GETTING STARTED! This is my chance... They don't come often. I have no intention of throwing it away. I am in the world title tournament... and by God I intend to win the thing!
(The crowd erupts into a wild frenzy.) Fortesque: Bob, I need to know what... (The crowd is going crazy. Bob interrupts Drew by taking the microphone from him.) Booreng: Hit my music! ("Hard Days Night" by the Beatles plays through out the arena. Bob steps through the ropes and hops down to the floor. He does a complete lap around the ring slapping hands with all of the fans at ringside. He finally makes his way up the ramp.)
Kulash: Is it just me or is Drew putting on a little weight? I mean - seriously. He needs to stay away from the twinkees.
Mazin: I don't know. Well, Booreng bashed the Syndicate. He didn't say one way or the other whether he, Blottie, or Chameleon were in any way involved in tonights happenings.
Kulash: Do you think Alan's attacker came from the grassy knoll? Mazin: Huh? Kulash: If you watch Alan get out of his limo earlier tonight... You can actually see his head go back and to the left... back and to the left.
Mazin: What the hell kind of lame Kennedy reference is that? I was talking about Booreng.
Kulash: Well, Slam certainly is boring. Mazin: Wait a second... Kulash: Isn't about time for another match? Win-less Alexander against the English-challenged Scottish Monster. It's after the commerical, right?
Mazin: Did you say... Kulash: Dan, don't try too hard... your three little brain cells are going to start a small fire from hitting overdrive...
Mazin: You suck. Kulash: You know, I was in the locker room earlier. Why was your bag vibrating? Mazin: Shut up! I am not going through this again. Kulash: It sounded like the batteries were going dead. Mazin: I hate you. (Commercial. As we come back, Soriano and Alexa are being loaded into another ambulance) Mazin: This certainly puts a fork in the ass of the main event... Kulash: Is that one of your little... Mazin: NEXT MATCH! WORLD TITLE TOURNAMENT: QUALIFYING MATCH Shaun McWhirter v. Adam Alexander Written by: ~Chris Kitching~ ("Devil Without A Cause" by Kid Rock begins to play) DF: Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is scheduled for one fall with a twenty minute time limit and is a ICPW world title tournament qualifying match-up. Introducing first, being accompanied to the ring by
Drake Istenia....hailing from Seattle, Washington.....weighing in at two-hundred and fifty-eight pounds....Shaun "Dragonfire" McWhirter!!
(McWhirter's music plays for ten seconds as McWhirter makes his entrance with a huge smirk across his face. McWhirter walks down the aisle not paying any attention to the fans. He enters the ring and leans in the corner with a pre-match prayer. His music cuts and is replaced with by
"Midnight" by Jimi Hendrix.)
DF: And his opponent.....hailing from parts unknown.....weighing two-hundred and forty pounds....he is a member of The
SyndicatE.......Adam Alexander!! (Alexander enters through the curtain and makes his way doing the aisle by his lonesome. He avoids the fans touching him and gets into the ring and stares acrss the crowd...)
GK: As we can see, Adam Alexander is without that muscle-head Mangler tonight as he is banned from ringside by Justin Russell. Smart move by
Russell.
(DING! DING!) DM: Now let's see if Alexander can bounce back this week and get into the tournament. Last week he lost but he was lucky that Justin Russell was impressed with him enough to give him another chance. Alexander and
McWhirter tie up....Alexander with a kick to the mid-section....now with an uppercut right to the chin of McWhirter that sends him back a few steps. Later tonight we'll see Brian J. Blottie and Chameleon take on
Alan Soriano and Johnny Coast in tag team action. That one is sure to be a classic.
GK: No. It won't be. Just sit tight and call your match, Dan. We're talking about four of the sorriest excuses of wrestlers I have ever seen in my life! Alan Soriano....Johnny Blaze...Brian J. Blottie....and
Chameleon! Come on? What kind of product is Russell trying to push down the fans' throats these days!?
DM: Well, they are four of the top wrestlers in the ICPW. GK: So, I am their top broadcaster and look who they put me next to! Dan f'n Mazin! What a joke.
DM: Um...well....back to the ring where Alexander whips McWhirter into the ropes....what a clothesline!
GK: Did you see McWhirter hit the mat!! Look at the flab forming on that guy! He needs some sort of ab work-out to tone those babies down!
DM: Alexander lifts McWhirter to his feet...whips him...Wait. Reversal...McWhirter whips Alexander into the turnbuckle...kick to the midsection, McWhirter with a front face DDT on Alexander! And he goes for a cover...
1... 2... DM: And a kick-out by Adam Alexander! GK: Very poor. DM: I don't see you out there wrestling every week! GK: I don't see you up there either so what is your point? DM: Geez. McWhirter with a chop to Alexander....and another...McWhirter sends him into the ropes...Lou Thesz press!! And now McWhirter is pummeling Adam Alexander!! Finally, Alexander somehow rolls McWhirter off of him!
GK: Surprised the hell out of me! I am guessing McWhirter weighs a good three-hundred and fifty pounds! Look at his upper-body!
DM: Try 258, smart ass. GK: Three-fifty eight?! DM: Dammit. Someone get him out of here! I have had enough of this! (A bit of a long pause as the match continues) DM: McWhirter has Alexander in his grasp.....spinebuster!! Alexander is in pain! Look at the grimace on his face!! What a move by McWhirter.
McWhirter now with a leg drop onto Alexander! And now an elbow drop! And another! McWhirter sure has turned the tables in this match!
GK: Sure, I guess you could say that. DM: McWhirter goes for the pin... 1... 2.. DM: And once again Adam Alexander kicks out on two. McWhirter lifts
Alexander to his feet....scoop slam! McWhirter is heading to the top rope.....guillotine leg drop!!!
