Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This Extreme Wrestling Alliance introduced itself on 1 March 1998 with a full info package before running a single card: how matches would be scored (roleplaying ability, flashing, and a submitted gameplan, never a simulator, because “it's not really fair when a 150 pound man can bodyslam a 600 pound man for the world heavyweight title”), house-show plans, and a full year of monthly supercard names running January's Wrestle Storm through December's Christmas Clash. It announced four titles — World Heavyweight, North American, Cruiserweight and World Tag Team — with no champion yet crowned.

The very next day a reply on the same newsgroup thread challenged the founder directly: “Didn't you have a fed of your own, with a different name? ... I feel that you used the late and certainly great EWA's initials,” and pointed out that several of the announced supercard names matched another promotion's own line-up.

No further document of this Extreme Wrestling Alliance survives; whether it ever ran a card is not known.
