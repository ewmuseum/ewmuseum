Extreme Wrestling Alliance — Founding

A Brand-New Fed Starting...

1 March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings e-wrestlers. Looking for an e-fed to test your skills and build your wrestler? Looking for an e-fed that won't shun you because you are "too hardcore"? Well, this is the place... I am starting out a brand-new e-fed, called the Extreme Wrestling Alliance (EWA), and you could be a part of it!!! We also have jobs for those of you who would rather work behind the scenes, such as Vice President and writers... so look of the info and then if you like e-mail me for an app.. Thank you! [4]: House Shows [5]: Supercards [6]:Titles =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [1]: Who Am I? Why Have I Started This Organization? My name is [name withheld], and I have been involved in e-wrestling for quite some time. Through that time, I have held many titles and have had a chance to see how an online federation works. Then I got this great idea, start an e-fed. Show all those other online wrestling organizations what I can do... build the biggest empire in the world, well, ok, maybe not that much. [2]: How Are Matches Determined? The EWA is a booked fed, meaning that match results will be based on your rollplaying ability, flashing ability, and individual gameplans that you will send in before your matches. I have choosen a booked fed, because I despise simulators. It's not really fair when a 150 pound man can bodyslam a 600 pound man for the world heavyweight title. So there you have it, that's why I choose to do it this way. [3]: Rollplaying Rules. The EWA puts it's emphasis strongly on rollplaying, after all, it's what makes it interesting. Therefore, in order to remain active, you must rollplay at least ONCE a WEEK... if you don't, the EWA management has all the rights in the world to suspend your wrestler for an indefinate amount of time. We do allow cursing in flashes, but it must be masked, meaningany word that can't be said on television must be typed in like this "Oh S*it!", or close to it. We run a closed flashing system, which means all your flashes will be sent out in a flash packet, usually on the weekend. [4]: House Shows. Ah. Now we're getting to the good stuff. The action-packed, excitement filled cards! Right now, the EWA will have one house show, Weekend Warzone, which will feature at least five matches and ten at the minimum, with four or five dark matches. As we get more writers, we will offer more house shows. [5]: Supercards. Supercards, or pay-per-views, will happen monthly. Here is a list of all the action: January: Wrestle Storm February: Agony & Ectasy March: Superclash April: Spring Slam May: No Guts, No Glory June: Battle at the Beach July: Heat Wave (followed by the year) August: Extreme Dreams September: Rage in the Cage October: Total War November: Hardcore Hell December: Christmas Clash [6]: Titles. Championship gold. The EWA offers a wide variety of titles, four to be exact. World Heavyweight Title North American Title Cruiserweight Title World Tag Team Title. There you have it....just a little info on this fed. E-mail [detail withheld] for some more info and an application. Don't forget, we are still looking for a VP to!!!
