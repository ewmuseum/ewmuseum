MAW: 10 Simple Rules Month

August 2012, after South East Grand Parade

in-game August 2012: the narrator's late-night telephone call from Jay Chord and the card for Iron; the saved copy carries no posting dates
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

*

The phone rang, jolting me awake. I looked at the clock, which read 12:05am. With a groan I half-stifled in the hope that Katie might not wake up, I picked up the receiver.

“Kid!”

“Rip? What... Rip, you know I’ve been up late these last few nights sorting out the extension to Aaron’s contract. What’s so important?”

“I’ve got someone who wants to speak to you!”

“I... What? Rip, who-“

There was the sound of the receiver on the other sound being handed over, and then the last voice I’d ever expected to hear on the other end of the line spoke to me.

“Hello James.”

“...”

“...”

I shook my head and tried a third time.

“Jay?”

*

My jaw dropped, and I fumbled with the buttons on the phone to put it on speaker. If this wasn’t some bizarre, late-night-fast-food induced dream, I wanted Katie to be able to verify it for me in the morning.

“Yes, it’s me. Long time, no see.”

“Yeah... no kidding. How are you, Jay?”

“I’m doing well. TCW has been good for me. Good to me, too.”

I nodded thoughtfully. Jay and Darryl Devine were the tag team champions – now at nearly five months into their reign. They’d only defended their belts three times, but that was a minor detail – they had a storyline going where they only defended the titles when required to do so by Tommy Cornell, which had earned them a fair bit of heat.

“Yes, we’ve been keeping a close eye on you. It’s good to hear from you.”

“Thanks. Listen, James...”

He paused.

“I’m sorry about how things played out last year between us.”

I was left speechless again.

“Really?” I asked.

“Really,” he replied. “It was unprofessional. Look... I’m not the... easiest person in the world to get along with- Sorry?”

Katie had clamped a slim hand over my mouth just in time to prevent me saying something I might have regretted.

“Nothing. I was just clearing my throat. Sorry, go on.”

“I’m not the easiest person in the world to get on with. I know that – I push myself hard to try and live up to Dad. Sometimes I blow off steam and it upsets people. I can be arrogant, and rude...”

I was nodding along.

“But out here in LA I’ve been seeing a psychiatrist about it all. Losing Mom, not having Dad around that much... It made it hard, growing up alone a lot of the time. So I’m trying to sort myself out, James. And a part of that is apologising to you, if you’ll accept it.”

I looked across at Katie, who shrugged. She had the same look of disbelief on her face that I no doubt had on mine. Covering the microphone with her hand, she quietly asked: “What harm can it do?”

Well, it could make me look like an idiot... But then, I was hardly blameless in the whole affair. I could have handled Jay a lot better from day one, and maybe he would still have been with us and the whole sorry affair would never have happened.

I nodded, and she uncovered the speaker.

“Jay, I accept your apology. I know it can’t have been easy for you to do this. I’m glad you’re happy out in Hollywood, but if we can ever be of help to you please let us know.”

“Thank you, James,” he said. “Sleep well.”

The phone was hung up, and Katie and I slumped back on the bed.

“Wow,” she said. I nodded my agreement. “What do you think brought that on?”

“I wish I knew,” I replied. “I wish I knew if it were permanent, or even if it were genuine. Maybe Rip and Jay are having a good laugh right now. Maybe Jay’s having a good laugh and Rip’s oblivious. Maybe no-one’s laughing and Jay’s for real. Who knows? But you’re right – it can’t do any harm to believe the best of him. He’s out west, a long way from us, so wishing him all the best is better for the soul at the very least.”

I shook my head.

“And if nothing else, at least he got Rip to call at something approaching a reasonable hour for once...”
