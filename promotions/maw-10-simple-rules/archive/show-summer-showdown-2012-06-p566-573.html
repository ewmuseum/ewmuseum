MAW: 10 Simple Rules Show

Summer Showdown: Friday, 12th June 2012

Friday, 12th June 2012 (in-game); the saved copy carries no posting dates
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Mid Atlantic Wrestling presents: Summer Showdown

Friday, 12th June 2012

Aaron Andrews came down to the ring dressed in a tailor-made suit.

“Fifteen thousand dollars,” he began. “That’s what it costs for a suit like this. That’s how well life’s treating me. That’s just how good I am. And as a further measure of how good I am there is no-one in this company who’s worthy of challenging me. I’ve been humouring my opponents for weeks now, giving out title shots when my opponent had no right to even lace my boots. Think about everyone I’ve beaten: Randy Bumfhole, Lassana Makutsi, Donnie J, Kirk Ja-“

Andrews was cut off mid-rant as the lights dimmed and pounding, fast-paced music greeted the arrival of KC Glenn to the stage.

“Aaron, on behalf of everyone in the back and out here in the arena, shut the hell up,” he said. He held up a briefcase. “I’m sure you know what’s in here: My contract for a guaranteed title shot. I’ve been biding my time, Aaron. I’ve been waiting for my moment... And I’ve been learning from people like Randy and Donnie who’ve pinned your shoulders to the mat, and of course Kirk Jameson who you took under your wing and who knows you better than anyone.

“So the decision’s been taken out of your hands, Aaron. Tonight it’s me versus you with the MAW Heavyweight title on the line!”

The crowd went nuts as Aaron pitched a fit in the middle of the ring.

*

Team Honor vs. The Second Sons

As of this show, we’re back up to ten full-time tag teams (two of whom are yet to officially debut). This match, and others to come, will help establish the pecking order in the division. Shorthand version, though, the heels are better than the faces. That rule of thumb was reaffirmed in this contest, which saw the more veteran team of Casey Valentine and Antonio beating the newly formed team when Casey nailed Jared Johnson with a Sweet Sweet Heartbreak.
Winners: The Second Sons (D)

*

Backstager, and Father Johan was talking to The Second Sons. Antonio and Casey Valentine seemed very interested in what the other man had to say but before we could find out the details, Kirk Jameson happened upon the trio. Father Johan eyed his enemy speculatively, before gesturing for the Sons to join him in The Fallen locker room.

*

Elsewhere, Citizen X was being regaled with the joys of falling, as explained by Firebird.

"So, what you're saying is, that by completing your fall you recognise that the world is the oyster of any man strong enough to reach out and take it," he said, thoughtfully. "That the societal functions and restrictions imposed on us by those with high ratio of fear to imagination are irrelevant if one is to recognise that man has created the world, and that man can claim it for his own whenever he chooses. Interesting..."

"Join us," Firebird said, sensing a convert. "Join us, and you will learn the meaning of true freedom."

"I-"

Before X could give his decision, KC Glenn burst onto the scene and took Firebird down with a hard clothesline.

"Not on my watch, birdie," he said. X took one look at Glenn and decided to seek freedom elsewhere.

*

Bradford Peverell vs. Father Johan

I’m really not sure what to make of this match. These two are Upper Midcarders and have great chemistry – yet the match they put on was no better than the preceding contest. I guess it’s just a case of clashing styles – and maybe their being towards the lower end of popularity for their position. It was an okay match, smoothly worked, but the fans weren’t too interested in it and seemed more relieved than anything when Father Johan scored with the Crucifix Powerbomb to score the win.
Winner: Father Johan (D)

*

Backstage, and Kirk Jameson approached Brandon Smith and Kashmir Singh.

"Guys, listen, I'm sure you've heard that I'm taking a stand against The Fallen. Can I count you in? I don't need much right now, just people willing to stand up to the vile message they're spreading."

"Damn straight you can count on us," Smith said, with a firm nod of his head. "We used to follow Firebird, back when he talked sense. Nowadays he's lost it – that's plain for the blindest man to see."

"Good. Like I said, I don't need much right now – just knowing there's people I can count on is good-"

"And much more than you can count on," Firebird said, emerging from the shadows. "You see, tonight these two will fall to my partner and I. And without their titles to console themselves they will fall, as surely as you will fall, Kirk Jameson, as those around you fall. Father Johan is correct – darkness begats more darkness. You must shine your light of hope and virtue and goodness at all times, because the darkness awaits the slightest flickering of your flame."

"There is always light, old friend," Kashmir Singh said. "There is always light. And so long as a single light shines, the darkness cannot win."

"But when comes the blackness, old friend..." Firebird said, a wide grin appearing on his face. "At that time... the light ends."

Sienna LeNoir emerged from the shadows.

"And tonight the blackness comes..." she whispered. "Tonight the blackness returns... For tonight returns the Black Eagle!"

Black Eagle emerged from the shadows as well, as Singh did a double-take.

"Oh, you're back, are you?" Smith growled.

"He is here," Sienna said, sounding almost in ecstasy.

"Well, I hope at least that you will learn to treat your lady better than she has been treated in your absence," Singh said, as Eagle fixed him with a thousand-yard stare. "Your friend here, and this Father Johan character, have been treating her shamefully."

Eagle moved forward so that he was nose-to-nose with Singh.

"I am his to do with as he wishes," Sienna said. "He is my master, I am his servant. Worry not for me, Kashmir Singh. Worry instead for your own safety as you step into the ring with my lord... the one who soars like the eagle, and who will show your claims to greatness to be as fragile as the light you claim to hold in your hearts..."

The Dark Raptors walked off, Sienna following in their wake. The three men left behind glared at them, their expressions ones of mutual loathing.

*

Smith & Singh (c) vs. The Dark Raptors for the MAW Tag Team title This was more of the calibre I expect from these two teams. The champions went after Eagle early on, but the freshly healed masked man avoided and evaded their attacks, before bringing Firebird in. Eagle’s partner was able to use precision strikes to wear down his opponents, carrying the bulk of the match as we noted that Eagle was untested since his injury. The champions fought hard, but the challengers had the element of surprise on their size and when Eagle ambushed Smith and took him over the top rope with a leg lariat, Firebird was able to capitalise on the confusion to get Singh down on the mat before scoring with the Phoenix Firebird Splash for the win and the titles!
Winners: The Dark Raptors (C-)

*

Backstage, and Sienna LeNoir was slinking her way down a corridor towards a door that had been rather gaudily decorated with sequins, glitter and a large, shiny star. Twitching her neckline to a more revealing position, she knocked on the door. It was answered by Eddie Powell.

"Well, hello there," he said, in a would-be seductive tone. "And what brings you to Eddie Powell's door?"

"My master wishes us to discuss matters of... mutual interest," Sienna said, in a tone that made Powell grin broadly.

"Well, come in, come in. Let Eddie Powell show you what a fine discusser he is..."

"Not today, Powell."

KC Glenn entered camera shot and put his arm across the doorway.

"Sienna, you don't have to do this. I can tell that you don't want to," he said, shooting a glance back at Powell. "Especially with him."

Sienna looked confused for a moment, before turning away without a word and walking off.

Glenn watched her go, before walking off in the other direction without a second glance at Powell, who was left leaning on his doorframe.

"What... just happened there?" he asked the open air.

*

Donnie J (c) vs. Eddie Powell vs. Citizen X for the MAW All Action title This match continued the rise in the quality of the show, as the three competitors tore it up from bell to bell. Powell tried to work with X in the early going, only for X to betray him and try and snatch victory as Rip noted that anarchy and allegiance were not compatible. The three men each had periods of dominance, but it was J’s high-flying that stood out, although Eddie Powell showed off some innovative brawling techniques as well. In the end, Donnie scored with the Death On Miami Beach to put X down for the count.
Winner: Donnie J (C)

*

Donnie J raised the All Action title above his head, but before he had more of a chance to celebrate he was jumped from behind by The Dark Raptors. The masked duo laid into the champion with hard strikes and kicks as Powell and X ignored the assault. On their way up the ramp, however, they were passed first by Bradford Peverell, then Kirk Jameson, and then KC Glenn who all dived into the ring and chased off Firebird and Black Eagle.

“If that’s how they’re going to play it, then I say we drawn the line here and no further,” Peverell snarled, as the masked men disappeared behind the curtain. “Look, we’ve all got issues with these jackasses, for whatever reason. I don’t like the way they bully people, and nor do you, Kirk. KC, I know that you hate the way they treat Sienna... and it gets stuck in my craw, too. And Donnie, you may be pretty new around here, but I know you’ve had your run-ins with our friends, this just being the latest.”

“So what are you saying?” Kirk asked.

“Back on the streets, it’s all very well telling to watch out for themselves, to take a stand... But they need a symbol. They need someone to show them how to take that stand. I’m saying let’s the four of us stand united, regardless of what happens from now on. Kirk you’ve had more trouble than most with The Fallen. I’m willing to follow your lead.”

Peverell extended his hand, and without hesitation Kirk shook it. Kirk and KC then shook hands, and the three men turned to face Donnie.

“Look, I’ve got no love for The Fallen,” he said. “But at the same time, I believe in freedom of speech. If they believe in this whole ‘fall’ thing... Well, I don’t think we’ve got any right to say they can’t try and recruit to their...”

“Cult,” KC said. “They’ve got the right to say what they want... But it isn’t just talk, Donnie. Listen, I know you’re from out west. You’re a bit more liberal than the rest of us, and that’s cool. But can you honestly say that you’re happy with how they conduct themselves?”

“No...” Donnie looked conflicted. “Look, I’m not here to hand out flyers or staff soup kitchens, okay? I’m not interested in politics. I’m here to fight – and I do that better than anyone I’ve seen. So I’ll help you where I can, but that’s in the ring, and not anywhere else.”

“That’s all I ask,” Kirk said, extending a hand. Slowly, Donnie shook it.

*

Amazing Fire Fly vs. JD Morgan (c) for the MAW Traditional title This was a cool-down match stemming from the fact that these two mesh surprisingly well. It’s not chemistry, exactly, just the fact that Morgan’s realistic style goes well with Fly’s out-there acrobatics. Plus Morgan is awesome. The two traded the advantage without going overboard before Morgan caught Fly with A Shot Of JD for the successful defence.
Winner: JD Morgan (C-)

*

JD Morgan was warming down backstage as Haley Buck chatted on her mobile phone to try and arrange his next match. The door to the locker room swung open and Father Johan, Firebird and Black Eagle filled the doorway, with Sienna LeNoir just visible behind them.

“Don’t even bother asking,” Morgan said, without turning around. “I needed you to get in last time. Now I’m here, and I’m a champion. Your crazy ---- didn’t work for me then, and it bloody well won’t work now.”

“And that is your final word?” Father Johan asked.

Haley was watching with her mouth open, but quickly gathered her senses and stood up.

“My client means that-“

“I mean that if you don’t leave now, I’ll give you a demonstration on why you’ve always been so keen to use me as your enforcer,” Morgan said, quietly, his back still to the group.

Father Johan laughed. “We do not make exceptions to our rule,” he said. “Stand with us, or kneel before us.”

Haley looked as though she wanted to speak, but a look from Morgan quelled her.

“I stand alone,” Morgan said. “And as for kneeling... I’ll do that when the Queen decides to give me a knighthood. And you may be an old woman, but a Queen you ain’t. So ------ off, you little gob-----.”

Father Johan smiled tightly, before turning and sweeping away imperiously.

“You stand alone?” Haley demanded. “Do you know what sort of opportunities we’d get if we aligned ourselves with them? How much money we can make?”

“Trust me, woman,” Morgan said. “You don’t pitch your tent on a viper’s nest if you can avoid it. I got out once. I’m not risking going back in again.”

“But the money...”

“If I’m not making you enough, then feel free to look for someone else,” Morgan responded shortly, before turning back to his stretches, leaving Haley looking stung.

*

Kirk Jameson vs. Providence

Providence emerged for this match without his usual mask. Instead, he was sporting a fully made-up clown face with blacked-out eyes and a red slash across his mouth.

The effect was sinister, and seemed to put Jameson off in the early going as the painted brawler held control for long spells. Jameson got back into the match after taking a heavy beating, and was able to slow and even stop Providence with his superior technical skills. But neither man was able to find the winning combination so the match was brought to a halt at the time limit with both contestants looking eager to keep going.
Winner: No-one (C-)

*

Kirk Jameson was patrolling backstage when he met Aaron Andrews coming the other way. The former friends regarded one another cautiously, before Andrews went to walk on by.

"Aaron, wait... I don’t know why I’m doing this, but... Look, I'm sure you know that The Fallen are recruiting. There's nothing they'd like better than to have you on board with them, I'm sure. So... be careful. We may not be friends anymore, but their way isn't the right way. I nearly ended up going down that path, and I'll do anything I can to try and stop it happening to anyone else. It's just not right. You know that, don't you?"

There was a long, long beat, and then Andrews said: "You’re right.

“I don’t know why you’re doing this either.”

Andrews walked off, leaving Jameson to look after him in dismay.

*

KC Glenn vs. Aaron Andrews (c) for the MAW Heavyweight title KC Glenn, welcome to the party. Although Aaron Andrews could make a stuffed platypus look good in the ring, Glenn more than held his own as he contributed to the match. The two are tremendous all-rounders, and fought each other to a near standstill for twenty minutes, thrilling the 1,500 in attendance with their stiff strikes and counter-wrestling. Glenn pushed Andrews to the limit, and on more than one occasion seemed to have the match and the title in his grasp. Andrews was forced to dig deep, inventing new moves on the fly as he tried to put the youngster down – eventually succeeding with a Twisting Face Crusher after his closest challenge yet.

Winner: Aaron Andrews (B-)

*

As Andrews raised the belt in celebration, Providence exploded from the crowd, leaping the guard rail and diving into the ring where he met the slowly-rising Glenn with a Spear. Andrews turned to see Providence raining fists down on the smaller man, and makes no move to stop him.

Providence's assault was...well, not interrupted by Kirk Jameson's group. Although they made the charge for the ring, they were ambushed by the rest of The Fallen A mass brawl erupted as Peverell and Jameson scuffled with Firebird and Father Johan. Black Eagle, meanwhile, worked over Donnie J – but seemed to be in trouble. However, before Donnie could overwhelm the masked man he was ambushed from behind by yet another attacker, this one a man with his face painted black and white kanji symbols painted on top. On commentary, Rock and Rip noted the man's familiar clothes and moves, but it was the Ninja Strike that put Donnie down and knocked him off the ramp to the concrete below that had Rock screaming "It's Fumihiro Ota!"

Donnie's assailants joined their comrades, and the numbers advantage quickly told its tale, the five members of The Fallen overwhelming the three faces. Jameson and Peverell were rolled into the ring with Glenn as The Fallen stood over them. Andrews, meanwhile, stood and watched the whole thing with a blank expression on his face.

Donnie was recovered from the arena floor and dragged into the ring as well, before being dumped on top of his comrades. The impact seemed to awaken Jameson, who tried to get upright. The Fallen laughed at him, before Father Johan tried to kick in him the head. Jameson blocked the move, and a dragon screw took Johan up and over – and into Firebird, taking both men down. Jameson was quickly attacked by Providence and Ota, but his resurgence seemed to revive KC Glenn somewhat as he latched onto Providence's leg, dragging the painted man down to the mat where he trapped him in a headlock.

That left Jameson with Father Johan and Black Eagle, who seemed unperturbed by their partners' misfortune. They circled the stricken man, weighing him up as he turned in place, trying to keep both men in view at the same time. Slowly he turned to face Andrews, who was still stood in one corner, his Heavyweight title belt in hand.

"Help us," he managed, before Eagle and Father Johan launched their assault. Andrews watched his one-time friend being torn apart as his gaze dropped to the belt in his hands. Slowly, he raised it up, holding it almost like a baseball bat as he threw himself forward and swung it as hard he could...

...as Eagle and Father Johan stepped aside to allow the shot to impact sickeningly with Jameson's head.

Before the crowd – who had risen in anticipation of his return to the light side – could draw breath, Andrews brought the heavy gold belt down on the back of KC Glenn's head, setting Providence free.

The members of The Fallen regained their feet, the five men facing Aaron Andrews as the bodies of their victims lay strewn around the ring. Father Johan extended a hand to Andrews, and the Heavyweight champion shook it without hesitation as the corrupt preacher laughed, and Andrews raised the belt above his head to end the show.
Overall: C (Fourth show to rise in importance)
