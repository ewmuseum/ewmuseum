High Noon: Friday, 5th June 2012

Friday, 5th June 2012 (in-game); the saved copy carries no posting dates

Mid Atlantic Wrestling presents: High Noon

Friday, 5th June 2012

Aaron Andrews started the night in the centre of the ring, Heavyweight title belt cinched firmly around his waist.

“Being the champion means that I get a lot of perks. I can pick and choose my challengers, for example. And I can choose when I go on during the night. Normally I’d go on last, so you all leave thinking of me, victorious.

“But tonight I’m in the mood for an early night. I’ve got other things to deal with, so I want to get done quickly. And with that in mind, I’ve picked the ideal opponent – Lassana Makutsi.”

Makutsi emerged, looking determined and a little offended to get his shot in such a manner.

*

Lassana Makutsi vs. Aaron Andrews (c) for the MAW Heavyweight title This match made for a strong opening, with Andrews carrying his challenger to a credible opening match. Makutsi offered a decent level of threat, but Andrews fulfilled his prediction of an easy night with a Standing Hot Shot for the win at just under ten minutes.
Winner: Aaron Andrews (C-)

*

Haley Buck peered at the camera from close range, her make-up immaculate, her skin flawless.

“As everyone knows, I’m not afraid of getting down and dirty in the quest for success. I’ve paired with a pirate. I’ve teamed with the ultimate thug, JD Morgan. And now I’d like to introduce my latest clients – the future of professional wrestling, the essence of glamour, Primus Allen and Lenny Brown: Always Breakin’ Hollywood!”

Brown and Allen stepped into shot.

“We are livin’ large!” Brown declared. “Breakin’ what we gotta, takin’ what we wanna and winning every damn time we step in the ring.”

“And for anyone who’s got a problem with that, well, we got three letters for you,” Allen said, his voice quieter and more menacing than Brown’s.

“A... B...H...” they said together.

*

The West Texas Peacekeepers vs. The Canadian Blondes vs. Always Breakin’ Hollywood

This was basically an introduction for the newly full-time team, who crushed the Peacekeepers with their bruising, high-impact offence. The Blondes didn’t even have the chance to get involved in the match before Brown crushed Marshall Dillon with The Star Treatment.
Winners: Always Breakin’ Hollywood (D)

*

Thea Davis, Jared Johnson and Bradford Peverell vs. Raphael, Eddie Powell and Lord Geoffrey Windameer

This was a wild six-man match with the referee struggling to maintain order. Powell was in control at one point, nailing dives on Peverell and Johnson before hesitating on the top rope to flirt with Davis – who responded by grabbing his leg and yanking it off the top rope, toppling Powell onto the top rope, leaving him straddling the cable with a pained look on his face. Elsewhere, Windameer and Johnson were engaged in a counter-wrestling clinic, leaving Peverell to blast Raphael with a Dream Left Hook, putting the blonde down for the count and giving the face team the win.
Winners: Davis, Johnson and Peverell (D+)

*

Donnie J (c) vs. Father Johan for the MAW All Action title in a Pole match Ugh. A match devoid in chemistry. Johan seemed equally devoid of the desire to win the title, instead using the no-DQ rules to bludgeon J with anything that came to hand. In the end, he allowed J to retrieve the belt – before snatching it from him and blasting him in the face with it.
Winner: Donnie J (D)

*

Backstage, and Kashmir Singh and Brandon Smith were warming up for their match.

“We know that it is easy to take us lightly,” Singh said, with a half smile. “It is a side-effect of our joy for life.”

“But when the title’s on the line, we’re stone-cold serious,” Smith said, his expression exactly that.

“Our challengers are worthy – no question,” Singh allowed.

“But tonight it’s all about the gold. And we’ve held these belts for eight months. We’re not letting go of them now,” Smith finished.

*

Smith & Singh (c) vs. The Second Sons vs. Ford & Speed for the MAW Tag Team titles This was the culmination of the previous singles matches between these six men, and was on about the same level in terms of quality, although the interplay between the experienced team members boosted things a little, as MAW fans do like to see teamwork. In the end, the champions retained when Smith caught Valentine with an Inverted Piledriver for the successful defence.
Winners: Smith & Singh (D+)

*

Steve Gumble vs. JD Morgan (c) for the MAW Traditional title Typically, Morgan carried his foe to a strong match which, what with one thing and another, was a little bit thrown together at the last minute – resulting in a match where we didn’t really care for a decisive finish. So we sent it to a draw.
Winner: No-one (C+)

*

Backstage, and Father Johan and Firebird were deep in conversation.

“The further our influence spreads, the faster it will spread,” Father Johan proclaimed. “The more people we can rally to our cause, the better.”

“We can recruit. There are those who I have... set free among the company. They have... lapsed, I believe,” Firebird said, looking pained by the words.

“Speak of them no more,” Father Johan said, looking stern. “A misjudgement on your part. But I have candidates in mind. Perhaps not those who are worthy of the word... but useful, nonetheless. And, of course-“

Suddenly Sienna, who had been slumped in the corner like a de-powered robot, jerked upright.

“He is coming...” she whispered, her voice nevertheless carrying around the room with perfect clarity. “He will return!”

Father Johan looked at her, and laughed.

*

Kirk Jameson vs. Firebird

These two put forward a typically strong match, with the advantage see-sawing back and forth. At one point, both men dived at one another and they came together in mid-air, crashing together and knocking each other down for several seconds. Firebird was tenacious in his assault, using cheap tactics to drop his opponent time and again – but Jameson would not stay down, forcing Firebird to dip deep into his considerable experience to snatch the advantage back from the rapidly-maturing Jameson, eventually putting him down with the Phoenix Firebird Splash just long enough to score the win.
Winner: Firebird (C+)
