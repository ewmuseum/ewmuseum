October 2010, after All The Leaves Are Brown

The mawrestling.com preview for Where It All Begins Again

Fumihiro Ota vs. Toxin – Rip says: There’s some bad blood brewing between these boys – The Fallen and their regular targets have been going at it for months now. Ota and Toxin are pretty well matched, and I’m expecting a good match here.

Amazing Fire Fly & Internet Favourites vs. Nelson Callum & Glamour And Devastation – Rip says: Showcasing some of our up-and-coming talent, everyone here has reasons to impress. With turnover in this company pretty high, you never know when or from where the next superstar might emerge.

Kashmir Singh vs. Sharp – Rip says: More bad blood here. Singh’s taken special offence to the tactics of The Fallen, so I expect him to want to make a particular point to his opponent.

Jonnie Perez vs. Primus Allen – Rip says: The Perez name carries a lot of weight in this business, and little Jonnie gets a chance to impress in this match. Allen’s getting called the Rookie Monster – and a year into his career he’s already a three-time champion here in MAW. I expect both men to put on a match that might, just maybe, be headlining shows in the future.

The Bailey Boys vs. Morgan & Benning vs. Antonio & Raphael vs. The Canadian Blondes – Rip says: This match will be special – I’ve got something up my sleeve to make sure of that...

Steve Flash vs. Hugh de Aske – Rip says: Flash is a legend in this business, and it’s always good to have someone like him around. He leads by example, and is in tremendous shape for a man in his mid-forties. People are comparing the Dread Pirate to Flash, and while I think he has a way to go, at the same time he’s a tremendous talent. This one could steal the show.

“Bulldozer” Brandon Smith vs. Firebird – Rip says: This is where I anticipate the bad blood spilling over. Smith has taken exception to Firebird’s recent attitude shift, and while he’ll fight to the death to protect Firebird’s right to choose the path his life will follow, he’ll also fight to the death to get him to change back. Firebird, on the other hand, is looking for more recruits, and turning the Bulldozer to his side would be a hell of a coup.

“Machine” Jean Cattley vs. Casey Valentine – Rip says: With control of The Firm at stake, expect this to be a furious battle. Valentine has a lot to fight for – his whole future with the company may be at stake. Jean, on the other hand, will be looking to stamp his authority on the next generation of stars. The deciding factor could be the way loyalties lie between the other members of The Firm – just whose side will Jared Johnson and Primus Allen choose, when the chips are down?

Aaron Andrews vs. Jay Chord – Rip says: Yeah, you’re not expecting a neutral opinion here, are ya? My boy’s going to walk away from this match with his title intact – no question.
