MAW: 10 Simple Rules Month

June 2012, after South East Madness

in-game June 2012: the narrator's news of Mexican Hardcore Wrestling and of SWF's pay-per-view and the card for Summer Showdown; the saved copy carries no posting dates

*

I don’t normally focus on events south of the border, but today’s main page of TEW.com was focused almost exclusively on event in Mexican Hardcore Wrestling.

Their weekly Acontecimiento Principal show was a barn-stormer last night, with Axxis Jr. retaining his World Heavyweight crown over Vicente Romero in an A-rated main event. Elsewhere, Soul Taker, Nicolas Lopez, Axxis Jr, El Leon and Ref Benn are all expected to ink contract extensions in the coming days.

MHW have been doing well over the last year. Their popularity has gradually been rising throughout Mexico, and they’ve gained exposure in Hawaii and the South West as well.

*

SWF finally returned to the USA for their pay-per-view at the third attempt after a woeful effort in Japan and a good-for-a-regional-fed showing in the UK. Times of Trouble featured some killer angles, generally decent matches and a barnstorming semi-final between Marc DuBois and Christian Faith that has to be a contender for match of the year. The main event, between Steve Frehley and Brandon James, was a decent effort as well. SWF’s main issue at the moment (apart from being at Cult) is that the relentless see-sawing in size has killed their prestige. If we could get to Cult anytime soon we could take their workers and run opposite them as we have a greater standing in the community than them – albeit that we’re virtually unknown to the public outside of our three home regions.
