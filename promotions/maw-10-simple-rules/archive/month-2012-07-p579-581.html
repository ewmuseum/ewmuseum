MAW: 10 Simple Rules Month

July 2012, after Hotter Than Hot

in-game July 2012: news of SWF's signings, the narrator's drink with Rip Chord over the loss of KC Glenn, their plan against Richard Eisen and the card for Great Lakes Grand Parade; the saved copy carries no posting dates
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

*

A busy day for SWF as they rise back up to Global status. John Greed and Eric Eisen sign up with the company – but Alex and Ricky DeColt stay with CGC.

As if losing Greed – Father Johan for those of you who didn’t recognise him – wasn’t bad enough, one of our finest young stars has up and signed with Supreme as well...

*

“Kid?”

“Come in, Rip,” I said. The half-pint of vodka sat on my desk, a liberal slug contained within the glass held in my hand.

“Not a good way of handling things,” he said, screwing the cap back on the bottle.

“Give me a break, Rip. We’ve got two shows to get the best out of KC Glenn. Two shows. ---- it, Rip. I wanted the kid here for years.”

Rip sat down on the other side of the desk, casting another glance at the bottle.

“That’s not how it works, kid. We build them up, and then they get cherry picked by the bigs.”

I paused. Rip's metaphors were something I'd never got used to, even after all this time. “If we could sign a deal with Eisen...” I tailed off. “It’d never work, would it?”

“Eisen’s scared, kid. He can’t keep that company of his stable, and if that punk Callum hadn’t screwed us over then we’d be even closer on his tail then we already are. Another year or so-“

“----,” I said. Rip snorted in amusement.

“I’m going to make him pay,” I said.

Rip raised an eyebrow.

“Is this the bit where I crack your cane over your head?” he asked.

“This isn’t personal, Rip. This is professional. He’s been pushing people around for far, far too long.”

“That’s not going to change, kid.”

“It is. He’s getting worse and worse with age. I’m going to break him in two. We’re going to break him in two, Rip, if you’re with me.”

“Taking down Eisen? Sure, kid, I’m in. One condition, though.”

“Name it.”

“We’re not going to define ourselves by Eisen, okay? I’m happy to put him out of business and take what’s left for ourselves. But we can’t do that now, and won’t be able to for years to come. If and when that day arrives, though...”

Rip grinned ferally.

“...we can break him in two.”
