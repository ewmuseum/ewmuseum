MAW: 10 Simple Rules Month

November 2011, after Eleventh Hour

in-game mid-November 2011: the locker room with the new signings, the extra thirty minutes for the main shows, the 17th November tour show, the SWF's raid on the roster and the meeting at Rip Chord's house; the saved copy carries no posting dates

I looked around the locker room, and frowned. It wasn’t as full as it had been – the eleven released workers had been replaced by four, with a fifth still considering our offer.

But the eleven let go hadn’t expected to be on the main shows. The new guys, it was fair to say, did. And there simply wasn’t room.

I turned and limped down the corridor to try and find Rip. He was in his office, playing Freecell on his computer, having recently switched from Minesweeper after he got stuck on the intermediate level.

“Rip, we need more time,” I said.

“Ten goes there... What?”

“Time, Rip. We need to go another thirty minutes on the main shows.”

He raised an eyebrow at me.

“Why?”

“Because of the new guys, and having an extra title on the main show – at least for the time being.”

He thought about it, and then nodded.

“Okay. Make sure the underneath guys keep getting their share of time.”

“I will,” I said. “And this should improve the show overall – the additional quality from the extra matches should offset the loss of interest from having the lower guys on.”

“You sold me on it already, kid,” Rip said, with a half-smile. “I know it’ll cost money, but maybe you’re right – maybe it’ll help everyone without costing show quality.”

“Good. I’ll get the posters and website updated,” I said.

Rip nodded, and went back to his game. After so much drama in the last couple of years, it felt odd to be able to decide company policy in a break between games again. Odd, but nice.

Richard had been busy. It was Sunday night, and we had a show scheduled for the following night.

In the last week or so, the SWF had re-signed Liberty and snapped up Troy Tornado, as well as recalling Kurt Laramee from a short stay in RIPW.

More damagingly to us, three of our brightest stars would be leaving to join them.

We were gathered in Rip's house. I'd been there occasionally, mainly when Rip had wanted to show off one toy or another. This time, he was using it to make sure that he was on home turf. Not that he wanted to fight with the departing trio – he just wanted to make it clear where their loyalties lay right up until the time they left the Mid-Atlantic.

"We just wanted to thank you for your efforts here," Rip said, with a wide smile at the three men. "And to make sure you understood how tomorrow night is going to go."

"You're going to put your opponents over to the best of your abilities," I said, making it as clear as crystal. "You're leaving with our thanks, with our best wishes for the future, our absolute belief that you'll make it... and our determination that in leaving us three names short of a roster you'll do the right thing on the way out."

All three men nodded. They understood. We chatted for a while about the matches they'd take part in the next day before they filed out with orders to get a full night's sleep and be in peak condition.

"What do you think?" I asked Rip.

"Well, the Eisens are back on top," he said with a shrug. "I'm glad you convinced me to stop chasing after them. God only knows what would have happened if I still felt that way. Anyway, who have you got lined up to replace them?"

"No-one," I said.

"No-one?"

"That's right. I didn't have any particular plans for any of them, they can all be replaced... It's fine."

Rip raised an eyebrow.

"They're all big names. It diminishes our star power."

"Not by that much," I said. "They're not our biggest names – not by a long chalk."

"Well, I hope you know what you're doing, kid." Rip said.

I nodded. "We've already agreed that it's time to move folks up. This just gives us the impetus we need. Besides, we're losing three or four guys, and we've got three who haven't debuted yet. It seems like the perfect opportunity." That, and the paucity of viable big name candidates out there on the independents these days. We'd got lucky snapping up Randy Bumfhole, for example. We wouldn't be as lucky again.

"Three or four?"

I frowned. "You didn't hear? There was another approach earlier today. He hasn’t made his mind up yet, but it's one that might need a bit more thought."

Rip scowled.

"Just get me in a room with Eisen," he began, and then shook his head. "Any word on Jay?"

"Richard seems to be avoiding TCW guys – which means that Aaron might be safe. But then, Randy got approached, so I guess there's still no love lost between them. I suspect that Jay will go to Japan in a couple of months. I wouldn't plan on him being around here again any time soon, Rip."

"Yeah, I know," Rip sighed. "One day, huh?"

"Yeah," I agreed. Why not? "First, though, this special show..."
