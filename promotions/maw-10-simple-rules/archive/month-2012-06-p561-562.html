“More rumours, Aaron? What the hell is wrong with you?”

I glared across the desk at the Heavyweight champion, who at least had the grace to act abashed.

“Look, it’s just fun.”

“Greg didn’t think so. Have you ever considered picking on someone... interesting?”

Andrews shrugged, and I sighed.

“Look. I’ve warned you, I’ve tried to be nice... This is persistent offending, Aaron. I don’t have a choice but to fine you.”

Aaron handed over the money and muttered something conciliatory on his way out. I’d find out later whether he apologies to Greg, which might make me have more faith in him. Otherwise, I was thinking seriously about how KC Glenn might look with the title around his waist. After all, GCG had faith in him: he was the new Openweight champion in that company with a victory over Mabuchi Furusawa.

*

If anyone’s wondering about the presence – or lack thereof – of the Internet Favourites of late, well, Max Mayhem’s contract ran down about two months ago without my noticing. I had noticed that Hugh Lee’s contract was up, and he was re-signed to a three month deal – but without Mayhem around I don’t have a lot of use for him. Truth be told, he’s probably the worst wrestler under contract, and I don’t really have enough space on my shows for the good wrestlers.

We’ve snapped up a couple of young guys to take the place of the IF, so we’ll have to see how that goes. It was a shame to see them go, as the gimmick was fun and backstage they were good guys... But it just never clicked for them. Hugh’s average match rating was an E+, and his high was a C- against Hugh de Aske. Mayhem had an E average, and a single D+ from a tag match against JD Morgan and Providence during their Fallen days.
