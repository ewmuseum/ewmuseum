Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

MAW: 10 Simple Rules is one author's diary of Mid Atlantic Wrestling, a small CornellVerse company that runs a monthly card at Stanley Hall in North Carolina, kept in the Total Extreme Wrestling world from January 2008 and posted on the Grey Dog Software forums. It is first-person fiction with the show reports set into it. Its narrator is a former SWF midcarder whose career ended when his best friend Rip Chord, drunk, tore his knee; years later Rip, now sober and MAW's owner, talks him back into the business as road agent and booker. The ten rules of the title hang on the locker-room door, with an eleventh scrawled beneath by a joker.

Each show is a card of matches with a letter grade for every one, an "Overall" grade and the narrator's remarks, with scenes between them in the locker room and the office and notes on the month's profit. The first ten shows, January to October 2008, build a roster of young workers around Erik Strong's heavyweight title: Antonio wins the Rip Chord Invitational, Jean Cattley and his Firm beat down everyone who resists, the Masked Patriot feuds with Citizen X, and Perez and Shaffer take the tag belts from the Canadian Blondes in a ladder match. In August a month of small shows tests two new regions.

Citizen X and Frankie Perez leave in September for bigger companies and are replaced by Jacob Jett and Stevie Grayson. At Where It All Begins Again in October Rip Chord wins a battle royal, a countdown of 85 days appears on the screens, and Jean Cattley wins his second heavyweight title in a three-way. The second chapter opens with Rip's confession that he has a son, and the range ends on the card for Remember November.

The diary's first ten shows are served here whole, as 22 pages (the months and the shows), with the roster, teams, titles and reigns they establish. The saved copy, 1,608 pages in all, is a compiled edition of the diary; the rest is collected and not yet served.
