[Rumahatat starts to play over the PA speakers. Outcast Rob Cole comes walking out with a medium sized sack. The crowd responds giving him a standing Ovation. Here is OUTCAST ROB COLEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!! Crowd: Yeahhhhh, Yeahhh, Outcast, Outcast!! [Fucking Hostile begins to play at a deafing sound. Satar comes walking out with the belt around his shoulder. He stands on the walk way and looks around him, the crowd starts to boo him and he replys with a simple laugh. He walks down to the ring.] Announcer: Making his way to the ring from Salt Lake City, Utah. Coming in at 6'10" 385 lbs, Here is the ILOC World Champion, SATARRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR!!!!!!!!!!!! Crowd: Booooooooo, go home you punk! You suck! [Satar stands on the turnbuckle and looks at the crowd and raises his arms in the air and yells.] Timmons: I wonder what's in that sack that Outcast has. Clark: It's probably a body bag for Satar. Timmons: Come on Clark, outcast is good but not that good. Clark: We'll see when the time comes. Timmons: Besides I've been hearing alot of rumors that this match is going to get bloody. Clark: Ohh yeah, there going to beat the crap out of each other. Timmons: No No you idiot. A lot of outside interference. Clark: Oh! Well anybody that wants to get in the ring with these two guys, which is also a title bout I might add, must be insane! Timmons: You have a point Clark, I wouldn't get in there. Clark: Let's get down to ringside for the start of our match. [Outcast Rob Cole calls John Neidrick over. He starts to tell him something.] Timmons: I wonder what he is saying? Clark: He's probably saying something like "Alright after I win I'll give you your share of the money", HAHAHA just kidding. Timmons: Hmm intersting. I think he is saying "Don't let Satar hurt me to bad!!". Clark: Yeah Right! [Neidrick walks over to Satar and starts to talk to him and starts to search him.] Clark: That Outsmarted kid is smarter than I thought, he is having the ref check Satar for any weapons. Timmons: Know something. Clark: What. Timmons: If Satar wanted to he could get Neidrick fired for offensive touching. Clark: What!? Timmons: Look how is searching him. Clark: Timmons just shut up, damn weirdo. [Neidrick searches around and finds a pair of brass knuckles on Satar. Satar starts to make execuses up about why they were there.] Clark: Ohhhhh! Look at that. Caught red handed this time pal. Old Brass knuckles was going to try and cheat Outcast out of the title like he did Dragon Lord. Timmons: First off there has to be a logical explantion for the brass knuckles to be there. Clark: Maybe he put there and he planned to smash Outcast's face in, hmmm that sounds logical enough to me. Timmons: Oh yeah, well what's in the bag down there in Outcast's corner. [Heidrick gives the brass knuckles to the guard and calls Michael Buffer up to the ring.] Clark: What does this guy have, that I don't. Coming in here and introducing the wrestlers. Timmons: He has let's see... A life, money, women, a voice, a career, and humor. Clark: Thanks alot Timmons. Michael Buffer: This match is for the ILOC World Championship Title Belt! Introducing First the Challanger, "Outcast Rob Coleeeeeeee!". Introducing out of the red corner and current Champion Satarrrrrrrrrr!. Ladies and Gentlemen LET'S GET READY TO RUMBLEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!! [the crowd starts to go nuts.] Timmons: Alright get the hell out of the ring Mike and let these two guys wrestle. [Satar takes his belt off and shows it to the crowd. Outcast Rob Cole finds a window of oppertunity. Outcast Rob Cole attacks SATAR before the bell. Clark: There we go, work him, work him. Timmons: I expected this to happen. Satar wasn't paying close attention to a guy who wants the belt real bad and who is real dangerous. Outcast Rob Cole executes a flying shoulderblock. Outcast Rob Cole goes for a kneedrop, but SATAR counters it with a roll away. SATAR whips Outcast Rob Cole into the ropes. SATAR executes a clothesline. Timmons: There goes his head up into the 15 row, hehehehe Clark: Oww, trying to be the funny guy huh. It was pretty funny watching Outcast attack Satar before the bell. SATAR takes him down with a fallaway slam. SATAR goes for the Doctor Bomb, but Outcast Rob Cole counters it with a backdrop In turn, SATAR counters it with a sunset flip. Timmons: A beautiful reversal by the champ. Clark: You must worship this guy don't you. Timmons: No more than you with Robski. Clark: Ahh, I respect the man for who and what he is. He is an execllent wrestler. Timmons: Well it's the same here. John Neidrick counts: One, two, kickout. SATAR executes a short clothesline. SATAR nails him with a bodyslam. SATAR is going for the pin. John Neidrick counts: One, two, shoulder up. SATAR goes for a forearm smash, but Outcast Rob Cole blocks it. Outcast Rob Cole runs into the ropes. Outcast Rob Cole misses with a kick. Outcast Rob Cole hits SATAR with an elbow. Outcast Rob Cole goes for a kick to the midsection, but SATAR blocks it. SATAR runs into the ropes. SATAR and Outcast Rob Cole get hit with a double clothesline. SATAR takes him down with a punch. SATAR nails him with a DDT. Timmons: Hey! Do you hear that. Clark: Here what? Timmons: That hollow noise from Outcast's head.HAHAHA. SATAR goes for a bodyslam, but Outcast Rob Cole counters it with a small package . John Neidrick counts: One, two, kickout. Outcast Rob Cole executes a clothesline. Clark: Look Timmons, there goes Satar's head into the 15th row. Outcast Rob Cole runs into the ropes. SATAR and Outcast Rob Cole get hit with a double clothesline. SATAR nails him with a short clothesline. SATAR goes for a roundhouse right, but Outcast Rob Cole blocks it. Outcast Rob Cole goes for a clothesline, but SATAR counters it with a duck-down move. SATAR catches him in a power bomb. Timmons: Outcast hit the mat like a ton of bricks. John Neidrick counts: One, two, shoulder up. SATAR goes for a belly-to-back suplex, but Outcast Rob Cole counters it with an elbowsmash. Outcast Rob Cole goes for a facebite, but SATAR counters it with a kneelift. Clark: Oh boy, that will take the wind out of you. Timmons: Not to mention it would mess your guts up. Clark: Did I ask you for your opinon nimrod, No! Timmons: Geez. Chill out. Outcast Rob Cole takes him down with a savate kick. Outcast Rob Cole whips SATAR into the ropes, but SATAR reverses it. SATAR goes for a clawhold, but Outcast Rob Cole blocks it. Outcast Rob Cole runs into the ropes. Outcast Rob Cole takes him down with a punch. Outcast Rob Cole nails him with an elbowsmash. Outcast Rob Cole takes him down with a dropkick. Outcast Rob Cole whips SATAR into the turnbuckle. Outcast Rob Cole nails him with a short clothesline. [Satar is laying on the mat staggering to his feet. Outcast Rob Cole looks to the crowd and starts to go nuts, he is pumped up.] Timmons: Stupid Stupid. He should be trying to pin him now but he's young and inexprienced.He'll learn in due time. Clark: Yeah, he could of had him but he is wasting to much time. [Outcast grabs Satar by his head and picks him up. he puts him into the Suplex Powerbomb position.] Clark: He's going for the Suplex Powerbomb. This could be it, there might be a new champion. Timmons: If Satar doesn't counter this with something he is going to be planted about 6 feet under. Outcast Rob Cole executes the Suplex Powerbomb and goes for the pin. [The crowd is on there feet trying to see if Outcast is going to get the pin.] Clark: YES!! YES!! Way to go Outcast. Timmons:All no, damn it Satar. John Neidrick counts: One, two, three. Clark: Yes! Outcast Rob Cole is new champion!! HAHAHA! [Outcast is going crazy, he is jumping around and yelling and the crowd is loving it. Satar crawls back into the corner and snaps his fingers. 6 hooded figures come jumping from the audience and onto the mat. Outcast picks one up and piledrives him, he gives another a punch but the other four grab him and give him a double DDT. They continue to stomp on him while is on the ground.] Timmons: There is mass confusion down there in the ring, Satar and his minions are beating Outcast to a bloody pulp. Clark: Satar makes me sick, he is such a sore loser. [Suddenly out of no where somebody punches through the mat and comes crawling out.] Timmons: I can't believe my eyes. This is something out of a horror movie. Clark: Yeah, It's Wednesday the 19th, The sequal. [Retribution grabs one of the minoins under and starts to pulvirize him. Clark: Hey. Timmons: What Clark: When Retribution pulled that guy under it reminded me of this movie I saw called C.H.U.D. [The Crowd starts to go crazy, Mr. America comes running down to the aisle. He gets in the ring and backdrops one of the Minions out of the ring, he gets down onto the concrete picks the minion back up and gives a Superplex,the minion is out cold. The Crowd is jumping around, throwing crap at the ring, they are going crazy.] Timmons: I wonder how this all started. Clark: Brass Knuckles called his hethians on Outcast because he lost. Timmons: No No. I mean why is Mr. America and Retribution down here also. Clark: I don't know. [King Tonka comes running down the aisle. He gets into the ring and grabs two minions and gives them a double head butt. he grabs one guy and gives them a piledrives and throws him out of the ring.] Timmons: Alright all we need now is a ref and we can have ourselves a Battle Royal now. Clark: Yeah really. [Satar runs up behind King Tonka and kicks him in the back of his knee. He then picks him up and gives him a powerbomb, he continues to pound on him with a seris of stomps and elbow drops. Mr. America walks over to Outcast and starts to pick him up and gives him a short clothes line. Retribution picks him up and they give Outcast a double backdrop.] Timmons: WHAT! I though he came down here to help him out not to beat him up. Clark: At last, Mr.America's true colors come out. I always knew he was a snake in the grass. [Mr. America and Retribution run over to Satar and give him a double clothesline. Two of Satar's minions give them a facebite. Outcast comes from behind and gives him a knee to the back. King Tonka is starting to get up when one of Satar's minions comes running over kicking him in the face. Mr. America grabs the minion and power bombs hard enough to send him to the center of the earth.] Timmons: Welcome everybody to the ILOC special Battle Royal, and with me is my co-host Clark. Clark: Thank you Timmons. So far there has been 4 of the six minions rendered immobilize. Satar,King Tonka,Mr.America,Retribution,and Outcast are still standing though. Timmons: I am putting my money on Mr. America and Retribution to win it all. [Retribution whips King Tonka into the ropes and goes for a power slam but King Tonka counters with a knee lift. Tonka grabs Retribution and throws him out of the ring. King Tonka goes through the ropes. King Tonka starts to slam Retribution's face into the Ring post and guardrail. The Crowd is terrified but enjoying the action. Mr.America comes from behind with the chair and tags King Tonka with it. Mr. America picks up the Ringsteps and drops them on King Tonka. Tonka is out cold. Timmons: That is one more gone, Retribution,Mr.America,Outcast,and Satar are still able to move. Clark: I think that King Tonka might need to have some reconstructive surgey done to his face, maybe get a couple of caps on his teeth. What the hell am I saying, he always needed that. [Two of Satars minions grab Outcast and hold him up. Satar starts to kick and punch Outcast repaidly. Mr. America comes out of no where and gives Satar a flying clothesline. Retribution picks up both of the Minons and gives them a choke slam. Retribution picks one of the Minions up and power bombs him down to the concrete. He grabs the other and gives him a massive Atomic Drop. The Minion squeals in pain.] Timmons: Alright it's just Mr.America,Retribution,Satar,and Outcast in the ring. Clark: Who's that. Timmons: Where! Clark: Right over there. Timmons: Oh man, not another one. [A large mysterious man comes running down to ringside and grabs the ILOC World Championship belt and takes off running. Nobody really noticed him except for Retribution.] Timmons: Oh my God!! He took the belt away from Outcast. He just came down there and took the belt. Clark: Who was that guy? I have never saw him before? Timmons: I don't know Clark. I'm just as confused as you are. [The ILOC Guards and the local police come down to ringside. Everybody in the ring sees them and starts to run. Mr. America and Retribution hobble away. Satar staggers away. Outcast is trying to but can't realy move. The Minions continue to lay there. The guards come down there and pulls the ringsteps off of King Tonka. A pool of blood is laying there. A couple of medics come down and take him away on a strecther. They call a couple more strecthers down for some of the minions. Some of the people turn there head and start to puke. They get in the ring and check on Outcast. They call a strecther down but he hops up and pushes the strecther away.] Timmons: Look at this place. Clark: I could send this show to Hollywood and they can make a war movie out of it, the many puddles of blood and that big hole in the middle of the ring. [Rumahatat begins to play. Outcast with the help of some guards walk's back on his own power. The crowd is roaring for him.] Timmons: He took all that punishment and fought all that way and he has nothing to show for it. I guess to see who he is. Timmons: Let's recap tonight. Dragon Lord beat the Kamikaze Kid, King Tonka beat Robski after a little help from the Crow. Clark: Don't worry he is going to get his.
