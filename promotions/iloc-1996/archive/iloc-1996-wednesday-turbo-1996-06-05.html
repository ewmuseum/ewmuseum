(The scene opens up with dark stormy clouds. Vague images of the ILOC wrestlers are seen doing their moves. Lightning bolts zap at the mat with a brilliant flash. As the flash dissipates the symbol ILOC is the only thing left to see.The camera floats thru the ILOC symbol and pans out to a blimp view of the outside of McNichols Arena in Denver, Colorado. A second camera shows a jam packed crowd inside the arena. The camera slowly circles around to the commentator's booth where Timmons and Clark are sitting with champagne and a nice spread of food.) Timmons: Welcome ladies and gentlemen to ILOC's Wednesday Nite Turbo. Clark: Yes, welcome to Denver, Colorado. Timmons: Last week we got to see the great SATAR win the Championship. Tonight we find out who will meet the champ for his belt. Clark: It dosen't matter really. All three contenders tonight can whip Satar's butt. Timmons: I wouldn't say that too loud Clark. You never know who could be lurking around here. Clark: I'm not afraid of any one around here. Hell i could beat any of one of these guys. Timmons: Yeah! Right Clarkie. Let's get down to ringside. (A steady bass beat is blaring out the speakers. The whole building is shaking from the heavy bass.) Announcer: Introducing from L.A. Californ..I...A, weighing 315 lbs.With his manager Amplifier. Here is Clarion..Bose.Kenwoooooooood. (As Kenwood and Amplifier come down the ramp. The fans are cheering and trying to get a handshake. Christ, Kenwood can really handle himself and Big Russ is giving away 40 or 50 lbs. Clark: Hey! The match is starting. Let's get down there. Big Russ nails him with a chokehold. John Neidrick counts: One, two, three. John Neidrick counts: One, two, three, four. Big Russ nails him with a flying axhandle. Big Russ takes him down with a palm blow. Big Russ executes a low blow. Big Russ takes him down with a chop. Big Russ whips Clarion Bose Kenwood into the ropes. Clarion Bose Kenwood misses with an elbow. Clarion Bose Kenwood hits Big Russ with an elbow. Both Amplifier and Lucky Eddy jump up on the apron. John Neidrick tries to send them back down. Timmons: He's a pretty good wrestler Clark. I think he might have the flu that's going around. Clark: Yeah Timmons, just keep makin' excuses. Timmons: You know Clark, with all those mouse traps coming out. I would have to say the fans here like the Avalanche. Clark: (looking through his notes) Ahhh! I don't think i know this guy Avalanche. Timmons: Oh God! Let's get to our next match. (Misery Machine's volume is slowing building intensity through the speakers of the arena. Colored lights flash to the beat.) Announcer: From Little Rock weighing in at 457 lbs. One of ILOC's biggest. Here'sssss The Refridgerator Raaaaderrrrrrrr! (Rader walks out to fan's booing him. Some are heckling him.Rader is throwing his hand in the air at them. He is also shouting at them. Now he's flexing in front of them and laughing at the fans.)
