An announced but unconsummated October 1997 rebrand by a departing operator of a different, prior fed, its own name never followed by an independent card, roster or title. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

IAFW announced itself in a single post to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 28 October 1997, headed “a new fed, an old dream.” Its own founder introduces the project as a break from where he had just come: “The NEFW recently lost me. I quit. Now I am on a revenge streak. Our goal is to bring back what I had in the old fed, but better.” He adds, tersely, “No fancy catch phrase, no begging and pleading. You know who I am, you know where to reach me.”

The post names the fed in full — the International Alligned Federation of Wrestling — and states nothing further about it: no roster, no card, no title, no venue. Nothing in its own founding post claims real wrestlers, so its roster is presumed fantasy rather than attested either way.

No second document survives under the IAFW's own name. What the record does carry, two days later, is its founder's own resurfacing — not with a new IAFW card, but back in charge of the NEFW itself, the fed he had just quit, alongside an unconfirmed community rumor that it might take the IAFW's own name instead. Whether the IAFW ever ran a show of its own isn't in the surviving record; the trail stops with that rumor, never resolved either way.
