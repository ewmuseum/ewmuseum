The Cyber Wrestling Union announced its own reopening on 24 February 2001, describing itself as newly returned “with a great staff” and looking for “talented handlers” to join, run from its own site on Tripod.

The post frames the reopened promotion as an active, hands-on operation, promising members that CWU was “dedicated and usually update our site daily.”

No founding date for CWU’s earlier, unattested run survives, and no card, roster, or further notice records what became of the promotion after this reopening; how CWU ended is not in the surviving record.
