Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Extreme World Wrestling Alliance dates its own founding to an inaugural card on 29 November 1999; its own handbook, posted 29 February 2000, is the fullest surviving account of a UK-based promotion.

By its own telling it grew fast: a first pay-per-view on 26 December 1999, a second weekly card added in January, a growing five-belt structure, and an interfederation pay-per-view co-hosted with the UWF booked for 9 March 2000, “When Feds Collide.”

Its handbook names five championships and their holders as of that February: a World Heavyweight Title held by Damien, an Intercontinental Title held by Scott Thunder, a TV Title “always hardcore rules” held by Matt Kraven, a Rookies Title open only to wrestlers with fewer than eight matches and first won by Dark Reaper on 27 February 2000, and Tag Team Titles held by The Disposers.

Two cards ran each week — Monday Night Mayhem, its main televised show, and a smaller Thursday Fight Night house show staged in UK arenas and generally reserved for newer talent — with a pay-per-view every two months and strategies due to its own booker two days ahead of any match.

No card, title change, or result survives past this handbook and the two recruiting posts that came before it. Whether the March pay-per-view with the UWF was ever run is not in the surviving record.
