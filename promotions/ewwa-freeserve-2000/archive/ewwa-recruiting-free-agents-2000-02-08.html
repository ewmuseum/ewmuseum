[EWWA] Recruiting Free Agents

8 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The EWWA has been running for a good couple of months now and we're looking for additions to our roster. We already have over
35 athletes and we're looking for more. We've just added a fifth belt (4 singles and tag) and the 2nd PPV is just around the corner.
We accept strats as well as RP's which are board based. Two cards a week, one large televised show with play by play results and a house show with summarised results and a 5 match limit. The main Monday show has around 7-8 televised matches and we're now having dark matches on top of that. We also have a pretty solid tag team division.
The pay per view will have all 4 singles titles on the line as well as a 13 man royal rumble with the winner getting a title shot. You can still be a part of the action if you apply quickly.
No overcomplicated rules, just explains stipulations for the titles and things like that. Anyone is accepted.
Look at www.thomson2273.freeserve.co.uk/ewwaindex.html and fill in an application form. Ignore the sample strategy bit, we don't really need that any more. Even if you don't decide to join, have a look at some past results and tell me what you think of the cards and where I can improve.
