EWWA Handbook www.thomson2273.freeserve.co.uk/ewwaindex.html contact: ew...@[detail withheld].freeserve.co.uk ******* Contents: 1) About the EWWA 2) People of the EWWA 3) Cards of the EWWA 4) Titles of the EWWA 5) Strats and RP's 6) Technicalities/Rules 7) The Website 8) In closing ******* 1) About the EWWA The Extreme World Wrestling Alliance (EWWA) has been running since 29/11/99 where we held our inaugural card. We had our first pay per view on 26/12/99 and have expanded to a second card in January. The second PPV happened just this
Sunday and we're holding an interfed PPV with the UWF on 9/3/00. 2) People of the EWWA [name withheld]: The head man. Controls everything. If you get on his wrong side expect trouble. Books all the matches.
Referees: Glenn Law (head official), Jim Townsend, Alan Jacobs, Bill Wight. Not a huge factor. They're not afraid to DQ anyone but are quite willing to give a little leeway and turn blind eyes to things.
Announcers, commentators, general dogsbodies: Larry Sadowitz, James Brown: Larry is a former heavyweight champion and the head commentator. JB used to just do interviews but since Lisa Bailey, former commentator took up wrestling full time he's been promoted.
Enforcer: Q4. When a match threatens to get ugly and there could be lots of interference, he'll come down and stop trouble before it starts. Hugely powerful man, has been known to special guest referee from time to time.
3) Cards of the EWWA Monday Night Mayhem: The main card. Usually 8 televised matches with about 5 dark matches. Lots of title defenses, if I remember rightly there's only been 1 MNM where no title has been on the line. Extended match summaries are written up for this card. It used to be reported play by play but matches became crappy and the new format makes matches better. The commentators will comment on things between matches.
Thursday Fight Night: A house show held in various small arenas in the UK. 5 match card limit with one or two exceptions. Generally reserved for newer talent and tournament but does host the odd title match.
PPVs: We hold a pay per view event every 2 months on the last Sunday of the month. The next regular
PPV will be on April 30th-May 1st called Weekend of Wrestling. The next PPV full stop will be on
March 9th, When Feds Collide, co-hosted by the UWF. The pay per views will have the same if not more matches than MNM and usually have as many dark matches which are summarised.
Note all cards are hand written. 4) Titles of the EWWA EWWA World Heavyweight Title: aka World, Heavyweight or just plain EWWA Title. The big cahuna. What you are in the EWWA to win. Only the best get this far. Current holder: Damien EWWA Intercontinental Title: surprisingly more hotly contested than the World Title. Often a stepping stone to the top belt, usually held by competitors just off main event status. Current holder: Scott Thunder
EWWA TV Title: always hardcore rules. Can be won quite early in your career and is notoriously difficult to defend, with only two holders having been able to defend the belt.
Current holder: Matt Kraven EWWA Rookies Title: only available to wrestlers with less than 8 matches under their belt as stated on the roster page. More than likely the first belt you'll get a chance to pick up, this belt was added to the
EWWA only recently and the first winner was crowned on 27/2/00. Current holder: Dark Reaper EWWA Tag Team Titles: the tag team belts. We've got a few tag teams contending the belt, tag team glory is not necessarily a step to singles but it can happen. Current
holders: The Disposers Notes: Only one singles belt can be held at any one time. You are automatically entitled to a rematch if you lose the belt. If you are suspended and hold a belt you keep it unless you are suspended during a
PPV event then you lose the belt. Suspensions are usually only handed out in extreme circumstances.
5) Strats and RPs It's recommended that you do both. If you don't do either and your opponent does one or you do one and your opponent does both you will lose. Strategies can be as long or as short as you want, generally they should include things like moves to try, any cheating you want to do, things like that. These should be e-mailed to me 2 days before a match. Roleplays should have some content but you don't need to go over the top. The RP board is password protected, the password will be mailed out to you when you join. The RP board will also be used for me to post things such as notices of card changes and news and rumours.
6) Technicalities/Rules Maximum of 3 wrestlers per handler. If you are inactive for too long you will be fired, killed or whatever
I choose. Only fake wrestlers allowed. You can request matches, ask to interfere in matches and suggest possible angles. I reserve the right not to use any suggestions you have if they're too silly. There is to be no
OOC remarks on the RP board, any thing like that will be deleted. If you lose, you lose. I'll consider rematch requests as I would any normal match. Only 1 rematch though. There are more things specific to
RP's on the board. 7) The Website Is located at www.thomson2273.freeserve.co.uk/ewwaindex.html I update it as often as possible or required. Only one card so far has been posted late out of 24 because of technical difficulties. The site includes the current roster, cards and archived results, title histories and current champions, the rules and a link to the RP board. It's a simple but functional design.
8) In closing That's all the basic details, if you have any questions look round the site and through some past cards, or give me an e-mail. The application can be found on the website (click Join).
