Houston, TX, August 12, (cW Newswire) – Today around 2:35 p.m. Core Wrestling Co-Owner, [name withheld], announced the hiring of a Chief Executive of Federation Affairs to help lead Core Wrestling into the big time. "We're pleased to have [name withheld] as a part of the Core Wrestling staff now," [name withheld] commented, "He will most definitely be a huge asset to Core Wrestling." Early in the morning, a Federal Court jury found [name withheld] not guilty on the charges of extortion. About a month ago, a bank [name withheld] use to work for helped the Grand Jury find enough evidence to place [name withheld] under arrest for charges if extorting money from the North Houston Bank. "I knew I was innocent, but now it's time to get back to work," [name withheld] stated. [name withheld] was released from the Federal Prison and has been back to working in cW Towers. "I got my money from my dad," [name withheld] finally said, "He died and I used the money he left me to continue his dream about a wrestling corporation." Upon his release and his return to cW Towers, [name withheld] processed all the paperwork to hire the new executive. After the new executive was moved into his office on the floor below [name withheld]'s office, [name withheld] began looking at the roster of Core Wrestling and started making some cuts. "I cut a lot of people from the cW payroll today for the simple fact that we are making room for development," [name withheld] said. Core Wrestling officials hope to bring in more talent in the upcoming days and weeks.
