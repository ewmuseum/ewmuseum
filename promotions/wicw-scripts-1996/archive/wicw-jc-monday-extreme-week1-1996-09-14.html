WICW (6 Sep 1996) — Card

"WICW Monday Extreme Week 1"

14 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
 Hello, Everyone I'm Jim Ross at the WICW First Ever card, MONDAY Extreme. Along with me is my Partner, Bobby "The Brain" Hennan. Tonight we will have 5 Triangle Matches for the Vacant WICW Belts and 1 Match for The
WICW Tag Team Titles. Let's go down to Ring Side for Matches
Howard Finkel: " Welcome Everyone to The First Ever WICW Card,
Our First match is scheduled for 1 Fall and it's a Elimination Style
Triangle Match for the WICW TV Title.
The participants in this match are Vader, The Ultimate Warrior and Stone Cold Steve Austin.
The Ultimate Warrior Defeats Stone Cold Steve Austin with a Sleeperhold at 24:15 Vader Pins the Ultimate Warrior with a Power Bomb at 27:01 Vader wins The WICW TV Title Howard Finkel: Our Next Match is Scheduled for 1 Fall Eliminaton Style
Triangle Match For The WICW Intercontinental Title,
The Participants in this Match are Bret Hart, Sid and Mr. Perfect. Sid Beat Mr. Perfect By DQ at 6:35 Bret Hart Pinned Sid with a enzuigiri at 10:25 Bret Hart Wins The WICW IC Title Howard Finkel: Our Next Match is Scheduled for 1 Fall Eliminaton Style
Triangle Match For The WICW World Crusier Weight Title,
The Participants in this Match are Sabu, Chris Benoit and Dean Malenko
Chris Benoit Pinned Dean Malenko with the Dragon Suplex at 29:50
Sabu Pinned Chris Benoit with the Moonsault at 29:59 Sabu Won The WICW World Crusierweight Title Howard Finkel: Our Next Match is Scheduled for 1 Fall Eliminaton Style
Triangle Match For The WICW United States Title,
The Participants in this Match are Doink The Clown, Ric Flair and Hollywood Hulk Hogan. Doink The Clown Pinned Hollywood Hogan with a German Suplex at 11:14
Doink The Clown Pinned Ric Flair with a German Suplex at 13:09
Doink Won The US Title Howard Finkel: Our Next Match is Scheduled for 1 Fall It's The Match
For The WICW Tag Team Titles,
Coming down to the ring is the team of Sting and Lex Luger, with Missy
Hyatt. Now Coming down to the ring is The Team of Hawk and Animal The Road
Warriors The Road Warriors beat Sting/Luger after Hawk Pinned Sting with the
Doomsday Device at 7:31
Road Warriors are The WICW Tag Team Champs The Last Match of the Night is Scheduled For 1 Fall it is a Elimination style Triangle match for The WICW World Heavyweight Title.
The participants in this Match are Shawn Michaels, Undertaker and Macho Man Randy Savage. Undertaker pinned Macho Man with The Tombstone at 32:09 Shawn Michaels Pinned The Undertaker With a Superkick at 35:42 Shawn Michaels won the WICW World Title The Undertaker was injuried he will be out 3 Cards Jim Ross: This was an Exciting Night of WICW Wrestling right Bobby? Bobby Hennan: It sure was Jim, But This Fridayt on Fury will be Even Better with the 6 Man Tag Team Match Everyone wants to See,
Sting/Luger and The Hitman Bret Hart VS. The Road Warriors and Hunter Hearst Helmsley Jim Ross: That Will Be a Great Match but Don't Forget about Vader Defending his TV Title against The Ultimate Warrior, Well That's all for This Monday Extreme, Check out out on Friday Fury with Info on the First WICW Pay Per View Event.
Good Night Everyone, I'm Jim Ross and For my partner Bobby Hennan, See You on Friday
