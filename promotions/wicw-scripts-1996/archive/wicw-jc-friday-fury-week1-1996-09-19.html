WICW (6 Sep 1996) — Card

"WICW Friday Fury (Week 1)"

19 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jim Ross: "Hello everyone and welcome to WICW Friday Fury!!! This is
Jim Ross along with Bobby "The Brain" Heenan.
Heenan: "Can't you just feel the excitement JR?? This crowd can't wait any longer!" JR: "That's right Brain so let's go down to ringside with Howard
Finkel for our first match!"
Finkel: "The first match tonight is scheduled for one fall, introducing first, accompanied by Marlena...Goldust! And, his opponent...Chris Benoit with Madusa!
Bell Sounds JR: "and the first match is underway!" Referee counts..1-2-3 Finkel: "Here is your winner..Goldust!" Heenan: "Goldust with an impressive victory, just like I predicted!" JR: "Yeah, sure..anyway, now let's take it back down to ringside for our next match!" Finkel: "This next match is scheduled for one fall, and is for the US
Title! First, the challenger, from Stone Mountain, Georgia, Jake "The
Snake " Roberts! And, his opponent, the US Title holder, from the
Circus, accompanied by Dink, Doink the Clown!"
Bell Sounds JR: "This should be an exciting match for the US Title" Referee counts 1-2-3 Finkel: "Here is your winner, and NEW US Champion....Jake "The Snake" Roberts!!!" Heenan: "Doink was lucky to get that title in the first place! He didn't have it for long!"
JR: "That's right, he did pull off a major upset..now, to our third match" Finkel: "This next match is scheduled for one fall..first, coming down the aisle, accompanied by Missy Hyatt, Arn Anderson and Ric
Flair! And, their opponents, Stone Cold Steve Austin and Mr. Perfect!" Bell Sounds Heenan: "This should be no problem for Flair and Anderson!" Bell Sounds again Finkel: "This match has been declared a draw as a result of the time limit expiring!" JR: "What a great matchup..." Finkel: "The next match is scheduled for one fall, first coming down the aisle, accompanied by the Sister Sherri, King Kong Bundy! And, his opponent, the Superfly Jimmy Snuka!
Bell Sounds Heenan: "There's no way Superfly can beat this mammoth!" Superfly executes flying headbutt referee counts 1-2-3 Finkel: "Here is your winner...the Superfly Jimmy Snuka!" Heenan: "I can't believe it!" JR: "Show's how much you know!" Heenan: "Quiet JR!" Finkel: "The next match is for the TV Title! First, the challenger, the Ultimate Warrior! And his opponent, accompanied by Sister Sherri, the TV Title holder, the man they call Vader!"
Bell Sounds referee counts 1-2-3 Finkel: "The winner of this match, and still the TV Title holder, the man they call Vader!"
Heenan: "Vader sure made short work of that clown!" JR: "Now let's go back to ring side for the main event!" Finkel: "The final match of this evening is a 6 man tag match! Introducing first, the Intercontinental Champion...Bret "Hit Man" Hart! And his teammates, Lex Luger and Sting accompanied by their
Managers Capt. Lou Albano and Missy Hyatt! Their opponents, first...Hunter
Hearst Helmsley! And his teammates, the WICW Tag Team Champions... The Road Warriors!" Bell Sounds JR: "This should be a great one!" Bell Sounds Finkel: "This match has been declared a draw as a result of the time limit expiring!" JR: "Well, this certainly was an exciting night of wrestling!" Heenan: "That's right JR and I can hardly wait until next time for the rematch of the 6 man tag match and this time with no time limit!"
JR: "Yes, that should be an exciting battle to the finish, well, until next time I'm Jim Ross along with Bobby Heenan saying goodnight to all...."
