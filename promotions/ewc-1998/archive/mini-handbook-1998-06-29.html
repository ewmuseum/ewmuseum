Elite Wrestling Corporation — Founding rulebook

Mini Handbook V1.0

29 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, there and my name is Kevin Norman. For the past One and a Half Years in e-wrestling I've been very active. I've been roleplaying like crazy, writing strats like crazy, and winning matches like crazy. :) But, I said to myself when I started in this hobby of e-wrestling, after a amount of time, after analyzing different organzations I said it would be time for another of the ten million organzations out there to be born, and that is the Elite Wrestling Corporation. I'm not going to sit here and give you a huge long essay just to make you tired and you'll close the e-mail, I'm going to make this short and sweet. So, here we go. I. Rules Very simple. No outrageous characters, and no real wrestlers. This is a original wrestler fed people! Also, no attacking wrestlers without the permission from me. However, you can attack as much jobbers as you want but if you attack a wrestler currently in the EWC without my permission you will get a warning, then a one week suspension, and then a firing from the EWC. So don't push your luck. :) II. Roleplaying Once again simple. You can roleplay as much as you want throughout the week, but, keep this in mind, the roleplays must be at least 3K or 30 lines of text. If they are not, they will not be sent out or be given to you as a push for your next matchup. So if you are going to roleplay 12, 2K's, It's not going to work. Roleplays will be sent out daily in, [EWC] Elite Roleplays. III. Titles The Elite Wrestling Corporation sanctions four singles titles: - EWC Skinnyweight Championship (No. 1 Cruiserweight Title in the EWC. 245 lbs. weight limit. Defended on every Wreckage and at every Supercard/PPV.) - EWC Television Championship (No. 3 Title in the EWC. You must defend this every week on Wednesday Wreckage and at every PPV/Supercard.) - EWC Midwest Champion (No. 2 Title in the EWC. This must be defended once a month and at every PPV/Supercard.) - EWC Elite Champion (No. 1 Title in the EWC. This must be defended at every Supercard/PPV.) IV. Programming - EWC Wednesday Wreckage The main and only EWC wrestling show. This is an hour long and holds 4 live matches, 2 darks, or 5 live 1 dark, or just 6 live. - EWC Back it Up A show that recaps Wednesday Wreckage on Thursday. - EWC In the Spotlight A show that features three EWC wrestlers being interviewed. This takes place on Friday. (Note - The wrestlers that appear on this show volunteer, so you'll get a little push if you come on) V. EWC Fun Stuff Here are the fictional people of the Elite Wrestling Corporation: EWC Color Commentator - Jim Houston EWC Heel Color Commentator - Buck Martinez EWC's Main Interviewer - Nick Diamond EWC's Interviewer - Johnny Gomez EWC's Ring Announcer - Frank Anderson EWC's Head Referee - "Tiny" Tim Thomas EWC Referee - Clubber Lang EWC's Main Sponsor - Mighty Mick's Boxing eSN - Electronic Sports Network, Channel that broadcasts EWC programming VI. EWC Rankings System Once again, simple. :) You get ... +30 if you win the EWC Elite Title +30 if you win the EWC Elite Tag Titles +15 if you win the EWC Midwest Title +10 if you win the Television Title +10 if you win the Skinnyweight Title +10 if you win by pinfall or submission +5 if you beat a jobber +1 for a draw I like the point rankings system because it's very simple and easy to use. And shots for titles will not be determined by records first, it will be determined by points. If there is a tie, then it will go to overall record. No points will be taken away if you lose a match.
