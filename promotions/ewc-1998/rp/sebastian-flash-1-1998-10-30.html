The Sebastian-Douglas War: Flash #1

30 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everyone, I am not usually one to post any of my work on RSPWF, but since the entire Mike Sebastian-Derek Douglas war went on here, I figure someone might actually be interested in seeing the flashes for the Sebastian-Douglas match that will take place in EWC on November 11th. Anyhow, here is Sebastian's first flash for the matchup. Each man gets four, so here is number one. If anyone feels the need to give feedback, flame, or cheer for the winner, it is welcome. I will not tirade the person(s) who choose to do this. It's open game. Anyhow, here you are. ____________________________________________________ [The scene fades in upon an extravagant room of some sort. Black leather couches and chairs fill the place, as well as tables and other various pieces of furniture. Paintings hang from the walls, as well as other draperies and wall hangings. And, with fine china being showcased in a giant, wooden hutch, it is now obvious -- this is the land of luxury. Soon, a man walks across the room wearing a nicely pressed, three-piece suit and a pair of polished Gucci loafers. The man's back is to the camera still, so his identity remains a secret. As he stops at the wet bar to pour himself a drink, he twirls around and faces the camera now. The newest member of EWC has revealed himself. This is "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian.] MS: EWC, eh? Ya finally got what ya wanted, I see -- Mike Sebastian headlining yer marquee program in a couple o' weeks. Yeah, ya all got what you wanted, I'll grant ya. But, come the eleventh, 'ol "Money Driven" is gonna get what _he_ wants... ... Douglas. On a silver fu[bleep]ing platter! [Sebastian grins, before continuing.] I ain't talkin' about Chris... or Shane... or any other Douglas that's out there, 'sides this one. Derek Douglas. My new opponent... my new rival... my new client... and after this is all said and through... My new bitch. [Mike turns, takes a sip from his drink now, and turns back towards the camera.] We been at this for a month now, Derek. All yer insults, yer putdowns, they were cute. But, the time for play is over, kid. Whatever you wanna run from that mouth o' yours from here on out ain't gonna mean too much come game time, kid-o. The fact is, you kept talkin' and talkin'... talkin' so much that I got a little sick o' hearin' it. But, I thought we'd play nice, Douglas -- settle it like men. Guess I was wrong. Seems as though you wanna put this thing in the ring, and see who the better man really is... [Sebastian thinks to himself once more.] Y'know, they say, "Be careful what ya wish for. Ya just might get it." Looks like ya got what ya asked for, junior. But, if you're askin' fer "Money Driven" now, I'd hate to see what yer Christmas Wish List looks like. [A smirk.] But, it's a shame we couldn't get this thing done earlier. From what I hear, you weren't the most idealistic person tuh work with. First off, you wanted some exploding table match inside a cage, with a... [Sebastian just stops, and continues, forgetting what he was saying.] ... something rather. Then, you wanted to go at it in a Ladder Match for _my_ N.E.W. Cruiserweight stap. Ain't happening, pal. But, two outta three falls works for me. You... me... EWC. Sounds like a plan. So, in two weeks time, Douglas, you'll get what you've been asking for, fer the past month... But, if at any time, yer feet get a bit cold, I want ya tuh gimme a call. Let me give ya my card... [Mike reaches into his back pocket now, pulling out a business card. He holds it up for the camera. It reads:] ------------------------------------------------------ | Sebastian Savings and Loan, Inc. | | | | (W) 555-BANK | | | | Owner: Mr. Mike Sebastian, M.D. | | Head Broker: Mr. Justin Miller | | | | "We break the bones... and the bank!" | ------------------------------------------------------ [Sebastian grins, and returns hands the card to the camera man.] MS: But Douglas, ya might like tuh call yer own broker 'fore the eleventh, kid, because if you thought the crashes o' '29 and '87 were bad... ... yer in fer one helluva surprise, jack. [Sebastian flashes his arrogant grin at the camera once more.] And believe me Derek, you can take _that_ and anything else you damn well please... ... to the bank.
