Rush: Unlimited (part 6 of 10)

19 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DS: Fans, we're back and Jon Massey is in the ring! So let's go, to the CEO! Jon Massey: Alright ladies and gentlemen, at this time, I wanna introduce to you a man, who we all know, and who we have all come to not respect the least bit. Here is the man who calls himself DJ, he is ULTRA ACE EXPRO!!!! DS: I heard that something interesting might be happening here but I'm not sure. I'm curious as to what this is all about. Ultra Ace is coming down the aisle and he enters the ring. Let's not forget, he defeated Ice "the Finisher" tonight, and afterwards, his man MAXX destroyed Ice! Jon Massey: Now, Ace, I'm sure you know why you're out here right now right? You know what this is all about right? Ace: Well, I'm not real sure why we're out here.....but......DO I GET MY MATCH WIT ZOTO? Jon Massey: This isn't about Zoto Ace. This is about you and your attitude you've been displaying. Now look, you get to debut as the new and improved Ultra Ace, and that's like the Mega Man of the WWC, so you should be happy about that. THEN, you also defeat H2O on numerous ocassions. But if that's NOT enough, you're given a plastic and cardboard, painted gold, WWC Jobber Title, by yours truly! I handed it to you! But you continue to display countless whining and bickering to me. Backstage, before every event, after every event, you try to tell me your getting screwed. It doesn't look that way! Another example, you just beat Ice tonight! Now you can't deny all your complaining and bickering can you? Ace: No.................DO I GET MY MATCH WITH ZOTO!? Jon Massey: Ace, I already told you, this isn't about ZOTO! But what this is about is YOU, and quite frankly, Acey Boy, if it wasn't for me, the CEO and the man in charge here, you'd be nothing but just another jobber to the stars! But let me tell you something ACE, I made you into a superstar, and you my friend, you have crossed the line with all your complaining and bickering. Therefore, I choose not to renew your contract which ends this month, BUT instead, I will waive the contract............AND TERMINATE YOUR SORRY ASS!!!!! Go on up to Stamford boy, so you can job to a Bouricua! Ace: So...when do I get my match with ZOTO!? Jon Massey: Jeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeez...........put it this way, I'll put in house show results that you lost to Zoto. How's that sound, you unmarketble nitwit? Then again, I got your match with Zoto! [The CEO pokes Ace in the eye and knocks him out with a right hand as the crowd goes wild] Jon Massey: Ladies and gentlemen, ULTRA ACE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! And he has been terminated my friends....... DS: MY GOD FANS! I can't believe it! We'll be right back! Stay Tuned... COMMERCIAL
