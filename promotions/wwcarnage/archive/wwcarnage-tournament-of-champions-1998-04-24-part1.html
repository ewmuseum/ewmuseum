Tournament of Champions (part 1 of 10)

24 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WWC Rush!!!! The Tournament of Champions!!! LIVE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [The camera zooms into the jam packed Seattle Arena where the crowd is going nuts and signs are everywhere. The fireworks errupt and the announcers begin the program, as the camera zooms in on them at the table at ringside] Derek Stallion: Hello everyone and welcome to Rush!!!! Welcome to the Tournament of Champions!! Tonight, we will crown a first ever WWC International Champion!!! Jesse Rhodes: That's right Derek, and the man will be the undisputed number one contender for the World Heavyweight Title!! DS: We've got the eight man tournament tonight, PLUS, we've got Basler and THP one on one and XXX taking on Clergy!! JS: It's gonna be a great night of action, and we'll kick things off, with Storm Richards in the ring!! DS: That's right Jesse, and well folks, remember back to RUSH unlimited, Rancid made a stunning announcement that he was going for the tag team championship. I mean... think about it. As long as Rancid has been here, he has never alluded to teaming up with anyone. In fact, he's made it quite clear that he doesn't want anything to do with anyone. We all saw, he turned down an opening from Sex and Violence. Maybe I read that invitation wrong at Beck's dedication, but thats what it seemed to be. JR: Yeah, and you know, this guy obviously hasn't had good experiences with friends and doesn't want any now. And tag teaming and comradeship go hand in hand. If he is looking to be a tag champ, I don't think he holds the right cards to win. And why did he say he's gonna win them to anger Jon Massey? DS: Lets go to Storm Richards to find out. Storm: Ladies and Gentlemen, please welcome, the former WWC Television Champion... RANCID!!!! [The fans erupt to chants of his name as One by Metallica plays and he comes out looking at all the fans as he approaches the ring. Rancid enters the ring and stares at Storm] Storm: Well.. ahem.. uh.. Rancid.. [Fans get louder] Storm: I don't understand this. You are obviously a sick person and these fans chant you, and you act like you don't care. Why? [Rancid grabs the mic in Storms hand] Rancid: You have to understans how hard it is to deal with this. I go through my life being shunned, I come here and everyone is on my side. Its weird. But let me make something perfectly clear, I don't care if you cheer me. I mean, don't get me wrong, I appreciate it, but whether or not you do, its not gonna affect me. I ain't gonna come out there and slap hands with ya, I ain't gonna promote this stupid merchandise that the WWC is selling with my picture on it, I am gonna do what I do, and cheer me or not. I'm glad you do but I don't care if you don't. [Rancid gets a good cheer from that as he throws the mic back at Storm] Storm: Down to buisness. You said you were gonna become a tag champion? Why? You never cared about teaming before? Rancid: Simple. It pissed the WWC off something awful when I won the TV title. Espcially, the second time. Now, I planted Massey on his head, nothing personal, but its kinda tradition. But its been made very clear to me by Massey and the WWC HQ, that they don't want me to be a champion here again in the WWC. They just want me to fight and they'll increse my pay. [Rancid gets a disgusted look] Rancid: Its never been about the money. Its about respect. And hell, at the moment, these fans are an asset to me. Because you know Massey, that if I deserve a shot at any title, and you don't give it to me, these fans are gonna let you know that its pissing them right. AM I RIGHT? [Rancid fans cheer] Rancid: Right, so the way I figure it, I got to do something to piss off Massey back. Storm: What do you mean back? Rancid: Well.. he took me out of the tournement. Storm: Because you piledrived him. Rancid: Yeah but look. Shane laid him out too and do you think thats gonna stop Massey from handing him a title? Shit no. Why? Cause Shane is the Franchise and a higher classmen like Massey. Its bullshit. Storm: Well then why not the world title? Rancid: Thats coming. I'll get there. Storm: Well. What about a partner? WHo in the hell would even consider teaming with you? Rancid: He's right behind you. DS: ITS BILL DEVONS!!! DEVONS HAS COME OUT OF THE CROWD AND GRABS STORM AND THROWS HIM INTO THE CROWD!!!! [Rancid grabs the mic] Rancid: The only man who would consider it. Bill Devons. And me and Bill have both stated this, we aren't looking for a friendship. Just a partner. Me and Bill have our past and it goes back to the beginning of the WWC. We trust each other. We don't like each other. Doom.... Mad Dog.. whether or not you are WWC or SWD, to me you are SWD cause you crossed the unreturnable line and those titles you hold, will be in the grip of Devons and Rancid very soon!! [Rancid's theme plays and Rancid begins to leave but Deevons grabs the mic] Devons: But understand Rancid... [His theme dies down] Devons: When we get the titles, I ain't just gonna drop them. I plan on getting those titles for good. I ain't fucking around. Now play the real theme. [Rancid gets a smirk and makes a 'come on' gesture. Devons' theme plays and hey both take off into the crowd] DS: What a shocker, Rancid chooses Devons as his partner!!! We'll be right back fans!! STAY TUNED..... Commercial
