We are in Chicago, Illinois for the second EWUK pay per view event. This is the first time we've been out of the EWUK colosseum in Liverpool, England, and we've decided to go world-wide and give some of the US wrestling fans an idea of what we're all about. I'm Joey Meher alongside Mike Burton and Tony "The Tiger" Flint. How are you doing today, guys? TF: Great! MB: Would that be grrrrrrreeeaaat, Tony? TF: You'll be laughing on the other side of your face when the Immoral Majority take home not only the tag team gold, but also the Heavyweight title. JM: Talking of the Immoral Majority, there was a shall we say unfortunate incident involving them earlier today. MB: No, let's say it was a case of what goes around comes around. Let's get to the video. [The Video is displayed on the big screen in the arena] [Camera view of the outside of the dressing room of Immoral Majority. The Atomic Annihilator and the Finisher are outside with brass knuckles and a baseball bat. The Annihilator breaks the door down with a running kick. They run right at Justice Served and The Devil first, with Senator Johnson staying back trying to avoid being attacked. The Annihilator hits Justice Served with the bat once in the mid-section doubling him over, and then again across the back. Then he smacks The Devil upside the head with it knocking the Devil right into The Finisher's grasp for a DDT through a wooden bench. Then the Annihilator pulls out his brass knuckles and smashes Justice Served in the face and finally throws him into the wall out of the way. Meanwhile, the Finisher has the Devil locked in a Scorpion Death-Lock, allowing the Atomic Annihilator to corner Senator Johnson with no place for the Senator to go. The Annihilator picks the Devil's Singapore cane which is lying on the floor, and hits the Senator with it repeatedly, before giving him a gutwrench suplex on the floor. The Finisher lets go of the Scorpion Deathlock] Annihilator: "ON WEDNESDAY NIGHT, YOU GUYS BROUGHT OUT THE WORST IN ME! NOW YOUR GOING TO FEEL IT, AND THIS IS ONLY THE BEGINNING! [The Annihilator then storms out] Finisher: "Ha, ha, ha, that's right. Now who looks worse for the wear? Good luck winning any matches tonight, Immoral Majority. Or should I say, good luck in even making it to the ring? Ha, ha, ha! [The Finisher then leaves as well] [Back to real time. Huge pop from the crowd after they see the video on the big screen] MB: Man, was that awesome! Let's roll it again, I've already watched it four times. TF: What the hell?!? That means the Immoral Majority win the match, right? JM: Nope! No such luck for the Immoral Majority. If they want to win the tag straps and keep the world title, they're going to have to get in the ring tonight. Or in the case of the world title match, get out of the ring, because that one is falls count anywhere rules. MB: Rules? Are you kidding me? When the Annihilator and any member of the Immoral Majority are against each other, you can throw the rule book out the window. JM: And how about the European title match? TF: Who cares? The Immoral Majority should have won that last Wednesday. JM: But they didn't, and tonight it's the long awaited showdown between Big Bobby Turner and Champion Reckless Ron Stevens. Any ideas on how that one's going to go Mike? MB: Absolutely no idea. TF: I hope they beat each others brains out. JM: That would seem likely actually. We have the tag team titles up for grabs first. All you people who have been following EWUK since we broke onto the wrestling scene will know that at the last pay per view event the Realm of Shadows were successful in capturing the tag team belts, but were forced to vacate them after they lost a loser leaves town match to the Immoral Majority. MB: Might I also add that it was only a loser leaves town until August match. TF: So you're telling me that in a month we might be seeing those idiots back in action? JM: Who knows. But what I do know is that it's time for our first match. Ring Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen...are you ready for EWUK TOTAL KAOS? [Crowd goes wild] Ring Announcer: Our first match of the evening is for the vacant EWUK Tag Team Titles. [Huge crowd pop] Ring Announcer: Introducing first, weighing in at a combined weight of 470 pounds, here are the team of JOHNNY MALONE and EDDY WINSTON. [Loud pop from the crowd as the high flying team of Malone and Winston make their way to the ring giving high fives to the fans] JM: We all know that lately Eddy Winston has had an illness in the family. I wonder if that will affect their wrestling tonight. TF: No, it's just their way of trying to weasel out of this one. JM: Well, they're here tonight. Talking of not being here, anyone seen Alex today? TF: Oh, yeah, I sold his plane ticket. JM: You did what?!? TF: Quiet, Meher, they're about to announce the Immoral Majority. Ring Announcer: Their opponents, weighing in at a combined weight of 544 pounds and representing the Immoral Majority, here are JUSTICE SERVED and THE DEVIL. [Boos echo around the arena as they make their way down the aisle towards the ring. Justice Served is dressed in his usual ring attire of a long, flowing judge's robe and masked wrestler the Devil is carrying his singapore cane. They are both looking a little the worse for wear following the Finisher and the Annihilator's attack before the show started] JM: You might not like these two, but Justice Served is one of the most powerful and focused men in wrestling today, and you can only describe the Devil as the extreme luchador. The Devil is an extreme, insane and borderline psychopathic competitor. MB: And listening to this reception, you can tell that like just about everyone else, Chicago does not like them. TF: Who cares if the fans like them or not? JM: I'm afraid Tony has a point. Their win-loss records and how they're looked at in the eyes of the championship committee certainly does not have anything to do with how much the fans like them. TF: Yeah, but tonight they've been attacked by those two fools, the Finisher and the Atomic Annihilator. [Ding Ding] JM: There's the bell, and we're underway. JM: It's the Devil starting things off for the Immoral Majority, and Eddy Winston's in there for his team. The Devil quick to get the advantage with an armdrag takedown. TF: Right there. Even though Malone and Winston might think they have the speed advantage, take a look at how fast the Devil got that move in. MB: It was impressive. The Devil whips Eddy Winston into the ropes, but Eddy Winston reverses it. Winston with a kick as the Devil comes off the ropes, and now runs off the ropes himself. The Devil though, is waiting for him as he comes off the other side and connects with a dropkick. The action is fast and furious with these two. JM: Look out...that dropkick just sent Eddy Winston to the outside. The Devil quick to capitalize on the situation, and runs off the ropes...SPRINGBOARD PLANCHA! MB: Winston gets out the way, and there's nobody home. Perhaps a mistake for the Devil to go for such a risky move so early on, but that's what happens with these high flyers. JM: Winston quick to get back in the ring. What happens if this one ends in a countout? MB: I don't know. Under normal circumstances, the title would not change hands, but as the title is vacant I guess it can be won by countout. JM: Well, that isn't an issue at the moment...the Devil's back on the ring apron. Winston's over there to meet him, but the Devil uses the top rope as a springboard and hits with a dropkick. Quick thinking and very resourceful. MB: Winston back to his feet and executes a monkey flip, but the Devil lands on his feet. Devil tags in Justice Served, and Winston gets hit with a clothesline by Justice. Winston rolls over to his corner and tags in Johnny Malone. TF: Justice Served with a fireman's carry takedown and you might as well give them the belts now. Malone and Winston are doomed. JM: I don't know about that, but Justice Served sends him into the ropes with an irish whip. Clothesline, but Malone ducks under. Malone hits Justice Served with a clothesline of his own, but Justice doesn't go down. Malone with a dropkick, and that takes Justice down. Justice Served is back up, and Malone goes for a bodyslam. He has a bit of trouble picking him up, and Justice nails him across the back with an axehandle like blow. Justice Served throws him over the top rope, and follows him out. The action goes to the outside once again in this one. MB: Justice sends Malone into the ringsteps, and gets back in the ring. [Justice Served distracts the ref while the Devil attacks Johnny Malone with the singapore cane] JM: That is typical of Immoral Majority. The ref's out there to stop the Devil now, but the damage has already been done. Justice Served is back out there, and drags Malone back in the ring. TF: I think my monitor went out again. What happened? JM: Immoral Majority cheated. TF: No, I didn't see anything. JM: Your monitor conveniently fails again. Come on, Tony. Justice Served makes the tag and the Devil's back in. Winston tries to come in, but the referee Jon McMahon's right there to stop him, giving the Immoral Majority the chance to double team again. MB: It's the Devil now, with a dropkick to the knee of Johnny Malone. The Devil sends Malone for the ride...FLYING CROSS BODY! Malone hooks the leg....1.....2.... TF: NO! Easy kickout by the Devil. [Johnny Malone executes a Victory Roll and gets another two count. Johnny Malone runs into the ropes, but the Devil hits him with an elbow smash. The Devil goes to the top rope] JM: Malone looks unconscious. That elbow by the Devil hit him clean in the face. The Devil jumps off the top rope...but NO! Malone moves. MB: Johnny Malone was playing possum, and now he gets the advantage. [Malone executes a standing moonsault, but the Devil lifts his knees. The Devil goes for a Frankensteiner, but Johnny Malone counters it with a power bomb] JM: WOW! Malone proves how versatile he is right there. You know, none of these high flying moves seem to be paying off. [Johnny Malone sends the Devil for the ride, but the Devil reverses the irish whip and hits with a backdrop. The Devil goes to the top rope and executes a somersault legdrop] TF: You spoke too soon again, Meher. Here's the cover...1...2...HE GOT HIM! JM: That was only a two count. [The Devil executes a DDT, picks him up and executes a thrust kick to the head. The Devil goes for a superkick, but Johnny Malone ducks. Malone kicks the Devil in the midsection doubling him up, and goes for a sunset flip, but the Devil sits down and gets a near fall] MB: Malone's been in there on hell of a long time. He's going to need to make a tag soon or he'll collapse of exhaustion. [Justice Served enters the ring to making it two on one. Eddy Winston attempts to come in, but the ref stops him, giving the Immoral Majority the opportunity to double team] JM: Things are looking worse than ever for Malone. [The Devil and Justice Served whip Johnny Malone into the ropes and hit Malone with a double dropkick. They then hit Johnny Malone with a double DDT] TF: Admit it Meher, that was amazing. JM: I've never said the Immoral Majority weren't a great team, I just don't approve of their methods of getting the victory. TF: There you go...Justice Served has left the ring. [The Devil takes him down with kick to the head. The Devil whips Johnny Malone into the turnbuckle, but Johnny Malone reverses it. Johnny Malone charges into the corner, but the Devil moves out the way and gets a roll up and a near fall] JM: The Devil goes for a standing dropkick, but Malone moves. MB: THERE'S THE TAG! [Eddy Winston is fast to get into the ring and hits the Devil with a dropkick. Justice Served comes in, but Winston hits him with a dropkick too. Eddy Winston goes for a crucifix on the Devil, but the Devil counters it with a Samoan Drop. The Devil sends him hard into the turnbuckle and executes a handspring elbow] JM: The Devil showing off his skills right there. [The Devil runs into the ropes but Eddy Winston hits The Devil with a backdrop. Eddy Winston executes a jumping neck snap] JM: Cover...1...2...Winston almost had him. [The Devil runs into the ropes and hits Eddy Winston with an shoulderblock. The Devil executes a small package] TF: Here it is!...ONE....TWO....YES! JM: Kickout. But that's the closest I've seen to a three count so far in this one. TF: Now Winston's in serious trouble...the Devil's just tagged in Justice Served. [Justice Served and The Devil send Eddy Winston into the ropes and the Devil hits him with a superkick on the way back. Justice Served nails him with a kneebreaker] TF: He's not going to running around the ring after that. JM: POWERBOMB! MB: Justice Served with the lateral press. Jon McMahon counts...1...2...Malone's in to make the save. JM: The Devil wastes no time in coming in too though, and throws Malone to the outside. [Justice Served and the Devil place Eddy Winston's neck over the ropes, and the Devil runs off the ropes, jumps over Winston and executes a Rude Awakening landing on the outside] TF: UNDERWORLD VISIONS!!! IT'S OVER!!! [Boos from the crowd] MB: Justice Served has Winston set up...OH NO... TF: SCALES OF JUSTICE!!! [Boos from the crowd] JM: This one's history I'm afraid folks. TF: ONE...TWO...THREE!!! YEAAAHHH!!! [Boos from the crowd] [Justice Served and the Devil get their arms raised in victory and are handed the EWUK tag team title belts] Ring Announcer: Here are your winners, and NEW EWUK TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS....Justice Served, The Devil....IMMORAL MAJORITY. [The fans start to boo, but as the Immoral Majority walk down the aisle the Finisher runs down the towards the ring with a baseball bat] JM: AHHHWWW MY GAWWWWWDDDD!!! [The Finisher levels Justice Served with the bat and starts to lay into the Devil with punches and kicks before hitting him with a tombstone piledriver on the concrete] TF: AAARRRRGGGGHHH!!! JM: OH NO! There's no padding on those floors. If we wanted padding on the floor, we'd be doing this show in disney world. TF: So you say every time someone gets put headfirst into the floor. But this isn't just SOMEONE...this is the Devil of Immoral Majority. GET SOME HELP! JM: I think some medical attention is required. [Senator Johnson comes out, followed by security and paramedics. The Finisher is pushed towards the locker rooms by the security guards, and the three members of Immoral Majority leave with the tag team titles, even though the Devil has to be carried out on a stretcher] JM: That makes twice the Finisher and the Annihilator have got the better of the Immoral Majority tonight, this time with the Finisher doing a number on the Devil. One more time, and the Annihilator will be walking away with the heavyweight title. TF: NEVER going to happen, Meher. NEVER. JM: Never say never, Tony. This could be the night that the world title leaves Immoral Majority headquarters. TF: What's the Annihilator going to do with the belt? He'll probably melt it down and trade it for a new bike. JM: I'm pretty sure he wouldn't do that. TF: You don't seem certain, Meher. The Annihilator is the only guy in EWUK who's shoe size is actually bigger than his IQ. MB: Well, the Annihilator has had a string of victories in EWUK, his only problem being with the Immoral Majority. He recently scored a pinfall victory over the Devil in a tag team match. JM: Yeah, and the tag match was the Annihilator and the Finisher against the new tag team champions, Justice Served and the Devil. So you can bet the Atomic Annihilator and the Finisher are going to want a match with the tag titles on the line. TF: That's if the Annihilator survives the title match against the Senator. MB: Well, you can bet the Immoral Majority have one or two tricks up their sleeves, but it's been the Annihilator that's had the upper hand tonight as far as surprises are concerned. TF: You mean sneaky, underhanded attacks, Burton. MB: Those words sneaky and underhanded remind me of only three people...the Immoral Majority. TF: You're only jealous of them, like everyone else is, Burton. Now they've got the tag team titles as well as the world title. They only need the European title and that new Warriors title and they've got all the gold. Only a matter of time. JM: The tournament for the Warriors title is starting in about a week, so stay tuned to the Slam and Wednesday Night Warriors for matches in that tournament. MB: Once we've crowned the first Warriors champion, I believe he has to defend on every Wednesday Night Warriors card. JM: That's correct. Up next on the pay per view though, we've got the debut of the Taxman, as he takes on El Tigra. TF: Get it right, Meher, it's that good for nothing tax cheating Mexican scum bag, El Tigra. Ring Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following match is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, making his EWUK debut, from Washington DC, weighing in at 260 pounds, here is THE TAXMAN. [Boos from the crowd as the 6' 4" newcomer makes his way to the ring in a suit, carrying a briefcase] TF: Hey, Taxman...Joey didn't pay his taxes this year. Come over here and audit him! JM: Please sit down, Tony. TF: Already I like the attitude of the Taxman. Come on...bring out that tax cheat, El Tigra. Ring Announcer: His opponent, from Mexico City, Mexico, weighing in at 240 pounds, here is EL TIGRA. [El Tigra runs down the aisle to a pretty loud face pop. He jumps into the ring and starts nailing the Taxman with rights and lefts] MB: It looks like El Tigra's taken exception to some of the Taxman's comments. JM: The Taxman, though slows down the action and executes a kneebreaker. Tigra quite obviously wants to keep the action fast, whereas the Taxman is going to want to keep things slow and utilize his technical skills. [The Taxman whips El Tigra into the turnbuckle and charges in with a clothesline, but El Tigra moves out of the way. El Tigra runs into the ropes, but the Taxman hits El Tigra with a shoulderblock. The Taxman whips El Tigra into the ropes, but El Tigra reverses it. El Tigra comes back off the ropes and staggers the Taxman with a shoulderblock] MB: Well, it looks like El Tigra's managing to keep this one fast, but nobody's really got much of an advantage yet. [El Tigra whips The Taxman into the ropes and ducks his head down, but the Taxman executes a piledriver] TF: He saw that coming from a mile away. It's over for Tigra. JM: Taxman with an irish whip and goes for a clothesline...MAN, THAT ALMOST TOOK HIS HEAD OFF! TF: This is too easy for the Taxman. They should have given him a challenge on his debut. Instead, he gets to make a fool out of El Tigra on world-wide TV. [The Taxman executes a swinging neckbreaker, then throws Tigra out of the ring] JM: The Taxman follows him out, and sends El Tigra into the ringpost. Taxman runs in shoulder first... MB: TIGRA MOVES! TF: OUCH! JM: There's no question about that hurting the Taxman. That would hurt anyone. [El Tigra gets back in the ring, runs off the ropes and sends the Taxman into the guardrail with a baseball slide] MB: Audit that! El Tigra's on the top rope...jumps, but the Taxman's got his briefcase... JM: HE JUST NAILED TIGRA! TF: Now that was a smart move. The Taxman sends El Tigra into the ringsteps. Tigra's not only lost this one, but I think he's lost out on ever being able to cheat on his taxes again. JM: It ain't over yet. [The Taxman executes a snap suplex on the outside] TF: Yes it is, Meher! [The Taxman throws El Tigra back in the ring and follows in. The Taxman then applies an STF] JM: Too close to the ropes. Tigra only has to reach out. TF: Yeah, but he's going to be put out first. There's no way he'll get out of this one. JM: He doesn't have to get out of it, he just has to get to the ropes. MB: And he's there. [The ref forces the Taxman to break the hold, but the Taxman is quick to continue the barrage of technical moves by executing a neck breaker, and then going for a figure four] JM: Again, they're too near the ropes, and Tigra reaches out and the ref forces the break. TF: He's not trying to make him give it up necessarily, Meher. He's just softening him up. [The Taxman goes for a DDT, but El Tigra counters it with a backdrop] JM: I don't know where Tigra got the energy to do that, but maybe we'll see him fight back and pull off a victory. [El Tigra executes a small package] JM: Not even a two count. The Taxman is right back up and takes him down with a bodyslam. [The Taxman whips El Tigra into the ropes, and runs off the other side and nails him with a flying clothesline] MB: OH MY! This is the beginning of the end for El Tigra. [The Taxman hits with a swinging neckbreaker, and drops the elbow] JM: One....two....was that a three? MB: Tigra's got his foot on the ropes. [The Taxman takes him down with a belly-to-back suplex, picks him up and hits with a vertical suplex before dropping the knee] TF: Here it comes...BRAINBUSTER! JM: The referee's there to make the count.....one MB: ....two.....three. [Boos from the crowd] TF: YEAH! He got him! Ring Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen, here is your winner...THE TAXMAN. JM: What a debut! TF: And what a victory, Meher! I can see big things in the future of the Taxman here in EWUK. There's still one or two more tax cheats for him to catch. MB: Apparently so, but he's yet to step in the ring with some real competition. Ring Announcer: The following match is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from Toronto, Ontario, Canada, weighing in at 243 pounds, here is PARALYZER PATRICK BORDEAU. [Bordeau comes out to a fairly good pop from the Chicago crowd. He is dressed in his usual ring attire, and looks ready for action] Ring Announcer: His opponent, from Rome, Italy, weighing in at 320 pounds, here is THE IMMORTAL. [The Immortal comes to the ring wearing his usual ring attire which includes a dark purple hooded cloak. He gets about the same pop from the crowd] JM: Well, here we go with another match. Both of these guys are class competitors. TF: Yeah, classed as poor. JM: Maybe in your estimation, but not mine. MB: Nor mine. These guys are talented athletes, and have had much success not only in EWUK, but also in various other federations. JM: Indeed. Here's the lock up, the Immortal with an inverted atomic drop, and a clothesline drops Bordeau. The Immortal has a fairly big size advantage, but that's never stopped Bordeau against other opponents. MB: The Immortal with a bulldog headlock, but Bordeau moves and the Immortal hits nothing but canvas. Bordeau quick to capitalize with a knee drop. TF: Bordeau's quick, but the Immortal is back to his feet and executes a side suplex. JM: Bordeau with a whip into the turnbuckle, and the Immortal goes in hard. Bordeau runs in shoulder first, and gets him. MB: Well, that took a lot out of the Immortal. Bordeau with a full nelson... TF: BORDEAU JUST TURNED IT INTO A BELLY TO BELLY! How d'he do that, Meher?!? JM: He has a large repertoire of manoeuvers out of the full nelson, but that belly to belly was a surprise, especially on such a big man. [Patrick Bordeau executes a jumping neck snap and sends the Immortal for the ride] TF: The Immortal comes off the other side and goes for a flying clothesline... JM: Bordeau quick to get out of the way of that one. Bordeau off the ropes now, but gets caught in a military press by the Immortal. The Immortal slams him down hard. That'll take a lot out of you. TF: Not me, Meher, I'd be right back up by now. JM: I sincerely doubt that, Tony. The Immortal going to the top rope. I've seen him do a leg drop off the top before, but the top rope isn't really the place for a big man. MB: Bordeau is right up, and goes over to the turnbuckle...SUPERPLEX! JM: Momentum rather than power on that one, folks. Bordeau with a standing moonsault. Cover...The Immortal kicks out on two. TF: It looked more like three from where I'm sitting. JM: Well, in any event, this one continues, and the Immortal with a kneelift and a backbreaker puts Bordeau on the canvas. [The Immortal picks him up, kicks Bordeau in the midsection and follows up with a DDT] MB: The Immortal with the advantage now, as he picks Bordeau up once again. The Immortal runs off the ropes, but what's this....Bordeau's got him...ICE PICK!!! JM: That'll do it...one....two....three. Bordeau gets the win. Ring Announcer: Here is your winner...PARALYZER PATRICK BORDEAU. <<Continued in part 2>>
