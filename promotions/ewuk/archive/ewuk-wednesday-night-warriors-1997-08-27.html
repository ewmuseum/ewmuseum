We are just four days away from the Pay Per View event everyone's been talking about, Wrestle War, and we have some great action tonight, including a Warriors title defence, a tag title defence, and Senator Johnson takes on J.Assassin in a non-title match. Not withstanding that, we have a whole lot more, so let's get down to the ring for our opening match! ============================================ Tag Title Match Duo of Annihilation vs. The Moral Majority ============================================ Ring Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following tag team match is scheduled for one fall and is for the EWUK Tag Team Titles! [Huge pop] Ring Announcer: Introducing first, the challengers, at a total combined weight of 490 pounds, here are Tony Dylan, Chip Syske... THE MORAL MAJORITY! [Really loud face pop from the crowd as the Moral Majority make their way to the ring, high fiving the fans on the way] Ring Announcer: Their opponents, weighing in at a combined weight of 620 pounds, here are the EWUK Tag Team Champions of the Wooooooorld... The Finisher, The Atomic Annihilator... THE DUO OF ANNIHILATION!!! [Huge face pop from the crowd as the Duo make their way to the ring] JM: Wow! I can barely hear myself speak. The fans really love these two teams. Take a look at all those Duo and Moral Majority signs in the crowd! TF: That just shows that the combined IQ of all of the fans here equals the IQ of an eggplant. JM: Will you stop. He we go, and it's the Atomic Annihilator and Chip Syske set to start things off. The Annihilator with a big forearm smash, and again staggers Syske. The Annihilator picks him up onto his shoulder for a running powerslam. TF: I guess you could say he has a chip on his shoulder! JM: Don't even go there. The Annihilator with the slam. A cover, but Tony Dylan makes the save. The Annihilator with the tag and in comes the Finisher. The Finisher with a diving shoulderblock, but Syske spikes him into the canvas. TF: That was a pretty good move, even if it was executed by a jackass. JM: Syske makes the tag and in comes Tony Dylan. TF: That guy's got a cool first name, but he's still a putz. JM: Dylan with an armdrag takedown, and applies a hammerlock on the canvas. TF: It looks like he applied it on the Finisher to me. JM: He did. TF: So why did you say he applied it on the canvas?! JM: What is this, open mic night? TF: Look out, Meher! Call the action... the Finisher just got nailed with a monkey flip by Dylan. I wonder if that guy likes bananas? JM: Dylan goes for another monkey flip, but the Finisher pushes him off. TF: A classic case of going to the well once too often, Meher. That's what comes with inexperience. You see, a seasoned veteran like myself would not make a mistake like that. JM: Seasoned? Veteran? TF: You said it, Meher. JM: The Finisher with a rana, and Dylan rolls to the outside to catch his breath. The Finisher though, runs off the ropes, and there's a baseball slide, sending Dylan into the guardrail. TF: Dylan hit his head hard on the guardrail, Meher, but I don't suppose it did much damage. JM: Dylan is trying to get back into the ring, and the Finisher helps him back in with a suplex. The Finisher makes the tag and in comes the Annihilator. The Annihilator goes for a big boot, but Dylan ducks under. TF: If he'd have connected with that, I think Dylan's head would be rolling around on the floor! Off with his head! JM: Dylan dives towards his corner... AND THERE'S THE TAG!!! The Moral Majority needed that tag, and Syske is in, and unloads with a series of European uppercuts. Syske with a dropkick, and again, staggers the big man, but can't take him off his feet. Dylan's there to help him out, and... DOUBLE SUPLEX!!! TF: That's blatant illegal double teaming! Maintain some order, ref! JM: Dylan leaves the ring, and Syske goes to the top rope. Syske dives off and hits with a diving headbutt! Syske with the cover, but a two is all he can get. Syske with the irish whip, but it's reversed. Syske off the other side, but gets caught by the Annihilator with the set up for Atomic Annihilation.... THERE IT IS... ATOMIC ANNIHILATION!!! TF: Here's the cover by the Annihilator... ONE.... TWO.... THREE!!! Ring Announcer: Here are your winners and STILL EWUK Tag Team Champions of the Wooooorld... THE DUO OF ANNIHILATION!!! ********************* Winner Duo of Annihilation ********************* JM: The Duo retain the titles, as they go into the Pay Per View ready to defend against the Aristocracy. Singles action up next! ====================== Destructor vs. Spawn ====================== Ring Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following match-up is set for one fall. First, from California, weighing in at 252 pounds, and making his way to the ring with Miss Venom, here is DESTRUCTOR!!! [Heel pop from the crowd] Ring Announcer: His opponent, from Hell, weighing in at 250 pounds, here is SPAWN! [Indifferent response from the crowd] JM: Well, if I were a betting man, I'd have to put everything I own on Destructor in this one. TF: For once we agree, Meher. Destructor really is a force to be reckoned with. JM: Spawn with a few punches to start off, but they don't really phase Destructor. Spawn with a dropkick, and that sends him back a few paces. Spawn runs off the rope... Body press, but Destructor catches him, and elevates him high over the top rope and to the concrete floor. TF: Did you hear that thud Spawn made when he hit the floor, Meher? I think he could be out already. JM: Destructor goes to the outside, and Spawn with a thumb to the eyes, and a takedown, but can't capitalize. I think Spawn is really hurt. Destructor shakes off the effects of that thumb to the eyes, and picks Spawn up and bodyslams him hard onto the concrete. Destructor picks him up with a gorilla press, and throws him back into the ring. TF: The ref might have to take a look at Spawn, I think he's bleeding. JM: Destructor gets back into the ring, and nails him with a few bicep smashes. Destructor picks him up and there's a vertical suplex. TF: Destructor is manhandling him, Meher. JM: Destructor with a DDT, and I think after that he should be able to put him away. TF: I'm enjoying this, Meher. JM: OH MY! Destructor executes a bulldog. This one's history, folks. TF: Not yet, Meher... we haven't seen that awesome finishing move of his yet. JM: Well, here it comes now... SWORD OF DAMOCLES! TF: One, two, three. You could have counted to a thousand though. Ring Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen, here is your winner... DESTRUCTOR!!! ********************* Winner Destructor ********************* JM: Well, chalk up another one in the win column for Destructor as he makes fairly easy work of Spawn. Eight man tag team action up next, as Johnny Malone, Eddy Winston and James and Lloyd Dodge take on the team of Top Dawg, Big Dawg, James Meadows and George Knight. ===================================================== Malone/Winston/Dodgems vs. The Dawgs/Meadows/Knight ===================================================== Ring Announcer: The following eight man tag team contest is set for one fall. Introducing first, at a combined weight of 932 pounds, here are Johnny Malone, Eddy Winston, James Dodge and Lloyd Dodge!!! [Huge crowd pop. Lots of cheers, and lots of high fives as Malone, Winston and the Dodgems make their way to the ring] JM: Fan favorites and proud of it! Ring Announcer: Their opponents, at a total combined weight of 1205 pounds, here are Big Dawg, Top Dawg, Hard dog James Meadows and Jumpin' George Knight!!! [Boos from the crowd] JM: The weight advantage for Meadows, Knight and the Dawgs is unbelievable. It's like they've got an extra man on their team. TF: I hope they've reinforced the ring, Meher, there's over 2000 pounds in there! JM: I'm sure they have. Johnny Malone and Top Dawg are set to start things off for their respective teams. Top Dawg executes a spin kick, and nearly takes Malone's head off! Top Dawg picks him up and puts him in a headlock. A tag to Big Dawg, who comes off the middle rope with an axehandle. TF: We know the individual tag teams work well as teams, but I think that the winners will be the team in which all four men can gel together as a team. JM: That's actually quite a good point. BIG DAWG WITH A T-BONE SUPLEX! Oh my! TF: I think Malone might be put away, Meher. JM: Let's see... here's the cover..... ONE.... TWO.... Winston makes the save. TF: I think he could have had him there. JM: Well, Malone is in desperate need to make the tag, and Big Dawg tags back to Top Dawg. TF: I don't think they want Meadows and Knight in the ring, Meher. JM: Well, that could be their downfall. Top Dawg with a flying bodypress.... ONE... TWO... THR... Malone kicks out. Top Dawg puts Malone on the top rope. Perhaps for some kind of superplex. No, Malone pushes him off. Top Dawg is getting back up, and Malone is on the top... BULLET DROPKICK! TF: Malone might be a moron, but at least he can pull of a move from time to time. JM: All of these eight can, but who can make the tag first. Top Dawg is nearing his corner... and he tags Big Dawg. Malone is nearing his... AND THERE'S THE TAG TO JAMES DODGE!!! Dodge is in there and nails Big Dawg with a running dropkick. Top Dawg comes in and gets nailed by a dropkick too. In comes Meadows and Knight, and they slow down Dodge with a double clothesline. TF: This is great, Meher! All eight of them are in there now, and they're beating the lives out of each other! I love it! JM: The Dawgs against the Dodgems at Wrestle War is guaranteed to be a great one, and judging by the action in this one, it could become a classic rivalry. TF: Lloyd Dodge just dropkicked Top Dawg over the top rope! JM: From behind Big Dawg clotheslines Lloyd Dodge and follows him out. I think there's been a tag, and the legal men are James Dodge and James Meadows. TF: My money's on James to win this one! JM: They're both called James. TF: I can't lose then! JM: Oh please. James Meadows with a side slam and drops the elbow for a two count. Meadows sets up for a samoan drop, but Dodge turns it into a crucifix for a near fall. TF: The action's too fast in this one, Meher. Slow it down. JM: Meadows with the tag to Knight and he's even faster. Knight goes off the ropes, and Dodge is up... leapfrog and Dodge nails him with a backdrop. James Dodge now, tags in Eddy Winston and Winston locks in an armbar. TF: Top Dawg makes the save, and Knight whips Winston into the corner and runs in, but Winston moves! Winston goes to the middle rope and drops the elbow. Winston with a cover, but a two is all he can get. JM: Winston now, with a senton and sets up Knight in the corner. Winston with a run up, and a handspring moonsault... BUT KNIGHT ELEVATES HIM OVER THE TOP ROPE! TF: Winston fell about 10 foot, Meher. Right onto the concrete! JM: Knight goes to the outside, and Meadows comes round to help him with a double suplex on Winston. TF: Here comes Malone and the Dodgems. And here come the Dawgs! All eight of them are brawling on the outside! They want to watch the ref's count. JM: He's up to about eight... nine... Knight slides back into the ring, and so does Eddy Winston. Winston goes for a vertical suplex, BUT KNIGHT WITH A SMALL PACKAGE. Knight has the ropes for leverage, and a handful of tights! TF: What?! I don't see anything, Meher! C'Mon ref... Count! JM: One, two and KICKOUT! Knight now, with the whip into the turnbuckle, but Winston reverses it. Knight reverses it again... INTO THE REF! TF: The ref is out cold, Meher! And the Bishop is running towards the ring! JM: Look out! The Bishop, Meadows and Knight are attacking the Dawgs! TF: They're on the same team, Meher! JM: I don't think they care. Meadows and Knight with a double suplex on Top Dawg... RIGHT THROUGH A TABLE!!! Big Dawg from behind levels Knight with a chair! This one's turning into a war right here, folks. TF: This is utter mayhem, Meher. They're bringing out a stretcher to take away the referee! JM: I think there's a classic Nitro ending to this one, folks! ********************* Winner Draw - ref KO'd! ********************* JM: Tag match next... Let's get back to the ring! ========================================== Arresting Development vs. Harper/Workman ========================================== Ring Announcer: The following tag team attraction is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, weighing in at a combined weight of 489 pounds, here are Mark McBain, Johnathon Kidd, ARRESTING DEVELOPMENT! [Pretty loud face pop] Ring Announcer: Their opponents, at a combined weight of 592 pounds, here are Jack Workman and James Harper! [Mild face pop] JM: Mark McBain and James Harper are set to start thing off in this one, folks. McBain with a fireman's carry takedown and locks in an armbar submission. Harper rolls over to the ropes, and the break is forced by the ref. TF: You know, Arresting Development are wanted criminals, Meher! JM: So the Taxman says, but I don't think it's true. TF: You don't sound certain, Meher. JM: OH MY! Harper with a clothesline. Harper goes to the middle rope and drops the elbow, but nobody home! McBain makes the tag, and so does Harper. Both the fresh guys are in. TF: Both men go for a clothesline, Meher! They aren't going to be fresh for long if this continues. JM: Workman is first to his feet, and pulls Kidd up. Workman executes a powerslam, and gets a two count. Workman sets up for a powerbomb, but Kidd counters with a backdrop and makes the tag. McBain is back in, but gets caught with a shoulderblock. Workman sets him up for a vertical suplex, and McBain goes down hard. TF: McBain needs to hit with a cheap shot, Meher. I recommend a rake to the eyes! JM: McBain generally doesn't cheat like you, Tony. Workman now, sets him up for the running powerslam, McBain slides out the backdoor. McBain goes off the ropes, and... BULLDOG! McBain now, has a side waistlock, and he's got him for a... GUTWRENCH SUPLEX!!! That took a lot of power on the part of McBain, as Workman weighs close to 300 pounds. TF: McBain is doing pretty well actually. I remember that time I punched his lights out! JM: I seem to remember it being him punching you, Tony. TF: Er... my mind's gone blank, Meher. JM: McBain is trying to lift him up in that torture rack. I think he's going to get him! He's got it! He calls that move the lock up, and Workman is in a lot of pain... He gives it up! Ring Announcer: Here are your winners...
