Extreme Wrestling/United Kingdom

A few days ago I had a vision. A new way to run an e-fed where the members of the organization chose how the matches would end. From that vision the EWUK has been formed. I don't know about you, but I am getting sick of feds originating from the Eastern Coast of the US. I myself am not from the UK, but it's a great place to start. I have went out and hired the best Vice President I could find and now I am still in need of wrestlers and board members. E-mail me if you're interested........
