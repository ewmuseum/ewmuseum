EWUK Wednesday Night Warriors

24 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<Scene opens outside of the EWUK Colosseum in Liverpool, England. A line stands outside waiting to get in the colosseum for an action packed night of wrestling from the United Kingdom. Camara fades> <Shot reopens inside the brand new EWUK Colosseum with a capacity crowd on hand. A purple and black ring sits in the middle of the floor area surrounded by 1200 screaming fans in the seating area. Camera pans down to the broadcast booth where our commentators Tony Flint and Alex Windhelm are sitting going through some papers.> AW: wha...? We're on? TF: HELLO AND WELCOME TO THE FIRST EVER WEDNESDAY NIGHT WARRIORS FROM LIVERPOOL, ENGLAND. We have a great card for you tonight including four matches in the first round of the EWUK World Championship tournament and a tag team exhibition featuring the Dragons, Yin and Yang. AW: Get ready for some EXTREME action from the United Kingdom as we go down to the ring for our first match. Ring Announcer: This is a first round match in the tournament for the first ever EWUK WORLD'S CHAMPIONSHIP. Introducing first, from Mexico City, Mexico weighing in at 240 pounds.....Here is EL TIGRA! <El Tigra makes his way to the ring to a medium face pop from the fans. He wears purple trunks and a purple mask> Ring Announcer: And his opponent, From Atlanta, Georgia weighing in at 235 pounds....here is STEEEEEEVIEEEEEEE HOSKINS! <Stevie Hoskins strolls to the ring to a chorus of boos from the fans at ringside. He stops by the guardrail and spits on one of the commentators> TF: We're underway here and Hoskins is chasing El Tigra around the ring. He can't catch him. This is gonna be a long night if Hoskins doesn't get his claws on El Tigra AW: Here's the lockup. Hoskins sends El Tigra for the ride....Duckdown move by El Tigra and he's coming off the other side. EL TIGRA NAILS THE CLOTHESLINE! Hoskins back up. Spinning Heel kick by El Tigra and it sends Hoskins back against the ropes. El Tigra takes Hoskins and whips him across the ring and nails him with a dropkick.... TF: El Tigra impresses me. I like his style so far. Stevie Hoskins is supposed to be one of the greatest superstars in the sport but he hasn't shown me anything yet. Look here, Hoskins is rolling out of the ring. Some Superstar..... AW: Watch this! El Tigra nails him with a baseball slide under the ropes. He climbs back into the ring and up on the top turnbuckle. Hoskins is getting up on the outside of the ring. DROPKICK FROM THE TOP ROPE BY EL TIGRA!!!! That took alot out of both men. I think El Tigra hit his head on the concrete. TF: Look at Hoskins....this is the perfect time to punish El Tigra but he throws him back in the ring instead. This guy isn't very smart. Hoskins all over El Tigra now. AW: I don't care what you say Tony, but I see alot of potential in both of these men. El Tigra breaks the armbar hold and gets away from Stevie. Hoskins wants a test of strength here and El Tigra complies with him. Hoskins is giving a few pounds away here and it shows as he goes to his knees. TF: Look, he's dumb....and weak. AW: Don't speak so soon, Hoskins powering out and he kicks El Tigra in the groin. Another kick and the test breaks up. Hoskins off the ropes and El Tigra frog jumps over him. From the othe side now El Tigra slides under Hoskins feet. El Tigra up and he nails Hoskins with a back body drop and goes to the ropes. He connects with a legdrop and goes for the pin.....1.....kickout. Hoskins is nowhere near finished.... TF: He was finished before he even got started.....This match will be over very soon.... AW: I don't think so, Tony. Hoskins still looks like he has a lot left in him. El Tigra helps him up and goes for the suplex....IT'S REVERSED!!!!! El Tigra down and Hoskins begins to pound away on him. El Tigra is able to reach the ropes and Hoskins if forced to break the hold. Both men up on their feet. El Tigra with a kick to Hoskin's head but Hoskins catches his foot..... TF: guess what move is coming next...... AW: ENZIGUIRI!!!!!! Hoskins is on the floor.....El Tigra back up top....Here we go this could be the end of it...El Tigra is in the air and here comes the knee drop...... TF: NO ONE HOME!!!! I guess Hoskins is smarter than I thought! Hoskins is up and he pulls El Tigra to his feet....Big DDT!!!!! Right on the cranium of El Tigra...better get the tylenol. AW: Looks like Hoskins is going for the submission as he locks in the figure four leg lock. That is a really painful move. TF: Yeah, i bet you would know. You've never wrestled anybody that was any good. I've had the likes of Brandon Charm lock that move on me. Now he's good...in fact he's the only guy I've never been able to beat. AW: Yeah sure. El Tigra makes it to the ropes. HE CAN"T WALK. Hoskins has El Tigra right were he wants him. Hoskins sets him up on the top turnbuckle and here is a superplex.....Hoskins with a pin....1...2...thr...kickout. El Tigra still has some left in him. TF:Yeah, that kid never gives up. He reminds me alot of Rey Misterio, Jr. Hoskins sets him up now for a belly-to-belly suplex....perfectly executed. Here's the cover....1...2....kickout. El Tigra and Hoskins now in the center of the ring.....they lock up. AW: El Tigra sends Hoskins for the ride.....Hoskins on the rebound, and STEVIE DROP!!!! That came out of nowhere. It's over.....here's the cover 1....2....3.... Hoskins wins! TF: Well, he still hadn't proven anything to me yet.......we'll have to see how he does in the future **result:Stevie Hoskins pinned El Tigra after a Stevie drop in 9:54 AW: What a way to start a great card. It's time for some more first round action. Let's go up to ringside to find out who's next. Ring Announcer: Introducing first, weighing in at 317 pounds from Washington DC....here is Justice Served!!!!! <Justice Served walk through the entrance in his long flowing black robe. The crowd booed as he walked to the ring. One spectator even through his soft drink at him.> Ring Announcer: And his opponent, from London, England weighing in at 290 pounds.....here is JACK WORKMAN!!!!! <Workman came out in his blue jeans and EWUK tee shirt to a loud face POP. He climbs into the ring and goes over to the far corner to work the crowd.> TF: We just got the stupid wrestlers didn't we. This guy is wasting too much time on the crowd. He should be paying attention to Justice Served. AW: Justice Served not wasting any time here as he hits Workman with a huge clothesline. Justice Served now laying the big boots on him and Workman is stuck with nowhere to go. TF: These guys should learn not to get themselves in these kind of situations. AW: Both men are up now and Justice is running into the ropes...WORKMAN HITS HIM WITH A HUGE BOOT! Workman locks in an armbar and holds Justice on the mat. TF: These guys sure love these wear down moves. I haven't seen anything that would really hurt yet. You know, in my career I've been busted open so many times it's pathetic. I remember one time when..... AW: Man, will you shut up! This is not about you. This is about real superstars. No one cares about your pathetic career.... TF: Oh yeah? And how many titles have you held? AW: Um......that's not the point.Gutwrench suplex by Workman here...... Crowd: huge POP..... TF: Listen to these losers...they don't know what real talent is. AW: Why do you always have to criticize our athletes. I don't see you out there. Workman attempting a gorrilla press here but he can't hold the big man. HUGE VERTICAL SUPLEX BY JUSTICE SERVED!!! TF: That didn't hurt.... AW: How should you know. The biggest person you ever fought was your momma..... TF: Have you ever seen my mother? She's pretty big... AW: Anyway.....let's get back to the action. Justice Served hits a big piledriver here and goes for the cover....1...2...kickout by Workman. Irish Whip by Justice here and here goes a flying clothesline.......WORKMAN DUCKS AND NO ONE'S HOME!!!!! Justice hits hard.... TF: Dumber and dumber as the matches get longer and longer..... AW: Workman sets him up on the ropes and he looks like he's going to attempt a superplex here.....NO..THE BIG MAN GRABS HIM BY HIS THROAT AND A HUGE CHOKESLAM BY JUSTICE SERVED..... TF: Ouch! AW: Justice Served now has him set up for his finisher...and he hits it, The Scales of Justice.... TF: Hey! That looked alot like my finishing move, the Playboy's Dream..... AW: And the Razor's Edge...how many other moves are there like that? Justice served for the cover....1....2....3....Justice Served will advance to the next round..... **Result: Justice Served pinned Jack Workman after the Scales of Justice in 11:23 AW: We couldn't ask for a better match to show off our big men here in EWUK....Up next we are going to show you an exhibition tag match that happened earlier this evening between Yin and Yang, the Dragons and the team of Eddy Winston and Johnny Malone. Let's go to the tape. <Cut to video.> Both teams came out to moderate crowd pops. The match was very high paced with many tags. Malone and Yang started the match off with some high paced action, but Yang's martial arts skills quickly shone through as he got the upper hand after hitting with several kicks. Yang tagged out to Yin who continued to keep his team ahead with similar martial arts moves. Yin went for a moonsault, but Malone moved and tagged out to Winston. Winston hit Yin with a small package and got a near fall. Winston then put Yin in a sleeper, but Yang came in and broke the hold. The teamwork of The Dragons then proved unparralled, as Yang tagged in and they hit Winston with a double martial arts kick to the head. Yang then hit Winston with a suplex and Yin hit with a moonsault immediately after. Malone tried to come in, but was cut off by Yang. Yang then went to the top rope and they executed their tag finisher, tranquility. **result: The Dragons beat Malone/Winston when Yang pinned Winston in 10:34 <back to real time> AW: Well that makes me expect alot in the weeks to come from our tag team division. Let's get back to ringside for more live action in the first round of the EWUK tournament. Ring Announcer: Coming to the ring at this time, from Newcastle, England at 302 pounds...here is James Harper......... <mixed Pop from the crowd as Harper walks to the ring> Ring Announcer: And his opponent, at 320 pounds from Rome, Italy....here is the Immortal.... <mixed reaction as the purple hooded figure makes his way to the ring.> TF: ooooh, scary...who does this guy think he is? AW: It doesn't matter, he's big. Both men are landing heavy shots to start this match up off. Harper gets the upper hand first and throws Immortal into the ring BUT HE TELEGRAPHS THE BACKDROP AND IS NAILED WITH A DDT.... TF: Idiot! I swear, I could beat half of these guys in under a minute..... AW: I'm sure you could...Series of bodyslams and backbreakers here by the Immortal and he's going up top... TF: Men that big shouldn't be doing this. They should leave it to small guys, like you...hehehehe AW: Shut up before I have to whup your ass....leg drop here by the Immortal but Harper is just able to get out of the way. TF: Told you. Big guys don't fly..... AW: Yeah yeah....Harper now with a well executed bodyslam. He picks him up and nails him with a powerslam followed by a gutwrench suplex. Look here Tony, another big guy going to the top ropes....Diving headbutt...AND HE HITS!!!! TF: humphh! AW: Cover and the count...1...2...thr.....The Immortal just got out of that one. Harper setting him up for a superplex but Immortal counters with a headbutt sending Harper back to the mat.. TF: Well, what goes around comes around....... AW: I guess so. Immortal hits an elbow drop from the middle rope. Immortal now hits with a backbreaker, followed by a piledriver and a side suplex. TF: PIN EM! PIN EM! C'mon ya got it won..... AW: Don't take sides......it's not right. TF: I'm not taking sides. I don't like either of these guys. I just want this match to be over. AW: I figured you'd say something like that. Harper into the ropes and the Immortal hits with a clothesline sending Harper outside the ring. Immortal follows him out and lands a series of punches and kicks before slamming him hard on the concrete. TF: Bustem open...Don't get soft on us now. WOOO....HOOOO!!! LET'S GET EXTREME! AW: Settle down...I thought you didn't like this match. TF: I changed my mind. There's nothing better than seeing two big guys beat the crap out of eachother. I love this sport. AW: You need help. Immortal sending Harper into the gaurdrail and back into the ring. He climbs back into the ring only to be met by Harper's big boot. Harper with a rollup and the count...1...2...thr.....kickout by the Immortal. TF: A big guy executes a rollup? What is this sport coming too? AW: Harper sets Immortal on the ropes. I wonder what he's thinking? TF: Holy Geez........did you see that? What was that? AW: I believe that was a lights out powerbomb. His finishing move. Here's the cover.....1....2....3. Harper didn't have a chance to get out of that. **result: The Immortal pinned James Harper after the Lights Out in 17:32 TF: And I was actually enjoying that match. Well, as you know it costs money to run a great show like this and to make the money we have to cut to the annoying thing that everyone hates when they watch television....the commercial. So go to the bathroom, get something to eat, or whatever, but don't you miss a minute of action. We'll see you in a few seconds.... <Cut to video> A black background. EWUK Action figures float across the screen. Among them are Jack Workman, the Immortal, Eddy Winston, the Terrorist, and many other superstars. Next, the official EWUK T-shirt and sweat shirt float across the screen. Voice over: For all of your EWUK memorabilia, you can either come to a EWUK event or order our Catalogue for only $39.95. Inside it you can find all of the official EWUK novelty items that you would find at one of our events. We guarantee that these items will not depreciate in value for at least 2 years. To order the catalogue call [detail withheld] . Calls cost $4.14 per minute and parents over 18 please get your kid's permission before calling. <cut back to real time. Alex Windhelm and Tony Flint are discussing something at the broadcast booth. They notice the camera and the action begins from there.> TF: I don't care what they say, it's a waste of money. When I took the James Harper action figure and held it over my cigarette lighter it wouldn't melt. Now what kid is gonna like a toy like that? AW: I don't know. Well, we're back and now for our final match of the evening. This match has the Terrorist and "Big" Bobby Turner facing off against eachother. Both men are already in the ring so let's get to the action. There's the opening bell and Bobby Turner's brawling ability is really showing through. He connects with a few kicks and punches...a choke here and there. Turner throws the Terrorist out of the ring and follows him out. Turner sends Shining Path into the ringsteps and he follows in. TF: Now I know these guys have got to be smarter than that. Look how Path just moved out of the way, allowing Turner to ram his shoulders into the ringpost. AW: Obviously he was smart enough to get out of the way. Terrorist taking advantage here with a shoulderbreaker. Now he throws "Big" Bobby Turner back into the ring. He sets Turner up for a piledriver, but Turner turns it into a backdrop. Piledriver now by Turner and here's the cover and the count.1...2...kickout. TF: Piledriver seems like the choice move of many of the superstars here tonight but they can't seem to capitalize on them at all. You'd think they would try something else. AW: Well, it looks like Turner wants to go outside the ring again. Both men are on the floor and Turner lands a vicious Heart Punch. Turner looks like he's going for the bodyslam here but Shining Path rakes his eyes. He follows it with a headbutt. TF: 5......6......7...they better get back into the ring...... AW: What the hell? Terrorist just threw Bobby Turner over the guardrail and climbed back into the ring. TF: 8.....9.....10.....Well, very unorthodox, but effective. I wonder if he knows he can't win any titles if he chooses to win by countout all the time? **result: The Terrorist defeated "Big" Bobby Turner via Countout at 3:12 AW: Well, that's all the time we have for you here tonight. Join us again next week for Wednesday Night Warriors when we come to you live again from the EWUK Colosseum in Liverpool, England. For Tony Flint and EWUK Productions, this is Alex Windhelm saying goodnight. <"Children" by Robert Miles plays as scenes from the night's action flash on the screen. We see El Tigra being Stevie Dropped by Stevie Hoskins, a spectators softdrink hitting Justice Served in the side of the head, Yin and Yang executing the tranquility, James Harper flying off of the top rope after being the reciever of the Light's Out powerbomb, and finally "Big" Bobby Turner lying stone cold in the seats of the EWUK Colosseum as the Terrorist stand arms raised in the middle of the ring.> <Overhead view of the Colosseum shows happy people emerging outside of the Colosseum> <fade to black> This program was brought to you by EWUK Productions and the Extreme Hardcore Sports Network (c) EWUK Productions 1997 (c) EHSN and Associates Comments welcome: [detail withheld]
