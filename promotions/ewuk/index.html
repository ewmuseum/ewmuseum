A Liverpool-branded Usenet fed founded in April 1997 whose own operator decided match outcomes by a peer/parliament vote among its board members. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Extreme Wrestling/United Kingdom announced itself on 12 April 1997 as a “vision”: an e-fed where, instead of one person booking every outcome, a panel of members would decide how matches ended. Its founding post admits the premise was borrowed rather than lived — “I myself am not from the UK, but it's a great place to start” — and calls for seven board members it nicknamed “the Parliament” alongside a hired Vice President and a roster of wrestlers.

Its weekly Wednesday Night Warriors broadcast opened 24 April 1997 from a purpose-built EWUK Colosseum in Liverpool, England, in front of a reported 1,200 fans, launching a bracket tournament for its own first EWUK World Championship: Stevie Hoskins beat El Tigra with a Stevie Drop, Justice Served beat Jack Workman with the Scales of Justice, and the Immortal beat James Harper with a finisher billed as the Lights Out.

Its pay-per-view Total Kaos ‘97 (30 June 1997) took the promotion to Chicago, Illinois, “the first time we've been out of the EWUK colosseum,” built around a feud between the villainous stable Immoral Majority and the rival Duo of Annihilation. The Duo ambushed Immoral Majority's dressing room before the card even started; Immoral Majority's Justice Served and the Devil won the vacant EWUK Tag Team Championship anyway, only for the Duo's own Finisher to run in afterward and drive the Devil through the arena floor with a tombstone piledriver.

By its 27 August 1997 broadcast, four days ahead of an advertised pay-per-view called Wrestle War, the Tag Team Championship had passed to the Duo of Annihilation outright, who retained it against the challenging Tony Dylan and Chip Syske with the finisher Atomic Annihilation; the same card crowned Arresting Development over Jack Workman and James Harper in tag competition. No document after that broadcast survives, and Wrestle War's own outcome is not in the surviving record.
