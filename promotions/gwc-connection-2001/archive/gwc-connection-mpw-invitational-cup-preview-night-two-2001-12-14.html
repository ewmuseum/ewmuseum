GWC/MPW Invitational Cup: Previewing Night Two

14 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
you are a loser On Fri, 14 Dec 2001 17:55:51 +1100, "Kiz" < [detail withheld] > wrote: >A tremendous first night of the GWC/MPW INVITATIONAL CUP has ensured that a >capacity crowd will be in attendance when night two takes place on SUNDAY, >DECEMBER 23 in the PATRIOT ARENA of FAIRFAX, VIRGINIA. Eight men advanced >through from an extremely tough first round and one by one, they will be >eliminated until there is only one remaining... the one who will be crowned >the 2001 GWC/MPW INVITATIONAL CUP CHAMPION. > >In the opener, "CAPTIVATING" COREY HART will get a chance to prove that last >week's win over the GWC/MPW Global Heavyweight Champion Jeff "No Mercy" >Floyd was no fluke when he takes on rising star "BLACK PRINCE" EDDIE CAIN. >Despite a controversial ending, Corey earned praised from pundits worldwide >after his victory. However, his task doesn't get any easier in the quarter >finals with a match against the undefeated Black Prince, who is quickly >building up a reputation for being one who enjoys inflicting pain on his >opponents. Several questions will be brought up in the lead-up to this >match, but it'll definitely be a great way to kick off the show. > >"THE ODYSSEY" DANTE MADISON and NOVEMBER are two of the hottest rookies on >the wrestling scene these days, but only one will advance to the semi-finals >of the GWC/MPW Invitational Cup after their quarter final match-up. Both >competitors are in excellent form and it will be one of the matches worth >watching on the night. No matter who comes out on top on the night, viewers >are guaranteed to be in for a treat... some top-notch action from two >wrestlers with very bright futures ahead of them. > >Former Global Heavyweight Champion CROSSBONES cruised through to the quarter >finals with a win over Nick Hart and nobody can doubt his abilities and >consistency, winning all but 2 of his matches in a GWC/MPW career that has >lasted well over 6 months. Looking to add to the tally will be DONALD >HUBERT, who's been nothing short of impressive since his GWC/MPW debut. A >win over Trans-Continental Champion Logan Armstrong last week confirmed his >status as a future star of the company and victory over Crossbones would all >but seal that fate. > >In the last of 4 quarter final matches, tournament favorite "COLD HEARTED" >JAMES MORGAN goes head-to-head with IGA's last hope in the tournament, >PANDEMONIUM. A hardcore match is anticipated and Morgan is confident of >winning after his success over Chris Page in the preliminaries. Never count >out Pandemonium though, he surprised many by upstaging former NEO Skyweight >Champion Psicopa at Night One and another upset victory isn't out of the >question. It might just come down to who has the better endurance levels in >the end. > >There will be four remaining after the quarter finals, two in the GWC/MPW >half of the draw and two in the "outsiders" half of the draw. The first >semi-final match will be all GWC/MPW, with the winner of "CAPTIVATING" COREY >HART and "BLACK PRINCE" EDDIE CAIN facing either CROSSBONES or DONALD HUBERT >for an opportunity to reach the final of the tournament, which is no mean >feat. > >The second semi-final will see "THE ODYSSEY" DANTE MADISON or NOVEMBER take >on either "COLD HEARTED" JAMES MORGAN or PANDEMONIUM, the outcome >determining who will be representing the outsiders in the final against one >of the four remaining GWC/MPW members. > >As part of a dual main event, fans will see a 15-man Battle Royal take place >with many familiar names participating in the event. Six of the eight First >Round losers return to Virginia, including GWC/MPW Global Heavyweight >Champion JEFF "NO MERCY" FLOYD, PSICOPA and "THE AMERICAN RAGE" CHRIS PAGE. >Also taking part are newcomers to GWC/MPW in LOGAN BARRETT, GIDEON FROST, >KTULU and STONE HARRIS as well as a couple of NEO wrestlers on trial >contracts. Names from there such as MATT SAUNDERS and COLT MONTANA will ring >a bell to the Virginia fans, as will IWF/WOW's rookie "WILDCARD" JACEN >CANADI and the ever popular STONE HARRIS. Nothing is for certain in this >Battle Royal and a winner is too difficult to predict. Whatever does go down >is sure to be entertaining, and fans can expect many twists and turns in >this match. The last man standing will be presented with a memorial >medallion on New Year's Eve as part of the GWC/MPW Awards Night. > >And in the second part of the main event and the last match of the card, >just two wrestlers will fight it out for the 2001 GWC/MPW INVITATIONAL CUP. >Whoever these two fighters are, the final is sure to be a 5 star match worth >remembering for the rest of eternity... the winner, their name etched in the >history books forever as the winner of the inaugural Invitational >Tournament. Will it be the hyped-up, so-called tournament favorite "COLD >HEARTED" JAMES MORGAN? Or will one of the underdogs like PANDEMONIUM >surprise everyone? Perhaps the trophy will remain in GWC/MPW until next year >if one of their representatives win the main event? All of these question >marks will be erased, but only in due time. Approximately three hours after >the start of the show, we will know _who_ walks out of Fairfax, Virgina as a >winner and who will walk out as a loser. By that time, everything will be >known and heroes will have been made. > >Don't miss out on seeing one of the biggest events of the year, Night Two of >the GWC/MPW Invitational Cup... only on Pay Per View. > >
