The GWC tours venues globally, as the name suggests. We are a booked league, meaning that match results are mainly decided through roleplaying, strategies and angles, plus other factors such as effort. Matches are written in play-by-play style, meaning we will utilize commentary on our shows, providing more detail, thus making the cards more enjoyable to read. The major card is Saturday Night Fury, while War of Words every Friday gives the wrestlers the opportunity to voice their opinions. Our aim is to make the federation as exciting as possible for our handlers, providing them with entertaining storylines and angles for the ultimate eWrestling experience. ##################### The Creation ##################### Planning of the Global Wrestling Connection began in mid-2000 when business tycoon Colin Fox got in touch with wrestling star Ben Tuckwell. They began planning every intricate detail of the league throughout the months leading to the grand opening, which was March 1st, 2001. Only two weeks later, the administration signed a deal with American Wrestling Promotions to run a talent exchange deal. In May, only 2 months after the original opening, Ben Tuckwell stepped down from his position as president of the GWC and in came Sebastian Manning, a more than qualified person for the job. He signed a new commissioner in "Elite Sensation" Eric Emerson, which provided the league with another edge in the admin department. With Manning's experience in the industry, Fox's financial backing and Emerson's booking and knowledge, the GWC is destined for success. ##################### Weekly Schedule ##################### Sunday: Weekly Update Monday: Off Day Tuesday: Scoops (once per month) Wednesday: Deadline (Flashes) Thursday: Deadline (Strats/In-Rings) Friday: War of Words Saturday: Saturday Night Fury =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= RULES AND REGULATIONS =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= ##################### Applications ##################### With applications, all characters must be of original creation. This means that the Global Wrestling Connection will not be accepting wrestlers with names such as "Stone Wall" Jim Austin, The Block, Scott Reiner or anything similar. Characters _may_ have the same finisher as a real wrestler, but it must have a different name. Another thing that is stressed is the use of original music. Note, all fields MUST be filled out on the apps unless marked as optional and the sample roleplay section may be left blank if you were invited to the league by one of the executive committee, otherwise it must be included. Applications will undergo a review process and you will be contacted on the status of your application within 2-3 days after being submitted. ##################### Roleplaying ##################### The GWC limits 2 flashes each week, one for War of Words and one in-ring at Saturday Night Fury. Please try to keep things realistic in flashes, so don't have your wrestler act like a superhero and fly, or run as fast as a locomotive. Although our characters are indeed fictional like Superman, we're aiming to be as realistic as possible. One of the major rules is to stay IN-CHARACTER during your interviews. This means to talk as your wrestler the entire time and not the man/woman behind him/her. Also, do not break kayfabe. Kayfabe is described in the RSPW FAQ "to mean not exposing that the business is worked." Therefore, do not call an opponent a "jobber" or refer to the "booker". These are terms rarely heard of televised wrestling to keep the illusion that wrestling is real. If you lose a match, do not blame officials unless it was scripted (eg. a fast count from the referee.) Finally, DO NOT under any circumstances include other wrestlers in your roleplays without the written permission of the respective handlers. GWC Officials reserve the right to refuse any inappropriate material that may be submitted to them. This includes prejudice towards race, gender, intellectual disabilities and sexuality. We have the final say on things and if you don't agree with the rules, please refrain from applying. ##################### Sneak Attacks ##################### Sneak attacks are allowed in moderation, but are only included if they are used to further an angle between two or more wrestlers. Normally, this is the case. Attacks that cause injury to other wrestlers, whether it be temporary of permanent, will be rejected unless there is permission from the victim's handler. All handlers are expected to "sell" attacks in their flashes and strats. Sneak attacks will never be used to affect the outcome of a match, unless it's part of the angle or the person being attacked is already booked to lose. If you have any complaints with the way your character was portrayed in an attack, please eMail the GWC administration to discuss the matter further. ##################### Other Rules ##################### If you lose a match, please don't go complaining to the administration with a head full of fire. A calm eMail to the executive committee will be sufficient. We take this rule very seriously and if it's not abided by, the offending handler will be released from the company immediately. If you have any doubts about out-roleplaying your opponent, you are probably right. If you lost, it's because administration believed you were beaten in flashes and strats. If it's due to angle, we'll try to let you know prior to the event to settle any problems. More rules will come here if we believe they are needed. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= STAFF =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= ##################### Real Staff ##################### The Global Wrestling Connection is owned and operated by Kiz, who serves as the main fedhead. He is assisted by Rob, who plays an on-screen commissioner in "Elite Sensation" Eric Emerson. However, we're always looking for more people to give us a hand and there are positions open for a Vice-President and match writers, so if you're interested, drop us a line. He noticed the potential revenue that pro-wrestling could generate after attending an event and decided that with a bit of help, he could create a company that could dominate the industry. Only time will tell if his prediction turns out to be correct. Sebastian Manning: A part of the original administration of American Wrestling Promotions, Manning was hired as GWC President after Ben Tuckwell stepped down from the position. Manning has several aspirations for the Global Wrestling Connection and with his enthusiastic style of leadership, many of these could become reality. Eric Emerson: Emerson is an extremely successful wrestler who has previously held the AWP American Heavyweight Title. His first GWC appearance came as a wrestler in a brief guest appearance, but he has recently been signed to the job as commissioner of the league. Has made a couple of big decisions already, and will be looking to leave an impact on the league in his time at the top. ##################### Announce Team ##################### Stewart Nash: The leading man on the announce team, Stewart Nash is a former journalist and a good friend of GWC Owner, Colin Fox. This is his first shot at wrestling commentary and he has been doing his research leading up to the first GWC show. "Malicious" Mel Jergin: A veteran wrestler known for his malicious tactics, Mel Jergin loves the sight of seeing someone being busted open in the ring. Mel is the color commentator for Saturday Night Fury and he generally favors the heels. He has worked as a commentator in the past for Skyscraper Championship Wrestling (SCW). ##################### Referees ##################### Danny Madden: A veteran wrestler and referee, Danny Graham knows the rules inside out. His vast knowledge and experience earned him the spot of GWC Head Referee. He prefers old-school wrestling and only officiates for normal matches. He is not afraid to call for a DQ and is the best referee in the Global Wrestling Connection and handles most of the main events. Jim Burley: Burley takes his job very seriously and is extremely strict with the rules. He is not intimidated by the wrestlers while doing his work and has the potential to become of the GWC's top referees. Zach Lewis: Zach loves the new wave of hardcore wrestling and enjoys standing back and watching wrestlers make each other bleed. He'll never call for a disqualification, even during a standard match and encourages the use of foreign objects. Ray McKeon: Ray is a newcomer to the refereeing scene and gets scared very easily. He's easily intimidated and fearful for his safety in the ring. However, he can referee any type of match and enjoys his job, somewhat. Ryan Bower: A former indy fed sensation, Bower quit wrestling due to some extreme circumstances. He's taking up the job of a referee in the Global Wrestling Connection and loves to see wrestling moves being executed to perfection. ##################### Misc. Staff ##################### Jimmy Evans Jr: A former boxing ring announcer, Evans Jr. made the switch to wrestling after being sacked from his previous job. He's in his mid-thirties and can handle the bumps he occasionally takes while sitting at ringside. Robbie Starr: Starr is an experienced in the interview department, working as a courtside announcer at college basketball games for over 4 years. He's easily intimidated by the wrestlers of the GWC, but executes his job professionally at all times. Starr is also the official spokesperson of the Global Wrestling Connection. Jenny Winston: Jenny is the female half of the interview/backstage team and has had past experience as part of the North American Wrestling Council (NAWC). She knows how to take care of herself and is not afraid to confront any of the GWC Superstars. Gary Johnson: GWC's Timekeeper. ##################### Jobbers ##################### The Global Wrestling Connection hires a small group of jobbers, who generally work in dark matches or at house shows. Below are brief descriptions of each and their win-loss record in the GWC. Jorge Perez: Jorge is a high-flying luchador out of Mexico, who showed potential in his initial try-out with the Global Wrestling Connection. He is still a rookie in the business and has a long way to go to make it to the top, serving his dues at the bottom of the league. GWC WIN-LOSS RECORD: 1-3-0 Vernon Walker: Vernon is a veteran of the sport and at age 52, he's still fairly fit. He hasn't had much success throughout his long career and yet to hold a Championship Belt. He's also failed to win a match in the last 2 years, signalling that the GWC might be his final chance to make a name for himself. GWC WIN-LOSS RECORD: 2-2-0 National Trust (Tag Team): The National Trust, consisting of Mike "First Degree" Burns and Snake Taylor were superstars on the Indy circuit but have yet to make a mark after their transition into the pro ranks. Could be a real force, on their night. GWC WIN-LOSS RECORD: 0-1-0 =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= TITLES =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= ##################### Global Heavyweight ##################### The best and most prestigious belt the Global Wrestling Connection has to offer. The holder of this belt is usually the top wrestler in the federation at that point in time. It is to be defended at least once a month. All title matches are with no time limit and top contenders are decided via rankings. PAST HOLDERS: Crossbones (current) ##################### Trans-Continental ##################### The second best title in the GWC. The Trans-Continental Title signifies you are a top class wrestler in the league, but aren't quite ready to take the next step. Most wrestlers use this belt as a stepping-stone to greater things. It is to be defended at least twice a month. All title matches are with a 20-minute time limit unless specified and top contenders are decided via rankings. PAST HOLDERS: Hypnosis, "The Real Deal" Brian Taylor (current) ##################### Junior Heavyweight ##################### The Junior Heavyweight division is a place where the high flyers can showcase their talents and this title recognizes the top man in this division. To be eligible for this division, a wrestler must be under 235 pounds to compete. All title matches are with a 20-minute time limit unless specified and top contenders are decided via rankings. PAST HOLDERS: "The Supreme Athlete" Joey Terrell (current) ##################### Global Tag Team ##################### The equivalent to the singles division Global Heavyweight Title, this championship title is reserved for the best tag team within the Global Wrestling Connection. It is to be defended at least once a month. All title matches are with no time limit and top contenders are decided via rankings. PAST HOLDERS: Inclement Weather (current) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= TELEVISION =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= ##################### Saturday Night Fury ##################### Held on Saturday Nights between 8:30 PM and 10:30 PM, Fury is the GWC's weekly card. Hosted by Stewart Nash and "Malicious" Mel Jergin, Fury usually contains anywhere up to 8 matches. If this is the case, an hour prior to the show containing dark matches written in summary style is used. Otherwise, all other matches are written in play-by-play style. ##################### War of Words ##################### War of Words is shown on Friday Nights and the main purpose of the show is to air all the flashes made by the wrestlers of the Global Wrestling Connection. ##################### Scoops ##################### Scoops, aired on the first Tuesday Night of each month is a short program that reviews the week of action produced by the Global Wrestling Connection and takes a look at some of the rumors floating around the scene at that point in time. ##################### Pay Per Views ##################### The Global Wrestling Connection holds six pay per view events each year, with one being held within each two-month cycle. The league's signature PPV event is Breaking Point, which is held in August. January/February: Survival March/April: Quake of Fire May/June: Fatal Bonds July/August: Breaking Point September/October: Annihilation [2001] November/December: GWC Invitational Cup =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= SPECIAL EVENTS =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= ##################### Annual Round Robin ##################### The first GWC champions will be decided using this format and it pits 16 men in 4 divisions. The top man from each division advances to the semi-finals. Held annually, it lasts the whole month of February, with the winner receiving a shot at the GWC Global Heavyweight Championship at Quake of Fire in April. PAST WINNERS 2001: Crossbones ##################### GWC Invitational Cup ##################### Held in November, it is the biggest event on the GWC calendar. The GWC Invitational Cup is a 32 man invitational tournament, with one half of the draw consisting of GWC wrestlers and the other half being made up of outsiders from other leagues wishing to taste some GWC action. The major purpose of the GWC Invitational Cup is to gain exposure for the league as well as attract some big names in hope of signing them to contracts.
