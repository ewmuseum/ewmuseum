[AWP/GWC] Press Conference

16 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade in: The AWP pressroom. The sounds of feet scuffling and muttering can be heard as the camera watches the various reporters take their seats. The camera then pans to Anderson Michael's, who is standing on a podium in the corner of the room.] Anderson Michael's: Ladies and Gentlemen of the press. Fans of American Wrestling Promotions. please welcome Akira Musashi. [Cameras flash as Akira Musashi walks behind a long wooden table situated in the center of the room. Akira dressed in a black business suit has his jet-black hair slicked back and he takes his seat, placing some papers on the table and shuffling through them before speaking.] Akira Musashi: First off I would like to thank you all for joining us tonight. I wont take much of your time so let's get right into this. As you know American Wrestling Promotions has been around for 5 months. In that time AWP has gone through many changes. Some for the better, some for the worse ... however, the main thing is AWP has become a wrestling company popular the world over. [Audience applause and cameras snap pictures.] Akira Musashi: With the success comes some growing pains as well as some planning for future. As some might now the talent department of the AWP has been closely following the growth and talent of its current roster. [Akira takes a sip of water.] Akira Musashi: In a meeting last week a decision was made that to keep the roster at a healthy size and to also help train some stars of tomorrow a developmental system for talent be arranged. We searched the industry for the right company to help implement this system. [Some buzz of ooh's and ahh's from the crowd.] Akira Musashi: Today we are proud to announce the alliance with Global Wrestling Connection Incorporated. The GWC will be the training ground for tomorrow's AWP superstar. Right now I would like to introduce to you the spokesperson for the GWC... Robbie Starr! [There are more mutterings in the crowd as a man waiting to the left of the room approaches the table. He is dressed in a typical tan business suit and has his light brown hair slicked back with gel. He has a huge grin on his face as he sits down at the table, right next to Akira Musashi.] Robbie Starr: Thank you Akira and let me be the first to express my appreciation at the deal signed between American Wrestling Promotions and the Global Wrestling Connection. Now I'm sure every single person in here is wondering what the hell the GWC is, and with good reason. We've only been around for 2 weeks, but a lot of planning has gone into the company and with the financial backing of Colin Fox, we're destined for success. The deal with AWP was made for several reasons, and our main goal is to promote a new generation of young wrestling superstars and get them exposure instead of having them wrestle on the undercard of a bigger promotion. We'll train the rookies of the AWP, groom them into top wrestlers and once they've conquered, they'll head over to American Wrestling Promotions to further their career. The GWC is using this deal to establish ourselves and we hope to provide a steppingstone for many, many years to come. [There is more applause from the crowd, which brings a smile to the faces of both men on stage. The camera pans over to Anderson Michael's, who is waiting to talk.] Anderson Michael's: Press, you may now ask your questions to both Akira and Robbie. Be advised however that there will only be enough time for approximately five questions to be asked, so make sure they're good ones. Please proceed. [The majority of reporters stick up their hands and Michael's points to a man in a black long sleeved dress shirt and gray slacks, who stands up. The camera then focuses on the table, at which Musashi and Starr are seated and remains there.] Man #1: Steve Carr, World of Wrestling Magazine. I've got a question for Robbie Starr about the Global Wrestling Connection and its future. Robbie, I don't want to sound negative but how can you expect the GWC to provide a steppingstone for many years to come when I'm sure you've got a goal to become the best wrestling promotion worldwide? Robbie Starr: Good question and I'll answer it as best I can. While the Global Wrestling Connection would love the prestige and honor of being the best federation in the world, we've got one simple aim at this current point in time. That aim is to establish the promotion into something that would benefit the wrestling community and I'm sure we can do that by promoting the best young superstars that we and American Wrestling Promotions have to offer. If we develop them into the best in the business, it builds up our reputation and everybody involved comes out as winners. Akira Musashi: Robbie put that well, but allow me to add to that. Whereas the GWC will be a stomping ground for future AWP talent, AWP will not stand in GWCs way of success and growth. We are partners and not competitors. Man #2: Brent Green, American Wrestling Digest. I have another question for Mr. Starr about who the GWC has signed already to put on the boots and wrestle for their company. I'm sure a lot of people wish to know this as well. Robbie Starr: Alright. Recruiting has begun quite slowly with the executive committee making only two notable signings. They are "California" Chris Morgan and Chris Caruso. Morgan is an incredible technical wrestler who will definitely leave a mark on the GWC, while Caruso has more of a power style and has the potential to dominate. Man #3: Jim Edgerton, Wrestleline.com. Akira, can you explain why you chose the Global Wrestling Connection? I'm sure there were others more established feds to seek a deal with. Akira Musashi: Of course there are others feds we could have went after. We indeed did look at some other feds. It came down to who offered more of what we were looking for. The GWC and their administration in the end had the best of everything we were looking at and I'm looking forward to a long and fruitful partnership between the two promotions. Man #4: Allen Flack, Pro Wrestling Torch. Robbie, when will the Global Wrestling Connection hold its first real show and how will its champions be decided? Robbie Starr: Our first card has been slotted in for March 31st. It will be Saturday Night Fury, held between 8:30 PM and 10:30 PM. Details on television times and venues will be released soon, once everything is finalized. This is not a set date and there is a possibility of the opening night to be pushed back to April 7. that all depends on roster size. As for champions, that will be decided in our Annual Round Robin. Four divisions consisting of four wrestlers will go up against each other, with the top man from each division going through to semifinals. The winners advance to a contest for the Global Heavyweight Championship, while the losers face off for the Trans-Continental Championship. Next question. Man #5: Brian Cosby, Ultimowrestling.com. I'm taking it this is the last question, so Akira, I'm asking you, when will this partnership officially begin? Akira Musashi: Obviously we hope to implement this partnership as soon as possible. GWC officials have been invited to attend the next AWP show Tuesday Night RAGE. We hope this will give him a chance to see our current batch of superstars in live action. From there, we will see how things progress. The GWC has also been invited to take part in the upcoming PPI Spring Revolt. We hope to see how things are by then. Robbie Starr: We'll definitely be there in the crowd at RAGE and the offer to take part in Spring Revolt will be looked at very seriously and will most likely be jumped upon with open arms. Although the King of the Cruisers Tournament is full, we'll look into promoting our league by pitting a couple of GWC stars against one another. I guess everyone will just have to wait and see what eventuates. [The camera goes back to Anderson Michael's.] Anderson Michael's: Ladies and gentlemen, thank you for attending this press conference. I apologize to those who didn't have the chance to ask their question, but expect more of this partnership to be announced in coming weeks and watch it flourish into something spectacular very soon. Once again, thanks for coming. [Akira and Robbie shake hands as the camera flashes go off. They stay in the pose for a moment until the cameras are satisfied and are applauded from the stage as the screen fades to black.] ================================================== Hey All... As you can see, Myself and Kiz have come to a working agreement between our two feds. The way things will work is, wrestlers from the AWP waiting list will go over to the GWC and show off their skills and once openings come up on our roster they'll brought into the AWP fold. Hopefully this will allow for AWP to keep a healthy roster size and lead to some interesting things down the road. As for the Global Wrestling Connection, there are spots on their roster as they are a relatively new promotion. So if you're looking for another fed to handle in, take a look at them. They have a round robin starting on March 31st to decide the champions and more information can be found from the website at http://www.efeduniverse.com/gwc/
