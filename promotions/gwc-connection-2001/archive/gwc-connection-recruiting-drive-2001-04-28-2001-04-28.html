GWC Recruiting Drive

28 April 2001

RSPWF, I just posted GWC's first pay per view on the newsgroup, entitled Quake of Fire. We've held 4 cards to date but need to recruit strongly in order for the league to survive. More information on the fed can be found at the website, located at http://www.efeduniverse.com/gwc/ and an update to it will come in the next couple of days. We've got a small, but talented roster and need to expand on it to develop the fed further.
