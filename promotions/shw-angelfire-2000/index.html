Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Simply Hardcore Wrestling opened in February 2000 as a one-man operation, its own president writing every card by hand for two weekly shows while recruiting role-players by e-mail. Its own recruitment copy called itself, without qualification, the most elite name in the sport — a boast repeated unchanged across every posting of the same pitch.

A month on, its own update reported the federation still standing and still recruiting, run entirely on the strength of one operator's own output. No card or result of its own survives; only the pitch for it does.
