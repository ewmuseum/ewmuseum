Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Extreme Fantasy Wrestling's one surviving document, dated 1 February 2002, describes a promotion returning rather than starting out — it had, in its own words, “recently was restarted back in early 2001” and was now working through “some major downsizing,” recruiting fresh talent to rebuild.

It claimed fair judging of matches and “interesting plot lines and twists,” and pointed to an archive of previous events on its own site as evidence of its record, while describing its post-downsizing roster as still “being reconfigured.”

No other document survives. What the federation looked like before this downsizing, and how or when it ultimately closed, isn't in the surviving record.
