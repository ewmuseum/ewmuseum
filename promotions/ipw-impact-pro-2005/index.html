A fantasy-roster federation on the fwrestling.com hub that ran in 2005, 2006 and 2008 under one name, with title histories for three belts, rules for roleplay-judged matches and a Pure title wrestled in rounds without pinfalls. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Impact Pro Wrestling's own site on the hub opens with the words IPW Returns and three vacant belts. Its About page tells a longer story than its dated record. By its account the fed grew out of Ultimate Championship Wrestling, opened in the summer of 2004 by two founders who parted in the spring of 2005, when one of them founded IPW with a former UCW Champion, Bear Lee Alive; the two companies worked side by side until both had closed by August 2005. IPW came back in the spring of 2006 with two brands and lasted to November 2006, had one founder buy out his partner in March 2008 and sell the company on to a Texas property tycoon, whose son is a professional wrestler. The About page also sets the fed against the real world: it says it is roughly the size of Ring of Honor and in a similar style, a local television deal, bi-weekly shows, bi-monthly pay-per-views, and a request to keep mentions of real wrestlers out of the roleplays.

What is dated is smaller. The title histories run from 1 July 2005, when the first IPW Champion won a Hostile Territory match, to 9 April 2008, the night of a last Uprising card on which a four-way ladder match crowned a champion and a Pure Title match was won, after which the belts were retired once more. Between come a 2005 reign that began on UCW's own Warfare show in the week before UCW closed, 2006 reigns for the main title and for three tag teams, and a results page naming four cards in March and April 2008. The rules ask for original wrestlers only, no women, a limit on roleplays per match and a Wednesday midnight deadline, and the Pure Championship is wrestled in three five-minute rounds with no pinfalls. No card or roleplay survives.
