Question: Do you have what it takes?

3 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The following is an ESWA promo. Read it, then join if you wish. The ESWA is a member of the National Wrestling Council. The NWC is a network of regional feds with an eventual unified title. The ESWA is the first running fed of the council, and has been going extremely strong for 2 weeks. We are looking for the best RPers RSPWF can offer. We hope, your looking for ESWA http://members.aol.com/[name withheld]/ESWA.html e-mail: [detail withheld]@aol.com ================================================== [As you open the door marked: Capone's Bar, you hear the faint sound of Italian opera pervading the establishment. As you walk up the bar, an older man with a white apron on stares at you. Finally, when he is done examining you, he points to the back of the eatery. As you walk to the back, your shoes make a loud noise against the hardwood floor. Finally, you see a man, slightly overweight, cracking nuts in his hand. He greets you] Man: Sit down, sit down.. My name is Capone, I am the commissioner of the Empire State Wrestling Association. [You pull up a chair and sit down, nervous] Capone: It's nice to make your acquiatance. Before we get down to business, I want to talk to you about my philosophy. Wait, I'm sorry, I'm being rude, you want a little something to drink? [He turns his attention away from you] Benny two espressos, yesterday! [Finally, Capone looks back at you] He's a nice guy, but a little hard of hearing. Alright, where were we? Oh yes, my philosophy. I remember going to Madison Square Garden with my father, I was nay high. It was such a big event! We got all dressed up and we went in the fancy car, to see the matches. Everyone was so excited about it! Some of the heels they were so hated, they needed security guards to walk down the aisle with them! The fans were on the edge of their feet, waiting with great anticipation for the next move. And these fans, they were top quality! They would react like nothing you see today. They let themselves become part of the match. And what do you have today? It's like a joke! The fans are cheering and cracking jokes while someone's being attacked. It's just a show to them. Like our sole purpose is for their amusement. And the wrestlers are just as bad. You can't tell good from bad with a microscope. What you have today, thats not wrestling. Thats "sports entertainment". Well I don't know. Maybe you wanna be entertained. That's fine. You can put down that espresso and leave right now. But maybe, just maybe, you wanna wrestle. You wanna be part of something special. In that case my friend, you've come to the right place! That's why I created the ESWA. Not for the money, not for the fame, but for the wrestling. And when I say wrestling, I mean it! Kid, if you are looking to go ten and zero off of a 5 move repetoire and 30 second mic work that generates less heat than an ice maker, keep looking. Now on the otherhand, if you want to work your butt off, if you want to earn your record whether its eight and two or two and eight, if you want to wrestle greuling, technical matches, if you want to make solid speeches that have the fans imaginations in the stratosphere, then you should ask what you are looking for by name: ESWA. I'll be up front with you. If you leave this table without a contract signed, I could care less. The ESWA is gonna go on whether you're a part of it, or not. Heck, theres a bunch of guys who wish they could be a part of the sort of wrestling I am talking about. That sort of magic. We're not gonna kiss up to you, we're not gonna pad your ego. If you're in this business for the competition and for the thrill of hard work paying off sign on the dotted line! Otherwise, get your ass out of my chair, and exit the premises. Are we clear? So what's it gonna be kid. Are you in?
