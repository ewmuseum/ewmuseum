ESWA 100% Pure! (part one)

22 November 1997

The camera zooms in on various signs which read: "I paid 4.50 for this, it better not suck!", "Shawn is a Smash hit!" and "DYNAMITE!". Suddenly, the letters E, S, W, A stamp out the screen and a fireball engulfs it. The famous ESWA voice says over the P.A.: WELCOME TO EMPIRE STATE WREEEEEEEEEEESTLING!!!!!":: ::The camera then cuts to the announcer's table ringside:: Jay Daily: Ladies and Gentlemen welcome to the very first Empire State Wrestling Association card! ESWA 100% Pure! We're here at the Edgemont High School Gymnasium and I'm joined with none other than former CWF personality The Shiek! The Shiek: What this? I am with you? HAHAHAHAHAHHAHAHAHAHAHAHA! You are with me! Jay Daily: Yeah well.. Folks, we have booked for you an exciting night of action in our debut. The fan favorite Shawn Smash will be in action. The superstar of Lucha Libre, El Fuego is set to make his mark. One half of the European Union, Pierre, is set to make his debut on American soil. He looks prime and ready to go!! . The Shiek: Was there ever any doubt? He is not American dog like you!! He has talent! Jay Daily: And of course folks, the man! One of the biggest names in wrestling. In our main event, Mr. Sinister!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! :::A group of fans cheer at the mentioning of this cult icon.:: The Shiek: Mr. Sinister is good! Very good! Not like you American infidels! Jay Daily: Shiek...I am not here to argue. Folks, don't change that channel..we'll be right back here on ESWA 100% Pure! :::Fade to black::: Voice Over: Fans in the Tri-State Area, if you like smash mouth, hardcore, in your face wrestling..then you'll LOVE The Empire State Wrestling Association!! Catch it at an arena near you! Call 1-800-555-ESWA or ticketmaster for details. :::The camera opens on the announcers table.:: Jay Daily: We're back Ladies and Gentlemen, on ESWA 100% Pure! wrestling. Our first match ever folks pits The Dial 0 Kid vs "The Natural" Sam Hansen. The Shiek: It is exciting matchup. The edge must go to Sam Hansen because even though he is American, he hates city folk, and that is better than nothing! HAHAHA! Jay Daily: That's real analytical Shiek... let's go the match. Ring Announcer: The following match is scheduled for 1 fall! From St. Paul Minnesota, clocking in at 6 feet, 2 inches tall and 255 pounds.... The Dial 0 Kid!!!! :::The fans begin booing loudly, as the Dial0Kid, one of the few recognizable ESWA superstars, comes out. He is carrying a telephone reciever with him and raises it in the air as he walks into the ring:: Ring Announcer: And his opponent! Standing at 6'10"and 294 pounds, the Texan with an attitude, "The Natural" Sam Hansen!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! ::The crowd is somewhat quiet, as they are not familiar with Hansen. He makes his way down with a Bull Rope, and mocking the fans at ringside. Eventually, he gets into the ring.::: As the opening bell sounds, Sam Hansen begins circling the smaller opponent. Dial0 will not have any of it, as he comes for Hansen. As the two lock up, Dial0 delivers a chop to the stomach and throws "The Natural" outside with a hiptoss!! Dial0 picks the Southerner up by the hair, and delivers a Round House Right, sending Hansen into the cades!!! Dial 0 then grabs his telephone and climbs to the top rope, as silence falls over the crowd. He immediately jumps for the dazed Texan, the telephone in his hand! As Dial is about to connect, Hansen side steps out of the way only to watch Dial COLLIDE with the barricades!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Jay Daily: What a high risk maneuver, too bad it did not pay off!! Sam Hansen can really take this opportunity to get back into the match. The Shiek: And I assure you, he WILL!! Sam Hansen, ever the opportunist, rolls Dial0 into the ring. He follows, and begins stomping on Dial. Hansen then bounces off the ropes, and launches into the air with a Leg Drop, but Dial0 rolls out of the way!! As Hansen falls, Dial rolls back over him, looking for the pin. 1 2...2 1/2 ....KICKOUT! Hansen lifts his arm in the air just in time!! Jay Daily: Wow! We have certainly got a match on our hands folks! Both men are in the ring, tired. Neither seems to be moving a muscle. 1.. 2... 3.. 4.. 5...Dial gets up!! He rises to his feet. As he does, Sam Hansen gets to his knees and lunges at Dial 0, bringing him down with a single leg take down! Hansen now plays the role of cat, and lifts Dial 0 up, who is dazed. He quickly brings The Kid back down to the mat with his Lonestar Lariat! Jay Daily: If Dial 0 loses this one, blame it on that high risk maneuver earlier, that has really taken him out of this match. Hansen grabs Dial by the neck and shoves him onto the turnbuckle, sitting him up top. he wraps the victim's neck and flies backward....HANGMAN'S NOOSE!!!!!!! Jay Daily: This could be it!! 1.... 2.....Dial0 grabs Hansen and reverses the pin attempt into a small package!!!!! 1... 2.... 2 1/2.... Sam Hansen turns it over at the last second!!!!! 1.... 2.....3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Jay Daily: Hansen gets the W!! Hansen pulls off the mild upset!!!!!!!! Ring Announcer: Your winner, via pinfa.... Sam Hansen pushes the Ring Announcer out of the ring, and grabs his Bull Rope and the mic. Sam Hansen: Now ya'll better shut up, because I ain't gonna have none of your crap. As I see it, this "Kid" tried to beat me. And for that, he will pay!!!!!! :::The crowd begins booing loudly, they are extremely unsure of what will happen next:: Sam Hansen proceeds to tie and knot one end of the bull rope around Dial 0 Kid's neck, and the other side to the top rope. He then grabs Dial0 and lifts him into the air!!!! Jay Daily: What is going on?? Commissioner Capone can not be happy about this. The Shiek: May I say one thing? HAHAHAHHAHAHAHAHAHHAHHAHAHAA!!!! Hansen throws Dial 0 Kid outside but as his feet are about to hit the floor, the rope SNAGS!!! Dial 0 begins choking and writhing in pain, as Sam Hansen pulls on the rope, tightening the knot! Sam Hansen: Now ESWA...from this point on, I suggest you shut up!!! ::laughing as Dial 0's face turns blue:: Jay Daily: This is inhumane!! Finally, Sam Hansen unties the knot on the top rope, and Dial 0 Kid drops to the concrete like a sack of potatoes. Jay Daily: ::shaking his head:: Folks, we have no choice but to cut to a commercial break. :::fade to black:: Voice Over: They come from the mean streets of Harlem, and there not about to take any shit from you. They are here for one thing, to win. And in this day and age, that's all the Rage. Doug and Brian Rage, The Raging Heat. Coming Soon.
