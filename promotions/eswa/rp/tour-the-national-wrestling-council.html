Tour the National Wrestling Council!

27 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(This is a follow up to the previous message regarding the NWC, the National Wrestling Council. Here is the roleplaying-style tour available exclusively at our site. Feel free to see the HTML-laden version at http://members.tripod.com/~nwcouncil/tour.html , or the whole NWC site at nwc.html. Enjoy your tour!) :::After deciding to be brave enough to further investigate this interesting new league you recently heard about, the NWC, you find yourself in front of the newly constructed NWC headquarters on Peachtree South in downtown Atlanta, GA:::: ::You are accompanied by your friend who referred you, NWC Executive Director Chris Schukar. He will assist you in your tour of the National Wrestling Council HQ and he walks straight in the door:: A Secretary chimes out with "Hello, Mr. Schukar! Who is that you've brought with you? He looks cut.. is this another recruit?" Chris says "Possibly, Leanne. He's just visiting for now.. hopefully the regionals won't put too much pressure on him." "That I would not count on, Boss," Leanne chimes in. "You have seen some of the tactics Capone will try, not to mention Lucente. The regions are fiercely swallowing up all sorts of fabulous talents.. I estimate that you w..." :::Just as you start to take interest in the secretary's spiel, Schukar cuts her off and subtly points at his watch::: Chris: C'mon. I have some explaining to do. You must be wondering what the NWC is all about. Take a look at these great photos along the hallway. :::He leads you into a hallway where a chill of familiarity runs down your back as you see such wrestling greets as Lou Thesz, Dick "The Bruiser" and the Brisco Brothers. One picture in specific shows a young boy standing next to a smiling "Nature Boy" Ric Flair. The caption underneath reads: "Executive Director Schukar's Best Day" and you notice Chris duck his head a little and grin::: Chris: That's a personal fave. You'll see much of the same along the hallway. Nostalgia from the days when wrestling itself really mattered. There was blood, but it was a fundamental part of the game, almost taken for granted. I fondly remember how the old NWA was, with its regions where Flair and company would travel and make appearances all over. THAT is a Golden Age. ::You nod somewhat, but then start to get a look of boredom on your face:: Chris: Yes, I know. You're wondering what this "Golden Age" has to do with anything, right? I can read that miles away. The old NWA is what we are patterning THIS NWC idea from. We have independent regions all throughout the nation, possessing a working agreement to cooperate in favor of everyone succeeding as a whole. The National Wrestling Council is in the business of making an atmosphere just like these old days, with a modern flare, of course. ::::You continue to follow Chris down the hall, where you see a brilliant logo on a large oak door that says "Big Apple Wrestling Federation." The logo has a large apple with several skyscrapers rising from behind it. Chris nods at you as you enter the doorway:::: "The doors are all the way down this wing of the building," Chris says to you as he walks away. "Come find me in my office when you've toured 'em." :::You then walk into the BAWF Office. Things look rather messy and unorganized. BAWF Commissioner Magic Ref seems a bit surprised and unprepared for your sudden visit.::: "Give me a minute or two, I'm not ready to begin." "In the meantime," he tells you, "look around and 'familiarize yourself with the environment.'" :::As Magic fumbles with some papers scattered all over his desk, you decide to browse around. Right away, something hits your eye. On the wall is a framed certificate. It reads, "Magic Ref - WWA Employee of the Month." Leaning on the wall, off to the side, is a dresser. The only objects on top of the dresser are two trophies. One reads "CWF RUSH - Program of the Month, November 1997," and the other, "CWF RUSH - Program of the Month, September 1997."::: :::Finally, Magic calls you over. The wood of his desk is now visible, and he is holding a pile of what looks to be thousands of sheets of paper in his hand. He slides out the chair facing his desk, and motions for you to sit. You sit in the chair and shake his hand.:::: "So," he says to you. "I hear you're interested in signing a contract to work in the Big Apple!" :::Trying to further interest Magic into signing you, you slowly nod your head, purposely conveying a mysterious quality to your posture.::: "Well," he says, "Let's go over the specifics." :::Magic takes a deep breath, runs his eyes over the top piece of paper resting in his hands, and begins to hastily read it, hardly articulating his speech. You can tell he wants to get this over with; and so do you. The hot Georgia sun peers into Magic's window and begins to beat on you like a drum.::: :::Sweat dripping down your face disorients you to a point where you can no longer even make out what Magic is saying. This is extremely discombobulating, for you do not want to give Magic the impression that you blatantly are not paying attention. You look at him as he reads the papers. You see his lips running a mile a minute, but you hear nothing. It appears as if he is almost done going through the pile of papers.::: :::All of a sudden, the sound of a sonic boom as if you just broke Mach One explodes in your ears.::: "So," proclaims Magic. "Sign on the dotted line!" :::Not knowing what to make of anything, you cheerfully smile and sign on the dotted line. You shake Magic's hand, get up from the chair, turn around, and walk out of the door.::: :::Next down the line is a door with a giant mag-wheel on it, with a spraypainted type logo of "MCW" on it. The door is somewhat open::: ::The nameplate on the door says, "Al Lucente - MCW Atlanta Office":: ::From inside the room a voice can be heard:: "COME IN!" ::Opening the door, you realize that, aside from several huge boxes sitting in the corner, the room is almost empty. All that's in view is a lone, bare desk, and a chair on either side of it. A short, fat, bald, middle-aged, "Danny Devito-ish" man can be seen standing with his back to you, and his face towards the window.:: "Lucente's the name... Al Lucente... Take a seat", the man says, as he continues to look out the window. "Wrestling...", Lucente sighs, "You know something... I love wrestling. It's my passion. I especially love the structure of the 'Old Generation' of pro wrestling. McMahon and Bischoff, they can keep their 'New Generation' mumbo-jumbo. Style is NOT more important than substance, nor will it ever be." ::Lucente starts moving some of the boxes around, until he finds the one he wants:: "Ahhhh, here it is!" ::After thumbing through the box for a minute, he comes across a photo album. He begins to look through it, and as he does, a huge smile comes across his face:: He continues.."The golden age of wrestling... NO SUPER, NO ULTRA, NO EXTREME, NO MEGA... Just plain, ole' fashioned, bare-knuckled wrestling." ::Upon seeing your curiosity peaked, Lucente hands you the photo album and continues to talk as you look through the nostalgia:: "Men like 'Strangler' Lewis, Lou Thesz, Bruno Sammartino, Verne Gagne, Dick 'The Bruiser', Harley Race, The Funks, The Vachons, and even up into the '80's with men like Dusty Rhodes, Ric Flair, and Rick Steamboat. Wrestling like it OUGHTA be." ::A moment of silence ensues. Lucente looks on as you continue to flip through the pages. He sees the expression on your face and smiles:: Al says, "Kid, lemme ask you a question, and I need for you to be completely honest with me, because your career just might depend on it. Come a bit closer." ::Setting the album aside, you look up and into his eyes and, while still in your seat, you move a little closer to him. He does the same.:: "Do you REALLY wanna be a pro wrestler?" ::Another moment of silence. Then a very confident nod from you is next. Lucente smiles and extends his hand, and you shake it:: A-OK... Well then, kid... Welcome to Motor City Wrestling! ::You grin and stand up, smiling at Lucente. The hallway awaits you again, as you move towards the next door down. As you walk up to the door, you see the words:: "Lone Star Wrestling" Commissioner Cooper ::What's funny is that hanging on the edge of the "r" in Cooper, there seems to be a small cowboy hat. You chuckle at the sight of this. But then you remember, that this "joke" could very well be the company you're made famous in.:: ::You again put on your game face and knock on the door with utmost confidence.:: The voice from inside is heard "Come on in, boy!" ::You slowly, but confidently turn the doorknob and prepare yourself for the task that lies before you. As you step foot into the office, you seemed astonished at the sight. Everywhere you turn, there are various photographs, awards, trophies, even bullropes hanging by the dozens.:: ::You take a moment to glance at the room. The tall, slender man that sits before you behind a big, wooden desk just stares at you with half a smile.:: He then utters the words, "Well, how're ya', boy?" ::You're still a little disorientated. You continue to look around the room.:: "Hey boy, I said how're ya'?" You finally turn to him, "Uh?", you reply. I said, "How're ya', boy?" "Oh, I'm fine", you retort. "Well, c'mon! Sit on down, boy!", he says. :::You slowly take a seat in front of him. He plops himself back down in his black leather chair. The shadow of his black cowboy hat covers his eyes. He lowers his cowboy hat a little and picks up a straw from his desk.::: :::He brings up to his mouth and chews on the straw for a few seconds. You pay no attention to him, as a wild photograph of a Texas Death Match between the legendary Terry Funk and "Cowboy" Bob Orton has caught your eye.::: "Yeah, ya' like that one, eh?", he says. :::You nod your head::: He continues, "That is how we like to run it down here in Lone Star Wrestling. Boy, like I tell everyone else, I'll make this short and sweet! Do you wanna' wrassle' for Lone Star Wrestling?" :::A familiar moment of silence fills the room. You stare deep into his eyes. You then look around the room at the amazing awards and photographs. You take a look behind Commissioner Cooper, on the wall you can see a bloodied bullrope with a spur attached to it.::: You look back into Cooper's eyes and utter the sole word, "Yes." "That is all I needed to know, kid. By the look in your eyes, I know you'll one day be a superstar!", he says. :::He then slides a contract in front of you, he hands you a pen. You take the pen and stare contentedly at the contract.::: Cooper says, "All ya' gotta' do is sign the dotted line, kid." :::You glance deep at the contract and quickly sign your name on the dotted line.::: He ends by confidently stating, "Welcome to Lone Star Wrestling, superstar." :::You stand back up, shake the man's hand and leave the office only to look into the next one. Before you enter, you glance towards the end of the hall and see the office of the NWC Executive Director, where you will end up at the conclusion of your tour::: ::As you grin and open the door marked: CAPONE'S BAR, you hear the faint sound of Italian opera in the establishment. As you walk up the bar, an older man with a white apron on stares at you. When he is done examining you, he points to the back of the eatery. As you walk to the back, your shoes make a loud noise against the hardwood floor. Finally, you see a man, slightly overweight, cracking nuts in his hand. He greets you:: Man: Sit down, sit down.. My name is...Capone. I am the commissioner of the Empire State Wrestling Association. :::You pull up a chair and sit down, nervous::: Capone continues.. "It's nice to make your acquaintance. Before we get down to business, I want to talk to you about my philosophy. Wait, I'm sorry, I'm being rude, you want a little something to drink?" ::He turns his attention away from you:: "Benny two espressos, yesterday!" ::Finally, Capone looks back at you:: "He's a nice guy, but a little hard of hearing. All right, where were we? Oh yes, my philosophy." Capone leans back in his chair.. "I remember going to Madison Square Garden with my father, I was nay high. It was such a big event! We got all dressed up and we went in the fancy car to see the matches. Everyone was so riled up about it. Some of the heels they were so hated, they needed security guards to walk down the aisle with them!" "The fans were on the edge of their feet, waiting with great anticipation for the next move. And these fans, they were top quality. They would react like nothing you see today. They let themselves become part of the match." He continues...."And what do you have today? The fans are cheering and cracking jokes while someone's being attacked. It's just a show to them. Like our sole purpose is for their amusement. And the wrestlers are just as bad. You can't tell good from bad with a microscope. What you have today, that's not wrestling. That is "sports entertainment". Well I don't know.... Maybe they wanna be entertained, that's fine. You can put down that espresso and leave right now. But maybe, just maybe, you wanna wrestle. You wanna be part of something special. In that case my friend, you've come to the right place!" As Capone getsmore heated, he says.."That's why I created the ESWA. Not for the money, not for the fame, but for the wrestling. Kid, if you are looking to go 10-0 off of a 5 move repertoire and 30 second mic work that generates less heat than an ice maker, keep looking. Now on the otherhand, if you want to work your butt off, if you want to earn your record whether its eight and two or two and eight, if you want to wrestle grueling, technical matches, if you want to make solid speeches that have the fans imaginations in the stratosphere, then you should ask what you are looking for by the name: ESWA." "I'll be up front with you. If you leave this table without a contract signed, I could care less. The ESWA is gonna go on whether you're a part of it or not. Heck, there's a bunch of guys who wish they could be a part of the sort of wrestling I am talking about. That sort of magic. We're not gonna kiss up to you, and we're not gonna pad your ego. If you're in this business for the competition and for the thrill of hard work paying off sign on the dotted line! Otherwise, get your ass out of my chair, and exit the premises. Are we clear? So what's it gonna be kid. Are you in?" :::As he finishes his long, poignant speech, you sign his piece of paper and he grins at you, with a look of justification. You then, just as you did before, get up and leave the room only to find the next door. As you approach it, it appears to be more of a lockerroom door. It reads simply, "CCW." ::: :::In the local gym this morning, they said the most intimidating part was taking that long walk down the side hall of James Naismith's gym in the CCW 'offices' at NWC Headquarters. Walking down the hallway with pictures of Naismith's wrestling accomplishments as well as pictures of other legends, you realize how mistaken they were. The lady at the front desk looks up at you and goes back to work without another thought::: "Ummm...I'm...uhh...I'm here to see Mr. Naismith about a tryout with..." A Receptionist looks at you and says,"Down the hall, third door on the left." :::The walk takes a few more minutes as your eye is caught by some other framed pictures. Finally, the desk of James Naismith is staring you down like an over-confident opponent. Hesitation first, then a knock can be heard throughout the area::: "Mr. Naismith, I...um...am here to learn about the CCW. My name is..." A janitor to your left notices and says, "...if you're looking for James, he's in the gym working out. Off to the right." :::"Great", you think to yourself. "I must have sounded like a fool talking to an empty room." Sweat starts pouring down as nerves never felt before start burning. As you arrive in the gym area, you finally see him. Wiping sweat away from his brow, Naismith prepares for another set of isolation curls. He looks up and catches you standing there::: JN: Come in. Pull up a seat. :::A few more nervous steps and finally the comfort of being seated sends out a reassuring feeling::: JN: Chris mentioned you. I've seen your tapes. You have talent. Unfortunately, not everyone with talent can make it in this sport. You see this? :::Gestures to the gym::: JN: In here, all time stops. Minutes become hours. Hours become days. Days become weeks. With all that, there are still no guarantees. Are you prepared to risk failure? Are you prepared to watch every dream you've had shattered by cold, hard reality? :::Slowly, you nod::: JN: Once you've accepted that, only then can you be truly great. This is the place to start. Canada is a great place to wrestle....the fans have always been known to treat wrestlers here like adopted children. Does that reassure you? ::Again, a nod::: JN: Keep that in mind when you're in the ring every night. There's no room for mediocrity. You're there for two reasons. 1) To wrestle your damnedest and win matches, and 2) to put on a good show for the fans who paid to see this. Laziness will not be accepted, both by me and by the fans. If I sign you right now and put you in the ring under the banner of the CCW, will you guarantee me that you'll work every night like it's for the NWC World Title? :::Suddenly, the weight of his words starts coming down on you. The realization of the responsibility this contract would mean...You shake it off and nod again::: JN: That's what I like to hear. Remember, every wrestler who ever lived started in a small region. Wrestling is like any other business. You pay your dues. You get whatever you put into it back tenfold. :::Naismith stops and stares at you for a second. Finally, after finding in your eyes whatever it was he was looking for, he extends a hand::: JN: Welcome to Canadian Championship Wrestling. Make me proud. ::::You beam at the message you've heard from Mr. Naismith and exit the gym area. Right as you make it out the door of the CCW area, Chris Schukar is standing directly in front of you with a wide smile:::: Chris pats you on the back and says... "Lucente just called.. then Cooper, and Magic too, and Capone a second ago. All saying they got a commitment from you. I guess you played 'em all for now, kiddo." ::You shrug your shoulders:: Chris looks at you and continues. "I understand how it is when you're green. These league heads all know how to sweet talk ya, and all of them have WONDERFUL organizations going for them that any person in their right mind would want to join. I doubt I could ever turn any of them down myself." "Especially Naismith... he'd rip me in half." ::You laugh out of politeness as Schukar leads you down the hall to the Executive Director's office. He cordially opens the door for you as you waltz in and take a seat. He swings around to his desk and sits as well:: "Hey, kid, its understandable. Don't look so glum. I told 'em all that you really didn't know what you were doing quite yet. You needed to get a look at every NWC affiliate and get a taste of what each one was about. That's what this little tour was for, pal. Consider yourself blessed to have been in these hallways, seeing such an eclectic group of men. From businessmen, to nostalgic fans, to former world champs, all cooperating with each other's leagues." ::Schukar pauses as he begins to notice you hinging on his words:: "A work of beauty, if you ask me." :::He takes a spin in his chair, grinning from ear to ear::: Schukar follows with..."You know you've got the stuff. I know it, Capone, Cooper, Lucente, Magic, and Naismith all know it. They would all love to have you. But the matter remains, you are GOING to work for the National Wrestling Council, and you will represent us and our ideals where. After understanding that, the simple idea of choosing what region to work under is a formality." :::You nod your head uneasily, and he shoots you a strange look::: "Don't worry," Chris adds. "The fact of the matter is this, and it will cut right to the heart of the issue. We represent what wrestling should be all about. We stand for the morals and the principles which guided this sport to the fame it has today, whether people would have you to believe it or not. Wrestling is the name on the marquis, as Gordon Solie said time and time again. If you want to be known forever and a day as a WRESTLER, the NWC is the place to be." :::You lean over the edge of your seat, wanting to know more::: Chris notices your enthusiasm and jumps on it, by saying "Okay, man, I get where you're coming from. You haven't said a whole lot, but I can read people. You want to know a little bit more about the NWC. You are considering joining. Well, check out our website at http://members.tripod.com/~nwcouncil/nwc.html and scout out the Affiliate Leagues from there. Then come back to the NWC site and fill out the application. I will PERSONALLY handle the transmission and approval of your application. We handle the best in e-wrestling, and we hope you want to add your name to the prestige as it grows." ::You flash a quick smile at that last comment:: "Hey, I'm just that nice of a guy! Thank me later. But for now, I think you have some browsing to do... make your decision wisely, but be assured that you will grow, prosper, and excel anywhere you go under the NWC banner. Take care." Chris Schukar - Handler of Jon Atlas The National Wrestling Council http://members.tripod.com/~nwcouncil/nwc.html "Whenever I feel like exercising, I just lie down until the feeling goes away.."
