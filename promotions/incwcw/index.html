Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Internet Network Championship World Class Wrestling opened on rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 29 April 1997, self-hosted at a GeoCities address, and pitched itself as a booked fed running a single Sunday card, Sunday Night Destruction, decided by role-play quality rather than a simulator: “the number of role-plays sent in doesn't matter at all, it is the quality of the role-plays.” It opened three titles — a World, a Regional, and a World Tag Team Championship — and welcomed both fantasy and real wrestlers.

By 4 May its own Control Center bulletin carried the first sign of a roster: three wrestlers, Landslide, Lightning Kid, and The Black Knight, all ranked 0–0, with the first Sunday Night Destruction pencilled for 17 May and challenges due in by 8 May. The same day, a second notice walked that date back: “Sorry! The first INCWCW Card will be on MAY 11! I had my dates mixed up.”

No card, result, or further roster update survives past that correction. Whether the fed's own first Sunday Night Destruction, however it was finally dated, ever actually ran isn't in the surviving record; the trail ends with three unranked wrestlers waiting for a bell that leaves no further trace.
