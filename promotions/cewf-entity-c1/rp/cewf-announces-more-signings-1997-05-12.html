CEWF announces more signings!

12 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
3 more wrestlers have been signed making 6 total, i'd like to get 16 to start an innovative triple knockout tournament to decide three singles champions. Let's here from the new guys! -------------- First here's the wrestling psychologist Dr. Sigmund E. Xavier -------------- Dr. Sigmund E. Xavier: For a long time, it seemed that the world of professional wrestling seemed a haven for the mentally disturbed. In fact the poor souls thrived. It was a case of the inmates running the asylum. Now, my skills will bring sanity back to this sport. My psychoanalytic genius may be the only hope for so many. Remember I'm here to help. ---------------- That guy is a little different...... Now lets here from the mysterious Prophet. ---------------- Prophet: My coming has been preordained. I am he whose wrestling boot others are not worthy to untie. I will lead the CEWF out of the darkness into a new era. Under my rule, the CEWF will become the premiere wrestling federation. ----------------- Also signed on is Pulsulating Pepe who has no comments at this time.
