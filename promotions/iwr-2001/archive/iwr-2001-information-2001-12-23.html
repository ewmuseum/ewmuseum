IWR Innovative Wrestling Revolution This is a roleplaying federation. The contests will largely be based on the strength of your roleplays. The Executive Council will be looking for creative and interesting roleplays that are of certain length.Keep in mind that the quality of the roleplay is more important than the quantity of the roleplay. We do not want to read 400 line roleplays that say the same thing over and over again and honestly, neither will any of the other competitors.
Swearing will be permitted in roleplays but keep in mind, overuse of expletives in your segments show a lack of creativity on your part and is just simply not good technique.
Racial slurs will not be permitted in any roleplays under any circumstances and will result in termination.
No physical attacks on other wrestlers will be permitted in segments as well. This organization is intended for fake wrestlers only. Being The Rock or Triple H may be great for some of you, but the Executive Council wants to see more creativity out of you than "If you smell-la-la-la what The Rock is cookin'?"
Use your noodles to make a character and then it'll be "Time to play the game." All roleplayers must E-mail their roleplays to the entire roster or else it may not be counted. If anyone has been added to the roster, the Executive Council will notify the roster immediately, so it shouldn't be hard to keep up with the changes.
All roleplayers must send in their roleplays by the deadline set by the Executive Council. Any roleplays sent after that will not be counted except in extreme circumstances.
If a roleplayer fails to roleplay within a two month period, that roleplayer will be released from the roster. If the roleplayer has a manager or valet, the
Executive Council reserves the right to keep that manager or valet on the roster and utilize that manager or valet however the Executive Council seems fit to use that character.
Remember that roleplaying is just for fun and entertainment and by no means is it personal. Therefore, don't take things personal because what is said in roleplays has no bearing on life, so don't take things personal and complain about it, simply laugh about it and one up your opponent by bashing him or her with a better roleplay.
In most cases if you request a match for a certain event, you will be granted that match, unless you did not allow a sufficient amount of time for the
Executive Council to make that happen. If you are going to be gone or unable to roleplay for any amount of time, it is your responsibility to inform the Executive Council. Do not assume that because you do not roleplay that you will not be scheduled for matches. Advanced notice, such as, a few days before you leave is needed but preferably let the
Executive Council know you will be MIA A.S.A.P Do not wait until after you are scheduled for a match to inform the Executive Council you will be MIA.
Execptions can and will be made because the Council knows emergencies can come up but make sure you discuss these matters with the Executive Council.
It is all right if you complain in a roleplay about losing a match but don't E-mail the Executive Council to complain about a loss or whatever your complaint is about because the Executive Council will not tolerate such behavior.
Cards will be run every two weeks. This is a casual E-Fed intended for people who do not have time to write up roleplays every single day. It's intended to be for your enjoyment and ours as well. The members of the Executive Council either have full time jobs or are college students who have jobs.
The Executive Council will try and pack the shows with wrestling action, angles and interviews to the best of our abilities and we expect you to roleplay to the best of your ability.
Roleplayers may request an on air interview for a particualr show if they would like, but the Executive Council must review and edit the interview you submit if they feel it needs editing.
Pay Per Views will be held every three months and will be full out spectacles worthy of the effort you put into them.
Recapped House Shows will also occur when the Executive Council feels they are appropriate. They do not require roleplays and will be based on and indicative of roleplaying trends at that time. The card for House Shows will be posted as soon as the final card has been set.
Most importantly...HAVE FUN! Championships: World (Heavyweight) Championship: This is your typical top of the line title. The grandaddy of them all. When a formula works, why change it? If you want to be the top dog in the IWR, this is the title you want to earn.
Tag Team Championship: Another basic tried and true championship. Who is the most dynamic duo in the IWR? This title answers that question and a whole lot more. It can belong to two singles wrestlers teamed up under strange circumstances or a team that competes together most often,so take a chance on this accolade, if you dare.
Other Championships: We'd prefer not to oversaturate our organization with titles to begin with but when the membership is stable, we will add more championships. Suggestions are always appreciated but not necessarily used. We like your input because even though we are "running" the fed, you make it happen just as much as we do.
Shows: Whatever It Takes: The IWR's huge bi-weekly television program featuring the best and the biggest matches going on in the organization. This is the show where the true superstars come out to shine and do whatever it takes to win while looking good doing it. It takes place on Tuesday nights and is written out in full detail with the three person announcing crew of David Mercedes and two other members to be named later.
Animosity: The IWR's program spotlighting the results of house shows in recapped format. These are going to be smaller programs and will most likely feature some tag team and six-man contests as main events more often then will
Whatever It Takes. It will also possibly feature an interview/interaction segment with one IWR superstar during the show. They will either occur once a week on Sundays or on the Sunday after Whatever It Takes.
