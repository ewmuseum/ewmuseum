Right now we are in the middle of a crisis here at OCW headquaters. We are being invaded by the HAF. They want to takeover our league. We need man support. The OCW is mainly open to AOLers, but we could work in you other guys. We need fantasy wrestlers, no real ones. All of the good ones have been taken. If interested, e-mail me at [detail withheld] . Note: I am not the President of OCW, in fact I'm not even the VP. I'm just the best roleplayer they have. OCW needs your help!
