RAW UK was originally to be a small regional fed, to be named British Championship
Wrestling. However, passion and coincidence led to it being given a RAW franchise, and to being named, imaginatively, RAW
UK. The UK thing really makes no difference to the fed, except perhaps a few culture references. But we have to put up with Ju-
Jubes in your feds, so…RAW UK, whilst not operating at a WWF/WCW level, will be operating just above the regional e-fed level, more on an ECW level. Any wrestlers showing amazing potential and ability will, if the so wish, be promoted to RAW itself.
Sing hosannas. Other things: When it comes to rules, RAW uses this philosophy: THINK. Use common sense. Before you send out a possibly offensive roleplay, re-read it and try and see if anybody will be bothered by it. Homophobia, racism, or misogyny will be treated with the utmost discipline. Anyone attempting to use such material in roleplays will first be given a warning, and if any more occurrences happen after that, will be fired, with no exceptions.
Never, e-e-ever use another person's characters in a roleplay, in-ring, etc. without the handler's permission!!!
Example, don't have Wrestler X's valet appear in your RP if Wrestler X's handler hasn't approved it. There will be punishments handed down for this. You can not attack any named RAW UK staff (commentators, interviewers, etc) without the permission of either of the
Presidents. Chances are that we'll always give you the OK but we need to know about it first!
Once again, just use common sense. ______________________________ ~II~ Who's Behind All This? ______________________________ Two main individuals operate RAW UK. --Fed Co-President-- [detail withheld] [name withheld] (also known as Dom or “That fat I-Tie f*cker”) is one half of the gestalt entity that runs RAW UK, taking the Jared Mayhew in-game role. Dom has over two whole years of e-wrestling experience (wow), most notably as Obsessive
Compulsive Disorder in both RAW and AWMC. Dom’s basically your bog-standard Ben Folds Five obsessive of Sicilian descent, and spends his time laughing at dead celebrity jokes. He hates
Goths, Soul Asylum’s 1993 hit “Runaway Train”, and the early 20th century author DH Lawrence. He doesn’t get out much either. --Fed Co-President-- [name withheld] [detail withheld] [name withheld] is a builder’s apprentice. [name withheld]- aspiring rock critic, neo-Marxist, and general working class intellect. [name withheld]- builder’s apprentice. Hmm. [name withheld] regularly quotes Claude Levi Strauss, [name withheld]
likes Blink 182. Guess which one of the partnership is the brains of the operation? ______________________________ ~III~ Participation ______________________________ RAW UK plans to focus heavily on storyline development. We want stories and we want heat. They co-exist. Plain and simple. We want wrestlers who can deliver that heat. In order to do that, you must roleplay. RP'ing helps your chances of winning enormously. In fact, if you don't RP, you don't win. To sum
up, you MUST RP. But why wouldn't you? It's all part of the fun, and if you don't want to be part of the fun, what the hell are you doing here? Please note that we view quality over quantity. One above average RP will beat 10 average RPs.
If you really want to win a match, strats (for the card) are important. In-rings are not necessary (although appreciated)
but strats are. They help the match writers piece together the match. Strats are important. If you don’t strat, you probably won’t win. In order of importance in the fed, the needs go like this: RPs, strats, angle participation, and general effort. All of these are important if you want to do well in RAW UK.
______________________________ ~IV~ Cards, PPV's, Etc. ______________________________ RAW UK’s weekly card is to be RAW UK Bedlam, held each Monday night (unless there was a PPV the night before). This match will have all the usual features of any wrestling flagship.
PPV’s themselves usually occur every 4-8 weeks, depending on how things are going at the time. They’ll probably have really good names, like, I dunno, Nuclear Winter or something.
______________________________ ~V~ Who's Who? ______________________________ Fictional faces - RAW UK Co-President: Jared Mayhew Jared Mayhew is the son of a successful businessman who, after seeing the current burgeoning popularity of pro-wrestling, and also noting that there was very little exploiting of this in the
UK, put up a large section of his father’s cash in order to set up the UK branch of RAW, in order to capitalise on this. As a result, he cares very little about the promotion, its fans, the wrestlers, or the backstage staff, instead preferring to make annoying smart-arsed comments about it
RAW UK Co-President: James Ryder James Ryder has long been a massive wrestling fan, and a chance encounter with Mayhew led to him being given a post as RAW UK co- president. Despite the different opinions between him and
Jared, Ryder continues to work with him, as it allows him to fulfil his dream, that of running a wrestling federation. Ryder cares for the fans, and will do anything in order to put on a good show for them.
--RAW Ringside Announcers:-- Alan Chevron: Play by play commentator. Has been involved, as a booker, in the British wrestling scene for over 25 years now, and remembers with fondness Big Daddy, Giant Haystacks, and other useless dead one move wonders. He now works for RAW UK, but still hankers for the old days, and views with distrust anything “hardcore”. He tries to treat all wrestlers equally, but he will tend to side with the faces, and those who don’t break the rules.
“One Man Cavalry” DC McLean: McLean was a large name in both Japanese junk feds and the indie extreme scene in America up until mid 1999, when a serious injury forced him to leave the grap game. Whilst undergoing surgery in a Harley Street clinic, he was approached by Ryder with a view to take up an announcers position. Still only in his 20s, DC is a massive fan of all things hardcore, and also isn’t too big a fan of the UK itself.
Inevitably, he tends to side with the heels. RAW UK Ring Announcer: Casey Parnell Talentless bimbo ring announcer, employed solely to give the fans something to look at RAW UK Backstage Interviewer: Nick Rivers Young journalist wannabe. Does most of RAW UK’s on-scene interviews, whether he wants to or not. Weedy, gets pushed about quite a lot, doesn’t know much about wrestling.
______________________________ ~VI~ What Can You Win? ______________________________ There are three major singles titles in RAW UK. Those titles are as follows... RAW UK Heavyweight Champion: Obviously, this is the main title in the fed. This will be held by the best wrestler in the fed at the time, and as such is what most wrestlers aspire to.
RAW UK TV Champion: The secondary title in the fed. This will be held by a wrestler on their way up to the top level of the fed, usually someone who, although showing lots of promise, hasn’t quite hit top level yet.
RAW UK Tag Titles: Go on, have a guess what these titles are.
