Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

RAW UK posted its own founding FAQ and Application on 20 May 2000, describing itself in its own words as "a sister and feeder fed to the more famous RAW e-fed" — a UK branch that had originally been conceived as a small regional promotion before being folded into the larger federation's franchise instead.

The federation ran a weekly card, Bedlam, and named three singles titles of its own: a heavyweight championship, a television title, and a tag division. Standout wrestlers were promised a path up to the parent federation itself once they had proven themselves.

A revised edition of the FAQ, posted at the end of August 2000, named a single sitting president in place of the pair the federation had opened with, alongside new fictional commentary and camera staff. The federation's own webpage went up the following month.

A surviving two-hour special, Bedlam, was broadcast live from Cambridge on 20 October 2000, the last card held in the surviving record. No closing statement for the federation survives.
