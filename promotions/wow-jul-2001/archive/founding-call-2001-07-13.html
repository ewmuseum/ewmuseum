WOW: Founding Call

13 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rest in Peace... Or so you thought. In the end, a fellow by the name of David McLane sold WOW Women of Wrestling. Picked up by a powerful businesswoman named Contessa St. Dumas, WOW intends to be back on the air in minimal time. Ms. St. Dumas has promised the world that she will compile the elite WOW wrestlers, featuring them on the new show. Please note that ROLEPLAYING in this league will be of paramount importance. Please at least know a decent amount about the character that you are applying with. Available characters are: BECKIE CHARLIE DAVIDSON BRONCO BILLIE DANGER BOOM BOOM DELTA LOTTA PAIN CALIENTE LOCA FARAH DISCIPLINARIAN JADE EZ RIDER LOTUS JACKLYN HYDE HEATHER STEELE JANE BLONDE LANA STAR JUNGLE GRRL PATTY PIZZAZ POISON ROXY POWERS ICE COLD SELINA MAJORS RIOT TERRI GOLD SLAM DUNK SANDY THUG SUMMER NIKKI LAW WENDI WHEELS CHRISTIE ORDER If your favorite WOW wrestler was left out by mistake, then please let me know and we'll see what we can do to get her in. Please remember, that although roleplaying and storyline will dictate MUCH of the outcome - try to remember how that wrestler was in WOW. For instance, we all know that Summer wouldn't be the WOW Champion anytime soon. That may change over time, however, but it's a ways off. This is just a warning: Beware of what wrestler you choose for the above reason. For now, all I require from potential handlers is the following: Your Name: Email Address: Wrestler you are applying for: What you know about this wrestler: Sample roleplay of SOME type: I really want this to be a good game, not one that folds in a week's time. And on my end, it WILL NOT. This is why I require good roleplayers - because I want this WOW e-league to be the best that it can. Please reply to [detail withheld] if interested. Thank you
