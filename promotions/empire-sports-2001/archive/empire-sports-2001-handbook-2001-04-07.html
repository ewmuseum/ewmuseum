****************************************** DEVELOPMENT ****************************************** * KAYFABE * Sitting at home one evening, Darren Donaldsworth was watching wrestling. After two hours of sitting on the couch, watching a sport he loved, he realized something...
wrestling has gotten stale. Same face after face on the screen, doing the same thing they've done since the TV show "Blossom" was cool. So, from that point on he thought to himself...
"i'm going to make wrestling cool again". Darren Donaldsworth, son of Philip & Mary Donaldsworth, who are two very successful business lawyers. Darren has ownership in a local chain of taverns in
Boston, Massachusetts. He also has a degree in business law and accounting. He knows the only way for a wrestling promotion to succeed is by doing well financially.
Donaldsworth, instantly took a flight to Hollywood, California and met with many Production companies. He wanted to start a new, fresh exciting wrestling promotion on prime time cable television. EMPIRE Entertainment took him up on his offer, as Donaldsworth was given the title of "President of Operations".
With Donaldsworth's financial backing, he got a deal for a Friday television show to take place every week live from EMPIRE Entertainment's studio. He also got a deal on GEN X-TV (which had WAO on their networks last summer/fall)
for Sunday nights. Now with timeslots, and staff in place... Donaldsworth is looking for only one thing... talent. And he has an unique way of getting it. Only tune in to find out.

We are here to provide fun and entertaining fed, as we hope you will enjoy these rules.
For the first 2-3 months, EMPIRE Sports Entertainment will be going its "pilot" season... Under the pilot season, no title matches will occur, no special pay per view events. Here's what will be going on during the Pilot season.
[Pilot season:] * No wrestlers are under "EMPIRE contract". * Every week on Sunday Night Destruction, four matches will take place. * Every match a "EMPIRE wrestling contract" will be on the line. The winner of each match will receive an EMPIRE wrestling contract, to work after the pilot season.
* Wrestlers who receive contracts will be eligible to work matches on EMPIRE LIVE! The studio wrestling show. * Once 32 wrestlers are signed under contract, the pilot season will be over. [After Pilot season:] * EMPIRE Sports Entertainment will also have a money system. The more you win, the more money you receive. Money can be used for title shots and priority as far as storylines, and pushes go. Here is the rubrick:
- A win by pinfall = $1,000 - A win by submission = $1,300 - A win by Count Out = $300 - A win by Disqualification = $250 - A loss by pinfall = -$700 - A loss by submission = -$950 - A loss by Count Out = -$200 - A loss by Disqualification = $-100 ****************************************** BROADCASTS ****************************************** EMPIRE Sports Entertainment has two shows a week... they are: __ _ __ _ _ _ / _\_ _ _ __ __| | __ _ _ _ /\ \ (_) __ _| |__ | |_ \ \| | | | '_ \ / _` |/ _` | | | | / \/ / |/ _` | '_ \| __| _\ \ |_| | | | | (_| | (_| | |_| | / /\ /| | (_| | | | | |_ \__/\__,_|_| |_|\__,_|\__,_|\__, | \_\ \/ |_|\__, |_| |_|\__| |___/ |___/ ___ _ _ _ / \___ ___| |_ _ __ _ _ ___| |_(_) ___ _ __ / /\ / _ \/ __| __| '__| | | |/ __| __| |/ _ \| '_ \ / /_// __/\__ \ |_| | | |_| | (__| |_| | (_) | | | | /___,' \___||___/\__|_| \__,_|\___|\__|_|\___/|_| |_| Sunday Night Destruction is the main show. ****************************************** CHAMPIONSHIPS ****************************************** EMPIRE Sports Entertainment has five titles under the organization. Those prestigious belts are...
EMPIRE Sports Entertainment World Heavyweight Title: The cream of the crop in EMPIRE Sports Entertainment. If you didn't come here to win the World Heavyweight Title, you shouldn't be here. This belt is defended once a month on live television, and every Pay Per View broadcast. Can you claim the strap?
Or will you be another name on the wall? EMPIRE Sports Entertainment Television Title: This is EMPIRE Sports Entertainment's secondary title. Second best is not bad. This title is defended every week on Sunday Night Destruction, and on every Pay Per View broadcast. The holder of this belt, should be looked as one of the next big stars in EMPIRE.
