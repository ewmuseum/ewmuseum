I would like feedback on this at [detail withheld] -Here are some informative materials that you may find useful: -FLASHES GO TO: [detail withheld] -ALL STRATS, CARD INTERVIEWS QUESTIONS, IDEAS, and ANYTHING ELSE GO TO: [detail withheld] AND [detail withheld] -OUR LEAGUE WEBSITE(KUDOS TO THE DESIGNER) IS: http://www.geocities.com/globalprowrestling : A LOT HAS BEEN UPDATED!!!! CHECK IT OUT(RANKINGS WILL BE UPDATED ON THE SITE ASAP) -VOTE FOR GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING ON E-ZONE AT: http://ewzone.virtualave.net -TO BE TAKEN OFF THE MAILING LIST CONTACT: [detail withheld] -DEADLINES FOR ALL CARD MATERIAL IS THURSDAY NIGHT AT 10:00 P.M. CST!!!!! I REALLY MEAN THIS....FROM NOW ON IF I GET STUFF AFTER THE DEADLINE, THERE IS A 99.999% CHANCE IT WILL BE TRASHED. ALSO, ROLEPLAY DEALINES COME AT 9:00 P.M. CST ON TUESDAYS AND THURSDAYS SO PLEASE GET THEM IN BEFORE THEN. ALSO, I WILL START SENDING VIGNETTES AND INTERVIEWS FOR CARDS BACK IF THEY ARE NOT PROPERLY FORMATTED. That means action in the << >> and the speaker's LAST AND ONLY LAST NAME IN ALL CAPS. For example, take the name Peter Caradine. It would be written like this and only this: [CARADINE]: "Text." Also, remember, the video screen is called THE GLOBAL VIEW AND NOTHING ELSE! -Please read Mr. Blain's speech on Meltdown for a schedule of things during the holiday season. I will be running Shockwave but that will be it. Also, on the weekend of Jauary 9th, there will be a LOADED Global Invasion followed up that Sunday by the best of GPW. A schedule will also be made soon about the pay-per-view to ensure it will get out on time. ============================================================================ ==== ********************** THE NEWS AND RUMOR ********************** -The big news this week is the ending to the main event on Meltdown. After Furious Steele won The King of Tennessee Battle Royale and had a scuffle with Eugene Robinson, the man who he will meet this Sunday in Birmingham, Alabama for the GPW World Heavyweight Title in a no disqualification match. However, we all saw the message on The Global View stating "LYING WILL GET YOU NO WHERE STEELE." This apparently got to Steele for some reason and, then, we found the note stating "At the final hour, the truth will be revealed.....". Things are getting weirder by the moment here in Global Pro-Wrestling but all signs of something big are pointing to next week at Meltdown....The 11th Hour. -A full schedule of events up until January 16th, 2000, the date of Future Fight 2000, will be made public to the league very shortly. It is imperative that you read the schedule of events so no one will be lost. In other news, the card for Future Fight 2000 will be made public during the Christmas Holidays. Word has it that the finishing touches on finalizing things will be made at The 11th Hour. -One has to wonder why Ric Studd would get himself re-suspended to help out Lew Spectre. Word has it that Spectre is not going to bow down to the GPW so easily. -Word has it that Chris Page is looking to allign himself with some very deadly individuals here in Global Pro-Wrestling. More on this as it comes. -You wanted it...you got it! For the first time in GPW history, a Brass Knux title match will be broadcast on Meltdown....The 11th Hour. T'Chamunga will finally get his shot on live Television due to all the requests of the fans. However, he will defend his title against not one man but two men....it will be a first fall wins Triple Threat Match against The Zinmaster and Zack Sharp! -Speaking of The 11th Hour, the full card has been released. In addition to the World Title Match and Brass Knux Title Match, Mad Valentine will have to bite a bullet in a first pin wins Four Corners Match for the American Title that will feature James Wilson, Ryan Stone, and The Phoenix. In addition, Rune and Rapier will make their first title defense as they will battle the new number one contenders to the World-Tag Team Titles, the impressive duo of Dagger Lorath and Shadowman -Speaking of the tag-team scene, Justice 2K will be on hand at Meltdown to give a reason as to why they were involved in the match between Law and Disorder and Rune and Rapier. It should prove to be very interesting -As announced on Meltdown, the first guest on Kris Evans' "Kraze's Corner" will be none other than the GPW Regional Champion, Chris Page. In addition to that, Chris Page will finally get his hands on Lance Love this Saturday in Huntsville at The Global Invasion -Word from the back has that a lot of people lost respect for Sebastian St. Clair for no showing his first scheduled appearence in the league. However, St. Clair reportedly could care less as he plans on doing things his way in the league. -Global Pro-Wrestling would like to announce that any wrestler in the league, who would be willing to, can buy ad-time on Meltdown for broadcast. All you need to do is contact the Global Pro-Wrestling front offices -Team Ego is set to make some waves this week as they continue their struggle with Matt Foxx and Bubba Wilson in addition to having reserved time on Meltdown. Should be interesting -In addition, Johnny Adonis has also reserved time on Meltdown this week as he is said to be ready to make a major announcement. -COMMENTS AND CRITICISM IS WELCOME ON THIS WEEK'S CARD RESULTS....HOWEVER, PLEASE REMEMBER THE FIRST RULE ON THE RULE PAGE ON THE WEBSITE. ============================================================================ ==== ******************************* COMINGS, GOINGS, AND INJURED ******************************* COMINGS: VIP GOINGS: Ric Studd(Re-Suspended) INJURED: NO NEW INJURIES ============================================================================ ==== ============================================================================ ==== GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING WEEKLY LINE-UPS ============================================================================ ==== ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^ GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING HOUSE SHOW LINE-UP FOR Dothan, Alabama (12/16/99): RECAPPED ON THE GLOBAL INVASION TELECAST (12/18/99) ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^ 1.Nicholas Richards vs. Ramon Lopez 2.Sebastian St. Clair vs. Samhein 3.VIP vs. Star Spangled Justice 4.Main Event: Bubba Wilson vs. Vic V.
