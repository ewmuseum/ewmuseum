The Global Invasion

9 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
============================================================================ ==== GLOBAL PROFESSIONAL WRESTLING PROUDLY PRESENTS: "THE GLOBAL INVASION" 1/8/2000 PHOENIX, ARIZONA ============================================================================ ==== <<The screen is all black.....all of a sudden, the opening notes of Metallica's "Turn the Page" begins to play and the blackness slowly turns into a shot of an empty arena. As the song slowly progresses, shots, slowly increasing in speed, of Global Pro-Wrestlers begin to flash over the screen while, on the other shot, the arena starts to get filled. Finally, as the song reaches the chorus...., the shots merge into an actual shot of an arena where the crowd is anticipating action. In the arena, we can see that the Global View Video Screen is present and that the ring has a majestic golden GPW banner on it. Above the ring, two GPW Banners are hanging while an impressive fireworks display goes off. Finally, as the fireworks dim down, a voice over begins to speak.....it is Aaron
Hayes....>> [HAYES]: "Good evening ladies and gentlemen and welcome to the recap of what had to have been one of the most explosive editions of The Global
Invasion. With only 8 days from Future Fight 2000 live in Los Angeles,
Global Pro-Wrestling turned things up a notch as we tore the roof off the house for the people in Phoenix. We're not going to waste any time here tonight so let us go straight to what happened earlier on."
<<The match footage begins to play for the people at home......>> *********************** CZAR VS. RAMON LOPEZ *********************** [HAYES]: "In our opener, two bitter enemies squared off as Czar met the crazy veteran Ramon Lopez. Both men tore into each other like a knife through butter as both desperately tried to climb up the ladder in the
Brass Knux division. As the match raged on, the fighting got more and more intense with blood flowing, tables breaking, and chairs swinging. Finally, as the brawl went to the back, both men hit each other upside the head with a steel chair, knocking each other out. The referee had no choice but to count both men down as they were on the ground, motionless, for a good twenty seconds. As a result, both men go into the Pay-Per-View undercard next week at the same place they were but, they also have to worry about one Bryan Meredith."
WINNER: NO ONE(DOUBLE KNOCK-OUT) ******************************** BRYAN MEREDITH VS. ZACK SHARP ******************************** [HAYES]: "For our next match, the GPW Brass Knux Champion, Zack Sharp, was set to sqaure off against Bryan Meredith in a non-title match. However,
Sharp had no intention of wrestling sharp as he brought his vertically challenged friend to the ring in his place. Meredith grew enraged as a result of this and flew out of the ring and dove onto Sharp, thus starting the match. Both men tore into each other like Czar and Lopez did to each other in the match before. However, as Meredith began to get the advantage, the crowd errupted as Dagger Lorath and Shadowman came down to the ring and turned the match into a three on one affair against Meredith.
As the beating continued, the crowd was stunned as, from out of no-where,
Chris Quiller ran to the ringside area and cleared Lorath and Shadowman out of the area. While this was happening, Sharp caught Meredith from behind and dropped him with a backdrop driver on the concrete, rolled him up, and scored the hard fought win against an impressive Brass Knux contender.
However, the story here is why Chris Quiller ran to the ring in the apparent aide of Bryan Meredith."
WINNER: ZACK SHARP ****************************** THE ZINMASTER VS. T'CHAMUNGA ****************************** [HAYES]: "Our next match was supposed to be T'Chamunga battling The Zinmaster but, for some reason, T'Chamunga was not in the building and this is where things got interesting. The Zinmaster laid down an open challenge for anyone in the building and, from out of no-where, Bryan Meredith ran out of the dressing room area and went tooth and nail wth The Zinmaster.
Actually, The Zinmaster did not know hit him as Meredith looked real intense after the loss to sharp earlier on. As the two continued to fight, the camera picked up Global Pro-Wrestling newcomer, Johnny Blades, wearing a T-Shirt saying "AA is for Quiters" selling beer in the crowd. Anyway,
Meredith scored the advantage as he bashed a steel chair upside The
Zinmaster's already injured leg, rolled him up, and got the win in a match he wasn't even signed for. As he left, Meredith's face was of pure intensity while The Zinmaster had to be helped up, yet he left under his own power.
WINNER: BRYAN MEREDITH ****************************** FLIC ROGERS VS. CAMERON KIDD ****************************** [HAYES]: "If you thought the last match was interesting, this one carried on the torch. Flic Rogers missed his introduction so Cameron Kidd came out first to a loud and impressive pop. When Rogers was announced again, he did not come out but, from the curtain area, none other than Chris Sunrise, made his return to Global Pro-Wrestling, came out and stepped in the ring against Cameron Kidd. Kidd accepted Sunrise's challenge and the two started to square off. Kidd once again dazzled the crowd with an impressive aerial display, although he showboated a tad too much and it cost him as the veteran Chris Sunrise took the match control away from him and went to town on Kidd. Sunrise began to school until Cameron fought back and regained control. When Sunrise was down, Kidd went for a 450 splash but, he waited to long and it allowed Sunrise to roll him up and score the pinfall. As Sunrise celebrated his win, from out of no-where,
Rex Watson stormed the ring and smashed a steel chair across the back of
Chris Sunrise. And, just like that, Watson left. As he left, Johnny
Blades offered him a beer and, when Watson took it, he threw it in Blades'
face. The ring was cleared shortly after but the questions raised during this match were why did Watson attack Sunrise and why did Rogers miss his ring introduction."
WINNER: CHRIS SUNRISE ************************************ CHRIS QUILLER VS. NICHOLAS RICHARD ************************************ [HAYES]: "The next match of the evening featured two of the most hungriest rookies in Global Pro-Wrestling as Chris Quiller reappeared to battle
Nicholas Richard, The Army of One. To start off, Nicholas unloaded on
Quiller with an offensive that mirrored a sheer bar-room brawl. However,
Quiller fought back with an impressive offensive display that featured heavy influences from the styles of Mexico and Japan. I don't think
Richard knew what hit him as Kid Charisma unloaded him on for nearly ten minutes. Finally, as Richard was totally winded, Quiller finished him off with a beautiful T-Bone Suplex and scored his first win in Global
Pro-Wrestling." WINNER: CHRIS QUILLER ************************************************************************** GPW AMERICAN TAG-TEAM TITLE MATCH: MATT FOXX AND BUBBA WILSON VS. VIP(C) ************************************************************************** [HAYES]: "In our first tag match of the evening, the ever popular duo of
Bubba Wilson and Matt Foxx squared off against Steve and William of the newly crowned GPW American Tag-Team Champions, VIP. VIP showed their experience as a team as they cut the ring off right from the get-go and really isolated Foxx from Wilson, who was on the outside for most of the match. VIP proceed to wear Foxx's knees down at this point and nearly had the match won with a Figure Four until the crowd started to get in the match. Foxx fought back and soon brought Wilson into the ring where he proceeded to clean house. As Wilson was on fire against William, Matt jumped in the ring and went at it with Steve. However, as the referee was trying to get Matt and Steve out of the ring, VIP's manager, Carl
Stevenson, jumped in the ring and blasted Wilson in the back of the head with one of the Title belts. This allowed William to roll Wilson up and steal the match away as they retained their GPW American Tag-Team Titles and looked towards the match between Justice 2K and Law and Disorder."
WINNERS: VIP ******************************** DREW ARCHER VS. ANDREW BAILEY ******************************** [HAYES]: "The next contest of the evening had a lot riding on it as Drew
Archer squared off against Andrew Bailey. Both men are rumored to be involved in that massive tag-match on the pay-per-view in which Chris Page and Adam Rogers will have to select a tag-team partner. However, for this match, Archer started off with a blaze of furry on Bailey as The Classic showed Andrew his knowledge of scientific wrestling. Archer unloaded on
Bailey for nearly ten minutes until Andrew reversed a suplex attempt by
Archer and things went in Bailey's direction from there on. As Bailey wore down Archer, the crowd began to cheer louder and louder for Andrew until, finally, he revved back and knocked the taste out of Archer's mouth with a beautiful superkick that ended up scoring the pinfall victory for Bailey.
However, as the referee hit three, Chris Page ran to the ring and slammed a steel chair down on Bailey's head. From there, Page went to work on Andrew while Archer jumped up and joined in. Finally, Adam Rogers ran to the ring and cleared out Archer and Page, allowing Bailey to get up and recieve a standing ovation."
WINNER: ANDREW BAILEY *********************************** JUSTICE 2K VS. LAW AND DISORDER *********************************** [HAYES]: "Our next tag match, which pitted Justice 2K against Law and
Disorder with a shot at VIP on the line, ended up being extremely interesting, to say the least. Willey started the match out against
Anarchy, who proceed to bend the rules a bit but the crowd loved it. As
Willey powered over Anarchy, he tagged in Muldoon to continue the job.
When Muldoon hit the ring, Willey grabbed him and gave him an inverted
Death Valley Driver from out of now-where that STUNNED the crowd along with
Law and Disorder. Willey then proceeded to stare at Law and Disorder before pointing to Muldoon and leaving the ring to a wave of boos. In the ring, Law and Disorder capitalized on things as Anarchy quickly rolled up
Jesse Muldoon and got the win. Law and Disorder celebrated winning the spot on the pay-per-view while Muldoon took a long time getting up. Why
Willey turned on Jesse is beyond me but there is definetly a story behind it."
WINNER: LAW AND DISORDER *********************************** ANARCHY VS. RAPIER VS. SHADOWMAN *********************************** [HAYES]: "Right as he won the shot to battle VIP at the PPV, Anarchy stayed in the ring as he was scheduled to battle in a triple threat match against
Rapier and Shadowman. As both men entered the ring for the no-disqualification triple threat match, Anarchy unloaded on them and the crowd loved it. However, as the match went on, both Rapier and Shadowman gained the advantage as the fight went all over the floor. Eventually,
Dagger Lorath tried to make his way down to the ring but was stopped cold by Justice and Rune. While Lorath was being taken care of by those two, from out of no where, VIP ran down to the area and started to fight with
Justice and Rune. This allowed Lorath to get to the ringside area where he took out a pair of Brass Knuckles. Anarchy took a wicked shot to the face by Lorath and, as Lorath was about to hit Rapier, Rune clipped his legs out from under him and saved his partner. However, as Rapier looked on at
Rune, Shadowman came up from behind and locked Rapier in a German Suplex into a pinning combination that scored the win for one half of the World
Tag-Team Champions. As Shadowman celebrated to the back, Rapier looked very upset and would not even walk back to the dressing room area with
Rune."
WINNER: SHADOWMAN **************************************************************************** GPW WORLD TELEVISION TITLE MATCH: SEBASTIAN ST. CLAIR VS. JOHNNY ADONIS(C) **************************************************************************** [HAYES]: "In the match for the World Television Title, Sebastian St. Clair met a very ill Johnny Adonis for his precious Title belt. Adonis nearly boycotted the match as he stated that St. Clair did not deserve to be in the same ring with a man the caliber of himself. This enraged St. Clair to the point of getting Adonis and throwing him into the ring. However, once in the ring, it was all Johnny Adonis as he took every cheap shot he could on the way to beating St. Clair don. Finally, Adonis grew tired of the situation and spiked St. Clair with his Hawaiian Delight Thunder Driver to get the win and retain his Title as he looks towards his defense at Future
Fight."
WINNER: JOHNNY ADONIS ***************************** LANCE LOVE VS. CARL BLAIR ***************************** [HAYES]: "Just as we saw Johnny Adonis defend his Television Title, Lance
Love and Carl Blair squared off against each other as both men fought to get a shot at Adonis on the Pay-Per-View. Mad Valentine, of course, was with Carl Blair at
ringside, however, his attention was not on the match as it was on himself. Love unloaded on Blair from the start as he showed the fans that intensity he is known to have. The match then proceeded to become an aerial display as both men traded high risk shots on each other for nearly five minutes.
Finally, with the referee knocked down, Mad Valentine casually threw the
American Title belt in the ring but Lance Love caught it. As he blasted
Carl with the gold, the referee got up and immediately disqualified Lance
Love for the illegal blow. This enraged Love who then proceed to wail away on Blair with vicious fists to the face. On the outside, Valentine was playing it up to the crowd before realizing what was happening to Blair.
He slid in the ring and proceeded to get Love out but, the damage had been done as Blair was busted open as a result of Love's rage. However, Carl
Blair will go to Los Angeles to meet Johnny Adonis for the Television Title now."
WINNER: CARL BLAIR ********************************* JAMES WILSON VS. PRIEST NOBLE ********************************* [HAYES]: "As the night continued, James Wilson squared off against one of the three possible wrestlers who will walk out of Los Angeles with the
World Title belt, Priest Noble. The match was a highly entertaining affair as both fan favorites worked their magic against each other in what could be called the match of the night. However, as the match carried on, Priest
Noble began to score the advantage over Wilson and was about to when it until Furious Steele came out and speared Noble to the mat. The bell was called for but Steele was all over Noble while Wilson tried to pull him off. However, as Wilson tried to do so, Mad Valentine came down to the ring and got Wilson's attention. As Wilson charged Valentine, Carl Blair jumped out from the entrance area and took Wilson to the ground. As Blair and Valentine put the boots to Wilson, the crowd errupted as Wilson got up and began to unload on both Valentine and Blair. Valentine, though, exited before Blair and it caused Carl to be the victim of several vicious fists by James Wilson, who proceeded to bust Carl Blair open even more. Back in the ring, Noble and Steele continued to fight until Chris Page, Adam
Rogers, and Eugene Robinson came down to the ring..... WINNER: PRIEST NOBLE **************************************************************************** **** MAIN EVENT: EUGENE ROBINSON AND ADAM ROGERS VS FURIOUS STEELE AND CHRIS PAGE **************************************************************************** **** [HAYES]: "Where they proceeded to get our main event of the evening underway. Priest Noble calmly exited the ring and watched from the floor as the main event was turned into a Texas Tornado style match. As Robinson and Steele went at it and Page and Rogers went at it, Noble gazed at the match once more before leaving through the entrance area. In the ring, though, things got a bit out of hand as Furious Steele looked like a untamed monster as he and Robinson went at it. Page also looked like a monster as he nearly drew blood on Rogers. Finally, as the match carried on, the lights went out and everyone suspected Noble to come out. A devilish laughter could be heard over the P.A. system once they came back on. When the lights were back on, Steele caught Robinson from behind with a spear while Chris Page rocked Rogers with the Cradle DDT and scored the pinfall win. Seeing Rogers pinned opened up a new intensity as Robinson began to unload on Steele and Page himself. Security had to be called to the ring to break things up but, the wild man in Robinson came out at that point."
WINNERS: FURIOUS STEELE AND CHRIS PAGE <<The camera shows a shot of an empty arena....>> [HAYES]: "So fans, there you have it....ANOTHER EXPLOSIVE NIGHT ON THE
GLOBAL INVASION. Remember, live....January 16th....The Staples Center in
Los Angeles, California.....Global Pro-Wrestling presents its first
Pay-Per-View of the year 2000.....FUTURE FIGHT 2000: THE BATTLE OF LOS
ANGELES. Eight big matches are on tap for the show, six of them being title matches and then you have that mega Triple Threat Ladder Match main event for the GPW World Heavyweight Title. In addition, there will be a full undercard for the event to showcase the young up and coming talent of
Global Pro-Wrestling. Until then, I'm Aaron Hayes saying so long and see you in Los Angeles!"
