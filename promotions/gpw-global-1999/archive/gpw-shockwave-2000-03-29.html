GPW 2/22/2000 **************************************************************************** **** ON THIS EDITION: 1] Mad Valentine opens the show at the hospital... 2] Carl Blair gets word from the doctors... 3] Tommy Gunn runs in to a couple of people at the hospital... 4] American Tag-Team Champion, The Zinmaster, looks towards Brass Knux
Champ, Rune... 5] Shadowman adresses his slate for this week... 6] Domination sets up a collision course with XTremeCharisma... 7] The Phoenix adresses the situation with Rune, Oyabun, and The Zinmaster... 8] Bryan Meredith has words for the GPW Tag-Team Division... 9] Adam Rogers speaks his mind about Alec Ace... 10]Johnny Adonis speaks on still being the GPW Television Champion... 11]Violence and Corruption 2000 make their Shockwave debut... 12]Frank Ewiak makes his official on-air debut in GPW... 13]Oyabun makes another vindictive rant towards quite a few... 14]Ric Donovan closes the program out for tonight... ============================================================================ ==== *************************************** 'SMOKING BARREL' MADISON J. VALENTINE *************************************** To: Chris Page, Johnny Blades, and that other guy... From: The Heavyweight Champion of the World... [The scene opens outside a local hospital, Chicago, Illinois. With the ring crew still hard at work back in the United Center following GPW's wild
'Meltdown' broadcast, the hospital parking lot, some twenty minutes across town from the arena, is pretty quiet at this hour. Powdery, halogen lamps provide the only illumination, as an ambulance pulls up outside the facility.]
[The sound of doors closing, two paramedics hops down on to the concrete, and head to the back of the vehicle. As the camera pans around to see what's happening, the doors swing open, and the two men ease a gurney out from inside, a third, female member of the team appearing along with it to assist them.]
[The man on the stretcher is obviously in some pain, propped up on his elbows, his right leg resting on pads, covered in towels and bags of ice.
The man is dressed only in yellow-and-black wrestling tights, in glassy latex, his long, dirty blond hair wet back. The man, clearly GPW's very own
'True Life Fantasy', Carl Blair, grimaces as two of the three EMTs wheel him into the hospital.]
[The third waits behind as yet another individual emerges from the back of the ambulance. With a little bit of help from the young woman, he steps down, gingerly, on to the concrete, and begins to wander almost aimlessly towards the entrance through which Blair was just taken. We can tell even before he steps into the glow of the lights that this is the 'Smoking
Barrel', Mad J. Valentine.] [He is still dressed in the pants of his black, pin-stripe suit, his violet shirt minus the matching tie, and unbuttoned right to the middle of his chest. His short, black hair is wet from the bag of ice he holds to his head, the usual twinkly in his jade-green eyes misted over slightly.
Absently, he stumbles forward, as the EMT, a young brunette who is strikingly pretty even without makeup, closes the doors of the ambulance and hurries after him, taking his arm to keep him from falling over.]
[MEDIC]: "This way, Mr. Valentine." [VALENTINE]: "Uh?" [MEDIC]: "Over here, through these doors." [She guides him to another entrance, through a pair of sliding, glass doors, which open and close with a 'swish'. MJ seems a little unsure on his feet, most likely suffering a concussion after the attack by Chris Page at the end of 'Meltdown'.]
[The reception area of the hospital is almost sombre in its silence. The lights are bright and Valentine seems a little pained by this, his head roaring with pain. A few people sit awkwardly quiet, some wearing medical dressings. The girl sets the 'Smoking Barrel' down in a chair like he were some kind of elderly person, and she bends down to talk to him. All he can do is look at her with glazed eyes.]
[MEDIC]: "Okay, Mr. Valentine?" [VALENTINE]: "Uh... just, yeah." [MEDIC]: "Okay. You just wait right here, and I'll go and register you. They'll probably call your name in about ten minutes, and then you can go and see the doctor. Okay?"
[He just nods, ice pack still pressed to the top of his head.] [MEDIC]: "Alright, then..." [With that, she heads over to the reception desk. Valentine slumps back in his chair, and groans, as his advisor, the red-headed Kingsley Sweete, rushes in through the doors. Dressed in typical attire, his wiry hair is slicked back into an oily pony-tail. He spends a few moments scanning over the people in the room, before his eyes come to rest on MJ, and he walks over to him briskly.]
[SWEETE]: "Jeez, Mad... you look pretty banged up..." [The usual vigour is gone from his voice, reduced to no more than a whisper, as he addresses Kingsley without even looking at him...]
[VALENTINE]: "Well thanks. That really makes me feel better." [SWEETE]: "Sorry. So... how _are_ you feeling, anyway?" [VALENTINE]: "Oh... on top of the world, Kingsley. Just fan_tastic." [Even though he can only just hear MJ, Sweete looks a bit humbled.] [VALENTINE]: "Is that all of your dumb questions done now?" [SWEETE]: "Uh... yeah. I mean, I think so." [VALENTINE]: "Did you grab my stuff?" [SWEETE]: "Err... yeah. It's all in the car." [VALENTINE]: "Well... at least you've done _something_ right." [He shuffles in his seat, craning his neck around the room. The EMT returns, pushes a hand back over her mid-length tawny brown hair.]
[MEDIC]: "Right, Mr. Valentine, that's all done, but you're probably gonna have to wait another ten minutes or so. S'that okay?"
[VALENTINE]: "Yeah. And, uh..." [His tongue clicks in his mouth, head still lolling about...] [VALENTINE]: "... thanks a lot. For your help." [MEDIC]: "No problem, sir. I hope you're feeling better soon." [With a smile, she leaves, the doors 'swooshing' open for her as she steps out into the night. Valentine tries to shake the cobwebs from his head, blinking his eyes in to some sort of blurry mis-focus...]
[SWEETE]: "Ten minutes? They're gonna make you wait ten minutes?" [VALENTINE]: "It's not a problem, Kingsley. Don't worry." [SWEETE]: "But you're _concussed_, MJ. This is _serious_." [VALENTINE]: "No it's not. It's nothing I can't handle, Kingsley." [SWEETE]: "But you need to get it checked out _right now_!" [VALENTINE]: "Why's that? I can't wait ten minutes?" [SWEETE]: "You've got a match next week, MJ!" [VALENTINE]: "So? I mean, I have?" [SWEETE]: "Yeah. So, y'know, ASAP, and stuff." [The 'Smoking Barrel' sighs.] [SWEETE]: "You _need_ to be ready for it. It's gonna be tough." [VALENTINE]: "What, you think I've never wrestled injured before?" [SWEETE]: "Uh..." [VALENTINE]: "Well then. No sweat. I _am_ the champ, after all." [SWEETE]: "Yeah... but... your concussion..." [He shakes his head again, ears ringing.] [VALENTINE]: "What, I'm gonna let Page think he put me on the shelf? Not a chance, Kingsley. I'll be back next week. Put money on it."
[SWEETE]: "But what if..." [VALENTINE]: "Oh, you and your 'but what ifs'. Listen, Kingsley. I've got
Chris Page back at that arena, cheesy little yellow grin on his face because he dropped my head on a chair about an hour ago."
[He takes the ice pack from his head, places his fingers to the wet patch of hair, and brings them away stained red ever so slightly.]
[VALENTINE]: "In fact, he's probably adding my name at the bottom of that
'hitlist' of his, right about now. If he knows how to hold a pencil, that is. You think I'm gonna let him get away with that?"
[Kingsley shrugs, blank.] [SWEETE]: "I dunno." [VALENTINE]: "Well, I'm not. He wants to try to put me out?" [He shrugs again.] [VALENTINE]: "Of course he does. He thinks he's this big badass, just because he put a few zeros into retirement. Well, guess what?"
[SWEETE]: "What?" [VALENTINE]: "Well, they don't call me the 'Great Canadian Badass' for nothing, Kingsley. Let's just say that Chris Page is staring down the barrel of one career-ending injury, huh?"
[For the first time, he actually looks at him.] [VALENTINE]: "And there ain't _no way_ he's dodging _this_ bullet." [Kingsley nods.] [VALENTINE]: "Y'know, that sounded pretty good, didn't it?" [SWEETE]: "Yeah. It did." [VALENTINE]: "Well, write it down somewhere Kingsley, remember it. Where's the fun in putting good lines to waste in some hospital waiting room? I'll wait until he's around to hear it next time..."
[SWEETE]: "Heh. Yeah." [He seems to be in good spirits, despite the throbbing pain. He rests his head in one hand, rubs his cheek, and sighs.]
[VALENTINE]: "So who's this Johnny I'm wrestling next week?" [SWEETE]: "Err... a-ha... it's funny you should say that..." [VALENTINE]: "It is?" [SWEETE]: "Yeah. You're wrestling Johnny Blades next week." [His head rolls back again, with a moan.] [VALENTINE]: "Y'know, maybe its just the morphine, or summin' like that,
Kingsley, but, uh... who the hell is Johnny Blades?"
[SWEETE]: "Well, uh... he's buddies with Chris Page, apparently. He was the guy in the mask who just beat up you and Carl. And Tommy."
[VALENTINE]: "What masked guy?" [SWEETE]: "Oh. Y'know... black ski-mask? Remember?" [He looks at the 'Smoking Barrel', hoping that he might have jogged his memory. The look he gets in reply, though, is just blank.]
[SWEETE]: "You don't. Well, okay." [He palms a hand over his fiery scalp.] [SWEETE]: "Okay, uh... a coupla months ago, remember in the back, when that drunk guy bumped into you, and asked if you wanted to tag up with him some time? And you just laughed, and walked off?"
[Valentine scrunches up his eyes, thinking hard.] [VALENTINE]: "Yeah, I think so. Did he have the kind of breath that could cut through bank vaults? I thought it was Steele at first..."
[SWEETE]: "That might be it..." [VALENTINE]: "Oh, my head hurts too much to think any more." [SWEETE]: "Well, never mind. It was probably Johnny Blades anyway." [His pained expression overlaps with mild shock for a moment.] [VALENTINE]: "That guy?" [SWEETE]: "Probably, yeah." [VALENTINE]: "I'm wrestling that guy?" [Sweete nods.] [VALENTINE]: "What did you say his name was? Johnny Breath?" [SWEETE]: "Blades." [VALENTINE]: "I'm wrestling a _drunk_? Me, MJV, the World Champion?" [SWEETE]: "Well, he's not a drunk any more, Mad. He's changed." [VALENTINE]: "That's what they all say." [SWEETE]: "Well, whether he is or he isn't, he's toughened up, anyhow. And he's hanging around with Chris Page and Furious Steele."
[VALENTINE]: "Well, how much drink did he have to get down the booker before he agreed to give him a World Title shot, huh?"
[Kingsley is quick to correct him.] [SWEETE]: "Oh... it's non-title, MJ." [VALENTINE]: "Well, at least the world's not gone totally crazy." [SWEETE]: "But, y'know, with Page and Steele at ringside..." [VALENTINE]: "Don't try and make me see him as a threat, Kingsley." [SWEETE]: "Oh. Okay." [VALENTINE]: "Listen, I'll beat Johnny Blades. No worries there. And to be honest with you, Kingsley, I couldn't care less whether Page and Steele are at ringside, or not. In fact..."
[He leans forward slightly, his speech a little slurred.] [VALENTINE]: "... I'd prefer it to be like that way. I'm gonna kick their face right up their ass, and I don't wanna have to do hide and seek before I kick their face up their ass. Ya' dig?"
[Sweete nods. Suddenly, the woman at the desk calls out...] [RECEPTIONIST]: "Madison J. Valentine?" [Valentine just lolls in his chair, silent. Sweete intervenes.] [SWEETE]: "Err... yeah! Right here!" [He points to the 'Smoking Barrel', sat on his right.] [RECEPTIONIST]: "Okay. The doctor will see you now." [Indeed, he will. The spectacled black man, probably in his early thirties, complete with the mandatory white coat and stethoscope, stands by the desk holding a clipboard under his arm. Sweete takes MJV by the arm, and leads him over to the Doc...]
[DOCTOR]: "Alright, Mr. Valentine?" [He nods, if absently.] [SWEETE]: "He's had a nasty blow to the head..." [DOCTOR]: "Yes... I can see..." [Valentine shakes his head and blinks his eyes hard...] [DOCTOR]: "Okay. Come with me please, Mr. Valentine..." [Supporting himself on Sweete's less-than-muscular shoulder, Mad stumbles unsteadily off after the doctor, up the corridor and out of sight. With that, the image fades away to black...]

['The Natural' straightens the collar of his pale yellow golf shirt, a choice of clothing that does very little to hide his well-chiseled physique. I guess I'll have to apologize to Carl for not seeing him. . .

I've got no idea how

Said it was 'confidential,' or some crap like that." [He shakes his head, as Taylor nudges him.] [GUNN]: "Oh. Shay, I'm sorry - this is Taylor Hayes. Taylor, Shay Peters." [PETERS]: "So, you think I'll have any luck getting in to see Carl? I was hoping to get an update to bring back to the front offices. . ."

[GUNN]: "That's what I said, man." [Tommy extends his hand, in a gesture of good-bye, as Taylor links her slender golden arm in his.]
[GUNN]: "Well, I'm sorry that you wasted a trip, Shay. We've got to get going, this place isn't anything but a dead-end."

You've got a huge match coming up this Saturday night on the Global Invasion - your fourth GPW main event - against the number one contender to Mad Valentine's World title,
Chris Page. Would you mind if I got a few comments from you, err, y'know - while we're both here? I wouldn't want to come away from this excursion
empty-handed." [He looks to Taylor for her support.] [GUNN]: "Taylor, would you mind? It won't take more than a few minutes." [HAYES]: "Yeah, it's not a problem. You do your thing, I'll wait over here." [She leaves them to pick up a magazine from the waiting room.] [GUNN]: "Sure thing, Peters. What do you want to hear?" [PETERS]: "Well, how are you going to approach this match? What's racing through your mind right now, knowing that you're going to be stepping into the ring with Chris Page, knowing his track record. He's a very dangerous man, and he's already done a number on quite a few superstars here in the
GPW, and--"

and blindside them with steel chairs. Invasion. To me, Page isn't special. He isn't any different than the rest of the pack, except that he's seen his opportunity to break through and he's grabbed it by the horns. . . Well, that ride that Page has been milking for the past few months now is nearing its end - and it's coming in the form of
'The Natural' - Tommy Gunn."
[PETERS]: "Fair enough." [Shay looks impressed with the quality of the answer that he received, as he shuffles through some notes that he has scribbled on some scraps of yellow legal paper.]

Andrew
