The Global Invasion

4 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
_____________ ________ ___ ___ / __________/\| __ |/\ \ /\ \ / /\_________\/| |__| |\ \ \ ___ \ \ \ / / / /__ /\ | _____| \ \ \_/\ \_\_\ \ / /_/___\_/ / / | |_____| \ \ \ \___________/ / |__| \ \______/\_______/ \__________\/ |__| \/_____/\/______/ GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING PROUDLY PRESENTS ============================================================================ ==== "THE GLOBAL INVASION" 1/29/2000 KANSAS CITY, MISSOURI ============================================================================ ==== <<The screen is all black.....all of a sudden, the opening notes of Metallica's "Turn the Page" begins to play and the blackness slowly turns into a shot of an empty arena. As the song slowly progresses, shots, slowly increasing in speed, of Global Pro-Wrestlers begin to flash over the screen while, on the other shot, the arena starts to get filled. Finally, as the song reaches the chorus...., the shots merge into an actual shot of an arena where the crowd is anticipating action. In the arena, we can see that the
Global View Video Screen is present and that the ring has a majestic golden
GPW banner on it. Above the ring, two GPW Banners are hanging while a small but impressive fireworks display ignites from the walkway to the ring.
Finally, as the fireworks dim down, a voice over begins to speak.....it is
Aaron Hayes....>>
[HAYES]: "Good evening ladies and gentlemen and welcome to yet another action packed edition of The Global Invasion. Tonight, you will be watching footage from our house show that occured earlier in the week in Springfield,
Missouri in addition to tonight's action that happened here in Kansas City,
Missouri. From there, we go to the TWA Dome tommorow night in St. Louis,
Missouri for our big Super Bowl Edition of Meltdown in which the crowd will be covered in blue and gold for their Rams. So, without further ado, let's get this show started!"
<<The match footage begins to play for the viewers.....>> **************************************************************************** **** GPW HOUSE SHOW REVIEW 1/27/2000 FROM SPRINGFIELD, MISSOURI **************************************************************************** **** ******************************** KRIS EVANS VS. FLIC ROGERS ******************************** [HAYES]: "To start things off in Springfield, Flic Rogers met the brash youngster, Kris Evans. The match was all Evans as he destroyed the former
Regional Champion. After playing around with Rogers for a couple of minutes, Evans grew tired of his opponent and drilled him with a sit-out powerbomb to get the win."
WINNER: KRIS EVANS *********************************** DEREK SWEGER VS. SHANE JACKSON *********************************** [HAYES]: "Following the win by Kris Evans, Derek Sweger made his return to the ring as he battled Shane Jackson. The crowd was completely behind the former SCW superstar as he dazzled them with a high flying offensive that let Jackson clueless on what to do next. After wearing Jackson down, Sweger finished him off with a beautiful Tornado DDT to get the pinfall win.
Afterwards, though, his opponent here in Kansas City, Lance Love, ran down to the ring and got into a little scuffle with Sweger."
WINNER: DEREK SWEGER ********************************** JOHNNY BLADES VS. LUKE ANDREWS ********************************** [HAYES]: "In the match that stole the show here in Springfield, Luke Andrews and Johnny Blades, opponents last week in the Regional Title Tournament, met again. Both men dazzled the crowd as they traded the control of the match for nearly 15 minutes. Blades wore Andrews down with his sheer power and brutality but Luke came back and rocked AA's worst nightmare with an impressive technical display. Finally, after 20 minutes of sheer action,
Andrews dropped Blades with a beautiful T-Bone suplex from out of no-where to get the pin. However, as Blades was leaving the ring, he was met by
Nicholas Richard who just flat out started to duke it out with Johnny before security could break it up. Nicholas is getting more and more daring each week."
WINNER: LUKE ANDREWS ***************************** THE FANTASM VS. RAMON LOPEZ ***************************** [HAYES]: "In our main event for Springfield, The Fantasm squared off against
Ramon Lopez. The Fantasm saw me before the show and nearly chewed me out for getting information about his manuevers wrong and I must apologize...it was a mistake. Well, he didn't take it too lightly as he tore Ramon to shreads with a display of sheer force and brutality. Ramon didn't know what hit him as The Fantasm ran through him like a mack truck. Finally, The
Fantasm drilled Lopez with a pump-handle slam before flying off the top rope and nailing him with his QUEBEC Leg-Drop. To add insult to injury, The
Fantasm locked Lopez in the Spells of The Fantasm Sleeper hold to get the submission victory. As Bruno celebrated with his manager, that same, dark mysterious figure appeared on the ramp way and The Fantasm chased after him.
However, the figure quickly disappeared." WINNER: THE FANTASM [HAYES]: "From there, folks, we'll go right into tonight's action from here in Kansas City so hold on!"
***************************** DEREK SWEGER VS. LANCE LOVE ***************************** [HAYES]: "In our opening fight, Derek Sweger and Lance Love continued their little skirmish they had in Springfield with a match here in Kansas City.
Suprisingly, Lance Love dominated the match with an aerial assault that rivaled Sweger's. However, the fans got behind Derek and he soon made a comeback. Once he did, though, none other than Jared Mathys ran to ringside and got Sweger's attention. With Sweger distracted, Lance Love drilled
Sweger with an inverted DDT to get the win. Afterwards, Love left with
Mathys while Dan Starr made his way to the ring to help his friend out."
WINNER: LANCE LOVE **************************************************** GPW BRASS KNUX TITLE MATCH:CZAR VS. ZACK SHARP(C) **************************************************** [HAYES]: "For our first title match of the evening, Czar got another chance at Zack Sharp's Brass Knux Title and tried to make the most of it. However, the reigning champion showed the world why he earned the name "Sick and
Twisted" with an offensive that we can't show you due to its violent nature.
However, we can tell you that Zack Sharp scored the pinfall win over Czar after none other than Shadowman ran to the ring, bashed Czar in the head with a steel chair and allowed Sharp to drill him with a spinning spinebuster. Afterwards, Sharp and Shadowman kicked away at Czar before leaving triumphantly with the Brass Knux Title high in the air."
WINNER: ZACK SHARP ***************************************** THE RAGE MACHINE VS. LAW AND DISORDER ***************************************** [HAYES]: "In a match that was originally going to be The Westside Riders battling Law and Disorder for the GPW American Tag-Team Titles, GPW had to change it up a bit as The Riders no-showed. So, The Rage Machine filled in and the match was turned into a non-title affair. Naturally, Law and
Disorder made short work of The Rage Machine and finished their opponents off with one THE most wicked looking stuff powerbombs I have ever seen.
Just as quickly as the match went, Law and Disorder got their belts, left through the crowd, and didn't look back on their decimated opponents."
WINNER: LAW AND DISORDER ************************ DREW ARCHER VS. RUNE ************************ [HAYES]: "In his first singles match since the split with Rapier, Rune battled Hi-C member and fellow Brit, Drew Archer in what was a very stiff and very brtual contest. Rune showed the state of Missouri that he means business when he says he is a brawler and he definetly showed Drew Archer as well. In what suprised a lot of people, Rune finished Archer off without a problem with a vicious Tombstone piledriver from the second turnbuckle to score the pinfall win. Afterwards, as Drew Archer was making his way to the back, he was confronted by Sabbath, Furious Steele, and Chris Page. Archer looked greatful for their presence....UNTIL THEY TURNED ON HIM and beat him within an inch of his life. The crowd was shocked as Hi-C turned on one of their members. Page was the most brutal in the attack as he gave Archer repeated blows to the face with a pair of taped fists. Finally, security pulled Hi-C off of Archer but his face was covered in blood and he had to be taken in for medical treatment. A shocking sight and proof that their is no honor among thieves."
WINNER: RUNE ****************************** JARED MATHYS VS. KRIS EVANS ****************************** [HAYES]: "Our next match of the evening featured the confident and cocky
Kris Evans going up against the seasonsed veteran, Jared Mathys. Evans controlled the early part of the match but soon lost is as Mathys read his opponent well and capitalized on a key mistake by Evans. From there, Mathys mat wrestled Evans for quite a bit in an attempt to get up "Kraze's" skin.
It worked as Evans was a ball of furry and soon got the advantage over
Mathys. He finally got Mathys worn down enough to drill him with his sit-out powerbomb but, as he was going for the pin, Lance Love ran down to the ring and distracted the referee. Just as Evans was about to get the referee back in the match, from out of no-where, Ric Studd ran in the ring and clocked Kris Evans with a pair of Brass Knuckles right in the face. He then pulled Mathys over Evans and the referee turned around to count Evans down for the three count. As Love, Mathys, and Studd celebrated on the outside, the crowd errupted as Dan Starr and Derek Sweger came out to fight with them all the way to the dressing room area while Kris Evans looked extremely aggitated in the ring."
WINNER: JARED MATHYS ********************************** JAMES WILSON VS. JOHNNY BLADES ********************************** [HAYES]: "Following that match, we were ready to watch James Wilson battle
Johnny Blades in what was sure to be a knock-down, drag-out contest. Wilson continued his "loner" personna as he came to the ring with zero emotion and wore simply a pair of blue jeans and wrestling boots. Blades was all high spirits when he entered the ring and it looked like he got under the skin of
James Wilson from the start. Wilson began to wail away on Johnny in the beginning and Blades looked like he didn't know what to do. However, Blades began to absorb the beatings and soon turned the tide of the match as he showed Wilson he could fight with the best of them. Finally, in the end, as
Wilson was going for his inverted Death Valley Driver, Blades turned the move around and caught Wilson in a wicked looking neckbreaker manuever that floored Wilson. Blades followed it up with a beautiful powerbomb and a pin attempt TO SCORE THE WIN OVER JAMES WILSON! As Blades cracked open a beer in celebration, Nicholas Richard and Rex Watson came to the ring and began to wail away on Johnny Blades and EACH OTHER! James Wilson soon got into the mix of things and security had a tough time pulling all four brawlers off of each other."
WINNER: JOHNNY BLADES ********************************** NICHOLAS RICHARD VS. REX WATSON ********************************** [HAYES]: "Well, Watson and Richard were in a perfect place during the brawl as their match between each other was up next. Watson and Richard did not miss a step in the brawl as they continued it when the bell rang and the crowd was hot for both of them. Nicholas showed a true "never say die"
attitude as he took The Insane Icon to the limits and back. However, Watson soon grew quite tired of Richard's antics and decked him with one of the stiffest fists to the face I have ever seen. With Richard stunned, Watson turned him around and spiked him to the mat with a sick looking screw-driver manuever to get a quick win over Nicholas. However, Watson continued to fight with Nicholas after the bell and both men took the brawl all the way to the back. The strange thing was that the crowd loved every minute of it."
WINNER: REX WATSON ************************************************************ GPW REGIONAL TITLE MATCH: CAMERON KIDD VS. SEAN WILLEY ************************************************************ [HAYES]: "For the GPW Regional Title Match, Cameron Kidd knew this was the biggest match of his life and he had some comments to make before the match..."
<<The footage of Cameron's pre-match interview begins to play...>> <<A backstage shot is shown, inside Cameron Kidd's dressing room. The Kidd is shown taping up a few of his fingers, index and middle, on his right hand, a look of determination in his eyes. He looks into the camera and winks.>>
[KIDD]: "You know, Willey. Nobody's ever given me a title shot before. Why? I don't know. Maybe because I never deserved one." <<He continues taping up his fingers, then pulls the tape taught and tears it off. He leans over to tighten his boot laces.>>
[KIDD]: "So far in the GPW I haven't even won a match. I don't deserve a title shot here tonight, but if you're willing to put your gold up against me, I'm willing to give you ALL I got."
<<He smiles a little.>> [KIDD]: "I ain't planning on going in there and lying down. I'm here to give the fans what they want to see, and what they NEED to see. And I'm here for myself. I'm here to CONVINCE myself that I have what it takes to be something in the GPW. Willey... you're just my stepping stone. Whether I can get passed you or not remains to be seen, but I'm ready to take as big a chunk out of you as I can. Are you ready?"
<<He finishes lacing up his boots and then here's his theme music playing.>> [KIDD]: "You know I'm ready." <<From there, his interview in the ring begins to play..."Thunderstruck" by
AC/DC begins playing and the crowd pops as the arena lights flash. They continue flashing everytime the boys yell out "THUNDER!" as Cameron Kidd steps out onto
the ramp. Dressed in his ring apparel, a simple blue and white singlet, Kidd raises both arms up to the sky as the crowd gets louder. He has a mic in his hand.>>
[KIDD]: "HELLO KANSAS CITY!!!" <<A huge pop from the hometown faithful. Kidd continues walking to the ring, slapping hands with the fans.>>
[KIDD]: "Since this is my very first match on THE GLOBAL INVASION, allow me to introduce myself..."
<<He pauses and looks around.>> [KIDD]: "You've seen me before as Cameron Kidd in the GPW, as I go into each and every match and I LOSE!"
<<Some boos from the crowd. What else can they do?>> [KIDD]: "But recently I got my face slapped a few times. Not by anybody else out there, especially not... SEAN WILLEY.."
<<Loud boos come from the crowd.>> [KIDD]: "But from the man that is REPLACING ME HERE TONIGHT!" <<The crowd is stunned.>> [KIDD]: "This man spoke to me when I heard that Sean Willey wanted to make me CRY LIKE A BABY! He spoke to me last week and..."
<<He pauses, stepping into the ring.>> [KIDD]: "And he's speaking to you right now. Ladies and gentlemen, I AM THE NEW CAMERON KIDD!" <<A nice crowd pop comes up.>> [KIDD]: "The NEW Cameron Kidd is here NOT to take any KANGAROO CRAP from
ANYBODY! The NEW Cameron Kidd is here TO MAKE SURE PEOPLE LIKE SEAN WILLEY
GET WHAT THEY DESERVE!" <<Another crowd pop comes up.>> [KIDD]: "And while I'm on the subject of Sean Willey..." <<Some boos from the crowd. He slingshots himself up to the top turnbuckle in which he sits upon.>>
[KIDD]: "Maybe I can look him in the eye from up here. Heh." <<He pauses again.>> [KIDD]: "Now some of you out there are probably wondering just HOW IN THE
HELL I got myself into this mess!"
<<He looks around.>> [KIDD]: "And what I mean by THIS MESS is this REGIONAL TITLE MATCH! Let me tell you." <<He pauses again.>> [KIDD]: "I came here to show the world what I can do, and so far it's only impressing my grandmother, who's always complimenting me on my clean teeth.
Now THAT'S where I draw the line. If they best she can think of is clean teeth, then I know I gotta start getting some of them knocked out. Hear me out now."
<<He pauses again, brushing his blonde hair back with his left hand.>>
[KIDD]: "Sean Willey saw this and he thought I was a pushover. He started telling people he wanted to make me cry. Like a baby, nonetheless. THAT'S when I woke up. That's when I knew I needed a little help. So I gave a call to an old buddy of mine. A man that goes by the name of 'The Dominator' Don
Cameron!"
<<Some cheers out of recognition of the name come up.>> [KIDD]: "So Don came down here from his hometown in Nova Scotia, and he straightened me out. I've been working with him for the past bit, and he's shown me what I need to do to at least compete with Sean Willey. Otherwise,
I don't think I would have as much success."
<<He stops and jumps off the turnbuckle.>> [KIDD]: "I'm here tonight to show EVERYBODY just what I can do. Don unlocked the potential I've always had, and I have a more positive mindset about everything right now. And the very first man I get to take on is none other than your Regional Champion. Whoever thought that CAMERON KIDD would EVER get a title shot. Well, this is one title shot that I'm going to give my best."
<<He pauses and wipes his brow. The crowd has settled in now, and is watching him in the ring.>>
[KIDD]: "Let me tell you what this shot means to me. I've been busting my ass for the past ten years in some small wrestling leagues you've never heard of. I never got a chance to get a taste of gold. I never deserved it.
But now I'm here and it looks like somebody either has taken pity on me, or they think I'm a pushover. Sean Willey." <<He pauses as there are some boos.>> [KIDD]: "And now I'm here to prove myself. I'm done talking for now. Because
I'm ready to TRY AND WIN ME SOME GOLD!"
<<He gets a loud crowd pop.>> [KIDD]: "Willey, you drag your overgrown ass out here, because I'm ready for you. I know you're a lot bigger than me, but there's a WHOLE LOT of sayings about the bigger man falling down. Give me what you got AND I'LL GIVE IT
RIGHT BACK!" [HAYES]: "From there, Sean Willey came out and Cameron Kidd was on him like white on rice. Kidd unloaded on Willey with his energetic aerial manuevers and all of Kansas City was behind the youngster. The match was a true David against Goliath affair as Willey just towered over his opponent. As the match went on, Kidd's heart didn't die as he would take every brtual shot from Sean and come back for more. Willey didn't know what to do, other than to beat Kidd down each time he got up. Kidd looked completely focused and did not let the crowd get to him as his focus was squarely on the match.
Finally, Kidd was going for a cross body block off the top rope but Willey caught him. He turned Kidd around like a sack of potatoes and put him in posistion for a Death Valley Driver until JOHNNY ADONIS ran to the ring and kicked Willey in the leg. The referee immediately called for the bell as the crowd booed because he had to disqualify Cameron Kidd. Once Adonis kicked Willey, he left the ring as Sean looked extremely aggitated. The bad thing was that Sean took his anger out on Cameron Kidd and began to beat him senseless. Finally, the crowd errupted as Adam Rogers and Luke Andrews rushed to the ring and got Kidd out of there. It was the right thing to do as Kidd needed someone to help him. Back inn the ring, Willey grabbed his title belt and stalked towards the back, looking for Johnny Adonis."
WINNER: SEAN WILLEY(DQ) ****************************** T'CHAMUNGA VS. THE IRONMAN ****************************** [HAYES]: "Our next contest was nothing pretty as The Zinmaster brought out his new recruit, The Ironman, to battle The Island Rat, T'Chamunga. Well,
The Zinmaster had a better shot fighting on crutches than The Ironman did because T'Chamunga tore him limb from limb. Like the Zack Sharp match, most of the action is too violent to show but, in the end, T'Chamunga stretched
The Ironman severely in The Panda Submission to score the win. As
T'Chamunga was walking back with Khan Troll, he was met by a chair shot from the curtain area and it turned out to be THE PHOENIX! The crowd errupted as both men began to duke it out to the dressing room area. When and if these two ever meet in the ring again, it is going to be World War III times TEN!"
WINNER: T'CHAMUNGA ************************ RAPIER VS. SHADOWMAN ************************ [HAYES]: "Starting his singles career again, Rapier squared off against former rival, Shadowman, in a suprisingly good singles match. With both men used to tag-team wrestling, they showed us that they were singles wrestlers before they won tag-gold. Both men traded control of the match and the crowd was definetly behind Rapier from the start. As soon as Rapier would hit a move, Shadowman would bend the rules to regain control and would level
Rapier with a move of his own. Finally, Zack Sharp came to the ring and distracted Rapier long enough for Shadowman to school-boy Rapier and steal the pin away from The Warrior of Truth."
WINNER: SHADOWMAN ***************************** LUKE ANDREWS VS. RIC STUDD ***************************** [HAYES]: "In the battle of who is the real California Love, newcomer Luke
Andrews met perennial star, Ric Studd, in a very symbolic encounter. Studd, the seasoned veteran, had control of most of the first half of the match as he wore Andrews down and really beat him badly for the whole "California
Love" image. However, the crowd began to get behind Luke and he soon found himself on the giving side of a tail whipping to one Ric Studd. Luke acted on pure adrenaline and took Studd to his limits until Ric caught Luke with a low blow followed by a devastating piledriver. As Studd was going for the pin, none other than Dan Starr, with his copy of that SCW National Title
Belt, stood in the entrance area and mocked Studd. Studd was infuriated and droppped his guard as Andrews came up from behind, turned him around, and drilled him with a T-Bone Suplex to STEAL THE WIN AWAY! The crowd went crazy as Andrews scored the pin over Studd and Studd himself was livid.
Security had to detain him as Starr looked on and laughed loudly." WINNER: LUKE ANDREWS ******************************* CARL BLAIR VS. FURIOUS STEELE ******************************* [HAYES]: "The next match was one of our two main events of the evening as
Carl Blair met Furious Steele in a match that had great potential. It was
Hi-C battling Team Ego and the crowd simply wanted to watch each man kill each other. However, Blair had a core group of fans behind him for his recent situation with Team Ego and Mad Valentine. The match itself was a highlight of both men's styles as Blair outpaced Steele with his quick and energetic offense. Blair really, in my mind, is one of the fastest men in wrestling today and he showed me in this match why. He was just moving all over the ring, jumping from the ropes to the ring, bouncing off the ropes, and so on. However, Steele absorbed the blows up until the point he was ready to take control. As Blair jumped off the ropes with a dropkick,
Steele brushed him away and began to wail away on Blair with vicious fists.
After the referee pulled him off of Blair, Steele got right back on him with more vicious fists. Finally, Steele caught an oncoming Blair with a devastating heart punch that floored Blair and gave Steele the pinfall win.
Afterwards, Steele invited Page and Sabbath to the ring where they gave
Blair a vicious beatdown and Team Ego was no-where to be found. Afterwards,
Hi-C left in triumph while Blair left furious at what just happened and what just didn't happen."
WINNER: FURIOUS STEELE *************************************** THE ACCOLADES OF EMMINENCE VS. VIP *************************************** [HAYES]: "In our main event of the evening, Eugene Robinson and Adam Rogers met Steve Stellar and William Intrigue of VIP in a great little tag-team affair. Robinson and Rogers lived up to their name and showed the world they are good singles wrestlers and good tag-team wrestlers. It was really a one sided affair as VIP wasn't prepared for the skill that the Accolades had coming into the match. In the end, Rogers caught Intrigue with a
Tilt-a-Whirl Tiger Driver while Eugene Robinson dropped Stellar with a cradle Belly to Back suplex to score the double pinfall win. As The
Accolades celebrated in the ring, Tommy Gunn and Mad Valentine stormed the ring and began to duke it out with Robinson and Rogers. The crowd errupted more as Hi-C soon got into the mix of things in addition to Luke Andrews. A massive brawl ensued as security tried desperately to settle things down.
From there, the show ended and every was looking towards tommorow night in
St. Louis!" WINNERS: THE ACCOLADES OF EMMINNCE <<The shot of the empty arena in Kansas City, Missouri is shown on the screen while Hayes wraps up the show...>>
[HAYES]: "So folks, remember, tommorow night, we invade The TWA Dome, home of the NFC Champions, St. Louis Rams. It is our special Super Bowl Edition of Meltdown and you will see the following action....Sabbath against The
Phoenix, Jesse Muldoon against Television Champion Johnny Adonis....Mad
Valentine defending his American Title against Adam Rogers...and a massive main event involving Tommy Gunn going against Priest Noble, The World
Heavyweight Champion, in a non-title match. Folks, we have all that and SO
MUCH more in store for you tommorow. Tune in when we invade St. Louis and show the folks a party that will rival the Superbowl!"
<<The camera fades to black as a slide show effect of clips from the action shown tonight plays to the music of "Turn the Page" by Metallica...>>
