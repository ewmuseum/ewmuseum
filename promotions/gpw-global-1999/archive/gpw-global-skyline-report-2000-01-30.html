============================================================================ ==== GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING'S GLOBAL SKYLINE REPORT Vol 2 #3 1/30/2000 ============================================================================ ==== ******************** TABLE OF CONTENTS ******************** 1]Notes From the Front Office/Informative Material: PLEASE READ 2]The News and Rumor Wire: TOUGH LOVE STARTS TO TAKE SHAPE 3]Comings/Goings/Injured 4]Weekly Line-Ups 5]Rankings 6]Most Popular/Most Hated 7]Question of the Week ============================================================================ ==== ****************************** NOTES FROM THE FRONT OFFICE ****************************** -Hey guys, I've decided to put together a weekly, informative newsletter so you guys can have something to look at concerning where you stand in the league, what the current rumblings are, where things are to go, and so on. I would like feedback on this at [detail withheld] -Here are some informative materials that you may find useful: -FLASHES GO TO: [detail withheld] -ALL STRATS, CARD INTERVIEWS QUESTIONS, IDEAS, and ANYTHING ELSE GO TO: [detail withheld] AND [detail withheld] -OUR LEAGUE WEBSITE(KUDOS TO THE DESIGNER) IS: http://www.geocities.com/globalprowrestling : A LOT HAS BEEN UPDATED!!!! CHECK IT OUT(RANKINGS WILL BE UPDATED ON THE SITE ASAP) -VOTE FOR GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING ON E-ZONE AT: http://ewzone.virtualave.net -TO BE TAKEN OFF THE MAILING LIST CONTACT: [detail withheld] -DEADLINES FOR ALL CARD MATERIAL IS THURSDAY NIGHT AT 10:00 P.M. CST!!!!! I REALLY MEAN THIS....FROM NOW ON IF I GET STUFF AFTER THE DEADLINE, THERE IS A 99.999% CHANCE IT WILL BE TRASHED. ALSO, ROLEPLAY DEALINES COME AT 9:00 P.M. CST ON TUESDAYS AND THURSDAYS SO PLEASE GET THEM IN BEFORE THEN. ALSO, I WILL START SENDING VIGNETTES AND INTERVIEWS FOR CARDS BACK IF THEY ARE NOT PROPERLY FORMATTED. That means action in the << >> and the speaker's LAST AND ONLY LAST NAME IN ALL CAPS. For example, take the name Peter Caradine. It would be written like this and only this: [CARADINE]: "Text." Also, remember, the video screen is called THE GLOBAL VIEW AND NOTHING ELSE! -Also, I would like to see you all start to treat Shockwave as the television program that it is. ANGLES can progress on this show and don't think that just because it isn't Meltdown, you shouldn't make an attempt to do so. Same goes for The Global Invasion. However, try to steer away from doing in ring interviews on Shockwave. Use Shockwave to get your character over in an out-of-ring setting and use Global Invasion and Meltdown for in-rings. -I would LOVE comments, thoughts, and criticism on the pay-per-view concerning presentation and look. If you have problems with the bookings, well, you know my policy on that. Also, I'd like to hear what you thought were the highlights of the ppv as well. -Guys, PLEASE REMEMBER THAT IS STILL THE BEGINNING OF FEBRUARY IN GPW! Please make no mention of the computer crash or anything that would have happened after the time we are at now. ============================================================================ ==== ************************** THE NEWS AND RUMOR MILL: ************************** -The biggest news coming out of this week is Commisioner Blain's 2 shocking announcements on Meltdown. The first being that Dan Starr and Ric Studd will finally square off against each other this coming week on Meltdown in Minneapolis and that the National Title situation will end. The second is actually two announcements in one. First, he ordered that a Dream Partners Main Event Match will occur next week on Meltdown in which PRIEST NOBLE AND EUGENE ROBINSON WILL BATTLE CHRIS PAGE AND MAD VALENTINE! To top that off, at "Tough Love" on February 13th, Eugene Robinson will meet Mad Valentine in a #1 Contender's Match while Priest Noble fights Chris Page for the GPW World Heavyweight Title in a Death Match! The next two weeks in GPW are going to be loaded with action!" -Also announced for Meltdown this week, Tommy Gunn, after beating Priest Noble, will meet Adam Rogers for Rogers' newly won GPW American Heavyweight Title. We all saw The American Title Match in which Carl Blair "accidentially" hit Mad Valentine but, the real question, is why did Furious Steele come down and pretty much assist Adam Rogers? Things just continue to get weirder and weirder here in Global Pro-Wrestling. Also on tap for Meltdown, the newly formed duo of Johnny Adonis and Sean Willey will battle the team of Cameron Kidd and a mystery partner. We all saw the ruse Adonis and Willey put on this week and, now, The Regional and Television Champions are together. Plus, we all saw Adonis deny Kidd his rightful shot at the Regional Title which was really heart-breaking -Many in the back speculated that Priest Noble was merely playing with Tommy Gunn and actually let Gunn beat him, just to annoy Team Ego and mostly Mad Valentine. However, this is not a confirmed statement. -We all saw him on Meltdown and, this week, he'll explain his actions: In Minnesota, Alec Ace will adress GPW for the first time since December! -In addition to the Dan Starr and Ric Studd epic battle that will take place this weekend, Commisioner Blain has also ordered Kris Evans and Derek Sweger to battle Lance Love and Jared Mathys on The Global Invasion from St. Paul. That one should be real heated as well -Word has it that a match pitting Violence and Corruption against XTremeCharismas at Tough Love for the GPW World Tag-Team Titles is about to be signed. If signed, it will most likely be a NO Disqualification Match. This week on The Global Invasion, Furious Steele meets Bryan Meredith in a NO DQ match-up. -The conflicts in Team Ego continue to grow as Carl Blair becomes more and more distant. Many people are stating it is only a matter of time before he leaves the group. -GPW would like to welcome a group of newcomers to the league. We wish the best of luck to the following superstars as they make their league debuts this week: Oyabun, Crazy J, and Damon Knight. -We regretfully report that the following people will be leaving The GPW family: Dagger Lorath, the team of VIP, Czar, Jesse Muldoon, and Drew Archer. Archer is leaving due to injuries suffered at the hands of Hi-C, Muldoon is taking an "extended vacation" but will fulfill committments through the weekend, Dagger Lorath turned in his notice, and VIP also did as well. VIP will also be fulfilling their committments this weekend just like Jesse Muldoon -Word has it that Commisioner Blain is ready to do something regarding the three way fight between Johnny Blades, Rex Watson, and Nicholas Richard. Insiders are looking at a potential cage match in the near future. Word also has it that James Wilson now wants a piece of all three men. -With Dagger Lorath gone, word has it that Shadowman will be teaming with Zack Sharp in the future. However, Sharp still is The GPW Brass Knux Champion and will defend his gold this weekend against Rune. -We're getting more mail each and every day about signing a T'Chamunga vs. The Phoenix match for Tough Love. People really want to see these two lunatics tear each other to shreads. -Word has it that Rapier is looking to get into the Television Title hunt along with Luke Andrews. Both have been very vocal about Johnny Adonis ducking and dodging them when it comes time for match assignments. -Samhain returns to GPW this week to due battle with The Phoenix. Should be interesting. -The mysterious figure once again showed up during The Fantasm's match and, no, we still don't know who it is. -The Zinmaster has recovered from his injuries and will be in action this weekend as he squares off against James Wilson. -ANY COMMENTS, CRITICISM, OR QUESTIONS....SEND THEM MY WAY :) ============================================================================ ==== ******************************* COMINGS, GOINGS, AND INJURED ******************************* COMINGS: Oyabun Crazy J Damon Knight GOINGS: VIP(After this Week) Jesse Muldoon(After this Week) Dagger Lorath Czar Drew Archer INJURED: No serious injuries ============================================================================ ==== ============================================================================ ==== GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING WEEKLY LINE-UPS ============================================================================ ==== ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^ HOUSE SHOW LINE-UP FOR: 2/3/2000: Duluth: RECAPPED ON THE GLOBAL INVASION ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^ 1.Oyabun vs. Shane Jackson 2.Crazy J vs. Flic Rogers 3.Kris Evans vs. Lance Love 4.Derek Sweger vs. Jared Mathys 5.MAIN EVENT: Samhain vs. Damon Knight ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^ GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING PROUDLY PRESENTS "THE GLOBAL INVASION" 2/5/2000 From ST.
