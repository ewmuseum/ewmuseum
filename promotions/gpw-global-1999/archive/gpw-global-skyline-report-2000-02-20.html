============================================================================ ==== GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING'S GLOBAL SKYLINE REPORT Vol 2 #6 2/20/2000 ============================================================================ ==== ******************** TABLE OF CONTENTS ******************** 1]Notes From the Front Office/Informative Material: PLEASE READ 2]The News and Rumor Wire: WE ARE ON THE ROAD TO DAYTONA! 3]Comings/Goings/Injured 4]Weekly Line-Ups 5]Rankings 6]Most Popular/Most Hated 7]Question of the Week 8]TENATIVE PPV LINE-UP ============================================================================ ==== ****************************** NOTES FROM THE FRONT OFFICE ****************************** -Hey guys, I've decided to put together a weekly, informative newsletter so you guys can have something to look at concerning where you stand in the league, what the current rumblings are, where things are to go, and so on. I would like feedback on this at [detail withheld] -Here are some informative materials that you may find useful: -FLASHES GO TO: [detail withheld] -ALL STRATS, CARD INTERVIEWS QUESTIONS, IDEAS, and ANYTHING ELSE GO TO: [detail withheld] -OUR LEAGUE WEBSITE(KUDOS TO THE DESIGNER) IS: http://www.geocities.com/globalprowrestling : The webmaster has said he will be out of town this week but will update things when he gets back. EMAIL [detail withheld] to TELL HIM WHAT YOU WOULD LIKE TO SEE ON THE WEBSITE. EMAIL ME A COPY AS WELL! -TO BE TAKEN OFF THE MAILING LIST CONTACT: [detail withheld] -DEADLINES FOR ALL CARD MATERIAL IS THURSDAY NIGHT AT 10:00 P.M. CST!!!!! I REALLY MEAN THIS....FROM NOW ON IF I GET STUFF AFTER THE DEADLINE, THERE IS A 99.999% CHANCE IT WILL BE TRASHED. ALSO, ROLEPLAY DEALINES COME AT 9:00 P.M. CST ON TUESDAYS AND THURSDAYS SO PLEASE GET THEM IN BEFORE THEN. ALSO, I WILL START SENDING VIGNETTES AND INTERVIEWS FOR CARDS BACK IF THEY ARE NOT PROPERLY FORMATTED. That means action in the << >> and the speaker's LAST AND ONLY LAST NAME IN ALL CAPS. For example, take the name Peter Caradine. It would be written like this and only this: [CARADINE]: "Text." Also, remember, the video screen is called THE GLOBAL VIEW AND NOTHING ELSE! IF YOU NEED REFERENCE MATERIAL FOR AN INTERVIEW OR ENTRANCE, READ MELTDOWN OR CONTACT ME. -Also, I would like to see you all start to treat Shockwave as the television program that it is. ANGLES can progress on this show and don't think that just because it isn't Meltdown, you shouldn't make an attempt to do so. Same goes for The Global Invasion. However, try to steer away from doing in ring interviews on Shockwave. Use Shockwave to get your character over in an out-of-ring setting and use Global Invasion and Meltdown for in-rings. MAKE THINGS INTERESTING AND STAND OUT. Shockwave is the character development program! -PLEASE REFRAIN from referring to other leagues in your flashes. It takes away from the image I'm trying to build of GPW being the only federation worth joining on the internet :) -I would LOVE comments, thoughts, and criticism on the pay-per-view concerning presentation and look. If you have problems with the bookings, well, you know my policy on that. Also, I'd like to hear what you thought were the highlights of the ppv as well. -Guys, you really need to read the following: A lot of you are getting lazy and are not participating while I have your characters in high profile angles. Nothing makes me madder than putting a character in a developed storyline and then have his handler not contribute or participate. Just because your wrestler is not on Meltdown DOESN'T mean you don't have to roleplay. The Global Invasion is JUST AS important as Meltdown. I would write full matches for Invasion if I could but I just don't have the time. It is up to you guys to carry your weight in the angles you are put in. You've got to make whatever your doing look like that's the best thing running and not worry about working the "main event" angles or anything. You have to prove to me your are capable of working that by accepting the other storylines your character is given. I try to advance everyone each week but, it's getting hard when only a certain group roleplays and the rest just sit idle. -There are 8 parts of Meltdown this week. If you didn't get some of it, let me know because my mail program is acting weird with mass mailings. ============================================================================ ==== ************************** THE NEWS AND RUMOR MILL: ************************** -Things continue to get weirder and weirder here in Global Pro-Wrestling as the Team Ego/Hi-C fued continues to develop. You all saw on Meltdown how Mad Valentine ignored Chris Page in his victory speech, how Chris Page nearly lost it as a result, how Hi-C just beat the hell out of their own team-mate, Sabbath and THEN, how they debuted a new member in Johnny Blades. From there, they proceeded to get the best of Team Ego and aggrivated an already injured knee of Carl Blair. Well, Commisioner Blain saw enough and decided to lay it out on the line as he went ahead and made the main event for Duel on the Dunes 2000 public. Chris Page will meet Mad Valentine inside of a steel cage match but, the catch is that the match will be a LADDER MATCH! A special cage will be designed and the World Title will be attached to the top of the cage. Two ladders will be inside the cage to be used and the man who grabs the Belt will be the undisputed World Heavyweight Champion. Commisioner Blain EXPECTS the fued to end with this match but I wouldn't count on it. This week, Mad Valentine makes the customary non-title fight as he will take on Johnny Blades in the main event in Las Vegas. However, word has it that Valentine is looking to weasel his way out of the match because the World Champion has never won the non-title match two weeks after winning the belt. In addition, Tommy Gunn will have to deal with Chris Page. One more thing, it wasn't me, Team Ego is catching on with the fans and they even made our MOST POPULAR LIST!" -In other news, we all witnessed the brutal assault by Sean Willey on Cameron Kidd this weekend. Word has it that Cameron will be no-where near Nevada this weekend. Sean Willey, though, will have a title defense on Meltdown against Kyle Cole and, on The Global Invasion, he will team with Johnny Adonis to meet XTremeCharisma in a non-title match. Speaking of Johnny Adonis, Commisioner Blain is close to signing a rematch betweem Adonis and Andrews for the PPV but, managers for Damon Knight are lobbying to get Knight involved as well. This Saturday in Reno, Knight will meet Luke Andrews. -Speaking of XTremeCharisma, we all saw their interview on Meltdown and then the attack by Domination afterwards. A tense war, in my mind, has been sparked between both teams and Daytona looks to be the collision course. We all know what XTremeCharisma will have to deal with this Saturday but, Domination has a test as well. They will meet "Violence and Corruption 2000", otherwise known as Furious Steele and Johnny Blades. -Breaking news from the talent department: Law and Disorder along with Zack Sharp gave their notices after fulfilling their committments this weekend. Sharp was scheduled to team with Shadowman in the coming weeks while Law and Disorder would have factored in well with Domination back. As a result, Shadowman will give an interview on Meltdown this Sunday in addition to working a house show match against Kyle Cole in Carson City. As you all saw on Meltdown, Law and Disorder lost their last match to Superior Darkness, Bruno Bordeaux and The Zinmaster. Afterwards, "The Black Knight" debuted and, word has it that Superior Darkness have been given the opportunity to defend the American Tag-Team Titles with any three members. This Saturday in Reno, two of the three members will defend the gold against The Rage Machine. -Speaking of The Zinmaster, he is still lobbying Blain to sign a Japanese Death Match for Duel on The Dunes 2000 against The Phoenix. Blain has yet to comment on the situation but word has it that he is seriously considering it. This Saturday, The Zinmaster will meet a friend of The Phoenix, Rune, for Rune's GPW Brass Knux Title while The Phoenix must deal with The Japanese Sensation, Oyabun. In my mind, these two matches could steal the card. Could you imagine what would happen if Rune and The Phoenix ever met Oyabun and his apparent ally, T'Chamunga in a tag-match? I get goose bumps at the thought. -A factor in the American Title situation will be settled this Sunday in Las Vegas as Alec Ace will finally get his shot at Adam Rogers. We should all be on the look out for Furious Steele for this one. Ace, though, will have to deal with James Wilson the night before so Rogers might be at an advantage. -Not one to waste any time, Commisioner Blain went ahead and signed a special tag-match to open Meltdown this Sunday. The apparently newly formed duo of Rex Watson and Ric Studd will meet Ric Donovan and Kris Evans. Donovan and Studd hate each other while Evans and Watson hate everyone. It should be a great match as both Evans and Watson showed real break-out levels this past weekend. Watson, though, will have to deal with Lance Walker in Reno and he might have a tough challenge with that one. -GPW would like to welcome newcomer Frank "The Flash" Ewiak to GPW. His first match will take place this week in Carson City, Nevada as he will take on Flic Rogers. -Global Professional Wrestling would like to announce its plans for after "Duel on the Dunes 2000" which will be on March 26th. It has been decided that GPW will take a two week hiatus in programming until Saturday, April 8th and Sunday, April 9th. On those two days, we will invade the country of Japan for our "Bridging the Gap" Tour. We will be in Osaka, Japan on the 8th and The Tokyo Dome on April 9th for the special edition of Meltdown entitled "Bridging the Gap." These two shows will be the only programs run in the month of April for Global Pro-Wrestling as we will give our wrestlers time off for personal reasons. Regular programming will return on May 6th and 7th with shows in Tempe and Phoenix, Arizona. Our May PPV will take place on Sunday night, May 28th from New Orleans, Louisiana and will be entitled "Starlight Galatic." We also would like to announce the cancellation of our New Jersey swing right before Duel on The Dunes 2000. So, before the PPV, we have shows in Nevada, Canada, and England, before returning to the states for the big showdown in Daytona Beach. -GPW would like to ask, you, the talent to send in submissions to The Global Skyline Report. If you have an editorial comment or rumors you want to drop, send them in and we'll try to air them!" -ANY COMMENTS, CRITICISM, OR QUESTIONS....SEND THEM MY WAY :) ============================================================================ ==== ******************************* COMINGS, GOINGS, AND INJURED ******************************* COMINGS: Frank Ewiak Domination GOINGS: Zack Sharp(Gave Notice) Law and Disorder(Gave Notice) Sabbath(AWOL) INJURED: Carl Blair-Knee Injury(Time out unknown) Samhain-Numerous-(Time out unknown) ============================================================================ ==== ============================================================================ ==== GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING WEEKLY LINE-UPS ============================================================================ ==== ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^ HOUSE SHOW LINE-UP FOR: 2/24/2000: Carson City, Nevada: RECAPPED ON THE GLOBAL INVASION ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^ 1.
