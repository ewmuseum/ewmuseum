Also, remember, the video screen is called THE GLOBAL VIEW AND NOTHING ELSE! -Schedule of things: THIS WEEK: Tuesday, Thursday: SHOCKWAVE Saturday: Global Invasion Sunday: A special edition of Shockwave(It will go out sometime in the afternoon-time is unknown as of yet) Monday, January 10th: 3:00 PM CST-ALL PAY-PER-VIEW STRATS, VIGNETTES, ETC MUST BE IN AT THIS TIME Sunday-Pay-Per-View -Also, I would like to see you all start to treat Shockwave as the television program that it is. ANGLES can progress on this show and don't think that just because it isn't Meltdown, you shouldn't make an attempt to do so. Same goes for The Global Invasion. However, try to steer away from doing in ring interviews on Shockwave. Use Shockwave to get your character over in an out-of-ring setting and use Global Invasion and Meltdown for
in-rings. ============================================================================ ==== ************************** THE NEWS AND RUMOR MILL: ************************** -We're going to go ahead and announce what is on tap for: FUTURE FIGHT 2000: THE BATTLE OF LOS ANGELES live January 16th from The Staples Center in Los Angeles, California. Under each match, an updated news brief has been added....
1.MAIN EVENT: TRIPLE THREAT LADDER MATCH FOR THE GPW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT
TITLE: FURIOUS STEELE VS. PRIEST NOBLE VS. EUGENE ROBINSON(C): After the assault Steele put on Adam Rogers during Meltdown this week, the level of violence this man is capable of reached new heights. He even went so far as to smashing Eugene Robinson's face into a steel chair. However, Priest
Noble came down and prevented Steele from doing any further damage.
Robinson, though, didn't know what to make of the situation as he just stared Noble down. In two weeks, all three men will get the fight out of their system when they fight in the first ever Triple Threat Ladder Match in Global Pro-Wrestling and the World Title will be up for grabs.
2.TAG TEAM GRUDGE MATCH: ADAM ROGERS AND TBA VS. CHRIS PAGE AND TBA: It is no mystery that Chris Page and Furious Steele are alligned in some way but the question now is how does Drew Archer fit into the equation, especially after helped Page and Steele out on Meltdown this week. Word has it that
Page is close to announcing Archer as his partner for this match on the pay-per-view. On the other hand, the favorite for Rogers' partner has to be the gutsy Andrew Bailey as he went in the belly of the beast to help
Robinson and Rogers out as they were being brutalized by Archer, Steele, and Page. However, both Page and Rogers have until the match itself to chose a partner for this NO-DQ tag-team match.
3.TEXAS TORNADO TAG-TEAM MATCH FOR THE GPW WORLD TAG-TEAM TITLES: RUNE AND
RAPIER VS. DAGGER LORATH AND SHADOWMAN(C): After winning the triangle match on Global Invasion, Dagger Lorath added to the momentum of winning the tag-belts back on December 19th. Toss that in with an apparent alliance with Zack Sharp, the team of Shadowman and Dagger Lorath appear to be on a collision course with greatness. However, the ever popular team of Rune and Rapier will try to derail their train in Los Angeles and become two time World Tag-Team Champions. Word has it that Rapier is taking the title loss pretty hard.
4.DOG COLLAR MATCH FOR THE GPW AMERICAN HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE: JAMES WILSON VS
MAD VALENTINE(C): You all saw Wilson tear into Valentine this week on
Meltdown after comments Valentine made during the World Television Title contendership match. Well, word has it that Wilson had to be held back by the entire Global Security Force in order to stop him from seriously injuring Valentine. When these two are locked together by a dog collar, the amount of blood loss that will occur might rival an Emergency Room on a real bad night. Remember, Carl Blair will be banned from ringside and, if he shows up, Valentine will lose the title on the spot.
5.GPW WORLD TELEVISION TITLE MATCH: TBA VS. JOHNNY ADONIS(C): The opponent for Johnny Adonis still has yet to be determined, even though Blair won the
Television Title contendership match on Meltdown. Righ after it went off the air, Global Pro-Wrestling officials declared that Lance Love was cheated out of the win and, therefore, on The Global Invasion that will occur on January 8th from Phoenix, Arizona, Lance Love will meet Carl Blair and the winner will once and for all go to Los Angeles to meet Johnny
Adonis.
6.GPW AMERICAN TAG-TEAM TITLE MATCH: TBA vs. VIP(C): After their title win,
VIP threw a kink into things concerning the American Tag-Team Title match at the pay-per-view. As of right now, they will defend the gold against the winner of the match that will pit Law and Disorder against Justice 2K.
The match will occur on January 8th in Phoenix, Arizona. One way or the other, VIP will get to meet one of the top teams in Global Pro-Wrestling for their first big title defense.
7.TRIPLE THREAT CAGE MATCH FOR THE GPW BRASS KNUX TITLE: THE ZINMASTER VS. T'CHAMUNGA VS. ZACK SHARP(C): This week on Global Invasion, The Zinmaster shocked the world as he scored a pin over Zack Sharp in a non-title match.
This came after Dagger Lorath and Shadowman assisted Sharp in the match but, as Sharp was about to finish things off, T'Chamunga came from out of no-where and clocked Sharp, allowing The Zinmaster to get the win. Now this match is heated more than ever as all three men are wanting to tear each other apart and the cage match will be the perfect spot.
8.LOS ANGELES DEATH MATCH: RYAN STONE VS. THE PHOENIX: Medical crews are getting prepared for this one as they are expecting both men to absolutely destroy each other. Global Pro-Wrestling has already told The Phoenix that only tables and chairs will be allowed to be used during the match and nothing else. Stone has been quite calm about this potential career ending match although one has to wonder what is going through his head as he is set to face The Phoenix in Stone's home town.
ALSO ON TAP FOR THE SHOW: LEW SPECTRE AND RIC STUDD'S SUSPENSION WILL BE
LIFTED ON JANUARY 16TH AND THEY HAVE BEEN GIVEN A CHANCE TO TELL THEIR SIDE
OF THE STORY AS TO WHY RIC STUDD WAS HELPING LEW SPECTRE INTO THE ARENA ON
DECEMBER 5TH. IN ADDITION, THE MYSTERIOUS FIGURE KNOWN SIMPLY AS "THE MAN"
WILL MAKE HIS IDENTITY REVEALED! PLUS: THE UNDERCARD HAS BEEN ANNOUNCED AND IT CAN BE VIEWED UNDER THE LINE-UP FOR THIS WEEK'S GLOBAL INVASION! -In other news, Global Pro-Wrestling would like to welcome Insane Rex Watson to the league. Watson just signed a contract with Global Pro-Wrestling and will make some type of appearence in Phoenix, Arizona on
January 8th What he has planned is unknown at this time.
-We would also like to welcome back Chris Sunrise to the league after an extended vacaction. He too will make an appearence on January 8th and, like Watson, what he has planned is unknown at this time.
-With the abrupt closing of Georgia Regional Wrestling, Global Pro-Wrestling has been contacted by several former members of the organization about joining. If you knew who contacted us, you would be
SHOCKED! Some might be debuting as early as after the pay-per-view or even during it at some time!"
-Please don't keep calling the company and asking what those interrupters were in Shockwave. We have no idea who it is but someone with an apparent love of Rage Against the Machine is interrupting Shockwave and, in addition, he got a hold of Meltdown this week as well.
-Word has it that Carl Blair was a bit ticked at Mad Valentine's lack of support after the attack from Lance Love on Meltdown. More on this as we get it. -It looks like The Brass Knux Division is really heating up as Bryan Meredith, Czar, and even Ramon Lopez impressed Global officials a great deal this week. They even signed Czar and Lopez to fight on The Global
Invastion this week.
-One has to feel sorry for Cameron Kidd as he lost to Nicholas Richard this week. Taking nothing away from The Army of One but Kidd really showed a lot of heart this week.
-We are sad to report that The Rough Riders have retired from active competition in Global Pro-Wrestling. We wish them the best of luck in the future. -ANY COMMENTS, CRITICISM, OR QUESTIONS....SEND THEM MY WAY :) ============================================================================ ==== ******************************* COMINGS, GOINGS, AND INJURED ******************************* COMINGS: Rex Watson Chris Sunrise GOINGS: The Rough Riders INJURED: Vic V.
