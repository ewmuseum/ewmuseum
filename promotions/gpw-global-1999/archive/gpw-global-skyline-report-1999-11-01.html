I would like feedback on this at [detail withheld] -Here are some informative materials that you may find useful: -FLASHES GO TO: [detail withheld] AND [detail withheld] -ALL STRATS, CARD INTERVIEWS QUESTIONS, IDEAS, and ANYTHING ELSE GO TO: [detail withheld] AND [detail withheld] -OUR LEAGUE WEBSITE(KUDOS TO THE DESIGNER) IS: http://www.geocities.com/globalprowrestling -VOTE FOR GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING ON E-ZONE AT: http://ewzone.virtualave.net -TO BE TAKEN OFF THE MAILING LIST CONTACT: [detail withheld] =========================================================================== ********************** THE NEWS AND RUMOR MILL ********************** -The major news this week has to be the inconclusive ending of the triangle match at Meltdown to determine the number one contender to the GPW World Heavyweight Title. Word directly from Global Pro-Wrestling headquarters is that a series of matches will occur up until the time of the Turkey Time Terror edition of Meltdown(November 28th) and the number one contender will be determined AND announced on November 17th, directly before an off-weekend for Global Pro-Wrestling. Word also has it that some of these determining matches will be announced publicly at determining matches while others will not. You never know when you will be reviewed! -As we all saw on Meltdown, Lance Love, after being thrown out of The Diamond Dynasty, quickly regrouped and enlisted the services of former SCW National Champion and former tag-team partner of Chris Page, Chris Spencer. Word has it that Love will not stop at Spencer and he has his eye on certain other individuals. -As a result of their attack on Law and Disorder, Thunderstorm will now get a shot at the World Tag-Team Champions at Turkey Time Terror.....however, they must first get through 2 singles matches: Anarchy vs. Crash and Justice vs. Blast. The outcome of these singles matches will determine what stipulation will be in effect for their meeting at that very special edition of Meltdown. Also, word has it that something fishy is up concerning this situation and we're in the process of trying to find out more. -Word has it that Phillip Starks is very ill about not getting a return match with James Wilson for the American Heavyweight Title. Currently, Wilson is having issues with Mad Valentine while getting assistance from Vic V. Hill. It will be interesting to see if Starks has a hand in the outcome of this battle. -Stemming from the attack they recieved at the hands of The Gangsta Posse, Westside Thunder will be out of action for an undisclosed amount of time. The Posse was subsequently fined an undisclosed amount for the brutallity enflicted in the assault. -Global Pro-Wrestling would like to announce its first annual "I Want to be a Valet" contest. A lucky lady will win a chance to escort a wrestler of her choice at Turkey Time Terror. Enter today! -Global Pro-Wrestling would like to announce that its second pay-per-view extravanga will happen in early January and it will be entitled "Fight for the Future." Word has it that Los Angeles is the front runner for the city to host the event. -Congratulations are in store to our new champions crowned this week: The Rough Riders(American Tag-Team Champions), Alec Ace(Regional Champion), and Brian Sullivan(Brass Knux Champion). Word has it that The Gangsta Posse has now set its eyes on The Rough Riders. Alec Ace is said to be boasting about something very suprising about to happen and Brian Sullivan was seen looking for Derrek Sweger and Bart Simmons after The Global Invasion tapings. He also ran into The Phoenix, the current Television Champion, and had a few words with him as well. Word also has it that Khan Troll is in the process of getting T'Chamunga signed for a Brass Knux Title match later on down the road. -Ric Studd has been leveled with yet another fine and now it's topping the $500,000 range along with the subsequent suspension that he's obviously ignoring. People are still trying to figure out who was responsible for the lights going out during Ric Studd's infamous incident on Global Invasion this week. -It appears that both Rune and Rapier are on a collision course with one Ike Harris and, subsequently, Merc. Word has it that Harris and Merc have a couple things up their sleeve for both Rapier and Rune. -Word has it that Brent Star, Ryan Stone, Westside Walavan, Flic Rogers, Jared Mathys, The Frasier Brothers, and Chris Sunrise are experiencing a slump and are trying desperately to break out of it. -After the attack on Sunday, news officials tried to get a word with Dagger Lorath but he just ignored them. More on this as it develops. -Finally, we have also been trying to get in contact with The Shadowman but he, as well, have ignored our requests. -COMMENTS AND CRITICISM IS WELCOME ON THIS WEEK'S CARD RESULTS....HOWEVER, PLEASE REMEMBER THE FIRST RULE ON THE RULE PAGE ON THE WEBSITE. =========================================================================== ******************************* COMINGS, GOINGS, AND INJURED ******************************* COMINGS: Derrek Sweger Chris Spencer GOINGS: Boltz(permanent) Richey Razorblade(unknown) The Aftermath(unknown) INJURED LIST: Carny McDevlin(concussion-indefinite) Westside Thunder(various-indefinite) =========================================================================== =========================================================================== GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING WEEKLY LINE-UPS =========================================================================== ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^ GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING HOUSE SHOW LINE-UP FOR LANSING, MICHIGAN 11/4/99: RECAPPED ON THE GLOBAL INVASION TELECAST ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^ 1.
