You can see the veins in his arms swelling as the blood pumps to his muscles, supplying them with the energy they need to finish this set. Zeppelin t-shirt with the sleeves cut off and a pair of blue sweat pants. His short blond hair, normally neatly combed, is mussed up and dripping wet with perspiration. Adam finishes his last few dumbbell repetitions, drops the weights to the ground, and turns towards the camera.>>
<<With his eyes glowing intensely, he begins to speak.>> [ADAM ROGERS]: "Take a good look into these eyes and tell me.…What do you see"? <<Adam pauses for a moment, allowing the camera to focus in on his intense gaze. He continues to speak.>>
[ADAM ROGERS]: "Fear? Doubt? Cowardice? Is that what you see in my eyes? <<Adam slowly shakes his head in disagreement>> No…I don't think you do. To think otherwise would just be a lie. Now, as I look forward to Meltdown, my career is at a crossroads.”
<<Adam slowly stands, extending his muscular arms and shaking them to relieve the burn. He stops, then moves the weights aside with his feet. [ADAM ROGERS]: “Meltdown, November 14, in Cleveland, Ohio…yes, this is indeed a crossroads for me. Just like Cleveland is trying to resurrect the Browns, I am trying to resurrect a career that was almost cut way too short. I’m sure most people…most so-called experts…aren’t even giving me a snowball’s chance in the devil’s backyard to win this match. I imagine that even the GPW Regional Champion himself, Alec Ace, isn’t giving me much thought. But you know what? All that means absolutely nothing.
Why? Because there is ONE man who believes in me, ONE man who gives Adam Rogers more than a CHANCE to win, but the BELIEF he can win. That man…is me. ANYONE thinks, except me. I KNOW I can win…I KNOW I can compete and succeed in Global Pro Wrestling…and that’s the only opinion that means
ANYTHING.”
<<Adam looks briefly to his left, taking a deep breath in the process. He looks a bit tired, if not fatigued, and certainly emotionally drained. He turns back to the camera with a seemingly far away look in his blue eyes.>>
[ADAM ROGERS]: “Maybe ol’ Robert Sental is right…maybe I DO have my head in the clouds, as he said last Meltdown. Maybe, just maybe…I’ve got no business being here in GPW. But then again, maybe not. Like I said, there’s none there…if you DO think you see it, you’d better look again, and look longer, because you’re getting it confused with something else. If the eyes are a mirror of the soul, as someone once said, then when you look in MY eyes you should be seeing determination…grit…belief…and most of all…fire.
Alec Ace, I’m know you are indeed a great champion already, you’ve proven that. It’s a volatile title situation in GPW, and you’ve managed to survive after beating Flic Rogers…who, by the way, is no relation.
But on Meltdown, Alec, you’ll be facing a different Rogers than before. You see, I’ve got the fire…the drive…the determination to win and make my name known nation…no, WORLD wide. Becoming the GPW Regional
Champion…well, that’s a step in the right direction. I fell short at
Genesis, Alec…I WON’T fall short again.”
<<The intensity in Adam’s eyes continues to grow. He pauses, reaching down beside him, and pulls up a bottle of water. He takes a few fast gulps, and you see water pouring down the sides of his mouth. He sets the water back on the ground beside him, then leans forward toward the camera.>>
[ADAM ROGERS]: “And as for Minute Maid Lance Love…you amaze me, Lance. I don’t think I’ve ever seen someone so full of himself as you are. Guys like you, Lance, you’re a dime a dozen…I’ve faced your kind plenty of times before, so don’t think that you’ve got something I’m not familiar with. No, Lance, I know your kind…you think you’re God’s gift to wrestling, and you have to find every chance to shoot your mouth off at or about someone for whatever reason…maybe it’s like in Eugene
Robinson’s case, you shoot your mouth off at him, calling him lucky, and why? Because he did something you couldn’t. Or maybe it’s like in the case with me…you obviously cannot handle someone having even close to the same nickname as you, so you go and shoot your mouth off again. So
Lance, what do YOU see in my eyes? Take a real good look, pal, because if you’re honest with yourself, you’ll find something you don’t really want to mess with. The sweat that I perspire, the hours upon hours of training that I put in…I do it to prove to people like you why I am who
I say I am…I do it to help bring a piece of respect back to this sport and to the fans who pay our salaries. What do you see, Lance? You’d better ask yourself that one last time, before you get into the ring with me. You want me? You got me. When it’s scheduled, you can believe I’ll be there…ready to teach you a lesson you won’t soon forget.”
[ADAM ROGERS]: “Alec, I respect your abilities in the ring, but you are not too much different from Lance Love, so I not only target you to win the GPW Regional Title, but to bring respect back to this sport. I know that you’ll no doubt have some rather pointed and arrogant things to say about me, but when it all comes down it, Alec…talk is cheap. All that really matters, when we both step into that ring in Cleveland, Ohio…all that matters is what’s in your eyes. What do YOU see in my eyes, Alec?

============================================================================ ==== *************************** The Warrior of Truth: Rapier *************************** To: Dagger Lorath, Lew Spectre, and all vying for the world heavyweight title From: The Warrior of Truth <<Shay Peters stands alone in the ring with hundreds of fans packed into the arena, screaming their heads off. .>>
[PETERS]: "Ladies and gentlemen, I want to bring out my guest at this time. This upcoming Saturday, this man will face Lew Spectre and Dagger Lorath in a Triangle match. He is the Warrior of Truth!!!!! THIS IS
RAPIIIIEERRRR!!!!!" <<Shay Peters does a sweeping gesture with his arm and points towards the backstage entrance. The sounds of "Princes of the Universe" by Queen are heard over the loudspeaker>>
# Here we are....Born to be kings. # We're the princes of the universe. # Here we belong....Fighting to survive. # In a world with the darkest powers. <<At the crescendo, the entrance way explodes with a short burst of blue fire. The flames die down and the man known as Rapier walks down the ring.
Rapier wearing his black half mask, black pants and boots, and his back scabbard with the black strap running across his chest. Only the hilt of the sword is seen, it's silver glistens in the bright lights of the arena.
Rapier has a determined look on his face, but not the look of pure defiance that we have seen in recent weeks.>>
# I am immortal...I have inside me blood of kings. # I have no rival...No man can be my equal. # Take me to the future of your world. <<Rapier climbs up the steps to the ring and steps through the second and third ring rope. The music dies down. Shay Peters walks over to the man from the Age of Legends, as the ovation for Rapier is heard by the fans.>>
[PETERS]: "Rapier, you and your partner Rune have found yourselves involved in a very volatile situation in recent weeks. A situation where several men are all vying to be the number one contender for the GPW World Heavyweight
Title. This situation gets more and more convoluted every week. And now, you and Rune find yourselves in two triangle matches. The winners of each triangle match will face each other this Sunday to determine the true number one contender to face Eugene Robinson. What are your feelings on the matter."
<<Rapier looks down at his feet for just a second. He looks up at the fans for a second and then turns to Shay Peters.>>
[RAPIER]: "Shay, first off, let me say to you that you should let Rune and myself determine what we are, before you and the fans determine it for us.
We happened to win a tag match last week, but it was more due to outside matters than our skill as a tag team. But for our first outing, I can't say that I am displeased. But, more about that in later weeks. Today, we are talking about the triangle match. One of three matches to determine the number one contender for the GPW World Heavyweight Title. Somehow, somewhere, someway, Rune and myself, find ourselves in the immediate title hunt. Call me a pessimist, but I view this as simply being in the right place at the right time."
[PETERS]: "Care to elaborate about that?" [RAPIER]: "Well it's real simple, Shay. Every week, there's a match to determine the top contender for the richest prize in this federation. And every week there's 20 or more guys running down in the main event trying to get noticed... trying to grab the attention of the world champ, the promoters, and the championship committee. What Rune and I have done is get involved, not to get involved in the world title picture, but to act as a buffer. To even the score as it were, Shay Peters! Now, whether we were rewarded for doing the right thing, whether they want us in there to act as a buffer, or whether they think that more tickets will be sold if we're in there, doesn't matter. Rune and I have been given our match assignments, and we are now a factor in the world title picture!"
<<The fans cheer loudly for this last bit, and several fans get on their feet clapping.>> [PETERS]: "Now, you are going in and facing two men, Dagger Lorath and Lew
Spectre. It's an explosive situation. What do you think you're chances are in this match?"
<<Rapier gets the familiar smirk on his face, and looks out at the crowd.>>
[RAPIER]: "Shay, with the fans behind me like they have been. The support of them, and the spirit of the Age of Legend. I'm sure that come this weekend, neither Dagger nor Spectre will forget the Warrior of Truth any time in the near future."
<<The fans give Rapier another ovation, which Rapier eggs on using his hands. This gets a smile from Shay Peters. Shay is about to ask Rapier another question, but Rapier pulls the microphone close to his face again.>>
[RAPIER]: "Okay, forgive me for playing it up for a second there, Shay. Now, I'll bring it hurtling back down to earth. My chances, quite frankly, are not that good. You see, you look at these men. You look at what they have done over recent weeks. You see that they work on this shock value. They bring out every man they have ever been partnered with, and every enemy of their opponent that they can muster, and they bring them down to ringside and get them involved in this huge master plan, that would make Vince
Lombardi go to bed with a headache along with a glass of warm milk and a plate of cookies."
<<This gets a reaction from the fans. Rapier waits for it to quiet down a minute.>> [RAPIER]: "You wanna know what my big plan every week is Shay? I run 2 extra miles a day. I work out an extra hour.... I watch the film of my opponents matches...."
<<Rapier suddenly draws his sword out of his back scabbard, which causes
Shay Peters to jump back a few steps. The fans chuckle at this.>>
[RAPIER]: "I sharpen my blade real nice and fine.... And I pack my crap into a duffel bag and head to the arena. That's my plan!"
<<The fans cheers become electric at this, and Rapier puts the sword back in his scabbard.>>
[RAPIER]: "So, if I say that I am a little down about my chances this week, it's only because. I'm a simple man. I come to the ring every week. I step through these ropes, every week. And I face each opponent, one-on-one, because that is the honorable form of combat, and the only way I know how to get the job done, every week. I must wrestle my match... my way. And if the spirits and the fates are willing, I will win. But you can rest assured, even if the fates kick me square in the balls, that the Warrior of Truth will wrestle to his utmost ability, win or lose!"
<<Once again, this gets a huge reaction from the fans.>> [ PETERS]: "One more question for you, Rapier. Obviously, it would be quite an accomplishment for both you and Rune to win your respective matches, but it is possible. If that were to happen, you would face the man you tagged with last week to determine the number one contender for the world title. I know you don't want to look ahead, but have you given any thought to this?"
[RAPIER]: "I've said before, that wrestling makes strange allies. It can also make quick enemies. Rune is a tremendous talent, a tremendous wrestler, and a tremendous man. Personally, I think that in a match, that I can beat him, but if I didn't think that, I should get out of this business today.
Because I know that Rune thinks that he can defeat the Warrior of Truth, as he should. I would enjoy a match against Rune, not only to determine which of us is the better gladiator, but also to give the fans of GPW a match where both men simply enter the ring and try to win the match by himself, without interference from other wrestlers. A match with a solid finish, and a clear-cut winner, would be a wonderful change of pace. I have not talked it over with Rune. I don't plan on burning that bridge before I get there."
[PETERS]: "Any final words on your upcoming triangle match?" [RAPIER]: "Just this. I know that Spectre and Dagger weren't looking at me as a serious threat to their challenging for the world title. I know that everyone has held their breath waiting for a challenger to emerge. To call myself anything would be premature, but the one thing I am is a catalyst. I make things happen gentlemen. And when I step through those ropes this weekend, prepare for the worst. My blade will be sharp, my attitude will be fierce, and I will give you a battle that you will not soon forget. Hear me!
My fellow combatants. That is the truth... My truth.... And Truth is Eternal." <<"Princes of the Universe" is heard once again. Rapier jumps over the top rope to the floor and begins clasping the hands of several of the fans at ringside.>>
[PETERS]: "Rapier seems confident going into this big weekend for GPW. We shall see if that confidence can help him to overtake Dagger Lorath and Lew
Spectre. It should be very interesting."
