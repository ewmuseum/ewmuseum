(UNLESS OTHERWISE NOTED) ============================================================================ ==== ********************** THE NEWS AND RUMOR ********************** -The big news from Global Pro-Wrestling this week is who was the person that dropped the steel cage during the match between Lew Spectre and Eugene Robinson. No one was seen in the production area, the area that controlled the cage, and no one has a clue on who it could be. The note left at the scene is being looked at but it is not much. As a result, Commisioner Blain has ordered a return match to be fought between Lew Spectre and Eugene Robinson this week on Meltdown from Tampa, Florida. The inside word, from many people in the back, has it that none other than Mr. Southstar himself ordered the cage dropped. However, this IS NOT confirmed. -On the injury front, Ike Harris is going to definetly be out for at least five months, Marc LeRoux will be seeing a orthopedic specialist this week and will get a prognosis on his injury, The Gangsta Posse will be out indefinetly after the attack they recieved on Global Invasion, and referee Fuhrman only suffered minor burns as a result of the fireball from Lew Spectre. -In breaking news, Phillip Starks has announced that this Saturday will be his last date with Global Pro-Westling. He has been scheduled to fight arch rival Lance Love in one of the main events on Global Invasion. Along with Starks, Rogers and Cooley will also be leaving. As a result, Chris Page has stated that he alone will carry on The Diamond Dynasty tradition. Best wishes to Starks, Rogers, and Cooley in the future -The suspension on Ric Studd is about to be lifted....no word on what he's been doing or what he's planning on for his return. -It seems the fans took a liking to T'Chamunga winning the GPW Brass Knux Title and GPW Offices have been flooded with requests to put him on Meltdown. However, GPW is standing firm in their decision that he is too violent for live television. -After what happened in the television title match, one can only wonder what The Phoenix will do to Ryan Stone in an act of revenge. Both men are scheduled to fight on Global Invasion and it will be a very interesting situation to see if their paths cross. -You wanted it, you got it: Shadowman will get his shot at Mad Valentine this week but, there will be some other parties involved. Shadowman will team up with Dagger Lorath to meet Mad Valentine and Carl Blair on Meltdown. Word has it that the newly formed team of Blair, Valentine, and Hill, who shocked us all in Austin, will call themselves "Team Ego", a very fitting title. -Priest Noble has requested time off for a couple of weeks and it has been granted. No word on why the rising superstar is taking a vacation at this time. -Speaking of tag-team wrestling, the tag-team division in GPW is starting to get red hot. This week on Meltdown, Thunderstorm will meet Raiper and Rune to determine the next opponents for Law and Disorder's World Tag-Team Titles. On the American Tag-Title scene, The Rough Riders have the difficult task of Sean Willey and Jesse Muldoon in front of them while teams like 100% All Natural, Simmons and Sweger, Wilson and Foxx, and Star Spangled Justice all are ready to strike at the belts. -Word has it that Lance Love might have something up his sleeve for Johnny Adonis and it won't be help from Chris Spencer....more on this as it comes in. -The Zinmaster is starting to impress some people as he will get his first true task this week, a match against The Phoenix on Global Invasion. -As a result of what happened on Meltdown, James Wilson will get a chance to seek revenge on Vic V. Hill this week on Global Invasion. However, one can only guess what Blair and Valentine will come up with. -Also, GPW Management has requested a return match for the GPW Regional Title between Page, Rogers, and Ace. This fued will hopefully come to an end when they meet on Global Invasion this week. -Finally, GPW Management has made an announcement that MR. BLAIN HIMSELF will make a special announcement in two weeks when Meltdown comes to Memphis, the old home of Southern Championship Wrestling. -COMMENTS AND CRITICISM IS WELCOME ON THIS WEEK'S CARD RESULTS....HOWEVER, PLEASE REMEMBER THE FIRST RULE ON THE RULE PAGE ON THE WEBSITE. ============================================================================ ==== *******************************
