============================================================================ ==== GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING'S GLOBAL SKYLINE REPORT Vol 2 #8 3/5/2000 ============================================================================ ==== ******************** TABLE OF CONTENTS ******************** 1]Notes From the Front Office/Informative Material: PLEASE READ 2]The News and Rumor Wire: THE PPV CARD IS RELEASED!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 3]Comings/Goings/Injured 4]Weekly Line-Ups 5]Rankings 6]Most Popular/Most Hated 7]Question of the Week ============================================================================ ==== ****************************** NOTES FROM THE FRONT OFFICE ****************************** -Hey guys, I've decided to just put the new pieces of important information in this section: -GUYS THIS IS IMPORTANT: Here is the way things will go for The PPV. Further down in this message, the line-up for the PPV is announced. The undercard line-up will come a bit later. As you know, the cut-off date for roleplaying and strats will be Monday, 17th. I will send a Shockwave out on that day and it will be the last thing I send out until Saturday night's PPV
Undercard show. For those on the undercard, I will let you know what you will be doing before I release the card so you can start making plans. As for those on the PPV itself, start getting those strats in now. It will not be good if you have them in at the last minute. I would like you to refrain from doing in-rings on the PPV but you can do backstage comments. If I want an in-ring for the PPV, I will contact you. HOWEVER, please feel free to do them on the undercard so you can spice things up a bit. Remember, PPV's are judged differently than Meltdowns. I take into account your entire body of work leading up until this point and I keep in mind the future of storylines as well. So, please remember that. If you have any questions, please ask me. We have quite a few rookies that have just joined the league and please keep in mind that you will be on the undercard.
-AS A REMINDER ALL STRATS, CARD INTERVIEWS QUESTIONS, IDEAS, and ANYTHING ELSE GO TO: [detail withheld] -OUR LEAGUE WEBSITE(KUDOS TO THE DESIGNER) IS: http://www.geocities.com/globalprowrestling : EMAIL [detail withheld] to TELL HIM WHAT YOU WOULD LIKE TO SEE ON THE WEBSITE. -Deadlines for card submissions are 10:00 PM CST On Thursday Night. If you need extra time, you need to contact me ASAP and have a good reason. -Also, I would like to see you all start to treat Shockwave as the television program that it is. ANGLES can progress on this show and don't think that just because it isn't Meltdown, you shouldn't make an attempt to do so. Same goes for The Global Invasion. However, try to steer away from doing in ring interviews on Shockwave. Use Shockwave to get your character over in an out-of-ring setting and use Global Invasion and Meltdown for in-rings. MAKE THINGS INTERESTING AND STAND OUT. Shockwave is the character development program!
-PLEASE REFRAIN from referring to other leagues in your flashes. It takes away from the image I'm trying to build of GPW being the only federation worth joining on the internet :)
-I would LOVE comments, thoughts, and criticism on the pay-per-view concerning presentation and look. If you have problems with the bookings, well, you know my policy on that. Also, I'd like to hear what you thought were the highlights of the ppv as well.
============================================================================ ==== ************************** THE NEWS AND RUMOR MILL: ************************** -YOU WANTED IT....YOU GOT IT....HERE IS THE FULL LINE-UP FROM TOP TO BOTTOM FOR "DUEL ON THE DUNES 2000: THE SIN AND SKIN SPECTACULAR" LIVE FROM THE OCEANS CENTER IN DAYTONA BEACH, FLORIDA!!!!!!!!!!! 1. MAIN EVENT: "DUEL ON THE DUNES 2000": GPW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE MATCH: STEEL CAGE LADDER MATCH: "The American Rage" Chris Page vs. "The Smoking
Barrel" Madision J. Valentine(C): This is it....THE DUEL ON THE DUNES. Page and Valentine will hook it up one more time and the World Heavyweight Title will be the prize. Ever since Valentine beat Page in Richmond for the vacant World Title, the tensions between Team Ego and Hi-C have been boiling. Before Daytona, though, Page and Valentine will have a face to face confrontation next week in London, England as GPW have changed the venue for Meltdown at the last minute. Once they enter the cage, they will notice a different structure. The cage will be higher than usual and will have a small opening on top to compensate for the hook that the belt will rest on. Two ladders will be locked inside the cage and, like all other cage matches, the structure will surround the entire ring and will leave about 5-6 feet open on the floor.
2. "AMERICA'S PAST TIME GONE BAD": GPW WORLD TAG-TEAM TITLE MATCH: TRIANGLE ELIMINATION MATCH: Violence and Corruption 2000 vs. Domination vs. XTremeCharisma(C): After their actions on Meltdown this past Sunday, Violence and Corruption 2000 have persuaded GPW officials to place them in the World Tag-Team Title match with XTremeCharisma and Domination. However, there will be a couple of catches. For one, it will be an elimination tag-team triangle match. Number two....Domination's Louisville Sluggers will be at ringside and WILL BE IN USE for any team who gets their hands on them. Expect a very, pardon the pun, violent match in this one.
Interestingly enough, Bryan Meredith, a British native, will fight in front of his countrymen this week in London against Volcano."
3. "THE AMERICAN NATURAL": GPW AMERICAN HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE MATCH: "The Natural" Tommy Gunn vs. "The Natural" Adam Rogers(C): In a match that can steal the show in Daytona, Tommy Gunn will try to follow in Mad Valentine's footsteps and become the American Heavyweight Champion. However, Adam
Rogers will try to stop him from doing so in this battle of GPW's
"Naturals". Both men stand to be huge stars after this one if everything goes just right. However, this Sunday in London, they have to BE PARTNERS as they will battle Violence and Corruption 2000 in the main event on
Meltdown. Will Rogers catch another superkick by Tommy Gunn?" 4. "DAVID VS. GOLIATH 2000": GPW REGIONAL TITLE MATCH: NO DISQUALIFICATIONS: Cameron Kidd vs. "The Living Holocaust" Sean Willey(C): After the brutal assault on Cameron Kidd earlier this month, Sean Willey will get another chance to do his worst as GPW signed up a rematch between the two very different individuals. Kidd has not been heard from since the attack while
Willey has been a one man wrecking crew in recent weeks. With the No
Disqualification stipulation in effect, the violence can only be amplified." 5. "REVENGE IS HELL": SIX MAN GANG WAR: Kris Evans, Alec Ace, and Lance Walker vs. The Playpen-Rex Watson, Ric Studd, and Ric Donovan: They played us for fools....Ric Donovan, Rex Watson, and Ric Studd showed their true colors this past Sunday on Meltdown as Donovan turned on Kris Evans and reformed The Playpen. However, Evans got a little help from some unlikely sources. Alec Ace and Lance Walker helped him out after a beatdown from the
Playpen. As a result, GPW has ordered the three men to team up and face
Watson, Studd, and Donovan in a 6 man Gang War match."
6. "MEDIA CORRUPTION": GPW WORLD TELEVISION TITLE MATCH: TBA vs. Johnny Adonis(C): In the only match that has not been fully finalized, Johnny
Adonis will defend his World Television Title against one of two men...Luke
Andrews or Damon Knight. The two men will battle each other this week on
Meltdown in London to see who will get the spot against Adonis. After the actions by Knight on Meltdown in Ottawa, you can rest assured that Adonis and Andrews will be gunning after Knight in the biggest way.
7. "INTERNATIONAL AGRESSION": GPW BRASS KNUX TITLE MATCH: Oyabun vs. Rune(C): If you wanted a war, you will get one when Oyabun battles Rune for the GPW Brass Knux Title. It will be Japan taking on Great Britain and you can guarantee both men will be putting it all on the line in this one.
Expect a stiff and brutal encounter between both men. However, you have to wonder if T'Chamunga, The Phoenix, or even James Wilson will factor into this one. On Meltdown this Sunday from London, Rune will adress his countrymen and, well, I wouldn't put it past Oyabun to do something drastic.
8. "THE DAYTONA DEATCH MATCH": NON SANCTIONED ANYTHING GOES: The Zinmaster vs. The Phoenix: Things have gotten extremely personal between these two individuals and it will come to a head in Daytona Beach. The Phoenix will be fighting in front of his hometown while The Zinmaster will look to humiliate him more than he already has. As a result of what COULD happen in this match, Global Pro-Wrestling has decided not to sanction the match and will not be repsonsible for any damage done by either man. Both men are stating they will be pulling out things never before seen here in Global
Pro-wrestling. -In other news, Global Pro-Wrestling is warming up for their second international run this weekend. We will be in Birmingham, England for The
Global Invasion and, as originally annnounced, London, England for Meltdown.
At one point, it was being advertised that Meltdown would take place in
Manchester, England but, at this point, it will take place at Earls Court in
London, England. The card has been announced along with the PPV line-up and, in addtion, the new duo of Shadowman and Nicholas Richard will open the show up as they try to defeat Superior Darkness for the GPW American
Tag-Team Titles. Also, a mysterious source has reserved interview time on
Meltdown for some reason along with Ric Studd giving an interview as well.
-Rumored matches for the PPV undercard has Carl Blair battling Johnny Blades, T'Chamunga battling James Wilson, and Frank Ewiak battling Kyle
Cole. Also, a possible American Tag-Team Title match with The Coast to
Coast Connection is rumored as well. The full line-up will be announced later this week.
-Global Pro-Wrestling would like to welcome newcomers Bloodshead, Kevin Winters, and Copycat to our family. All three men will make their debut, one way or the other, this Saturday on The Global Invasion
-ANY COMMENTS, CRITICISM, OR QUESTIONS....SEND THEM MY WAY :) ============================================================================ ==== ******************************* COMINGS, GOINGS, AND INJURED ******************************* COMINGS: Copycat Bloodshead Kevin Winters GOINGS: None INJURED: None ============================================================================ ==== ============================================================================ ==== GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING WEEKLY LINE-UPS ============================================================================ ==== ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^ GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING PROUDLY PRESENTS "THE GLOBAL INVASION" 3/11/2000 From BIRMINGHAM, ENGLAND ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^ 1.
