We've grown out of our regional routes to develop a prominent worldwide promotion that is the thing to watch. In Global
Professional Wrestling, talent, consistency, and creativity are rewarded.
You are placed according to your level and will not be thrown to the wolves or go on a killing spree of people beneath you. One important factor in this league is that we try to get everyone involved in some capacity
================================================================== [2] [The Storyline You Are Coming Into] The Storylines are very important. We try to get everyone involved and not just have random matches. We like to build fueds, friendships, rivalries, make enemies of wrestlers, form unexpected tag-teams and partnerships.
We also like to interweve storylines and angles. Title matches and championships are extremely important but not necessary to make you a superstar. Hard work, dediction and committment to improving yourself and that GROWTH is key. We look for people who are not doing this just for themselves. Remember that. Because I always have it in the back of my mind when I chat with characters.
We want characters that draw heat and add fire to the fuel. We want people who make others want to roleplay and be involved. We like characters that get under our skin! not complaining wise of course...but you get the drift.
================================================================== [3.] [ Characters of the GPW ] A. Casey Blain: The commisioner, boss, and head honcho of Global Pro-Wrestling. A well respected former play-by-play announcer for Southern Championship Wrestling turned Executive after the court order that spawned the birth of Global Pro-Wrestling. Mr. Blain never shows favoritism to any of his wrestlers and only does things that are in the best interest of his league and its fans.
B. Aaron Hayes: An energetic and colorful play-by-play announcer who is also somewhat of a student of Casey Blain. Mr. Hayes was the play-by-play announcer for Wild West Wrestling before it folded, however, Mr. Blain liked
Mr. Hayes' performance in Wild West Wrestling and thus gave him the chance to anchor the announce team.
C. Robert Sental: An old favorite from Southern Championship Wrestling, still doing color commentary for Global Pro-Wrestling. Mr. Blain did not want to see his former partner without a job so he gracefully gave Mr.
Sental the chief color commentating posistion on "Meltdown." Mr. Sental, however, being the pompous, money hungry, arrogant, and egotistical one that he is, is not satisfied and is quite jealous of Casey.
D. Trent Lawrence: A former senior official for Southern Championship Wrestling turned Security Chief at the request of Mr. Blain. Mr. Lawrence is quite enthusiatic about keeping order and control during his show and he will not allow any kinks to ruin his system
E. Rick Thompson: The ring announcer for Global Pro-Wrestling. He does the introductions and kicks off all editions of Meltdown and Pay-Per-Views
F.Shay Peters: The official interviewer for Global Pro-Wrestling. A former
SCW employee who was given this job by Mr. Blain to maintain continuity and not to disrupt things in the eyes of the fans.
Referee listing: Mike Fuhrman: Senior Official. Calls is STRAIGHT down the line, showing no favoritism for anyone.
Jason Taylor: 2nd Senior Official and a referee who is willing to let things go a little bit beyond the rules to please the fans
Kenny Thomas: A young referee who started his career in Southern Championship Wrestling and is continuing his education in Global Pro-Wrestling Ricky Youngblood: A brash and cocky referee from Wild West Wrestling who likes to call things in "his own fashion" and tends to show a bit of favoritism.
================================================================== [4] [Rules, Regulations and Guidelines of GPW] [MAKE SURE YOU READ THEM!!!!!] The following are the rules and regulations of the GPW. Follow them carefully. Once you send back an application, it verifies that you have read all the information and consent to what we abide by. At any time does the
GPW reserve the right to revoke your wrestler's contract if rules are violated. When you commit to participating it means that you will do just that *participate*. Anything else that you do, unless discussed with the booker will result in your losing matches and departure from the leagie
1] THE FLEETWOOD MAC RULE
=========================== You guys know their song "The Chain" where they keep on singing over and over "Never break the chain"? Well, somebody lost their title to you, so it is your obligation when the time comes to lose it to the next person.
Vacated titles are bad....it is a hassle to fill them, plus it completely ruins the credibility of the title. It is your responsibility as a champion to lose it to the next champion, even if you leave the fed unexpectedly.
A good champion lost it to you, so when it is your time, you must lose it to the next person. Never break the chain. :)
2] The "What You Can and Cannont Say" Rule ============================================ 2. Ok, this one's important as well. Since alot can be said on television that could not be said in the past, this rule is kind of hard to follow. However, the words that have NEVER been allowed on television cannot be used. Also, I do not tolerate any references that degrade a religious figure, any sexual references(overt ones), any racial slurs(THAT's A BIG
ONE
YOU BETTER FOLLOW), or anything that will offend any group of people. If you have a question about a certain piece of text, CONTACT ME BEFORE YOU
RUN IT. Otherwise, it is subject to severe editing. I'm not going to list a string of profanity that won't be able to be used so check with us if you have a question
3] THE INTERFERENCE RULE ========================== We try to fit in all angles. But we can't fit them all. I want you to keep on sending in interferences/strats, etc. because they help you win. Just know that we may or may not be able to fit it in due to the amount of other angles already sent in. If you are already in the match or a stablemate of someone in the match...we'll do your strat first. Then we'll try fitting in the strat of someone you're feuding with. After that....if we can fit them in...we'll try doing everybody else's strats up until the point that we can't fit in any more. Just be aware that sometimes we can't do all the angles.
4] ATTACKING A GPW OFFICIAL - ====================================== If you decide to attack a referee or official of the GPW, your wrestler will be punished accordingly (That doesn't mean he will get thrown out, but rather suspend for a card...or stripped of a title...I would only do this however to set up an angle or fued). This is not WWF/WCW where we rampantly beat on our staff.
5] DEFENDING TITLES ==================== Champions MUST defend ONCE every FOUR weeks. There are no Excuses...you will be stripped of your title otherwise.
6]FLASHES/ROLEPLAYS ===================== Participation plays a big part in you winning your matches. Therefore, there is a Minimum of two roleplays/flashes a week. PLEASE do not do more than *FOUR.* Otherwise they will be held to the next weeks or next batches group. You can always try and get an interview on a live card or review show!
7] THE MAGIC EQUATION ===================== Alot of people sometimes wonder how we do things here. I have what's known as the magic equation that helps me decide the outcomes of matches. It is the following
Good Roleplays [65%] Strats (when necessary) [20%] Other Participation
(ie. Getting interviews and showcase matches in on time. Discussing future stuff and adding to the league with suggestions [15%]
8] THE REALISM RULE ====================== The realism rule is the rule stating that no reference, inside, blatant, or whatever else, can be made to real life promotions(WCW, WWF, and ECW).
That means, no mentioning of the organization, their talent, or anything that is exclusive to that organization. Somethings that are included:
A. The Hell in The Cell: This is a WWF trademark and cannot be mentioned in Global Pro-Wrestling. However, Wargames can be used as it is a match that has been done in other promotions other than WCW
B. Certain moves like The Outsider's Edge, Diamond Cutter, Michinoku Driver, and so on cannot be mentioned. They got to be called by their real name. Outsider's Edge=Crucifix Powerbomb, Diamond Cutter=Front Face Neckbreaker, Michinoku Driver=Thunder Drive. If you need a real name for a move that is named after a wrestler, let me know and I'll get it for you.
C. Anything else like catch-phrases, gimmicks, slogans, or references to certain shows or promotions if we're in a certain city(Philadelphia,
Atlanta, or New York to be exact) is not tolearted.
9] THE EVERYTHING GOES THROUGH ME RULE =========================================== Like it says, everything goes through me. If you want a certain angle, run-in, interview, or whatever else to be considered, I have to have the request in my email box otherwise it won't happen. It's just a way for me to have something I can use so I won't forget. Also, all angles are finalized by me so do not waste your time planning an angle out with another handler piece by piece. I'll try to give you some independance on where your characters are to go but, like always, I'll have the final say on where they're going to be placed.
10] HOW TO FAIL & WHAT WILL HAPPEN ================================== Above you saw how to succeed. Now I'm gonna give you the crash course in failure. If you don't roleplay (without notifying me that somethings up)
you will lose your matches. After losing for a few weeks in a row, you will then be suspended for one week. If you do not reply after that week you will be given your walking papers and that's the end of your stint in the SCW
11] MASS MAILINGS ARE PROHIBITED!!!! ================================== Let me forewarn you now, that anyone, and I MEAN ANYONE, who uplifts the league e-mail list and uses it to advertise or to rant and rave about things, you will automatically JOB and then be THROWN OUT. There is no acception. Advertising is a no no without permission. If you complain via mass mailer, you will be thrown out of the league. If you have a gripe, problem, issue, question, or whatever...come to me ALWAYS and we will discuss it.
12]WHO TO GO TO? ================== I feel that I am an extremely approachable person. I see myself, just like you guys, the roleplayer that wants to have fun and do well, except now,
I
am simply running a league. If you need to talk about the leauge and your character I am always there to listen and open to suggestion. Now this doesn't mean I will answer a letter immediately that second or be able to grant all your wishes, but believe me, I will try to respond to them and do what I can, to make your stint here as enjoyable as possible.
13]THE BOTTOM LINE..... ====================== The bottom line is we're all here because we wanted to live out the dream of being part of professional wrestling. we do it by having our own character and making them the kind of wrestler we think they should be. This is for fun and it will always be the main motive. EVERYONE wins, and everyone loses. No matter how good you are. That is a fact. NOTHING done through
GPW is a personal attack on your character. Don't get angry or distressed or walk out on us, or start venting to the league if things don't go
100%
your way. We try to make the fun as realistic as possible and in real life not everything can go perfect. So, with that in mind, try to relax, remember it's just a game, and enjoy. Make some friends...interact and help make this be the best it can be.
================================================================== [5] [GLOBAL PROFESSIONAL WRESTLING - Titles] A. GPW World Heavyweight Title: The centerpiece of the federation and the most prestigious title around. Only the best and only the dedicated can strive to hold this title. It is defended at a minimum of once every 30 days however, due to extensive T.V. and National coverage, that usually never happens as the champion defends the belt quite regularly. The belt is defended against an opponent who is proven himself to be worthy of a
World
Title shot. It is also defended with a minimum of a 30 minute time limit. B. GPW American Heavyweight Title: The notch right below the World Heavyweight Title. Once you climb this ladder, you are automatically the number one contender to the World Title. However, that does not release you from your duty of defending the title at least once every thirty days and most likely more than that. It is defended against those individuals who are on the verge of making that next step in their careers and the
American
Heavyweight Title is one of their ultimate tests. It is defended with a minimum of a 30 minute time limit.
C. GPW World Television Title: This title means you are one, if not the, best wrestler on Television. Since Global Professional Wrestling is such a large television entity, this belt is very hard to hold on to. It is defended each week on one of our two television programs or at a pay-per-view. Special exceptions can be made for the pay-per-view clause.
It is defended always with a 15 minute time limit unless otherwise noted D. GPW Regional Heavyweight Title: This title is somewhat of a proving ground for the young talent in Global Pro-Wrestling. A memorial to the regional roots that Global has, the Regional Title is an establisher for those who are ready to make an impact in this organization. It is defended quite frequently and it is always fought with a 15 minute time limit, regardless.
E. GPW Brass Knux Heavyweight Title: This title goes to those who are the toughest hombres in the organization and have the drive to prove it. It, however, is not an Extreme or Hardcore Title. The belt is defended with no-disqualification rules with no weapons brought to the ring by the wrestlers being allowed. Holding this belt means you are one tough cookie and it is defended at the call of the GPW Executive Committee.
F. GPW World Tag-Team Titles: These title belts go to the team who are on top of their game and on top of everyone else in the tag-team ranks in
Global Pro-Wrestling. Holding this title means you are the top tag-team to contend with and it also means a load of challengers coming your way.
The World Tag-Team Titles are defended at a minimum of once every 30 days but, like the World Title, that number usually increases within the 30 day span.
The belt is defended at a minimum of a 30 minute time limit G. GPW American Tag-Team Titles: These title belts are just like their single namesake and they go to the team who's just about ready to climb the ladder to the World Tag-Team Titles. They too are the number one contenders to the World Tag-Team Title and they are defended under the same rules of the World Tag-Team Title belts
================================================================== [6] [ Roleplays/Interviews ] Basically, we're looking at a minimum of (2) roleplays/interviews a week. Please do not do more than four. This is not including any interview that you are scheduled to do on a weekend show. I'll let you know right now that if you miss doing an interview without letting us know, it's not going to look good.
When doing roleplays/interviews, it's not quantity but rather quality. I'm specifically looking for things that draw "heat" and other attention. Make the world spin people. Under *no* circumstance may any character of GPW or wrestler be used without the consent of that person.
If anyone wants to use a character of GPW, you must get *my* permission prior to use. Do not use any one out of character. If done so you will not be able to use them again.
================================================================== [7] [ Weekly Shows/Cards ] 1. GPW's Meltdown: The flagship show of Global Pro-Wrestling, presented to you each week live from one of America's premiere sporting arenas. It is a traveling show that spans all across the country and, in the future, even international lands. Meltdown is a top notch production that features the premiere angles and matches for Global that week. Usually 3-5 matches plus in-ring interviews and vignettes are run on the show. On Meltdown, the announcers sit in a broadcast booth that is at the start of the entrance ramp. There is a video screen that shows the match as it is happening on live television and wrestlers can use that said screen for a snazzy entrance video or graphic. Meltdown occurs each Sunday night and, on occasions, it will be a theme show in which it will feature a special name and will be the prominent Meltdown for that month. Aaron Hayes and Robert Sental head up the announce team for Meltdown
2. GPW's Global Invasion: The lead-in to Meltdown each week, Global Invasion features the talent that is about to break out in GPW. Matches for
Global Invasion are taped on a Friday Night and are broadcasted in a tape delay the next day. On Global Invasion, several matches and in-ring interviews occur that build up Meltdown for that week. The matches are reviewed with video clips of each brawl shown to the viewers at home. At times, Global
Invasion
will be used as a special lead in for a pay-per-view that month and will come from the same city and arena that the pay-per-view is originating in.
Shay Peters heads up the announcing for this program 3. GPW's Weekly Recap: A Studio show that recaps the week that was in Global Pro-Wrestling and the week that will be as well. Aaron Hayes and Shay
Peters do the honors for this program as they recap video footage of the fueds and angles currently going down in the organization. Interviews and vignettes are also a part of this program as is a run down of the card for the week. Recap usually occurs on Tuesday evenings
4. Pay-Per-View: Pay-per-views are a special thing in Global Pro-Wrestling and they do not occur monthly so that the heat and atmosphere for the pay-per-view will be that much more special. A listing of pay-per-views will be made avaiable at a later date
