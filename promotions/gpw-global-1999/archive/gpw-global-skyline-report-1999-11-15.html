After weasling his way into the World Title match with Eugene Robinson in two weeks, Lew Spectre has made NUMEROUS enemies in the dressing room area, primarily Dagger Lorath. Right after Meltdown went off the air, Mr. Blain made a ruling stemming from the brawl in the dressing room area and Lew Spectre's recent actions. He has declared that the match between Lew Spectre and Eugene Robinson at Turkey Time Terror will be none other THAN A LUMBERJACK MATCH. Surrounding the ring will be Dagger Lorath, Ike Harris, Priest Noble, Rune, and Rapier. Also, Big Sir and Richard Ray Vaughn will be confined to the dressing room area, under the guard of security chief Trent Lawrence. Commisioner Blain has said this match will be as fair as possible with absolutely no interference ruining this one. -Congratulations to Jasmine Rogers of Los Angeles, California. She won the "I Want to be a Valet" Contest and will now escort Eugene Robinson to his match against Lew Spectre at Turkey Time Terror. -It has been confirmed....as a result of what has happened in the past two singles challenge matches, Law and Disorder will meet Thunderstorm at Turkey Time Terror in a No Disqualification and a No Count-out Match. However, a couple of spins have been put on it. First, Ramon Lopez will not be allowed in The Frank Erwin Center that night and, since the match will be in the heart of Texas, Global Pro-Wrestling has deemed it the perfect time to have a TEXAS TORNADO TAG-TEAM MATCH. Therefore, all four men will fight at the same time to determine who will become the World Tag-Team Champions. -As a result of recent actions, Global Pro-Wrestling has officially announced that James Wilson WILL be defending the GPW American Heavyweight Title against the number one contender, Mad Valentine. However, as a result of Carl Blair joining Mad Valentine, Global Pro-Wrestling has ordered that a specal guest referee be included in the match. None other than Vic V. Hill himself will put on the striped shirt and will be the official for the title match. In addition, if Carl Blair comes within striking distance of the ring, Valentine will be disqualified on the spot. In a related topic, Valentine has to watch out for Shadowman, especially after what went down in Columbus on Saturday. It would be too good an opportunity for Shadowman to pass up as he can really get under Valentine's skin in this one. -Speaking of Shadowman, he will be forming a very odd team in two weeks as he and Dagger Lorath will battle the upstart duo of Rune and Rapier in Dallas, Texas, the night before Turkey Time Terror. Rumor has it that Shadowman and Lorath are planning to blow away their doubters in this match. -Stemming from what happened in Columbus, Global Pro-Wrestling has signed a special grudge match for The Global Invasion in Dallas, Texas. Priest Noble will meet Ike Harris in what is sure to be a war to end all wars. -When Global Invasion comes to Dallas on the 27th, it will be billed as the "WWW Reunion Show." Mr. Southstar will be on hand and a special match has been signed for the evening: Phillip Starks will meet Bart Simmons in a match that never got to happen in Wild West Wrestling. -Also on this coming edition of Global Invasion, T'Chamunga will finally get his Brass Knux title shot as he meets Derek Sweger......two high flyers in Carl Blair and Marc LeRoux will hook up....Matt Foxx will continue to go higher as he meets Chris Spencer....the newly dedicated Ryan Stone will meet rising Brass Knux Title hopeful Zach Sharp....In a match stemming from what happened in Columbus, the suprising duo of Jesse Muldoon and Sean "Justice" Willey will meet Rogers and Cooley of The Diamond Dynasty.....and, in one of our main events, Alec Ace and Adam Rogers will continue their epic war from Meltdown but they get company as Lance Love and Chris Page will make the match a four corners afair. -Word from the back has that Sean "Justice" Willey and Jesse Muldoon will make some very interesting comments during their scheduled interview on Turkey Time Terror. Also scheduled for an interview is Merc......someone who hasn't been seen since he abandonded his partner, Ike Harris, last week. -Word has it that Lance Love and Chris Spencer, 100% All Natural, are THIS close to adding a new member to make things a trifecta. However, The Diamond Dynasty do not seem to be worried as they still will have strength in numbers. More on this as it develops. -After his impressive display on Meltdown, Adam Rogers is now an extremely hot commodity in Global Pro-Wrestling as SEVERAL superstars are looking to make a parternship with the talented superstar. -Ryan Stone has been heard in the dressing room area saying "we haven't seen anything from him yet." -Global Pro-Wrestling officially announced that the next special edition of Meltdown will be on December 19th from Birmingham, Alabama. It will be our special holiday edition of Meltdown and will be called "The 11th Hour." You can rest assured that it will be a major factor in setting up our first pay-per-view event, "Future Fight: The Battle of Los Angeles" from The City of Angels itself, Los Angeles, California. -REMEMBER: NO MAILINGS WILL COME OUT PAST WEDNESDAY OF THIS WEEK. ONE FLASH PACKET WILL COME OUT AND IT WILL BE ON WEDNESDAY. FROM THURSDAY TO LATE SUNDAY, I WILL BE OUT OF TOWN AND WILL RESUME MAILINGS, HOPEFULLY, WITH THE FLASH PACKET SCHEDULED FOR THAT TUESDAY. SO, THREE FLASH PACKETS WILL BE FACTORED FOR GLOBAL INVASION AND MELTDOWN -COMMENTS AND CRITICISM IS WELCOME ON THIS WEEK'S CARD RESULTS....HOWEVER, PLEASE REMEMBER THE FIRST RULE ON THE RULE PAGE ON THE WEBSITE. ============================================================================ ==== ******************************* COMINGS, GOINGS, AND INJURED ******************************* COMINGS: None GOINGS: None INJURED: Westside Thunder(Various-Day to Day) Eugene Robinson(Sore Shoulder-Will Not Miss Any Action) Johnny Adonis(Sore Knee-Will Not Miss Any Action) Brian Sullivan(Minor Concussion-Day to Day) Marc LeRoux(Minor Concussion-Day to Day) ============================================================================ ==== ============================================================================ ==== GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING WEEKLY LINE-UPS ============================================================================ ==== ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^ GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING HOUSE SHOW LINE-UP FOR Ft.
