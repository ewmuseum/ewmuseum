============================================================================ ==== GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING'S GLOBAL SKYLINE REPORT Vol 2 #10 4/30/2000 ============================================================================ ==== ******************** TABLE OF CONTENTS ******************** 1]Notes From the Front Office/Informative Material: PLEASE READ 2]The News and Rumor Wire: What has been going on 3]Comings/Goings/Injured: A LOT! 4]Weekly Line-Ups 5]Rankings 6]Most Popular/Most Hated 7]Question of the Week ============================================================================ ==== ****************************** NOTES FROM THE FRONT OFFICE ****************************** -Hey guys, I've decided to just put the new pieces of important information in this section: -GUYS THIS IS IMPORTANT: We are officially back on real time now. In your roleplays, you need to acknowledge that we have skipped most of April and it is now 4/30/2000. The first Shockwave of the week will be out on 5/2/2000 and will be dated 5/2/2000/ -With a lot of new members in the league, you need to know the format for a roleplay. This is all I require: ******************************************************* YOUR FULL NAME IN ALL CAPS UNDER THE HEADER OF STARS ******************************************************* TO: Who it is to FROM: Who it is from It isn't a hard thing to remember and if you have any questions, please contact me. -Deadlines for card submissions are 10:00 PM CST On Thursday Night. If you need extra time, you need to contact me ASAP and have a good reason. Also, please make notices of the format for interviews on Meltdown and Global Invasion. If you are new to the league, I know you got Meltdown so please study how the interviews were formatted. If you have any questions, please contact me. -Also, I would like to see you all start to treat Shockwave as the television program that it is. ANGLES can progress on this show and don't think that just because it isn't Meltdown, you shouldn't make an attempt to do so. Same goes for The Global Invasion. However, try to steer away from doing in ring interviews on Shockwave. Use Shockwave to get your character over in an out-of-ring setting and use Global Invasion and Meltdown for in-rings. MAKE THINGS INTERESTING AND STAND OUT. Shockwave is the character development program! -PLEASE REFRAIN from referring to other leagues in your flashes. It takes away from the image I'm trying to build of GPW being the only federation worth joining on the internet :) (However, if it is part of an angle or you have permission from me, you can do so. I just don't want any federations with words like MAIL or CYBER or INTERNET mentioned AT ALL) -I welcome comments, criticisms, and suggestions. Also, please fill out that questionaire for the federation review ============================================================================ ==== ************************** THE NEWS AND RUMOR MILL: ************************** -Well, what an off period it has been! In the next section, you will see a list of new talent that Global Pro-Wrestling has signed up and they all will make their debut this weekend in Arizona! A big welcome goes out to Barry Gradwell, Will Geddings, Jacob Azrael, Matthew Funk, and Sargon Shammas! -Now on to the news...after Meltdown went off the air back on April 9th, Commisioner Blain was quick to make a decision about what to do with Hi-C's actions. After no-showing their interview segment and costing Adam Rogers the GPW American Heavyweight Title to Ric Donovan, Commisioner Blain has made this ruling. On Sunday in Phoenix, Arizona, Hi-C will be forced to fight Adam Rogers and two partners of his choice and, if they no-show, they will be STRIPPED of their titles. HOWEVER, Rogers has the option of not revealing his partners until they are announced and you can rest assured that he won't. The obvious choice for Rogers would be Tommy Gunn and Mad Valentine but, I've heard some rumors about other possible additions as well. Domination is a strong possibility as they have been looking to find a third person to form an alliance with. In any case, I think Hi-C's actions just came back and bit them in the rear. Also, on the Global Invasion this week, Mad Valentine and Furious Steele will headline in the main event. We've also been told that COMMISIONER BLAIN WILL BE ANNOUNCING THE STARLIGHT GALACTIC MAIN EVENT IN PHOENIX AS WELL! -Ric Donovan is your new GPW American Heavyweight Champion and he's already letting the fact the he's the number one contender to Page's World Title get to his head. He is forgetting the fact that Furious Steele won the belt for him after putting Rogers down back in Tokyo. However, Donovan is not letting that get to him and, this Sunday, he will make his first title defense against Carl Blair. We all saw Blair interfere in Darren Skiles' match in Toyko....will Skiles cost Blair the American Title? Also, one must have to wonder what newcomer Will Geddings will do. He too is a wrestler with AWMC ties so he might be drawn into this conflic as well. Plus, you always have to factor in the likes of Studd, who faces Skiles on The Global Invasion, Walker, Evans, and Alec Ace. In any case, the American Title Match is going to be interesting. -The tag-team scene continues to heat up here in Global Pro-Wrestling as The Coast to Coast Connection are brining honor back to the American Tag-Team Titles while everyone is lining up to get a crack at Violence and Corruption 2000. On Meltdown this Sunday, Sex and Violence will fight Derek Sweger and Frank Ewiak in a big contenders match. In addition, The Coast to Coast Connection will fight Domination in a non-title match on The Global Invasion, XTremeCharisma will fight Alec Ace and Kris Evans, plus Ric Studd will be interviewing The Coast to Coast Connection on The Global Invasion while Domination has been given interview time on Meltdown. It should be another wild week in the tag-team ranks. -After the situations that went down on Meltdown, Global Pro-Wrestling officials are intrigued at a possible future match-up between Oyabun and The Phoenix. This Saturday, on The Global Invasion, both men will square off in a non-title match but I don't think this will be the last time we see them fight. Also, Rune might be factored back into the mix and a possible three way for the title could soon occur. Also, Rune will be battling Rex Watson on The Global Invasion this week. -David Smith would like to formally announce the start of his new company, "Flash and Cash" Promotions. He states that he can make anyone a star with the right marketing and the right ammount of money thrown at him. We all saw his actions on Meltdown when he "assisted" Damon Knight so, could Knight be Smith's first client? Who knows. We do know that Damon Knight will be defending his Television Title this week on Meltdown against the very impressive Bloodshed. Will Smith be involved? Or will The Coast to Coast Connection and Cameron Kidd have a word in this match? It should be interesting to say the least. Speaking of Cameron Kidd, he has his title match with Kyle Cole this Saturday on The Global Invasion! -Well, he wanted it and he's going to get it...The Zinmaster will get his crack at Tommy Gunn this Saturday on The Global Invasion. I personally don't like The Zinmaster's odds in this one. Speaking of Gunn, he will be on Meltdown this Sunday and might adress this situation with the press release regarding CCW. I do not know why we got it but it did state that Gunn has ties to CCW so he might be able to shed some light on it. -Watch the new talent very closely this week. Word has it that they are hungry to earn their spots and that we should be very impressed. From the looks of things, it appears we have a very good mix of talent. -It's coming up....Starlight Galactic on May 28th from The Superdome in New Orleans, Louisiana. Mark your calenders now! ============================================================================ ==== ******************************* COMINGS, GOINGS, AND INJURED ******************************* COMINGS: Barry Gradwell Will Geddings Jacob Azrael Matthew Funk Sargon Shammas GOINGS: T'Chamunga(AWOL) James Wilson(Time off) ============================================================================ ==== ============================================================================ ==== GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING WEEKLY LINE-UPS ============================================================================ ==== ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^ GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING PROUDLY PRESENTS "THE GLOBAL INVASION": 5/6/2000 FROM TEMPE, ARIZONA ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^ 1.Matthew Funk vs. Flic Rogers 2.Barry Gradwell vs. Nicholas Richard 3.Sargon Shammas vs. Shadowman 4.Will Geddings vs. Shane Jackson 5.Jacob Azrael vs. Kevin Winters 6.Rune vs. Rex Watson 7.GPW REGIONAL TITLE MATCH: Kyle Cole vs. Cameron Kidd(C) 8.XTremeCharisma vs. Alec Ace and Kris Evans 9.Ric Studd vs. Darren Skiles 10.Tommy Gunn vs. The Zinmaster 11.NON-TITLE MATCH: Domination vs. The Coast to Coast Connection 12.NON-TITLE MATCH: The Phoenix vs. Oyabun 13.MAIN EVENT: Furious Steele vs. Mad Valentine PLUS: RIC STUDD INTERVIEWS THE COAST TO COAST CONNECTION INTERVIEWS: FEEL FREE TO SEND VINGETTES AND IN-RINGS PROPERLY FORMATTED IN FOR THE SHOW(PLEASE NOTICE WHERE YOU ARE LISTED ON THE LINE-UPS: IF YOU ARE LISTED FIRST, YOU ARE COMING OUT FIRST) **************************************************************************** **** GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING PRODULY PRESENTS: MELTDOWN 5/7/2000 LIVE FROM THE AMERICA WEST ARENA **************************************************************************** **** 1.TAG-TEAM WAR: FRANK EWIAK AND DEREK SWEGER VS.
