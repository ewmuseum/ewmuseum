============================================================================ ==== ********************** THE NEWS AND RUMOR ********************** -We're going to go ahead and announce what is on tap for: FUTURE FIGHT 2000: THE BATTLE OF LOS ANGELES live January 16th from The Staples Center in Los Angeles, California. Under each match, an explanation as to why it has been signed has been given by Global Pro-Wresting.
1.MAIN EVENT: TRIPLE THREAT LADDER MATCH FOR THE GPW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT
TITLE: FURIOUS STEELE VS. PRIEST NOBLE VS. EUGENE ROBINSON(C): After Priest
Noble's revelation this past week on Meltdown and the subsequent breakdown of the Steele/Robinson encounter, Global Pro-Wrestling has ordere the main event to be fought under triple threat rules. However, as an added bonus, the match will be a ladder match and, to top it all off, each man will be able to bring a ladder with them to use. The first man to grab the belt will become the GPW World Heavyweight Champion.
2.TAG TEAM GRUDGE MATCH: ADAM ROGERS AND TBA VS. CHRIS PAGE AND TBA: After their subsequent involvement during the World Heavyweight Title match this past week on Meltdown, Global Pro-Wrestling has ordered the following match between Chris Page and Adam Rogers. However, each man has requested that they be granted a partner for the match, thus turning the match into a tag-match. Who the men will select as partners can and most likely will remain a mystery up until this match. As an added bonus, there will be no disqualifications or count-outs in this match.
3.TEXAS TORNADO TAG-TEAM MATCH FOR THE GPW WORLD TAG-TEAM TITLES: RUNE AND
RAPIER VS. DAGGER LORATH AND SHADOWMAN(C): Coming off of two weeks of title changes and a furious fued between these two teams, Global Pro-Wrestling has ordered that the final confrontation between these two teams shall occur in Los Angeles and it will be fought under Texas Tornado rules. Will
Rune and Rapier become the first 2 Time GPW World Tag-Team Champions or will Shadowman and Dagger Lorath hold on to their newly won tag belts?
4.DOG COLLAR MATCH FOR THE GPW AMERICAN HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE: JAMES WILSON VS
MAD VALENTINE(C): After the recent events involving Mad Valentine and
James Wilson, Global Pro-Wrestling has decided to end this once and for all and signed a Dog Collar Match between these two men for Valentine's
American Heavyweight Title. To top things off, Carl Blair will be barred from the area and, if he shows his face in the match, Valentine will forfeit his title belt to Wilson.
5.GPW WORLD TELEVISION TITLE MATCH: TBA VS. JOHNNY ADONIS(C): The opponent for Johnny Adonis has yet to be determined but the smart money right now is on Matt Foxx or Carl Blair with the outside track of Lance Love. Word has it that these three will square off on January 2nd from New York City with the winner getting Adonis as the pay-per-view.
6.GPW AMERICAN TAG-TEAM TITLE MATCH: LAW AND DISORDER VS. JUSTICE 2K(C): The team of Law and Disorder will try to complete the monopoly on tag-team wrestling in this league as they go after the American Tag-Team Titles held by Jesse Muldoon and Sean Willey....Justice 2K. These two fan favorite teams have had heated words on Shockwave recently and it will all come to a head in The City of Angels on January 16th.
7.TRIPLE THREAT CAGE MATCH FOR THE GPW BRASS KNUX TITLE: The Zinmaster vs. T'Chamunga vs. Zack Sharp(C): After the onslaught of support from the fans for airing a Brass Knux Title Match on Meltdown, Global Pro-Wrestling will try to capture lightning in a bottle twice as a rematch from The 11th Hour will occur at Future Fight 2000. The newly crowned GPW Brass Knux
Champion, Zack Sharp, will meet the man he pinned for the belt, T'Chamunga, along with The Zinmaster in that ominous steel cage.
8.LOS ANGELES DEATH MATCH: RYAN STONE VS. THE PHOENIX: This fued will end on January 16th one way or the other as The Phoenix, who will be cleared to wrestle by then, will finally get his hands on Ryan Stone, the man who cost him his Television Title, in an affair where Death Match rules apply. That means the winner of the match will be the man who beats his opponent so bad, he cannot answer a ten count after each pin. Naturally, this match will be no-disqualifications or no-countouts.
ALSO ON TAP FOR THE SHOW: LEW SPECTRE AND RIC STUDD'S SUSPENSION WILL BE
LIFTED ON JANUARY 16TH AND THEY HAVE BEEN GIVEN A CHANCE TO TELL THEIR SIDE
OF THE STORY AS TO WHY RIC STUDD WAS HELPING LEW SPECTRE INTO THE ARENA ON
DECEMBER 5TH. PLUS: POSSIBLY ONE MORE MATCH WILL BE ADDED IN THE COMING WEEKS ALONG WITH
THE ENTIRE UNDERCARD. THE UNDERCARD WILL MOST LIKELY BE ANNOUNCED DECEMBER
26TH OR THE WEEK FOLLOWING -In other news, Global Pro-Wrestling would like to welcome the following people to the league: Czar, Cameron Kidd, Drew Archer, Bryan Meredith, and
Andrew Bailey. We wish each and everyone of these new recruits the best of luck in Global Pro-Wrestling
-Rumblings in the back has that Furious Steele and Chris Page are starting to be seen together quite often.....could this be the start of some un-holy alliance between these two?
-The same goes for Adam Rogers and Eugene Robinson, although, there alliance was solidified this past week on Meltdown. Word has it that this group is possibly looking to expand
