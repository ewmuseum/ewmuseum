The Global Invasion

30 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
=========================================================================== GLOBAL PROFESSIONAL WRESTLING PROUDLY PRESENTS: "THE GLOBAL INVASION" 10/30/99 FROM SPRINGFIELD, ILLINOIS =========================================================================== <<The screen is all black.....all of a sudden, the opening notes of Metallica's "Turn the Page" begins to play and the blackness slowly turns into a shot of an empty arena. As the song slowly progresses, shots, slowly increasing in speed, of Global Pro-Wrestlers begin to flash over the screen while, on the other shot, the arena starts to get filled. Finally, as the song reaches the chorus...., the shots merge into an actual shot of an arena where the crowd is anticipating action. In the arena, we can see that the Global View Video Screen is present and that the ring has a majestic golden GPW banner on it. Above the ring, two GPW Banners are hanging while a small but impressive fireworks display ignites from the walkway to the ring. Finally, as the fireworks dim down, a voice over begins to speak.....it is Aaron Hayes."
[HAYES]: "Good evening ladies and gentlemen and welcome to beautiful Springfield, Illinois for tonight's first ever edition of The Global Invasion. The matches you are about to see were recorded earlier in the day and, boy, did the crowd leave impressed. So much happened here today, I can't even find a place to start. However, the good folks in the video production truck do so I'm just following their cue folks. Let's get this show underway and watch us as we embark on our first Global
Invasion!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!" <<The video of the matches taped earlier in the day as the majority of the video is of the ring and in moving video clip form with Aaron Hayes doing commentary>>
=============================== T'CHAMUNGA VS. TRACEY STORM =============================== [HAYES]: "Our first match of the evenig was set to be the menancing islander by the name of T'Chamunga going up against an old Southern favorite, Tracey Storm. However, if you saw the recent vignettes turned in by T'Chamunga, you'd know he is out in Utah somewhere and no-where near
Springfield, Illinois. Tracey Storm hit the ring but, when he saw that
T'Chamunga was not in the ring, he quickly high tailed it and got out of the ring.....getting one of the biggest pops of the night. So much for our opening contest as the whereabouts of T'Chamunga are, or should I say, were unknown at that point."
================================ SHADOWMAN VS. JOHN BRADSHAW ================================ [HAYES]: "Our first real wrestling match of the night featured the suprisingly popular wrestler by the name of the Shadowman. He got a great reaction as he entered the arena to take on the veteran John Bradshaw. The match was a complete blow-out for Shadowman as he tore Bradshaw limb from limb. It was a technical clinic as Shadowman basically schooled Bradshaw, much to the delight of the Springfield crowd. In the end, after drilling
John Bradshaw with a single-arm DDT, Shadowman rearranged Bradshaw's dental work with a devastating superkick he calls "The Shadowkick" to get the easy pin. As he was walking back to the dressing room, he had some choice words for Mad Valentine that cannot be aired on this show for obvious reasons."
WINNER: SHADOWMAN ============================================ STAR SPANGLED JUSTICE VS. THE RAGE MACHINE ============================================ [HAYES]: "Our first tag-match of the evening featured an up and coming duo in Star Spangled Justice. With an ever present wave of patriotism, they commanded a great reaction from the crowd as they fought valiantly against
The Rage Machine. After dazzling the crowd with an impressive display of double team and tag-team wrestling, Loredo Collins nailed Rage I with an impressive superplex while his partner, Jack Michaels hit a breath-taking frog splash off the top-rope that quickly got the win for this impressive tag-team duo. I'll say it now, to the winners of the American Tag-Team
Title match and to Law and Disorder, watch out because this team is going places."
WINNERS: STAR SPANGLED JUSTICE [HAYES]: "Right now, before we get to our next match, let us take a look at some pre-recorded comments from another rising tag team who is fighting for the American Tag-Team Titles tommorow in Chicago, The Rough Riders."

<< Payne looks at Stallion and then smiles>> [PAYNE]: "You see what these two teams fail to realize is this. I am partnered with one of the greatest wrestlers in this industry. Not only can he work a mic but he can leave you laying in a heap wishing your mama wasn't
watching you on television tonight because you just got your ass handed to ya! Now Fraiser boys, you may be brothers but hey so are we. John married my

about it!" <<The scene ends as the Riders head into their locker room and slam the door in the cameraman's face. The camera then fades back into the video of the matches in Springfield with the next two wrestlers in the ring>>
===================================== JARED MATHYS VS. CHRIS SUNRISE ===================================== [HAYES]: "The next match of the evening featured two great veterans in "The
Panther" Jared Mathys taking on Chris Sunrise, a former Wild West Wrestling
Western States Champion. As the match began, both men threw out regard for the rules as it quickly hit the outside and got a great reaction from the crowd as a result. Mathys and Sunrise tore into each other on the outside and failed to realize the standing ten count that was in place. Needless to say, both men were counted out but it did not prevent them from brawling all the way back to the dressing room area in a fight that had to be broken up by several security members."
WINNER: NONE: DOUBLE COUNT-OUT ==================================== THE MERCENARY VS. ADAM ROGERS ==================================== [HAYES]: "Right on the heels of the fight between Mathys and Sunrise, we had another one brewing as The Mercenary, one of the remaining members of
The Root of All Evil, faced the up and coming superstar and fan favorite,
Adam Rogers. As soon as Rogers hit the ring, Merc, as he likes to be called, used every illegal trick in the book to wear down and humiliate his opponent. When the referee wouldn't look, Merc would use a chain or an illegal choke or, well, you get the picture. However, as the crowd began to get into the match, Adam Rogers began to slowly fight back. Eventually, as the match went on, Rogers began to get the upper hand on Merc when, finally, Rogers hit his trademark tilt-a-whirl tiger driver and the crowd was on their feet. However, as Rogers went for the pin, Merc blantantly whipped out the chain and decked Rogers in the face with the foreign object. Obviously, Merc was disqualified as the referee saw the situation unfold but Merc did not care. He casually left the ring with a smug look on his face while Rogers slowly regained his composure, much to the help of the incredible reaction he got from the crowd."
WINNER: ADAM ROGERS [HAYES]: "Before we continue, let us take a look at some very disturbing comments made by one of the participants in the triangle match main-event tommorow on Meltdown."
<<The Global View opens to a TV studio. A GPW logo is in the backround, w/ a pale blue studio-set in front of it. To the left stands the 6'10, 500+ pound Big Sir, and to his right, Manager to the Stars, Richard Ray Vaughn

He holds a mic, and looks a bit disguested>> [SPECTRE]: "I am a man of GREAT patience. I have NO problem waiting for my turn. I am not known to jump the gun. This GPW, this "Outlaw" promotion, has the GULL to enter me in a World Title torny without my concent.
NEVERMIND I defended MY title in RIO the night before..I showed up for this
PPV, and I went to the SEMI-FINALS! Not too damn bad...I faced IDIOTS!
Total Embrassments! Poor excuses for Pro Wrestlers! I was DISGUESTED,

GUTTER for long!"
<<The back wall changes from a GPW logo to a sign for the big Triangle
Match this weekend>>
[SPECTRE]: "And for the FINAL insult, they book me into a match where -I- can LOSE without even being in the ring! A match for a shot at a title I don't even want! A match with two pieces of trask SO incompitant I wouldn't even WORK OUT with them! They bring me some Ike...Ike Turner or somethin..
This promotion bills you as a fine mat wrestler? A Master of Counterholds? WHAT? Umm... I'm Lew Spectre..I have NO IDEA who the HELL you are, but Mat
Wrestling is MY game, SON! I didn't spend 16 YEARS mastering the ART to have some would-be PUG try to upstage me! And our other oppent? Some punk who thinks he's a Mexican! A jerk who bounces from rope to rope, with no sequence, no plan, no pattern. You rely on SPEED, on LUCK, on the strgenth of a top rope! And then, you have the NERVE to adopt the last name of a TRUE showman..You are no Carny, my friend."
<<Lew looks upset as he backs up, the camera zeroing in on his face>>
[SPECTRE]: "You see, what you two have in common is you have NO idea what your in for. You both did EVERYTHING you could to win the World Title..and
FAILED! I merely showed up and made it to the finals. I put forth NO
EFFORT.. I used NONE of MY SKILL, barely TOUCHED my ABILITIES...You have NO
IDEA what your in for..and Being UNEDUCATED is just bad business! So this weekend, as
the three of us climb into the ring, Just remember, Many are called, But few are Chosen..and as everyone WORLDWIDE knows...-I- am the CHOSEN ONE...
It's Nothing Personal, Ike -n- Half-Pint, Just STRICTLY BUSINESS!!!" <<The camera zooms back into the ring where the next match is about to begin>> ================================== LANCE LOVE VS. WESTSIDE WALAVAN ================================== [HAYES]: "After the crowd heard those disturbing words from Lew Spectre, they were then in store for the start of something very interesting. Our next match was Westside Walavan going against a member of The Diamond
Dynasty, Lance Love. The match was a see-saw encounter throughout, favoring
Love moreso than Walavan, but, as it went on, things began to get interesting. Walavan took control of the match and began to stomp a mud-hole in Love. At this point, Love pretty much was expecting some help from The Diamond Dynasty but he got none. Word had it that, in the back,
Rogers, Starks, Page, and Cooley were all watching Love on the monitor get assaulted in the ring. Finally, as Walavan was going for a body slam, Love found an opening and rolled him up for a small package and the win.
Afterwards, Love stormed back to the dressing room area and he did not look very happy at all."
WINNER: LANCE LOVE =========================================== THE GANGSTA POSSE VS. THE DIAMOND DYNASTY =========================================== [HAYES]: "Suprisingly, the next match of the evening featured The Diamond
Dynasty's tag team, Cooley and Rogers. Before the match began, Lance Love was seen chewing The Dynasty out for not helping him out but The Dynasty was said to be uninterested. For the match itself, Cooley and Rogers were shocked out of the starting gate as McDaddy and Ruthless Villian opened up a can of intensity and poured it on The Dynasty. As the match went on, though, the more experienced Diamond Dynasty took charge of things by wearing The Posse Down and resorting to several illegal tactics. Finally, in the end, as The Posse were going for a stuff powerbomb on Rogers,
Phillip Starks rushed the ring and blasted McDaddy upside the head with a pair of brass knuckles while the referee was busy getting Susan out of the area. After a shot on The Villan, Starks left the ring and allowed Rogers to steal the pin and the win away from The Gangsta Posse. After the match, people in the back said Lance Love was about to go through the roof when he saw this action take place."
WINNER: THE DIAMOND DYNASTY [HAYES]: "Right now, we're going to let you watch an interview conducted at the show by Shay Peters and everyone's favorite Frenchman.....Marc LeRoux."
<<The video of the interview begins to roll on The Global View........>>
<<All the lights in the arena turn off, and the fans are silent as they are unsure how to act at this change in environment. Strobe lights begin to flash, as on the video screen, an image of a Stealth Jet taking off appears, as the early portion of ELO's Fire on High plays on the PA system.
The crowd erupts in cheers as they expect the fan-loving "Frenchman" Marc

His walk down the aisle includes slapping the hands of many fans whose arms are outstretched for him. When he gets to the ring apron, he sprints over to the turnbuckle and leaps from the apron to the top turnbuckle facing the fans. He raises his arms to their cheers and applause, then performs a backflip into the ring for their entertainment. When he lands, the music and strobe effect end and the arena lights come back on. The crowd goes wild as LeRoux stands in the middle of the ring with a huge grin on his face, basking in the glow that is fan approval.>>
[LEROUX]: "GOOD EVENING GLOBAL PRO FANS!!!!!!!" <<The fans turn raucous as LeRoux goads them on.>> [PETERS]: "Welcome, Mister LeRoux, to Global Pro Wrestling. It certainly is a pleasure to have you here with us this evening."
[LEROUX]: "Zee pleasure, my friend, is all mine. Hearing zee fans cheering for me..." <<He pauses as the crowd erupts once more.>> [LEROUX]: "Zis is what tells me, zat I have finally made it to zee big time." [PETERS]: "Well, many would argue that your performance in the rings of
Southern Championship Wrestling is what pushed you into true stardom here in the wrestling world. Nobody would debate that your high-risk, acrobatic nature and classic brawling style has made you the focus of the new wave of wrestlers."
[LEROUX]: "I would not want to say zat I am zee new wave of wrestling. I have a long way to go before I could ever lay claim to somezing as larzhe as zat. [PETERS]: "You certainly have proven that throughout your wrestling career, this past weekend being no exception. Without a doubt, your match with the

[LEROUX]: "Yes, I must say zat it was good to receive the applause and recognition of the fans boz after zee match and when I walked out here zhust a few minutes ago. Zen, no sooner do I win zee belt, and zee SCW folds, and I am forced to fight anozer champion for the right to even hold a belt. I must admit, zere is a slight feeling of bitterness in zat knowlezhe."
[PETERS]: "From the sound of your voice, it sounds like you are not done on your quest for the GPW Television Title."
[LEROUX]: "You could not be any more correct, my friend. Phoenix, you may be zee champion now, but you need to understand zat I am watching you. And when you least expect it, zis terror will divebomb you from above. I wish you luck. You will need it."

[HAYES]: "Fans, right now, you're about to witness some very brutal footage, much we can't show on the air. If you thought T'Chamunga was in
Utah, well, just look for yourself."
============================= T'CHAMUNGA VS. TRACEY STORM ============================= [HAYES]: "Like I said, if you thought T'Chamunga was in Utah, you better watch this footage. As we were getting ready for Phillip Starks against
Brent Star, all of a sudden, Tracey Storm stumbled out of the entrance area in a heap. Quickly following him was none other than the menancing and monstorous T'Chamunga who was accompianed by Khan Troll. We got word both men had made it to the arena during the Lance Love/Westside Walavan match and they were looking for Storm. Well, they found him as T'Chamunga absolutely tore him limb from limb. And, when the referee was busy trying to control the island beast, Khan was busy decking Storm with a hubcap from their bus that they had been driving. In the end, T'Chamunga locked in his
Panda Submission hold and quickly got a tap-out win. However, he would not break the hold and it prompted several security forces to enter the ring.
When they entered the ring, T'Chamunga was poised for an attack when Khan took him aside and escorted him out of the area. Definetly a devastating indvidiual in T'Chamunga."
WINNER: T'CHAMUNGA =============================== PHILLIP STARKS VS. BRENT STAR =============================== [HAYES]: "The actual match between Starks and Star never actually happened as the extra-curricular activities going into this match got in the way.
As soon as the match was set to begin, Lance Love, apparently mad at A LOT
OF THINGS, rushed the ring and immediately began to duke it out with Brent
Star... Starks just looked on in amazement at what Love was bringing himself to do. At this point, Cooley, Rogers, and Page rushed the ring and
Love began to think they were about to help him. However, the crowd was shocked at what happened next as The ENTIRE Diamond Dynasty assaulted Lance
Love for interferring in Starks' match. They nearly beat him to within an inch of his life before security escorted them out of the ring. As Love was left in the ring, The Dynasty had some of the most arrogant looks on their faces as they just proved how quickly they could snap and destroy someone. The medical officials helping Love did not do that much good as
Love got up and stormed the dressing room area in a complete rage."
WINNER: NO ONE-MATCH NEVER STARTED ================================ RYAN STONE VS. DAGGER LORATH ================================ [HAYES]: "The next match of the evening featured "Hollywood" Ryan Stone going up against the very impressive Dagger Lorath. Surpisingly, Lorath got right to business as he unloaded on Stone with an impressive techincal display that pretty much shocked the majority of the fans in the arena while getting a great reaction from his die-hard fans. Lorath went from move to move, slowly disecting Stone's offensive and defensive attack while, in the process, showing the entire league what a dangerous man he really is. Finally, after working hard on Stone's legs, Lorath locked him in a Texas Cloverleaf submission hold which prompted the referee to stop the match after Stone failed to answer the referee's call. Lorath quickly let go of the hold and stared dead into the camera at this point. He then poined to Stone and finally made a motion of a belt going around his waist before leaving the ring. I believe he just sent himself a message to the league, and one Eugene Robinson, he is not one to mess with."
WINNER: DAGGER LORATH ============================= PRIEST NOBLE VS. CHRIS PAGE ============================= [HAYES]: "Our final non-title match of the night was, without a doubt, the match of the night as the dark but extremely popular Priest Noble took on the ever dangerous Chris Page of the Diamond Dynasty. As the match began,
Page unloaded on Noble with what appeared to be the leftover rage from the assault on Lance Love. However, Noble would not go down. Finally, Page nailed him with a vicious powerbomb and went for the pin. Still, Noble would not go down. After missing an attempted elbow drop off the top rope,
Chris Page soon fell victim to the assault of Priest Noble. Noble began to practically take Page apart with his impressive offensive display.
Finally, out of no-where, Noble cracked Page's skull with a devastating
Thunder-Driver and took the pin away from one Chris Page. As Noble was leaving the ring, Ric Studd for some reason came out with Justin Hawk by the hair. Noble gazed at Studd for a moment and then slowly left to the dressing room area.
WINNER: PRIEST NOBLE [HAYES]: "At this point, the SUSPENDED Ric Studd crashed the show and went on about he could care less about Global Pro-Wrestling's suspension that they laid down at "Genesis." Finally, he said he had a little demonstration to make for the crowd and he proceeded to absolutely ruin
Justin Hawk's life by tearing him limb from limb. At the end, Studd had
Hawk in a modified figure four leg-lock that was practically ripping Hawk's leg off. Suddenly, the lights went down and the crowd went crazy.
However, when they came back on, no one was in the ring, other than a stunned Ric Studd an a near unconscious Justin Hawk. Finally, a full security team broke Studd off of Hawk and forcefully escorted him out of the ring. At press time, I was told that Ric Studd had been hit with yet another fine, this time of $50,000. This man DEFINITELY needs to be stopped."
====================================================== GPW REGIONAL TITLE MATCH: ALEC ACE VS. FLIC ROGERS(C) ====================================================== [HAYES]: "In our first title match of the evening, the newly crowned champion, Flic Rogers, met up with a rising young superstar in the much hated Alec Ace. Rogers didn't know what hit him at the beginning as Ace, with the help of his manager Benny, unloaded on the champion with a sheer intensity. However, as the match progressed, the tough Texan in Flic
Rogers came back and began to wear down Ace with his sense of bar room brawling. However, as Rogers was going to end it, Benny got on the apron and distracted the referee long enough for Ace to nail Rogers with an illegal groin shot and then a devastating DDT. Once Benny saw Rogers on the mat, he let the referee go as Ace was going for the pin. The referee's hand hit the mat three times and the crowd was stunned as Alec Ace became the NEW GPW REGIONAL CHAMPION. Both him and Benny celebrated in the ring but it did not last long as Rogers got back to his feet and chased them all the way to the back and the dressing room area. What a shocker, we have a new Regional Champion and he is "Superstar" Alec Ace."
WINNER AND NEW REGIONAL CHAMPION: ALEC ACE ================================================================ GPW BRASS KNUX TITLE MATCH: BRIAN SULLIVAN VS. BART SIMMONS(C) ================================================================ [HAYES]: "In our main event of the evening, "Hardcore" Brian Sullivan stepped up to the plate to try and knock off the current Brass Knux champion, "The Bulldog" Bart Simmons. Like any Brass Knux match, this match quickly brewed to the outside and ended up going across the entire arena. Both men absolutely ripped into each other and blood spilled from start to finish. At one point, Sullivan gave Simmons a near concussion with a brainbuster on the concrete but Simmons answered with a gutwrench powerbomb into a table. Finally, as both men were virtually out of it,
Simmons was going for his patented shooting star press onto the table when, all of a sudden, Sullivan moved out of the way and Simmons went flying through the wood. A quick roll up later and Brian Sullivan shocked the world, much like Alec Ace, as he became the NEW GPW BRASS KNUX CHAMPION.
High on the win, Sullivan got his composure back and began to mug Simmons with the title belt while the crowd was left shocked. However, they nearly busted through the roof as none other than DYNAMITE DERREK SWEGER JUMPED
OVER THE GUARD-RAIL AND TACKLED SULLIVAN OFF OF SIMMONS. Sweger, a former
Brass Knux champion, challenged Sullivan to a fight but the new champion took his belt and left while Sweger checked on Simmons."
WINNER AND NEW GPW BRASS KNUX CHAMPION: "HARDCORE" BRIAN SULLIVAN. <<The camera switches to a shot of Aaron Hayes, standing in front of the majestic Global Pro-Wrestling logo, while the crowd noise behind him slowly dies down...>>

Eugene Robinson's Title.....plus three mega title matches all on
