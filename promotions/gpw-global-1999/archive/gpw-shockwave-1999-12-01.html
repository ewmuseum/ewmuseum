============================================================================ ==== ****************************** "THE NATURAL" ADAM ROGERS ****************************** To: Chris Page and the Excedrin headache himself, Alec Ace From: "The Natural" Adam Rogers <<The camera opens in what is obviously an airport terminal. The first shot we see shows the view down a hallway, where a flood of people walk in each direction either going or coming to the city. The monitors which show each flight's status are filled with information, some of which changes as the monitors are in our view. The camera pans to the left, focusing in on one gate in particular. We see a sign reading "D5"
above a small desk, where two airport employees are taking and verifying tickets. A few people are sitting in the small and uncomfortable red chairs in the waiting area, but most are empty. A small TV hangs from a corner, with a football game playing on it. The camera focuses in on a man who is watching the game, sitting alone in one of those red seats.
From this view we can see his short blond hair, which is neatly cropped against his neck. The camera continues to pan around, revealing the man to be "The Natural" Adam Rogers. he is watching the game, but doesn't seem to really be paying all that much attention to it. He looks away and notices the camera, which reveals his rather somber look. He glances down at the ground, then shifts to his left to better face the camera.>>
[ROGERS]: "I have to be completely open with everyone watching right now. I fully expected this vignette to be one where I celebrated being the new GPW Regional Champion. In fact, I spent quite a bit of time figuring out exactly what I was going to say. And as you know, I jumped the gun a little bit there. <<Adam gives a slight grin>> Guess I learned a valuable lesson about putting the cart before the horse. You can probably tell that my confidence level has been on the rise over the last few weeks, and well it should be. I think I've really been making a name for myself here in GPW. I've been climbing the ranks and getting noticed more and more each week, and that was really my first goal to reach here. But now that I've done that, it's time for me to do just like the GPW slogan says, and take it to the next level."
<<A voice comes over the intercom system, but it is difficult to make out what is said due to background static. Adam glances down at a watch on his wrist, then looks over his shoulder to the runway, where a Boeing
747 is docked at his gate.>>
[ROGERS]: "So now the defining moment comes...just how do I take it to the next level? When a defining moment comes along, either you define the moment, or the moment defines you. There's only one way for me to define this moment...I have to get the monkey known as Alec Ace off my back and _finally_ win the GPW Regional Title. For the third week in a row, I'll get a shot to do just that. It's not often that an opportunity like this knocks on your door for three consecutive weeks, so I do consider myself quite fortunate to get one more shot at the gold."
<<Adam pauses, letting out a muffled sigh, while looking away from the camera. He then returns his gaze into the camera eye.>>
[ROGERS]: "The first two times, the chances slipped through my fingers. In my first match with Ace, I had my openings, but wasn't able to finish the job. And last Saturday, well...anyone could have emerged from the mayhem as the winner, but once again it happened to be Alec Ace. I won't call him _lucky_, but I will say he was fortunate to walk out of
Dallas with the Regional strap still around his waist. But you know what they say...the third time's the charm. So now, the four corners match from last week is narrowed down to three, with Lance Love out of the picture. And judging from Ace's comments on Meltdown, he doesn't seem too concerned about his competition."
<<Adam chuckles slightly to himself, giving a tight grin as he does. He shakes his head, and rubs the back of his head with his hand, then looks back into the camera, still sporting that grin.>>
[ROGERS]: "Now I heard Alec out on Meltdown showcasing his 'perfect' mike skills, which took all of about two minutes. I heard him singing all the praises of Lance Love, and I guess he should be, since Love saved him from a beating at my hands. Well Alec, you can come out and talk about how Lance took you to the limit all you want...but the fact is, you did nothing more than _escape_ with that title. You think I'm not a concern to you? You better think again. I would have figured that by now, you'd know better than to play me off like some low-level nobody. I figured that after being in the ring twice with me, you'd be man enough to show me some respect."
<<Adam leans forward toward the camera and stares right into it. His eyes are glowing with an intensity and focus that is uncommon for what you've seen from him so far.>>
[ROGERS]: "Well, Alec, this Saturday night...I'm going to get the respect I've earned. We'll be in my home state, right in my backyard.
The crowd is going to be behind me like you've never seen, and you'd better believe that I'm going to be on an adrenaline high of unmatched proportions. If you think Lance gave you a fight last Saturday, then
Alec, as Bachman-Turner Overdrive said...you ain't seen nothing yet.
After I get done with you in Jacksonville, all you'll have to do is go back home to Memphis and hang out with the Elvis freaks at Graceland.
You see, it's not just about titles anymore...it's about _respect_. On
Global Invasion, you _WILL_ learn the true meaning of perfection...you
_WILL_ learn the true meaning of respect...and I'll be the one doing the teaching."
<<A lady's voice again comes over the intercom, this time much more audibly and very pleasant sounding.>>
[VOICE]: "We are now boarding for flight 238 non-stop to Orlando, Florida." <<Adam pauses, then stands up and grabs a small bag that is sitting on a chair beside him. However, instead of starting toward the gate, he turns back to the camera.>>
[ROGERS]: "Believe me, Alec, I'll be more than a concern to you Saturday...I'll be a thorn in your side, a bulldog on your heels, and win or lose, Alec...you'll _KNOW_ what 'The Natural' Adam Rogers is all about."
<<Adam stares into the camera briefly, then turns his back to the screen and walks toward the gate. He hands a stewardess his ticket and disappears onto the ramp, as we fade to black.>>
============================================================================ ==== ********************************************* EUGENE ROBINSON (GPW WORLD CHAMPION) ********************************************* To: "Strictly Worthless" Lew Spectre and friends. From: "Tellin' it like it is" Eugene "Sparks" Robinson "Simply a Champion" ---------------------- [Voice]: "It's not whether you win or lose, it's how you play the game." [Fade Up.] [The voice echoes over the once faded black screen that slowly comes into focus. Our eyes capture a picture worth a thousands words. A glimpse of
Eugene "Sparks" Robinson resting with his arms behind his head as his palms brace his sandy blond hared head. He get a fantastic look at his razor ripped triceps as his tight royal blue shirt fits highly up on his arms.]
[Sitting on the gray desk before him is the fifteen pound strap of golden medallions known as the GPW World Title. The leather straps are neatly tucked underneath the large glistening frame that easily reads GPW as the letters are etched in like a stone carving.]
[ROBINSON]: "Some of Spectre just seem to be a little better at the game than others…" [The well deserved and obviously backed up rare cocky words exit Eugene’s mouth so naturally. They simply roll of his tongue as he lounges in his new
GPW office. Comes with the title I suppose. Eugene’s bottom half can not be seen as the frame of the desk hides him from the camera’s sight. Eugene continues to relax as a picture perfect scenario of the great outskirts of
Texas can be seen out the window behind him.]
[ROBINSON]: "You didn’t really expect to win in a state where I debuted to this very sport known as wrestling. Please Lewie, I made my mark on the world in a little organization known as Lone Star Wrestling. The fans love me here, they are behind me 100%, give or take a few… But it seems someone out there is looking out for you Lewie because this weekend you get another shot at the title, my title."
[Eugene removes his hands from behind his head and places them down on the desktop in front of him. He folds his hands together interlocking each finger with it’s respectful opposite.]
[ROBINSON]: "Don’t ask why your getting another shot, just be grateful. I mean please, you tried to pull every stunt in the book on me inside that steel cage including having an seemingly innocent valet by the name of
Jasmine Rogers jam her spiked heel into the small of my back. I’m surprised you didn’t by off Mr. Southstar himself to come down and attack me. But I guess not everyone can be bought Lewie, or can they?"
[These simple words seem to run into your mind really making you think. Does everyone really have a price? Or did these people simply help Little Lewie because he’s a nice guy? Doubtful… Nevertheless the Champ is speaking and we owe it to him to listen.]
[ROBINSON]: "It all started with the little light show that occurred as the lumberjacks were setting to come out. Please Lewie… A cage? Now I can make several assumptions and blame many of people for this act, but whoever it was, if they were trying to see me, the pure athlete himself, be lost inside a place where he isn’t accustom too then they didn’t do their homework. Is everyone forgetting how I won this title…"
[Eugene places his right hand over the gold medallion in the center of the belt. He gently pats treating it like a rare piece of art.]
[ROBINSON]: "It was on the night of GPW’s birth when I defeated Carny
MacDevlin to rightfully be able to wear this title around my waist, in a steel cage. The odds were stacked in his favor but like Carny learned, and like Little Lewie learned, the odds are never against the champion! But it didn’t end there did it Little Lewie? Not it didn’t…"
[Eugene calmly shakes his head as he lets out a large sigh of exhaustion from a very long weekend. Yeah he’s tired, he’s only human. Contrary to many peoples believes he does bleed, breathe air, and walk on two feet like everyone else, he’s just a normal man who pushes the limits of humanity.]
[ROBINSON]: "Your long list of cheap shots continued. From the opening bell when you attacked me with a blindsided forearm smash to the second the match was declared a "No Rules" match where you immediately snatched a pair of brass knuckles from your tights. Coincidence, I think not? Sure you can go on and on and say you were just *prepared* by bringing them to the ring.
What, were you gonna try and pull a fast one over the GPW officials by sneaking in a cheap shot? Tsk…tsk… Lewie, I thought you were better than that. I would have believed a man who worked his butt off, and yes I will give credit where it’s due, to get to this match would not be willing to throw it all on the line to cheat. There’s a difference between risking it all to be the champion, and being a moron by cheating."
[Eugene reaches over to a stack of papers on the left side of the desk. He brings them over towards him and places some of them in a large folder.]
[ROBINSON]: "You know what these are Little Lewie, these are my records. Read em’, learn em’, remember them. It’s my story but I’m gonna sum it up to you in a few simple sentences. I’ve been around the block, I’ve won, I’ve lost, but not once, never in my life, have I forced to breaking the rules in a match. It’s not necessary. If I’m the best at what I do then short cuts are not needed. If you think your better than me then prove it in the ring, with skill against skill. Now your getting an opportunity that few do, and that’s a rematch against a World Champion, but not just any champ, the GPW
World Champion, me…"
[As he tucks the papers away he places the folder on the end of his desk for open reading to anyone that chooses to stop by. He has nothing to hide from his past, at least nothing any normal man would think to read about. Yes their is a hidden side to the champion, but what? Where? When? But as
Eugene sits here before us, nobody could even take a wild guess to what it is…or was.]
[ROBINSON]: "Now we’ve both felt what impact and force that the other can bring to the ring. But far be it from me to come to the conclusion that you’ve shown me all you have to offer, and vice versa. It is impossible to learn everything about someone’s style from one given match. Several days ago you learned what it’s like to have a man continuously drill at your back with quick yet forceful moves. Who knows, maybe this weekend it’ll be your legs, your neck, or nothing in particular. There is no educated guess to how
I may come out, the only thing you hope for is your plan of attack is stronger than mine, and more efficient. But this week there will be no steel cage for you to hurl my body against, at least not that I know of. So take your fireballs, brass knuckles, spiked heels, your little eating fork, and whatever else you’ve got and leave them in a bag at home. Cause like your learned last time, there no help in your favor. Sure you might hit me with and knock me down, but keeping me down is a whole nother story."
[Eugene stands up from behind the desk revealing his royal colored blue shirt to be neatly tucked into a simple pair of black dress pants with a black belt as well. He steps aside from behind the desk as he slowly paces without saying a word. The camera locks in on his dead silence as the viewers at home turn up the volume on their television sets just waiting to see what he is about to say.]
[ROBINSON]: "Now I don’t know for sure who is behind all this mystery nonsense. But whoever you are, whatever your plan is, I can’t really say if it’s working. If your after the title, show your face, earn a shot at it.
If you just want to try and ruin things in the GPW your fatal attempts at dropping a cage only boosted the ratings. So whatever your plan is, come forward, show yourself. I’ve seen these mystery bits a million times and just ends up being someone who is trying to gain some cheap heat and catch our attention. Well you’ve caught my attention mystery boy, you’ve caught it indeed. But when you do step out from the shadows of fear, I’ll be waiting for you and I’ll give you a greeting that only I can give."
[Eugene leaves us with that comment as he pulls open his office door. He steps out into the hallways of GPW headquarters and slowly closes it behind him. Eugene slowly makes his way down the long decorated hallways and eventually rounds the corner leaving our sight as the camera slowly fades to black.]

A greater awareness leads to less mistakes and that my friends, is the absolute truth. Lets get inside and have a cup of Joe, it's freezing out here.
