After Priest Noble's pinfall win against Eugene Robinson, Dagger Lorath's "win" against Lew Spectre, and the ending of the tag-team main event on Global Invasion, Global Pro-Wrestling has ordered the following: This week on Global Invasion from Columbus, Ohio.....two triangle elimination matches will occur between Ike Harris, Lew Spectre, Priest Noble, Rune, Rapier, and Dagger Lorath. The winners of these two matches will meet each other the following night on Meltdown to ONCE AND FOR ALL determine who will face Eugene Robinson for the World Heavyweight Title at Turkey Time Terror. -Speaking of Eugene Robinson, after the upset loss by Priest Noble, the World Champion looks to rebound this week. However, he has a very hard task ahead of him in Johnny Adonis, his opponent in the main event of Meltdown this week from Cleveland, Ohio. Robinson now must deal with the loss from Noble, ward off a very deadly Johnny Adonis, and then look ahead to who he will fight at Turkey Time Terror. It is definetly not a good time to be on top. -Rumblings have it that, if things go like they did this past week during Anarchy's match with Crash, the World Tag-Team Title match might in fact be fought with no-disqualifications and no-countouts. However, this HAS NOT been made official yet. -Word from the back has that Mad Valentine has something very sneaky up his sleeve to convince Global Pro-Wrestling into giving him a title match against James Wilson, the American Heavyweight Champion. Word also has it that it might come as soon as Turkey Time Terror. He also has a burning situation to deal with involving the mysterious Shadowman as he completely ruined Valentine's car this past weekend. -If history repeats itself, the cage match between Johnny Adonis and The Phoenix at Turkey Time Terror will be a blood bath and Global Pro-Wrestling is preparing for it. They have ordered that two medical attendants be placed INSIDE the steel cage in addition to Marc LeRoux being the special guest referee. The Phoenix has gone on record stating he is about to turn his intensity and violence factor up a notch. -Global Pro-Wrestling would like to welcome our new accquisitions to the league. Over the past week, the following wrestlers finalized contracts to appear in Global Pro-Wrestling: Carl Blair, Jesse Muldoon, Matt Foxx, Sean "Justice" Willey, and Zach Sharp. Also, Ramon Lopez(of Law and Disorder) also signed a temporary wrestling contract as well. On the same subject, Muldoon and Willey will in fact team up this week as they battle the deadly duo of Lance Love and Chris Spencer. -Speaking of Love and Spencer, strong word from the back has it that their organization WILL definetly be expanding as they hit a roadblock this past Sunday in their war with The Diamond Dynasty. A new ally could be made any day now. -Word has it that t he team of Rapier and Rune have been seen talking to a current Global superstar in the back and, if you knew who he was, you'd be very suprised. However, this has not yet been researched as cold hard fact, YET. -It is official, Khan Troll has successfully obtained a Brass Knux Title shot for T'Chamunga this week against the new champion, Derek Sweger. However, he will have two others to worry about: the two past champions, Bart Simmons and Brian Sullivan as there will be a four way Brass Knux Title match this Saturday on The Global Invasion -When reached for a comment about why he turned on his partner, Ike Harris, Merc quickly hung up the phone and would not return our calls. Something major is happening with him in our opinion. -Word has it that revenge is in store for The Gangsta Posse for their recent actions here in Global Pro-Wrestling, however no one is claiming responsibility for these threats. The Posse are now set to face The Rough Riders for the American Tag-Team Titles this week on Global Invasion -Mystery still continues to shroud Global Pro-Wrestling as several unrecognizable individuals had an impact on the shows this weekend. One was a man who looked somewhat like Dagger Lorath helping Shadowman and, the other, was a man dressed in all black that "assisted" Dagger Lorath. There might be more than meets the eyes in this situation. -Rumblings have it that The Diamond Dynasty are growing very ill of current happenings in Global Pro-Wrestling and something is about to give in which a lot of people will soon be paying. -Word has it that Ryan Stone is going to say something very important in his return to live television this Sunday on Meltdown. -The match just signed between Adam Rogers and Alec Ace for Ace's Regional Title belt has attracted a lot of attention from the back as both men have been scouted by several individuals in the past few weeks. -We would like to announce the three finalists for the "I Want to be a Valet" contest: Amy Fields of Ft. Lauderdale, Florida......Jasmine Rodgers of Corpus Christi, Texas.....and Shay Clevenger of Los Angeles, California. One of these three lucky young ladies will be picked, at random, THIS SUNDAY on Meltdown to determine who gets to be a valet at Turkey Time Terror from Austin, Texas. -COMMENTS AND CRITICISM IS WELCOME ON THIS WEEK'S CARD RESULTS....HOWEVER, PLEASE REMEMBER THE FIRST RULE ON THE RULE PAGE ON THE WEBSITE. ============================================================================ ==== ******************************* COMINGS, GOINGS, AND INJURED ******************************* COMINGS: Carl Blair Matt Foxx Sean "Justice" Willey Zach Sharp Jesse Muldoon GOINGS: Ric Studd(30 Day Suspension) ============================================================================ ==== ============================================================================ ==== GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING WEEKLY LINE-UPS ============================================================================ ==== ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^ GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING HOUSE SHOW LINE-UP FOR Akron, Ohio 11/11/99: RECAPPED ON THE GLOBAL INVASION TELECAST ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^ 1.
