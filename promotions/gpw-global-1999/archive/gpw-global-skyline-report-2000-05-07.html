============================================================================ ==== GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING'S GLOBAL SKYLINE REPORT Vol 2 #11 5/7/2000 ============================================================================ ==== ******************** TABLE OF CONTENTS ******************** 1]Notes From the Front Office/Informative Material: PLEASE READ 2]The News and Rumor Wire: What has been going on 3]Updated Roster 4]Weekly Line-Ups 5]Rankings 6]Most Popular/Most Hated 7]Question of the Week ============================================================================ ==== ****************************** NOTES FROM THE FRONT OFFICE ****************************** -Hey guys, I've decided to just put the new pieces of important information in this section: -With a lot of new members in the league, you need to know the format for a roleplay. This is all I require: ******************************************************* YOUR FULL NAME IN ALL CAPS UNDER THE HEADER OF STARS ******************************************************* TO: Who it is to FROM: Who it is from It isn't a hard thing to remember and if you have any questions, please contact me. -Deadlines for card submissions are 10:00 PM CST On Thursday Night. If you need extra time, you need to contact me ASAP and have a good reason. Also, please make notices of the format for interviews on Meltdown and Global
Invasion. If you are new to the league, I know you got Meltdown so please study how the interviews were formatted. If you have any questions, please contact me.
-Also, I would like to see you all start to treat Shockwave as the television program that it is. ANGLES can progress on this show and don't think that just because it isn't Meltdown, you shouldn't make an attempt to do so. Same goes for The Global Invasion. However, try to steer away from doing in ring interviews on Shockwave. Use Shockwave to get your character over in an out-of-ring setting and use Global Invasion and Meltdown for in-rings. MAKE THINGS INTERESTING AND STAND OUT. Shockwave is the character development program!
-PLEASE REFRAIN from referring to other leagues in your flashes. It takes away from the image I'm trying to build of GPW being the only federation worth joining on the internet :) (However, if it is part of an angle or you have permission from me, you can do so. I just don't want any federations with words like MAIL or CYBER or INTERNET mentioned AT ALL)
-I welcome comments, criticisms, and suggestions. Also, please fill out that questionaire for the federation review ============================================================================ ==== ************************** THE NEWS AND RUMOR MILL: ************************** -Well, what a MELTDOWN the fans in Phoenix got! A quick run down of things saw Tyler Boyd, Brendan Blade, and Axl Blaze crashing the party and declaring themselves "Amerikaz Most Wanted", Sean Willey and Johnny Adonis put down The Catalysts of Excitement after a visit from "Charles" occured,
Damon Knight stole a win from Bloodshed but was soon met with an attack by
Kidd and then Domination who in turn sided with BLOODSHED!, Darren Skiles cost Carl Blair the American Heavyweight Title against Ric Donovan,
Commisioner Blain laid out several matches of Starlight Galactic, including a massive main event, and, in Meltdown's Main Event, XTremeCharisma turned out to be the partners of Adam Rogers and, in turn, with a little help from
Team Ego, took the win from High Intensity Carnage.
-Disecting things piece by piece, we must look at the massive main event announced for Meltdown in Charlotte this week. It will be a 12 man, 4 team
War-Games Match. Two rings will be placed in the arena while a steel cage will cover them both. High Intensity Carnage, Team Ego, Adam Rogers and
XTremeCharisma, and Domination and Bloodshed, will all meet in this tremendous main event. When one takes a look at it, we have two underlying situations that will develop in this match. One will be the build-up for the main event at Starlight Galactic, Chris Page, Adam Rogers, Tommy Gunn, and Mad Valentine, all battling it out for the GPW World Heavyweight Title in a 4 corners elimination match. On the other side, we also have 3 of the
4 tag-teams that will fight each other at the pay-per-view in the other 4 corners elimination match. Violence and Corruption 2000, Domination, and
XTremeCharisma will all hook it up in the cage plus in New Orleans when Sex and Violence will get thrown in as well. Plus, Bloodshed and Carl Blair, to help out their partners, will also be in the cage in Charlotte. It is going to be a very hostile situation as one will have to watch a growing tension between Tommy Gunn and Mad Valentine, two newly formed duos fighting together, Hi-C in a match that is designed for them, and so much more. It all takes place this Sunday in Charlotte.
-Speaking of Sex and Violence, Johnny Adonis and Sean Willey will not be idle this weekend. After the debut of "Charles" this Sunday, Sean Willey will go to Greensboro on Saturday to fight Lance Walker in the main event of
The Global Invasion. Then, the next day on Meltdown, Sean Willey has requested an interview for some reason while Johnny Adonis meets GPW Brass
Knux Champion, Oyabun, in a special non-title match.
-Speaking of Meltdown, two other matches have been announced for this stellar card. One, you already know, and this is Alec Ace and Kris Evans battling Ric Studd and Ric Donovan in a Charlotte City Street Fight. The other is a showcase of our new talent as "Flyking" Will Geddings will battle
Brendan Blade of Amerikaz Most Wanted. It should be noted that Blade has not signed a full contract with Global Pro-Wrestling and is appearing on a nightly basis. Speaking of Blade and Geddings, Brendan will also have to deal with Kyle Cole on The Global Invasion while Geddings will have to face
The Punisher. Blade's Amerikaz Most Wanted partner, Axl Blaze will meet Sha dowman at the House Show in Raleigh on May 11th and then GPW newcomer Cody
Carlson on May 13 in Greensboro. Tyler Boyd will have to deal with Flic
Rogers on The Global Invasion in Greensboro this Saturday.
-Did you all notice the referee situation during the match between Oyabun and The Phoenix on The Global Invasion? Well, I won't say much about it now but, if it continues, it might be something to take a look at. We'll see if it comes up this Sunday when Oyabun battles Johnny Adonis on Meltdown.
-I'm guessing you all saw the latest chapter in the story between Damon Knight and C3C. This weekend, Knight got the upper hand as several security officers took out either Kidd or The Coast to Coast Connection in opportune times to attack the World Television Champion. Then, we saw that situation with the fan on Meltdown and you have to wonder if it was all staged from the start. If it was, who did it and is his name David Smith? This
Saturday on The Global Invasion, Tony Richmond of The Coast to Coast Connection will battle Damon Knight while Kidd defends the GPW Regional
Title against Kevin Winters. Plus, on Meltdown this Sunday, C3C have scheduled an interview segment to adress this situation with Damon Knight.
-Word has it that the situation between Alec Ace, Kris Evans, Ric Studd, and Ric Donovan is about to explode. We all heard the announcement about the two pay-per-view main events plus the Charlotte City Street Fight but, I'm feeling that something else might happen between these four individuals.
-After a tough loss on Meltdown, the promising duo, The Catalysts of Excitement, will battle The Phoenix and Rune on The Global Invasion. Can
Sweger and Ewiak get back in the win column or will Rune and The Phoenix show how tough they really are and put the duo down while looking towards
Oyabun's Brass Knux Title.
-Well, the new crop of talent did not disappoint this weekend as all the debuting superstars made an immediate impact on things. However, word has it that Barry Gradwell made the quickest impact on GPW officials with his blunt and controversial comments on The Global Invasion. This weekend,
Gradwell must face Rex Watson at the House Show in Raleigh while facing GPW newcomer, Nigel Payton, in Greensboro. Speaking of Payton, Nigel will make his debut in Raleigh on May 11th when he faces Jacob Azrael in the main event. It should be a tough test for Nigel Payton but, from what we have seen, he should be up for the test. Other matches featuring GPW newcomers have Cody Carlson making his GPW debut in Raleigh against Shane Jackson and then the match with Axl Blaze on The Global Invasion, Matthew Funk battling
Guff Sawyer on the same show, and Jacob Azrael battling The Zinmaster on The
Global Invasion.
While lips remain sealed, it seems Global Pro-Wrestling is on the verge of a cross-promotional exercise of enormous proportions. Details are sketchy, but preliminary leaks are indicating that GPW have reached an agreement with an internationally-renowned celebrity to increase mainstream publicity - and it isn't a 'He.' Her identity may remain a mystery for the time being, but speculation is already running rampant and to quote one particularly smug source "You ain't gonna believe it... and you ain't gonna wanna miss it..."
