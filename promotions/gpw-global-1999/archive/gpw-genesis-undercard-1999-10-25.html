However, Sullivan got a tad bit too cocky as, when he was showboating for the crowd, Boltz took an advantage and quickly planted Sullivan with his screwdriver finisher and ended up getting the win. Boltz looked very impressive in this match along with Sullivan who showed signs of being a great brawler here in Global
Pro-Wrestling."
