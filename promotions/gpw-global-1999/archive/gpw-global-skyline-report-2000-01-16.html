============================================================================ ==== ************************** THE NEWS AND RUMOR MILL: ************************** -What a pay-per-view Future Fight 2000 was! And what a load of mess there is to sort through as a result! First off, the results: The Phoenix beat
Ryan Stone, Zack Sharp won the Triple Threat Brass Knux Title match, Law and Disorder beat VIP to claim the American Tag-Team Titles, Johnny Adonis beat Carl Blair in the best 2 of 3 falls Television Title Match and what might very well been match of the night, Mad Valentine beat James Wilson,
Dagger Lorath and Shadowman outlasted Rune and Rapier, and, in one of the most violent main events in history, Priest Noble won the GPW World
Heavyweight Title. -Now on to issues stemming from the pay-per-view. The most important issue is that of what Chris Page did to Andrew Bailey and Priest Noble. First, it is our sad duty to report that the injuries Chris Page gave to Andrew
Bailey were severe enough for doctors to force Bailey into early retirement. To top it off, Page then proceeded to nearly kill Priest Noble with his car. As a result, Commisioner Blain issued a statement from his office STRIPPING Chris Page of the GPW Regional Title for actions unbecoming of a champion in Global Pro-Wrestling. In addition, Page will be suspended for one week without pay and will not be allowed to step foot in the shows this week in Panama City, Orlando, and Miami, Florida. Page's allies, Furious Steele and Drew Archer of High Intensity Carnage, merely laughed at the statement and said the worst is yet to come. To fill the vacancy, Commisioner Blain has ordered a 16 man tournament to be fought over the course of this weekend with the finals occuring on Meltdown from
Miami.
-What a main event we saw at Future Fight and it ended up crowning a new GPW World Heavyweight Champion in Priest Noble. However, the ending of the match is up for review as some are saying Robinson might have grabbed hold of the title first. Yet, since the belt was in Noble's hands when they fell to the mat, the match and title was given to Noble. The funny thing here is that Noble refused the title belt at the end of the match and gave it to this Danielle charcter who he saved from getting hit by Chris Page's car. In a related note, Mad Valentine is said to be making rumblings about wanting his guaranteed World Title shot for holding The American Title.
Also, in a related note, this week's main event for Meltdown will pit the three explosive challenging factions for the World Title against each other as Eugene Robinson and Adam Rogers, Team Ego, and Furious Steele along with a MYSTERY partner will hook it up in a triangle tag-team match. Steele would not drop any hints as to who his partner would be, although many are expecting it to be Drew Archer.
-Now on to the Team Ego situation as it exploded on the pay-per-view when Mad Valentine alligned himself with Tommy Gunn AFTER Gunn cost Carl Blair the Television Title. Valentine said Blair is over-reacting about the situation but Blair himself nearly lost it when we told him that. If you didn't see, Gunn turned out to be "The Man" and I guess Mad Valentine couldn't pass the opportunity up of getting Gunn in the Team Ego camp and alienating Carl Blair in the process. As we said, Valentine is said to be wanting his World Title shot REAL soon. Also, James Wilson is said to be hunting Tommy Gunn after what he did. He also said he means business when he said he would retire Gunn.
-Welcome backs are in store for Ric Studd, Dan Starr, and Jared Mathys who all made their return to Global Pro-Wrestling at the pay-per-view. Ric
Studd practically tore the house down as he ripped Lew Spectre apart and then Dan Starr turned out to be the one causing all the interrupters in GPW programming. When Studd started talking about the SCW National Title, it got the best of Starr and the two ignited a rivalry that has gone on for some time. Mathys, on the other hand, ended up jumping out of the crowd after Ryan Stone's loss to the Phoenix. Mathys shook his former partner's hand the two left together in the back. Word has it that we might be seeing a retooled version of "Showtime" here in the new future and as early as Sunday because Mathys and Stone are scheduled for interview time.
-The troubles of Rune and Rapier continue as their problems cost them the World Tag-Team Titles against the champions, Dagger Lorath and Shadowman. Even though they are experiencing troubles, they are booked to fight Law and Disorder, the newly crowned GPW American Tag-Team Champions and the first ever team to run the table in tag belts here in Global, this Saturday on The Global Invasion. On Meltdown this week, Dagger Lorath and Shadowman will make a defense against a new team...that of Chris Quiller and Bryan
Meredith. This is steaming from Dagger and Shadow's attack on Meredith during the undercard of the pay-per-view in which they cost him a shot at
Zack Sharp's Brass Knux Title.
-Speaking of Zack Sharp, he is set to face Czar on The Global Invasion this week and it should be red hot, especially after the attack Czar gave Sharp after his successful title defense on the pay-per-view. Speaking of the
Brass Knux Division, word has it that The Phoenix might enter it soon and, with T'Chamunga back from "JDGPW", it is as strong as ever.
-Bad news for The Zinmaster as he knees are giving him trouble and he will be out of action for a while. Some are saying that he might actually announce his retirement this Sunday when he speaks on Meltdown.
-The tension between Rex Watson and Johnny Blades is getting thick. Word has it that Nicholas Richard wants a piece of both of them just because he doesn't like them. That comes directly from "The Army of One"'s mouth.
-Lance Love is said to be fed up with all the attacks that he has been given and is said to be ready to do something drastic -Word has it that Sean Willey is the favorite for the Regional Title tournament, although Jesse Muldoon is set to stop the charging train that is Willey -The Fantasm made his debut this past weekend and, if you saw the match, you saw some sort of mysterious character in the crowd. Who or what it was is unknown to us at this time but The Fantasm showed a lot of promise in his debut
-ANY COMMENTS, CRITICISM, OR QUESTIONS....SEND THEM MY WAY :) ============================================================================ ==== ******************************* COMINGS, GOINGS, AND INJURED ******************************* COMINGS: Luke Andrews Tommy Gunn GOINGS: NO ONE INJURED: Priest Noble: Numerous, will not miss any action The Zinmaster: Knee problems, time out unknown ============================================================================ ==== ============================================================================ ==== GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING WEEKLY LINE-UPS ============================================================================ ==== ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^ HOUSE SHOW LINE-UP FOR Panama City, Florida 1/20/2000: RECAPPED ON THE GLOBAL INVASION ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^ POOL A OF THE REGIONAL TITLE TOURNAMENT: FIRST ROUND 1.
