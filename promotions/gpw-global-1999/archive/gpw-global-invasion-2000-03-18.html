In the arena, we can see that the Global
View Video Screen is present and that the ring has a majestic golden GPW banner on it. Above the ring, two GPW Banners are hanging while a small but impressive fireworks display ignites from the walkway to the ring. Finally, as the fireworks dim down, a voice over begins to speak.....it is Aaron
Hayes....>>
[HAYES]: "Good evening ladies and gentlemen and welcome to yet another jam packed edition of The Global Invasion, coming to you tonight from beautiful
Norfolk, Virginia. All the action you see here tonight is a build up to our mega-event coming us this Sunday from Richmond. It is our special Meltdown for February and will be entitled "Tough Love"! The NEW GPW World
Heavyweight Champion will be decided inside a steel cage and so much more. But, before we can get there, let us take a look at all the action that happened earlier in the week and earlier tonight!"
<<The video from the house show begins to play...>> **************************************************************************** **** GPW HOUSE SHOW REVIEW 2/10/2000 VIRGINIA BEACH, VIRGINIA **************************************************************************** **** ************************ KYLE COLE VS. CRAZY J ************************ [HAYES]: "In our opening match of the week, the returning Kyle Cole, a former young lion in Southern Championship Wrestling, met the challenge of
Crazy J. The match was, no doubt, one sided as Cole dazzled the crowd with an impressive aerial and high risk display. Cole looked like he hadn't missed a step as he was throwing things towards Crazy J at a lightning quick pace. Finally, in the end, Cole nailed Crazy J with a Shooting Star Press to get the easy win in his first match back."
WINNER: KYLE COLE ************************ OYABUN VS. FLIC ROGERS ************************ [HAYES]: "Still angry about being on a house show, Oyabun took his frustrations out on his first opponent of the week, Flic Rogers. The Yakuza tore Rogers apart, piece by piece, and apparently did serious damange to
Flic's shins with several stiff kicks in that area. Tired of having his talent wasted in the match, Oyabun finished Rogers off quickly with the
8-9-3 which drew blood as Rogers' nose looked like it got broken because of the kicks."
WINNER: OYABUN ****************************** DAMON KNIGHT VS. KRIS EVANS ****************************** [HAYES]: "In what turned out to be the match of the night in Virginia Beach,
Damon Knight battled Kris Evans in a very stiff and fast paced match-up.
Evans entered the contest arrogantly and apparently thought Knight was a push over but, when Damon clocked Kris with an inverted leg-sweep manuever to start things off, "Kraze" saw he had his work cut out for him. The battle drug on as Evans tried to use his brawling and techincal skills while
Knight took to the air with a variety of suicidal manuevers. In the end, though, Evans caught Damon Knight in powerbomb posistion and was about to hit his sit-out powerbomb but, as Knight went up, he flipped Evans with a huracanrana, hooked the legs, and scored the upset pin over Evans. The crowd errupted as Knight quickly left the ring and Evans was extremely heated towards the referee."
WINNER: DAMON KNIGHT *************************** SAMHAIN VS. LANCE WALKER *************************** [HAYES]: "Our match pitting Samhain vs. Lance Walker was set to be a good old fashioned fight but, it never happened, because as both men were about to come to the ring, they were assaulted by none other than Bruno Bordeaux and The Zinmaster, Superior Darkness. Like Law and Disorder last week,
Samhain and Walker were this week's stop on "The Random Acts of Violence to the Extreme" Tour as Bruno and The Zinmaster took them out in the back with steel chairs. Both men left the area before security could catch them but the match between Samhain and Lance Walker had to be scrapped as a result."
WINNER: NO ONE ****************************** CARL BLAIR VS. JARED MATHYS ****************************** [HAYES]: "In our main event in Virginia Beach, Carl Blair took on Jared
Mathys, who was led to the ring by Ric Studd. It should be noted that Carl took on Ric Studd in the main event for tonight and will be seen later on.
However, for the match in Virginia Beach, it was all Carl Blair as he outpaced, outworked, and outskilled Jared Mathys. It seemed that Mathys could not adapt to Blair's Japanese influenced style and The Panther paid for it. Blair ended the match quickly, though, as he caught Mathys with his
"Living the Dream" Running Powerbomb and then followed it up with a breath-taking Frog Splash for the win. Afterwards, Blair could be seen mouting off to Studd that "he was next."
WINNER: CARL BLAIR [HAYES]: "Well fans, that is all the action that went down in Virginia
Beach. Let us go directly to what happened here, earlier tonight, in
Norfolk!" ********************* OYABUN VS. CRAZY J ********************* [HAYES]: "Much like his match with Flic Rogers in Virginia Beach, Oyabun was none too happy about fighting Crazy J in the opening contest tonight. The menancing and vicious Japanese wrestler took Crazy J out of commision extremely early but then decided to make an example of him. Oyabun gave
Crazy J repeated kicks to the shin followed up by vicious palm blows that began to draw blood on Crazy J's face. Referee Jason Taylor would not stop
Oyabun's assault as Oyabun tore into Crazy J. Finally, Oyabun took the bloody Crazy J and drilled him with the 8-9-3 for the win. It should be noted that Khan Troll, manager of T'Chamunga, was seen watching Oyabun from the back. What this means is unknown to us at the time but it could be very interesting."
WINNER: OYABUN *********************************** ALEC ACE VS. SEBASTIAN ST. CLAIR *********************************** [HAYES]: "Making his return to the rings of GPW, Alec Ace, the former Regional Champion, met Sebastian St. Clair, an opponent hardly worth of
Ace's skill. Ace schooled St. Clair from the start and apparently had a good time at it to. Ace outpowered, outskilled, and outmatched St. Clair and the fans did not buy Ace's arrogant approach to beating St. Clair into a worthless pulp. Finally, Ace apparently grew tired of Sebastian and finished his opponent off with his Superstar Crunch, a vicious jumping jawbreaker that knocked St. Clair out cold as Ace followed up with the pin."
WINNER: ALEC ACE *********************************** BRUNO BORDEAUX VS. LANCE WALKER *********************************** [HAYES]: "Angry at what happened in Virginia Beach, Lance Walker hit the ring and immediately opened up on his opponent, Bruno Bordeaux, as the crowd went crazy. However, Bordeaux was ready for Walker and drilled him with an unseen low blow in the middle of Walker's offensive that stopped Lance dead in his tracks. The match was all Bordeaux at that point as Bruno outpowered his opponent and continued to show signs of improvement. Finally, the
Superior Darkness member dropped Walker with a Pump-Handle slam and then dropped his huge leg-drop off the top-rope to get the win over one Lance
Walker."
WINNER: BRUNO BORDEAUX *************************************************** GPW BRASS KNUX TITLE MATCH: JARED MATHYS VS. RUNE *************************************************** [HAYES]: "In our only title match of the night, it was Jared Mathys fighting the newly crowned GPW Brass Knux Champion, Rune. Well, let's just say,
Mathys is not a Brass Knux caliber wrestler and it showed as Rune completely dominated the match. I don't know what it is but Mathys was not all there during this match and it was just m opportunities for Rune to decimate him.
The match was pretty violent at times as Mathys was cut open as the blood trickled down from his match. Rune, though, ended things fairly quickly by dropping Mathys with his second-rope tombstome piledriver for the win to retain the title belt."
WINNER: RUNE ******************************* DAMON KNIGHT VS. ZACK SHARP ******************************* [HAYES]: "Our next match of the evening featured former GPW Brass Knux
Champion, Zack Sharp, going against Damon Knight. Like Kris Evans, I think
Sharp under-estimated Knight from the start as Damon came out of the gates with a furry and unloaded on Sharp with his highly innovative style.
However, Sharp got the advantage back and was just brutalizing Knight in the middle of the ring for a good five minutes before the referee could stop
Sharp. Yet, Zack continued his onslaught as he proceed to go under the ring and pull out a PIECE OF BARBED WIRE! The crowd was shocked and the referee warned Sharp about using it but, during the process, Knight swept Sharp's legs out from under him and Zack landed on the barbed wire. After that,
Knight dropped him with a Death Valley Driver, rolled him up, and scored another upset as he pinned Zack Sharp. While Knight was celebrating in the ring, Sharp got back up and put on a vicious choke hold on Knight. Sharp refused to let it go as Knight was gasping for air. Finally, security broke the incident up but Sharp had a, pardon the pun, "Sick and Twisted" look in his eye afterwards."
WINNER: DAMON KNIGHT **************************** SAMHAIN VS. THE ZINMASTER **************************** [HAYES]: "Like Lance Walker, Samhain unloaded on his opponent for the night,
The Zinmaster, in revenge of what happened in Virginia Beach. He really took The Zinmaster to the limit in the beginning moments of the match and even drew blood with a couple of real stiff fists. However, as The
Zinmaster looked on at his bleeding forehead, his mood changed and he began to just unload on Samhain with a brutal assault. Without remorse, The
Zinmaster beat Samhain down and finally ended him with a diving headbutt off the top rope to get the pinfall win. Afterwards, The Zinmaster kicked
Samhain in the ribs a bit before leaving the ring and yelling out challenges to an absent Law and Disorder."
WINNER: THE ZINMASTER *************************** KRIS EVANS VS. RAPIER *************************** [HAYES]: "Still angry about his loss to Damon Knight, Kris Evans took his frustations out on Rapier, who was fighting his last match in GPW. I don't think the former World Tag-Team Champion knew Evans would be this angry as
"Kraze" just dominated Rapier from start to finish. Evans looked as if he had something to prove and he did it in a big way with a massive beatdown on
Rapier. To finish him off, Evans drilled Rapier with TWO sit-out powerbombs and then topped him off with a third for good measure to get the win.
Afterwards, Evans look a tad bit happier but he still had an angry look on his face as he heard the boos from the crowd."
WINNER: KRIS EVANS *************************** TOMMY GUNN VS. KYLE COLE *************************** [HAYES]: "In the battle of the high-flyers, Kyle Cole took on "The Natural" Tommy Gunn in an explosive and high impact encounter. Cole had the advantage at the start as he wanted to prove something by taking it to the member of Team Ego with a beautiful aerial offensive. However, Gunn was resiliant and resisted Cole's offense as he anticipated a move by Cole and then capitalized on the following mistake. From there, it was all Tommy
Gunn as he rocked Cole and the crowd with a dazzling offensive display that was capped off with a lightning quick superkick to Cole's face for the pinfall win. Chock another impressive victory up for one Tommy Gunn."
WINNER: TOMMY GUNN ********************************************************************** REX WATSON AND NICHOLAS RICHARD VS. JOHNNY BLADES AND JAMES WILSON ********************************************************************** [HAYES]: "If a match ever resembled a warzone, the following tag-match between Rex Watson and Nicholas Richard going against Johnny Blades and
James Wilson would win that designation. From the start, it was utter chaos in the ring as partners fought with partners while still holding off their opponents. At one point, James Wilson entered the ring and then Johnny
Blades entered right behind him. Blades turned Wilson around and decked him with a pair of brass knuckles, unseen to the referee, and knocked Wilson out. Blades proceeded to leave Wilson to the wolves, Watson and Richard, as they tore into the former American Champion before tearing into each other.
As Watson and Richard were about to get the win, though, Blades ran back down to the ring, jumped in, and decked a running Nicholas Richard with one of the stiffest lariats I have ever seen. To follw up, Watson got clocked with a fist to the side of the head by Blades and it allowed Blades to steal the win over Richard and set in the triple threat cage match, next week on
Meltdown."
WINNERS: JOHNNY BLADES AND JAMES WILSON ****************************** LUKE ANDREWS VS. SEAN WILLEY ****************************** [HAYES]: "In a non-title match, GPW Regional Champion, Sean Willey, took on
"California Love" Luke Andrews in a very exciting and heated encounter. As you all know, Andrews wants the GPW Television Title, currently held by
Willey ally, Johnny Adonis. To get to Adonis, though, Andrews had to get through Willey. The match was a see-saw encounter from the start as Andrews did not let Willey's size intimidate him one bit. "California Love" went right to Willey as if he was of normal height and weight. The no-fear attitude by Andrews did not amuse Willey as he, at one point, drilled the oncoming Luke Andrews with a massive boot to the face. With Andrews stunned, Willey rocked him with a vicious powerbomb that nearly knocked
Andrews' teeth straight out of his mouth. Finally, to everyones' suprise,
Willey took Andrews and drilled him with his reverse Death Valley Driver to score the pin over "California Love"! Afterwards, Willey began to stomp away at Andrews until Cameron Kidd came down for the rescue. "David"
confronted "Goliath" again and the two almost went at it but, from the curtain area, Johnny Adonis hit the ring and suddenly began the next match..."
WINNER: SEAN WILLEY ********************************* JOHNNY ADONIS VS. CAMERON KIDD ********************************* [HAYES]: "With fire in his heart, Cameron Kidd unloaded on the Television
Champion as the crowd went crazy. He was pulling everything out that he had and just wailed away on Adonis who didn't know what hit him. However, the crafty veteran in Adonis came back and haunted Kidd as Kidd was going for a spin kick but Adonis brushed it to the side at Kidd hit the mat hard.
Adonis was all over him after that and the crowd desperately cheered for
Kidd to get up. Adonis continued to overpower his opponent while taunting the crowd about their fallen hero. However, Kidd came back as the crowd got behind him. Nothing began to effect him as Adonis did not know what to do.
Finally, Kidd had Adonis right where he wanted him and locked him in his
"Nighty Night" Sleeper hold. With Adonis about to go down, the crowd was stunned as Adonis reached his leg back up and kicked Kidd in the groin.
Kidd, stunned and hurt, never saw what happened next as Adonis caught him and spiked him with The Hawaiian Delight, put his feet on the ropes, and stole the pin away from Cameron Kidd. It was a real shame to see Kidd fall to Adonis like that."
WINNER: JOHNNY ADONIS *************************************** MAIN EVENT: CARL BLAIR VS. RIC STUDD *************************************** [HAYES]: "In our main event of the evening, it was the old guard vs. the new guard as Ric Studd battled Carl Blair. The crowd was quite behind Blair on this one as they did not approve of Studd's retiring Dan Starr last week.
Blair ate all the attention up and taunted Studd about it from start to finish. However, the veteran grew ill of Blair's tactics and tore into the much smaller Carl Blair. The majority of the match was all Studd as he was just too big and powerful for Blair's type of match. However, near the end, the crowd buzzed as Kris Evans walked down to the ring and eyeball Ric
Studd. Evans was holding a steel chair in his hand and pointed it in Ric
Studd's direction. As Studd was about to go after Evans, Blair came up from behind and dropped him with an inverted DDT which was followed by his Frog
Spalsh off the top rope. As Evans walked back up the ramp, Blair got the win and a great crowd reaction as the show ended."
WINNER: CARL BLAIR <<The shot of the empty arena is shown....>> [HAYES]: "So fans, another edition of The Global Invasion is in the books as we now are focused squarely on tommorow night in Richmond. A historic card will take place as we GUARANTEE TO YOU there will be a new World Champion by the end of the night. It will be the winner of the Eugene Robinson and Mad
Valentine lumberjack match battling Chris Page inside a steel cage for our richest prize. In addition, T'Chamunga finally battles The Phoenix and
Violence and Corruption tries to take the GPW World Tag-Team Titles off of
XTremeCharisma! Until then, I'm Aaron Hayes saying so long and good night!"
<<The camera fades to black while a slide show effect of the action from tonight is shown on the screen...>>
