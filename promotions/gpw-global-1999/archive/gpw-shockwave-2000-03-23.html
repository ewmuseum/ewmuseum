============================================================================ ==== ******************************************* ADAM ROGERS - GPW AMERICAN CHAMPION ******************************************* To: The list is getting too long. You know who you are, anyway. From: 'The Natural' and GPW American Champion (for a long time to come),
Adam Rogers [The scene opens with a close-up of a statue of some sort. The dark colored metal used to shape the character's features has a slight glare reflecting from the midday sun that shines overhead. The camera pans back somewhat, giving us a much better view of this statue, revealing it to be a tribute to the late voice of the Chicago Cubs, Harry Carey.]
[So that's where we are... downtown Chicago, just outside of Wrigley Field. The camera zooms out to give us a shot of the whole scene, and we now see the GPW American Champion, GPW's only true 'Natural', Adam
Rogers, standing beside the statue. Looking down, he appears to be reading the plate set on the base of the statue. His short blond hair moves gently in the mid-strength wind that blows through the Windy
City. He is dressed rather simply as usual -- pullover golf shirt, khaki pants, brown loafers... nothing extravagant at all, but not too shabby either.]
[As he finishes reading the plate, Adam lifts his head up, taking another look at the statue, a small grin coming over his face. Being a lifelong baseball fan, he can't help showing his appreciation for Harry, reminiscing on many of Harry's blunders and calls over the years.
Finally, he turns and walks away from the statue, as a few other random fans wander up behind. He begins walking toward the camera, which moves backward in time with his pace.]
[ROGERS]: "Good ol' Harry Carey, now _there's_ a guy who left his mark on the world. I doubt anyone will forget his thick voice, signing 'Take
Me Out To The Ballgame' in a semi-drunken stupor, while everyone in
Wrigley sang along. Seems like the strangest things make people immortal. One thing's for sure, everyone knew who Harry Carey was."
[Adam pauses, seemingly showing his respects to the late broadcasting legend.] [ROGERS]: "Seems like everyone's out to make a name for themselves, especially in Global Pro Wrestling. Heck, I remember not too long ago when yours truly was out to do that exact thing. Of course, I succeeded in accomplishing that, and in the process learned something very valuable... when you're the big name, people want to make their names known at your expense. Ever since I stepped it up, joined forces with
Eugene, became the most popular athlete in GPW, and won the American
Title, it seems like my name has been added to hit list after hit list around here. But you know, I don't mind at all. When you're at the top, people line up to knock you off of the hill. And it seems like there's a line the length of a line at Disney to knock _me_ off the hill."
[A slight smile and quiet laugh comes from Adam's face. Strangely, it seems like he enjoys being the hunted now. Proceeding down the street,
Adam comes to a stop outside of the entrance to Wrigley Field.]
[ROGERS]: "First it was Steele, then Tommy Gunn, then Alec Ace, and now it seems like Ric Donovan _and_ Ric Studd want to get in line as well.
Studd mentioned that he beat me before, and it's true... he _did_ beat me, at the Genesis Undercard, in my first match in over a year. And if you remember, he also gave me a nice 'welcome back' present in the form of a beating after the match. Well, Studd, I'll just say this... you've still got your receipts coming for what you did to me back then, and I
_never_ let a tab go unsettled."
[The American Champion pauses, looking over toward the stadium. Still a few weeks from opening day, there is nothing going on at the field. He keeps moving on, now walking past the stadium and onto the famous
Waveland Avenue.] [ROGERS]: "And as for your good friend Ric Donovan, the self-proclaimed
'Heir to the Throne'... well, Ric, you seem to be bursting with confidence. You seem to think that just because you were this and that in SCW, that you can walk into GPW... into Springfield, Illinois... into
The Global Invasion, and take this title off my waist. Here's a news flash, pal... you are living a lie. It takes a _whole_ slew of brains to come in here and call me a paper champion, whatever the hell _that_ really means. But as long as you're saying these overused catch phrases, here's one you might follow... your ass is grass, and I'm driving the John Deere. Donovan, this isn't SCW, and I'm not any of the people you faced there. What you need is a good dose of reality, and come Saturday night you can believe I'm going to shove it right down your throat. The fact is, you haven't _earned_ this title shot, and I'm going to make _sure_ that I teach you a thing or two about paying your dues, because lord knows I had to pay mine."
[As he walks down the road, a few people shout and wave to him. Turning his attention their way, Adam stops long enough to wave back and acknowledge their support.]
[ROGERS]: "It's people like those folks right there who make putting up with the crap worthwhile. And when I say crap, I mean Alec Ace especially. So, Alec, you hate me? You hate me with every bone in your body? Wow, I must admit, I'm so incredibly hurt. In fact, I just can't go on with life now, because the 'great' Alec Ace hates me."
[OK, the sarcasm was pretty obvious there.] [ROGERS]: "Please. Do you _really_ think that I, or anyone else for that matter, _cares_ what you think about me? The fact is, Ace, no one cares about you, period. I could hear the collective yawn in Richmond when you came out and made an idiot of yourself when I called you out.
Things aren't the same as they were a few months ago, are they? _I'm_ the one the people care about, _I'm_ the one the people cheer for, and
_I'M_ the one wearing the sixteen pounds of gold. And you? Well, you're back to being the nobody you always were. Comparing today to what happened over the Regional Title is like comparing apples to peanuts... there _is_ no comparison. You only beat me _once_, Alec, contrary to what you might believe. Pins on Lance Love in triangle matches don't quite count as pins on 'The Natural'. You may _think_ you're breathing down my neck, but in essence all you're doing is breathing hot air."
[Adam pauses, glad that he's done wasting precious air time addressing someone like Ace. Stopping beside a parked car... a familiar red Lexus to be exact... Adam continues on.]
[ROGERS]: "So you really think you're inside my head, Ace? You give yourself _way_ too much credit. Even the master of mind games himself,
Furious Steele, can't get in my head... and he's been trying for months. Well, Steele, it seems like your plan backfired once again.
Putting even my worst enemies in a crumpled heap at my feet isn't going to bring me to your side. You know, I've been thinking, and I have figured out what your deal is. The solution is simple, too... you need to attend one of those anger management classes, because you're just out of control. Anger is a weakness, Steele... a weakness that I will never let overtake me. While people like you get angry, people like me get the job done. That's why you failed in your attempt to win the World
Title, that's why you failed in your attempt to win the World Tag Team
Titles... and that's why you'll fail in bringing me to Hi-C. All that anger inside you is just going to lead to the same fate that your beloved Jim Morrison suffered, and when it does happen... don't say I didn't warn you."
[Adam takes a set of keys from his pocket, pushing a button on a small remote. With a 'beep!', the lights on the Lexus flash, indicating that the alarm is off. Adam opens the driver side door and climbs into to car. In no time, he brings the V-8 engine to life, and the roar of the engine is all we hear as the camera fades to black.]
