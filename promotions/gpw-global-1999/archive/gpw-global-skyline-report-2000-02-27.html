MORE TO COME NEXT WEEK! ============================================================================ ==== ****************************** NOTES FROM THE FRONT OFFICE ****************************** -Hey guys, I've decided to just put the new pieces of important information in this section: -AS A REMINDER ALL STRATS, CARD INTERVIEWS QUESTIONS, IDEAS, and ANYTHING ELSE GO TO: [detail withheld] -OUR LEAGUE WEBSITE(KUDOS TO THE DESIGNER) IS: http://www.geocities.com/globalprowrestling : EMAIL [detail withheld] to TELL HIM WHAT YOU WOULD LIKE TO SEE ON THE WEBSITE. -Deadlines for card submissions are 10:00 PM CST On Thursday Night. If you need extra time, you need to contact me ASAP and have a good reason. -Also, I would like to see you all start to treat Shockwave as the television program that it is. ANGLES can progress on this show and don't think that just because it isn't Meltdown, you shouldn't make an attempt to do so. Same goes for The Global Invasion. However, try to steer away from doing in ring interviews on Shockwave. Use Shockwave to get your character over in an out-of-ring setting and use Global Invasion and Meltdown for in-rings. MAKE THINGS INTERESTING AND STAND OUT. Shockwave is the character development program! -PLEASE REFRAIN from referring to other leagues in your flashes. It takes away from the image I'm trying to build of GPW being the only federation worth joining on the internet :) -I would LOVE comments, thoughts, and criticism on the pay-per-view concerning presentation and look. If you have problems with the bookings, well, you know my policy on that. Also, I'd like to hear what you thought were the highlights of the ppv as well. ============================================================================ ==== ************************** THE NEWS AND RUMOR MILL: ************************** -Well what a weekend it was! While in Nevada this weekend, we saw the tension between Hi-C and Team Ego explode once more thus forcing Commisioner Blain to sign an impromptu 6 Man Gang War Match between the two factions. It started in Reno when Chris Page got the win over Tommy Gunn, carried over to Meltdown where Johnny Blades discovered Carl Blair's injury was a put-on, and finished in the main event when Taylor Hayes cost Hi-C and, namely, Johnny Blades the match-up. Afterwards, a bombshell was dropped on the league: Next week, in Ottawa, Commisioner Blain ordered a TEN MAN TAG-TEAM WAR that will feature, get ready for this, Team Ego and The Accolades of Emminence going against Hi-C and Sex & Violence. I can only dream of what that match will become. I have a feeling we will have our hottest edition of Meltdown in history next week when we cross the border for our first International Meltdown...live from Ottawa, Ontario, Canada. -In other news, two more pay-per-view matches have been signed for Duel on The Dunes 2000-"The Sin and Skin Spectacular". Domination will get a crack at XTremeCharisma's World Tag-Team Titles and, in a triangle elimination match, the tension between Tommy Gunn, Furious Steele, and The American Heavyweight Champion, Adam Rogers, will come to a head. In related news, Canadian Chris Quiller will be on Meltdown this Sunday as he will face Wick of Domination. You can expect the crowd to be biased in this one. -A couple of rising stars have caught the eyes of GPW management and they are Oyabun and Damon Knight. Both have cracked the top ten and, after this past Saturday in Reno, Damon Knight looks to be the number one contender to the GPW World Television Title. However, I think Luke Andrews might have a problem with that. Oyabun, however, is still involved in this bitter war with Rune and has brought T'Chamunga into it for some reason. On Meltdown this Sunday, Oyabun makes his live wrestling debut as he TEAMS with T'Chamunga to face The Phoenix and Rune in the first ever live broadcast of a BRASS KNUX RULES TAG-TEAM MATCH! -Speaking of The Phoenix, word has it that his rumored match with The Zinmaster is about to be signed for Duel on The Dunes 2000. Both men have requested it to be a Japanese style Death Match and, at the moment, GPW management is still working on the specifics. They said, however, if they do sign it, they will not sanction the match-up. Also, I've heard rumors that The Phoenix and Rune have been spotted talking to James Wilson in the dressing room area. Could we possibly be seeing an alliance forming? Rumor has it that Rune might be facing Oyabun at the PPV but, another rumor going around has James Wilson and Rune teaming up to face T'Chamunga and Oyabun. -Kris Evans can't be a happy camper after this week as an accidental chair shot from Ric Donovan cost him the win in the tag-match with Ric Studd and the impressive Rex Watson. This Sunday in Ottawa, though, Evans will go for revenge as he will get Studd in a singles contest. Donovan and Watson, though, will be at ringside. In related news, Rex Watson will meet Alec Ace this weekend as Ace tries to rebound after his loss, Ric Studd will meet former partner Lance Walker, and Ric Donovan will meet the returning Derek Sweger. It should be a very interesting set of matches. -Sean Willey has vowed to be even more brutal this week on Meltdown after being arrested for his actions against Kyle Cole. Word has it that Cameron Kidd, against the orders of doctors, might actually fly up to Ottawa this weekend. If he did, it would be a big mistake in my book. Sex and Violence have a full slate this Sunday. In addition to the 10 Man Tag-Team War, they will meet Tommy Gunn and Mad Valentine on The Global Invasion. -Speaking of The Global Invasion, other key matches signed include a singles match with Carl Blair and Chris Page. Page has stated he will go through each member of Team Ego before getting to Valentine. He has already gotten a win over Gunn and now Blair is in his sights. In addition, the main event in Thunderbay this Saturday will be the interesting match-up pitting The Accolades of Emminence going against Violence and Corruption 2000! -Nicholas Richard has stated that he has a suprise in store for Kyle Cole this saturday in Thunderbay. Both men will open the show in Thunderbay and Richard has told this publication that he has once again put top emphasis on his career here in Global Pro-Wrestling. The question, though, is why did T'Chamunga attack him on The Global Invasion and why did Khan Troll order him to? Richard has also stated that, no matter what, he will be a part of the PPV and get to T'Chamunga in some fashion -You all saw his return on Meltdown....David Smith is now a part of the GPW family. After his notorious career in Southern Championship Wrestling, Commisioner Blain signed Smith to an on-air contract which means he can be used as a commentator, manager, or anything else. However, his contract states that HE WILL NOT be an active competitor in the ring and, apparently, Smith has no desire to be one either. -Global Professional Wrestling would like to announce its plans for after "Duel on the Dunes 2000" which will be on March 26th. It has been decided that GPW will take a two week hiatus in programming until Saturday, April 8th and Sunday, April 9th. On those two days, we will invade the country of Japan for our "Bridging the Gap" Tour. We will be in Osaka, Japan on the 8th and The Tokyo Dome on April 9th for the special edition of Meltdown entitled "Bridging the Gap." These two shows will be the only programs run in the month of April for Global Pro-Wrestling as we will give our wrestlers time off for personal reasons. Regular programming will return on May 6th and 7th with shows in Tempe and Phoenix, Arizona. Our May PPV will take place on Sunday night, May 28th from New Orleans, Louisiana and will be entitled "Starlight Galatic." We also would like to announce the cancellation of our New Jersey swing right before Duel on The Dunes 2000. So, before the PPV, we have shows in Nevada, Canada, and England, before returning to the states for the big showdown in Daytona Beach. -GPW would like to ask, you, the talent to send in submissions to The Global Skyline Report. If you have an editorial comment or rumors you want to drop, send them in and we'll try to air them!" -ANY COMMENTS, CRITICISM, OR QUESTIONS....SEND THEM MY WAY :) ============================================================================ ==== ******************************* COMINGS, GOINGS, AND INJURED ******************************* COMINGS: Derek Sweger GOINGS: None INJURED: None ============================================================================ ==== ============================================================================ ==== GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING WEEKLY LINE-UPS ============================================================================ ==== ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^ GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING PROUDLY PRESENTS "THE GLOBAL INVASION" 3/4/2000 From THUNDERBAY, ONTARIO, CANADA ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ^^^^ 1.
