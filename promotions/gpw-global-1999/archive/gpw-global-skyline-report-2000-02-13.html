I would like feedback on this at [detail withheld] -Here are some informative materials that you may find useful: -FLASHES GO TO: [detail withheld] -ALL STRATS, CARD INTERVIEWS QUESTIONS, IDEAS, and ANYTHING ELSE GO TO: [detail withheld] AND [detail withheld] -OUR LEAGUE WEBSITE(KUDOS TO THE DESIGNER) IS: http://www.geocities.com/globalprowrestling : A LOT HAS BEEN UPDATED!!!! CHECK IT OUT(RANKINGS WILL BE UPDATED ON THE SITE ASAP) -VOTE FOR GLOBAL PRO-WRESTLING ON E-ZONE AT: http://ewzone.virtualave.net -TO BE TAKEN OFF THE MAILING LIST CONTACT: [detail withheld] -DEADLINES FOR ALL CARD MATERIAL IS THURSDAY NIGHT AT 10:00 P.M. CST!!!!! I REALLY MEAN THIS....FROM NOW ON IF I GET STUFF AFTER THE DEADLINE, THERE IS A 99.999% CHANCE IT WILL BE TRASHED. ALSO, ROLEPLAY DEALINES COME AT 9:00 P.M. CST ON TUESDAYS AND THURSDAYS SO PLEASE GET THEM IN BEFORE THEN. ALSO, I WILL START SENDING VIGNETTES AND INTERVIEWS FOR CARDS BACK IF THEY ARE NOT PROPERLY FORMATTED. That means action in the << >> and the speaker's LAST AND ONLY LAST NAME IN ALL CAPS. For example, take the name Peter Caradine. It would be written like this and only this: [CARADINE]: "Text." Also, remember, the video screen is called THE GLOBAL VIEW AND NOTHING ELSE! -Also, I would like to see you all start to treat Shockwave as the television program that it is. ANGLES can progress on this show and don't think that just because it isn't Meltdown, you shouldn't make an attempt to do so. Same goes for The Global Invasion. However, try to steer away from doing in ring interviews on Shockwave. Use Shockwave to get your character over in an out-of-ring setting and use Global Invasion and Meltdown for in-rings. MAKE THINGS INTERESTING AND STAND OUT. Shockwave is the character development program! -I would LOVE comments, thoughts, and criticism on the pay-per-view concerning presentation and look. If you have problems with the bookings, well, you know my policy on that. Also, I'd like to hear what you thought were the highlights of the ppv as well. -Guys, you really need to read the following: A lot of you are getting lazy and are not participating while I have your characters in high profile angles. Nothing makes me madder than putting a character in a developed storyline and then have his handler not contribute or participate. Just because your wrestler is not on Meltdown DOESN'T mean you don't have to roleplay. The Global Invasion is JUST AS important as Meltdown. I would write full matches for Invasion if I could but I just don't have the time. It is up to you guys to carry your weight in the angles you are put in. You've got to make whatever your doing look like that's the best thing running and not worry about working the "main event" angles or anything. You have to prove to me your are capable of working that by accepting the other storylines your character is given. I try to advance everyone each week but, it's getting hard when only a certain group roleplays and the rest just sit idle. -There are 9 parts of Meltdown this week. If you didn't get some of it, let me know because my mail program is acting weird with mass mailings. ============================================================================ ==== ************************** THE NEWS AND RUMOR MILL: ************************** -A new era has dawned in Global Pro-Wrestling as Mad Valentine is the NEW GPW World Heavyweight Champion. He defeated "The American Rage" Chris Page in one of the most memorable Meltdown main events in recent memory. Valentine will make the traditional post title win interview this Sunday on Meltdown when we invade The United Center in Chicago, Illinois. A brief piece of history will tell you that Chicago was the host to the first ever Meltdown broadcast. -Speaking of Chris Page, his "hit list" grew this Sunday as he shattered the knee-cap of one Eugene Robinson. Robinson will be out indefinetly as his knee heals and regains strength. After the loss to Valentine, Page had to be physically restrained from breaking into Team Ego's locker room on numerous occasions. -Well, they swerved us again as Carl Blair showed us what fools we were in believing him. On Meltdown, Blair rejoined Team Ego as Mad Valentine clinched his spot in the subsequent Title Match later in the evening. As a result of this and the tension between Hi-C, Carl Blair and Tommy Gunn will meet Violence and Corruption this Sunday in Chicago for Meltdown's Main Event. -Is it just me or did people actually cheer Valentine this Sunday when he won the belt? Could the fans actually be accepting Team Ego? Only time will tell. -The American Title picture is getting hazier and hazier by the day as the triangle between Alec Ace, Furious Steele, and the champion, Adam Rogers, continues to grow. We all saw the exchange on Meltdown and, this week, Alec Ace and Adam Rogers will have something in common. Alec Ace will battle Ric Studd while Rogers will battle Studd's new found enemy, the returning Ric Donovan. Both matches will happen on The Global Invasion but, one can only wonder what else will happen and who else will show up during the proceedings. Commisioner Blain has been tossing around an idea of a possible round robin tournament to determine the number one contender for the belt. -Commisioner Blain is considering punishing Oyabun for the comments the Japanese Superstar made on Meltdown this week. Oyabun refused to comment on the situation but he grew extremely aggitated once he found out that he will be opening The Global Invasion again this week as he will battle Samhain. -It's now or never for Cameron Kidd as he will finally get to meet Sean Willey for the Regional Title this Saturday on The Global Invasion. Willey has thrown in a provision that, if Kidd doesn't beat him, Cameron cannot challenge Willey for the gold ever again. In releated news, Luke Andrews will finally get his hands on Johnny Adonis this Sunday in Chicago for the Television Title. Both matches have the potential to steal each show. -Also on tap for Meltdown this Sunday, Commisioner Blain is ordering Law and Disorder to make a defense of their American Tag-Team Titles against Superior Darkness, The Zinmaster and Bruno Bordeaux. The new "Bad Boys" of GPW continued their "Random Acts of Violence" Tour this weekend with hits on Samhain and Lance Walker. Word has it that they have something special planned for the American-Tag Team Champions. -In the fourth and final match announced for Meltdown, we will see Johnny Blades, Rex Watson, and Nicholas Richard battle inside a steel cage in a first fall wins Triple Threat Match. As you all know, James Wilson is going to be the referee for this one. After his display this weekend, Johnny Blades is the favorite to win this critical contest but Rex Watson has stated he will stop at nothing to win the match. The winner will be line for a major run in the Television and American Title divisions. -Word has it that The Zinmaster is trying to convince Global Pro-Wrestling to do a Japanese Style Death Match at "Duel on the Dunes 2000". GPW has yet to comment on the situation but, when he learned of the proposal by The Zinmaster, The Phoenix showed interest in the match as well. -What is up with former GPW Brass Knux Champion Zack Sharp? He went off the deep end this Saturday after a loss to Damon Knight and, apparently, he said he will continue this attitude this week when he fights none other than The Phoenix on The Global Invasion. Sharp's tag-team partner and ally, Shadowman, has not been seen since being injured by T'Chamunga. Word has it that Shadowman is contemplating his future in the league while recovering. Speaking of T'Chamunga, after his win over The Phoenix this week, he will now meet Rune on The Global Invasion for the GPW Brass Knux Title. -Damon Knight pulled out two impressive wins this week over Zack Sharp and Kris Evans. Word has it that a couple of groups are starting to take notice to this newcomer. However, he might hit a brick wall this Saturday as he has to fight Chris Page who is, well, not in a happy mood right now. -Word has it that Kris Evans is planning on something big this week to get under Ric Studd's skin. One can only wonder what "Kraze" has in mind. -Global Professional Wrestling would like to announce its plans for after "Duel on the Dunes 2000" which will be on March 26th. It has been decided that GPW will take a two week hiatus in programming until Saturday, April 8th and Sunday, April 9th. On those two days, we will invade the country of Japan for our "Bridging the Gap" Tour. We will be in Osaka, Japan on the 8th and The Tokyo Dome on April 9th for the special edition of Meltdown entitled "Bridging the Gap." These two shows will be the only programs run in the month of April for Global Pro-Wrestling as we will give our wrestlers time off for personal reasons. Regular programming will return on May 6th and 7th with shows in Tempe and Phoenix, Arizona. Our May PPV will take place on Sunday night, May 28th from New Orleans, Louisiana and will be entitled "Starlight Galatic." We also would like to announce the cancellation of our New Jersey swing right before Duel on The Dunes 2000. So, before the PPV, we have shows in Illinois, Nevada, Canada, and England, before returning to the states for the big showdown in Daytona Beach. -ANY COMMENTS, CRITICISM, OR QUESTIONS....SEND THEM MY WAY :) ============================================================================ ==== ******************************* COMINGS, GOINGS, AND INJURED ******************************* COMINGS: Ric
