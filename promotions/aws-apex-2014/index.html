Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Apex Wrestling Syndicate, AWS, was founded in July 2014 by its Owner and CEO, Mr. Troy, and its General Manager, who made the graphics. It billed itself as a roleplay fed that tried to implement some storylines, drama free and welcoming to players of many skill levels. It ran one show a week, Ascension, on Wednesdays, with a pay-per-view on the last Wednesday of each month in place of that week's show, Its menu listed five championships, World Heavyweight, Intercontinental, Tag Team, Television and Hardcore, and under its championship awards the Lord Of Ascension and the Syndicate Royale, beside a hall of fame and the Apex Awards.

Its rules, last updated 1 March 2015, allow only created characters, in a universe with no WWE, TNA or Hulk Hogan, and require each character to be a heel or a face. Winners were decided by roleplays, judged for writing, story telling, cohesion, length and promo skills; the final deadline for a roleplay was Tuesday night at 11 PM Eastern, and a handler could post only one roleplay in the last 24 hours. The About page records that the Apex Wrestling Syndicate closed on 14 April 2015, and the home page told visitors that the fictional e-fed had closed permanently and invited them to look around its little ghost town.
