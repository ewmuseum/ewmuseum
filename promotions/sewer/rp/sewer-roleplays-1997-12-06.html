SEWER Roleplays, 12-6-97

7 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:-):-):-) S E W E R Roleplays 12-6-97 :-(:-(:-( -------- Line-up: -------- -Masked Mauler -Just Another Muslim -Masked Mauler '''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''''' [MASKED MAULER] (the camera opens with a shot of the Masked Mauler, behind him stands his #1 fan, the everpresent, Man In Black. The infamous SEWER logo hangs behind them on the wall.) <the Masked Mauler> Tuesday, December 9th at the SEWER Smokeout #1 . . . the marquee match of the evening between the international wrestling superstar, JAM and myself, the Masked Mauler, the SEWER's newest resident . . . (the Masked Mauler turns to his right and walks off . . . still talking. The Man In Black flashes a big smile and gives a Peace sign as the "Master of Wrestling Mayhem" remains out of view . . . ) . . . a match so huge, it could not take place within the confines of the squared circle and had to be moved to the street . . . (the Masked Mauler comes back into view before disappearing in the opposite direction . . . again, still talking . . . oblivious to the fact that he is no longer in camera view. The Man In Black gives a big thumb's up as MM passes once again.) . . . a match-up so hot, they're literally setting the streets aflame! (the Masked Mauler steps back into view and stops . . . finally.) Like my opponent, JAM . . . I, the Masked Mauler, am a man of few words, preferring to let my abilities in the ring, or in this case, the streets, do my talking for me . . . ++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ [JUST ANOTHER MUSLIM] ***Scene opens*** JAM: Hey Mauler, you're a dirty, sucky jerk! ***Scene closes*** :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: [MASKED MAULER] (the camera opens with a shot of a man with his back to us as he is standing in front of a urinal finishing his "business". The man gives a quick "shake and adjustment" before he zips. Turning around, we see it is SEWER Commentator, Jason Dollar with his "Shirt of the Day" bearing the saying . . . "May The Farts Be With You" . . . very appropriate for this dingy and dirty restroom. Dollar does another adjustment before speaking.) <Dollar> Greetings SEWER fans . . . Jason Dollar here . . . in the lavatory of Rudy's Diner. (the camera pans the bathroom showing us a place that truly needs to be cleaned.) <Dollar> I know you may be asking yourself "What is the Big J doing hanging out in the bathroom of a greasy spoon?" It seems that I am not the only one affiliated with the SEWER who hangs out in this dump. (as if on cue . . . the door opens and as the camera pans to the right, we see the Masked Mauler's #1 fan, the Man In Black walk in with a newspaper tucked under his arm. He looks at the cameraman then to Dollar and back to the cameraman before turning around and starting to leave. Dollar moves over to intercept him.) <Dollar> Whoa . . . wait a second there big guy . . . (he takes ahold of the Man In Black's arm, spinning him around to face the camera.) <Dollar> I would just like to ask a few questions . . . (the Man In Black clears his throat before giving a quick nod of his head, agreeing to answer a few.) <Dollar> Ok . . . and just where is the Masked Mauler now? Is he in the gym training for his match against JAM? (MIB shrugs his shoulders.) <Dollar> What do think of the Masked Mauler's chances next Tuesday in his debut match against JAM? (the Man In Black gets a smile on his face and gives a big thumbs up.) <Dollar> What do you see for JAM? I understand that he has been working out behind the local 7-11, going as far as igniting the back parking lot to get a feel for their Fire In The Street match. (the Man In Black ponders the question briefly before giving a big thumbs down on JAM's chances for victory.) <Dollar> I see . . . rumor has it that JAM has the sponsorship of the Southland Corporation. If this is true, do you feel that he will have an advantage, albeit a slight one, against the Masked Mauler? (the Man In Black shakes his head slowly . . . no.) <Dollar> Well then, do you think we will be seeing "the Valve", the Masked Mauler's famed scissorlock/figure-4 headlock finisher? (the Man In Black nods his head up and down . . . slowly. A big grin spreads across his face. He then crosses his legs and looks longingly at the open and inviting bathroom stall. He looks at Dollar, then back at the stall . . . and finally, back at Dollar this time with an almost frantic look on his face.) <Dollar> Just one last question . . . will you be present Tuesday night at the first SEWER Smokeout to root for the Masked Mauler? (the Man In Black nods his head very quickly before turning and running into the empty stall, slamming the door shut. Dollar turns back towards the camera. >From the stall we can hear unbuckling and a zipper being pulled as Dollar starts to talk.) <Dollar> This has been Jason Dollar for the SEWER, live from the men's room at Rudy's Diner . . . goodnight!
