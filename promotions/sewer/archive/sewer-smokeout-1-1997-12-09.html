SEWER Smokeout #1

9 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- S & E & W & E & R S % M % O % K % E % O % U % T Number (#) 1 December 9th, 1997! LIVE FROM SEWER ARENA!!!! Santa Hitch, RI -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [[[FIREWORKS...would be nice. Too bad we can't afford them.]]] Voice-over: Ladies and gents, not the CWO, not the IWL, not the EWA...this is SEEEEEEEEEEEEWWWWWWWWWWWEEEEEEEEEERRRRRRRRRRR!?!?!?!?!?!?!?!? [Scene opens on a less-than-jam-packed, not-so-enthused crowd at the SEWER Arena, AKA Tim Gully's grandmother's yard! A peaceful setting (as compared to what's going down later on!)...a big yard carpeted with leaves. Swingset, plus an old tire swing can be seen in the background...The only thing that separates the SEWER fans from the beat up old SEWER ring is lots and lots of shoe strings all tied together. Camera focuses on the SEWER Announcer's Booth (the hood of a 1992 Volvo 940) where Jason Dollar and Color Rake sit. They're just a couple of chubby mid-30ers. Dollar is wearing a blue shirt with the words "TEACHER'S OUT OF THE CLASSROOM" in big white print and a pair of gray slacks. Rake is sporting a yellow polo-style shirt with dirty old blue jeans. No need for any sort of sound amplification here...no mics. to be found in SEWER cuz none are ever needed (not a large enough crowd to generate any kind of noise). Finally, Dollar begins to speak...] Dollar: WELLLLLLLLLLCOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOMMMMMMMEEEE TOOOOOOOOOOOOO SEEEEEWWWWWEEEERRRRRRR SMMMMMMMOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOKEOUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUTTTTTT!!! Rake: Long enough intro? Dollar: *catching his breath* What? Rake: Nevermind. Dollar: This is the very FIRST SEWER CARD EVER! SEEEEEEEWWWEEEEERRRR SMOOOO- Rake: I think they get the picture. Now that the start thingy's out of the way, let's go get some BBQ. Dollar: BBQ? Rake: BARBECUE! Dollar: Uh...I think we have to stay here and do the show. Rake: Whatever. Dollar: Before we do get underway...SEWER would like to extend a big thank you to "Slick" Rick Baptist and his Extreme Wrestling Association for a little SEWER plug a while back. EWA...this one's for you! [Scene switches to (obviously an old crappy video) a shot of thousands of children joining hands to spell out "THANKS". Right before we see the children forming the word "K-MART", the scene switches back to SEWER Arena.] Dollar: All you millions of SEWER fans out there, check out our line-up for TONIGHT! [Flashing on the screen...] ________________________________________________________________________________ :/:/:M A I N E V E N T:\:\: <><><>FIRE IN THE STREET MATCH!<><><> (Street will be set ablaze for grapplers to battle in!) MASKED MAULER vs. JUST ANOTHER MUSLIM /*/*/*/CAT-THROAT MATCH!\*\*\*\ (Must produce the throat of a cat, and show it to the ref to win) "BLACKBALLS" RYAN vs. SKITTLES THE GIRL [[[TENNESSEE SHACK MATCH]]] [Fought near a shack resembling a shack from Tennessee] PERRY "BADDER THAN ALL" CARTON vs. DANIEL THE COCKER SPANIEL AN INTERVIEW WITH A FAN IN ATTENDANCE! PLUS MORE! ________________________________________________________________________________ Rake: Just a sec, Jase...You said millions of fans. Then why the [expletive] can't we draw more than about 30 to our shows. [Jason sticks fingers in his ears.] Dollar: I'm sorry, what? I had my fingers in my ears. Rake: I said, why can't we draw more than 30 fans to- [Dollar interrupts and starts coughing violently.] Dollar: I'm sorry, Color, I couldn't hear you over all the coughing. What were we talking about? Rake: I was about to sa- Dollar: That's great, Rake! But um...I think we have to get to one of our matches, right? [Just then a fan in attendance runs over to the announcer's table and holds a sign over Dollar's head that reads "SUCKS BALLS FOR MONEY". Laughter is heard throughout the yard, Color Rake most prominently. Alas, no SEWER Arena Security means no trouble for that fan...] Rake: How ya like them beans, Jase? [Dollar mumbles something under his breath. Just then, out comes Y Irvin, SEWER Head/Only Referee. He has the usual ref attire on, zebraishy. He is carrying a little black tape recorder. He makes his way down to the ring...] Jason: It does appear that it's time for our first match. I have no idea which one it is...our boss, Tim Gully, gives us no notes or anything. [Inside the ring, Irvin holds up the tape recorder and hits play. Out comes the voice of Tim Gully.] Gully's voice: Hello SEWER fans. This is your President, Tim Gully, here. Since we have no ring announcers signed to a SEWER contract, I must wheel (huh?) in. Introducing first, weighing in at 168 pounds...from Hostile Beach, Kansas...standing 5'8"...PERRY "BADDER THAN ALL" CARTON! [Camera turns to find Carton emerging from a car parked alongside the road. He is wearing a plain white t-shirt and a pair of tattered old khaki pants. His hair is sandy blonde, and he has a well-tanned, muscular body. There is one thing that seems to stick out about him, however. He has no left arm. He makes his way to the ring as many of the fans in attendance chastise him about the missing limb. He just shrugs it off...] Gully's voice: His opponent, hailing from the Santa Hitch, Rhode Island "Dawg Pound"...weighing in at 18 pounds, and not usually standing vertically... DANIEL THE COCKER SPANIEL! [Pretty big crowd pop as a small Cocker Spaniel is let out of it's cage near the Volvo 940 (announcer's booth). Daniel is white with brown spots, and is wearing a small green dog sweater with "DANNY THE MANNY" on it. He, apparently, has enough training to know how to race to the ring and prepare for his bout. Pretty impressive...] Dollar: We are seeing what I think to be an e-wrestling first...but I may be mistaken. A dog wrestling a one-armed man! Rake: I hope ol' Danny boy rips off Carton's other arm. *mumbling* It'd serve him right, pretty boy punk... Gully's Voice: This match is a Tennessee Shack Rules Match, meaning that this bout will be fought near a shack resembling a shack from Tennessee! Dollar: We have some queer stips here. [In the background two men are seen setting up a piece of white posterboard in a chair. The posterboard reads "SHACK FROM TENASEE".] Dollar: Well, apparently this is best we can do for a Tennessee shack...*sigh* Rake: Let's just get this match understay...er, underway. [[[CLANK CLANK]]] Dollar: Ah yes, the familiar sounds of pots and pans being beaten together. Gully really should invest his money into a bell instead of always blowing it on replica gumball machines... Rake: Yupper. Finally, the match has started! Our first cratch in SEWER HISTORY!!! Dollar: Well, Carton is eying Daniel up...Daniel is just kinda staring off into space. Carton has the slight height and weight advantage here. Carton charges in for a lock-up! Daniel sorta strays off to the side. Rake: Funny you say that, Jase...I bet Danny is a stray! Dollar: *groan* Anyway, Carton continues the chase. Finally he catches up with Daniel. He grabs his hind legs with that one arm. He's wheeling him around like a wheelbarrow!!! HOLY [EXPLETIVE]!!!!! Rake: Ah-oh...Danny's gettin' POed quicker than a 12 year-old doin' time! [Strange looks exchanged between Rake and Dollar.] Dollar: Carton wheels Danny over to the ropes. Daniel grabs the ropes with his teeth! He flips "Badder Than All" over the top ropes onto the ground, right on top of his head! [[[SNAP!!!]]] Dollar: Incredible display of strength by this Cocker Spaniel! He just flipped a man who almost weighs 10 times more than him over the top ropes using only his two hind legs! [Carton lies motionless on the outside of the ring...] Dollar: Well, it looks like Perry "Badder Than All" Carton may be dead. Rake: Get used to it, it said in our contracts that we'd probably end up getting killed, too. Don't worry about it...Gully has a bring em' back to life potion anyways. Dollar: I suppose you are right. Well, Y Irvin, our ref, is going out to check on Perry while Daniel The Cocker Spaniel just sort of does his own little strut around the ring. [Irvin starts to check for Carton's pulse. After a few seconds, he waves his hands in the air and claims Carton dead. No real reaction from the crowd.] Rake: Well, Carton's dead. I suppose this is what SEWER means when it says "unrealistic extremeness"...but what the troy is a "curate's egg"? [Dollar is too stunned by Carton's death to respond...] Rake: Lighten up, Jase, all it is is a man losing his life...sheesh. [Y Irvin drags Carton's body into the ring. He then goes and grabs Danny...then places him on top of Carton's limp body and holds him down with one hand while slapping the mat with the other...] 1 2 3! [[[CLANK CLANK CLANK]]] Rake: Geez, these refs don't choose favorites. [Y Irvin grabs the tape recorder from outside and hits play...] Gully's voice: The winner of this match...Hey Irvin, hold up the winner's hand or paw... [The ref grabs the paw of Daniel The Cocker Spaniel and raises it in the air. Then, for no apparent reason, he kicks Daniel in the side...causing Danny to, well, start talking like a human!] Daniel: Well hello Y...how are you? Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay bait. Gay ba- [Irvin kicks him again and the human voice is gone...The fans never even noticed this extraordinary event...The scene momentarily fades out...] [The scene fades in to a shot of Tim Gully standing in front of a SEWER logo on a plain white wall. Gully looks to be in his mid-20s...short, medium build... blond hair. He has on a blue Alaska t-shirt, as for pants...who knows? They can't be seen.] Gully: Hi SEWER fans. I am sorry to inform you that due to time constraints... the interview with a SEWER fan and the "cat-throat match" between "Blackballs" Ryan and Skittles The Girl has been canceled. Sorry you won't get to witness all the action, but I can just break a ton of kayfabe here and give you my bookings for the match anyway. I would have had Ryan win cuz girls are no match for guys...anyone knows that. Bye now... [Scene fades out, then back in to SEWER Arena at the Volvo 940.] Dollar: Well, there you have it from our esteemed Prez. Rake: Hey, why doesn't he ever come to any SEWER shows? Dollar: We're supposed to tell the fans that he has important SEWER executive business to tend to. But the real reason is that he just doesn't want to hang around trash like you, me, the wrestlers, and the fans. Rake: Oh, I see. Dollar: We are truly strapped for time right now...so we desperately need to get our very first SEWER Smokeout main event underway ASAP. Let's do that. [Y Irvin makes his way back down to the ring. He has that same ol' tape recorder. As he is getting in the ring, we see a sign in the background that reads "I'd Rather Be At Super Rare"...somewhere "Slick" Rick is smiling. Y Irvin hits play...] Gully's voice: SEWER fans...are you ready for the MAIN EVENT!!?? [Little crowd response.] Gully: Well then, let's do this thing! Introducing first, weighing in at 96 pounds, standing 4'11", from Rockfalls, Iowa, representing the Internet Wrestling League and the PCWF, one-half of the tag team known only as WU-JAM...JUST ANOTHER MUSLIM! [[[SMALL CROWD RESPONSE. Actually loud for a SEWER card.]]] [An actual grown man, even though he doesn't look a day over 12, emerges from the woods (???) northwest of the ring. He is dark-skinned and wears powder blue trunks (with a noticeably small bulge in them). About zero muscle-mass...how did this guy get into pro wrestling??? But, then again, this is SEWER, they let babies and animals wrestle here. He slaps hands with the fans, but one of them accidentally knocks him down...He gets back up and enters the ring.] Gully's voice: His opponent, standing 6'1", weighing 210 pounds...from parts unknown, not an actual place...we just don't know exactly where he is from because he refuses to tell. Anyway, here he is...THE MASKED MAULERRRRRRRRR!!!!! [[[BIGGEST CROWD POP OF THE NIGHT. Of course, still minuscule in comparison to any fed that can draw triple digits to their cards.]]] [The lights (we have lights?) dim briefly as "Bad To The Bone" by George Thurogood and the Destroyers begins to play out of well, nowhere. They come back up as the Masked Mauler walks to the ring with his arms raised, playing to the crowd. Entering the ring, he circles it once for all the crowd to see.] Rake: They love this guy! He's just a big ol' grizzly bear! Dollar: Of course, he's no Gunnar Gaines. Rake: Who? Anyway, since I am the "heel" commentator, I guess I better start belittling this guy. He sucks. He's stupid. He watches "Barney". He's a jerk... [Masked Mauler is decked out in an all-white costume with a few yellow highlights, not easily spotted, though. He even is wearing a white cape...just a real class-act all around!] Gully: This bout is a "Fire In The Street Match"...another SEWER first. This match will start in the ring, but the only place you can pin your opponent is in the blazing streets, which will be set on fire by some local Santa Hitch trouble-makers. [In the background, three small average-looking children are dousing the nearby street in gasoline. A match is lit...the streets are ablaze!] Gully: May the best man win! [[[CLANK CLANK]]] Dollar: Well, here we go. Mauler and JAM meet in the center of the ring and shake hands. I sometimes don't understand why Tim Gully makes two nice guys put their lives on the line... Rake: He's a messed-up guy...plus, everyone competing here has a wife and kids to look out for. Dollar: How you figure? I mean, we have little girls, dogs, and look at the size of JAM's crotch? Who'd want any of that? Rake: I was just reading the script. Dollar: So was I. Rake: OK. Dollar: Alright, the two men lock up. Mauler overpowers JAM and whips him into the ropes, then charges at him...BIG LARIAT BY THE MASKED MAN! Rake: Geez, that nearly took JAM's head off! Dollar: Mauler wastes no time and throws JAM through the middle ropes. The blazing streets are not far now! Mauler joins JAM outside the ring. He picks him up and bodyslams him on the ground! Rake: A bodyslam on grass probably feels better than one in the ring at least. Dollar: True. A standing dropkick from Mauler...he's dominating JAM in the early-going! He picks JAM up. Oh my gosh...he Irish-whips him into the FIRE!!! Rake and Dollar (simultaneously): HOLY [EXPLETIVE]!!!!! [JAM quickly breaks through the blaze and ends up on part of the road not burning. JAM staggers back through the flames again! He is back in the yard now, near Mauler, about to collapse! Mauler charges at JAM...who somehow has the initiative to put his head down...he backdrops Mauler!!! Mauler lands near the road, the flames almost licking him!] Dollar: What a comeback by JAM! Rake: I didn't think the little guy had it in him! [Suddenly a man dressed all in black emerges from the woods (is there a dressing room back there or something?) and is carrying a sign that reads "MASKED MAULER'S NUMBER ONE FAN". He is silent. He sits down in one of the folding chairs at ringside and watches...] Dollar: Is that "The Man In Black"? Rake: I believe so... Dollar: This couldn't be good news for JAM. [JAM hobbles over to the fallen Mauler and grabs Mauler's mask. He attempts to pull it off, but Mauler shoves him away.] Rake: It's not everyday that a masked man gives up his mask...if it was, we wouldn't have nearly as many masked men. I think that made sense... [Mauler begins pleading with JAM on his hands and knees not to remove his mask. JAM pulls away for a split second, and when he does...] [[[BOING!!!]]] [A shot to the old family jewels by Mauler! The crowd laughs as JAM staggers backwards...] Dollar: Well, maybe Mauler isn't all that nice after all... [Mauler gets up and DDTs JAM!] Rake: Good move by the masked one. Say, do we have some sort of frozen yogurt stand in the trunk of this Volvo 940? [Dollar just ignores Rake and continues with his commentary...] Dollar: Mauler looking to put JAM away here. He picks him up and places him over his shoulder, head first. He is taking him over to the crowd! Rake: Wha??? Dollar: He carries JAM over to the Man In Black...Referee Y Irvin cautions the Man In Black not to touch JAM or risk a DQ. But then Mauler informs the ref that there are no DQs in SEWER. [Mauler proceeds to slap the ref!] Rake: I'm really starting to like this guy! Kinda grows on you like Dentyne. Dollar: The Man In Black pulls out...what is that? A black magic marker and starts writing on JAM's face! [The camera does a close-up of JAM's face...MIB has written "ASTHMATIC 4 LIFE" on it...(what the #$%$^?)] Rake: What the [EXPLETIVE]? [Mauler then pulls JAM, still draped on his shoulder, away and begins running with him toward the fire...POWERSLAM INTO THE FLAMES!!!] [[[POP!!!!!!!]]] Dollar: Sweet Moses!!! Mauler has JAM pinned in the flames! [Y Irvin, a little scared of getting into the blaze, falls to his knees about 15 feet away and starts to count...] 1 2 3!!! [[[CLANK CLANK CLANK]]] [Some fans in the crowd jump the shoestrings and run to the blaze, dumping buckets of water and using their shirts to quench the flames. Mauler jumps up, then realizes he is on fire, and quickly does the standard drop, stop, and roll technique to put out the flames.] Rake: Good Zachary! Get JAM some help! [Man In Black and Mauler disappear into the woods and JAM is being crowded around...] Dollar: Fans, we are just about out of time! [The scene begins to fade...] Dollar: We'll try to update JAM's condition soon...for Color Rake, I'm Jason Dollar...this has been SEWER Smokeout #1!
