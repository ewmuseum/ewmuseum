Suicidal E-Wrestling Excitement Rassociation [[[[[ ***** \ | / ()()() RRRRR [ * \ | / () R R [[[[[ *** \ | / ()() RRRRR [ * \ | / () R R [[[[[ ***** ==|== ()()() R R Suicidal E-Wrestling Excitement Rassociation "OPEN TO ALL!" ^ | | DATE #1 • 11/27/97 (November Twenty-Seventh, Nineteen Hundred Ninety-Seven) | ================================================================================ CWO, EWA, IIWF, IWL, MLWO...All the Others...Thanks for giving us something to not conform to! Everyone else who says "But there's a billion feds like yours, you won't last!", thanks for the motivation! ================================================================================ ~ * ~ * ~ SEWER IS OPEN FOR BIDNESS! ~ * ~ * ~ Yeah...we're officially open...You'd probably like to know a bit more about SEWER, so here's the FAQ... @@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@ [[[NOTE: SEWER Prezo, Tim Gully, was half-asleep/pepped-up on anabolic steriods (huh?) when he answered the questions for this FAQ, so bear with us...]]] Q: What is SEWER? A: It's...Suicidal...E-Wrestling...Excitement... Q: And the "R"? A: Oh yeah...Rassociation. Q: "Rassociation"? A: Yep. Q: What's SEWER all about? A: Pretty much sockin' it to the big name feds. Q: "Socking it to the big name feds"??? A: A year from now we may not be the best...heck, we may not even be around then, but at least we can say we did it OUR way. Q: What sets SEWER apart from the pack? A: SEWER is open to everyone. Men, women, children, and animals are all welcome to sign up! There are no divisions, so a man could end up wrestling a baby, an animal wrestling a woman, and so forth. We have a lot of our wrestlers killed in the ring. We do lots of inhumane stuff. We aren't a very contentional fed to say the least. Q: "Contentional"? Don't you mean conventional? A: No. Q: So, what cards are there? A: There's "SEWER Smokeout", always held on Thursdays at SEWER Arena, located in my grandmother's yard in Santa Hitch, Rhode Island. It's not really an arena, we just say that cuz we hope people will come. That's pretty much our biggest weekly card, seeing as how it's our ONLY weekly card. Q: PPVs? A: PPVs? Q: Pay-per-views! A: Oh...we don't have those cuz we don't have the money to. We might have a SuperCard here and there, though. You know, drop a few bricks in the freezer. Q: OK...What about your strange behavior? A: *silence* Q: Moving on...What titles are there to compete for? A: We don't really have titles. If I feel you're really good...I'll give you a polaroid of my lunch. Q: Wha? A: You heard me. Q: Who works for SEWER? A: Well sir, here's our staff... ~ President - Tim Gully (the baddest, awesomest man alive, the guy that is in charge!) ~ Face Commentator - Jason Dollar (chubby guy in his mid-30s, always wears a shirt with a demoralizing slogan on it. What's that you say? Yes, I know he is the face commentator...it's a mixed up, messed up world, son.) ~ Heel Commentator - Color Rake (also chubby, also in his mid-30s, his eyebrows are not slanted like your typical villain...sorry.) ~ Ring Announcer - A tape recorder with Tim Gully's voice introducing the competitors...(we're low-budget, OK!?) ~ Referee - Y Irvin (yep, his first name is Y. He's a bald, chubby man in his early 40s. *whispering* I hear he can be paid off...) That's basically it. Sometimes the local law enforcement officers will stop by our shows and serve as our security, or possibly arrest me. Q: Uh...you mentioned having wrestlers killed in the ring and men, women, babies, and animals wrestling. Is that legal? A: Yep. Even if they do die...I have potions that can bring them back to life. Q: Not even gonna touch that one. So...is SEWER a booked or simmed fed? A: I should probably say something cute or clever here, but I'll give you straight answers from here on out. We are a booked fed, meaning the emphasis is on roleplaying, strategies, but mostly PARTICIPATION. You put the effort in, you'll be successful. Q: How are the roleplays sent out? A: In a packet whenever we collect about 4 or 5. Oh yeah, all roleplays are to be sent to [detail withheld] . There is a couple of rules on roleplaying... 1. The 2+1 rule. That means that you can roleplay a maximum of *2* times per week of a scheduled match, with those 2 RPs pertaining to your upcoming match. There is also an option for a third roleplay called a "character-building" RP, which explores your character and basically builds the character. You must label that RP as "character-building", or else it will be assumed an RP dealing with the upcoming match. Character-building roleplays have no affect on the upcoming match. If no match is scheduled for your wrestler that week, you can still do the 1 CB RP. Thanks go out to Slicky Ricky, President of the Extreme Wrestling Association...cuz this rule is basically his own doing. 2. No real rule on swearing in RPs. I personally do not like it when people swear, but SEWER has that nasty ol' "FREEDOM OF SPEECH" rule in effect. Q: Any other rules we should know about? 1. No disqualifications or countouts in ALL matches. Pretty much anything goes! This rule will NEVER be overturned! 2. No tag teams or managers/valets/bodyguards, etc. allowed in SEWER. Sorry, but that's the rule! 3. Try not to start out-of-character fights. There is no reason in the world to start freakin' legit arguments over e-wrestling! Q: What is the rankings system? A: Well, there is no numbers ranking system, as in 1, 2, 3, and so on. However, there is 5 different categories, I suppose you could say rankings, that your wrestler could fall under. They are... ~ MOWSERS Category (the very best...usually only 2 or 3 will be here) ~ CHI Category (close to the best (only 3 or 4 most of the time) ~ POND Category (pretty good...5 or 6 located here) ~ SWANK Category (mediocre...the majority will be here) ~ BLANKET Category (crappy...hopefully not many will inhabit this one) Q: What about the roster size? A: No limit. Of course, after a certain number, we'll only except the better roleplayers. Q: Well, that just about covers it...How would one go about joining SEWER? A: Just request an application from [detail withheld] . We'll probably require a sample roleplay before you get in. Yes, to be as laidback as we are, we're picky ol' jerks... Q: Alrighty, thanks for your time, Gully. A: No prob. Now I must get back to listening to my MxPx CD. Q: Okely doke. A: See ya. @@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@@ - FIRST CARD COMING SOON! - SEWER Smokeout #1 will be coming your way soon, Tuesday, December 9 to be exact!
