The scene opens up inside of a nice, cozy looking house. In front of you is a big screen television which is currently showing "Rugrats." The camera man aims down for a second, showing you that you're behind a couch. Lying on the couch is a man that is laughing at "Rugrats." The man sits up to reach for a can of "Citra" that is sitting on the table across from him. As the man sits up, you can see that it is "Playboy" Aidan Madison. . . ]-
-[ . . . Aidan Madison has his short, blonde hair spiked up. He is wearing an Old Navy tech vest, along with cargo shorts with no socks or shoes. Aidan turns around and looks at the camera. His bright blue eyes seem to twinkle when the lights of the camera hit them. He sees that the camera is recording and puts a huge smile on his face, showing off a nice smile. He chuckles a little, then begins to talk . . . ]-
[Aidan Madison]: "Dang, I love these Rugrats. I mean, Tommy is so brave, Chucky is funny, Phil and Lil are funny, and, not to mention, Angelica is P.H.A.T. as in, pretty hot and tempting. You know, one of these days I'm going to meet that little gal. Heck, maybe she'll fall in love with my looks and ask me to marry her. Then the whole world would be jealous of me."
-[ . . . Aidan gets rid of his smile, looks serious, wipes some lit off his vest, and begins to talk again. . . ]- [AD]: "It is nice to be back in the spotlight again. I've taken a long time off, around five months to be exact, and now I'm back. I know a lot of people are wondering what I did in my time off. Well, really, I just sat at home and watched cartoons. Yeah, Yeah. . . I did train every now and then, but dang it,
I'm in love with Angelica and I _HAVE_ to see her constantly."
"AH! I'm doing it again, I have to stop talking about Angelica. . . Don't you agree? Anyways, let me get to the MWA. The MWA, in my opinion, is going to be the biggest challenge of my young career. I look up and down the roster and all
I see are big stars, big names, and vetrans. Seriously, though, I'm proud to be on the same roster with such notable people. . .and one of them is none other than Eric Forbes."
"Mr. Forbes, it seems to me that you and I are going to get to know each other quite well while we are in the MWA. I mean, we _DO_ already have a nice little match with each other. But, not just a match, a 'Battle of the Brains.' Sounds interesting, doesn't it? I guess the President of the MWA saw that we're both the smartest, most intelligent, brightest guys in the MWA and gave us this match. . . the title of the match fitting well."
"And to make things even more interesting, Eric, is that it is a ladder match! The first one out of the two of us to climb the ladder and grasp the dictionary is the winner. Yeah, sounds simple enough. . . but it is going to be a lot harder for you, Forbes, than it shall be for me. Eric, I'll go more in depth with this match at a later date. Thank you for your time."
-[ . . . Aidan turns back around and begins to watch the end of "Rugrats." Angelica comes into the scene and Aidan begins to hoot and holler, telling her to take it all off. The camera man shakes his head in embrassment and shuts the camera off. . . ]-
------------------------------------------------------------------ FORBES ------------------------------------------------------------------ [There is Upon inspection, the camera is shown inside a Lincoln Continental. Sitting in the drivers seat is Eric Forbes. He is wearing a white short sleeved plain shirt, and a pair of khaki pants. To his left in the passanger seat is
Eric's manager Chris Stevens. Eric is driving in a small town, outside of Miami
Florida. It is a nice warm day. There is a slight breeze, as outside of the car the tree brances move a bit.]
Chris Stevens:Just FOLLOW the directions. Haven't you ever been to Miami before? Eric Forbes: Shh, I need concentration. It's not as easy as it looks, and I'm trying as best as I can! I've only been there once, I took an airplane! Sue me!
Chris Stevens: OK, OK. What road are we on? Eric Forbes: Umm...Something Grove. (Glances down at a map spread across his lap.) I'm not exactly sure, I got a D+ in geography or something. I suppose if
I was superman I could use my extra ability of thinking to get us there?
Chris Stevens: Haa haa, that wasn't funny Eric. What will be really funny, is if you can make it to Miami by tonight!
Eric Forbes: It just takes a bit of time. Before you know it, you will be eating pop corn, chewing on taffy, yelling at the other wrestlers, and having a merry time. I know we'll enjoy it in the MWA. I hear they have some talent there. Even a Playboy character, he's my oponent.
Chris Stevens: Watch the ROAD! [Eric then makes a sharp turn into the other lane, almost running into a car ahead of him. As he passes into another lane he cuts off a different car, who honks their horn at Eric loudly.]
Eric Forbes: Don't even start, Chris. (pauses) You know, I've got about a million jokes that I could tell you about Playboy ads. Wanna hear one?
Chris Stevens: Actually, Eric, no I don't. Eric Forbes (angrily): You, you just ruin all the.... [Suddenly the car stops as it runs into a car, waiting for the red light to turn. The man begins yelling at Eric Forbes, who has been slammed into his steering wheel, because the air bag did not work properly.]
Eric Forbes: Whooo! That was fun! Can we do it again!? [A badly hurten Chris Stevens warily looks at Eric Forbes in bewildermant. He shakes his head and then begins speaking, while wipeing off the blood on his face.]
Chris Stevens: I cant believe you? You just got us in a car wreck! You want to do it again! Are you crazy?
Eric Forbes: Umm... I'm sorry. At least now we know what its going to feel like to be "Playboy" Aidian Madison. Ya know?
Chris Stevens: I cant believe you? You're cutting a promo right after a car wreck? Eric Forbes: Hey, thats a good idea! First, I want to ask a question to the MWA
Staff? Who exactly is this match for? I don't quite understand, I don't understand alot of things, but this one I really don't understand. I'm not a brainiac,"Playboy" most likely isnt, so who is? Is it a ploy to draw attention to the fans of the MWA, so that they might possibly watch this match? Is it a clever scheme to get me to go to back to college? Did my mother put you up to this? These are my questions, among with a few others; so MWA Administration if you ever have the time please answer them! Anywho, lets address my actual match. It consists of a dictionary, a ladder, and a porn star. I must say, I've been in a few strange matches, I've even considered strange if you can believe it, but this? An opponent, most likely working for Playboy, a dictionary, whatever you use that for? And a ladder, something only used in specialty matches. I only understand one thing, I'm SPECIAL! Besides that I haven't a clue. I really am, yes I am. I'm SPECIAL, SPECIAL!
Chris Stevens: Uhh.. Eric with the strength left in my body I'm going to kill you! Eric Forbes: Why, I didn't do anything wrong. But if you want I could call a towtruck. Would that be to your liking, or shouldn't I go to all the trouble?
[Chris Stevens glares at Eric. Eric Forbes grabs a cell phone out of his left pocket and opens it. He quickly dials a number, and waits for a few moments.]
Eric Forbes: Exscuse me sir? We had an accident on some street. Do you think you could help us, maybe fix my car? I'd really appreciate it. By the way, I'm special and my names Eric Forbes. Tell your boss that.
Eric Forbes: Yeah, Its umm... (after a very long while of staring at the map) Blairs Ferry Grove. And did you tell your boss about me being Eric Forbes, how
I'm special. I think he should know, also I'm wrestling "Playboy" Aidan
Madison. They even think I'm smart? Eric Forbes: What how dare you! Goodbye! (turning towards Chris Stevens) I can't believe those people.
Chris Stevens: Why, what happened? Eric Forbes: Can you believe this, they said they would have a tow truck out here in 15 minutes! I can't wait that long!
[There is a rambling response from Chris Stevens, and an unsmart response from
Eric Forbes that cannot be heard as the camera is slowly fading awy into the background. After a few moments there is nothing but complete darkness. The question left is, will Eric Forbes and Chris Stevens ever make it to Miami? And will Chris kill Eric before they get there?]
------------------------------------------------------------------ NOBLE ------------------------------------------------------------------ [BEEP BEEP BEEP** [The sound of a microwave is heard as the camera fades in, in a kitchen looking at the microwave. Then Ike
Noble slides across the hardware floor wearing his socks, black Nike shorts, and a tee shirt.He opens up the microwave, and takes out what is a hot dog. He plops it on a plate, and goes and sits down at a table, the TV is on
in the background, he takes a big bite, then looks up at the camera and talks.] "MWA. Whoop de doo. Wee, here I am feel free to abuse me, screw me, what ever,
I don't give a fuck. MWA is no different then any others, all it will do to me is screw me, make a mockery of my name. Here I am fighting against William
Oreck. Who is this man? Yet another to use outside interference to beat me? Is that what he is?"
[Sigh.] "I don't know about this fellow. Himself and I have never crossed pathes before. I know nothing of this man. If he fights me a good clean fight, he is sure to fall, no one has ever defeated me in a _CLEAN_ match, I doubt no one ever will. William Oreck, the name sounds clean, but sounds like he should rather be in a spelling bee then a wrestling match. That is OK, because no matter who he is, or who he thinks he is, if he wants to step into the squared circle with me, he can, I will gladly kick his butt."
[Ike bites some hot dog.] "I am ready to raise some crap up in MWA. I will without a doubt win the Down
South title sooner or later. I have all the qualities of a champion. I have never been fairly beaten... I am a screw job waiting to happen, not to mention
I am from Georgia. So you know how ever we are going to decide who is winning this title, guaruntee I will be there."
[Pause.] "There is what, four people in MWA? Well I don't know a damn one of them, and that really doesn't matter... because I know one thing, I am better then them all. Better by far."
[Bite.] "What ever MWA has to offer... I will over come... period." [Fade Out.]
