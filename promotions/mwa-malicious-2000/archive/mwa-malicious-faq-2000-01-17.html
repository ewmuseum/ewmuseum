Information on The MWA <//=------ a) Area of The MWA: The Malicious Wrestling Association, is located throughout the state of Georgia and Alabama, of course it being a indy promotion. It originally started throughout Florida, Georgia and Alabama, though with a limited amount of money
President Chris Williams, was forced to stick with his Georgia, now expanding into Alabama as well.
b) History: The Malicious Wrestling Association, first started two years ago, under the name; Extreme Wrestling Federation ran by two men, Sea Scorpion and Stingray
Stormer, though the action was stale and they soon went bankrupt, though Chris
Williams bought the federation, and after months of planning it has opened in
November of '99, and still going strong through the new millennium!
c) Miscellaneous: MWA, is a NET fed, still becoming one, it originally started as one, though I for some reason over recruited AOL RPers and they dominated the federation, though very soon I will be changing it to all NET styles.
I decide sometimes to hold a SuperCard every other month, when I feel it is right to push angles and such, it is entitled, Clash of The Titans, it is sort of like a big-time houseshow.
Every other week I have a newsletter come out, though once a month, I have the best wrestler who deserves it the most become Wrestler of The Month, usually if you receive this spot you get a big push for quite some time, received only to the most dedicated, most talent and BEST GIMMICK! :)
------=//> 2. Official Rules <//=------ a) The Rules: 1. No Tossing: What this basically means is no Tossing, if caught, you will be fired immediately. Though the MWA is BCCed it will be very hard to TOS. 2. Each handler, may only be allowed one character, I don't like somebody handling two people simply because quality dissolves, and there is a bigger work load, only will I allow someone having two characters if part of an angle.
3. Original characters only, what this means is I do not want any real life characters (i.e. WWF's, Mankind) and such, they must be created solely by
_YOU_ and I do not want to see the implying a real life charactor (for example,
Triple X)
4. The MWA is a booked federation, RPs are decided by quality, not breaking kayfabe, the quality of strategies and angle involvement. 5. Please keep swearing to a minimum, if there is the more major curses in your RP, you know what I am talking about, the flash may possibly not be sent. 6. There is a maximum of three flashes per day, though I doubt there will be any spammers an the federation, so this will not be a major issue. 7. Please, I don't want to know how immature you are, when you whine about a loss. You can go cry me a river for all I care, it is not going to do you any good. 8. No maiming of people in your RPs, other handlers charactors are allowed in your RPs /if/ you have the transcript of his permission of using his charactor, though it is almost a must to keep him in-charactor.
9. Have FUN b) Style of RPing: I accept only NET RPers starting sometime in late January of 2000, which means no color, macros, pitchures, etc. You get the pitchure, right?
c) Strategies: Strategies are a major thing here in the MWA, they can be the deciding factor in a very close match, AND they improve the quality of your match greatly, and smiled upon by the Prez, me. :-)
------=//> 3. Weekly Event <//=------ a) MWA Friday Escapade: Men are like stars: some generate their own light while others reflect the brilliance the receive, guess which kinda of men are on this card. :)
[Editors Note: All tag teams and stables formed require a storyline and a plot, I do not just allow them to pop out of nowhere, I also require a in-ring, just one, from both parties, I will send both to how every many members there are, they will get together, and manipulate it into one.]
b) Pay-Per-Views: There is one Pay-Per-View a month, sometimes one every two since the MWA is a indy federation, and cannot afford all the extra equipment put up for
Pay-Per-View extravaganzas, Pay-Per-Views do not have traditional names which are an institution, which means the same name, staying the same name that month(s) every year, it will almost always change, unless I decide it needs to be a tradition.
c) Houseshows: Houseshows are never set on a date, if I feel there should be an angle or character put over or something or if there is a massive recruitment of rookies, I slap-together a house show to see where they stand.
