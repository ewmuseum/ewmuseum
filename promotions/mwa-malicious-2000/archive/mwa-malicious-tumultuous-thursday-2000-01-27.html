Tumultuous Thursday

27 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene fades in, a spectacular display of fireworks ignites from the entrance ramp, very dainty for a smalltime federation, a small arena, jammed pack with roaring fans, signs dangle from balconies, and occupy whole rows.
[Camera zooms in on Stephen Erickson and Marcus Favre, both men, wearing head-sets and black MWA Promotional shirts, Erickson's eyes glimmer staring at the large crowd on hand, Favre grinning.]
Favre: "Whoo-boy, I love announcing, it's almost better than sex, but of course feeble-minded friend, nothin' is as good as that."
Erickson: "Of course not." [Scene fades in on the back locker room, a man clad in a black tuxedo, black pants to match, and black suede shoes, the camera zooms in closer, revealing him to be 'Corporate' Christopher Williams.]
Williams: "Welcome all, to the Malicious Wrestling Association, here live from Atlanta, Georgia on this beautiful Thursday evening. I just wanted to thank all of you for joining us tonight, and our friends watchin' on ESPN, it truly is a great joy, to know all our hard-work and dedication truly pays off."
[A middle-aged woman walks into the room, Williams seems a bit started.] Williams: [Stuttering:] "Uhh ... umm... hi, Pamela, how was your day?" [In an angry tirade.] Pamela: "What? YOU CAN'T CALL ME DEAR? OR HONEY? HUH? WHAT DO YOU MEAN, 'HOW WAS MY DAY'? WERE YOU HOPING FOR ME TO HAVE A BAD DAY? HUH? HUH? _HUH!_?" [Williams adjusts his collar, beads of sweat droplets roll down his cheek as the scene fades out.]
[Commercial Break.] ------------------------------------------- Dark Match: Crimson versus Flyin' Brian Blaze ------------------------------------------- Both men came out to no pop at all, the two began brawling, Blaze executed and missed many high-flying moves, Crimson countered many of those moves, the match went back and forth, both men making rookie mistakes but in the end... Blaze pulled the victory.
[Commercial break.] Favre: "And, now a bit earlier today, with Eric Forbes, proving to the world he's not a ballerina, he just had a few lessons, HA!"
[Even, Erickson finds a chuckle out of Favres comment.] ------------------------------------------- Promotianal Video Eric Forbes with Christopher Stevens ------------------------------------------- - [There is a slight increase in clarity, as the scenery fades into view. The air is crisp, as many producers and anchors are walking around the area. Waiting in the Channel 9 Waiting Room is of course Eric Forbes and Chris Steven's, Eric's Manager. Both are sitting in their chairs, glancing at magazines, sat on the table in front of them. Eric has his legs crossed as a girl for some reason, as Chris is talking to him.] -
Chris Steven's: Eric stop crossing your legs! We're in public now. Don't you understand? Eric Forbes: If Madison can take a shower on live television, and people can call me a ballerina, I can cross my legs! If a dictionary can be hung over a wrestling ring, and a porn star can try to grab it, then I can cross my legs.
If angelica can be considered more beautiful than me... Chris Stevens: OK, OK, I get the point. Just keep your legs the way they are. Anyway, can we go? I don't think they are going to let you talk to the producers. It's not that big of a deal anyway.
Eric Forbes: Being a ballerina is a big deal. It's like having a rubber duck and being in love with Madison! Don't you understand? Anywho, we need to get this fixed, I am a wrestler. I may have taken a few ballerina lessons as a little boy, but hey!
- [Eric Forbes gets up, dropping his magazine and walks to the front counter, a few feet away from where he was originally sitting. Above the counter on the wall are 3 television screens, all showing Channel 9 news from different views.
Standing behind the counter is a rather stocky woman, wearing a nice gray black suit.] - Channel 9 Lady: Mr. didn't I tell you earlier that we would contact the producers as soon as we have time. Now, if you would please sit down and watch the show I'm sure...
Eric Forbes: No one understands! I don't need to be special to get in here, and
I don't need a rubber ducky! Just let me talk to the producers, that's all I want.
Channel 9 Lady: Sorry sir, I can't let you... - [Eric Forbes ignores the lady and walks over to the door that says "Producers Only" to the left of the counter, quickly followed by Chris Stevens. Eric pushes the door open, to find a smoke filled room, occupied by overweight people in suits and ties. He goes up to the first one he can grab a hold of, and gets his attention.] -
Eric Forbes: Excuse me, could you tell me who I would speak to, to talk about this mornings broadcast?
Producer: Why me of course; did you enjoy it? I'm sure you did, there was this guy on there that we showed as part of our new idea, to be more creative. We had a whole episode about this wrestler who we called a ballerina, everyone got a big laugh about it. So, what do you want?
Eric Forbes: Big laugh! Haa haa! Guess what? Producer: Haa ha, what? Eric Forbes: It was me! Producer: I umm... Eric Forbes: I won't stand this mockery, I'm putting a retraction in your news! I deserve one too. Plus I'm special! - [Eric pushes the producer, who stumbles back and falls onto another man sitting in one of the chairs. They both begin arguing as Eric passes through another door which says "On Air." Eric moves onto the scene. Where a woman and a man are sitting, who seem to be discussing some major event in the news. They both turn at the same to see Eric walking over right next to them, and pulling up a chair. Stunned, they just look at Eric who begins talking.] -
Eric Forbes: Nice to be here today guys, thanks for having me as your guest. I know you've always wanted me on your show, and I finally decided to come on. I guess it's luck you could call it, or maybe something else. Say, a ballerina!
Yeah that's right! I'm the ballerina, I'm the wrestler that you guys made fun of this morning. Everyone heard about it, and I've got to tell you it really hurt my feelings. I almost cried, all because of you. And then I scared my pal
Steve... Am I rambling on? Well, lets get to the subject.
I am not, a ballerina, I'm special! Lets address Aidan Madison, the Playboy guy. Sorry children at home watching. Anywho, I have a bone to pick with the
MWA Administration! Why did Madison get a hotel with a hole in his bedroom floor, a shower with eight showers, and even pet rats! I didn't get any of that, in fact all I got was a television and a 7 11 room. No holes, rats, or nothing! It's just not fair, it's prejudiced!
Now, now, I know I'm scaring people with my scary voice, so lets tone things down a bit. Madison, you disrespected me to the final end. Or maybe not that far, but a little bit. You said I wouldn't look good in a ballerina suit, and that I wouldn't be able to touch the ladder! I really took offense to the ballerina comment, you know. That time I actually did cry.
Madison I'm special, you're not, I'm special, you're not, I know what a dictionary is, you don't, I like pizza, you do! Don't you see, I've got everything on my side. I can't lose! I'm going to win the Battle of the Brains match!
Male Anchor: Umm. Mister, I think we need to take a break... - [Then suddenly the screen is crowded with producers seen from the other room, as the camera begins to fade, and a lot of arguing is heard. The song "Good Ol' Days" begins to play, as the camera turns back towards the MWA Arena.] - ------------------------------------------- "Straight Shoota" Ike Noble versus William Oreck ------------------------------------------- [Entrances inserted up here.] Stephen: "This should be quite a match-up, lets just hope, Noble doesn't get a screw-job." Favre: "Dude, you sicko!" Stephen: "Leave, it to Marcus to twist things around in that perverted mind of his." *DING*DING*DING* [The two lockup, Oreck being the much larger of the two takes control, applying a side-head lock.]
Stephen: "Ouch, Oreck really putting some fortitude into the side-head lock." Favre: "For a defensive guy, he's pretty offensive." Stephen: "May be for his size, what he's doin' now is considered barely offensive?" Favre: "Possibly." Stephen: "And this crowd, chanting for the Georgia native." [Oreck releases the hold startled at the fans chants. [Ike capitalizes executing a devastating spinning heel kick.] Stephen: "Wowzer, Oreck doesn't even flinch." Favre: "I guess, Orecks defense is comin' into play." Stephen: "Ike, is going to really have to utilize his speed if he wants to overcome this massive Goliath."
[Ike, seeing Oreck has turned around, backs away, he comes back with a flying forearm. [Oreck stumbles backwards, Ike begins taking shots at Orecks legs.] Favre: "Smart strategy by Ike Noble, but I STILL want Oreck to win." [Oreck on one knee now, Ike wraps his legs around Orecks massive neck. [Oreck counters with a one-knee'd powerbomb, he holds the position.] 1!!!!! 2!!!!! 3... KICK-OUT! Stephen: "Oh so close." [Oreck lifts Ike to his feet, and oddly it is almost as if Oreck does not know to do with the sprawled Ike Noble.]
Favre: "Do something, smarty." [Almost as if Oreck heard: he lifts Ike to his feet scoop slamming him back onto the mat.] Stephen: "Look at William haul! Shish, he hit Ike with a big elbow drop, but did you see his speed? Stunning."
Favre: "Yeah, probably came from those football days." [Oreck tries another basic scoop slam, easily Ike slides out, turns Oreck around, and executes a head scissors takedown.]
Favre: "Nice, counter." Stephen: "Now, Ike sitting on the top rope, Oreck quickly back up. But only to receive a missile drop kick to his bad right knee, I guess Noble figured it out."
[Ike drags Oreck to the center of the ring, preparing for a one-legged Boston crab, but Oreck gives him a good right hook to his jaw.]
Favre: "Did you see that? The guy made no attempt to block? How queer." [Oreck staggers to his feet limping towards Ike he pushes him into the corner, wailing his fist at Ike.
[Oreck lands every punch, Ike not even making an attempt to avoid those wailing fist.] Favre: "Well, not even trying to avoid those clumsy punches isn't going to help him a his endeavor."
Stephen: "Oreck tries to toss Ike across the ring, Ike seeing it coming with a last ounce of energy left in that beaten' body executes a facebuster, and both men are down."
[Archibull begins to count;] Archibull: "1 ... 2 ... 3 ... 4 ... 5 ... 6 ...." [Oreck is back on his feet, he whips Ike into the ropes.] Stephen: "For once Ike is able to avoid that clothesline." [Ike stops halfway, and gives a kick to Orecks abdomen, another, and another, and a jumping, roundhouse kick planting it on Orecks jaw.
[FAN POP!] Stephen: "Oreck stumbling backwards, and Ike clotheslining Oreck over the top-rope, and for once he gets this big man off his feet, and into no-mans territory."
Favre: "Ya know, usually Noble is a really funlovin' guy, but he has really taken' a disliking to William Oreck, believing he is not worthy of being a TRUE professional wrestler."
[Ike dashes across the ring, springboards of the top turnbuckle, and soars through the air, hitting Oreck with a cross body block.
[Both men are out, the referee counting, Ike clutching his knee in pain.] Stephen: "I think it's safe to say, Ike mighta hurt his knee." [Oreck still out, forgetting about reentering the ring, grabs a chair, after both men are counted out slams the chair across Ikes forehead.]
Stephen: "What he do that for?" Favre: "May be, he's tryin' to be the stank outta this trailer park dweller?" [Ike staggers the other direction, tossing anything he can grip, throwing them at Oreck, trying to get away.
[He throws a microphone at Oreck, while blocking the flying object, Ike takes the moment to sling his body, tackling Oreck, the two begin to brawl.]
Stephen: "Some heat between these, two." [Referees after a few minutes of brawling, break up the two.] Announcer: "As a result of a double count out, this match results an a draw!" [The fans begin to boo like maniacs.] [Commercial break.] [Scene fades back in, zooming in across this small, quaint arena. [After a moment or so of exploiting the fans up-close, it pans in on Favre and
Erickson.] Stephen: "Controversial ending to a controversial match, Oreck seems to have taken this 'quote master' Ike Noble's words offensively, and well, attacked him with a chair."
Favre: "Nicely put, and I always thought: wrestlers had no brains?" Stephen: "Well, Forbes and Madison already proved it." *KABOOM*KABOOM*KABOOM*KABOOM*!!!! [Loud explosions fill the arena, at an earth-shattering sound. Followed by a golden midst forms around the arena.
[An the background: "A Certain Shade of Green" song plays, lyrics written by
Incubus as a man, a bit larger then most men, his body divinely craft emerges from the back, his golden hair braided back, a much older man, trails behind him, around his mid-sixties, the younger man, begins taunting the crowd, and flaunting the woman, he continues down the aisle, the camera zooms in on him, a sorrid smile accompanies that young body, revealing him to be ...
[KERRY POWELL!!!! [He hops onto the top rope, slides easily into the ring, the older man, now obviously Rufas, stepping through as well, having more trouble.]
Erickson: "IT CAN'T BE!?! CAN IT?!?" Farve: "IT IS!! IT IS!! IT'S KERRY POWELL!!!!" [Powell slowly struts to the far side of ring, waving his hand for a mic. One is quickly tossed in and Powell just as quickly, hands it to Rufas to hold for him.]
Powell: "Twenty.... Four..... Karat...... [Pause with a huge pop.] That's right folks! 24K has come to the M..W..A!!!! [Longer pause.] You may have heard it from you buddies, or maybe on the internest... So all the wrestlers in the back might have had some time to prepare... But _nothing_.. And I mean NOTHING.. Can stop this Goldrush...
People just don't seem to get it.. They think.. 'Yea, I can beat Kerry Powell, he ain't nothin!'.. Oh boy.. Do I got news for you! You may have heard 'He's just a pretty-boy, the only reason he win is his money, the only reason he's in the business is for the attension'.. Again.. _Wrong!_.. I have more talent in my little finger than you have in your entire bodies! I've been through shoot-fighting in Japan.. I've beaten the best UWC has to offer.. And I _will_ beat the 'best' here in MWA..
I've seen the likes of people like 'Playboy' Aidan Madison... Eric Forbes... and [shudder] William Oreck.. Oreck, you think you have _half_ the money I do.. Personally, I don't know _anyone_ who owns an Oreck vacuum and, trsut me.. I asked around.. Now, while I don't wanna keep all you good people from your
'wonderful' wrestling show, I do want to make it better.. Do you know how I'll do it? Hmm..."
[Some fans in crowd begin to yell things.] Powell: "You there, yea you.. [Points to an overweight woman.] What did you say? Did I hear you right when you said 'Make it better by my rocking my world!'..."
[The woman stands up thinking Powell will go for it, she must have no idea who he is because she should know, and blows him a kiss.]
Powell: "Heh.. Ha.. Haha... HAHAHAHA!!.. Sorry... I _had_ to laugh!! It would be phyisically impossible for me to 'rock' your world.. Hell, I doubt I could
_move_ you.. not to mention 'your world'!!
But, I swear.. WIth my talent on board MWA... The ratings have nowhere to go... but up......"
["A Certain Shade of Green" by Incubus plays over the speakers as the green and gold lights begin to shine. Powell stands on the top rope with his arms in the air for a few seconds, almost absorbing the crowds boos.. He smiles, jumps down and walks out of the ring, to the back.]
[Commercial Break.] ------------------------------------------- Ladder Match Eric Forbes versus. 'Playboy' Aidan Madison ------------------------------------------- Announcer: "Coming to the at this point an time, standing 5'8", and weighing 130 pounds... "PLAYBOY" AIDAN MADISON!!!"
[ The lights in the arena go out, and blue strobe lights begin to flash on and off. MXPX's "The Downfall of Western Civilization" comes blasting over the loud speakers. The crowd erupts in _MASSIVE_ cheer as "Playboy" Aidan Madison walks out from behind the curtain to a shower of fireworks.]
Announcer: "Now making his way to the ring at this time, standing 5'11.5," 185 pounds... ERIC FORBES!" [A huge pop from the crowd is heard, but is mixed with cheers and boos. After a few short moments, "Good Ol' Days" begins to play as Eric Forbes enters the ramp. He spins around so everyone can see his beauty, before walking down to the ring. He is not accompanied by his manager. Once Eric enters the ring he gets on his knees and raises his arms. He then gets up and starts bouncing back and forth off the ring ropes. After a few moments, he stops and notices the dictionary, above the ladder. Madison's music begins to play.]
Stephen: "Barrage of punches, Madison capitalizing on the midsection." Favre: "Madison Gouge to the eyes; Forbes stumbling back. Running DDT! The
Canadian, porn star controlling."
Stephen: "Nicely put... Forbes thrown into the ropes, coming back, clothesline! Forbes is on the ground, trying to get up. Madison stomping away on the back of Forbes... Obviously the inexperience setting in."
Favre: "Or perhaps Madison is just the superior athlete. You think?" [Forbes grabs the leg of Madison, twisting it. Madison falls to the ground, as
Forbes climbs with the rope back to his feet.]
Stephen: "Perhaps you spoke to soon Marcus." [Forbes grabs Madison's arm by the wrist and lifts it so that Madison is behind him and moves his arm over Madison's shoulder. Forbes pulls down on Madison's arm in an attempt to hyperextend or break Madison's elbow.]
Favre: "Intelligent, maybe this is why they are in the 'Battle of the Brains' match? I after all am smarter than the both of them, but Forbes is showing some innovation. Madison should pull off the victory though..."
Stephen: "Forbes is moving over towards the untouched ladder, he begins to climb... 1 step, 2 step, 2 steps down!! Madison knocks him back down to the ground. Madison starts to climb, knocked off!"
Favre: "Gee, maybe I was wrong. They aren't very intelligent. What ingenious moves." [Elbow drop is tried by Forbes, but Madison moves out of the way and follows up with his own elbow drop.]
Stephen: "Madison obviously the more experienced wrestler here. It's the rookie mistakes that might catch Forbes off his feet. He is doing very well though, he might have a chance at that dictionary."
Favre: "Sure, have you been sleeping? I know this match isn't the most interesting, but if you've watched any of it you would have had to see Madison pummeling Forbes."
[Madison, grabs the ladder, which was in his reach, he spins around hitting
Forbes in the back of the head, blood splatters everywhere, Madison, begins jumping up and down his hands raised in the air, the fans begin cheering.
[Forbes getting to his feet, not aware that Madison is behind him. Madison then executes a perfect bull dog, putting Forbes back on the mat. Madison then begins to punch Forbes, before starting to climb up the ladder, for the dictionary.]
Stephen: "Madison going for the dictionary again ... he's almost there! Two more rungs to go..." Favre: "NO! Forbes knocked the ladder down, putting Madison outside of the ring. So close..."
Stephen: "Very, very close. Madison almost one the match. It looks as if Forbes is going to go after Madison outside of the ring." Favre: "Huh? I thought this was the battle of the brains? He should go for the dictionary!"
[Forbes slides out of the ring, and moves towards Madison. He then brings
Madison up to his feet, and slams his head against the steel steps. Forbes turns around, and begins to blow kisses to the crowd, obviously proud of himself. When Forbes turns around he is caught by a Spinning Heel Kick though!
Madison falls to the ground, along with Forbes, using up all his strength. They both lay there for a few moments before both begin crawling in different directions.]
Favre: "Snails going in different directions." Stephen: "Both headed towards the dictionary though." [After a few moments of Forbes and Madison moving to the other sides of the ring, outside, they finally stand up. They both begin to crawl in the ring at the same time.]
Stephen: "Who's going to make it first?" Favre: "Madison, c'mon, c'mon..." [The two continue to, both climbing the ladder, rung by rung, they slowly, but inevitably reach the top, they both grab the dictionary, and begin fighting for it, pulling it to and fro, the spine suddenly begins to tear, and the book rips in half, both half one side.]
Favre: "How are they going to decide the match? Madison clearly has the large portion." Stephen: "Look at them catch some air-time!" [Both men, with the strength they were pulling the very thick dictionary, fall about ten feet away from the ladder, Forbes landing through the table, Madison, rough 50 pounds lighter, goes a bit further, landing into the crowd.]
Announcer: "By decision of the front office, this match has ended an- a draw!" [Crowd boos.] Stephen: "Odd ending, to an odd match, folks we have to go, thanks for joining us!" [Scene fades.]
