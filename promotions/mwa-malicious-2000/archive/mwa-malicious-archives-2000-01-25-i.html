Ike is sitting on a chair staring into space. He is wearing a plain black shirt, and black Adidas wind pants. Not looking at the camera, he lets out a sigh and speaks.]
[IKE:] "William Oreck... the exact opposite of I. He is a president owner of a major Corporation. He is rich. Yet what am I? Wrestling is my life, yet I am used and abused like trash. I take everything that goes on in the wrestling ring to me to the heart. What can it mean to do? You have other priorities. My family doesn't own shit.
I work hard for the money. People that are like you?" [Shakes Head.] [IKE:] "Stuck up snobs. I am not a pretty boy. I work as hard as I can for one thing ... wrestling. Now look what wrestling has done for me? It has scared my soul, spit on my pride. Yet it is my only priority ... the only thing I can do.
The only thing I am good at. VERY good at. I don't let people like you beat me. You don't deserve it. Go sell your Vacuum cleaners, or whatever. Stay out of _MY_ business. The business of a real wrestler. Or as I like to call my self a warrior. Oreck think it matters if you wife is hot?"
[Smirk.] [IKE:] "No that doesn't help you at all. Maybe you will have a good night with her after I kick your butt. That going to help you? No. The only thing that would help you, is if you wouldn't show up. Your lame rich a[BEEP] is going to have a boot up it, once you leave our match."
[Ike sighs.] [IKE:] "I hope your commercials are good because god knows you can't wrestle for s[BEEP]." [Fade Out.] ------------------------------------------------------------------ FORBES ------------------------------------------------------------------ [There is a gradual increase in visibility as the sequence begins. A Lincoln
Continental is just pulling into the 7 11 Hotel Parking lot, in Miami Florida.
After a few moments, the vehicle is put in park in one of the spaces. The driver's door, and passenger door slowly opens, as Eric Forbes and Chris
Stevens step out. Eric walks towards the trunk of the vehicle, takes his key and opens it up. He then grabs two suitcases, and hands one to Chris. Eric is wearing the same clothes as earlier, a white short sleeved plain shirt, and a pair of dockers khaki pants. Now in the sunny day he also wears a pair of silver sunglasses. Chris on the other hand is wearing a nice black suit.]
Chris Stevens: We drove by the MWA arena before we got here, do you remember where it is? Eric Forbes: Of course I do, I'm the one who found it. Remember, we got the
Lincoln fixed and I was speeding and they pulled us over right next to it? That was cool...
Chris Stevens: Childish. Actually we don't have time. We're going to check in, and then you've got some training to do before Thursday. It's in your schedule.
Eric Forbes: Why look at it if I have you? Can we at least watch a little television in the hotel guest lounge? You know, after we check in. I love TV.
Chris Stevens: No. [Eric then reaches the door, and opens it. He walks inside, as Chris follows him. To the right of the camera is a check in desk, with many awards on the wall. A beautiful brunet lady, stands behind the counter. To the left is a few chairs, and a painting on the wall. The wall is painted a shade of white, and the carpet is a beautiful maroon. Eric and Chris walk up to the counter.]
Eric Forbes: Watch this, Chris. [Eric then begins to repeatedly hit the bell, which is usually used for signaling an employee. After about 6 times, the lady behind the desk stops him, by putting her hand over the bell.]
Eric Forbes: Sorry. Check In Employee: Excessive use is not needed. How may I help you? Chris Stevens: We'd like a room for a week. We will be here until Friday of next week. We are members of the Malicious Wrestling Association. We need a place to stay before while we find a new place to live.
Check In Employee: Well, the 7 11 is a civilized place. We don't have room for
(rudely) wrestlers.
Eric Forbes: Wha..what? The 7 11 is a piece of trash. Are you telling me that... Chris Stevens: He didn't mean anything by that. (Elbows Eric in the shoulder) You must of misheard us. We work for the ballet, I am my friends manager. Were performing for the uhh...umm, Malicious Wartime play. Its a play about World
War 2.
Check in Employee: My mistake, I thought I heard you say that you were a wrestler. We surely have a room for you two. Let me check in the book for available rooms.
[The lady walks over to a shelf where she takes out a black book. She begins leafing through it, as Eric and Chris begin to argue about what Chris just said. After a few moments, the lady smiles and looks back up. She grabs a key off the wall and moves back towards the counter.]
Check in Employee: Here you are gentleman. 2nd floor, 3rd room down. You wont miss it, we only have 20 rooms up stairs. I hope you enjoy your stay here...
[Eric and Chris both turn, Chris holding the key. They begin to walk down the hallway while talking.]
Eric Forbes: I can't believe you told her I was a ballerina. We could stay somewhere else, c'mon. We can't let people think I'm a ballerina! I have a little pride, if you don't know.
Chris Stevens: Yeah, yeah I know. Just settle down Eric, we need a place to stay. This place is as good as any. And besides, no one is going to know that you posed as a ballerina.
Eric Forbes: You better be right. And, what if that guy Madison finds out. He'll be all over me more than Angelica, that TV character. You know what, I actually do kind of like Angelica, but were just friends. He's got a major thing for her, what if he finds out that I pretended I was a ballerina? Do you know what Will happen?
Chris Stevens: Eric, what did I tell you? No one is going to find out, and you need to get over those TV cartoons. You're a grown man, and I don't want you acting like Madison. He's borderline insane, I don't want you becoming him.
Eric Forbes: Speaking of Madison, do you think I could shoot a promo now? (Speaking nonsense) OK, thanks. I appreciate it, yeah yeah, yep, I see what you're saying. Uh-huh, well maybe. Yep. Lemme get to work OK?
Chris Stevens: Just do it! Eric Forbes: OK, OK, you don't need to get so negative... Madison, even though
Chris made a mean comment about you, I think your right about something. The
MWA must think we're the most intelligent, smartest, genius people in the MWA.
After all look how smartest isn't a word. Anywho, 800 pages. That's an awful lot, and I have a concern. Is either one of us going to be able to grab an 800 page dictionary? This thing is huge, I've already seen it. It's like 20 by 20 feet.
Chris Stevens: (coughing) exaggeration (coughing) Eric Forbes: Shut up! Maybe it's not that big, but its big! Now, where was I? ...Dictionary brain match. I'm a tyrant, you know it, I know it. A tyrant, gee lemme think what that means. (grabs a dictionary out of his bag he is carrying)
I'll check in my trusty dictionary. Its ... an absolute ruler unrestrained by law or constitution. Gee, that's deep. Lemme check through this, this book is kind of cool. Chris, did you know that the dictionary gave all these cool definitions.
Chris Stevens: That's why they made it Eric. Eric Forbes: Gee, I really never understood what they were for until now. Anyway, shall we read the dictionary a bit? HEY! Let's look up bit, Madison. (looks through the dictionary, while smiling.) A small piece or quantity of some material thing. Could we say that's what you just made a funny! That was hilarious!
Chris Stevens: OK, that's enough. This is what I was talking about becoming psychotic. Eric Forbes: OK, OK... Madison and I are smart though, we can't help it. [After the long talk, Chris opens up the room door as they both enter. The door slowly closes, as Eric and Chris are still talking. After blocking the view from the camera it slowly fades to complete darkness. In the background "Good
Ol' Days" can be heard, as a promotional video is played promoting the Battle of the Brains match.]
------------------------------------------------------------------ MADISON ------------------------------------------------------------------ -[ . . . The scene opens up inside of a very small gas station. The smell of grease, oil, and gas is terrible as you walk through the door. A old man is standing behind the counter. His beard is covered in oil, and he is wiping his hands off...apparently he just got done working on a car . . . ]-
-[ . . . The door behind the camera man swings open as "Playboy" Aidan Madison burst into the scene. He has a "Nike" golf hat on backwards, showing his spiked blonde hair sticking up. He is wearing a "Gap" t-shirt with black tear-away pants. . . ]-
[Aidan Madison]: "Dang it, I'm having the same trouble that Eric Forbes had in
Miami, but I'm lost in Atlanta. Who would have ever thought a hick state would have a city this big?"
[Gas Station Guy]: "Howdy! You seem lost. Ain't 'ya gots an map?" [AM]: "Sir, if I had a map, do you think I would be complaining about being lost? I don't think so! So, if you could speak a little english here, tell me where to find the arena around this area?"
[GSG]: "Well, yes-sur-we, I can tell 'ya wheres that arena is. You go down Elm and take a left. You come to a stop sign, then you tak. . ."
-[ . . . "Playboy" Aidan Madison cuts the guy off, grabs a map off the selve, and throws it on the counter. . . ]- [AM]: "Listen, just sell me this map. I'll find it myself." [GSG]: "I sees that 'ya gots no patience for southern speakers like me here. Here is you's map, that will be one dollar and twenty- two cents." -[ . . . Aidan Madison throws the money down on the counter, grabs the map, and walks back out to his rented station wagon. He pulls onto the streets and drives around not even paying attention to the map. . . ]-
[AM]: "You know Eric Forbes, I don't know why they put the two of us in a match together when we don't even know how to drive around cities. But, hey, atleast
I'm not the one getting into a car wreck. Then again, I'm the one driving a rented station wagon that I've been driving for about three years without paying for."
"The funny thing is, you describe being in a car wreck to being like me in our match Thursday. Very good, Eric. You came up with something intelligent, something that is fitting for our match. Although, I don't think there are going to be any car involved. Only thing that is going to be involved is a 15 foot steel ladder that I'm planning on using againest your head if the time suits right."
"I wonder the same thing you do, Eric. Why are we in a match called 'Battle of the Brains?' You openly admit that you aren't very intelligent, and I'll be the second to admit that I'm not all bright light bulbs in the brain either. But, hey, if the match makes me look smart, atleast my mommy will be proud."
"Eric, this _IS_ a very strange match. You're right when you say this match has a 'Playboy' in it, you're right when you say it has a dictionary in it, and you're right when you say it has a ladder in it. But, what I don't understand, is why you say you're special. I mean, if I were you, I wouldn't feel too special if I were stuck in a match with a ladder and a 'Playboy' involved. Then again, you are 'special.' The kind of 'special' where you are deemed, mentally ill."
"I'm happy to see that you're letting everybody know that you are fighting I,
'Playboy' Aidan Madison. Heck, you even let a tow truck guy know. . . that would be where I, usually, draw the line. Anyways, I guess I'm done for now. I still have to find this arena."
-[ . . . The scene fades out showing Aidan Madison pulling over to the side of the road to look at the map. A confused look comes over his face as he looks at the camera one more time. . . ]-
[AM]: "Oh yeah, Forbes, good luck finding Miami." -[ . . . Aidan Madison shoots a cocky smile at Forbes, then the screen cuts to a MWA promo . . .]- ------------------------------------------------------------------ FORBES ------------------------------------------------------------------ [The camera slowly increases in clarity, until the scenery can be seen. The focus is on room 18, of the 7 11 hotel. A man sits perfectly erect in front of the television set. He has long blonde hair, wears a pair of jeans, and a gray shirt. He looks as if he has just gotten up. The man is, Eric Forbes. The wind, cold and forbidding, was rattling the windowpanes which had been enough to pull
Eric out of his deep sleep. Eric slowly reached up and turned the barely audible sound up as loud as it could go, as just after that a yell was heard.
Chris Stevens, Eric's manager, was laying sleeping in his bed.] Chris Stevens: I was sleeping. Can't I get any sleep around here? It's always lets play monopoly, lets watch TV, hey, what are you doing watching the news?
Eric Forbes: I woke up, turned it on, and do you know what was on? A picture of me. Underneath it said "Ballerina" Eric Forbes... You told me no one would hear about that!
[Eric and Chris both look at each other, staring. Then suddenly Chris springs from his bed, in his pajamas and starts running for the bathroom. Eric gets up and follows, right behind Chris. But just in time Chris slams the bathroom door shut on Eric.]
Eric Forbes: Come on Chris. I won't hurt you, just open the door. I'm not upset about the ballerina thing, really... OPEN THE DOOR!
Chris Stevens: [From behind the door] Notta chance. If I come out of there you are going to beat me! I'm safest here, and you know it. How about we just work things out between the door?...
Eric Forbes: Sure, sure. I can understand that, but really, nothing's going to happen. I'd never hurt you, Chris. So how about we open the door, and talk about this like civiliiiiized (mispronouncing the word) people.
Chris Stevens: You're not fooling me. You may be in a Battle of the Brains match, but you can't fool me. It's not my fault that the check in lady told the news Eric Forbes was a ballerina. How can you blame that on me?
Eric Forbes: Was that a put down! First you tell everyone I'm a ballerina, then you tell me I'm not a genius? How dare you. And, as for blaming it on you, sure
I do. Actually I'd rather blame it on Madison, but you're the closest one in sight.
Chris Stevens: OK, I'll open the door if you promise not to hurt me. OK? Eric Forbes: Fine, fine. Chris Stevens: Put it on paper. [Eric then walks over to the table a few feet a way, and tears a part of the newspaper. He quickly grabs a marker laying on the table and writes "I won't hurt Chris" and signs his name on it. He then walks over to the door, and slides it under. After a few moments the door slowly and reluctantly opens.]
Eric Forbes: NOW, you call that television station and tell them that I'm not a ballerina! Or else I'll tell that tow truck guy were you are. I think he liked you, I'm sure he'd like to meet you again.
Chris Stevens: OK, OK. [Chris walks over to the bed, were the telephone sets. He grabs the phone book and starts shuffling through it. The camera then turns back towards Eric who is now sitting on the other bed, getting ready to speak.]
Eric Forbes: Ahem, Ahem. Ladies and Gentleman, esteemed cups of coffee, I would like to present to you s playboy! ...Well I don't really have one, my Mom wouldn't let me buy one. But wasn't that a great introduction? Anywho, let's talk about Aidan Madison, and the Battle of the Brains match. Both Madison and
I have a clean cut to ourselves. My reputation as a good Samaritan slightly nicer. I don't go under the name "Playboy." Although, except for that we are both considered by the MWA to be very smart people... Notice I said wrestler, not ballerina... OK, back to the match. I've talked to the tow-truck employee, the crash victim, to Chris, and even to myself. That basically includes everyone. Everyone knows about this match. One of the reasons everyone knows about this match is because I'm special! Don't you all see, don't you see
Madison? You asked why I was special, I'm special for a very special reason.
I'm in a specialty match! Therefore, I must be special. How often do people get to compete for a dictionary 15 feet in the air, against a porn star? Not very often! That's what makes me special!
Firstly, I want to say, I found MIAMI! Looks like I proved you wrong Madison, I know when you said "good luck" you meant "haa haa." See I've read your story,
I've studied you inside and out. Even inside once more. When you said "I'm not the one getting into a car wreck" you really meant "I wish I got into a car wreck like Eric." See, I know everything about you, or at least a little bit.
This goes to my advantage, because of my extensive brain study of you, I should do great in the match.
Aidan, we may be in a match, but we have something in common. You've been driving a rented station wagon for 3 years, without paying. I've been driving a
Lincoln Continental, my grandfathers, for 2 years now. Luckily I got it fixed yesterday. You know with the car wreck and everything. Those cars out there are dangerous, I don't know why they let maniacs drive. Speaking of maniacs, I really don't have much more to say about them. But, I can talk about you, forever.
Chris Stevens (off camera): Please don't. Eric Forbes: Fine fine, but did you call that television station yet? Chris Stevens: Umm...uhh..I sorta can't find... [Then the camera becomes darker, as a large amount of arguing is heard between
Eric and Chris. The theme music "Good Ol' Days" plays, as on the screen in white letters it says "Battle of the brains", then blinks, "Which one has a brain." Then pictures of Eric Forbes and Aidan Madison pop up on the screen.]
