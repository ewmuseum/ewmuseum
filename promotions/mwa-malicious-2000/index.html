Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A promotion calling itself the Malicious Wrestling Association opened across Georgia and Alabama in November 1999, its own account describing the purchase of an earlier, unrelated two-year-old federation by a new owner determined to make something more lasting of it.

Its January 2000 FAQ laid out a booked federation run on plainly stated terms: one character per handler unless an angle required otherwise, no maiming another wrestler's persona without a transcript proving consent, a three-flash daily ceiling, and a monthly Wrestler of the Month push reserved for the roster's most dedicated hand. A SuperCard, Clash of the Titans, was promised every other month; in between, the federation ran its own weekly Friday Escapade and Sunday Breakdown broadcasts.

The roster's own build-up vignettes staged one wrestler alone in a basement measuring himself against a rival he had never met, and two more on a farcical road trip to their own arena, complete with a mistaken identity at a hotel front desk. Tumultuous Thursday opened to fireworks from Atlanta, Georgia, ran a dark match and a mocked-up battle of intellects between two of its own stars, and closed on a shock, self-announced arrival that upended the card it had just aired.

Within days a newsletter announced a change of on-screen president, promising a pay-per-view already billed for Georgia and a Mid-South championship to sit beside the promotion's existing Down South title.

The bare acronym MWA was never this promotion's alone. Six other federations recorded elsewhere in this museum carried the same three letters across a decade on either side of this one, and three further 2000-dated federations share it as well; none of their own full names, hosts, or rosters overlap with this one's.

No further activity survives. The promotion's own last-dated document here is a 31 January 2000 reposting of its handbook, and no closing notice of any kind follows it.
