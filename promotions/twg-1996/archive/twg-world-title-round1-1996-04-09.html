The Wrestling Greats — Tournament, Round One

"TWG: "THE WRESTLING GREATS" #2"

9 April 1996

TWG WORLD TITLE TOURNAMENT
April 9, 1996
From The MetroDome, Minneapolis, Minnesota
ROUND ONE
Nikita Koloff (Black wrestling tights with Black Boots) vs. Tully Blanchard (Red wrestling tights with Red and White Boots) with JJ - Nikita uses power moves to dominate the first part of the match but Tully
eventually gets Nikita off his feet and goes to work on him. Nikita then gets the upperhand again and looks to be on the verge of finishing
Tully off when JJ Dillon starts to interfere. Nikita catches Tully with the Russian Sickle but JJ is on the ring apron arguing with Tommy Young so there is no referee to make the three count. Then Arn Anderson runs in and
attacks Nikita and gives him a quick DDT, but Dusty Rhodes comes to Nikita's defense and when Tommy Young turns around Dusty and Arn are going at it in the ring. While Young tries to get them out of the ring Tully is being handed something by JJ, Nikita runs at Tully for another Russian Sickle but
Tully ducks and Nikita hits JJ. The crowd goes wild but when Nikita turns around Tully nails him with JJ's shoe and goes for the cover just as Young turns back around to make the three count. 15:43
Sting (Red wrestling tights with Red and Blue Boots plus Red & Blue makeup) vs. Sid Vicious (Black wrstling tights with Black Boots) - A power vs. finese match as Sid tries to pummel Sting into oblivion but Sting uses his quickness and agility to get in his fair share of shots on big Sid. Sid finally slows Sting down and prepares to PowerBomb him when
Sting somehow slips out of Sid's grasp and through his legs. Sting then gives Sid a big dropkick, whips him into the ropes and gives him a cross body block and goes for the cover. Sid though throws Sting off and all the way through the ropes and on to the floor. However, Sting lands on his feet and while Sid prances around the ring flexing his muscles he climbs
to the top rope and when Sid turns around he performs a beautiful flying cross body block and gets the three count. 12:19
Bret Hart (Black and Pink wrestling tights with Black and Pink Boots) vs. Rick Martel (Red wrestling tights with Red and White Boots) - A classic matchup of mat technicians. Hart and Martel give everything they have for over 30 minutes of "pure wrestling". Martel eventually gets Hart in his infamous Boston Crab but he just will not quit. Hart finally makes it to the ropes to break the hold. Then it was Hart's turn as he applied the sharpshooter to Martel. Martel fought it hard but he just couldn't reach the ropes and he eventually gave in. 30:25
Ricky Steamboat (Green Wrestling Tights with Green Boots) vs. The Undertaker (Black outfit and purple gloves) with Paul Bearer -Wow! Talk about wrestlers with different styles. This match was wild as the Undertaker pummeled Steamboat time and time again, but Steamboat would not quit and he kept mounting comeback after comeback. Finally, the Undertaker prepared to give Steamboat the Tombstone Piledriver but somehow Steamboat managed to slip out of it and roll the Undertaker up in an inside cradle for the three count. The Undertaker was stunned and he took out his frustrations by continuing to attack Steamboat. He even gave
Steamboat The Tombstone before Sting and Nikita Koloff came to his aid.
14:37
Dusty Rhodes (Black wrestling tights and Red and White Boots) vs. Ric Flair (Purple wrestling tights and Purple and White Boots)with JJ -A very physical match as Rhodes and Flair pounded each other until both were bleeding profusely. Rhodes had gotten the advantage and after giving
Flair two or three atomic elbows he went for the cover, but Flair kicked out at two and half. JJ Dillon then got up on the ring apron again and tried to distract Tommy Young as Arn Anderson ran in to the ring. However, this time Dusty caught Arn coming in and nailed him with an atomic elbow sending him flying over the top rope. Ric Flair then nailed Dusty in the back of the knee while he was turned around and rolled him up, grabbed some
tights, and placed his feet on the ropes to get the three count. 21:53
Shawn Michaels (Red wrestling tights with Red Boots) vs. Diesel (Black and Silver outfit with Black Boots) -A fairly even match as Shawn Michaels took a pretty good beating from the big man in the early going, but came back and simply outwrestled Diesel until he missed a flying drop kick from the top rope. Diesel then went to the tope rope and tried to finish Michaels off with flying splash but
Michael's rolled out of the way and then caught a stunned Diesel with the
SuperKick for the three count.
Robert Gibson (Baby Blue wrestling tights with White Boots) vs. Stan Lane (Green Wrestling tights with Green Boots)with Jim Cornette - This was a high paced, high flying match with lots of action in and out of the ring. In ended when Gibson was setting up to give Stan Lane a big drop kick, but Lane grabbed referee Nick Patrick and pulled him in front of him and Gibson nailed Nick Patrick instead, knocking him out of the ring.
With Patrick down Lane and Gibson continued to whip each other from one side of the ring to the other. As Gibson bounced off the ropes Jim
Cornette nailed him in the back with his tennis racket. Lane went for the cover and referee Scrappy Magallon ran in and made the three count sending
Lane to round two.
Lex Luger (Black wrestling tights and White Boots)with JJ Dillon vs. Arn Anderson (Black wrestling tights with Red and White Boots)with JJ - A wierd situation here as two of the dreaded four horsemen were scheduled to go at it. JJ Dillon and Arn got into the ring and asked for the microphone. JJ then began to explain to the crowd and Lex as well that he had a "plan". Arn then laid down on the mat and JJ proded Luger to cover him. Luger seemed confused and suspicious but he reluctantly knealt down and covered Arn as JJ told Tommy Young to make the three count. He did and then Arn held Luger's hand up and although Luger still looked confused he walked out with Arn and JJ. 00.03
ROUND ONE
From The Georgia Dome, Atlanta, Georgia
Randy Savage (Blue and Yellow wrestling tights with Blue and White Boots) vs. Barry Windham (Yellow wrestling tights with Red and White Boots) -This was a great match between two guys who don't know the meaning of the word quit. Savage seemed to have the win on several occasions as he rolled up Windham in small packages, back slides, and sunset flips but each time
Windham kicked out at 2. Windham eventually got control of the match and after giving Savage the Superplex from the top rope he covered him, but
Savage kicked out at 2 3/4. Windham then took Savage and placed him on the top turnbuckle again and prepared to give him another Superplex but Savage pushed him off the top rope and Windham landed hard on his back. Savage took advantage of this and quickly delivered his flying elbow drop on
Windham to get the three count. 13:46
The British Bulldog (Red, White, and Blue wrestling tights and Boots) vs. Roddy Piper (Blue wrestling tights and Blue Boots) -The Bulldog took it to Piper with big clotheslines, power slams, and press slams. But the nutcase Piper kept battling back. He took the Bulldog outside of the ring several times to work him over and even tried to pile drive him on the floor but the Bulldog reversed it and tossed piper over his back. Once they got back in the ring the Bulldog started to punish the "Rowdy" one. Again Piper fought back and poked The Bulldog in the eye.
He went to the tope rope and dove off but the Bulldog caught him, delivered
the running power slam, and covered him for the three count but Curt Hennig came running in and attacked the Bulldog. Piper was disqualified, but he didn't care as he joined the attack. The Bulldog, however, fought off the attack and chased both men out of the ring. 10:26
Hawk (Black and Red wrestling tights, Black Boots, and Black and Red makeup) vs. Rick Rude (Multi-colored wrestling tights and Boots) with Bobby Heenan -A physical match that went back and forth. Rick Rude seemed to have the match in hand when he gave Hawk the "Rude Awakening" but somehow Hawk kicked out at 2 3/4. Hawk then took control and after he gave Rude a big power slam he climbed to the top rope but Bobby Heenan grabbed his leg and tried to pull him off. Hawk promptly kicked Heenan in the face sending him to the floor and then executed a perfect flying clothesline to get the three count on Rude. 9:31
Ricky Morton (Baby Blue wrestling tights with White Boots) vs. Jerry Lawler (Red and Black wrestling tights with Black Boots) -Lawler punished Morton a lot in this, even busting him open, but Morton did not give up easily. He fought and fought but Lawler finally nailed him with the flying fist drop to get the pin. 17:11
The Ultimate Warrior (White wrestling tights, White Boots, Blue & White MU) vs. Jake Roberts (Baby Blue wrestling tights with snake skin boots) -The Warrior dominates this match as he pins Jake after giving him the Warrior Splash. After the match ,however, as The Warrior posed for the crowd Jake slipped up behind him and threw Damian around his neck. The
Warrior seemed scared at first, but he then took the snake, pressed it above his head, dropped it, knocked Jake out of the ring and then gave
Damian the Warrior Splash. Jake couldn't beleive it and chased the Warrior out of the ring with a chair. The Warrior, with his music playing, then raced around the ring shaking hands and then ran all the way back to the dressing room as the crowd went wild and an obviously upset Jake tried to put Damian back in his bag. 5:34
The Great Muta (Red tights, Red Boots, Red&Green MU) with Gary Hart vs. Bobby Eaton (Green wrestling tights with Green Boots) -No Jim Cornette to help Eaton in this one as he had decided to be with Stan Lane in Minnesota. Eaton still put up a good fight, but it was not to be as the awesome Muta nalied him with the Moonsault for the three count.
12:04
Hulk Hogan (Yellow wrestling tights with Yellow Boots) vs. Curt Hennig (Dark Blue wrestling tights and White Boots) -"Mr. Perfect" gave the Hulkster everything he had in this one and if it wouldn't have been for Roddy Piper trying to "Help" him he might have pulled off the upset. But Piper screwed up as the nutcase came running to the ring not when Mr. Perfect was in trouble but when he had Hogan right where he wanted him. Hennig had the Hulkster in the sleeper when Piper ran in and climbed to the top rope. The referee warned Piper not to jump but he did anyway. However, the Hulkster turned "Mr. Perfect" around and Piper nailed him in the back. The Sleeper was gone and Hogan was "Hulking" up.
He nailed Piper and knocked him over the top rope and then he grabbed
Hennig, whipped him into the ropes, gave him the big boot and then the leg drop for the pin. 14:19
Animal(Red and Black tights, Black Boots, Red and Black face paint) vs. Vader (Red and Black outfit with Black Boots)with Gary Hart -A very hard fought, physical, and high impact match between two awesome men. Animal gave it everything he had as he slammed, clotheslined, and even piledrived the big man but Vader was just too much as he caught Animal with the Vader Splash, not from the second rope, but from the third for the pin.
17:45
