The Wrestling Greats — Tournament, Round Two

"TWG World Title Tourny-Round 2"

TWG WORLD TITLE TOURNAMENT
APRIL 10, 1996
ROUND TWO
From The MetroDome, Minneapolis, Minnesota
Sting (White wrestling tights, Blue Boots, Blue and White face paint) vs. Tully Blanchard (Black wrestling tights with Red and White Boots)with JJ - A back and forth match with both men getting the upperhand several times. However, Tully seemed to be on the verge of putting Sting away when he executed the Slingshot Suplex, but somehow Sting kicked out at 2 3/4. Tully
was frustrated and began punching and kicking Sting with everything he had. Sting continued to fight back and eventually gave Tully the Stinger Splash and prepared to put the Scorpian Death Lock on him when JJ Dillon jumped on the ring apron and started arguing with Tommy Young. Sting let Tully go and proceeded to go and grab JJ by the neck and started choking him. Tully saw his opportunity and ran at Sting to knee him in the back, but Sting moved and Tully hit JJ. Sting then rolled Tully up in a small package for the victory. 17:54
Ricky Steamboat (Red wrestling tights with Red Boots) vs. Bret Hart (Pink and Black wrestling tights and Boots) - What a match! Definetly the best match of the first two rounds. Both men came into the match a little worse for wear. Hart was coming off a very physical match with Rick Martel and Steamboat was still recovering from the beating he had received from The Undertaker. Still, both men gave it everything they had as they battled each other for over 30 grueling minutes.
It ended with an exhausted Hart diving through the second and third ropes to land on an equally exhausted Steamboat who was outside the ring. The force of Hart flying through the air and hitting Steamboat sent them crashing into a table with Ricky's back and head hitting it first. Both men were almost out cold but Hart managed to roll into the ring before the
10 count. A nearly unconscious Steamboat did not make back into the ring and Hart was awarded the victory. Several wrestlers had to come help both men back to the dressing room. 31:21
Shawn Michaels (Baby Blue wrestling tights and Boots) vs. Ric Flair (Green wrestling tights and Yellow boots)with JJ Dillon - Another classic as the "Nature Boy" and "The Heartbreak Kid" went at it. It was back and forth until Flair executed a hanging suplex on Michaels and they came down on top of referee Tommy Young. With the referee out, here came Tully Blanchard and Arn Anderson down to interfere again. This time ,however, Dusty Rhodes and Nikita Koloff came down to counter the attack.
While those four and Flair went at it, JJ Dillon started waving for somebody to come from the back, presumably Lex Luger, but no, it was Diesel who ran into the ring. He gave Michaels a JackKnife and then Flair covered him as
JJ had gotten Tommy Young back up just in time to make the three count. The
Horsemen were then quickly chased to the back by Dusty and Nikita, but
Diesel was stopped by "Mean Gene" for an interview. As Diesel talked he was attacked by Michaels. They got in the ring and started going at with
Michaels nailing Diesel with a flying forearm. Michaels then stood in the corner and was preparing to give Diesel the SuperKick after he got up, when The Undertaker coming running in and nailed Michaels in the back.
He then gave Michaels the Tombstone PileDriver and Diesel gave him another
JackKnife before Dusty and Nikita could get back out to help him. 23:45
Lex Luger (Blue wrestling tights with White Boots)with JJ Dillon vs. Stan Lane (Purple wrestling tights with Purple Boots)with Jim Cornette - This was kind of an anti-climactic match as Luger made relatively short work of Lane, putting him in the "Torture Rack" to get the victory. After the match "Mean Gene" interviewed Dillon and Luger. Dillon was about to have a spazm because Ric Flair and Luger were now supposed to meet in the
Quaterfinals until Arn Anderson came down and reminded him that they had a
"Plan" and not to worry. JJ calms down but Luger is obviously more confused and suspicious than ever. He tries to get Arn to tell him what "Plan" he is talking about but Arn just tells him everything is under control and not to worry. The leave with Luger looking really concerned. 9:32
From The Georgia Dome, Atlanta, Georgia
Randy Savage (Black and White wrestling tights with White Boots)with Liz vs. The British Bulldog (Red, White, and Blue wrestling tights and Boots) - The Bulldog proves that he desrves to be considered one of the top wrestlers of all time as he takes the Macho Man to the limit. Savage, however, battled back from near devastattion as he always does and got the victory after catching the Bulldog with the flying elbow drop. 16:11
Hawk (Black and Blue wresling tights and Boots, Black and Blue face paint) vs. Jerry Lawler (Red and Black wrestling tights and Boots) - Hawk pummels Lawler throughout most of the match, but Lawler is able to slow him down and methodically work him over on several occasions. Then the nutcase "Rowdy" Roddy Piper shows up at ringside to "support" Lawler.
Animal soon follows to protect Hawk's back. Eventually, Piper and Animal got into it on the floor after Piper had nailed Hawk when the referee wasn't looking. Hawk still seems to have control of the match, however, when after knocking Piper over the railing Animal decides to "help" Hawk a little bit. Animal climbed to the top rope and dove at Lawler. Lawler moved and Animal nailed Hawk instead. Piper is back up and he grabs Animal and pulls him outside the ring. Lawler then covers Hawk for the three count. Piper and Lawler head to the locker room as Animal tries to explain to Hawk what had happened. Eventually, they head to the locker room also, but Hawk was very steamed at Animal. 13:09
The Ultimate Warrior (Orange wrestling tights and Boots, Orange/Black FP) vs. The Great Muta (Red wrestling tights and Boots, Red/Green FP) with Gary
Hart - A fantastic high impact match with the Warrior pounding on Muta and Muta using his quickness and agility to keep the Warrior off balance. The match ends with Muta missing the Moonsault when the Warrior moves out of the way.
The Warrior then tries to finish Muta off with the Warrior Splash but Muta rolls out of the way. Gary Hart then distracts the referee and Muta blows
Green Mist in the Warriors eyes. Muta goes to the tope rope and catches an almost blind Warrior with a flying sunset flip for the three count. 27:34
Hulk Hogan (Yellow wrestling tights and Boots) vs. Vader (Black and Red wrestling outfit)with Gary Hart - A back and forth match for over 20 minutes with both men pounding the crap out of each other. Hogan eventually gets the victory by Disqualification when The Great Muta and Gary Hart interfere. Hogan was
"Hulking" up and he gave Vader the Big Boot. He then bounced off the rope and was going to go for the Big Leg Drop when Muta came tearing into the ring and nailed him in the side, therefore disqualifying Vader.
Muta and Vader then went to work on Hogan. Randy Savage came to help Hulk but he was pummeled also. Hogan was given the Vader Spash several times and Muta nailed Savage with the Moonsault twice as Vader and Hart held him.
Muta and Vader left the ring area with Hogan and Savage beaten to a pulp. 26:28
The Quaterfinals, Semi Finals, and Finals will take place next week from The SuperDome in New Orleans, Louisiana.
The Quaterfinal matchups:
Sting vs. Bret Hart Lex Luger vs. Ric Flair Randy Savage vs. Jerry Lawler Hulk Hogan vs. The Great Muta
