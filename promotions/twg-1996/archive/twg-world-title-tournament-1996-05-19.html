The Wrestling Greats — Tournament, Complete

"TWG: TWG World Title Tournament"

17-19 May 1996

TWG: THE WRESTLING GREATS
TWG WORLD TITLE TOURNAMENT
ROUND ONE
FROM THE METRODOME IN MINNEAPOLIS, MINNESOTA
MAY 17, 1996
Tully Blanchard pins Nikita Koloff after interference by Arn Anderson and then hitting him with JJ Dillon's shoe. 15:19.
Sting pins Sid Vicious with a flying cross-body block from the top rope. 12:54.
Bret Hart makes Rick Martel submit to the Sharpshooter after surviving the Boston Crab in this grueling match. 30:32.
Ricky Steamboat pins The Undertaker by rolling him up after slipping out of
The Tombstone. Steamboat got pummeled by The Undertaker after the match.
14:56.
Ric Flair pins Dusty Rhodes with the small package and some held tights after a very physical and bloody match. 20:03.
Shawn Michaels pins Diesel after nailing him with The SuperKick. 13:13.
Stan Lane pins Robert Gibson after Jim Cornette hits Gibson with his tennis racket. 9:23.
Lex Luger pins Arn Anderson when Anderson laid down and let Luger cover him. Arn explained that he did this because The Four Hosemen have a plan.
Luger seemed confused by what Anderson was talking about but covered him for the pin anyway. 00.03.
ROUND TWO
FROM THE GEORGIA DOME IN ATLANTA, GEORGIA
MAY 17, 1996
Randy Savage pins Barry Windham after a very hard fought match with the Flying Elbow Drop. 25:42.
The British Bulldog defeats Roddy Piper by disqualification when Curt Hennig interferes. 8:23.
Jerry Lawler pins Ricky Morton with the Flying Fist Drop after a very hard fought and bloody match. 16:12.
Hawk pins Rick Rude with the Flying Clothesline from the top rope. 10:56.
The Ultimate Warrior pins Jake Roberts with the Warrior Splash. The Warrior even gives Damian the Warrior Splash after Roberts tried to put the snake on him after the match. 11:02.
The Great Muta pins Bobby Eaton with the MoonSault. 13:08.
Vader pins Animal after giving him the Vader Bomb. 18:34.
Hulk Hogan pins Curt Hennig with the Big Boot and The Leg Drop after interference by Roddy Piper backfired on Hennig.
ROUND TWO
FROM THE METRODOME IN MINNEAPOLIS, MINNESOTA
MAY 17, 1996
Sting pins Tully Blanchard with the small package after interference by JJ Dillon backfires on Blanchard. 17:12.
Bret Hart defeats Ricky Steamboat by countout after Steamboat crashed into a table at ringside and couldn't get back into the ring before the Ten
Count. The best match so far. 32:41.
Ric Flair pins Shawn Michaels in a great match. Interference by Blanchard and Anderson was thwarted by Rhodes and Koloff but Diesel came running out and gave Shawn Michaels a Jackknife while referee Tommy Young was knocked out. Dillon got Young up to make the three count as Flair covered
Michaels. After the match Dillon was giving Diesel his payoff at ringside when Michaels attacked Diesel. However, The Undertaker came running in and helped Diesel pummel Michaels. The Undertaker gave him a Tombstone and
Diesel gave him another Jackknife before Rhodes and Koloff could get back out to save Michaels. 26:16.
Lex Luger made Stan Lane submit to The Human Torture Rack. After the match
JJ Dillon was upset because now Flair and Luger are matched up to go at it in the Quaterfinals but Arn Anderson reminded Dillon they had a plan. This calmed Dillon but Luger demanded to know what plan Anderson was talking about. Anderson told Luger not to worry about it and the three went to the dressing room with Luger feeling really suspicious and confused. 6:12.
ROUND TWO
FROM THE GEORGIA DOME IN ATLANTA, GEORGIA
MAY 17, 1996
Randy Savage pins the British Bulldog with the Flying Elbow Drop after coming back from a tremendous beating at the hands of The Bulldog. 21:10.
Jerry Lawler pins Hawk after Animal accidently nails him in the back. Hawk was very upset with Animal after the match. 16:37.
The Great Muta pins The Ultimate Warrior after blowing green mist in his eyes and catching the blinded Warrior with a Flying Sunset Flip from the top rope. 10:59.
Hulk Hogan defeats Vader by disqualification when The Great Muta interferes. Randy Savage tries to help Hogan but Vader and Muta pummel the
MegaPowers. 9:43.
QUATERFINALS
FROM THE SUPERDOME IN NEW ORLEANS, LOUISIANA
MAY 19, 1996
Bret Hart pins Sting with a roll-up after probably the greatest match in the history of professional wrestling. 45:45.
Ric Flair pins Lex Luger in another outstanding match. When Luger got to the ring Arn Anderson and JJ Dillon tried to get Luger to take a dive for
Flair like Anderson had down for Luger in the first round but Luger would have none of it. As Flair made his way to the ring he could see the argument brewing in the ring. He told Anderson to come over and tell him what was going on. When Anderson told him Flair looked perturbed and asked for the microphone. Flair - "Shut up all you fat, out of shape, redneck's.
I've got something to say. Double A, I appreciate the thought but the
"Nature Boy" Ric Flair doesn't need anyone to take a dive for him. Luger, you are the most awesome physical speciman in wrestling today but if you want a piece of the Nature Boy you're gonna get your ass kicked. But if somehow I was to lose to you, I'll shake your hand and say you are the better man. But I want you to do the same when I kick your ass back to the gym, brother. Deal? (Luger nods). Oh, one last thing -
Wooooooooooooooooooooo!". Flair threw the microphone down and after Dillon and Anderson tried one last time to stop the match, Flair and Luger put on a great match as Flair got a clean pin with a Flying Sunset Flip from the top rope. After the match Flair and Luger shook hands, Dillon and Anderson breathed a sigh of relief, and Tully Blanchard joined them for a one for four, four for one chant. 36:49.
Randy Savage pins Jerry Lawler with the Flying Elbow Drop. 19:23.
Hulk Hogan pins the Great Muta with the Big Boot and Leg Drop after interference by Gary Hart and Vader backfired on Muta. 13:38.
SEMI-FINALS
FROM THE SUPERDOME IN NEW ORLEANS, LOUISIANA
MAY 19, 1996
Ric Flair pins Bret Hart with the figure-four after Bret had passed out from the pain. Hart had hurt his knee earlier in the match when he had gotten it twisted up in the ring ropes. He surely had torn ligaments in his knee but he had continued to fight Flair for 15 minutes more before succumbing to the figure-four. 26:16.
Randy Savage defeated Hulk Hogan by countout after Hogan had been attacked by Vader and The Great Muta. Savage and the referee, Earl Hebner had run into each other and knocked each other out. Hogan had fallen outside of the ring when Muta and Vader attacked. When Hebner came to he made the Ten
Count on a very bloodied Hogan before he could get back in the ring..
After the match the bloodied Hogan tried to find out why Savage didn't help him and Savage tried to explain he was knocked out, but there was obvious tension between the two. 17:09.
FINALS
FROM THE SUPERDOME IN NEW ORLEANS, LOUISIANA
MAY 19, 1996
Ric Flair pinned Randy Savage to win the TWG World Title. The Horsemen tried to interfere on Flair's behalf but Hulk Hogan came out to help "The
Macho Man". Arn Anderson was trying to get in the ring with a chair but
Hogan grabbed it and they fought over it. As Hogan finally pulled the chair away from Anderson, Flair had sent Savage towards the ropes and the momentum of the chair nailed Savage in the head. Flair made the cover for the three count as Luger and Blanchard kept Hogan from getting in the ring to stop the count. After the match Savage was going balistic but somehow he and Hogan managed to patch things up just enough to make it to the locker room together. However, Savage was seen leaving the SuperDome in a different Limo than the one he and Hogan had arrived in earlier.
