TWG "The Wrestling Greats"
Welcome to TWG, The Wrestling Greats . This is simply a work of my imagination. I always wondered what would happen if 32 of the greatest wrestlers (in my opinion these are the 32 greatest) could have come together when each of them were in their respected primes and wrestled in the same association.
I will basically be the booker for this league, with the help of The
Wrestling League Simulator 5.1. I will try my best to provide exciting, entertaining, and surprise filled story lines and angles. I know many of you will disagree with some of my chosen wrestlers and my story lines, so I want your input.
I am doing this for my enjoyment and hopefully your entertainment. Please send me your comments about my imaginary wrestling league: TWG-"The
Wrestling
Greats" and if there is enough request for a particular wrestler or story line I will do my best to please you.
E-mail me at: [detail withheld]
Thanks and Enjoy!
And now, introducing the wrestlers:
"The Nature Boy" Ric Flair ('83-'92)- a member of the dreaded Four Horsemen and managed by J.J. Dillon.
Hulk Hogan ('84-'90) - one half of the Mega Powers.
"The Macho Man" Randy Savage ('85-'92) - one half of the Mega Powers and managed by the lovely Elizabeth.
Sting ('88-present) - Famous for his Scorpion Death Lock
The Ultimate Warrior ('89-present) - Famous for his Warrior Splash
The Undertaker ('90-present) - The Man from the Darkside is managed by Paul Bearer
"The Total Package" Lex Luger ('87-present) - a member of the dreaded Four Horsemen and managed by J.J. Dillon
Vader ('90-present) - This Big man is mean, nasty, and can fly around the ring like a man half his size. He is managed by Gary Hart.
Ricky "The Dragon" Steamboat ('81-'90) - One of wrestlings greatest competitors.
Jerry "The King" Lawler (Early 80's-Early 90's) - The real King of Memphis.
"The American Dream" Dusty Rhodes (Late 70's-Mid to Late 80's) - one half of the SuperPowers.
Barry Windham ('87-Early 90's) - The Big Texan.
The Great Muta ('89-Present) - The great one from the Orient is managed by Gary Hart.
Tully Blanchard ('85-'90) - a member of the dreaded Four Horsemen and managed by J.J. Dillon.
Nikita Koloff ('85-Late 80's) - The Russian Nightmare is one half of the SuperPowers.
Sid Vicious ('89-present) - This man is psycho.
Diesel (Early 90's-present) - Big Daddy Cool.
"Rowdy" Roddy Piper (Early 80's-Early 90's) - The man that brought you Piper's Pit.
Bret "The Hitman" Hart (Mid 80's-Present) - "The best there ever was, the best there is, and the best there ever will be".
Rick Martel (Mid 80's-'88) - One of the greatest AWA World Champions of all-time.
"Mr. Perfect" Curt Hennig (Mid 80's-Early 90's) - No injuries will stop him in TWG.
"The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (Late 80's-Present) - He loves to play a little "chin music".
"Ravishing" Rick Rude (Mid to Late 80's-Early 90's) - he is managed by Bobby "The Brain" Heenan.
Jake "The Snake" Roberts (Mid to Late 80's-Early 90's) - Damian is watching you.
"The British Bulldog" Davey Boy Smith (Mid 80's-Present) - A powerful high flyer.
"The Enforcer" Arn Anderson (Mid 80's-Present) - a member of the dreaded Four Horsmen and managed by J.J. Dillon.
Hawk (Mid 80's-Early 90's) - one half of the Road Warriors and a member of The Legion of Doom managed by "Precious" Paul Ellering.
Animal (Mid 80's-Early 90's) - one half of the Road Warriors and a member of the Legion of Doom managed by "Precious" Paul Ellering.
"Beautiful" Bobby Eaton (Mid 80's-Early 90's) - one half of The Midnight Express and managed by Jim Cornette.
Ricky Morton (Mid 80's-Late 80's) - one half of The Rock'n'Roll Express.
"Sweet" Stan Lane (Mid to Late 80's-Early 90's) - one half of The Midnight Express and managed by Jim Cornette.
Robert Gibson (Mid 80's-Late 80's) - one half of the Rock'n'Roll Express
TWG will begin with a Tournament for the TWG World Title on April 9, 1996. All 32 wrestlers will compete in this single elimination tournament. Rounds one and two will be held on two seperate nights in two seperate cities. 16 wrestlers in each city. The cities have not been chosen as of this writing. All wrestlers will be seeded by a random draw.
The Quaterfinals, Semi-Finals, and Finals will take place one week after
Rounds one and two in a yet to be determined location.
The First Round Matchups:
Nikita Koloff vs. Tully Blanchard Sid Vicious vs. Sting Bret Hart vs. Rick Martel The Undertaker vs. Ricky Steamboat Ric Flair vs. Dusty Rhodes Diesel vs. Shawn Michaels Robert Gibson vs. Stan Lane Arn Anderson vs. Lex Luger
Barry Windham vs. Randy Savage Roddy Piper vs. The British Bulldog Rick Rude vs. Hawk Jerry Lawler vs. Ricky Morton The Ultimate Warrior vs. Jake Roberts The Great Muta vs. Bobby Eaton Curt Hennig vs. Hulk Hogan Animal vs. Vader
