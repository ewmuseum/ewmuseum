Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This International Wrestling Alliance reached rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 1 April 1998 already carrying itself as an established name rather than a newcomer: “I really don't want to sit here and hype a fed that needs no hype,” its recruitment notice read, inviting wrestlers looking to “have some fun and kick some butt” to a page hosted on GeoCities' Colosseum address.

The notice was signed by Brickhouse Brown, “The Hardest Working Prez on the Net!!” — the same self-styled president persona and the same numbered GeoCities account behind two other promotions on record, one running from July 1997 to its own September 1998 close and another opening its doors the following January.

No roster, card, or closure notice survives beyond this one recruitment post; how this particular promotion under that persona fit alongside the others he ran, and how or when it itself ended, are outside the surviving record.
