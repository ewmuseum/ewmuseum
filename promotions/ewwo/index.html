Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The self-titled “E.xtreme W.orld W.restling O.rganazation” [sic] opened hiring on 30 November 1997 under a strict house rule: “No REAL wrestlers they must be ORIGINAL.” Its own launch ad, signed by its self-titled president, capped applicants at two singles or one tag team apiece and asked for a full build sheet — height, weight, style, alignment, an optional manager, a finisher and its theme music — ahead of a homepage promised to go up shortly.

Two days later a reply circulating in the same newsgroup thread gave it little chance of lasting: “i give the fed two weeks,” signed off as a supporter of a rival camp.

Nothing later than 5 December 1997 survives. No card, roster or result of any kind is in its surviving record, and no document confirms whether the predicted two weeks ever ran out.
