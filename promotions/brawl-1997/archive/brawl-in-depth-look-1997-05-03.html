An in-depth look at BRAWL

3 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Stuff about BRAWL Rather than post some flash commercial or the list of regulations, I thought I'd elaborate a bit on what BRAWL will be about and what it will involve. Basically what I'd like my fed to be is as mature and real as possible. Original, realistic, wrestlers only. No lame copies or indestructible monsters. I plan on screening entries so only serious and quality wrestlers will get in. I'm more than willing to work with people to help them make and fine tune their characters. I've seen too many feds that were filled with immature, hormonaly over-active, 12 year old, Beavis and Butt-Head clones. I want BRAWL to be a league for serious, mature PBM role-players. There's a definite line between fun and childishly ridiculous. BRAWL is going to be simmed using TNM 6.2+. However, since TNM allows me to adjust a wrestler's chances of winning, I'll be adjusting a wrestler's "push" every match based on how well they were role-played. Left alone, simming is just too random and impersonal. The players get the sensation of not really being in control of their wrestlers, an aspect I personally always disliked. Booking however is completely unfair as far as I'm concerned. Some people just aren't as good of writers or role-players as others. Such people shouldn't be penalized for something that is not entirely their fault. It's taken me nearly twenty years to get to where I'm at creativity-wise (which might not really be saying that much) and some people's talents simply might lie in other areas. The way I plan on running BRAWL, everyone will have an equal chance because I'll be taking quality, quantity, AND effort into consideration. Effort is just as important as effect as far as I'm concerned. I plan on utilizing NPCs (Non-Player Characters, or in e-wrestling's case, Non-Handler Wrestlers) in my league. I realize alot of people dislike NPCs because they think the fedhead will favor them. That will not be the case in BRAWL. I've played RPGs such as Dungeons and Dragons, Shadowrun, Vampire: The Masquerade, and various others for the better part of half my life. I know how to properly use NPCs without favoring them. In fact, the NPCs that will be appearing in BRAWL are characters from a pro-wrestling RPG I made several years ago. In all honesty, there are several of them that I would just LOVE to see turned into meat waffles. The reason for the NPCs is 1: to populate various division that are lacking in wrestlers. I've seen several feds whose Cruiser Weight division only contained 3-6 guys so the same guys were fighting each other over and over again. BORING. The NPCs will help to fill the ranks and add some variety. 2: With the NPCs, I will be able to work plots, angles, and storylines that could involve the players. A player could suddenly find himself caught in a violent fued between two NPC or might even form a tag team with one. 3: By using NPCs, I'll be able to participate somewhat. As an experienced RPG game master, I know all too well how frustrating and annoying it is to do all the work so that everyone else can have all the fun. The NPCs will allow me to get in on some of the fun. Occassionally I might ask one of the players to temporarily role-play one of the NPC or maybe even let them take them over permanently. I guess I should say a little something about myself. As I've stated before, I've been playing RPGs for well over 12 years so I'm quite familiar with role-playing and role-playing games. I've developed at least 10 RPGS of my own though I never marketed any of them. I also write science fiction as a hobby and have written two novel length stories as well as numerous other short stories. My experience pertaining to e-wrestling is admittedly somewhat limited but I'm confident my RPG experience and creative talents will more than compensate...if you can't dazzle 'em with your brilliance, baffle 'em with your bullsh*t :) Well that about covers all the stuff that I feel is important for potential players to know. All the technical stuff like weekly cards, PPVs, titles, rules/regs, etc can be found on the BRAWL home page at: http://www.geocities.com/TimesSquare/Dungeon/5455 If you've got any questions, just let me know. [name withheld] BRAWL FedHead
