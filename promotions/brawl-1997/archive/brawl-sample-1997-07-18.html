A sample of BRAWL

18 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, BRAWL is in the process of recruiting new wrestlers. Unfortunately it's not going well. There's been over 160 hits on the BRAWL website since I set it back up but only two people have shown any interest. Most of the guys who were in BRAWL before have returned which is a good thing but it's rather difficult to operate a fed with only 8 wrestlers. I haven't had another Sting wanne-be apply so I really can't do another ranting and raving session, which is what I think attracted peoples' attention last time. So I guess I'll just post the two card BRAWL had as a sample of what BRAWL has to offer. If anyone should bother to read these cards and decide that BRAWL is something you'd like to participate in, check out the website at http://www.geocities.com/~brawl
