IWC (Insane Wrestling Corporation) — Roleplay Archive

"A Few Words From Your Next North Eastern Champion" — 13 June 1998

13 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"A Few Words From Your Next North Eastern Champion" >>The scene shows a vacant parking lot to a workout center by the name of "The Q", which is in Memphis, TN. It is very early in the morning. Too early for

anybody to be working out. The birds are chirping, the chilly air is blowing,

and we can see the beautiful sunrise in the background. The quiet morning is

changed when we hear the loud screetch of a tire spinning off the road. All of

a sudden a brand new Dodge Viper speeds up in the parking lot and comes to a

screetching halt in a parking spot right next to the building. The car is

turned off and a man steps out of the vehicle. He has a black and white Nike

wind suit on, a pair of Oakley's covering his eyes, and a black Nike hat on his

head. When he walks from behind the car, we notice that he's got a gym bag in

hand and a cup of coffee in the other. This man is "Dangerous" Dusty Love. He

stops when he sees the camera man and then walks up to him. He then begins to

speak.<<

"Dangerous" Dusty Love: You guys are up pretty early this morning aren't ya? Well, that's good because I have a few things to say right now. And I guess

I'll take this time to say it. First of all, all you IWC shoe shinin' job boys

better listen up and listen up good to what I have to say. The name's Dusty

LOVE, not Dusty BLADE. But when I get'cha in that Battle Royal, you'll know

what the name is because it'll be ringin' in your ears. Dusty Blade, eh? I

expect that kinda disrespect from some of you guys that haven't seen me in

action, but I expected more from you Robby Blaze. I'm sittin' at home watching

the tube and I see you in an interview calling me Dusty Blade. I don't like it

and I don't think it's a bit funny. You know who I am, and you know what I can

do, but it looks like you're gonna have to get a closer look this Monday. You

disrespect me? You ACTING like you don't know my name? Well, looks like you

need to be taught a lesson. And at Monday Madness, I'm gonna take you to

school. I'm a former World Champion. What are you? You're a nothing in my

eyes. You're no threat to me, and you never will be. And you're right, I AM

great. But you're nowhere near better. And you're right, I CAN and WILL beat

you. But you CAN'T and WON'T beat me. Robby, you stepped over the line, pal,

and now I'm gonna have to show you who's boss. You brought it upon yourself.

You said it, and now you'll have to pay for it. Next, I wanna talk about the

Candyman. I hear you running your mouth on TV too. You blab on and on about

how you should be the Champion because you BEAT Nevermind. Well, Nevermind is

a NOTHING compared to me, pal. And during the Battle Royal at Monday Madness,

I'ma show you exactly what I mean. You might not know me and you might not

care for me, but I'm here to stay, and I'm coming to Monday Madness for one

thing and one thing only... To become the next IWC North Eastern Heavyweight

Champion and I'll do it at ALL costs. You can't stop me, Robby Blaze can't

stop me, and better yet, NOBODY can stop me. Listen to what I'm saying because

it's the gospel truth, my friends. Candyman says I don't have a chance of

winning.

>>Dusty takes a sip of his coffee and then laughs a little bit before continueing.<< "Dangerous" Dusty Love: Don't have a chance of winning, eh? Well, I'll make

sure of it that I throw your ass over the top rope and to the floor. I don't

care who you are, if you're across the ring from the "Love Man", you're gonna

be the next victim of the "Love You to Death" (Death Valley Driver) and you can

take it to the bank and cash it because it's the truth. I've been here in the

IWC about two weeks now, and to tell you the truth, I haven't seen not ONE guy

that can beat me in the ring. Not ONE. I feel that I'm way above the best

here in the IWC and once I get the chance, I'll prove it. I'm in a whole

'nother class compared to you peons. Look at this car. Look at my house.

Look at my girlfriend. Most of you guys only DREAMED of having the luxury life

that I do. But I have it because I'm just that good. Take a step back, take a

long look, I am your next North Eastern Champion just like I've said time and

time again. Whether ya like it, or ya don't like it, learn to love it because

I'm the best damn thing going in the IWC.

>>Dusty Love then turns around and walks off. He knocks on the front door of the "Q" and someone lets him in. Dusty then walks into a room entitled, "Locker Room". A few minutes later, he walks back out. He's wearing a "Q" muscle shirt and a pair of Miami Dolphins shorts. He see's the camera as he

sits down on the weight lifting bench. He then begins to speak.<<

"Dangerous" Dusty Love: You guys still here? If you're gonna film me working

out, you outta put it on the market for the chicks so they can dig this bod.

Camera Man: I just wanted to get a few more comments on the Battle Royal that

you're participating in on Monday.

"Dangerous" Dusty Love: That's cool, bro. Got the questions, then shoot. Camera Man: Sure. What do you think of the Hardcore Champion, Al Robertson? "Dangerous" Dusty Love: Well, for starters, Al Robertson is a marked man. And

so are the rest of the guys in the Battle Royal. Robertson hasn't shown me

anything since I've been here. And I don't think he will in the Battle Royal.

I've been Hardcore Champion before and I was a damn good one too. And if I see

him in the Battle Royal, I'll show him why I have been Hardcore Champion when I

put a Hardcore Death Valley Driver on his ass. I ain't comin' to play and I

don't care if he's the Hardcore Champion or not... One way or another, he's

gettin' thrown over the top and to the floor. Just like all the rest of 'em

are gonna be. Dusty Love finally has his chance to showcase in the IWC and

I'ma do it at Monday Madness in the Joe Louis Arena in Detroit, Michigan.

There'll be alot of good wrestlers that I'll have to go through, but that'll be

no problem because there's one difference between them and me - They're good,

but I'm GREAT. And come belltime, I'm gonna prove that once and for all.

>>Dusty gets up and starts putting some heavy duty waits on the bar. He lays down to do a few benches and when he's done he sits back up and begins to speak

once again.<<

"Dangerous" Dusty Love: I know a lot of the guys that are in this thing, and

then again, some of the are new to me. But it makes no difference. Ya got

guys like James Pillow. NEW World Champion. I've stepped in there with him

before and I'm gonna do it again. We've been enemies before and if that's what

it comes to, we'll be enemies again. I might have all the respect in the world

for him but that don't mean I'll take it easy on him. James, you and I both

know each other pretty well and we know each other's moves well too. Last

time, you came out on top. But this time, I'm taken' it when it counts. I'm

sure we'll face off on Monday, and if we do, I'm coming harder, faster, and

tougher than ever. Robby Blaze, like I said, we know each other too. You will

be out wrestled, out fought, out classed, and out DONE in every way, shape, and

form. Blaze, that belt's not yours 'cause it's MINE. Candyman, you're

dreamin', pal. You couldn't lace my boots so you sure as hell can't beat me in

the ring. I'm better than you are and I think EVERYONE's gonna see that on

Monday. Al Robertson, I don't know you, you don't know me, but I think we'll

know each other pretty well after this thing gets done. Everyone and their

momma's gonna be there to see it, and all of 'em are gonna see the crowning of

a new North Eastern Champion by the name of "Dangerous" Dusty Love. Thank you

very much. Now get outta here so I can do my business.

>>The camera man thanks Dusty for his time and then the camera fades out as Dusty Love continues his workout. He's ready to become North Eastern Champion

so watch out.<<
