Lets rundown the complete card . . . ============================== "Danger Zone 98" The McKnicols Arena - Denver, Colorado June 28th, 1998 ============================== [M A I N E V E N T] EMT [c] VS. Nevermind -The IWC world title is on the line in this blockbuster. Nevermind finally gets his big break at the big time as he sqaures off against the IWC world champion,

EMT. EMT has all ready knocked out Nevermind once when he leveled him with his

championship. But, will EMT be able to knock Nevermind down for the three

count?

[The Crowning of the FIRST EVER Can-Am Champ] The Trixxster VS. Twilight Prince -These two men have all ready developed quite a fued. They have been at each other's throats ever since the initiation of the Can-Am Title. The Twilight

Prince, a man of dignity and respect will battle the Trixxster, a man with no

dignity nor respect. Who will win the match and be named FIRST ever IWC Can-Am

Champion?

[Loser's _TITLE_ Leaves IWC] Al Robertson [IWC Hardcore Champion] VS. The Candyman [IWC North Eastern Champion] -This match is quite interesting since after this match is over, the IWC will be minus a title. The Candyman will be defending the North Eastern title

against Al Robertson and his Hardcore Championship. The aftermath of this match

could possibly be better than the match itself. If Robertson loses, many of the

Hardcore specialists will be quite angry and the same thing holds true for the

Candyman and his North Eastern title.

[Floyd Takes On Mental Patient 187] "Brutal" Brian Floyd VS. Jason Kreuger -Ever since the North Eastern Title battle royal, a great fued has escalated between "Brutal" Brian Floyd and Jason Kreuger. This match is definently for

the fan ready to see a nice clean match-up...NOT! This match will be

everything, but that! As two of the most hardcore men in the sport today will

collide. Also, will Jim McDaniel's animosity towards Jason Kreuger play a

factor?

[For the International Title] Drew Mahony [c] VS. "Darkheart" Tommy Rifles -Drew Mahony will have his first ever title defense against Tommy Rifles. Tommy Rifles, now known as "Darkheart" is out on a mission. A mission that starts

with the International title and a mission that will one day, hopefully for

Rifles, end with a world title reign. Will Tommy Rifles be on top of his game

once again or will "The Apocolypse For Gold" remain strong?

[Tag Team Title Match] The Best of the Best [c] VS. The Goths -The Best of the Best will be defending their titles, which they have held for a decent while now, against the number one contenders, the Goths. Will the

Goths have enough in them to dethrone the champs or will the Best of the Best

keep rolling?

[Crowbar Gets Vicious] Steve Vicious VS. Crowbar -The rediscovered "vicious" attitude of Steve Vicious has made the man once known as "Steve Douglas" a much more confident man. Will his rise to the top

begin with the defeat of Crowbar or will Crowbar show the world hes a worthy

challenger?

[Number One Contener Match For Tag Titles] Team Elite VS. Shwag -After much negotiating, the owner of the IWC, Jim McDaniel, has re-signed Shwag. And soon after this great signing, the team of Shwag is put up against

Team Elite. Will Shwag show any ring rust and allow "Glamour For Gold" to take

a step in the right direction? Or will Shwag defeat the always tough team of

Team Elite?

[Number One Contender Match For International Title] "The Sexiest Wrestler Alive" Scottie Extreme VS. "Simply the Best" James Pillow -Recently on Monday Madness, James Pillow defeated the Great Milenko to earn this match. The Great Milenko put up a heck of a fight, but in the end, Pillow

won the match. So, how will Scottie Extreme cope with the absence of Glamour?

Will he be able to carry "Team Elite" and bring gold back to the once dominant

"Glamour For Gold" slogan. Or will James Pillow get a shot at the International

Title?

[Once Great Friends, Now Bitter Enemies] "Dangerous" Dusty Love VS. Robby Blaze -Until this week, Dusty Love and Robby Blaze were good friends. However, the North Eastern title quickly tore apart their friendship. Both men had the goal

of becoming the next North Eastern Champion. But, their desire to win cost them

their friendship. The fued these two men have gotten into could develop into

one of the greatest fueds ever.

[The Backyard Brawl] Hardcore Carey VS. "The Die Hard" Tony Grant -Another match many people are looking forward to is the Back Yard Brawl. Jim McDaniel's corporate pet, Tony Grant, will try and do good for the boss as he

squares off against one time friend, Hardcore Carey. Will the Hardcore Icon

prevail or will the Die Hard reign victorious?

[The Real Deal IS Back] "The Real Deal" Rick Wright VS. The Outsider -Rick Wright will make his long awaited return to the IWC ring and take on the Outsider. The IWC has had a history of unsuccessful returns. Will Rick Wright

travel the opostie direction or will the Outsider get everything back on track?

[Enforcer VS. Excellence] "The Enforcer" Eric Cooper VS. Mr. Excellent -Mr. Excellent, who nearly was in the Can-Am Finals can not be in a great mood right now. And Eric Cooper can not be focussed. He must have his mind on Tommy

Rifles and a match with the Dark Heart. How will Mr. Excellent respond to

defeat? That question will be answered when Mr. Excellent battles Eric Cooper

[Apocolypse VS. 420] The Great Milenko VS. Justin Hellborn -This match could be the beginning of some stable wars in the IWC. Until now, the Apocolypse has been the best stable. However, the 420 Family is back. Which

group will dominate the IWC? This match could be the beginning.
