IWC Brawl For It All The Market Square Arena (Indiana, Indianapolis) May 10, 1998 [M A I N E V E N T] The Gambler [c] Will Defend Against . . . EMT EMT and Jim McDaniel have finally put aside their differences and EMT will once

again step into an IWC ring and in the Main Event. The Gambler will defend his

precious IWC Heavyweight title against the dangerous EMT.

[For The International Strap] "The Golden Boy" Johnny Glamour [c] VS. King Snake With Special Guest Referee Magnus Colby!!! -The newly crowned International Champion, Johnny Glamour will have his first PPV title defense against the veteran, King Snake. Some may argue why King

Snake is getting the oportunity, but the reasons are obvious. Just look at the

job King Snake has done in the IWC, the Colby Cup in particular. Now onto the

other major factor, look who the referee is. The man who has had the longest

reign as champion ever in the IWC, Magnus Colby. Everyone knows the deal

between him and Glamour. Will Colby screw Glamour out of the title or will he

call it right down the middle?

[Tag Team Title Match] Team Elite [c] VS. The Best of the Best -Team Elite were crowned new IWC tag team champions when Shwag retired from the sport of wrestling. Since Team Elite were the #1 contenders for the gold, they

were awarded the titles. However, the IWC was faced with a way to determine who

their opponents would be. So, the IWC had a mini tournament which included the

PPD, the Terrorisers, The Best of the Best, and The Goths. The Best of the Best

won the mini tournament and earned the right to fight Team Elite.

[For The North Eastern Title] Nevermind [c] VS. The Candyman -The Candyman defeated Hardcore Carey to earn the right to fight Nevermind for the North Eastern Strap. And Nevermind defeated the legend, Highway Star, to

become just the second North Eastern Champion ever. Will Nevermind continue the

legacy Highway Star recently lost, or will the Candyman become the third ever

North Eastern Champion.

[Will Milenko See The Light?] The Twilight Prince VS. The Great Milenko -This match was demanded by the wrestlers themselves. The Twilight Prince has tried to convince the Great Milenko to join him on the path to superstardom. However, Milenko has declined on numerous ocasions. This is without a doubt

the last chance The Twilight Prince has in convincing The Great Milenko to join

him. Will Milenko join the Prince or will he decline yet again?

[The Hardcore Title Match] "The One Man Riot" Alain Fiscus [c] VS. "The Die Hard" Tony Grant VS. Scottie Extreme -This match will be a ladder a match or should we refer to it as the Stairway To Hell Match. The Hardcore title will be dangling 20 feet off the ground and

the only way to win is to capture the title which can only be reached via

ladder. You think you have seen hardcore IWC action, you have not seen anything

yet till you see this one.

[The Enforcer VS. The Real Deal] "The Enforcer" Eric Cooper VS. "The Real Deal" Rick Wright -These two have been at each other throats for years now. These two men want to bash each others faces in. This is not going to be a wrestling match, this is

going to be a war. Which man wants it more?

[Rifles Has Revenge On His Mind] "The Greatest of All Time" Tommy Rifles VS. Brett Kreuger IN A BARB WIRE MATCH!!! -Rifles recently flipped out after his recent loss to Jason Krueger. Tommy Rifles was never so embarrassed in his life when he lost to the nutcase, the

mental patient of the IWC, Jason Krueger. Rifles has a lot more then revenge on

his mind, he wants to make Kreuger suffer. And with the ropes as barb wire,

anything could happen!!

[Blade VS. Cobra] Bladerunner VS. "Cobra" Steve Douglas -In a rematch from a recent Monday Madness, Steve Douglas is demanding a match with Bladerunner. The match really had no clean winner when the Great Milenko

interfered. WIll the Cobra finally establish himself as a top wrestler in the

IWC or will Bladerunner ruin Steve Douglas's ambition?

[Revolution VS. Torn] "Simply the Best" James Pillow VS. Destructo -The Stable wars continue as two top members from two of the top stables will fight it out when Destructo fights James Pillow. Will Destructo finally step

out of the shadow of EMT's with a big victory or will James Pillow continue his

winning ways?

[Tag Team Action] The Goths VS. The Perfectly Perfect Duo -In a match which is pretty much a number one contender match, the Goths will do battle with the Perfectly Perfect Duo. Both team coming off a loss will try

and bounce back with a victory. Which team can handle losing ways?

[A Debuting Superstar] "Rock Solid" Chip Newton VS. "The Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh -Chip Newton will make his IWC debut and it will not be a cake walk as he faces a real tough man in Ian Walsh. Many people under estimate the Human Torture

Rack, will Chip Newton make the same mistake and lose his debut match?
