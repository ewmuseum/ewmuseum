The UWO is Looking for a Few Good Handlers!

7 March 2007

Once again, the UWO has decided to spread its wings, and we are back with as strong a base as we had a few years back. However, one thing is missing, and that is a few good handlers to fill in ours holes. A little background information on the UWO, we have been around for 9 years, and are currently being run by E-Fed Veterans [name withheld], [name withheld], [name withheld] and [name withheld]. Since the group of four of us are well versed in e-fedding and the UWO, we are always looking to give everyone an enjoyable time, whether that comes from the shows we produce to the characters we develop, or the feuds we create. The UWO currently holds a weekly show (UWO Overdrive) with a bi- monthly PPV. In the months where a PPV is absent, we hold a Super Show, which is our version of the old WCW Clash of the Champions. Our RP limits are 2 for our weekly show, 3 for our Super Shows, and unlimited for our PPV's. We also currently have 3 singles titles (World, IC, TV) and 1 Tag title active. The current champions are as follows: UWO World Heavyweight Title: Platinum Playa UWO Intercontinental Title: Gavin Williams UWO Television Title: Vacant UWO World Tag Team Champions: Paul Chambliss and Tigerclaw We are looking for handlers who enjoy playing tag teams or singles wrestlers, since we are in need of both. We are an E-MAIL federation, so all of our RPs, shows, cards, and other information is sent via e- mail. Custom wrestlers only. If this sounds like the federation for you, feel free to stop by and give it a look.
