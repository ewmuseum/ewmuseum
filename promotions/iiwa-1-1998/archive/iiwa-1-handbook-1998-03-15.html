Introduction Hello E-Wrestlers, My name is [name withheld] and I have been a part of E-Wrestling nearly two years now and honestly it has been one helluva ride so far. In the past, I have ran a few successful E-Wrestling organizations such as the WOWA which is now part of a well known league called ONE and ACEW which had great success with big stars, but due to lack of help from the handlers it had to shut down.
Currently, I am the Vice-President of the Mainstream Wrestling Federation and a weekly writer for SOW. Although, writing is fun, you do not have the control in which a fed-head has, so I have decided to venture into the world of running an E-Fed once again.
I am really excited about this newest venture into running the IIWA. Due to following many successful leagues and learning the ropes of E-Wrestling I am ready to take on the workload of a running a fed. Of course, I _will_ not be able to make IIWA a success without the support of handlers, writers, and others in this industry. That's all enjoy the packet and I hope you will join the IIWA.
Sincerely, [name withheld] IIWA President 2. What is IIWA? Like I said before IIWA it is an E-Wrestling League. For those of you who are familiar with E-Wrestling I use the word "booked" to describe it. However, it is more than just based on your ability to roleplay it is based on angles fued developement and overall fun.
For those of you who are not familiar with E-Wrestling. Here is the basics, you create a wrestler using the application at the bottom of the handbook, you then roleplay with this wrestler in flashes and gameplans, I give you matches with other "wrestlers,"
you RP against each other, I decide the victor and I write out the matches in a play by play format.
2a. Titles Here are the various championships in IIWA: IIWA World Heavyweight Championship: This title is the _big_ prize everybody in the IIWA is gunning for. It _must_ be defended on a monthly basis against someone in the top five and on just about every supercard.
IIWA World Television Championship: This is the secondary singles championship in the IIWA. The champion is the workhorse of the IIWA, defending the championship every week.
IIWA World Tag Team Championship: The big tag team prize in which all the dynamic duos are heading for. This title is defended on a very regular basis.
2b. Shows IIWA Inside the Squared Circle: On Sunday night, the IIWA treats you with a review of the past card and a preview of the next card. Plus, other features.
IIWA Talkin' Trash: The roleplay ship for IIWA sent out just about everyday. IIWA Grapplefest: The _big_ card in IIWA every Saturday night coming to you live from the IIWA Dome in Orlando, Florida. This is where fueds are started, titles are defended, and everything else in IIWA goes down. 5-7 matches a week.
3. Staff President: [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) About 8-10 writers needed. Why so many writers? I plan to run the writing schedule on a bi-weekly basis. So one week you write the next week you don't write.
