"A new(er) idea in fantasy wrestling.....the FWC"

8 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everyone, my name is [name withheld] and I have played E-wrestling for a while now...I have decided to start a new E-league not to compete with other more estabished e-feds, but to do something different with it. Most E-leagues are based on competition. This one would be more like the "real" pro-wrestling buisness. Yes, each manager gets to pick 3 single wrestlers or tag teams(3 total, 2 single 1 tag, 1 tag 2 single, ect..)which can be real or fantasy wrestlers, but each manager gets to act as a booker for a house card(or numerous house cards). Here is a rundown of the basic rules. 1. As I said each address is allowed 3 single or tag team wrestlers, real or fake, check the bottom or E-mail me for a creation sheet. 2. For each wrestler choose whether he is a main-eventer, mid-carder, or jobber(limit one main-eventer per stable) 3. There will be 3 titles(world, T.V., tag team) 4. 6 PPV's, I will book all of them 5. On PPV's main-eventers have a 75% chance of beating mid-carders, 100% chance of beating jobbers, mid-carders have 75% chance of beating jobbers 6. YOU get to book the house cards, and can let whoever you want win, with certain restricitons. 7. Booking Restrictions: a) no man in your stable may WIN a title at a house card that you book! b) no man in your stable may pull an upset(jobber over mid-carder or main-eventer, mid-carder over main-eventer) 8. Advancement and relegation of wrestlers: a) jobber becomes mid-carder after either 4 straight wins over mid-carder or main-eventer b) mid-carder becomes main-eventer after 3 straight wins over main-eventer c) main-eventer drops down to mid-carder after 3 straight losses to mid-carder or jobber d) mid-carder drops down to jobber after 4 straight losses to jobber 9. a PPV will take place after every 7 house cards. You may send house cards anytime after the league starts. In all cases the earilist card I get takes precidence, and any significant changes(title change, status change ect..) will be e-mailed to later bookers for possible correction. 10. All results are logged into TNM 6.2 11. creation sheet for fantasy wrestlers: Name: Height(be realistic): Weight: Hometown: status(main-eventer, mid-carder, jobber): style of wrestling(brawler, mat technition, luchador ect...those who have TNM know what I want) Heel or face(doesn't matter for jobbers): finisher(and what EXACTLY it is please) 12. PPV listings: #1 Royal Rumble #2 Battle Bowl #3 WrestleMania #4 Summer Slam #5 Survivor Series #6 Great American Bash(ok so there not exactly in the right order..does it really matter? :) 13. cost: DUH!!! nothing!!! 14. Just one other thing that a lot of other presidents/commisioners/whatevers leave out of their rules that I believe is very important. For now and the future(and this goes for anything, not just E-wrestling) PLEASE USE COMMON SENSE!!! People who do stupid things will not be tolorated, this is all about fun, not about stupidity!! Thank you and hope to hear from you, [name withheld] [detail withheld]
