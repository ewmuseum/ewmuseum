SCWA — Rules

Rules and About the SCWA

4 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
About the SCWA The SCWA started from one man's idea. Actually, from one woman's idea backed by one man. Anyway, the story is that a professional wrestler known as the Masked Marauder was in a severe car crash. The accident ended his in-ring career, but not his entire wrestling career. The
Masked Marauder
took off his mask to reveal Lou Thompkins who under the urging of his friend, Stevie
Dawson, opened
the Southern Collegiate Wrestling Association.
Wrestlers in the SCWA are from various colleges from Georgia, Florida, Tennessee,
North Carolina,
South Carolina, Alabama, Mississippi, Kentucky, or Virginia. Wrestlers come from two-year and four-year institutions, both public and private. Each wrestler has a varied background in wrestling whether it be amateur or beginning professional. The wrestlers in the SCWA are not just
part-time players. The wrestlers in the SCWA hope to be recruited by larger wrestling organizations such as WCW,
WWF, or even ECW. Talent in the SCWA come in all shapes and sizes, male and female, extremely beautiful and horrifically grotesque, unbelievably courteous and unimaginably evil. Each person within the SCWA must find their place in the world as well as in the organization. The SCWA hopes to further the careers of all who join their ranks.
Rules for the SCWA: 1. Each handler can have one male singles, one tag team (male or female), and one singles female. The tag format is either one male team or one female team. You can't have both. 2. Wrestler must attend college or just have graduated college. The college must be in one of the following states: Virginia, Kentucky, Tennessee, South Carolina, North Carolina,
Alabama, Mississippi, Georgia, or Florida. 3. All wrestlers must be created. No "Hitman", "Stone Cold", "Heartbreak Kid", "Big Sexy", or any other nickname currently or formerly used in WCW, WWF, or ECW. 4. Each handler must flash at least once during a two week period. If you don't flash within the two week period, you're fired. No suspensions, no fines, just fired. Each card that is sent out will have who needs to flash on it. Be sure to get your name off the list as soon as possible.
5. There are several titles within the SCWA: 1.SCWA Men's Title: This is the top title for a male singles competitor, like a world title. This title must be defended at least once a month.
2.SCWA Men's Regional Title: This is a top contender's title for men, like the US title or the Intercontinental title. This title must be defended twice a month
3.SCWA Men's Cruiserweight Title: This is the top title for cruiserweights. This title must be defended twice a month.
4.SCWA Men's Tag Titles: This is the top title for male tag teams. These titles must be defended at least once a month.
5.SCWA Women's Title: This is the top title for a female singles competitor, like a world title. This title must be defended at least once a month.
6.SCWA Women's Regional Title: This is a contender's title for females. See the men's titles. This title must be defended twice a month.
7.SCWA Women's Tag Titles: This is the top title for female tag teams. These titles must be defended at least once a month.
6. Each match, and ultimately title, is decided by the quality of flashes. If you send a dozen flashes that only say "I'm gonna kick your teeth down your throat," you're probably not gonna win unless your opponent doesn't flash at all. Please remember that if that was all that 'Stone
Cold' Steve Austin ever
said, he'd be very boring and we wouldn't bother watching it. Please say something that will interest your readers. Don't put us to sleep by boring us.
7. The SCWA will not have hard-core matches unless both wrestlers involved request a particular type of match. Of course, weapons will be used at ringside. That's the nature of wrestling. However, don't expect a barb-wire, exploding mat, death match at every card.
8. Flashes should be labeled as the following: [SCWA] Flash for (wrestler's name) 9. Strats will help. Be cautioned. I won't always use them. If I think that your idea is useful, it will be used, but just because you send one in doesn't mean it's gonna be used. 10. Strats should be labeled as the following: [SCWA] Strat for (wrestler's name) 11. Some foul language is allowed. I will allow anything that can be said on network television to be used here. If you need to use other language in your role-plays that would not be allowed on the networks, please use alternates such as: F^$@ you, or S@!$ My D(*&!, okay?
12. Mention of WCW, WWF, and ECW recent events and characters is allowed. After all, the SCWA wrestlers hope to get into these federations one day. They should know what is going on in them.
You just can't be one of the characters in one of those feds. See rule 2. 13. Send all flashes and strats to The President at [detail withheld] 14. There will be a PPV every other month. January: First Night Out March: Irish Mayhem May: Memorial of Pain July: Summer Slugfest September: Back to School Brawl November: Thanksgiving Rumble 15. There will be one card a week. SCWA Tuesday Tussles. It will be a Raw or Nitro type of show. Some interviews, lots of matches and commentary, and a few surprises. 16. There will be two flash shows a week. All flashes for Tuesday Tussles are due on Sunday at 9pm EST. They will be sent out in a flash packet called Monday Mumbles. Any flashes in response to or challenges from Tuesday Tussles can be sent in by Thursday at 9pm EST. They will be published in a flash packet called The Weekend Rumor Mill on Friday nights.
17. All strats are due on Sundays at 9pm EST. 18. Any challenges or requests for special matches should be sent to The President at [detail withheld]
