Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Southern Collegiate Wrestling Association traces its own founding to a car crash: a masked wrestler billed as the Masked Marauder, his in-ring career ended by the wreck, unmasked as Lou Thompkins and, urged on by his friend Stevie Dawson, opened the promotion.

Its own rules restricted the roster to current or recently graduated college wrestlers from nine southern states, barred any ring name “currently or formerly used in WCW, WWF, or ECW,” and built a seven-title ladder split by gender: a Men's and a Women's championship, each with a Regional title and Tag Team titles beneath it, plus a Men's Cruiserweight title.

It planned one weekly card, SCWA Tuesday Tussles, “a Raw or Nitro type of show,” with matches decided by the quality of handlers' own written “flashes” rather than by strategy alone: “If you send a dozen flashes that only say 'I'm gonna kick your teeth down your throat,' you're probably not gonna win.” A bimonthly pay-per-view calendar ran from a January debut it called First Night Out through a planned November closer, Thanksgiving Rumble.

No card, roster, or title change survives past that founding document.
