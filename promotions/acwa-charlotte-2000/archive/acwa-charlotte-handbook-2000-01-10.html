Championships [7]. ACWA Personalities [8]. Wrestler Application [9]. Closing Statements --------------------------------------------------------- * Word from the President... The American Championship Wrestling Alliance follows the trend laid down by the original National Wrestling Alliance... Truly hardcore, traditional wrestling.
In it's glory days, the National Wrestling Allaince was a better 'traditional' wrestling federation than World Championship Wrestling, and a better 'hardcore' wrestling federation than both the World Wrestling Federation and Extreme Championship Wrestling. The difference lies in the fact that to the National Wrestling Alliance, their traditional wrestling was more hardcore than anything found today. We will have the specialty matches similar to the World Wrestling
Federation, but ofcourse we will have many more traditional matches without bloodloss or anything like that. However, every time you step into the ring in the American Championship Wrestling Alliance, you run the risk of serious injury. As for championships, the competitors in the American Championship
Wrestling Alliance understand that these titles are something to be honored and fought for with every fiber of their being. Here, the champion does not give credibility to the title, the title gives credibility to the champion! You are not walking into this federation and getting a title without a fight. You are not going to become the number one contender without a little old-fashioned blood, sweat, tears, and dedication... but that's what 'real' professional wrestling is all about, isn't it?
[name withheld] President, ACWA --------------------------------------------------------- * The People behind the Scenes... The ACWA is operated by two men: [name withheld] and [name withheld]. Both of us are willing to give what it takes to make this federation one of the major promotions on the internet. Personally, I have had a nice taste of success with the American Championship Wrestling Alliance before, having originally opened the federation in August of 1998, and would love to see it again... Myself and
Dave are looking to take the ACWA right into the new Millennium on top! --------------------------------------------------------- * Codes of Conduct... There are several 'no-brainer' rules in e-wrestling, and I am hoping you are atleast somewhat aware of what I mean when I say that. Therefore, I will only cover those rules which the ACWA stresses more than any others. However, these
'no brainers' should be recognized as unwritten rules, and should be followed. [1]. This is number one, because it is the most important... have some fun! If you come into the American Championship Wrestling Alliance looking for nothing but fun and excitement from roleplaying , as well as good angles and cards, then that is exactly what you will get.
[2]. This promotion is run through a closed-system format. Meaning that all flashes will be sent to myself and Dave, and compiled into the ACWA's flash show: 'ACWA: Talk Zone'. The maximum number of flashes you are allowed on a single show is one, therefore if you feel like writing quite a bit, make it into one flash. If you have any questions about this, you can contact me.
[3]. If you have a match, you _must_ roleplay to win. It does not matter to me, if you are the biggest e-wrestling 'name' on the internet. If you do not flash, then I guarantee you will not win. Simple as that. I do not say this to be mean, but roleplays are the threads that keep a federation together. Dave and I can only do so much to keep this place running, flashing is what you guys can do to keep us from closing down.
[4]. There is no specific length your roleplay has to be, as long as it is quality. If I were going to guess, I'd say 20-35 lines is a good average. This gives you enough dialogue to get your point across, but not too much to where you repeat yourself over and over... These are not official minimums and maximums, they are just suggested guidelines.
If you have any questions about these, email Dave or myself. The ACWA, primarily, does not go around suspending everyone. We will definitely be quite lenient, but if someone disobeys to an extreme extent, they will be punished...
See, now the rules werent that bad were they? --------------------------------------------------------- * ACWA Events The American Championship Wrestling Alliance has signed an exclusive deal with the United States Satellite Broadcasting network, to show all weekly cards on the Home Box Office. We pride ourselves on delivering quality professional wrestling to homes nation-wide once a week. Our mix of red hot wrestling action, with twisting and turning plots leaves the viewer on the edge of their seat!!
[a]. The ACWA's main brodcast is known as 'ACWA: Breakdown'. Hosted by Shannon Young and Ian Hayes, two of the top colour commentators in the business today. Their chemistry on the microphone is fantastic, and you will love to hear them argue with eachother every week. There are normally four or five matches a week on this program, with the possibility of interviews from some of the federation's top stars. Title defenses are usually of the Television, as well as the Lightheavyweight or Tag Team Titles. Only during a non-Special Edition month is the World Heavyweight Title defended here.
[b]. In the American Championship Wrestling Alliance, our pay-per-views [known as Special Editions] are second to none. Everyother month, the American
Championship Wrestling Alliance holds a Special Edition card... this is where all feuds will end, begin, or become even more personal!! We gurantee that when you witness an ACWA pay-per-view, that you are seeing the best professional wrestling possible. Try getting that kind of a gurantee from some other federation!! [note: the first Special Edition is tenatively scheduled for
Friday night, February 25th, and is entitled 'ACWA: The Resurrection'.] [c]. To go along with the weekly television shows, and bi-monthly Special Editions, the ACWA also puts on several house show events throughout the month... During these shows, you will always see the best action in thw world today, which can only be brought to you by the American Championship Wrestling
Alliance. While major title matches are a rarity, and title changes are even moreso, they do occasionally happen... and since not all of our fans can attend every house, we give you the full results during 'ACWA: Talk Zone' every
Sunday, Tuesday, and Thursday night [note: these results will not be in full writeup fashion. just brief descriptions of each match].
--------------------------------------------------------- * Roleplays and Angles... I mentioned roleplays already in the rules, but let me touch upon them once again. Roleplays should be done entirely in character. They should not be for the most part shoot comments. I personally dont mind a shoot roleplay every once in a while if it is in good fashion, but let's not get too carried away. I give an inch, let's not try to take a mile. ACWA has a one roleplay per show limit. I am not forcing anyone to do a roleplay, but trust me, if you have a match and do not roleplay for it, you will _not_ win! Now if you don't have a match, I would still ask you to do a roleplay, just because it surely can not hurt you, but can only help you. I see you trying even though you dont have to and I smile and your name just might get a nice push. Everything you get out of the ACWA revolves around what you put in.
[a]. 'ACWA: Talk Zone' is a program that airs on Sunday, Tuesday, and Thursday nights, and features comments from ACWA superstars as well as a rundown of recent house show results, and hypes upcoming weekly shows and|or Special
Editions... Hosted by Shannon Young and Ian Hayes, you'll not only get to hear from your favorite ACWA superstar, but you'll also be privy to any rumors circulating throughout the American Championship Wrestling Alliance...
Definitely not a show you want to miss. Now, I can honestly say angles are my favorite things in any federation. I am going to say right now, sometimes an angle, if it is good may make you lose a match even if you did have the better roleplay. Sometimes that is just the way the cookie crumbles. But hey, it is all in good fun. Now on angles ... if you are a wrestler in this fed and feel you are going nowhere and need a good angle or feud with someone let either Dave or myself know. We would be more than happy to help you. Hell, that is one of the main reasons we are here. We want our wrestlers to succed as well as this promotion.
--------------------------------------------------------- * Championships... Each title has permanent stat bonuses. Television and Lightheavyweight are lesser, while Heavyweight is larger. Tag Teams have their own bonuses, seperate from the singles division... If you lose a title, a rematch will be signed for two to three weeks after the time you lose it. The rematch will be against whomever holds the title, not the person you lost it to, some times in a Triple
Threat Match, but usually in a one on one situation... Title holders do not have to accept anyone's challenges, however it would be in your best interest to accept reasonable ones. As advice, it isn't a good idea to go straight for the Heavyweight Title. Start with the Television Title, then work up to the higher belt. The more you work, the better you get. That is a huge rule here...
World Heavyweight Title: Quite possibly the most prestigious title in professional wrestling today. Such men as 'Iceman' Shawn Hayes, Angel Reaper, and Mark Andrews have all held this gold around their waists at one time or another. To capture this belt, is to state you you are among the elite in the industry today. It is defended atleast once a month, usually during a pay-per-view. If there is not a pay-per-view that month, then the title must be defended on a broadcast of Breadown, usually the last one of the month.
World Tag Team Titles: May be the most prestigious title in tag team wrestling. To capture this belt, is to state you are among the best of the best in tag team wrestling that the industry has to offer. The belts are defended twice a month, including pay-per-view defenses. If there is not a pay-per-view that month, then the title will be defended on a broadcast of Breakdown.
Television Title: This is definitely an endurance title. The holder of this title definitely has the most grueling schedule of all other champions, as the gold must be defended once a week, during ACWA Breakdown. Although the schedule is a hard one to keep up with, winning this gold throws you into the driver's seat... as you become the number one contender for the World Heavyweight Title.
Lightheavyweight Title: The most prestigious title in professional wrestling, for anyone under 225lbs. To capture this belt, is to state you are among the best of the best cruiserweights in the industry today. It is defended twice a month, not including super card defenses. If there is not a pay-per-view that month, then the title will be defended on a broadcast of Breakdown sometime during the month.

matches will take place, as well as anything else concerning the wrestling talent in the ACWA. [name withheld] can either be your best friend, or your worst enemy... he doesn't let any of 'the boys' push him around.
Vice President, [name withheld]: Handles many of the problems occurring within the American Championship Wrestling Alliance, alongside President [name withheld]. Enforces the rules of the ACWA to the fullest extent of his ability... Smith doesn't care who you are, or how important you are to the company. If you break the rules, you're gonna have to deal with the Vice President.
Commentator, Shannon Young: Possibly one of the best play by play men in professional wrestling... A damnnear mirror image of Jeff Hardy, this 25 y/o kid has been dreaming of becomging a professional wrestling since he was in his early teens, and is now the main man behind the microphone for 'Breakdown'; He is liked by virtually all wrestlers and wrestling fans. He pulls no punches, and excells in telling it like it is. There are times when he can get caught up in the moment, and his mouth gets him introuble. But, to get to Shannon you have to go through his color man...
Commentator, Ian Hayes: The former NAIWF superstar has decided to hang up the tights, for a seat behind a microphone alongside Shannon Young for 'Breakdown'.
Although he has been a solid face throughout his career, he doesn't mind cheering for the heel in some situations. Hayes isn't afraid to let the people listening at home know his feelings towards any particular athlete. It's not like anyone is going to say something about it... at 6' 3", 258lbs. 'Hybrid'
can pretty much do and say whatever he pleases. Interviewer, Rick Douglas: If you want a story, this is the man to get it for you. Douglas is one hell of a reporter. There are very few wrestlers in the back that can avoid his onslaught of questions... He gets pushed around a bit when he's got a microphone and camera-crew with him, but this never discourages him from giving it his best shot at getting the big scoops.
