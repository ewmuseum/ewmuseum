Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Empire Wrestling Federation opened on 9 July 1997 as a booked promotion for original characters only — “no Steve Austin's or Bret Hart's, sorry” — recruiting through its own website rather than by open newsgroup application.

Two more recruitment posts followed within days, both from its own president, restating the same pitch: fill out the join form, send an audition roleplay, and the federation promised “the best wrestling and dependability of any e-wrestling federation.”

No card, roster, or result survives beyond these three announcements. Whether the Empire Wrestling Federation ever ran a show isn't in the surviving record.
