What should I do? ===================================================== 1. What is the purpose of this fed? DVW stands for Desert Valley Wrestling. The brainchild of former wrestler turned business proprietor, Rob Magnum. DVW is an independent fed. No true wrestling company begins at the worldwide level in days without the proper time, energy, and crew. We are based in the quaint, yet rundown Tarnished Star bar/saloon in Purgatory, New Mexico: a small town just west of Truth or Consequences... created as a pit stop for bikers in the late 1960's. Like I said, it's small and quaint. If you want to see guys jumping off of jumbotrons or powerbombing people off of the top of a tank.... you came to the wrong place. 2. Who runs this thing? (Fantasy) The DVW is owned by former UEW Ultimate and GFWA World Champion, Rob Magnum. For several years in the late 90s, Magnum was one of the marquee names in the wrestling industry. However, due to a series of crippling injuries, and several failed comebacks, Magnum left the sport having realized his time was up. For several years after, Magnum rested on his laurels. He watched as time slipped slowly passed and an industry he once helped build slowly crumbled. He watched as every great federation deteriorated into nothingness. Leaving behind a ghost town of memories that rivals his new home in Purgatory, NM in both despair and emptiness. Time didn't only gnaw at Rob Magnum's mind, it gnawed at his heart. To pass the time, Magnum used some of the money he had made in wrestling and re-opened an abandoned bar by the name of The Tarnished Star Saloon. It was nothing special, but it was something to work on, something to fix. While this helped quelch Magnum's thirst for something to pass the time, a hunger slowly burned his soul. Unexpectedly, construction began in late 2006 on a new addition to the Tarnished Star Saloon, followed by a brief press conference in which Magnum stated his plans to open Desert Valley Wrestling. The construction was completed on March 22nd, 2007 on the new 300 seat capacity wrestling hall. 3. Who run this thing? (IRL) [name withheld], obv. Some of you know me, some of you didn't have the opportunity. I was in e-wrestling from 1996-2000 and again from 2002-2003. I've grown to miss not just the writing, but also working with the different handlers. I've seen an increase in the demand of e-wrestling, as it appears my feels aren't isolated to my demented mind. I just don't see a whole lot out there and would like to create something memorable. I pride myself on timeliness, and plan on having the cards come out on time for the most part, at most a day or two late, but you won't be waiting until June 5th, 2028 for the May 3rd, 2007 card to come out. While I respect that quality is paramount, I also feel that reliabiity is as well. And I do a diservice to the handlers to delay the release of the writing they worked hard on. As for my "fed head" qualifications. I was a writer for UEW, I-Slash, and NBW. I also ran the short lived LVPW, that alot of people seemed to enjoy. I had some family things happen at the time that took me away from LVPW, but I always enjoyed working on the shows and look forward to workin' on this endeavor as well. 4. What are the titles here? The DVW currently sanctions ONE championship, and I'm hoping that at some point in time we'll be able to put out a tag division. +o+o+o+o Desert Valley Title +o+o+o+o Reserved for only the best, only the bravest, only the _toughest_. The DVW Title is not only the top prize, it's the only prize. If you want respect, if you want glory, then you'll want this belt. There is no second best. 5. What are the cards? "Between Heaven and Hell" The big show. Hosted bi-weekly by the owner "No Worries" Rob Magnum and accompanied by Purgatory's own, Terry Thunder. Every two weeks the town of Purgatory empties into The Tarnished Star Saloon to fulfill their bloodlust. BWaW will consist of 3-4 matches, depending on the match, and overall length of the card. Run on local channel 102, and broadcast on alternating Sundays. "The Road to Hell" On the off week the wrestlers still need to work, and still need to make money. As a former wrestler, Magnum is well aware of this. So DVW will release the results of their weekly house show in summary format. Be sure to read, because while not broadcast on television, important events can still occur. DVW Update This'll include things like rankings, rumors, card lineups, injuries, roster, etc. Make sure you read this one if you want to know what's going on. If you enjoy being a clueless buffoon as much as I do, feel free to skip it. Super cards Due to our size, DVW won't be housing PPVS. But we will be having quarterly supercards. Hopefully, someday, with proper growth we will be able to have PPVs Until then, we will be theming our supercards around the current goings on. 6. Yo, what I got to do in this fed?! - ROLE PLAYS: Ha-ha! Role plays don't exist in the DVW. So, you may ask how we go about things here, well, on cards. Like we've stated before, DVW is not a big time fed, production costs would not allow us to be able to film numerous clips and produce an extra show until revenue and fan base grows. You are required to do an on card if you have a match, simple. Strats and angle enhancements are also encouraged but not required for winning. - SWEARING: We really can't afford to be paying the FCC for harsh language, so please *bleep* yourself. - KAYFABE: Don't mention "real" wrestling. That's all we ask... don't mention it. Not that "real" wrestling is something anybody would want to mention anymore, but still ... DVW is real wrestling... the WWE does not exist. It's not hard to comprehend. Also, let's keep business in the DVW about the DVW. Only if feuds call for it, should you bring up talk about the XBC federation... got it? - STRATS: Strats are important in the DVW. Will it replace an RP in booking weight? No. But it definitely can be the difference maker in a close match. I don't expect strats with counter for every move, but a good general offense, defense, mindset, spots, and finish are worlds of help for the writers. Just so long as there is nothing about "at the 13:21 minute mark, have me hit a gourdbuster". Cause I will proceed to tear my eyes out. - ANGLES: The backbone to any fed is good angles. Without them, you've just got a bunch of imaginary guys rolling around on the mat. We want to know why your guy wants to win, why he hates his opponent, why he wears an eye patch in the ring, why he freaks out when his opponent goes after his knee. Its stuff like that that makes people enjoy your character's work, and makes them want to see more of him/her/them. Angles aren't "required", but without them, you'll be as lost as an Amish farmer in Manhattan. Booking Policy The DVW is a BOOKED fed with the decisions behind made on basis of Oncards/strats/angles/heat. The Dalai Lama wont come to us in a spiritual session and let us know who the heavens and spirits believe we should choose as the winner. If you lose, well, its probably because the other person looked a li'l better to us. If you win, hey.. good job. But please, if you want to know why you lost, talk to us. No bitching. I think we'll all done this in our times in e-w, and I'm sure we've all grown up now and we can discuss things as adults... well, maybe. 7. Do you need help? Where can I help? I don't really mind either way, if you want to help great. But the biggest help would be having fun. 8. Okay I want to join. What should I do? Fill out the application... And send it in.
