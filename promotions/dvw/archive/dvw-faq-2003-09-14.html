What is the purpose of this fed? DVW stands for Desert Valley Wrestling. The brainchild of former wrestler turned business proprietor, Connor Reed. DVW is an independent fed. No true wrestling company begins at the worldwide level in days without the proper time, energy, and crew. We are based in the quaint, yet rundown Tainted Star bar/saloon in Purgatory, New Mexico: a small town just west of Truth or Consequences... created as a pit stop for bikers in the late 1960's. Like I said, it's small and quaint. If you want to see guys jumping off of jumbotrons or powerbombing people off of the top of a tank.... you came to the wrong place. Oh and the major thing, only two people are allowed to have egos in this federation... And that's the two who run it. So, keep it to yourself or find the door hitting you on the way out. K? 2. Who runs this thing? (Fantasy) The DVW was founded by Connor Reed in 2003. Following a shattered kneecap that ended his promising independent career.. The former wrestler, opened up the Tarnished Star bar and saloon to get away from the world of wrestling but soon found out, that his life really was wrestling and thought of nothing else but to bring wrestling back to its roots, pure entertainment. And what better place, then the bar, in which he had a ring constructed in the back, to house the small federation. 3. Who run this thing? (IRL) That would be the creative geniuses that are [name withheld] and [name withheld]. Chris and Carlos have been in this game we call e-wrestling since 1996 meeting in the now defunct LWWF. Yes, Chris and Carlos have known each other for 7 years and we've collaborated before on numerous angles, feuds and federations. Experience? Chris has run the BWA before and Carlos was known for running the MWF, LEGENDS and HOWL back in the heyday of e-w :) Between the two, they've racked up numerous e-w titles, numerous feuds/angles of their respective federations and a history of cooperation with other handlers. 4. What are the titles here? The DVW currently sanctions two championships, and in the future tag team titles. These tag belts with be fought for and won, by different combinations of our singles roster. 4.1. Desert Valley Title The Desert Valley Title is the big dog title in the DVW. It's what everyman in the federation strives for and is defended once every 60 days. 4.2. Tarnished Star Title The DVW's secondary title and named after the home of the DVW. Having this title makes you the #1 contender to the World Title (technically), and also a target for a lot of people. 5. What are the cards? 5.1. "Between Heaven and Hell" The big show. Hosted bi-weekly by Connor Reed and "California Gold" George Finley, this card shows the DVW at it's finest. This is where the really big matches between super cards go, and where the major angles are pushed. Usually every match is in a full write up form with each show usually ranging from 4-6 matches. 5.3. DVW Update Not really a show as much as a mailing. This'll include things like rankings, rumors, card lineups, injuries, roster, etc. Make sure you read this one weekly if you want to know what's going on. 5.4. Super cards We won't be doing pay per views until the DVW has grown in fan base and popularity and then we will expand to the campus of the University of New Mexico for the major super cards. 6. What I got to do in this fed? 6.1. Role plays Ha-ha! Role plays don't exist in the DVW. So, you may ask how we go about things here, well, on cards. Like we've stated before, DVW is not a big time fed, production costs would not allow us to be able to film numerous clips and produce an extra show until revenue and fan base grows. You are required to do an on card if you have a match, simple. Strats and angle enhancements are also encouraged but not required for winning. - NO SWEARING: No swearing, plain and simple. Not even bleeping them out... because WE CAN'T AFFORD TO HAVE THE CENSORS WATCHING US! Although in a bar, Connor Reed does not like his wrestlers using foul language. And I'm sure you can get your characters over without having to use swearing. - NO ATTACKS: This means no beating up other wrestlers, managers, referees, announcers, etc. Get permission first. - KAYFABE: Don't mention "real" wrestling. That's all we ask... don't mention it. DVW is real wrestling... the WWE does not exist. It's not hard to comprehend. Also, let's keep business in the DVW about the DVW. Only if feuds call for it, should you bring up talk about the XBC federation... got it? - FORMAT: If you can't spell, at least use spell check. If you donât have great grammar, use something like word to fix it. The more work we have to do editing, the more time we are losing on working on the card. - LIMITS: 1 on card which serves as a type of locker room rematch piece on the show. If you don't have a match, no need for an on card unless you really need an in-ring. If you want to make sure you get a spot, let us know ahead of time you'll need one. 7.2. Strats Strats are semi-important in the DVW. Will it replace a RP in booking weight? No. But it definitely can be the difference maker in a close match. I don't expect strats with counter for every move, but a good general offense, defense, mindset, spots, and finish are worlds of help for the writers. Just so long as there is nothing about "at the 13:21 minute mark, have me hit a gourdbuster". Cause I will proceed to tear my eyes out. 7.3. Angles The backbone to any fed is good angles. Without them, you've just got a bunch of imaginary guys rolling around on the mat. We want to know why your guy wants to win, why he hates his opponent, why he wears an eye patch in the ring, why he freaks out when his opponent goes after his knee. Its stuff like that that makes people enjoy your character's work, and makes them want to see more of him/her/them. Angles aren't "required", but without them, you'll be as lost as an Amish farmer in Manhattan. 8. Booking Policy The DVW is a BOOKED fed with the decisions behind made on basis of Oncards/strats/angles/heat. The Dalai Lama wont come to us in a spiritual session and let us know who the heavens and spirits believe we should choose as the winner. If you lose, well, its probably because the other person looked a li'l better to us. If you win, hey.. good job. But please, if you want to know why you lost, talk to us. No bitching. I think we'll all done this in our times in e-w, and I'm sure we've all grown up now and we can discuss things as adults... well, maybe. 9.
