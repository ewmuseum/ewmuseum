CuseTroy — Apr 1, 2009 at 5:05 AM As of this very moment, sign-ups for the 2009 TEAM Invitational Tournament have been officially CLOSED!
We've got 36 competitors lined up, and brackets will be ready to go by the time they're announced LIVE via AIM chat tomorrow night at 8 p.m. EST. If I don't know you're AIM SN already, post it here or PM it to me and I'll send you an invite to the chat once I'm on tomorrow night.
As far as RP schedule goes, because of the 36-man field, there's going to be four play-in matches to claim the remaining spots. To expediate everything, those matches will have a four-day RP period from Thursday to Sunday, and as soon as I have the judging in, I'll do a quick EPICENTER with the results and we'll start with the first two brackets the next day. The opening round will be staggered, with two regions going one week and the other two going the next, but after that, as has become tradition, everyone will go at once.
Thanks for signing up, everyone, and I'm excited to get this thing going. See you online later tonight!
