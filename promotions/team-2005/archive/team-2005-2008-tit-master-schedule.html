2008 TEAM Invitational Tournament — Master Schedule and Full Bracket

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TH — Mar 1, 2008 Your 2008 TiT Regions: LONDON (O2 Arena, London, England), ORLANDO (Arena of Champions, Orlando, FL), MINNEAPOLIS (Target Center, Minneapolis, MN), LOS ANGELES (Staples Center, Los Angeles, CA)

March 3 - Live Bracket Announcements via AIM Chat, 8:00 PM EST

March 4 - March 10 - First round RP for the LONDON (Liacouras Center, Philadelphia, PA and Patrick Gym, Burlington, VT) and LOS ANGELES (Maples Pavilion, Stanford, CA and McCarthy Athletic Center, Spokane, WA) regions

March 11 - March 17 - First Round RP for the ORLANDO (Coleman Coliseum, Tuscaloosa, AL and Moody Coliseum, Dallas, TX) and MINNEAPOLIS (Breslin Student Events Center, East Lansing, MI and Hammons Student Center, Springfield, MO)

March 19 - March 25 - Second Round RP for ALL FOUR regions (same venues)

March 28 - April 3 - Sweet Sixteen RP for ALL FOUR regions (see first note about venues)

April 5 - April 11 - Regional Final RP for ALL FOUR regions

April 14 - RP for TEAM SuperShow VIII: Final Four '08 begins (Phillips Center, Atlanta, GA)

Around April 23 or so - RP for TEAM SuperShow IX: Merritt Trophy '08 begins (Phillips Center, Atlanta, GA)
