Awards, title shots, props, general things Cup related

9 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TH — 12-09-06, 09:28 AM Alright, here's the thread where I give out Dupree Cup related title shots! Of course, as was announced in-character, the only official award given out is the Most Valuable Wrestler, and we have Co-MVWs in HAL and Spoiler. Please give these two guys a round of applause. With their awards, each guy will get a CoC shot at their whim.
Now, for the unofficial awards... The [name withheld] Memorial... wait a second, he's still alive? Well, I'm still calling it a Memorial Award, dammit! - NAPW as a whole. You guys came out and promoed early and often for the Cup matches. I don't think I saw as much fedwide enthusiasm for the tourney as I saw with you guys, and that needs to be rewarded. So, you guys will get an automatic berth into the four way match that will decide who wins the TEAM Challenge Championship, and taking that berth will be Ravager.
The Broad-Shoulders Award - This one goes to Jason Payne, who carried Team NFW to the playoffs with little help whatsoever until Nova showed up. Talk about drive, Jason came out and busted his ass in every round, whether it was against LoC or RDJW. For his efforts, he will receive a spot in a #1 Contenders Match for the CoC, as well a guaranteed shot at the Challenge Championship on NFW programming after it's been decided.
The Frank Gore Award for the Chronically Underrated - This award goes to James Irish, who didn't drop a single decision in his run in the Cup. His efforts were so under-the-radar that I didn't even realize how well he was doing until [name withheld] reminded me. Not just for his in-ring, in-character work though, because James also had the task of herding cats trying to get his A1E teammates in order. For his efforts, James will receice a spot in a #1 Contenders Match for the CoC (against Jason Payne for those keeping score at home!) and a guaranteed shot at the Challenge Championship on A1E programming after it's been decided.
The Jockstrap Award for Athletic Support - While Payne carried Team NFW early, he got a huge boost later from Nova, who had a hell of a performance in the Cup Playoff round in support of Payne. Had they had Nova all along, maybe they would have been the 3 seed instead of LoC. Who knows. However, I do know this. For receiving this award, Nova gets a spot in the four way match to decide the TEAM Challenge Championship.
Best Supporting Wrestler on a Championship Team or Event Award - This one goes to Chaos, who came out and brought the pain each week. His enthusiasm was a big help for NEW along the way, and for his efforts, he receives a spot in the four way match to decide the TEAM Challenge Championship.
The It's Too Early To Think of a Witty Name Award - This one goes to IrishRed, who was the second best player on the second best team. For his efforts, he's also going to get a spot in the four way match to decide the TEAM Challenge Championship.
Once again, thank you to everyone who participated in the Cup. I feel that it was a success, outside of the long wait to get the last card up, but nothing's perfect. I couldn't have done it without you, and in my book, you're all winners.
