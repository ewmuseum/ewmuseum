TH — 09-20-06, 04:59 PM - The most pressing matters come first. Everyone please wish [name withheld] (or [name withheld] if you're Katz :p) a very happy birthday today. She's been invaluable to TEAM ever since I got this thing off the ground with the TEAM Invitational Tournament, so in return, please grace her with spiced rum and Derek Jeter voodoo dolls.
- Another one of those "most pressing matters..." Week 3 is in the books. After I get the judging from my other judge, we'll be ready to roll. I anticipate a writeup on Friday, Saturday at the latest. Then, we'll settle any remaining ties if there are any and it's off to the playoffs.
- Looking ahead past the Cup, we're tenatively set to have two SuperShows between the end of the Cup and the beginning of the... get ready for it... 2nd Annual TEAM Invitational Tournament! I figure having two SuperShows will give us enough time to tie up the CoC program between D! and Mr. A! without letting it drag until into next year, and it'll give me a chance to be able to introduce two more secondary titles. Yep, that's right, two. But neither will be regular, straight up titles. Nope, gimmick mania!
I'm tenatively looking to schedule TEAM SuperShow II: FREE FOR ALL~! in mid-to-late October and TEAM SuperShow III: Bracketology for the first week of November. Then, we'll get started with the TEAM Invitational.
For SSII, we're going to have a double main event. The first one, well, I have to talk to the participants, but it'll have something to do with the build to Battle of the Exclamation Points II. The other one will be the first ever TEAM Free For All Championship Match. Basically, the format is anyone who wants to challenge for the title is able to. The match is a giant battle royale. The title will always be defended in that format. I'm hoping the FFA Title will attract a wide variety of people from several different feds and be the place to go when you just wanna RP for RPing's sake.
For SSIII, I'm hoping to have that epic CoC Match as well as use the show as a conduit to announce the brackets for TEAM Invitational II. Plus, there'll be a third title to be announced and contested, but more on that later. I want to keep that a surprise :)
- I would just like to take this opportunity to thank everyone who's come out and RPed for any TEAM event thus far in the last year.
