SuperShow IX Extra: This Just In...

19 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TH — Jun 19, 2008 FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

Atlanta, GA --

Word has leaked that another match has been added to SuperShow IX. Sources say the ink on the contract is dry, but they haven't been able to identify the superstars involved. One rumor persists that one of them is looking to have his final match ever against a close and personal friend. Speculation is that it's the long-awaited Dan Ryan/Lindsay Troy match, but there are conflicting reports from spies across the spectrum of FW feds that are reporting that it could be anyone from Big Dog to Eli Flair. We will keep you posted as details come out.
