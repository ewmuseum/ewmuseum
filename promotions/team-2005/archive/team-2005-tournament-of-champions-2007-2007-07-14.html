TH — 07-14-07, 03:08 PM [Fade into an image of D! holding up the Championship of Champions belt at the Wachovia Spectrum, fans cheering him in the background.]
TOM HOLZERMAN V/O: Last year, a streaking comet obscured in the beginning by bright-shining stars burst onto the scene and staked his claim as the best of the best. It was as unexpected as it was heartwarming, a story unlike any in 2006.
[Cut to YORI YAKAMO, JR. leveling PROFESSOR TREMENDOUS and the TEAM T MIDGET with a Van Midgetator.]
TH V/O: This year, there are no real unknowns. [Cut to RAVAGER leveling NOVA with a headbutt inside the cage at SuperShow I.] TH V/O: This year, men known all over the galaxy compete for a chance at immortality. [Cut to a promotional shot of CHRISTIAN LIGHT, KHALID JAD and DAVID PAIGE.] TH V/O: This year, the stars shine brightest. [Cut to ROCKO DAYMON DDTing BRYAN STORMS.] TH V/O: This year, the Tournament of Champions leaves a crater in the middle of Chicago. Which heavenly body will be the last one shining in the sky? Find out next as EPICENTER comes live from the United Center!
[Cue up "Down" by Stone Temple Pilots. Cut to JASON PAYNE, shouting out in primal fury with a man's blood as his war paint. Cut to MR. AMAZING! delivering Amazing Grace to JAKE HIX. OVER THE TOP ROPE! Entertainment goes crashing to the floor, and folks, that looks like it hurts. Mr. E was okay though. He is a trained professional you know.

Douglas didn't even see that one coming. Just goes to prove that the Old Dogs know more than their fair share of battle royale tricks. Remember, Big Dog is a two-time winner of the Pier Six Brawl in A1E.
[Cut to Big Dog with Dusk on his shoulder.] TH V/O: Dusk seems to be the whipping boy here, taking the ride to the Dog Pound, but a funny thing happened on the way. The door swung open and Dusk escaped. He slides right off of Dog's back and shoves the A1E Champion into the ropes. Dog spreads out his arm on the way and just happens to knock Larry Tact over the top rope. Score an elimination by Dusk using Big Dog as a weapon.
[Cut to Ravager and Nova dueling it out.] TH V/O: Flashback to the first Challenge Championship match, where Ravager bested Nova to win the strap he has now. Nova still remembers that, and after being quiet for most of the match, he reverses this Ravager Irish whip into one of his own, sending the White Collar Assassin to the floor with a back body drop.
[Cut to Dusk charging at Professor Tremendous.] TH V/O: Finally, last elimination, and this one's a doozy. Dusk is going to try and lariat the Good Professor over the top rope. HE does, but the momentum takes Dusk with him. Both men carom over the top and fall to the floor, a crumpled heap of three bodies. Three you might ask? The third body is that of Team Tremendous member Angleholic Anonymous. The replay shows that both men came crashing to the floor at the same time, but Dusk's feet hit first, while the Professor's didn't at all. That's because Angleholic Anonymous seemingly laid out to save his leader from certain elimination. It was later revealed, however, that he was only returning to his spot ringside and tripped over his bootlaces. Regardless, he saves Professor Tremendous and shuttles him off to the final eight, along with Tchu, Bryan Storms, Big Dog, Kin Hiroshi, Nova, Christian Light and Rocko Daymon.
[Cut back to the EPI-desk, with Steve Murray and an Ashley Scott inset.] SM: Some of you might be wondering where Ashley Scott was in all these proceedings.
