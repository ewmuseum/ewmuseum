TEAM Presents... SuperShow II, Live from Austin!

8 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TH — Jan 8, 2007 TEAM's first venture into the Lone Star State will see two new Champions crowned, scores settled and plenty of heated action, coming to you live from the Frank Erwin Center in the state's capital of Austin!
When D! was stripped of the Championship of Champions, shockwaves were sent across the wrestling world. What made the Face of New Alberta Pro Wrestling go so bad? Was he just trying to be a bad-ass, or was he playing the TEAM crowds the whole time? Or, in a shocking and uncharacteristic case, was he trying to get himself stripped of the title rather than face
Mr. Amazing!? Whatever the case is, he's gone and now in order for Amazing! to claim the title he thinks he deserves, he has to go through former Global Wrestling World Champion
Jake "The Big Dawg" Hix . Hix, undoubtedly angered after losing his title to John "The Prophet" Adams, will be looking for gold to replace his lost Championship belt. Mr. A!, who hasn't been seen from since his fall from the scaffold against IrishRed at A1E's Golden Dreams, will be looking to return to the ring in a big way.
In the second main event, there's going to be a FREE FOR ALL~! deep in the heart of Texas! All comers are invited to compete in the first ever
FREE FOR ALL~! match. Anyone can show up to the party in this no holds barred, over the top battle royale. There are no rules, so the action is bound to get out of hand, and the winner will be awarded the TEAM FREE FOR ALL~! Championship. Who will be the first King of TEAM's Mountain?
In the third huge match of the evening, Jason Payne and Derecho renew acquaintances in a no holds barred, falls count anywhere brawl that could very well land both gentlemen, not to mention TEAM Chairman Jess Chapel, in jail. Their rivalry started in the Dupree Cup's first round and escalated to gory and felonious heights before they were separated and restrained legally from even breathing on each other. Now, with the chains taken off and the police guaranteed not to get involved, will Payne finish the job he started in the Dupree Cup, or will Derecho gain the upper hand and tear Payne limb from limb?
In other action... Yori Yakamo, Jr. and his dancing sex cyborg ROBOYORI will challenge Professor Tremendous and a member of Team Tremendous of his choosing in a CSWA UNIFIED* Championship match. The one who scores the fall shall become the next CSWA UNIFIED* Champion. (or not)
In what is rumored to be an Evening Gown match, Shawn Jessica Hart, PhD will grapple with a returning Sensational Sean Edmunds Larry Tact , the man currently a thorn in the side of one Juliet Marceau, takes on a challenge of a different sort when he battles the man many regard as the best techincal wrestler in the world today,
"Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin Muffins and Eggo Waffles unite as Kin Hiroshi and Cameron Cruise to take on the team of Rocko Daymon and Phenomenal Frankie Scott They met at SuperShow I and Ulysis Solian pinned Ravager to the mat to end a four-way elimination match. Now they meet in singles action to kick off SuperShow II.
Also, the brackets for the 2nd Annual TEAM Invitational Tournament will be announced on this show. You can watch SuperShow II live on Alternative Sports Network! Check your local cable company for availability.
