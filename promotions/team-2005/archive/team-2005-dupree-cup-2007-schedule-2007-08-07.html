2007 Official Dupree Cup Schedule

7 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TH — Aug 7, 2007 Here you go, the Dupree Cup schedule. I'll go over things you need to remember first. 1. You win a meet by winning more matches than the other team. There are three matches per meet, one regular singles, one gimmick singles and one tag team. If your team wins all three matches, they get three points. If they win the meet by some other means than winning all three, they get two (2 wins, 0 draws; 2 wins, 1 draw; 1 win, 2 draws), a drawn meet, both teams get one point. No points are awarded for a loss.
2. All RP happens in the Fresh Meet forum in the appropriate threads. All stacking rules apply in every match. You can read them in detail in the "All About TEAM" thread, but the abridged version is in singles matches, you wait 48 hours to post a 2nd RP if you were the last person in the match to RP and 0 hours if your opponent was the last one. In tag matches, you wait 48 hours if you were the last one to RP, 24 hours if your partner was the last one to RP, and 0 hours if your opponent was the last one. Even though one match per meet will have a gimmick attached to it in character, there will be no gimmicked RP in this tournament. There will also be no bias against style, so if you're from PTC land and want to write in narrative, be my guest.
3. No b*tching about results on TEAM forums. If you have a complaint, come to me privately and I'll address it as such. If you complain about them on your own forums, don't be surprised to see me there giving you what you're giving to me.
That's the major stuff. Of course, you can read things in further detail in the official announcement thread or the All About TEAM thread. Now, without further ado... the brackets!
