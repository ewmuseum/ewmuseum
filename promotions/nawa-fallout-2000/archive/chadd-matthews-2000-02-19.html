Chadd Matthews

19 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ The camera turns on. The arena is empty, all seats are soul-less except for one seat. In this seat Chadd Matthews sits, wearing a long sleeve white shirt.
It has purple flames coming down from the top and from the bottom touching lime green letters that spell out "Chadd Matthews". Hes got tan cargo kikwear pants on. The NAWA Television Title hangs over the seat infront of him. ]
Chadd Matthews: "So many tears, blood, heart and sweat is wasted here in this arena. This federation is based apon what us as superstars do, how we act and how we preform. Everyday we take hits, punishing blows, broken bones and chair shots for your personal enjoy ment. And fans of the NAWA as you sit their infront of your Television Set booing at me. Your Television Champion the very thing your looking at is owned by myself. I have put my blood, my sweat and my anger my happiness into a thing you guys just boo. The Television Title is owned now by the very wrestler you hate and it sickens you but that makes me laugh because soon enough my fellow americans the very wrestler you cant stand is no longer going to hold just the Television Title but aswell the World
Title. No longer will I only be your Role Model, Your God, Your Idol, Your F'n
Studd I will now become and forever hold my status as your, no, as the champion. Mark Marino you stand in my way. Tuesday semi finals the night when two worlds collide, The night when the two single greatest wrestlers in this sport will meet to decide who moves on, who stands alive. Marino I have my scores to settle, I must redeem myself for you and only you hold the only win over Chadd "F'n" Matthews. Marino you are going to be the victim, Great Fate has chosen to knock on your door, Great Fate has chosen to call your house,
Great Fate is even e-mailing you. Marino the entire federation is based on your shoulders. Can Marino keep the only win over Matthews or will Matthews make up for his first mistake. Mark yes I wish they would take down that loss but they wont just like they wont take your pleeds and begs to be back in this tourniment after I take you out and advance, Then advance to become the North
American Wrestling Alliance World & Television Champion. Marino face it you were good, you were better, you were the man, but now im good, now im better and now I'm going to beat the man. No Retreat No Surrender, T-Money my oppenet, aswell as the other wrestler involved the NAWA World Title Tourny. T-Money you are just like a bug, a insect. Easy to kill but annoying if missed. T-Money when the N.R.N.S. rolls around you will be wishing, praying, hoping for a chance but you wont get it. You will be begging for forgiviness but I wont allow it. T-Money your trying to take what is mine in the states we call that stealing, but in the NAWA we call that fu[beep]ing with someones elses shit.
And T-Money you dont dance with the devil or your going to get roasted, toasted but most of all your going to meet defeat."
[ The camera zooms out a little bit but then Matthews gets up grabs his title and begins to walk off. ]
