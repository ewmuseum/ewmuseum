EIWZ Christmas Bash 1998

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pay Per View Results Welcome to Christmas Bash 1998 we have all the titles up for grabs tonight folks. Lets get right to the action. Match 1: Pump Daddy/Flint vs. Pain Match: Pain getting the upper hand early on in the match. He seems to have prepared a lot for this match. He hits Flint with a short arm clothline but in the Christmas spirit Pump Daddy gives Pain one of his own. Flint and Pump Daddy start beating on Pain. Pain is beggining to make a comeback but two men is just too hard. Flint and Pump Daddy open a can of whoop ass on Pain. Flint and Pump give him a double superplex off the top ropes. Flint sets him up for the Flint bomb and Pump Daddy hits the Pumped Up. Flint and Pump Daddy climb out of the ring together. Winner: Flint and Pump Daddy. Well its time for the next match folks. Match 2: Jazz vs. Keeper Match: Keeper gains the early lead but Jazz hits a back breaker and begins to mount a comeback. Keeper Hits Jazz with a series of right hands and than a suplex. Jazzmine walks to the ring. Keeper begins to focus on Jazzmine and as he turns around he gets a face full of chair. Jazz goes for the pin. One....Two....Three. Winner: Jazz via pinfall Well that was a hell of a match especially when Jazzmine came to the ring. Well now its the light weight Royal Rumble. Match 3: Light Weight Royal Rumble Competetors: Cool Moe "D", Rey Mysterio, Paul Knox, Shaggy 2 Dope, Papa Jiggy, Triple X, Jazz, Spyder, Creep, Chaos Pac, Necromancer Match: Everyone is in the ring and Cool Moe "D" and Jiggy double clothes line Paul Knox out of the ring. Shaggy 2 Dope was than eliminated by Mysterio after a drop kick. Mysterio went to the top ropes to attempt another drop kick but XXX throws him out. Jazz gives Spyder a series of right hands and Creep clothes lines Spyder and Jazz out of the ring. We are down to 5 people. Cool Moe "D" is thrown out by XXX and Jiggy gets revenge on XXX by throwing him out. 3. Necromancer throws out Jiggy. 2. Chaos Pac goes for the clothes line but Necromancer ducks and hits Chaos Pack out of the ring. Winner: Necromancer and new EIWZ LIGHT WEIGHT CHAMP Well that was a good match but the best is still to come. Match 4: European Title Tournement Contestants: Pump Daddy, Mandriller, Rey Mysterio, Annihilator, Paul Knox, Shaggy 2 Dope, Dirty Don Flamingo, Triple X, Havok, Xavier X. Xanadu, Fatty Magee, Pain(buy), The Keeper, SCSU Pimpster, The Necromancer. Matches: Round 1: Pump Daddy vs. Mandriller Winner: Pump Daddy via pinfall after hitting the Pumped up. Round 1: Rey Mysterio vs. Annihilator Winner: Annihilator via DQ after Rey Mysterio used a chair. Round 1: Paul Knox vs. Shaggy 2 Dope Winner: Shaggy 2 Dope via pinfall after hitting the X-Plosive Round 1: Dirty Don Flamingo vs. Triple X Winner: Triple X via pinfall after XXX distracted the ref and bambie hit DDF with a brick from her purse. Round 1: Havok vs. Xavier X. Xanadu Winner: Havok via pinfall after he gave XXX the Spinbuster. Round 1: Fatty Magee vs. The Keeper Winner: Keeper via countout after Fatty Magee walked away 5 minutes into the match. Round 1: SCSU Pimpster vs. The Necromancer Winner: The Necromancer via pinfall after he hit the Conjured. Round 2: Pump Daddy vs. Pain Winner: Pain via pinfall after he gave Pump Daddy absulute PAIN. Round 2: Annihilator vs. Shaggy 2 Dope Winner: Annihilator via pinfall after he hit the Fallout. Round 2: Triple X vs. Havok Winner: Havok via countout after he hit the Spinebuster outside the ring. Round 2: The Keeper vs. Necromancer Winner: Necromencer via pinfall after The Keeper layed down and allowed the pin. Round 3 Semi-Finals: Pain vs. Annihilator Winner: Pain after he put Annihilator through the Absulute PAIN. Round 3 Semi-Finals: Havok vs. Necromancer Winner: Havok via pinfall after he hit the Spinebuster. Round 4 Finals: Havok vs. Pain Match: Havok began attacking Pain to get the upper hand but Pain made the comeback. The match was even but Pain was beggining to wear down Havok. Jazz and Jazzmine walk to the ring. The ref is destracted by Jazzmine and Pain recieves the Split Leg Moonsault. Havok than hits the Spinebuster and Pins Pain. Winner: Havok via pinfall. The new EIWZ EUROPEAN CHAMPION Well that was a well fought tournement now on to the other half of the wrestlers in the North American Tournement. Match 5: North American Title Tournement Final Match: Flint vs. Jazz Match: The match was all even until Flint hit the Flint Bomb. Jazzmine distracted the ref but Zuel came down and pulled her off the ring and she hit her head on the safety rail knocking her out. Pump Daddy came into the ring and gave Jazz the Pumped up. Havok came to the ring and set Flint up for the Spinebuster. The ref turned around just in time to see Havok hit the Spinebuster. Winner: Flint via DQ. New North American Champion. Match 6: Tag Team Title Royal Rumble Match: After 20 hard faught minutes we are down to two wrestlers. XXX and Pimpster. The two men faught for about twenty minutes when Pimpster picked XXX up over his head and threw XXX into the first row. Winners: Cool Moe "D"/Pimpster. The New EIWZ Tag Team Champions. Well I didn't expect them to win now the final match. Main Event Match 7: The EIWZ EXTREME TITLE ROYAL RUMBLE. Match: After ten minutes there are ten men left. They are Cool Moe "D", Reaper, Annihilator, Papa Jiggy, Pain, The Creep, Keeper, Pimpster, Flint, and Grim Reaper. Flint was eliminated by Grim Reaper who was than eliminated by Reaper. Creep eliminated Keeper. Cool Moe "D" eliminated Reaper. Pain eliminated Cool Moe "D". 5. Annihilator took out Pimpster and than was knocked out by Jiggy. 3. Jiggy was in a two on one situation. Creep was eliminated by Jiggy and as he turned around he got clotheslined. 2. He was than given Absulute Pain. Pain then easily eliminated Jiggy. Winner: Pain. THE NEW EIWZ EXTREME CHAMPION. Thats all for this PPV see you next month.
