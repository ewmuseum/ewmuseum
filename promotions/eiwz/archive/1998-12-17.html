EIWZ December 17, 1998

Wednesday Night Extreme Results Thursday December 17 Match 1: The Creep vs. Necromancer (Lightweight title) Match Highlights: Creep went for the Creep Crash but Necromancer moved out of the way. He than gave Creep the Conjured Winner: Necromancer via pinfall. Match 2: Pump Daddy vs. Violent J Match Highlights: Violent J went for a suplex but Pump Daddy reversed it and gave him the Pumped Up. Winner: Pump Daddy via pinfall. Match 3: Annihilator vs. Havok(European Title) Match Highlights: Havok out of no where gave Annihilator the Spinebuster. Winner: Havok via pinfall Match 4: Creep/Pain vs. MoD (Scafold Match) Match Highlights: Creep and Pain (wearing rain coats) both ducked and 20 men led by billy the terd threw dog crap into the ring. Creep then got the pin. Winner: Creep/Pain via pinfall. Match 5: Flint vs. Triple X(North American Title) Match Highlights: Flint set XXX up for the Flint Bomb but XXX reversed it. XXX than delivered the X Rated. Winner: XXX via pinfall NEW NA CHAMPION Match 6: Fatty Magee vs. Johnny Roquette Match Highlights: Flint and Pump Daddy came out and they each delivered their finishers to Roquette. Flint "I would like to bring out two wrestlers at this time. (Two men wearing black masks come out)These men are replacing us because Pump and I retire. The man that is replacing me is a man that is 6'6" and weighs 327 pounds his name is Damian. The man replacing Pump is 6'8" and weighs 354 his name Psycho." Winner: Johnny Roquette via DQ. Match 7: Mandriller vs. Dirty Don Flamingo Match Highlights: Mandriller didn't make it to the ring because DDF announced his retirement. Winner: NO CONTEST Match 8: Main Event: Pain vs. Jazz(Extreme Tilte) Match Highlights: Pain gave Jazz a superplex and then gave Jazz the Absulute Pain. Winner: Pain via pinfall.
