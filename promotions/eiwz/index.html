A roleplay-judged GeoCities e-fed whose Christmas Bash 1998 crowned or defended all five of its own titles across a twenty-bout card, then defended three and turned over a fourth five days later, before those same belts were later unified, years on, into CWF-Pro's own title system. Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Extreme Internet Wrestling Zone ran to a short, plainly stated rulebook — weekly roleplay required of every member, a three-wrestler cap per handler, original wrestlers only, a match decided by roleplay quality — and its surviving record opens at its own biggest night. Christmas Bash 1998 put all five of the fed's own championships up for grabs in one card: a tag match, a singles bout, a Light Weight Royal Rumble won by Necromancer, a European Title tournament won by Havok over Pain in the final, a North American Title match won by Flint over Jazz by disqualification, a Tag Team Title match won by Cool Moe "D" and SCSU Pimpster, and a ten-man Extreme Title Royal Rumble main event won by Pain. Five days later, on December 17, the fed defended three of those five belts and turned over the fourth — Triple X taking the North American Championship from Flint — on a card that also saw Flint and Pump Daddy announce their own retirement, introducing two masked replacements, Damian and Psycho.

None of that is the fed's own last word. Years after this holding's own December 1998 edge, CWF-Pro's own served title ledgers — already held in this museum, independently mined — record all five EIWZ titles unified into CWF-Pro's own: Pain's Extreme Title, Havok's European Title, Necromancer's Light Weight Title, Flint's North American Title, and Cool Moe "D" and SCSU Pimpster's Tag Team Titles all fall, by name, to CWF-Pro challengers on CWF-Pro's own undated reign ledger. One CWF-Pro bio card places its own subject's EIWZ run as an early-career stop years before a 2006 CWF-Pro contract — the fed plainly ran on well past what this holding alone shows, a run this wing's own thin GeoCities capture only opens.

Affiliations

CWF-Pro

Acquired EIWZ (1998) (undated (after December 1998))
CWF-Pro’s own already-served title histories record all five of EIWZ’s own titles unified into CWF-Pro’s: Jason Star defeats Pain to unify the EIWZ Extreme Title with the CWF-Pro Ultra Extreme Hardcore Title; Brad Bruns defeats Havok to unify the EIWZ European Title with the CWF-Pro Canadian Title; Hero defeats Necromancer to unify the EIWZ Lightweight Title with the CWF-Pro Royal Title; Beastman defeats Flint to unify the EIWZ North American Title with the CWF-Pro Intercontinental Title; and the Hot Guys (Barney and PK) defeat Cool Moe D & Pimpster to unify the EIWZ World Tag Team Titles with the CWF-Pro World Tag Team Titles. Every defeated EIWZ-side name matches a champion crowned or defending at EIWZ’s own held Christmas Bash 1998 or December 17, 1998 broadcasts. CWF-Pro’s own reign histories carry no exact date for any of the five unification matches; one CWF-Pro bio page places its own subject’s EIWZ tenure as an early-career stop years before a 2006 CWF-Pro contract, so the unification reads as a later event than EIWZ’s own December 1998 run, not a contemporaneous one.
