EAW "Yet Again" Recruitment Ad, 30 December 1997

30 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sorry to bother you again, but while this little fight over the HWA goes on, I would like to inform you that a federation that is extreme AND has class is open. It is called Extreme American Wrestling. We do not fight in Camel Dung or in Iodine. But we do have Aztec Blood matches where a pyramid of barbed wire is over the ring and you must climb up it, over it, and down it. We also have actual matches where you actually wrestle. I know it is a little far fetched, but some matches end clean, fair, and wihout weapons. Pretty wild idea huh? Anyways, check us out. Hope to hear from you. http://members.tripod.com/~STOOL2/EAW.html
