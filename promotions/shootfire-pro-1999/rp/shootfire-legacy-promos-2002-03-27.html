Shootfire Pro: Legacy promos

27 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey e-people. This is Aj, I have the privilege of being in charge here. I'm not used to posting these cards to the RSPWF but I want my talent to get the respect and kudos they deserve. Please maximize your windows to read the show because I haven't gotten the hang of posting nice little neat mails yet. Bear with me. What is above is our pay per view of the year, so we're looking for a little honest feedback. Here are a few of the characters featured on Legacy: First off, Jasy Brians, "Phoenix" Mike McCarey with a cameo by Gregory Brians ===SPW==== Doo doo doo doo doo- [Jasy answers her cell phone] Jasy: Road Kill Cafe. You kill it, we grill it. GB: [on other end] Would you stop that? Jasy: No. GB: OK, listen up. We got some big shiat going down at Legacy. The L-Man's got Keenan under wraps, and I'm gonna take AsH to the cleaners. But Phoenix is in this Toughman thingamabob. Jasy: Thanks for the update, Recap Man. GB: Mikey is the new guy in the Threat. And quite frankly, Brotha Jake and I are a little concerned he'll make an ass of himself. So, as the other junior member, it's your job to get him ready. We do not suffer fools gladly in the T3. Jasy: Then why are you still around? GB: I ... It ... Shut up. CLICK [Jasy clicks her phone shut and looks at the camera] Jasy: Why are you still here? Fast forward! [Fast forward to two hours later in the Alamo Gym in Dallas, TX. The doors swing open to show the full fitness center and rings; one for boxing, the other for wrestling. The place is packed with weight-lifters, boxers and the Dallas Cowboy Cheerleaders.] GB: Woooo [Camera moves in to Phoenix running on the treadmill and Jasy monitoring his process] Phoenix: [looking around] Where did that come from? Jasy: Who knows, who cares. You keep moving. If you don't win this Toughman Competition, it'll be my ass. Phoenix: He really said that he doubted me? Jasy: I quote [doing an impression of GB] "Mikey is the new guy in the Threat. And quite frankly, Brotha Jake and I are a little concerned he'll make an ass of himself." Un-quote. Phoenix: He does a well enough job on making an ass out of himself. I don't know why he's so concerned about me. Anyways, who are my competitors again? Jasy: Ian Lightfoot. Phoenix: He's still alive? Jasy: Ceaser Risley. Phoenix: Who? Jasy: Perry Vaughn. Phoenix: My main worry. Jasy: Seth Draken. Phoenix: Next. Jasy: Jake Patrick. Phoenix: What? Jasy: Sage. Phoenix: Suicide wanna-be. Jasy: And the newest addition, Todd Lucchesi. Phoenix: Wow, that makes two people to be concerned about. I've beaten him though. Jasy: At least you show no fear. Phoenix: Fear, my dear, is just a four letter word. Jasy: So is "lose". Keep working. I'll get you a bottle of water. Guy 1: Hey Mike. Who's the babe with the short haircut? Phoenix: Ex-wife. Guy 2: Ouch man. Did she turn to the other team, if you know what I mean? Phoenix: What? No- Yes, yes she did. She actually got married to another lady. Guy 2: Wow man. Why is she here with you? Phoenix: Ah, well, her and I have called a truce and are in the same stable together. Sometimes it's a pain in the ass but then again it has its rewards. [Camera shows Jasy bending over with her ass sticking straight out for everyone to view. The guys' jaws drop as they all stare. Phoenix stops to stare too and damn near falls off the treadmill but regains his composure and gets back on pace. Jasy stands up straight and walks over to Mike and hands him the bottle of water] Jasy: Gentlemen, if you don't mind, Michael has a very important match to prepare for. Move along. [The guys act disappointed as they leave, trying to catch a glimpse of Jasy's backside again] Jasy: Alright that's 40 on the mill. Hit the ring. [A young man in about his early 20's enters the shot and taps Jasy on the shoulder.] Guy 3: Did I happen to see an ex-sister-in-law of mine hanging around here with her ex? [Jasy turns around and smiles at the man and gives him a hug] Jasy: Shawn, its been so long. Shawn: Too long Jasolyn. What are you doing here with my brother? Jasy: Training for Legacy. He's in some type of Toughman thing. So GB has sent me here to get his ass in gear. Phoenix: I'm in perfect shape. I don't need to worry about anything. Shawn: Except getting your ass whipped. [He laughs] How are you Mike? Phoenix: Perfectly fine, little bro. Nice gym ya got here. How's Devon? Shawn: He's good. It looks like you're going to need a sparring partner. Let me get my stuff on and we'll have a go at it. Phoenix: Just like old times. [Fast forward] Shawn: Now what type of match is this. Wrestling? Boxing? Jasy: That's the thing. No one knows. Phoenix: We'll say both for the hell of it. [The bell rings and the two set up for what looks like a boxing round. Phoenix does a quick jab, Shawn moves out of the way and throws a straight right to Phoenix's nose.] Phoenix: Owww. What the hell?!?! Shawn: That's for stealing my G.I. Joe when I was 8. Phoenix: Damn that was like eons ago. Why did you have to punch me in the nose? Shawn: You deserved it. Besides it was a lesson. Don't leave your face uncovered. If at all possible don't leave anything uncovered. Also when you are boxing watch the eyes. Not the body. Phoenix: Damn when did you get so knowledgeable about boxing? Shawn: Since I opened this gym. Trust me bro, you take everything I say like the bible. You will be kicking these guys ass and finally on your way to winning your first title. Phoenix: That was low man. Besides I do have tag and Diamond Titles under my belt. Jasy: Yea and one you lost to a woman. Elyse Lightfoot, mind you. Phoenix: You had to bring up old rotting memories didn't you? BAM Phoenix: Son of a bitch!!! [Phoenix quickly kicks Shawn right in the nuts] Phoenix: HAHAHAHAHAHA [Suddenly three Dallas Cowboy cheerleaders jump in the ring and surround Shawn] Cheerleader 1: Oh Shawn! Are you alright??? Cheerleader 2: Quick, come with us, away from this jerk. We'll make you feel better. Cheerleader 3: To the sauna! [The cheerleaders help Shawn up and walk off camera] Phoenix: Goddammit. Shawn always got the beautiful ladies. Jasy: *ahem* Phoenix: You heard me. [Fast forward] Phoenix: Can we go yet? I tell you, I have the juke, I have the jive, and you better believe I got the wail. Jasy: What are you going to do out there!?! Phoenix: I'm gonna show them that the Phoenix is no joke! People are pegging this as Lucchesi's match. Well he doesn't have anything on me! Jasy: Who are you going to do it to?!? Phoenix: I'll kill Lightfoot! Mach II will crash when I'm done. Sage had better commit suicide before he thinks of facing me! Jasy: What else?!?! Phoenix: Patrick is done! Vaughn has met his own nightmare. I'll bite out Lucchesi's heart and chew it up! Jasy: Aspercream? Phoenix: Please. Jasy: And why are you going to do it??? Phoenix: Because I'm in the T3. And nobody fucks with The Threat. The Phoenix has spoken. Jasy: You need a new catchphrase. Phoenix: You're a bitch. Jasy: Fair enough. ==SPW======== Here's one from 'The American Nightmare' Perry Vaughn: ===SPW======= [The camera cuts to the back where Perry Vaughn is seen walking through the hallways, his arms at his side and his long black hair tied back away from his face as he looks as intense as ever. A huge pop from the arena breaks the silence as the sceen shows up on the SPWtron. Dan Heller quickly rushes up behind Vaughn calling out to him.] Heller: Perry? Hold up! Can I get a word with you? [Vaughn stops suddenly in his tracks, Heller quickly comes to a hault before he runs into Vaughn's back. Perry turns around slowly his head held downward as he look to Heller, his lips turning back in a wicked grin] Vaughn: For you Dan...I'll make the time... [Heller nods and holds a mic between the 2 of them] Heller: Well Perry you're only a few minutes away from the Toughman Gauntlet, you're stepping into the ring with 6 other men, 6 of the best that the SPW has to offer? What do you think of your odds? [Vaughn closes his eyes for a moment and laughs softly] Vaughn: Well...if I said I had great odds...I'd be a fool. My name is Perry Vaughn, not Jazz Madison...I know when my back is against the wall. I know that this is the time when the primal animalistic instinct kicks in...it's times like this I look forward, every now and then we all need to see what we're really made of. Heller: I don't think anyone doubts what you're made of, even Phoenix expressed concerns about facing you tonight. [Vaughn smiled, it was quite genuine this time. A low chuckle in the back of his throat floating over the mic] Vaughn: As he should, he tried to one up me, he tried to have me taken out of the arena in an ambulance at Blood Brothers. He failed, if push comes to shove and we end up facing off tonight, I'll return the favor, and unfortunatly...I don't like fail, so I'll try just a little bit harder then normal. I've been to hell and back, I've been in wars for the ages and no matter what....with a broken collar bone, 3 broken ribs, a dislocated jaw and internal bleeding...I was still able to walk out of the arena on my feet that night before going to the hosptial. Heller: I've seen that match, I saw you pass out, so I know you're not exagerating. Vaughn: Maybe I'm too stupid to know better, or to dumb to give up when I should, maybe that makes me a toughman. Or maybe I've backed up everything I've said since I came here in the SPW, and maybe..just maybe...I'm one of the few guys that everyone in the back thinks twice about before putting their name on the dotted line, reguardless...tonight I get to prove it one way or another once again. Heller: What about your opponents tonight? What are your thoughts on them... [Vaughn grins as he holds up his thumb and begins counting off names] Vaughn: Phoenix...will be getting what's coming to him. Since I've been in SPW Phoenix has been a main stay, I'm not gonna take anything away from him, because I know exactly how dangerous he is. I'm also not going to give him any more respect then he deserves in the ring, which tonight...equals none. So when I kick your head off your shoulders, you won't wonder why, and if Jasy wants to get involved...well, I'm done being a "gentleman" [Vaughn holds up his index finger] Vaughn: Jake Patrick, I've been in the ring with him, I've made him bleed once, I'll do it again, and until the kid realizes that I'm not to be taken lightly, and the ramifications that come along with getting into the ring with me...his career doesn't show much promise... [Vaughn raises his middle fingers and then his ring finger.] Vaughn: Cesar Risley and Seth Draken! Jazz's best bitches! [HUGE FACE POP!] Vaughn: Someone said that Jazz is a pimp and all these wrestlers he has working for him are just hos doing what he says when he says. Never has a truer statement been uttered. I know you boys have your orders tonight, make sure I don't walk away from the match with a win and more importantly the arena on my feet. Well guess what, orders are only as good as the general giving them, and when you're follow orders blindly, you end up walking to your doom. [whispers] In this case, you're walking into a nightmare that you won't be able to wake up from. [HUGE POP!] Vaughn: So tonight...in the Gauntlet...I'll be more then happy to give you both a message to send back to Madison. I doubt he'd like it though... [holds up his pinky making 5] Vaughn: Then there's Sage...someone trying to make a splash in quicksand. It's not possible...just like you coming away from this match with a win. We've clashed a bit in the past, we will in the near future...and we are in the present...here tonight! [Vaughn only holds up his index finger now as he looks at Heller and then to the camera before lowering his arm back down to his side.] Vaughn: Ian Lightfoot...Elysa's husband...I have no problem with you. I think we can leave it at that, Elysa was my manager and partner for a little while. So whatever happens between us in the ring, will stay there in respect. Remember, it's business, nothing personal...and I want to come away with the win tonight so bad I can taste it. [Vaughn bites sharply down into his lip as Dan winces] Heller: Ouch! Why did you do that?! [Vaughn smiles a bit as he raises his hand to his lip and moves it away as the trickle of blood rests on his finger, his tongue slipping over his lip] Vaughn: I taste blood...and like a shark... [Vaughn grins darkly as he nods slightly to Dan and turns around heading through the hallway as the camera cuts back to the arena.] =====SPW======== And one from David Hagen... =====SPW======== [The end of the old. The beginning of the new. The old renewed. The new replaced. The old revised. The new has turn bad. The return of the man. The return of David Hagen!!!] [The scene opens on the man, the myth of the wrestling world. The absolute best Shootfire Pro wrestler to ever step into the squared circle. David Hagen! Hagen is abnormally cheerful as he stand in front of a Shootfire podium. Hagen pretty much looks the same, if anything has added muscle in his time off. But the difference in the look, is a smile on his face. The man is happy? What is that all about? Well, beyond the smile is still the same vicious, tasteless individual that put wrestling greats in the hospital, such as Creed and Palmer. Hagen quickly begins his speech as a crowd of reporters are hushed at the return of the 6'7" legend.] Hagen: I have returned. I haven risen from the dead. I have been reborn. The truth of the matter is. I was once lost, but now... Now I am found. A new beginning for David Hagen is to begin. I have come from heaven, gone to hell and have risen from the depths of a fiery pit. I have no fear left in me. I have no shame, nor do I stand down from anyone or anything. But I am, I am hear to strike fear into those who still shiver at the name David Hagen. [Hagen rubs his goatee has he continues.] Hagen: Is any man tempted to take me on? Is any man stupid enough to step toe to toe with the Paralyzer? The most tasteless man in the industry? I didn't think so. But unfortunately for those who are scared. Those who are terrified. I have returned. And you are my target! Who? Everyone! Especially certain people who act as if they are not afraid. But yet they still wet there pants at the whisper of the name... David Hagen! Are you man enough? Is anyone man enough to take me on??? [Hagen raises his arms and flexes for the cameras that begin to snap left and right at the great form of the man who would crush your skull with a blow that only a God could posses.] Hagen: But now that you know I am back. I will answer one question from the media. But only one, as time is money and there is no time left in this announcement. As you all know, nothing can last forever, and that goes along with all of your stupid questions. SO fire away one swift and beautiful remark that I can so graciously answer. [The reporters talk amongst themselves before coming up with one small little question that is completely un important.] Reporter: What lays ahead for you and Angela Brians? [David Hagen snaps his fingers and stares coldly at the screen.] Hagen: Dumb, but unexpected question. I have run out of time boys... But lets just say... Nothing Lasts Forever! [Fade to Black] ======SPW============= Okay, here's one from Axel Schmidt... ======SPW============= (The scene opens to show Axel Schmidt wiping his face in the Lucky 7 Locker room. Dylan Dice walks through and pats Axel on the back signalling a job well done. Schmidt then throws the towel and looks into the cameras that have converged on him. He begins to speak.) Axel: At Conquest, you saw what the Lucky 7 Club was all about. Justin Starr thought he was going to try to get extreeme with two masters of the art and look what happened to him? Dylan Dice gave him a glimpse of what he is in store for come Legacy this week. Justin Starr obviously you have no idea what I am capable of. I would go to the ends of the earth to take your job from you because you have no idea how to do it and you have run this fed that men like Dice and myself BUILT. That's right, I have held the World Title 2 times, more then you can even dream of holding it. It is guys like you that have ruined this fed for Mr. Black. Mr. Black gave me an opportunity when I first started here and to see a guy like you run it to the ground makes me sick. I am going to put a quick and definative end to that at Legacy though. (Axel begins looking around for something. On the bench he is sitting on he notices something that catches his eye. He picks it up and holds it in front of the camera to show it off. It appears to be the bloody razorblade that Dice slashed Starr with. He begins laughing and then starts speaking again.) Axel: You see that Starr? Do you see the beginning of the end for you and your cronies? Tonight was just a glimpse of what could happen to you when we get in that ring at Legacy with a briefcase on top of that ladder. You may not just lose your job, you may lose your career in the process. You see I am not someone that just likes to win a match. I like to finish careers in the process. You can talk all you want about being the antichrist and how you are "extreme" but when we step foot in the ring at Legacy you are going to find out just how wrong you are. (Axel takes the towel to his head again as he begins to bleed again. He throws it down and looks into the camera again.) Axel: You talk about how hard this job is and how a guy like myself that can't cut a promo for shit couldn't do your job. Let me explain something to you Justin. Ian Lightfoot did your job just fine. I can bash that twit for a lot of things but he did manage to keep SPW afloat even after a star like myself parted ways a couple of years ago. No matter how much I dislike Ian Lightfoot and how much fun I had kicking his ass in the past the man has given me a shot that I will not fail in. I never lost the SPW Title and I certainly won't lose to the likes of you Starr. Be prepared to hand your job over to me at Legacy and prepare to be my coffee bitch when you do. If you are catching my drift. (Axel stands up for a minute and starts to walk to the showers, then stops and looks into the camera again.) Axel: I also have another thing to add tonight. The likes of guys like Eric Henry had better take notice at Legacy because their lives here in SPW are going to be a lot different the morning after Legacy. They are no longer going to get the silver spoon treatment. Guys like Brians and Davis are going to have to report to a real commissioner and when they do there is going to be hell to pay. Just keep that on the brain SPW.........because Axelvision is coming to SPW. (Axel turns away from the camera and heads towards his shower as the camera fades out.) =====SPW=========== Well there ya go, a few more are on the PPV itself... well, I hope you enjoy the shows, they were a lot of fun ...and time... and I'm glad to share them with the rest of you.
