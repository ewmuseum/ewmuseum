A hardcore Usenet e-fed whose 2002 run staged its Legacy pay-per-view, crowned four Hall of Famers at Shootfire Icons and broadcast weekly Conquest and Off the Chain shows. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Shootfire Pro Wrestling is first known through its own website bulletin of 1 December 1999, which named “Flaming” Bob Muretic its Heavyweight Champion and advertised its self-billed biggest card of the year, Winter Warfare I, for 19 December 1999 from Buffalo, New York. Its own FAQ of February 2000 records that it grew out of the National Extreme Wrestling Federation.

Through the first months of 2000 it posted cards such as Charity Carnage, Slamdance and Rumble in the Jungle, and a weekly roleplay show called Off the Chain. By 2002 its weekly broadcast was Conquest, with Off the Chain still beside it.

Its flagship pay-per-view, Legacy, came from the Dallas Sportatorium in March 2002. Axel Schmidt won a ladder match to become its sole Commissioner, Todd Lucchesi won the Diamond Title in a House of Anarchy three-way, Gregory Brians won the Platinum Title from AsH in a half-hour Ironman match, and the Reaper kept the World title against Donovan Keltrane in a Three Stages of Hell match fought through a collapsing ring.

In June 2002 Shootfire Icons, a tournament held in an underground arena called Area 911, crowned the promotion's first four Hall of Famers: Mike McCarey, who won his way in wearing another man's mask, Vile Vince Viper, AsH by the judges' choice, and Jeff Keenan. The next month its Conquest broadcast announced an alliance with the International Grappling Association from 11 September 2002.

Its last surviving document, a Conquest broadcast of 2 August 2002 from Tallahassee, set Keenan to challenge Gregory Brians for the World title at a pay-per-view called Fuck the System on 17 August. How that card, or the promotion, ended isn't in the surviving record.
