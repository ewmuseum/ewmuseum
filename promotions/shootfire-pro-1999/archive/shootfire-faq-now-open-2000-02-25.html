An Overview Shootfire Pro was formally known as the National Extreme Wrestling Federation [NEWF]. As the NEWF was looking for some talent and more participation, we decided to go a different route and re-work the structure of the company. The NEWF was a great idea, however lacked a structured staff, which Shootfire Pro now has. I, [name withheld], am the founder of the NEWF and AJ will be my main man along side me as the co-president of the
Shootfire Pro Promotion.
Shootfire Pro will provide the standard e-wrestling guru with a little twist in the game. For starters, we are going to focus more so on the wrestling aspect as opposed to the "sports entertainment" aspect.
Shootfire Pro will also provide some other tid-bits of goodies, which you can look for in the next section.
2. What Shootfire plans on accomplishing in the e-wrestling world? Shootfire Pro plans on creating huge waves in the world of e wrestling. For starters, we want to focus on a more professional style of action and not the childish "gimmick" wrestling that was popular in the 80s and early 90s.
We want this experience to be fun and somewhat closer to a real life feeling and not something that goes beyond the realm of reality.
Shootfire Pro will focus mainly on character development. We want to make sure that characters have their own niche and are not just a blatant rip-off of some of the "real" wrestlers in the big three promotions. We want this fed to be original and focus on pure wrestling action.

The only way this will happen is if you.ROLEPLAY! The rules are simple. Anything will go. Swearing is permitted, however make it so it makes sense and doesn't get too excessive and just down right stupid. If it gets out of hand, I'll boot you from the fed, no warnings or anything, you WILL be gone.
As for the other rules. All we ask is that you roleplay once a week to advance your character's state in the federation. It should be pretty lengthy, not a one to two sentence roleplay that says, "Wrestler A will kick
Wrestler B's ass and that's the bottom line." it should be lengthy and should envolve a setting and a good dialog.
Roleplay packets will be sent out once a week on Thursday's by AJ. All roleplays are to be sent to AJ
[detail withheld] and [detail withheld] . 5. Weekly Shows "Off the Chain" will be Shootfire's Weekly Flash Program that will be hosted by Jeffery Dylan Marsh and the President of Shootfire, [name withheld]. Both of them will discuss the roleplays and pump up the up coming card for the week. This will be out on Thursday's.
"Slamdance" will be Shootfire-Pro's weekly action show that will consist around 8 matches. This is where the main angles come into play and most of the hard work is shown. This will be out on Sundays.
6. Titles Shootfire-Pro Heavyweight Title: Top dog of Shootfire, defends at least every 30 days and at ALL Supercards.
Shootfire-Pro Platinum Title: The most hardcore SOB in the Shootfire Promotion. Title is defended every 20 days and at ALL Supercards. Shootfire-Pro Heavyweight Tag Team Titles: The best team that can combine their talent to win. Belts defended every 30 days and at ALL Supercards.
More titles will be added as the fed grows... 7. Super cards Every once in awhile, we will have what is called a "Super card" which is a form of a pay per view, except you don't PAY anything. These will happen usually around every two months and will have a catchy name attached to them. All titles are defended on Super cards and usually gimmick matches are sprinkled through out them.
