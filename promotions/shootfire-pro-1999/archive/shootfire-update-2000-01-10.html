"Guillotine" Adam Cage January 10th, 2000 - M-Cup/Slamdance - Bob Muretic vacated the Heavyweight Title and Jack Macmillian begain the M-Cup for the title and the M-Cup trophy. Adam Cage came out of a 24 person tournament to face the final three men in a three way battle for the title. Ian Lightfoot was eliminated by Axel Schmidt and Cage eliminated Axel Schmidt. 1. "Flaming" Bob Muretic October 31, 1999 - Halloween Scream - Defeated Michael Roman, Dylan Dice, Jinsei Asaka, Aspire, Ian Lightfoot, Stallion, Palmer in an eight man free for all. ----------------------------------------------------------------- === SPW Platinum Title === ----------------------------------------------------------------- 2. "Rockin'" Roddy Taylor January 4th, 2000 - Charity Carnage - Defeated Havoc and Palmer in a three way battle for the Platinum Title. 1. "The Epitome of Excellence" Havoc December 19, 1999 - Winter Warfare - Defeated Elyse Lightfoot in a semi-final match and went on to defeat Palmer in the final round of the Platinum Title tournament. ----------------------------------------------------------------- === SPW Tag Team Titles === ----------------------------------------------------------------- 1. Palmer and "Lightning" Jake McCreary December 19, 1999 - In a four way dance for the tag titles, Palmer and Lightning defeated The Lightfoots, The Operators and Mobius to become the first ever Shootfire-Pro Tag Team Champions. ----------------------------------------------------------------- === SPW Womens Title === ----------------------------------------------------------------- 1. Jasolyn O'Neil December 19, 1999 - Winter Warfare - Defeated Laura Stern and Gwendolyn in a three way dance for the Shootfire Womens title. ======= SPW ========================================== SPW ====== EVENT CENTER ======= SPW ========================================== SPW ====== FOLLOWING THE M-CUP TOURNAMENT FOR THE HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE, SHOOTFIRE PRESENTS A SHOW SO DEADLY EVEN JEFFERY DAUMER WOULD SHIT HIS PANTS!
