Winter Warfare I — Official Card

19 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I made some minor changes to the card...I totally forgot to put Taylor and Stallion on the card in a match, which just slipped my mind. Pretty much everything else is set. Oh yeah, the NIWF Cruiserweight Title will be up for grabs in the Simmons/Lightfoot match as well as per [name withheld]'s request on Slamdance. After Winter Warfare, we'll introduce the SPW Cruiserweight Title on a Slamdance, so for all of the guys with wrestlers 220 and below, you can give me a shout and tell me if you want your character up for the title or not. We'll probably hold some sort of gimmick card in January where a Rumble of sorts will take place. You will soon find out that this January card will lead up to some big things, like the SPW's Version of Wrestlemania in February. [It'll be held on Feb. 29th, which is a Tuesday, but since it comes once every four years, I thought I'd toss it in.] Also, Winter Warfare will more than likely be out on Monday instead of Sunday just do to the simple fact that if I am going to produce a 200+ K card, I want to make sure I have everything perfect. This card will go down as our greatest card, hands down, but only if I can count on some match writers. Our regular writers, you should be receiving your matches tonight which are due Saturday by Midnight. We have eleven matches and I need a few more match writers to do 15-20K matches. The following either have a match or should be getting their match tonight. James War Dr Pepper AJ is gone for two weeks, so I'll pretty much be doing solo work. So if you can help out [remember, I asked those of you if you could write matches on the application] please come forth. I'll be emailing some of you individually about writing a match. OTC will be out tomorrow and roleplays are due tonight by midnight... Richard...the card is beneath OFFICIAL CARD!!! -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- WINTER WARFARE I =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= LIVE!
