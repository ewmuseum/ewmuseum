FCW SuperBrawl Countdown

21 February 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey all, This is a new non-participation league called FCW, Flanagan Championship Wrestling. I am writing this like everything that has already happened was "broadcasted".
*********************************************************************** (Scenes flash of many wrestlers. Included is JJ Dillion with The New
Horsemen, The Original Horsemen, Hunter Hearst Helmsley, The Road
Warriors, and many other superstars. Finally there is a row of statues in a hall. The statues are of Cactus Jack, Sting, Psicosis, Big Bubba
Rogers, and in the middle of those four a statue of Man Mountain Rock hodling the World Title aloft. Cut to Rob Kelber and Disco Joe LIVE from Gund Arena in Cleveland)
[RK] Hello and welcome to Gund Arena in Cleveland, Ohio, home of the
Rock N Roll Hall of Fame!!! Where we are coming to you LIVE!! Tonight
Flanagan Championship Wrestling presents SUPERBRAWL ONE!!!!
[DJ] Where that punk Man Mountain Rock will FINALLY lose the World
Title, to VADER!!!! [RK] Well, I don't know about that, but Vader will have the shot at the so far undefeated Man Mountain Rock for the World Title. Also, we will see Psicosis defend his US title against The Undertaker, and Diesel meets Big Bubba Rogers for the TV Title. The World Tag Champs Cactus
Jack and Sting team up with The Road Warriors to do battle with Sabu,
The 1-2-3 Kid, and The Headhunters in a Survivor Series match!! That and much more tonight from Cleveland. Now let's look at how many of these events came to be. First, the World Title match.
(Scene cuts to the FCW Premeire show. The World Title Match) [RK(voice-over)] At the FCW Premeire Show, Lex Luger was to meet a mystery opponent to decide the first ever FCW World Champ. That mystery opponent ended up being none other than Man Mountain Rock.
(Scene cuts to Man Mountain Rock in the ring facing off with Lex Luger) Man Mountain Rock SHOCKED the world when he managed to overcome Lex and become the FIRST World Champ. Since then he has defeated John
Nord, Yoshiaki Fujiwara, and Razor Ramon to hold onto the title, but tonight he faces his toughest challenge, with Jim Cornette, VADER!!!
(Cut to shot of Vader pummeling Masa Saito at FCW Premeire Show) Vader made one shot at the tag titles, but alas, he and his mystery partner, revealed to be Ted DiBiase, lost to the champs, The Road
Warriors.
(Cut to Hawk winning the second fall with a small package on DiBiase) But Vader came back in a triangle match on hte next card. (Cut to Vader pinning Arn Anderson and Paul Roma in the match) But Anderson evened the score by defeating Vader and Paul Roma in a triangle match, but then Vader won the Royal Rumble to get this title shot.
(Cut back to Joe and Rob) [RK] Well, I've just been informed we're running out of time, so I'll finish by explaning the situation of the Horsemen. You see, JJ Dillon arrived here proclaiming a NEW group of Horsemen would rule, and he introduced them as Lou Marconi, Dean Malenko, Chris Benoit, and MR.
PERFECT!!! [DJ] Who are the only TRUE Horsemen, but Ric Flair felt he had to bring back his old timers to stop this. So first, Anderson and BLanchard premeired. In a Survivor Series match, Blanchard, Anderson, Lex Luger, and Dr. Death Steve Williams took on the New Horsemen, but were rejected. So then ric Flair comes in, and again the same challenge is made, with Flair in the Docs place. But this made NO difference, since they were humialted even worse!!
[RK] Well, I'm informed that we're gettin ready to start in 5....4....3....2...(fade out) (c) 1996 by Sports Entertainment Productions (SEP)
