Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

On 23 November 1992, in the middle of an argument about whether fantasy wrestling belonged on rec.sport.pro-wrestling at all, one poster listed the federations then running: “I don't see what it's held on a pedastal above the EMWA, UeWF, PWA, NEWA, ILIW, GCW, SWL or WeWF.” ILIW is the fifth of the eight that sentence sets beside the FWF, and the sixth of the nine it names in all.

That sentence is the whole of its surviving record. No card, no roster and no result of its own is held here or anywhere this project has looked, and the newsgroup's own traffic before and after it carries nothing else that is this federation.
