Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Extreme Wrestling Entertainment, EWE on its banner and eWe in its menus, was an e-fed run on a free ProBoards message board, and said so in the first line of its welcome: “Here you can create your own superstar or use your favorite superstar from WWE, WCW, ECW, TNA, and more!” The board was laid out as a ring and a backstage: EWE Nitro for the weekly show, a board for each pay-per-view, a show history, chat, a wrestling board, a feedback board and a graphics board, and an Information Hall for the roster, the alliances and the champions' hall.

Its owner and the general manager of Nitro were staged as Vince McMahon and Eric Bischoff. In May 2005 only the European title carries a name, “The Regulator” Mike Caesar; in February 2006 the list read Randy Orton, Christopher Daniels, Chris Jericho, Sandman, the Road Warriors and Chris Pyro, and by March 2006 Christian Cage and Edge had taken the Heavyweight and Television titles. In February 2006 it ran Souled Out, its eleventh pay-per-view, from Boston; a month later it billed Dawn of Destiny from Toronto as its twelfth and biggest and a Nitro from the Tokyo Dome, beside a Beta League developmental camp.

The board counted 60 members and 391 topics in May 2005, 119 members and 5,409 topics in February 2006 and 463 members and 9,120 topics in February 2007. Its welcome of February 2007 calls the fed Version 6, almost three years and more than six versions along, under two founders, with all four titles vacant, a Monday Nitro on N.B.C. in its third episode and a pay-per-view, Metamorphosis, set for 26 February to 4 March.
