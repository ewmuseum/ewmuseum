Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This promotion opened on 9 January 1996 with its first annual pay-per-view, Season's Beatings, from the All Wrestling Arena in Ottawa, Ontario, headlined by a thirty-two-man tournament to crown “our very first LAW! Heavyweight Champion.” Called live by Magilla and “the French Fury” Jean De Mort, the broadcast carried an announcement from its own President, Jack Rummy, that the rest of its championships would be decided across upcoming TV cards, each in its own randomly drawn eight-man tournament, rather than in one night.

The tournament final was a ladder match between Sandman Sid McKnight and Big Bob Rem, worked in front of celebrity guests billed for the night: Courteney Love keeping time, Richard Simmons as guest commentator, and Frank Sinatra on the microphone. After a champagne revival, a ladder yanked out from under him by his opponent's own manager, and a timekeeper's table broken in half by a body going through it, Sandman Sid McKnight climbed back up to pull down the belt and become, in Frank Sinatra's own call, the new “HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION OF THE WORLD” — having beaten six men to do it in one night.

No further document survives under this promotion's own name after that single pay-per-view.
