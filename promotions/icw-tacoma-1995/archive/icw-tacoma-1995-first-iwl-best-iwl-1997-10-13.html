In article <01bcd7a6$e47a6220$c8a2d8cf@jpdecosse>, "JP says... > >I wonder if anyone else is reading these posts, besides you & I. Actualy I've been following this for a bit because I went threw this before, and at times feel I should do so again. I run/ran the Internet Championship Wrestling since September of 1995. And everyone knows just how many ICW's there are out there. I at times TRY and ask the prez's to change names out of respect (somehow most do), but other times they don't. And if their fed isn't called "Internet Championship Wrestling", I can't do anything about the acronym, no one can. Just look at World Championship Westling and Windy City Wrestling (WCW). You don't think Bishoff would have done something by now if he could? Or or WWF (wrestling) and WWF (World Wildlife Foundation). Honestly, there isn't much at all either you can do except fight/talk on here. Neither of you will let your guard down. Now from a neutral point of view, I like BOTH of your feds. I'm not a big fan of "real wresters feds", but I did follow a few, and this IWL is far better than any others around. As for the "other" IWL, it's more to my taste of "original wrestlers" and I think it's also far better than any others I've been in or seen. Your both good. As for the acronym problem in this newsgroup when posting, I think people will know which fed it is simply by seeing who posted the cards. Saying "The best IWL" is like JP said, just an opinion and probably shouldn't be mentioned. But again.. ah never mind this isn't going anywhere :) Just an opinion from a neutral party (Not that it's worth anything :P) [name withheld]
