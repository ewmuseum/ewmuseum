Just YOU and ME! Just like you want it! [Quigley has been walking towards Kauffman, and now stands eye-to-eye with him... Kauffman just stares back, and says...] DK: Hey, go win that rumble, pal. JL: Don't go in! Don't go in!! [The fans count down the seconds. 5...4...3...2...1...0] ["Starfox" Curt Crane makes his way down the aisle.] JL: Curt Crane is on his way! Ouch that had to hurt Riser once he hit the concrete floor. Quigley is trying to rip Hudson's arm out of the socket. The Harlem Hitman has released the hold and Harrison is barely walking. I'm being told that a messenger for Rick Showboat has handed my producer a note and Rick Showboat is claiming to have knocked out Peter Bell. Apparently he did not appreciate Bell smashing his cane in half. Well poor Rick Showboat. JL: Don't make fun of this Mike, that cane cost Rick a lot of money. Well now Bagwell has managed to tie up Todd Newkirk and Bagwell heads outside towards Peter Bell. Bagwell just threw Bell back in the ring and Bagwell also enters the ring. Bagwell lifts him up and crushes his back with a nicely executed back breaker. Bagwell just picks him up and flings him over the top rope. So much for The Total Package. JL: Crane picks Kondor up and throws him into the turnbuckle. Crane climbs up and begins punching Kondor in the face with repeated right hand shots. Brian Anthony is standing in a neutral corner now waiting for the right opportunity while Hudson has managed to get Quigley in sharpshooter. Newkirk is still tied up and Bagwell has gone outside the ring searching for something, I have no idea what Bagwell could be looking for. Harrison squats low and he thrusts himself back up, the Hitman has gone over the top rope halfway but he is still hanging onto Harrison.
