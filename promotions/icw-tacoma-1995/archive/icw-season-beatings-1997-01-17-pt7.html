[ICW] Season Beating's Part VII

17 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ICW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH: -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= "Nature Boy" Todd Newkirk vs. Magus ------------------------------------------ RA: This next match is sceduled for one fall, and is for the ICW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE!!!! Currently in the ring with his manager Tianna, the challenger and winner of the 1996 Season Beating's Royal Rumble, hailing from Starkville, Mississippi, weighing in at 250 pounds, here is the "NATURE BOY" TODD NEWKIRK!!!! [Todd is a caucasian man with a slightly above average muscle tone and a good tan. When coming to the ring, he wear a red robe with "Nature Boy" written in cursive on the back of his robe. Tianna is wearing a black mini-skirt and a red low-cut blouse. When wrestling, Todd wears red trunks with "NB" written in white on the left side and "Nature Boy" written in white on the back of the trunks. Todd wears red boots with the letters "TN" in white on each boot.] MW: Newkirk is in the ring already, resting up as he waits for Magus to come out. JL: Rest up Newkirk, because this might be your first and last chance at the ICW World title! RA: AND HIS OPPONENT!!!! Hailing from Des Moines, Iowa! Accompagnied to the ring by his manager Natalie, weighing in a 241 pounds, here is THE CURRENT ICW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION..... MAGUS!!!!!!!!!!!! ["Until it Sleeps" by Metallica begins to play over the PA system, and the fans go absolutly nuts. Magus is wearing a suit like Bret Hart's that is black and dark blue with Magus in white old English writing. He has medium length brown/black hair and he's well toned.] MW: Huge ovation for the ICW champion! JL: I don't either of them, so I ain't gonna cheer. MW: Suit yourself. And look at both these two guys sportsmanship, they shake their hands before the match begins. Magus just congratulated Newkirk on his win I guess. JL: I find that so stupid man, first you wish the guy good luck, the next minute your trying to kill him to get the win. Why bother? MW: And this championship match is underway ladies and gentlemen! Newkirk and Magus lock up . . . Newkirk lifts Magus off the ground in chokeslam postion . . . but Magus boots him in the face! Magus goes up top while Newkirk is stunned . . . Magus flies off with a drop kick from up top! JL: Newkirk isn't ready for Magus's speed! He needs to just get a hold of him then its all over. MW: Newkirk goes for a clothesline . . . Magus ducks than lands a nice fist in Newkirk's gut! JL: Newkirk just had to last over an hour with 29 other guys, the man isn't going to last very long against a fresh Magus. MW; Magus lands a single leg take down then followed with a boot to Newkirk's head. Magus's got Newkirk by the arm as he climbs to the second rope . . Nice Japanease Armdrag off the second rope! Magus's got Newkirk tied up now with his arm behind his back . . . Magus rams his knee in Newkirk's lower back! JL: Newkirk breaks Magus's hold with an elbow to the face! Newkirk goes for the lock up, but Magus lands a drop toe hold into a side head lock! MW: Magus has switched that head lock into a choke hold! The referee is checking to see if its legal . . . he telling Magus to release it. JL: Magus is just ignoring the ref! The referee is making the three count. . . Magus releases the hold and now he's getting in referee's face! What's up with Magus tonight? MW: Newkirk clotheslines Magus from behind! Magus is layed out! Newkirk stomps of his head a few times before bringing back up for a Back Breaker!!! Newkirk tosses the limp Magus into the corner . . . following with a corner splash!!! Magus is draped over the ropes as Newkirk chokes him with the top! JL: Newkirk has stopped choking and goes for an irish whip across the ring . . Magus reverses it and sends Newkirk into the corner! MW: And Magus follows with a HANDSPRING ELBOW!!!!! Newkirk gets rocked! Magus goes to the tope rope and dives onto Newkirk with a splash!!!! Magus covers... 1................ JL: Newkirk throws off Magus! Newkirk doesn't look too happy and neither does Magus. He's complaining about a slow count! There wasn't even a count! MW: Newkirk just lands several forearm shots to Magus's head! Now Newkirk's setting him up for a Side Suplex!!! Magus is struggling to his feet . . Newkirk grabd him by the throat and takes him for a CHOKESLAM!!!! Newkirk goes for the cover with one foot... JL: But MAGUS SWEAPS HIS LEG!!! Magus follows with a standing drop kick as Newkirk gets back on his toes! Magus flies off the ropes with a Cross Body Block!!!! MW: The referee goes for the count... 1............... JL: Newkirk lifts Magus off and rolls him to the side! Newkirk goes to kick him but Magus ducks! Magus lands a Back Brain Kick!!! I love that move! MW: I don't think Newkirk does! Magus caught him real good! Magus is already going up top and flies off with clothesline knocking Newkirk back down to the mat! Magus is bouncing off the ropes as Newkirk gets back up again, Frankensteiner!!!!! That one came out of no where!!! JL: Magus covers again... 1............... 2................ MW: Newkirk gets his shoulder up so Magus gets back to his feet setting Newkirk up for a DDT . . . Newkirk blocks it! Newkirk nails Magus with a right hook cleanly in the face! JL: Magus is dazed! I think he bleeding from the lip! Magus flies off the ropes and nails Newkirk with leaping Shoulder Cannion!!! Magus flies off the ropes again, but Newkirk dodges his attack . . . MW: And Magus flies over the top to the outside floor! JL: Hehehe On the floor, now that's Newkirk's home court. Newkirk has already taken the liberty of exiting the ring. Newkirk lands a few blows into Magus before picking him up to drop him on the Guard Railing!!! MW: And we were having a clean match until this! Newkirk hurls Magus into the ring steps!!! Now he's got his head, but Magus blocks Newkirk's attempt at ramming his head into the steps! Magus knees Newkirk in the groin!!! JL: Magus hops up onto the apron and LANDS A BULLDOG ONTO THE FLOOR!!!!!!! MW: Magus gets back into the ring as Newkirk gets back to his feet . . . Magus dives out of the ring with a beautiful Plancha Dive!!! Newkirk's head gets rammed into the guard railing! Magus takes Newkirk back into the ring and covers... 1................ 2................ JL: Newkirk gets his shoulder up! Magus lifts him up for a Twilt-a-Whirl Back Breaker!!! And he covers again... 1................... 2.................... MW: Newkirk kicks out of the pin! Magus seems really concerned now. He looks at the referee before going for an armlock . . . which Newkirk breaks quickly! Newkirk with several closed fists then a HUGE KNEELIFT!!! Newkirk putting Magus in position for a piledriver . . . JL: Magus reverses it with a Back Body Drop!!! Magus gives Newkirk his own Kneelift!!! Magus tosses Newkirk into the corner and follows with a RUNNING VERTICAL SPLASH!!! Magus goes to the second rope for a FIST DROP!!! Magus is going wild!!! MW: Magus goes to the top again . . . but Newkirk shakes the ropes and Magus falls to the outside! JL: The referee is trying to keep Newkirk in the ring. MW: Magus rolls back into the ring. JL: Newkirk got Magus for a Gorrilla Press . . .and Magus comes crashing down to the mat! Newkirk lifts him up for an Atomic Drop, then some sort of a face slam!!! MW: Newkirk goes for the cover, we could have a new champion.. 1................. 2................. MW: Magus got his foot on the rope! Newkirk can't beleive it! He was that close to becoming the ICW World champion! Meanwhile, Magus gets up . . . JL: Magus charges Newkirk but Newkirk catches him into a powerslam!!!! MW: Magus rolls Newkirk up by surprise in a small package.. 1............... 2............... JL: Newkirk breaks out! Magus lifts Newkirk up for a DDT . . . but Newkirk sticks a thumb in his eye! Newkirk boots Magus in the gut and goes for the pin.. 1................. 2................. JL: 1/2 as Magus kicks out at the last second!!! MW: Newkirk is getting real tired he's been wrestling for over 90 minutes!! Magus gets to his feet and lands a SAVATE KICK AS NEWKIRK TURNS AROUND!!! Magus lifts up the stunned Newkirk for a DDT!!!!!!!! JL: Magus covers the big man! 1................. 2................. MW: Newkirk kicks up!!! Magus is now going at it with the referee about a slow count, but he sees Newkirk getting up . . . He lands another SAVATE KICK!!! JL: Now, he's going or THE EXECUTOR!!!!!!!!!! MW: He's got that thing locked on hard!! Newkirk is in too much pain! JL: The referee is checking on Newkirk.. MW: THERE'S THE BELL!! NEWKIRK COULDN'T HOLD ON!!! JL: Wimp! RA: This winner of this match... AND STILL ICW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, MAGUS!!!!!!! ["Until it Sleeps" by Metallica begis to play and the fans go nuts.] MW: What? The guy wins the royal rumble, and then had to face a fresh World champion in Magus. This is one of the toughest events in wrestling today, and Newkirk came so close. He deserves a whole lot of respect! JL: Respect? I guy who losers deserves no respect at all. MW: And what an ovation for Magus! And look what a great sport he is, he's helping up Newkirk and what a Kodak momnet! Magus and Newkirk have their arms up high and the crowd is going nuts! JL: Makes me want to puke! MW: Folks, thanks for joining up, we'll see you all next Sunday on Extreme! So long everyone!!!!! [Fireworks begin to go off as Magus and Newkirk shake each others hands and both pose for the crowd. But out of nowhere, two people jump into the ring and hammer both Newkirk and Magus with chair shot knocking both out cold. Both men begin to pound on Todd Newkirk... Credits begin to roll]
