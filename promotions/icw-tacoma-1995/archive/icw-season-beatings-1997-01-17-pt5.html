[ICW] Season Beating's Part V

17 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MW: Wow, things just never stop here in the ICW! First [name withheld] shows up, next Rick Showboat signs an ICW contract, "Diamond" Dan Round returns here tonight, what next!? JL: I think that question is just about to be answered! Some kind of distrurbance in the crowd as it appears that there is someone taking a seat in the front row.. I can't see who it is.... MW: Your eye sight is real bad isn't it, but then again, I can't see either. JL: That's because your old! MW: I'm in my golden ages ok? JL: OH SHIT!!! MW: Jerry!!! Your language!! JL: Who cares about that! LOOK AT WHO IT IS!!!!! MW: Oh my God, what is he doing here? Another surprise here in the ICW!!! JL: That's maybe the greatest World Champion of all time!!! And Todd Newkirk's biggest enemy! MW: THAT"S "ICEMAN" BRIAN KNIGHT!!!!! The man's held the NCW, FWLI and FoPEW World Titles, and he's taking a seat here for the royal rumble!!!! JL: Did he sign a contract too? MW: I don't know!! Not that I know of. Maybe he's just here to watch the ICW's best in action. JL: I dought that, if he's here, it's for a damned good reason, and I'm sure Newkirk is part of it!! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ICW ROYAL RUMBLE MATCH: -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 30 ICW Superstars, Total Mayem ------------------------------------------ MW: It's time to move on to our main event of the evening. JL: That's right Mike, tonight thirty of the ICW's top super stars will battle it out in an over the top rope royal rumble. I can't wait to see all of the action tonight! MW: Well I understand that all of the wrestlers have already drawn their entry number so lets hand things over to the ring announcer and get this match underway. [The control center switches to camera #2] RA: Ladies and gentlemen, here are the rules for tonight's royal rumble match. The match will begin with two ICW wrestlers and another wrestler will enter the ring every minute there after. The only way to eliminate a wrestler is by throwing him over the top rope. The wrestlers have already drawn numbers backstage and they will all enter the royal rumble according to the number that they drew. [Crowd pop] RA: Now that we all know the rules, lets get ready to RUUUUUUUUMBLE! Our first competitor coming down the aisle, hailing from Melbourne, Australia, weighing in at 229 pounds, here is "BULLYWUG" GLENN HUDSON!!!! ["Bullywug" Glenn Hudson walks down the aisle and enters the ring.] RA: And our second competitor, hailing from Starkville, Mississippi, weighing in at 250 pounds, here is the "NATURE BOY" TODD NEWKIRK!!!!!!! ["Nature Boy" Todd Newkirk makes his way down the aisle.] MW: The fans are really supporting both of these men and I think it is well deserved. These guys always put their heart and soul into every single match. JL: That may be true Mike but the bottom line is this match will be determined by what number you draw. I doubt we will see either of these men towards the end of the match. MW: I disagree with you Jerry but we shall soon find out as the ring announcer exits the ring and the referee signals for the opening bell. [The referee calls for the bell. ****DING****DING****DING****] JL: We are underway! Hudson and Newkirk are staring each other down right now, come on Hudson hit him where it hurts! MW: I didn't know you were a Glenn Hudson fan Jerry. JL: I'm not Mike, I just hate Newkirk more than I hate Hudson! Well Newkirk just got shoved by Hudson, now Newkirk retaliates and Hudson has a huge smile on his face. Hudson charges at Newkirk but Newkirk catches Hudson with a drop toe hold. What a cheater Newkirk has turned out to be Mike. MW: Newkirk is not a cheater! That was a smart move by Todd. Todd now has Hudson in a headlock and it's almost time for our third superstar to make his appearance. [The fans count down the seconds. 5...4...3...2...1...0] JL: "Hercules" Chris Harrison is the third man! ["Hercules" Chris Harrison makes his way down the aisle] MW: Hercules is now in the ring and he is not sure who to attack first. Newkirk has Hudson down on the matt and Harrison is still staring. Harrison walks over to Newkirk and he kicks him in on his lower back. Now Hercules lifts Newkirk up and power slams him to the mat. Hercules is covering him for the pin fall but that will not help him because the only way to eliminate someone is to throw them over the top rope. JL: Now Hudson is back on his feet and he is coming to the aid of Newkirk, what an idiot this Glenn Hudson is. Harrison saves him and this is the thanks he gets? You guys have to stick together if you want to win, actually they should just keep fighting each other because I don't want any of them to win. MW: Now Hudson and Newkirk are double teaming Harrison with a series of left and right hand punches to the gut. It's almost time for another wrestler to make his way to the ring. [The fans count down the seconds. 5...4...3...2...1...0] [J'ster makes his way down the aisle and receives a mixed reaction from the fans.] JL: The Joker has nothing on this man Mike, he has potential but he looks like he's already been through a war tonight. J'ster is inside of the ring and he is going to the aid of Harrison. J'ster nails Hudson with a drop kick to the face and he then goes after Newkirk. Newkirk and J'ster are exchanging right hand blows! Newkirk just knocked J'ster down to the mat, Harrison is kicking Hudson in the head! MW: Now Newkirk climbs up to the top turnbuckle and he is pointing to all of his fans. Now Newkirk leaps off the top turnbuckle but he lands face first on the mat. J'ster rolled out of the way just in the nick of time. Harrison is now taunting Hudson! JL: I told you J'ster is smart, he will be a tough guy in this rumble. That's how bad all of these guys want to win this match Mike, even the goodie two shoes are acting differently in this match. Harrison just picked up Hudson and whipped him into the turnbuckle. Harrison is charging in and he nails Hudson with a clothesline. Down goes Hudson! MW: Well it's almost that time again folks, who will be number 5? We will all find out soon enough. [The fans count the seconds down. 5...4...3...2...1...0] [Sable Dragon makes his way down the aisle.] JL: Darn it! When will the real stars make they way to the ring? This is getting worse than a soap opera! Well this Sable Dragon character is now inside of the ring and he decides to take a breather in the corner. MW: Breather? He hasn't done anything yet, he's just standing there watching everyone else fight. JL: It's like my mother always said Mike, why do anything if someone else will do it for you instead? Right now he is in good shape and he might be able to get away with this for a while. Sable Dragon just chop blocked Harrison's knee now he's working on his other knee. This guy is trying to turn Hercules into a cripple! MW: He actually did Hudson a favor, now while Hudson rests J'ster has Newkirk in a boston crab. This is actually a good move by J'ster. Hudson is still down on the mat in pain and we are a few seconds away from out sixth rumble participant. [The fans count the seconds down. 5...4...3...2...1...0] ["The Fantasy" Brett Young makes his way down the aisle] JL: Oh not another one! Where the hell is the real talent? I'm going to file a protest with the ICW committee about this situation. Well Brett Young is in the ring and he immediately goes after Hudson. He's stomping on Hudson with those huge feet of his. Newkirk is still in that Boston crab hold and Harrison is still down on the mat in pain. MW: Sable is now setting Harrison up for a figure four, he wraps his leg around and he has the hold locked in! Harrison is screaming out in pain. JL: Isn't this great Mike? We've got two guys tied up in knots like pretzels and another guy is on the mat getting stomped on like a cheap grape. I love it! MW: J'ster has the hold locked in but Newkirk is trying to reach the ropes. I don't think the ropes can save Newkirk in this type of match, the ref isn't even inside of the ring. Harrison has just reversed the figure four leg lock but Sable releases the hold in a hurry. Harrison is still in a lot of pain and Sable is slowly getting up off of the canvas. Brett Young is doing quite a number on Hudson, he has him in an abdominal stretch. JL: So far I have to say Brett Young is the man to beat, he's the freshest one and he has not suffered any punishment yet. I think it's time for the next victim to make his way down to the ring. [The fans count the seconds down. 5...4...3...2...1...0] ["Diamond" Dan Round makes his way towards the ringside.] MW: That's Diamond Dan Round, a former two time champ Jerry. JL: Former champ is the key word Mike, but lets see if he still has what it takes. I still can't believe what is happening here tonight with the numbers that everyone has received. MW: Dan Round is in the ring and he just clobbered Brett Young with a double axe handle blow to the lower back. Now Dan DDT's Brett Young, Dan is staring at Hudson and he kicks him while he is laying on the matt. Now Dan picks up Hudson and puts he sets him up high in the air, SPINEBUSTER BY DAN ROUND! JL: This guy has been impressive so far but lets see how he will do in the long run. Newkirk is yelling out loud and is grabbing the bottom rope but J'ster refuses to relinquish the hold. Sable is now tying Harrison up in the ropes, WOW WHAT A DROP KICK BY SABLE! Harrison is lucky he was tied up, otherwise we would have been out of here. MW: Dan Round is now going to work on Brett Young, Brett Young gets irish whipped into the ropes and Dan catches Young off the ropes and power slams him to the mat. Hudson is crawling on all fours just trying to survive and Sable is just kicking away on a defenseless Harrison. J'ster has finally released the hold but the damage has been done. JL: It's hard to believe but it's almost time to introduce the next wrestler who is about to enter this madness known as the royal rumble. [The fans count down the seconds. 5...4...3...2...1...0] [Othello Anthony makes his way towards ringside.] MW: Othello Anthony is the eight man to come down the aisle and so far no man has been eliminated. Anthony is inside of the ring and he goes right after Hudson. Anthony with a big leg drop to the back of Hudson's head. Now Anthony lifts Hudson up and nails him with an atomic drop. J'ster has kicked Newkirk out of the ring but Newkirk will be allowed to reenter the ring because he was not thrown over the top rope. Sable is still chopping away on Harrison in the corner and Dan Round now has Brett Young in a choke hold. JL: Go for the family jewels Dan! Hey that Dan is not such a bad guy, too bad he cares what the fans think about him. That makes him a loser. Well I'm looking around this crowded ring now and I see Sable choking Harrison against the ropes. I also see J'ster outside of the ring whacking Newkirk with a steel chair and I see Dan Round choking the life out of Brett Young. Anthony now has Hudson in a sleeper hold. Who does he think he is Piper? MW: Now the clock is winding down and the fans are just dying to find out who the next wrestler is going to be. [The fans count the seconds down. 5...4...3...2...1...0] ["The Shocker" Brian Anthony makes his way towards the ring.] JL: The ninth man is Brian Anthony, he looks very determined but I don't think he will go far in this match but we shall soon find out. MW: Brian Anthony is still outside of the ring and he is going after J'ster who is still outside pounding on Newkirk. Brian Anthony grabs J'ster by his head and throws him into the steel guard rail. Brian is now kicking J'ster's left knee. Anthony lifts J'ster up and suplexes him onto the concrete floor! JL: J'ster is wiggling around like a spineless jelly fish! Back inside of the ring Sable is raking Harrison's eyes and Hudson has Othello Anthony in a reverse arm bar hold. Dan Round is still giving Brett Young a hard time over in the corner. Now Dan is pounding Brett's head into the top turnbuckle. I can't believe it's time for our next wrestler to make his way down the aisle. [The fans count the seconds down. 5...4...3...2...1...0] [Shawn Riser makes his way to the ring.] MW: It's Shawn Riser, this guy is truly a gifted athlete Jerry. JL: Well he is something in my opinion but gifted is not the word that comes to mind. [Jerry laughs] MW: We now have ten ICE super stars in that ring, well actually we have 7 in the ring and 3 out of the ring but your people at home understand what I mean. Brian Anthony has just thrown J'ster back inside of the ring and Brian follows him in. Newkirk is still out on the floor and Sable still has Harrison tied up in between the ropes. Sean Riser is inside of the ring being extremely cautious and Othello Anthony now has Hudson in a surf board. Brett Young has fallen through the second rope to the outside and Dan Round follows him to the outside floor. JL: Newkirk is laying flat on his back looking up at the ceiling! Newkirk is right for the picking but no one notices him out there with all of this other action in the ring. So far no one has been eliminated and all ten of these men are in a dog fight. Newkirk may still be outside but he has been worked on severely. [The fans count the seconds down. 5...4...3...2...1...0] [Mark Malone makes his way down the aisle.] MW: Well it's Mark Malone and he looks a little upset. Maybe he was hoping for a higher number in the draw. Well Mark is now inside of the ring and Shawn Riser goes right at him! Riser belts Malone with a right hand and Malone retaliates with a right hand of his own. Now Shawn picks him up and scoop slams him to the mat. Newkirk is still taking a breather on the outside and Harrison is finally free from the ropes. Sable now has Harrison on the tope rope but Harrison manages to give Sable a thumb to the eye which allows him to get off of the ropes. JL: Brett Young gave Dan Round a back breaker and Othello Anthony now has Hudson in a bear hug. Othello is really working on Hudson's lower back. Brian Anthony is on the second turnbuckle, he leaps off, and he delivers an elbow to the back of J'ster's head. I wonder if Newkirk will ever get back in that ring? [The fans count the seconds down. 5...4...3...2...1...0] [Rick Showboat makes his way down the aisle.] MW: Here comes Rick Showboat Jerry. I wonder if he is prepared for a match of this magnitude on such short notice. JL: First of all Mike it's Mr. Showboat to you! Now to answer your second question Rick Showboat is prepared for any situation. He is the man to win this thing tonight Mike. I'm glad to see we have some worthy competition in this match after all, I was beginning to get nautious. MW: If Rick Showboat is so great then why is he coming to ringside with a cane? JL: That's just because he has a sore ankle but he is such a great athlete that he told me he would not miss this match for anything. MW: Oh brother. [shakes his head] Well Showboat is outside staring at Newkirk now and Showboat is smiling at him. Showboat leans over and apparently he is saying something to Newkirk. Showboat is helping him up to his feet and NewKirk is now smiling as well. Showboat shakes his hand and Showboat enters the ring while Newkirk remains on the outside. JL: Showboat has just made a friend here tonight Mike, I told you Rick is a decent guy. ShowBoat walks up to Shawn Riser and he nails him with that cane! Shawn is on the mat in serious pain. Rick picks up Mark Malone and he motioning him to attack Riser. Malone walks up to Riser, stands him on his own two feet and begins to unload a series big right hands. Riser falls down to the mat once again and Malone turns around. Showboat is waiting for him and WHAM!!!!! Showboat just whacked Mark Malone with that wooden crane! MW: Showboat picks Malone up and body slams him to the mat while still holding onto that wooden cane. Now Showboat picks Malone up and he tosses him over the top rope. Mark Malone is the first man to get eliminated in this match! JL: Yeah Showboat threw that bum out like yesterday's garbage! The fans are now counting down the seconds. [The fans count down the seconds. 5...4...3...2...1...0] [Dark Vanity is making his way towards the ring.] MW: Dark Vanity is the thirteenth man and he looks ready for this one. Othello Anthony now stands Hudson up on his feet and he throws a wild right hand that misses Hudson. Hudson throws a right hand of his own and he stuns Othello Anthony. He grabs Othello and he executes a swinging neck breaker! Now Hudson puts Othello in a sleeper hold! JL: Othello is going out fast! Newkirk has finally decided to get back in the ring. Showboat is outside of the ring now hiding his cane and Harrison just drop kicked Sable through the second rope. Brett Young and Dan Round are still slugging it out and Brian Anthony has J'ster in an STF submission hold. Shawn Riser is finally back on his feet after getting hammered by Showboat's cane. Riser sees Showboat outside and he goes right after him. Dark Vanity is just standing outside of the ring waiting for the perfect moment to strike. MW: Brian Anthony just drop kicked J'ster over the top rope and onto the floor! J'ster has been eliminated. Hudson just released the sleeper hold on Othello Anthony is leaning against the rope for support. Hudson charges in and catches him with a tremendous clothesline which sends Othello Anthony over the top rope. Othello is history folks. [The fans count the seconds down. 5...4...3...2...1...0] ["Badass" Dave Dixon makes his way down the aisle.] JL: Here comes the BADASS himself! I'm looking forward to seeing this guy. Harrison just powerslammed Sable and the ring literally shook after that move. Harrison now lifts Sable up and he throws Sable out over the top rope. Sable is history ladies and gentlemen. Now Shawn Riser has caught up with Rick Showboat and he is clobbering Rick with big right hand shots. Showboat fires back but it isn't affecting Shawn, now Shawn runs right at Rick and Showboat moves out of the way. Riser runs right into the steel guard rail and Showboat moves in for the kill. Badass is now inside of the ring and he is met by Dark Vanity who greets him with a right hand punch. MW: Brian Anthony grabs Harrison from behind and he catches Harrison with a reverse neck breaker. Newkirk runs over and he helps Brian Anthony double team Harrison. NewKirk is holding Harrison while Anthony climbs up to the top turnbuckle, Anthony leaps off and nails Harrison with a double ax handle to the forehead. Harrison falls and Newkirk begins kicking Harrison in the head. Anthony motions Newkirk to stop and Anthony picks Harrison up, Newkirk helps Anthony and they both carry Harrison over to the ropes. Newkirk and Anthony can't throw Harrison over the top rope. Now Newkirk and Anthony are exchanging blows. JL: Well good old Hercules falls back onto the canvas [Jerry laughs.] Brett Young just walked up from behind and head butted Dark Vanity and Badass into each other. Both men fall down hard on the matt. [The fans count down. 5...4...3...2...1] ["Rudedog" Alex Reed makes his way down the aisle.] MW: Brett Young picks up Badass and he slingshot suplexes him onto Dark Vanity. Now Young rolls Badass off of Vanity and he proceeds to throw Vanity into the ropes. Vanity springs off of the ropes and Brett gives him a boot to the forehead that sends him all the way back to the ropes. Brett Young picks up Badass and he throws him into Vanity. Both men have fallen over the top rope! Dark Vanity and Badass can both say good night Irene. JL: Brett Young is laughing at both men and Alex Reed just drop kicked Brett Young from behind. Brett Young is dangling on the top rope and Alex Reed is punching Brett Young with his right hand while trying to push him off of the top rope with his left hand. Hudson joins Alex Reed and both men are now pounding on Brett Young. MW: Dan Round just came out of nowhere and pulled Alex Reed away from Brett. Dan just suplexed Reed while Hudson is still trying to eliminate Young. Meanwhile Rick Showboat is on the outside choking Shawn Riser with some thick video cable that is laid out at ringside. It's almost time for the next wrestler to make his appearance. [The fans count down the seconds. 5...4...3...2...1...0] [White Angel makes his way down the aisle.] JL: Well look what we have here, it's the White Angel. [Jerry chuckles] MW: Don't judge a book by it's cover Jerry, this guy is awesome. JL: Yeah I've heard it before Mike so spare me the drama, I'll just watch the match thank you very much. Well now Dan Round is walking over to Hudson and he clips takes Hudson's right knee out with a chop block. The force of that blow caused Hudson to collide with Brett Young. Brett Young falls over the top rope and onto the concrete floor. Bye bye Brett my boy! [Jerry laughs] MW: Todd Newkirk just attacked the White Angel from behind! Newkirk with a belly to back suplex, then Newkirk follows it up with a pile driver. Newkirk sets him up for the figure four leg lock, he gets him with the figure four. The Angel is screaming at the top of his lungs, I think the Angel would give up if he could. JL: Dan Round is still roughing up Hudson while Brian Anthony goes to work and Alex Reed. Shawn Riser is laying down outside of the ring and Showboat is climbing up to the ring apron. Showboat leaps off and Riser moves out of the way. Rick went for a flying cross body splash but he ended up hitting the floor. Somebody should disqualify Shawn for cheating. [The fans count down the seconds. 5...4...3...2...1...0] ["Bad Boy" Mark Bagwell makes his way down to ringside.] MW: Now this is a man people have to worry about in this match. His nick name describes him well. The one and the only Mark Bagwell. Riser is on the outside using Rick Showboats stomach for field goal practice! Now he grabs Rick and tosses him inside of the ring. Bagwell is now inside of the ring and he just yanked Dan Round by his hair, he's yelling into his ear and he executes a snap smare suplex. Dan Round on the mat and Bagwell wastes no time in dropping a knee on Round's chest. Alex Reed has just been eliminated! I missed it! JL: I didn't, Alex Reed had the White Angel a bit staggered and he ran in full speed for the kill, White Angel hit the deck and pulled the top rope down. The rest is history and so is Alex Reed. The White Angel just got leveled by a Hudson clothesline!
