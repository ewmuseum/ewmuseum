[ICW] Season Beating's Part IV

17 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ICW CRUISERWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH: -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= "Sarfox" Curt Crane vs. "Shotgun" Chris Scott ------------------------------------------ RA: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit . . . and it is for the ICW CRUISERWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP!!!! [cheers from the fans in anticipation of the contest] ["I Want It All" by Queen blares over the speakers, a mixed crowd reaction to the music] RA: First, let me introduce the challenger, accompanied by his manager Fara Phoenix, from Sydney River, Nova Scotia, Canada, weighing in at 236 pounds, he is . . . CURT "STARFOX" CRANE!!!! [The lights dim to red as Crane approaches the ring, in gold tights with black boots and the word "Starfox" written on one leg of the tights. He is accompanied by his manager, Fara Phoenix. A red spotlight follows him to the ring. Instead of his normal energetic entrance, he has a determined look on his face. He jumps over the top rope into the ring and whips off his black training jacket, then reclines into a corner] MW: A serious look on the challenger's face! What's your pick for this match, 2?? JL: Well, this one should be a classic, no doubt about it!! Look for an aerial assault by Crane, and look for Scott to counter this with his own aerial maneuvers. This one should be an aerial battle, the likes of which we've never seen!! In the end, I've gotta go with Crane. He's got the experience and the confidence to take this one!! MW: I disagree. The Shotgun has been, if anything, one of the most vocal champions in ICW history!! He's got the confident, and he's got the talent, and I think he's gonna walk out here in possession of that ICW Cruiserweightt belt!! JL: But you've gotta count for the Phoenix Squad!! "The Shocker" Brian Anthony is in the building, and you've gotta wonder how he's gonna play a part in this. MW: Well, there's only one way to find out!! Let's go back to ringside for the champ's introduction!! RA: And his opponent, from Buffula, New York, weighing in at 240 pounds, he is the ICW Cruisereight Champion . . . "SHOTGUN" CHRIS SCOTT!!!!! DING!! DING!! DING!! MW: All right! This one's set to get underway! Crane, vying for his second ICW title, going up against the champion, Scott. The two are measuring each other up. They meet in the center of the ring, and Scott is bad-mouthing Crane!! And Crane is just returning the insults!! JL: There's no question that there's a lot of similarity between the two. MW: Looks like the insults are through, and Scott just SPIT right in Crane's face!! [groans from the fans] JL: Crane wipes the spit off, and SLAPS Scott right in the face!! MW: This one's set to get rowdy! Crane and Scott lock up, Scott takes Crane down with a quick headlock take over, and turns it into a head-scissors! JL: Crane, trying to escape the hold, puts his back on the mat and does a nip-up to a vertical basis!! Scott gets to his feet, and Crane with a quick armdrag takedown!! MW: Now Scott put down on the canvas!! Trying to shake the fox but Crane has that armbar cinched in pretty good! Scott manages to reach his knees, but still can't shake the armbar!! Manages a little leverage, runs around the ring, and dives towards the ropes!! And DITCHES CRANE through the ropes to the outside!! [mixture of cheers and jeers] JL: Crane quick to get to his feet, and even quicker to get to the apron!! And SCOTT SENDS HIM CRASHING DOWN WITH A RIGHT HAND!! Crane dazed on the outside, and Scott goes OVER THE TOP WITH A FLYING BODY PRESS! And now he's laying the rights on Crane outside the ring!! [crowd pop as they are getting into this match] MW: The referee is laying down the count, but Scott can loose this thing outside the ring and still keep the belt! Scott picks Crane up by the hair and whips him to the post! But Crane reverses and Scott goes FLYING INTO THE RING POST!! (*dink*) Crane follows in but Scott avoids it and Crane hits the post!! JL: A surprisingly amateur move by Crane! MW: Maybe that shows just how bad he wants it!! At any rate, both wrestlers have re-entered the ring and it looks like Scott has the upper hand, stomping on Crane's head!! Picks up the fox and hooks the head . . . TEXTBOOK SUPLEX!! Scott for the cover. 1............ MW: Kickout by Crane!! This thing's too early yet!! JL: Even so, Scott is looking good here early in the contest! Takes Crane over to the corner, and he's laying those short knees into the stomach of Crane! Irish whips Crane to the other corner, and comes in with a DROPKICK TO THE CORNER!! MW: He absorbed the full force of that dropkick! Scott takes Crane in a headlock . . . DDT!! Cover!! 1............ 2............. Kickout!! JL: Scott's yelling at some fans in the front row with a "Starfox" sign!! [boos by the fans] He needs to follow up, not yell at the fans!! MW: It's amazing how split these fans are about this contest! On any given day, they'd be booing either of these stars!! JL: Scott takes Crane over to the corner, laying in those short knees again. Looks like he's trying to knock the wind out of Crane. Sends him to the other corner . . . BIG CORNER SPLASH!! And Crane had better get some offense quickly, or this one will be over! I'm surprised how well Scott is handling Crane! MW: Scott, picks Crane up and send him to the ropes . . . standing dropkick!! Picks him up again and sends him to the ropes once more! Connects with a clothes line . . . NO!! Crane ducks it and bounces off the ropes!! Ducks another clothesline and . . . . SPINNING HOOK KICK TO SCOTT!! [mixed reactions, "ooos" from the fans] JL: He caught Scott square on the side of the head with that!! Beautifully executed!! MW: Crane's getting his wind in the corner, waiting for Scott to get to his feet. Scott, dazed, finally gets up . . . SAVATE KICK BY CRANE!!! [*snap* as the foot hits Scott's chin] JL: Crane carefully circling Scott, landing a few stomps to the head and neck area. Looks like he's going to concentrate on the neck area of Scott, possibly in anticipation of his flying legdrop finisher, "The Guillotine Drop". MW: Crane backs Scott into the corner, and SLAPS him in the face!! (Boos) And now he's going to work with some knife edge chops!! Whips Scott to the other side and DROPKICKS Scott as he wanders out!! JL: Crane, leaps up to the top rope . . . FLYING AXEHANDLE!!! COVER!! 1............... 2............... JL: KICKOUT!! MW: It's gonna take more than that to dethrone Scott!! JL: True, but Crane can do some deadly things with the advantage!! He wasn't named "ICW Wrestler of the Year" for nothing!! MW: Crane goes back to work on Scott. Sits him down in the corner and starts stomping on the neck!! Solid, if not brutal, strategy by Crane. And now Crane's laying in the chokeholds on Scott!! JL: The referee goes in to break it up, and Crane almost backhands him!! MW: That's an easy way to loose the match!! Fara's admonishing Crane, and yelling at him to follow up. JL: Crane picks up Scott now and brings him to the center of the ring. Sets up Scott in a front facelock, swings Scott around . .
