[ICW] is now opened!

20 November 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Internet Championship Wrestling is now opened and accepting new entries. Our current roster is about 25, but we want to expand to about 40-50 in total. We have two cards a week "Monday Night Extreme" and "Thursday Night's Main Event". For all the rules and current roster of the ICW, check out the home page at: http://www.odyssee.net/~badboy/icw.html If your interested in joining, e-mail me with the subject heading ICW NEW ENTRY. Please send me a flash/interview with the wrestler(s) as well. You can send a maximium of 3 wrestlers and can combine two of them to form a tag team. If you don't have a web browser, please let me know and I'll e-mail you the ICW rules.
