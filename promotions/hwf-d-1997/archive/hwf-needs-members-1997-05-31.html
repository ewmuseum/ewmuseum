HWF Needs Members

31 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JOIN THE HWF! The Hardcore Wrestling Federation is accepting members! just fill out the form below and send it to me. (If yopu have a tag team, you must fill out 1 for each member, just tell me that they're a tag team) Wrestler's Name: Height: Weight: Finisher: Submission or regular: Brief description of finisher: Manager: Any special Foreign object? Will he use chairs: good or bad: What Style:(put an X next to only one style) Amature Technition(old Bob Backlund style) Brawler/technition(Bret Hart) Top Notch Brawler(Kevin Nash) Stiff Martial Artist/Grappler(Great Muta) Basic High Flyer(Syxx,Ultimo Dragon) Basic Brawler(Steve McMichaels) All Around(Sting) Submission(Ken Shamrock) Basic Wrestler(Jesse James "aka Jobber") Brawler/aierelly skilled(Vader) Daredevil(Sabu,Cactus Jack) I will make a list of the people who are in. I am setting up Tournaments to see who the champions are going to be. The first 16 people to join will have the chance to be in the HWF World Heavyweight title tourny. The other belts are: HWF Intercontinental Title(second place in world title tourny gets this) HWF World Death Match Title(seperate tourny) HWF TV Title(third place in world title tourny gets this) HWF Lightweight Title(seperate tourny) HWF World Tag Team Titles(seperate tourny) HWF U.S. Tag Team Titles(second place in tag team tourny) You may enter these tournaments, just tell me which one. You may only enter one wrestler in a tournament!For ex. if you have The punisher, and Devil, you cant enter them both in the HWF World Title Tourny, but you can enter onr in the World Tourny and one in the Death Match tourny! You can have up to 4 wrestlers,one may be a real wrestler such as, Sting, the others you must make. There will be at least 4 cards per week: Monday Night Prime Time Tuesday Night Terror Thursday Night Havoc Friday Night Blood Bath and every other week there will be Saturday Night Main Event. To challenge someone, put your wrestlers name in followed by a colon, and state the challenge, what kind of match, championship, spot in the top ten or whetever, and when. You can also put a message to the wrestler. Heres an example Sting:Syxx, I challenge you for your spot in the top ten at Monday Night Prime Time, do you accept punk? Then you send this out to everyone. There will also be a PPV each month, these will vary. As soon as I have 16 people, I will make the tournaments and wil send out a list of screen names and wrestlers. There is no member limit, so don;t worry about being kicked out. If you want to create a stable, just tell me!
