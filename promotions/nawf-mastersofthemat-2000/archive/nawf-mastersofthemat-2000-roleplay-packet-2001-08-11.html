NAWF Roleplay Packet 8-11-01

August 11, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- NAWF Roleplay Packet Packet #2 8-11-01 -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Table of Contents 1) "Homeless" Jesus 2) Mordred 3) Hellraiser -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 1) "Homeless" Jesus Title- The Homeless Icon! *Date: Tuesday, August 7, 2001* *Location: Greyhound Bus Station in Las Vegas, NV* *Time: 9:15 PM* [Cameras open up to the outside of the Greyhound Bus Station as one can see buses pulling in and out of the place. People are also seen walking in and out of the bus station with bags of luggage. Cameras cut to the inside of the bus station, the room is well lit and many people are sitting around waiting to board their buses, there is also a line of people waiting to purchase tickets.
Cameras pan around as it catches Homeless Jesus sitting in the corner of the room on the floor beside a Coke machine. Homeless Jesus is wearing a dirty, torn up, worn out, heavily stained blue denim jacket, with a dirty white tank top underneath; a pair of dirty, torn up, blue jeans; and a pair of torn up black boots. Homeless Jesus is holding a bible in his right hand, he looks up at the camera with his dirty face.]
Homeless Jesus: Jesus here folks! Just sitting back here waiting to board my bus to Detroit, where on the eighteenth of this month, your lord and savior will take his first step--since my latest resurrection of course--to SAVE the world of pro-wrestling. And by how you ask? By defeating a guy by the name of
Mordred. Just by the sound of his name, he sounds evil, he probably looks evil too, but above all else, I sense a vibe that he's probably a minion of that beast that I defeated long ago, Satan. And if all that's the case, then YOUR
HERO and EVERYONE'S HERO, me...Jesus Christ, will have all the motivation in the world to take out this evil entity, Mordred.
Loud Speaker Voice: Bus 515 to Houston, Texas now boarding......Bus 515 to
Houston, Texas now boarding.
Homeless Jesus: Ya know, it's not going to be an easy job, but somebody's got to do it. This guy Mordred, if he is to win this match, then he could possibly continue to spread the message of evil that Satan has corrupted him with, and that's why I, Jesus, YOUR SAVIOR, is going to beat some sense into him on the eighteenth of this month. Maybe I can beat some good into him, and maybe after our match, he will see the light....or maybe not. Now, I know what many of you are thinking right now...."how can a guy like Jesus resort to violence? Jesus is a peaceful character isn't he?" Well folks, sometimes you just HAVE TO resort to violence. I mean, how do you think I defeated Satan thousands of years ago? By a foot race? No! I gave him the 'ole Holly one-two, and just like I will give Mordred the ole' Holly one-two.
[Homeless Jesus jumps to his feet as he starts throwing pathetic looking punching combos into the air, he looks like some first grader trying to impersonate an episode of "Power Rangers."]
Homeless Jesus: Now, not only is this match a contest between good versus evil, the winner of this match advances in a tournament to crown a new United
States Champion. And with a championship belt, means a big pay check! As many of you know, the world has changed a lot since my previous existence here, and well, life hasn't exactly treated me--although it should, since I died for all your guy's sins--the best, but just thinking about winning that championship belt makes my mouth water, and why? Because with a big pay check, gone are the days where Jesus is sitting on a street corner asking to "borrow" money, gone are the days of $0.49 tacos at Taco De Mexico, and IN are the days of $3.99
Whopper Value Meals at Burger King, and more cheap vodka that my Holly mind can ever imagine. And if that's not enough motivation to win, I don't know what is.
[Homeless Jesus looks up into the sky, imagining the benefits of being a champion. Homeless Jesus starts spinning around like a idiotic little school girl all while in a euphoric trance dreaming of the possibilities.]
Homeless Jesus: Mmmmmm, Whoppers.....cheese, tomatoes, lettuce! And.....French fries....Mmmmmmmm! [Just then, Homeless Jesus bumps into a middle aged man walking by. The
Middle-aged man shoves Homeless Jesus aside.]
Middle-Aged Man: Watch where you're going you idiot. [Homeless Jesus snaps out of his trance as he gives the man who is walking away a stern look. Then, Homeless Jesus reaches into his pocket pulls out a folded piece of paper with a pen, cameras zoom in as one can see the paper titled,
"Going to Hell!" as Homeless Jesus writes a description of the middle-aged man who just pushed him onto the list of growing names on the sheet.]
Homeless Jesus: Ya see Mordred, just like that, you can go to Hell! [Screaming at the Middle-aged man walking away.] Homeless Jesus: YOU JUST MADE A BIG MISTAKE PAL! [Facing the camera once again.] Homeless Jesus: And is that where you want to end up at Mordred, huh? Hell? Well, then I suggest you no-show at Detroit on the eighteenth of this month, or you, Mordred, could very well be added to this list of growing names.
[Homeless Jesus displays the list to the camera while nodding his head in assurance.] Loud Speaker Voice: Last call for Bus 515 leaving for Houston, Texas.....Bus
515 bound for Houston, Texas will be departing in five minutes....Once again,
Bus 515 will be leaving for Houston, Texas in five minutes.
Homeless Jesus: Mordred, I hope I can deprogram whatever Satan has implanted into your mind, and hey, maybe after the match, we can be friends! Oh yeah,
I'm always looking for a more mortals to carry the good message of Christ.
Remember Mordred, this upcoming match will be for your own good. Ya know, you might not thank me right away, but one day, I'm sure that you'll come around.
Remember....Jesus loves you Mordred.....but not in a homosexual kind of way. [Homeless Jesus makes a pouting face as he shakes his hands putting emphasis on his last statement.]
Loud Speaker Voice: Bus 200 to Detroit, Michigan now boarding.......Bus 200 to
Detroit, Michigan now boarding.
Homeless Jesus: Well, that's my call, look out Detroit, cause Jesus is on his way! [Cameras follow Homeless Jesus as he makes his way out of the building. Homeless Jesus however takes a back route avoiding the ticket collectors where people who actually pay for tickets would go through. Cameras cut away to a parked Bus 200 bound for Detroit as Homeless Jesus approaches the bus, he then looks to his left, then to his right looking out for people, then in a quick move, he reaches under the bus for the luggage department, throws a couple of bags out and jumps in while closing the door behind him....a cheap homeless traveling scheme! A couple of seconds later, the door of the luggage department opens as Homeless Jesus gives the cameras a quick thumbs up before closing the door again. Cameras fade.]
-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 2) Mordred Title- Mordred in the huse (Scene opens on Mordred doing pull-ups in an expensive looking gym. As Mordred is at the top of one of his reps, he hears a knock at the door. Mordred scowls before getting down and walking to the door. Mordred opens the door to a young black woman wearing a stylish black skirt suit standing in the corridor outside the gym.)
Monica White: Hello, I'm here from the NAWF to talk to you about your upcoming match on Saturday. My name is Monica W....
Mordred: The Lord of Steel knows who you are. But where are my manners? Please, come in. MW: Um, thank you. (Monica enters and Mordred closes the door behind her.) M: You'll have to excuse the Black Knight. I'll admit I wasn't expecting visitors. MW: Really? I was told it had all been arranged with your agent. M: That doesn't surprise me. You'd be amazed how little we actually communicate. Still, if you'd met the little oik you'd understand why.
(Monica smiles uneasily for a second.) M: Now then. Why don't you find somewhere to sit. I have to say, gyms aren't the best place for this sort of thing. Anyway, let us begin so I can get back to training.
(Monica sits on a bench and pulls out an electronic notepad. Mordred pulls one up and sits opposite her. Mordred looks expectantly at Monica.)
MW: OK, so, next Saturday, on the inaugural event you are headlining in a cage match against "Homeless" Jesus. Now you've obviously been training hard for your match. Are you worried at all?
(Mordred raises his eyebrow.) M: What ever gave you that impression? No, the Lord of Steel is more than confident about the outcome of his match next Saturday. The Black Knight fears no man. Having said that, as the epitome of mental and martial prowess the
Black Knight is, it is always wise to be prepared. After all, I wouldn't want to beat this "Homeless" person into a bloody pulp, only to be too tired to climb out of the cage. Still, The Lord of Steel doesn't see that as being a problem. Not for a second.
MW: You sound confident of being able to beat "Homeless" Jesus. I take it you have scouted him. How much do you know about this competitor?
M: Lamentably, not as much as the Lord of Steel would like. To paraphrase Sun
Tzu, "He who knows both himself and his foe may never fear defeat." The Black
Knight is used to never fearing defeat. However, as the Black Knight has no reference from which to draw knowledge on his opponent on Explosion, I must rely on my vast intelligence and arsenal of technical abilities in order to out-think and out-wrestle this shaven ape who calls himself "Homeless" Jesus.
By the very nature of a cage match, the Lord of Steel is fully aware of the inescapable proximity of one's opponent. But I shall not bore you with tactics.
Rest assured the main event at the inaugural event of the NAWF will be one to remember. MW: Great. I'm sure there are a lot of people who will be looking forward to it. So do you have any other comments?
M: Indeed I do. Now as I'm sure you'll realise, The Lord of Steel is markedly younger than most if not all of the other competitors on the roster. At only
21, I have, in my relatively short career, had much success in this business, holding several title belts in more than one federation. Since coming to wrestle in America, I have met with meteoric success. This is of course quite understandable, for I, Mordred, am by far superior in every way to most if not all the competitors I have worked with in America. The Black Knight is the very avatar of valour. As the Black Knight has risen to the top of so many other organisations, so it will be with the NAWF. At the soonest possible opportunity, the Lord of Steel shall take the North American Championship, as well as any other titles the Black Knight can lay his hands on. In fact that draws me to another point. What small amount of research the Black Knight has undertaken as to the nature of his competitors, a man by the name of Walter
Munson has come to my attention. I understand he fancies himself as quite the
American hero, but to the Black Knight he is just another small minded, genetically inferior oaf that by his very existence disgusts me. But that is of little import at this time. The Lord of Steel will ensure he is at peak condition for his cage match on the 18th, then I, Mordred, will with great zeal and dedication become the first Englishman to hold the NAWF's most coveted title. Now then, is there anything else you would like to ask the Lord of
Steel, Miss White?
MW: No, I believe I have enough here. It was, um, good to meet you. M: The pleasure was all mine Miss White. (Mordred takes Monica's hand and kisses it.) M: Now then, allow me to see you out so that I may finish my workout. (Mordred opens the door for Monica and she leaves. Mordred closes the door as the scene fades out.)
