AW-WELCOME TO THE SHOW THAT'S GONNA MAKE YOU REALLY WANNA PRESS THAT ORDER PPV BUTTON! RB- That's right ANthony, tonight only 1 man can survive the Flamming Ladder as well as the World title match.
AW- Well I really don't want to do this but we have to. Let's send it to the loud mouth Cole Parker he's got Mantis.
[Fades over to parker.] CBP- IT'S COLE "BOOMER" Parker! GOT IT! M- I sense some hostility here. Maybe I should go. CBP- No no. Mantis what's the plan? You got Cyric Logan! M- Cyric Logan my ass! The only thing that will be good about this match is that I'm gonna kick Logan's ass, the get what me and Johnson want! That's right the Tag Team titles!
CBP- But guess what? M- huh? CBP- You two are the ONLY NAWF Tag Team. M- Well hopefully someone WILL STEP UP! CBP- Ok good. But how are going to accomplish that? M- You will see! AW- Ok I can tell it's a sensitive subject. Well since we are LIVE on ABC let's goto commerical break!
[Fades to commericals for D.O.C. Eyewear, Campbell's soup, preview of Who
Wants to be a millionare for Tonight. Then Fades back over to Boulda &
Walker.] AW- Welcome back to NAWF Lowdown, The Masters of the Mat Pay Per View Preview show Live on ABC.
RB- Oh yes, well we got some good things going on. Let's take a look at this interviewed that the former NAWF employee Shawn McNeil cut with TheBroker, infact it was suppose toa ir on Terror.
[Fades over to McNeil and Broker.] SN-Broker how do you plan on wrestling in this match setup by Fredricks? I mean let's face, The ladder could claspe underneath you, or you could be set on fire and be burned to the extent of Death.
Broker-"Hell, you gotta be prepaired for that type of thing in this type of match. Now I'll be the first to admit that hardcore is not my prefured style...after all I am man of breeding (Large Heel pop) but then again if I have a chance to take down the power fo the NAWF then hell, I'm going to take it.
SN-Well would you like to wrestle Mitch Fredricks' right hand man? That being Shawn "Snake Eyes" Jensen???
Broker-Would I like it...hhmmm, I think that I would, as long as I won. Not that I doubt my abilities mind you but I think that the best way to take him out would be to give him $5 and he would go down...just like his momma. Now I am not used to any $5 ho. word in the street is that if your hungry for it and do not want to pay much then she is the one to see. But Jensen hiself. I see him as nothing but a stooge of the Presidents. He wants to climb the ladder via their back pocket. But little known to him tha Broker is a man of innumerable resources. Should we meet then he will realise that he has bitten off a buit more than he can chew.
SN-My final question is really probaly a very simple one for you. Would you rather see Reed Harrington or Ruben Patterson champion?
Broker-I think that I would like to see Ruben pull of the win. I know that he is the man who can do it....hell I could do it. But you can never tell with
Reed. He vacated a title a while back, so heck knows whats going through his head. Maybe I'll just have to get involved myself on that one...you never know, might even get a title shot out of it. Then again, I do not think that either man has much love for Team Fredericks deep down and I may want to get them to join me in my battle with the power of the NAWF...after all if you listen to speculation the NAWF magazine said that these two could shock the world. Yep...I think that I should have a hand in that one. As to what it will involve...I guess you'll just have to wait and find out.
[Fades back over to Walker and Boulda.] AW- Wow that was a great interview, we should have aired that on Terror. RB- yes we should have. Do I blame you? AW- No I was just hired last week, I started Thursday on Insider! RB- Oh ok. Remember, right here on ABC you can catch NAWF Insider every
Thursday at 7:30pm!
AW- Not to forget, Later in Lowdown we will have Slasher verus Number 1! RB- Both men have been quiet as of late huh? AW- Yes they really have! RB- Well what's send it over to the man known simply as Jeff Smith... Guess who he's got? AW- I'm gonna go out on a limb here and say Tyler Johnson? RB- Good guess.. But no. He's got actualy an update on the Slasher/Number 1 match. [Fades over to Jeff Smith.] JS- Hey guys, Good news for you Slasher fans... Number 1 is running behind, here in Detroit was some Thursday snow fall so his flight was delayed. He had to wait till today to get on a plane to get here.
[Fades back over to Boulda and Walker.] AW- Well let's take a commerical then we'll be back for our LowDown Main
Event! Slasher verus Number 1!
