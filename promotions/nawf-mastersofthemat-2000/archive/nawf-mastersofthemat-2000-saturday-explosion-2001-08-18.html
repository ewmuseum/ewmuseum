NAWF Saturday Explosion [Part 1/2]

I am the man who will be bringing you play by play every week James Styles! Along with my partner former CAWF, LVPWF and
UWA wrestler Thunder Warrior.
TW- Styles it's good to be here. I am proud to be the man who will carry the broadcast team.
JS- Whatever man. TW- I am glad you acknowledged my LVPWF days even though I lost every LVPWF match it was still a good time.
JS- Yes Mr. Warrior, you teamed with Mantis is UWA and LVPWF. Excpet in UWA you won the titles.
TW- Yes I did. JS- Now let's get to the action. *Money,Power, Respect by Puff Daddy plays.* Ring Annoucer- Making his way to the ring from Detroit, Michigan, he is the owner of the NAWF... ROBERT LILE!
[Lile walks down the ramp and enters the ring. He waits as the crowd settles.] RL- WELCOME TO THE NORTH AMERICAN WRESTLING FEDERATION! *Crowd Pops* What better city to choose then Detroit, Michigan! *Crowd pops* For the introduction of the NAWF. We will be making our way around North America throughout the year making sure we deliever to all our fans. From the little homeless mexican boys, to those jackass canadians! NAWF is here for you!
JS- This man is a asshole but he runs the federation well. TW- Stop sucking up. RL- Now as you can tell I brought in a great broadcasting team. Thunder Warrior is an excellent color man while James Styles will watch the action and give play by play.
JS- That I will. TW- He speaks the truth. RL- Now, I had to go through alot of trouble but. At the end of Hour 1 we will have the return a NAWF and UWA legend! I will allow you to see the man and hear his name then and only then! Plus I will give out what tonights main event will be then. I would like to welcome all of you again to NAWF!
[Scene cuts to Styles and Warrior as Lile leaves the ring.] JS- Tonights card is gonna be very good. TW- Hell, the main event will be awesome. JS- But do not forget about the qualifying matches. TW- Yeah the whole card is looking good. JS- Mordred and "Homeless" Jesus are looking to be basically the top feud in the company. TW- "Homeless" Jesus is damn good. I heard when he arrived in Detroit, the
NAWF staff sent him a Whooper and an order of fries.
JS- Well, let's move off "Homeless" Jesus' dick and start the action up. But before that we must take a commerical break.

The man is wearing a Fubu 05 Detroit Jersey. He is also wearing black guess jeans and a skull cap.]
Man- I'm here! The man who made NAWF and UWA! You can call me that American
Made Icon! Or you can call me Tyler "American Made" Johnson!

Thunder Warrior.]

EEEEEEEIIIIIIGHT! *Dirk begins moving his leg.* NNNNNNNIIIIINE! *Dirk tries to get pop up but can't* TTTTTTTEEEEEEEN! *Ding... Ding.. JS- We are in shock here at the Joe Louis Arena! TW- Completely. I can tell everyone was behind Gremlin! JS- Are you crazy? Everyone was behind Dirk. TW- Your crazy. JS- Anyways folks its time to go to break. We hope you enjoyed the first half hour of NAWF Explosion!
TW- I didn't! That match ruined it for me! JS- Shutup you idiot. Anyways we will be right back! *Camera fades in from black.*   Looking down from about 100 ft' there is a graveyard there.  You seem to be flying along when you see a man step out from behind a headstone of an angel.
 One wing has fallen off of the gravestone.  The man appears to be wearing a black jacket with silver sleaves.  Black jeans and has long black hair.  
Suddenly the camera starts to dive and you are getting closer to the man.   Then as you are about 30 ft' from the man he lifts his right hand into the air. Then the camera angle changes to see a rather large crow land on his arm.   You can see now that he is wearing a black leather jacket with shiny chainmale on both of the sleeves.  He is also wearing a pair of dark sunglasses so his eyes can not be seen.  The crow looks at the man and caws loudly twice.  "CAW CAW"
In a rather flat voice showing no emotion the man says"Well hello there Majik.  Enjoy your flight today?" MAJIK:"CAW CAW" MAN:gives the bird a smile and says"Well good.  Then you must be ready for the big show to begin."  Then the man places the crow on his shoulder and looks at the camera.  His smile gone.  Replaced with an evil, emotionless fase.  
"So the show begins.  The lights are on and the camera rolling.  The fans cheering and chanting for the men they love.  Well then it must also be time for the lights to go out.  For the darkness to rise up from the ashes of the past.  Years I have spent waiting for this moment when I would rise up to take the world.  And so it begins.  The darkness is here.  The darkness which
has lay buried deep in your souls.  The fear, the hatred, the terror laying in your hearts has come back to haunt you all.  All your fears are now reality.  All your hatred feeding my hunger.  I am your fear.  I am your hatred.  I am your darkness.  And just as I have always done, I will rule you.  Your dreams.  Your nightmares.  Your life.  All in my hands.  Can you
feel it.  Can you smell the fear.  Be carefull where you step.  For I am jusr around the corner.  Waiting to take your soul to places your mind could not even imagine.  Yes the darkness is here my freinds.  And the light that fades will be y!
ours." CAW CAW Camera fades to black as the man and the crow stare into the camera. *Show goes to a commercial break.* *Cameras fade back from commerical.* [The cameras fade to a scene backstage. Instead of it being a flashy lockerroom, all we see this time a bathroom. A man comes out of the bathroom.
The man sees the camera and nods his head to it. he begins to walk away but stops. He stares into the camera and begins to speak.]
Man- "You know, I gave the NAWF all I had back in 99. I came in as the CAWF hardcore champion. I decided hell, if I can't make it in the new IWC, I would come to NAWF. I came here, I decided that I would help the federation out. I meet Thunder Warrior and me him began to talk. Soon the man left his job as a commentator and teamed with me. We were The Rowdy Bastards. Only the NAWF wasn't man enough to stay open long enough for me to prosper. They merged with a bitch ass fed named NAWA. Then what happened to Mantis? Mantis held the new fed, UWA's tag team titles with Thunder Warrior. So when the fed reopens, do they call Mantis and invite him to be apart of it? HELL NO!
Mantis gets a free ticket after he calls Lile and asks if he can have a part in the new NAWF. But I was told no! I was told I could watch the first show for free then I would get to be a part of the party afterwards celebrating the show. But you know what? It's not gonna be like that! See Lile you think that I got this air time for free? Hell no! I know right now your wondering why The Hardcore Game is on your show! Here's whats going down! Mantis is here and I will be apart of the new NAWF! You can have your security teams follow me everywhere if you want. It doesn't matter, I will be showing up!
By the way, your cameramen will do anything for 50 bucks and a stack of porno! So get this through your head, Mantis is gonna run this show. You may think Tyler "American Made" Johnson scares me. But the little bastard doesn't. So let me put this into your head MANTIS IS NOW IN THE NAWF!
[Cameras cut to a NAWF technical difficulties logo. Then we fade back into the arena. We goto James Styles and Thunder Warrior who are seated at the annoucers table.]
JS- What the hell is Mantis gonna do? TW- I don't know! He called me earlier today and started bitching so I hung up! JS- The man seems hellbent on taking over the NAWF! I guess that in hour #2 our President is gonna address the situation.
TW- We gotta hope that he does. Cause if not theres pry gonna be some problems. JS- Well now it's time for our second match of the evening. Brandon Drake taking on Norm Alguy.
TW- These two men look like bad comepetion. JS- What do you mean? TW- Drake is undermatched. Hell kick Alguy's ass! JS- Let's not forget Alguy was the NAWF's first signed wrestler. Let's goto
Jesika Plesco with the introductions!

JS- Well it's time now for our next match. =-=-=-=-=-=-=- Match #4 Steel Cage Match "Homeless" Jesus vs. Mordred Writer- mike ( [detail withheld] -=-=-=-=-=-=- "Jesus Christ Superstar" by Andrew Lyod Webber begins to play over the speakers as "Homeless" Jesus staggers out of the curtains. "Homeless" Jesus looks around at the fans in the arena as he begins to walk down the aisle in a peaceful manner with his hands folded in front of him.
Ring Announcer: "Ladies and gentlemen, introducing first.....from "The Holly Land," he weighs two-hundred and forty pounds, he is....'Homeless'.....JESUS!"
"Homeless" Jesus is wearing a large white gown covered with stains from his lifestyle. He makes his way thru the cage door and he walks across the ring apron stopping in the center, as he faces the camera, he extends his arms out to the fans saying, "I love you all my children." "Homeless" Jesus then steps through the ring ropes and into the ring as he approaches the ring announcer giving him a hug, then he approaches the referee giving him a hug as well as both are dumbfounded on the man's delusional state of mind.
"Homeless" Jesus takes off his white cloak as he is wearing a pair of ripped up blue jeans, torn up shoes, and a dirty white ripped up tank top.
All the lights in the arena go dark, then words come over the PA and the -tron, “HAIL UNTO ME, FOR I AM THE
LORD OF STEEL!” With the word ‘steel’, there is a boom of pyrotechnics around the top of the ramp. Dark blue lighting comes on as strobe lighting hits and
Mordred’s music begins to play. Mordred walks out to the top of the ramp and looks around the arena before beginning his purposeful stride towards the ring.
Ring Announcer: And his opponent ladies and gentlemen... Mordred! Upon getting to the bottom of the ramp, Mordred runs, throws the cage door open and and slides under the bottom rope. He strides over to the far ring-post and stands on the second turnbuckle, roaring. After a few seconds Mordred gets back down and stalks the ring as the music fades and the lighting returns to normal.
"Homeless" Jesus rushes the much smaller man for a powerful clothesline and is leapfogged. Both wrestlers bounce off the ropes and Mordred catches him with a hurricanrana. Landing hard on his back Jesus is racked in pain and Mordred hits a running knee lifts as he starts to stand. Mordred gets up and is grabbed by
Jesus and lifted and dropped with a pumphandle slam. With a crazy smile Jesus drags him back up and rakes his face acrossed the cage opening him up. With a burst of speed Jesus is caught in a very tight small package by the bloody Mordred who seems to be fighting on instinct.
1... 2... Kick out Jesus seems enraged when he powers out and rolls to his feet and lands several savage kicks to Mordred's head and follows up with a bone jarring belly to belly suplex. Mordred tries to roll out of the ring and hits the cage wall. Jesus walks around the ring in a daze.
Recover Mordred locks Jesus in a headlock. Mordred releases the hold as Jesus lands a dirty low blow.
Jesus lifts Mordred and drops him with a over the knee back breaker. Jesus pulls him up and drops him again with a DDT. Jesus flops acrossed Mordred
1... 2... Leg on ropes Jesus pulls Mordred up and Mordred grabs Jesus and takes him down with a double leg takedown and rolls him into a boston crab. Screaming in pain for over a minute Jesus finally breaks the hold useing the ropes.
Mordred goes for his finisher and throws Jesus into the ropes and goes for the spinebuster and Jesus reverses it into a inverted atomic drop, follows with a kick to the gut, and then hits his finisher the
Holly Punishment(Inverted Death Valley Driver). The cover and 1... 2... 3... Ring Announcer: Your winner "Homeless" Jesus!!! JS- What a great match! TW- That match was a true NAWF match! JS- Well we have to go to break now, so lets head to it!
