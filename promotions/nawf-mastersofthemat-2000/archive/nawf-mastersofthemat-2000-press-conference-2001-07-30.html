NAWF (Jan 2000) — News

NAWF Press Confrence

July 30, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Cue to a black screen... Then a NAWF 2001 logo comes up.] (v/o- The following is a production of North American Wrestling Federation.) [Fade to shots of a hotel, the hotel is the Ramada Inn. The sign says the
Ramada Inn of Detroit, MI. Reporters are gathered in the confrence, camera crews from CNNSI, MSNBC, NBC Sports, ESPN, Fox Sports and Local Detroit News stations can be seen. At the front of the room is a row of tables with a NAWF logo behind them. Microphones are setup as are cups with water in them.
Suddenly a man walks out from the side and sits at the middle of the table. He adjusts the mic and begins to speak.]
Man (James Styles)- "Hello, I am James Styles the head annoucer for the NAWF. Today August 1st of 2000 and 1 the NAWF officals annouces its opening. The NAWF has signed three superstars so far. The men "Homeless" Jesus, Mordred and Norm
Alguy are some of the best talents. The men have agreed to sign with NAWF as the NAWF begins its second reign. Now I would like to introduce the man responsible for the NAWFs return. Ladies and Gentlemen please welcome NAWFs
President of Daily Operations Robert Lile!"
[As Robert Lile comes across the stage flash bulbs pop. Lile walks over and is seated to the right of Styles. Lile then begins to speak.]
Robert Lile- "Ladies and Gentlemen welcome to the first NAWF press confrence. We will be holding a press confrence once every six weeks to introduce upcoming pay per view cards. Today, our topic is the present. Today the NAWF debuts as a federation with alot of promise and a small roster. Our plan is to acquire more talent in the coming weeks. Our website
http://nawf.8m.net will keep you updated on you joins." JS- While we are discussing the website, you can goto the site and get the current news and rumors as they break.
RL- Why don't you Mr. Styles shut the hell up and allow me to introduce my secretary so she can handle that part. Please welcome my secertary, the lovely
Kelly Coates.
[As Coates walk across the stage to her seat on the right of Lile. Several reporters whistle at her as flashbulbs pop.]
Kelly Coates- Yes Mr.Lile, our website offers man features. We have a indepth look at our past as well as a look into our future. As Mr.Styles said the site does feature many great news and rumor articles. We offer a look at our staff, we will soon offer a look at our wrestlers. In the next few weeks when promos begin to be aired we will spotlight diffrent wrestlers.
RL- Now on the subject of promos, you can catch the promos on the website or on your local Public Access channel. We will air Explosion on the FX network, every Saturday evening at 9PM sharp! Speaking of Explosion, you can join James
Styles and Thunder Warrior each Saturday Night! Now onto federation business.
The reason we are introducing the NAWF is to debut to the guys who will send in tapes to join us. We will also be acquiring talent from other feds. You can expect NAWF to start in two weeks! Thats August 18th!
Kelly Coates- As Mr. Styles said we currently have three wrestlers. These wrestlers will be the first of many to come.
RL- So now we would like to discuss our headquaters. Unlike some federations we have choosen to have our headquaters in a 10 story in Detroit, Michigan. We have choosen a very modest lake front building. We also have setup training facilities in Phillidelphia and New York.
KC- We are going to have wrestlers cutting promos from all three locations. RL- We also will have our shows at several great arenas. We will start off at
Joe Louis Arena and then work our way through to arenas all over the country!
KC- But before we can talk about the shows, we will have to wait until we sign more wrestlers.
RL- Thats true. Now I would like to close this press confrence by taking questions from the reporters that are gathered here.
JS- Reporters may now raise their hands and I will call on you in a timely and orderly fashion. Yes the tall white dude in the back.
Reporter #1- Yes Mitch Album from the Detroit News, What about the only NAWF to ever do anything in the UWA Tyler Johnson. Will he return?
RL- Tyler Johnson may return in the future. He maybe in the fed in time for the
Pay Per View.
JS- Yes the short black guy up front. Reporter #2- So why no two cards a week? RL- We feel that one card a week gives our wrestlers the right ammount of time to cut promos. Then they can have time to rest after matches. There is no card on the week of pay per views.
JS- Last question, the white lady upfront. Reporter #3- Yes why no west coast offices? RL- We feel that the midwest and east coast give us plenty of chances to reach fans. We will have shows on the westcoast. We will do most of our shows in the west and in the south. We also plan a Canada tour down the road and even a show in Mexico.
JS- That ends the press confrence thank you for coming. Please check out the
NAWF website http://nawf.8m.net . [Camera fades to NAWF logo.] -------------- 2001 North American Wrestling Federation http://nawf.8m.net
