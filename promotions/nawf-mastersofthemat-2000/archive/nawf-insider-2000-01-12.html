NAWF Insider!!

12 January 2000

[Fade to black] (V/o: Wanna get it?) [Shots of Ruben Patterson and Reed Harrington standing face to face.] (V/o: Get inside!) [Shots of Tyler "American made" Johnson and Reptile standing face to face.] (V/O: GET INSIDE THE NAWF!) [Shots of TheBroker and Anthony Falcone standing face to face. Suddenly an X goes over Falcone. A photo of Dr.D. Capitator replaces it.]
(V/O:SATURDAY!) [Shots of Colin Jmaes with the continential Title.] (V/O: Masters of the MAT!) [fades to the NAWF INSIDER STUDIO] [As we fade in we notice neither Jeff Smith or Jim Styles sitting next to Cole
"Boomer" Parker but a new more professional looking guy...]
CBP- Welcome to NAWF Insider I'm not your host, but Boulda wants me to introduce you to our new Insider/Storm Host, Anthony Walker.
AW- Thank you Cole, I just wanna say to all you NAWF fans that as of now the
NAWF will be improving!
CBP- I hope so, Fredricks being gone certainly helped me. AW- Well now as a matter of fact speaking of change, the NAWF annouced a new
Schedule for programming... On Saturday you will get the Pay per View Masters of the mat this week. But on Friday Night you can catch all your favorite North
American Wrestling Federation superstars on Friday Night Storm!!!
CBP- Hell yeah, I get the whole freaking week off! AW- That's nice Cole, but after that on Monday, North American wrestling Federation peresents.. MONDAY NIGHT MADNESS! Thats the big show, that will be replacing Terror. There where some problems with our associate Federation IWC in our schedule.
CBP- Bastards jealous we stole their ratings? AW- No not that, we are behind them; but any ways it's gonna be some show. NAWF on Mondays and Fridays and because we know you IWC fans really wanna see the
NAWF action we have opened up our nightly schedule.
CBP- Yeah right like anyone watchs that garbage ass federation. AW- Carefull Boomer, remember James Hall could have you fired like that. CBP- Hey I just wanna say that the NAWF has been really improving since Hall advised Boulda on getting Fredricks to resign.
AW- Yes indeed, including the hiring of me! CBP- Too bad i couldn't have gotten a freaking Sub instead of having you here. AW- awwww i never knew you felt that way cole! CBP- I DON'T , Let's get on with the show!! -=-=-=-=-NAWF -=-=-=-=-=-=-RUMORS AW- That's right it's rumor time. We got some fresh rumors off the mill for you today! CBP- Yeah a good one right here... A NAWF executive has informed me that ANTHONY "The Falcon" Falcone has resigned! AW- That's gonna be confirmed by Robert Boulda who has a guest with him, Dr.D.Capitator. CBP- DOC's gonna be North American champ? AW- We shall see! Another Rumor that's gonna be very controversial, Mitch
Fredricks resignation. Was it legit?
CBP- I hope so! AW- Well then, we will air Mitch Fredricks press confrence which will be nationaly aired, but will be exculsively aired in it's intirety for our NAWF fans!
CBP- You are getting to become a real snot nose you know that Walker? AW- Well I am doing my damn job. -=-=-=-=-NAWF -=-=-=-=-=-NEWS CBP- That's right folks NAWF NEWS TIME! AW- We are gonna send it to our Nes Man, Jeff Smith! Take it away Jeff! [Fades over to Jeff Smith sitting in a chair at a desk that has a NAWF Logo
Carved into the front.]
JS- Thanks guys, glad I am not the one putting up with Cole. So heres our Top
Story, Anthony Falcone resigns. Why? Well Falcone was at the 3rd week of his 2 week NAWF deal and got a better offer.
Next on the agenda, Loaded, Falcon , Cain Tainer and Johnny Lidbeo, What do they have in common?
Well these men are said to be on the top of President Boulda's recruitment list, the only one Boulda can say he has almost locked is Loaded. But hey if
Loaded comes back the NAWF will only get better!
Well now Mitch Fredricks has left. but hey it's news confrence time, we'll send you out to matt Croft.... I mean the soon to be Fired Matt Croft, he is right along side Mitch Fredricks and is ready to introduce Mitch to the media.
[Fades to a very nervous Matt croft who has a Short Sleeved Blue shirt on with a NAWF Logo on the pocket.]
MC- Thanks alot guy, anyways now we have MITCH FREDRICKS!!! Ladies and Gentlemen I give to you the former President of NAWF... Mitch Fredricks. [Fredricks steps up to the stage.] MF- I am here to officaly annouce that, Mitch Fredricks, Charles Lambert, Shawn jensen and Shawn Mcneal are leaving the NAWF! Sorry to say but I can't take
Boulda's s*** no more. As for me I will open my own Federation soon and become
BETTER THAN BOULDA! Thank you for your time. NO QUESTIONS!
[Fredricks walks off stage as his security team crowds him.] MC- Let's send it back to Jeff Smith! [Fades to where Jeff Smith is sitting .] JS- Thanks Matt, well as for me... I will be your NAWF Backstage interviewer! Matt Croft will be hitting the unemployment section of the newspapers. But two guys that are here to stay in NAWF are Cole "Boomer" parker and Anthony Walker, let's send it back to them!
[fades over to where Boomer is yelling at Walker. Walker clears his throat and begins talking like nothing happened.]
AW- Well we lose two staff members now huh? CBP- Yeah and good redience my paycheck should look nice after their firing! AW- yeah but hey who cares? Right, cause saturday is the Masters of the Mat Pay per View! For the complete card we send it now over to Jim Styles!
-=-=-=-PAY PER VIEW UPDATE!-=-=-=- JS- It certainly looks good guys! First Number 1 takes on Slasher... Then probaly the hottest match at the card except the flamming ladder match, Cyric
Logan verus Mantis IN NO DQ!
But wait guys, theres more! NAWF also has THE REPTILIAN FURY MATCH! The loser could be on the self for months! Tyler "American Made" Johnson along with the hotty Angela take on Reptile!
Then for the TV Title Dante Kinderfeld looks to pull an upset over Ryan "13
Dreams" Jones! Then we were suppose to have a continential title match but No one was able to face Colin, so Colin gets the night off.
Then a Flamming ladder Match, TheBroker vs. Dr.D.Capitator... Dr.D was named champ based on a technicality found in the match between he and Falcone. When
Falcone quit the belt was freed for Dr.d.
Then THE MAIN EVENT! "Perfect Ten" Ruben Patterson looks to end Reed "The Real
Man" Harrington's world title dreams.
IT'S LIVE ON PAY PER VIEW SATURDAY! BACK TO COLE AND ANTHONY! [fades back over to CBP and AW.] AW- Falcone's gone and now Dr.d gets the North American prize. CBP- Dr.D is now into Falcone's problems. Broker should be a hard task for the
Icon, but hell Dr.D can pull off a win.
AW- WE HOPE! SEE YOU ALL SATURDAY AT NAWF MASTERS OF THE MAT!!! [Fades to credits as they roll...]
