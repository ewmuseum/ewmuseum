Why? It's unclear but after he was paid 500 dollars for the win against Mordred, he was seen at a Detroit Liquor store. -Saturday, the contracts of Tyler Johnson and Mantis will be signed. Afterwards the match for Harvest Bash 2001 will be signed. -Thunder Warrior competed with Mantis in IWC and NAWF, but will Thunder Warrior join Mantis again? Rumors are flying around the lockerroom that Mantis was brought in by the NAWF Color commentator. -Several talents have been signed by NAWF, Scott Von Braun, Dirk Sandecker, Mike Manson and the Pyromaniac have all been recently signed. -NAWF has purchased facilities in the Detroit Michigan area. The building which is 5 miles away from NAWF headquaters is rumored to be the home of the NAWF Arena. The arena will be used for Saturday Explosion.
