{NAWF} Axxis (Critique)

19 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens in front of a local Virginian gym. The view zooms into the automatic double doors at the front of the building, which open as we get closer. As we go through the establishment, many of the clients are clearly rattled and upset by something. An excited murmur is abound, with many members of the gym's staff asking if the police need to be called. A hand, presumably the cameraman's, comes into view, as a door is opened and we are now clearly in the dressing/changing room of the gym. We pass several dull gray steel gym lockers, take a left, and are face to face with newcomers to the North American Wrestling
Federation Hannibal Carver and Taurus, collectively known as Axxis. Taurus is pacing back and forth, clearly upset. Hannibal Carver watches him as he would a tennis ball during the U.S. Open. He finally slams his fist into a nearby gym locker. This startles Taurus, who then looks over at Carver, awaiting an explanation.)
Taurus: What the hell did you do that for? Hannibal Carver: I could ask you the same god damned question? That guy was almost done his reps, you couldn't have waited one f'in minute?
Taurus: He was giving me a look I didn't care for. Hannibal Carver:My student...in this life there are a great many things you must learn to expect. Death. Taxes. Fat-assed, borderline retarded fans whenever you wrestle in America. Another thing you must learn to expect is to get strange looks when you go out in public wearing a black spandex mask!!!
Taurus: Oh, so I should let them see what years of Japanese Death
Matches has done to my face?
Hannibal Carver: No, I expect you to use the brain you were born with. I expect you to use a little common sense when we are in a new redneck town. I expect you to not act like a complete Down's Syndrome case and force me to stop in the middle of a workout! This isn't like you, I know all my teachings haven't fallen on deaf ears. What is going on with you today?
Taurus: You really want to know? Fine. I'll tell you what's been eating up my insides. We roll into this godforsaken state, NAWF has a house show, and they don't even ask if we want a spot! What the hell are we here for, if not to work? Wha the hell are we...window dressing? I came here to make a living wrestling, not signing autographs for snot-nosed kids!
Hannibal Carver: You need to calm down. You are being for to rash, for too brazen. As I've said, we need to prove ourselves to the higher-ups in this company. What we've done in the past is just that. In the past.
I have to be assured that we are on the same page here. To make sure that no one ever forgets the name Axxis. And to make that a reality, we can't have any of these childish outbursts. Today, you acted too much like the Bison, running out blindly on the plain, only to have his flesh ripped off his bones by vultures and coyotes. We must be like the
King Cobra...laying silently in the tall grass, waiting for the perfect time to strike our unwitting opponents. You must show these scum why you were given the moniker of "The Japanese Akita" in Nippon Pro.
Taurus: ...Yes. You,are of course correct, my sensei. Please forgive my foolishness. Hannibal Carver: Of course. You acted with the brashness of youth, nothing more. Only natural, and nothing to be ashamed of. Go work off your anger in the gym...after assuring the staff that there will be no further....ourbursts, of course.
(Taurus nods, and walks off. Hannibal Carver rubs his head and sighs. He looks up, seeing that the NAWF cameras are still in his presence. He begins to speak.) Hannibal Carver: Yes, he does make the mistakes of youth, but do not be mislead. When Taurus steps in the squared circle, and the opening bell is run, he is all business. So if you think you can use his temper against him, you are sadly mistaken. Angering either member of Axxis will get you only one thing, my friends. Traction. I won't lie. I would have liked to have seen action tonight at The Scope. But such is life.
We are the untested newcomers. With a house show, the creative team had no oppurtunity to tell our story. That's there mistake. The story of
Axxis is the stuff that nightmares are made of. If you wish to continue to sell "Nukeman Beanie Babies" to your milk babies, then it would be in your best interests that the general populance knows as little as possible about Taurus and myself. The only story you need to know will take place this saturday during the opening match of Destruction at the
Richmond Coliseum. The story begins with popcorn, programs, and large foam fingers being sold. The meat of the story is filled with screams of pain and the twisting of bones and ligaments. The ending, a tap out, of course. There is no Part Two to this tale, however. For the ultimate result is all those who dare stand in my way out of a job, not able to properly use their arms and legs any longer, possibly death. No, I'm not claiming to be a murderer or any such foolishness, just saying that the loss of what most take for granted can be too much for some men, and they take an..easier way out, shall we say. Nukeman, Too Fly J,
Dennis Fonatine, Mike Dynasty. Cowards, every last one. Perhaps you think maintaining a dignified silence will grant you amnesty from the pain that Axxis is so eager to visit upon you. Wrong again. All that will accomplish is having your careers end with a whimper, rather than a bang. Your professional wrestling careers, your lives... They can both be summed up with a simple word. Sayonara.
(The scene fades to black as Hannibal Carver opens a locker, takes out a gym bag with "Carver" written on the side in black marker. He walks out of view as the screen is completely engulfed by darkness.)
