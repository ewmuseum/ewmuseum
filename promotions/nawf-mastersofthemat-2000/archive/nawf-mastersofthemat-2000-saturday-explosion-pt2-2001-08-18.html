NAWF Saturday Explosion [Part 2/2]

August 18, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade back into the arena.] JS- We are now getting ready for the main event! ***Cue Money Power Respect by Puff Daddy and The Bad Boy family.*** JS- Looks like the boss took a little time but will now do his little anoucement. TW- That seems to be the case! JP- Now coming to the ring, the president of the NAWF, ROBERT LILE! [Robert Lile walks down the ramp and down to the ring. He is wearing a black armani suit. He is tossed a mic and he begins to speak.]
RL- I am sorry for not coming out earlier and any confusion that the broadcasters may have caused by saying I would be out earlier. Now let me put the whole thing together. Earlier tonight I decided on a main event and gave the job of annoucing it to Mr.Styles. He took care of it, so I had one less thing to do when I came out here.
You probably are wondering why would the president of this federation put himself out here? Why would he come to the ring and give precious minutes of his life to you people. Well, It's simple. I aim to please myself and coming out here pleases me. So let me get down to the point. You seen Tyler Johnson in the arena. He is tonights Ring Annoucer for the main event. We couldn't get our first champion Reed Harrington so we used one of our most successful wrestlers Tyler Johnson. So let's allow Mr. Johnson to come to the ring.
*Cue- Bad Boy for Life by P Diddy* RL- Coming to the ring weighing in at 235 pounds, THE AMERICAN MADE ICON! Hailing from Lincoln, Nebraska... HE IS TYLER "AMERICAN MADE" JOHNSON! *Crowd pops* [Johnson strolls down the ramp and comes to the ring. He is wearing a black
NAWF Legends T-Shirt. He gets in the ring and raises his arms. He takes the mic.]
T"AM"J- MANTIS... You_dare_speak_against_the_american_made_icon? Do you not realise that if i want to I can get your ass in the ring and fight you? But guess what? Neither of us are NAWF wrestlers, we have no NAWF contracts so thats not gonna happen! So anyways let me get to the point, Mantis you could never beat and you will never get a chance at me cause we simply are not in the same federations.
*Mantis jumps over the security railing and starts shouting at Johnson. He grabs a mic from the annoucers table and begins to speak. Mantis is in Street clothes, a black plain T-Shirt and Liquid Denim jeans.*
Mantis- LISTEN UP NUT SLAP! Now I am trying to steal a line but you dare mess with The Hardcore Game. You came to this ring for one reason, Lile paid your ass off. I gurantee you that Lile is so damn smart, that he will give us a match!
*Lile cuts in.* RL- You two want a match? Well, let me say this! A retirement match! The match will be a last man standing match! The date... NAWF's first Pay Per
View! Harvest Bash 2001! But I will give you two a few practice matches at other men and at each other before the match!
[Tyler Johnson cuts in.] T"AM"J- I tell you this, why don't next Monday Night you sign the us officaly into the NAWF!
M- That sounds good to me. Johnson why don't you take on your little task and
I will go handle my training!
[Mantis leaves and Lile exits the ring and goes to the annoucers table.] JS- Now it's time for the match! TW- The main event match! T"AM"J:"Ladies and gentlmen, now entering the ring weighing in at two hundred and seventy-five pounds, Hellraiser!"
The lights drop and there is silence for about 5 seconds. (BOONGNGNG....BOONGNGNG.) Some in the crowd are booing while others are cheering. (BOOOOM) as the blue pyro at the entryway goes off. Full music starts to play(AC/DC Hells Bells) The lights come back on. Hellraiser steps out form the back and stops. Looks around at the crowd and gives them a half hearted smirk. Then he walks to the ring rather slowly. When he gets about half way down the ramp a large crow flys out from the back and lands on his shoulder.
JS:"And were ready to get this match underway. Here comes Hellraiser making his way to the ring with his crow.
TW:"What the hell is with the bird on his shoulder. Who takes a bird to the ring with him. This man has got to be the dumbest sob around. I mean. A bird for christ sake.
Hellraiser climbs into the ring and instantly walks over to Jessica Plesco and holds out his hand. She swallows hard and holds out the mic. Hellraiser takes the mic and gives Jessica a bit of a smile. Then signals for his music to stop. the music stops and Hellraiser looks toward the entry ramp and begins.
HR:"Tyronne "Dirty Washingtub. My name is Hellraiser. This is Majik*pointing to the crow on his shoulder*. Such a beautiful creature dont you all think. The crow. The most magickal creature you will ever see. The free'er of souls. A creature of death. there is nothing more powerful in your lives as death. As humans you either fear it or you long for it. We deliver it. You said that I will be your first victim. You dont know the meaning of the word victim. But thats ok. I can teach you. I can teach you the pain and the pleasure of death. I can teach you the true meaning of
"victim" And I will start tonight.....Ladies and gentelmen. Class is in session. We will begin with the first lesson. Victim 101. Now if my star pupil will make his way down the ring. We can begin your lesson. One you will never forget.Ha ha ha*Majik joins in the laughter*hahaha..."
JS:"Well that explains whats with the crow. We await his opponant,Tyronn
Dirty Washingtub...I mean Washington to come down to the..."
TW- We gotta goto break first! We will be right back! [Fade to commericals.] [fade back into the arena.] -=-=-=-=-=- Match #5 Main Event Tyronn "Dirty" Washington V/s Hellraiser Writer- President Lile --=-=-=-=- JS- We go back to the guest ring annoucer Tyler "American Made" Johnson. [The lights go out..."Let's get Dirty" by Redman starts playing and different flashing lights come on. Tyronn "Dirty" Washington walks out wearing black pants, and his black shirt with white writing that says "Who's da baddest?"
on the front, and on the back it says "Dirty".] T"AM"J: Weiging in at 264 pounds, standing 6 foot 6...He is...Tyrrrrrrrronn
"Dirrrrrty" Washington!
[He gets into the ring, takes off his shirt and throws it into the crowd. The light comes back on.] [Ding... Ding... Ding] JS- This match should go long and hard! [Hellraiser whips Washington into the ropes, spear by Hellraiser.] JS- Great move! TW- That was just basic! [Hellraiser locks in a armbar. Washington creeps towards the ropes.] 2 FEET AWAY! 1 Foot away! HE'S GOT IT! JS- God I thought it woulda been over! TW- Nah this is a early stage of the match. Both men are fresh. [Hellraiser sends Washington into the ropes. Washington on the comeback attempts a clothlesine! DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE!]
Tw- Both men wanted the same move! JS- Now both men are out! REF- ..........1 *Hellraisers legs are moving.* ...........2 *Tyronn is stirring* ..........3 *Tyronn crawls over and covers Hellraiser.* REF- .......OOOOOOONNNNNEEE! *kickout by Hellraiser* JS- Hellraiser was out of it until the cover. TW- Washington's body weight woke him up. [Washington pulls Hellraiser up and connects a scoop slam.] JS- That basic move was executed well. TW- No lie! [Washington pulls up Hellraiser and sends him into the ropes... POWERSLAM!] TTTTTTTTTTHHHHHHUUUUUUUDDDD! JS- That had to hurt! [Washington pulls up Hellraiser, Hellraiser hits a elbow into Washington's stomach. It doesn't phaze Washington. Implant DDT by Washington!]
JS- OH MY GOD! TW- That was a great move! [Washington goes for the cover.] REF- .........OOOOOONNNNNNNEEEE! ..........TTTTTTTTWWWWWWWOOOOO! *Kickout by Hellraiser.* [Washington pulls Hellraiser up and whips him into the corner. Washington runs into the corner attempting a splash, HELLRAISER MOVES! Washington collaspes and falls down in the corner.]
TW- DAMN! JS- This match is exciting! [Hellraiser goes onto the top turnbuckle and sets Washington up for a SUPER
DDT! He connects Washington is out!]
TTTTTTTTTHHHHHHHHUUUUUUDDDDDD!!!! JS- Damn that had to hurt! TW- No kidding! [Hellraiser slowly crawls over and covers Washington.] REf- ...........OOOOOOONNNNNNNEEEE! .............TTTTTTWWWWWWWWWOOOOO! *Kickout by Washington!* JS- Close call! [Washington and Hellraiser rise to their feet. The two begin to exchange blows. Washington directs Hellraiser into the corner by hitting him harder.
Hellraiser falls onto the ropes. Washington connects a DDT.] TW- Washington actual is using some skill. JS- Thats what happens when you train! [Washington going for the DIRTY DROP! HE CONNECTS!] JS- It's over! TW- This is incredible! [Cover by Washington.] REF- .........OOOOONNNNNEEEEE! ...........TTTTTWWWWWWWOOOO! .............TTTTTTHHHHHRRRRREEEE! [Ding... Ding.. Ding] Js- Thats the ending of the match! TW- Also its the ending of the event! JS- Well, folks we hope you enjoyed the first ever NAWF Saturday Explosion! TW- I know I did! [Cameras fade to a NAWF logo.] NAWF.8m.net !
