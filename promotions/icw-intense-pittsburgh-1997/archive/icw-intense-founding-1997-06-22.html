Intense Championship Wrestling

Hi, My name is Adam E. Dasterdly and I run the fantasy wrestling federation known as the ICW. Intense Championship Wrestling. This fed is VERY young and we have only had one card. (I will post the card also) Several people have come to me and told me that my cards are the best, so be sure to check that out. I have included the application below, if you are at all interested in joining the ICW, be sure to e-mail me at [detail withheld] . Thats pretty much all I have to tell you right now, be sure to look through this application and read the card. Thats all I ask is that you at least give me a chance and not just write me off. Thanks. -Adam E. Dasterdly ICW Intense Championship Wrestling Welcome to Intense Championship Wrestling. I am Adam E. Dasterdly, (WhiteyDog) and I am the president of the ICW. By joining the ICW, you join the REAL revolution of fantasy wrestling. Not like the so-called federations that have more nick-names than wrestlers or jingles than matches. This is the true revolution. In the ICW, I will NOT let any below average roleplayers grace the locker rooms. This isnt a sign up, your in fed...you gotta make an impressive application, or you can take a powder. Well, enough intro, lets get to the info! Cards- Two cards a week on Monday's and Friday's. I am not going to set a limit on the number of matches per card or anything like that. I leave it up to you, (9 times out of 10) to make your own matches. Sometimes, I will make a match, just for the hell of it. Titles- World title, North american title, and world tag team titles are the titles that the ICW will offer. I feel that if there are too many titles, then the prestigous'ness of the titles wears away. Thats why, there are three. If there happens to be a demand for a new title, I will introduce it. TOSing- This is a big deal. I have seen far too many feds go down the toilet due to TOSing of members. If you cant handle swearing, or trash talking...DO NOT GO ANY FURTHER DOWN THIS APPLICATION! At the first sign of TOSing, unless I know who done it, the ICW will shut down. It makes everything a lot harder for everyone, and we just cant have it happening, PERIOD. well, I think thats about all that I need for now. If you are interested to join the true revolution, fill out the application below. Thankyou for your time and patience. -ICW President, Adam E. Dasterdly. Application. Wrestlers name- Ht- Wt- Appearence- Gimmeck- Hometown- Entrance Music- Finishing move AND a description of it in detail- 5-15 other favorite moves- Manager (optional)- Manager description- Heel/Face/Neutral- E-mail address- (for tag teams, just fill out the application for two wrestlers. You can have up to 4 wrestlers. How you divide them among tag teams and singles wrestlers is up to you) Please attach a sample roleplay below. The reason for this is so that I can weed out the good roleplayers, from the bad. Thanks -President Dasterdly
