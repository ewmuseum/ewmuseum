A website fantasy fed that said it would turn into a CAW league, a create-a-wrestler game league, once its owner had the equipment; its pages list champions and ranked contenders billed from film, television, games, music and sport, annual awards, a hall of fame and a 2011 event calendar. Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Extreme Madness Wrestling, EMW, called itself an F-Fed on its welcome page, “which will soon turn into a CAW League once I get the equipment to do so,” made “for entertainment and non-profit purposes.” Its pages never say the change was made. They carry a roll call of champions and up to five ranked contenders for each: Al Bundy as World Heavyweight Champion, Zangief as Internet Champion, James Rolfe as Cruiserweight Champion, Olivia Munn as Women's Champion, Megan Fox as Starlets Champion, the Grove Street Gang as World Tag Team Champions, and Kelly Bundy and Sasha Grey as Unified EMW/NIWA World Women's Tag Team Champions. Its names come from film, television, games, music and sport.

The Bonecrusher Awards page lists the nominees for the first awards, to be given on EMW ShowTime of 31 December 2009, one name to an award, among them Human Tornado as Superstar of the Year, Gina Carano as Starlet of the Year and Bruce Lee against Chuck Norris at MegaBrawl 3 as Match of the Year. Its Hall of Fame, presented on MegaBrawl weekend, inducted Wade Needham, Bobby Spade, Jay & Silent Bob, James Bond, The Babe and Ari Gold in 2009, and Ami Mizuno, Duke Nukem, Human Tornado, Pulp Fiction and Superman in 2010, and says it also honors icons of other CAW leagues. Its 2011 Mega Event Calendar ran from Extreme Anniversary on 26 June to MegaBrawl 5 on 25 December, with EMW/NIWA Ultimate Showdown on 6 November. Its card for EMW School Daze, 24 April 2011 in Newark, billed Al Bundy against five others for the World Heavyweight Championship in a Hell's Playground match, and a Hell's Gauntlet match for the first challenge to the Unified EMW/NIWA women's tag title.
