King of the CyberRing Tournament

14 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
New York City, New York. September 3. The IWL King of the CyberRing tournament is an annual tradition in the Internet Wrestling League. Each year, sixteen of the best IWL superstars are invited to compete for the coveted crown of IWL's "King". This year, the rules have somewhat changed. During an airing of PTC Radio, IWL Chairman, [name withheld], announced two special changes to this year's format. First instead of the winner becoming the #1 contender to the IWL World Championship... this year's winner will become the new IWL Internet Heavyweight Champion. The title, currently vacant, is one of the IWL's six prestigious championships as previously held by the likes of The Punisher, Axel Young, and more recently Machine Knight K'. The next change is probably a historic one in terms of the IWL's history. The tournament has always been open only to the IWL roster. Now for the first time ever, this year's tournament is now open to the world. [name withheld] announced on PTC Radio that the tournament will now feature a special "World Bracket" that will challenge the IWL's Bracket. Though only IWL superstars can win the IWL Internet Championship, anyone may win the IWL King of the CyberRing title! For more information about King of the CyberRing, visit it's mini-site on IWLe.com. Any interested free or signed talent may participate. To register for one of the sixteen seeds, visit the King of the CyberRing registration site. The registration will continue up to Thursday evening and will be closed afterward. Tournament Information * The tournament is a 32 person single elimination tournament with no consolation match and random seeding. * 16 entries composed of all IWL superstars will make up the "IWL Bracket". 16 entries composed of non-contracted superstars will make up the "World Bracket" * The winner of the IWL Bracket will win the IWL's vacant Internet Heavyweight Championship. * The winners of the two brackets will meet in the finals to be held on IWL PPV for the title of IWL King of the CyberRing. * Preliminary round matches will be held on IWL TV events aired on Mondays and Thursdays. A 1 RP minimum is in place but there is a 5 RP maximum. Finals will be held at an IWL Pay Per View. * Judging for all preliminary rounds will be done by the IWL staff. But the finals will be judged by an independent party. The judge's decision in all rounds are final. * All tournament participants not contracted to the IWL may still participate in non-tournament matches by request. * Tournament is for all levels. * All participants whether IWL or not are expected to abide by the IWL Rules. About the Internet Wrestling League The Internet Wrestling League, founded on December 1, 2000 by [name withheld], was founded as part of the Geeksoft.Net series of federations. During it's time there, IWL was ranked #7 on the list of best federations as determined by Geeksoft.Net. When Geeksoft closed it's doors to it's federations in early 2002, the IWL was without a home and sent on hiatus. Now in 2004, [name withheld] is looking to restore the IWL name as a leader in online sports entertainment. For more information about the IWL, visit http://iwl.iwle.com
