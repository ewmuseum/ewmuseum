Press Release: IWL Entertainment Re-Opens Doors to the Internet Wrestling League

28 January 2004

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE IWL Entertainment Re-Opens Doors to the Internet Wrestling League NEW YORK CITY, New York. At a press conference held Friday afternoon, IWL Chairman [name withheld] announced that the IWL Entertainment's flagstaff promotion would end its period of hiatus by hosting its first event in almost one year. The event will naturally be held at the World's Most Famous Arena, Madison Square Garden. The event will be a bit downscaled then normal but attendance expectations will be slightly more then half the total capacity of the arena itself. "The IWL is something that not only the fans have been asking for", quoted [name withheld] at the conference, "its something that promoters want to see comeback as well." "The Internet Wrestling League was one point in time one of the better promotions out there. Now at a time where many new and coming promotions are closing within a month of opening, I think it would be a perfect opportunity for the IWL and other organizations with similar backgrounds to return to the world and its fans." A public relations director IWL Entertainment announced that the IWL will be hosting their first two events in the first weeks of February. Their first event will be the returning IWL OverDrive which will be hosted live at Madison Square Garden. IWL OverDrive is currently scheduled for air February 5, 2004. Shortly afterward, the IWL will be hosting their second event, IWL Monday Night. IWL Monday Night will emanate live from the First Marina Area on February 9, 2004. [name withheld] briefly mentioned that he will and his committee will start to seek out free agented wrestlers in hopes of having them sign a contract to work for the IWL. About the IWL The Internet Wrestling is a three-year old wrestling organization that originally started as Geeksoft.Net G-Fed back in December of 2000. The IWL was the longest running organization in the G-Fed network at that time. Now restarting in its fourth year of operation, this time as an independent e-federation, [name withheld], the head and found of the IWL now returns hopefully with the same dynamic form of entertainment that it once brought to all their fans and the world of wrestling not too long ago. About [name withheld] [name withheld] started his in wrestling like everyone probably did at one point in time, as a wrestler. His first sting in the WOWFED was a short one however he later developed and honed his skills as a wrestler. After several more stints in several more organizations, such as UHW, UEWF3, WHWF, ICPF, GWA, and several others, [name withheld] began to realize what it took to run a promotion. When Geeksoft opened its doors for the G-Fed 2.0 network, [name withheld] made it a priority to have his promotion as one of the first to open their doors. Many consider him to be one of the better role-players and owners there was. Now he has his sights set on re-opening the IWL but no one knows exactly how far it will go now in its fourth year of operation For More Information Those who want more information are welcomed to the visit.
