WLW "Night of the Hunted" Part 10

13 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BALDWIN..."Whoa! What do you think of that?" TOWNSAND..."I'll tell ya, Baldwin! Midnight Mayhem is gonna be perfect for me, cause midnight is when I'm at my best! Just ask your wife! Ha ha!" BALDWIN..."Now, wait..." TOWNSAND..."Oh, I'm just teasing ya! Don't crumble your cookies over it!" BALDWIN..."Well, Johnny it's time for the cage match between The Executioner and Vrolok. What about this one?" TOWNSAND..."I have been waiting for this one. Two big mean men locked inside a steel cage. I love it. But I think that Ventura screwed up by not putting those two she-devils, Francesca and carmilla in the cage, then we would have a good old fashioned cat fight. Ha Ha Ha!" The WLW ring crew is busy setting up the fifteen foot high steel cage. The fans around the Sky Dome are starting to get noisy. They are yelling and screaming at the ring crew to hurry up. Some fans on the 200 level are trying to start a wave. BALDWIN..."Listen to these fans Johnny! They can't wait for this action to continue." TOWNSAND..."Ya, and you can't wait for that hot dog vendor to come by again." The ring crew does one final check of the cage, then exit the arena. BALDWIN ..."Well Johnny, looks like we are all set to go again. Let's go to Tom Russel who is with the Executioner. Take it Tom." Russel is in the back with a determined looking Executioner. RUSSEL..."Executioner your about to enter the cage with Vrolok, who says he will rule the WLW." EXECUTIONER..." Just a few more minutes and then into a steel cage I will go. In a few minutes the world will see that I am worthy of moving up the ladder to the position to start taking on the Champions here in the WLW. The question is will the steel cage be enough to contain all the action that will take place between me and Vorlok. Enough talk, time for me to get ready for the cage. Enjoy the show, its Going to be Wild." Executioner leaves Russel and heads for the ring. RUSSEL.."Executioner sound like he has his sights on some gold. He better have his sights set on Vrolok tonight. Back to you guys." Crumb climbs into the steel cage. CRUMB..."Ladies and Gentlemen this next contest is a grudge match...and it will be held inside a fifteen foot high steel cage!!...Introducing first along with his manager Francesca...from D.C.... weighing 289 lbs...here is the EXECUTIONEEEEER" Francesca and the Execuionter enter the arena, to a chorus of boo's from the crowd. The pair exit the cart, Executioner drops his noose and enters the cage. BALDWIN..."Executioner sure looks determined, Johnny." TOWNSAND..."Of course he does. The Executioner has something to prove, He 's been around here for awhile now this new guy Vrolok comes in and challenges him. The executioner will be out to make a point, that he won't be pushed around." BALDWIN..."Speaking of Vrolok, I believe Tom is standing by. Take it Tom.." Russel stands in the back with Carmilla and Vrolock. RUSSEL..."Vrolock, any words before entering the cage with the Executioner?" VROLOK..."The Feast may now begin. Children of Darkness...here my call! I will be victorious!!" Vrolok and Carmilla leave and head towards the arena. RUSSEL..."There you have it guys, a very confident Vrolok." CRUMB..."And his opponent..being escorted to the ring with his manager Carmilla...from the Carpathian Mountains...weighing 310 lbs...VROLOOOOOK!" Hells Bells pounds over the speakers as the two ride towards the cage. Once again the crowd starts to boo. TOWNSAND..."Listen to these dummies, Baldwin. They're booing both men." BALDWIN..."That's because they don't know who to cheer for. Both men are disliked here in the WLW" TOWNSAND..."Well, I can't stand you and Russel, but if you guys were to duke it out, I'm sure I'd cheer for one of ya." BALDWIN..."Would you stop." Vrolok gets some last words from Carmilla, before entering the ring. Executioner continues to stretch. As soon as Vrolock enters the cage, Executioner attacks, smashing him with a forearm. The door is closed and the bell rings. Executioner contiues his assault by raking the face of his stunned opponent. Executioner quickly delivers a boot to the mid-section, doubling over Vrolock. He sinches in his opponent, and smashes him to the canvas with a vicious snap suplex! TOWNSAND..."Whoaa! Executioner has exploded here!" Carmilla and Francesca glare at each other as Executioner picks up Vrolock. He whips him towards the bars, and Vrolok smashes hard into the cage! Executioner charges at his opponent, but is met with a knee lift. As Exe staggers back, Vrolock runs and sends him to the mat with a huge clothesline. Vrolock stalks his opponent. He picks him up by the hair and whips him to the cage. Executioner hits face first, busting him open! Showing no mercy, Vrolock grabs him by the back of the head, and runs him into the cage a second time! BALDWIN..."Oh, this is vicious Johnny, I think Executioner has already been busted open!" TOWNSAND..."Your right Baldwin, He's bleeding all over the place. I love it! This is what a cage match is supposed to look like, yuo have got to use that cage as a weapon!!" BALDWIN..."You're sick Johnny!' Inside the ring Vrolock goes for a boot to the mid-section, but Executioner catches his foot, spins him around 360 degrees and nails him in the head with a big punch. Executioner whips Vrolock, but it is reversed and Executioner is sent smashing into the bars for a third time! Vrolock looks outside to Carmilla, who nods. He walks over to executioner wraps his large hands around his neck, and drops him with the Nosferatu. Vrolock starts towards the door, he stops, turns around and then stalks executioner once again. BALDWIN..."What is vrolock doing, he could have easily made it out of the cage and win the match!?" TOWNSAND..."Looks like Vrolock wants to inflict more pain on the Executioner." BALDWIN..."This could be a huge mistake!" Vro picks up his opponent, but quickly lets go of him, as executioner claws at his face with a face rake. Exe sneaks up behind his opponent and drops him with a quick back suplex! Executioner tries to whip Vrolock but it is reversed, and he is sent crashing to the mat with a clotesline. Vro picks upthe fallen exe and smashes him to the mat with a belly to belly suplex. Without letting up, Vrolock picks him back up and delivers another one. Vrolock heads for the door, but before he can reach the floor, executioner laeps accross the ring, grabs him by the foot and pulls him back in. BALDWIN..."That was a close call for the executioner!" The two men begin to pound on each other, Executioner gets the upper hand and sends Vrolock to the mat with a vicious punch to the bridge of his nose. Vrolock nose starts to gush blood. Executioner picks him up and drops him with a belly to belly suplex of his own. With out releasing his grip Executioner picks him up and delivers a second belly to belly suplex. Vrolock is now hurting. Executioner jumps on top of his opponent and sinches in a sleeper hold. TOWNSAND..."Vrolock better get out of this in a hurry or the match will be over." Vrolock struggles, and is finally able to over power Executioner and get to his feet. He drops down, and breaks the hold with a jawbreaker. Vrolock gets back up, and nails executioner with a ear ringer, and follows it up with a knee lift. He goes for a whip, but it is reversed and Vrolock is sent smashing into the cage face first! His head is now beginning to pour blood. Executioner walks over to the bloody Vrolock, picks him up, and rocks him with an ear ringer. With Vrolock stunned, Exe sinches him and drops him with a big back suplex. Exe continues to stalk his opponent. He picks up Vrolock, and whips him to the cage, it is reversed and Exec hits back first against the cold hard metal! As Exe staggers back Vrolock grabs his opponent, lifts him high into the air and smashes him to the mat with a power bomb. TOWNSAND..."What a move by Vrolock! He pulled that out of nowhere!" With Exe down on the mat Vrolock climbs the ropes. He leaps and drives a flying elbow smash into the throat of his opponent! Exe lies on the mat quivering in pain as Vrolok stands over him. Carmilla screams intructions to her man, while Franseca pleads to the Exe. Vrolok grabs Exe's legs and drags him part way to the corner. He sinches his legs and drops, slingshoting the Exe into the cage! Exe bounces off the metal and crashes onto his back. His face is covered with blood. BALDWIN..."My god! Did you see that?!" TOWNSAND..."That's what I mean, Baldwin! The cage is there for a purpose. That being, to hurt your opponent! Vrolok knows, and that's exactly what he's doing!" Vrolok leaves Exe on the mat and walks to the corner closests to the runway. He looks down, the calls Carmilla over. They talk about something, both pointing up the aisle. BALDWIN..."Hey, I don't like this, Johnny! What are they pointing up the aisle for?" TOWNSAND..."How the hell should I know? Maybe Vrolok doesn't want Carmilla to watch anymore of the match. Who knows?" As Vrolok talks with Carmilla, Exe gets to his feet. The fans scream as he closes in on Vrolok from behind. He grabs, smashes an elbow to the side of his head, and follows it up with a vicious knee lift. Vrolok crashes to the mat. Franseca screams at Exe to "get out"! Exe nods, but turns back to Vrolok. He pulls him to his feet, whips him to the ropes and back body drops Vrolok into the cage! TOWNSAND..."Whoa! He hit so hard I'm surprised the cage didn't come down! I'll tell ya, Baldwin, this ...hey.. what..who's that?" The fans near the entrance jump to their feet as someone runs down the aisle. BALDWIN..."I can't see wh...wait...that's Wave of Destrucion! What the hell are they doing out here?!" TOWNSAND..."Vrolok's got friends, Baldwin! I guess these are two of them!!" Tidalwave and Soundwave quickly make their way down the aisle, ignoring the booing fans and the garbage that they are throwing. In the ring, Exe begins to climb out of the cage. The fans scream, but none of them scream as loud as Carmilla. Vrolok rolls over and looks around, finally seeing Exe at the top of the cage. He crawls over as Exe puts a leg over the top. TOWNSAND..."Look at this, Baldwin! Executioner is winning it!" Vrolok gets to the corner and begins to climb up after Exe. He grabs the back of his pants and pulls. Exe stops his climb, and tries to kick away Vrolok, but before he can, Vrolok nails him with an uppercut. Exe is stunned. Vrolok gets his arm between Exe's legs and slams him to the mat! The fans gasp as Exe plunges the fifteen feet, bouncing off the mat. TOWNSAND..."Unbelieveable! This is worth the ticket price right here, Baldwin!" BALDWIN..."Johnny, why are Wave of Destruction out here?" W of D talk with Carmilla outside the cage. They applaud Vrolok after the slam. Exe is hurt bad. Vrolok picks him up and rams his back into the cage! He drops him, and Exe falls between the ropes, right infront of Carmilla and Wave of Destruction. Vrolok yells out to the them, then holds Exc in place. Tidalwave pulls out a pair of handcuffs, and Soundwave and Vrolok hold the Excutioners' arms. BALDWIN..."What are they doing?! THey've got hand cuffs!" TOWNSAND..."Looks like they're making sure Vrolok wins this!" Franseca runs around the ring as Tidalwave snaps on the handcuffs, cuffing Executiner to the cage. Exe pulls madly, but his arm is locked. Vrolok gets off of him and slowly starts for the door. Franseca comes face to face with Carmilla and Wave of Destruction. TOWNSAND..."Hey, she better be careful! They won't hesitate to take her out!" BALDWIN..."Johnny, Vroloks at the door. Vrolok reaches the door while Executioner screams and bangs on the cage. The ref opens it. Vrolok slowly climbs out and reaches the floor! The ref signals the bell. TOWNSAND..."Vroloks done it!" BALDWIN..."Not on his own!" Wave of Destruction congratulate Vrolok. CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen... the winner of the match.. VROLOOOOK!!!" The fans boo, but the celebration comes to a quick end as Executioner screams and bangs on the cage. He renches his arm and the cuffs break free. He gets ups and madly shakes the cage. Vrolok, W of D, and Carmilla rush into the awaiting cart and make their way away from the ring. They are littered with garbage on their way out of the SkyDome. IN the ring, Franseca tries to calm the insenced Executioner. He is irrate, but eventually exits the cage and leaves the Dome. TOWNSAND..."I think I need a drink after that one! What a match! What a night so far!" BALDWIN..."johnny, I would'nt want to be Vrolok when the Executioner gets his hands on him! What he pulled tonight was dirty!" TOWNSAND..."yeah, but what are you going to do? Hey, with guys like Wave of Destruction watching his back, I don't think Vrolok has got too much to worry about!"
