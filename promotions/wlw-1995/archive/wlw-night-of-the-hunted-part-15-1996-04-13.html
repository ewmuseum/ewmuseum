WLW "Night of the Hunted" Part 15

13 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tom Russel climbs into the ring. The fans are cheering and waving to him. RUSSEL..."Alright everyone, we're just a few minutes away from the big one! But first, I want you all to give yourselves a big hand...because each and every one of you have helped the WLW set the brand new, all time SkyDome attendance record!!!" The fans cheer. RUSSEL..."That's right! Now, if you'll all direct your attention to the JumboTron...we'll get the numbers! Drum roll please!" A drum roll blasts out of the speakers. Graphics appear on the screen of a giant slot machine. The lever gets pulled and the numbers spinning, stopping one by one......74,579!!!! RUSSEL..."There it is!!! 74,579!! Thank you all for making the Night of the Hunted a success! Now, without further adu...I'll turn it back over to Bernie!" The fans continue to scream and yell as Russel climbs out of the ring. BALDWIN..."Well, there's the numbers, Johnny! A brand new attendance record!" TOWNSAND..."Hey, I wonder if they counted you twice!" BALDWIN..."Hey, knock it off!" TOWNSAND..."75,000 of them, baldwin! And you know, half of them are my close, personal friends!" BALDWIN..."I wouldn't believe you if you told me you had 2 friends!" The lights in the SkyDome dim and spotlights swirl over the capacity crowd. BALDWIN..."Alright fans, this is what we're all here for!" TOWNSAND..."This is it, Baldwin! The match of a lifetime! The match we never thought we'd see! I'm fired up!" BALDWIN..."We're all fired up! The talk is over, and the matches awaits! So let's go...what..okay...fans I'm told that we're going to Tom Russel, Tom?" TOWNSAND..."Oh, now what?!" Russel stands alone in the interview area. RUSSEL..."Guys, I just got word that for some reason, [name withheld] has requested that Gigantor enter the stadium first! Now, that's definately uncharacteristic of Rose, who as you know, loves the spotlight! I can't figure this one out! Back to you guys!" BALDWIN..."Well, what about that, Johnny?" TOWNSAND..."That idiot couldn't figure out a one coloured Rubic's Cube! Rose is just pullin' a psychout, Baldwin, but we're talking about Samhain here! I don't know if psychoouts work on this guy!" BALDWIN..."Well, we'll find out in a few minutes, Johnny! Let's go down to the ring!" CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen...it is now time for the Main Event of the Night of the Hunted!!" A roar comes from the crowd. BALDWIN..."Listen to them, Johnny!" TOWNSAND..."They love it, Baldwin! You know, this is what the expression "main event" was meant for! Come on, let's get it going!" CRUMB..."Would you welcome at this time, our guest ring announcer! You know him from his hits 'Dumb and Dumber' and 'The Mask'...the Riddler himself...Mr. JIM CARREY!!" Carrey comes running down the aisle, screaming and smiling as he does. BALDWIN..."Look at him, Johnny! He's too excited to wait for the cart!" TOWNSAND..."Well he should be! He's announcing the biggest main event that wrestling has ever seen! He better not screw it up!" The fans go wild as Carrey jumps into the ring and bounces of the ropes. Crumb hands him the mic. CARREY..."HOLD ON TO YOUR LUG NUTS...IT'S TIIIMMMEE FOR THE MAAIINNN EVEENT!!!" Carrey makes his famous 'big' smile and the fans love it. CARREY..."Alright everyone, this is the big one! It is for the WLW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP! First, by request..here is the champion! Along with his mananger [name withheld]...from parts unknown...weighing 355 lbs... he's the WLW World Champion...GIGANTOOORRRR!!! Come on, Big Guy!" The spotlight all focus on the entrance. As the cart slowly rolls in, the crowd gets their first look at Gigantor, and the cage that holds him. Rose walks beside the cart, holding the championship belt. BALDWIN..."There's the champion, Johnny!" TOWNSAND..."Yes he is, Baldwin. You know, Carrey must have balls of stone calling Gigantor 'big guy'! If he gets his hands on him, there's gonna be clown white all over the walls of the SkyDome!" BALDWIN..."Oh, come on Johnny!" Gigantor roars and madly shakes the cage. TOWNSAND..."Look at him, Baldwin! I've never seen him so wild!" BALDWIN..."Alright, I'm putting you on the spot! Pick a winner!" TOWNSAND..."Are you kiddin'?! The bookers in Vegas are pacin' on this one, you idiot! The winner will be the one who survives. It's that simple!" The cart comes to a stop, and Rose steps on the ring platform. Gigantor shakes and pounds on the cage until finally, he tears the door off, sending it crashing beside the ring. Some fans scream as the big man emerges from his cage. He stands by it and roars, pounding on his chest. Rose points to the ring, and Gigantor climbs in. CARREY..."Easy, big fella!" Rose paces around the ring. He hands the belt to Gigantor, who holds it up and roars. CARREY..."And the challenger...along with his Priest.. from the Darkest corner of Hell...weighing 251 lbs.. "The Lord of the Dead"...SAMHAAAIINNN!!! Come on down!" Carrey climbs out of the ring as the stadium is plunged into darkness. The Halloween music pounds out over the speakers. High above the ring, the full moon draws the attention of the crowd. To the surprise of everyone, it ignites, bursting into flames! BALDWIN..."Oh my god!" Pieces of buring paper and material shower down from the roof, landing in the ring. Rose runs for cover, but GIgantor doesn't move. The fire continues until suddenly, it goes out as fast as it started. The crowd looks on in awe as all that remains is the image of a giant jack-o-lantern, complete with glowing eyes. The giant pumpkin seems to stare down at Gigantor. TOWNSAND..."Oh my god, Baldiwn! Look at that!" The fans look on in darkness until the jumbotron picks up an image. A glowing pumpkin is slowly coming down the aisle. BALDWIN..."Look at that Johnny! It's a pumpkin!" TOWNSAND..."You know what that means! Here he comes!" BALDWIN..."Would somebody please get these lights turned back on!" The pumpkin travels down the long runway, coming closer and closer to the ring. The stadium is almost silent, as the fans strain their eyes to see in the darkness. The only light is from the video screen. The pumpkin comes to a stop at the end of the runway. NOthing happens. BALDWIN..."Oh, come on! The lights! Get the lights turned on!" TOWNSAND..."Your scared, aren't you Baldwin!" The cameras pick up the sound of Gigantor roaring in the ring. Samhain's music stops, and the Dome is quiet. Gigantor continues to roar. Rose grabs the microphone from the time table. ROSE..."What the hell is going on! Get these lights turned on, now!" The pumpkins still hasn't moved. Suddenly, the lights return, and Samhain is standing in the ring with Gigantor! TOWNSAND..."What the h." The Priest moves to the cart and picks up the jack-o-lantern, then stands in the corener. Samhain stands and studies Gigantor, but before he can disrobe, the giant charges. Gigantor races and smashes his huge fists against Samhain's head. He pounds over and over. As the time keeper rings the bell, he whips Samhain to the ropes and smashes him in the stomach with a shoulder block. Samhain doubles over. BALDWIN..."We're underway, Johnny!" TOWNSAND..."I don't believe this, Baldwin! Samhain can't even get his robe off!" Gigantor wraps his huge hand around Samhain's throat. He squeezes, and the ref tries to break the hold. He doesn't. He attempts a choke slam, but Samhain smashes his arm away. Samhain rams a knee to Gigantor's mid section, then whips him to the ropes, nailing him with an elbow. Gigantor doesn't move. Samhain comes at him, and the two begin to pound each other left and right. Gigantor gets the upper hand as Samhain staggers back. Gigantor swings again and misses. As he does, Samhain steps back, hits the ropes and nails Gigantor with the Sickle! Gigantor staggers, but doesn't go down. BALDWIN..."Whoa! He just hit him with the Sickle!" TOWNSAND..."Yeah, but he didn't get all of it! He didn't have the room he usually does to get the speed!" Rose pounds on the mat on the outside. High over head, the pumpkin looks down. Samhain runs and nails gigantor with a knee lift, again, staggering the big man. Samhain drops to one knee infront of Gigantor and punches him in the gut. Gigantor doubles over. As he does, Samhain hooks his arms, and plants him with a double underhook piledriver! Gigantor crashes to the mat. BALDIWN..."Gigantor is down!" TOWNSAND..."What a move! I can't believe he got him up!" Samhain tears off his robe and throws it out of the ring. Samhain pulls Gigantor to his feet and chops away. He whips him to the ropes, and bounces off the other side. As they race at each other, both men sticks out his arm. Both men crash to the mat with a double clothesline! TOWNSAND..."There both down!" The ref stands over them and starts his count. Rose is frantic on the outside, pounding on the mat and screaming. The priest doesn't move. The crowd screams and chants. It is still unclear who they are cheering for. The ref reaches 7 and both men sit up. Gigantor comes at Samhain, swings and misses. Samhain grabs him, and hits an inverted atomic drop. As Gigantor staggers, Samhain floors him with a clothesline. Gigantor lies on the mat, and Samhain stares at him, turning his head from side to side. Samhain pulls Gigantor to his feet, and whips him to the ropes. Gigantor reverses it and nails Samhain with another shoulder block. Samhain slams into the corner, then crashes to the mat. TOWNSAND..."Whoa! Did he ever nail him!" BALDWIN..."Well, Samhain has got the speed advantage, but the power definately goes to Gigantor!" TOWNSAND..."Oh god, look at this!" Gigantor picks up Samhain and sits him on the top turnbuckle. He climbs to the top, and to the shock of everyone, Gigantor superplexes Samhain to the mat! Both men land hard. Rose screams from the outside "pin him!" Gigantor covers..1..2..kick out!! Gigantor rolls off, then picks up Samhain. He tries for a suplex, but Samhain blocks it and drops Gigantor with a face slam! Gigantor lands hard. Samhain hauls the giant to his feet, whips him to the ropes, but it's reversed. Gigantor catches him, sinches his head, and drops him with spinning neck breaker! TOWNSAND..."Another move by Gigantor! I'll tell ya, Rose has got him ready! Who would have thought we'd see Gigantor doing superplexs and neck breakers!" Gigantor picks up Samhain, then gorilla presses him into the air. BALDWIN..."There's the power!" Gigantor growls as he holds up Samhain. Flash bulbs flicker around the Dome. Gigantor lets go, and Samhain falls the ten feet back to the mat. He lands on the small of his back, and he's hurt. Gigantor picks him back up and again plants him on the top turnbuckle. the fans begin to cheer. TOWNSAND..."my god, Baldwin! He's doin' another one!" Gigantor crawls up, and drops Samhain with a second superplex! The Priest watches from outside, but doesn't move. Gigantor covers...1..2..kick out!! Rose is irrate, screaming "slow count" to the referee. Gigantor roars and shakes the ropes. Samhain gets to his feet and Gigantor sees him. the giant comes at him, but is stopped as Samhain boots him in the stomach. Samhain scoops him, and brings Gigantor down, nailing him with a back breaker. Gigantor falls to the mat. Samhain hits the ropes and plants an elbow into the small of the big mans back. Samhain hauls up Gigantor and reaches for another pick up. As he does, Gigantor grabs his throat and choke slams him to the mat! BALDWIN..."What a power move!" TOWNSAND..."Samhain's got the brains, but Baldwin, Gigantor is just too powerful! Samhain can't control him!" Gigantor picks up Samhain and places him on his shoulder. He backs to the corner and runs, smashing Samhain to the mat with a power slam. Samhain lies motionless. Gigantor covers...1...2...kick out!! TOWNSAND..."How is Samhain kicking out of these?!" Gigantor comes at Samhain again and reaches to pull him up. As he does, Samhain swings up and nails him with a low blow. Gigantor doubles over. TOWNSAND..."ha ha! No matter how big you are, that's always gonna hurt you, baldwin! Well, maybe not you!" Samhain grabs Gigantor's arm and levels him with a short arm clothesline. He whips the giant to the ropes and nails him with a boot to the mid section. As Gigantor doubles, Samhain hits the ropes and drops him with a spinning DDT! The crowd jumps to their feet as Samhain covers...1..2..kick out!! The cameras pick up Rose, who whips the puddles of sweat from his forehead. Before Samhain can roll off, Gigantor grabs him and begins to bite. Samhain kicks and pounds on the mat, as the ref tries to stop the giant. Finally, Gigantor let's go. Samhain gets to his feet, hits the ropes, but is caught by Gigantor. The big man hoists Samhain into the air and choke slams him back down to the mat! BALDWIN..."This is back and forth, Johnny! What a main event!" TOWNSAND..."Baldwin, we ain't seen nothin' yet! They're both still breathing!"
