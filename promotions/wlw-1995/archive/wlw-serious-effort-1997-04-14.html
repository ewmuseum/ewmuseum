Serious Effort Towards This eFed About to Begin

14 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD LEAGUE WRESTLING IS IN NEED OF AN ORGANIZED STAFF OF PEOPLE TO HELP KEEP THE FLOW OF THE LEAGE GOING STEADY. WITH AN ORGANIZED STAFF OF PEOPLE, THESE ARE THE BENEFITS... -CARDS COME OUT ON TIME. -SINCE THIS IS A BOOKED LEAGUE, WITH A 5 PERSON TEAM THAT DECIDES MATCHES, IT'S MORE FAIR THAN JUST ONE PERSON DECIDING WHO WINS. -3 TO 4 CARD WRITERS WILL HELP TO HAVE THE CARDS OUT ON TIME SO IF ONE CARD WRITER ISN'T AVAILABLE FOR ONE DAY, WE STILL HAVE 3 OTHERS TO DO THE CARD. WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR RIGHT NOW ARE: -5 PEOPLE TO HELP DECIDE MATCHES(INCLUDING ME) -4 CARD WRITERS (INCLUDING ME) -2 BOOKERS(BOOKING SCHEDULES)(INCLUDING ME) -2 PEOPLE TO HANDLE FLASHES AND STUFF. -1 PERSON TO HANDLE THE MAILING LIST. IF INTERESTED IN ONE OF THESE POSITIONS, EMAIL ME. THE WAY I SEE IT, THIS IS A VERY GOOD WAY TO RUN A GOOD EFFICIENT LEAGUE. THANKS.
