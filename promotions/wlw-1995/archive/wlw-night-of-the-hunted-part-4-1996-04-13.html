WLW "Night of the Hunted" Part 4

13 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BALDWIN..."Alright, we're gonna go now to Tom Russel, who I believe, is somewhere on the floor!" Russel stands beside a group of fans near the ring. RUSSEL..."Thanks Mike. The stars, and the superstars are out tonight, all wanting to be a part of the Night of the Hunted! And a couple of them are with me now! From the Toronto Raptors, Damon Stoudamire, and Oliver Miller! Welcome to the Hunted, gentleman!" DAMON..."Thanks, Tom. It's good to be here!" RUSSEL..."Now, this place is like home to you guys! Ever seen it like this?" OLIVER..."No way, man! We came close when we took the Bulls, but this is wild!" RUSSEL..."Any match you guys are looking forward to?" DAMON..."Gigantor! Definatley!" RUSSEL..."Mighty Mouse and the Giant! Any hidden meaning?" OLIVER..."Just wishful thinking!" Oliver laughs and Damon gives him a friendly nudge. RUSSEL..."Hey, you too want to climb in the ring?" OLIVER..."No way, man. Cause you'll be givin' me all the belts!" RUSSEL..."Alright guys, all the best for the rest of the season! Mike and Johnny, back to you!" BALDWIN..."How about that, Johnny!" TOWNSAND..."Baldwin, if those two would spend time practising instead of sittin' around here, maybe they'd win a game!" BALDWIN..."Oh, come on!" TOWNSAND..."Enough of that! It's time, right? This is it?" BALDWIN..."The TV Title match is next, Johnny!" TOWNSAND..."I'm looking forward to this!" BALDWIN..."Alright, the match is here! NOw will you tell me who the consultant is?" TOWNSAND..."Baldwin, your like a kid at christmas! You'll find out in just a few minutes! All I'm gonna say, is hold on to your socks! Ha ha!" BALDWIN..."On that note fans, let's go to Tom Russel, who's standing by with the challenger!" Russel stands in the interview area. RUSSEL..."Alright guys, we are moments away from the start of Televion Title match. Joining me now are Kyle Kamikaze, the lovely Tiffany and Guido. Kyle are you ready for this match?" KAMIKAZE..."Ready, come on! I was ready to beat Flamie right after the tournament." RUSSEL..."Where did you find Guido and why do you need him?" TIFFANY..."Duh, we need him for protection and to prevent outside interference in Kyle's matches. And, where we found him, none of your business." RUSSEL..."Now for the big question, who is the special consultant?" TIFFANY..."We have waited long enough for this match so let's get to it." Kyle, Tiffany and Guido exit. RUSSEL..."Well, the secret lives on, at least for a few more minutes. Back to you guys." BALDWIN..."Come on, now this is getting ridiculous! Who is it, Townsand?!" TOWNSAND..."All good things to those who wait, Bald Man! Ha ha!" BALDWIN..."Your impossible. Fans, let's get down to the ring!" Crumb climbs into the ring as a the crowd roars. A wave is making it's way around the 500 level of the SkyDome. TOWNSAND..."Hey, check it out, Baldwin! We got a wave goin' around the Dome!" CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen...the following contest is scheduled for one fall..and is for the WLW TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP!! Introducing first, the challenger. Making his way to the ring along with his manager, Miss Tiffany, and the body guard Guido...from New York City...weighing 243 lbs...here is KYLE KAMIKAAAZEEEE!!!" Boos bounce off the roof as the cart enters the stadium. TOWNSAND..."Listen to them, Baldwin! They love him!" BALDWIN..."What?! Hey, I don't see anyone with them!" TOWNSAND..."How observant! Now, go reward yourself with another Big Mac!" Guido stands at the back of the cart with his arms folded, while Kyle points out and provokes some of the rowdy fans. CRUMB..."And his opponent...making his way to the ring along with Ebony Flame...from Sydney, Australia... weighing 295 lbs...he is the WLW TV Champion... SHADOW FLAAMMMEEE!!!" The capacity crowd boos even louder as the two roll down the runway. TOWNSAND..."Listen! They love him even more!" Shadow motions to Kyle, holding up his arms saying "where are they?" Kyle smiles and paces around the ring. Guido stands in the corner. the cart reaches the end of the runway, and Shadow and Ebony climb into the ring. Shadow rips off his belt and hold it up while Ebony grabs his arm and thrusts it in the air. The fans boo. Kyle walks to the ropes and motions to Crumb to hand him the mic. TOWNSAND..."Ha ha, here we go!" Kyle takes the mic and looks at Tiffany and Guido. KYLE..."Hey, I think we forgot someone!" The fans stand and look down the runway. KYLE..."Our special consultant is not new to WLW. Many fans know who he is. He has been around for quite some time. He will come as a surprise to many of you including Shadow Flame! Ladies and gentlemen, I give you the..... .ASTROCREEP!!" Aa cart rolls into the Dome and on it is Shadow Flames old manager, Astrocreep. Shadow jumps up on the ropes and yells at his former manager. BALDWIN..."That's Astrocreep!! He was Shadow Flame's former manager! What a coup de grace!!" TOWNSAND..."A what? Hey, isn't Kyle brilliant? What a better way to psychout an opponent than to get some one who was close with them on your side! I love it!" Shadow and Ebony are irrate in the ring. Shadow throws the belt down as Astro climbs out of the cart and moves to Kyle's corner. He and Tiffany shake hands, and he points up at Kyle. The time keeper rings the bell. Shadow charges and Kyle moves out of the way. Shadow kicks the ropes, obviously frustrated. TOWNSAND..."Look how this is bothering Shadow! If he doesn't keep his head straight, he'll be in trouble!" Shadow moves toward Kyle and they lock up. They struggle for a moment, each trying to get the upper hand. Shadow shoves Kyle to the ropes, misses a clothesline, then gets nailed with one of Kyles. Astro and Tiffany applaud on the outside. Kyle walks over to his corner and leans throught the ropes. Astro explains something and Kyle nods. As he comes back in, Kyle winks at Shadow. Some of the fans cheer.
