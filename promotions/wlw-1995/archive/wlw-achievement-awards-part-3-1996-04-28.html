WLW ACHIEVEMENT AWARDS! PART 3

28 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BALDWIN..."Welcome back everybody! This next award is one of the ones that I like. Feud of the Year! We've had alot of them so far, guys!" TOWNSAND..."yes we have, but I'm sure we ain't seen nothin' yet!" BALDWIN..."Alright, let's see the nominess!" FEUD OF THE YEAR flashes across the video wall. A montage of the greatest moments of each one follows. Voice over... "The nominees for Feud of the Year are... Gigantor vs. Rainbow Warrior Rick Styles vs. Shockwave Samhain vs. Rainbow Warrior The Alliance vs. The X-Treme Kyle Kamikaze vs. Shadow Flame Bret Young vs. Dropkick Vrolok vs. Executioner and Spartan vs. Alex Extreme" BALDWIN..."Okay, and the winner is..." RUSSEL..."I think you and Townsand should win this!" TOWNSAND..."Oh, your real funny, Russel!" BALDWIN..."The winner for Feud of the Year is... hey..we've got a tie! The winners are THE ALLIANCE VS. THE X-TREME and BRET YOUNG VS. DROPKICK!!!" TOWNSAND..."They were both great feuds! But we may not have seen the last of Young and Dropkick!" RUSSEL..."Well, guys, this leaves us with the big ones! Up next, Match of the Year!" TOWNSAND..."This one should be close! With all of the great matches we've seen so far, how the hell do you choose?!" BALDWIN..."Well, Johnny, a choice was made! So let's see what it is! The nominees please." MATCH OF THE YEAR lights up on the wall. Scenes from each match follow. Voice over.... "The nominees for the WLW's Match of the Year are... The Hell Match (Samhain vs. Rainbow Warrior) Styles vs. Banger (at the Hunted) Kamikaze vs. Spartan (TV Tournament) Gigantor vs. Rainbow Warrior (from Massacre) Battle Royal Lord of the Ring Rumble and The Massacre Match (War Games)" TOWNSAND..."Give me this one! Okay, and the winner is... ah, damn it! Another tie! GIGANTOR VS. RAINBOW and BATTLE ROYAL!!!" RUSSEL..."Both of these were outstanding matches!" TOWNSAND..."Yeah, and they were both won by Gigantor!" BALDWIN..."You know guys, the Tag Team scene here the WLW is really starting to heat up! So lets take a look at the nominees for Tag Team of the Year!" The graphics for TAG TEAM OF THE YEAR flash across the screen, showing each team in action. Voice over... "The nominees for the WLW's Tag Team of the Year are... The Masters of Motion Wave of Destruction The Shock ` Mayhem Inc. Total Destruction and the American Dreams" BALDWIN..."And the tag team of the year is...alright, the WLW Tag Team Champions...THE MASTERS OF MOTION!!!" TOWNSAND..."You know, I think they're both a couple of punks, but I gotta give them some credit. They climbed to the top with record speed and captured the belts in one of the greatest tag team contests I've ever witnessed!" RUSSEL..."Well, those are some surprising comments! I'll agree with, Johnny! They've shown everyone just what they're made of!" TOWNSAND..."Yeah, but like I said, they're still punks!" BALDWIN..."Okay guys, this is the big one! The Award for the WLW's Entertainer of the Year!" TOWNSAND..."Save your time, and just give it to me now, Baldwin!" BALDWIN..."Your not even nominated!" TOWNSAND..."An over site, that's all!" BALDWIN..."Roll the nominees!" The graphics of WLW ENTERTAINER OF THE YEAR flash across the scene, showing each man in his greatest moment. Voice over.... "The nominees for the WLW's Entertainer of the Year are... Gigantor Spartan Rick Styles Samhain Alex Extreme Kyle Kamikaze ` Mayhem Inc. Masters of Motion Johnny Banger Wave of Destruction Dropkick and Vrolok" BALDWIN..."And the WLW Entertainer of the Year is... "Top Dog" RICK STYLES!!!" TOWNSAND..."Alright! The perfect choice! Not only entertainer of the year, but he also gets the Townsand Award! What more could you ask for! Looks like I'm gonna be doin' some drinkin' tonight, boys! Ha ha!" BALDWIN..."Alright folks, that's it..." VOICE OVER..."Ah, Mike. There's still one more award to present. That being, the WLW PERSONALITY OF THE YEAR!" TOWNSAND..."Figures you trying to duck outta here before that one, Baldwin! Always gotta be first in line at the buffet, don't ya!" VOICE OVER..."And the nominees are... Mike Baldwin "Screaming" Tom Russel "Big J" Johnny Townsand Randy Ventura and Bernie Crumb" BALDWIN..."Good luck, Tom" RUSSEL..."Same to you, Mike." VOICE OVER..."And the WLW Personality of the year is...." TOWNSAND..."Hold it! I want a drum roll for this one!" A drum roll sounds over the speakers. VOICE OVER..."The winner is..."Big J" JOHNNY TOWNSAND!!!" TOWNSAND..."Ha ha ha! I knew it! It had to be me! The greatest colour commentator in the world today! I knew I was gonna win, so lucky for you, I prepared a little speech. Now, when I first came here...." Russel and Baldwin walk off the stage as the lights begin to turn off one by one. The credits roll over the monitors, and the audience slowly exits the room TOWNSAND..."I told myself, Johnny, your the greatest! You are the only one who...hey...where did everyone go?" The screen fades to black. THIS HAS BEEN A SPECIAL PRESENTATION OF WORLD LEAGUE WRESTLING. \ / | \ / \ /\ / | \ /\ / \/ \/ |_ _ \/ \/ World League Wrestling Copyright 1996.
