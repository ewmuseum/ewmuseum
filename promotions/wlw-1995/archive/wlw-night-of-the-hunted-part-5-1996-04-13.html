WLW "Night of the Hunted" Part 5

13 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kyle walks to the center and the two lock up again. Shadow reaches under and quickly slams Kyle to the mat. He stomps away on his head until the ref pushes him back. Shadow grabs a handful of Kyle's hair and yanks him to his feet. He measures, and smashes a fist into Kyle's face. Tiffany and Astro yell from outside. Ebony paces around, making sure to stay clear of Guido. Shadow whips Kyle to the ropes, and drops for a back drop. Kyle bounces off the ropes and swings, nailing Shadow with an uppercut! TOWNSAND..."Whoa! Shadow just made a cardinal mistake!" Kyle sinches Shadow heads and drops him to the mat with a Russian leg sweep. Ebony pounds on the mat. Kyle hits the ropes and plants an elbow on Shadow then covers..1..2..kick out!! Kyle picks up Shadow, lifts him and smashes him down with a back breaker. As Shadow lays on the mat, Kyle climbs to the second ropes and drops and elbow on Shadow's neck. He covers again..1..2..kick out!! Astro screams in instructions as Kyle pulls Shadow back to his feet. Kyle scoops, but Shadow rakes his eyes. The fans boo. Shadow swings and nails him with a big clothesline. Ebony applauds as Shadow walks to Kyle's corner and gives Astro the finger. TOWNSAND..."Ha ha, look Baldwin! Shadow's saying 'hi'!, Ha ha!" Shadow walks over to Kyle, pulls him and runs, sending him over the top rope! He lands hard beside Ebony. The fans jump to their feet as Ebony kicks away at Kyle. Guido races around the ring and grabs Ebony from behind. He spins him and drops him with a huge punch. The ref screams at him through the ropes to back off. Guido waves him off and rolls Kyle back into the ring, then returns to his corner. Ebony is down on the floor. Shadow wraps Kyle's hair around his hands, picks him up, and swings him by his hair. Kyle screams and falls to the mat. BALDWIN..."Hey, it's a good thing that's not you, Johnny, or Shadow would be going home with a souvenir! Ha ha!" TOWNSAND..."Oh, your hilarious!" Shadow continues his attack. Kyle begins to get up, and Shadow stomps on his fingers. He then boots him in the kidneys, knocking him back down. Kyle rolls to his corner. Shadow rushes over and swings through the ropes at Astro. He misses. He pulls Kyle to his feet, whips him to the ropes, and powerslams him to the mat. He covers...1..2..kick out!! Shadow bounces off the ropes, drops and elbow, but misses. Kyle gets up and quickly drops an elbow. Kyle climbs the ropes... TOWNSAND..."This is it, Baldwin! If he hits, new champion!" Shadow lies on the mat as Ebony screams at him from the outside. The fans are on their feet. Astro and Tiffany applaud from the outside. Kyle perches himself then leaps with his moonsault. As he plumets, Ebony screams "now" and Shadow pulls up his legs. Kyle crashes down onto them and rolls off holding his stomach. Shadow rolls on him and covers...1..2..21/2..kick out!! Shadow looks frustrated. He pulls Kyle to his feet and sinches a piledriver. He jumps in the air and brings Kyle down hard! He covers..1.. Tiffany reaches in the ring and puts Kyle's leg on the rope. ..2...thr.. The ref jumps and pats Shadow on the back. Shadow leaps up and thrusts his arms in the air. The fans boo. TOWNSAND..."What the hell just happened there?" BALDWIN..."Did Shadow win?" The ref pulls down Shadow's arm and points to the ropes, indicating that Kyle had his foot on them. Shadow is irrate. TOWNSAND..."Brilliant move by Tiffany! See Baldwin, now that's a woman. Beautiful and smart! Not like that pig Krissy Banger!" BALDWIN..."Please, I think Krissy Banger is good looking!" TOWNSAND..."No kidding! Look at the pig you go out with!" BALDWIN..."Do you mind?!" Shadow shoves the ref out of the way and grabs Kyle. As he pulls him to his feet, Kyle rolls him in a small package! The ref drops...1..2..kick out!! Shadow jumps up, scoops Kyle, and throws him over the top rope! Kyle lands beside Guido, who begins to help his man back in the ring. Ebony races around the ring and nails Guido in the back of the head with a forearm. The big man falls to the mat. Shadow climbs out of the ring, and he and Ebony turn their attention to AStrocreep. BALDWIN..."We've got pandemonium breaking out on the floor!" TOWNSAND..."Uh oh! Look at this, baldwin! THey've got Astrocreep!" Shadow and Ebony each grab a side of Astor's head and ram him into the ring post! The manager falls to the mat, as blood trickles out of his forehead. Kyle is starting to get to his feet. Guido is down beside him. Tiffany is on the opposite side of the ring. Ebony motions to Shadow to "hold him", and Shadow nods. He pulls Kyle to his feet, spins him around, and sinches his arms. The ref screams at the men to get back in the ring. He attempts to count. Ebony races at Kyle and swings. Kyle pulls free and Ebony smashes Shadow in the face with a forearm. Shadow falls backward and smashes his head into the guard rail. Guido gets to his feet. He races at Ebony and tackles him to the ground. They pummel each other. Kyle climbs into the ring. Shadow lays motionless on the mat. Kyle climbs the ropes. TOWNSAND..."What's this?!" A sea of flash bulbs flood the Dome as Kyle leaps off the top rope and nails Shadow with a moonsault on the floor! TOWNSAND..."What a move!" BALDWIN..."I've never seen a move like that in my life!" Kyle picks up Shadow and rolls him into the ring, as Guido and Ebony continue to battle. Tiffany helps Astro back to his feet. Shadow lays on the mat hurting. Kyle draps himself across Shadow and the ref drops and counts...1..2..3!!! Kyle rolls off of Shadow as the ref signals for the bell. Tiffany grabs the belt from the time keeper and races into the ring. She hands it to Kyle and he thrusts it in the air. Some cheers can be heard. TOWNSAND..."He's done it, Baldwin!" CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen...the winner of the match... and the NEEEWWWWW....WLW TELEVISION CHAMPION....KYLE KAMIKAAAZEEEE!!!!" Guido belts Ebony in the face one last time and leaves him on the ground. Kyle jumps off the ring and Guido catches him. THe four climb into a cart and leave the SkyDome with the gold. Shadow and Ebony get up and walk up the aisle to the boos and jeers of the fans. TOWNSAND..."What a match! I like Shadow Flame, but Kyle, buddy, my hats off to ya! Come on, Baldwin, admit it! It was great!" BALDWIN..."Johnny, I'll agree that that was the best TV title match I've ever seen! But I don't know if we've seen the last of these two!" TOWNSAND..."Shadow is gonna be steamin' for awhile, and he's gonna want to get his belt back! You can bet he'll try it, anytime and anywhere! Hey, you got a pen on ya?" BALDWIN..."Why?" TOWNSAND..."Cause I gotta keep track of all the parties I gotta go to tonight!" BALDIWN..."Please!"
