WLW SATURDAY NIGHT SUPERSTARS! PART 3

7 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BALDWIN..."Alright, let's try and get things back together around here. Uh..let's get down to..what? Um, I'm being told that Tom Russel is standing by. Take it Tom!" Tom Russel is standing on the live interview stage next to the runway. RUSSEL..."Alright fans. At the requst of [name withheld], would you please welcome the WLW North American Champion, "Top Dog" RICK STYLES!" Rose and Styles enter the arena to a chorus of boos. Rose is quick to get on the platform. Styles struts up the steps, with his belt drapped over his Armani suit. RUSSEL..."Alright, let's get this over with. The fans are here to see that action, not to listen to you two!" ROSE..."My man here has something to announce. All you punks out there can wait!" RUSSEL..."Now wait a minute, Rose!" STYLES..."Listen Russel, I've got something important to say. When I'm done, you can get on with your matches!" RUSSEL..."Well then? What is this big news?" STYLES..."This week, I sent out a message to each member of the Stable and informed them that a war between myself, Spartan and them was about to happen, and I offered each of them an opportunity to join the winning side, which is the "Top Dog's" side. To be honest, one of them ignored me, one of them told me to go fuck myse..." RUSSEL..."Hey! Watch the language!" STYLES..."..and the other told me to go to hell." RUSSEL..."Well, I would think so..." STYLES..."However...on Thursday night, one of them changed their mind and agreed to join the "Top Dog" Rick Styles in ending the Stable. RUSSEL..."Did you say what I think you just said?" ROSE..."Clean out your ears, Russel! We have a new ally, but this new ally is not ready to reveal himself yet. We told him to lay low, deny everything. Talk amongst yourselves, Stable, and wonder which of you is the traitor. The one who will be the final nail in the coffin of the stable. Now go back to your matches, Russel!" Rose and Styles climb off the stage and exit the arena to the boos of the crowd. BALDWIN..."What the hell are they talking about now! What, they've talked some member of the Stable into turning? I don't believe it!" TOWNSAND..."You don't have to believe it, Baldwin. But just keep in mind, Rose is a sweet talker, and maybe he found himself a sweet tooth! Ha ha!" BALDWIN..."This is getting too weird! Fans, let's get down to the ring!" CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen...this next contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from Harlem NY...weighing 324 lbs...here is DISSSSCIPLINNNE!!!" The fans look on as Discipline enters the arena. He is dressed in an old police uniform, that he stole from a cop after beating him mercilessly. He ingnores the booing fans as he climbs into the ring. TOWNSAND..."Oh boy, I lookin' forward to this one!" CRUMB..."And his opponent...from parts unknown...weighing 289 lbs... here is PHANTOMMM KNIGHT!!!" The giant enters the arena and Discipline leaps onto the ropes calling him on. BALDWIN..."I can't get over the size of the Knight! He's huge!" TOWNSAND..."Look at this! Discipline's waving him on!" Discipline jumps out of the ring and races down the aisle, tackling the knight to the ground. The fans on the aisle seats cheer on as Discipline hammers away with lefts and rights. He pulls the big man to his feet and rams his head into the steel railing. Knight falls to the floor holding his head, and Discipline walks slowly back to the ring. The ref races down the runway and helps Knight to his feet. The Knight sees Discipline waiting in the ring and he shoves the ref out of the way. Knight runs and slides under the bottom ropes. Disc catches him, and drives a series of knees into the small of his back. Knight rolls out of the ring as the ref climbs in and orders Disc to move back. Knight gets back in and the two lock up in the center. Knight slaps on a head lock and shoves Disc to the ropes. Disc bounces off and attempts a shoulder block, but the big man doesn't move. Disc hits the ropes again, this time leaping for a dropkick. Knight moves and he crashes to the mat. Knight pulls him to his feet, hooks him and drops him with a belly to back suplex. The fans scream as Disc lands hard. Knight measures him and plants an elbow. He stands and drops another, but Disc moves! Disc jumps to his feet and grabs Knight, hauling him up. Disc whips him ,and Knight crashes hard into the turnbuckle. Disc runs and smashes against him with a running splash. Knight staggers out of the corner but doesn't go down. Disc runs and nails him in the back of the head with a clothesline. Knight goes down hard. Disc pulls him up, tucks his head, and lifts him for a powebomb. The fans jump to their feet as Disc brings him down hard against his knee! Knight collapses to the mat holding his back. TOWNSAND..."What a move!" Disc covers...1..2..kick out!!" Disc is frustrated and pounds on the mat. He runs, but Knight grabs his leg and sweeps him to the mat. Disc crashes infront of him. Knight wraps his huge hand around his neck and chokes away. The ref counts as Disc kicks and pounds on the mat. Knight doesn't break the hold. The ref counts again, and KNight lets go before he gets to 3. Knight stands over Disc, who lies on the mat holding his throat. Knight pulls him up and slaps on a full nelson. Disc wiggles in pain trying to get out, while the ref checks his condition. Before he can ask, Knight renches, and suplexes Disc hard to the mat! BALDWIN..."Unbelieveable! What a move!" TOWNSAND..."He could have broken his neck with a move like that!" Knight lays across Disc as the ref counts..1..2..kick out!! TOWNSAND..."How are they kicking out of these?!" Knight yanks Disc to his feet, whips him to the ropes, and ducks for a back drop. Disc catches him with a boot to the face, staggering the Knight. Disc hits the ropes and nails him with a clothesline. Knight crashes to the mat. Disc stands in the middle of the ring, looking out at the fans. He turns and makes his way to the turnbuckle. TOWNSAND..."He's goin' up!" The fans scream and yell as Disc perches himself on the top rope. Knight gets to his feet and looks for his opponent, seeing him just as he leaps. Disc soars through the air and nails Knight with a flying clothesline! The fans scream as Knight folds in half and crashes to the mat. Disc drops over him and hooks a leg...1..2..3!!! The fans boo as Discipline jumps up and thrusts his arms into the air. The ref tries to raise one, but Discipline shoves him away and jumps out of the ring. CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen..the winner of the match... DISSCIPLINNNE!!!" Discipline walks up the aisle with his arm in the air. The fans boo him as he exits. In the ring, the ref helps Knight to his feet, but is quickly shoved off. Knight climbs out of the ring and exits in disgust. BALDWIN..."What a match! I don't think we've seen the last of these two, JOhnny!" TOWNSAND..."Maybe not, but the fans have seen the last of the hotdogs! Christ Baldwin, ya ate 'em all!" BALDWIN..."Would you stop! I did not!"
