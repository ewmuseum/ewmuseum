WLW "Night of the Hunted" Part 12

13 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As Styles looks at the ref, Banger swings and clotheslines Styles over the top rope! Style crashes to the floor. Rose tries to help him up, but Banger jumps to the floor and shoves him away. He grabs Styles, picks him up, and rams his back into the ring post! The fans are going wild. Banger lets go of Styles, and he falls to the floor. Rose stands by the steps afraid to get near Banger. Styles slowly gets to his feet, but is quickly sinched by Banger. As the fans screams and cheer, Banger DDT's Styles on the ring platform! TOWNSAND..."Somebody stop that idiot!" Styles lies on the ground in obvious pain. Banger pulls him up and rolls him into the ring. He looks out at Krissy and she nods to him. TOWNSAND..."What the hell is that supposed to mean?!" Banger pulls Styles to his feet, and to the delight of the thousands of fans, he slaps on the Boop! BALDWIN..."Oh my god, Johnny! Banger's got the Boop on Styles! He's got him!" TOWNSAND..."What's this...look at Rose!" As Banger adds more pressure to the hold, Rose reaches under the ring and pulls out a chair. He climbs into the ring. The ref is still down. Styles is near unconsciouness as Rose swings the chair, smashing it into the back of Bangers head! He falls to the mat, and Styles falls ontop of him. Rose throws the chair out of the ring and drops to the mat and counts .1..2..3!!! The fans boo and yell. TOWNSAND..."That's it! The match is over! Give him the belt!" BALDWIN..."Are you nuts?! Rose can't count him out!" TOWNSAND..."He just did!" Rose grabs Styles and starts to shake him. Styles begins to revive. Krissy screams at the ref, but he still hasn't moved. Styles slowly gets to his feet and sees that Banger is down. He looks out at all the fans who are screaming at him to "drop dead!" Rose yells at him to "do it!". Styles grabs Bangers legs and straps on the S.T.F! Banger struggles and screams as the pressure is applied. Rose grabs the ref and begins to shake him as well. As he does, Krissy jumps into the ring, runs, and kicks Styles in the stomach! The fans go wild. TOWNSAND..."Look at that woman! She and Banger should be disqualified!" Styles crumples to the mat. The ref begins to come to and sees both Banger and Styles down. Rose rolls out of the ring. The ref gets to his feet and braces himself with the top rope. He begins to count..1..2..3..4.. The fans count along. Rose and Krissy scream at their men to get up. ..5..6..7..8.. Banger sits up. ..9.. Styles sits up. The ref waves off the count. Banger gets to his feet and pulls Styles to his. Styles looks up at Banger and jams a finger in his eye. As Banger doubles over, Styles sinches him, and then drops him with a snap suplex! He covers..1..2..kick out!! TOWNSAND..."What a move by Styles! He pulled that outta no where!" Styles pulls Banger to his feet, whips him to the ropes and nails him with a tilt-a-whirl back breaker. Banger falls to the mat. Styles covers..1..2..kick out!! Styles begins to look frustrated. He pulls Banger to his feet and again, slaps him across the face. He screams into the injured man face, prauding him with a finger in the chest. He shoves him to the mat then looks out to the crowd. Banger gets to his feet and grabs Styles from behind, smashing him down with an inverted atomic drop. Styles bounces off of Bangers knee and falls to the mat. The fans go wild. Banger goes to the top rope. BALDWIN..."he's going up!" TOWNSAND..."As apposed to going down?!" BALDWIN..."would you stop!" Styles gets to his feet and looks around for Banger. Banger leaps and nails Styles with a flying forearm, knocking him through the ropes to the outside. Rose rushes over. Banger goes to the floor and grabs Rose, throwing him to the ground. He grabs Styles and slams him down on the ring platform. the ref starts a count. Banger picks up Styles, rolls him back in the ring and again goes to the top. Styles lies on his back. Banger leaps for a flying splash. Styles puts up his legs, and Banger crashes hard ontop of them! BALDWIN..."Desperation move by Styles!" TOWNSAND..."Desperation?! That's skill you idiot!" Banger grabs at his stomach, gasping for breath. The ref checks his condition. Styles pushes the ref out of the way and hauls Banger to his feet. The fans scream as he lifts him and drops him with a stomach breaker. Banger collapses in agony. Styles backs off and whips the sweat from his face. Both men are drenched. Styles measures Banger, and plants an elbow onto the small of his back. He rolls Banger over and covers...1..2..kick out!! The fans begin to cheer Banger on hoping to inspire him. Rose climbs onto the apron and yells instructions to Styles. The US champ picks up Banger and goes for a whip to the ropes. Banger reverses it, and Styles crashes into Rose! Rose falls off the ring and lands on the outside, while Styles lands on the mat. Banger dives on him..1..2..kick out!! The fans leap to their feet thinking the ref counted 3. He quickly waves them off. Bamger whips Styles to the ropes and catches him, powerslamming him to the mat. He covers...1..2..kick out!! BALDWIN..."Where is Styles getting this from?!" TOWNSAND..."This is unbeliveable, Baldwin! They're both fighting like champions!" Banger pulls Styles up, whips him to the ropes, and drops him with a huge back body drop. Flash bulbs flood the Dome as Styles soars through the air. He lands hard, and Banger covers...1..2..kick out!! Rose gets back to his feet. Banger is obviously frustrated. He picks up Styles, whips him to the ropes and plants him on the mat with a spine buster! BALDWIN..."What a move!" Banger covers...1..2..kick out!! TOWNSAND..."Styles has to roll out of the ring and get some air! If he doesn't break Banger's momentum, it's over!" Banger dives on Styles chest and pounds away with lefts and rights. The ref finally has to pull him off. Banger pulls up Styles and whips him to the ropes. Styles reverses it, and Rose leaps up and pulls down the top rope. Banger flies over top and down to the floor. The crowd boos and throws things at Rose. Banger gets up and comes at Rose. Styles climbs the ropes and leaps, nailing Banger with a double axhandle. Banger falls to the mat. Styles and Rose kick away at him until the ref jumps out of the ring and pushes them off. Styles climbs back in the ring to a tremendous boo from the crowd. Banger slowly gets to his feet and climbs onto the apron. Styles comes at him and lands a punch to the side of his head. He sinches him, then belly-back suplexs him back into the ring. The fans scream. Styles covers...1.. Banger puts his foot on the ropes, and Rose pushes it off. ...2...3!!! The ref jumps up and signals for the bell as the shocked crowd looks on. Rose leaps into the ring jumping around screaming and yelling. He helps Styles to his feet and raises his arm in the air. TOWNSAND..."He did it! He did it! I told you Baldwin! Styles is the North American Champion!" BALDWIN..."Banger had his foot on the ropes! We all saw it! Rose pushed it off!" TOWNSAND..."I didn't see that! My monitor must have gone out! Bottom line, Baldwin, Styles leaves with the gold!" Krissy argues with the ref about Roses actions, but the ref shrugges his shoulders. Banger rolls out of the ring, exhausted. Styles leans on Rose, almost too tired to stand. Alicia and Crumb enter the ring. Rose grabs the belt off the tray and straps it on his man. CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen...the winner of the match.. and the FIRST EVER....WLW NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION... "Top Dog" RICK STYYYLESSSS!!!!" The audience is outraged as Banger and Krissy are driven up the aisle and out of the SkyDome. In the ring, Styles walks around with the belt around his waist. The fans boo and scream, but he and Rose love it. Another cart comes, and Styles and Rose climb in. As it starts off, Styles orders the driver to stop. He waves Alicia over, and she climbs in. The cart rolls down the runway, and out of the arena. TOWNSAND..."Hey, look at that, Baldwin! Big surprise she took off with the winner! Someone's gonna be gettin' it tonight! ha ha!" BALDWIN..."Your disgusting, Johnny! Fans, I think Tom Russel has caught up with Styles. Let's go to the back." Russel walks into the scene of Rose and STyles. Gigantor is in the cage behind them. RUSSEL..."What happened out there..." ROSE...takes the mic "What happened out there is that Styles is the North American champion. I said it would happen and it did. I'm one for one and soon I'll be two for two!" RUSSEL..."What about your actions at ringside?!" ROSE..."We've got some celebrating to do! This interview is over!" RUSSEL..."He seems a little defensive! Back to you guys!" TOWNSAND..."Well, there's another party! Ha ha! Baldwin, I'm gonna be so pissed to..." BALDWIN..."As much as we'd like to hear about your drinking abilities, anything else regarding the match?" TOWNSAND..."About the match? What's left to be said? Rick Styles...North American Champion! It's got quite the ring to it, don't ya think?"
