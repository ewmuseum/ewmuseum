WLW "Night of the Hunted" Part 13

13 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BALDWIN..."Fans, I believe "Screamin' Tom" has found someone else on the floor. Tom, are you there?" Russel is standing in the crowd. Sitting together are Joe Carter and Doug Gilmour. RUSSEL..."Alright guys, I'm here with two guys who are practically trademarks of Toronto! From the Maple Leafs, Doug Gilmour, and from the Blue Jays, Joe Carter. Welcome to the Hunted, guys!" DOUG..."Thanks, Tom. Good to be here." RUSSEL..."Now Joe, you had probably one of the greatest moments ever in the SkyDome. So how does this compare?" JOE..."Well, its coming close, but I still have to side with the home run." RUSSEL..."Doug, hockey is considered one of the violent sports. Think you might want to climb in the ring here?" DOUG..."Well, that depends. Get that Townsand guy in there, and I might step in." RUSSEL..."Whoa, I think we have a challenge! Thanks guys, enjoy the rest of the night! Back to you guys!" BALDWIN..."Well, Joe Carter and Dou..." TOWNSAND..."Hey, that punk wants to climb into the ring with me? I'd knock the rest of his teeth out of his mouth!" BALDWIN..."Alright, Johnny, coming up, we've got the explosion of the American Dreams! You know, this is a match I never thought I'd see! These guys are cousins! They were best friends, and now..." TOWNSAND..."Oh, stop ballin'! The fact is, the team was wrapped in controversy! Young wasn't happy! He was sick of carrying that loser Dropkick, and he got rid of him!" BALDWIN..."Young went after his fiancee!" TOWNSAND..."Big deal! So he gave her a little tickle, so what! Bottom line, Bald Man, Bret Young is gonna wipe the mat with his cousin!" BALDWIN..."Fans, let's get down to the ring!" Crumb steps into the ring. CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen...the following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from Miami, Florida...weighing 236 lbs...here is "Mr. Execitment"...DROOOOPKICK!!!" The fans go wild as Dropkicks music pounds over the speakers. They see him on the jumbo tron before he enters the stadium, and the women scream and yell. BALDWIN..."Listen to this, Johnny! They love him in Toronto!" TOWNSAND..."That's only cause they don't know any better! Let's get back to Russel who's with the 'real' Amercian Dream, Bret Young!" RUSSEL..."Alright, thanks guys! Bret Young, come on in here." YOUNG..."What the hell do you want?" RUSSEL..."This is the biggest match of your career. How do you go into a match where your opponent is your cousin?" YOUNG..."Listen Dropkick, I had your fiancee pinned and screaming. Now I'm going to do the same to you HAHAHAHAHAHA!" RUSSEL..."Well don't you think he knows alot about you?" YOUNG..."HAHAHAHAHA He sucks! Why do you think I turned on him. I'm WLW's BADBOY, and I always will be! WOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!" Young exits. RUSSEL..."Imagine having that guy at your family reunion. Back to you guys!" Dropkick paces around the ring. CRUMB..."And his opponent...from Miami, Florida... weighing 228 lbs...here is "The Fantasy" BRET YOOUUUNG!!!" The fans erupt in boos as Young makes his way to the ring. Dropkick jumps up on the ropes and waves his cousin down. Bret does the same from the cart. TOWNSAND..."They can't wait to get at each other!" As Youngs cart continues along, Dropkick runs from the ring and climbs on it. BALDWIN..."Look at Dropkick!" The driver stops the cart. Dropkick climbs on and grabs hold of Bret. The struggle, then Dropkick pulls his cousin out of the cart and slams him on the concrete! Bret crashes hard. The fans love it. Most watch the screen because they can't see up the aisle. Dropkick grabs Bret, sinches him, and drops him on the conrete with a suplex! TOWNSAND..."Soembody should stop that idiot! He should be disqualified right now!" Dropkick picks up Bret for a powerslam and carries him to the ring. He climbs onto the platform and rams Bret, shoulder first, into the ringpost. He drops him on the mat and climbs into the ring. The fans go wild. The time keeper, finally rings the bell. young pulls himself up and slides into the ring. Dropkick grabs him, whips him to the ropes and plants him with a spine buster! BALDWIN..."What a move!" Dropkick waits for Young to get to his feet, then takes him down with a head scissors. He tightens his legs and turns it into a submission! The fans jump to their feet. The ref drops beside Bret and checks his condition. Brett tries to wiggle out, but can't. He stretches out and manages to get his foot on the rope. The ref orders Dropkick to let go, but he doesn't. Instead, he tightens the hold. The ref finally pulls him off and begins to repremand him. Bret catches his breath and climbs the ropes. Dropkick sees him, and catches him on the top, slamming off the turnbuckle! The stadium erupts. Dropkick stands in the corner, again waiting for Bret to get up. As he does, Dropkick leaps nailing Bret with a diving elbow smash. Bret crashes to the mat. TOWNSAND..."What the hell is going on! Who the hell is that referee? Get him back, ref!" BALDWIN..."It looks like Dropkick knows quite a bit about Bret!" TOWNSAND..."Shut up, Baldwin!" Bret gets back up and is dropped again as Dropkick nails him with a jumping DDT! The Dome is rocking with screams, cheers and air horns as Dropkick stands over his former partner. He pulls him up, whips to the ropes, but Bret reverses it! As Dropkick flies at him, Bret nails him with a back heel kick! Dropkick falls to the mat, as the cheers instantly turn to boos. TOWNSAND..."Ha ha! That oughta shut these people up! Here we go!" Bret comes up behind Dropkick and slaps on a cobra clutch. He renches back and Dropkick screams out in agony. TOWNSAND..."Listen Baldwin! You can hear his bones snappin' from here!"
