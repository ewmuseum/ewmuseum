WLW "Night of the Hunted" Part 2

13 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Raekwon picks up Hayes and rams him into the ring post. The ref begins a count. Raekwon holds Hayes head against the ring post and rears back for a head. Hayes ducks and raekwon smashes his own head into the ring post! BALDWIN..."Raekwon just posted himself!" TOWNSAND..."That was a major error, Baldwin! He better watch the ref's count! It's gettin' close!" Hayes rolls into the ring as the count reaches 7. The fans count along ..7..8..9..Raekwon slides into the ring. Hayes hauls him to his feet, scoops him and slams him hard to the mat. He hits the ropes and nails the big man with a knee drop. Raekwon looks winded. Hayes pulls him back up to his feet and sinches a suplex. He renches but can't get him up. Raekwon renches and nails Hayes with a vertical suplex. he stands over him and measures, then rams a fist drop into Hayes's neck. Raekwon picks up Hayes and plants him on his shoulder. He walks to a corner and runs, planting Hayes with a powerslam. The fans scream as Hayes hits the mat. Raekwon looks around at the capacity crowd, all of them booing him. Rick points to the turnbuckle screaming "do it!" Raekwon nods. He pulls Hayes up and walks him to the ropes. Raekon climbs up and pulls Hayes up after him. the fans jump to their feet screaming 'no"! TOWNSAND..."he's pullin' him up, Baldiwn! He nails this and it's over!" Raekwon tucks Hayes head and prepares for his powerbomb. As he renches, Hayes flips Raekwon, backing body dropping him from the top rope! Raekwon crashes to the mat, gasping from breath! Hayes leaps from the top rope and crashes down onto Raekwon with a splash! He covers...1...2..3!!! The fans erupt as Hayes rolls out of the ring, being greeted by an elated Corley. They climb into the ring cart and head up the aisle to a tremendous ovation from the fans, leaving an irrate Raekwon and Slick Rick in the ring. BALDWIN..."Hayes got him, Johnny!" TOWNSAND..."Baldwin, that was a total upset! Raekwon had him where he wanted him, but Hayes had to play dirty!" BALDIWN..."Dirty?! What are you talking about?" TOWNSAND..."Hayes was scratchin' and bitin' his way throught the entire match! Didn't you see it?" BALDWIN..."Your nuts! Alright fans, I'm being told that Tom Russel is standing by a special interview!" Russel enters the Dome and climbs up onto the interview platform, waving to the fans. RUSSEL..."Good evening, everyone! "Top Dog" Rick Styles (croud boos, Russel waits for them to settle down) has requested interview time here, at Night of the Hunted where he has promised something special. Well, whatever it is, let's hope it he leaves his manager behind. Ladies and gentleman, the U.S. champion, "Top Dog" Rick Styles!!" Styles's music begins to play. He makes his way to the podium with his fist in the air and a big smile ignoring all the boos. Russel looks relieved that [name withheld] is not with him. TOWNSAND..."Hey, it's Styles! I didn't know about this interview! Badlwin, would you put down that damn hot dog and pay attention!" BALDWIN..."Do you mind?!" STYLES..."As promised, I have something special to show the fans tonite. Tonite, for a change, I will conduct this interview with MY guest." RUSSEL..."YOUR guest? Who are you interviewing?!" STYLES..."I an interviewing none other than Krissy Banger, herself!" RUSSEL..."What? I don't believe it. There is no way, Krissy would agree to be interviewed by you!" STYLES..."Believe what you will. But feast your eyes, ladies and gentleman, Krissy Banger!" Johnny Banger's music plays as a woman wearing a wig, dressed in drag and chewing bubble gum heads towards the interview area in a dizzy fashion. STYLES..."Hello, Krissy Banger." Styles nonchalantly takes the mic from Russel. RUSSEL..."That is not Krissy Ba..." STYLES..."Krissy, I have brought you here in order to show the fans of WLW just how intelligent you are. A lot of our fans like to know the political views of our stars. Tell me and the world, Krissy, who will you vote for in this year's upcoming Presidential election?" BIMBO...(with a very confused look) "vote?" STYLES..."OK, perhaps, politics is a bit much for you. Tell us about yourself. What are your goals for the future?" BIMBO..."Well, I want to cure world hunger..or be a model." Bimbo smiles as she starts to play with her gum. STYLES..."Indecisive. Let's try math. What's one plus one?" BIMBO..."Ooooh, I know that one! Four!" STYLES..."That's wrong, Krissy." BIMBO..."Well, I know four is the answer to one of those things." STYLES...(Pats the bimbo on the head) "Uhmm, yes it is. Tell you what, let's talk about something you should know about. Let's talk about wrestling. Who are you picking to win my match with your husband and cousin, Johnny-boy later?" BIMBO..."It'll be a 6th round knockout." STYLES..."That's Boxing, Krissy, this is wrestling." BIMBO..."Is that the one with the touchdowns?" STYLES..."Uhm no." BALDWIN..."This is unreal. Someone should put a stop to this." TOWNSAND..."This is a riot. It alone is worth the price of admission. Hey Baldwin, pass me a kleenex, would ya? He's got me laughing so hard, I'm crying like a chick!" STYLES..."So tell me, Krissy, something I know I've been wondering. Why does Johnny bring you to ringside. What function do you serve. What worth are you at ringside? What is it that her keeps you around for?" The bimbo thinks for a moment and then starts to bounce up and down. STYLES..."Now, I understand. Very impressive. Dosen't that impress you, Russel?" RUSSEL...(standing with his arms folded and annoyed) "This is disgusting." STYLES..."You just didn't get a good view. Krissy, bounce up and down again." (The Bimbo jumps up and down again) BIMBO..."Can I go now, Johnny's waiting for me." STYLES..."I guess we can't keep Johnny away from the body he paid so much money for. Yes Krissy, you're excused." He gives her another pat on the head. The bimbo begins to walk away from the podium tripping over each step. Styles then turns to Russel. RUSSEL..."Ha Ha, very funny. I guess your proud of yourself." (The bimbo comes walking back) STYLES..."That way, Krissy, that way. Yes, I'm proud of myself, Russel because.." (The bimbo is seen in the croud lost) "That way, Krissy, That way! Because, not only can I give a good interview but I'm the best technical wrestler in the sport and Johnny-boy I know you're watching and very shortly, you're going to find that out first hand that Rick Styles is the "Top Dog" of the WLW!!" Styles music starts to play and he walks off the podium. RUSSEL..."I..don't know what to say. Fans, I have to apologize to you for this. I don't know what to say." The cameras cut back to Baldwin and Townsand. Baldwin shakes his head while Townsand wipes the tears from his eyes. TOWNSAND..."Oh, that was beautiful!" BALDWIN..."That was disgusting! I think Styles just threw gasoline on the fire, cause when Banger get him in that ring..." TOWNSAND..."yeah, yeah! Don't we have a match to see, or are you gonna yap all night?!"
