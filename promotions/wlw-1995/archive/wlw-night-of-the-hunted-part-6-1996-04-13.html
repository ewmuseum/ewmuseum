WLW "Night of the Hunted" Part 6

13 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BALDWIN..."Alright fans, coming up, we've got the 2 out of 3 falls match! What about it, Johnny?" TOWNSAND..."Baldwin, these matches are probably the hardest to pick, but then again, I did pick Gigantor over the Rainbow Warrior, now didn't I?" BALDWIN..."Yes you did." TOWNSAND..."Thank you. I wanted to hear you admit to it. Agenar's got him!" TOWNSAND..."Well, there's the prediction. Let's get down to the ring!" Crumb climbs into the ring. CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen...before we get on with our next match, I would like to draw your attention to the Night of the Hunted banner that hangs just below the second deck. Would you at this time, give a warm Toronto welcome,...to the one...the only..."Big J" JOHNNY TOWNSAND!!" BALDWIN..."What the hell?!" TOWNSAND..."Ha ha! I knew you'd be jealous, Baldwin!" Townsand drops his headset and stands up on the desk. The announcers booth is open, and sits on the far right side of the Dome. Townsand stands up and flexs. There are mostly boos, but a few cheers can be heard. Townsand sits back down. TOWNSAND..."Did you hear that, Baldwin? They love me here! I saw a sign that said "Johnny for Prime Minister!" BALDWIN..."Isn't that the one you made yourself?" TOWNSAND..."Your so jealous Baldwin! Here's 2 bucks. go get yourself another hot dog! Ha ha!" CRUMB..."Thank you, Johnny. Alright, this next contest is a 2 OUT OF 3 FALLS MATCH! Introducing first, from Myst... weighing 315 lbs...here is AGENARRRR!!!" Boos echoe throughout the Dome as Agenar rides down the runway. He mouths off at the fans, then climbs into the ring. CRUMB..."And his opponent...making his way to the ring, along with his manager, Krissy Banger...from New York... weighing 302 lbs...here is SHOCKWAAVEEE!!!" Shockwave and Krissy receive a tremendous ovation as they enter the Dome. BALDWIN..."See, now that's the sound they make when ' they love someone!" TOWNSAND..."What are you talking about? They love me so much here, I might have to move!" BALDWIN..."Shockwave looks ready for this." TOWNSAND..."Yeah he does, but..." BALDWIN..."Hey, why is the cart stopping?" The ring cart stops half down the aisle. Shockwave and Krissy say something to the driver. BALDWIN..."What's going on?" TOWNSAND..."I don't know. Hey..wait a minute!" Shockwave's music stops and the crowd stands watching. Agenar stands on the ropes yelling at Shockwave. Shockwave yells at the driver to move the cart. He turns and says something to Krissy, and as he turns, the driver stands and belts him in the face! Shockwave loses his balance and falls out of the cart. Krissy screams. BALDWIN..."What the hell is he doing?" TOWNSAND..."Look at him, Baldwin! I know him from somewhere!" The driver climbs off the cart, grabs Shockwave and rams his head into the side of it! Shockwave falls to the floor, bleeding from the top of his head. The fans boo and throw things and the driver picks up Shockwave and holds him, then slams his head into the concrete with a tombstone pildriver! Shockwave lies on the floor. He doesn't move. BALDWIN..."Oh my god! What the hell?!" TOWNSAND..."That's the Grave Digga, Baldwin! I knew I recognized him!" BALDWIN..."He's not scheduled to be here! What's he doing?!" Digga picks up Shockwave and hits him with another "burial"! Secruity rushes down the aisle, followed by the Masters of Motion. The fans cheer when they see the youngsters. They all swarm around Digga. He pushes some of them off, but gives in and heads to the back. The fans boo and scream at him as he leaves. A stretcher is brought down for Shockwave. Krissy stands in tears as they load her man and roll him out of the SkyDome. Tasker and Sanders lead Krissy out of the Dome. TOWNSAND..."Ha ha! Thanks for stoppin' by, Shockwave!" BALDWIN..."Your disgusting! That man had no business being here!" TOWNSAND..."Well, he made some business of his own!" BALDWIN..."Tom, are you there?" The scene switches to the back, where Tom is in the middle of a crowd of people. Shockwave is being loaded into an ambulance. RUSSEL..."Guys, they're not telling me anything, except that Shockwave is hurt bad. They are rushing him to a near by hospital. That's all I know. Back to you!" The crowd stands shocked and silent. BALDWIN..."I can't believe what we've just witnessed. What was Grave Digga trying to prove?!" TOWNSAND..."He's making a statement, Baldwin! And I think he made it perfectly!" BALDWIN..."And what statement is that?" TOWNSAND..."It was a message to everyone! Grave Digga is in the WLW! And the message just rang loud and clear! Right Shockwave?! Ha ha ha!" BALDWIN..."Alright, Johnny! As every match goes by, we draw closer to the big one!" TOWNSAND..."That's right! My palms are sweatin' just thinkin' about it!" BALDWIN..."We're gonna take you now to some pre-recorded comments from the WLW Champion, Gigantor!" [name withheld] stands infront of the cage that contains the Champion. ROSE..."Samhain, tonite you go up against YOUR biggest nightmare. You meet a man who is bigger than you, stronger than you and, who is not afraid of you." GIGANTOR..."AAAAAARRRRRRGGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!" ROSE..."You pulled your ace card out of the deck and used it already, Samhain. You played your biggest hand and it backfired. All you managed to do was prove that Gigantor is not intimidated by your tricks. Later tonite, I'm going to let Gigantor out of this cage and all of his rage will be focused on you. So, Samhain, bring your fire, bring your smoke, bring your mystic pumpkin, bring it all. Gigantor will walk through it all and hold onto the WLW championship. Mark my words!!" GIGANTOR..."RRRRRRRRROOOOOOOOAAAAAARRRRRRR!!" Gigantor begins to shake the cage. Rose laughs as the camera fades. BALDWIN..."I wish someone would lock that guy in a cage!" TOWNSAND..."Shut up, Baldwin! That man manages two champions, and he deserves respect! As for the comments, I don't know what to say! You never know what Gigantor is going to do, and when it comes to Samhain, well, he's even harder to figure out!" BALDWIN..."It's all coming up in a little while!"
