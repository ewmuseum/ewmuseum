WLW "Night of the Hunted" Part 14

13 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bret releases the hold, scoops Dropkick and slams him hard to the mat. Dropkcik gets back up, but is brought back down with a spinning toe hold. Bret stands over his cousin, pointing down at him. The fans boo as loud as ever. he pulls him up and whips him to the ropes. He ducks for a back drop, and Dropkick boots him in the stomach. As BRet staggers, Drop takes him down with a belly-back suplex. Bret seems to fold in half as he lands on his neck. TOWNSAND..."Whoa!" BALDWIN..."Young landed right on his neck, Johnny! I... he's covering!!" Dropkick covers...1...2..kick out!! The fans boo. Dropkick pins him again...1...2...kick out!! Bret rolls to the center of the ring and gets to his feet. As he does, he's met with a superkick from Dropkick. He falls to the mat. With lightening speed, Dropkick races up the ropes, and leaps, nailing him with a moonsault. A moment later, he follow's it up with a bulldog! BALDIWN..."That was the heart breaker!" TOWNSAND..."I know that, you idiot!" Bret lies motionless on the mat. Dropkick covers...1...2...21/2..kick out!! The fans cheer thinking it was three, but the ref waves it off. Dropkick grabs Brets legs and slingshots him into the turnbuckle. Bret crashes into it and bounces off. Instead of covering, Dropkick pulls Bret to his feet and goes for another DDT. Bret blocks it and back drops him. Bret climbs up the ropes and leaps for a splash. As he comes down, Dropkick puts up his legs and Bret crashes ontop of them. He falls to the mat holding his stomach. TOWNSAND..."He may have broken some ribs, Baldwin!" Dropkick pulls him up and slaps on a sleeper. Bret grabs his head and drops, catching him with a jaw breaker. As Dropkcik staggers, Bret nails him with a spinning back kick. The fans cheer as Dropkick still doesn't go down. Bret measures him and levels him with a superkick. Dropkick falls to the mat holding his face. TOWNSAND..."Ha ha! Young just kicked the taste right outta his mouth!" Bret pulls him to his feet and slaps on an abdominal stretch. The fans boo as Bret renches it tight. TOWNSAND..."This is it! Hey, I just heard him give up!" BALDWIN..."You did not! Would you be a professional for once in your career!" TOWNSAND..."I'm sorry, Baldwin. Here, please accept this twinkie on my behalf!" BALDIWN..."Knock it off!" Dropkick renches and flips Bret over with a hiptoss. Dropkick misses with an elbow. Bret grabs him and drops him with a snap suplex! Bret hits the ropes and drives an elbow into Drpkicks chest. He covers...1..2..kick out!! Bret pulls him up, whips him to the ropes and nails him with a clothesline. Dropkick jumps back up, but Bret catches him with another superkick. Keeping his momentum, he hauls Dropkick to his feet and sinches a suplex. He renches, but Dropkick blocks it and nails one of his own. The fans go wild. BALDWIN..."What a match! Back and forth, and back and forth!" Dropkick picks up Bret and plants him with a gut wrench suplex. Dropkick pulls him back up and knocks him down with a clothesline. He covers..1..2..kick out!! Bret tries to get up, but Dropkick pulls him back down and covers again..1..2..kick out!! Both men lie winded on the mat. Both start to get up and in the blink of an eye, Dropkick smashes Bret down with another DDT! The Dome exlodes with cheers. Bret lies on the mat staring up at the lights. Dropkick, obviously tiring, hauls him up and, after a moments pause, raises Bret high and holds him in a vertical suplex. Flash blubs go off everywhere as he holds him, then finally brings him crashing back down to the mat. Dropkick slowly gets to his feet. TOWNSAND..."They're gettin' tired, Baldwin." BALDWIN..."After all this, it's no wonder." Dropkick hits the ropes and misses with a leg drop! Bret gets up and catches Dropkick in a flying lariat. Both men hit the mat. bret gets up, runs, hits the ropes and crashes into Dropkick with a shoulder block, sending him out to the floor. The fans chant for Dropkick to get back into the ring as the ref counts. Bret leans against the ropes taking a breather. BALDWIN..."I can't believe the action we're getting here tonight, Johnny." TOWNSAND..."Hey, Baldwin, when these guys get to be a part of the big ones, they all want to be the best. My hat's off to these two right here. There kicking the hell outta each other! That's what it's all about!" As the ref's count reaches 8, Dropkick rolls back into the ring and gets to his feet. He doesn't see Bret charge, and gets nailed with a football tackle, which almost sends him back outside. Bret grabs him by the hair and pulls him up, sinches a suplex, and brings him down hard. The fans chant and cheer hoping to inspire Dropkick. Bret picks up and goes for a slam, but Dropkick rakes his eyes. He tucks Bret and plants him with a powerbomb! He covers. BALDWIN..."Oh my god!" TOWNSAND..."Get up!!" The ref counts...1...2...21/2...kick out!! Dropkick grows frustrated and drops and elbow across Bret's nose. He hauls him back up and whips him to the ropes. As Bret flies at him, Dropkick swings and misses. Bret hits the opposite ropes, leaps and nails Drop with a bulldog. The crowd leaps to it's feet as Dropkick smashes to the mat. Bret covers...1...2...kick out!! TOWNSAND..."This is unbelievable, Baldwin!" BALDWIN..."Johnny, Young's going up!" Bret perches himself on the ropes and leaps, nailing a moonsault. He covers again...1..2...kick out!! TOWNSAND..."What the hell is that ref doing! George Burns could have counted faster than that, and he'd dead!!" BALDWIN..."Hey, would you stop!" Bret jumps to his feet and smacks Dropkick, across the face. A hush goes over the crowd. Dropkick gets to his feet. Bret slaps him again! Dropkick just stares at Bret. Bret laughs and looks out to the crowd, then swings around for another slap. He misses, and Dropkick spins him around and brings him down with a back slide! The fans jump to their feet...1...2..kick out!!! Both men leap to their feet. Bret lurches, grabs Dropkicks head and drops, nailing him with the Dreamer! The fans scream as Dropkick collapses. TOWNSAND..."He hit it!" Bret falls across Dropkick. The ref counts...1..2..3!!! Bret rolls off of Dropkick and out of the ring. The cart is just coming down, but Bret walks up the aisle with his hands in the air. The fans boo. CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen...the winner of the match 'The Fantasy" BRET YOOUUNNG!!!" TOWNSAND..."He did it, Baldwin, don't you love it!" Dropkick sits up shaking his head. The fans cheer him as he climbs out of the ring and leaves the SkyDome. BALDWIN..."I though Dropkick had him. Young pulled that Dreamer out of no where." TOWNSAND..."Ha ha, I love it! Young humiliates that chump one more time! Well, there's another party! You know, by the time this night is over, I'm gonna be the most popular guy in Toronto! Tell Ventura I might not be at work tomorrow!" BALDWIN..."Well, on that note, let's take a look at this!" Commercial... "If you missed the Valentine's Day Massacre, you missed the big War Games match! You missed the Heavyweight Championship Survival match! You missed the My Bloody Valentine's match! You missed 5 big title matches as well as a steel cage "I quit" match. However, now The Valentine's Day Massacre can be seen on home video. If you missed all the pulse-pounding action, order it today!!!!!" BALDWIN..."I've got my copy, Johnny!" TOWNSAND..."Baldwin, don't be a shmuck!"
