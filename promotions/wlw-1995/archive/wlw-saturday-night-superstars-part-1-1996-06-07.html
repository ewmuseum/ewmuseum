WLW SATURDAY NIGHT SUPERSTARS! PART 1

7 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD LEAGUE WRESTLING "Where the World Comes to Play!" \ / | \ / \/\/ |_ _ \/\/ __ __ /_ /\ / /_ __/ A T U R D A Y / \/ I G H T __/ U P E R S T A R S The introduction graphics explode into a live shot of the Rose Garden in Portland, Oregon. Cameras pan over the excited fans as they scream and wave. The Superstars banners hang from the rafters, and the giant SNS sits at the end of the runway. Fireworks explode high over the ring, whipping the crowd into an energetic frenzy. The cameras pan across the fans, stopping on one holding a sign which reads.. ""Mr. Personality" Big J!!". The scene switches to Mike Baldwin and Johnny Townsand on the broadcast stage. BALDWIN..."Good evening everyone, and welcome to Portland! It's Saturday Night, and that can only mean one thing! It'.." TOWNSAND..."It's time for you to shut up! We're just a week away from the match, Baldwin!" BALDWIN..."Are you referring to the Seven Sins Match?" TOWNSAND..."Of course I am, you idiot! I still don't understand it! I'm tellin' you Baldwin, by the time it's over, there's gonna be one less man in the WLW!" BALDWIN..."I'll agree with you on that one, Johnny, but let's worry about tonight! We've got another action packed card! Spartan's here tonight, Johnny!" TOWNSAND..."Big deal! When Moo Kame gets his hands on him, it's gonna get ugly!" BALDWIN..."The Fantasy Bret Young will be meeting newcomer Slick Ric!" TOWNSAND..."That's right, and we've got Discipline and Phantom Night! I'm waiting for that one!" BALDWIN..."Also fans, we've got one half of Wave of Destruction, Tidalwave, taking on another newcomer here, Jimbo the Terrible! And in our main event, Mayhem Inc. will go head to head with Reckless Abandon! Come on Johnny, what do you say we get this going?" TOWNSAND..."Let's just get it over with! I'm leaving for Vancouver right after the show!" BALDWIN..."Fans, let's get down to the ring!" WLW Ring Announcer Bernie Crumb stands in the ring as the fans hoot and holler. CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen...welcome to the Rose Garden... and welcome to SATUUURDAY NIGHT SUUUPERSTARRRRSSS!!! This is our opening contest, and it is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, making his way to the ring along with his manager "Money Bags" Mike Watson...from Painsville NY...weighing 265 lbs...here is JIMBO THE TERRRRRIBLLLEE!!!" The fans boo as the SNS separates and the two enter the arena. Jimbo's face is covered by the black spiked hockey mask that he wears. Fans swing at him as he storms by. Watson holds open the ropes, and Jimbo enters to a tremdous chorus of boos. CRUMB..."And his opponent...escorted to the ring by his manager Poseidon...from South Bend, Ind....weighing 323 lbs... here is TIDALLLLWAVVVEEE!!!" Again the fans boo, this time for Tidalwave and Poseidon. The two ignore the rowdy fans and quickly make their way down the runway and into the ring. BALDWIN..."Listen to these fans, Johnny! They're hot tonight!" TOWNSAND..."They should be hot! They're watching the greatest wrestling action going! Either that, or they're all high!" BALDWIN..."They're what?!" TOWNSAND..."Well how the hell do you think they got the name "Portland PotHeads?!" BALDWIN..."Your making that up!" Jimbo and Tidalwave pace in the ring as the time keeper rings the bell. JImbo leaps at Tidal, nailing him in the throat with an elbow. The big man staggers back. Jimbo grabs him and sends him to the ropes, leaping for a dropkick. Tidal grabs the ropes and stops himself as Jimbo crashes to the mat. Tidal runs at him and slams an elbow down across his chest. Tidal pulls him up and sinches a suplex, then drops him to the mat. He covers and only gets a one count. Watson bangs on the mat, screaming instructions to his man. Jimbo rolls around on the mat, holding his injured back. Tidal measures him and delivers a big boot right to the small of his back. Poseidon applauds. Tidal yanks Jimbo to his feet and whips him to the ropes, flooring him with a vicious clothesline! The fans scream as Jimbo does a 360 in the air. BALDWIN..."My god, what a shot!" TOWNSAND..."He almost took his head off with that one! You know Baldiwn, we're seeing a different Tidalwave than when he's with Soundwave." BALDWIN..."Do you think we've seen the last of Wave of Destruction?" TOWNSAND..."See, there you go! I make one little comment, and you turn this into Entertainment Tonight! No comment, Baldwin!" Tidal pulls Jimbo to his feet, but is stopped with a thumb to the eyes. As Tidal staggers, Jimbo grabs him and plant him with a belly to belly suplex. tidal slams against the mat. Jimbo covers...1..kick out!! Jimbo scoops him up and plants him on his shoulder. Tidal wiggles to get free, but Jimbo runs and powerslams him to the mat. Tidal gasps for breath as Jimbo draps across him..1..2..kick out!! Jimbo jumps to his feet and screams at the ref about the slow count. Tidal rolls to the ropes and Poseidon rushes over, slipping something to his man. BALDWIN..."Hey, what are they doing?! Poseidon just slipped Tidalwave something!" TOWNSAND..."What are you talking about?! I didn't see anything!" Jimbo turns his attention back to tidal. He pulls him up and sinches a belly to back suplex. He renches him up, and Tidal swings, smashing his loaded fist against the brigde of Jimbos nose. Both men fall to the mat, Tidal lying across the unconscious Jimbo. the fans leap to their feet as the ref drops and counts...1..2.. Tidal fires the object out of the ring. ...3!!! Tidal rolls off of Jimbo and out of the ring. He and Posiedon walk smuggly up the aisle, as Watson screams at the ref, holding the object that Tidal had used on his man. CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen...the winner of the match.. TIIDALLLWAVVVEEE!!!" TOWNSAND..."Well, Tidalwave wins again! You know, he must be getting close to some singles titles, Baldwin!" BALDWIN..."Johnny, he just cheated! Don't you have anything to say about that?!" TOWNSAND..."Yeah, it's what I'da done!" BALDWIN...Alright fans, we were supposed to have a live interview for you with the WLW Tag TEam Champions, the Masters of MOtion, but unfortuneately, they're on the road and couldn't be here. Howve..." TOWNSAND..."They're not here?! Ha ha! You know why they're not here, Baldwin? Cause Mayhem Inc. are in the building, and the Masters don't want to get within 500 feet of them! That's why they're not here! They're scared! Ha Ha!" BALDWIN..."They are not! You stop that! Alright, now as I was saying, we do have some pre-recorded comments. Let's hear from them now."
