WLW "Night of the Hunted" Part 3

13 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BADLWIN..."Alright, coming up, we've got the first of the blockbusters! It's the Texas Death Match Johnny!" TOWNSAND..."I'm ready for this one, Baldwin! I'm so sick of hearing the Spartan whine about everything! Alex Extreme is going shut this guy up for good!" BALDWIN..."Fans, let's get down to the ring!" Crumb climbs into the ring as a group of female fans scream out his name. He poits down at them and gives them a wink. CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen...this next contest is a TEXAS DEATH MATCH! The rules are simple... after a pin..a man has a 10 count to get to his feet. If he doesn't, he loses. Introducing first... from Los Angeles...weighing 252 lbs...here is SPARRRTANN!!!" The fans erupt as Spartan enters the Dome and makes his way to the ring. BALDWIN..."There he is, Johnny!" TOWNSAND..."Big deal! Let's go to some pre-recorded comments from a real man!" Fade up to a Black stretch Ferrari Limosine pulls up to Skydome. Flash bulbs are poping off everywhere. Out steps three gorgeous ladies in formal attire followed by Alex Extreme in an Armani suit and Ray Bans...Extreme looks straight into the camera. ALEX..."Spartan, you worthless busted condom!!! You don't stand a chance tonight. I've spent seven of the last ten days on the southside of Chicago getting into all sorts of Chicago Street Fights!!! Brother, Mr.Excitement gonna style and profile all over your ass tonight!!! So Skydome, PPV audience get ready for Extreme Excitement cause Alex Extreme is gonna bust a move and a condom!!!!" Extreme walks off into the dome...fade to black. TOWNSAND..."ha ha ha! Oh, god Baldwin, hand some of them napkins of yours! That guy cracks me up!" BALDWIN..."He sounds pretty confident, Johnny!" TOWNSAND..."That's 'extremely confident'! And why shouldn't he be?!" BALDWIN..."I give up! Fans, let's get down to the ring!" CRUMB..."And his opponent...from Chicago...weighing 240 lbs...here is "Mr. Excitment" ALEX EXTREEMMMEE!!!" A chorus of boos greets Alex as his cart enters the Dome. It slowly travels down the runway as Alex looks at the fans he passes. He holds up his hand to the driver and orders him to stop. BALDWIN..."What's he doing?" Slex climbs out of the cart and walks over to a lovely young lady. He says something to hear and she nods. She wraps her arms around his neck and he lifts her over the guard rail. They climb into the cart and continue down the runway. Alex hands her won of his blach armani jackets, and as she puts it on, he lays her out with a passionate kiss. BALDWIN..."Oh, come on!" TOWNSAND..."Ha ha! Now thats class!" The cart reaches the ring and the two climb out. the girl stands in his corner, and Alex makes his way around the ring handing roses to the young ladies. Spartan is growing impatient. He jumps out of the ring and nails Alex with a shot to the head. Roses go everywhere. Spartan rolls Alex into the ring as the bell rings. Alex gets up and grabs Spartan as he climbs into the ring. He picks him up and hits him with a backbreaker. Spartan falls to the mat holding his back. Alex whips him to the ropes and nails him with a neck scissors, bringing him down to the mat. He doesn't let up, pulling Spartan back up and leveling him with a reverse neck breaker! Spartan goes down hard. TOWNSNAD..."What a move!" Alex grabs Spartan's hair and drags him to the corner. He holds him as he climbs the ropes. The fans scream as Alex jumps onto Spartan and brings him crashing down with a Frankensteiner! Alex gets up, hooks Spartan and drops him with a double underhood backbreaker! He drops and covers...1..2..kick out!! TOWNSAND..."What a start by Alex! Come on, Baldwin, you've gotta be impressed!" BALDWIN..."Spartan isn't out of this yet, Johnny!" As Alex rolls off, Spartan grabs him by the throat. He gets to his feet, still choking him, then nails him with a vicious uppercut. Alex staggers. Spartan grabs him and goes for a pile driver. The fans scream as alex back drops him, and then plants an elbow. Alex pulls Spartan to his feet and nails him with a standing dropkick. Spartan falls against the ropes but doesnt go down. Alex grabs him and levels him with a short clothesline. Spartan goes down hard. Alex winks at the lady in the corner, then pulls Spartan to his feet. He sinches his head and drops him with the DDT! The fans scream. TOWNSAND..."He nailed it!" Alex covers...1..2..3!! BALDWIN..."Oh my god!" The fans boo as Alex moves to the corner and takes a breather. The ref counts Spartan..1..2..3..4..5..6..7..8..Spartan gets up and the fans go wild. Alex charges out of the corner and nails Spartan with a flying dropkick. Spartan crashes into the turnbuckle and bounces out, falling in the center of the ring. Alex picks him up and plants him with a back breaker. Alex backs off allowing Spartan to get to his feet. As he does, Alex charges. Spartan drops to the mat sending Alex flying over top, nailing the referee with a shoulder block! The fans jump to their feet as the ref crashes hard to the mat. He doesn't move. Alex grabs Spartan and whips him over the top rope. BALDWIN..."The ref's out, Johnny!" TOWNSAND..."Yep! The perfect time to go to the floor!" Alex leaps over the top rope and lands beside Spartan. He picks him up and runs with him, whipping him into the time keepers table! BALDWIN..."Oh god!" The equipment flies off of the table as Spartan crashes on to it. The staff scrambles out of the way as Alex comes after Spartan. He rams his head against the table. He grabs somebodies can of coke and smashes it over his head. spartan is hurt bad. alex positions him on the table and climbs into the ring. The fans scream as Alex climbs the ropes. Flash bulbs flood the Dome as he leaps, smashing down on to Spartan! The table shatters and both fall to the floor. TOWNSAND..."What a high risk manouver!" BALDWIN..."Spartan's out of it, Johnny!" Alex picks up Spartan and rolls him into the ring as the fans boo and chant at him. He rolls Spartan to the center of the ring and covers him. The ref is still down. Alex pounds the mat and gets off. He crawls over to the ref and shakes him awake. TOWNSAND..."Oh, come on! He's won it! I've already counted to 20!" BALDWIN..."I didn't realize you could count that high!" TOWNSAND..."i just used all of your hot dog rappers to help me!" The ref crawls over as Alex covers again. He counts..1..2..3!!! TOWNSAND..."Ha! That's 2!" Alex rolls to the corner as the fans boo him. The ref starts to the count on Spartan..1..2..3..4..5..6..7..8..9..Spartan gets up!! The fans erupt. Alex is irrate and runs at the ref screaming at him and pointing his finger. Spartan crawls up from behind and rolls him up! The ref counts..1..2..kick out!! Alex is irrate and flies at Spartan, but is armdraged to the mat. Spartan leaps and slams a knee into Alex's chest. The fans are going wild. Spartan picks him up, and drops him with a snap suplex. TOWNSAND..."Spartan has just exploded here!" Spartan pulls Alex to his feet and nails him with a doulbe underhood pile driver! Alex's head bounces off the mat. Spartan keeps on him. He hauls him up, whips him to the ropes and hammers a knee to Alexs mid section. Alex does a 360 in the air and crashes to the mat. Spartan hits the ropes and drops a leg across Alex's chest. The Dome is rocking. Spartan pulls him up and nails him with a reverse neck breaker of his own. Alex goes down hard and the fans love it. Spartan picks him up and gets thumbed in the eye. He grabs spartan and whips him to the ropes. Spartan reverses it back drops Alex to the mat. Spartan stands over Alex and looks out to the crowd. Their cheers seem to fire him up. He points to the turnbuckle and they go wild. He scoops up Alex and slams him hard to the mat. He goes to the top. BALDWIN..."This is it! If he nails it, it's over!" Spartan perches himself on the ropes and leaps, crashing down onto Alex with a moonsault! He covers as the stadium explodes with cheers. The ref counts..1..2..3!!! Spartan rolls off. The ref starts the count, but Spartan shoves him off, picks up Alex and whips him to the turnbuckle. Alex smashes chest first and bounces out. spartan races up thd ropes and leaps again, nailing another moonsault! He covers again...1..2..3!!! THe fans are going wild. Spartan rolls off and lets the ref count..1..2..3..4..5..6..7..8... Alex starts to stir. ..9.. He rolls to his elbow and gets a hand on the ropes. He starts to pull himself up. ...10!! The fans explode as Alex falls to the mat exhausted. BALDWIN..."He's done it! Spartan's won it!" TOWNSAND..."Thanks to that idiot referee! Alex had him beat ages ago!" Spartan celebrates in the ring. Alex gets to his feet and leans in the corner. He waves his hand in the air as if he's calling some one. BALDWIN..."What's he doing?" TOWNSAND..."He's hot! He's getting air!" CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen...the winner of the match...SPARRRTAANNN!!!" The fans are rocking as Spartan's music pounds over the speakers. Alex continues to wave. Fans near the entrance jump to their feet as someone comes tearing down the aisle. The man reaches the ring, and slide umder, tackleing Spartan from behind. The fans go silent as the man hammers away with boots to the back of Spartans head. BALDIWN..."Who the hell is that?!" TOWNSAND..."Baldwin...that's...thats Moo Kame!" Kame is dressed in his street clothes. He pulls Spartan to his feet and chops him down. Alex joins in, kicking away at Spartan's head. The fans are booing. Kame picks him up and nails him with the head-take-kick! Spartan crashes to the mat. Alex hits the rops and plant an elbow onto his neck. Officials are running down the aisle. Alex picks up Spartan, sinches him and drops him with the DDT! Spartan is hurt bad. Kame grabs Alex;s arm and thrusts it into the air. The fans boo. The two exit as the officials get there, leaving Spartan lying on the mat. They exit the SkyDome to a tremendous chorus off boos. BALDWIN..."That was disgusting!" TOWNSAND..."Hey, Spartan may have won the battle, but Alex took a big step in winning the war! As for Kame, he's always hated Spartan! What better place to kick the hell outta someone you hate, than infront of the entire world! You know, Baldwin, I'm just havin' too much fun here tonight!" BALDWIN..."Your sickening!"
