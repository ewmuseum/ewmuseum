WLW "Night of the Hunted" Part 1

Apr 13, 1996, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"5 seconds till world feed...4...3...2...1...GO!!!" Blank screen --------------------------------------- WARNING!!! The following program contains violence of a graphic nature. Viewer discretion is advised! Any unauthorized exhibition of this broadcast, without the written consent of World League Wrestling, is prohibited! ----------------------------------------- Screen goes blank. WLW Logo graphics.. "From the deserts of Africa, to the jungles of South America.... WORLD LEAGUE WRESTLING "Where the World Comes to Play!" A black screen opens on a full moon. The sound of night creatures echoes in the back ground. The sound of heavy breathing and footsteps grows louder and louder. "For centuries...people have been terrorized by the stories of the legendary Giants who once roamed the earth...destroying everything and everyone who stood in their way..... But there were also stories of the Demons of hell...who would rise...seeking to punish and murder the land of the living... Tonight...these two myths collide.... There's nowhere to run... There's nowhere to hide... IT'S THE NIGHT OF THE HUNTED!!!!!!!!!!!!" The image of the moon is replaced with an imitation full moon that hangs from the roof of the SkyDome. The camera pans down and show the stadium, and the thousands apon thousands of fans surrounding the tiny ring which sits in the middle. Graphics appear over top... __________________________________________ _ | | \ / |_ | | | |__ | \/ |_ | | | . . . \ / | \ / \/\/ |_ _ \/\/ |\ | ___________________ |_| _________ | \| I G H T O F T H E | | U N T E D S K Y D O M E Toronto, Ontario, Canada! __________________________________________ The cameras pan across the Dome which has been done up to look like a jungle. Fireworks explode high in the rafters of the stadium, enough to make the fourth of July look pathetic. On various parts on the floor, thick patches of trees have been set up, all equipped with glowing eyes. The long runway which stretches from the ring to the far left corner of the stadium, is lined with bushes, and covered with dirt and weeds. Leaves and branches hang off the facings of each level. A full moon is hanging high above the ring. The "Night of the Hunted" banners hang over the ring, and the graphics appear on the JumboTron video screen. The cameras pan over the excited fans, some dressed as the favourite superstar, some sporting home made championship belts, Canadian flags, and a few others hold plastic jack-o-lanterns. The cameras continue to pan, stopping on WLW President, Randy Ventura, who stands in the ring. VENTURA..."Good evening, Toronto!!" The crowd cheers. VENTURA..."Good evening, World!! Welcome to the magnificent SkyDome!! And welcome to the NIGHT OF THE HUNTED!!!!" The fans continue to scream and cheer as the cameras pan across them. Air horns echoe in the back ground. VENTURA..."Would you please join me at this time, in welcoming Maverick recording artist...and Canada's own....Miss ALANIS MORRIESETTE!!!" The fans cheer and chant for her as she climbs into the ring. She is dressed from head to toe in black leather. ALANIS..."Thank you....(she sings the anthems)" The fans sing along then cheer and wave to her as she leaves. The cameras move room the ring and pan up to a "Night of the Hunted" banner which hangs off of the second level of the Dome. Behind it, in the broadcast booth, Mike Baldwin stands decked out in a tuxedo. BALDWIN..."Welcome everyone to Toronto, Ontario! And welcome to the NIGHT OF THE HUNTED!! I'm your host Mike Baldwin...and with me as always, is "Big J" Johnny Townsand! Get in here, Johnny!" Townsand struts into the booth dressed in a camoflauge tuxedo. BALDWIN..."Johnny, what the hell are you wearing?" TOWNSAND..."It's Hunting Season, Baldiwn! And I'm makin' sure that no one finds me!" BALDWIN..."Look at this place, Johnny! Can you believe all of these people?!" TOWNSAND..."Baldwin, I was here when Carter hit that homerun over the right field wall! I was here a few weeks ago when the Raptors upset the Bulls! And if you combine those two nights, the volume of them doesn't equal this place right now! I'm fired up!" BALDWIN..."You should be fired up! We've got the biggest card in WLW history about to take place! We've got some of the greatest matches ever signed! Every title will be on the line tonight, Johnny!" TOWNSAND..."That's right! We've got the main event that the entire world is talking about! Gigantor and Samhain! You know, I don't think the roof of this place is strong enough, because when they get out here, they're liable to tear it right off!" BALDWIN..."We've got the scaffold match for the tag team titles!" TOWNSAND..."4 of the biggest tag teams in wrestling, tearing each other apart for the gold!" BALDWIN..."We'll have the crowning of the first ever WLW North American Champion!" TOWNSAND..."That's the one I'm waitin' for! My guy, Rick Styles is gonna do a tap dance on Bangers head! Then he's walkin' out the new champion!" BALDWIN..."We've also got the..." TOWNSAND..."Baldwin, would you stop yappin'! We got work to do! Let's go HUNTING!!!" BALDWIN..."Alright fans, let's get down to the ring to our ring announcer, Bernie Crumb!" Crumb stands in the ring dressed in a tuxedo. The fans are going wild around him waving their banners and signs. CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen...this is the opeining contest of the NIGHT OF THE HUUUNNNTED!!" It is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, along with his manager Slick Rick...from parts unknown...weighing 330 lbs...here is RAEEKWOOONNN!!!" The spotlights flash to the entrance at the end of the long runway. A giant WLW banner hangs over head. A cart enters the arean which has been done up to look like a hunting basket that sits on the back of elephants. In the cart, Raekwon and Rick standing gesturing to the unwelcoming crowd. TOWNSAND..."Hey, check out that cart, Baldwin!" BALDWIN..."That's like an old fashioned hunting basket." TOWNSAND..."Actually, I thought it looked more like that Gremlin you drive!" BALDWIN..."I don't...oh never mind! We're underway, JOhnny! So let's go to some pre-recorded comments from the two combatants!" RAEKWON..."Hayes, me and you tonight! I'll show you what remorse is all about when I make you eat your own foot after I jam it down your throat! You are very brave boy sigining on to meet me, The Master of Destruction. Now you will see what a great mistake you've made after I'm done with you punk!" BEN HAYES..."Raekwon, well buddy boy, I'm not sure what I'm gonna do to you in the ring! But I'll tell you one thing, it won't be pretty for you. Nope. I'm gonna mess you up bad. Only a face a mother could love. Raekwon will be Raekloser." RIPBURGER..."WLW, I brought in Ben Hayes, and I know he can fight. He's won he first match a beat the snot out of Lord something and he left the WLW. Now, I'll make sure my prize fighter will be there. I have no control of him when he's in the ring though. Raekwon, you're in over your head." HAYES..."This is great. I finally get to fight again! I've been waiting. I'll wrestle for food. Heck, I fight for the sake of fighting. LET ME AT HIM!" (He goes nuts and punches a hole thru a door) RIPBURGER..."WLW, get ready, pretty boys, because it won't be pretty for you. And soon my new finding will be here to fight in the WLW. You'll find out soon enough who he is. Ben is out of control and can't be stopped!" Raekwon paces around the ring as his cart heads back up the aisle. CRUMB..."And his opponent...making his way to the ring along with his manager Corley Ripburger...from Miami... weighing 272 lbs...here is "No Remorse" BEN HAAAYYYES!!!" The fans cheer as Hayes' music pounds over the SkyDome speakers. Their cart makes it's way down to the ring. BALDWIN..."Well, here we go, Johnny! The waiting is over!" TOWNSAND..."That's right Baldwin! Listen to these fans, they've been waiting for this for months!" The cart reaches the ring and Hayes climbs into the ring. Raekwon charges as Hayes gets into the ring, smashing away at him with forearms. Crumb jumps out of the ring as the time keeper rings the bell. TOWNSAND..."Whoa! Here we go!!" Raekwon whips Hayes to the ropes, swings and misses a clothesline. Hayes hits the opposite ropes and nails the big man with a dropkick. Raekwon staggers back but doesn't go down. Hayes hits the ropes again and smashes him with a clothesline. Raekwon remains on his feet. Hayes flies to the ropes, leaps and nails Raekwon with a huge dropkick. Raekwon falls back and gets tied in the ropes! The fans go wild as Hayes pounds away at the vulnerable big man. The ref tries to stop it, but Hayes pounds away. Rick fights with the ropes and finally untangles his man. Hayes charges and gets caught as Raekwon back drops him over the top rope! He crashes hard ontop of the ring platform. TOWNSAND..."Whoa! Raekwon almost put him in the 10th row!" Raekwon climbs out of the ring. He stalks Hayes from behind, grabbing his head and ramming it into the apron! Hayes bounces off and falls to the mat.
