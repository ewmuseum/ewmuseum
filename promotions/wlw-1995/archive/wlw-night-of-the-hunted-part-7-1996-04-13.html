WLW "Night of the Hunted" Part 7

13 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The WLW ring crew makes their way down the aisle. Two trucks drive slowly down the runway carrying the pieces of the scaffold. The construction begins and the fans look on anxsiously. BALDWIN..."We're almost ready for it, Johnny!" TOWNSAND..."That's right, Baldwin, the biggest tag team title match in the history of pro wrestling! 4 teams! Scaffolds, ladders, blood, guts..." BALDWIN..."Okay, okay! Fans, let's go back to Tom Russel, Tom?" Russel stands in the interview area. RUSSEL..."Alright everybody, this is one of the matches that has brought everyone here tonight! Bryan Sanders and Chris Tasker, come on in here!" The Masters of Motion and Sunburst walk onto the set. RUSSEL..."Alright gentlemen, you are about to go to the ring to try and capture gold in the scaffold match. Guys, quickly, you are the crowd's favorite to win the gold, how do you think that will help you?" TASKER..."Since at least most of the crowd are behind us, it is going to give us the extra edge to be victorious." RUSSEL..."There's gonna be alot of managers out there, guys! Thing they'll have an influence in the outcome of the match, and if so, does this hurt your chances?" SANDERS..."I can see some of the other managers trying to stick their noses in, but they have to realize, it isn't one man who they are trying to screw out of a win, it isn't even two. It is six other guys who want to win just as much as the other two who he is looking after. So if one of them don't pop the manager, the other five will. So no, I can't see this giving us any more a disadvantage than anyone else." RUSSEL..."If you do become the WLW tag champions, who gets the first shot at the gold?" TASKER..."When we win, we'll defend these belts each and every time the WLW asks us to. We won't be any closet champions, we're gonna be the fightingest champs ever!" The Masters exit. RUSSEL..."Alright, there you have it! A young team determined to leave the SkyDome tonight with the gold! Back to you guys!" The crew contines to work on the structure. The fans are getting antsy. BALDWIN..."Well, what do you think, Johnny? TOWNSAND..."They don't have a snowballs chance in hell, or in your case, a donuts!" BALDWIN..."What do you mean?" TOWNSAND..."They will be surrounded by big, powerful men in there, Baldwin! Men who are looking to destroy the young pretty boys!" BALDWIN..."No way, Townsand! They've got the speed and agility that can take them up those ladders faster than anyone! Alright, earlier today, we got some pre recorded comments from Wave of Destruction. Let's take a look." TIDALWAVE..."Total Destruction, eh?.... what a joke... Soundwave and I don't even know who you two are... don't even know your names... but that don't matter... we're still gonna kick your asses... it seems to us that the WLW just placed some rookie team in this 4 corner match because they are short on tag-teams... well, when we're done tonight, they are gonna be short one more team... cuz you pansies aren't gonna make it to the night of the hunted....." SOUNDWAVE..."You heard the big man!! WOD is back, and we want our damn belts again! That's all I have to say, cuz the Tower of Power, Tidalwave, has said all there is to be heard! Wooooo!!" POSEIDON..."My men are fired up, as you can hear, and you, Total Destruction, are going to feel just what destruction is. Be forewarned, there will be a surprise!! Muahahahahuahahahhaha.... Shall the seas drive you to land!" TOWNSAND..."ha ha, I like them guys, Baldwin!" BALDWIN..."Well, what do you think?" TOWNSAND..."They're big and bad, but I don't see them winnin' it either!" BALDWIN..."Then who is your pick?" TOWNSAND..."Ha ha, the guys standing by with that little toad, Russel!" Russel stands awaiting Mayhem Inc. RUSSEL..."Mayhem Inc, come on in her..." LEGEND..."Get the fuck out of our way!" Legend and Mayhem push Russel out of the way and walk past. RUSSEL..."Hey, watch it! We're on the air you know! Watch the language! Back to you guys! Jesus!" BALDWIN..."Well, they look fired up!" TOWNSAND..."Ha ha ha! Oh, Russel always looks like a ping pong ball when they're around! They've got it Baldwin! They are the biggest and the baddest! We're talking about a team where one half won the biggest rumble in WLW history! And in this match they're together! They can't lose!" BALDWIN..."It looks like we're ready, Johnny! Let's get down to the ring!" Crumb stands in the ring surrounded by the massive structure. From each corner of the ring, a ladder leads up to the scaffold that leads from corner to corner. It stands fifteen feet high. The fans are going wild as spotlights swirl over the ring. CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen...this next contest is the 4 CORNER SCAFFOLD MATCH...and it will determine the new WLW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS!!! Introducing first, team 1. From California along with their manager, Sunburst...with a combined weight of 479 lbs...here are the MASTERS OF MOOOTIOOONN!!!" The fans go wild as the Master music pounds over the speakers. They ride their way to the ring waving at their many fans. CRUMB..."And team 2...along with their manager, Poseidon...from Indiana and California respectively... with a combined weight of 640 lbs...here are WAVE OF DESTRUCTIOOONNN!!!" Boos echoe througout the Dome as the team rolls down the runway. THe Masters are in the ring stretching on the ropes. CRUMB..."And team 3...making their way to the ring along with their manager The Dark One...from the Theatre of Pain...with a combined weight of 770 lbs... here are TOTAL DESTRUCTIOOONNN!!! More boos as the team heads to the ring. WOD look on from the ring pointing and laughing as Totol aproaches. BALDWIN..."look at the size of these men!" TOWNSAND..."And they keep gettin' bigger, Baldwin!" CRUMB..."And finally...along with their manager, Legend ...from parts unknown...with a combined weight of 701 lbs...here are MAAYYHEM INC.!!!" Again, the crowd boos as Mayhem make their way to the ring. TOWNSAND..."We've got 2590 lbs. in that ring, Baldwin! It's like you and your mom!" BALDWIN..."Would you stop! Look at these men, Johnny! They all want it!" Mayhem climbs into the ring. CRUMB..."And....would you please welcome...the star of too many movies to name...the lovely and talented... Miss DREW BARRYMORE!!!" The fans turn and look up the runway as Drew makes her way down to the ring. She holds a tray lined with red velvet, on which sit the new Tag Team Title belts. BALDWIN..."How about that, Johnny! Drew Barrymore is here!" TOWNSAND..."Yeah, well see was knockin' on my door too! Looks like she did Ventura a little favour!" BALDWIN..."What?! Oh, never mind! Check out the belts!" TOWNSAND..."Hey, you think they look good now, wait till Mayhem straps them on!" Drew climbs into the ring and parades the belts around, showing everyman. Referee James Beard takes the belts off of the tray and holds them up for the audience to see. The all cheer. Beard holds both belts under his arm and makes his way up one of the ladders. He cautiously walks across the scaffold and places them at the intersection. TOWNSAND..."Jump!" The ref gets back down to the ring. Drew and Crumb exit and sit at the time keeper table. The time keeper rings the bell. All four teams charge at each other! WoD tears into Total. They pound each other with lefts and rights. Masters dive onto Mayhem and grab them, pounding them in the head. TOWNSAND..."Here we go!" Soundwave picks up Conquer and slams him to the mat. He follows up with a quick elbow. Vanquish nails Tidalwave with an uppercut, staggering the big man. He leaps and clotheslines him, sending him to the mat. Mayhem 2 gets hold of Tasker, picks him up and runs, slamming him against the ladder. The structure shakes. Mayhem 1 wraps both hands around Sanders neck hoists him into the air and chokes away. Mayhem 2 stomps away on Taskers back. The fans boo. TOWNSAND..."See what I mean! Masters just aren't big enough!" The fans leap to their feet as #2 starts to climb one of the ladders! The frame shakes as he climbs. Tasker crawls throught the ropes and heads up behind him. He grabs Mayhem by the head and rams it into ladder! #2 loses his balance and falls to the floor. Tasker jumps down ontop of him, planting an elbow. Vanquish grabs Tidals head and rams it into the ladder! The big man snaps back with blood trickling from a cut. Vanquish dives on him and bites the wound. When he pulls away, blood is pouring out of it. TOWNSAND..."i guess he's hungry! Sorta reminds me of you at a barbecue! Hqa ha ha!" Soundwave picks up Conquer and drops him, catchin his neck on the top rope. Sound stands over him and drives a foot into his throat. Mayhem 1 sees the battle outside, and drops Sanders. He climbs through the rpes and jumps catching Tasker in the back with a double axhandle. mayhem Inc hammer away on Tasker, driving fists and boots onto his back.
