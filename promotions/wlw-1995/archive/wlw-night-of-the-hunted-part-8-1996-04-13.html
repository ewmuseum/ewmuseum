WLW "Night of the Hunted" Part 8

13 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mayhem 2 picks up Tasker and rams him into the guard rail. Close by fans reach out trying to smack Mayhem, but they miss. Sanders gets to his feet in the ring, climbs the ropes and leaps, nailing mayhem1 with a clothesline. Both men crash to the stadium floor. Sound pulls Vanquish off of Tidal and whips him to the ropes, nailing him with a powerslam. The scaffold shakes as they crash to the mat. Tidal gets to his feet, and together, he and Sound double suplex Vanquish. Conquer crawls through the ropes and starts to climb the ladder. Sound points at him and Tidal charges, nailing a low blow through the rung of the ladder. Conquer screams out and falls to the floor. Tidal and Sound whip Vanquish to the ropes and nail him with a double clothesline. Mayehm 2 pulls Tasker to his feet and fires him into the ring. He follows him in, dropping a knee on to his back. Sanders gets the upper hand on the outside, slamming him onto the concrete. Mayhem crashes hard. Sanders walks to the time keepers table and grabs the bell! TOWNSAND..."Hey, what the hell is he doing?" Sanders comes at Mayhem with the bell. He raises it over head but is stopped as Legend grabs it from behind. Sanders turns and nails Legend with and elbow to the side of the head! The manager falls to the floor and the fans go wild. TOWNSAND..."He should be disqualified for putting for putting his hands on a manager!" Sanders takes the bell and smashes it over mayhen 1's head! Mayhem slumps to the mat. In the ring, Mayhem 2 whips Tasker to the ropes and plants him with a spine buster! Conquer climbs into the ring as WoD double slam Vanquish. Conquer charges and nails Tidal from behind with a clothesline, sending him through the ropes to the floor. Sound turns to him and gets nailed with one as well. The fans near the entrance jump to their feet as someone enters the Dome. TOWNSAND..."Hey Baldwin, check that out! Who's that?" BALDWIN..."I have no idea, but whoa, she's lovely!" The cameras pick up a beautiful young women walking down the runway. TOWNSAND..."What is she, a fan?" Conquer pulls Sound to his feet and plants him with a powerbomb! Sound crashes hard. Vanquish picks him up and whips him over the top rope! Vanquish points up at the belts. On the outside, Sanders picks up Mayhem 1 and slams him down onto the concrete. In the ring, Mayhem comes up on Tasker, grabs his legs and straps on the Death Attack! TOWNSAND..."He's got it on, Baldwin!" BALDWIN..."Johnny, look at this woman!" The woman reaches the ring and climbs the steps onto the apron. Total D stop on the their tracks when they see her. The fans look on. Sound and Tidal reach under the ring each pulling out a chair. The woman walks back and forth on the apron saying something to Total D. Suddenly, she rips off her shirt! BALDWIN..."What the hell?!" TOWNSAND..."Hello! Hey Baldwin, look at those!" The woman walks bear chested up and down the ring apron. The fans scream and yell, and Total D look on in shock. Sound and Tidal slide into the ring and run, each smashing a chair over the head of Total Destruction! Both men flip over the top rope and crash to the floor. The woman jumps down and runs up the aisle out of the Dome. mayhem 2 releases his hold on Tasker and runs, nailing both Wave of Destruction from behind with a clothesline! Both fly over the top rope. Sanders gets off of Mayhem 1 and slides into the ring. TOWNSAND..."Hey Badlwin, is that...." A large man dressed like Mayhem Inc. climbs out of the crowd and runs to Mayhem 1. He rolls him under the ring and stands up. BALDWIN..."What the hell is he doing?! Who is that?!" TOWNSAND..."It looks like the Legend has bought himself some insurance!" BALDWIN..."That's another Mayhem!" Sander runs and dropkicks Mayhem 2, sending him crashing to the mat. The new Mayhem starts to climb up the ladder. The ref hasn't seen the switch. The fans are screaming and yelling at the ref pointing under the ring. Sanders climbs through the ropes and starts to climb a ladder on the opposite side of the ring! TOWNSAND..."They're both goin' up!" The fans jump to their feet as the new mayhem gets a leg up ontop of the scaffold. Samders continues to climbs while Sunburst screams at him. The new Mayhem walks across the shacky scaffold. He stops himself trying to keep his balance. Sanders gets to the top on the opposite side! The new mayhem walks closer to the belt. Legend screams from the outside "hurry!" The scaffold shakes badly as both men walk on it. Mayhem is three feet from the belts and he bends to pick one up. Sanders runs... BALDWIN..."My god! He's gonna fall!" TOWNSAND..."Look at this! Mayhem's gonna win it!" Mayhem's hand is inches from the belts. Sanders leaps and catches mayhem with a dropkick! The fans scream as Sanders looses his balance and toples off the edge! He grabs on and is hanging from the scaffold! Mayhem struggles to keep his balance. The dropkick moved him to the edge. Sanders pumps his legs, causing the scaffold to sway. Mayhem staggers, swinging his arms to hold on. Mayhem loses his balance and falls! The crowd screams as the man falls fifteen feet and crashes hard onto the mat! TOWNSAND..."Holy shit!" BALDWIN..."Sanders is holding on!" Sanders pulls himself up as the fans go wild. Tasker is on his back in pain but he cheers his partner on. Sanders gets back onto the scaffold and crawls to the belts. He reaches out and grabs them both! The ref signals for the bell. BALDWIN..."They've done it, Johnny! The Masters of Motion are the new champions!" TOWNSAND..."I don't believe this, Baldiwn!" The fans explode as Sanders climbs down and hands a belt to his partner. They thrust them into the air as Sunburst runs into the ring with tears in her eyes. Mayhem Inc carry the new mayhem out of the ring. Total Destruction and Wave of Destruction are battling their way up the aisle. Security is trying to break them up. CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen...the winners of the match...and the NEEEWWWWW....WLW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS.... THE MASTERS OF MOOOTIOOONNN!!!!" The Masters music pounds over the speakers as they celebrate in the ring. BALDWIN..."History has been made here in the SkyDome! We have new champions!" As the celebration continues, the scaffold begins to sway. TOWNSAND..."Hey, what's going on?" The lights in the SkyDome go out! The masters music is cut off and in its place some eery music begins. A voice booms out of the speakers...."Prepare for my vengance." TOWNSAND..."Who said that? What is..." The sound of crashing metal echoes throughout the Dome. The fans scream not knowing what is happening. BALDWIN..."What the hell is going on?!" The lights come back on in the Dome, and it is revealed to the crowd that the scaffold has collapsed and lies in pieces all over the ring. The crowd is silent and stunned. The voice continues..."This is only the beginning...." The music fades out. BALDWIN..."It's collapsed!" TOWNSAND..."That idiot ring crew forgot to double bolt it!" BALDWIN..."I don't think so, Johnny! Fans, I don't know what's happened here! I'm being told that we are going to take an intermission while the mess is cleaned.
