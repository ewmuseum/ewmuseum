WLW Rulebook!!!

27 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WLW RULEBOOK SNEAK ATTACKS... Sneak attacks are good for starting feuds but there must also be a meaning behind the attacks. There are no random attacks allowed just for the sake of attacking someone. If I think the attack has no purpose or doesn't follow or go with the fued, I will deny it and won't use it. When sending in attacks, state who you want to attack, how you will attack...explain in detail exaclty what you want to do. Attacks can happen at matches or in the locker rooms, etc.....anytime during the card, attacks can happen. INTERVIEWS/FLASHES You must do atleast one flash or interview a week, they are important because it's considered as roleplaying and helps you to win matches. If you don't send in any flashes or interviews, you will lose your matches no questions asked. Good, creative flashes will help you to win matches. Remember, it's not the quantity but the quality of the flashes that will help you to be successful in the WLW. The better you make your wrestler look to the fedheads, the better the chances for you wrestler to do good. Flashes/Interviews: If you want a certain flash or interview to be included on one of the cards and at a certain place on the card(like at the beginning, middle, end, after a certain match, etc...) please indicate that in your email. STRATEGIES This is also a part of roleplaying. When your wrestler has a match, sending in a strategy helps increase your chances of winning a match. In your strategies, explain how you want your wrestler to wrestle, certain things you want him to do during the match or whatever. Strats could also include your manager's involvement but careful because your manager might get caught by the ref and will be called for interfering. Pretty much, a strategy is exactly what it is: a strategy, it's pretty much simple and straight forward. TITLES WLW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP - This is the title everyone is gunning for. This title is defended atleast once a month and at all PPV's. WLW EXTREME TITLE - This is for all the HARDCORE wrestlers. This title is defended twice a month and at all PPV's. There are weapons allowed in these matches and anything goes. WLW TELEVISION TITLE - Defended every week and at all PPV's. WLW WORLD TAG TITLES - Titles are defended atleast once twice a month and at all PPV's. *************************************************************************** *************************************************************************** *************************************************************************** WLW CREATION SHEET - BE CREATIVE BECAUSE THIS SHEET ALSO HELPS TO DETERMINE OUTCOMES OF MATCHES!!!
