WLW SATURDAY NIGHT SUPERSTARS! PART 2

7 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scene opens in a deluxe hotel suite in a city on the WLW world tour. CHRIS TASKER..."Ah, the WLW is really treating it's stars in style! Look at the suite we got." BRYAN SANDERS..."A grand piano, two bedrooms, a fully stocked bar... it's got everything, even HBO!" SUNBURST..."Every wrestler in WLW is getting this treatment, and this goes for it's managers too. If this is for a simple tour, then I can't wait to see what they're gonna do for Parade of Champions." TASKER..."This is the life. And when Parade of Champions comes round, Mayhem Inc. is goin from the penthouse to the outhouse! Mayhem, if you think for one moment at all that we're about to lose the belts to you, then forget it." SANDERS..."No way in hell are we going to lose to a couple of masked morons like yourselves. The posh lifestyle agrees with us and a roachtrap motel is what suits you boys. Anything else you want to add, Sunburst?" SUNBURST..."Yeah. Rose, everyone knows you bought off that award for top manager, so don't act like it means anything. Besides, we got the gold and the penthouse key. Whaddayou got?" Fade out on the WLW tag team title belts, resting on matching pillows in a glass cabinet. BALDWIN..."haha, alright! The WLW Tag TEam champions, livin' it up in style! How about that, Johnny!" TOWNSAND..."I'll tell you what, you boys can shoot your mouths off all you want! You better live it up in all the hotels you can, cause next week at this time, your both gonna be layed up in the hosptial, and those two belts are gonna be strapped around the wastes of men worthy of being called Champions! You got that?!" BALDWIN..."Well, we'll see about that, Johnny! Alright fans, we have a big one coming up! These two men have been feuding since..." A man is seen walking up the aisle and towards the commentator's table. BALDWIN... "Oh no." TOWNSEND... "[name withheld], welcome to the commentator's table. To what do we owe the honor?" ROSE... "When I saw this match was coming up next on the program, I had to come down and get a closer look. It should be a good one." BALDWIN... "Wait a minute, your men aren't wrestling tonight. How did you get a copy of the program?" TOWNSEND... "Stop being so nosy, Baldwin. Look, they're signalling you over there." BALDWIN... "They ARE signalling me so I guess we have to go to ringside. Take it away Crumb. (to Rose) How did you get..." CRUMB..."Ladies and Gentleman, the following special attraction is scheduled for one fall. First, coming down the aisle, weighing in at 209 lbs, from Hong Kong, here is MOOOOO KAAAAME!!!" Moo Kame slowly makes his way up the aisle to a chorus of boos. Kame looks towards the fans and gives them a dirty look. BALDWIN... "I still don't believe that you came all this way just to see this match, Rose." ROSE... "I'm also here with Rick Styles. He has a big announcement to make later." BALDWIN... "A big annoncenment? Styles isn't scheduled to be interviewed. When is he..." TOWNSEND... "They're signalling you again, Baldwin. Come on, man, you're supposed to notice this stuff without my help. You see what I have to put up with, Mr. Rose?" ROSE... "You definitely could use better quality of help here, Johnny." BALDWIN... "Hey, who invited you here, anyway, I'm told we have Tom Russel standing by. What's up, Tom? ROSE... "Another winner." RUSSEL..."Mike, I was trying to get an interview with the Spartan but he just ran past me and he's heading towards the ring." TOWNSEND... "And here he comes now." Spartan runs down the aisle as Moo Kame is nearing the ring and nails him from behind with a clothesline knocking him down. Spartan jumps on top of him and starts to hit him with lefts and rights. The ref runs from the ring and tries to separate them. Spartan throws the ref out of the way. Moo Kame then rakes Spartan in the eyes and walks towards ringside as Spartan follows him. ROSE... "Moo Kame's attacks could be the best thing that ever happened to Spartan as it put some fire into him." TOWNSEND... "Boy, it's good to hear some clever insight out of someone other then myself for a change." Spartan runs towards Kame but Kame grabs him by the head and rams it into the turnbuckle post. Kame then grabs a chair and swings it but Spartan ducks and Kame ends up hitting the post with the chair. Spartan kicks the chair up so it hits Kame in the face as he's holding it. Kame drops the chair and starts to back off towards where the commentators are sitting. Spartan picks up the chair and starts to follow him. Spartan hits Kame in the back with the chair causing him to fall to one knee. As Spartan lifts the chair over head, Kame spins around and hits him with a right fist in the groin causing Spartan to keel over. Kame then rams his head into the commentator's table. BALDWIN... "Hey, has anyone noticed that we haven't heard a bell and the ref is nowhere in sight?" TOWNSEND... "This is something else. I haven't seen anything this violent since you were at that chinese buffet and they announced last call." ROSE... (laughs) TOWNSEND... "You should've seen it, he was shoving people out of the way, he knocked this one geek into the plate of eggrolls..." BALDWIN... "The kid tripped and... Can we please go back to the action?" Kame kicks Spartan in the stomach and then walks towards the commentator's table. He begins to point at Spartan and badmouth Mark R. Rose. As Kame flexes in front of Rose badmouthing him, Spartan gets up, positions himself and catches Kame in the head by surprise with a clothesline. Kame staggers forward. ROSE... "Watch out behind you, Kame, hah hah." Kame and Spartan begin to throw punches at each other. Spartan grabs Kame by the throat. Kame hits Spartan in the eyes. Suddenly a half dozen referees run down the aisle and pull the two men off of one another. As two referees hold Spartan back, Kame is escorted out by the remaining officials. Spartan runs into the ring, climbs the top turnbuckle and points down towards Kame who, from the aisle, points back yelling something that the cameras do not pick up. Spartan then stands in the center of the ring and holds his arms up in the air as the croud cheers him. TOWNSEND... "I don't know what Spartan's celebrating for. There never was a match, therefore, he dosen't walk away with any money." BALDWIN... "But, I'm sure he gained respect out of Moo Kame tonight." TOWNSEND... "Somehow, I doubt that." BALDWIN... "Mr. Rose, considering recent events, I have to ask you your opinion on what we've just seen." ROSE... "I'm just surprised Ventura didn't come out here declare the tag titles vacent due to this match, the way he makes decisions." TOWNSEND... "I see you're not overly thrilled with his decision regarding your man, Gigantor and Samhain." ROSE... "7 Deadly sins, give me a break. Ventura set this up, primarily to keep the belt off of Gigantor's waist but don't worry, [name withheld] has already set plans in motion to put the odds back in Gigantor's favor." BALDWIN... "I noticed you completely avoided my question, Mr. Rose." ROSE... "Hey, you noticed something that dosen't give you calories, I'm impressed." TOWNSEND... "Come on, Baldwin, stop harrasing our guest. Show some hospitality." BALDWIN... "Who invited this guy, anyway?" TOWNSAND..."I did! Now shut up and do your job!" BALDWIN..."Alright, coming up we've got newcomer Slick Ric taking on the mosted hated man in the WLW, "The Fantasy" Bret Young. What about it, gentlemen?" TOWNSAND..."I don't know much about this guy, do you Mr. Rose?" ROSE..."Johnny, I've been scouting this guy for a long time. I've traced his career over the years, and I can assure you that he should not be taken lightly. The man has won 16 world titles." BALDWIN..."16? My lord! Well, Bret Young may certainly have his hands full tonight. Fans, let's get down to the ring!" Crumb climbs into the ring as a group of ladies in the front row toss him a bouquet of roses. Crumbs rushes to the railing, gives each of the ladies a kiss on the cheek, and climbs back into the ring. TOWNSAND..."What the hell is this, the Dating Game?!" CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen...this next contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from Jackson, Michigan... weighing 245 lbs...here is SLICK RIIIIC!!!" The fans turn and look down the runway as the SNS slides open, and Ric enters the arena. Some of the fans point and yell, when they see that Ric is wearing a world title belt. Ric stops in the aisle, points at his belt and laughs, then makes his way to the ring. BALDWIN..."Hey, wait a minute! Why is he wearing that belt? That title is not recognized here in the WLW!" TOWNSAND..."That's pretty ballsy! ROSE..."This guy walks around proclaiming to be the 'real world champion'. Well, let me tell you something, come next Saturday night, there WILL be a world champion, and his name is Gigantor!" CRUMB..."And his opponent...from Miami, Florida...weighing 228 lbs.. here is "The Fantasy" BRET YOOUNNNG!!!" The arena erupts into a tremendous chorus of boos as Young enters the arena. He struts down the aisle, stopping infront of a group of fans holding a Dropkick poster. Young tears it from a youngsters hand and rips it to shreds. He throws the pieces at the fan, and continues to the ring. BALDWIN..."Did you see that?! That was disgusting!" ROSE..."I'll have to admit, now that Young has gotten rid of that sidekick of his, I kind of like his style. Whoa! Speaking of 'style', I've got to run. Thanks again, Johnny." TOWNSAND..."Any time, Mr. Rose. You can take Baldwins chair!" BALDWIN..."Do you mind? Hey, what do you suppose he's up to?" TOWNSAND..."Baldwin, even if I knew, I wouldn't tell you!" BALDWIN..."Thanks alot!" Ric unsnaps his belt and shoves it in Youngs face. Young looks at it, the uses it's reflection to fix his hair. TOWNSAND..."Ha ha! I love it!" Ric angrily throws down the belt and swings at Young. Young ducks, catches him and nails him with an atomic drop. Ric bounces off and lands on the mat. Young leaps, grabs his head, and hits a rolling neck breaker. Ric lays back on the mat holding his neck. Young stands over him and boots him in the chest. TOWNSAND..."He's still got it, Baldwin!" Young bends and grabs Ric by the hair, pulling him to his feet. He snarls at the man, and slaps him hard across the face. Ric falls to one knee. The ref starts to warn him, and Young grabs him, forcing him back into a turnbuckle. He holds the ref and screams at him. The ref pushes him off, and threatens to disqualify him. BADLWIN..."What the hell is Young doing? He's lost it, Johnny!" TOWNSAND..."I'll have to agree with ya, Baldwin. If he's not careful, he may blow this match!" Young stalks Ric and grabs him. As he pulls him up, Ric swings through and nails him with a low blow. The fans cheer as Young doubles over in pain. Ric stands and chops away at him, sending him to the mat. Young lies in pain, holding his groin. Ric stands over him and plants a knee against his throat. He holds it there until the ref counts him off. Ric hauls Young to his feet and sinches a suplex. Flash bulbs fill the arena as he renches him, and holds him perfectly verticle. Ric then falls, bringing Young down hard. Ric covers..1..2..kick out!! Young sits up and jams a finger in Ric's eye, sending him back. Young rolls out of the ring and rushes over to the commentators table. BALDWIN..."Hey, wait a minute! What the hell are you doing?!" YOung grabs Badlwin and shoves him to the floor! The fans are booing madly. TOWNSAND..."Hey, easy Bret!" Young screams something at Townsand and grabs the mic from Crumb. YOUNG..."Hey there, Ricky!" The fans continue to boo and throw garbage. YOUNG..."You think your a champion?! Take alook at "The Fantasy!!!" Young throws down the mic and vaults onto the ring apron. BALDIWN..."Ah, my..god! He's nuts, Johnny! He's lost it, and he's out of control!" TOWNSAND..."I'll agree, Badlwin! Anyone that puts there hands on me has got to be nuts!" Ric rushes over and tries to grab YOung as he climbs back into the ring. Young catches him with an uppercut, and gets into the ring. He races at him, nailing him with a clothesline, sending Ric to the mat. Young screams at him to get up. As he starts to move, Young grabs him and slaps on the Dreamer, smashing him down! The fans leap to their feet booing, as Young stands and kicks away at the semiunconscious Ric. The ref pushes Young away, and warns him again. Young looks around at the fans, then extends an arm to the ref. The ref waves him off. Young charges at him, sinches him, and nails the ref with the Dreamer! BALDWIN..."What the hell?!" Young stands in the ring. Beside him lay Ric and the ref. The fans boo and liter the ring with garbage, as Young leaps onto a turnbuckle, gesturing to the crowd. A group of officials rush to the ring, trying to force Young out. He fights away, nailing a few of them. Finally, the grab him and force him out of the ring. Young paces around the ring as the officials revive the ref. Crumb steps towards the crowd and gets the decision. CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen...the winner of this match.. as a result of a disquailification...SLICK RIIIIC!!" Young angrily points at the officials, then threatens to smack Baldwin before departing the arena. The fans boo him as he leaves. BALDWIN..."Something has got to be down about this guy! He's out of control!" TOWNSAND..."Well, you can bet old Ventura is going to be emptying some of Youngs' bank account!" BALDWIN..."As well he should! He layed out an official, Johnny! Fans, I don't know what's up with this guy! Some action will have to be taken, and I only hope that it's sever!"
