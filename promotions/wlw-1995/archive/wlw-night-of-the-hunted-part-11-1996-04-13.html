WLW "Night of the Hunted" Part 11

13 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BALDWIN..."Johnny, we're getting closer to the big one!" TOWNSAND..."I'm startin' to sweat over it, Baldwin! I can't wait to see how it turns out!" BALDWIN..."Fans, we're gonna take you now to some pre- recorded comments from the challenger for the World Title, Samhain." Samhain and the Priest stand in the middle of SkyDome. All of the lights are off. Only their silhouettes are visible, cast in the glow of the Priest's jack-o-lantern. SAMHAIN..."As the cold surrounds the building...and the darkness covers it like a blanket...the time draws closer, Gigantor. The paths have been walked...and your destiny has been written. When the fires surround you...you will know that you stand on the threshold of hell. As the flames intensify...and the flesh melts from your bones...you will come unto me. The path to hell has been paved with the souls of the giants who have fallen before you. Follow them...follow their screams... follow their tears...let their anguish lead you to me. The darkness awaits you.... Samhain raises his arms, and the light of thousands upon thousands of jack-o-lanterns fill the Dome. BALDWIN..."Am I the only one with goose bumps?" TOWNSANDS..."No, but you are the only one with mustard on your lips! Baldwin, this match, I don't know what to say! For once, I'm speechless! Let's just get them in the ring, and we'll talk about it when the smoke settles!" BALDWIN..."Good thinking! Alright, Johnny! It's time for the North American title match!" TOWNSAND..."It's about time! I've been waiting for this for too long, Baldwin! In just a few minutes, Rick Styles will take a giant leap into WLW history when he becomes the first ever WLW NA Champion! I can't wait!" BALDWIN..."Well, that remains to be seen. Let's go to Tom Russel who is standing by with Rick Styles!" Russel stands in the interview area, along with Rick Styles and [name withheld]. RUSSEL..."Alright, I'm here with the current WLW US Champion, Rick Styles, and of course, his manager, Mark R. Rose. Gentlemen, the talk is over, and the match is at hand." ROSE..."All the waiting is over, champ, the match is finally here. Now, why don't you reveal for Johnny Banger and all the simpletons out there like this guy (nodding his head towards Russel) what's been going on." STYLES..."I'll answer it with a question. Tell me, Johnny boy, have I pissed you off? Have I frustrated the hell out of you? Are you beating up lockers in the back room in anticipation of getting your hands on me? Then my job is done. The old rule says that cooler heads prevail and you don't seem to be walking into our match very cool. Of course, that was the game plan from the beginning." ROSE..."And we all know, hot heads lead to big mistakes." STYLES..."And all it takes is one, and I'll have the S.T.F. on you so fast, you won't even remember screaming uncle and I'll be the first ever North American Champion. Stay angry, Johnny boy, run to protect your slut in distress, it's what I wanted in the first place." ROSE...(to Russel) "Do you understand what happened here?" RUSSEL..."Of course, I see what you two did..." ROSE..."Then chances are, so does everyone else. Let's go, Ricky, the North American belt awaits." Rose and Styles exit. RUSSEL..."There you have it, fans. A cocky champion, and an even cockier manager. Back to you guys!" Styles music begins to pound over the stadium speakers. CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen...this next contest is scheduled for one fall. It is a title vs. title match, and it will decide the first ever, WLW NORTH AMERCIAN CHAMPION!! Introducing first, along with his manager, [name withheld]...Bayside, New York...weighing 248 lbs... he is the WLW US Champion..."Top Dog" RICK STYYLESSSS!!!" The stadium rumbles with boos as the cart carries the two men down the runway. Styles holds up his arms as Rose points at the glimmering belt. TOWNSAND..."There he is, Baldwin! The greatest professional wrestler in the world today! And what a US Champion he has been, winning it 3 months ago, and successfully defending up until today! Ricky, my hats off to ya!" BALDWIN..."I'll agree that he's a great athelete, Johnny, but..." TOWNSAND..."That's right! And tonight, his old belt will be retired, and he'll walk outta here with a new one! I love it! What a world we live in!" BALDWIN..."Johnny, incase you have forgotten, there's another man involved in this match, and if he has his way, Styles will be walking out of the SkyDome with nothing but [name withheld]!" TOWNSAND..."Yeah, well that's a prize in itself!" The cart reaches the end of the runway. Styles climbs into the ring and thrusts his arms into the air. the crowd boos, but he and Rose seem to love it. CRUMB..."And his opponent...making his way to the ring, along with his manager, Krissy Banger...from New York.. ` weighing 234 lbs...he is the WLW Internet Champion... JOHNNY BANNNGERRR!!!" The two enter the stadium to a tremendous ovation. As the cart moves along, Krissy waves to the fans; Johnny stares at Styles in the ring. Styles climbs the turnbuckle closest to the runway and waves at Banger to "come on". BALDWIN..."There they are, Johnny! The Internet Champ, and the lovely, Krissy Banger!" TOWNSAND..."Hey, Styles already showed the world how lovely she is. Look at her, Baldwin! She's wavin' at him!" BALDWIN..."She is not!" Banger and Krissy climb into the ring and Styles and rose step back. Both are smiling. The cameras pan over the crowd and everyone is standing. CRUMB..."Now...fans, would you please welcome...WLW President, Randy Ventura, and...the lovely and talented star of 'Clueless' and 'The Crush'...Miss ALICIA SILVERSTONE!!!" Cheers and whistles echoe throughout the Dome as the cart carryies the two to the ring. Ventura waves at the fans, and Alicia holds a tray lined with red velvet. On it, sits the North American Title Belt. BALDWIN..."How about that, Johnny! Alicia Silverstone is here to present the belt to the winner!" TOWNSAND..."You know, Baldwin, she's been callin' me for weeks trying to get some tickets off of me! Looks like the only way she could get in is by working! Ha ha!" BALDWIN..."Would you be nice!" TOWNSAND..."Baldwin, she's wanted me for years! I keep tellin' her that she's too young, but you know, she just wants to meet the 'big' in "Big J"! ha ha!" BALDWIN..."Townsand, your outta line!" Ventura holds the ropes open for Alicia, and the two enter the ring. Styles and Banger are each in their corners. Alicia shows the belt to Banger, then walks over to Styles. Rose looks at the belt, rubbing his hands together. Styles focuses his attention on Alicia. She winks at him, then climbs out of the ring and sits with the time keeper. Ventura waves the men to the center of the ring. Both walk to him. Ventura motions to the belts and holds out his hands. Both men simultaneously unstrap their belts and slowly fold them up. Banger looks at his and slowly raises in into the air. The fans cheer. He takes a last look and hands it to Ventura. Styles looks at his for a few seconds and then thrusts it into the air. The fans boo. He hands it to Ventura. The president holds them up one last time, then exits the ring, taking the belts out of the SkyDome. BALDWIN..."Well, there they go, Johnny." TOWNSAND..."That's right! The past just walked out the door, and the future is standing in the ring! Let's get this goin'!!" The time keeper rings the bell. Rose and Krissy climb out of the ring as Styles and Banger begin to circle each other in the ring. The volume of the crowd begins to grow. Styles runs, hits the ropes and stops himself, smiling at Banger. The fans boo. Banger waves him on. Styles comes at him , and they lock up. TOWNSAND..."Here we go!" Banger quickly locks on a head lock. Styles shoves him to the ropes, but is shoulder blocked to the mat. The fans cheer. Banger slaps his biceps as Styles gets to his feet. The two run at each other. Styles slips through and rams a knee to Bangers mid section, doubling him over. STyles plants an elbow on the back of his neck, sending him to the mat. Styles bounces off the ropes, but misses another elbow, as Banger rolls out of the way. Banger pulls Styles to his feet and whips him to the ropes. Styles grabs them and stops himself, then jumps out of the ring. Rose rushes over, and they begin to discuss something. the fans boo. BALDWIN..."Oh, come on!" TOWNSAND..."Hey, they're pullin' a pysch-out, Baldwin! I love it!" The ref starts to count and Styles rolls into the ring. He rushes over to Banger and slaps him across the face! The stadium erupts with boos. BALDWIN..."Whoa!" TOWNSAND..."Ha ha! That's what you call an attention getter!" Banger leaps at Styles and shoves him, sending him crashing into the turnbuckle. As he bounces out, Banger charges and nails him with a clothesline. He scoops him and slams him hard to the mat. He covers...1..kick out by Styles! Rose pounds on the mat screaming at Banger. Banger tries to swing at him but misses. Styles comes from behind and nails him in the back with a high knee. As Banger screams out, Styles drops him with a belly-back suplex. He covers...1...2...kick out!! STyles gets up and starts to stomp away at Bangers left leg. TOWNSAND..."He's softening up those legs, Baldwin! You know what that means!" STyles pins Bangers leg to the mat and drops a knee onto it. Banger cries out. Krissy bangs on the mat as Rose applauds his man. Styles drags Banger to the corner and jumps out of the ring. He pulls Bnager to the ring post, and smashes his knee against the metal! Banger screams and rolls around holding his leg. Styles struts around ringside to the boos of the fans. The ref drops down beside Banger, checking his condition. Styles jumps up onto the apron, then climbs the ropes. The fans begin to scream. the ref turns in time to see Styles leap, and he rolls out of the way as Styles smashes down onto Banger's leg with an elbow. TOWNSAND..."What a move by Styles! He can smell the gold, Baldwin!" BALDWIN..."Johnny, Banger's hurt bad!" Banger grabs at his leg, with a look of agony on his face. Styles smiles at Rose, then points over at Krissy. He slowly walks towards her, smiling and saying something that the cameras don't pick up. Krissy turns away and looks out at the sea of fans. She looks back at Styles, then slowly walks the ring steps. The fans scream "No!". Styles holds out his arms and walks to Krissy. TOWNSAND..."Look at this, Baldwin! I told you she wanted him!" BALDWIN..."I don't believe this! She's sold out Banger!" Krissy holds out her arms and wraps them around Styles shoulders. The fans are boing wildly. Styles releases the hug then looks at Rose. As he turns around, Krissy swings and smacks Styles across the face! The fans explode with cheers. Styles staggers back as Banger gets to his feet. he grabs him, spins him around, and levels him with a clothesline. Banger dives ontop of him and slaps on a choke. Styles struggles to get free, but Banger's hold is too strong. The ref counts, but Banger doesn't release. He counts again, and still Banger continues to choke. Rose jumps up onto the apron screaming at the ref. Banger let's go of Styles and runs at Rose, grabbing him by the throat. The ref runs over and struggles to separate them. STyles gets to his feet and runs. At the same time, Banger pushes the ref away... Styles swings at Banger but ends up nailing the ref! The official crashs to the mat, not moving. BALDWIN..."Johnny, the ref is out!" TOWNSAND..."This could be a major problem, Baldwin!"
