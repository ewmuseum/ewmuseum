WLW "Night of the Hunted" Part 9

13 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= \ / | \ / \/\/ |_ _ \/\/ |\ | ___________________ |_| _________ | \| I G H T O F T H E | | U N T E D WILL RESUME IN 00:00:01... =-=-=-=-=--=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- BALDWIN..."Welcome back, everyone! We've got things under control again, and Johnny, I don't know what to make of what we just saw!" TOWNSAND..."I don't either, Baldwin! Things are gettin' creepy around here! Plus, we don't even know who these people are! They want to come and play where the rest of the world's at, and they want to be noticed right away! That's all I can make out of it! We didn't have an earth quake did we?" BALDWIN..."Of course not!" TOWNSAND..."Well, then I give up! Hey, Baldwin, where did I put my billy club?" BALDWIN..."What?" TOWNSAND..."Hey, we may be way up here, but with this weapon match comin' up, I want to protect myself?" BALDWIN..."I can't see this one lasting long. One shot with one of those weapons and it's all over!" TOWNSAND..."Your amazing, Baldwin! You've got that big Harvard education, and they've taught you that if you get hit with a weapon, you'll get hurt! How the hell did you get this job?!" BALDWIN..."Would you give me a break?!" CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen...this next contest is a special WEAPON MATCH, and it is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from Montreal, Quebec..weighing 218 lbs...here is LE REBELLLLL!!!" The cart carrying Le Rebel enters the Dome. Rebel holds a baseball bat that he smacks in his hands. TOWNSAND..."A baseball bat! Ha ha, I love it!" BALDWIN..."Well thank god it's not a gun!" TOWNSAND..."Hey, you can hide a gun you know!" Rebel climbs into the ring and smashes the belt against the turnbuckle. The fans boo him. CRUMB..."And his opponent...from Vancouver, BC... weighing 310 lbs...here is HIGHTOWWWERRR!!!" The fans cheer as Hightower rolls into the Dome. Around his neck, a five foot steel chain glitters in the lights. TOWNSAND..."I swear Baldwin, I've died and gone to heaven! A baseball bat and a chain! I love it!" BALDWIN..."Johnny, I'm starting to think that Ventura may have gone too far!" TOWNSAND..."Hey, I just thought of something! THis event is being held in Canada! And here we've got two Canadians about to smash the hell outta of each other with weapons! Says alot about the country, don't you think?" BALDWIN..."What?!" TOWNSAND..."Oh, shut up and eat your cheese doodles!" The cart reaches the ring and Hightower climbs the ring steps. As he climbs through the ropes, Rebel charges, swingin the bat. The bat hits the ropes and flies back, out of Rebels hands. The fans go wild. Hightower climbs into the ring. Rebel backs up trying to search for the bat, but not wanting to take his eyes off of his opponent. Hightower leaps at him swinging the chain! Rebel drops to the mat and rolls out of the way, finding his bat. He grabs it and leaps to his feet. Both men hold their weapons, staring at each other. The time keeper rings the bell. The two circle each other as the volume in the Dome increases. They circle with the weapons and the ref dives in the middle pointing to each corner. Both wrestlers refuse, but the ref orders the weapons to be placed in their respective corners. TOWNSAND..."What the hell is that idiot ref doing?" BALDWIN..."Johnny, the rules state that the weapons must sit in the corner. If the wrestler can get to them during the match, then he can use them." TOWNSAND..."Oh, what the hell! Give me a break!" Both men reluctanly place their weapons in the corners. Rebel charges at Hightower, tackling him to the mat. He smashes away with lefts and rights as Hightower tries to push him off. Rebel gets up and plants a knee onto Hightowers throat. Hightower rolls around grabbing his throat. rebel stands and walks to his corner. The fans scream as he gets closer to the bat. Hightower sees him, gets to his feet and charges. he grabs his head and slams it into the turnbuckle. As Rebel boucnes out, hightower floors him with a clothesline. hightower looks down at the bat and the fans cheer. TOWNSAND..."Hey, wait a minute!" Hightower picks up the baseball bat and the fans go wild. Rebel crawls away as Hightower approaches. He raises the bat high over head ready to smash it down. As he swings, Rebel kicks out, catching Hightower with a very low blow. hightower convulses and drops the bat as the pain hits. Rebel grabs the bat and swings it, smashing it against Hightowers knee! Hightower screams and falls to the mat holding his knee. BALDWIN..."Oh my god!" TOWNSAND..."What a shot! That was a homerun, Baldwin! I think it went farther than Carters! Ha ha!" BALDWIN..."Your sickening, Johnny Townsand!" Rebel stands over Hightower holding his bat. He spikes the end of the bat into Hightowers stomach! He stands back and waits for Hightower to get to his feet. The fans boo and jeer Rebel. Hightower slowly gets to his feet. He holds his stomach and can't put much weight on his knee. Rebel holds the bat over head and charges. AS he swings, Hightower grabs the bat! The fans erupt. He boots Rebel in the gut. Rebel doubles over and Tower grabs the bat from him. He smashes the but end of it into the Rebels gut, doubleing him over. Hightower whips Rebel to the ropes. As he Rebel bounces off, Hightower holds out the bat, and catches Rebel in the throat! Rebel crashes to the mat gasping for breath as the fans cheer. Hightower holds up the bat for the fans then drops it, pointing at his chain. The fans go wild. Hightower walks over and grabs his chain as Rebel gets to his feet. Hightower raps the chain around his fist and runs, nailing Rebel in the forehead! Rebel falls through the ropes to the platform with blood pouring out of his forhead. Hightower beckons to his fans as Rebel gets to his feet and leans against the ring. Tower runs over and raps the chain around his neck. He pulls it over the top rope and renches, hanging Rebel with the chain! TOWNSAND..."He's trying to kill him! Where the hell are them mounties when you needed em'!" Rebel kicks and pulls trying to get out of the metal noose. Finally, tower releases, and Rebel falls to the floor choking. Hightower moves back as Rebel sits on the floor. Rebel pulls up the apron and disappers under the ring. Hightower goes back over, but doesn't see his opponent. Rebel reappears on the other side of the ring and slips back into the ring. Rebel grabs his bat and runs, smashing it over hightowers head! The fans scream as Hightower collapses to the mat. BALDWIN..."(screams)" TOWNSAND...."Holy shit!" Rebel falls ontop of Hightower. The ref drops and counts...1...2..3!!! Hightower doesn't move. BALDWIN..."Johnny, he's out cold!" TOWNSAND..."He almost knocked his head off with that shot!" Le Rebel holds up his bat as the cart carryies him out of the Dome. The fans boo as he leaves. Rebel sees the paramedics bring the stretcher to the ring, and he laughs as they pass. CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen..the winner of the match... LE REBELLLL!!!" BALDWIN..."Ventura went too far with this match! Look, Hightower still hasn't moved, Johnny! There could be some serious head injuries!" TOWNSAND..."Yeah, but you know what the good news is? With a guy like Hightower, you'll never be able to tell if there were! Ha ha!" BALDWIN..."Johnny Townsand, you are a sick man!" TOWNSAND..."That's what it will say on my head stone!" BALDWIN..."Alright, take a look at this..." Commercial... The sound of a church bell tolls in the background. The night is dark, and the moon is full. Voice over... "As the clock strikes twelve, on the hottest night of the year, you will know that the time has come. The time for pain...the time for torture...and the time to suffer. Darkness will cover the land..... ...as World League Wrestling Presents... M I D N I G H T M A Y H E M!!! Coming this summer.........
