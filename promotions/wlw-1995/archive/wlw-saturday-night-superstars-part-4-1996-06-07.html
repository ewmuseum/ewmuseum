WLW SATURDAY NIGHT SUPERSTARS! PART 4

7 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BALDWIN..."Alright fans, it's time for our main event, so let's get down to the ring!" CRUMB..."Ladies and gentlemen..the following contest is a Tag Team match..and it is our Main Event!! Introducing first, making their way to the ring along with their manager Legend...from parts unknown...with a combined weight of 701 lbs...here are MAYHHHHEMMM INC.!!!" The fans erupt into boos as the team and their manager make their way to the ring. Mayhem 2 slaps at a fan that threw his drink on him. Legend pulls him away and gets his team in the ring. TOWNSAND..."Your lookin' at the uncrownd Tag Team Champions, Baldwin!" BALDWIN..."I don't think, Townsand! The Masters beat them at the Night of the Hunted, and they'll do it again!" CRUMB..."And their opponents...from Detroit and Miami respectively... with a combined weight of 516 lbs... here are RECKLESSS ABANNNDONNN!!!" The fans jump to their feet cheering. The cameras aim at the entrance, but the SNS doesn't move. Mayhem stand in the ring, talking to each other, and pointing at the entrance. BALDWIN..."What's going on?" TOWNSAND..."Well? Where the hell are they?" Crumb takes the mic and looks around. CRUMB..."umm..RECKLESS ABANDON!!!" The teams music continues to play, but still, they don't enter. BALDWIN..."I don't understand this. Tom Russel is standing by. Tom are you there?" Russel stands in a hallway outside the dressing room of Reckless Abandon. RUSSEL..."Guys, we've been following this since the start of the show tonight. Reckless Abandon are not here! They never showed up! No one around here knows anything about it. All we know is that Reckless Abandon are not here!" The cameras cut back to Townsand and Baldwin as an official makes his way down the runway. TOWNSAND..."They're scared!" BALDWIN..."They are not! Something must have happened." Mayhem are yelling at Crumb and the officials. The official speaks to Crumb, then explains something to Mayhem. The team listens in, then Legend grabs his mens arms and thrusts them into the air. The fans boo. CRUMB..."Uh, ladies and gentlemen...on behalf of the WLW, I appologize, but there will be no tag team match tonight. I have just been informed that the team of Reckless Abandon have left the WLW." The fans boo and scream as Mayhem and Legend laugh their way up the aisle. TOWNSAND..."Ha ha! I told you, Baldwin! They were scared, and they ran outta here! I love it! Mayhem Inc. scares another team away!" BALDWIN..."I don't understand this! I can't believe Reckless Abandon would back down like this!" TOWNSAND..."Believe it, Baldwin! Hell, I wouldn't be surprised if sometime this week we hear that the Masters of Motion have run away too!" BALDWIN..."No way, Townsand! They'd never do that! The match will take place! Fans, we'll try and get some more information on the departure of Reckless Abandon. But, the stage is now set for Vancouver, and for the Parade of Champions! All the titles on the line!" TOWNSAND..."That's right! And the big one. Samhain and Gigantor in the Seven Deadly Sins Match for the WLW World Title! That's it, Baldwin! I gotta get to the airport! I'm outta here!" BALDWIN..."Alright fans, for Tom Russel and Johnny Townsand, I'm Mike Baldwin saying we'll see ya next week for the Parade of CHampions! Good night, everyone!"
