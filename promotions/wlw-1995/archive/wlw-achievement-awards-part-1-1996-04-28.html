WLW Achievement Awards! Part 1

Apr 28, 1996, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"The following is a Special Presentation of World League Wrestling" WORLD LEAGUE WRESTLING "Where the World Comes to Play!" ---------------------------------------------------------------- "COMING TO YOU LIVE, FROM THE WLW TOWER IN DOWNTOWN TORONTO, ITS... THE FIRST ANNUAL \ / | \ / /\ \ /\ / | \ /\ / /--\ C H I E V E M E N T \/ \/ |_ _ \/ \/ /\ /--\ W A R D S ! ! ! "HERE ARE YOU HOSTS...MIKE BALDWIN, TOM RUSSEL, AND "BIG J" JOHNNY TOWNSAND!!!" The three stand on a platform, constructed in the main lobby of the building. On either end of the stage sit two giant award statues, which are shaped in the WLW logo. On the lecturn, two more sit, which are actual size. The statue is in the shape of a hand, holding the globe in it's palm. A giant video wall is lit up behind the three men, displaying the Achievement Awards logo. Baldwin and Russel are dressed in tuxedos, while Townsands sports a gold jacket and gold pants. A an audience has gathered infront of the stage, consisting of fans, journalists, and WLW staff. BALDWIN..."Good evening everyone, and welcome to the first ever, WLW ACHIEVEMENT AWARDS! I'm one of your three hosts for the evening, the other to being of course, Tom Russel and Johnny Townsand." RUSSEL..."Good evening every...." TOWNSAND..."That's right, Bald Man! It's a big night for the WLW, and it's an even bigger night for me! I'm walkin' outta here with all the awards, and there's nothin' that you or peanut head over there can do about it! Ha ha!" Some of the crowd laugh, and others boo. BALDWIN..."No come on, Johnny! Don't start!" RUSSEL..."Hey, who's he calling 'peanut head'?!" TOWNSAND..."Well, with Baldwin's huge head, I obviously wasn't talkin' about him!" BALDWIN..."If we could please get on with the show... Now, tonights awards have been voted apon by those involved with World League Wrestling. We had a tremendous response!" RUSSEL..."We certainly did, Mike! It seemed like everyone wanted to get their picks in, and boy did they!" BALDWIN..."Now, due to vigorous touring schedule of the WLW, none of our wrestlers could be here in person. How ever, we have a special satelite hook up with each on of the shows that is taking place as we speak. The plan is, as soon as the wrestlers find out the winners, we'll begin to hear from them!" TOWNSAND..."Are you two gonna yap all night, are can we get this thing goin'?! I gotta a hot date, and I'm not gonna miss it on account of you two losers!" BALDWIN..."Alright then, lets get the show on the road! Our first catagory is for the BEST GIMMICK for a wrestler!" RUSSEL..."Alright Mike, here are the nominees..." The video wall lights up with the graphics "BEST GIMMICK". A montage begins of the wrestlers involved, with a voice over.... "The nominees for the Best Gimmick in the WLW are... "Top Dog" Rick Styles Samhain "Mr. Excitement" Alex Extreme J'ster "The Fantasy" Bret Young Vrolok Gigantor and Raekwon" RUSSEL..."Can we have the envolpe please?" A beautiful women in a slinky black sequened gown, brings the envelope. RUSSEL..."Thank, yooooou!" TOWNSAND..."Ah, keep it in your pants, will ya Russel?!" RUSSEL..."Alright, the winner of BEST GIMMICK is... oh boy...the WLW World Heavyweight Champion..SAMHAIN!!" TOWNSAND..."Ha ha! I'm sure winning awards are right on top of his 'to do' list!" BALDWIN..."Well guys, they may not like his actions, but they certainly like his style! And, speaking of style, Johnny, what the hell are you wearing?" TOWNSAND..."Don't you worry about what I'm wearing, Baldwin! My pants cost more than your house!" RUSSEL..."Guys, if your finishing your trashin', maybe we could get on with the award for Best Trash Talker!" TOWNSAND..."It's a good thing it ain't for Best Bullshitter, Russel, cause you'd win hands down!" BALDWIN..."Ahhh, roll it, please!" The wall lights up with the graphics BEST TRASH TALKER. A series of clips follows, with the nominees at their mouthiest! Voice over... "The nominess for the WLW's Best Trash Talker are... Alex Extreme Spartan Samhain "Top Dog" Rick Styles Kyle Kamikaze Bret Young The Master Vrolok and Executioner" BALDWIN..."And the Best Trash Talker is...... "Top Dog" RICK STYLES!!!" TOWNSAND..."Alright! That's just the first of many! I guarantee it! Ha ha!" RUSSEL..."I'll agree with you there, Johnny. No one can shout his mouth off like Rick Styles!" TOWNSAND..."Hey, watch it, Russel! [name withheld] might come out here and take this mic from ya too! Ha ha!" BALDWIN..."Hey, am I going to have to separate you two?" TOWNSAND..."Baldwin, just worry about separating your Oreos!" Alright, what's next?" RUSSEL..."Most Popular!" TOWNSAND..."Ha ha! Well, I know it ain't you! Let see them!" The wall lights up with the graphics MOST POPULAR WRESTLER. A montage of each nominees greatest moment follows. Voice over... "The nominees for the WLW's Most Popular Wrestler are... Spartan Johnny Banger "Mr. Excitment" Dropkick Shockwave Rainbow Warrior Hightower and the Masters of Motion" TOWNSAND..."Alright, let me tear this sucker open. The winner for Most Popular is.....ME!! HA HA!" BALDWIN..."Oh, give me that! The winner is...SPARTAN!!!" TOWNSAND..."What?! That loser! Give me a break!" RUSSEL..."Hey, he's one of the most loved wrestlers in the world, Johnny!" TOWNSAND..."Yeah, well there's a difference between bein' loved, and gettin' love! Me, I'm a getter! What about you Baldwin?" BALDWIN..."I have no idea what your talking about, Johnny. If we could, I'd like to get on with the show." TOWNSAND..."Sure no problem..just stand beside Russel, will ya!" BALDWIN..."What?!..Oh, never mind! Alright fans, up next is the award for the Most Hated Wrestler." RUSSEL..."Here you go, Johnny!" The graphics MOST HATED WRESTLER appear on the wall. A montage of each mans most notorious attacks follows. Voice over... "The nominees for the WLW's Most Hated Wrestler are.... Bret Young Gigantor Mayhem Inc. Samhain Alex Extreme Raekwon Moo Kame Rick Styles and Executioner" RUSSEL..."And the WLW's Most Hated Wrestler is...oh, it figures...BRET YOUNG!!!" TOWNSAND..."What?! What do they hate him for? He's a great role model for those little punks out there!" BALDWIN..."He attacked his own cousin! That was the most dispicable act I have ever seen! The award is well deserving!" TOWNSAND..."Hey, in the eyes of many, being the most hated is better than a belt! You keep that title for ever!" RUSSEL..."Your living proof of that!" TOWNSAND..."You keep pushin' me Russel, and knock you on that pointed head of yours!" RUSSEL..."My what?!" TOWNSAND..."Your head, Russel! It's that lump on your shoulders that three feet above your ass!" BALDWIN..."Alright, that's enough! Johnny, your outta line! Now, let's get on with the awards. Now, the next one, is for Best Newcomer, I believe." RUSSEL..."That's right, Mike. And with the number of them that we have, it's a difficult choice." The wall lights up with the graphics BEST NEWCOMER. A montage of the nominees follows. Voice over.... "The nominees for the WLW's Best Newcomer are.... Steve Austin Destroyer Jimbo the Terrible Discipline Grave Digga Hit Man Chad Shade Mike Johnston Phantom Knight and the Phantom of the Opera" BALDWIN..."And the winner is...oh god.. the GRAVE DIGGA!!!" TOWNSAND..."Ha ha! A perfect choice! You know, he should be given two awards!" BALDWIN..."Two?" TOWNSAND..."Yeah, Best Newcomer, and 'The most likely to make Shockwave cry'! ha ha ha!" BALDWIN..."Your sick! Your just a riot tonight, aren't you?" TOWNSAND..."Well, with you two stiffs standing here, someones gotta keep the people awake!" BALDWIN/RUSSEL..."Do you mind?!" BALDWIN..."We'll be back right after this!" Commercial break....
