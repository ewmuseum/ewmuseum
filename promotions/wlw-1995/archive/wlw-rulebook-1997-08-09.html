WLW WLW WLW... (August Rulebook)

9 August 1997

WLW RULEBOOK WELCOME TO WOLRD LEAGUE WRESTLING! This fed has simple rules but to the point. It's all pretty much straight forward. To know everything you need to know to participate in this, read on.... SNEAK ATTACKS... Sneak attacks are good for starting feuds but there must also be a meaning behind the attacks. There are no random attacks allowed just for the sake of attacking someone. If I think the attack has no purpose or doesn't follow or go with the fued, I will deny it and won't use it. When sending in attacks, state who you want to attack, how you will attack...explain in detail exaclty what you want to do. Attacks can happen at matches or in the locker rooms, etc.....anytime during the card, attacks can happen. Attacks help to create and further angles/storylines. By creating angles and being creative about them gives your wrestler a bigger push and helps them to win. INTERVIEWS/FLASHES You must do atleast one flash or interview a week, they are important because it's considered as roleplaying and helps you to win matches. If you don't send in any flashes or interviews, you will lose your matches no questions asked. Good, creative flashes will help you to win matches. Remember, it's not the quantity but the quality of the flashes that will help you to be successful in the WLW. The better you make your wrestler look to the fedheads, the better the chances for you wrestler to do good. Flashes/Interviews: If you want a certain flash or interview to be included on one of the cards and at a certain place on the card(like at the beginning, middle, end, after a certain match, etc...) please indicate that in your email. Note: If you're gonna swear, please BLEEP it out. STRATEGIES This is also a part of roleplaying. When your wrestler has a match, sending in a strategy helps increase your chances of winning a match. In your strategies, explain how you want your wrestler to wrestle, certain things you want him to do during the match or whatever. Strats could also include your manager's involvement but careful because your manager might get caught by the ref and will be called for interfering. Pretty much, a strategy is exactly what it is: a strategy, it's pretty much simple and straight forward. ========================================================================== So basically, creative angles, good creative flashes and strategies will help you to be successful here in the WLW. This fed is 100% and I get the final word of everything. If you want to complain, this isn't a fed for you because I don't want to hear any whinning. I have been in e-wrestling long enough to know how things are run, I know what I am doing. There will be times when the greatest of role-players will lose matches time to time to make things fair and balance it out. It wouldn't be fair if one person never lost, would it? They might lose depending on the angle they're in, to further storyline, whatever the case. BOTTOM LINE... Make a good impression on the fed head. Show that you really care about the fed, be active, show him/her that you are participating. Just follow these simple rules and you'll be fine. =========================================================================== TITLES ****** WLW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP - This is the title everyone is gunning for. This title is defended atleast once a month and at all PPV's. WLW REGIONAL TITLE - Defended twice a month an at all PPV's.
