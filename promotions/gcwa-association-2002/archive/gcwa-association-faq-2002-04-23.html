Gulf Coast Wrestling Association FAQ version 1.0 -------------------- Table of contents -------------------- 1- What is the GCWA? 2- Booking procedure a)Roleplaying 3- GCWA Titles 4- GCWA weekly schedule 5- GCWA supercards 6- GCWA Personalities 7- GCWA Application =============================================================== What is the GCWA? =============================================================== The GCWA [Gulf Coast wrestling Association ] is the brain child of David Bishop, a New Orleans based buisness man. As a child David enjoyed watching wrestling and always believed that he could run a successful wrestling promotion and has decided to do just that. The GCWA is a regional federation based along the Gulf of Mexico, mostly Louisana, Alabama and Mississippi to start with tours of Texas and Florida planned in the future. ============================================================== Booking Policy ============================================================== 1) Roleplaying: This is the most important aspect of booking. If you don't roleplay, you won't win. It's a really simple equation. Roleplaying is pretty much self-explanatory. 2) Strats: Strats are basically you writing up how your character will wrestle, how he will defend himself, attack someone else, what his game plan is, ect. A strat basically tells me what you want your character to do in a match and on a card. Strats aren't as important as roleplaying and are used more for tie breakers than anything else. 3) Heat: Heat is a very important factor in e-wrestling. If you have it, then you're the life of the party in the fed. If you don't have it, then you're not doing something right. Heat tells you how over you are with me and the other handlers. Past heat will be a factor if I've followed your character or know of him. I'll make sure it comes into play. Heat isn't that important where it concerns booking, but it does play a factor. 4) Effort: This is the second biggest thing as far as I'm concerned. A good rule of thumb in e-wrestling is that you won't be able to roleplay as well as some people. Effort is something that can help you if you aren't the most spectacular roleplayer in the fed. Putting forth a consistent effort even when you seem to be down on your luck in the fed won't go unoticed. 5) Fedhead discretion: This simply put means I reserve the right to book as I see fit. This doesn't mean I'm out to screw you over. This rule will come into effect under special circumstances. ============================================================== RolePlaying ============================================================== 1)The big one -- don't use other people's characters in your RPs without their permission. Doing joint RPs is fine by me because it makes for some interesting work. 2)Basically, if it can't be said on tv then it can't be said on the flash show unless you bleep it. Also do not cross the line with sexual explict remarks. If I people will be offended by the RP I WILL send it back to you to have you rework it. 3)Quality over quantity. What I am looking for is a good, quality roleplay. If you can muster one out in a paragraph that's great. 4)Shoots. We all do them. Some of us are good and others suck. Here's my rule on shoots. I won't care. If you want to work a shoot, that's great. I will warn you once again if I feel things are crossing the line I will send it back. 5)Fedhead discretion means I reserve the right to run and not to run roleplays. If I don't run an RP, you'll know well in advance why I don't want to run it and what I'd like to see changed. There won't be much to budge on in the way of this. ============================================================== GCWA Titles ============================================================== GCWA Heavyweight Championship: This is the big title in the GCWA, it means at the moment you are the best of the best. But you need to be looking over your shoulder as you have a target painted on you. GCWA Television Championship: This is the secondary title but it will be defended every single card. The television title will be the hardest to hold on to. So if you can hold it for an extended period of time you've earned it. GCWA Tag Team Titles: See the heavyweight championship. The only difference is you are the best tag team. ============================================================== GCWA Schedule ============================================================== Moments Of Glory: This is the bi-weekly Sunday Night television show for the GCWA. The show is hosted by Johnathan Stagel and Alex Sage. Total Access: This is the bi-weekly recap show for the GCWA. It is here that you get the true feelings of the GCWA superstars as well as the opion of the GCWA's very own Michael Parker and AC Gold. ============================================================== GCWA Supercards ============================================================== One Night Only: The January PPV to kick off the year for the GCWA. Diamonds in the Rough: The GCWA PPV that hightslights the future stars in the wrestling buisness. Ascension: This is the big show for GCWA. Every July the stars of GCWA will rise to the occassion! End Game: The GCWA will end all their games in October! ============================================================== GCWA Personalities ============================================================== ************************************* THE RINGSIDE ANNOUNCING TEAM: ************************************* Johnathan Stargel: (The face announcer) After the AWMC disappeared David Bishop could not resist signing the experience of Stargel. He brings years of knowledge and understanding of the buisness to the ringside area. Alex Sage: (The heel announcer) Along with Michael Parker this "newcomer" to the game was going to be the announcing team of the AWMC's second show. Sage is in his late twenties and Bishop is hoping that his youth brings a life to the show. ************************************* THE TOTAL ACCESS ANNOUNCING TEAM: ************************************* Michael Parker: The roving reporter and a very opinionated individual. He doesn't hold back because he knows that if anyone lays a finger on him they are fired. Michael will usually back the heel, but he understands hard work and if someone is working hard in the ring he won't hesitate to mention it. AC Gold: Gold has been around the block from the AWMC to the CSW and now he's the host of Total Access. Bishop broke up the duo of GOld and Stargel because he hopes that the experience of the two men wears off on the youngsters. ============================================================== GCWA Application ============================================================== Send all applications to [detail withheld] ************************************* Personal information. ************************************* Handler name: Email address: AIM/IRC Nickname: ************************************* Singles information. ************************************* Wrestler's Inring name: Wrestler's Real Name: Hometown (in character): Height: Weight: Theme music: Entrance (in cut and paste form).
