WWF Pandamonium: Intro

8 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 8 July 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome everyone to Southern California, we are live from the San Diego Zoo for WWF Pandamonium! My co-hosts for the evening are none other than Jonathan Marx and Vic Waters.
JONATHAN MARX: Welcome back Brandon, it is good to see you up and about and I am glad to be here to support such a good cause by the World Wildlife Fund.
VIC WATERS: Bah, everyone always wants to save the cute animals like the pandas and dolphins. Don’t ugly animals get any love?
JONATHAN MARX: I don’t know, do you? VIC WATERS: You are the reason that some animals eat their young. BRANDON JACOBS: Will you guys stop monkeying around? We have a show to get to. JONATHAN MARX: It isn’t my fault that Vic is acting like an uncivilized beast. VIC WATERS: Well you are behaving like an ass… BRANDON JACOBS: Settle down you two, you guys are one step away from throwing dung at each other, WE ARE AT THE WORLD’S GREATEST ZOO…
VIC WATERS: Next to the Bronx Zoo… BRANDON JACOBS: Quiet, there will be no referencing the Yankees tonight, we have a hell of a show for everyone here tonight.
JONATHAN MARX: This is the last show before the Cherry Blossom Chaos PPV live from Washington, DC and things always seem to happen on the last show before a major PPV, people are always jockeying for position, trying to one up their opponent before their big match. I am really looking forward to this.
VIC WATERS: Who even knows if everyone on this show will actually be on the PPV… BRANDON JACOBS: With LOVE and Michaels & Bartleby out in full force, you may be right. We may have to make changes to the PPV lineup because of what they do tonight.
JONATHAN MARX: While things have gotten out of control, we haven’t had to change a PPV lineup this close to the show before because of those attacks so lets hope our streak of good luck continues.
VIC WATERS: Enough about the PPV though, what match are you looking forward too? JONATHAN MARX: There is the double dog collar match, a strap match, a cage match… there is the debut of that swine Bartleby.
BRANDON JACOBS: I hope Gideon gives him what he deserves but good. JONATHAN MARX: There is the Falls Count Anywhere match and even the debut of Rosman… it is a tough choice. I’m going to go with either Anarky vs. Tact in the strap match because Anarky is going to be vicious with Tact after meddled in his business to save his friend or maybe the Falls Count Anywhere match between Rat Fink, Golem, and Pulsar just because those three brawling all over the zoo in a falls count anywhere match is just going be pure fun.
VIC WATERS: I’m really looking forward to the cage match with Manson and El Arco Iris teaming up against Felix Red and Psycho. Do you remember what happened the last time LOVE got into a cage with El Arco Iris?
JONATHAN MARX: They isolated El Arco Iris and beat him down in a four on one until he had no choice to give up.
VIC WATERS: Well tonight they are going to make what they did to him like a children’s birthday part and I will take great joy in El Arco Iris being destroyed. LOVE doesn’t like the fact that they got beaten by Copycat on the last show and they are going to punish his little buddy for it.
BRANDON JACOBS: LOVE disgusts me, but at least when you are a target, you know it. VIC WATERS: Exactly, El Arco Iris has to go into the match prepared for the hell that awaits him. It is going to be quite interesting.
BRANDON JACOBS: The match I am really looking forward to is the double dog collar match between the team of Copycat & Iron Lion against Jared Wells & Blue Cat. Copycat and Wells have a lot of history and it should be fun to see Copycat and Blue Cat lock up for the first time before their big match up at the PPV. Iron Lion is also looking to do some damage to Jared Wells in order to soften him up before their big BAD World Heavyweight Title match.
VIC WATERS: Imagine being on the other end of a chain attached to Iron Lion. Now, Jared Wells has had an incredible streak as of late, but how do you over come being attacked to a monster of a man like Iron Lion? I love Wells, but that is going to tough to pull off.
JONATHAN MARX: The biggest man I ever faced was Dan Ryan, and Iron Lion is even bigger than him. If I didn’t have the athletic background I do, I’d crap my pants. Not that Jared Wells would because he is tough, but still. It goes through your mind when you enter the ring that it could be your last when you step in the ring with someone that size.
BRANDON JACOBS: There is so much pure goodness on this card it almost feels like a PPV, I am so glad to be back home with you guys, even Vic to call tonight’s show. I’d like to thank all the fans at home who sent get well wishes to me when I was recovering at home. I still don’t remember much from before the Superbowl and on so I’ve watched the tapes so I’m caught up on everything that happened, I just don’t remember being there. I hope I’ll get those memories back some day.
VIC WATERS: As much as I hate to admit it, I missed you too. Being stuck here with a pompous ass like Marx isn’t much fun at all.
BRANDON JACOBS: Thank you Vic. VIC WATERS: Plus you don’t take sips of my Poland Spring water when I am not looking… JONATHAN MARX: I have never done that! I barely touch alcohol… VIC WATERS: The blood of Jesus Christ has a lower blood alcohol ration than you do. BRANDON JACOBS: Hey now… lets not offend the Christians here today on my first day back. VIC WATERS: If they don’t like it, we can just feed them to the lions. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we are going to take a short commercial break, but when we comeback, Lane will square off against Rosman! STAY TUNED!
