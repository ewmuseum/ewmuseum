Guerra Interminable: Manson (c) vs. El Arco Iris (Second Fall)

19 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 19 November 2004 JONATHAN MARX: Manson has lost two straight falls, one to Anarky and now one to El Arco Iris. He needs to break this snide and fast if he wants to remain champion.
VIC WATERS: Winning and losing streaks are a part of wrestling, but Manson will not let El Arco Iris win. He has worked too hard to lose it like this.
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris doesn’t even need to win this match by pinfall or submission now, if Manson gets disqualified, El Arco Iris is the new WFW World Heavyweight Champion.
VIC WATERS: Damn evil Lucha rules. *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: There is the bell and the two minute break is over! MANSON CHARGES AND HE IS LAYING THE HAYMAKERS RIGHT INTO THE FACE OF EL ARCO IRIS!
JONATHAN MARX: Manson better becareful to keep his anger in check or El Arco Iris could catch him off guard with a second fall.
VIC WATERS: I can’t believe this is happening. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson grabs the arm of El Arco Iris….. MANSON HITS EL ARCO IRIS WITH A SHORT ARM CLOTHESLINE!
VIC WATERS: Down goes El Arco Iris…. JONATHAN MARX: Manson nearly took his head off with that. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON PICKS UP EL ARCO IRIS….. MANSON HITS THE DDT! JONATHAN MARX: Manson planted El Arco Iris into the mat BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON GOES FOR THE COVER! The ref goes down to count! 1-------------------------------------------------2--------------------------------------- EL ARCO IRIS MANAGES TO GET THE SHOULDER UP!
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris isn’t going to give this fall up easily. VIC WATERS: Manson will get him, it is only a matter of time. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON LEG SCISSORS THE HEAD OF EL ARCO IRIS AND TRYING TO HYPER EXTEND EL ARCO IRIS’ RIGHT ARM RIGHT OUT OF ITS SOCKET!
JONATHAN MARX: Manson must have been studying his Japanese tapes. VIC WATERS: If Manson can weaken that shoulder, he’ll be in prime position to lock on the Tourniquet.
CROWD: EL ARCO IRIS! EL ARCO IRIS! EL ARCO IRIS! BRANDON JACOBS: The fans are getting behind El Arco Iris, but Iris howling out in pain as Manson is trying to rip that arm out of the socket.
JONATHAN MARX: Manson could do permanent damage if El Arco Iris can’t find a way to escape this.
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris is too far away from the ropes to escape, he should just give up the fall and try to comeback to get him in the next one.
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris isn’t going to concede the fall unless it is his last possible option.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is going down to check on El Arco Iris….. EL ARCO IRIS IS YELLING NO AT THE REFEREE!
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris is a fighter, I’ll give him that. VIC WATERS: I don’t know how he is going to break out of this. BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS IS USING HIS LEGS TO KICK OFF IN AN ATTEMPT TO MOVE HIMSELF CLOSER TO THE ROPES!
JONATHAN MARX: This is going to be hard for El Arco Iris with the size disadvantage, but he has a lot of heart and that makes up for it.
VIC WATERS: Right now, I think he’d rather have another six inches. BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS IS CLOSE TO THE ROPES! HE IS TRYING TO STRETCH OUT HIS LEGS TO BREAK THE HOLD….. MANSON LETS GO OF THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: Why did Manson let go of the hold? He could have had it on another ten to twenty seconds.
JONATHAN MARX: Manson must have something else planned… BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON GRABS THE LEG OF EL ARCO IRIS AND HE IS DRAGGING HIM BACK TOWARDS THE CENTER OF THE RING!
VIC WATERS: YES! GET HIM! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson grabs the arm of El Arco Iris….. EL ARCO IRIS GRABS A HANDFUL OF HAIR AND ROLLS HIM UP IN A SMALL PACKAGE!
JONATHAN MARX: We could have a new WFW World Heavyweight Champion! BRANDON JACOBS: THE REF GOES DOWN TO COUNT! 1-------------------------------------2--------------------------------- MANSON KICKS OUT! SO CLOSE, SO VERY CLOSE!
JONATHAN MARX: Oh man, he almost had him. VIC WATERS: I may just have to give El Arco Iris that hug after all. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON IS THE FIRST ONE UP AND HE KICKS EL ARCO IRIS IN THE MIDSECTION! Manson picks up El Arco Iris for the Wild Buck Bomb…..EL ARCO IRIS IS FIGHTING TO BREAK FREE AND MANSON LOSES HIS GRIP AND EL ARCO IRIS FALLS DOWN BEHIND HIM!
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris was sweating so much, Manson lost his group. VIC WATERS: Come on Manson! BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS GOES FOR THE HAPPY DANCE….. MANSON DUCKS OUT OF THE WAY OF THE KICK AND SPINS EL ARCO IRIS AROUND…. MANSON KICKS HIM IN THE MIDSECTION AND HITS THE SWEET DREAMS!
VIC WATERS: Thank god, COVER HIM! JONATHAN MARX: Manson got all of that one! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson makes the cover! THE REF GOES DOWN TO COUNT! 1------------------------------------------------------------------------------2-----------------------------------------3!
VIC WATERS: All tied up! RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the second fall….. MICHAEL MANSON! BOTH MEN ARE TIED AT ONE FALL A PIECE!
JONATHAN MARX: Both men are going to have to buckle down in this last fall because now it is all or nothing.
BRANDON JACOBS: There will be another two minute break before the final and deciding fall… FOLKS, WE WILL BE BACK AFTER THIS COMMERCIAL BREAK, STAY TUNED!
