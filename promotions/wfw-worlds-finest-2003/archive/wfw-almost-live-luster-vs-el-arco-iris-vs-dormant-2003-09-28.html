WFW Almost Live: Luster vs. El Arco Iris vs. Dormant

28 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 28 September 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Sep-28-03 AT 12:52&nbsp;PM (EST)]BRANDON JACOBS: Into every life, a little rain must fall.
VIC WATERS: I hate the rain. I just want to go home. BRANDON JACOBS: Well, if you want to go home, that can easily be arranged… VIC WATERS: What do you mean by that? BRANDON JACOBS: Your hero just walked out the door, if you want to follow, that is fine with me. All I have to do is make one call to Zack Sirius and he’ll here in twenty minutes. He is from the Boston area you know.
VIC WATERS: I know that all too well… No, I’m not leaving. I made a promise to Hart that if I lost the bet, I would shift allegiances to C.O.C.K.S. and I’ve only got one thing to say…. ::Vic reaches into a bag and pulls out and over sized C.O.C.K.S.:: I LOVE THE C.O.C..K.S. BABY!
BRANDON JACOBS: ::hangs head:: You are like a cockroach. You have no ethics whatsoever. VIC WATERS: Listen, I love Doc, I’ll always love Doc. But Doc is gone now, it is time to move on.
BRANDON JACOBS: You are unbelievable. I’ve never met a bigger weasel in all of my life. Folks, coming up we have the debut of a young talent who could be one of the future champions here in WFW….. Dormant.
VIC WATERS: I’ve heard of his reputation from the underground. This kid has a lot of skills, but now he needs to make the transition into the WFW ring and tonight is a big first step at doing that.
BRANDON JACOBS: It won’t be easy, because tonight he is going to face the red hot Luster and the hard luck warrior of happiness El Arco Iris in a three way match.
VIC WATERS: Luster is a hard man to beat right now. It is hard not to make him the odds on favorite in this match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Let’s send this one to ringside and get this one started! (CUEUP: “Ariels” by System of Down) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, standing at over 6’2, two hundred and twenty pounds, from Chicago, Illinois…. Making his WFW debut…. DORMANT!
::Dormant walks down to ringside to the pop of the crowd and slides under the bottom rope::
(CUEUP: “Tunak Tunak Tun” by Dahler Mendhi) RING ANNOUNCER: And his first opponent, from over the rainbow, the Warrior of Happiness, EL ARCO IRIS!
::El Arco Iris walks down to a huge pop from the crowd, dozens of children can been seen in the crowd wearing El Arco Iris masks, and enters the ring::
(CUEUP: “Funkytown” by Disco Fever) RING ANNOUNCER: And last but not least, the Funkiest Man in all of WFW and the innovator of Ask Luster, the hottest wrestler in all of WFW…. LUSTER!
::Luster dances down to the ring to the boos of the crowd and enters the ring:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Dormant, Luster, and El Arco Iris are circling… El Arco Iris politely offers Dormant the chance to lock up with Luster first…. Dormant nods his head and Luster and Dormant lock up! DORMANT IS SHOWING HIS POWER AND HE DRIVES LUSTER DOWN TO HIS KNEES!
VIC WATERS: Dormant is showing off his incredible strength. I’m impressed by the rookie. BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER DRIVES HIS HEAD FORWARD BUT DORMANT BACKS UP AND ONLY GETS HEADBUTTED ON HIS INSIDE THIGH! Dormant lets go and knees Luster in the head!
VIC WATERS: Damn, that shot looked like it caught Luster on the jaw. BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER GETS UP AND HE TACKLES DORMANT! He mounts Dormant and starts laying in rights to his head!
VIC WATERS: Luster appears to have lost his cool. BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS COMES FROM BEHIND AND DROPKICKS LUSTER IN THE BACK! Dormant reserves the position on Luster and starts firing away with shots on his own! The ref is making him break because he feels Dormant was using closed fists…
VIC WATERS: Dormant may have got caught up in the moment there. BRANDON JACOBS: Dormant picks Luster up…. Dormant bounces off the ropes…. HE NAILS HIM WITH THE FLYING FOREARM TO THE HEAD! El Arco Iris runs over to Luster and picks him up and calls out for the Crown of Hathor… TORNADO DDT! El Arco Iris goes for the cover! 1----------------2----------------- DORMANT PULLS EL ARCO IRIS OFF!
VIC WATERS: Looks like Dormant may be a slight bit upset. BRANDON JACOBS: Both men here are trying really hard to get their first win here so the pressure is on both men. El Arco Iris apologizes to Dormant and tells Dormant he is willing to work as a team.
VIC WATERS: All of this cooperation is starting to sicken me. Dormant tells El Arco Iris to go up to the top. Dormant picks up Luster…. RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX! El Arco Iris flies off…. MOONSAULT! El Arco Iris hits and bounces off… Dormant moves Luster to the center of the ring and makes the cover! 1---------------------2----------------- EL ARCO IRIS DIVES AND BREAKS UP THE THREE!
VIC WATERS: This is starting to get really frustrating. One man is going to have to take out the other if there ever is going to be a pinfall here.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dormant is hot and he picks up El Arco Iris and throws him like a ragdoll into the corner! DORMANT STARTS RAMMING EL ARCO IRIS REPEATEDLY WITH SHOULDER BLOCK AFTER SHOULDERBLOCK IN THE CHEST! El Arco Iris folds up in the corner and Dormant goes back to work on Luster.
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris has a lot of energy, if you can wind him, you can slow him down and Dormant may have just done that.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dormant goes to pickup Luster from the mat… LUSTER GRABS THE HAIR OF DORMANT AND GIVES HIM A KICK TO THE MIDSECTION! Luster gets up and starts nailing Dormant with forearm shots right to the back!
VIC WATERS: This is smart wrestling, Luster is building to his Funk & Wagnell Powerbomb here and by weakening the back, he is making that move even more effective.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster picks up Dormant….. HE NAILS HIM WITH A BACKBREAKER AND HE HOLDS IT IN ORDER TO APPLY MORE PRESSURE TO THE BACK! Dormant is trying desperately to break free and Luster finally lets go of the hold!
VIC WATERS: Dormant is starting to grab his back now, these young kids are resilient and can take quite a bit of punishment unlike many old timers, but after awhile, all the punishment even builds up on them too.
BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER PUSHES DORMANT INTO THE CORNER STOMACH FIRST AND STARTS NAILING DORMAT WITH ALTERNATING KNEE LIFTS RIGHT INTO THE LOWER BACK!
VIC WATERS: A knee to the back is even worse than a knee to the stomach because there is even less padding there.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is starting to stir and he sees Luster viciously attacking Dormant in the corner. LUSTER PICKS DORMANT UP OVER HIS SHOULDERS FOR AN ARGENTINA BACKBREAKER!
VIC WATERS: If he keeps this on, this could be all she wrote for Dormant in his debut! BRANDON JACOBS: Dormant is screaming in pain! The ref is checking to see if Dormant is willing to give up… Dormant keeps on screaming no!
VIC WATERS: He isn’t going to be able to last long at this rate if he can’t break free from this.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris sneaks up from behind… EL ARCO IRIS ROLLS UP LUSTER WHILE HE HAS THE ARGENTINA BACKBREAKER LOCKED ON! Dormant looks like he fell hard on the back of his head. Luster drops Dormant and the ref goes down among the sea of bodies to see if Luster’s shoulders are down on the mat…. HE MAKES THE COUNT! 1---------------------------2------------------KICKOUT! Luster barely got the shoulder up!
VIC WATERS: That was good thinking of Luster to break the hold on Dormant as soon as he did or the weight of Dormant would have pinned him for sure.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster gets up and he is furious at El Arco Iris… EL ARCO IRIS CHARGES WITH A CLOTHESLINE BUT 6’6 250 LUSTER DOESN’T MOVE! Luster charges El Arco Iris with a clothesline… BOTH MEN GO OVER THE TOP ROPE AND TAKE A SPILL TO THE FLOOR! Luster looks like he may have took a nasty bump to the head…. WAIT A SECOND, HEEL AND SCOTTY MICHAELS ARE COMING OUT TO RINGSIDE!
VIC WATERS: Here come the reserves! BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris climbs up unto the apron…. Michaels & Heel are helping Luster up off the ground…. EL ARCO IRIS NAILS ALL OF THREE OF THEM WITH AN ASAI MOONSAULT TO THE POP OF THE CROWD!
VIC WATERS: Man, El Arco Iris is going to pay for that…. El Arco gets up and rolls back into the ring. HEEL gets up first and he tries to charge the ring… the ref will none of that and steps between HEEL and the ring. El Arco Iris is making faces at Luster on the outside of the ring while a still dazed Dormant is just now regaining his senses.
BRANDON JACOBS: LOOK! SCOTTY MICHAELS JUST GRABBED THE RING BELL AND IS HEADED INTO THE RING!
VIC WATERS: This can’t be good. BRANDON JACOBS: SCOTTY MICHAELS CHARGES EL ARCO IRIS WITH THE RING BELL… Whoa… wait a sec… A dazed Dormant is getting up…. SCOTTY MICHAELS ACCIDENTLY PLASTERS DORMANT WITH THE RING BELL! El Arco Iris turns around and sees an upset Scotty Michaels with the ring bell on the mat…. DROPKICK BY EL ARCO IRIS TO SCOTTY! El Arco Iris tells the ref to turn around and makes the cover on Dormant! 1-----------------------------------------2----------------------------------------3! *GOD POP*
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. EL ARCO IRIS! BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is ecstatic! HE HAS FINALLY DONE IT! HE HAS FINALLY WON A MATCH HERE IN WFW!
CROWD: IRIS! IRIS! IRIS! VIC WATERS: Oh man, HERE COMES HEEL AND LUSTER! BRANDON JACOBS: They are coming right for El Arco Iris and are pounding him down into the mat! Scotty Michaels gets back up and grabs the microphone.
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Luster, at the Spirit of NGEN, you did a favor for me... well here's your payback, as I'm giving you the services of HEEL, for future considerations...
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is furthering sealing the alliance for hese triad of destruction! First C.O.C.K.S, now this! EL ARCO IRIS NEEDS SOME HELP HERE BEFORE THESE THREE TAKE HIM OUT FOR GOOD! Luster picks up El Arco Iris…. FUNK & WAGNALL POWERBOMB! Luster stands over El Arco Iris gyrating his hips. This is disgusting! THIS KID FINALLY WINS HIS FIRST MATCH AND THEY ARE LAYING HIM OUT! …………WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES COPYCAT WITH A CHAIR! Everyone clears house, if Copycat hadn’t saved El Arco Iris, this could have very well been his last match.
VIC WATERS: Michaels is yelling at Copycat. They will have their chance to go at it later tonight in the main event.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is raising the hand of El Arco Iris and the fans here are on their feet. El Arco Iris walks over to help Dormant get up and raises his hand. Dormant sure put up a hell of a fight here for his debut match in WFW and has one hell of a future.
VIC WATERS: Dormant is amazingly skilled. Once he gets a few wins under his belt, he'll be on his way to the top.
BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we will be right back after this commercial break with “The System” Richard Gideon taking on Felix Red for a shot at the WFW North American Title belt at the upcoming PPV! STAY TUNED FOLKS!
