WFW: NYC Unplugged: Adam Benjamin vs. El Arco Iris

6 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 6 September 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back, and in our first match, WFW management after months of negotiation, has finally landed “Yours Truly” Adam Benjamin, who will be making his WFW debut.tonight against El Arco Iris. El Arco Iris has been on a bit of a losing streak so far, but he remains tremendously upbeat and full of energy.
VIC WATERS: It isn’t in his nature to be anything other… and boy is it sickening. This man can find the bright side of anything.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is a role model to all the young children out there, don’t let the negatives of this world get you down. If you keep a smile on your face, eventually all your dreams will be come true.
VIC WATERS: If you bust out in “When You Wish Upon on Star,” I’m loading my gun. BRANDON JACOBS: Someone is Mr. Unhappy Pants today, well before anyone gets seriously wounded, let’s send this one to ringside and start off our show!
(CUEUP: “Tunak Tunak Tun” By Dahler Mendhi) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first from the end of rainbow, weighing in at 207 pounds, EL ARCO IRIS!
::the masked man with a peace sign on his mask makes his way down to ringside to the cheers of the crowd and enters the ring::
(CUEUP: “Voodoo” by Godsmack) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, making his WFW debut here tonight, a Good Will Games Gold medalist from the United Kingdom, “YOURS TRULY” ADAM BENJAMIN!
::the ref pats down both men:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Benjamin and Arco Iris lock up… Benjamin snaps mares Arco Iris down and locks in a reverse chin lock! El Arco Iris reaches up to grab Benjamin’s hair but Benjamin moves his head back…
VIC WATERS: Benjamin trying to ground El Arco Iris so he can’t exhibit the high flying skill that he is known for.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris uses his legs to thrust himself up and Benjamin looses his group on Iris! Benjamin grabs the head of El Arco Iris and locks him in a dragon sleeper! EL ARCO IRIS IS STRUGGLING FURIOUSLY TRYING TO GET HIS WAY OUT!
VIC WATERS: He has to be becareful, this move can tap you out quickly if you catch your opponent at the right time.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris stretches his body and a toe breaks the plain of the bottom rope… the ref is asking for a break!
VIC WATERS: There really should be more contact necessary if the ref is going to order a break.
BRANDON JACOBS: A tired El Arco Iris uses the ropes to help himself up, the reverse chinlock and the dragon sleep has left him winded.
VIC WATERS: Benjamin needs to pounce on Arco Iris before he has a chance to catch his wind.
BRANDON JACOBS: ADAM BENJAMIN RUSHES EL ARCO IRIS AND NAILS HIM WITH A KNEE LIFT IN THE GUT! Benjamin picks him up…. HEAD AND ARM SUPLEX! He covers! 1-------------------------2--------------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Very close call there… BRANDON JACOBS: Adam Benjamin picks El Arco Iris up off the mat for a shoulder breaker…. EL ARCO IRIS STRUGGLES AND ESCAPES BEHIND BENJAMIN! El Arco Iris hooks in a waist lock…. GERMAN SUPLEX! El Arco Iris suplexes Benjamin awkwardly into the ropes…
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris didn’t look like he knew exactly where he was in the ring there but the move was still effective.
BRANDON JACOBS: Benjamin rolls to the outside of the ring for a moment to gather his thoughts.… El Arco Iris bounces off AND JUMPS THROUGH THE ROPES AND NAILS HIM WITH THE TOPE CON GIRO! Benjamin is driven back first into the guardrail in back of him!
::fans get up and stand on their seats to get a better look at the action:: VIC WATERS: For those not fluent in French, that is flying headbutt with a twist. BRANDON JACOBS: Spanish, but you never let details get in the way of reporting your version of the fact. El Arco Iris climbs back on to the ring apron… HE IS GOING TO THE TOP!
VIC WATERS: A groggy Adam Benjamin is getting up… he has no idea what is coming! BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS FLIES OFF THE TOP AND NAILS ADAM BENJAMIN WITH A MOONSAULT PRESS! The fans are chanting Arco! Arco! Arco!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris has certainly become a darling with these fans, if he can only get a few wins under his belt, he could really go someplace.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris throws Benjamin back into the ring… Benjamin looks totally out of it. This is prime time for El Arco Iris to strike and get his first win!
VIC WATERS: High flying moves have high impact and El Arco Iris can fly with the best of them.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is on the apron… IT LOOKS LIKE HE IS GOING FOR A SLINGSHOT…. Benjamin is slowly getting up…. HE TURNS AROUND AND EL ARCO IRIS GOES FOR SLINGSHOT FLYING FOREARM…. BENJAMIN DUCKS AND EL ARCO IRIS LANDS ON HIS SHOULDER!
VIC WATERS: Oh man, El Arco Iris is screaming out in pain. He looks like he is in bad shape.
BRANDON JACOBS: Benjamin picks up El Arco Iris… TWIRLING FISHERMAN SUPLEX! The ref goes down for the count! 1------------------------2------------------ KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: He should have stayed down! El Arco Iris looks like is in a hell of an amount of pain and Benjamin is a top amateur wrestler. This can’t be good.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris slowly gets up holding his shoulder... ADAM BENJAMIN NAILS HIM WITH THE SHINNING WIZARD RIGHT IN THE SHOULDER AND EL ARCO IRIS SCREAMS IN PAIN!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris should have never gotten up from the three count…. BRANDON JACOBS: Adam Benjamin picks El Arco Iris off the mat… YOURS TRULY 2K3 POWERBOMB INTO A SITOUT PILEDRIVER!
VIC WATERS: It is going to be hard for him to kickout of anything with his shoulder as badly damaged as it is.
BRANDON JACOBS: Benjamin holds the legs down and the ref makes the count….. 1-----------------2------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. ADAM BENJAMIN! ::ref raises Adam Benjamin’s hand to the boos of the crowd:: VIC WATERS: Benjamin did nothing wrong, he did what every smart wrestler does, capitalize off mistakes. These fans are just upset that their hero lost.
BRANDON JACOBS: We can expect big things from Adam Benjamin. He is one of the finest pure wrestlers ever to grace a WFW ring and he has a hell of a future. He may go down with the alltime greats by the time he is done.
::Benjamin leaves to go to the back:: BRANDON JACOBS: Medical attendants are helping El Arco Iris up and the fans hear are on their feet chanting for the Solider of Happiness El Arco Iris. They are helping him to the back….
::a little kid looks with a picture and pen looks dejected as El Arco Iris comes past:: VIC WATERS: Look at that little kid there, looking pathetic. El Arco Iris hurt his shoulder, he can barely lift his arm and he is all upset that his hero can’t sign his picture.
::El Arco Iris breaks from attendants and walks over to the little boy and pats him on the head, he asks for the pen and signs the picture, he grabs his shoulder and he continues to walk to the back as the fans chant Arco Iris over and over::
BRANDON JACOBS: Man, El Arco Iris is one hell of a man. His shoulder was in a world of pain, but he couldn’t live with the thought of sending that kid home disappointed so he put aside he pain and signed the picture. This kid has incredible heart. I wish we had a whole locker room of El Arco Iris’.
VIC WATERS: He has a weak heart. He’ll do anything for these fans. It makes me sick. BRANDON JACOBS: When we comeback folks, Luster will take on Pulsar, another newcomer making his debut tonight right here in Central Park. Stay tuned folks!
