WFW — Card

Rage in a Cage: Manson & Felix Red vs. Michaels & Eian

21 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 21 June 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back and we are moments away from Manson teaming with Felix Red in his debut against Scotty Michaels and Andre Eian. With the heat between Michaels & Eian towards Manson and Felix Red making his debut, this tag match has a lot of intrigue.
VIC WATERS: Scotty Michaels is a very smart man, he knew that there could be for his backstage attack and Scotty Michaels decided to arrive at the show ten minutes before his match under heavy security to make sure nothing happened.
BRANDON JACOBS: Like Manson would ever stoop to Michaels’ level, well maybe if he was really upset I could see… well, yeah, it was a good choice waiting so late to get here.
VIC WATERS: I rest my case. BRANDON JACOBS: That Scotty Michaels deserves it though, you can’t play by two sets of rules. If your enemy is going to pull those sneak attacks on you, your enemy is fair guy if he doesn’t want to conduct himself as a gentleman.
VIC WATERS: Oh, you are finally seeing it my way, are you? BRANDON JACOBS: Now I’m starting to scare myself… let’s get out of here and send this one to ringside to see what will happen when Michaels & Manson finally meet!
::”Mechanical Animals" by Marilyn Manson blares on the loud speakers:: RING ANNOUCER: Introducing first, from Chicago, IL! The Former BAD World Heavyweight Champion and current WFW North American Champion, the man, the myth, the legend... MICHAEL MANSON!
::big pop from the crowd as Manson limps out and makes his way down to the ring:: :: Bawitdaba" by Kid Rock wails over the loud speaker:: RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing second and the captain of the second team, from Trondheim, Norway, the former XGW World Champion, THE SHADOW ANDRE EIAN!
::Andre Eian comes out to a pop of the night and slowly enters the ring while keeping his eye on Luster::
::Cocky by Kid Rock rocks over the loud speaker:: RING ANNOUNCER: And his partner, the Viewer’s Choice, from Philadelphia, PA…. THE SUPERSTAR SCOTTY MICHAELS!
::Scotty Michaels walks down to ringside to a mixed reaction from the crowd:: ::"Lux Aeterna" by Clint Mansell and the Kronus Quartet blares over the loud speaker:: RING ANNOUNCER: And finally, the man making his WFW debut tonight, from Nowhere, “The Boy Who Destroyed The World” Felix Red!!
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is screaming over to Scotty Michaels to start the match but Michaels has given Eian the opportunity to start the match.
VIC WATERS: Michaels is laughing at Manson and Manson is getting hot. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is going to start the match against Eian for his team despite Michaels standing on the apron and we are going a preview of the main event on the next WFW show, Scars and Stripes Forever! It will be an outdoor show and there will be fireworks immediately preceding the show.
VIC WATERS: There is nothing better than an outdoor show on the Fourth of July. I remember when I had a lot more time than I do now and I always use to go to ballparks on firework night and they would put on a show. WFW management is going to look to put on a show so it should be excellent.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson and Eian lock up.. Eian locks in a side headlock and starts pounding away with closed fists to the head! MANSON PICKS HIM UP WITH THE HEADLOCK STILL ON AND BELLY TO BACK SUPLEXES HIM DOWN TO THE MAT BREAKING THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: Manson is showing that besides being a great fighter, that he is also a great wrestler and he knows how to get out of any move that someone can put on him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson rolls over on Eian and puts his knee on the throat of Eian and Eian is squirming to get out! The ref is making Manson break the hold!
VIC WATERS: Manson is yelling things in Michaels general direction as he leans in with the knee and puts the pressure on Eian.
BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS RUNS IN AND KICKS MANSON IN THE FACE AND HE FALLS OFF OF EIAN! Manson got hit hard with that!
VIC WATERS: This isn’t going to be a tag match like the one Michaels had against Rat Fink where Rat Fink danced around in order to avoid Michaels. Michaels is going to take it to Manson but he is going to pick his opportunities to do so.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson gets up and he charges the corner where Michaels is and Michaels jumps off the apron! He yells angrily down to Michaels on the floor…. EIAN LOCKS IN THE WAISTLOCK…. RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX ON MANSON! Manson gets back up and charges Eian…. URANGE BY EIAN!
VIC WATERS: Manson showed great resiliency by getting back up after that German Suplex but he got caught dead in his tracks with the urange.
BRANDON JACOBS: Eian is setting up for a spear and he is waiting for Manson to get up…. Manson is slowly getting up… Manson turns around… SPEAR BY EIAN AND MANSON IS PROPELLED THROUGH THE ROPES TO THE FLOOR! OH MY GOD!
VIC WATERS: Now that was a spear, Eian at 6’6 has a lot of power in his body and he used all his energy in order to show Manson what he is about.
BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels drops down the apron and he starts stomping away on Manson as this crowd boos him heavily!
VIC WATERS: This is just good tag wrestling, BRANDON JACOBS: Eian comes out to help his partner after distracting the referee… Michaels rips up the mats at ringside… THEY ARE GOING AFTER ThE INJURED LEG! Eian picks Manson up…HE DROPS HIM DOWN HARD ON HIS RIGHT LEG AND MANSON IS CLUTCHING IT!
VIC WATERS: Here comes Felix Red! BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red comes around and starts pounding away on both Eian and Michaels! HE GRABS THEM BY THE HEAD AND HE RAMS THEM TOGETHER! Felix Red is checking on how Manson is, he really looks like he is an awful lot of pain.
VIC WATERS: Eian & Michaels have firm control of the match. How is Manson going to dominate anyone when he can’t stand on his own two feet?
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels comes from behind AND CLIPS FELIX RED IN THE BACK OF THE KNEE! Michaels stands over Felix Red and laughs in his face.
VIC WATERS: Michaels can really be a bastard at times, even above and beyond the call of duty.
BRANDON JACOBS: Eian grabs Manson and rolls him back into the ring! He is going to try to finish him off when he has the injured wheel. Michaels jumps up onto the apron and he asking for a tag from Eian. Eian is yelling at Michaels that he wants to finish him off but Michaels is insisting that he tags in. EIAN MAKES THE TAG! THE MATCH UP THAT WE HAVE BEEN WAITING FOR WILL FINALLY HAPPEN!
VIC WATERS: Go Michaels Go! Go Michaels Go! BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels grabs the leg of Manson… HE HOOKS ON THE FIGURE FOUR! Manson is screaming in pain!
VIC WATERS: Manson has never tapped, but he has never had an injury which has plagued him as much as this bad lag. We may see a first here tonight at Rage in the Cage!
BRANDON JACOBS: The fans are chanting for Manson in order to rally up his spirit! Manson pounding his arms into mat because of the incredible pain that he is in!
VIC WATERS: Have you ever seen the amount of sweat on Manson’s face that he has on him right now? He is going to have to give in or his leg is going to be snapped in two!
BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second, Felix Red is getting up bad leg and all… EIAN TRIES TO GRAB HIM BEFORE HE CAN ROLL IN BUT RED GETS IN BEFORE EIAN CAN GET HIM! Red drops the leg onto Michaels and Michaels finally breaks the hold!
VIC WATERS: The damage has been done, it is all a matter of time until Manson gets pinned. There is no possible way he can comeback from this, even if he is Manson.
BRANDON JACOBS: SPINNING HEEL KICK TO THE FACE OF MICHAELS! Michaels goes down! Eian comes in… ANOTHER SPINNING HEEL KICK!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red is on fire! But is that enough to carry his team with Manson in the condition that he is?
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is going up to the top… Michaels gets up and starts slowing heading over… RED FLIES AND HE NAILS MICHAELS WITH AN INCREDIBLE MOONSAULT DDT! He covers Michaels! 1----------------2--------------- EIAN BREAKS UP THE PINFALL! Felix Red gets up locks in a front face lock… TORNADO DDT! He covers Eian! The ref goes down to make the count! 1--------------------2------------MICHAELS MAKES THE SAVE!
VIC WATERS: What the ref doing counting the pinfall on Eian, he wasn’t the legal man?! BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels grabs Felix Red and bounces him off the ropes… HE CATCHES FELIX RED IN THE BELLY TO BELLY! Michaels covers! 1----------------2---------- MANSON MAKES THE SAVE!
VIC WATERS: The ref has just lost all control of this match. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson grabs Eian and throws him over the top rope! Manson has both fists up and he is ready to go one on one with Michaels! HE NAILS HIM WITH A RIGHT! HE NAILS WITH A LEFT! ANOTHER RIGHT! A LEFT! ANOTHER RIGHT! Michaels is wobbling! HE KICKS HIM IN THE STOMACH! SWEET DREAMS…. BLOCKED! MICHAELS PUSHES HIM INTO THE ROPES… HE NAILS MANSON WITH A SPEAR! Michaels makes the cover! 1-----------------2-------------FELIX RED DIVES OVER TO SAVE MANSON!
VIC WATERS: Michaels is infuriated! He thought he had Manson beat and he is yelling at the referee! Felix Red returns to his corner and reaches out his hand! Felix Red has yet to officially be in this match! Manson is crawling over to his corner… the ref turns around… HE SEES THE TAG! FELIX RED IS IN THE MATCH!
BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED COMES IN LIKE A HOUSE OF FIRE! He dropkicks Michaels down to the mat! Felix Red bounces off the ropes… TWISTING LEG DROP ON MICHAELS! He makes a cover! 1-----------------2------------MICHAELS GETS HIS FOOT ON THE ROPES!
VIC WATERS: So very close, Felix Red almost had a big win over Scotty Michaels in his debut!
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is dragging Scotty Michaels to the center of the ring, he isn’t going to make the same mistake twice. Felix Red is going through the ropes to the other side of the apron… what magical thing is Felix Red going to do now?! Michaels gets up and he begins to charge… SPRINGBOARD BICYCLE KICK ON MICHAELS! The fans are coming unglued for Felix Red’s offense!
VIC WATERS: This Felix Red is damn impressive. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is calling for the Ecstasy of Agony! He picks him up… MICHAELS SLIPS HIMSELF OUT OF THE HOLD! He locks his arms around arms around Felix Red… GERMAN SUPLEX! OH MY GOD, HE LANDED RIGHT ON HIS NECK!
VIC WATERS: That is scariest thing that I’ve seen in a long, long time. You never like to see that even when it isn’t a guy that you are pulling for.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is trying to get up though and Michaels is starting to get scared! HE STOMPS AWAY ON HIM TO TRY TO DRIVE HIM BACK DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red is crawling dangerously close into his own corner to tag Manson, but who knows how much Manson has left!
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels tries to grab the leg of Felix Red… KICK TO THE FACE OF MICHAELS! He makes the tag to Manson and Manson comes in limping but you can see the fire in his eyes! He wants so badly to get his hands on Michaels!
VIC WATERS: This is mistake, Manson isn’t rested yet. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels drops to his knees and he is begging off.. WAIT A SECOND, EIAN IS COMING FROM BEHIND… HE WHIPS MANSON AROUND AND HE NAILS HIM WITH A SHADOW DRIVER! Manson is out of it!
VIC WATERS: Manson took his eye of Eian because he wanted Michaels and he had him in his sights.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels goes over and raises Eian’s hand as a celebration… Eian turns around and plays to the crowd… MICHAELS SPEARS EIAN AS SOON AS HE TURNS AROUND AND EIAN ROLLS OUT TO THE OUTSIDE?! Why did he do that for?
VIC WATERS: Because Michaels is a bastard and he wants Manson all to himself… BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels grabs the dazed Manson who is beginning to regain consciousness… BRAINBUSTER BUSTER SUPLEX BY MICHAELS! He covers! 1--------------2-------------- FELIX RED DIVES BUT HE IS SHORT ------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of this match, the team of Scotty Michaels and Andre Eian! BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red was very close to making the save but he just came up a bit short. This crowd is booing Michaels, not only for his attack on Manson, but for also what he did to his partner Eian. Michaels is quickly exiting the building as a furious Eian starts heading to the back.
VIC WATERS: The Shadow Driver had Manson down for the count, if Eian had made the cover, he would cleanly pinned the North American Champ the match before they are going to face off. If he locks in that hold on Scar and Stripes Forever, he’ll have that match won.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is helping Manson up and he raises Manson’s hand for all the effort that he put forth tonight despite his injured. Both men seemed to have gained a measure of respect for each other tonight and this crowd is applauding their effort.
VIC WATERS: Felix Red really impressed me, with some time, he could easily be WFW World Heavyweight Champion.
BRANDON JACOBS: He definitely has incredible skills, I haven’t seen a wrestling like him in a long, long time and this crowd seems to have already taken a liking to him. Alright folks, we have to take a commercial break, we will back right after this with the match to determine who will face the WFW World Champion on the next PPV, Gideon or Shawn Hart. Stay tuned folks!
