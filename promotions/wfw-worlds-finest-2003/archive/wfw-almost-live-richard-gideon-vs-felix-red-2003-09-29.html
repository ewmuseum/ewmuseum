WFW Almost Live: Richard Gideon vs. Felix Red

29 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 29 September 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver out of WFW for ninety days and El Arco Iris wins his first match ever, what will happen next?
VIC WATERS: Way too much news tonight for my taste. I prefer dull and boring and long for the predictable world we lived in before.
BRANDON JACOBS: Come on Vic, if things were like how you said, what fun would that world be to live in?
VIC WATERS: I’d be perfectly content with my Yankees winning every year and Doc Silver as champion until the day I die, thank you.
BRANDON JACOBS: Sadly for you, we must move on and we have a hell of a match coming up. Richard Gideon will take on Felix Red for a shot at the WFW North American Title which is currently held by Scotty Michaels.
VIC WATERS: I have to go with Richard Gideon. Gideon is the most success wrestler in WFW who hasn’t won a title and he has been on the verge of winning several times.
BRANDON JACOBS: It should be a hell of a battle, but I’m going with Felix Red. With his agility and high flying maneuvers, I think he will be able to catch Gideon off guard long enough to get the three count.
VIC WATERS: Gideon is the veteran in this situation though, he has been in this situation several times before and he has learned what to do and what not to do when the pressure is on. Who is to say Felix Red won’t take an unnecessary risk and cost himself a shot at the WFW North American Title?
BRANDON JACOBS: Well… VIC WATERS: All it takes is for one move to be off or for Gideon to move out of the way last second. Felix Red is an amazing performer, but in this situation, it calls for someone to focus more on the ground game in order to not make mistakes. It is like the Ravens during the Superbowl year, they realized all they had to do was to keep their QB from making mistakes and they would win, and they did.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is just so exceptionally good at what he does though. He is the aerialist who breaks the mold and becomes something more. But I can understand where you are coming from, but there is only one way to find out, let’s send this one to ringside and find out!
(CUEUP: “Lux Aeterna” by Clint Mansell and the Kronus Quartet) ::Felix Red comes down to a big pop from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “Remember” by Disturbed) ::Richard Gideon comes down to yet another big from the crowd and enters the ring:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon charges Felix Red and bombards him rights and lefts! Felix Red is backed into the corner… GIDEON STARTS NAILING HIM WITH LOUD CHOPS TO THE CHEST REPEATEDLY!
VIC WATERS: Gideon is really laying into Felix Red. BRANDON JACOBS: Richard Gideon grabs the neck and snap mares Felix Red down to the mat and locks on a reverse chinlock!
VIC WATERS: Gideon appears to be trying to take Felix Red out by literally taking his head out of the game with all these punches to the head and the reverse chinlock which is pretty smart strategy on his part.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is trying to break out of it and he is reaching his arms out in order to loosen the grip Gideon has on his head. The ref is checking to make sure Gideon doesn’t turn this into a choke, it a fine line between a reverse chinlock and choking someone out.
VIC WATERS: Gideon is taking Felix Red out of his game. He needs to be able to get back to his feet to truly be at his best.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is trying to stretch his legs out in order to reach the rope but he is just falling short.
VIC WATERS: If only his mother had married a taller man… BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon breaks the hold and starts pounding Felix Red in the forehead from behind! Felix Red rolls away towards the ropes and the ref is telling Gideon to back away in order to let Felix Red get out of the ropes.
VIC WATERS: Gideon has been playing it smart so far, but he needs to stay on Felix Red. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon charges Felix Red BUT FELIX RED FIRES BACK WITH A SPINNING HEEL KICK… GIDEON DUCKS AND TACKLES THE GROUNDED LEG OF FELIX RED! Gideon and Felix Red spill out to the floor!
VIC WATERS: Gideon was very lucky he was able to duck that or it would have taken his head off.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is the first up and he drops the elbow on the back of the head of Gideon as he tries to get up and Gideon loses his balance getting up and goes face first into the ground. Felix Red climbs up onto the ring apron…. Gideon is slowly getting up not realizing that Felix Red is behind him… BICYCLE KICK TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red’s legs are lethal weapons. If Gideon takes to many shots like those, he could be seriously hurt.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red rolls Gideon under the bottom rope into the ring and comes in after him. In a single instance, Felix Red has gained the advantage in this match. Now he has to follow up….
VIC WATERS: Momentum can change just like that… BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red picks Gideon up…. SPINNING HEEL KICK TO THE HEAD! GIDEON SOMEHOW REMAINS ON HIS FEET…. ANOTHER SPINNING HEEL KICK AND THIS TIME GIDEON GOES DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Gideon needs to regain his senses and start blocking some of these kicks. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red drags Gideon to the center of the ring…. Felix Red bounces off the ropes…. TWISTING LEGDROP RIGHT ON THE HEAD OF GIDEON! Felix Red goes down to make the cover! 1-----------------------------2----------------------KICKOUT! Gideon has just been hit like a mack struck with this momentum change.
VIC WATERS: Felix Red is highly skilled in the martial arts while Gideon is a wrestler who doesn’t have any martial arts training. If this is a battle of martial arts, Felix Red is going to win this one.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red picks Gideon off the mat… he is calling for his famous Tornado DDT! Felix Red puts Gideon in a front face lock… GIDEON CATCHES THE LEGS OF FELIX RED BEFORE HE CAN USE IT CREATE THE MOMENTUM FOR THE TORNADO DDT AND SIDE SLAMS HIM DOWN TO THE MAT! Both men are down the ref is counting both!
VIC WATERS: Gideon really caught a break there. He has been taking a lot of kicks and that DDT could have put him out cold.
BRANDON JACOBS: 1----2----3----4--- Gideon is the first one up onto his feet… Gideon picks him up… HE IS STARTING THE G-FORCE ROLLING BRAINBUSTER SUPLEX! HE PICKS FELIX RED UP ONCE…. HE NAILS IT! HE PICKS HIM UP TWICE…. HE NAILS! HE PICKS HIM UP THREE TIMES…. HE HITS THE TRIFECTA!
VIC WATERS: Earlier in the match Gideon had been working on Felix Red’s head and now he is capitalizing on that all over again.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon picks Felix Red off the mat and puts him in a sleeper hold! GIDEON IS USING ALL THE STRENGTH IN HIS BODY TO SQUEEZE IT IN TIGHT AND THE DAZED FELIX RED IS STRUGGLING TO GET OUT!
VIC WATERS: Many people would have tried for a pin after the G-Force, but he sensed that Felix Red still had enough life in him to kick out so he locked in the sleeper hold to sap his strength. We will see if it pays off or not.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is struggling but Gideon won’t let go of his grip. FELIX RED IS SLOWLY GOING OUT! His arms have stopped thrashing around and he is giving into the sleeper!
VIC WATERS: Come on Gideon. BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED IS THROWING ALL HIS WEIGHT BACKWARDS AND BACKING GIDEON TOWARDS THE ROPES! Gideon is being inched backwards toward the ropes but Gideon is fighting to regain his ground…. The ref is checking to see if Gideon is in the ropes…. FELIX RED NAILS GIDEON WITH A MULE KICK TO THE CROTCH WHEN THE REF WASN’T LOOKING!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red is really a master of the kicks. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon falls down to his knees in pain, he really took a shot! FELIX RED NAILS HIM WITH A SPINNING BACK KICK RIGHT TO THE HEAD AND GIDEON GOES DOWN LIKE HE HAS BEEN SHOT!
VIC WATERS: Gideon is allowing Felix Red too many opportunities to get back into this match, it is going to wind up costing him in the end if he isn’t careful.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is calling for the Shattered Horizon! Felix drags him over to the corner… Felix Red is climbing up the ropes to the top… Gideon is out of it… FELIX RED PERCHES FROM THE TOP… SHATTERED HORIZON SWANTON BOMB…. GIDEON ROLLS OUT OF THE WAY AND IT LOOKS LIKE FELIX RED HAS INJURED HIS KNEE!
VIC WATERS: YES! BRANDON JACOBS: Richard Gideon drags Felix Red to the center of the ring…. RICHARD GIDEON LOCKS IN THE FIGURE FOUR LEG LOCK! Felix Red is screaming in pain! This could be it right here!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red is reaching out and he is trying to pull himself and Gideon towards the ropes using the strength of his arms… but even if Felix Red can break out of this, his leg won’t be the same and if you can take out his leg, that kills a bulk of the offensive maneuvers he can do.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red will find a way. It is that never say die attitude that he possesses which has driven him this far in his hunt for WFW Gold. Felix Red reaches out for the ropes…. HE MISSES! Gideon is trying to pull him back towards the center and Felix Red looks utterly desperate! If Gideon can pull him back, this match may be over! Felix Red makes one last attempt… HE GRABS THE ROPE!
VIC WATERS: NO DAMNIT! BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is forcing Gideon to break the hold. He is trying to pull Gideon’s legs apart! GIDEON FINALLY BREAKS!
VIC WATERS: Gideon knows the only way he is going to be able to combat all of these mixed martial art moves is to weaken the legs of Felix Red.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is using the ropes to get back up, but man, you can see the pain in his eyes. He can barely walk. GIDEON RUNS OVER AND PUTS A WAISTLOCK AROUND THE BACK OF FELIX RED…. GIDEON CLUTCH (GERMAN SUPLEX INTO A LEGLOCK!
VIC WATERS: This has to be it! Felix Red won’t be able to take this any longer! BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red eyes are glazing over! You can see the sheer anguish on his face. He sees that he is a good five feet away from the ropes and you can tell he just wants give up but he is fighting it will all his might.
VIC WATERS: This has been a classic wrestler vs. mixed martial artist match. Both men have shown their strengths here today, but they are still evenly matched.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is trying to pull his way towards the ropes… HE IS FINALLY MAKING PROGRESS…. WAIT A SECOND, GIDEON IS PULLING BACK AND WE ARE RIGHT WHERE WE STARTED!
VIC WATERS: Gideon can’t let Felix Red up or that will be the end of him. We’ve seen it several times earlier tonight.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is telling both men that they only have five more minutes left. VIC WATERS: What happens if this goes to a draw? BRANDON JACOBS: One of the President elects will make that decision then since we don’t have an elected President yet.
VIC WATERS: Makes sense to me. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is checking to see if Felix Red is willing to give up… Felix Red looks like he is almost totally passed out here. WHOA, GIDEON BREAKS THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: I have no idea why Gideon is doing this! BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon must think it will be easier to get a pinfall and he knows that time is running out. Felix Red knows all he has to do is hang on five minutes and he may be the one facing Scotty Michaels at the PPV for the WFW North American Title.
VIC WATERS: Very good point, when racers see the finish line, it gives them a second wind to hold on until the very end.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon picks Felix Red up…. HE BODYSLAMS HIM DOWN TO THE MAT! Gideon is climbing up to the top… HE IS GOING FOR IT… WILL HE GET IT? RICHARD GIDEON NAILS THE SYSTEM FAILURE (450 INTO A FROG SPLASH) ON FELIX RED!
VIC WATERS: BAM! BRANDON JACOBS: Richard Gideon makes the cover! 1-----------------2----------------KICKOUT! Richard Gideon hooks the leg, 1----------------2--------------KICKOUT! Felix Red won’t stay down!
VIC WATERS: Come on Gideon! You are running out of time! REF: LESS THAN TWO MINUTES REMAINING! BRANDON JACOBS: RICHARD GIDEON LOCKS ON THE FINAL JUDGEMENT STF! FELIX RED LOOKS COMPLETELY EXHAUSTED AFTER KICKING OUT OF THOSE TWO PINFALL ATTEMPTS!
VIC WATERS: I have no idea how Felix Red is managing to stay conscious with all he has been through here tonight.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref bends down and he checks on Felix Red! Felix Red is fighting it…. FELIX RED’S EYES JUST ROLLED IN BACK OF HIS HEAD AND IT LOOKS LIKE JUST PASSED OUT! The ref lifts up his hand once…. IT DROPS! HE LIFTS UP HIS HAND TWICE…. IT DROPS!
*bell rings* VIC WATERS: YES! GIDEON HAS DONE IT! HE GOT FELIX RED TO TAP RIGHT BEFORE THE THIRTY MINUTE MARK!
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref and time keeper are consulting. VIC WATERS: Oh man, this can’t be good. RING ANNOUNCER: The ref has made his official decision and has declared this match…. A DRAW!
(CUEUP: “Dragnet theme”) ::Shawn Hart walks out with a microphone in his hand:: SHAWN HART: Shawn Hart has always been about the fairness and while you put on a hell of a show tonight, it wouldn’t be fair for me simply to give the win to you because Scotty Michaels was promised a match against the winner of this match. And since I have a great deal of love for my pal Scotty Michaels, since both of you went to a time limit draw, YOU ARE BOTH WINNERS AND SCOTTY MICHAELS WILL HAVE TO PUT UP THE TITLE IN A THREE WAY DANCE AT THE PPV! *crowd pop* ::Hart smirks::
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart is just doing this because he hates Scotty Michaels. Talk about a blatant abuse of power! Gideon deserves the title shot!
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart may be the devil himself, but it is a fair ruling. These two match traded moves for over thirty minutes and there still wasn’t a clear winner. Gideon over came Felix Red’s martial art kicks and Felix Red battled out of Gideon’s wrestling holds time after time…. He may have gotten him that last time, but we will never know because he had battled out of hold after hold earlier in the match.
VIC WATERS: I still say this shot should belong to Gideon. BRANDON JACOBS: Okay folks, it is time to live up to the brawling history this building is knowing for, coming up next, Rat Fink will put his BAD World Heavyweight Title on the line against the returning Psycho! It doesn’t get better than that, STAY TUNED!
