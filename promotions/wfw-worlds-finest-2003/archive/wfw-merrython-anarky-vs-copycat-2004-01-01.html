WFW Merrython: Anarky vs. Copycat

1 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 1 January 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: It is time to bring some holiday cheer to this show. Copycat and Anarky will battle in a Santa’s Sack O Weapons.
VIC WATERS: Neither of these men remotely look like Santa Claus. But I’m never one to deny anyone bearing loot…
BRANDON JACOBS: Knowing these two, you don’t want to know. These are two ambitious men who want nothing better than to win this match in order to get WFW Board of Director’s attention. Both have what it takes to be champion, but in order to get to the top of the mountain, they have to win matches like this emerge from the pack and after witnessing Anarky’s last match with Manson, Copycat is in for a war tonight.
VIC WATERS: Anarky is warrior and when you face him, you are the never the same. Look at Diablo, he isn’t even around anymore. Look at Manson plunged in his pit of darkness, he may never be able to fight his way out of it. It is one thing to face guys like Maelstrom who will beat you up, but it is another thing to face guys like Anarky who not only beat you up, but get into your head in the process. Those are the truly dangerous opponents.
BRANDON JACOBS: One has to wonder though if Manson is in Anarky’s head still as well. A match like those two had lingers in your mind and you replay all the little things over again because of the high level of emotion in the match. Copycat can use that to his advantage and take advantage of him if he is distracted. Copycat has a brilliant mind and when he sees an opportunity to capitalize, he’ll take it.
VIC WATERS: Copycat has a hell of a mind, but Anarky has just been unstoppable lately. How is Copycat going to outsmart a fighting machine? Manson and Diablo certainly didn’t stop him. This may be an even greater challenge than the four man Copycat competed in to get the WFW North American Title shot.
BRANDON JACOBS: There is only one way to find out, let’s send this one to ringside and get this match underway!
(CUEUP: “Sober” by Tool) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing, a world champion ten times over and former Mr. Vicious title holder, from Hartford, Connecticut… ANARKY!
::Anarky comes down to the ring to the loud boos of the crowd:: CUEUP: Copycat voice/over “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, LEND ME YOUR EARS!” followed by “My Way” by Butch Walker) RIN G ANNOUNCER: And last but not least, the former WWL World Heavyweight Champion, from Kalamazoo, Michigan… COPYCAT!
::Copycat walks down to the ring with microphone in hand and enters the ring:: Copycat: People of New York City! Lend me your ears! *cheap pop* Now as you all know, tonight, I'll be going up against Anarky. And as I'm sure you also know, Anarky is a very, very bitter man. Now I'm not a betting man, but I'd say I'm fairly certain that Anarky is not feeling the holiday spirit here tonight. I can't possibly hope to convince him otherwise...but maybe all of you can make him feel it! So on three, I'd like to hear you ALL wish Anarky "Happy Holidays!" One...two...three...
Audience: HAPPY HOLIDAYS, ANARKY! Copycat: Hm...he doesn't look any happier. Why don't you guys try it again, a little louder? One...two...three...
Audience: HAPPY HOLIDAYS, ANARKY! Copycat: Still no improvement? One more time! One...two...three... Audience: HAPPY HOLIDAYS, ANARKY! Copycat: Well...it looks like Anarky just doesn't want to feel the holiday spirit. But I'm not ready to give up hope just yet. Because you see, I just so happen to have a little bit of holiday cheer right here!
::Copycat shows his knuckles to the camera. Written across them is "MERY XMAS" knuckles...::
Copycat: So you can be assured that Anarky is DEFINITELY going to feel these tonight! And that, people of Manhattan, is just all there is to it! *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat and Anarky come out swinging and they use their sacks as weapons in order to knock the other off their feet! ANARKY TAKES A SHOT UPSIDE THE HEAD! Anarky comes back with a hard shot of his own…. AND HE KNOCKS THE BAG OUT OF COPYCAT’S HANDS AND IT FALLS TO THE FLOOR BELOW!
VIC WATERS: Anarky must think he is Manny Ramirez. BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky nails Copycat with his bag again and drives Copycat down to his knees as he repeatedly assails Copycat with his bag! Anarky opens up his sack…. ANARKY HAS AN ALUMINUM BASEBALL BAT!
VIC WATERS: Good lord, what was the WFW thinking when they put Anarky in a match like this after what happened on the last show.
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky takes the bat and presses it to Copycat’s neck in a camel clutch to choke him out! Copycat is trying to pull the bat away from his throat but Anarky is leaning all the way back trying to crush the jugular of Copycat.
VIC WATERS: .Copycat is a big man and big men need their wind if they are going to have any sort of stamina in the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is trying to get Anarky to break the hold, even though this is no DQ, the refs are still here to make sure this match doesn’t get too out of hand.
VIC WATERS: We don’t want another Savage/Steamboat on our hands. BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky finally breaks the hold. Anarky is yelling at the ref to mind his own business…. ANARKY PUSHES THE REF DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Down with the man! BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky is going for the boot of Copycat… HE IS TRYING TO PULL IT OFF! Anarky has Copycat’s left boot off!
VIC WATERS: I don’t even want to know what Anarky is going to do next. BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky winds up…. HE SWINGS AND NAILS COPYCAT’S LEFT FOOT WITH ALL OF HIS MIGHT AND COPYCAT CURLS UP INTO A BALL IN PAIN!
VIC WATERS: They say a cat always lands on his feet, so if you take out his feet, he won’t be able to stand.
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky grabs the left foot…. DOUBLE FOOT STOMP ON THE LEFT FOOT OF ANARKY! Copycat is shrieking in agony!
VIC WATERS: Anarky seems to have come into this match with a game plan. He is slowly going to incapacitate Copycat to the point where the size advantage no longer is an advantage.
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky grabs the left foot of Copycat…. HE LOCKS ON THE ANKLELOCK ON THE WORKED OVER LEFT FOOT!
VIC WATERS: This is ingenious, the foot has already been worked over and is in pain, now he is twisting the foot in order to do damage to his ankle.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is in a great deal of pain. Copycat is trying to make his way to the ropes.
VIC WATERS: The ref doesn’t have to make Anarky break, but maybe if he gets to the ropes, he can slip out to the outside and get away from Anarky.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is almost to the ropes… HE REACHES…. COPYCAT HAS THE ROPE AND HE GRABS ON FOR DEAR LIFE! Anarky is trying to pull him away but Copycat won’t let go!
VIC WATERS: Copycat is in the battle of his life here… if he doesn’t get to the outside, he is toast.
BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY LOSES HIS GRIP ON COPYCAT’S FOOT AND COPYCAT STUMBLES OUT TO THE OUTSIDE! Anarky is going into his bag…. HE HAS THE TASER! Icekold is on the outside trying to help Copycat up.
VIC WATERS: Women shouldn’t be allowed at ringside, wrestling is a man’s profession. Next thing you know they will be demanding the right to vote.
BRANDON JACOBS: Every second I spend with you Vic feels like a year with an angry mob. Icekolds rips off a section of her shirt and she wraps it around the injured ankle of Copycat. ANARKY IS COMING TO THE OUTSIDE WITH THE TASER AND ICEKOLD JUMPS IN HIS WAY FROM GETTING AT COPYCAT!
VIC WATERS: She is going to get herself hurt, Anarky is libel to do anything. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is telling her to get out of the way…. ANARKY SHOCKS ICEKOLD WITH THE TASER AND SHE FALLS DOWN ONTO THE GROUND! Copycat with his injured ankle crawls over to Icekold to keep her from being attacked again. The ref is coming out and he is trying to keep Anarky from using it again.
VIC WATERS: Yeah, maybe after all these years, Anarky will start listening to powers that be and do what he is told.
BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY SLUGS THE REFEREE AND TELLS HIM TO STAY OUT OF HIS BUSINESS! Anarky stands over the ref…. HE SHOCKS WITH THE TASER AND HE IS JUST LAUGHING!
VIC WATERS: Anarky is loving this… BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT IS UP AND HE WAISTLOCKS ANARKY FROM BEHIND…. RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX INTO THE STAIRS! Anarky crashed in head first!
CROWD: BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat collapses down and grabs his injured left foot. Copycat uses the apron to help himself get back up.
VIC WATERS: It should be interesting to see if Copycat can last throughout the match with his injured wheel.
BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT GRABS A CHAIR FROM THE SOMEONE IN THE CROWD! Anarky is bleeding from the German Suplex into the stairs but is somehow trying to get up to continue to fight….. COPYCAT NAILS HIM WITH A CHAIR SHOT TO THE BACK DRIVING ANARKY DOWN TO THE GROUND!
VIC WATER: That chair certainly looks worse for wear now. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat looks down… HE SEES THE TASER AND HE GIVES THE CROWD A LOOK WHICH STARTS A COPYCAT CHANT!
VIC WATERS: Copycat really isn’t a babyface, but when you are facing someone who has done what Anarky has done and handing him his ass, the people love you.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat picks up the taser…. ANARKY IS STARTING TO MOVE AGAIN BUT HE LOOKS LIKE HE IS IN A GREAT DEAL OF PAIN! He is slowly trying to crawl away from Copycat…. Copycat limps after Anarky and he grabs the leg! Copycat goes to shock Anarky…. ANARKY KICKS THE TASER AWAY BEFORE HE CAN BE SHOCKED AND THE CROWD BOOS!
VIC WATERS: Anarky is a survivor. He won’t be beat at his own game. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat limps over to Anarky…. HE ROLLS HIM BACK INTO THE RING! Copycat rolls back in…. Copycat is going try to finish him off when he can.
VIC WATERS: Copycat has taken a lot of abuse in this match, now it is payback time. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat pulls Anarky into the middle of the ring…. COPYCAT BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES….. HE NAILS HIM WITH A BIG SPLASH RIGHT INTO THE RIBS!
VIC WATERS: Copycat is one of the few men who can pull that move off effectively. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat picks Anarky off the mat…. He waistlocks Anarky…. OVER THE HEAD BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX! Copycat crawls over and covers Anarky! 1-------------------------------------------2----------------------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Copycat executed the move effectively, but Anarky has a will that won’t let him give off the fight even if he is in trouble.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat picks up Anarky….. HE LOCKS IN A BEAR HUG! Anarky is trying to break free but Copycat has his hands locked in tight!
VIC WATERS: Copycat has picked a part of Anarky and has decided to attack as all great strategists do.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is checking to see if Anarky wants to give up but Anarky is shaking his head no. COPYCAT PICKS ANARKY’S FEET OFF THE GROUND AND SHAKES HIM AROUND LIKE A RAG DOLL!
VIC WATERS: He is a regular Cabbage Patch Kid. BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky looks like he is on the verge of passing out. Copycat breaks the hold! I have to question his logic, Anarky looked like he was ready to submit.
VIC WATERS: It is much easier to get a pinfall victory over Anarky than one via submission. The submission holds are more of a tool in order to get the pin easier.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is going into Anarky’s bag…. He dumps out the remain two items in Anarky’s bag, two thick gloves wrapped in barbed and a pair of handcuffs! I wonder which one he will choose.
VIC WATERS: I’d go with the gloves and give Anarky a shot in the mouth. BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT HAS THE EMPTY BAG IN HIS HAND! VIC WATERS: That was my next guess. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat picks Anarky up off the ground… HE PUTS THE BAG DOWN OVER ANARKY HEAD AND ANARKY IS STUMBLING AROUND WITH HIS ARMS TRAPPED AT HIS SIDE!
VIC WATERS: This is dangerous, Anarky can’t defend himself! BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat sets up…. HE KIT KAT KICKS ANARKY AND IT SOUNDS LIKE IT CONNECTED! Copycat makes the cover! The ref is telling Copycat that he can’t make the count because he can’t tell if Anarky’s shoulders are making contact with the mat!
VIC WATERS: This ref is very peculiar. Most would have just made the three count. This may wind up costing him the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat picks Anarky up and he pulls the bag off Anarky… ANARKY SHOVES THE BAG IN COPYCAT’S FACE AND BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES…. HE NAILS HIM WITH A FLYING FOREARM!
VIC WATERS: Copycat would be better off putting the bag back on. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is getting back up…. ANARKY GRABS THE BAT ON GROUND ON THE MAT AND NAILS COPYCAT IN THE STOMACH! Copycat falls down to his knees!
VIC WATERS: Anarky is taunting Copycat while holding the bat to get back up but Copycat really took a bat shot to the gut.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat grabs the leg of Anarky in an attempt to get up…. ANARKY RAMS THE BARREL OF THE BAT INTO THE BACK OF COPYCAT AND COPYCAT GOES DOWN AS ANARKY LAUGHS!
VIC WATERS: Copycat made all these jokes at Anarky’s expense, it looks like Anarky is paying him back.
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky drops the bat down and picks Copycat off the mat…. HE NAILS HIM WITH THE SWINGING NECKBREAKER! Copycat is out of it! Anarky is looking around for something….
VIC WATERS: Copycat dumped out everything in his bag in the ring, everything except the taser is inside of the ring.
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky sees Icekold who has recovered outside of the ring with Copycat’s still unopened bag… ANARKY HEADS TO THE OUTSIDE AND HE IS CHASING ICEKOLD AND ICEKOLD IS TRYING TO GET AWAY…. ANARKY CATCHES UP AND KNOCKS HER DOWN TO THE GROUND! He picks up the bag and he is headed back to the ring.
VIC WATERS: Copycat and Anarky got so involved using Anarky’s bag, Copycat’s bag hasn’t even been touched!
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky is bringing the bag back into the ring and COPYCAT TRIES TO GRABS THE BAG AWAY FROM ANARKY…. ANARKY KICKS HIM IN THE STOMACH AND COPYCAT CRASHES ON TOP OF THE REFEREE!
VIC WATERS: It almost looked like Copycat dove on the referee instead of knocked him do. BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky opens up the bag…. OH MY GOD THE BAG JUST EXPLODED AND A PAINT BOMB HAS BLINDED ANARKY FACE WITH RED AND GREEN PAINT!
::huge crowd pop:: VIC WATERS: IT WAS A TRAP! BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat knees him in the stomach…. COPYCAT PICKS ANARKY UP FOR THE LITTERBOMB…. HE HITS IT! Copycat rolls him up for the pin! 1--------------------------------------------2-----------------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match….. COPYCAT! CROWD: COPYCAT COPYCAT! COPYCAT! VIC WATERS: Anarky isn’t going to be happy when he gets all of that paint out of his eyes and realizes what has happened here.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat and Icekold are headed to the back like thieves in the night and these fans couldn’t be happier. I thought I had seen it all but that ending takes the cake. Folks, we have to go to a commercial break, but when we comeback, the next match up is the Santa’s Surprise match where the team of Manson, El Arco Iris, and Good Eddie will team up against the team of Minion, Terrell Mason, and Luster…. STAY TUNED!
