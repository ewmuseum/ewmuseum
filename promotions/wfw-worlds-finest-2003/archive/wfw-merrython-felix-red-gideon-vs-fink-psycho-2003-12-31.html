WFW Merrython: Felix Red & Gideon vs. Fink & Psycho

31 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 31 December 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back and it is time for the first No Rope Barbed Wire Cage Street Fight Tornado Death Match match ever in WFW History. This match will be held under BAD rules so anything goes.
VIC WATERS: This match seems like it would clearly favor the challengers, Psycho and Rat Fink, but I’m being told that Rat Fink has suffered a rumored pulled hamstring and won’t be completely mobile tonight for his match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hamstring? When I saw him backstage, he was wearing a cast on his left leg. You don’t wear a cast when you pull a hamstring.
VIC WATERS: Why would Rat Fink make it up? BRANDON JACOBS: Hell, I’d make an injury up to avoid being part of this match. This is going to be a bloody match and most likely someone is going to get hurt. Barbed wire is nothing to mess with.
VIC WATERS: Are you saying Rat Fink is scared? The Former BAD World Heavyweight Champion scared? HA! Rat Fink has been in some of the most extreme matches in BAD History. He isn’t scared of anything.
BRANDON JACOBS: Still and all, that leg injury by Rat Fink sounds a bit strange. Maybe he is smuggling a weapon.
VIC WATERS: This is a No Rope Barbed Wire Cage Street Fight Tornado Death match. Needless to say this is no DQ. Why would he go through such an extreme to smuggle weapons into the match?
BRANDON JACOBS: I guess that is a good point. Hmm, I have absolutely no idea what Rat Fink is up to them.
VIC WATERS: Nobody ever does. That is why he is Rat Fink, an evil mastermind who even gave Manson trouble.
BRANDON JACOBS: There is only one way to find out, the ring crew has finally assembled the cage, let’s send this one down to ringside!
(CUEUP: “"If Born From This Soil: Treatments For the Injected Foetu" by Minor Procedure and Shai Hulud)
RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, Making his way to the ring, weighing in at 213 pounds and standing at 6 feet, three inches, “The Boy Who Destroyed The World”, FELIX RED!
::Felix Red comes down to the ring to the roar of the crowd and enters the cage:: (CUEUP: “Remember” by Disturbed) RING ANNOUCER: Introducing second, his partner, from Buffalo, NY, THE SYSTEM RICHARD GIDEON!
::Gideon comes down to the ring to the roar of the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first of the opposing team,the former WFW World Heavyweight Champion, PSYCHO!
::Psycho comes down to a mixed reaction from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “Megalomaniac” by KMFDM) RING ANNOUNCER: And his partner, the leader of the New Age Revolution and the former BAD World Heavyweight Champion, RAT FINK!
::Rat Fink limps down to the ring in a cast with the Satan’s Clause walking behind him and Elfbot, all three enter the ring::
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink is signaling the ref to come over. RAT FINK IS HANDING THE REF A NOTE! The ref is calling for a microphone from the time keeper.
REF WITH MIC: Rat Fink will be unable to compete here tonight because of a leg injury during training which has left him without medical clearance. By edict of WFW President Alex Wylde, SATAN CLAUSE WILL TAKE RAT FINK’S PLACE IN THE MATCH TONIGHT!
::crowd boos:: ::Rat Fink snickers as Elfbot helps Rat Fink out of the ring:: VIC WATERS: How can this crowd boo Satan Clause at Christmas time? It just isn’t right. This isn’t Philly.
BRANDON JACOBS: This changes the whole entire dynamic of this match. Satan Clause is 6’7 280 pounds. Rat Fink was 6’3 255 which is big, but Satan Clause is even bigger. The champs could be in trouble here.
VIC WATERS: They were in trouble even before Rat Fink was replaced. These two are in their natural environment and they would love nothing better than to pin either Gideon or Felix Red to setup a title match down the road.
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: In a match as violent as this one, I don’t expect it to go all that long, but is going to be a bloody contest.
VIC WATERS: Wrestlers aren’t stupid. They know the sooner this match ends, the better it is long term for their careers. Felix Red and Richard Gideon are champions and don’t want to put their title reigns in jeopardy.
BRANDON JACOBS: Satan Clause is receiving instructions from the vocal Rat Fink on the outside. FELIX RED COMES FROM BEHIND AND DROPKICKS SATAN CLAUSE INTO THE BARBED WIRE CAGE! Gideon and Psycho are brawling. All hell has broken loose!
VIC WATERS: The war has officially begun. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red grabs the head of Satan Clause and HE RUBS HIS HEAD INTO THE BARBED WIRE CAGE AND SATAN CLAUSE’S FOREHEAD IS BEING SLICED AND DICED!
VIC WATERS: Satan Clause is donning his Christmas color. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho whips Gideon off the ropes…. HE NAILS HIM WITH A STUN GUN INTO THE BARBED WIRE CAGE BUT GIDEON MANAGES TO GET HIS ARMS UP TO AVOID TO CUTTING UP HIS FACE! Gideon’s hands are bleeding from the block.
VIC WATERS: Thank god he got his hands up. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red lets go of Satan Clause…. HE NAILS HIM WITH A SPINNING HEEL KICK! Satan Clause falls back…. OH MY GOD! SATAN CLAUSE’S HAIR HAS GOTTEN TANGLED IN THE BARBED WIRE CAGE! Felix Red grabs the legs of Satan Clause and HE IS TRYING TO PULL HIM OFF AND SATAN CLAUSE IS SCREAMING IN PAIN!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is coming over to help Satan Clause! BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO YAKUZA KICKS FELIX RED FROM BEHIND AND FELIX RED LETS GO! Psycho is trying to help his partner get his hair out of the barbed wire cage… Psycho manages to get it out but it looks like Satan Clause lost a lump of hair in the process. SATAN CLAUSE GRABS PSYCHO BY THE NECK!
VIC WATERS: What is Satan Clause doing?! BRANDON JACOBS: SATAN CLAUSE CHOKESLAMS PSYCHO DOWN TO THE MAT! Satan Clause picks up Felix Red…. HE POWERSLAMS HIM DOWN TO THE MAT! Satan Clause is choking Felix Red out! SATAN CLAUSE HAS SNAPPED!
VIC WATERS: Satan Clause has lost it. He is just battling to survive now. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is trying to pry Satan Clause’s hands off his neck but they can’t be budged! Felix Red is screaming for help…. GIDEON GRABS THE HAIR OF SATAN CLAUSE IN AN ATTEMPT TO PULL HIM OFF!
VIC WATERS: Gideon just poured more gasoline onto the fire. BRANDON JACOBS: Satan Clause lets go of Felix Red and he turns around and looks at Gideon with a fire in his eyes…. SATAN CLAUSE CHARGES WITH A CLOTHESLINE BUT GIDEON DUCKS! Satan Clause comes dangerously close into running into the cage!
VIC WATERS: Satan Clause wants nothing more to do with that cage. BRANDON JACOBS: Satan Clause turns around…. GIDEON KNEES HIM IN THE GUT! Gideon LIFTS THE MAN UP FOR A BRAINBUSTER SUPLEX…. HE NAILS THE FIRST ONE... HE IS GOING FOR ANOTHER… HE NAILS A SECOND ONE…. HE ROLLS AND LIFTS HIM UP AGAIN…. HE NAILS WITH A THIRD…. G-FORCE TIMES THREE! This crowd is on its feet giving Gideon a standing ovation for handling the big man!
VIC WATERS: Wait a second, Psycho who took a nasty bump to the head is stirring and he sees Gideon.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho to be fiddling around pulling something out of his tights…. PSYCHO HAS FLASH PAPER! As if the barbed wire surrounding the ring wasn’t dangerous enough. FELIX RED IS CALLING TO GIDEON TO BE CAREFUL! Gideon doesn’t appear to hear Felix Red over the crowd and he is pulling Satan Clause up to finish him off.... PSYCHO IS UP!
VIC WATERS: This can only lead to no good… BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO TURNS GIDEON AROUND AND THROWS THE FIREBALL AT HIM…. WAIT… NO! GIDEON DUCKED AND SATAN CLAUSE IS GRABBING HIS EYES! Felix Red gets up…. SPINNING BACK KICK TO THE FACE OF PSYCHO! Psycho goes down!
VIC WATERS: Gideon and Felix Red need to strike when the fire is hot and finish this match off.
BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED NAILS SATAN CLAUSE WITH A SPINNING BACK KICK AS WELL! Gideon grabs the feet of Satan Clause and drags him to the center of the ring. Gideon is calling for Felix Red to stand in the corner…. GIDEON IS CLIMBING UP ONTO THE SHOULDERS OF FELIX RED….. HE NAILS HIM WITH THE SYSTEM FAILURE 450 IN A FROG SPLASH! Gideon goes for the cover! 1-------------------------------------------------2-------------------------------------------------3!
(CUEUP: “Remember” By Disturbed) RING ANNOUNCER: The winners of the match…. THE TEAM OF FELIX RED AND RICHARD GIDEON! BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is raising the arms of the winning team and hands them their titles…. Felix Red shakes Richard Gideon’s hand and leaves the cage…. RAT FINK RUNS TO THE DOOR AND PUTS ON A LOCK ON IT TO PREVENT GIDEON FROM GETTING OUT!
VIC WATERS: This all was a trap to get Gideon! BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon turns around…. PSYCHO NAILS HIM WITH A FIREBALL AND GIDEON IS YELLING BLOODY MURDER! Rat Fink is taking off his cast and he throws it into Psycho! PSYCHO TAKES THE CAST AND HE SHATTERS IT OVER THE HEAD OF GIDEON TO PUT HIM DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red is trying to break in to save Gideon but there is nothing he can do. That lock could keep an herd of cattle in their pen.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho picks up Gideon…. HE NAILS HIM WITH THE TIGER DRIVER IN THE DEAD CENTER OF THE RING! Psycho grabs Gideon’s title and he raises it up in the air and points to himself! Psycho throws the title down onto the lifeless body of Gideon and spits on him.
VIC WATERS: Here comes security! BRANDON JACOBS: Security is breaking open the lock, but it is too late, Gideon has suffered another dastardly attack by Psycho. The door opens and Felix Red goes to the side of Gideon to check on him. Psycho walks out of the door to a chorus of boos.
VIC WATERS: Psycho is just showing everyone what to do when you want something, you have to go and take it.
BRANDON JACOBS: This feud surely isn’t over. Gideon is starting to stir but he looks pretty shaken up. Folks, we have to take another commercial break, when we comeback, Anarky will go one on one with the man getting the WFW North American Title shot at the PPV, Copycat in a Santa’s Sack O Weapons Match! STAY TUNED!
