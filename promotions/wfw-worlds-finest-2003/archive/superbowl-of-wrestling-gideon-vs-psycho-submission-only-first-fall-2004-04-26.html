WFW — Card

Superbowl of Wrestling: Gideon vs. Psycho - Submission Only (First Fall)

26 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 26 April 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: With this match, Gideon officially breaks the record as the longest reigning WFW North American Champion of All-time beating out Manson’s previous record of five months, which is quite an accomplishment when you consider the league has only been around a year.
VIC WATERS: But tonight, that record that Gideon has worked so hard to accomplish is coming to an end at the hands of Psycho.
BRANDON JACOBS: This show will officially mark Gideon’s one year anniversary in wrestling and he’d like to top off an already unprecedented WFW North American Title win with a big win at the Superbowl of Wrestling.
VIC WATERS: That may not be so easy, two out of three falls in the match seem like they would favor the challenger.
BRANDON JACOBS: Let’s go over the stipulations for the two out of three falls match. The first fall will be submissions only, the second fall will a Four Corners of Hell match with a pallet covered in barbed-wire, a tray filled with thumbtacks, a trashcan filled with lightubes and staple guns in each corner, and the third fall will be a Texas Death Match.
VIC WATERS: Can there be a starker contrast between two matches than a submission match and a Four Corners of Hell Match?
BRANDON JACOBS: If there is one, I can’t think of it. This is going to be a knock out, drag out fight between two men who have been seemingly feuding forever. But tonight, that all ends, this will be the last chapter in the feud, at least for awhile to determine who is the better man.
VIC WATERS: Hopefully both men have saved their best for last because both will need it. Over the last year, I’ve gained a lot of respect for Gideon from the days where he went nose to nose with Doc to his record breaking title reign with the WFW North American Title, Gideon has been one of the most accomplished wrestlers of WFW’s first year. I may not like him, but I’ve learned to respect him. I don’t really know who will win this fight, I’ve seen Gideon over come greater odds than this but Psycho is the same man who basically ended Doc’s career with the damage he did to him at Rage in the Cage.
BRANDON JACOBS: I’m not sure who to pick either, this is going to be an awful close match and it quite possibly could steal the show. Folks, let’s send this one down to ringside and get it underway!
(CUEUP: "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first of the opposing team,the former WFW World Heavyweight Champion, PSYCHO!
::Psycho comes down to a mixed reaction from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “Remember” by Disturbed) RING ANNOUCER: And now, the champion, from Buffalo, NY, THE WFW North American Champion…. THE SYSTEM RICHARD GIDEON!
::Gideon comes down to the ring to the roar of the crowd and enters the ring:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: This is going to be a match for the ages. PSYCHO CHARGES GIDEON AS HE GIVES RING ATTENDANT THE BELT!
VIC WATERS: Psycho isn’t wasting any time. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is pummeling into the corner! PSYCHO CLIMBS UP TO THE SECOND TURNBUCKLE AND HE STARTS POUNDING AWAY WITH CLOSED FISTS TO THE HEAD!
VIC WATERS: I haven’t seen Psycho this motivated since he won the four way at Cherry Blossom Chaos.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is trying to get Psycho to open up his fist…. PSYCHO FRANKENSTEINERS GIDEON OUT OF THE CORNER!
VIC WATERS: Psycho strategy seems like to do as much damage as quickly as possible so Gideon will be weakened in the later falls.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho locks on a reverse chinlock on Gideon. It looks like Psycho is beginning the wear down process.
VIC WATERS: Psycho is attempting to cut off the blood supply to the head in order to tire Gideon out.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is struggling to break free… he is reaching back in an attempt to grab to Psycho… HE HAS A HANDFUL OF THE MASK!
VIC WATERS: Gideon with a hell of a lucky grab there. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho refuses to let go and he is trying to pull his head out of the grasp if Gideon… GIDEON PULLS THE MASK DOWN OVER THE EYES OF PSYCHO AND PSYCHO LETS GO!
VIC WATERS: That is the disadvantage of wearing a mask. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon bounces off the ropes…. DROPKICK TO THE FACE OF PSYCHO! VIC WATERS: I can’t believe that Gideon is taking advantage of a blinded man. BRANDON JACOBS: In the heat of the battle, you need to take advantage of every advantage that you get. Gideon puts Psycho in a front face lock…. GIDEON PICKS PSYCHO UP IN A VERTICAL SUPLEX AND BOUNCES HIM OFF THE TOP ROPE…. GOURDBUSTER DOWN INTO THE MAT FACE FIRST!
VIC WATERS: The G-Clef! BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon hooks the left arm and locks in the necklock! PSYCHO IS SCREAMING IN PAIN!
VIC WATERS: How often do you see someone bust out a neck lock in this day and age? BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is putting pressure right on the back of the neck of Psycho… this is going to be awfully hard for Psycho to get out of.
VIC WATERS: What do you do here if you are Psycho? Do you give up the fall and face the uphill battle or do you cut your losses and do what is best for the other two falls?
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon has been nearly impossible to beat. I wouldn’t let Gideon get up 1-0 or Psycho is dead in the water. GIDEON IS YELLING AT PSYCHO TO GIVE IT!
VIC WATERS: Gideon is trying to get into Psycho’s head. If he could manage to crack someone as difficult as Psycho to submit with his godly like pain tolerance, he might to be able to put in that shadow of doubt and come in for the kill.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon lets go of the hold! GIDEON STARTS ELBOWING PSYCHO REPEATEDLY IN THE BACK OF THE NECK! Psycho is trying to crawl away…. PSYCHO GETS TO THE ROPES!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is doing the smart thing and getting away from Gideon when he has the momentum.
BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON GRABS THE FEET OF PSYCHO AND TRIES TO DRAG HIM BACK TO THE CENTER BUT PSYCHO HOLDS ON WITH BOTH HANDS TO THE BOTTOM ROPE! Gideon lets go and Psycho rolls to the outside to catch a breather.
VIC WATERS: Psycho needs some time outside to group. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon goes through the ropes to the outside…. PSYCHO GRABS THE LEG OF GIDEON AND PULLS HIM OUT OF THE RING AND GIDEON STUMBLES TO THE FLOOR! Gideon bangs his head on the mat on the floor!
VIC WATERS: Those mats aren’t all that thick, they are better than nothing, but it only provides you with an inch or so of padding.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho picks up Gideon…. PSYCHO RAMS THE BACK OF THE NECK OF GIDEON IN THE RING AND GIDEON GRABS THE BACK OF HIS NECK IN PAIN!
VIC WATERS: This is Psycho’s chance to finish him! BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is calling for both men to get back in the ring and Psycho rolls Gideon in. Psycho rolls in after him and goes after the ailing Gideon.
VIC WATERS: Psycho is just vicious today. Gideon is in for a hell of a fight. He needs to get away.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho drags Gideon to the center of the ring….. PSYCHO HITS HIM WITH A TIGER DRIVER AND GIDEON’S NECK CRUNCHES RIGHT INTO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Gideon is in bad shape, Psycho has him right where he wants him. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho picks Gideon off the mat…. FOR THE LOVE OF GOD! PSYCHO LOCKS IN THE FULL NELSON!
VIC WATERS: Come on Psycho! BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON IS YELLING OUT IN PAIN! Gideon is trying to power out…. but Psycho has it on tight! You can see by looking into Gideon’s eyes that he is an whole lot of pain….. GIDEON GIVES UP! .
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the first fall…. PSYCHO! There will now be a two minute break to setup the ring for the Four Corners of Hell Match!
BRANDON JACOBS: The officials are prying a smirking Psycho off of Gideon and telling him that if he doesn’t go into corner and wait while they set up the second fall. They are going to give his match to Gideon. Folks, we will take a short break and when we comeback, we will have the second fall of Gideon verses Psycho, A FOUR CORNERS OF HELL MATCH! STAY TUNED!
