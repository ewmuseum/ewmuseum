WFW Cherry Blossom Chaos - Eian vs. Starr

31 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 31 May 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back, sorry about that, we had some brief technical problems. VIC WATERS: The joys of being live on PPV… BRANDON JACOBS: This match will be quite interesting because the ref in this match has a history with Andre Eian, but he has sworn to WFW officials that he will call this match right down the line.
VIC WATERS: Tonight is a whole new chapter in Mike McGee’s career as a referee. Mike McGee had to reduce his schedule and hasn’t felt that he has enough time to properly train for matches but he is willing to dawn a referee shirt and be an official here in the WFW. McGee is a real company man and you can’t ask for a better representative of management out there in the ring.
BRANDON JACOBS: Are we talking about Mike McGee? He is a notorious cheater and suck up. I really doubt that he can referee a fair match considering all of history with Eian and I’m appalled that management would put a man like that in this position.
VIC WATERS: Johnny Starr isn’t the same man that he used to be. He wants a clean match right down the middle. Why do you think he would want McGee to cheat in favor of him? He is a flag waving moron pandering to the people.
BRANDON JACOBS: Is there something wrong with being proud of where you live? He believes in all that is good in North America and tonight he wants to take that first step towards winning the North American Title.
VIC WATERS: He is seven feet tall, he doesn’t need the help of all the humanoids out there. He can get by on his sheer size and strength instead of having to rely on the fans to give him an emotional high.
BRANDON JACOBS: Starr is a tremendous athlete and a hell of a fighter. He doesn’t need the fans, but never underestimate the value of the support of the WFW faithful which shows up and packs buildings all around the country. You can’t imagine the rush of adrenaline when 20,000 faithful are screaming for you to win and you are fighting and feeling that energy to drive you to new heights.
VIC WATERS: Doc Silver doesn’t need it and he is both a champion and a legend. Having the fans behind you is vastly overrated. You need to be free to do whatever is necessary to beat your opponent and if that makes breaking a few rules every now and then, so be it.
BRANDON JACOBS: You’ll never get it Vic. We can debate this for hours but you’ll still only see your point of view and won’t let the sun shine in. This should be a hell of a fight and I have no idea who will win. Let’s send this match to ringside and get the match underway!
::Real American blasts over the loud speaker to a huge pop, fans collectively turn their heads and look to the back::
RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, standing at over seven feet tall, hailing from Washington, DC…. MR. NORTH AMERICA…. JOHNNY STARR!!
::Johnny Starr walks down to the ring in a American flag mask, red and blue trunks, and red and white wrestling boots to a huge pop from the DC crowd::
:: Bawitdaba" by Kid Rock wails over the loud speaker:: RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing second, from Trondheim, Norway, the former XGW World Champion, THE SHADOW ANDRE EIAN!
::Andre Eian comes out to a pop and slowly enters the ring while keeping his eye on Starr::
::Mike McGee checks both guys for foreign objects:: BRANDON JACOBS: Eian and Starr are face to face! Both big men have a lot at stake here tonight on the road to the North America Title. One man will take a step forward, another two steps back.
VIC WATERS: I can’t help but notice that these fans hear sound like they are more behind Johnny Starr despite both being fan favorites.
BRANDON JACOBS: We are in Washington, DC so it should come as no surprise. EIAN LAYS INTO STARR WITH SEVERAL QUICK CHOPS! STARR IS TAKEN A BACK!
VIC WATERS: Those were so loud they sounded like gunshots. Eian isn’t holding anything back tonight.
BRANDON JACOBS: STARR FIRES BACK WITH HAYMAKERS OF HIS OWN! Eian starts really backwards! VIC WATERS: Those look like closed fists! BRANDON JACOBS: McGee is warning Starr to keep his fist open or he is going to be disqualified.
VIC WATERS: McGee is here to keep order and it looks like he is calling it right down the middle.
BRANDON JACOBS: Starr picks up Eian AND LAUNCHES HIM INTO THE CORNER! His back went in hard! What amazing power by Johnny Starr!
VIC WATERS: Starr spends a lot of time in the gym, but he has the unnatural advantage of being seven foot which means he can carry more muscle on his body naturally than most guys that step into a wrestling ring. Starr looked like he was throwing around a kid in there and Eian is 6’6.
BRANDON JACOBS: Starr climbs up to the second rope… HE STARTS LAYING INTO HIM WITH PUNCHES AS THE CROWD COUNTS ALONG! 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9….. TEN!
VIC WATERS: LOOK! LOOK! McGee is trying to pull Starr down! The crowd doesn’t seem to like McGee’s efforts to keep Starr under control.
BRANDON JACOBS: This is wrestling, LET THEM FIGHT! Starr starts kicking away at Eian to keep him from getting up out of the corner on his own volition.
VIC WATERS: When you get into that wrestling ring, one of the biggest parts of winning is being able to maintain control even if it is a simple thing like not letting your opponent get up.
BRANDON JACOBS: Starr picks Eian up… OVERHEAD THROW INTO THE MIDDLE OF THE RING! The fans here are on their feet excited about the mighty power of Starr!
VIC WATERS: Alright, that even impressed me, treating Eian like he was some sort of ragdoll. Don’t think it isn’t getting into Eian’s head either.
BRANDON JACOBS: Eian is slowly trying to get up… STARR LOCKS IN THE WAIST LOCK… BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX! Eian crashed down hard on his back!
VIC WATERS: On a guy the size of Eian, working over the back is crucial because it prevents him from doing the power moves that a guy his size is capable of doing.
BRANDON JACOBS: Starr is trying to turn Eian over on his back to do some more damage but Eian is fighting being turned over…. STARR BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES… DOUBLE FOOT STOMP ON THE STOMACH OF EIAN!
VIC WATERS: I don’t care what size you are, that takes the wind right out of you and makes it difficult to move. When a seven foot guy crashes down on you with all his weight, that could kill you.
BRANDON JACOBS: Eian is grabbing his stomach, he rolls out of the ring to buy himself sometime and he is gasping for air. Hopefully the double foot stomp didn’t break a rib.
VIC WATERS: Eian needs as much time as possible to recover here, he isn’t going to last if he continues taking a beating like this without anytime to recover. Starr is just showing these fans how badly he wants to win this match for them right here in Washington, DC.
BRANDON JACOBS: Eian is laying on his back just trying to catch his breath… WAIT A SECOND! Starr is on the apron and he is going to go for a double foot stomp from the apron!
VIC WATERS: If Starr hits this, Eian should just lay on the outside and take the count out and be thankful that he walks out here alive.
BRANDON JACOBS: EIAN ROLLS OUT OF THE WAY AND STARR MISSES! Starr goes to pick Eian off the ring mats at ringside… EIAN RAMS HIM IN THE STOMACH!
VIC WATERS: Eian is dishing out some payback for what happened earlier and it is about damn time.
BRANDON JACOBS: EIAN RAMS STARR IN THE STOMACH AGAIN! Starr looks like he is winded! VIC WATERS: Starr keeps himself in tremendous shape, but a guy his size can’t afford to take blows like that in a match of any great length because it will wear his energy down quickly. He is no normal man.
BRANDON JACOBS: Eian picks Starr up…. FLAPJACK! Starr crashed down stomach first on the steel barrier at ringside!
VIC WATERS: Look at those fans, they are patting Starr on the back and telling him to continue on. Like anyone in their right mind wants to hear that mush in the middle of battle…
BRANDON JACOBS: Eian pulls Starr off the guardrail and STARTS CHOKING HIM WITH THE TOP PART OF THE MEDAL RING BARRIER! WHAT THE HELL IS THE REF DOING WHILE ALL THIS IS HAPPENING?!
VIC WATERS: The ref is counting, letting them fight like you wanted them to earlier. BRANDON JACOBS: This is totally uncalled for though, he could cause serious damage to the throat of Starr if he doesn’t break this up soon. A FAN THREW HIS COKE INTO THE FACE OF EIAN AND EIAN BREAKS THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: That fan should be ejected from the ringside area and arrested, he has no right to throw things at the wrestlers. It just shows the kind of low life fans who show up when you promote a free show in Washington, DC.
BRANDON JACOBS: He shouldn’t have thrown the Coke at him, but we are proud at all of the WFW fans who came here today to support the promotion on our very first PPV. He was just trying to help his hero out.
VIC WATERS: Eian rolls back in and rolls out to make sure he stops the slow count…. Hell, even my grandmother could beat that count back in!
BRANDON JACOBS: Smart move by Eian, while he was upset about what happened, he was still listening to the ref and he didn’t let it distract him. Eian grabs Starr and he rolls him back into the ring to do some more damage.
VIC WATERS: Look at what Eian is doing… he moving Starr into the center of the ring, a submission move must be coming up.
BRANDON JACOBS: Eian picks Starr up off the mat….. BEAR HUG! Eian is going to try to sap the strength from the big man!
VIC WATERS: Look at Starr wincing in pain, it looks like those shots on the floor really did a lot of damage. I love it. Eian waited for Starr to make a mistake and then he capitalized on it like every good wrestler should.
BRANDON JACOBS: Starr looks like he is fading fast but these fans keep on chanting USA, USA, USA to try to get behind him! Starr is waving his arms trying to get this crowd going… STARR TRIES TO BREAK FREE ! STARR HEADBUTTS EIAN…. BUT EIAN REFUSES TO LET GO! Eian just locks it in tight and you can see his arms shaking as he is using all the strength he has in order to keep Starr in the hold! STARR TRIES THE HEADBUTT AGAIN… EIAN BREAKS AND THIS TIME HE IS BUSTED OPEN!
VIC WATERS: Starr is winded, but those headbutts that Eian took has him dazed. BRANDON JACOBS: Starr picks up Eian…. TILT A WHILE BACKBREAKER! Starr covers! 1--------------------2--------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Eian took some nasty shots, but Starr needs to take more out of him before he goes for another pinfall attempt.
BRANDON JACOBS: Starr picks Eian off the mat and goes for a waistlock… HE IS TRYING FOR A BELLY TO BACK IT LOOKS LIKE…. NO REVERSAL!
VIC WATERS: Eian needs to becareful not to take an elbow to the jaw here or he will really be out.
BRANDON JACOBS: EIAN DUCKS THE ELBOW! EIAN DELIEVERS A HIGH RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX ON STARR!
VIC WATERS: Eian didn’t get all of it though, the pain in his stomach prevented him from getting the elevation he would usually get on that hold.
BRANDON JACOBS: Starr really landed hard on his head on that… the fans are looking on to see if Starr is okay.
VIC WATERS: A dazed Eian is getting up, it looks he isn’t done! BRANDON JACOBS: Eian looks like he is getting ready to spear Starr…. Starr is getting up… HE TURNS AROUND AND EIAN NAILS HIM WITH FORCE IN THE STOMACH!
VIC WATERS: What a spear! BRANDON JACOBS: Starr is clutching his stomach, he doesn’t look good. Eian looks like he is headed for the top to do more damage!
VIC WATERS: You have to strike when the iron is hot, this is Eian’s chance to win the match when he has the seven footer down.
BRANDON JACOBS: Starr is getting up…. EIAN FLIES OFF WITH A MOONSAULT PRESS! OH MY GOD!! STARR SOMEHOW MANAGED TO CATCH EIAN COMING OFF THE TOP!
VIC WATERS: NO DAMNIT NO! BRANDON JACOBS: STARR MODIFIES HIM IN HIS ARMS AND PILEDRIVERS EIAN INTO THE MAT! HE COVERS! 1---------------------2-------------------------EIAN GETS FOOT ON THE ROPE! HE WAS TOO CLOSE AND STARR IS AGGRAVATED!
VIC WATERS: Starr is looking at the top rope, what is he thinking of doing?! BRANDON JACOBS: Starr picks up Eian and places him on the top rope and climbs up to the second turnbuckle… IT LOOKS LIKE WE ARE GOING TO SEE A GOOD OLD FASHIONED SUPLEXPLEX! IF HE NAILS THIS, THIS MATCH IS ALL OVER!
VIC WATERS: Come on Eian come on! BRANDON JACOBS: THE BLOODY EIAN IS TRYING TO FIGHT OUT OF THE FRONT FACE LOCK! VIC WATERS: PUNCH HIM DAMNIT! PUNCH HIM! BRANDON JACOBS: EIAN NAILS HIM WITH A FOREARM SMASH AND STARR BREAKS THE FRONT FACE LOCK! Eian grabs Starr in a headlock…. SHADOW DRIVER DDT FROM THE TOP!
VIC WATERS: HE HIT IT! BRANDON JACOBS: Eian goes for the cover…. 1-------------------2-----------------WAIT HIS FOOT ON THE ROPES-- 3!
VIC WATERS: MIKE MCGEE WAS LOOKING AT THE SHOULDERS OF STARR AND NEVER LOOKED UP TO SEE THE FOOT!
BRANDON JACOBS: Eian kicks his foot off the ropes and the ref raises Eian’s hand! This is a travesty of justice! “Mr. North America” Johnny Starr got screwed out of his North American Title shot right here in DC at the hands of those two men, Eian and McGee! Eian better remember that payback is a #####.
RING ANNOUNCER: THE WINNER OF THE MATCH AND THE MAN WHO WILL FACE THE NORTH AMERICAN TITLE HOLDER IN HIS FIRST DEFENSE AFTER CHERRY BLOSSOM CHAOS, “THE SHADOW” ANDRE EIAN!
BRANDON JACOBS: That just makes me sick, to have such a tremendous match ruined by a finish like that. If there was a real ref in there instead of some wannabe like McGee, he would have gotten the job done.
VIC WATERS: Excuses, excuses, excuses, sometimes a call goes to the other team. Live with it.
BRANDON JACOBS: We will be back next with the North American Title ladder match between Manson, Rat Fink, and the “Viewer’s Choice” Scotty Michaels! Stay tuned folks!
