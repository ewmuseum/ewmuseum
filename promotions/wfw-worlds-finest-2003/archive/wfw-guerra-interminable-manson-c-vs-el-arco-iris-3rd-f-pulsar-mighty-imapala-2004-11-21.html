MANSON ESCAPES THE CLUTCHES OF THE REFEREE AND HE DIVES ONTO EL ARCO IRIS AND STARTS POUNDING AWAY AT HIM WITH CLOSED FISTS RIGHT IN THE HEAD!

EL ARCO IRIS IS JUST SAPPING THE ENERGY OUT OF MANSON WITH THIS AND MANSON MAY BE TOO FAR AWAY FROM THE ROPES TO DO ANYTHING ABOUT IT!

JONATHAN MARX: I can’t remember the last time Manson tried to beat another man into submission like this.

The ref is really ripping Manson a new one and Manson can’t do anything or he risks losing his WFW World Heavyweight Title.
JONATHAN MARX: Manson barely said anything, I wonder what bug is up this guys bum. VIC WATERS: Referees deserve 100% respect. They should not settle for anything less. BRANDON JACOBS: Meanwhile… El Arco Iris is getting up to his feet… EL ARCO IRIS TELLS THE REF TO MOVE OUT OF THE WAY AND HE CHARGES AND CATCHES MANSON BY SURPRISE WITH THE SHINING WIZARD RIGHT IN THE FACE AND MANSON GOES UP AND OVER THE TOP ROPE TO THE FLOOR!
JONATHAN MARX: Manson took a really nasty spill. VIC WATERS: The referee was blocking his line of site and El Arco Iris just ambushed him with that one.

CROWD: ARCO IRIS! ARCO IRIS! ARCO IRIS! JONATHAN MARX: This crowd appreciates the extremes that these two men are going through for the gold.

JONATHAN MARX: It is all about shifting your weight properly. VIC WATERS: After taking that superkick earlier, who knows what kind of condition Manson’s head is in already… a german suplex could scramble him.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is doubling up on his effort…. EL ARCO IRIS PUTS SOME EXTRA OOMPH IN IT AND HE SENDS MANSON FLYING…. MANSON IS ABLE TO FLIP OVER AND LAND ON HIS FEET!
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris with a little too much power on that. . VIC WATERS: It was all Manson, he was able to change his direction in midair before he could go splat.
BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS TURNS AROUND AND HE CHARGES MANSON WITH A RUNNING DROPKICK….. MANSON TURNS AROUND AND MOVES OUT OF THE WAY BEFORE EL ARCO IRIS CAN CONNECT!
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris has the best dropkick in the business, but he was just missed. VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris is grabbing his leg, he looks like he is pain. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON IRISH WHIPS EL ARCO IRIS INTO THE CORNER AND EL ARCO IRIS GOES IN SHOULDER FIRST!

JONATHAN MARX: That was an NFL like tackle. VIC WATERS: Manson is going to lay on a Ron Artest like beating. JONATHAN MARX: He is not in the NFL, but that is fitting. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON RAMS ANOTHER SHOULDER RIGHT INTO EL ARCO IRIS AND A WINDED EL ARCO IRIS FALLS DOWN TO THE MAT!
JONATHAN MARX: I thought I heard a rib go crunch. VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris may need to change his pacing in the match from now on in order to avoid getting winded.

Referee Umberto Gonzalez goes down for the count! 1------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------2---------------------------------------------------------- EL ARCO IRIS MANAGES TO KICK OUT! EL ARCO IRIS FLIES OFF WITH A PERFECT MOONSAULT BUT MANSON ROLLS OUT OF THE WAY AND EL ARCO IRIS LANDS ON HIS SHOULDER!
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris needs to get out of the ring and fast before Manson can get a hold of him.
