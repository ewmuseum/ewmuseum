BOMB Voyage: Dice/Lane, BARTLEBY STRIKES AGAIN!

11 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 11 June 2004 JONATHAN MARX: Folks, welcome back and get ready for an explosive rematch between two men who made an impressive debut here last week… Michael Dice is going to go one on one with Michael Lane.
VIC WATERS: I was sort of surprised with how Michael Dice took it to Lane who is known for his toughness but I’m sold on both men now. This should be a hell of a fight.
JONATHAN MARX: Wait a second, I’m getting word…. THERE HAS BEEN AN ATTACK! We are trying to get cameras back there.
VIC WATERS: WHO HAS BEEN ATTACKED?! JONATHAN MARX: We are about to find out! ::A camera man enters the cabin where the commotion took place and pans down on Michael Dice who is laying on the floor in a pool of his own blood next to a cracked Champaign bottle… on the body of Michael Dice is a card which simply reads Bartleby::
JONATHAN MARX: BARTLEBY HAS STRUCK AGAIN! VIC WATERS: Look, there is a message on the wall! ::camera pans up on the wall to a message written in Dice’s blood, ”’Who LOVE too much, hate in the like extreme, And both the golden mean alike condemn.’ – Alexander Pope… You will not be able to SURVIVE me.”
JONATHAN MARX: It has been awhile since I’ve graduated, but that quote looks like it referenced the demise of both LOVE and Jacobs.
VIC WATERS: What kind of sadistic fool would take LOVE on head on? That it a death wish. JONATHAN MARX: The medics are coming in to help Michael Dice, he’ll be okay, but it looks like Mike Lane will advance to his match with Jared Wells without having to left a finger.
VIC WATERS: This is a hell of a break for Lane because he’ll still be fresh by the time he faces Wells. I smell a new BAD World Heavyweight Champion Tonight.
JONATHAN MARX: We are going to take a short commercial break and when we comeback, Anarky is going to go one on one with the seven foot three Iron Lion…. STAY TUNED!
