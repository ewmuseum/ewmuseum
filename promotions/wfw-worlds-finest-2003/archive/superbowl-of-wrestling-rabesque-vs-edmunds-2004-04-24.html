Superbowl of Wrestling: Rabesque vs. Edmunds

24 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 24 April 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: It is time for what looks to be one of the best technical matches of evening as the God of technical wrestling Jean Rabesque goes head to head with WFW’s own Sean Edmunds. Any predictions for the match Vic?
VIC WATERS: I’m going with Edmunds. BRANDON JACOBS: Whoa, what? VIC WATERS: Rabesque is something like 11-0 against Edmunds, right? BRANDON JACOBS: Correct. VIC WATERS: But the laws of probability have to catch up to Rabesque eventually, right? RIGHT?
BRANDON JACOBS: Technically yes, but Rabesque is Rabesque and Edmunds has been going through one of the worst stretches of his career. I’m always one to root for an underdog but Edmunds doesn’t really have much of a chance. He’ll probably do what he has done on several other occasions and just come up a bit short.
VIC WATERS: All I know is one dollar on Edmunds can win me a months’ worth of Poland Spring Bottles.
BRANDON JACOBS: Knowing the way you drink, that is a hell of a lot of money. VIC WATERS: It is…. SO GO EDMUNDS! BRANDON JACOBS: I hope you are right, but you really don’t have a good track record on these things. Remember the A-Rod jersey…
VIC WATERS: ::hangs head:: BRANDON JACOBS: Sorry. But with all the personal problems Edmunds have been happening lately, it is going to take a lot to get the job done against Rabesque tonight. Maybe you are right though. Tonight could finally be the night. There is only one way to find out. Folks, let’s send this one down to ringside and get this one underway!
(CUEUP: "Shapes of Things" by The Jeff Healy Band) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, a GLCW Legend and a Leader in the NFW South, a wrestler proclaimed by some as the god of technical wrestling, JEAN RABESQUE!
::Jean Rabesque comes down to the boos of the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: "Brain Stew" by Green Day) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, currently residing in his summer residence in Escandido, California, the former WFW World Heavyweight Champion….. 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds!
:: Edmunds comes down with Sherri and enters the ring to a loud ovation from the crowd:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: This is the first match of the night between two wrestlers born in Canada so this officially meets our Canadian content requirements for airing in Canada.
VIC WATERS: Go Canadians! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds and Rabesque meet in the center of the ring and they are talking back and forth. EDMUNDS HITS RABESQUE WITH AN OPEN HAND SLAP RIGHT ACROSS THE FACE!
VIC WATERS: You can sense Edmunds is a bit agitated over what Rabesque has been saying in recent weeks.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque counters with a hard chop right across the chest of Edmunds! Edmunds grimaces in pain… RABESQUE HITS HIM WITH ANOTHER STIFF SHOT THE CHEST!
VIC WATERS: Rabesque is showing Edmunds that if he wants to go the more aggressive route, Rabesque can go with him toe to toe.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque whips Edmunds off the ropes… RABESQUE TAKES EDMUNDS DOWN WITH A DROP TOE HOLD AND IMMEDIATELY GOES TO LOCK IN A HALF BOSTON CRAB ON EDMUNDS’ RIGHT LEG!
VIC WATERS: Rabesque is such a smart worker because he is constantly working towards his finisher with the moves he does in the ring. Everything he does, he does to build to his figure four league lock which no one in the business can top.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is squirming and he is trying to get to the ropes…. IT LOOKS LIKE HE IS MAKING SOME PROGRESS…. Rabesque pulls him away from the ropes to the boos of the crowd.
VIC WATERS: That is just smart wrestling… BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is repeatedly ramming his left leg into the right leg of Rabesque in an attempt to get him to break the hold but Rabesque won’t budge!
VIC WATERS: Rabesque has face some of the toughest wrestlers in the world and he has taken his share of pain. It would take a hell of a lot of pain to get to him.
BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS GRABS THE SHIRT OF THE REF AS HE IS ASKING IF HE WANTS TO GIVE UP OR NOT! Rabesque lets go of the hold and pries Edmunds off the ref.
VIC WATERS: That was a pure desperation move by Edmunds. BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque picks Edmunds up off the mat…. RUSSIAN LEG SWEEP BACK DOWN TO THE MAT! Rabesque hooks the right leg of Edmunds and makes the cover! 1--------------------------------------------------2---------------------- KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: If Rabesque hasn’t worked over the leg, that may have only been a one count but because he hooked the worked over right leg, that added a full second and a half of damage.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque picks up Edmunds… ANOTHER SMALL PACKAGE! 1---------------------------------2--------------------- EDMUNDS KICKS OUT AGAIN!
VIC WATERS: He is going to wear Edmunds out by doing all of this. BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque picks up a winded Edmunds off the mat… RABESQUE SNAP SUPLEXS EDMUNDS DOWN SLIGHTLY ON HIS SIDE SO HE LANDS ON HIS WORKED OVER RIGHT LEG!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is clutching that leg and we aren’t even a full ten minutes into this match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque pulls Edmunds towards the corner and he slides to the outside. He grabs the legs of EDMUNDS AND PULLS HIS LEGS THROUGH AROUND THE POLE!
VIC WATERS: This can’t be good. BRANDON JACOBS: RABESQUE RAMS THE SIDE OF THE RIGHT LEG INTO THE STEEL POLE! VIC WATERS: Oh man… BRANDON JACOBS: RABESQUE RAMS EDMUNDS’S LEG IN AGAIN TO THE BOOS OF THE CROWD! VIC WATERS: Rabesque is just trying to get this over as quickly as possible. BRANDON JACOBS: RABESQUE IS GRABBING THE LEGS OF EDMUNDS AND HE IS HOOKING ON A FIGURE FOUR LEG LOCK AROUND THE POST!
VIC WATERS: Rabesque could break his leg this way! BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is comes out to give a five count! ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! Rabesque breaks before the five but the damage has been done.
VIC WATERS: Edmunds looks like he is an awful lot of pain. BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque rolls Edmunds back into the ring and he rolls in after him. Rabesque drags Edmunds to the center of the ring…. RABESQUE IS CALLING FOR HIS FINISHER!
VIC WATERS: If he locks this on after the damage of the figure four around the ring post, it is over!
BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque goes to pick up one of the legs…. EDMUNDS KICKS RABESQUE RIGHT IN THE FACE! An irritated Rabesque goes right back after Edmunds… EDMUNDS GRABS THE HAIR OF RABESQUE AND CATCHES HIM IN A SMALL PACKAGE! 1-----------------------------------------------------------------------2------------------- RABESQUE KICKS OUT!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds desperately doesn’t want to go down for his third straight loss. BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque bounces off the ropes to drop a knee on the head of Edmunds…. BUT EDMUND GETS HIS HANDS UP TO BLOCK IT AND MANAGES TO HOOKS THE RIGHT LEG AND HEADSCISSOR RABESQUE!
VIC WATERS: This is a real tripped out move, Edmunds is pulling back on right leg of Rabesque to do damage to the thigh muscle while head scissoring Rabesque head which means however Rabesque tries to break out, he either does damage to his back from trying to break free or if he gives in, it works over his thigh.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is reaching back into his playbook because he knows that because of the extensive damage Rabesque has done to that right leg that it can’t support the weight for a lot of his offense.
VIC WATERS: Even with the way the headscissors if, Edmunds is putting all the strain of holding Rabesque on his left leg in order to keep from doing any more damage to his right.
BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is going down to check on Rabesque but he is repeatedly saying no. Rabesque is very thankful to be on the offense for most of the match because he is a lot fresher than Edmunds is right now and he can endure the pain.
VIC WATERS: Rabesque is trying to reach out and move himself closer to the ropes in an attempt to break the hold without trying to move forward too much and put the strain on his back.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque is getting closer…. Edmunds hears the crowd react to Rabesque almost getting there and he breaks the hold and PULLS RABESQUE BACK TO THE CENTER!
VIC WATERS: The crowd is all important twelfth man in this match. BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS LIFTS UP THE LEG OF RABESQUE AND REPEATEDLY RAMS THE RIGHT KNEE INTO MAT!
VIC WATERS: You don’t think Edmunds is going to try to beat Rabesque with his own hold, do you?
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds may be cocky, but he wouldn’t attempt to do that….. WAIT, MAYBE I SPOKE TOO SOON! Edmunds is turning over Rabesque…. HE IS LOCKING ON THE FIGURE FOUR IN THE CENTER OF THE RING!
VIC WATERS: YES! TAP RABESQUE TAP! BRANDON JACOBS: This may backfire on Edmunds if Rabesque ever gets out of this. Rabesque is going to be mad as hell.
VIC WATERS: Rabesque looks like he is an awful lot of pain… we’ve seen before what a figure four can do to someone, look at how Melton got the current CSWA World Heavyweight Champion Dan Ryan who is a giant of a man to submit.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque is screaming as these fans are clearly on their feet supporting Edmunds to beat Rabesque right here…. The ref goes to ask Rabesque if he wants to give up but he is shaking his head no!
VIC WATERS: Look at Edmunds… he is reaching to the ropes… BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is checking on Rabesque…. EDMUNDS IS GRABBING THE ROPES FOR LEVERAGE ON THE FIGURE FOUR AND RABESQUE IS SCREAMING SOMEONE PUT HIS NUTS A VICE!
VIC WATERS: If ever a ref could miss something, now is the time…. BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque is screaming for the ref to look at Edmunds…. Edmunds lets go BUT THE REF NOTICES THE ROPES SHAKING SO HE IS CALLING FOR BREAK!
VIC WATERS: It could have just been the wind, there is no call for that! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds has finally managed to put this match back on equal footing twenty minutes into the match. Edmunds is pull up the limping Rabesque…. EDMUNDS PUSHES RABEQUE INTO THE CORNER AND STARTS REPEATEDLY RAMMING HIS SHOULDER INTO HIS MIDSECTION!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is finally starting the process of softening Edmunds up for the Northern Lights.
BRANDON JACOBS: One has to wonder if it is too late… EDMUNDS CRUNCHES ANOTHER SHOULDER INTO RABESQUE HARD AND RABESQUE FALLS DOWN CLUTCHING HIS RIBS!
VIC WATERS: The shoulder is one of the hardest parts of the body, Rabesque is feeling that right now.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is going up to the top rope as Rabesque is struggling to get up…. Rabesque is up and EDMUNDS FLIES OFF AND NAILS HIM WITH A REVERSE CROSS BODY BLOCK!
VIC WATERS: Again, right onto the ribs of Rabesque! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds goes for the cover! 1----------------------------------2--------------- RABESQUE GETS THE SHOULDER UP! This match is turning into a much more competitive match than it was at the beginning, if Edmunds keeps this up, he may be able to finally pull this off.
VIC WATERS: Don’t jinx him. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds picks up Rabesque… STOMACH BREAKER! Edmunds drives the knee right into the midsection of Rabesque!
VIC WATERS: Come on Edmunds! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds turns Rabesque onto his back and he is going back up to the top rope… . Edmunds starts climbing the turnbuckle and Rabesque is motionless…. EDMUNDS IS PERCHED ON THE TOP ROPE… HE FLIES WITH THE ELBOW DROP BY RABESQUE MOVES OUT OF THE WAY AND EDMUNDS LANDS ELBOW FIRST ON THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: No damnit! BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque gets up to his knees and he grabs the injured elbow of Edmunds and BEGINS TO REPEATEDLY NAIL HIM WITH FOREARMS!
VIC WATERS: That has to hurt, but what can Rabesque follow this up with in order to capitalize, there are very few moves that work over the elbow.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque is a master technician. He’ll think of something…. RABESQUE IS UP AND HE CHICKEN WINGS THE INJURED ARM OF EDMUNDS AND EDMUNDS IS YELPING IN PAIN!
VIC WATERS: The dreaded chicken wing of death! BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque is trying to drag Edmunds to the center but Edmunds is digging his feet to avoid it!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds won’t be able to last in this for long, he really fell hard onto his elbow.
BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS IS TRYING TO ELBOW RABESQUE WITH HIS LEFT ARM TO BREAK IT! VIC WATERS: Edmunds is throwing those elbows wildly back at Rabesque! BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS FINALLY CONNECTS AND RABESQUE LETS GO OF THE HOLD! VIC WATERS: Edmunds hit him right on the jaw. BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES FOR A FLYING FOREARM…. RABESQUE CHARGES AND KICKS HIM IN THE MIDSECTION FOLDING EDMUNDS OVER! Rabesque picks up Edmunds…. PILEDRIVER!
VIC WATERS: If we were in Mexico, Rabesque would be disqualified by now. BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque quickly picks up Edmunds and locks him in a full nelson to take advantage of the injured neck!
VIC WATERS: I’m kind of surprised he didn’t go for a pin fall after the piledriver, but Rabesque knows what he is doing so I’m not going to second guess him.
BRANDON JACOBS: RABESQUE IS SWINGING EDMUNDS AROUND LIKE A RAGDOLL AND HE IS PUTTING ALL HE HAS INTO PUTTING PRESSURE ON THE BACK OF THE NECK OF EDMUNDS!
VIC WATERS: Rabesque is deceivingly strong for his frame. He isn’t built like a bodybuilder, but he really backs some punch.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is checking to see if Edmunds wants to give up but Edmunds still wants to fight. With everything Edmunds has been through in recent weeks, who could fault him for giving up, but the stubborn bastard keeps on fighting even when all the odds look like they are against him.
VIC WATERS: One of the greatest attributes of a champion is that he continues to fight even when the fight looks lost. It takes a certain level of stupidity in order to reach greatness.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque is about to make it a perfect 12-0 if Edmunds can’t manage to get out of this hold. EDMUNDS LOOKS LIKE HE IS READY TO PASS OUT!
VIC WATERS: With all that has been happening lately, his stamina can’t be at its best right now. We are at over the thirty minute mark now.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque lets go of the hold! VIC WATERS: Why would he do that? Edmunds looked like he was almost out. BRANDON JACOBS: He most have thought it would be easier to get a pin than to submission on Edmunds…. WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES SHERRI DOWN TO RINGSIDE!
VIC WATERS: Didn’t Edmunds tell her to stay in the back? BRANDON JACOBS: Sherri isn’t about to let her meal ticket get hurt at the hands of Rabesque…. RABESQUE HITS EDMUNDS WITH THE DDT! This should be over!
VIC WATERS: With all the neck work, I can’t see how Edmunds could kick out. BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque makes the cover on Edmunds… SHERRI JUMPS ONTO THE APRON AND THE REF GOES OVER TO TELL HER TO GET DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Rabesque is furious at Sherri…. BRANDON JACOBS: RABESQUE BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND BUMPS THE REF INTO SHERRI AND SHERRI FALLS OFF THE APRON TO THE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: That should be a DQ! BRANDON JACOBS: The referee turns around and Rabesque is apologizing to the ref telling him that he didn’t see him and Sherri there.
VIC WATERS: Rabesque must be as blind as a bat. BRANDON JACOBS: An angry Edmunds is beginning to get up…. RABESQUE DROPS THE ELBOW ON HIM TO KEEP HIM DOWN ON THE GROUND!
VIC WATERS: Rabesque isn’t giving Edmunds any air at all… BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque is dragging Edmunds into the corner… Rabesque is going for his patented middle rope elbow! Rabesque is climbing to the second rope….. SHERRI IS UP AND SHE GRABS THE FOOT OF RABESQUE TO KEEP HIM FROM DIVING OFF!
VIC WATERS: The ref is checking on Edmunds, he doesn’t see any of this. BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS IS GETTING UP AND HE IS HEADED TO THE CORNER! Rabesque tries to get away from Sherri by climbing up to the top rope but Edmunds is coming up the ropes behind him… EDMUNDS HITS RABESQUE WITH A TOP ROPE SUPERPLEX ONTO THE MAT BELOW!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds needs to capitalize on this! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds crawls over and he is going for the cover! 1-----------------------------------------2------------------------ RABESQUE GETS THE SHOULDER UP!
VIC WATERS: NO! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds picks up Rabesque and drags him over towards the corner…. SWINGING NECKBREAKER! Edmunds plants Rabesque down in the corner.
VIC WATERS: After the superplex and the swinging neckbreaker, Rabesque has to be out of pretty badly. This is the time to hit the elbow drop!
BRANDON JACOBS: I don’t know if I’d go for the move twice…. Edmunds is climbing up to the top rope….. BIG ELBOW DROP OF RABESQUE AND THIS TIME IT CONNECTS!
VIC WATERS: Thank god… BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds collapses in exhaustion. The ref is putting a count on both men! …..TEN! ……..NINE! ………EIGHT! ……..SEVEN! ……SIX! Edmunds is getting back up…
VIC WATERS: Probably too much time has passed for Edmunds to go for a cover… BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is picking Rabesque off of the mat…. HE WHIPS EDMUNDS OFF THE ROPES….. EDMUNDS IS GOING FOR AN INVERTED ATOMIC DROP…. RABESQUE LIMPING POPS HIM WITH AN ELBOW RIGHT TO THE FACE!
VIC WATERS: That was so loud it almost sounded like it cracked his jaw. BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque kicks the dazed Edmunds in the midsection… RABESQUE IS GOING FOR THE FISHERMAN SUPLEX…. HE HITS IT! 1------------------------------------------------------2---------------------------- -----EDMUNDS KICKS OUT JUST IN THE NICK OF TIME! Rabesque is complaining to the referee that it should be a three.
VIC WATERS: Look, Sherri taking something out of her cleavage… SHE IS THROWING EDMUNDS THE BRASSKNUCKLES! A dazed Edmunds grabs it and puts it around his hand.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rabesque goes to pickup Edmunds… EDMUNDS HITS HIM WITH THE KNUCKS RIGHT IN THE FACE AND RABESQUE STAGGERS BACKWARDS!
VIC WATERS: How did Rabesque not go down after a shot like that?! BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS GETS UP AND HE HITS RABESQUE WITH A ANOTHER SHOT AND A DAZED RABESQUE STILL WON’T GO DOWN!
VIC WATERS: The lights are on but nobody is home. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds knees him in the stomach….. NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX! The ref is going down for the count! 1----------------------------------------2--------------------------------3!
VIC WATERS: HE DID IT! RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match in forty four minutes and fifty seconds…. SIMPLY SENSATION SEAN EDMUNDS!
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is slipping the knucks in the tights of Rabesque so the ref doesn’t find them when he raises his hand. I’m glad to see a WFW guy come home with the win, but Rabesque was robbed there after a hell of a match against Edmunds.
CROWD: EDMUNDS! EDMUNDS! EDMUNDS! EDMUNDS! VIC WATERS: 1-11 baby… Edmunds finally ended years of frustration by finally pinning Rabesque.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds may not be so lucky next time he faces Rabesque. Folks, we have to take a commercial break but when we comeback, Copycat will go one on one with Dan Ryan…. STAY TUNED!
