WFW Road To Glory: Introduction

10 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 10 March 2004 ::Camera pans in on a moonlit night at Disney California Adventure in Anaheim, California where a capacity crowd has formed at Paradise Pier for Road To Glory::
BRANDON JACOBS: If there is a more beautiful place in March than Southern California, it is hard to imagine. Mid sixties at 7:30 at night, gentle breeze, no humidity… I love this place. Welcome everyone to WFW Road To Glory.
VIC WATERS: I love these paid vacations. Plus tonight while this is a very important show, there will be only two matches so my voice will still be in tact by the end of the night.
BRANDON JACOBS: If we play our cards right, we could be out of here by 11PM for the Electrical Parade.
VIC WATERS: Did you know that the theme song for the Main Street Electrical Parade for the intro of the very first Michinoku Pro show ever? It even had a Battle Royal on it.
BRANDON JACOBS: I do. I don’t know what it is, but being here makes me feel like a kid again. A week at Disneyland capped off with the best in WFW battling it out for who will have their chance to shine at the Superbowl of Wrestling in the main event against the winner of the World Title match between Sean Edmunds and Alex Wylde.
VIC WATERS: This is the show of shows. Talent is coming from all over the country to be a part of this show and there may even be a dream match or two. Being in the main event will mean a huge pay day to the winner and a chance to win the WFW World Heavyweight Title with everyone’s eyes focused on the WFW at their one year anniversary show.
BRANDON JACOBS: There is an interesting story about the Rumble, let me bring in Marx into the booth here for a second.
:VIC WATERS: Good lord. ::Marx comes in and shakes Vic and Jacob’s hands and sits down and puts on his headset:: JONATHAN MARX: Good evening gentlemen and with you Vic, I use that term loosely. VIC WATERS: It is always a pleasure to see you too Jon. BRANDON JACOBS: Jonathan, you were telling me earlier that you were in attendance at the meeting of the board of directors when they came to a decision to do a Rumble match in order to determine who would get the World Title shot at the Superbowl of Wrestling. Can you tell us all about it?
JONATHAN MARX: While I can’t tell you who the four board of directors are because my father would have my hide, let me give you a little summary. Basically the four old men were sitting around the board table trying to figure out who should get the World Title shot. My dad was arguing for Copycat, his best friend was arguing for Manson, the big guy was arguing for Michaels, and the small guy sporting the El Arco Iris t-shirt was trying to get them to go for Gideon, Felix Red, El Segundo or El Arco Iris.
VIC WATERS: No Anarky? JONATHAN MARX: The board has other plans for Anarky. Tact and Wells didn’t come up because they wanted to give the chance to someone who has been in the company for the majority of the year.
VIC WATERS: Understand. JONATHAN MARX: The debate got very heated and everyone started yelling at each other. Veins popping out of their head… at their age, I thought one of them was going to have a stroke. Finally my dad steps up and says he has had enough, he has an idea. They are going to settle it with a game of poker and before rumors get started, no my father isn’t Doc Silver, anyways the small guy starts handing everyone their ass and everyone is enjoying themselves with several adult beverages.
BRANDON JACOBS: Liquor and booking don’t mix. JONATHAN MARX: The small guy is cleaning up and before you know it, he is winning all the title shots away from his four partners and by the end of the night he had all of them and as a result, the small guy would be allowed to pick who got the WFW World Heavyweight Title shot and everyone asks him who is he going to pick. The small guy stands up with his beer and he slurs what sounds like “El” and passes out on the ground.
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris?! JONATHAN MARX: It could have been but even I wasn’t sure. So my dad walks over to his best friend, “He said all, right?” and that is how the board of directors agreed on the Rumble here tonight.
BRANDON JACOBS: God bless alcohol. VIC WATERS: Amen to that… JONATHAN MARX: Quite frankly, even if they didn’t do it sober, I think they made the right decision. There are so many men in this company which have been outstanding so all of them deserve a chance to compete here tonight for a shot to be at the main.
VIC WATERS: How is it fair to WFW World Heavyweight Champion Sean Edmunds? The title over the last month and a half has been going through chaos finding its rightful heir and if he manages to beat Wylde tonight, he will only have until early April to prepare himself for his big World Title match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Don’t discount Wylde. Wylde has been waiting his whole lifetime for this shot at the World Title and he sees this as his chance to win the title and step up onto the grand stage for the world.
VIC WATERS: Imagine the power he would weld as both WFW World Heavyweight Champion and WFW President. He would be the most powerful man in all of wrestling.
BRANDON JACOBS: That is kind of scary for one man to have all that power. But it could happen, here tonight. Edmunds has endured a really hectic schedule since winning the four way and he hasn’t had time to breathe in about two months. What guys like Doc Silver and Shawn Hart did were remarkable because the hectic schedule involved in defending the WFW World Heavyweight Title can wear at you. It is almost like champions could use a break after they win the title but they have go on and be at their best in order to maintain their status.
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is a three time WWL World Heavyweight Champion. He has traveled down the road and back and has faced some of the great competitors ever in this sport. Is he really going to let Wylde who has never won a World Title stop his momentum after that grueling ironman match with Scotty Michaels at Great Expectations?
JONATHAN MARX: But that is what makes Wylde such a threat, he has that hunger in him to be a World Champion and he see this as his opportunity after beating Gideon to seize the bull by the horns and finally accomplish the only thing that he hasn’t accomplished in his career, winning the World Heavyweight Title belt.
BRANDON JACOBS: Jonathan is right, Wylde is hungry, but I’m torn on whether he can beat Edmunds. Edmunds has been a juggernaut and it will take a hell of effort to stop him especially this close to being in the main event of the first Superbowl of Wrestling in over five years. This is a show, which historically has left a lasting impact on this sport. The wrestlers know that, the fans know that, and the media knows that. This is the grand daddy of them all and when we comeback, we will begin the ROAD TO GLORY! Stay tuned!
