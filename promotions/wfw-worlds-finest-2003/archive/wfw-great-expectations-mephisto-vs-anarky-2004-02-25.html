WFW Great Expectations: Mephisto vs. Anarky

25 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 25 February 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: All of a sudden, there is a rash of masked men popping out all over the place. Somewhere Dusty Thompson is watching and he is a proud, proud man.
VIC WATERS: Indeed he is. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, you are about to witness one of the most wild and crazy matches in the history of professional wrestling. All four sides of the ring are triggered to explosions and once Randalls calls for the bell, those explosives on the side of the ring will be turned on. At the end of forty five minutes, if neither man has scored the pinfall, a final set of explosions will go off and heaven help Mephisto and Anarky.
VIC WATERS: While I’ve seen a couple of matches like this on tape, this is the first time I ever saw something like this in person. Why would two men even agree to such a dangerous match?
BRANDON JACOBS: While I don’t agree with Anarky’s philosophy, I respect Anarky for being a fearless warrior. He is one of the toughest men ever to compete in this sport and he never turns down a challenge and that is why he has had all the success he has had.
VIC WATERS: If there is one guy you don’t want to meet in a dark alley, it is Anarky. BRANDON JACOBS: But even more curious is Mephisto has been rumored to be half a dozen people including Maelstrom, Bloodhunt, Manson, Pestillence, and Diablo. The only name rumored which we can cross off is Mike Randalls because he is referring the match.
VIC WATERS: I’ve been watching the promos like all of you have but I still can’t figure it out. I thought that he gave a couple of clues early on which made me think he was one guy but by the end, he gave a whole different set of clues and I didn’t really have any idea what to think. The best way to figure out who it is, is to watch him wrestle.
BRANDON JACOBS: I was thinking that too, but given all the effort Mephisto has given to hiding his identity, I think there may be a chance that he may camouflage his style.
VIC WATERS: You think so? BRANDON JACOBS: Definitely. Why would Mephisto give up his identity if he doesn’t have to? This way, it will keep his opponent off guard because he won’t know what is coming next. For Anarky, it will be like wrestling an opponent for the very first time but without doing his homework.
VIC WATERS: But there is the x factor on top of everything else, how will “The Wolf” Mike Randalls impact the match as referee?
BRANDON JACOBS: Mike Randalls has made a tremendous transformation over the years from the violence and vicious “Devastating” Mike Randalls to the much more tranquil “The Wolf” Mike Randalls so this isn’t the same Randalls which feuded with both earlier in their career. This Randalls is might more tranquil and ideal man to keep this match a one on one encounter because the name Mike Randalls both demands respect and a symbol of authority in the ring.
VIC WATERS: Sort of like modern day John Wayne… BRANDON JACOBS: I have full faith in Mike Randalls. Folks, we are getting a signal from the back that the ring crew is ready to start. LET’S SEND THIS ONE DOWN TO RINGSIDE AND GET THIS UNDERWAY!
::Mike Randalls is standing in the ring:: (CUEUP: “'In My Time of Dying' by the Black Crowes and Jimmy Page) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, your referee for match, A Four Time Unified Champion and former NGEN Mr. Vicious Title holder, “THE WOLF” MIKE RANDALLS!
::Mike Randalls raises his arm to acknowledge the crowd:: (CUEUP: “Sober” by Tool) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing, a world champion ten times over and former Mr. Vicious title holder, from Hartford, Connecticut… ANARKY!
::Anarky comes down to the ring to the loud boos of the crowd and enters the ring:: BRANDON JACOBS: This is the moment we have all been waiting for… (CUEUP: Wild Thing) ::Mephisto slowly walks down the ramp:: BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, THERE IS ANOTHER MEPHISTO IN THE RING RIGHT BEHIND ANARKY… VIC WATERS: Which one is the real one? BRANDON JACOBS: THE MEPHISTO IN THE RING LOW NAILS HIM WITH A LOW BLOW! VIC WATERS: Randalls should call for a DQ! BRANDON JACOBS: MEPHISTO SPINS ANARKY AROUND…. HE PICKS UP ANARKY AND NAILS HIM WITH A RUNNING POWERBOMB! I’ve seen that before somewhere.
VIC WATERS: It couldn’t be… BRANDON JACOBS: Mephisto pulls off his mask…. CROWD: COPYCAT! COPYCAT! COPYCAT! BRANDON JACOBS: IT IS COPYCAT GETTING REVENGE FOR WHAT HAPPENED TO HIM IN THE NORTH AMERICAN TITLE MATCH! Randalls is ordering Copycat to leave the ring and Copycat leaves to a huge ovation from the crowd.
VIC WATERS: This has turned into a blood feud between Copycat and Anarky. BRANDON JACOBS: The real Mephisto rolls into the ring under the barbed wire… MEPHISTO IS SIGNALING TO RANDALLS TO CALL FOR THE BELL AND TO TURN THE EXPLOSIVES ON!
*bell rings* VIC WATERS: After seeing El Arco Iris and Copycat cost their victories, these people tend to believe unfairly that Anarky is behind it all when he wasn’t present at either attack. They are demonizing him just like the did O.J.
BRANDON JACOBS: Mephisto picks up Anarky from behind…. HE LOCKS ON A FULL NELSON AND SUPLEXES HIM DOWN TO THE MAT WITH A DRAGON SUPLEX! Anarky landed right on the back of his neck which took a shot when Copycat Litterbombed him right in the middle of the ring.
VIC WATERS: How many careers have we seen recently get shelved for a year because of a major neck injured? Mephisto has it out to hurt Anarky for whatever he has done,
BRANDON JACOBS: MEPHISTO PICKS UP ANARKY AND LOCKS HIM IN A DRAGON SLEEPER! Randalls goes down to check Anarky to see if he wants to give up but Anarky is repeatedly telling Randalls no.
VIC WATERS: Anarky is too tough to give up, but I don’t think that is Mephisto’s purpose. If Mephisto can wear down Anarky enough, it will be easier to get the pinfall later on in the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, THE TWO MASKED MEN FROM EARLIER ARE COMING DOWN TO THE RING TOGETHER!
VIC WATERS: All four sides of the ring are electrified and hooked up to the explosives. The Masked Men can’t even think of entering the ring until either Mephisto or Anarky deactivates a side by throwing the other man into the barbed wire.
BRANDON JACOBS: Randalls walks over towarded the masked men and warns them not to interfere or they will be kicked out of the building.
VIC WATERS: They could merely be coming down to enjoy the match. This building is sold out, it isn’t like they could have bought a seat.
BRANDON JACOBS: Randalls is simply laying down the law, all of these good people here in Anaheim paid to see Mephisto and Anarky go at it to the end and that is what they are going to get. ANARKY YANKS AT THE BACK OF THE MASK OF MEPHISTO AND RANDALLS IS CALLING FOR A BREAK!
VIC WATERS: There is no rule that you can’t pull on the other guys mask. BRANDON JACOBS: It is unethical. The mask is a sacred, El Santo who had the biggest funeral in all of Mexico was buried in his mask it meant so much to him.
VIC WATERS: El Santo, the Castor Strife of bad cheesy horror films. BRANDON JACOBS: Mephisto starts stomping away on Anarky who has had a really tough start to this match. Anarky has had a chance to show yet what makes him one of the most feared wrestlers on the planet.
VIC WATERS: He will, it is only a matter of time before Mephisto makes a mistake and Anarky reverses the early momentum in this match.
BRANDON JACOBS: That bomb goes off in thirty minutes, they don’t only have to beat the other man down, they have to beat the clock if they want to win the match. Mephisto is picking up Anarky into a front face lock…. SWINGING NECKBREAKER!
VIC WATERS: Mephisto is concentrating on that neck of Anarky. He clearly came into this match with a plan.
BRANDON JACOBS: Mephisto makes the cover on Anarky! Randalls goes down for the count! 1--------------------------------------------2-----------------------KICKOUT! About two and a half right there.
VIC WATERS: Mephisto has to work him over more before he tries for another pinfall attempt. It is still way too early.
BRANDON JACOBS: Mephisto is pulling Anarky up…… MEPHISTO STARTS LAYING INTO ANARKY WITH HARD PUNCHES RIGHT TO THE FACE!
VIC WATERS: Those look like closed fists to me. BRANDON JACOBS: Randalls is warning Mephisto to keep his fist open and keep the match clean.
VIC WATERS: Has anyone ever got disqualified in an exploding barbed wire death match? BRANDON JACOBS: There is always a first time. The referee has to show both men who the boss is.
VIC WATERS: Tony Danza? BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY IS BEING PUSHED BACK TOWARDS THE BARBED WIRE BY THE PUNCHES AND ALMOST FALLS BACK! He would have triggered the first set of explosions if he was move half a foot farther back.
VIC WATERS: With the beating Anarky has taken so far, he’d be in deep trouble if he took that now.
BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY BEGINS TO FIGHT HIS WAY OUT OF THE CORNER! Mephisto is being driven back towards the other side!
VIC WATERS: Anarky is experiencing a rush of adrenaline that comes after near certain doom.
BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY BACKS UP AND HE CHARGES MEPHISTO WITH A FLYING FOREARM…. MEPHISTO CATCHES ANARKY AND STUN GUNS HIM CHEST FIRST ONTO THE BARBED WIRE! OH NO!
::there is a flash of fire and the first huge explosion goes off filling the air with smoke::
VIC WATERS: OH MY GOD! BRANDON JACOBS: Not only did the stun gun trigger the explosive, the force with which Anarky got stun gunned in along with Mephisto addition weight ripped the barbed wire from the turnbuckle and both men fell all the way down to the floor around the ring.
VIC WATERS: Aren’t there explosives all over ringside?! BRANDON JACOBS: They should be okay, they are triggered by timer only, but they really should be careful out there just in case.
VIC WATERS: Anarky and Mephisto better be careful. We don’t want this show becoming famous for bad reasons.
BRANDON JACOBS: Mephisto pulls two pieces of barbed wire and throws them away and keeps one for himself to use on Anarky.
VIC WATERS: Technically, you are allowed to use the ring as a weapon, but I don’t think this is what the creators had in mine.
BRANDON JACOBS: Mephisto rolls Anarky into from the open side and rolls in himself with the piece of barbed wire in his hand. MEPHISTO TAKES THE BARBED WIRE AND WRAPS IT AROUND ANARKY NECK IN ORDER TO CHOKE HIM OUT! Anarky gets his fingers up to protect his neck but that barbed wire is going right into his head.
VIC WATERS: It is either his life or his fingers, Anarky has absolutely no choice. BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY PICKS MEPHISTO UP ON HIS BACK…. ANARKY RAMS MEPHISTO HEAD FIRST INTO THE TURNBUCKLE AND MEPHISTO FALLS OFF THE BACK OF ANARKY!
VIC WATERS: Look at that mark that the barbed wire left on the neck of Anarky! If his fingers weren’t there, he could have been seriously hurt.
BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY JUMPS ON TOP OF MEPHISTO AND STARTS POUNDING AWAY WITH CLOSED FIRSTS RIGHT IN THE FACE OF MEPHISTO!
VIC WATERS: This has been a long time coming! BRANDON JACOBS: Randalls is asking Anarky to open up the first and Anarky starts cursing him out as he lays waste to Mephisto. RANDALLS GRABS THE FIST OF ANARKY AND ANARKY LOOKS BACK AT RANDALLS! Anarky complies and gets up off Mephisto.
VIC WATERS: What is Anarky up to… BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY GRABS THE PIECE OF BARBED WIRE WHICH WAS WRAPPED AROUND HIS NECK AND HE WRAPS IT AROUND HIS FIST! Anarky is calling for Mephisto to get up!
VIC WATERS: Have you ever noticed that whenever someone calls for the other guy to get up, he should stay down?
BRANDON JACOBS: That is not the time to be pointing that out. Mephisto is starting to get up…. ANARKY BLASTS MEPHISTO WITH A CLOSED FIST WRAPPED IN BARBED WIRE AND MEPHISTO GOES DOWN! Mephisto gets up again…. ANOTHER SHOT BY ANARKY!
VIC WATERS: That is going to leave a mark. BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky is taking the barbed wire off his fist…. AND HE IS WRAPPING AROUND HIS FOREARM!
VIC WATERS: Last time he tried the flying forearm, the was the one who went right into the explosives. This is a gutsy call.
BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY CHARGES MEPHISTO AND MEPHISTO STUMBLES BACK INTO THE BARBED WIRE TRIGGERING THE SECOND SET OF EXPLOSIVES!
::there is a flash of fire and the second huge explosion goes off filling the air with smoke::
BRANDON JACOBS: GOOD LORD! MEPHISTO IS ON FIRE! The ring attendants rush over with fire extinguishers to put him out!
VIC WATERS: This is the first time we are doing this, all the bugs aren’t out of the system yet.
BRANDON JACOBS: Thankfully it looks like no actual damage was done, the suit was specially designed with a coating on it to insulate or otherwise Mephisto would be toast. It appears one of the sleeves on Mephisto’s outfit was partially burned off.
VIC WATERS: This is not good. BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky drags Mephisto out of the smoke and ash and goes for the cover! This should be it!
VIC WATERS: Come on Anarky! BRANDON JACOBS: Randalls goes down for the count! 1-----------------------------------------2----------------------- MEPHISTO KICKS OUT! HOLY COW!
VIC WATERS: He was just set on fire and exploded! How much more is it going to take to keep Mephisto down?!
BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY IS RIPPING OFF THE REST OF THE BURN OFF SLEEVE OF MEPHISTO AND HE LIFTS IT UP IN THE AIR AS A TROPHY FOR ALL TO SEE!
VIC WATERS: That explosion left an exposed area on Mephisto and you knew he was going to take advantage of it.
BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY TAKES THE SLEEVE AND WRAPS IT AROUND THE NECK OF MEPHISTO AND STARTS CHOKING HIM WITH IT! Mephisto is gasping for air!
VIC WATERS: Mephisto started out so strong, but Anarky has taken over control of this match firmly.
BRANDON JACOBS: Randalls is putting a five count on Anarky! ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! Anarky breaks! Anarky wraps it around his neck again! RANDALLS COUNTS AGAIN ONE, TWO, THREE…. FOUR! Anarky breaks! Randalls grabs the sleeve away from Anarky and he throws it away.
VIC WATERS: There are few people who are big enough and tough enough stars in this business to do that to Anarky, and Mike Randalls is one of them.
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky lets go of Mephisto and Mephisto falls down onto his knees. Anarky is just standing over Mephisto taunting him….
VIC WATERS: Anarky is trying to get into Mephisto’s head, well if there is any room after Mephisto has been traumatized from the explosions and being set on fire.
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky stands in front of Mephisto…. ANARKY LIFTS MEPHISTO UP IN THE AIR FOR A BRAINBUSTER AND DRIVES HIM DOWN HARD ONTO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Anarky is getting revenge for all the work Mephisto did to his neck early. Now he knows how he feels.
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky goes for the cover on Mephisto! 1--------------------------------------2------------------------------ ANARKY PULLS MEPHISTO UP! Anarky is shaking his finger to crowd that he isn’t done!
VIC WATERS: Anarky doesn’t know who Mephisto is, but he wants to send a message to him and everyone else who thinks they can mess with Anarky and get away with it.
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky pulls an exhausted Mephisto off of the mat…. ANARKY GOES FOR A REVERSE CRESCENT KICK…. NO! MEPHISTO CATCHES HIS LEG AND SPINS HIM AROUND!
VIC WATERS: NO DAMNIT! BRANDON JACOBS: Mephisto kicks Anarky in the gut…. MEPHISTO LIFTS UP ANARKY AND SLINGSHOT SUPLEXES HIM ONTO THE BARBED WIRE SETTING OFF THE THIRD SET OF EXPLOSIONS!
::there is a flash of fire and the third huge explosion goes off filling the air with smoke::
VIC WATERS: Mephisto’s moveset reminds me of someone but I can’t put my finger on it. BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky is bent over in pain front taking the explosion and barbed wire right in the midsection…. He doesn’t look well at all.
VIC WATERS: In a blink of an eye, Mephisto completely turned around the momentum in the match and the explosions helped too.
BRANDON JACOBS: Mephisto picks up Anarky…… HE DEATHVALLEY DRIVERED HIM RIGHT INTO THE TURNBUCKLE! That looked nasty.
VIC WATERS: A DVD can splattered your damn brains all over the mat if you aren’t careful. BRANDON JACOBS: Mephisto goes to make the cover! Randalls goes down for the count! 1-------------------------------------------------2---------------------------------- ANARKY GET HIS SHOULDER UP WITH BARELY A FRACTION OF A SECOND LEFT!
VIC WATERS: How do these two men do it? How do they keep on coming back from certain death?
BRANDON JACOBS: These are two of the toughest SOBS I have ever encountered. They amazed even me.
VIC WATERS: Time is running out, we are about forty minutes into the match and the explosion and we only have five minutes left.
::Loud bombing sirens begin to signal the five minute mark has began:: BRANDON JACOBS: Mephisto picks up Anarky…. MEPHISTO LOCKS ANARKY IN A COBRA CLUTCH… MEPHISTO LIFTS HIM UP AND DROPS HIM ON THE FOURTH SET OF EXPLOSIONS SETTING THEM OFF!
::there is a flash of fire and fourth third huge explosion goes off filling the air with smoke::
VIC WATERS: I RECONGIZE THAT MOVE ANYWHERE! THAT IS THE DEVIL’S RAGDOLL! THAT IS DIABLO! BRANDON JACOBS: HOLY ****! YOU ARE RIGHT! WE HAVEN’T SEEN HIM SINCE THAT GHOULISH GAMES FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE IN THE GRAVEYARD MATCH! Diablo is back!
VIC WATERS: I can’t believe Diablo pulled this off. BRANDON JACOBS: Mephisto pulls Anarky out of the of the barbed wire and he goes for the cover…. ONE OF THE MASKED MEN JUMPS INTO THE RING AND RANDALLS GOES OVER TO TELL HIM TO GET DOWN! The other masked man climbs up the turnbuckle behind Randalls back…. STARDUST PRESS ONTO MEPHISTO WHO IS COVERING ANARKY! Randalls turns around and the other masked man bails out of the ring.
VIC WATERS: It was all a diversion to draw Randalls attention away! What a plan! BRANDON JACOBS: Both men are down and we have less than two minutes to go! WAIT A SECOND, ANARKY IS SLOWLY GETTING UP! Anarky pulls Mephisto up and they begin exchanging punches back and forth! ANARKY KICKS DIABLO IN THE STOMACH…. CHAOS BREAKER REVERSE NECKBREAKER ON MEPHISTO WITH LESS THAN A MINUTE TO GO!
VIC WATERS: PIN HIM OR THE BOMB IS GOING TO GO OFF! BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky goes down to make the cover but Randalls is yelling at him for the interference! ANARKY GETS UP AND STARTS YELLING AT RANDALLS! THIS IS GETTING HEATED!
VIC WATERS: COME ON! MAKE THE COUNT! BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky goes down to make the cover! EXPLOSION COUNTDOWN BY THE CROWD: TEN, NINE, EIGHT, SEVEN, SIX, FIVE…. BRANDON JACOBS: Randalls goes down to make the count! 1------------------------------------2--------------- THE EXPLOSION GOES OFF!
::the mother of all explosions goes off completely surrounding the ring in dust, none of the participants are visable::
BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, A SECOND MEPHISTO IS COMING OUT OF THE CROWD WITH A CHAIR WRAPPED IN BARBED WIRE AND HE ENTERS THE RING IN THE CLOUD OF SMOKE!
VIC WATERS: I THINK I JUST HEARD A CHAIR SHOT! BRANDON JACOBS: I think I just heard a chair shot too…. AND ANOTHER ONE! The Memphisto who came in with the chair is bailing out!
VIC WATERS: The smoke looks like it is finally starting to clear…. WHAT THE HELL?! BRANDON JACOBS: THE TWO MASKED MEN ARE DOWN AND UNMASKED DIABLO IS LAYING ON TOP OF ANARKY! Randalls whipes the dust out of his eyes and goes down for the count as the crowd counts along! 1---------------------------------------2--------------------------------------3!
*god pop for Diablo beating Anarky* RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. DIABLO! BRANDON JACOBS: Randalls is raising Diablo’s hand in victory and this crowd is chanting for Diablo!
VIC WATERS: Diablo wasn’t Mephisto, the man who left through the crowd was Mephisto! This was all a trap for Anarky, Wells, and the mystery man.
BRANDON JACOBS: Randalls exits the ring…The two masked men are getting up…. AND THEY ATTACK DIABLO! ONE PICKS DIABLO UP….. BIG POWERBOMB DOWN ON THE MAT! The other goes to the outside and pulls a table into the ring and grabs some barbed wire. Anarky is beginning to get up and he instructs the masked man to climb up to the top! The bigger masked man is trying Diablo down to the table with barbed wire while the smaller main perches from the top….. A SECOND STARDUST PRESS RIGHT ONTO DIABLO AND THROUGH THE TABLE!
VIC WATERS: Diablo may have won, but he certainly doesn’t look like a winner now. BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky is instructing the masked men to take off their masks…. THE FIRST ONE UNMASKS…. IT IS JARED WELLS! HE IS THE GUY WHO ATTACKED COPYCAT EARLIER IN HIS MATCH!
VIC WATERS: That is just like Wells. BRANDON JACOBS: The smaller masked man climbs up onto the turnbuckle and removes his masks….. IT IS THE FORMER WFW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION PSYCHO!
VIC WATERS: You got to be kidding me, Anarky, Wells, and Psycho are all together working as a team? Do you know what kind of destruction those three will cause?!
BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second, Anarky is taking the mic. ANARKY: "I told you, Mephisto... I <BLEEP>ING TOLD YOU... that there would BE no evolution. That nothing could be gained. But you didn't believe me. And now look at Diablo. "Lying on his back like some kind of dead animal... weak and powerless to stand against me, to even beg me for mercy... not that I would give him any. "Here's your <BLEEP>ing example, Mephisto. Here's your great morality play to the world. Good and evil don't mean <BLEEP> because you have no POWER to stop me. All you have is weakness. "Now the whole world knows... that when you <BLEEP> with ME... when you <BLEEP> WITH US... there will be consequences. There will be penance. And no one will go unscathed. "DO YOU HEAR ME, MEPHISTO?! IS THAT COMING THROUGH CRYSTAL CLEAR FOR 'YA?!?" BRANDON JACOBS: The war has only just begun as Copycat, El Arco Iris, Gideon, and Diablo found out tonight.
VIC WATERS: This is a War Games match waiting to happen. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, when we comeback, the big match is coming up next, Scotty Michaels puts his WFW World Heavyweight Title on the line against Sean Edmunds…. STAY TUNED!
