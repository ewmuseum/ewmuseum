Almost Live: Intro - Wylde/Hart, new stips for Manson/Doc

26 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 26 September 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome to Wonderland. It has been a long road to Camelot and tonight, two of the greatest in the history of the WFW and all of wrestling will step into the ring here in Revere, Mass chutes, A city which is historically symbolic of what happens here tonight, either Manson or Doc Silver will make that long ride to challenge to the top and compete at the next WFW PPV against the World’s Finest Wrestling Champ… “The Phenom” Shawn Hart in an attempt to wrestle the power C.O.C.K.S. has away from terrorists who have run rough shot over our league against the evil doers in a struggle for power.
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart got lucky. Doc Silver beat him cleanly on two other occasions. I’ve heard the rumors about Doc not being a 100% from his devastating match with Psycho, but he has beaten Manson over and over again. He owns him like the Yankees own the Red Sox. All he needs to do is get past Manson somehow and I’m sure he will step up to the occasion and knock Shawn Hart down to where he belongs.
BRANDON JACOBS: All he has to do is beat Manson? One of these days, just like the Red Sox will eventually beat the Yankees, Manson is going to have his day over Doc and tonight I think is the night. Manson is finally going to beat Doc and once he beats Doc, he’ll finally have a chance to wrestle for the WFW World Title.
VIC WATERS: I’d bet anything that Doc Silver is going to go over tonight, ANYTHING! (CUEUP: “Dragnet theme”) SHAWN HART: Vic, did I happen to hear you say anything? ::comes out of the dressing room and walks over the announcer’s booth::
VIC WATERS: ::shocked:: Yeah, I said that. SHAWN HART: Let’s see if you are willing to put your money where your mouth is, if Manson beats Doc Silver, Doc Silver is suspended for thirty days and you become the lead announcer for C.O.C.K.S!
VIC WATERS: I don’t know, what do I get in return if I win… SHAWN HART: As soon as I become WFW President, I’ll give you a generous raise and I’ll fire Jacobs and let you pick his replacement.
VIC WATERS: Hmmm… what is in it for Doc if he wins the match? SHAWN HART: I’ll make it a ladder match for the WFW World Heavyweight Title, Doc Silver has never lost a ladder match.
VIC WATERS: YOU ARE ON! *crowd pops* (CUEUP: "Diesel Power" by Prodigy) ALEX WYLDE: Wait a second son, what makes you think YOU are going to win the election? SHAWN HART: Can’t you see all of the fans out there in their C.O.C.K.S. t-shirts? ALEX WYLDE: There is only one COCK I see and I’m looking right at him! ::crowd pops and Shawn Hart gets angry:: ALEX WYLDE: I’ll tell you what, I’ll let all of what you’ve done stand…. But let’s make it more interesting. If Doc loses the match, Manson gets Shawn Hart in the match of his choice and Doc will be gone for SIXTY DAYS!
SHAWN HART: Pffft. If you had any balls and really believed Manson would win, you’d make it NINETY DAYS!
*crowd gasps* ALEX WYLDE: Fine, if you want ninety days, YOU GOT IT! And let me say, it is going to be a pleasure to beat your ass in the election. *throws down his mic and leaves*
SHAWN HART: Vic baby, it will be a pleasure having you on board Team C.O.C.K.S. VIC WATERS: In your dreams son… SHAWN HART: ::throws a shirt in Vic’s face and walks off:: BRANDON JACOBS: Man Vic, you’ve certainly made a mess of things here tonight Vic. VIC WATERS: DOC WILL WIN! He’ll put Hart in his place! BRANDON JACOBS: I hope you are right, folks, when we comeback, we aren’t going to make you wait…. DOC SILVER VS. MANSON FOR THE SHOT AT SHAWN HART IS NEXT!
