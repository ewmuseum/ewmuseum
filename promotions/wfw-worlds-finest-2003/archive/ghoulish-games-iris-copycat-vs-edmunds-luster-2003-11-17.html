Ghoulish Games - Iris/Copycat vs. Edmunds/Luster

17 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 17 November 2003 (Black Rose catches up with 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds as he is walking out his dressing room door.)
ROSE: "Simply Sensational! Can we get a few words from you on your upcoming Double Dog Collar Match tonight?"
EDMUNDS: "Ah, Black Rose ... how fitting a name perhaps I should send you to Copycat as a gift ..."
ROSE: "Um, wel.." EDMUNDS: "I didn't say you could interrupt me .. you speak when I'm finished, and I'm not finished. This match tonight means nothing. Its just a throw together. Let's put four wrestlers, two proven, one up and comer, and another .. well .. I dont know what Luster is .. but lets put them in a match where they are tied together by dog collars. That's to provoke fan interest. Now mind you, I don't fault anyone in the WFW Offices. It is the best our great President Wylde could do with the likes of Copycat, El Arco Iris and Luster being in the match. So he needed to do something to draw the fan's interest. Now talk."
ROSE: "Where is Sherri and will she be a factor in tonight's match like she was at New World Madness?"
EDMUNDS: "First of all, you can address her as SENSATIONAL Sherri. Second of all, what do you mean she was a factor at New World Madness."
ROSE: "She fou.." EDMUNDS: "I didnt say you could talk! I'll specifically tell you when its ok for you to talk. Sherri will not be a factor in this match. You see, the outcome of this match is already in stone. We all know what is going to happen .. so whether or not Sherri lends a hand, or a nail, or a shoe, or whatever, it will not effect the outcome because its going to happen whether she does or not. As for where
she is, she's in the dressing room getting ready ... she needs to be extravagent for the pay per view .. Talk.."
ROSE: "And what about Lus..." EDMUNDS: "Actually, I'm finished talking to you. I've got more important things to do than listen to you babble on. Tonight, at Ghoulish Games, Copycat and El
Arco Iris will be in for a hell of a match. Its unfortunate, however, that they'll be on the losing end of the stick .. see you out there (pauses) boys."
(Fade back to ringside as Edmunds turns away without even mentioning anything more to Rose.)
VIC WATERS: Sean Edmunds is right, this is nothing more than a gimmick to make people care about Copycat and El Arco Iris. I think it is insulting to the fans that we put a dog collar around someone has classy as Sean Edmunds.
BRANDON JACOBS: Yeah, it has nothing to do with Edmunds and Luster being SNEAKS who would like nothing better than to RUN AWAY and avoid a beating. Nothing whatsoever…
VIC WATERS: I sense a hint of sarcasm in your voice. BRANDON JACOBS: There four have been involved in all out war for over a month now, the dog collars are around their necks to insure that this war gets settled TONIGHT.
VIC WATERS: You don’t think Luster wants to get his hands on Copycat and El Arco Iris after they cost an innocent man the WFW World Heavyweight Title? Luster could have been up their with the Maelstroms, the Michael Mansons, the Hornets, the Dan Ryans, hell, he might have even been mentioned in the same breath as Damian Priest.
BRANDON JACOBS: No doubt, Luster winning the WFW World Heavyweight Title would have been huge for his career. But he got what he deserved for poking his head into other people’s business…
VIC WATERS: Aren’t Copycat and El Arco Iris men of character? Shouldn’t they turn the other cheek? What you want is a world of utter chaos….
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster is the SAME MAN who also robbed Manson of his WFW North American Title. He deserves everything he gets.
VIC WATERS: You are nothing but a war monger. BRANDON JACOBS: Sometimes force is the only solution. Besides, you are one to talk with your Doc Silver worship and his love of thuggery.
VIC WATERS: How dare you slander the name of Doc…. BRANDON JACOBS: The truth hurts bud. But I’m not here tonight to teach you a lesson, we are here to call a match, let’s send this one to ringside and end this petty arguing.
VIC WATERS: Whatever. (CUEUP: “Thriller” by Michael Jackson) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, weighing in at over 508 pounds, the charismatic team of COPYCAT AND EL ARCO IRIS!
::the fog machine starts and Copycat dance down to the ring dressed as Michael Jackson with a red jumpsuit and a beret and El Arco Iris is dressed as La Parka except in rainbow colors::
BRANDON JACOBS: Look at those two bust a move as the crowd cheers them on! VIC WATERS: ::hangs his head in shame:: BRANDON JACOBS: Come on Vic, get into the spirit of the holidays! VIC WATERS: Bah humbug. BRANDON JACOBS: That is our Christmas show, this is only Halloween. ::Copycat and El Arco Iris enters the ring and Copycat takes off his outfit and hands it to Icekold::
VIC WATERS: Who would have ever thought that the guy dressed like Michael Jackson would be the third last sexually perverse man in a tag team match.
BRANDON JACOBS: ::nods:: (CUEUP: “"Funkytown"-Disco Fever) RING ANNOUNCER: And now, the first man of the opposing team, the man who helped end Manson’s WFW North American Title reign and the sexiest man in all of WFW, from Atlanta, Georgia… LUSTER!
::Luster comes down dancing to the ring as the crowd boos:: VIC WATERS: Now Luster is a man who can really dance, he knows how to get funky. BRANDON JACOBS: The only funk you know is your own body odor. VIC WATERS: Hardy har har… (CUEUP: "Good Boys" by Blondie as the lights go low. A spotlight goes on the entrance and an arm comes through with a blue sequined sleeve hanging down .. with blue feathers hanging off it. As the chorus reaches, Sensational Sherri steps out, wearing a marvelous blue and blacked sequin dress, with the arms
tapered with blue feathers, almost as if she had wings. Her face is glittered and she has tons of blue eyeshadow on in an exaggerated fashion. She approaches the steps and ascends into the ring. She rips the microphone out of the announcer's hand.)
SHERRI: "Isn't the dress fabulous?" (The crowd boos) SHERRI: "Oh you wouldn't know fashion if you woke up next to it in bed after a drunken haze. I mean, this dress took HOURS to make. It was relatively easy
however ... (she stares at El Arco Iris) it was just catching the Blue Jays and listening to their chirps for help as I ripped out their feathers that was the
hard part." (Sherri laughs as El Arco Iris looks like he's about to shed tears.) SHERRI: "Of course, after that all I had to do was wash off the little blood stains and sew them on ... "
(Iris buries his head in his hands..) SHERRI: "But without further adeux.." (CUEUP: "Brain Stew" by Green Day. 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds comes out to a chorus of boos. He is wearing his entire, but he is carrying a
present wrapped in a bow.) SHERRI: "Weighing in at 228 pounds and standing 6 feet tall. From BOSTON, MASSACHUSETTS ... the REAL winner of the match at New World Madness in my opinion .... 'SIMPLY SENSATIONAL' SEAN EDMUNDS!"
(Edmunds ascends the steps and hands Sherri the present. She puts it in the corner and helps Edmunds take off his WM9 Luger-esque top.)
BRANDON JACOBS: Upon the orders of President Wylde, the referee is strapping El Arco Iris to Sean Edmunds and Copycat to Luster.... this match will be run under tornado falls rules. Neither man has to tag in or out, this is just four men brawling and the first man to get a pinfall on a member of the opposing team wins.
VIC WATERS: Makes sense to me… *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat and Luster are having a tug of war with the steel chain! Edmunds pulls El Arco Iris towards him…. SHORT CLOTHESLINE!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is using the size advantage to its fullest. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is pulling Luster towards him with the chain…. LUSTER CHARGES WITH A FOREARM TO THE FACE! Copycat follows up with several of his own! LUSTER IS REELING BACK! Copycat picks the slack of the chain and starts wailing away on Luster!
VIC WATERS: Copycat is just too damn big and strong. BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS IS CHOKING OUT EL ARCO IRIS WITH THE CHAIN AND EL ARCO IRIS IS FLAILING HIS ARMS TRY TO GET THE CHAIN OFF HIS NECK! Edmunds breaks and stands over El Arco Iris taunting him.
VIC WATERS: It didn’t take too long for the choking to begin. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster is already busted open! Copycat uses the chain to whip Luster off the ropes…. COPYCAT JUMPS UP AND NAILS HIM WITH A KNEE LIFT TO THE FACE!
VIC WATERS: OH NO! BRANDON JACOBS: Luster falls through the ropes and Copycat grabs the chain around his neck to keep from being sent through the ropes after his…. GOOD LORD! COPYCAT HAS LUSTER IN A HANGMAN’S NOOSE!
VIC WATERS: Luster is grabbing the collar trying to get some relief from the pressure on his neck but Luster won’t be able to keep this up for long!
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat goes through the ropes and breaks the noose around Luster’s neck. Luster slumps down in exhaustion…. DOUBLE AXE HANDLE ON THE BACK OF LUSTER DRIVING HIM DOWN INTO THE MATS AT RINGSIDE!
VIC WATERS: Luster is really taking a beating here. BRANDON JACOBS: Mean while, El Arco Iris is regaining his breath and Sean Edmunds picks El Arco Iris up off the mat… Edmunds wraps the chain around his fist…. HE DRIVES IT INTO THE GUT OF EL ARCO IRIS!
VIC WATERS: Is it me or is El Arco Iris gaining weight? BRANDON JACOBS: Iris has been winded…. Edmunds picks up Iris for a piledriver…. HE NAILS IT! Edmunds makes the cover! 1-----------------------------2-----------------KICKOUT! Iris barely gets his shoulder up!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is looking really strong tonight. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds takes the chain and he starts ramming it into the face of El Arco Iris…. BLOOD IS COMING OUT OF EL ARCO IRIS’ NOSE! Edmunds wraps his legs around El Arco Iris in a vice and puts El Arco Iris in a sleeper headlock!
VIC WATERS: Look what is happening with Copycat and Luster on the outside! BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat went to whip Luster into the stairs but Luster grabbed the chain and sent Copycat up and over them instead! Luster is winded, but he is walking over to Copycat… Luster grabs some of the slack in the chain…. LUSTER STARTS WHIPPING COPYCAT WITH THE CHAIN AND COPYCAT IS TRYING TO CRAWL AWAY!
VIC WATERS: Look at those welts already forming on Copycat’s back. Luster isn’t anything back tonight.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris isn’t looking so good, he looks like he is ready to give it up and the ref is there to check to see if he is okay. The ref is down on the mat checking on El Arco Iris but he keeps on shaking his head no…
VIC WATERS: A lesser man would have given up already, El Arco Iris is tougher than he appears to be.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds decides to break the hold… he picks up El Arco Iris off the mat…. HE NAILS HIM WITH A JAWJACKER!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds has really been working over the head of El Arco Iris in order to take his mind out of the game.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris falls down to his hands and knees and he grabs his jaw…. EDMUNDS DIVES OVER EL ARCO IRIS WITH A SUNSET FLIP CRADLE! 1------------------------------2--------------------------------- EL ARCO IRS JUST GETS THE SHOULDER UP!
VIC WATERS: Copycat needs to recover to help him out or El Arco Iris is going to be the man taking the fall for his team.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is tad busy with Luster… LUSTER IS RAKING THE FACE OF COPYCAT ON THE STEEL GUARDRAIL!
VIC WATERS: Luster is getting pay back for Copycat costing him the WFW World Heavyweight title.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is yelling at Luster to roll Copycat back into the ring so he can get a piece of him and Luster grabs Copycat by the hair and rolls him into the ring.
VIC WATERS: Payback time… BRANDON JACOBS: Luster starts banging El Arco Iris head against the mat and Edmunds is standing over Copycat…. EDMUNDS WRAPS HIS ELBOW WITH THE STEEL CHAIN… HE IS CALLING FOR THE CAT’S MEOW ELBOW MOCKING COPYCAT!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds wants to let everyone know who was the real star of WWL. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds sets up…. EDMUNDS DROPS THE ELBOW RIGHT IN THE FACE OF COPYCAT! Edmunds makes the cover! 1---------------------------2-KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds barely got the two on that, Copycat will not let his own move beat him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds tells Luster to move El Arco Iris closer to the corner… Edmunds moves Copycat towards the corner…. EDMUNDS IS GOING UP TO THE TOP…. El Arco Iris is totally out of it… Edmunds is perched on the top…. COPYCAT GRABS THE CHAIN OF EDMUNDS AND PULLS HIM DOWN FROM THE TOP ROPE AND COPYCAT ROLLS OUT OF THE WAY AND HE FALLS BETWEEN THE BODIES OF EL ARCO IRIS AND COPYCAT!
VIC WATERS: Double Dog Collar matches aren’t really the best time to do top rope maneuvers.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat rolls over and puts an arm on Edmunds! 1-----------------------2------------LUSTER PULLS UP COPYCAT TO BREAK UP THE PINFALL! Luster waistlocks Copycat… GERMAN SUPLEX! Wait a second, El Arco Iris crawls over and makes the cover on Edmunds! The ref goes down to make the count! 1--------------------------------2------------------EDMUNDS GETS HIS SHOULDER UP!
VIC WATERS: That was very close, Edmunds really hit his head hard on the mat and it almost cost him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is rolling out of the ring and he pulls El Arco Iris out of the ring under the bottom rope with the chain. EL ARCO IRIS TAKES EDMUNDS DOWN WITH A SINGLE LEG TAKEDOWN AND STARTS USING THE CHAIN TO BEAT EDMUNDS DOWN AFTER TAKING HIS ABUSE ALL MATCH LONG!
VIC WATERS: Listen to this crowd, they have certainly been waiting for it. BRANDON JACOBS: Sherri is begging El Arco Iris to stop…. SHERRI IS OFFERING EL ARCO IRIS A CHANCE TO HUG! El Arco Iris stops beating on Edmunds and looks up….
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris has never been one to turn down a hug… BRANDON JACOBS: Sherri hugs El Arco Iris! What a manipulative dog’s mother she is… wait a second, Sherri has a gift for El Arco Iris!
VIC WATERS: People think that Sherri is a bad person, but she isn’t, she has a heart of a child…
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco jumps up and down and opens the present…. EL ARCO IRIS SCREAMS IN TERROR! El Arco Iris puts his head in hands…. OH MAN! EL ARCO IRIS HAS SNAPPED! EL ARCO IRIS STARTS CHASING SHERRI AROUND THE RING AND HE IS DRAGGING THE BODY OF SEAN EDMUNDS BEHIND HIM AS EDMUNDS IS GRABBING HIS COLLAR FOR DEAR LIFE TO KEEP FROM CHOKING!
VIC WATERS: And apparently, the heart was in the box… My god, what was in it? BRANDON JACOBS: Whatever it was, it certainly backfired because El Arco Iris has snapped! EL ARCO IRIS DIVES AND HE CATCHES THE HEELS OF SHERRI! SHERRI KICKS EL ARCO IRIS WITH HER SPIKED HEELS AND BUSTS EL ARCO IRIS OPEN THROUGH THE MASK! El Arco Iris is grabbing his head!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds could take advantage here if he wasn’t so damn exhausted from being dragging around the ring at fifty miles per hour.
BRANDON JACOBS: Sherri runs into the ring and El Arco Iris and Sean Edmunds comeback into the ring. Luster and Copycat are having another tug of war over the chain…. EL ARCO IRIS RUNS AFTER SHERRI AND… OH MY! THE CHAINS HAVE CROSSED AND BOTH COPYCAT AND LUSTER ARE BEING CHOKED TO DEATH BY THE DOG COLLAR! Sherri escapes to the outside unscathed…
VIC WATERS: That was bound to happen with everyone involved here… BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT GRABS EL ARCO IRIS AND UNTANGLES THE CHAINS BY THROWING HIM VIA OVERHEAD PRESS SLAM ONTO LUSTER! The ref goes down to make the count! 1-------------------------------2------------------- EDMUNDS PULLS EL ARCO IRIS OFF WITH THE CHAIN! Copycat grabs the slack of the chain and starts WHIPPING LUSTER WITH IT SO HE CAN’T GET UP! Edmunds jumps on Copycat back with a sleeper in order to stop him from whipping Luster! COPYCAT IS TRYING TO KNOCK HIM OFF!
VIC WATERS: This has really been a violent match. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris climbs up the ring ropes…. HE FLIES OFF AND NAILS EDMUNDS WITH A HAPPY DANCE SUPERKICK TO THE HEAD! Edmunds falls off of Copycat!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds went down like he was shot! BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat picks Luster up off the mat…. HE NAILS HIM WITH THE LITTERBOMB! Copycat covers and El Arco Iris covers him jumps on him to add his weight to the cover! 1----------------------------------2----------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. The tag team of Copycat and El Arco Iris! ::the camera pans down to the box on the ground which started so much chaos and two dead blue jays are in it::
(CUEUP: "Thriller" by Michael Jackson) BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat and El Arco Iris dance off victorious to the cheers of the crowd, they have finally settled the score with these two men… for the moment.
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is starting to rise… EDMUNDS RIPS HIS COLLAR OFF AND STARTS TO BEAT ON LUSTER WITH THE CHAIN! Edmunds was quite vocal about the lack of time he had training with his partner for his match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Officials are coming down to pry Edmunds off…. This is going to take awhile to clear up, folks, when we comeback, Good Eddie will make his debut against the WFW President Alex Wylde. STAY TUNED!
