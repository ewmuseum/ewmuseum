WFW: NYC Unplugged: Diablo (Bc) vs. Rat Fink

7 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 7 September 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome back everyone, it is now time for the first ever BAD World Heavyweight Title defense since it has been separated from the WFW World Heavyweight Title. This should be a hell of a battle because as you know, BAD has a long history of not playing by the rules. BAD is about hardcore wrestling and the battle between some of the toughest men on the planet.
VIC WATERS: Everyone knows the long and illustrious history of BAD. That is the promotion which launched the Manson vs. Doc Silver feud which lasts to this very day and did hardcore wrestling old school style better than any other promotion on the planet. That is exactly why after Rat Fink has repeatedly been screwed out of the WFW World Heavyweight Title, that I think Rat Fink will do the impossible and beat the undefeated Diablo tonight for the BAD World Heavyweight Title.
BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo is a beast. While C.O.C.K.S. may be lead by Shawn Hart, many people call Diablo the heart of the group. He is a vicious, vicious man who ended the promising career of Andre Eian at the Spirit of NGEN by driving him through a flaming table. Rat Fink may be devious, but he has never faced a man like Diablo before and today it is going to be a falls count anywhere…. no DQ, no countout match.
VIC WATERS: Aren’t all Falls Count Anywhere matches no DQ and no countout? BRANDON JACOBS: You know, I’ve never seen a falls count anywhere match that wasn’t, but they always stress that there is no DQ or countout. It is a possible to do a Falls Count Anywhere match with dqs and countouts, but it wouldn’t be as much fun.
VIC WATERS: Definitely. I remember the falls count anywhere matches of the early nineties and how special those were, you didn’t have that kind of wildness in matches on a regular basis back then so when they happened, you were seeing some that you knew was special.
BRANDON JACOBS: Tonight, Diablo and Rat Fink will try to continue that tradition and you can hear this crowd is buzzing for the match, let’s send this one to ringside!
(CUEUP: Megalomaniac by KMFDM) ::Rat Fink comes down to a mixed reaction from the crowd with his cheesy looking Sci-Fi Robot wearing a Redskins jersey and The American and enters the ring::
(CUEUP: “Clint Eastwood” by Gorillaz) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, one of the founding members of C.O.C.K.S. and one of the best young wrestlers in all of wrestling today and current BAD World Heavyweight Champion, DIABLO!
:Diablo comes down in his C.O.C.K.S shirt to mixed boos and cheers from a crowd: and enters the ring, he hands his world title belt to the referee who lifts the title belt up for all to see::
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: If Diablo is going to win this match, he is going to need to overcome three guys to do it.
::”LET’S GO DIABLO!” chant breaks out:: VIC WATERS: Why are all these fans cheering Diablo after everything C.O.C.K.S. has done in WFW?
BRANDON JACOBS: Well, 1. We have a lot of fans who came in from Spanish Harlem for this show and they look at Diablo as a hero despite all the bad things that he has done 2. It is a three on one, Diablo appears to be screwed 3. Footbot is wearing a Redskins jersey
VIC WATERS: Well yeah… BRANDON JACOBS: It is like expecting a Canadian to be a heel in his own home country, it just ain’t going to happen.
VIC WATERS: Logic in wrestling, who would have thought? BRANDON JACOBS: DIABLO RUSHES RAT FINK, THE AMERICAN, and FOOTBOT! DIABLO CLOTHESLINES FOOTBOT OUT OF THE RING!
VIC WATERS: Diablo is insane rushing into the fight like this. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink and the American are wailing on Diablo from both sides! Diablo turns around and starts wailing on the American, then he switches back and wails on Rat Fink! DIABLO IS STARTING TO GET THE BETTER OF THE TWO!
VIC WATERS: Diablo needs to escape because eventually this is going to get the better of him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo turns around again to fight American… RAT FINK GRABS HIS HAIR AND DIABLO SCREAMS IN PAIN! Rat Fink hooks Diablo’s arms and The American starts laying into Diablo with hard leg shots!
VIC WATERS: A good leg shot is worth more than a good punch because most people have a lot more power in their legs and they are taking advantage of that to truly take it to Diablo.
BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo is starting to slump in Rat Fink’s arms… The American bounces off the ropes… DROPKICK TO THE KNEE OF DIABLO! Rat Fink drags Diablo to the center of the ring and lets go and Diablo falls down to the mat!
VIC WATERS: That was a hell of a shot to the knee… BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo is on all fours trying to get up… The American nails Diablo WITH A KNEE TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD! Diablo goes down hard!
VIC WATERS: The knee is one of the hardest parts of the body, when that comes crashing into your school, you are going to go down and hard.
BRANDON JACOBS: The American picks up the knee of Diablo… HE PICKS HIM UP AND DROPS HIM KNEE FIRST ONTO THE MAT! Diablo grabs his knee! He looks like he is in a world of pain right now!
VIC WATERS: The American is working over the leg to set up the figure four that he is known for.
BRANDON JACOBS: The American grabs the injured leg of Diablo and HE GOES FOR A SPINNING TOE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: OLD SCHOOL SUBMISSION! BRANDON JACOBS: The American twists it in once! HE TWISTS IT IN TWICE! Diablo is screaming in pain! HE TWISTS AGAIN ONE MORE TIME! Diablo looks like he wants to give up but I don’t know if there is any quit in him!
VIC WATERS: Diablo better give up or it could wind up costing him his career. BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK COMES OVER AND HE STARTS STOMPING ON THE FACE OF DIABLO! With Diablo locked in the spinning toe hold, there is nothing he can do!
VIC WATERS: Diablo is in a no win situation here, if he submits, he saves himself. But in the process, he may incur the wrath of the WFW World Heavyweight Champion Shawn Hart.
BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo looks like he is about to pass out! HE DOES! The American is telling Rat Fink to make the cover! Rat Fink covers Diablo…. 1------------------------2------------KICKOUT! Diablo was playing possum! Diablo quickly rolls to the outside of the ring and starts trying to crawl away.
VIC WATERS: Run away, live again to fight another day has always been my motto. BRANDON JAOCBS: Rat Fink and The American are coming out after him, he isn’t safe on the floor because falls and submissions count anywhere in Central Park. DIABLO IS REACHING… HE GRABS A WOMAN’S PURSE!
VIC WATERS: Great way to pickup some extra cash. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink and the American slowly stalk Diablo who is trying to buy himself sometime as he crawls away…. DIABLO PULLS OUT SOME PERFUME! The American goes to pull him up… HE SPRAYS IT IN HIS EYES AND THE AMERICAN IS TRYING TO GET THE BURNING PAIN OUT!
VIC WATERS: Diablo is using his surroundings to his advantage here. He may just be able to survive yet.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink kicks the bottle of perfume out of the hand of Diablo. DIABLO GRABS THE PURSE AND THROWS IT RIGHT AT RAT FINK’S HEAD! Rat Fink really go clunked with that!
VIC WATERS: Women are carrying more and more in their purse these days. BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo is helped up by some of the fans and Rat Fink is coming after him. Diablo grabs the cane from a little old man and he wacks Rat Fink with it in the gut! Rat Fink is doubled up!
VIC WATERS: Diablo is really raising cane here… BRANDON JACOBS: DIABLO NAILS HIM ACROSS THE BACK WITH IT AND THE WOODEN CANE SNAPS! Rat Fink falls onto his knees but Diablo pulls him back. Diablo is telling the fans to move out of the way….. DIABLO THROWS RAT FINK THROUGH THE ROPES OF CHAIRS AND RAT FINK CRASHES DOWN TO THE GROUND!
VIC WATERS: Nasty spill by Rat Fink. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink is crawling towards the fountain… DIABLO COMES UP FROM BEHIND AND HE GRABS RAT FINK BY THE HEAD…. HE THROWS RAT FINK RIGHT INTO THE FOUNTAIN!
VIC WATERS: Looks like Rat Fink may have been busted open when he crashed into the concrete…
BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo is grabbing another woman’s purse… HE HAS CAN OF HAIRSPRAY! What is he going to do now? He already blinded The American! OH NO, HE HAS OUT THE MATCH PAPER… THIS CANNOT BE GOOD!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink looks over… he sees it! BRANDON JACOBS: DIABLO THROWS THE FIREBALL UP AND SPRAYS THE HAIR SPRAY SENDING THE FIREBALL FROM THE BLOW TORCH TOWARDS THE FOUNTAIN BUT RAT FINK DIVES INTO THE WATER BEFORE HE IS HIT! Holy cow!
VIC WATERS: What kind of fans are these? They are on their feet chanting Diablo! It is like they wanted Rat Fink to be set on fire!
BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second, someone is coming through the crowd… IT IS THE AMERICAN! HE COMES FROM BEHIND AND THROWS DIABLO IN THE FOUNTAI!N
VIC WATERS: Those Americans are sneaky bastards. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink grabs Diablo and rams him head first into the side of the fountain! DIABLO HAS BEEN BUSTED OPEN! Rat Fink is calling to the American to grab the pretzel vender’s cart! Don’t tell me Rat Fink is hungry at a time like this!
VIC WATERS: Knowing Rat Fink, it has to be something more sinister than a craving for a snack.
BRANDON JACOBS: The American brings the pretzel card over…. RAT FINK GRABS THE PRETZEL SALT AND RUBS IT INTO THE OPEN WOUNDS OF DIABLO! Diablo is screaming bloody murder!
VIC WATERS: Salt and open cuts don’t mix. BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK NOW GRABS THE HEAD OF DIABLO AND TRIES TO DROUND HIM UNDER WATER! Wait a second, the ref jumps into the pool and onto Rat Fink’s back! He may have just saved the life of Diablo there!
VIC WATERS: The ref needs to stay out of Rat Fink’s business. This is no DQ. Rat Fink can do whatever he wants.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink tries to pry the ref off…. DIABLO JUMPS ONTO THE SIDE OF THE FOUNTAIN AND USES IT AS A SPRINGBOARD TO DROPKICK RAT FINK RIGHT IN THE FACE SENDING HIM AND THE REFEREE OUT OF THE FOUNTAIN!
VIC WATERS: Damn referees. BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo picks Rat Fink up off of the referee…. HE NAILS HIM WITH A DEVIL’S RAGDOLL RIGHT ON THE CONCRETE! Diablo makes the cover but the ref is still groggy! 1--------------------------------2------------------THE AMERICAN BREAKS UP THE FALL!
VIC WATERS: Very close, Diablo was just a second away from overcoming the tremendous odds working against him here tonight.
BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo gets up…. THE AMERICAN NAILS HIM A ROUND HOUSE KICK TO THE HEAD! Diablo got clocked by that one and he just managed to avoid being sent into the fountain once more!
VIC WATERS: Diablo needs to learn how to defend all these kicks to the head, or his time with the BAD World Heavyweight Title will be short.
BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK GRABS A CHAIR! Diablo is getting up from the round house kick…. BAM! RAT FINK NAILED HIM ACROSS THE HEAD HARD! Diablo some how manages to stay on his feet, he dares Rat Fink to nail him again…. RAT FINK NAILS HIM AGAIN AND THIS TIME DIABLO GOES DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Those were some chair shots from hell that Diablo took. Things don’t look good for him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink is calling for his finisher… HE IS GOING FOR A CRUCIFIX POWERBOMB! Rat Fink lifts him up…. DIABLO ESCAPES OUT! Rat Fink charges Diablo…. DIABLO CATCHES HIM WITH A DDT! The American grabs the chair and swings at Diablo…. DIABLO KICKS IT IN HIS FACE! THE AMERICAN IS DOWN! This crowd is going nuts!
VIC WATERS: Diablo is fighting like only he knows how.. BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, FOOTBOT…. WHERE DID HE COME FROM?! FOOTBOT TAKES THE PRETZEL CART AND HE RAMS IT INTO THE BACK OF DIABLO SENDING HIM CRASHING TO THE GROUND! He ran him down with a damn pretzel cart!
VIC WATERS: Oh man. BRANDON JACOBS: Footbot is trying to wake up Rat Fink…. Rat Fink gets up and he sees the bloody Diablo on the ground… RAT FINK PICKS DIABLO UP… FALCON’S ARROW! The ref goes down to make the count! 1-----------------------------2------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match and NEW BAD WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION…. RAT FINK! VIC WATERS: This crowd looks awfully pissed. They are throwing stuff at Rat Fink and company.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink grabs the belt and The American and Footbot head quickly towards a taxi which is waiting for them. Damn thieves.
VIC WATERS: Diablo just couldn’t over come all three of them. This match had no rules, so Rat Fink who has been screwed over more than anyone else in the WFW just took advantage of that.
BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo may have finally been beat, but it took three men to do it. If it wasn’t for that damn Footbot, Diablo would still be champion.
VIC WATERS: I doubt this is the last time these two will meet. BRANDON JACOBS: That is for sure, okay folks, when we come back from this commercial break, we are going to be back with a match that was originally scheduled to be the main event for NYC Unplugged, but because of heated words between Edmunds & Copycat and Gideon & Felix Red, we’ve called and audible and that is the new main event of the show. Our next match is the dream team of Manson & Doc Silver taking on Shawn Hart & Alex Wylde in WFW debut! It should be great, stay tuned folks!
