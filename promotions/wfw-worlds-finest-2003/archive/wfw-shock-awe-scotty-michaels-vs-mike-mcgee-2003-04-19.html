WFW Shock & Awe: Scotty Michaels vs. Mike McGee

19 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 19 April 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Apr-19-03 AT 12:08&nbsp;PM (EST)]BRANDON JACOBS: We are back from our commercial break and we are set to find out the #1 contender to the winner of the Manson vs. Rat Fink match tonight!
VIC WATERS: Mike McGee has lost some really close matches lately, but I have a feeling that streak may come to an end tonight. McGee when he applies himself is one of the best performers in all of wrestling and tonight, he has something to prove.
BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels has made such a strong impact since his debut though, it may take a lot to stop his current momentum. The fans have really taken to Scotty Michaels, love him or hate him, he has certainly impassioned these wrestling fans here tonight.
VIC WATERS: That he has, but tonight he needs to take the big step up and become a title contender.
BRANDON JACOBS: Let’s not keep this crowd waiting any longer, lets send this match down to ringside!
::Generic Office Muzak blares over the loud speaker:: RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from the Home Office in New Brunswick, NJ! Terry Tate’s worst nightmare, “MR. MIDDLE MANAGEMENT” MIKE MCGEE!
::Mike McGee walks gingerly down to the ring to the loud boos of the crowd:: ::Livin’ on a Prayer” by Bon Jovi rocks over the loud speaker:: RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Philadelphia, PA…. THE SUPERSTAR SCOTTY MICHAELS! ::Scotty Michaels runs down to ringside and rushes into the ring:: ************************************************** ************************************************** ***********
#1 CONTENDER MATCH: “The Viewer’s Choice” Scotty Michaels vs. Mike McGee ************************************************** ************************************************** ***********
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Mike McGee rushes Michaels and NAILS HIM WITH A KNEE LIFT TO THE CHEST Mike McGee tries to lift Michaels up for the spinning Brainbuster… BLOCKED BY MICHAELS!
VIC WATERS: Mike McGee is showing he is all business here tonight! No wasted time! BRANDON JACOBS: SCOTTY MICHAELS BLOCKS IT! HE LIFTS MCGEE UP FOR HIS OWN BRAINBUSTER…. HE DOES IT… AND HE NAILS HIM WITH IT!
VIC WATERS: That is his setup, this match could be over quickly! BRANDON JACOBS: McGee is dazed and confused and Michaels is going up to the top rope… HE IS GOING TO TRY TO END IT ALL! McGee is getting up… THE HEARTBREAKER HOUSTON HANGOVER! He clocked McGee cleanly with that!
VIC WATERS: This match is over! BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels covers McGee! 1----------------2--------------KICKOUT! VIC WATERS: My God, how did he kick out of that?! BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels drags him to the center of the ring…. He picks McGee up off the mat…. HE LIFTS HIM UP…. MCGEE BREAKS LOOSE AND FALLS BEHIND HIM! McGee rolls him up! 1-------------2------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: That was oh so close, where McGee is getting this from I don’t know. BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS STRIKES MCGEE WITH A FOREARM SMASH! THAT CLOCKED HIM HARD ON THE CHIN!
VIC WATERS: If he had hit any him harder, that shot may have knocked him out cold! BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels bounces off the ropes… HE NAILS MCGEE WITH A FLYING FOREARM! VIC WATERS: ARRIBA! BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels lifts the barely conscious McGee up… BRAINBUSTER! Michaels makes the cover! 1-------------2------------3!!! SCOTT MICHAELS WINS THE MATCH AND IS THE NEW #1 CONTENDER TO THE WFW NORTH AMERICAN TITLE!
VIC WATERS: Even though that match was short, I haven’t seen something as intense as that in a long, long time. McGee just made a few fatal mistakes and he found himself yet again looking up at the ring lights.
BRANDON JACOBS: Try to calm yourself Vic, because coming up next, Doc Silver & Shawn Hart are teaming up against Richard Gideon & Psycho in a Falls Count Anywhere match!
VIC WATERS: D-O-C! DOC! DOC! DOC! BRANDON JACOBS: We will be back with the World Champion’s match, right after this commercial break!
