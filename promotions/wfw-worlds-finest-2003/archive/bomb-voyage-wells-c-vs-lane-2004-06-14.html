WFW — Card

BOMB Voyage: Wells (c) vs. Lane

14 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 14 June 2004 JONATHAN MARX: It is time for the BAD World Heavyweight Title match between Jared Wells and Mike Lane. In order to win the match, one man has to throw the other over the side of the boat and into the water.
VIC WATERS: Thankfully these waters off the coast of Hawaii are around seventy degrees all of the time and rather pollution free. So any foreign substances they put in their body to poison themselves is their own doing. ::takes a sip of his Poland Spring water::
JONATHAN MARX: Mike Lane put up a hell of a performance out of the gate against Michael Dice and it would be interesting to see who would have won that match, but now Lane is on equal footing with the champ after that attack by Bartleby.
VIC WATERS: Personally, I don’t see why Mike Lane is even getting this title shot. What has he done to earn it? Wells has been rolling over people left and right. Lane isn’t even a match for him.
JONATHAN MARX: The WFW wanted to reward the two newcomers for such a fine performance in their match and since they showed such a affinity for brawling, the winner of the match just seemed like a natural choice. Mike Lane and Wells are very similar in many aspects so I expect this to be a close match.
VIC WATERS: Wells is my favorite wrestler in WFW right now. He doesn’t take any guff from anyone or bog himself down with excuses. He has come a long way from how he was when he first entered the league.
JONATHAN MARX: Now that I agree with, Wells has made one hell of a comeback and he is showing everyone right now why he was a former WWL World Heavyweight Champion. But tonight he is facing a hell of a challenge in Mike Lane, this is a guy who came from similar experiences with a toughness you rarely see today. It many ways, it is sort of like Wells is facing himself.
VIC WATERS: Some of the toughest battles we face are with ourselves… But Wells ISN’T facing himself, Wells is facing Mike Lane and Mike Lane isn’t even in Wells’ league. Now stop all this malarkey and lets get on with this damn match already.
JONATHAN MARX: Fine, fine, folks, lets send this match to the ring and get this one underway!
(CUEUP: “Nasty Ho" by Nuerotic Outsiders) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, one of the toughest men in all of wrestling, from Braintree, Massachusetts, MIKE LANE!
::everyone patiently waits for Lane to come out but Lane doesn’t come out:: JONATHAN MARX: Where is Mike Lane? VIC WATERS: Maybe he missed his cue, or maybe Bartleby took care of him as well. JONATHAN MARX: They are just going to go ahead and introduce Wells. (CUEUP: Busted In The Hood by Cypress Hill) RING ANNOUNCER: and his opponent, the former three time WWL World Heavyweight Champion and current BAD World Heavyweight Champion…. “RAGE ‘O FIRE” JARED WELLS!
::Lane drags a bloody Wells from the corridor through the curtain:: JONATHAN MARX: LANE ATTACKED WELLS BEFORE THE BELL IN THE BACK! VIC WATERS: How long have these two been fighting?! JONATHAN MARX: Who knows but Wells is bleeding a gusher from the start… LANE THROWS WELLS INTO THE CROWD OF PEOPLE AND HE LANDS ON A COUPLE OF SENIOR CITIZENS!
VIC WATERS: Didn’t Wink pay Lane off to behave? JONATHAN MARX: Apparently not enough… Wells is starting to get up…. LANE CHARGES AND HE HITS WITH A FOREARM SMASH RIGHT TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD! This Lane is a cheap shot artist.
VIC WATERS: This is for the BAD World Heavyweight Title, there are no rules except the winner has to throw the other one into the ocean. Lane knows that and he is taking it to Wells.
JONATHAN MARX: Lane is yelling for people to get out of the way and people are fleeing every which way. LANE PICKS WELLS UP IN THE AIR…….. STALLING BRAINBUSTER RIGHT ONTO THE DECK OF THE SHIP!
VIC WATERS: Wells is going to be digging splinters out of his head for awhile. JONATHAN MARX: MIKE LANE TAKES A WOODEN CHAIR AND HE BASHES WELLS REPEATEDLY IN THE BACK WITH IT!
VIC WATERS: Lane is stepping it up a notch against Wells because he knows he let Dice comeback in that match and it ultimately cost him the victory.
JONATHAN MARX: LANE FINALLY BREAKS THE CHAIR OVER THE BACK OF WELLS AND WELLS BACK IS ALL RED FROM BEING SCRATCHED UP!
VIC WATERS: You would almost would rather be beaten by a metal chair because it is safer. JONATHAN MARX: Mike Lane picks up Wells and he is telling everyone to clear out…. HE IS HEADED TOWARDS THE RAIL OF THE SHIP! The fans are moving out of the way.
VIC WATERS: Things don’t look good for Wells…. JONATHAN MARX: MIKE LANE IRISH WHIPS WELLS INTO THE SIDE OF THE SHIP AND WELLS GOES BACK FIRST INTO SIDE!
VIC WATERS: Wells has to get out of there or he is going to lose his title he worked so hard to win.
JONATHAN MARX: Wells is trying to catch his breath but Lane is giving him a chance to recover. Lane slowly walks over to Wells as Wells is trying to slowly pull himself away.
VIC WATERS: This Lane is mentally deranged. JONATHAN MARX: LANE COMES UP FROM BEHIND WELLS AND HE HOOKS ON A WAISTLOCK! LANE IS TRYING TO GERMAN SUPLEX WELLS OFF OF THE SHIP!
VIC WATERS: Damn, that could go in our TV intro. JONATHAN MARX: LANE IS TRYING TO GET WELLS TO GO UP BUT WELLS IS FIGHTING IT…. WELLS HITS HIM WITH AN ELBOW RIGHT IN THE FACE! HE NAILS HIM WITH ANOTHER AND ANOTHER! Lane’s nose is bleeding…
VIC WATERS: Wells fired back with everything he had… JONATHAN MARX: WELLS GRABS A CHAIR AND HE HURLS IT AT LANE WHO DUCKS AND IT FLIES OFF THE SIDE OF THE BOAT INTO THE WATER! Wells fires again…. THIS TIME THE CHAIR CATCHES LANE RIGHT IN THE FACE AND LANE GOES DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Who would have thought flying chairs would be a great threat than seagulls on a cruise.
JONATHAN MARX: Wells is taking some rope that was on the deck of the ship and a chair and he is headed over to Lane…
VIC WATERS: This doesn’t look good for Lane… JONATHAN MARX: WELLS PUTS THE CHAIR ON THE BACK OF LANE AND HE STANDS ON AND HE WRAPS THE ROPE AROUND THE NECK OF LANE AND HE STARTS PULLING BACK WITH ALL HE HAS!
VIC WATERS: He is riding him like a jet ski… JONATHAN MARX: Lane is grabbing the rope trying to give himself a little breathing room from the lock it has on his neck!
VIC WATERS: After Lane jumped and bloodied Wells before the match, Wells has no compassion for Lane.
JONATHAN MARX: THE CHAIR CRACKS AND WELLS LETS GO OF THE ROPE! VIC WATERS: I don’t know whether that is a blessing or a curse.. JONATHAN MARX: Wells takes the broken part of the seat and throws the rest away…. WELLS IS TRYING TO CUT OPEN THE FOREHEAD OF LANE WITH THE SHARD OF THE CHAIR!
VIC WATERS: Jesus Christ… JONATHAN MARX: WELLS CUTS LANE OPEN AND THE REFEREE IS COMING OVER TO MAKE SURE HE DOESN’T GO TOO FAR!
VIC WATERS: With both busted open, when one goes over the rail into the salt water, it is really going to sting with those open wounds.
JONATHAN MARX: The referee is trying to pull the piece of chair out of Wells hands….WELLS LETS GO AND THE REFEREE FALLS BACK ONTO THE DECK!
VIC WATERS: The referees in this place should get hazard pay. JONATHAN MARX: The referee appears to have busted himself open when that shard of the chair cut his forehead when Wells let go.
VIC WATERS: That referee looks almost as bad as Wells and Lane. JONATHAN MARX: Wells is going over to check on the referee to make sure he will be able to declare a winner.
VIC WATERS: Wells may have just screwed himself. JONATHAN MARX: Wells is slapping around the referee trying to wake him up…. VIC WATERS: Wait a second, look at Lane! JONATHAN MARX: A bloodied and battered Lane is getting up and he has the rope! LANE RUNS UP BEHIND WELLS AND PUTS THE ROPE AROUND HIS THROAT!
VIC WATERS: Lane has a look of desperation in his eye which I don’t like… JONATHAN MARX: LANE IS DRAGGING WELLS OVER TOWARDS THE RAIL OF THE SHIP BY HIS NECK! VIC WATERS: You don’t think he is planning… JONATHAN MARX: YES I DO! LANE LIFTS WELLS UP AND HE HITS HIM ON THE RAILING! HE IS GOING TO HANG WELLS! Here comes the bloodied and dazed referee…
VIC WATERS: Wells better think of something fast… JONATHAN MARX: LANE IS TRYING TO PUSH WELLS OFF THE RAILING BUT WELLS IS WRAPPING HIS ARMS AROUND THE RAILING TO KEEP FROM FALLING OFF!
VIC WATERS: HOLD ON WELLS! JONATHAN MARX: Lane gives up trying to push Wells off and HE SITS IN THE RAILING AS WELL AND HE STARTS EXCHANGING PUNCHES WITH WELLS!
VIC WATERS: This is dangerous for both men. JONATHAN MARX: LANE LOOKS LIKE HE IS GETTING THE BETTER OF IT! Lane fires a right hand….. WELLS FALLS OFF THE RAILING BACK ONTO THE DECK!
VIC WATERS: Thank god he fell on the deck and not into the water or he could have lost his title.
JONATHAN MARX: LANE IS CLIMBING UP ONTO TOP OF THE RAILING… Wells is getting up…. DOUBLE AXE HANDLE RIGHT TO HE BACK OF WELLS AND WELLS GOES HEAD FIRST INTO THE DECK!
VIC WATERS: Lane took an unnecessary risk there, but it paid off. JONATHAN MARX: Lane walks over and grabs several wooden chairs which he throws down on the ground one by one, what could he be up to?
VIC WATERS: This is Lane, he’ll do anything. JONATHAN MARX: LANE THROWS WELLS FACE FIRST INTO THE PILE OF CHAIRS! Lane is slowly walking over to Wells who looks utterly exhausted.
VIC WATERS: Wells has been through hell and back because of Lane, he hasn’t had a match as wild as this since the Superbowl of Wrestling when he had to fight off three other men to win the title.
JONATHAN MARX: Lane picks Wells off the ground… LANE IS GOING FOR THE 1999! Lane hooks the arms…. HE HIT IT AND LANE WENT HEAD FIRST INTO THE CHAIRS!
VIC WATERS: Now all Lane has to do is throw him over the railing and he has this match won.
JONATHAN MARX: LANE IS DRAGGING AN UNCONSCIOUS WELLS BY THE HAIR TOWARDS THE RAILING ALONG THE DECK! My god…
VIC WATERS: Wells has no more energy left to fight. JONATHAN MARX: Lane picks up Wells and stands him against the edge of the ship near the white life preserver (with rope attached)… LANE IS CALLING TO ASKING FOR A RING ATTENDANT TO HAND HIM A CHAIR BUT THE RING ATTENDANT REFUSES! Lane walks over and HE SNATCHES ONE OUT OF THE HANDS OF A FAN!
VIC WATERS: Those ring attendants know that if they help Lane in any way, they are going to be punished for it.
JONATHAN MARX: Lane folds up the chair…. HE CHARGES WELLS AND WELLS DUCKS OUT OF THE WAY! Wells kicks the chair out of Lane’s hands!
VIC WATERS: YES! JONATHAN MARX: Lane goes to pick up the chair…. WELLS GRABS THE LIFE PRESERVER AND PUTS IT ON LANE TO KEEP HIM FROM BEING ABLE TO USE HIS ARMS!
VIC WATERS: This is Jared Wells’ chance! JONATHAN MARX: WELLS HITS LANE WITH THE SAVATE KICK AND LANE STUMBLES BACK AGAINST THE RAILING! He almost went over that time!
VIC WATERS: Lane can’t protect himself… he can’t defend himself for long! JONATHAN MARX: WELLS BACKS UP INTO THE AUDIENCE… VIC WATERS: Uh oh… JONATHAN MARX: WELLS CHARGES LANE WITH A LARIAT BUT LANE DUCKS DOWN AND BACK BODY DROPS HIM OVER THE RAILING! OH MY GOD, WELLS MANAGED TO HOLD ONTO THE RAILING ON THE WAY OVER!
VIC WATERS: Lane almost won the title! JONATHAN MARX: Lane turns around and he Wells is still hanging on…. LANE FREES ONE OF HIS HANDS FROM THE LIFE PRESERVER AND TRIES TO PRY WELLS HANDS OFF THE RAILING!
VIC WATERS: Wells has to hold on! JONATHAN MARX: WELLS REACHES UP IN DESPERATION…. WAIT A SECOND, WELLS JUST SMILED…. WELLS HOLDS ON WITH ONE HAND AND HE GRABS THE ROPE ATTACHED TO LANE’S LIFE PRESERVER AND LANE GOES UP AND OVER THE SIDE INTO THE WATER! WELLS HAS WON IT!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. AND STILL BAD WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION…. JARED WELLS!
JONATHAN MARX: WAIT A SECOND, THIS ONE ISN’T OVER…. WELLS HAS PULLED HIMSELF BACK ONTO THE RAILING!
VIC WATERS: He has this won, what is he going to do here?! JONATHAN MARX: WELLS PERCHES ON THE RAILING…. WELLS FLIES OFF WITH AN ALABAMA JAM FROM THE HEAVENS RIGHT ONTO LANE IN THE LIFE DESERVER BELOW!
VIC WATERS: Good god… JONATHAN MARX: Thank god Lane is in that life preserver of that shot could have killed him.
VIC WATERS: Wells came this close to losing this match the same way that he won it when Rat Fink fell off the platform to his defeat.
JONATHAN MARX: Folks, we are going to take a short commercial break to fish these two out of the water, when we comeback, IT IS TIME FOR SOME SIX MAN ACTION…. STAY TUNED!
