BOMB Voyage: Anarky vs. Iron Lion

12 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 12 June 2004 JONATHAN MARX: We are back and it is time for two titans of the ring to collide, the massive seven foot three Iron Lion will go one on one with Anarky. Your thoughts Vic?
VIC WATERS: There is no doubt that Iron Lion is impressive and he is on the rise, but Anarky is the same man who defeated Maelstrom. He is the same man who made people fear for their life when he almost caused a riot wrestling Manson. How is someone as young as Iron Lion going to be able to beat him?
JONATHAN MARX: Anarky is the greatest brawler that I have ever seen in my lifetime and I’ve seen a lot of the great brawlers on television and in the ring. He has a way of fighting against the odds and over coming any obstacle in his way. But I don’t know how he is going to deal with someone as big as Iron Lion, Jared Wells and Anarky had trouble taking him on in a two on one, how is either man going to be able to do it alone?
VIC WATERS: LOVE was just a bit too cocky when they saw Scotty Michaels no showed early and they let the advantage get to their head, Iron Lion is sort of like the Pistons… they may look like have knocked down the legend but legends have a way of getting back up. Anarky is going to win this match. It won’t be easy, but come crunch time, Anarky is really going to step it up.
JONATHAN MARX: I’ve stood side by side with Anarky in G.O.D., you don’t need to sell me on him. It was the honor of my life to compete next to him and Manson. Even though he has turned to the dark side and takes great joy in reeking havoc, I still respect him. Anarky has never been one for conformity. But the last big man Anarky had to face was Copycat and we all know what happened in that one.
VIC WATERS: Yeah, I sure do, that was a fun match, it marked the end of any chance of Copycat getting a World Title Shot.
JONATHAN MARX: That is another argument for another match, folks, LETS SEND THIS ONE TO THE RING!
(CUEUP: “Is This The End” by Creed) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from Anchorage, Alaska, a man focused on bringing honor, pride, and strength back to professional wrestling, IRON LION!
::Iron Lion walks down to the roar of the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: Military Marching Music) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, a world champion ten times over and former Mr. Vicious title holder, from Hartford, Connecticut… ANARKY!
::Anarky comes down to the ring in camouflage warpaint and combat boots, camo pants and a green sleeveless shirt while wrapped in an American flag and he waves back and forth as the crowd boos him::
JONATHAN MARX: What is Anarky up to here? VIC WATERS: He is behaving very strangely even for Anarky. *bell rings* JONATHAN MARX: ANARKY THROWS THE FLAG OUT OF THE RING AND THEN SPITS ON IT! VIC WATERS: Now that is the Anarky I know. JONATHAN MARX: An angered Iron Lion walks towards the center of the ring as Anarky is heckling the crowd…. IRON LION GOES TO ATTACK BUT ANARKY TURNS AROUND AND DIVES FOR THE LEG OF IRON LION AND TAKES HIM DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Smart move by Anarky, if he can take out the legs of Iron Lion, this will make the match much easier.
JONATHAN MARX: Anarky stands up while holding the right leg of Iron Lion and starts twist the leg in a legbar….. IRON LION IS TRYING TO KICK ANARKY WITH HIS OTHER LEG BUT ANARKY IS CONSTANTLY MOVING TO AVOID GETTING HIT!
VIC WATERS: Anarky while he can take a lot of physical abuse is also very good at getting himself out of the way of punishment which can switch the momentum of the match.
JONATHAN MARX: IRON LION KICKS AGAIN AND THIS TIME ANARKY CATCHES THE OTHER LEG! Anarky turns Iron Lion over and HE LOCKS ON THE SCORPION DEATHLOCK!
VIC WATERS: Anarky isn’t doing this to get a submission, he is doing this right now in order to wear his opponent down.
JONATHAN MARX: While Anarky doesn’t do many submission, remember, he won the War Games cage for his team with a leglock submission. IRON LION IS TRYING TO PULL HIMSELF TO THE ROPES AND HE IS USING ALL HIS STRENGTH TO MAKE IT TO THE OTHER SIDE OF THE RING!
VIC WATERS: Iron Lion at his size has natural strength which makes him a tough adversary for anyone.
JONATHAN MARX: Iron Lion has almost got it….. ANARKY BREAKS THE HOLD AND HE PULLS IRON LION BACK TO THE CENTER OF THE RING TO THE BOOS OF THE CROWD!
VIC WATERS: So close… JONATHAN MARX: Anarky grabs the right leg…. SPINNING TOE HOLD BY ANARKY! Anarky is twisting and twisting around and Iron Lion face is now showing the effects of the pain he is in as he grimaces while biting his lower lip.
VIC WATERS: Anarky has struck a nerve, who would have thought that Anarky would have came into the match with a technical game plan like this. I think it caught Iron Lion a bit off guard.
JONATHAN MARX: Anarky breaks the spinning toe hold and he turns him over and HE STARTS RAMMING IRON LION’S KNEE REPEATEDLY INTO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Iron Lion’s knee has a lot of weight to support, he is going to be in sorry shape after Anarky is done with him.
JONATHAN MARX: Anarky lets go and Iron Lion clutches the right knee in agony as Anarky looks on with a demented smile on his face.
VIC WATERS: Anarky enjoys every second of this after the abuse he took at Shock & Awe at the hands of Iron Lion.
JONATHAN MARX: ANARKY DRAGS IRON LION OVER TO THE CORNER….. ANARKY SLIDES OUT OF THE RING AND HE PULLS IRON LION’S LEGS BETWEEN THE POLE!
VIC WATERS: Now this is the Anarky I remember… JONATHAN MARX: ANARKY RAMS THE RIGHT LEG REPEATEDLY INTO THE SIDE OF THE POLE! Iron Lion tries to recoil his legs put Anarky is hanging onto them with all the weight he has to keep Iron Lion from getting away.
VIC WATERS: Anarky is going for the kill, he isn’t going to let him get away. JONATHAN MARX: ANARKY GRABS THE LEGS OF IRON LION AND HE HOOKS ON A FIGURE FOUR AROUND THE RING POST! IRON LION IS HOLLERING IN PAIN! The ref yells at Anarky to break the hold but Anarky is ignoring him so he heads to the outside.
VIC WATERS: He could break his leg and end his career right here! JONATHAN MARX: The ref slides to the outside and he is trying to pry Anarky off as he counts! 1-----------2-------------3------------4-------- ANARKY JUST BREAKS THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: The referee have been told that they don’t want matches plagued with DQs and count outs so our referees are a bit more liberal than usual, but Anarky really could have gotten himself DQed there.
JONATHAN MARX: Anarky rolls back into the ring…. And he is yelling at Iron Lion to get up….
VIC WATERS: Anarky is trying desperately to get into Iron Lion’s head and it may be working.
JONATHAN MARX: Iron Lion is using the ropes to get up…. ANARKY KICKS HIS LEG OUT FROM UNDER HIM SENDING IRON LION BACK TO THE MAT TO THE BOOS OF THE CROWD!
VIC WATERS: Look, Iron Lion is trying to get back up! JONATHAN MARX: ANARKY IS KICK AWAY AT IRON LION’S LEG…. IRON LION THRUSTS HIMSELF ON ANARKY AND BULL RUSHES HIM INTO THE CORNER! Anarky crashes back first hard into the turnbuckle!
VIC WATERS: Iron Lion can’t move all that well so he is just going to back Anarky into a corner and go to town, Iron Lion is always thinking, on the a man his size, it makes dangerous lethal.
JONATHAN MARX: IRON LION STARTS CHOPPING AWAY AT ANARKY IN THE CORNER REPEATEDLY! Anarky is going to be covered with welts in the morning from those shots.
VIC WATERS: Anarky has to get out of that corner immediately for his sake. JONATHAN MARX: Anarky is starting to slump over…. Iron Lion picks up Anarky over his shoulder…. IRON LION RAMS HIM INTO THE TURNBUCKLE HARD BACK FIRST AGAIN AND AGAIN!
VIC WATERS: My god, Iron Lion is manhandling him! JONATHAN MARX: Iron Lion limps from the corner… and Anarky falls down to his knees in pain…. IRON LION CHARGES WITH A KICK TO THE FACE BUT ANARKY MOVES OUT OF THE WAY AND NAILS WITH A SHOT TO THE GROIN!
VIC WATERS: Iron Lion was blocking his view, the referee didn’t even see it. JONATHAN MARX: ANARKY JUMPS ON IRON LION AND HE STARTS POUNDING ON IRON LION WITH CLOSED FISTS REPEATEDLY IN THE HEAD! The ref tries to go over to stop him… ANARKY PUSHES THE REF DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Uh oh… JONATHAN MARX: ANARKY STARTS CHOKING IRON LION WITH BOTH HANDS AND THE REF IS TRYING TO REGAIN HIS SENSES! Anarky has gone nuts!
VIC WATERS: More than usual… JONATHAN MARX: Iron Lion is for his life here…. HE IS ABLE TO PUSH ANARKY OFF OF HIM! VIC WATERS: Thank god for Iron Lion… JONATHAN MARX: Iron Lion is struggling to get up on that injured right leg…. ANARKY BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND HE HITS IRON LION WITH A FLYING FOREARM AND SEVEN FOOT IRON LION GOES OVER THE TOP ROPE!
VIC WATERS: This is the Anarky I remember, this is the Anarky which countless legends like Maelstrom.
JONATHAN MARX: The referee is up…. HE GRABS ARM OF ANARKY AND ANARKY TURNS AROUND AND THE REFEREE IS GIVING HIM AN EARFUL FOR WHAT HE DID… THE REF IS GETTING INTO ANARKY’S FACE AND BACK HIM TOWARDS THE ROPES!!
VIC WATERS: Referees shouldn’t let their anger get the best of them, they are suppose to be neutral. This a Billy Martinesque like rant by the ref
JONATHAN MARX: An injured Iron Lion is starting to get up… IRON LION PULLS ANARKY’S LEGS FROM UNDER HIM AND ANARKY CRASHES FACE FIRST INTO THE MAT! Iron Lion slides Anarky to the outside under the bottom rope!
VIC WATERS: Uh oh… JONATHAN MARX: IRON LION STARTS FIRING AT ANARKY WITH EUROPEAN UPPERCUTS AND ANARKY IS REELING BACKWARDS!
VIC WATERS: He is driving him towards the steps! JONATHAN MARX: ANARKY FALLS OVER THE STEPS AND HE CRASHES ONTO THE GROUND! Iron Lion limps over… HE PICKS UP THE STEEL STAIRS AND THROWS THEM DOWN ONTO ANARKY LEGS!
VIC WATERS: Those damn steps weight a ton… JONATHAN MARX: IRON LION DROPS A LEG DROP DOWN ON THE HEAD OF ANARKY AND HE LOOKS LIKE HE IS OUT OF IT! Iron Lion eyes our broadcast table…oh no….
::Marx and Vic get up in a hurry:: JONATHAN MARX: IRON LION IS CLIMBING ONTO THE BROADCAST TABLE AND HE FLIES OFF AND HITS THE STRENGTH OF HONOR (LIONSAULT) ON ANARKY!
VIC WATERS: 360 pounds down on the chest of Anarky…. JONATHAN MARX: Iron Lion kicks off the steps with his good leg and he picks up Anarky and he rolls him back into the ring.
VIC WATERS: This could be all she wrote for Anarky… JONATHAN MARX: Iron Lion rolls back in and he goes for the cover! 1----------------------------------------------2--------------------------------------- ANARKY MANAGES TO GET THE SHOULDER UP!
VIC WATERS: Anarky is certainly a fighter, but even I don’t know how he can take all of this abuse.
JONATHAN MARX: Iron Lion picks up Anarky…. TRIPLE RIB BREAKER OVER IRON LION’S LEFT LEG! Anarky is clutching his ribs in pain!
VIC WATERS: Anarky shouldn’t have kicked out, this is only going to be worse on him. JONATHAN MARX: Iron Lion picks up Anarky….. ANARKY FIRES AWAY WITH PUNCHES AT IRON LION! Iron Lion is trying to keep upright with his one bad wheel…
VIC WATERS: FALL DAMNIT FALL! JONATHAN MARX: IRON LION IS TEETERING…. IRON LION SHOVES ANARKY INTO THE ROPES…. ANARKY ARMS GET CAUGHT UP IN THE TOP ROPE AND HE IS TRAPPED!
VIC WATERS: The ref needs to help him out of here! JONATHAN MARX: IRON LION CHARGES ANARKY AND HE STARTS NAILING HIM WITH BIG KNEE LIFTS RIGHT INTO THE RIBS! This mighty behemoth is going to down on Anarky!
VIC WATERS: Someone needs to stop him! JONATHAN MARX: IRON LION GOES FOR ONE MORE KNEE LIFT AND THE TOP ROPE BREAK HOLDING ANARKY!
VIC WATERS: Damn, that just shows the power behind those knee lifts. JONATHAN MARX: Iron Lion drags him to the center of the ring and goes for the cover! The ref goes down for the count! 1-------------------------------------------------2--------------------------------- ANARKY GETS THE SHOULDER UP AGAIN! The ref goes to signal to the back that they are going to need the ring fixed.
VIC WATERS: Anarky got hit so hard by that kneelift that the top rope broke, and yet he is still able to fight!
JONATHAN MARX: Iron Lion picks Anarky up…. ANARKY FIGHTS OUT OF IRON LION’S GRASP AND ELBOWS IRON LION IN THE STOMACH! Anarky bounces off the ropes…. ANARKY ELBOWS IRON LION RIGHT IN THE HEAD AND A DAZED IRON LION IS WOBBLING ON HIS ONE GOOD LEG!
VIC WATERS: Iron Lion looks awfully tired, the abuse he took in the first ten minutes of the match to his legs and all of the offense on Anarky look like they have worn him down.
JONATHAN MARX: ANARKY RACING TO THE CORNER… ANARKY IS CLIMBING UP TO THE TOP ROPE…. TOP ROPE CLOTHESLINE BY ANARKY… NO WAIT! IRON LION CAUGHT HIM IN MIDAIR BUT HE IS HAVING TROUBLE HOLDING ANARKY’S WEIGHT BECAUSE OF THE BAD KNEE….
VIC WATERS: Come on! JONATHAN MARX: IRON LION FALLS BACK AND ANARKY IS ON TOP FOR THE PINFALL! The ref goes down for the count! 1-------------------------------------2--------------------------- IRON LION MANAGES TO GET HIS SHOULDER UP!
VIC WATERS: NO DAMNIT! JONATHAN MARX: Anarky backs up from Iron Lion and he is waiting for him to get up… looks like he is going for the running shoulder block…. IRON LION TURNS AROUND AND ANARKY CHARGES… IRON LION SEES ANARKY AND PLANTS HIS GOOD LEG….. ANARKY HITS HIM FULL STEAM BUT HE REFUSES TO GO DOWN!
VIC WATERS: OOOOH… JONATHAN MARX: IRON LION PICKS UP FOR THE PRIDE HAMMER (Running Powerbomb)…… HE HITS ON ANARKY! Iron Lion goes for the cover! 1-------------------------------------2-----------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of this match…. IRON LION! JONATHAN MARX: Iron Lion looks like he is grabbing his knee though, when he tried to hold Anarky back when he was delivering that running shoulder block, it seems like it did more harm than good. He’ll be okay for the next show though, you just have to hope it is 100% before the PPV.
VIC WATERS: Anarky came so close to beating Iron Lion, but he just had several bad breaks including a rope literally break which in many matches would have stopped the match right there. Whenever these two meet again in singles, it could be a completely different match.
JONATHAN MARX: Anarky looked much more focus like the Anarky of old…. But Iron Lion is just a mountain of a man and he found that out here today. Now Wells will have to answer the challenge if Iron Lion at the PPV and hope that he can stop him.
VIC WATERS: That is going to be an awfully tall task for Wells. JONATHAN MARX: Speaking of Wells, folks, when we comeback, Jared Wells will make his first BAD World Heavyweight Champion defense here tonight against the newcomer Mike Lane….. STAY TUNED!
