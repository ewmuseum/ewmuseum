Shock & Awe II: Anarky/Wells vs. Iron Lion/???

21 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 21 May 2004 JONATHAN MARX: Welcome back folks, we apologize for the delay but Jacobs has been seriously. We hope to have an update by night end on the extent of Jacobs’s injuries.
VIC WATERS: I don’t particularly like Brandon. But what Michaels & Bartleby did was completely wrong. Touching a ring announcer is a no no no matter how you slice it.
JONATHAN MARX: Have we gotten any word if Wink has found a replacement yet? VIC WATERS: Wink has been asking around but either people already have a match or they are too scared to face off against LOVE.
JONATHAN MARX: ::grabs his ear piece in order to hear what is being said more clearly:: We are getting news from the back that Iron Lion is DEMANDING TO FIGHT WELLS AND ANARKY ALONE!
VIC WATERS: Alone? JONATHAN MARX: I don’t know if this is the wisest choice to make either but we will see. VIC WATERS: We have already had way too much chaos here tonight already. JONATHAN MARX: LET’S SEND THIS ONE DOWN TO RINGSIDE AND GET THIS ONE UNDERWAY! (CUEUP: Busted In The Hood by Cypress Hill) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the former three time WWL World Heavyweight Champion and current BAD World Heavyweight Champion…. “RAGE ‘O FIRE” JARED WELLS!
::party lights flash and Wells is wheeled out by his personal secretary Jenna, who is wearing next to nothing, pushing Wells in a shopping cart who has the BAD World Heavyweight Title over his shoulder::
::Jenna leaves the ringside area and Wells exits the cart and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “Sober” by Tool) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, a world champion ten times over and former Mr. Vicious title holder, from Hartford, Connecticut… ANARKY!
::Anarky comes down to the ring to the loud boos of the crowd:: (CUEUP: “Is This The End” by Creed) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Anchorage, Alaska, a man focused on bringing honor, pride, and strength back to professional wrestling, IRON LION!
::Iron Lion walks down to the roar of the crowd and enters the ring:: JONATHAN MARX: The referee is telling Anarky and Wells to decide amongst themselves who is going to start this match…. WELLS AND ANARKY RUSH IRON LION!
*bell rings* VIC WATERS: If the ref thinks he is going to make Anarky and Wells tag in and out in this, he is dead wrong.
JONATHAN MARX: IRON LION IS PUSHED INTO THE CORNER AND THEY ARE FIRING AWAY WITH RIGHT HANDS!
VIC WATERS: I don’t care how big Iron Lion is, if these two can do what they want in that ring, Iron Lion is toast.
JONATHAN MARX: IRON LION KICKS ANARKY IN THE CHEST AND ANARKY GOES DOWN! VIC WATERS: Iron Lion with a HUGE boot to the chest! JONATHAN MARX: Wells backs up from Iron Lion…. IRON LION CHARGES WELLS AND NEARLY TAKES HIS HEAD OFF WITH A CLOTHESLINES!
VIC WATERS: Iron Lion isn’t going to go down with a good fight despite his partner abandoning him.
JONATHAN MARX: Iron Lion picks up Wells…. TRIPLE RIB BREAKER! Iron Lion drives the knee right into the chest of Wells and Wells is coughing as he is trying to regain his breath!
VIC WATERS: Well has been on such a role lately here in the WFW. I haven’t seen someone handle him like Iron Lion is right now in a long time.
JONATHAN MARX: You can be the toughest SOB on the block, but a man the size and intelligence of Iron Lion is very hard to overcome. I haven’t seen a young prospect like him come around in years.
VIC WATERS: Must be something in the water up there in Alaska. JONATHAN MARX: Iron Lion grabs the ropes and he is trying to drive his foot into the injured ribs of Wells…. ANARKY IS GETTING UP… ANARKY BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND HE HITS IRON LION WITH A FOREARM FROM BEHIND!
VIC WATERS: Anarky clocked him with it hard! JONATHAN MARX: Iron Lion turns around and looks at Anarky… IT DIDN’T EVEN BUDGE HIM! VIC WATERS: Iron Lion has good foot and a 120 on both of these guys. JONATHAN MARX: IRON LION STARTS CHOPPING AWAY AT ANARKY AND HE IS RIPPING HIM WITH SOME BRUTAL SHOTS!
VIC WATERS: I can hear those without my monitor. JONATHAN MARX: IRON LION GRABS THE ARM OF ANARKY AND HE GOES CHEST FIRST INTO THE TURNBUCKLE! Anarky grabs his chest like he just got punched.
VIC WATERS: Anarky took a hell of a blow to the sternum. JONATHAN MARX: Iron Lion picks up the stumbling Anarky…. MILITARY PRESS DOWN TO THE MAT! VIC WATERS: Iron Lion is looking so strong, I don’t even know if Maelstrom could take this monster of a man down.
JONATHAN MARX: Wells is up.... WELLS BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND HE HITS IRON LION WITH A KNEE FROM BEHIND RIGHT TO THE LOWER BACK! Iron Lion falls down to his knees in pain!
VIC WATERS: Wells finally was the one to knock the big man down to size. Now he has to stay on him.
JONATHAN MARX: WELLS STARTS UNLOADING WITH A SERIES OF KICKS TO THE BACK! Iron Lion falls down to the mat.
VIC WATERS: When Iron Lion falls down from his knees, he is almost the size of a teenager falling down.
JONATHAN MARX: WELLS LOCKS ON A CAMEL CLUTCH ON IRON LION! HE IS SITTING RIGHT ON THAT LOWER BACK OF IRON LION AND PULLING BACK ON THAT NECK!
VIC WATERS: PULL ON IT! PULL ON IT! JONATHAN MARX: Imagine the kuo Wells could pull off if he could manage to submit Iron Lion. He would shoot right up the rankings and get a shot at Manson.
VIC WATERS: Hell, the way Wells is going, he could make the BAD World Heavyweight Title greater than the WFW World Heavyweight Title and he wouldn’t need Manson’s belt.
JONATHAN MARX: The ref is checking on Iron Lion and he keeps on shaking his head no… ANARKY IS GETTING UP AFTER THE MILITARY PRESS AND HE WALKS OVER AND HE IS SLAPPING THE RESTRAINED IRON LION RIGHT IN THE FACE AS HE IS YELLING AT HIM TO GIVE UP!
VIC WATERS: Anarky is a master of psychology, he is able to get into your head and once he gets in, he is in there for life.
JONATHAN MARX: IRON LION IS TRYING TO PUSH HIS BODY FORWARD INTO ORDER TO GET WELLS TO BREAK THE HOLD! Anarky tells Wells to let go…. ANARKY KICKS IRON LION RIGHT IN THE BACK OF THE HEAD RIGHT AS RELEASES HIM!
VIC WATERS: I don’t know if it is good idea to let him go. JONATHAN MARX: Anarky is screaming at Wells to grab the other end of Iron Lion… ANARKY AND WELLS LIFT IRON LION UP AND PUT HIM ON THEIR SHOULDERS FOR A COMBINATION TORTURE RACK!
VIC WATERS: I’ve never even heard of this, let alone seen it. Who knows what devilish things are in the back of the mind of Anarky.
JONATHAN MARX: That list is way too long to get into here. The referee is checking to see if Iron Lion wants to give up and he is screaming in pain.
VIC WATERS: Back injuries for big men are a serious problem. Iron Lion might want to give up to fight another day.
JONATHAN MARX: Iron Lion isn’t a quitter. He came out tonight knowing that he didn’t have a partner because he has the will and heart of a champion to fight even when the odds are against him.
VIC WATERS: Looks, Iron Lion is trying to fight out of this. JONATHAN MARX: Come on Iron Lion… IRON LION MANAGES TO WIGGLE HIMSELF FREE AND HE FALLS BEHIND ANARKY AND WELLS RIGHT ON HIS FEET!
VIC WATERS: Those two better run, duck and jive is my motto. JONATHAN MARX: IRON LION RAMS THE HEADS OF ANARKY AND WELLS TOGETHER! Anarky grabs his head and he falls face first onto the mat. WELLS COMES BACK WITH A SERIES OF PUNCHES!
VIC WATERS: Wells has killed so many brain cells from his party lifestyle throughout his career, a headbutt isn’t even going to phase him.
JONATHAN MARX: I love Jared Wells. I really do. WELLS IS BACKING UP THE BIG MAN! VIC WATERS: Iron Lion is starting to wobble a bit from all that work they did on his back. JONATHAN MARX: WELLS HITS IRON LION WITH A SAVATE KICK AND HE FALLS DOWN TO THE MAT! VIC WATERS: TIMBER! JONATHAN MARX: Wells makes the cover! 1---------------------------------------2------------------------- IRON LION GETS THE SHOULDER UP!
VIC WATERS: He almost had him. JONATHAN MARX: Wells bounces off the ropes…. WELLS DROPS A LEGDROP! VIC WATERS: He is trying to keep Iron Lion down because while he is down on that mat, it takes out his whole size advantage.
JONATHAN MARX: Wells is going to the turnbuckle… WELLS IS CLIMBING UP TO THE ROPE… HE PERCHES ON THE TOP AND FLIES OFF WITH A TOP ROPE LEGDROP!
VIC WATERS: Wells may have it here! JONATHAN MARX: Wells makes the cover! 1---------------------------------------------2------------------------------------- IRON LION GETS HIS FOOT ON THE ROPES AS THIS CROWD COLLECTIVELY GASPS!
VIC WATERS: So damn close… JONATHAN MARX: Anarky is up in the corner and he is signaling to Wells that he needs to get him up for the Ragebomb. Wells is asking how he can do that with Iron Lion being so big and Anarky is coming over to help.
VIC WATERS: Anarky is such a kind and caring individual. JONATHAN MARX: After all the abuse he has taken today by Iron Lion, he wants to finish him off for good. WELLS ATTEMPTS PICK IRON LION UP ON HIS BACK AND ANARKY IS TRYING TO PUSH HIM UP INTO POSITION FROM BEHIND!
VIC WATERS: If they pull this off, it will be an absolute miracle. JONATHAN MARX: WAIT A SECOND, WELLS LOSES HIS GRIP ON IRON LION AND HE FALLS BEHIND HIM! Anarky waistlocks Iron Lion…. IRON LION STARTS THROW ELBOWS BACK IN AN ATTEMPT TO HIT ANARKY BUT ANARKY IS MOVING OUT OF THE WAY!
VIC WATERS: Those elbows could do some real damage. JONATHAN MARX: Wells starts firing at Iron Lion with closed fists and the ref is warning him to keep those hands open!
VIC WATERS: The ref hasn’t even made an attempt to get one of the members of LOVE out on the apron, but he is worried about a closed fist?
JONATHAN MARX: One has to wonder if Edmunds isn’t having some fun assigning Iron Lion the referees that he has. ANARKY LETS GO AND IRON LION STUMBLES FORWARD…. WELLS KICKS HIM IN THE MIDSECTION…. DDT! Wells planted him right on the mat!
VIC WATERS: Cover him! JONATHAN MARX: Wells goes down to the mat! 1------------------------------------------------2------------------------------------ IRON LION KICKS OUT AGAIN!
VIC WATERS: Anarky is pushing Wells aside… IT LOOKS LIKE HE IS CALLING FOR THE CHAOS BREAKER!
JONATHAN MARX: Anarky lifts him up…. IRON LION BEGINS WITH BOTH MEN ALTERNATING PUNCHES BETWEEN THEM! Both men are beginning to stagger… WELLS JUMPS AT IRON LION AND RAKES HIS EYES!
VIC WATERS: Iron Lion has one hell of a fighting spirit. JONATHAN MARX: ANARKY BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES…. HE HITS HIM WITH THE FLYING FOREARM AND THIS TIME IRON LION GOES DOWN!
VIC WATERS: These two may be the best tag team in wrestling today bar none. I don’t know two guys I’d rather have stand before me than those two.
JONATHAN MARX: Anarky is telling Wells to go for the RAGE BOMB AGAIN BUT THIS TIME WITHOUT HIS HELP! Wells picks up Iron Lion…. HE HIM HOOKED WITH HIS ARMS AND IS TRYING TO GET HIM OVER HIS SHOULDER!
VIC WATERS: What an incredible display of strength by Wells here even getting him up! JONATHAN MARX: NO! IRON LION FALLS BACK ONTO HIS FEET AND HE PUSHES WELLS INTO ANARKY AND ANARKY ROLLS TO THE OUTSIDE! A stunned Wells turns around….
VIC WATERS: Don’t turn around! JONATHAN MARX: IRON LION CATCHES HIM WITH A HIGH ANGLE SPINEBUSTER AND DRIVES WELLS DOWN ONTO THE MAT! This crowd has erupted!
CROWD: LION! LION! LION! VIC WATERS: Iron Lion is clutching his back in pain… JONATHAN MARX: Iron Lion picks up Wells…. CANADIAN BACKBREAKER AND IRON LION HAS WELLS OVER HIS SHOULDER OVER SIX FEET IN THE AIR AND WELLS IS YELLING FOR ANARKY!
VIC WATERS: Anarky seems to have taken a powder on the outside JONATHAN MARX: The ref is checking to see if Wells wants to give up but he is shaking his head no!
VIC WATERS: How much longer can Wells keep this up fight this big man like he has? JONATHAN MARX: Wells looks like he is on the verge of giving up….. IRON LION DROPS HIM DOWN TO THE GROUND BELOW!
VIC WATERS: Iron Lion wouldn’t let go unless he had something equally as painful in mind. JONATHAN MARX: IRON LION IS CALLING TO THE CROWD FOR THE PRIDE HAMMER! Anarky is waving to the back for help!
VIC WATERS: Uh oh. JONATHAN MARX: IRON LION PICKS UP WELLS….. HE DELIVERS THE PRIDE HAMMER RUNNING POWERBOMB! HERE COME PSYCHO AND FELIX RED WITH CHAIRS FROM THE BACK!
VIC WATERS: They are going to need a bazooka with this guy. JONATHAN MARX: Iron Lion makes the cover! 1---------------------------------------------- PSYCHO HITS IRON LION IN THE BACK WITH A CHAIR TO BREAK UP THE PINFALL AND THIS REF HAS SEEN ENOUGH! He is calling for the DQ!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of this match by disqualification….. IRON LION! VIC WATERS: LOVE is going to administer a good old butt whopping to Iron Lion to teach him a lesson for disrespecting one of their champions.
JONATHAN MARX: FELIX RED AND PSYCHO ARE PUMMELING IRON LION WITH CHAIRS AS HE TRIES TO GET UP! IRON LION HAS BEEN BUSTED OPEN!
VIC WATERS: Anarky is coming into the ring now with a chair of his own…. JONATHAN MARX: Anarky is setting the chair down on the mat…. ANARKY HITS IRON LION WITH A CHAOS BREAKER RIGHT ONTO THE CHAIR! Anarky is laughing as he dips his hand in the blood of Iron Lion as he wipes it on the chest of his fellow members of LOVE.
VIC WATERS: Look who is coming from the back… JONATHAN MARX: IT IS LARRY TACT WITH A BASEBALL BAT AND LOVE IS GETTING THE HELL OUT OF DODGE! Thank god Larry Tact came out here before this beating got even worse. Iron Lion may be a seven foot tall monster of a man but against four guys with steel chairs, there is much anyone could do.
VIC WATERS: This cannot be good. JONATHAN MARX: Larry Tact is helping up Iron Lion…. A BLOODY IRON LION IS YELLING AT WELLS THAT HE IS COMING FOR HIM AND HE IS GOING TO TAKE AWAY THAT JOKE AROUND HIS WAIST!
VIC WATERS: Oh boy. JONATHAN MARX: Folks, when we comeback from commercial break, Copycat and Larry Tact are going to square off, if Tact wins, he gets to face the person of his choosing at the PPV, if Copycat wins, he stays right in step with Edmunds on the rode to their first ever match on the PPV, STAY TUNED!
