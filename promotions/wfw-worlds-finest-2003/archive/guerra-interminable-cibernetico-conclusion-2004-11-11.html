PaulNJ21 — 11 November 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back from commercial break and we are now down to seven. Shawn Hart, Golem, Rocko Daymon, and Thirteen on Team Hart. Anarky, Felix Red, and John Doe on Team Anarky.
JONATHAN MARX: Team Hart has the man advantage, but Team Anarky is going to be awfully hard to close out with Anarky and Felix Red still in the mix.

JONATHAN MARX: I respect Shawn Hart’s judgment as a former WFW World Heavyweight Champion, but I think he is making a mistake here. Golem may not be 100%.
VIC WATERS: Golem is a tough bastard, he’ll find a way to get through this and get the better of Doe.
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: There is the bell to restart this match…. GOLEM SURPRISES EVERYONE AND HE BULL RUSHES JOHN DOE AND RAMS DOE INTO THE CORNER! Golem jumps up…. LEG LARIAT RIGHT IN THE FACE OF DOE AND DOE FALLS FACE FIRST TO THE MAT!
JONATHAN MARX: Golem is a hell of a technical wrestling, he has been involved in a lot of fights here in WFW so he hasn’t always had a chance to show it, but he can wrestle with the best of them.

JONATHAN MARX: Doe is in a mess of trouble. VIC WATERS: For someone who had a bite taken out of him by Lane, Golem sure isn’t showing any visible effects so far.

DOE GETS UP AND HE RAMS HIS SHOULDER INTO THE BACK OF THE KNEE OF GOLEM AND GOLEM FOLDS LIKE A HOUSE OF CARD! Doe rolls up the legs and is going for the cover!
VIC WATERS: Golem should have never taken his eye off Doe. BRANDON JACOBS: THE REF GOES DOWN FOR THE COUNT! 1--------------------------------------------2---------------------------------------- GOLEM KICKS OUT!
JONATHAN MARX: Damn it BRANDON JACOBS: DOE AND GOLEM ARE TRYING TO GET BACK TO THEIR FEET…. GOLEM LUNGES AT DOE AND HE IS GOING FOR THE CLAW BUT DOE MOVES OUT OF THE WAY!

BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky charges for Daymon AND THE WAR IS ON! BOTH MEN ARE TRADING PUNCHES BACK AND FORTH IN ATTEMPT TO GET THE BETTER OF EACH OTHER!
