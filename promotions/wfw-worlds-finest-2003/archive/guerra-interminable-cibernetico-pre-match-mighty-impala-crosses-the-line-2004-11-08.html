Guerra Interminable: Cibernetico Pre-Match, Mighty Impala Crosses The Line

8 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 8 November 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back and it seems that Lonestar has certainly endeared himself to the masses.
VIC WATERS: Lone Star is a straight shooter, I like that. He holds nothing back. That is the kind of straight forwardness I wish most politicians would have.
JONATHAN MARX: Don Rickles eat your heart out. BRANDON JACOBS: It should be interesting to see who that masked man really is and who he sets his sights on.
JONATHAN MARX: Lone Star is going to go after the best of the best, they do everything big in Texas.
VIC WATERS: Now if they could only spell their name… BRANDON JACOBS: Thank you Bobby Heenan, now lets move on to the Cibernetico, what are your thoughts on this match?
JONATHAN MARX: These two teams are pretty closely matched on paper, LOVE has been on a hot streak, but Hart and his gang have been a pain in their side for awhile now, so I am going to make this prediction based on the x-factor. Pulsar has never lost a match as part of a six man tag or greater so I am going to go with Team Hart to win it all.
VIC WATERS: You’d say that if Alexander the Great was on Team Anarky with your irrational love for Pulsar. Team Hart is going to go down to Team LOVE once and for all.
JONATHAN MARX: I believe in Team Hart, besides from Pulsar, I think Rocko Daymon and Thirteen are going to try to hold the store and make an impact right here in their WFW debut.
BRANDON JACOBS: This is only Thirteen’s first match and there seems to be heat already between him and Felix Red. Felix Red has finally found someone as bizarre as himself.
JONATHAN MARX: Felix Red may be a bit eccentric but all geniuses are. Thirteen is just down right nuts believing in all this mumbo jumbo. His manager is a headhunter for heaven sake. They both scare the hell out of me and this is coming from someone who has and will take many a beating from Manson.
VIC WATERS: Thirteen and Voudin are just misunderstood. Voudin is a hell of a guy. A little kid came up to him and asked him for a shrunken head and do you know what he did? He said he would do him one better and offered him two heads… for one of his. The poor Mexican boy just ran back to his mother crying. It was hilarious.
BRANDON JACOBS: That isn’t funny, that was just a little kid. We are ambassadors of the WFW. The poor horrified kid is going to have nightmares for a week.
VIC WATERS: It isn’t like Voudin is the first head hunter in WFW. BRANDON JACOBS: Who was the first? VIC WATERS: “The Head Hunter” Beau Michaels, but that was a totally different. JONATHAN MARX: Please, we don’t want to offend the red states tonight. BRANDON JACOBS: It looks like all fourteen men are being lead down to ringside by their beautiful female valets here at Arena Mexico. Because of TV time, we unfortunately couldn’t show all fourteen entrances for the sake of time.
VIC WATERS: The fans seemed like they were really into Thirteen’s entrance and he had a huge reaction in his first night here in the company. It reminded me of Larry Tact’s ovation at Merrython in his own hometown of Manhattan.
BRANDON JACOBS: All fourteen men at ringside and they are ready to go, wait, someone is missing….
JONATHAN MARX: Where is Mighty Impala? VIC WATERS: You know, I haven’t seen him the last day and a half, but I just figured Mighty Impala was in a five star hotel away from the other riff-raff.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second, something is coming on the videotron… . (CUT TO: THE MIGHTY IMPALA, standing in front of a ranch house and open prairie, smirking at the camera. Standing next to him is a figure wearing a potato sack over his face.)
TMI: First, let me say hello to all of the competitors in the match, especially to those members of my team WORLD TEAM EEEM PAA LAAAH who have so graciously decided to do my bidding after they all realized I am the man with the superior GEN EE TALL IAA (TMI hold his arms out wide, mimicking his crotch size) being a man of NOBLE ANCESTREE and of ROYAL HER EEEE TAGE. Now, I am sorry that I could not be in attendance for this evening's matchup but my duties as a ruler of the MOSSI PEOPLE, the greatest people of West Africa, along with my governmental dooo ties serving the nation of COTE D'IVOIRE requires me to be elsewhere. My mission also coincides well with my personal mission in the WFW Promotion in slowly going forth on IMPALA QUEST which will end with my ANTELOPE BOOT on the throats of ALL as I will be the rightful ruler of THEES ENTIRE EEEN DUSS TREE. I scouted for months ONE MAN ONLY to be my personal manservant, Pulsar, and gave him the opportunity to lick my boots clean however, instead, HE DEFIED ME! Pulsar, I gave you a hand up instead of a hand out and instead you attacked me, a noble man, like the begging peasant you are! I told you, my arch-nemesis, I would swear vengence upon thee and have begun doing so EEE MEE DIATEEE LEEE.
(The camera pans around.) Does thees LAND look FAMILIAR, Pulsar? IT SHOULD, for it is the LAND UPON WHICH YOU GREW UP! The vacant RANCH LAND of rural Australia. Your childhood house, now tended to by your... (cackles) LOVING SISTER!
(TMI sneers and turns left and snatches the potato sack off revealing a 20-ish blonde who looks emotionally devastated.)
SISTER: Pulsar, I'm sorr-- TMI: SILENCE! I forbid you from speaking! In fact, I demand you go inside the house and peel potatoes for the Royal Afreecan Stew I will be enjoying later tonight! GO AND FETCH ME MY MEAL!
(She walks off.) Pulsar, your meager earnings as a wrestler have not been enough. Your sister is behind in the mortgage, behind in bill payments and I, as a result, have BOUGHT YOUR FAMILY'S RANCH! Your sister will ABIDE MY EVERY COMMAND or if not she will be sent out on the street like a homeless woman or perhaps if she wrongs me it will be CHOPCHOP with her hand! Pulsar, you WILL be my servant or I will not just destroy your family's ranch but I will personally destroy you... it will be like a LION RAPING A RABBIT when I am done crushing your will to live!
BRANDON JACOBS: An enraged Pulsar is heading for the back…. What a low down dirty trick for Mighty Impala to play but this is just typical of the depths that he’ll go to. FOLKS, WE ARE GOING TO TAKE A COMMERCIAL BREAK! When we comeback we will have the start of this six on six match up.
