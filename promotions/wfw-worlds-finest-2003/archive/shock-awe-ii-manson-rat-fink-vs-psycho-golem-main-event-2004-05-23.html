Shock & Awe II: Manson & Rat Fink vs. Psycho & Golem (Main Event)

23 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 23 May 2004 JONATHAN MARX: I always thought Brandon had a cushy job as an announcer in WFW, but now that I’ve had a chance to call the action tonight, I think I’m safer in the squared circle.
VIC WATERS: This hasn’t been an extraordinary night in many respects. When Jacobs started off the show fearing doom for all, I never expected that he would be right.
JONATHAN MARX: He isn’t right very often, but when he is, he is in spades. VIC WATERS: Indeed. (CUEUP: "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing the first member of the opposing team for this boot camp match where falls count anywhere in the ringside area, the former WFW World Heavyweight Champion, PSYCHO!
::Psycho comes down to a mixed reaction from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “The Day The World Went Away” by Nine Inch Nails) RING ANNOUNCER: And his partner, one of the toughest wrestlers in the world today, making his WFW debut. GOLEM!
::Golem walks down to the crowd to a big ovation from the crowd and enters the ring:: RING ANNOUNCER: And now introducing… ::A Black Van comes through the curtain and it crashes into the ring collapsing one side of the ring and causing both Psycho and Golem to fall::
JONATHAN MARX: GOOD LORD, that black van sure came out here like a bat out of hell. VIC WATERS: Look who is coming out… JONATHAN MARX: IT IS RAT FINK AND MANSON! Boy, do they know how to make an entrance. VIC WATERS: They destroyed the damn ring in the process to so they are going to have go for pinfalls around the ring and in the aisle.
JONATHAN MARX: Well, they could go for a pinfall in the ring, but it is completely on a tilt and it would make it really hard to move. Rat Fink is finally stepping out of the van with two by four over his shoulder dressed like Fidel Castro.
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink could be Fidel Castro for all we know, I’ve never seen Rat Fink and Castro in the same place.
JONATHAN MARX: HERE COME GOLEM AND PSYCHO OUT OF THE REMAINS OF THE DESTROYED RING AND THEY DO NOT LOOK HAPPY!
VIC WATERS: How would you look if the floor underneath you collapsed? JONATHAN MARX: Manson is climbing on top of the van… HE REACHES UP ON THE RACK AND PULLS DOWN A NET HE THROWS ON PSYCHO AND GOLEM!
VIC WATERS: They are caught like rats in a trap! JONATHAN MARX: HERE COMES RAT FINK AND HE NAILS PSYCHO ACROSS THE BACK WITH THE TWO BY FOUR BREAKING IT IN HALF!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink must be visiting BALCO. JONATHAN MARX: GOLEM ESCAPES THE NET AND HE PULLS MANSON AS HE IS GETTING DOWN FROM HIS VAN AND MANSON FALLS DOWN ONTO THE CONCRETE FLOOR BELOW!
VIC WATERS: It wasn’t all that far of a fall, but he fell awkwardly. JONATHAN MARX: Golem is reaching into the van…. GOLEM HAS A CANOE PADDLE! VIC WATERS: Obviously that was in his van from the last time he needed to dump a body out in his boat.
JONATHAN MARX: Manson is trying to crawl away…. GOLEM HITS MANSON IN THE REAR END WITH A HARD SHOT WITH THE CANOE PADDLE!
VIC WATERS: Finally the beating his mother never gave him. JONATHAN MARX: Manson is up and GOLEM AND MANSON ARE FIGHTING OVER THE PADDLE! Rat Fink is dragging Psycho to the back of the van with the net!
VIC WATERS: Sometime tells me Psycho isn’t going to like this. JONATHAN MARX: RAT FINK IS OPENING UP THE BACK OF THE VAN AND HE IS PULLING OUT A TANK OF PIRANHAS! GOOD GOD!
VIC WATERS: Is there anything Manson doesn’t have in that van?! JONATHAN MARX: MANSON GAINS CONTROL OF THE CANOE PADDLE AND GOLEM SPRINTS FOR THE BACK OF THE VAN!
VIC WATERS: Manson doesn’t look happy.. JONATHAN MARX: GOLEM JUMPS OVER THE FISH TANK AND UP THE AISLE…..MANSON CLEARS THE FISH TANK AS WELL! Golem runs over to the corner and grabs a spotlight!
VIC WATERS: Ignore Manson and Rat Fink for a second, look at what Rat Fink is trying to do!
JONATHAN MARX: RAT FINK IS TRYING TO SHOVE THE FACE OF PSYCHO INTO THE TANK OF PIRANHAS! PSYCHO ELBOWS RAT FINK THOUGH AND STOPS HIM IN THE NICK OF TIME!
VIC WATERS: They may call him Psycho, but he isn’t that crazy. JONATHAN MARX: PSYCHO IS TAKING OFF THE NET WHICH HAD HIM TRAPPED AND HE GRABS RAT FINK BY THE HAIR!
VIC WATERS: Pay back time. JONATHAN MARX: PSYCHO LIFTS UP RAT FINK OVER THE PIRANHA TANK…. HE BODYSLAMS HIM INTO THE TANK! OH MY!
CROWD: BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! VIC WATERS: I’d say that is the first time those fish have ever had a chance to eat a Cuban dictator but they are Manson’s fish so I know better than to say that.
JONATHAN MARX: Rat Fink jumps out of the tank and there are parts of his outfit missing along with several fish on his back! RAT FINK JUMPS DOWN TO THE GROUND AND BEGINS TO ROLL AROUND TO GET THEM OFF!
VIC WATERS: Look over at what Manson and Golem are doing! JONATHAN MARX: MANSON AND GOLEM ARE HAVING A SWORD DUEL WITH A LIGHTING POLE AND A CANOE PADDLE!
VIC WATERS: USE THE FORCE GOLEM! JONATHAN MARX: GOLEM NAILS MANSON’S CANOE PADDLE WITH A HARD SHOT AND IT CRACKS IN HALF! Golem jumps onto Manson and he pressed the lighting pole up to Manson’s neck and he trying to choke him to death!
VIC WATERS: He is pressing the steel pole right up to the neck of Manson with little or no regard for his well being. I like this guy.
JONATHAN MARX: MANSON IS USING HIS STRENGTH TO PUSH THE POLL AWAY… GOLEM LOSES CONTROL OF THE LIGHTING POLE! Manson gets up and he charges Golem… GOLEM KICKS MANSON IN THE MIDSECTION!
VIC WATERS: The great equalizer… JONATHAN MARX: GOLEM PICKS MANSON UP ON POWERBOMB FORMATION AND THEN STUN GUNS HIM ON THE STEEL GUARD RAIL! Manson is grabbing his neck!
VIC WATERS: Oh man, Psycho has one of the piranhas and he is trying to shove it down Rat Fink’s throat!
JONATHAN MARX: HE IS GOING TO CHOKE HIM TO DEATH! RAT FINK BATS IT AWAY AND HE TURNS OVER PSYCHO AND HE STARTS POUNDING ON HIM WITH MOUNTED PUNCHES RIGHT IN THE FACE!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink does not look pleased… JONATHAN MARX: Someone from the audience just threw Rat Fink a hockey glove and he puts it on his hand AND RESUMES PUNCHING PSYCHO IN THE FACE!
VIC WATERS: Those gloves are dangerous because they cover a bigger surface area than you would normally get with a regular punch so they can really pack a wallop.
JONATHAN MARX: RAT FINK IS PULLING AWAY A SECOND OF THE GUARDRAIL FROM THE AISLE WAY AND HE DUMPS IT ON THE GROUND! Psycho is trying to crawl away…
VIC WATERS: He must know what is coming… JONATHAN MARX: Rat Fink picks up Psycho…. HE HITS HIM WITH A FALCON’S ARROW RIGHT ON THE STEEL GUARDRAIL! Rat Fink is going for the pin and the ref comes over to make the count!
VIC WATERS: This could be it! JONATHAN MARX: 1---------------------------------------------2---------- PSYCHO GETS HIS SHOULDER UP RIGHT BEFORE THE TWO COUNT!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is use to matches like this and how to take an extraordinary amount of punishment.
JONATHAN MARX: Getting back to Manson and Golem, will wonders never cease… GOLEM HAS MANSON IN A WRESTLING HOLD! GOLEM HAS A FUJIWARA ARMBAR LOCKED ON THE RIGHT ARM OF MANSON!
VIC WATERS: I never expected that in this match. JONATHAN MARX: Despite Golem’s legendary toughness, he is a hell of technician in that ring and knows the perfect time to do everything.
VIC WATERS: Maybe this will slow Manson down from pummeling Golem with plunder. JONATHAN MARX: EXACTLY! The ref is coming over to Manson and Golem to see if he wants to give up….. MANSON REACHES OVER AND HE GRABS HALF OF THE CANOE PADDLE AND HITS GOLEM RIGHT IN THE STOMACH! Golem finally breaks the hold!
VIC WATERS: I’ve never seen that move broken up like that before. JONATHAN MARX: Manson lifts up Golem over his shoulder and he is running towards the fan…. MANSON RUNS WITH GOLEM RIGHT INTO THE SIDE OF HIS VAN AND GOLEM FALLS DOWN TO THE GROUND BELOW!
VIC WATERS: Oh man, it looks like Psycho and Rat Fink are brawling into the crowd… JONATHAN MARX: I’m having a flashback to their BAD World Heavyweight encounter… PSYCHO GRABS A BEER BOTTLE FROM A FAN AND HE HITS RAT FINK OVER THE HEAD WITH IT! Down goes Rat Fink!
VIC WATERS: Manson sees Rat Fink is in trouble and he is coming over to help. JONATHAN MARX: Psycho drops the beer bottle… PSYCHO YAKUZA KICKS MANSON RIGHT IN THE FACE AND MANSON GOES DOWN! Psycho is standing over a see of bodies…. HE IS HEADED FOR THE TRUCK OF MANSON!
VIC WATERS: That van is sort of like a magician’s hat, you never know what someone is going to pull out of there.
JONATHAN MARX: PSYCHO IS PULLING OUT A POOL TABLE! VIC WATERS: A pool table?! Is this the same one he brought to his NFW match? JONATHAN MARX: I see some blood stains on it so it is entirely possible, or being Manson it could be a whole entire different table entirely.
VIC WATERS: This isn’t good. JONATHAN MARX: Psycho has tne pool table all set in position and he goes over to Manson… PSYCHO PICKS UP MANSON AND SLAMS HIM ONTO THE TABLE!
VIC WATERS: That has to hurt. JONATHAN MARX: Psycho is climbing up to the top of the van… WAIT A SECOND. GOLEM IS GETTING UP AND HE IS CRAWLING ONTO THE POOL TABLE WITH MANSON…. GOLEM PICKS UP A DAZED MANSON AND HITS HIM WITH A DEATH VALLEY DRIVER!
VIC WATERS: Damn. JONATHAN MARX: Golem has a chain and he is trying to choke Manson as Psycho is watching from the top of the van and he is screaming at Golem to get off Manson…. WAIT A SECOND, A BLOODY RAT FINK IS UP AND HE IS CRAWLING UP ONTO THE VAN FROM BEHIND ON PSYCHO!
VIC WATERS: Turn around Psycho! JONATHAN MARX: Golem is picking up Manson up on his feet on the table…. GOLEM BACK DROP DRIVERS MANSON ONTO THE CONCRETE FLOOR FROM THE POOL TABLE AND MANSON IS BUSTED OPEN!
VIC WATERS: Golem looks like he is exhausted… JONATHAN MARX: A frustrated Psycho goes to climb down… BUT RAT FINK IS RIGHT BEHIND HIM! VIC WATERS: Oh man… JONATHAN MARX: RAT FINK HITS PSYCHO WITH A STEINERLINE AND HE FALLS DOWN ONTO THE ROOF OF THE VAN!
VIC WATERS: DOWN GOES PSYCHO! JONATHAN MARX: Rat Fink is picking Psycho… IT LOOKS LIKE HE IS GOING FOR THE CRUCIFIX POWERBOMB ONTO THE POOL TABLE BELOW! Rat Fink picks Psycho up in a crucifix….. OH MY GOD!
CROWD: BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! JONATHAN MARX: Rat Fink nailed him with the crucifix bomb right onto the pool table! These men are going to need medical assistance after this…. RAT FINK PUTS HIS ARM OVER PSYCHO TO MAKE THE COVER!
VIC WATERS: FIGHT IT PSYCHO FIGHT IT! JONATHAN MARX: 1------------------------------------------2-------------------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winners of the match…. THE TEAM OF RAT FINK AND MANSON! JONATHAN MARX: Wait a second, RAT FINK ISN’T DONE! RAT FINK GRABS GOLEM AND HE THROWS HIM OVER THE GUARDRAIL! Rat Fink pulls Psycho from the table and throws him through the section of empty guardail!
VIC WATERS: What is Rat Fink doing?! JONATHAN MARX: Rat Fink drags Manson over to the collapsed ring…. RAT FINK RUNS TO THE BACK OF THE VAN AND HE PUTS THE POOL TABLE BACK IN! He slams the door shut and he hops in the car…. He couldn’t be doing what I think he is doing…
VIC WATERS: HE IS! JONATHAN MARX: RAT FINK IS BACKING THE CAR OUT OF THE ENTRANCE WAY…. HE IS STEALING MANSON’S CAR FOR THE SECOND YEAR IN A ROW!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink gets more vans that way… JONATHAN MARX: A barely conscious Manson smirks at Rat Fink as he leaves through the curtain.
VIC WATERS: History is quite ironic… JONATHAN MARX: Folks, I’d like to thank everyone for joining us, our next show will be BOMB VOYAGE and it will be held on a cruise ship going to Hawaii, the two matches already set in stone are… Manson will put his WFW World Heavyweight Title against Richard Gideon in his first World Heavyweight Title defense and Edmunds and Tact will go one on one and this time, there will be no Sherri to interfere! From what I’m being told, I’m going to have to fill in for Jacobs for at least two shows but he promises that as long as he isn’t dead, he is going to be back for the PPV Cherry Blossom Chaos at the Mall in Washington, DC! THANK YOU FOR JOINING US EVERYONE, GOODNIGHT AND GOD BLESS!
VIC WATERS: Thank god it is over.
