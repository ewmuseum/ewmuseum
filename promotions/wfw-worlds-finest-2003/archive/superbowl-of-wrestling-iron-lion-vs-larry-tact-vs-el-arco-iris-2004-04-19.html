WFW — Card

Superbowl of Wrestling: Iron Lion vs. Larry Tact vs. El Arco Iris

19 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 19 April 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: It is time for the second Superbowl of Wrestling to finally begin! VIC WATERS: With some leagues, a four year absence between anniversary shows could be considered annual.
BRANDON JACOBS: Sort of like Jack Benny birthdays, some things can’t be rushed. Any last minute thoughts on the three way elimination match?
VIC WATERS: I’m having second thoughts, I was leaning more towards Iron Lion, but with all of Larry Tact’s recent success, I’m going to predict an upset here tonight.
BRANDON JACOBS: That is a bold prediction by you. VIC WATERS: Tact is one of the finest technicians if not the greatest technician in wrestling today. Iron Lion may be big, but Tact’s skills are so refined that I think he may figure out a way to outsmart Iron Lion.
BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion is damn intelligent for a big man. Usually wrestlers the size of Iron Lion don’t develop their brains because they depend on their physical endowments and never become complete wrestlers, but Iron Lion is quite the opposite which makes him so dangerous.
VIC WATERS: Tact though was very angry about his position on the card and seem highly motivated to work his way up the ladder.
BRANDON JACOBS: None of these men belong in opening matches, but this card is just so stacked with top matches that someone has to go first. Do you put Shawn Hart verses Scotty Michaels first with all the history those two men have as former WFW World Heavyweight Champions? No. Do you put the BAD World Heavyweight Title tournament first when BAD is the company which gave birth to the Superbowl of Wrestling? No. Do you put Rabesque verses Edmunds in the opener when the great Rabesque graciously accepted our invitation to face the former champ Sean Edmunds who while struggling still has been a fixture in this company over the last year? No. The positioning of these matches are merely based on who has established feuds with other wrestlers and both Iron Lion and Larry Tact are relative newcomers.
VIC WATERS: I thought it was because El Arco Iris had an early bed time. BRANDON JACOBS: Quiet you. I’m going to get that hug from you before year end after El Arco Iris wins his first title here in WFW.
VIC WATERS: You freak. You are never going to get that hug. BRANDON JACOBS: Hell, I may even get El Arco Iris to hug you in my place. VIC WATERS: No, no, no… I didn’t agree to that. El Arco Iris is so happy he scares me. BRANDON JACOBS: Maybe if you hugged more often, you wouldn’t be so negative. VIC WATERS: I’m perfectly content being who I am thank you. BRANDON JACOBS: You’re loss. Folks, the time for chatter is over, lets send this one down to ringside and get it underway!
(CUEUP: “Tunak Tunak Tun” by Dahler Mendhi) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, hailing from over the rainbow, the Prince of Prismatic, EL ARCO IRIS!
::El Arco Iris walks down to a huge pop from the crowd, dozens of children can been seen in the crowd wearing El Arco Iris masks, and enters the ring::
(CUEUP: “Is This The End” by Creed) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Anchorage, Alaska, a man focused on bringing honor, pride, and strength back to professional wrestling, IRON LION!
::Iron Lion walks down to the roar of the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: "Karma" by Diffuser) RING ANNOUNCER: And last but not least, one of the best technicians in all of wrestling today, Manhattan, New York… LARRY TACT!
::Larry Tact comes down to the ovation from the crowd and enters the ring:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: It is quite remarkable that El Arco Iris is even here today after what happened in the War Games cage.
VIC WATERS: L.O.V.E. gave him a hell of a beating. You could see that he was still feeling the effects of the attack when he was visiting those kids in the hospital.
BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion and Larry Tact are meeting in the center of the ring eye to eye and El Arco Iris is trying to make sure both men remain calm so the bout doesn’t turn into an angry brawl.
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris is just trying to protect himself. BRANDON JACOBS: LARRY TACT CHOPS EL ARCO IRIS IN THE CHEST! El Arco Iris chops him back with one of his own and both men engage in a chop fest back and forth!
VIC WATERS: Larry Tact got ticked that El Arco Iris was breaking his concentration on Iron Lion and went at him without holding back.
BRANDON JACOBS: Those two may be regular tag team partners, but both men want to prove something here tonight in order to move up in the rankings and the competition in WFW is pretty steep.
VIC WATERS: LOOK! IRON LION CAUGHT THE ARM AS TACT WAS GOING FOR A CHOP ON EL ARCO IRIS! BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion locks on an armbar on Larry Tact and he is wrenching it in… EL ARCO IRIS GRABS THE OTHER ARM OF LARRY TACT AND APPLIES AN ARMBAR!
VIC WATERS: They are turning him into a pretzel! BRANDON JACOBS: LARRY TACT DROPS HIS LEGS OUT FROM UNDER HIM IN ORDER TO GET HIS OPPONENTS TO DROP HIM BUT AFTER A NEAR STUMBLE EL ARCO IRIS REPLANTS HIMSELF IN POSITION!
VIC WATERS: It almost worked, El Arco Iris is really showing his strength here. BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion and El Arco Iris swing him back and forth by his arms…. THEY LET GO AND HE CRASHES HARD ONTO THE MAT ON HIS BACK!
VIC WATERS: That mat can be unforgiving. BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS GOES TO MAKE THE COVER! 1---------------------------------2---------------------- LARRY TACT KICKS OUT WITH AUTHORITY!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris flew right off of Tact. BRANDON JACOBS: Larry Tact goes to get up but Iron Lion catches him and HITS THE TRIPLE RIB BREAKER! Tact falls down to the mat and grabs his ribs!
VIC WATERS: If Iron Lion can work over the ribs enough, he could limit the stamina of Larry Tact and make this a more even match if this goes anything length of time.
BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion holds Larry Tact down with a boot to the throat and he is calling to El Arco Iris…. EL ARCO IRIS CLIMBS UP TO THE TOP ROPE…. MOONSAULT FROM THE TOP ROPE ONTO TACT!
VIC WATERS: That was picture perfect. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris makes the cover on Tact! 1------------------------------2------------------------- TACT GETS HIS FOOT ON THE ROPE!
VIC WATERS: That is Larry Tact for you, he always knows where he is in that ring at all times.
BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion drags Larry Tact to the center of the ring… Iron Lion picks Larry Tact off the mat…. LARRY TACT FIRES A SERIES OF FOREARMS RIGHT TO THE FACE OF IRON LION!
VIC WATERS: Those caught Iron Lion by surprise! BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS SEES IRON LION IN TROUBLE AND BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES…. FLYING FOREARM BUT LARRY TACT DUCKS AND HITS IRON LION AND IRON LION STUMBLES THROUGH THE ROPES TO THE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: Larry Tact is showing why he is championship material with his lightning quick reflexes.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris says to Iron Lion the shot wasn’t meant for him… LARRY TACT COMES FROM BEHIND EL ARCO IRIS…. RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX ON EL ARCO IRIS AND HE CRASHES DOWN ON THE BACK OF HIS NECK!
VIC WATERS: Damn! BRANDON JACOBS: LARRY TACT PICKS HIM UP AGAIN… ANOTHER RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX RIGHT ONTO THE NECK OF EL ARCO IRIS! Larry Tact isn’t showing any mercy! Larry Tact grabs the injured El Arco Iris and locks on the Sky Lock Inverted STF!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris is still banged up from the War Games match. The smart thing to do would be to give up.
BRANDON JACOBS: Even with the mask on, you can tell by looking at El Arco’s face that he is in a lot of pain right now and he is no where near the ropes to break the hold.
VIC WATERS: The referee is checking on El Arco Iris but he is repeatedly saying no. BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS STRUGGLES AND GETS TACT FLAT ON HIS BACK! The referee goes down to make the count! 1--------------------------------------2---------------------- TACT GETS HIS SHOULDER UP IN TIME!
VIC WATERS: That was very close. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is struggling to get Tact flat on his back again…. HE DOES IT AGAIN AND THE REF GOES DOWN FOR THE COUNT! 1----------------------------------------------2----------------------- TACT GETS HIS SHOULDER UP AND HE BREAKS THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris was having too much success getting Tact in a pinning position to keep it on but the damage has been done to El Arco Iris.
BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion is beginning to get up and he climbs onto the apron…. LARRY TACT CHARGES IRON LION AND HITS HIM WITH A LARIAT BUT IRON LION HOLDS ONTO THE TOP ROPE TO AVOID FALLING DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Iron Lion is a big man, if he fell off the apron, he could have hit his head on the guardrail.
BRANDON JACOBS: Larry Tact grabs the hair of Iron Lion and tries to pull him into the ring… IRON LION CLIMBS ONTO THE SECOND ROPE…. LARRY TACT WAISTLOCKS IRON LION AND HITS HIM WITH AN OVER THE HEAD BELLY TO BELLY INTO THE RING!
CROWD: HOLY ****! HOLY ****! HOLY ****! BRANDON JACOBS: Larry Tact makes the cover on Iron Lion! 1------------------------------------------2----------------------- A BATTERED EL ARCO IRIS BREAKS UP THE PIN!
VIC WATERS: Why did El Arco Iris do that? This is an elimination match! Tact could have had him there.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris must have done it because he wants these two men to wear each other down some more before one of them is pinned. LARRY TACT GETS UP, KICKS HIM IN THE MIDSECTION AND HITS EL ARCO IRIS WITH A KICK SWING DDT! Tact makes the cover! 1--------------------------------------------2---------------------------------- IRON LION BREAKS UP THE PINFALL!
VIC WATERS: Now why did he do that? BRANDON JACOBS: He must have been paying back the favor for earlier in the match. Iron Lion is a man of honor and a man of honor always repays his debts. LARRY TACT IS SWITCHING BACK AND FORTH BETWEEN EL ARCO IRIS AND IRON LION! STOMPING ON BOTH OF THEM!
VIC WATERS: Larry Tact’s patience looks like it is wearing thin. BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion is trying to get up and Tact is KICKING AWAY AT THE HEAD OF IRON LION AS HE IS TRYING TO GET UP! Iron Lion gets up to his knees…. HE CATCHES THE LEG OF LARRY TACT AND SPINS HIM AROUND…. BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX!
VIC WATERS: Get up Tact, you can’t let Iron Lion do you in. BRANDON JACOBS: Tact is trying to get up…. IRON LION BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND HITS LARRY TACT WITH A YAKUZA KICK RIGHT IN THE FACE!
VIC WATERS: God, it almost looked like he knocked his head off. BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion is calling for his finisher to the roar of the crowd…. IRON LION PICKS UP TACT FOR THE PRIDE HAMMER…. TACT GRABS THE HAIR OF IRON LION SO HE CAN’T HIT HIS FINISHER!
VIC WATERS: Tact has taken so much abuse and he still wants to keep on fighting. BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second…. EL ARCO IRIS IS UP AND HE IS HEADING FOR THE TOP…. HE PERCHES ON THE TOP ROPE…. MISSILE DROPKICK RIGHT INTO THE FACE OF LARRY TACT AND IRON LION DELIVERS THE PRIDE HAMMER ON TACT! Iron Lion makes the cover! 1----------------------------2-----------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: Larry Tact has been eliminated! BRANDON JACOBS: We are down to two men and one can not envy the position El Arco Iris is currently in.
VIC WATERS: Iron Lion can’t take anything for granted. He needs to finish off El Arco Iris and end this match as quickly as possible.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris walks over to Iron Lion and the two men shake hands. That is an incredible display of sportsmanship between these two men.
VIC WATERS: Iron Lion just said good by El Arco Iris. The Prince of Prismatic is about to be dethroned.
BRANDON JACOBS: Both men are circling each other… EL ARCO IRIS GOES FOR THE SINGLE LEG TAKE DOWN BUT IRON LION AVOIDS GOING DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Taking a big man like this down is nearly impossible. BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS SNEAKS BEHIND IRON LION AND ATTEMPTS TO WAIT LOCK IRON LION BUT HIS HANDS ARE A BIT TOO SHORT!
VIC WATERS: Iron Lion is a massive mountain of a man. BRANDON JACOBS: IRON LION LIFTS EL ARCO IRIS ONTO HIS BACK AND PUSHES HIM BACK FORWARD INTO THE TURNBUCKLE SQUASHING HIM IN THE CORNER! Iron Lion backs off and El Arco Iris falls down to one knee to catch his breath.
VIC WATERS: Iron Lion should be going after El Arco Iris and not letting him up. Make him pay for what he did in the So Cal Rumble.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris gets up…. HE IS CHALLENGING IRON LION TO A TEST OF STRENGTH! One has to question El Arco Iris’s sanity there…
VIC WATERS: You are just figuring this out now? BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion extends his hands… EL ARCO IRIS GOES FOR IRON LION’S LEGS AND TAKES DOWN BY SURPRISE!
VIC WATERS: Whoa. I wouldn’t expect El Arco Iris to deceive Iron Lion like that. BRANDON JACOBS: I wouldn’t call it deception, even God’s happiest of creatures play tricks on each other. EL ARCO IRIS LOCKS ON A STUMP PULLER AND HE IS TRYING TO WORK OVER THE MASSIVE RIGHT LEG OF IRON LION!
VIC WATERS: If El Arco Iris can somehow take out a leg, he might be able to weaken Iron Lion just a bit down to the point where he might be beatable.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is trying to hyperextend the knee…. IRON LION USES HIS LEFT LEG TO KICK AWAY ON EL ARCO IRIS WHO FURIOUSLY TRYING TO HOLD TO HIS HOLD!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris is going to have bruises on his legs tomorrow. BRANDON JACOBS: IRON LION IS USING HIS ARMS TO PULL HIMSELF CLOSER TO THE ROPES… HE MAKES IT TO THE ROPES AND THE REF IS MAKING HIM BREAK THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: Iron Lion saw that El Arco Iris wasn’t going to let go so he did the only thing he could do there.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris breaks the hold and Iron Lion uses the ropes to help himself up… it looks like he is having a bit of trouble putting weight on that right leg.
VIC WATERS: Say what you want about El Arco Iris, but he is a hell of a mat worker to go with his remarkable aerial skills.
BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS CHARGES IRON LION AND DROPKICKS IRON LION ON HIS RIGHT LEG! Iron Lion drops down to one knee!
VIC WATERS: The fact that Iron Lion hasn’t killed El Arco Iris yet is a remarkable feat in itself.
BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND CLOCKS IRON LION WITH A SHINING WIZARD! EL Arco Iris goes for the cover! 1------------------------------------------------------2-------------------------------- IRON LION KICKS OUT!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris may be small, but a well placed knee to the head even by a guy El Arco Iris size can equalize anyone.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris goes down to the mat… HE CHICKEN WINGS IRON LION’S ARM AND TAKES HIS SHOULDERS DOWN TO THE MAT FOR PIN ATTEMPT! 1-----------------------------------------------2-------------------------------- IRON LION KICKS OUT AGAIN!
VIC WATERS: This has to be extremely frustrating for El Arco Iris to come so close and not be able to finish the job.
BRANDON JACOBS: IRON LION TACKLES EL ARCO IRIS FROM BEHIND AS EL ARCO IRIS IS TRYING TO GET UP…. IRON LION WAISTLOCKS EL ARCO IRIS… EL ARCO IRIS ELBOWS IRON LION HARD RIGHT INTO THE CHEST IN ORDER TO BREAK FREE!
VIC WATERS: That could have been ugly if Iron Lion caught him. BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES FOR A FLYING FOREARM BUT IRON LION CATCHES HIM IN THE AIR AND LIFTS HIM UP OVER HIS HEAD…. IRON LION MILITARY PRESSES EL ARCO IRIS TWICE OVER HIS HEAD AND LETS HIM FALL DOWN ONTO THE MAT BELOW!
VIC WATERS: Mommy. BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lions looks down at El Arco Iris and over to the top rope. IRON LION IS GOING TO THE TOP! Iron Lion is going to the corner and he climbing up to the top rope… he has to be a good twelve feet up in the air…
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris is starting to get up… if he hits this, this should finish it right here.
BRANDON JACOBS: IRON LION PERCHES…. EL ARCO IRIS IS UP AND IRON LION FLIES OFF THE TOP WITH A MISSILE DROPKICK, IT DIDN”T LOOK LIKE IT GOT ALL OF HIM BECAUSE OF THE WEAKENED LEG BUT EL ARCO IRIS WENT DOWN LIKE HE WAS SHOT! Oh no, the ref took a spill as El Arco Iris fell backwards.
VIC WATERS: It looked to me that El Arco Iris purposely fell onto that referee. BRANDON JACOBS: When you get hit by a missile dropkick by a guy the size of Iron Lion, you can’t really control where you fall because of all the force behind it.
VIC WATERS: Sure. BRANDON JACOBS: IRON LION GOES FOR THE COVER ON EL ARCO IRIS AND HE IS YELLING AT THE REFEREE TO MAKE THE COUNT! Iron Lion is banging his hand on the mat in order to get his attention!
VIC WATERS: I think the referee is out of it. BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion gets up and he trying to revive the referee in order to make the count.
VIC WATERS: Try smacking him around a bit! BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion is waving to the back that the referee is down and they need another official in order to finish the match…. Never mind, it looks like the ref is starting to come through.
VIC WATERS: We don’t pay these damn referees to sleep on the job. BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second, El Arco Iris is getting up with help from the ropes….. I’ve never seen anyone more determined in my life. EL ARCO IRIS IS SETTING FOR THE HAPPY DANCE!
VIC WATERS: The question is, can El Arco Iris reach him with the kick with the great height disadvantage that he has?
BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is leaning on Iron Lion in order to get up…. IRON LION TURNS AROUND AND EL ARCO IRIS HITS IRON LION WITH THE HAPPY DANCE! DOWN GOES IRON LION!
VIC WATERS: NO DAMNIT! BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris goes down for the pin! 1-----------------------------------------------------2-----------------------------------------------------3! YES!
::kids are jumping up and down in the audience:: RING ANNOUNCER: THE WINNER OF THE THREE WAY MATCH…. EL ARCO IRIS! BRANDON JACOBS: Holy cow, El Arco Iris did it! VIC WATERS: Of all the people Iron Lion has faced so far in the Battle Royal and in singles action, the only one to give him any trouble is El Arco Iris. It is like he is Iron Lion kryptonite.
BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion could have easily won this match if the referee didn’t take the spill that he did. But I thought it was a really good match by all those involved. All three men have a tremendous future in the WFW and I can’t think of a better way to start off the Superbowl of Wrestling.
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris has an angel on his shoulder. BRANDON JACOBS: You may be right. Folks, when we comeback, it is time for one of the biggest grudge matches in WFW history, Shawn Hart will return for one night in order to get revenge at Scotty Michaels for what he did to him at Merrython, we will be back right after this commericlal break, STAY TUNED!
