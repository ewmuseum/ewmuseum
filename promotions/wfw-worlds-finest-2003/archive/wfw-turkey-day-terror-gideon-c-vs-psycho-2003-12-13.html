WFW Turkey Day Terror: Gideon (C) vs. Psycho

13 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 13 December 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome back everyone and it is time for the main event. What is your thoughts on the main?
VIC WATERS: It took Psycho a few matches, but he finally looks like he has returned to the form which won him the WFW World Heavyweight Title. I know everyone loves a Cinderella Story, but the clock is ready to strike twelve here tonight. .
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon has a long history of digging down deep against opponents of Psycho’s level and raising his game another notch. Every time I doubt Gideon can overcome the obstacles set before him, he puts up performance that even surprises me when I’ve seen him do it before time and time again. Psycho is the epitome of all that. He has won the WFW World Heavyweight Title. He has inflected serious injuries on Doc Silver. The question is, can Gideon do it again or will it finally catch up to him?
VIC WATERS: There is only so many times you can deny fate before it catches up with you. While I respect Gideon, I fear Psycho and I fear for what will happen here tonight.
BRANDON JACOBS: Even with all that has gone here on tonight, you think it could be worse than all that?
VIC WATERS: Psycho is a desperate man trying to reclaim his past glory and he looks at this as his shot to get back into the crème de la crème of WFW. There is nothing Psycho won’t do to get back and Gideon can’t understand because of the rage in Psycho’s heart. This is going to be interesting, quite interesting.
BRANDON JACOBS: It is such a shame to see two men who were so good of friends fight like this. But what has been done, has been done. Let’s send this one to ringside and get the main event underway!
CUEUP: "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the man challenging for the WFW North American Title here tonight, the former WFW World Heavyweight Champion, PSYCHO!
::Psycho comes down to a mixed reaction from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “Remember” by Disturbed) RING ANNOUCER: And now, the champion, from Buffalo, NY, THE WFW North American Champion…. THE SYSTEM RICHARD GIDEON!
::Gideon comes down to the ring to the roar of the crowd and enters the ring:: . *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho and Gideon lock up…. Gideon slips behind Psycho and chicken wing’s Psycho’s arm. Psycho tries to reach back to grab the hair of Gideon but Psycho falls short… GIDEON WRENCHES THE ARM UP EVEN MORE!
VIC WATERS: Gideon is already thinking about that Tiger Driver and how he can avoid being placed in it.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon reaches down and grabs the leg…. HE SUPLEXES HIM BACK ON THE CHICKEN WINGED ARM! Psycho is grabbing his shoulder, he may have had his arm pulled out of his socket for a second there.
VIC WATERS: The way Psycho was talking, I would have thought this would have been a wild brawl, but it is still early yet.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon grabs the injured arm of Psycho and locks on a Fujiwara armbar! PSYCHO IS SCREAMING LIKE A BANSHEE AND GIDEON IS PULLING BACK WITH ALL OF HIS MIGHT! Psycho is trying to reach over to pull off a boot of Gideon but he is unable to budge his boot.
VIC WATERS: Creative idea trying to remove the boot of Gideon to try to break out of this, but he is stuck in this hold onto Gideon wants to break it or Psycho submits.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is checking on Psycho but he keeps on shaking his head no. GIDEON BREAKS THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is one of the toughest wrestlers in all of wrestling. I think he’d rather die than submit, especially in a match like this.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon picks Psycho up…. HE GRABS THE INJURED ARM OF PSYCHO AND NAILS HIM WITH A CLOTHESLINE WITHOUT LETTING GO! Psycho is wobbing on his feet!
VIC WATERS: Gideon should try to go for the kill and end this one early. BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON WAISTLOCKS PSYCHO FROM BEHIND…. GERMAN SUPLEX INTO A LEGLOCK…. GIDEON CLUTCH! Gideon has moved from the arm to the leg and he is systematically breaking him down.
VIC WATERS: I maybe even would have tried a pinfall after that German Suplex with the way Psycho looks.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is trying to fight his way to the ropes, but because his right arm is in so much pain, he is relying on his left to move him closer. PSYCHO STARTS USING HIS RIGHT ARM AND SCREAMS AFTER EACH MOVEMENT TOWARDS THE ROPES…. PSYCHO STICKS HIM ARM AS FAR AS IT CAN GO…. HE GRABS THE ROPES! The ref is calling for the break but Gideon is leaving it on for a few extra seconds to send a message! He finally breaks and the referee warns him not to take his time again.
VIC WATERS: Psycho needs to be psycho and turn this from a wrestling match back into a brawl.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is reaching into his tights…. HE HAS A FORK AND HE HIDES IT IN HIS HANDS SO JUST THE PRONGS ARE STICKING OUT THREE OR FOUR CENTIMETERS! Gideon goes to pickup Psycho…. PSYCHO NAILS HIM WITH A PUNCH WITH THE FORKED LEFT HAND! Gideon stumbles backwards and falls over the ref! THE REF IS DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Oh man, things are about to get ugly. BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO JUMPS ON GIDEON AND STARTS POUNDED ON HIM WITH THE FORKED HAND AND PSYCHO HAS BUSTED OPEN GIDEON!
VIC WATERS: The carnage I’ve seen tonight, now I know what it is like to be a turkey on Thanksgiving Day.
BRANDON JACOBS: Just on Thanksgiving Day? Psycho puts the bloody fork back in his trunks. PSYCHO WHIPS GIDEON OFF THE ROPES….. HE NAILS HIM WITH THE YAKUZA KICK TO HIS BUSTED OPEN FOREHEAD! Gideon goes down! Psycho is going to the outside… HE WRESTLES A CHAIR AWAY FROM A FAN AND HE SLIDES BACK IN! Psycho is moving Gideon towards the corner and he lays the chair down on the head of Gideon… PSYCHO IS GOING UP TOP…. HE NAILS THE 450 LEGDROP AND DRIVES THE CHAIR INTO THE SKULL OF GIDEON! The ref is beginning to stir! Psycho throws the chair to the outside… PSYCHO MAKES THE COVER!
VIC WATERS: This ref is barely conscious, but with all the abuse Gideon has taken, Psycho should be able to get him here.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref checks to see if Gideon’s shoulders are down…. The ref makes the count! 1---------------------------------------------------------2-----------------------------------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Good lord, Gideon kicked out. That was a damn long three count too. BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO THROWS GIDEON THROUGH THE ROPES TO THE OUTSIDE AND GIDEON TAKES A SPILL ON THE FLOOR! Psycho follows Gideon out and he puts him neck first onto the guardrail…. Psycho looks back at the dazed referee who is still regaining his bairings AND PULL OUT THE FORK AGAIN! HE FIRST THE FORK AND RAMS THE ENDS RIGHT INTO THE HEAD OF GIDEON REPEATEDLY AS FANS ARE STARTING TO BACK AWAY FROM GIDEON BECAUSE THE BLOOD IS REALLY STARTING TO FLOW!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is channeling the spirit of the great Abdullah the Butcher tonight. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho puts the fork back into his trunks. It looks like he finally might be coming to his senses…. PSYCHO IS BITING THE HEAD OF GIDEON AND GIDEON IS SHRIEKING!
VIC WATERS: All of this blood, all of this chaos, how did it get this far? BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO GRABS GIDEON AND HE WHIPS HIM INTO THE GUARDRAIL! The ref is coming out and he wants both men to get back into the ring! Psycho goes over to Gideon and he rolls him back into the ring.
VIC WATERS: Psycho better be careful, with how Gideon is bleeding, the ref can call this for excessive blood loss and Gideon could retain the WFW North American Title.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho picks Gideon off the ground… Gideon is trying to fight but it is no use, he is too damn weak from the beating he has suffered. PSYCHO IS TRYING FOR A TIGER DRIVER BUT PSYCHO KEEPS ON LOSING HIS GRIP BECAUSE OF HIS INJURED RIGHT ARM AND GIDEON FALLS DOWN TO THE GROUND HANDS FIRST TO BRACE HIS FALL! Psycho is irate.
VIC WATERS: Psycho has used that Tiger Driver on many of an opponent, but Gideon worked over his right arm so well, he is unable to use it here to put him away.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho picks Gideon off the ground with a front face lock using is left arm….. DDT! Psycho is calling for his finisher and this crowd is booing the hell out of him!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is only doing what is needed to be done. BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO IS CALLING FOR THE PSYCHO FLIGHT! Psycho is headed towards the corner and he is climbing to the top… HE IS PERCHED AND HE FLIES OFF WITH THE PSYCHO FLIGHT…. HE NAILS IT! Psycho bounces off on impact clutching his ribs!
VIC WATERS: COVER HIM! BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho crawls over onto Gideon! The ref goes down to make the cover! 1-------------------------------------------2------------------------------------KICKOUT! MY GOD! GIDEON KICKED OUT!
VIC WATERS: HOW DID HE DO THAT?! BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is looking down at his blood stained hands and he is in utter shock. PSYCHO GOES TO MAKE THE COVER AGAIN ON GIDEON! 1---------------------------------------------2---------------------------------KICKOUT! PSYCHO IS PISSED!
VIC WATERS: That seems like the right word for it. BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO TAKES OUT THE FORK IN FRONT OF THE REFEREE AND STARTS POUNDING GIDEON RIGHT IN THE FOREHEAD WITH IT! The ref grabs the arm of Psycho… HE SEES THE FORK AND HE IS CALLING FOR THE DQ!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of this match by disqualification…. “THE SYSTEM” RICHARD GIDEON!
BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO GRABS THE REFEREE BY THE THROAT AND THROWS HIM DOWN TO THE GROUND! PSYCHO GRABS THE FORK AND STARTS RAMMING IT INTO THE HEAD OF THE REFEREE AND HIS BLOOD HAS BEEN SPILLED AS WELL! The officials are running down to ringside to get Psycho off of the ref!
VIC WATERS: Psycho has snapped! BRANDON JACOBS: The referees are peeling Psycho off…. This is utter chaos! Finally, Psycho raises the fork up and the air and looks like he is headed to the back. Both the referee and Richard Gideon will need medical attention.
VIC WATERS: Both of them are going to need some stitches. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we’d like to thank you all for joining us tonight, hopefully we won’t have as much chaos at Merrython when WFW World Heavyweight Champion Shawn Hart puts the title on the line against Scotty Michaels at Rockefeller Center in New York City and Gideon and Felix Red team up in a No Rope Barbed Wire Cage Street Fight Tornado Death Match! GOOD NIGHT EVERYONE!
