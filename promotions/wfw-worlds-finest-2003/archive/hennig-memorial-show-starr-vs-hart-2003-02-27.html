WFW — Card

Hennig Memorial Show: Starr vs. Hart

27 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 27 February 2003 Brandon Jacobs: We finally have all the blood cleaned up and we are ready for our next match between “The Phenom” Shawn Hart and Mr. Incredible Johnny Starr!
Zack Sirus: It is a beautiful day in the day neighborhood, but is hell inside Madison Square Garden as wrestlers are fighting it out for the last two spots to compete in the second round.
Brandon Jacobs: Everyone wants to be the World Finest Wrestling’s World Heavyweight Champion and it is a special honor to be the very first one. Years from now, wrestlers are going to be able to tell their children that they were the very first man to hold this title.
Zack Sirus: Mr. Incredible Johnny Starr is going to be a star of the future. You don’t see too many big men in this sport anymore. Wrestling has become the world of the crusierweights. Back when I started, it was rare for a someone of my size even to win the title but then I changed the world proving that cruiserweights could hold their own in the ring. Now it has gone to the opposite side of the spectrum, it is almost like you are discriminated against if you are a bigger worker. I love it. Get those big stiffs out of the ring. Johnny Starr is the exception to the rule, that boy can go.
Brandon Jacobs: Lets go to ringside and start our next match! :: I Touch Myself" by the Divinyls plays over the loud speakers:: RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from Orlando, Florida! The current eWu World Heavyweight Champion, “The Phenom” Shawn Hart!
::Shawn Hart comes out to a mixed reaction from the crowd:: ::Killed or be Killed, by Mobb Deep blares over the loud speakers:: RING ANNOUNCER: His opponent, from Las Vegas, Nevada, the biggest man in WFW today, Mr. Incredible Johnny Starr!
:: Incredible walks slowly down the ring and eyes Shawn Hart, he steps over the top rope and enters the ring::
::bell rings:: Brandon Jacobs: Johnny Starr and Shawn Hart lock up! Johnny Starr powers Shawn Hart down into the corner! Shawn Hart gets up and locks up again! HE POWERS HIM DOWN AGAIN! Shawn Hart is on his knees looking very frustrated. He gets up slowly and asks for a test of strength. SHAWN HART CHEAP SHOTS JOHNNY STARR IN THE STOMACH! That was dangerously close to being a DQ! Shawn Hart throws Johnny Starr into the corner and starts chopping away with really loud sound chops! JOHNNY STARR BRUSHES THEM OFF AND REVERSES! HE NAILS HART WITH SEVERAL LOUD CHOPS AS THE CROWD GOES WILD! Shawn Hart rolls out to regroup!
Zack Sirus: Time for a time out, it is a good plan. He can regroup and figure out a way to tame this giant.
Brandon Jacobs: Johnny Starr goes after Shawn Hart and Shawn Hart runs around the ring and back in on the other side! Johnny Starr enters the ring.... HART NAILS HIM WITH A SUPERKICK TO THE FACE! Johnny Starr is dazed! Hart kicks him in the stomach and doubles up Starr, he nails Starr with a piledriver! In a matter of moments, Hart has taken control of the match once again. Hart locks on a reverse chinlock to sap the strength out of his opponent! Starr is trying to break free but Hart has it locked in tight!
Zack Sirus: Hart is using his head here. He needs to tire Starr down and use his conditioning to his advantage.
Brandon Jacobs: Starr is kicking his legs out to move closer to the ropes and get the break... REF IS FORCED to call for the break! Starr gets up and stares at Hart. He charges Hart and starts nailing him with lefts and rights! He kicks Hart in the stomach and goes for a BIG BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX! Hart lands hard on the mat and grabs his back! Starr stays on Hart and FOLLOWS UP WITH A TILT A WHIRL BACKBREAKER! Starr is holding Hart’s back over his knee for good measure in hopes of slowing the quicker Hart down!
Zack Sirus: Very good strategy here by Hart, his primary advantage here is his quickness. Brandon Jacobs: The ref is checking here to see if Hart wants to give up but he is shaking his head no! Starr breaks the hold and lets Hart fall face first to the mat. Starr is motioning for the chokeslam! Hart is slowly getting up... STARR LOCKS HIS HAND AROUND HART’s THROAT... CHOKESLAM! He is calling for his finisher! Starr picks him up in the crosshold and HART STARTS STRUGGLING AND BREAKS FREE! Starr turns around.... HART ATTACK (Diamond Cutter) ON STARR! The big man falls down to the ground! Hart makes the cover! 1-----------------2---------------KICKOUT! Starr barely got the shoulder up there! Hart drags him to the center of the ring and picks him up again... ANOTHER HART ATTACK! He hooks the leg and covers! 1-----------2------------3! SHAWN HART WINS THE MATCH AND ADVANCES TO THE SECOND ROUND OF THE TOURNAMENT!
Zack Sirus: That was a hell of a match but ultimately Hart’s blitz at the end was too much for Starr to recover from. He’ll be back though and will learn from this to be even more scary wrestler. Once he learns to better refine his skills, he will be an unstoppable monster capable of doing anything he wants in that ring and god help the WFW when that day happens.
Brandon Jacobs: Next up is the long awaited main event between Doc Silver and Psycho, stay tuned because the best is coming up next!
