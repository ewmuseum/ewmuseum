Bio for Christian Sands

21 September 2004

PaulNJ21 — 21 September 2004 NAME: Christian Sands HEIGHT: 6'3" WEIGHT: 270lbs HOMETOWN: Barrie, ON, CA ENTRANCE MUSIC: "Dark Machine" - Paul Oakenfold FIVE SIGNATURE MOVES: Grounded dragon sleeper, seated strait-jacket submission hold, rolling neck snap, fireman's carry into an over-the-knee neckbreaker, any variety of suplex SETUP: anything FINISHER: The Sandman's Clutch (Cobra clutch crossface) or the Sand Blaster (Cobra clutch bomb) WHO YOUR CHARACTER IS: Broke into the professional business in August of 2002, debuting on GXW's Onslaught by destroying two jobbers. Romped his way through the undercard before moving up in 2003, defeating "Kamikaze" Randy Cobb in the King of the Cage tournament and taking John Miller to his limits in that same tournament. Also saw action in SCW as a member of G.O.D., scoring victories over such notables as Sean "Triple X" Stevens, Kendall Codine, and Dallas Winston. Claimed the SCW International Title from Scott Johnson and feuded with Dan Ryan until SCW folded. In GXW, feuded with his brother Devon, who buried him under a woodpile and set him on fire to put him on the shelf for six months. Upon his return, Sands reappeared in NFW and scored a number of victories until running awry of Avery Prosser's Seven Deadly Sins. Returned to GXW with an unprovoked attack on Boogie Smallz, then wrestled Jean Rabesque at Battleground Britain 2004 in an epic 66-minute draw. At that same show Sands founded the New Team Phenom with Eric Davis and Clapper, laying out CSWA Champion Dan Ryan backstage. Also wrestled for EPW, where he defeated Beast to win his first World Title. Shortly afterwards, he signed a CSWA contract, which he later reneged on despite a victory over Hornet. Shortly afterwards he walked out on NFW, leaving that promotion behind to focus solely on Empire Pro. Back on the home front he feuded bitterly with Beast, eventually losing the title to him at Unleashed. However, he still stood tall alongside Lindsay Troy, helping her betray Beast and lay him out. He and Troy now operate as a team intent on ravaging EPW's tag ranks. RELEVATANT TITLES WON: EPW World Heavyweight Champion, SCW International Champion (retired the title)
