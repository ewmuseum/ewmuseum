WFW: Spirit of NGEN: Introduction

16 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 16 August 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome everyone to Orlando, Florida and jam packed TD Waterhouse Centre! Tonight at the Spirit of NGEN, we celebrate the history of heritage of the old NGEN and those wrestlers who are no longer with us by putting on a show worthy of their memory!
VIC WATERS: In a day and age where guys stay around until they are senior citizens, you rarely hear about the forgotten generation which came before them who laid the path for the stars you see competing today. But a lot of the great ones have left this sport for the great beyond, never to comeback again, Zack Sirus, Earl of Doom, Max Knight, Glitter Boy, Dusty Berray, Senor Lobo, Werewolf, Striker, Mirzak Hitler, Pestilence, Famine, and Mike “The Terminator” Day.
BRANDON JACOBS: From the original 1992 NGEN, which lasted until our financial backer Prodigy went insane and limited people’s time on bulletin boards and then restarted in early 1995 when Prodigy came back to its senses, there are only three wrestlers still competing actively today, there are only three wrestlers still competing actively today, one is Golden Hawk who is in a heated feud with Jonathan Marx in GLCW, the other is King Krusher who is the commissioner of GLCW and active competitor in NFW, and… this third one may surprise some people, a very, very young “The Viewer’s Choice” Scotty Michaels back when he competed as the “Sexy Boy” Scott Michaels.
VIC WATERS: Scotty Michaels looks like he hasn’t aged a day. BRANDON JACOBS: Well, he started when he was very, very young so he is in his prime as we speak…
VIC WATERS: Manson is in trouble tonight with that bad knee of his, Scotty Michaels has been steam rolling opponents one by one. He looks better now than he has ever been in his career. I think tonight on the eleventh anniversary of the Spirit of NGEN, one of our founding members is going to break through and finally win the title from Manson inside of a steel cage.
BRANDON JACOBS: That is Manson’s domain though, that is like challenging Doc Silver to a ladder match, you just don’t do that. Manson is a tough SOB and he’ll find a way somehow. I believe in him.
VIC WATERS: Time catches up to everyone and I think tonight is the night he breaks through and takes it all.
BRANDON JACOBS: Speaking of a champion without much time left, Doc Silver is putting his title on the line in a best out two of three fall match for the title. Shawn Hart has been denied at the alter twice, but I don’t think he’ll go down swinging tonight. I smell a title change.
VIC WATERS: Doc Silver owns Shawn Hart. Plain and simple… People can say like the referee of the main event tonight can say that Doc is a washed up 45 year old all they want. Doc Silver is still one of the best in the sport today and he is going to keep that snot nosed punk Shawn Hart from winning the title. Before Doc Silver won the title, I heard from people like Zack Sirus and others than Doc Silver couldn’t go anymore. That he wasn’t able to be the top dog anymore and what did Doc Silver do? He won the WFW World Title, not once, BUT TWICE. Beating Psycho in a match he still hasn’t recovered fully from. You like to say you believe in Manson, well I believe in Doc, I know he is the best in wrestling today and everyone who disagrees can go to hell as far as I’m concerned.
BRANDON JACOBS: I have to give Doc Silver his due, he has lied and cheated his way to the top better than anyone else, but don’t you think Shawn Hart has begun to out do Doc at his own game? Don’t you remember what C.O.C.K.S. and Gideon did to Rat Fink & Doc Silver on the last show? They are the two most devious men in this company and they laid waste to them. Even if those two are more devious, C.O.C.K.S. are more hungry for championship Gold and I have a feeling that they will break through tonight. Now, I’m going to say something which I hope to never say again, but I really do hope Doc Silver goes out there and defies the odd. I can’t imagine what Shawn Hart and Diablo would do if they held the WFW World Heavyweight Title. They would be even more obnoxious than they currently are and I don’t even know how that is possible. Hopefully the special referee in the match calls it down the line and makes it a fair fight.
VIC WATERS: Has there been any word on who the special referee really is? BRANDON JACOBS: I’ve heard rumors who it is absolutely not, but I don’t know who it is. We’ll all find out in due time… let’s get this show on the road tonight, when we comeback, we will have our first match of the evening, Diablo will take on “The Shadow” Andre Eian in a Flaming Tables match, Andre Eian has said that if he loses the match, that he will retire!
VIC WATERS: Diablo is eating table. BRANDON JACOBS: We will be back, right after this commercial break!
