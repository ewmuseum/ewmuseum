WWF Pandamonium: Manson & Iris vs. Psycho & Felix Red (Main Event)

23 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 23 July 2004 (BROADCAST BOOTH) BRANDON JACOBS: I can’t believe the sun has almost set on yet another show. JONATHAN MARX: I love this zoo, it was an honor and privilege to be able to do play by play from the greatest zoo in the world. My dad would always take us on trips to California when I was a kid and besides from Disneyland, the San Diego Zoo was my favorite stop. I use to ride around on the San Diego Zoo tour bus and I would get all excited when I saw the elephants and giraffes. My dad would tell some really bad jokes as we passed the monkeys about wrestlers in rival promotions.
BRANDON JACOBS: When I was a kid, I use to get Ranger Ricks and Zoo Books on a regular basis. I didn’t have the money to visit the San Diego Zoo, but I discovered the world through nature magazines. Kids these days are so lucky to have channels like Animal Planet, The National Geographic Channel, and Discovery. All I had was PBS.
VIC WATERS: I never really was an animal lover. Well, except for that one time I got real drunk. It is true what they say if you drink enough, after a point, anything looks good.
JONATHAN MARX: ::shakes head:: BRANDON JACOBS: You so have to be on the next Survivor. VIC WATERS: No Poland Spring Water, no Vic… JONATHAN MARX: All of this drinking can’t be healthy for your liver. VIC WATERS: We are getting to the day and age where when and if I need a new liver, they can take it out of my clone and all will be right with the world.
JONATHAN MARX: Wouldn’t your clone be a big fan of Poland Spring Water too? VIC WATERS: Nah, I take good care of my spare parts. JONATHAN MARX: Sometimes I wonder about you… BRANDON JACOBS: Alright, we’ve talked too much about our animal love and spare livers, we have a main event upon us….
JONATHAN MARX: This is going to be one hell of a cage match. LOVE have been waiting forever for a chance get Manson and El Arco Iris in a cage and if I know them, they are going to make sure that Manson is in no condition to walk out of the WFW World Heavyweight Title match with the title.
VIC WATERS: A cage match is all about survival of the fittest… we all saw what LOVE did to El Arco Iris the last time he was in a cage. How is El Arco Iris going to be about to stop them from LOVE’s wrath?
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is one of the brightest young superstars today, but without a doubt, he is going to have to overcome a lot for Manson and him to walk out victorious in one piece. I actually think they will.
VIC WATERS: You are way too optimistic, always looking on the bright side of life. This is going to be pure carnage on a mass scale.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris will get his revenge, you just wait and see. Folks, the time for talking is over, lets send this one down to ringside and get this one underway!
RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, a member of Team LOVE, weighing in at 213 pounds and standing at 6 feet, three inches, “The Boy Who Destroyed The World” and former BAD World Heavyweight Champion, FELIX RED!
::Felix Red comes down to the ring to the loud boos from the crowd and enters the cage:: (CUEUP: “Tunak Tunak Tun” by Dahler Mendhi) RING ANNOUNCER: His opponent, hailing from over the rainbow, the Prince of Prismatic, EL ARCO IRIS!
::El Arco Iris walks down to a huge pop from the crowd, dozens of children can been seen in the crowd wearing El Arco Iris masks, and enters the cage::
(CUEUP: "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing third, the second member of Team LOVE, the current WFW North American Champion, PSYCHO!
::Psycho walks down to the ring to the boos of the crowd and enters the cage:: (CUEUP: "Mechanical Animals" by Marilyn Manson) RING ANNOUNCER: And now the champion, from Chicago, IL, the WFW World Heavyweight Champion, the man, the myth, the legend…. MICHAEL MANSON!
::Manson is walking down slowly to the ring to the roar of the crowd, he enters the cage and hands his title to the ring attendant::
*bell ring* BRANDON JACOBS: There is the bell and this match is underway, both teams are conferring before the match officially begins.
VIC WATERS: Hey Marxie, what do you think about Manson putting a bounty on himself? JONATHAN MARX: I am not really sure what to think. On one hand, Manson is also a fellow genius so I defer to his judgment on certain things since he has been round longer than I have. But on the other hand, there is a fine line between genius and insanity and he may have crossed it. I think I understand what he is doing, but it also could backfire on him. It is certainly a risk.
VIC WATERS: Would you really want to risk getting on the bad side of LOVE? For the pain and suffering they could cause you, sometimes the title is not enough.
BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED AND PSYCHO ARE WALKING OVER TO MANSON AND EL ARCO IRIS WHO TURN TO FACE THEM… PSYCHO PUSHES MANSON AND MANSON PUSHES BACK! Felix Red charges Manson…. SPINNING HEEL KICK TO THE FACE!
JONATHAN MARX: The war is on! BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris charges Felix Red and he hits him with a stranding dropkick! PSYCHO IS UP AND HE BLIND SIDES EL ARCO IRIS WITH A CLOTHESLINE!
VIC WATERS: Psycho isn’t going to let El Arco Iris take his WFW North American Title away from him.
BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO JUMPS ONTO EL ARCO IRIS AND HE STARTS POUNDING HIM WITH CLOSED FISTS RIGHT TO THE HEAD! El Arco Iris is trying to escape the punches but Psycho is holding him down the best he can.
VIC WATERS: Psycho is trying to send a message to El Arco Iris that he isn’t a man to be messed with.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red and Manson are both up and they are exchanging chops back and forth! Those two are beating the bloody hell out of each other.
JONATHAN MARX: That is going to be a hell of a PPV main event. Both men are going to go above and beyond in order to get the job done at Cherry Blossom Chaos.
BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS GRABS ONE OF THE ARMS OF PSYCHO AND HE LOCKS ON A FRONT FACE LOCK ON PSYCHO!
JONATHAN MARX: That is one way to keep him from punching him. VIC WATERS: He had to do something or his goose would have been cooked. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is trying to get back up to his knees with a front face lock applied on Psycho….. EL ARCO IRIS IS FINALLY UP BUT PSYCHO IS PUSHING HIM TOWARDS THE ROPES! El Arco Iris climbs onto the second rope….. HE HITS PSYCHO WITH THE CROWN OF HATHOR! El Arco Iris grabs the head of Psycho and locks on a figure four sleeper right on the head of Psycho!
JONATHAN MARX: Sweet move, Psycho was just trying back him into the cage and El Arco Iris realized where he was and was able to fight back.
VIC WATERS: Manson doesn’t look he is doing as well as El Arco Iris. BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED HAS MANSON BACKED INTO THE CORNER AND FELIX RED IS HITTING HIM REPEATEDLY WITH SPIN KICKS RIGHT TO THE HEAD!
JONATHAN MARX: Felix Red’s feet are lethal weapons. VIC WATERS: It is good to see someone finally take it to Manson. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is slumping over in the corner… FELIX RED JUMPS UP AND HE TAKES HIM DOWN TO THE MAT WITH A FRANKENSTEINER!
JONATHAN MARX: Red is always full of energy, but If he isn’t careful, he is going to wear himself down and not have anything left for when this match comes down to the nitty gritty.
VIC WATERS: If Felix Red can end it now, he should end it now. Manson has incredible endurance and can take a lot of punishment. You are just as likely to pin Manson now as you are later.
JONATHAN MARX: That is true enough. BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED IS CLIMBING UP THE TURNBUCKLES TO THE TOP…. MANSON IS TRYING TO GET UP…. FELIX RED MOONSAULTS OFF THE TOP AND HITS MANSON WITH A DDT! El Arco Iris breaks the figure four sleeper.
VIC WATERS: What a maneuver. JONATHAN MARX: Nobody flies through the air like Felix Red. BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED GOES DOWN TO MAKE THE COUNT! 1-------------------------------2--------------- EL ARCO IRIS DIVES TO MAKE THE SAVE FOR MANSON!
JONATHAN MARX: Manson seems a bit off tonight. VIC WATERS: I don’t think Manson is off, Felix Red is simply that good. JONATHAN MARX: That is an excellent point, Manson hasn’t faced many wrestlers like Felix Red before because there really isn’t any and it has been awhile since they squared off. Manson is trying to acclimated to Felix Red’s style but he is a step behind.
VIC WATERS: Felix Red could be the man who dethrones Manson. JONATHAN MARX: I think Manson will find a way, but I am certainly worried. BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS IS CHOPPING AWAY AT FELIX RED AS HE TRIES TO GET UP! El Arco Iris grabs the arm of Felix Red…. HE WHIPS HIM OFF THE ROPES… NO REVERSAL BY FELIX RED…. EL ARCO IRIS JUMPS OFF THE SECOND ROPE AND HITS FELIX RED WITH A QUEBRADA RIGHT ONTO FELIX RED!
JONATHAN MARX: Pin him! BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS HOOKS THE LEG OF FELIX RED! The ref goes down for the count! 1--------------------------------------------2----------------------------- FELIX RED GETS THE SHOULDER UP!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris gets a lucky two count on Felix Red, but that is all it was, luck.
JONATHAN MARX: You really don’t like El Arco Iris, do you? VIC WATERS: I hate his guts. BRANDON JACOBS: A dazed Psycho is crawling up on El Arco Iris and Felix Red from behind…. EL ARCO IRIS PULLS FELIX RED UP BY THE HAIR…. FELIX RED PUSHES EL ARCO IRIS BACK AND HE FALLS BACKWARDS AND TRIPS OVER PSYCHO!
VIC WATERS: The great thing about LOVE is that they work as a team. They know what to do to get the job done. They suffered a couple of losses lately because of some bad referring, but they don’t let it effect them.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is telling Psycho to pick El Arco Iris up and hold him! JONATHAN MARX: Beating time coming for El Arco Iris…. BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO PICKS UP EL ARCO IRIS AND HE HOLDS HIS ARMS BACK FOR FELIX RED! JONATHAN MARX: Manson is still down from the DDT earlier by Felix Red. VIC WATERS: And I hope he stays down… BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red winds up….. SPINNING HEEL KICK RIGHT TO THE FACE OF EL ARCO IRIS AS PSYCHO HELD HIM THERE!
JONATHAN MARX: Normal kicks are effective, but that was even worse because Psycho held him in place when he delivered the blow to make it even more effective.
BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED IS ORDERING PSYCHO TO HOLD EL ARCO IRIS DOWN ON THE MAT! What could he be planning now?!
JONATHAN MARX: With Felix Red’s aerial ability, it could be anything. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red exits through the ropes to the apron on the outside…. There is much room there with the cage…. FELIX RED IS CLIMBING UP THE ROPE USING THE CAGE AS LEVERAGE…. FELIX RED FLIES OFF WITH A TWISTING LEGDROP RIGHT ON THE HEAD OF EL ARCO IRIS!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red drove that leg drop right into the face of El Arco Iris. If that didn’t knock him out cold, I don’t know what will.
JONATHAN MARX: Felix Red had so much air on that, he had to be a good seven to ten feet in the air when he flew off the rope.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson sees that El Arco Iris is in trouble and he is trying to get up…. FELIX RED GOES DOWN TO MAKE THE PINFALL! 1--------------------------------------2-------------------------------- MANSON DIVES TO BREAK UP THE PIN AND HEADBUTTS FELIX RED IN THE SIDE TO KNOCK HIM OFF IN THE NICK OF TIME! Felix Red is grabbing his ribs in pain.
JONATHAN MARX: Felix Red wasn’t prepared for that shot and got clocked pretty good right in the ribs. When you do as much flying as Felix Red does, if you can’t breath, it is going to be awfully hard to go like you usually do.
BRANDON JACOBS: An irate Psycho picks up Manson off of El Arco Iris…. PSYCHO PICKS UP MANSON AND HE HITS HIM WITH A JUMPING PILEDRIVER!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is going after the head that rammed hard into Felix Red’s ribs, while it hurt a lot more for Felix Red, you also do damage to yourself in the process.
JONATHAN MARX: Psycho really compressed his neck right into the mat. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is going to check on Felix Red but he may have cracked a rib. Psycho is berating Felix Red to get up so we can finish these two off but Felix Red is having trouble getting up…. PSYCHO GOES BACK TO WORK ON EL ARCO IRIS AND HE IS STOMPING AWAY AT THE RIBS OF EL ARCO IRIS AS PAY BACK FOR WHAT MANSON DID!
VIC WATERS: An eye for an eye, a tooth for a tooth. BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO PICKS UP EL ARCO IRIS…. HE DROPS EL ARCO IRIS RIGHT ONTO HIS KNEE RIGHT INTO THE MIDSECTION!
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris is trying to catch his breath desperately. Psycho really took the wind out of him.
BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO IS DRAGGING EL ARCO IRIS OVER INTO THE CORNER! This can’t be good…. PSYCHO IS SIGNALING FOR THE PSYCHO FLIGHT!
VIC WATERS: His patented Stardust Press! BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO IS CLIMBING UP QUICKLY TO THE TOP ROPE…. WAIT A SECOND, HE ISN’T STOPPING AT THE TOP ROPE… HE IS CLIMBING UP TO THE TOP OF THE CAGE!
VIC WATERS: The last time he did this it was against Doc! JONATHAN MARX: He nearly killed himself too in that match. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is yelling at El Arco Iris to move out of the way…. But El Arco Iris isn’t moving anymore. PSYCHO IS PERCHED AT THE TOP…. MANSON GOES TO PULL EL ARCO IRIS AWAY…. PSYCHO FLIES OFF WITH THE STARDUST PRESS BUT MANSON PULLS EL ARCO IRIS OUT OF THE WAY BEFORE HE CAN CONNECTION! OH MY! Psycho crashed down hard into the mat.
VIC WATERS: Psycho is a crazy man, that why they call him Psycho. He knows his partner is down and he wanted to put the match away for good when he saw the chance.
JONATHAN MARX: Psycho gambles big, but he wins big too. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red looks over at Psycho who really practically fell from the damn heavens… MANSON IS CRAWLING TOWARDS PSYCHO TO MAKE A PIN….. FELIX DIVES FOR MANSON EVEN BEFORE HE CAN MAKE A COVER AND GRABS HIS LEG!
JONATHAN MARX: Psycho needs time to recover, Psycho bought Felix Red time, now Felix Red has to buy Psycho time if they want to win this match.
BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON GETS UP TO HIS KNEES AND HE STARTS THROWING UPPERCUTS RIGHT INTO THE FACE OF FELIX RED… FELIX RED GETS UP TO HIS KNEES AND HE STARTS CHOPPING AWAY AT MANSON!
CROWD: MANSON! MANSON! MANSON! JONATHAN MARX: When these two do ultimately do meet for the title, is going to be a hell of a war. When the big strap is on the line, as much as they are going at it now, nothing will be held back on the PPV. They are going to hit each other with everything including the kitchen sink.
BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON IS GETTING UP TO HIS FEET AND THIS CROWD IS ROARING FOR MANSON! Felix Red is trying to get up…. MANSON KICKS FELIX RED IN THE CHEST BEFORE HE CAN GET UP!
VIC WATERS: The boot was well placed, but you can’t keep Felix Red down. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON RUSHES FELIX RED AND HE HITS HIM SHINING WIZARD! Felix Red falls down to the mat!
JONATHAN MARX: He clocked him with that knee like nobody’s business. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON IS GOING FOR THE PIN! The ref goes down for the count! 1------------------------------------2------------------------------------- FELIX RED KICKS OUT!
JONATHAN MARX: That had to be pure instinct, Felix Red is barely there after that shot. VIC WATERS: You could say he doesn’t even exist. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson gets up and he pulls Felix Red up off of the mat…. MANSON SNAP SUPLEXES HIM DOWN ON THE MAT HARD RIGHT ON HIS BACK AND NECK!
JONATHAN MARX: The great thing about the snap suplex is it sets up both of Manson’s finishers. He doesn’t have to target two totally different parts in order to go for either one of his finishers.
BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON PICKS UP FELIX RED AGAIN…. HE NAILS HIM WITH ANOTHER SNAP SUPLEX! VIC WATERS: Shades of Richard Gideon here… BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is going for one more…. HE HOOKS THE TIGHTS OF FELIX RED…. HE HITS HIM WITH A THIRD AND FINAL SNAP SUPLEX DOWN TO THE MAT!
JONATHAN MARX: COVER HIM! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson looks over at Psycho to check to see if he is going to get up…. JONATHAN MARX: With the way everyone has been breaking up pinfalls, I would too. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON PICKS UP PSYCHO OFF OF THE MAT! VIC WATERS: Psycho has had time to recover, but not nearly enough. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON GOES FOR THE KICK TO THE STOMACH DOUBLING PSYCHO OVER…. SWEET DREAMS…. NO! PSYCHO PUSHES HIM AWAY LAST SECOND AND MANSON GOES HEAD FIRST INTO THE STEEL CAGE BUSTING HIM OPEN!
JONATHAN MARX: It was a good plan in theory. VIC WATERS: Theory doesn’t win matches though…. BRANDON JACOBS: An exhausted Psycho bends off as he tries to catch his breath…. El Arco Iris is sitting in the corner regrouping and watching all of chaos…. EL ARCO IRIS GETS UP AND SNEAKS UP ON PSYCHO…
VIC WATERS: Psycho turn around! BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS CHICKEN WINGS THE ARMS AND TAKES PSYCHO DOWN WITH A BACKSLIDE! The referee goes down for the count! 1------------------------------------------------------2------------------------------- PSYCHO KICKS OUT!
VIC WATERS: Psycho didn’t battle all this time to lose like that. El Arco Iris is going to have to go to hell and back in order to get this win.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris picks up Psycho…. INSIDE CRADLE ON PSYCHO! 1------------------------------------------2------------------------------ PSYCHO KICKS OUT AGAIN!
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris may just have Psycho’s number! BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris bounces off the ropes…. HE CATCHES PSYCHO WITH A FLYING FOREARM IN THE SIDE OF THE HEAD AND PSYCHO GOES DOWN!
VIC WATERS: This is a setup for the Happy Dance! JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris has him just where he wants him….. ALL PSYCHO HAS TO DO IS GET UP AND THIS MATCH IS OVER!
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red looks over at his partner who is in trouble and he struggling to get up… FELIX RED USES THE ROPES TO HELP HIMSELF UP! Psycho is beginning to get up….
JONATHAN MARX: HURRY AND HIT HIM WITH IT! BRANDON JACOBS: A bloody Manson sees Felix Red standing behind El Arco Iris….. MANSON BULL RUSHES FELIX RED…. EL ARCO IRIS TURNS AROUND AND GOES TO HIT FELIX RED WITH THE HAPPY DANCE SUPERKICK BUT HE ACCIDENTALLY HITS MANSON!
VIC WATERS: Was it an accident?! JONATHAN MARX: Of course it was, you always trying to cause problems. VIC WATERS: Well… there is the bounty Manson put on himself out there. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is apologizing to Manson… FELIX RED COMES FROM BEHIND AND NAILS HIM WITH THE ECSTASY TO AGONY AND EL ARCO IRIS IS OUT COLD!
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris was too worried about Manson and not enough about himself and got caught off guard. El Arco Iris strength is his greatest weakness, but is admirable.
VIC WATERS: Weakness is weakness. BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED PICKS UP A GROGGY MANSON OFF OF THE MAT…. FELIX RED PUSHES HIM TOWARDS PSYCHO! Psycho picks up Manson…. TIGER DRIVER RIGHT INTO THE MAT!
JONATHAN MARX: Good lord, if there was ever a setup move that was a finisher, that is the one because it means totally annihilation.
BRANDON JACOBS: OH MY! PSYCHO IS TAKING OUT A FORK OUT OF HIS TIGHTS AND HE IS REPEATEDLY RAMMING IT INTO THE HEAD OF MANSON IN ORDER TO SPILL EVEN MORE OF HIS BLOOD! Felix Red is climbing up to top of the cage while Psycho’s is committing this utter atrocity on Manson!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris is down, they have him all to themselves now. BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED IS ON TOP OF THE CAGE AND HE IS GIVING THE SIGNAL TO PSYCHO THAT MANSON IS DONE…. PSYCHO PICKS UP MANSON AND HE BODYSLAMS HIM DOWN TO THE MAT! Felix Red perches…. HE FLIES OFF FROM THE TOP OF THE CAGE AND HITS HIM WITH THE SHATTERED HORIZON!
JONATHAN MARX: Whoa. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red bounced off of Manson with the impact…. FELIX RED CRAWLS OVER AND MAKES THE COVER AND PSYCHO HOLDS THE TIRED FELIX RED DOWN! The ref goes down for the count! 1-----------------------------------------------------------------2-------------------------------------------------------------------3!
VIC WATERS: THEY DID IT! RING ANNOUNCER: The winners of the match….. THE TEAM OF FELIX RED AND PSYCHO, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS TEAM LOVE!
BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO IS SIGNALING TO THE BACK AND ANARKY AND WELLS AND RUNNING DOWN TO THE RING! Wells takes a pair of hedge clippers and breaks the lock and they enter the cage.
JONATHAN MARX: Come on, there is no call for this! BRANDON JACOBS: TEAM LOVE IS BEATING DOWN MANSON AND EL ARCO IRIS! HERE COMES SECURITY IN ORDER TO TRY TO STOP THEM BUT WELLS ISN’T LETTING ANYONE INTO THE CAGE!
JONATHAN MARX: Look at what Psycho is doing…. HE IS TRYING TO RIP OFF THE MASK OF EL ARCO IRIS…. HE HAS DONE IT AND EL ARCO IRIS IS TRYING TO COVER UP HIS FACE! THIS HAS GONE TOO FAR!
(CUEUP: “One Shot At Glory” By Judas Priest) BRANDON JACOBS: THAT IS WINK’S MUSIC! WINK IS COMING OUT AND HE HAS A MICROPHONE IN HIS HAND!
CHRIS WINK: STOP IT! YOU FOUR HAVE GONE FAR ENOUGH! Psycho… You’ve caused enough trouble tonight doing what you did in the BAD World Heavyweight Title #1 Contender’s match that is why we are going to make things more interesting for you…. BECAUSE YOU SCREWED OVER RAT FINK AND PULSAR…. I’M PUTTING THEM IN THE WFW NORTH AMERICAN TITLE MATCH WITH YOU AND EL ARCO IRIS…. AND YOU ARE QUITE FAMILIAR WITH THE RULES… FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE ON THE MALL!! If you manage to come out champion, Golem will have the choice between you and Wells for who he wants his title shot against.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is irate at Wink and Wells giving Psycho a nasty look, Psycho is cursing up a storm.
CHRIS WINK: Anarky…. Don’t think I have forgotten you…. I AM UNSUSPENDING SCOTTY MICHAELS AND HE WILL BE AT CHERRY BLOSSOM CHAOS AS PART OF A THREE WAY DANCE WITH LARRY TACT!
::Anarky gets a smile on his face:: CHRIS WINK: As for you Wells and Felix, you will feel that wrath of Iron Lion and Manson soon enough. No punishment I could dish out would equal the amount of pain those two will deal to you. GOOD LUCK!
::Wink throws down the mic and goes to the back:: BRANDON JACOBS: Security is trying to take down the cage and it looks like LOVE is finally leaving. What a way to close this show.
JONATHAN MARX: If all this happened here tonight, I don’t even want to know what the PPV will be like.
VIC WATERS: And DC thought it was violent before…. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, I’d like to thank you all for joining us here tonight, we hope to see you all at our PPV Cherry Blossom Chaos, which will feature Blue Cat going one on one with Copycat and if Copycat wins, he gets a world title shot, Anarky will be facing off against the man who attacked him after his match with Maelstrom, Scotty Michaels and his old nemesis from tonight, Larry Tact, Jared Wells will be putting the BAD World Heavyweight Title on the line against the seven foot three Iron Lion, Psycho will be putting up the WFW North American Title against El Arco Iris, Rat Fink, and Pulsar in Falls on the Mall II, and last but not least, Manson will be putting the WFW World Heavyweight Title up against Felix Red who beat Manson here tonight! It should be a hell of a show! Thank you for joining us and we see you all in DC!
