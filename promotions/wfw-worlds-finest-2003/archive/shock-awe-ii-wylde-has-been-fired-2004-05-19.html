Shock & Awe II: WYLDE HAS BEEN FIRED!

19 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 19 May 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome back everyone and we are thankfully back after that last match. What do you think of this current match coming up?
VIC WATERS: Edmunds seems like he is primed to be picked off here coming off his win against Rabesque. He is going through a mid life crisis, his grandmother died… he has been going through a rough week.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar has shown he can win against some big time opponents. But in order to make a run at a title, he needs to string some wins together and get some momentum behind which he hasn’t been able to do. Edmunds just came off his big win against one of the best technical wrestlers in the world in Jean Rabesque, while I love Pulsar, one would think that this would be Edmunds night despite all the other circumstances around this match.
VIC WATERS: You have to remember though, before that last win, he had quite a losing streak going and even though he may be feeling confident after his big win against Rabesque, if things don’t go well early, Pulsar may be able to get in Edmunds head and get the right to face anyone he wants at the PPV.
BRANDON JACOBS: You may be right. That losing mentality is hard to break, but I have faith Edmunds will prevail.
VIC WATERS: Damn this is ass backwards, usually you are the one singing the praises of Pulsar and trashing Edmunds.
BRANDON JACOBS: I have great respect for both men, it just that I like Pulsar more. But I have a feeling Edmunds wants to make a big impression here tonight tonight along with everything else in his first match against President
VIC WATERS: Life is filled with disappointment, Edmunds is just going to have to deal with it.
BRANDON JACOBS: Man, you are harsh, are you sober or something? VIC WATERS: Leave me alone or I won’t let you have any later if business picks up. BRANDON JACOBS: I apologize, folks, let’s send this match down to ringside and get this one underway!
(CUEUP: “Whiskey in a Jar” by Metallica) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from Australia, standing at over 6’3, PULSAR! ::Pulsar receives a huge roar from the crowd as he comes down to the aisle and enters the ring::
(CUEUP: "Brain Stew" by Green Day) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, currently residing in his summer residence in Escandido, California, three time WWL World Champion and current President of the WFW….. 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds!
:: Edmunds comes down to the ring with Sherri with a microphone in his hand and enters the ring to the loud boos from the crowd::
BRANDON JACOBS: For crying out loud, can’t we get to the match already? VIC WATERS: Quiet, our President is about to speak! EDMUNDS: This is a GREAT day for World’s Finest Wrestling as we approach the beginning of a new age in this rinky-dink promotion. After months and months of Alex Wylde’s mismanagement, I have come to awaken the sleeping giant so that we can ascend to the APEX of the wrestling world as the greatest promotion. But first, I would like to take this time to thank the one man who has made my stay here in the WFW ever so pleasurable. Alex Wylde .. if you would be so kind to join us.
(Wylde comes from behind the curtain with a smile on his face. He walks down the rampway but is stopped at the bottom before he can get in the ring.)
EDMUNDS: “Sorry, I have strict rules about who can and cannot be in the same ring as myself. Since this match with Pulsar was signed before my coronation as President, I must fulfill this last duty as a mere WFW wrestler. But Alex, you have been there for me as I was making, no, tearing my way through the WFW fodder to the top of the promotion. You gave me that summer home in Escandido. You gave me dinners and a very large credit line on the company’s credit card, which I assure you I appreciate ever so much. You always said that there were only two people you’d like to see in the Presidency. Alex Wylde, or a friend of Alex Wylde’s, and my friend, I’m glad that you’re content. These past few months have been a blast. From winning my World title shot, to help putting you over at Christmas Carnage. You’ve truly been one of the closest people I’ve been with, aside from Sherri, for the past few months.”
(Edmunds walks around the ring. He looks down at the mat, then looks back up.) EDMUNDS: “You’re fired, now get the hell out of my building.” (RING ANNOUNCERS) (The bodyguards surround Wylde and begin taking him up the ramp as he is cursing and spitting.)
EDMUNDS: “IM the President of WFW now. IM the one who calls the shots around here. How DARE Alex Wylde think that he could take MY World Title and not have any consequences coming his way! Alex Wylde is a figment of the PAST. Alex Wylde is NO long employed by WFW now … AND NEVER WILL BE AGAIN. SEAN EDMUNDS is the FUTURE of World’s Finest Wrestling. Pulsar will see it tonight, Copycat will find out in the future … and eventually even that backstabbing Michael Manson will find out that I DON’T LIKE TO LOSE. Don’t adjust your television sets, I *am* this sensational!”
BRANDON JACOBS: OH MY GOD! Edmunds has just fired the former WFW World Heavyweight Champion Alex Wylde.
VIC WATERS: I never liked Wylde. BRANDON JACOBS: This is a travesty of justice, how can Edmunds even do this? VIC WATERS: Apparently Wylde cooked the books and the board of directors gave him permission.
BRANDON JACOBS: Alex Wylde is a true Hall of Famer, he deserves better than this. Folks, we are going to take a short commercial break while the police get Wylde out of here who doesn’t seem like he wants leave.
CROWD: WYLDE! WYLDE! WYLDE! BRANDON JACOBS: Stay tuned!
