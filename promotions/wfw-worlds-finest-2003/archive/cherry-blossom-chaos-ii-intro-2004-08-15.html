Cherry Blossom Chaos II: Intro

15 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 15 August 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome everyone to Cherry Blossom Chaos II, we are live from Washington, DC in front of the Reflecting Pool where hordes of fans have gathered to witnesses today’s PPV show!
VIC WATERS: They remember what happened last year at Cherry Blossom Chaos when the underdog Psycho won a four way Brawl at the Mall against Gideon, Shawn Hart, and Doc Silver in order to dethrone Doc Silver as champion.
JONATHAN MARX: In many ways, I think that show was the turning point of the promotion. The fans talked about that match for a long, long time afterwards. They never saw people brawling up the stairs of the Lincoln Memorial and be thrown into the Reflecting Pool. People realized at that moment that WFW was committed not only to bringing their fans the world’s finest wrestling, but some of the world’s best brawling as well.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is going to have a hell of a task of defending his title against El Arco Iris, Rat Fink, and Pulsar all in one match as Doc learned at last’s year show. When all four men are out to get you, it is nearly impossible to walk out as champion.
JONATHAN MARX: To show you how good this show is, this year the Brawl at the Mall isn’t even the main event!
BRANDON JACOBS: This is going to be a loaded show with all the big matches that we have on this one. Let’s go through the show match by match… Luster makes his long awaited WFW return against Bartleby and Luster has personally told me that he will attempt to take Bartleby’s mask off and find who he really is.
VIC WATERS: Do you still have no recollection at all on who Bartleby really is? BRANDON JACOBS: I really wish I did. But my brain is still swiss cheesed because of what Michaels and Bartleby did to me….
JONATHAN MARX: Sort of like Vic’s brain after too many Poland Spring bottles… VIC WATERS: Nah, forgetfulness has it perks, tomorrow I am going to be able to watch this show again and it will all be new to me. I can sit back, relax and listen to my own witty banter for the first time. It is absolutely wonderful.
BRANDON JACOBS: Killing brain cells is bad…. Moving on to our next match, Golem fresh off winning his big match at the San Diego Zoo despite interference from Psycho will go one on one with one of the toughest SOBs in all of wrestling, Mike Lane.
VIC WATERS: Mike Lane has really impressed me lately. He has comeback and he has shown that he can still go. All he has to do is get a couple of big wins now and he’ll be in the hunt for a title.
JONATHAN MARX: I am going with Golem in this match, I think he will win this match against Lane and go on to the winner of the Iron Lion/Jared Wells match and bring home the BAD World Heavyweight Title. He showed a lot to me fighting back and overcoming the odds when everything was against him in his match in San Diego.
BRANDON JACOBS: I’ve been thinking about which way to go in this and it just seems too close to call so anything I say is just a guess… let’s move on to our next match on the show, the longest reigning WFW North American Champion ever Richard Gideon will go one on one with relative new comer John Doe.
JONATHAN MARX: I’ve had a chance to wrestle John Doe and he is a tremendous wrestler, and I need not speak about the incredible skill of Gideon who went nearly an hour against the WFW World Heavyweight Champion Manson. I am going with Gideon in this match, but I expect it to be an awfully close match.
VIC WATERS: Who is John Doe? The answer is simple, a winner and I predict he is going to start off his career with a huge win on PPV tonight against Richard Gideon. Now I know what you are thinking and yes I remember how Gideon took it to Doc and how he took it to Manson, but I just think this young Doe is just that good and he’ll find a way to win.
BRANDON JACOBS: I am not sure which way it will go, but I think Doe vs. Gideon will shock everyone and may be the best match on an incredible show… this is a match that us wrestling fans really look forward too and I don’t think it will disappoint. Our next match up is the three way match between Anarky, Micharels, and Larry Tact…
JONATHAN MARX: This match is made for Anarky, he use to wars and this is going to be an out and out war. Anarky wants to get revenge on Michaels for attacking his post match after Anarky did the impossible and beat Maelstrom and Tact will be wanting some revenge for losing a really close strap match.
BRANDON JACOBS: I am kind of surprised you picked Anarky over Tact. JONATHAN MARX: Tact is one of the best wrestlers I have ever faced in my life time and each and every time I face him it is an honor, but as great as his technical prowess is, Anarky is one of the alltime great brawlers.
VIC WATERS: What you all said was true, Anarky is great brawler, Scotty Michaels is a former WFW World Heavyweight and North American Champion, but I am going with Larry Tact. Michaels and Anarky will be so preoccupied with each other, Tact will have the advantage because he will be able to use their anger for each other to catch his opponents off guard and when they are least expecting, Tact will use that Pentium V brain of his to figure out a way to win the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: My pick is Scotty Michaels, I just think he has something up his sleeve knowing his long history of dirty tricks… Our next match up is Copycat verses Blue Cat and if Copycat wins, he gets a world heavyweight title shot as promised by President Edmunds.
VIC WATERS: There is no way in bloody hell that Edmunds is going to let Copycat win this match. He’ll do anything in his power to make sure that Blue Cat wins and Copycat goes even longer without a title shot.
JONATHAN MARX: But on the other hand, Copycat has worked so hard to get this chance to get a World Heavyweight Title shot… Can you imagine how angry Copycat would be if Edmunds helped Blue Cat win and prevent him yet again from getting his much deserved shot? Copycat would devour everyone and anyone until justice was served. I hope Copycat finally pulls through and wins the shot tonight and all will be right with the world.
BRANDON JACOBS: I want Copycat to win, but I am going with Blue Cat. Copycat is like the Red Sox when it comes title time, something always happens to cost him the shot and until I see otherwise, I am going with Blue Cat… and now, on to a match I have really been looking forward to, Jared Wells will put up the BAD World Heavyweight Title on the line against Iron Lion.
JONATHAN MARX: Iron Lion has been owning LOVE left and right. He battled against LOVE to get a victory down two on one, he managed to get a singles victory over Anarky, and he helped Copycat get the tag victory against Blue Cat & Wells. He has been on a real roll and it will be very hard for Wells to stop him.
VIC WATERS: Jared Wells is my favorite wrestler so you know where my heart lies on this one. I really hope he figures out a way to beat Iron Lion but it is going to be a hell of a task to do it. Hopefully LOVE resorts to some good old cheating action and they figure out a way to beat him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rarely do you ever call the champion the underdog but this is one of those cases. I think Wells can beat Iron Lion, but when you have someone the size and skill of Iron Lion, you don't have much room for any mistakes. I don't know who is going to win, but it is going to be a hell of a fight.
VIC WATERS: ::lifts up his bottle of Poland Spring Water:: Jared Wells we salute you! BRANDON JACOBS: Thank god you weren't saluting Anarky or we have been thrown off the air... Last but not least, Manson will put up the WFW World Heavyweight Title on the line against Felix Red.
JONATHAN MARX: Felix Red has been on a roll lately and with his lightning fast speed and martial art abilities, he is thee most dangerous men in all of wrestling today. Manson is going to have a hell of a battle on his hands if he wants to walk out the WFW World Heavyweight Champion.
BRANDON JACOBS: So… who are you picking? JONATHAN MARX: Manson…. Wait… Felix Red… I really don’t know to be honest. Felix Red has the ability to pin his opponent in a flash of offense, he doesn’t need to work you down like so many other wrestlers do. If Felix Red gets a spurt on Manson, all it takes is three seconds for a new World Heavyweight Champion to be crowned. How about you Vic?
VIC WATERS: Can the boy who destroyed the world destroy Manson? If I knew, I would have a bet in at Vegas and get out of this place. Honestly, it is a pick’em. Manson managed to get a submission out of Gideon a second before the time limit , he managed to finally beat, the legendary Doc Silver, so he has shown that he can do the impossible. Manson is a clutch performer who has repeatedly stepped up in the clutch BUT…. He has had instances like when Shawn Hart retained the WFW World Heavyweight Title in Manson’s own home town of Chicago. It could go either way, but I lean more towards Manson than Felix Red.
BRANDON JACOBS: Last year, Psycho shocked the world when he won the World Heavyweight Title over Gideon, Shawn Hart, and Doc. This year I think Felix Red is going to be the one shaking things up. Don’t ask me why I think that, but I just have this feeling that tonight may be his night.
VIC WATERS: It will never happen. BRANDON JACOBS: We will see. Folks, we have a hell of a show for you and we aren’t going to keep you waiting any longer, when we comeback, we will start off the show with BARTLEBY VERSES LUSTER AS LUSTER ATTEMPTS TO UNMASK BARTLEBY! STAY TUNED!
