Shock & Awe II: Larry Tact vs. Copycat

21 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 21 May 2004 (BACKSTAGE) (Copycat is seen walking backstage and El Arco Iris shows up in his path.) El Arco Iris: Hello Señor Copycat, it's been a while since we've talked! Copycat: ...Gotta go. I have a match. El Arco Iris: El Arco Iris knows this, but El Arco Iris just wants to say something (Copycat says nothing, looking at El Arco Iris expectantly) El Arco Iris: El Arco Iris has been watching you recently and El Arco Iris is a little worried. El Arco Iris thought you were starting to understand how hatred is of use to no-one, but now you seem to be filled with this innate anger. It's troubling.
(Copycat seems puzzled, almost offended.) Copycat: Hatred? It isn't hatred. All I ever ask is that people respect me, and when no one does...that sort of thing makes me a little peeved.
(El Arco Iris tils his head to the side quizzically.) El Arco Iris: But does attacking them with a steel chair make you any more worthy of respect?
Copycat: "Worthy" of respect? I'm ALREADY worthy of respect. I just beat the CSWA World Heavyweight champion, for Christ's sake. But am I getting any respect? NO!
El Arco Iris: Listen, El Arco Iris respects you, you know that, but El Arco Iris respects you because you are a man of sound judgement and of good character, who should realise that pain only begets more pain!
Copycat: Let me ask you something, Arco. Do you think you understand my dilemma here? Because you DON'T. Do you even know what it's LIKE to have any respect?
(El Arco Iris doesn't make any motion to respond.) Copycat: I'm sure you don't. But I do, and I should be getting the respect I command, but I'm not. Not from the announcers, the executives, the fans, the wrestlers...(Glaring menacingly)...and maybe not even my friends.
El Arco Iris: You are focusing way too much on what other people think of you! And thus your own self is decaying.
Copycat: (Incensed) Decaying??! (He backs Iris up against the wall) Copycat: You say you respect me. But maybe you just don't know me very well. Don't try to convince me this isn't worth it.I'm going to go out there tonight and I'm going to get the respect I deserve from Larry Tact. One way or another, I am going to get the RESPECT that I deserve...And YOU are not going to stand in my way.
(Copycat storms off, but El Arco Iris shouts after him.) El Arco Iris: El Arco Iris won't try to force you in any direction, but El Arco Iris WILL arn you. You are slipping down the wrong path!
(El Arco Iris pauses) El Arco Iris: WAIT! (Copycat stops walking. El Arco Iris runs up to him, and hugs him.) El Arco Iris: ...And good luck! (El Arco Iris runs off. Copycat glares after him, wide-eyed and obviously not happy about that.)
(BROADCAST BOOTH) VIC WATERS: Copycat sure has been in a mood lately. JONATHAN MARX: When you beat the current CSWA World Heavyweight Champion, shouldn’t you get some sort of title shot? Between Sean Edmunds as President and LOVE hating Copycat’s guts, they are conspiring to make his life a living hell and it is totally undeserved.
VIC WATERS: What do you mean? He was the one who instigated the feud with LOVE and he sure didn’t make things easy with him and Edmunds.
JONATHAN MARX: Copycat was merely trying to win a match with Anarky and found a way to outsmart him. Does that mean Copycat has to pay from now to eternity?
VIC WATERS: While Anarky’s short term memory isn’t that good, he’ll hold a grudge for a long, long time.
JONATHAN MARX: ::pushes his ear piece closer to his ear:: I’m being told that we have a pre-recorded interview with Larry Tact from earlier this evening. Let’s send this back to the production truck.
(PRODUCTION TRUCK) (Larry Tact sits on a steel chair in a room, wearing his ring attire. Black backdrops surround him, his expression one of disdain.) TACT: Tonight is the night Larry Tact resurges forward again. Copycat, you can't worry about anyone coming out to attack you. No grudges to take out. If you do, it's only going to be a mistake. And I won't allow myself to not take advantage of that. Tonight...I will let myself go just as I always have. I'll give my best, like I always expect to. At the same time, there will be a difference. I'm not wrestling only for a win, this time. I'm wrestling off five months of problems. I'm going to put these past five months in WFW behind me, and wrestle how i should. That means you shouldn't worry about anything more, Copycat, than what I'm going to bring. The man across the ring from you should be your top concern....otherwise, you'll have decided this match before the bell even rings. (BROADCAST BOOTH) VIC WATERS: Tact has a hell of a point. Copycat has seemed distracted recently. JONATHAN MARX: Both men have been distracted. This is a harder league than most because of the level of competition here. It took over a year for Manson to win the World Heavyweight Title while in other places in recent history he won it within a month. Hell, on the show before the Superbowl of Wrestling, Pulsar got a victory over Manson in a tag match.
VIC WATERS: That was in Wylde’s last win too. Wylde and his running buddy Pulsar against Manson and Rat Fink. God rest his soul.
JONATHAN MARX: Personally, I think he is in the other place. So, who do you think will take this match?
VIC WATERS: I have a feeling Tact is going to win this match. JONATHAN MARX: Didn’t you have a feeling that Tact was going to win his match at the Superbowl of Wrestling before he got eliminated first? Are you trying to jinx him?
VIC WATERS: Everyone is entitled to a bad night. El Arco Iris has been on a hot streak, in NEW where he earned a shot at the NEW World Heavyweight Title against Larry Tact at the Superbowl of Wrestling he beat Tact and Iron Lion in a three way match, and tonight he is in a three way for a shot at the WFW World Heavyweight Title… It took both El Arco Iris and Iron Lion in order to defeat Larry Tact and you just saw the fight Iron Lion gave Anarky and Wells all alone…
JONATHAN MARX: So you are going with Tact? VIC WATERS: I’m going with Tact to win the match and the right to face anyone he wants at the PPV. Who are you going with as if I didn’t know?
JONATHAN MARX: This may surprise you, but I’m not going with the technician, I’m going with Copycat.
VIC WATERS: Really? Why? JONATHAN MARX: Copycat beat Dan Ryan who has just been rolling through competition wherever he goes. I’ve had my ass kicked by Dan Ryan and nine times out of ten, I’ll go with the guy who kicked the guys ass of the guy who kicked my ass.
VIC WATERS: Didn’t Tact kick your ass too? JONATHAN MARX: He did, but I was able to walk out under my own power. He is a hell of a wrestler, but what most people don’t know about him is that he is also very physical as well, when I wrestled Rabesque, I felt okay a day or two after, but after my encounter with Tact, I was hurting for a week. He is a powerful man.
(CUEUP: "Brain Stew" by Green Day) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, currently residing in his summer residence in Escandido, California, three time WWL World Champion and current President of the WFW, and your special referee for Copycat verses Tact….. 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds!
:: Edmunds comes down to the ring in a stripped short sleeve referee shirt and bicycle shorts and enters the ring to the loud boos from the crowd, Mike McGee and Mighty Impala accompany him to ringside as his bodyguards::
JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds is the special referee, what the hell?! VIC WATERS: Of all the horror I’ve seen tonight, Edmunds in bicycle shorts takes the cake. JONATHAN MARX: Quiet you. I can’t believe that Edmunds would tarnish this match just to get at Copycat.
VIC WATERS: If you remember, Edmunds and Tact have a history too. Sherri blatantly cost him a match like he cost Pulsar a match here tonight. So by being biased against both men, he is really unbiased.
JONATHAN MARX: What kind of logic is that? Can’t two men wrestle for once? ::sigh:: (CUEUP: "Karma" by Diffuser) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, one of the best technicians in all of wrestling today, Manhattan, New York… LARRY TACT!
::Larry Tact comes down to a loud ovation from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: Copycat voice/over “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, LEND ME YOUR EARS!” followed by “My Way” by Butch Walker) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, the former WWL World Heavyweight Champion, from Kalamazoo, Michigan… COPYCAT!
::Copycat walks down to the ring to a mixed reaction with microphone in hand and enters the ring, Copycat throws the mic down to the boos of the crowd::
*bell rings* JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds is reading the riot act to both men…. COPYCAT TURNS HIS BACK TO HIM AND EDMUNDS GRABS HIS SHOULDER AND TELLS HIM TO TURN AROUND!
VIC WATERS: Uh oh! JONATHAN MARX: COPYCAT IS GETTING ALL UP INTO EDMUNDS FACE ABOUT GETTING INVOLVED IN HIS MATCHES!
VIC WATERS: Look, Tact is sneaking up from behind! JONATHAN MARX: TACT WAISTLOCKS COPYCAT AND HITS HIM WITH AND OVER THE HEAD BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX!
VIC WATERS: Copycat has to do his best to ignore Edmunds if he wants to win this match. JONATHAN MARX: Tact goes down to the mat and he locks on a step over armbar on the right arm of Copycat!
VIC WATERS: Tact is trying to take away one of the arms of Copycat in order to keep his power moves limited. Good strategy on his part.
JONATHAN MARX: Copycat is squirming under the pain and he is trying to use his available arm to reach towards the ropes but he is still a good two feet away.
VIC WATERS: Those two feet seem like a mile right now. JONATHAN MARX: Copycat is pulling himself towards the ropes… he is good foot away now…. WAIT A SECOND, TACT LETS GO OF THE HOLD AND HE DRAGS COPYCAT BACK INTO THE CENTER OF THE RING!
VIC WATERS: Positioning is so important in wrestling, it is often the difference between victory and give your opportunity a chance to comeback.
JONATHAN MARX: LARRY TACT ROLLS COPYCAT OVER ON HIS SIDE AND SCISSOR LOCKS THE RIGHT ARM WITH HIS LEGS AND GRABS THE LEFT ARM OF COPYCAT AND PULLS BACK!
VIC WATERS: RINGS OF SATURN! JONATHAN MARX: No Vic, we are in Albany, but Copycat wishes he was on Saturn right now instead of this hold. Edmunds is asking Copycat if he wants to give up but Copycat is saying no.
VIC WATERS: Tact is going to keep working him over and working him over until he has no choice but to give up.
JONATHAN MARX: You can see the perspiration coming down the face of Copycat as Tact is putting everything he has behind the move.
VIC WATERS: This is the Tact that should have showed up at the Superbowl of Wrestling. This guy would have won.
JONATHAN MARX: Tact is going back to what he use to do when he was successful and because of that, he is firing on all cylinders.
VIC WATERS: As they say, If it isn’t broke, don’t fix it. JONATHAN MARX: COPYCAT SHIFTS HIS WEIGHT RAPIDLY SO TACT IS ON HIS BACK! Edmunds goes down for the count! 1------------------------------------------------------------2----------------------- TACT BREAKS THE HOLD AND KICKS OUT!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds was a bit slow on that count. JONATHAN MARX: Tact is annoyed that Copycat got him over but looks please as he sees Copycat moaning in pain as he is trying to put his hands down to help himself up…. COPYCAT CAN’T SUPPORT HIMSELF AND HE COLLAPSES!
VIC WATERS: Tact looks like he has done some serious damage. JONATHAN MARX: Copycat turns over on his back and he inches his way towards the ropes and tries again to pull himself up…. TACT CHARGES OVER TO COPYCAT AND HE GRABS HIS RIGHT ARM AND HITS HIM WITH A SHORT ARM CLOTHESLINE JUST AS COPYCAT MANAGED TO GET UP!
VIC WATERS: He is trying to frustrated Copycat right now., JONATHAN MARX: Larry Tact is taunting Copycat to get up…. A ANGRY COPYCAT IS STRUGGLING BUT HE MANAGES TO GET BACK UP TO HIS FEET!
VIC WATERS: Copycat on his feet is BAD. JONATHAN MARX: TACT WAISTLOCKS COPYCAT AND HE IS GOING FOR AN OVER BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX…. COPYCAT THRUSTS HIS HEAD FORHEAD AND HITS TACT WITH A HEADBUTT!
VIC WATERS: Tact grabs his head… JONATHAN MARX: COPYCAT GRABS TACT’S FOREHEAD AGAIN AND REPEATED HITS COPYCAT WITH THRUST HEADBUTTS! Tact is busted open and he is wobbly back and forth!
VIC WATERS: Copycat is showing that just because your arms are impaired, that doesn’t mean it has to slow you down.
JONATHAN MARX: Copycat bounces off the ropes…. SHOULDER TACKLE RIGHT INTO TACT AND COPYCAT CRASHES ONTO TO TACT WITH ALL HIS WEIGHT!
VIC WATERS: All three hundred bounds down on the chest of Tact. JONATHAN MARX: Copycat moves himself on the mat onto the side of Tact AND DRIVES THE CALF RIGHT INTO FACE OF TACT REPEATEDLY!
VIC WATERS: Copycat is really wrestling smart here, he is avoiding using his arms until they have a chance to recover from how they were menaced from Tact.
JONATHAN MARX: TACT GRABS THE LEG OF COPYCAT AND HE GETS UP AND TURNS IT INTO AN ANKLE LOCK!
VIC WATERS: Nice counter by Tact JONATHAN MARX: Copycat is screaming his head off here, Tact could quite easily break the ankle of Copycat if he is not careful.
VIC WATERS: Ankles take a long time recover because they are very hard to rest unless you lay in bed.
JONATHAN MARX: Copycat is trying to crawl towards the ropes…. COPYCAT IS MAKING PROGRESS AND EDMUNDS IS RIGHT THERE TO SEE IT…. EDMUNDS WALKS AWAY TO TALK TO TACT AS COPYCAT GRABS THE ROPES! COME ON REF! That isn’t very unbiased of him.
VIC WATERS: It could be that Edmunds just don’t want Copycat to abuse the rope break. JONATHAN MARX: Rope breaks my ass. WAIT A SECOND, COPYCAT IS USING HANDS TO PULL HIMSELF UP!
VIC WATERS: It looks like Copycat has recovered from what Tact did to him earlier…. JONATHAN MARX: COPYCAT IS HOPPING ON ONE FOOT WHILE TACT HAS THE OTHER IN AN ANKLE LOCK! VIC WATERS: Incredible display of strength by Copycat! JONATHAN MARX: Copycat hops away from the ropes… COPYCAT USES THE ANKLE LOCK TO PULL TACT CLOSER…. TACT LETS GO OF THE HOLD AND TACT STUMBLES BACK INTO COPYCAT WHO PROMPTLY HITS HIM WITH A RUDE AWAKENING!
VIC WATERS: OLD SCHOOL! JONATHAN MARX: An angry Copycat looks over at Edmunds who is giving him a “who me?” look…. COPYCAT SIGNALS FOR THE CAT’S MEOW!
VIC WATERS: After that reverse neckbreaker or Rude Awakening, if he hits this, he might be able to win this match.
JONATHAN MARX: COPYCAT BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES…. HE DROPS THE CAT’S MEOW (PEOPLE’S ELBOW) RIGHT ON THE HEAD OF TACT!
VIC WATERS: That elbow landed flush on the jaw of Tact. JONATHAN MARX: Copycat is going for the cover! Edmunds reaches into his shorts and pulls out a banana and begins peeling it. THIS ISN’T THE TIME DAMNIT!
VIC WATERS: I always knew Edmunds was a banana smuggler, Copycat is yelling at Edmunds to get his ass down and make the count.
JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds throws the peel down and goes down to make the count! 1---------------------------------------------------2------------------------------ TACT KICKS OUT JUST AT THE THREE!
VIC WATERS: See, that count was much better than the last one. JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds had a banana in his hand! Of course it was slower… COPYCAT IS UP AND HE IS FURIOUS AT EDMUNDS FOR DISRESPECTING HIM AND TACT!
VIC WATERS: This is just what Copycat has been saying over and over again. He gets repeatedly disrespected.
JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds throws the banana at Copycat who ducks and lands on the outside… COPYCAT PUSHES EDMUNDS AND EDMUNDS STUMBLES BACK AND SLIPS ON THE BANANA PEEL AND HITS HIS HEAD!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds looks like he is really out of it, his head landed hard on that mat. JONATHAN MARX: Tact is starting to crawl over towards Copycat and Copycat comes over and picks Tact up off the mat… COPYCAT WAIST LOCKS THE BLOODY TACT AND TACT STARTS FIRING ELBOWS BACK INTO THE FACE OF COPYCAT!
VIC WATERS: Copycat throws some wicked suplex. JONATHAN MARX: TACT FINALLY HITS AND BREAKS FREE OF COPYCAT’S GRASP! VIC WATERS: Tact is showing everyone why he is a fighter. JONATHAN MARX: Copycat charges Tact…. TACT CATCHES COPYCAT AND SLAMS HIM DOWN TO THE MAT WITH A PUMP HANDLE SLAM!
VIC WATERS: Shades of Wink tonight who is in the building. JONATHAN MARX: Tact looks over at Edmunds who is still out and decides against the cover…. TACT ROLLS OVER COPYCAT AND SITS ON HIS BACK AND LOCKS ON A DOUBLE CHICKEN WING! Tact is going back to work on the arms he worked over earlier in the match.
VIC WATERS: You would have to think that if Edmunds gets up and Copycat utters anything close to “I give up” that he’ll give this match to Tact.
JONATHAN MARX: Who knows when that will be since we are on Edmunds time. COPYCAT IS BELLOWING IN AGONY! Tact may have him….. COPYCAT HAS PASSED OUT COLD!
VIC WATERS: I told you Tact could do! JONATHAN MARX: Tact lets go of the hold and he goes to wake up Edmunds….. wait a minute…. COPYCAT JUST OPENED HIS EYES… HE WAS PLAYING POSSUM!
VIC WATERS: Turn around Tact! TURN AROUND! JONATHAN MARX: Tact turns his head to check on Copycat but Copycat closes his eyes and remains still. TACT GOES BACK TO WAKING UP EDMUNDS AND HE SLAMS HIM ON THE FACE!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is looking like he is starting to come to! JONATHAN MARX: COPYCAT IS GETTING UP AND HE SNEAKS UP BEHIND TACT FOR A RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX! Edmunds is beginning to wake up and he looks over at Copycat and Tact.
VIC WATERS: Tact is dead in the water… JONATHAN MARX: TACT IS ABLE TO BREAK FREE FROM COPYCAT’S WAISTLOCK AND PUTS A WAISTLOCK ON COPYCAT OF HIS OWN!
VIC WATERS: The reason that Tact was able to break free is that he spent all that time working over the arms of Copycat. I love it when a plan comes together.
JONATHAN MARX: Copycat is struggling to break out of Tact’s clutches in order to avoid the German suplex… EDMUNDS IS GETTING UP AND HE IS YELLING AT COPYCAT FOR PUSHING HIM DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Give him hell Edmunds! JONATHAN MARX: Copycat curses at Edmunds! EDMUNDS SUPERKICKS COPYCAT…. NO! COPYCAT DUCKED AND EDMUNDS HIT TACT! Now Edmunds is cursing himself!
VIC WATERS: NO! NOT AGAIN! JONATHAN MARX: COPYCAT CATCHES A STUNNED TACT BEFORE HE FALLS DOWN AND LOCKS HIM IN THE CAT’S CLAW!
VIC WATERS: Come on Tact! You can get out of this! JONATHAN MARX: Tact’s face is a crimson mask and he looks over at Edmunds…. TACT SPITS IN THE FACE OF EDMUNDS…. TACT IS TRYING TO FIGHT BUT COPYCAT HAS IT ON TIGHT! IT IS OVER! TACT IS OUT ON HIS FEET AND EDMUNDS IS FORCED TO CALL FOR THE BELL!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of this match…. COPYCAT! JONATHAN MARX: Copycat lets go of the hold AND HE GOES AFTER EDMUNDS WHO BAILS OUT OF THE RING LIKE A BAT OUT OF HELL AND MCGEE AND IMPALA RUSH TO THE SIDE OF EDMUNDS!
VIC WATERS: That may not be enough… JONATHAN MARX: Copycat is running after Edmunds…. EDMUNDS PUSHES MIGHTY IMPALA INTO THE WAY OF COPYCAT AND COPYCAT LOCKS IN THE CAT’S CLAW! SECURITY IS RUSHING DOWN TO GET BETWEEN BOTH MEN AS COPYCAT IS RAVAGING MIGHTY IMPALA IN FROM OF EDMUNDS TELLING HIM THAT HE WILL BE NEXT!
VIC WATERS: Copycat has fully gone off the deep end. JONATHAN MARX: Folks, we have to take a commercial break, but when we get back, IT IS TIME FOR THE THREE WAY DANCE TO DETERMINE WHO IS GOING TO THE PPV TO FACE THE CHAMP! Stay tuned!
