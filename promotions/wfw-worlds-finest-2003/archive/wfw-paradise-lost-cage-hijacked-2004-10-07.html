WFW Paradise Lost: Cage Hijacked

7 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 7 October 2004 [Outside of Fenway Park] CHRIS WINK: WHAT DO YOU MEAN THE TRUCK WITH THE CAGE WAS HIJACKED? TRUCKER: I was waiting at a red light when these three or four big masked guys walked right up to the trucked and tapped on my window with a baseball bat. They told me if I got out they wouldn’t hurt me.
CHRIS WINK: That is great, that is just great…. There is a ballpark for of wrestling fans waiting for a cage match and there is no cage. Did they look like wrestlers?
TRUCKER: Oh yeah, but I couldn’t recognize any of them with their ski masks on. CHRIS WINK: Those bastards… I bet it was Edmunds or LOVE. They would like nothing better than for Copycat to not get his title shot at the PPV.
TRUCKER: Wait, wasn’t the cage match just announced tonight? How could LOVE know about it? CHRIS WINK: Someone must have leaked the format sheet or accidentally left it around and it fell into the wrong hands. What am I suppose to do now?
TRUCKER: Beats me, I am only a trucker. CHRIS WINK: GET THE HELL OUT OF HERE! ::Wink scares the trucker and the trucker flees for his life:: CHRIS WINK: What the hell am I going to do now? ::Black Rose walks up to Wink smoking a cigarette:: BLACK ROSE: Boss, I have an idea. ::Wink leans in to hear what Black Rose has to say:: CHRIS WINK: Why not…. They are ALL in the back, lets do it. [BACKSTAGE] JONATHAN MARX: What did Black Rose say to Wink? BRANDON JACOBS: I don’t know, but we will be back with the main event, RIGHT AFTER THIS!
