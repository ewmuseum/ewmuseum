WFW Great Expectations: Manson vs. Mason

19 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 19 February 2004 VIC WATERS: This is the one match I’ve been dreading since the day Terrell Mason joined the WFW.
BRANDON JACOBS: How come? VIC WATERS: Manson, Mason, it is like a bad Abbott and Costello routine. Do you know how hard it is to keep those two names straight?
BRANDON JACOBS: One letter separates one of the alltime greats and a rapping fool. VIC WATERS: Terrell Mason is street tough and is one of the main reasons Manson along with Scotty Michaels that he isn’t WFW World Heavyweight Champion today.
BRANDON JACOBS: Don’t think that Manson doesn’t remember that and tonight he is looking for revenge.
VIC WATERS: Terrell Mason also has something to prove too. After an explosive debut, he has been fighting his way to move up on the card and make an impact and there is no greater way to make an impact than to put on a show that everyone will remember live on PPV against Manson.
BRANDON JACOBS: That is very true, when are you a top dog, guys are always trying to make a name off of you. You never have a night off.
VIC WATERS: Sort of like the New York Yankees, teams live off beating the best of the best.
BRANDON JACOBS: This is going to be an awfully interesting match, let’s send this one down to ringside and get this one underway!
(CUEUP: "Mechanical Animals" by Marilyn Manson) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the former WFW North American Champion, the man, the myth, the legend…. MICHAEL MANSON!
::Manson is walking down slowly to the ring:: BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES TERRELL MASON AND HE WALLOPS MANSON WITH A CHAIR RIGHT TO THE BACK AND MANSON FALLS DOWN TO HIS KNEES!
VIC WATERS: Mason is attacking before the bell in order to get the advantage, he is showing his street smarts here.
BRANDON JACOBS: Terrell Mason grabs Manson by the hair and HE THROWS MANSON RIGHT INTO THE STEEL STEPS BACK FIRST!
VIC WATERS: Mason looks like he came in with a plan, he is going after that back injury which has plagued Manson for large stretches of his career.
BRANDON JACOBS: Terrell Mason picks up Manson…. HE SLAMS HIM RIGHT ONTO THE STEEL STEPS! VIC WATERS: It looks like Mason is setting Manson up for something. BRANDON JACOBS: MASON IS PULLING OUT A LADDER AND THIS ANAHEIM CROWD IS ON ITS FEET! Mason is setting up the ladder right outside of the guardrail…. He couldn’t be planning what I think he is planning!
VIC WATERS: Mason is going completely balls out here, it is killed or be killed on this. BRANDON JACOBS: Mason is climbing the ladder and a dazed Manson is lying on the steel stairs… MASON IS PERCHED ON THE TOP!
VIC WATERS: He is going to kill himself! BRANDON JACOBS: MASON FLIES OFF FOR THE 5 STAR FROG SPLASH BUT MANSON MOVES OUT OF THE WAY LAST SECOND! Thank god, or he would have been dead but Mason landed on his right shoulder!
VIC WATERS: Someone better get the paramedics out here! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson grabs Terrell Mason who is screaming in pain and throws him into the ring.
*bell rings* VIC WATERS: This match is official now, but for how long, who knows. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson rolls in and Mason is grabbing the injured shoulder. Mason is pleading with Manson to show mercy on him and his hyperextended shoulder.
CROWD: KICK HIS ASS! KICK HIS ASS! KICK HIS ASS! VIC WATERS: This crowd here in Anaheim is showing absolutely no mercy for Terrell Mason. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson goes for the pinfall to the boos of the crowd! 1----------------------------------------------2--------------------MANSON PULLS MASON UP TO A LOUD POP!
VIC WATERS: NO DAMNIT! BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON LOCKS ON THE TOURNIQUET! MASON IS SCREAMING IN PAIN! HIS SHOULDER IS JUST IN TOO MUCH PAIN! MASON TAPS!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. MICHAEL MANSON! BRANDON JACOBS: The paramedics are coming out. Terrell Mason is going to need some work on that shoulder. It may be awhile before we see him again.
VIC WATERS: Mason was thug born, thug life bred, but now he is thug life dead. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, when we comeback, WFW President Alex Wylde and the next man to get a shot at the WFW World Heavyweight Title after Great Expectation will face off against former BAD World Heavyweight Champion Rat Fink! STAY TUNED!
