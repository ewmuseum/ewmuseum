WFW Ghoulish Games - Wylde vs. Eddie

17 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 17 November 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back and after that bloody dog collar match, we move onto the WFW debut of Good Eddie who will face none other than the WFW President Alex Wylde.
VIC WATERS: We employ Texans? What, do we have meet quotas or something? BRANDON JACOBS: Good Eddie isn’t a Texan, he is from New Mexico. VIC WATERS: I was in the locker room earlier, he smelled like a Texan to me. BRANDON JACOBS: Those are just bad archaic stereotypes about people from Texas. Most people from Texas are nothing like that. They are fine, outstanding people who actually do bathe on a regular basis.
VIC WATERS: You forgot intelligent. BRANDON JACOBS: I did? Let’s send this match to ringside and get this one underway. (CUEUP: “"Cat Scratch Fever" by Ted Nugent) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the man making his WFW debut here tonight, from Albuquerque, New Mexico…. GOOD EDDIE!
::Good Eddie slowly walks down in his dark brown trenchcoat like Eastwood, and a red scarf tied around his neck::
(CUEUP: "Diesel Power" by Prodigy) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, the current WFW President and the captain of Team Wylde at Almost Live, ALEX WYLDE!
::Alex Wylde walks down to a loud pop and enters the ring:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde and Good Eddie lock up… Good Eddie powers Wylde off the ropes… LOU THESZ PRESS! Good Eddie starts firing away with right hands right into the kisser!
VIC WATERS: Good Eddie is really laying into Wylde with the punches. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde reaches out with his arm and gets his hand under the rope and the ref is ordering Good Eddie to get off. Good Eddie curses off the ref and STARTS STOMPING AWAY ON WYLDE TO KEEP HIM FROM GETTING UP! Wylde rolls out of the ring and his hands against the ring apron in frustration.
VIC WATERS: This newcomer has started off really well against the President… if he wants to beat Wylde, he needs to keep it up.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde tries to enter the ring…. GOOD EDDIE WHIPS HIS BODY OFF THE ROPES AND KNOCKS WYDLE OFF THE APRON! Wylde stumbles but he lands on his feet and he yells at the ref to move Good Eddie back so he can enter the ring.
VIC WATERS: Eddie is playing mind games with a master of mind games. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is backing Good Eddie away and Wylde reenters the ring. Wylde charges Good Eddie but Good Eddie side steps him and puts him in a hammerlock! Wylde tries to swing with his loose arm for Eddie’s head but he moves out of the way before he can connect.
VIC WATERS: I don’t remember the last time I saw a hammerlock. BRANDON JACOBS: Good Eddie pushes Wylde towards the turnbuckle… Good Eddie release the hammerlock and he rams his shoulder repeatedly into the lower back of Wylde! Wylde grabs his lower back… Good Eddie picks up Wylde and nails him with a backbreaker!
VIC WATERS: Eddie is trying to take Wylde apart systematically. By taking out Wylde’s back, it is going to make it awfully hard for him to kick out later on in the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Good Eddie holds his knee into the back of Wylde and uses his strength to press down on Wylde almost bending him in two.
VIC WATERS: He is just trying to inflict some extra pain, the knee is one of the hardest parts of the body and when that is rammed into your back, it does damage.
BRANDON JACOBS: Good Eddie breaks the hold, he hooks the leg and makes the cover! The ref goes down for the count! 1---------------------------2--------KICKOUT! Wylde still has too much energy to try for the pinfall.
VIC WATERS: My only thought is he is trying to tire him out so he doesn’t have as much force behind the kickouts.
BRANDON JACOBS: Good Eddie whips Wylde off the ropes…. HE CATCHES WYLDE IN A SPINEBUSTER BUT BUMPS THE REF IN THE PROCESS!
VIC WATERS: That is a bad break for Eddie. BRANDON JACOBS: Good Eddie goes to wake up the ref… he really took a nasty bump on the head. He slaps him around a little to try to wake him up…. WAIT, WYLDE IS GETTING UP AND HE IS SETTING FOR THE SPINNING HEEL KICK. Eddie has the ref coming to… Good Eddie turns around… DOWN GOES EDDIE!
VIC WATERS: HE CLOCKED HIM WITH THAT! BRANDON JACOBS: But wait, Eddie is trying to get up… WYLDE STARTS LAYING INTO HIM WITH CHOPS! Wylde grabs the arm of Eddie and whips him off the ropes…. HE CATCHES HIM AND NAILS HIM WITH THE STUN GUN ACROSS THE THROAT!
VIC WATERS: Wylde just dropped Eddie’s throat right on the top rope! BRANDON JACOBS: Eddie is gasping for air! He having trouble catching his breath…. WYLDE LOCKS HIM IN A SLEEPER!
VIC WATERS: As if the shot to the throat wasn’t bad enough, this is really going to put his lights out.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde is synching it in tight… the ref checking to see that it is a legal sleeper and not a choke. EDDIE IS STILL FIGHTING IT BUT HE IS GOING OUT FAST! The ref is checking on the arm…. IT FALLS ONCE! The ref is checking on the arm a second time… EDDIE IS ABLE TO KEEP IT UP SLIGHTLY!
VIC WATERS: Wylde almost has him. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde decides to let go! Why in heavens name did he do that? VIC WATERS: Wylde is a smart man, he must have something else in mind to finish off Good Eddie once and for all.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde is mocking Good Eddie… HE IS CALLING FOR GOOD EDDIE’S FINISHER THE LAST PRAYER PEDIGREE! Wylde picks up Eddie off the mat and double arms Eddie…. EDDIE BACK BODY DROPS WYLDE! Wylde grabs his already injured back!
VIC WATERS: It is never a good idea to steal someone’s finisher. It always backfires. BRANDON JACOBS: Eddie picks Wylde off the mat and hooks the arm…. BACKSLIDE DOWN THE MAT! 1------------------------------------2----------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Get him Eddie! BRANDON JACOBS: Eddie chicken wings both arms and starts HEADBUTTING WYLDE IN THE BACK! Wylde is screaming in pain…. Eddie is having his way with him.
VIC WATERS: Eddie has shown that he is very proficient in that ring. BRANDON JACOBS: GOOD EDDIE IS CALLING FOR HIS FINISHER THE LAST PRAYER PEDIGREE! VIC WATERS: If he hits this, it will be all over! BRANDON JACOBS: Eddie picks Wylde off the mat… WYLDE STARTS FIRING AT GOOD EDDIE WITH EUROPEAN UPPERCUTS! Wylde knows his back is against the wall and this is one last burst of energy in desperation!
VIC WATERS: Good Eddie needs to finish this quickly. BRANDON JACOBS: GOOD EDDIE IS BEING BACKED UP… WYLDE JUMPS UP AND NAILS HIM WITH A STANDING DROPKICK! Good Eddie goes down and Wylde grabs his back in pain.
VIC WATERS: That dropkick isn’t the best thing for your back, but he has to leave it all out there if he wants to win against this impressive newcomer.
BRANDON JACOBS: Good Eddie charges Wylde…. WYLDE DUCKS THE LARIAT! Wylde kicks Good Eddie in the chest… ROCKER DROPPER! Eddie goes down!
VIC WATERS: Wylde has somehow been able to comeback from near death. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde picks Good Eddie up off the mat…. IT LOOKS LIKE HE IS GOING FOR THE WYLDE BUSTER! Good Eddie is trying to fight Wylde from lifting him up…. WYLDE’S BACK BACK IS GIVING HIM PROBLEMS!
VIC WATERS: All that work on the back has paid off for Good Eddie! BRANDON JACOBS: GOOD EDDIE IS FIRING AWAY WITH RIGHTS! Wylde angry and pain pushes him away and Good Eddie goes chest first into the ropes…. WYLE SEES THE OPPORTUNITY AND NAILS HIM WITH THE GATECRASHER SUPERKICKS HIM TO THE HEAD! Wylde goes down for the cover and hooks Good Eddie’s trunks! 1-----------------------------------2----------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. ALEX WYLDE! BRANDON JACOBS: Now that was a comeback, Good Eddie took Wylde to his limit and Wylde stepped up big time like Reggie Miller playing in the fourth quarter at MSG to escape with the win.
VIC WATERS: Good Eddie is a hell of tough, technically sound wrestler. With a bit more experience, he could be a WFW World Heavyweight Champion someday.
BRANDON JACOBS: He really impressed me too. Okay folks, it is time for the match which has been grounded in a tradition which has last over ten years, up next… live from a local cementary…. ANARKY WILL TAKE ON DIABLO IN A FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE IN THE GRAVEYARD! Stay tuned folks!
