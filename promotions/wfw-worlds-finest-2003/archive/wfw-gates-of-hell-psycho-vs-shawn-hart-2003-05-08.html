WFW: Gates of Hell: Psycho vs. Shawn Hart

8 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 8 May 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back from commercial break and boy do we have a match, Shawn Hart will square off with Psycho to determine whom will be the number one contender to the World Heavyweight Title!
VIC WATERS: This is one of those matches I can just sit back and watch without having to take a rooting interest for either one. It should be a hell of a match, these two men want so much to be the top man in this company and wear the ten pounds of gold.
BRANDON JACOBS: Both men have great work ethics, they have a never say die attitude which you need to be a top guy in wrestling these days. Either of these guys could hang with the greats of the old days, they are just that good. Lets stop the stalling, the fans are getting antsy, so let’s send this one to the ring!
:: "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne blares over the loud speaker:: RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from parts Unknown, “THE AMERICAN DANGERMAN” PSYCHO! :: Psycho walks down to the ring to pop from the crowd and enters the ring:: :: I Touch Myself" by the Divinyls plays over the loud speakers:: RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Orlando, Florida! The man who proclaims that he is the real World Heavyweight Champion of the World, “The Phenom” Shawn Hart!
::Shawn Hart walks down to the ring without his bodyguard Tiny:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Both Psycho and Hart come out of there corner and they are trading blows back and forth! Psycho is getting the advantage!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is world class brawler, it isn’t a good idea to get into a fight with him and let him dictate the tone of the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho whips Hart off the ropes… SIDEWALK SLAM! Hart grabs his back! Psycho picks up Hart…. URANGE! Hart crashed down hard!
VIC WATERS: That mat doesn’t have all that much give, two take two shots like that could cripple Hart’s chance of winning.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho turns over Shawn Hart and locks on the camel clutch! Hart is pounding on the mat in pain as Psycho uses his body to put the pressure on the lower back of Hart.
VIC WATERS: This is a very smart move, by trying to take on Hart’s back, that limits how much movement he can have later on in the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second, it looks like Psycho is hooking in the camel clutch a little lower than the head and is going for Hart’s throat!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is going for the kill early! I’m starting to like this boy. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref sees it and starts to count… 1-----2----3----4—PSYCHO LETS GO OF THE HOLD! HE LOCKS IT ON AGAIN! AGAIN HIS ARMS ARE AROUND THE THROAT OF HART 1---2---3---REF CLIMBS ON TOP OF PSYCHO AND TRIES TO GET HIM TO BREAK! He does but the ref falls back onto his head!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is just showing how badly he wants to win. That shows you how much that title around Doc Silver’s waist means to everyone in this company.
BRANDON JACOBS: SHAWN HART NAILS PSYCHO WITH A LOW BLOW WHEN THE REF WAS LOOKING! VIC WATERS: Win, lose, but always, cheat! BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho has been brought down to his knees and he is pain! Hart is slowly getting up and he nails Psycho with a knee to the head to bring him down to the mat! Hart picks Psycho off the ground and pushes him down into the corner of the ring!
VIC WATERS: Payback time! BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart takes the boot and rams the bottom of it into the face of Psycho repeatedly! OUCH! IT LOOKS LIKE THE TIP OF THE BOOT MAY HAVE CAUGHT PSYCHO IN THE EYE!
VIC WATERS: Hart is hitting each and every one of the weaknesses of every great fight right now, he reminds me a lot of Doc in that he is a such a smart worker.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart whips Psycho off the ropes… SLEEPERHOLD! He has it locked in tight in the center of the ring!
VIC WATERS: Psycho better do something fast or he is a dead duck. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is thrashing his arms around trying to break out! Hart won’t get go! This capacity crowd is on its feet for Psycho!
VIC WATERS: Psycho may finally have his chance to face Doc for the title, while he’ll still lose, it is a great honor none the less.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is reaching for the ropes but I don’t know how much longer he is going to be able to keep conscious! Hart has had this on for almost five minutes now and Psycho is in a battle against himself to see how long he can persevere! Psycho is stretching his arms and he is using all his strength to reach the ropes….HE LUNGES HIMSELF FORWARD…. JUST SHORT!
VIC WATERS: Very close, but no cigar BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho tries again…. HE HAS THE ROPE AND THE REF MAKES HART BREAK THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: Now the question is how much damage has been to Psycho…. BRANDON JACOBS: SHAWN HART GOES FOR THE SUPERKICK…. PSYCHO DUCKS! Psycho nails him with the Yazuka kick dead center in the face! Hart goes down and Pyscho is going to the top!
VIC WATERS: These two competitors are holding nothing back, this has been nothing short of a war.
BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO NAILS THE MOONSAULT! He covers! 1----------2-----------KICKOUT! VIC WATERS: It looks like the moonsault took away energy from both competitors had left because both men are slow getting up.
BRANDON JACOBS: Both men get to their feet and Psycho starts chopping away to Hart with some hard knife edges! HART FIRES BACK WITH SOME PUNCHES OF HIS OWN! Psycho staggers backwards… PSYCHO RETALIATES WITH CHOP TO THE FACE OF HART! Hart goes down!
VIC WATERS: Hart is showing a lot of courage in this match for being on the defensive as much as he has been so far.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho picks Hart up over his shoulder for a fireman’s carry slam…HART TRIES TO ESCAPE THE HOLD BY ESCAPING TO THE MAT! Psycho locks Hart in tombstone piledriver position… HE NAILS IT! He steals a move from Hart’s playbook! Hart is dazed but he out of instinct he rolls out of the ring and to the floor in order to recover.
VIC WATERS: There are no timeouts in wrestling, but this is as close as you can come. Hart should take full advantage of the 20 count to get some rest and comeback into the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: A dazed Hart starts to get up…. PSYCHO NAILS HIM WITH THE SPACE TIGER DROP! Psycho takes his boot and he rubs it in the face of Hart for what he did earlier! HART GRABS THE FOOT… DRAGON SCREW! Psycho grabs his knee!
VIC WATERS: Sometimes it is better to be the better man and not bring yourself down to your opponent’s level.
BRANDON JACOBS: Those are big words coming from a Doc Silver fan. Wait a second! Hart is grabbing the injured knee of Psycho and he RAMS IT INTO THE STEEL STEPS! Psycho’s knee is bleeding!
VIC WATERS: It looks like he scrapped his knee on the metal on the steal stairs… BRANDON JACOBS: Hart chicken wings Psycho’s leg… he drops him onto the guardrail knee cap first!
VIC WATERS: Very, very vicious… BRANDON JACOBS: Hart rolls Psycho into the ring after getting the better of him on the outside!
VIC WATERS: Hart is beating Psycho at his own game, Hart can’t delay any longer, he needs to end this quickly.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart pounds away on Psycho with discus punch after discus punch! Psycho is staggering!
RING ANNOUNCER: Five minutes remaining the match, five minutes remaining… BRANDON JACOBS: HART GOES FOR A CLOTHESLINE BUT PSYCHO DUCKS! PSYCHO RETALIATES WITH ONE OF HIS OWN…HART PUTS HIS HEAD DOWN… PSYCHO KNEES HIM IN THE FACE!! Hart has been busted open! Psycho hooks the arms AND NAILS HART WITH A TIGER DRIVER!
VIC WATERS: He should go for the cover right here! BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho moves Hart more towards the corner and calls for his finisher… A limping Psycho goes up to the top for the Psycho Flight… PSYCHO TAKES OFF BUT HART ROLLS OUT OF THE WAY! Hart covers Psycho! 1----------2-------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: That is why they call it a high risk maneuver. BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart picks Psycho off the mat and nails an INVERTED ATOMIC DROP! Psycho is in a great deal of pain.
VIC WATERS: Psycho is in so much pain it looks like his eyes went cross eyed. RING ANNOUNCER: Two minutes remaining in the match, two minutes! BRANDON JACOBS: SHAWN HART GOES FOR THE EL CODO EXPLOSIVO… PSYCHO NAILS WITH A YAZUKA KICK OUT OF NO WHERE! Hart is stunned! Psycho bodyslams him down to the mat!
VIC WATERS: Looks like Psycho is going for it all! He better hurry because time is running out!
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho goes up to the top…. PSYCHO FLIGHT STARDUST PRESS! Psycho hit it cleanly on Hart! Ref goes down to make the count! 1-------2--
*ring bell rings signifying the match has ended* BRANDON JACOBS: Did Psycho win?! What happened?! VIC WATERS: The ref and time keeper are talking, and now they are talking to the ring announcer.
RING ANNOUNCER: The thirty minute time limit has expired and this match has been ruled a DRAW!
BRANDON JACOBS: So, who faces the winner of Doc Silver vs. Gideon at the PPV?! ::Chris Wink is coming down to ringside with a message in his hand:: VIC WATERS: Looks like we are about to find out… RING ANNOUNCER: Since neither man technically lost the match, the WFW Board of the Directors have ruled that both Shawn Hart & Psycho are equally qualified to compete for the WFW World Heavyweight Title so they have decided that BOTH MEN WILL HAVE THE OPPORTUNITY TO COMPETE FOR THE TITLE AT CHERRY BLOSSOM CHAOS!
*crowd pops for the decision* BRANDON JACOBS: I guess under the circumstances that was the only fair thing they could have done.
VIC WATERS: Fair to who? It is certainly not fair to Doc. Why does he have to face two opponents? He should have just been given the PPV off or a token title defense against Aitsu Sugawara.
BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver’s title reign is on the clock, if Gideon doesn’t get him tonight, either Hart or Psycho will.
VIC WATERS: Doc is the Mike Tice of World Champions, he has as much time as he wants. Eventually though, he’ll have to vacate it and retire undefeated.
BRANDON JACOBS: When we come back from commercial break, we will have the first match to determine who get the North American Title Number #1 contender match between Johnny Starr vs. Chris Bradford.
VIC WATERS: Should be a good one… BRANDON JACOBS: We will be back!
