WFW — Card

Cherry Blossom Chaos - Manson vs. Michaels vs. Rat Fink

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 2 June 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Jun-02-03 AT 08:20&nbsp;AM (EST)]BRANDON JACOBS: Before we start our next match folks, we have received word that earlier tonight Manson was brutally attacked by a masked man with a lead pipe on the power part of his left leg. Manson has tried to keep it quiet because he didn’t want to have any excuses if he lost the match tonight but he needed to have his lower leg wrapped by our trainers so we heard the news through the grapevine. Manson refuses to comment how bad the injury is, but Manson is a man who never asks for medical attention for anything, so Manson may be worse than he is letting on.
VIC WATERS: Manson is the kind of wrestler who if you chopped off his leg, he’d ask for a piece of scotch tape so he could go out their and wrestle his match later than night. The only reason this injury is a factor tonight is because Manson has to climb the ladder in order to reclaim his North American Title belt from Rat Fink.
BRANDON JACOBS: Who do you think did it? I’m betting that this is another dastardly deed by Rat Fink to keep Manson from getting his title back.
VIC WATERS: I’m not so sure. It seems like something Rat Fink would do, but it just seems way too obvious.
BRANDON JACOBS: I’m all pumped up for this one, even with Manson’s injured leg, this should be a hell of a fight.
VIC WATERS: I really think tonight is Scotty Michaels’ special night. With the amount of hatred between Manson & Rat Fink, I don’t know how Manson is going to be able to focus on his goal and Scotty Michaels has been off the charts great lately. We will see.
::Livin’ on a Prayer” by Bon Jovi rocks over the loud speaker:: RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, the first member of the opposing team, from Philadelphia, PA…. THE SUPERSTAR SCOTTY MICHAELS!
::Scotty Michaels walks down to ringside to a mixed reaction from the crowd:: ::Megalomaniac by KMFDM blares over the speakers:: RING ANNOUNCER: And his partner, from WCW Special Forces, one of the greatest super villains of our time, RAT FINK!!
::Rat Fink walks down to the ring with his cheesy sci fi looking robot manager:: ::”Mechanical Animals" by Marilyn Manson blares on the loud speakers:: RING ANNOUCER: And finally, from Chicago, IL! Former BAD World Heavyweight Champion, the man, the myth, the legend... MICHAEL MANSON!
::big pop from the crowd as Manson limps out and makes his way down to the ring:: *Bell Rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Now because we are outdoors, the belt is hanging from a pole that extends from a corner of the ring out into the center. It is a good twelve feet between that title and the mat so if you fall from the ladder, it is definitely going to hurt.
VIC WATERS: I’ve never fallen off a ladder, but that can’t be good for your back. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels and Rat Fink are looking up at the title while Manson is smiling at Rat Fink. You can sense that tonight will be the night when he finally gets his hands on him and this crowd is aching to see it.
VIC WATERS: But will Rat Fink let him, that is the question… BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON CHARGES RAT FINK BUT RAT FINK PUSHES SCOTTY MICHAELS IN THE WAY! VIC WATERS: Manson moves pretty well for a gimp! BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels was lucky enough to clock the charging Manson with an forearm to the face which has Manson stunned! RAT FINK PUSHES SCOTTY MICHAELS OUT OF THE WAY AND HE NAILS MANSON WITH A STEINERLINE WHICH SENDS MANSON DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink is playing it smart, he using the third man in order to run interference for him in order to achieve his goal of screwing over Manson by using Michaels as a human shield.
BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK MOUNTS MANSON AND POUNDS ON HIS FACE WITH CLOSED PUNCHES! Wait a second, Scotty Michaels has pulled Rat Fink off! Rat Fink is in Scotty Michaels’ face asking him why he did that when he had Manson where he wanted him! MICHAELS PUSHES RAT FINK DOWN! Rat Fink gets back up and get into a heated argument with Michaels.
VIC WATERS: These two need to settle their differences and work together for the great cause, ending Manson’s title reign.
BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK SPITS IN THE FACE OF MICHAELS! Manson is back up and neither man notices… MANSON GRABS RAT FINK AND SCOTTY MICHAELS HEADS AND RAMS THEM TOGETHER! These fans are going nuts! MANSON THROWS SCOTTY MICHAELS OVER THE TOP! IT PAYBACK TIME!
VIC WATERS: For the love of god no…. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson puts the stunned Rat Fink in a front face lock…. DDT! MANSON ROLLS RAT FINK OVER AND STARTS PUNCHING AWAY AT HIM RIGHT IN THE FACE! The ref is trying to get Manson off Rat Fink because he is using a closed fist! Manson gets up and turns around to get into the referee’s face…. LOW BLOW BY RAT FINK FROM BEHIND! REF DIDN’T SEE IT!
VIC WATERS: Manson needs to keep his eye on Rat Fink every second or he isn’t going to walk out of this match as champion.
BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK CLIPS MANSON’S INJURED LEFT LEG AND MANSON IS SCREAMING! Rat Fink pulls Manson towards the center of the ring… HE IS GOING FOR A FIGURE FOUR! MANSON KICKS RAT FINK AWAY WITH HIS GOOD LEG! A frustrated Rat Fink grabs the leg again and LOCKS ON THE FIGURE FOUR!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink needs to squeeze on the vice so by the time he breaks the hold, it does permanent damage to Manson.
BRANDON JACOBS: Look at Rat Fink! He is looking at Manson with an evil smirk on his face, he is delighting in the fact that he is seeing Manson in pain! What an evil bastard.
VIC WATERS: I love Rat Fink, I so love Rat Fink. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is trying to move his body closer to the ropes… HE REACHES FOR THE ROPES… HE IS A BIT SHORT!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink breaks the hold and PULL MANSON TOWARD THE CENTER OF THE RING! HE DROPS THE KNEE ON THE BAD LEG! Manson reaches down and grabs his leg!
BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels is coming back in the ring! He is assessing the situation as Rat Fink starts kicking away on the bad wheel.
VIC WATERS: What side will Scotty Michaels take here… BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels walks over…. HE LIFTS THE LEG OF MANSON AND DRIVES IT INTO THE MAT! Rat Fink extends the hand to Michaels and Michaels accepts it!
VIC WATERS: Good, they have mended their fences, let the beating begin now. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink is directing Scotty Michaels. Scotty Michaels drags Manson out of the ring while Rat Fink is going to get a chair. Scotty Michaels pulls Manson over towards the steel step… HE RAMS THE LEG HARD INTO THE STEPS! Scotty Michaels places Manson’s leg on the steps… RAT FINK TAKES A SWING AND NAILS MANSON HARD IN BAD PART OF HIS LEG! The ref is coming out to stop Rat Fink from using the chair again but it appears to be too late as Manson is rolling around on the ground in pain.
VIC WATERS: Scotty Michaels and Rat Fink have accomplished their mission of taking Manson out of the match. No more Manson as North American Champion, WFW has improved dramatically today!
BRANDON JACOBS: What the hell are you saying? Don’t you see all those fans looking to see how Manson is right now? People love Manson. Even when he is a heel, people love Manson. No league is better without Manson.
VIC WATERS: Without Manson, this league will be all Doc and how can that be a bad thing? BRANDON JACOBS: Don’t remind me. Rat Fink is jaw jacking with the crowd… WAIT A SECOND! Scotty Michaels has picked up the chair that the referee took away from Rat Fink… HE NAILS BLIND SIDES RAT FINK WITH THE CHAIR! Rat Fink crashes down to the ground! Michaels spits in Rat Fink's face for the disrespect he showed him earlier in the match.
VIC WATERS: These guys turn on each other faster than lawyers. BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels picks Rat Fink up… GERMAN SUPLEX! Scotty Michaels gets up and he sees the steps! Scotty Michaels picks Rat Fink up again… OH MY GOD! GERMAN SUPLEX INTO THE STEEL RING STEPS! Rat Fink is dead!
VIC WATERS: Vicious move by Michaels, he may be even more evil than Rat Fink. BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels takes Rat Fink and dumps him into the crowd! Scotty Michaels goes into the crowd… PILEDRIVER INTO THE GRASS NOLL AT RINGSIDE! Scotty Michaels is looking at the ladder!
VIC WATERS: If he can get that ladder into the ring, that gold is as good as his. BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels grabs the seven foot ladder and slides it under the bottom rope… Michaels reenters the ring… NO! MANSON HAS GRABBED THE LEG OF MICHAELS AS HE HAS TRIED TO RE-ENTER!
VIC WATERS: Manson is a cockroach, when the world ends, he will be one of the few still around.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is trying to shake Manson’s hand off his ankle but Manson won’t let go… MANSON PULLS MICHAELS OFF THE APRON AND HE CRASHES FACE FIRST INTO THE APRON!
VIC WATERS: Look at those crazed eyes of Manson, I don’t envy Michaels. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson kicks Michaels in the stomach… DDT ON THE FLOOR! Manson rolls into the ring and starts to setup the ladder… Manson is limping badly, it looks like Rat Fink & Michaels really did a number on his leg. Michaels is getting up and he is looking at Manson in the ring.
VIC WATERS: Manson is starting to climb the ladder! COME ON MICHAELS GET IN THERE! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is trying but because of his wheel he can’t climb that fast… MICHAELS LOOKS AT MANSON…. MICHAELS SPEARS THE LADDER AND MANSON GOES CRASHING DOWN ON THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Good lord, all Michaels had to do was simply knock Manson over, he didn’t have to take the ladder with him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is grabbing his shoulder, it looks like he may have done some damage when he rammed the steel ladder. Michaels re-sets the ladder up and starts climbing... MICHAELS REACHES UP.. HE CAN'T GRAB WITH HIS RIGHT ARM! Scotty Michaels climbs up one more step and reaches with his left.. HE ALMOST HAS IT... MANSON TIPS THE LADDER OVER AND MICHAELS CRASHES DOWN TO THE MAT WITH A THUD!
VIC WATERS: We were three inches away from a new champion. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is slowly using the ropes to get up….. MICHAELS NAILS HIM WITH LEFT! Manson uses the ropes to keep himself up. It looks like Michaels really hurt that right shoulder and he has a problem throwing punches with it.
VIC WATERS: Manson is smiling; he sees a weakness in Michaels. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON GRABS THE RIGHT ARM AND PUTS ON A CHICKEN WING ON MICHAELS! Michaels is screaming in pain! MICHAELS ELBOW MANSON WITH LEFT ELBOW BUT MANSON REFUSES TO LET GO!
VIC WATERS: Manson is in so much pain from his bad wheel, I doubt he feels any pain elsewhere.
BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON NAILS MICHAELS IN THE BACK OF THE HEAD WITH A FOREARM AND LETS GO OF THE HOLD! Manson is calling out to the fans… HE IS GOING FOR THE TOURNIQUET! HE GRABS THE RIGHT ARM AND HE LOCKS IT ON!
VIC WATERS: WAKE UP RAT FINK! WAKE UP! BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is screaming in pain…. MICHAELS IS TAPPING! HE WANTS TO GIVE UP! But he can’t, he can’t give up! The only way this match can end is if someone climbs that ladder and grabs the title.
VIC WATERS: Michaels could be tapping to try to fool Manson to break the hold, which is smart wrestling because your natural instinct is to break the hold after you hear your opponent give up!
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is much too smart for that, he won’t be happy until he rips him arm off and beats him with it!
VIC WATERS: Manson, our fan favorite… BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is talking trash to Michaels for what he did to his leg on the outside and is making him suffer for his sins.
VIC WATERS: Michaels looks like he is on the verge of passing out! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson breaks the hold and he throws Michaels over the top! Manson grabs the fallen ladder and takes it to the outside…
VIC WATERS: What is he planning of doing here?! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is taking the ladder and is sliding right behind the ring post in the ring… MANSON IS REACHING INTO HIS TIGHTS… HE HAS HANDCUFFS! HE HANDCUFFS THE GOOD ARM OF MICHAELS TO THE LADDER! Manson is reaching into his boot… HE HAS ANOTHER PAIR OF HANDCUFFS! MICHAELS IS TRYING TO MOVE AWAY BUT MANSON NAILS WITH A HARD RIGHT TO THE CHIN! Manson puts the handcuffs on Michaels and attaches his right arm to the ladder!
VIC WATERS: Oh my god, Manson has crucified Michaels in the corner! He can’t escape because he shackled each arm of Michaels to the ladder and that ladder is inside of the ring!
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson had to get Michaels out of the way so he could deal with Rat Fink who has finally gotten back to his feet. Rat Fink is bleeding and now Manson has Rat Fink all to himself. May god have mercy on Rat Fink’s soul.
VIC WATERS: RUN RAT FINK RUN! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is slowly walking over to Rat Fink… RAT FINK SEES HIM AND STARTS RUNNING! Manson, slowly limping, is following him around the ring…. RAT FINK ROLLS INTO THE RING AND MANSON COMES IN AFTER HIM!
VIC WATERS: This won’t be pretty… BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK DROPS DOWN ON HIS HANDS AND KNEES AND IS BEGGING MANSON NOT TO HURT HIM!
VIC WATERS: Manson should show him mercy and let him go. He’ll have his title soon enough. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson pulls Rat Fink up by the hair… RAT FINK IS CRYING! MANSON CLOTHESLINES RAT FINK DOWN TO THE MAT! Manson stands over Rat Fink and he spits in his face! MANSON STARTS STOMPING AWAY ON RAT FINK! Rat Fink rolls out of the ring and Manson goes out after him.
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink looks like he is trying to wear Manson down, the more walking Manson has to do. The more his leg will be sore after all the punishment he has taken.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink runs to the corner where Scotty Michaels is crucified to the corner of the ring and tries to escape through the crowd… MANSON MAKES A MADE DASH AND CATCHES RAT FINK BY THE TRUNK! RAT FINK PUSHES MANSON INTO SCOTTY MICHAELS…. OH MY GOD, MICHAELS HAS PUT A BODY SCISSORS AROUND MANSON AND IS KEEPING HIM IN THE CORNER AGAINST HIS WILL!
VIC WATERS: People always seem to forget but the strongest muscles in the body are in the legs. That body scissors is ten times more effective than a bear hug.
BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK STARTS LAYING IN CHOPS TO MANSON AND MANSON CAN’T MOVE TO AVOID THEM!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink is trying to steal a chair from a little old lady at ringside but she is putting up a fight… HE HAS IT!
BRANDON JACOBS: HE IS GOING TO GO FOR A STEINERLINE WITH THE CHAIR! If he hit this, Manson is done for! RAT FINK CHARGES AND MANSON DUCK! RAT FINK CLOCKS SCOTTY MICHAELS AND SCOTTY MICHAELS IS OUT COLD!
VIC WATERS: Look at Manson, he isn’t wasting anytime. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson reaches under the ring and pulls out another ladder! Rat Fink is apologizing and trying to wake Michaels to no avail. Manson pushes the ladder in the ring and slides under the bottom rope.
VIC WATERS: Get back into the ring Rat Fink, Manson is setting up the ladder! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson looks at the distracted Rat Fink and starts to slowly but surely climb the ladder…. RAT FINK SEES MANSON! Manson reaches up for the title… HE IS A COUPLE RUNGS SHORT! Rat Fink runs into the ring and starts climbing the ladder as fast as he can! Manson reaches up again but he is just a bit off center… RAT FINK FINALLY REACHES THE SAME LEVEL MANSON IS ON AND MANSON AND RAT FINK BEGIN TO EXCHANGE BLOWS! Rat Fink with a right… Manson with a right..RAT FINK WITH THREE CONSECUTIVE PUNCHES!
VIC WATERS: DOC! DOC SILVER AND ANDRE EIAN ARE COMING DOWN TO THE RINGSIDE AREA WITH WIRE CUTTERS TO TRY TO FREE SCOTTY MICHAELS!
BRANDON JACOBS: Damn those two, can’t they mind their own damn business for once? Silver should stop worry about making Manson’s life hell and worry about his match which is coming up next. MANSON IS WOBBLING OFF THE LADDER! MANSON REGAIN HIS BALANCE AND HE NAILS RAT FINK WITH A RIGHT FLUSH TO THE JAW!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink looks like he just had his clock cleaned! Doc & Eian have finally freed Michaels from the ladder!
BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON NAILS RAT FINK ANOTHER RIGHT…. AND THIS TIME RAT FINK FALLS OFF THE LADDER EIGHT FEET AND ONTO THE MAT HARD!!
VIC WATERS: NO! DAMN IT! NO! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson reaches up…. HE FINALLY HAS IT! MANSON HAS REGAINED HIS TITLE AND HAS WON THE MATCH!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match and still WFW North American Champion…. MANSON! *fans pop huge* VIC WATERS: Wait a second, look at the video screen! They are showing footage of the masked man attacking Manson! I didn’t know that was video taped!
BRANDON JACOBS: LOOK! HE IS TAKING OFF THE MASK…. OH MY GOD ITS SCOTTY MICHAELS! VIC WATERS: NEW #1 MOST WANTED! Manson looks utterly pissed. BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver & Andre Eian are escorting Michaels back to the dressing room as fast as they can. Manson is trying to climb down the ladder but his bad leg is slowing him down. Despite Manson’s victory here today, the damage done to his leg could comeback to haunt him down the road in his match against Eian. I bet this was a conspiracy by Michaels, Silver, Eian, and Rat Fink to rob Manson of his title and take him out of action.
VIC WATERS: Only time will tell, Manson may have settled his feud with Rat Fink here today, but a whole new page in the book of Manson has begun with Scotty Michaels.
BRANDON JACOBS: That Michaels… he may be even worse than Rat Fink, I didn’t even think that was possible. He has shown his true colors here today and that he is willing to do anything to win.
VIC WATERS: Michaels is a man after my own heart! BRANDON JACOBS: We have one match to go folks and this one is for the WFW World Heavyweight Title between Doc Silver, Shawn Hart, Richard Gideon, and Psycho! Stay tuned everyone!
