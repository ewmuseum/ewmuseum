2) no exact setup to redemption it can happen almost anywhere Finishers 1) Fall From Grace: Seton Bomb 2) Redemption: dragon sleeper The name Minion has been a wrestling stable since 1991. The originator of the name Minion, Tom Parker, was a force to deal with leaving a wake of destruction and bodies in his path. Tom went on to high acclaim and attracted many fans, but on a cold day in 1998 Tom Parkers life was snuffed out in an automobile accident. The Minion we see today is the second incarnation and is nothing like the original. Busting onto the scene in 1998 claiming to be the true Minion and Tom Parkers greatest fan he has quickly grown to be one of the most disliked wrestlers in the history of the sport. Claiming to not only have been Tom Parkers greatest fan and idol worshipper their relationship outside the ring is shrouded in mystery as some accounts say this Minion was actually trained under Tom Parker while others say Tom Parker abused this poor fan one night after a match and snapped wanting only revenge. Fast, agile, and sadistic are just a few of the words used to describe this Minion. Stalker, blood thirsty, and cold have all been used to describe this unique unorthodox wrestler.
