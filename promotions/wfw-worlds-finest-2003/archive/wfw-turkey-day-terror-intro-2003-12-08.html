WFW Turkey Day Terror Intro

8 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 8 December 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome everyone to Turkey Day Terror, we are live from Foxwoods Casino in Ledyard, Connecticut! Folks, you are in for a feast of great wrestling action. We’ve got the WFW North American Champion Richard Gideon defending the title against the former WFW World Heavyweight Champion Psycho
VIC WATERS: Long, long awaited match up… BRANDON JACOBS: We’ve got a four way elimination match to determine our championship schedule for the next four shows with WFW President Alex Wylde, former WFW North American Champion Scotty Michaels, Copycat, and Sean Edmunds..
VIC WATERS: Should be the most heated match of night… BRANDON JACOBS: We’ve got a down right war between Manson and Anarky after what transpired after the World Title Match at Ghoulish Games
VIC WATERS: Those two men are going to kill each other. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink is putting up his BAD World Heavyweight Title against dangerous and daring Felix Red..
VIC WATERS: Felix Red has the ability to pin anyone with the bat of an eye and gave both Gideon and Scotty Michaels a run for their money in the three. Rat Fink’s winning streak may finally come to an end.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is seeking revenge against Minion after his reprehensible actions against Adam Benjamin…
VIC WATERS: Minion is going to bite the head of El Arco Iris and rip it off… but it still won’t be the most violent match of the night.
BRANDON JACOBS: And the opening match, the team of Shawn Hart, Terrell Mason, and Good Eddie will face the team of Luster, Pulsar, and Dormant..
VIC WATERS: This show is so damn good, our champion is working the opener. That tells you all you need to know about the amount of action coming your way tonight. No sleep induced comas here, the wrestlers are excited, management is excited, and these fans are pumped. I was barely able to get to my broadcast position tonight because the autograph hounds were in full force.
BRANDON JACOBS: Vic, those girls looked like they were working to me. VIC WATERS: Horse feathers, they seemed like nice girls to me. Can I help it that every woman finds me irresistible?
BRANDON JACOBS: For $100, they would find Attila the Hun attractive. VIC WATERS: Good, then take your $100 Attila and let me call the show. BRANDON JACOBS: ::flustered:: I don’t hang out with the same kind of women you hang out with. The ladies that I court have class.
VIC WATERS: All remedial. But let’s stop the bickering, this show is greater than some dick measuring contest between us, which isn’t even close, this is going to be a wonderful night. I haven’t been this excited since Doc was champion. We have three matches which could be main events on almost any other show. I mean, how hard has management worked to get Richard Gideon and Psycho together for a one on one match and on top of all that, it is a WFW North American Title defense, the second highest title in all of the land.
BRANDON JACOBS: Those two have been friends forever but time and circumstances has pitted them against each other. It looked like the match was never going to happen for awhile but finally, it came all together and should be a special Thanksgiving treat.
VIC WATERS: And it isn’t just about retaining the title, Richard Gideon also has to survive… Doc’s match with Psycho took a lot out of him to the point where he still isn’t the same man that he was. But that match isn’t all…. I get to witness Anarky beat Manson within an inch of his life. I’ll tell you, the pleasure that I get from that will be almost as great as a night with those dozen women you saw before.
BRANDON JACOBS: You are a sick man, sick. VIC WATERS: Manson deserves it. I’m tired of all of his fans making him into the uncrowned champion of the WFW. Anarky is going to show the world that the name Anarky should come BEFORE Manson and not AFTER it. But even as great that is… that is not all. We have a four way match, which while it won’t be the main event since the WFW North American Title will be defended tonight, deserves to be. That match is going to be off the charts great.
BRANDON JACOBS: Give me your prediction on who you think is going to win. VIC WATERS: With all my extensive wrestling knowledge, I’ve come to a conclusion on that match…. I don’t have a damn clue. Edmunds and Copycat have yet to get a pinfall or submission on each other they are so closely matched. You have President Wylde who so incredibly gifted and I’m not just saying that because he makes my checks, if he wins a World Title shot, he could easily win the World Heavyweight Title which has alluded his grasp. Then…. Then…. You have Scotty Michaels who made the promise to everyone who wanted to hear him that he would win the WFW World Heavyweight Title before Spring begins. If he could come in first or second, he could make everyone who thought he couldn’t do it, eat their words and show everyone the championship blood which runs through his veins.
BRANDON JACOBS: Winning the World Heavyweight Title is an incredibly hard thing to do. It took Hart three tries to become WFW World Heavyweight Champion. Manson has had two opportunities to win the WFW World Heavyweight Title and was turned away. Richard Gideon faced Doc twice for the WFW World Heavyweight Title and despite nearly giving me and all of Doc’s other fans a heart attack, he couldn’t do it. The only man who has ever won the WFW World Heavyweight title on his first shot was Psycho.
VIC WATERS: It is a matter of gaining experience. There is no equal to fighting in a championship match for preparing for one, but anything can happen in any given match once you win your way into a title match. Shawn Hart is going to have to wrestle in three World Heavyweight Title matches over the span of a month if he wants to retain the WFW World Heavyweight Title and he could be stopped on any step of his voyage.
BRANDON JACOBS: Things are really starting to pick up, things could get interesting, quite interesting. Let’s start off the wild month we have before us and go to commercial break, we will be back with six man action as the team of Shawn Hart, Terrell Mason, and Good Eddie will square off against Luster, Pulsar, and Dormant. STAY TUNED!
