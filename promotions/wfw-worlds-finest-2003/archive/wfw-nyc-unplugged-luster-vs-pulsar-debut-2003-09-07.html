WFW: NYC Unplugged: Luster vs. Pulsar (debut)

7 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 7 September 2003 *Segment taped earlier in the day* (FADEIN to a hotel near Central Park earlier in the day. A white limo pulls up, and HEEL steps out first... afterwards, we see Scotty Michaels step out with the North American championship belt over his shoulder, cocky as always..)
BRANDON JACOBS: Now Scotty, I saw some of the stuff you've been doing, basically making a mockery of the Mexicans and the Canadians... and you say that you're the perfect North American champion, and representative of the..
(Scotty grabs the mic..) SCOTTY MICHAELS: Jacobs, let me tell you something. Ever since the day I stepped into the WFW, I've done exactly what I've said I was going to do. I won Number one contender match after number one contender match for this belt, and when I got my chance... in a standard match, I won it. Now that I've got the North American championship, I set my sights on the world title. Having this belt right here, it makes me number one contender.
BRANDON JACOBS: But Scotty, the way you've been celebrating lately... do you really think you'll have it in you? I mean, you passed out at a bar in Philadelphia..
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Oh, one night of indulgence. I talked to that Luster character, and he told me I needed that. But getting on to Luster... he helped me out at the Spirit of NGEN, guess he's a fan. The fact of the matter is that I've got fans all over this damn continent... and therefore, I am the greatest North American champion ever, and soon enough, I will be the world heavyweight champion.
BRANDON JACOBS: At the card before the pay per view, it seemed like you and Shawn Hart had some kind of comraderie, though...
*back to real time* BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels is never a man at a loss for words. VIC WATERS: Scotty Michaels can be that confident because he is that good. BRANDON JACOBS: Maybe, but he really needs to watch his drinking around all these impressionable youth.
VIC WATERS: So you mean… he should be a role model like Luster? BRADNON JACOBS: Ye… no. Good point. VIC WATERS: So, what have you heard anything about Pulsar? BRANDON JACOBS: I really don’t much about his past, except he has a really thick Australian accent it looks from the scars on his body that he has been in a lot of wars in his life. He is the type of guy who is tough enough to maybe be the one to put Luster in his place after what he did to Manson.
VIC WATERS: Luster is a hero for Manson haters everywhere. How could you want to see him ever want to see him derailed?
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster & Scotty Michaels took Manson by surprise, that’s all, but when the day comes that Manson, Michaels, and Luster are in the ring again, they may not escape again so lucky.
VIC WATERS: We will see, Luster has finally hit a hot streak, I doubt anyone is going to be able to set him back.
BRANDON JACOBS: There is only one way to find out, let’s send this one to the ring! (CUEUP: “Funk Town” by Disco Fever) RING ANNOUNCER: On his way to the ring now, from Atlanta, Georgia, standing at 6’6” and weighing in at 250 pounds, he is the master of style, LUSTER!
::Luster comes down to the ring to the boos from the males and the mixed cheers of the females in the crowd::
(CUEUP: “Whiskey In A Jar” by Metallica) ::Pulsar walks down to the ring to the cheers from the crowd and enters the ring:: ::ref pats both men down:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second, Scotty Michaels & HEEL are watching by the entrance. I wonder what they want.
VIC WATERS: Well, it is pretty obvious, Luster obviously is a big fan of Michaels and he is just keeping an eye out for a friend. Makes sense to me.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster and Pulsar go to lock up… LUSTER RAKES HIM IN THE EYES! Luster grabs Pulsar by the head and RAMS him head first into the turnbuckle! Pulsar stumbles back and takes a swing at Luster… LUSTER DUCKS!
VIC WATERS: Luster is really taking it to Pulsar and is trying to establish his authority here early on.
BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER NAILS PULSAR WITH A CLOTHESLINE AND HE GOES DOWN TO THE MAT! Luster grabs the legs…. HE DROPS DOWN AND HEADBUTTS PULSAR IN THE LOWER MIDSECTION!
VIC WATERS: That was dangerously close to a low blow there… BRANDON JACOBS: Luster grabs the right leg of Pulsar and starts kicking away… Luster drags him to the center of the ring… it looks like he is going for the sharpshooter!
Luster goes to grab the leg… PULSAR KICKS LUSTER TO THE HEAD AND LUSTER FALLS BACK! VIC WATERS: Come on Pulsar! BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar goes to the ropes and uses them to help him up… Luster is getting up…. PULSAR STAGGERS TOWARDS LUSTER AND NAILS HIM WITH A SHOULDER BLOCK AND LUSTER IS SENT BACK WITH FORCE INTO THE CORNER! This crowd is going nuts!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar showed true grit there overcoming his pain to charge him that shoulder block when he could have used to time to recuperate…
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar picks Luster up off the mat…. PULSAR IRISH WHIPS LUSTER INTO THE CORNER…. PULSAR STAGGERS IN AND NAILS LUSTER WITH A RUNNING TURNBUCKLE SPLASH! Luster falls face first down to the mat!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar has completely changed the momentum of this match. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar drags Luster away from the corner… Pulsar climbs through the ropes onto the ring apron… SOMERSAULT LEGDROP ONTO THE BACK OF THE HEAD OF LUSTER! Pulsar turns Luster over! 1---------------------2----------------KICKOUT! Pulsar hooks the leg again! 1-----------------------2--------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Luster is tiring out, if Pulsar keeps at it, he is going to wear him down. BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR IS CALLING FOR THE BLACK HOLE! VIC WATERS: Michaels & HEEL are walking down to ringside and HEEL is yelling at the ref. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref goes to the edge of the ring and warns Michaels and HEEL to stay away from the ring…. Pulsar sets for the Black Hole… LOW BLOW BY LUSTER! Pulsar falls to his knees!
VIC WATERS: Michaels is a genius. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster and Pulsar are both being counted by the ref! 1----2---3---4----5----6--- LUSTER IS ABLE TO GET UP! Luster picks up Pulsar by the hair…. LUSTER STARTS LAYING INTO PULSAR WITH RIGHT HANDS AND BACKS HIM INTO THE CORNER!
VIC WATERS: Michaels buying Luster some time seems to have given him a new lease on life. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster backs up from an exhausted Pulsar in the corner…. LUSTER NAILS HIM WITH A SPEAR AND PULSAR IS SANDWICHED INTO THE CORNER!
VIC WATERS: That is bound to take the wind out of you. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster picks Pulsar off the mat and he whips him off the ropes… SPINEBUSTER! Luster goes for the cover! 1-----------------2------------- KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Luster is yelling at the ref for the slow count, he almost had him there. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster picks Pulsar up and whips him off the ropes… LUSTER CATCHES PULSAR IN A SLEEPER HOLD! With all the wind he took out of him, this may do it!
VIC WATERS: HE HAS THIS HOOKED IN TIGHT! BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is flaring around his arms trying to break free but he can’t manage to escape! Pulsar is going out fast….. IT LOOKS LIKE HE IS OUT! The ref drops his hand once…. IT FALLS! The ref drops his hand twice…. IT FALLS FOR A SECOND TIME! The ref goes to raise his hand one last time…… PULSAR SOMEONE MANAGES TO KEEP IT UP! I have no idea where Pulsar is getting the energy to fight this after all the abuse he has taken tonight.
VIC WATERS: It is that competitive spirit to complete in a WFW ring and become a success. Pulsar has showed tonight that he is a tough, tough man.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster breaks the hold… Pulsar didn’t look like he had much left, I really don’t understand why he did it.
VIC WATERS: Luster is gyrating his hips over the body of Pulsar… he needs to take this match seriously and go for a pin!
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster lays over Pulsar and goes for a pin! How disgraceful! 1---------------------------2-------------------KICKOUT! PULSAR KICKED OUT! Luster is shocked that Pulsar kicked out!
VIC WATERS: Come on Pulsar! BRANDON JACOBS: Luster picks Pulsar off the man… PULSAR FIRES AT LUSTER WITH A SERIES OF RIGHTS AND LEFTS AND LUSTER IS BEING BACKED UP!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar is having a surge of energy here, it may be enough to put him over the top.
BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR CHARGES WITH A SHOULDERBLOCK…. LUSTER PUTS UP HIS KNEE AND PULSAR GOES HEAD FIRST INTO LUSTER’S KNEE! OH MY!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar is out of it! HE GOT CLOCKED WITH THAT ONE! BRANDON JACOBS: Luster picks up Pulsar off the mat… LUSTER’S FINISHER THE FUNK AND WAGNELL POWERBOMB! Luster this time hooks the leg and makes the cover! 1---------------------------------2-----------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match….. LUSTER! BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is a hell of a fighter, and he may have won if Michaels hadn’t distracted the ref early on, but Luster just had a little bit more tonight.
VIC WATERS: I’m impressed with Pulsar. He was left for dead several times but he refused to die. It is only a matter of time before Pulsar is a factor here in the WFW.
BRANDON JACOBS: Okay folks, we comeback from this commercial break, Diablo will put the BAD World Heavyweight Title on the line against Rat Fink…. STAY TUNED EVERYONE!
