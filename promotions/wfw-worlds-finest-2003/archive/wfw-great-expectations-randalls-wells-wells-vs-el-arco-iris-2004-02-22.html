WFW Great Expectations: Randalls/Wells, Wells vs. El Arco Iris

22 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 22 February 2004 (BACKSTAGE) ::Jared Wells and Mike Randalls, sporting a referee shirt, is walking the halls and come face to face::
'RAGE 'o FIRE': Well, Mike Randalls what a big surprise. Your more than likely unsure of who I am right? Funny, I've been in this business since 1996, become 3 time WWL World Champion, and held numerous titles......but for some reason we've never ever been in the same building...why? It was guys like you who thought they could do whatever they want....I tried to become that main stream guy but the f'n politics just would not let me....but forget the past man. After years and years I finally get to say my peace.....I've noticed your a special ref tonight in the Anarky vs Mephisto matchup tonight....I'll be looking over you pal......my time has come remember that....
RANDALLS: If your time has come, Wells...I look forward to seeing your many accomplisments in the ring...
:: RANDALLS walks forward, his smile fades yet he is not angry...his emerald eyes look straight into Wells' unfearful and unrepentant...::
RANDALLS: But never for one moment, did I ever hold you down. I've always been man enough to face all competitors around the world. If you want to blame me for your choice in not taking advantage of that...it won't be the first time. Just understand, that what you believe isn't always the TRUTH.
::Randalls walks away from WELLS...:: RANDALLS: Watch me all you want, Jared. Just understand, that your time will NOT come during this match...and NOT at MY expense.
(BROADCAST BOOTH) BRANDON JACOBS: There seems to be some hostility by Wells towards Randalls. VIC WATERS: Wells is a bitter, bitter man, but we love him for that. BRANDON JACOBS: I remember as a kid watching guys like Jimmy Snuka and Barry Windham and wondering why they never got more opportunities to challenge for the World Title despite having a world of talent. Thankfully though, every so often a guy like that, like Eddy Guerrero breaks through who has beaten his problems. That all being said, I don’t see how it is Randalls fault that he never got ahead.
VIC WATERS: Top stars never want the young promising guys to come up and take their spot. It is an adage as old as time.
BRANDON JACOBS: Randalls isn’t like that, some older guys are, but you can’t judge all of them based on the actions of a few.
VIC WATERS: I say blast them all and let God sort them out. BRANDON JACOBS: ::shakes head:: Wells better keep his focus because tonight he has a hell of a challenge on his hands. Both Jared Wells and El Arco Iris are in the midst of a resurgence and win tonight for either man would cement that.
VIC WATERS: With the long history Jared Wells has with Edmunds, imagine if Edmunds wins the title here tonight. Both he and Copycat already have an extensive history with Edmunds here in WFW and back in the WWL and knowing the WFW booking committee, that could throw both right in the mix.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris though tonight has redemption on his mind because Jared Wells pinned his tag team partner Copycat in the three way tag match and if they had won their matches, they would have had the stipulations of their choice tonight for their matches.
VIC WATERS: Did you really want to see First Hug and Smurf or Surrender? BRANDON JACOBS: With every fiber of my being and I know I’m not alone when I say that. VIC WATERS: You are a sick man. BRANDON JACOBS: You need a hug. VIC WATERS: Stay away from me you freak. BRANDON JACOBS: It could be the reason you are always angry. You just may not get hugged enough.
VIC WATERS: I’ll hug you the day El Arco Iris wins a single’s title. BRANDON JACOBS: YOU ARE ON! VIC WATERS: Damn it, I didn’t mean that literally. BRANDON JACOBS: Too late, you said it. VIC WATERS: Argh.. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, let’s send this down to ringside and finally get this underway! (CUEUP: “Here to stay” by Korn) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from the state which has produced two of the three WFW World Heavyweight champions, the former three time WWL World Heavyweight Champion…. “RAGE ‘O FIRE” JARED WELLS!
::Jared Wells comes out to a mixed reaction from the crowd:: (CUEUP: “Tunak Tunak Tun” by Dahler Mendhi)
RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing the first man from the final team, hailing from over the rainbow, the Warrior of Happiness, EL ARCO IRIS!
::El Arco Iris walks down to a huge pop from the crowd, dozens of children can been seen in the crowd wearing El Arco Iris masks, and enters the ring::
::Jared Wells grabs the mic:: 'RAGE 'o FIRE': This is it, time is up. I turn around El Arco Iris and your hiding all of the sudden. Do you feel right like that? None of the pieces of the puzzle seem to be connecting do they? You feel like your doing the battle all by your lonesome self right? Let me tell you how things are going to run tonight. I will walk down that aisle.....you will meet your maker, and you feel the rage. Your happy days of (BLEEP) are over....I've been in this business for way too long to let some rainbow wearing fruity pants rain on my parade. Again, I apologize for my actions but things need to change.....just so happens Mr. Happy man, your in the way.....READY TO DANCE? RAGE......HE LEADS........
::Jared Wells charges El Arco Iris and begins to pound him in the head with the mic:: ::bell rings:: BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is pleading with him to stop by Jared Wells wraps the chord around El Arco Iris’s neck and begins to choke him out!
CROWD: HUG HIM UP IRIS, HUG HIM UP! HUG HIM UP IRIS, HUG HIM UP! VIC WATERS: This crowd is annoying me something fierce. BRANDON JACOBS: Iris has his hands underneath the wire now and thankfully that chord can’t do full damage. JARED WELLS GRABS THE EYE HOLES OF EL ARCO IRIS AND HE IS TRYING TO RIP HIS MASK!
VIC WATERS: RIP IT! Lets see who this love monger really is. BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS FLIPS JARED WELLS OVER AND DOWN ONTO THE MAT! El Arco Iris removes the chord from around his neck and the ring announcer reels it back to him before it can be used again. El Arco Iris falls to the ground and attempts to catch his breath.
VIC WATERS: If you look careful, you can see the mark that Wells left from the chord around El Arco Iris’ neck. It is a good thing he was able to get that off when he did or serious damage could have been done.
BRANDON JACOBS: Jared Wells is crawling over to El Arco Iris…. AND HE LOCKS HIM IN A SLEEPER HOLD AND DRAGS HIM TO THE CENTER OF THE RING!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris hasn’t had nearly enough time to recoup yet. This could do it. BRANDON JACOBS: Jared Wells it locked in tight… the ref is checking to make sure this doesn’t become a choke.
VIC WATERS: You have to wonder if Wells is sending a message to Randalls in the back with this match
BRANDON JACOBS: WELLS HAS SLIPPED INTO A CHOKE! The ref goes to check but he moves it up just in time.
VIC WATERS: The kids are going to cry themselves to sleep tonight. BRANDON JACOBS: WELLS SLIPS IT BACK INTO A CHOKE! El Arco Iris yelps for the ref to check… HE IS MAKING WELLS BREAK THE HOLD AND IRIS FALLS DOWN ON THE GROUND! He is having trouble catching his breath.
VIC WATERS: Jared Well has a big smile on his face, he knows he has El Arco Iris just where he wants him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wells chases after El Arco Iris…. EL ARCO IRIS CRAWLS BACKWARDS BETWEEN WELLS’ LEGS…. El Arco Iris pulls Jared Wells’ legs out from underneath him and Wells goes face first into the mat!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris and Felix Red are the two quickest men in all of WFW and El Arco Iris showed why right there.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris gets up and bounces off of the opposite ropes… EL ARCO IRIS JUMPS ON THE SECOND ROPE AND DOES A 360 FOOT STOMP RIGHT ONTO THE BACK OF WELLS! Wells reaches back in pain!
VIC WATERS: I have never even seen that before, you can tell El Arco Iris studies his history.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco locks in a stepover armbar on Jared Wells…. LA MAGISTRAL! VIC WATERS: With the shot Wells just took to the back, this is going to be awfully hard to kick out of.
BRANDON JACOBS: AROUND AND AROUND WELLS GOES, WHERE HE STOPS… NOBODY KNOWS! El Arco Iris stops the spinning. The ref goes for the count! 1-----------------------------------------------2------------------------------ Wells just got his right shoulder up!
VIC WATERS: That is enough to make anyone dizzy. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris release the arm bar and he drags Wells over to the corner! El Arco Iris is climbing up to the top rope….. EL ARCO IRIS FLIES OFF AND GOES FOR A MOONSAULT…. WELLS GETS HIS KNEES UP AND EL ARCO IRIS GRABS HIS MIDSECTION!
VIC WATERS: Iris took a risk in order to put this away and it wound up costing him. BRANDON JACOBS: Wells crawls over and goes for a pinfall on El Arco Iris! 1-------------------------------2-----------------------------KICKOUT! Almost got him there!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris looks like he is hurt. He could have easily cracked a rib. BRANDON JACOBS: Jared Wells picks up El Arco Iris off of the mat… JARED WELLS POWERSLAMS EL ARCO IRIS DOWN ON THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: He just pancaked him! BRANDON JACOBS: Wells goes down for the cover on Iris…. 1---------------------------------2------------------------ JARED WELLS PULLS EL ARCO IRIS UP! How cocky can you be?
VIC WATERS: It isn’t a great secret that Anarky, Wells, and the masked man have a hatred for Copycat and they would like nothing better than to take out his little buddy.
BRANDON JACOBS: Jared Wells picks El Arco Iris up in a bear hug and is squeezing El Arco Iris’ injured ribs with all he has!
VIC WATERS: This is the only kind of hug I ever want to see El Arco Iris in. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is checking to see if he wants to give up but El Arco Iris is shaking his head no!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris is dead center in the ring. The ropes aren’t going to be able to help him.
BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS PUTS HIM IN A FRONT FACE LOCK….. Jared Wells lets go of the bear hug and EL ARCO IRIS DELIVERS THE CROWN OF HATHOR TORNADO DDT!
VIC WATERS: What a counter! BRANDON JACOBS: Jared Wells is down but he is trying to get up…. EL ARCO IRIS HITS WELLS WITH THE SHINNING WIZARD! Down goes Wells!
VIC WATERS: NO! BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris goes for the cover! 1---------------------------------------2-------------------------- JARED WELLS JUST BARELY GETS HIS SHOULDER UP!
VIC WATERS: Way too close… BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris chicken wings the arms of Jared Wells and sliders his shoulders down to the mat! 1-----------------------------2---------------------- WELLS SWINGS HIS LEGS AND TIPS OVER THE PIN ATTEMPT!
VIC WATERS: Wells escapes! BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris picks up Jared Wells off the mat… EL ARCO IRIS IS TRYING TO GERMAN SUPLEX THE BIGGER WELLS AND WELLS IS FIGHTING IT…. WELLS TRIES TO ELBOW EL ARCO IRIS BUT MISSES!
VIC WATERS: FIGHT WELLS FIGHT! BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS DELIVERS THE GERMAN SUPLEX AND WELLS LANDS HARD ON HIS HEAD! VIC WATERS: Wells got clocked hard on that one. BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, A MASKED MAN IS COMING FROM UNDERNEATH THE RING AND HE IS HIDING ON THE SIDE!
VIC WATERS: That looks like the masked man who helped Anarky and Wells at Mind Over Matter!
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is waiting for Wells to get up…. Wells is staggering to his feet…. El Arco Iris bounces off the ropes for a Flying Forearm…. THE MASKED MAN TRIPS EL ARCO IRIS! NO DAMNIT!
VIC WATERS: YES! BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco stumbles forward towards Wells…. WELLS CATCHES HIM AND LIFTS HIM UP HIGH FOR A RAGE BOMB…. HE HITS IT! Wells makes the cover! 1---------------------------------------2--------------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. RAGE O’ FIRE JARED WELLS! BRANDON JACOBS: The masked man comes into the ring and he and Jared Wells are beating El Arco Iris down! Isn’t it enough that the masked man cost him the victory? DO THEY HAVE TO ADD INSULT TO INJURY!
VIC WATERS: KILL HIM! BRANDON JACOBS: Jared Wells picks up El Arco Iris again…. HE NAILS HIM WITH A SECOND RAGE BOMB!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris is clutching his ribs, he is going to need some time to recoup after this one.
BRANDON JACOBS: HERE COMES COPYCAT WITH A CHAIR IN HIS HAND AND HE CHASES JARED WELLS AND THE MASKED MAN AWAY! The paramedics are coming down to see if El Arco Iris is okay.
VIC WATERS: Maybe I should go down there and offer El Arco Iris a hug. BRANDON JACOBS: Quiet you, have some respect. Folks, when we comeback, we will have our first title match of the evening as Felix Red puts the BAD World Heavyweight Title on the line against Minion and Good Eddie…. STAY TUNED!
