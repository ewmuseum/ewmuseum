Guerra Interminable: The Almighty Has Arrived..

15 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 15 November 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: It should be noted that Dan Ryan is usually accompanied by Lindsay Troy down to the ring, but as stated on the previous show, Lindsay Troy will make her debut in the corner of Christian Sands at Ghoulish Games.
VIC WATERS: Yeah, we don't want to give all these ungrateful fans down in Mexico TOO much. They are lucky to be getting a show at all.
JONATHAN MARX: It seems like every show another top talent is.... ["Breathe" by Fabolous begins to play in the arena as the lights dim, and a series of spotlights swirl about the arena, moments later a few fireworks go off....
BRANDON JACOBS: What is going on here?! [...a man stands dressed in black, black trenchcoat, hair, sunglasses, jeans, t-shirt, and gloves...he has his hands folded near his legs and his head down and the lights come up a bit as the man raises his head to show Sebastian Dodd. He stands surveying the scene, and begins a slow, confident, borderline cocky march to the ring, as the song continues to play...]
JONATHAN MARX: OH MY, It is Sebastian Dodd! One of the top ring technicians in all of wrestling today...
He climbs the ring steps, wiping his feet on the ring apron before turning and leaning against the ropes looking for some reaction, it comes in the form of a mix of boos from people who know what he's all about, and some cheers from some people who like that type...Dodd steps through the ropes and stands triumphantly in the middle of the ring, as the music cuts off and he withdraws a microphone from his trenchcoat.]
Dodd "I'm sure that some of you are well aware of who I am. And I'm sure some of you out there are wondering exactly who I am to warrant this type of entrance and exposure on WFW TV. Well...for those of you in that category.
I am Sebastian Dodd. I am the finest wrestler on the planet. I am your hero... I am your idol... If I dare say so myself... AND I DO. I am your f*cking Messiah. Bow before he who will lead your federation to heights that it has never dreamed.
[Those that were indifferent moments ago, now are booing vociferously with those who were booing before at the arrogance of this man.]
Dodd "I've been around long enough to know that this isn't the forum to extol on what I've done, and where I've done it. So I won't bother to do that. I'm here because now is the time for Revolution. Now is the time for change. Now is the birth of the Messiah on the WFW scene, and make no mistake about it, I am here to make an impact, and I WILL DO IT.
I've come here to WFW because it's the hot ticket. And I'm only going to make it hotter. That's right, I'm not out here uninvited, Seb Dodd signed on the line, and I will make my WFW debut...just as soon as management sees fit.
I'm going to turn this federation on it's ear, I'm going to turn it right the hell upside down...right down to the letters...it's going to go from WFW...World's Finest Wrestling to MFM...Messiah...Facing...Midcarders."
[Dodd smiles to himself and soaks up the boos.] Dodd "I will be your very life... I will be your very soul... You people will learn to hang on my every word, my every action. Get ready for the Revolution of the Messiah of Wrestling. I know that you people out there are waiting right now for one of your big stars to come from the back right now to shut the mouth of this newcomer...
But they won't. Not because I'm not worth shutting up...I'll put it out there right now...there's not a man in the locker room that can. And I defy any one of them to walk to the ring right now, man up, and put Seb Dodd in his place.
Good. Now...I know that you people have been listening with held breath to every word I've said...and now...you can...
BREATHE. [The lights dim, the music cues up again, and as the lights come back on Dodd is...Gone.] JONATHAN MARX: The Almighty Dodd is here in WFW! It makes my heart feel good to have a ring technician of his caliber in World's Finest. He puts the "W" in wrestling.
VIC WATERS: Wasn't he the man who cost you the Empire Intercontinental Title? JONATHAN MARX: Yeah, but it took a hell of a match to do it. He is one of the best I have ever faced.
BRANDON JACOBS: That match you had with Dodd was a barn burning, probably only behind the match you had with Manson over the last six months.
JONATHAN MARX: I like Dodd's fire. He is all business when it comes to wrestling. Reminds me a lot of Gideon.
BRANDON JACOBS: If he has half the run Gideon has had in WFW, he should have a hell of career. Folks, we have to take another short commercial break, but when we come back, we will have Maelstrom vs. Wells... WE PROMISE! Stay tuned!
