WWF Pandamonium: Rat Fink vs. Golem vs. Pulsar

13 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 13 July 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: It is time for our three way falls count anywhere elimination match. JONATHAN MARX: This match will be held under BAD rules, which means… there are NO rules. This is the type of match where Rat Fink and his minions excel.
VIC WATERS: I really don’t know how this one is going to turn out, Pulsar is coming off pinning the number one contender Felix Red, Rat Fink is coming off pinning Psycho, and Golem was involved in one of the wildest brawls I have ever seen when he collided with Psycho and Felix Red as his partner against Copycat & El Arco Iris.
JONATHAN MARX: He threw Pulsar off the damn cruise ship too. BRANDON JACOBS: But he came back to help out his partners when they needed him the most, that has be the best save I have ever seen.
VIC WATERS: He is going to have to save his own ass against Rat Fink and Golem if he has any hopes of winning this match tonight.
JONATHAN MARX: In a match where three men are so close in abilities, I’d go with Rat Fink because he is by far the genius of the three. He is going to have something planned to counter act anything Golem and Pulsar have planned.
VIC WATERS: I think you are under estimating the fact that Golem wants nothing better to win the shot at the BAD World Heavyweight Title and get back at LOVE by stealing possession of it away from Wells in order to get his revenge on Psycho.
BRANDON JACOBS: Would Golem really let himself be absorbed in hatred like that he would go to such an extreme as to cause friction with LOVE?
VIC WATERS: He sure would. BRANDON JACOBS: Works for me, but I am going with Pulsar, folks, lets send this one down to ringside and get it underway!
(CUEUP: “Whiskey in a Jar” by Metallica) RING ANNOUNCER: The following match is a FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE ELIMINATION MATCH! Introducing first participant, from Australia, the current NWL International Champion, standing at over 6’3, PULSAR!
::Pulsar receives a huge roar from the crowd as he comes down to the aisle and enters the ring::
(CUEUP: “The Day The World Went Away” by Nine Inch Nails) RING ANNOUNCER: The second man of this way elimination match, known not only for his toughness but great technical skill, from Death Valley, California…. GOLEM!
::Golem walks down to the crowd to a big ovation from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “Megalomaniac” by KMFDM) RING ANNOUNCER: And last but not least, the third man in this three way elimination match, the leader of the New Age Revolution and the former BAD World Heavyweight Champion, RAT FINK!
::Rat Fink walks down to the ring wearing his “BAD KICKS ASS” sweat shirt with the Mr. Che and Pandabot and attempts to enter the ring:: :
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR CHARGES RAT FINK AND MR. CHE WITH A DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE AND HE KNOCKS THEM OFF THE APRON BEFORE THEY CAN ENTER AND DOWN TO THE GROUND!
JONATHAN MARX: Pulsar is looking to get the upper hand by striking first. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is climbing up to the top rope…. PULSAR FLIES OFF ONTO MR. CHE WITH A FLYING CROSS BODY PRESS!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar has way too much energy. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink is picking up Pulsar by the hair… HE RAMS HIM INTO THE GUARDRAIL! A frustrated Golem exits the ring and he heads off with a chair towards Rat Fink!
VIC WATERS: Maybe he is offering him a seat. BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM FLATTENS PANDABOT WITH THE CHAIR! VIC WATERS: Die panda die. BRANDON JACOBS: Golem means business and he is heading towards Rat Fink with that chair… RAT FINK DUCKS THE CHAIR SHOT BY GOLEM!
JONATHAN MARX: Golem had the Bonds like swing with that shot. BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK KICKS THE CHAIR OUT OF GOLEM’S HANDS! Golem charges Rat Fink…. RAT FINK HITS GOLEM WITH A SIDE SLAM!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink is a master at surviving, he is at his best when things look the bleakest.
BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK PICKS UP THE CHAIR HE KNOCKED OUT OF THE HANDS OF GOLEM AND HE IS WAITING FOR GOLEM TO GET BACK UP! Wait a second, both Pulsar and Golem are getting up at the same time….
JONATHAN MARX: Rat Fink needs to kick it into rapid fire mode. BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM AND PULSAR BOTH GRAB RAT FINK’S CHAIR BEFORE HAS A CHANCE TO SWING AND RAT FINK LETS GO AND BAILS OUT INTO THE CROWD!
VIC WATERS: This is a good tactical retreat by Rat Fink’s part. BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM AND PULSAR ARE HAVING A TUG OF WAR BACK AND FORTH WITH THE CHAIR…. GOLEM LETS GO AND PULSAR FALLS DOWN TO THE GROUND!
JONATHAN MARX: Golem isn’t about to let Rat Fink get away from here. BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM IS GOING INTO THE AUDIENCE AFTER RAT FINK AND THEY ARE HEADED TOWARDS THE FLAMINGO CAFÉ! The audience is clearing out of the way as Rat Fink is stumbling as he tries to get away.
VIC WATERS: They make the best club sandwiches. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is getting up…. AND SO IS MR. CHE! Mr. Che looks like he has no idea where he is….. PULSAR WAILS HIM IN THE BACK OF THE HEAD WITH THE CHAIR AND MR. CHE GOES BACK DOWN TO THE GROUND!
VIC WATERS: Damn it, Mr. Che is the greatest warrior in all of history and nobody will let him get up and fight.
JONATHAN MARX: He must not have steel chair combat experience. VIC WATERS: It is not fair damnit. BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM CATCHES UP TO RAT FINK AND THEY ARE BRAWLING UP THE RAMP INTO THE CAFÉ! Pulsar is headed toward the café after them.
JONATHAN MARX: Imagine sitting in the café, wanting to eat your lunch in peace and two wrestlers come brawling through…
VIC WATERS: They could ruin a perfectly good lunch in the process too. BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM GRABS A NAPKIN HOLDER AND RAMS IT INTO THE HEAD OF RAT FINK KNOCKING HIM DOWN TO THE GROUND BUSTING HIM OPEN!
JONATHAN MARX: His forehead must have caught a jagged edge. BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM THROWS RAT FINK INTO A TABLE IN THE DINNING AREA AND PEOPLE ARE BAILING LEFT AND RIGHT! Pulsar comes through the door and he grabs a garbage can…. PULSAR THROWS THE GARBAGE CAN INTO THE DINING AREA AT GOLEM BUT GOLEM MOVES OUT OF THE WAY!
JONATHAN MARX: They are going to make this place a total mess. BRANDON JACOBS: Golem is signaling to Pulsar to come and get him….. PULSAR RUSHES AT GOLEM AND BOTH MEN ARE TRADING PUNCHES BACK AND FORTH!
VIC WATERS: These two are beating the hell out of each other. JONATHAN MARX: They know that it is going to take a hell of a lot of abuse to get a pinfall in this match with a BAD World Heavyweight Title shot on the line.
BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR IS GETTING THE ADVANTAGE OF GOLEM! PULSAR GRABS A SERVING TRAY AND WRAPS GOLEM IN THE HEAD WITH IT! Pulsar picks Golem up off of the ground…. INVERTED SUPLEX ONTO THE FLOOR!
JONATHAN MARX: Pulsar needs to go for the pinfall here! BRANDON JACOBS: IT LOOKS LIKE PULSAR ISN’T DONE! PULSAR IS CLIMBING UP ON THE TABLE…. SOMERSAULT LEGDROP ONTO GOLEM ON THE FLOOR!
JONATHAN MARX: PIN HIM PULSAR! BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is going for the pinfall and the ref goes down for the count! 1------------------------------------------------------2----------RAT FINK GETS UP AND IN A FIT OF RAGE TURNS OVER THE TABLE ONTO GOLEM AND PULSAR TO BREAK UP THE COUNT!
VIC WATERS: I don’t even think Rat Fink was a aware that a pinfall was going down otherwise he wouldn’t have broke it up.
JONATHAN MARX: Rat Fink is a smart cookie, but he doesn’t like to be outstaged. BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK PICKS THE TABLE OFF OF GOLEM AND PULSAR AND GRABS GOLEM BY THE HAIR! Rat Fink rams Golem head first into the wall!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink is upset about being busted open and now wants to get his revenge on Golem.
BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK GRABS A PLASTIC KNIFE OFF OF THE TABLE AND REPEATEDLY RAMS IT INTO THE FOREHEAD OF GOLEM! Golem has been busted open!
VIC WATERS: An eye for an eye, a tooth for a tooth. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is up and he walking over to a gift shop on the side inside the café…. PULSAR HAS A POSTCARD STAND AND HE CARRYING IT OVER HIS SHOULDER!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink doesn’t even notice, he is laying those boots right into the head of Golem in order to bust him open even more.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is setting behind Rat Fink…. PULSAR TAPS HIM ON THE SHOULDER…. RAT FINK TURNS AROUND…. GREETINGS FROM THE SAN DIEGO ZOO!
JONATHAN MARX: My god, he swung that post card rack right into Rat Fink and knocked him hard against the wall.
VIC WATERS: There are post cards scattered all over the place with blood on them. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is going back to the gift shop and he has a tray full of glass snow globes!
JONATHAN MARX: Whoever thought a snow globe would be evil. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar dumps all the snow globes on a table and makes sure all of them are glass ball up! HE PICKS UP RAT FINK OFF THE GROUND AND ONTO HIS SHOULDER AND CLIMBS ONTO A CHAIR!
VIC WATERS: Good gawd. BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR IS CLIMBING UP ONTO THE TABLE WITH RAT FINK…. THE BLACK HOLE FACE FIRST INTO THE SNOW GLOBES!
JONATHAN MARX: DAMN! VIC WATERS: There are shard of glass and water everywhere! BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar rolls a blood Rat Fink on his side and clears the snow globes off the table in order to attempt to go for a pin!
VIC WATERS: There is no way he could get his shoulders down right now with all the mess that is on the table.
JONATHAN MARX: It doesn’t matter if they are up on the air or on the ground, as long as the shoulders are touching the surface flatly. That is all you need.
BRANDON JACOBS: Golem is getting up and he is staggering towards Pulsar… Pulsar rolls Rat Fink onto his back… GOLEM SIGNALS AT PULSAR TO COME AND GET HIM AND PULSAR CHARGES GOLEM WITH A RUNNING SHOULDER BLOCK AND BOTH MEN GO CRASHING INTO A RACK OF CLOTHES IN THE ADJOINING STORE!
JONATHAN MARX: Pulsar should have gone for the pin there on Rat Fink. VIC WATERS: Golem may still want to get his revenge on Rat Fink before he is eliminated. BRANDON JACOBS: BOTH MEN ARE ROLLING AROUND TRYING TO GET PUNCHES IN ON EACH OTHER! Golem breaks free and he dumps the clothes rack onto Pulsar!
VIC WATERS: That was a smart move, he had to break up Pulsar’s momentum before he got leveled liked Rat Fink as well.
BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM GRABS A BAMBOO WALKING STICK AND HE CLIMBS ONTO THE CLOTHES RACK ON PULSAR AND BEGINS TO CHOKE HIM WITH IT!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar has so much weight on his chest right now, it will be impossible to get it off.
BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM IS CHOKING PULSAR WITH THAT BAMBOO WALKING STICK AND PULSAR IS TRYING TO GET HIS FINGERS UNDERNEATH IN AN ATTEMPT TO ALLEVIATE THE PAIN!
JONATHAN MARX: Pulsar has to get out of his if he has any chance of winning this match. BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM IS MAKING THE COVER! 1-----------------------------------------------------2----------------------------- WHAT THE, A FAN JUST RAN IN AND BROKE UP THE THREE COUNT!
JONATHAN MARX: That is no fan, that IS PSYCHO AND HE HAS BAG WITH HIM! BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO SWINGS HIS BAG RIGHT INTO HIS FACE AND GOLEM GOES CRASHING DOWN INTO THE BOOKCASE AS MERCHANDISE COMES SPILLING OUT AND GOLEM ATTEMPTS TO COVER UP!
JONATHAN MARX: Look, a bloody Rat Fink is finally getting up. VIC WATERS: After Pulsar nearly killed Rat Fink before, I didn’t think that he’d be back in this match.
BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK HAS A WOOD CHAIR AND HE COMES FROM BEHIND AND HITS PSYCHO IN THE BACK!
JONATHAN MARX: You can’t have loose mad men involved in a match when the BAD World Heavyweight Title is at stake.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is trying to struggle to get up from under the clothes rack…. Rat Fink sees him and walks over… PSYCHO JUMPS UP ONTO THE BACK OF RAT FINK AND RAT FINK IS TRYING TO GET HIM OFF!
VIC WATERS: Psycho has a history of bad blood with Rat Fink too. God bless LOVE and their laundry list of enemies.
BRANDON JACOBS: A bloody Golem is getting up…. HE IS ATTEMPTING TO PUSH THE BOOKCASE OVER! JONATHAN MARX: That book case is big enough that it would fall onto the clothes rack which Pulsar is under. He has to get out of there NOW.
VIC WATERS: It looks like Pulsar’s leg is trapped! BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM PUSHES OVER THE BOOKCASE AND IT CRASHES DOWN ON THE BACK OF PSYCHO WHO IS ON RAT FINK AND THE BOOK CASE AND ALL SPILL ONTO THE CLOTHESRACK THAT PULSAR IS BURIED UNDERNEATH!
JONATHAN MARX: Damn it, we are tearing this gift shop apart. BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is coming over…. PULSAR HAS BEEN KNOCKED OUT , HIS SHOULDERS ARE DOWN AND A BARELY CONSCIOUS RAT FINK IS ON TOP OF HIM WITH THE BOOKCASE ON TOP OF HIM! The referee goes to make the count! 1--------------------------------------------------------2-------------------------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: PULSAR HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JONATHAN MARX: That is so not fair to Pulsar… god damn Psycho cost him this match. BRANDON JACOBS: Golem is coming over and he is lifting the book case off of Psycho and Rat Fink. Both men are out of it. Golem lifts the metal clothes rack off of Pulsar and Pulsar finally escapes from underneath.
VIC WATERS: See, Golem has a heart of solid gold. JONATHAN MARX: No, Golem just lifted the rack so the medics could take him out so he wouldn’t be around to interfere. It just makes good sense.
BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM THE COVER ON RAT FINK! The referee goes down for the count! 1---------------------------------2--------------------------------- PSYCHO DIVES AND BREAKS UP THE COUNT!
JONATHAN MARX: Oh come on, Golem had Rat Fink and Psycho has cost him another win yet again!
BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM GETS UP AND HE IS IRATE AT PSYCHO! Golem kicks Psycho in the midsection…. POWERBOMB INTO A STUN GUN RIGHT ON THE COUNTER!
VIC WATERS: What did Psycho do to deserve that? JONATHAN MARX: Should I make a list? BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is crawling over to the corner of the store… GOLEM IS GOING FOR THE BAMBOO WALKING STICK THAT WAS USED EARLIER!
JONATHAN MARX: Payback time. BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM CHARGES PSYCHO…. PSYCHO THROWS A FIREBALL RIGHT IN GOLEM’S FACE! Golem is screaming in pain!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is a master of evil. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is looking around for his back…. PULSAR JUMPS UP FROM BEHIND THE COUNTER AND CLOCKS PSYCHO RIGHT IN THE HEAD WITH IT!
VIC WATERS: He isn’t even in this match, he should be disqualified. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is reaching into the bag…. HE IS PULLING OUT A VICE CLAMP! JONATHAN MARX: Good god, what was Psycho planning? PULSAR: I’M GOING TO PUT THIS RIGHT ON YOUR BALLS! JONATHAN MARX: All of a sudden, this is turning into File, Michaels, Grossard… BRANDON JACOBS: A blinded Golem is wandering around the store looking for Psycho… PSYCHO PUSHES HIM INTO PULSAR AND GOLEM JUMPS ON PULSAR AND HE IS BEATING HIM LIKE THERE IS NO TOMORROW!
JONATHAN MARX: I am sick of all this down right chaos LOVE causes on a weekly basis. This is totally uncalled for.
VIC WATERS: I’m pretty sure originally Psycho was only targeting Golem but this all spiraled out of control.
BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK IS UP AND PSYCHO DOESN’T SEE HIM! VIC WATERS: Neither does Golem. JONATHAN MARX: Quiet you. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is trying to convince Golem that he isn’t Psycho… GOLEM IS FINALLY STOPPING!
VIC WATERS: Psycho looks like he is having time of his life. BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK GRABS AN PANDA UMBRELLA AND HE NAILS PSYCHO RIGHT IN THE BACK OF THE HEAD! A dazed Psycho grabs his head and Rat Fink continues to beat him repeatedly with the umbrella… GOOD GOD HE BROKE IT ALREADY! Psycho is still staggering on his feet…
JONATHAN MARX: Look at the welts already forming on Psycho. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink charges Psycho with a Steinerline…. PSYCHO BACK BODY DROPS RAT FINK DOWN ONTO THE GROUND!
JONATHAN MARX: Half a second and he would have clocked him one good. BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, PULSAR PUSHES A BLINDED GOLEM ONTO RAT FINK TO MAKE A COVER! 1-------------------------------------------------------------------2-------------------------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: Rat Fink has been eliminated, the winner of this match and the man who will get the first shot BAD World Heavyweight Title shot after the PPV…. GOLEM!
JONATHAN MARX: Even by BAD standards, this was much more chaotic than usual. VIC WATERS: BAD rules baby… BRANDON JACOBS: Security is coming in to keep everyone from getting at each other and separate this mess. The league may have to step in on this one to set everything right before the night is through in order to set things right.
VIC WATERS: The league should keep their paws out of this, BAD rules are BAD rules. JONATHAN MARX: A truer statement has never been said. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we have to take a commercial break, but when we comeback, Bartleby will make his long awaited debut against the longest reigning WFW North American Champion of alltime, “The System” Richard Gideon! STAY TUNED!
