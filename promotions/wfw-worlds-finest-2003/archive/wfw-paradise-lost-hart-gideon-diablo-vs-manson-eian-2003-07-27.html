WFW Paradise Lost: Hart/Gideon/Diablo vs. Manson/Eian

27 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 27 July 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: OH MY GOD! While we were on commercial break, Diablo charged into Eian’s dressing room and nailed him with a fireball right in the eyes which has left him blinded. Eian is being taken to the hospital for further observation but before he left, Eian said that if he does not beat Diablo at the PPV in the Flaming Tables match that he will retire from wrestling!
VIC WATERS: Eian may be jumping the gun a bit in the heat of the moment, but that is a serious blow to Manson. That now makes the match a three on one.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson cannot buy a break here tonight. (CUEUP: "Livin' on a Prayer" by Bon Jovi) BRANDON JACOBS: What is he doing here?! This isn’t his match. ::Scotty Michaels makes his way through curtain and heads to the announce table with HEEL at his side::
VIC WATERS: Scotty, or should I say, the next WFW North American Champion, come sit down next to me.
SCOTTY MICHAELS: It is always a pleasure to talk to something with as much talent as yourself.
VIC WATERS: Didn’t I say this man is great? BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome Scotty, while I admit that I may not have liked your actions against Manson, I have great respect for you as a wrestler.
SCOTTY MICHAELS: You have to do what it takes to get the job done, winning the WFW North American Title from Manson, a man who has held the title since the conception of WFW, will put me in the spotlight and in contention for the WFW World Heavyweight Title down the road.
(CUEUP: “Look At Me" by Geri Haliwell) BRANDON JACOBS: Oh my… is the match starting already? ::Shawn Hart comes out through the curtain and heads to the announce table with TINY at his side and sits next to Brandon Jacobs::
SHAWN HART: Next WFW North American World Champion, meet the next WFW World Heavyweight Champion. I’ve really admired your work against Manson.
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Why thank you, you’ve done a pretty good job yourself against Doc. BRANDON JACOBS: Hart, if we can stop the lovefest here for a second, why are you out here? SHAWN HART: I don’t need to wrestle tonight. I’ve told Diablo & Gideon that I have all the faith in the world in them to get the job done against Manson two on one.
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Couldn’t you go out there and soften him up a bit more for me? *laughs* SHAWN HART: Everyone saw what Diablo did to Eian in back, Manson is crapping in his pants right now. I don’t want him committed to asylum because he folds under the pressure before he loses the title.
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Good point, hey Hart, you know, when I win that title from Manson, that officially makes me the number #1 contender. And as the “Viewer Choice,” you know the public wants nothing more than to see us go one on one for the title.
SHAWN HART: While C.O.C.K.S. mania may be running wild, the higher ups determine who I have to face, but I’ll definitely put the suggestion into the committee.
BRANDON JACOBS: Aren’t you guys getting a little ahead of yourself? Michael Manson & Doc Silver are two of the greatest legends this sport has ever produced.
VIC WATERS: The higher ups really have a hard on for Manson, they think he is God or something, but Hart, you really are delusional if you really think that you can beat Doc. Did you see his match with Psycho? Doc Silver isn’t human, he can take punishment and he keeps on coming back for more. How are you going to beat that Hart? You’ve faced Doc twice before and have been turned away. Why is it your time now?
SHAWN HART: Doc may be a legend, but I am a legend killer, and I’m going to go out there and take what I’ve learned from facing Doc twice before and finish up the job. Plus, it doesn’t hurt that I’ve had Gideon by my side for the last month. He is fountain of information when it comes to beating Doc.
VIC WATERS: Psycho did an awfully good trick disappearing like that and leaving an irate Richard Gideon in his wake.
SHAWN HART: Awfully convent, wasn’t it? BRANDON JACOBS: As much as I enjoy talking to you, let’s dispense of the chit chat and send this one to ringside!
(CUEUP: “The Theme From Dragnet”) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing First, one of the founding members of C.O.C.K.S. and one of the best young wrestlers in all of wrestling today, DIABLO!
:iablo comes down in his C.O.C.K.S to a mixed reaction from a crowd: and enters the ring:: ::Shawn Hart gets up and gives Diablo a standing ovation:: SCOTTY MICHAELS: I hear Diablo makes great toast. (CUEUP: “Remember” by Disturbed) RING ANNOUNCER: And his partner, from Buffalo, NY, Doc Silver’s worst nightmare, “THE SYSTEM” RICHARD GIDEON!
::Richard Gideon comes down to a big pop from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: "Mechnical Animals" by Marilyn Manson) RING ANNOUNCER: And their opponent, taking both men on in a handicap match, from Chicago IL, the WFW North American Champion, MANSON!
SCOTTY MICHAELS: BOOOO! BOOOO! SHAWN HART: HISS! ::Manson comes out to the biggest pop of the night and enters the ring:: BRANDON JACOBS: Looks like Diablo will start the match… ::bell rings:: BRANDON JACOBS: Manson rushes Diablo and starts pounding him! CLOTHESLINE OVER THE TOP ROPE! Manson goes to the outside… MANSON RAMS DIABLO’S HEAD INTO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: This match is losing control very quickly. SHAWN HART: Come on Diablo! Don’t let us down! BRANDON JACOBS: Richard Gideon is coming over to help Diablo… MANSON GRABS GIDEON BY THE HAIR AND HE RAMS THE HEAD OF GIDEON AND DIABLO TOGETHER!
SCOTTY MICHAELS: It looks like C.O.C.K.S. is impotent to Manson’s attacks. VIC WATERS: Stop stealing my lines. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is ripping up the mats at ringside… HE WANTS TO KILL THEM BOTH! SCOTTY MICHAELS: How can the fans cheer a monster like this? He crosses the line and then some and dishes out even more punishment.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson grabs Gideon and Diablo in a double front face lock… LOOK LIKE HE IS GOING FOR A DOUBLE DDT ONTO THE CONCRETE FLOOR…. NO WAIT, BACKBODY DROP BY DIABLO & GIDEON!
SHAWN HART: Serves him right, Diablo would never do anything heinous like Manson was attempting to do right there.
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Unless it involves roasting someone like a marshmallow. SHAWN HART: Well maybe that is true. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon grabs the injured leg of Manson and HE RAMS THE SIDE OF IT INTO THE STEEL STAIRS! Gideon is softening up Manson for Michaels at the PPV! I’m hope you are happy that you have these two guys doing your biding for you.
SCOTTY MICHAELS: I’m ecstatic. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is screaming out in pain, things aren’t looking so good now. DIABLO IS GOING TO GRAB THE RING BELL! WHERE IS THE DAMN REFEREE?! Wait, the referee is finally coming out to stop Diablo and he puts himself in the way to keep Diablo from using it!
SHAWN HART: Damnit, get around him! BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo pushes the ref aside…. MANSON MOVES HIS KNEE OUT OF THE WAY IN THE NICK OF TIME! MANSON ROLLS BACK IN!
SCOTTY MICHAELS: If Diablo was working for me, he would be fired right now. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon rolls back in and he grabs the injured leg of Manson… MANSON GRABS THE HAIR… SMALL PACKAGE! The ref is still on the outside though and Gideon breaks free before the ref can get back in!
SHAWN HART: I hope I don’t get this referee in my match against Doc. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon grabs the left leg of Manson and he locks in a Half Boston Crab! SCOTTY MICHAELS: Manson may be a bit too close to the ropes he, he should have dragged him to the center of the ring first.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson looks like he is in a hell of a lot of pain here. One of these days, he needs to get that injured left leg operated on but Manson is so god damn tough, he refuses to take any time off. He would rather die than relinquish the WFW North American Title and disappoint the WFW fans.
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Manson is an intelligent man, but he does have one flaw. He never knows when to give up. He has never once submitted and sometimes you have to give in to the pain.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is reaching for the ropes… HE MISSES! He tries again… HE JUST GETS HIS FINGER NEAR THE ROPES AND THE REF IS MAKING HIM BREAK!
SHAWN HART: That was hardly enough contact to constitute a break. These fans are influencing the ref to make calls on Manson’s behalf. You’d think Michael Jordan was still playing with all these calls this boy from Chicago is getting.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is looking down at the damage he has done… Gideon is dragging Manson by the arm to the center of the ring. HE IS CALLING FOR THE FIGURE FOUR! Gideon grabs the bad leg of Manson… MANSON REACHES UP AND NAILS GIDEON WITH A CLOSES FIST TO THE JAW! Gideon is trying to shake it off.
SCOTTY MICHAELS: I’ve been on the receiving end of some of those punches and Manson throws some great ones for a girl.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon goes to pick Manson up for a brainbuster… MANSON BLOCKS IT! MANSON KNEE LIFTS GIDEON IN THE CHOPS! MANSON NAILS GIDEON WITH A SNAP SUPLEX!
VIC WATERS: That hardly is going to keep Gideon down… BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is get back up… MANSON KICKS HIM IN THE STOMACH AND NAILS HIM WITH A DDT! This crowd is getting behind Manson’s comeback!
SHAWN HART: Boy this crowd is loud, way too much sugar. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson whips Gideon off the ropes… HURRICANRANA! Manson covers! 1------------2-----------KICKOUT! Gideon barely gets out!
VIC WATERS: That may be the first time I’ve seen Manson do that move here in WFW. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson picks Gideon off the mat… WILD BUCK BOMB! Gideon looks like he hurt his back on that one! Manson goes for the cover! 1----------------2---------DIABLO MAKES THE SAVE! Manson fell on the ref when he was trying to get up!
SHAWN HART: Go Diablo Go! BRANDON JACOBS: DIABLO IS LOCKING ON THE DRAGON SLEEPER! The ref is trying to get him to break it because he isn’t the legal man… WAIT A SECOND, MANSON POWERS OUT OF THE DRAGON SLEEPER AND TURNS IT INTO A POWER SLAM!
::crowd comes unglued:: SHAWN HART: Holy cow. ::Michaels leaves the booth and grabs a chair and jumps onto the apron:: BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON WHIPS MANSON OFF THE ROPES FOR A DRAGONSCREW LEGWHIP BUT MANSON SNEAKS UNDER…. OH NO! SCOTTY MICHAELS NAILS MANSON WITH A CHAIR! NO DAMNIT NO! The ref didn’t see it!
SHAWN HART: FINISH HIM OFF GIDEON! BRANDON JACOBS: Richard Gideon is going up to the top rope…. SYSTEM FAILURE! Diablo is waking up the ref… RICHARD GIDEON MAKES THE COVER! 1----------------2-----------------3!
SHAWN HART: Excuse me fellas, that is my queue. ::leaves the announce table and runs into the ring::
BRANDON JACOBS: HART, DIABLO, AND MICHAELS ARE BEATING DOWN MANSON! Hart is motioning for Gideon to join in!
VIC WATERS: Is that the favor Gideon was talking about? BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon decides to join in and they are working over the bad leg of Manson. WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES FELIX RED WITH A CHAIR AND EVERYONE CLEARS OUT OF THE RING! ::huge crowd pop:: Felix Red is holding C.O.C.K.S. and Michaels & Heel at bay! Hart is motioning to his men to leave, thank god!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red should mind his own business. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red saved Manson because Manson brought Felix Red into the WFW and he has been helping him out on strategy for his match with Rat Fink. These fans here are certainly giving Felix Red a loud ovation for saving Manson tonight. There were too many people out to get him tonight and there is no way he could have overcome the odds alone after what Diablo did to his partner.
VIC WATERS: That is true. BRANDON JACOBS: Okay folks, the big match is next, the reason why this card was called Paradise Lost, the biggest two heels in the WFW will go one on one and Doc Silver will find out if he really is the most evil man in all of wrestling! Stay tuned folks!
