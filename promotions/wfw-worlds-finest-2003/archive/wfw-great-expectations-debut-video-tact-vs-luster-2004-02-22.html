WFW Great Expectations: Debut Video, Tact vs. Luster

22 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 22 February 2004 (LIGHTS GO OFF IN EDISON FIELD AND THE JUMBROTRON IN CENTERFIELD GOES ON) (The video screen goes dark. After a second, a heavy, regular drum rhythm begins to play.) DUMM. DUMM. DUMM DUMM DUMM. DUMM. DUMM. DUMM DUMM DUMM. (As the beat plays, words slowly fade into being across the darkness, presented in a vivid golden-yellow...) PRIDE. STRENGTH. HONOR. WFW HERALDS THE ARRIVAL OF... IRON LION (In a blur of gold flame, the splash screen abruptly burns away as the chorus of "Is This The End" by Creed hits. The screen now shows the image of a monstrously huge figure, golden-maned and bearded, standing at at least 7' tall. The camera rotates around the man as he raises one fist in the air.)
(CUTTO: The monster crushing someone with a running powerbomb!) (CUTTO: The same monster springing off the ropes with an awe-inspiring lionsault!) (CUTTO: Iron Lion with his arms crossed over his broad chest, standing with his back to an intense light; his face and body are cast in shadow.)
(Then back to darkness as the drum beats again.) COMING SOON TO WFW (Fadeout.) (LIGHTS GO BACK ON) (BROADCAST BOOTH) BRANDON JACOBS: My god, I’ve never seen a big man move like that before. VIC WATERS: All I can say is I’m glad Doc Silver retired so he wouldn’t have to face him. Let him be someone else’s problem. Let Scotty Michaels worry about him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Amen. Our next match up is a match between to distinctly different personalities in Larry Tact and Luster.
VIC WATERS: That is putting it mildly. Larry Tact even called a fine woman like P-Nut a skank. Luster did not seem happy at all.
BRANDON JACOBS: Whether it is true or not, considering what Luster has done in the past, I wouldn’t want to make him angry. He has screwed both Hart and Manson out of their respective titles with his co-conspirator Scotty Michaels.
VIC WATERS: While I really enjoyed the Manson screwjob, that Hart one was even too much even for me…. pretending to be his handicap brother. Even with what Hart did to Doc, he did not deserve that.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster wants to add Tact’s name on long list of people he and Michaels has tortured. Can Tact avoid the wrath of Luster? Let’s send this one to ringside and get it underway!
(CUEUP: “"Funkytown" by Disco Fever) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the man who helped end Hart’s WFW World Heavyweight Title reign and the sexiest man in all of WFW, from Atlanta, Georgia… LUSTER!
::Luster comes down dancing to the ring as the crowd boos loudly:: (CUEUP: "Karma" by Diffuser) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, one of the best technicians in all of wrestling today, Manhattan, New York… LARRY TACT!
::Larry Tact comes down to the ovation from the crowd and enters the ring:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Both men are standing face to face, I don’t think we have ever had a match in history where both competitors were ever this identical and height and weight before.
VIC WATERS: Luster is only two pounds heavier than Tact. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster is acting cocky and he is getting into Tact’s face… TACT NAILS LUSTER WITH OPEN HAND SLAP AND AN IRATE LUSTER SWINGS AT TACT WITH A RIGHT HAND!
VIC WATERS: Tact is weaving and bobbing and he got out of the way of that punch. BRANDON JACOBS: Tact grabs the right arm of Luster and chicken wings it behind his back. LUSTER THROWS A WILD ELBOW BACK BUT TACT GRABS THE ARM AND LOCKS ON A HALF NELSON!
VIC WATERS: I may not like Tact, but man, he is a brilliant technician, I’ve never seen anyone do that before.
BRANDON JACOBS: Tact has really good instincts. Luster is trying to reach out over to the ropes with his freed left hand but he is about a foot too short.
VIC WATERS: I’ve noticed something, it it wasn’t for all these rope breaks, there would be a lot more submission around this place.
BRANDON JACOBS: Breaking submissions by getting to the ropes is merely the easiest way to break many holds. Luster is ALMOST to the ropes…. TACT BREAKS THE HALF NELSON AND GRABS THE LEFT ARM OF LUSTER AND ARMDRAGS HIM DOWN TO THE MAT! A frustrated Luster pounds on the mat and looks up at Tact who has been putting on a technical wrestling display here.
VIC WATERS: Luster can’t let Tact wrestle his kind of match or he’ll fall behind the eight ball and won’t be able to get back.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster stands up and puts his hands at his sides and as he curses to himself. Luster extends his arms up for a lock up with Tact, looks like Luster is going to try to out wrestle Tact!
VIC WATERS: Luster, Luster, Luster… BRANDON JACOBS: Tact extends his arms for a lock up… LUSTER RAKES THE EYES OF TACT! Tact covers his eyes! It is going to take a couple of minutes before the spots go away.
VIC WATERS: I don’t care how muscular you are, the eyes are always a weak point. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster stands by Tact’s side AND DRIVES TACT FACE FIRST INTO THE MAT WITH THE STO! Tact is covering his head and he attempts to roll away from Luster…. LUSTER DROPS THE ELBOW AND PLANTS HIM WHERE HE IS!
VIC WATERS: Luster got everything on that one. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster makes a cover! 1--------------------------------2--------KICKOUT! Tact got his shoulder up at two and a half! That elbow temporarily stunned him.
VIC WATERS: Luster needs to stay on him. BRANDON JACOBS: Tact is slowly getting up and Luster is waiting behind him to go for a clothesline…. TACT TURNS AROUND AND LUSTER CHARGES WITH A CLOTHESLINE BUT TACT DUCKS DOWN! Luster turns around…. TACT PICKS LUSTER UP WITH A PUMPHANDLE…. HE POWERSLAMS HIM DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: If Luster had hit that clothesline, Tact would have been dead in the water but Tact again is showing that he is thinking where he is in every moment of the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is counting both men down! ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE--- Tact is using the ropes in order to help himself up!
VIC WATERS: Both men are still feeling the effects of their assault on one another. BRANDON JACOBS: Tact is up and he drags Luster to the center of the ring…. KICKSWING DDT! Tact goes for the cover! 1---------------------------2---KICKOUT! Tact picks up Luster and goes for a cradle! 1-----------------------------------2-------------------------KICKOUT! Almost got him with that one!
VIC WATERS: Tact needs to wear him down some more. BRANDON JACOBS: Larry Tact picks up Luster and he locks in a dragon sleeper! If he doesn’t get him with this one, he’ll at least wear him down to the point where he can take some of the impact out of his offense.
VIC WATERS: Luster looks like he is starting to go out. Tact has that locked on pretty tight.
BRANDON JACOBS: What a feather in Tact’s cap it would be to submit Luster in his very first PPV match for the WFW. Luster looks like he is fading fast and the referee bending down to check on Luster…. LUSTER GRABS A HANDFUL OF THE REFS SHIRT!
VIC WATERS: That was a blind grab in desperation and he is very lucky he made contact. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is yelling at Tact to let go and Tact reluctantly breaks the hold by pulling Luster by his feet away from the referee. Tact grabs the leg of Luster… AND HE LOCKS ON A FIGURE FOUR! Luster is screaming in pain and is trying desperately to turn it over…
VIC WATERS: The referee may not want to break this after Luster took liberties with him. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster is trying to get the figure four turned over…. HE IS HALF WAY THERE…. HE TURNS IT AND TACT GRABS THE ROPES TO BREAK!
VIC WATERS: Luster is showing while his senses have been dulled, his strength is still there.
BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER DRAGS TACT IN THE FIGURE FOUR TO THE CENTER OF THE RING AND SWITCHES INTO HIS PATTERNED STANDING ANKLE LOCK!
VIC WATERS: Tact has to becareful Luster doesn’t shatter his ankle in the process. Luster has been known to do some pretty extreme things.
BRANDON JACOBS: TACT STARTS KICKING AWAY WITH HIS LEFT LEG RIGHT INTO THE BACK OF LUSTER’S LEGS! Luster legs are getting wobbly…. LUSTER BREAKS THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: Tact has Luster very well scouted because it looks like he as anticipating that attack all match but he has plenty of other ways to beat Tact.
BRANDON JACOBS: A limping Tact is trying to get up…. LUSTER CLOTHESLINES HIM DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Luster looks agitated about the ankle lock, I haven’t seen him clothesline anyone like that in a longtime.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster picks up Tact…. SIDE SHUFFLE KICK RIGHT IN THE HEAD! Tact goes down! THIS COULD BE IT HERE! Luster goes down for the cover! 1--------------------------------------2------------------------------- TACT BARELY KICKS OUT!
VIC WATERS: Tact took that kick right in the temple. It could be awhile before he is back on his own two feet.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster is calling for the Funk & Wagnall Powerbomb! He is going for the kill!
VIC WATERS: Come on Luster, finish him off! BRANDON JACOBS: Luster tries to gutwrench Tact up by Tact is grabbing the bottom of Luster’s trunks so he can’t pick him up! LUSTER TRIES AGAIN… TACT STILL HAS A HANDFUL OF TIGHTS! Luster pulls Tact’s hand up…. HE BITES HIM RIGHT IN THE HAND! Tact recoils his hand back in horror.
VIC WATERS: Tact is going to need his shots after this match. BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER KICKS TACT IN THE STOMACH DOUBLING HIM OVER! Luster gutwrenches him up for a powerbomb….. TACT HOOKS HIS LEGS AROUND LUSTER’S HEAD AND LOCKS HIM IN A LEG SCISSORS SLEEPER HOLD!
VIC WATERS: Tact just won’t go gentle into the night. BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is checking to see if Luster wants to give up but Luster is trying desperately to pull Tact off and nail him with his finisher!
VIC WATERS: Luster has to reach back and use any power he has left. BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER TRIES TO ROLL FORWARD…. HE TURNS IT INTO A SUNSET FLIP! The ref goes down to count! 1-----------------------------------------2------------------- TACT ROLLS BACK AND REACHES BACK IN A SITDOWN POSITION AND HOOKS BOTH LEGS OF LUSTER! 1--------------------------------------------2----------------------3!
BRANDON JACOBS: HOLY COW! HE DID IT! What a finish! RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match….. LARRY TACT! VIC WATERS: Now I didn’t see that coming. Tact was amazing and Luster some how was able to keep with him throughout and gave him a hell of a wrestling match.
BRANDON JACOBS: You have to wonder if Luster came in here today wanting to prove a lesson that despite his high living lifestyle, he takes his business in the ring very seriously and damn, did he show it tonight.
VIC WATERS: Both men should be applauded for their effort. BRANDON JACOBS: Okay folks, when we comeback, two of the hottest rising stars in the WFW will go one on one as Jared Wells faces off against El Arco Iris…. STAY TUNED!
