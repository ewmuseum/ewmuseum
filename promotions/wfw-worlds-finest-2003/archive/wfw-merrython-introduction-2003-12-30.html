WFW Merrython: Introduction

30 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 30 December 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome everyone to Merrython, we are in the greatest city of them all to spend Christmas, NEW YORK CITY! Tonight we will come from various locations all over the city, including Rockefeller Center where are a broadcasting live at ringside on the closed off ice skating rink. Fans are looking on from railings above and the center is packed with fans who want to see LIVE World’s Finest Wrestling action!
VIC WATERS: We will only have four live matches from Rockefeller Center, but the fans will be able to watch Wylde vs. Edmunds vs. Jared Wells and Michaels vs. Hart live on our Jumbotron or in person at FAO Swartz and Central Park.
BRANDON JACOBS: The age old tradition of Closed Circuit TV which dates back before PPV. VIC WATERS: Now that is old school. BRANDON JACOBS: Months ago, Scotty Michaels made a promise to the fans. He told the fans that he would win the WFW World Heavyweight Title before the year was through and tonight, Scotty Michaels has his chance.
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart is the man who beat both Doc Silver and Manson. Scotty Michaels is a very talented wrestler, but the odds are stacked against him tonight. Shawn Hart has been a juggernaut with the title. Who is Scotty Michaels to think that he will be able to spoil Shawn Hart’s World Title run at Christmas time?
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels has roots that go back to the original NGEN. He has been around a long time and he has beaten the greats of our era like Manson and Mike Randalls. Scotty Michaels may be arrogant, but he is a superstar of the highest level. Hart is going to have his skills put to the test. While this match with Michaels may not be the obstacle that beating Manson in Chicago on PPV was, Michaels may be even more of a threat and may be the man to beat him because Shawn Hart’s body has gone through a lot of abuse over the last month and a half. People think winning the title is the hardest thing to do in wrestling when it is not, having to go out there as champion and on a regular basis have to face the best of the best every other show is the tough part.
VIC WATERS: If Hart manages to get past Michaels, he still has to face Minion and Edmunds in the next month and a half. While that isn’t going to be easy, I believe in Shawn Hart. Shawn Hart has been one of the cornerstones of the WFW since the inception when even though he didn’t win the WFW World Heavyweight Title or the WFW North American Title in the three way, he took it to Doc and Manson and showed his greatness to the world. He battled and battled his way to the top and even though he had a couple of setbacks, he kept on fighting. That is the true test of a champion. Everyone thinks that the measure of a man is how they handle success, but the truth is, failure is the greatest test of all because when people suffer a setback, they have doubts about their greatness and start changing what they do when it has been successful for them in the past. Even the greatest of men fail every so often, because great men take risks in order to achieve success. If I’ve learned one thing, it is that you can’t let a loss effect you, have to keep on going and do the best that you can do. Hart did that and that is why Hart has become the champion that he is.
BRANDON JACOBS: I’m not questioning the greatness of Shawn Hart, even if he has terrorized the WFW with COCKS for months, I can still recognize all that he has accomplished. But Scotty Michaels is a man on a mission and I think he may get the job done, but I’ve been wrong before.
VIC WATERS: And you will be wrong again… BRANDON JACOBS: We will see about that, folks, I’m getting signaled we have to go to commercial, folks, when we comeback, the great technican Larry Tact will make his WFW debut against a man who has pulled off the impossible more times than I can count, PULSAR! STAY TUNED!
