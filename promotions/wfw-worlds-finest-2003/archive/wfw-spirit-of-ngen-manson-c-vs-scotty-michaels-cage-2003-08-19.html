WFW: Spirit of NGEN: Manson (c) vs. Scotty Michaels (Cage)

19 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 19 August 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Aug-19-03 AT 10:27&nbsp;PM (EST)](Footage taped earlier in the show)
Scotty's gonna roll up to the arena in a limo, but he's traveling alone... Scotty does a short interview..
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Manson, I've been waiting for this for a long time now. HEEL, I don't need him. He's got other fish to fry... if he shows up, that's out of my power. Tonight, I take your pride... tonight, I take your title.
(End of Footage) BRANDON JACOBS:: Bold words by Scotty Michaels, but can he back then up, he has a lot of balls to challenge Manson to a match like this. I can’t remember anyone ever beating Manson in a cage. It is nearly impossible.
VIC WATERS: Manson is an animal. He may have all these folks conned into believing he is a good guy down deep, but he loves nothing more than ripping people into shreds. Anyone who has called Scotty Michaels the stupidest man alive isn’t too far off and I love Scotty.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson still has the injured left leg which he will get operated on immediately after this match, but even with that glaring weakness, this just isn’t sane.
VIC WATERS: This should be a hell of a fight though, Manson wants nothing better than to tear into Michaels for what he did to him at the last PPV and Scotty Michaels craves that title like a drug. These two are going to hold nothing back, this is going to be an all out war.
BRANDON JACOBS: War is a much too peaceful term for what is going to happen next, let’s not keep these people waiting anymore, the cage is all setup, let’s send this one to ringside!
(CUEUP: “Livin’ On A Prayer” by Bon Jovi) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the man loved by viewer’s worldwide and their pets, from Philadelphia, PA, THE SUPERSTAR SCOTTY MICHAELS!
::Michaels comes down to boos from the crowd with the gigantic barbed wire baseball bat in his hands and enters the cage::
BRANDON JACOBS: HEEL is not accompanying Michaels tonight as he told HEEL to stay in the back because he wanted to win this one on his own.
RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Chicago, IL, the WFW North American Champion, MICHAEL MANSON!
::Manson is walking down slowly to the ring with a slight limp with a smile on his face to a huge pop and enters the ring::
*grabs the barbed wire baseball bat from Michaels, throws it out of the door, closes the cage, and the bell rings*
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is just in there to count the pinfall, but the WFW has taken steps to beef up security so the fiasco which happened in Doc Silver vs. Psycho doesn’t happen again.
VIC WATERS: I doubt anything could ever top that match. That was pure chaos. The cage was there, but it was like it wasn’t.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson & Michaels are standing eye to eye…. MICHAELS SPITS IN MANSON FACE AND MANSON STARTS WAILING ON MICHAELS WITH HAYMAKERS!
VIC WATERS: It is never a good idea to spit in the face of someone who can be as evil as Manson can be if he chooses to be.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson grabs Michaels by the hair…. HE RAMS HIM HEAD FIRST INTO THE CAGE AND MICHAELS STAGGERS BACK! Manson grabs Michaels by the hair again…. HE RAMS HIM INTO THE OTHER SIDE! Michaels falls down to the mat!
VIC WATERS: The fans here are eating this all up. They are 100% Manson tonight. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson pulls the left leg out AND STOMPS UP AND DOWN MICHAELS LEFT LEG! HE IS GIVING HIM A TASTE OF HIS OWN MEDICINE!
VIC WATERS: Michaels is trying to inch himself away from Manson but it isn’t working. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson grabs the left leg and drags Michaels to the center of the ring…. HE DROPS THE KNEE ON THE LEFT LEG OF MICHAELS!
VIC WATERS: Manson is systematically working over the leg of Michaels so he evens the advantage that Michaels has against him. It is really smart plan.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson grabs the leg… FIGURE FOUR LEGLOCK! HE LOCKS IT IN TIGHT AND MICHAELS IS SCREAMING IN PAIN!
VIC WATERS: Remember, Michaels can’t break this by getting to the ropes! There is no DQ in a cage match so the ref can’t force the break.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is trying to use his power to get to the ropes! He is slowly inching his way to the side of the cage.
VIC WATERS: I don’t understand what he is attempting to do here. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels reaches the ropes…. HE IS USING THE POWER OF HIS ARMS TO POWER HIS WAY UP THE ROPES…. HE REVERSES THE FIGURE FOUR WHILE HOLDING ONTO THE TOP ROPE! INGENIOUS MOVE BY MICHAELS!
VIC WATERS: I have to take my hat off to Michaels, I never saw that coming. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson pulls himself away and breaks hold! Thankfully he broke that quickly or it would have done further damage to his left leg. Michaels is trying to stand but he is having trouble putting weight on both legs.
VIC WATERS: Michaels needs to get back to form quickly or he is toast. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson kicks Michaels in the midsection… HE IS GOING FOR A SNAP SUPLEX…. MICHAELS FIGHTS IT! HE COUNTERS WITH A SNAP SUPLEX OF HIS OWN AND MANSON IS WHIPPED INTO THE CAGE AND STUMBLES DOWN ON THE OTHER SIDE OF THE ROPES! OUCH! THAT HAD TO HURT!
VIC WATERS: Devastating counter by Michaels… BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is tied up in the other side of the ropes… MICHAELS IS CLIMBING UP TO THE TOP ROPE…. HE DROPS AN ELBOW ON THE TANGLED MANSON! Manson has been flattened!
VIC WATERS: That was a really risky move by Michaels, if he had missed that. He could have injured his own arm.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels rolls out from the other side of the ropes and rolls Manson from under the bottom rope back into the ring. Michaels is taking great pleasure in what he just did!
VIC WATERS: Anytime you can beat on the champion, it is a good day. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is dragging Manson to the center of the ring… MEXICAN SURFBOARD ON MANSON! You can see that Manson is really in pain!
VIC WATERS: Michaels is busting out the lucha libre tonight! BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is checking to see if Manson wants to submit but he is shaking his head no.
VIC WATERS: Manson has never submitted in his career and it would be quite odd if it was to a Mexican Surfboard.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels breaks the hold… but not before the damage was done. Michaels picks Manson off the mat…. BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX! Michaels makes the cover! 1------------------2-----------KICKOUT! Manson just got up that shoulder in time!
VIC WATERS: Manson has to feel like his body was put through a blender with all the punishment he has taken tonight.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels turns Manson over and goes for a camel clutch! Michaels is leaning back on the hold and trying to add pressure onto the lower back of Manson!
VIC WATERS: While Manson has never given up, if you work over someone back long enough, it will kill their ability to kick out of a hold and Michaels knows that.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is trying to use his strength to move himself over towards the ropes… he is making some progress… Michaels breaks the hold and drags Manson back to the center of the ring!
VIC WATERS: Smart move by Michaels there! BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels picks up Manson and drags him towards the cage… RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX RIGHT INTO THE SIDE OF THE CAGE!
VIC WATERS: HOLY COW! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is really taking some punishment tonight! Michaels pulls Manson in from the other side of the ropes and drags him to the center of the ring…. MICHAELS GOES FOR THE COVER! 1-------------------------2------------------------KICKOUT! How he kicked out of that I will never know.
VIC WATERS: Manson might be better if he stays down. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels picks Manson up… FOREARM TO THE SIDE OF MICHAELS HEAD! ANOTHER AND ANOTHER! Michaels is being backed off here as Manson is fighting for his lfie!
VIC WATERS: It is now or never for Manson. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON JUMPS UP AND MICHAELS WITH A FRANKENSTEINER AND THIS PLACE COMES UNGLUED! Manson hooks the legs of Michaels! 1------------------2---------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: The reason that Manson is able to do that with his injured leg is that the frankensteiner only requires the top part of the legs to be effective, if it didn’t, Manson would be furthering injuring himself.
BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON IS POUNDING AWAY WITH HAYMAKERS TO THE HEAD OF MICHAELS! HE IS REALLY LAYING IT IN! Michaels is trying to struggle to break free!
VIC WATERS: He has really got Manson angry here tonight and it payback time now. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson picks Michaels up and he alternates between kneelifts between the right and left leg… HE PICKS UP MICHAELS UP…. WILD BUCK BOMB! 1--------------------------2---------------------------KICKOUT! Manson doesn’t let go though… HE LIFTS HIM UP BY THE LEGS AGAIN…. REF GETS BUMP AND DRIVES HIM DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Manson better hope that ref recovers and fast. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson picks Michaels up… DDT! Manson picks Michaels up…. EVENFLOW DDT! Manson is standing over Michaels taunting him by slapping him across the face hard!
VIC WATERS: There isn’t much that he can do with the ref down. All he can do is wait for the ref to wake up so he might as well be punishing Michaels.
BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON IS CALLING TO THE FANS AND THEY ARE ROARING RIGHT BACK! He motions for the Tourniquet! Manson grabs the lifeless body of Michaels…. HE LOCKS IT IN AND HE PULLING BACK WITH ALL HIS MIGHT! As soon as the ref wakes up, this one is over because Michaels will be stretched beyond all belief!
VIC WATERS: Come on Michaels! ::lights go out and the crowd gasps:: BRANDON JACOBS: WHAT IS GOING ON HERE?! BRANDON JACOBS: THE LIGHTS HAVE GONE OFF! THIS CAN’T BE GOOD…. I THINK I JUST HEARD MANSON SCREAM! OH MAN…
VIC WATERS: Are you telling me Manson is afraid of the dark? BRANDON JACOBS: THE LIGHTS ARE BACK ON! OH NO…. MANSON IS DOWN! MANSON IS DOWN! VIC WATERS: LOOK, THE DOOR IS OPEN! BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is pointing at someone… OH MY GOD, LUSTER IS STANDING IN THE ENTRANCE WAY WITH A VIEWER’S CHOICE T-SHIRT AND PIPE!
VIC WATERS: ::in utter shock:: LUSTER?! LUSTER?! BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels is calling for his new movie…. VIEWER’S DELIGHT BRAINBUSTER SUPLEX! Michaels goes for the cover! 1---------------------2-----------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: THE WINNER OF THE MATCH AND NEW WFW NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION…. “THE VIEWER’S CHOICE” SCOTTY MICHAELS!
VIC WATERS: LUSTER?! BRANDON JACOBS: This is highway robbery! Manson had Michaels beat and Michaels and Luster cooked up this scheme to steal his title away!
VIC WATERS: LUSTER?! BRANDON JACOBS: The fans here are throwing garbage into the ring. Michaels is holding up the WFW North American Title and he kisses it! Michaels sees that Manson is waking up so he heads out the cage door towards the back. I can’t believe it. Michaels and Luster have screwed Manson.
VIC WATERS: LUSTER?! BRANDON JACOBS: Yes f’n Luster, Luster has screwed us all. We can only hope that Doc Silver doesn’t let us down and somehow can keep the title from getting into hands of C.O.C.K.S. ringleader Shawn Hart. We will be back with the WFW World Title matched. Stay tuned.
