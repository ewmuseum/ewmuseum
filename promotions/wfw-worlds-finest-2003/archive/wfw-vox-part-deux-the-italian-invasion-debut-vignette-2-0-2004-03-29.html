WFW Vox Part Deux: The Italian Invasion (Debut Vignette 2.0)

29 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 29 March 2004 As the cameras went back on air for the WFW Anniversary Show the view was nothing but the white tiles from one of the backstage area's many hallways and off in the near distance, rhythmic footsteps could be heard. The camera panned upwards. Long, olive oil, creamy legs broke into view and flowed seemingly perfectly. One foot stepped in front of the other, and the other followed with no effort as the camera slowly moved vertically. The next scene was just as inviting; a pair of finely curved hips switching from side to side, hypnotizing every male in the view's perimeter. The hips fell to the bottom of the screen, introducing a slim waist to the cameraman's delight before patiently disappearing to make way for another curve, completing the "coke bottle" shape of a woman's figure. Finally, the camera reached the visage of the body's owner. Fine, luscious red lips glimmering with sparkles turned moist as a tongue danced around them, completing the full circle before entering back into it's lair. Dark, brown, curly hair bounced with every step as it made it's way down just below the woman's shoulders like a grapevine descending an invisible wall, single strands curling up on her face with both vibrant, hazel eyes looking straight forward.
Was she a fantasy dream girl living in reality? No. Was she an angel fallen from heaven to earth? No. Was she available?! You wish. The camera panned away and behind her, focusing on the hips who flowed side to side before stopping as her back arched slightly inwards and popped out a voluptuous backside. But she was no longer alone. As her right hand ran across the finely toned, muscular chest of a tall, olive oil tanned youngster, the man's face bore an arrogant grin as if to say 'you wish this was you don't you?'
Dressed in a navy blue, short mink fur robe with diamonds glistening in the camera's lens the young man ran a hand through his jet black hair, neatly slicked backwards. His attire was simple, a pair of dark green tights; the cursive letters AG were printed on each side in a crimson font with faux rubies superposed over them. The view changed, now showing both man and woman paired together like a scene from a romance movie. That's when he broke the silence.
"Ahh... The World's Finest Wrestling federation. So many good things I have heard from this organization. It is renowned world wide as a federation of class, of elegance and of course... of the world's finest wrestlers." He paused. "Which is why I am here. You are probably wondering who I am and I will tell you. I am Alessandro Giovanni. The Italian Stallion."
Alessandro put his arm around the woman next to him, still caressing his chest gently with her hand.
"And this is my lovely, elegant, sexy companion Adrianna." The woman smiled a sensual smile. "One of God's finest creations."
The camera zoomed in on Giovanni. "However, let's not stray from the situation before us, for I have been hired by World's Finest Wrestling to once and for all demonstrate that we, italians, are far superior than the rest of you paisans. Our people dress with style, creating all the latest fashions which everyone steals and make profits off our backs. We eat like kings amongst men, serving gourmet dishes from morning to night. Our culture is refined, flowing with class and rightful pride while in America, people eat like pigs, dress like hopeless slobs from the gutter, and most importantly, think they are above everyone else with no knowledge of how to function as a civilized individual... such as myself."
Adrianna peeled the robe off Alessandro's shoulders, letting his hands show his defined physique.
"Look at me. 6 feet 3 inches, 250 pounds of pure muscle. No more than 3 perfect of body fat resides in this perfect structure before you. I am a former bodybuilding champion, soccer superstar and academic genius. Very little men can praise their work like I can. I started as a young boy helping his father in a olive oil export factory, earning a few dollars and cents every day to pay for the bread and butter that fed our family. But we grew. We grew until my father's company became the leading olive oil exporter in Eastern Europe. As dollars and cents turned into thousands and millions, my life was not at the end of it's rope... it had only begun." He grinned. "In the University, money was never an issue. I went to the finest schools of Venice, trained with the greatest athletes and socialized with high society. Succeeding in every one of these fields. Life was perfect it seemed but then came America."
The arrogant look turned fiery. "With your cheap fast foods and cheaper women. You think of yourselves on top of the world, ruling the planet with a buffoon governing your people." Alessandro spat violently to the floor. "Vaffanculo! You are nothing but a disgrace to the human kind. This is why I have come here, to prove you are nothing compared to us. We have better food, better women, better artists, better music and of course... better athletes."
Adrianna placed the robe back on Giovanni's back as the camera shot a close-up of his face.
"This is just the beginning, my friends. Soon you will all learn we are the best. So to my first opponent, whoever you are... Buona Fortuna!
You will need it."
