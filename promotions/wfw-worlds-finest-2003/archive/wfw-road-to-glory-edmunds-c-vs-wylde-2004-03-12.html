WFW Road To Glory: Edmunds (c) vs. Wylde

12 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 12 March 2004 (BACKSTAGE) ::The camera opens backstage on Psycho. He stands with backstage reporter BLACK ROSE:: BLACK ROSE: Psycho...after not hearing a word from you during all the hype of So Cal Rumble you finally make an appearence and cost Richard Gideon a shot at the WFW Title just as Anarky and Jared Wells cost Copycat...my only question is why?
::He laughs loudly:: PSYCHO: You want to know why? Well I'll tell you why. ::He rips the microphone out of Black Rose hand:: PSYCHO: First for you Gideon. It started out a few months ago. At first it was just for kicks. You were a smart ass rookie. You hadn't paid your dues yet. I...
::pounds his chest:: PSYCHO: I took it upon MYSELF!!! To make sure you had paid your dues and I have. You've been punished, you've been bleed and you a little bit of your own credit...
::He smiles:: PSYCHO: Well you've even showed some backbone. Yet along the lines something better came along. Something BIGGER came along.
::laughs again:: PSYCHO: Something ELITE came along..their names were Anarky and Jared Wells. TOGETHER!!!....together we are the FINEST the WFW has to offer. Now, because of them OUR business needs to end Gideon. OUR bad blood needs to be spilled ONE LAST TIME! WE...we need a match to settle the score. Yet not just one simple match.
::He shakes his head:: PSYCHO: No Gideon ::He holds up two fingers then three:: PSYCHO: That's right Gideon. Two out of three falls and here is the capper Gideon. Here is the CHERRY on top of your sundae. I...yes I am going to let you pick the stips. That's right...you pick them. I DON'T CARE!!! You wanna wrestle...we can wrestle. You want barbed-wire...GO AHEAD!!!!!!! You want to trade turns busting lightubes over our heads and falling on thumbtacks
::He let's loose a sinister laugh:: PSYCHO: I'D LOVE THAT!!! ::Psycho tosses the microphone back to BLACK ROSE:: PSYCHO: You got any more questions? BLACK ROSE: I'm still curious as to why you didn't enter the So Cal. Rumble? ::Psycho turns and stares Black Rose down:: PSYCHO: Because it's real simple. Do I look stupid? BLACK ROSE: No PSYCHO: <getting closer> Do I look like I need to grovel? BLACK ROSE: No ::Psycho turns back to the camera:: PSYCHO: Your damn right. I've pissed of alot of people. You think I'm getting in the ring with everyone that just hates me or is simply jealous that they are not on the level of Anarky, Wells and Psycho. No I'm not. Besides I was the WFW Champion. I had the belt around my waist. Nobody pinned me for it, Nobody made me submit and worst of all nobody gave me a rematch. If I want a shot a the WFW Title I'll find the champion...I'll beat him senseless and I'll do it till the coward has the guts t put his strap
on the line. I'll do it on MY TERMS...I'll do it on MY TIME!!! Now what else is on your mind?
BLACK ROSE: Well for what reason did you guys cost CopyCat a shot at the title? ::Psycho pauses for a moment then chuckles:: PSYCHO: You know what Rose. I guess that's just an added bonus ::Psycho let's loose a loud laugh, turns his back and walks away:: <FTB> (RINGSIDE) BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky, Wells, and Psycho are just plain evil. VIC WATERS: But in the end, they always accomplish their objective… BRANDON JACOBS: Okay folks, it is time for the main event and the winner will move on to face the former WFW North American Champion Michael Manson in the main event of the Superbowl of Wrestling.
VIC WATERS: Manson is going to be a huge challenge for whoever wins this match. As much of a pain in the ass Copycat has been to Edmunds, Edmunds at least knows Copycat like the back of his hand. To the best of my knowledge, Edmunds and Manson haven’t faced each other in years.
BRANDON JACOBS: What makes you think Edmunds is going to walk out of here as WFW World Heavyweight Champion? Wylde has nine years of frustration going into this match and if he could beat Edmunds here tonight, he would secure his place in history as one of the alltime greats. While I may not agree with everything Wylde has done, everyone has to be rooting for Wylde on a certain level to finally accomplish the last thing he has left to accomplish.
VIC WATERS: Some people retire without ever getting that brass ring, Patrick Ewing, Don Mattingly, Dan Marino, Ernie Banks…. You can be an alltime great without winning it all. Although there is a certain sadness to it all when guys just can’t get the job done. I just think Wylde is going to be one of those guys. Edmunds is a three time WWL World Heavyweight champion and he is now the WFW World Heavyweight Champ.
BRANDON JACOBS: This is going to be really tough match for Wylde. If he beats Edmunds, he is going to have to go to hell and back. Folks, let’s send this one down to ringside and get the main event underway!
(CUEUP: "Diesel Power" by Prodigy) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the challenger… The Current WFW President and Leader of men… ALEX WYLDE!

'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds!

VIC WATERS: Edmunds is going for the knock out early. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds picks Wylde up and locks him in a front face lock…. EDMUNDS IS TRYING TO GO FOR A DDT BY WYLDE GRABS THE TIGHTS OF EDMUNDS TO BLOCK!
VIC WATERS: Smart thinking by Wylde. BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS PICKS WYLDE UP IN THE AIR FOR A VERTICAL SUPLEX INSTEAD AND HOLDS HIM UP IN THE AIR…… HE DROPS HIM DOWN ON THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is putting on a clinic on what to do when a move is reversed. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds crawls over on top of Wylde but he sees the foot of Wylde is on the rope. WYLDE CHARGES AND HITS EDMUNDS WITH A SPINNING HEEL KICK WHICH KNOCKS HIM THROUGH THE ROPES AND OUT THE FLOOR!

BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde is a tough wrestler and he knows that every opportunity to win the WFW World Heavyweight Title is precious so he is going to go until he can’t go anymore. EDMUNDS PICKS UP WYLDE AND BODYSLAMS HIM DOWN TO THE MAT! Edmunds positions Wylde and Edmunds is going to the corner.
VIC WATERS: I think Edmunds is going to the corner to deliver his patented top rope elbow right to the chest.

Edmunds is stirring and he looks over at the referee who has been knocked down and he throws his hands up in the air. Edmunds tries to slap the referee in order to wake him up. The ref is finally starting to come to.
VIC WATERS: COVER HIM! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds makes the cover on Wylde! 1-----------------------------------------------2--------------------------------------------KICKOUT! Wylde got the shoulder up on the slow three count!
VIC WATERS: Three count? That was at least a five count. Damn referee. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds gets up and he yells at the referee for the slow count. Edmunds picks up Wylde as he yells at the referee… WYLDE BLOCKS A DDT AND TAKES EDMUNDS DOWN WITH A SNAP SUPLEX! Wylde clutches his ribs!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds let the count get in his head and he took his concentration off Wylde for a split second and that is all it took.
