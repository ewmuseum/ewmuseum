Cherry Blossom Chaos II: Richard Gideon vs. John Doe (debut)

20 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 20 August 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: I don’t know about you, but I am quite excited to see John Doe’s debut. I have seen him wrestle every from NEW to Kalamazoo and he always is a force in the squared circle.
JONATHAN MARX: Doe has a hell of a challenge on his plate in Richard Gideon. Gideon has been on a roll. Last week he defeated former WFW World Heavyweight Champion Shawn Hart in his debut and he would love nothing more than to spoil John Doe’s debut here tonight.
VIC WATERS: Last year, Shawn Hart, Richard Gideon, Psycho, and Doc were battling for the World Heavyweight Title at Cherry Blossom Chaos and Gideon put up a hell of a fight.
JONATHAN MARX: Doe made some remarks about how if Gideon is so great, why hasn’t he heard of him before? Let me just say, if you don’t know Richard Gideon, you are not a wrestling fan. He has taken it to all the best in this sport, Michael Manson, Doc Silver, and Shawn Hart and has beaten them on several occasions. He tears it up in the ring, he can brawl with you on the outside, he can take punishment with the best of them… Gideon is the man.
BRANDON JACOBS: I am sold on Gideon, but I think Doe is going to beat him tonight and establish his presents here in WFW. Lets send this match down to ringside and get this one underway!
(CUEUP: “Remember” by Disturbed) RING ANNOUCER: Introducing first, from Buffalo, NY…. the former and longest reigning WFW North American Champion of alltime….. THE SYSTEM RICHARD GIDEON!
::Gideon comes down to the ring to the roar of the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “Who Said” By Planet Funk) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent making his debut here tonight, a mystery man even to himself, from parts unknown, JOHN DOE!
::John Doe comes down to the boos of the crowd and enters the ring:: *bell rings* VIC WATERS: John Doe stole my gimmick, I wake up every day and it takes me a good hour or two to figure out who and where I am.
JONATHAN MARX: Yes, but your condition is Poland Spring related. BRANDON JACOBS: Quiet you two, we have a match underway, Gideon and Doe are circling each other looking for a chance to lock up. Gideon extends his arms to Gideon and THEY LOCK UP! DOE TAKES GIDEON DOWN WITH A HIPTOSS!
JONATHAN MARX: Doe has lightning quick speed in the squared circle. He caught Gideon off guard with that.
VIC WATERS: As great as Gideon is, he has never faced Doe and there is always a feeling out process with new opponents.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon gets up quickly and he charges Doe again… THEY LOCK UP AND GIDEON MANAGES TO GET A SIDE HEADLOCK ON DOE! Gideon takes him down and locks on a reverse chinlock on Gideon in an effort to wear Doe down.
JONATHAN MARX: Looks like Gideon is starting to get serious… VIC WATERS: This is a smart move here because Doe has great endurance, if he can wear him down just a little bit early, it will come back to haunt him later in the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Doe is trying to break free from the reverse chinlock but Gideon has it in tight. Doe is trying to extend his legs towards the ropes in order to get a referee break but he is a good three or four feet away.
JONATHAN MARX: Both of these men know where they are in the ring at all times which just shows you how smart these two guys are for their age.
VIC WATERS: We don’t really know how old John Doe is, he doesn’t even know how old he is. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon lets go of the reverse chinloc! He grabs the arms of Doe and he puts the knee right into the back of the neck of Doe and he pulling his arms back to drive that knee even further into his neck.
JONATHAN MARX: He is wearing down Doe for those rolling brainbusters of his later on. BRANDON JACOBS: Doe is wincing in pain as the referee is checking to see if Doe wants to give up but he is repeatedly telling the ref no.
VIC WATERS: Doe would never quit this early, he is a fighter. BRANDON JACOBS: DOE IS STRUGGLING TO BREAK FREE! Gideon loses his grip and Doe’s feet break the plane of the ropes in order to force the ref break. The ref is telling Gideon to back off and let Doe get up.
JONATHAN MARX: If Gideon held him in that hold any longer, Doe would have been dead meat. VIC WATERS: It looks like the work to the neck had its effect because he is grabbing the back of his neck in pain as he is trying to get up.
BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON ISN’T GIVING HIM A CHANCE TO GET UP AND HE CHARGES DOE! DOE USES THE ROPES TO LIKE A BALANCE BEAM TO PUSH OFF HIS BODY UP OFF THE MAT AND HE DELIVERS A HEAD SCISSORS TAKE DOWN ON GIDEON TAKING HIM DOWN TO THE MAT!
JONATHAN MARX: This Doe fellow is really agile… BRANDON JACOBS: Doe gets up quickly…. GIDEON IS GETTING UP AND HE CHARGES DOE…. HURACANRANA ON GIDEON AND HE HOOKS THE LEGS! The ref goes down for the count! 1---------------------------------2----------------------------- GIDEON KICKS OUT!
VIC WATERS: Doe almost had him! BRANDON JACOBS: Doe gets up… AND HE IS HEADED TO THE TOP ROPE! Gideon is getting up but he looks really groggy… THE FLYING FOREARM SMASH FROM THE TOP ROPE BY DOE CONNECTS AND GIDEON FALLS BACK AND ROLLS OUT OF THE RING!
JONATHAN MARX: Gideon always knows where he is in that ring and he knew that the way Doe was going, he had to get out of that ring fast or Doe may have had his number.
BRANDON JACOBS: Doe is going out after Gideon! Gideon is trying to get up with help via the guardrail.
VIC WATERS: The guardrail is a wonderful thing… BRANDON JACOBS: Doe pulls Gideon off the guardrail…. HE HOOKS HIM IN THE STO AND GIDEON GOES FACE FIRST INTO THE MATS AT RINGSIDE!
JONATHAN MARX: Those mats are incredibly thin, they barely absorb any of the punishment if any.
BRANDON JACOBS: DOE IS GETTING UP AND HE CLIMBS UP ONTO THE RING APRON AND HE IS HEADED TOWARDS THE CORNER TURNBUCKLE!
VIC WATERS: I wonder what he has planned here. JONATHAN MARX: I would have just thrown Gideon back into the ring and attempted a pinfall. Doe has the advantage right now, he doesn’t need to do anything high risk.
VIC WATERS: It is going to take a lot to take out Gideon. BRANDON JACOBS: DOE IS PERCHED ON THE TOP TURNBUCKLE…. DOE FLIES OFF WITH THE SENTON BOMB BUT GIDEON MOVES OUT OF THE WAY!
JONATHAN MARX: Pure instinct right there, Gideon sensed impending doom and he just moved out of the way in the nick of time.
BRANDON JACOBS: Doe looks like he is clutching that back…. VIC WATERS: As Marx just said, those mats don’t give you much padding if at all. BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is counting both men and Gideon appears to be the first man onto his feet. GIDEON ROLLS BACK IN TO BREAK THE COUNT AND ROLLS BACK OUT!
JONATHAN MARX: Gideon is watching the clock, he doesn’t want to get counted out. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon rolls back out…. GIDEON PICKS DOE UP OFF THE MAT AND HE PUTS ON A WAISTLOCK… GIDEON GERMAN SUPLEXES HIM BACK DOWN AND TRANSITIONS INTO A FIGURE FOUR LEG LOCK!
JONATHAN MARX: HE HAS IT LOCKED ON TIGHT! VIC WATERS: He can’t get the submission out there. BRANDON JACOBS: Doe looks like he is in complete agony as Gideon has this figure four on full force!
JONATHAN MARX: Doe is in a tough position, he just took that German Suplex to his back and had his neck worked over earlier so it is painful for him to even rest his upper body as he fights off the excoriating pain in his lower legs.
BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is coming out of the ring and he is asking Gideon to break the hold… GIDEON IS GRABBING THE GUARDRAIL FOR LEVERAGE AND THE REF IS PUTTING A COUNT ON GIDEON! ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! Gideon breaks the hold!
JONATHAN MARX: He had too or he would have been disqualified, this ref means business. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon picks Doe off the ground… GOOD GOD! GIDEON GERMAN SUPLEXES HIM HEAD FIRST ONTO THE APRON!
VIC WATERS: Gideon is going after that neck of Doe and he just dealt him a hell of a blow that he’ll have to work with for the rest of the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is screaming at Gideon to get back into the ring and he picks Doe off the ground and he is finally rolling him back into the ring.
JONATHAN MARX: The best thing for Doe would be to take the pinfall right now and come back to fight another day. He needs to get a week off and rest that neck before any serious damage is done.
VIC WATERS: I hate to agree, but Doe should pack it in and live again to fight another day.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon rolls back in and the ref comes back in as well…. GIDEON COVERS DOE! This could be all she wrote! 1---------------------------------------------------------------2-------------------------------------- DOE KICKS OUT!
JONATHAN MARX: Doe is showing a lot of courage here. VIC WATERS: Courage is one word for it… BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON PICKS UP DOE BY THE HAIR AND HE REPEATEDLY STARTS POUNDING HIM WITH FOREARM SMASHES RIGHT TO THE SIDE OF THE HEAD!
JONATHAN MARX: Doe’s neck is taking a hell of a lot of punishment because he isn’t letting those shots have any give by holding him there.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon lets go of Doe and Doe is wobbling on his feet! GIDEON IS CALLING FOR THE G-CLEF!
VIC WATERS: If Gideon hits this, Doe is done for. JONATHAN MARX: Doe may forget what he barely remembers after all of this. BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON GOES TO LIFT DOE UP FOR THE BRAINBUSTER BUT DOE GRABS THE SIDE OF GIDEON’S TIGHTS!
JONATHAN MARX: Doe is fighting for his life here! BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON IS TRYING TO PULL HIM OFF BUT DOE IS FIGHTING IT WITH ALL THAT HE HAS LEFT! OH MAN, DOE JUST DROVE THE ELBOW RIGHT INTO THE FACE OF GIDEON IN ORDER TO BREAK HIS GRASP!
VIC WATERS: He may have lost a couple of teeth on that one it was so hard. BRANDON JACOBS: DOE WAISTLOCKS GIDEON… I DON’T KNOW IF HE IS GOING TO BE ABLE TO PULL HIS OFF WITH THIS BACK OF HIS BUT DOE IS GOING FOR BROKE…. DOE LIFTS UP GIDEON AND GERMAN SUPLEXES HIM DOWN TO THE MAT!
JONATHAN MARX: THAT IS ONE! VIC WATERS: Good Jon, here is a cookie. BRANDON JACOBS: DOE PICKS GIDEON OFF THE MAT WITH THE WAISTLOCK STILL ON AND HE DELIVERS ANOTHER BIG GERMAN SUPLEX DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Doe is finally getting some payback! BRANDON JACOBS: DOE IS GOING FOR THE THIRD AND FINAL ONE…. OH NO! DOE JUST GRABBED HIS BACK!
JONATHAN MARX: Probably one too many… BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is slowly getting back up and he sees Doe on his knees in agony…… GIDEON GETS UP AND HE PICKS UP DOE….. BRAINBUSTER SUPLEX RIGHT INTO THE MAT! The G-Clef is back on!
JONATHAN MARX: Gideon has worked on his neck and back for the whole entire match, if he completes this, he should put him away.
BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON ROLLS DOE BACK UP…. DOE IS LIFTED UP IN THE AIR….. HE DELIVERS ANOTHER BRAINBUSTER AND DOE CRASHES DOWN ON HIS HEAD!
VIC WATERS: Look Doe is still moving, he is trying to get back up. JONATHAN MARX: Doe has a lot of heart I’ll give him that. BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON PICKS DOE UP A THIRD TIME….. GIDEON HITS THE TRIFECTA! Gideon is going for the cover! 1-------------------------------------------------------2--------------------------------------- JOHN DOE KICKS OUT!
JONATHAN MARX: Oh my god, how did he kick out of that?! VIC WATERS: Doe has an iron will, all he has to do is wait for his opportunity and he’ll be back in this match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is yelling at the referee for the slow count but he quickly starts dragging Doe over towards the corner….
VIC WATERS: NO! Gideon don’t do it. Last time Doe tried a high risk move when he had control of the match it cost him the advantage.
JONATHAN MARX: I have seen Gideon miss The System Failure on several occasions, but when he hits it, he hits it big.
BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON IS ASCENDING TO THE TOP TURNBUCKLE…. Doe is starting to move…. GIDEON SEE DOE STARTING TO MOVE AND CLIMBS UP TO THE TOP AS QUICKLY AS POSSIBLE…. GIDEON PERCHES FROM THE TOP…… SYSTEM FAILURE 450 IN A FROG SPLASH RIGHT ONTO THE DOE!
JONATHAN MARX: HE GOT HIM! VIC WATERS: But will he get him…. BRANDON JACOBS: THE REFEREE GOES DOWN FOR THE COUNT! 1--------------------------------------------2-----------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. THE SYSTEM RICHARD GIDEON! JONATHAN MARX: Doe fought valiantly, but he did all that he could once Gideon injured his neck on the outside.
VIC WATERS: Doe will be okay, I am really impressed with how he did against Gideon considering how Gideon gave Doc and Manson a run for their money.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon was on edge all night. He looked like he was out for the kill tonight.
VIC WATERS: This could be a whole new page for Gideon. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we have to take a commercial break but when we comeback, Golem and Lane will go one on one…. STAY TUNED!
