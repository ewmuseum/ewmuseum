WFW Vox Part Deux: Iron Lion vs. Mighty Impala

27 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 27 March 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back and it is time for our first match of the evening. VIC WATERS: This should be quite interesting, when was the last time you saw someone from African try to slay a line in person?
BRANDON JACOBS: Having never been to African, I would say this is my honest to goodness first time. I personally don’t like Mighty Impala’s chances.
VIC WATERS: Mighty Impala is a brilliant, brilliant man. I’m sure he’ll use his quickness and smarts to dodge his attacks as much as possible.
BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion comes off as a well educated man himself. He isn’t one of these huge monsters without a brain, he is educated which makes him very dangerous indeed.
VIC WATERS: Mighty Impala descends from royalty. He went all of the finest school and has had an opportunity to see the world. How could you even put him in the same class as Iron Lion?
BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion may not be a prince, but he has his own level of sophistication. He is a man among boys.
VIC WATERS: Well I’m predicting that Mighty Impala is finally going to break his losing streak right here tonight and Vegas is going to have a stroke when everyone who has a bet on Mighty Impala cashes in.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hell, they could retire on that money. Let’s see if you are right and let’s send this one down to ringside!
(CUEUP: The Cote D’Ivoire National Anthem) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the Prince of the Mossi Tribe, THE MIGHTY IMPALA! ::The Mighty Impala walks out to the books of the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “Is This The End” by Creed) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Anchorage, Alaska, a man focused on bringing honor, pride, and strength back to professional wrestling, IRON LION!
::Iron Lion walks down to the roar of the crowd and enters the ring:: *bell ring* BRANDON JACOBS: Mighty Impala and Iron Lion meet in the center of the ring and Iron Lion is staring down at Mighty Impala.
VIC WATERS: Look at how he just towers over him! BRANDON JACOBS: MIGHTY IMPALA SLAPS IRON LION ACROSS THE FACE AND YELLS AT IRON LION THAT HE, MIGHTY IMPALA, IS THE PARADIGM OF RESPECT IN WFW!
VIC WATERS: Poor egotistical Impala. BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion growls at Mighty Impala has a sheer look of terror on his face realizing what he has done. Mighty Impala runs towards the ropes but Iron Lion grabs him by his tights before he can escape! IRON LION PICKS UP MIGHTY IMPALA IN A MILITARY PRESS OVER HIS HEAD!
VIC WATERS: Mighty Impala is a good eight feet up, this is no way to treat a prince! BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion dumps him over the side and HE CRASHES DOWN ON THE FLOOR BELOW! Good gawd!
VIC WATERS: Mighty Impala smacked down on the padding at ringside face first and those pads aren’t all that thick when you are falling from the height he was.
BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion steps over the top rope and jumps down to the ground below. Mighty Impala is trying to crawl away but Iron Lion grabs him by the hair to stop him.
VIC WATERS: Mighty Impala needs to do something fast or this match will be over before it even begins.
BRANDON JACOBS: MIGHTY IMPALA REACHES UP AND HE RAKES IRON LION RIGHT IN THE EYES! Iron Lion is swinging wildly as he is trying to regain his eyesight.
VIC WATERS: Great move by Mighty Impala, now he needs to capitalize. BRANDON JACOBS: Mighty Impala grabs a chair from the time keeper! If he isn’t careful, he is going to get himself DQed.
VIC WATERS: You are forgetting, Mighty Impala has diplomatic immunity and is a friend of Wylde so he can’t be DQed.
BRANDON JACOBS: You are right, that is just such an unfair advantage but it is going to take everything he has to take Iron Lion down. MIGHTY IMPALA RUNS FROM THE GUARDRAIL AND ONTO THE STEEL STEPS WITH A FLYING CROSSBODY WITH A CHAIR AT IRON LION… IRON LION GETS UP HIS LEG AND HE YAZUKA KICKS THE CHAIR RIGHT INTO MIGHTY IMPALA’S FACE AND MIGHTY IMPALA IS BUSTED OPEN!
VIC WATERS: Did you see that?! He purposely kicked that chair right into the Prince’s face.
BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion was just trying to protect himself after being blinded. It was pure reaction. The ref is telling both men to get back into the ring!
VIC WATERS: Mighty Impala is in dreamland. I doubt he even hears him. BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion walks over to the body of Mighty Impala and he rolls him back into the ring. Iron Lion steps over the top rope and re-enters as this crowd is roaring its approval at this newcomer.
VIC WATERS: I don’t understand why this crowd hates Impala. Any woman in this crowd would jump at the opportunity to marry someone of the wealth and stature of the Mighty Impala.
BRANDON JACOBS: Don’t go stereotyping, not all women are like that. Iron Lion is showing some mercy and is going for the cover! 1---------------------------------------------------2------------------------------ MIGHTY IMPALA KICKS OUT!
VIC WATERS: I would have stayed down, Mighty Impala is losing an awful lot of blood. BRANDON JACOBS: IRON LIONS PICKS UP MIGHTY IMPALA AND DELIVERS A TRIPLE RIB BREAKER WINDING MIGHTY IMPALA!
VIC WATERS: This can only go from bad to worse. BRANDON JACOBS: Mighty Impala rolls towards the rolls and tries to regain his breath. VIC WATERS: After what happened the last time, I wouldn’t suggest him going back out there.
BRANDON JACOBS: IRON LION TRIES TO PULL HIM AWAY BY HIS FEET FROM THE ROPES BUT MIGHTY IMPALA IS CLINGING ONTO THE ROPES WITH BOTH HANDS FOR DEAR LIFE IN MID AIR!
VIC WATERS: It is a smart move, as long as he is holding to the ropes, he can’t be pinned and maybe he can buy enough time to figure out a counter attack.
BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion drops Mighty Impala legs and his body goes crashing down onto the mat! Iron Lion bounces off the ropes…. STRENGTH IN HONOR BUT MIGHTY IMPALA ROLLS OUT OF THE WAY AND IRON LION HANDS HARD ON HIS CHEST!
VIC WATERS: Come on Mighty Impala! BRANDON JACOBS: A tired and bloody Mighty Impala bounces off the ropes…. DROPKICK TO THE SHOULDER OF IRON LION!
VIC WATERS: Mighty Impala finally got some offense in! BRANDON JACOBS: MIGHTY IMPALA PULLS IRON LION AWAY FROM THE ROPES ON HIS STOMACH AND LOCKS ON A FUJIWARA ARMBAR!
VIC WATERS: This is a brilliant move, if he can take his arm away from it, it will be much harder for him to do any power moves.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second, why is Iron Lion smiling…. OH MY GOD, IRON LION IS GETTING UP AND HE IS PICKING MIGHTY IMPALA UP IN THE AIR WITH THE FUJIWARA ARMBAR STILL ATTACHED…. .
VIC WATERS: ::scared:: Mother… BRANDON JACOBS: A frightened Mighty Impala lets go but not before Iron Lion has him set on his shoulders…..
VIC WATERS: This is his finisher right here! BRANDON JACOBS: IRON LION SENDS THE MIGHTY IMPALA CRASHING DOWN HARD ONTO THE MAT WITH THE PRIDE HAMMER (A STIFF RUNNING POWERBOMB)! Mighty Impala is out! The ref is going down to make the count! 1---------------------------------------------2-----------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. IRON LION! VIC WATERS: Sometimes you slay the lion, sometimes the lion slays you. BRANDON JACOBS: He was literally toying with Mighty Impala. I’ve never seen anyone do that before.
VIC WATERS: Beginner’s luck. Let’s see how he fairs at the Superbowl of Wrestling against Larry Tact and El Arco Iris.
BRANDON JACOBS: Poor El Arco Iris. Folks, we have to take a break, when we comeback, it is time for Michaels & Tact verses Edmunds & Luster…. STAY TUNED EVERYONE!
