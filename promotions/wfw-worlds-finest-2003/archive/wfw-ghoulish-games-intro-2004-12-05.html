WFW Ghoulish Games: Intro

5 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 5 December 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome everyone, we are live from the United Center for Ghoulish our final show before the Turkey Day Terror PPV and a lot of things will be decided tonight which will be a factor on the PPV.
JONATHAN MARX: We have a Four Corners of Fear match between Shawn Hart, Golem, Rocko Daymon, and John Doe with the winner getting a shot at Psycho at the PPV.
VIC WATERS: This is the same building and event where a year ago, Shawn Hart successfully defended the WFW World Heavyweight Title in Manson’s own home town of Chicago. If he could pull off another amazing performance and win the four way, he could very well beat Psycho for the WFW North American Title.
JONATHAN MARX: Psycho has held onto that title for over seven months now and somehow he always finds a way to pull the match out even when things look their darkest, But with four men fighting it out for the shot, the one who comes out of that may be strong enough to finally be the man to do it.
BRANDON JACOBS: Then we have the barn burner between Felix Red and Thirteen fighting it out in our annual falls count anywhere in the Graveyard match which has been an annual tradition dating back over ten years now to the original NGEN, if Thirteen manages to win this match, he gets a shot at Jared Wells’ BAD World Heavyweight Title at the PPV.
JONATHAN MARX: Jared Wells has had a hell of a BAD World Heavyweight Title reign so far, but Thirteen has showed in the Cibernetico that he can more than hold his own against LOVE.
VIC WATERS: Last year Anarky beat the promising young Diablo in that match and for all extensive purposes, his career came to an end. Felix Red hopes to follow in Anarky path and do the same to Thirteen.
BRANDON JACOBS: Tonight we also have three new comers making their in ring debut here tonight, Christian Sands will take on Mike Lane, Sebastian Dodd will take on El Arco Iris, and a much talked about mystery man will make his debut tonight against Richard Gideon.
JONATHAN MARX: From what I heard, that may not be the end of the new people showing up here tonight, we could have a couple of awfully big names showing up and making their presence felt but I am not going to give anything away.
VIC WATERS: Damn, It is like the flood gates have opened or something. You can’t be happy, they are spending your inheritance.
JONATHAN MARX: I may have to find myself a rich widow at this rate. BRANDON JACOBS: We also have a big three way dance between the team of Maelstrom & Dan Ryan, Edmunds & Luster, and Iron Lion & Rat Fink.
JONATHAN MARX: A lot of fans have been buzzing about that match with a lot of people cutting promos on each other left and right. Edmunds was really ripping into people, it reminded me of the old Edmunds who won the WFW World Heavyweight Title.
VIC WATERS: Edmunds may have lost the presidency to Felix Red starting at the Turkey Day Terror PPV, but it may have relight the fire within him to step it up to a whole new level.
BRANDON JACOBS: And last but not least, we have a Day of the Dead match which will be a six man tag between the odd couple team of Michael Manson, Copycat, and Scotty Michaels taking on LOVE in a six man tag where not only do you have to pin your opponent, they have to stay down for a ten count in order to win.
JONATHAN MARX: As much as I wish it didn’t, I think this match has to favor LOVE, they are a well oiled machine, more than any other group in all of wrestling and I can’t see Manson, Copycat, and Michaels working as a team even against LOVE with the hatred between them.
VIC WATERS: Manson is in the bad predictament of everyone in the match hating his guts. We could very well see a five on one beat down….
JONATHAN MARX: Worse comes to worse, Manson has his monkey by his side. VIC WATERS: I don’t care if he has Grodd in his corner, he better watch his back. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, when we comeback, Pulsar is going to challenge Mike McGee for the right to face Mighty Impala at the PPV and win back his sister. The stakes don’t get any bigger than that, STAY TUNED!
