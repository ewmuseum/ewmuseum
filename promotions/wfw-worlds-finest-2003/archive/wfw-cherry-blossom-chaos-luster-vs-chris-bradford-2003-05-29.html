WFW Cherry Blossom Chaos - Luster vs. Chris Bradford

29 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 29 May 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: This feud started back at WFW Gates of Hell when Chris Bradford, in an attempt to get revenge for what he felt was the arrogance of Luster, helped Andre Eian throw a fire ball into the eyes of Luster in order to help Andre Eian advance in the tournament to determine the #1 Contender post PPV for the WFW North American Title.
VIC WATERS: Bradford is a hell of a wrestler and I love the fact that he stood up to Luster because he felt he was being disrespected. But Luster is a man after my own heart, he has the looks of a God and the wrestling skill to back it up. Luster is going to be a very angry man tonight and he will be looking for his revenge.
BRANDON JACOBS: Let’s send this match down to ringside and begin the show! ::“Funkytown” Disco Fever plays over the loud speakers and a disco ball descends from the ceiling as the lights flash::
RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from Atlanta, GA, The Master of Style, LUSTER! ::Down by 311 blares over the speakers as the fans get up and dance along:: BRANDON JACOBS: The fans seem to be really taking to Luster charm and charisma, especially the ladies out there.
RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Lowell, MA, one of the best pure wrestlers in the WFW today, CHRIS BRADFORD!
::Chris Bradford comes down to a pop from the WFW hardcore fans and enters the ring:: BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER ATTACKS BRADFORD BEFORE THE BELL AS HE TRIES TO ENTER THE RING! Luster hooks the inner leg… CAPTURE SUPLEX INTO THE CENTER OF THE RING! Bradford grabs his back and he is reeling as his grabs his back and starts to inch away!
VIC WATERS: Luster is showing Bradford no mercy! BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER KICKS BRADFORD IN THE FACE AND BRADFORD GOES DOWN! Luster picks Bradford off the mat… BRADFORD REACHES UP AND EYE GOUGES LUSTER! Bradford bounces off the ropes…. DROPKICK! Luster is still on his feet! Bradford bounces off the ropes… HE NAILS HIM WITH A DROPKICK AND THIS TIME HE GOES DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Bradford has the best dropkick in the business today. BRANDON JACOBS: Bradford hooks the leg of Luster, SNAP SUPLEX DOWN ON THE MAT! Luster grabs his lower back! Bradford bounces off the ropes… DROPKICK TO THE FACE OF LUSTER! He goes for a cover! 1--------------2------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Luster got nailed cleanly with that dropkick but somehow he found a way to survive and these fans are trying to rally behind him to get him going!
BRANDON JACOBS: Bradford waits behind Luster as he slowly gets up…. WAISTLOCK BY BRADFORD! Luster tries to swing his elbows back… HE CATCHES BRADFORD FLUSH IN THE JAW! Bradford is staggering around dazed!
VIC WATERS: Chris Bradford needs to roll out of the ring and buy himself sometime to recover!
BRANDON JACOBS: Too late! Luster captures Bradford in a front face lock…. DANGEROUS DDT! Luster makes a cover! 1-----------------2-----------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Bradford is still grabbing his jaw in pain, by looking at the pain in his face, he may have fractured something.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster picks Bradford off the mat and bounces off the ropes…. BRADFORD GOES INSIDE OUT WITH A CLOTHESLINE! Luster is sensing it is time to go for the kill and gives the signal to the crowd for the Funk & Wagnall!
VIC WATERS: If he nails this, it will be all over! BRANDON JACOBS: Luster plants Bradford between his legs and grabs him underneath his midsection… FUNK & WAGNELL POWERBOMB! He covers! 1---------2-------------3!! LUSTER HAS BEATEN THE YOUNG WRESTLING SENATION CHRIS BRADFORD!
VIC WATERS: A very impressive win by Luster to start off the show and the fans here are going crazy for this ladies man.
BRANDON JACOBS: We will be right back with fight between two of the youngest and brightest stars in wrestling today as Diablo faces off against Chris McMillan! Stay tuned!
