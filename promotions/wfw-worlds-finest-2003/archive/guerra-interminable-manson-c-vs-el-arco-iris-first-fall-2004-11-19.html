Guerra Interminable: Manson (c) vs. El Arco Iris (First Fall)

19 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 19 November 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back and it is time for the WFW World Heavyweight Title match. El Arco Iris has returned back to Mexico, his second home, for a chance at World Heavyweight Title and the fans are out tonight in full support of El Arco Iris with thousands of children decked out in their El Arco Iris masks throughout the crowd.
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris will never beat Manson for World Title. I don’t care where we are. You and El Arco Iris are never getting that hug out of me.
BRANDON JACOBS: You seem pretty sure of yourself… VIC WATERS: If El Arco Iris wins, I’ll get down on my hands and knees and kiss El Arco Iris’ ass.
JONATHAN MARX: I believe in El Arco Iris and so do all of the fans here in Mexico. This may not be as simple as you and Manson think….
VIC WATERS: Are you telling me El Arco Iris is going to walk out of here WFW World Heavyweight Champion? In a league with Shawn Hart, Scotty Michaels, Copycat, Anarky, Sean Edmunds, Jared Wells, Maelstrom, Dan Ryan….along with a dozen other of the top names in all of wrestling, El Arco Iris will be king of them all?
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris matches up really well with Manson, he is very quick, he has a really good amateur wrestling background, he has a never say die attitude…
VIC WATERS: He is going to say die after Manson kills him. JONATHAN MARX: We will see. CROWD: EL ARCO IRIS! EL ARCO IRIS! EL ARCO IRIS! BRANDON JACOBS: My god, the fans are chanting for El Arco Iris and he isn’t even out here yet.
JONATHAN MARX: The preverbal 12th man is out in full force tonight. VIC WATERS: Must be all of the tequila these people drink. BRANDON JACOBS: We better not keep this crowd waiting any longer, folks lets send this one down to ringside and get this one underway!
(CUEUP: “Tunak Tunak Tun” by Dahler Mendhi) *god pop* RING ANNOUNCER: THIS MATCH IS A TWO OUT OF THREE FALLS MATCH FOR THE WFW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE UNDER LUCHA LIBBRE RULES! Introducing first, hailing from over the rainbow, the Prince of Prismatic, EL ARCO IRIS!
::El Arco Iris walks down to a huge pop from the crowd, dozens of children can been seen in the crowd wearing El Arco Iris masks::
(CUEUP: "Mechanical Animals" by Marilyn Manson) *chorus of loud boos* RING ANNOUNCER: And now the champion, from Chicago, IL, the WFW World Heavyweight Champion, the man, the myth, the legend…. MICHAEL MANSON!
::Manson is walking down slowly to the ring to a loud heel reaction from the crowd, he enters the ring and hands his title to the ring attendant::
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco extends his arms to Manson and offers him a hug….. MANSON CHOPS HIM ACROSS THE CHEST AND EL ARCO IRIS FIRES BACK WITH A CHOP OF HIS OWN! MANSON AND EL ARCO IRIS ARE TRADING CHOPS BACK AND FORTH!
JONATHAN MARX: These two are not wasting anytime in going at each other with all they got. VIC WATERS: Manson needs to give him a good beating and instill a little reality into him. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON IS GETTING THE BEST OF EL ARCO IRIS…. MANSON WHIPS EL ARCO IRIS OFF THE ROPES…. MANSON PUTS HIS HEAD DOWN FOR A BACK BODY DROP AND EL ARCO IRIS LEAP FROGS OVER HIM!
JONATHAN MARX: Manson made a big mistake putting his head down. VIC WATERS: When was the last time he faced someone who did a leap frog? BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND MANSON TURNS AROUND….. SHIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIINNNIIIIIIIINNNNN G WIZARD!
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris nearly took Manson’s head off with that knee. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is staggering around trying to regain his senses….. EL ARCO IRIS IS DOING HIS JIG…. THIS CROWD IS COMING UNGLUED….. EL ARCO IRIS HITS MANSON IN THE CHIN WITH THE HAPPY DANCE!
VIC WATERS: NO! This can’t be happening! BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris goes down for the cover and the ref makes the count! 1-------------------------------------------------2------------------------------------------------3 MANSON KICKS OUT JUST AFTER THE THREE! El Arco Iris has taken the first fall and all he needs is one out of the next two and he is the new WFW World Heavyweight Champion.
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the first fall….. EL ARCO IRIS! JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris caught Manson in a bull rush to start the match and we able to catch him off guard for the three. Now all he needs to do is win one of these falls
VIC WATERS: If we weren’t under Lucha rules tonight, El Arco Iris would be the new WFW World Heavyweight Champion right now.
BRANDON JACOBS: Damn, what is going to happen next, there is a two minute break between falls and when we comeback, El Arco Iris will be looking to do the impossible and beat Manson in two straight falls to win it all. STAY TUNED!
