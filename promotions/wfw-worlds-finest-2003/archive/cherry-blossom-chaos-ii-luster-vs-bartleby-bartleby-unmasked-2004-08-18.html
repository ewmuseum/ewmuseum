Cherry Blossom Chaos II: Luster vs. Bartleby, BARTLEBY UNMASKED!

18 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 18 August 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back and it is finally time to kick off the show with Luster verses the mysterious newcomer Bartleby who has been reeking havoc through out the league for months now.
JONATHAN MARX: It feels like Luster has been gone an eternity. VIC WATERS: The only retirements which stick in wrestling is death. JONATHAN MARX :This should be an interesting match, on one hand, you have Bartleby who got off to bad start when LOVE came down and distracted him in his match against Gideon and he wants to get back onto the winning path with a big win over Luster. On the other hand, you have Luster who recently turned on Scotty Michaels before he had to take his most recent hiatus and he is going to want to get that momentum that he had before he left going again and establish himself as a force in WFW.
VIC WATERS: Luster has been behind numerous controversies here in the WFW throughout his history and many speculated that Luster was indeed the Bartlelby who attacked Anarky when it happened.
JONATHAN MARX: Luster has been part of some low down dirty scheme. He even posed as Scotty Michaels’ wheelchair bound brother in order to cost Shawn Hart the world title.
BRANDON JACOBS: We will haven’t seen Shawn Hart since that incident in a WFW ring. That bastard Scotty Michaels manipulated Luster into doing his bidding and shortly after the stress over came Hart and he decided to take a break.
JONATHAN MARX: There is awful lot of stress involved with being the World Heavyweight Champion here in WFW with all the great wrestlers and the taxing schedule that you have to endure in order to be the top dog. To some extent, the same thing happened with Doc even though the injuries at the hands of Psycho did play their part.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster hopes that he can muster up a win here against Bartleby and get in line for a title shot. Luster was the one who ended Manson’s WFW North American Title reign and I am sure he would love to be the one who ended his WFW World Heavyweight Title reign as well.
JONATHAN MARX: Luster should just be banned from all title matches unless he is in them, he even cost Scotty Michaels the WFW World Heavyweight Title in the iron man match against Sean Edmunds.
VIC WATERS: Nobody is safe when Luster is around. He has an aptitude for making things happen.
BRANDON JACOBS: That is why I have full confidence in Luster tonight that he’ll unmask this mad man Bartleby and put an end to all the turmoil he has been causing. I even promised a bonus to Luster if he manages to yank off the hood tonight.
VIC WATERS: Don’t get your hopes up Jacobs. Bartleby certain doesn’t want that hood coming off or he wouldn’t have it to begin with. It is going to be months before we see the face of Bartleby and we know who this masked man really is.
BRANDON JACOBS: I hope you are wrong, but realistically I have to agree with you. But I think at least Luster will give Bartleby a good old fashioned button whopping in order to send a message to him and his group of thugs that he can’t just do anything he wants whenever he wants to do it.
VIC WATERS: Can’t you guys give peace a chance? There is no reason for all this unnecessary violence.
BRANDON JACOBS: Never. Folks, let’s send this one down to ringside and get this one underway!
(CUEUP: “"Funkytown" by Disco Fever) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the man who helped end Hart’s WFW World Heavyweight Title reign and the sexiest man in all of WFW, from Atlanta, Georgia… LUSTER!
::Luster comes down dancing to the ring as the crowd cheers loudly:: (CUEUP: “Look At Me" by Geri Haliwell) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Parts Unknown making his PPV debut here tonight, BARTLEBY!
::the masked Bartleby walks down to the ring slowly to the boos to the crowd and enters the ring::
VIC WATERS: Oh great, last show he stole Doc’s entrance music, now he is stealing Shawn Hart’s.
BRANDON JACOBS: This fellow is quite the deceptive pain in the butt, but Luster will give him his.
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: BARTLEBY CHARGES LUSTER BEFORE THE BELL AND JUMPS ON TOP OF HIM AND STARTS POUNDING AWAY WITH LEFTS AND RIGHTS!
VIC WATERS: Man, what got into Bartleby? BRANDON JACOBS: THE REFEREE IS TRYING TO PRY BARTLEBY OFF OF LUSTER…. BARTLEBY THROWS THE REF DOWN! Luster tries to crawl away towards the ropes but Bartleby grabs his legs to avoid letting him get away!
JONATHAN MARX: Luster has a firm grip on those ropes, if the referee can force a break. Luster may have the chance to regroup that he needs.
BRANDON JACOBS: Bartleby lets go of legs of Luster… BARTLEBY GRABS THE HAIR OF LUSTER IN ORDER TO PEEL HIM OFF THE ROPES… LUSTER LETS GO OF THE ROPES AND HE RAKES BARTLEBY RIGHT IN THE EYES!
VIC WATERS: The great equalizer! BRANDON JACOBS: Bartleby is grabbing his eyes…. JONATHAN MARX: This sort of reminds me of Unicron after the Autobots flew through his eyes.
VIC WATERS: Stop dating yourself. JONATHAN MARX: Somebody has to… BRANDON JACOBS: Luster is using the ropes to get up…. LUSTER IS COMING BACK WITH AN OFFENSIVE ASSAULT OF HIS OWN WITH A SERIES OF RIGHTS AND LEFTS DRIVING BARTLEBY BACK! Bartleby is staggering…
VIC WATER: Stay on your feet Bartleby! BRANDON JACOBS: Luster bounces off the ropes… LUSTER CLOTHESLINES BARTLEBY DOWN TO THE MAT!
.JONATHAN MARX: DOWN GOES BARTLEBY! BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER PUTS BARTLEBY IN A HEADLOCK ON THE GROUND AND HE IS UNTYING THE STRINGS ON THE BACK OF THE MASK! WE ARE FINALLY GOING TO FIND OUT WHO BARTLEBY IS!
VIC WATERS: The referee should DQ Luster for this! If this was El Arco Iris, you guys would be screaming bloody murder.
BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is coming over and he is warning Luster not to take it off… THE REFEREE JUMPS ONTO LUSTER’S BACK TO KEEP HIM FROM UNMASKING BARTLEBY!
JONATHAN MARX: Damn it! VIC WATERS: Taking someone’s mask is like stealing their identity and we all know from those VISA commercials that identity theft is bad.
BRANDON JACOBS: Bartleby is trying to get up… BARTLEBY CHARGES AND HE NAILS LUSTER WITH A LOW BLOW AS THE REFEREE IS HANGING AROUND HIS NECK! The referee crashes back into the turnbuckle and bangs the back of his head.
JONATHAN MARX: How can he not be DQed for that?! VIC WATERS: The referee was in no position to call it hanging on the back of Luster… then he got killed.
BRANDON JACOBS: BARTLEBY THROWS LUSTER THROUGH THE ROPES AND OUT TO THE OUTSIDE! VIC WATERS: Things are about to get really nasty here… BRANDON JACOBS: Bartleby is going through the ropes out of him…. BARTLEBY GRABS LUSTER BY THE ARM AND HE WHIPS HIM RIGHT INTO THE STEEL STAIRS!
JONATHAN MARX: My god, Luster’s back is going to be spasming for a week after that. BRANDON JACOBS: BARTLEBY IS GRABBING THE TIME KEEPER’S CHAIR AND HE IS GOING AFTER LUSTER! Luster doesn’t see him and he trying to get up… BARTLEBY BLASTS HIM WITH THE STEEL CHAIR RIGHT OVER THE HEAD AND NOW LUSTER IS BUSTED OPEN!
VIC WATERS: Now there is a biological terror threat if I ever saw one…. JONATHAN MARX: LOOK! LUSTER IS STILL STAGGERING ON HIS FEET! HE DIDN’T GO DOWN! BRANDON JACOBS: BARTLEBY GOES FOR ANOTHER STEEL CHAIR SHOT BUT THIS TIME LUSTER GETS HIS HANDS UP AND THEY ARE STRUGGLING OVER THE CHAIR!
VIC WATERS: Both men know whoever gets that chair is going to use it as a weapon so they aren’t going to let go and let themselves get clobbered.
BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER PULLS THE CHAIR AWAY FROM BARTLEBY…. WAIT A SECOND, BARTLEBY CHARGES LUSTER WITH EL CODO EXPOSIVO RIGHT INTO THE CHAIR RAMMING IT INTO LUSTER’S FACE CAUSING HIM TO GO DOWN!
JONATHAN MARX: There goes Bartleby, stealing from Shawn Hart again. BRANDON JACOBS: Bartleby is standing tall while both Luster and the referee are down…. VIC WATERS: This isn’t a wrestling match, this is a fight and Bartleby wants nothing more to give Luster the beating of a lifetime just as I told you he would.
BRANDON JACOBS: BARTLEBY IS GOING TO PICK THE BLOODY AND UNCONSCIOUS LUSTER UP AND HE ROLLS HIM BACK INTO THE RING! It looks like he is going to try to end this now!
VIC WATERS: He is a fool if he doesn’t. BRANDON JACOBS: Bartleby rolls back in…. NO! SOMEONE GRABBED HIS FEET! JONATHAN MARX: LOOK! OH MY GOD, THERE ARE THREE BARTLEBIES! BRANDON JACOBS: They are dragging him under the ring… WAIT A SECOND, BARTLEBY IS EMERGING FROM UNDER THE RING! Thank god he escaped, this is undoubtedly some ploy from LOVE.
VIC WATERS: You can’t prove that you know. JONATHAN MARX: This Bartleby looks a little bit different… BRANDON JACOBS: BARTLEBY ENTERS THE RING AND HE TRYING TO WAKE UP THE REFEREE IN ORDER TO GO FOR THE PINFALL…
VIC WATERS: That ref really had his clocked clean badly. JONATHAN MARX: It looks like the ref is finally shaking some cob webs off. BRANDON JACOBS: Bartleby walks over to pick Luster off the mat…. LUSTER GOES FOR A SMALL PACKAGE ON BARTLEBY! The referee goes down for the count! 1-------------------------------------------------2-------------------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. LUSTER! BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER HAS DONE IT AND HE GOES TO THE SECOND TURNBUCKLE TO RAISE HIS ARMS IN VICTORY! Wait a second, ANOTHER BARTLEBY COMES FROM UNDER THE RING AND ROLLS BACK IN AND HE IS ARGUING THAT LUSTER DIDN’T PIN THE REAL BARTLEBY!
JONATHAN MARX: This is all so confusing… BRANDON JACOBS: ANOTHER BARTLEBY IS COMING INTO THE RING…. LUSTER RUSHES OVER, KICKS HIM IN THE MIDSECTION, AND HE FUNK & WAGNALL POWERBOMBS HIM DOWN TO THE MAT! The other Bartleby who Luster pinned is getting up… HE RUSHES TO THE AID OF THE POWERBOMBED BARTLEBY…. LUSTER KICKS HIM IN THE MIDSECTION AND FUNK & WAGNALL POWERBOMBS HIM DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: We are down to one Bartleby again! BRANDON JACOBS: BARTLEBY PUSHES THE REF ASIDE AND HE CHARGES LUSTER! Luster kicks in the midsection… HE IS GOING FOR THE FUNK AND WAGNALL! Bartleby is trying to escape…. LUSTER GRABS A HOLD OF BARTLEBY’S MASK AND BARTLEBY PULLS AWAY… OH MY GOD! BARTLEBY HAS BEEN UNMASKED!
CROWD: SHAWN HART! SHAWN HART! SHAWN HART! JONATHAN MARX: Oh my god, that is Shawn Hart! THE FORMER WFW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION SHAWN HART IS BACK!
VIC WATERS: Former and greatest, next to Doc. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster is in shock! SHAWN HART BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND HE NAILS LUSTER WITH THE EL CODO EXPLOSIVO!
(CUEUP: “Dragnet TV Theme”) JONATHAN MARX: Shawn Hart is leaving the ring, but this clearly isn’t over. BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart has to be pissed, Luster not only will go down in the record books as beating Bartleby, but he also unmasked by Luster, the same man who cost him his WFW World Heavyweight Title.
JONATHAN MARX: He deserves it after his attacks on you and Anarky. Karma is a *****. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we have to take a commercial break, but when we comeback, RICHARD GIDEON AND JOHN DOE WILL GO ONE ON ONE! STAY TUNED!
