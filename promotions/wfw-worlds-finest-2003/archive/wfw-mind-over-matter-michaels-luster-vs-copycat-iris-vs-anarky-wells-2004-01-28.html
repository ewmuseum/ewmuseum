WFW Mind Over Matter: Michaels/Luster vs. Copycat/Iris vs. Anarky/Wells

28 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 28 January 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: Time for the three way dance! The team of the WFW World Heavyweight Champion Scotty Michaels and his partner in crime Luster will take on the team Copycat & El Arco Iris and Anarky & Jared Wells in a three way dance with the winners being able to name the stipulation to their match.
JONATHAN MARX: What happens if Anarky’s team wins? Isn’t the stipulation for his match already made?
BRANDON JACOBS: Thanks for bringing that up, if Anarky’s team wins, Anarky can ADD a stipulation to his match or save that right for another show sometime down the road, sort of as mad money for when he needs it.
VIC WATERS: How is that fair to Scotty Michaels and Luster? They can’t save their stipulations if they win.
BRANDON JACOBS: Life isn’t always fair. Plus, this match was before the WFW made before Anarky vs. Mephisto was signed and this is the WFW’s way of making it up to Anarky that they intervened and set the stipulation for him. But anyways, all this info may not even matter if either Scotty Michaels & Luster or Copycat & El Arco Iris win the match instead. Who are you going with in this match Jonathan?
JONATHAN MARX: I’m going to go with Copycat & El Arco Iris. Both men have really strong wins coming off of their matches at Merrython and Copycat has proven that he can outsmart Anarky pretty easily.
VIC WATERS: Is that what wrestling has turned in to? A game of petty trickery? Copycat’s actions in his match with Anarky were despicable.
JONATHAN MARX: Wrestling has and will always be about playing games with your opponent. Trying to out think him, trying to trick him, trying to get into his head… Copycat has gotten into Anarky’s head.
VIC WATERS: That is Copycat propaganda. Copycat is not in Anarky’s head and I were Copycat, I’d watch myself tonight because Anarky is going to be looking for revenge for what happened at Merrython.
JONATHAN MARX: So, who are you picking to win? VIC WATERS: I’m going with Michaels and Luster who have screwed over some of the biggest legends in this sport working together as a team. Tonight they are adding Copycat, Anarky, Jared Wells, and El Arco Iris to their list.
JONATHAN MARX: Never, they won’t get them. VIC WATERS: We’ll see smart man. BRANDON JACOBS: You two can fight it out later, let’s send this one down to ringside and get this match underway!
(CUEUP: “It Is My Life” by Bon Jovi) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the current WFW World Heavyweight Champion, the man loved by viewer’s worldwide and their pets, from Philadelphia, PA, THE SUPERSTAR SCOTTY MICHAELS!
::Michaels comes down to the loud boos from the crowd:: (CUEUP: “"Funkytown"-Disco Fever) RING ANNOUNCER: And now…. the second man of first team, the man who helped end Hart’s WFW World Heavyweight Title reign and the sexiest man in all of WFW, from Atlanta, Georgia… LUSTER!
::Luster comes down dancing to the ring as the crowd boos:: (CUEUP: “Here to stay” by Korn) RING ANNOUNCER: The first man of the second team, from the state which has produced two of the three WFW World Heavyweight champions, the former WWL World Heavyweight Champion…. “RAGE ‘O FIRE” JARED WELLS!
::Jared Wells comes out to a mixed reaction from the crowd:: (CUEUP: “Sober” by Tool) RING ANNOUNCER: And now the second man of the second team, a world champion ten times over and former Mr. Vicious title holder, from Hartford, Connecticut… ANARKY!
::Anarky comes down to the ring to the loud boos of the crowd:: (CUEUP: “Tunak Tunak Tun” by Dahler Mendhi) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing the first man from the final team, hailing from over the rainbow, the Warrior of Happiness, EL ARCO IRIS!
::El Arco Iris walks down to a huge pop from the crowd, dozens of children can been seen in the crowd wearing El Arco Iris masks, and enters the ring::
CUEUP: Copycat voice/over “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, LEND ME YOUR EARS!” followed by “My Way” by Butch Walker) RING ANNOUNCER: And last but not least, the former WWL World Heavyweight Champion, from Kalamazoo, Michigan… COPYCAT!
::Copycat walks down to the ring with microphone in hand and enters the ring:: Copycat: People of Princeton! Lend me your ears! (cheap pop) As I'm sure you all know, my tag-team partner and I have recently discussed how to cheer up our depressingly sad opponents. And as I'm sure you all know, we've decided that the best way to cheer up ANYONE is with birthday cake. Now, I'm sure there must be a Cat Lover out there in the audience whose birthday is today, and we're going to celebrate it right here in the ring. Anyone? An old, old lady comes to the restraining barrier. Security lets her into the ringside area. Copycat: And what's your name, young lady? Old Lady: My name is Mildred Sampson. Copycat: And how old are you today, Mildred? Old Lady: I am 83 years old. Copycat: And still as full of life as you ever were, I'm sure. Now, if one of my opponents would be so kind as to cut the cake, we can get this thing started. ::Copycat pauses::. Copycat: Actually, on second thought, I don't think I want any of you getting your hands on a knife. But once this match is over, we can all have some birthday cake, and I'm sure you'll all be cheered up. Mildred, you can wait at ringside. ::El Arco Iris helps Mildred out of the ring:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY PUSHES THE OLD WOMAN FROM BEHIND AND SHE CRASHES DOWN ON THE FLOOR! El Arco Iris rushes to her side to help her up.
JONATHAN MARX: There is no call for treating an 83 year old woman like that. VIC WATERS: She doesn’t belong in the ring. JONATHAN MARX: She definitely used poor judgment. BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels is giving Anarky a thumbs up as Copycat looks on with a horrified look on his face. MICHAELS, LUSTER, ANARKY, AND WELLS ALL JUMP ON COPYCAT AND PROCEED TO START BEATING THE LIVING HELL OUT OF HIM!
VIC WATERS: A good old Lucha four on one… BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels is holding Copycat’s arms back and Anarky, Wells, and Luster begin to lay waste on him with a series of quick closed fists. A troubled El Arco Iris sees Copycat in trouble and leaves Mildred’s side, EL ARCO IRIS IS CLIMBING UP TO THE TOP ROPE! Michaels is warning everyone to look behind them…. EL ARCO IRIS FLIES OFF WITH A CROSSBODY ONTO ALL FIVE MEN AND ALL FIVE MEN GO DOWN!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris looks incensed over what happened to Mildred. JONATHAN MARX: He should be, an 83 year old woman can’t fall down like that without breaking a hip. It looks like Mildred will be fine, but she is very lucky.
BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels is the first man up, sometimes it pays to be the man in the back of a pile. SCOTTY MICHAELS WHIPS EL ARCO IRIS OFF THE ROPES…. FLYING FOREARM! Scotty Michaels goes for the pin! 1------------------------2—WELLS GRABS MICHAELS BY THE HAIR AND BREAKS UP THE QUICK TWO!
JONATHAN MARX: It was much too soon to go for a pin, but when everyone else is down in a match like this, you have to take your chance because opportunities like that doesn’t arise all the time.
BRANDON JACOBS: JARED WELLS RAMS THE HEAD OF MICHAELS REPEATEDLY INTO THE NOW COVER TURNBUCKLE PAD!
VIC WATERS: And that move still hurts, padding or not… BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels comes out of the corner dazed… Wells kicks Michaels in the midsection to double him over and he picks him up in the piledriver position…. JUMPING PILEDRIVER!
JONATHAN MARX: Nothing like a jolt to the neck on the show right before the PPV to make things interesting.
VIC WATERS: Michaels is the golden boy of the WFW. You’d think they would outlaw piledrivers on the champ.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is grabbing his neck…. LUSTER COMES OVER TO HELP WELLS…. Luster missed with the clothesline…. WELLS NAILS LUSTER ON THE JAW WITH THE SAVATE KICK! Luster goes down!
JONATHAN MARX: The last time Michaels and Luster team together, Luster took the pinfall and you know that it is in the back of Luster’s mind that he wants to make up for that and not let it happen again.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wells is lining up the bodies and he is heading up for the top… this crowd is on its feet…. LEG DROP OFF THE TOP ROPE ONTO MICHAELS AND LUSTER! Well goes for the cover! 1-----------------------------------------2-------- COPYCAT BREAKS UP THE PINFALL!
JONATHAN MARX: It is a good thing that he did, I don’t think they would have survived after that double Alabama Jam Bobby Eaton style.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat picks Wells up off the mat…. COPYCAT AND WELLS ARE BRAWLING BACK AND FORTH! Wells is getting the best of Copycat…. HERE COMES EL ARCO IRIS AND HE COMES TO THE AID OF COPYCAT! Wells is trying to fight both men off!
JONATHAN MARX: Jared Wells is looking really impressive tonight. VIC WATERS: This isn’t the Jared Wells show, where is Anarky? BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky took the grunt of El Arco iris’ bodypress and had a couple of people hand on him hard…. WELLS IS HOLDING COPYCAT AND EL ARCO IRIS OFF ALTERNATING SHOTS BETWEEN BOTH MEN!
JONATHAN MARX: Jared Wells won’t be able to keep this up forever. BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS GET A HARD SHOT IN AND SO DOES COPYCAT! Copycat picks up Jared Wells and bodyslams him down to the mat! Copycat is signaling to El Arco Iris to bounces off the ropes…. COPYCAT CATCHES IRIS WITH A RELEASE BELLY TO BELLY LIKE THROW RIGHT ONTO WELLS WHO EL ARCO IRIS NAILS WITH A HURRACANRANA!
CROWD: BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris holds the legs and keeps Wells a the pinning position! The ref goes down for the count! 1----------------------------------------2----------------------- WELLS KICKS OUT!
JONATHAN MARX: Nice double team work between Copycat and El Arco Iris, but the just came up a little bit short.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is calling for Copycat to catch his foot…. El Arco Iris charges Copycat AND COPYCAT FLIPS HIM OVER AND HE DELIVERS A MOONSAULT SPLASH RIGHT ONTO WELLS!
JONATHAN MARX: These two are using everything but the kitchen sink to get the pinfall early and win this match.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris has the cover! The ref goes down for the count! 1-----------------------------------------------2------------------------------ANARKY BREAKS UP THE PINFALL ATTEMPT!
VIC WATERS: Thank god Anarky is back, Wells couldn’t keep these two at bay forever. BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY GRABS EL ARCO IRIS BY THE MASK AND HE THROWS HIM OUT OF THE RING! JONATHAN MARX: There was no call for that, absolutely no call. BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky and Copycat lock up….. HIPTOSS BY ANARKY! Copycat gets back up and ANARKY CATCHES THE BIG MAN IN A SLEEPER!
JONATHAN MARX: Big men are much more susceptible to moves like this than smaller men because it takes more oxygen for a big man to wrestle a competitive match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels and Luster are getting up…. MICHAELS PUTS A SLEEPER ON ANARKY! Three way sleeper!
JONATHAN MARX: You’d think one man would break it to get out of the other sleeper. BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT IS GOING OUT! The ref raises Copycat’s hand…. IT FALLS ONCE! The ref raises Copycat’s hand a second time…. IT FALLS! Michaels is ordering Luster to break up Anarky’s sleeper!
VIC WATERS: If he doesn’t, this match is over. BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER BREAKS OPEN THE ARMS OF ANARKY AND RESCUES COPYCAT TO AVOID ANARKY FROM WINNING! Luster gets into Anarky’s face…. ANARKY LOCKS THE SLEEPER ONTO LUSTER!
JONATHAN MARX: HA! BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat gets up… AND HE IS GOING TO BACK TO PUT A SLEEPER ON MICHAELS! Four way sleeper!
JONATHAN MARX: This doesn’t seem like it is accomplishing anything. BRANDON JACOBS: Here comes El Arco Iris from the outside… HE CRAWLS ONTO THE SHOULDERS OF COPYCAT AND STARTS WALKING ON THE BRIDGE OF HUMAN BODIES! El Arco Iris jumps up and down on the shoulders OF ANARKY AND ANARKY IS SEPERATED FROM BOTH MICHAELS AND LUSTER LEAVING COPYCAT ALONE WITH A SLEEPER ON MICHAELS!
JONATHAN MARX: Okay, I have…. NEVER…. Seen anything like that before. BRANDON JACOBS: Jared Wells is finally up and he waistlocks Copycat? JARED WELLS GERMAN SUPLEXES COPYCAT WHO STILL HAS THE SLEEPER ON MICHAELS AND BOTH MEN GO CRASH DOWN ON THE MAT! Copycat let go of Michaels mid move and the momentum sent Michaels through the ropes and down to the concrete floor below!
JONATHAN MARX: Michaels looked like he bumped his head really hard on the ground. BRANDON JACOBS: Wells goes for the cover on Copycat! Here comes El Arco Iris and he breaks up the pinfall attempt even before the ref could count!
VIC WATERS: In order for anyone to win in this match, they are going to have to get opponents on the outside so they could easily get a cover.
BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS CATCHES WELLS BY SURPRISE AND DELIVERS LA MAGISTRA ROLLING CRADLE PIN!
JONATHAN MARX: Around and around he goes…. BRANDON JACOBS: This crowd is on their feet, El Arco stops rolling and the ref goes down for the count! 1---------------------------------------------------------2---------------------- ANARKY BREAKS UP THE PINFALL ATTEMPT! Jared Wells is trying to stand up but he has a bad case of vertigo. Anarky pushes El Arco Iris into the corner and begins to unload on him.
VIC WATERS: Like Wells doesn’t know what that feels like. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster still looks out of it from the sleeper but he is picking himself back up. LUSTER CHARGES JARED WELLS WITH A SPEAR…. HE HITS! Luster makes the cover! The ref goes down to make the cover! 1-----------------------------------------2----------------------ANARKY BREAKS UP THE PINFALL!
JONATHAN MARX: LOOK! EL ARCO IRIS HAS CLIMBED UP TO THE SECOND TURNBUCKLE! BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky turns around to get back to work on El Arco Iris…. EL ARCO IRIS CATCHES HIM IN THE CROWN OF HATHOR TORNADO DDT! El Arco Iris goes for the pin on Anarky! Luster and Jared Wells are fighting out with punches flying fast and furious! The ref goes down for the count on Anarky! 1--------------------------------------2--------LUSTER HITS JARED WELLS AND WELLS FALLS BACK AND BREAKS UP THE PINFALL ON ANARKY!
JONATHAN MARX: Wells with the nice unintentional save… VIC WATERS: Wells did that on purpose, the man is a genius. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris PUSHES Jared Wells off of him…. EL ARCO IRIS CHARGES LUSTER AND LUSTER NAILS HIM WITH THE SPINEBUSTER! El Arco Iris is grabbing his lower back. The impact of that must have sent a chill sensation through his spine.
JONATHAN MARX: The spinebuster is one of the most effective and least used moves on the planet sadly. Probably because it isn’t flashy enough for today’s kids.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster turns El Arco Iris over and drops an elbow drop right in the center of his back! Copycat is getting back up but the wait of Michaels along with his own body weight sent him really hard down on his head and he looks like he is not totally there right now. LUSTER KNEE LIFTS COPYCAT IN THE CHEST AND HITS THE DDT!
VIC WATERS: If Luster covers Copycat, it is over! Copycat is almost out cold! BRANDON JACOBS: Luster covers! The ref goes down for the count! 1----------------------------------------2-------------------------ANARKY PULLS LUSTER OFF OF COPYCAT AND SCREAMS A LUSTER “HE’S MINE!” AND THROWS LUSTER OVER THE TOP ROPE ONTO SCOTTY MICHAELS WHO WAS ATTEMPTING TO GET UP!
JONATHAN MARX: Michaels can’t seem to catch a break in this match. BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky drags Copycat to the center of the ring… El Arco Iris charges Anarky…. ANARKY NAILS HIM WITH A CHEAP SHOT RIGHT TO THE STOMACH! ANARKY NAILS EL ARCO IRS WITH THE CHAOS BREAKER AND IS ORDERING JARED WELLS TO ROLL EL ARCO IRIS OUT OF THE RING! Jared Wells picks up El Arco Iris over his shoulder… Luster and Michaels are getting up on the outside…. JARED WELLS THROWS EL ARCO IRIS ONTO LUSTER AND MICHAELS!
VIC WATERS: This is it, Anarky is going to get his revenge on Copycat! BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second, Mildred has jumped to the ring and she is yelling at Anarky. Anarky just cursed Mildred... that is no way to treat an old woman!
JONATHAN MARX: Mildred better get out of there, there is no one to save her now. BRANDON JACOBS: MILDRED KICKS ANARKY IN THE GROIN AND ANARKY IS DOUBLED OVER IN PAIN! Anarky grabs the old woman by the hair as she tries to escape…. How dare he do this to an 83 year old woman… WAIT A SECOND, HER HAIR IS COMING OFF…. ANARKY PULLS OFF A MASK OFF OF HER… THE OLD WOMAN WAS ICEKOLD! Icekold escapes to the outside and Anarky at the wig in his hand!
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is slowly getting up behind Anarky…. Copycat is out of it, but may have enough left to finish this off…. ANARKY TURNS AROUND AND COPYCAT DELIVERS A DEVASTATING KIT KAT KICK!
VIC WATERS: NO DAMNIT! Copycat got Anarky again! BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat covers Anarky! 1---------- THE LIGHTS HAVE GONE OUT ALL THROUGHOUT THE BUILDING!
::lights turn off:: JONATHAN MARX: What happened? VIC WATERS: Did I pass out again? ::lights go back on again:: BRANDON JACOBS: THE LIGHTS ARE BACK ON AND A MASKED MAN IS STANDING OVER THE BODY OF COPYCAT! Copycat is trying desperately to crawl away…. JARED WELLS CATCHES COPYCAT AND PICKS HIM OFF THE MAT… RAGE BOMB RIGHT INTO THE MAT! Jared Wells makes the cover! 1---------------------------------------------2---------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match… THE TEAM OF ANARKY AND JARED WELLS! BRANDON JACOBS: Icekold goes to the aid of Copycat who has been laid in the ring and is barely conscious and is trying to get him out of the ring. Jared Wells and the Masked Man are helping the irritated Anarky up to his feet.

BRANDON JACOBS:Anarky, Jared Wells, and the Masked Man raise each other’s hands in victory. What we have witnessed tonight is possibly the birth of a great alliance and knowing what evil these three men are capable of, god help us.

Michaels and Luster are screaming at Anarky, Jared Wells, and the Masked Man that they will get their revenge.
