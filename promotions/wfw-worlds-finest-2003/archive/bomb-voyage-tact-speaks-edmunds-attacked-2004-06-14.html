BOMB Voyage: Tact Speaks, EDMUNDS ATTACKED!

14 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 14 June 2004 (BACKSTAGE) (Tact sitting in a steel chair once again, surrounded by black backdrops this time.) TACT: "It's about time people stopped *****ing around here. Among other things, I've recognized the past few weeks just how many people DO ***** and moan around this promotion. And, sadly, I've had to admit to myself that I AM one of those people....or, I was during the recent past of WFW. But that's going to change. Cuz whether I complain about a questionable outcome or someone interfering in a match, what does it do? Where does it get me? I'm still lacking the serious progress I wanted to begin making in December.
I've yet to establish that I can handle top tier opposition here. And possibly the worst of it....I'm still not satisfied. Although that's not a wholly bad thing, with this situation I'm in."
(Tact leans forward, eyes narrowed, clasped hands bracing his chin.) TACT: "Because in being unsatisfied, it's forced me to look over my performances, time and again for about a month now. And in that, I've found something that was missing for a while. I've found what might just be that spark I need. Watching those tapes has HUMBLED.....me. It took a bit of time, but I've rediscovered the humble path again. So Sean Edmunds, if you think you have an edge on me, cuz I'm doubting myself? I'll show you that I'm not doubting my abilities. I'll show you that, humble as I am, I'm no fool. I know how good I am, and I know that 2004 is STILL going to be MY YEAR. Just as tonight will be...simply...."
(Tact's eyes widen) TACT: "TACTILIZING!" (RINGSIDE) JONATHAN MARX: Not so fast, we are being told the President is backstage and he has something to stay. Let’s send it back to Black Rose.
(BACKSTAGE) ::Black Rose is standing next to Edmunds in front of a back drop:: BLACK ROSE: Mister President, you’ve heard Tact’s comments, would you like to respond? ::holds up the microphone to Edmunds, all of a sudden a fan comes into the picture, security rushes in::
SEAN EDMUNDS: Let him go. ::security backs off:: FAN: I’m sick and ****ing tired of you holding down Copycat and Tact. They deserve your respect and admiration instead of being repeatedly crapped on. Why are you keeping Copycat from getting title shots? Why are you keeping Tact down in the midcard makes filmsy excuses like you have a luau to get to?
SEAN EDMUNDS: Because just like that pig I’m roasting, they are both swine. ::the fan pushes Edmunds and Edmunds falls back over a speaker and grabs his knee:: BLACK ROSE: GET THIS IDIOT OUT OF HERE! Are you okay Mister President? ::Edmunds in hollering in pain:: BLACK ROSE: Somebody get a doctor out here! (RINGSIDE) JONATHAN MARX: We are going to take a short commercial break while the paramedics tend to Edmunds, hopefully he’ll be well enough to wrestle Tact but who knows at this point, STAY TUNED!
