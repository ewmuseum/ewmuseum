WFW Ghoulish Games: Maelstrom/Ryan vs. Edmunds/Iron Lion (Kidnapping & Sneak Attacks)

21 December 2004

PaulNJ21 — 21 December 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: Sean Edmunds and Iron Lion aren’t the most likely of partners, they have been bickering back and forth over who deserves a title shot and who doesn’t. But tonight, in order to win this match against Inner Circle, they are going to have to work as a team if they want to beat Maelstrom and Dan Ryan.
JONATHAN MARX: Maelstrom and Dan Ryan are going to be tough to beat. They may not go out every night and drink together, but they are bound together by a business relationship both men want to succeed. They are going to do everything in their power to send Edmunds and Iron Lion home with their tail between their legs.
VIC WATERS: What makes things even worse is that it was El Arco Iris who forced these men to team up, the same man who was the very first person to beat Iron Lion here in WFW. Don’t think Iron Lion doesn’t remember that.
BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion seems be a new man. He has a whole new ideology on life. JONATHAN MARX: In wrestling, you take your bumps and you become the person that you are going to be. Everything happens for a reason.
VIC WATERS: While Edmunds is a former WFW World Heavyweight Champion, he is clearly the smallest man in the match and you can sense both Maelstrom and Dan Ryan will work to isolate Edmunds and try to finish him off.
JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds career has had many peaks and valleys here in WFW, but what makes him so great is when he has those peaks, he is among the elite of the elite. I would put Edmunds among any of the alltime greats as his best. If he could ever just avoid falling into those valleys as far as he does, he would be even more of a force than he is.
VIC WATERS: We all saw how Maelstrom took it to Wells and Ryan stood side by side with Copycat against Psycho & Michaels. Both Edmunds and Iron Lion will have to be at the peak of their game to beat these and even then it isn’t even a sure thing.
BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, lets send this down to ringside and get this match underway! (CUEUP: “Is This The End” by Creed) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from Anchorage, Alaska, a man focused on bringing honor, pride, and strength back to professional wrestling, IRON LION!
::Iron Lion walks down to the roar of the crowd and enters the ring:: (Cue up: "Zero" by Smashing Pumpkins.) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, a former CSWA World Heavyweight Champion and member of Inner Circle, from Houston, Texas….. “Ego Buster” Dan Ryan!

(Ryan walks down to ringside with Lindsay Troy at his side as pyro erupts along the ramp beside him. He rolls under the bottom rope and climbs a corner turnbuckle and simply glares through the sunglasses into the crowd.)
CUEUP: "Brain Stew" by Green Day) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, currently residing in his fall residence in the Catskills, a three time WWL World Champion and current President of the WFW….. 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds!
:: Edmunds comes down to the ring accompanied by Sherri and enters the ring to the loud boos from the crowd::
(CUEUP: “Enter Sandman” by Metallica) ::god pop:: RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, a member of the Inner Circle, the former and greatest GLCW Heavyweight Champion of Alltime, from Pandora, Island….. MAELSTROM!
::Maelstrom comes down to the ring to the ovation of the crowd and enters the ring:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Both sides are talking to their partners and they agree that Maelstrom and Edmunds will start off the match.
JONATHAN MARX: This is a good decision, as well conditioned as Dan Ryan and Iron Lion are, bigger men get tired faster.
VIC WATERS: To the best of my knowledge, these two haven’t faced off since their WWWL days and that was awhile ago.
JONATHAN MARX: When you get back into the ring with an old opponent, it all comes right back to you. I am sure these two have been reviewing tapes and brushing up on each other in order to get prepared for this match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Maelstrom walks to the center of the ring and he extends his arms to Edmunds for a lock up. Edmunds is looking a little weary.
JONATHAN MARX: There may be wrestlers bigger, but Maelstrom is clearly the strongest wrestler I have ever faced. When he rips into you, he really rips into you.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds walks up Maelstrom to lock up….. but he pulls them back and walks away with second thoughts.

VIC WATERS: He is going to be awfully dizzy when this finally stops. JONATHAN MARX: If Maelstrom can throw off Edmunds equilibrium, he might be able to take away Edmunds’ speed advantage.

JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds has a high tolerance, he spent some of his best years with Jared Wells on the road and he has tremendous… conditioning.
