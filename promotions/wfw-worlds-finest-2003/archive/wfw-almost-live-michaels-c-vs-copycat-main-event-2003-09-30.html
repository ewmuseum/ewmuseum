WFW Almost Live: Michaels (c) vs. Copycat - Main Event

30 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 30 September 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Some people think Michaels is looking too far ahead too see after winning his first tile in six years. The WFW World Heavyweight Champion Shawn Hart is not an easy man to beat.
VIC WATERS: No, he isn’t. But there is plenty of time left, if Shawn Hart can somehow get past Luster to Manson, who knows what kind of condition he will be in when he wrestles his next match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson can be very vicious when he has something in his sights that he wants. Shawn Hart is one of the corner stones of this federation, but beating Manson is going to be an incredible task sort of like Michaels attempting to retain the title tonight against Copycat.
VIC WATERS: Michaels is a man motivated by ambition. He wants nothing else but to be WFW World Heavyweight Champion like half a dozen other guys in the back, but he has shown time and time again that he is willing to take shortcuts to get there and his main goal is the big prize at the end.
BRANDON JACOBS: Absolutely. Between C.O.C.KS. and the team of Scotty Michaels/Luster/Heel I don’t know who I fear more. But if there is one man not named Manson who can put Scotty Michaels in his place, it is Copycat. Copycat has a world of talent and the fans here have really taken a liking to him. The WFW North American Title is his first step to achieving WFW World Heavyweight gold and he knows that. If he can somehow win tonight, he can use this to be parlayed into a World Title shot against Shawn Hart.
VIC WATERS: Definitely. WFW in reality has two really strong champions, Shawn Hart and Scotty Michaels that are both main eventers in the eyes of the people even if they hate them to death.
BRANDON JACOBS: That they are, well Vic, it is finally time for the main event, the long awaited match between Scotty Michaels and Copycat is finally going to happen. LET’S SEND THIS ONE TO RINGSIDE!
(CUEUP: Copycat voice/over “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, LEND ME YOUR EARS!” followed by “My Way” by Butch Walker) RING ANNOUNCER: And the first the challenger for the WFW North American Title, former WWL World Heavyweight Champion, from Kalamazoo, Michigan… COPYCAT!
::Copycat walks down to the ring with microphone in hand and enters the ring:: Copycat: PEOPLE OF REVERE! LEND ME YOUR EARS! As you know, my opponent tonight is one Scotty Michaels. Scotty, of course, refers to himself as the "Viewer's Choice." And he also claims that he is considered one of WFW's major stars. Now, I've challenged his claim in recent days, but I suppose I could be wrong. So I think the fair thing to do would be to ask the people whose opinions matter the most on this issue: The fans *crowd pop*. So, how many people here tonight are Scotty's "Viewers"? *crowd boos* And how many people here in REVERE, MASSACHUSETTS... *cheap pop* ...are Cat Lovers? *big pop* Well now...I hesitate once again, because I could be wrong...but it seems to me that there are a lot more people here tonight to see the Cat than there are to see the "Viewer's Choice." And THAT, people of Revere *god pop*, is just all there is to it!
(CUEUP: “Livin’ On a Prayer” By Bon Jovi) ::Ring Announcer is handed a card from a ring boy:: RING ANNOUNCER: And now the WFW North American Champ, a man who single handedly beat Manson at the Spirit of NGEN, from Philadelphia, PA… “THE VIEWER’S CHOICE” SCOTTY MICHAELS!
::Scotty Michaels walks down to the ringside alone, hands the title to the referee and enters the ring::
::the ref holds the title up for all to see and hands it to the attendant:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels and Copycat meet face to face in the ring. Both are doing a lot of trash talking back and forth. MICHAELS SWINGS WITH A PUNCH BUT COPYCAT BLOCKS IT! Copycat locks in a waistlock… OVER THE HEAD BELLY TO BELLY!
VIC WATERS: Copycat is showing his sheer amount of power right here, Michaels might be in for more than he bargained for.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels grabs his back… HE CHARGES COPYCAT…. COPYCAT CATCHES HIM AGAIN…. ANOTHER OVER HEAD BELLY TO BELLY!
VIC WATERS: Michaels need to regroup here and slow this match down. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is backing away… he uses the ropes to get up and Copycat has a big smile on his face. Copycat lifts his arms up to for a test of strength… Michaels is shaking him off. He isn’t going to fall for that.
VIC WATERS: Copycat has fifty pounds and three inches on Michaels. If Michaels wants to win this, he needs to use his head.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels walks up to Copycat… MICHAELS SLAPS COPYCAT IN THE FACE! VIC WATERS: Oh man, Michaels is waking up a volcano. BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT THROWS A RIGHT AND MICHAELS CATCHES HIM AND TAKES HIM DOWN WITH AN ARMDRAG! Michaels locks in a reverse chinlock and Michaels has finally taken Copycat down to the mat!
VIC WATERS: Michaels is slowing the match down, he can’t match impact move for impact move with Copycat but he can try to out wrestle him and even possibly out smart him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is reaching back in an attempt to grab the hair but unsuccessfully… Michaels breaks the reverse chinlock and starts pounding on the head of Copycat from behind! Copycat tries to get away…. MICHAELS GRABS COPYCAT WITH A WAISTLOCK AND TAKES HIM DOWN TO THE MAT WITH A WHEEL BARRELL SUPLEX!
VIC WATERS: Copycat really took that suplex on the back of his head. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is starting to get up… MICHAELS CHARGES WITH A LOU THESZ PRESS AND HE STARTS PUNCHING AWAY ON COPYCAT WITH A FLURRY OF PUNCHES! The ref is stepping in to make sure that he isn’t using a closed fist!
VIC WATERS: Michaels is trying to put a little fear into Copycat so he isn’t as aggressive as he was when he opened the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is no scaredy cat. He won’t be able to instill fear into him. COPYCAT PUSHES MICHAEL OFF OF HIM!
VIC WATERS: Copycat may have just saved himself there. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels charges Copycat and nails him with a series of knee lifts… MICHAELS BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES…. FLYING FOREARM TO COPYCAT AND COPYCAT FALLS DOWN TO THE GROUND!
VIC WATERS: Scotty Michaels nailed him flush in the face with that one. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is trying to use the ropes to get himself up…. MICHAELS COMES FROM BEHIND AND LOCKS IN A SLEEPER HOLD AND HE DRAGS HIM TO THE CENTER OF THE RING!
VIC WATERS: Brilliant strategy there… he is trying to sap the strength of Copycat and he knew if he kept on the sleeper near the ropes, he’d be able to get out of it so he just dragged him to the center of the ring.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is waving around his arms trying to break loose from the sleeper… COPYCAT DECIDES TO FALL BACK AND USE HIS WEIGHT ADVANTAGE TO PRESS MICHAELS LIKE A PANCAKE ON THE MAT! Both men are down!
VIC WATERS: Copycat is a smart man, even I cannot deny that. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is putting a double count on both! 1—2—3—4—5! Copycat is the first man to his feet! Copycat picks up Michaels off the mat… BODYSLAM! Copycat is calling to the crowd, HE IS GOING FOR THE CAT’S MEOW! Copycat bounces off the ropes…. HE DROPS THE CAT’S MEOW ELBOW ON THE HEAD OF MICHAELS! Copycat goes for the cover! 1------------------------------2-------------------KICKOUT! Michaels just got his shoulder up there!
VIC WATERS: That elbow had a lot of force behind it and Michaels couldn’t kickout. BRANDON JACOBS: This Copycat is a champ in the making, whether he wins the title tonight or some time down the road, even Manson never looked as good as Copycat has tonight against Michaels.
VIC WATERS: I’ve been really impressed. BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT PICKS MICHAELS UP FOR AN OVERHEAD PRESS….. he drops him down in the center of the ring! Copycat drags him over to the closer to the corner… I think he is setting up the frog splash here.
VIC WATERS: Michaels needs to get up… NOW! BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat walks over to the corner…. Michaels starts to get up… COPYCAT COMES OVER AND HE STARTS POUNDING ON MICHAELS SO HE STAYS STILL! Michaels grabs the hair of Copycat and ROLLS HIM UP! The ref goes down for the count! 1--------------------------2----------------COPYCAT IS IN THE ROPES!
VIC WATERS: Quick reactions there by Michaels…. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat gets up first… HE LOCKS IN AN OVERHEAD BELLY TO BELLY AND THROWS MICHAELS INTO THE CENTER OF THE RING! Michaels folds up like a cheap accordion! Copycat rolls him up! 1-------------------------------------2------------------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Michaels needs to change the momentum because if he doesn’t, it is only a matter of time before he loses the title.
BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT IS CALLING FOR THE KIT KAT KICK! Michaels is struggling to get up… IF HE NAILS THIS, THIS MATCH IS OVER! Michaels is getting up… COPYCAT FIRES… MICHAELS PUSHES THE REFEREE IN THE WAY AND THE REF TAKES IT FLUSH IN THE JAW!
VIC WATERS: YES! BRANDON JACOBS: We lose more perfectly good referees that way. WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES HEEL WITH A CHAIR! Copycat is kicking away on Michaels…. HEEL slides into the ring AND PLASTERS COPYCAT WITH A CHAIR SHOT! HEEL picks up Copycat…. BIG CHOKESLAM! HEEL picks him up again… HE NAILS HIM WITH HIS VERY OWN MOVE THE LITTER BOX! HEEL picks up Copycat over his shoulder… he is signaling for something! HEEL is headed to the back with Copycat!
VIC WATERS: I’m beginning to believe this HEEL is not such a good guy. BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, P. NUT HAS SLIPPED OUT FROM UNDER THE RING! HE IS DRESSED AS COPYCAT! P. Nut lays down on the mat, Michaels looks up and that smile on his face says a thousand words.
VIC WATERS: THIS IS GENIUS! BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is trying to wake up the referee, the ref is finally coming too. Michaels hooks the leg of P. Nut and urges the ref to count. The groggy ref goes down for the count!
VIC WATERS: YES! BRANDON JACOBS: 1-----------------------------2--------------------------- WAIT A SECOND, the REF HOLDS UP! He realizes it isn’t Copycat!
VIC WATERS: Who would have known Michaels would be given the only referee in the company which wasn’t blind?
BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, LET’S GO BACKSTAGE, THERE IS A FIGHT GOING ON! ICEKOLD ATTACKS HEEL AND HEEL DROPS COPYCAT! Copycat is finally awake and aware of what is happening… HE RUNS DOWN THROUGH THE CURTAIN TO THE RING!
VIC WATERS: Why isn’t the ref putting the count on Copycat? THIS SHOULD BE COUNT OUT! BRANDON JACOBS: This ref may be smarter than he looks. COPYCAT COMES DOWN INTO THE RING AND STARTS UNLOADING ON MICHAELS AND DROPS HIM ON THE MAT! P. Nut jumps onto the ring! The ref goes over and tells him to get down! Copycat waistlocks Michaels…. OVER THE HEAD BELLY TO BELLY! He covers! BUT THE REF IS STILL DISTRACTED BY P. NUT! Copycat gets up and he grabs P. Nut…. WAIT A SECOND, LUSTER HAS COME FROM UNDER THE RING…. HE THROWS A PAIR OF BRASS KNUCKLES TO SCOTTY MICHAELS! Michaels grabs the brass knuckles and puts it on his fist!
VIC WATERS: Michaels & Luster might be great masterminds the even C.O.C.K.S. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat floors P. NUT! Copycat calls the LITTER BOMB! The fans here are going nuts! MICHAEL TAKES A SWING AT COPYCAT…. HE MISSES! HE GUTWRENCHES MICHAELS UP IN THE AIR FOR THE LITTERBOMB…. MICHAELS TAKES ONE LAST DESPERATE SWIPE WITH THE BRASS KNUCLKLES…. HE NAILS HIM RIGHT IN THE HEAD AND COPYCAT FALLS DOWN TO THE GROUND WITH MICHAELS ON TOP OF HIM! Michaels hooks both legs! The ref goes down to count! 1----------------------------------2----------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match and STILL WFW NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION….. “THE VIEWER’S CHOICE” SCOTTY MICHAELS!
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat got robbed just like Manson got robbed by the devilish mind of Michaels & Luster.
VIC WATERS: After seeing what they did to Copycat tonight, I wouldn’t be shocked if Luster beat Shawn Hart for the title at New World Madness with the way these two are batting 1.000.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels, Luster, HEEL, and P. Nut are headed for the hills. What a night we've had here in Revere, Doc Silver has been suspended for 90 days, El Arco Iris win his first ever match, Gideon & Felix Red battle to a time limit draw, a street fight where three new WFW superstars shinned, and to cap it all off, Copycat came this damn close to beating Scotty Michaels. We should just retire because there is no way we are going to be able to top this show, but since we are WFW, we will find a way. Folks, we’d like to thank you for joining us here tonight, well back next in Little Italy in New York for New World Madness in a couple of weeks, SEE YOU THEN!
