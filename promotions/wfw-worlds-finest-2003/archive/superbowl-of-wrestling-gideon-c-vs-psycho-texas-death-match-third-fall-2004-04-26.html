Superbowl of Wrestling: Gideon (c) vs. Psycho - Texas Death Match - (Third Fall)

26 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 26 April 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: It is time for the final and deciding fall, after losing the submission match to Psycho, Gideon took the Four Corners of Hell match to even this at one fall each.
VIC WATERS: I would have originally gone with Psycho to take this last fall, but I’m not so sure after the Four Corners of Hell match. I think this is dead even.
BRANDON JACOBS: It is going to come down to this final Texas Death fall. The one who can’t answer the ten count after a pin fall will lose the match and their grasp on the title
*bell rings* VIC WATERS: There is the bell! BRANDON JACOBS: They have done a remarkable job cleaning up the ring and the ringside area considering the time they had to work with. The only thumb tacks I can see are the ones still in Gideon’s back.
VIC WATERS: Psycho was trying to wipe some of the blood off of himself before the match started and he almost completely soaked a smaller white towel.
BRANDON JACOBS: Both men are circling, Psycho still looks a bit out of it from the blood loss but he is hanging in there.
VIC WATERS: Psycho looked like he was almost out of it when he faced Doc but he kept on fighting to the very end.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon and Psycho lock up…. GIDEON KNEE LIFTS PSYCHO RIGHT IN THE FACE AND HE FALLS DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Now that was a shot! BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon jumps on top of Psycho and he drives the shin right into the throat of PSYCHO AND PSYCHO IS TRYING TO BREAK FREE!
VIC WATERS: Gideon is pretty down really hard on the wind pipe of Psycho. He is already losing a lot of blood as it is.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref pulling Gideon off to get him to break…. Gideon drags Psycho to the center of the ring…. GIDEON PUTS A LEG SCISSORS AROUND THE HEAD OF PSYCHO!
VIC WATERS: Submissions don’t count in a Texas Death Match because whenever a wrestler would lock in a submission hold, he could give up and easily break the hold by simply answering the ten count. But this move should wear Psycho down even more…
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho really looks like he is out of it…… Psycho’s struggling stops and Gideon breaks the hold…. GIDEON IS GOING FOR A COVER! 1----------------------------------------------------2-------------------------------------------3!
VIC WATERS: Gideon got him, now he needs to get the ten count! BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is telling Gideon to get up and go to a neutral corner as he makes the count on Psycho!
ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! SIX! Psycho is beginning to get up…. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is signaling the match must continue…. GIDEON CHARGES WITH A CLOTHESLINE…. PSYCHO DUCKS…. PSYCHO KICKS GIDEON IN THE MIDSECTION! DDT!
VIC WATERS: Gideon’s neck still isn’t in all that great of shape from the abuse he took in the first fall.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho and Gideon down trying to regain there their breath and Psycho is slowly getting back up. GIDEON AND PSYCHO ARE EXCHANGING BLOWS BACK AND FORTH!
VIC WATERS: I don’t know where these two find the energy to compete on the level that they do.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is getting the advantage…. PSYCHO BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND HE HITS GIDEON WITH A JUMPING SHOULDER BLOCK RIGHT IN THE FACE SENDING GIDEON’S NECK BACKWARDS AND GIDEON CRASHES DOWN ON THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Gideon just got clocked. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho adjust where Gideon is in the ring and Psycho goes outside to the ring apron…. PSYCHO SLINGSHOT HIMSELF IN FOR A 450 LEG DROP AND NAILS IT! He is going for the cover! 1---------------------------------------------------------------------------2-----------------------------------------------3!
VIC WATERS: YES! BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is now telling Psycho to go to a neutral corner and he is going to make the ten count on Gideon!
ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! SIX! SEVEN! Gideon is starting to get up and he manages to get back up to one knee. VIC WATERS: Both these guys are literally exhausted after the two previous falls and while you don’t want to say it, these guys may be giving up the pinfall in order to buy some time to rest and stop the momentum of the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: That is a very keep observation. PSYCHO CHARGES GIDEON AND HE WHIPS HIM THROUGH THE ROPES AND GIDEON CRASHES ONTO THE FLOOR BELOW!
VIC WATERS: Thankfully this time he didn’t land on his head. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is going out after him…. PSYCHO IS RIPPING UP THE MATS AROUND THE RINGSIDE AREA!
VIC WATERS: A damaged neck and a concrete floor never mix. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho picks up Gideon….. PSYCHO HITS THE PILEDRIVER! Poor Gideon, that could be it for his title reign right there.
VIC WATERS: He had a good long run, he should be proud of himself. BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, PSYCHO IS PICKING GIDEON UP FOR A SECOND PILEDRIVER….. HE HITS IT AGAIN!
VIC WATERS: Ouch. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho rolls Gideon into the ring and rolls in after… VIC WATERS: He should go for the pin fall here and not waste anymore time…. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho pulls Gideon towards the center of the ring…. PSYCHO IS HEADED FOR THE CORNER! I thought he was going to try for a pin fall but obviously he has other things in mind.
VIC WATERS: It can only be one thing. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is ascending to the top turnbuckle… HE IS PERCHED ON THE TOP… PSYCHO FLIES OFF THE TOP AND GIDEON SOMEHOW GETS HIS KNEES UP JUST IN THE NICK OF TIME!
VIC WATERS: I think I heard a crunch on impact, Psycho may have broken a couple of ribs. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is crawling over to the corner for a breather as he watches Psycho writhe in pain. Gideon has been through a lot over the last year and the reason he got his knees up is the same reason he fought Doc so hardly when the league originally began. He has the survival instincts of a champion and it was only that which saved himself from the Psycho Flight.
VIC WATERS: Look, a wheezing and bloody Psycho is crawling over into the corner towards Gideon…
BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON IS GETTING UP AND HE KICKS PSYCHO RIGHT IN THE CHOPS WITH HIS SHIN AND PSYCHO ROLLS OVER AND GRABS HIS RIBS!
CROWD: GIDEON! GIDEON! GIDEON! VIC WATERS: The crowd is sensing Gideon may be able to close this out! BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is stomping away on the injured ribs of Psycho and Psycho is increasingly unable to fight back. Gideon needs to finish Psycho off here and now!
VIC WATERS: Psycho won’t give up. He’ll never give up. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon picks up Psycho and moves him closer to the corner… STOMACH BREAKER AND GIDEON IS HOLDING IT IN! Psycho looks like he just coughed up some blood.
VIC WATERS: The ref may have to stop this. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon looks over to the corner…. He is climbing up the turnbuckle…. WAIT A SECOND, AN EXHAUSTED PSYCHO IS GETTING UP AND HE IS HEADING TO THE CORNER….
VIC WATERS: I don’t even know how Psycho has the strength to get up! BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is trying to escape Psycho by climbing up to the top… PSYCHO IS CLIMBING UP FROM BEHIND AND HE IS SETTING FOR A SUPERPLEX!
VIC WATERS: This is dangerous for both men in their current condition. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is trying to fireback with elbows…HE HITS PSYCHO IN THE FACE BUT PSYCHO REFUSES TO BUDGE…. PSYCHO SUPERPLEXES GIDEON FROM THE TOP! Psycho crawls over and puts his arm on Gideon!
VIC WATERS: Jesus. Gideon really landed hard on the back of his neck. I don’t know if he is going to be able to comeback from this.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref goes down to count the fall! 1------------------------------------------------------2----------------------------------3!
VIC WATERS: Psycho takes the fall! Both men are down, Psycho didn’t even have enough energy to crawl to a neutral corner.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is making the ten count now! ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! Gideon is still down and he isn’t moving at all! FIVE! SIX! SEVEN! GIDEON IS STARTING TO COME TO! EIGHT! GIDEON IS HEARS THE COUNT! NINE! Gideon is trying to get up…… GIDEON FALLS DOWN TO THE MAT! TEN! BRANDON JACOBS: IT IS OVER! RING ANNOUNCER: THE WINNER OF THE MATCH... AND NEW WFW NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION…. PSYCHO! (CUEUP: “"Hurt" by Johnny Cash) BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is raising Psycho’s hand AND PSYCHO RAISES THE WFW NORTH AMERICAN TITLE! Psycho collapses down on the ground in exhaustion and the paramedics are coming out to make sure that he is okay.
VIC WATERS: Psycho has been through a lot tonight. BRANDON JACOBS: A frustrated Gideon grabs the WFW North American Title and looks at it….. he walks over to a weary beaten and bruised Psycho and hands him the title.
VIC WATERS: Look who is coming down! BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky, Jared Wells, and Felix Red are coming down to help the injured Psycho to the back and Gideon salutes Psycho and exits the ring. That was a classy move of Gideon acknowledging the battle that Psycho gave him tonight even if he lost the title.
VIC WATERS: Gideon put up a hell of a fight and he had a hell of run. He has nothing to be ashamed of.
BRANDON JACOBS: LOVE is helping Psycho get out of the ring. They are two for two tonight already. Will Anarky be able to make it three for three?
VIC WATERS: Anarky is in for a rough night. As bad as Psycho looks right now, he might be even worse after Maelstrom gets through with him.
BRANDON JACOBS: You may be right, folks, when we comeback, unless we are tragically killed or the promotion closes down, Anarky vs. Maelstrom…. STEEL CAGE! Stay tuned!
