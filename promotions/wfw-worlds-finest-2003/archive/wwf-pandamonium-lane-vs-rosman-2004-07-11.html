WWF Pandamonium: Lane vs. Rosman

11 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 11 July 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome back everyone, sorry for the technical difficulties. VIC WATERS: That is the last time we run electrical cables through the lion’s den. JONATHAN MARX: Poor kitty… BRANDON JACOBS: Is anyone else here looking forward to Mike Lane vs. Zack Rosman? JONATHAN MARX: Ever since Zack Rosman has come in, management has been talking very highly over him saying that he is a future WFW World Heavyweight Champion in the making. They think he is going to be a high impact performer who will take the world by storm.
VIC WATERS: I’ve seen some footage of Rosman and I hate to say it, but he is damn impressive. Mike Lane has a hell of a challenge ahead of him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Mike Lane really took it to Dice and he really took it to Wells who has been rolling through everyone lately. I don’t want to write Lane off because he has had one of best debuts in WFW in quite sometime battling through the competition, but tonight is going to be a hell of a challenge for Lane.
VIC WATERS: The natives look like they are getting restless Brandon. BRANDON JACOBS: Okay, lets not keep anyone waiting any longer, LET’S SEND THIS ONE DOWN TO RINGSIDE!
(CUEUP: “Nasty Ho" by Nuerotic Outsiders) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, A WWL Legend and one of the toughest man in all of wrestling, from Braintree, Massachusetts, MIKE LANE!
::crowd with a mixture of boos and cheers and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “"Youth of the Nation" by P.O.D) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, the man making his debut here tonight, from Orangeburg, South Carolina, ZACK ROSMAN!
::Rosman comes down to the loud cheers from the crowd and enters the ring:: BRANDON JACOBS: LANE CHARGES BEFORE THE BELL AND NAILS ROSMAN WITH A REVERSE FOREARM SHOT TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD!
JONATHAN MARX: Rosman seemed too caught up in the hoopla and was caught off guard. *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Rosman is scrambling to get up off of the mat… LANE COMES FROM BEHIND AND HOOKS ON A WAISTLOCK… RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX INTO THE OTHER SIDE OF THE RING!
JONATHAN MARX: His neck crunched into that mat like an accordion! BRANDON JACOBS: LANE KIPS UP! VIC WATERS: Damn. BRANDON JACOBS: Lane walks over to Rosman…. LANE IS SIGNALING FOR THE 1999 AND THIS CROWD IS BOOING!
JONATHAN MARX: Fight Rosman fight! BRANDON JACOBS: Lane picks Rosman off the ground…. LANE HAS THE ARMS HOOKED… HE HITS THE 1999! Lane makes the cover!
VIC WATERS: It is over. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref goes down to make the count! 1---------------------------------------2------------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match in two minutes and three seconds…. MIKE LANE! JONATHAN MARX: That was a bloody slaughter… BRANDON JACOBS: Rosman got caught off guard right from the get go and Lane didn’t let him have a chance to even throw a punch in this match.
VIC WATERS: It is just how I predicted, Lane won easily. JONATHAN MARX: Rosman will be back. It could have been worse. VIC WATERS: How? JONATHAN MARX: He could have tripped on his way to the ring. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we will be back with the three way elimination falls count anywhere in the San Diego Zoo match between Rat Fink, Golem, and Pulsar…. STAY TUNED!
