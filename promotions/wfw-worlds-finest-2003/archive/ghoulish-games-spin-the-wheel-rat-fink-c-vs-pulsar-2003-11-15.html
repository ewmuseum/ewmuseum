WFW — Card

Ghoulish Games - Spin the Wheel - Rat Fink (c) vs. Pulsar

15 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 15 November 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome back everyone, it is time for our first title defense of the evening, Rat Fink will put up the BAD World Heavyweight Title against the up and coming superstar Pulsar who won the match for his team in the six man and then proceeded to beat Dormant for the shot at the PPV tonight.
VIC WATERS: This should be a great battle, I’m looking forward to Satan's Clause beating the hell out of Pulsar.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is the most kick ass new superstar to debut in the WFW in a long time. He has really impressed me and I think tonight is the night where Rat Fink’s title reign finally comes to an end.
VIC WATERS: It all depends on the stipulation who has the advantage. Certain matches favor one wrestler or the other.
BRANDON JACOBS: We’ve got a surprise for the viewing audience at home. We have a celebrity in the house who was born right here in Chicago who will be our guest spinner tonight.
VIC WATERS: Whoa, cool, who did we get… Cindy Crawford, Harrison Ford, or Dennis Frantz? BRANDON JACOBS: Just a notch lower… VIC WATERS: George Wendth, John Hughes…. John Belushi?! BRANDON JACOBS: Still a little too high… VIC WATERS: Joe Mantegna, Martin Mull… I don’t know… Who is it? BRANDON JACOBS: Spinning the wheel tonight will be none other than Chicago’s own native son, the star of the new Get Smart…. ANDY DICK!
VIC WATERS: Ugh. I guess the WFW can’t afford to pay a big name celebrity with how much they are over paying you.
BRANDON JACOBS: Andy Dick was great on Newsradio. VIC WATERS: And how many years ago was that now? I can’t stand Manson, but how can the same city that produced Manson produce Andy Dick?
BRANDON JACOBS: Every city can have an off day. Let’s send this one down to backstage and see what match comes up!
VIC WATERS: Hopefully Andy Dick can spin better than he acts. RING ANNOUNCER: There are eleven choices on the wheel, Texas Death Match, Coal Miner’s Glove, Russian Chain Match, I Quit, Barbed Wire, Prince of Darkness, Lumberjack Match, Cage, Dog Collar Match, First Blood Match, Texas Bullrope Match, and Viewer’s Choice…. Andy Dick, come on up and spin the wheel!
::Andy Dick gets up from his seat to a mixed of cheers and boos and spins the wheel:: RING ANNOUNCER: Around and around it goes, where it stops, NOBODY KNOWS! ::the wheel slows down and goes by Viewer’s Choice, Texas Death Match and finally stops at Coal Miner’s Glove::
RING ANNOUNCER: RAT FINK WILL DEFEND THE BAD WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE IN A COAL MINER’S GLOVE MATCH!
BRANDON JACOBS: Now that is old school. VIC WATERS: I’ve never understood why coal miner’s need to wear loaded gloves. BRANDON JACOBS: See, the problem is, over the last decade, when people did Coal Miner’s Glove matches, they didn’t use a coal miner’s glove. They used a regular work glove, which you would use when cutting a piece of wood. A real coal miner’s glove is really, really thick and is coated with special chemicals in order to give it a hard coating to prevent toxins for seeping into the glove and doing damage.
VIC WATERS: How do you know all that? BRANDON JACOBS: I dated the coal miner’s daughter once. VIC WATERS: Gah, that is enough of that. BRANDON JACOBS: But my point is, for this occasion, the WFW has bought itself a genuine coal miner’s glove just in case the wheel landed on that spot and this match will finally be done right.
VIC WATERS: Did we buy special hoods for the Prince of Darkness match? BRANDON JACOBS: Along with fifty feet of barbed wire… VIC WATERS: We can always use the barbed wire if we ever need to build a chicken coup, but what are we ever going to do with the black hoods?
BRANDON JACOBS: It is all tax deductible. And besides, you made you the WFW’s accountant all of a sudden?
VIC WATERS: I just wanted to make sure we didn’t sign any of those GLCW accountants. BRANDON JACOBS: ::gulps:: No, we didn’t sign any GLCW management types. Don’t go spreading nasty rumors.
VIC WATERS: I apologize. BRANDON JACOBS: How did we get onto the subject of account? WE HAVE A COAL MINER’S GLOVE MATCH TO GET TO!
VIC WATERS: Oh yeah BRANDON JACOBS: Let’s send this one to ringside and get the match underway! (CUEUP: “Whiskey in a Jar” by Metallica) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from Australia, standing at over 6’3, the man who won the six man tag for his team at Almost Live, PULSAR!
::fans cheers Pulsar as he comes down to the aisle and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “Megalomaniac” by KMFDM) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, the leader of the New Age Revolution and the current BAD World Heavyweight Champion, RAT FINK!
::Rat Fink walks down to the ring with the Satan’s Clause walking behind him, Satan Clause enters the ring and Rat Fink sits down in a chair at ringside::
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar charges Satan Clause and rams him head first into the turnbuckle! PULSAR IS LAYING INTO SATAN’S CLAUSE, SWITCHING BETWEEN FOREARMS TO DRIVE SATAN CLAUSE DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Look at Rat Fink he is getting out of his chair! BRANDON JACOBS: Remember, this match is under BAD rules so there is no DQ. Rat Fink can do whatever he wants and it looks like he is using Satan Clause as a distraction. RAT FINK IS CLIMBING UP THE TURNBUCKLE… Pulsar looks over his shoulder and sees Rat Fink making his way to the top and SPRINTS ACROSS THE RING! Pulsar climbs up and Rat Fink and Pulsar start to exchange punches back and forth!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar was lucky that he saw Rat Fink when he did because I wouldn’t want to think what Rat Fink would do to Pulsar with that glove if he ever got it.
BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR GAINS THE ADVANTAGE AND HE STARTS REALLY LAYING INTO RAT FINK…. RAT FINK HANGS ON BY ONE HAND NOW AND HE NEARLY FELL DOWN TO THE MAT! Satan Clause is slowly crawling over to the corner…. PULSAR NAILS RAT FINK WITH ANOTHER PUNCH FOR GOOD MEASURE AND HE FALLS DOWN TO THE GROUND BELOW!
VIC WATERS: That was a nasty spill Rat Fink took to the floor. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is only a few steps away from being able to reach up and grab the coal miner’s glove… SATAN CLAUSE GRABS THE LEG OF PULSAR! Pulsar is trying to shake him off!
VIC WATERS: Satan Clause knows that if Pulsar grabs the Coal Miner’s Glove and nails him or Rat Fink, this match is over!
BRANDON JACOBS: SATAN CLAUSE PULLS PULSAR OFF THE SECOND ROPE AND PULSAR FALLS FACE FIRST ONTO THE TOP TURNBUCKLE!
VIC WATERS: That had to hurt. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref puts the count on both men…. 1------2-----3---- 4---- SATAN CLAUSE STARTS TO GET UP! Satan Clause pulls Pulsar towards the center of the ring and HE LOCKS ON A CAMEL CLUTCH!
VIC WATERS: Satan Clause is taking advantage of the whiplash that Pulsar suffered when he fell and hit his head on the top turnbuckle.
BRANDON JACOBS: Just look at Satan Clause, he is using all the strength in his body in order to make Pulsar give up but somehow Pulsar is holding on.
VIC WATERS: Even if Pulsar gets out of this hold, his neck will be a factor for the rest of the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Plus, this match cannot end until someone gets the Coal Miner’s Glove so even if Pulsar wanted to submit, he can’t. SATAN CLAUSE BREAKS THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: This is his chance to climb the pole and grab the Coal Miner’s Glove. BRANDON JACOBS: Satan Clause walks over towards the corner and begins to climb to the top… Pulsar is trying to get up…. SATAN CLAUSES HAS CLIMBED TO THE TOP TURNBUCKLE… PULSAR IS UP…. PULSAR FALLS ON THE ROPES AND SATAN CLAUSE LOSES HIS FOOTING AND CROTCHES HIMSELF ON THE TOP TURNBUCKLE!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar caught a big break there. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar slowly walks over to the corner and begins to climb the ropes…. Pulsar locks him arm behind Satan Clause…. BACKDROP SUPLEX DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: That was a sweet move. BRANDON JACOBS: Satan Clause really landed hard on the mat and Pulsar is up… WAITING FOR SATAN CLAUSE TO GET UP ON HIS FEET! He is all set to nail Satan Clause… Satan Clause is starting to get up.
VIC WATERS: Listen to Rat Fink, he is screaming to Satan Clause to stay down but he is too dazed to comprehend what he is saying.
BRANDON JACOBS: SATAN CLAUSES IS UP…. HE TURNS AROUND…. SATAN CLAUSE NAILS HIM WITH THE RUNNING SHOULDER BLOCK AND HE CRASHES DOWN HARD ON THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: WHAT AN IMPACT! BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar looks up at the Coal Miner’s Glove… RAT FINK JUMPS ON THE APRON AND HE TAKES A SWING AT PULSAR… PULSAR MOVES OUT OF THE WAY AND FLIPS RAT FINK INTO THE RING BY THE HAIR! Listen to this crowd, they can’t wait to see Pulsar get the Coal Miner’s Glove and give Rat Fink what he so truly deserves.
VIC WATERS: Why does Rat Fink always have to be Satan? Just because he is a third world dictator?
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is climbing the turnbuckles…. HE IS UP TO THE TOP… HE REACHES UP…. HE HAS THE COAL MINER’S GLOVE! Now all he has to do is use it on either Rat Fink or Satan Clause and this match is his!
VIC WATERS: That glove is a lethal weapon. It is a crime that it is in the hands of someone who loves violence as much as Pulsar does.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink is trying desperately to wake up Satan Clause but Pulsar has climbed down and he is now stalking Rat Fink…. PULSAR TAPS RAT FINK ON THE SHOULDER…. RAT FINK TURNS AROUND AND HE SEES PULSAR SMILING FROM EAR TO EAR!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar looks like a kid at Christmas. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink drops to his knees and he is begging Pulsar not to him! I THOUGHT RAT FINK HAD MORE DIGNITY THAN THIS!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink is mastermind. Nothing is as it appears. BRANDON JACOBS: Sure Vic, sure. PULSAR PICKS RAT FINK UP BY THE HAIR AND RAT FINK IS SQUEALING LIKE THE VERMIN THAT HE IS!
VIC WATERS: Oh come on now. BRANDON JACOBS: Satan Clause is starting to get up…. PULSAR WINDS UP AND RAT FINK DUCKS AND PULSAR NAILS SATAN CLAUSE WITH THE COAL MINER’S GLOVE!
VIC WATERS: SATAN DOWN! SATAN DOWN! SATAN DOWN! BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink looks down at Satan Clause and looks up at Pulsar and begins to back away. PULSAR DOESN’T LOOK LIKE HE WANTS TO GET THE VICTORY BY PINNING SATAN CLAUSE… HE WANTS TO DO IT BY PINNING THE DEVIL HIMSELF!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink doesn’t deserve this, just because he made Manson’s life a living hell and we are in Chicago doesn’t mean that he deserves this.
BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES THE AMERICAN AND HE HAS A BAG IN HIS HAND! The American jumps on the ring apron and he is yelling at Pulsar… Pulsar hasn’t turned his head away from Rat Fink…. THE AMERICAN SPITS ON PULSAR AND PULSAR TURNS AROUND! Pulsar is yelling at the American…. The ref is trying to regain some order here by getting him to go to the back… THE AMERICAN THROWS THE BAG HE HAD BEHIND HIS BACK OVER THE REF AND PULSAR INTO THE HANDS OF RAT FINK!
VIC WATERS: What could possibly be in the bag that could help Rat Fink against a loaded glove?
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink opens up the bag… IT IS A GREEN BAY PACKERS FOOTBALL HELMET AND RAT FINK IS PUTTING IT ON!
VIC WATERS: This is brilliant! Rat Fink can’t get him glove full force if he is wearing the football helmet!
BRANDON JACOBS: The American slips off the ring apron… Rat Fink crouches down so Pulsar doesn’t see the helmet! Pulsar walks up to Rat Fink and pulls him up by the arm… RAT FINK TURNS AROUND AND SHOWS PULSAR HIS FOOTBALL HELMET…. PULSAR NAILS RAT FINK WITH THE LOADED MINER’S GLOVE…. NO EFFECT!
VIC WATERS: Look at how angry Pulsar is right now, he thought he had his chance to do serious damage to Rat Fink and Rat Fink’s goons have once again changed the momentum of another title match.
BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK GRABS PULSAR BY THE HAIR…. HE HEADBUTTS PULSAR IN THE HEAD WITH HIS HELMET! GOOD LORD! RAT FINK NAILS HIM AGAIN, AGAIN, AND AGAIN! Pulsar has been busted open and he has fallen down to the ground!
VIC WATERS: You never, ever wanted to hit your head on a football helmet with any force behind it or it could knock you clean out.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink grabs the arm of Pulsar… HE PULLS OFF THE LOADED GLOVE AND HE PUTS IT ON HIS OWN HAND! Pulsar is bleeding profusely but he refuses to stay down.
VIC WATERS: Pulsar needs to get out of the ring immediately if he has any chance of winning this.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is getting up….. Rat Fink winds up…. PULSAR LUNGES AT RAT FINK AND PULLS DOWN HIS FACE MASK SO RAT FINK CAN’T SEE!
VIC WATERS: He may have bought himself the time he needed in order to regain the advantage.
BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR DUCKS UNDER THE BOTTOM ROPE AND RIPS A SEAT AWAY FROM SOMEONE AT RINGSIDE! Oh my, Pulsar is going to take advantage of Rat Fink not being able to see with some good old fashioned wrestling justice!
VIC WATERS: The damn guy can’t even see! BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar rolls under the bottom rope with the chair…. Rat Fink has the helmet adjusted…. PULSAR NAILS HIM ACROSS THE BACK WITH A CHAIR AND RAT FINK FALLS DOWN TO HIS KNEES! Satan Clause is starting to get back up…. PULSAR WINDS UP AGAIN….. SATAN CLAUSE GRABS FROM BEHIND AND HOLDS IT IN THE FACE OF PULSAR IN ORDER TO KEEP HIM FROM MOVING!
VIC WATERS: Satan Clause may have just saved Rat Fink from a beating. BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR TRYING TO BREAK FREE BUT SATAN CLAUSE HAS THAT CHAIR PRESSED IN HIS FACE LIKE A DEMENTED BEAR HUG AND THAT STEEL IS GRINDING INTO BLOOD OF THE ALREADY OPEN WOUND!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink is getting up! BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink is getting up and Pulsar has the chair right in his face….. RAT FINK SWINGS WITH THE COAL MINER’S GLOVE AND PUNCHES THE CHAIR RIGHT INTO PULSAR’S FACE!
VIC WATER: Oh my…. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink goes down to make the cover! The ref goes down for the count! 1-----------------------2--------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. AND STILL BAD WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION….. RAT FINK!
BRANDON JACOBS: Man, that was a violent match, these two really went to the limit and beyond tonight.
VIC WATERS: Even though Pulsar lost tonight, he really impressed me. Not many men would have been able to fight back after those headbutts with the football helmet, but somehow he found the will to continue and I have to tip my hat to him.
BRANDON JACOBS: This is going to be a mess to clean, folks, we got to take a commercial break, when we comeback, Minion will make his WFW and PPV debut against Adam Benjamin…. STAY TUNED EVERYONE!
