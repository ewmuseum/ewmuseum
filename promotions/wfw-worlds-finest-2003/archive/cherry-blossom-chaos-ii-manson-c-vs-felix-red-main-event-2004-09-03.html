WFW — Card

Cherry Blossom Chaos II: Manson (c) vs. Felix Red (Main Event)

3 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 3 September 2004 JONATHAN MARX: During the break, they were showing some of those promos between Manson and Felix Red and let me just say, they were some of the best I have ever heard by both men and that is saying a lot.
VIC WATERS: You understood what Felix Red was saying? JONATHAN MARX: Every word, but I read way too much. I am going to be wearing bifocals by the time I am forty.
BRANDON JACOBS: I am really looking forward to this match and I think the momentum is on Felix Red’s side. Wells figured out a way to beat the gigantic Iron Lion, Psycho pulled out Falls on the Mall Part II against three hungry challengers who did everything in their power to try to take his title away, and Anarky came awfully close to getting his revenge against Michaels before Hart & Gideon interfered. If Felix Red could somehow pull this off, LOVE would have a monopoly on WFW once and for all.
JONATHAN MARX: This is a huge match for LOVE, the WFW World Heavyweight Title is the last card that has to fall into place. The question is, can Felix Red beat this Jesus like figure in Manson to get the job done? How do you go about beating an immortal?
VIC WATERS: Remember, Felix Red is a former BAD World Heavyweight Champion and he was the one who brought down Rat Fink’s legendary reign where he was running rough shot through everyone.
BRANDON JACOBS: I remember that, Rat Fink seemed unstoppable. VIC WATERS: Just like Manson seems now. There is no doubt that Manson is one of the all time greats, but with Felix Red’s lightning quick speed, he aerial ability, and mixed martial art skill, he is one of the most dangerous men in all of WFW. All it takes is for him to catch Manson off guard or stun him for a three count and we could very well have a new world heavyweight champion.
JONATHAN MARX: That is true. VIC WATERS: I have a feeling that Felix Red is going to pull this one out, it will be a hell of a battle, but he’ll figure out a way to win.
BRANDON JACOBS: Well, you were right on Wells earlier…. JONATHAN MARX: Manson will figure out a way like he did against Gideon even though it took him a full fifty nine minutes to do so.
VIC WATERS: We will see. BRANDON JACOBS: There is only one way to know for sure, folks, lets send this one down to ringside and begin the WFW World Heavyweight Title match!
(CUEUP: “"If Born From This Soil: Treatments For the Injected Foetu" by Minor Procedure and Shai Hulud)
RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the challenger, making his way to the ring, weighing in at 213 pounds and standing at 6 feet, three inches, “The Boy Who Destroyed The World” and former BAD World Heavyweight Champion, FELIX RED!
::Felix Red comes down to the ring to the loud boos from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: "Mechanical Animals" by Marilyn Manson) RING ANNOUNCER: And now the champion, from Chicago, IL, the WFW World Heavyweight Champion, the man, the myth, the legend…. MICHAEL MANSON!
::Manson is walking down slowly to the ring to the roar of the crowd, he enters the ring and hands his title to the ring attendant::
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: This DC crowd is at a fevered pitch, as these two men circle, waiting for these two to lock up.
JONATHAN MARX: Both men are looking for the opening, because a good start can establish the momentum of the match early.
BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON GOES TO LOCK UP BUT FELIX RED FIRES BACK WITH A SPINNING HEEL KICK WHICH MANSON JUST DUCKS OUT OF THE WAY OF!
JONATHAN MARX: Manson better be careful he doesn’t get caught with one of those kicks right in the head or he won’t be walking out of here champ.
VIC WATERS: I barely even saw the kick his feet were so fast. JONATHAN MARX: It was a quick kick, but I think the Poland Spring Water has affected your vision.
BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED GOES FOR ANOTHER KICK BUT MANSON DUCKS OUT OF THE WAY AND CATCHES HIM WITH A WAISTLOCK FROM BEHIND!
JONATHAN MARX: Good reaction there by Manson, he saw his opportunity open up and he took it and he only had a split second to make that decision.
BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED SWINGS HIS ARMS WILDLY AND MANAGES TO CATCH MANSON WITH AN ELBOW IN THE FACE!
VIC WATERS: That shot could take out a few teeth. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is caught off guard with the shot…. FELIX RED JUMPS UP AND NAILS A SPINNING HEEL KICK RIGHT TO THE HEAD AND MANSON GOES DOWN TO ONE KNEE AND IS GRABBING HIS JAW!
JONATHAN MARX: Manson needs to avoid taking all of these shots to the head or as well conditioned as he is, he isn’t going to be around later in the match to take these shots.
BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED TAKES MANSON DOWN TO THE MAT WITH ARM DRAG! Felix Red wrenches in a reverse chinlock on Manson and is trying to work over that injured jaw of Manson….
VIC WATERS: Felix Red is trying to slow the match down a little bit while he works over Manson’s injured area so he has more energy for later.
JONATHAN MARX: Felix Red is a loaded gun ready to go off at any time, he is merely saving his bullets.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is reaching back for the hair but he is unable to grab a handful of it and his grasp and Felix Red tightens up reverse chinlock even more in an attempt to drain the energy out of Manson.
VIC WATERS: That kick must have been powerful because Manson really looks effected by the chinlock.
BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON REACHES BACK FOR THE HAIR BUT FELIX RED MOVES HIS HEAD OUT OF THE WAY IN THE NICK OF TIME! Felix Red releases the hold…. AND HE LOCKS A LEG VICE AROUND THE HEAD OF MANSON INSTEAD!
JONATHAN MARX: Good god, when was the last time someone busted out a leg vice, I think Regan was still in office.
VIC WATERS: And being written for by Stan Lee… JONATHAN MARX: Stan Lee wrote for everyone back then. It was the eighties. BRANDON JACOBS: GOOD GOD! Felix Red is going to break Manson’s head off the way he has that locked on. The ref is checking to make sure it isn’t a choke which it doesn’t look like it is but the ref has to keep a close eye on this.
JONATHAN MARX: Choking someone with your legs can much more dangerous than choking someone with your arms because most people generally have more power with their legs and in the case of Felix Red, that is definitely the case.
BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON IS STRUGGLING AROUND TRYING TO GET FREE AND FELIX RED IS TRYING TO HOLD ON… FELIX RED SHIFTS HIS LEGS AND LOCKS ON THE CHOKE AROUND HIS NECK IN THE LEG VICE! Manson is gasping for air!
JONATHAN MARX: The ref needs to make him break this! BRANDON JACOBS: ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! Felix Red breaks the hold before the five. To be fair to Felix Red, Manson most likely caused that situation when he tried to break out of the hold.
VIC WATERS: Manson was trying to break out of the hold at any and all cost before anymore damage was done and he did that.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is backing Felix Red away so Manson has a chance to get up and catch his breath to make sure he is okay to continue.
VIC WATERS: WFW is just protecting its golden boy, they should let Felix Red get in there and give him the what’s for.
JONATHAN MARX: Felix Red did have the choke on regardless of how he got into so Manson should be given a second before this continues.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is backing off at request of the ref and Manson is finally getting back up… Felix Red is allowed back at Manson and he goes to pick him up by the hair…. MANSON HITS FELIX RED WITH A NUT SHOT RIGHT IN FRONT OF THE REFEREE!
VIC WATERS: That bastard Manson, he is trying get himself disqualified. JONATHAN MARX: All is fair in war with LOVE. BRANDON JACOBS: THE REF IS WARNING MANSON THAT IF HE DOES THAT AGAIN, HE IS GOING TO BE DISQUALIFIED! Felix Red is doubled over in pain!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red looks in sad shape. JONATHAN MARX: The boy who destroyed the world meet the boy who destroyed your nuts. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON PUSHES FELIX RED OUT OF THE WAY AND HE KICKS HIM BELOW THE BELT! JONATHAN MARX: Manson knows that the referee won’t disqualify him because Manson would retain. The referee is showing good judgment here and Manson is using his brain.
VIC WATERS: They should disqualify Manson and hand Felix Red over the title. JONATHAN MARX: The rules don’t work that way. Manson has found his own version of Felix Red’s dangerous mixed martial arts kicks. It is brilliant.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson quickly takes advantage of the downed Felix Red and waistlocks him from behind…. MANSON HITS THE GERMAN SUPLEX!
JONATHAN MARX: Manson isn’t done yet! BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON HOLDS ON TO THE WAISTLOCK AND PICKS FELIX RED UP OFF THE MAN AGAIN…. HE HITS WITH A SECOND GERMAN SUPLEX DRIVING FELIX RED BACK FIRST HARD INTO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Looks like Manson is going for one more… BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON PICKS FELIX RED UP OFF THE MAT…. MANSON HITS THE TRIFECTA WITH A THIRD GERMAN SUPLEX AND HE FINALLY LETS GO OF THE WAISTLOCK!
JONATHAN MARX: After the two nut shots and three German suplexes, this may be it! BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON GOES DOWN FOR THE COVER ON FELIX RED! 1-----------------------------------------------------------2------------------------------------- FELIX RED MANAGES TO KICK OUT!
VIC WATERS: That was damn close. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON PICKS UP FELIX RED IN A BEAR HUG AND HE RAMS HIM BACK FIRST INTO THE TURNBUCKLE! Manson lets go of Felix Red and Felix Red slumps down in the corner.
JONATHAN MARX: Manson seems like he is softening him up for the Wild Buck Bomb and even further down the road, the tourniquet.
VIC WATERS: Those German suplexes certainly didn’t do Felix Red’s neck any favors. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON BEGINS REPEATEDLY KICKING FELIX RED IN THE CORNER DRIVING THOSE BOOTS RIGHT INTO HIS CHEST!
JONATHAN MARX: Manson is getting his pound of flesh when he can. There is nothing more important to him than holding onto the WFW World Heavyweight Title and keeping it out of LOVE’s grasp. With the pack mentality that LOVE has, it would be nearly impossible for them to lose the World Heavyweight Heavyweight title once they won it.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is trying to get in there to keep Manson from going over the line… Manson is yelling at the referee to move out of the way… FELIX RED MANAGES TO SLIP OUT OF THE RING!
VIC WATERS: That is a good move there, Felix Red had to get out of there or Manson would have tenderized him with those boots some more. Now he’ll have a chest to rest and recoup on the outside.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is yelling at the ref for letting Felix Red escape… MANSON IS GOING TO OUTSIDE AFTER FELIX RED! Felix Red is trying to get away…
VIC WATERS: Run Felix run… BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON GRABS FELIX RED BY THE LEGS AND HE KEEPS HIM FROM GETTING AWAY! Manson is pulling up the mats at ringside… IT LOOKS LIKE HE IS GOING TO TRY TO FINISH UP FELIX RED WITH A WILD BUCK BOMB RIGHT ON THE CONCRETE!
JONATHAN MARX: If he hits this, it would be a devastating blow to Felix Red. I don’t know if he could even he could recover from that.
VIC WATERS: If the ref had some balls, he wouldn’t put up with these shenanigans. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON PICKS UP FELIX RED FOR A WILD BUCK BOMB….. FELIX RED SHIFTS HIS POSITIONING AND DELIVERS TORNADO DDT ON MANSON RIGHT INTO THE CONCRETE!
CROWD: BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! JONATHAN MARX: What a counter by Felix Red… VIC WATERS: Felix Red now needs to keep this momentum and pay Manson back for all he has done by winning the WFW World Heavyweight Title.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is down on the ground and he looks dazed… FELIX RED HOPS THE GUARDRAIL AND HE IS CLIMBING UP ONTO THE STEEL GUARDRAIL… Manson is getting up… FELIX RED JUMPS OFF AND DELIVERS A BICYCLE KICK RIGHT TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD OF MANSON AND MANSON GOES FACE FIRST INTO THE CONCRETE!
CROWD: LETS GO MANSON! LETS GO MANSON! LETS GO MANSON! VIC WATERS: These fair weather fans turn on you at the drop of a hat, just a minute ago they were marking out for his tornado DDT.
JONATHAN MARX: These fans love Manson even though they know he isn’t a saint. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson has been busted open and boy, is he bleeding. JONATHAN MARX: He got driven into that concrete around the ring twice which the ring is set on to avoid it sinking into the ground with all this overcast Washington, DC weather threatening rain on us throughout the show.
VIC WATERS: This is the last match, we may be able to make it out of here before the storm hits.
BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED IS REPEATEDLY RAMMING HIS KNEE INTO THE BUSTED OPEN HEAD OF MANSON AND HE IS TRYING TO GET THE BLOOD FLOWING EVEN MORE!
JONATHAN MARX: Felix Red better be careful this doesn’t work against him because if Manson is bleeding too badly, he could walk out of here WFW World Heavyweight Champ which is exactly his mission.
VIC WATERS: That would be just like Manson to try everything in the book. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red picks Manson off of the ground…. FELIX RED ROLLS MANSON BACK INTO THE RING AND HE ROLLS AFTER HIM!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red has to try to get this cover before Manson finds a way to comeback or weasel out of the match.
JONATHAN MARX: Manson doesn’t weasel out, he is taking full advantage of the rules. This is war and in war there are no rules.
BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED HOOKS THE LEG AND HE IS GOING FOR A COVER! The ref goes down for the count! 1-----------------------------------------------------2----------------------------------- MANSON KICKS OUT!
JONATHAN MARX: Manson was less than half a second away from losing the title. But after working a year to win the title, he isn’t going to let the WFW World Heavyweight Title slip away when there is still breath in his body.
VIC WATERS: Felix Red will fix that. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red picks up Manson by the hair…. FELIX RED HITS MANSON WITH A SPINNING HEEL KICK AND MANSON FALLS DOWN TO HIS KNEES!
JONATHAN MARX: Felix Red is brutalizing Manson with these kicks. VIC WATERS: A few more shots like this and the title will be his. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red bounces off the ropes…. SHINING WIZARD ON MANSON RIGHT IN THE FACE AND MANSON GOES DOWN TO THE GROUND! Felix Red goes for the cover!
JONATHAN MARX: No! BRANDON JACOBS: THE REF GOES DOWN FOR THE COUNT! 1-------------------------------------------2-------------------- MANSON MANAGES TO KICK OUT AGAIN!
VIC WATERS: Oh that shining wizard shot, Felix Red caught him right where he busted Manson open,
JONATHAN MARX: That knee cap went right into his head. BRANDON JACOBS: A frustrated Felix Red drags Manson over towards the ropes and lays him over the second rope face first…. FELIX RED IS KICKING MANSON REPEATEDLY WITH HIS SHINS RIGHT TO THE BACK!
JONATHAN MARX: Felix Red is try to soften up that back so Manson will stay down for the pin.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is bellowing out in pain as Felix Red is wailing away at him with these hard shots. Felix Red is vicious!
JONATHAN MARX: This means so much to Felix Red’s career, if he wins this, he will go down with the immortals who have worn the WFW World Heavyweight Title and take his career to the next level. If he loses, who knows when he’ll get his next opportunity like this to win the title.
BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED GRABS THE LEGS OF MANSON AND HE IS TRYING TO PULL HIM INTO THE CENTER OF THE RING BUT MANSON IS GRASPING ONTO THOSE ROPES WITH ALL HE HAS!
JONATHAN MARX: Manson has to hold because if Felix Red drags him to the center of that ring, this time he may not kick out.
BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED LETS GO OF MANSON AND HE STARTS KICKING AWAY AT MANSON AND MAKE HIM LET GO OF HIS GROUP… MANSON FINALLY LETS GO BUT HE SLIPS OUT OF THE RING BEFORE FELIX RED CAN GRAB HIS LEGS!
VIC WATERS: Manson lucked out there. JONATHAN MARX: Nah, survival is a skill. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is slowly crawling toward the guardrail and he is trying to get up….. FELIX RED BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND HE DIVES THROUGH AND HITS MANSON WITH A TOPE AND HE TOPPLES OVER THE GUARDRAIL INTO THE AUDIENCE!
JONATHAN MARX: Felix Red with an amazing dive which caught Manson right in the sternum! VIC WATERS: Thank god he hit that or all the momentum he worked to build up would have been gone.
BRANDON JACOBS: Both men are down… but Felix Red is the first man to get up, Manson is trying to get back up… FELIX RED CLIMBS OVER THE GUARDRAIL AND HE IS COMING FOR MANSON!
VIC WATERS: Get him! Don’t let Manson get away. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson looks like he just grabbed something from a fan…. Felix Red pulls Manson up by the hair…. MANSON BREAKS A BOTTLE OVER FELIX RED’S HEAD AND BUSTS HIM OPEN!
JONATHAN MARX: HOLY COW! That broke into a million pieces! VIC WATERS: Felix is down and it appears that he is out… BRANDON JACOBS: Manson picks up Felix Red…. HE LEANS FELIX RED AGAINST THE GUARDRAIL AND HE STARTS ASSAULTING FELIX RED WITH CLOSED FISTS RIGHT TO THE HEAD!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red is a bloody mess. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON GRABS AN UMBRELLA FROM A FAN AND USES IT TO CHOKE FELIX RED! JONATHAN MARX: Felix Red is trying desperately to get his hands under the umbrella in order to minimize the damage but things aren’t looking good for him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson throws down the umbrella…. HE SNAP SUPLEXES FELIX RED BACK INTO THE RINGSIDE AREA!
::the rain starts to fall:: VIC WATERS: Uh oh, it looks like is starting to rain. JONATHAN MARX: Thankfully most of the equipment is covered already, it has looked like it was about to storm for awhile now.
::the rain stars to fall much harder:: VIC WATERS: It is starting to down pour actually, Geez… of all the times to do this. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is coming over the guardrail and he throws Felix Red back into the ring. MANSON IS COMING BACK IN AFTER HIM!
JONATHAN MARX: The one good thing about this rain is that with as much as Felix Red and Manson have been bleeding, it is going to wash all the blood off their face so it shouldn’t be a detriment to either man now.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref goes down and he checking on the bloody of Felix Red after the bottle shot and he is letting this match continue.
VIC WATERS: Come on Felix, you’ve worked too hard to let Manson beat you now! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is calling for the Wild Buck Bomb… MANSON LIFTS FELIX RED UP AND HITS THE WILD BUCK BOMB! Manson has the cover! 1---------------------------------------------------2--------------------------- FELIX RED MANAGES TO GET THE SHOULDER UP!
VIC WATERS: Too close… JONATHAN MARX: Felix Red is not going to be able to hold on much longer here unless he does something…
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is calling for the Sweet Dreams…. HE IS WAITING FOR FELIX RED TO GET UP!
JONATHAN MARX: Manson is just waiting on him to get up to strike… ::starts to really downpour:: VIC WATERS: Come on Felix! BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED IS STARTING TO GET UP….. MANSON GOES FOR THE SWEET DREAMS BUT FELIX RED PUSHES HIM CHEST FIRST INTO THE TURNBUCKLE TO AVOID THE STUNNER!
VIC WATERS: Thank god! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson staggers backwards… FELIX RED SPRINGBOARDS OFF THE ROPES WITH A MOONSAULT AND DELIVERS A DDT! Felix Red goes down for the cover! 1--------------------------------------------------2------------------------------------------ MANSON KICKS OUT!
JONATHAN MARX: Damn it, that was close! These ropes are starting to get slippery so Felix Red better be careful.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is trying to get back up to his feet…. FELIX RED PICKS HIM UP AND SLAMS HIM DOWN TO THE MAT! Felix Red is going up to the top rope!
JONATHAN MARX: This could be the Shattered Horizon right here! VIC WATERS: FINISH HIM! BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED FLIES OFF THE TOP WITH SHATTERED HORIZON TWISTING SWANTON BOMB AND HITS MANSON DEAD ON!
JONATHAN MARX: Wait a second, the referee just slipped on the wet mat trying to get out of the way and bumped his head.
VIC WATERS: NO DAMN IT! GET THE HELL UP AND COUNT MANSON! BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is begging the ref to get the hell up to get up and count the three….
VIC WATERS: These damn referees, if there were two of them for world title matches, none of this would ever happen.
BRANDON JACOBS: THE REF IS STARTING TO MOVE AND HE CRAWLS OVER TO MAKE THE COUNT ON MANSON!
VIC WATERS: Let this be it! BRANDON JACOBS: 1------------------------------------------------2------------------------------------------- MANSON MANAGES TO KICK OUT!
JONATHAN MARX: Referee must have taken a hell of a spill because that really was a slow count.
VIC WATERS: Come on Felix, bring this one home! BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED IS CALLING FOR THE ECSTASY TO AGONY! Felix Red picks Manson up off the mat… FELIX RED LOSES HIS GRIP ON THE WET MANSON!

BOTH MEN BEGIN CHOPPING EACH OTHER BACK AND FORTH AND THEY THROWING SOME OF THE LOUDEST CHOPS AS THEY RIP INTO EACH OTHER THAT I HAVE EVER HEARD!
JONATHAN MARX: These two are leaving nothing in their tank, at this point is all or nothing and nothing isn’t good enough.
