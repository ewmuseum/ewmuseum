WFW Shock & Awe: Starr/Bradford vs. Eian/Luster 2nd Fall

18 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 18 April 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Oh yeah baby, we are back after some technical difficulties but now we are ready to go! I still can’t believe what we have seen so far.
VIC WATERS: This ref has choked worse than Mike Piazza trying to throw a runner out. ::bell rings:: BRANDON JACOBS: Andre Eian has taken the time between falls and he has managed to get himself into the corner. Both Starr & Bradford are still cursing at the ref for what happened but the ref is turning a deaf ear.
VIC WATERS: It looks like Eian will start the second match for his team after all, what a trooper working with the injured knee that he has. He is going to be shark bait for Bradford when he gets a hold of them.
BRANDON JACOBS: Eian and Bradford lock up… Eian gets a waistlock on Bradford! Bradford goes to deliver an elbow to Eian’s face… EIAN MOVES OUT OF THE WAY! Eian synchs his hands…. HIGH RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX! Bradford crashes down on the mat!
VIC WATERS: Nice exchange by those two there, Eian did a good job of getting out of the way of the back of that elbow.
BRANDON JACOBS: Eian picks Bradford up and puts him on his knee… Eian pushes down on Bradford down onto his uninjured knee right into the back of Bradford! Eian is looking Bradford in the eye and is talking trash! The ref is counting to make sure he breaks the hold before the five! 1-----2-----3----4. BREAK! Eian breaks just in time! Eian puts it back on! 1------2-----3----4 BREAK!
VIC WATERS: Eian is playing it awfully close with this referee, the ref has shown that he is willing to tow the line tonight so Eian has to be very careful not to over step any boundaries.
BRANDON JACOBS: Eian lets go of the hold and shouts at Bradford to get up…. Bradford turns his body and tries to get away… Eian comes up from behind…another waistlock…. HIGH RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX! Eian goes for a cover! The ref goes down for the count… 1----------2---------KICKOUT! Bradford just got that shoulder up. Eian hooks the leg! 1-----------2-------KICKOUT! He barely escapes yet again!
VIC WATERS: Bradford needs to get to his corner and make a tag soon if his team has any chance of winning this match. Starr & Bradford are already down a fall.
BRANDON JACOBS: Eian stomps away on Bradford to keep him down on the mat! He is ramming those big boots of his right into his face! Eian drops an elbow right into the throat of Bradford! Bradford clutches his throat and starts coughing!
VIC WATERS: Eian is all fired up because of that early attack, it looks like their strategy has comeback to backfire on them.
BRANDON JACOBS: Eian picks Bradford off the mat by the hair and puts him in a front face lock. BRADFORD TRIES TO FOREARM EIAN TO IN THE CHEST TO BREAK THE HOLD BUT EIAN HOLDS ON! HE NAILS HIM AGAIN! Eian breaks the hold! BRADFORD GOES FOR A CLOTHESLINE…. EIAN DUCKS! Bradford turns around… URANGE BY EIAN! Eian goes for the cover! The ref makes the count…. 1-----------2------STARR DIVES IN AND KNOCKS EIAN OFF!
VIC WATERS: That was very close, this match was almost over right there! BRANDON JACOBS: Eian stands up to confront Starr… STARR CLOTHESLINES EIAN DOWN! Starr picks up Eian…. STARR GRABS EIAN BY THE NECK! CHOKESLAM! CHOKESLAM! CHOKESLAM!
VIC WATERS: Starr curses Eian out and at the refs urging goes back to his corner. BRANDON JACOBS: Bradford picks up Eian…. RAPTURE FLOWING DDT! Bradford covers! 1-----------2---------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: How did he kick out of that?! With all the punishment he took, that is inhuman!
BRANDON JACOBS: Bradford picks Eian up off the mat…. INSIDE CRADLE BY BRADFORD! 1-------------2-------------3!! The second try got him! We are all tied up at one fall a piece!
VIC WATERS: Bradford took advantage of the bad knee, it is hard to break free from a cradle when you are in pain!
BRANDON JACOBS: We will be back for the third and deciding fall right after this commercial break!
