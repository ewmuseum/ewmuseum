WFW Paradise Lost: Felix Red vs. El Arco Iris vs. Luster

27 September 2004

PaulNJ21 — 27 September 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: This next match is a rematch of sorts of the match where Felix Red won the right to face Manson at the PPV.
JONATHAN MARX: Except instead of Gideon, Luster is the third man and he has a long history with El Arco Iris.
VIC WATERS: An undefeated Luster coming off his big win over Shawn Hart… BRANDON JACOBS: Luster had his best match in ages against Shawn Hart so it looks like the time off really did him some good. He looks in really good condition and he has a renewed vigor in his step.
JONATHAN MARX: Both Felix Red and El Arco Iris are coming off two really close title matches and now both men are looking for a rebound victory to get back into title contention.
VIC WATERS: Johnny, your father is one of the owners. What are the rumors that your father has an announcement to make after this match?
JONATHAN MARX: I am not exactly sure what it is about, but if Wink is going to take a break from all of the legal stuff happening behind the scenes with the Inner Circle to come out here, it must be important. Wink did say that the announcement would fulfill a dream of the WFW’s when it first started so it must be big.
BRANDON JACOBS: If Wink is excited about it, it must be big. But the owners have strange tastes so who knows. All I know is we are in for a hell of a match between Felix Red, El Arco Iris, and Luster.
JONATHAN MARX: I am not really sure who has the advantage in this match, history would say Felix Red but El Arco Iris’ outing at Cherry Blossom Chaos amidst all those brawlers was tremendous.
VIC WATERS: With all the animosity between Felix Red and El Arco Iris, if Luster played his cards right and picked his opportunities, he could walk out of this match with the win.
JONATHAN MARX: That is very true, I’ve seen that happen in a lot of three way matches, you have to keep your focus that you are battling two people instead of one and you can’t get too carried away with any one person or it will work to your disadvantage.
BRANDON JACOBS: This one looks like it is going to be a dead heat, lets send this one to the ring and get this underway!
RING ANNOUNCER: THE FOLLOWING MATCH IS A THREE WAY DANCE! The first man to get a pinfall or submission, wins the match. Introducing first, a member of Team LOVE, weighing in at 213 pounds and standing at 6 feet, three inches, “The Boy Who Destroyed The World” and former BAD World Heavyweight Champion, FELIX RED!

(CUEUP: “"Funkytown" by Disco Fever) RING ANNOUNCER: And last but not least, the man who helped end Hart’s WFW World Heavyweight Title reign and the sexiest man in all of WFW, from Atlanta, Georgia… LUSTER!

JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris is such a good sport letting El Arco Iris lock up with Felix Red. I wish all of my opponents were the gentleman El Arco Iris is.

JONATHAN MARX: These ladies like Luster dirty…err… that came out wrong. JONATHAN MARX: Listen to those shots, El Arco Iris is just tearing into him. BRANDON JACOBS: NOW LUSTER IS COMING BACK WITH SOME OF HIS OWN AND THE MUCH SMALL EL ARCO IRIS IS ROCKING BACK AND FORTH TAKING THE CHOPS FROM THE MUCH BIGGER LUSTER!
VIC WATERS: Rip him to shreds! BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS IS ON SHAKY GROUND… LUSTER CHARGES EL ARCO IRIS AND THEY BOTH GO OVER THE TOP ROPE ONTO THE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: Damn, Luster put his own body at risk to take out El Arco Iris, it just goes to show you how much he wants to keep this winning streak alive.

JONATHAN MARX: He is going to need it if he wants to survive these two. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster picks El Arco Iris up…. BOTH MEN ARE EXCHANGING PUNCHES BACK AND FORTH! A tired Felix Red is trying to get up amidst all of the fans… EL ARCO IRIS REACHES OUT AND GRABS THE HAIR OF FELIX RED AND PULLS HIM TOWARDS THE GUARDRAIL…. EL ARCO IRIS LOCKS ON A DRAGON SLEEPER ON FELIX RED ON THE OTHER SIDE OF THE GUARDRAIL AND IT IS DIGGING RIGHT INTO THE BACK OF FELIX RED!

JONATHAN MARX: All he needs to do is wake up enough to count the there because it looks like El Arco Iris has him.

EL ARCO IRIS FINALLY STOPS!
