Shock & Awe II: Mike Lane vs. Michael Dice (debut)

19 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 19 May 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back and it is time for our first match of the evening, two exciting newcomers will square off one on one against each when Michael Dice takes on Mike Lane. Who do you like in this match Vic?
VIC WATERS: I haven’t seen either of these guys so all I know about them are their press clippings. I really like Michael Dice though. He seems like my kind of guy.
BRANDON JACOBS: An alcoholic? VIC WATERS: Just because you see someone with a drink in their hand all the time doesn’t make him an alcoholic. Dean Martin use to do it all the time. Was he an alcoholic?
BRANDON JACOBS: Why yes, yes he was. VIC WATERS: Even still… it adds an element of class. He isn’t some coke addict who snorted ten million dollars up their nose. Now that is just isn’t insulting.
BRANDON JACOBS: A drug is a drug, whether it be alcohol or cocaine. It isn’t good for you and it sends a bad message to all the kids at home.
VIC WATERS: The difference is though if you do cocaine, it will clear out your bank account. Just say no expensive drugs.
BRANDON JACOBS: There may be a good message in there somewhere, folks, let’s send this one down to ringside and get this one underway!
(CUEUP: "Tumbling Dice" - The Rolling Stones) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from Las Vegas, Nevada, one of the biggest players in all of WFW, MICHAEL DICE!
::Michaels Dice comes down to the cheers from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “Nasty Ho" by Nuerotic Outsiders) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, making his return to the ring after his tragic car accident five years ago, from Braintree, Massachusetts, MIKE LANE!
::crowd with a mixture of boos and cheers and enters the ring:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: I can’t recall the last time we had two such strong new wrestlers debut around the same time.
VIC WATERS: I think you would have to go back to when Edmunds, Copycat, and Wells all came in together.
BRANDON JACOBS: These two are staring each other down and both men are trash talking each other.
VIC WATERS: Both men come from rough backgrounds, while both of these guys are wrestlers, they can mix it up with the best of them if necessary.
BRANDON JACOBS: MIKE LANE FIRES THE FIRST PUNCH AND HE DRIVES DICE BACK WITH SEVERAL QUICK SHOTS! Michael Dice is sent back reeling….
VIC WATERS: Mike Lane loves to dish out the pain. He really has nothing to lose in this match after what he has been through.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels Dice is fighting back with several shots of his own! Lane is starting to stagger… Dice charges Lane with a clothesline…. LANE DUCKS!
VIC WATERS: Lane is use to fighting while impaired so a few shots to the head aren’t going to hurt him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dice bounces off the ropes… LANE GOES FOR A BELLY TO BELLY BUT DICE HITS LANE WITH SEVERAL RAPID FIRE FOREARMS RIGHT TO THE FACE TO BREAK IT UP!
VIC WATERS: A lot of good counter wrestling here to start between this two… BRANDON JACOBS: Dice has Lane stunned…. DICE HITS LANE WITH AN EXPLODER SUPLEX! VIC WATERS: T-Bone Baby! BRANDON JACOBS: GOOD GOD, LANE LANDED RIGHT ON HIS HEAD! VIC WATERS: Lane took a hell of a shot there! BRANDON JACOBS: Dice is going for the cover! The ref goes down for the count! 1--------------------------------------------2--------------------------------- KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Lane somehow managed to get his shoulder up there. BRANDON JACOBS: Dice may have gone for the pin fall to early there, but he wants to make an impression on this crowd in his debut here in WFW.
VIC WATERS: When you are money, you want to make a money impression. BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAEL DICE LOCKS LANE IN A DRAGON SLEEPER! Lane is struggling to break free!
VIC WATERS: As if the effects from the Dragon Sleeper wasn’t enough… BRANDON JACOBS: MIKE LANE IS BITING THE UNDER ARM OF MICHAEL DICE AND DICE LETS GO AND CURSES AT LANE!
VIC WATERS: It looks like he penetrated the skin of Dice because his arm is bleeding… Dice is going to need his rabi shots!
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is warning Lane not to bite Dice again…. DICE RUSHES IN AND HE STARTS STOMPING RIGHT ON THE FACE OF LANE!
VIC WATERS: Kick him like the dog he is! BRANDON JACOBS: MIKE LANE LEG WHIPS DICE DOWN AND HE COLLIDES WITH THE REFEREE KNOCKING HIM DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: The referee was way too close to both men and paid for it. BRANDON JACOBS: He was just doing his job. LANE IS UP AND HE IS WAITING FOR DICE TO GET UP…… Dice is slowly trying to get back to his feet on his bad leg… REVERSE FOREARM SMASH TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD AND DICE FALLS DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: There is no call for hitting someone on the back of the head like that when they can’t even see it coming.
BRANDON JACOBS: I’d have to agree with that. Lane just likes to punish people. LANE GRABS DICE’S BY HIS HAIR AND STANDS OVER HIM AND NAILS HIM WITH SEVERAL FOREARM SHOTS RIGHT TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD! The referee is still down so Lane is allowed to get away with all this madness!
VIC WATERS: If Wink wants to lay down the law, why isn’t he out here now to keep something like this from happening so the line doesn’t get pushed further away from what is acceptable?
BRANDON JACOBS: The Board of Directors believe in letting things go as much as possible so the wrestlers can decide things instead of the owners. But this is just unacceptable… MIKE LANE IS TRYING TO WAKE UP THE REFEREE!
VIC WATERS: He may have him here after that brutal assault. BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is starting to come too… Mike Lane turns around and he picks up Michael Dice…. LANE PICKS MICHAEL DICE UP FOR AN OVER THE KNEE BACKBREAKER AND GOES FOR THE COVER!
VIC WATERS: Smart move by Lane working over that back before he tried to pin him even if he has had the hell beaten out of him by Lane.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref crawls over the make the count! 1-----------------------------------------------------2----------------------------------------- MICHAEL DICE KICKS OUT AND LANE LANDS RIGHT ON TOP OF THE REFEREE!
VIC WATERS: This poor ref hasn’t gotten a break yet today. BRANDON JACOBS: Lane is cursing at Dice and the referee…. LANE PICKS UP THE REFEREE BY THE HAIR BACK ONTO HIS FEET…. THE REFEREE FALLS DOWN BACK TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: He is out of it! BRANDON JACOBS: Lane is ignoring the ref being down and he is going to go back to work on Dice…. Dice is trying to get up…. LANE POWERS DICE UP AND LIFTS HIM UP IN THE AIR….. STALLING BRAINBUSTER AND HE DRIVES HIM RIGHT DOWN INTO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: He dropped him head first right into the mat. Both of these men are literally trying to kill each other to get this match over with but neither is succeeding in putting the other one away.
BRANDON JACOBS: Lane is trying to go wake up the referee…. DICE ROLLS UNDER THE BOTTOM ROPE ONTO THE FLOOR AND A FRUSTRATED LANE GOES OUT AFTER HIM!
VIC WATERS: Dice was able to regain his senses enough that he knew he was close to the ropes and that if he could merely roll himself out, he’d be able to buy himself some time. Of course, I don’t know if I would want to be on the outside of the ring with Michael Lane.
BRANDON JACOBS: We only have five minutes left in this twenty minute match for these two to put each other way, we are getting down to the nitty gritty right now.
VIC WATERS: Both men want to walk out of here a winner, but only one man can. BRANDON JACOBS: Mike Lane picks up Michael Dice off the ground…. BOTH MEN BEGIN EXCHANGING PUNCHES BACK AND FORTH!
VIC WATERS: I have no idea where Dice is getting this reserve to take all this punishment from Lane and still comeback.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dice is having trouble overcoming all of Lane’s punches after all the abuse he has taken… LANE WHIPS DICE INTO THE GUARDRAIL…. REVERSAL BY DICE AND LANE CRASHES BACK FIRST INTO THE GUARDRAIL!
VIC WATERS: That is enough to break your back if you go into that wrong. BRANDON JACOBS: DICE IS RIPPING UP THE MATS AT RINGSIDE LIKE A MAN POSSESSED! VIC WATERS: Dice sees that at his only shot to put Lane away and he may be right. BRANDON JACOBS: Michael Dice walks over to Lane who is still feeling the effects of crashing into the guardrail and he drags him over to the exposed portion of concrete floor… The ref who has finally regained his senses is up and he yelling at both men to get back in the ring.
VIC WATERS: If there is a God, which he is, he’ll hit this. BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAEL DICE PICKS UP MIKE LANE IN A SIDE SUPLEX AND DRIVES HIM DOWN WITH A POWER BOMB… BLUE THUNDER DRIVER RIGHT INTO THE CONCRETE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: YES! HE HIT IT! But time is running out, we are under the one and a half minute mark, he has to get him back into the ring to get the pin fall.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michael Dice picks up Mike Lane quickly and rolls him under the bottom rope…. NO! MIKE LANE GRABBED THE ROPE TO PREVENT HIMSELF FROM BEING ROLLED IN AND MICHAEL DICE IS PUNCHING AWAY WITH CLOSED FISTS RIGHT IN THE FACE OF MIKE LANE IN ORDER TO MAKE HIM LET GO!
VIC WATERS: The ref is signaling twenty seconds! BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAEL DICE PULLS HIM OUT OF THE RING IN ORDER TO TRY TO GET HIM BACK IN….. LANE KICKS DICE IN THE MIDSECTION!
VIC WATERS: Time is about to run out! BRANDON JACOBS: MIKE LANE PICKS UP MICHAEL DICE…. 1999 (STYLES’S CLASH) INTO THE CONCRETE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: That may have been even worse than the Blue Thunder Driver! BRANDON JACOBS: Lane is trying to pick up Dice and roll him into the ring… BUT HE COLLAPSES ON THE FLOOR IN EXHAUSTION!
VIC WATERS: Both men have taken a lot of punishment. BRANDON JACOBS: THE REFEREE IS CALLING FOR THE BELL! RING ANNOUNCER: This match has been declared…. A TIME LIMIT DRAW! CROWD: FIVE MORE MINUTES! FIVE MORE MINUTES! FIVE MORE MINUTES! BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is shaking his head no looking down at both competitors. Some ring officials are coming down to help both men to the back and make sure they are okay. What a hell of a debut for these two competitors.
VIC WATERS: I haven’t seen two guys make a debut like this since Pestillence and Famine when they almost killed each other in a wild brawl in their debut match. I wouldn’t want to be Jared Wells today because he has two new challengers to worry about.
BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we will be back with Sean Edmunds verses Pulsar, STAY TUNED!
