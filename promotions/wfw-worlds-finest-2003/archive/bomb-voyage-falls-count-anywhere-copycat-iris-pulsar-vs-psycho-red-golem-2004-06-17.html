BOMB Voyage: Falls Count Anywhere: Copycat/Iris/Pulsar vs. Psycho/Red/Golem

17 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 17 June 2004 JONATHAN MARX: Durring the break, we found that Copycat had not dropped Blue Cat into the water, but dropped him into a lifeboat hanging from the side of the ship instead.
VIC WATERS: That bastard is just trying to play with the head of everyone. JONATHAN MARX: There are plenty of people trying to make Copycat’s life difficult too. He is just taking the offensive instead of constantly being on defense.
VIC WATERS: This has all turned into some gang war, everyone is at each other’s throats. Can’t we all just get along?
JONATHAN MARX: I don’t think that is possible in this day and age. Everyone is battling for titles and power. I haven’t seen a struggle like this in ages.
VIC WATERS: LOVE has dug in their heels and they won’t rest until one of them wins the WFW World Heavyweight Title. Whether Copycat wants to deal with him or not, he is going to have to if he wants to win the World Heavyweight Title because they are both aiming for same goals.
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris is lucky he has Copycat and Pulsar by his side because otherwise this could all end very badly for him.
VIC WATERS: We all saw what Felix Red did in order to get a WFW World Heavyweight Title shot, imagine what Psycho would do to retain the WFW North American Title.
JONATHAN MARX: This is going to be a war, after what happened to Copycat and El Arco Iris at War Games, you know they will want to get their hands on LOVE and finally settle the score. Folks, we won’t keep you waiting any longer, lets send this one down to ringside and get this one underway!
(CUEUP: “Whiskey in a Jar” by Metallica) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from Australia, standing at over 6’3, PULSAR! ::Pulsar receives a huge roar from the crowd as he comes down to the aisle and enters the ring::
(CUEUP: “"If Born From This Soil: Treatments For the Injected Foetu" by Minor Procedure and Shai Hulud)
RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing second, making his way to the ring, weighing in at 213 pounds and standing at 6 feet, three inches, “The Boy Who Destroyed The World” and former BAD World Heavyweight Champion, FELIX RED!
::Felix Red comes down to the ring to the loud boos from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “Tunak Tunak Tun” by Dahler Mendhi) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing second member of the first team, hailing from over the rainbow, the Prince of Prismatic, EL ARCO IRIS!
::El Arco Iris walks down to a huge pop from the crowd, dozens of children can been seen in the crowd wearing El Arco Iris masks, and enters the ring::
(CUEUP: “The Day The World Went Away” by Nine Inch Nails) RING ANNOUNCER: And his partner, one of the toughest wrestlers in the world today, making his WFW debut. GOLEM!
::Golem walks down to a mixed reaction and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing the first member of the opposing team for this boot camp match where falls count anywhere in the ringside area, the current WFW North American Champion, PSYCHO!
(CUEUP: Copycat voice/over “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, LEND ME YOUR EARS!” followed by “My Way” by Butch Walker) RING ANNOUNCER: And the final member of the first team, the former WWL World Heavyweight Champion, from Kalamazoo, Michigan… COPYCAT!
::Copycat walks down to the ring to a mixed reaction with microphone in hand and enters the ring, Copycat throws the mic down to the boos of the crowd::
*bell rings* JONATHAN MARX: THERE IS THE BELL AND THESE TWO TEAMS HAVE LAUNCHED INTO AN ALL OUT WAR! Psycho is going after El Arco Iris, Copycat is going after Felix Red, and Golem is going after Pulsar!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar has earned a reputation for being the king of the six man tag, but if he wants to win this match, he is going to have to beat a member of LOVE or Golem to do it.
JONATHAN MARX: COPYCAT PICKS UP FELIX RED IN A MILITARY PRESS AND THROWS HIM THREE ROWS DEEP INTO AUDIENCE!
VIC WATERS: Good god! JONATHAN MARX: PSYCHO CLOTHESLINES EL ARCO IRIS OVER THE TOP AND GOES OVER THE TOP WITH HIM TO THE FLOOR! PULSAR HITS GOLEM WITH A RUNNING SHOULDER TACKLE AND HE FALLS OUT OF THE RING!
VIC WATERS: This one didn’t stay in the ring for very long. JONATHAN MARX: Thankfully Felix Red landed on some senior citizens which broke his fall so he doesn’t look all that hurt…. HERE COMES COPYCAT OVER THE GUARDRAIL AND RIGHT FOR FELIX RED AND THESE TWO BRAWLING THROUGH THE CROWD!
VIC WATERS: Why did we even bother to give them seats? JONATHAN MARX: COPYCAT SWINGS WITH A PUNCH BUT FELIX RED DUCKS! Felix Red hits a spinning heel kick on Copycat!
VIC WATERS: DOWN GOES COPYCAT! JONATHAN MARX: Psycho goes to pick up El Arco Iris… EL ARCO IRIS JUMPS UP AND HITS PSYCHO WITH A HURACANRANA AND PSYCHO FLIPS BACK FIRST INTO THE AUDIENCE!
VIC WATERS: The audience saw that one coming and was able to move out of the way just in time. The lack of guardrails in these matches on the cruise ship make this a dangerous spectator sport.
JONATHAN MARX: Pulsar and Golem are at each other’s throats… GOLEM THROWS PULSAR DOWN INTO A COUPLE OF ROWS OF CHAIRS!
VIC WATERS: I can’t imagine being a referee in this match when there are three fights constantly going on at the same time.
JONATHAN MARX: Felix Red picks up Copycat and he WHIPS HIM HEAD FIRST INTO THE SIDE OF THE FUNNEL!
VIC WATERS: Funnel? JONATHAN MARX: Funnel, whatever it is called, The round curved thing that comes out of the ship…
VIC WATERS: You are certainly no seaman, but you certainly leave a stain on this show. JONATHAN MARX: Jacobs is the expert on seaman, not me. PULSAR KICKS GOLEM IN THE GROIN WHEN HE GOES TO PICK HIM UP AFTER A SERIES OF STOMPS!
VIC WATERS: Golem doesn’t appear to be an expert either. JONATHAN MARX: COPYCAT WAS BUSTED OPEN BY THE PIPE… FELIX RED HITS COPYCAT WITH A STANDING DROPKICK AND HE CRASHES DOWN ON THE DECK OF THE SHIP!
VIC WATERS: Many people feel that Copycat is the uncrowned number one contender and Felix Red wants to change that image.
JONATHAN MARX: FELIX RED PICKS UP A MESH CHAISE AND HE THROWS IT DOWN AT COPYCAT! Felix Red grabs the suntan lotion on the table next to where the chaise was and walks towards Copycat.
VIC WATERS: I wouldn’t trust Felix Red with a bottle of water let alone suntan lotion. JONATHAN MARX: EL Arco Iris and Psycho have been struggling back and forth for position on a suplex…. EL ARCO IRIS TAKES PSYCHO DOWN WITH A GERMAN SUPLEX RIGHT INTO THAT WOODEN DECK!
VIC WATERS: Damn, that has to hurt. JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris wastes no time and he picks Psycho off the ground…. HE LOCKS ON A DRAGON SLEEPER!
VIC WATERS: If he could submit Psycho before the PPV, it would be a major feather in his cap to getting the psychological advantage. MY GOD, LOOK WHERE GOLEM AND PULSAR ARE!
JONATHAN MARX: MY GOD! THEY ARE ON A LIFE BOAT HANGING BY THE HOOKS OVER THE SIDE OF THE SHIP AND THEY ARE EXCHANGING PUNCHES BACK AND FORTH AS IT ROCKS SIDE TO SIDE!
VIC WATERS: If that boat crashes into the water, neither man is going to be a factor in this match.
JONATHAN MARX: Golem stuns Pulsar with a right to the jaw…. GOLEM PICKS PULSAR UP OVER HIS SHOULDERS AND HE DEATH VALLEY DRIVERS HIM ONTO THE FLOOR OF THE LIFE BOAT!
VIC WATERS: Thank god those things are sturdier than they once were or he could have gone through.
JONATHAN MARX: Felix Red after having applied suntan lotion to himself has now covered his hands with them and he is going after Copycat… COPYCAT IS TRYING TO CRAWL AWAY! If he gets him in the eyes with that stuff or on the head where he was busted open, he is going to have to get that stuff off quickly or it is going to burn like hell.
VIC WATERS: Suntan lotion psychology! JONATHAN MARX: Copycat tries to get away on his hands and knees… BUT FELIX RED CATCHES THE CAT FROM BEHIND AND HE COVERS HIS FACE WITH THE SUNTAN LOTION! Copycat is screaming his head off and he bolts off towards the pool.
VIC WATERS: Thank god the pool is on the other side of this deck, because salt water would burn even worse.
JONATHAN MARX: FELIX RED DARTS AFTER HIM AND COPYCAT CANNONBALLS INTO THE POOL! VIC WATERS: Chlorine is better than salt, but it still stings a bit. JONATHAN MARX: FELIX RED DIVES AT COPYCAT AND TOPES RIGHTS ON TO HIS CHEST! VIC WATERS: DAMN! JONATHAN MARX: Meanwhile on the other side of the ship, the referee is checking on Psycho who managed to fight out of the dragon sleeper before El Arco Iris reapplied it and this time it looks like Psycho have already met his limit.
VIC WATERS: While Psycho is tough and he’d never give up, this could make him pass out where that won’t be a factor.
JONATHAN MARX: PSYCHO REACHES OUT AND HE PULLS THE REFEREE INTO EL ARCO IRIS AND HE BREAKS THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: Smart thinking by Psycho… he was lucky to break that hold once. JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris took quite a shot on the noggin when he hit the referee’s head dead on. A worn out Psycho picks up El Arco Iris…. HE WHIPS HIM INTO THE CROWD BUT THE CROWD CATCHES EL ARCO IRIS…. PSYCHO CHARGES AND HE HITS HIM WITH A YAKUZA KICK RIGHT IN THE FACE AND THIS TIME EL ARCO IRIS GOES DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Those fans though they were doing him a favor by bracing him but they only made it harder on him.
JONATHAN MARX: PSYCHO GRABS EL ARCO IRIS BY THE MASK AND DRAGS HIM ACROSS THE DECK TO THE CHAIN FOR THE SECOND ANCHOR!
VIC WATERS: I hate El Arco Iris and I don’t even know if I like where this is headed… JONATHAN MARX: OH MY GOD! PSYCHO PICKS UP EL ARCO IRIS AND HE TIGER DRIVERS EL ARCO IRIS INTO THE SPINDLE OF CHAIN FOR THE ANCHOR! El Arco Iris is bleeding through his mask.
VIC WATERS: The referee is still down and he looks out of it. JONATHAN MARX: Uh oh, LETS GET BACK TO THE BOAT ON THE SIDE OF THE SHIP! GOLEM HAS THROWN SAND BAGS ON TOP OF PULSAR AND HE IS NOW ON THE DECK TRYING TO RELEASE THE BOAT INTO THE WATER! Pulsar is trying to get up…. GOLEM FINALLY RELEASES THE ROPE AND PULSAR THE BOAT CRASH DOWN IN THE WATER BELOW!
VIC WATERS: GOLEM HAS TAKEN PULSAR OUT OF THE MATCH! JONATHAN MARX: That bastard knows his rep in matches like this, this unbelievable. Now Golem, Felix Red, and Psycho has a three on two advantage! GOLEM GOES OVER TO HELP PSYCHO OUT AND PSYCHO IS ASKING GOLEM TO FIND SOME ROPE!
VIC WATERS: There are way too many foreign objects on a ship it seems. JONATHAN MARX: GOLEM PULLS SOME ROPE OUT OF A BOX ON THE DECK AND HE TAKES IT OVER TO PSYCHO WHO BEGINS TO TIE UP THE ARMS AND LEGS OF EL ARCO IRIS!
VIC WATERS: With all this water around, this can be nothing but evil. JONATHAN MARX: Meanwhile on the other side of the ship, Copycat is beating the bejesus out of Felix Red after taking that tope… COPYCAT DRAGS OVER A BLOW UP BOAT IN THE WATER AND STICKS FELIX RED INTO THE BOAT! Copycat is climbing out of the water…. COPYCAT RUNS OVER TO THE DIVING BOARD AND HE JUMPS OFF THE DIVING BOARD ONTO FELIX RED WITH A FROG SPLASH!
VIC WATERS: That has to be the first frog splash ever to make a splash. JONATHAN MARX: Copycat is trying to drag Felix Red out of the pool…. WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES GOLEM AND PSYCHO WITH A TIED UP EL ARCO IRIS! They are threatening to throw El Arco Iris into the pool if he doesn’t let go of Felix Red!
VIC WATERS: Copycat has a choice, he can either leave El Arco Iris behind and get the pin over the number one contender and possibly take his title shot away or he can save his friend.
PSYCHO: What is going to be Copycat? JONATHAN MARX: A pissed off Copycat lets go of Felix Red and Felix Red swims toward the diving board side of the pool, Golem & Psycho race over and they throw El Arco Iris down on the ground.
VIC WATERS: It is now a three on one, nobody can save Copycat now. JONATHAN MARX: OH MY GOD, LOOK WHO IS BACK… PULSAR HAS CLIMBED BACK ONTO THE DECK OF THE SHIP AND HE SLOWLY SNEAKING BEHIND GOLEM, PSYCHO, AND FELIX RED!
VIC WATERS: TURN AROUND! JONATHAN MARX: PULSAR HAS AN OAR AND HE NAILS FELIX RED IN THE BACK AND HE FALLS INTO THE POOL! PSYCHO TURNS AROUND AND HE HITS PSYCHO RIGHT IN THE HEAD AND HE FALLS BACK INTO THE POOL! Golem charges for oar… he grab it in time before he gets hit and Pulsar and Golem are wrestling over it!
VIC WATERS: Now that is what I call a save. JONATHAN MARX: COPYCAT IS BEATING THE HELL OUT OF FELIX RED AND PSYCHO! Pulsar finally wrestles away the oar but hits himself in the head when Golem lets go, GOLEM FALLS DOWN RIGHT ONT THE CONCRETE!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar just made the ultimate sacrifice for his team. JONATHAN MARX: WAIT A SECOND, PSYCHO AND FELIX RED ARE GETTING THE ADVANTAGE OF COPYCAT NOW!
VIC WATERS: Look at El Arco Iris… he has gotten his hands free! JONATHAN MARX: EL ARCO IRIS MANAGES TO GET HIMSELF UPRIGHT AND HE HOPS ON HIS BOUND UP FEET ON THE DIVING BOARD….. A dazed Golem yells at Psycho & Felix Red to turn around…. THEY TURN AROUND AND EL ARCO IRIS FLIES OFF THE DIVING BOARD AND HITS PSYCHO WITH A MISSLE DROP KICK WHICH SENDS HIM FLYING INTO FELIX RED WHO HITS HIS HEAD ON THE SIDE OF THE POOL!
VIC WATERS: Oh man. JONATHAN MARX: COPYCAT IS GETTING OUT OF THE POOL AND HE PICKS UP A SEMICONCIOUS FELIX RED…. LITTER BOMB RIGHT ONTO CONCRETE AROUND THE POOL! Golem is getting up and he is headed over to help Felix Red.
VIC WATERS: This could be all she wrote for Felix Red! JONATHAN MARX: COPYCAT IS MAKING THE COVER! 1---------------------------------------------2------------------------------ GOLEM DIVES TO MAKE THE SAVE!
VIC WATERS: So very close… JONATHAN MARX: GOLEM PICKS UP COPYCAT AND THEY START BRAWLING TOWARDS THE OTHER SIDE OF THE SHIP! El Arco Iris is trying to get out of the pool with his tied up feet but Psycho is holding on to them.
VIC WATERS: Psycho is half conscious, but he knows he can’t let El Arco Iris get out of the pool and make the save or this match is over.
JONATHAN MARX: A BLOODY PULSAR RUNS OVER AND HE PICKS FELIX RED UP…. BLACK HOLE RIGHT ON THE CONCRETE! The ref goes down for the count! 1-------------------------------------------------------2--------------------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. The team of Copycat, El Arco Iris, and Pulsar! JONATHAN MARX: HERE COMES GOLEM, HE HEARD THE ANNOUNCEMENT AND HE DOESN’T LOOK ALL THAT HAPPY…. HE MAKING A B-LINE FOR PSYCHO WITH A CHAIN IN HIS HAND HE WRAPS AROUND HIS HAND AND HE STARTS PUNCHING PSYCHO REPEATEDLY WITH IT!
VIC WATERS: He is tired of losing, this is the second straight time he has lost a match because of Psycho and he isn’t going to take it anymore.
JONATHAN MARX: THE OFFICIALS ARE COMING OVER TO TAKE GOLEM AWAY! Everyone is wet and bloody, this certain has been a mess. Folks, we are going to take a commercial break, when we comeback, MANSON PUTS UP THE WFW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE AGAINST THE LONGEST REIGNING WFW NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION EVER, RICHARD GIDEON! STAY TUNED!
