WFW: Rage in a Cage: Richard Gideon vs. Shawn Hart

21 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 21 June 2003 BLACK ROSE: We are live backstage with Richard Gideon with a pre-match interview. RICHARD GIDEON: Thank you Black Rose, C.O.C.K.S may have interfered last week in several matches, but won’t dare do it tonight nor in the main event. After all, Shawn Hart may be a primadonna, but he isn't stupid and he knows the consequences. I still owe Psycho one for all that he is done for me, so consider your back watched Psycho, razorwire and welded door notwithstanding.
BLACK ROSE: I wouldn’t worry Gideon. Nobody is going to be able to break into that thing. It has greater security than the most recent Harry Potter book. What could happen?
RICHARD GIDEON: There are a lot of devious men in this federation who crave the WFW World Title gold who will stop at nothing to get it.
BLACK ROSE: I always thought the purpose of a cage match was to keep people from interfering. Oh well, lets send this one back to Brandon Jacobs & Vic Waters!
BRANDON JACOBS: Thanks Black Rose, I can’t tell you how much I’m looking forward to this match tonight between Shawn Hart and Richard Gideon. Shawn Hart & C.O.C.K.S may be arrogant, but after all the heart and soul that Shawn Hart put into that match at Cherry Blossom Chaos. I can’t help but want to see him win the match tonight.
VIC WATERS: You aren’t the only one Brandon, when I walked into the building today, I saw a lot of fans with “I Love C.O.C.K.S” t-shirts showing their support for their favorite stable here in the WFW. You love C.O.C.K.S too Brandon?
BRANDON JACOBS: I can’t get enough of them. Shawn Hart is going to be a hard man to beat tonight but Gideon has proven that he can beat anyone on any given night after his series with Doc.
VIC WATERS: I’m pulling for Gideon tonight and let me explain why. Gideon may be a royal pain in the ass, but Shawn Hart was the one who pinned Doc, not Gideon. Gideon also has shown the potential to give any champion fits and I want to see Psycho suffer like he has never suffered before.
BRANDON JACOBS: That makes perfect sense in its own weird way. It is strange that I’m pulling for the rule breaker while you are pulling for the fan favorite.
VIC WATERS: In some parallel universe, we are calling this match like we normally would call it for this world so all is good.
BRANDON JACOBS: I can’t wait any longer to start the show, let’s send this match to ringside!
::Remember by Disturbed wails over the loud speakers:: RING ANNOUCER: Introducing first, from Buffalo, NY, THE SYSTEM RICHARD GIDEON! ::Gideon comes down to the ring to the roar of the crowd and enters the ring:: ::"Look At Me" by Geri Haliwell plays over the loud speakers:: RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Orlando, Florida! The man who proclaims that he is the real World Heavyweight Champion of the World, “The Phenom” Shawn Hart!
::Shawn Hart walks down to the ring without his bodyguard Tiny:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: I was told before the match that both Hart and Gideon agreed that will be no interference match. They want to prove to each other who is the better man and the winner will go on to face the WFW World Heavyweight Champion at the PPV.
VIC WATERS: You rarely see two men do that, but that just shows you how high class these two guys are. They don’t want some screwjob ending and someone going on undeservedly to a PPV title shot. They want a decisive winner.
BRANDON JACOBS: It doesn’t get better than this… BOTH MEN LOCK UP! Gideon grabs the arm and chicken wings it behind Hart! Hart tries to elbow out of it… Gideon ducks! Gideon grabs the OTHER ARM AND LOCKS ON A DOUBLE CHICKEN WING!
VIC WATERS: Richard Gideon has great reflexes, most people don’t even expect to be elbowed, let alone act fast enough to grab the other arm and lock it in.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon LIFTS HIS KNEE AND RAMS IT INTO THE LOWER BACK OF HART! Hart got nailed hard! HE NAILS WITH ANOTHER KNEE SHOT TO THE LOWER BACK!
VIC WATERS: Hart needs to break this hold quickly or these shots will cost him the shot at the WFW World Heavyweight Title.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart throws himself back quickly and Gideon and sandwiched into the mat! Hart is able to free arm and he grabs the hair of Gideon and snap mares him over to the mat! Hart has broken the hold and listen to these fans applaud the escape!
VIC WATERS: These two guys can really go on the mat, it is rare to see exchanges like this instead of guys killing the other with big move after big move.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart grabs the arm of Gideon and pulls him off the mat and whips him off the ropes… INVERTED ATOMIC DROP! Gideon just went cross eyed because of the pain!
VIC WATERS: Brandon, can you explain to me in a league where the nutshot is banned why a guy can do an inverted atomic drop?
BRANDON JACOBS: It is rather simple, the atomic drop done properly most of the time gives a jolt to the spinal cord limiting their mobility, in reality, the move doesn’t crotch their opponent… most of the time.
VIC WATERS: So why does everyone grab their nads afterwards? BRANDON JACOBS: Well, not everyone does it perfectly, and it is too close to tell whether it was a good atomic drop or a crotch shot so refs left it go.
VIC WATERS: Looks like Hart didn’t hit that too well… OR DID HE? *Dr. Evil like laugh* BRANDON JACOBS: SHAWN HART CLOTHESLINES GIDEON DOWN TO THE MAT! VIC WATERS: That clothesline looked like it nailed Richard Gideon flush in the jaw. Hopefully he’ll be able to recover quickly.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart stands over Gideon and grabs him by the ears… HART USES THE EARS OF GIDEON AS HANDLES AND RAMS HIS HEAD INTO THE MAT! Good gawd!
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart has been to the dance twice before without winning the title and you can sense how much winning the title means to him. The WFW World Heavyweight Title is the most prestigious in the world having descended from both the NGEN & BAD World Heavyweight Titles and having that gold around your waist can make your career.
BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is stepping in because Shawn Hart is getting out of control. Hart backs off and he lets a dazed Gideon try to get up. GIDEON TAKES A SWING AT SHAWN HART AND MISSES! Shawn Hart winds up…. DISCUS PUNCH! Gideon goes down once again!
VIC WATERS: Gideon doesn’t look like he is all there right now, it would be good if he could find an opportunity to roll out and get a timeout outside of the ring.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart signals to the crowd that is going for a tombstone piledriver… HE PICKS UP GIDEON OVER HIS SHOULD AND TRIES TO CENTER HIM… GIDEON IS TRYING TO CLIMB DOWN TO THE MAT AND HE GRABS THE LEG OF HART!
VIC WATERS: Gideon has a lot of spirit to fight this move, it is awfully hard and awfully dangerous to try what he is doing right now.
BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON LEG SCISSORS THE HEAD OF HART AND TAKES HIM DOWN TO THE MAT WHILE STILL HOLD THE LEG AND TRANSITIONS INTO A HALF BOSTON CRAB!
VIC WATERS: Whoa, I didn’t even know that was possible. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is leaning back with the hold but Hart is using all his strength to get to the ropes… HART REACHES FOR THE ROPES…. HE MISSES! Gideon looks back and notices how close Hart is the ropes and HE PULLS HIM TO THE CENTER OF THE RING AND GRABS THE OTHER LEG AND TURNS IT INTO A FULL BOSTON CRAB!
VIC WATERS: You always have to be aware of where you are in that ring because your location in the ring is oh so important in determining what your next move is going to be.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is leaning back all the way on the Boston Crab and Hart is squirming as the pain is increasingly become less and less tolerable. WAIT A SECOND, What is Hart doing? He is using his power to tuck his head under his body… HE ROLLS GIDEON FORWARD WITH HIS LEGS HOOK ON HIS ARMS! The ref makes the count! 1---------------------2--------------GIDEON KICKS OUT!
VIC WATERS: That was an incredible piece of wrestling by Hart, that just shows you how smart he is and why he is always on the top of the card.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon gets up and he DROPKICKS THE BAD LEG OF SHAWN HART! Shawn Hart curses at Gideon for attacking his leg and Gideon laughs as he stalks the injured Shawn Hart.
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart looks like he is in a great deal of pain, I don’t know how this match is going to go that long when he can barely stand.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon picks Shawn Hart off the mat…. FISHERMAN SUPLEX HOOKING HART’S BAD LEG! 1---------------------2----------------- HART KICKS OUT! It didn’t look like he had the hands locked and it cost him!
VIC WATERS: If Gideon had a firmer grip, he’d be WFW World Title bound by now. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is upset with himself but he is going to go for it again! Gideon hooks the leg… SMALL PACKAGE BY SHAWN HART! 1---------------2----------------KICKOUT! Gideon broke out of the pinfall with authority once he did but he cut it awfully close.
VIC WATERS: The small package is the most dangerous move in all of wrestling because anyone could be caught with it.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon gets up and charges Shawn Hart…. SHAWN HART GOES FOR THE SUPERKICK. BUT GIDEON GRABS THE LEG! Gideon laughs at Hart because he he has his bad leg in his hands… SHAWN HART NAILS HIM WITH AN ENZIGURI TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD! The may have been an act of desperation! If he didn’t hit that, his goose would be cooked.
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart is very, very lucky he was able to nail that. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon has taken a awful lot of shots to head in this match. I don’t know how he keeps on getting up but his drive has not faltered once today.
VIC WATERS: That is the drive of the champion which makes the great ones great, that is how Doc Silver is always able to manage to kickout of so many moves. He’d rather die than lose and that is the kind of mentality that you have to have in that ring.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is going for the tombstone piledriver again…. VIC WATERS: You would think that he would learn his lesson against this crafty Gideon. BRANDON JACOBS: Hart picks up Gideon over his shoulder, he sets him in position… BIG TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER INTO THE MAT! Oh no, the ref got knocked over when he was trying to position Gideon.
VIC WATERS: Oops. BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart makes the cover on Gideon, he doesn’t realize that the ref has been knocked down! The fans are counting as Shawn Hart is pounding his hand on the mat for the ref to count…
VIC WATERS: Counting the pinfall for youself doesn’t count, hell, I wish it did or Silver would have never lost to Psycho.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart looks back and realizes the ref is down, he limps over to tend to the ref! The looks like he is out cold… GIDEON CLIPS HART FROM BEHIND WITH HIS SHOULDER TO HIS INJURED LEG AND HART SCREAMS IN PAIN!
VIC WATERS: Gideon is looking outside of the ring, he is going outside of he ring to get something.
BRANDON JACOBS: HE HAS A CHAIR! Gideon rolls back into the ring and places it on the injured leg of Hart… GIDEON IS GOING TO THE TOP? IS HE GOING TO? NO HE CAN’T BE… OH MY GOD, YES HE IS! HE FLIES OFF WITH THE SYSTEM FAILURE AND DRIVES THE CHAIR INTO THE BAD LEG OF HART WITH HIS OWN BODY!
VIC WATERS: And it is all perfectly legal, the ref is out cold. BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second, HERE COMES DIABLO! The ref is starting to get up…. Gideon gets up to keep Diablo from entering the ring…. DIABLO THROWS A FIREBALL AT GIDEON BUT GIDEON DUCKS AND IT NAILS THE REFEREE!
VIC WATERS: Who is going to count falls now?! BRANDON JACOBS: Officials are taking Diablo back to the dressing room so he can’t cause anymore damage. HERE IS THE NEW REF, OH NO, IT IS ZACK SIRUS!
VIC WATERS: I thought he was ran out of this promotion when I got here?! BRANDON JACOBS: Apparently not, this can’t be good…. Gideon picks up Shawn Hart… “THIS ONE IS FOR DOC!” …. HE NAILS SHAWN HART WITH THE ACES FULL! NOBODY KICKS OUT OF THE ACES FULL! Zack Sirus goes down to make the count! 1----------------------2------------HART GETS HIS FOOT ON THE ROPE!
VIC WATERS: *phew* Gideon winning with Aces Full would have been disgraceful. BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo is trying to comeback down to ringside to help Hart despite officials holding him back and Sirus is yelling at him to get back or Hart will get disqualified! HART NAILS THE DISTRACTED GIDEON WITH A LOW BLOW!
VIC WATERS: Damn that Diablo straight to hell. BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart calls for his finish…. HE NAILS GIDEON WITH THE HART ATTACK! Hart calls Zack Sirus to turn around and make the count! 1----------------------2----------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Zack Sirus may have cost Shawn Hart the victory there by making Hart wait for him to make the three count.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart picks up the exhausted Gideon… Gideon can barely stand on his own two fet… SHAWN HART BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND HE NAILS HIM WITH THE EL CODO EXPLOSIVO! Gideon goes down like he has been shot!
VIC WATERS: Damn those C.O.C.K.S! BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart hooks the leg! Zack Sirus goes down to make the count! 1----------------------2---------------3!!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match and the man who will advance to the PPV to face the WFW World Heavyweight Champion… SHAWN HART!
BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo and Tiny are coming out and are celebrating Hart’s victory over Gideon. These two men had an incredible match and Gideon really came close to becoming a World Title contender once again.
VIC WATERS: This is so sickening, they are celebrating like they just won the Superbowl. BRANDON JACOBS: Let Hart have his moment. Listen to this crowd give Hart a standing ovation.
VIC WATERS: Didn’t Hart say this would be a clean match? BRANDON JACOBS: Well, it was relatively… folks, we all know what is next, Rage in the Cage between the World Champion Psycho taking on his bitter rival and the former champion Doc Silver! Stay tuned folks, we will be back after this!
