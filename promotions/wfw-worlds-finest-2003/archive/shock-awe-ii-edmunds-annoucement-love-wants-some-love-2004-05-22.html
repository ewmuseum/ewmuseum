Shock & Awe II: Edmunds' Annoucement, LOVE Wants Some Love

22 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 22 May 2004 (BROADCAST BOOTH) JONATHAN MARX: We are back from commercial break and Edmunds has still not left the ring. VIC WATERS: It looks like Edmunds has an announcement to make. JONATHAN MARX: Let’s send this down to the ring so we can hear what Edmunds has to say. (RINGSIDE) EDMUNDS: “Well now its set. The match the WORLD has been waiting for. For YEARS the fans have been writing, calling, demanding that Edmunds versus Copycat happen. And before your very eyes here tonight we have made that happen. EDMUNDS v COPYCAT at the pay per view.”
(Crowd cheers) EDMUNDS: “Or at least, that’s what you’d like to think!” (Crowd quiets) EDMUNDS: “You see, as PRESIDENT of World’s Finest Wrestling, I have the option of … lets say, changing, matches. So as President of WFW, I am announcing that at the pay per view, I, ‘Simply Sensational’ Sean Edmunds, will NOT be facing Copycat!”
(Crowd begins to boo) EDMUNDS: “In fact, Copycat will be facing one an opponent of MY choosing. In fact, I do believe he’s out back right now …”
(Trash litters the ring) EDMUNDS: “Copycat used to enlist the help of his friend BLACK CAT … well, I went out and searched … and searched … and searched. And who did I find? Well, hot damn … I found Copycat’s opponent at the pay per view…”
(The lights dim and a spotlight goes on the curtain … all of a sudden from behind the curtain a wrestler comes running down to the ring. He is wearing a blue cape, has on blue tights, and has a blue cat mask over his face.)
EDMUNDS: “Introducing Black Cat’s brother … and the man who will be taking my spot in the match at the pay per view ….. BLUE CAT!”
(Crowd boos heavily. Blue Cat enters the ring and walks next to Edmunds, he’s about Edmunds’ height as weight. Edmunds goes to give him the mic, but Blue Cat declines.)
EDMUNDS: “Ah that’s right. You see, after all the abuse that Blue Cat received at the hands of Black Cat when they were younger, he is too frightened to talk.. you may even call him a scaredy cat … Blue Cat is going to take out all his frustrations … all those years of being held back by Black Cat and Copycat … and he’s going to do so at the pay per view .. and if somehow, someway Copycat manages to get passed Blue Cat … then I *MIGHT* just let him get a match with me.”
(The crowd starts aiming the trash at Blue Cat now, who is “licking” his paw.) EDMUNDS: “Day after day we have to listen to Copycat whine about respect. But what about the respect he never gave Blue Cat? You gotta give to receive …. And I’m sure that everyone in this building, and at home, want to see Blue Cat get the respect he deserves from Copycat at the pay per view. I’m sure you understand, Copycat. But I’m doing this for my good friend here Blue Cat.”
(Sherri takes Blue Cat by the “paw” and starts leading him from the ring.) EDMUNDS: “I guess you’ll just have to wait a little bit longer, folks … because Edmunds versus Copycat … JUST AIN’T HAPPENIN’…. Don’t adjust your television sets …. I *AM* this sensational!”
(CUEUP: ‘Brain Stew” by Green Day as Edmunds walks out of the ring … the bodyguards then protect him, Sherri and Blue Cat begin to walk up the rampway.)
(BROADCAST BOOTH) JONATHAN MARX: That Edmunds has some nerve. People have been waiting forever for Edmunds verses Copycat to happen and HE ABUSES HIS POWER AS PRESIDENT TO UNDO THE MATCH ONCE COPYCAT EARNED THE RIGHT TO HAVE IT! That is bull****.
VIC WATERS: Do you know anything about this Blue Cat? JONATHAN MARX: I don’t know and I don’t want to know with all the evil masked men have caused around this place tonight…. I wasn’t even suppose to be here tonight.
(CUEUP: "Pet" by A Perfect Circle) VIC WATERS: What is that?! (RINGSIDE AREA) ::Edmunds and company stop in their tracks as LOVE (Anarky, Jared Wells, Psycho, and Felix Red) walk through the curtain::
ANARKY: "Congratulations, Mr. President. You've just inherited quite a situation. Consider us your own perosnal Bin laden. Difference
is Mr. President. We won't be as hard to find. But since I've known you for so long, Sean... I'm gonna cut you a break. I'm gonna give you a choice. A choice like I gave Lion.
"You can either give us what we want... or you can spend the rest of your Presidency cowering in fear, wondering when the next attack will be. It could be
tomorrow. Or the next day. It could be right before your match, or right after. Hell, we could do it right when you walk in the Arena.
"First things first, Sean. This little ban they got on me and Manson? That's bull<BLEEP>. More politics to protect the Champ. So you lift that ban, Sean. I
ain't askin' you for a Title Shot. I can take care of that. But I can't have more WFW politicians trying to tell ME what to do. Tell ME who I can and can't fight. It don't work like that. We make the rules now.
"Our second demand is very simple. My man Jared here... JARED WELLS: I’m the BAD World Champion. And since that's gonna stand for something now, instead of another way to screw over the guys who busted their asses all these years... I am going to make my OWN stips. That's right. From now on... it's my way or the highway.
(Wells hands the microphone back to Anarky) ANARKY: "Next up... you know a man named Copycat. He should be in line for a Title Shot. He should be. But since you're the President now... and you hate him as much as I do... he's not going to get one in THIS lifetime.
"Finally... for the man that would call himself the World's Finest Wrestler... Michael Manson... you make it happen. I don't give a <BLEEP> if it's for the
Title. I don't give a <BLEEP> if it's sanction. I don't give a <BLEEP> if it's tomorrow or six months from now.
"You're going to make it happen. And you're going to make it as violent as possible. And Michael... Michael's going to find out exactly what I am... and
it ain't his lackey... and it ain't his whore... "Mikey's gonna learn real quick what happens when your run your mouth with LOVE in charge. "So you've got some work to do, Sean... but just remember. You've got a taste... just a TASTE... of things to come. You back out on me now, pal... and you're gonna wish you never heard the name LOVE. You're gonna wish you never won that election. We can do this the easy way...
"... or the FUN way. It's all up to you now." (Anarky throws down the microphone on the ground and a stunned Edmunds and company watch on as LOVE exits through the curtain)
(BROADCAST BOOTH) JONATHAN MARX: The inmates are taking over the asylum. Someone needs to stand up to LOVE before they get what they want and take over this whole entire damn league.
VIC WATERS: Johnny, when are you going to learn that LOVE is the greatest force in the universe?
JONATHAN MARX: These guys are even more evil than COCKS ever dreamed of being. Hopefully stands up to these guys and puts them in their place before its to late.
VIC WATERS: I don’t know, I think LOVE would look good on the marquee. JONATHAN MARX: Folks, when we comeback from this commercial break, we will have the three way match everyone has been waiting for…. STAY TUNED!
