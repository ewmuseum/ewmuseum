WFW Vox Part Deux: WAR GAMES

1 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 1 April 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: I was thinking about something during the break. VIC WATERS: Do you need some Advil? BRANDON JACOBS: I’m fine, thanks. We’ve been swerved so many times by Luster already. What if the people from the institution were all staged in order to make people think that Luster is gone when he really is the mystery man in the War Games tonight?
VIC WATERS: Have you ever known Luster to plan some elaborate scheme in order to trick us? ::thinks to himself:: Good point.
BRANDON JACOBS: Plus, Copycat and Luster have issues from the past when he worked for Scotty Michaels so there is a history there.
VIC WATERS: Now that I think of it, who did Copycat Kit Kat Kick after Luster clobbered Michaels with a chair?
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster… It is all starting to make sense now. VIC WATERS: Most elaborate schemes do when you really think about it. BRANDON JACOBS: The only thing which makes me have my doubts is the fact that Luster has already been taken out of the match with Michaels and Shawn Hart verses Scotty Michaels has been set in stone. Would Luster sabotage his highest profile match to date on the biggest show of the year?
VIC WATERS: No, no he wouldn’t and if he did, that would make you think Michaels and Luster patched it up and Michaels is never going to forgive Luster for costing him the WFW World Heavyweight Title ending his title reign prematurely.
BRANDON JACOBS: That isn’t something you forgive in a month. You can’t even say it was an accident because of what Luster did after the match.
VIC WATERS: Exactly. BRANDON JACOBS: I’ve learned one thing from Anarky, Psycho, and Jared Wells… expect the unexpected. Copycat, Gideon, El Arco Iris, and Felix Red are going to have their hands full.
VIC WATERS: When you have that many evil minds collected in one place, they are going to be awfully hard to beat.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is feuding with Anarky, Gideon is feuding with Psycho, Jared Wells is feuding with El Arco Iris… there will be a lot of stories within the even bigger story of the war games.
VIC WATERS: You never want to say a Wargames is secondary but for Psycho, if he could walk out of hear having softened up Gideon even if his team doesn’t win the match, it would be a major victory.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho has been trying desperately to get back into pinnacle of where he once was and he knows by beating Gideon it would be his first step on his path back to the WFW World Heavyweight Championship.
VIC WATERS: Psycho is starting to remind me of the man who had that war with Doc Silver again and if he returns to that form, nobody in WFW will be safe.
BRANDON JACOBS: Then you have the much bigger feud between Copycat and Anarky which all started at Merrython when Copycat used a paintbomb in his Sack O Weapons match in order to get the victory and ever since then, Anarky has literally been obsessed with Copycat.
VIC WATERS: Wrestling has a long history of feuds started by seemingly little things that somehow become big things.
BRANDON JACOBS: I just hope that this Wargames will settle the issue between these two men and order can be restored to the league so this war doesn’t spill over to the anniversary show.
VIC WATERS: Chaos makes things much more fun. BRANDON JACOBS: With all the outside talent coming in as guests of the WFW and with wrestling’s eye on WFW as we celebrate our one year anniversary, how will it look to the wrestling world if we can’t contain this feud for one night?
VIC WATERS: Like any other wrestling company? BRANDON JACOBSS: Quiet you. Folks, before we begin, there are some items we have to address. Because the match will be going on, only the first two men from each team will have formal ring introductions since we don’t want to distract the wrestler in the middle of the match. Secondly, before the show, Richard Gideon and Jared Wells were present for the flipping of the coin in order to determine which team would be the first to get the two man advantage, Gideon’s team lost the toss so Wells’ team will be the first to have the two man advantage at the five minute mark and every man after that will come out in alternating two minute intervals. When all eight men are finally in the ring, the wargames will officially begin and the only way to win is to make a member of the opposing team submit or surrender.
VIC WATERS: So the submission won’t count until seventeen minutes in during which each team will try to weaken another member down to the point where he gives up.
BRANDON JACOBS: Exactly. Now that we have all the explanations under way, LET’S SEND THIS DOWN TO RINGSIDE AND GET THE WAR GAMES UNDERWAY!
(RINGSIDE) (CUEUP: Copycat voice/over “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, LEND ME YOUR EARS!” followed by “My Way” by Butch Walker) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the former WWL World Heavyweight Champion, from Kalamazoo, Michigan… COPYCAT!
::Copycat walks down to the ring in his robe and beret, hands his robe to the ring attendant and enters the cage::
CUEUP: “Sober” by Tool) RING ANNOUNCER: And the first member of the opposing team, a world champion ten times over and former Mr. Vicious title holder, from Hartford, Connecticut… ANARKY!
::Anarky comes down to the ring to the loud boos of the crowd:: BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES ICEKOLD FROM BEHIND…. ICEKOLD CLOBBERS ANARKY FROM BEHIND WITH A BEER BOTTLE AND BREAKS IT IN HALF! Anarky falls down to the ground like a shot!
VIC WATERS: That was totally uncalled for. BRANDON JACOBS: This is war and you have to do everything in your power to win because you know Anarky and all of those other thugs would do the same thing. The ring attendants are coming down to help Anarky.
VIC WATERS: Isn’t there a rule that if you don’t make it down to the ring before the time elapses that you forfeit your turn?
BRANDON JACOBS: Yes it is, but thankfully for Anarky he has five minutes to enter the ring unlike the usual two. The ring attendants are helping up Anarky…. ANARKY PUSHES THEM AWAY AND THE BUSTED OPEN ANARKY IS HEADING TO THE RING!
VIC WATERS: Peace? How will these two ever make peace? The war is on! BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky enters the cage and the ring attendants are locking the door behind him. LET THE WAR GAMES BEGIN! ANARKY AND COPYCAT ARE EXCHANGING PUNCHES AND COPYCAT IS GETTING THE BEST OF THE BLOODIED AND DAZED ANARKY!
VIC WATERS: Thank god Anarky and the rest of the team have the advantage because otherwise I could easily see Anarky being the one to submit.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat knees Anarky in the gut… COPYCAT LIFTS ANARKY UP OFF THE GROUND FOR A PRESS SLAM… COPYCAT THROWS ANARKY INTO THE CAGE AND ANARKY STUMBLES DOWN ON THE OTHER SIDE OF THE ROPES!
VIC WATERS: A bloody Anarky was just thrown viciously into the cage. I’ve never seen a man treat Anarky like that.
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky crawls out from the bottom of the rope…. COPYCAT PICKS HIM UP OFF THE MAT … COPYCAT THROWS HIM INTO THE SECOND RING AND ANARKY CRASHES DOWN ON THE MAT WITH A THUD!
VIC WATERS: The blood from Anarky’s head left an imprint of his face on the mat. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat goes through the ropes and slowly walks towards Anarky who is trying desperately to make it to the ropes. COPYCAT GRABS ANARKY BY THE HAIR…. COPYCAT KICKS UP ANARKY AND BODYSLAMS HIM DOWN IN THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: These sick fans have been waiting a long time to see Anarky beaten down like this when Anarky & Wells teamed to beat Copycat & El Arco Iris in the three way dance.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat signals to the crowd, COPYCAT BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES… THE CAT’S MEOW (People’s Elbow)!
VIC WATERS: The Cat’s Meow is the most ridiculous move in all of wrestling. It just an elbow drop and this crowd is going ape ****.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat drove the blow right onto the blood forehead of Anarky after Icekold broke a bottle over his head. He could have pinned him with that move if this wasn’t a wargames match.
VIC WATERS: Maybe you are right… BRANDON JACOBS: THE CLOCK IS TICKING DOWN! TEN! NINE! EIGHT! SEVEN! SIX! FIVE! FOUR! THREE! TWO! ONE!
VIC WATERS: IT IS JARED WELLS! BRANDON JACOBS: The ring attendants are opening the cage and Jared Wells is waiting to get in… JARED WELLS CHARGES IN AND THE CAGE IS LOCKED BEHIND HIM!
VIC WATERS: Wells knows if Anarky doesn’t get some help soon, they will be at a man disadvantage for the rest of the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat turns around and he puts up his dukes as Jared Wells crosses over to the other ring…. JARED WELLS TACKLES COPYCAT AND STARTS POUNDING HIM WITH MOUNTED PUNCHES!
VIC WATERS: GO WELLS! BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat reverses and he starts pounding away at Wells! Copycat picks Wells off of the mat… COPYCAT GOES FOR A WAISTLOCK TO HIT WELLS WITH A BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX BUT WELLS GRABS THE HEAD OF COPYCAT AND STARTS BITING HIM TO BREAK THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: These are the kinds of things you need to do in a fight. This is a war, you can’t hold anything back because you are moral or ethical.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat in agony grabs his face…. WELLS HITS COPYCAT WITH A SAVATE KICK AND COPYCAT GOES DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Anarky needs to get up and take advantage of the current situation to double team Copycat before the two minutes run out.
BRANDON JACOBS: IT IS TOO LATE FOR THAT! THE CLOCK IS TICKING OFF! TEN! NINE! EIGHT! SEVEN! SIX! FIVE! FOUR! THREE! TWO! ONE!
VIC WATERS: Who is going to come out to help Copycat? BRANDON JACOBS: IT IS EL ARCO AND HE IS MAKING A B-LINE TO THE RING! VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris may look little, but he packs a big punch. BRANDON JACOBS: The ring attendants OPEN UP THE CAGE AND EL ARCO IRIS RUNS IN!! VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris is going to have a fresh Wells and an almost recovered Anarky to deal with.

VIC WATERS: Both boots of El Arco Iris went right into the face of Jared Wells. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is trying to help Copycat back up to his feet… WAIT A SECOND, ANARKY IS GETTING UP…. HE CLIPS THE KNEE FROM BEHIND ON EL ARCO IRIS!
VIC WATERS: OH MAN! BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is slowly getting up… JARED WELLS AND ANARKY JOIN ARMS… DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE ON COPYCAT AS HE TURNS AROUND AND COPYCAT GOES DOWN! The beret falls off of the head of Copycat to the other side of the rope.

VIC WATERS: Even if El Arco Iris wanted to submit, he can’t until all the men involved in the match enter the cage.
BRANDON JACOBS: Jared Wells is climbing to the top rope…. TOP ROPE LEG DROP ONTO THE BACK OF THE HEAD OF EL ARCO IRIS!
VIC WATERS: This kid has a lot of courage to walk into a minefield like this. BRANDON JACOBS: IT IS ABOUT TO GO FROM BAD TO WORSE! THE CLOCK IS TICKING DOWN! TEN! NINE! EIGHT! SEVEN! SIX! FIVE! FOUR! THREE! TWO! ONE!

BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is taking his time to get down here while Jared Wells holds Copycat as Anarky smears the blood from the beer bottle shot to the head right into the eyes of Copycat as he tries to break free!
VIC WATERS: That savate kick really must have thrown Copycat for a loop because he hasn’t looked 100% since.
BRANDON JACOBS: YOU MAY SPOKE TOO SOON! COPYCAT HAS BREAKS FREE OF WELLS AND HE CLOTHESLINES ANARKY! Wells charges Copycat…. BIG BACK BODY DROP AND WELLS LANDS HARD ON HIS BACK!
VIC WATERS: God damn, I cursed them. BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, PSYCHO HAS MADE HIS WAY IN THE RING AND HE IS STANDING PERCHED FROM THE FIRST RING ON THE TURNBUCKLE…. COPYCAT TURNS AROUND… PSYCHO FIGHT ONTO COPYCAT!

YAKUZA KICK RIGHT TO THE FACE!

VIC WATERS: Copycat and El Arco Iris are down so he is going to have to fight off all three to buy his partners sometime if he wants to win this match.

VIC WATERS: If you saw those three in a dark alley, wouldn’t you be? BRANDON JACOBS: You raise a hell of a point. ANARKY, WELLS, AND PSYCHO ARE BACKING HIM INTO A CORNER… THEY CHARGE GIDEON AND GIDEON CLIMBS UP THE TOP TURNBUCKLE AND FLIPS OUT OF THE CORNER AS THEY CHARGE!
VIC WATERS: Gideon better use all the fancy moves he has now because he won’t be able to do them while he is in his wheelchair.
BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT IS CALLING TO GIDEON! GIDEON TURNS AROUND AND CATCHES COPYCAT’S HAT!

GIDEON CLOBBERS PSYCHO WITH A PUNCH AND PSYCHO GOES DOWN! JARED WELLS CHARGES AND GIDEON FIRES A RIGHT AT HIM AS WELL AND IT SENDS HIM DOWN HARD TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: What was in that beret, a can of spinach? BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY CHARGES GIDEON BUT ANARKY DUCKS OUT OF THE WAY THE LAST SECOND! VIC WATERS: Smart thinking by Anarky. BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND HITS GIDEON WITH A FLYING FOREARM AND HE FALLS BACK INTO THE ROPES!
VIC WATERS: Anarky has that never say die attitude. I never remember seeing him submit before.
BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON COMES BACK OFF THE ROPES AND HIT ANARKY WITH RIGHT HAND WHICH SENDS ANARKY DOWN FOR THE COUNT! Gideon raises his hand!
VIC WATERS: LOOK! GIDEON HAS BRASS KNUCKLES AROUND HIS FIST! He must have pulled it out of Copycat’s beret!
BRANDON JACOBS: THE GREAT EQUALIZER! THE CLOCK IS RUNNING DOWN! LET’S FIND OUT WHO THE MYSTERY MAN FOR PSYCHO’S TEAM IS! TEN! NINE! EIGHT! SEVEN! SIX! FIVE! FOUR! THREE! TWO! ONE!
VIC WATERS: IT’S….. NO ONE! NO ONE IS COMING OUT! BRANDON JACOBS: They must have missed their cue. They have two minutes to get down to the ring or Psycho’s team will forfeit their fourth member.
VIC WATERS: That is totally unfair. You saw what Icekold did to Anarky earlier, maybe Copycat clobbered the mystery man.
BRANDON JACOBS: Who know but the match must go on… GIDEON IS HANDCUFFING WELLS UNDER THE BOTTOM ROPE TO THE CAGE!
VIC WATERS: NO DAMNIT! How is Wells suppose to help Anarky and Psycho if he is handcuffed to the damn cage.
JARED WELLS: GET THESE OFF OF ME! BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat and Gideon didn’t get where they are without being extremely bright wrestlers. Gideon is calling El Arco Iris and Copycat over to work on Psycho and Anarky and Copycat is helping El Arco Iris up.
VIC WATERS: Look, Psycho is beginning to get up! You could hit Psycho with a mack truck and you couldn’t keep him down.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon throws the brass knuckles back over to Copycat. AN IRRITATED PSYCHO ATTACKS GIDEON AND THEY BEGIN TO BRAWL TOWARDS THE CORNER!
JARED WELLS: GET HIM PSYCHO! GET THE KEY! VIC WATERS: When these two meet at the Superbowl of Wrestling, the roof is blow right off the building.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat slips on the brass knuckles…. COPYCAT HITS ANARKY AND HE FALLS BACK INTO THE ARMS OF EL ARCO IRIS WHO PUSHES HIM BACK AT COPYCAT WHO HITS HIM A SECOND TIME WITH THE BRASS KNUCKLES!
VIC WATERS: They are making Anarky into a human punching bag. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat grabs the arms of Anarky… EL ARCO IRIS HITS HIM WITH THE HAPPY DANCE! Anarky falls face first onto the mat!
VIC WATERS: Anarky got off to a terrible start after the beer bottle shot and after that he was behind the eight ball for the rest of the match.

COPYCAT IS HOLDING ANARKY UP FELIX RED….. WAIT A SECOND, FELIX RED JUST SPINNING HEEL KICKED EL ARCO IRIS!
