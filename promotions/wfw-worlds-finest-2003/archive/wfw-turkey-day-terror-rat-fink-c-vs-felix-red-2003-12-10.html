WFW Turkey Day Terror: Rat Fink (c) vs. Felix Red

10 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 10 December 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back and it is time for our first title defense of the evening, Rat Fink will put his BAD World Heavyweight Title on the line tonight against Felix Red. As much as I love Felix Red, it is hard to pick against Rat Fink with the current hot streak.
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink has really been on an impressive run as champion and he has really opened up a lot of eyes here in WFW. This current run only matches his initial run against Manson when he was in a feud for the history books. Rat Fink, unlike a majority of his opponents, plays the BAD rules to perfection and outwits and out mans his opponents. You can complain about Rat Fink’s tactics as being unfair, but if everyone is allowed to break the rules and do what they want and they see what Rat Fink has done, they should know what they are getting themselves into. Thinking about it, in many ways, Rat Fink has done for the BAD World Heavyweight Title what Manson did for the WFW North American Title.
BRANDON JACOBS: That is a hell of a comparison and while Manson’s title reign has been longer, Rat Fink has had to defend the BAD World Heavyweight Title on virtually every show since he won. He can’t afford to have a bad day or he will walk out without the strap. This Felix Red though, he is a guy who can end a title reign in the blink of an eye. Rat Fink has to be very careful.
VIC WATERS: Don’t get me wrong, I’m not underestimating Felix Red, I saw what he did to Michaels in the WFW North American Title match, but Rat Fink is ten times more evil and devious than Scotty Michaels.
BRANDON JACOBS: Definitely, with the exception of Doc Silver, Rat Fink has been the top man you don’t want to get on his bad side since the inception of the league. Sometimes he scares even me.
VIC WATERS: Me too, me too. BRANDON JACOBS: Let’s send this one to ringside and get this BAD World Heavyweight Title match underway!
(CUEUP: “"If Born From This Soil: Treatments For the Injected Foetu" by Minor Procedure and Shai Hulud)
RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the challenger, making his way to the ring, weighing in at 213 pounds and standing at 6 feet, three inches, “The Boy Who Destroyed The World”, FELIX RED!
: :Felix Red comes down to the ring to the roar of the crowd:: CUEUP: “Megalomaniac” by KMFDM) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, the leader of the New Age Revolution and the current BAD World Heavyweight Champion, RAT FINK!
::Rat Fink walks down to the ring with the Satan’s Clause walking behind him and Elfbot, Satan Clause enters the ring and Rat Fink sits down in a chair at ringside::
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: The ref shows Felix Red the title and hands it to the time keeper. VIC WATERS: Look at the sheer size of Satan Clause! Felix Red is in for a long night. BRANDON JACOBS: Satan Clause and Felix Red lock up…. SATAN CLAUSE POWERS FELIX RED BACK INTO THE CORNER! Felix Red rubs his chin and comes out of the corner… FELIX RED EXTENDS HIS HANDS FOR ANOTHER LOCK UP!
VIC WATERS: Foolish mortal. BRANDON JACOBS: Satan Clause goes to lock up…. SPINNING HEEL KICK BY FELIX RED! Satan Clause is sent stumbling back to the turnbuckle and this crowd is on its feet for Felix Red.
VIC WATERS: Felix Red’s martial art ability makes him a dangerous man despite the size disadvantage in this match.
BRANDON JACOBS: SATAN CLAUSE RUSHES FELIX RED AND RAMS HIM WITH HIS CHEST DOWN TO THE GROUND! Satan Clause picks him up by the throat…. SATAN CLAUSE THROWS HIM INTO THE CORNER!
VIC WATERS: I think Felix Red has officially been put on the naughty list. BRANDON JACOBS: SATAN CLAUSE LIFTS HIS LEG AND TRIES TO CHOKE OUT FELIX RED WITH HIS BOOT! Felix Red arms are flaring trying to escape the choke. The ref is trying to peel Satan Clause off of Felix Red, but Satan Clause just gives him a look and he backs off.
VIC WATERS: The only BAD rules is that there are NO rules. The ref is there to look after the safety of both men, but he is unable to DQ either man for their actions in or out of the ring.
BRANDON JACOBS: Satan Clause lets go of the hold, finally, he is showing a little bit of mercy. Satan Clause picks Felix Red up over his shoulder….. RUNNING POWERSLAM! Felix Red is out of it!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red has to figure out a way to overcome Satan Clause’s size or he is doomed to an early death.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink grabs a table and he slides it into the ring… He is telling Satan Clause to piledrive him through so he doesn’t have to face Felix Red ever again.
VIC WATERS: They have faced each other an awful lot lately. BRANDON JACOBS: Satan Clause sets up the table…. Felix Red is beginning to stir. Satan Clause picks up Felix Red and dumps him on the table… FELIX RED STANDS UP ON THE TABLE…. HE NAILS SATAN CLAUSE WITH A TORNADO DDT AND SATAN CLAUSE GOES HEAD FIRST INTO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: That is the quickness of Felix Red coming into play and Satan Clause is going to have a migraine when he wakes up.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red puts Satan Clause onto the table. Felix Red is going over to side of the apron opposite of where Rat Fink is facing…. TWISTING LEGDROP ONTO THE BODY OF SATAN CLAUSE…. But the table didn’t break!
VIC WATERS: That must be one of those industrial strength tables we are using tonight. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red moves the table over towards the corner and pounds on Satan Clause so he stays down! Felix Red is going up to the top rope… HE IS GOING FOR THE SHATTERED HORIZON! IF HE NAILS THIS, THIS MATCH IS OVER…. Wait a second, Rat Fink has grabbed the leg of Felix Red. Rat Fink is climbing onto the apron… BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX ONTO THE FLOOR BY RAT FINK! GOOD LORD!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink knew if he didn’t stop Felix Red, his BAD World Heavyweight Title would have been in serious trouble.
BRANDON JACOBS: Satan Clause rolls off the table and is taking a second to compose himself. Satan Clause is heading to the outside of the ring. Elfbot is helping up Rat Fink. Rat Fink picks up Felix Red and holds his arms back…. SATAN CLAUSE TAKES SOME SLEIGH BELLS FROM ELF BUT AND LAYS INTO FELIX RED WITH SEVERAL PUNCHES! Felix Red has been busted open and Rat Fink lets go so of Felix Red who collapses down on the ground.
VIC WATERS: Satan Clause really busted him open. I don’t know how long Felix Red is going to be able to go now in his condition.
BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK IS RIPPING UP THE MATS AT RINGSIDE! He wants Satan Clause to finish the job and nail him with the jumping piledriver onto the concrete.
VIC WATERS: If he hits this, Felix Red is done for. BRANDON JACOBS: Satan Clause picks Felix Red off the ground…. LOW BLOW ON SATAN CLAUSE! VIC WATERS: NO! NOT SATAN’S BALLS! BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink charges Felix Red…. FELIX RED BACKBODY DROPS FELIX RED ONTO SATAN CLAUSE! Elfbot charges Felix Red… SPINNING HEEL KICK!
VIC WATERS: It is that survivalist instinct, that rush of adrenaline you get when all seems to be lost that Felix Red is feeding off now.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is a house of fire. FELIX RED ROLLS RAT FINK INTO THE RING AND RAT FINK IS BEGGING FOR MERCY!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red has been waiting awhile for this moment, it isn’t going to be pretty.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red picks Rat Fink up off the ground… RAT FINK RAMS THE SHOULDER INTO FELIX RED TAKING THE WIND OUT OF HIM! Rat Fink bounces off the ropes…. HE NAILS HIM WITH THE STEINERLINE AND FELIX RED IS TURNED INSIDE OUT!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red hesitated if only for a second and when he did, Rat Fink came up with his battle plan to keep him at bay until his cronies could get up and help.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink is moving the table over towards the corner…. Maybe setting up for his finisher later on in the match. If he nails the top rope crucifix powerbomb through the table, stick a fork in him, he is done.
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink really has a mean streak like no other. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink picks up Felix Red… he is going for a Falcon’s Arrow but Felix Red is fighting it…. RAT FINK KNEES HIM IN THE STOMACH! WINS THE BATTLE AND NAILS HIM WITH THE FALCON’S ARROW! Rat Fink goes for the cover! 1-----------------------------------2-------------------------KICKOUT! Rat Fink almost got the three there.
VIC WATERS: You can tell that Felix Red’s energy is slipping and all he needs is one more big move to finish off this match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink is calling for his finisher…. He is calling Satan Clause and Elfbot to help hold the table in place so this time when the impact is made, Felix Red will go through.
VIC WATERS: With how strong that table is, it may still not be enough. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink drags Felix Red over by the hair…. HE PLACES HIM ON THE TOP ROPE! WAIT A SECOND…. TWO FANS JUST BOLTED TOWARDS THE RING! Rat Fink starts climbing up to the second rope unaware of what is happening…
VIC WATERS: I recognize them, that is Toxick White and Krist Blue! BRANDON JACOBS: Toxick runs in….. HE KICKS SATAN CLAUSE IN THE STOMACH…. DIAMOND CUTTER! Satan Clause rolls out of the ring! Krist Blue jumps off the ropes… QUEBRADA ONTO ELFBOT! Elfbot rolls out of the ring!
VIC WATERS: NO DAMNIT! This is blatant cheating! BRANDON JACOBS: All of this is legal under bad rules. Rat Fink is looking back to see what all the chaos is… TOXICK THROWS SOME WHITE POWDER IN RAT FINK’S EYES! Rat Fink is trying to rub his eyes to get it out…. FELIX RED ELBOWS RAT FINK IN THE HEAD AND RAT FINK FALLS BACK ONTO THE THE TABLE! JESUS CHRIST…. THAT TABLE STILL DIDN’T BREAK AND RAT FINK LANDED HARD ON HIS BACK ON THE TABLE!
VIC WATERS: Damn, that table could be a BAD World Heavyweight Title contender. BRANDON JACOBS: A confused Felix Red climbing down off the top as Toxick and Krist Blue are cheering for him. Felix Red is telling both of them to get out and they leave the ring.
VIC WATERS: Looks like Felix Red wasn’t in on their plan. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red picks up the injured Rat Fink off of the table… RAT FINK TAKES A SWING AT HIM BUT HE DUCKS! Felix Red catches Rat Fink off balanced and lifts him up…. ECSTASY THROUGH AGONY…. AND THROUGH THE TABLE FROM HELL! Felix Red makes the cover! 1----------------------------------2------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. AND NEW BAD WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION…. FELIX RED!
BRANDON JACOBS: Holy cow, what a match, 19 minutes of sheer brutality. Rat Fink’s reign of terror is finally over and Felix Red has finally won his first title here in the WFW.
VIC WATERS: That match could have gone either way and Rat Fink gave Felix Red a hell of a beating. I wouldn’t want to be Felix Red tomorrow morning. It should be interesting to see if Felix Red can match Rat Fink’s over three month reign of defending the BAD World Heavyweight Title.
BRANDON JACOBS: That is going to be awfully hard to do, the BAD World Heavyweight Title, unlike the WFW World Heavyweight and WFW North American Title, is defending on every show and the sheer brutality in title defenses is world’s higher than either belt.
VIC WATERS: There hasn’t been a title like it since the old Mr. Vicious title. BRANDON JACOBS: No where even close, okay folks, we are going to take a short commercial break, but when we comeback, Manson and Anarky will face off for the first time ever here in the WFW. That one should be a classic, STAY TUNED!
