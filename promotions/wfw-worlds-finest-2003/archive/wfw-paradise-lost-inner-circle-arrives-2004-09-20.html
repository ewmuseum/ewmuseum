WFW Paradise Lost: INNER CIRCLE ARRIVES!

20 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 20 September 2004 [The camera cuts to the inner bowels of Fenway Park where Black Rose stands at the garage entrance area with cigarette in his hand.]
BLACK ROSE: This is Black Rose and I'm told that a limo carrying the members of The Inner Circle is on its way to the arena as we speak, and it is expected that we will get our first glimpse of the mysterious new faction as they make their way into the arena to meet with Chris Wink and work out some last minute contract details.....
[The camera pans over and we see a long stretch limousine appear.] BLACK ROSE: It appears we're about to have our answer! [The limo makes its way to the players entrance of Fenway Park where Black Rose is standing. Black Rose puts out his cigarette and walks up to the limo.]
BLACK ROSE: Ladies and gentlemen..... [The door begins to slowly open......and someone steps out..... BLACK ROSE: ...apparently the Inner Circle is..... [Black Rose gasps] ["THE QUEEN OF THE RING" LINDSAY TROY.] BLACK ROSE: LINDSAY TROY?? BRANDON JACOBS: (voice over) Good god, she must be coming to get me for screwing her out of Survivor. HIDE ME!
JONATHAN MARX: (voice over) Calm down, I’ll protect you damn coward. [Another figure slides out behind her and stands up. CHRISTIAN SANDS. Black Rose's gaze move quickly from Troy to Sands in surprise, his eyes wide.]
BLACK ROSE: CHRISTIAN SANDS???? JONATHAN MARX: (voice over) One of the greatest wrestlers in GXW history… [As Black Rose keeps his gaze on Sands and Troy, he almost misses the massive man who steps out of the car on the opposite side.]
["THE EGO BUSTER" DAN RYAN.] VIC WATERS: (voice over) Holy s**t, look at the size of that guy. BRANDON JACOBS: (voice over) THAT IS DAN RYAN! The best big man in all of wrestling… JONATHAN MARX: That is the former CSWA World Heavyweight Champ… who has been a champion ten times over… business is about to pick up. .
[Black Rose stares in stunned silence as Ryan smirks and moves to the side to allow one final member to step out of the vehicle.]
[MAELSTROM.] CROWD: MAELSTROM! MAELSTROM! MAELSTROM! JONATHAN MARX: ::spits his water out:: BRANDON JACOBS: (voice over) Good god, there goes your inheritance. I heard these signings would be big, but we broke the damn bank.
BLACK ROSE: **** ...I'm at a lost for words....Miss Troy....Sands.... Ryan....MAELSTROM.....do you have anything you'd like to say to the fans here tonight??
[Troy says nothing, but starts to walk toward the arena door. Sands follows and ignores Black Rose. Maelstrom glances at Dan Ryan and gives a little smirk, which Ryan returns and they too head into the arena without a word leaving a stunned Black Rose standing alone.]
[Broadcast Booth] JONATHAN MARX: Is tonight our season premiere? BRANDON JACOBS: Wrestling companies don’t have seasons, but if we did, yes it would be. This is the biggest happening since the Superbowl of Wrestling and time will only tell how big this day will be in World’s Finest Wrestling history.
VIC WATERS: LOVE has another stable which is going to give them a run for their money… BRANDON JACOBS: Manson, Copycat, Anarky….. this is going to affect everyone. VIC WATERS: The question is… whose side are they on? BRANDON JACOBS: I have a feeling we will find out soon enough. Folks, as much as I hate to do this, we have to take a commercial break, when we comeback, WFW President Sean Edmunds till take on Iron Lion…. STAY TUNED!
