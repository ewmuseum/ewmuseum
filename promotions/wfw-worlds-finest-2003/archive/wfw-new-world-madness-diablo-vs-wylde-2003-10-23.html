WFW: New World Madness - Diablo vs. Wylde

23 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 23 October 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back from commercial break and coming up next is Alex Wylde making his singles debut against Diablo in a soap box vs. flaming table match.
VIC WATERS: The soap box is actually bigger than a regulation soap box, it is more of the size of a large crate.
BRANDON JACOBS: This should be quite a violent match. We all remember the last flaming table match Diablo was in when he ended the promising career of Andre Eian before it even began.
VIC WATERS: Diablo was brutal in that match, he showed no mercy on Eian at all and took great pleasure in retiring him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo has already ended one career in a flaming tables match, Wylde could be next, LET’S SEND THIS ONE TO RINGSIDE!
(CUEUP: “Sangre de Mis Manos” by Gorillaz) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, one of the founding members of C.O.C.K.S. and one of the best young wrestlers in all of wrestling today, DIABLO!
:Diablo comes down in his C.O.C.K.S shirt to mixed boos and cheers from a crowd: and enters the ring::
(CUEUP: "Diesel Power" by Prodigy) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, the current WFW President and the captain of Team Wylde at Almost Live, ALEX WYLDE!
::Alex Wylde walks down to a loud pop and enters the ring:: ::the ref pats Diablo down for foreign objects:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde and Diablo meet face to face in the ring… DIABLO SLAPS HIM ACROSS THE FACE!
VIC WATERS: Knowing Wylde, that was probably deserved. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde and Diablo are exchanging punches back and forth. The natural brawler Diablo is gaining the advantage on Wylde.
VIC WATERS: Wylde probably should have tried to make this into a wrestling match, but with the stips in this match, that may have been damn near impossible.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde is wobbling on his feet… Diablo bounces off the ropes… HE NAILS WYLDE WITH A FLYING FOREARM AND WYLDE TUMBLES OVER THE TOP ROPE!
VIC WATERS: There have been so many heated fights tonight. BRANDON JACOBS: With the competition getting better and better by the card, men have to fight harder to work their way up the ladder and they know they have to make the most out of every shot they are given. Diablo is exiting the ring and is going to the outside.
VIC WATERS: Wylde needs to slow this match down, they are already on the outside for Christ sake. He is one mess up from losing this match.
BRANDON JACOBS: DIABLO IS LAYING IN THE CHOPS INTO WYLDE WHO IS BACKED UP AGAINST THE GUARDRAIL!
VIC WATERS: Those knife edge chops are cutting through Wylde like butter. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde is trying to escape over the guardrail… DIABLO LOCKS WYDLE IN THE DRAGON SLEEPER!
VIC WATERS: There is nothing I’d like to see more than Diablo putting Wylde’s ass to sleep and slamming through a flaming table for all of his remarks about Shawn Hart being the first loser.
BRANDON JACOBS: SOME FANS ARE PULLING ON WYDLE’S LEGS AND HE BREAKS FREE FROM DIABLO’S GRIP!
VIC WATERS: Must be Wylde supporters…. BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo climbs over the guardrail…. WYLDE THROWS A CHAIR IN DIABLO’S FACE! The corner seemed to nick in him the side of the head and Diablo has been busted open.
VIC WATERS: Flying chairs can be dangerous. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde grabs Diablo’s arm and whips him into a row of chairs as people bail out of the way! Diablo tries to use a chair to get up but it slips and he falls back down.
VIC WATERS: Damn plastic chairs… BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde walks over to Diablo and he puts his leg onto of the head of Diablo…. ROCKER DROP ON THE CONCRETE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: The rocker drop is a move which has crippled a man for life. t is definitively a move you don’t want to take on the concrete floor.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde turns over Diablo and the blood has now made Diablo’s face a crimson mask… Wylde picks up Diablo by the hair and leads him back towards the guardrail…. DIABLO GRABS THE ARM OF WYLDE AND IRISH WHIPS HIM INTO THE GUARDRAIL!
VIC WATERS: Diablo really looks mad. BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo picks Wylde off the ground… SWINGING NECKBREAKER ONTO THE CONCRETE! Damn!
VIC WATERS: YES! BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo puts his hands on his face and covers his hands in the blood. DIABLO IS USING THE BLOOD TO WRITE C.O.C.K.S. ON THE CHEST OF WYLDE! Good lord!
VIC WATERS: Diablo is sending a message to everyone in the back that he is a dangerous man and he is someone that they should not only respect, but fear.
BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo dumps Wylde over the guardrail and moves Wylde to the setup table. Diablo grabs the lighter off of the time keeper’s…. HE LIGHTS THE TABLE ON FIRE!
VIC WATERS: Now will feel the hell the of Diablo. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde is slowly getting up…. DIABLO NAILS WYLDE WITH A DROPKICK AND HE STUMBLES BACK INCHES AWAY FROM THE FLAMING TABLE!
VIC WATERS: Come on Diablo, get the job done! BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo climbs up onto the apron and DIVES AT WYLDE….. WYLDE DUCKS AND DIABLO GOES THROUGH THE FLAMING TABLE HIMSELF!
VIC WATERS: OH MY GOD! BRANDON JACOBS: Attendants are rushing over to Diablo but it looks like most of the fire went out when he went through. This match can only end when Wylde goes through a flaming table or Diablo goes through a soap box.
VIC WATERS: I don’t know if you noticed Brandon, but Diablo is looking a lot more agile tonight. In order to get in better wrestling shape, he has lost a lot of weight and that was the reason he looked so agile on that dive.
BRANDON JACOBS: I certainly noticed that. The old Diablo wasn’t this quick. This is a better Diablo than ever before. Wylde is the first one to get up here and he pulls Diablo over toward the soap box. WYLDE GRABS THE HEAD OF DIABLO AND REPEATEDLY RAMS HIM INTO THE CRATE!
VIC WATERS: That is certainly an interesting way to put your opponent through a table. It is sort of like trying to cut a five dollar steak with a butter knife.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde isn’t doing this to put Diablo through the table, he is doing this to bust him open even more for what he did to him on the outside.
VIC WATERS: Look at that crate, it is covered in Diablo’s blood already and he hasn’t even gone through it yet.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde picks Diablo up…. HE STUN GUNS HIM FAST FIRST INTO THE CRATE! The crate looks like it is partially broken on the side, but since he didn’t go all the way through. It doesn’t count.
VIC WATERS: Tell that to the face of Diablo. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde is calling to the crowd that he is going to put Diablo through! Wylde climbs on the to apron and he tries to pull Diablo up…. DIABLO GRABS THE TIGHTS AND WYLDE FALLS ONTO THE TOP OF THE CRATE AND BANGS HIS HEAD BUT DOES NOT GO THROUGH!
VIC WATERS: That crate is the toughest mofo in this match. BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo picks the dazed Wylde up from his slumped position against the apron…. DIABLO IS CLIMBING ONTO THE BOX…. IT LOOKS LIKE HE IS GOING TO TRY TO PILEDRIVER HIM THROUGH!
VIC WATERS: This isn’t going to be pretty. BRANDON JACOBS: He picks up Wylde…. WYLDE LEG SCISSORS DIABLO’S HEAD TO BLOCK THE PILEDRIVER AND USES HIS LEGS TO PROPEL DIABLO THROUGH THE ROPES INTO THE RING!
CROWD: BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde is catching his breath on the outside. He almost went through his own soap box which by the looks of it so far and pretty damn sturdy.
VIC WATERS: Wylde would have been out for quite sometime if he nailed that one. Diablo could have done anything he wanted with him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo is really enraged after having the piledriver foiled by Wylde and he is heading back to the outside…. DIABLO GRABS THE HAMMER FOR RINGING THE BELL AND GOES AFTER WYLDE!
VIC WATERS: Wylde doesn’t even see him yet. BRANDON JACOBS: DIABLO THROWS THE HAMMER AT WYLDE’S HEAD BUT HE THROWS IT A BIT SHORT AND IT GETS LODGED IN THE TOP OF THE CRATE!
VIC WATERS: Damn! BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo runs and lunges towards Wylde and THE TWO MEN BEGIN TO BEAT THE CRAP OUT OF EACH OTHER ROLLING AROUND ON THE GROUND!
VIC WATERS: This really has gotten completely out of control. BRANDON JACOBS: DIABLO RAMS WYLDE’S HEAD ONTO THE CONCRETE FLOOR AND WYLDE LOOKS OUT OF IT!
VIC WATERS: We may need to get security out here shortly. BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo walks over to the crate…. HE PULLS OUT THE HAMMER AND HE IS GOING AFTER WYLDE!
VIC WATERS: GET HIM DIABLO! GET HIM! ::a masked man runs down to ringside with a chair:: BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, SOMEONE IS COMING DOWN HERE! DIABLO TURNS AROUND AND HE GETS PLASTERED WITH THE CHAIR!
VIC WATERS: What is going on?! BRANDON JACOBS: The mystery man helps Wylde up…. WYLDE GRABS THE LIGHTER AND HE SETS THE CRATE ON FIRE…. GOOD LORD! Wylde grabs Diablo and climbs onto the apron…. HE FALLS BACK AND DIABLO IS DRIVEN FACE FIRST THROUGH THE FLAMING CRATE WITH A STUN GUN! The ref is calling for the bell!
CROWD: REST IN PEACE! REST IN PEACE! REST IN PEACE! RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. ALEX WYLDE! BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde is heading towards the back and the mystery man picks up and bloodied Diablo and rolls him into the ring. THE MYSTERY MAN IS HANDCUFFING DIABLO!
VIC WATERS: The back is signaling we have to go to a break… BRANDON JACOBS: NOW?! Okay folks, we will be right back with the aftermath of this and the three way dance…. STAY TUNED!
