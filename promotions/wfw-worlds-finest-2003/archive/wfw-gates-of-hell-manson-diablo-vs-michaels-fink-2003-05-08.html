WFW Gates of Hell: Manson & Diablo vs. Michaels & Fink

8 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 8 May 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON May-08-03 AT 10:49&nbsp;PM (EST)]BRANDON JACOBS: Finally, we have reached our co-main event which will help determine who Manson faces in a title defense at the very first WFW PPV!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink has possession of the title and possession is nine tenths of the law. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink stole that title! The title belongs to Manson, and tonight, Manson is going to get it back.
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink has repeatedly made Manson look like a fool, and I don’t suspect anything will change tonight.
BRANDON JACOBS: We will see Vic, we will see. Let’s send it ringside for the introductions!
::"Clint Eastwood" by the Gorillaz blares over the loud speakers:: RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from Spanish Harlem, NY… DIABLO! ::Diablo walks slowly down to ringside, adjusting the tape on his wrist and enters the ring::
::Livin’ on a Prayer” by Bon Jovi rocks over the loud speaker:: RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, the first member of the opposing team, from Philadelphia, PA…. THE SUPERSTAR SCOTTY MICHAELS!
::Scotty Michaels walks down to ringside to a mixed reaction from the crowd:: ::Megalomaniac by KMFDM blares over the speakers:: RING ANNOUNCER: And his partner, from WCW Special Forces, one of the greatest super villains of our time, RAT FINK!!
::Rat Fink walks down to the ring with his cheesy sci fi looking robot manager:: ::”Mechanical Animals" by Marilyn Manson blares on the loud speakers:: RING ANNOUCER: And finally, from Chicago, IL! Former BAD World Heavyweight Champion, the man, the myth, the legend... MICHAEL MANSON!
::big pop from the crowd as Manson comes out and makes his way down to the ring:: ************************************************** ************************************************** ***************** ULTIMATE JEOPARDY MATCH: Manson & Diablo vs. Rat Fink & Scotty Michaels
************************************************** ************************************************** ***********
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo & Scotty Michaels will start the match. Diablo and Scotty Michaels are circling… THEY HOOK UP! DIABLO TAKES SCOTTY MICHAELS DOWN WITH AN ARMDRAG AND LOCKS IN A REVERSE CHIN LOCK!
VIC WATERS: While Diablo can brawl with the best of them, he is showing the world that he is a world class wrestler.
BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo breaks the hold and delivers a dropkick to the back of the neck of Scotty Michaels! Scotty Michaels goes face down into the mat!
VIC WATERS: A very effective and vicious shot by Diablo.. BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo picks Scotty Michaels off the mat… he nails him with a swinging neckbreaker!
VIC WATERS: You can tell unlike many other young wrestlers that Diablo came into this match with a game plan, he is picking a body part to focus on and strategically working him over.
BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels is grabbing the back of his neck. Diablo grabs him by the hair and tags in Manson.
VIC WATERS: Diablo and Manson are showing good team work, very often when you stick too singles together, they are more obsessed with getting the job done on their own, then they are winning.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson picks Scotty Michaels with gutwrench and drives him down to the mat with a big powerbomb onto the worked over neck of Michaels! Manson motions to the crowd he is going to go for the DDT! Michaels grabs the tights of Manson and prevents him from hitting the move!
VIC WATERS: Very smart move by Michaels, a DDT there could have put his chances of winning the match in serious jeopardy.
BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON GOES FOR A CLOTHESLINE AND MICHAELS DUCKS! MICHAELS NAILS MANSON WITH A FOREARM SHIVER AND MANSON FALLS DOWN!
VIC WATERS: This may be the break that Michaels needs to make the tag to Rat Fink! BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels crawls over to his corner…. AND RAT FINK WALKS AWAY! He is refusing to tag in because he doesn’t want to go one on one with Manson!
VIC WATERS: You don’t know that, maybe Rat Fink just wants Scotty Michaels to do more work in the match before Rat Fink has to come in and carry the load.
BRANDON JACOBS: You aren’t unbelievable. Manson catches up with Michaels and drags him by the tights back into his corner. Rat Fink returns to his corner and sticks out his hand mockingly to Michaels and laughs.
VIC WATERS: They don’t call Rat Fink the great Satan for nothing. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson pushes Michaels into his corner and instructs Diablo to hold him in place! MANSON STARS LAYING INTO MICHAELS WITH BIG CHOPS TO THE CHEST OF MICHAELS!
VIC WATERS: Those chops are loud, I can hear them all the way up here in the booth. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is getting all red… MICHAELS BREAKS FREE FROM DIABLO’S GRASP AND JUMPS ON MANSON WITH A THESZ PRESS! Michaels starts punching away with closed fists as Manson is trying to get Michaels off of him!
VIC WATERS: Michaels is just trying to escape with his life, if he has to cut a few corners, I have no problem with that.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels grabs Manson by the hair and throws Manson into his own corner! What the heck is Michaels doing?
VIC WATERS: I can’t figure it out either… BRANDON JACOBS: Manson tags out to Diablo… AND MICHAELS CHARGES HIS CORNER AND TAGS IN RAT FINK! OOOOOOH, I GET IT!
VIC WATERS: Michaels knew that if Manson was in the ring, Rat Fink wouldn’t tag in. Now Michaels gets a chance to rest and Diablo and Rat Fink will face off. Rat Fink is a master of head games.
BRANDON JACOBS: THE FANS ARE STARTING UP A LOUD “WE WANT MANSON!” CHANT! Diablo is looking over to his corner and teasing that he will tag Manson in…. RAT FINK BLINDSIDES DIABLO WITH A STEINERLINE AND HE FALLS DOWN TO THE MAT! Rat Fink drags Diablo over to his own corner…
VIC WATERS: This is great tag team wrestling at its finest… BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink lifts Diablo up vertically… HE HOLDS DIABLO UP IN THE AIR FOR A GOOD THIRTY SECONDS AND DRIVES DIABLO FACE DOWN INTO THE MAT! Diablo grabs his nose! It looks like he landed on it hard! Diablo tries to get up… RAT FINK KICKS DIABLO IN THE FACE!
VIC WATERS: Take a look at Manson, he is getting steamed with the actions of Rat Fink and he is giving Rat Fink the evil eye.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink looks over at Manson and laughs… WAIT A SECOND, LOOK! LOOK! Diablo is back up and on his feet… AND BOY DOES HE LOOK UPSET! Rat Fink punches Diablo… DIABLO CATCHES HIS FIST! DIABLO WHIPS HIM AROUND… INVERTED DDT ON RAT FINK! Diablo goes for a cover! 1--------------2----------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink got under Diablo’s skin and Diablo is riding a way of pure adrenaline.
BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo picks up Rat Fink and whips him off the ropes…. RAT FINK DUCKS THE CLOTHESLINE! Rat Fink bounces off the ropes… HE JUMPS ON DIABLO WITH A TACKLE AND STARTS WAILING AWAY WITH RIGHTS AND LEFTS TO THE HEAD!
VIC WATERS: Just like that, Rat Fink took the fans back out of this match. They cheered when Diablo made his comeback but they just went dead silent.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink picks Diablo off the mat… HE CLOTHESLINES DIABLO OVER THE TOP ROPE AND GOES OUTSIDE AFTER HIM! Rat Fink is pulling up mats at ringside…. WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES MANSON! Rat Fink sees him and he makes a mad dash to avoid him…
VIC WATERS: RUN RAT FINK RUN! BRANDON JACOB: OH MY! RAT FINK TRIPPED ON THE STAIRS! Rat Fink’s robot goes to see if Rat Fink is okay… Manson is slowly walking over to Rat Fink because he sees him within his sights… RAT FINK PUSHES HIS CARDBOARD ROBOT MASCOT INTO MANSON! Manson grabs the robots and laughs in general direction.
VIC WATERS: What is going on here?! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson grabs a beer from a fan… IT LOOKS LIKE HE IS OFFERING IT TO THE ROBOT! The relieved robot nods his head and goes for the beer in Manson’s hand… WAIT, MANSON POURS THE BIG CUP FULL OF BEER ALL OVER THE CARDBOARD ROBOTO! Manson is reaching into his trunks for something!
VIC WATERS: Oh my god, he is going to sodomize the robot! BRANDON JACOBS: No, no, no… HE IS PULLING OUT A LIGHTER… OH NO, HE ISN”T…. HE IS! HE SETS THE ROBOT ON FIRE!
VIC WATERS: OH MY GOD! BRANDON JACOBS: The robot runs to the back fully ablaze as Manson looks at a now horrified Rat Fink…. RAT FINK ROLLS INTO THE RING…. DIABLO IS UP AND HE IS WAITING FOR HIM INSIDE! DIABLO KICKS HIM IN THE STOMACH…. DDT! He covers! 1----------2----------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: With everything that has been going on, how did he kick out of that?! BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo picks him up and kicks Rat Fink in the stomach again… ANOTHER DDT! Scotty Michaels runs in and kicks Diablo off Rat Fink before the ref can count! Scotty Michaels picks Diablo off the mat and whips him off the ropes… THE REF BUMPED INTO DIABLO AND THE REF TOOK A TUMBLE OUT TO THE FLOOR! Diablo bounces off the ropes… MICHAELS CATCHES HIM WITH A POWERSLAM!
VIC WATERS: Manson & Diablo were close, but Diablo accidentally knocking out the ref will comeback to cost them.
BRANDON JACOBS: Here comes Manson into the ring to save his partner! Michaels picks up Diablo with a gorilla press and throws him into Manson’s arms…. MICHAELS NAILS HIM WITH A DROPKICK! Both men roll to the outside while Scotty grabs Rat Fink and drags him over to his corner… the ref is coming to… RAT FINK TAGS SCOTTY MICHAELS AND IT COUNTS! Manson rolls his partner Diablo back in and Diablo yells at Manson! SCOTTY MICHAELS ROLLS UP DIABLO! 1------------2-----------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Manson had no right to roll his partner before he was ready. BRANDON JACOBS: DIABLO NAILS SCOTTY MICHAELS AND HIS INJURED NECK WITH THE LARIAT! Michaels refuses to go down! He kicks Diablo in the midsection… MICHAELS TRIES TO LIFTS HIM UP FOR A BRAINBUSTER SUPLEX…. HE BLOCKS IT! Diablo whips Michaels around… COBRA CLUTCH LOCKED IN AND HE DRIVES MICHAELS DOWN TO THE MAT WITH A COBRA CLUTCH BOMB! Diablo has the pin!
VIC WATERS: Manson is trying to wake up the ref! The ref comes into the ring to make the count! 1---------------2-------------RAT FINK COMES IN TO BREAK UP THE FALL!
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is irate! He comes into the ring with a chair to nail Rat Fink! Rat Fink tries to escape but Diablo grabs his arms and holds him in place! MANSON SWINGS FOR THE FENCES… RAT FINK USES ALL HIS BODYWEIGHT TO PULL DIABLO DOWN AND MANSON CLOCKED DIABLO RIGHT IN THE HEAD! Diablo is busted open!
VIC WATERS: Diablo looks like he is out cold! BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels clotheslines Manson out of the ring! MICHAELS CLOTHESLINES RAT FINK OUT OF THE RING! Michaels picks up the unconscious Diablo… HE NAILS HIM WITH A BRAINBUSTER SUPLEX! Michaels grabs Diablo’s leg and rolls him up in a small package! THE REF GOES DOWN TO MAKE THE COUNT! 1-----------------2---------------3!! SCOTTY MICHAELS HAS WON THE MATCH FOR HIS TEAM!
RING ANNOUNCER: THE WINNERS OF THE MATCH… THE TEAM OF SCOTTY MICHAELS AND RAT FINK! SINCE SCOTT MICHAELS WON THE MATCH FOR HIS TEAM, AT CHERRY BLOSSOM CHAOS THERE WILL BE A THREE MAN LADDER MATCH BETWEEN RAT FINK, MANSON AND MICHAELS FOR THE TITLE!
BRANDON JACOBS: What an ending to that match…. VIC WATERS: Rat Fink has his title and he is running off through the crowd. It looks like he has escaped Manson yet again unscathed.
BRANDON JACOBS: His time is running out, Manson can’t be happy about what happened here tonight and I’m scared to see what Manson will do to Rat Fink if he was willing to set his robot compadre on fire.
VIC WATERS: They will rebuild him and he will be stronger. BRANDON JACOBS: Wrestling fans, it is time for the main event, our next match will be Doc Silver’s first title defense against a man who seemingly has his number, “The System” Richard Gideon! We will be back with that after this commercial break!
