WFW — Card

Superbowl of Wrestling: Scotty Michaels vs. Shawn Hart

21 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 21 April 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: When one looks back at the last year, the names Shawn Hart and Scotty Michaels are interlocked in the history of the first year of WFW just as George Washington and Paul Revere are an integral part of early American History. The feud started back when both men were respectively World Champion and North American Champion and as pillars of the league looking to out do each other, they became natural rivals. At Merrython when after months they finally met one on one for the World Heavyweight Title, in one of the most controversial nights ever in WFW history, Michaels with the help of Luster ended the reign of the longest reigning WFW World Heavyweight Champion Shawn Hart when Luster disguised as Scotty Michaels paralyzed brother brother came out of his wheelchair and ambused the champion in the middle of Central Park. Tonight, Luster is banned from the building and these two men will have a chance to find out who is the best, one on one.
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart has been off since Christmas. Don’t you think he’ll be a little a rusty?
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart was the longest reigning champion in WFW history. He is the greatest champion we ever had and the WFW came in its own with Shawn Hart on top.
VIC WATERS: He was just lucky to be champion at the right the time. It could have easily been four or five other people.
BRANDON JACOBS: Blasphemy. VIC WATERS: Scotty Michaels could have easily been in Shawn Hart’s place and he probably would have taken WFW to even greater heights. Scotty not only is the Viewer’s Choice, he is the choice of all of Hollywood. He is the star who has broken through and became the mainstream icon. We are just lucky he still says hello to us.
BRANDON JACOBS: He says hello to you? He doesn’t say hello to me. VIC WATERS: You just aren’t special. BRANDON JACOBS: ::hangs head:: Folks, let’s send this one down to ringside and get this underway!
CUEUP: “It Is My Life” by Bon Jovi) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, a former WFW World Heavyweight Champion, a man loved by viewer’s worldwide, from Philadelphia, PA, THE SUPERSTAR SCOTTY MICHAELS!
::Michaels comes down to the loud boos from the crowd:: (CUEUP: "Look At Me" by Geri Haliwell) RING ANNOUNCER: And now, from Orlando, Florida, the former WFW World Heavyweight of the World, “THE PHENOM” SHAWN HART!
::Shawn Hart walks down to the ring to the loud cheers from the crowd and enters the ring::
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: HART CHARGES SCOTTY MICHAELS AND ASSAULTS HIM WITH A FLURRY OF PUNCHES DRIVING HIM BACK!
VIC WATERS: I pity the fool who thought this was going to be a technical match. BRANDON JACOBS: HART CLOTHESLINES MICHAELS OVER THE TOP ROPE TO THE ROAR OF THE CROWD! A nervous Michaels stumbles to the floor and uses to guardrail to help himself up.
VIC WATERS: Hart looks like he is really upset over the Luster deal still. BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart goes through the ropes to the outside AND CRASHES ONTO SCOTTY MICHAELS WITH A DOUBLE AXE HANDLE AS HE TRIES TO GET AWAY AND MICHAELS FALLS DOWN ONTO HIS KNEES!
VIC WATERS: OOOH YEAH! BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart grabs Scotty Michaels…. HE WHIPS HIM RIGHT INTO THE STEEL STAIRS! The ref is giving these two a lot of latitude, he isn’t even counting either one.
VIC WATERS: The ref should be doing his job instead of worrying about upsetting the fans. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is clutching his back! Shawn Hart picks Michaels up…. HE CLOTHESLINES HIM OVER THE STAIRS AND SCOTTY MICHAELS TUMBLES OVER THEM AND LANDS ON THE BACK OF HIS HEAD HARD!
VIC WATERS: Somebody needs to inform Hart that Michaels has to wrestle after Hart leaves again.
BRANDON JACOBS: SHAWN HART TAKES THE CABLE FROM ONE OF THE CAMERAS AND HE STARTS CHOKING OUT MICHAELS WITH IT! Hart may be going a bit too far with this!
VIC WATERS: A bit too far? Where is the damn ref? BRANDON JACOBS: Here comes the ref and the ref is trying to get Shawn Hart to break the hold. The fans are booing the ref without mercy.
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart is letting the ref distract him and Michaels is crawling away…. BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS JUST SLIPPED UNDER THE RING AND SHAWN HART IS GOING AFTER HIM! VIC WATERS: That is ingenious. Most guys wouldn’t be smart enough to do that. BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart grabs the feet of Michaels and pulls Michaels out…. Shawn Hart goes to pick Michaels up…. MICHAELS CLOCKS HART WITH A WRENCH RIGHT IN THE HEAD AND HART IS BUSTED OPEN AND HE FALLS BACK ONTO THE GUARDRAIL!
VIC WATERS: Hart was blocking the refs view so he didn’t see any of that! BRANDON JACOBS: SCOTTY MICHAELS IS RIPPING UP THE MATS AT RINGSIDE! What is he going to do to Hart now?!
VIC WATERS: He isn’t going to do anything to Hart that Hart wouldn’t do to him. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels pulls Hart off of the guardrail…. GERMAN SUPLEX HEAD FIRST ONTO THE CONCRETE! Hart’s head smacked that cold concrete floor!
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart attacked early on with a brawling attack in order to end this early, but it looks like he will be the one to suffer from his own strategy.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is telling both men to get back into the ring! Scotty Michaels smirks at the ref and rolls Hart into the ring.
VIC WATERS: Michaels knows he has Hart where he wants him. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels rolls in… HE MAKES A COVER ON HART! 1-------------------------2--------------------------- HART GETS HIS SHOULDER UP!
VIC WATERS: I probably would have gone for a pinfall there too after all the abuse Hart took, but it is way too early to get a pin fall on a guy like Hart.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is going down to the mat… he locks Hart into the Mexican Surfboard!
VIC WATERS: Look at the blood, it is spilling into the eyes of Hart as he has this locked on and is impairing his vision.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is creaming in pain…. Hart may not be able to kick out of another pinfall and if he can’t get out of this move and fast.
VIC WATERS: When you work on an opponent’s back, you can’t go wrong. BRANDON JACOBS: HART IS STRUGGLING TO BREAK FREE… MICHAELS SHOULDERS ROLL FLAT TO THE MAT AND REF GOES DOWN FOR THE COUNT! 1--------------------------------2-------------------------- MICHAELS BREAKS THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: After the abuse Hart has taken in the last five minutes, I’m shocked he was able to do that.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is wiping the blood out of his eyes… MICHAELS IS UP AND HE STARTS STOMPING ON THE BUSTED OPEN FOREHEAD OF HART!
VIC WATERS: If this match was in Baltimore, Maryland, this match may have been called already.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels picks Hart up off of the mat… MICHAELS WHIPS HART OFF THE ROPES AND CATCHES HIM WITH A LOU THESZ PRESS! Michaels starts uploading with right hands to the head! Those don’t appear to be closed either.
VIC WATERS: The referee is doing a good job letting both men fight this out instead of butting in.
BRANDON JACOBS: Strange, I remember you saying something different earlier. VIC WATERS: Well you are a senile old bat so you would… BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is coming over and he is telling Michaels to open up the hand…. MICHAELS IS NOW SLAPPING HART RIGHT IN THE HEAD WITH SOME VICIOUS SHOTS!
VIC WATERS: Hart is being tenderized. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is going for another pinfall! 1----------------------------------------------2---------------------------- HART MANAGES TO GET THE SHOULDER UP AGAIN!
VIC WATERS: Hart is a fighter, I’ll give him that. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is yelling at the ref for the slow count as he picks Hart off of the mat. MICHAELS IS GOING FOR A WAISTLOCK TO HIT HART WITH A BELLY TO BELLY BUT MICHAELS HANDS ARE SO COVERED WITH BLOOD FROM HART THAT HE CAN’T GET A GRIP ON HART…
VIC WATERS: Blood can be very slippery. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is wiping off his hands on his trunks as a barely conscious Hart is just standing there dazed all slumped over.
VIC WATERS: Boy, wrestling is a civilized sport. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels goes for the waistlock to deliver the belly to belly…. BUT SHAWN HART ELBOWS MICHAELS RIGHT IN THE NOSE BUSTING HIM OPEN!
VIC WATERS: God damn. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is cursing out Hart and both men are trading punches wildly back and forth… HART IS STARTING TO GET THE ADVANTAGE!
VIC WATERS: Hart is going right after that nose! I feel a tornado coming. BRANDON JACOBS: HART HITS MICHAELS WITH THE DISCUS PUNCH AND MICHAELS DOWN LIKE A SHOT! VIC WATERS: DOWN GOES MICHAELS! BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is rolling out of the ring to regroup… MICHAELS HITS THE GUARDRAIL IN FRUSTRATION… .. WAIT A SECOND, HART IS BOUNCING OFF THE ROPES… MICHAELS TURNS AROUND ON THE OUTSIDE AND HART GOES THROUGH THE ROPES AND HITS MICHAELS WITH A TOPE DRIVING HIM OVER THE GUARDRAIL INTO THE AUDIENCE!
VIC WATERS: AIR HART! CROWD: LET’S GO HART! LET’S GO HART! LET’S GO HART! BRANDON JACOBS: This man was once one of the most hated men in all of WFW and now the fans are cheering for him like he is one of their own.
VIC WATERS: He is no Scotty Michaels… BRANDON JACOBS: AN OLD WOMAN IS POUNDING ON SCOTTY MICHAELS WITH HER PURSE AS HE IS TRYING TO GET UP!
VIC WATERS: Grumpy old people… BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is getting up and he is climbing onto the guardrail and telling everyone to get away… Scotty Michaels is getting up…. HART JUMPS OFF THE GUARDRAIL WITH A FLYING FOREARM AND ONTO MICHAELS!
VIC WATERS: Where is Hart getting all this energy after being away since Christmas… BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart has been at home visualizing getting revenge on Michaels ever since he ruined his Christmas. Tonight is going to be night he gets it, win or lose. We are already twenty minutes into this match and Hart looks like he could go another fourty.
VIC WATERS: We will see. BRANDON JACOBS: Hart picks Michaels AND HE RAMS HIM BACK FIRST INTO THE GUARDRAIL! That is payback for the Mexican Surfboard Michaels applied earlier in the match.
VIC WATERS: The difference is, one move was done in a wrestling ring, one move was done into pure steel. I don’t know how the fans can cheer this man.
BRANDON JACOBS: HART IS TAKING THE PURSE OF THE OLD WOMAN…. HART CHARGES MICHAELS AND CLOCKS HIM RIGHT IN THE HEAD WITH IT AND MICHAELS GOES UP AND OVER THE GUARDRAIL BACK INTO THE RINGSIDE AREA!
VIC WATERS: He could have knocked out his teeth if that purse was a little lower. Is this match under BAD Rules and nobody told me about it?
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is just letting these two men go. Hart opens up the purse of the old lady, takes out a piece of bubble gum and returns the purse.
VIC WATERS: Why would Hart be chewing gum at a time like this? BRANDON JACOBS: Heavens if I know, but apparently you can kick ass and chew gum at the same time.
VIC WATERS: He could choke on it. Wait a second, let him eat it, nevermind. BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is going over the guardrail and he picks up Scotty Michaels… HART IS BLOWING A BUBBLE AND HE TAKES IT OUT OF HIS MOUTH AND POPS IT ON SCOTTY MICHAELS FACE!
VIC WATERS: The salt in the gum is going to ruin it. He just wasted a perfectly good piece of bubble gum.
BRANDON JACOBS: I don’t think he’ll have an urge to eat it after it has been in Scotty Michaels bloody face… MICHAELS IS TRYING TO GET THE GUM OFF OF HIS FACE BUT IT IS STICKING NEAR HIS EYES LIKE IT WAS APPLIED WITH CRAZY GLUE!
VIC WATERS: I’ve never even though of using gum as a weapon. BRANDON JACOBS: Hart backs up and heads to the guardrail in the corner…. HE CHARGES MICHAELS BUT MICHAELS DUCKS DOWN AND BACK BODY DROPS HIM DOWN TO THE GROUND!
VIC WATERS: Great instincts by Michaels. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels finally takes the bubble gum off of his face and with an irritate look he starts stomping on Michaels before he has a chance to get as Hart is trying to crawl away.
VIC WATERS: GET HIM! BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS TACKLES HART ON THE GROUND AND THEY BEGIN TO WRESTLE AROUND FOR POSITION! Michaels and Hart are trying to choke each other!
VIC WATERS: This is barely resembling a wrestling match… BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels winds up on Hart… AND HE RAMS THE SIDE OF HART’S HEAD INTO THE STEEL STAIRS! This is getting out of control.
VIC WATERS: Finish him Scotty! BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is finally coming out to make sure these two men don’t go too far and the ref is trying to pull Michaels off Hart… MICHAELS PUSHES THE REF BACK BUT ACCIDENTALLY PUSHES HIM DOWN!
VIC WATERS: The ref can’t just come down and jump on people. Referees aren’t suppose to get physically involved.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels gets up and yells at the referee for getting in the way… SHAWN HART LOW BLOWS MICHAELS FROM BEHIND AND MICHAELS FALLS TO HIS KNEES!
VIC WATERS: Now I saw the ref saw that and there was absolutely no call. BRANDON JACOBS: Never get on the bad side of a referee…. SHAWN HART GRABS MICHAELS BY THE TIGHTS AND SLINGS HIM SHOULDER FIRST INTO THE STEEL POST AND MICHAELS GRABS HIS SHOULDER!
VIC WATERS: How many times have we seen wrestlers injure their shoulders and ultimately lose the match? When you can’t move you arm, the match is as good as lost.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart rolls Scotty Michaels into the ring and then rolls the ref after him.
VIC WATERS: Hart is rolling the dead bodies back in. BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart rolls back in and goes right back to work on Michaels. HART PICKS UP MICHAELS AND HITS HIM WITH A SHOULDERBREAKER!
VIC WATERS: The ref is still semi-out though so he can’t go for a pin fall attempt. BRANDON JACOBS: I don’t think that is what Michaels has in mind anyways… HART PICKS UP MICHAELS AND HE SCISSOR KICKS MICHAELS RIGHT ON HIS INJURED SHOULDER DRIVING HIM BACK DOWN ONTO HIS KNEES!
VIC WATERS: Michaels does not look good. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is grabbing his shoulder and it looks like he is wincing in pain…. HART GRABS HIS ARM AND LOCKS ON A FUJIWARA ARMBAR!
VIC WATERS: Hart is pulling on the arm which is attached onto the injured shoulder in attempt to do even more damage.
BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS IS SCREAMING BUT THE REFEREE IS STILL OUT OF IT! VIC WATERS: Someone needs to help out Michaels. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster is gone, Michaels will have to help himself if he wants to get out of this and win the match.
VIC WATERS: I believe in Michaels, but in a hell of a predicament. BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is starting to come through and he is asking Michaels if he wants to give up. You can tell that he does but he keeps on shaking his head no repeatedly.
VIC WATERS: Come on Scotty! BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS IS TRYING TO USE HIS OTHER FREE HAND TO MOVE HIS BODY CLOSER TO THE ROPES! This is going to be tough.
VIC WATERS: Michaels leg is just a half a foot away of breaking the plane of the bottom rope and getting a ref break.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is pulling back on the arm with all the strength he has, you can see it in his face that he is putting all he has into this. MICHAELS LOOKS LIKE HE IS ABOUT TO PASS OUT!
VIC WATERS: Scotty can’t lose like this. Scotty Michaels is a man of endurance who has gone over an hour on several occasions. We are only thirty minutes in this, if he can just reach back into his reserve and last a little bit longer, all will be right with the world.
BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS MAKES ONE MORE ATTEMPT TO MOVE HIMSELF TOWARDS THE ROPES…. THE TIP OF HIS TOE BREAKS THE PLANE OF THE BOTTOM ROPE AND THE REF IS CALLING FOR A BREAK!
VIC WATERS: Thank god… BRANDON JACOBS: The damage has been done though and if Michaels is going to survive, he is going to have to work around his injured shoulder.
VIC WATERS: Michaels is smart enough to do it. BRANDON JACOBS: Hart breaks the hold and the ref is asking for Hart to give him a little breathing room so he can get up….. HART CHARGES MICHAELS AND HE KICKS HIM IN THE FACE!
VIC WATERS: I sure hope that happens to Hart later in the match because what is good for the goose is good for the gander.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels bloody splattered on the mat after that brutal shot. Hart picks up bloody Michaels off of the mat…. HART IS CALLING FOR THE EL CODO EXPLOSIVO!
VIC WATERS: If he hits this on Hart, it is all over! BRANDON JACOBS: HART BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND CHARGES MICHAELS WITH THE EL CODO EXPLOSIVO BUT SOMEHOW MICHAELS MANAGES TO DUCK!
VIC WATERS: Michaels is tapping into his backup tank now. BRANDON JACOBS: Hart turns around… MICHAELS KICKS HIM IN THE MIDSECTION AND HITS HIM WITH THE DIAMOND CUTTER OUT OF NO WHERE!
VIC WATERS: Michaels has life again! BRANDON JACOBS: Both men are down and the referee is making the count on both men! ONE!........ TWO! …….THREE!....... FOUR! ……….FIVE! ………….SIX! …..SEVEN! Michaels is starting to get back up and he breaks the count.
VIC WATERS: Both men have worked so hard to preserve, one man has to walk out of here a winner if there is any justice in this world.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels picks up Hart from behind with a waistlock…. GERMAN SUPLEX BY MICHAELS ONTO HART AND HART CRASHES DOWN ON THE BACK OF HIS HEAD!
VIC WATERS: Hart has lost an awful lot of blood. One has to wonder how many shots like that he can take before he is unable to go anymore.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart has the heart of a lion. As long as there is life in his body, he will continue to fight. He isn’t just fighting for revenge, he is fighting to preserve his legacy here tonight by beating the man who beat him.
VIC WATERS: Legacy, smegacy. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels drags Hart into the corner in agony and he looks over towards the top rope.
VIC WATERS: With the way his shoulder is, there is no way he is going to be able to setup the Heartbreaker with the brainbuster suplex.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is just going to have to get it done without if he wants to win this match. MICHAELS IS GOING TO THE CORNER AND HE CLIMBING THE TURNBUCKLE!
VIC WATERS: Come on! BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart in agony rolls farther away from Michaels and a frustrated Michaels climbs down and goes after Hart!
VIC WATERS: He has to do something to plant Hart if he wants to beat him at further risk to himself if he wants to win this match. It look inevitable.
BRANDON JACOBS: SCOTTY MICHAELS PUTS HART IN A FACE LOCK WITH HIS LEFT ARM AND HITS HIM WITH A DDT!
VIC WATERS: Maybe I spoke too soon. BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels bounces off the ropes… HE DROPS THE LEG RIGHT ON THE HEAD OF HART!
VIC WATERS: That should be enough to do it by the grace of god. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels drags Hart over towards the corner and for the second time he is headed to the top turnbuckle.
VIC WATERS: Nobody has ever kicked out of the The Heartbreaker. If he hits this, it will be automatic.
BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS IS PERCHED ON THE TOP ROPE…. MICHAELS FLIES OFF THE TOP WITH THE SOMERSAULT LEGDROP… HART MOVES OUT OF THE WAY BUT STILL GETS GRAZED BY THE LEG BY THE LEG!
VIC WATERS: Hart has a cut across his face from the boot. He doesn’t look so pretty now. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels goes for the cover! 1-------------------------------------2------------------------------------ HART MAKES THE KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: If Michaels had hit that all, Hart wouldn’t have a chance. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels picks up Hart….HE IS CALLING FOR THE BRAINBUSTER BAD SHOULDER AND ALL!
VIC WATERS: He has to do it, it is the only way. Hart is fourty minuts into the match and despite being off since Christmas, he won’t stay down and die.
BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS ATTEMPTS TO LIFT HART IN THE AIR BUT HART IS FIGHTING IT! VIC WATERS: GET HIM UP! BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS LIFTS HART UP IN THE AIR BUT HE SHOULDER GIVES AWAY AND HART CRASHES DOWN BEHIND HIM ON HIS FEET!
VIC WATERS: NO DAMNIT! BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart bounces off the ropes… MICHAELS TURNS AROUND AND HE GETS HIT WITH THE EL CODO EXPLOSIVO! BOTH MEN ARE DOWN!
VIC WATERS: GET UP SCOTTY! BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is making the ten count on both men! ONE!! ……… TWO!........ THREE!....... FOUR!....... FIVE!....... SIX!...... HART IS TOO EXHAUSTED TO GET UP!..... SEVEN!....... EIGHT!..... SCOTTY MICHAELS IS TRYING TO GET UP…..NINE!........ MICHAELS FALLS DOWN ON THE MAT!..... TEN! THE REFEREE IS CALLING FOR BELL!
RING ANNOUNCER: THE REFEREE HAS DECLARED THIS MATCH A DOUBLE KNOCKOUT! BRANDON JACOBS: The paramedics are rushing down to attend to both men who have been bloodied and bruised in one of the most physical forty minute encounters I have ever seen.
VIC WATERS: They gave each other their all and it still wasn’t enough. BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels is being helped up by the paramedics and he is shrugging them off…. MICHAELS IS GOING OVER TO ATTACK HART AGAIN! Michaels is picking a tired and bloody Hart off of the mat….
VIC WATERS: Finish him off for good Scotty! BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, SCOTTY IS EXTENDING HIS HAND TO HART….. after all Michaels has done to Hart, how can he offer his hand expecting Hart to shake it? Hart is a good man, but he isn’t Jesus.
::Hart extends his hand to Scotty Michaels and shakes his hand out of respect:: VIC WATERS: Holy cow, he is Jesus. BRANDON JACOBS: This crowd is cheering the effort of both men here tonight. They put on a hell of a show and we still have six matches left. God help me, my voice is going to go out before the night is through.
VIC WATERS: But that is a good thing. BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is raising the hands of both men. What a moment this is…. Folks, when we comeback, it is time for the BAD World Heavyweight Title Falls Count Anywhere Tournament featuring some of our most evil at their finest. STAY TUNED!
