WFW Vox Part Deux: Manson/Rat Fink vs. Wylde/Pulsar

30 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 30 March 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: Tonight will be a historic night because the two men who will meet in the Superbowl of Wrestling will meet here in Revere for the very time in WFW. This will give both men a chance to feel each other out and get a better sense for what they are in for when the money is really on the line.
VIC WATERS: I’ve heard rumors that there may be a special stipulation for their match on the anniversary show but I don’t want to give anything away.
BRANDON JACOBS: That is great, just tease the fans watching at home. VIC WATERS: I try to do my best to torture the good fans of the WFW as much as possible. BRANDON JACOBS: So, what is the stipulation that they are going to add to the match? VIC WATERS: I’ve been sworn to secrecy. But I will say this, it is a stipulation that Wylde himself campaigned for that Doc Silver himself would have asked for if he was main eventing against Manson this year.
BRANDON JACOBS: OH! That could steal the show if it happens. By the way, why isn’t Doc Silver going to be on the anniversary show?
VIC WATERS: Doc wanted to be on the show, but he had prior commitments as an executive producer of that hit show FW Survivor which has been putting up all the ratings.
BRANDON JACOBS: For the record, it is a shame he can’t be a part of the show. Doc was a huge part of this company in its infancy and love him or hate him, what he did for the league early on helped lead to the success we have today.
VIC WATERS: Doc is God. What can I say? BRANDON JACOBS: If it wasn’t for him, you wouldn’t be here today. VIC WATERS: What can I say, Doc recognizes talent. BRANDON JACOBS: That and the fact Sirius ran down Doc way too much for his tastes. VIC WATERS: Superstars have to be treated a certain way. Limos, five star catering, and unlimited Poland Spring water ::takes a swig::
BRANDON JACOBS: Do you really need to be Poland Springing it up this early in the show? We still have two important matches to call and I don’t need you to be in the bag.
VIC WATERS: This is going to be a tag team street fight, nothing goes better with a little violence than some sweet, sweet Poland Spring Water.
BRANDON JACOBS: I hope you have another liver in the freezer. VIC WATERS: Well if you don’t be quiet, I may just have one by the end of the night. BRANDON JACOBS: I may need this liver if I work with you long enough. Rat Fink and Pulsar sort of get lost in the shuffle with the story between Manson and Wylde, but it is quite interesting when you think that Manson and Rat Fink feuded nearly a year ago now and they are teaming up and young Pulsar has come so far that Wylde choose him as his partner tonight because he felt he could hang with him in a war with Manson and Rat Fink.
VIC WATERS: Pulsar has a lot of raw energy and aggression. He is the kind of guy who won’t let you down in a fight. It just goes to show you how smart the Donald Trump of Professional Wrestling, Alex Wylde, is inside and out of the ring.
BRANDON JACOBS: I’ve got nothing but respect for Pulsar. One of these days he is going to break out in a big way and when he does, heaven help anyone who crosses his path. We have a lot of veteran guys here in WFW so when I see a guy like Pulsar rising in the ranks, it puts a smile on my face.
VIC WATERS: There are guys who were headlining when I first started that are still headlining in other leagues and that was a long time ago.
BRANDON JACOBS: That is why guys Pulsar come here to grab the brass ring. They know they are going to be given the opportunities to prove themselves if they work hard and show fighting spirit in the ring.
VIC WATERS: Winning helps too. BRANDON JACOBS: That is true. Pulsar was really looking forward to this match back when he thought it was a six man because Pulsar has an undefeated streak in six man tags going…. But the match has been changed and these four men are going to make up for it with a war of all wars. I’m excited to see this one myself. Folks, let’s send this one down to ringside and get this match underway!
(CUEUP: “Whiskey in a Jar” by Metallica) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from Australia, standing at over 6’3, PULSAR! ::Pulsar receives a huge roar from the crowd as he comes down to the aisle and enters the ring::
(CUEUP: “Megalomaniac” by KMFDM) RING ANNOUNCER: The first of the opposing team, the leader of the New Age Revolution and the former BAD World Heavyweight Champion, RAT FINK!
::Rat Fink walks down to the ring with the Satan’s Clause and Paul Reverebot and enters the ring:: :
CUEUP: "Mechanical Animals" by Marilyn Manson) RING ANNOUNCER: And his partner, the former WFW North American Champion, the man, the myth, the legend…. MICHAEL MANSON!
::Manson is walking down slowly to the ring:: (CUEUP: "Diesel Power" by Prodigy) RING ANNOUNCER: Last but not least, the Donald Trump of the WFW, the current WFW World Heavyweight Champion and Current WFW President ,ALEX WYLDE!
::Alex Wylde walks down to the cheers of the crowd and enters the ring:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Manson and Rat Fink attack and the war is on! VIC WATERS: This a tornado rules tag team street fight so there is no tagging in and out. This is just an all out war and both of these men are former BAD World Heavyweight so they certainly know how to brawl.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson and Wylde are fighting it out, Pulsar and Rat Fink are exchanging blows back and forth!
VIC WATERS: Look at the fire in Manson’s eyes. That is the same fire he had at the So Rumble, he sees the WFW World Heavyweight Title in his grasp and he is going to go all out to get it.
BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON BACKS HIM INTO THE CORNER AND RIPS INTO HIM WITH ALTERNATING CHOPS AND KICKS!
VIC WATERS: Wylde is going to wake up with welts tomorrow. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is taking it to Rat Fink! PULSAR KICKS RAT FINK IN THE GUT AND HITS HIM WITH AN INVERTED SUPLEX!
VIC WATERS: Manson better keep an eye on Pulsar because he is the kind of guy who can pin someone in a flash once he gets on a roll.
BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON GRABS THE HAIR OF WYLDE AND REPEATEDLY HEAD BUTTS WYLDE IN THE HEAD IN AN ATTEMPT TO BREAK HIM OPEN!
VIC WATERS: I think Wylde may be better off being broken up at this point but Manson hasn’t been able to make any progress.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson picks up Wylde and he bodyslams him down to the mat! MANSON IS GOING TO THE TURNBUCKLE!
VIC WATERS: Look at Pulsar, he driving that shin right into the throat of Rat Fink so he can’t get up while he pounds on him with closed fists.
BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON IS PERCHED AT THE TOP…. HE FLIES OFF AND NAILS THE FLYING HEADBUTT AND WYLDE IS BUSTED OPEN! Manson goes for the cover! 1----------------------------------------------2--------------------------------------- PULSAR PUSHES MANSON OFF OF WYLDE!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar saw what was happening and he busted his ass to get up and help his partner.
BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR IS PUNCHING AWAY ON MANSON AND MANSON FIRES BACK WITH CHOPS OF HIS OWN! Neither man is budging!
VIC WATERS: These two men are warriors. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is challenging Pulsar to hit him with his shoulder block…. PULSAR BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND HITS MANSON WITH A RUNNING SHOULDER BLOCK! Manson doesn’t budge! Pulsar is challenging Manson…. MANSON BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND KICKS PULSAR IN THE GROIN TO THE ROAR OF THE CROWD AND PULSAR DROPS DOWN ON HIS KNEES!
VIC WATERS: Manson isn’t going to be involved in any of this immovable object battles. He just wants to beat his opponent down and win the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson picks up Pulsar…. KICK TO THE GUT….. SWEET DREAMS STUNNER! Manson makes the cover! A bloody Wylde breaks up the pinfall before the ref can even start counting!
VIC WATERS: Wylde isn’t going to let Manson win this match this easily. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink gets up and picks Wylde off of Manson…. RAT FINK HOLDS THE ARMS OF WYLDE AND MANSON STARTS CHOPPING AWAY AT WYLDE!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink is still bitter after that loss to Wylde. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink wants that WFW World Heavyweight Title and he’ll do anything, including teaming with Manson to do it. Rat Fink is calling over instructions to Manson… Rat Fink lets go of Wylde and both MANSON AND RAT FINK LIFT WYLDE UP FOR A STALL SUPLEX!
VIC WATERS: All the blood is rushing right to Wylde’s head. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON AND RAT FINK DROP HIM DOWN TO THE MAT! MANSON GOES DOWN FOR THE COVER ON WYLDE! 1------------------------------------ RAT FINK PULLS MANSON OFF AND GOES FOR THE COVER ON HIS OWN TO THE DISGUST OF MANSON! 1--------------------------------------------2--------------------- PULSAR DIVES AND KNOCKS RAT FINK OFF!
VIC WATERS: Manson could have stopped Pulsar right there but he was still upset with Rat Fink. If these guys want to win, they need to work as a team.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink picks up Pulsar…. RAT FINK STEINERLINES HIM DOWN TO THE MAT! Rat Fink mounts Pulsar and starts punching away with closed punches!
VIC WATERS: And those are legal too since this is a street fight… BRANDON JACOBS: Manson goes back to work on Wylde and he is stomping him in his busted open head!
VIC WATERS: If Manson could get the blood flowing a bit more, he could compromise Wylde’s vision.
BRANDON JACOBS: WYLDE CATCHES THE FOOT OF MANSON AND TAKES MANSON DOWN WITH A DRAGON SCREW!
VIC WATERS: Wylde is using his brain here and that is what he needs to do to win this match. Manson takes an incredible amount of punishment and keeps on coming back for more. If he is going to beat him, he needs to outsmart him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde stands up and he starts stomping away on the inner leg of Manson… WYLDE GRABS THE LEGS…. AND HE TURNS OVER MANSON FOR A SHARPSHOOTER!
VIC WATERS: Manson very rarely submits, but if Wylde plays his cards right, he could weaken the legs down to the point where it would be easier to pin him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson looks like he is in a hell of an amount of pain. Manson is trying to reach for the ropes but the ref isn’t going to order an automatic break
VIC WATERS: Manson’s best hope would be for him to scream to Rat Fink for help but both these men are two stubborn to do that.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink picks up the battered Pulsar off the mat… FALCON’S ARROW RIGHT INTO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink has him right where he wants him! BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink looks over at the exposed Wylde who has Manson in the sharpshooter….. RAT FINK KICKS WYLDE RIGHT IN THE FACE AND WYLDE BREAKS THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: Wylde may need to invest in another set after this match is over the way he is going.
BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK WALKS OVER THE CORNER AND HE STARTS TO UNDO THE TURNBUCKLE! VIC WATERS: Rat Fink is making a dangerous match even more dangerous by doing this. BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK PULLS OFF THE TURNBUCKLE! Rat Fink grabs Wylde by the hair…. RAT FINK RAMS WYLDE’S BLOODY FACE RIGHT INTO THE EXPOSED TURNBUCKLE! Wylde collapses in the corner.
VIC WATERS: Wylde is ripe for the pickings here. BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK BACKS UP TO THE OTHER SIDE OF THE RING…. RAT FINK CHARGES WYLDE WITH RUNNING SHOULDER BLOCK BUT WYLDE MOVES OUT AND RAT FINK GOES CRASHING INTO THE CORNER SHOULDER FIRST!
VIC WATERS: This is Wylde and Pulsar’s chance to capitalize if they want to win this match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar and Manson are going at it. Pulsar is all beaten up and Manson’s legs are a bit wobbly. MANSON KICKS PULSAR IN THE MIDSECTION!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar would have trouble standing toe to toe with Manson without having taken the beaten that Rat Fink dished out to him earlier let alone now when he is still in a great deal of pain.
BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON SNAP SUPLEXES PULSAR DOWN TO THE MAT! Pulsar clutches the back of his neck… MANSON GOES DOWN TO THE MAT AND LOCKS ON THE TOURNIQUET AND PULSAR IS SCREAMING IN PAIN!
VIC WATERS: This could be it right here! Once Manson locks this on, the match is over because there is no escape from this move!
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is a tough kid. He doesn’t give up easily. Pulsar looks like he is on the verge of passing out from the pain.
VIC WATERS: Wylde is still down from the kick to the face but he is slowing signs of stirring.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is screaming out to Wylde in pain for help and Wylde is slowly getting up…. PULSAR LOOKS LIKE HE JUST GAVE UP STRUGGLING!
VIC WATERS: The ref is going down to check to see if Pulsar is out or not, this could be over already.
BRANDON JACOBS: WYLDE DIVES ON THE REF BEFORE HE CAN CHECK! Wylde starts kicking Manson repeatedly in the head and he finally breaks the hold on Pulsar!
VIC WATERS: I’m not so sure Pulsar didn’t give up, but referee just didn’t see it. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is getting up….. WYLDE HITS HIM WITH ANOTHER SPINNING HEEL KICK… BUT MANSON DOES NOT GO DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Manson looks like he is upset over Wylde breaking up the Tourniquet. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde and Manson are exchanging chops back and forth! WYLDE IS BACKING UP MANSON!
VIC WATERS: Wylde is really ripping into him. BRANDON JACOBS: WYLDE HITS MANSON WITH ANOTHER SPINNING HEEL KICK AND THIS TIME MANSON FALLS DOWN TO ONE KNEE!
VIC WATERS: My god, he almost took his head off with that one. BRANDON JACOBS: WYLDE DROPS MANSON HEAD FIRST INTO THE MAT! VIC WATERS: Now that is a move that could paralyze you. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde hooks the leg and makes the cover! 1-----------------------------------------------2----------------------------- MANSON KICKS OUT JUST IN THE NICK OF TIME!
VIC WATERS: Manson is showing his toughness here. Lesser men would have taken the pinfall and just gotten this match over with in order not to take too much abuse before their big title match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde bounces off the ropes… SEATED DROPKICK RIGHT INTO THE FACE OF MANSON!
VIC WATERS: He took both boots right into the face. Manson isn’t going to look pretty for his publicity photos.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde goes for the cover again! 1--------------------------------------------------------2--------------------------- RAT FINK DIVES OVER AND MAKES THE COVER!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink came out of no where with that save! BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde assails Rat Fink with a series of European Uppercuts right into the face!
VIC WATERS: European uppercuts really pack a wallop even more than a regular punch because it has a greater surface area than a punch but can do more frequently than a clothesline without damaging your arm.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde whips Rat Fink off the ropes…. STUN GUN RIGHT ON THE ROPES! Rat Fink lands throat first right onto the top rope!
VIC WATERS: It doesn’t matter how big you are or how much you work out, that throat is a weak point for every man.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is beginning to get up…. WYLDE BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES… WYLDE GOES FOR A FLYING DROPKICK BUT MANSON MOVES OUT OF THE WAY AND WYLDE CRASHES DOWN ON THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Wylde look like he landed hard on his him on that. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson picks up Wylde and whips him off the ropes…. HURRICANRANA! A dazed Wylde goes crashing down on the mat.
VIC WATERS: You wouldn’t think a wrestler like Manson would use a hurricanrana but that is the great thing about Manson, he is unpredictable and can strike his opponent in so many ways.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde is slowly getting up and Manson is waiting to take advantage with the Sweet Dreams… WAIT A SECOND, RAT FINK IS CALLING FOR MANSON TO GET OUT OF THE WAY!
VIC WATERS: What is Rat Fink going to do now? BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON MOVES OUT OF THE WAY AND RAT FINK CHARGES WYLDE WITH A RUNNING SHOULDER TACKLE AND WYLDE FALLS THROUGH THE ROPES TO THE FLOOR! Ouch, it look like he hit his head hard on the guardrail!
VIC WATERS: That is the last thing Wylde needs right now this close to the Superbowl of Wrestling.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink goes to the outside to work over Wylde while Manson stays in the ring to work over Pulsar but wait a second, here comes MIKE MCGEE AND MIGHTY IMPALA TO PROTECT WYLDE!
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is looking on at McGee and Mighty Impala and is telling them that if they get involved in this match, he will have their hide. WAIT A SECOND, PULSAR IS SNEAKING UP FROM BEHIND PICKS UP MANSON AND PUTS HIM IN AN AIRPLANE SPIN!
VIC WATERS: Now this is old school! BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink curses to himself and he rolls back in to help his partner. VIC WATERS: I think Rat Fink wanted to see if he could take Wylde’s goons all by himself. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink charges from the corner and Pulsar dumps Manson…. PULSAR PICKS UP RAT FINK IN THE AIRPLANE SPIN NOW TO THE OVATION FROM THE CROWD!
VIC WATERS: This move screws with your equilibrium badly and can leave you disoriented for awhile.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar puts Rat Fink down and he is trying to regain his balance…. RAT FINK SWINGS WILDLY AT PULSAR BUT MISSES BADLY…. PULSAR CHARGES AND CLOTHESLINES HIM OVER THE TOP ROPE!
VIC WATERS: Manson is up and he waiting for Pulsar to turn around… BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON DELIVERS A COUPLE OF MONGOLIAN CHOPS RIGHT TO THE NECK! VIC WATERS: Baba eat your heart out. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is starting to stagger… MANSON GRABS THE ARM OF PULSAR AND SHORT ARM CLOTHESLINES HIM DOWN TO THE MAT! He nearly took his head off with that!
VIC WATERS: Manson is systematically working over the neck to soften him up for the tourniquet. With Wylde laying on the outside after that shot to the head, this is the time to strike and go for the kill!
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson lifts up Pulsar…. WILD BUCK BOMB! Pulsar landed hard on its head. Manson makes the cover! 1-------------------------------------------2-------------------------------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar is showing his toughness by kicking out of that. Lesser men would have been pinned because Pulsar’s head really smacked the canvas.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is measuring Pulsar and he is waiting for Pulsar to get up so he can hit with a lariat.
VIC WATERS: Wait a second, look at Wylde, he is finally getting up from that running shoulder block from Rat Fink where he took that awkward spill out of the ring and he is waiting behind Manson.
BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON DOESN’T SEE IT! Manson bounces off the ropes…. GATECRASHER TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD OF MANSON! Manson stumbles forward… PULSAR CATCHES MANSON MID STUMBLE….. THE BLACK HOLE!
VIC WATERS: Manson down! BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar makes the cover! 1--------------------------------------------------------2--------------------------------------------------3! What a win for Pulsar!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. THE TEAM OF ALEX WYLDE AND PULSAR! BRANDON JACOBS: This could be the win that launches Pulsar’s career. Only a handful of guys can say they ever beat Manson and tonight Pulsar becomes one of them even if it was from a blatant double team between Wylde and Pulsar in order to take Manson out.
VIC WATERS: Manson is Manson, you have to do what you have to do to beat him. BRANDON JACOBS: Let me tell you, I can’t wait to see Wylde and Manson go one on one at the Superbowl of Wrestling. Now both men have a better feel for each other and I expect both men will develop a hell of a game plan to walk out of the main event victorious.
VIC WATERS: Will Manson fail to ascend to the top and capture the crown that has eluded him for over a year or will the most powerful man in all of wrestling, the WFW President and World Heavyweight Champion Alex Wylde, fall from grace? Personally, I hope Wylde wins and keeps the spirit of Doc Silver alive and well.
BRANDON JACOBS: We will see. Folks, when we comeback, we will announce the lineup for the Superbowl of Wrestling and we will get the War Games to end all War Games underway! STAY TUNED!
