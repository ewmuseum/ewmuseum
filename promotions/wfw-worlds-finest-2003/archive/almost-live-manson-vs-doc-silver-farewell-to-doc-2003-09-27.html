Almost Live: Manson vs. Doc Silver - Farewell to Doc?

27 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 27 September 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Vic, do you have any idea what you’ve done? You’ve signally thrown this organization into chaos by opening up your trap. God damn it, wasn’t Manson vs. Silver good enough for you without opening up this damn Pandora’s Box?
VIC WATERS: Doc can’t lose. He is sure money. Has Manson ever beaten Doc? BRANDON JACOBS: No. VIC WATERS: Why would he win tonight then? Doc owns Manson! Think about it…. Just because Doc suffered a few injuries against Psycho? BIG DEAL!
BRANDON JACOBS: There is also word going around that he is suffering from a sore throat. VIC WATERS: He is no Manny Rameriez or Pedro Martinez, he is a clutch performer. He isn’t going to take a night off because of a little booboo.
BRANDON JACOBS: Considering what you have wagered on this Vic, you need to stop talking. You are going to jinx Doc Silver and yourself. God’s doesn’t like pride.
VIC WATERS: Doc Silver is God, he makes his own destiny. I don’t have to worry about shutting my mouth.
BRANDON JACOBS: I can’t believe you just said that. VIC WATERS: He is a two time World’s Finest Wrestling Champion damnit. Show some respect. BRANDON JACOBS: I never said I didn’t respect Doc, not once. VIC WATERS: With a click on my fingers, once Doc beats Manson, I can have you replaced. Remember that Brandon, I have connections you know. Anyone would die to be the play by play man for WFW.
BRANDON JACOBS: Okay, okay, the people don’t want to hear us fight. Let’s send this one to ringside for the long awaited match between Manson and Doc Silver!
(CUEUP: “I’m Only Happy When It Rains” by Garbage) ::Vic Waters stands up and gives Doc a standing ovation as he comes down to ringside and enters the ring::
(CUEUP: “Mechanical Animals” by Marilyn Manson) ::Manson comes out to a huge pop from crowd sensing this may be the night Manson finally beats Doc::
BRANDON JACOBS: DOC SILVER RUSHES MANSON AS HE IS COMING THROUGH THE ROPES! Manson crashes down to the mat! Doc Silver picks up Manson and begins ramming his head into the turnbuckle!
VIC WATERS: GET HIM DOC! BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver irish whips Manson to the opposite corner… DOC SILVER COMES IN WITH A BIG SPLASH! Manson is wobbling in the corner… Doc Silver unloads with rights and keeps Manson trapped in the corner!
VIC WATERS: POUND HIM! BRANDON JACOBS: Doc puts Manson on the top rope and he begins to climb up…. Looks like he is going for a superplex… THIS IS HIGH RISK RIGHT HERE!
VIC WATERS: Come on… BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON IS BEGINNING TO FIGHT BACK! Silver is rocking back and forth! Manson climbs down and walks the second rope in order to set Silver on the top! DOC SILVER AND MANSON AND TRADING BLOWS BACK AND FORTH! Either man could easily fall down to the ground below…. This is too dangerous considering all that is at stake.
VIC WATERS: That bastard Shawn Hart, I bet he is sitting back there is smiling at all this watching these two beat the crap out of each other.
BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON GAINS THE ADVANTAGE WITH A COUPLE OF JABS…. MANSON HEAD SCISSORS DOC SILVER’S HEAD AND NAILS HIM WITH A TOP ROPE HURRICANRANA AND DOC CRASHES DOWN TO THE GROUND! Good lord!
VIC WATERS: Oh man. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson goes for the cover! 1--------------------------2---------------KICKOUT! Very close there, that fall seems like it took a lot out of Doc.
VIC WATERS: It was the cage match he had against Psycho. He still isn’t full healed yet. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson picks up Doc… BODYSLAM! Manson calls out Psycho’s name and is headed up the ropes to the top….. FLYING HEADBUTT... SILVER WAS NAILED FLUSHED IN THE HEAD!! Silver retracts and he brushes his hand over his busted open head!
VIC WATERS: Manson has gotten first blood, but last blood is all that counts. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson picks Silver up by the hair…. DDT RIGHT INTO THE MAT! That DDT left a big blood stain on the ring mat! Manson goes for the cover! 1---------------------2-------------------KICKOUT! Silver again gets the shoulder up!
VIC WATERS: The refs should call this a no contest because of all the blood. Doc could pass easily pass out at this rate.
BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver is trying to crawl away from the Manson, the blood is coming down his face, he is just trying to escape Manson’s wrath right now.
VIC WATERS: Doc is a master strategy. He is just buying time until he regains his momentum.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson grabs Doc Silver by the hair…. LOW BLOW! REF DIDN’T SEE IT! DOC SMALL PACKAGES MANSON! 1-----------------------------2--------------------------KICKOUT! HE BARELY GOT OUT OF THAT!
VIC WATERS: Man, Manson looks ten times more angry than before the nearfall. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson calls out Shawn Hart’s name… MANSON WHIPS DOC OFF THE ROPES…. MANSON GOES FOR THE EL CODO EXPLOSIVO… DOC DUCKS AND BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES…. KICK TO THE MIDSECTION! Manson is doubled over in pain… OH NO…
VIC WATERS: MANSON GOES DOWN! ACES FULL! ACES FULL! ACES FULL! HE NAILED HIM WITH THE ACES FULL! WE WON!
BRANDON JACOBS: THAT BASTARD DID IT! HE BEAT MANSON AGAIN! Doc is going for the cover! 1----------------------------------2-----------------------------------KICKOUT! *GOD POP*
VIC WATERS: DAMN MANSON! Damn him to hell! BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, I don’t know how he did it, but Manson just became the first person to kick out of the Aces Full here in the WFW. That move has been death by everyone who has been hit by it.
VIC WATERS: Its okay, Doc can recover from this, Doc can do it. BRANDON JACOBS: Doc is arguing with the ref! HE CAN’T BELIEVE MANSON KICKED OUT! Manson sneaks up from behind… ROLL UP! THE REF GOES DOWN TO MAKE THE COUNT! 1-------------------------2------------------KICKOUT! Doc barely kicked out of that.
VIC WATERS: Doc needs to regain his focus. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson calls out Psycho’s name… HE GRABS THE ARMS OF DOC SILVER AND PICKS HIM UP…. TIGER DRIVER 91 IN THE CENTER OF THE RING!
VIC WATERS: Doc is dead. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson goes for the cover, 1--------------------------2------------------KICKOUT! DOC SOMEHOW MANAGED TO KICKOUT OUT OF IT!
VIC WATERS: Doc will find a way, Doc will find a way… BRANDON JACOBS: Manson picks Doc up off the mat by his hair… A DAZED DOC NAILS MANSON WITH A FLURRY OF RIGHTS AND LEFTS AND MANSON IS CAUGHT BY SURPIRSE!
VIC WATERS: SECOND WIND! He came back from near death against Psycho, HE COULD DO IT AGAIN HERE TONIGHT!
BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver whips Manson off the ropes… DOC SILVER MISSES THE SUPERKICK! Manson bounces off the ropes…. KICK TO THE STOMACH….. YES! YES!
VIC WATERS: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON NAILS DOC WITH THE SWEET DREAMS AND THIS CROWD HAS COLLECTIVELY RISEN TO ITS FEET! Manson goes for the cover! 1------------------------------------2----------------------3!! MANSON HAS FINALLY DONE IT! MANSON HAS BEATEN DOC SILVER AND HE WILL GO ON TO FACE SHAWN HART FOR THE WFW WORLD TITLE AT THE PPV!
VIC WATERS: I can’t believe it… BRANDON JACOBS: C.O.C.K.S. now officially owns your soul and Doc is gone for ninety days. Are you happy with yourself?
VIC WATERS: ::starts crying:: BRANDON JACOBS: Yeah, you better cry, with all the bending over C.O.C.K.S. is going to make you do, your ass is going to be damn sore.
VIC WATERS: ::starts sobbing uncontrolablly:: BRANDON JACOBS: On top of all of that, you got your hero Doc Silver kicked out of WFW for ninety days…. And on top of everything which happened here today, I can’t believe you tried to get me fired.
VIC WATERS: Sorry. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we have to take a commercial break, but when we come back, we will return with Luster vs. Dormant vs. El Arco Iris! STAY TUNED!
