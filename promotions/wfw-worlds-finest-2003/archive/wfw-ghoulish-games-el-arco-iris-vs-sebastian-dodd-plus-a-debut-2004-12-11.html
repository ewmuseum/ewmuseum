WFW Ghoulish Games: El Arco Iris vs. Sebastian Dodd (plus a debut)

11 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 11 December 2004 JONATHAN MARX: This should be an excellent match, I am high on Dodd from having wrestled against him in Empire, and El Arco is coming off a hot streak where he has been winning matches left and right.
VIC WATERS: All except the big ones…. JONATHAN MARX: They said that about Wylde too. They kept on going on and on about how Wylde couldn’t win the big win, but guess what? He did and El Arco Iris could to. To me, it is only a matter of time before he breaks through and once he does, he is on a path for greatness.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is one of the most unique if not these most unique performer in all of wrestling and he has the amateur wrestling/lucha libre influence to boot. You’d have to call him the favorite in this match tonight.
JONATHAN MARX: Maybe, but Dodd has really been tearing up Empire having received several Intercontinental Title shots already, having yet to be pinned. Can El Arco Iris do what all of those people tried to and couldn’t?
VIC WATERS: You guys aren’t even factoring in the karma factor… JONATHAN MARX: Karma factor? VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris had his car totaled earlier tonight. Going into this match, he has a streak of bad luck going on.
BRANDON JACOBS: That is right, I forgot all about that. Lonestar totaled it trying to kill Manson.
VIC WATERS: Tonight just doesn’t seem to be his night. JONATHAN MARX: I can’t believe you believe in all that hooey. You make your own luck. VIC WATERS: Mmmhmm… sure. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris has had a bad night so far, but El Arco Iris isn’t like other people. When he is faced with a negative, he doesn’t let it carry over into the next thing that he does. His resiliency is part of what makes him what he is.
VIC WATERS: I am not saying El Arco Iris is strung like a normal person… but I am just saying there is a way that these things work, we will see who is right.
BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, lets send this down to ringside and get this one underway! CUEUP: “Tunak Tunak Tun” by Dahler Mendhi) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, hailing from over the rainbow, the Prince of Prismatic, EL ARCO IRIS!
::El Arco Iris walks down to a huge pop from the crowd, dozens of children can been seen in the crowd wearing El Arco Iris masks::
RING ANNOUNCER: And now, a man who is making his WFW debut here tonight, from New York, New York… “THE MESSIAH” SEBASTIAN DODD!
::”The Messiah” Sebastian Dodd comes out to a mixed reaction from the crowd and enters the ring::
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris and Dodd begin to circle… EL ARCO IRIS GRABS THE LEG AND TAKES DODD DOWN!
JONATHAN MARX: Nice takedown by El Arco Iris. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris grabs the right leg of Dodd and locks on the spinning toe hold…. Iris is twisting and twist Dodd’s leg around in order to take away some support from his upper leg muscle.
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris is set an area of attack early and go to work on it from here on. JONATHAN MARX: Dodd is an incredible smart wrestler, if you don’t come in with a plan, it will cost you.
BRANDON JACOBS: DODD KICKS EL ARCO IRIS IN THE CHEST WITH HIS FREE LEG AND EL ARCO IRIS LOSES GRIP OF THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: Dodd caught him off guard. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris charges Dodd again and THIS TIME DODD GETS HIM WITH THE TIP OF HIS BOOT RIGHT IN THE EYE! El Arco Iris grabs his eye….
JONATHAN MARX: What makes this even worse for El Arco Iris is with that mask, he can’t fully get at that eye to clear the water out from the tearing.
VIC WATERS: Wearing a mask isn’t all poop and giggles… BRANDON JACOBS: DODD IS GETTING UP! Dodd kicks El Arco Iris in the midsection and picks him up…. SIDE BACKBREAKER! El Arco Iris is clutching his ribs.
JONATHAN MARX: Dodd is trying to wind El Arco Iris in hope that it saps away some of his energy.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dodd bounces off the ropes…. HE DROPS THE LEGDROP RIGHT ON THE CHEST OF EL ARCO IRIS!
JONATHAN MARX: Most people automatically drop a legdrop on the head of their opponent when it can be dropped anywhere and be effective.
VIC WATER: Amen brutha… BRANDON JACOBS: Dodd gets up and he is goating the injured El Arco Iris to stand up…. VIC WATERS: Dodd sure isn’t lacking on self esteem, I like that. JONATHAN MARX: There is no reason to add insult to injury. He had his car destroyed earlier tonight and Dodd nearly poked out his eye with his boot earlier.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is struggling… BUT HE IS GETTING UP! Dodd is there waiting for him… HE HOOKS HIS ARMS AROUND EL ARCO IRIS FOR A GERMAN SUPLEX BUT EL ARCO IRIS IS FIGHTING IT!
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris is showing Dodd his remarking fighting spirit that separates him from 90% of the other performers in this sport.
BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS THROWS BACK A FOREARM AND IT CATCHES DODD IN THE SIDE OF THE HEAD! Iris lifts Dodd up in a fireman’s carry and takes him down to the mat!
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris looks like he is grabbing his side, that throw may have aggravated something.
VIC WATERS: Dodd has to becareful because the mat is El Arco Iris’ playground. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris ignores his own pain, he drops to one knee and he grabs the arms of Dodd and locks him in a double chicken wing!
JONATHAN MARX: If El Arco Iris can work over Dodd’s arms enough, he may may not be able to lock in his patented D-O-DT or The Ascension with enough venom to keep him down.
VIC WATERS: Every little edge counts. BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS IS BACKSLIDING DODD DOWN TO THE MAT! The referee goes down to count! 1------------------------------------2---------------------------- DODD MANAGES TO KICK OUT!
JONATHAN MARX: Dodd is close to the ropes so the ref is asking El Arco Iris to give him his distance while he is getting up.
VIC WATERS: The ref is making a fair call here. Dodd should be allowed to get back to his feet.
BRANDON JACOBS: DODD GETS UP AND HE CHARGES EL ARCO IRIS… EL ARCO IRIS MOVES THE REF OUT OF THE WAY AND DODD CLOBBERS HIM WITH A DROPKICK RIGHT TO THE FACE!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris is way too compassionate about other people, he should have let the ref take the blow and then attacked Dodd after that.
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris didn’t want to see the ref get hurt. He isn’t that kind of a person.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris landed on his injured side when he fell down from that dropkick…. DODD PICKS EL ARCO IRIS UP OFF THE MAT…. DODD WHIPS EL ARCO IRIS OFF THE MAT AND TAKES HIM DOWN WITH A SIDEWALK SLAM!
VIC WATERS: All impact baby… JONATHAN MARX: Dodd doesn’t look it, but he has considerable strength for a man his size. BRANDON JACOBS: DODD HOOKS THE LEG AND GOES FOR A PIN! The referee goes down to count! 1---------------------------------------------------2-------------------------------- EL ARCO IRIS MANAGES TO GET THE SHOULDER UP!
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris has taken so many top guys to the limit lately, any time you have a chance to go for a pin on him, you have to take that opportunity if you want to beat him.
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris nearly beat both Psycho and Manson for heaven sake. Imagine that nut case as our World Heavyweight Champion.
JONATHAN MARX: If we only granted World Heavyweight Title shots to those without issues, we’d have a very baron world title scene.
BRANDON JACOBS: Sebastian Dodd is the first man up and he is picking El Arco Iris up off the mat…. HE LIFTS HIM UP IN THE AIR AND SLINGSHOTS EL ARCO IRIS ON THE TOP ROPE AND SUPLEXES HIM BACK DOWN TO THE MAT!
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris looks all out of sorts from this onslaught by Dodd. VIC WATERS: Dodd has to play this cool and wait for his moment to strike. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is down and Dodd is heading to the corner, he is climbing up the turnbuckle to the top rope…. WAIT A SECOND, EL ARCO IRIS IS SOMEHOW GETTING UP!
JONATHAN MARX: Dodd better be careful or he could totally change the moment of the match towards El Arco Iris’ favor.
BRANDON JACOBS: DODD SETS AT THE TOP AND HE FLIES OFF WITH A CROSS CHOP WHICH SENDS EL ARCO IRIS BACK DOWN TO THE GROUND!
VIC WATERS: The chop of death! JONATHAN MARX: Now all he needs to do is bust out the Mongolian Chops… BRANDON JACOBS: DODD GOES FOR THE COVER ON EL ARCO IRIS! 1---------------------------------------------2------------------------------------ EL ARCO IRIS MANAGES TO KICKOUT AGAIN!
JONATHAN MARX: Dodd looks like he is trying to figure out what he can do to keep El Arco Iris down.
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris has driven many a wrestler nuts with his technical skill and attitude.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dodd waistlocks El Arco Iris and deadlifts him up off the mat…. DODD GERMAN SUPLEXES EL ARCO IRIS…. OH MAN, IT LOOKED LIKE EL ARCO IRIS LANDED ON HIS NECK AND EL ARCO IRIS HAS ROLLED OUT TO THE OUTSIDE!
JONATHAN MARX: I don’t think it was intentional. VIC WATERS: I told you before this match that this just wasn’t El Arco Iris’ day. JONATHAN MARX: The match isn’t over yet. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is down on his hands and knees on the ground on the outside and he is clutching that neck and what appears to be obvious pain.
JONATHAN MARX: Hopefully Dodd didn’t do any serious damage because neck injuries can take a long time to recover from.
BRANDON JACOBS: DODD EXITS THROUGH THE ROPES ONTO RING APRON…. El Arco Iris is down on his hands and knees….. DODD TAKES A RUNNING LEAP OFF OF THE RING APRON AND DRIVES A FLYING ELBOW DROP RIGHT TO THE BACK OF THE NECK OF EL ARCO IRIS!
JONATHAN MARX: Good god almighty… VIC WATERS: With the way El Arco Iris’ neck must have felt after that German Suplex, that is like hitting him with a block of cement right in the back of the neck.
JONATHAN MARX: Don’t give LOVE any ideas. BRANDON JACOBS: Dodd lifts El Arco Iris up by the mask…. EL ARCO IRIS FIRES BACK AT SEBASTIAN DODD WITH A SERIES OF QUICK PUNCHES RIGHT TO THE FACE!
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris may be fighting for his career right here. BRANDON JACOBS: DODD KICKS EL ARCO IRIS IN THE MIDSECTION AND DOUBLES HIM OVER! Dodd waistlocks El Arco Iris… OH MY GOD, HE IS GOING TO GERMAN SUPLEX HIM NECK FIRST INTO THE STEEL GUARDRAIL!
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris needs to fight this. VIC WATERS: DO IT DODD DO IT! BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS UNLEASHES AN ELBOW INTO THE FACE OFF DODD TO BREAK THE WAISTLOCK! Dodd looks like he is temporarily stunned…
VIC WATERS: Dodd needs to regain his senses and fast…. BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS CHARGES DODD AND HITS HIM WITH A SHINING WIZARD AND DODD GOES UP AND OVER THE GUARDRAIL INTO THE CROWD! El Arco Iris pulls the guardrail closer to the ring….
VIC WATERS: That is a good idea, Dodd has further to go to get the guardrail to help himself up.
JONATHAN MARX: Dodd took a nasty spill onto the chairs on the way down to the ground. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is rolling back into the ring…. VIC WATERS: Maybe he is going to do the smart thing and take the count out victory, why do any more work than you have to?
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris doesn’t want to win like that… BRANDON JACOBS: Dodd is slowly getting up and he is fighting to get himself free of the fans that surround him… EL ARCO IRIS BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND HE FLIES THROUGH THE ROPES AND OVER THE GUARDRAIL AND HITS DODD HEAD FIRST WITH A TOPE CON GIRO!
JONATHAN MARX: Oh dios mio! VIC WATERS: I can’t believe El Arco Iris did that with a bad neck. JONATHAN MARX: He wants to show all the fans and everyone in back that he deserves to be among the best of the best.
VIC WATERS: Yeah, but what he did was insane in his condition… JONATHAN MARX: No risk, no reward… BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is going outside to check on both men…. HE IS NOW PUTTING A TWENTY COUNT ON BOTH MEN!
VIC WATERS: Shouldn’t he have been doing this earlier? JONATHAN MARX: The referees tend to enforce the rules very selectively. One of these days we’ll break their union and be able to improve the standards.
REF: ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! SIX! SEVEN! EIGHT! NINE! TEN! ELEVEN! TWELVE! THIRTEEN! FOURTEEN!
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is finally beginning to stir…. REF: FIFTEEN! SIXTEEN! BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris grabs Sebastian Dodd and he rolls him back into the ring! REF: NINETEEN! BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris rolls back in just before the twenty count and the ref is letting his match continue. EL ARCO IRIS MAKES THE COVER ON DODD!
JONATHAN MARX: This could be it, Dodd has barely moved in the last thirty seconds. He appears to be almost totally out of it.
VIC WATERS: Come on Dodd! Kick out! BRANDON JACOBS: The referee goes down for the count! 1-----------------------------------------------------------2---------------------------- DODD GETS HIS FOOT ON THE ROPES---- 3! HE GOT HIM! El Arco Iris gets up and he pumps his fist….
JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris pulled it out by the skin of his teeth, but he got it. VIC WATERS: No he didn’t! Dodd’s foot was on the ropes… BRANDON JACOBS: The referee looks at Dodd…. HE SEES THE FOOT AND HE IS SAYING THAT THIS MATCH MUST CONTINUE! El Arco Iris is look at the referee in disbelief. The referee walks to the other side of the ring to yell at the time keeper to keep the clock going…
VIC WATERS: That is the same man you saved El Arco Iris… the same man. JONATHAN MARX: El Arco Iris is not having a good day. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris walks over to Dodd to pick him up off the mat…. LOW BLOW BY DODD! THE REFEREE DIDN’T SEE IT AND EL ARCO IRIS IS BENT OVER IN PAIN!
JONATHAN MARX: UNFOULE! BRANDON JACOBS: Dodd gets up quickly and he locks him in the D-ODDT (DDT/Russian Leg Sweep Combo)…. HE DELIVERS! Dodd is calling the ref over! 1------------------------------DODD PUTS BOTH FEET ON THE ROPES FOR LEVERAGE--------2----------------------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match….. “THE MESSIAH” SEBASTIAN DODD! JONATHAN MARX: NO DAMNIT NO! BRANDON JACOBS: The referee looks over and sees the ropes shaking but he shrugs it off. VIC WATERS: The era of Dodd has officially begun! BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is yelling at the referee that his feet were on the ropes and all the ref has to do is ask the fans… BUT THE REFEREE IS IGNORING EL ARCO IRIS!
JONATHAN MARX: What a dick… BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS ROLLS OUT OF THE RING AND HE GRABS A CHAIR FROM A FAN….. The referee lifts the hand of Sebastian Dodd up in the air in victory….. EL ARCO IRIS ROLLS BACK IN AND HE PLASTERS BOTH OF THEM IN THE BACK WITH A CHAIRSHOT!
JONATHAN MARX: Good lord, El Arco Iris has snapped! VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris has been hanging on by a thread for a long time, it was a matter of time.
BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS IS ASSAULTING DODD WITH THE CHAIR REPEATEDLY RIGHT IN THE BACK AND DODD LOOKS LIKE HE IS BLEEDING FROM THE BACK OF THE HEAD!
JONATHAN MARX: I don’t agree what the referee or Dodd did to him in this match, but El Arco Iris needs to regain some control….
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris has the microphone…. IRIS: Yeah...is THIS what you troglodytes want? Pain? Suffering? Anguish? Well, that's as much as you're gonna get, El Arco Iris isn't gonna be your puppet!
BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, ADAM BENJAMIN IS RUNNING DOWN TO THE RING AND HE IS PLEADING WITH EL ARCO IRIS TO STOP!
JONATHAN MARX: When we last saw Adam Benjamin, it was El Arco Iris who saved him from an even worse injury at the hands of Minion….
BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS IS BEGINNING TO SHAKE IN ANGER AND HE IS YELLING AT BENJAMIN, HE IS CALLING HIM A TRAITOR! El Arco Iris throws the chair at Benjamin but Benjamin moves out of the way in the nick of time and El Arco Iris is finally leaving the ring.
JONATHAN MARX: I have never seen El Arco Iris look so angry before. What has Dodd and Lonestar done?!
VIC WATERS: I don’t know, but I like it. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, when we comeback, it is time for the Mystery Man verses Gideon…. STAY TUNED!
