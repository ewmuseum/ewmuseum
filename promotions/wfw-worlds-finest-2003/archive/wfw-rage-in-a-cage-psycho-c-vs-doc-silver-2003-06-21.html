WFW: Rage in a Cage: Psycho (c) vs. Doc Silver

21 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 21 June 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Jun-22-03 AT 04:07&nbsp;PM (EST)]VIC WATERS: I have a letter here directly from management which I would like to read, as the former champion, Doc Silver has the right to add a stipulation to the match and his stipulation is that not only can you win the match via pinfall or submission, you can also win the match by escape!
BRANDON JACOBS: Look at that cage, the top is covered with razorwire and the cage door has been wielded shut. We have to lower the cage because there is no way to get the wrestlers in or out. How is anyone going to escape this cage? It is baffling.
VIC WATERS: There is no such thing as an inescapable prison. BRANDON JACOBS: This is Psycho’s house, I’m not saying that Doc won’t be competitive, but even if you have the chance to escape, this is still home field advantage for Psycho.
VIC WATERS: Before we went on the air tonight, I went to church and prayed for Doc to survive this match and win the title. I know what Psycho can do, you are damn right I’m scared for him but I believe in Doc.
BRANDON JACOBS: There is an old saying, you can wish in one hand and crap in the other and see which gets filled first. Let’s send the gabbing and send this match to ringside!
::Only Happy When It Rains by Garbage wails over the loud speaker:: RING ANNOUNCER: His opponent, from Orlando, Florida, one of the greatest legends and a 4 time AAWC World Champion, DOC SILVER!
:: Doc Silver walks out to the loudest heel heat of the night and enters the ring:: :: "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne blares over the loud speaker:: RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from parts Unknown, the World Finest Wrestling World Heavyweight Champion, “THE AMERICAN DANGERMAN” PSYCHO!
:: Psycho walks down to the ring with his belt to pop from the crowd. He hands it to the attendant at ringside and he enters the ring::
*cage starters to lower as the lights flicker* BRANDON JACOBS: The cage is lowering and the fans are going nuts! This is the scariest looking cage I’ve ever seen.
VIC WATERS: That looks a good twenty feet high or so… very scary looking cage. *cages finishes lowering and the bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho and Doc Silver are meeting in the center of the ring and are looking at each other eye to eye. Psycho is trash talking Doc. DOC PUSHES PSYCHO! Psycho doesn’t leave his feet and he gets back into Doc Silver’s face. Doc is starting to back off. PSYCHO STARTS WAILING ON HIM WITH HAYMAKERS RIGHT TO THE FACE AND DOC IS BEING BACK UP TOWARDS THE CAGE!
VIC WATERS: Doc is just trying to tire Psycho out by making himself a punching bag, Great strategy.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho charges Silver… BACKBODY DROP INTO THE CAGE AND PSYCHO GETS TANGLED UP IN THE OTHER SIDE OF THE ROPES AND FALLS DOWN TO THE GROUND!
VIC WATERS: Air Psycho! BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is slowly getting up…. DOC SILVER FIRES AT PSYCHO WHO IS TRAPPED BEHIND THE ROPES WITH A SUPERKICK BUT DOC MISSES AND KICKS THE CAGE WHEN PSYCHO FALLS OUT OF THE WAY! Man, that cage shook when he delivered the superkick!
VIC WATERS: Doc has a lot of power in his legs, if he had nailed it, Psycho would not have only been hit with the kick but he would have banged his head into the cage!
BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO DIVES FOR THE LEGS OF DOC SILVER AND HE TAKES HIM DOWN! PSYCHO AND DOC ARE ROLLING AROUND ON THE MAT PUNCHING AWAY AT EACH OTHER AS THIS CROWD IS GOING NUTS!
VIC WATERS: This isn’t wrestling, this is just two guys in a fight over what they care about most, the WFW World Heavyweight Title.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho lands on top and he nails Doc with some hard forearms to the face! DOC USES HIS LEGS AND RAMS HIS THIGH INTO THE BACK OF PSYCHO AND PSYCHO FALLS OFF OF DOC! Psycho grabs Doc by the hair AND HE THROWS HIM INTO THE CAGE! HE GRABS THE BACK OF DOC’S HEAD AND RAKES HIS FACE ALONG THE WALL OF THE CAGE VICIOUSLY!
VIC WATERS: It looks Doc has already been busted open! That cage is unforgiving against your body and can leave you scared up for weeks.
BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES THE GREEN MACHINE! He is scaling the cage with a pair of wire cutters and is trying to cut open the right corner!
VIC WATERS: Wooohooo, reinforcements! BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is yelling to the referee to get him down but Green Machine is cutting away at the corner of the cage rather quickly!
VIC WATERS: HERE COMES MANSON AND HE HAS A CHAIR! Manson begins to climb the cage and Green Monster drops his cutters into the ring and tries to climb down before Manson can get him! Green Monster falls down to his feet and bails for the exit and Manson drops down to the floor and chases after him!
BRANDON JACOBS: The damage has been done though, it looks like a person might be able to squeeze through that whole in the top of the cage if they tried hard enough. Doc has done it again. There is now an opening in the cage.
VIC WATERS: Doc is genius at finding ways around difficult situations. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho looks at Doc and he goes after him with an increased vigor! He knows he needs to pin Doc in the center of the ring before he has a chance to escape. Psycho picks up Silver and he rubs Silver’s blood right into his own eyes! Doc has been blinded by his own blood and he is swinging his arms around wildly!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is really is psychotic. BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO NAILS THE BLINDED DOC SILVER WITH A YAZUKA KICK AND HE CRASHES DOWN HARD ONTO THE MAT! Doc is grabbing his face.. it looks like the kick may have damage his broken his nose because the blood flow has increased out of Doc!
VIC WATERS: This is horrible, somebody needs to stop Psycho before he does serious damage to Doc.
BRANDON JACOBS: Silver deserves it with the way he has treated Psycho in the past. This is payback time. Psycho grabs Silver and he SLAMS HIM DOWN HARD ON THE MAT! Psycho is motioning that he is going to go up to the top!
VIC WATERS: MOVE OUT OF THE WAY DOC! BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO FLIES OFF THE TOP ROPE AND HE NAILS DOC WITH A MOONSAULT! He covers! 1----------------2-------------KICKOUT! Doc barely got the shoulder up!
VIC WATERS: I swear, Doc Silver most not be able to sleep because everytime he gets his shoulders down for two seconds, he kicks out.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho picks Doc up…. PILEDRIVER! Psycho goes for the cover! 1-----------------2------------KICKOUT! Psycho is looking up to the heavens, what in god’s name is his planning?
VIC WATERS: This can not be good for Doc. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho picks up the wire cutter and he is climbing to the top of the cage where Green Machine has already cut a piece off for Doc to escape. Psycho starts cutting away as Doc is laying motionless on the ground.
VIC WATERS: Why isn’t he trying to escape? The hole is right there! BRANDON JACOBS: This isn’t just about the title, this Doc Silver and Psycho feud is personal and Psycho wants to make sure Silver thinks twice before he misses with him again…. OH…. MY… GOD! HE HAS CUT OFF A CHUNK OF BARBED WIRE AND HE IS WRAPPING HIMSELF IN IT! The fans are starting up a Holy She chant and they are screaming on the top of their lungs! Psycho takes the last step and he perches himself on top of the cage… DOC IS STILL MOTIONLESS…. PSYCHO FLIES OFF AND HE CRASHES DOWN ONTO DOC FROM THE TOP OF THE CAGE WITH PSYCHO FLIGHT AND THIS CROWD HAS BECOME UNGLUED!
VIC WATERS: THAT MAN IS SUICIDAL! BRANDON JACOBS: He is also the world champion… the momentum of the move was so great that Psycho bounced off when he hit him! Psycho is slowly using the ropes to help himself up and Doc is out cold! Psycho has a huge smile on his face and he walks up to Doc and motions that he is the world champion and he is going to escape the way Doc had planned to! THROUGH THE TOP OF THE CAGE!.
VIC WATERS: This is just adding insult to injured, they need to stop the match. Doc needs medical assistance.
BRANDON JACOBS: The crowd is a buzz… SCOTTY MICHAELS HAS RUN DOWN TO RINGSIDE AND HE IS CLIMBING THE CAGE WITH A SLEDGE HAMMER IN HAND! Psycho doesn’t see him! Scotty Michaels has neared the top of the cage and he is waiting for the dazed Psycho to get there! Psycho is slowly crawling up the top of the cage… WAIT HE SEES SCOTTY MICHAELS! SCOTTY MICHAELS SWINGS THE SLEDGE HAMMER AT PSYCHO’S HAND BUT HE RELEASES IT AND HANGS ON FOR DEAR LIFE WITH HIS OTHER HAND!
VIC WATERS: I LOVE SCOTTY MICHAELS! I love Scotty Michaels baby! BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels swings and he NAILS PSYCHO IN THE HAND AND HE FALLS OFF OF THE CAGE ONTO THE MAT BELOW FROM A GOOD FIFTEEN FEET UP! The fans are in shock! They thought Psycho had this won and then THIS OF ALL THINGS HAPPENS!
VIC WATERS: Look! Doc is moving! He sees Psycho… BRANDON JACOBS: Doc can barely move, but I swear he just smiled when he saw Psycho down on the mat. Doc uses his last bit of energy… HE ROLLS OVER ONTO PSYCHO! NO DAMNIT! IT CAN’T END THIS WAY! The ref goes down for the count! 1---------------------------2-------------------------------KICKOUT AND THIS CROWD IS GOING NUTS! HOW DID PSYCHO KICKOUT OF THAT?!
VIC WATERS: ::throws his water bottle down:: BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver crawls to the ropes and he uses them to help him get up! HE GRABS HIS RIBS! It looks like the Psycho Flight off the top did a lot of damage! Doc Silver is reaching into his boots…. DOC SILVER HAS A PAIR OF HANDCUFFS! Doc takes the handcuffs and puts on Psycho’s right arm and handcuffs him to the top rope!
VIC WATERS: HERE COMES RICHARD GIDEON AND MANSON WITH THE CROWBAR SCOTTY MICHAELS USED ON HIM!
BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels is bailing out is climbing down from the cage! Michaels drops down and Manson chases him through the crowd! DOC SILVER IS STARTING TO CLIMB THE CAGE! Richard Gideon grabs Psycho’s WFW WORLD TITLE BELT AND TRIES TO STOP SILVER FROM LEAVING THE RING! Doc is yelling at Gideon to get out of his way BUT GIDEON KEEPS ON TRYING TO KICK SILVER IN THE FACE! Silver is growing increasingly frustrated with Gideon… WAIT A SECOND, PSYCHO IS GETTING UP!
VIC WATERS: Who is Psycho? THE HULK?! BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is trying to break the handcuffs with force…. PSYCHO SEES THE WIRE CUTTERS AND TRIES TO BREAK THEM… HE DOES AND HE IS CLIMBING THE CAGE TO GET TO DOC!
VIC WATERS: Madman Psycho on Doc’s tail and Gideon on top protecting the exit, Doc Silver is a dead man!
BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO GRABS DOC AROUND HIS WAIST! HE IS GOING TO GO FOR A BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX FROM THE TOP OF THE CAGE!
VIC WATERS: Both guys have been through hell and back and now this?! BRANDON JACOBS: DOC SILVER REACHES UP AND HE GRABS THE BELT FROM GIDEON! PSYCHO PROPELLS HIMSELF BACKWARDS AND DOC NAILS HIM WITH THE TITLE BELT ON THE WAY DOWN….. OH NO! OH NO!
::crowd does silent as Doc Silver and Psycho crash onto the mat and go right through the ring to the floor::
VIC WATERS: By some miracle, Doc Silver landed on top of Psycho and Psycho took the grunt of the fall! Don’t pass out Brandon, this is your job.
BRANDON JACOBS: ::starts shaking:: VIC WATERS: YES! YES! YES! DOC SILVER IS CRAWLING OUT UNDERNEATH THE RING… HE MAKES IT RINGSIDE AREA! DOC SILVER HAS ESCAPED THE CAGE!
RING ANNOUNCER: THE WINNER OF THE MATCH… AND NEW WFW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION FOR THE SECOND TIME….. DOC SILVER!!
::Only Happy When It Rains by Garbage plays over the loud speakers:: VIC WATERS: Justice has been served Brandon, sure Gideon came down there to scare Michaels away and had no intention of letting that WFW World Heavyweight Title slip into Doc’s hands but those are the breaks. Psycho really fought his heart out though. That was such an incredible match and these fans will remember it forever. It looks like the paramedics are going to help Psycho to the back. He has seen hell and survived. A lot of courage that guy has, but in the end, he couldn’t beat Doc. Brandon, are you still with us? Don’t mind my partner, he is a little shook up. Well, on behalf of everyone here, I’d like to thank you for joining us, we’ll be back on July 4th for Scars & Stripes Forever! Thanks for watching everyone, GOOD NIGHT!
