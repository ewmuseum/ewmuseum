WFW — Card

Superbowl of Wrestling: Intro

18 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 18 April 2004 ::The Camera pans over a filled to capacity Gillette Stadium in Foxoboro, Massachutes and then onto the announcers::
BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome everyone to the Superbowl of Wrestling, where tonight we will celebrate the one year anniversary of World’s Finest Wrestling!
VIC WATERS: God, has it been a year already? BRANDON JACOBS: It doesn’t seem like it. These anniversaries just creep up on you. Tonight we have one of the finest cards ever assembled with talent from WFW, GXE, CSWA, NEW, and even the old GLCW.
VIC WATERS: That double steel cage match between Anarky and Maelstrom has a Boston Red Sox level curse on it. Several leagues have had several unfortunate series of events on the way building up to that match and have been forced to close before Maelstrom verses Anarky took place.
BRANDON JACOBS: It is sort of like saying the name MacBeth when you are performing the play. But the risk is worth the reward, these two men have been trying to face each other for over four years and tonight, we will have it for you once and for all.
VIC WATERS: That is just like a Boston Red Sox fan not to believe in the curse and get their hopes up.
BRANDON JACOBS: Curses are all psychological. It is just a bunch of hogwash. VIC WATERS: You keep on telling yourself that. BRANDON JACOBS: Maelstrom verses Anarky would be enough to headline any show but there are several main event level matches on tonight’s show. One I’m really looking forward to is Shawn Hart against “The Viewer’s Choice” Scotty Michaels.
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart should have stayed retired. Doesn’t he remember what Scotty Michaels did to him last time when he took his title with a little help from Luster? Scotty Michaels doesn’t even have Luster weighing him down anymore, Michaels is even better than the last time he faced Hart. He has even finally beaten the one man that he couldn’t, Sean Edmunds.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster weighed Scotty Michaels down? What are you smoking? Luster helped him win the WFW North American Title against Manson and the WFW World Heavyweight Title against Hart. I have to admit, I was impressed that Scotty Michaels finally beat Edmunds, even with all the problems Edmunds has been having lately. But Shawn Hart is Shawn Hart, he is arguably the greatest world champion in WFW history and helped make this company what it is today. You don’t think Shawn Hart is going to step it up on the grandest stage of them all?
VIC WATERS: I have no doubt that he will try, but he is no Scotty Michaels. Scotty Michaels would still be champion today if it wasn’t for Copycat’s interference.
BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels simply got a taste of his own medicine. He has repeatedly been screwing people left and right. He has no reason to complain to anyone.
VIC WATERS: That is the difference between you and me, I recognize greatness like Scotty Michaels and you are always making some excuse why he won this or that. Everyone should look up to wrestlers like Scotty Michaels as role models of what they can aspire to be if they work hard.
BRANDON JACOBS: Please, your love of Scotty is making me sick. Even without Luster, he had to cheat to beat Edmunds in the tag. Speaking of which, Sean Edmunds is going to try to break his losing streak since losing the title in a match against a man he has never beaten in his career, Jean Rabesque.
VIC WATERS: Jean Rabesque is one of the best technical wrestlers of alltime and one of the favorites to win it all in the NFW South. How can Edmunds possibly beat him with the snide he has been on?
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is a former WFW World Heavyweight Champion and he has beaten some of the finest wrestlers that WFW has to offer. He has been in a little bit of a rut lately, but I have a feeling he is about to bust out in a big way here tonight.
VIC WATERS: Rabesque is the God of workrate, he is hero to his home country, and he OWNS Edmunds. He is 17-0 lifetime against him.
BRANDON JACOBS: The odds are against Edmunds I will say… VIC WATERS: Let me put this in terms that you will understand, Edmunds winning would be like the Cubs winning the World Series. Edmunds is a talented wrestler, he has all the tools to beat Rabesque, but It is not going to happen in our lifetime.
BRANDON JACOBS: Blasphemy. You have to believe. VIC WATERS: Copycat has a better chance of beating the current CSWA World Heavyweight Champion Dan Ryan, and that isn’t going to happen either.
BRANDON JACOBS: That is going to be a tough match for Copycat. Copycat is a big man, but Dan Ryan is huge and has an impressive streak of victories over Windham & Southern. I’m going to be rooting for him to give Dan Ryan hell, but it is going to be an uphill battle.
VIC WATERS: I was backstage today with the board of directors and they said there is very short list of wrestlers outside of WFW that they would pony up the cash on top of their Ruthian sized payroll to bring into WFW. Guess who the top two are?
BRANDON JACOBS: Was this on the record? Who are they? VIC WATERS: It has been said enough off the record that it is on the record. The two men are Dan Ryan and Maelstrom.
BRANDON JACOBS: That is just a testament to the stars they are. But not only are the fighting for the pride of WFW, they are fighting for positioning as the number one contender for the WFW World Heavyweight Title.
VIC WATERS: This whole series was constructed by Wylde to determine who is really the number one contender for his title.
BRANDON JACOBS: More like as a way to punish soften up potential contenders to his title if he manages to get past Manson. But I can’t say that I’m not excited to see it, it is in the spirit of original Superbowl of Wrestling which saw two sides fighting it out to determine who is best. These men may not be from the same league, but they represent the very best wrestling has to offer against our best.
VIC WATERS: As much of a war as that may seem, the battle for the vacant BAD World Heavyweight Title seems to have nuclear heat. Jared Wells, Rat Fink, Pulsar, and Felix Red are going to have a battle of epic proportions after Minion ended his own career winning the title by running through a wall at Edison Field and had to retire prematurely.
BRANDON JACOBS: Poor Minion. That was his first title ever. Whether I approved of his actions or not, one has to admire Minion’s drive to go all out to win the BAD World Heavyweight Title in the spirit of those before him. This could have been his chance to shin with the whole world watching. One these men will have a chance to win the BAD World Heavyweight Title, the organization where the great tradition of the Superbowl of Wrestling started.
VIC WATERS: There is a sense of history and toughness that no other World Title has compared to the BAD World Heavyweight Title. It was the first World Title that Manson ever won on his rise to where he is today. It descends from the Mr. Vicious Title which all of the greats of their day fought over in the golden age of wrestling.
BRANDON JACOBS: I remember as a kid watching NGEN and watching in marvel that two men could dish out the level of punishment to one another and keep on coming back for more. There aren’t four men in the company which better personify the spirit of this title and I’m excited over the fact we will see a new champion crowned.
VIC WATERS: Speaking of being in awe, is there anyone in the company hotter than Iron Lion right now? I don’t like his self righteous attitude about this sport, but in that ring he has made me crap my pants on more than one occasion. That guy is legit scary.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris who went through hell in back in War Games and the NEW World Heavyweight Champion Larry Tact have a hell of a task in front of them to overcome the size of Iron Lion. That three way match will be an elimination match so if either Tact or El Arco Iris hope to win, they are going to have to eliminate Iron Lion in order to win the match.
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris is a good two feet shorter than Iron Lion. After the abuse El Arco Iris took in the War Games match, this may not be much better. Larry Tact who is a big man is good half foot shorter than Iron Lion and he is use to being the bigger man in most matches he is in.
BRANDON JACOBS: Going from one champion to another, Richard Gideon is going to put his WFW North American Title on the line against the former WFW World Heavyweight Champion Psycho. A lot of people have been calling Gideon during this time of turmoil over the WFW World Heavyweight Title the Real World Heavyweight Champion so Psycho is going to have a hell of a fight if he wants to wrestle the title away from him.
VIC WATERS: When Richard Gideon took the fight to Doc like he did in those matches early on in WFW, I thought he may have been wrestling over his head but the more time removed from that, I think Gideon really is that damn good. He has repeatedly beaten every challenge put in front of him. Psycho however is a former World Heavyweight Champion and he knows what it takes to pull off the impossible. He was an underdog at Cherry Blossom Chaos in that four way against Hart. Silver, and Gideon and he managed to emerge victorious. We are ten months removed from Cherry Blossom Chaos and Psycho is looking for lightning to strike twice.
BRANDON JACOBS: Both men with have a tough battle getting two falls on the other man under various stipulations and both of these men know each other like the back of their hand because they have been both tag team partners and have had several matches with each other. I think this could be a classic.
VIC WATERS: With all the great matches on the show, it would seem like any main event would get over shadowed, but I’m pumped to see the Donald Trump of the WFW, Alex Wylde put his WFW World Heavyweight Title on the line against Manson and hopefully deny him yet again of his chance of winning the World Title.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson gave Wylde the ladder match stipulation that he wanted. But in order to make sure that Wylde didn’t use his power to cause chaos, the board of directors on behalf of Manson has enlisted the help of Melton in order to make sure Manson gets a fair shot.
VIC WATERS: The Board of Directors can do all the want to protect their golden boy Manson but Wylde isn’t going to lose that strap anytime soon. Wylde has battled over nine years to win a World Title, now he is going to beat Manson in his first title defense in a long line of title defenses in a reign which we will talk about at the next Superbowl of Wrestling.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson has been denied that title for over a year by Doc Silver and Shawn Hart. I think tonight is the night when Manson finally gets it done and he’ll get it done in front of a world wide stage.
VIC WATERS: If Manson hasn’t gotten it done yet, why would tonight be the night when under less of a spotlight he came up a bit short from his dream of wearing the WFW World Heavyweight Title? Not all stories can end with a happy ending and Manson is going to realize that tonight. Remember, the last time Manson wrestled at the Superbowl of Wrestling, while he became a star in his effort against Doc, he still didn’t win the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: That was a different show and a different night. That streak will end tonight.
VIC WATERS: We will see. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, it is time for us to stop our gabbing and get the wrestling started. We will be right back after this commercial break with our first match of the night, Iron Lion will Larry Tact and El Arco Iris in an elimination match, STAY TUNED!
