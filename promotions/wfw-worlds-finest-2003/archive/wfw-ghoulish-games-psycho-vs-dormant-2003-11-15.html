WFW Ghoulish Games - Psycho vs. Dormant

15 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 15 November 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Our first match up tonight features the Former WFW World Heavyweight Champion Psycho taking on the tough youngster who has been down on his luck, Dormant.
VIC WATERS: Dormant hasn’t made his mark yet in the WFW, but I know for a fact that there are a lot of people in the back who are afraid to face him. This kid is as tough as nails and a lot of people think Psycho is rip to be picked off here in Dormant’s own home town. Psycho hasn’t been himself since the layoff.
BRANDON JACOBS: We all know what Psycho did to Doc Silver, but he hasn’t been the same either since that match. If Psycho wants to regain his position among the elite in the WFW, he has to step up tonight and beat Dormant and show people that he is back to his old Psycho self. Dormant does scare the hell out of me. But Psycho has beaten some of the greats here in WFW… he needs to reach back and channel his experience to overcome this young powerhouse Dormant. I don’t know if Psycho can do it, but let’s send this one to ringside and find out for ourselves!
CUEUP: “Ariels” by System of Down) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, standing at over 6’2, two hundred and twenty pounds, from Chicago, Illinois…. Making his WFW PPV debut…. DORMANT!
::Dormant comes down to a huge pop from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, the former WFW World Heavyweight Champion, PSYCHO! ::Psycho comes down to a mixed reaction from the crowd and enters the ring:: BRANDON JACOBS: DORMANT CHARGES PSYCHO WITH A SPEAR TACKLE AND PSYCHO JUMPS UP WITH A LEAP FROG….. DORMANT RAMS HIS SHOULDER INTO THE RING POST!
VIC WATERS: My god, Dormant hit his right shoulder so hard, I’m shocked it is still in his socket.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho grabs Dormant from behind…. RELEASE BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX AND DORMANT LANDS HARD ON HIS INJURED SHOULDER!
VIC WATERS: The ref is going over to check on Dormant, he may have done more damage than he thought with that spear from hell.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dormant is yelling in pain, Psycho picks Dormant up so the ref can’t check. This damn Psycho isn’t giving the ref to check on Dormant at all. The ref should just call this match for Dormant gets any more seriously hurt.
VIC WATERS: I’m sure Dormant doesn’t want to quit, he is in his hometown of Chicago. He wants to suck this up and somehow pull out this match despite being in severe pain.
BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO IS CALLING FOR THE TIGER DRIVER! He picks Dormant up…. DORMANT IS TRYING TO FIGHT BUT PSYCHO HAS IT LOCKED ON…. HE NAILS HIM WITH THE TIGER DRIVER! Psycho makes the cover and the ref goes down for the count! 1-----------------2---------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match….. PSYCHO! VIC WATERS: Dormant caught a bad break, he injured himself early on and he just couldn’t over come the pain.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho stepped it up though and this is a huge victory for him on the road back to the top. Okay folks, after commercial break, we will be back with Rat Fink defending BAD World Heavyweight Title against Pulsar! STAY TUNED!
