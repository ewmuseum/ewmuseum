WFW Scars and Stripes: Gideon/Doc vs. Hart/Psycho

11 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 11 July 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome back everyone, one of our commercials ran long so all the competitors are currently in the ring, but before we start the match, here are the Ultimate Jeopardy stipulations once again!
If Gideon pins Hart, he gets his shot at Doc. If Psycho pins Doc, he gets a shot at him that night. If Doc pins Psycho, Psycho doesn't get his rematch. If Gideon pins Psycho, Psycho picks the stipulation for his match with Gideon If Shawn Hart pins Gideon, he'll take Tiny's spot for one month. If Shawn Hart pins Doc, he gets to name the stipulation for his match at the PPV LET’S GET THIS MATCH STARTED! RING ANNOUNCER: This match is a one fall, the thirty minute time limit for this contest HAS BEEN WAIVED! The competitors will fight to a decisive finish! LET’S HOOK EM’ UP!
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver and Psycho have elected to start off the match. Doc is taunting Psycho over losing the title to him in Rage in the Cage and Psycho looks like he is starting to lose his composure.
VIC WATERS: Doc rarely starts out a tag match, this is very uncharacteristic of him but he must want to get into his opponents head because he has recently cost both of them the title over the last month and a half.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho and Doc Silver are trading blows back and forth! PSYCHO BEGINS TO GET THE ADVANTAGE AND HE BACKS DOC SILVER BACK TOWARDS THE CORNER!
VIC WATERS: Doc needs to fight his way out of the corner or Psycho is going to take advantage of his ring positioning!
BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver falls back into the corner… PSYCHO NAILS DOC SILVER WITH A YAZUKA KICK WITH HIM BACKED IN THE CORNER!
VIC WATERS: He stapled him into the corner and took off his head with that shot! Doc won’t die this easily, he’s a fighter! Come on Doc!
BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver falls down in the corner, Psycho picks him… BODYSLAM! VIC WATERS: Psycho is the most dangerous man from the top rope in all of the WFW. Doc needs to somehow move out of the way or he is toast.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho climbs to the top rope… MOONSAULT! HE NAILED DOC PERFECTLY! He covers! 1-------------------2--------RICHARD GIDEON RUNS ACROSS THE RING AND MAKES THE SAVE!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is giving Gideon a look. He can’t be happy with his friend costing him his title shot.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref escorts Richard Gideon back to his corner and Psycho starts stomping away on Doc! Psycho picks Doc off the mat… DOC CHOPS PSYCHO IN THE THROAT! What a low down dirty thing to do!
VIC WATERS: Doc saw an opportunity and he took it, that is what he is the WFW World Heavyweight Champion.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho reaches for Doc while coughing and trying to catch his breath… DOC SILVER MOVES OUT OF REACH AND NAILS PSYCHO WITH A SUPERKICK ON THE CHIN! Psycho goes down!
VIC WATERS: Doc Silver drags Psycho to his corner and makes the tag to Gideon! BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon comes in… GIDEON WHIPS PSYCHO OFF THE ROPES… HE CATCHES HIM WITH A SLEEPER HOLD! Shawn Hart is pounding on the top rope trying to get Psycho going but Psycho is already looking tired!
VIC WATERS: Gideon really that sleeper locked in tight, I don’t know how long Psycho will be able to last in this.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref raises Psycho’s hand once… IT FALLS! He lifts it up a second… it goes down again! SHAWN HART RUNS IN AND NAILS GIDEON FROM BEHIND BEFORE PSYCHO’S HAND COULD FALL ONE LAST TIME!
VIC WATERS: Thank god Hart came in when he did or this match would be over. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon gets up off the mat… SHAWN HART NAILS HIM WITH THE EL CODO EXPLOSIVO! Gideon goes down like he has been shot! The ref is making Hart return to his corner and both men are layed out in the center of the ring!
VIC WATERS: This is Psycho’s chance to make the tag to the fresh Shawn Hart. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is exhausted but somehow, SOME WAY, he is slowly crawling over to his corner… Gideon really hasn’t moved since Hart nailed him with his finished. Psycho is extending his hand to Hart… PSYCHO MAKES THE TAG!
VIC WATERS: Looks like we are going to see Gideon vs. Hart! BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is starting to move towards his corner… HART GRABS HIS BOOTS AND TRIES TO PULL HIM BACK…. GIDEON’S IS FIGHTING HART TRYING TO MAKE IT TO HIS CORNER WITH ALL HIS MIGHT… THE BOOT COMES OFF! Gideon thrusts his body forward and tags in Doc Silver!
VIC WATERS: LETS GO DOC! BRANDON JACOBS: We may be witnessing a preview of the upcoming PPV the Spirit of NGEN if Doc Silver can make a successful title defense against Rat Fink at Paradise Lost. Doc Silver and Shawn Hart meet in the center of the ring and they are getting into each other’s face. The fans here are chanting for Hart despite the evil ways of the C.O.C.K.S.
VIC WATERS: DOC SILVER SPITS IN THE EYE OF SHAWN HART AND HART GOES APE ON SILVER! BRANDON JACOBS: HART STARTS THROWING WILD PUNCHES AT DOC! Doc Silver rolls out to the floor as Shawn Hart curses Doc on the outside.
VIC WATERS: The ref is trying to back up Shawn Hart so Doc Silver can reenter the ring and we can get this match going again.
BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver rolls back in with a smug look on his face. SHAWN HART STARTS POUNDING THE BEJESUS OUT OF DOC AND DRIVES HIM DOWN TO THE MAT WITH A SERIES OF DOUBLE AXE HANDLES!
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart knows if he works over the back of Doc Silver, it will be harder for him to kickout of the whatever punishment that Hart deals out.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart drags Doc Silver to the center of the ring… he lifts Doc up… TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER! Shawn Hart makes the cover! 1---------------2-----------KICKOUT BY DOC! Shawn Hart picks Doc up again… ANOTHER TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER! Shawn Hart tags in Psycho!
VIC WATERS: This makes no sense, Shawn Hart should go for the pin or he’ll lose his right to add a stipulation to his match with Doc! Unless he thinks that wrestling Psycho again one on one will take more out of Doc so he’ll be ripe by the time the PPV comes.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho looks at Doc, he has been waiting for this moment! He picks Doc Silver off the mat… HE NAILS THE TIGER DRIVER!
VIC WATERS: OH MY GOD, HE HAS TO BE DEAD! BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho covers Doc! 1---------------2------------- RICHARD GIDEON MAKES THE SAVE AGAIN!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is swearing up a storm at Gideon! The ref is trying to get Gideon out of the ring and Psycho is arguing with Gideon all the way back to his corner!
BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver is slowly waking up… it looks like he pulled something from his trunks… HE HAS A PAIR OF BRASS KNUCKLES!
VIC WATERS: Psycho has no idea what is coming. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho goes to pick Doc Silver off the mat… DOC NAILS HIM IN THE HEAD AND ROLLS HIM UP! 1-----------------2------------- SHAWN HART BREAKS UP THE PINFALL!
VIC WATERS: Doc Silver just threw the brass knuckles to Gideon who put them in his tights to keep them away from sight of the referee.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is asking the ref to check Doc but he pads him down and he can’t find it!
VIC WATERS: Gideon & Doc are awesome as a team, Gideon knew where to be and at the right time. That is a sign that he is a real ring veteran.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart has bought Psycho enough time that he has been able to recover. The ref is making Hart go back to the outside. Psycho picks up Doc… HE NAILS HIM WITH A BACKBREAKER! Doc really banged his head on the mat hard there.
VIC WATERS: That mat can feel very hard at times. If it just a matter of hitting your head wrong and it can leave you dazed.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho goes to the top rope… Doc Silver is down… HE NAILS THE PSYCHO FLIGHT!
VIC WATERS: DAMNIT! BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho rolls over onto Doc Silver! Psycho is going to have his rematch afterall AND TONIGHT! He goes for the cover! 1--------------2------------GIDEON DIVES IN AND FOR THE THIRD TIME, HE SAVES DOC SILVER FROM GETTING PINNED!
VIC WATERS: Look at Psycho, I think he just mentally checked out. BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO IS PISSED! HE COMES INTO THE RING WITH A CHAIR…. HE NAILS GIDEON OVER THE TOP OF THE HEAD! HE NAILS SILVER IN THE HEAD! Psycho has snapped!
VIC WATERS: I’ve never seen Psycho this upset and he is screaming to his partner in his corner!
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart tags himself in… HART CLOTHESLINES PSYCHO OUT OF THE RING! VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart may have saved his own life because by the look in Psycho’s eyes, Shawn Hart was next!
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart looks down on both men… What is he going to pick? The stipulation in the WFW World Title match on PPV or Gideon as his lackey? OH MY GOD! HE IS GOING TO TAKE BOTH! Shawn Hart covers both men! 1----------2---------3!!
VIC WATERS: Oh my, he pinned both men! RING ANNOUNCER: The winners of the match, the tag team of Shawn Hart & Psycho! As a result of the double pinfall, Shawn Hart will be able to name the stipulation for his WFW World Title match and Richard Gideon will become Hart’s lackey for a month!
BRANDON JACOBS: We will be back right after this with the main event between Manson & Eian for the title!
