I thought I had seen everything tonight between Inner Circle arriving, Anarky & Manson burning down [name withheld]’s bar, a cage being hijacked, and MFL legends as lumberjacks but this clearly takes the cake. From all of us to all of you, we would like to thank you for joining us for Paradise Lost. We will be back in a couple of weeks with Guerra Interminable from Arena Mexico which will feature four big matches, there will be a 14 man Torneo Cibernetico between the teams of Shawn Hart and Anarky, the winning side will face each other at a later date to determine which one of them will have the WFW North American Title shot at the PPV, speaking of LOVE…. The BAD World Heavyweight Champion Jared Wells will face off in a non title match against the debuting Maelstrom in what should be a hell of a war, there will be a Partners Incredible between the team of Scotty Michaels & Psycho against Copycat and the debuting Dan Ryan, and in the main event, Manson will put up his WFW World Heavyweight Title against El Arco Iris in a best two out of three falls match under Lucha rules. It should be a hell of a show and I hope to see you all there, goodnight folks and see you at GUERRA INTERMINABLE!
