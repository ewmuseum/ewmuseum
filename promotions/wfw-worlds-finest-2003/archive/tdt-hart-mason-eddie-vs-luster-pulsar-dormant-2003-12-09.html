WFW — Card

TDT: Hart/Mason/Eddie vs. Luster/Pulsar/Dormant

9 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 9 December 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Time for some six man action, I’m going to predict a huge upset here tonight. I think the team of Luster, Pulsar, and Dormant are going to defeat the team of Shawn Hart, Terrell Mason, and Good Eddie.
VIC WATERS: Oh come on now, The WFW World Heavyweight Champion Shawn Hart did the impossible. He pinned Manson after nailing him with his finisher and got a pinfall victory. His partner Terrell Mason is a street tough thug who had the balls to interfere in Manson’s match and Good Eddie took it to Alex Wylde before a quirk of faith turned things in Wylde’s favor. Luster has lost two straight, Pulsar is coming off his defeat to Rat Fink, and Dormant has still to win his first match here in the WFW. How could anyone in their right mind say that they are going to win? Now don’t get me wrong, all three are very talented, but all three are colder than Frosty the Snowman and wrestling is a sport of momentum.
BRANDON JACOBS: There you go writing people off because they lose a couple matches. The WFW has so much talent right now from top to bottom, it is hard keep any sort of momentum going. When a single guy rolls through all of the talent in some other leagues, it shows how weak the talent really is because the odds of someone cracking off ten in row should be nearly impossible if a promotion has a strong roster. That doesn’t happen and can’t happen here. It is so easy to lose a couple of matches. Everyone here wants to be here and works hard, that is why I love this group so much.
VIC WATERS: That and the fact that you would be unemployed… BRANDON JACOBS: That does help, but I would do this job for free. Anything can happen in any given match here in the WFW. You may be right, but I’m holding to my prediction in this one. Let’s send this one to ringside and start the show!
(CUEUP: “Ariels” by System of Down) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, standing at over 6’2, two hundred and twenty pounds, from Chicago, Illinois…DORMANT!
::Dormant comes down to a pop from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “"Cat Scratch Fever" by Ted Nugent) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the man making his WFW debut here tonight, from Albuquerque, New Mexico…. GOOD EDDIE!
::Good Eddie slowly walks down in his dark brown trenchcoat like Eastwood, and a red scarf tied around his neck::
(CUEUP: “Whiskey in a Jar” by Metallica) RING ANNOUNCER: And Dormant’s partner, from Australia, standing at over 6’3, the man who won the six man tag for his team at Almost Live, PULSAR!
::fans cheers Pulsar as he comes down to the aisle and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: "The Crown” by Ja Rule) RING ANNOUNCER: And now… Good Eddie’s partner… Making his WFW debut here tonight, the man who made his presence felt at Ghoulish Games, from Queens, New York, Terrell Mason!
(CUEUP: “"Funkytown"-Disco Fever) RING ANNOUNCER: And now…. the last final man of the opposing team, the man who helped end Manson’s WFW North American Title reign and the sexiest man in all of WFW, from Atlanta, Georgia… LUSTER!
::Luster comes down dancing to the ring as the crowd boos:: (CUEUP: "Look At Me" by Geri Haliwell) RING ANNOUNCER: Last but not least, the champion, from Orlando, Florida, the leader of C.O.C.K.S. and the WFW World Heavyweight of the World, “THE PHENOM” SHAWN HART!
::Shawn Hart walks down to the ring to the boos from the crowd, enters the ring, and grabs the mic::
HART: Well Foxwoods... I hate to say it, but I TOLD YOU SO!! ::The crowd voices their displeasure in the thousands.:: HART: Ever since I stepped foot into World's Finest Wrestling, hell... ever since I made my professional DEBUT in this sport, NOBODY has taken me seriously!! I've won my titles, I've beaten jackhole after jackhole after JACKHOLE, and nuthin'. NOTHING!! All I ever got from ANY of my... esteeeeeemed colleeeeagues, was DISRESPECT!
::Hart takes the WFW title belt off of his shoulder, holds it out in front of him, and looks at it, momentarily.::
HART: Now, with that bein' the case. And the cards being dealt as they have... ::Hart holds the title belt out before him, proudly.:: HART: "What do you SONS OF B(FCC)TCHES think of me now?! HUH?!" ::He drapes the belt back over his left shoulder.:: HART: Last week, I beat the HELL from SATAN himself!! And with GOD as my witness, that was ooooooonly the BEGINNING, nnnnnnnndaddio!! Oh yeah!! Cuz, like it or not, the PHENOM runs the show!! As far as WFW's concerned, I'm GEORGE HERMAN RUTH, and from this point forward, I'M callin the shots!! Call it an OUTRAGE, call it a MOCKERY, I call it the TRUTH... and there's not ONE damn person in this place that can...
::Shawn Hart throws down the mic and walks over to his corner:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Both teams are gathered in their corners talking and it appears that Dormant and Good Eddie will begin the match, it looks like Mason and Good Eddie had some heated words but Hart was able to mediate.
VIC WATERS: Good Eddie showed some great technical skill in is PPV debut match against Wylde even with Wylde as skilled as his is. Dormant is more of a power wrestler than a technical wrestler so Good Eddie has the clear advantage if he wants to take him to school.
BRANDON JACOBS: Good Eddie and Dormant lock up… Good Eddie slips behind Dormant and locks on a chicken wing. Dormant is struggling to break free…. DORMANT REACHES BACK IN AN ATTEMPT TO GRAB THE HAIR by Good Eddie keeps his head away! Good Eddie pushes the chicken winged arm up and really is making Dormant squirm under the pressure!
VIC WATERS: Very smart move here by Dormant, by going after the arms, Dormant won’t be able to do the power moves that he is use to without being in pain himself.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dormant throws an elbow back at Good Eddie…. GOOD EDDIE BREAKS THE HOLD AND LOCKS ON A FULL NELSON!
VIC WATERS: Good Eddie manipulated Dormant into throwing that elbow so he could get Dormant in position to easily lock in the full nelson.
BRANDON JACOBS: GOOD EDDIE IS REALLY DIGGING IN AND DORMANT IS BEING TREATED LIKE A RAGDOLL AS HE IS STRUGGLING TO BREAK FREE! Dormant let his legs go loose and the chin of Good Eddie collides with Dormant’s head as he falls down to the ground!
VIC WATERS: Good Eddie is really grabbing his jaw, it looks like that escape really left its mark.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dormant slips behind Good Eddie and locks on the Camel Clutch and he is pulling back on the injured chin with all of his might.
VIC WATERS: Thank god he isn’t Jay Leno or he would have had to submit already. BRANDON JACOBS: TERRELL MASON RUNS IN AND KICKS DORMANT IN THE FACE BREAKING THE HOLD! Dormant gets up….. DORMANT SPEARS TERRELL MASON! Shawn Hart comes into help…. ANOTHER SPEAR BY DORMANT!
VIC WATERS: This crowd is really behind Dormant. Shawn Hart has rolled out of the ring and he is taunting Dormant to come outside. The ref is yelling for Shawn Hart to get back into his corner….
BRANDON JACOBS: GOOD EDDIE COMES FROM BEHIND AND TRIES TO ROLL UP DORMANT! 1----------------------------------------------2------------------------------KICKOUT! Dormant almost got caught by surprise…
VIC WATERS: That was a very long count there. BRANDON JACOBS: Dormant charges Good Eddie…. GOOD EDDIE WITH A BIG BELLY TO BELLY! Good Eddie makes the cover! 1---------------------------2------ KICKOUT! Good Eddie picks Dormant up off the mat…. DORMANT GRABS THE ARM OF GOOD EDDIE AND IRISH WHIPS HIM INTO HIS CORNER! Luster and Pulsar are holding him and laying into Good Eddie as Dormant is taking his time to walk over to his corner.
VIC WATERS: Dormant is showing he has an excellent knowledge of where he is in the ring at all times.
BRANDON JACOBS: DORMANT CHARGES THE CORNER WITH A FOREARM TO THE FACE AND GOOD EDDIE SLUMPS DOWN FROM THE BLOW! Dormant reaches out to Pulsar…. TAG IS MADE AND PULSAR IS NOW THE LEGAL MAN IN THIS MATCH!
VIC WATERS: Things really aren’t looking good for the tag team of Hart, Mason, and Eddie, I know it is still early, but you may be right about this one Jacobs.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar comes in like a house of fire and starts unloading on Good Eddie…. PULSAR PICKS GOOD EDDIE UP IN THE AIR…. INVERTED SUPLEX! Pulsar bounces off the ropes…. Knee drop! Pulsar makes the cover! 1---------------2---KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Good Eddie still has a lot of life in him yet. BRANDON JACOBS: Terrell Mason and Shawn Hart are waiting in the corner for the tag and they are taunting Pulsar to fight them. Pulsar whips Good Eddie of the ropes… SLEEPER HOLD! WAIT A SECOND, TERRELL MASON COMES INTO THE RING FOR A SNEAK ATTACK ON PULSAR…. Pulsar hears his foot steps and spins Good Eddie around and Mason accidently unloads on Good Eddie! PULSAR AND TERRELL MASON ARE TRADING BLOWS BACK AND FORTH! Both of these men love to fight and they are showing it here!
VIC WATERS: Good Eddie looks highly annoyed at what all has happened. BRANDON JACOBS: Mason is gaining the advantage in the fight… MASON KNEES PULSAR IN THE STOMACH… GUTWRENCH SUPLEX! Mason stands over Pulsar and he is getting in his face shouting obscenities… OH MAN, GOOD EDDIE PUSHES MASON FROM BEHIND! Good Eddie and Mason are in each other’s faces… MASON TAKES A SWING AT GOOD EDDIE… GOOD EDDIE FIRES OFF A RIGHT AND LEFT AND ANOTHER RIGHT… Mason is staggering backwards… Good Eddie whips Mason off the ropes… JUMPING SHOULDER BLOCK!
VIC WATERS: Hart is pleading with Good Eddie to calm down but Good Eddie isn’t even listening.
BRANDON JACOBS: Good Eddie picks Terrell Mason up… LAST PRAYER PEDIGREE! Good Eddie walks over to his corner… he tags out to Shawn Hart and… IT LOOKS LIKE HE HAS HAD ENOUGH AND IS LEAVING HIS PARTNERS BEHIND!
VIC WATERS: This match is falling apart for the team of Hart & Mason. BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart is pleading Good Eddie to stay but he is ignoring it all and is heading to the back. Hart comes in and he is yelling at Mason for not controlling his temper… LOOK! Pulsar is almost to his corner…. HE MAKES THE TAG TO LUSTER! LUSTER B-LINES RIGHT FOR HART AND STARTS GOING TO WORK ON THE CHAMPION! A disgruntled Terrell Mason finally has left the ring and he is waiting in the corner for the tag in.
VIC WATERS: Mason has a really big chip on his shoulder. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster has the champion backed up in the corner and is pounding him with closed fists. The referee is trying to get Luster to open up his hands. LUSTER IRISH WHIPS HART INTO THE OTHER CORNER… HART GOES STERNUM FIRST INTO THE TURNBUCKLE! Luster is climbing up to the top rope…. Hart is slowly getting up…. MISSLE DROPKICK! He nailed Hart right between the eyes with that one!
VIC WATERS: Ever since Luster’s lost to Hart, he has been replaying that match in his head over and over again and you can bet he has learned from what he did in that match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is trying to get up…. LUSTER NAILS HIM WITH THE STO! Luster is signaling for his finisher…. LUSTER PICKS SHAWN HART UP ON HIS SHOULDER WITH THE POWERBOMB… HART NAILS HIM WITH THE EL CODO EXPLOSIVO AND LUSTER TUMBLES BACK AND THEY BOTH TUMBLE OUT TO THE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: Luster went down like he was shot! That just shows why the El Codo Explosivo is the most dangerous move in all of wrestling today.
BRANDON JACOBS: Terrell Mason goes over to Luster…. TERRELL MASON THROWS LUSTER INTO THE STEPS HEAD FIRST! Pulsar and Dormant come running to protect their partner and Mason backs off. Dormant picks up Hart…. HE WHIPS HIM INTO THE GUARD RAIL! Pulsar sets…. HE NAILS HIM WITH A BIG SPLASH!
VIC WATERS: Hart looks like he took the wind out of him with that one. BRANDON JACOBS: The referee comes out and he is ordering everyone back to their corner. It is about time the referee to charge of this match. Luster is down by the steps and Hart is down by the railing and the ref is checking to see if both men want to go on. Luster looks like he was busted open by the steel stairs but he is getting up and walking over towards Hart. Luster picks Hart up by the hair…. HART NAILS HIM WITH A DISCUS PUNCH! Luster staggers back…. HART NAILS HIM WITH ANOTHER! Hart grabs Luster and rolls him back into the ring.
VIC WATERS: After taking the beating that Hart took against Manson, it is going to be hard for anyone to match the sheer level of abuse ever again.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart whips Luster off the ropes…. SHAWN HART NAILS HIM WITH A CLOTHESLINE…. But Luster does not go down! Shawn Hart calls for the Hart Attack! KICK TO THE MIDSECTION BY HART! Shawn Hart grabs the neck of Luster…. LUSTER PUSHES SHAWN HART OFF AND SHAWN HART COLLIDES AND KNOCKS HIS PARTNER MASON WHO WAS YELLING AT SOME FANS OFF OF THE RING APRON!
VIC WATERS: Mason was so busy chewing out the fan, I don’t even know if he knows that Hart didn’t do that on purpose.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart turns around… LUSTER IS CHARGING…. EL CODO EXPLOSIVO! Hart falls down to his knees and goes to make the cover on Luster…. TERRELL MASON CHARGES THE RING AND PULLS SHAWN HART UP OFF LUSTER! Shawn Hart is screaming at Mason… KICK TO THE MIDSECTION BY MASON! Mason picks Shawn Hart up…. CLAP BACK!
VIC WATERS: I’ve never seen a team self destruct like Hart, Mason, and Good Eddie. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster crawls over and covers Hart…. 1-----------------------------------------2----------------------- MASON PICKS LUSTER UP BY THE HAIR…. 1-2-25 TO LIFE STUNNER! Mason stands over both men’s bodies and spits on both of them…. MASON IS LEAVING THE RING AND NOW HART IS ALL ALONE!
VIC WATERS: Why is it the champion always finds himself in these impossible situations? At the PPV, he had to beat Manson by his lonesome and now he has to beat three men if he wants to win this match.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is putting a double count on Luster and Hart! 1--------2------3-----4-----5------ 6----- 7----- HART IS STARTING TO GET UP! Hart is stumbling to get to his feet, thank god those ropes are there or he’d have no chance…. HART PULLS LUSTER TO THE CENTER OF THE RING AND HE IS WAITING FOR LUSTER TO GET UP! Hart is setting up for one last El Codo Explosivo to finish him off….
VIC WATERS: Pulsar and Dormant look like they are planning something on the outside. They have to be awfully nervous that Hart will nail this and some how win this match alone.
BRANDON JACOBS: DORMANT CHARGES THE RING…. BIG KNEE TO THE CHEST…. CLUSTER VERTICAL SUPLEX INTO A DDT! Dormant is calling in Pulsar! Pulsar picks Hart up…. HE NAILS HIM WITH THE BLACK HOLE! Luster is getting up… HE PICKS HART UP ON HIS SHOULDERS…. HE NAILS HIM WITH THE FUNK AND WAGNALLS! Luster makes the cover and Pulsar and Dormant help to cover him! The ref goes down to make the count! 1-------------------------------------------2--------------------------3!
::huge crowd pop:: RING ANNOUNCER: The winners of the match, the team of LUSTER, PULSAR, AND DORMANT! BRANDON JACOBS: My god, they did it! VIC WATERS: Why didn’t the ref attempt to get Pulsar and Dormant out of the ring? BRANDON JACOBS: The referee may have been showing mercy on Hart because this match could have gotten a whole lot worse for him if he was forced to fight these three men alone. This was a hell of a victory and just like I predicted, the “underdogs” walked out victorious.
VIC WATERS: You have to give it to the team of Luster, Pulsar, and Dormant, unlike their opponents, they worked like a team and went all out there to prove themselves as three young talents on the rise. This is Pulsar’s second huge victory in six man action.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is a future champion and when I see him in these six man’s, you see how big he comes up in pressure situations. But not only that… Luster who did get robbed in his match with Hart scored the pinfall victory tonight over Hart. Hart is going to have to regroup over the next month and a half and get back on the right track if he wants to stay champion.
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart has a tough road. I don’t envy him. BRANDON JACOBS: Okay folks, when we comeback, El Arco Iris will try to get revenge for his friend Adam Benjamin when he faces off with the monster MINION! STAY TUNED!
(BACKSTAGE) ::a dejected Shawn Hart walks into the locker room and runs into Minion, both men engage in a staredown.... Minion walks past Shawn Hart and say "You suck." Shawn Hart nails Minion in the back of the head with the belt busting him open. Minion gets up and licks the blood off his fingers and smiles. Minion grabs Hart by the throat and throws him into the wall, laughs, then walks away::
