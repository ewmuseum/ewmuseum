WFW — Card

Vox WFW World Heayweight Title Final Manson vs. Silver vs.

1 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 1 April 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Finally, after all the buildup, it is time for the main event, which has been three shows in the making! Michael Manson, Shawn Hart, and Doc Silver will fight in out in an epic battle to determine the two champions which will be the mold for all the stars that come! The World Heavyweight champion and the North American champion will alternate title defenses from show to show, only defending the title on the same show live on PPV every four or five shows. Who will be the two men who will have to take the responsibility to shoulder this company on their own back and forge their own legacies?
VIC WATERS: Doc Silver and some other guy.... BRANDON JACOBS: Man, you are such a shill. Doc this, Doc that. Manson and Hart are going to put Doc in his place tonight and you'll have your tail between your legs by the time this is over.
VIC WATERS: As a great champion once said, "Always believe". BRANDON JACOBS: Let's send this match down to ringside for the ring introductions! :: Only Happy When It Rains by Garbage wails over the loud speaker :: RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from Orlando, Florida, one of the greatest legends and a 4 time AAWC World Champion, DOC SILVER!
:: Doc Silver walks out to the loud boos from the crowd and enters the ring :: :: I Touch Myself" by the Divinyls plays over the loud speakers :: RING ANNOUNCER: His opponent, from Orlando, Florida! The current eWu World Heavyweight Champion, "The Phenom" Shawn Hart!
:: Shawn Hart walks down to the ring accompanied down to the ring by his bodyguard Tiny, Hart enters the ring ::
:: "Mechanical Animals" by Marilyn Manson blares on the loud speakers :: RING ANNOUCER: And finally, from Chicago, IL! Former BAD World Heavyweight Champion, the man, the myth, the legend... MICHAEL MANSON!
:: Big pop from the crowd as Manson comes out and makes his way down to the ring :: BRANDON JACOBS: All three men are finally in the ring. The ref calls for the bell, and the match is on!
:: SFX: bell rings :: BRANDON JACOBS: And all three men are off to a tentative start trying to see who will make the first move…Manson and Hart are eying each other, and it’s Manson and Hart as they double-team Silver with a massive double-clothesline! Manson stands over Silver and begins taunting him to get up. Manson slaps begins to slap Silver around. Hart is standing there with a big grin on his faces, taking the opportunity to repeatedly kick Silver in the legs.
VIC WATERS: I wouldn’t put it past either of them to trust the enemy simply for the sake of getting after Doc Silver.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson gets into the face of Hart and tells him he wants Silver all to himself, Hart gives him the finger and Manson rakes Hart in the eyes! The referee warns Manson, but he is ignoring the warnings of the referee and laying into the now doubled-over Hart with closed fist punches to the ribs. Hart falls to the floor as Manson tears in with some really stiff kicks to the ribs!
VIC WATERS: Manson really isn't holding anything back, you can hear those kicks all the way up here in the booth. It just shows you how much Manson wants to be the very first WFW World Heavyweight Champion.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson goes to the ropes…and comes off with a BASEBALL SLIDE DROPKICK RIGHT INTO THE SIDE OF HART! The momentum sends Shawn Hart back first into the steel guardrail around ringside! Shawn Hart reach back and grabs his back! He looks like he is in a tremendous deal of pain!
VIC WATERS: Your back is one of the most crucial parts of your body because it helps the support the weight on your body and if you are injured, it can seriously limit your mobility. Working on Shawn Hart's back is a good idea because he has tremendous speed and by taking a few shows, Manson & Silver can slow down the pace of the match and take Hart out of his element.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson gets back up off the mat.. BUT SILVER GETS BACK UP AND HITS MANSON WITH A BOOT TO THE FACE BEFORE HE CAN CAPITALIZE! Silver is back in the game.
VIC WATERS: Brandon, I am disappointed in you. Doc Silver is a legend of wrestling. He has kicked out of many a wrestler's STRONGEST FINISHERS. You think he can be put out of commission simply by a double-team?
BRANDON JACOBS: There’s no doubting the toughness of Silver, but that was still a devastating double-clothesline. Manson is rubbing his jaw which caught force of that shot dead on. MANSON GETS BACK UP TO HIS FEET AND DOC SILVER AND MANSON ARE EYE TO EYE! Shawn Hart is on the outside taking his time to get back into the ring.
VIC WATERS: You can see fear in the eyes of Manson. He knows he’s been beaten by this man and can be beaten by him again. He’s just afraid, plain and simple.
BRANDON JACOBS: Now I know you are on drugs, Manson doesn't sweat anyone. He is one of the toughest wrestlers ever to walk into that ring. Manson and Doc start trading knife edge chops back and forth! Manson isn't really holding anything back! Manson grabs Silver's Garbage t-shirt and rips it off of his body! He doesn't want the shirt to dampen the impact of his strikes!
VIC WATERS: You do not desecrate Silver's garbage t-shirt. That is rule #1. Manson better remember that payback is a #####!
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson throws the t-shirt to the crowd! Silver is getting really angry.... SILVER NAILS MANSON WITH A FOREARM SMASH! THEN ANOTHER! AND ANOTHER! Manson is sent reeling backwards! Silver is setting up for a superkick! MANSON DUCKS THE KICK.... DDT BY SILVER!
VIC WATERS: Manson has suffered Doc's wrath and it is only going to get worse for him. BRANDON JACOBS: Silver pulls Manson up off the mat... MANSON ROLLS DOC SILVER UP IN A SMALL PACKAGE! 1----------------2---------------KICKOUT! Manson and Silver standup and start jaw jacking in each other’s face! Meanwhile, Hart looks like he has finally recovered attack from Manson, he reenters the ring CLOTHESLINES BOTH MEN DOWN! SIMULTANEOUSLY! Hart hooks the leg and covers Manson 1----------------2-----------------KICKOUT! I guess neither of the three are going to be put down too easy tonight, Vic. All three men are back up, and we’re back to them testing the waters, waiting to see who will attack again!
VIC WATERS: Hart is so tragically funny as he thinks he has a chance. Answer me this, Brandon. Would you want someone who holds a paper title like the eWu world championship, and who comes out to the song “I Touch Myself” to be your world champion? Hmmm?
BRANDON JACOBS: I won’t bother to question your musical tastes. Now Hart tries ANOTHER double clothesline, but both Manson and Silver catch his arms! Now Manson and Silver hook his head, and hit Hart with a double-team vertical suplex! Hart has got to be feeling it in his back and in his ribs right now! Now Silver and Manson are both laying into his chest with VICIOUS boots. But now right in the midst of the kicking Manson VAULTS over Hart’s prone form and hits Silver with a flying shoulder tackle!
VIC WATERS: Bah! Of course Manson wouldn’t want to fight FAIR and face a man who was READY to fight!
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson now mounts Silver and begins to punch him in the face! :: SFX: Crowd counting along with Manson’s punching :: BRANDON JACOBS: …6, 7, 8, 9, 10! Silver looks like he doesn’t know where hs is. Manson then turns Silver over and locks him into a camel clutch! He has him in tight! Manson wrenches on the neck of Silver hoping to make him submit and possibly become the first World Champion of the WFW.
VIC WATERS: Here’s another clown I wouldn’t even want to touch the title! He thinks he’s God, or Satan, or some other deity incarnate, but we KNOW Silver is TRULY of divine power!
BRANDON JACOBS: He’s not looking so divine right now as Manson has him reaching for the ropes in hope of making him release that camel clutch…But Hart comes right behind Manson and YANKS him off Silver! Hart just won’t stay down! He hits the surprised Manson with a diving reverse DDT! Hart goes after Silver! Silver is grabbing his neck.... SMALL PACKAGE BY HART ON SILVER! 1------------------2-------------KICKOUT! How did Silver kickout of that?
VIC WATERS: Instinct, when you have been in wrestling as long as Doc has, kicking out simply becomes second nature. Doc even has a hard time jobbing to sleep.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart starts laying into him with discus punches! Silver can barely stand now! HE IS CALLING TO THE FANS FOR HIS FINISHER! El Codo Explosivo! DOC SEES IT COMING AND FALLS DOWN TO THE MAT FOR MERE SURVIVAL!
VIC WATERS: Nothing wrong with falling down to the ground to avoid if it can save you the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart sneaks up behind Silver... Silver slowly gets up wondering where Hart has gone.... HE SEES HART!
VIC WATERS: Oh no! BRANDON JACOBS: HART WITH A KICK TO THE GUT OF SILVER! We all know what this is setting him up for... HART ATTACK TIME! THIS COULD BE IT! .... NO! Silver pushes Hart off him and into the waiting arms of Manson!
VIC WATERS: Didn’t I tell you? Silver wouldn’t let a mere mortal like Hart hit HIM with his finisher, no matter how little effect it would have on him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Should I build my altar now or later? Manson has Hart hooked for a belly-to-belly right next to the ropes, but Hart switches, and BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEXES MANSON OUT OF THE RING! Hart goes right after Silver again, and they lock up, Silver seeming to get the better of Hart, getting him in a side headlock, then hooking the far arm, and turning it into an abdominal stretch! Hart is screaming in agony as the rib area is being stretched like a rubber band!
VIC WATERS: As a great champion also once said, FEEL THE POWER!!! BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is checking to see if Hart will submit, Hart says no, despite the excruciating pain. Silver now starts delivering blows to the ribs while he still has that abdominal stretch locked in! Manson’s back in the ring now…
VIC WATERS: Let’s see what exercise in futility he has planned NOW. TURN AROUND DOC! MANSON IS BEHIND YOU!
BRANDON JACOBS: And Manson delivers an enziguri kick RIGHT TO THE HEAD OF SILVER! Silver releases the abdominal stretch…Hart is now getting up slowly, using the ropes for support, and favoring his rib area. Manson goes after Hart and tries for a kick to the gut, but Hart catches his foot! Hart now spins Manson around, and THROWS MANSON BACK OUT OF THE RING WITH AN EXPLODER SUPLEX!
VIC WATERS: He’ll be lucky if he can pull a fancy stunt like that on Silver… BRANDON JACOBS: Silver is now back up, grabs Hart from behind, but Hart executes a standing switch! Silver then executes a standing switch of his own! Hart switches again! BACKDROP DRIVER FROM HART! HART JUST HIT SILVER WITH THE BACKDROP DRIVER! Hart is on FIRE, but Manson pulls Silver out of the ring before Hart can cover!
VIC WATERS: Hmmm…You know, I first thought Manson was nothing but an ingrate, but I think he’s finally showing the respect to Doc he deserves!
:: SFX: crowd now popping HUGE for Hart as he taunts Manson and Silver :: BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is now feeding off the crowds energy, waiting for either man to come back in the ring and deliver the coup de gras. Manson is consoling Silver apparently…but now simply slaps Silver and throws him back into the ring!
VIC WATERS: THAT UNGRATEFUL LITTLE SCAB! He will soon have to face Silver’s wrath for this sin!
BRANDON JACOBS: Well, we both know there is no such thing as compassion or friendship inside this ring. Hart now picks up Silver, kicks him in the gut, and sets him up for the Hart Attack! But NO! Manson is now back in the ring and charges Hart with a forearm to the face! It was all just a distraction! Doc Silver cowers in the corner having now been saved from the Hart Attack by Manson!
VIC WATERS: Treating a DEITY like a mere piece of bait? That’s the ULTIMATE transgression. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson and Hart are trading blows, Manson with a fierce jab to the ribs! Hart is now doubled over in sheer agony!
VIC WATERS: Manson thinks he’s such hot stuff now, doesn’t he? BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON NAILS HART WITH THE SWEET DREAMS! BUT SILVER BOLTS OUT OF THE CORNER AND CLOTHESLINES MANSON OVER THE TOP!
VIC WATERS: Doc is showing why he is the smartest wrestler in the sport today. BRANDON JACOBS: DOC SILVER COVERS THE UNCONCIOUS SHAWN HART! WAIT, SOMEBODY IS HOLDING MANSON'S LEGS AND HE CAN'T GET IN TO BREAK UP THE FALL! The ref goes down for the count! 1--------------2-----------------3!!!!
VIC WATERS: DOC WINS! DOC WINS! DOC WINS! RING ANNOUNCER: THE RUNNER UP AND NEW WORLD'S FINEST WRESTLING NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION... MICHAEL MANSON!
THE WINNER AND NEW WORLD'S FINEST WRESTLING HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION.... DOC SILVER! ::"Only Happy When It Rains" blares over the loud speaker:: BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON HAS BEEN ROBBED! An irate Manson pulls the mystery man from underneath the ring.... IT IS RAT FINK! HE MUST HAVE SNUCK UNDER THE RING DURING THE POWER OUTAGE! Manson and Rat Fink are brawling to the back! They will face each other in a week and a half at WFW Shock and Awe at the Pepsi Arena in Albany, New York for the WFW North American Title!
VIC WATERS: Doc Silver as champ and a great night of action, all is good with the world, on behalf of my partner who is having a heart attack and I, goodnight everyone!
