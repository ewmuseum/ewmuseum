HEIGHT: 6'3 WEIGHT: 213 HOMETOWN: nowhere ENTRANCE MUSIC: "Lux Aeterna" by Clint Mansell and the Kronus Quartet FIVE SIGNATURE MOVES: moonsault into a DDT tornado DDT spinning heel kick springboard bicycle kick twisting leg drop SETUP: for E of the A: spinning back kick for the O of the S H: diving reverse DDT FINISHER: Ecstasy of the Agony: It starts off like the Angle Slam, but Felix does a half turn while he's dropping the dood, so it ends like the dominator... if the doods too big... A Shattered Horizon: Twisting Swanton Bomb.. I guess he could do one after the other as well.. WHO YOUR CHARACTER IS: He started off as one of the bah-gillions of guys in the Anime Brigade, the dancing platinum painted Robot Boy. Then he, Toxick "Whitedust" narcotic and Midiot broke off to form the Crack Rock Crowd in IWF. Felix changed his name and gimmick to the slightly more dignified "robot," who was supposed to be a rip off of Keanue Reeves character in the Matrix. Felix took the philosophy that the world as he knew it was a fallacy to heart. He started randomly switching identities. Over the course of a year he was George W. Bush, Mr. Exfixiation, the Cock, and finally just eX. Eventually, Midiot began to overshadow the bejesus out of Toxick and Felix, so they turned on him and went to the WWL to join the new incarnation of the anime brigade, or "light" brigade. Felix operates under the assumption that every thing around him is his own psychotic hallucination, that Nothing is real in that nothing exists outside his own perception, he desires chaos so that’s what he’s surrounded by, and anything can be destroyed and remade in his own image if he focuses on it enough. The resulting wrestling style reckless high flying moves mixed with martial arts “in which he’s well
Versed,” And the occasional innovative grapple. Picture ah...RVD meets Hyabussa meets X-Pac.
