WFW Vox Part Deux: Superbowl Lineup Annouced, Old Man Revealed

31 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 31 March 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: Thank god we got back in time. Folks, I’m getting word from the back that one of the four old men on the board of the directors will be here tonight, IN THAT VERY RING, to announce the lineup making a one time appearance.
VIC WATERS: That is huge, huge news since the four old men value their secrecy. BRANDON JACOBS: The board of directors never wanted to let their presence upstage the wrestlers in front of the camera by introducing themselves as on air talent, but they felt that this was such a very special moment that one of them had to break the veil of secrecy and come to the show tonight to announce the lineup themselves.
VIC WATERS: This must be a hell of a lineup for one of them to do that. BRANDON JACOBS: I’ve just heard rumors about this lineup but you know how rumors are, until the matches are announced, you don’t know what is official and what is not. There is only one way to find out…. LET’S SEND THIS ONE DOWN TO RINGSIDE!
(RINGSIDE) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen, may I have your attention please, it my honor and privledge to announce one of the four old men on the board of directors, put your hands together for the FORMER MFL WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION CHRIS WINK!
(CUEUP: “One Shot At Glory” By Judas Priests) BRANDON JACOBS: Whoa. ::the fans stand on their feet and give Wink a standing ovation as Wink walks down to ringside with a clipboard::
CHRIS WINK: Thank you everyone, it is an honor to be here in front of all you great fans in Revere, Massachusetts. It is great to be back at the place which BAD called home for so many years.
CROWD: BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! CHRIS WINK: [detail withheld] Okay, I won’t keep you guys waiting any longer…. IT IS TIME TO ANNOUNCE THE LINEUP FOR THE SUPERBOWL OF WRESTLING!
CROWD: Superbowl! Superbowl! Superbowl! CHRIS WINK: We are proud to announce that we will make a return to where it all began… Foxboro, Massachusetts. But thankfully it won’t be at the ****hole which looked like a poor college stadium where we held it the first time, no, it will be at the much improved Gillette Stadium and you’ll actually be able to park.
*big pop* CHRIS WINK: Our first match of the evening will be a three way elimination match. A man after my own heart, the undefeated seven foot three Iron Lion will take on the ring technician Larry Tact and the ever energetic El Arco Iris.
CROWD: IRIS! IRIS! IRIS! IRIS! CHRIS WINK: Our second match of the show will be a match that would headline any other show. The former WFW World Heavyweight Champion Shawn Hart and our greatest champion to date will come out of retirement to face the man whose devious planning ended his title reign and won him the WFW World Heavyweight Title, “The Viewer’s Choice” Scotty Michaels. In order to finally settle this once and for all, Luster will be BANNED from the building.
*crowd pops* CHRIS WINK: Our third match of the show will be for the BAD World Heavyweight Title vacated by Minion who tore a ligament in his leg while winning the title at Edison Field after running through a wall. Rat Fink, Felix Red, Pulsar, Jared Wells, Good Eddie, Mighty Impala, the debuting Alessandro Giovanni, and a special mystery opponent in a Falls Count Anywhere In Gillette Stadium match.
CROWD: BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! CHRIS WINK: In the first of three matches against outsiders from other promotions as a test by Wylde and the Board of Directors to see who is and who isn’t worthy of a title shot at the WFW World Heavyweight Title, two of the biggest men in all of wrestling will face off. WFW’s own Copycat will square off against the current CSWA World Heavyweight Champion Dan Ryan.
*big pop* CHRIS WINK: In the second of three matches, Former WFW World Heavyweight Champion Sean Edmunds will face off against the greatest technical wrestler alive today and a man he has never beaten, Jean Rabesque!
*big pop* CHRIS WINK: And now a match that I’m very proud to announce, the WFW Board of Directors has been trying desperately to do this match for over six months now but it will finally happen at the Superbowl of Wrestling… The WFW North American Champion Richard Gideon will put his title on the line against Psycho in a two out of three falls match, each with different stipulations!
CROWD: GIDEON! GIDEON! GIDEON! CHRIS WINK: As if that was not enough, folks, you are going to have to sit down for this next announcement. After a good two months of negotiations, the WFW has signed a dream match that has eluded several promotions that is over four years in the making…. ANARKY WILL FACE OFF AGAINST THE MAN WHO I CONSIDER WHO HAS TAKEN THE LIKES OF MANSON, RABESQUE, AND MARX TO THEIR LIMIT AND ONE OF THE FEW MEN WHO CAN GO TOE TO TOE WITH ANARKY IN A FIGHT, THE FORMER GLCW WORLD CHAMPION MAELSTROM IN A DOUBLE STEEL CAGE MATCH!
CROWD: MAELSTROM! MAELSTROM! MAELSTROM! MAELSTROM! MAELSTROM! CHRIS WINK: Settle down folks, settle down. I’m excited to see him too especially with the way Anarky has been running his mouth lately. I hope Copycat gives him hell tonight.
*big pop* CHRIS WINK: And last but not least, the WFW World Heavyweight Champion Alex Wylde will put up the title against Michael Manson…. But there will be two special stiupaltions… CSWA LEGEND JOEY MELTON WILL BE THE SPECIAL REFEREE….
*big pop* CHRIS WINK: Joey Melton will be there to make sure in order to make sure that Wylde and his group of thugs won’t play a factor in the match. But in order to make sure there is a clear winner, at the request of Alex Wylde…. THE WFW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE WILL BE SUSPENDED FIFTEEN FEET IN THE AIR AND IT WILL BE A LADDER MATCH!
CROWD: LADDER! LADDER! LADDER! CHRIS WINK: The first man who climbs to the top of the ladder and retrieves the WFW World Heavyweight Title will be the champion. I’d like to wish both men the best of luck.
*pop* CHRIS WINK: On behalf of the board of directors, I’d like to thank all of the fans who have supported us all throughout our first year and we promise that year two will be even better. With the talented group of wrestlers we have in the back which impossibly seems to get better and better as we go on, we promise that the best is yet to come. Thank you everyone, enjoy the rest of the show.
CROWD: THANK YOU! THANK YOU! THANK YOU! (ANNOUNCERS BOOTH) BRANDON JACOBS: Damn, how are we going to top next year’s Superbowl of Wrestling with this lineup?
VIC WATERS: We will cross that bridge when we come to it. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, when we comeback, it is time for WarGames: SUBMIT OR SURRENDER….. STAY TUNED!
