WFW Shock & Awe: Starr/Bradford vs. Luster/Eian 1st Fall

18 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 18 April 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back from commercial break and our next match up is the team of Luster & Andre Eian taking on the team of Chris Bradford & Johnny Starr in a Best Two Out of Three falls match. I can't wait.
VIC WATERS: I haven't seen a good old fashioned multiple fall match in a long, long time. I'm kind of excited.
BRANDON JACOBS: Let's send this one to ringside! :: Killed or be Killed, by Mobb Deep blares over the loud speakers:: RING ANNOUNCER: Our first team, weigh in at over 525 pounds, the lethal combination of Chris Bradford and Johnny Starr!
::Starr & Bradford come out to the booing of the crowd:: ::"Bawitdaba" by Kid Rock blares over the loud speakers:: RING ANNOUNCER: And their opponents, weighing in at 500 pounds, the team of ANDRE EIAN AND LUSTER!
::Eian and Luster run down to the ring and Starr & Bradford meet them fighting inside of the ring::
BEST TWO OUT OF THREE FALLS: ANDRE EIAN & LUSTER vs. JOHNNY STARR & CHRIS BRADFORD *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: JOHNNY STARR GRABS ANDRE EIAN BY THE THROAT AND SLAMS HIM TO THE FLOOR OUTSIDE! EIAN SEEMS TO HAVE LANDED HARD ON HIS KNEE!
VIC WATERS: That was incredibly vicious slam, Eian is going to be limited in what he can do in this match from now on.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is trying to get Johnny Starr to go to his corner to stop the stomping on the part of Starr & Bradford. Starr puts up his arms and tells the ref that he is willing.
VIC WATERS: What a display of sportsmanship! BRANDON JACOBS: Bradford picks Luster off the mat... SNAP SUPLEX! Luster landed hard on his back! Bradford sets him up again... ANOTHER SNAP SUPLEX! Luster is grabbing his lower back!
VIC WATERS: Bradford needs to take advantage of Luster's back here to prevent him from being able to kickout later in the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Bradford turns Luster over on his stomach.... he puts his knee in Luster's lower back and pulls both arms back! The ref is checking to see if Luster is willing to give up but he keeps on shaking his head no.
VIC WATERS: This Luster guy is tough, I'll give him that. BRANDON JACOBS: Bradford relinquishes the hold! Luster tries to crawl to the ropes to get up but Bradford grabs him by the back of his silver trunks! LUSTER LOW BLOWS BRADFORD WITH HIS HEEL TO HIS LOWER EXTREMITIES! Ref didn't see it!
VIC WATERS: Luster has used this opportunity to finally get to the ropes and he has gotten himself back to his feet.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster bounces off the ropes... DROPKICK TO THE SHIN OF BRADFORD! Bradford falls down to one knee! Luster bounces off the ropes... KNEE LIFT TO THE FACE! Bradford goes down!
VIC WATERS: Bradford is groggy, he got his clocked cleaned good on that knee lift. BRANDON JACOBS: Bradford slowly getting up.... LUSTER LOCKS ON THE SLEEPER! HE HAS IT IN TIGHT! Bradfords' arms are wailing like a rag doll! VIC WATERS: WAIT! JOHNNY STARR IS COMING IN TO TRY TO BREAK IT UP! NO! THE REF PUT HIS BODY IN THE WAY AND IS TELLING STARR TO GO BACK TO HIS CORNER!
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster shifted his arm and he is now CHOKING BRADFORD! BRADFORD IS REALLY GASPING FOR AIR!
VIC WATERS: Johnny Starr sees what is happening and SIDETRACKS THE REF AND NAILS LUSTER WITH A BOOT TO THE HEAD! HE BREAKS THE CHOKE!
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is issuing a warning to Johnny Starr & Bradford that if they keep this up, they will get DQed.
VIC WATERS: Typical blind ref who has no clue what is going on around him... BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is putting Johnny Starr out of the ring. Bradford is working Luster over with punches.... WHOA! WHAT HAPPENED?!? Luster has fallen to the ground and is grabbing his crotch?
VIC WATERS: I didn't see anything either. Is there a replay or something? BRANDON JACOBS: The ref turns around is checking on Luster... THE REF IS CALLING FOR THE BELL! The ref is going to meet with the ring announcer, we should have a clarification rather shortly.
RING ANNOUNCER: JOHNNY STARR & CHRIS BRADFORD HAVE BEEN DISQUALIFIED FOR ILLEGAL USE OF THE NUT SHOT!
::Crowd goes nuts for the scam Luster pulled:: VIC WATERS: LUSTER IS A GENIUS! BRANDON JACOBS: Oh man, this crowd looks like it is ready to riot, we will be back for the second fall after this commercial break!
