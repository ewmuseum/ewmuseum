WFW: Spirit of NGEN: Richard Gideon vs. Sean Edmunds

19 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 19 August 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: It is time for Sean Edmunds to make his debut against “The System” Richard Gideon! This match is one of the few matches which doen’t have any history behind it. Psycho who was upset with himself for plastering Gideon so he exiled himself on an extended vacation leaving Richard Gideon high and dry without an opponent for the Spirit of NGEN show. So WFW started scouring the globe to find someone who could possibly fill the ex-WFW World Heavyweight Champions shoes, and that man was the one, the only “Simply Sensational” Sean Edmunds.
VIC WATERS: Sean Edmunds is a legend in this sport, and as tough as Psycho is, as he showed in the cage match against Doc, I think WFW did a hell of a job of finding someone to take his place for the PPV.
BRANDON JACOBS: I think so, it should be interesting to see if Sean Edmunds can hang with Richard Gideon. I mean, Richard Gideon is the man who took Doc Silver to his limit. Let’s send this one to ringside!
(CUEUP: “Remember” by Disturbed) RING ANNOUCER: Introducing first, from Buffalo, NY, THE SYSTEM RICHARD GIDEON! ::Gideon comes down to the ring to the roar of the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “Brain Stew” by Green Day) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, making his WFW debut here tonight, from Boston, Massaschutes, “SIMPLY SENSATIONAL” SEAN EDMUNDS!
::Sean Edmunds comes out to the boos of the crowd and enters the ring:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds and Gideon lock up… Gideon locks Edmunds into a front face lock. GIDEON STARTS NAILING EDMUNDS WITH EUROPEAN UPPERCUTS TO THE CHEST! Edmunds manages to push Gideon away… they lock up again… GIDEON GETS THE SIDE HEADLOCK!
VIC WATERS: The youngster is taking the veteran to school but I doubt it will last. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is trying to wiggle himself out of it…. EDMUNDS GRABS GIDEON AROUND THE WAIST AND NAILS HIM WITH A BACK DROP! Gideon refuses to break!
VIC WATERS: Gideon is showing incredible strength here to keep on that side headlock despite taking a shot like that to the back of the head!
BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS THRUSTS HIS BODY UP WHILE IN THE SIDE HEADLOCK TO COVER GIDEON! The ref goes down to make the count! 1-----------2----- KICKOUT! Gideon breaks the headlock and Edmunds is point to his head!
VIC WATERS: Gideon is looking at Edmunds with a sarcastic smile. These two are trying to one up each other left and right.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is extending his hands for another lockup…. Edmunds comes in… KNEE TO STOMACH! Gideon falls down to one knee! What a dirty trick!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is all about winning. He doesn’t care how he does it. He just wants to put this boy in his place.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds locks in a waistlock and lifts Gideon off the ground and over his shoulder! EDMUNDS IS PUSHING DOWN WITH ALL HIS STRENGTH ONTO GIDEON’S MIDSECTION TRYING TO ROB HIM OVER HIS WIND!
VIC WATERS: This is a really clever bit of wrestling by Edmunds, Gideon has the youth advantage here and if he could knock out his stamina, he could take the ball out of Gideon’s court.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is checking to see if Gideon wants to give up…. Gideon keeps on shaking his head no…
VIC WATERS: The crowd here is trying to get Gideon going but unless they can pry open Edmunds’ hands, Gideon is in trouble.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds decides to break the hold and he drops Gideon back first onto the mat! The winded Gideon grabs his back!
VIC WATERS: He is suffering on both ends right now! BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS STARTS STOMPING AWAY ON THE STOMACH OF GIDEON! The fans here are starting to heavily boo Edmunds as he lays the boots in!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is a vicious, vicious competitor. He is willing to do anything to win. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds picks up Gideon and drags him to the corner… BODYSLAM! Edmunds is going up to the top!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is going for his moonsault! If he hits the already winded Gideon in the guts, he may be able to get the win here.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is perched on the top rope…. HE NAILS THE MOONSAULT AND BOUNCES OFF! Gideon is writhing around in pain! Edmunds pulls himself over to Gideon and makes the pin! 1----------------------------2------------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: After all that Gideon has taken, I’m shocked he was able to kick out of that. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds picks Gideon off the mat… HE NAILS HIM WITH A STANDING DROPKICK IN THE GUT! The momentum causes Gideon to fall through the ropes and to the floor!
VIC WATERS: He may have kicked Gideon a bit too hard there, now he’ll have to get him back in the ring to pin him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds goes to the outside and Gideon is crawling away… Edmunds catches up to Gideon… HE LIFTS GIDEON UP AND DROPS HIM MIDSECTION FIRST ONTO THE STAIRS! GOOD HEAVENS! HE COULD HAVE BROKEN A RIB ON THAT!
VIC WATERS: The ref needs to step in here to make sure Edmunds doesn’t go too far. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds throws Gideon into the ring and rolls back in after him. The ref is going to check on Gideon to see if he is okay. EDMUNDS PUSHES THE REF OUT OF THE WAY! HE LIFTS OF GIDEON BY THE HAIR…. HE SMACKS HIM ACROSS THE FACE!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is just adding insult to injury here, I’m loving it.. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is still standing…. EDMUNDS SLAPS HIM AGAIN WITH ANOTHER RIGHT HAND ACROSS THE FACE! GIDEON IS MOTIONING FOR EDMUNDS TO HIT HIM AGAIN!
VIC WATERS: Damn, this Gideon is a glutton for punishment. BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS FIRES BACK FOR A SLAP BUT GIDEON CATCHES HIS HAND! GIDEON FIRES AWAY WITH A SERIES OF RIGHTS TO EDMUNDS RIGHT IN THE FACE! Edmunds is beginning to wobble!
VIC WATERS: Where is Gideon getting this burst of energy? BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON WHIPS EDMUNDS OFF THE ROPES… KICK TO THE STOMACH BY GIDEON… HE GRABS THE ARM OF EDMUNDS…. GORDBUSTER BY GIDEON! But Gideon is grabbing his midsection… he is still sensitive from all of the abuse he has taken earlier!
VIC WATERS: I must give Gideon credit, he has a lot of heart, but he needs to becareful what he does until he heals up.
BRANDON JACOBS: BOTH MEN ARE DOWN AND THE REF IS PUTTING ON A DOUBLE COUNT! 1-----2----3----4----5----6----7…. EDMUNDS IS BACK TO HIS FEET FIRST AND GIDEON IS SLOWLY GETTING UP AFTER HIM!
VIC WATERS: That was a hell of a gordbuster to take Edmunds out like that. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds goes for a clothesline… GIDEON DUCKS! Gideon kicks him in the stomach… HE LIFTS HIM UP… PILEDRIVER! He didn’t quite him with all of it but Edmunds is still dazed.
VIC WATERS: That is going to come back to haunt Edmunds if Gideon ever has a chance to lock in his STF Final Judgment.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon pulls Edmunds over to the corner… HE IS GOING FOR THE BUFFALO BOMB… EDMUNDS TRIES TO FIGHT IT…. GIDEON NAILS THE SECOND ROPE PILEDRIVER! OH MY!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds fought all he could on that, but it was over no use, Gideon is determined not to lose this match at any cost.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon goes for the cover! 1------------------2-------------KICKOUT! Edmunds barely got his shoulder up!
VIC WATERS: It was closer to a two count than a three, but Edmunds needs to becareful or Gideon is going to establish momentum if things continue to go like they have in the last five minutes.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is going up to the top rope… Edmunds is starting to wake up… GIDEON PERCHES ON THE TOP…. 450 INTO A FROG SPLASH! Oh no! Gideon bounced off and bumped the ref! It looks like he may have hurt his leg!
VIC WATERS: This is just not Gideon’s night. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon makes the cover! He doesn’t see the ref is down! The crowd is count to three but nobody is there to count it!
VIC WATERS: You have to constantly be aware of where the ref is in that ring, pinfalls don’t count unless there is a ref there to count them.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon sees the ref is down and goes to help him up…. EDMUNDS FROM BEHIND NAILS HIM WITH A LOW BLOW! Edmunds with the roll up! The ref makes the count! 1--------------------2--------------KICKOUT! GIDEON BARELY KICKED OUT OF THAT!
VIC WATERS: The ref is limping around, it doesn’t look good for him as an enforcer of the rules in this match. The guys will be able to do whatever they want at will.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds and Gideon are both slowly getting up… EDMUNDS GOES FOR A REVERSE CRESCENT KICK…. GIDEON DUCKS AND THE REF TAKES IT SQUARE IN THE JAW! Edmunds stands over the ref trying to wake him up and he is upset with himself!
VIC WATERS: He should be. He just had a close pinfall on Gideon and he went and accidently knocked the ref out.
BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON PUTS EDMUNDS IN A WAISTLOCK FROM BEHIND! GIDEON CLUTCH GERMAN SUPLEX INTO A LEGLOCK! Gideon has Edmunds in the center of the ring and he is tapping! NO REF!
VIC WATERS: Maybe it is a good thing Edmunds knocked the ref out… BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon breaks the hold… HE PICKS EDMUNDS UP…. THE G-FORCE ROLLING BRAINBUSTER SUPEX COMBO! HE NAILS ONE… HE PICKS HIM UP AGAIN… HE NAILS A SECOND… HE PICKS HIM UP A THIRD TIME…. TRIFECTA! Edmunds is out cold on the mat!
VIC WATERS: Gideon is breathing hard, he may be on the offensive, but his wind isn’t what it usually is from all the punishment that Edmunds inflected earlier in the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is trying to wake up the ref… the ref is starting come through finally. Gideon goes for a cover! 1------------------------------------------2-------------------------KICKOUT! Edmunds kicked out of one of the slowest three counts of alltime!
VIC WATERS: Must be one of those refs from one of the senior leagues. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon starts stomping away on the right leg of Edmunds to setup the STF! VIC WATERS: That is what we call tenderizing the meat before we cook it. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon picks up the leg… HE DRIVES IT HARD DOWN INTO THE MAT! Edmunds may have hurt his knee on that one!
VIC WATERS: The mat can be very unforgiving. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon pulls Edmunds into the center of the ring… HE LOCKS IN THE FIGURE FOUR LEGLOCK! EDMUNDS IS FLAILING HIS ARMS AROUND!
VIC WATERS: I’ve been in a figure four and they hurt like hell. If feels like your leg is being made into a twig and can break at any second.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is checking to see if Edmunds wants to give it up but he keeps on shaking his head no! GIDEON IS SUMMONING ALL HIS STRENGTH TO APPLY ALL OF THE PRESSURE HE CAN INTO THE FIGURE FOUR!
VIC WATERS: Even if Edmunds escapes this hold, he is toast because he won’t be able to walk.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is screaming… the ref goes down to check to see if he wants to give up… EDMUNDS GRABS THE REF AROUND THE NECK AND WON’T LET GO! Gideon breaks the hold and peels the ref away from Edmunds! The ref is trying to regain his composure.
VIC WATERS: The ref in this match is being very lenient and letting these two go at it to find who really is the better man.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon goes to pickup Edmunds… EYE GOUGE FOLLOWED BY A SMALL PACKAGE BY EDMUNDS! The ref goes down to make the count! 1------------------2-------------KICKOUT! GIDEON BARELY KICKED OUT OF THAT!
VIC WATERS: A match can end that quickly, it only takes three seconds to beat someone so anything can happen on any given day.
BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON KICKS EDMUNDS IN THE FACE! Gideon picks Edmunds off the mat… SWINGING NECKBREAKER! Edmunds is grabbing his neck! Gideon is calling for the Final Judgment STF! WAIT A SECOND, EDMUNDS IS CRAWLING AWAY AS QUICK AS POSSIBLE TO GET AWAY! Gideon turns around and Edmunds has already made it to the floor!
VIC WATERS: You can’t take your eye off of your opponent even for a second. It is a classic example of a veteran take advantage of a mistake by a younger wrestler.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon goes after Edmunds and goes to the outside… GIDEON GRABS THE LIMPING EDMUNDS BY THE HAIR AND THROWS HIM OVER THE RINGSIDE TABLE NEARLY INTO THE LAP OF THE TIME KEEPER! Gideon walks over to pick up Edmunds… EDMUNDS NAILS GIDEON WITH THE HAMMER USED TO RING THE BELL! Gideon has been busted open!
VIC WATERS: I guess you could say Gideon had his bell rung. BRANDON JACOBS: If you make another one of those stupid puns, so help me I’m going to have you replace by Sirus.
VIC WATERS: Fine, fine, we don’t need to go and ruin the league by doing that. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref didn’t see it! But the ref is coming out to make things don’t get further out of control… EDMUNDS PICKS UP GIDEON AND STUN GUNS HIM INTO THE RING BELL ON THE ANNOUNCER’S TABLE! OH MY!
VIC WATERS: That bell is made out of solid steel. I have no idea how Gideon is going to be able to recover from that shot.
BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS STANDS ON THE STOMACH OF GIDEON WHICH HE INJURED EARLIER AND RAISES HIS HANDS!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is #1! BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is making him get off and get back into the ring! Edmunds picks up Gideon and rolls him back into the ring! Edmunds is mocking the crowd for their love of Gideon.
VIC WATERS: The pinfall is simply academic at this point. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds picks up Gideon and whips him off the ropes… INVERTED ATOMIC DROP! Gideon is stumbling around hard… that shot sent a jolt up his spine…. EDMUNDS NAILS GIDEON WITH THE NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX… 1------------------------------------2-----------------------KICKOUT! Edmunds didn’t have the hands hooked well enough to keep Gideon from kicking out! Edmunds is in shock!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds looks furious! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds picks Gideon up… WAIT, GIDEON GRABS EDMUNDS’ HAIR AND ROLLS HIM UP WITH A SMALL PACKAGE! 1---------------------2--------------EDMUNDS REVERSES IT! 1-----------------------2----------------- GIDEON REVERSES IT! 1-------------------------2-------------------KICKOUT! Edmunds and Gideon get up…. BOTH MEN CLOTHESLINE EACH OTHER DOWN TO THE MAT! The ref makes the count! 1----------2---------3------ 4-------5 Gideon is getting back up!
VIC WATERS: This kid Gideon doesn’t know the meaning of quit! BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon calls for the STF…. He goes to pickup Edmunds… EDMUNDS GOES FOR A SMALL PACKAGE! 1-----------------2------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: THE WINNER OF THE MATCH…. “SIMPLY SENSATIONAL” SEAN EDMUNDS! BRANDON JACOBS: What a match between those two! Gideon showed such an incredible heart but he just came up a little bit short!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is asking for a microphone! ::Edmunds takes a microphone from the attendant:: EDMUNDS: Gideon, I told you time and time again leading up to this match that I would shut you down. And you kept on saying that you were going to prove me wrong. I hope you can find another experienced veteran so you can finally get your first pinfall on a pay per view. Because Richard, (pauses), you have failed once more.
BRANDON JACOBS: What an ass…. EDMUNDS IS LAYING THE BOOTS INTO GIDEON! WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES FELIX RED AND EDMUNDS BAILS OUT!
VIC WATERS: I have a feeling we haven’t seen the last between these two. That was a hell of a match, Edmunds was just a little bit better but that is all it takes in this game.
BRANDON JACOBS: Okay folks, it is time for the WFW North American Title match between Manson and Scotty Michaels inside of a steel cage, we will be back, right after this short commercial break!
