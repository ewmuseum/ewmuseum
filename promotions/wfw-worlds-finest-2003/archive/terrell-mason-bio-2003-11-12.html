Terrell Mason Bio

12 November 2003

PaulNJ21 — 12 November 2003 Name: Mason Real Name: Terrell Lee Mason Nicknames: Yo Mamas Pimp, Amerikkka's Most Wanted, Da Truth Height: 6'3 1/2" Weight: 249 lbs Hometown: Queens, New York Entrance Music: "The Crown", by Ja Rule Signature Moves: Common Spots- Superkick, Evenflow DDT, Flipping Neckbreaker, Gut-Wrench Suplex, Belly to Belly Overhead Suplex, German Suplex, Hard Back Breaker
Cruiserweight Spots- 5 Star Frog Splash, 450 Splash, Shooting Star Press, Top Rope Fame-Asser, Mero Moonsault, Running Split-Leg Moonsault, Swanton Bomb, Horizontal 360 Splash
Heavyweight Spots- Spinning Emerald Fusion, Running Sitdown Powerbomb Pin, Death Valley Driver, Fireman's Carry DDT, Two-Handed Sitdown Chokeslam, Irish Whip Military Toss into Powerslam, Gore
NOTE: All moves signified in Superheavyweight Spots are Mason's trademark maneuvers Superheavyweight Spots- Look At Cha Now: Mason sets up his opponent for a 619 but instead he grabs the opponents head while spinning and lets go. Causing both wrestlers to be launched to the arena floor landing in a DDT fashion.
420: Mason sets up his opponent outside on his knees with his chest and neck area on the railing. He slides back into the ring and runs onto the ropes and back, flipping over the top rope and landing on his opponent's shoulders.
Setup: Kick to the stomach Finisher: 1- 2- 25-to-Life [Stunner] 2- The Clap Back [Fireman's Carry into High Angle Flatliner] Relevant Titles Won: SDW Heavyweight Title. SDW United States Title. SDW Tag Titles. HW Championship. HW Television Championship.
Character: Mason is the street's typical african-american. Born and raised in Queens, New York, he quickly learned that he had to hustle to get anything in this world. Along with his rapper looks and ebonics language, Mason's swagger and extreme confidence are his biggest traits. The streets were his trainer and now the part-time wrestler, full-time hood are here to get to the top. But it's not as deep as you think, it's all for the money.
