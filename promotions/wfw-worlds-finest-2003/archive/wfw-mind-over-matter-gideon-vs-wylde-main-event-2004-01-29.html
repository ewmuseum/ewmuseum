WFW Mind Over Matter: Gideon vs. Wylde (Main Event)

29 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 29 January 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: Alex Wylde has had a Hall of Fame Career in every sense of the word beating the best of the best in every league he has been in. He has won every major title imaginable, The US, The North American Title, TV Title, the Presidential Title, except…. the World Heavyweight Title. Tonight Wylde looks to the defy the odds when he goes for a shot at the big title that has repeatedly slipped out of his grasp. His opponent is Richard Gideon, one of the best young wrestlers in WFW today and the current WFW North American Champion. Gideon has been a pillar of hard work and great fundamental wrestling and has dazzled us with many great wrestling matches in his career. Gideon beat the current WFW World Champion Scotty Michaels to win his WFW North American Title, but in order to put all doubt to rest about him being the best, Gideon needs to beat Michaels again to win the WFW World Heavyweight Title. Both men want this title shot. The winner will take on the man who walks out of the Ironman Match between Scotty Michaels and Sean Edmunds at the Great Expectations in their next WFW World Heavyweight Title defense. This is going to be a big one folks.
JONATHAN MARX: This is one of these matches that makes you happy to be a wrestling fan. When two guys who deserve a title shot so much square off to prove who is the better on and advance to take on the champion, this is the epitome of great wrestling. I love it.
BRANDON JACOBS: Both men have endured a long and tedious road to get here, while Wylde has been around for years. Gideon, over the course of a year, has taken a crash course against some of the greats in this sport, Doc Silver, Scotty Michaels, Sean Edmunds… and he has managed to hold his own and even at times better them at times. Beating Wylde is going to be no easy task.
VIC WATERS: As hungry as Gideon is, I think Wylde is even hungrier sort of like an aging John Elway, still at the top of his game, but knowing his youth is behind him and the clock is ticking and if he doesn’t act fast, he’ll end up like Dan Marino retiring without the championship like Dan Marino.
JONATHAN MARX: For some people, the great story doesn’t happen. Perfect example of that is Brett Favre from this year. His father dies of a massive stroke. The Arizona Cardinals win on a miracle play over the Vikings to put the Packers into the playoffs. Win an overtime game with a great Seahawks team on a TD reception by Al Harris to advance to the second round. How does it end? An overtime field goal by the Eagles setup by a Favre interception…
VIC WATERS: There is a certain joy in watching someone crash and burn. JONATHAN MARX: Heartless prick. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde or Gideon, Whose dream will survive?! Let’s send this one down to ringside and get this one underway!
(CUEUP: "Diesel Power" by Prodigy) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the current WFW President and leader of men… ALEX WYLDE!
::Alex Wylde walks down to a mixed pop and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “Remember” by Disturbed) RING ANNOUCER: And now, the champion, from Buffalo, NY, THE WFW North American Champion…. THE SYSTEM RICHARD GIDEON!
::Gideon comes down to the ring to the roar of the crowd and enters the ring:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Alex Wylde and Richard Gideon hook up… Richard Gideon grabs the left arm of Wylde and locks on an arm bar! Wylde looks like he is in a great deal of pain and Gideon in twisting in the armbar even harder.
JONATHAN MARX: I can’t recall the last time I saw someone use a standing armbar. It is like doing an armbar is taboo.
VIC WATERS: Gideon is the man of a 1,000 holds. I used to have a list of them somewhere. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde is trying to get to the ropes to break the armbar… GIDEON PULLS WYLDE AWAY FROM THE ROPES! Wylde drops down to his knees and he GRABS THE RIGHT LEG OF GIDEON AND FALLS BACK TO BREAK THE HOLD!
JONATHAN MARX: This crowd is applauding the effort of Wylde to break the armbar. VIC WATERS: Wylde is a smart cookie. He can break out of anything. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde is up and he is trying to move his left arm to see how much damage has been done and he visibly winces in pain. Gideon is up and he sees that his arm work has been effective and smirks at Wylde.
VIC WATERS: The younger is playing mind games with the vet. JONATHAN MARX: He has learned well. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde goes to lock up with Gideon again…. GIDEON NAILS WYLDE WITH A DROPKICK! Wylde gets up quick… GIDEON NAILS HIM WITH ANOTHER DROPKICK! Wylde tries to get up again but he slips.
JONATHAN MARX: Gideon is really displaying his speed here, he is picking his shots and then attacking.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon grabs the left arm of Wylde and repeated delivers a series of arm breakers and Wylde is screaming after every one.
JONATHAN MARX: He is trying to hyperextend the left arm. By working over the left arm, he is trying to take the Wild Buster out of play.
VIC WATERS: Excellent planning by Gideon, not many wrestlers come in with a game plan like Gideon has. Sadly, it will not be enough to get the best of Wylde.
JONATHAN MARX: We will see. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon armdrags Wylde down and puts him in a reverse chinlock. Wylde reaches back trying to grab Gideon’s hair but he moves his hair away from Wylde’s grasp. Wylde tries to use his feet to reach the ropes… Wylde is a bit too far away.
JONATHAN MARX: So far this has been a much different match than what we saw in earlier matches, there is more of importance being focused on the wrestling.
VIC WATERS: It is still early, these two will take it up another notch sooner than later and that is when everything will hit the fan.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde pushing himself towards the ropes… HE GETS HIS FOOT UNDER THE ROPE! The ref is making Gideon break and Wylde grabs the ropes. Gideon goes after Wylde and tries to attack him from behind…. WYLDE GRABS HIM BY THE HAIR AND THROWS HIM TO THE OUTSIDE!
JONATHAN MARX: Time to get Wylde baby… BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde goes to the outside and he grabs the head of Gideon with his right arm and RAMS HIM INTO THE STEEL GUARDRAIL! Wylde is grabbing his left arm in pain.
VIC WATERS: Wylde needs to knock the senses out of Gideon a bit if he wants to have a chance in this match.
BRANDON JACOBS: WYLDE CHOKES GIDEON OVER THE STEEL GUARDRAIL! The ref is count from outside of the ring and is telling Wylde to break or he’ll call for the DQ!
VIC WATERS: That referee seems like he is protecting Gideon. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde breaks the hold! Gideon grabs his throat and he is trying to get away from Wylde…. WYLDE NAILS HIM WITH THE SPINNING HEEL KICK RIGHT IN THE HEAD AND GIDEON FALLS BACKWARDS OVER THE STEPS ONTO THE FLOOR!
JONATHAN MARX: I’ve been very lucky not to face anyone who can do a spinning heel kick as well as Wylde or Felix Red. It looks like it nearly takes your damn head off.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde is pulling up the mats! The referee is going out and is warning both men to get back into the ring. WYLDE PICKS UP GIDEON AND ROCKER DROPS HIM RIGHT INTO THE CONCRETE FLOOR! Gideon is busted open!
JONATHAN MARX: Wylde is changing the whole complexion of this match. This wrestling match has been turned into a fight.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde goes to do more damage but the referee gets in the way. He is telling both men to get back into the ring or he is going to DQ both of them. Wylde picks Gideon up off the floor and he rolls him back into the ring.
VIC WATERS: Imagine getting disqualified in a match like this. These opportunities don’t grow on trees.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is really bleeding badly, he is trying to get back up….. Wylde is waiting from behind…. GIDEON GETS UP AND WYLDE HITS THE BULLDOG FROM BEHIND!
VIC WATERS: Wylde used his injured right arm on that. He is working around his own injury. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde makes the cover! The ref goes down for the count! 1-------------------------------------------------------2--------------------------GIDEON GETS THE SHOULDER UP!
JONATHAN MARX: When it comes time, Wylde is going to have trouble getting that left shoulder up from all that arm work earlier. Wylde has yet to soften Gideon up at all, he just hit him with pure impact moves and that may have cost him the crucial second he needs between victory and defeat.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde bounces off the ropes… Gideon tries to sit up…. WYLDE HITS THE SEATED DROPKICK!
JONATHAN MARX: Gideon got hit flush in the face. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde makes a cover! 1---------------------------------------------2---------------------GIDEON KICKS OUT AND WYLDE CRASHES DOWN ON HIS BAD LEFT ARM!
JONATHAN MARX: That just shows that even on something like a kickout you can do damage to yourself. You never think about it, but it happens.
VIC WATERS: That has to be great consolation for Wylde, thanks Marx. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon crawls over to Wylde who is in agony on the ground and he LOCKS IN A HALF NELSON ON THE BAD LEFT SIDE OF WYLDE!
JONATHAN MARX: Gideon didn’t even have enough in his tank to do this standing up, but it doesn’t matter whether he applies this on the ground or on both feet.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde looks like he is ready to tap. The pain on his face looks like he is a passing a stone.
VIC WATERS: Please, there are males listening to this commentary at home who don’t want to imagine that.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is check to see if he wants to give up by Wylde is shaking his head no. WYLDE TURNS GIDEON OVER ON HIS BACK AND HAS HIM IN A PINNING POSITION! 1----------------------------------------2----------------- KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Wylde almost took him by surprise there. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde went up in the air on the kick out and land hard down on the mat. It doesn’t look like he landed on his left arm, but from this angle I can’t really tell.
JONATHAN MARX: If Wylde wants to win this match, he has to put Gideon out quickly when he still can and before Gideon figures a way to use that bad left arm and shoulder to get himself a WFW World Heavyweight Title shot.
BRANDON JACOBS: Both men are down on the ground and the ref is administering a ten count! REF: 1-------2-------3-------4--------5---------6---------7! BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon gets up to his feet barely before the eight count! Gideon pulls Wylde up to his feet…. HE LIFTS WYLDE UP…. ROLLING BRAINBUSTER SUPLEX! Gideon holds…. G-FORCE! Gideon nails a second brain buster suplex and lifts him up again to nail a third!
JONATHAN MARX: Gideon stamina is amazing. This man could go an hour without breaking a sweat.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon goes for the cover on Wylde! 1------------------------------------------------2------------------- WYLE MANAGES TO GET THE RIGHT SHOULDER UP!
VIC WATERS: Wylde had trouble getting his left shoulder up, all of those moves has left an effect on him so he wants to kick out of anything. He has to count on his right shoulder to get the job done.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon pulls Wylde over towards his corner…. GIDEON PICKS WYLDE UP AND FURTHER INJURES THAT LEFT SHOULDER BY HITTING HIM WITH A SHOULDER BREAKER!
JONATHAN MARX: You can tell how much the WFW World Heavyweight Title means to Gideon. He isn’t showing Wylde any mercy whatsoever.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is climbing the ropes…. HE IS PERCHED ON THE TOP AND WYLDE IS LAYING IN AGONY ON THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: This is a move which has won Gideon many a match. BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON FLIES OFF AND HITS THE SYSTEM FAILURE 450 INTO A FROG SPLASH ON WYLDE BUT IN THE PROCESS THE REF TOOK A BUMP AND HIT HIS HEAD HARD ON THE MAT!
JONATHAN MARX: That is a really break for Gideon, the ref was totally out of position and put himself in the way of the action.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon makes the cover and he bangs his hand on the mat and counts the three along with the crowd!
VIC WATERS: He can pound his hand on the mat until the cow comes home, as long as it isn’t the ref making the count, it doesn’t count.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon looks over and he sees that the referee is down. Gideon puts his head in his hands and screams for the referee to get up.
JONATHAN MARX: I don’t think that will do any good, he looks out of it. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon grabs Wylde and pulls him off the mat…. HE CHICKEN WINGS THE BAD LEFT ARM AND WYLDE IS SCREAMING THAT HE GIVES UP BUT NOBODY IS THERE TO HEAR HIM! Wylde is trying to make his way to the ropes…. BUT GIDEON PULLS HIM BACK TO THE CENTER!
VIC WATERS: There is absolutely no call for this. Gideon should be trying to wake up the referee, not taking advantage of him being down.
JONATHAN MARX: 90% of the time when wrestlers do that, they get beat. You have to stay on your man like a rabid dog if you want to win and not let up.
BRANDON JACOBS: OH NO! Wylde has collapsed from the pain! Gideon lets go of the hold and he goes to wake up the referee to get the pin! WAIT A SECOND, WYLDE IS STARTING TO STIR!
VIC WATERS: Wylde was faking in order to get Gideon to break the hold! BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde pulls a metal object out of his tights…. WYLDE IS LOADING HIS BOOT RIGHT BEHIND GIDEON’S BACK! The referee is starting to wake up…. WYLDE USES THE ROPES TO HELP HIMSELF UP!
JONATHAN MARX: This is Wylde’s chance, he has to capitalize now if he wants to win this! BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon turns around…. WYLDE ATTEMPTS THE GATECRASHER SUPERKICK BUT GIDEON CATCHES THE LEG! GIDEON APPLIES FINAL JUDGMENT STF!
JONATHAN MARX: Gideon has his finisher locked! VIC WATERS: Damn it, Wylde had this win in his finger tips. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde’s eyes are bulging out of his head! The dazed referee is asking if wants to give up…. WYLDE GRABS THE REFEREE BY THE COLLAR! The referee tries to pull him off who accidentally moves him closer to the ropes…. WYLDE GRABS THE BOTTOM ROPE!
VIC WATERS: Wylde is thinking three or four moves ahead. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon breaks the hold and is yelling at the referee! GIDEON IS INCENSED! Wylde is trying to pull himself up using the ropes and I’ve never seen a man in more pain in all of my life.
JONATHAN MARX: Bad call aside, Wylde has a tremendous heart to fight through all this. Lesser men would have given up but Wylde won’t quit.
BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, WYLDE IS UP…. WYLDE IS SET…. WYLDE HITS THE GATECRASHER TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD OF GIDEON! Gideon falls like a tree right at the referee but the ref jumps out of the way! Wylde makes the cover!
VIC WATERS: This is it! BRANDON JACOBS: The referee goes down to make the count! 1--------------------------------------2----------------------------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match and the man who will get the post PPV title shot…. ALEX WYLDE!
JONATHAN MARX: Sometimes the best way to win a match is to take a great deal of abuse and wait for your opponent to leave you your opening and that is what Wylde did here tonight and it still almost wasn’t enough to beat Gideon.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon had a hell of a showing here tonight, but the breaks all went against him, if Gideon had superkicked Wylde, the ref would have taken a spill and this match would still be going on.
VIC WATERS: Does it matter? WYLDE IS GOING FOR THE TITLE! BRANDON JACOBS: He sure is, even if I think all the calls went against Gideon, both men worked really hard and this was a hell of a match to end the show with. I have to give credit to both men for the effort they showed here tonight.
JONATHAN MARX: But as great as this show was…. The next one will be even bigger and better when WFW comes back to PPV with Great Expectations live from Edison Field in Anaheim California.
VIC WATERS: I guess if you have to do an outdoor show because of the Exploding Death Match, it might as well be someplace which is warm in February instead of the freezing cold Northeast.
BRANDON JACOBS: Couldn’t have said it better myself Vic. This is going to be a loaded show with nine matches on it and will run over four hours headlined by WFW World Heavyweight Champion Scotty Michaels putting his World Title on the line in an IRONMAN match against former WWL World Heavyweight Champion Sean Edmunds. Richard Gideon, who put on a hell of a show tonight, will put up the WFW North American Title against Copycat and Psycho. And the reason we are holding this show outdoors…. Anarky will be facing off against Mephisto Exploding Barbed Wire Death match for the first time on PPV in this company’s heritage. There are of other great matches on this show, but we have to run! Folks, we’d like to thank you for joining us tonight, I hope to see you all at WFW Great Expectations! So long from Princeton, GOODNIGHT EVERY BODY!
