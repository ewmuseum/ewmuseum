Hennig Memorial: Michaels vs. Sugawara & Rat Fink vs. Bobby

27 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 27 February 2003 Brandon Jacobs: IT IS TIME FOR THE BATTLE OF THE SEXES! Zack Sirus: Beau Michaels is a man’s man and tonight on behalf of all men, he will put Aitsu Sugawara in her place, back in the kitchen!
Brandon Jacobs: That attitude is so 1950s. Aitsu Sugawara is a hell of worker, regardless of her sex.
Zack Sirus: If you notice, back in the old days, wrestling didn’t use to suck either. Brandon Jacobs: Well, it isn’t her fault, lets go to the ring for the start of the match! :: "Suri Suri Masuri" by Lee Hyun Jung blares over the loud speaker:: RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first from Kyoto, Japan, The WP’s Woman’s champion, Aitsu Sugawara
::Aitsu Sugawara slaps the hands of fans at ringside as she comes down and enters the ring::
::'Freeek!' by George Michael wails over the loud speakers:: RING ANNOUNCER: Her opponent, from West Hollywood, California, : 'Extremely BiSexual' Beau Michaels!
::Beau Michaels comes down to ringside to the boos of the crowd and plays to the audience before entering the ring::
::bell rings:: Brandon Jacobs: Michaels and Sugawara lockup! Sugawara chicken wings Michaels’ arm behind his back. Michaels tries to throw an elbow back to break the hold but Sugawara moves out of the way. Michaels grabs Sugawara by the head and slams her down with an overhead slam down to the mat and locks on a chinlock! Sugawara struggles to try to break free and she kicks her legs to move herself closer to the ropes. She is able to get there and the ref asks for a break. Michaels breaks cleanly and both go back up to their feet
::crowd cheers:: Zack Sirus: This has been the best display of technical wrestling so far on the show. Sugawara is bringing the workrate tonight!
Brandon Jacobs: Sugawara and Michaels lock up, Sugawara puts on a front face lock and nails Michaels repeatedly with nails Michaels with kneelifts to the stomach! Sugawara locks a leglock around the stomach of Michaels and puts him in a vice grip!
Zack Sirus: Those knee lifts look like they have taken effect because Michaels is starting to look winded already. Sugawara working over his cardiovascular areas, if this goes too long, the advantage will be clearly in her court.
Brandon Jacobs: Michaels is wailing at Sugawara’s torso with forearms trying to break the hold! Sugawara refuses to break!
Zack Sirus: Sugawara is showing a lot of courage refusing to break here despite taking some heavy shots.
Brandon Jacobs: Michaels grabs the rope and the ref forces Sugawara to break the hold! Sugawara doesn’t let Michaels get up and nails him with a double foot stomp to the midsection!
Zack Sirus: Michaels is starting to get upset here.... Brandon Jacobs: Sugawara picks up Michaels up and whips him off the ropes! SLEEPER HOLD BY SUGAWARA! Michaels is struggling to escape! Michaels is starting to go out! The ref is checking on Edmund! The ref raises his hand once.... hands fall once! He raises his hand twice.... HANDS FALLS A SECOND TIME! The ref raises his hand a third time.... IT FALL..... NO! Michaels gets the hand up! Michaels whips Sugawara off the ropes.... CLOTHESLINE!
Zack Sirus: Good lord, I guess the wrestling match is over. Sugawara was clotheslined out of her boots!
Brandon Jacobs: Sugawara gets to her feet... MICHAELS NAILS SUGAWARA WITH A DROPKICK! Sugawara gets up again... ANOTHER DROPKICK! Sugawara is still trying to get up! SWINGING NECKBREAKER BY MICHAELS! Sugawara is staying down!
Zack Sirus: Sugawara has a lot of spirit, but even a man couldn’t take that punishment and get up from that.
Brandon Jacobs: Michaels bounces off the ropes... ASAI MOONSAULT ONTO SUGAWARA! Michaels makes the cover! 1--------------2--------------KICKOUT!
Zack Sirus: Michaels can’t believe it! Michaels is going to have to use his finisher to take care of her.
Brandon Jacobs: Michaels is calling for the Running Crotchslam! He picks up Sugawara.... HE NAILS IT! 1--------2------3! MICHAELS WINS AND ADVANCES TO THE SECOND ROUND! Michaels takes his hand off Sugawara in disgust.
Zack Sirus: Michaels may have beaten Sugawara tonight, but he had to turn it into a fight to do it.
Brandon Jacobs: It was a hell of a match. Sugawara showed that she is one of the best technicians in WFW. Moving on to our next match, because of snow down south, Bobby Lee couldn’t make his flight to tonight’s show so Rat Fink wins his match by forfeit. Our next match after the commercial break will be MIKE MCGEE VS. THE SHADOW!
Zack Sirus: They have really been going back and forth with each other on the stick, that should be very intense.
Brandon Jacobs: We will be back after this commercial break!
