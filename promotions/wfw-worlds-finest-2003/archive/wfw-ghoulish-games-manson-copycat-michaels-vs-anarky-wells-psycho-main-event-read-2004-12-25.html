What, but the discovery that an equally international infamous fellow had happened into that same bar on the same night, recognized our associates, and taking exception to their political views, was in the process of physically berating our associates. Not being of the loyal sort, The Rage of Fire and myself watched with amusement as this chance acquaintance broke liquor bottles over the heads of these two gentleman zealots, until they finally lost consciousness.
And there, with all other patrons having fled the scene of violence, with the floor covered in broken glass, with a disposed dictator and a terrorist lying in pools of blood and cheap beer, and my mind shining with chemical exuberance, I offered this self styled cowboy a job with the WFW, and he gladly accepted.
I wasn’t going to say anything until the PPV. But hey, f*ck it. Edmunds, I repeat. You’ve gained enemies tonight, and resurrected an equally antagonistic ghost of your past.
If you’re going to be ringside during the PPV world title match, then I’m sending in a special enforcer to ensure that WHEN you try something stupid, you pay dearly.
I believe you’ve met my recent acquaintance? Shane Southern? (CUEUP: “Ain’t going down” by Garth Brooks) ::Shane Southern comes out through the curtain:: JONATHAN MARX (voice over): OH MY GOD, IT IS SHANE SOUTHERN! FTB
