WFW Shock & Awe: Boot Camp: Manson vs. Rat Fink

20 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 20 April 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: I’ve been looking forward to this match for awhile. Manson will finally be able to get revenge for what Rat Fink did to him in the tournament final! Manson has been on a rampage, and who can blame him, he was screwed out of the WFW World Heavyweight Title.
VIC WATERS: Doc Silver would have beaten Manson anyways, it was his night. BRANDON JACOBS: Would you have thought the up and coming Gideon would have beaten Doc Silver tonight? Anything can happen on any given night.
VIC WATERS: The kid got lucky. I congratulate him on being able to put on his resume that he has pinned Doc Silver. But what we saw tonight was not a title match, Doc will do everything in his power to keep that title as he has shown time and time again.
BRANDON JACOBS: I guess we will find out next month, but we have an even bigger match tonight. The only way to win the match is by pinfall or submission. No DQs and anything goes! This is just Manson’s kind of match! Let’s send this one to ringside!
::Megalomaniac by KMFDM blares over the speakers:: RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from WCW Special Forces, one of the greatest super villains of our time, RAT FINK!!
::Rat Fink walks down to the ring with his cheesy sci fi looking robot manager:: RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, OH MY GOD! IT IS BLACK VAN! ::A black van with the North American Title on the side mirror crashes through the entrance and rams the ring sending one side down::
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is dressed in street clothes-black jeans, street, heavy boots with his leather jacket and he is ready to fight!
VIC WATERS: OH MY GOD, THE LIGHTS HAVE GONE OUT! BRANDON JACOBS: I THINK IS JUST HEARD A LOUD CHAIR SHOT! MAKE THAT TWO CHAIR SHOTS! ::the lights come back on and Manson is layed out with blood coming out of his forehead near his car with Rat Fink standing over him a chair::
BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK IS TAKING OUT A PAIR OF HANDCUFFS OUT OF HIS JEANS AND IS ATTACHING MANSON HANDS AROUND TO THE MIDDLE OF THE RING POST BETWEEN THE SECOND AND THIRD ROPES! Rat Fink has the mic!
RAT FINK: First off, I’d like to thank Doc for dimming the lights, if you guys thought I came here tonight to allow myself to get beaten up by Manson, you are all dead wrong! I call the shots around here, I’ve cost you the WFW Heavyweight Title, I’ve spoiled your first WFW Title defense main event, now I’ve stolen the only gold you got, do you want a piece of my ass Manson?! Well then, you are going to have to wait.
::crowd boos loudly:: ::Rat Fink spits in the irate handcuffed Manson face and leaves the ringside with Manson’s title to the crowd throwing trash at him::
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink is going to have hell to pay when Manson gets a hold of him. That is no way to treat someone like Manson.
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink has balls the size of grapefruits. BRANDON JACOBS: LOOK! Here comes the ring crew to get Manson out of the cuffs! ::Rat Fink looks around in fear and jumps in Manson’s car… he jumps in and backs his way out of the building and exits out of the entrance::
VIC WATERS: Good lord, Rat Fink even stole his car! He is deader than Saddam! BRANDON JACOBS: The ring crew has finally freed Manson! Thankfully he made it easier by damaging the ring earlier so they could just undo the rope from the ring without cutting anything!
VIC WATERS: MANSON RUNS TO THE BACK TO SEE IF HE CAN CATCH UP WITH RAT FINK!. BRANDON JACOBS: We will be back here on April 30th at the Hammerstein Ballroom in NYC for WFW Gates of Hell which will feature Doc Silver defending his title against Richard Gideon and Rat Fink & Scotty Michaels facing off against Manson & Diablo in an Ultimate Jeopardy Match which will decide who Manson will be facing at the PPV!
VIC WATERS: Sounds like a hell of a show, if Rat Fink is still alive. BRANDON JACOBS: Thanks for tuning in everyone, we will see you at the WFW Gates of Hell, goodnight everyone!
