Hennig Memorial Show: Mike McGee vs. Andre Eian

27 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 27 February 2003 Brandon Jacobs: The hottest feud in World Finest Wrestling today is between “Mr. Middle Management” Mike McGee and his opponent, the Shadow Andre Eian.
Zack Sirus: The Shadow has been very impressive, but as I told you earlier, my pick to win the very first WFW World Heavyweight Title is Mike McGee. He has a great mind and a raw energy that reminds me of a young Zack Sirus. He isn’t as guapo as I am, but who can be?
Brandon Jacobs: True, he is no Conan O’Brien Lets send this one to the ring and get this match underway!
::Generic Office Muzak blares over the loud speaker:: RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from the Home Office in New Brunswick, NJ! Terry Tate’s worst nightmare, “MR. MIDDLE MANAGEMENT” MIKE MCGEE!
::Mike McGee walks gingerly down to the ring to the loud boos of the crowd:: :: Bawitdaba" by Kid Rock wails over the loud speaker:: RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing second, from Trondheim, Norway, the former XGW World Champion, THE SHADOW ANDRE EIAN!
::Andre Eian comes out to the loudest pop of the night and slowly enters the ring while keeping his eye on Mike McGee::
Brandon Jacobs: Andre Eian attacks before the bell! He throws Mike McGee to the outside and they are changing blows fast and furious! Andre Eian drives Mike McGee head first into the ring post! Mike McGee stumbles around dazed and focused! Andre Eian rams Mike McGee’s head into the steps face first! It looks like Mike McGee may already be broken open!
Zack Sirus: The sharks are in a feeding frenzy! Brandon Jacobs: Andre Eian throws Mike McGee into the ring and enters the ring after him. Mike McGee is begging off from Andre’s offense! Andre asks the crowd what he should do and they are cheering him on! MIKE MCGEE THRUSTS HIMSELF FORWARD AND NAILS ANDRE WITH A HEADBUTT TO THE STOMACH! Andre falls into the ropes and gets his right arm tangled in the ropes and falls to the outside!
Zack Sirus: Mike McGee exits the ring and pulls up the mat that Eian is standing on and he falls back and hits his head on the ring apron! Mike McGee backs up... HE CHARGES EIAN WITH A LARIAT! Eian breaks free of the rope and falls down to the floor. McGee picks up Eian... GORDBUSTER ON THE EXPOSED CONCRETE! Eian is busted open!
Brandon Jacobs: Both men are already bloody and this match is still early! The ref is coming to the outside to try to get both men to bring this back into the ring!
Zack Sirus: McGee needs to go for the pin here and get this match over with, he still has to think about the next round of this tournament and the longer he goes, the more beaten down he will be.
Brandon Jacobs: McGee throws Eian in the ring and puts him in a waist lock... GUTWRENCH SUPLEX! Eian lands on his neck. It looks like he is already starting to set him up for Pink Slip! McGee picks Eian up... GORILLA PRESS!
Zack Sirus: Amazing show of strength by McGee, it looks like has the brawl to go with the brains.
Brandon Jacobs: McGee lowers him from the gorilla press and puts him in the backbreaker! Eian is struggling to break free but he has it locked! The referee is asking if Eian wants to give up but he keeps on shaking his head vigorously no! Eian struggles to break free... MCGEE LOSES HIS GRIP! Eian lands behind McGee.... HIGH RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX BY EIAN! McGee crashes down hard on the mat! Eian stands behind McGee motioning for him to get up... McGee gets up slowly.... EIAN NAILS THE SPEAR! EIAN GOES FOR THE COVER! 1------2------ LEG UNDER THE BOTTOM ROPE!!
Zack Sirus: Much too close for McGee there, he is going to make a full out of me if he doesn’t get out of the first round.
Brandon Jacobs: Eian drags him to the center of the ring, he picks McGee off the mat, HE NAILS HIM WITH A URANGE! He makes another cover! 1-------------2----------------- 2 3 / 4 KICKOUT! Eian realizes that he has the advantage and the time to win is now, he picks up McGee and kicks him the gut! EIAN IS CALLING FOR HIS FINISHER BY DRAGGING HIS THUMB ACROSS HIS THROAT! Eian puts McGee in the front face lock... MCGEE GRABS THE REF ON THE WAY DOWN AND BLOCKS THE MOVE! Eian bangs the back of his neck hard onto the back of the mat! McGee turns Eian over... HE LOCKS ON THE PINK SLIP CAMEL CLUTCH! Eian is in pain! He can’t hold on much longer! The ref has recovered and he checks on Eian, Eian is shaking his head no as the blood runs down his face! Eian is losing consciousness, EIAN HAS PASSED OUT! THE REF CALLS FOR THE BELL! MIKE MCGEE HAS WON THE MATCH AND WILL ADVANCE TO THE SECOND ROUND!
Zack Sirus: I really thought that Eian had him there. That was a hell of a match, my hat is off to him. On another day, in another match, who knows if McGee could beat him again.
Brandon Jacobs: We are going to have to take a break to clean off the mat and help Eian and McGee to the back, if the war was just saw is a sign of things to come, STAY TUNED!
