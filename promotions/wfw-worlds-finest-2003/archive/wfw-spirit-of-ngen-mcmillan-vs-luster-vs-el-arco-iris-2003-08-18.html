WFW: Spirit of NGEN: McMillan vs. Luster vs. El Arco Iris

18 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 18 August 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Aug-18-03 AT 07:41&nbsp;PM (EST)]BRANDON JACOBS: It is now time for a three way dance, the rookie sensitive Chris McMillan will face the up and coming Luster and the wily newcomer El Arco Iris! This should be a hell of a match.
VIC WATERS: This match will only be single elimination, so you never know what may happen. Luster & El Arco Iris have been on bit of a losing streak lately but one of them may finally break it tonight and once you change your momentum, you never know where it may take you.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster and El Arco Iris both have so much personality that the fans here in WFW have taken to them so this should be a hot crowd here tonight. Let’s send this one to ringside!
CUEUP: “Superheros” by Daft Punk) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first from the end of rainbow, weighing in at 207 pounds, EL ARCO IRIS!
::the masked man with a peace sign on his mask makes his way down to ringside to the cheers of the crowd and enters the ring::
(CUEUP: “Funk Town” by Disco Fever) RING ANNOUNCER: On his way to the ring now, from Atlanta, Georgia, standing at 6’6” and weighing in at 250 pounds, he is the master of style, LUSTER!
::Luster comes down to the ring to a big pop to the screams of the females in the crowd:: (CUE UP: “Hair of The Dog” by Nazareth) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, standing at 5’8” and weighing 190 pounds, “The Wolf” Chris McMillan!
::McMillan walks out to a pop from the crowd:: ::bell rings:: BRANDON JACOBS: McMillan & El Arco Iris start out.... Luster rests in the corner while those two start it out…. McMillan goes to lockup… DROPKICK BY ARCO IRIS! McMillan goes down and gets right back up again… ANOTHER DROPKICK!
VIC WATERS: Arco Iris has so much energy. He is the Energizer Bunny of the WFW. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris hooks on a reverse chin lock! Luster walks out of the corner and starts stomping McMillan in the stomach with boots to the chest! El Arco Iris holds the arms of McMillan… Luster bounces off the ropes… HE DROPKICKS MCMILLAN IN THE FACE!
VIC WATERS: Both boots of Luster right in the face of McMillan! That had to hurt! BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris hooks the legs and covers McMillan! 1-----------2----KICKOUT! VIC WATERS: Much too early to go for a pin, he barely got the two there. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris picks McMillan up.... HE LOCKS IN THE WAISTLOCK IN THE GERMAN SUPLEX… MCMILLAN SWINGS HIS ELBOW BACK AND NAILS IRIS IN THE FACE BREAKING THE WAISTLOCK! Luster charges McMillan… Clothesline! McMillan staggers backwards... LUSTER NAILS MCMILLAN WITH A SPEAR! McMillan momentum knocks him to the outside!
VIC WATERS: That was a hell of a spear. Luster wasn’t holding anything back on that. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster is going to the outside… WAIT, El Arco Iris grabs him by the shoulder and spins him around! SEVERAL QUICK PUNCHES TO LUSTER AS HE IS AGAINST THE ROPES! El Arco Iris whips him off the ropes…. HE NAILS HIM WITH A FLYING FOREARM!
VIC WATERS: Luster was so distracted by McMillan, he totally forgot about Iris and that is a rookie mistake which he can not afford to make.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris moves him towards the corner… he climbs up the ropes… HE NAILS LUSTER WITH THE MOONSAULT! El Arco Iris makes the cover! 1--------------2-------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Luster barely kicked out of that! That was a picture perfect moonsault by Iris!
BRANDON JACOBS: McMillan is getting up on the floor… Iris is waiting for the dazed Luster to get up… McMillan rolls into the ring…. IRIS NAILS LUSTER WITH THE HAPPY DANCE AND LUSTER GOES OVER THE TOP ROPE AND TO THE FLOOR! Iris goes out after Luster… MCMILLAN GRABS IRIS BY THE MASK AND CATCHES IRIS ON THE APRON! McMillan grabs the tights of Iris… VERTICAL SUPLEX INTO THE RING!
VIC WATERS: HOLY COW! BRANDON JACOBS: McMillan makes the cover! 1-------------2---------KICKOUT! McMillan grabs the leg for another try! 1-------------2-----------KICKOUT! A groggy Luster is getting up. McMillan picks Iris up off the mat… HE LOCKS IN A HALF NELSON AND SUPLEXES HIM DOWN ONTO THE MAT! Luster rolls in under the bottom rope! McMillan drags him to the center and makes the cover on Iris… 1-------------------2----------------- LUSTER DIVES AND BREAKS UP THE PIN!
VIC WATERS: Luster just broke up that pinfall on Iris. This has been an incredible match so far.
BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER AND MCMILLAN AND TRADING RIGHTS AND LEFT BACK AND FORTH! McMillan gains the advantage… MCMILLAN WHIPS LUSTER TO THE ROPES… SPINNING LEG LARIAT TAKES LUSTER DOWN! Luster is dazed but he is struggling to get up… MCMILLAN BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND NAILS LUSTER WITH A SPRINGBOARD SPINNING LEGDROP TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD! McMillan goes to cover Luster but Luster’s foot is in the ropes so the ref doesn’t go down to make the count.
VIC WATERS: McMillan has looked fabulous here tonight. It is going to take a hell of a man to beat him here tonight.
BRANDON JACOBS: McMillan is getting up but he doesn’t see Iris behind him…. IRIS GRABS MCMILLAN FROM BEHIND WITH A WAISTLOCK…. GERMAN SUPLEX! IRIS HOLDS ON…. He nails McMillan with another GERMAN SUPLEX! McMillan looked like he took a bad bump on the back of the head on that one.
VIC WATERS: McMillan looks like he doesn’t know where he is. BRANDON JACOBS: Iris is setting up for the Happy Dance Superkick… McMillan is slowly getting up…. HE NAILS IT!
VIC WATERS: ITS OVER! All Arco Iris has to do is cover him! BRANDON JACOBS: A dazed Luster is up…. HE NAILS EL ARCO IRIS WITH SHUFFLE SIDE KICK! LUSTER THROWS HIM OVER THE TOP ROPE!
VIC WATERS: Oh man, poor Iris, he almost had the victory! BRANDON JACOBS: Luster picks up McMillan….. FUNK & WAGNELL POWERBOMB! LUSTER COVERS! 1------------2--------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: THE WINNER OF THE MATCH……..LUSTER! BRANDON JACOBS: The fans here are up and are on their feet… LUSTER HAS SNAPPED HIS STREAK by beating one of the most promising young stars in wrestling today in the young and tenacious Chris McMillan and the energetic and innovative El Arco Iris!
VIC WATERS: Incredible match by those three, you have to feel happy for Luster. It looks like he is on his way to bigger and better things.
BRANDON JACOBS: We are going to take a commercial break folks but when we comeback, Rat Fink will square off one on one with Felix Red! Stay tuned everyone!
