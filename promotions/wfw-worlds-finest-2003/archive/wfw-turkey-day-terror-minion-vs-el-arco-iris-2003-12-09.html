WFW Turkey Day Terror: Minion vs. El Arco Iris

9 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 9 December 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back and boy, it Shawn Hart having a bad day. First he gets ditched by both of his partners and then he has a nasty run in with Minion which leaves him thrown against a wall.
VIC WATERS: Some days it doesn’t pay to wake up in the morning. BRANDON JACOBS: As hated as Shawn Hart is by many after he beat Manson, when the backstage footage aired of the incident behind the scenes with Minion, you could tell that the fans were clearly in Shawn Hart’s corner.
VIC WATERS: This Minion shows absolutely no mercy whatsoever. He takes great joy in destroying guys.
BRANDON JACOBS: May god have mercy on El Arco Iris’s soul. The last thing I want to see is all of the children of the WFW lose their hero so close to Christmas.
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris brought this on himself being concerned for others. If he thought only of himself, he would be better off.
BRANDON JACOBS: Sometimes you have to take a stand against bullies like Minion. I’m proud of what El Arco Iris did and it takes a lot of courage to get in the ring with this monster.
VIC WATERS: Don’t mistake courage for stupidity. BRANDON JACOBS: Maybe it is, we’ll find out soon enough. Let’s send this one to ringside and get this underway.
(CUEUP: “Tunak Tunak Tun” by Dahler Mendhi) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from over the rainbow, the Warrior of Happiness, EL ARCO IRIS!
::El Arco Iris walks down to a huge pop from the crowd, dozens of children can been seen in the crowd wearing El Arco Iris masks, and enters the ring::
(CUEUP: “Where Boys Fear To Tread” by Smashing Pumpkins) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, one of the most sadistic men in all of wrestling, from New York City…. MINION!
::Minion walks down to the ring to the boos of the crowd and enters the ring:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Minion and El Arco Iris meet in the center of the ring…. MINION GRABS THE MASK OF EL ARCO IRIS AND PULLS IT DOWN SO HE CAN’T SEE! MINION CLOTHESLINES EL ARCO IRIS DOWN TO THE MAT AND JUMPS ON TOP AND STARTS POUNDING ON EL ARCO IRIS WITH LEFTS AND RIGHTS!
VIC WATERS: Minion is such a punk. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris tries desperately to fix the mask and as soon as he does, Minion rakes the eyes of El Arco Iris! MINION STARTS NAILING EL ARCO IRIS WITH LOUD ALTERNATING SLAPS TO THE HEAD!
VIC WATERS: Minion is really terrorizing El Arco Iris, he is in trouble, deep trouble. BRANDON JACOBS: Minion picks El Arco Iris off the mat…. DDT! The ref is going to check on El Arco Iris…. Minion is going over to the turnbuckle… HE IS REMOVING THE TURNBUCKLE PAD SO THE METAL IS EXPOSED!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris is going to die. BRANDON JACOBS: MINION PICKS UP EL ARCO IRIS UP FOR A POWERBOMB…. EL ARCO IRIS COUNTERS WITH A HURACANRANA! The ref goes down for the count! 1--------------------------------2-----------KICKOUT! Minion gets up and he looks shocked that El Arco Iris almost got him.
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris has lightning like quickness, Minion really shouldn’t underestimate him.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris charges with the SHINNING WIZARD…. Minion catches him…. T-BONE SUPLEX! THAT IS THE SAME MOVE THAT MINION INJURED ADAM BENJAMIN WITH!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris doesn’t look good, he grabbing his neck. He looks like he is a hell of an amount of pain.
BRANDON JACOBS: Minion is calling for the move again… the ref is trying to stop him…. MINION PUSHES THE REF DOWN AND HE BUMPS HIS HEAD ON THE MAT! Minion picks El Arco Iris up for another T-Bone Suplex… GOOD LORD!
VIC WATERS: Man, the kids at ringside are crying. BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES SHAWN HART WITH A CHAIR! HE NAILS MINION RIGHT IN THE HEAD WITH IT AND MINION IS STOPPED FROM DELIVERING A SECOND T-BONE SUPLEX! Minion laughs at Hart and comes back at him for more!
VIC WATERS: Jesus Christ. BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart throws down the chair! VIC WATERS: If Shawn Hart threw down the chair, there is only one thing he could be planning.
BRANDON JACOBS: SHAWN HART NAILS MINION WITH THE EL CODO EXPLOSIVO…. BUT IT IS STILL NOT ENOUGH! Minion doesn’t go down…. EL ARCO IRIS NAILS HIM WITH THE HAPPY DANCE KICK! Down goes Minion! El Arco Iris goes for the cover! The dazed ref crawls over to make the count! 1--------------------------------2---------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. EL ARCO IRIS! VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris did it! HE BEAT THE MONSTER AND GOT REVENGE FOR ADAM BENJAMIN! BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second, Minion is getting back up…. HE CLOTHESLINES EL ARCO IRIS OVER THE TOP! HE CLOTHESLINES SHAWN HART OVER THE TOP! Minion is pissed and he motioning to Shawn Hart on the outside that he is coming for his betl.
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart and El Arco Iris both nailed their finishers on this guy and it wasn’t enough to keep him down.
BRANDON JACOBS: Things do not look good, we’ll try to get this scene under control and when we comeback, Rat Fink will put the BAD World Heavyweight Title on the line against Felix Red! STAY TUNED!
