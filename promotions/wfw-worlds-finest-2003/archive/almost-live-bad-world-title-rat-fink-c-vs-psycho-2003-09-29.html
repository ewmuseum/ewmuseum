WFW — Card

Almost Live: BAD World Title: Rat Fink (c) vs. Psycho

29 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 29 September 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: It is now time to get the fun and games started. BAD has a long history of great wild matches and I don’t think it is any secret that management has just been looking for an opportunity to relieve the greatness of a period of time which was so revolution to what wrestling has become today.
VIC WATERS: But has it been for the better Brandon? BRANDON JACOBS: People always have a tendency to over do. To try to out do themselves match after match in order to draw the fans in and raise their expectations. While you can’t do a whole card of wild matches every week, because after a while the matches start to lose their meaning and a special match is no longer special, you can easily do one or two in the spirit of that special time and the fans will really get into it.
VIC WATERS: So it is sort of like taking a kid out for ice cream every day, after awhile, they stop appreciating it and you have a fat kid who doesn’t appreciate what he is given.
BRANDON JACOBS: Exactly. VIC WATERS: Wrestling is a beautiful but violent art. People like to see guys turned into Jackson Pollocks with blood splattered all over their body and occasionally missing an ear like Van Gough. People love a good brawl because it is purest form of fighting. Two men raising their fists in order to beat the bejesus out of each other until another one gives up…. I love it.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink & Psycho also know how to wrestle too, they are masters of their craft and when they go out there, they can do whatever they need to do in order to win. Of course, it helps Rat Fink that he has the American out there to help him win his matches because of the no DQ or CO rules.
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink is just defending the BAD World Heavyweight Title like it was meant to be defended. Rat Fink time after time was persecuted by management by being forced to wrestle matches under WFW rules and getting repeatedly bad calls by the WFW referees. It was like management didn’t want Rat Fink to win so the stacked the deck against him repeatedly.
BRANDON JACOBS: I wouldn’t go that far, but Rat Fink certainly thrives in this lawless land of the BAD World Heavyweight Title. But tonight, I finally think he has met his match in Psycho, a man who has not only been the WFW World Heavyweight Champion, but probably the best brawler in all over WFW today. This man nearly killed Doc Silver repeatedly in that cage and you can see by his loss to Manson that all of those injuries he suffered finally cost Silver a must win match. It is going to be ages before Silver is the same wrestler ever again and that was because of his cage match with Psycho.
VIC WATERS: I do have to admit, I don’t think Doc Silver was the same man after that match with Psycho. He seemed a couple of step slower and he constantly looked in pain. Defending the WFW World Heavyweight Title is murder on the body and can take a lot out of you because of all the stiff competition here in WFW. This isn’t where older guys come to take a vacation, you have to actually work here if you want to hold that strap.
BRANDON JACOBS: Very true, this is going to be a hell of a fight and I can tell by looking at the fans that they are getting antsy. Let’s send this one to ringside and get ready for our falls count anywhere first title defense of the night!
(CUEUP: "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, making his comeback match after 60 days away from WFW, the former WFW World Heavyweight Champion, PSCYHO!
::Psycho comes down to the ring to a huge “WELCOME BACK!” chant from the crowd:: (CUEUP: “Megalomaniac” by KMFDM) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, the leader of the New Age Revolution and the current BAD World Heavyweight Champion, RAT FINK!
::Rat Fink walks down to the ring with the The American walking behind him and they enter the ring::
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink and Psycho meet eye to eye in the ring…. RAT FINK SPITS ON PSYCHO AND RAT FINK PULLS THE AMERICAN IN FRONT OF HIM AND PSYCHO STARTS WAILING ON HIM!
VIC WATERS: That certainly angered Psycho. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink bails out of the ring. Psycho fires in the haymakers and backs The American up! Psycho grabs the arm of the American and irish whips him off the ropes…. PSYCHO NAILS THE AMERICAN WITH A YAZUKA KICK!
VIC WATERS: DAMN! HE NAILED RIGHT IN THE FACE! BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho bounces off the ropes… somersault legdrop on The American! He makes the cover! 1-----------------------2--------------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Much too early to go for pinfalls… BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho takes his shin and he presses it into the throat of the American! The ref is trying to get Psycho to break!
VIC WATERS: Psycho has been gone for a long time, he knows if he could capture the BAD World Heavyweight Title, it would cement his comeback after his rather long absence.
BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK COMES INTO THE RING AND GRABS THE HAIR OF THE HAIR OF PSYCHO TO GET HIM OFF OF THE AMERICAN! Psycho nails Rat Fink with an elbow to the midsection and leaves Rat Fink breathless! PSYCHO STARTS FIRING LEFTS AND RIGHTS AT RAT FINK…. CLOTHESLINE BY PSYCHO!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is doing pretty well countering the two on one onslaught he is facing tonight but eventually it is going to catch up to him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho drops an elbow onto the chest of Rat Fink! HE GETS UP QUICKLY AND DROPS ANOTHER! HE GETS UP A THIRD TIME… HE HITS IT AGAIN!
VIC WATERS: Besides from your knee, your elbow is hardest part of your body so when you really dive it into someone, it hurts like a Dickens.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho jumps on Rat Fink and tries to choke him! The ref puts the count on Psycho! 1----2----3--- 4--- THE REF IS TRYING TO PULL PSYCHO OFF!
VIC WATERS: This match is no DQ and Psycho is simply playing by the rules. BRANDON JACOBS: The American gets up and he pulls Psycho up… BUT PSYCHO IS REFUSING TO LET GO OF RAT FINK’S NECK! The American elbows Psycho in the head repeatedly…. PSYCHO FINALLY BREAKS!
VIC WATERS: The American may have just saved Rat Fink’s life there. BRANDON JACOBS: The American throws Psycho through the ropes to the floor in order to buy Rat Fink some time to get his energy back! The American heads to the outside and he picks up Psycho off the ground… HE RAMS HIM HEAD FIRST INTO THE STEEL STAIRS!
VIC WATERS: That was a very loud shot, The American wasn’t holding anything back on that. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho stumbles around… THE AMERICAN TAKES PSYCHO DOWN WITH A DROPKICK TO THE KNEE! Psycho grabs his knee and he is rolling around on the ground in pain!
VIC WATERS: Very smart strategy here, Psycho is a risk taker and without his legs, he will have trouble doing the moves that he needs to do to win.
BRANDON JACOBS: The American rips a woman’s purse away from an old lady at ringside and he is BEATING PSYCHO RIGHT IN THE KNEE WITH IT REPEATEDLY! Cosmetics, change, and tampons are flying all over the place as he repeatedly beats Psycho with it!
VIC WATERS: Look at that woman scream in terror. She had no idea what she was getting into when she came here today.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is trying to crawl away… The American takes the straps from the purse and wraps them around Psycho’s neck! HE USES THE PURSE IN ORDER TO DO A CAMEL CLUTCH! The ref is coming out to see if Psycho is going to give up!
VIC WATERS: Psycho will never give up, but this will at least take the wind out of him if he holds in on long enough.
BRANDON JACOBS: THE AMERICAN REALLY PULLS BACK ON THE PURSE… THE PURSE STRAP SNAPS! That may have just saved Pyscho!
VIC WATERS: They don’t make purses like they use to anymore, do they? BRANDON JACOBS: A battered and beaten Psycho tries to crawl away… HE IS GOING UNDER THE RING! The American grabs his legs…. PSYCHO BLOWS A FIRE extinguisher right in the American’s face!
VIC WATERS: Let me tell you something, the stuff in those things can really burn your eyes. It make look funny to the masses, but that stuff is like MACE!
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho uses the ring to help himself up…. HE IS MOTIONING FOR PEOPLE TO MOVE OUT OF THE WAY! The American is swinging his arms, flaring at will hoping that he will hit something! PSYCHO COMES FROM BEHIND WITH A WAISTLOCK…. OH MY GOD… GERMAN RELEASE SUPLEX INTO THE ROWS OF CHAIRS IN THE CROWD!
CROWD: BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! BRANDON JACOBS: The American is out of it! Psycho pulls the steel ring barrier closer to the ring. PSYCHO IS CLIMBING ONTO THE RING APRON!
VIC WATERS: What is he going to do now? BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second, HERE COMES RAT FINK WITH A SHOT! PSYCHO DUCKS AND NAILS HIM WITH A RIGHT INSTEAD! He looks back at the American who is getting up slowly…. PSYCHO USES THE SECOND ROPE TO PROJECTED HIMSELF INTO THE CROWD ONTO THE AMERICAN WITH AN ASAI MOONSAULT!
VIC WATERS: These fans are behind Psycho all the way even though he was showing no mercy to Rat Fink earlier.
BRANDON JACOBS: Fans still remember what Rat Fink did to Manson and they still hate him for that. Both men are down in the crowd… Psycho is starting to look tired. His inactivity in the ring may be catching up to him in this match…. PSCYHO CRAWLS OVER TO THE AMERICAN AND MAKES THE COVER! The ref goes down to make the count! 1---------------------------2---------------------KICKOUT! The American somehow managed to kickout!
VIC WATERS: Psycho has nailed him with everything but the kitchen sink and the night is still young, we may just see the kitchen sink the way these two are going at it.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink is coming out of the ring and he climbs over the barrier, Rat Fink is rested up and he ready go again! Psycho gets up…. RAT FINK AND PSYCHO EXCHANGE BLOWS BACK AND FORTH! Rat Fink rakes the eyes of Psycho…. HE TACKLES HIM DOWN TO THE GROUND!
VIC WATERS: Ground and pound time baby! BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink pounds away on the face of Psycho! HE IS REALLY LAYING INTO HIM FOR TRYING TO CHOKE HIM OUT EARLIER!
VIC WATERS: You know what they say about payback. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink gets up off of Psycho and he grabs an old man! Psycho turns over and tries to get unsuccessfully to get up… OH MAN, RAT FINK IS TAKING THE BELT OFF OF THE OLD MAN…. HE PULLS THE BELT OUT AND THE OLD MAN SPINS AND FALLS TO THE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: Violence against the elderly, GOLD! BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK STARTS WAILING ON THE BACK OF PSYCHO WITH THE BELT! CROWD: ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! SIX! SEVEN! EIGHT! NINE! BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink rears back for one more… HE STOPS AND POINTS AT HIS HEAD AND CROWD BOOS! Rat Fink takes the belt and he ties around the feet of Psycho! Rat Fink is helping the American up… HE YELLS OUT FIRE AND POINTS TO THE KNEES OF PSYCHO!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink is a brilliant general. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink grabs Psycho and holds him still as The American lays into him with strikes to the legs repeatedly! Psycho is wincing in pain as American is laying into him with really loud shots! Rat Fink calls him off and throws Psycho down to the ground! RAT FINK MAKES THE COVER!
VIC WATERS: This could be it right here! BRANDON JACOBS: The ref goes down to make the count! 1-----------------2------------KICKOUT! A frustrated Rat Fink picks Psycho up and walks him towards the back to the concession stand!
VIC WATERS: SHADES OF MEMPHIS! BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO RAKES THE EYES OF RAT FINK! Psycho sees Pizza Hut pepperoni bar pizza and HE PICKS IT UP AND RAMS IT INTO RAT FINK’S FACE!
VIC WATERS: That pizza is right out of the oven so it is hot as hell! BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink is whipping the pizza out of his face… PSYCHO HAS MUSTARD! HE AIMS IT AT RAT FINK BUT RAT FINK DUCKS…. OH MAN! IT GOT THE AMERICAN IN THE EYES WHO WAS COMING OVER TO HELP RAT FINK!
VIC WATERS: IT IS A ONE ON ONE MATCH NOW! BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho grabs the sauerkraut and throws it at Rat Fink! RAT FINK CHARGES AND THEY ARE BATTLING ON THE CHECKOUT COUNTER! Psycho reverses it and RAMS RAT FINK’S HEAD INTO THE CASH REGISTER! Psycho makes the cover! 1------------------------2-------------------RAT FINK ROLLS OVER AND PSYCHO CRASHES ONTO THE FLOOR! The ref goes around the counter… the ref makes the count! 1--------------------------2---------------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: I never thought I see a series of nearfalls like that. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho gets up and he looks at the hamburger grill and looks down at Rat Fink… GOOD LORD! This could be the end for Rat Fink!
VIC WATERS: Psycho has snapped! BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink is trying to hold on to the shelves under the counter… BUT PSYCHO IS TOO STRONG! Psycho pulls Rat Fink away … WAIT A SECOND RAT FINK HAS AN OLD SCHOOL HEINZE KETCHUP BOTTLE IN HIS HANDS…. HE CRACKS PSYCHO IN THE BACK OF THE HEAD WITH IT!
VIC WATERS: OH MAN! BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK MAKES THE COVER! 1---------------------------2--------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match AND STILL BAD WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION…. RAT FINK!
::Rat Fink grabs the belt and the blinded American and they head for the back:: BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we have to go clean up this mess, but when we comeback, things are going to get even more chaotic with a six man street fight between Shawn Hart, Diablo, and Jared Wells against the team of Alex Wylde, Sean Edmunds, and Pulsar…. STAY TUNED!
