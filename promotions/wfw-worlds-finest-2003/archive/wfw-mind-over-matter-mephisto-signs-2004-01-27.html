WFW Mind Over Matter: Mephisto Signs

27 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 27 January 2004 (BEFORE THE SHOW IN WYLDE’S OFFICE) ::Wylde is in a dark room at his desk talking to a hooded figure who is repeatedly nodding::
WYLDE: Exploding Barbed Wire Death Match? Anarky? At WFW Great Expectations PPV coming up? Mephisto, I smell buyrate. But WFW would be on my ass if I was paying someone when I don’t even know who it is. How much money do you want?
MEPHISTO: ::shakes his head to indicate that he doesn’t want any money:: WYLDE: You are willing to work for free? ::Wylde pushes a contract across the table in front of Mephisto who quickly signs the deal::
::Mephisto gets up from his chair and hands Wylde the contract. Mephisto takes out a lighter::
WYLDE: I’m sorry Mephisto, but there is no smoking in here. ::Mephisto sets the desk on fire in the shape of a pentagram as a horrified Wylde looks on as the masked mask laughs as he leaves the room::
::Mephisto vs. Anarky: Exploding Barbed Wire Death Match: Great Expectations flashes onto the screen::
(RINGSIDE) VIC WATERS: I’d be upset if I was Wylde too. Do you know how much that desk COSTED? That was handcrafted in Italy.
JONATHAN MARX: I wonder who the masked man is. BRANDON JACOBS: Who hasn’t Anarky pissed off at one time or another? It could be practically anyone. It could be Maelstrom who has been waiting for years to get his shot at Anarky but has repeatedly turned away. It could be Diablo who hasn’t been seen since his injury at the hands of Anarky. It could be Manson who is still looking for revenge. It could be Mike Randalls who Anarky feuded with years ago. The list goes on and on.
JONATHAN MARX: You’ll tell me after the show right? BRANDON JACOBS: I really don’t know who it is yet. Nobody ever tells me anything. VIC WATERS: That is because you blabber on and on like a woman. You couldn’t keep a secret if your life depended on it.
BRANDON JACOBS: Quiet down you Yanni lover, folks, when we comeback, it will be time for the three way dance with the winner getting to name their own stipulation to their PPV matches!
VIC WATERS: ::looks at Brandon with despise for the Yanni comment:: BRANDON JACOBS: Stay tuned!
