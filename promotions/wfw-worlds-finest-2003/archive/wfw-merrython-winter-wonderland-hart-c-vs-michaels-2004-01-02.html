WFW Merrython: Winter Wonderland: Hart (c) vs. Michaels

2 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 2 January 2004 (RETURN TO ROCKEFELLER CENTER) BRANDON JACOBS: Are you ready Vic? VIC WATERS: I’m ready as I’ll ever be. BRANDON JACOBS: That FAO Schwartz match was a hell of a battle, can this main event top it?
VIC WATERS: I don’t know, I guess we’ll find out. BRANDON JACOBS: Once again, just a reminder. Since there will be no ring in Central Park and the fight will just happen in a large open area, there will be no ring introductions. It is time for the Winter Wonderland Match between the WFW World Heavyweight Champion Shawn Hart and Scotty Michaels. Will Scotty Michaels fulfill his goal of being champion before the end of the WFW Calendar Year by beating Shawn Hart? LET’S SEND IT TO CENTRAL PARK TO FIND OUT!
(CAMERA FADES INTO CENTRAL PARK) ::large crowd has gathered with chairs and picnic baskets in Central Park to witness the main event, including Shawn Matthews who is with his wheelchair bound brother Steve::
::the ref is reading both men the rules, the ref rings the bell:: BRANDON JACOBS: When Scotty Michaels arrived, he looked a bit inebriated. VIC WATERS: He looked a bit drunk too. He still does. BRANDON JACOBS: They say drinking calms the nerves. But that is no reason to show up in this condition for a WFW World Heavyweight Title match…. SHAWN HART SLAPS SCOTTY MICHAELS ACROSS THE THROAT IN DISGUST! Scotty Michaels wobbles back and forth and rubs his cheek.
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart is infuriated at the arrogance that Scotty Michaels has displayed with his promise and showing up in this condition. He wants nothing better than to beat the crap out of him.
BRANDON JACOBS: SCOTTY MICHAELS PUSHES SHAWN HART! Hart moves a couple of steps backwards…. SHAWN HART PUSHES SCOTTY MICHAELS ONTO THE GROUND!
VIC WATERS: Scotty Michaels went down really easy too. He really is drunk. BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart walks over to Scotty Michaels who is trying to get away and KICKS HIM IN THE FACE! Shawn Hart jumps on Scotty and pounds his head into the frozen ground!
VIC WATERS: It has to be in the thirties out here easily. You couldn’t tap that ground with a pick.
BRANDON JACOBS: SCOTTY MICHAELS GRABS THE LEGS OF HART AND ROLLS HIM DOWN ON HIS SHOULDERS! The ref goes to make the count! 1-----------------------------2------------------KICKOUT! Shawn Hart was almost caught off guard!
VIC WATERS: Michaels is a tricky bastard, Hart needs to watch out. BRANDON JACOBS: Both men are back up to their feet and they lock up…. HART NAILS HIM WITH AN INVERTED ATOMIC DROP! Scott Michaels is in a great deal of pain…. SHAWN HART NAILS HIM WITH A CLOTHESLINE THAT TAKES HIM NEARLY OUT OF HIS BOOTS! 1----------------------------------------------2-------------------------KICKOUT! Michaels just got the shoulder up!
VIC WATERS: You wouldn’t think you’d want to slow do your reaction time in a matches where seconds count but Michaels has done that and if he isn’t careful, it could cost him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart grabs Michaels by the hair and he is leading him through the park as the crowd moves with them…. HART DRIVES MICHAELS HEAD FIRST INTO THE WOODEN PARK BENCH! Michaels falls over onto the ground!
VIC WATERS: This park is the home of many drunk derelicts and is always willing to welcome one more.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart stands on the wooden park bench and he is pulling Scotty Michaels onto the bench…. Hart is trying to pick him up for a TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER BUT SCOTTY MICHAELS REVERSES IT INTO A TOMBSTONE OF HIS OWN…. SCOTTY MICHAELS JUMPS OFF THE BENCH WITH HART AND DELIVERS A TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER ONTO THE CONCRETE!
VIC WATERS: Michaels’ drunkenness actually helped Michaels there because he Hart was unable to get Michaels up properly and finish the move.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is really out of it…. Michaels is picking Hart off the ground. VIC WATERS: I’m surprised he isn’t going for a pinfall attempt here. BRANDON JACOBS: SCOTTY MICHAELS PICKS HIM UP…. GERMAN SUPLEX ONTO THE CONCRETE PATHWAY! Hart is screaming in pain! This doesn’t look good for Hart.
VIC WATERS: While the concrete and the frozen ground are almost equally hard, what makes the blacktop even worse is the unevenness of the ground. You can really scrape yourself up with the concrete.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is walking over to the street vendor across the pathway and he STEALS THE VAT OF MUSTARD! Scotty Michaels puts the vat of mustard on the ground and climbs up onto the park bench…. SCOTTY MICHAELS DOUBLE FOOT STOMPS THE MUSTARD AND IT SPRAYS INTO THE EYES OF HART AND HART IS TRYING TO DESPERATELY GET IT OFF HIS FACE!
VIC WATERS: Insert lame joke about Shawn Hart being a hot dog here. BRANDON JACOBS: A fan has rolled his Poland Spring bottle to Hart and Hart is trying to use it to wash the mustard out of his eyes…. SCOTTY MICHAELS KICKS IT AWAY FROM HART AND REPRIMANDS THE LITTLE GIRL FROM GETTING INVOLVED!
VIC WATERS: A regular Steve Bartman she is. BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart was able to get enough water out before Scotty Michaels kicked the bottle away in order to clean his face enough to see what is going on. SCOTTY MICHAELS PICKS UP HART AND HE LOCKS HIM IN THE SLEEPER HOLD!
VIC WATERS: With all the shots Hart has taken so far, if he can keep this on, he may be able to win the title with the sleeper hold.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart looks like he is going out! The ref is there and he is checking to see if Hart wants to give up.
VIC WATERS: Hart won’t give up. After all the battles he had with Doc to win the title, he is never going to say “I Quit.”
BRANDON JACOBS: HART IS PICKING MICHAELS UP ON HIS BACK AND HE IS HEADED TOWARD A TREE! Hart ducks down AND HE RAMS MICHAELS IN HEAD FIRST AND HE CRASHES DOWN ON THE GRASS! Hart makes the cover! 1---------------------------------------------2------------------------------KICKOUT! Michaels got the shoulder up!
VIC WATERS: If Scotty Michaels wasn’t drunk before, after that shot to the head, he certain is now.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart picks a branch which has fallen off the tree and HE BEGINS BEATING SCOTTY MICHAELS WITH IT! Scotty Michaels is trying to roll away but in the process Hart has nailed him with several kidney shots as he is trying to make his way back to the concrete path!
VIC WATERS: And nature is suppose to be peaceful…. BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart is coming after Scotty…. SHAWN HART PICKS UP A ROCK AND HE THROWS IT AT SCOTTY MICHAELS NAILING HIM IN THE BEHIND! Michaels gets up and he is trying to get away.
VIC WATERS: Scotty Michaels is just a tad slower today and that is giving Shawn Hart the advantage.
BRANDON JACOBS: SHAWN HART SPRINTS AFTER HIM AND TACKLES HIM AS HE IS TRYING TO GET AWAY! Michaels is trying to kick Hart off of him and he nails him with a shot to the face!
VIC WATERS: Scotty Michaels needs a breather in order to get his game plan back together. BRANDON JACOBS: SCOTTY MICHAELS GRABS A BUTTERED POPCORN FROM AN OLD MAN AND HE TAKES A HANDFUL WITH THE HOT GREASY BUTTER ON IT AND RUBS IT IN THE FACE OF SHAWN HART! WAIT A SECOND, THE OLD MAN IS ATTACKING SCOTTY MICHAELS WITH HIS CANE!
VIC WATERS: New Yorkers are tough. BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels pushes the old man down and over the bench! He grabs the wooden cane and begins assailing Hart with several hard shots to the ribs! MICHAELS BREAKS THE CANE OVER HART AND HART IS DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Hart may be better off taking the pinfall and trying to win the title back than taking all of this abuse.
BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels picks the winded Hart up off the ground…. HE NAILS WITH A BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX! Hart cried out on impact! Michaels is standing over Hart and he is taunting him.
VIC WATERS: Michaels can take all the time he wants now, he has this match won. BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, MINION IS COMING THROUGH THE CROWD WITH A CHAIR AND HE PLASTERS MICHAELS OVER THE TOP OF THE HEAD! Minion is repeatedly pounding Michaels over and over with the chair! Security grabs the chair away from Minion and Minion laughs at them.
VIC WATERS: Minion wants to be the one to take the title from Hart and he isn’t going to let anyone else do it.
BRANDON JACOBS: Minion picks up Michaels…. T-BONE SUPLEX ONTO THE CONCRETE! The police are surrounding Minion with their guns and are instructing him to move away. Minion taunts Michaels and leaves the park.
VIC WATERS: Minion is the great equalizer. Now we have two men who are unable to fight. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is checking on both men to see if they want to continue. Both men are in a great deal of pain but they are shaking their head yes. These are two of the toughest men in all of WFW.
VIC WATERS: I know I couldn’t have taken all of the abuse they have taken today. BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is crawling over to cover Michaels…. 1--------------------------------2-------------------------KICKOUT! Oh so close. Hart is crawling over to the bench and he is using it to help himself out. HART IS CALLING OUT TO THE CROWD…. HE IS SIGNALING FOR THE HART ATTACK!
VIC WATERS: It is all a matter of waiting know, Michaels can’t take this move in his condition.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is slowly getting up….. HART KICKS MICHAELS IN THE STOMACH…. MICHAELS BLOCKS THE HART ATTACK AND PUSHES HART FORWARD AND HE TUMBLES OVER THE PARK BENCH!
VIC WATERS: Michael in his exhaustion has collapsed down to the ground on his knees. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels has been waiting so long for this moment, he isn’t ready to give up his dream just yet.
VIC WATERS: Neither is Hart, this is his livelihood. Wearing the WFW World Heavyweight Title brings him fame, fortune, and prestige. He can ride in limousines and get any woman he wants and unlike some champions in some other leagues, he is old enough to still do something with them.
BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS GRABS A GUITAR FROM A HIPPIE WATCHING IN THE CROWD! He is coming after Hart. Hart is trying to get up and all he can see is the feet of Michaels.
VIC WATERS: Hart is still out it, he may have banged his head after he tumbled over the bench.
BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS SWINGS BUT HART BLOCKS THE SHOT! THEY ARE FIGHTING OVER THE GUITAR!
VIC WATERS: It is a shame to see an expensive guitar like that get trashed. Kids today have no respect for other’s personal property.
BRANDON JACOBS: HART WINS CONTROL OF THE GUITAR AND HE SMASHES IT OVER MICHAELS’ HEAD! Michaels goes down!
VIC WATERS: Way too many foreign objects have been used here tonight. BRANDON JACOBS: Hart takes a piece of the guitar and cuts Michaels open! MICHAELS HAS A CRIMSON MASK!
VIC WATERS: It looks like he cut him a little too deep, but that may be his plan, if Michaels can’t finish this and the referee rules the excess of blood as hazard. Hart would walk out of the match as champion.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is calling for the El Codo Explosivo! IF HE NAILS THIS, THE MATCH IS OVER!
VIC WATERS: Come on Michaels! BRANDON JACOBS: Hart picks Michaels up…. HART WINDS UP AND NAILS MICHAELS WITH THE EL CODO EXPLOSIVO! Michaels goes down like he was shot! Hart goes down for the cover! 1-------------------------------2------------------ STEVE MATTHEWS JUMPS OUT OF HIS WHEEL CHAIR AND MAKES THE SAVE!
VIC WATERS: HE CAN WALK! IT IS A MIRACLE! BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is getting up looking bewildered. STEVE MATTHEWS GRABS THE WHEELCHAIR AND NAILS SHAWN HART OVER THE HEAD WITH IT! WHAT IS GOING ON HERE?! Steve Matthews grabs the unconscious Scotty Michaels and puts his body over Shawn Hart! The ref goes down for the count! 1-----------------------------------------2--------------------------------------------------3!
::huge crowd pop:: RING ANNOUNCER: (from Rockefeller Center of the speakers setup at Central Park) THE WINNER OF THE MATCH AND NEW WFW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION…. “THE VIEWER’S CHOICE” SCOTTY MICHAELS!
CROWD: SCOTTY! SCOTTY! SCOTTY! ::Scotty Michaels grabs the title belt and falls down to his knees and kisses the ground:: BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty did it just as he promised! HE HAS BROUGHT DOWN THE REIGN OF C.O.C.K.S. WHICH HAS TERRORIZED THE LEAGUE FOR OVER SIX MONTHS NOW! He is dirty, lying, cheat…. BUT HE DID IT!
VIC WATERS: I still can’t believe it…. Michaels master minded the whole thing, he is an f’n genius.
::Hart is beginning to wake up and Michaels cues the video to roll which is playing on a portable jumbotron at the park::
(FADEIN to Scotty Michaels, being driven up to a Central Park bench, in the car we saw his doctor pulling up in during an earlier promo. Scotty gets out, lays on the bench, and covers himself with a newspaper...)
(CUT TO: Scotty Michaels laying on the floor in his hotel room, next to his bed.. we hear him *****ing to the camera man..)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Damn, I can't wait till this whole angle's over. This beard's making my face itch...
(CUT TO: Scotty Michaels standing with a bartender, holding an empty bottle of vodka under a faucet, filling it with water...)
::a furious Hart is slowly getting up:: SHAWN HART: YOU BASTARD! BRANDON JACOBS: SHAWN HART CHARGES SCOTTY MICHAELS BUT STEVE MATTHEWS GETS IN HIS WAY! Hart is telling Steve to get out of his way….. STEVE MATTHEWS RIPS OFF HIS MOVIE MASK…. OH NO! HE HAS DONE IT AGAIN!
VIC WATERS: First Manson, now Hart… BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER HAS SCREWED SHAWN HART! Shawn Hart’s mouth has dropped again…. SHAWN MATTHEWS NAILS HART FROM BEHIND WITH A CHAIR!
VIC WATERS: Matthews may have done him a favor, all of this is too much to take all at once.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Matthews and Luster raise their hands in victory….. SCOTTY MICHAELS PLASTERS SHAWN MATTHEWS IN THE HEAD WITH THE CHAIR!
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Sorry Shawn, you weren’t in our plans. ::Michaels walks over the body of Shawn Matthews to the barely conscious body of Shawn Hart and begins to speak:: Shawn Hart, you know... everybody was right. This was all an ELABORATE SET-UP! You see, Steve Matthews never existed... it was just a notion I came up with to make my opponents think that I really was down and out. I lost the North American title, WHEN I wanted to lose it. I let myself get pinned by Sean Edmunds, because I did want to win the title by the end of the year. Oh yeah, and as you saw in that little video package... all the promos were set up too. The only thing that's real, I do have a drinking problem... but I haven't touched a drop of alcohol in months. Last but not least, I'd like to thank Luster... a man who hasn't gotten the attention he's so rightly deserved. Now it's not only MY TIME to shine, it's his as well! WFW, when you thought that I couldn't get any lower, you just saw it happen. Ladies and gentlemen, your NEW WFW world heavyweight champ, "The Viewer's Choice" SCOTTY MICHAELS!!
(FADEOUT, as Michaels walks away with Luster by his side... holding the WFW world title high in the air...)
(ROCKEFELLER CENTER) BRANDON JACOBS: I didn’t like what C.O.C.K.S. did to WFW for all those months, but this simply isn’t right, Shawn Hart was a great champion. I take that back, Shawn Hart was our GREATEST CHAMPION. He deserves better than to have it end like this.
VIC WATERS: I wouldn’t wish what happened today on anyone. BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is going to get revenge. He isn’t going to let this go and he is going to make Michaels pay. God save the champ. On that note, folks we will be back in mid January at the Jadwin Gym on the campus of Princeton University for WFW: Mind Over Matter, our last show before the PPV. Thanks you joining us everyone, goodnight.
