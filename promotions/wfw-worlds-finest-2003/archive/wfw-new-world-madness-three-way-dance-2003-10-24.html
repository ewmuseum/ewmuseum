WFW: New World Madness: Three Way Dance

24 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 24 October 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: First off, lets go over the rules. This match is an ultimate jeopardy match fought under BAD anything goes rules... If the team of Scotty Michaels & Rat Fink win, they can pick the stipulations for their match at the PPV If the team of Manson and Felix Red win, Diablo, Tiny, Luster, P-Nut, HEEL are banned from ringside for the WFW North American Title match at the PPV If Richard Gideon/Psycho win, Richard Gideon gets to pick his stipulation for the match with Scotty Michaels and Psycho gets the first crack at the WFW North American Champ after the PPV. Got it?
VIC WATERS: Sounds simple enough… BRANDON JACOBS: This match can go so many different ways, Richard Gideon & Psycho have the most experience tag teaming together, but Scotty Michaels & Rat Fink are both singles champions. You can’t rule out Manson & Felix Red though who are both on hot streaks going into match and if they win this, they would help their cause tremendously.
VIC WATERS: What are you talking about? Michaels & Rat Fink are the champions. BRANDON JACOBS: The two best singles wrestlers don’t always win in a tag match because a match like this is about TEAM WORK.
VIC WATERS: Team work, scheme work. BRANDON JACOBS: Well, there is only one way to find out, let’s send this match to ringside and get this started!
(CUEUP: Megalomaniac by KMFDM) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, team #1, the WFW North American Champion and the BAD World Heavyweight Champ, the team of Scotty Michaels & Rat Fink!
:: Scotty Michaels and Rat Fink come down to a mixed reaction from the crowd with his cheesy looking Sci-Fi Robot and Satan Clause and enters the ring::
(CUEUP: “Remember” by Disturbed) RING ANNOUNCER: And now team number two, the team of the former WFW World Heavyweight Champion and the man who gave Doc Silver more fits than anyone else in his career, the team of Psycho and Richard Gideon!
::Gideon and Psycho walk out together and enter the ring:: (CUEUP: "Mechnical Animals" by Marilyn Manson) RING ANNOUNCER: And last but not least, team number #3, the team of the former WFW North American Champion and the most innovative wrestling in wrestling today, the team of Michael Manson and Felix Red!
::Felix Red and Manson run down and slide under the bottom rope:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: All four men attack Felix Red and Manson! PSYCHO CLOTHESLINES FELIX RED OVER THE TOP ROPE AND GOES OUT AFTER HIM! IT IS A THREE ON ONE ON MANSON!
VIC WATERS: Manson is a hated, hated man. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink is holding the arms of Manson and Michaels and Gideon are taking turns punching away!
VIC WATERS: Gideon despises Rat Fink and Scotty Michaels, but he knows Manson is a tough man to beat and he needs to weaken him down in order to keep him from playing a factor in this match
BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON IS CHOPPING AT THE CHEST OF MANSON! ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! SIX! SEVEN! EIGHT! NINE! MICHAELS PUSHES GIDEON ASIDE BECAUSE HE WANTS HIS TURN… Michaels winds up for a punch…. GIDEON GRABS THE ARM OF MICHAELS .... SHORT ARM CLOTHESLINE!
VIC WATERS: Gideon was hogging all the Manson to himself. He had no right to do that. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink drops Manson and HE CHARGES GIDEON AND STARTS POUNDING ON HIM WITH MOUNTED PUNCHES!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink is not a guy you want to upset. BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO ON THE OUTSIDE JUST NAILED FELIX RED ON THE FLOOR WITH A MOONSAULT! Michaels is stomping away on Gideon now while Rat Fink is still wailing on him with punches. Psycho rolls back into the ring and DIVES SHOULDER FIRST ONTO RAT FINK!
VIC WATERS: Betty bar the door… BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels picks up Gideon…. BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX! Michaels picks Gideon up again…. ANOTHER BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX! Michaels goes for the pin! 1----------------------2------------MANSON KICKS MICHAELS OFF OF GIDEON!
VIC WATERS: Match almost ended earlier there… BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is livid about the attack earlier… MANSON KICKS MICHAELS IN THE STOMACH AND NAILS HIM WITH A DDT! Psycho whips Rat Fink towards Manson…. KNEE TO THE MIDSECTION OF RAT FINK…. HE NAILS RAT FINK WITH A DDT! This crowd is coming unglued!
VIC WATERS: This match is getting quite hard to follow. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson picks Gideon off the ground top help him up…. GIDEON PUSHES MANSON! He is signaling to Manson that he is going to take his WFW North American Title and he is going to be the first one to challenge him for the WFW North American Title. MANSON CLOTHESLINE GIDEON OUT OF HIS BOOTS! Psycho goes for the pin on Michaels who was taken out with the DDT! Psycho covers! 1--------------------------------2-------------------------KICKOUT! Michaels just gets the shoulder up! Psycho is going to try to cover Rat Fink now! 1----------------------------2-----------------KICKOUT! Rat Fink managed to get the shoulder up as well despite being spiked really bad by Manson.
VIC WATERS: Psycho may have been a bit late on that, but it was worth a shot. BRANDON JACOBS: Here comes Felix Red and he is going up to the top rope…. Psycho doesn’t see him….. MOONSAULT INTO A DDT AND PSYCHO IS OUT! Manson pulls up Gideon by the hair… MANSON NAILS HIM WITH THE SWEET DREAMS! Both Felix Red and Manson cover at the same time! 1-------------------------2----------------------- MICHAELS AND FELIX RED DIVE TO BREAK UP THE RESPECT PINFALLS!
VIC WATERS: Many people feel that Psycho isn’t back to his old form yet so he is a walking target in the ring right now.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red and Manson get up and they head over to Michaels… Manson is signal for Felix Red to go to the top…. MANSON PICKS UP SCOTTY MICHAELS AND HE NAILS HIM WITH THE WILD BUCK BOMB!
VIC WATERS: GOOD LORD! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson picks up Scotty Michaels up onto his shoulder and drops down to one knee… Felix Red is perched up on the top… FELIX RED NAILS SCOTTY MICHAELS WITH A BICYCLE KICK FROM THE TOP!
CROWD: BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink runs over Manson… HE NAILS HIM WITH THE SHINING WIZARD! RAT FINK GRABS THROWS SCOTTY MICHAELS THROUGH THE ROPES AND TO THE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink obviously thought Michaels could have been pinned so he stopped the pinfall the only way he knew how, he threw him out of the ring.
BRANDON JACOBS: With partners like Rat Fink, who needs enemies. Rat Fink stands over Manson and he is taunting him to get up…. OH MAN! RAT FINK DOESN’T SEE FELIX RED BEHIND HIM…. FELIX RED TAPS HIM ON THE SHOULDER AND HE NAILED RAT FINK WITH A SPINNING HEEL KICK WHEN HE TURNED AROUND!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red knows his martial arts and a shot like Rat Fink took right there could put you out for a week.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red helps Manson up…. Felix Red is calling for a double snap suplex… they grabs the tights of Rat Fink…. THEY NAIL IT! Psycho is getting up…. PSYCHO RAMS HIS SHOULDER RIGHT INTO THE BACK OF THE KNEE OF FELIX RED FROM BEHIND! Felix Red is grabbing his knee in pain!
VIC WATERS: Psycho clipped him because after the moonsault into a DDT, he knows now that with Felix Red being such a big factor in this match, that the pinfall could come at anytime if he had two health legs and Psycho has now solved that problem.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson starts pounding on Psycho… Manson whips Psycho off the ropes…. HURRICANRANA! Psycho looks dazed as tries to get up…. MANSON CLOTHESLINES PSYCHO OUT OF THE RING!
VIC WATERS: The less men in the ring, the easier it is to get the pinfall. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson now has his choice of Gideon or Rat Fink, but his partner is still down so…. GIDEON RUSHES MANSON! Gideon is repeatedly nailing Manson with knee lifts and Manson has been brought down to his knees! Gideon picks Manson up…. G-FORCE ROLLING BRAINBUSTERS! GIDEON NAILS HIM WITH ONE…. GIDEON NAILS HIM WITH TWO…… HE LIFTING MANSON UP AGAIN…. HE NAILS HIM FOR THE THIRD TIME! WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES SHAWN HART AND HE IS HEADING TO THE BOOTH!
VIC WATERS: Hart comes just in time to take away the spotlight from the action of the ring when Manson getting what he richly deserves.
BRANDON JACOBS: Well fans, I guess we're being joined here by our world heavyweight champion...
SHAWN HART: Indeed you are Mr. Jacobs... Vic 'The Dick'... I saw that JACKHOLE Manson stinkin' it up like granny's unwashed panties backstage and felt it my duty, AS WFW Champion, to get down here and save the quarter-hour!
BRANDON JACOBS: Richard Gideon nails him for a FIFTH TIME! A limping Felix Red gets up… Gideon sees him and finally lets go of Manson…. FELIX RED CHARGES GIDEON BUT GIDEON DUCKS DOWN AND BACK BODY DROPS FELIX RED ONTO THE GROUND! Gideon gets up and he throws Felix Red to the outside! Gideon makes the cover on Manson! 1----------------------------2------------------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Damn, that was close. Felix Red managed to buy Manson enough time in order to kickout. So why are YOU here Hart?
SHAWN HART: Eeeeeeasy tiger, one more outburst like that and you'll be Tiny's teddy bear for the night. Anyhow, I'm not here to steal anybody's thunder, I just gotta say, as much as I LOATHE people like Scotty Michaels and Alex Wylde, Mr. Manson there... as his face gets smashed into the canvas by Richard Gideon, has just won the Wanker of the Week award. So at the P-P-V, I'm making it my personal mission to utterly DISGRACE him like he ain't never been disgraced before!"
BRANDON JACOBS: Well, first thing's first Shawn, because if I'm not mistaken, you still have to make it through Luster. GIDEON PICKS UP MANSON AND TRIES FOR ANOTHER SMALL PACKAGE! The ref goes down to make the count! 1--------------------------------------2--------------------- RAT FINK SAVED MANSON FROM THE THREE!
SHAWN HART: "Small Potatoes, BJ... may I call you that? BJ?! Heh, small potatoes... aaaaaaaand I'M bringin' the gravy!"
VIC ATERS: "How clever..." BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK THROWS MANSON TO THE OUTSIDE AND SETS HIS EYES ON GIDEON! SHAWN HART: "I make it a point to be as such, but alaaaaaas... I've got a match to prepare for. Mazel Tov!! ...and keep my seat warm!!"
::Shawn Hart takes a sip out of Vic’s Poland Spring Water and runs down to Manson on the outside AND SPITS IT INTO HIS EYES!::
BRANDON JACOBS: How disgraceful. OH MAN! Manson is yelling like he has been maced! WHAT WAS IN YOUR BOTTLE OF POLAND SPRING WATER VIC?
VIC WATERS: Vodka of course, how do you think I make it through all these lonely days without Doc around?
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels who has been lounging around on the outside grabs the blinded Manson and he and Psycho take turns laying into Manson on the outside.
VIC WATERS This isn’t going to be pretty. BRANDON JACOBS: Richard Gideon and Rat Fink are trading blows back and forth… Rat Fink is gaining the advantage! HE WHIPS GIDEON OFF THE ROPES AND HE NAILS HIM WITH A STEINERLINE! Gideon is trying to get up….
VIC WATERS: Richard Gideon has a lot of heart. BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK PICKS UP GIDEON OFF THE MAT…. HE NAILS HIM WITH STALL SUPLEX! Looks like Gideon really took that shot on the head…. GIDEON IS STILL TRYING TO GET UP! Rat Fink picks Gideon up… HE NAILS HIM WITH THE FALCON’S ARROW! Gideon is out!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink is on fire here! BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho stops pounding on Manson and he runs into the ring… RAT FINK TRIES TO STEINERLINE HIM BUT PSYCHO DUCKS….. PSYCHO NAILS RAT FINK WITH A YAZUKA KICK AND RAT FINK IS PROPELLED THROUGH THE ROPES AND TO THE FLOOR! Psycho goes out after Rat Fink.
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink had Gideon dead…. DAMN PSYCHO! BRANDON JACOBS: A dazed Felix Red is getting up off the ground and he sees Gideon down in the ring…. FELIX RED ROLLS IN! Gideon is starting to stir….
VIC WATERS: Felix Red needs to pounce now if he wants to win this one for his team. FELIX RED CALLS FOR HIS FINISHER! Felix Red picks Gideon up off the mat… ECSTASY OF AGONY (MODIFIED ANGLE SLAM)! The ref goes down for the count! 1-----------------------------------2---------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winners of the match…. The team of Felix Red and Michael Manson! BRANDON JACOBS: YES! VIC WATERS: Gideon was just in the wrong place at the wrong time. It looks like Felix Red finally has shown that he can beat Gideon, the question now is if he can beat both Gideon and Scotty Michaels at the same time to win the title at the PPV.
BRANDON JACOBS: With the win, Diablo, Tiny, Luster, P-Nut, HEEL are banned from ringside for the WFW World and North American Title matches. If Hart, Luster, or Michaels want to win in their PPV title matches, they are going to have to do it without the help of all their bodies.
VIC WATERS: What has Scotty Michaels and Luster ever done to deserved that? BRANDON JACOBS: What have they done…. What haven’t they done? Michaels has screwed over both Manson and Copycat recently for starters and C.O.C.K.S….. you saw that C.O.C.K.S. did to Manson tonight and this wasn’t even for the damn title.
VIC WATERS: You are a very untrusting person. BRANDON JACOBS: Okay folks, we need time to clean up all the bodies, but when comeback, it is time for the main event, Shawn Hart will put up the WFW World Heavyweight Title for the first time against Luster…. Should be a doozey! Don’t you dare switch that dial.
