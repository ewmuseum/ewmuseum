1ST CANDIDATE PHONE SUPPORT SHUTS DOWN NY POWER

15 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 15 August 2003 AND NOW, A MESSAGE FROM WFW'S FIRST PRESIDENTIAL CANDIDATE... (A lightning bolt and the words "NEWSFLASH" appear on the screen) FLOOD OF PHONE SUPPORT FOR WFW'S 1ST PRESIDENTIAL CANDIDATE SHUTS DOWN NORTHEAST POWER! V/O (female): WFW's First Presidential Candidate conducted a secret test of his message and phone line yesterday to disastrous results. Immediately upon opening the line was flooded with calls from devoted fans. Though the official phone number had yet to be uncovered obsessive supporters appear to have found the number anyway.
(The screen shows interlaced shots of people picking up their phones and dialing frantically.)
V/O (female): Within the first 5 minutes of operation, the "1st Presidential Party Line," situated in Southern New Jersey, received an estimated 900 million calls from followers wishing to pledge their support to WFW's First Presidential Candidate. This overload of phone circuits throughout the Northeast caused a massive power failure and shut down through 4 states and two Canadian provinces.
(The screen shows overhead news footage of thousands of people waiting for ferry rides out of New York.)
V/O (female): With the phone line hopelessly jammed, millions of supporters took to the streets in efforts to vocally pledge their support to the First Candidate. Thousands could be seen screaming and crowding the waterfront area closest to New Jersey, where the First Candidate was rumored to be staying.
(The screen shows Transportation workers helping to move people out of subways.) V/O (female): When questioned about the future availability of power to the North East... NJ Transit and NY Port Authority workers said that it is totally dependent on the will of WFW's First Presidential Candidate and how he chooses to have his supporters contact him. They also added that the First Candidate is a truly great and amazing man who emits the odor of greatness with every breath.
(The silhouette of the First Presidential Candidate appears on screen, seated in a chair.) V/O (female): The First Candidate himself is considering organizing a letter writing campaign to allow future followers to contact him without danger to the dozens of non-fans worldwide. He also reminds each and every one of you to remember to vote for the First Candidate on WFW Election day.
(The jacketed silhouette holds up one finger and nods. The screen blanks to the green-and-black 'check' voting logo.)
V/O (female): Thank you and God Bless. THE PRECEDING MESSAGE HAS BEEN PAID FOR BY WORLD'S FINEST WRESTLING.
