Shock & Awe II: Edmunds vs. Pulsar

20 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 20 May 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: That Wylde firing is in indication of the turmoil yet to come isn’t it? VIC WATERS: Pure chaos. Don’t you love it? BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is calling for the bell! Edmunds vs. Pulsar under way! *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar charges Edmunds as he is lifting the rope for Sherri to leave the ring! SHERRI FALLS DOWN TO THE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: They say there is no wrath like a woman scorn, I would never want to get Sherri on my bad side.
BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR WHIPS EDMUNDS OFF THE ROPES…. PULSAR JUMPS UP AND HITS EDMUNDS WITH A SHOULDER BLOCK RIGHT IN THE FACE!
VIC WATERS: A shoulder can feel like a brick wall when it goes right into your face. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is covering up his face as he tries to get up…. PULSAR LOCKS ON A SLEEPER HOLD FROM BEHIND RIGHT ON HIS CHIN!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is a great wrestler, but he doesn’t have a boxer’s chin, he doesn’t take shots to his face very well but that may be why he still has his looks.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is wailing his arms around and he is going out fast! VIC WATERS: It is just like I told you, Pulsar is going to do him in early. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is checking to see if it isn’t a choke… VIC WATERS: There are plenty of people who want to choke Edmunds, but Pulsar is oddly obeying the rules here.
BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS PICKS PULSAR ON HIS BACK AND HE HEADS INTO THE CORNER…. EDMUNDS RAMS HIM BACK FIRST INTO THE TURNBUCKLE!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar is still holding on! BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS TRIES TO RAM HIM IN AGAIN…. THIS TIME PULSAR LETS GO AND HE COLLAPSES IN THE CORNER! Edmunds falls down to his knees and catches his breath.
VIC WATERS: You can tell that Edmunds Is has something taken out of him by the sleeper. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds turns around on his knees and HE STARTS FIRING AWAY WITH PUNCHES TO PULSAR RIGHT IN THE FACE WHO IS COLLAPSED SITTING IN THE CORNER!
VIC WATERS Oompa Loompa offense! BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is bending down to check to see if he is firing closed fists in at Pulsar.
VIC WATERS: You think we would be able to get referees with better vision but the way our refs take abuse, I guess we have to settle for volunteers.
BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is warning Edmunds about the closed fist! EDMUNDS BEGINS TO CHOKE AWAY ON PULSAR!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds isn’t even attempting to hide this from the referee. He is a blatant cheater. Can I switch my alliances mid match?
BRANDON JACOBS: It wouldn’t be the first time…. THE REF IS COUNT! 1--------2-------3-------4 EDMUNDS BREAKS THE HOLD! Edmunds goes for another choke!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds better becareful or the ref is going to send him home early tonight before Pulsar has a chance to beat him.
BRANDON JACOBS: THE REF IS TRYING TO PRY EDMUNDS OFF PULSAR…. EDMUNDS GETS UP AND HE YELLS AT THE REFEREE FOR GETTING INVOLVED IN HIS BUSINESS!
VIC WATERS: Poor guy is just trying to do his job… BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is trying to crawl outside of the ring…. EDMUNDS GRABS THE LEGS OF PULSAR AS HE IS HALF WAY OUT!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar is trying to get to the outside to catch his breath when he has a chance in order to comeback and mount an offensive. He almost made it too.
BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR IS CLINGING ONTO THE SIDE OF THE RING! Edmunds walks over to Pulsar…. EDMUNDS REACHES THROUGH THE ROPES AND RAMS PULSAR’S HEAD INTO THE MAT WITH BOTH HANDS!
VIC WATERS: That edge of the ring is quite hard… BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds lets go and Pulsar falls to the outside of the ring as he smirks to himself.
VIC WATERS: Look at Edmunds’s blatant display of arrogance, that is just the type of thing that will cost him this match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is going to the outside… WAIT A SECOND, PULSAR REACHES UP AND HE GRABS THE BOOT STRAPS OF EDMUNDS AND HE IS TRYING TO PULL EDMUNDS OFF THE APRON!
VIC WATERS: Get him Pulsar! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is trying to shake him off his leg but Pulsar keeps on pulling as Edmunds is using the ropes to keep himself on the apron…. EDMUNDS HOLDS ONTO THE ROPES FOR DEAR LIFE AND KICKS HIM IN THE HEAD WITH THE OTHER FOOT AND PULSAR CRASHES DOWN AND BANGS HIS HEAD ON THE STEEL STAIRS!
VIC WATERS: No damnit! I thought he had him there. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds walks over on the apron to the steels stairs… AND HE STARTS KICKING AWAY AT THE HEAD OF PULSAR REPEATEDLY WITH QUICK KICKS!
VIC WATERS: He is turning Pulsar’s head into a hackeysack. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds wind up for a kick… PULSAR MOVES HIS HEAD OUT OF THE WAY AND EDMUNDS KICKS THE STEEL STEP FULL BLAST! Edmunds grabs his foot in pain…
VIC WATERS: Next thing you know you are going to blame Pulsar for injuring Edmunds’s foot by moving his head out of the way.
BRANDON JACOBS: If the shoe fits… PULSAR JUMPS UP ONTO THE LEG OF EDMUNDS AND PULLS HIM DOWN TO THE GROUND! Edmunds crashes down hard. He barely got his hand down to cushion that blow or he would be toast.
VIC WATERS: He still took a hell of a fall there. BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR PICKS THE INJURED EDMUNDS UP…. BODYSLAM DOWN ONTO THE GROUND! VIC WATERS: Pulsar slammed the already banged up Edmunds onto the ground hard. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is calling for his patented legdrop! Pulsar slides under the bottom rope and sets up on the inside of the ring… Edmunds is down…. SOMERSAULT LEGDROP TO THE OUTSIDE ONTO EDMUNDS! HE HITS IT!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar hit him flat in the face with the bottom of leg and Edmunds really looks out of it.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar picks up Edmunds and rolls him into the ring. Pulsar goes in after him…. SHERRI RUNS OVER AND TRIES TO GRAB PULSAR BUT PULSAR KICKS HER DOWN WITH HIS SHOE!
VIC WATERS: Sherri needs to stay in her corner before she gets hurt. Pulsar isn’t playing a game here, he is trying to win a match and he won’t put up with her tomfoolery.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar enters the ring and Edmunds is begging off! VIC WATERS: He is stalling, don’t let him get away with it! BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR NAILS EDMUNDS IN THE HEAD WITH THE SHINING WIZARD AND DRIVES HIS KNEE RIGHT INTO HIS HEAD!
VIC WATERS: That is it, no mercy. BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR IS MAKING THE COVER ON EDMUNDS AND THE REF GOES DOWN FOR THE COUNT! 1-----------------------------------------------------2----------- EDMUNDS GETS THE SHOULDER UP!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar is wearing him down, he needs to try to finish him as soon as possible. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar picks up Edmunds…. HE NAILS HIM WITH A BLITZ OF ALTERNATING FOREARM SHOTS TO THE HEAD!
VIC WATERS: With everything that has happened this week, Edmunds senses may be a bit dulled here tonight so he is trying to slow down his reaction time even more.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is stumbling… PULSAR KICKS HIM IN THE MIDSECTION AND LIFTS HIM UP FOR AN INVERTED SUPLEX!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar is displaying his awesome strength right here for a man his size. BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR DRIVES HIM DOWN INTO THE MAT! Pulsar picks Edmunds off the mat….. SMALL PACKAGE BY PULSAR! The ref goes down for the count!
VIC WATERS: Brilliant move by Pulsar.. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref goes down for the count! 1--------------------------------------------2-------------------------- EDMUNDS JUST KICKS AT THE LAST SECOND!
VIC WATERS: He is getting closer. BRANDON JACOBS: Both Pulsar and Edmunds gets up…. EDMUNDS GOES FOR A REVERSE CRESCENT KICK BUT PULSAR DUCKS UNDER! Pulsar grabs the leg of Edmunds and Edmunds is hopping around on one leg…. EDMUNDS GOES FOR AN ENZIGURI… HE HITS AND PULSAR GOES DOWN LIKE A LIGHT!
VIC WATERS: This could be the opportunity Edmunds needs to get back in this match. BRANDON JACOBS: A dazed Edmunds picks Pulsar off the mat…. SWINGING NECKBREAKER! He plants him right into the ring!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is going for the cover! BRANDON JACOBS: The ref goes down for the count! 1---------------------------------------2----------- PULSAR KICKS OUT WITH AUTHORITY!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar is still the freshest man in this match and after all the abuse Edmunds has taken, it going to be hard to keep Pulsar down.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds grabs the arms of Pulsar… AND HE CRUCIFIXES THE ARMS OF PULSAR AND TAKES HIM DOWN TO THE MAT! The ref goes down for the count! 1-----------EDMUNDS PUTS HIS FEET ON THE ROPES------------------2------------------------------ THE REF NOTICES THE LEGS AND BREAKS THE COUNT!
VIC WATERS: If the ref noticed that half a second later, Edmunds would have walked out of this match a winner.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds gets up and he is arguing with the ref to count faster or his ass will be out in the street…
VIC WATERS: That is a blatant abuse of power. BRANDON JACOBS: A tired Pulsar crawls up behind Edmunds….. PULSAR HITS HIM WITH A NUTSHOT AND THE REF TURNS HIS HEAD AWAY LIKE HE DIDN’T SEE IT! Pulsar should have really been DQed there.
VIC WATERS: My monitor went out, what happened? BRANDON JACOBS: Likely story… PULSAR ROLLS UP EDMUNDS WHO IS DOUBLE OVER IN PAIN! The ref goes down for the count! 1------------------------------------------2----------------------------- EDMUNDS MANAGES TO KICK OUT!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar needs to get this pin as quickly as possible, Edmunds has had a way today of coming back repeatedly with his blatant cheating.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar picks up Edmunds and HE HITS HIM REPEATEDLY WITH A SERIES OF KNEE LIFTS RIGHT IN THE STOMACH! He is taking a page out of Edmunds book and softening up the midsection like he normally does to setup the Northern Lights Suplex.
VIC WATERS: When you wind a guy, it makes it a lot harder for them to kick out. BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR IRISH WHIPS EDMUNDS INTO THE CORNER AND HE CRASHES IN BACK FIRST! PULSAR CHARGES BUT EDMUNDS PULLS THE REFEREE IN THE WAY AND THE REFEREE TAKES THE GRUNT OF THE RUNNING TURNBUCKLE SPLASH! Pulsar look like he hurt him stomach when he crashed into the referee’s oversized belt buckle!
VIC WATERS: The referee should call for the DQ, Edmunds blatantly cheated right there! This is an outrage.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is slumped over in the corner and Pulsar is going to check on the referee….. WAIT A SECOND, SHERRI JUST THREW EDMUNDS HER PURSE!
VIC WATERS: Sherii has a god damn brick in there. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is turning around…. EDMUNDS CLOCKS PULSAR WITH THE PURSE AND THROWS IT TO THE OUTSIDE! Edmunds makes the cover and he is shouting to the ref to come over and make the count!
VIC WATERS: Stay down ref! Stay down! BRANDON JACOBS: The dazed referee inches over to make the count! 1---------------------------------------------------2-------------------------------------- PULSAR MANAGES TO KICK OUT!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar refuses to lose! BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR CHARGES EDMUNDS… EDMUNDS HITS PULSAR WITH THE NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX! Edmunds has a handful of tights!
VIC WATERS: Ref! The hands! The hands! BRANDON JACOBS: The ref goes down to make the count! 1------------------------------------2-------------------------------------------------------3! EDMUNDS BARELY GETS HIM AS PULSAR KICKS OUT AFTER THE THREE! Edmunds wins the match! COPYCAT VERSES EDMUNDS IS GOING TO HAPPEN!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. SIMPLY SENSATIONAL SEAN EDMUNDS! He will go on to face Copycat at the PPV!
BRANDON JACOBS: Finally, after years and years of waiting, Copycat verses Edmunds will finally happen on PPV right here in WFW.
VIC WATERS: Pulsar got totally screwed by Edmunds there. We need two referees out there when Edmunds wrestlers because he did to Pulsar just what he did to Tact. Having Sherri is a very valuable asset.
BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we are going to take a short commercial break, when we comeback, it is time for Scotty Michaels & Iron Lion to take on Anarky & Jared Wells! STAY TUNED EVERYONE!
