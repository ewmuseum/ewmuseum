WFW: Spirit of NGEN: Lumberjack - Doc vs. Hart - 1st Fall

20 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 20 August 2003 VIC WATERS: LUSTER?! BRANDON JACOBS: Vic, we must move on. Our hopes now rely on Doc Silver to save this company from a hideous fate.
VIC WATERS: Doc can do it, he has beaten Shawn Hart twice before. How hard can it be to beat Shawn Hart for a third time? We’ve brought the expert and he’ll do his service.
BRANDON JACOBS: I hope you are right. This match should be wild affair. It will be a two out of three falls match, the first call being a lumberjack match, the second fall being a cage, and the third fall will be last man standing. Everyone has been wondering who is the 1st candidate and special referee for the match. Let’s send it to ringside now and get the match started so we all find out!
RING ANNOUNCER: At this time, I’d like to introduce the man who will be microphoned and referee all three falls of tonight’s WFW World Heavyweight Title match.
(CUEUP: “Diesel Power” by Prodigy) RING ANNOUNCER: The 1st Presidential Candiate, from Atlantic City, New Jersey… ALEX WYLDE! BRANDON JACOBS: Whoa, Alex Wylde is here in the WFW? VIC WATERS: He absolutely is and I know for a fact he hates both men so I’m pretty sure he’ll call it right down the middle.
BRANDON JACOBS: I hope you are right. (CUEUP: "Look At Me" by Geri Haliwell) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the challenger, from Orlando, Florida, the leader of C.O.C.K.S. and a finalist in the initial WFW World Heavyweight Title tournament, “THE PHENON” SHAWN HART!
::Shawn Hart walks down to the ring to a mixed reaction from the crowd and enters the ring::
(CUEUP: “Only Happy When It Rains” By Garbage) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, one of the true legends in this sport, the two time WFW World Heavyweight Champion, defending the title in his very own home town of Orlando, Florida… the one, the only…. DOC SILVER!
::VIC WATERS AND BRANDON JACOBS BOTH GET UP AND GIVE DOC A STANDING OVATION:: VIC WATERS: GO GET HIM DOC! We are going to ignore the fact that Shawn Hart is trying to win the title in his hometown, correct?
BRANDON JACOBS: Correct. VIC WATERS: Here comes Richard Gideon, Rat Fink, Diablo, Sean Edmunds, Felix Red, Copycat, Adam Benjamin, El Arco Iris, HEEL, Tiny, and Pulsar… Scotty Michaels, Luster, and Manson will not be involved.
BRANDON JACOBS: What happened to Michaels & Luster? VIC WATERS: Security was in fear of their life so they had them go home. We don’t want this turning into a huge war. This is about the title.
BRANDON JACOBS: Good thinking by the WFW executives. Manson needs a bit of time to cool off before coming out to whip people.
ALEX WLYDE: If either Doc Silver or Shawn Hart go to the outside, beat the living crap out them. When you are done, throw them back. ::Wlyde turns his attention to Doc::
BRANDON JACOBS: Oh man. ALEX WYLDE: ::pats down Doc for weapons:: You have anything Hart? ::Hart shakes his head:: RING THE BELL!
*bell rings* ALEX WLYDE: Let’s go boys! BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver and Shawn Hart meet in the center of the ring. Shawn Hart is acting all cocky tonight mouthing off in Doc Silver’s face but Doc isn’t saying a word. Shawn Hart is laying in with the insults but Doc is just walking away from him. SHAWN HART GETS ANGRY AND TURNS DOC SILVER AROUND…. DOC LAYS INTO HIM WITH A RIGHT HAND! AND ANOTHER AND ANOTHER!
VIC WATERS: There is no greater fury than Doc scorn! BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver whips Shawn Hart off the ropes…. DOC SILVER BACKBODY DROPS SHAWN HART OVER THE TOP ROPE ONTO GIDEON AND DIABLO WHO CATCH HIM!
VIC WATERS: Bastards. BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo & Gideon roll Shawn Hart in under the bottom rope. An annoyed Doc Silver picks Shawn Hart off the mat…. BACK SUPLEX! DOC SILVER JUMPS ON SHAWN HART AND STARTS POUNDING HIM WITH CLOSED PUNCHES!
VIC WATERS: TAKE IT TO HIM DOC! BRANDON JACOBS: SHAWN HART GOUGES THE EYES AND DOC IS PUSHED OFF BY SHAWN HART! Shawn Hart jumps on top of Doc and nails him with another eye gouge! The ref should step in here!
VIC WATERS: WYLDE is jaw jacking with Rat Fink over on the side! HOW IS THIS PROPER REFFERING!
BRANDON JACOBS: SHAWN HART IS RAKING SILVER’S FACE WITH HART’S BOOT LACES RIGHT ACROSS THE FACE! Doc is able to push Hart off and back into the corner finally!
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart is a low down dirty rat! BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver is trying to get up in the corner… SHAWN HART NAILS DOC WITH A DISCUS PUNCH! SILVER BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES…. HE TAKES ANOTHER DISCUS PUNCH BY HART BUT HE REFUSES TO GO DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Doc is use to taking a lot of abuse, he isn’t going to be able to get him down so easily.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart nails Doc with an elbow! Doc is moved out of the corner… CLOTHESLINE OVER THE TOP ROPE! RAT FINK, GIDEON, AND DIABLO ARE BEATING DOC UP UNMERCIOUSLY! Felix Red and El Arco Iris someone manage to make their way through and are able to roll Doc back into the ring. Gideon and Diablo are giving Red and Arco a nasty look.
VIC WATERS: Doc really hasn’t made many friends in either of his two title reigns. BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart rolls Doc with his foot to the center of the ring and covers! Wlyde goes down to make the count! 1--------------------2-----------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Thank God Doc kicked out! BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart picks Doc up and whips him off the rope…. INVERTED ATOMIC DROP! Doc is bended over in pain!
VIC WATERS: He didn’t have his knee all the way through so that was really a low blow! Wlyde is letting it go though!
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart is reviving up for a superkick…. HE NAILS IT AND DOC SILVER FALLS BACK INTO THE CORNER! Hart charges into the corner…. BIG SPLASH! Doc falls to the ground!
VIC WATERS: COME ON DOC! BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is calling for the El Codo Explosivo! Hart picks Doc up…. DOC PULLS WLYDE IN THE WAY AND HART ACCIDENTLY NAILS WLYDE!
VIC WATERS: WYLDE IS DOWN! BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is conducting traffic! DIABLO AND GIDEON ARE RUNNING INTO THE RING! GIDEON GRABS THE ARMS OF DOC! Diablo reaches into his tights… FIRE PAPER….. GIDEON HOLDS DOC…. DIABLO THROWS A FIREBALL RIGHT INTO DOC’S EYES AND DOC IS CRYING OUT IN PAIN!
VIC WATERS: DAMN IT! BRANDON JACOBS: WFW SECURITY IS COMING DOWN AND THEY ARE USHERING DIABLO AND GIDEON OUT OF THE BUILDING! THEY HAVE BEEN EJECTED! Shawn Hart is waking up the ref!
VIC WATERS: Wlyde looks out of it! BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart grabs the blinded Doc and whips him off the ropes…. SLEEPER HOLD! WLYDE SEES DOC SILVER CRYING OUT IN PAIN! HE IS CALLING FOR THE BELL!
RING ANNOUNCER: THE WINNER OF THE LUMBERJACK MATCH AND THE FIRST FALL, “THE PHENOM” SHAWN HART!
BRANDON JACOBS: Doc got robbed! VIC WATERS: He sure did, the wrestlers are leaving for the back and now Doc has two minutes to regain his vision. A nurse has come down to ringside to spray some water in the eyes of Doc as the cage is being lowered.
BRANDON JACOBS: We will have a few minutes before they can lower the cage because they are working on Doc, let’s go to a commercial break and hopefully Doc will be ready to fight for the second fall!
