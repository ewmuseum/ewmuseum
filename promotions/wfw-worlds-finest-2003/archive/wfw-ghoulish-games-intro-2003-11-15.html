WFW Ghoulish Games - Intro

15 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 15 November 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome everyone to United Center, we are live in Chicago on PPV for Ghoulish Games! Tonight, for the first time since the inception of the league, Michael Manson will challenge for the WFW World Heavyweight Title against the champion Shawn Hart.
VIC WATERS: Wrestling experts have said over and over that Manson is the best wrestler in all of WFW, but he just couldn’t beat Doc. On that faithful night when our first champion was crowned all those months ago, Shawn Hart took the pinfall in the three way match to determine the two champions which would define what this league is all about, the World’s Finest Wrestling. On one hand, you had Doc Silver trying to fight off Shawn Hart, Richard Gideon, and Psycho in order to keep the WFW World Heavyweight Title around his waist and prove to the world that he was still a living legend. On the other, you have Michael Manson, who has been practically undefeated except in a couple of tag matches, was a juggernaut who made the WFW North American Title even bigger in some of the fans eyes than even the WFW World Heavyweight Title. His epic matches against Rat Fink and Scotty Michaels are legendary and he’d still be champion today if Scotty Michaels and Luster didn’t conspire to make sure that Manson didn’t walk out of that cage champion. Two men who are forever entwined fell from grace on the very same night and since both men were worthy of taking on champion, the WFW decided to determine who would get the title shot by making a number one contender match between Manson and Doc Silver…. Well due to an unfortunate night and vengeful god, Doc Silver was sent home for ninety days where he is still recovering from his injuries in the cage with Psycho which were worsened at the hands of Manson and Manson walked out with the victory over Doc and the shot at the WFW World Heavyweight Champion Shawn Hart. That is how we gotten to tonight here in Chicago.
BRANDON JACOBS: When you think about how hard Manson has worked to get this title shot, it is almost like there is more at stake for Manson than Shawn Hart. Shawn Hart has already the man who gave Manson so much trouble, Doc Silver, and he has beaten the man who robbed Manson of the WFW North American Title, now all he has to do is beat Manson himself to complete the trifecta and further cement his legend among the immortals who have held a title which has a lineage which goes back to the golden age of wrestling.
VIC WATERS: The longer this goes, the harder it will be for Manson to get to the top with all the top talent coming out of the wood work to be a part of World’s Finest Wrestling. This month alone we’ve added both Minion and one of the alltime greatest brawlers if not thee best, Anarky. Plus, we are in Chicago, Manson’s hometown… even when Manson hasn’t always been the biggest fan favorite, the people of Chicago have always loved him because he is one of them and his work ethic respresents all the hard working members of this city, all ten of them. If he doesn’t win tonight, he is the new Steve Bartman. Chicago doesn’t win many titles and Manson is their best shot at being affiliated with a champion.
BRANDON JACOBS: I wouldn’t go as far to call him Steve Bartman if he loses, but a win here tonight would be huge for Chicago with the GLCW dead and the Cubs having to wait yet another year, this is like an early Christmas present if Manson wins here tonight.
VIC WATERS: Can he beat Shawn Hart is the question, Shawn Hart is a hell of a survivor… Doc took him to his limited and he cameback for more. Plus, who knows what tricks Shawn Hart is up to.
BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo and Tiny are banned from ringside tonight for the WFW World Heavyweight Title Match. There won’t be any of that C.O.C.K.S. interference tonight saving Shawn Hart’s skin in Chicago and if Diablo or Tiny do interfere, they will be suspended for ninety days.
VIC WATERS: That is kind of harsh. BRANDON JACOBS: That is the only way you can deal with a group like C.O.C.K.S. You have to lay down the law. Nobody wants to see an interference laden main event with a million damn run ins. They want to see Manson and Hart battle it out man to man in a Chicago Street Fight.
VIC WATERS: Manson is not going to show any mercy what so ever. This could be a very bloody match for both men.
BRANDON JACOBS: It certainly will not be for the faint of heart. Plus, we’ve got a whole card full of other goodness on this show, Scotty Michaels will be putting his title on the line against Richard Gideon and Felix Red, Rat Fink will be putting the BAD World Heavyweight up against Pulsar in a Spin The Wheel Make a Deal Match, Anarky and Diablo will fight in a falls count anywhere in the graveyard match, and we will have a Double Dog Collar match with Copycat & El Arco Iris going against the team of Sean Edmunds & Luster plus a whole bunch of other goodies…
VIC WATERS: I have a feeling this is going to be a great night. BRANDON JACOBS: As do I, this crowd is really getting antsy too so instead of hearing us talk, after we get back from this commercial break, we are going to send this one down to ringside and start the show. STAY TUNED EVERYONE! The show is starting up…. NEXT!
