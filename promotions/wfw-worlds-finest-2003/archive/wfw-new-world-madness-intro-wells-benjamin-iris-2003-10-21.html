WFW: New World Madness Intro (Wells & Benjamin/Iris)

21 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 21 October 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome everyone to New World Madness; tonight we celebrate Columbus Day in front of a jam packed crowd here at Plymouth, Mass! We have a big evening of action for you, but we open tonight up with some sad news, GLCW is no longer among the living. After a year and half, they have closed their doors due to financial reasons.
VIC WATERS: It is just impossible for regional promotion to afford the level of talent that they had and had a bunch of people making way more than they should have. ::looks at Jacobs::
BRANDON JACOBS: ::looks around innocently:: Be that as it may, they were a hell of a promotion and they will be missed.
VIC WATERS: This is a bad month to be from Chicago, first GLCW closes down, then the Cubs can’t finish off the Marlins to save the World Series ratings, and then, Chicago lost a native son, one half of the most famous tag team champions ever in wrestling, Road Warrior Hawk.
BRANDON JACOBS: That is why we’ve decided that the next WFW Show, our Halloween Show, Ghoulish Games will be live from Chicago on PPV! The winner of the Shawn Hart vs. Luster match will go on to defend the WFW World Heavyweight Title against Michael Manson in the PPV main event.
VIC WATERS: I remember the old incarnation of the old NGEN Halloween Show called Ghost World and that was always one tripped out show. They really got into the spirit of Halloween and played it up to the hilt, hopefully this can live up to how great those past holiday shows were.
BRANDON JACOBS: With all the incredible talent that has been joining which has added onto a legendary core of wrestlers to begin with, I don’t see how it can’t. But as good as that will be, this show here tonight may be even better than that one
VIC WATERS: I’m really looking forward to the Ultimate Jeopardy Three Way Dance and the Luster/Shawn Hart match. I haven’t seen a classic old style three way dance in a long, long time.
BRANDON JACOBS: I remember when the concept of a three way dance was revolutionary. VIC WATERS: We’ve changed Presidents since then, you know. BRANDON JACOBS: Yes, I know, but with the Ultimate Jeopardy Rules and with the level of talent involved, I think it will be something we all will remember for a long time.
VIC WATERS: Now I agree with that. BRANDON JACOBS: We also have Shawn Hart making his very first title defense here against Luster.
VIC WATERS: Luster has really stepped up his game as of late and his association with Scotty Michaels has cemented his rise to the top. I wouldn’t doubt if Luster actually beat Shawn Hart here tonight in his first title defense. Luster has just been that damn good.
BRANDON JACOBS: I don’t know which way to go in that match, it should be hell of a battle because Luster knows how important this title shot. But as if those matches weren’t enough, we have some other great matches on the show, WFW President Alex Wylde will be going head to head with the C.O.C.K.S. #2 Man Diablo in a soap box vs. flaming table match, Dormant will be taking on Pulsar for the BAD World Heavyweight Title shot at the PPV, and two of the greatest WWL World Heavyweight Champions, Copycat and Sean Edmunds will face off head to head in a battle over bragging rights over who was the better WWL champ.
VIC WATERS: It should be noted that originally Jared Wells was suppose to be in the match, but unfortunately he suffered an arm injury while in training and will not be able to compete tonight.
BRANDON JACOBS: It is a real shame, but we expect Jared Wells to be back around Christmas time. If he makes the same impact that he made in his debut, he’ll be a hell of a force when he returns.
VIC WATERS: ::looks at the sheet:: You forgot to mention the El Arco Iris match. BRANDON JACOBS: The El Arco Match has already happened. Earlier today, a local children’s hospital asked if we could put on a match for the children who couldn’t leave the hospital to be here today, so El Arco Iris and Adam Benjamin fought in a falls count anywhere match throughout the children’s ward before hitting him with a loaded Care Bear to get the pin.
VIC WATERS: …. A loaded Care Bear? BRANDON JACOBS: Funshine to be exact… VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris sickens me. BRANDON JACOBS: You just can’t stand to see anyone happy. Speaking of which, it time to get this show started, when we get back, Dormant will be taking on Pulsar with the winner getting a BAD World Heavyweight Title shot, STAY TUNED FOLKS!
