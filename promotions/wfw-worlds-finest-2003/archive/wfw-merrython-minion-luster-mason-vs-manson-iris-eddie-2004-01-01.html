WFW Merrython: Minion/Luster/Mason vs. Manson/Iris/Eddie

1 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 1 January 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome everyone, it is time for our main event from our Rockefeller Center location before we go to FAO Schwartz and Central Park for our final two matches of the evening.
VIC WATERS: You know, this six man match has drawn quite a bit of interest. Manson verses Minion in a teacher verses student war of words while Manson is suffering in purgatory after his loss to Hart. El Arco Iris trying to improve the morale of both men to rise from the darkness into the light. Luster being the man that we all know and love. This has been a beautiful six man.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is the established veteran of his team who will impart his wisdom to youngsters like El Arco Iris and Good Eddie. Manson may not be in the main event like has been for the vast majority of the year, but this six man is one of the hottest matches on the show and a lot of people who came out today, came out especially to see this six man.
VIC WATERS: I don’t know, it seems like Manson is being punished for something to me, like causing a near riot or maybe losing to Hart.
BRANDON JACOBS: Just because C.O.C.K.S. has been terrorizing the WFW for months and Manson was our best chance to get Hart out as champion and he was partially responsible for causing a near riot with Anarky that almost got our unlicensed revoked, doesn’t mean we would punish Manson. I know it looks bad for us considering the events of the last two shows, but Manson simply fit the six man tag. He has issues with both Mason, Luster, and even Minion. Plus, we have gotten a lot of email from the children who watch WFW asking us when their two favorite wrestlers, Manson and El Arco Iris would be teamed up and this six man was a great opportunity to do so.
VIC WATERS: The ultimate team up of Eeyore and Tigger, Oscar the Grouch and Big Bird, and Grumpy and Funshine…. This is a team of epic proportions!
BRANDON JACOBS: Now you are just being silly. VIC WATERS: Minion. Manson, and Luster don’t care about that. They are going to take great pleasure in destroying Manson and El Arco Iris beyond all recognition, fans be damned. They would like nothing better than to ruin the Christmas of all the children out there who have been waiting for this team up. Minion especially after all that has been said… Minion wants to prove that he is greater than the teacher and have some momentum going into his WFW World Heavyweight Title match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Minion has really been rolling through everyone, he did take a pinfall loss to El Arco Iris, but Minion dominated the match and things could have been a lot worse for El Arco Iris if Hart hadn’t stuck his nose in there.
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris is in for some serious payback tonight. Minion doesn’t take kindly that his record has been blemished.
BRANDON JACOBS: You may be right, let’s cut out the chit chat and send this one to ringside for our final match tonight from Rockefeller Center!
(CUEUP: “"Cat Scratch Fever" by Ted Nugent) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first from team Manson, the John Wayne of the WFW, from Albuquerque, New Mexico…. GOOD EDDIE!
::Good Eddie slowly walks down in his dark brown trenchcoat like Eastwood, and a red scarf tied around his neck::
(CUEUP: “"The Crown", by Ja Rule) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing the first man of Team Minion, standing at 6’3 249 pounds, from Queens, New York…. TERRELL MASON!
::Terrell Mason walks down to the ring and keeps his eye on Good Eddie as he enters the ring::
(CUEUP: “Tunak Tunak Tun” by Dahler Mendhi) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing the second man from Team Manson, from over the rainbow, the Warrior of Happiness, EL ARCO IRIS!
::El Arco Iris walks down to the ring with a Santa hat on and a big sack full of rainbow colored candy canes which he throws out to the audience to a huge pop from the crowd, dozens of children can been seen in the crowd wearing El Arco Iris masks, and enters the ring::
VIC WATERS: Bah humbug, El Arco Iris is a terrorist. He is rotting their teeth with sugar! BRANDON JACOBS: Quiet you. Look at how happy the kids are. El Arco Iris does a lot for kids like he does with his “Smile For Every Face” charity.
VIC WATERS: I hate kids. (CUEUP: “"Funkytown"-Disco Fever) RING ANNOUNCER: And now…. the second man of Team Minion, the man who helped end Manson’s WFW North American Title reign and the sexiest man in all of WFW, from Atlanta, Georgia… LUSTER!
::Luster comes down dancing to the ring as the crowd boos:: (CUEUP: "Mechanical Animals" by Marilyn Manson) RING ANNOUNCER: And now, the captain of Team Manson, the former WFW North American Champion, the man, the myth, the legend…. MICHAEL MANSON!
::Manson is walking down slowly to the ring:: (CUEUP: “Where Boys Fear To Tread” by Smashing Pumpkins) RING ANNOUNCER: And his captain of the opposing team, one of the most sadistic men in all of wrestling, from New York City…. MINION!
::Minion walks down to the ring to the boos of the crowd and enters the ring:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: The presents containing various weapons are surrounding the ring and we are all set to get started. El Arco Iris is offering a hug to Good Eddie and Manson. Good Eddie dashes out of the ring and yells at El Arco Iris. El Arco Iris gives an expressionless Manson a hug. A dumbfounded Manson points to El Arco Iris and tells him to start the match. It looks like Luster will be starting the match for the other team.
VIC WATERS: All of this hugging, it isn’t kosher. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is offering Luster a hug. Luster opens his arms and gives El Arco Iris a big hug. It is good to see two men can express emotion like this in this day and age. LUSTER SLIPS HIS HAND DOWN AND FONDLES THE BACKSIDE OF EL ARCO IRS!
VIC WATERS: Good lord! BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris pushes Luster away and yells BAD TOUCH! At Luster. Luster is apologizing profusely to El Arco Iris saying it was instinct and is offer another hug to make up for it. El Arco Iris scrapes his chin as if he is thinking about it and extends his hand for a handshake. LUSTER SLAPS IT AWAY!
VIC WATERS: Luster is such a playa. Whenever he hugs anyone, his hands go there by instinct. I know it has happened to me.
BRANDON JACOBS: I don’t doubt it has. LUSTER CHARGES EL ARCO IRIS AND BEGINS TO POUND AWAY…. LUSTER WHIPS EL ARCO IRIS OFF THE ROPES…. LUSTER NAILS HIM WITH A CLOTHESLINE!
VIC WATERS: It is like El Arco Iris just ran into a brick wall. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster walks over to El Arco Iris…. HE GIVES HIM A BACK RAKE! Luster scraped his nails up and down the back of El Arco Iris!
VIC WATERS: That hurts like a dickens because there is so little skin on the back to begin with.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster picks El Arco Iris up…. HE NAILS WITH AN STO AND HE GOES FACE FIRST INTO THE MAT! Luster covers El Arco Iris! 1----------------------------------2-KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Way too early to be trying a pinfall. LUSTER IS CALLING TO MASON TO THROW HIM A PRESENT!
BRANDON JACOBS: Mason picks up a present and throws it into Luster… LUSTER OPENS UP THE PRESENT…. LUSTER PULLS OUT AN ISAAC HAYES RECORD! Luster smashes it over the head of El Arco Iris!
VIC WATERS: What is a record? BRANDON JACOBS: Luster takes a shard of the broken record and tries to cut open the mask of El Arco Iris…. MANSON RUNS IN TO BREAK IT UP AND HE RUNS INTO LUSTER AND KNOCKS HIM DOWN WITH A SHOULDER BLOCK! Manson stands over Luster and he yelling at him for going after El Arco Iris’ mask.
VIC WATERS: That mask is sacred to El Arco Iris. It is his whole identity. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is trying to get Manson to go back to his corner. MINION RUNS IN AND HE PICKS UP EL ARCO IRIS… MINION NAILS EL ARCO IRIS WITH BALDO BOMB AND INSTRUCTS LUSTER TO COVER! Manson is yelling at the ref to turn around but he isn’t paying attention. Minion returns to his corner and the ref turns around… THE REF IS MAKING THE COUNT! 1-------------------------------2-------------------- EL ARCO IRIS KICKS OUT!
VIC WATERS: Slow count. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster looks like he may have suffered a concussion. Luster drags El Arco Iris over to his corner…. HE MAKES THE TAG TO MINION! Minion is calling for Mason to throw him a present… MINION CATCHES IT! Minion unwraps the present…. IT IS TOASTER! Minion pushes El Arco Iris into the corner and climbs onto the second rope…. MINION STARTS POUNDING EL ARCO IRIS HEAD WITH THE TOASTER! The ref is trying to break it up…. It looks like El Arco Iris is starting to bleed through his mask and this crowd filled with children is beginning to look awfully worried.
VIC WATERS: Their hero is going to die! BRANDON JACOBS: Minion dumps the toaster and MINION IS TRYING TO RIP THE MASK OFF EL ARCO IRIS BUT EL ARCO IRIS IS HOLDING ONTO HIS MASK FOR DEAR LIFE! Minion is a bastard!
VIC WATERS: He is worse than Manson, there is nothing Minion won’t do. Manson at least has his limits.
BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS LOW BLOWS MINION! Minion is bent over in pain and this crowd is cheering for El Arco Iris. EL ARCO IRIS IS CLIMBING TO THE TOP ROPE…. Minion is bending upright… MOONSAULT PRESS ONTO MINION! The ref goes down to make the count! 1-------------------------2----------------KICKOUT! Minion kicks out with authority!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris caught him off guard and it almost cost him. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is calling to Manson to throw him a present quickly… Manson picks up a round object and throws it into El Arco Iris… EL ARCO IRIS OPENS UP THE PRESENT…. IT IS A TAMBOURINE! Minion charges El Arco Iris…. HE NAILS HIM WITH THE TAMBOURINE! Minion staggers backwards… EL ARCO IRIS HITS HIM AGAIN!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris is making sweet music with his head. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris bounces off the ropes… HE THROWS THE TAMBOURINE TO MINION WHO CATCHES IT….. EL ARCO IRIS DROPKICKS IT RIGHT INTO HIS HEAD! Manson is yelling at El Arco Iris to make the tag! El Arco Iris runs over into his corner… HE TAGS IN MANSON!
VIC WATERS: The battle is about to begin! BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON RUNS IN AND HE STARS RIPPING INTO MINION WITH KNIFE EDGE CHOPS! Minion is about to fall over… MANSON CALLS FOR A PRESENT FROM GOOD EDDIE! Good Eddie throws the present in… Manson opens it up…. IT IS THE HULK HANDS! Manson puts the Hulk Hand on his fist and he NAILS MINION AND MINION FINALLY GOES DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Manson…. ANGRY! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson climbs onto Minion and starts pounding him away with the Hulk Hand! He is getting revenge for what happened to El Arco Iris earlier in the match. MINION HAS BEEN BUSTED OPEN!
ViC WATERS: Manson is channeling the power of the Hulkster. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is calling to El Arco Iris is calling to El Arco Iris to bring in another present! EL ARCO IRIS GRABS A BIG PRESENT AND THROWS IT INTO THE RING! Manson opens up the present….. IT IS THE BARBIE DREAM HOUSE!
VIC WATERS: Hey, that is what you wanted for Christmas! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson picks up Minion…. HE WILD BUCK BOMBS MINION RIGHT ONTO THE BOX AND IT MAKES A LOUD CRUNCH!
VIC WATERS: Obviously, Manson has no idea how much those things cost. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is pulling the box away from underneath Minion. MANSON IS MAKING THE COVER! 1----------------------------------------2------------------------- MASON DIVES IN AND BREAKS UP THE PINFALL!
VIC WATERS: It is about time Mason did something. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson kicks Manson in the gut…. HE NAILS WITH A DDT! Manson is calling to El Arco Iris for another present…. EL ARCO IRIS HURLS A PRESENT INTO THE RING AND MANSON MAKES THE CATCH! Manson rips open the present…. IT IS A KARAOKE MACHINE!
VIC WATERS: GOOD LORD! THAT IS PURE EVIL! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson rips it out of its box and begins pounding on the back of Mason… MANSON NAILS HIM REPEATED WITH IT…. THE KARAOKE MACHINE HAS FINALLY BROKEN INTO TWO PIECES!
VIC WATERS: Thank god for that. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson goes back to Minion…. MINION NAILS MANSON JAWBREAKER AND MANSON IS GRABBING HIS CHIN! That certainly had to rattle all of his teeth! Minion is calling to Luster to throw in a present! LUSTER THROWS IN A PRESENT AND MINION CATCHES IT! Minion takes off the wrapping… IT IS A BARBED WIRE BASEBALL BAT!
VIC WATERS: Minion won the luck of the draw and present he could use. BRANDON JACOBS: MINION STARTS POUNDING MANSON WITH THE BARBED WIRE BASEBALL BAT ACROSS THE BACK AND MANSON CRASHES DOWN TO THE MAT! Minion straddles Manson’s back and HE RUBS THE BARBED WIRE BAT UP AND DOWN IN ORDER TO DRAW BLOOD! Mason is leaving to go back to his corner.
VIC WATERS: Minion is a vicious man. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is trying to get this crowd behind Manson by repeatedly slapping the turnbuckle! These fans are behind El Arco Iris and Manson 100% tonight! MINION IS CALLING FOR THE REDEMPTION! Wait a second, MINION IS PUTTING THE BARBED WIRE BASEBALL BAT UNDER HIS ARM AND HE LOCKS ON THE DRAGON SLEEPER!
VIC WATERS: As if a regular Dragon Sleeper wasn’t bad enough! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is struggling to breath! MINION IS SHOVING THAT BARBED WIRE BASEBALL BAT RIGHT INTO HIS THROAT!
VIC WATERS: The ref is checking to see if Manson is willing to give up, but Manson is the kind of wrestler who would do damage to his own well being before letting Minion hear the words I quit.
BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS RUNS IN AND HE JUMPS ONTO MINION WITH A SLEEPER HOLD! Minion lets go of Manson…. MINION CARRIES EL ARCO IRIS ONTO HIS BACK AND HE RAMS HIM INTO THE CORNER REPEATEDLY! This stubborn El Arco Iris won’t let go though! He doesn’t want to see him hurt Manson anymore!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris may be lacking a few brain cells, but he certainly does have heart for helping out a friend like this at his own expense.
BRANDON JACOBS: MINION RAMS HIM INTO THE CORNER HARD AND EL ARCO IRIS FINALLY LETS GO! Manson is grabbing his throat and he is back away from Minion…. MINION GRABS THE BARBED WIRE BASEBALL BAT AND IS COMING BACK FOR MORE! Minion is reaching into his tights…. HE HAS A LIGHT AND HE LIGHTING THE BAT ON FIRE!
VIC WATERS: Minion is one of the last people in the world I would trust with a flaming baseball bat.
BRANDON JACOBS: MINION AIMS FOR MANSON’S CROTCH AND MANSON SPLITS HIS LEG JUST IN TIME TO AVOID A CROTCH FULL OF FIRE! Wait, GOOD EDDIE IS GOING UNDER THE RING FOR SOMETHING… HE HAS A FIRE EXTINGUISHER! Good Eddie runs into the ring…. He taps Minion from behind… MINION TURNS AROUND AND HE SPRAYS HIM RIGHT IN THE FACE AND PUTS OUT THE FIRE ON THE BARBED WIRE BASEBALL BAT! Minion has been blinded but he is swinging that barbed wire baseball bat like a mad man…. MINION CONNECTS WITH GOOD EDDIE AND KNOCKS HIM DOWN WITH SO MUCH MOMENTUM AND FALLS BACK AND TUMBLES THROUGH THE ROPES! Minion drops the barbed wire baseball bat and he is trying to get the white foam out of his eyes.
VIC WATERS: Minion needs to make a tag here…. All three men of the opposing team are struggling and someone needs to finish the job!
BRANDON JACOBS: Minion walks over to his corner…. HE TAGS IN MASON! Mason picks Manson up in a Fireman’s Carry and walks over to the center of the ring….. HE NAILS HIM WITH THE CLAP BACK! IT IS OVER! Mason makes the cover on Manson! 1------------------------------------------------2-----------------------------------------EL ARCO IRIS DIVES IN AT THE LAST SECOND TO BREAK UP THE PIN!
VIC WATERS: Thank god for El Arco Iris. BRANDON JACOBS: Mason picks up the metal fire estingisher and SWINGS IT AT EL ARCO IRIS’S HEAD…. EL ARCO IRIS DUCKS…. MASON SWINGS AGAIN…. EL ARCO IRIS DUCKS AGAIN…. EL ARCO IRIS SHINNING WIZARDS THE RED METAL CONTAINER INTO MASON’S FACE AND MASON GOES DOWN!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris is going to feel that in the morning, but he’ll do anything for these children and Manson.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is slowing getting up…. HE CALLING TO EL ARCO IRIS TO GET HIM A PRESENT! El Arco Iris runs outside and throws a present inside to Manson…. MANSON OPENS UP THE PRESENT…. OH MY! IT IS LOADED FUNSHINE CAREBEAR FROM THE HOSPITAL! Manson is telling El Arco Iris to go to the top rope… MANSON THROWS THE CARE BEAR TO EL ARCO IRIS AND MANSON GRABS THE ARMS OF MASON!
VIC WATERS: Care Bear power baby! BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris flies off the top rope…. HE NAILS MASON OVER THE TOP OF THE HEAD WITH THE LOADED FUNSHINE BEAR TO A HUGE POP FROM THE CROWD!
VIC WATERS: Mason is totally out of it! MINION IS COMING BACK IN…. EL ARCO IRIS SPEARS MINION!
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson picks up Mason…. SWEET DREAMS! Manson makes the cover! 1-------------------------------------------2------------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the Santa’s Surprise Match…. The Team of Michael Manson, El Arco Iris, and Good Eddie!
BRANDON JACOBS: What a six man, it just goes to show you what good team work will do and for the second straight match, El Arco Iris has played a hand in sending Minion’s downfall.
VIC WATERS: Poor El Arco Iris is going to die. He just doesn’t know it yet. BRANDON JACOBS: MINION IS IRRATE AND HE HAS THE BARBED WIRE BASEBALL BAT AND HE IS CHASING AFTER LUSTER WHO DIDN'T COME IN TO MAKE THE SAVE!
VIC WATERS: BRANDON JACOBS: Okay folks, we have to go to a commercial break, but when we comeback, we will be live in FAO Schwartz FOR EDMUNDS VERSES WELLS VERSES WYLDE IN A FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE MATCH! Stay tuned everyone!
