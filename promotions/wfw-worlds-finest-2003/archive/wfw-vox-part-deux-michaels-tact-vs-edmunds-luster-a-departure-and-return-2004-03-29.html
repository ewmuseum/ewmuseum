WFW Vox Part Deux: Michaels/Tact vs. Edmunds/Luster (A Departure and Return)

29 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 29 March 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back and it is time for our first tag team match of the evening, former WFW World Heavyweight Champion Scotty Michaels will team up with Larry Tact against the team of former WFW World Heavyweight Champion Sean Edmunds and Luster. Both sides have issues with each other, this could turn out to be quite an interesting match.
VIC WATERS: I was talking to Scotty Michaels earlier and he really wants Luster dead after costing him the title. Things could really get out of hand here tonight.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster has a reason to be upset too. Luster was repeatedly used by Scotty Michaels for his own gain and he never got anything out of it except numerous death threats.
VIC WATERS: Luster’s name was spoken as a curse word among fans world wide. He gained as much if more than Scotty Michaels. He has cost both Manson and Shawn Hart the WFW World Heavyweight Titles and there are people out there to this day who want to kill him for that. That kind of fame lives on forever.
BRANDON JACOBS: That isn’t the good kind of fame though and Luster finally realized it when he attacked Scotty Michaels. Luster wants to earn his fame by beating Scotty Michaels tonight and beating him at the grandest stage of them all, the Superbowl of Wrestling.
VIC WATERS: It is never going to happen. Scotty Michaels is a master of strategy and he’ll find a way to beat Luster as his own game and prove to the world that he was the soul mastermind behind screwing Manson and Scotty Michaels.
BRANDON JACOBS: Don’t under estimate Luster, many a man has made that mistake and suffered. Folks, lets send this one down to ringside and this one underway!
(CUEUP: "Karma" by Diffuser) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, one of the best technicians in all of wrestling today, Manhattan, New York… LARRY TACT!
::Larry Tact comes down to the ovation from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: "Brain Stew" by Green Day) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, currently residing in his summer residence in Escandido, California, the former WFW World Heavyweight Champion….. 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds!
:: Edmunds comes down he comes down with Sherri and enters the ring to a mixed reaction from the crowd::
(CUEUP: “It Is My Life” by Bon Jovi) RING ANNOUNCER: And now, the man who ended the longest world heavyweight title reign in WFW history, the man loved by viewer’s worldwide and their pets, from Philadelphia, PA, THE SUPERSTAR SCOTTY MICHAELS!
::Michaels comes down to the loud boos from the crowd:: (CUEUP: “"Funkytown" by Disco Fever) RING ANNOUNCER: And last but not least, the most controversial and sexiest man in all of WFW, from Atlanta, Georgia… LUSTER!
::Luster comes down dancing to the loud pop of the crowd:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels and Luster are starting off the match for their team much to the chagrin of Scotty Michaels who seems a bit hesitant to step in there with Luster.
VIC WATERS: It isn’t fear, Michaels simply doesn’t trust himself. He doesn’t want to wind up in jail for beating Luster within an inch of his life.
BRANDON JACOBS: I’m sure that is the case. MICHAELS AND LUSTER LOCK UP… LUSTER STARTS REPEATEDLY KNEEING MICHAELS IN THE CHEST!
VIC WATERS: Luster is showing Michaels absolutely no mercy with those shots. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster whips Michaels off the ropes…. LUSTER CLOTHESLINES MICHAELS DOWN TO THE MAT AND MICHAELS GOES INSIDE OUT!
VIC WATERS: Michaels did a complete 360 on that clothesline. Luster put everything he had into that one.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster climbs on top of Michaels and lets loose with a series of quick closed punches right to the head! Michaels is trying to escape but the flurry is too much for him.
VIC WATERS: Those are illegal right hands, the ref should call for the DQ. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster doesn’t care. He wants to prove a lesson to Michaels here tonight. MICHAELS IS ABLE TO PUSH LUSTER OFF AND GET TO THE ROPES!
VIC WATERS: Michaels needs to get a bit of a breather from Luster’s attack. BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER PUTS A SLEEPER ON FROM BEHIND ON MICHAELS AND DRAGS HIM TO THE CENTER OF THE RING! LUSTER HAS MICHAELS IN THE SLEEPER IN THE DEAD CENTER OF THE RING!
VIC WATERS: Michaels isn’t looking so good… BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels looks like he is beginning to fade. TACT RUNS INTO THE RING WITH A DOUBLE AXE HANDLE FROM BEHIND ON LUSTER!
VIC WATERS: Sometimes you have to run in to save your partner earlier rather than later because there was no way Michaels was going to escape that hold and Tact being a thinking man’s wrestler would rather break his partner free now than later when he is more wear down. Excellent thinking by Tact.
BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER RAKES THE EYES OF LARRY TACT! VIC WATERS: Now that was totally uncalled for. Tact was just trying to help his partner. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster grabs the head of Michaels and Tact…. HE RAMS THEM TOGETHER HEAD FIRST AND BOTH MEN GO STUMBLING AROUND!
VIC WATERS: Luster is on fire. BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER CLOTHESLINES TACT OVER THE TOP ROPE AND TACT GOES OVER THE TOP ROPE AND HANDS ON HIS FEET AND STUMBLES BACKWARDS! Luster eyes Michaels who is slowly getting up….. LUSTER SETS WITH THE THREE POINT STANCE AND NAILS HIM WITH A SHOULDER BLOCK! This place is coming unglued for Luster.
VIC WATERS: The crowd has wanted to see Luster and Michaels go at it for a long, long time. It doesn’t surprise me at all these fears are cheering this brutality.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels rolls out of the ring… AND HE IS WAIVING TO THE BACK! What does Michaels have planned?
VIC WATERS: Michaels always has something up his sleeve, that is why he is one of the smartest men in all of wrestling.
BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, A WHOLE BUNCH DOCTORS IN WHITE COATS ARE COMING OUT AND THEY ARE HEADED TO THE RING! The doctors are slowly surrounding the ring and Luster looks awfully nervous.
VIC WATERS: I don’t understand this. BRANDON JACOBS: The paramedics rush the ring and Luster makes the tag to Edmunds as he attempts to escape! THE PARAMEDICS STUN LUSTER AND THEY PUTTING LUSTER IN A STRAIGHT JACKET!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is pleading with the guys to let Luster go so he could at least finish the match but the doctors will have nothing of that.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is laughing his ass off as Luster is being taken away. VIC WATERS: What is this all about? BRANDON JACOBS: There was rumor going around that Luster had missed the Road to Glory show because he had a mental breakdown but when he was scheduled for tonight’s show, I thought that it was just a lie that Michaels was spreading in order to tarnish the name of Luster. Apparently Michaels found out about Luster’s breakdown and called the doctor’s to show up tonight in order to embarrass him on national television and get him off the Superbowl of Wrestling show where Michaels was scheduled to face him.
VIC WATERS: Scotty Michaels is a bastard. Boy do I love him. BRANDON JACOBS: But now because of the actions of Michaels, Edmunds is without a partner tonight. He is going to have to fight Michaels and Tact alone if he wants to win. Considering everything Edmunds has gone through lately with his health, it is going to be a hell of a task to over come these two.
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is a former WFW World Heavyweight champion, if he beat Michaels once, if he is really as great as he says he is, he should have no trouble beating Michaels and Tact all by himself.
BRANDON JACOBS: This isn’t remotely fair. Both Tact and Michaels are back in the ring and Edmunds backs up to the center of the ring.
VIC WATERS: GET HIM GUYS! BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS CHARGES AND EDMUNDS DELIVERS A RIGHT TO BACK MICHAELS UP! TACT CHARGES AND EDMUNDS FIRES A SHOT TO DRIVE HIM BACK!
VIC WATERS: It is only a matter of time, Edmunds is outmanned. BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS JUMPS ONTO THE BACK OF EDMUNDS AND EDMUNDS IS TRYING TO GET HIM OFF! Tact kicks Edmunds in the ribs and Edmunds falls down to his knees.
VIC WATERS: Beat him! BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels holds Edmunds’ arms back and Tact fires away with closed punches right to the face! This crowd is really is really booing Michaels and Tact.
VIC WATERS: Guilt by association... BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is making Michaels go back to the corner and Tact is now the legal man. I think he has it reversed but never the less. TACT LOCKS EDMUNDS IN THE ABDOMINAL STRETCH!
VIC WATERS: Tact is trying to stretch the insides of Edmunds in order to cut down his mobility. Tact is from the old school of if you can move without hurting you have the advantage.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is trying to reach the ropes but he is a good three feet away from the ropes… WAIT A SECOND, EDMUNDS IS MAKING PROGRESS TOWARDS THE ROPES AS THIS CROWD IS CHANTING FOR EDMUNDS!
VIC WATERS: Michaels and Tact can’t let Edmunds win this one, this one is already in theirs, they just have to put this away.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is a foot away from the ropes! TACT SIDESLAMS EDMUNDS DOWN TO THE MAT BEFORE HE CAN GET THERE! Tact goes for the cover! 1------------------------------------------------------2----------------------- EDMUNDS GETS HIS FOOT ON THE ROPES!
VIC WATERS: Damn it. BRANDON JACOBS: Tact picks up Edmunds off the mat…. OVER THE HEAD BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX! Edmunds clutches his back…. TACT IS UP AGAIN… ANOTHER BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX ON EDMUNDS! Tact is destroying Edmunds!
VIC WATERS: Tact feels that he got a raw deal because of Sherri last time and he is looking to prove that he is the better man.
BRANDON JACOBS: Tact gets up and he picks up Edmunds off the mat…. BACKBREAKER AND TACT HOLDS THE BACKBREAKER IN OVER HIS KNEE!
VIC WATERS: Tact is ramming that knee right into the back of Edmunds in order to soften up his back for a pinfall.
BRANDON JACOBS: Tact finally breaks the hold and he drags Edmunds back to the center of the ring…. Tact goes to pickup Edmunds… EDMUNDS KICKS TACT IN THE HEAD!
VIC WATERS: Tact has to becareful to watch the feet. BRANDON JACOBS: Tact goes go pickup Edmunds again… EDMUNDS PULLS THE HAIR OF TACT AND CATCHES HIM IN A SMALL PACKAGE! 1------------------------------------2----------------------------- TACT REVERSES IT!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds’ back was too weak to all the abuse to hold it. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref goes down to count! 1---------------------------------------------2--------------------------- EDMUNDS KICKS OUT! Both men are down and look winded!
VIC WATERS: That was scarily close for both men. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds and Tact are both struggling to get up…. EDMUNDS MANAGES TO GET UP FIRST AND HE CLOTHESLINES TACT!
VIC WATERS: Come on, they can’t let Edmunds get back into this thing. Michaels got rid of Luster, this should be cake for these two.
BRANDON JACOBS: Tact is trying to get back up to his feet…. EDMUNDS BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND HITS THE SUNSET FLIP ON TACT! The ref goes down for the cover! 1-------------------------------------------------------------2-------------------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: That was way too close. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds races towards the corner and Tact is getting up and starts heading over…. REVERSE CROSS BODY ONTO TACT! Edmunds makes the cover! 1-------------------------------------------2-------------------------------------------- TACT JUST GETS HIS SHOULDER UP TO BEAT THE THREE!
VIC WATERS: Look at Michaels, he is about to have a brain hemorrhage from all of this. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds picks up Tact and he starts assailing him with fast rights and lefts right into the midsection! EDMUNDS WAISTLOCKS TACT AND PUTS HIM OVER HIS SHOULDER AND RAMS HIM INTO THE CORNER!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is trying to set him up for the Northern Lights Suplex. BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS RAMS HIM IN AGAIN! Edmunds backs up and Tact falls down onto his knees. Edmunds picks him back up…. HE LIFTS UP TACT AND SETS HIM ON THE TOP ROPE!
VIC WATERS: Tact is a big man, he is never going to be able to do this. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds climbs up to the second rope and he double underhooks both arms of Tact…. DOUBLE UNDERHOOK SUPERPLEX DOWN TO THE MAT! Edmunds goes for the cover!
VIC WATERS: Kick out Tact Kickout! BRANDON JACOBS: The ref goes down for the cover! 1---------------------------------------------------2---------------------------- MICHAELS DIVES IN AND BREAKS UP THE PINFALL ATTEMPT!
VIC WATERS: Thank god. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels picks up Edmunds…. KICK TO THE MIDSECTION! Michaels lifts Edmunds up…. BRAINBUSTER SUPLEX! The ref is escorting Michaels back to the corner! Tact goes for the cover!
VIC WATERS: Come on ref! Turn around! BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels finally exits the ring. The ref turns around and goes down for the count! 1-----------------------------------------------------------2----------------------------------- EDMUNDS JUST KICKS OUT!
VIC WATERS: We need better referees. Edmunds was done if he turned around quicker. BRANDON JACOBS: Tact picks up Edmunds off the mat…. KICKSWING DDT! Tact drags Edmunds to his corner…. TACT TAGS IN MICHAELS!
VIC WATERS: Tact has been in there for awhile. This is a smart call. Michaels is the fresher man.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels picks up Edmunds.... MICHAELS GERMAN SUPLEXES HIM DOWN TO THE MAT! He planted him right on his head!
VIC WATERS: This is an unwinnable fight for Edmunds, he should just lie down and take the pin instead of putting his body through all of this abuse.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is only one of two wrestlers who hold a pinfall victory over Michaels in his tenure here. He might just be able to pull this off. Michaels picks up Edmunds off the mat….. MICHAELS DRAGS HIM TO THE CENTER OF THE RING AND LOCKS IN A FULL NELSON!
VIC WATERS: Michaels is working over Edmunds neck to capitalize on the German suplex. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds looks like he is going out fast. Michaels has that locked in tight and he is shaking him around like a rag doll! Edmunds is fading…. MICHAELS BREAKS THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: Michaels must want to do more damage to Edmunds before he pins him. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is a bastard. That is what he is. Michaels picks Edmunds up, it looks like he is setting for a piledriver… EDMUNDS GRABS THE BOTTON OF MICHAELS TIGHTS AND IS PUTTING UP A FIGHT!
VIC WATERS: My god, Edmunds won’t die. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels repeatedly elbows Edmunds viciously right into the back that Tact worked over earlier!
VIC WATERS: Those elbows might as well be sledgehammers the way he is assaulting Edmunds right now.
BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS PICKS UP EDMUNDS FOR A PILEDRIVER…. NO WAIT! EDMUNDS GOES UP AND OVER MICHAELS!
VIC WATERS: Michaels used a bit too much power getting Edmunds up there and Edmunds capitalized on it.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds hooks the arms of Scotty Michaels… EDMUNDS FALLS DOWN ONTO HIS KNEES AND TAKES MICHAELS DOWN WITH A BACKSLIDE! The ref goes down for the count! 1-------------------------------2------------------------ MICHAELS GETS A SHOULDER UP JUST IN THE NICK OF TIME!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds almost did it! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds picks Michaels up off the mat quickly… HE DOUBLE UNDER HOOKS THE ARMS…. EDMUNDS PLANTS HIM WITH A DDT RIGHT INTO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Tact needs to get in here and help his partner out but Tact looks like he is still exhausted right now.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds makes the cover! 1--------------------------------------------------2----------------------------------- MICHAELS GETS HIS FOOT ON THE ROPE!
VIC WATERS: God bless the ropes. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds picks Michaels up quickly… SMALL PACKAGE BY EDMUNDS! 1------------------------------------------------2--------------------------- NO! MICHAELS REVERSES IT AND REACHES OUT TO GRAB THE ROPES FOR LEVERAGE! 1---------------------------------2-------------------------------------------3! MICHAELS & TACT WIN IT!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. THE TEAM OF SCOTTY MICHAELS AND LARRY TACT! BRANDON JACOBS: Larry Tact comes in and Michaels and Larry Tact are stomping a mud hole into Edmunds! This is totally uncalled for, isn’t it enough Michaels cheated to win?
VIC WATERS: Don’t you remember what Sherri did to Tact? It is damn called for. ::huge roar from the crowd as someone bolts down the aisle way:: BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, DO MY EYES DECEIVE ME?! THAT IS SHAWN HART AND HE IS WELDING A CHAIR! Larry Tact rolls out of the ring…. BUT MICHAELS GETS CLOBBERED BY THE CHAIR AND GOES OVER THE TOP ROPE!
::Hart grabs a microphone from the ring announcer:: HART: It’s been THREE, looooooooong months since last I stepped into this ring... all that time spent, standing idly by while YOU, Scotty Michaels, ruined everything I worked so hard to build. For almost a YEAR I helped mold this company into one of the TOP promotions in the land. I set the standard for what a true CHAMPION should be, and for MONTH after MONTH, I went out EACH and EVERY night and made good on that standard. Like me or not, I EARNED the respect of every... single... person in the back, aaaaaaaaaall the people who tuned in our shows and came to the events, and showed the world that, as far as ROCKIN’ a show goes, NOBODY does it BETTER... than the PHENOM!!
::huge face pop from the crowd:: HART: Then you came along, Mr. Viewer’s Choice. You came in like the son of a B(FCC)TCH you are and proceeded to piss on everything I’ve done, everything I’ve stood for! So I took a step back... I’d been slapped around, STOMPED on and beaten away at YOUR hands. That bein’ the case, I did what any respectable man would do and tipped my hat. You took that ball out of my hands fair and square, Scotty, and me, being that respectable man, decided to pass that torch and let you run with it...
::crowd boos:: HART: Now, that’s all good and well, but here’s where we have just a liiiiiittle bit of a problem. Y’see, I’ve been accused of being ALOT of things in my time, some of them true... well, heh.. alot of them true actually, but Scotty... I’ve got to be honest with you... a RESPECTABLE MAN has NEVER been one of those things!!
CROWD: PHENOM! PHENOM! PHENOM! HART: ...And from the looks of it, that proverbial torch you’ve been handed has been EXTINGUISHED. So the way I see it, I’ve got two options here... A, I can continue to sip daquiris and watch reruns of Judging Amy... or TWO...
(He grins maliciously, then kicks his feet back against the mat.) HART: ...I can show up at the SUPERBOWL of WRESTLING, and kick your JACKHOLE ass like Luster never could!! I dunno, wadda you guys think?!
CROWD: ::Hart holds mic to crowd:: KICK HIS ASS! KICK HIS ASS! KICK HIS ASS! HART: Well VIEWER’S CHOICE... the viewers have choooooosen!! I’ll have to TIVO the X-Files, but hey... how can you celebrate a YEAR of World’s Finest Wrestling without the World’s FINEST Wrestler?! The PHENOM... has left... the BUILDING!!
::Michaels looks on with stunned disbelief:: BRANDON JACOBS: I told you he would be back for revenge. This should be awesome. VIC WATERS: Just like Scotty Michaels proved tonight by beating the only man who held two victories over him, he doesn’t need Luster to win.
BRANDON JACOBS: We will see. It is great to have our greatest champion ever Shawn Hart back. Things haven’t been the same without him.
VIC WATERS: Yeah, they have been better. BRANDON JAOCBS: Quiet you. Folks, when we comeback, Manson and Rat Fink will team up against the Donald Trump of the WFW and current World Heavyweight Champion Alex Wylde and his partner Pulsar. STAY TUNED!
