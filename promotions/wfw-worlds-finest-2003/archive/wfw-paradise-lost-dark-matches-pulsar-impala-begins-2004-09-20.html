WFW Paradise Lost: Dark Matches, Pulsar/Impala begins

20 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 20 September 2004 [A African American man is sitting behind a news desk dressed in a tie and black formal suit at WFW Headquarters.]
Hello everyone, my name is Watts Gnu and this is WFW Control Center. Due to time limitations, three matches that happened tonight happened before we went on the air and we here to update you, the WFW fan on what happened.
In our opening match of the evening, Man Beast went out of control nearly getting DQed several times in his match with Striker. Striker valiantly fought back but Man Beast kept on cheating at every turn. When it looked like Striker could take no more, Arquito Iris, the mini version of El Arco Iris, ran down to ringside and in an attempt to save Striker…. mooned Man Beast. The deranged Man Beast was so enraged that it caused him to be momentarily distracted and Striker rolled him up from behind to get the three and the victory. Man Beast grabbed the microphone and swore death to all midgets as Striker carried off Arquito Iris on his shoulders.
In our second match of the night, John Doe squared off against Michael Dice who was making his return after being ambushed and put on the shelf by Shawn Hart at Bomb Voyage. Dice looked to be in excellent shape, but Doe played on his lack of ring conditioning and slowly wore down Dice who hadn’t wrestled in months throughout the course of the match and it was more than Dice could over come. Doe finished Dice off at the twenty one minute mark with Amnesia Attack for the victory. It took several minutes before Dice was able to come to and be helped to the back.
In our final dark match of the evening, in a match under BAD Rules, Pulsar got the big upset win against Rat Fink after battling against interference from Satan Clause and Buckybot throughout. Pulsar was busted open early on by a steel chair shot early in the match but he fought to overcome the blood which impaired his vision and Rat Fink took advantage of it throughout the match. Rat Fink and Pulsar brawled in the crowd using various objects including a violin from a local member of the Boston Symphony Orchestra, a broken baseball bat which belonged to Jim Rice, and kitchen sink. Pulsar managed to get the pinfall with the Black Hole at the seventeen minute mark after several near falls by both men.
But the real story HAPPENED after the match… Lets go to live footage of what happened after the match.
[Recorded Earlier] BRANDON JACOBS: What a big for Pulsar. JONATHAN MARX: Pulsar continues to show why he is one of the most promising young prospect in all of wrestling.
VIC WATERS: Bah, Rat Fink got robbed. (CUEUP: Tribal Drumming) [Lights dim, the Mighty Impala walks out in his flowing tribal robes with a microphone in hand down to the ring and Pulsar looks up confused.]
BRANDON JACOBS: My god, Mighty Impala is back, we haven’t seen him in quiet some time. JONATHAN MARX: When you run a country, you get awfully busy. THE MIGHTY IMPALA: Ah, do not be afraid, Pulsar! I know you are not used to being around people of my ROYAL Her-EEE-tage and NOBLE BLOOD! But I am not here to kick dirt in your face because of your background of poverty, I am here to offer you a HAND UP! Pulsar, you have impressed me with your physical attributes and technical skills. And I am a man of a high education who does not get impressed easy! As such, Pulsar, I offer you the chance of a lifetime! You are the one I have scouted and I am offering you the chance to learn my TUTELADGE and work for me in my quest at eternal glory... I am offering you the chance to work for me as my MANSERVANT as I procure myself championship gold!
[Pulsar looks at him like he is nuts.] BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar looks like he doesn’t know what to make of the Mighty Impala. JONATHAN MARX: He is quite an odd fellow. VIC WATERS: Rich people are allowed to be eccentric. BRANDON JACOBS: Shhh, he is speaking again. THE MIGHTY IMPALA: I know this must be overwhelming for you to consider right now, the chance to do whatever I command... Pulsar, show me you accept my gracious offer and get down on your knees and kiss my royal feet and tell me you want to be my servant!
[The Mighty Impala hands Pulsar the mic and points for Pulsar to kneel.] BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar doesn’t kneel to anyone, he has too much damn self respect, what nerve the Mighty Impala has.
PULSAR: I... I can have the chance to be your servant? [The Mighty Impala shakes his head yes.] PULSAR: I can be the servant to a man whose ASS I BEAT in this ring in my debut match? [The Mighty Impala starts to get an angry look.] PULSAR: I say take your offer and shove it. Get lost, pal. THE MIGHTY IMPALA: You will regret this! [The Mighty Impala attacks Pulsar…] BRANDON JACOBS: MIGHTY IMPALA JUST AMBUSHED PULSAR! JONATHAN MARX: GIVE HIM THE WHAT FOR PULSAR! [Pulsar turns the tide and gets the upper hand, pummeling him with lefts and rights.] CROWD: PULSAR! PULSAR! PULSAR! [Pulsar leaves the ring. The Mighty Impala then picks himself up, his nose bloodied...] THE MIGHTY IMPALA: (having a tantrum) You will regret soiling my flesh! I will best you yet! You will kneel before me! I WILL BE CHAMP-EE-ON! I COME FROM ROYAL HER-EEE-TAGE!
[Back to WFW Headquarters] WATTS GNU: It looks like things are just beginning between the returning Mighty Impala and Pulsar….
[grabs ear piece] WATTS GNU: I am being told that we have to throw things back to Fenway Park because THE INNER CIRCLE IS WITHIN FIVE MINUTES OF THE BUILDING! God help us all…
[Commercial: FMLL: No es su madre que lucha. http://fwrestling.com/host/fmll ] GreggG — 20 September 2004 (CUT TO: The locker room. The flag of Cote D'Ivoire hangs in the background, over Johnny Damon's locker. He is holding his bloodied nose, a look of pure disdain on his face.)
TMI: Puhls-ar. You had an OPPOR-TUNITY. I gave you a CHANCE OF THE LIFETIME! I gave you the CHANCE to BOW DOWN BEFORE ME and KISS MY FEET in AHL-LEE-GANCE. I gave you a chance to EHS-SCAPE THE POVERTY YOU COME FROM to serve me... a man of NOBLE BLOOD and ROYAL HER-EEE-TAGE. I gave you a chance to work FOR ME AS MY PERSONAL MANSERVANT. I gave you a CHANCE to procure me the WFW World CHAMP-EEE-OWN-SHIP, a chance to SERVE ME as I ascend to my rightful place as the MOST POWERFUL MAN IN THEES EEEN-DUS-TREE.
(TMI moans in pain, holding his nose.) EEN-STEAD, Puhl-sar... EEN-STEAD OF KNEELING BEFORE ME LIKE THE SCURRYING ANT YOU ARE... you disobeyed your superior... YOU ASSAULTED THE MAN WHOSE GRANDFATHER ONCE RULED THE WESTERN PLAINS OF GHANA! YOU ASSAULTED THE MAN WHOSE GREAT UNCLE SAT ON THE THRONE OF TOGO! You assaulted the man who, on the snap of his fingers, commanded Tutsi forces to humiliate the Hutu nuns of Monrovia! CHOP!CHOP! I say and 45 women were left without right hands! (Impala cackles in glee.) Puhl-sar... NO ONE IN WESTERN AFR-EE-CA DISOBEYS ME... and NO ONE IN THEES PRO-MO-SHUN DISOBEYS ME! For your sins, you shall have a worse fate than the rebels in Sierra Leone who defied me, the rebels whose sons I killed and whose daughters I raped. Puhl-sar, you had a CHOICE to serve me but now you have no choice. You will be CRUSHED under my boot, publicly humiliated in surrender, physically and emotionally beaten and then FORCED TO DO AS I BID!
You will, my Outback poverty-streeken friend... serve the man of noble blood... and royal HER-EEE-TAGE.
(TMI groans some more as the camera goes FTB.)
