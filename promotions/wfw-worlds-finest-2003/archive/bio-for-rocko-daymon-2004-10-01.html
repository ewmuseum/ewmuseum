FINISHER: "Brain Rocker Redux" (Double-Underhook Pulling Piledriver) WHO YOUR CHARACTER IS: Daymon has over six years of professional wrestling experience, with occassional breaks during his career due to injuries sustained in the neck or right knee. He made his findings in [name withheld]'s "Superior Championship Wrestling". After the federation closed, he did time in [name withheld]'s "Major Championship Wrestling" and "Empire Pro Wrestling", and Chad Dupree and [name withheld]'s "Global Wrestling Evolution". Following his departure of the latter two, Daymon has competed in a Japanese indy fed with his brother-in-law.
While usually known for his somber attitude toward his opponents and the industry as a whole, Rocko Daymon returns more as a confident fan favorite with moderate experience. To him, it's no longer about success; he's solely competing for participation, knowing he can do something well while still in his prime.

PaulNJ21 — 31 July 2006 This e-mail is being sent out to all of the owners of the federations I'm currently in, in regards to a few edits I'm making to Rocko Daymon. This is to cut down on any confusion, perhaps leading people to believe that my character is actually two seperate people. I'm trying to adhere him to a standard that fits all federations.
For starters, I've decided to drop "Rocko" from his name. If I had known how ridiculous that name was eight years ago when I crafted him, I probably would have gone with something more practical (and ACTUAL name, maybe). From now on, Rocko Daymon will simply be known by his last name, "Daymon." This is how I'd like the commentators to refer to him as from now on, although I wouldn't get angry if I saw his first name pop up a time or two.
Second, Daymon's offical entrance music from now on will be "Stones From The Sky" by Neurosis. I know I've been changing it a lot in the past year, which is why I'm going to stick with this, my most recent pick. His entrance will otherwise be the same; no pyros or light effects, just the music and he comes out to fight.
Last, I'm not sure if this applies to all federations, but the Brain Rocker Redux will be described as a double-underhook face-first piledriver with a 180 degree twist. I think I've described it differently in other federations (usually as a snap piledriver, which is the old-school Brain Rocker, sans Redux).
That is all for Daymon. Thanks for listening. -Ryan
