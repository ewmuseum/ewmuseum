Superbowl of Wrestling: Gideon (c) vs. Psycho - Four Corners of Hell - (Second Fall)

26 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 26 April 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back for the second fall and before we get started. Let’s go over what is in each corner of the Four Corners Of Hell Match. In the first corner will be a pallet covered in barbed-wire, in the second corner will be a tray filled with one thousand thumbtacks, in the three will be a trash a trashcan filled with fifty lightubes and the last will have a staple gun. Other than that, normal rules will apply, you can either beat your opponent by submission or pinfall.
VIC WATERS: The ref is signaling that the two minute rest period is over. *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: During the break, some medics came in to check on Gideon and from what they can tell, he just sprang his neck and he should be fine. There is no serious damage…
VIC WATERS: Yet, but the match isn’t over yet… BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is catching a breather by the ropes…. PSYCHO IS GRABBING A LIGHT TUBE AND HE IS COMING AFTER GIDEON…. GIDEON ROLLS OUT OF THE WAY AND PSYCHO SMASHES THE LIGHT TUBE ON THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: That was a Ruthian like swing by Psycho. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon grabs the pallet of barbed wire…. PSYCHO COMES BACK FROM THE GARBAGE CAN FULL OF LIGHT TUBES AND TAKES ANOTHER SWING AT HIM…. GIDEON BLOCKS THE SHOT WITH THE BARBED WIRE PALLET!
VIC WATERS: Gideon is using the weapon as a shield. He is a bright cookie. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon gets up…. GIDEON HITS PSYCHO WITH THE BARBED WIRE PALLET OVER THE HEAD AND PSYCHO FALLS DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: With as angry as Gideon has been at Psycho, I don’t think putting weapons in his hands is a good idea either.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon puts the pallet face down on Psycho…. Gideon bounces off the ropes…. DOUBLE FOOT STOMP ONTO THE BOARD BY GIDEON!
VIC WATERS: That barbed wire can get tangled up with the skin, Psycho has to becareful. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon drags Psycho over to the corner…. Gideon is climbing up to the rope rope…. SYSTEM FAILURE 450 IN A FROG SPLASH RIGHT ONTO THE BOARD ONTO PSYCHO! Gideon clutches his ribs!
VIC WATERS: Gideon is hitting him with everything he has! BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon pushes the barbed wire pallet off of Psycho and makes the cover! 1-------------------------------------------------------2------------------------------- PSYCHO MANAGES TO GET THE SHOULDER UP!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is an iron man. BRANDON JACOBS: It is going to take a whole lot of abuse to keep Psycho down for a three in a hardcore match. Gideon walks over to the corner and he grabs two light tubes to use on Psycho.
VIC WATERS: Four down, forty six to go. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is attempting to get up…. GIDEON STARTS USING THE TWO LIGHT TUBES LIKE KENDO STICKS…. GIDEON BREAKS A LIGHT TUBE OVER THE STOMACH OF PSYCHO AND THEN BREAKS THE OTHER ONE OVER PSYCHO’S HEAD!
VIC WATERS: There is starting to be glass all over the ring. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho falls down to one knee…. GIDEON CHARGES PSCYHO AND HITS HIM WITH A SHINING WIZARD AND PSYCHO GOES DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: I’ve never seen anyone as motivated as Gideon to keep his title. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is going over to the corner…. HE GRABS THE TRAY OF ONE THOUSAND THUMBTACKS AND HE SPILLS IT ALL OVER THE RING!
VIC WATERS: You would expect this kind of thing from Psycho, not Gideon. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is calling for the G-Force! GIDEON PICKS UP PSYCHO FOR A BRAINBUSTER…. GIDEON BRAINBUSTERS PSYCHO INTO THE THUMBTACKS!
CROWD: BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! VIC WATERS: Whoa. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon picks him up for another brainbuster…. HE HITS IT AGAIN! VIC WATERS: Look at the back of Psycho, he is almost completely covered in thumbtacks. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon lifts up Psycho one last time…. HE HITS A THIRD AND FINAL BRAINBUSTER!
VIC WATERS: That had to be the three most devastating brainbusters of alltime. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon goes to make the cover! The ref starts sweeping away a spot to go down to the mat and count the fall.
VIC WATERS: This ref is a damn *****. He could be costing Gideon the fall because he is afraid of a couple thumbtacks.
BRANDON JACOBS: The referee finally goes down for the count! 1---------------------------------------------------------------2------------------------------------- KICKOUT! Psycho got the shoulder up damnit!
VIC WATERS: Gideon looks irate too, he is arguing with the ref that he cost him the fall. BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second… PSYCHO REACHES UP FROM BEHIND AND ROLLS HIM UP INTO THE THUMBTACKS! The ref goes down to count! 1-----------------------------------------------2-----------------------KICKOUT WITH AUTHORITY!
VIC WATERS: Gideon wanted him to count quicker BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon charges Psycho… DROP TOE HOLD BY PSYCHO! VIC WATERS: Psycho may have just saved his own life there with the look Gideon has in his eyes.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho runs over to the corner and he grabs the trash can full of light tubes…. Gideon is getting up AND PSYCHO THROWS IT RIGHT AT GIDEON WHO CATCHES IT! Psycho bounces off the ropes…. HE YAKUZA KICKS THE GARBAGE CAN RIGHT INTO THE FACE OF GIDEON AND GIDEON FALLS BACK TO THE MAT AND THE GLASS FROM A DOZEN OR SO BROKEN LIGHT TUBES SPILLS ONTO HIM ON THE GROUND!
VIC WATERS: Look at the sheer carnage in the ring! BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is walking over to the corner…… HE IS GETTING THE STAPLE GUN AND HE IS HEADED OVER TO GIDEON! Psycho mounts the struggling Gideon who is fighting the staple gun off!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is going to ruin Gideon’s pretty little face…. BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON MANAGES TO KNOCK THE STAPLE GUN OUT OF PSYCHO’S HAND AND IT FLIES TO THE OUTSIDE OF THE RING!
VIC WATERS: Gideon just lucked out on that one. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho takes the barbed wire board and he nails Gideon over the head to keep him in place! PSYCHO IS GOING TO THE OUTSIDE TO GET THE STAPLE GUN!
VIC WATERS: Psycho seems set on causing as much carnage as humanly possible. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is starting to get up and HE ROLLS OUT OF THE RING! A aggravated Psycho charges him with the staple gun…. BACK BODY DROP BY GIDEON!
VIC WATERS: That was a pure survival tactic by Gideon! BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is getting up and he grabs the WFW North American Title…. PSYCHO IS GETTING UP…. GIDEON HITS HIM OVER THE HEAD WITH IT!
VIC WATERS: What a shot BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon takes the staple gun…Gideon puts the staple gun on Psycho CROTCH….Gideon climbs up on the ring apron… PSYCHO THANKFULLY MOVES OUT OF THE WAY JUST IN TIME BUT GIDEON HITS A DOUBLE STOMP ON THE RIGHT LEG OF PSYCHO!
VIC WATERS: It is better than a staple somewhere else. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon picks up the staple gun…. HE PICKS PSYCHO UP AND HE NAILS HIM OVER THE HEAD WITH THE SIDE OF HIM AND HE IS NOW VISIBLY BLEEDING THROUGH THE MASK! .
VIC WATERS: Those metal stapler guns hurt even if you don’t get stapled with them. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon rolls Psycho in and he is bringing the WFW North American Title into the ring.
VIC WATERS: Is that legal? BRANDON JACOBS: If the ref doesn’t say anything, it is legal…. GIDEON SLAMS PSYCHO DOWN TO THE MAT AND HE PUTS THE TITLE ON THE CHEST OF PSYCHO! Gideon is going to the ropes…
VIC WATERS: This is an awfully big risk for Gideon, if he misses this, it could cost him the North American Title which he hold so dear.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is climbing the turnbuckles…. HE IS PERCHED ON THE TOP ROPE….. HE FLIES OFF WITH THE SYSTEM FAILURE 450 IN A FROG SPLASH AND CONNECTS!
VIC WATERS: Psycho needs to get out of the ring before Gideon can pin him. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon makes the cover! 1---------------------------------------2---------------------------- GOOD GOD PSYCHO GOT THE SHOULDER UP!
VIC WATERS: Look at all the blood covering Psycho’s chest and back but Psycho won’t stay down despite.
BRANDON JACOBS: A FRUSTRATED GIDEON IS SPILLING THE REMAINING LIGHT TUBES ONTO THE MAT! VIC WATERS: This can’t lead to any good. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is picking up a barely conscious Psycho…. TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER INTO THE LIGHT TUBES! Gideon makes the cover!
VIC WATERS: I think Gideon has it this time! BRANDON JACOBS: The referee goes down for the count! 1--------------------------------------------2----------------------------------3! GIDEON TAKES THE SECOND FALL!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the second fall…. RICHARD GIDEON! There will now be a two minute rest period to clean to clean the ring in preparation for the third fall….. A TEXAS DEATH MATCH!
CROWD: TEXAS DEATH! TEXAS DEATH! TEXAS DEATH! BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we are going to take a short break, but when we comeback, it is time for the third and deciding fall!
