WFW: Spirit of NGEN: Doc vs. Hart - Cage - 2nd Fall

20 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 20 August 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: During the break, Shawn Hart attacked the paramedics to keep them from fully helping Doc and Hart is now stomping away on Doc as the cage is lowering!
VIC WATERS: This isn’t fair to Doc, he barely had any break. Now he has to compete in a cage match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart grabs Doc Silver by his legs…. GIANT SWING! HE SWINGS DOC AROUND ONE….. TWO…. THREE…. FOUR…. FIVE… SIX…. SEVEN…. HART LEGS GO AND DOC GOES FLYING! A dizzy Shawn Hart tries to regain his senses.
VIC WATERS: Hart is really making Doc’s head a mess burning Diablo burning him off and spinning him around like a top. I don’t know how he’ll be able to comeback from this.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart picks Doc Silver up… HE WHIPS HIM OFF THE ROPES! HART TRIES TO CATCH HIM IN A SLEEPER BUT DOC SIDE STEPS IT AND RAMS HIM INTO THE CAGE HEAD FIRST! Doc takes the head of Hart and rakes it across the cage! Hart is busted open!
VIC WATERS: Die Hart Die! BRANDON JACOBS: DOC SILVER RAMS HART’S HEAD INTO THE TURNBUCKLE… AND AGAIN! AND AGAIN! THIS CROWD IS COUNTING WITH DOC! 3---4---5---6---7---8 ---9---10!
VIC WATERS: The fans love to see Hart taking this beating and Doc is more than happy to give it to him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Doc puts Hart in a front face lock… SWINGING NECK BREAKER! HE GOES FOR THE COVER! 1-------------------2--------------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: He almost had him! He needs to strike when the iron is hot and finish him off! BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver picks Hart up… LOW BLOW BY HART! Wlyde is shaking at his finger at Hart and he tells him not to do that again!
VIC WATERS: DQ! CALL FOR THE DAMN DQ! BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart lifts Doc up… INVERTED ATOMIC DROP! He kicks him in the stomach… HART ATTACK! Hart covers! He could be our new champ! 1-------------------2----------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: THANK GOD! BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver is getting up slowly… HART IS GOING FOR THE EL CODO EXPLOSIVO! DOC DUCKS!
VIC WATERS: YES! BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart turns around…. DOC KICKS HIM IN THE STOMACH! ACES FULL! DOC COVERS! 1-------------------2------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: THE WINNER OF THE SECOND FALL, DOC SILVER! BRANDON JACOBS: WE ARE ALL TIED UP AT 1-1! WE WILL BE BACK FOR THE LAST FALL WHICH WILL BE LAST MAN STANDING!
