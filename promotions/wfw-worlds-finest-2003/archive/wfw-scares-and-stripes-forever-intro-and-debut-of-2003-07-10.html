WFW: Scares and Stripes Forever: Intro and Debut of...

10 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 10 July 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Jul-10-03 AT 01:47 PM (EST)]BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome everyone to Scares and Stripes Forever! We are live here from the Milltown Fairgrounds with a jam packed crowd ready to catch some explosive action!
VIC WATERS: Doc Silver is champion, all is right with the world once again. Tonight should be an excellent night of action, I’m really looking forward to the show.
BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver may not be champion by the time the night is over, if Psycho pins Doc, he gets the rematch… RIGHT HERE TONIGHT IN MILLTOWN, NEW JERSEY!
VIC WATERS: I thought Psycho was dead after the last show’s main event but that guy is as tough as nails, he keeps on coming back for more punishment. Doc Silver needs to avoid Psycho at all costs, because if Psycho pins him in the tag match, I can’t see Doc stopping him two shows in a row after the beating that Doc Silver took in that match. Doc needs to lay low and hope he recovers in time for next show, where he will face either Rat Fink or Felix Red for the title.
BRANDON JACOBS: Not only that, Manson will defend the North American title against up and coming Andre Eian. Andre Eian is a modern day success story, he has been climbing up the ranks and tonight, he gets his title shot against one of the great wrestlers in the world today, Michael Manson. That should be a hell of a fight between those two.
VIC WATERS: I’m going to go on record, I think Eian will be the one to beat Manson here tonight and shock the whole wrestling world. Manson’s knee has been getting progressively worse since Michaels injured it and he can’t last much longer. That North American title is without a doubt the most prestigious secondary title in all of professional wrestling today because Manson never got pinned to get it. Manson has beat challenger after challenge, will Eian be added to that list tonight or will be the one who stops the streak?
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson had that epic battle with Rat Fink and he barely survived against him with Rat Fink pulling every dirty trick in the book. I don’t know how much longer he can hold on to that title but Manson is going to die trying. Now let’s move on to the other matches….
LOUD SPEAKERS: “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, LEND ME YOUR EARS!” ::“My Way” by Butch Walker plays as the crowd pops huge:: BRANDON JACOBS: WHAT THE… This isn’t on the format! ::Copycat walks out:: BRANDON JACOBS: Oh my god, it is Copycat! HE HAS RETURNED! ::Copycat gives his beret to a little kid in the front row and enters the ring:: Copycat: PEOPLE OF MILLTOWN! LEND ME YOUR EARS! (Cheap pop for that) Come on, you knew this had to happen eventually...the Cat is back, as a part of World's Finest Wrestling! As some of you know, for the past seven months, I haven't been seen much in the wrestling world. I've appeared on some TV shows. I've directed some music videos. I've produced an album. Hell, I even sang on one of the songs. And so when I told the WFW board of directors I wanted to get back into the business, they were naturally a little bit curious. They wanted to know...why come back? Why leave behind the stardom you've built for yourself? Why give up the big paydays? Why give up the bright lights? Why give up the glory? Well, I've got a couple of reasons, and I think they're good ones. Reason number one...there are a lot of real confident guys back in the WFW locker room. Guys who've built names for themselves in this business. Guys who, time and time again, act like they can't be beaten. So I guess it's just in my nature to shatter an illusion or two, and show a few people who think they're unbeatable just how susceptible to defeat they really are. And reason number two...reason number two is very important. Reason number two is really the top reason I decided to come back. Reason number two is one that a lot of people should acknowledge...reason number two is you people. (Cheap pop) All of you people in this arena, and all of you people watching at home. Even if I could leave behind the wrestling lifestyle, I can't leave behind the wrestling fans. Somebody has to come out here and tell the fans just how appreciated they are when everyone else acts like they don't care, and somebody has to come out here and tell the Doc Silvers and Michael Mansons of the world that the fans are the ones who MAKE this business, not their enormous egos. So the next time one of those overconfident punks in the back starts thinking he's invincible, he'd better watch out, because the CAT...IS...BACK, BABY!
::“My Way” by Butch Walker plays as Copycat leaves the ring:: BRANDON JACOBS: Awesome way to start the show, Copycat is here in the WFW! VIC WATERS: I’ve seen Copycat beat legendary performer after legendary performer, he is a hell of a competitor and has beaten both former WWL World Champion Nevada Smith and one of the greatest brawlers of alltime, King Krusher.
BRANDON JACOBS: I grew up watching King Krusher, he was one of the best ever that I’ve ever seen in that ring. He didn’t win as much titles as other guys, but that guy had tremendous heart and he was the king of the brawlers. He could fight like no one’s business.
VIC WATERS: Should we resume going over the show or should we just start this sucker up? BRANDON JACOBS: Let’s start this sucker up, fans already have their juices going so we shouldn’t make them sit through the preview. We will be back with Luster vs. McMillan for a shot at the North American Title #1 Contender spot right after this commercial break!
