WFW: Spirit of NGEN Doc vs. Hart - Last Man Standing - 3rd F

20 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 20 August 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Aug-20-03 AT 12:02&nbsp;PM (EST)]BRANDON JACOBS: We are tied up at 1-1 and this fall will be for all the marbles. Both Doc Silver and Shawn Hart have had the two minutes between matches to rest finally so they have finally had a chance to catch their breath.
VIC WATERS: Once you get hit by the Aces Full, it is all over. No man has ever kicked out of it. It is like trying to beat Doc in a ladder or cage match, it is nearly impossible.
BRANDON JACOBS: ALEX WLYDE IS CALLING FOR THE BELL! IT IS TIME TO START THE THIRD AND DECIDING FALL!
VIC WATERS: LET’S GO DOC! *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: A tired Doc Silver and Shawn Hart come out of their corner… DOC IS GOING ON THE OFFENSIVE! HE STARTS NAILING HIM WITH LEFTS AND RIGHTS AND BACKS SHART HART INTO THE CORNER! He starts ramming the shoulder into Shawn Hart to take the wind out of him!
VIC WATERS: The thing which makes it so tricky to get a victory in a Last Man Standing Match is that you have to do enough damage to keep your man down for ten seconds so it isn’t like planning a strategy to get a three.
BRANDON JACOBS: DOC SILVER GRABS SHAWN HART AND NAILS HIM WITH A OVERHEAD THROW BELLY TO BELLY! The ref makes the count on Shawn Hart! 1-----2-----3----4------5------6-----Shawn Hart gets up to break the count!
VIC WATERS: Doc showing his power there on that throw. I’m shocked he has this much energy left in him.
BRANDON JACOBS: If Doc is still alive after that cage match with Psycho, there is nothing that he can’t do. DOC SILVER CHARGES SHAWN HART WITH A CLOTHESLINE AND HE NAILS IT! Silver jumps on top of Shawn Hart and starts nailing him with elbows to the face to rub in the two El Codo Explosivos which have backfired in his face already in this match!
VIC WATERS: It looks like there is some blood coming out of Shawn Hart’s nose! BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver gets up off Shawn Hart…. HE TAKING OFF HIS BOOT! DOC ISN’T ALLOWED TO USE A FOREIGN OBJECT IN THIS! HE COULD GET HIMSELF DQED!
VIC WATERS: This is one of the rare cases where a DQ would actually cost you the title. BRANDON JACOBS: WLYDE GRABS THE BOOT OF DOC SILVER! WLYDE IS LOOKING AT THE BOTTOM! WLYDE: “Made in the USA!” ::Wlyde gives the boot back to Doc:: BRANDON JACOBS: DOC STARTS WAILING ON SHAWN HART WITH THE BOOT RIGHT IN THE FACE! Shawn Hart is struggling to get up despite the shots with the boot but amount of blood pouring down his face is increasing!
VIC WATERS: The ref needs to call this match for an excess of blood! BRANDON JACOBS: We aren’t in Baltimore, Maryland, this Orlando, Florida and this is a cock fight! DOC SILVER WINDS UP AND HE HITS SHAWN HART WITH THE BOOT AND HE GOES BACK DOWN AGAIN! Doc Silver puts the boot back on as the ref counts Shawn Hart! 1----2----3-----4----5-----6----7----8---- SHAWN HART GETS UP TO BREAK THE COUNT!
VIC WATERS: I don’t know how Shawn Hart does it. BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver picks Shawn Hart up…. BODYSLAM! Doc Silver bounces off the ropes…. SHAWN HART MOVES OUT OF THE WAY OF THE KNEE DROP! Doc looked like he hurt himself on that!
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart grabs the injured leg of Doc Silver…. HALF BOSTON CRAB! BRANDON JACOBS: This is going to give Shawn Hart a chance for a breather while he is doing damage to Doc Silver’s leg!
VIC WATERS: Good plan by Shawn Hart, even if I dislike him with a passion. BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver is trying to get himself to the ropes… he is using all his strength to get there…. HE REACHES OUT…. HE MISSES! He tries again…. HE JUST TOUCHES IT AND WLYDE IS MAKING HART BREAK!
VIC WATERS: Good referring by Wylde here! BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart grabs the legs of Doc and pulls him to the center of the ring… he goes to grab the hair…. DOC KICKS SHAWN HART IN THE HEAD WHEN HE TRIES TO GET CLOSE TO HIM! Shawn Hart tries again and this time he picks Doc Silver up and puts him front face lock!
VIC WATERS: Don’t die on me Doc. BRANDON JACOBS: SHAWN HART NAILS DOC WITH A DDT! He picks up Doc again…. THIS TIME HE NAILS HIM WITH A TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER! Wlyde makes the count on Doc! 1----2----3----4-----5---- DOC SITS UP AND STRUGGLES TO HIS FEET!
VIC WATERS: Doc hasn’t taken nearly the punishment that Shawn Hart has in this fall. BRANDON JACOBS: SHAWN HART NAILS DOC WITH A SUPERKICK! Doc is down! Alex Wlyde makes the count! 1---2---3----4---5----6----7 DOC IS GETTING UP! Shawn Hart can’t keep him down!
VIC WATERS: Doc is a resilient warrior which makes him so great. BRANDON JACOBS: SHAWN HART GOES FOR ANOTHER SUPERKICK BUT DOC CATCHES HART’S LEG AND HE SPINS SHAWN HART AROUND…. DOC GOES FOR A SUPERKICK DUCKS…. DOC NAILS SHAWN HART WITH THE DDT! The ref makes the count on Hart! 1----2----3----4---5----6----7--- 8--- SHAWN HART IS GETTING UP!
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart got caught really off guard on that shot! BRANDON JACOBS: DOC SILVER SLAPS SHAWN HART ACROSS THE FACE! SHAWN HART RESPONDS WITH OF A SLAP OF HIS OWN! DOC SILVER NAILS SHAWN HART WITH A RIGHT AND SHAWN HART GOES DOWN! Wlyde makes the count! 1----2----3----4----5---- 6--- Shawn Hart is getting up!
VIC WATERS: You can feel the hatred that these two men have for each other! BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart gets up and Doc and Shawn Hart start trading blows back and forth! BOTH MEN ARE STAGGERING ON THEIR FEET! Shawn Hart bounces off the ropes…. HE NAILS DOC WITH THE EL CODO EXPLOSIVO…. DOC FALLS BACK OFF AND ROPES AND SOMEHOW MANAGES TO NAIL HART WITH A SUPERKICK! Wlyde is counting both men down!
ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! BOTH MEN ARE BEGINNING TO STIR! SIX! DOC IS TRYING TO USE THE ROPES TO GET BACK UP! SEVEN! HART IS STARTING TO MOVE AND IS TRYING TO STAND ON HIS OWN! EIGHT! SILVER IS HAVING TROUBLE PUTTING THE WEIGHT ON INJURED KNEE! NINE! HART IS UP AND DOC LOSES HIS GRIP ON THE ROPE AND FALLS DOWN TO THE MAT! TEN! ::Wlyde signals for the bell:: ::fans pop for the finish:: VIC WATERS: NO DAMNIT! RING ANNOUNCER: THE WINNER OF THIS MATCH… AND NEW WFW AND BAD WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION…. “THE PHENOM” SHAWN HART!
(CUEUP: “We Are Champions” By Queen) (Diablo and Tiny come out to celebrate with Champaign in hand, Shawn Hart falls to his knees and hugs the title)
BRANDON JACOBS: The third time was a charm for Shawn Hart, he has finally beaten Doc Silver in his third attempt to win the WFW World Heavyweight Title. Doc is a hell of a competitor though, if his knee hadn’t buckled, he would have beaten out that ten count.
VIC WATERS: We are living in a C.O.C.K.S./Viewer Choice world now, the world we knew a few short hours ago is gone.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart has the microphone! SHAWN HART: Diablo, you've helped me get where I am today and I truly appreciate that. That is why I've decided that I'm going to give you the BAD World Heavyweight Title I won tonight because I want the title to be defended in the manner worth of the BAD World Heavyweight Title, hardcore style! Tonight in your match against Eian, you proved to me that you are worthy of defending this title for me. Wear it well.
::Shawn Hart & Diablo hug:: BRANDON JACOBS: You can't just hand a title to someone? Can you? VIC WATERS: Dispicable, but Diablo is undefeated and is in the mold of all of those old wrestlers of BAD's past. I don't have too much of a problem with it. Poor Manson though, part of the reason he has been seeking Doc's title is that he'd wanted to win the BAD World Heavyweight Title since before it closed down.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson will be able to do it. I wouldn’t count out either Doc Silver or Manson, they will make it back to the top again and C.O.C.K.S. will be overthrown. Trust me. We will see you all in Central Park in New York City for our next show, NYC Unplugged! Thanks for joining us everyone!
VIC WATERS: ::stands up and gives a standing ovation for Doc as attendants help him to the back::
BRANDON JACOBS: ::joins Vic and numerous others in the crowd in the standing ovation:: FTB
