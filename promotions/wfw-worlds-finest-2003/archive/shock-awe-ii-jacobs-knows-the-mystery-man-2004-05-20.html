Shock & Awe II: Jacobs Knows The Mystery Man

20 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 20 May 2004 (BACKSTAGE EARLIER IN THE SHOW) (CUT TO: Scotty Michaels, entering the arena... Brandon Jacobs catches up with him..) BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty, Scotty, can I have a word? We weren't even sure you were going to show up because we barely heard from you since this card's been announced... are you afraid of what could happen tonight against...
(Scotty grabs the mic out of Jacobs' hand...) SCOTTY MICHAELS: Afraid? Never. There's been a lot of things going on lately in my life, and I'm sorry if I didn't have time to address this match to its fullest extent, like everyone else and their brother. I'm not happy, Brandon... not at all. Why in the HELL would they put me in their with Iron Lion as a partner? My tag team partner is Bartleby. To many, he's unwanted, not necessary, not needed, but he "prefers not to leave." But, I'll deal with it on my own terms. I've told Anarky, that I'm taking back what's mine, and it starts tonight...
(Scotty shoves the mic back into Jacobs' chest..) BRANDON JACOBS: What the hell is he talking about? Bartleby? (RING ANNOUNCERS) VIC WATERS: Do you have any idea who Bartleby is? BRANDON JACOBS: ::takes a sip of Vic Waters’ Poland Spring Water:: I think I do, I was walking by Michaels’ dressing room really early and I thought I saw something peculiar. But I really shouldn’t say…
VIC WATERS: Everyone at home wants to know who it is, come on, spill the beans. LOVE may even induct you as an honorary member.
BRANDON JACOBS: It isn’t my place to give away any secrets… when it is found out, it will be found out.
::Michaels and Bartleby walk out through the curtain and run towards the ring announcers:: VIC WATERS: BRANDON, TURN AROUND! ::Vic jumps over the desk, reaching back for his water bottle::
BRANDON JACOBS: Oh crap! SECURITY! SECURITY! MARX! WINK! MANSON! ANYONE! ::Michaels and Bartleby stand behind Jacobs, Michaels puts his hand around Jacobs throat:: SCOTTY MICHAELS: Did you really think that we were going to let you tell our little secret on the air?
BRANDON JACOBS: ::gasping for breath:: I don’t know who Bartleby is… honest. I was just making it up. LET ME OUT!
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Why do I have the feeling that I can’t believe you? ::Michaels picks Brandon Jacobs up in a double arm choke:: BRANDON JACOBS: ::in a weak voice:: Boy Scout’s honor! ::Michaels double arm chokeslams Brandon Jacobs into the ring table, Jacobs hits his head on one of the TV monitors::
SCOTTY MICHAELS: I never liked boy scouts. ::Marx, Wink, Manson all run out with ring official down to ringside and Michaels and Bartleby bolt through the crowd::
::Chris Wink picks up the microphone:: CHRIS WINK: (with mic in hand) YOU TWO MOTHER****ERS ARE BANNED UNTIL JACOBS CAN ANNOUNCE AGAIN AND IF YOU EVER DO ANYTHING LIKE THIS AGAIN, I’LL FIRE BOTH YOUR ASSES! The announcers are off limits, they are only here to call matches! You god damn ***** wimps, pick on someone your own damn size.
JONATHAN MARX: ::at Jacobs’s side:: WINK, WE ARE GOING TO NEED SOME PARAMEDICS, JACOBS CAN’T FEEL ANYTHING! ****…
::the paramedics rush down to ringside to help Jacobs:: CHRIS WINK: (with mic in hand) I’m sorry, but we are going to have to take an extended commercial break while Jacobs gets ambulanced out, when we comeback, Iron Lion and someone is going to be taking on Anarky & Wells.
::Chris Winks looks sullen down at Jacobs who is lying on the piece of the table below:: JONATHAN MARX: BASTARDS! FTB
