WFW: Spirit of NGEN: Rat Fink vs. Felix Red

18 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 18 August 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back and the next match scheduled up is Rat Fink taking on daredevil sensation Felix Red. Both of these men have been the center of some controversy lately.
VIC WATERS: C.O.C.K.S. actually had the balls to interfere and both handcuff Doc Silver and Rat Fink on WFW’s last show and Manson, after being beaten down earlier in the night, came down to make the save. Before they stuck their nose in things, Rat Fink was this close to winning the title from Doc.
BRANDON JACOBS: You would think after all that Rat Fink has done to Manson that Hart & Diablo would know better to keep their nose out of Rat Fink’s business but some people are slow learners. Rat Fink has one of the most evil and dastardly minds in all of WFW and to interfere in his first shot at the WFW World Heavyweight title, well, Hart & Diablo are going to pay.
VIC WATERS: This should be a good fight, the fans have really taken a liking to Felix Red and he could his aerial skills to overcome the power of Rat Fink. These two are pretty evenly matched in the ring, but Rat Fink makes up for it in the evil department.
BRANDON JACOBS: I think this will be an extremely close battle too. Let’s send this one to ringside!
::: Cue up: "Lux Aeterna" by Clint Mansell and the Kronus Quartet ::: RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, Making his way to the ring, weighing in at 213 pounds and standing at 6 feet, three inches, “The Boy Who Destroyed The World”, FELIX RED!
::Felix Red comes down to the ring to the roar of the crowd:: (CUEUP: Megalomaniac by KMFDM) ::Rat Fink comes down to a mixed reaction from the crowd with his cheesy looking Sci-Fi Robot and enters the ring::
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink looks all fired up and ready to go… RAT FINK CHARGES FELIX RED BUT FELIX RED MOVES OUT OF THE WAY! Rat Fink charges again…. Felix Red continues to avoid contact with Rat Fink!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red is dancing around trying to avoid contact with the rived up Rat Fink which may be a good strategy because Rat Fink looks awfully angry.
BRANDON JACOBS: An infuriated Rat Fink puts his head down and offers Felix Red a headlock… Felix Red looks a little resistant… FELIX RED RUSHES IN AND RAKES THE EYES OF RAT FINK! FELIX RED NAILS RAT FINK WITH A DROPKICK INTO THE CORNER!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red gets so much air on those dropkicks. I’ve never seen anyone like him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red starts chopping away at the chest of Rat Fink… RAT FINK IS WINCING IN PAIN! These shots have been brutal! FELIX RED JUMPS UP AND NAILS HIM WITH A SPINNING HEEL KICK TO THE FACE! Rat Fink slumps down in the corner!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red is quite deceptive for his size and those kicks are the quality of a martial artist.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red drags Rat Fink to the center of the ring… bodyslam! FELIX RED JUMPS UP AND NAILS HIM WITH A TWISTING LEGDROP! Felix Red makes the cover! 1-----------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink kicked out of that with authority! Felix Red nearly flew off! BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red picks up Rat Fink by the hair… FELIX RED GOES FOR THE SPINNING BACK KICK BUT RAT FINK DUCKS! OH…. RAT FINK CHARGES AND TURNS FELIX INSIDE OUT WITH A STEINER LINE!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink has the best damn clothesline in all of the WFW. When that hits you, it is like running into a brick wall.
BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK JUMPS ON FELIX RED AND NAILS HIM WITH CLOSED RIGHT HANDS TO THE HEAD! The fans are booing Rat Fink for beating the snot out of Felix Red! The ref is trying to get Rat Fink off!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red has been playing mind games with Rat Fink all match. That is what he gets for trying to one up someone as great as Rat Fink.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink stops punching and looks at the ref with an evil eye… AND HE CONTINUES PUNCHING ON FELIX RED! The ref grabs Rat Fink and tries to pull him off…
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink needs to becareful here, there is nothing wrong breaking the rules, but you need to becareful not to get disqualified.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink finally stops the punching and picks Rat Fink off the mat…. RAT FINK LIFTS FELIX RED UP… STALL SUPLEX… HE IS HOLDING FELIX RED UP THERE FOR ALL TO SEE… AND HE DROPS HIM DOWN! Rat Fink makes the cover! 1------------------2-------------KICKOUT! Felix Red barely got out of that one!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink needs to work more on Red’s legs if he wants to take him out of the match. Beating the hell out of Felix Red is fine but if he can overcome the punishment, he is still a threat.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink pulls Felix Red toward the corner… Rat Fink slids out of the ring and grabs a hold of Felix Red’s legs… Rat Fink must have been listening to you because he is about to crotch Felix Red…. OH MAN! FELIX RED IS SINGING SOPRANO AFTER THAT!
VIC WATERS: Tony Soprano? BRANDON JACOBS: Quiet you. Rat Fink grabs the leg… HE RAMS THE INSIDE RIGHT LEG OF FELIX RED RIGHT INTO THE POST! Felix Red is screaming in pain!
VIC WATERS: The ring posts which support the ring are very unforgiving. They are like a legal foreign object within the match so you have to do you best to use what they give you to your advantage.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink grabs the legs of Felix Red… FIGURE FOUR AROUND THE RINGPOST! FELIX RED IS SCREAMING IN PAIN AND THE REF IS COMING OUT TO TRY TO GET RAT FINK TO BREAK IT!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink and the ref have been going back and forth at each other the whole match. The ref needs to take a chill pill and relax and let the two men fight.
BRANDON JACOBS: Chill pill? Rat Fink is finally letting go of the figure four…. RAT FINK IS GOING TO GET A CHAIR BUT THE REF RUNS IN FRONT OF RAT FINK TO KEEP HIM FROM GETTING THERE!
VIC WATERS: Let them fight, the people want to see a good brawl! BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is slowly getting up but he is hopping around on his left leg, he climbs out onto the apron… RAT FINK PUSHES THE REF DOWN! Felix Red calls to Rat Fink… RAT FINK TURNS AROUND…. FELIX RED JUMPS OFF THE APRON ON ONE FOOT AND NAILS RAT FINK WITH A TORNADO DDT!
VIC WATERS: HOLY COW! ::BAD KICKS ASS chant erupts from the crowd:: BRANDON JACOBS: Both men are laid out on the floor! Felix Red is slowly getting up on his good left leg and he climbs up onto the ring steps… Rat Fink is slowly getting up…. MOONSAULT INTO A DDT BY FELIX RED! Felix Red gets up and limps over to table… Rat Fink is out cold! FELIX RED IS GOING FOR THE CHAIR! The ref is getting up…. He is warning Felix Red to put the chair down!
VIC WATERS: Finally, the ref is stepping in and giving Rat Fink a fair shake for once. BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second…. FINK BOT JUMPS ONTO FELIX RED TO TRY TO GET HIM TO PUT DOWN THE CHAIR! FELIX RED SWINGS THE CHAIR AND NAILS FINK BOT ON THE TOP OF THE HEAD AND KNOCKS HIM OFF! The ref grabs the chair away Felix Red!
VIC WATER: Fink Bot is the man stepping in like he did. He may get the new tinfoil he wants for Christmas at this rate.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink is getting up… Felix Red is still distracted with Fink Bot and the ref is holding the chair…. RAT FINK RUNS INTO THE BACK OF THE REF WITH A SHOULDER BLOCK AND THE CHAIR GOES FACE FIRST INTO THE HEAD OF FELIX RED! OH MY GOD!
VIC WATERS: Now that is the proper usage of a referee! BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink grabs the groggy Felix Red and rolls him under the bottom rope! Rat Fink is calling for a ref! What balls!
VIC WATERS: All referees used by he WFW are 100% biodegradable. BRANDON JACOBS: FINALLY, A SECOND REFEREE IS COMING DOWN TO RINGSIDE! Rat Fink makes a cover! 1------------------2---------------KICKOUT! Rat Fink is complaining about a slow count!
VIC WATERS: Looks like this referee isn’t any better than the last. BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED WITH A ROLL UP! 1------2-----KICKOUT BY RAT FINK! VIC WATERS: That referee blatantly fast counted Rat Fink! BRANDON JACOBS: Sure seemed like it to me. Rat Fink gets up…. FELIX RED NAILS RAT FINK WITH A SPINNING KICK WITH HIS GOOD LEFT LEG TO SIDE OF THE HEAD! Rat Fink drops to one knee…. FELIX RED NAILS RAT FINK WITH A SHINNING WIZARD! Rat Fink falls like a redwood in the forest down to the mat!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink really got clocked by that knee to the head. BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED TURNS RAT FINK OVER… HE IS CALLING FOR A SHATTERED HORIZON TWISTING SWANTON BOMB! He is going to the top rope… Rat Fink slowly waking up… HE FLIES OFF AND HE NAILS IT! Felix Red makes the cover!
VIC WATERS: LOOK! His foot is under the bottom rope! BRANDON JACOBS: THE REF MAKES THE COUNT! 1-------------------------2------------------------3! The ref is calling for the bell! Felix Red pulls Rat Fink away from the ropes.
RING ANNOUNCER: THE WINNER OF THE MATCH…. FELIX RED! VIC WATERS: Rat Fink was robbed there. That is the second match in a row where the refs have screwed him over, even though Doc would have made a miraculous comeback to win at Paradise Lost to win.
BRANDON JACOBS: Both men put on an amazing performance, it is a shame that the match had to end like that but sometimes the breaks don’t always go your way. Felix Red was very courageous fighting back after his left leg was injured after that figure four around the ring post earlier. Lesser men would have given up.
VIC WATERS: The most amazing part of that was that Felix Red showed that he could adapt his usual style in order to win and such much of his offense depends on his legs.
BRANDON JACOBS: We are down to one last match before we get to the title matches, Sean Edmunds will make his WFW debut against one of the brightest young stars in all of wrestling today, Richard Gideon!
VIC WATERS: Richard Gideon has come a hair shy away from winning the title before, it is only a matter of time before he is a champion here in World’s Finest Wrestling. Anyone who took it to Doc the way he did is a true champion.
BRANDON JACOBS: I definitely agree with that, we will be back with the match, right after this!
