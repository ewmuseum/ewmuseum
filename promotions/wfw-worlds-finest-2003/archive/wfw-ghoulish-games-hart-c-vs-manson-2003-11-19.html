WFW Ghoulish Games - Hart (c) vs. Manson

19 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 19 November 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: It is time for the main event and this crowd is a buzz after Gideon finally broke through and won the WFW North American Title, now the question is if Manson can follow Gideon’s lead and beat Shawn Hart tonight.
VIC WATERS: I really have to take issue with what Manson said about Hart, saying that he does not deserve to be champion and that he is only champion by default. Hart has worked his ass off to get to the top; he had to battle Doc Silver three times to get that strap. He may be the SOB who rid Doc Silver from the WFW and he has used C.O.C.K.S. to his full advantage, but I respect him. Shawn Hart is one of the corner stones of this league and just because a person doesn’t suck up to the fans or takes liberties with the rules, doesn’t mean his credibility should be dismissed. The WFW World Heavyweight Title is the greatest in all of the land and you need to beg, borrow, and steal to get it. If you want to knock Shawn Hart integrity, fine…. But the man deserves to wear that strap around his waist. He has been a cornerstone of the league since inception when he stood up and got the job done when others could not and others disappointed.
BRANDON JACOBS: Who has worked as much as Manson has to get to this point? While Manson may have overstated the fact, Manson took the WFW North American Title and established it as the title of such high esteem as Richard Gideon wears around his waist today. He had to fight the likes of Rat Fink and Scotty Michaels in order to keep the WFW North American Title around his waist and those two were willing to do anything to get the title away from him. Manson has been through hell and back, hell, what Michaels and Luster did to him in the cage may go down as the greatest screwjob of alltime. With all the abuse Manson has taken, Manson is almost a Christ like figure. Manson is just frustrated. He hasn’t held a heavyweight title since January when he lost to Maelstrom.
VIC WATERS: They say when a man is cornered and he has all the odds against him, that is when you find out the real substance of a man. If Shawn Hart wants to win tonight, he will not only have to beat Manson, but he will have to do it right here in Chicago. This is Shawn Hart’s greatest challenge to date and possibly his great challenge ever. If Shawn Hart wins this match, Manson and people like Manson won’t be able to make remarks about him being less than champion. In a way, this match means more to Hart than Manson because it could define his legacy as WFW World Champ.
BRANDON JACOBS: That just speaks to greatness of Manson. I have a feeling that tonight, after nearly a year in waiting, Manson will finally achieve his dream of being WFW World Heavyweight Champion. It is never easy to beat the champ and I’m not saying Hart won’t give him his best, but Manson wants to win the title in front of his home town crowd and send them home happy. How many opportunities do you have to win the World Heavyweight Title in front of your hometown crowd? Chicago has suffered through a lot of trouble recently, this would mean a lot to the people to see Manson win tonight.
VIC WATERS: Chicago has the Bears, Cubs, and Bulls…. Okay, you are right. BRANDON JACOBS: This is shaping up to be a magical evening, let’s send this down to ringside and find out if this PPV will end with tragedy or a storybook ending….
(CUEUP: "Mechanical Animals" by Marilyn Manson) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the former WFW North American Champion and Chicago’s own native son…. MICHAEL MANSON!
::Manson is walking down slowly to the ring to a huge pop and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: "Look At Me" by Geri Haliwell) RING ANNOUNCER: And now, the champion, from Orlando, Florida, the leader of C.O.C.K.S. and the WFW World Heavyweight of the World, “THE PHENOM” SHAWN HART!
::Shawn Hart walks down to the ring to the boos from the crowd and enters the ring:: ::the referee hands the title to the special timekeeper of the match Steve Bartman who promptly gets booed::
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart charges Manson and starts firing away with discus styled punches and Manson is taken aback at the aggressiveness of Hart! HART CLOTHESLINE MANSON OVER THE TOP ROPE! This battle is being taken to the outside!
VIC WATERS: There is a Chicago Street Fight so they can battle wherever they want for as long as they want, but they have to comeback to ring in order to get the pin.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart goes through the ropes…. HART WHIPS MANSON INTO THE GUARDRAIL AND MANSON GOES IN BACK FIRST! Hart charges Manson… HART GOES FOR A SPLASH BUT MANSON DUCKS DOWN AND HART GOES FLYING OVER THE GUARDRAIL INTO THE FRONT ROW OF SEATS!
VIC WATERS: Hart really took a nasty spill. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson steps over the guardrail and he takes a purse from an old lady…. Hart is trying to get up…. MANSON NAILS HIM WITH THE PURSE AND HART GOES FLYING BACK INTO SOME CHAIRS! Hart tries to get up…
VIC WATERS: Even all the little old ladies are on Manson’s side, Manson is among his people… Hart needs to get the hell out of there.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson grabs Hart by the hair and he leads him up the stairs… MANSON RAMS HART’S HEAD DOWN INTO THE CEMENT STAIRS! Manson goes to pick him up…. HART FIRES AND ELBOW BACK AND MANSON FALLS DOWN THREE OR FOUR STAIRS BEFORE THE FANS KEEP HIM FROM FALLING ANY FURTHER!
VIC WATERS: These damn fans should mind their own business. These two men are competing for the World Title for Christ sake. This wouldn’t be allowed in other sports.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is heading up the stairs and he reaches the top…. HART IS HEADING INTO THE HEART OF THE UNITED CENTER! Manson is getting up and he is heading after Hart, it looks like he hurt his back slightly when he fell back.
VIC WATERS: Manson needs to becareful what he is rushing into… BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON WALKS INTO THE ENTRANCE WAY AND HART COMES OUT OF NOWHERE AND NAILS HIM WITH A GARBAGE CONTAINER RIGHT IN THE HEAD! Manson goes down!
VIC WATERS While the container wasn’t metal, that is certainly going to slow Manson down. BRANDON JACOBS: Hart picks up Manson from underneath…. TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER ONTO THE CEMENT FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: That is going to send a jolt down his spine. BRANDON JACOBS: Hart walks over to the merchandise table and grabs a Manson t-shirt…. HART WRAPS THE SHIRT AROUND THE NECK OF MANSON AND BEGINS TO TRY TO CHOKE HIM OUT! Manson is gasping for breath!
VIC WATERS: Hart with the ironic product placement… BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is trying to fight his way up with Hart on his back choking him out…. MANSON IS GETTING UP AND HE DUMPS HART DOWN ON THE GROUND! Manson grabs Hart…. HE THROWS
HART OVER THE MERCHANDISE TABLE AND IT TIPS OVER AS HE GOES OVER THE SIDE! VIC WATERS: These guys better not bleed all over it. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson lifts the table back up and picks Hart up off the ground…. MANSON CLEARS OFF THE REMAIN MERCHANDISE AND PICKS HART UP ONTO THE TABLE! Manson sets his feet…. WILD BUCK BOMB THROUGH THE TABLE! Hart has been busted open!
CROWD: MANSON! MANSON! MANSON! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson slowly gets up as he yells for the fans to make room…Manson grabs Hart and he is heading for the ramp down to the parking lot! MANSON GRABS THE HART OF HAIR AND WHIPS HIM DOWN THE RAMP AND HE TUMBLES DOWN TO THE GROUND!
VIC WATERS: This is the last platform before the ram down to the bottom. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson picks up a garbage container and lays it down on the ground…. Manson walks over to Hart…. HE PICKS HIM UP AND SNAP SUPLEXES HART BACK RIGHT INTO THE GARBAGE CONTAINER! Hart reaches back and grabs his back.
VIC WATERS: If Hart survives this match, he is going to be awfully beat up for awhile. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON THROWS HART DOWN ANOTHER RAMP AND HARTS ROLLS DOWN ON TO THE CEMENT! Manson sees the parked cars and he grabs Hart and throws him into the parking lot!
VIC WATERS: This is a graveyard of metal and glass, a man can do an awful lot of damage in a parking lot.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson picks up Shawn Hart off the ground…. GERMAN RELEASE SUPLEX ONTO THE HOOD OF A BUICK! Manson climbs onto the car…. MANSON HURACANRANAS HART OFF THE CAR ONTO THE CEMENT!
VIC WATERS: Hart’s time as champion is running out. BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second, a car is putting up to Manson and Hart blasting its music… SOMEBODY GET HIM OUT OF THERE! There is a match going on!
VIC WATERS: God damn security… BRANDON JACOBS: Manson walks over to the car…. THE DOOR OPENS QUICKLY AND IT GOES RIGHT INTO THE MIDSECTION OF MANSON!
VIC WATERS: THAT IS TERRELL MASON! FORMER SDW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMP! BRANDON JACOBS: Terrell Mason fireman carrys Manson and nails him with a flatliner onto the concrete! MANSON IS BUSTED OPEN!
VIC WATERS: Terrell Mason calls that move the Clap Back. BRANDON JACOBS: Whatever it is, it is damn effective. Terrell Mason is getting back into his car and he is leaving the building. I’d run too because if Manson ever wakes up, Mason is going to get his ass punked.
VIC WATERS: Or worse.. BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is starting to move… but he looks completely out of it. Hart picks Manson up…. HE NAILS HIM WITH A SUPERKICK AND THEN COLLAPSES DOWN ON THE GROUND HIMSELF!
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart has taken so much punishment, if he wins this one, it will be a damn miracle.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart grabs Manson and they are headed for the player’s entrance… SHAWN HART RAMS MANSON HEAD FIRST INTO THE DOOR!
VIC WATERS: Hart knows that the only way he can end this match is to get it back in the ring.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart opens the door and leads Manson by the hair through the backstage area…. WAIT A SECOND, MANSON HAS REACHED OUT AND GRABBED ONE OF THE LIGHTS FOR BACKSTAGE INTERVIEWS! Hart doesn’t see it…. MANSON BLIND SIDES HIM FROM BEHIND WITH THE LIGHT RIGHT IN THE HEAD!
VIC WATERS: DAMN! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is grabbing a trunk! Manson picks up Hart…. HE SLAMS HIM INTO TRUNK! Manson goes for the catering table and he grabs a jug of Gatorade and HE GIVES HART A GATORADE SHOWER!
VIC WATERS: Bears fans haven’t seen that in awhile. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON CLOSES THE TRUNK AND HE DRAGS THE TRUNK THROUGH THE CURTAIN AND HE IS HEADING BACK DOWN THE ENTRANCE WAY!
CROWD: MANSON! MANSON! MANSON! BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES SCOTTY MICHAELS! Scotty Michaels runs out and grabs the other handle of the trunk! MICHAELS AND MANSON ARE HAVING A TUG OF WAR OVER THE BODY OF HART!
VIC WATERS: I don’t know who Hart wants to win this one… BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels bad leg gives out and Manson loses his balance and Hart tumbles out of the trunk! Hart is on all fours and he is crawling back to the ring.
VIC WATERS: The further away from these two mad men, the better. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels gets up and he goes to pickup Manson who fell back awkwardly in the struggle… BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX BY MICHAELS!
VIC WATERS: This whole thing has gotten out of control, first Manson gets attacked by Terrell Mason and then he gets attacked by Scotty Michaels.
BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels is ripping up the mats at ringside… SCOTTY MICHAELS NAILS MANSON WITH A BRAINBUSTER RIGHT ONTO THE CONCRETE FLOOR! The fans have begun throwing garbage onto Michaels!
VIC WATERS: This could get really ugly, I’m going to tell my driver to get the car running.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart has finally made it back into the ring…. Hart is just sitting in the corner trying to get some rest. Michaels rolls Manson into the ring for Hart…
VIC WATERS: This is the first time both men have been in the ring in over twenty minutes now.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart walks over to the lifeless body of Manson…. Hart picks Manson up…. HART ATTACK! Hart makes the cover! 1-----------------------------------2---------------------KICKOUT!
CROWD: MANSON! MANSON! MANSON! VIC WATERS: I have no idea how Manson kicked out of that. BRANDON JACOBS: Hart throws his hands on his head in disgust… Hart is calling for Michaels to throw in a chair and Michaels throws in a chair to Hart!
VIC WATERS: Manson is trying to get up… BRANDON JACOBS: Hart winds up with the chair…. HE NAILS MANSON RIGHT IN THE HEAD! Manson starts to sway…. BUT HE DOESN’T GO DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Manson is inhuman… BRANDON JACOBS: HART NAILS HIM ACROSS THE HEAD WITH THE CHAIR AGAIN! Manson stumbles back and forth a couple of steps…. BUT HE IS STILL STANDING!
CROWD: MANSON! MANSON! MANSON! BRANDON JACOBS: Hart bounces off the ropes with the chair…. HE NAILS HIM AGAIN BUT MANSON STANDS PERFECTLY STILL AND LOOKS HART IN THE EYE!
VIC WATERS: ::gulp:: BRANDON JACOBS: Hart bounces off the ropes… HART GOES FOR AN EL CODO EXPLOSIVO BUT MANSON SPITS THE YELLOW MIST IN THE EYES OF HART! Manson small packages Hart! 1----------------------------------2--------------------- KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Hart has been blinded! He won’t be able to last long now. BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels runs into the ring to help Hart…. MANSON NAILS HIM WITH AN ELBOW TO THE FACE! Michaels tries to get up…. HE NAILS HIM AGAIN! Manson picks Michaels off the mat…. SWEET DREAMS!
VIC WATERS: Goodnight Scotty Michaels… BRANDON JACOBS: Manson rolls Michaels out and yells at him for interfering in his match…. Hart backs his way towards the corner… HE IS SCREAMING FOR SOMEONE TO GIVE HIM SOME WATER TO CLEAR OUT HIS EYES! Steve Bartman runs over to Hart and hands him his water and Hart dumps the water all over his face in an attempt to get the poisoned mist out of his eyes!
VIC WATERS: Thank god for Steve Bartman. BRANDON JACOBS: Hart gets up and he is pretending that he is still blinded… MANSON TURNS AROUND… HE GRABS HART IN A FRONT FACE LOCK FOR A DDT…. HART BACK BODY DROPS MANSON!
VIC WATERS: Manson only took his eye off the ball for a second… BRANDON JACOBS: Manson begins to get up…. HART BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND NAILS MANSON WITH AN EL CODO EXPLOSIVO! Manson goes down! Hart goes down for the cover! 1------------------------------------2----------------------------3! HART DID IT!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. AND STILL WFW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION…. “THE PHENOM” SHAWN HART!
BRANDON JACOBS: Holy cow, he did it! Diablo and Tiny are coming out to help Shawn Hart to the back…
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart did what three chair shots to the head couldn’t with the El Codo Explosivo.
BRANDON JACOBS: Both men put out a tremendous effort tonight, if it wasn’t for Scotty Michaels, Terrell Mason and Steve Bartman, Manson would be the champion today. WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES ANARKY!
VIC WATERS: Anarky and Manson were aligned together in GLCW. BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky reaches out his hand to help Manson up off the mat… Manson takes his hand…. ANARKY RAISES MANSON’S HAND UP IN THE AIR!
CROWD: MANSON! MANSON! MANSON! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson may not have won the title tonight, but we know who these fans think is the champion…. Folks, we would like to thank you for joining us tonight, tonight was a historic night… Richard Gideon finally broke through and won the WFW North American Title, we saw Copycat and El Arco Iris get their revenge against Luster and Edmunds, Anarky and Ultimo Diablo put on a show in the graveyard and Anarky showed why he is one of the best wrestlers in wrestling today, and Shawn Hart pulled off the impossible and beat Manson dead center of the ring to retain his WFW World Heavyweight Title and live to fight another day… from all of us in Chicago…
VIC WATERS: LOOK IN THE AISLE, THINGS AREN’T OVER YET! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson and Anarky are brawling their way to the back! From what we are being told, Anarky made a snide remark to Manson, to which Manson yelled at Anarky, Anarky pushed Manson and Manson pushed back, from there, both men started brawling towards the back. Officials are trying to break up both men, we will have more on what happened later on.
VIC WATERS: But things are just starting to get good… BRANDON JACOBS: Our next show will be a Thanksgiving Celebration in Leyyard, Connecticut with the our Native American pals at the Foxwoods Resort Casino! Thanks for joining us and goodnight everyone….
