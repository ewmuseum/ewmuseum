WFW Great Expectations: Gideon (c) vs. Copycat vs. Psycho

24 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 24 February 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome back everyone and it is now time for the WFW North American Title match. The WFW North American Title match is usually second from the top on WFW PPVs, however we have been informed that because of the fact that it will take awhile setup and deconstruct of Exploding Barbed Wire Deathmatch, Anarky vs. Mephisto will be second from the top.
VIC WATERS: Psycho and Richard Gideon both want a piece of each other. I know that the next match is a barbed wire exploding death match, but this match could wind up being even more violent than that one. If Copycat plays his cards right, he could simply pick off the winner.
BRANDON JACOBS: Both men have sort of been underestimating the champ, calling him a paper tiger. It is almost like Gideon has something to prove tonight going in there with two former World Heavyweight Champions.
VIC WATERS: In this business, you have to prove it in the ring. But even if Gideon does put on a tremendous performance tonight, anything can happen in a three way match. He doesn’t have to take the fall to lose his WFW North American World Title. Gideon could simply turn his back and Psycho could pin Copycat to win. A three way match is very hard to win.
BRANDON JACOBS: Very true, because not only do you have to beat one man down enough to get a pinfall, you have to incapacitate the other and make sure that they can’t break up the pinfall. You know, Copycat could be in trouble too. We all saw that masked man lurking about who took out El Arco Iris. It only figures that they might go after Copycat as well.
VIC WATERS: The masked man already struck once. What are the odds he would strike twice in one night? Sure, if you are Copycat, you have to have that in the back of your mind, but it is no longer a surprise. Besides, if you disliked Copycat, wouldn’t you let him win the WFW North American Title and then attack him and steal his title away later on?
BRANDON JACOBS: I could see that. VIC WATERS: Then you have Psycho who wants to taste WFW gold once again and he could do it at the expense of Richard Gideon who attacked him at Mind Over Matter after what Psycho did to him on the cage. Psycho’s greatest opponent tonight though will be himself because he will have to keep his rage focused in order to not become too consumed with Gideon.
BRANDON JACOBS: This should be interesting to see how it pans out. Let’s send this one to ringside and get this match underway!
(CUEUP: "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first of the opposing team,the former WFW World Heavyweight Champion, PSYCHO!
::Psycho comes down to a mixed reaction from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: Copycat voice/over “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, LEND ME YOUR EARS!” followed by “My Way” by Butch Walker) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing second, the former WWL World Heavyweight Champion, from Kalamazoo, Michigan… COPYCAT!
::Copycat walks down to the ring with microphone in hand and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “Remember” by Disturbed) RING ANNOUCER: And now, the champion, from Buffalo, NY, THE WFW North American Champion…. THE SYSTEM RICHARD GIDEON!
::Gideon comes down to the ring to the roar of the crowd and enters the ring:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon and Psycho are staring at each other like a couple of animals ready to go for the kill as Copycat goes over to corner and sits on the top turnbuckle. GIDEON AND PSYCHO BEGIN EXCHANGING CHOPS BACK AND FORTH AND YOU CAN HEAR THE OUT ALL THE WAY OUT TO THE VALLEY!
VIC WATERS: These two men who were once good friends absolutely hate each other now. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is sitting in the corner laughing to himself…. Gideon and Psycho stop chopping each other…. PSYCHO AND GIDEON RUN OVER TO THE CORNER AND DOUBLE HIPTOSS COPYCAT DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: I guess they didn’t want Copycat to feel unwelcome. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon and Psycho bounce off the ropes…. DROPKICKS FROM BOTH SIDES TO COPYCAT! Gideon goes for the cover! GIDEON GRABS HIM BY THE HAIR AND GETS INTO HIS FACE…
VIC WATERS: I didn’t think the teamwork would last long. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon and Psycho beginning chopping each other again and this time Gideon is getting the advantage! Psycho is being back into the corner… GIDEON BEGINS REPEATEDLY RAMMING HIS SHOULDER INTO THE MIDSECTION OF PSYCHO!
VIC WATERS: A lot of Psycho’s offense requires him to have the energy to pull off all these high flying and high impact moves, but taking his air away, this could hurt him later on in the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon pulls the winded Psycho away from the corner…. GIDEON PICKS PSYCHO UP…. BRAINBUSTER SUPLEX….. HE LIFTS HIM UP A SECOND TIME…… HE HITS THE SECOND ONE! A winded Gideon picks up Psycho for a third one….. G-FORCE!
VIC WATERS: Psycho really took a shellacking there. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon stands over Psycho cursing him out… COPYCAT IS UP AND HE CLOTHESLINES GIDEON FROM BEHIND! Both Psycho and Gideon are down and Copycat is standing tall. Copycat position both bodies right next to each other.
VIC WATERS: Maybe he is going to pin both of them. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat bounces off the ropes…. DOUBLE SPLASH! Copycat covers! 1---------------------------------------------2--------------------BOTH MEN KICK OUT!
VIC WATERS: If he had pulled that off, that would be an amazing win. BRANDON JACOBS:Copycat picks up Gideon…. OVER THE HEAD BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX AND GIDEON CRASHES DOWN HARD ON THE MAT! Copycat goes to pick up Psycho… HE LIFTS HIM UP OVER HIS HEAD…. PRESS SLAM DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Copycat is exploding on these two. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat positions Psycho…. COPYCAT BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND HITS THE PEOPLE’S ELBOW ON PSYCHO! Copycat makes the cover! 1---------------------------------2------------------------- GIDEON DIVES AND BREAKS UP THE PIN!
VIC WATERS: Thank god Gideon did that because Psycho looks out of it. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat picks up Gideon and COPYCAT PUSHES GIDEON IN THE CORNER! Copycat starts wailing away with forearms on Gideon like Vader use to.
VIC WATERS: Copycat is really beating the crap out of Gideon! BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat waistlocks Gideon…. RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX GIDEON OUT OF THE CORNER!
VIC WATERS: Nobody delivers a suplex like Copycat does. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat positions Gideon…. COPYCAT IS GOING OVER TO THE CORNER AND HE IS CLIMBING UP TO THE TOP ROPE! Psycho gets up and he darts over to the corner…. PSYCHO THROWS COPYCAT OFF THE TOP!
VIC WATERS: That is the second time today that has happened. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho bounces off the ropes as Copycat staggers up to his feet…. YAZUKA KICK RIGHT IN THE FACE! Copycat falls down to the mat!
VIC WATERS: TIMBER! BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho goes to the turnbuckle and starts climbing his way to the top…. PSYCHO PERCHES…. STARDUST PRESS 450 SPLASH RIGHT ONTO COPYCAT! Psycho makes he cover! 1---------------------------------------2--------------------- COPYCAT KICKS OUT AND SENDS PSYCHO FALLING THROUGH THE ROPES TO THE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: DAMN! BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat gets up and he rolls out to the outside. COPYCAT GRABS PSYCHO BY THE HAIR AND HE THROWS HIM INTO THE STEPS! Psycho grabs his back and he tries to crawl away.
VIC WATERS: Copycat looks focused on winning the title here tonight. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat goes after him and he grabs Psycho from behind…. COPYCAT RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEXS PSYCHO RIGHT INTO THE GUARDRAIL!
VIC WATERS: OH MY! This is triggering versions of the amount of abuse Doc took against Psycho at Rage in the Cage.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat walks over and he starts stomping away on Psycho as Psycho tries to get up but Copycat won’t let him.
VIC WATERS: Copycat is just laying in those big boots into Psycho. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat picks Psycho up by the hair and he rolls him into the ring. Gideon who is shaking off the effects of the german jumps on top of Psycho and goes for the cover! 1---------------------------------------2---------------------------- COPYCAT DIVES IN AND BREAKS UP THE PINFALL ATTEMPT!
VIC WATERS: He barely got there. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon grabs the arm of Copycat…. SHORT ARM CLOTHESLINE! Gideon goes to cover Psycho! 1-------------------------------2---------------------- PSYCHO KICKS OUT!
VIC WATERS: Too much time passed, Psycho had too much time to recover. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon picks up Psycho and puts him in a bearhug! He is working on the midsection area which he worked over earlier in the match.
VIC WATERS: Gideon is a powerful man. Psycho has to fight his way out of this or he is going to be in serious trouble.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is calling out to Copycat to help him but Copycat is still shaking out the cobwebs from the short arm clothesline. Psycho is trying to synch in the bear hug! Psycho goes go grab the hair of Gideon… BUT GIDEON SQUEEZES EVEN HARD AND PSYCHO’S HANDS RECOIL!
VIC WATERS: Gideon would love nothing better than to dish out some revenge here tonight. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is beginning to get up…. COPYCAT WRAPS HIS ARMS AROUND GIDEON AND PUTS HIM IN A BEAR HUG OF HIS OWN!
VIC WATERS: Gideon needs to let go and try every effort to break out of this! BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT LIFTS UP GIDEON AND IN THE PROCESS LIFTS UP PSYCHO….. COPYCAT RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEXES BOTH OF THEM INTO A MESS OF BODIES ON THE MAT WITH PSYCHO LANDING ON TOP OF GIDEON!
CROWD: COPYCAT! COPYCAT! COPYCAT! VIC WATERS: Good lord. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is trying to get up…. COPYCAT PICKS UP PSYCHO OVER HIS SHOULDER AND LITTERBOMBS HIM ON TOP OF GIDEON BUT HE ACCIDENTLY KNOCKED DOWN THE REFEREE!
VIC WATERS: Gideon is dead… BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat drags the two bodies over towards the corner…. He puts the body of Psycho on top of the body of Gideon and he begins climbing up to the top rope. WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES THE MASKED MAN!
VIC WATERS: This can’t be good. BRANDON JACOBS: THE MASKED MAN PUSHES COPYCAT OFF OF THE TOP TURNBUCKLE AND HE FALLS DOWN ONTO THE GROUND!
VIC WATERS: YES! BRANDON JACOBS: The masked man picks up Copycat… THE MASKED MAN WAILS HIM WITH THE CHAIR BUSTING HIM OPEN! The masked man picks up Gideon…. ANOTHER BIG CHAIR SHOT!
VIC WATERS: That doesn’t look like the same masked man as before. BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES ICEKOLD DOWN TO RINGSIDE AND SHE IN! Psycho is starting to get up! The masked man is going after Icehold…. ICEKOLD PULLS OUT SOME PEPPER SPRAY…. THE MASKED MAN DUCKS AND PSYCHO GETS IT RIGHT IN THE EYES!
VIC WATERS: This is all starting to break down into mass confusion. This damn referee needs to get the hell up before this gets even worse.
BRANDON JACOBS: The Masked Man goes after Copycat…. ICEKOLD CROTCHES THE MASKED MAN AND THE MASKED MEN BENT OVER IN PAIN CHASES ICEKOLD OUT OF THE RING!
VIC WATERS: This is why I always say, a wrestling ring is no place for a woman, who knows what will happen to her if he catches him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat picks up the blinded Psycho…… HE NAILS HIM WITH THE KIT KAT KICK AND HE IS OUT IN THE DEAD CENTER OF THE RING!
VIC WATERS: If the ref would only come too, Copycat would have this match won. Thank god he is out cold.
BRANDON JACOBS: The referee could still wake up and set things right damn it. Copycat goes for the cover but there is no ref! THE MASKED MAN WHO IS CHASING ICEKOLD AROUND THE RING ROLLS BACK IN AND PICKS UP THE CHAIR…. THE MASKED MAN WALLOPS COPYCAT RIGHT IN THE BACK WITH IT!
VIC WATERS: YES! BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, AN ANGRY COPYCAT IS GETTING UP AND HE IS SHAKING OFF THE CHAIR SHOT LIKE IT WAS NOTHING!
VIC WATERS: The masked man is about to die. BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT IS TRYING TO TAKE OFF THE MASK! THE MASKED MAN IS HOLDING ONTO THE MASK FOR DEAR LIFE!
VIC WATERS: I hate Copycat, but I want to know who it is too. BRANDON JACOBS: THE MASKED MAN KICKS HIM IN THE CROWN JEWELS AND COPYCAT FALLS TO HIS FEET!
VIC WATERS: This wouldn’t have happened if he followed Bob Barker’s advice and had himself neutered.
BRANDON JACOBS: The Masked Man picks up the chair…. HE HITS HIM RIGHT OVER THE HEAD WITH IT AND COPYCAT IS DOWN!
VIC WATERS: The masked man is finally leaving with chair in hand and Icekold is trying desperately to revive Copycat.
BRANDON JACOBS: Icekold isn’t going to have much luck. Copycat looks really out of it and the blood is really pouring out of his head. He is going to need some stitch him up later.
VIC WATERS: Better be soon. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is slowly getting up and he sees both Psycho and Copycat have been laid out, Copycat from the chairshots and Psycho from the Kit Kat Kick.
VIC WATERS: He has his choice of victims here. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon goes to wake up the referee who looks like he is finally starting to come to. Gideon picks up the unconscious body of Psycho and pulls it over to the corner… GIDEON CLIMBS TO THE SECOND ROPE AND GIDEON LIFTS PSYCHO UP IN A PILEDRIVER….. GIDEON NAILS THE BUFFALO BOMB!
VIC WATERS: That can’t be good for Psycho’s neck. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon makes the cover and the referee makes the count! 1---------------------------------------------2-------------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. AND STILL WFW NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION….. “THE SYSTEM” RICHARD GIDEON!
VIC WATERS: The complete opposite of what I expected happened tonight, instead of Copycat winning because of the war between Psycho and Gideon, Gideon won because of the war between Psycho, the Masked Man, and Copycat. Gideon is a smart cookie.
BRANDON JACOBS: That he is and that is why he is still our WFW North American Champion. I’m still amazed he survived that bearhug spot which should have killed him but some how, he reached down and mustered that extra effort to get the job done.
VIC WATERS: There were a whole bunch of what ifs in this match, if the Masked Man hadn’t ducked the pepper spray, Psycho might have walked out here victorious. If the Masked Man hadn’t come down to stick his nose in things, Copycat could have pinned Psycho after he was blinded and hit with the Kit Kat Kick instead of Gideon. Sometimes the drive by gets the other guy, sometimes the drive by gets you.
BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, when we comeback, it is finally time for the match that everyone has been waiting for, Anarky will go against Mephisto in a Barbed Wire Exploding Death Match…. The best is yet to come…. STAY TUNED!
