WFW Gates of Hell: McMillan vs. Aitsu Sugawara

7 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 7 May 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Have you ever noticed that right before you go away to do a show, you always get the package you’ve been anticipating but because you can’t get into it because you have to leave to catch a flight?
VIC WATERS: Life is tragically ironic that way. BRANDON JACOBS: It sure is. We are back commercial break and we have the highly anticipated debut of Chris McMillian against Aitsu Sugawara who has been on a losing streak as of late. Let’s send this to ringside!
:: "Suri Suri Masuri" by Lee Hyun Jung blares over the loud speaker:: RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first from Kyoto, Japan, The WP’s World Champion, Aitsu Sugawara!
::Aitsu Sugawara slaps the hands of fans at ringside as Aitsu comes down and enters the ring::
::”Hair of the Dog” by Nazareth explodes over the loud speaker:: RING ANNOUNCER: And Aitsu’s opponent, from Cadillac, MI, “The Wolf” Chris McMillan! ::Chris McMillan comes down to a big pop from the fans:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Chris McMillan rushes Aitsu and nails Aitsu with three or four quick forearms to the face! Aitsu looks temporarily stunned! McMillan bounces off the ropes… HE NAILS AITSU WITH A
FLYING FOREARM! Aitsu goes down to the mat! VIC WATERS: Chris McMillan has had an impressive start, but the match isn’t over yet. BRANDON JACOBS: McMillan goes to the outside of the ring on the apron… SPRINGBOARD SPINNING LEGDROP! McMillan goes for the pinfall! 1------------2---------KICKOUT! Aitsu barely got the shoulder up!
VIC WATERS: Very close there, Aitsu needs to change the momentum or Aitsu will be looking up at the ring lights.
BRANDON JACOBS: McMillan picks up Aitsu… HE LOCKS IN THE HALF NELSON AND SUPLEXS AITSU’S HEAD INTO THE MAT! Aitsu is out of it! McMillian is calling for his finish!
VIC WATERS: If he hits this, it is all over. BRANDON JACOBS: McMillan double underhooks the arms…. DREAD DRIVER (Tiger Driver 91!)! McMillan makes the cover and hooks the leg! The ref goes down for the count! 1-------------2-----------3!!! *crowd pops*
::”Hair of the Dog” by Nazareth plays as the ref raises McMillan's hand in victory:: VIC WATERS: That was an impressive debut for “The Wolf” Chris McMillan. Looks like Doc may finally have a little competition around here for the title.
BRANDON JACOBS: A very impressive debut by the energetic WFW newcomer, his future looks extremely bright. We will take a short commercial break and we will be back with Shawn Hart going one on with Psycho for a shot at the WFW World Heavyweight Title at the PPV!
