BOMB Voyage: #1 Contender Felix Red Speaks

14 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 14 June 2004 JONATHAN MARX: Welcome back everyone, it is time for the six man tag! VIC WATERS: Actually, there has been a schedule change, Edmunds said he was hosting a celebrity luau right after the show and needed to have his match earlier in the show.
JONATHAN MARX: That was suppose to be the semi-main event! Edmunds is just out of control as President rearranging everything thing, I have a good mind to…
CUEUP: “"If Born From This Soil: Treatments For the Injected Foetu" by Minor Procedure and Shai Hulud)
JONATHAN MARX: Wait a second, what is Felix Red doing out here? He doesn’t have interview time scheduled.
::Felix Red comes down to the ring to the loud boos from the crowd and enters the ring, he asks from the microphone from the ring attendant::
FELIX RED: The funny thing is, it wasn’t supposed to be me. It was never supposed to be me. It was supposed to be Anarky. No one saw this com---
CROWD: F*CK YOU FELIX!!!! (CLAP, CLAP, CLAPCLAPCLAP) F*CK YOU FELIX!!!! (CLAP, CLAP, CLAPCLAPCLAP) F*CK YOU FELIX!!!!
FELIX RED: Please, quiet down, I’m trying to say someth---(chant continues) Could you just, for a second, listen???…(chant continues) Please?.....(still chanting) SHUT THE F(Bleep)K UP!!! LISTEN!!! For once in your tiny, insipid, desperate little lives, hear me. I want to communicate something. I want to surpass the static you’ve placed before your eyes to shield you from everything you don’t want.
I’m not supposed to be the number one contender. But I am. Just because something’s meant to be doesn’t mean it will. With enough grit focused into the correct channels, any individual can circumvent destiny. Things only always work out for the best if that’s what you really want. Movie screens, like most things, are just mirrors. You want the Mighty Ducks to win, so they do. You want Charlize Theron to feel guilty about all the people she killed, so she does. Because only ugly people are evil. Because monsters wear skull face paint and masks. They aren’t anything like you.
Except this isn’t a movie. You don’t have any power here. CROWD: LOVE IS GAY!!! LOVE IS GAY!!! LOVE IS GAY!!! Maybe LOVE doesn’t have all the power, but it is awe-fully curious how every single time I turn on my television and watch WFW vignettes, LOVE is mentioned at least once every 45 seconds. My name is spoken at least once every two minutes.
But we’re really not Al Queda to WFW’s America. Wargames wasn’t 9/11. This isn’t about religion. I’d like to believe, if anything, we have relatively few illusions about what’s we’re doing here.
When I become world champion, it wont make WFW a better TV show. In fact, it will erase from the world title, from the company, what little credibility is left. And I’m glad.
In the world I envision, my world, my forever, the greatest thing I could ever do means nothing.
In my world "legendary" is another word for "tragic." In my world, "Richard Gideon" is another word for "cadaver." In my world, eternity abruptly ends again and again and again and again, the beginning is the ending is the beginning is the ending, because The only way I can live without boundaries is if nothing matters. When nothing matters, I can do anything I want…
And that’s the main reason why I’m going to win the world championship. I want to. The other reason is that I can. Manson can’t stop me, Gideon definitely can’t stop me. LOVE could, and maybe Sean Edmunds could, but if either wanted to stop me they would have already. With the Liars Oppressors and Violence Experiment triumphant, vindicated, exuberant, and Sean Edmunds passively wagging a disapproving finger; Your champion, your hero will stumble and fall. And I will embrace everything you don’t want. Everything that’s not supposed to happen. Exactly what I’ve always wanted.
Which probably isn’t what Copycat, El Arco Iris, or Pulsar want. At all. And that’s unfortunate. But ah, well…What’s that old expression about wanting to make an omelette? You do see my point, Don’t you?
::Felix Red throws down the mic and walks away:: JONATHAN MARX: Those are some strong words by Felix Red, whoever wins the title match here tonight is going to have a hell of a battle on their hands.
VIC WATERS: This crowd was very rude to Felix. JONATHAN MARX: He did win his title shot by trying to kill a bunny, folks, we are going to take a short commercial break, and as long as Edmunds doesn’t rearrange the schedule again, SEAN EDMUNDS VERSES LARRY TACT WITH SHERRI BANNED FROM RINGSIDE IS NEXT! Stay tuned!
