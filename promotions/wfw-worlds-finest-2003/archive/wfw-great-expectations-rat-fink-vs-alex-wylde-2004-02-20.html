WFW Great Expectations: Rat Fink vs. Alex Wylde

20 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 20 February 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: Since Turkey Day Terror when Wylde was denied his shot at the WFW World Heavyweight Title, Wylde has been undefeated beating Sean Edmunds, Jared Wells, and Richard Gideon. Tonight though, his road to the WFW World Heavyweight Title doesn’t get any easier as he will have to face the man who single handedly beat Manson and Minion in a Bare Buckles Match at Mind Over Matter.
VIC WATERS: I’m kind of split on which way I should go in this match, since that match at Turkey Day Terror, Wylde has been showing that edge that he has lacked, but Rat Fink has been on a tear lately and we all know how unstoppable Rat Fink can be from his BAD World Heavyweight Title… Hmmm… I think I’ll go with Rat Fink. He’ll still the guy most likely to take over a third world country.
BRANDON JACOBS: That Rat Fink is a charmer he is. VIC WATERS: He makes Saddam Hussein look like a choir boy. BRANDON JACOBS: Well, you certainly know what choir boys look like. But I’m going to go with Wylde, he knows how important it is to keep the momentum going into his match WFW World Heavyweight Title match with either Michaels or Edmunds because momentum is so hard to regain once you have lost it. Wylde has been up and down the road and he knows how valuable time is when trying to achieve his goal. This upcoming chance could be the best chance he ever has to win it all.
VIC WATERS: He’ll blow it tonight. BRANDON JACOBS: We will see. Let’s send this match to ringside and let’s get this one underway!
(CUEUP: “Megalomaniac” by KMFDM) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the leader of the New Age Revolution and the former BAD World Heavyweight Champion, RAT FINK!
::Rat Fink walks down to the ring with the Satan’s Clause and Rally Monkeybot and enters the ring:: :
(CUEUP: "Diesel Power" by Prodigy) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, the current WFW President and leader of men… ALEX WYLDE! ::Alex Wylde walks down to a mixed pop and enters the ring:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Alex Wylde and Rat Fink meet in the center of the ring and the ref is reminding both men of the rules. RAT FINK JUST PUSHED WYLDE AND WYLDE STUMBLES BACK! Rat Fink charges with a clothesline and Wylde goes down and Rat Fink looks down at Wylde with a big smile on his face.
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink wants to get the size advantage into Wylde’s head. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink always likes to play his mind games. Wylde uses his hands in order to help himself up…. RAT FINK STOMPS ON THE HAND OF WYLDE AND WYLDE RETRACTS HIS LEFT HAND BACK IN PAIN AND STARTS CURSING OUT RAT FINK!
VIC WATERS: I’m pretty sure those seven words that he uttered don’t belong on the air. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde gets up AND HE CHOPS RAT FINK HARD IN THE CHEST! Rat Fink grabs his chest….. RAT FINK FIRES BACK WITH A CHOP OF HIS OWN TO WYLDE! Wylde is grimacing in pain.
VIC WATERS: These two are leaving it all in the ring. BRANDON JACOBS: WYLDE NAILS RAT FINK WITH TEN FAST CHOPS TO THE CHEST AND RAT FINK BACKS UP TOWARDS THE CORNER!
VIC WATERS: Wylde just kicked it into hyper mode. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde jumps up and nails Rat Fink with a spinning heel kick! RAT FINK SLUMPS FORWARD INTO THE ARMS OF WYLDE!
VIC WATERS: That was damn close to being a knock out blow.l BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde waistlocks Rat Fink…. HE DROPS HIM ON THE ROPES WITH A STUN GUN! Wylde goes for the cover! 1--------------------------2—KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Too early to go for a pinfall, but the crowd thought it could be after the rather quick opening match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink is grabbing his throat, he may have hurt his larynx when he landed on the top rope. Wylde picks up Rat Fink off the mat…. EUROPEAN UPPERCUT RIGHT TO THE THROAT! Rat Fink is coughing as he is trying to catch his breath.
VIC WATERS: Wylde is using the asthmatic psychology trying to take out the bigger man by working over his throat so he has trouble catching his breath.
BRANDON JACOBS: Alex Wylde locks on a front headlock on Rat Fink. The ref better check to make sure Wylde’s arm doesn’t slip and make this into a choke.
VIC WATERS: The WFW President would never cheat. He is of the highest morale fiber and I’m appalled that you would think that.
BRANDON JACOBS: WYLDE SLIPS INTO A CHOKE AND RAT FINK IS TRYING TO PUSH HIMSELF AWAY! The ref is going underneath to check…. Wylde slips his arm away from the neck of Rat Fink and puts him back in a headlock without the ref seeing it.
VIC WATERS: My monitor what went out. What happened? BRANDON JACOBS: After the show, make sure you return the monitor back to Bobby. WYLDE SLIPS IT BACK INTO THE CHOKE BUT THIS TIME THE REF SEES IT! The ref is giving Wylde a five count to break! ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! Wylde breaks the hold!
VIC WATERS: Wylde has five second to break, what he did was perfectly legal. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde goes to lock on the front headlock on again…. RAT FINK RAMS HIS SHOULDER INTO THE MIDSECTION OF WYLDE! Wylde tries to secure the front headlock again…. RAT FINK NAILS HIM WITH ANOTHER SHOT TO THE MIDSECTION!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink is about to explode here. BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK WHIPS WYLDE OFF THE ROPES AND HE HITS HIM WITH A LOU THESZ PRESS! Rat Fink starts pounding away on Wylde with closed fists and the ref is warning him to open his hand or he is going to be DQed!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink is taking a lot of liberties with the rules here. Boy, do I love both men.
BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is trying to get Rat Fink off of Wylde but Rat Fink won’t stop! This crowd is solidly behind Rat Fink.
CROWD: LET THEM GO! LET THEM GO! LET THEM GO! VIC WATERS: A lot of fans are upset at Wylde and the board of directors from keeping Anarky and Manson apart and you can tell by how much they are getting behind Rat Fink here.
BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK GRABS THE HAIR OF WYLDE AND REPEATEDLY BANGS WYLDE’S HEAD INTO THE MAT! The referee jumps onto the back of Rat Fink in order to get him off!
VIC WATERS Man, this referee has a death wish. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink gets up with the referee on his back…. RAT FINK RAMS HIM BACK FIRST INTO THE TURNBUCKLE AND THE REFEREE COLLAPSES IN THE CORNER!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink could get DQed for that, but I’m not sure he cares at this point. BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK GRABS WYLDE BY THE HAIR AND HE THROWS HIM THROUGH THE ROPES TO THE OUTSIDE WHERE SATAN CLAUSE AND RALLY MONKEYBOY AWAIT!
VIC WATERS: Wylde may have been safer in the ring. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink is going over to the referee and is apologizing for what he did claiming that he thought it was a fan. RALLYBOT IS RIPPING UP THE MATS AT RINGSIDE AND SATAN CLAUSE HAS WYLDE BY THE THROAT! SATAN CLAUSE DELIVERS A BIG CHOKESLAM RIGHT ONTO THE GROUND!
VIC WATERS: Wylde really looks like he hit his head hard. BRANDON JACOBS: Satan Clause is picking up Wylde yet again….. POWERSLAM! Wylde is clutching his back. Rallybot rolls Wylde back into the ring. Why does Rat Fink have to use this outside inference when he doesn’t need it?
VIC WATERS: That is why he is Rat Fink. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink drags Wylde to the center of the ring….. RAT FINK PICKS WYLDE UP IN HE AIR WITH A SUPLEX…. HE IS HOLDING HIM THERE AND HOLDING HIM THERE…. AND FINALLY FALLS BACK WITH THE STALL SUPLEX!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink was letting all the blood rush to his head in order to make him dizzy.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink cockily covers Wylde. The dazed referee goes down for the count! 1-----------------------------------------2------------------------------- WYLDE GETS THE SHOULDER UP! Rat Fink gives the referee a look!
VIC WATERS: I swear, our referees should get hazard pay. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink picks Wylde off the mat and he whips him off the ropes… RAT FINK IS GOING FOR A SIDESLAM BUT WYLDE LEG SCISSORS THE ARMS AND WYLDE CATCHES HIM WITH A CRUCIFIX! The ref goes down for the count! 1----------------------------------2---------------- RAT FINK KICKS OUT! He was almost caught totally off guard by that.
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink needs to get back on him and maintain the momentum that he had. BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK CHARGES WYLDE AND HE TAKES HIM DOWN WITH A FLAPJACK AND RAT FINK GOES FACE DOWN ONTO THE MAT! Wylde bounces off the ropes…. DROPKICK RIGHT INTO THE FACE OF RAT FINK! Wylde goes for the cover! Wait a second, Satan Clause just jumped onto the apron and the referee is going over to get him down.
VIC WATERS: Satan Clause is buying Rat Fink time until he recovers. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde gets up…. AND HE DROPKICKS THE REFEREE FROM BEHIND RIGHT INTO SATAN CLAUSE WHO FALLS OFF THE APRON ONTO THE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: Domino effect! BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is down and he is awful lot of pain from the beating he has taken from both Rat Fink and Wylde tonight.
VIC WATERS: Maybe if he did his job, they wouldn’t have to kill him. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde picks up Rat Fink….. ROCKER DROP! Rat Fink is out cold! Wylde drags the referee over! The ref slowly starts to make the count! 1-------------------------------2------------------ RALLY MONKEYBOT PULLS THE REFEREE OUT OF THE RING BEFORE HE CAN MAKE THE THREE COUNT!
VIC WATERS: I think he had him there too. BRANDON JACOBS: THE REFEREE UNLOADS ON RALLY MONKEYBOT AND HE TAKES A TUMBLE DOWN TO THE GROUND!
VIC WATERS: The referee strikes back! No doubt that is Randalls’ influence. BRANDON JACOBS: The referee slides back into the ring and Wylde is utterly frustrated. WYLDE PICKS UP RAT FINK….. KICK TO THE STOMACH! Wylde is going for the WILD BUSTER! NOOOO…. RAT FINK BACKBODY DROPS WYLDE RIGHT ONTO THAT BACK SATAN CLAUSE WORKED OVER EARLIER!
VIC WATERS: Rally Monkeybot bought Rat Fink enough time to regain his senses. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink picks up Wylde….. RAT FINK WHIPS HIM OFF THE ROPES…. WYLDE DUCKS UNDER…. GATECRASHER SUPERKICK TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD!
VIC WATERS: He got him! BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde falls down and is grabbing his back, that duck under may have further injured something. Wylde crawls over and makes the cover on Rat Fink! The referee goes down to make the count! 1---------------------------------------------2--------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. The current WFW President and #1 Contender for the WFW World Heavyweight Title…. ALEX WYLDE!
VIC WATERS: That is the great thing about the Gatecrasher, Wylde can hit that out of anywhere and get the victory.
BRANDON JACOBS: It was a hell of a match and Wylde is damn lucky that he was able to escape Rat Fink and his evil minions.
VIC WATERS: Hopefully Wylde’s back doesn’t play a factor in his WFW World Heavyweight Title match or Rat Fink may just have his revenge afterall.
BRANDON JACOBS: Yeah, that would be a kick in the pants, okay folks, we are going to take a small break to shill even more WFW merchandise, when we comeback, PULSAR WILL GO ONE ON ONE WITH THE DEBUTING MIGHTY IMPALA!
