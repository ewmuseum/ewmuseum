WFW Great Expectations: Felix Red (c) vs. Minion vs. Good Eddie

23 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 23 February 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: Tonight Minion goes against Felix Red for the title which launched young Manson to stardom, The BAD World Heavyweight Title, but Minion has an uphill battle to face because despite being one of the most dangerous men in all of wrestling, he has yet to win a World Title. Felix Red, one of the brightest young stars in all of WFW, wants to leave with Minion’s record of never winning a major singles title in tact. Your thoughts Vic?
VIC WATERS: I’ll take great pleasure in watching Felix Red crash and burn. Minion has been an animal and despite Felix Red’s deadly offense and lightning quick speed, that may not be enough to stop Minion.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is always taking on opponents which claim to be bigger and badder than him but he always finds a way to overcome the odds. You may not remember, but in that three way between Gideon, Michaels, and Felix Red, the person who took that fall is the current WFW World Heavyweight Champion Scotty Michaels and Felix Red held his own again both unfortunately wasn’t factored into the decision.
VIC WATERS: Luck, pure luck. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink was one of the most dominant champions in WFW History and Felix Red was the one to put a stop to that.
VIC WATERS: Okay, well that was impressive, but as evil as Rat Fink is, he still has his sanity. Minion is mentally disturbed. When he wants something, there is nothing that he isn’t willing to do.
BRANDON JACOBS: Well, I do agree with that. I saw what he did to Adam Benjamin and even Manson. He is utterly ruthless in what he will do to get the victory.
VIC WATERS: Yet you still pick Felix Red over Minion. BRANDON JACOBS: Call me crazy but Felix Red still has the hot hand. I’ve seen him defy the odds so much that until it actually happens, I think Felix Red is going to prevail.
VIC WATERS: We will see, but you were wrong when you thought El Arco Iris was going to beat Wells. I’m going to go up 2-0 tonight on this one and I’m going to be leaving in your Cadillac by night’s end.
BRANDON JACOBS: There is only one way to find out. Let’s send this one to ringside and get this one underway!
(CUEUP: “Where Boys Fear To Tread” by Smashing Pumpkins) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, one of the most sadistic men in all of wrestling, from New York City…. MINION!
::Minion walks down to the ring to the massive boos of the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “"If Born From This Soil: Treatments For the Injected Foetu" by Minor Procedure and Shai Hulud)
RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, making his way to the ring, weighing in at 213 pounds and standing at 6 feet, three inches, “The Boy Who Destroyed The World” and BAD World Heavyweight Champion, FELIX RED!
::Felix Red comes down to the ring to the roar of the crowd and enters the ring:: BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second, where is Good Eddie? VIC WATERS: He was booked in a three way earlier with the Hilton Sisters and said he didn’t want to work twice so an inebriated Wylde gave him the night off and in order to make up for it, made this a Falls Count Anywhere in Edison Field match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Makes sense to me. *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Minion charges Felix Red…. BIG TACKLE BY MINION AND BOTH MEN ARE ROLLING ON THE GROUND EXCHANGING RIGHTS AND LEFTS! They roll out of the ring!
VIC WATERS: This match is being fought under a BAD rules for the BAD World Heavyweight Title. Who knows what trouble these two could get into….
BRANDON JACOBS: MINION THROWS FELIX RED INTO THE GUARDRAIL! Did you hear how Felix Red’s back connected with the cold steel?
VIC WATERS: Felix has to becareful because his strength is his quickness, if he has trouble moving his back, he won’t be able to react as quickly when Minion tries to nail him with everything including the kitchen sink.
BRANDON JACOBS: MINION CHARGES FELIX RED AND FELIX RED BACKBODY DROPS HIM INTO THE CROWD…. WAIT, MINION LANDS ON HIS FEET!
VIC WATERS: Minion got really lucky there, that was the perfect landing, maybe tonight is his night after all.
BRANDON JACOBS: Minion reaches over the barricade and german suplexs him from the ringside area into the crowd! Minion goes out after him…. An injured Felix Red tries to crawl away as fast as possible.
VIC WATERS: This crowd is parting like the Red Sea. BRANDON JACOBS: Minion goes to pick up Felix Red… FELIX RED GRABS A SNAPPLE BOTTLE OUT OF A FANS HAND AND BREAKS IT OVER MINION’S HEAD BUSTING HIM OPEN!
CROWD: BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! VIC WATERS: Minion looks like he got hit by a truck. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red pockets a shard of glass on the side of his tights for later and goes back to work on Minion. Felix Red grabs Minion AND HE WHIPS HIM INTO A ROW OF FOLDING CHAIRS AS THEY CONTINUE TO MOVE CLOSER AND CLOSER TO THE FENCES IN CENTER FIELD!
VIC WATERS: This is starting to get really wild. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red takes a shard of the Snapple bottle AND HE STARTS CUTTING OPEN MINION’S HEAD EVEN MORE AND THE BLOOD IS GOING ALL OVER THE PLACE!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red looks like he may have tapped a vein. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red grabs some food from a fan! He takes the hot shrimp lo mein right out of the hands of a fan…. AND HE DUMPS IT ON MINION’S HEAD AND MINION IS TRYING DESPERATELY TO GET THE HOT LO MEIN OFF HIS EXPOSED CUTS!
VIC WATERS: Blood and shrimp lo mein doesn’t go together all that well.. BRANDON JACOBS: MINION IS SHRIEKING IN PAIN FROM THE HOT GREASE WHICH HAS COVERED HIS EXPOSED CUTS! He sounds like a wounded dog!
VIC WATERS: When a dog sounds like that, they shoot him. BRANDON JACOBS: Felx Red picks Minion up… MINION STARTS POUNDING AWAY CATCHING FELIX RED OFF GUARD! Minion waistlocks Felix Red…. OVER THE HEAD REALLY BELLY TO BELLY AND FELIX RED LANDS HARD ON HIS BACK!
VIC WATERS: That was vicious, but after what Felix Red did, well deserved. BRANDON JACOBS: Minion grabs a Rally Monkey out of the hands of a fan…. HE STARTS BEATING FELIX RED WITH IT REPEATEDLY!
VIC WATERS: DIE MONKEY DIE! BRANDON JACOBS: You just have issues from that first round elimination a couple of years ago. The stuffed Rally Monkey is getting covered in blood and Felix Red is trying to crawl away. MINION GRABS A PURSE FROM AN OLD WOMAN AND TIES IT AROUND THE NECK OF FELIX RED!
VIC WATERS: Minion has given Felix Red a leash! BRANDON JACOBS: Minion reaches into the purse and grabs a compact WHICH HE SMEARS INTO THE EYES OF FELIX RED!
VIC WATERS: That dust is going to be impossible for him to get out. BRANDON JACOBS: MINION STARTS LEADING HIM TO THE WALL! VIC WATERS: This can only lead to no good. BRANDON JACOBS: Minion whips Felix Red into the wall shoulder first but Felix Red SOMEHOW remains standing looking completely exhausted. MINION BEGINS RUNNING IN THE OPPOSITE DIRECTION…. HE IS A GOOD FIFTY FEET AWAY…. What is he planning on doing?
VIC WATERS: He can’t win the match this way… BRANDON JACOBS: MINION MAKES A MAD DASH TOWARD FELIX RED WHO IS STILL LEANING AGAINST THE WALL…… FELIX RED MOVES OUT OF THE WAY AND MINION CRASHES THROUGH THE WALL!
VIC WATERS: HOLY ****! CROWD: REST IN PEACE! REST IN PEACE! REST IN PEACE! BRANDON JACOBS: A dumbfounded Felix Red walks through the hole in the wall and looks down at Minion who is writhing in pain… Felix Red looks down to Minion and then he looks up at the fountain… FELIX RED IS CLIMBING TO THE TOP OF THE ROCK FOUNTAIN!
VIC WATERS: I’d say that Felix Red would be better off just pinning him instead of taking an unnecessary risk, but when he hits this, he is going to be on every sports cast across the country.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is perched on top for the Shattered Horizon…. FELIX RED FLIES OFF BUT MINION SOMEHOW ROLLS OUT OF THE WAY AND FELIX RED LANDS HARD ON THE GROUND!
VIC WATERS: Minion just rolled out of the way for his life. There is no way he could have taken that move in his condition.
BRANDON JACOBS: Minion is getting up slowly and he grabs Felix Red. Minion picks up Felix Red and pushes him up on top of the fountain and climbs up after him.
VIC WATERS: In Minion’s condition, the fact that he can do this is a downright miracle. BRANDON JACOBS: Minion is standing up on the fountain… Minion goes to pick up Felix Red… FELIX RED NAILS HIM WITH A SPINNING HEEL KICK AND MINION STUMBLES BACK CLOSE TO THE WATER JETS OF THE FOUNTAIN! Minion is able to maintain his balance though!
VIC WATERS: Minion has to becareful. Those kicks of Felix Red are a lethal weapon. BRANDON JACOBS: FELIX RED CHARGES MINION… MINION HIS HEAD DOWN AND BACKBODY DROPS FELIX RED INTO THE MAIN JET OF THE FOUNTAIN AND LANDS RIGHT ON THE BACK OF HIS HEAD!
VIC WATERS: That is the enema from hell right there. BRANDON JACOBS: Minion steps into the jet and washes the blood and lo mein off his face and sees an even bigger rock. MINION PICKS FELIX RED UP OUT OF THE JETS…. BALDO BOMB RIGHT BACK DOWN ONTO THE ROCKS!
VIC WATERS: Minion wants to make sure he was down, but what could he be planning? BRANDON JACOBS: Minion is climbing up onto the even bigger rock over the jets of the fountain….
CROWD: MINION! MINION! MINION! VIC WATERS: This crowd may not like Minion, but they love the spectacular and I have a feeling we are about to see it.
BRANDON JACOBS: MINION TAKES A FLYING LEAP AND NAILS FELIX RED WITH THE FALL FROM GRACE! Minion makes the cover! The ref goes down to make the count! 1-----------------------------------------------2--------------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. AND NEW BAD WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION…. MINION! BRANDON JACOBS: Minion grabs the title belt from the time keeper and raises it up in the air!
VIC WATERS: This was an all out war which could have gone either way, but you have to wonder if all those battles with Manson helped to strengthen Minion for his match here today.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red will be back, you can count on it. Minion better keep a close eye over his shoulder. Folks, when we comeback, it is time for the WFW North American Title match as Richard Gideon puts the title on the line against Copycat and Psycho! We will be right back!
