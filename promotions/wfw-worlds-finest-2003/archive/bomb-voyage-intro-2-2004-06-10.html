Bomb Voyage: Intro #2

10 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 10 June 2004 ::Jonathan Marx and Vic Waters are sitting at ringside aboard the WFW Cruise Ship, passengers fill the upper to decks of the boat and the main deck in order to catch a view of the action. On the beach, fans gather around closed circuit to watch action from the filled capacity boat as Chris Wink, Good Eddie, Mike McGee, Mighty Impala, Black Rose, Weekend Warrior, Silly Simon, El Segundo, Arquito Iris, PHENOMITO, HEEL, and Tiny meet and greet the fans::
JONATHAN MARX: WELCOME EVERYONE TO BOMB VOYAGE! We are beach side in sunny Honolulu, Hawaii. Life is beautiful isn’t it?
VIC WATERS: It would be a lot better if I didn’t have Jacobs ugly mug on my TV. He isn’t dead. That is all you need to know.
JONATHAN MARX: It is more of a matter that he knows the identity of Bartleby who along with Michaels had the balls to attack Anarky after his victory against Maelstrom. We all know what Michaels has been capable of in the past, if he has found himself another partner in crime, we could all be in trouble because there is nothing Michaels won’t do to get ahead.
VIC WATERS: Fifty dollars says its Luster. JONATHAN MARX: It could very possibly be Luster because those two are as thick as thieves, but after how things ended where Michaels called the asylum on him, I think the swerve will be that it ISN’T Luster.
VIC WATERS: We will see. JONATHAN MARX: I’m really excited about tonight’s show, we have Manson defending the WFW World Heavyweight Title against Gideon, WFW President Sean Edmunds will be going one on one with Larry Tact with Sherri banned from ringside, a six man falls count anywhere on the boat between Copycat, El Arco Iris, and Pulsar against Psycho, Felix Red, and Golem, Wells will put up his BAD World Heavyweight Title for the very first time against the winner of Michael Dice vs. Mike Lane, and Anarky will go one on one against one of the hottest newcomers in the wrestling world today, Iron Lion.
VIC WATERS: You forgot the midgets! JONATHAN MARX: The politically correct term is minis. VIC WATERS: I prefer fun size. JONATHAN MARX: WFW will debut our first two minis as Arquito Iris and PHENOMITO go one on one in our opening match. This is going to be a fun show and after Shock & Awe, boy do we need one.
VIC WATERS: The WFW self destructed last week, it should be interesting to see if we can return to normalcy with LOVE running all over the place causing their havoc.
JONATHAN MARX: Wink has pleaded to them repeatedly to behave with all the passengers we have on the ship, he doesn’t want anything to get too wild and he has even promised them extra money if they don’t do any serious damage to the boat.
VIC WATERS: When all else fails, dish out the cash. JONATHAN MARX: This show I’m really looking forward to because I think we are going to have two tremendous WRESTLING matches on top after all the chaos and brawling at Shock & Awe. First we have Larry Tact who is a tough and brilliant technician who is fresh off of FW Survivor having outlasted Manson, Melton, Miles, Dan Ryan, and American taking on a former WFW World Heavyweight Champion in Sean Edmunds who has finally gotten himself back on track after wins over Rabesque and Pulsar.
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is a brilliant, brilliant man and an even better wrestler so I have no doubt that he’ll find a way to beat Tact and continue his winning streak. Edmunds doesn’t need Sherri to beat Tact and he’ll prove tonight.
JONATHAN MARX: I never said that Edmunds needed Sherri. He is former WFW World Heavyweight Champion, but he is use to having her around causing distractions or hitting people over the head with her purse when he is down and out and in trouble.
VIC WATERS: Edmunds will win, trust me on that. JONATHAN MARX: Then we have the main event, the longest reigning WFW North American Champion Richard Gideon will face the current WFW World Heavyweight Champion, Manson. I know it is June and we still have half a year to go still, but I think this could be a match of the year candidate.
VIC WATERS: We may even have a new WFW World Heavyweight Champion since no one has defended the title successfully in awhile. It is really ironic that Manson and Doc’s first challenger was Gideon. He gave Doc fit after fit and slowly wore him down to the point where he lost the title at Cherry Blossom Chaos.
JONATHAN MARX: I love Manson, but he has to be very careful because Gideon could very well make him a former champion in the bat of an eye he is that good. I personally think he should have won the PWIMag Rookie of the Year after all he did in his first year.
VIC WATERS: There is one big difference from the Gideon who faced Doc and the Gideon who is facing Manson…. Gideon has a year’s worth of experience now and he is going to use that to his advantage with Manson here tonight. I’m going to predict that the trend continues and Manson loses his title here tonight.
JONATHAN MARX: I don’t really know either way, but he should be a hell of match. VIC WATERS: You know the match that I’m really looking forward to? JONATHAN MARX: Which one? VIC WATERS: Six Man Tag… Falls Count Anywhere on the Ship…. Felix Red, Psycho, and Golem…. Three of the toughest brawlers ever to come down the pike are going to go head to head with Copycat, El Arco Iris, and Pulsar… and they can brawl to anywhere on the ship. Our cameras will follow them anywhere they go… I think this could be a really fun match up, especially for LOVE who doesn’t want to see El Arco Iris take away Psycho’s North American Title.
JONATHAN MARX: LOVE is absolutely psycho. After what they tried to do to that poor rabbit last week, who knows what they might to do to El Arco Iris here tonight? You remember what they did to El Arco Iris in the cage… it just isn’t right. Thankfully he has his good friend Copycat and Pulsar, the king of the six man tag in his corner.
VIC WATERS: It may not be enough. I have ere feeling that by the time this night is done, Felix Red, Psycho, Jared Wells, and Anarky are going to make this the LOVE Boat.
JONATHAN MARX: I seriously hope you are wrong… we all went through hell at Shock & Awe with all the chaos but I have learned never to underestimate the power of LOVE….. Vic, why is the camera man doing the slash his throat sign at me?
VIC WATERS: He must have seen one of your promos… actually he is signaling for a commercial break.
JONATHAN MARX: Folks, we have to take a commercial break, when we comeback…. El Arquito Iris will take on PHENOMITO! Stay tuned!
