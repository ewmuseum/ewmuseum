WFW Paradise Lost: Doc Silver (c) vs. Rat Fink

27 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 27 July 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Jul-27-03 AT 01:30 PM (EST)] BRANDON JACOBS: Finally, the match that we have all been waiting for, Rat Fink will challenge Doc Silver to find out who the most evil man in WFW really is!
VIC WATERS: Doc Silver isn’t evil, he just takes full advantage of the rules. But Doc Silver can be devious and he definitely is more devious than Rat Fink…
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink really gave Manson a run for his money, denying him at every turn from extracting revenge up until the end. I think that somehow, Rat Fink will come up with a way to win this match and keep the long awaited singles match between Doc Silver and Shawn Hart from happening.
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink will never beat Doc, never… BRANDON JACOBS: IT IS TIME FOR THE MAIN EVENT! Let’s send this one to ringside and get the main event started!
(CUEUP: Megalomaniac by KMFDM) ::Rat Fink comes down to a mixed reaction from the crowd with his cheesy looking Sci-Fi Robot and enters the ring::
(CUEUP: “Only Happy When It Rains” by Garbage) :oc Silver comes out to the loudest heel heat of the night with the WFW World Heavyweight Title belt over his shoulder and he walks down to the ring and enters::
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink and Doc Silver meet face to face in the ring and they are trash talking each other back and forth. DOC SILVER PUSHES RAT FINK! Rat Fink looks highly annoyed… HE PUSHES DOC SILVER BACK! Doc Silver stumbles back… CLOTHESLINE BY RAT FINK! Doc goes down!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink is looking really aggressive tonight. BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK GRABS THE HEAD OF DOC SILVER AND RAMS IT INTO THE TURNBUCKLE! Doc stumbles backwards… BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX! Rat Fink dumps Doc Silver right on his head!
VIC WATERS: Doc looks like he landed awkwardly there. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink mounts Doc Silver and starts wailing at him with closed fists! HE IS REALLY LAYING INTO DOC! The ref is trying to get in there to make him open up his fist!
VIC WATERS: The refs need to start doing a better job tonight or they are going to find all of their asses on the unemployment line.
BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK STARTS NAILING DOC WITH ELBOWS TO THE FACE! Rat Fink just busted Doc Silver open right over one of his eyes! The ref is trying to pull Rat Fink off to check on the cut!
VIC WATERS: I’ve never seen Rat Fink this vicious before. You can tell how much the WFW World Heavyweight Title means to him. This is his very first WFW World Heavyweight Title shot and he doesn’t want it to go to waste.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink is arguing with the ref…. WAIT A SECOND, DOC REACHES UP AND HE NAILS RAT FINK WITH A NUTSHOT RIGHT IN THE GROIN IN FRONT OF THE REF! The ref is calling for the bell! But wait, why is Rat Fink laughing?
VIC WATERS: Call for the DQ! BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink grabs the ref and shows him that he had on an iron cup… THE REF IS GOING TO LET THIS MATCH CONTINUE! The ref takes the cup by the tip of his fingers and dumps it out of the ring!
VIC WATERS: I don’t think that was necessary. BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver is grabbing his hand in pain, he may have broke a finger or two trying to nail Rat Fink with the nutshot.
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink should be DQed for carrying an illegal object into that ring! BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink grabs the hands of Doc Silver AND HE NAILS THEM WITH A DOUBLE STOMP! Doc Silver is retreating into the corner!
VIC WATERS: Finally, the ref is checking to see how Doc is doing with the open cut and the injured fingers.
BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK RUSHES INTO THE CORNER AND NAILS DOC WITH A KNEE LIFT TO THE FACE! Rat Fink picks Doc Silver up… HE BODYSLAMS HIM INTO THE CENTER OF THE RING AND DOC SILVER IS BEGGING OFF!
VIC WATERS: Doc Silver needs to buy sometime to recover and regroup to get back to his original game plan.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink pulls Doc Silver up off the mat… RAT FINK PICKS DOC SILVER UP FOR A STALL SUPLEX… HE IS HOLDING HIM UP THERE FOR ALL TO SEE!
VIC WATERS: The worst part about that kind of suplex is that when you are held up there for so long, you grow dizzy because all the blood rushes to your head. It will take a few minutes before Doc can get reoriented with what is going on in the ring.
BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK DROPS HIM BACK! Rat Fink goes for the cover! 1--------2---------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: That was a fast count! Damn stupid refs tonight! BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink picks Doc Silver off the mat…. HE NAILS HIM WITH A STEINERLINE! The momentum of the shot drives Doc Silver back and he stumbles through the ropes to the floor!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink has some amazing power. He nearly took Doc’s head off with that one. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink goes to the outside to get Doc… DOC SILVER NAILS RAT FINK IN THE GUT! Doc grabs Fink by the head and tries to ram him into the guardrail…. FINK BLOCKS IT AND RAMS DOC SILVER HEAD IN INSTEAD! That opened up the cut over Doc Silver’s eye even more!
VIC WATERS: Sooner or later, this ref is going to have to stop this match for excessive blood loss. Rat Fink better get the job done before this contest is ruled a no contest or all his work will be for not.
BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink grabs Doc Silver and he drags him into the aisle…. RAT FINK WHIPS DOC SILVER INTO THE GUARDRAIL! Fink grabs a sign which says “Doc Silver for President” from a fan and HE WHIPES THE SHARP EDGE OF THE SIDE OF POSTERBOARD RIGHT OVER DOC’S HEAD! My god, Rat Fink is vicious tonight!
VIC WATERS: Where is damn count by the ref?! Why is he letting this all go?! BRANDON JACOBS: I really have no idea. The ref isn’t counting at all. Rat Fink grabs Doc Silver…. HE PILEDRIVES HIM INTO THE DOC FOR PRESIDENT SIGN AND BLOODIES IT ALL UP! Rat Fink grabs the sign from under Doc and hands it back to the fan.
VIC WATERS: That will be on EBay in a week. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink grabs Doc Silver by the hair and leads him back to the ring… DOC SILVER GRABS THE WAIST OF RAT FINK AND RAMS HIM STOMACH FIRST INTO THE MAT! Rat Fink looks like he was winded!
VIC WATERS: Come on Doc! BRANDON JACOBS: DOC GRABS RAT FINK… BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX! Rat Fink hit so hard that he bounced back up!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink is reaching back and grabbing his back. It looks like Doc finally managed to do some damage.
BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver picks Rat Fink up… HE RAMS HIM BACK FIRST INTO THE RING! Rat Fink slumps down! Within the span of a minute, Doc has crushed both sides!
VIC WATERS: Doc needs to wind him, Rat Fink has been pouring on the assault to slow him down.
BRANDON JACOBS: DOC SILVER IS CALLING FOR THE SUPERKICK… DOC FAKES THE SUPERKICK AND A TIRED RAT FINK SEES IT AND HE DUCKS OUT OF THE WAY… DDT BY DOC!!
VIC WATERS: GO DOC GO! BRANDON JACOBS: Doc rolls Rat Fink into the ring and rolls in after him! Doc has some how changed the momentum in this match, but Rat Fink could comeback at anytime so he needs to put him away.
VIC WATERS: I believe in you Doc, COME ON! BRANDON JACOBS: Doc lifts Rat Fink off the match…. SWINGING NECKBREAKER IN THE CENTER OF THE RING! Doc Silver goes for the cover! 1------------2--------------KICKOUT! Rat Fink just barely kicked out of that! It looks like those shots to the back Rat Fink took slowed his ability to kickout because he has taken far less abuse than Doc has in this match.
VIC WATERS: That is one of the main reasons you work over the back, you want to keep your opponent from kicking out.
BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver picks Rat Fink off the mat… BIG BACKBREAKER! Doc Silver is holding onto it and he is driving the knee into the back of Rat Fink!
VIC WATERS: Listen to Rat Fink, he is screaming in pain! Doc Silver is using all of the strength in his body to keep Rat Fink in that hold!
BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver stands over Rat Fink and HE SLAPS HIM ACROSS THE FACE! Silver is bad mouthing Rat Fink for thinking his is the baddest man in the WFW!
VIC WATERS: Doc loves to play the head games, he is treating Rat Fink like he owns him. BRANDON JACOBS: A bloody Doc Silver is calling for the ACES FULL! Once Doc hits the Aces Full, I don’t care who you are, the match is over!
VIC WATERS: The Aces Full is the one move in all of wrestling when it is hit, you might as well chalk up a win for Doc!
BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver whips Rat Fink off the ropes… KICK TO THE CHEST… DOC GOES FOR THE ACES FULL BUT RAT FINK GRABS THE WAIST OF DOC AND NAILS HIM WITH A BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX! This place is getting behind Rat Fink! Doc Silver is slowly getting back up…. RAT FINK BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND NAILS DOC SILVER WITH A SHOULDER BLOCK!
VIC WATERS: Doc Silver went inside out on that one! BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink makes the cover! 1-----------------2--------------- DOC KICKS OUT! HOW HE KICKED OUT, I DO NOT KNOW, BUT HE KICKED OUT!
VIC WATERS: How Doc does it I’ll never know… BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FINK WHIPS DOC SILVER INTO THE CORNER! Doc folds like an accordion down to the ground! Rat Fink walks over… OH MY… HE IS PLACING DOC SILVER ON THE TOP ROPE! RAT FINK IS GOING TO GO FOR HIS FINISHER!
VIC WATERS: Doc needs to break free somehow! BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink begins to climb the ropes, the fans here are on their feet sensing that title may change here tonight!
VIC WATERS: Come on Doc! Come on! Doc Silver losing here tonight would be like the ball going between Buckner’s legs, so very close to getting the chance to beat Hart for the third straight time on PPV! FIGHT DOC FIGHT!
BRANDON JACOBS: RAT FiNK HAS THE MOVE SET… WAIT, DOC IS STRUGGLING TO BREAK FREE! Rat Fink is losing his grip on Doc! Rat Fink turns around AND HE ELBOWS DOC INTO THE CUT BREAKING IT OPEN EVEN MORE! Rat Fink smears the blood in Doc’s eyes to blind him!
VIC WATERS: The ref really should call this damn match and award it to Doc! BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink turns back around… HE HAS THE CRUCIFIX SET! HE IS GOING TO DO IT! VIC WATERS: Doc can’t see and apparently neither can the ref! BRANDON JACOBS: HE DID IT! RAT FINK NAILED THE TOP ROPE CRUCIFIC POWERBOMB! VIC WATERS: OH MY GOD! DOC IS DEAD! Doc is going to need be a miracle to be saved now! BRANDON JACOBS: SHAWN HART, GIDEON, and DIABLO RUN DOWN TO RINGSIDE AND THEY IMMEDIATELY ATTACK RAT FINK! THEY ARE HANDCUFFING RAT FINK TO THE ROPES!
VIC WATERS: YES! YES! :iablo and Gideon handcuff Doc Silver to the ropes:: VIC WATERS: NOOOO! BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is calling for the bell! DIABLO CLOTHESLINES THE REF OVER THE TOP ROPE!
VIC WATERS: These arrogant young punks, we were in the middle of an epic battle and they think they can just come out here and interrupt the damn match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is taking off his C.O.C.K.S. shirt and he throws it into Doc’s face!
::Richard Gideon waves goodbye to Hart leaves the ring through the crowd:: RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match by disqualification, RAT FINK! SHAWN HART: Doc, did you really think I was going to let you lose to Rat Fink this close to the PPV? I don’t only want to win the WFW World Heavyweight Title, I want to pin your ass to do it.
::Shawn Hart turns around to face Rat Fink:: SHAWN HART: Sorry Rat Fink, it isn’t personal. You just didn’t fit into C.O.C.K.S plans. ::a seriously pissed off Rat Fink tries to break free but he is unable to:: SHAWN HART: My friend Diablo here has a present for you Doc… :iablo begins to light a fireball::
BRANDON JACOBS: SOMEONE NEEDS TO HELP DOC! IF DOC IS BLINDED, WE WON’T HAVE A MAIN EVENT FOR THE PPV!
::Vic Waters takes off his headset and runs out booth into the ring:: BRANDON JACOBS: That is the noblest thing that I’ve ever seen Vic do! OH NO… DIABLO SEES VIC CHARGING HIM AND THROWS A FIREBALL IN HIS FACE! OH MY! ::Vic is rolling around on the ground in agony::
BRANDON JACOBS: Who is going to save Doc Silver & the WFW now?! OH MY GOD! MANSON IS LIMPING OUT WITH A CHAIR TO SAVE DOC & RAT FINK! Why would Manson come out here to save two people who have made his life a living hell?!
::Shawn Hart & Diablo bail out of the ring:: BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is finally able to restore order…. But is it too late?!? A NEW SATAN HAS ARRIVED AND HIS NAME IS SHAWN HART!
::Shawn Hart & Diablo smile at the ring as the walk backwards to the dressing room:: FTB
