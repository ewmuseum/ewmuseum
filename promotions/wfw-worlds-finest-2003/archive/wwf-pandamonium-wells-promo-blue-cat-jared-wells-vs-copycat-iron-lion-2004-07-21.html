WWF Pandamonium: Wells Promo / Blue Cat & Jared Wells vs. Copycat & Iron Lion

21 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 21 July 2004 (Backstage) (Jared standing wearing his black sleeveless LOVE shirt with "RESPECT" and an arrow pointing down to his crotch on the front. The B.A.D World title is around his waist. Wearing a folded bandana around his head, his face is painted for war looking like a fool)
BLACK ROSE: I'm standing backstage with LOVE member and the B.A.D World Heavyweight Champion, Jared Wells. There are rumors flying around all of the WFW and Jared, your a marked man.
JARED WELLS: All the rumors, what ifs, and denial can be thrown out the window. Of course I'm a marked man because thats how I run the show. I'm the B.A.D World Heavyweight Champion. I'm the ******* walking controversy 'round here. LOVE says jump, and the rest say how high?
BLACK ROSE: Copycat and Iron Lion seem to be a pretty impressive team. Any kind of strategy going into this match?
(Jared reaches down and picks up two baseball bats and lays one on his right shoulder and the other on his left shoulder)
JARED WELLS: Strategy? You think I'd roll up in here without a plan? Iron Chef, the big sissy of the WFW. Yeah I know his size is impressive but his words make him half the man. He defines honor, he defines respect. It's all bull <BLEEP>. I mean really if thats the case why is he here? There are no rules Iron Chef. NONE! I'm not looking for a win, I'm looking to draw first blood. As far as Copycat. Jesus christ, the only thing I can think of is Dr. Phil. Crybaby Cat wants respect. He wants this, he wants that. Our history is rich and deep. But time only takes you so far. Crybaby Cat, just like your buddy Iron Chef, I WILL DRAW FIRST BLOOD.
BLACK ROSE: What about Blue Cat? JARED WELLS: <BLEEP> him. Want to impress me? Do the job or become hunted. BLACK ROSE: Any final comments? (Jared pulls the bats off his shoulders and raises them up in the air) JARED WELLS: Welcome to The Liars, Oppressors and Violence Experiment kids. Ready to dance?
(Wells walks up to the camera and taps the lens with his bat) JARED WELLS: ........JW, the B.A.D World Heavyweight Champion.......he leads! (Jared spits into the camera as it fades) (Broadcast Booth) BRANDON JACOBS: Some interesting words from Wells... VIC WATERS: Wells has easily become my favorite wrestler. JONATHAN MARX: Since joining LOVE, Wells has really stepped up his game and has showed the world what he is all about.
BRANDON JACOBS: Lets get back to business, It is now time for the double dog collar match between Copycat & Iron Lion and the team of Blue Cat & Jared Wells.
VIC WATERS: Usually when you put a chain around a wild animal, it is to restrain him. But when you do it wrestling, it is to unleash their inner beast and today right here in the San Diego Zoo I think we are going to see a hell of a wild match.
JONATHAN MARX: Poor Wells & Blue Cat. How would you like to be attached to monsters likes Iron Lion & Copycat? That has to be hell.
VIC WATERS: If there are two men who can overcome it, it is the BAD World Heavyweight Champion Jared Wells and Blue Cat. Jared Wells has been one of the hottest wrestlers in all of wrestling right now.
JONATHAN MARX: While I may be rooting for Copycat & Iron Lion to teach Wells and Blue Cat a lesson, on behalf of many of here in WFW, we are all rooting for Wells to kick Rabesque ass all over the building in FWSS.
VIC WATERS: Here,here… I hate Rabesque with a passion. BRANDON JACOBS: Oh come on guys, there are worse things than Rabesque… VIC WATERS: Cancer. Maybe… BRANDON JACOBS: That is totally unfair, he may have made some harsh remarks about the BAD World Heavyweight Title, but Rabesque is legendary…
VIC WATERS: So is Lou Gehrig’s disease… BRANDON JACOBS: *laugh* Stop it. Rabesque is the NEW World Heavyweight Champion. You may not like him or the things he says, but he is still one of the best wrestlers I have ever seen. But I must admit, I am pulling for Wells to beat Rabesque. Rabesque comments about BAD World Heavyweight Title are totally out of line. The BAD World Heavyweight Title means more than I can put into words. It wasn’t about fighting in wild gimmick matches or total disregard for the rules, it is about toughness, hard work, dedication, and not letting the bs which plagues every promotion stop a worker who actually can go. As a wrestling fan, the old BAD was home for some of the fondness memories I have ever had and even the old BAD, the IWF, was home for many things you see today in the NFW East which has been one of the places to be in wrestling for the last four or five months.
JONATHAN MARX: BAD gets a bad rap, it was old school too. They may have done wild things from time to time, but they also believed in great wrestling and I think it speaks volumes that the young people who walked through those doors became some of the legends that you see around today like a Michael Manson
VIC WATERS: I don’t like Manson, but if you don’t respect the BAD World Heavyweight title and its history, you don’t know wrestling. Ask Doc about how great BAD was. He’ll tell you about it. That is one of the reasons he was one of the first people to sign with the WFW.
BRANDON JACOBS: Anyways, enough ranting about Rabesque, what do you guys make of the Blue Cat?
VIC WATERS: All I know about Blue Cat I learned from watching his promos with Sean Edmunds and Sherri. Blue Cat seems to live his gimmick.
JONATHAN MARX:: He really is a *****? VIC WATERS: I resent that. Blue Cat seems to be a kitty of high fiber. Not like Copycat who whines about not getting respect…
JONATHAN MARX: Copycat litterbombed Felix Red which allowed Pulsar to get the victory. How many people does Copycat have to beat until he gets the title shot that he deserves?
VIC WATERS: I don’t know, your father is one of the owners of this company. You tell me. JONATHAN MARX: He has campaigned vigorously to get Copycat a World Heavyweight Title shot but there are three other owners plus a President in Edmunds who he has to convince in order to get the contract signed.
BRANDON JACOBS: Just another sign of democracy at its finest… JONATHAN MARX: Wink has been trying to help too but between him and my father they are one vote short to get the job done. The other big guy hates Copycat and the smaller guy just loves Sean Edmunds.
VIC WATERS: The same smaller guy who loves El Arco Iris? JONATHAN MARX: Yes, it amazing how someone can have such good judgment on one thing and totally be wrong on another…
VIC WATERS: I agree, his love for Edmunds shows he has taste. BRANDON JACOBS: Alright enough you too, people want to get back to the action so we are going to stop all the ranting about Rabesque and Copycat getting a title shot and concentrate on what we can control, getting this match started.
JONATHAN MARX: Amen… VIC WATERS: You just want us to change the subject… JONATHAN MARX: There may be some truth to that… BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, lets send this one to the ring and get this one started! (CUEUP: “Is This The End” by Creed) RING ANNOUNCER: THE FOLLOWING CONTEST IS A DOUBLE DOG COLLAR MATCH! Introducing first, from Anchorage, Alaska, a man focused on bringing honor, pride, and strength back to professional wrestling, IRON LION!
::Iron Lion walks down to the roar of the crowd and enters the ring:: CUEUP: "Brain Stew" by Green Day) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, currently residing in his summer residence in Escandido, California of three time WWL World Champion and current President of the WFW Sean Edmunds….. BLUE CAT!
:: Blue Cat comes down to the ring and enters the ring to the loud boos from the crowd:: (CUEUP: "Friday” by Ice Cube) RING ANNOUNCER: And his partner, the former three time WWL World Heavyweight Champion and current BAD World Heavyweight Champion…. “RAGE ‘O FIRE” JARED WELLS!

JUMBOTRON: EDMUNDS: “I know how much you want to face me at the pay per view, Copycat … I know how much you HATE the fact that you have to go up against one of my little lemmings. I will give you a shot at … (thinks) THE WORLD TITLE! I know you want it … and a World title shot you’ll get … but the only way you get it is by taking on Blue Cat. So, Copycat … take it or leave it …”
