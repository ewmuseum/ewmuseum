WFW: NYC Unplugged: Manson & Silver vs. Hart & Wylde

7 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 7 September 2003 :::several ring attendants are around the ring hosing down the blood, Shawn Hart is being helped to the back by Diablo::
BRANDON JACOBS: Oh man. VIC WATERS: Come on Brandon, speak! Let everyone know what happened during the “break” BRANDON JACOBS: It would be best if we started from the very beginning. After we went to a commercial break, Alex Wylde immediately came down to the ring and demanded that we start the match which quickly drew out Doc Silver. Doc Silver started having words with Wylde and had to be separated by officials. Manson came out to help break up the fight and then Shawn Hart finally came out to see what all of the commotion was about. Wylde kept on demanding that the match start immediately so all four men agreed to start the match before we came back from commercial break. The bell rang, Wylde decided to let Shawn Hart start off the match and Wylde then proceeded to blind side Shawn Hart with a steel chair which caught the team of Doc Silver & Manson by surprise. Wylde told Manson to cover Hart but a confused Manson refused, Doc Silver then promptly pushed Manson out of the way and covered the champion for the three count.
VIC WATERS: But that is… NOT ALL! BRANDON JACOBS: Vic is correct, Doc Silver and Wylde continued to beat down Shawn Hart until Diablo ran down into the ring with a steel chair and made the save. Wylde & Doc Silver left through the crowd. Manson and Diablo had words, but no blows were exchanged.
VIC WATERS: Which means…. BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver will go one on one with Manson at Almost Live which is the first time since the league’s inception that they will face off and this time, it is a one on one battle for the right to face Shawn Hart.
VIC WATERS: Should be excellent… BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, when we come back, Sean Edmunds & Copycat will collide against the tag team of Richard Gideon & Rat Fink in the main event and this time… WE PROMISE WE WILL BE BACK WITH THE BEGINNING OF THE MATCH AFTER THE COMMERICALS ARE OVER! Stay tuned!
