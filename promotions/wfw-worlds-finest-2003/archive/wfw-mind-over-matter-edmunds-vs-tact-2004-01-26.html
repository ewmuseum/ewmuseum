WFW Mind Over Matter: Edmunds vs. Tact

26 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 26 January 2004 (BACKSTAGE EARLIER IN THE NIGHT) (Larry Tact sits in a room, with his body from the torso and up showing. The room is masked in black all around Tact.)
Tact: And so the night has come, Edmunds. The night when you will be put to the test of a Champion. Not me, though....you. See, you're the one who's going into this match with expectations of becoming WFW Heavyweight Champion at the Pay-Per-View, but first you have to prove you're still capable of giving a Championship performance, after all the grueling matches you've been in. It's time for you to put up...or go in against Michaels having suffered two straight losses. Tonight, you will be feeling the pressure. You're going to have to work hard if you want to find a way past me, Sean. And quite frankly....I don't think you have what it takes! I think you've been softened up a little, already; not physically, but mentally. Fitting, since this is Mind Over Matter...and your mind just isn't sharp enough to top mine. Not tonight, Sean.
(The corners of Tact's mouth edge up into a small grin.) Tact: And what's my incentive? What do I get for beating you? Recognition, I guess...but that's not what I'm in it for. Not for pride, or glory. For me, it's about raising the bar. Not my own bar, but the bar others have set for me. I'm going to take you, Sean, and make you help me give people a reason to start talking about Larry Tact! It's time they did...and that's why I'm doing something to accomplish that task. I'm not going to just sit and whine about it, I'm taking initiative to do something about it. And once our match has come to an end, Sean, there will be heads turning toward me. Whether you like it or not....whether the WFW likes it or not.
(BROADCAST BOOTH) JONATHAN MARX: It is really hard not to admire a technician like Tact. In a world filled with so many brawlers and high flyers, he is such a refreshing change of pace and has such good matches.
VIC WATERS: How is Tact ever going to beat Edmunds though? Edmunds has been on fire lately.
BRANDON JACOBS: Tact is a smart cookie, you are right about Edmunds being on fire and having a lot of momentum on his side despite his loss at Merrython, but anything can happen on any given night. There have been a lot of happenings behind the scenes for this match too.
VIC WATERS: Spill the beans. BRANDON JACOBS: Two motions were put to Wylde before match from Tact and Michaels but Wylde rejected them both. The first request was that Tact wanted Sherri banned from ringside. President Wylde felt that Scotty Michaels may inject his presence here tonight and denied his request in order to keep things even. The second request by Scotty Michaels was to be special referee, Wylde didn’t feel that Scotty Michaels was impartial and said he didn’t trust him after faking having a brother in a wheelchair.
VIC WATERS: Wylde is showing his clear bias for his friend Edmunds. JONATHAN MARX: I don’t know, they both sound like perfectly reasonable decisions to me. I feel sorry for Tact having to put up with Sherri, but Scotty Michaels may easily interfere in the match since interference tends to happen on every major show before a PPV.
VIC WATERS: But that is one of those facts we are suppose to conviently ignore as announcers…
JONATHAN MARX: OH! Sorry… BRANDON JACOBS: Let’s send this one to ringside and get this one underway! (CUEUP: "Karma" by Diffuser) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, one of the best technicians in all of wrestling today, Manhattan, New York… LARRY TACT!
::Larry Tact comes down to the ovation from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: "Brain Stew" by Green Day) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, currently residing in his summer residence in Escandido, California, three time WWL World Champion….. 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds!
:: Edmunds comes down he comes down with Sherri, enters the ring, and grabs the mic:: EDMUNDS: MIDCARD? Tact has driven me down to the MIDCARD? ::shakes head:: Well Tact, this is it. This is your chance to take on the very BEST of the WFW. We’re out here in front of the fans in this arena, and those watching at home. You better make the most of this opportunity, because its going to be a long time before you’re ever going to get the chance to step in the ring with ‘Simply Sensational’ again. Don’t worry about the result. You can spin your loss tonight just like Richard Gideon spun his loss against the best. Just think, Tact. For months after your poor display here tonight, you can say that you took me to the limit. And while in actuality that may not have been true, just like Gideon, you can say it so much that you might start to believe it. It’s just a shame that its going to be the highlight of your career here.
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Tact and Edmunds lock up… Tact puts him in a headlock and takes him down to the mat! Tact locks in a reverse chinlock.
JONATHAN MARX: By cutting down his air supply, later on when Tact goes for pinfall or attempts a superkick, it will be easier for Tact to get the pinfall because his reaction time will be slower.
VIC WATERS: Tact is the bigger man, he should be using his size in order to take advantage of the thirty pound weight and height advantage he has over Edmunds.
JONATHAN MARX: At Tact’s weight, he can easily slip between doing power moves and technical wrestling. I’m sure we will see the power of Larry Tact later on in the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is trying to break out of the hold… HE GRABS A HANDFUL OF TACT’S HAIR! The ref is telling Edmunds to break but he won’t let go… TACT BREAKS AND EDMUNDS ELBOWS TACT IN THE STOMACH FROM A SITTING POSITION!
JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds is really getting nasty with Tact, there was no reason for that elbow, Tact had let Edmunds go.
VIC WATERS: Edmunds saw his opening and he took it, that is just smart wrestling. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds gets up and bounces off the ropes… EDMUNDS DROPKICKS TACT RIGHT IN THE RIBS AND TACT GOES DOWN! Edmunds picks Tact up and he locks him in a bear hug tight. Tact is starting to look a bit winded.
JONATHAN MARX: People don’t realize that you don’t have to break a rib to cause damage, if you merely compress your opponents chest, they are unable to breath as freely as they need to in a highly competitive match like this one.
BRANDON JACOBS: Listen to Sherri, she is screaming her head off to Edmunds to put him away when he has the chance.
VIC WATERS: She has a heavenly voice doesn’t she. JONATHAN MARX: If by heavenly you mean a first show Amercian Idol reject, she sure does. BRANDON JACOBS: Tact’s eyes are starting to bug out… TACT THRUSTS HIS HEAD RIGHT INTO EDMUNDS TO BREAK THE HOLD!
JONATHAN MARX: Ouch, that had to be painful. BRANDON JACOBS: Both men look much worse for wear on that one but Tact looks like he is the first one to recover. Tact grabs Edmunds in a waistlock…. OVER THE HEAD BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX!
JONATHAN MARX: Oh man, did you see Tact grab his side like he pulled something. All that work by Edmunds had its effect. Tact is in pain.
BRANDON JACOBS: Tact still got the move off…. TACT GOES FOR THE COVER! 1----------------------------------2-------------------------KICKOUT! Edmunds gets the shoulder up!
JONATHAN MARX: The pin attempt was probably too early, but with Tact hurt, he had to take the chance.
BRANDON JACOBS: Tact and Edmunds lock up. Tact turns him around and takes Edmunds down with a backslide! 1------------------------------------2---------------------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Look, Sherri has jumped on the apron and she is yelling at the referee! JONATHAN MARX: This is a blatant stall tactic to stop the momentum of Tact. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds staggers back up after the whirlwind of pinfall attempts…. POINT BLANK SUPERKICK BY TACT! Tact makes the cover…. NO! THE REF IS TRYING TO GET SHERRI DOWN BUT SHE REFUSE TO GO!
JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds should be DQed, Sherri just cost Tat the match. BRANDON JACOBS: Tact gets up and he is telling Sherri to get off the apron…. SHERRI SLAPS TACT ACROSS THE FACE! Tact grabs Sherri… SHERRI NAILS TACT WITH THE LOADED PURSE OUT OF VIEW OF THE REFEREE!
VIC WATERS: Sherri was simply trying to defend herself. BRANDON JACOBS: Tact stumbles backwards and falls over the body of Edmunds covering him with his legs. Edmunds slowly puts him arm over Tact…. THE REF GOES DOWN FOR THE COUNT! 1------------------------------------2------------------------------- TACT AND EDMUNDS BOTH KICK OUT!
JONATHAN MARX: Both men were covering the other, in case like that, it is better off to be safe than sorry.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is crawling to the ropes… HE GRABS THE SECOND ROPE AND PULLS HIMSELF UP! Edmunds is walking around like he has been shot after taking that superkick.
VIC WATERS: When you take a shot to the head, there is always a chance of a lingering affect and so damn close to his PPV World Title shot.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds grabs Tact by the legs and drags him over to the corner… EDMUNDS IS GOING TO TOP FOR A MOONSAULT!
JONATHAN MARX: This is extremely risky, Edmunds doesn’t have his senses yet and could lose his balance.
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is one of the alltime greats, his automatic is better than some people’s first gear.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is climbing up the turnbuckles… EDMUNDS HAS REACHED THE TOP… he looks back to see if Tact is in place…. EDMUNDS FLIES OFF AND HE NAILS THE MOONSAULT PERFECTLY RIGHT INTO THE MIDSECTION MAKING TACT CONVULSE IN PAIN!
JONATHAN MARX: That moonsault belonged on the front of a Wheaties box it was so sweet. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds goes for the cover! 1----------------------------2----------------------------TACT’S FOOT IS ON THE ROPES AND THE REFEREE SEES IT!
VIC WATERS: Tact saved himself there, but he won’t be able to save himself twice. BRANDON JACOBS: A frustrated Edmunds pulls Tact to the center of the ring… HE BEGINS DROPPING KNEES ON THE LEG OF TACT IN REVENGE! Edmunds has snapped!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds wants to teach Tact a lesson, if he is going to break up the pinfall by putting his foot on the ropes, his leg is going to be destroyed six ways from Sunday.
BRANDON JACOBS: Tact is trying to crawl away but Edmunds grabs the leg and pulls him back to the center of the ring. Edmunds grabs the leg…. EDMUNDS HAS ON THE FIGURE FOUR!
JONATHAN MARX: Just when Tact’s ribs are beginning to heal, Edmunds picks another body part to work over to keep him off balanced.
BRANDON JACOBS: Tact is screaming out in pain… EDMUNDS IS TRYING TO BREAK THE LEG OF TACT IN HIS SECOND MATCH OUT!
VIC WATERS: Tact should tap. A broken leg could put him out of action for six months. He doesn’t need this win against Edmunds.
JONATHAN MARX: Larry Tact is a warrior. If he wins this match and Edmunds goes on to win the WFW World Title, Larry Tact will be in line for a World Title shot. He isn’t going to throw that away.
BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is asking Tact if he wants to give up but Tact keeps on shaking his head no… WAIT A SECOND! TACT IS ATTEMPTING TO TURN IT AND THESE FANS ARE GETTING BEHIND HIM!
VIC WATERS: He’ll never do it. Tact’s ribs are still hurt. JONATHAN MARX: The figure four by Edmunds has given him a bit of a breather. He may have recovered enough that he could attempt to break the hold without hurting himself too much.
BRANDON JACOBS: TACT ALMOST HAS IT! HE ALMOST HAS IT TURNED! Edmunds is fighting though… Edmunds almost has it back… TACT TRIES AGAIN…. HE IS ABLE TO TURN THE FIGURE FOUR OVER AND EDMUNDS IS SCREAMING OUT IN PAIN! The ref is checking to see if Edmunds wants to give up!
VIC WATERS: Come on Edmunds! BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS IS TRYING TO PULL HIMSELF TO THE ROPES BUT TACT IS FIGHTING HIM! Wait a second, SHERRI REACHES IN AND SHE GRABS THE HAND OF EDMUNDS! Edmunds used Sherri to reach the ropes!
JONATHAN MARX: That god damn Sherri, that is twice she has cost Tact this damn match. VIC WATERS: Sherri is the best. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is yelling at Sherri that if she does anything else, she is being sent to the back.
JONATHAN MARX: About time… BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is trying to escape the outside but TACT GRABS HIM BY THE HAIR! The limping Tact drags Edmunds to the center of the ring…. HE IS CALLING TO THE CROWD FOR THE HUMBLING!
VIC WATERS: I can’t believe it, he is going to do it! BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS ELBOWS TACT IN THE SIDE OF THE HEAD! Tact lets go of Edmunds… EDMUNDS NAILS HIM WITH THE REVERSE CRESCENT KICK! Tact goes down!
VIC WATERS: FINISH HIM OFF! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is dragging Tact toward the corner…. EDMUNDS IS GOING UP TO THE TOP….
JONATHAN MARX: Tact has taken two hard shots to the head, Edmunds better hit it or this will give Tact time to recover.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is perched from the top… EDMUNDS FLIES OFF WITH THE FLYING ELBOW DROP AND HE DRIVES THE ELBOW RIGHT INTO THE MIDSECTION OF TACT!
JONATHAN MARX: This isn’t the same Edmunds I saw that won the four way, this is a Sean Edmunds with little or no concern for himself or his opponent. He is nailing Tact with everything but the kitchen sink.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is standing over Tact who is trying to catch his breath… EDMUNDS IS TRASH TALKING TACT! COME ON!
JONATHAN MARX: This makes my damn blood boil. VIC WATERS: Edmunds is sending a message through Tact to Michaels and everyone who thinks they are going to get his title when he wins it at the PPV.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds shouldn’t get ahead of himself. EDMUNDS JUST SPIT IN TACT’S FACE! VIC WATERS: Show him who is boss Edmunds! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is calling for the Northern Lights and this crowd is booing… Edmunds picks Tact off the mat… TACT IS GRABBING HIS SIDE AND HE IS FIRING A SERIES OF QUICK PUNCHES WITH HIS RIGHT HAND AT EDMUNDS! Edmunds is starting to wobble….
JONATHAN MARX: Tact is putting up a hell of a fight, if he is going down, he is going to go down in a blaze of glory.;
BRANDON JACOBS: TACT CHARGES EDMUNDS…. EDMUNDS CATCHES TACT AND NAILS HIM WITH THE NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX! The ref goes down for the count! 1-----------------------------------------------2-------------------------------------------------3! HE GOT HIM!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match… “SIMPLY” SENSATIONAL SEAN EDMUNDS! JONATHAN MARX: Tact put up a hell of a battle, if it wasn’t for Sherri which cost him two big opportunities, he would have had him.
VIC WATERS: Close only counts in horseshoes and hand grenades. BRANDON JACOBS: I have a feeling these two will meet again down the road and if Sherri isn’t in the equation, this might be a fair battle. Look at this, Tact getting up to his feet as Edmunds is celebrating with Sherri as his hand is being raised. Tact is walking over to Edmunds. ROUND TWO IS ABOUT TO BEGIN!
JONATHAN MARX: Give him one Tact! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds turns around…. TACT IS OFFERING HIS HAND TO EDMUNDS IN SPORTSMANSHIP!
JONATHAN MARX: Tact is a better man than I am. BRANDON JACOBS: That anger on Edmunds face has gone away and he is extending his hand to Tact in sportsmanship! I knew the old Edmunds would show his true colors. Edmunds extends his hand to Tact… TACT AND EDMUNDS SHAKE HANDS!
JONATHAN MARX: This just shows the level of respect between wrestlers, that despite having engaged in a battle like this, that they could shake hands and show their respect to each other under the circumstances.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is raising the hand of Tact and this crowd is on their feet. CROWD: TACT! TACT! TACT! TACT! VIC WATERS: This love fest is making me sick. BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS NAILS TACT WITH A REVERSE CRESCENT KICK RIGHT IN THE HEAD! VIC WATERS: YES! CROWD: EDMUNDS SUCKS! EDMUNDS SUCKS! EDMUNDS SUCKS! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is standing over Tact…. WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES COPYCAT AND EL ARCO IRIS WITH CHAIRS! Edmunds and Sherri bolt out of the ring.
JONATHAN MARX: Thank god for Copycat and El Arco Iris. Who knows what Edmunds would have done if he had the chance. Edmunds seems to have it in for Tact.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat and El Arco Iris are helping Tact to the back and this crowd is on its feet giving Tact a well deserved standing ovation. Folks, we have to take a short commercial break, but when we comeback, Minion will take on Rat Fink and Manson in a Bare Buckles match…. STAY TUNED!
