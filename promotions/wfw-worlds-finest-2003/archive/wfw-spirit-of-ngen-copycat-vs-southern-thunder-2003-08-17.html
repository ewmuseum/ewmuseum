WFW: Spirit of NGEN: Copycat vs. Southern Thunder

17 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 17 August 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Aug-17-03 AT 11:55&nbsp;AM (EST)]BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome back everyone, it is now time for the #1 Contender’s match for the North American Title between Copycat and Southern Thunder. Do you have any predictions for us Vic?
VIC WATERS: I’m going to have to go with Southern Thunder. He has a world of talent and this will be his big chance to face Manson if Manson can somehow retain and Southern Thunder has been waiting for his shot to face Manson for a long, long time.
BRANDON JACOBS: Southern Thunder certainly is a driven man, but Copycat has really made an impact since coming here and I don’t think Southern Thunder will be the man to stop him. I think we are looking at a future World Champion when we look at Copycat and tonight is only his first step towards the gold that everyone wants, the WFW World Heavyweight Title. Let’s send this one to rings!
(CUEUP: “Turn the Page” by Metallica) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from parts unknown, weighing in at 245 pounds and standing at over 6’4, SOUTHERN THUNDER!
::the masked man slowly makes his way down to the ring to a mixed reaction from the crowd and enters the ring::
(CUEUP: Copycat voice/over “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, LEND ME YOUR EARS!” followed by “My Way” by Butch Walker) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Kalamazoo, MI, the former WWL World Heavyweight Champion, COPYCAT!
::Copycat comes down to a loud pop from the crowd and enters the ring:: COPYCAT: PEOPLE OF ORLANDO! LEND ME YOUR EARS! (cheap pop) I come before you tonight not to announce the forthcoming thrashing I am about to inflict upon my opponent, but to bring to light an important public issue. You see, in just a few short weeks, World's Finest Wrestling will be electing its very first president. And I'd just like to take this opportunity to announce my candidacy in that election. Unlike many of my opponents, coughShawnHartcough...be assured that, if elected, I will treat this position with the utmost respect. I will do everything in my power to reduce the amount of material similar to that of Southern Thunder, and increase the amount of material that you, the fans, can actually enjoy. I will decrease the use of Rob Zombie and Korn in entrance music, and replace it with music that pleases the ears instead of offending them with vile CRAP. And perhaps most importantly of all...significantly less Sean Edmunds on television.
VIC WATERS: He had to take a shot on Edmunds, didn't he? COPYCAT: So even if this election is an internal election, I want to bring my campaign to you, the fans...because that's who I'll be working for if I win this election, and that's who's IMPORTANT - more than ANYONE sitting backstage right now, and certainly FAR more than my opponent tonight. Am I right? (crowd voices its approval) So I guess from now on, you can call the Cat "The People's Choice" in this election. And THAT, people of Orlando (cheap pop)...is just all there is to it!
::bell rings:: BRANDON JACOBS: The two men lock up, SOUTHERN THUNDER POWERS COPYCAT INTO THE ROPES…. SLEEPER BY SOUTHERN THUNDER! Copycat falls down and NAILS SOUTHERN THUNDER WITH A JAWJACKER!
VIC WATERS: Looks like Copycat nearly knocked out some teeth with that one! BRANDON JACOBS: Southern Thunder is holding his jaw… SOUTHERN THUNDER BLOCKS THE KIT KAT KICK AND SPINS COPYCAT AROUND! BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX BY SOUTHERN THUNDER! Copycat gets up… SOUTHERN THUNDER CATCHES COPYCAT OVER HIS SHOULDER AND RAMS HIM INTO THE TURNBUCKLE FULL FORCE!
VIC WATERS: These two are really taking the action to each other! BRANDON JACOBS: Southern Thunder starts railing on Copycat with chops to the chest! The fans are wooing loudly in unison! Copycat begins to slump over in the corner…. Southern Thunder backs away… AND HE CHARGES THE CORNER WITH A SPEAR… COPYCAT MOVES OUT OF THE WAY AND SOUTHERN THUNDER GOES SHOULDER FIRST INTO THE TURNBUCKLE! Southern Thunder smacked into the steal pole hard! It looks like he is hurt!
VIC WATERS: I’ve never heard such a sickening thud before. It sounds like he went in with too much force on that one.
BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT LOCKS IN THE CAT’S CLAW HALF NELSON CLAWHOLD AROUND SOUTHERN THUNDER’S INJURED SHOULDER! Southern Thunder is trying to fight it but he is screaming out in pain! THE REF IS SIGNALING FOR THE BELL! SOUTHERN THUNDER HAS GIVEN UP!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of this match and the man to advancing to face the North American Champion …. COPYCAT!
VIC WATERS: Southern Thunder is a much tough competitor than that, he must have separated his shoulder when he went in for the spear. It will be awhile before we see him compete in a WFW ring again.
BRANDON JACOBS: It such a shame too, he was one match away from Manson as well. Hopefully he recovers soon and will be back in a WFW ring before we know it. Okay fans, we are going to take a commercial break, but after this commercial, there will be a three way dance between Luster, Chris McMillian, and El Arco Iris! Stay tuned!
