Guerra Interminable: Psycho & Michaels vs. Copycat & Dan Ryan

15 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 15 November 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: Now that all that business with the Cibernetico has come to conclusion, it is time for our Partners Incredible match between the team of Copycat & Dan Ryan and Scotty Michaels & Psycho.
JONATHAN MARX: This should be an interesting match, both sides want to tear into both their opponents and themselves, but they will have to put aside their bad feelings and work as a team if they want to win this match.
VIC WATERS: You never want to lose your momentum as you march into a PPV, you may not like your partner, but you want to have that hot streaking going into a big match.
JONATHAN MARX: If I had to place a wager, I think I would go with Scotty Michaels and Psycho. Scotty Michaels is a man who knows how to put his differences aside with his opponents or he would have never been able to form an alliance with Shawn Hart. Psycho is a bit nuts and you never know what he will do, but he has shown a lot of loyalty to LOVE.
VIC WATERS: Copycat may be a snide little weasel who only cares about himself, but Dan Ryan is in his corner and he is a monster of a man. Copycat is the smartest player in the game and he is going to show it by taking full advantage of Dan Ryan to pull Scotty Michaels without mercy and make his path to the WFW World Heavyweight much easier.
BRANDON JACOBS: Personally, I think the dangerous man in that ring tonight is Copycat. He is going to want to rip Scotty Michaels from limb to limb. Dan Ryan may be big, he may have been champion in a lot of big places, but nothing compares to that hatred you feel when someone tries to sidetrack you on the way to the top. Copycat will take no prisoners tonight. This is a war and thank god Psycho is in Scotty Michaels corner because he has been in more wars since day one than anyone in this company. With that being said, lets send this down to ringside and get this underway!
(CUEUP: "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, a member of LOVE and the WFW North American Champion, PSYCHO!
::Psycho walks down to the ring to the boos of the crowd. Psycho hands his belt to the ring attendant and enters the ring.::
(CUEUP: Copycat voice/over “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, LEND ME YOUR EARS!” followed by “My Way” by Butch Walker) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, the former WWL World Heavyweight Champion, from Kalamazoo, Michigan… COPYCAT!
(CUEUP: “It Is My Life” by Bon Jovi) RING ANNOUNCER: And now, a former WFW World Heavyweight Champion, a man loved by viewer’s worldwide, from Philadelphia, PA, THE SUPERSTAR SCOTTY MICHAELS!
::Michaels comes down to a mixed reaction from the crowd enters the ring:: (Cue up: "Zero" by Smashing Pumpkins.) RING ANNOUNCER: And last but not least, making his WFW debut here tonight, from Houston, Texas….. “Ego Buster” Dan Ryan!
(Thunderous crowd pop) (CUT TO: 'Ego Buster' flashes across the screen in rapid contrasting black and white. CUT TO: Dan Ryan gorrilla presses Kevin Powers from inside the ring to the floor below. CUT TO: The word "YOU" flashes on the screen. CUT TO: Dan Ryan throws 'Living Legend' Mark Windham from the second level of Key Arena down to the first level. CUT TO: the word "ARE". CUT TO: Dan Ryan clotheslines 'Cocky' Craig Miles, nearly taking his head off. CUT TO: the word "BUSTED" CUT TO: rapid shots of Dan Ryan pulverizing opponents with the Humility Bomb, a last ride power bomb landing high angle on the neck.)
(Ryan walks down to ringside as pyro erupts along the ramp beside him. He rolls under the bottom rope and climbs a corner turnbuckle and simply glares through the sunglasses into the crowd.)
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is insisting that he start this match for his team and Copycat seems to have agreed to let Dan Ryan start off this match.
JONATHAN MARX: Both World Title contenders at the PPV appear to be saving themselves for later.
VIC WATERS: They want to be fresh at crunch time so they are able to put their opponent away.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan walks up to Psycho and he is smirking right in his face. VIC WATERS: Dan Ryan is too bit too cocky, that is the WFW North American Champion. BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO JUMPS UP AND NAILS DAN RYAN WITH AN OPEN HAND SLAP ACROSS THE FACE! JONATHAN MARX: Damn, he has balls. BRANDON JACOBS: DAN RYAN LOOKS PERTURBED AND PSYCHO IS DANCING AROUND THE RING EGGING ON DAN RYAN TO COME AFTER HIM!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is living up to his name, who else would ever do this to Dan Ryan? BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO GOES IN FOR A SECOND SLAP BUT DAN RYAN CATCHES HIS FIST AND HE POWERS HIM DOWN TO HIS KNEES!
JONATHAN MARX: This move is working over the wrist Psycho, he isn’t going to be able to throw those matches punches as fast as he normally would.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan lets go… AND HE CLOBBERS PSYCHO RIGHT IN THE FACE WITH A KNEE! Psycho is trying to get his bearings as rubs his chin to get back some feeling in his chin and asks Michaels if he wants to come in but Michaels shakes his head no thanks.
JONATHAN MARX: Michaels wants no part of Dan Ryan, he has a World Heavyweight Title match coming up he has to be a part of.
VIC WATERS: Didn’t Dan Ryan give you a hell of a beating at the show after the NFW Allstar Break?
JONATHAN MARX: He beat me with an inch of my life, he is a hell of a physical competitor and smart too. I don’t blame Michaels for wanting to stay on that apron as long as possible.
BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO GETS UP AND HE CHARGES DAN RYAN AND DAN RYAN CATCHES HIM IN A WAISTLOCK AND NAILS PSYCHO WITH A BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX! Psycho tries to get up but he grabs his ribs and stays down in pain.
JONATHAN MARX: All of that weight of Dan Ryan crashed down into Psycho’s chest when they landed on the mat.
VIC WATERS: Psycho is tough though, he has fought some of the most physical intense matches here in WFW, from Falls on the Mall to Rage in the Cage against Doc Silver where they both crashed through the mat from the top of the cage.
BRANDON JACOBS: Ryan grabs the legs of Psycho and he flips Psycho over…… RYAN DROPS AN ELBOW TO THE BACK OF THE THIGH OF PSYCHO AND PSYCHO TRIES TO CRAWL AWAY BUT RYAN PULLS HIM BACK!
JONATHAN MARX: This is what makes Dan Ryan so unique, so many big men want to keep their opponents on their feet, but Dan Ryan can take it to his opponents just as well if not better on the mat and he is working over Psycho for a submission right here.
VIC WATERS: It makes sense though because with most big men, if you take them off their feet, they are fair game. But since Dan Ryan is skilled on the ground, the normal big man attack doesn’t work.
BRANDON JACOBS: DAN RYAN STEPS OVER AND HE LOCKS ON THE STF ON PSYCHO AND HE IS PULLING BACK ON THE HEAD OF PSYCHO!
VIC WATERS: Imagine if Dan Ryan could get a submission victory on Psycho, a man who doesn’t know the meaning of the word quit.
JONATHAN MARX: There are plenty of other words Psycho know the meaning of, like sedative and restraint.
VIC WATERS: Psycho’s style is what makes him the force that he is. He is willing to go all out to win which is why he was loved by the fans for so long before he joined LOVE. Now they hate him for the very same reason that they use to cheer him, because he’ll do anything to win.
BRANDON JACOBS: DAN RYAN IS PULLING BACK WITH ALL HIS MIGHT ON THE HEAD OF PSYCHO AND PSYCHO IS BELLOWING OUT IN PAIN!
JONATHAN MARX: Even Dan Ryan doesn’t submit Psycho, this will at least weaken Psycho down and may comeback to haunt him later on.
VIC WATERS: Psycho is in a bad position, he is too far away from the ropes and with someone the size of Dan Ryan holding him down, there isn’t much he can do.
BRANDON JACOBS: SCOTTY MICHAELS RUNS IN AND HE KICKS DAN RYAN IN BACK OF THE HEAD IN ORDER TO BREAK THE HOLD! The referee is making Scotty Michaels back to his corner and Dan Ryan is up and he yelling at Scotty Michaels. The referee is trying to keep these two separated.
JONATHAN MARX: That save by Scotty was huge, he showing the instincts of a champion. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is getting up…. PSYCHO JUMPS ONTO THE BACK OF DAN RYAN AND HE LOCKS ON A SLEEPER!
VIC WATERS: Psycho used Michaels run in to his full advantage and when you are fighting someone the size of Dan Ryan, that is what you have to do.
JONATHAN MARX: Hack a Shaq lives. BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan is fighting to get Psycho off him but Psycho won’t let go! DAN RYAN IS TRYING TO SHAKE PSYCHO OFF BUT PSYCHO IS HOLDING ON FOR DEAR LIFE AND NOW IT SEEMS LIKE THE SLEEPER HOLD IS STARTING TO TAKE EFFECT ON RYAN!
VIC WATERS: Psycho needs to sap that energy out of Dan Ryan. BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan goes down to one knee finally! The referee is checking to see if Dan Ryan wants to give up but Dan Ryan won’t quit.
VIC WATERS: Psycho is showing the survival skills which have won him both a WFW World Heavyweight Title and a WFW North American Title.
BRANDON JACOBS: DAN RYAN IS REACHING BACK AND HE IS TRYING TO GRABS THE MASK OF PSYCHO…. Psycho is trying to keep his head away from Ryan’s hands but the size disadvantage is making it difficult.
JONATHAN MARX: One great advantage Psycho has is that mask, it is easier to pull a guy around by the hair than to use the mask for your advantage.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is really wrenching in hold and he has a vice like grip on the head of Dan Ryan… DAN RYAN REACHES BACK AGAIN AND HE FLIPS PSYCHO OVER AND ONTO THE MAT TO BREAK THE HOLD! Dan Ryan falls down to the other knee in exhaustion and Psycho is scrambling to his corner to make the tag.
VIC WATERS: Dan Ryan needs to cut him off before he makes the tag. BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan tries to get up before Psycho can make the tag…. Michaels looks reluctant to tag in... PSYCHO BLASTS HIM ONE IN THE HEAD AND TAGS OUT WITH A PUNCH TO THE HEAD OF MICHAELS! Michaels falls down and lands on the apron and the ref is calling it a legal tag.
JONATHAN MARX: Well, he did tag him one, but I don’t know if that is the exact same thing… VIC WATERS: Psycho didn’t get his reputation for being nuts for nothing. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels rolls into the ring and he is yelling at Psycho for punching him in the face.
JONATHAN MARX: Psycho has been playing around with Michaels, trying to get into his head. Maybe he is trying to start something win Michaels in case he beats Manson at the PPV so he has a title shot lined up.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan gets up and he is sneaking up behind Michaels…. PSYCHO IS TELLING MICHAELS TO TURN AROUND BUT MICHAELS IS CONTINUING TO TELL PSYCHO OFF!
VIC WATERS: Damn it, after that punch to the face, Michaels doesn’t think Psycho is telling the truth.
JONATHAN MARX: That usually happens… BRANDON JACOBS: DAN RYAN LOCKS ON A WAISTLOCK AROUND MICHAELS AND MICHAELS REALIZES PSYCHO WAS TELLING THE TRUTH AND HE BEGIN TRYING TO STRUGGLE TO GET OUT! Psycho waves goodbye to Michaels….. DAN RYAN DELIVERS A RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX ON SCOTTY MICHAELS AND SENDS HIM FLYING ACROSS THE RING!
JONATHAN MARX: A lot of authority behind that German Suplex, Scotty Michaels really got some air.
VIC WATERS: This is totally unfair to Scotty Michaels, he is former WFW World Heavyweight Champion and the viewers choice, he shouldn’t be treated like this.
BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels is trying to get up as Dan Ryan is slowly walking towards him…. Scotty Michaels gets down on his knees and he starts backing away from Dan Ryan unknowingly closer and closer to Copycat…
VIC WATERS: Look at the face of Psycho, he is having a good laugh at all of this. JONATHAN MARX: Do I need to remind you what Michaels, Hart, and Gideon did to Tact? He still hasn’t fully recovered from that beating he took.
BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT LEANS OVER THE ROPES AND HE GRABS MICHAELS BY THE HAIR! Michaels turns around…. AND MICHAELS STARTS FLAILING AWAY AT COPYCAT HITTING HIM WITH A COUPLE OF HARD SHOTS! DAN RYAN CHARGES THE CORNER AND SPLASHES SCOTTY MICHAELS INTO THE TURNBUCKLE!
JONATHAN MARX: Good team work by Ryan and Copycat. VIC WATERS: That was a blatant hair pull by Copycat. BRANDON JACOBS: DAN RYAN TAGS TO COPYCAT AND COPYCAT FINALLY HAS HIS CHANCE TO GET AT MICHAELS!
VIC WATERS: Poor Michaels, Copycat is still bitter over the Paradise Lost match, he is going to be even more fired up than before.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat comes in and picks Michaels up off the ground… COPYCAT LIFTS MICHAELS HIGH IN THE AIR OVER HIS HEAD… HE DROPS HIM FACE FIRST DOWN ONTO THE MAT!
JONATHAN MARX: After being squashed by Dan Ryan in the corner, that can’t feel all that good.
VIC WATERS: Copycat and Dan Ryan have the size advantage, they have to figure out a way to keep away from them because if this because a matter of power, they are going to be over matched.
JONATHAN MARX: Michaels is powerful in his own right, but he is in there with these two mighty behemoths.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is grabbing his nose, it appears that he is bleeding. JONATHAN MARX: You aren’t a wrestler until you have officially injured your nose half a dozen times.
BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT STARTS STOMPING RIGHT ON THE HEAD OF MICHAELS RIGHT ON HIS NOSE TO GET THE BLOOD FLOWING EVEN MORE!
CROWD: COPYCAT! COPYCAT! COPYCAT! VIC WATERS: How can the fans cheer this man? He is taking advantage of a fallen opponent. JONATHAN MARX: Copycat has had so many obstacles placed in his way under the Edmunds administration and even the Wylde to a degree that the fans want to see him break through and win the title despite all of their interference. In a a man verses the system, people back the man 99% of the time.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is positioning Michaels in the ring…. COPYCAT BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES…. HE DROPS THE CATS MEOW RIGHT INTO THE FACE OF MICHAELS! Copycat is going for a cover!
JONATHAN MARX: This could be it! BRANDON JACOBS: The ref goes down for the count! 1-----------------------------------------2----------------------------------- MICHAELS MANAGES TO KICK OUT!
JONATHAN MARX: The blood is really starting to pour out of Michaels nose now. That elbow was driven straight into his nose.
VIC WATERS: I know coke addicts who bleed less than he is right now. BRANDON JACOBS: That blood is getting all over the body of Michaels…. Copycat picks up Michaels and it looks like he is going for his patented over the head belly to belly suplex to possibly knock the wind out of him…. COPYCAT LOCKS IN THE WAISTLOCK…. COPYCAT IS HAVING TROUBLE GETTING A GRIP AND MICHAELS STARTS FIRING AWAY WITH PUNCHES TO THE HEAD!
VIC WATERS: Michaels is feeling the sense of urgency that he needs to comeback now or this match will be over.
JONATHAN MARX: I think you are right on that, if he takes another belly to belly, this match may be over.
BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCATS HANDS BREAK FREE! Michaels blinds Copycat by raking him the eyes….
JONATHAN MARX: Eye gouges are legal in Mexico? VIC WATERS: No, but at least they aren’t a nutshot. BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND HITS COPYCAT WITH A FLYING FOREARM RIGHT SMACK INTO THE FACE AND COPYCAT IS STAGGERING BACK! Dan Ryan is calling to his partner to make the tag but Copycat seems temporarily stunned at the moment….
JONATHAN MARX: Michaels has been cheating left and right to win this match, imagine what he’ll do against Manson and Copycat at the PPV.
VIC WATERS: Michaels for the most part is reformed and this isn’t typically how we see him wrestle these days, but he realizes that he is in there with Ryan and Copycat and his partner is a loose canon who could go off at any time so he is just trying to protect himself against these mongrels.
JONATHAN MARX: That is a good point, it just may be my distrust of Michaels back in the old days who pulled off some pretty devious things in his day. When Snidely Whiplash reforms though, it just raises eye brows.
VIC WATERS: He has never tied Lindsay Troy to the tracks, but there is always tomorrow. BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS KICKS COPYCAT IN THE MIDSECTION….. DIAMOND CUTTER ON COPYCAT! Michaels makes the cover!
VIC WATERS: Could Michaels win two straight against Copycat?! BRANDON JACOBS: The ref goes down for the count! 1------------------------------------------------2------------------------------------- DAN RYAN RUNS IN AND MAKES THE SAVE! Michaels is yelling at the referee to get Dan Ryan out of the ring and ref is trying to move Ryan back to his corner.
JONATHAN MARX: While Copycat hasn’t taken the amount of abuse that Michaels has, Michaels had a lot of momentum going into that pinfall and very often that not, when you have the momentum, you usually can catch the guy off guard and get the pin.
BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS GRABS COPYCAT BY THE HAIR AND HE DRAGS COPYCAT OVER TO HIS CORNER! Michaels is holding back the arms of Copycat and he is telling Psycho to go up to the top…. PSYCHO DECIDES TO LISTEN AND HE IS GOING UP TO THE TOP….. PSYCHO FLIES OFF WITH A YAKUZA KICK INTO THE FACE OF COPYCAT AND COPYCAT FALLS DOWN TO THE MAT! Michaels is telling Psycho to get out and he bails quickly before the referee turns around.
VIC WATERS: See? And you thought those two couldn’t work together… they have bounded over a common goal, their hatred for Copycat.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is calling the ref over…. MICHAELS IS GOING FOR THE COVER! 1-------------------------------------------2--------------------------------- COPYCAT GETS THE SHOULDER UP!
VIC WATERS: This would be a perfect opportunity for Michaels to bust out his brainbuster, but because we are under Lucha Libre rules, he’d be DQed because it is way too close to a piledriver.
JONATHAN MARX: That an excellent point, Michaels needs to dig into his arsenal and find a move to put Copycat down once and for all.
BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is telling everyone he is going to put an end to this match…. Michaels picks Copycat up off the mat…. HE HITS COPYCAT WITH THE FLAPJACK!
JONATHAN MARX: Michaels showing some power… VIC WATERS: COVER HIM! BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS HOOKS THE LEG AND HE IS GOING FOR THE COVER! Ryan is trying to get through the ropes and over to make the save! The ref goes down for the count! 1--------------------------------------------------------2------------------------------------ RYAN DIVES AND MAKES THE SAVE JUST IN THE NICK OF TIME!
JONATHAN MARX: If he wasn’t such a big fellow, he wouldn’t have been able to break that one up.
VIC WATERS: Dan Ryan doesn’t want to go down on the losing end in his debut. He wants to show the WFW Board of Directors and the fans what he is capable of.
BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO COMES THROUGH THE ROPES AND BLINDSIDES DAN RYAN! Psycho pulls out a fork and he is trying to bust open the head of Dan Ryan with it but Dan Ryan is trying to fend him off.
VIC WATERS: Is Psycho trying to get Michaels disqualified?! JONATHAN MARX: Psycho may be going into business for himself here. BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO BUSTS HIM OPEN WITH THE FORK AND NOW DAN RYAN IS BLEEDING! Michaels is trying to keep the referee from seeing what is happening.
JONATHAN MARX: Imagine what Psycho would do if we held this show in Puerto Rico. VIC WATERS: Abdullah the Butcher would be proud. BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT IS GETTING UP AND HE CHARGES MICHAELS AND DOWN GOES THE REFEREE! Michaels and Copycat are fighting it out!
JONATHAN MARX: All hell has broken loose! BRANDON JACOBS: DAN RYAN THROWS PSYCHO OFF AND PSYCHO DROPS THE FORK! Psycho charges Dan Ryan….. DAN RYAN KICKS PSYCHO IN THE MIDSECTION AND HITS HIM WITH THE BURNING HAMMER AND PSYCHO ROLLS OUT IN PAIN!
JONATHAN MARX: Down goes Psycho! VIC WATERS: Psycho may be out for awhile after that. BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS LIFTS COPYCAT UP….. HE HITS HIM WITH THE BRAINBUSTER! VIC WATERS: DOWN GOES COPYCAT! JONATHAN MARX: It looks like Dan Ryan and Michaels are going to have to be the one to finish this one.
BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS AND RYAN ARE STARING EACH OTHER DOWN AND THIS CROWD IS GOING NUTS! HERE WE GO! DAN RYAN AND SCOTTY MICHAELS ARE CHOPPING THE HELL OUT OF EACH OTHER!
JONATHAN MARX: I can hear those chops all the way out here. VIC WATERS: They are holding nothing back at all. BRANDON JACOBS: DAN RYAN IS BEGINNING TO WOBBLE…. Michaels bounces off the ropes….. MICHAELS MISSES WITH CLOTHESLINE AND SCREECHES TO A HALT…. Michaels turns around…. DAN RYAN TAKES MICHAELS INSIDE OUT WITH A CLOTHESLINE!
JONATHAN MARX: Good god, Michaels almost did a complete 360. VIC WATERS: Dan Ryan is a powerful man. BRANDON JACOBS: RYAN IS CALLING FOR HIS FINISHER…. Dan Ryan picks Michaels up….. HE HITS HIM WITH THE HUMILITY BOMB AND MICHAELS IS DROPPED HARD ON THE NECK! Ryan is going for the cover!
JONATHAN MARX: The ref still appears to be down from when he got sandwiched between Copycat and Michaels.
VIC WATERS: STAY DOWN REF! STAY DOWN! BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second…. PSYCHO IS GRABBING A CHAIR FROM RINGSIDE AND HE ROLLS BACK INTO THE RING!
JONATHAN MARX: Because of the blood loss, Dan Ryan must not have fully connected with the burning hammer because when you hit the burning hammer, most of the time your opponent is dead.
BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO PLASTERS DAN RYAN IN THE BACK WITH A CHAIR AND DAN RYAN IS KNOCKED OUT COLD! Psycho is rolling Dan Ryan out of the ring!
VIC WATERS: Maybe now that they have Dan Ryan out of the ring, Psycho and Michaels can take Copycat out once and for all.
JONATHAN MARX: This damn referee better wake up soon, this is getting out of control. BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO PICKS A BARELY CONCIOUS MICHAELS UP AND HE SLAPPING HIM ACROSS THE FACE AND IS YELLING AT HIM TO WAKE UP!
VIC WATERS: Michaels just got powerbombed, he doesn’t appear to be all there yet. BRANDON JACOBS: A groggy Michaels picks Copycat up off the mat as Psycho is instructing and he is doing his best to hold the arms back of Copycat…..
JONATHAN MARX: What could Psycho be planning here?! VIC WATERS: I don’t know, but Michaels doesn’t look to have much energy to hold onto Copycat. It looks like the lights are on but nobody is home.
BRANDON JACOBS: MICHAELS IS HOLDING THE ARMS OF COPYCAT…. COPYCAT DUCKS AND HE BLOWS THE MIST RIGHT INTO THE EYES OF SCOTTY MICHAELS!
VIC WATERS: NO DAMN IT NO! BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat slumps down to the ground to catch his breath and blinded Michaels is flaring around the ring trying to connect with an opponent that isn’t there. PSYCHO IS YELLING AT MICHAELS FOR LETTING GO OF COPYCAT AND HE FLIPS MICHAELS OFF AND IS HEADING OUT OF THE RING TO THE BACK!
VIC WATERS: Psycho wants nothing to do with Michaels after he let Copycat break free. JONATHAN MARX: It wasn’t his fault, he was dead on his feet from what Ryan did to him. BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is getting back up…. Dan Ryan is getting up on the outside and he has the chair, he is calling for Psycho to comeback down to the ring and take the beating he deserves.
JONATHAN MARX: Now he gets up, that is just great…. BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT IS GETTING UP….. MICHAELS IS WANDERING AROUND THE RING….. COPYCAT HITS MICHAELS WITH THE KIT KAT KICK RIGHT IN THE CHIN! Copycat is going for the cover!
JONATHAN MARX: I think Copycat finally has him this time. VIC WATERS: Come on Michaels! BRANDON JACOBS: THE REF GOES DOWN FOR THE COUNT! 1---------------------------------------------2--------------------------------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winners of the match….. THE TEAM OF COPYCAT AND DAN RYAN! JONATHAN MARX: That is a hell of a win for those two and Dan Ryan really impressed me. He came into a war zone situation and he more than stood his own against Michaels and Psycho. Dan Ryan is on a path for greatness here in WFW.
VIC WATERS: Michaels got a bad break with Psycho leaving him or he may have been able to pull this one off.
BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we have to take a commercial break, when we comeback, it is time for Maelstrom vs. BAD World Heavyweight Champion Jared Wells in a non title match…. STAY TUNED!
