WFW Christmas Card: Black Rose Interviews Anarky

29 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 29 January 2005 (BACKSTAGE) (CUTTO: BLACK ROSE standing in front of ANARKY's locker room.) ROSE: "I'm here in front of Anarky's locker room, ready to do an exclusive interview... Anarky?" (He knocks on the door, which opens slightly with his knock. He pushes open the door and finds ANARKY leaning in a corner, his head down and his arms hanging over his knees. Both of his fists appear to be bleeding, and the cause of the bleeding seems to be the annihilated locker room. The mirror is smashed in several places; broken furniture covers the room; a flipped table rests in front of a wall that's now covered in food.) ROSE: "Anarky... ? WFW management told me I could do an interview with you... it's ah... it's in your contract. I know how you hate these things." (Silence.) ROSE: "Well we've heard from strong words from your opponents, 'Nark. Mael mentioned several times about your stint in WWL, wh--" ANARKY: "F*ck Mael." ROSE: "Er... right... well on to another subject... it appears that Joey Melton has had no kind words for your past title r--" ANARKY: "F*ck Joey Melton." ROSE: "Um. Well, 'Nark... listen... I'm just trying to do my job here. If you don't wanna talk... I can get out of your hair." ANARKY: "Talk... ? What's left to talk about, Rose? What do I care what any of these marionettes say to me? These singing, dancing corpses of WFW?" ROSE: "Perhaps I can come back at another time... " ANARKY: "And what do you think I'll say, then, Rose? Do you think I'll be more in the mood for your desired verbal masturbation? Do you think I'll come out here, bragging about all the titles I've won and my win/loss percentage? Would you prefer I resort to cutting edge smart remarks like Mael and Red? Who the f*ck do you think I am? "Do you think I have any need for words now? What good will they do me? So I can join the circle jerk? So I can impress everyone with my f*cking resume? What is this, a f*cking job application? "The f*ck do I care? This invasion bullsh*t... Dan Ryan and Mael can't even stand each other... Lindsay Troy's only around for her own version of masturbation, and Sands? Sands? "Meanwhile, Joey's already emasculated Cruise for us, saving us the trouble of pointing out that he's more than happy to bite the hand that feeds him. And my own f*cking partner... the WFW Prez... f*ck man, you ever talk to that guy? You ever really talk to him? Sh*t, man... he just ain't there. Drugs are just an excuse for his psychotic ramblings... bless his soul... "But really... the f*ck am I doing here? I ain't got sh*t to prove to any of these assholes. Why would I? They don't learn a g*ddamn thing. Mael's out there shootin' his mouth off like he buried me. Ryan doesn't give a flying f*ck what anyone does as long as he's entertained. Cruise is just around to make Melton feel better about leaving his protective little umbrella, if you know what the F*CK I MEAN... "Wells and Psycho bathe in false gold and Red is hiding behind a Presidency when he's run out of ideas... "... and I'm bathing in my own hatred... wishing just one of these motherf*ckers would have the courage to end this once and for all. Wish they'd just put me out of my f*cking misery. "But they can't, and so it will continue as it always has. They can't stop me... because they don't understand me. They're perplexed because I don't whip it out and compare... because I don't give a f*ck where you've been or what you've done... no past or future... these motherf*ckers don't understand a word of it. "Nah, Rose... I can sit here and speak, but the words are coming out all weird and nobody understands anything anyone else says anyway because they're too busy patting themselves on the f*cking back for cracking some lame-ass joke. "F*ck you, Rose... f*ck Ryan and f*ck Mael... f*ck their useless sidekicks... f*ck Melton for spitting on Cruise... f*ck Cruise for taking it like a b*tch... f*ck Red for thinking he's something... f*ck me for caring at all... "F*ck all of it, Rose. The only language I have left to give is force. Violence. My disease, spreading one chair at a time. "Talking time is over now. Now... it's my time." (ROSE goes to say something but ANARKY gives him the finger... ROSE takes the hint and leaves.) (Commercial for the new "F*ck Maelstrom" t-shirt)
