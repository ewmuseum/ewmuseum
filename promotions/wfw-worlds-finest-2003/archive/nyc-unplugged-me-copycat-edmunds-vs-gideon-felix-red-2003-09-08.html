NYC Unplugged: ME: Copycat & Edmunds vs. Gideon & Felix Red

8 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 8 September 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: It is time for the main event, any predictions on who you think is going to wink?
VIC WATERS: I’m going with Edmunds and Copycat. They may have not always seen eye to eye in the past, but they know what is at stake here, a shot at the prestigious WFW North American Title.at the PPV. Copycat already has his shot at the title and he and Michaels will headline a card which features Doc Silver vs. Manson. Then, you have Sean Edmunds, who exploits are known world wide, who made a successful debut against “The System” Richard Gideon who is probably the best wrestler in this company never to have worn a title. I don’t see how you can go any other way than Edmunds & Copycat to take it all.
BRANDON JACOBS: I really think Gideon wants this, I’ve seen a certain fire which I haven’t seen before from him and I think he knows this is his chance to ride this all the way to the top. Felix Red has also been on one hell of a hot streak. In all my life in wrestling, I’ve never seen anyone with the energy and agility of Felix Red in the ring. All of these fans out here have taken to him and it is only a matter of time before he wins his first title.
VIC WATERS: This should be a hell of a fight. BRANDON JACOBS: Let’s send this one to ringside and start the MAIN EVENT! (CUEUP: “"If Born From This Soil: Treatments For the Injected Foetu" by Minor Procedure and Shai Hulud)
RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, Making his way to the ring, weighing in at 213 pounds and standing at 6 feet, three inches, “The Boy Who Destroyed The World”, FELIX RED!
::Felix Red comes down to the ring to the roar of the crowd:: CUEUP: Copycat voice/over “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, LEND ME YOUR EARS!” followed by “My Way” by Butch Walker) RING ANNOUNCER: And the first member of the opposing team, from Kalamazoo, MI, the former WWL World Heavyweight Champion, COPYCAT!
::Copycat comes down to a loud pop from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “Brain Stew” by Green Day) RING ANNOUNCER: And his partner, former champion of all the world, from Boston, Massaschutes, “SIMPLY SENSATIONAL” SEAN EDMUNDS!
::Sean Edmunds comes but is jumped by Richard Gideon from behind: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS AND GIDEON ARE FIGHTING IN THE AISLE WAY! THEY ARE TRADING BLOWS BACK AND FORTH! Gideon is getting the better of him!
VIC WATERS: Gideon is full court press mode to win this match. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat comes over to aid Edmunds! Copycat drives him back with rights and lefts of his own! WAIT A SECOND! HERE COMES FELIX RED AND FELIX RED JUMPS ON THE BACK OF COPYCAT AND STARTS POUNDING HIM IN THE HEAD!
VIC WATERS: This is already totally out of control. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat drops back towards the guardrail and Felix Red crashes hard against the guard rail! Edmunds gets the advantage on Gideon and grabs him by the hair and rolls him under the bottom rope! The ref is saying that Gideon and Edmunds are the legal men and they will start the match.
VIC WATERS: You can sense from the buzz of the crowd the anticipation to see these two square off. These two had what many said was the best match of the night at the Spirit of NGEN and one of the top matches of the year.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds starts stomping to the shoulder blades of Gideon, looks like he is trying to weaken him down for the northern lights suplex. LOOK AT GIDEON THOUGH! GIDEON IS REFUSING TO STAY DOWN! He trying to work his way up and this crowd is getting behind him!
VIC WATERS: If Edmunds wants to win this match, he needs to break Gideon’s will. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds grabs Gideon in a front face lock… HE SWINGS GIDEON’S ARMS TO THE RIGH… TO THE LEFT… SWINGING NECKBREAKER BY EDMUNDS!
VIC WATERS: Gideon is grabbing his neck, he really got hit by that one. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds grabs onto the back of Gideon and locks his hands around his head… CAMEL CLUTCH! Both Copycat and now Felix Red have returned to their corners… Felix Red looks a little groggy though. Edmunds it pulling back with all his might on Gideon’s head and Gideon is wincing in pain! The ref is going to check to see if Gideon wants to give up but he keeps on shaking his hand no!
VIC WATERS: This is an excellent strategy by Edmunds right here, this not only puts pressure on the neck, but it also puts pressure on the back which helps him setup his finisher.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon reaches out to try to help move his body towards the rope… HE IS MOVING! GIDEON IS SLOWLY SLITHERING TOWARDS THE ROPES AND THIS CROWD IS ON ITS FEET BEHIND HIM!
VIC WATERS: Look at the look on Edmund’s face, he sees Gideon is almost there and he is in a sheer panic.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds breaks the hold and drags him feet first back towards the center of the ring. That bastard!
VIC WATERS: When I die, if Doc Silver fans go to heaven, I hope I have the privilege to see these two wrestle for all of eternity.
BRANDON JACOBS: You just hate Gideon with a passion. Edmunds bounces off the ropes… QUEBRADA ONTO THE BACK OF GIDEON! Edmunds rolls Gideon over! 1----------------------2—GIDEON GOT THE SHOULDER UP QUICK AFTER THE TWO COUNT!
VIC WATERS: It is still too early go for a pinfall, but it will drain his energy to fight all of these attempts so it is effective.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds gets up and stands behind Gideon…. Gideon is slowly getting up…. DROPKICK TO THE LOWER BACK BY EDMUNDS! Gideon falls forward onto his hands and knees! Edmunds rushes towards the ropes and starts climbing… ELBOW DROP TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD OF GIDEON DRIVING HIM FLAT INTO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: This should do it! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds turns Gideon over! 1--------------------2-------------- FELIX RED DIVES IN AND MAKES THE SAVE! Edmunds and Felix Red exchange blows back and forth… FELIX RED NAILS HIM WITH A SPINNING HEEL KICK TO THE JAW AND EDMUNDS GOES DOWN! The ref is trying to get Felix Red out of there!
VIC WATERS: The ref really needs to do his job, Felix Red just may have changed the whole momentum of that match with the run in.
BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT COMES IN AND GRABS EDMUNDS AND DRAGS HIM OVER TO HIS CORNER FOR THE EASY TAG! THEY ARE CHEATING!
VIC WATERS: Well, the refs back is turned, what do you expect? BRANDON JACOBS: The ref turns around… EDMUNDS MAKES THE TAG TO COPYCAT! Copycat rushes over and covers Gideon! 1----------------------2----KICKOUT! Too much time had passed to make the pinfall attempt again, but that elbow drop was a hell of a shot to Gideon.
VIC WATERS: He had to try. That shot would have killed any mortal man. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat picks Gideon up in a waistlock…. RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX! Gideon landed hard on his back!
VIC WATERS: But look though, Gideon is closer to his corner now, that may have been a mistake.
BRANDON JACOBS: You may be right. Copycat walks over to Gideon… he is calling for the Kit Kat Kick! Felix Red is yelling at Gideon to watch out! Gideon gets up… HE DUCKS THE KICK BY COPYCAT! Gideon rushes him and puts Copycat into a necklock… G-CLEF REBOUND GORDBUSTER OFF THE ROPES! This place has come unglued! Both men are down!
VIC WATERS: Gideon really needs to make the tag here. He has expended way too much energy in this match and if you need energy, there is no one better than Felix Red.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon rolls over and starts crawling towards his corner… he reaches out to make the tag… FELIX RED IS STRETCHING OUT AS FAR AS HE CAN…. HE MAKES! FELIX RED IS IN THE MATCH!
VIC WATERS: Oh man. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is getting up…. Felix Red goes through the ropes to the apron… SPRINGBOARD BICYCLE KICK TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD OF COPYCAT AND COPYCAT GOES DOWN! Felix Red flips Copycat over and bounces off the ropes… TWISTING LEG DROP! He covers! 1----------------------2-------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red simply has the momentum on his side right now. Copycat is the much bigger man, but Felix Red has taken this ring by storm.
BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red drags Copycat over by his hair to his corner… it looks like he is going for a tornado DDT! Felix Red puts on a front face lock climbs up to the second turn buckle… COPYCAT STARTS FLARING AWAY AT FELIX RED! FELIX RED BREAKS THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: Uh oh. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat climbs up to the second rope and Felix Red climbs up to the top! GIDEON TRIES TO GRAB THE LEG OF COPYCAT BUT COPYCAT KICKS HIM AWAY! Copycat locks in a waist lock… HE COULDN’T BE DOING WHAT I THINK HE IS GOING TO DO!
VIC WATERS: He is! BRANDON JACOBS: OVER THE HEAD BELLY TO BELLY ON FELIX RED BY COPYCAT! Gideon just barely missed grabbing the leg of Copycat. Man, did Felix Red land hard on that mat.
VIC WATERS: Felix Red has died for our sins. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is getting up… he walks over to Felix Red… BIG BODY SLAM IN THE CENTER OF THE RING! Copycat is going to a netural corner… you know, it just isn’t natural for 300 pound men to fly like lightweights.
VIC WATERS: Come on, cats always land on their feet. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat begins to climb…. HE IS PERCHED ON THE TOP ROPE! HE FLIES OFF AND HE NAILS HIM WITH A FROG SPLASH! Copycat makes the cover! 1--------------------------2----------------GIDEON RUNS IN AND DIVES TO BREAK UP THE PINFALL! That was the only way they were going to break that one up because Felix Red has been squashed from both sides!
VIC WATERS: Felix Red can take a hell of a lot of punishment. It comes from training in the high risk style, after a certain point, you build up a hell of a tolerance for pain and you become stronger because of it.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon starts laying Copycat with hard chops to the chest! GIDEON KICKS IN THE MIDSECTION AND LIFTS COPYCAT UP… HE IS GOING FOR THE G-FORCE! HE LIFTS HIM UP FOR ONE BRAINBUSTER SUPLEX… HE NAILS HIM! HE LIFTS HIM UP FOR A SECOND G-FORCE SUPLEX….. Wait a second, Edmunds runs in and HE NAILS GIDEON WITH A REVERSE CRESCENT KICK SENDING BOTH GIDEON AND HIS PARTNER COPYCAT CRASHING TO THE GROUND!
VIC WATERS: This is utter chaos, the ref needs to take command of this match. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds throws Copycat over the top rope and out of the ring! Edmunds picks Gideon off the mat and bounces him off the ropes… HE NAILS HIM WITH A HURRICANRANA! The ref goes down to make the count! 1----------------- 2---------KICKOUT BY GIDEON!
VIC WATERS: What a second, neither man is legal. BRANDON JACOBS: A groggy Felix Red is taking the opportunity to go to the top rope… Edmunds looks down and he can’t find Felix Red… he turns around… FELIX RED NAILS EDMUNDS WITH A MISSLE DROPKICK! Gideon makes the cover on Sean Edmunds! 1------------------------2--------------------KICKOUT! Edmunds barely kicks out and he wheels his way backwards towards the ropes. Edmunds is screaming to the ref that he isn’t the legal man.
VIC WATERS: Refs are so easy to confuse. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red and Gideon are backing Edmunds’s towards the ropes… COPYCAT ROLLS BACK INTO THE RING AND DOUBLE CLOTHESLINES BOTH OF THEM FROM BEHIND! A relieved Edmunds gets up and shakes Copycat’s hand… COPYCAT CLOTHESLINES EDMUNDS AND EDMUNDS ROLLS OUT!
VIC WATERS: That isn’t good, this tag team match isn’t over yet. These two need to work together.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat yells at Edmunds on the outside for almost getting pinned. FELIX RED COMES FROM BEHIND… SUNSET FLIP ON COPYCAT! The ref goes down to make the count! 1------------------------------2-------------------------KICKOUT! The crowd thought he had him!
VIC WATERS: So did I, so did I. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat gets up…. CLOTHESLINE ON FELIX RED! Copycat picks up Gideon and throws him out of the ring! We are back down to the two legal men in the ring.
VIC WATERS: Thank god, this was getting confusing. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is calling for the Litter Box… HE PICKS UP FELIX RED…. HE NAILS HIM WITH THE LITTERBOX…. Oh no! The ref has gone down! Copycat is covering Felix Red but there is no ref to make the count!
VIC WATERS: Just when this match seemed to be getting back to a little order. BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES EDMUNDS WITH A CHAIR…. COPYCAT GETS UP AND HE PLASTERS HIM WITH IT! Edmunds rolls Copycat out of the ring and covers Felix Red! He is yelling at the ref to wake up! Wait a second, who is that coming through the crowd! A MAN WEARING A BLACK MASK AND TRENCH COAT HAS ENTERED THE RING WITH A BASEBALL BAT BEHIND EDMUNDS!
VIC WATERS: What has Edmunds done to deserve all this. BRANDON JACOBS: THE MAN WITH THE BASEBALL BAT HAS LAYED OUT EDMUNDS! He is giving Gideon a big thumbs up! Gideon rolls into the ring…. HE LOCKS ON THE FINAL JUDGEMENT ON EDMUND WHO IS OUT COLD! The mask man drags Felix Red out and hides on the side.
VIC WATERS: The ref is coming to! THIS IT OUT RIGHT CRIME! BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is up…. HE CHECKS ON EDMUNDS… HE IS CALLING FOR THE BELL! RING ANNOUNCER: The winners of this match and two which will be squaring off for the WFW North American Title … the team of RICHARD GIDEON AND FELIX RED!
*crowd pops* BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is just coming to and he sees the masked man in the ring. The masked man slaps Edmunds and he finally comes too.
VIC WATERS: Who is this masked man who has such a vendetta against these two to cost them their chance at the WFW North American Title?
::the man slowly peels off his mask:: BRANDON JACOBS: IT IS THE FORMER THREE TIME WWL WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION JARED WELLS! JARED WELLS: Copycat, Sean Edmunds, what's up pals? You guys remember me right?! Sure 'ya do. I'm that guy you keep running away from everytime I come around. Here we are dude, the WFW, and the ink is still wet on my contract. Yeah that's right, I signed a few hours ago. So what do we do now? SEAN, you remember the last time we stood in the ring? I laid down for you after I won the WWL World title. You remember that? After the d-XXX split what did you do? You turned on me man. You turned on me and my fans. It's been years since I wanted to get my hands on you. If I were you, I'd watch your back. And Copycat, we have some unfinished business pal. If I remember I beat you 1-2-3 last time. Again, I'd watch your back. You guys ready to dance? Well RAGE.........................he leads!
...TIme has come To Rage.... ::Jared Wells throws down the mic and leaves the ring:: (CUEUP: Did my TIme by Korn) BRANDON JACOBS: Jared Wells made an impact tonight and he may have changed the course of WFW history. It is a shame a match like that couldn’t have come to a definitive conclusion, but Gideon and Edmunds will face again sometime down the road and that is another story for another day.. Boy, this was a hell of a show tonight, and this was a great New York crowd.
VIC WATERS: Sure was. BRANDON JACOBS: And as good as this show was, I’m not exaggerating when I say that Almost Live may be the show of the year, Scotty Michaels will put his WFW North American Title on the line against Copycat, Manson will square off against a man that he has never beaten, his long and hated rival Doc Silver, for a chance to face Shawn Hart at the PPV, you can’t get any bigger than that… but that isn’t all that is on the show, we two of the young superstars of our age, Richard Gideon and Felix Red facing off for a shot at the North American Title at the PPV, Jared Wells will makes his debut as part of a six man tag as the partner of Shawn Hart & Diablo against the team of Alex Wylde, who plasted Shawn Hart with a chair earlier in the night, Sean Edmunds who will definitely be seeking his revenge against Jared Wells, and the young and very talented Pulsar….
VIC WATERS: That should be a hell of a six man war… BRANDON JACOBS: On top of all that, we will have the victorious Adam Benjamin taking on Demonite in his debut, Luster will be a three way match with El Arco Iris and Dormant, and last but not least, a former WFW World Heavyweight Champion will be making his comeback, a man who redefined hardcore wrestling here in the WFW…. PSCYHO and he will be facing off against RAT FINK FOR THE BAD WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE!
VIC WATERS: Damn! BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho vs. Rat Fink could headline any normal show alone, that should be a bloody, bloody war and as in all BAD World Heavyweight Title defenses, there are no DQs or countouts.
VIC WATERS: Psycho almost killed Doc last time they were in the ring together. This man is tougher than anything I’ve ever seen.
BRANDON JACOBS: Should be a classic. I thank everyone for joining us, we’ll be back later this month with Almost Live, SEE YOU THEN!
