WFW Great Expectations: Mighty Impala vs. Pulsar

20 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 20 February 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back and it is time for a match that has caught the eye of a lot of fans.
VIC WATERS: I hate to say it, but Pulsar is developing quite a following. I’ve been seeing a lot of Pulsar signs in the crowd and people just seem to love his raw energy.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is a very excitable guy, he is very high impact. Mighty Impala is the classic technician. He is very well bred and knows the finer points about wrestling. He is going to need if he wants take Pulsar down.
VIC WATERS: From the time I spent with Mighty Impala before the show, if this comes down to a battle of wits, Pulsar is going to be unarmed. If Pulsar wants to win this match, he has to attack Mighty Impala from the get go and not let him rest for a second to regroup. I don’t think Pulsar though is going to be able to get that advantage because Mighty Impala will be two or three steps ahead of Pulsar right from the get go.
BRANDON JACOBS: This is a classic battle of brain vs. brawn, not to take anything away from Pulsar’s intelligence. It is just that Mighty Impala has been taught at the finest schools and has had an advantage over the common man all of his life.
VIC WATERS: The rich man’s advantage… BRANDON JACOBS: Exactly, and I’m sure these fans here in Anaheim are going to love to see Mighty Impala get what is coming to him.
VIC WATERS: Just because a man is which rich and takes pride in his status doesn’t make him a bad guy. It is like how I take pride in being a Yankee fan. I just know my team is in a whole different class than all these B teams like the Red Sox and Angels.
BRANDON JACOBS: I seem to remember the Angels beating some team in the first round a couple of years ago.
VIC WATERS: Pure fluke. They didn’t even make the playoffs the next year, how good could they have been?
BRANDON JACOBS: It is your pride as a Yankee fan which will make the Red Sox beating the Yankees this year that much sweeter.
VIC WATERS: You can dream all you want, but that day will never come. Yogi had a great quote that he made to Bernie Williams and I told it to Mighty Impala before he went out because he seemed a bit nervous. “Relax, we've been playing these guys for 80 years -- they're never gonna beat us."
BRANDON JACOBS: We will see, let’s send this one down to ringside and get this one underway!
(CUEUP: “Whiskey in a Jar” by Metallica) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from Australia, standing at over 6’3, PULSAR! ::Pulsar receives a huge roar from the crowd as he comes down to the aisle and enters the ring::
(CUEUP: The Cote D'Ivoire National Anthem) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, hailing from West Africa, the Prince of the Mossi Tribe… THE MIGHTY IMPALA!
::The Mighty Impala walks down to the ring in his expensive silk robes to the boos of the crowd and enters the ring::
VIC WATERS: The Cleveland Indians have ruined the good name of Tribes everywhere. *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: The Mighty Impala folds his silk robe and hands it over to the time keeper…. PULSAR CHARGES THE MIGHTY IMPALA AND PULLS THE SILK ROBE OUT OF THE HANDS OF THE TIME KEEPER! Pulsar puts the silk robe over the Mighty Impala’s head and starts pounding away!
VIC WATERS: This is blatant cheating, Mighty Impala can’t even see! BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar irish whips Mighty Impala into the corner…. RUNNING TURNBUCKLE SPLASH! Mighty Impala falls down face first! This crowd is on its feet!
VIC WATERS: Mighty Impala needs to get that silk robe off his head and quickly. BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR IS SETTING INTO PLACE FOR THE RUNNING SHOULDERBLOCK….. The blinded Mighty Impala gets up…. PULSAR CHARGES MIGHTY IMPALA BUT MIGHTY IMPALA STAGGERS OUT OF THE WAY AND PULSAR GOES SHOULDER FIRST INTO THE TURNBUCKLE!
VIC WATERS: That was genius! BRANDON JACOBS: That was sheer dumb luck! Mighty Impala picks Pulsar off of the mat…. BIG SHOULDER BREAKER ON THE INJURED RIGHT SHOULDER OF PULSAR! Pulsar is grabbing his shoulder in agony.
VIC WATERS: If Mighty Impala can take out that injured arm and keep him grounded, he might be able to make this match his.
BRANDON JACOBS: Mighty Impala kneels behind Pulsar and locks in a arm wringer on the right arm of Pulsar.
VIC WATERS: This may look like it only does damage to the lower part of the arm, but there is pressure also put on the socket when Mighty Impala is really giving his arm a twist.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is trying to grab a handful of hair but Mighty Impala is moving the twisted arm of Pulsar around to keep him just far enough away from grabbing a handful.
VIC WATERS: This is what Pulsar gets for trying to trash the Mighty Impala’s expensive silk robe.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is trying to use his power by stretching his body out in order to get his foot under the ropes despite being locked in the clutches of the Mighty Imapala. Pulsar is a good three feet away from the ropes.
VIC WATERS: Pulsar needs to break free soon or there may be lasting damage from all of this.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is using his legs in order to move the Mighty Impala closer to the ropes…. PULSAR QUICKLY REACHES BACK AND GRABS THE HAIR OF THE MIGHTY IMPALA!
VIC WATERS: Mighty Impala was trying to keep Pulsar away from the ropes and momentarily had a memory lapse to keep his hair away from Pulsar.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar has his hands on Mighty Impala’s dreads and he will not let go! PULSAR PULLS MIGHTY IMPALA DOWN BY HIS HAIR WITH HIS GOOD LEFT ARM RIGHT ONTO THE MAT IN FRONT OF HIM!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar just showed some incredible strength there, he isn’t even left handed. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar grabs his shoulder and finally stands up. Mighty Impala is using the ropes to help himself up… PULSAR WAISTLOCKS MIGHTY IMPALA AND DELIVERS A RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX! Impala landed hard on his head.
VIC WATERS: Pulsar has really raised his game to another level in this match. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar pulls Mighty Impala closer to the ropes. Pulsar walks through the ropes and sets himself on the outside on the apron… SOMERSAULT LEGDROP OVER THE TOP ROPE! Pulsar got everything on that one!
VIC WATERS: The bottom of Pulsar’s boot went right into Mighty Impala’s face. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar goes for the cover! 1-----------------------------------2------------- MIGHTY IMPALA GETS HIS FOOT ON THE ROPES!
VIC WATERS: Mighty Impala even in his current state knew exactly where he was and got his foot on the ropes.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar pulls Mighty Impala into the middle of the ring and goes for the cover again! 1----------------------------------------2--------------- KICKOUT! Mighty Impala had too much time to rest.
VIC WATERS: Pulsar really should have skipped that second pinfall attack and went straight into another attack to weaken him down.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar picks Mighty Impala off of the mat… MIGHTY IMPALA COUNTERS WITH A SERIES OF EUROPEAN UPPERCUTS MOVING PULSAR BACK!
VIC WATERS: Mighty Impala is fighting for his life here. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is being moved back….. PULSAR KICKS MIGHTY IMPALA IN THE MIDSECTION! Pulsar locks Mighty Impala in a front face lock… PULSAR LIFTS MIGHTY IMPALA UP FOR AN INVERTED SUPLEX AND IMPALA GOES FACE FIRST INTO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: This could be it right here! BRANDON JACOBS: Mighty Impala ROLLS out of the ring! It looks like he busted his nose when he went face first into the mat.
VIC WATERS: Mighty Impala is very lucky he got out of there, period. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar rolls under the bottom rope and he grabs Mighty Impala! The blood is really pouring out of his nose. He may have broken something.
VIC WATERS: Pulsar has ruined his beautiful face. That is no way for a foreign ambassador to be treated.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar goes to ram Mighty Impala into the apron but Mighty Impala blocks it with his hands! MIGHTY IMPALA DROP TOE HOLDS PULSAR FACE FIRST INTO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Mighty Impala reached back for that one. BRANDON JACOBS: Mighty Impala grabs Pulsar off the ground and puts him in a hammerlock…. MIGHTY IMPALA HAMMERLOCK SUPLEXS PULSAR RIGHT INTO THE STEEL STAIRS AND PULSAR IS SCREAMING IN PAIN!
VIC WATERS: Mighty Impala is going back to the arm he worked over previously in the match. BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is warning Mighty Impala that he has gone over the line but Mighty Impala is telling the ref that he has diplomatic immunity so it is all legal.
VIC WATERS: I wish I had diplomatic immunity, I’m tired of getting fined for parking in handicap spaces.
BRANDON JACOBS: Mighty Impala rips a chair out of the hands off a fan and he puts the chair up against Pulsar’s shoulder…. IMPALA DROPKICKS THE CHAIR RIGHT INTO HIS SHOULDER!
VIC WATERS: Impala is showing absolutely no mercy, he knows he has to be at his absolutely best if he wants to deal Pulsar a loss right here on PPV.
BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is going to check on Pulsar and he is asking him if he wants to give up. After what we saw in the opener, we don’t want to have any repeats of that.
VIC WATERS: Mighty Impala is a civilized gentlemen. He’d never purposely injure anyone and I resent that thought.
BRANDON JACOBS: Mighty Impala is rolling Pulsar back into the ring. What the difference a trip outside of the ring can make.
VIC WATERS: Mighty Impala needs to finish him as quickly as possible. BRANDON JACOBS: Mighty Impala picks Pulsar off the mat….. GUTWRENCH SUPLEX! Pulsar has been planted right in the middle of the ring and it doesn’t look like he will be able to go much longer.
VIC WATERS: You can tell that Mighty Impala smelling that the end is near. BRANDON JACOBS: MIGHTY IMPALA IS CALLING FOR THE DIPLOMACY IN ACTION! Pulsar is slowly starting to get up…. Pulsar falls back down on his knees before Mighty Impala can hook it on!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar was very lucky there. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is trying to crawl away but Mighty Impala stalking him from behind… MIGHTY IMPALA PICKS PULSAR UP BY THE HAIR FROM BEHIND! Shades of what Pulsar was doing earlier with his dreads!
VIC WATERS: LOCK IT IN! BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR NAILS MIGHTY IMPALA WITH A MULE KICK! That should be an automatic DQ but the referee is letting it go! MIGHTY IMPALA IS DOUBLE UP IN PAIN!
VIC WATERS: Looks like the royal blood line is going to end with Mighty Impala. BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES….. BIG CLOTHESLINE ON MIGHTY IMPALA AND IMPALA GOES DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar’s right arm is so messed up, he delivered that shot with his left arm. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is grimacing in pain but he continues on….. PULSAR IS SETTING HIM UP FOR THE BLACK HOLE! MIGHTY IMPALA TRIES TO GRAB THE HAIR IN DESPERATION BUT PULSAR NAILS IT BEFORE HE HAS THE CHANCE!
VIC WATERS: He just missed the hair by that much. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar makes the cover on Mighty Impala! The ref goes down to make the count! 1--------------------------------------2-------------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match….. PULSAR! CROWD: PULSAR! PULSAR! PULSAR! VIC WATERS: Mighty Impala showed me a lot tonight, but Pulsar just wanted it more and he had all the fans in his corner.
BRANDON JACOBS: Both men could have given up at different points of the match, but both kept on fighting and despite their different styles, they adapted to each other and worked over each other’s strengths and weaknesses.
VIC WATERS: I have a feeling when these two meet again, this match could have a completely different outcome.
BRANDON JACOBS: We have to take a commercial break, but when we comeback, we will see a clip of a debuting newcomer and Larry Tact will take on the man whose name has been uttered as a curse word by more than one superstar, Luster. We will be back right after this!
