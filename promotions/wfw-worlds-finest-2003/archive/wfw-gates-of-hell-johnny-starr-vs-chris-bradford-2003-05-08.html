WFW Gates of Hell: Johnny Starr vs. Chris Bradford

8 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 8 May 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: I’ve heard rumors that tonight will be the birth of a new Johnny Starr. VIC WATERS: Starr has tremendous size and he could easily be a major player in the WFW if he buckled down more and put more work into his wrestling. He is going to have do the impossible tonight in order to out wrestle Chris Bradford who may be the best wrestlers in the WFW today.
BRANDON JACOBS: Over the last couple of months, Bradford has developed a huge follow among the WFW faithful for his great wrestling matches. One of these days, everything is going to start click for Bradford and he is going to be on his way to a World Heavyweight Title. Maybe that night is tonight.
VIC WATERS: I’m pulling for Starr, he is big, mean, and tough… the kind of guy who you wouldn’t want to take home to mother.
BRANDON JACOBS: I don’t take home guys to mother, I have no idea what you do. VIC WATERS: Jacobs, don’t make me beat you, it is an old saying people back in my day used before Growing Pains was on the air.
BRANDON JACOBS: Oh, you mean back in really old days…. Let’s send this one to ringside and get started!
::Down by 311 blares over the speakers in the area:: RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from Lowell, MA, one of the best pure wrestlers in the WFW today, CHRIS BRADFORD!
::Chris Bradford comes down to a pop from the WFW hardcore fans and enters the ring:: ::Real American blasts over the loud speaker to a huge pop, fans collectively turn their heads and look to the back::
RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, MR. NORTH AMERICA…. JOHNNY STARR!! ::Johnny Starr walks down to the ring in a American flag mask, red and blue trunks, and red and white wrestling boots::
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Both men are shaking hands in a show of sportsmanship. It is rare that you see such respect in the ring between two great competitors such as Starr & Bradford.
VIC WATERS: Bah. Sportsmanship goes out of the window when you are competing for a chance at a title.
BRANDON JACOBS: Both men lock up! Johnny Starr grabs the advantage and puts on a side headlock…. CHRIS BRADFORD PUSHES HIM INTO THE ROPES… KICK TO THE MIDSECTION!
VIC WATERS: Chris Bradford is really going all out early in hopes to score a quick pinfall!
BRANDON JACOBS: Bradford goes for the Flowing DDT… BLOCKED! HE BACK BODY DROPS BRADFORD…. HE LANDS ON HIS FEET!
VIC WATERS: Awesome landing by Bradford! BRANDON JACOBS: Starr grabs Bradford from behind…. TILT A WHIRL BACKBREAKER! He goes for the cover! 1--------2----- KICKOUT! Starr goes to pickup Bradford…. SMALL PACKAGE BY BRADFORD! 1-------------2----------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: That was a close series of near falls between those two, they know each other so well, either man getting a pin isn’t going to be easy.
BRANDON JACOBS: Both men get up to their feet… DROPKICK BY BRADFORD! Starr is staggering backwards… Bradford bounces off the ropes… HE NAILS WITH ANOTHER DROPKICK AND THIS TIME STARR GOES DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Bradford has the best dropkick in the business. BRANDON JACOBS: BRADFORD WAITS BEHIND STARR AS HE GETS UP… RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX ON THE SEVEN FOOTER!
VIC WATERS: GOOD GAWD! BRANDON JACOBS: Starr landed on his head! Bradford bounces off the ropes… LEGDROP! Bradford makes the cover! 1-----------2----------LEG ON THE ROPES!
VIC WATERS: Starr is very lucky to have gotten his foot on the ropes when he did or this match would be over!
BRANDON JACOBS: Starr is calling for his finisher… HE GRABS STARR AND SAYS HE IS GOING TO NAIL STARR WITH A RAPTURE DDT FROM THE TOP!
VIC WATERS: If he nails this, it is over! BRANDON JACOBS: JOHNNY STARR BREAKS FREE AND CATCHES BRADFORD ON THE TOP ROPE…. HE NAILS HIM WITH THE SPOTLIGHT RAZOR’S EDGE!
VIC WATERS: OH MY GOD! BRANDON JACOBS: Starr hooks the leg and makes the cover! 1-----------2---------3!! *crowd pops huge*
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of this match and the man who advances to the #1 Contender match at Cherry Blossom Chaos…. JOHNNY STARR!
VIC WATERS: That was a pretty fast count, but I have to give Starr credit, he was practically dead in that match but somehow he pulled it off.
BRANDON JACOBS: Starr goes out into the crowd and celebrates… Wait a second, Bradford is upset…. HE CLOTHESLINES THE REF OUT OF THE RING AND GOES TO THE OUTSIDE IN DISGUST! There is no call for that! He grabs the mic, Bradford has something to say!
Bradford: 'Starr if your going to celebrate with the fans with your new image'. 'Your going to do this the right way!' ( Bradford goes under the ring and grabs an American Flag gets back into the ring and hands it to Starr )
*crowd starts a huge Bradford chant* ::Real American plays and Bradford & Starr leave through the crowd:: BRANDON JACOBS: Starr may have won the match, but both men tonight have won the hearts of the crowd.
VIC WATERS: One has to wonder if Manson would be able to stop someone the size of Starr if he somehow manages to retain the title at the PPV.
BRANDON JACOBS: I wonder about that myself. We will be back after this commercial break with Luster vs. Andre Eian, see you soon!
