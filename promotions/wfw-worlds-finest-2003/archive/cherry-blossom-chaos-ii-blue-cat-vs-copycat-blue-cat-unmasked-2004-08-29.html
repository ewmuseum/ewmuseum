Cherry Blossom Chaos II:Blue Cat vs. Copycat (Blue Cat Unmasked)

29 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 29 August 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back and it is time for the long awaited match between Copycat and Blue Cat, if Copycat wins, he will get his long awaited WFW World Heavyweight Title shot against the current champion at the WFW PPV.
JONATHAN MARX: I am all psyched for this one. Copycat has been waiting an awfully long time for his title shot and this is finally his chance to get it done.
VIC WATERS: What makes you think he’ll get it done against Blue Cat? Everytime he gets close to the title, something happens to cost him his shot.
JONATHAN MARX: You sounds like a Yankee fan talking about the Red Sox, you make your own destiny. There are no such things as curses and Copycat is going to prove it hear tonight.
VIC WATERS: When Blue Cat crosses his path, he is going to wish he was the Red Sox. BRANDON JACOBS: I think tonight will be the night which launches the Copycat era. He has waited too long and fought too hard to be held back now.
VIC WATERS: I believe in Blue Cat. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, lets send this one down to ringside and get this one underway! (CUEUP: "Brain Stew" by Green Day) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, currently residing in his summer residence in Escandido, California of three time WWL World Champion with the current President of the WFW Sean Edmunds….. BLUE CAT!
:: Blue Cat comes down to the ring with Blue Lioness and enters the ring to the loud boos from the crowd::
(CUEUP: Copycat voice/over “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, LEND ME YOUR EARS!” followed by “My Way” by Butch Walker) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, the man who is challenging for the World Heavyweight Title shot at the next PPV, the former WWL World Heavyweight Champion, from Kalamazoo, Michigan… COPYCAT!
::Copycat walks down to the ring to a mixed reaction with microphone in hand and enters the ring, Copycat throws the mic down to the boos of the crowd::
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: These is a match that is over a year in the making, Copycat verses Edmunds’ very own protégé Blue Cat.
JONATHAN MARX: I can see why Edmunds likes Blue Cat, they sort of have a similar build. VIC WATERS: This one is far more handsome than Edmunds though. BRANDON JACOBS: Both men are circling each other… . COPYCAT LOCKS ON A SIDE HEADLOCK…. BLUE CAT IS TRYING TO FIGHT OUT OF IT BUT HIS MASK LOOKS LIKE IT IS SLIPPING!
JONATHAN MARX: PULL IT OFF! VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart already lost his mask tonight, do you want to unmask all the masked men in the company?
JONATHAN MARX: No, El Arco Iris can keep his, I have seen what he looks like without it. VIC WATERS: Hey, I am doing the heel commentary heel. JONATHAN MARX: Sorry. BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT ALMOST HAS IT…. WAIT A MINUTE, BLUE CAT GETS HIS ARM AROUND THE WAIST OF COPYCAT AND HIM DOWN TO THE MAT! Blue Cat is pulling down his mask tight in order to keep it from coming off.
VIC WATERS: Smart move by Blue Cat, Copycat was too pre-occupied with the mask and he took advantage of him.
JONATHAN MARX: Copycat is getting back up and this time he looks like he wants to take off Blue Cat’s head.
BRANDON JACOBS: Blue Cat sees the angry Copycat and he trying to get him to calm down. Blue Cat is extending his hand in friendship to Copycat.
JONATHAN MARX: You mean fiendship? Copycat is too smart to buy this one. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is calming down and he extends his hand to Blue Cat…. I can’t believe he is falling for… COPYCAT GRABS THE ARM OF BLUE CAT AND NAILS HIM WITH A SHORT ARM CLOTHESLINE! Blue Cat goes down!
VIC WATERS: That was a blatant cheap shot. JONATHAN MARX: Copycat knew what was coming so he tricked Blue Cat first. BRANDON JACOBS: Blue Cat is dazed but he is trying to get back up…. COPYCAT IS BEHIND HIM WAITING TO LOCK ON THE WAISTLOCK FOR THE RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX!
VIC WATERS: Blue Cat doesn’t seem to have his wits to him yet. BRANDON JACOBS: BLUE LIONESS IS YELLING FOR BLUE CAT TO TURN AROUND… BLUE CAT TURNS AROUND SUDDENLY AND COPYCAT HOOKS A WAISTLOCK AND THROWS BLUE CAT DOWN TO THE MAT WITH A BIG BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX!
JONATHAN MARX: My god, did you see the height Copycat got on that one? VIC WATERS: I have seen a cat fly like that since There’s Something About Mary. BRANDON JACOBS: Blue Cat is clutching his back… COPYCAT LUMBERS OVER TO BLUE CAT AND HE LIFTS HIM UP IN THE AIR WITH A BEAR HUG AND HE IS SWINGING HIM AROUND LIKE A RAG DOLL!
JONATHAN MARX: Copycat is going for the early kill, he knows that Blue Cat is a smaller wrestler and most likely has a longer endurance than he has so it is in his best interest to keep this match from going long.
VIC WATERS: Copycat is expending a lot of energy here, if he doesn’t get the job done quickly, he is going to be awfully tired.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is asking Blue Cat if he wants to give up but Copycat is shaking him around so much he can’t get a clear answer out of him.
JONATHAN MARX: Blue Cat is doing an awful lot of screaming. BRANDON JACOBS: BLUE CAT GRABS THE HAIR OF COPYCAT AND COPYCAT IN ORDER TO KEEP HIM FROM WILDLY PULLING HIS HAIR!
VIC WATERS: Copycat could be a hairless cat by the end of the night if he isn’t careful BRANDON JACOBS: THE REF IS WARNING BLUE CAT TO BREAK THE HOLD! JONATHAN MARX: As far as I know, Copycat would get the win whether he won this match by pinfall, submission, or disqualification so if Blue Cat wants to get DQed, he is going to have to answer to Edmunds
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is putting the count on Blue Cat! ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! Blue Cat lets go of the hair pull… AND HE IMMEDIATELY STARTS BITING THE HEAD OF COPYCAT!
VIC WATERS: Blue Cat will do anything to get out of this cold. He knows if he lets down Edmunds, this will be the last we ever see of him.
BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT LETS GO OF THE BEAR HUG AND BLUE CAT IS LET GO! It looks like Blue Cat opened him open with that bite to the forehead because the blood is dripping into the eyes of Copycat.
JONATHAN MARX: Blue Cat really got the blood flowing, Copycat is trying to wipe it out of his eyes.
BRANDON JACOBS: BLUE CAT GETS UP AND HE HITS COPYCAT WITH A STANDING DROPKICK WHICH STAGGERS HIM BACK BUT NOT DOWN! Copycat was caught completely off guard with that one.
JONATHAN MARX: Copycat is having trouble seeing him coming right now. VIC WATERS: This is Blue Cat’s opportunity to get back into this match. BRANDON JACOBS: BLUE CAT CHARGES COPYCAT AND HITS THE HURRICANRANA ON COPYCAT TO TAKE HIM DOWN TO THE MAT! Blue Cat goes for the cover! 1-----------------------------------------2-------------------------------- COPYCAT KICKS OUT!
JONATHAN MARX: Copycat kicked out with authority, while his vision may be impaired. He still has a lot of energy in him.
VIC WATERS: Blue Cat has the speed and agility advantage, you don’t have to wear your opponent down to the bone, all you have to do is catch him off guard for a three count.
BRANDON JACOBS: BLUE CAT IS GETTING UP AND HE DROPS A LEG ON THE HEAD OF COPYCAT! Blue Cat is heading to the ropes and he is climbing to the top rope.
JONATHAN MARX: I think he is going for the patented Sean Edmunds moonsault right here and taking a page out of his mentor’s book.
VIC WATERS: Edmunds does a great moonsault, but I doubt he could teach a new guy how to hit it perfectly in so little time.
JONATHAN MARX: Blue Cat could have been training with Edmunds for a year, we don’t really know much on his background.
VIC WATERS: True. BRANDON JACOBS: BLUE CAT PERCHES ON THE TOP ROPE….. HE FLIES OFF AND HITS AN INCREDIBLE LOOKING MOONSAULT ON COPYCAT!
JONATHAN MARX: That was a sweet move. VIC WATERS: I take it back, Edmunds is an incredible teacher. He is a chip off the old block.
BRANDON JACOBS: THE REF GOES DOWN FOR THE COUNT! 1--------------------------------------2----------------------------- COPYCAT MANAGES TO KICKOUT AGAIN!
JONATHAN MARX: Copycat has to figure out something to ground Blue Cat so he can’t bust out these aerial moves like they are going out of style.
BRANDON JACOBS: Blue Cat picks up Copycat…. BLUE CAT IRISH WHIPS THE DAZED COPYCAT INTO THE CORNER AND HE CRASHES IN HARD! Blue Cat charges… THE BLUE CAT SPLASH RIGHT IN THE CORNER AND HE PRESSED HIS BODY RIGHT INTO THE RIBS OF COPYCAT!
JONATHAN MARX: That is a move that I don’t even see Edmunds doing these days. Blue Cat is doing his homework to expand his repertoire beyond his teacher’s range.
VIC WATERS: Copycat probably prepared for a lot of Edmunds’ moves expecting that Blue Cat would be taught all of the moves Edmunds has mastered during his three WWL World Heavyweight Title reigns.
JONATHAN MARX: And his WFW World Heavyweight Title reign as well.. BRANDON JACOBS: BLUE CAT IS KICKING AWAY AT THE MIDSECTION OF COPYCAT IN AN ATTEMPT TO WIND THE BIGGER AND POSSIBLY DO SOME DAMAGE! Blue Cat is being down right vicious with these kicks!
JONATHAN MARX: Just a thought, he may be thinking of using his mentor’s finisher the Northern’s Lights Suplex and if he weakens the midsection, it will be easier for him to get the pinfall.
VIC WATERS: Come on Blue Cat, beat him and beat him good. BRANDON JACOBS: BLUE CAT LIFTS UP COPYCAT AND HE PLACES HIM UP ON THE TOP TURNBUCKLE! JONATHAN MARX: What could he going for here? It is going to take an awful lot of strength to do a move on Copycat off the top rope.
VIC WATERS: I hate to agree with you, but this is going to take a hell an amount of effort.
BRANDON JACOBS: BLUE CAT IS CLIMBING UP TO THE TOP ROPE AND HE IS TRYING TO DOUBLE UNDERHOOK THE ARMS OF COPYCAT!
JONATHAN MARX: The underhook will help a bit with the leverage, but this is still a feat. BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT HOOKS HIS FEET UNDER THE ROPES AS BLUE CAT IS TRYING TO SUPERPLEX HIM OFF THE TOP ROPE! Blue Lioness is coming over and she is trying to pry Copycat’s feet free!
VIC WATERS: Lioness is trying her damndest, we’ll have to see if it is enough. BRANDON JACOBS: BLUE CAT LETS GO OF THE UNDERHOOK AND HE IS BEATING COPYCAT DOWN WITH FOREARMS SHOT TO THE BACK TO WEAKEN HIM DOWN!
JONATHAN MARX: Blue Cat might be better off just getting Copycat down and working him over on the mat. This too much of a battle.
VIC WATERS: No risk, no reward. BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT REACHES UP AND HE RAKES THE EYES OF BLUE CAT! Blue Cat is having trouble seeing… COPYCAT IS CLIMBING UP TO THE TOP ROPE AND HE GRABS THE MASKS OF BLUE CAT…… THE MASK COMES OFF AND BLUE CAT FALLS DOWN TO THE MAT!
JONATHAN MARX: Who is it?! VIC WATERS: I can’t tell, Blue Cat is covering up his face. BRANDON JACOBS: BLUE LIONESS IS TAKING OFF HER SKIRT AND SHE THROWS IN TO BLUE CAT IN ORDER FOR HIM TO COVER UP HIS FACE!
JONATHAN MARX: Copycat is climbing down with Blue Cat’s mask in his hand, whoever Blue Cat, he is deep trouble.
VIC WATERS: Blue Cat is getting up but he is having trouble seeing that that skirt obscuring his head.
BRANDON JACOBS: BLUE CAT IS ASKING THE REF HIS MASK BACK! The ref is asking for Copycat to give Blue Cat back his mask…. COPYCAT TAKES THE BLUE CAT MASK AND HE HEAVES IT INTO THE CROWD TO A HUGE OVATION!
CROWD: COPYCAT! COPYCAT! COPYCAT! VIC WATERS: No damnit! That should be a DQ! We can’t lose two masked men in one night. Their mask is their life! JONATHAN MARX: Unless Blue Cat can wrestle with a skirt on his head, he is doomed. BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT CHARGES AND HE STARTS PUNCHING AWAY AT BLUE CAT WHO IS TRYING TO FIGHT BACK BY SWINGING WILDLY BUT COPYCAT IS EASILY DUCKING HIS SHOTS!
JONATHAN MARX: I am feeling a cool breeze all the way back here. BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT REACHES OUT AND HE TRYING TO PULL BLUE LIONESSES SKIRT OFF OF HIS HEAD BUT BLUE CAT IS FIGHTING TO KEEP IT!
JONATHAN MARX: That skirt is going to rip faster than a dress on prom night with those two yanking at it.
VIC WATERS: Hopefully Blue Lioness only uses industrial strength skirts. BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT FINALLY PULLS THE DRESS AWAY AND BLUE CAT TRIES TO COVER UP HIS FACE WITH HIS ARMS!
JONATHAN MARX: Make him spread those arms Copycat! VIC WATERS: Come on this is totally unfair, if it was El Arco Iris, you’d be screaming bloody murder by now.
JONATHAN MARX: I a man of multitudes, AND ALL OF THEM WANT TO SEE THE FACE OF BLUE CAT! BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT GRABS THE ARMS OF BLUE CAT AND HE FINALLY SPREADS THEM APART…… IT IS SEAN EDMUNDS! SEAN EDMUNDS IS BLUE CAT!
VIC WATERS: I am shocked! JONATHAN MARX: How could that be?! Edmunds was on the Jumbotron while Blue Cat was in the ring at Bomb Voyage.
VIC WATERS: It must have been a pretaped message. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is smirking at Copycat…. COPYCAT TURNS HIS HEAD AWAY AND THEN SMILES BACK! A scared Edmunds looks at Blue Lioness who is running towards the back without her skirt and Edmunds starts to back away…. COPYCAT GRABS THE ARM OF EDMUNDS AND HE WHIPS HIM OFF THE ROPES…. COPYCAT PICKS HIM IN THE MIDSECTION AND HE PICKS HIM UP FOR A BIG OVERHEAD PRESS SLAM WHICH SENDS HIM DOWN TO THE MAT!
JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds is really taking a beating! BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT IS GOING FOR THE COVER! The ref goes down to count! 1--------------------------------------------------------------2--------------------------------- EDMUNDS KICKS OUT!
VIC WATERS: Thank god! BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT IS DRAGGING EDMUNDS OVER TO THE CORNER…. BIG BODY SLAM! Copycat is headed for the top rope!
JONATHAN MARX: Copycat is going airborne! VIC WATERS: This is a big risk to take. BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT IS PERCHED ON THE TOP ROPE…. COPYCAT FLIES OFF AND HE DRIVES ALL OF HIS WEIGHT DOWN ON EDMUNDS WITH A FROG SPLASH! Copycat grabs his ribs in pain!
JONATHAN MARX: Those are the same ribs that Edmunds worked over earlier in the match. VIC WATERS: Edmunds need to manage to get up before Copycat can crawl over and pin him. BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT REACHES OVER AND HE COVERS EDMUNDS WITH ONE ARM! The ref goes down the count! 1----------------------------------------------------2------------------------- EDMUNDS GETS HIS FOOT ON THE ROPES!
CROWD: TWOOO! JONATHAN MARX: Oh come on, the ref needs to count faster than this. Copycat has worked too long to be stopped now by a slow ref.
VIC WATERS: Jon, that count was normal. JONATHAN MARX: I may just be a bit anxious. Copycat has worked so damn long and hard for this, I just him win this one and finally get his shot.
BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT IS DRAGGING EDMUNDS TO THE CENTER OF THE RING…. COPYCAT IS LINING UP THE ARMS AND LEGS OF EDMUNDS!
JONATHAN MARX: YES! We are going to see it, it is the Cat’s Meow! VIC WATERS: Oh calm yourself.. BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES….. COPYCAT DROPS THE ELBOW BUT EDMUNDS ROLLS OUT OF THE WAY!
VIC WATERS: YES! JONATHAN MARX: Damnit. BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS GRABS THE INJURED ARM OF COPYCAT AND HOOKS THE OTHER ARM…. HE TAKES COPYCAT DOWN FOR A PINFALL WITH A CRUCIFIX! THE REF GOES DOWN TO MAKE THE COUNT! 1------------------------------------------------------2------------------------------------------------- COPYCAT MANAGES TO KICK OUT IN THE NICK OF TIME!
JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds almost pulled this match out of no where. VIC WATERS: He is lightning quick and he is going to do everything he can to pull this one out.
BRANDON JACOBS: A SHELL SHOCKED COPYCAT IS TRYING TO GET UP…. EDMUNDS DIVES OVER COPYCAT AND TAKES HIM DOWN WITH A SUNSET FLIP! The ref goes down for the count! 1-------------------------------------------------------2------------------------------------ COPYCAT KICKS OUT AGAIN! MY GOD THAT WAS CLOSE!
JONATHAN MARX: I am going to need a nap after this one. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds when he fell back he hit his head and both men are down on the mat and both are breathing hard.
JONATHAN MARX: The action has been fast and furious for the last twenty minutes and both men are simply trying to catch their breath.
VIC WATERS: This is where all the worked Edmunds did earlier in the match pays off, he worked on that midsection of Copycat and not only does that set up his finisher, it keeps Copycat from having the stamina that he would normally have.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is giving both men a ten count! ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! SIX! SEVEN! A bloody and dazed Copycat is starting to get back up! EIGHT! Edmunds is getting back up as well and the ref halts the count on both men.
JONATHAN MARX: How much longer can these two realistically go at it with the way they have been beating each other up?
VIC WATERS: Blood feuds never die. BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT TAKES A SWING AT COPYCAT BUT EDMUNDS DUCKS AND HE DELIVERS AN INVERTED ATOMIC DROP ON COPYCAT WHICH DOUBLES HIM OVER!
JONATHAN MARX: This is a setup for the Northern Lights Suplex! VIC WATERS: GET HIM! BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS HOOKS THE ARMS AROUND COPYCAT’S WAIST…. COPYCAT FIRES BACK WITH A SERIES OF ELBOWS RIGHT TO THE FACE AND EDMUNDS LETS GO!
VIC WATERS: Copycat did his homework, but he won’t be able to fight out of that move twice in the condition that he is in.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat tries to keep himself up but he momentarily slips… . EDMUNDS CHARGES AND COPYCAT REGAINS HIS BALANCE AND HITS HIM WITH THE KIT KAT KICK RIGHT IN THE CHIN!
JONATHAN MARX: THE KICK HEARD AROUND THE WORLD! BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is going down for the count! 1---------------------------------------------2------------------------------------- EDMUNDS GETS THE SHOULDER UP!
JONATHAN MARX: What will it take to keep Edmunds down for heaven sake?! VIC WATERS: Edmunds is determined not to let Copycat win the first war between these two. Edmunds is bleeding from the kick and he is trying to wipe away the blood with his hands.
JONATHAN MARX: It could have been the elbow too. BRANDON JACOBS: A frustrated Copycat is picking up Edmunds…. HE IS GOING FOR A LITTERBOMB! COPYCAT PICKS HIM UP OVER HIS SHOULDER…. WAIT A SECOND, EDMUNDS SLIPPED OUT BEFORE HE COULD GET HIM!
VIC WATERS: Oh man! BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat turns around…. EDMUNDS HOOKS ON A NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX AND NAILS HIM WITH IT!
JONATHAN MARX: I can’t believe, this can’t be happening! VIC WATERS: YES! BRANDON JACOBS: THE REF GOES DOWN FOR THE COUNT! 1------------------------------------------------2------------------------------------ COPYCAT BREAKS FREE SOMEHOW AND KICKS OUT!
CROWD: COPYCAT! COPYCAT! COPYCAT! VIC WATERS: Nobody has ever kicked out of the Northern Lights Suplex! What the hell happened?
JONATHAN MARX: I am being told by the truck that Edmunds’ hands were covered with his own blood and he couldn’t hold on to Copycat.
BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS IS YELLING AT THE REFEREE FOR SLOW COUNTING HIM! Copycat is using the ropes to slowly get up….. EDMUNDS TURNS AROUND AND COPYCAT CHARGES AND LOCKS ON THE CAT’S CLAW!
JONATHAN MARX: He only busts this out on rare occasions. He is bringing out the heavy artillery in order to beat Edmunds.
VIC WATERS: He is going to have to because Edmunds has found a counter for everything else he has done.
BRANDON JACOBS: THE REFEREE IS CHECKING ON EDMUNDS! Edmunds is going out fast! The ref is checking to see if Edmunds wants to give up but he is yelling no at the referee but his voice is slowly fading.
JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds is losing a lot of blood. I don’t know how much longer he can go without passing out.
VIC WATERS: A lot of people don’t associate Edmunds with being tough, but he is as tough as the best of them. He always fights a way to battle back and when push comes to shoves, he doesn’t play games in the ring.
CROWD: COPYCAT! COPYCAT! COPYCAT! BRANDON JACOBS: THE BLOOD IS COMING DOWN COPYCAT FACE AS HE IS USING ALL HE HAS LEFT TO GET EDMUNDS TO TAP OUT….. Edmunds is trying to fight….. EDMUNDS FINALLY PASSES OUT! The referee is calling for bell! COPYCAT DID IT!
JONATHAN MARX: HOLY COW, COPYCAT DID IT! RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match….. COPYCAT! (CUEUP: Copycat voice/over “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, LEND ME YOUR EARS!” followed by “My Way” by Butch Walker) BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat lets go of the hold and he collapses down to the mat to catch his breath. LISTEN TO THIS CROWD, IT IS GIVING HIM A STANDING OVATION!
JONATHAN MARX: ::wipes his eye:: Copycat deserves it. VIC WATERS: Both men deserve it. Edmunds put up a hell of a fight as well. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is checking on both men… Copycat is finally getting up off the mat.
CROWD: COPYCAT! COPYCAT! COPYCAT! BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat walks over to Edmunds to extend a hand… Edmunds takes his hand and he helps himself up…. BUT HE PUSHES COPYCAT AWAY AND FALLS AGAINST THE ROPES! HE CLINGS TO THE ROPES TO KEEP HIMSELF UP AND IS CALLING FOR A MICROPHONE!
EDMUNDS: You know, Copycat.. months have gone by and all we’ve had to listen to was your whining. About how you’re not getting title shots, about how you’re stuck facing mid-card fodder.. well tonight that ALL changed. You faced THE WFW’s GREATEST wrestler. Things may not have gone as planned, but you got your wish. And now ..you’re going to get what you deserve … A WORLD TITLE SHOT! I’m having the papers made up as we speak … and believe me … after tonight, your *****ing and moaning better CEASE … or else I’ll have to take matters even MORE into my own hands.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds throws down the microphone and the referee and time keeper come over to help him to the back.
JONATHAN MARX: It looks like Edmunds finally gave Copycat what he wanted after all these months.
VIC WATERS: Thank god for that, but there is no way he is going to be able to beat Felix Red for the title.
JONATHAN MARX: You mean Manson. VIC WATERS: No, no I don’t. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we have to take a commercial break, when we comeback, Jared Wells will put his BAD World Heavyweight Title on the line against Iron Lion…. STAY TUNED!
