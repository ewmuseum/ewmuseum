WFW Vox Part Deux: Intro

27 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 27 March 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome everyone to Wonderland Park, we are live from Revere, Massachutes for Vox Part Deux! Tonight is the last show before WFW’s one year anniversary show, the Superbowl of Wrestling, and we have a hell of a main event in store for everyone here.
VIC WATERS: War Games: Submit or Surrender, tonight two sides with bitter hatred are going to fight it out in order to settle their differences so the men in both matches can move on to the title hunt having settled their scores.
BRANDON JACOBS: Both sides have been feuding with each other for months so the WFW Board of Directors thought it was time to let these two groups go at it before the big anniversary show so the war wouldn’t plague the show.
VIC WATERS: The big anniversary show won’t be filled with utter chaos? What is the fun in that?
BRANDON JACOBS: There will be plenty of excitement, but the anarchy has gotten out of control so the board is tightening the screws to try to attempt to regain some control of the league. The WFW World Title has been changing hands left and right because of people like Copycat & Luster butting in and that damn group of hardcore misfits. WFW has made a promise to their fans that this match will end all of that and everything will be settled between the eight men in the ring. Wylde has even gone as far to ban Avery Prosser and everyone not affiliated in the match from the building.
VIC WATERS: Wylde is sending everyone in the back home after their matches?! BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde would rather be safe than sorry and you know what, I think he is making the right call.
VIC WATERS: Personally, there is another match I’m looking forward to more, I can’t wait to see Manson & Rat Fink teaming up against the team of Alex Wylde & Pulsar in a street fight match. Alex Wylde has become the Donald Trump of wrestling by holding the WFW World Heavyweight Title and WFW Presidency at the same time and if he can get into Manson’s head tonight, he might be able to beat Manson in the main event just like Doc did all those years ago at the first Superbowl of Wrestling.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is much too smart to even give Wylde the chance to do that. I have a feeling that tonight we are going to see a Manson that we haven’t seen in a long, long time, trying to do some damage to Wylde to use in their match at the one year anniversary show.
VIC WATERS: And you know Rat Fink and Pulsar are going to take it to the limit in order to impress the board of directors to give them title shots after the Superbowl of Wrestling. Imagine if Rat Fink or Pulsar scored a pin fall on Wylde or Manson on the show before the big main event, it would be huge.
BRANDON JACOBS: Especially for Pulsar. But there is another person looking to make a big impression tonight and that is Iron Lion.
VIC WATERS: I was in the back when I saw Iron Lion in person for the first time and let me say, I have never seen a bigger human being in all of my life. He is like the size of two human beings stacked on top of each other. I don’t envy the task of Mighty Impala trying to break his losing streak against that monster.
BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion looked really impressive at the So Cal Rumble throwing out several guys one after one after one. He could have easily steam rolled through the whole entire ring if they didn’t jump on him all at once.
VIC WATERS: Iron Lion is over half a foot taller than the other tallest competitor is and built like a mack truck. He was like the overgrown fourth grader left back on a playground full of third graders. If they didn’t all team up, he could have gone straight to the top and onto the WFW World Heavyweight Championship.
BRANDON JACOBS: Speaking of World Champions, two formers WFW World Heavyweight Champions, Sean Edmunds and Scotty Michaels will collide with Luster and Larry Tact as their partners. There is no love loss between those four men.
VIC WATERS: The stories around this match almost totally engulfs it. Michaels hates Luster for costing him his WFW World Heavyweight Title, Tact is still upset about Sherri costing him his match against Edmunds, Luster is upset at Michaels for using him, and Edmunds is seeking to prove that he is 100% after his most recent trip to the hospital in his only match up before the Superbowl of Wrestling he has to get back into wrestling shape.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds has been the most likable person lately, but you have to feel for what he is going through and trying to overcome.
VIC WATERS: When I came in, I saw a lot of signs in support of Edmunds. Hopefully he’ll be all right because from what I’ve heard the board of directors has a big surprise for him at the Superbowl of Wrestling.
BRANDON JACOBS: I am eagerly awaiting the announcement of the full line up for the Superbowl of Wrestling show. I’ve heard some of the rumors and if they are true, this could easily be the best show WFW has ever done with all the talent that will be involved.
VIC WATERS: I’ve heard those rumors too, any idea how we are going to top next year? BRANDON JACOBS: We’ll cross that bridge when we come to it. But in the meantime, we have a hell of a show coming up here tonight in Revere. Folks, when we comeback, Iron Lion will make his singles debut against Mighty Impala…. STAY TUNED!
