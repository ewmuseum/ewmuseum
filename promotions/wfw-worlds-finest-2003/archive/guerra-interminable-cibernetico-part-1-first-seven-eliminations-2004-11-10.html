Guerra Interminable: Cibernetico: Part 1 - First Seven Eliminations

10 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 10 November 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: We are down to twelve men and this match hasn’t even officially begun. JONATHAN MARX: I can’t believe how despicable Mighty Impala is, it is one thing going after Pulsar, but to go after his sister? Apparently is nothing is sacred with him.
VIC WATERS: Mighty Impala is a high ranking foreign diplomat. I am sure he has his reasons for kidnapping Pulsar’s sister.
JONATHAN MARX: What, he needed Pulsar’s sister to identity a kangaroo? *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: There is the bell and Shawn Hart and Anarky are starting off this match for their teams. SHAWN HART IS WINDING UP AND HE IS DRIVING ANARKY BACK WITH HIS PATENTED DISCUS PUNCHES! Anarky is being driven back.
JONATHAN MARX: Shawn Hart sure has a lot of spit and vinegar tonight. BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY TACKLES SHAWN HART DOWN TO THE GROUND AND HE STARTS WAILING ON HIM WITH RIGHTS TO THE HEAD! Umberto Gonzalez is warning Anarky about the closed punches.
JONATHAN MARX: The rules for this show are basically the same as for WFW except that piledrivers and low blows are automatic disqualifications.
VIC WATERS: We have the low blow rule in WFW, but it isn’t always automatic the way the refs call it, but tonight it will be.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is trying to inch away from Anarky….. ANARKY GETS UP AND HE THROWS HIM INTO HIS CORNER! Anarky puts his throat over the rope…. ANARKY STEPS ON THE NECK OF SHAWN HART AND HE IS DRIVING HIS THROAT RIGHT INTO THE RING ROPE WHILE HOLDING ONTO THE ROPES FOR LEVERAGE!
VIC WATERS: Give him hell Anarky! BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is putting a five count on Anarky! ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! Anarky breaks and the referee is trying to push him back…. RAT FINK WHO IS STANDING AT RINGSIDE WITH HIS TEAMMATES RUSHES UP AND HE IS PULLING THE HEAD OF HART DOWN OVER THE ROPE AS ANARKY IS DISTRACTING THE REFEREE!
VIC WATERS: Anarky isn’t distracting the referee, the referee was trying to get him away from the ropes. It is the referee’s fault Shawn Hart is being choked to death.
JONATHAN MARX: You are a master spin doctor…. VIC WATERS: Thank you, I do my best. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink lets go and Anarky goes to pick Shawn Hart up off of the rope…. Shawn Hart is grabbing his throat and it looks like he having some trouble breathing already
JONATHAN MARX: This isn’t a good sign, Shawn Hart is the captain of his team and he is already in trouble.
BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY CALLS RAT FINK UP ONTO THE APRON AND HE TAGS IN RAT FINK! Rat Fink picks Shawn Hart up….. RAT FINK STEINERLINES HIM RIGHT IN THE THROAT AND SHAWN HART GOES INSIDE OUT AND DOWN TO THE MAT! Rat Fink is going down for a cover! 1----------------------------------2---------------------------------------- SHAWN HART MANAGES TO GET THE SHOULDER UP!
VIC WATERS: That was too damn close, Shawn Hart needs to somehow make a tag before it is too late.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart is trying to crawl towards his corner… BUT RAT FINK GRABS HIS LEGS! Shawn Hart is pleading for mercy but Rat Fink is picking him off the mat by his hair….. RAT FINK LIFTS SHAWN HART UP AND HE IS GOING FOR THE FALCON’S ARROW BUT SHAWN HART IS BATTLING! Shawn Hart flips over Rat Fink!
VIC WATERS: YES! BRANDON JACOBS: SHAWN HART TURNS AROUND AND HE PUSHES RAT FINK INTO HIS CORNER AND RAT FINK HITS HIS HEAD ON THE TOP TURNBUCKLE!
JONATHAN MARX: Rat Fink really took a blow to the head. BRANDON JACOBS: Rat Fink is staggering around….. SHAWN HART HITS HIM WITH THE HART ATTACK (DIAMOND CUTTER)! Hart is going for the cover! 1---------------------------------------------2--------------------------------- RAT FINK MANAGES TO GET HIS SHOULDER UP!
VIC WATERS: Blasted Rat Fink, why doesn’t he just stay down. JONATHAN MARX: It takes a hell of an amount of punishment to keep Rat Fink down. BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart drags Rat Fink into his corner and he calls Thirteen, Thirteen jumps onto the apron and Shawn Hart tags him in.
JONATHAN MARX: Finally, we’ll have a chance to see what Thirteen can do. BRANDON JACOBS: THIRTEEN WHIPS RAT FINK INTO THE ROPES……. THIRTEEN HITS HIM WITH A SPEAR AND RAT FINK GOES DOWN!
JONATHAN MARX: Thirteen drove that shoulder right into the chest of Rat Fink, just below or at the sternum.
BRANDON JACOBS: Thirteen sets Rat Fink in position…. THIRTEEN IS GOING TO TOP AND HE LOOKS LIKE HE IS GOING FOR THE 13x13!
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink hasn’t taken a lot of abuse, but he has taken the abuse in all the right places. Rat Fink needs to wake up and get out of the way before Thirteen can connection.
JONATHAN MARX: Rat Fink doesn’t look like he can get up. BRANDON JACOBS: THIRTEEN IS PERCHED ON THE TOP ROPE…… THIRTEEN FLIES OFF AND HITS RAT FINK WITH THE THIRTEEN BY THIRTEEN (FROG SPLASH)!
JONATHAN MARX: He hit it, now he has to cover him. VIC WATERS: Thirteen is grabbing his ribs. BRANDON JACOBS: Thirteen crawls over onto Rat Fink! The ref goes down for the count! 1-------------------------------------------------------2---------------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: RAT FINK HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! Team Hart has the six on five advantage. BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky is sending in John Doe to take his place and John Doe rushes the ring and attacks Thirteen!
JONATHAN MARX: Doe was so quick, Thirteen didn’t even have a chance to set himself. BRANDON JACOBS: DOE HITS THIRTEEN WITH A FLYING FOREARM SMASH RIGHT IN THE FACE! Thirteen is struggling to keep upright….. DOE GRABS THE ARM OF THIRTEEN AND HE IRISH WHIPS HIM INTO THE TURNBUCKLE!
JONATHAN MARX: This could be the Amnesia Attack! BRANDON JACOBS: THIRTEEN CLUTCHES THE TURNBUCKLE TO KEEP FROM CALLING INTO DOE’S TRAP…. THIRTEEN IS CALLING FOR A TAG AND GIDEON JUMPS ONTO THE APRON AND THIRTEEN TAGS HIM IN!
JONATHAN MARX: Doe may have forgotten what corner he was in since everyone is standing around the ring instead of on the ring apron.
VIC WATERS: John Doe, forget something? Never! BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon and Doe are circling. These two always bring the wrestling and I am sure this won’t be an exception. GIDEON AND DOE LOCK UP AND GIDEON LOCKS IN A CHICKEN WING ON THE ARM OF DOE!
JONATHAN MARX: If Gideon can take out the arm of Doe, that would seriously cut into his arsenal.
BRANDON JACOBS: Doe is trying to break out and he is reaching back with his free hand in an attempt to break the move….. GIDEON GRABS HIS OTHER ARM AND HE PUTS IN A DOUBLE CHICKEN WING!
VIC WATERS: A couple of inches can mean the difference between both arms being tied up and a hair pull to break the hold, you have be extremely careful and with a technician like Gideon, you don’t get those breaks.
BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON DOUBLE CHICKEN WING SUPLEXES DOE DOWN TO THE GROUND AND DOE GOES HEAD FIRST INTO THE MAT!
JONATHAN MARX: Doe took a hell of a shot to the head, that could come back to haunt him. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon goes down for a cover! 1------------------------------------------2---------------------------- JOHN DOE KICKS OUT! Gideon picks Doe up off the mat and he is signaling for the G-Force!
VIC WATERS: This is the last thing in the world John Doe needs right now after taking that shot to the head.
BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON LIFTS DOE UP IN THE AIR…. BRAINBUSTER DOWN TO THE MAT! JONATHAN MARX: Doe needs to find a way to escape and fast or he is cooked. BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON LIFTS HIM UP AGAIN…. ANOTHER BRAINBUSTER INTO THE MAT! IS HE GOING TO GO FOR THE TRIFECTA?!
VIC WATERS: If he hits this, Doe is done! BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON PICKS DOE UP FOR A THIRD AND FINAL BRAINBUSTER…. NO! DOE FIGHTS IT AND HE SPITS IN GIDEON’S FACE! Gideon drops him down and he is trying to get the spit out of his eye.
JONATHAN MARX: That was uncalled for, couldn’t he find another way out of that hold? VIC WATERS: Doe is willing to do whatever it takes to win, he wants a shot at Psycho and his WFW North American Title.
BRANDON JACOBS: DOE IRISH WHIPS GIDEON INTO THE CORNER AND HE STUMBLES BACK…… DOE HITS THE AMNESIA ATTACK! Doe hooks the leg and goes for the cover! 1----------------------------------------------------------------------2-----------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: RICHARD GIDEON HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! BOTH TEAMS ARE TIED UP AT FIVE MEMBERS A PIECE!
JONATHAN MARX: It doesn’t matter when you get hit by the Amnesia Attack, but when you do, it is lights out and you can see its effectiveness by looking at what it did to Gideon.
VIC WATERS: If Doe can do that someone the level of Gideon, he is going to hell of a night.
BRANDON JACOBS: An aggravated Shawn Hart is sending in Rocko Daymon to take Gideon’s place.
CROWD: ROCKO! ROCKO! ROCKO! ROCKO! JONATHAN MARX: This crowd wants to see the man who took out Gideon get eliminated and Daymon just may be the man to do it.
VIC WATERS: Rocko Daymon is one of the few guys who are directly effected by this show being down in Mexico. If he does his Brain Rocker Redux and the ref sees it, it is an automatically disqualification.
JONATHAN MARX: It may not seem fair, but you have to go by the rules of the land. BRANDON JACOBS: Doe looks tried from all the abuse he took from all the abuse he took from Gideon but Doe and Rocko are eye to eye in the middle of the right. DOE IS TELLING ROCKO TO GIVE HIM HIS BEST SHOT!
JONATHAN MARX: Whether you like his tactics, you have to admire the spirit of Doe. He never backs down from anyone.
BRANDON JACOBS: ROCKO DAYMON GOES FOR THE HAND OF GOD (DISCUS PUNCH) BUT DOE DUCKS! VIC WATERS: Watch yourself Doe. BRANDON JACOBS: DOE GRABS THE ARM AND HE CRUCIFIXS THE OTHER ARM WITH HIS LEG AND TAKES ROCKO DAYMON DOWN TO THE MAT! The ref goes down to count! 1----------------------------------------------------2------------------------ ROCKO DAYMON KICKS OUT!
VIC WATERS: Doe almost had him. JONATHAN MARX: Doe has an angel on his shoulder. BRANDON JACOBS: Rocko Daymon gets up…. HE KICKS DOE RIGHT IN THE GUT! Rocko Daymon hooks the arms….. DOUBLE ARM DDT AND DOE IS DRIVEN HEAD FIRST INTO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Doe isn’t going to last long if he keeps on taking all of these shots to the head.
BRANDON JACOBS: DAYMON HOOKS THE LEG AND MAKES THE COVER! The ref goes down for the count! 1--------------------------------------------2---------------------------------- DOE MANAGES TO KICK OUT!
JONATHAN MARX: Daymon thought he had the pinfall there. VIC WATERS: Doe needs to get out of there before he gets eliminated. BRANDON JACOBS: Rocko Daymon picks up Doe…… HE WHIPS HIM CHEST FIRST INTO THE TURNBUCKLE AND DOE CRASHES IN HARD INTO THE CORNER!
JONATHAN MARX: Maybe if he takes some of the wind out of Doe, he’ll be able to get the pinfall.
VIC WATERS: This match is a matter of endurance if you want to be one of the few lucky men to walk out of this a survivor.
BRANDON JACOBS: ROCKO DAYMON CHARGES THE CORNER AND HITS HIM WITH THE PHANTOM TRAIN SPLASH RIGHT IN THE BACK AND DOE IS PRESSED INTO THE CORNER LIKE A PANCAKE!
JONATHAN MARX: Daymon needs to go for the cover. VIC WATERS: Come on Doe! BRANDON JACOBS: Daymon grabs Doe by the hair…. DOE LETS AN ELBOW FLY AND HE CLOCKS HIM IN THE CHIN! Doe is trying to make his way to his corner and Edmunds jumps on the apron and he is waiting for him.
JONATHAN MARX: Daymon needs to tackle him, Doe is having trouble making it to his corner, he needs to take him down before he can make the tag.
VIC WATERS: In order to win this, you have to keep guys out of the corner, every person you let tag, you dig a bigger hole for yourself.
BRANDON JACOBS: DAYMON CHARGES DOES AND HE DIVES TO TAKE OUT THE LEGS…. EDMUNDS CALLS OUT TO DOE AND DOE MOVES OUT OF THE WAY IN THE NICK OF TIME! Doe dives and he tags in Edmunds!
VIC WATERS: The WFW President and former WFW World Heavyweight Champion is in the ring… things are going to get much harder for young Mister Daymon.
JONATHAN MARX: You can’t get much better than Edmunds. When he is on, he is one of the immortals.
BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS CHARGES IN AND HE STOMPS DAYMON RIGHT IN THE HEAD AND HE ISN’T LETTING HIM HAVE A CHANCE TO GET UP!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is showing his smarts, he won’t let Daymon get back up to a vertical base.
JONATHAN MARX: Daymon is still trying to get up despite Edmunds doing his best to keep him down, Daymon has a lot of heart.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds bounces off the ropes…. QUEBRADA RIGHT ONTO THE BACK OF DAYMON! CROWD: EDMUNDS! EDMUNDS! EDMUNDS! JONATHAN MARX: These fans are cheering for Edmunds for bringing the lucha. VIC WATERS: Edmunds style a Mexican influence to it so he has always been a favorite down here in Mexico despite what the fans up in the states think of him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds rolls Daymon over…. EDMUNDS IS GOING FOR THE COVER ON DAYMON! The ref goes down for the count! 1-----------------------------------------2------------------------------------ DAYMON KICKS OUT!
JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds needs to work him over some more. VIC WATERS: He needs to weaken his midsection and set him up for the Northern Lights Suplex.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds picks up Daymon… EDMUNDS STARTS NAILING DAYMON WITH A SERIES OF KNEELIFTS AND HE DRIVING DAYMON BACK INTO THE CORNER!
VIC WATERS: Daymon may not have known Sean Edmunds before he came to WFW, but he’ll sure know him after.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds picks up Daymon…. HE LIFTS HIM UP AND SETS HIM ON THE TOP ROPE! Edmunds is climbing up the turnbuckle…
JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds is known for his double underhook suplex and I have a feeling we are going to see it shortly.
VIC WATERS: Daymon has a superplex of his own so you can bet he knows what is coming. BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS IS CLIMBING UP THE TURNBUCKLE AND HE IS SET AT THE TOP ROPE….. EDMUNDS HOOKS THE ARMS OF DAYMON AND HE IS GOING FOR THE SUPERPLEX…. NO! DAYMON HAS LOCKED HIS FEET UNDER THE ROPES AND IS REFUSING TO GO DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Damon knows he is toast if Edmunds hits this after all the abuse he has taken. BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS IS TRYING AGAIN BUT DAYMON CONTINUES TO HOLD ON! Edmunds lets go of the underhook and he starts railing back on Daymon….. DAYMON HITS EDMUNDS WITH THE HAND OF GOD AND EDMUNDS FALLS DOWN TO THE MAT!
JONATHAN MARX: That is a hell of a right punch he has there. BRANDON JACOBS: Daymon is climbing up to the top rope….. ALABAMA JAM ONTO EDMUNDS! Daymon hooks the leg and goes for the cover!
VIC WATERS: NO DAMNIT! NOT THE WFW PRESIDENT! BRANDON JACOBS: THE REF GOES DOWN FOR THE COUNT! 1--------------------------------------------------2------------------------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: SEAN EDMUNDS HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! Team Hart has the five on four advantage!
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky is sending Mike Lane to take Edmunds’ place and Lane enters the ring and he asking Daymon to tag Golem in…. DAYMON WALKS OVER AND HE TAGS IN GOLEM!
JONATHAN MARX: This is it, we are finally going to see these two go at it to a finish right here in Mexico and this crowd is eating it up.
VIC WATERS: Show him who is boss Lane. BRANDON JACOBS: Golem jumps onto the apron and enters…. NO! LANE CHARGES GOLEM AND HE KICKS IN THE GROIN AS HE ENTERS THE RING! The referee is yelling at him telling him that it is a disqualification.
JONATHAN MARX: Maybe Lane didn’t know…. BRANDON JACOBS: Golem is doubled over in pain….. LANE DRAGS GOLEM TO THE CENTER OF THE RING….. GOD LORD, HE IS PICKING UP FOR A PILEDRIVER!
VIC WATERS: Lane is looking to get himself disqualified and he is going to leave his mark on Golem in process.
BRANDON JACOBS: MIKE LANE HITS THE PILEDRIVER ON GOLEM AND THE REFEREE IS CALLING FOR THE DQ ON LANE!
RING ANNOUNCER: MIKE LANE HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! Team Hart now has the five on three advantage!
BRANDON JACOBS: Lane smirks and he is leaving the ring as Luster is coming in to tend to Golem to help him out of the ring….. ANARKY IS YELLING AT LANE AS HE LEAVES AND ANARKY IS GOING TO ENTER THE RING TO TAKE LANE’S PLACE!
VIC WATERS: Lane has put his team at a hell of a disadvantage. Team Anarky has no room for error or this is going to over in a heart beat.
JONATHAN MARX: That is probably why Anarky went in there himself in order to make sure there were no more mess ups.
BRANDON JACOBS: The referee and Luster and helping Golem out of the ring….. ANARKY ATTACKS LUSTER FROM BEHIND!
JONATHAN MARX: Luster has been on a hell of a roll since he came back, he has even beaten Shawn Hart.
VIC WATERS: Luster has a way of turning everything topsy turvy that he touches. BRANDON JACOBS: Team Hart pulls Golem out and they are making sure that he is okay. ANARKY IS POUNDING THE SNOT OUT OF LUSTER!
VIC WATERS: That is a considerable amount considering this is flu season. JONATHAN MARX: Anarky is in a desperate situation. He needs this pin badly if his team has any chance to win.
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky grabs the arm of Luster…. ANARKY WHIPS LUSTER OFF THE ROPES AND HE NAILS HIM WITH A FLYING FOREARM WHICH KNOCKS LUSTER OFF HIS FEET!
CROWD: LUSTER! LUSTER! LUSTER! JONATHAN MARX: Imagine how big a win by Luster over Anarky would be. It could set him on a fast track for the WFW World Heavyweight Title.
VIC WATERS: Come on Anarky! BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky goes to pick up Luster…. LUSTER RAKES HIM IN THE EYES TO KEEP ANARKY AWAY FROM HIM!
JONATHAN MARX: Cheap, but effective. VIC WATERS: This is a war, you need to do everything and anything to win. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster gets up…. LUSTER BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND HE KNEE LIFTS ANARKY RIGHT IN THE MIDSECTION!
JONATHAN MARX: That was dangerously close to being a low blow. VIC WATERS: Anarky looks like he is trying to get his wind back right now. BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER RUSHES ANARKY AND HE HOOKS THE ARM AND LEG….. CAPTURE SUPLEX BY LUSTER!
JONATHAN MARX: Luster is a powerful man. VIC WATERS: Anarky has a lot resting on his shoulders. He can’t be beaten by Luster. BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER MAKES THE COVER ON ANARKY! THE REF GOES DOWN TO COUNT! 1-------------------------------------------------2----------------------------------- ANARKY FINDS A WAY TO KICK OUT!
VIC WATERS: That was way too close. JONATHAN MARX: Luster needs to keep on him, if he lets Anarky up for a moment, he could lose all the momentum he has worked for.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster gets up…. HE DROPS AN ELBOW RIGHT ON THE FACE OF ANARKY! THEN ANOTHER! AND ANOTHER!
JONATHAN MARX: Luster is relentless with those elbows, and he needs to be! BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER IS GOING FOR ANOTHER COVER! 1--------------------------------------2------------------------- ANARKY KICKS OUT AGAIN!
JONATHAN MARX: Anarky keeps on fighting. He won’t let himself get pinned when his team needs him the most.
VIC WATERS: He better becareful or he will be. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster picks Anarky up off the mat… he is going to need a bigger move to put Anarky away if he wants to win this….. LUSTER WHIPS ANARKY OFF OF THE ROPES…. LUSTER GOES FOR A SPINEBUSTER BUT ANARKY DUCKS UNDERNEATH!
JONATHAN MARX: Close call for Anarky! BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY HITS LUSTER WITH A SHOULDER BLOCK AND LUSTER FALLS THROUGH THE ROPES AND OUT TO THE FLOOR RIGHT IN FRONT OF DOE AND FELIX RED! Team Anarky is stomping away on Luster as the ref is yelling at Anarky for knocking him out of the ring.
JONATHAN MARX: Anarky is yelling right back at the referee and he is buying his guys some time to work over Luster. This is despicable!
BRANDON JACOBS: Doe is removing the padding at ringside…. FELIX RED PICKS UP LUSTER….. ECSTASY TO AGONY RIGHT ONTO THE CONCRETE FOR LUSTER!
JONATHAN MARX: Poor Luster, he doesn’t deserve this. BRANDON JACOBS: Doe is putting back the mat and Felix Red rolls Luster back into the ring. Members of Team Hart are coming over to warn Felix Red and Doe to stay away from their guy or there will be hell to pay.
VIC WATERS: It is a little too late for that, isn’t it? BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky stops mouthing off to the referee…. ANARKY IS CALLING FOR THE CHAOS BREAKER! Anarky grabs the neck of Luster and turns toward Hart…. LUSTER IS TRYING TO FIGHT HIS WAY OUT BUT ANARKY HAS HIM THE NECK!
JONATHAN MARX: Luster is in a real dangerous situation, he could break his neck if he isn’t careful.
BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER IS TRYING TO GRAB FOR THE ROPES…. DOE REACHES IN AND GRABS LUSTER’S FOOT TO KEEP HIM STILL OUT OF THE REF’S SIGHT…. ANARKY HITS THE CHAOS BREAKER ON LUSTER! Anarky lays back over Luster! 1--------------------------------------------------2------------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: LUSTER HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! Team Hart has a four on three advantage! The surviving members of Team Hart consist of Shawn Hart, Golem, Thirteen, and Rocko Daymon to the three surviving members Team Anarky is Anarky, Felix Red, and John Doe.
BRANDON JACOBS: There have been seven eliminations so far, who of these seven men will survive? We will take a short five minute halftime and when we comeback, we will find out how many of these seven men will survive, STAY TUNED!
