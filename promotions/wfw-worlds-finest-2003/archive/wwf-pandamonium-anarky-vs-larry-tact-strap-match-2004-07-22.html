WWF Pandamonium: Anarky vs. Larry Tact (Strap Match)

22 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 22 July 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back and it time finally for the strap match between Anarky and Larry Tact.
JONATHAN MARX: What are the official rules of the strap? BRANDON JACOBS: I am glad you asked, the rules of the match are that in order to win the match, each man has to drag their opponent to each corner and touch the turnbuckle. In order to win the match, the person has to touch all four corners in succession without their opponent breaking their momentum.
VIC WATERS: Sounds easy enough… BRANDON JACOBS: While by beaten down by the strap that connects them… JONATHAN MARX: That has to hurt. VIC WATERS: What did these two men do to warrant a strap match? BRANDON JACOBS: Larry Tact ran down in order to save El Arco Iris and Anarky became irate at Tact for getting in his business. This match is in order for these two to settle their score.
VIC WATERS: It would see seem like this match would favor Anarky. JONATHAN MARX: People don’t know this about Tact, but he is a versatile wrestler. He may be a great technician in the ring, but he can take it to you if he wants like nobody’s business. Tact is a truly one of the most under looked performers today and may someday be a great World Champion here just like he was in NEW.
BRANDON JACOBS: We can only hope. VIC WATERS: But if Tact ever wants to get a shot at the World Title, he has to get passed LOVE. Look at how they bogged Copycat down for months and months and kept him away from the title…
BRANDON JACOBS: If LOVE wasn’t trying to win the World Title from Manson, I would almost think they are on his payroll distracting his future opponents from the ultimate prize in all of wrestling, the WFW World Heavyweight Title belt.
JONATHAN MARX: Manson would never form an alliance in order to win the World… oh wait, nevermind.
VIC WATERS: Exactly. BRANDON JACOBS: Who do you guy favor in this match? JONATHAN MARX: Naturally I am pulling for Larry Tact, he is a hell of a wrestler and one of the best wrestlers I have ever faced.
VIC WATERS: Come on though Jon, this is Anarky’s kind of match, how is Tact ever going to be able to over come the circumstances to win?
JONATHAN MARX: Larry Tact is fresh off his big win of the former WFW World Heavyweight Champion Edmunds and Anarky did just lose to Iron Lion. Larry Tact has all of the momentum coming into the match. I have to admit it is an uphill battle, but he can clearly win it.
VIC WATERS: We will see. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, lets end all of this talk and send this match down to ringside and get this underway!
(CUEUP: "Karma" by Diffuser) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, one of the best technicians in all of wrestling today, Manhattan, New York… LARRY TACT!
::Larry Tact comes down to a loud ovation from the crowd and enters the ring:: CUEUP: Military Marching Music) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, a world champion ten times over and former Mr. Vicious title holder, from Hartford, Connecticut… ANARKY!
::Anarky comes down to the ring to the loud boos of the crowd:: BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is attaching to the strap to both men and Anarky is throwing verbal jabs at his opponent Tact.
JONATHAN MARX: Some of those words sound like the seven you can’t hear on TV… VIC WATERS: They are too low to hear, thankfully I can read lips. BRANDON JACOBS: The referee has both men attached! *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: THERE IS THE BELL AND THIS MATCH IS UNDERWAY! Anarky and Tact lock up…. TACT GOES BEHIND WITH A WAISTLOCK! Anarky starts throwing elbows back hoping to catch Tact flush in the face!
JONATHAN MARX: It is too early to go for a suplex, but a good waistlock could be transitioned in any one of numerous moves.
BRANDON JACOBS: TACT CATCHES THE ARM OF ANARKY AND LOCKS IN A CHICKEN WING HOLD! JONATHAN MARX: Tact is going to work on the arms of Anarky, this is going to make it harder for him later on when he goes for the swinging neckbreaker and Chaos Breaker in order to hold him in right.
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky is cursing at Tact to let go of his arm of he is going to be sorry…. WAIT, TACT IS THE SLACK ON THE STRAP AND HE IS PUTTING IT AROUND ANARKY’S NECK WHILE HE IS STILL LOCKED IN THE CHICKEN WING FOR A COMBINATION CHOKE/CHICKEN WING!
VIC WATERS: Anarky is trying to dig that free hand under the strap in order to aleve the leverage he has on him!
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky is screaming to the referee about the choke but the referee can’t do anything because chokes are perfectly legal…. TACT IS DRAGGING ANARKY BACKWARDS TOWARDS THE CORNER…. But Tact doesn’t have a free hand to touch the top turnbuckle!
JONATHAN MARX: That is the problem with a move like this, if you don’t have a free hand, it is useless to you when you in a strap match.
VIC WATERS: Haw haw… BRANDON JACOBS: TACT IS PUTTING THE STRAP THAT HE IS CHOKING ANAKARY WITH IN HIS TEETH! JONATHAN MARX: Good lord, this man is brilliant! BRANDON JACOBS: TACT REACHES BACK AND HE HITS THE FIRST TURNBUCKLE! VIC WATERS: He has one, but he is never going to be able to get all four in a row… BRANDON JACOBS: Tact is dragging him to the next turnbuckle and Anarky is starting to drag his feet in order to slow Tact down… TACT IS REACHING BACK…. HE IS JUST A LITTLE BIT SHY ON THE SECOND ONE!
JONATHAN MARX: He has to drag Anarky a bit further if he is going to get the second turnbuckle…
BRANDON JACOBS: TACT IS TRYING TO DRAG ANARKY CLOSER TO THE CORNER…. TACT REACHES FOR THE SECOND TURNBUCKLE… HE HITS IT BUT ANARKY MANAGES TO BREAK FREE!
VIC WATERS: Tact lost his grip when he was extending himself. BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY TURNS AROUND AND HE STARTS FIRING RIGHT HANDS RIGHT INTO THE FACE OF TACT AND HE IS BACKING TACT INTO THE CORNER!
JONATHAN MARX: Anarky is just unleashing some bombs Tact’s way! BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY GRABS THE HEAD OF TACT AND HE RAMS IT INTO THE TURNBUCKLE AND TOUCHES THE BUCKLE!
VIC WATERS: That is one! BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY GRABS TACT BY THE HAIR! Anarky drags him to the second turnbuckle…. HE RAMS TACT’S HEAD INTO THE SECOND TURNBUCKLE AND TOUCHES THE BUCKLE!
VIC WATERS: That is two! JONATHAN MARX: Good lord, it can’t end this way… BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky is dragging a dazed Tact towards the third turnbuckle…. ANARKY RAMS TACT’S HEAD INTO THE THIRD TURNBUCKLE AND ANARKY TOUCHES THE BUCKLE!
JONATHAN MARX: OH MAN! BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY IS ONE TURNBUCKLE AWAY! Anarky grabs Tact by the hair… TACT GRABS A SLACK OF THE ROPES AND HE CATCHES ANARKY BACK IN A CHOKE!
VIC WATERS: Damn it! BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second, ANARKY IS DRAGGING TACT ON HIS BACK TOWARDS THE FOURTH AND DECIDING TURNBUCKLE…. ANARKY IS REACHING OUT FOR THE BUCKLE BUT HE IS GOOD THREE FEET SHORT!
CROWD: LETS GO TACT! LETS GO TACT! LETS GO TACT! BRANDON JACOBS: TACT MANAGES TO PULL ANARKY BACK TOWARDS THE PREVIOUS TURNBUCKLE…. TACT TOUCHES THE BUCKLE WHILE DRAGGING ANARKY AND HE BREAKS THE MOMENTUM!
JONATHAN MARX: That is one… VIC WATERS: Aww damn… BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY MANAGES TO ELBOW TACT IN THE RIBS! Tact is doubled over in pain…. JONATHAN MARX: Very few people realized how painful an elbow to the ribs is, your elbow is the hardest parts of the human body.
VIC WATERS: Next to an another unmentionable part on Beau Michaels while wrestling Peter File and Madonna Wayne Grossard…
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky bounces off the ropes…. ANARKY HITS TACT WITH A FLYING FOREARM AND TACT GOES UP AND OVER THE TOP ROPE!
JONATHAN MARX: Tact got clocked with that one. VIC WATERS: Now, if these two are on the outside and they managed to touch all four corners, does that count?
BRANDON JACOBS: Yes, but the person with momentum has to touch each turnbuckle afterward so unless they are a giant, they can’t really do that from the outside of the ring.
VIC WATERS: Rats. BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY GRABS THE ARM OF TACT AND HE WHIPS HIM HARD INTO THE STEEL GUARD RAIL! Tact goes back first in and he is clutching his lower back in pain.
JONATHAN MARX: Outside of the ring with Anarky is one of the last places you ever want to be.
BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY IS GATHERING UP THE SLACK IN THE STRAP AND HE IS TURNING TACT OVER ON HIS BACK! Anarky winds up….. HE WAILS ON HIM WITH A HARD SHOT RIGHT TO THE LOWER BACK! HE HITS AGAIN! AND AGAIN! AND AGAIN! AND AGAIN! AND AGAIN!
JONATHAN MARX: Anarky is vicious! BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY HAS NAILED HIM WITH TEN SHOTS OF THE STRAP RIGHT TO THE BACK AND TACT IS BLEEDING FROM HIS BACK!
VIC WATERS: Tact must wish he was wrestling Sean Edmunds right now. BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky grabs Tact by the hair…. HE IS ROLLING HIM BACK INTO THE RING! Thank god for Tact.
VIC WATERS: I think Anarky is softening in his old age… BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky rolls back in and he stands over Tact and he is trash talking Tact telling him to get up…. WHOA, TACT IS TRYING TO SOMEHOW GET BACK UP!
JONATHAN MARX: Tact has a lot of toughness and heart. He isn’t go easy unto the night. BRANDON JACOBS: TACT IS BACK UP…. NO! ANARKY KICKS HIM IN THE MIDSECTION! This crowd is really booing Anarky! ANARKY LOCKS ON A SWINGING NECKBREAKER AND DRIVES HIM INTO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: He planted him with that one. BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky grabs Tact by the hair…. HE DRAGS HIM TO THE FIRST TURNBUCKLE AND ANARKY TOUCHES THE FIRST BUCKLE!
VIC WATERS: That is one, Tact is totally out of it, I don’t know how he is going to get out of this one.
BRANDON JACOBS: TACT BREAKS FREE OF ANARKY’S GRIP AND DIVES FOR HIS LEG IN A SINGLE LEG TAKE DOWN… BUT ANARKY STILL HAS TOO MUCH ENERGY TO BE DRAGGED DOWN AND ANARKY DRAGS TACT TO THE SECOND TURNBUCKLE ON HIS LEG AND TOUCHES THE SECOND!
VIC WATERS: TWO MORE TO GO! BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky starts stomping away at the blood back of Tact in an effort to get Tact bleeding even more and separate him from his leg!
JONATHAN MARX: It is much harder to drag someone by the leg who can eventually let go than to drag them by their hair…
BRANDON JACOBS: TACT FINALLY LETS GO OF ANARKY’S LEG! Anarky picks up Tact on his shoulder…. ANARKY RAMS TACT INTO THE TURNBUCKLE HEAD FIRST FOR THE THIRD TURNBUCKLE AND THEN ANARKY TOUCHES THE TURNBUCKLE HIMSELF!
JONATHAN MARX: Damn… BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky is now going for the fourth and final turnbuckle…. TACT SLIPS OUT OF THE HANDS OF ANARKY BECAUSE OF ALL THE BLOOD BEFORE ANARKY CAN GET TO THE FOURTH TURNBUCKLE!
VIC WATERS: That is sheer dumb luck that Tact was able to manage to escape. JONATHAN MARX: Tact will take all the luck he can get. BRANDON JACOBS: TACT IS TRYING TO CRAWL AWAY FROM ANARKY…. ANARKY CHARGES TACT…. DROP TOE HOLD BY TACT TAKING ANARKY DOWN TO THE MAT!
JONATHAN MARX: Tact has him right where he wants him! VIC WATERS: Huh? BRANDON JACOBS: TACT LOCKS THE STRAP AROUND ANARKY’S THROAT AND LOCKS ON AN STF! Anakry is screaming out in agony!
CROWD: LETS GO TACT! LETS GO TACT! LETS GO TACT! JONATHAN MARX: The fans are really behind Tact. They want Tact to make Anarky passout. VIC WATERS: Imagine the horror of being in the STF and not being able to submit. That has to be the most helpless feeling in the world.
BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY IS TRYING TO PULL HIMSELF CLOSER TO THE ROPES BUT TACT HAS IT LOCKED IN TIGHT!!
VIC WATERS: What is Anarky doing? The referee can’t call for a ref break, this is no DQ. JONATHAN MARX: Correct, but if he gets to the ropes, he can use the ropes to help him get out of the hold.
VIC WATERS: Anarky looks like he is finally making some progress! BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY IS MOVING TOWARDS THE ROPES! HE HAS THE ARMS EXTENDED…. NO! WAIT A MINUTE, TACT BREAKS THE HOLD BEFORE ANARKY CAN GET THERE!
JONATHAN MARX: Tact as a mat wrestler may not want to let Anarky get to the ropes because when are trapped in that area, it is anyone’s ballgame.
BRANDON JACOBS: TACT PICKS THE STRETCHED OUT ANARKY UP OFF THE MAT…. KICKSWING DDT! JONATHAN MARX: Tact used his body’s momentum to get the full impact on that DDT. Hopefully for him it is enough to keep Anarky down long enough for him to touch all four corners.
BRANDON JACOBS: TACT IS LACING THE STRAP AROUND ANARKY’S NECK LIKE A COLLAR AND HE IS DRAGGING HIM TO THE FIRST CORNER…. TACT TOUCHES THE FIRST CORNER!
JONATHAN MARX: Tact has the first one! BRANDON JACOBS: Tact drags Anarky by the strap towards the second one…. ANARKY GRABS THE ROPES IN ORDER TO KEEP TACT FROM GETTING THERE!
JONATHAN MARX: The momentum isn’t broken until Anarky touches a turnbuckle of his own… all Tact has to do is pull Anarky off and get to that second turnbuckle and he still has the momentum going.
VIC WATERS: HOLD ON ANARKY! HOLD ON! BRANDON JACOBS: TACT IS PULLING ANARKY BY HIS NECK WITH ALL HIS MIGHT AND ANARKY LOOKS LIKE HE IS CHOKING HE IS REFUSING TO LET GO OF THE ROPES…. TACT WALKS OVER AND HE KICKS ANARKY IN THE FACE AND HE FINALLY LETS GO! Tact drags Anarky over to the second turnbuckle…. TACT TOUCHES IT!
CROWD: TWOOOOOOOO! JONATHAN MARX: Tact has two turnbuckles now! Two more to go! BRANDON JACOBS: Tact is dragging Anarky by the throat towards the third turnbuckle… ANARKY USES HIS LEGS TO HOOK HIMSELF ONTO THE BOTTOM ROPE!
VIC WATERS: That is it Anarky, use the ring to your advantage. Don’t let Tact get you! JONATHAN MARX: People always think of Anarky as one of the greatest brawlers of alltime, but he has a hell of a brain on him and he knows where he is in that ring at all times despite the numerous shots he has taken to the head.
BRANDON JACOBS: Tact s flabbergasted… TACT UNHOOKS THE STRAP FROM ANARKY’S NECK AND HE STARTS BEATING ANARKY RIGHT IN THE LEGS WITH VICIOUS SHOTS THAT ARE ECHOING OVER THE SOUND SYSTEM!
JONATHAN MARX: Tact is showing shades of Anarky right here and he is proving to the world that he is no only a great wrestler, but he is as tough as nails.
VIC WATERS: Lenny Dykstra? JONATHAN MARX: Sure, whoever he … BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY GRABS HIS LEG AND FINALLY LEGS GO OF HIS SCISSORS GRIP ON THE ROPES! Tact pulls Anarky by the strap and he is dragging him to the third turnbuckle…. TACT CONNECTS! TACT HAS THREE TURNBUCKLES!
JONATHAN MARX: One more and Tact will forever be able to say he beat one of toughest men alive in a strap match. This is going to go down in Tact’s history as one of the defining moments of his career if he gets this fourth and final one.
VIC WATERS: Come on Anarky! You can’t let him beat you in your own match! CROWD: LETS GO TACT! LETS GO TACT! LETS GO TACT! BRANDON JACOBS: TACT IS PULLING ANARKY TOWARDS THE FOURTH AND FINAL TURNBUCKLE…. ANARKY JUST ROLLED OUT OF THE RING!
VIC WATERS: YES! JONATHAN MARX: Tact can still get the fourth and final one…. BUT HE HAS TO GET ANARKY BACK IN THE RING PRONTO!
BRANDON JACOBS: TACT IS GOING TO THE OUTSIDE OF THE RING…. Tact picks up Anarky off the ground…. LOW BLOW BY ANARKY!
VIC WATERS: The great equalizer! BRANDON JACOBS: Tact is doubled over in pain! ANARKY HITS TACT WITH A REVERSE CRESCENT KICK RIGHT IN THE JAW AND DOWN GOES TACT!
JONATHAN MARX: He clocked him with that shot! BRANDON JACOBS: An beaten and bruised Anarky climbs up onto the ring apron and he is pulling Tact up on the apron…. ANARKY
JONATHAN MARX: If Tact could just managed to touch that fourth and final turnbuckle in control of Anarky, he could still win this match because his momentum has yet not been broken!
BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY IS PULLING TACT BY THE STRAP TOWARDS THE OTHER TURNBUCKLE… TACT GRABS THE STRAP AND THEY ARE HAVING A TUG OF WAR OVER THE STRAP ON THE APRON!
JONATHAN MARX: Both of these men are quite strong, this could go either way. BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY HAS THE ADVANTAGE…. HE YANKS TACT HIS WAY AND TACT GOES CRASHING TOWARDS ANARKY AND RIGHT INTO THE STEEL POLE! Anarky reaches up and touches the top turnbuckle!
VIC WATERS: ANARKY BROKE THE MOMENTUM OF TACT! BRANDON JACOBS: Now all Anarky needs is three more to win this match… Tact looks like he was busted open when he crashed into the steel pole.
JONATHAN MARX: This doesn’t look good for Tact… BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY LACES THE STRAP AROUND TACT’S NECK AND PULLS HIM UP TO THE APRON…. ANARKY LIFTS HIM UP AND HITS HIM WITH A BRAINBUSTER!
JONATHAN MARX: I don’t know how Anarky pulled that off with so little room, but he did. BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY DRAGS TACT ON THE APRON TO THE SECOND TURNBUCKLE… ANARKY TOUCHES! VIC WATERS: Anarky has two! JONATHAN MARX: Anarky only needs two more finish off Tact… Tact needs to comeback soon or this match is over…
BRANDON JACOBS: TACT ROLLS OFF THE APRON AND ANARKY DROPS DOWN FROM THE APRON TO THE FLOOR!
JONATHAN MARX: Hopefully Tact can pull an Anarky here and find a way to change the momentum…
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky drags Tact on the outside around the corner towards the other turnbuckle…. ANARKY GOES TO PICK UP TACT…. TACT GOES FOR THE NUTSHOT BUT ANARKY LIFTS HIS KNEE AND BLOCKS IT! Anarky grabs Tact by the arm…. HE WHIPS HIM HEAD FIRST INTO THE POLE OF THE THIRD TURNBUCKLE!
VIC WATERS: Tact is fighting for his life here, he almost got in a good shot by Anarky being the cheater that he is knows cheating better than anyone else…
JONATHAN MARX: Technically it isn’t cheating in a no disqualification match… BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY CLIMBS UP ONTO THE APRON AND DRAGS TACT UP…. ANARKY TOUCHES THE THIRD TURNBUCKLE! HE ONLY NEEDS ONE MORE!
JONATHAN MARX: Tact needs to think of something fast… BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky doesn’t drop down, he side steps onto the other side of the pole and he is leading Tact towards the fourth and final corner…. TACT RUSHES ANARKY AND HE SIDE SUPLEXES HIM DOWN ONTO THE GROUND! Anarky took a hard spill onto the concrete floor.
JONATHAN MARX: The mats at ringside don’t offer all that much padding. VIC WATERS: Anarky has only one more turnbuckle to go, his momentum hasn’t been broken yet so he can still win this thing…. AND HE HIS SO DAMN CLOSE TOO!
BRANDON JACOBS: Tact is ripping up the mats around ringside….. TACT PUTS ANARKY IN AN ABDOMINAL STRETCH…. HE TAKES HIM DOWN WITH A SIDE SLAM RIGHT INTO THE GROUND!
CROWD: LETS GO TACT! LETS GO TACT! LETS GO TACT! VIC WATERS: Anarky landed really hard on his back… BRANDON JACOBS: Tact picks up Anarky off of the mat… HE LOCKS ON A BEAR HUG ON ANARKY IN ORDER TO DO DAMAGE TO HIS BACK!
VIC WATERS: Fight it Anarky! BRANDON JACOBS: TACT IS SWINGING ANARKY AROUND LIKE A RAGDOLL! HE HAS HIM NOW! WAIT… Oh man, Tact’s back look like it just gave out…. all of that damage Anarky did to him earlier in the match from the beating with the strap caused him to fall down to the ground.
VIC WATERS: WOOOOHOOO! BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky picks Tact off the ground…. CHAOS BREAKER ONTO THE CEMENT! JONATHAN MARX: Damnit, Tact was so close. BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY WRAPS THE STRAP AROUND TACT’S NECK AND HE DRAGS HIM TOWARDS THE FOURTH AND DECIDING TURNBUCKLE….
VIC WATERS: He may finally have him here… BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY CLIMBS ONTO THE APRON AND TACT AND HIM ARE FIGHTING FOR CONTROL! TACT IS TRYING TO PULL ANARKY OFF THE APRON WITH ALL OF HIS MIGHT BEFORE HE CAN TOUCH THE LAST TURNBUCKLE….Tact can’t hold him though because of his back…. TACT GRABS HIS BACK AND HE PASSES OUT! Anarky reaches over….. ANARKY TOUCHES THE FOURTH AND FINAL TURNBUCKLE!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. ANARKY! VIC WATERS: He did it! JONATHAN MARX: Tact took it to Anarky and he took it to Anarky, but Anarky kept on fighting back and when he got his opportunity. He took it. It was a hell of a match for both men.
VIC WATERS: I have never seen anything like this before, this two men left it all out there. As much as I dislike Tact, it takes a hell of a man to take the fight to Anarky.
JONATHAN MARX: I am sure this feud isn’t over yet, Tact will have his day yet. BRANDON JACOBS: What a match, folks, we have to take a short commercial break, but when we comeback, it is finally time for the main event, Manson & El Arco Iris will team up against Felix Red and Psycho in a cage… STAY TUNED!
