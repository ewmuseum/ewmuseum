Guerra Interminable: Wink Speaks, Sets Matches for GG & PPV

11 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 11 November 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: When you look at a lineup and only see four matches, you would think that would mean it would be a short show but this going to go a full four hours after that Cibernetico took nearly two hours and that is without formal intros.
JONATHAN MARX: Certain things can’t be rushed, wrestling is a marathon, not a sprint. VIC WATERS: I can’t wait to hit the bars after the show is done for a little r and r. BRANDON JACOBS: Our next match up…. (CUEUP: “One Shot At Glory” By Judas Priest) JONATHAN MARX: THAT IS WINK’S MUSIC! ::Chris Wink comes through the curtain and walks down the aisle to the ring:: CHRIS WINK: LOVE may have been able to pull the wool over the eyes of referee Umberto Gonzalez, but I was backstage and I saw what they did to Thirteen in order to win the match. If you two think that Psycho is going to get the night off, you are dead wrong. I AM GOING TO STRIP YOU TWO OF THE RIGHT TO FACE PSYCHO AND I AM GOING TO PUT IT UP FOR GRABS BETWEEN FOUR PEOPLE WHO DESERVE IT! Rocko Daymon, John Doe, Golem, and Shawn Hart will changed for the right to face Psycho at the PPV in an Four Degrees of Separation match, an elimination match where the stipulations change after each elimination until we have a winner.
CROWD: PSYCHO SUCKS! PSYCHO SUCKS! PSYCHO SUCKS! CHRIS WINK: But I am not done yet…. Felix, you and Thirteen still have a lot of issues to settle and I’ve decided that you two will get the honor of meeting in WFW’s annual falls count anywhere in the graveyard match with Anarky and Diablo ripped up the place with last year after which Diablo was never the same.
::the fans roar in approval:: CHRIS WINK: But there is the question of what to do with LOVE, they keep on beating challenger after challenger that we send against them….. well I have hand selected two men in order to get the job done….. FELIX RED AND ANARKY WILL FACE MAELSTROM AND DAN RYAN AT THE PPV! They may be our last hope to stop LOVE once and for all.
CROWD: MAELSTROM! MAELSTROM! MAELSTROM! CHRIS WINK: I know it may not seem like it, but the show is just beginning! Thank you all for coming tonight and we are going to end this show like a ball of fire. GOODNIGHT EVERYONE AND GOD BLESS WFW!
::Wink exits the ring and heads to the back:: BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we have to take another commercial break, when we comeback, the Partners Incredible match for sure…. STAY TUNED!
