PaulNJ21 — 12 December 2003 (EARLIER TONIGHT) (FADEIN to Scotty Michaels, getting out of a taxi. He's wearing a Brian Westbrook jersey, and an Eagles hat turned backwards. His hair's a mess, and he's got a five-o'clock shadow. He gets out, and jumps up on his crutch..)
INTERVIEWER: Scotty, how's the leg? We saw you walking around that town, and your limp was barely noticeable...
SCOTTY MICHAELS: It was doing pretty well, but it's that walk earlier today that aggravated it. I'm not doing too well, but as those three guys will see today... it's not two good legs that's going to win that match. It's HEART! I've got more of it than anyone will ever know. To quote Jon Bon Jovi, it's my life, and it's now or never..
(FADEOUT, as Michaels hobbles away..) (LIVE) ::Brandon Jacobs is sitting in front of the camera reading an officially looking piece of paper::
BRANDON JACOBS: “WFW President Alex Wylde and the WFW Board Of Directors has decided that as a result of the consequences of the Michael Manson vs. Anarky match that both men will be barred from facing each other indefinitely, whether it be in singles, tag, or six man action. While it isn’t the WFW’s intent to deprive the fans of privilege of two great legends facing off against each, these two men pose a grave risk to each other and to the fans because of their intense hatred for each other. If either man is caught attacking the other physically, they will face serious consequences.”
“WFW management apologize to the fans for letting the situation getting out of hand to the point of riot. WFW prides itself on being a promotion for all ages where fathers and mothers can take their children to see live action without any threat to their safety. The WFW promises to you the fan that we will take proper actions to make sure this will never happen again.”
“Thank you for supporting the WFW.” VIC WATERS: I really was worried that WWIII was going to break out, you know, the one without the god awful 90 man battle royal.
BRANDON JACOBS: Things really did look like they got ugly and I am thankful the police was able to get this show back under control so we could resume our normal broadcast. Connecticut’s finest did a hell of a job.
VIC WATERS: They really don’t have much to do besides busting Letterman for speeding tickets.
BRANDON JACOBS: That is a lot of work in itself. Moving right along, we are on the verge of one of the most anticipated matches in all of WFW history and thankfully for all of you in TV land who couldn’t be here tonight, you will have the honor to see this. There isn’t a doubt in my mind that all four men have what it takes to be WFW World Champion, and not only that, but a good one.
VIC WATERS: I know sometimes I have a tendency to play Devil’s Advocate, but I agree 100% with that comment. I’m hoping Scotty Michaels can pull this off and get his shot at the PPV, but with how evenly these guys are matched, anyone can finish one, two, three, or four in this match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Now, just as a reminder, we’ve seen some wild matches tonight and while the four way will be great, it will not be tornado rules, wrestlers will have to take in and out until there are only two left.
VIC WATERS: With all the wildness we’ve seen tonight, it will be cool to see a good old fashioned wrestling match… well as old fashioned as a four way elimination match can be. A lot of people don’t seem to understand that when everything is wild, nothing is wild. Everything lives in contrast.

VIC WATERS: Looks like we are expecting guests. Hopefully they bring their riot gear. BRANDON JACOBS: Seems like an awfully big section to be roped off, personally, my heart can’t handle all the surprises and action that has happened tonight so far. Oh well, what doesn’t kill you makes you stronger. Let’s send this one down to ringside and get this one underway!
