WWF Pandamonium: Gideon vs. Bartleby

16 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 16 July 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back after a rather long commercial break. JONATHAN MARX: Apparently we had some trouble tracking down Bartleby to let him know his match was up next.
VIC WATERS: With the list of enemies Bartleby has, you can’t really stand around in plain site.
JONATHAN MARX: He wasn’t hiding, he was getting loaded in the bar with Scotty. VIC WATERS: You make public drinking sound like a bad thing. JONATHAN MARX: This is coming from someone whose blood alcohol level is so high they have to test it for blood.
VIC WATERS: Hardy har har… BRANDON JACOBS: Now, now, we don’t bring back Vic’s problems, we are enablers. VIC WATERS: Amen to that. ::Vic raises his Poland Spring Bottle and takes a sip:: JONATHAN MARX: You are absolutely killing your kidneys. VIC WATERS: My kidneys are the Doc Silver of drinking, they take a licking and keep on ticking.
JONATHAN MARX: At this point, even Doc is say holy cow, that is insane. BRANDON JACOBS: Alright, enough boozer jokes… VIC WATERS: All Boozer jokes should be about that sap of an owner of his ripping up his contract thinking that he would resign.
JONATHAN MARX: In fairness to them, Gunds have always been known to be soft. BRANDON JACOBS: Enough basketball jokes too…. Who do you guys think is going to win this match between Bartleby and Gideon?
JONATHAN MARX: It is really hard to give a fair assessment of Bartleby since we don’t really know his background or have seen him in action. We know that he can cause mass chaos which isn’t pretty but that is it.
VIC WATERS: Bartleby has been rolling through people left and right even before stepping into the ring. I fear for Gideon.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon just went almost an hour with Manson and came a second away from getting a draw. I don’t know who this Bartleby is, but he is definitely no Manson.
VIC WATERS: Someone sounds a little bitter at Bartleby. BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels and Bartleby near broke my damn neck and put me in a wheel chair for the rest of my life. Of course I am bitter and I will take great joy in seeing Gideon give him what is coming to him.
VIC WATERS: We will see. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, lets cut out all the chit chat and send this one to the ring! (CUEUP: “Remember” by Disturbed) RING ANNOUCER: Introducing first, from Buffalo, NY…. the longest reigning WFW North American Champion of alltime….. THE SYSTEM RICHARD GIDEON!
::Gideon comes down to the ring to the roar of the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “Only Happy When It Rains” by Garbage) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Parts Unknown making his debut here tonight, BARTLEBY!
::the masked Bartleby walks down to the ring slowly to the boos to the crowd and enters the ring::
VIC WATERS: I can’t believe the nerve Bartleby has stealing Doc’s music. :: JONATHAN MARX: He can try to be Doc Silver all he wants, he is no Doc Silver. He probably just chose that music to get into the head of Gideon.
BRANDON JACOBS: That is actually a pretty good plan. *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: And there is the bell and Bartleby and Gideon meet in the center of the ring and they appear to be having some sort of chat.
JONATHAN MARX: Both men are trying to get into each other’s heads with a few choice words here and there.
BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON BEGINS THE ASSAULT WITH A SERIES OF HARD FAST CHOPS RIGHT TO THE CHEST OF BARTLEBY! JONATHAN MARX: Bartleby is being backed up to the ropes. BRANDON JACOBS: BARTLEBY IS FIGHTING BACK WITH BIG ROUND HOUSE RIGHTS! He driving Gideon back… DISCUS PUNCH RIGHT TO THE FACE AND GIDEON GOES DOWN!
JONATHAN MARX: Shades of Shawn Hart right there. VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart never connected like that before with anyone. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is trying to get back up to his feet… BARTLEBY KICKS GIDEON IN THE GUT! He picks Gideon up… SITOUT FRONT SUPLEX!
VIC WATERS: That sort of looks like Wild Buster to me… JONATHAN MARX: Bartleby seems familiar but I just place him. BRANDON JACOBS: BARTLEBY IS GOING FOR THE COVER! He hooks the leg and the ref goes down for the count!
JONATHAN MARX: Bartleby wants an impressive victory and he is going for it right here. BRANDON JACOBS: 1-------------------------------------2---------------------------- GIDEON GETS THE SHOULDER UP!
JONATHAN MARX: He went for that pin a bit prematurely. VIC WATERS: So I guess that shades of Jonathan Marx? JONATHAN MARX: Quiet you. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon wasn’t able to do any research for Bartleby because he doesn’t know who he is. It is going to take awhile under he understands what he wrestles like and develops a game plan of his own.
JONATHAN MARX: He’ll find his weakness, that is what Gideon does the best. BRANDON JACOBS: Bartleby picks up Gideon off the mat…. A BIG TILT A WHIRL SLAM! VIC WATERS: Gideon really landed hard on his back. JONATHAN MARX: He appears to be clutching it in pain. All it takes is to land badly and that is all she wrote.
BRANDON JACOBS: BARTLEBY DRAGS GIDEON OVER TOWARDS THE CORNER AND HE GOING TO THE CORNER TURNBUCKLE TO CLIMB TO THE TOP ROPE!
JONATHAN MARX: Gideon has to find a way to stop Bartleby’s moment or he is going to find himself looking up at the ring lights.
BRANDON JACOBS: BARTLEBY IS PERCHED ON TOP… HE FLIES OFF WITH A FROG SPLASH ONTO GIDEON! Bartleby grabs his ribs in pain as he bounces off Gideon with all his weight.
VIC WATE$RS: Did you see the kind of air he got there? Damn! JONATHAN MARX: This Bartleby certainly has refined his skills that is for sure. BRANDON JACOBS: Bartleby is crawling over to make the pin on Gideon…. The ref goes down for the count! 1------------------------------------2------------------------- GIDEON GETS HIS FOOT ON THE ROPES JUST IN THE NICK OF TIME! Bartleby is cursing the referee for the slow count.
JONATHAN MARX: I didn’t think that count was slow. VIC WATERS: I could have gone to Tijuana and back in the time it took to count that pinfall
BRANDON JACOBS: Bartleby is grabbing his side as he is getting up off the mat….it looks like he is in a great deal of pain.
JONATHAN MARX: He probably injured a rib. BRANDON JACOBS: Bartleby reaches down and he picks Gideon up off the mat…. SHOULDER BLOCK BY GIDEON RIGHT INTO THE CHEST OF BARTLEBY! Bartleby tries to fight Gideon off…. BUT GIDEON NAILS HIM WITH ANOTHER SHOULDER BLOCK TO THE CHEST!
JONATHAN MARX: Gideon is fighting back for his life here… BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON WHIPS BARTLEBY OFF THE ROPES….. LOU THESZ PRESS BY GIDEON! JONATHAN MARX: LOOOOOOOOOOOU! BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is punching away with closed fists right to the face of Bartleby! GIDEON HAS HAD ENOUGH!
VIC WATERS: Gideon is trying to get some payback. BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is trying to pry Gideon off…. GIDEON BREAKS AND THEN LOCKS ON A REVERSE CHINLOCK! Gideon is trying to remove Bartleby’s mask with his other hand as he is trying to wear him down!
JONATHAN MARX: Maybe we’ll finally find out who this pain in the butt really is. BRANDON JACOBS: THE REFEREE IS TRYING TO PRY GIDEON OFF OF BARTLEBY! VIC WATERS: It is illegal to go for a wrestlers mask. He could get disqualified for doing that.
JONATHAN MARX: Gideon would be doing the world a favor if he took one for the team. BRANDON JACOBS: I wish I could remember who Bartleby was an all of this wouldn’t be necessary… .
JONATHAN MARX: If you can’t remember, you can’t remember. You went through a lot. BRANDON JACOBS: That doesn’t change the fact Bartleby is reeking havoc on everyone in WFW…. THE REF IS GIVING GIDEON A FIVE COUNT TO BREAK! ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! Gideon breaks the reverse chinlock… the ref is lecturing him about grabbing the mask!
JONATHAN MARX: Gideon seems to have twisted the mask on Bartleby and his mask is covering his eyes.
BRANDON JACOBS: BARTLEBY IS SWINGING WILDLY HOPING TO HIT GIDEON! Gideon bounces off the ropes… GIDEON CLOTHESLINES HIM DOWN TO THE MAT AND TURNS HIM INSIDE OUT!
VIC WATERS: What a shot by Gideon, he may be a wrestler, but he really packs a punch. BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon stands over Bartleby…. GIDEON IS CALLING FOR THE G-FORCE! JONATHAN MARX: These are without a doubt the best suplexes in all of the sport. VIC WATERS: If that clothesline didn’t knocked the senses out of him, this will. BRANDON JACOBS: Bartleby is trying to get up…. GIDEON PICKS HIM UP FOR A BRAINBUSTER…. HE DRIVES HIM DOWN WITH THE MAT WITH THE FIRST SLASHING HIS HEAD HARD INTO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Thank god Bartleby has a hard head. BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON ROLLS HIM UP OFF THE MAT FOR A SECOND BRAINBUSTER SUPLEX…. AGAIN HE DRIVES HIM DOWN INTO THE MAT AND BARTLEBY LOOKS LIKE HE MAY NOT BE ABLE TO TAKE A THIRD WITH THE WAY HE IS LOOKING NOW!
JONATHAN MARX: GET HIM GIDEON! BEAT HIM LIKE A DOG! BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON PICKS HIM UP FOR A THIRD TIME…. HE HITS THE THIRD AND FINAL BRAINBUSTER TO HIT THE TRIFECTA!
VIC WATERS: Gideon has to go for the pin. BRANDON JACOBS: A tired Gideon looks over to Bartleby and attempts to make a cover… THE REFEREE CUTS GIDEON OFF AND TELLS HIM TO STAY BACK FOR A SECOND BECAUSE BARTLEBY IS GRABBING HIS NECK AND HE WANTS TO CHECK HIM OUT!
JONATHAN MARX: This referee seems to have an agenda to cost Gideon the win. BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON IS SCREAMING HIS HEAD OFF AT THE REFEREE…. THE REFEREE TURNS AROUND AND HE GETS IN GIDEON’S FACE ABOUT FOLLOWING THE RULES!
JONATHAN MARX: Every so often, you just get a bad referee and tonight is one of those nights for Gideon.
VIC WATERS: Wait a second, A DAZED BARTLEBY JUST TOOK A PIECE OF METAL OUT OF HIS TIGHTS AND PUT IT IN HIS MASK!
JONATHAN MARX: He is loading his mask and the referee is too busy arguing with Gideon to see it!
BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON PUSHES THE REFEREE OUT OF THE WAY AND GOES AFTER BARTLEBY! The referee is yelling at him still but Gideon refuses to listen.
JONATHAN MARX: Gideon has tried his best to listen to this ref, but he is just totally out of control at this point.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon picks Bartleby off of the mat…. GIDEON IS TRYING TO TAKE OFF THE MASK AGAIN!
VIC WATERS: Why is he so obsessed with that mask? Bartleby has never done anything to him. JONATHAN MARX: The way Bartleby is going, it is only a matter of time. BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON HAS THE MASK OFF UP TO THE MOUTH OF BARTLEBY! HE MAY BE ABLE TO DO IT!
VIC WATERS: Can you recognize him? JONATHAN MARX: Not yet… GIDEON JUST A LITTLE BIT MORE! BRANDON JACOBS: THE REFEREE JUMPS ON GIDEON’S BACK AND GIDEON LETS GO OF BARTLEBY! VIC WATERS: Neither of you would be complaining about the referee trying to save the guy if it was El Arco Iris.
JONATHAN MARX: I never said I wasn’t a hypocrite. BRANDON JACOBS: A battered Bartleby moves towards and ropes and he is using them to help him up…. BARTLEBY IS UP! Gideon is finally able to shake the ref off!
VIC WATERS: It is showtime. BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON CHARGES BARTLEBY AND BARTLEBY BACK DROPS GIDEON OVER THE TOP ROPE! JONATHAN MARX: LOOK! Gideon was able to gain his balance and land on his feet on the ring apron.
BRANDON JACOBS: BARTLEBY TURNS AROUND…. GIDEON SWINGS WITH A PUNCH AND MISSES! Bartleby grabs the head of Gideon….. BARTLEBY DELIVERS A HEADBUTT WITH HIS LOADED MASK RIGHT TO THE HEAD OF GIDEON AND GIDEON FALLS OFF THE APRON TO THE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: Go loaded mask! BRANDON JACOBS: Bartleby is going to the outside…. BARTLEBY IS RIPPING UP THE RING APRON TO EXPOSE THE CEMENT PAVEMENT BELOW!
JONATHAN MARX: This ground is by no means proper for taking any sort of abuse. Gideon could be seriously hurt.
BRANDON JACOBS: BARTLEBY PICKS GIDEON UP…. GIDEON IS TRYING TO FIGHT BACK….. BARTLEBY NAILS HIM WITH ANOTHER HEADBUTT WITH THE LOADED MASK! Bartleby picks up Gideon….. POWERBOMB ONTO THE CEMENT!
JONATHAN MARX: Poor Gideon. VIC WATERS: You got after someone’s mask and that is what happens. JONATHAN MARX: Gideon’s back is all bloody too from the cement. He scrapped himself something good.
BRANDON JACOBS: BARTLEBY ROLLS GIDEON BACK IN AND ROLLS IN AFTER HIM! VIC WATERS: Bartleby has the advantage, now he has to capitalize on it and get the win. BRANDON JACOBS: BARTLEBY PICKS GIDEON UP OFF THE MAT….. DDT! He picks him up again…. ANOTHER DDT! He signals for one more…. HE HITS A THIRD!
JONATHAN MARX: I remember this setup, Diablo use to do it all the time! VIC WATERS: And because of it…. Gideon is ripe to be picked off right now… BRANDON JACOBS: BARTLEBY CALLING FOR A STUNNER! (CUEUP: “The Unbirthday Song”) ::Anarky comes down to the ring wheeling a cake wearing an apron with the LOVE logo on it and wheels it right up to the ring::
VIC WATERS: Oh no, not LOVE again… JONATHAN MARX: This is one of the first times I am actually glad to see them. BRANDON JACOBS: BARTLEBY WALKS OVER TOWARDS THE ROPES AND HE IS TELLING ANARKY TO LEAVE! JONATHAN MARX: This crowd is actually booing Bartleby over Anarky…. VIC WATERS: Look, Anarky is grabbing a mic! : ANARKY: "Awwww, c'mon Bartleby... I stayed up all night baking this cake. And now you aren't even gonna taste it? But then you won't get to the gooey center!!"
BRANDON JACOBS: Bartleby is turning around to go back to work on Gideon who is still lying there dazed on the mat…
(The top blows off the cake, and a stripper pops out, with only red, white and blue tassells on her boobs, and she's holding two sparklers in her hands and has an American flag-designed top hat on.)
VIC WATERS: NOW THIS HAS MY ATTENTION! BRANDON JACOBS: It has Bartleby’s attention too! JONATHAN MARX: Down Vic down… BRANDON JACOBS: LOVE is trying to con Bartleby into joining their side…. THOSE BASTARDS! Imagine how evil they would be if that were to happen.
JONATHAN MARX: They would be even harder to stop than they already are. BRANDON JACOBS: Bartleby is putting his head through the ropes to give the stripper a kiss…. ANARKY IS SLOWLY WALKING TOWARDS BARTLEBY AND BARTLEBY TURNS TO ANARKY…. Gideon is getting up…
VIC WATERS: What is that stripper pulling out? JONATHAN MARX: Hopefully not her manhood. BRANDON JACOBS: THE STRIPPER HAS A CROWBAR AND SWINGS THE CROWBAR AT BARTLEBY’S HEAD…. WAIT A SECOND, GIDEON SCHOOL BOYS BARTLEBY FROM BEHIND BEFORE THE STRIPPER CAN CONNECT! The ref goes down to make the count!
VIC WATERS: NO DAMNIT! BRANDON JACOBS: 1-----------------------------------------------------2------------------------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. RICHARD GIDEON! BRANDON JACOBS: Bartleby is irate! Anarky has spoiled his WFW debut! JONATHAN MARX: Good lord, has Gideon joined LOVE? Is this all a setup? VIC WATERS: I don’t know what to believe right now. Who knows… ANARKY: "Oops, Sorry, Bartleby... I'd have done it myself, but she told me she wanted to have the pleasure. Roxy here... she told me she was tired of you giving whores a bad name.
ANARKY: "And I'm inclined to agree. Why don't you stay down on your knees, Bartleby, like the good little whore that you are. I’ll see you around."
BRANDON JACOBS: BARTLEBY JUST SPAT AT ANARKY! Gideon is looking on…. He doesn’t know what to make about this.
::Anarky smiles at Bartleby and wheels her out as she waves to the obviously excited fans::
BRANDON JACOBS: We may have just witnessed the birth of World War III. VIC WATERS: Heaven help us all. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we have to take a short commercial break, but when we comeback, Wells & Blue Cat will team up in a Double Dog Collar match against Copycat & Iron Lion…. STAY TUNED!
