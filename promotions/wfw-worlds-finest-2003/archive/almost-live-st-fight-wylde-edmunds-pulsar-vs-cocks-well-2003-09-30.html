Almost Live: St Fight - Wylde/Edmunds/Pulsar vs. COCKS/Well

30 September 2003

PaulNJ21 — 30 September 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Sep-30-03 AT 11:40&nbsp;AM (EST)] BRANDON JACOBS: SIX MAN STREET FIGHT TIME! Nothing like a Japanese garbage style six man war with six of the biggest names in all of WFW, plus this match will feature in ring WFW debut of both Jared Wells and Alex Wylde.
VIC WATERS: I’m pumped for match. Now we should tell the people how this match is different than the BAD World Title Match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Good point Vic, while this match will be a street fight, in this instance, pinfalls will only count INSIDE of the ring. But other than that, tornado rules, no tagging in and out, three men verses three men for all the marbles. This match will end when a member of one team pins any member of the other for the three. Which side are you going with?
VIC WATERS: I pick the side with Onita on it. BRANDON JACOBS: Onita isn’t in this match. VIC WATERS: No Onita? Damn, I’ll go with C.O.C.K.S. then. Diablo is a former BAD World Heavyweight Champion, Shawn Hart is the current WFW World Champion, and Jared Wells is a former WWL World Champion. With all that history of gold on one side, you couldn’t ask for a better threesome.
BRANDON JACOBS: But you can’t argue the credential of the other side either, Sean Edmunds is a former WWL World Champion, Alex Wylde is a former CSWA Presidential Champion, and Pulsar…. Well Pulsar is new.
VIC WATERS: Everyone knows everyone and their mother is going to be going after Pulsar. This is a huge match for him because he has yet to get a win and this is his first venture into the wrestling scene. That is like bleeding over a tank full of sharks.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar has amazing heart and he has all the tools to become a superstar here in the WFW. All he needs is the experience against top notch talent like that which is in the six man street fight tonight.
VIC WATERS: You are the kind of guy who would throw a little kid who couldn’t swim into the deep end, aren’t you?
BRANDON JACOBS: No, no. But I think Pulsar will hold his own tonight, Diablo just lost the BAD World Heavyweight Title after a grueling match title match against Rat Fink & The American. Although he is extremely skilled in the art of brawling, sometimes one loss can get into your head and it can build to another loss.
VIC WATERS: I’m thinking Alex Wylde could be submitted clean by Hart if Hart is irate as he was the last time those two faced off at NYC Unplugged.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart was quite upset, but Wylde has been focused on beating Hart, both in the election and in the ring.
VIC WATERS: The champ is a dangerous man tonight. Wylde may be a bit too focused on winning his campaign and could be drained.
BRANDON JACOBS: There is only one way to find out, let’s send this one to ringside! (CUEUP: "Diesel Power" by Prodigy) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the former CSWA Presidential champion and winner of the WFW primary, ALEX WYLDE!
::Alex Wylde walks down to a loud pop and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “Dragnet Theme”) RING ANNOUNCER: And now, coming out as a team, the two some which has been terrorizing the WFW ever since they joined forces, the former BAD World Heavyweight Champion…. DIABLO, and the current WFW World Heavyweight Champion… “THE PHENOM” SHAWN HART!
::Shawn Hart & Diablo walk down to ringside and enter the ring and begin eyeing Wylde:: (CUEUP: “Whiskey in a Jar” – Metallica) RING ANNOUNCER: Teaming with Alex Wylde, one of the young lions of World Finest Wrestling, from Australia, standing in at over 6’3, 245 pounds…. PULSAR!
::Pulsar runs down to ringside to make sure C.O.C.K.S. doesn’t attack Wylde before the match starts::
(CUEUP: "Brain Stew" by Green Day) RING ANNOUNCER: And their partner, the former WWL World Heavyweight Champion, SEAN EDMUNDS!
::Sean Edmunds walks down to ringside and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “Here to stay” by Korn) RING ANNOUNCER: And last but not least, a man make his debut here tonight, from the state which has produced two of the three WFW World Heavyweight champions, the former WWL World Heavyweight Champion…. “RAGE ‘O FIRE” JARED WELLS!
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: The C.O.C.K.S. team attacks quickly! Shawn Hart is going after Wylde, Diablo is going after Pulsar and Jared Wells is going after Sean Edmunds!
VIC WATERS: This should be good… BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart nails Wylde with a series of punches and drives him back… Wylde is beginning to wobble… BIG DISCUS PUNCH BY HART! Wylde goes down! Diablo and Pulsar are over in the corner chopping the hell out of each other! Wells already has Edmunds in a bear hug! Team C.O.C.K.S. is firmly in control.
VIC WATERS: So much action going on at the same time, it is hard to know in what direction to look.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart climbs on top of Wylde and starts slapping him around with big open handed slaps! Wylde is growing irate but he can’t escape! Wylde manages to get one of his arms free and he nails Hart in the side of the head with an uppercut! Pulsar backs away from Diablo who is slumped over in the corner… FAST PACED RUNNING SHOULDER BLOCK INTO DIABLO WHO IS SQUEEZED LIKE A PANCAKE INTO THE CORNER! Good lord!
VIC WATERS: That was a hell of a shot by Pulsar, Diablo looks disoriented. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is trying to break free, PULSAR ATTACKS WELLS FROM BEHIND SO HE BREAKS UP THE BEAR HUG! Wylde has fought his way up after a series of uppercuts…. Wylde whips him off the ropes…. FLYING DROPKICK! Diablo is starting to come too in the corner but Pulsar comes right back over to work on his man.
VIC WATERS: Wylde with a really crisp flying dropkick there. He caught Hart right in the face with it.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar picks Diablo off the mat and he CLOTHESLINES HIM OVER THE TOP! Pulsar rolls under the bottom rop and he is setting up a table! Edmunds is up finally and he goes for swinging neck breaker on Wells. Edmunds is holding his ribs in pain and he looks up to the top rope and begins his ascent. Wylde quickly picks Hart up…. ROCKER DROP INTO THE MAT! Wylde makes a cover! 1----------------2-------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar is really show a vicious streak here. He wants to make a name for himself by showing Diablo that he can go and that he can be a serious threat to the number two man of C.O.C.K.S.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar puts Diablo in an inverted face lock… DIABLO BITES THE INNER ARM OF PULSAR! Pulsar lets go and Diablo starts firing backs with a series of lefts! Edmunds is perched on the top rope…. HE FLIES OFF AND NAILS WELLS WITH THE MOONSAULT! Edmunds grabs his ribs as he lands! That can’t be a good sign. Wydle picks Hart off the mat… HART TAKES A FEW WILD SWINGS BUT HE MISSES! HART GRABS HIM AROUND THE WAIST…. STUN GUN! Hart grabs his throat as he crashes down on the mat!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds needs to becareful. He may still not be 100% from his hospital stay, but you have to give him a lot of credit for going out there and sucking it up when he can’t go like he wants to.
BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo has a lighter! HE SETS THE TABLE A FLAME! He looks over at Pulsar and it looks like he is going for the Devil’s Ragdoll Cobra Clutch Bomb! DIABLO COMES FROM BEHIND AND LOCKS ON THE COBRA CLUTCH…. WAIT! PULSAR LIFTS HIM ON HIS BACK AND PUTS HIM IN A FIREMAN’S CARR…. DIABLO IS DROPPED BACK FIRST INTO THE FLAMING TABLE! HOLY COW!
CROWD: BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! BRANDON JACOBS: Attendants are putting on the fire, it looks like most of the fire went out when Diablo landed. That could have been a lot worse than it did but Diablo’s back still may have been singed.
VIC WATERS: If you play with fire, you are going to be burnt. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds hooks the arms of Wells and takes him down with a crucifix! The ref goes down to make the count! 1--------------------------2-----------------KICKOUT! The injured Edmunds probably waited a bit too long to do that move! Edmunds picks up Wells by the hair…. HE NAILS HIM WITH A REVERSE CRESCENT KICK AND WELLS FLIPS OVER THE TOP ROPE TO THE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: This is becoming quite the wild match. BRANDON JACOBS: Meanwhile in the ring, WYLDE HAS TAKEN OFF ONE OF HIS BOOTS TO CHOKE HART WITH! Hart is struggling to break free but to no avail! Wylde finally lets go and RAKES THE BOOT LACES OVER FACE OF HART LIKE A CHEESE GRATER!
VIC WATERS: Wylde is trying to scratch up the pretty face of Hart, I don’t know what this will do other than to anger Hart even more.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds picks Wells and whips him into the steel guard rail… WAIT, REVERSAL BY WELLS AND EDMUNDS CRASHES IN HARD RIBS FIRST! Edmunds falls down to the ground grabbing his ribs… Wells climbs up onto the stairs… DOUBLE FOOT STOMP ON THE CHEST OF EDMUNDS! Edmunds is not looking so good. Pulsar takes a piece of the extinguished table…. HE RUNS OVER AND NAILS WELLS ON THE BACK OF THE HEAD WITH! Wells falls down to his knees, Pulsar rolls Wells into the ring and comes back after him!
VIC WATERS: Right now, a member of each teams is down on the ground, Diablo from that flaming table shot and Edmunds is down from the whip to the guard rail and double foot stomp to the chest.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde lifts Hart up by the hair… HE IS GOING IS SWINGING FOR THE FENCES WITH THAT BOOT OF HIS… HART DUCKS! Shawn Hart superkicks the boot into Wylde’s face and Wylde crashes down to the ground! WYLDE HAS BEEN BUSTED OPEN! Shawn Hart takes the boot of Wylde and positions it on the ground… he lifts Wylde up…. TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER INTO THE BOOT!
VIC WATERS: That poor boot had no idea what it was in for before this match began. BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart makes the cover! 1---------------------2---------------- WYLDE KICKS OUT! Shawn Hart turns Wylde over and begins nailing him with his own boot several times in the back!
VIC WATERS: It looks like Hart is weakening his back so he can’t kickout as quickly which is really smart move.
BRANDON JACOBS: WELLS AND PULSAR ARE EXCHANGING BLOWS BACK AND FORTH… WELLS AND PULSAR STUMBLE BACK OVER SHAWN HART AND WYLDE AND START FIGHT ON THE GROUND!
VIC WATERS: So many bodies, so little space… BRANDON JACOBS: Wells winds up on top and he unloads on Pulsar! Wells picks Pulsar off of the mat…. RUNNING POWERSLAM! Wells stands over Pulsar and tells him to get up… Pulsar looks winded but he is fighting his way back to his feet… PULSAR STARTS FIRING BACK! Wells lays into Pulsar with shots of his own… WELLS TAKES HIM DOWN WITH A LARIAT FROM HELL!
VIC WATERS: That looks like it may have caught Pulsar right between the eyes! BRANDON JACOBS: Wells moves Pulsar near the ropes…. Edmunds is beginning to shake out the cobwebs on the outside…. WELLS FLIES OFF WITH A TOP ROPE LEG DROP! He goes for the cover! 1-----------------------2------------------ EDMUNDS PULLS THE REF OUT! Wells gets up and yells at Edmunds on the outside… wait a second, Diablo is up and he is headed toward Edmunds from behind… SPEAR BY DIABLO ON THE OUTSIDE! Edmunds didn’t even see that one coming!
VIC WATERS: Poor Edmunds just got hit by a mack truck! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds got rammed into the mat chest first! Pulsar is finally starting to stir and Wells turns around to refocuses his attention on him. Hart picks up Wylde…. INVERTED ATOMIC DROP! After all those shots to the back, Hart is now working over the spine!
VIC WATERS: There is nothing worse than the tingling sensation caused throughout your body by the atomic drop. It is a jolt to your whole spinal chord.
BRANDON JACOBS: SHAWN HART KICKS WYLDE IN THE MID SECTION AND HE NAILS HIM WITH THE HART ATTACK STUNNER! Hart makes the cover! 1------------2------ WELLS NAILS PULSAR WITH A SAVAGE KICK AND HE FALLS BACK ON THE REFEREE BREAKING UP THE COUNT!
VIC WATERS: If Wells was a second later, Hart may have gotten him. BRANDON JACOBS: Hart and Wells are having a heated “discussion” in the ring! Meanwhile on the outside, Diablo is RIPPING UP THE MATS AROUND RINGISDE AND EXPOSING THE COLD HARD CONCRETE!
VIC WATERS: If he nails him with the DDT, Edmunds will be done for the evening. BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo picks up Edmunds in a front face lock…. EDMUNDS DROPS DOWN TO HIS KNEES TO BLOCK IT! Diablo picks Edmunds up by the hair… EDMUNDS DUCKS THE CLOSELINE BY DIABLO…. EDMUNDS GRABS THE FRONT FACES THE HEAD AND NAILS DIABLO WITH A DDT ON THE EXPOSED CONCRETE!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds would be better off getting the hell out of there and back into the ring as quickly as possible.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is climbs up on to the apron… Diablo is starting to get up…. REVERSE CROSSBODY ONTO DIABLO! The fans here are on their feet and are getting behind Edmunds’s comeback!
CROWD: EDMUNDS! EDMUNDS! EDMUNDS! BRANDON JACOBS: Wells and Hart have now worked out their differences and are taking turns double teaming Wylde and Pulsar. Hart whips Pulsar into the ropes…. DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE BY HART & WELLS! Now they are going after Wylde… Wylde is whipped off the ropes… WYLDE DUCKS THE DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE JUMPS ON BOTH WITH A SPLASH! The ref goes down to make the cover! 1-----------------2---------------DOUBLE KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Wylde almost had them both there! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds rolls in Diablo and rolls in after him. Edmunds goes to help Wylde out and begins attacking Wells! EDMUNDS IS LAYING IN THE CHOPS INTO THE CHEST OF WELLS! Edmunds nails him with a knee lift doubling him up! EDMUNDS HOOKS THE ARMS…. DOUBLE ARM SUPLEX DOWN TO THE MAT! Wylde and Hart are exchanging blows back and forth! Edmunds goes for the cover on Wells! 1-----------------------------------2-----------------------DIABLO DIVES AND PUSHES EDMUNDS OFF BEFORE HE CAN GET THE THREE!
VIC WATERS: Diablo looked like he was still out of it, apparently he was just playing possum to wait for his opportunity to help.
BRANDON JACOBS: WYLDE CLOTHESLINES HART OVER THE TOP ROPE! Edmunds is simply pounding on Diablo! WELLS IS SIZING UP EDMUNDS FROM BEHIND…. WAIT… PULSAR IS ON HIS FEET BEHIND WELLS…. HE NAILS HIM WITH A SHOULDER BLOCK WHICH SENDS WELLS TUMBLING OUT OF THE RING!
VIC WATERS: IT IS A THREE ON ONE NOW AND DIABLO IS SCREWED! BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde is playing ring general and he is instruct Edmunds to keep Shawn Hart out of the ring! Shawn Hart is trying to get back into the ring… EDMUNDS DROPKICKS HIM OFF THE APRON! Wylde is instructing Pulsar now! Pulsar grabs Diablo and holds him up in the air with his head facing the ropes…. OH MAN! IF THIS IS WHAT I THINK IT IS… YES IT IS! WYLDE IS GOING UP TO THE ROPE ROPE!
VIC WATERS: If he hits this, it is all over! BRANDON JACOBS: WYLDE FLIES OFF THE TOP ROPE WITH THE GATECRASHER SUPERKICK TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD OF DIABLO AND PULSAR DROPS HIM FACE FIRST ONTO THE MAT! HOLY COW! Pulsar picks the fallen Diablo up… THE BLACK HOLE! Pulsar makes the cover!
VIC WATERS: Wells is on the apron and is bolting back in! BRANDON JACOBS: The ref goes down to make the count on Diablo! 1-----------------------2------------------------- WYLDE DIVES AND TACKLES WELLS TO PREVENT FROM BREAKING IT UP--------------3!
(CUEUP: “Whiskey in a Jar” By Metallica) RING ANNOUNCERS: The winners of this match… the team of Alex Wylde, “Simply Sensational” Sean Edmunds, and PULSAR!
BRANDON JACOBS: Man, what a match, chaotic but these two really had a war. VIC WATERS: I was wrong, Pulsar really stepped it up here and really contributed to the victory here tonight as did Wylde and Edmunds. This was a hell of a showing by all.
BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo just broke up the pin on Wells right before Wylde’s team was able to clear the ring. He tried his best but just came up a bit short tonight.
VIC WATERS: I love the WFW. BRANDON JACOBS: So do I, so do I. Okay folks, it is main event time coming up next, we will be back with SCOTTY MICHAELS PUTTING HIS NORTH AMERICAN TITLE ON THE LINE AGAINST COPYCAT…
VIC WATERS: WAIT! WAIT! Scotty Michaels is coming out! SCOTTY MICHAELS: First off Shawn Hart, after Luster lost his match tonight because of that damn Dormant, I went straight to management and petition the WFW to give Luster a WFW World Title shot against you at our next show Columbus Day Chaos! But if he doesn’t get the job done against you, I WILL! I know that I'm the best wrestler this federation has ever seen, and on my word.... I'm telling you right now, that if I don't capture the WFW world heavyweight championship by the time that 2003 comes to a close, I will leave the federation forever!
::throws the microphone down and leaves:: BRANDON JACOBS: Michaels is a man on a mission and if it wasn’t apparent that those two hate each other, it is now! WE HAVE TO GO TO BREAK, WE WILL BE RIGHT BACK AFTER THIS!
