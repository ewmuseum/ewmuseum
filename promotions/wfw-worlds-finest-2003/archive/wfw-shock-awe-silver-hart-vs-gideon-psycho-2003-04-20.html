WFW Shock & Awe: Silver & Hart vs. Gideon & Psycho

20 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 20 April 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Back in the early nineties, Falls Count Anywhere matches were all the rage. Up until then, matches were rather conservative then a few great brawlers came around and changed the whole damn sport in front of our eyes.
VIC WATERS: Was it really for the better? BRANDON JACOBS: Everything becomes over done at some point, but I remember how special those first Falls Count Anywhere matches I saw were. That is why this match holds a special place in my heart.
VIC WATERS: Forget about all those matches you’ve seen before, this one is going to be the best ever because the WFW World Heavyweight Champion Doc Silver is in it. He is going to hand Gideon & Psycho their behinds and set a new standard.
BRANDON JACOBS: These are four highly talented men, I wouldn’t doubt it. Let’s go to the ring for ring announcements!
:: Remember by Disturbed rocks over the loud speakers:: RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first at a combined wait of 550 pounds, the team of craziest man in all of wrestling… PSYCHO & the #1 Contender…. RICHARD GIDEON!
::Psycho & Gideon walk down to the ring to the cheers of the crowd:: ::Only Happy When It Rains by Garbage plays over the loud speaker:: RING ANNOUNCER: And their opponents, at a combined weight of 444 pounds, the current eWu World Champion SHAWN HART & THE WFW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION… DOC SILVER!
::Doc Silver & Shawn hart make their way to ringside to the boos of the crowd:: ************************************************** ********************** FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE: DOC SILVER (c) & SHAWN HART vs. RICHARD GIDEON & PSYCHO ************************************************** ********************** *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart and Psycho chosen to start out for their selected teams! VIC WATERS: It looks like we are going to have to wait a little longer to see Doc demolish the young Gideon. Wrestling promotions always do that. They should force young Gideon to start the match, that is what this crowd is here to see.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart starts laying into Psycho with punches and catches Psycho off guard! Psycho begins to stagger backwards…. DISCUS PUNCH BY HART! Psycho goes down!
VIC WATERS: It has been years since I’ve seen someone do a discus punch like Shawn Hart can.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart picks up Psycho and bounces him off the ropes…. HE CATCHES HIM WITH AN INVERTED ATOMIC DROP! Psycho is in pain!
VIC WATERS: The great equalizer! BRANDON JACOBS: SHAWN HART NAILS PSYCHO WITH A CLOTHESLINE! Shawn Hart makes a cover! 1------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Much too early to go for a pinfall, but it had looked like Psycho had taken that clothesline flush in the jaw.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart picks up Psycho… it looks like he is going to lift him up for a Tombstone Piledriver… BLOCKED! PSYCHO LIFTS SHAWN HART UP AND DROPS HIM BACK WITH A BIG BACK BODY DROP! Shawn Hart clutches his back!
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart looked like he was a bit over confident, he is no Doc Silver, he needs to be careful or he going to lose this match for his team.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho nails Shawn Hart with a series of kicks to his back! Shawn Hart turns around and begs off!
VIC WATERS: Not a smart move… BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO NAILS HIM WITH YAZUKA KICK TO THE FACE! The force sends Shawn Hart back and he stumbles through the ropes!
VIC WATERS: This may finally given Shawn Hart the break he needs to recover from this onslaught! This is wrestling, not MMA!
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Harts starts getting back up to his feet… Psycho bounces off the ropes… HE JUMPS OVER THE TOP ROPE ONTO SHAWN HART WITH A SPACE FLYING TIGER DROP! THE FANS ARE ON THEIR FEET CHANTING HOLY SHET AT PSYCHO!
VIC WATERS: My god, Psycho is the demon seed! BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is ripping the mats up off the ringside area where Hart is…. DOC SILVER RUNS OVER AND CLOTHESLINES PSYCHO FROM BEHIND! He picks Psycho up off the ground… SWINGING NECK BREAKER ONTO THE CONCRETE FLOOR! The ref is coming out to get Doc Silver to go to his corner, even though this is a falls count anywhere match, we are still trying to keep tag rules in effect!
VIC WATERS: Great save by Doc there, I don’t know what Psycho was going to do, but that man is a psycho and it had to be insane!
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart puts an arm over Psycho…. The ref comes back over to make the count! 1-------------2------PSYCHO KICKS OUT!
VIC WATERS: Very close nearfall there, if the ref didn’t have such a load in his pants, he would have been back in time to count the three.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart rolls Psycho into the ring and goes in after him. Hart pulls the dazed Psycho to the middle of the ring…. TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER! He didn’t get it completely though because Hart’s back was giving on him when he was trying to hold Psycho up! Hart goes for the cover! 1------------------2---------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Closer than I would have thought… BRANDON JACOBS: Hart pulls Psycho over to his corner and makes the tag to Doc Silver! VIC WATERS: Finally, Doc is in the match! This is a rematch here of the first round of the tournament, Psycho put up a good fight then too but he wound up losing at the end!
BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver comes in and pick up Psycho from behind… waistlock… BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX BY DOC!
VIC WATERS: Doc is calling for another! BRANDON JACOBS: He picks up Psycho again… ANOTHER BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX! Doc makes a cover! 1----------------2-------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is running on empty, he needs to make the tag to Gideon as soon as possible!
BRANDON JACOBS: DOC IS CALLING FOR THE SUPERKICK! Psycho is getting up slowly… he doesn’t see it coming… he turns around… DOC NAILS HIM WITH IT! Psycho gets driven back and falls towards his corner! Gideon is reaching out to make a tag! All Psycho needs is another two or three feet and Gideon is in! DOC RUNS OVER TO GRAB THE FEET OF PSYCHO AND PULL HIM TOWARDS THE CENTER OF THE RING!
VIC WATERS: Very smart move by Doc there, you need to keep your opponent away from his corner to prevent him from making a tag!
BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver goes to pickup Psycho… KICK TO THE HEAD BY PSYCHO! Doc tries to pick him up again… ANOTHER KICK! Psycho tries to crawl towards his corner….. HE MAKES A LUNGE! JUST A BIT SHORT! Doc grabs the leg again and this crowd is chanting for Psycho to make the tag! Gideon reaches as far as he can…. DOC PULLS HIM AWAY YET AGAIN!
VIC WATERS: Doc is starting to get aggravated with this ch BRANDON JACOBS: Doc Silver grabs the legs of Psycho… HE LOCKS IN THE FIGURE FOUR! Psycho is wailing his arms in pain!
VIC WATERS: When you are locked in the figure four, it feels like your leg is going to be snapped like a twig!
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is trying to turn it over and he is going to need all the energy he has to do this! HE IS ABLE TO TURN IT OVER! DOC SILVER IS SCREAMING IN PAIN!
VIC WATERS: BREAK IT DOC! BREAK IT! BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho released the hold! He looks in Doc’s face and starts cursing him out! HE LIFTS HIM UP… OH MY GOD, TIGER DRIVER 91! Doc may be dead after that! Psycho grabs Doc’s leg and pulls him over to his corner! HE MAKES THE TAG TO A FRESH GIDEON!
VIC WATERS: DAMMIT! BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON COMES INTO THE ROAR OF THE FANS! He picks Doc up… A GERMAN SUPLEX SUPLEX INTO A LEGLOCK – THE GIDEON CLUTCH! DOC IS SCREAMING LIKE I NEVER HEARD HIM SCREAM BEFORE! If he can’t break this, he may pass out from the pain!
VIC WATERS: Fight it Doc! Fight it! BRANDON JACOBS: Doc is close to the ropes… HE REACHES! HE JUST MISSED! HE REACHES AGAIN… HE GRABS IT! Gideon breaks the hold but he drags Silver to the middle of the ring… HE PICKS HIM UP… BRAINBUSTER! Silver tries to get up… GIDEON NAILS HIM WITH A DRAGON SCREW LEGWHIP!
VIC WATERS: Doc may be in trouble! BRANDON JACOBS: Jacobs is calling for the System Failure! HE IS GOING TO THE TOP ROPE…. 450 INTO A FROG SPLASH! GIDEON HOOKS THE LEG! 1---------------2-------------3!!! RICHARD GIDEON HAS BEATEN THE CHAMPION! THIS CROWD IS GOING INSANE!
VIC WATERS: I’m shocked, this relative newcomer has gotten a pinfall over thee Doc Silver! RING ANNOUNCER: THE WINNERS OF THIS MATCH THE TEAM OF PSYCHO AND GIDEON! ::ref raises their hand in victory:: BRANDON JACOBS: The fans are giving these two a standing ovation, they sense how historic this night may be because in two weeks, Doc Silver & Richard Gideon are meeting for the title!
VIC WATERS: I didn’t think Gideon would win the Rumble, and I definitely didn’t think he’d pin Silver tonight, but how could Gideon beat Doc Silver twice in a row?
BRANDON JACOBS: I don’t know, but that boy seems rather magical, don’t you think? We will be back right after this with the Boot Camp main event between Manson & Rat Fink for the WFW Heavyweight Title!
