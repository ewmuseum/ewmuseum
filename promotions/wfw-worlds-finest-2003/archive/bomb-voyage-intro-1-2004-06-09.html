BOMB Voyage: Intro #1

9 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 9 June 2004 (TAPED EARLIER BEFORE THE SHOW) ::Jonathan Marx is below deck somewhere in the bowels of the cruise ship, he looks around nervously before entering and enters the depressed Jacobs room::
JONATHAN MARX: How are you feeling? BRANDON JACOBS: I still can’t remember anything from the last couple of months. Everything including the Superbowl of Wrestling is a complete blank to me. Did Copycat get his title shot yet?
JONATHAN MARX: No, not yet. BRANDON JACOBS: You should speak to your father about that. He should have gotten the World Title shot at the Superbowl of Wrestling. Who won the election?
JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds beat out Wylde. BRANDON JACOBS: So the champ doesn't have the Presidency anymore? JONATHAN MARX: Wylde lost the title to Manson. BRANDON JACOBS: WHAT?! Manson finally won the WFW World Heavyweight Title? Good for him, he has been working at it for a long time now. But I have a feeling Wylde is going come after Manson and get his title back.
JONATHAN MARX: Wylde has fired by Edmunds. He isn't coming back. BRANDON JACOBS: Man, I picked a complicated two or three months to forget. The doctor says it may take awhile for me to regain my memory.
JONATHAN MARX: Michaels & Bartleby scared you ****less and the stress of the whole situation just made you block everything out. How is the neck feeling?
BRANDON JACOBS: This brace does help a bit, but I think I may have some nerve damage in my hands. The doctor says if I wanted, I could work the show as I am now but I want to give it some time.
JONATHAN MARX: You are getting lazy Brandon. The old Brandon would have sucked it up and went up four or five decks and called the match. All we would have to do is sit you in a wheelchair and I'd wheel you up top. You wouldn’t even have to walk.
BRANDON JACOBS: I don’t know Jonathan, I’m really not feeling so well. ::grabs the back of his neck::
JONATHAN MARX: How bad can it… ::a beautiful red headed nurse walks in with an a couple of pillows and a DVD, Jacobs eyes light up and a smile comes across his face::
NURSE BECKETT: Mr. Jacobs, how is my favorite patient doing today? BRANDON JACOBS: Good Holly, and I told you to call me Brandon. ::a dumbfounded Jonathan shakes his head:: NURSE BECKETT: It doesn’t feel proper as long Mr. Marx’s father is paying me… BRANDON JACOBS: Is he still getting drunk and singing in the karaoke bar when the passengers are all asleep?
NURSE BECKETT: You bet, he is having a howling good time along with the other board of directors.
::looks up:: JONATHAN MARX: Hello! I’m in the room. NURSE BECKETT: I’m sorry Mr. Marx. JONATHAN MARX: That is quite alright Holly. But lets keep with my father does quiet, he wants his identity kept a secret.
NURSE BECKETT: Yes, yes, he and the board of directors explained that all to me, your father is really a dashing old man.
JONATHAN MARX: Good looks run in the family. NURSE BECKETT: Oh, was his father good looking too? JONATHAN MARX: ::sigh:: It was good finally meeting you Holly, you two have fun watching the Philadelphia Story.
::Jonathan gives Jacobs a shameful look and Jacobs turns away:: JONATHAN MARX: You take care of yourself… BRANDON JACOBS: I will…. JONATHAN MARX: I was talking to Holly. ::smirks:: NURSE BECKETT: Hmmm? ::Marx leaves and closes the door:: JONATHAN MARX: I guess if you live long enough, you really DO see everything. ::Marx walks away from Jacobs door as the theme from the Love Boat plays:: (BACK TO LIVE FOOTAGE) ::the camera pans over the coast line and WFW commercials including one for the new LOVE t-shirt comes on the screen::
