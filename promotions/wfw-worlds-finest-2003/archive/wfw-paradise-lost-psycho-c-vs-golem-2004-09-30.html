WFW Paradise Lost: Psycho (c) vs. Golem

30 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 30 September 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: It is now time for our WFW North American Title match and this one has been building for quite sometime.
JONATHAN MARX: In the cards building up to our last PPV, Cherry Blossom Chaos, WFW President Edmunds stuck Golem into several tags with members of LOVE, but Golem always came out on the losing side despite never losing a match for his team. Frustrations between Golem and his partners grew until at Bomb Voyage, Golem could take no more and he attacked Psycho after his match in order to get revenge. That seemed like it would be the end of it for the moment because of Psycho’s scheduled match with El Arco Iris at the PPV, but Psycho decided that he would get revenge and he did so at WWF Pandamonium when he caused sheer mayhem in the Falls Count Anywhere in the San Diego Zoo match between Golem, Rat Fink, and Pulsar. It looked like Golem was going to be successful after Psycho had blinded him, but that is when Pulsar decided to step in and he helped Golem get the victory in order to get the first crack at the BAD World Heavyweight Title after the PPV. In the back however, WFW management was watching and Chris Wink decided to set right the wrongs committed in that match by giving Golem the opportunity to face the champion of his choice from LOVE in order to make up for the chaos Psycho caused and he forced Psycho to face Pulsar and Rat Fink as well in the second Falls in the Mall Part II. Psycho, being the warrior that he is, pulled off the impossible for the second year in a row and walked out the winner retaining his WFW North American Title while Golem defeated Mike Lane earlier in the show. Tonight, these two men are finally going to settle their differences and the WFW North American Title will be on the line and hopefully we will find out who the better man is once and for all.
VIC WATERS: You can breathe now… JONATHAN MARX: Thank god I keep myself in good or I’d blow myself up with that recap. BRANDON JACOBS: Golem has been through hell and back, I hope he gets his revenge tonight by winning the title.
VIC WATERS: Psycho has had an impressive stretch so far, but last year the Falls on the Mall took so much out of him that he lost the WFW World Heavyweight Title back to Doc Silver in Rage in the Cage. But I think Psycho is going to prevail in his match against Golem…
JONATHAN MARX: I’m leaning towards Golem, but I think it is going to be an awfully close match. Golem has yet to be pinned regardless of his win-loss record and he has been perfect in singles matches. Plus as you’ve stated, Psycho has been some all out wars over the last couple of months, he has to be very beaten up from all of it.
VIC WATERS: The great thing about Psycho is the more of the beating he takes, the stronger he gets. By wrestling the style that he does, he trains his body to take greater and greater beatings in order to build up his toughness to make himself and even bigger warrior.
BRANDON JACOBS: I can see both sides, I want to say Golem but Psycho has a way of getting the job done. There is only one way to find out, lets send this one down to ringside and get it underway!
(CUEUP: “The Day The World Went Away” by Nine Inch Nails) RING ANNOUNCER: THIS MATCH IS FOR THE WFW NORTH AMERICAN TITLE! Introducing first, the challenger, the man who has competed every where from the IWF to the FWF bringing terror into the hearts of men, from Death Valley, California…. GOLEM!
::Golem walks down to the crowd to a big ovation from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne) RING ANNOUNCER: And now the champion, the man who has won two straight Falls on the Mall and your WFW North American Champion, PSYCHO!
::Psycho walks down to the ring with a fork in his hand to a mixed ovation from the crowd sporting a Yankees Suck! T-shirt and a fanny pack. Psycho hands his belt to the ring attendant and enters the ring.::
BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM AMBUSHES PSYCHO AS HE STEPS THROUGH THE ROPES! *bell rings* JONATHAN MARX: Golem saw the fork and made a pre-emptive strike. VIC WATERS: Golem better be prepared for a war, because that is what he is going to get. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho has fallen down to the ground and dropped the fork! Golem picks up the fork and he stabs the top turnbuckle In order to set it in place.
JONATHAN MARX: You just know that the fork is going to come into play later. BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM PICKS UP PSYCHO OFF THE MAT AND THROWS HIM HEAD FIRST INTO THE SECOND TURNBUCKLE FROM THE TOP AND PSYCHO GRABS THE BACK OF HIS NECK!
JONATHAN MARX: Golem jammed Psycho’s neck with that jolt. VIC WATERS: Psycho better be careful, if he wants to beat Golem, he can’t let him get off to such a strong early start or it is going to make his life even more difficult.
BRANDON JACOBS: Golem lifts Psycho up by the mask…. GOLEM PUTS PSYCHO IN HANGMAN AND HE IS TRYING TO WRENCH THAT NECK TO DO EVEN MORE DAMAGE!
JONATHAN MARX: Psycho has an incredible pain tolerance, but I don’t know how much even he can take of this.
VIC WATERS: Golem is trying to take Psycho’s head out of the game so he can’t concentrate on what he needs to do.
BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM HITS PSYCHO WITH HANGMAN NECKBREAKER! Psycho grabs the back of his neck and he rolling around in pain.
JONATHAN MARX: Nobody would think anything less of Psycho if he to end the match early because he got hurt.
VIC WATERS: Psycho’s isn’t about to hand over the WFW North American Title to Golem, that means the world to him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Golem stands over Psycho…. GOLEM JUST SPIT IN HIS FACE! JONATHAN MARX: The issues between these two run deep, but there is no call for that. VIC WATERS: Golem is trying to get into Psycho’s head. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho tries to go after Golem but he grabs the back of his neck and Golem just laughs. GOLEM PICKS PSYCHO OFF THE MAT AND HE LOCKS HIM IN A FULL NELSON!
JONATHAN MARX: Golem is applying pressure directly to the back of Psycho’s neck and it doesn’t look like Golem is holding anything back.
VIC WATERS: It isn’t in Psycho to submit, but sometimes the body is weak even when the mind is strong and he could pass out from all of this pain if he can’t find a way to break free from this.
BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM IS REALLY DIGGING IN THE FULL NELSON AND PSCYHO IS TRYING TO FIGHT IT!
JONATHAN MARX: It is hard to get someone hands undone when they are right on your neck. VIC WATERS: Psycho’s best chance is to power out of this, thankfully Psycho and Golem are close to the same size.
BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO LOOKS LIKE HE IS MAKING PROGRESS…. PSYCHO BREAKS OUT! No…. GOLEM GRABS PSYCHO BY THE MASK AND PULLS HIM BACK INTO THE FULL NELSON!
VIC WATERS: Damn it, he nearly escaped. BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM DRIVES HIM INTO THE MAT WITH A FULL NELSON FACE SLAM! JONATHAN MARX: Some people think those mats are cushioned and when you take shots like that it can’t hurt as much as they look, having been in the squared circle, let me tell you, it hurts and it hurts bad. Those mats don’t have all that much give in them because of the behemoths that the ring needs to hold so if you were to weaken the ring, they would just fall right through.
BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM IS MAKING THE COVER! The ref goes down for the count! JONATHAN MARX: If Psycho knows what is good for him, he won’t kick out and he’ll come back to fight again another day.
VIC WATERS: Would you want to face Anarky, Wells, and Felix Red in the back if you lost the title?
JONATHAN MARX: Good point. BRANDON JACOBS: 1---------------------------------2------------------------------------------ PSYCHO GETS THE SHOULDER UP!
JONATHAN MARX: While I may not agree with Psycho’s hardcore ways, he is one of the toughest wrestlers I have ever seen.
VIC WATERS: Come on Psycho! BRANDON JACOBS: Golem is up first and he picks up Psycho from behind… GOLEM KNEES PSYCHO IN THE BACK OF THE NECK!
JONATHAN MARX: Good god, there was no call for that. BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is in Golem’s face and the referee is telling Golem he won’t put with any of his dirty tricks.
JONATHAN MARX: He could have easily broken his neck. VIC WATERS: I think that is exactly what he was trying to do, the more I see of Golem, the more I like him.
BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, PSYCHO IS OPENING UP HIS FANNY PACK AND JUST PULL A HANDFUL OF SOMETHING!
JONATHAN MARX: The referee had no time to take the fanny pack off of him because Golem attacked him right away.
VIC WATERS: Psycho is only doing what has been done to him. Golem would like nothing better to take Psycho out. Sometimes the best defense is a good offense.
BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM PUSHES AWAY THE REFEREE…. Golem goes to pick up Psycho…. PSYCHO THROWS THE THUMBTACKS RIGHT IN THE FACE OF GOLEM AND GOLEM IMMEDIATELY COVERS UP HIS EYES!
JONATHAN MARX: Damn that Psycho! That is illegal. VIC WATERS: I agree, the referee should hand the title back to Psycho and let him walk out with his title.
BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO IS GETTING UP BACK TO HIS FEET! Psycho starts punching away at the blinded Golem!
JONATHAN MARX: Psycho is blatantly taking advantage of a blinded man. I think he has sunk to a new low.
VIC WATERS: BEAT HIM LIKE A DOG! BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM IS STARTING TO LOSE HIS BALANCE…. PSYCHO BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES….. YAKUZA KICK RIGHT IN THE FACE OF GOLEM AND GOLEM GOES UP AND OVER THE TOP ROPE!
JONATHAN MARX: Golem is lucky he didn’t break a leg the way he fell onto the ground. VIC WATERS: Golem didn’t have his arms to brace himself because he was covering up his eyes when Psycho hit.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho walks over to the turnbuckle…. HE PULLS OUT THE FORK THAT GOLEM PLACED THERE AND HE RAISES IT UP IN THE AIR!
CROWD: BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! JONATHAN MARX: What is with this crowd in Boston being so into the LOVE tonight? VIC WATERS: They like rooting for a team which gets the job done because the Red Sox can’t.
JONATHAN MARX: Mark my words, the Red Sox will beat the Yankees this year and kill the curse.
VIC WATERS: You keep on telling yourself that. BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO IS CLIMBING UP TO THE TOP ROPE WITH THE FORK…. HE IS PUTTING THE FORK IN HIS FIST A QUARTER OF AN INCH EXPOSED…. PSYCHO FLIES OFF AND HITS GOLEM RIGHT IN THE HEAD WITH THE FLYING FIRST DROP! Golem rolls to his side…. GOOD GOD, IT LOOKS LIKE HE BUSTED HIM OPEN WITH THE FORK!
JONATHAN MARX: That fork is a dangerous weapon, whether it benefits Psycho or not, the referee should really disqualify him. A man’s career is at stake.
VIC WATERS: It is good that you are finally seeing things my way. Now if only the referee agreed.
BRANDON JACOBS: The referee isn’t going to let Psycho walk out of here champion that way. PSYCHO CRAWLS OVER TOWARDS GOLEM WITH THAT FORK AND KNEELS WITH HE KNEES RIGHT ON HIS CHEST AND HE IS REPEATEDLY TAKING THAT VERY EDGE OF THAT FORK AND IS TRYING TO BUST GOLEM OPEN EVEN MORE!
JONATHAN MARX: Both men are becoming a bloody mess. VIC WATERS: You can’t expect two men with such bad history together to just go out there and wrestle a clean wrestling match. They want to hurt each other because it is personal. It would be much more disturbing if they were exchanging wristlocks then bent on all out destruction of the other.
JONATHAN MARX: Every match requires its own unique approach because each match has its own history. Sometimes you can’t help but let your anger take control when you are in a situation as important as this.
BRANDON JACOBS: The referee is coming out of the ring to help Golem… HE GRABS PSYCHO’S ARM AND HE IS WARNING HIM THAT HE’LL AWARD GOLEM THE TITLE IF HE KEEPS THIS UP!
JONATHAN MARX: Finally, the referee is taking a stand! VIC WATERS: That is totally beyond his powers to do that though, it is an idle threat. JONATHAN MARX: With the way Wink has been trying to right wrongs, I wouldn’t bet on it. BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO PUSHES DOWN THE REFEREE! HE JUMPS ON TOP OF THE REFEREE AND HE STARTS ASSAILING THE REFEREE WITH THE FORK BUSTING HIM OPEN!
JONATHAN MARX: That is a WFW referee, he is going to get fined heavily for that if not worse.
VIC WATERS: Psycho has totally snapped. BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM TACKLES PSYCHO AND THEY ARE BATTLING OVER THE FORK ON THE OUTSIDE! Golem rams Psycho’s hand with the fork right into the guardrail…. FINALLY PSYCHO LETS GO AND THE FORK GOES INTO THE CROWD!
JONATHAN MARX: Golem may have save that referee’s life the way Psycho was going. VIC WATERS: Psycho is already nuts to begin with, Golem trying to cripple him by going after his neck earlier certainly didn’t help things.
BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM JUMPS UP AND DROPS A DOUBLE KNEE ON THE RIGHT ARM OF PSYCHO AND LOCKS IN A FUJIWARA ARMBAR!
JONATHAN MARX: WFW is the only place where you can see a transition from a man being stabbed in the head with a fork into a Fujiwara armbar.
VIC WATERS: I think it adds a touch of class. BRANDON JACOBS: Golem is trying to rip that arm out of its socket and the blood is pouring down Golem’s face into his eyes and he pulls with all he has. Psycho is bellowing in pain but the referee is down and it looks like he will be down for awhile.
JONATHAN MARX: Wait a second, here comes the WFW’s Cuban backup referee Umberto Gonzalez. He is finally going to get his break and referee a match.
VIC WATERS: You would think with all the referee that get hurt in WFW, that we would have more backups.
JONATHAN MARX: When you bring in Maelstrom, Dan Ryan, Lindsay Troy, and Christian Sands, you can’t exactly be spending extra money on referees.
VIC WATERS: Good point. BRANDON JACOBS: Gonzalez is telling Golem that they have to get back in the ring or he is going to declare this a count out.
JONATHAN MARX: Hopefully Golem doesn’t snap on Gonzalez for taking authority like Psycho did.
BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM LETS GO OF THE FUJIWARA ARMBAR! Psycho grabs his arm… and he is trying to get away from Golem.
VIC WATERS: Psycho is trying to buy himself some time so he can recoup. BRANDON JACOBS: Golem picks Psycho off the ground and he rolls him into the ring and comes in after him. Finally, both men are back in the ring.
JONATHAN MARX: Maybe we will have some order in this match now. BRANDON JACOBS: Golem drags Psycho into the middle of the ring… HE LOCKS IN A HALF NELSON EXPLOITING HIS NECK AND INJURED ARM! The ref comes in and he is checking on Psycho.
JONATHAN MARX: That is what is great about Golem, he can both brawl and wrestle with the best of them. He can adapt to any situation and those are the kinds of tools that would make him a great North American Champion.
VIC WATERS: If Golem wants to win the title, he is going to have to put Psycho away and that is very hard to do.
BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM SUPLEXES PSYCHO DOWN WITH A HALF NELSON SUPLEX AND PSYCHO LANDS RIGHT ON HIS HEAD!
JONATHAN MARX: My god, Psycho got spiked on that…. All those shots to the head can’t be good for you.
VIC WATERS: I don’t know how much more abuse Psycho can take. BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM LAYS OVER PSYCHO AND HE IS GOING FOR THE COVER! The ref goes down for the count! 1---------------------------------------------------------------------------2----------------------------------- PSYCHO KICKS OUT! We almost had a new WFW North American Champion!
JONATHAN MARX: Golem needs to use the heavy artillery to keep Psycho down and finish him off when he has the chance.
BRANDON JACOBS: Golem gets up and he starts yelling at Gonzalez for a slow count! VIC WATERS: It is his first time refereeing here in WFW, give him a break. JONATHAN MARX: A referee should not be learning in a match as big as this with so much on the line.
BRANDON JACOBS: Golem wipes the blood from his face and grabs the mask of Psycho….. PSYCHO NAILS HIM WITH A HARD PUNCH TO THE GUT AND WINDS GOLEM!
JONATHAN MARX: People always think you have to go for a nutshot to do serious damage, but a well placed shot to the stomach can do the same thing even if you are in as great of shape as Golem is.
VIC WATERS: Psycho is a really hard puncher too, he’d give Tyson a run for his money. JONATHAN MARX: Especially now… BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO GETS UP AND HE BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES….. PSYCHO HITS GOLEM WITH A FLYING FOREARM THAT SENDS HIM DOWN TO THE MAT!
JONATHAN MARX: Psycho got nailed right in the head with that shot. It was perfectly timed and Psycho got major air on that.
VIC WATERS: The flying forearm is without a doubt the most underrated move in all of wrestling.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho drags Golem towards the corner…. PSYCHO IS CLIMBING THE TURNBUCKLES ALL THE WAY TO THE TOP!
JONATHAN MARX: With Psycho having a bad neck and arm, I sort of question his judgment here.
VIC WATERS: Psycho didn’t get his name without being fearless. BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO IS SET ON THE TOP ROPE…. HE HITS THE FOUR FIFTY LEGDROP RIGHT ON THE HEAD OF GOLEM!
JONATHAN MARX: That is enough to put any man out! BRANDON JACOBS: The referee goes down for the count! 1--------------------------------------------2------------------------------------ GOLEM GETS HIS FOOT ON THE ROPES! Golem was a bit too close to the corner for that one.
JONATHAN MARX: Ring position is all so important, but with the shots that Psycho has taken tonight, one can understand why he is a bit out of it.
VIC WATERS: Psycho has his second wind and it couldn’t come at a better time. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho picks Golem up and it looks like he has decided he needs to do a little more damage to Golem if he is going to keep him down. PSYCHO STARTS DESTROYING GOLEM WITH KNEE LIFTS RIGHT INTO THE CHEST TO WORK OVER THOSE RIBS!
JONATHAN MARX: As great of a technician as Golem is, if he is having trouble breathing, there is only so much he can do.
BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, GOLEM IS FIGHTING BACK WITH RIGHT HANDS AND HE IS TRYING TO KEEP PSYCHO AT BAY! Psycho fires back with punches of his own and this has turned into an all out brawl!
CROWD: BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! BAD KICKS ASS! VIC WATERS: The hatred between these two men runs deep. Both men are trying to beat the other one senseless.
BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM WINDS UP AND HE NAILS PSYCHO WITH A DISCUS ELBOW WHICH SENDS HIM DOWN TO THE MAT! Psycho is trying to get back up…. PSYCHO CHARGES GOLEM AND GOLEM HITS HIM WITH A BACK DROP DRIVER AND DRIVES HIS HEAD HARD INTO THE MAT! Golem falls down in exhaustion from the knee lifts earlier.
JONATHAN MARX: Both men are physically exhausted. I don’t know how either man will be able to continue.
VIC WATERS: The WFW North American Title is on the line, this is the time when champions dig down deep and pull out their reserves.
BRANDON JACOBS: THE REFEREE IS PUTTING A COUNT ON BOTH MEN! ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! SIX! SEVEN! Golem is the first man up! Golem looks over at Psycho who is down….. GOLEM IS CALLING FOR THE CLAW!
JONATHAN MARX: Golem has been perfect in singles so far, but he has yet to have a chance to apply this move on anyone.
VIC WATERS: This move is illegal! JONATHAN MARX: The move is very close to the mandible claw which is technically legal so Wink greenlighted that it could be used without any repercussion. But it can do some serious damage…
BRANDON JACOBS: GOLEM IS CALLING FOR PSYCHO TO GET UP…. Psycho is trying to get back up to his feet…. PSYCHO TURNS AROUND AND GOLEM GOES FOR THE CLAW…. PSYCHO GRABS HIS HAND IS TRYING TO KEEP HIM FROM APPLYING IT!
CROWD: GOLEM! GOLEM! GOLEM! JONATHAN MARX: These fans want to see a title change. VIC WATERS: Psycho is going to do his damndest to prevent it from happening. BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO KICKS GOLEM IN THE GUT AND DOUBLES HIM OVER! VIC WATERS: He just saved his title right there. JONATHAN MARX: This match isn’t over yet. BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO HOOKS THE ARMS…… OH MY GOD! HE HIT GOLEM WITH THE TIGER DRIVER! VIC WATERS: YES! PIN HIM! BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO GOES FOR THE COVER! 1------------------------------------------------------------2--------------------------------------------- GOLEM KICKS OUT TO SAVE HIMSELF!
JONATHAN MARX: How did he kick out of that?! I thought for certain this was over. BRANDON JACOBS: This crowd can’t believe it either and Psycho has a look of bewilderment on his face trying to figure out what he can do to keep Golem down for the three. PSYCHO IS DRAGGING GOLEM OVER TO THE CORNER!
VIC WATERS: He must be going for the Psycho Flight. JONATHAN MARX: I’ve seen Psycho live and die on this move, it was because of the Psycho Flight that he lost the WFW World Heavyweight Title.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is climbing the ropes to the tope….. GOLEM IS BEGINNING TO MOVE! JONATHAN MARX: Golem is unbelievable! How is even moving after that Tiger Driver!? VIC WATERS: Psycho isn’t going to have time to do the Psycho Flight or he is going to be dead meat.
BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO SEES THAT GOLEM IS ABOUT TO STIR AND HE RUSHES UP TO THE TOP ROPE….. PSYCHO MOONSAULTS OFF THE TOP…. OH NO! Psycho didn’t get a full rotation and he did a 180 and fell head first into the ribs onto Golem!
JONATHAN MARX: Oh my god, someone better get some help for Psycho. BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO REACHES BACK WITH ARM AND MAKES THE COVER ON GOLEM! The ref goes down for the count! 1---------------------------------------------------------2----------------------------------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match….. AND STILL WFW NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION….. PSYCHO!
VIC WATERS: Psycho nearly killed himself for that win. JONATHAN MARX: Thankfully Psycho is moving, but he doesn’t look like he knows what is going on. It looks like he may have suffered a concussion.
BRANDON JACOBS: Golem gave Psycho a hell of a fight, Psycho is lucky to walk out of here WFW North American Champion.
JONATHAN MARX: No, that half rotation wasn’t any accident. Psycho saw that Golem was moving so he realized he didn’t have time for a full moonsault.
BRANDON JACOBS: You mean he purposely spiked his head into Golem in order to finish him off? Psycho is insane but…
JONATHAN MARX: Did you see him look back? BRANDON JACOBS: My god, you are right. He did look back and see Golem moving…. HE DID IT ON PURPOSE!
VIC WATERS: And that is why Psycho is still your WFW North American Champion…. BRANDON JACOBS: We have to get the medics down here to help everyone, folks, we have to take a commercial break, when we comeback, we will have more with the Inner Circle…. STAY TUNED!
