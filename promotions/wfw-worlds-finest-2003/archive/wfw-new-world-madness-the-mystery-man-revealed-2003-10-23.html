WFW: New World Madness - The Mystery Man Revealed

23 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 23 October 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: I have no idea what is going on. Why did Wylde leave so suddenly for the back?
VIC WATERS: Maybe he had a hot date… (CUEUP: "Diesel Power" by Prodigy, as Alex Wylde makes his way to the ring. Wylde is freshly showered and dressed from his previous match. He's wearing street clothes and carries with him a contract.)
BRANDON JACOBS: HE HELD UP THE WHOLE ENTIRE SHOW TO TAKE A SHOWER AND GET DRESSED? WYLDE: *Ahem*... ::the mystery man dressed in black stands by Wylde side:: V/O (over the PA): AND NOW, A MESSAGE FROM YOUR FIRST WFW PRESIDENT... WYLDE: Thank you. Well, good afternoon ladies and gentlemen. This is your first president, Alex Wylde, here to announce my FIRST official signing as WFW president. (He waves the contract to the crowd) It relates to a number of issues that I perceive in the WFW that need to be dealt with. Specifically...the emergence of a certain faction in this league. The C.O.C.K.S. around here are getting too big for their britches! (Crowd hoots)...What?...wha...get your mind out of the gutter. ANYWAY...now...any leader will tell you that unions and worker organizations are entirely unhealthy for an efficient workplace...and I'm sure each of you fans out there knows that. As such...I went to work immediately upon election...looking to find a man who could remedy this situation. Someone who could come here...provide some excitement...compete all over the card...and at the same time help to cut the C.O.C.K.S. short...so to speak. (Crowd hoots again)...dammit! It says something about you people that you get that excited at the thought of male genitalia. Fortunately...I completed the first step in my plan to improve this league by the addition of a great talent and the subtraction of a degenerate faction. Ladies and gentlemen...I introduce to you...the man who will help me take DOWN my enemies. A man who will electrify this ring and sell you tickets. He is a President's best friend. THIS... IS... ANARKY!
::Anarky pulls off the masks and laughs:: BRANDON JACOBS: OH MY GOD! ANARKY IS HERE IN WFW! VIC WATERS: Anarky is a living legend, he has won the FWF World Heavyweight Title, FWF Frontier Title, CSWA EN Heavyweight Title, CSWA Greensboro Title, ASWF World Heavyweight Title, ASWF US Title, AAWC US Title… and that is all before lunch.
BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY IS SLAPPING DIABLO AROUND AND IS TELLING DIABLO THAT ANARKY IS THE BEST BRAWLER IN ALL OF WRESTLING!
VIC WATERS: Well it is the truth… BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES SHAWN HART AND TINY WITH CHAIRS TO SAVE DIABLO! Anarky is motioning to Hart that he has something that HE wants and Hart is giving him a mouthful!
VIC WATERS: Anarky has fired off the first shot in what looks to be a much bigger war. God help the WFW.
BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we are going to take another commercial break, when we comeback, it is time for the three way dance to determine the stips for the PPV! We will be right back!
