WFW Turkey Day Terror: Manson vs. Anarky

11 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 11 December 2003 (BACKSTAGE) TOXICK: So I'm sitting there in nothing but a Bon Jovi T-shirt, bottle of scope in one hand, copy of the Boston Herald in the other, and all I could think of to say to this crazy ***** was "There are seven levels." And she tells me she'll blow me for some cra--
::a battle worn Felix Red approaches Toxick and roughly pulls him around:: TOXICK: Felix! Good match man. Wanna smoke crack with me 'n Luster in the parking lot? FELIX RED: Why are you here? TOXICK: Cuz we're getting the Crack Rock Crowd back together, bro!! All we need is t'find a suitable replacement for what's his name, and we'll own this rinky dinky little show ina mon---
FELIX: my previous association with you was a complete waste. Your petty materialistic ambition and you attempts to manipulate makes bile rise in my throat. your decadence has destroyed whatever was left of your value as a human being. I'm going to repeat to you what I said months ago...stay away from me, or I will bleed you dry. (Felix raises an eyebrow, shoots toxick a "**** off" smile, and storms off)
TOXICK: (takes a beat and shouts down the hallway...) Well WHAT THE F(BLEEP) DO YOU EXPECT?!?!?! My f(BLEEP) NAME is TOXICK!!!!! (another beat...Toxick sighs in frustration, pulls an empty Poland spring bottle with holes punched into
the sides and tin foil jammed around the edges of the top into the shot and takes a hit...)
(BROADCAST BOOTH) BRANDON JACOBS: So apparently Felix Red wasn’t in on tonight’s plan…. Interesting. Okay folks, it is time for one of three matches that could easily be called the main event of tonight’s show…. Manson will face off against Anarky for the very first time in WFW history. Your thoughts Vic?
VIC WATERS: These two have so much pent up emotions, this is going to be a match to remember. Both men are legends and want to prove to the other that they are the top dog.
BRANDON JACOBS: The deeper psychological context to this one is ordinary, although I doubt we will see much psychology in the match except utter destruction of the other. This one won’t be for the weak at heart so if you are viewing with children, send them to their rooms until the match is over. Let’s send this one to ringside and get this one underway!
(CUEUP: "Mechanical Animals" by Marilyn Manson) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the former WFW North American Champion, the man, the myth, the legend…. MICHAEL MANSON!
::Manson is walking down slowly to the ring:: BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second, WFW President Alex Wylde is out and he has ORDERED a dozen police officers to escort Manson to ringside for his own safety. Manson doesn’t appear happy about it at all.
VIC WATERS: Manson doesn’t need a dozen cops to protect him until he gets to into the ring.
(CUEUP: “Sober” by Tool) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, a world champion ten times over and former Mr. Vicious title holder, from Hartford, Connecticut… ANARKY!
::Anarky comes down with his leather coat walks through a sea of police officers and shakes their hand as he makes his way through the crowd and enters the ring to a mixed reaction::
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky is looking into his coat…. HE PULLS OUT A NIGHTSTICK AND HE CHARGES MANSON AND NAILS HIM UPSIDE THE HEAD!
VIC WATERS: DOWN GOES MANSON! ::fans throw garbage into the ring:: BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky is wailing away at the ribs of Manson will that huge black nightstick and Manson is screaming in pain! The ref is coming over to try to wrestle the nightstick out of Anarky’s hands… HE PUSHES HIM DOWN! Anarky continues to beat on Manson! This match has gotten out of control and the bell hasn’t even officially run yet!
VIC WATERS: One has to wonder if Wylde set Manson up. Who in their right mind would bring all these weapons down to ringside for Anarky to use? Manson would have been better off being jumped.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde is ordering the police into the ring in order to get nightstick out of Anarky’s hand. Anarky turns around and looks at the police and they are all sporting their own nightsticks. Anarky throws the nightstick to the outside of the ring and police back off, but the damage has been done. ANARKY LOCKS MANSON IN THE BEAR HUG!
::Wylde on the outside encourages the ref go back in there and referee the match:: VIC WATERS: This ref looks scared for his life. That makes it awfully hard to referee a match like this.
::bell rings:: BRANDON JACOBS: The match is now officially underway over five minutes after the action first began. Manson is screaming out in pain and the fans are already chanting Manson’s name in order to try to get him back into this match!
VIC WATERS: While Manson has never given up, when you injure the ribs of someone like Anarky did, it can really take the wind out of a wrestler in a highly competitive match like this one.
BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY LIFTS MANSON UP IN THE AIR AND HE IS TREATING HIM LIKE A RAGDOLL! The ref wants to check on the arm but Anarky moves him away….
VIC WATERS: Anarky must know he is just doing this to wear Manson down so he doesn’t want to bother with the referee checking Manson’s status.
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky dumps Manson down on the ground…. ANARKY BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES..... KNEE DROP INTO THE INJURED RIBS! Manson is rolling around on the ground gasping for air! The ref goes down to check to see if Manson wants to give up by he is shaking his head no!
VIC WATERS: Manson isn’t going to give up that easily. BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky picks Manson up…. ANARKY DROPS MANSON STOMACH FIRST ON HIS KNEE AND MANSON IS COUGHING UP A STORM! Anarky makes the cover! 1--------------------------------2---------------------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Manson must really be hurt for Anarky to have gotten a near fall like that so early in this match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky picks Manson off the mat…. Anarky backs up and sets…. ANARKY SPEARS MANSON AND HE GOES TUMBLING OUT OF THE RING ONTO THE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: Manson took a nasty spill out onto the floor. He may have even bumped his head on the guardrail from the noise I heard.
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky reaches into his coat…. ANARKY HAS A PAIR OF HANDCUFFS AND HE SHACKLES MANSON TO THE GUARDRAIL!
VIC WATERS: I guess this explains why Anarky spent so much time shaking hands with the police officers on the outside. HE WAS ROBBING THEM BLIND!
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky is walking over to the time keeper’s tabe… HE HAS THE RING BELL AND HE IS LIFTING IT UP FOR ALL TO SEE! If he nails Manson with this, he is finished! The ref is telling Anarky to put it down but he won’t listen…… WYLDE IS INSTRUCTING THE POLICE TO SURROUND MANSON UNTIL THEY HAVE HIM UNCUFFED!
VIC WATERS: Wylde is over stepping his bounds interfering in this match. BRANDON JACOBS: This match isn’t a falls count anywhere match and it most certainly isn’t being fought on BAD World Heavyweight Title rules. The fans want to see a winner in this match and if Anarky nailed him with the bell, the ref would have no choice but to call for a DQ.
VIC WATERS: Management should have known what they were getting into when they signed this match.
BRANDON JACOBS: The police finally have Manson uncuffed… ANARKY IS BATTLING HIS WAY THROUGH THE POLICE…. HE SWINGS AT MANSON WITH THE RING BELL BUT MANSON DUCKS AND ANARKY CLOCKS A POLICE OFFICER ACCIDENTLY WHO FALLS BACKWARDS ONTO THE REF! Manson charges Anarky…. HE KICKS HIM IN THE FACE AND ANARKY GOES DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Manson with his first breath of life in the match. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson takes the nightstick from the fallen officer and STARTS BEATING THE CRAP OUT OF ANARKY WITH HIS NIGHTSTICK AS THE COPS ARE CHEERING HIM ON!
VIC WATERS: I haven’t seen cops enjoy beating someone this much since Rodney King. BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky is trying to crawl away…. MANSON TAKES THE NIGHTSTICK AND TRIES TO SODOMIZE ANARKY AND ANARKY CRAWLS AWAY FOR HIS LIFE!
VIC WATERS: Sodomy is technically legal now. BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky is reaching into his coat…. ANARKY HAS A TASER! Anarky is warning Manson to stay back… MANSON LUNGES AT ANARKY AND THEY ARE FIGHTING OVER THE TASER AS THE FIRST SEVERAL ROWS MOVE BACK IN FEAR OF A MISFIRE!
VIC WATERS: This is really getting out of control now. The police need to step in and break this up.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde runs by…. HE GRABS THE TASER OUT OF THEIR HANDS! Anarky runs after Wylde but MANSON TACKLES HIM ON THE FLOOR! Manson starts laying into him with lefts and rights to head! Anarky is trying to push him off to no avail!
VIC WATERS: Thank god Wylde was here or this could have turned really ugly. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson reaches into Anarky’s coat…. HE HAS POLICE OFFICER’S STAR! Manson takes the star and HE IS CUTTING INTO THE FOREHEAD OF ANARKY IN ORDER TO DRAW HIS BLOOD!
VIC WATERS: This is barbaric. Where is the damn referee to put a stop to this? BRANDON JACOBS: Here comes the referee… and Manson throws the star down but Anarky is now bleeding. THE REF IS ORDERING BOTH MEN TO GET BACK INTO THE RING OR HE IS GOING TO DQ BOTH OF THEM! Manson rolls Anarky into the ring and goes in after him.
VIC WATERS: The referee is finally asserting some control in this match. Hopefully things will quiet down now that they are in the ring.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is pull Anarky towards the center of the ring…. MANSON IS PULLING THE LEATHER JACKET OVER ANARKY’S HEAD…. THE JACKET IS FINALLY OFF!
VIC WATERS: That jacket is like Inspector Gadget’s Hat, you never know what is going to come out of that thing.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson picks up Anarky off the mat…. WILD BUCK BOMB! Anarky grabs his back! That had to hurt something fierce.
VIC WATERS: Manson is trying to turn this into a wrestling match which would steer the advantage back towards him. While Manson was an elite brawler early on his career, he has focused more in recent years on the wrestling and proving himself as one of the best in that ring.
BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON NAILS ANARKY REPEATEDLY WITH HEAD THRUST INTO THE LOWER BACK! VIC WATERS: Besides from the knee and the elbow, the head is the hardest part of the body which is why great wrestlers like Harley Race and Chris Benoit used the flying headbutt.
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky is down and he is trying to slink away from Manson…. Manson picks Anarky up…. ARGENTINA BACKBREAKER! Manson is trying to break the back and there for the will of Anarky and he is using all of the strength in his body to do so.
VIC WATERS: Manson doesn’t usually use this move, which shows the great respect that Manson has for Anarky because he is digging deep into his repertoire in order to beat him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky to trying desperately to break free…. HE FALLS BEHIND MANSON AND PUSHES HIM IN TO THE ROPES… EVENFLOWE DDT BY ANARKY! Anarky tries to get up…. But his back is hurting him too much and he cannot stand!
VIC WATERS: That isn’t good, if Anarky’s back is shot, it is only a matter of time before Manson will capitalize on it.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is putting the count on both men! 1---2---3---4---5------6--- 7----8---- ANARKY IS STARTING TO GET UP BUT HE NEEDS THE HELP OF THE ROPES TO DO IT!
VIC WATERS: Manson’s ribs and Anarky’s back… this war has taken a toll on both of them. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is getting up slowly… Anarky bounces off the ropes…. FLYING FOREARM! Anarky makes the cover! 1----------------------------------------2-------------------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Anarky is clearly frustrated that he can’t keep Manson down. BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky picks up Manson…. HE IS SETTING HIM UP FOR A SWINGING NECK BREAKER… MANSON IS FIGHTING THE HOLD AND ANARKY CAN’T PUT UP MUCH RESISTANCE WITH HIS BAD BACK! Manson nails Anarky with a snap suplex!
VIC WATERS: Manson is all out of breath from lifting up Anarky with the bad ribs. The referee may need to call this match.
BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON TAKES OFF HIS BOOT AND HE BEGINS WAILING ON THE LEGS OF ANARKY! The ref is warning Manson that if he continues he’ll be DQed. Wylde jumps on the apron and says that since the boot came wearing the boot that it should be allowed since it isn’t foreign to the match.
VIC WATERS: By that right, you could strangle someone with your shoe laces and that would be perfectly legal.
BRANDON JACOBS: Well, if you can strangle someone with your shoe laces, they aren’t much of a competitor. MANSON CONTINUES TO GO TO DOWN AND ANARKY IS YELPING IN PAIN! The ref finally has enough and grabs the shoe of Manson and throws it to the outside and this crowd is rightfully booing the referee.
VIC WATERS: The ref is going over the head of Wylde. I don’t understand how one hopes to be employed when he does that.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson grabs the legs of Anarky…. HE LOCKS ON THE SHARPSHOOTER! The referee goes to check on Anarky… but Anarky is refusing to tap!
VIC WATERS: Anarky is a tough SOB, Manson basically tenderized Anarky’s legs and Manson injured his back, but he fighting through the pain.
BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky is fighting…. But it looks like the hold is sapping his energy…. Anarky is trying desperately to use his arms to pull himself towards the ropes… ANARKY IS REACHING…. MANSON PULLS HIM AWAY!
VIC WATERS: Anarky looks exhausted, I don’t how much more of this he can take. BRANDON JACOBS: The referee goes to check on Anarky…. ANARKY GRABS THE REFEREE’S SHIRT AND THE REF FALLS DOWN INTO MANSON BREAKING THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: This ref is taking a lot of abuse tonight, that is why I’m glad I’m announcer. BRANDON JACOBS: Manson grabs the referee and throws him down to the ground. Manson charges Anarky…. LARIAT OVER THE TOP ROPE AND ANARKY CRASHES INTO THE GUARDRAIL!
VIC WATERS: Anarky managed to avoid crashing down on his injured back, but man, Anarky looks irate.
BRANDON JACOBS: ANARKY PICKS UP THE GUARDRAIL AT RINGSIDE AND THROWS IT DOWN BREAKING THE CONNECTION! Some people in the first few rows are throwing Cokes at Anarky…. OH NO! ANARKY IS ATTACKING THE FANS WHO THREW THE SODA AT HIM AND OTHER FANS ARE COMING TO HELP!
VIC WATERS: Oh ****, we need security out here pronto! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is yelling at Anarky…. Anarky turns around in the mob…. MANSON BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND HE DIVES ONTO ANARKY IN THE MIDDLE OF THE CROWD!
VIC WATERS: GOOD GOD! BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON PICKS ANARKY UP OFF THE GROUND…. MANSON GRABS A BEER BOTTLE FROM A FAN… HE CRACKS ANARKY OVER THE HEAD WITH IT AND IT SHATTERS! The referee is calling for the bell and is sending security down to break up the chaos! ANARKY IS SOMEHOW STILL STANDING TRYING TO FIGHT THROUGH THE CROWD AND THE POLICE TO GET TO MANSON… THE POLICE ARE DRAWING THEIR GUNS TO KEEP THESE TWO MEN AWAY FROM EACH OTHER AND TO BREAK UP THE RIOT! Wylde is coming in and he jumps on Anarky to subdue him! The police are coming in and they are handcuffing Anarky and Manson and taking to the back!
::Vic Waters grabs his bottle of Poland Spring Water and starts walking towards the back:: BRANDON JACOBS: THE POLICE ARE TRYING TO GET THE PEOPLE TO CALM DOWN! Several other people are being cuffed and being taken out for security reasons…
RING ANNOUNCER: THE REF HAS DECLARED THIS MATCH A DOUBLE DISQUALIFICATION! BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we will try to get this situation calmed down and when we comeback, Sean Edmunds, Scotty Michaels, Copycat, and Alex Wylde will face off in a four way elimination match…. STAY TUNED!
