26 August 2004

PaulNJ21 — 26 August 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: Now we kick off the second half of the show after our brief intermission with a match that has people talking, Anarky will square off in a three way dance with Scotty Michaels and Larry Tact and there is absolutely no room for error because this is one fall to a finish.
JONATHAN MARX: This will be one of those matches that people will be talking about at next year’s Cherry Blossom Chaos. Anarky is out for revenge against Michaels, Tact wants a piece of Anarky because of what he did to him in the strap match, and Michaels would like nothing better to make an impact and defeat Anarky in his first match back from his suspension.
VIC WATERS: You have to love Scotty Michaels, other people are turning to run when they see LOVE but he is one of the few with balls enough to stand up to them.
JONATHAN MARX: There is an argument to be made that they are even more despicable than LOVE.
VIC WATERS: How can that possible be when LOVE is four guys of the caliber of Anarky, Felix Red, Psycho, and Wells and Michaels & Hart are only two men?
JONATHAN MARX: Remember, Shawn Hart was one half of COCKS and they terrorized this league for a large part of WFW’s first year with his partner Diablo who coincidentally retired after he beat Anarky in that Exploding Barbed Wire Death match… and Scotty Michaels is a former WFW World Heavyweight Champion.
VIC WATERS: So is Psycho… and by the time LOVE is done tonight, so will Felix Red so two out of four will have their name on the WFW World Heavyweight Title history and Jared Wells is holding that BAD World Heavyweight Title taking it to new heights.
BRANDON JACOBS: Jared Wells may wind up challenging Rat Fink when all is said in done with the legacy he brought the BAD World Heavyweight Title.
JONATHAN MARX: Jared Wells isn’t getting passed Iron Lion. VIC WATERS: Blasphemy! BRANDON JACOBS: You would have to think that the winner of this three way match may be in line for a World Heavyweight Title shot at the next PPV.
VIC WATERS: Felix Red vs. Anarky would be a hell of a match…. The great high flyer with great martial arts skill against one of the alltime great brawlers in Anarky.
JONATHAN MARX: What makes you so sure Felix Red is going to beat Manson? VIC WATERS: Manson has been wrestling a lot of close matches lately and Felix Red has the ability to put his opponent away at any moment.
JONATHAN MARX: We will see, I have a feeling Manson has a few tricks up his sleeve as well.
BRANDON JACOBS: Before we write in Anarky for the WFW World Heavyweight Title shot, he needs to beat Michaels and Larry Tact and Larry Tact almost BEAT Anarky in ANARKY’S own match and he is a former NEW World Heavyweight Champion. Maybe, just maybe he’ll do it here tonight and go on to be the first man ever to capture both titles.
VIC WATERS: Keep on dreaming. JONATHAN MARX: I hope so, Larry Tact is one of my favorite wrestlers today, I would love nothing better to see him as WFW World Heavyweight Champion someday defending his title against the best that wrestling has to offer on a show in and show out basis.
BRANDON JACOBS: This is going to be a close battle, anyone could win this one, folks, lets send this one down to ringside and get this one underway!
(CUEUP: "Karma" by Diffuser) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, one of the best technicians in all of wrestling today, Manhattan, New York… LARRY TACT!
