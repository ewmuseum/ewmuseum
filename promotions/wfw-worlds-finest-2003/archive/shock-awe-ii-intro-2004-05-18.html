Shock & Awe II: Intro

18 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 18 May 2004 ::Black Rose who is doing a report outside the building interviewing fans for a new WFW commercial to air during NEW TV all of a sudden sees Chris Wink pull up to the building in a black Hummer. Chris Wink rushes up to the car:
BLACK ROSE: Wink, what are you doing here? The board of directors almost never shows up at these things.
::Chris Wink grabs his bag as he exits the car:: CHRIS WINK: One of the board of directors asked me to come here tonight to my hometown of Albany, New York in order to make sure that El Arco Iris and Richard Gideon have a fair chance to win the match tonight with LOVE running rampant throughout World’s Finest Wrestling.
BLACK ROSE: Are you going to be a special referee or get physically involved tonight? CHRIS WINK: Our own bylaws prohibit that from happening. This league isn’t about the board of directors, it is about the wrestlers inside of the squared circle which you see competing each and every week. BUT WE WILL NOT LET OUR LEAGUE BE MANIPULATED BY A BUNCH OF THUGS! It is bad enough what they did to Copycat and El Arco Iris at War Games, we won’t let it happen twice. Justice will prevail.
BLACK ROSE: Thank you for your time Wink. CHRIS WINK: Now get the hell out of my way, I have things to do. ::pushes Black Rose and Wink walks towards the building, Black Rose looks bewildered at Wink for his actions::
(RING ANNOUNCERS) BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome everyone to the Pepsi Arena in Albany, New York, tonight we bring you Shock & Awe II!
VIC WATERS: Do you remember the debacle that was the first Shock & Awe? BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon who was teaming with Psycho got a pinfall victory over Doc Silver & Shawn Hart when Gideon pinned Doc with the System Failure and in the main event, the ring was literally destroyed Manson and then Rat Fink stole both Manson’s North American Title belt and his van. It remains to this day the only WFW show where the main event was a no contest.
VIC WATERS: And that is covering a lot of ground now as we start year two with this show… BRANDON JACOBS: I just thank god we have a more mild mannered crew and we won’t see it happen again… ::looks down at his preparation sheet:: Good god, it is all going to happen again, isn’t it? That is why they wanted us to go over all this back history… Can’t we just have a nice normal show where nothing happens? They know LOVE is going to try something, that is why Wink is here, and who knows what Scotty Michaels and this mystery man are planning tonight.
VIC WATERS: Calm down, get out your inhaler, it is going to be a long night. BRANDON JACOBS: And what about Copycat verses Edmunds, is either man going to win their match tonight to make sure it finally happens at the PPV? They have been waiting for years to face off one on one but Pulsar and Tact are going to do their damnedest to make sure that doesn’t happen.
VIC WATERS: Maybe this time it will be different ::breaks out in laughter:: BRANDON JACOBS: This isn’t funny. Do you know how many people actually want to see that match? We get letters about that match all the time from people demanding that it will happen.
VIC WATERS: Let them write their letters. I’m telling you know, there is a better chance of you winning Survivor than Copycat verses Edmunds ever happening.
BRANDON JACOBS: ::shakes head:: We also have a three way tonight to determine who will get the shot at the PPV against Manson between Felix Red, El Arco Iris, and the former and longest reigning WFW North American Champion of alltime, Richard Gideon. I hope and pray that the seven foot tall Wink being here means that all three men will be allowed to have a fair chance.
VIC WATERS: When will you learn Jacobs that the power of LOVE cannot be stopped? BRANDON JACOBS: I believe in Wink and I believe that either El Arco Iris or Gideon will step up and prevail.
VIC WATERS: What is the name of the show? BRANDON JACOBS: Shock & Awe… oh damn, ::puts his head in his hands:: we are all doomed ::reaches over for his inhaler::. VIC WATERS: Folks, when we comeback, we will have the debut of two newcomers to WFW, Mike Lane and Michaels Dice… as Jacobs would say… STAY TUNED! ::looks over at Jacobs:: You want some of my Poland Spring water?
