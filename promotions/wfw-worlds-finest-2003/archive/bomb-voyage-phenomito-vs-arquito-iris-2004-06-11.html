BOMB Voyage: PHENOMITO vs. Arquito Iris

11 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 11 June 2004 JONATHAN MARX: We are back and it is time for our opening match of the evening. Who do you like in this one Vic?
VIC WATERS: PHENOMITO! He loves the boobies and who can blame him. JONATHAN MARX: Certainly not I. What about Arquito Iris? He is all heart. VIC WATERS: Psst. Everyone in the minis division is his size. JONATHAN MARX: Oh yeah, well then I can throw away with cliché. ::throws away a note card:: But I still think Arquito Iris is going to find a way to win this one and do El Arco Iris proud…
VIC WATERS: Is he going to go around hugging people like the maxi version? If so, I’m going to have to get my boots on and do some shrimping.
JONATHAN MARX: You’ll do nothing of the sort, these minis are protected by the league. We aren’t going to have them be fodder to LOVE and Michaels & Bartleby every week so are there are going to be stiff penalties for punking them out.
VIC WATERS: Is WFW running a midget nature reserve or something in an effort to reintroduce them into the wild world of wrestling?
JONATHAN MARX: Just think of it if NFW introduces their own line of minis, Craig Quarter-Miles, Joey Melted, and little Hornie.
VIC WATERS: What did you say? JONATHAN MARX: LITTLE HORNIE! VIC WATERS: I sure am, LETS BRING OUT THE MIDGETS! JONATHAN MARX: Now you are trying to creep me out… but what the hell, lets send out the minis and get this one underway!
(CUEUP: “"Opblaaskrokodil" by Ome Henk”) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, El Arco Iris’ inner child…. ARQUITO IRIS! ::Arquito Iris walks down the aisle to a god pop from the children on the ship:: (CUEUP: “"You're The One I Want" by John Travolta and Olivia Newton-John from the Grease soundtrack)
RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, every woman’s wet dream…. PHENOMITO! ::PHENOMITO walks down to the boos of the crowd, he hands his robe to the ring girl and promptly pinches her behind before rolling in the ring::
JONATHAN MARX: Damn lecherous minis… VIC WATERS: That sexual harassment was fun size. *bell rings* JONATHAN MARX: Arquito Iris rushes over and dropkicks PHENOMITO as he tries enter the ring! PHENOMITO FALLS THROUGH AND GRABS THE BOTTOM RING ROPE WITH ONE HAND TO AVOID FALLING TO THE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: That is a nasty spill when you are three feet tall. JONATHAN MARX: PHENOMITO grabs on with the other hand… ARQUITO IRIS GRABS THE SECOND ROPE AND HE USES IT TO JUMP UP AND DRIVE BOTH FEET RIGHT INTO THE FACE OF PHENOMITO THROUGH THE FIRST AND SECOND ROPE AND PHENOMITO LOSES HIS GRIP AND FALLS OUT TO THE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: PHENOMITO did his best but he couldn’t hold on… JONATHAN MARX: An irritated PHENOMITO grabs the dress of a lady at ringside and PULLS OFF HER SKIRT AS HE TRIES TO GET UP! Look at Arquito Iris…. ARQUITO BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES…. THE OLD WOMAN CLUNKS PHENOMITO IN THE HEAD AND HE TURNS AROUND AND ARQUITO IRIS FLIES THROUGH THE FIRST AND SECOND ROPES AND NAILS PHENOMITO WITH A TOPE RIGHT IN THE CHEST!
VIC WATERS: Arquito Iris is living up to his name sake… JONATHAN MARX: Both men are down and the referee is putting the twenty count on both! 1----2-----3---4----5-----6—Arquito Iris gets up first and he picks PHENOMITO up by the back of the head… ARQUITO IRIS STARTS LAYING INTO PHENOMITO WITH HARD CHOPS TO THE CHEST!
VIC WATERS: PHENOMITO is going to beet red tomorrow and when you are his size, you can’t be mistaken for a beet.
JONATHAN MARX: Wait a second, PHENOMITO IS COMING BACK WITH SOME CHOPS OF HIS OWN! 11---12--- 13---- PHENOMITO AND IRISH WHIPS HIM INTO THE RING AND ARQUITO IRIS CRASHES IN HEAD FIRST!
VIC WATERS: Ouch… JONATHAN MARX: PHENOMITO runs over to the ring and he tries to get back into the ring…. PHENOMITO CAN’T JUMP UP HIGH ENOUGH TO GET IN!
VIC WATERS: This could be a double countout right here! JONATHAN MARX: 15--- 16-- PHENOMITO is calling to a fan in the audience to throw him a chair and PHENOMITO ducks as it goes soaring for his head! PHENOMITO picks up the chair and sets it up…. PHENOMITO grabs Arquito Iris and he climbs up on the chair and he pushes in Arquito Iris and rolls in after him to JUST beat the twenty count! I think the ref may have been liberal when he realized both men were short on time.
VIC WATERS: That isn’t the only thing they are short on. JONATHAN MARX: PHENOMITO picks up Arquito Iris… DISCUS PUNCH RIGHT TO THE FACE! Arquito Iris grabs his chin and stumbles backwards…. PHENOMITO NAILS HIM WITH A SECOND DISCUS PUNCH AND THIS TIME ARQUITO IRIS GOES DOWN!
VIC WATERS: PHENOMITO is nail him with hard rights right in the face. Thank god he is wearing that mask because he isn’t going to be pretty in the morning.
JONATHAN MARX: PHENOMITO rushes to pick Arquito Iris off the mat… HE IS GOING FOR A TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER! PHENOMITO picks Arquito Iris up… HE NAILS IT!
VIC WATERS: PHENOMITO has completely turned around this match in his favor, but he can’t waste any time, he needs to put his opponent away as soon as possible.
JONATHAN MARX: PHENOMITO is going for the cover! The referee goes down for the count! 1---------------------------2--------------------- ARQUITO IRIS KICKS OUT AND PHENOMITO LANDS ON THE BACK OF THE REFEREE!
VIC WATERS: He is quite a strong fellow. JONATHAN MARX: THE REFEREE GETS UP AND HE IS TRYING TO GET PHENOMITO OFF OF HIS BACK AND HE RUNNING AROUND IN A CIRCLE LIKE A CHICKEN WITH HIS HEAD CUT OFF!
VIC WATERS: He must be afraid of midgets. JONATHAN MARX: A dazed Arquito Iris gets up….. HE DROP TOE HOLDS THE REF AND HE TAKES HIM DOWN TO THE MAT FACE FIRST!
VIC WATERS: That is one way to stop him. JONATHAN MARX: ARQUITO IRIS STEPS ON THE BEHIND OF THE REFEREE AND HE GRABS PHENOMITO FROM BEHIND WITH A WAISTLOCKI!
VIC WATERS: The ref is not going to appreciate this. JONATHAN MARX: Arquito Iris german suplexes PHENOMITO off of the back of the referee and down to the mat! A spooked referee gets up and he yells at both men who are down.
VIC WATERS: You couldn’t pay me enough to be a referee in this match. JONATHAN MARX: ARQUITO IRIS DIVES ONTO PHENOMITO TO MAKE THE COVER! 1--------------------------------------------2----------------------- PHENOMITO KICKS OUT!
VIC WATERS: Very close… JONATHAN MARX: Arquito Iris picks a dazed PHENOMITO off of the mat and he climbs onto the second rope of the ring ropes….. PAPER CROWN OF HATHOR….PHENOMITO BLOCKS IT AND HE THROWS ARQUITO ONTO THE LEGS OF THE REFEREE AND HE STUMBLES DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Watch out for flying midgets… JONATHAN MARX: PHENOMITO is waiting for Arquito Iris to get up so he can hit his Sparkler of Doom… Arquito Iris is getting up along with the referee…. PHENOMITO CHARGES WITH THE ELBOW BUT THE ARQUITO IRIS DUCKS AND HE CLOCKS THE REFEREE RIGHT IN THE HEAD!
VIC WATERS: We know whose midget he is now… JONATHAN MARX: PHENOMITO is yelling for the referee to get up by he got clocked something fierce… WAIT A SECOND, ARQUITO IRIS IS SET UP FROM BEHIND TO HIT THE HAPPY LITTLE DANCE…
VIC WATERS: Don’t turn around PHENOMITO! JONATHAN MARX: PHENOMITO TURNS…. ARQUITO IRIS HITS HIM WITH THE SUPERKICK RIGHT IN THE HEAD!
VIC WATERS: Poor PHENOMITO… JONATHAN MARX: The referee is starting to get up on his hands and knees… ARQUITO IRIS IS CALLING TO THE CROWD…. HE IS CALLING FOR THE TRIPLE LINDY!
VIC WATERS: The Triple Lindy? What is the triple lindy? JONATHAN MARX: Arquito Iris runs to the other side of the ring….. ARQUITO IRIS CHARGES THE ROPES AND HE JUMPS ON THE SECOND ROPE AND DOES A FLIP ONTO THE BACK OF THE REFEREE, HE THEN FLIPS OFF THE REFEREE’S BACK RIGHT ONTO PHENOMITO! HOLY COW!
CROWD: IRIS! IRIS! IRIS! VIC WATERS: Eat your heart out Rodney. JONATHAN MARX: Arquito Iris makes the cover! 1-------------------------------------------------2------------------------------------------3! HE GOT IT!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. ARQUITO IRIS! JONATHAN MARX: What a match and what a way to start off the legacy of the minis. VIC WATERS: One small step for man, one giant leap for midgets everywhere. JONATHAN MARX: Folks, when we comeback, Mike Lane and Michael Dice will face off to determine who will get the BAD World Heavyweight Title shot later on against Jared Wells…. STAY TUNED EVERYONE!
