WFW: Spirit of NGEN: Andre Eian vs. Diablo

17 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 17 August 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: I can’t believe Andre Eian is putting his career on the line tonight. I still remember his great feud with McGee on the very first WFW show in the first round and how both he and McGee stepped up big time to steal the show.
VIC WATERS: Sadly, that was the highlight for both performers. Eian was close though to winning the WFW in his battle with Manson, but Diablo went and spoiled his opportunity to win the WFW North American Title. That is why we are here today, Eian wants to get revenge on Diablo come hell or high water and he even agreed to a Flaming Tables match to get it. Foolish boy.
BRANDON JACOBS: Eian isn’t foolish. He just has a lot of pride. He worked hard to get that shot at Manson and Diablo went and spoiled it. Tonight he is a on a mission to prove to C.O.C.K.S. and the rest of the company that he is a player in this sport and he is going to make a stand tonight against Diablo to prove he belongs with the best collection of talent on the planet.
VIC WATERS: Eian still has his back to the wall tonight. I hope he does beat C.O.C.K.S. tonight, nobody hates C.O.C.K.S. more than I do, but Diablo is a menace.
BRANDON JACOBS: You may be right Vic, but there is only one way to find out, let’s send this one to ringside!
::Cue up: “Bawitdaba” by Kid Rock:: RING ANNOUNCER: This match is Flaming Tables match! The first man to put other through a flaming table wins the match, Eian has said if he loses the match, that he will retire! Introducing first, on his way to the ring, standing at 6’6” and weighing at 246 pounds, “The Shadow” Andre Eian!
::the crowd pops as Eian makes his way down to the ring past the four tables surrounding the ring::
CUEUP: “The Theme From Dragnet”) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, one of the founding members of C.O.C.K.S. and one of the best young wrestlers in all of wrestling today, DIABLO!
:Diablo comes down in his C.O.C.K.S to the boos from a crowd: and enters the ring:: ::bell rings:: BRANDON JACOBS: EIAN RUSHES DIABLO AND DIABLO IS TAKEN DOWN WITH A TACKLE! Eian jumps on Diablo and starts pounding him with haymakers to the face! Diablo is trying to struggle to break free!
VIC WATERS: Eian has the most fire I’ve seen since he debuted here in WFW. BRANDON JACOBS: He knows what is at stake in this match, this match is for the whole ball of wax. If he wins this, he will cement himself as a superstar here in WFW. If he loses, he goes home. DIABLO RAKES EIAN IN THE FACE! Eian rolls off and grabs his eye! DIABLO KICKS EIAN IN THE SIDE OF THE HEAD! DIABLO PUTS HIS FOOT ON THE THROAT OF EIAN AND PULLS ON THE ROPES TO CEMENT HIS FOOTING! The ref is telling Diablo to break but he will have none of it!
VIC WATERS: The only way this match can end is if someone is put through a table. Diablo can’t get DQed for choking Eian so he is going to take full advantage of the rules as he knows how!
BRANDON JACOBS: EIAN MANAGES TO PULL HIMSELF OUT OF THE RING AND OUT OF THE CLUTCHES OF DIABLO! Eian is coughing on the outside trying to regain his break as he uses the table to try to help him get up. Diablo climbs onto the apron… DOUBLE AXE HANDLE ONTO EIAN! Eian’s head collides right into the table and he is busted open!
VIC WATERS: That really looks like a nasty gash, the blood is streaming down his face. He needs to becareful that the blood doesn’t effect his vision.
BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo grabs Eian… HE THROWS HIM INTO THE STEPS AND EIAN TUMBLES IN HARD! What is Diablo doing?! He is taking the blood of Eian and smearing it on his face as a kind of war paint!
VIC WATERS: Diablo is one sick individual you don’t want to meet up with in a dark alley. COME ON EIAN!
BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo picks Eian up by the hair… FOREARM SMASH TO THE FACE! Diablo stumbles back!
VIC WATERS: Where did that come from?! Eian looked like toast! BRANDON JACOBS: EIAN PICKS DIABLO UP… DIABLO IS FIGHTING… HE CAN’T AVOID IT! EIAN URANGES DIABLO THROUGH THE UNLIT TABLE!
::fans chant R-I-P at Diablo:: VIC WATERS: If Eian had lit the table on fire, this match would be over already but Eian just wants to inflect punishment to Diablo before he beats him!
BRANDON JACOBS: Eian picks up Diablo from the scattered debris on the ground….. EIAN SLAMS HIM BACK DOWN INTO THE SHARP REMNANTS OF THE TABLE!
VIC WATERS: It looks like Diablo’s back is bleeding! One of the pieces of the table must have nicked him when Eian slammed him down.
BRANDON JACOBS: Eian picks up Diablo by the hair and drags him to next side of the ring! DIABLO BREAKS FREE! DIABLO NAILS EIAN WITH LEFTS AND RIGHTS! Eian looks like he is ready to fall! DIABLO CLOTHESLINES EIAN AND HE LANDS ON TOP OF THE TABLE! DIABLO CLIMBS ON TOP OF THE TABLE! HE PICKS EIAN UP… DEVIL’S RAGDOLL COBRA CLUTCH BOMB BY DIABLO AND EIAN GOES THROUGH THE TABLE! Again, the table was unlit so the match MUST CONTINUE!
::fans chant BAD KICKS ASS:: BRANDON JACOBS: Why aren’t these guys lighting the table?! VIC WATERS: That bastard Diablo is paying Eian back. He won’t be happy unless Eian goes out of here on a stretcher!
BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo is getting up slowly… Eian is out cold! DIABLO IS GOING TO GET A CHAIR! WHY DOES DIABLO NEED A CHAIR! HE JUST PUT EIAN THROUGH A TABLE!
VIC WATERS: Diablo wants send a message that he isn’t someone to mess around with! BRANDON JACOBS: Eian is getting up slowly…. DIABLO NAILS HIM WITH A CHAIR SHOT TO THE HEAD AND EIAN STUMBLES BACKWARDS INTO THE STAIRS! The blood is pouring down Eian’s face as he lays over the steps!
VIC WATERS: Eian doesn’t even have enough energy to stand up on his own two feet. The ref really should call this match before he gets seriously hurt.
BRANDON JACOBS: Eian put his career on the line! The ref isn’t going to call this match until someone goes through a flaming table! Although with the way Eian is looking, he really should quit before he gets beaten too badly. DIABLO GRABS EIAN AND HE SHOWS HIS FACE TO THE CROWD!
DIABLO: THIS IS WHAT I’M GOING TO DO TO MANSON! BRANDON JACOBS: DIABLO RAMS EIAN INTO THE GUARD RAIL AND EIANS CRASHES TO THE FLOOR! Eian looks out of it! DIABLO IS ASKING THE REF FOR THE LIGHTER TO SET THE TABLE ON FIRE! Looks like this match will finally come to an end, poor Eian…
VIC WATERS: Eian isn’t dead until Diablo puts him through that table! Don’t give up faith in Eian yet, he isn’t going to let Diablo win if there is an ounce of life left in his body.
BRANDON JACOBS: Look at him Vic, he is done! Eian is bleeding like a stuck pig and has been treated like a ragdoll!
VIC WATERS: It isn’t over, until it’s over. BRANDON JACOBS: DIABLO GRABS THE LIGHTER FROM THE REF AND SHOWS IT TO THE CROWD FOR ALL TO SEE AND THE CAPACITY CROWD HERE IN ORLANDO FLORIDA IS BOOING HIM MERCIOUSLY! Diablo is waiving the lighter in front of Eian’s face…. EIAN GRABS IT AND PULLS THE TRIGGER TO START IT! Diablo bails to get out of the way… DIABLO TRIPS ON THE STEPS! Eian sets fire to the table! Eian goes after Diablo and Diablo crawls towards the last unlit table! Eian drops the lighter and grabs Diablo around the waist… EIAN NAILS DIABLO WITH A RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX THROUGH THE NON FLAMING TABLE!
VIC WATERS: Listen to the fans, they are all chanting Eian! Eian has the crowd behind him! BRANDON JACOBS: Eian throws Diablo into the ring! He is signaling to the crowd that he is going to ELIMINATE DIABLO BY CLOTHESLINE HIM OVER THE TOP AND THROUGH THE FLAMING TABLE! Eian picks Diablo up…. HE NAILS HIM WITH THE SHADOW DRIVER!
VIC WATERS: Without a doubt, Eian has the best Implant DDT in all of wrestling. BRANDON JACOBS: Eian is waiting for Diablo to get up… Diablo looks dazed… he is slowly getting up…. HE IS UP! EIAN CHARGES…. WAIT, DIABLO DUCKS DOWN… EIAN IS BACK BODYDROPPED OVER THE TOP ROPE AND THROUGH THE FLAMING TABLE! OH MY GOD! Eian’s career in WFW is over!
VIC WATERS: Poor Eian, he almost had it. RING ANNOUNCER: THE WINNER OF THIS MATCH…. DIABLO! ::crowd boos Diablo as he bails for the exit:: BRANDON JACOBS: Attendants rushed over to Eian to make sure the fire was put out quickly after he went through… Eian doesn’t look good. WAIT A SECOND, HE COMES MANSON! He is coming to see how Eian is! Eian is refusing medical help, he wants to get to the back on his own!
::fans chant EIAN, EIAN, EIAN over and over again loudly:: BRANDON JACOBS: Manson is helping Eian get up and he is helping him to the back, Manson and Eian are walking slowly to the back and the tears are coming down Eian’s face.
VIC WATERS: This match meant so much too him, he came so this close to winning, but in the end it didn’t really matter.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson turns Eian around and raises his hand and the crowd here is giving Eian a standing ovation and damnit, he deserves it! That kid has so much heart, I know he’ll be back someday!
VIC WATERS: Diablo is such a bastard. BRANDON JACOBS: ::Manson and Eian walk through the curtain together:: I don’t know how we can continue after that, but we must, after this commercial break, we will be back with a #1 Contender’s Match for the WFW North American Title between Copycat and Southern Thunder! Stay tuned!
