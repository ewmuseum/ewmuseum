WFW Merrython: Edmunds vs. Wells vs. Wylde

2 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 2 January 2004 (FAO SCHWATZ) (camera opens up to a semi dark room as Jared Wells is sitting in a chair relaxed wearing black trench coat, black attire, black face paint, with a white swirl. camera zooms onto the back of his head as he begins to speak)
'RAGE 'o FIRE': So here we are. At last, the time has come. Sean Edmunds, Alex Wylde, they have both chosen their path and now Rage will determine who survives or not. Strangers, people of all kinds have come up to me and asked "What's with the evil look"? Simple......I'm just making a point. All of the sudden JARED WELLS comes back from an injury, brings a little attitude, a little fear, all of the sudden PEOPLE START TO THINK! People start to wonder what the hell is going on here? It's a beautiful thing isn't it? The power I have to control what people THINK, or FEEL. I've seen the guys in the back locker room, I've seen their eyes. No games, no jokes. Alex Wylde, you are the WFW President......but let me tell you something....I DID MY TIME! I came to the WFW for one reason....but I've all changed that. DONT-CHA see the light in the sand ALEX? Do 'ya? You wonder where I stand? Well tonight, you just so happen to be in my path........PATH OF RAGE. And Seany Edmunds baby....don't think I forgot about you buddy. Hm, the journey stops tonight......all the *****in', the complaining, it ends tonight........Even though I'd rather have you one on one..........but hell....I don't mind ruining two careers at the same time. Besides I really don't like ya both. You guys done talking? You ready for the RAGE? I don't think you are......
(Wells turns towards the camera and starts an evil smile as you see some portions of his face from the light)
(BACK TO ROCKEFELLER CENTER) BRANDON JACOBS: There never seems to be a quiet moment around here. Why couldn’t I have taken a nice peaceful job somewhere that runs shows once a month and doesn’t make me work over holidays?
VIC WATERS: I’ve been so involved with this show, I haven’t been able to fully enjoy my Christmas loot. I’ll be glad for the break that comes after this one.
BRANDON JACOBS: Amen, well we are in the home stretch. Two matches to go and Merrython is in the books. IT IS FAO SCHWARTZ TIME BABY!
VIC WATERS: With the huge Toys R’ Us which opened up in the old Gimbels’s, FAO Schwartz isn’t thee toy store in New York City anymore, but it has a long tradition of being one of the biggest landmarks in the city. Kids from all over the world have come here and visted FAO Schwartz. This in many ways is their Disneyland.
BRANDON JACOBS: I can’t think of a better place to hold for Merrython than FAO Schwartz unless we went to Santa’s Workshop itself.
VIC WATERS: We’d have to deal with those damn drunken lewd elves. You haven’t been heckled until you’ve been mooned by a half dozen elves in the bag.
BRANDON JACOBS: The North Pole gets nippy this time of year, You have to have a few shots in you to keep warm if you work up there.
VIC WATERS: You are just an enabler Brandon, making excuses for people’s actions. ::Vic takes a sip from his ‘water’ bottle::
BRANDON JACOBS: I’ve learned from the best. Just a note before we start, since neither match will happen in wrestling ring, there will be no ring introductions. When the referee rings the bell, that will signal the start of the match and we will get right into the action. LET’S SEND THIS ONE TO FAO SCHWARTZ AND BEGIN THE SHOW!
(FAO SCHWARTZ) ::cameras fade into a nearly empty FAO Schwartz building late at nigh, the ref is reading Wylde and Edmunds instructions on the rules of the match, Wells is no where in sight::
::there is a commotion outside of the store, the camera fades to Wells jumping over the rail from the crowd with an Olympic style look wearing totally black and a black trench coat. Jared Wells’ face is painted black with a white swirl, Wells bursts through the door and darts for Edmunds::
BRANDON JACOBS: Wells rams Edmunds into FAO’s famous 3-Story Clock Tower as camera flashes go off from tourists and press! Edmunds goes down!
VIC WATERS: I was thinking about this the other day and Jared Wells has a Johnny Depp from Pirates of the Caribbean Jack Sparrow like quality to him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde comes to the aid of Edmunds and he dropkicks Wells backwards into a stand of teddy bears dressed as the Nutcracker, several of which that falls onto Wells! Wylde takes a teddy bear and puts it on the face of Wells…. WYLDE RAMS THE TEDDY BEAR INTO WELLS’ FACE WITH THE BOTTOM OF HIS BOOT!
VIC WATERS: This is going to be a nasty fight. BRANDON JACOBS: Wells grabs the foot of Wylde AND PULLS LEG FROM UNDER HIM AND DRAGS HIM OVER THE PLASTIC MUSICAL KEY PAD! Wells drags Wylde over the pad blurring the notes together!
VIC WATERS: Wells sounds like a regular Jack Benny. BRANDON JACOBS: Wells spreads the legs of Wylde and delivers a headbutt right to the groin of wild sounding off the keyboard at the same time! Edmunds is coming over… Wells and Edmunds begin to duke it out and they are headed to the stuffed animal section!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds looked slightly aggravated that his picture of Wells driving him into the clock tower was going to be all over the local papers tomorrow.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wells whips Edmunds down into the stuffed animals and THE DISPLAY CASE FALLS DOWN COVERING EDMUNDS WITH STUFFED ANIMALS AND A SHELF! Wells climbs up onto the shelf and poses with his bat for the crowd as the photographers take pictures.
VIC WATERS: Wells is really hamming it up today. BRANDON JACOBS: He has every right to be, this is his first match back after an injury which put him on the shelf for a couple of months. The real test though is how the arm will feel later on in the match once he has been worn down and worked over…. THE SHELF IS STARTING TO MOVE AND EDMUNDS PUSHES IT OFF OF HIMSELF!
VIC WATERS: It looks like Wells is starting to get into Edmund’s head because Edmunds looks plain furious right now.
BRANDON JACOBS: WELLS DIVES INTO THE STUFFED ANIMALS AND GRABS A GIANT PANDA BEAR…. WELLS CLOCKS EDMUNDS UPSIDE WITH IT! EDMUNDS GRABS A PINK FLAMINGO AND COUNTERS WITH A SHOT OF HIS OWN!
VIC WATERS: Stuffed animal death match…. ONLY IN THE WFW! BRANDON JACOBS: JARED WELLS DIVES ONTO EDMUNDS AND THEY ARE ROLLING AROUND OUT OF SIDE UNDER THE PILE OF STUFFED ANIMALS! I CAN’T EVEN TELL WHAT IS GOING ON!
VIC WATERS: Whatever it is, it can’t be good. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds reaches his arm out of the pile, it looks like he is trying to escape! Wells pokes his head up out of the pile of stuffed animals…. WELLS PICKS EDMUNDS UP OVER HIS HEAD…. POWERBOMB BACK DOWN INTO THE PILE! Wells goes back in to fight with Edmunds even more!
VIC WATERS: Can’t we get our camera men in there? BRANDON JACOBS: No, we don’t pay them enough. WYLDE GRABS AN FAO SCHWARTZ BLOCK OFF A DISPLAY AND HE IS HEADING FOR THE PILE! Edmunds is emerging from the stuffed animals…. HE IS PICKING WELLS UP FOR A PILEDRIVER…. HE DRIVES DOWN INTO THE STUFFED ANIMALS!
VIC WATERS: Wells and Edmunds are having the best match nobody will ever be able to see. BRANDON JACOBS: WYLDE CLOCKS EDMUNDS WITH THE GIANT BUILDING BLOCK OVER THE HEAD AND KNOCKS EDMUNDS BACKWARDS! Edmunds tumbles out of the pile! Wylde goes down into the sea of stuffed animals and picks Wells up by the hair….. HE NAILS WELLS WITH THE BUILDING BLOCK! Wells is busted open!
VIC WATERS: I wish these kids would learn to play nice together. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde throws down the block and he drags Wells out of the pile…. WYLDE SMASHES THE HEADS OF EDMUNDS AND WELLS TOGETHER AND THEY BOTH FALL DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Wylde is returning the match back to sanity. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde picks up Wells… HE LEADS HIM OVER TO THE CHRISTMAS TREE PAST THE TRAIN SET AND THROWS WELLS INTO THE BOXES UNDER THE TREE!
VIC WATERS: Wylde really wants a piece of Wells after what he did to him at Turkey Day Terror.
BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde pulls at a string of Christmas tree lights that are lit and WRAPS THEM AROUND THE NECK OF WELLS! WELLS COULD GET ELECTROCUTED IF HE IS NOT CAREFUL!
VIC WATERS: I think that is the point. BRANDON JACOBS: WELLS IS BEING CHOKED OUT WITH THE WIRE AND WELLS IS TRYING TO REMOVE THE CHRISTMAS TREE LIGHT STRING FROM AROUND HIS NECK! Wells pushes Wylde back into the tree…. CERAMIC BULBS BEGIN TO FALL FROM THE TREE ONTO WYLDE AND WYLDE BREAKS THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: That could scrape up his whole entire back if he isn’t careful. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is staggering over to where Wylde and Wells are fighting… EDMUNDS THROWS WYLDE FACE FIRST FROM BEHIND INTO THE TRAIN SETUP AROUND THE TREE AND WYLDE CRASHES FACE FIRST INTO A CERAMIC HOUSE! Edmunds yells “Sorry.” And he is headed over to fight with Wells who is training to regain his breath.
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is quite a polite young man, always smiling too. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds picks up Jared Wells…. TILT A WHIRL TOMBSTONE! Edmunds looks down at Jared Wells and then he looks up at the Christmas tree, he looks down at Wells again…. EDMUNDS IS CLIMBING THE 25 FOOT CHRISTMAS TREE!
VIC WATERS: That is tree is going to tip over. It is meant to hold that much weight. BRANDON JACOBS: Wells is still not moving. EDMUNDS IS ALMOST TO THE TOP OF THE CHRISTMAS TREE…. HE IS THERE…. Edmunds looks down at Wells below….. EDMUNDS LETS GO AND DROPS AN ELBOW FROM 25 FEET UP IN THE AIR ONTO WELLS!
::Rockefeller Center erupts into a BAD KICKS ASS chant as they watched on the closed circuit TV set up in the ring::
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is nuts. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is crawling over to Wells and he makes the cover! 1-----------------------------------------2--------------------------WYLDE BREAKS UP THE PINFALL! Wylde makes the cover on Wells! 1-------------------------------------2--------------------------WELLS KICKS OUT!
VIC WATERS: Thank god for Wells that this is a three way match or he would have been toast right there.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds sets up behind Wylde as he gets up…. EDMUNDS NAILS HIM WITH A REVERSE CRESCENT KICK AND WYLDE GOES DOWN LIKE A LIGHT! Edmunds grabs Wylde by the hair and he is leading him to the wooden block aisle!
VIC WATERS: FAO Schwartz is going to be a disaster area by the time we get out of this place.
BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS HURLS WYLDE INTO THE AISLE OF BLOCKS AND WYLDE’S BACK CRASHES ONTO THE SHELF AND SEVERAL BOXES OF WOODEN BLOCKS OPEN UP AND POUR ONTO WYLDE BELOW AS HE TRIES TO TAKE COVER!
VIC WATERS: Wylde really took a few lumps to the head there. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is grabbing a triangle wooden block and he grabs the hair of Wylde…. HE STARTS REPEATEDLY RAMMING HIM INTO THE HEAD WITH IT…. EDMUNDS HAS DRAWN BLOOD FROM WYLDE!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds was reluctant earlier on to take it to Wylde, but he is sure taking it to him now.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is clearing away block so his feet can touch the floor…. Edmunds picks up Wylde…. NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX ON THE BLOCKS! The referee is coming to count but Wylde’s shoulders are uneven with the floor.
VIC WATERS: Edmunds doesn’t appear pleased. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds grabs Wylde and he throws him FACE FIRST INTO THE DOOR OF THE ELEVATOR! When Wylde crashed in, he appears to have hit the button! A DAZED WELLS COMES FROM BEHIND AND ATTACKS EDMUNDS! Wylde is getting into the elevator and is heading to the second floor.
VIC WATERS: This will give Wylde a bit of a breather from all of the chaos, Plus he can lay some booby traps for when Wells and Edmunds get up here.
BRANDON JACOBS: WELLS GRABS EDMUNDS BY THE HAIR AND THROWS HIM ONTO THE ESCALATOR! Wells walks onto the escalator and HE IS POUNDING ON EDMUNDS AS THE ESCALATOR IS TAKING BOTH MEN UP TO THE SECOND FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: Shades of the classic battle held at Penn Station years ago… BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds crawls off the escalator and both men are currently in the board game section! WELLS GRABS A CHESS BOARD AND POUNDS EDMUNDS REPEATED IN THE BACK!
VIC WATERS: It is the human game of chess. Much more interesting that the actual game. BRANDON JACOBS: Wells picks Edmunds up…. HE SLAMS HIM DOWN ON THE FLOOR! Wells clears off a display table and climbs on top… HE NAILS HIM WITH HIS PATTENED LEG DROP!
VIC WATERS: Do not pass Go. Do not collect $200. BRANDON JACOBS: Wells makes the cover on Edmunds! 1-------------------------------2-------------------------- WYLDE MAKES THE SAVE AS HE KICKS WELLS OFF! Wylde picks up the board game Trouble and he rams the bubble right into Wells’ head setting it off!
VIC WATERS: This has been a dead even match up between the tree of them. Something big is going to have to happen to one of them to stay down for the three count.
BRANDON JACOBS: WELLS CHARGES WYLDE AND WYLDE BACK BODY DROPS WELLS INTO AND THROUGH THE CANDYLAND DISPLAY!
VIC WATERS: Goody, goody, gumdrops. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde makes the cover on Wells! 1-----------------------------2------------------- ANARKY HAS COME OUT OF NO WHERE AND HE NAILS WYLDE WITH A BASEBALL BAT TO BREAK UP THE PINFALL!
VIC WATERS: Wylde is grabbing his back! BRANDON JACOBS: Anarky is going to town with that baseball bat on Wylde’s back…. EDMUNDS STARTS THROWING BOARD GAMES AT ANARKY TO DRIVE HIM AWAY!
ANARKY: "Wylde... or is it Mr. President... don't you EVER... EVER think... that you can tell ME what to do.I don't care if you brought me here and I CERTAINLY don't care who elected you. I fight who I want, when I want, HOW I want. And if I want Manson, I'll GET Manson. And if you cross me again, so help me God...
You, too, will know my wrath. Same thing goes for you too Edmunds." ::Anarky walks off:: BRANDON JACOBS: A distracted Edmunds is yelling at Anarky as he leaves…. WELLS IS MAKING THE COVER ON WYLDE! The ref goes down for the count! 1-------------------------------------------2------------------------------- EDMUNDS DIVES TO BREAK UP THE THREE!
VIC WATERS: Wells is one hell of a sneak. BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS GRABS WELLS BY THE HAIR AND LEADS HIM INTO THE STAR WARS SECTION! Edmunds grabs a light saber toy and starts beating Wells repeatedly with it!
VIC WATERS: Thank god that isn’t a real light saber or Wells would be cut into little pieces by now.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds grabs a talking Yoda doll and RIPS IT OUT OF THE PACKAGE! Edmunds pulls the string on the back…. “DO OR DO NOT DO. THERE IS NO TRY!”…. EDMUNDS PUTS YODA ON THE FACE OF WELLS AND HE DROPS THE ELBOW!
VIC WATERS: Wells is taking a beating. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is climbing up to the elevated Darth Vader…. EDMUNDS PERCHES HIMSELF FROM THE RAILING….. MOONSAULT ON WELLS BELOW! Edmunds grabs his ribs!
VIC WATERS: That is the thing about moonsaults, that can hurt you almost as much as your opponent.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds crawls over and covers Wells….. WYLDE DIVES TO BREAK UP THE PINFALL BEFORE IT EVEN STARTS BUT WYLDE IS CLUTCHING HIS BACK! Edmunds picks up Wylde and he is leading HIM INTO THE BARBIE SECTION!
VIC WATERS: I seem to remember a promo from Wells about this. BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS RAMS WYLDE INTO A BARBIE TAPE PLAYING MACHINE! ::”Barbie Girl” By Aqua begins to play as they start brawling:: VIC WATERS: Good lord, make it stop! BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS PICKS UP A GIANT BARBIE HEAD AND NAILS WYLDE IN THE HEAD WITH IT! Wylde is staggering! EDMUNDS SWINGS AGAIN AND WYLDE GOES DOWN! Edmunds goes for cover! The ref goes down for the count! 1-------------------------------2---------------------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: That would be pretty embarrassing to be pinned by a Barbie Head. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds goes over to the Barbie Clothing section and starts ripping open packages!
VIC WATERS: I don’t understand what this is all about. BRANDON JACOBS: EDMUNDS HAS A HANDFUL OF BARBIE CLOTHES AND HE IS JUMPS ON WYLDE! HE IS GOING TO TRY TO SHOVE THEM DOWN HIS THROAT! Wylde and Edmunds are struggling for position.
VIC WATERS: How can two such good friends fight like this? BRANDON JACOBS: JARED WELLS WALKS INTO THE BARBIE SECTION WITH A BAT IN THEIR HANDS AND BOTH MEN STOP THEIR STRUGGLE TO BACK AWAY! Wells looks over at the tape player….. WELLS SWINGS THE BAT BREAKS THE TAPE PLAYER TO STOP THE HORRID MUSIC!
VIC WATERS: Our savior! BRANDON JACOBS: Wells drops the bat and he charges Wylde and Edmunds! ALL THREE MEN ARE DUKING IT OUT AND IT THEY ARE FIGHT THEIR WAY TO THE DOWN ESCALATOR! WELLS RAMS EDMUNDS HEAD INTO THE WALL! WELLS RAMS WYLDE’S HEAD INTO THE WALL!
VIC WATERS: That escalator is awfully dangerous. Those are metal steps and they could do a lot of damage.
BRANDON JACOBS: WELLS NAILS WYLDE WITH A JUMP PILEDRIVER ON THE FIRST STEP OF THE ESCALATOR AND WYLDE SLOWLY GOES DOWN THE ELEVATOR TO THE BOTTOM! Edmunds is trying to back away from Wells…. WELLS GRABS EDMUNDS BY THE HAIR AND SLIDES HIM DOWN THE METAL SIDE ON THE RIGHT SIDE OF THE ESCALATOR AND LANDS FACE FIRST INTO A PILE OF HOKEY POKEY ELMOS!
VIC WATERS: Wells is really taking it to them. BRANDON JACOBS: Wells runs down the escalator and makes the cover on Edmunds! 1-----------------------------------2------------------------- WELLS CRIES OUT IN PAIN AND JUMPS OFF EDMUNDS! There is a big purple paintball welt on his back! Wells jumps up and he is looking to find who is doing this.
VIC WATERS: Someone apparently has it out for Wells besides Edmunds. BRANDON JACOBS: WYLDE BULLDOGS WELLS FROM BEHIND! Wylde picks up Wells and he is leading him to the gingerbread house! WYLDE RAMS HIM HEAD FIRST INTO THE GINGERBREAD HOUSE! Wylde goes for the cover! 1------------------------2--- A PURPLE PAINTBALL SHOT THIS TIME HITS WYLDE TO BREAK UP THE PINFALL! Wylde is up and he is looking furious.
VIC WATERS: We have ourselves a paintball sniper. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is open, worse for wear, but he is heading for the Gingerbread house…. EDMUNDS AND WYLDE BEGIN BRAWLING! Wells is trying to get up…. SHERRI STANDS ON THE GINGERBREAD HOUSE WITH HER PAINTBALL GUN…. SHE SETS IT DOWN AND FLIES OFF THE GINGERBREAD HOUSE WITH A MIRROR AND BREAKS IT OVER THE HEAD OF WELLS! Security is trying to keep her out of the action!
VIC WATERS: Wells is down, and it looks like he is out as well! BRANDON JACOBS: WYLDE SENDS EDMUNDS WITH A RIGHT AND HE FALLS AGAINST THE SHELF! Edmunds is trying to get back up though because he knows if Wylde covers Wells he is going to get the win.
VIC WATERS: Two men in a race against the clock. BRANDON JACOBS: Wylde sees Wells covered with pieces of the mirror and brushes him off! WYLDE MAKES THE COVER! The ref goes down to make the count.... Edmunds is trying to get up to make the save….. THE 25 FOOT CHRISTMAS TREE JUST FELL DOWN BEHIND EDMUNDS! Edmunds turns around in shock! The ref makes the count! 1---------------------------------2------------------------------3! Edmunds yells in disgust!
VIC WATERS: Alex Wylde has won it! Edmunds might have been able to break the pinfall up too if that tree fall down dangerous close to him.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is raising Wylde’s hand. Wait a second, an official is coming down to offer Edmunds a present. A dumbfounded Edmunds opens up the present…. IT IS EMPTY! WAIT, COPYCAT COMES FROM BEHIND THE COUNTER…. HE PUTS A WREATH OVER HIS HEAD AND NAILS HIM WITH THE KIT KAT KICK!
COPYCAT: MERRY CHRISTMAS, SEAN! VIC WATERS: It must have been Copycat who pushed down that tree! BRANDON JACOBS: I bet you were the brightest in your family. VIC WATERS: I’m not one to brag. BRANDON JACOBS: Okay folks, we have one more match to go, the main event, The WFW World Heavyweight Champion Shawn Hart will put his title on the line against Scotty Michaels in A Winter Wonderland Match! STAY TUNED EVERYONE!
