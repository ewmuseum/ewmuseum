WFW Gates of Hell: Andre Eian vs. Luster

8 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 8 May 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Next up is Andre Eian vs. Luster, in his short stay in the WFW, Luster has caused a lot of controversy. Tonight, Luster will make his singles debut and show the world if he can live up to his hype.
VIC WATERS: I really dig Luster, he is a good getter. Other people sit back and let the world come to them but Luster goes out and makes things happen. His finisher is disgusting but what can you do.
BRANDON JACOBS: I’m really excited to see how this turns out, let’s go to the ring! ::“Funkytown” Disco Fever plays over the loud speakers and a disco ball descends from the ceiling as the lights flash::
RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from Atlanta, GA, The Master of Style, LUSTER! ::Luster comes out to the pop from his hometown crowd and enters the ring:: :: Bawitdaba" by Kid Rock wails over the loud speaker:: RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing second, from Trondheim, Norway, the former XGW World Champion, THE SHADOW ANDRE EIAN!
::Andre Eian comes out to a pop of the night and slowly enters the ring while keeping his eye on Luster::
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second, Chris Bradford has come down to the ring! Bradford has hopped on the apron and is talking to the ref.
VIC WATERS: If I remember right, earlier in the week, Bradford r was cutting a promo on Bradford for getting involved in his business!
BRANDON JACOBS: Whoa, Andre Eian has flash paper and he is sneaking on Luster who is distracted… HE NAILS LUSTER WITH A FIREBALL! LUSTER CAN’T SEE! Andre Eian hook’s Luster’s arms… SHADOW DRIVER IMPLANT DDT!
VIC WATERS: Bradford makes the ref turn around! BRANDON JACOBS: Eian covers! 1---------2---------KICKOUT! OH MY! How did he get out of that? Luster tries to get up and he is wailing crazily all over the place!
VIC WATERS: Luster is better off getting pinned, it means he can get to the back and get attention sooner.
BRANDON JACOBS: EIAN KICKS BLINDED LUSTER IN THE STOMACH! ANOTHER SHADOWN DRIVER DDT! He covers! 1---------2------------3!! Andre Eian advances to the number #1 Contender match at the PPV!
VIC WATERS: :Eian may have thrown the fireball, but it was Bradford who caused the distraction which made a reality. I have a feeling we may be seeing Luster vs. Bradford at the PPV. Luster is going to have a lust for blood.
BRANDON JACOBS: Attendants are helping Luster back to the dressing room because he still hasn’t regained his sight. When Luster & Bradford meet, that should be a wild brawl, we will be back after this commercial break with the ultimate jeopardy match, Manson & Diablo vs. Rat Fink & Scotty Michaels!
