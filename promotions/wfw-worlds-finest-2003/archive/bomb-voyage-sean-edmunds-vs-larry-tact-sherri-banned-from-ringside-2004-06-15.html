BOMB Voyage: Sean Edmunds vs. Larry Tact (Sherri Banned from Ringside!)

15 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 15 June 2004 JONATHAN MARX: Over the break, the doctor suggested to WFW officials that Edmunds shouldn’t wrestle tonight, but Edmunds pleaded with management to let him wrestle here tonight.
VIC WATERS: See, how can all these people say that Edmunds is abusing his power when he is going to go out there and wrestle when he has been given the option of opting out?
JONATHAN MARX: Maybe it is me, but I don’t trust Sean Edmunds. When was the last time a fan got that far backstage to the point where he could attack a wrestler? Someone had to have given him access.
VIC WATERS: Gee, do you think Edmunds may have an enemies? JONATHAN MARX: The better question may be if he has any friends, but I see your point. But something isn’t kosher in all of this…
VIC WATERS: Sean Edmunds is a man of integrity. He doesn’t need the office of Presidency protecting him or Sherri in his corner. You are way too paranoid. Were you hanging outside LOVE’s dressing room again?
JONATHAN MARX: It wasn’t like I was standing out there for long. It was maybe a couple of minutes or so.
VIC WATERS: More like a couple of hours… JONATHAN MARX: But that has nothing to do with this, I have a feeling Sean Edmunds is up to something, I don’t know what but I’m sure it is evil.
VIC WATERS: So, you miss the integrity of our last President, Alex Wylde, brought to the office?
JONATHAN MARX: Quiet you, folks, lets send this one to the ring and get this match underway!
(CUEUP: "Karma" by Diffuser) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, one of the best technicians in all of wrestling today, Manhattan, New York… LARRY TACT!
::Larry Tact comes down to a loud ovation from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: "Brain Stew" by Green Day) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, currently residing in his summer residence in Escandido, California, three time WWL World Champion and current President of the WFW….. 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds!
:: Edmunds comes limping down to the ring and enters the ring to the loud boos from the crowd::
*bell rings* JONATHAN MARX: Sean Edmunds is wearing a knee brace over that injured leg in order to reduce the pain.
VIC WATERS: Those things help a lot, but if you have a bad knee, you still have a bad knee.
JONATHAN MARX: Tact extends his arms with Edmunds to lock up…. EDMUNDS AND TACT LOCK UP AND TACT POWERS EDMUNDS INTO THE CORNER! Edmunds grabs his knee…
VIC WATERS: Edmunds really shouldn’t be wrestling tonight. JONATHAN MARX: The ref pushes his way in and asks Edmunds if he wants to continue but he nods his head yes.
VIC WATERS: How is Edmunds going to last a whole match with his knee like that? JONATHAN MARX: The ref moves out of the way….. TACT CHARGES IN AND STARTS BOMBARDING EDMUNDS WITH EUROPEAN UPPERCUTS!
VIC WATERS: Tact is beating the hell of Edmunds. JONATHAN MARX: TACT IRISH WHIPS EDMUNDS OUT OF THE CORNER AND EDMUNDS STUMBLES AND FALLS DOWN ON THE GROUND! It looks like his knee gave out.
VIC WATERS: Edmunds needs to roll out here and buy himself some time. JONATHAN MARX: Tact goes over to Edmunds… HE PICKS HIM OFF THE GROUND AND HE LOCKS ON AN ABDOMINAL STRETCH!
VIC WATERS: Tact knows that Edmunds knee is injured already so he is going to soften up the rest of him for the Tactful Surrender.
JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds is trying to reach for the ropes but he is too far away, VIC WATERS: The only possible thing that Edmunds could do is flip Tact over and Edmunds knee isn’t strong enough to do that right now.
JONATHAN MARX: WAIT, HERE COMES BLUE LIONESS DOWN TO THE RING! She is standing at ringside watching the match. What is she doing down here? She no right to be here.
VIC WATERS: Well it is obvious that this Blue Lioness and Sean Edmunds have sort of relationship, with how banged up Edmunds is, maybe Blue Lioness is concerned that Edmunds may hurt himself.
JONATHAN MARX: Let’s hope that is the only reason down here and they aren’t planning on screwing Tact.
VIC WATERS: When have you known Edmunds to do that? JONATHAN MARX: TACT PICKS UP EDMUNDS IN THE ABDOMINAL STRETCH AND HITS HIM WITH A SIDE SLAM!
VIC WATERS: People forget that while Tact is a technician, he is also very powerful because of his size.
JONATHAN MARX: Tact goes over to the ropes and yells at Blue Lionness to stay out of his match. Boy, what does Tact have to do to get a clean match against Edmunds?
VIC WATERS: Blue Cat & Blue Lionness haven’t even done anything yet. JONATHAN MARX: Tact goes to pick up Edmunds… EDMUNDS GRABS THE HAIR OF TACT AND ROLLS HIM UP! The ref goes down for the count! 1-------------------------------------------2-------------------------- TACT KICKS OUT!
VIC WATERS: Tact has to focus on the match, if Blue Lioness gets into his head, Edmunds might still be able to pull this match out.
JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds and Tact both get up…. TACT MISSES A CLOTHESLINE AND EDMUNDS GRABS THE ARM OF TACT AND TAKES HIM DOWN TO THE MAT WITH A CRUCIFIX! The ref goes down for the count! 1------------------------------------2------------------------------------ TACT KICKS OUT AGAIN!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds has almost had him twice now in the matter of thirty seconds. JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds gets up first this time…. HE NAILS TACT AS HE GETS UP WITH THE RESERVE CRESCENT KICK WITH GOOD LEG RIGHT IN THE FACE OF TACT AND EDMUNDS GOES DOWN GRABBING HIS KNEE!
VIC WATERS: That really wasn’t a wise choice by Edmunds. JONATHAN MARX: The ref is putting a double count on both men! 10----9----8---- 7----- 6-----5------4---- EDMUNDS IS GETTING UP FIRST BUT HE IS GRIMACING IN PAIN!
VIC WATERS: You’d be surprised in the number of moves which involves having a healthy knee to execute without hurting yourself.
JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds picks Tact off of the mat…. SWINGING NECKBREAKER ON TACT! Edmunds drags Tact to the center of the ring…. EDMUNDS STARTS STOMPING AWAY WITH HIS GOOD LEG RIGHT INTO THE MIDSECTION OF TACT!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is trying to soften up that midsection of Tact for his patented northern lights suplex.
JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds can hit his move out of anywhere which makes him quite dangerous. Tact better becareful…. EDMUNDS IS PICKING TACT UP…. This could be it right here!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds knows that he won’t be able to go all that long on his bad wheel so he is going for the kill early.
JONATHAN MARX: EDMUNDS PUTS HIS HANDS AROUND TACT’S WAIST FOR A NORTHERN SUPLEX BUT TACT HEAD BUTTS EDMUNDS TO BREAK IT UP!
VIC WATERS: What a shot! JONATHAN MARX: TACT IS CHOPPING AWAY ON EDMUNDS AND HE IS DRIVING EDMUNDS BACK TOWARDS THE ROPES!
VIC WATERS: Tact throws those chops as good as anyone in wrestling. JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds is using those ropes to support himself… TACT CHARGES AND EDMUNDS DUCKS DOWN…. TACT STOPS DEAD IN HIS TRACKS AND HE GRABS THE HEAD AND TIGHTS OF EDMUNDS AND SNAP SUPLEXES HIM DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Tact is always thinking in there, that is what makes him dangerous. JONATHAN MARX: EDMUNDS IS CLUTCHING HIS KNEE AND THE REFEREE STEPS IN TO KEEP TACT AWAY AND TO SEE IF THEY NEED TO STOP THE MATCH! Tact backs up…. WAIT A SECOND, BLUE LIONNESS HAS HER PURSE…
VIC WATERS: This is all vaguely familiar. JONATHAN MARX: BLUE LIONNESS NAILS TACT IN THE BACK OF THE HEAD WITH HER LOADED PURSE AND TACT GOES DOWN TO THE MAT! Blue Lionness jumps down to the ground. DAMNIT! What will it take for Tact to get a clean match?
VIC WATERS: Pigs flying maybe… JONATHAN MARX: EDMUNDS KIPS UP AND HE IS DOING A JIG IN THE RING! THAT BASTARD WAS FAKING ALL ALONG!
VIC WATERS: IT IS A MIRACLE! JONATHAN MARX: Tact looks up and he mutters something at Edmunds, thankfully that the sound on the ring was lowered or we’d have to do some bleeping. Edmunds disgusts me.
VIC WATERS: Don’t speak that way about our fine President. JONATHAN MARX: . I’ll speak any way I want…. Edmunds asks Blue Lioness where Blue Cat is but she shrugs her shoulders. EDMUNDS PICKS TACT UP AND HE SLAMS HIM DOWN IN THE CORNER NEAR THE TURNBUCKLE!
VIC WATERS: If I know Edmunds, and I do, he is going up to the top for his patented elbow drop.
JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds is climbing up to the top turnbuckle….. EDMUNDS FLIES OFF AND HE HITS TACT IN THE RIBS WITH A BIG ELBOW DROP!
VIC WATERS: If there is anything worse than an elbow going into your ribs, it is an elbow from that elevation going right into your ribs.
JONATHAN MARX: Tact is coughing and trying to regain his breath. Edmunds picks Tact up…… EDMUNDS LOCKS ON A BEAR HUG ON TACT AND DRAGS HIM TO THE CENTER OF THE RING!
VIC WATERS: He usually tries going for the inverted atomic drop here after the elbow drop but he must think he needs to wear Tact down more because of the level of conditioning Tact has.
JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds is trying his darndest to squeeze the life out of Tact and Tact is barely about to fight it!
VIC WATERS: Tact is in bad shape. JONATHAN MARX: Tact is fighting it but it looks like he is running out of steam… TACT’S ARMS HAVE GONE LIMP! The referee is going to over to check to see if he passed out.
VIC WATERS: After how close Tact came in their previous match, Edmunds really went above and beyond the call of duty to devise a plan to beat Tact. Edmunds is one smart cookie.
JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds certainly is something. The referee grabs the arm of Tact…. He lifts it once….. IT FALLS!
VIC WATERS: YES! Come on! JONATHAN MARX: The referee lifts it a second time…. IT FALLS AGAIN! ALL EDMUNDS NEEDS IS FOR THE ARM TO DROP ONE MORE TIM!
VIC WATERS: He can do it! JONATHAN MARX: Wait a second, look at the video wall! Copycat: Sean! HEY SEAN! Yeah, over here! Y'know, Sean, I wasn't too surprised when you decided to back out of a one-on-one match with me, and I can't say I was too shocked that you'd abuse your presidential power. But even if these things don't surprise me, they still upset me. See, Sean, as you might have noticed, I'm getting a little peeved that I'm not being given
the respect I'm due around here, and YOU are the worst offender of all. So I'm here tonight to ask you to do the right thing, be a man, and face me yourself at the pay-per-view.
::Edmunds lets go of Tact and curses the video wall:: (Copycat pauses for a second, but before Edmunds can respond, he continues) Copycat: Because Sean, if you DON'T give me what I want, I'm going to have to get it in my own way. If you still INSIST that I must face Blue Cat at the pay-per-view, I'll just have to do something to convince you otherwise.
(The camera pans over to the side, and we see Blue Cat gagged and tied to something that looks like a large scratching post. He is dangerously close to an edge of the ship)
Copycat: I have no doubt, Sean, that you won't respect me until we go one-on-one in the ring, even if I have to take that respect by force. But until I can get you in the ring, I can't get that respect. I stand to gain nothing from defeating Blue Cat, so the solution is, of course, to see to it that Blue Cat doesn't make it to the match.
(Copycat walks over to Blue Cat and puts his hand on the side of the scratching post) Copycat: You see, Sean, desperate men do desperate things. And you've left me no choice but to go through with my experiment in violence. I said that tonight, I was going to do something NOBODY ever thought they'd see the Cat do. I HOPE, Sean, that this will TEACH you what happens when you toy with
the Cat. And speaking of cats...I hope Blue Cat's got nine lives, because although everyone knows cats always land on their feet, everyone also knows that CATS CAN'T SWIM!
(With that, Copycat pushes Blue Cat, scratching post and all, over the edge of the ship. He looks over the edge for a moment, then turns back to the camera)
Copycat: Do the right thing, Sean. I know I have tonight. (RINGSIDE) JONATHAN MARX: Oh my god, he threw him in the water! Somebody get him some help! VIC WATERS: This is going to screw up the PPV lineup, isn't it? JONATHAN MARX: WAIT A SECOND, TACT HAS REGAINED CONCIOUSNESS… EDMUNDS TURNS AROUND AND HE STARTS ELBOWING EDMUNDS REPEATEDLY IN THE MIDSECTION!
VIC WATERS: No damnit! JONATHAN MARX: TACT GRABS THE ARM OF EDMUNDS AND HE WHIPS HIM INTO THE ROPES..... BLUE LIONNESS GRABS THE LEG OF SEAN EDMUNDS AND PULLS HIM OUT OF THE RING! This crowd is booing Blue Lionness.
VIC WATERS: What has Sherri… Blue Lionness done? She pulled her own man out, she didn’t hit Tact, well this time.
JONATHAN MARX: Sean Edmunds is being consoled by Blue Lionness… this is just a cheap stalling tactic to STOP TACT’S MOMENTUM! WAIT A SECOND, TACT HAS SLIPPED OUT OF THE RING AND HE IS SNEAKING UP BEHIND BLUE LIONESS!
VIC WATERS: There is no call for this, he is just an innocent bystander. JONATHAN MARX: Blue Lionness charges… TACT HITS HER WITH THE POINT BLANK RIGHT IN THE CHIN! Tact and Edmunds are now all alone!
VIC WATERS: Tact has been waiting for this for a long time, now it is time to nut up or shut up.
JONATHAN MARX: TACT GRABS EDMUNDS AND HE ROLLS HIM BACK INTO THE RING! VIC WATERS: Edmunds needs to fight for his life. JONATHAN MARX: Tact tries to roll in… EDMUNDS IS KICKING AT TACT TRYING TO PREVENT HIM FROM ENTERING THE RING!
VIC WATERS: That is a hell of a plan, if he can’t get into the ring, he can’t pin him. JONATHAN MARX: TACT JUMPS ONTO THE APRON AND EDMUNDS CHARGES HIM.... TACT MOVES OUT OF THE WAY AND HE PULLS THE HEAD OF EDMUNDS RIGHT INTO THE TOP ROPE ON HIS NECK!
VIC WATERS: Ouch. JONATHAN MARX: Tact finally makes his way in and Edmunds is clutching his throat…. TACT CLIPS EDMUNDS KNEE FROM BEHIND AND EDMUNDS GOES DOWN SCREAMING HIS HEAD OFF!
VIC WATERS: Tact should be DQed, if this was football, he would have gotten at least fifteen yards for that shot.
JONATHAN MARX: Karma is a *****. TACT PICKS EDMUNDS OFF OF THE MAT AND HE NAILS HIM WITH A PUMPHANDLE POWER SLAM RIGHT IN THE MIDDLE OF THE RING! Tact makes the cover!
VIC WATERS: This could be it! JONATHAN MARX: 1----------------------------------------------------2------------------------------- EDMUNDS SOMEHOW KICKS OUT! Uh oh, it looks like Tact hurt himself when he picked Edmunds up for that power slam.
VIC WATERS: That is the same area that Edmunds worked over extensively earlier in the match…
JONATHAN MARX: Tact looks like he is in a lot of pain and he is cursing to himself as he seems to realize he has to finish him off and soon if he has any chance to win this match.
VIC WATERS: How can you tell? JONATHAN MARX: I can read lips, he said “I have to finish him off.” Along with another couple of words I can’t repeat without fines from the FCC.
VIC WATERS: Come on Edmunds! JONATHAN MARX: Tact turns over Edmunds…. TACT REPEATEDLY DROPS THE KNEE INTO THE THIGH OF EDMUNDS AS EDMUNDS TRIES TO SQUIRM AWAY! Tact pulls Edmunds back! TACT IS SIGNALING FOR THE TACTFUL SURRENDER!
VIC WATERS: LOOK, BLUE LIONESS IS GETTING UP! JONATHAN MARX: TACT GRABS THE LEG AND HEAD OF EDMUNDS AND LOCKS ON THE TACTFUL SURRENDER! Blue Lioness has jumped on the apron to distract the referee!
VIC WATERS: Thank god! JONATHAN MARX: TACT BREAKS THE HOLD AND HE HITS BLUE LIONESS WITH AN UPPERCUT RIGHT OFF THE RING APRON!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is trying to get up! JONATHAN MARX: EDMUNDS CATCH TACT OFF GUARD AND HE HITS HIM WITH AN INVERTED ATOMIC DROP! VIC WATERS: We all know what is coming after this! JONATHAN MARX: EDMUNDS GOES FOR THE NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX! NO DAMNIT! The referee goes down for the count! 1-------------------------------------------2--------------------------------- TACT KICKS OUT!
VIC WATERS: WHAT?! JONATHAN MARX: Edmunds looks like he wasn’t able to support the bridge and he is grabbing his leg.
VIC WATERS: Oh man. JONATHAN MARX: TACT TURNS EDMUNDS OVER ONE MORE TIME AND HE GOES FOR THE TACTFUL SURRENDER! BLUE LIONESS IS DOWN AND THIS IS HIS CHANCE! The referee goes down to check to see if Edmunds gives up….. EDMUNDS IS TRYING TO FIGHT THROUGH THE PAIN BUT IT DOESN’T LOOK LIKE HE CAN HOLD ON MUCH LONGER!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds has gone an hour with Scotty Michaels, he has fought the best of the best. Edmunds won’t give up, not now, not ever.
JONATHAN MARX: EDMUNDS IS SCREAMING IN PAIN….. EDMUNDS COLLAPSES! EDMUNDS HAS PASSED OUT THE REF IS CALLING FOR THE BELL!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. LARRY TACT! JONATHAN MARX: What a win for Larry Tact… VIC WATERS: Hey, what happened to Blue Cat? JONATHAN MARX: Hopefully someone saved him because Edmunds sure didn’t go out of his way to.
VIC WATERS: Cats have nine lives. What are chances he was on his last one? JONATHAN MARX: True, folks, we got to take a commercial break, and when we comeback after finishing Blue Cat out of the water and picking up the bodies in the ring, IT IS TIME FOR THE SIX MAN TAG! STAY TUNED!
