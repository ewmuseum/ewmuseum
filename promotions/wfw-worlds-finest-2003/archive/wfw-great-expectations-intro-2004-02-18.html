WFW Great Expectations: Intro

18 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 18 February 2004 (FADEIN: Brandon Jacobs and Vic Waters are sitting in a television both overlooking a capacity crowd at Edison Field, Vic Waters is sporting a new A-Rod Yankee jersey)
BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome everyone to WFW Great Expectations, we are live on PPV from Anahiem, California! Thank you for joining us on what looks to be one of the greatest nights of action ever in WFW history. Rarely does a PPV’s name symbolize a PPV so perfectly as Great Expectations represents this show.
VIC WATERS: How often do events and people live up to the expectation of others though? This show could royally suck. To be on the safe side, I always assume people are going to let me down and proceed from there.
BRANDON JACOBS: That is an awfully negative attitude for you to take. VIC WATERS: What if Anarky pulls off the mask on Mephisto and it is revealed that it is Damien Priest? What if Michaels dominates Edmunds so badly he has the match won in the first twenty minutes? It could happen you know!
BRANDON JACOBS: I guess it could, but when has WFW ever disappointed you like that? VIC WATERS: What about that time Psycho and Hart took so much out of Doc that he wasn’t at full strength for his match with Manson?
BRANDON JACOBS: That wasn’t our fault. It is the nature of this business that if you compete in that squared circle, you are going to get banged up. Everything can’t end in a classic.
VIC WATERS: I remember reading Beowulf in high school and all throughout the story, they were building to the epic match up between Grendel and Beowulf like it was going to be a classic battle between the irresistible force and the unmovable option. How did it end? BEOWULF RIPPED THE ARM OFF THE MONSTER GRENDEL AND MADE HIM HIS *****!
BRANDON JACOBS: That was kind of disappointing. VIC WATERS: Kind of? I remember it killing British Literature for me. BRANDON JACOBS: Well the story tonight won’t be wove orally by Germanic tribes over hundreds of years The story tonight will be told by some of the finest wrestlers competing in the squared circle today.
VIC WATERS: Put your money where your mouth is. Put up your Corvette. BRANDON JACOBS: Little Susie?! VIC WATERS: Yes little Susie, is this show isn’t great, if it isn’t the **** as the kids say, I get the keys to your Corvette.
BRANDON JACOBS: I’ll tell you what, I’ve got so much confidence this show will actually live up to its hype. I’ll put my car on the line if this show doesn’t win you over.
VIC WATERS: Deal and no weaseling out… BRANDON JACOBS: Now, what are you going to put on the line if you are wrong? VIC WATERS: I don’t need to put material possessions on the line, but I’ll will put up something that is worth even more than your little red sports car. If you win, I’ll tell everyone that I was wrong.
BRANDON JACOBS: I’m putting up a Corvette, you are just weaseling out of having to put up anything of substance.
VIC WATERS: What do you want? BRANDON JACOBS: Put up the A-Rod jersey. If you lose the bet, we throw the A-Rod jersey into a metal trashcan and light it on fire.
VIC WATERS: Do you know how many strings I had to pull to get this jersey so quickly? How could you even think about desecrating something as holy as this?
BRANDON JACOBS: Take it or leave. VIC WATERS: I’ll tale it. Stupid Red Sox fan. BRANDON JACOBS: Arrogant Yankee fan. Folks, when we comeback, we are going to start this show off with a bang as Manson will try to get revenge against Mason for what happened to him in his last PPV match against Shawn Hart, STAY TUNED!
