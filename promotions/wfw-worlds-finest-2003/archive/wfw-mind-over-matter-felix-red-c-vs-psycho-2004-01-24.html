WFW Mind Over Matter: Felix Red (c) vs. Psycho

24 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 24 January 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back and it is time for the BAD World Heavyweight Title defense. I’m really excited to see these two go at it, Psycho had a hell of a match with Rat Fink and came really close to winning the BAD title but interference by Rat Fink’s goons kept him from winning. This time he finally has a fair chance to win it all.
JONATHAN MARX: People always talk about guys like DC Stratton when they talk about extreme wrestlers, but personally Psycho puts the fear of God in me more than anyone else. I remember watching that cage match that Psycho had with Doc and I said to myself, there is no way that Doc is ever going to be the same after that and he wasn’t. I have to give mad props to Richard Gideon and Felix Red, they are better men than I am.
VIC WATERS: That says a lot considering you’ve been in the ring with Maelstrom in some really psychical bouts.
JONATHAN MARX: There is one major difference though, Psycho is a tad nuts as those in the psychological profession would say. There is nothing Psycho wouldn’t do to win a match. There are a lot of people who say that, but most of the time, they do have their own limit. Psycho would never mentally give up.
BRANDON JACOBS: While I don’t approve of how far Psycho has gone recently against Gideon, it is hard not to give him his due. Since WFW’s inception, he was one of the men upon which this company was built. Felix Red is going to have to overcome a lot to beat Psycho. This is Psycho’s ideal match. Felix Red is going to have to rely on his quickness and his dangerous feet if he wants to beat Psycho here tonight and that may still not be enough.
JONATHAN MARX: While I prefer to wrestler, I remember watching BAD at home on my TV when I was younger and it was the most fun you could have in an hour each and every week. It is an honor to be here to witness a BAD World Heavyweight Title defense live especially with a wrestler who truly does the title justice like Felix Red.
BRANDON JACOBS: You will get your wish Jonathan, let’s send this match down to ringside and get this match underway!
(CUEUP: "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the former WFW World Heavyweight Champion, PSYCHO! ::Psycho comes down to a mixed reaction from the crowd:: BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND! GIDEON IS COMING UP FROM BEHIND PSYCHO…. GIDEON SPINS PSYCHO AROUND AND HITS HIM WITH HIS CHAIN WRAPPED FIST RIGHT IN THE HEAD!
JONATHAN MARX: Gideon is trying to get a measure of revenge for what happened to him at Merrython.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho staggers back…. GIDEON NAILS HIM WITH SEVERAL QUICK PUNCHES BEFORE PSYCHO FINALLY GOES DOWN! Psycho is busted open and he is bleeding profusely. Gideon looks down at what he did and unwraps the chain from around his fist, thank god he is finally coming to his senses…. GIDEON WRAPS CHAIN AROUND PSYCHO’S NECK AND HE WALKS DOWN TO THE RING AND THROWS PSYCHO OVER THE TOP ROPE WHILE STANDING OF THE FLOOR! Psycho is grasping at the chains trying to break free!
VIC WATERS: Gideon has finally snapped. (CUEUP: “"If Born From This Soil: Treatments For the Injected Foetu" by Minor Procedure and Shai Hulud)
::Felix Red comes down to the ring to the roar of the crowd:: BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red is pleading Gideon to let Psycho go before he goes too far, but Gideon doesn’t want to seem to let him go. Felix Red whispers something in Gideon’s ear…. GIDEON FINALLY LETS HIM GO!
JONATHAN MARX: I have a feeling I don’t want to know what Felix Red said. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red enters the ring. *bell rings* VIC WATERS: This match is finally official now. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red picks up the barely conscious and bleeding Psycho…. FELIX RED CLOBBERS HIM WITH THE SPINNING BACK KICK! Felix Red goes down for the cover! 1----------------------------------------------2----------------------------- PSYCHO MANAGES TO KICK OUT BEFORE THE THREE!
JONATHAN MARX: Good lord, how did Psycho do that? BRANDON JACOBS: Many a man has made the mistake of underestimating Psycho. FELIX RED IS CALLING FOR GIDEON’S CHAIN AND HE CATCHES IT IN MIDAIR! Felix Red is wrapping his boot with the chain!
JONATHAN MARX: Pure genius. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red picks Psycho up… SPINNING HEEL KICK WITH THE LOADED BOOT! Psycho goes down and he is bleeding even worse than before.
JONATHAN MARX: The ref really should call this match, Psycho looks in pretty bad shape. BRANDON JACOBS: Felix Red lifts Psycho off the mat…. HE PUTS PSYCHO IN A SLEEPER! Psycho is trying to fight it…. PSYCHO HAS PASSED OUT!
JONATHAN MARX: Psycho has lost way too much blood. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref lifts the hand once… IT FALLS! He lifts the hand a second time…. IT FALLS! He lifts it one final time…. THE REF IS CALLING FOR THE BELL! FELIX RED HAS WON THE MATCH!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match and STILL BAD WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION…. FELIX RED!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is screwed again in his second BAD World Heavyweight Title shot. He would have never given up if he didn’t pass out. He even managed to kickout after Gideon’s attack with the chain.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon and Felix Red leave the ring and the paramedics are coming down to help Psycho. Psycho is trying to crawl away from a paramedics, he really shouldn’t fight this. He needs medical attention.
JONATHAN MARX: LOOK! PSYCHO IS WRITING SOMETHING ON THE MAT! BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is writing in his own damn blood! Psycho has finally passed out. ::the camera man pans down at the ring at the message written in blood.... “GIDEON, YOU’LL PAY”::
JONATHAN MARX: Psycho is passed out being taken to the back and he still puts the damn fear of God in me. I do not envy Gideon.
BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we have to take a commercial break, when we comeback, we’ll have a good old fashioned wrestling match between the number one contender Sean Edmunds and Larry Tact! STAY TUNED!
