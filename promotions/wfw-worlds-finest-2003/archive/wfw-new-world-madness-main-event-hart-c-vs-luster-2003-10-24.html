WFW: New World Madness - Main Event - Hart (c) vs. Luster

24 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 24 October 2003 (BACKSTAGE) EL ARCO IRIS: Señor Copycat, El Arco Iris sees that you are very sad, and with good reason. I think the one thing Copycat deperately needs right now is a big, heartfelt El Arco Iris hug! COPYCAT: Look, I don't need you to cheer me up, get lost. EL ARCO IRIS: Oh, but Señor Copycat, nobody should ever refuse a good hug! Hugs are the greatest gift a person can give to another person! COPYCAT: LOOK, I SAID...well, actually, maybe there is something you can do to cheer me up. Walk with me. EL ARCO IRIS: Oh, okay. I fully understand if you don't want to get hugged in front of other people. Copycat then leads El Arco Iris away with a sly look on his face. (RINGSIDE) BRANDON JACOBS: We are moments away from the main event of New World Madness. Any predictions Vic?
VIC WATERS: As much as I hate Shawn Hart, I think Shawn Hart retain the title against Luster tonight and will go on to face Manson at Ghoulish Games. What do you think?
BRANDON JACOBS: For some reason, I think Luster is going to shock the world tonight and pin Shawn Hart in the center of the ring. Luster has been on a real hot streak lately and anything can happen in a first title defense, which this is for Shawn Hart. Imagine, if Luster wins the title tonight, Manson will have to beat the man who helped cost him the WFW North American Title in order to achieve his dream of being World Champ. Nobody thought Luster would be the man to cost him the WFW North American Title and tonight we have a similar scenario, a lot of people think Shawn Hart is going to win tonight, but Luster may just be the man who could shock the world twice.
VIC WATERS: Luster has been smoldering hot lately as we’ve witnessed in the World Series, once you get your chance to go for the championship, anything can happen. Nobody expected the Marlins of all teams to go back to Yankees Stadium with a 3-2 lead with the Yankees having to take two to win it. It is possible, I just don’t see it. Hart worked so hard to get to the top, I don’t think he’ll let Luster take the title from him as long as there is life in his body. Sure, he was a bit cocky, but he has every right to be, he is the golden child of the WFW.
BRANDON JACOBS: I can see everything you are saying, but I just have this feeling Luster is going to pull this out somehow.
VIC WATERS: You are dreaming son, it will never happen. BRANDON JACOBS: I guess we’ll find out. Let’s send this one to ringside and get this match started!
(CUEUP: “Funkytown” by Disco Fever) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the challenger, the Funkiest Man in all of WFW and the innovator of Ask Luster, the hottest wrestler in all of WFW…. LUSTER!
::Luster dances down to the ring to the boos of the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: "Look At Me" by Geri Haliwell) RING ANNOUNCER: And now, the champion, from Orlando, Florida, the leader of C.O.C.K.S. and the WFW World Heavyweight of the World, “THE PHENON” SHAWN HART!
::Shawn Hart walks down to the ring to a mixed reaction from the crowd and enters the ring::
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Luster and Hart walk toward the center of the ring and Hart is smirking at the challenger. Luster looks more serious than I’ve ever seen him before. Luster extends his hands for a lock…. HART LOCKS UP WITH LUSTER AND CHICKENWINGS THE ARM BEHIND LUSTER’S BACK!
VIC WATERS: There has been a lot of brawling on this show, I’m glad to see two guys wrestling for once.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster tries to reach back to grab the hair but Hart moves out of the way. HART GRABS THE SECOND ARM TO MAKE IT A DOUBLE CHICKENWING!
VIC WATERS: Hart is trying to frustrate Luster… BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER PICKS HART UP ONTO HIS BACK AND DUMPS HIM OVER ONTO THE GROUND! VIC WATERS: Luster has a seven inch height advantage on Hart and this is one situation where size has really paid off.
BRANDON JACOBS: It doesn’t matter how big you are, it is what you do with it that counts. VIC WATERS: Funny how you are always saying things like that. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster stalks Hart who is sitting on the mat and is backpedaling away from his grasp… LUSTER PICKS HART OFF THE MAT AND SLAMS HIM DOWN! Hart landed hard on his back! Hart tries to get up…. LUSTER DROPS AN ELBOW ON HART TO KEEP HIM DOWN!
VIC WATERS: Good move there by Luster. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster takes his shin and he places it over the throat of Hart! HART IS TRYING TO PRY LUSTER’S SHIN OFF OF HIM AND THE REF IS PUTTING THE COUNT ON! 1---2----3----4--- BREAK! HE DOES IT AGAIN 1---2----3---4--- BREAK!
VIC WATERS: Luster is working the rules to his advantage. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster picks Hart up… he irish whips him off the ropes…. SLEEPER HOLD! HART IS CAUGHT IN IT!
VIC WATERS: Hart doesn’t do a lot of flashy moves like an aerialist, but he wrestles with a lot of energy and if you take his endurance away, the match is shifted in your advantage.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is struggling to break free… HE IS REACHING OUT TO THE ROPES BUT LUSTER HAS HIM TOWARDS THE CENTER! Luster is squeezing it in tight…
VIC WATERS: The ref is checking to make sure this doesn’t become a choke. After what Luster was doing earlier, I wouldn’t be surprised.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster knows how much the WFW World Heavyweight Title means and he is going to do anything it takes to win. This is shot at greatness. Shawn Hart is reaching out towards the ropes… AND HE FINALLY MAKING SOME PROGRESS HEADING TOWARDS THE ROPES… Luster is trying to hold him but Hart has some momentum….. HART REACHES… HE GRABS THE ROPE!
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart realized if he didn’t break out quickly, it would impair him badly for the rest of the match if not worse.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is making Luster break the hold… Shawn Hart is using the ropes to keep himself up. Shawn Hart appears to be surprised by the offensive that Luster has put up in the match already. LUSTER IRISH WHIPS THE WINDED HART OFF THE ROPES….. HE NAILS HIM WITH A SPEAR RIGHT INTO THE STOMACH!
VIC WATERS: Luster man a full impact right into the chest of Hart! BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is crumpled over trying to regain his breath…. Luster walks over to Hart….. LUSTER JUMPS ON HART’S STOMACH WITH A DOUBLE FOOT STOMP!
VIC WATERS: That is 250 pounds right into the stomach of Hart! BRANDON JACOBS: Luster hooks the leg and makes a cover! The ref goes down for the count! 1----------------------------------------------2-----------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: That was a close call there. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster picks Shawn Hart up…. HE LOCKS IN A BEAR HUG! WAIT, HART REACHES UP BEFORE HE CAN GET IT FULLY APPLIED AND EYE RAKES LUSTER!
VIC WATERS: Very quick thinking by Hart. BRANDON JACOBS: Hart picks Luster up…. INVERTED ATOMIC DROP ON THE BLINDED LUSTER! Hart has nailed Luster at both ends and that tingling sensation from the jolt to his spinal chord is going throughout his body!
VIC WATERS: Hart is exploding on Luster with a quick offensive. BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart winds up…. HE NAILS LUSTER WITH THE DISCUS PUNCH AND LUSTER FALLS DOWN TO A KNEE! Luster gets back up…. SHAWN HART CLOTHESLINE LUSTER AND THIS TIME HE FALLS DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: You don’t see many people who know how to throw a discus punch as well as Shawn Hart can. He knows how to use his body to get the momentum he needs behind the punch to bring down a man the size of Luster.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart drags Luster towards the center of the ring…. HE IS GOING FOR THE TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER! Hart picks up the dazed Luster over his shoulder and sets him in position…. HE NAILS THE TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER!
VIC WATERS: Luster looked like he landed badly on his neck. BRANDON JACOBS: Hart folds Luster’s arms in mocking form to signal that he is dead and makes the cover! 1--------------------------------------2---------------------------- LUSTER LIVES AND KICKS OUT OF THE THREE!
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart is going to need to do a lot more to Luster if he wants to get the victory.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart gets up off the mat…. HE DROPS THE KNEE ON THE HEAD OF LUSTER! VIC WATERS: The elbow and knee are the two hardest parts of the body and Shawn Hart knows that from what he showed you right there.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart gets up… AND HE RAKES THE EYES OF LUSTER WITH HIS BOOT TO ADD INSULT TO INJURY!
VIC WATERS: I don’t care how tough you are, a rake to the eyes will impair anyone big or small.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart puts his knees on the chest of Luster and starts talking trash…. SHAWN HART SLAPS LUSTER IN THE FACE AND TELLS HIM THERE IS A REASON WHY HE IS CHAMP!
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart is trying to get into his head, but Luster is tough, I don’t know if it will work.
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster is trying to squirm to get off Hart…. BUT HART STARTS CHOKING LUSTER! The ref counts! 1---2---3--- LUSTER LIFTS HIS KNEE AND HE RAMS HART RIGHT IN THE BACK! Hart falls off of Luster and grabs his back and he is cursing out Luster! Luster is using the ropes to help himself get up.
VIC WATERS: Hart may have over did, he needs to regain control of this match before Luster gets back on the offensive.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart gets up.... Hart grabs the arm of Luster and whips him off the ropes…. HART GOES FOR THE SUPERKICK… LUSTER DUCKS…. HART IS GRABBING HIS BACK! Luster grabs Hart…. HE TAKES HIM DOWN WITH AN STO!
VIC WATERS: Luster is one big move away from pinning Hart here. Jacobs I’ll owe you a steak if you were right on this one.
BRANDON JACOBS: Filet Mignon…..Luster is setting up…. LUSTER GETS READY FOR THE THREE POINT STANCE! A dazed Hart is getting up…. HE TURNS AROUND…. LUSTER NAILS HIM! Luster goes down for the pin! 1---------------------------------------2---------------------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: I have no idea how Hart kicked out of that one. BRANDON JACOBS: Luster is calling for the Funk & Wagnall Powerbomb! Luster goes to pick up Hart…
VIC WATERS: If he nails this, it will be all over! Nobody kicks out of out of the Funk & Wagnall Powerbomb!
BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second, WAIT A SECOND! HERE COMES COPYCAT! COPYCAT HAS A CHAIR AND HE IS ON THE RING APRON! The ref is telling him to get down!
VIC WATERS: Wait, what was that rainbow that streaked through the crowd… BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER HAS SHAWN HART UP FOR THE FUNK & WAGNALL BOMB…. WAIT A SECOND, EL ARCO IRIS IS BEHIND LUSTER AND HE HAS A CHAIR…. EL ARCO IRIS CONNECTS AND HART ROLLS THROUGH AND LANDS ON THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Luster was so damn close too. BRANDON JACOBS: LUSTER KICKS OUT AND HE CHARGES EL ARCO IRIS…. EL ARCO IRIS NAILS HIM WITH THE CROWN OF HATHOR TORNADO DDT! El Arco Iris pulls the body of Hart over Luster!
VIC WATERS: This is even worse than what Luster did to Manson & Copycat. What kind of justice is this?!
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris bails out and hides under the ring and Copycat jumps off. The referee turns around to see Shawn Hart covering Luster! The ref goes down to make the count!
VIC WATERS: NO DAMNIT! BRANDON JACOBS: 1-------------------------------2----------------------------KICKOUT! Luster barely got the shoulder up.
VIC WATERS: Luster is just a damn god. That is the only way I can explain it. BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart picks Luster off the ground… HE NAILS HIM WITH THE EL CODO EXPLOSIVO! Shawn Hart makes the cover!
VIC WATERS: Good night Luster. BRANDON JACOBS: 1----------------------------------------2------------------------------3! RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of this match and still WFW World Heavyweight Champion, SHAWN HART!
BRANDON JACOBS: Luster gave him the scare of his life, but Shawn Hart was able to prevail yet again. This man has a Yankee like aura around him, just when you think he is down and out, he pulls victory out of the jaws of defeat.
VIC WATERS: I didn’t give Luster enough credit, he gave Shawn Hart a hell of a battle. BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES MANSON! HE WANTS A PIECE OF HART BEFORE THE PPV! VIC WATERS: We won’t have to wait! BRANDON JACOBS: Manson slides into the ring and Hart gets up and has words with Manson…. THE OFFICIALS ARE RUNNING DOWN TO RINGSIDE TO KEEP THESE TWO FROM GOING AT IT! Manson is motioning with a finger on his throat that Hart is dead and he is coming for the belt!
VIC WATERS: This is going to be a hell of a battle…. BRANDON JACOBS: MANSON BREAKS FREE AND HE STARTS POUNDING ON HART! THE REFS ARE TRYING TO PRY MANSON OFF! The building security is coming down to help the referees.
VIC WATERS: I don’t envy Hart. BRANDON JACOBS: We are totally out of time, I’d like to thank everyone for tuning in to New World Madness, we will be back soon with our special Halloween show Ghoulish Games with Shawn Hart putting his title on the line against Manson in his first title shot since the initial tournament in an old fashioned Chicago Street Fight!
VIC WATERS: Whoa… BRANDON JACOBS: Scotty Michaels will also be putting his WFW North American Title on the line against both Richard Gideon and Felix Red in a three way dance…. RAT FINK WILL BE PUTTING UP HIS BAD WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE IN A SPIN THE WHEEL MAKE THE DEAL MATCH…. Anarky will be making his WFW debut against Diablo in a Falls Count Anywhere in the Graveyard match… Copycat & El Arco Iris will be taking on Luster & Sean Edmunds in a double dog collar match… Alex Wylde will be facing the debuting Good Eddie… Psycho will be taking on Dormant…and last but not least, Minion will be making his WFW Debut against Adam Benjamin!
VIC WATERS: Sounds like a hell of show. BRANDON JACOBS: We’d like to thank you all for joining us. I hope you all will join us at Ghoulish Games, we may even have a couple of surprises as well. GOOD NIGHT EVERYONE!
