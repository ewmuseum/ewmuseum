WFW Ghoulish Games - Minion vs. Benjamin

16 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 16 November 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back and we are MOMENTS away from the WFW debut of Minion. VIC WATERS: Minion is a tad deranged and from the sound of his interview, rightfully mad at the fans at his fans for forgetting all of those epic battles he had with Maelstrom and Golem.
BRANDON JACOBS: Out of sight, out of mind, but I take issue, nobody has forgotten about Minion legacy. Minion has had a hell of a career and once he steps inside of a WFW ring with some of the best talent wrestling has to offer, people will remember the name Minion.
VIC WATERS: Fans are fickle. They love you one minute and they hate you the next. That is why I couldn’t be bothered trying to appease them, I am my own man. I live for myself.
BRANDON JACOBS: You can still be your own man and be a fan favorite, Manson doesn’t exactly kiss babies and the fans love him. You are just bitter because nobody likes you.
VIC WATERS: I’ll have you know that I get plenty of fan mail on a weekly basis asking how I can constantly put up with you and I always tell them the same thing.
BRANDON JACOBS: What is that? VIC WATERS: Whiskey.. ::takes a sip out of his Poland Spring Bottle:: Good stuff. BRANDON JACOBS: You should feel lucky that the WFW has a liberal drug use policy. VIC WATERS: You should feel lucky to have me as a partner, Sirius could be here in my place and you’d be the one needing the “Poland Spring Bottles.”
BRANDON JACOBS: Good point, and with that, let’s send this match to ringside for Minion anxiously awaited debut against Adam Benjamin!
(CUEUP: “Voodoo” by Godsmack) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, a Good Will Games Gold medalist from the United Kingdom, “YOURS TRULY” ADAM BENJAMIN
::Adam Benjamin walks down to the ring to the cheers from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “Where Boys Fear To Tread” by Smashing Pumpkins) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, one of the most sadistic men in all of wrestling, from New York City…. MINION!
::Minion walks down to the ring to the boos of the crowd and enters the ring:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Minion and Benjamin meet in the center of the ring and exchange words back and forth… MINION CHARGES BENJAMIN AND STARTS NAILING HIM WITH A SERIES OF FOREARMS TO THE FACE! Benjamin is wheeling backwards!
VIC WATERS: Minion is putting all that he has into these shots. BRANDON JACOBS: Minion grabs the arm of Adam Benjamin and whips him off the ropes… Minion puts his head down and sets Adam Benjamin up for a backdrop…. BENJAMIN SUNSET FLIPS MINION DOWN TO THE MAT! The ref goes down for the count! 1------------------2------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Adam Benjamin is a master mat technician, you can’t put your guard down for a second.
BRANDON JACOBS: Benjamin is the first up… BENJAMIN STARTS LAYING IN THE CHOPS INTO MINION! LISTEN TO HOW HARD BENJAMIN IS LAYING INTO MINION!
VIC WATERS: Benjamin is putting up a hell of a fight. BRANDON JACOBS: But wait a second, Minion is LAUGHING AT BENJAMIN! These chops aren’t making any impact at all!
VIC WATERS: Uh oh… BRANDON JACOBS: Minion puts the arm of Adam Benjamin over his right shoulder…. BIG T-BONE SUPLEX!
VIC WATERS: GOOD LORD! BENJAMIN LANDED RIGHT ON HIS NECK! BRANDON JACOBS: Minion is standing over Benjamin taunting him and Benjamin still isn’t moving…
VIC WATERS: Whenever you land on your neck, it is extremely dangerous. BRANDON JACOBS: Minion is going over to the corner….. HE IS RIPPING OFF THE TURNBUCKLE COVER! The ref is trying to see if Benjamin is okay and hasn’t been paying a bit of a attention to Minion.
VIC WATERS: If Minion does what I think he is going to do, Benjamin is in a lot of trouble.
BRANDON JACOBS: Minion walks over to Adam Benjamin and picks him up in the power bomb position…. COME ON! THERE IS NO CALL FOR THIS!
VIC WATERS: Benjamin sure could use come help here! BRANDON JACOBS: THE REF IS TRYING TO GET IN MINION’S WAY…. BUT MINION TURNS AND SWINGS THE FEET OF BENJAMIN TOWARDS THE REF AND THE REF MOVES OUT OF THE WAY!
VIC WATERS: The ref certainly doesn’t want to die. BRANDON JACOBS: Minion stabilizes Benjamin and charges toward the corner… POWERBOMB ONTO THE EXPOSED METAL TURNBUCKLE! BENJAMIN IS DONE!
VIC WATERS: Benjamin is the best damn technical wrestler we have here, why does Minion have to pick him to make an example of!
BRANDON JACOBS: Minion drags the body of Benjamin towards the center of the ring… Minion arrogantly puts the foot over Benjamin and makes the cover. The ref goes down to make the count! 1--------------------------2-----------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. MINION! BRANDON JACOBS: Wait a second, MINION ISN’T DONE! He is going outside for a table! VIC WATERS: The WFW needs to get some damn officals out here! BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT…. HERE COMES EL ARCO IRIS WITH A CHAIR AND MINION BAILS THROUGH THE CROWD! El Arco Iris had seen enough.
VIC WATERS: Minion REALLY went too far tonight. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is going to Adam Benjamin’s side, these two may have had their differences, but there is code among wrestlers that while you do try to beat your opponent, you never purposely try to end their damn career.
VIC WATERS: Minion is walking up the stairs laughing at this whole situation. He is a sick BASTARD…. SICK!
BRANDON JACOBS: Adam Benjamin is refusing the paramedics help.. he wants to get the back on his own and El Arco Iris is helping him up so he can walk to the back.
CROWD: BENJAMIN! BENJAMIN! BENJAMIN! BENJAMIN! VIC WATERS: Whenever Adam Benjamin can over come this neck injury, he is going to make Minion pay.
BRANDON JACOBS: This crowd is on their feet as Adam Benjamin and El Arco Iris head to the back. I can’t believe he did this to Benjamin, one of the most promising stars in all of wrestling today. This just isn’t right. He was on the verge of becoming a break out start and Minion had to do something like this.
VIC WATERS: Benjamin will be back, he has the heart of a lion. ::El Arco Iris and Adam Benjamin walk through the curtain:: BRANDON JACOBS: On that sad note folks, when we comeback, we will have our Double Dog Collar between the teams of Copycat & El Arco Iris and Sean Edmunds & Luster…. STAY TUNED!
