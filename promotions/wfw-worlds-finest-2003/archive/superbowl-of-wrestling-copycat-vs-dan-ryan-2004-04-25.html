WFW — Card

Superbowl of Wrestling: Copycat vs. Dan Ryan

25 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 25 April 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: I tend to agree with Copycat. Why isn’t Copycat getting title shots? Why did Wylde get the board of directors to put Felix Red on his team only to turn on him against him and his team at War Games? Copycat is one of the best men we have and he is repeatedly sacrificed to the wolves.
VIC WATERS: The worst thing that ever happened to Copycat was winning that match against Anarky. He was on the road to a title shot when he managed to piss off Anarky and Wells and the rest is history. Plus, he managed to invoke the wrath of Wylde by beating him in the four way match and ever since then, he has been out to make Copycat’s life a living hell.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat has taken more abuse than Jesus did in the The Passion and if you didn’t see that, that is a whole damn lot.
VIC WATERS: Take a look at Manson, he went through a stretch where he went through what Copycat is going through now and he emerged with a shot at the World Championship at the Superbowl of Wrestling.
BRANDON JACOBS: Manson shouldn’t have gone through that either… and why is Copycat facing Dan Ryan?
VIC WATERS: There is a long line of history behind the match here tonight and WFW choose him because they felt he was their very best. Things are great now, but do I have to remind anyone of the bad blood between the old NGEN and CSWA back in its day? Do you realize the history in a current CSWA World Heavyweight Champion standing and competing in a WFW ring?
BRANDON JACOBS: No, no you don’t. It is honor for Copycat to be the one here today defending the honor of the WFW against a man of the size and stature of Dan Ryan. But still, why couldn’t he have faced Edmunds instead?
VIC WATERS: Because there are going to be other shows AFTER the Superbowl of Wrestling…. I know everyone wanted to see Edmunds verses Copycat, but we were already way too loaded as it is. Shouldn’t Edmunds verses Copycat when it happens be the center of the show instead of getting lost on the card?
BRANDON JACOBS: I guess you are right. VIC WATERS: You are damn right I am skippy. Besides, Copycat shouldn’t worry about who he isn’t facing, he should be worry about who he is facing. Dan Ryan is a whole lot of man.
BRANDON JACOBS: I can assure you Copycat isn’t taking Dan Ryan lightly. He wants management to take notice so he can finally break through the glass ceiling and there is no better way than for him to beat Dan Ryan here tonight. But it won’t be easy, Dan Ryan has rolled through names like Southern and Hornet in the CSWA.
VIC WATERS: I’ve seen Copycat beat some of the best here in WFW and there is no doubt in my mind that he possibly beat Ryan. Is it likely? I don’t really know. Copycat hasn’t faced anyone bigger than him yet alone someone the size of Ryan yet in the WFW so it hard to tell how he will do.
BRANDON JACOBS: This match is going to be awfully interesting, let’s send this one down to ringside and get this match underway!
(CUEUP: Copycat voice/over “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, LEND ME YOUR EARS!” followed by “My Way” by Butch Walker) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, the former WWL World Heavyweight Champion, from Kalamazoo, Michigan… COPYCAT!
::Copycat walks down to the ring with microphone in hand and enters the ring, Copycat throws the mic down to the boos of the crowd::
(CUEUP: ‘Zero’ – Smashing Pumpkins, a strobe effect takes over the arena and the crowd comes to their feet almost in unison as “EGO BUSTER” rapidly flashes in alternating black and white contrast on the screen.)
(On the big screen……CUTTO: The word “YOU” metal construction gray font/black background. CUTTO: RYAN beheading CRAIG MILES with a clothesline that rockets him into a 360 tailspin onto his head! CUTTO: The word “ARE” CUTTO: RYAN Gorilla Pressing EVAN AHO high above his MASSIVE frame. CUTTO: The word “BUSTED. ” CUTTO: A rapid fire sequence shot of RYAN pulverizing his opponents in the ‘Humility Bomb’ – a High Angle Last Ride styled Powerbomb.)
RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, standing at 6’7, 330 pounds, the CSWA World Heavyweight Champion, DAN RYAN!
(A roar from the crowd as Ryan appears on the stage – the flickering strobe lights flashing across his face. Pyro explodes behind him as he makes his way to the ring, rolls under the bottom rope and climbs a nearby turnbuckle. Once again the crowd cheers as he thrusts his arms into the air with a yell. After a moment, he hops down, the music cuts out and he walks slowly around the ring and enters.)
*bell rings* VIC WATERS: Somewhere a whole bunch of school children collectively had a seizure from the rapid strobbing light effects. Evil strobbing.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hush you, I think it is cool. The ref is reading Copycat and Dan Ryan the riot act to make sure order is maintained in the match.
VIC WATERS: If these two wanted to get wild, there is a referee in the world who could stop these two behemoths from doing what they want.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat looks up at the taller Dan Ryan and smiles. VIC WATERS: That can’t be good…. BRANDON JACOBS: DAN RYAN FIRES THE SHOT WITH A HARD RIGHT HAND AND COPYCAT COMES BACK WITH ONE OF HIS OWN!
ViC WATERS: THE WAR IS ON! BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan is backing Copycat up to the ropes…. DAN RYAN CLOTHESLINES COPYCAT OVER THE TOP ROPE AND BOTH MEN SPILL ONTO THE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: Thank god we aren’t calling this match at ringside. BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan picks Copycat up by the hair…. BOTH MEN BEGIN EXCHANGING PUNCHES AGAIN!!
VIC WATERS: I haven’t seen a match like this since the days of the Sportatorium BRANDON JACOBS: DAN RYAN KICKS COPYCAT IN THE MIDSECTION! Dan Ryan rams the head of Copycat hard right into the steal stairs! Copycat has been busted open!
VIC WATERS: Dan Ryan strikes first blood. BRANDON JACOBS: DAN RYAN RAMS COPYCAT’S HEAD RIGHT INTO THE STEEL GUARDRAIL! Copycat falls down on his hands and knees.
VIC WATERS: Dan Ryan is showing the world why many consider him the most dominant wrestler in the world.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan is ripping up the mats at ringside! What could he been planning on doing here?!
VIC WATERS: Who knows, but why isn’t the ref stopping this? BRANDON JACOBS: With Wylde’s hatred of Copycat, why he would he stop this early before Dan Ryan can do his damage?
VIC WATERS: Good point. BRANDON JACOBS: DAN RYAN IS PICKING UP COPYCAT….. OVER THE HEAD BELLY TO BELLY ONTO THE CONCRETE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: Good God. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat grabs his back in pain…. DAN RYAN RUSHES TO PICK UP COPYCAT AGAIN… ANOTHER OVER THE HEAD BELLY TO BELLY ON THE CONCRETE!
VIC WATERS: I’m starting to understand why management gave Copycat this match. BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan rolls Copycat back into the ring and he steps over the top rope and re-enters. Dan Ryan goes for the cover! It could be over right here!
VIC WATERS: Copycat has been thrashed. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref goes down for the count! 1-------------------------------------------------------2------------------------------- KICKOUT! Copycat somehow managed to kickout!
VIC WATERS: Damn it. BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan picks up Copycat….. HE IRISH WHIPS COPYCAT INTO THE CORNER AND HIS BACK CRASHES INTO THE TURNBUCKLE!
VIC WATERS: The best way to cut into a big man’s mobility it to make it hard for him to move and working over the back is a hell of a way to do that.
BRANDON JACOBS: DAN RYAN CHARGES COPYCAT IN THE CORNER AND DRIVES ALL 330 POUNDS INTO COPYCAT!
VIC WATERS: Ryan is being smart and he is really using his size to his advantage. BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan moves out of the corner and Copycat is still SOMEHOW MANAGING TO HOLD HIMSELF UP ON THE ROPES!
VIC WATERS: As the feud with LOVE proved, Copycat can take a hell of amount of abuse and still comeback for more.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan tries to ARM DRAG the exhausted Copycat down to the mat…. COPYCAT CLOTHESLINES DAN RYAN OVER THE TOP ROPE WITH HIS OTHER ARM AND DAN RYAN FALLS TO THE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: They say cats have nine lives, but this is ridiculous. Where did that come from?
VIC WATERS: God bless adrenaline. BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan is having trouble getting up…. COPYCAT IS GOING UP TO THE TOP ROPE…. COPYCAT DIVES OFF ONTO DAN RYAN WITH A FROG SPLASH AS DAN RYAN WAS TRYING TO GET UP!
CROWD: COPYCAT! COPYCAT! COPYCAT! VIC WATERS: Dan Ryan DOWN! BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is getting up and he is clutching his ribs, he is waiting for Dan Ryan to get up… A dazed Dan Ryan is trying to get up….. KIT KAT KICK ON DAN RYAN AND DAN RYAN STUMBLES BACKWARDS OVER THE STAIRS! The list of people hit by the Kit Kat Kick reads like a whose who in wrestling.
VIC WATERS: Dan Ryan looked like he may have injured the leg when he fell over those stairs.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat goes to pick up Dan Ryan off the ground and he rolls him back into the ring.
VIC WATERS: Thank god they are taking this back in the ring. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat re-enters the ring and he locks Dan Ryan in a bear hug! VIC WATERS: This is a smart move because it gives Copycat back a chance to rest up from the abuse he took earlier and a chance to work over the ribs of Dan Ryan which he attacked with the Frog Splash.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan looks awfully tired. VIC WATERS: How often does Dan Ryan get to go toe to toe with someone his own size? It is enough to tire anyone.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is really squeezing in the bear hug and Dan Ryan is barely able to fight it. Copycat is asking the ref to check on Dan Ryan.
VIC WATERS: This would be a big feather in his cap if he got the submission here. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref raises Dan Ryan’s hand once…. IT FALLS… WAIT, DAN RYAN THUMBS COPYCAT IN THE EYE AS IT DROPS AND COPYCAT LETS GO OF THE BEAR HUG!
VIC WATERS: The ref just aided him there in setting Copycat up! I bet he has money on Ryan.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is grabbing his eye…. A TIRED DAN RYAN POPS RIGHT IN THE FACE WITH A RIGHT AND ANOTHER RIGHT…. DAN RYAN WINDS UP AND HITS COPYCAT WITH ANOTHER AND COPYCAT GOES DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Copycat is flopping around trying to get up after that shot to the eye… BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan is setting up…. Copycat is slowly getting up…. DAN RYAN HITS COPYCAT WITH A SUPERKICK AND COPYCAT FALLS BACK AND HIS ARMS GET TANGLED IN THE ROPE!
VIC WATERS: Dan Ryan has him right where he wants him. BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan starts kicking Copycat in the midsection repeatedly with his big boots! The referee is trying to get the ropes undone from Copycat’s arms but he is struggling to get him free.
VIC WATERS: Those ropes can be like a vise, he is going to be hurting for awhile tomorrow. BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan bounces off the ropes…. YAKUZA KICK RIGHT TO THE FACE OF COPYCAT! The ropes come undone around Copycat’s arms and he falls down to the mat.
VIC WATERS: That shot could have taken out some teeth. BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan picks up the stunned Copycat off of the mat and waistlocks him around the midsection…. RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX DOWN ONTO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Right onto that back Dan Ryan worked over earlier! BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan makes the cover! 1----------------------------------2------------------------------- KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: The crowd thought he had him there and so did I. BRANDON JACOBS: A frustrated Dan Ryan yells at the referee and picks up Copycat off the mat…. DAN RYAN WHIPS COPYCAT HARD INTO CORNER!
VIC WATERS: Dan Ryan is on the verge of putting Copycat away, you can sense it. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat stumbles out of the corner… DAN RYAN GOES FOR AN OVER HEAD BELLY TO BELLY BUT DAN RYAN’S LEG BUCKLES AND COPYCAT FALLS ON TOP OF HIM!
VIC WATERS: That was the leg he injured when he fell over steel stairs after the Kit Kat Kick.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat starts pounding away with right hands to the face of Dan Ryan! Dan Ryan is trying to get him off of him but he struggling to do with leg bothering him.
VIC WATERS: I remember when I had trouble with my knee, I wouldn’t wish that on anyone. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat picks the grimacing Dan Ryan up off the mat…. OVER THE HEAD BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX ON DAN RYAN!
VIC WATERS: Copycat does that move better than anyone. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat puts the legs together of Copycat…. COPYCAT BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND HE HITS THE CAT’S MEOW (PEOPLE’S ELBOW)!
CROWD: COPYCAT! COPYCAT! COPYCAT! BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is going for the cover! 1----------------------------------------2---------------------------------- DAN RYAN JUST GETS THE SHOULDER UP!
VIC WATERS: One of these guys has to hit the other with everything including the kitchen sink if they want to pin the other.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat drags Dan Ryan over to the corner…. Copycat climbs up to the second rope…. DOUBLE FOOT STOMP ON THE INJURED LEG OF DAN RYAN!
VIC WATERS: Copycat just drove all of his weight on the injured leg of Ryan. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is checking to see if Dan Ryan wants to continue and he is saying he does. Dan Ryan certain has the heart of a champion. There is no quit in him.
VIC WATERS: That is the reason he has successfully defended his title against some of the greats in this sport like Southern and Hornet.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is dragging Dan Ryan to the center of the ring…. HE GRABS THE LEGS OF DAN RYAN AND HE HOOKS ON A FIGURE FOUR LEGLOCK!
VIC WATERS: Who would have ever though that two men as big as Copycat and Dan Ryan would be exchanging leg locks?
BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan looks like he is almost ready to give in! VIC WATERS: Melton who is reefing the main beat Dan Ryan with this hold in the NFW War Games match.
BRANDON JACOBS: What people don’t realize is that these bigger wrestlers have thicker legs to compensate for carrying their size so it is much harder to get submission to a leg lock on a bigger man than a smaller man, thankfully Copycat is as big as they come.
VIC WATERS: Look at Dan Ryan, the sweat pouring off of him. BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan is as well conditioned as they come, but when you hurt your back or injured your knee, it eats in your endurance like nobodies business. DAN RYAN YELLS OUT IN PAIN AS COPYCAT DIGS IT IN SOME MORE!
VIC WATERS: Dan Ryan can’t hold on forever. BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan is trying to reach for the ropes…. HE SHOULDERS FALL BACK ON THE MAT! The ref goes down to count! 1--------------------------------------2-------------------- RYAN GETS HIS SHOULDERS UP JUST IN TIME!
VIC WATERS: It is very hard not to lay back because with the pain of the figure four, you are focusing on not submitting.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan is pulling himself closer to the ropes with his hands…. … HE REACHES AND COMES UP A COUPLE OF INCHES SHORT!
VIC WATERS: He just needs a little bit more. BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan tries again…. HE GRABS THE ROPES! The ref is calling for the break.
VIC WATERS: Thank god. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat bends over to pick up Dan Ryan…. DAN RYAN GRABS THE HAIR AND PULLS COPYCAT OUT OF THE RING AND HE STUMBLES OUT TO THE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: This is going to buy Dan Ryan some time to recover.. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat gets up AND HE GRABS THE LEGS OF RYAN AND PULLS HIM OUT OF THE RING! Copycat starts laying into Dan Ryan with a series of rights!
VIC WATERS: That was totally unfair of Copycat to pull Dan Ryan out of the ring when he wasn’t expecting it. They should keep this match in the ring like two civilized gentlemen.
BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT WHIPS DAN RYAN TOWARDS THE GUARDRAIL….. DAN RYAN REVERSES IT AND COPYCAT GOES IN BACK FIRST INTO THE COLD HARD STEEL!
VIC WATERS: Right into that back again! When Dan Ryan hits Copycat with that Humility Bomb, it is going to be all over because there isn’t a chance in hell Copycat can kick out of that with his back being the way it is.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan limps over to Copycat…. DAN RYAN PUTS COPYCAT IN A FULL NELSON…. DRAGON SUPLEX ONTO THE CONCRETE FLOOR!
VIC WATERS: Goodnight Copycat! If you were watching carefully, Dan Ryan was using his left leg to power Copycat back in order to avoid putting weight on his right.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan rolls Copycat into the ring and rolls in after him. WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES ICEKOLD AND SHE LOOKS LIKE SHE IS WORRIED ABOUT COPYCAT’S SAFETY!
VIC WATERS: Copycat is a big man, he can take care of himself. She has no reason to be at ringside other than to collect his body after Ryan is done with him.
BRANDON JACOBS: There is nothing wrong with showing some concern for your fellow human being, if you had any sort of heart, you would understand that.
VIC WATERS: Being the cold heartless bastard I am, I don’t understand the meaning of the word.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan picks Copycat off the mat and puts him in a front face lock…. DAN RYAN GOES FOR THE DDT BUT COPYCAT REACHES OUT TO GRAB DAN RYAN’S TIGHTS AND ACCIDENTALLY GRABS THE REFS SHIRT!
VIC WATERS: Wait a second, here comes Icekold…. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat tries to get untangled…. DAN RYAN FALLS DOWN TO THE MAT IN ALL THE COMMOTION! Copycat goes over and he is yelling at Icekold to get out of the ring and let them fight!
VIC WATERS: It is because of all this blatant inference that Copycat doesn’t get the respect that he deserves.
BRANDON JAOCBS: He is trying his best to get Icekold out of the ring but she is refusing to go… HERE COMES THE REFEREE AND HE IS SAYING TO ICEKOLD IF SHE DOESN”T LEAVE THE RING SHE IS GOING TO GET COPYCAT DQED!
VIC WATERS: Dan Ryan wins, YAH! BRANDON JACOBS: Dan Ryan looks at Copycat to see what all the commotion is about…. Copycat shrugs… AND HE KICKS DAN RYAN IN THE GROIN DOUBLING HIM OVER IN PAIN!
VIC WATERS: Dan Ryan has a whole lot to hurt too. BRANDON JACOBS: Icekold jumps down and the referee goes back to the match. COPYCAT PICKS UP DAN RYAN…… HE HITS HIM WITH THE LITTERBOMB!
VIC WATERS: No damnit! BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat makes the cover! 1-------------------------------2-----------------------------------3! COPYCAT WINS!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match….. COPYCAT! BRANDON JACOBS: It is a shame a match like this had to end like that…. Wait a second, COPYCAT HAS THE MIC!
COPYCAT: Now it's time for you to learn some RESPECT! BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT PICKS UP DAN RYAN AND LOCKS ON THE CAT’S CLAW! OFFICIALS ARE RUNNING DOWN TO RINGSIDE TO PRY COPYCAT OFF OF DAN RYAN!
VIC WATERS: This is totally uncalled for, Dan Ryan is our guest, he doesn’t deserve this damn it!
BRANDON JACOBS: Officials are pulling Copycat off and Dan Ryan is mad as hell at Copycat…. NOW DAN RYAN IS TRYING TO GO AFTER COPYCAT BUT OFFICIAL ARE JUMPING ON HIM TO KEEP HIM AWAY!
VIC WATERS: This has turned into a near riot! BRANDON JACOBS: More people are coming down to keep these two apart…. COPYCAT IS TRYING TO BREAK FREE BUT THE OFFICIALS TACKLE HIM IN TIME!
VIC WATERS: Someone get Copycat under control before he causes even more of an incident. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we are going to take a commercial break and when we get back, hopefully everything will be settled and we’ll have our WFW North American Title match between Richard Gideon and Psycho, STAY TUNED!
