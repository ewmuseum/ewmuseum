WFW: New World Madness - Dormant vs. Pulsar

22 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 22 October 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome back everyone, it is finally time to kick off the show, this should be a hell of a match.
VIC WATERS: If Dormant can win this match, like Manson, he will have the home field advantage at the PPV since it will be located in Chicago.
BRANDON JACOBS: That is a hell of a point. Rat Fink has been in battle after intense battle lately whenever he has put up the BAD World Title, even when he has had help to win. As devious as Rat Fink is, I do not know how many World Title defenses he has left in him with all the abuse he has taken.
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink is a tough SOB. He showed that against Manson, whoever faces Rat Fink will have a hell of a battle on their hands if they want to wrestle the title out of that man’s hands.
BRANDON JACOBS: Let’s send this one to ringside and find out who it will be! (CUEUP: “Whiskey in a Jar” by Metallica) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from Australia, standing at over 6’3, the man who won the six man tag for his team at Almost Live, PULSAR!
::fans react with a mixture of boos and cheers for Pulsar as he comes down to the aisle and enters the ring::
(CUEUP: “Ariels” by System of Doom) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Chicago, Illinois, the man who almost single handedly beat tonight’s World Heavyweight Title Challenger, DORMANT!
::crowd cheers loudly as Dormant comes down the aisle and enters the ring:: BRANDON JACOBS: DORMANT CHARGES PULSAR WITH A SPEAR BEFORE THE BELL AND DRIVES HIM DOWN TO THE MAT!
*bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Dormant jumps on Pulsar and pounds away on his head with several quick rights! The referee is stepping in to make sure that the punches aren’t closed fists.
VIC WATERS: Dormant realizes that if he is going to beat Pulsar, he needs to get the advantage quickly because Pulsar has a lot of momentum on his side.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dormant grabs the arms of Pulsar and tries to pin his shoulders down to the mat! 1----------------------2--------SHOULDER UP! Dormant powers his arms down again 1---------------2----------SHOULDER UP!
VIC WATERS: Those pinning attempts there may have been simply to get into Pulsar’s head that he the advantage to fluster him a bit.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dormant goes to grab the arms of Pulsar and PULSAR PULLS DORMANT TOWARDS THE ROPES AND THE REF FORCES THE BREAK!
VIC WATERS: Smart move there by Pulsar… BRANDON JACOBS: Dormant stands up and he grabs the hair of Pulsar and pull him up. DORMANT IRISH WHIPS PULSAR OFF THE ROPES… Dormant bounces off the ropes… DORMANT NAILS PULSAR WITH A FLYING FOREARM!
VIC WATERS: Dormant didn’t give Pulsar anytime to get up and gain the advantage. He pounced on him right away and got the job done there.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dormant is setting up behind the dazed Pulsar for his release German Suplex… Pulsar is slowly getting up…. DORMANT HAS ON THE WAISTLOCK! PULSAR KNOWS HE IS CAUGHT AND HE FIRES AND ELBOW BACK INTO THE FACE OF DORMANT AND HE NAILS HIM RIGHT IN THE MOUTH!
VIC WATERS: That was a truly vicious elbow by Pulsar there, it nailed him right in the mouth, it could have knocked out a couple of teeth.
BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR CHARGES DORMANT AND NAILS HIM WITH A CLOTHESLINE AND DORMANT GOES DOWN! Pulsar runs over to the ropes and exits through and sets himself on the apron… Dormant is on the ground and he looks groggy… HE NAILS HIM WITH A SLINGSHOT SOMERSAULT LEGDROP OVER THE TOP ROPE!
VIC WATERS: That is an incredible move for a man his size. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar hooks the leg and makes the cover! 1----------------------2---------KICKOUT! Dormant tries to get up… PULSAR NAILS DORMANT WITH AN ELBOW DROP THE FACE!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar is focusing on the head of Dormant in an effort to dazed and confuse him long enough to get the pin.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar picks Dormant up and puts him in an inverted face lock… INVERTED SUPLEX AND DORMANT GOES FACE FIRST INTO THE MAT! Pulsar rolls Dormant over! He covers! 1------------------2----------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: He didn’t hook the leg there and it may have cost him. BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR IS CALLING FOR THE BLACK HOLE! Pulsar goes to pick Dormant off the mat….. SMALL PACKAGE BY DORMANT! The ref goes down to make the count! 1-------------------2---KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar was almost caught by surprise there. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar gets up and he stomps away on Dormant in anger! PULSAR THROWS DORMANT TO THE OUTSIDE!
VIC WATERS: Dormant took a nasty tumble on the way out there. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar exits the ring and drops the double axe handle on the back of Dormant right into the lower back from the apron! Pulsar pulls Dormant up… HE GOES TO IRISH WHIP DORMANT INTO THE GUARDRAIL BUT DORMANT REVERSES IT AND PULSAR GOES IN BACK FIRST!
VIC WATERS: Dormant may have finally caught his break to turn this match around. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar grabs his back… DORMANT COMES FROM BEHIND AND LOCKS IN THE WAISTLOCK… PULSAR GOES FOR AN ELBOW BUT THIS TIME DORMANT DUCKS! DORMANT NAILS PULSAR WITH A RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX RIGHT ONTO THE FLOOR!
::crowd erupts into a BAD KICKS ASS chant:: VIC WATERS: You have to give Dormant credit there, he learned from his mistake earlier and he delivered the move like only he can.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dormant is ripping up the mats at ringside! He grabs Pulsar and sets up for a powerbomb… HE PICKS HIM UP… PULSAR NAILS HIM WITH LEFTS AND RIGHTS AND DORMANT IS STRUGGLING NOT TO FALL BACK!
VIC WATERS: Come on Pulsar! BRANDON JACOBS: Dormant regains his balance… HE POWERBOMBS HIM DOWN ON THE CONCRETE HARD! VIC WATERS: Pulsar is dead meat. BRANDON JACOBS: Dormant rolls Pulsar into the ring and goes in after him… VIC WATERS: Pulsar looks totally out of it, his anger got the best of him and it may have cost him the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Dormant is calling for the CLUSTER! Dormant picks Pulsar up in a vertical suplex…. PULSAR STRUGGLES AND DORMANT LOSES HIS GRIP AND PULSAR FALLS BEHIND DORMANT!
VIC WATERS: YES! BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar puts Dormant in an inverted facelock…. THE BLACK HOLE (ROLL OF THE DICE)!
VIC WATERS: He nailed it! BRANDON JACOBS: An exhausted and dazed Pulsar makes the cover! 1-------------------2---------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match and the man going on to face Rat Fink for the BAD World Heavyweight Title, PULSAR!
BRANDON JACOBS: Very close match, but Pulsar just squeaked out the victory. Dormant really impressed me here tonight and I’m sure that he will be challenging for world titles in no time.
VIC WATERS: This was a very close match, I just thought lady luck was on Pulsar’s side tonight and it was on his side in the six man tag where he did the improbable and got the victory for his team. Pulsar is a very talented young wrestler, Rat Fink may just be the first step on a greater road to the top.
BRANDON JACOBS: Okay folks, when we comeback, we will be right back with Copycat vs. Sean Edmunds for WWL bragging rights! STAY TUNED!
