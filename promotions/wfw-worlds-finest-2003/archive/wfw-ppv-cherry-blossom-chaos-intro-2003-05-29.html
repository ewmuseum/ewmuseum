WFW PPV: Cherry Blossom Chaos Intro

29 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 29 May 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome everyone to Cherry Blossom Chaos, we are live from Washington, DC in front of the Reflecting Pool where over 20,000 fans have gathered to witnesses today’s free PPV show!
VIC WATERS: You should make these bastards pay, free doesn’t pay the bills. BRANDON JACOBS: The WFW Booking Committee wanted to give something back for America and all of those who have served and died for their country. Some things are bigger than money Vic, we love our country and we want to show the world what great action World’s Finest Wrestling has to offer.
VIC WATERS: I’ve been having horrible nightmares all week since I’ve arrived here so speak for yourself.
BRANDON JACOBS: What kind of nightmares have you been having? VIC WATERS: I keep on dreaming that Doc Silver is sitting in a theater watching a play when some figure, which I can’t make out, comes from behind and kills him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Jesus Christ, you have some violent dreams. VIC WATERS: I’ve already gone to Doc and I’ve told him not to show up today but that silly fool insists on being here to defend his title being the champion that he is.
BRANDON JACOBS: There is no such thing as premonitions, so relax. VIC WATERS: I go back a long time with Doc, I love the man like he is my own son. I can’t relax. There are four guys who are bent on winning the title at any price.
BRANDON JACOBS: I’ve never seen four men so equally matched in all of my life. If any one of them takes it, I wouldn’t be shocked.
VIC WATERS: I really love Shawn Hart, if Doc for some reason doesn’t win tonight, I want him to go all the way. Since the beginning of the league, he has been one of the cornerstones from which this league was built.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart is a tremendous performer, but can he win the big title? Shawn Hart was one of the three initial men who fought for the two titles which are the backbone of this league and he just came up a little bit short. I’m going to go out of a limb and say that either Gideon or Psycho wins the title.
VIC WATERS: Gideon has shown a lot of heart lately, and he has made life extremely difficult for Doc but remember, he is only a rookie. This is his first PPV main event and you never know how a guy steps up for the big show until it happens. Tonight is a four way elimination match which means that if Gideon wants to win, he is going to have to most likely beat more than one guy to do it. He may have Doc’s number, but does he have Shawn Hart’s or Psycho’s?
BRANDON JACOBS: Speaking of Psycho, he couldn’t ask for a match that more perfectly fits who he is as a wrestler. This is going to be an all out brawl and that is his specialty. Since day one, I haven’t seen anyone bust their but as much as he has. If he didn’t get the horrible seeding of having Doc Silver in the first round, he could have gone all the way to the finals and won it.
VIC WATERS: Psycho’s major thorn in his side is and will always be Doc Silver. He has been foaming at the mouth for his chance to face him for the title and tonight he has a chance… his chance to fail like he did in the tournament.
BRANDON JACOBS: I wouldn’t be too over confident, Psycho is will to do anything and I mean anything in his power to win. He reminds me a lot of our North American Champion Manson who will have to take on both Scotty Michaels & Rat Fink in order to reclaim his title belt.
VIC WATERS: Rat Fink has Manson’s number but I’m scared for Rat Fink if Manson gets his hands on him.
BRANDON JACOBS: It is like playing with fire in an explosives factory, eventually you are going to slip and the worst is going to come.
VIC WATERS: Scotty Michaels has a real opportunity to win the title tonight because Manson wants more than anything to get his hands on Rat Fink.
BRANDON JACOBS: That is very true, but if Manson doesn’t reclaim the title as well as get his hands around Rat Fink’s scrawny neck, Rat Fink will still have won. Rat Fink stole Manson’s title, even if Manson doesn’t want it because he wants the WFW World Championship title, it is still Manson’s property and Rat Fink stole it make a fool out of him. Manson is nobody’s fool and he is going to pay Rat Fink back for what he did.
VIC WATERS: The wrath of Manson is one that nobody should feel. BRANDON JACOBS: We’ve already done enough talking, let’s get the show on the road, when we come back, we will start the show with Chris Bradford verse the up and comer Luster!
VIC WATERS: ::shakes head:: BRANDON JACOBS: We will be back after this!
