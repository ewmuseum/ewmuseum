WFW: New World Madness: Copycat vs. Edmunds

23 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 23 October 2003 (BACKSTAGE) COPYCAT: Look, I told you I don't need you at ringside, alright? So don't follow me out there. ICEKOLD: What is WRONG with you lately? COPYCAT: Nothing is WRONG with me lately. But you weren't a whole lot of help with Scotty Michaels when I was getting QUADRUPLE-TEAMED for Christ's sake. ICEKOLD: Will you let that go? What did you want me to do, take on all three of them at once? COPYCAT: Look...whatever. Just don't be at ringside, all right? Edmunds is probably expecting you to be there anyway. So blow off. ::An incensed Icekold then storms off.:: (RINGSIDE) VIC WATERS: Well, someone is being catty tonight. BRANDON JACOBS: I can understand Copycat being upset after what happened to him against Michaels. Four men conspired to keep him from winning the WFW North American Title. That wasn’t right.
VIC WATERS: The dude needs to get over it. BRANDON JACOBS: It was for the WFW North American Title, the title that Manson put on the map and made the most prestigious secondary title in all of wrestling.
VIC WATERS: I just don’t understand why Copycat would send Icekold to the back when he knows Sensational Sherri will be in Edmunds’ corner.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat knows what he is doing… VIC WATERS: HIS FATHER IS THE DISTRICT ATTORNEY! BRANDON JACOBS: If I ever meet the FOX executive who thought it was a good idea to run that ad for Skin a million times during a playoff game, I’m going to jail for murder.
VIC WATERS: This match has a playoff game atmosphere so I can drop the reference. BRANDON JACOBS: These two men are two of the finest wrestlers ever to come out of WWL and while they were called “WWL legends” by the WFW hierarchy who put together this match at Jared Wells’ request, before he got hurt and told management that he would not be able to participate tonight, that wasn’t meant to imply they were some leftovers from a dead promotion. They just all shared a common bond of being WWL World Champions and were quite proud of their history, so that is why this match was put together. Both of these men are talented and have the talent to be WFW World Heavyweight Champions and this is a simply a celebration of their history.
VIC WATERS: Let the dead stay dead Jim. BRANDON JACOBS: This should be a hell of a match and I expect these two to go all out tonight to prove to each other that they are the better man, let’s send this match to ringside and get this match started.
CUEUP: Copycat voice/over “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, LEND ME YOUR EARS!” followed by “My Way” by Butch Walker) RING ANNOUNCER: And the first member of the opposing team, from Kalamazoo, MI, the former WWL World Heavyweight Champion, COPYCAT!
::Copycat comes down lethargically to a loud pop from the crowd and enters the ring, he picks up the mic to speak…::
COPYCAT: ::looks at the microphone and throws it down:: (CUEUP: “Good Boys" by Blondie) ::Sensational Sherri comes walking down to the ring wearing a sexy white tight fitting dress with a gold belt. She has gold face glitter on and her eye makeup is extravagant as usual. She rips the microphone out of the announcer's hand. The crowd boos::.
VIC WATERS: FOXY LADY! SHERRI: "Oh shut up! Prepare to be graced with the presence of a REAL Superstar!" (The crowd boos) SHERRI: "Introducing, from Boston, Massachusetts. Weighing 228 pounds, the former (stresses) THREE time WWL World champion.. SIMPLY SENSATIONAL SEAN EDMUNDS!"
(Crowd boos) (CUEUP: "Brain Stew" by Green Day. Edmunds comes down to the ring wearing his usual sparkling white and gold trimmed attire. He enters the ring and Sensational
Sherri takes off the WM9-esque Luger top. ) BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT HAS SEEN ENOUGH AND HE ATTACKS EDMUNDS BEFORE THE BELL! Sherri bails out of the ring away from the chaos.
VIC WATERS: Copycat was playing possum. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is backed into the corner and Copycat is laying it into Edmunds! Edmunds is starting to slump in the corner from the impact of the blows.
VIC WATERS: Copycat is showing his power here and Edmunds is trapped with nowhere to go. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat puts a waistlock onto Edmunds… OVER THE HEAD BELLY TO BELLY! Edmunds crashes hard on his back and he reaches back in pain!
VIC WATERS: Copycat throws the phattest Belly to Belly in all of wrestling today. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is slowly getting up…. COPYCAT NAILS HIM WITH ANOTHER OVERHEAD BELLY TO BELLY! Copycat goes for the cover! 1-----------------------2----------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds needs to get the momentum back before he winds up losing this match. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat picks up Edmunds and puts him over his shoulder…. COPYCAT RAMS EDMUNDS HARD INTO THE TURNBUCKLE BACK FIRST! Copycat keeps Edmunds over his shoulder…. AND HE RAMS HIM INTO THE OTHER TURNBUCKLE FOR GOOD MEASURE!
VIC WATERS: Copycat is an angry, angry man. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat sidewalk slams Edmunds down to the mat on that injured back. Copycat calls to the crowd for the Cat’s Meow! Copycat bounces off the ropes…. HE DROPS THE ELBOW! Copycat makes the cover! 1--------------------------2-----KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds got the shoulder up rather quickly after the two, while his back seems like it has taken a lot of punishment, Edmunds still has a lot of will power left and if Copycat wants to win, he is going to need to break Edmunds first.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat turns Edmunds over and locks in a surfboard! Copycat is lodging that knee in the lower back of Edmunds and he is pulling back on his arms with all the power that he has!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is really beginning to squirm. Copycat’s size really is an advantage in this situation.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is checking to see if Edmunds gives up but he keeps shaking his head no. Copycat is beginning to grow frustrated.
VIC WATERS: Edmunds has wits of steel to resist all of this pain. BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT BREAKS THE HOLD! Copycat stands over Edmunds who is face first down on the mat and Copycat pulls his head up…. HE NAILS HIM WITH A FOREARM SHOT TO THE SIDE OF THE HEAD!
VIC WATERS: That is an old school football tactic, but hitting the guy on the side of the head, it leaves a ringing in your ear.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is moving off Edmunds and he is taunting him to get up and give him a fight.
VIC WATERS: Copycat is torturing Edmund tonight. He has so much venom in his eye tonight. I’m really starting to like him.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds uses the ropes and he is trying to get back up to his feet… HE FINALLY BACK UP! EDMUNDS TAKES A SHOT AT COPYCAT BUT COPYCAT DUCKS AND EDMUNDS WOBBLES ON HIS FEET!
VIC WATERS: Copycat is just toying with him like a ball of yarn. BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT GOES FOR A CLOTHESLINE BUT EDMUNDS DUCKS! Edmunds leg scissors the left arm of Copycat and brings him down to the mat with a crucifix! The ref goes down for the count! 1-----------------------2------------KICKOUT! That totally took Copycat by surprise!
VIC WATERS: Copycat may have been a bit over confidence and he wasn’t expecting Edmunds to comeback like that.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat gets up…. Edmunds bounces off the ropes…. COPYCAT DUCKS…. EDMUNDS TAKES COPYCAT OVER WITH A SUNSET FLIP! 1---------------------2-------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Copycat is really looking angry now. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat gets up and charges Edmunds… HURRICANRANA BY EDMUNDS! 1-------------------2---------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds looked like he hurt his back on that one. BRANDON JACOBS: The motion of the hurricanrana can really do a number of your lower back, but Edmunds knew that Copycat has been dominating so far so he had to do everything in his power to get a quick pinfall victory when Copycat wasn’t expecting it.
VIC WATERS: Both men are down! BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is putting a double count on both men! 1---2----3---4---5----6---- COPYCAT IS THE FIRST MAN TO HIS FEET! Copycat picks Edmunds off the mat by the hair…. He locks on the waistlock…. RELEASE GERMAN SUPLEX RIGHT INTO THE CENTER OF THE RING!
VIC WATERS: If Copycat wants to win this, he needs to stop wasting time and pin him already when his back is still raw from all the punishment he has taken.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat seems to be taking your advice because he is now dragging Edmunds towards the corner. Copycat turns Edmunds over onto his back and laughs. Copycat points to the ropes and he is headed for the top.
VIC WATERS: Looks like he is going to go for the frog splash… if he hits this, Edmunds will be done for.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is climbing the ropes…. WAIT A SECOND, SHERRI HAS GRABBED THE LEG OF COPYCAT! COPYCAT IS TRYING TO SHAKE SHERRI OFF!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds is still out of it though, all of this assistances by Sherri is futile.
BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT SHAKES SHERRI OFF HIS LEG AND HE REACHES THE TOP! He perches…. HE FLIES OFF….. EDMUNDS MOVES OUT OF THE WAY OF THE FROG SPLASH AND COPYCAT’S RIBS CRASH DOWN HARD INTO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: I stand corrected, Sherri was able to buy Edmunds enough time to role out of the way. She did a good job there even if she couldn’t stop Copycat from doing the Frog Splash.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is slowly getting up… he pulls up Copycat…. STANDING DROPKICK RIGHT INTO THE RIBS! Copycat is gasping for air here!
VIC WATERS: All of these moves are going to make it easier for Edmunds put Copycat in the Northern Lights.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is slowly trying to get up… Edmunds is heading for the ropes…. He climbs up…. REVERSE CROSSBODY BLOCK ONTO THE RIBS OF COPYCAT! Edmunds makes the cover! 1-----------------------------2---------------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Copycat barely kicked out there and boy does he looked winded. You have to wonder if Edmunds didn’t take half that punishment in the beginning in order to tire Copycat out.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds puts on the bearhug on Copycat in the center of the ring! Copycat is reaching up and he is screaming in pain!
VIC WATERS: If Edmunds holds this on, he might be able to get the submission because Copycat is reacting like a man in a lot of pain.
BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is struggling to break free to no avail. You can see Edmunds is tapping all the strength that he has to take Copycat out of commission here.
VIC WATERS: Copycat looks like he is on the verge of passing out. BRANDON JACOBS: The ref takes the hand of Copycat…. IT FALLS ONCE! The ref lifts up the hand off Copycat a second time…. IT FALLS TWICE! The ref picks up the hand of Copycat a third time…. WAIT! EDMUNDS HAS LET GO OF COPYCAT!
VIC WATERS: What… WHAT IS EDMUNDS DOING? BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is calling for the Northern Lights, he wants to beat Copycat with his own finisher.
VIC WATERS: It takes balls for Edmunds to try this. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds locks him in a waistlock…. HE NAILS THE NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX! THE REF GOES DOWN TO MAKE THE COUNT! 1-------------------------2---------- THE REF STOPS THE COUNT! Copycat’s leg was under the ropes.
VIC WATERS: Edmunds looked like he had enough room to do that move, but Copycat is so damn tall that the end of his foot was just under the bottom rope.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is grabbing his back and yelling at the referee but the referee is pleading his case to him. Edmunds grabs Copycat and pulls him to the center of the ring. HE IS CALLING FOR IT A SECOND TIME!
VIC WATERS: This time, there is no way that he can possibly get his foot under the rope again.
BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds puts on a waistlock… HE GOES FOR IT A SECOND TIME…. EDMUNDS BACK HAS GONE OUT FROM ALL OF THE WORK ON IT EARLIER AND COPYCAT LANDS ON EDMUNDS! The ref goes down to make the count! 1-----------------------------2---------------------------KICKOUT! Edmunds somehow found a way to kickout!
VIC WATERS: What a rotten time for Edmund’s back to give out. He had Copycat dead if he could have completed that move.
BRANDON JACOBS: Both men are down on the ground in pain! The ref is putting on the double count! 1----2----3----4-----5-----6----7---8--- COPYCAT MANAGES TO MAKE IT TO HIS FEET!
VIC WATERS: Copycat has been granted a second life here. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat is calling for the Kit Kat Kick and he is waiting for Edmunds to get up….. Edmunds face looks like he is in such pain, if he gets hit with this, it is all over.
VIC WATERS: COME ON EDMUNDS! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is starting to get up…. HE DOESN’T SEE COPYCAT! Copycat is all set for the kick…. EDMUNDS TURNS AROUND AND HE GETS NAILED WITH THE KIT KAT KICK…. OH MY! Edmunds falls and stumbles through the ropes down to the floor!
VIC WATERS: That may have saved him from getting pinned. BRANDON JACOBS: Copycat goes through the ropes to the outside and Edmunds is trying to crawl away…. SHERRI JUMPS ONTO THE BACK OF COPYCAT AND COPYCAT IS TRYING TO WRESTLE HER OFF! SHERRI RAKES COPYCAT’S EYES WITH HER SHARP NAILS AND COPYCAT FALLS DOWN TO THE GROUND!
VIC WATERS: Thank god for Sherri. RING ANNOUNCER: FIVE MINUTES REMAINING IN THE THIRTY MINUTE MINUTE! FIVE MINUTES! BRANDON JACOBS: Sherri is attending to Edmunds but Edmunds looks in bad shape. Sherri is handing Edmunds her purse! COME ON REF! STEP IN HERE!
VIC WATERS: It looks like the ref didn’t see it! BRANDON JACOBS: An angry Copycat picks up the stairs and throws it down in Sherri’s general direction and Sherri bails out of the way. COPYCAT PICKS UP EDMUNDS AND IRISH WHIPS HIM BACK FIRST INTO THE GUARDRAIL!
VIC WATERS: Edmunds still managed to hold on to the purse for dear life but his back took a real toll on that guardrail. If he takes a couple more shots like that, the ref is going to have to stop this match.
BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT CHARGES EDMUNDS WITH A CLOTHESLINE BUT EDMUNDS BLASTS HIM IN THE HEAD WITH THE PURSE! Both men are down!
VIC WATERS: Here comes the ref! HE IS FINALLY TELLING BOTH MEN TO GET BACK INTO THE RING! BRANDON JACOBS: The Wylde appointed referee didn’t apparently see the purse shot on Copycat earlier.
VIC WATERS: Referees can’t see everything and sometimes anything. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds rolls on top of Copycat and starts to choke him out with the purse! The ref is trying to pull the purse away… WAIT A SECOND, HERE COMES ICEKOLD WITH A CHAIR! The referee finally pulls it away from Edmunds BUT HE FALLS BACK AND HITS HIS HEAD ON THE STAIRS!
VIC WATERS: This can’t be good. BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds picks up the purse and looks back at Copycat…. ICEKOLD BLASTS EDMUNDS IN THE BACK WITH THE CHAIR! Edmunds goes down!
VIC WATERS: Copycat told her not to come down. BRANDON JACOBS: It looks like Copycat is yelling at her to stay out of her business…. WAIT A SECOND, HE COMES SHERRI FROM BEHIND AND SHE ATTACKS ICEKOLD WITH HER HEEL!
VIC WATERS: Sherri is a vicious, vicious woman, how I love her. RING ANNOUNCER: ONE MINUTE REMAINING! ONE MINUTE! BRANDON JACOBS: Sherri and Icekold are brawling and Sherri is driving Icekold away from ringside. Edmunds is desperately trying to wake up the referee. HERE COMES THE SECOND REFEREE!
VIC WATERS: Time is ticking! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds picks up Copycat by the hair and tries to roll him into the ring…. COPYCAT ASSAILS EDMUNDS AND LOCKS IN THE CAT’S CLAW!
VIC WATERS: The ref is putting the double count on these two men! REF: ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is fading fast, Copycat has this hooked on tight! REF: SIX! SEVEN! EIGHT! NINE! TEN! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds looks like he is about to pass out. Copycat better watch the count or he will wind up being counted out in the process.
REF: ELEVEN! TWELVE! THIRTEEN! FOURTEEN! FIFTEEN! BRANDON JACOBS: Edmunds is out of it and Copycat breaks the hold! HE HAS THE MATCH WON IF HE CAN JUST RE-ENTER THE RING!
VIC WATERS: LOOK! BRANDON JACOBS: OH NO! SHERRI IS PULLING ON COPYCAT’S FOOT AND THE REF DOESN’T SEE IT! REF: SIXTEEN! SEVENTEEN! EIGHTEEN! BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT IS TRYING TO FREE HIMSELF BUT SHERRI IS HANGING ON FOR ALL OF HER LIFE! Wait a second… EDMUNDS REACHES UP AND GRABS THE OTHER LEG OF COPYCAT…. EDMUNDS PULLS COPYCAT DOWN AND HE FALLS FACE FIRST ONTO THE MAT!
REF: NINETEEN! TWENTY! ::calls for the bell:: RING ANNOUNCER: The referee has declared this match a double count out, the official time of the match is 29:57 seconds!
BRANDON JACOBS: Man, what a war between these two, it made Red Sox/Yankees look like a day in the park. These two have a lot of issue to settle and undoubtedly these two will face again, most likely at Ghoulish Games to solve their problems once and for all.
VIC WATERS: That was a hell of a match. BRANDON JACOBS: COPYCAT IS IRRATE AND HE WANTS A PIECE OF EDMUNDS! The referees from the back are filing out and they are trying to keep these two men separated!
VIC WATERS: I’ve never seen Copycat this angry before. I pray to for Edmunds’ soul. BRANDON JACOBS: Okay folks, hopefully by the time we comeback, these two will be separated, our next match up is Wylde vs. Diablo in a soap box vs. flaming table match. STAY TUNED!
