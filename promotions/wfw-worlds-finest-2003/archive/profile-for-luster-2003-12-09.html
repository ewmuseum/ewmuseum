PaulNJ21 — 9 December 2003 Wrestler Name: Luster Height: 6'6" Weight: 250 Hailing From: Atlanta, GA Handedness: Right Gimmick: Luster is an usual character. He is from Atlanta, GA which is known for it's strip joints. Luster, of course, is exotic dancer turned wrestler. He decided to try wrestling to increase his fan base. He is almost poetic when he speaks. He has rhythm, he has grace, and all the ladies love him.
Ring Attire: Typically he is wearing spandex as his normal everyday attire. In the ring he is swinging in his silver jogging suit and silver trunks that seem to highlight his package.
Looks: Very long brown hair and very curly. He is very good looking. Very tan. Ring Entrance: His entrance is very dramatic. He music starts and struts to the ring. Disco ball and lights a must.
Theme Music: "Funkytown"-Disco Fever Tactics/Style: Techinical. Very dirty wrestler. 15 Moves: Back Rake Eye Rake Capture Suplex Low Blow Missle Dropkick Spear Dangerous DDT Shuffle Side Kick Curtain Call Spine Buster Elbow Drop Sharpshooter STO Clothesline Submission/Weardown holds: Sleeper Hold Ankle Lock Finisher: Funk And Wagnell. Powerbomb followed by Luster Strengths: 1) Lots of Stamina 2) Very Agile Weaknesses: 1) Distracted by Ladies in the audience. Regularism for Life.
