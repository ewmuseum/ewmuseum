WFW — Card

Cherry Blossom Chaos - Doc vs. Hart vs. Psycho vs Gid.

3 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 3 June 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: Every so often, wrestling has a magic moment which people remember long after it happens. I have a feeling tonight will be the beginning of something big. I don’t know what, I don’t know how, but I have that feeling something big is going to happen here tonight in this main event. Doc may duck and choose and he may have had trouble with this young upstart Gideon who is breaking through, but has been a wonderful first champion for the WFW. He may be a bastard, but he is our bastard. I think tonight right here on PPV that we will have a title change, his opponents are three of the best that WFW has to offer and anyone of them could win this match. Sometimes when you get involved in wrestling, even though you have your favorites, when you see people work so hard, you hate to see any of them lose.
VIC WATERS: Every wrestler, if they are talented and work hard enough will have their day eventually, sadly for Hart, Gideon, and Psycho, it is not tonight tonight. DOC IS GOING TO WIN BABY!
BRANDON JACOBS: Doc has some tremendous obstacles he has to overcome to win this match. He is a lying, cheating bastard who will do anything he can to survive and in the World Tournament when Silver pinned Hart to win the title, he showed the world that he knows what to do to get to the top. But the question is, can Doc Silver beat this young upstart Gideon who has been giving him all this trouble? Can Doc Silver beat Psycho who is one of the best brawlers on the planet right now in a Falls Count Anywhere match? And last but not least, Hart has been on a tear as of late and he is looking like he will be the next guy to break through to the next level in this sport. He is one of the cornerstones of this company. Silver may have lucked out the first time against Hart, but can he avoid the much younger and better working star who may take his spot? It isn’t going to be easy for Doc Vic.
VIC WATERS: I’ll believe in Doc until the bitter end. BRANDON JACOBS: Everyone here in Washington, DC is up and on their feet. They know that this Falls Count Anywhere in the Mall will go anywhere and everywhere. Let’s send this one to ringside and get this match started!
::Remember by Disturbed wails over the loud speakers:: RING ANNOUCER: Introducing first, from Buffalo, NY, THE SYSTEM RICHARD GIDEON! ::Gideon comes down to the ring to the roar of the crowd and enters the ring:: :: "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne blares over the loud speaker:: RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing second, one of the most hardcore men on the planet, from parts Unknown, “THE AMERICAN DANGERMAN” PSYCHO!
:: Psycho walks down to the ring to pop from the crowd and enters the ring:: :: I Touch Myself" by the Divinyls plays over the loud speakers:: RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from Orlando, Florida! The man who proclaims that he is the real World Heavyweight Champion of the World, “The Phenom” Shawn Hart!
::Shawn Hart walks down to the ring without his bodyguard Tiny:: ::Only Happy When It Rains by Garbage wails over the loud speaker:: RING ANNOUNCER: Last but not least, the current WFW World Heavyweight Champion, from Orlando, Florida, one of the greatest legends and a 4 time AAWC World Champion, DOC SILVER!
:: Doc Silver walks out to the loudest heel heat of the night and enters the ring:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: All four men are in their corners waiting for the first man to make their move. You can feel the level of anticipation by this crowd for this match.
VIC WATERS: This crowd realizes that they are witnessing history here tonight. This PPV will be remembered as the first WFW PPPV of many and the man who wins this match will become part of history.
BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart walks out to the center of the ring and he offers his hand to Doc Silver. He is telling Doc that they should team up against Gideon & Psycho like they did in the past. Doc looks a little bit weary of Hart but he shakes his hand!
VIC WATERS: Just because you are battling for a title doesn’t mean that you should let it get in the middle of a beautiful friendship.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart grabs Doc Silver’s arm… SHORT CLOTHESLINE! PSYCHO, GIDEON, and SHAWN HART START STOMPING ON THE AMBUSED DOC SILVER!
VIC WATERS: That traitorous bastard! BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart picks Doc Silver off the mat…. EL CODO EXPLOSIVO TO THE FACE! Doc goes down! Hart makes the cover! 1---------------2------------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: This is definitely not good. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho lifts Doc off the mat… HE NAILS HIM WITH THE TIGER DRIVER! He covers Doc! 1-----------2----------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: What is this?! PICK ON DOC DAY?! BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is saying it his turn next! HE BODYSLAMS DOC! Gideon goes up to the top rope as Psycho & Gideon are centering Doc in the middle of the ring. HE NAILS HIM WITH THE SYSTEM FAILURE 450 INTO A FROG SPLASH! Gideon covers! 1----------------2------------------ 2/3 KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Stop this Hart! Come to your damn senses! BRANDON JACOBS: All three men are standing around wondering what to do next. Shawn Hart walks around to the corner and thinks… HE HAS AN IDEA! SHAWN HART GRABS THE HEAD OF GIDEON & PSYCHO AND RAMS THEM TOGETHER!
VIC WATERS: Thank god he has come to his senses! BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart is helping Doc up and telling them that they were the ones who conned him into doing the triple team! Doc looks at both of them with an appalling look! Gideon is blaming Psycho for his actions and Psycho is blaming Hart! DOC KICKS HART IN THE STOMACH AND NAILS HIM THE ACES FULL! DOC THROWS HART OVER THE TOP ROPE!
VIC WATERS: I see a fire in Doc’s eyes! BRANDON JACOBS: Doc tries to leave the ring but Psycho & Gideon attack from behind! Gideon holds Doc Silver’s arms back… YAZUKA KICK BY PSYCHO!
VIC WATERS: Doc got hit flush in the jaw by that! BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon grabs the head and hooks the right leg…. fisherman suplex! The ref goes down to make the count! 1--------------2--------------KICKOUT! Gideon goes to pick up Doc off the mat… SMALL PACKAGE BY DOC! 1----------------2------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: This is what Doc has to do in order to survive the match for any length of time. He has to take advantage of each every mistake.
BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO CLIPS DOC’S LEG FROM BEHIND AS HE GETS UP! Doc is grabbing his knee, he doesn’t look in good shape.
VIC WATERS: Hart needs to wake up and save Doc here from these two mad men. There is no telling what they will do if left to their own devices.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon waits for Doc to slowly get up and HE LOCKS ON A WAISTLOCK…. GIDEON CLUTCH (GERMAN SUPLEX INTO A LEGLOCK)! Doc is in a lot of pain! HART IS GETTING UP AND HE ROLLS INTO THE RING!
VIC WATERS: Finally, someone is going to help Doc! BRANDON JACOBS: Shawn Hart sees Doc Silver locked in the figure four…. SHAWN HART BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES WHILE GIDEON HAS THE LEGLOCK IN AND HE NAILS DOC SILVER WITH A LEG DROP TO THE HEAD! The ref is checking on Doc… DOC IS OUT COLD AND HE IS CALLING FOR THE BELL!
RING ANNOUNCER: DOC SILVER HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart is going to pay for what he did, if not tonight, sometime soon. Count on it. Doc doesn’t take well to losing.
BRANDON JACOBS: When Doc lost, this peaceful feeling has just passed over me. VIC WATERS: Yeah, that is because you are a Manson mark. BRANDON JACOBS: We are down to three men and it doesn’t look good for Shawn Hart. It is going to be a two on one for the rest of the match.
VIC WATERS: In the heat of the battle, don’t count on any friendship remaining intact when there is a title on the line.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho & Gideon double clothesline Shawn Hart out of the ring and onto the floor! Looks like the brawling across the mall is ready to begin!
VIC WATERS: Shawn Hart needs to find a way to eliminate one of these men and turn it into a wrestling match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon throws Hart hard into the barricade! Hart’s back went into hard into the steel guardrail at ringside. Psycho is undoing the barricade at ringside, looks like the ringside area just got a whole lot bigger.
VIC WATERS: The Mall is huge, these three guy could brawl for hours out here and never hit the same place twice.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is dragging Shawn Hart through the crowd into an open area before you cross the street to get to the Reflecting pool. PSYCHO PICKS HART UP AND NAILS HIM WITH A SHOULDER BREAKER!
VIC WATERS: Gideon is trying to fight off the autograph seekers in order to get where Psycho is.
BRANDON JACOBS: PSCYHO GRABS THE ARM OF SHAWN HART AND LOCS ON THE FUJIWARA ARMBAR! Hart is screaming in pain!
VIC WATERS: I have no idea how Shawn Hart is going to be able to break out of this one! There isn’t any ropes to grab or anything! If Psycho wanted, he could hold onto this until his arm ripped off!
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is using his hands and he grabs a handful of dirt and throws it in the face of Psycho! PSCYHO GETS SOME DIRT IN HIS EYES AND BREAKS THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: What resourcefulness by Hart! I would have never thought of that. BRANDON JACOBS: GIDEON CHARGES HART… HART DUCKS DOWN AND GIVES GIDEON A BACK DROP! Hart locks on the waistlock… HE GERMAN RELEASES SUPLEXES GIDEON INTO THE BLOCKED OFF STREET! Gideon is grabbing the back of his head! He really took a shot there!
VIC WATERS: Psycho has finally cleared the debris from his eyes and he is coming to help Gideon out.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart and Psycho start trading blows left and right! Psycho is gaining the advantage… LOW BLOW BY SHAWN HART!
VIC WATERS: When all else fails and the rules are waived, go for the long blow. It is the great equalizer of man. Big or small, they will all fall.
BRANDON JACOBS: SHAWN HART NAILS PSYCHO WITH A DISCUS PUNCH! Psycho is staggering… HART NAILS HIM ANOTHER AND PSYCHO GOES DOWN! Shawn Hart picks him up…. INVERTED ATOMIC DROP!
VIC WATERS: Psycho looks like he is in a lot of pain. BRANDON JACOBS: HART GOES FOR A ROLL UP! 1--------------------2-------------KICKOUT! Not much authority on that one though… A BLEEDING GIDEON COMES OVER AND DROP KICKS SHAWN HART IN THE BACK! Shawn Hart goes face first into the ground!
VIC WATERS: With the way Shawn Hart keeps himself immaculately groomed, its weird to see him covered with dirt and grime.
BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon grabs his head and rubs it into the ground! He is getting all that dirt and gravel into the face of Hart! Psycho and Gideon grab Hart by the hair and they are dragging him into the street.
VIC WATERS: There are police cars and barricades blocking off the road to keep the traffic from coming though so they have the whole entire street to beat the hell out of each other.
BRANDON JACOBS: PSYCHO RAMS SHAWN HART INTO HEADLIGHT OF THE POLICE CAR! Shawn Hart is busted open!
VIC WATERS: That was a vicious, vicious shot by Psycho. Shawn Hart might be better off taking the pin that continuing to take all this abuse.
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho goes for the cover! 1-------------2-------------- KICKOUT! The dazed Hart seems to be going on instinct! Psycho is calling over Gideon to help and Gideon grabs the arms of Hart. PSYCHO GRABS A GARBAGE CAN FROM THE SIDE OF THE ROAD AND GOES TO RAM IT INTO HART’S HEAD…. HART FALLS DOWN AND GIDEON IS CLOCKED!
VIC WATERS: Psycho really hit him hard with that! BRANDON JACOBS: Gideon is really upset… HE GOES TO CLOTHESLINE PSYCHO BUT PSYCHO DUCKS! Psycho knees him in the midsection…. HE SENDS GIDEON CRASHING DOWN ONTO THE STREET WITH A TIGER DRIVER! Poor Gideon! We need to get some medical attention out there because that was one of the nastiest bumps I’ve ever seen in my life.
VIC WATERS: HART! HE HAS THE TRASH CAN! BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho turns around…. HART HITS HIM WITH THE TRASH CAN AND PSYCHO GOES DOWN! Wait, he is getting back up…. HART DELIVERS A DDT!
VIC WATERS: Hart has retaken control of the match, there has been a lot of battling back and forth but that just shows how evenly everyone in this match is.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart pulls Psycho onto the hood of the car and lays the haymakers right into to Psycho’s face. HART KNEES PSYCHO IN THE STOMACH AND LIFTS HIM UP FOR A PILEDRIVER…. HE NAILS IT! Hart makes a cover! 1-------------------2------------PSYCHO GETS HIS SHOULDER UP BEFORE THE THREE! Hart pulls Psycho off the hood of the car and onto the other side of the street. WAIT A SECOND, RICHARD GIDEON JUST THREW A CUP OF COKE AT HART!
VIC WATERS: Why is Gideon trying to get Hart’s attention when he is still hurt from the Tiger Driver? He should have sat their and have been quiet and Hart could have let him live.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart is coming for Gideon and he is crawling to the side of the street with the Reflecting Pool! HART GRABS HIM BY THE TIGHTS AND FINALLY CATCHES HIM! Hart pulls Gideon up and starts laying into him with haymakers! Gideon is being driven back closer and closer to the pool!
VIC WATERS: It isn’t like Gideon will drown because the Reflecting Pool is only one and a half feet deep. But when you are wrestling, getting wet doesn’t help your grip when you have to do moves and the chlorine can really burn if you have an open cut from wrestling a wild brawl like this one. Gideon is way too close to the edge!
BRANDON JACOBS: SHAWN HART SUPERKICKS GIDEON INTO THE REFLECTING POOL! Gideon is wailing and trying to get up! Shawn Hart is going into the water and he pulls Gideon out of the pool by his hair!
VIC WATERS: Gideon looks like he swallowed a little bit over water there when he fell in because he was really coughing.
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart drags him over to the grass… HE NAILS HIM WITH A HART ATTACK! Hart covers! 1------------------2---------------3!! GIDEON MANAGED TO KICKOUT AFTER THE THREE BUT IT WAS MUCH TOO LATE!
RING ANNOUNCER: “The System” Richard Gideon has been eliminated by the match! BRANDON JACOBS: We are down to two men for all the marbles, either Shawn Hart or Psycho will walk out of this match WFW World Heavyweight Champion!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is up and he is heading towards the Lincoln Memorial. Hart is running after him from behind…. HE TACKLES HIM!
BRANDON JACOBS: Hart grabs Psycho and he throws him into the first level of steps! HART GRABS AN UMBRELLA FROM A LITTLE OLD LADY AND USES IT TO START CHOKING PSYCHO!
VIC WATERS: No rain today, but with the weather as bad it has been lately, it pays to come prepared. GO HART!
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is using all his strength to prevent Hart from crushing his throat with the umbrella… PSYCHO KICKS HART AND HE FALLS BACKWARD ON TO HIS BACKSIDE! Psycho is trying to escape by climbing up higher!
VIC WATERS: Psycho should be taking it too Hart instead of running away. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is just trying to get higher ground where he can fight without having to worry about being so close to the steps! Hart is climbing back up and he reaches the first platform with Psycho! LOOKS LIKE WE ARE GOING TO GET OUR SHOWDOWN!
VIC WATERS: This should be fun, GO HART! BRANDON JACOBS: HART CHARGES PSYCHO AND PSYCHO TAKES HART DOWN WITH A HURRICANRANA! Hart gets up again… ANOTHER HURRICANRANA AND PSYCHO SENDS HART INTO THE STEPS! Hart looks scared like he has lost the momentum in this match. Hart is climbing up second layer of stairs to get away from Psycho!
VIC WATERS: Hart is just trying to wear out Psycho, isn’t that the excuse that you made for your boy?
BRANDON JACOBS: These two men are so evenly balanced, it will take a major mistake for either man to lose this match. PSYCHO GRABS THE LEG OF HART BEFORE HE CAN GO ANY FARTHER! Hart falls down and crashes face first into the steps! It looks like he may have busted open his nose!
VIC WATERS: Hart didn’t get his hands all the way down and that cost him. BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho grabs the arms of Shawn Hart…. IT LOOKS LIKE HE IS GOING TO HIT HIM WITH THE TIGER DRIVER AND IF HE DOES, IT IS GOING TO BE OVER! Psycho locks the arms… BACK BODY DROP BY HART AND PSYCHO FALLS BACK FIRST INTO THE STEPS! Psycho is reaching back and grabbing his back. If Hart has any chance of winning this match, he has to win it now!
VIC WATERS: LETS GO HART! BRANDON JACOBS: Hart picks up injured Psycho…. TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER! Hart looks down at Psycho but then looks up as he sees something on the stairs… ROPE BARRIER POLE!
VIC WATERS: DO IT HART! END PSYCHO CAREER ONCE AND FOR ALL! Hart is going to do it! BRANDON JACOBS: Hart ascends the stair case and grabs the poll! HART HOLDS UP THE POLE FOR EVERYONE TO SEE! The fans are booing Hart!
VIC WATERS: Psycho is a pest. This needs to be done for the sake of humanity. BRANDON JACOBS: WAIT A SECOND, DOC SILVER COMES FROM BEHIND SHAWN HART AND NAILS HIM WITH A STEEL CHAIR ACROSS BACK!
VIC WATERS: Doc has gotten his revenge! I told you so Brandon, I told you so. BRANDON JACOBS: SHAWN HART ROLLS DOWN THE STEPS TO THE BOTTOM! Psycho has been out of it, he didn’t see anything that happened… HE PICKS SHAWN HART AND NAILS HIM WITH A TIGER DRIVER! He covers! 1-------------------2--------------3!!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match… AND NEW WFW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, PSYCHO! ::We Are The Champions blares over the loud speakers as the fans surrounding the Lincoln Memorial repeatedly chant Psycho over and over again::
BRANDON JACOBS: Poor Shawn Hart, he was this close to beating Psycho and if it wasn’t for that damn bastard Doc Silver. He would have finally won the WFW World Heavyweight Title.
VIC WATERS: Here is the bigger question, did Psycho know about Doc Silver being in wait at the Lincoln Memorial? Was this a setup in case Doc didn’t win the title?
BRANDON JACOBS: Psycho is of higher morals than Doc. He would never agree to something like that.
VIC WATERS: Some men will do anything to win the World Title. Did Psycho join the likes of Scotty Michaels and Rat Fink tonight?
BRANDON JACOBS: The fans here are lifting the injured and bloody Psycho on their shoulders and are carrying him down. These fans love Psycho because when he goes out there, he wears his heart on his sleeve and he shows everyone that hard work does pay off.
VIC WATERS: Here comes the paramedics to tend to Shawn Hart. BRANDON JACOBS: Diablo comes rushing to Shawn Hart’s side and Shawn Hart is saying to get the damn paramedics away from him. Diablo brushes them all aside and he helps Shawn Hart down the stairs as the fans gathered here give Hart a standing ovation for his effort. They may not agree with C.O.C.K.S. actions earlier on the show against McMillan, but there is an even greater hatred for the manipulative Doc Silver who has conducted his reign of terror in order to maintain his power.
VIC WATERS: Doc is a saint, he is just misunderstood. BRANDON JACOBS: That is like saying Hitler was a little bit evil. ::as Hart and Diablo pass by, Jacobs gets up and gives them a round of applause::
VIC WATERS: You sentimental bastard you… BRANDON JACOBS: Just you wait Vic, Shawn Hart is going to get that title and Doc Silver yet and you’ll be out in the cold.
VIC WATERS: I’m going to go check to see how Doc is doing. You wrap up the show. BRANDON JACOBS: You go and kiss Doc’s ass some more. I can take care of the show by myself. I’d like to thank everyone here and watching at home for watching our very first PPV. Our next show will be Rage in the Cage featuring Psycho putting his title on the line against Doc Silver inside of a steel cage the mandatory title rematch and Shawn Hart will be facing Richard Gideon for the first title shot at the newly crowed champion on our next PPV. Goodnight everyone and God bless the WFW!
