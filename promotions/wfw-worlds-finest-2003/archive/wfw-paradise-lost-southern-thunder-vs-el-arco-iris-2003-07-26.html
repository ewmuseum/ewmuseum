WFW Paradise Lost: Southern Thunder vs. El Arco Iris

26 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 26 July 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back with first match of the night that which we be aired for the viewers at home and it should be tremendous. Southern Thunder is going to go face to face with El Arco Iris.
VIC WATERS: This El Arco Iris seems very chipper. I hate him already. BRANDON JACOBS: I don’t know, if we had more people like El Arco Iris, this world would be a much better place. He almost reminds me of a young child in spirit.
VIC WATERS: Well tonight, Southern Thunder is going to be a child abuser and he is going to teach Iris the meaning of pain.
BRANDON JACOBS: This has been quite the battle of words, but it is time for these two to face off in the ring, let’s send this match down to ringside.
(CUEUP: “Superheros” by Daft Punk) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first from the end of rainbow, weighing in at 207 pounds, EL ARCO IRIS!
::the masked man with a peace sign on his mask makes his way down to ringside to the cheers of the crowd and enters the ring::
(CUEUP: “Turn the Page” by Metallica) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, from parts unknown, weighing in at 245 pounds and standing at over 6’4, SOUTHERN THUNDER!
::the masked man slowly makes his way down to the ring to a mixed reaction from the crowd and enters the ring::
::bell rings:: BRANDON JACOBS: Southern Thunder comes swinging with lefts and rights and backs Arco Iris into the corner! SHOULDER BLOCK INTO THE CHEST OF IRIS! ANOTHER!
VIC WATERS: Southern Thunder is trying to wind his opponent to take away his energy. It is a smart game plan by Thunder.
BRANDON JACOBS: Southern Thunder irish whips Arco Iris into the other corner! Southern Thunder comes in with a splash…. ARCO IRIS MOVES OUT OF THE CORNER AND SOUTHERN THUNDER CRASHES INTO THE TURNBUCKLE! Arco Iris comes up from behind Southern Thunder… GERMAN SUPLEX! Southern Thunder landed really hard on his head!
VIC WATERS: Southern Thunder’s eyes are starting to look a bit glazer, but he trying to get back up.
BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris grabs Southern Thunder in a front face lock and jumps on the ropes… TORNADO DDT! El Arco Iris makes the cover! 1--------------2----------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Very close there! Southern Thunder just got the shoulder up! BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris picks up Southern Thunder… NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX! The ref goes down to make the count! 1---------------2------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: This crowd is really getting behind all the near falls on Southern Thunder. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris picks Southern Thunder up… BIG BODYSLAM! Iris is signal to the fans that he is going to go up top!
VIC WATERS: Southern Thunder really needs to get out of the way here or he will be done. The energy of this rookie is incredible!
BRANDON JACOBS: Iris perches from the top… MOONSAULT BUT SOUTHERN THUNDER MOVES OUT OF THE WAY! Southern Thunder gets up… El Arco Iris is getting up slowly… THUNDER BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND NAILS IRIS WITH A BULLDOG! Iris is grabbing his head!
VIC WATERS: Iris head hit the mat flush on that shot. I’m shocked he isn’t totally out. BRANDON JACOBS: Southern Thunder is calling for a spear… and this crowd is booing him for manhandling this energetic newcomer! Southern Thunder picks Iris up off the mat by his hair…. HE WHIPS HIM OFF THE ROPES AND HE NAILS HIM WITH A SPEAR! Southern Thunder covers! 1----------------2-------------- IRIS GETS HIS FOOT ON THE ROPES!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris looks out of it, it is only a matter of time now unless Iris can pull something from his hat.
BRANDON JACOBS: Southern Thunder is growing frustrated… HE IS CALLING FOR THE SPEAR AGAIN! Southern Thunder picks Iris off the mat…. EL ARCO IRIS WHIPS SOUTHER THUNDER OFF THE ROPES!
VIC WATERS: Iris caught him by surprise! BRANDON JACOBS: SOUTHERN THUNDER DUCKS THE FLYING FOREARM! Arco Iris recovers… Southern Thunder bounces off the ropes… HE NAILS EL ARCO IRIS WITH A FLYING CLOTHESLINE!
VIC WATERS: Oh my god, Iris is dead! BRANDON JACOBS: Southern Thunder makes the cover! 1-------------2-------------3! RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of this match…. SOUTHERN THUNDER! VIC WATERS: That was a hell of a match. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris showed a lot of heart but the big 6’4 Southern Thunder was just too much for him to handle tonight. That lariat by Southern Thunder was one of the best lariats I have ever seen. Southern Thunder waited for his opportunity and just took him out..
VIC WATERS: This Southern Thunder knows his wrestling and he is going to be a force to deal with for Copycat at the PPV. Copycat better watch Southern Thunder’s lariat or he could be looking up at the lights like Iris now.
BRANDON JACOBS: Okay folks, we are going to take a short commercial break, we will be back with FELIX RED vs. LUSTER ONE ON ONE!
