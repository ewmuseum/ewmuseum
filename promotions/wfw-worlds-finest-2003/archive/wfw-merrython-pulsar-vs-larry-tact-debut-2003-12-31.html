WFW Merrython: Pulsar vs. Larry Tact (debut)

31 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 31 December 2003 BRANDON JACOBS: We are back from commercial break and we are moments away from the debut of Larry Tact.
VIC WATERS: It seems as we get further and further along as we approach the one year mark, new talent is jumping on the WFW Bandwagon in order to try their skills against best and hardest working roster from top to bottom in all of wrestling.
BRANDON JACOBS: I’ve always enjoyed Larry Tact matches in the GLCW and IWF. Tact is one of the wrestlers who makes technical wrestling an art, which is a freshing change of pace in today’s wrestling world which relies so much on brawling and aerial moves.
VIC WATERS: You have to give the fans what they want since they pay the tickets. You have to get yourself noticed.
BRANDON JACOBS: You can do the same thing with technical wrestling, but the problem is so few do it well, that when these proletarians give it a try, they put everyone to sleep and they think mat wrestling is boring.
VIC WATERS: Sort of like your endless ranting? BRANDON JACOBS: *sighs* I’m trying my best, but my mind is still on a holiday schedule. If I begin ranting again, just slap me across the face.
VIC WATERS: That is probably why you’ve barely mentioned Pulsar who when it comes to upsets and doing the impossible, is king. Pulsar could go in there tonight and kick Tact’s ass and this whole lovefest will be for nothing.
BRANDON JACOBS: I’m not underestimating Pulsar. Pulsar is one of my favorite young wrestlers today. He reminds me of a young Pestilence from the early days of NGEN. That boy has a lot of spirit and is awfully tough. Tact is going to have to take it to him on the match which is what Tact does best if he wants to win this match.
VIC WATERS: This is the same man who took helped lead to the champ’s defeat on the six man on the last show. Pulsar is ready to break out and I think tonight is the night.
BRANDON JACOBS: This should be a good match to start off with, let’s send this match doing to ringside and let’s start the show.
(CUEUP: “Whiskey in A Jar” by Metallica) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from Australia, the King of the Six Man Tag, PULSAR! ::Pulsar comes out to a mixed reaction from the crowd and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: "Karma" by Diffuser) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, making his WFW Debut here tonight, in his hometown of Manhattan, New York… LARRY TACT!
::Larry Tact comes down to a huge ovation from the crowd, enters the ring and grabs the mic::
Tact: You know, when I decided to come here, I knew not to expect any 'easy' matches. And I still don't expect it; like I said, Pulsar, I expect your all tonight. But after preparing the past couple weeks, getting into this mindset and being in as good a condition as I am, I have no fear. Only focus. And after tonight, I'm going to focus more on what I touched on for a bit during this past week. That's looking around at this league, and making my mark where I see fit. I want to find new challenges here, and I mean to get the people who I feel are suitable challenges.
Tact pauses and walks to a corner, leaning back against it as he brings the mic back up. Tact: Now I know some people might find that an arrogant remark, so let me make my point on that. Honestly, I've got goals just like everyone else, and I don't care if it draws criticism or praise. I have a path and I have every intention of getting to the end of that path. In fact, right here and now, I'll say that at the next show....Sean Edmunds, if you want a 'warm-up' opponent for your title match, I'll personally volunteer to be your opponent. I know Shawn Hart, I've taken him down in the past. So provided you aren't concerned with taking a loss before your big title match...(grins slightly)...then you should step up to the plate.
Tact pauses and rubs his chin for a moment. Tact: Anarky...another intense competitor. I know you've had your dealings with Manson, and you might not be done, but hey...how many people are looking to face you? Call me crazy, maybe I am...but I'm not here to play with the small fish. Copycat! Shawn Hart! Psycho! DOC SILVER, if you make it back to these parts....any or all of you are welcome to take up this offer. Of course, I'm not going to name everyone, or there'd be no point. But I know there are others around WFW who would give me a good match. But these are the ones I'll start with. Arrogant? Sure, I could be. But I didn't come here to play around. I'm here to do what I enjoy most, and that's wrestle...improve...and succeed. And right now, I have a match to win.
::Tact throws down the mic:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: AN AGGRAVATED PULSAR CHARGES TACT AND HE NAILS HIM WITH A RUNNING SHOULDER BLOCK WHICH SENDS HIM REELING BACKWARDS INTO THE CORNER! Tact hits the back of his head on the turnbuckle!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar looks really aggressive here. He’d like nothing better than to knock Tact off in his own debut in his home town to prove a point.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar climbs up to the second turnbuckle and he starts pounding away at Tact’s head with closed fists as the crowd counts! ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! SIX! SEVEN! EIGHT! NINE! Pulsar shakes his finger and climbs down before the count of ten to the boos of the crowd.
VIC WATERS: It was the last number that the crowd knew. BRANDON JACOBS: A dazed Tact is slumped over in the corner. Pulsar pulls him out of the corner by the hair…. KNEE TO THE GUT BY PULSAR! Pulsar picks Tact up….. INVERTED SUPLEX! Pulsar goes for the cover! 1-----------------------------2-------------------KICKOUT! Tact gets the shoulder up!
VIC WATERS: By charging Tact at the get go, he ambushed him and kept Tact from setting the tone of the match.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar goes to pickup Tact....TACT LOCKS ON A LEG SCISSORS ON THE HEAD OF PULSAR! Pulsar is trying to pry Tact off!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar has never encountered like this before! BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR GRABS THE LEGS OF TACT AND HE TRIES TO SPIN TACT IN ORDER TO PRY HIM OFF!
VIC WATERS: Around and around Tact goes, where this stops, nobody knows! BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is getting tired…. TACT USES THE FORCE IN HIS LEGS TO TAKE PULSAR DOWN TO THE MAT! Pulsar is grabbing his head, the vice on his head appears to have tired him out.
VIC WATERS: The legs are the most powerful part of the body. That was a brilliant move by Tact.
BRANDON JACOBS: Larry Tact picks up Pulsar and locks in the abdominal stretch and Pulsar is trapped dead center in the middle of the ring.
VIC WATERS: When you work over someone’s abdominal region, it will help you if the match goes longer because it effects their ability to breath comfortably and if they are in discomfort breathing, they can’t pay attention to what is going on the ring.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is asking Pulsar if he wants to give up but he is shaking his head no. I really doubt that Tact will ever be able to get a submission on someone like Pulsar. This youngster doesn’t know the meaning of the word quit.
VIC WATERS: I bet he doesn’t know the meaning to a lot of words. BRANDON JACOBS: Don’t let Pulsar’s appearance fool you, he is an extremely bright young man who knows what he is doing in that ring. TACT PICKS PULSAR UP IN THE ABDOMINAL STRETCH AND SIDE SLAMS HIM DOWN TO THE MAT!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar took another shot to the back of the head on the side slam. BRANDON JACOBS: Larry Tact grabs the left leg of Pulsar and starts stomping the inner leg of Pulsar. He must be beginning to soften him up for the Skylock Inverted STF.
VIC WATERS: Pulsar still hasn’t regained his senses all the work Tact did on his head, if Tact can lock this on, he won’t be able to last as long as normal.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is clawing backwards trying to move to the ropes but Tact desperately trying to hold on to his leg. TACT PULLS HIM BACK TOWARDS THE CENTER OF THE RING! Tact drops a headbutt to the inner leg of Pulsar!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar looks like he is in a whole lot of pain. Tact drove his head in his head with a lot of force.
BRANDON JACOBS: Tact is calling for the Skylock Inverted STF… Tact steps between the legs of Pulsar…. HE SCISSORS THE LEG, APPLIES THE CROSSFACE AND TURNS HIM OVER! TACT HAS THE SKYLOCK INVERTED STF ON PULSAR! The ref is going down to the mat to check on Pulsar!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar looks like he is about to pass out he is in so much pain. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar keeps on shaking his head no repeatedly. Tact is giving it all that he has got! PULSAR IS REACHING FOR THE ROPES BUT HE IS A FEW FEET SHORT! Pulsar tries to use the strength in his hands to move him closer to the ropes…. PULSAR IS MAKING PROGRESS!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar isn’t a quitter. He’ll fight until his last breath if necessary. Tact has no idea what he is in for.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is getting closer… he extends his arm…. THE TIPS OF HIS FINGERS MAKE CONTACT WITH THE BOTTOM ROPE AND THE REF IS CALLING FOR A BREAK!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar got a lucky break there. BRANDON JACOBS: Tact breaks the hold cleanly and Pulsar is grabbing his right leg. Pulsar is trying to make it to his feet on his good leg. TACT RUSHES IN AND HE NAILS HIM WITH A RUSSIAN LEG SWEEP! Tact makes the cover! 1---------------------------2----------------------KICKOUT! Pulsar barely got the shoulder up!
VIC WATERS: Tact needs to stay on him and finish him off here. BRANDON JACOBS: Tact picks Pulsar off the mat…. INSIDE CRADLE BY TACT! 1-------------------------------2------------------------------PULSAR FINDS A WAY TO KICKOUT AGAIN!
VIC WATERS: Tact is starting to look like he is beginning to lose his cool here. BRANDON JACOBS: Tact is dragging Pulsar back to the center of the ring… Tact grabs the leg of Pulsar…. PULSAR KICKS TACT IN THE HEAD! Pulsar is crawling towards the outside for a breather!
VIC WATERS: If Pulsar makes it, the whole completion of this match could change. Tact needs to keep him in the ring at all costs!
BRANDON JACOBS: TACT DIVES ONTO PULSAR AND KEEPS HIM FROM ESCAPING THE RING! Tact gets up and he pulls Pulsar back towards the center by both legs…. TACT WAISTLOCKS PULSAR AND IS GOING FOR A GERMAN SUPLEX…. PULSAR ELBOWS TACT IN THE JAW TO BREAK IT UP!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar finally has some life again. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar catches Tact stunned…. PULSAR IS GOING FOR THE BLACK HOLE (ROLL OF THE DICE)! Pulsar has on the front face lock…. TACT HAS A HANDFUL OF THE TIGHTS OF PULSAR! Pulsar breaks the front face lock… AND CLOTHESLINES TACT DOWN FROM BEHIND!
VIC WATERS: Tact was smart enough to grab the tights because if Pulsar nailed the Black Hole, this match is over because nobody kicks out of the Black Hole. He must have been studying his tapes.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar limps over to Tact on his bad leg… PULSAR GRABS THE HEAD OF TACT AND BEGINS RAMMING IT INTO THE MAT! ONCE! TWICE! THREE TIMES! With that bad wheel, Pulsar has to figure out a way to do the most damage without moving and Pulsar relies so much on his energy. But this seems a bit extreme…
VIC WATERS: Geez, Pulsar is vicious. He is a wrestler who truly represents the spirit of this city, even if he isn’t from it.
BRANDON JACOBS: Tact is down and the limping Pulsar has Tact at his feet. PULSAR IS CALLING FOR THE BLACK HOLE!
VIC WATERS: After Pulsar rammed Tact’s head into the mat repeatedly, if he just even gets half of this, Pulsar is going to pull the match out.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar picks Tact up off the mat…. TACT NAILS PULSAR WITH A FOREARM SHOT! TACT WAISTLOCKS PULSAR AND HE NAILS HIM WITH AND OVER THE HEAD BELLY TO BELLY! Tact is calling his finisher!
VIC WATERS: Tact is now showing the fortitude that Pulsar showed earlier on in this match. Both of these men have hearts of champions.
BRANDON JACOBS: TACT IS GOING FOR IT! Tact scissors the leg, applies the crossface, and tuns Pulsar over…. TACT HAS THE SKYLOCK INVERTED STF LOCKED IN! Pulsar is reaching out but he is screaming in pain! The ref is going down to see if Pulsar will give up… PULSAR HAS PASSED OUT! THE REF IS CALLING FOR THE BELL!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match in 18:06 …. LARRY TACT! VIC WATERS: I thought Pulsar was done for early on, but he came back and he gave Larry Tact the battle of his life.
BRANDON JACOBS: I was really impressed both men here tonight and this crowd in Rockefeller Center is on their feet giving an ovation to both the hometown hero Larry Tact and Pulsar.
VIC WATERS: Fans are just happy to cheer for a winner with the Knicks so bad. BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we have to take a commercial break, but when we comeback, get ready for a No Rope Barbed Wire Cage Street Fight Tornado Death Match under BAD rules because the team of the WFW North American Champion Richard Gideon and BAD World Heavyweight Champion will team up against the former WFW World Heavyweight Champion and the man who put the BAD World Heavyweight Title on the map, Rat Fink! STAY TUNED EVERYONE!
