SETUP: DDT FINISHER: Rage-Bomb (It's a reverse powerbomb landing on their face/stomach WHO YOUR CHARACTER IS: Rage was created in 1995 on the old Prodigy boards. He is a guy who at a young at had money, world titles, everything. Stuff went to his head and lost everything. Got into drug use and pretty much ruined his career. Left wrestling in 1999 and made a full comeback in the GLCW in Jan 2003. Clean and sober, he is better than ever.
