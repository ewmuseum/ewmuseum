WFW Mind Over Matter: McGee vs. Pulsar vs. Good Eddie

23 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 23 January 2004 BRANDON JACOBS: Welcome back, it is time to begin the show. Who do you like in this upcoming match?
VIC WATERS: I’ll go with the debuting Mike McGee, he has something to prove after his long absence and wants to look strong in his return to prove that he is back in form.
BRANDON JACOBS: Jonathan? JONATHAN MARX: I’m going to go with Good Eddie. He is best technically sound wrestler of all three of him and from what I saw in his promo, he really has that killer instinct. He reminds me a bit of Golden Hawk. Who are you going with Brandon?
BRANDON JACOBS: I think Pulsar is going to surprise everyone and he is going to find a way to beat both Mike McGee and Good Eddie tonight single handedly.
JONATHAN MARX: Pulsar is an energetic young lad. If he can ever harness all of his power and refine his skills a bit more, he has all the makings of a champion.
BRANDON JACOBS: You should have seen how big he came up in both six mans he was in Jonathan. He is a primetime performer that can rise to the occasion in the big spot. That is why I’m going with him tonight.
VIC WATERS: I have no doubt that Pulsar is going to put a fight, but McGee when WFW first began was one of the men who looked like he was going to lead this company, tonight is a new beginning for him and he doesn’t want to let down President Wylde.
JONATHAN MARX: Heaven forbid he lets down President Wylde. Wylde might have to remove McGee’s lips from around his…
BRANDON JACOBS: ::grabs Jonathan’s mic:: Remember, this is a family show. JONATHAN MARX: The Manson family? VIC WATERS: Except without the Pez addictions… BRANDON JACOBS: Do not take the good name of Manson in vain, we’ve got to get this match started. Let’s send this match down to ringside and get this underway.
(CUEUP: “Whiskey in a Jar” by Metallica) RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first, from Australia, standing at over 6’3, PULSAR! ::fans cheers Pulsar as he comes down to the aisle and enters the ring:: (CUEUP: “"Cat Scratch Fever" by Ted Nugent) RING ANNOUNCER: And his opponent, the resident gunslinger of the WFW, standing at 6’4 and weighing in at 250 pounds, from Albuquerque, New Mexico…. GOOD EDDIE!
(CUEUP: “Generic Office Muzak”) : RING ANNOUNCER: And last but not least, making his redebut here tonight, from the Home Office in New Brunswick, NJ! Terry Tate’s worst nightmare, “MR. MIDDLE MANAGEMENT” MIKE MCGEE!
::Mike McGee walks gingerly down to the ring to the loud boos of the crowd:: *bell rings* BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar comes out like a house of fire and starts pounding away on McGee and Good Eddie!
JONATHAN MARX: Listen to this crowd get behind Pulsar, this rookie has developed quite a fan following here in WFW.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar nails Mike McGee with a high knee! PULSAR NAILS GOOD EDDIE WITH A HIGH KNEE!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar is a very excitable young lad. BRANDON JACOBS: Good Eddie and Mike McGee charge Pulsar… PULSAR DUCKS DOWN AND GOOD EDDIE AND MIKE MCGEE HIT HEADS!
VIC WATERS: That really scrambles your brain. BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar picks up Good Eddie… INVERTED SUPLEX! A dazed Mike McGee goes to attack Pulsar… PULSAR HITS THE CHARGING MCGEE WITH A SHOULDER BLOCK RIGHT TO THE FACE! McGee grabs his jaw…. PULSAR CHARGES MCGEE WITH A SPEAR AND MCGEE FALLS THROUGH THE ROPES TO THE FLOOR AND BANGS HIS HEAD ON THE GUARDRAIL!
JONATHAN MARX: MCGEE DOWN! BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar picks Good Eddie up…. DDT! Pulsar hooks the leg and goes for the cover! 1--------------------------------------------2------------------------------ GOOD EDDIE BARELY KICKS OUT!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar is utterly destroying these two. I don’t know how they are going to get out of this.
BRANDON JACOBS: Pulsar is going to the outside for the somersault leg drop… MCGEE BOLTS UP FROM BEHIND PULSAR AND RELEASE GUTWRENCH SUPLEXES HIM OFF THE APRON ONTO THE CHAIRS IN THE FRONT ROW OF THE AUDIENCE!
JONATHAN MARX: Pulsar nearly got killed there. McGee has no respect for Pulsar, there is a difference between wrestling and trying to seriously hurt someone.
VIC WATERS: Pulsar is barely moving. He is going to be down and out for awhile. BRANDON JACOBS: Mike McGee slides under the bottom rope and enters the ring…. MIKE MCGEE GOES FOR THE COVER ON GOOD EDDIE! 1---------------------------------------------------2-------------------------KICKOUT!
VIC WATERS: Mike McGee is a smart man, he knew the beating Pulsar laid into Good Eddie… so before Pulsar could finish him, he stole Good Eddie for himself.
JONATHAN MARX: Just like McGee, always looking for the easy way out of doing work. VIC WATERS: He is streamlining in order to achieve maximum performance. BRANDON JACOBS: Mike McGee turns Good Eddie over for a Boston Crab… Mike McGee goes to grab of Good Eddie BUT GOOD EDDIE CRAWLS TOWARDS THE ROPES AND OUT OF THE GRASP OF MIKE MCGEE!
VIC WATERS: That Good Eddie is awfully sneaky. BRANDON JACOBS: Mike McGee charges Good Eddie but Good Eddie bolts up and gets up his fists…
JONATHAN MARX: McGee ran right into the line of fire. BRANDON JACOBS: Mike McGee is telling Good Eddie to calm down and he extends his hand for a handshake…. Good Eddie looks down at his hand…. HE SLAPS MCGEE ACROSS THE FACE!
JONATHAN MARX: Good Eddie isn’t going to fall for a trick as old as time. He didn’t just fall off the turnip truck.
VIC WATERS: What is wrong with being civilized? I think it was a noble gesture by McGee. JONATHAN MARX: When a handshake is deserved, yes, but when McGee shakes your hand, you have to count your fingers after you are done.
BRANDON JACOBS: Mike McGee and Good Eddie are exchanging blows back and forth and Good Eddie is getting the advantage…
VIC WATERS: Those right hands of the Cowboy are leaving their mark. BRANDON JACOBS: Good Eddie bounces off the ropes… THE REF GOES DOWN… Good Eddie charges with a lariat… MIKE MCGEE DROPS TO ONE KNEE AND GOES DOWNSTAIRS WITH A BLATANT LOW BLOW!
VIC WATERS: NUTSHOT! NUTSHOT! NUTSHOT! JONATHAN MARX: That is a blatant foul! Mike McGee should be disqualified. That isn’t the way someone from management should conduct himself.
BRANDON JACOBS: The ref is getting back up after having missed Mike McGee hijinks. Mike McGee sees an opportunity and grabs the tights of Good Eddie. He lifts him up in the air… GORDBUSTER! Good Eddie goes face first into the mat!
JONATHAN MARX: Now that is old school. BRANDON JACOBS: Mike McGee turns Good Eddie over and is going for the cover! 1-------------------------2-------------------------KICKOUT! Mike McGee is yelling at the ref to pick his count up. McGee starts laying the boots into Good Eddie and he is repeatedly stomping him in the face.
JONATHAN MARX: After being hit with the Gordbuster and all these stomps, Good Eddie’s face must feel like a tenderized piece of meat.
BRANDON JACOBS: Mike McGee picks up Good Eddie and pushes him into the corner… MIKE MCGEE STARTS WAILING AWAY AT THE CHEST OF GOOD EDDIE WITH HARD CHOPS!
JONATHAN MARX: Those are some of the loudest chops I’ve ever heard, McGee isn’t holding anything back.
BRANDON JACOBS: Good Eddie FIRES BACK WITH A COUPLE OF BIG PUNCHES…. Mike McGee staggers back… MIKE MCGEE RUSHES GOOD EDDIE AND RAKES HIM IN THE EYES!
JONATHAN MARX: This crowd is really getting on Mike McGee for the eye rake. You can tell this crowd wants to see the cowboy beat the suit up and badly and they are just waiting for it.
BRANDON JACOBS: Mike McGee picks up the blinded Good Eddie…. SPINNING BRAINBUSTER! Good Eddie eyes are glazed over, he is grabbing his neck and he looks like he is in a whole lot of pain. Mike McGee looks over at the barely conscious Pulsar and sees his opportunity to go for the kill.
JONATHAN MARX: As bad as Pulsar looks right now, I think I would keep on working over Good Eddie because Good Eddie looks like he is in much worse condition Pulsar right now despite being awake.
BRANDON JACOBS: Mike McGee delivers gutwrench suplex…. PULSAR LANDS ON HIS FEET! Mike McGee turns around… PULSAR NAILS HIM WITH A SHOULDERBLOCK! Mike McGee stumbles back up onto his feet…. PULSAR NAILS HIM WITH ANOTHER SHOULDER BLOCK! Mike McGee tries to get up by he falls down onto the ground…. PULSAR PICKS MIKE MCGEE OFF THE MAT AND HE IRISH WHIPS HIM INTO THE TURNBUCKLE!
VIC WATERS: Mike McGee had Pulsar ready for the kill but the time he spent over working Good Eddie allowed Pulsar some time to rest.
BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR CONNECTS WITH THE RUNNING TURNBUCKLE SPLASH! Mike McGee is wobbling on his feet…. PULSAR NAILS HIM WITH A BULLDOG FROM BEHIND!
VIC WATERS: Pulsar has total control of this match. If he nails him with his finisher here with Good Eddie down, he could pull off the upset.
BRANDON JACOBS: PULSAR NAILS MIKE MCGEE WITH THE BLACK HOLE! Good Eddie charges out of the corner and LARIATS Pulsar over the top rope and he crashes down onto the concrete floor!
JONATHAN MARX: Good Eddie was lying in wait and when he saw his chance, like a good gunman, he took his shot.
BRANDON JACOBS: Good Eddie kicks Mike McGee in the gut…. HE NAILS HIM WITH THE LAST PRAYER PEDIGREE! Pulsar is trying to get back into the ring. The ref goes down for the count! 1-------------------------------------------------2------------------------------------3! Pulsar just misses making the save!
RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the match…. GOOD EDDIE! JONATHAN MARX: That was a hell of a match to start things off with. It went back and forth so many times, I really didn’t know who was going to win until the end. Pulsar almost ended this whole match early too. Both men did a hell of a job fighting Pulsar off early just to last to the end.
VIC WATERS: McGee didn’t win, but just wait, he is coming off from a six month layoff and looked damn good. Sometimes it takes a match or two to get back in form and when McGee gets there, he’ll be a force to reckoned with.
BRANDON JACOBS: Folks, we have to take a short commercial break, when we comeback, Felix Red puts up the BAD World Heavyweight Title against Psycho. STAY TUNED!
