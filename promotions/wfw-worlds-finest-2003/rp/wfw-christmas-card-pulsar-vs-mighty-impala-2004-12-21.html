stuclif — 29 December 2004 FADE IN: SCENE OPENS WITH PULSAR, DRESSED IN TRACKPANTS AND A SINGLET, SITTING ON THE EDGE OF A COUCH, FISTS CLENCHING AND UNCLENCHING. HE STARES AT THE TELEVISION NOT BLINKING OR CHANGING HIS FOCUS AS THE PERSONAL CAMERAMAN (PC) WALKS IN.
PC: Hey man, how is it goin? PULSAR: Fine. PC: What you up to? PULSAR: (STILL NOT LOSING HIS FOCUS ON THE TV) Homework, I cant afford to slip once at Christmas Card, its too important.
(CAMERA PANS OVER TO THE TV, VIDEO CASSETTES ARE PILED ON TOP OF THE TV, WFW GREAT EXPECTATIONS CAN BE SEEN AMONGST OTHERS. ON THE SCREEN WFW VOX PART DEUX IS PLAYING, IRON LION CAN BE SEEN GOING FOR THE COVER ON THE MIGHTY IMPALA)
PULSAR: (STILL FOCUSED ON THE TV) You see.. thats it, (POINTS AT THE TV) the Lion tore Impala apart, and at Great Expectations Impala took me to the limit.
PC: I think you'll have him covered. But we gotta promote this match first. (PULSAR REACHES FORWARD, GRABS THE REMOTE CONTROL TURNS OF THE TAPE, SLOWLY TURNS TO THE CAMERA)
PULSAR: (LOOKS AND SOUNDS ANNOYED) What the hell are you talking about? PC: You know... cut your promo. Company policy PULSAR: And why the f*** would I wanna do that for. This is not the type of match I need my fans to see. (NOW SHOUTS)This is the type of match that should be happening in the god damn street. Don't come to me with this s**t, alright.
PC: OK.. I'll go. (CAMERAMAN TURNS TO LEAVE) PULSAR: Wait (CAMERA TURNS BACK TO PULSAR WHO IS NOW STANDING) PULSAR: Look John, man I'm sorry ok. Its just that... this son of a b***h Impala, he's gone out and made this personal, he bought my family into this whole mess, so its time for me to take him out of it. Because of him, at Christmas Card, my sister will stuck inside a cage above the ring with two men fighting over her like she is a piece of meat. I dont care where the hell he's from, in the civilised world innocent people dont deserve to be kept in a cage. So you can see why I'm a bit edgy.
PC: Hey no need to apologize... just make you remember what time it is PULSAR: (LOOKS AT HIS WATCH) What time is it? PC: Its time to cue the excitement brother. (PULSAR GIVES A SLY GRIN) FADE OUT GreggG — 2 January 2005 (CUT TO: The Mighty Impala, looking regal in his tribal robes, stands in the living room of Pulsar's childhood home.
