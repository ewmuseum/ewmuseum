"Zero Girl," by Sam Kieth (CUTTO: Felix Red, still in his red black and white wrestling trunks and boots, sitting on his locker room tile floor, smoking a black clove cigarette, staring blankly at the wall, still all sweaty 'n gross 'n sh#t, cuz he hasn't even taken a shower yet...)
"The boy who destroyed the world" The funny thing is we were actually a bit nervous for a while there... The inner circle was foretold to be a collection of the industry's best and brightest. The upper echelon. Untouchables. Enochian angels unleashed by the WFW god to purge the world it made of wickedness and lies through the wholesale slaughter of all. And the rumors were, indeed correct. This is the prophesized microcosm apocalypse.
But just because the world's ending doesn't mean it's anything to get all tied up in knots over. Quite to the contrary.
There's no turmoil in the end of everything, only familiarity. Comfort. I am a child, surrounded by new and delightful presents, right around 11 a.m. on eX-Mas mourning. We're all going to die, and I feel absolutely f*cking cozy. For real. Does anyone else feel like snuggling?
Circles are endless and invincible, until they encounter the fury and hate incarnate that is a 90 degree angle...
And, while I may be badly mistaken, I believe we've got at least four of those...
