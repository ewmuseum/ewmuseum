ViewersChoice — 9 October 2004 (FADEIN to Scotty Michaels, sitting in front of his 51" Sony Wega. He's watching "Back to the Future"... Scotty pauses the movie..)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: That Christopher Lloyd.. one hell of an actor. He gives justice to Doc Brown like no one else could have. His "catch-phrases", so embedded in our daily culture that we fail to recognize the fact that there even there. After I pinned Copycat in our match at Paradise Lost, a lot of people were left dumb-founded, saying things such as "GREAT SCOTT!"
But Christopher Lloyd, by no means is he a Robert Deniro or a Tom Hanks. Kind like Copycat being no Scotty Michaels. (laughs) Ya see, not many people thought that I could actually go out there and pin Copycat, fair and square, but it happened... DONE! You wanted RESPECT Copycat, well I gave it to you. You wrestled one HELL of a match, and it showed in the end.
I decided to keep you involved in the match at the pay per view because I knew that you deserved it, because I knew that you needed to be there. But when we get to that match, Turkey Day Terror, you're going to wish that I had excluded you... not given you your just desserts.
But getting back to the whole idea of GREAT SCOTT, yeah.. that's me. Anyway, I'm still the same cocky mother-f**ker that I've always been, but this time I've got a purpose. I've been winning championships here and in other places, for years. But how many of those have I actually gained on my own god-given ability? Few, if any.
As I've stated before, there's always been a gameplan. Where there's been a Plan A, there's been a Plan B... in case of failure. Hell, there's Plan C's, D's, and Q's. I've always got it figured out, and it always took me to where I needed to be.
This time, though, I'm doing it on my own. You didn't see Shawn Hart or Richard Gideon out there because I didn't need them to win that match. I keep company with them because I respect those guys more than I respect any of the scoundrels in L.O.V.E... and that brings it back to my tag team partner, Psycho.
Psycho is a man who I have NO RESPECT for, WHATSOEVER... not as a wrestler, but as a human being. I mean, look at the way he follows Anarky and the rest of that group around. No respect, whatsoever. Hell, I'd rather take Copycat on as a partner before I'd take on you, Psycho.
The only reason you're carrying around the WFW North American championship belt is because I don't want it. It's small beans compared to the big prize I'll be fighting for very soon.
But there is something that intrigues me in the WFW. This new group, I believe they're called the Inner Circle. The last time I heard Inner Circle, weren't they singing the "Bad Boys" Theme? I don't know, could be wrong.
But the fact of the matter lies in this, Dan Ryan. You've been running roughshod over the CSWA, and I'm sure many other federations for years now... and I'm assuming you came to the WFW because you figured you could do the same here. Well let me tell you something, Ryan. It's not going to happen on MY TIME, in my federation.
A few months ago, when I returned from my suspension, my point of declaration was that I'd be TAKING BACK WHAT'S MINE! That relates to the world heavyweight championship, without a doubt. But it also means taking back my federation.
A lot of people have asked me why I still stick around, because I've won all the titles there are to win here. It's not just a matter of nobility, me trying to win belts without running any interference. It's because every guy that's been here. The Copycat's, the Shawn Hart's, the Richard Gideon's... they've all been here since this place set up shop.
Just when I thought L.O.V.E. might be the greatest obstacle to this plan coming to fruition, Ryan and a bunch of his buddies from the CSWA show up. Well Danny boy, good luck in this tag team match. I hope you bring it all to the table.. because you're going to need to do exactly that. There's no shortcuts, NOTHING.. when it comes to beating Scotty Michaels.
I'm the best damn wrestler in this business, INTERFERENCE.. or NOT! After I beat you and Copycat at the next card, you're going to be saying GREAT SCOTT as well... except it'll go something like this. "That Scotty Michaels is a GREAT wrestler."
I'll be waiting for you, Ryan and Copycat... and Psycho, if you know what's best for you, you'll tell Anarky and the rest of your chronies to stay in the back.
