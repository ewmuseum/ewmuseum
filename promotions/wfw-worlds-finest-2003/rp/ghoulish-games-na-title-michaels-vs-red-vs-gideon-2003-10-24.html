Ghoulish Games - NA Title - Michaels vs. Red vs. Gideon

24 October 2003

PaulNJ21 — 24 October 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Oct-24-03 AT 10:02&nbsp;PM (EST)] The RP/Angle dealine is Monday, November 3rd 11:59 PM. If all all possible, make your RP Halloween or Day of the Dead themed.
Cut your promos here. Methos17 — 25 October 2003 (It's midnight. As the clouds part and the moon shows itself in the night sky, Richard Gideon stands in jeans and a t-shirt digging a hole. As he toils, his body heaves with each dig of the shovel he slowly works his way out of the hole. Apparently finished, he begins to throw his gear into the hole. After his gear has been collectively dumped, he begins to fill the hole. Looking at the camera occasionally, but with his faced obscured as he continues to fill, he begins to speak:)
Richard Gideon: Well Red, you proved you could be Richard Gideon...The System Failed, and you came out on top. However (shovels more) *pant* I do hope you don't assume that you're going to run me over come Ghoulish Games. And neither should Michaels. Because, as you can see *pant* I'm taking care of something I should have done a long time ago...You have just witnessed the burial of Richard Gideon. What was alive is dead, what was old..new again. I've buried my past gentlemen...my past accolades and failures have been effectively nulled. The System is going back to zero. At Ghoulish Games...you'll see a new side of me. Saint or Sinner, Good or Bad...it will be someone, and something new. No posturing here (turns head) No need for it. At Ghoulish Games I'm walking out the champ. I realized that the second the ref counted three. I'm at Zero Now....no negative is possible. The only thing left is to go up. And if that means going through a fireplug that has beaten me twice straight and the North American Champion the company wants to back, so be it.
(Gideon pats the grave twice, and then turns toward the camera, forcing it to pan down as he does so.)
Gideon: Gentlemen, be prepared...I have nothing to lose...your title Michaels to gain...your respect Red to gain...and somehow...perhaps a little of my life.
(Fade to black) NotorisSTD — 26 October 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Nov-01-03 AT 02:07&nbsp;PM (EST)]“One cherished child who can create will be more important than ten who can produce…or fifty who can believe.” – Anton Szandor LaVey
(CUEUP: “Haunted” by Poe….) (There’s a house on ash tree lane that isn’t supposed to be there. Felix Red isn’t supposed to be sitting on the porch, sunglasses hanging low off his nose, purple FLCL T-shirt shrouding an athletic frame, black dickies and combat boots keeping the chilly autumn at bay, smirking and sipping lemonade with his feet up on a coffee table with a plate of candy apples to the side…Nor are you supposed to be watching this. But the house is there, so is Felix, and you are watching this. So ##### you. Deal….)
“the boy who destroyed the world…” To Mr. Gideon, I want you to know I don’t need to hear your excuses, for I take little pride in what I’ve done to you. I never claimed to be any less opportunistic than anyone else. I never claimed I wouldn’t be in the right place at the right time, which is what I was…That isn’t to say I don’t equate dropping you on the top of your skull with the purest of rapture. If we could do it all again, I would do everything the exact same way…What I’m trying to say here is you were a beaten man well before I got to you. So please spare me your bitching. There’s no need to get suicidal….yet. As it stands between us, you haven’t “really” beaten me, and I haven’t “really” beaten you….unless of course I did, didn’t I?…But wait…let me start from the beginning….
Your endless self indulgent grandstanding and childish whimpering has been weary for some time now. Now you’ve dove into wholesale melodrama…You’re not a Calvinist Klein model masquerading as an actor, and this isn’t the WB, unless it is. I have little confidence in your ability to reinvent yourself in any meaningful sense. Not yet. You imbecile. Of course you have something to loose…Your body, your cognoscente, every scrap of triviality in this world you still cling to. You, for all practical intents and purposes, already beat me once, except you didn’t, and I don’t respect you for it. Are you sincerely deluded enough to think if it happens again, unless it doesn’t, anything will change? Do you realize how futilely ineffectual any attempt you could make to do anything would be? You have no idea how far you are from becoming like me…
I do not relish in having to hear your voice again. I do not relish in casting an eye towards your sour visage…However I shudder with anticipation, born knowing that as I dismantle you, it will be the last time I will ever entertain the notion of your existence….
You couldn’t hope to conceive of the lengths I will go to ensure I needn’t f(bleep)k around with the likes of you for another second…
To Mr. Michaels, As I’ve stated several seconds previously, I never claimed not to be opportunistic. I’ve been called nihilistic by nihilism’s ambassador to professional wrestling. Faith is a product of necessity. I don’t need anything. I don’t need your title. I don’t need to be successful. I’m not as weak as you are. I have things I want….your blood, for example. If I happen to obtain titles and success in the process…then, well…c’est la vie…
I want you to take a good hard look at what’s already happening to Mr. Gideon. I’m not about to tell you that’s your future. There is no future. I want you, only to realize I’ve really only grazed him, and he’s falling all to pieces. He was never a priority. The lad was already an insecure egomaniacal half wit, unable to handle failure or rejection. He’s everything you would be, only his inadequacy was already fused into him…There wasn’t a pedestal to knock him off, and there’s clearly nothing of the sort now. You can presently support your illusions to elevation…You have so much farther to fall than he…
I don’t want to pin Mr. Gideon and win the North American title. I want to cripple what’s left of Mr. Gideon, and pin you. Destruction and creation aren’t opposites. They are means to the same end. I want to change things. I want to see what happens when another world falls apart. I take your title, I undermine your self image as the best, I’ve reset your entire life. You’re a fetus and I am the beginning born knowing and everything you’ve ever done or known was such a waste and I am the razor blade in your candy apple, Mr. Michaels….Or I’m not. Faith, sir, as to that matter I don’t believe one half of it myself….
Showtime24 — 31 October 2003 (FADEIN to a Haunted House, somewhere in North Philadelphia. The camera takes us through the house, until we finally come to a room with cobwebs all over the place, and a boiling cauldron in the middle of the room. Out in front of the camera jumps a man in a werewolf suit, screaming. He stops the hysterics, takes the mask off.. and we see that it's "The Viewer's Choice" Scotty Michaels...)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Sometimes I wonder why I'm such a nice guy.. helping out the local Boys and Girls Club associations with this haunted house. You see, this is a side of Scotty Michaels that you never thought you'd see before. "The Viewer's Choice", he's just a pompous ass who is concerned with winning the big prize... LIVING, BREATHING, and DYING, just for a chance to walk away with the WFW world heavyweight championship. The Scotty Michaels you see before you right now, is a pillar of the community... whose fans include a 12 year old boy named Mitch, and a group of his friends. Everyone always plays me to be the bad guy, and most of the time... I like it that way. You see, my life does not revolve around the sport of professional wrestling. I was out of the sport for FIVE YEARS, and during that time I realized just how short life is. I had some problems here and there, and it really showed me my mortality.
(Some kids are heard coming closer to the room... Michaels quickly throws the mask back on, and hides in a dark corner. The children look afraid, but continue on into the room. Michaels jumps out, and scares the living bejesus out of the kids. They run from the room, practically in tears... Scotty once again removes the mask)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: In the WFW, I'm a ring general... in this haunted house, I'm a general as well. (laughs) Fact of the matter is that I've been sitting back for the past week or so, just listening to what Felix Red and Richard Gideon have been saying about what they're going to do. Both, seem deadset on winning the North American championship belt. Well all I've got to say is, good for them. They can try, try, and try again... it's not going to happen. Now while my goals in wrestling may be winning the world title, I'm certainly not just going to stop, drop, and roll for these two clowns. I've proven to the entire world that I'm the best wrestler in these parts... and I know for a FACT, that Felix Red and Richard Gideon aren't going to be able to prove me wrong. I've worked too hard for too long to get back to where I'm at. There's not a chance in HELL of either of them beating me for my North American championship... because you see, my North American championship reign ENDS... only when *I* SAY IT DOES!
(Some kids sneak into the room, catching Scotty with his mask off..) KID #1: See, I told you it was Scotty all along.. KID #2: He looks like a big idiot!! (One of the kids picks a tomato out of the pot, and whips it at Scotty's head... smashing on his forehead)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: You little B**TARDS!! Just like these two kids, who bit off more than they can chew... it's going to be the same way for Red and Gideon. When it's all said and done, I walk out of Ghoulish Games the same way I walked in, as the North American champ!
(FADEOUT, as Michaels picks up a few tomatoes and starts to run after the kids..) Methos17 — 1 November 2003 (The camera opens to Richard Gideon walking out of a church. Dressed nicely to suit the occassion, he waves the camera off. A block past the church, outside of a small Brooklyn bodega, he stops and begins to speak)
RG: I watched the stellar interviews by both of my opponents, and though this is the Day of the Dead...I want to celebrate life. Like the old Russian proverb says, "The hammer shatters glass but forges steel." The past few months have been highs and lows, but I'm still a contender for a major title. Felix, you can talk all you want about how you will destroy me or some such thing at the PPV...but that can't happen. I came into this league to prove I was the best and no one, NO ONE will stop me from reaching that pinnacle. Melodrama, you say....there's nothing melodramatic about a man starting over Felix. Maybe when you hit bottom from this little streak of yours you'll see that. I've given legends sleepless nights and just in my rookie year. If crippling me is your method du jour, I say happily and without fear, bring it. You think you can put me out, try it. You'll see I'm made sterner than you think. You're infantile threats are what the stuff of melodrama are made.
(Gideon laughs a little to himself, then continues) Gideon: Don't get wrong Felix, I appreciate the enthusiasm you bring to the table vis a vis my "impending status of handi-capable"...I just don't believe it. You see Felix, you beat me. But at the end of the day, after that three seconds passed, I was able to get up and walk out. When I drew Doc Silver for the WFW Title, I was bleeding all over myself and barely conscious. And therein lies the rub. You'll have to give me a beating better than the toughest hombre here to keep me down. And I just don't think you got that in you cupcake.
(Gideon starts to head back to the church) Gideon (while walking): And don't think I forgot about you Scotty. You're the North American Champion after all. And you say you won't lose it until you think it's time. Well, by my watch it's a month too late. We get into that ring, unlike Felix, I'm going to be looking to beat the champion, not the other challenger. Michaels, you find yourself in quite the situation. You want to challenge for bigger and better things, but not at the expense of being shamed in this arena...but don't you worry, I'll make sure you can challenge for the World Title all ya want. Without the North American Title to "hold you down" you won't even need to give a second thought to who beat ya for it...I'll be seeing you gents...good luck.
(fade to black as Gideon goes back into the church) Showtime24 — 2 November 2003 (FADEIN to "The Viewer's Choice" Scotty Michaels, walking down a dirt road. It's near midnight, and he's by himself. It's very hazy out, a rainstorm just passed. Scotty's wearing a pair of jeans and a leather jacket. Scotty continues to walk through the night...)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: There's times in life when you need to reflect.. to reflect on all of the things that you could've done differently in your life. Seven years ago, I was the CSWA EN world heavyweight champion... I was the brightest star to come along in quite some time. I was, in essence, the next big thing. I had all of the skills, the style, the look, the charisma... you name it, and it was there. However, I let it all slip away from me. The drugs, the alcohol, the partying, I took it all with stride. I couldn't play a guitar to save my life, but in my mind... I was living like a rock star. And then, it all suddenly slipped away from me. October 30th, 1997.. six years ago, that's the night that would change my life forever. It was down this road that I traveled in my Lexus.. right after a long night of partying. I wasn't drunk, but I was high as hell. It was dark, it was hazy.. and who would've known that that kid would've been walking along the side of the road. Yeah, that's right... I hit him. I hit a 19 year old kid, because I was in a world of my own. You want the truth, that's the truth. He's alive, but he's sitting in a wheelchair right now. I could've killed him, and many were amazed that he's still alive. The police were never able to prove that I was high.. and I deny that to myself, to this day. I took five years off from the wrestling business because I needed to assess my life. Who else would I hurt, out of stupidity. There it is, the truth that I've been speaking of for the past year.
(Michaels continues to walk down the dark road, silent for nearly a moment...) SCOTTY MICHAELS: I've been here for close to a year, and I've been very hungry... HUNGRY for the world heavyweight championship. I've been able to live with myself, and I've even become good friends with the victim. Fact of the matter, Gideon... I'm more focused now than I've ever been before in my life. Since the day I stepped foot in this federation, I started something that NO ONE, and I mean NO ONE... is going to be able to stop. Sure, I've lost a match or two, but no one has pinned THESE SHOULDERS to the mat. No one's even come close to it. And with your attitude, you're not going to be any different. You're too focused on taking out Felix Red, a man who I don't think is even really going to figure into the match. There's your mistake. Now while I've definitely been focused and deadset on winning the world heavyweight championship... don't think I haven't been taking time to study the tapes, and the matches... and I've discovered each and every fault and weakness that you and Red have. You guys just aren't cut out for the North American title... it's MINE, at least until I say it's not anymore.
(Michaels gets to his car, standing there for a minute...) SCOTTY MICHAELS: You've all learned a lot about me tonight, more than I ever thought that I would let on. Oh yeah, and that kid? It was my cousin, Shawn Matthews' little brother. Live with that... overcome the hurt that you experience in a situation like that, and you'll see that nothing, NO ONE.. will ever stop me. Try all you want to, Gideon... bring your little buddy Red over, and I'll show you why I'm the champ.
(FADEOUT, as Scotty Michaels gets in his car and drives off...) NotorisSTD — 3 November 2003 (CUEUP: "Dream of Waking" by AFI....) (blank white wall. brightly lit room. Felix Red stands in the center of the shot, rocking tattered jeans with gibberish scribbled all over them them with a sharpie, and an old IWF T-shirt reading "KCARC si gurd-itna ym." Blue dreads hang before standard sunglasses, bemused yet hollow grimace....)
"The boy who destroyed the world..." We seem to have a failure to communicate... I waited patiently for you, Mr. Michaels. I hoped you would tell me something, anything, profound or moving or even interesting. Patience is a virtue. I suppose I should have known nothing useful would come of it. You taught me that you thought you were going to be victorious once faced with Mr. Gideon and myself, and that you're not well liked by children. I've always known this. Sadly, it is beyond your abilities to produce anything new. You, a walking cliché, intelligent or lucky enough to exploit the ease with which mediocrity can be marketed, stupid enough to think you're special. Obviously your title reign wont end until you deem it so...However your declaration needn't be eloquent and witty. You might not have to say anything at all. The sounds of your face splitting against my boot, the crunching of your spine against canvas, this is the only way you could ever communicate anything substantial. You'll teach me more about yourself with the squish of something crumbling inside you than with all the drippy after school special esq. nonsense you can muster...So very unfortunate that THC has such a troublesomely long half life. Stoners can get anyway with anything, it's true. Even driving without incident in most cases...Unless they can't. Perhaps you haven't denied a thing. Perhaps you didn't strike the lad because you were ripped. Perhaps he can't walk anymore because you're a klutz.
Then again, perhaps you wanted to hit him. Perhaps, in the midst of such decadence, you wanted to get away with murder. Perhaps you condemned your dear relation to the life of an invalid, because you knew you could. Interesting, but hardly relevant. I'm far more curious about aesthetics than morals. I don't hate you because endless glory tripping robbed you of your humanity. I hate you because you're stupid....
I'm not going to be a factor in this match?.....You pompous, sad, wretched, miserable whore. I want you to know I remember what you said. In light of your newfound regard for your position, I no longer expect you to run from me. This doesn't mean it wont happen, and if you leave Mr. Gideon and myself to our own devices, I will not accept petty appeasement. I will hunt you down like an animal, Mr. Michaels. Hush. Listen closely....You are not better than me and I am going to ruin you, am ruining you, already ruined you...never for fame, never to force others to pay attention to me...For the exact same reason you ruthlessly crippled your little cousin. Because you did, just as now, I can...
While I'm on the subject of cripples, Mr. Gideon, You tell us you don't believe I can cripple you. Well, neither do I. I'm nihilistic, remember? I presumed, given your recently confessed state of abject despair, you would have an easier time believing this than I. You went and decided getting pinned wasn't the end of Richard Gideon after all. Unless it was. You explained to me how I'll feel your pain once I hit bottom...(cackles) Come now. "hit bottom?" I'm the boy who destroyed the world, you simpleton. I am the f(bleep)king bottom. I understand that everything you've ever valued is worthless. I've sought out what you’ve hidden from. The creature within me. The creature within us all. I will know, found out, am discovering what I am. The end is the beginning born knowing, you pansy...Rapture has never given birth to progress. Through suffering and madness, mankind has advanced. Destruction is creation is destruction is creation. I will bleed, you will bleed, we will all bleed for my knowledge. I do not see this as mad egotism or a grandeur soaked hallucination. This is necessity. This word is, was, will be a place for cattle, and I can not inhabit it any longer than I must. To cry, I try, to break the static keeping me here in between....I want to see what's on the other side of the mirror...then I want to show you...
