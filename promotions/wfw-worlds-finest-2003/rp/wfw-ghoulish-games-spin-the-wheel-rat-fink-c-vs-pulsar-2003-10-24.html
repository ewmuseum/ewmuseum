WFW Ghoulish Games -Spin the Wheel - Rat Fink (c) vs. Pulsar

24 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 24 October 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Oct-24-03 AT 10:04&nbsp;PM (EST)] The RP/Angle dealine is Monday, November 3rd 11:59 PM. If all all possible, make your RP Halloween or Day of the Dead themed.
Cut your promos here. stuclif — 30 October 2003 FADE IN: NOT AN OLD TATTERED RING BUT A FAIRLY NEW LOOKING RING WITH THE WFW LOGO IN THE MIDDLE, PULSAR SITTING IN THE MIDDLE WEARING A PAIR OF CARGO PANTS AND SNEAKERS, LEGS STRAIGHT OUT IN FRONT PROPED UP BY HIS ARMS BEHIND HIM. ONLY SEEMS TO BE BOTHERED BY HIS RIBS WHICH ARE BANDEGED.
PULSAR: Alot of people are probably watching this at home and saying to themselves 'What the hell is he doing, why isnt he training in the gym?'...........Well you see, coming up at Ghoulish Games I'll be getting the biggest opportunity of my short wrestling career, a dream come true?...... You bet your #####, but all you people out there dont have to worry , i havent let it go to my head, i havent quit preparing myself, i just thought i'd a bit of time off and reflect....... reflect on those champions who have gone before me, reflect on what it took to get here. Basically to just savour the moment.
PULSAR GETS UP, SLOWLY HOLDING HIS RIBS. THE CAMERA FOLLOWS AS HE BACKS INTO A CORNER. PULSAR: But it seems that noone is going to miss out on having their dreams come true, but unfortunately for (POINTS TO THE CAMERA) you Rat Fink, the time of the year will ultimately effect the pleasure you derive from such a gift, for the worse im afraid (LOOKS SADLY INTO THE CAMERA). Halloween's upon us and those dreams you are having, i like to call them nightmares, can only come once a year ......... you know the ones, they're filled with burning tables, exposed concrete, steel chairs to the head and in the end, when the referee has to stop me from pounding your bloody head, the bell sounds and i raise the BAD WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE ABOVE MY HEAD!
TAKES DEEP BREATHES TO RELAX PULSAR: At New World Madness ...(PAUSES, WITH A THOUGHTFUL LOOK ON HIS FACE)... i wont lie, Dormant took me to the extreme, took me to hell and back, as i predicted. HE POWERBOMBED ONTO THE FLOOR FOR GODS SAKE!......... but here am once again, with another win under my belt. Rat Fink, i dont know you, and you dont know me. i've got no idea whats going on in that head of yours, i dont wanna know. But if you thINK (SEETHING) FOR JUST ONE SECOND THAT THIS ROOKIE CANT STAND WHAT YOU CAN DISH OUT, THINK AGAIN. ILL TAKE WHAT YOUVE GOT AND ASK FOR SECONDS, (LOOKS TO BE GETTING FURIOUS AT NOBODY, STARTS SCREAMING, VEINS IN HIS HEAD AND NECK ARE THROBBING) I WONT GO AWAY, YOU CANT GET RID ME, NOT UNTIL I HAVE WHAT YOUVE GOT.
HEAD IN HIS HANDS AND STARTS CRYING TEARS OF RAGE FADE OUT ChrisHorowitz — 31 October 2003
(Cue up "Begin the World Again" by Ray Manzarek) (Fade into Rat Fink behind his desk and seal of the NAR, he sits in front of the Wheel of Possible Opponents, a wheel that is ready to be spun.)
Rat Fink: Fans of the Revolution I present you with the state of the revolution in a moment where we have suffered another insult. Once again, despite our position as champions of planet earth we are given a complete non threat as an opponent. A man as almost lame and cliched as the name of the event is set to oppose the revolution, what a joke. It would seem the offensive defenders of the old order wish to fall upon their sword and send us their puniest guardians instead of an army capable of threatening our might.
But that does not mean we will send an inferior warrior into the battle on the side of the evil power of NAR. That would be quite impossible actually since we have no weak individuals in our ranks, and at best have one sub par janitor on staff. We would send him to fight, but his wife is pregnant and he has sick leave, so it would seem Pulsar will not be beaten by a man with a slop bucket... this time.
But NAR will send into the ring a fine soldier committed to worldwide evil. And for that task we have the widely beloved "Wheel of Possible Opponents", the world's fastest growing spinning sensation.
(Rat Fink stands up and turns to face the wheel, and then spins it. It turns and turns... and turns and finally begins to slow and lands on "Satan's Clause")
Rat Fink: Another fine choice in a series by our spectacular wheel. A man who used to make children smile as Santa Claus discovered the truth about the awful surreality of Santa and now has devoted his life to the Lord.
This is especially bad for you Pulsar, a man so committed to those things in this life that he so rigidly opposes. He will give you your chance to repent, but in the end you will be beaten into the ground by the purifying evil of our lord and savior, no matter the persona you envision him in.
Your yelling and carrying on will only make things worse my young friend, and your jealousy will only condemn you to a lower level of hell, for remember that envy is a sin, and it is very easy to envy the greatness that has been achieved by NAR, especially compared to the meager pathetic accomplishments of your short meaningless career.
But don't let that discourage you from being the best non threat to ever face the NAR and have his face shoved into the canvas, we wouldn't expect any less of the surreal world than to get your hopes up.
(Fade out)
