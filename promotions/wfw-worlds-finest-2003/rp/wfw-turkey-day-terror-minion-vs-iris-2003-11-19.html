WFW Turkey Day Terror - Minion vs. Iris

19 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 19 November 2003 The RP/angle deadline is Tuesday, December 2nd 11:59PM, send all angles to [detail withheld]
Diablo — 25 November 2003 (CUE UP: “New Wave Jacket” (Extended Mix) by Polysics) (FADEIN: The screen becomes bombarded with all of the colors of the spectrum in rapid succession as the music cues up, and that fades into El Arco Iris, standing in front of a rainbow backdrop, wearing his mask, a rainbow tie-dye t-shirt and rainbow corduroy shorts, all covered with a rainbow tie-dye satin robe.)
El Arco Iris: With El Arco Iris’ conquering of the forces of anger, El Arco Iris’ place in WFW is finally consecrated, and there is no stopping the joyous inundation! El Arco Iris must now deal with a man who El Arco Iris thinks with a little patience, can be taught to be happy again, if he opens his heart to El Arco Iris. Señor Minion is a man who seems to have trouble coping with death, and that is a situation that El Arco Iris has seen wrench at the hearts of even the toughest of men, and El Arco Iris understands that, but the problem is that the death Minion has trouble coping with happened some years ago, and it seems to plague him to this day! This makes El Arco Iris sad, having to see a man carrying grief within his heart so heavy, that it drives him to emulate the deceased, almost as if he wants to replace him! El Arco Iris is ready to help, Señor Minion, and there are easy ways to get you out from under the clouds of grief and into the sunshine of gaiety and exuberance…
(El Arco Iris reaches above camera shot and pulls down a chart that says “Step 1: Steal every Static X and Linkin Park CD in the world, Step 2: Destroy them, Step 3: Profit”)
El Arco Iris: Whoops, wrong chart, that’s for my seminar this weekend. Let’s see… (El Arco Iris fiddles with something above camera shot and then pulls down the correct chart.)
El Arco Iris: There we go. Now, the first step to finally getting on with one’s darn life is to realize that the past cannot be changed. Only the present can be affected by one’s actions, so one must leave the past in the past and save tomorrow for tomorrow. El Arco Iris only wakes by this morning’s sunrise and rests with this evening’s sunset, and only inhales the sweet scent of this afternoon’s breeze, and that is why El Arco Iris’ mind is free of any unnecessary preoccupations. Yesterday is merely but a yoke on one’s shoulders and tomorrow is the lock that binds it. If one can simply accept that there is only today, then you are no longer bounds, and you are free to frolic in the fields of your newfound joy!
(El Arco Iris examines the chart, and then continues) El Arco Iris: Ah yes, El Arco Iris adjusted this chart to fit your specific situation. El Arco Iris emphasizes this point especially. Señor Minion, why do you find you must emulate a man so long lost? El Arco Iris is sure that within you, as well as within every person, there is an individual spirit that is waiting to burst out and cast its own unique light upon the world! Bet you choose to eclipse this light by carrying another man’s name and legacy. El Arco Iris could have chosen himself to follow in the paths of others, and let my animosity and rage cloud my heart and mind, and dress myself in black and shave my head and pierce my flesh, but no, El Arco Iris followed his own path to become the soldier of happiness in a one-man army. The time has come when you should make that decision for yourself, to BE yourself. El Arco Iris is sure that instead of following in someone else’s footsteps, if you tread your own path anew, you will walk every step with a SMIIIIILE (traces the smile on his mask with his thumb)…on your face!
(Fade to rainbow gradient) DavidMc — 1 December 2003 (Screen fades in as we see ourselves in a very dimly hallway. Lights flicker on and off as the green walls and red carpet floor hallway seems to go on forever. On the walls are stains and writings in text unreadable. The camera wonders down the hallway and it becomes obvious that no one else but the Minion lives here. Doors are open into bare apartments that have been stripped or that way to dark to look into. The camera wanders further down the hallway and comes to an open door and enters. We are now in the Minions home. We see nothing has changed since the last time. The television producing static is the only source of light besides a candle that has almost burned to its end. The camera wanders around the apartment and we can see that some of the news paper articles and magazine clippings have been ripped down or stained with blood. The camera goes into the bathroom where we see Minion staring at himself in the mirror. The camera man places himself in a position where we can see Minion clearly without the camera interfering in the background. The Minion begins to smile but as quickly as he displays that emotion he frowns and shakes his head and pounds his fist into the sink and then smiles and repeats the process. About four or five times he begins.
Minion: Last week I graced the WFW with my presence….I told the world of my condition…some laughed it off at first…some thought I was joke…some even dared me to do the things I talked about…and what happened WFW…what happened when the Minion was unleashed upon the lambs of the WFW…what happened to precious Adam…I will tell you what happened…I shortened his career…where is Adam now…not giving interviews...I really doubt he can speak…he is for sure not wrestling I saw fit to it that he would never wrestle the same way again…the wrath of the Minion has begun and it will never stop…well it will stop…the day I get what I want…GOLD…beautiful gold strapped on my waist…beautiful gold that warrants a beautiful paycheck and respect…that is when the bloodshed…that is when the mercy might be given…keyword might…though until then perhaps I need to set an example of what I really meant…before I could end the career of Adam someone caught my eye…and it is never good to catch the shadows eye…for when you move and catch the attention of the shadows they never let go of your soul…they never let up until the person who has caught their attention has been disabled … destroyed… mutilated… humiliated…sadistically and systematically destroyed…Iris step on up…and spin the wheel of your own destruction…for I love the taste of hero’s as much as the taste of fallen angels.
(Minion begins to twitch and then slaps himself to stop. He looks into the mirror perplexed about what just happened then without warning punches a hole in the wall)
Minion: Iris why did you stop me…what did you have to gain…did you not want to see your friend get hurt…did you not what to see an innocent soul be destroyed…where were you Iris when my heart was turned black and my soul eaten by the devil…I was innocent once I was pure once…where were you to stop the demons from entering into my head and eating me alive…where were you when the money…power…fame…corrupted me…where were you IRIS WHERE WAS MY HERO!...where was my hero when I fell from grace and burned in the fires of hell…hero’s are a rare breed Iris…they are rare because they don’t exist…they don’t exist because people like me destroy people like you without even batting an eye as to what we are doing…a hero is brave soul but always the first to die…it is a noble cause but foolish…for one day the cows that are the fans will cheer you…then the next day they will boo you for no reason…I should know I saw it happen to Tom Parker…it sickened me…it twisted me…it left a scar on me and I am what you see now…a shell of what I used to be only hoping that one day I can be destroyed so the pain…the misery…the hatred in my body will die too…but I am cursed and will never die…no I am not immortal…all I am saying is that I will not die…I am not lucky enough…and suicide is a cowards way out…I would miss the pleasures of life…the pleasures like sex…feeling bones break…and tasting an opponents blood mixed in sweat as it runs down the body…I want your blood…I want to rub your blood all over my face and my chest…I want your blood to pour down my face in rivers and stop at my feet in pools…I want to slip in it…I want to cover my face in it and smile like a Cheshire cat and show you my teeth…teeth like gods shoe shine…they shimmer…sparkle…and then fade as I frown at your weakness…because you are weak Iris…the weak will gain nothing in life but misery and defeat at my hands….you should be eradicated…you should be destroyed to breed better competition for people like me…because I gain nothing out of beating you…I gain nothing out of it except the pleasure of breaking you….destroying you…and the knowing that I am getting rid of something as pathetic as you…you will wish you had never stopped me…you will wish you never caught the attention of this shadow…but never fear it will all be over…hush your mouth sweet child because soon it will all be over…hush your mouth because soon the demons will take over.
(Minion looks again into the mirror and the light behind him flickers and sparks out. In a sudden flash Minion lights a candle. He pauses and looks at the light that has burned its final light and then back into the mirror smiles…pauses…smiles again…but then frowns at something and begins.)
Minion: You think I am copying something…you thinking my fascination with a dead wrestler is unhealthy…that I need to be my own man…I am on my own man Iris…I merely take the name Minion to improve upon what was once created…to improve upon someone that was a failure and not repeat history…to take this name to new heights and beyond….to go places you will have only dreamed about and read about in those stupid wrestling magazines…Minion is a legacy…I am the legacy not some dead fake that once took this name…how dare you insult me…how dare you compare me to Tom Parker…you will suffer…the rage…hatred grows…the time has come for you to fall…the time has come for all the little boys and girls to hide because the demons have come out to play…always keep your eyes on the shadows Iris cause we are constantly watching…constantly planning for the moment…and when it comes nothing in the WFW will stop it…nothing you can do will stop me from achieving the ultimate goal…Iris watch tapes...prepare yourself…not to win…not to lose because we know that will happen…but not to get hurt because I am coming to end your career…because I am coming to destroy you and make sure you suffer…I have no mercy…I don’t care if you have a wife and a hundred kids I will make you earn a new lively hood because I love the taste of fallen angels and you are about to become one.
(FADE TO BLACK)
