ChrisHorowitz — 15 September 2003
(Cue up "Messiah" by Fear Factory) (Fade into a press conference setting where Jay Wilkes, the official spokesman of WFW is at the podium.)
JW: Ladies and gentlemen, the president of World's Finest Wrestling, Mr. Rat Fink. (Rat Fink walks out and stands behind the podium.) Rat Fink: Beloved subjects of World's Finest Wrestling, I stand before you as your leader today, but not as a god, not a man apart from his fellow men. I will be a leader of the people, not a leader above the people. This was the mistake made by the old regimes they began to feel they were gods themselves and lost touch with reality, being consumed entirely by the surreal.
And not only now do we the New Age Revolution have the presidency of WFW we have the symbol of our victory, a symbol to unite the world around.
We have, the BAD World Championship. The American one of the finest athletes in the world has proven his value to the NAR Revolution. He has made the world see that we are their shining saviors that would throw back the veil of surreality and unveil the charms of the evil that had brought them into the world of reality.
But of course there are constant challenges to the NAR revolution, and the first one has reared its ugly head.
Psycho will be the first to challenge our new leadership of this broken world. He desires to take our spoils, the prize we took by force from the old kings. This will not stand. The old kings will not achieve victory by sending their servants to take back our prize.
Psycho, you ravished victim of the surreal aristocracy how could you let yourself become a slave to a dying world? How could you follow the same path as Doc Silver, Shawn Hart, and Michael Manson? How could you let their values of goodness overtake your soul?
Can't you feel the pureness of evil, how it can cleanse the world? Or have they so deeply brainwashed you that you are incapable of seeing the truth? See how they have tossed you aside not to pursue the dreams they have made you lust for but to do their bidding? Yet you so willingly plunge into battle not for your aims but for theirs.
And now you will not face the easy pickings the old kings have placed before you until now, but the ultimate test, The American. The best of my officers, a man of countless years of experience and amazing talent.
But here you stand a shattered beliefless man who does the bidding of others. Who cannot see the reality that their power is waning and that the new power of evil has all but freed humanity from their clutches. You must open your eyes and see how we have ended their terror, how we have ended their lies. By continuing to fight for them you just fight for one thing, your quick demise.
The American has defeated their appointed champion, and a more pathetic champion he could not have asked for. It was a testament to their desperation that they would make Diablo their guardian of the symbol of power, and further evidenced by their choice of retreiver. Will you be their dog Psycho? Will you attempt to play fetch with the BAD World title, a belt that will never leave the coffers of the NAR revolution?
Just think of how silly all this sounds, that you will take this belt back for your masters, when their original guardian could not do so. You are just their lackey, one they hope to delay their final defeat a bit longer, to prevent final justice from landing upon their head.

(Cue up "The Purpose Of Existence" by Ray Manzarek) (Fade into the great hall of the American's estate, in black and white where he sits doing a jig saw puzzle. The huge puzzle lays scattered across the table as he slowly puts it together.)
The American: Psycho broken hero of the old republics, the ancient dictatorships, the fallen kingdoms, and the surreal.
It's amusing how pathetically stupid you are Psycho, a pathetic mass clinging to only what he knows, unaccepting of anything that may have surprised him. The old order fell and yet you hold on to it for dear life. Now you see that I will face you in this bout and yet cling to your metaphors for a match with the glorious leader Rat Fink.
The past you knew is lost, yet it seems to be all you can think of. Be it your opponent, your allegience, or your entire persona you cannot change. You are frozen in ice, stubborn, refusing to change. And why should someone like yourself feel he must change? You have had some modest accomplishments in your career and you feel that you may become set in your ways.
Sadly for such a minor talent you have grown such an ego to think you may stop the world at will and return to the past that no longer exists. Let me remind you that the future ain't what it used to be Psycho, the N.A.R. has changed the surreal back to reality and given the picture such a clear picture of what the world is, and what you old tricksters made them think it was.
Or perhaps you let yourself be fooled by the utopian view they gave you to trick the world into believing. Did you fall for your masters magic? Isn't it so easy to fall for the dream of a utopia only to realize that you are only supporting facism? To see yourself gain so much influence only to have it wane away until the only newsworthy thing you can do is die? It cannot be a pleasent thought Psycho, yet it is now all that you have.
The thing is, we already have the bait that you claim to have. The world title is in our hands, the precious symbol of the surreal system has fallen into the hands of those who will build the evil utopia.
We realize you are mentally instable, yet totally stable in the way that your handlers desire you to be. They want you to appear as this puzzle does, impossible to solve, yet with such a clear pattern if you just study it. Look at you, you have such a deliberate pattern to your service to your masters.
Of course you are ignorant of how your strings are pulled, and possibly rightfully so. They have let you think you are crazy, so you think you have no one to answer to for your actions. You think you are unstoppable. They have let you convince yourself of all of this, when it is all so painfully pathetically untrue.
This match will be the first chance you have for the wool to be pulled from your eyes. Finally the light of a new day may come unto you, and you may finally be given over to reality, to be taken from slavery.
Or will you just continue living the lie. For some reason i feel it will be the latter. No matter how badly you are beaten you can find such comfort in the lies of your masters, where you can hide and believe that you serve no one. You can feel you are second to no man despite your crippling losses, your failures, your short comings, your lacking.
Sad. But we will be there, to retain ownership of your ruler's symbol, so that their surreal empire may not make a return. Until the people have all rallied behind the new world this symbol still has its uses to us, and it must not be relinquished.
Not to you Psycho, not to a second rate contender like Diablo, to no one the old empires can throw at us. This is the property of the people now, and they will not be destroyed by your masters any longer.
(Fade out)
