NYC Unplugged: BAD World Title Defense: Diablo vs. Rat Fink

20 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 20 August 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Aug-20-03 AT 11:49&nbsp;AM (EST)]Cut your promos for the match here. All RP/Promos are due on Friday, August 29th at 11:59 PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
Diablo — 20 August 2003 (Cue up: “Latin Simone (Que Pasa Contigo)” by Gorillaz) (FADEIN: Diablo is seated on the ragged couch in his otherwise bare apartment. He sits with the BAD world title in his lap, not regarding it with any particular reverence, but a smirk still on his face. The lights are pretty low. We can see that it is late twilight.)
Diablo: Such a nice belt. All gold, no fancy colors, simple, yet conveying the proper sense of sovereignty. It’s too bad this belt stood for a fed that was a complete joke, but fortunately died a quick, merciful death. Not surprising when people like Doc Silver and Manson were at the helm. But now a new generation has taken control. All remaining vestiges of the past will not be allowed to interfere with the future. Our new future. The future…the future is dark.
(Diablo chuckles, and the smirk widens into a full smile. He takes the belt in his hand and holds it at arms length.)
Diablo: As little as this belt has meant in the past, I will take it upon myself to MAKE it mean something. While this belt is in my clutch, it will become an object of undeniable importance. I will illustrate this importance in tremendous fashion with my first defense against the self-professed Rat Fink, the super-villain of ALL super-villains. (Diablo chuckles some more) But what hasn’t been said about Rat Fink already? He’s a meek coward who wins his matches by oh so (smile widens) dastardly tactics. And he’s a deranged madman who wants to take over the world. Cute.
(Diablo leans back a little and assumes a relaxed position) Diablo: Now, if I hadn’t seen people just like Rat Fink rendered in ink and paint so many times before, I would be a little intimidated. The archetypal…”evil” person. Ooooh. Most people have such a narrow-minded definition of evil. People who have claimed themselves evil in the past were only evil through acts and speech. To be TRULY evil…one has to be…INFECTED with it. It has to seep down to the very core of your being, permeating every pore in your skin, and permanently distort the way you see, feel, and interact with the very world that so shunned you! Nobody IS evil…evil becomes you. I’ve seen you attack your victims with your formulaic and clichéd methods. Simple physical injuries don’t do much beyond hurt the person. Torn ligaments, broken bones, pulled muscles, all can be healed in a matter of time, and the victim knows he will evetually heal. But the one tool I use, the very first invention of man, is so looked down upon these days.
(Diablo takes out a book of matches and hastily, almost hungrily, pulls out a match and lights it.)
Diablo: FIRE! (Diablo takes a second to look into the flame with an amorous gaze.) Diablo: When I throw fire in someone’s face, it is not simply an act of brutality. It is a blissful melodic composition of pain and fear. When you break someone’s knee, it’s a quick moment of dull pain. When you are burned with fire…it is a constant, un-relenting agony that makes time STOP. The entire world around you is GONE, and you only know the lick of the demon’s many tongues. And when it is all over, it never ends. Fire SCARS! Fire will stay with you. The flame burns in your eyes when you try to sleep at night! It is the power of the flame that has brought men to their knees, reduced to a huddling mass of seared flesh and screams. When it burns, it burns to the soul. Strive as you must towards the enslavement of the human race, Rat Fink, but in my hand I will always hold the key to destruction and dominion over man. Diablo Fuego always burns…
(Diablo let’s go of the match but then catches it in his closed fist, extinguishing it. Now there is barely any light at all.)
Diablo: …and the Darkness draws power from the light. (FTB) ------------------------------ "I blow your mind. You just blow." ChrisHorowitz — 26 August 2003
(Cue up "Tell All The People" by the Doors.) (Fade into the international headquarters of The New Age Revolution in the unclaimed wilderness of Afghanistan. Rat Fink sits behind the desk of the Emperor for Life of the New Age Revolution, his hands folded.)
Rat Fink: My dear friends of the new order, I have come to a conclusion. As we sit on the dawn of a new era we must begin to change the scope of our armed forces. As I sit here in Afghanistan, land that neither the English, the Russians, nor the Americans could conquer, I have come to realize that I am too important to the revolution to soil my hands with such minor threats.
And now, the N.A.R. Revolution is faced with its most minor threat to date. Diablo, yet another worthless son of the old regimes, one so overcome with the surreal that he now thinks himself to be satan and plays with fire.
Of course it seems the surreal also causes idiocy, as he claims that man invented fire, when fire occurs naturally. I guess it was invented when man invented iron and gold, and the dinosaurs. More tactics of the old regime, telling the population of the worth of mankind because they were able to invent fire. "Look how great you are, look what humanity has accomplished! Think of how much you will accomplish if only we'd let you try..." So now Diablo you carry around a false title along with your false dreams, your false name, it will all lead to your demise along with that of your fearful leader Shawn Hart.
But I must ask myself, why should I Rat Fink future leader of the newly freed world deal with a peon of an unimportant untalented hack like Shawn Hart? The answer is clear to me, and to every supporter of the new order. These hands must not be soiled with the human waste that is Diablo. His hackneyed antics, those of some pathetic peon are an insult to the N.A.R. and I must only assume that this is another direct insult from WFW and their handlers in the old world.
So I have issued a declaration that these peons will no longer be handled by myself, as I must prepare for the coming overthrow. My generals, my army will now deal with people like you Diablo. So I have constructed a device which will even handedly and fairly choose an opponent for you. Do not assume fairly means that you will be evenly matched, for me to find an opponent as pathetic and untalented as you I would have to go into urban America and find a hobo who has nearly starved to death. He'd likely still win, but it might be a fair fight.
So now I present you with the tool that will tell you the nature of your destruction. Do to trademarks many names I considered I was unable to use... so I present you with.. THE WHEEL OF YOUR POSSIBLY OPPONENTS.
(Cue up dramatic music.) (We pan to the wheel which includes Ronnie Raygn, Dick Nickson, The American, Moe Lester, Mickey Mouse, Alex Kelly, Satan's Clause, and Dic Haire.)
(Rat Fink spins the wheel... it goes around several times until it slows on Mickey Mouse... but then the arrow moves one final time and lands on the American.)
Rat Fink: So there you have it Diablo, peon of Shawn Hart. Your opponent will be the legendary The American. The American, one of my finest generals, one of the most upstanding members of the N.A.R. organization. Even a delusional wreck of an human like yourself must realize that it is futile to even bother to fight The American. But you can feel proud of one thing Diablo. Remember that you are so pathetic that you forced me to change the entire direction of the Revolution, and allow my generals to do the fighting as fighting you would have destroyed any credibility the new order has.
(Fade out) Diablo — 28 August 2003 (CUE UP: "Double Bass" by Gorillaz") (FADEIN: Diablo is seated on the couch in his apartment, BAD world title in his lap. He has a bemused expression on his face.)
Diablo: Rat Fink, I am not surprised in the least, as you once again show the one quality about you that stands out among the rest. COWARDICE! That, and an incredible knack for misperception. Again I am referenced in the same sentence as "Satan", the concept of pure evil created by the narrow-minded. There is just so much more to Diablo than a name. Satan is the concept of a punisher of the evil and unjust, no saying he himself would be evil. I represent a force that does not intend to punish the unjust, but to indiscriminately infect the world, "good" and "bad", with our own...special brand of evil.
(Diablo let's out an impish smirk, which then dissapears.) Diablo: It is a force beyond your comprehension, Rat Fink, for sure. My darkness...it consumes whatever it grasps, friend or enemy, its victim or its wielder. When the darkness infects, one essentially becomes a step away from a corpse. Every thread of your being begins to unravel slowly, until you are left as but a pile of refuse, while the darkness lives still, searching to infect more, who in turn infect more, and in turn infect...more. It grows at an exponential rate as we speak, and it is, inescapable, as well as incurable.
(Diablo picks the title up and holds it at arm's length reverently.) Diablo: While this title is in my grasp, the grasp of DIABLO FUEGO, it will not be relinquished. And while the TRUE new era, is wrought by us, the C.O.C.K.S., while we weild myself, the vehicle of this plague, it will inevitably consume this federation--and the world--with despair and decay. Tell me some more of your idealistic "evil" ideas, I could use a good laugh. Call the kettle that is Shawn Hart black. He is but a figurehead. Looks good at public appearances. I am the holder of the true power. Send your sheep to the slaughter, and step back and watch as he, too, falls to the flame.
(Diablo stands up and looks straight into the camera) Diablo: Whether you will face me yourself, or not, you or your minions will find no succour. Be it now, or be it later, Rat Fink, I WILL PUT MY EVIL INSIDE OF YOU!
(FTB) ------------------------------ "I blow your mind. You just blow."
