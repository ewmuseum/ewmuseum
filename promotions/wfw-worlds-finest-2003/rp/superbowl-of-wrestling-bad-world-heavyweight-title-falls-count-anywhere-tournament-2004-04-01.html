But now there is no chance for hope. No chance for salvation, and no chance for victory. They go against a force that the world has never seen before. They go up against an opponent that the world cannot comprehend. They go up against the will of an entire world which has been opressed, and that will is personified in the army that they will face physically. How can one hope to defeat such a force? How can one contend with a power so mighty that it dwarfs their own by so many thousand times?
They only answer is that they simply cannot. They cannot contend with the N.A.R. they can simply sit as mute witnesses to the victory of the revolution. They can be in the ring as the Revolution once again captures its goal. They can be pressed to the mat when the revolution becomes the B.A.D. World Championship's caretaker for a second time. They will be witness to history, and be a part of history.
They will be the last vestige of hope for the old order, they will be the last grasp for power that is pushed back by the forces that oppose it. The N.A.R. cannot be denied by such a sickly gesture, and it cannot be stopped by men as inconsequential as the ones in this tournament.
That is why destiny is on the side of the revolution, and a return to reality is in the cards for humanity, and this planet earth.
(Fade out.)
