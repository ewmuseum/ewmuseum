Manson — 1 April 2004 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, in black "HAVE YOU CONSIDERED TAKING MICHAEL MANSON AS YOUR SAVIOR?" long-sleeved shirt and black leather pants sitting midway up a ladder in a dark basement with chains hanging from the wall.))
MANSON: Recently, it was like that I woke from a dream. I saw the WFW, I saw the world, and I saw myself. I really saw myself. Did the whole make breakthroughs and revelations about self thing. So I went won the Royal Rumble so that on the grandest stage of all, on the grandest show in the industry, at the Superbowl of Wrestling, I can win the WFW title.
Years ago, when I was young and more impetuous, I was at the last Superbowl of Wrestling and in a ladder match, I am willing to admit I lost. Such a loss led to me revisualizing the entire scape of the wrestling world as nothing more than a shadow show, which of course, led to philosophies and worldviews that expanded outward until I was a man without conscience, but always with a plan.
And I would carry my plans with varying degrees of success, poisoning and mutilating opponents, winning titles, smiting entire promotions, and living up to those psuedo-Satanic analogies everyone always made about me.
I was good at it and I enjoyed it. When I came to the WFW, I had such plans of finally avenging that ladder match loss years ago and I did, but by then Doc Silver was drunk on life and depression and it was so hollow. And other thing didn't work out so well.
But then...I woke up...as if from a dream.....and I had figured one or two things out. I always knew I wasn't invincible, the sole man in the entire that has never and will never submit aside, everyone just thought I was. Burning down entire promotions evidently had elevated me to anti-hero cult leadership like worship among my peers and the next generation of coke addicts and porn freaks that comprise this industry.
They all came for me, of course, some for revenge for something I didn't even remember doing, others for the sake of beating Michael Manson. And being me, no one came to my aid, no on helped. Truth be told, I didn't want anyone to, because it's always better to do it yourself.
Eventually, I did..hence the title shot....another chance..another opportunity...but I can't help but wonder if I received no help for a reason. People have wondered how I have not manipulated the presidency of this promotion.
In other words, why hasn't Alex Wylde used his position and power to the advantage of Michael Manson?
The simplest reason is that I didn't ask for it. But it goes deeper, so as deep as the mythical legend of Michael Manson. because being associated with me is more of a curse than anything. You'll win, you'll go on an adventure that few ever do, but you're branded. Accused of things you've never done, things I did. Things I will do. And always.....always....you live in the shadow of my schemes...my plans....my larger than life and death presence in the wrestling world....
...and that's Alex Wylde. While things went like the Spanish Inquisition for me, he won the presidency and parlayed that into the WFW world title. Finally, he has his chance...but there was always me...lurking in the background as I do....
You were my friend, or whatever I see as a friend...now loyalty I appreciate, but it is not necessary. I would almost thank you for casting me aside, since the most indomitable will in the WFW should be more than able to get by with his own means.
I may have lost the last tag match, due to mitigating factors, but that's not important now. That was merely the prelude.
This is the war. The WFW title has eluded me for the last time. I won't allow it again. I know you, Alex, as no one does. You were my partner in things horrible and beautiful, but you do not know me. No one does.
Why does Michael Manson do the things he does? It's really an obvious answer, but you and everyone else always gets it wrong. You've seen my dungeon, I've seen your penthouse. But the dungeon goes further. You never lashed out against me, never actually betrayed me, certainly, you haven't done to me what I've done to so many others. But the WFW title is the divider, and there can be only one.
Presidency aside, the title of the world's finest wrestler belongs not to you, not to Scotty Michaels, Sean Edmunds, or Doc Silver.....but to the aesthetically perplexing, mind-warping, sado-masochotic, pez-eating lord of the underground Doctor Doom type comic book level ant-hero that is me.
Now...you might be excited that Maelstrom with a whole suitcase of size 9 heels for his shoe fetish....you might be poisoning that gimp mask for Jean Rabesque...and you might be trying to find out who the hell Dan Ryan is....
...but in front of the collective wrestling community, every name....with Joey Melton as the referee.....
You are going to fall, not like me, not like the devil. All the world is a show and there can be only one featured player. The one who plays it the best.
To get here, I've cut through men, literally, had my back mangled, and travelled to brave and bold new worlds and lands where they chanted my name and sang my songs as I crucified their heroes...
You will not stop me now. Not in another ladder match. What you should have done, Alex, was taken care of me first....when you were president...you should have handled everything.
Because now, I might just have to flay your skin before I ascend that ladder. You may claim that you wanted it like this, predestination being what it is, that you wanted to be the one, the man to bring down Mike Manson finally in front of all the world and more, the one who actually convinced him they were friends, that learned of his life and secrets, things that no one else did....

They'd just as soon lick your hand as tear your arm off. So the people that live with them are well-versed on where the line is drawn. Anyone who makes dangerous company has a perfect understanding of the nature of the threat. And in the case of my particular dangerous associate...I've been watching him in the ring since we both were green rookies.
Michael has injured people in the past...but he has certain tactics he's very fond of...and he's been known to be predictable at times. Predictable in a way that the average wrestler could not perceive...but over years you pick certain things up. And the truth is folks...once you get to know Michael Manson...he's not nearly as scary. Sadistic? Yes. Crafty? I'll grant that. But not frightening. In fact...to me...he's more...quirky. Yeah...that's a good word for it. He's quirky. Eccentric. Not unlike a drunk Uncle...you just shake your head at his antics and make sure you never leave him alone at your place.

Because, Manson, while the rest of the world calls you the devil...to me...you're just Mike.
