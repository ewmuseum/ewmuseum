WFW — Roleplay

Your #1 Tag Team Contenders

22 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GreggG — 22 January 2005 (CUT TO: Holly Beckett, standing in the back, a little bit intimidated.) HB: Well, folks, we have some surprise guests here at Christmas Card. Some folks not on our--
CALVIN: Shut your hole, you little tramp, and stand there and hold the microphone like my momma paid you to do! I'll do the introductions!
(From the left, NFW South President and self-proclaimed Manager of Champions Calvin Carlton, holding his trusty tennis racket Sweet Betty Lee, does The Fargo Strut. Tailing right behind him are "Fabulous" Frankie Fargo and "The Last American Gigolo" Brandon Mueller -- shirtless, wearing cut-off dungarees and Confederate Flag bandanas, follow cackling in delight, holding two tag team championship titles. Calvin grabs Holly's hand, holding it tight as he smirks at her. She's repulsed but stands there.)
CALVIN: (Laughing) It just seems to be that this is the greatest day in WFW history! Yes, it is true. The rumors are true. I, Calvin Carlton, the greatest mind in this sport today, am coming here to WFW with my charges, The Original ShowStoppers. If you're one of my millions of fans who've watched me singlehandedly bring the NFW from the doldrums to make it the TOP wrestling promotion in this industry... or if you're one of the millions of women who squirm and turn at the site of my two charges (Mueller strokes his bleach-blonde 1985 mullet)... then you know that WE MEAN BUSINESS. But in case you've been living in a cave like Osama Bin Afghanistan or Taliban or whatever his name is then YOU need an introduction. My name is Calvin Carlton, and I am the greatest manager in the HISTORY of this sport. Y'see, my momma... the beautiful darling angel princess whose eyes sparkle with the intensity of 1,001 fireflies... she bred me to carry on the Carlton tradition. My family has dominated anything we set out to do. Fine art? Politics? Industry? THE CARLTON FAMILY ALWAYS GETS ITS WAY. And my momma... being the wealthiest, most powerful and greatest woman on this planet... she wanted me to get my start by conquering the world of professional wrestling. And you know why that is, Molly?
HB: It's Holl-- CALVIN: My momma wanted me to come to pro wrestling as her gift to mankind. You see, being a wealthy and powerful captain of industry and leader of high society has its demands. She is an important person and, unfortunately, does not have time in her day to associate with the poor, the wretched refuse, the lowest common denominator, the dregs of our planet. You know... the same people who like wrestling. So she sent me here to TEACH the poor classes a lesson, that by showing them if they mind their manners, if they realize they if you were born into parents who make less than $250 K a year that your place is to simply say "Yes, Sir" or "No, madame" -- if you realize that your place is to do what you are told like the animal you are... then things are fine. Now, I've already taken over and dominated the NFW and have that promotion bent to my will. So you know what brings me here, Dolly?
HB: It's Holl-- CALVIN: Because my momma, the angel descendant from heaven that she is, she has done a demographics study of the WFW's fanbase. And she has come to the conclusion that the WFW's fans are amongst the poorest, most worthless, illiterate, most down-syndromed, crippled and degenerate people on this entire planet. So I am here to do what the Carlton family has always done... to start giving out orders and to get our way! And I brought with me my charges here, who are going to help me take over this league and show the WFW fans that a real-life rich person cares about them and wants them to have running water, electricity and toilet paper! And do you know how we will do this, Polly?
HB: It's Holl-- CALVIN: We are going to do this by being the first team to procure and hold the newly announced WFW World Tag Team Championship titles. Y'see... my boys here... Fabulous Frankie and The Gigolo (Fargo and Mueller give each other a "Double Dukes'" hand gesture) were the greatest tag team of the South in the 1980's. And they were also the greatest tag team of the South in the 1990's. And now, today, three decades into their illustrious career... ther are now the GREATEST TAG TEAM WHO EVER LIVED. And you know what they have to prove that fact? THESE? (Calvin taps the NFW World Tag Team title belts.) They are the current NFW WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS. And I don't care what your carnival trash president had to say about it, this makes them the NUMBER ONE TAG TEAM in any promotion, ANYWHERE. If this promotion was smart, they would just HAND US the title belts right now. But it's fine if they don't... because The OHHH ESSS ESSS loves to do three things... that's beat you up, steal your women, and then beat you up some more! Tell 'em, Frankie!
(Fargo snatches the mic from Beckett, staring at her, threatening to punch her. Mueller cackles in the back, fixing his mullet, using his belt as a mirror. Calvin stands behind Beckett, eyeing up her rack.)
FARGO: So, anothah promoshun is startin' up a TAG TEAM division. Well, there's only ONE damn tag team in this sport worth mentionin'. And that's me and The Gigolo here... Raised on 100 Proof, comin' strait atcha from the Mississippi Delta. Now, ah don't know who any of ya'll got lahned up. An' frankly, AH DON'T REALLY CARE. Y'see, a few years ago, ah had a little bit of a scrap with Tommy Wildfire Rich involvin' a 13-year-old runaway and a pool cue... and it ended up with me gettin' put in jail and havin' to serve a few hard, long years in the Alabama state pen for CRAHMS EYE DID NOT COMMIT. An' because of that... ah stopped carin' about how other people felt. To get what AH want and what AH deserve... a man of mah SOUTHERN HERITAGE... ah'll do WHATEVUH IT DONE TAKES. If that means ah hafta bust some gay boy's face in from behand to make sure ah get mah place in the cafeteria lahne... AH'LL DO THAT. If that means ah hafta gut a man in the ribcage with a homemade knahf made from a chicken bone to git anothuh pack of smokes.... AH'LL DO THAT. And if that done means ah gotta raise some hell, lace up mah sh(BLEEP)kickers and beat some punks face in... AH'LL DO THAT. Me an' The Gigolo here... we want what's RAHTFULLY OURS... and that's the spot of bein' the best damn tag team ALIVE. And here, with our manager, the great man Calvin Carlton, and his beautiful momma leadin' the way... NO ONE is done gone STOP US.
CALVIN: I got one more message for you, Trolley-- HB: It's Hol-- CALVIN: All these other tag teams... you'd better send out an SOS... because you're going to cross paths... with the ESSS OHHHH ESSS.... The Originals are stronger... for even longer! Tell your sister! Tell your daughter! But don't tell my momma... hahaha... BECAUSE SHE ALREADY KNOWS. Let's get struttin', boys!
(All three do the Fargo Strut off camera as Hollly shakes her head in disgust.)
