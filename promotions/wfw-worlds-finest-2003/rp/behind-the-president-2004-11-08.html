Behind the President

8 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JLevinson — 8 November 2004 (CUEUP: "Behind the Music" theme song.) (FADEIN to a fake "Behind the Music" montage, showing various clips of Sean Edmunds in a suit and in the ring, usually with a rather dumb look on his face -- very tabloidesque.)
(CUTTO: A picture of Sean Edmunds walking down to the ring... the shot is in slow motion, and then is bled of all color.)
(Then, suddenly, we hear the voice of The Official Sounding Behind the Music guy... ) BEHIND THE MUSIC GUY: "Some men spend their whole lives trying to accomplish what Sean Edmunds did when he won the WFW Presidency. So why, then, is he running away from his record? Why is Sean Edmunds hiding behind silly attack ads instead of pointing out his own accomplishment?
"It's very simple: he doesn't have any. "Sean Edmunds let LOVE run wild on the WFW. Sean Edmunds has the effectiveness of an average footstool. Even his attempts to keep Copycat down eventually highlighted his spectacular failure as both a President and a man.
"Let's see how Sean Edmunds' last term has affected the fans of this great sport." (CUTTO: A picture of a kid in a "Sensational" Sean Edmunds t-shirt. His faced is pockmarked in acne.)
NERD: "When Edmunds got elected, I thought it'd be the coolest thing ever, y'know? I figured he'd show everyone what was up. Especially that loser, Copycat!
"But instead, he took orders from LOVE! His only actions were their demands. Sure, he fired Alex Wylde, but it's not like that wasn't gonna happen anyway! Heck, even I could've fired Alex Wylde, and I still live in my Mom's basement!
"He really isn't any better than a footstool. At least with the footstool, you don't expect much. Sean used to be my hero. Now he's just another politician that doesn't do anything until it's campaign season again.
"Sean used to be a plus-three broadsword with piercing, but now he's barely a leather plate armor. If he was in my clan, we'd call him a NEWB!!! Hahahaha, 31337 out."
(CUTTO: A very nice looking old lady in a purple blouse and matching purse with white capri pants.)
OLD LADY: "Oh, that Sean used to be such a very nice lad. But perhaps if he didn't spend so much time smoking crack and having sex with young children, he could have accomplished so much more.
"As for that nice young boy, Mr. Red, well, perhaps he'll slay us all and eat the husks of our unborn children, but at least it would be more interesting than a footstool."
(CUTTO: A homeless guy that's obviously been paid to say what he's saying. He can barely stand up on his own two feet.)
HOMELESS GUY: "Y'know... I saw Sean on a street corner once... and I say... hey Sean... when are you gonna show those guys in LOVE what's up... stop letting them use you, y'know?"
"And you know what he said? "He said he'd give me a hit of that sweet rock if I just sucked him off for a few minutes. "Needless to say, I slept well that night. But I don't think he's really Presidential material. At least, his c*ck wasn't, that's for sure."
(CUTTO: Another still of Sean Edmunds standing in the center of the ring, looking Presidential.)
VOICEOVER GUY: "Yes, Sean Edmunds would rather get his d*ck sucked than bother showing up for a Cabinet Meeting or making even the slightest effort to be an effective or worthwhile President.
"Perhaps if Sean spent as much time working in office as he does creating such nice attack ads, he would have had an effective candidacy. Instead, much like his impotent rod, it is time to get rid of the dead weight in this election.
"In just a few short weeks, you will have a choice between a footstool and a man that promises to let the entranceways flow with the blood of the nonbelievers.
"Maybe it's not my place to say that genocide would be better than a Sean Edmunds candidacy...
"... but at least genocide won't give you genital warts." (FADEOUT.) THIS AD WAS PAID FOR BY THE CONCERNED CITIZENS FOR ANTI-FOOTSTOOL PRESIDENTS AND MOTHERS AGAINST SEXUALLY TRANSMITTED DISEASES. PLEASE SMOKE CRACK RESPONSIBLY.
