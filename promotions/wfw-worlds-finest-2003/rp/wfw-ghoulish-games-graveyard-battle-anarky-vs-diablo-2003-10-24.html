WFW Ghoulish Games - Graveyard Battle - Anarky vs. Diablo

24 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 24 October 2003 This match is a Falls Count Anywhere in the Graveyard match, this is old match which dates back to NGEN and has a long history, do it proud. The RP/Angle dealine is Monday, November 3rd 11:59 PM. If all all possible, make your RP Halloween or Day of the Dead themed.
Cut your promos here. Diablo — 25 October 2003 (CUE UP: "Blackwater Park” by Opeth) (FADEIN: Exterior, dead of night. The place is a small park area near Spanish Harlem in New York, nothing fancy, just a few benches, a water fountain, some swings. The camera pans to one bench, and on it we see a figure, dressed in just a black NOFX t-shirt, even though it’s October in New York, and army green cargo pants, his auburn hair done up in a red bandana. He sits hunched forward, resting his elbows on his knees. We close in on his face, and our assumptions are affirmed, it is indeed Diablo.)
Diablo: Yes, I am called out here, into Abyss itself, so that I may confront it. There have been some set backs lately in our plans. Too many. But now the true obstacle is revealed. No longer am I confronted with a mere figurehead in Wylde, but the man who dares disrupt the feast. The world today is full of so much hatred, anger, and pain, it is all building up, and the very blackness of every man’s soul is beginning to bare itself. The Darkness must partake! So many new eligible hosts, but the plague so far has spread too little. The embers of Diablo Fuego must be stoked, re-ignited, so the flame can burn once more. Anarky…you are full to the brim with hate. It was just all too delicious to see your own darkness permeating every bit of your being as you handcuffed me to the rope and shouted in my face, telling me how better than me you are and everything, it was a sight to behold.
(Diablo gets up from his bench and begins walking around the park, illuminated by little more than the street lights.)
Diablo: At Ghoulish Games, we will be participating not in a match, but in a gruesome struggle. This is where you can let all of your anguish out. Let it overtake you...it will in the end anyway. In the dead of night, you will become consumed. The Blackness will engulf you, and it will all be your own doing. Your hate will flow like a life-giving stream, and I will draw strength from it. With every fury-strained breath you take, you shall crumble, piece…by…piece…
(Diablo rubs his fingers against his palms in a crumbling gesture and then blows away the imaginary refuse.)
Diablo: Another cinder left to sweep. Though the sun may rise the next day for the rest of them, you will not see its shine, nor feel its warm caress, for the Darkness shall cloud your heart, chill you to your core, and eclipse all hope. Long has it been since I have seen my last dusk, Anarky. The hours of my night never end, and there is no promise of sunrise. As twilight passes, and the scene is set for the great climax, there will be the dawning of a new, sunless day. On Halloween night, when the Darkness reigns supreme, and every man submits to its will, the onslaught shall begin. No longer will the Darkness deal with petty resistance. Nothing shall stand in its way. Anarky, you may have been a great hell-raiser in your time, you may have been the first, great hell-raiser, but come Ghoulish Games, I will make sure that [u]I am the last. ¡Yo será el último, y me voy a consagrar mi lugar con el fin del primero!
(Diablo stops walking and faces the camera fully) Diablo: In that graveyard, it shall be a convergence of all evil. The evil we both harbor, and the evil of every man that is released into that inky sky, like a back pollen to the wind, shall feed this Eternal Night, and the world will know it’s presence, and they will know the dominion it holds over each…and every heart! It will reap the seeds you have sown yourself, Anarky, in your own soul, and you will fall. As others before you have, and those after you will, to my Darkness. You will fall to…Último Diablo.

JLevinson — 25 October 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Oct-25-03 AT 04:10&nbsp;PM (EST)](FADEIN to a beautiful autumn afternoon in Bushnell Park, Hartford, Connecticut. A couple walk by, holding hands, watching as an old man feeds bread to some nearby pigeons. The camera pans up, towards an old, run-down building. Standing on a balcony is the man known only as Anarky, wearing his leather jacket, but devoid of the facepaint we normally see him in. A small smile stretches over his lips as he begins to speak... )
ANARKY: "Oh, Diablo... silly little Diablo. That was precious. For a moment there, I was whisked away into some kind of medieval frenzy of darkness and suffering there. You speak to me... to ME... of darkness. You speak to ME... of never seeing daylight again.
"You profess your ignorance with such zeal, Diablo. I wish I could be like you. To feel passionately about something like darkness. To still think I could change the world. To think that I could do anything that mattered.
"I'm starting to wonder, though, Diablo, if you've chosen the right profession. Perhaps you'd be more suited towards poetry? You certainly have a way with words. But you have not yet begun to even understand my hatred or my darkness."
(He pulls out a cigarette and a lighter, igniting the cigarette in one fell swoop and placing the lighter back in his pocket as he takes a long, deep drag... )
ANARKY: "You think me slapping you around like a little <BLEEP> is hatred? You think that you CAN EVEN BEGIN TO KNOW MY HATRED? No, Diablo... you have not yet begun to understand. You have not yet begun to feel. You have not begun to die the slow death we all die here...
"Evil. Darkness. Abyss. Such sad words wielded like weapons, Diablo... but you'll have to do better than that to strike fear into MY heart. For whatever part of me that was human once is now long gone. The only thing that's left, Diablo... is bigger than evil, or darkness, or this abyss you speak so eloquently of.
"That thing, Diablo... is a piece of my hatred... and a piece of my darkness. That thing is my addiction. My ceaseless agony that stretches out over time like the plague you know so little about.
"For the only moment when I am alive... the only moment WHEN I CAN FEEL ANYTHING AT ALL... is that moment in that ring... around that ring... when I can bring The Truth to those who deny it. When I can speak in the language of FORCE... because that is the only language people understand, Diablo. Pain and agony can lead us further than any bittersweet poetry you speak.
"You think you know of Anarky. You think you can read about it, or hear about it. You think you can watch videotape of it. But such a thing was not meant to be seen... but to be experienced. Such a thing, not meant to last, needs to be drawn from you.
"I have already fallen into a darkness you could know nothing about. You can teach me nothing. But I... Diablo... can teach you much. For you will learn about the chaos... about the anarchy... that screams out and from me in great, sweeping circles... and engulfs all who stand in its way.
"And when I stand over your blood-soaked body... and you beg me for a mercy that I could never give you... a peace which eludes even me... than you will know the Truth.
"And what is that Truth? That there is no Truth. There is no Darkness. There is no good or evil. There is only chaos...
"... and it will swallow us whole." (FADEOUT.) Diablo — 27 October 2003 (FADEIN: Último Diablo’s apartment. Bare as usual, except now there are some cheap drug store cotton cobwebs strung about, with plastic spiders on them, in the Halloween spirit. Último Diablo sits in the center of the room, covered by newspapers, with a large pumpkin in front of him with a knife in his hand.)
Último Diablo: (cracks a grin) Aha. I am absolutely elated! Now that I get a chance to see you really talk, Anarky, I just get to see the hatred pouring out of you. I admit, I was wrong as to just how polluted you’re psyche is, just how WROUGHT with hate you are. But now I see it in all it’s glory. I see it oozing between your teeth and flicked off your lips with every word you speak. I see it seeping out every single pore in your skin. It’s just too AWESOME a sight to behold! I want you to teach me of your hate, Anarky, teach me everything you can about just how you know nothing but pain, and sorrow, and anger…Último Diablo quiere aprender…
(Ú.D. is now smiling fully, as he plunges the knife in his hand deep into the top of the pumpkin, carving a circle around the stem.)
Último Diablo: Hmm. Yes. This pumpkin has a hard, thick exterior, but with a sharp enough tool, it is easily penetrable…
(Ú.D. successfully removes the top piece, and now tilts the open pumpkin on its side, scooping out the innards.)
Último Diablo: …and the thick husk certainly belies its soft inside. (takes a little bit of pumpkin flesh and puts it in his mouth.) Mmm. Tart, but with a certain sweetness about it. The pumpkin is such a versatile squash. You can use it’s husk for a nice decoration, and the insides make a most delicious pie…it’s sort of how things will turn out at Ghoulish Games, Anarky. It will be a wondorous scene...
(Ú.D. contemplatively twirls the knife in his hands, then rests it on the newspaper) Último Diablo: First I will cut you open…the exact method I have not decided, but it always happens one way or another in these kinds of matches…then I will scoop out all the delicious malevolence of your mind, every little bit, and it will make a most delectable meal. I still haven’t decided whether or not carving a toothless grin into your flesh would be taking it a step too far...
(Ú.D. chuckles a little to himself, but then assumes semi-serious expression.) Último Diablo: Teach me, Anarky. Just pour everything you can into this match. It will be simply more filling for the crust of my pie. Every drop of sweat that drips off your body, every drop of blood you bleed, and every tear you weep—and yes I will make SURE you weep—will be another crumb in the voracious belly of the Darkness. Oh yes, I'm pretty sure I will bleed, too, one way or another, and I will sweat, and I’m sure we will clasp our blood-drenched bodies in a bitter grapple all over that graveyard, but in the end, every little bit of your own darkness shall be sapped from you, swallowed, and digested, and we will grow stronger while you still struggle.
(Ú.D. crosses his legs and adjusts himself so he is facing towards the camera.) Último Diablo: There is a difference between our own darkness, Anarky. You describe yours as a “slow death”. Mine can only be described as a roaring flame. As it consumes both of us, you are only made frail, and you crumble, where I…I burn, furiously, and all I touch catches with my same flame. Your darkness is addiction, where mine is destruction. In that graveyard…perhaps we will both turn ourselves to little more than dust, but my fire will continue burning. No puedes extinguir… ¡Último Fuego!
(FTB as Último Diablo returns to carving the pumpkin) ------------------------------ "I blow your mind. You just blow." JLevinson — 28 October 2003 (FADEIN to a darkened graveyard. Thick fog covers the area, and in the center of a group of headstones stands Anarky, his arms crossed, his face covered in a black and white skull that's further illuminated by the moon above.)
ANARKY: "Pretty scary, huh? Got your gravestones. Got your fog. Got your moonlight. Of course... it's all bull<BLEEP>.
"The gravestones... " (He picks up one of the gravestones and snaps it in half, exposing it for painted styrofoam.)
ANARKY: "The fog... " (All of the machines click off at once and the fog disappates.) ANARKY: "Even the goddamned moonlight... " (Lights click on and the entire "graveyard" is shown to be a cheaply pulled together set. Anarky remains standing in the center, his painted face stretching into a strange grin.)
ANARKY: "Which brings us to YOU, Diablo. Mr. Lord of the Darkness. Mr. I'll Carve You Up Real Good. Mr. I Can't Even Stay Serious For Five <BLEEP>ing Minutes because I'm just Shawn Hart's Little <BLEEP>.
"Yeah, real <BLEEP>ing cute, Diablo. First, we get the twenty minute diatribe about how the darkness is going to consume us all. You go ON and ON and ON about how <BLEEP>ing EVIL you are, and how the sun will never rise again for me.
"Then... you have the audacity... to mock me... ? A man who speaks so eloquently about scooping the malevolence from my brain while simulataneously serving as the right-hand man of a group known as C.O.C.K.S.? And you mock me... ? Come now, Diablo..."
(His grin widens even more, into a simply maddening smile, his eyes glowing in anticipation.)
ANARKY: "You see, Diablo... this graveyard... it's a lot like you. It's an illusion. A facade. I expect you to understand what I'm talking about since you don't seem at a loss for big, scary words.
"You talk the talk. You speak in your big, grown-up scary voice. You make a lot of threats. You talk about how you're going to CUT ME. How you're going to MAKE ME BLEED. You talk, Diablo, about how your ROARING, FLAMING HATRED.
"Well all I can say is... "... I <BLEEP>ING HOPE SO. I'm sick and tired of any moron who can string a few sentences together suddenly thinks he's a tough guy. You want my respect, Diablo? Try doing this for TEN <BLEEP>ING YEARS, DIABLO. Don't sit here and wax poetic about how DARK and EVIL and SCARY you are. I've seen it all before, buddy. I've been gutted by bigger and scarier than you.
"And y'know what? I'm still here. And I'm still dangerous. And I'm still ready. And that's bigger than any glib interpretation of violence than you or I could ever make.
"Y'know what the difference between you and I, Diablo? The fundamental difference? I don't need to scare you. I don't need to impress you. I could give a flying <BLEEP> whether or not you think my hate is authentic or worrisome. You wanna mock me? Be my guest, pal.
"Because sooner or later... you WILL come face to face with me. And you WILL find... that this is not some <BLEEP>ing GAME, Diablo. I don't PLAY GAMES. I do what I do best. I hurt people. And I've been doing it for a long, long time... and I'm gonna keep doing it. And there ain't a damned thing you can do about it.
"But until we do, Diablo... until we meet in that ring for a war the likes of which you've never known... until you step up to me, face to face.. MAN TO MAN...
"... you can keep on talking. Because that's all it is. Talk. "And I'll be here. Waiting. Watching. "Hunting." (FADEOUT.) Diablo — 2 November 2003 (CUE UP: “Forest of October” by Opeth) (FADEIN: Último Diablo sits cross-legged on a rug in the center of his apartment, the Halloween decorations are still up, but the pumpkin, now with a typical jack-o-lantern grin, sits in a far corner.)
Último Diablo: As I sit here marveling at the specimen of raw, unadulterated emotion you are, Anarky, I see that perhaps all that hate built up in you has distorted your mind more than I would hope, because recent comments would show that you seem to think me as little more than a mouthpiece, spouting off hollow eloquences just for the sake of eloquence. As our match draws nearer, I think it is time to abandon floridity and just to make sure you know what it is you will be facing in that graveyard, and know why it is that ultimately I will be the only one who will be able to walk out of that graveyard with enough flesh still on their body.
(U.D. adjusts himself on the rug so that he is facing the camera) Último Diablo: Now, I know you’re knew here to these parts, and this match will be quite the fitting introduction, but just in case you didn’t know, Último Diablo has dealt his share of suffering to his opponents.
(U.D. raises his hands in front of his face demonstratively) Último Diablo: These two hands of mine? Yes, they may show the results of millennia of evolution in their opposable digits, but in the heat of battle, they become little more than tools of carnage. My hands will do nothing other than inflict pain on whatever they can touch, after the first bell rings, and before the last bell rings, in between, and maybe outside. You have all seen, Último Fuego has welled up inside of me, and I have spat it into the faces of other human beings. People with living souls. People who can feel, love, hate, want, and need. People who may have family, wives, children, sisters, brothers, but the Darkness will never hold its hand, MY hands. I have sent bodies crashing through tables, through flames, and watched their bodies fall limp as their heads came crashing down onto cold, unforgiving concrete. I had taken the bright, rising star of Andre Eian in my hands, and extinguished it, never questioning if I should. I may speak well, Anarky, but my hands possess a vocabulary of agony that is far, FAR more profound. They will speak volumes as they flail, slap, and tear at you until you can bear no more, and you will be they're greatest audience.
(U.D. Stands up as the camera follows, as he wrings his hands and stares into the camera.) Último Diablo: I will open your eyes, Anarky, to the undeniable reality of the Darkness, and of Último Fuego. You may have been all across this whole wide world, putting your life on the line for 10 years, but the Darkness does not bother measuring its time. It is eternal, and it is inescapable. These hands of mine will show you the same world of torment that it has shown everyone else, and maybe a little more. As we whisk each other just that much faster to Death’s doorstep, you will know. You will know the Darkness, and you will know Último Fuego, and what is real to you, and what is an illusion to you…will become one...and the same.
(FTB) ------------------------------ "I blow your mind. You just blow." JLevinson — 4 November 2003 (FADEIN to a balcony in an urban sprawl. None other than Anarky stands at the edge, a lit cigarette in hand, staring down vacantly as people walk below, chattering away mindlessly. His moderately long mop of hair hangs over his face and he takes a long, slow drag of his cigarette before he throws it down below and exhales into the dark night air.)
ANARKY: "You've got your eyes open, Diablo. But you don't see me. You're looking right at me... but you don't see me at all. You only see what you want to see. You only see another victim. Another future carcass that bends to your ever-enforcing will.
"Just a mouthpiece, Diablo? Did I say that you were just a mouthpiece? Or maybe you've got us all mixed up. I'M not the one guaranteeing victory. I'M not the one making poetic threats. You want to talk about weapons, Diablo?
"You think you'll just walk away from the graveyard, Diablo? Such a little boy. Such a pathetic, ignorant little creature you are. You don't know the first thing about me, for if you did, you wouldn't make such threats towards me.
"Win or lose, Diablo... victory or defeat... you will NOT just WALK AWAY from me. You will taste something new. And you will come to find... that I am not the young, rising star of Andre Eian. You want names, Diablo? I've got names. I can sit here and list names until the sun sets and rises again, Diablo. But I don't need to. Do I care if you're impressed? If you're scared?
"Hell no." (He stops leaning and stands up. He grabs what appears to be a water balloon off of a chair and drops it over the railing. A moment later, we hear a splash and a young women squeal. He only smiles... )
ANARKY: "Did you see, Diablo? Did you see how with just the flick of wrist, I ruined that woman's night? It's that easy, Diablo. One move... one gesture... and everything changes.
"You think you're going to beat me. You have confidence. You have vision in your own Darkness. In a sense, I admire you. I admire you because I used to be like you. I used to think I could simply tear through the ranks... spreading evil and darkness as I did.
"But then I realized, Diablo... that I am nothing but the Harbinger of Chaos. It would exist with or without me. I am simply the byproduct.
"But that doesn't change anything. I'm still here. And I still can't just walk away. Every bone that crunches... every drop of blood that spills... every sweet scream of agony... that fills my hole, Diablo. It makes me human again. I don't have to feel so <BLEEP>ING NUMB ANYMORE."
(He calms himself again and speaks quietly and distinctly.) ANARKY: "You think you've seen emotion, Diablo? You've only seen what I've shown you. You've seen a man talking and walking and breathing. You've seen the gaping emptiness. You've seen the lack of a man.
"But you WILL see, Diablo. You will know my frustration and my ceaseless suffering. You will come to know firsthand... that there only guarantee when you get into the ring with Anarky... is chaos.
"Can you walk into the face of chaos, Diablo, and come out unscathed? Can you simply crush me with your hands and will alone? Can you do what none other has done before you, and finally fill me up?
"Because Diablo... when that bell rings... when we rise and strike... we will not be so different. We will be one. One dancing and rising and destroying beast. One mass of hatred and bloodlust. One... whole... thing........
"... and as I stand above you... soaked in your own blood... and you scream my name, for mercy... for death... I will finally... be alive.
"Until then, Diablo... I must wait. I must suffer. "Come to me, Diablo... bring me the darkness... for I shall swallow it whole." (FADEOUT.)
