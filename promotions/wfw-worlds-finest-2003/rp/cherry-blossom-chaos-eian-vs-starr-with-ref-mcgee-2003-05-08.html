DarkReign666 — 9 May 2003 Fade In: ‘Mr. North America’ Johnny Starr is sitting in his classy dressing room and sits on a black leather couch watching the celebration of himself defeating Chris Bradford. Starr is dressed in a white silk robe with the lettering written on the back that reads in red ‘Mr. North America’. He also still wears his red white, and blue American Wrestling mask and boots to go with it. There is a small kitchen in the back of the room and you can hear quietly a microwave cooking some popcorn in the distance.
SNDFX: ’DING’ Chris Bradford one half of their tag team of 24/7 comes walking in with a bowl of popcorn and offers some to Johnny Starr....
Bradford: ‘Want some?’ Starr: ‘Na, I’m too excited to even think about eating anything’. ‘Chris I want you to know this win over you at The Gates Of Hell has nothing to do with our friendship’. ‘As far as I’m concerned we are still 24/7!!’ ‘I’m looking back at this tape here because I’m reading how well the fans stand behind Mr. North America’. ‘It’s such a great feeling walking into an arena and you have thousands of fans screaming out your name’. ‘I can’t describe it’.
Bradford: ‘It’s cool man’. ‘You deserve it’. ‘It was my pleasure Wrestling Mr. North America’. ‘However if you do get really far in this North American Title hunt, I want a first crack at that Title’. ‘Chris Bradford won’t let you off the hook that easily’. ‘I deserve a shot’.
Starr: ‘Anytime, it’ll be a privilege’. ‘But now let’s look at my opponent at Cherry Blossom Chaos’. ’He doesn’t even compare to Chris Bradford’. ’Look at this guy’. ’Someone tell him don’t quit his day job’. ’It’s a coincidence that Mr. North America has a match in Washington D.C.’ ’What better place but to start this North American Title hunt’. ’It’s home of the brave, home of the President of the United States’. ’It has such great land marks’. ’Such as the Washington Memorial’. ’Where one former great President of Abraham Lincoln’s image remains forever’. ’It hosts the home of the U.S. President the big beautiful White House’. ’It holds the Pentagon, and the headquarters of the FBI’.
‘But most of all Bradford, it’s going to be the start of Mr. North America’s trial and era of becoming the nations Champion’. ’They throw Eian at me Chris’. ‘He’ll be a piece of cake for he’s no match for Mr. North America’. ‘We beat them before as 24/7 and I’ll beat him at Cherry Blossom Chaos!!’ ‘He has no great background such as I’. ‘He is not an American Idol’. ‘He doesn’t have thousands of screaming fans calling his name’. ‘He is not the icon of WFW’.
Bradford: ‘What kind of name is this for a Show?’ ‘Cherry Blossom Chaos?’ ‘It reminds me of a time when I popped a high school girl’s cherry’.
Starr: ( Hahahah ) ‘For real’. ‘Speaking of real, guys don’t you think you can come up with better ring music for me?’ ‘For now on I want the ring crew to use... ‘Through The Fire by Larry Greene’. ‘It fits Mr. North America’s image’. ‘I’m not no 60 year old has been with a blonde mustache and a bald head’. ’I don’t use Real American’. ’Don’t ever put me in the same ranks as that dinosaur’. ’Heck Mr. North America is going to bigger’. ’My name will travel across the nation!!’ ’The only thing you got going for you Eian is the fact that WFW put you in the same ring with me’. ’You should be lucky’. ’Mr. North America will make you famous’.
‘A new icon in WFW is born, a new era is here, most of all, Mr. North America Johnny Starr is here!!’ ’Why?’ ’Because I’m the star!’ ’I’m the star!’
<<<<<FADE TO BLACK>>>>>> andreshadow — 18 May 2003 Fade In: Washington DC, Lincoln Memorial. The Shadow is walking directly in front of the prestigious Lincoln Memorial. He is completely decked out in black (including trench coat), despite the sharp sunlight. He turns to the camera and the light reflects off his sunglasses directly into the camera. He comes closer to said apparatus, and removes his glasses. His eyes are bloodshot. His goatee is unkempt and his hair is flowing freely in the wind. He turns and laughs.
Ah, the WFW. It's been a while since I've given a **** about doing some promos for you people. But of course, as with all feds you guys ave the bloodhound gang of camera men hunting me down. Nothing less to expect, I suppose. But hey, it's a beatiful day, my daugher just turned a year old, and life is in general very nice for "The Shadow". So I don't mind much.
He spins in a circle, almost gleefully. His coat billows out, revealing the red 'X' on his shirt. He stops and turns to the camera.
And what is it now, huh? Come for some biting commentary on my US title match with Johnny Starr? "Mr. North America"? Ha! What is there to say? All that can be said and done will be said and done when it happens.
Johnny Starr. Mr. North America. Half of '24/7'. Should I be impressed? Should I forfeit in fear? Your musings with your buddy Bradford have done nothing but further my objective to win our match at Cherry Blossom Chaos. And while I may not have some half-assed tag partner to watch my back, I am almost certain that I won't need it. Sure, my record may be less than stellar, but I am not deterred by faliure. I am driven by it. The last time we had a show, whatever it was called, I successfully beat the hell out of Luster the SoCal man whore. I am more than willing to beat up 'Mr. North America' himself. You see, while you may be counting on the fans for support, I do not. When it all comes down to it, the fans won't help you. Only your own ability can do that...
Suddenly he takes off up the stairs to the memorial itself. The camera follows. Eian bursts through a crowd of German tourists to stop at the feet of Lincoln himself, so to speak. The tour guides are yelling at him, but that does not deter him from inexplicably climbing the statue. The camera follows up the stairs, and zooms in on Eian, who stands in Lincoln's lap, arms crossed.
When it all comes down to it, Starr, you're welcome to be Mr. North America. A self proclaimed moniker is something everyone is entiteled to. Wether you are, in fact, the true human reincarnation of North America stands to be seen, but you're welcome to have it. Me, I'm perfectly content being The Shadow. I'm perfectly content in being a crass bastard. I'm perfectly content in pissing on people if I feel so inclined.
He turns to the face of Lincoln and flips it off, then leaps off the statue. He lands hard on the ground, then raises himself to his full height. Several German people run away in semi-fear of the screaming man. A few security guards run up the stairs to observe Eian from afar. He smiles and puts his sunglasses on.
What did I do now? The security guards close in, speaking softly. One of them come within arm's reach of Eian. He lunges out, but Eian grabs his hand, pulls him off balance and drives his elbow directly into the man's neck. He falls down in a heap. Eian smiles. Another guard lunges at him, and Eian dodges him as well. This one gets a knee to the gut for his trouble. The last of the guards approaches.
Tough guy, eh? Eian smiles, but doesn't move at all. He stands there rigidly, as the cop circles him. Suddenly, without warning, he launches a vicious Savate (spin) kick to the gut of the guard. The guard doubles over in pain. Eian laughs and jumps up with a knee directly into his face. The guard screams in pain and falls to the ground bleeding from the nose.
Starr, be careful what you wish for. It may not be exactly what you expect. I'll be seeing you real soon...
He walks off into the distance as the camera focuses on the groaning cops on the ground. Fade to black...
DarkReign666 — 22 May 2003 Fade In: A short video is shown before we cut to the promo of ‘Mr. North America’ , Johnny Starr. Starr is seen somewhere climbing a gigantic mountain somewhere in the Appalachian Mountains. The wind is gusting a good 30 to 40 miles per hour and the snow drifts from the peaks are blowing harshly into the mask and face of ‘Mr. North America’. Starr is wearing climbing gear along with a pair of thick jogging pants, sneakers, and a jogging shirt with a hood dangling from the back. As he struggles to the top he also holds the greatest thing a person could carry on such an obstacle. He holds the American Flag in his right hand and uses all his might and muscle to climb to the top of this mountain.
His music is being played in the background as the camera zooms in to capture his journey to the top. Finally the moment has come as he nears the top he places the American flag to the side as he gets up on his knees catching his breath. He rises to his feet and praises his adventure and places the flag and buries the pole deep into the ground of the mountain. He raises his arms in the air in glory and yells out.... ‘I’M ON TOP OF THE WORLD!!!!!’
“End Video clip” Cutto:> ‘Mr. North America’ is now seen in Washington D.C. carrying another American Flag in his right hand as he wears casual street clothes this time. As he walks around town he wears a light color pair of denim jeans and a white muscle shirt with a little American Flag stitched to the right side of the shirt. Along side of Starr is Chris Bradford his friend and Tag Team Partner of 24/7. They both walk side by side touring the great scenery of the providence of D.C.
As they pass the small lake of the tall monument they stop and embrace the precious awe of America.....
Bradford> That was a great video Starr. How long did it take you to climb that mountain? Starr> Not long Chris. As a matter of fact it would take me as long as it takes to achieve any goal such as climbing a gigantic mountain. When “Mr. North America” was born in the WFW he was destined for conquering greatness. He will go any lengths to get where he has to go. For example Chris, take this North American Title. I will go any distance, I shall overcome any obstacle. They can throw rain, snow, thunder and lightning at me and I’ll still come crawling to get breathe in that glory. If you put your mind to it Chris you can achieve anything.
Bradford:> I know your not too worried about this Eian charecter, how about referee Mcgee? Starr:> Same thing, I’m not too worried about this guy. Long as he stays out of my way there should be no problems. He does not want to get in the way of a star! Not only am I going to win this Title Chris, I’m winning it for the great fans. I’m doing for the people who never thought of coming as far as I have. It’s for that taste of victory everyone thirsts for.
I bring something new to the fans of WFW. I bring passion, I bring pride, and I bring glory! All the thoughts and prayers will help over come beating Eian. It’s keeping in shape and taking my vitamins that will get me over this match. I mean what goes better with the name of this Title? Eian or “Mr. North America” Johnny Starr!!!!
Bradford:> I have to choose? Starr:> No you don’t it’s all about fate Chris. It’s about destiny. You bet your A** nothing will interfere with my fate or destiny of becoming North American Champion!! Why? Because I’m the star! I’m the star!
<<<<<FADE TO BLACK>>>>>> andreshadow — 22 May 2003 We fade in to a cafe somewhere in Washington, DC. It's a beautiful day outside. "The Shadow" sits by a small table, dressed in all black, per usual. He's reading a paper and sipping coffe from a small coffee cup. The sun shines brightly off his reflective sunglasses. He notices the camera and puts down the cup. He folds his paper and crosses his legs. He smiles.
Aaaah, my personal Papparazzi. What a great fringe benefit it is to have people interrupt my afternoon coffee for an interview. I feel so special.
He sets his paper on the table. He grabs the cup and gulps down the remainder of the contents. He takes a $10 out of his pocket and sets it under the coffee saucer. He grabs his coat from the back of the chair, swings it around and puts it on. He walks up to the camera.
Walk with me, will you? He starts to walk down the street, and the camera follows. You know, I have to wonder. Every once in a while, when I actually do come out of the woodwork for a promo, my opponent takes it as an instigation to retaliate, and as per usual, Starr is no different. Starr, my last little "interview" was a rhetorical one": it was not meant to be answered. But again, I'm forced by these lunks at HQ to do another promo as a retaliatory statement. Well, whee, man, whoop-de-doo! You've got me now.
He takes a pack of sigarettes out of his pocket. He takes a "cancer-stick" out, sticks it in his mouth and lights it. He takes a long drag, followed by an exhale. He continues.
'Mr. North America' Johnny Starr. Aah, what a pleasure it will be to shove this propaganda down your throat. I'm getting sick of it. Please, stop your self centered ramblings so I can retaliate with something half-humorous, instead of this tripe I spew as we speak. By the time I'm done with you, you'll be teaching mountain climbing for the handicapped in northern Wyoming. I'm sure you won't be so happy to be 'North American' then. Because, even if this continent is blessed with the occasional great beauty, the absolute sh*t fgreatly outnumbers it, and only when you live in the middle class will you figure this out. But hey, I'm not here to torture you economically. Only our esteemed referee can do that...
He takes another drag of his cigarette. Speaking of, how are you, Mike? It's been a while since I've seen your ugly mug plastered on my TV, but I can't say I haven't missed you. While you may be the most boring man this side of Ben Stein, I have to give you credit for, well, being, uh...
F*ck, I don't know what to give you credit for. You haven't done anything of note in a while, so I really have no comment. Except for this: stay the hell away from me.
He discards his cigarette. Again back to Starr. As I've mentioned before, Starr, I hope your little buddy Bradford doesn't get involved. I mean, I don't want to have to hurt him later. In fact, I'm getting mighty tired of both of you. Therefore, I issue a little challenge, if I may. Next show, whatever it may be, I want a match with Chris Bradford. And hey, if I beat Starr, then I might as well try and go 2-0 over 24/7...
He turns the corner sharply. He looks back at the camera. Now if you'll excuse me, I have to go piss on the White House lawn... He smiles michieviously. The camera pans up to a road sign marked 'Pennsylvania Ave'. The screen fades to black...
