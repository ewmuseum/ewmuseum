BOMB Voyage: Anarky vs. Iron Lion

23 May 2004

JABolich — 24 May 2004 (FADEIN: The deck of the cruise ship that will play host to WFW's BOMB Voyage show. Standing at the very bow of the ship is the man known as IRON LION. In contrast to his typical ring attire he wears a pineapple Hawaiian shirt and a pair of white slacks. His forehead is bandaged. The camera views him as if from over his shoulder, catching his face in profile. His golden hair ripples in an errant breeze as he gazes out over the water.)
IRON LION: Such an ironic choice of venue. For some, a cruise to Hawaii is a little slice of paradise. For the soldiers of LOVE, however, this voyage is a much bigger slice of hell.
Anarky. Prior to Shock And Awe, I told you to do your worst to me, but ultimately it wouldn't matter because I would continue to confront you and your pack of hoodlums until you surrendered or fell. Do you know what has changed since Shock and Awe?
Absolutely nothing. Well, save for one thing. After Shock And Awe, I am more determined than ever to bring you to your knees. With Scotty Michaels out of action, I chose to confront you alone, because I knew full well that I had the strength to destroy both of you. And I did, didn't I? Despite being outnumbered two to one I manhandled both of you. In fact, if you'll recall I had the match one after destroying Jared Wells with the Pride Hammer. Yes, your cronies did indeed deny me the privilege of securing the pinfall when they forced a disqualification, but the fact remains - I confronted two of LOVE's finest soldiers alone and PREVAILED.
That night, the world saw for itself that the power of pride, strength, and honor was greater than lies, oppression, and evil. That night, they saw me prove that I am not to be taken lightly.
Thus begins the war, Anarky - the war of Iron Lion to dismantle LOVE man by man. I bring no army with me, though those who wish to join me are welcome to do so. Regardless, I lean on no other. As at Shock And Awe, I will confront LOVE with my own skills and my own strength. I am not weak enough to have a band of rabble-rousers at ringside to rescue me from defeat.
It starts with you, Anarky. Last week you failed to defeat me even with Wells at your side. This week I will ensure that you fail again. The war has begun, and you, Anarky, are the first in the line of fire. Perhaps next week it will be Psycho, or Felix Red, or Wells and the fool's gold he passes off as a legitimate championship. Regardless, I will confront every man of you and bring you all down. And as I said at Shock And Awe, I WILL NOT STOP UNTIL YOU ARE DEFEATED.
Prepare yourself, because at BOMB Voyage I am the iceberg. Like it or not, LOVE is inevitably cast in the role of the Titanic.
Hope you can swim. (FADEOUT) JLevinson — 24 May 2004 (FADEIN to the cruise ship at night, where Anarky is walking quietly, smoking a cigarette. He flicks it off the side of the ship and walks into a room...
He stops. Sitting in the room are three men. One of them is apparently a Mexican with a giant sombrero, his feet up on a table, snoring loudly.
The next is apparently a Russian with a big fur hat, face down on the table, a half-empty bottle of vodka sitting next to him.
Lastly is an Asian, sitting in a corner and furiously punching numbers into a calculator, occassionally writing things down.)
ANARKY: "What the hell... " (The Mexican wakes up and looks around, finally speaking with a heavy accent.) MEXICAN: "Que pasa, gringo? Whatchu need?" ANARKY: "Where the hell am I?" MEXICAN: "This is the Stereotype Room, compradre." ANARKY: "I... took too much peyote again, didn't I?" MEXICAN: "No soy, amigo... I'm just a lazy Mexicano... I no know what you mean... " ANARKY: "So I guess you're not a U.S. citizen, then, hm?" MEXICAN: "Nah. I'd have to pay taxes and get a job. That's not the Mexican way! And I can only be the way of the Mexican Stereotype."
ANARKY: "Kinda like Iron Lion is the stereotype of the lionhearted man full of pride, strength and honor?"
MEXICAN: "Si." (The Mexican goes back to sleep. Anarky walks over to the Russian and prods him, but the man can only speak gibberish. Finally, 'Nark walks over to the Asian and taps him on the shoulder. The Asian ignores him for a minute and then turns around, looking annoyed.)
ANARKY: "Hey, uhhh... did I OD?" ASIAN: "Of course not. You're just living in Iron Lion's fantasy world." ANARKY: "Oh. Why didn't I think of that?" ASIAN: "'Cause you are uneducated American slob. You no worry. I have done calculations to help you."
ANARKY: "Oh yeah? And what'd you figure out?" ASIAN: "According to these numbers... Iron Lion is a Care Bear." (Anarky just stares at him.) ASIAN: "Is possible, you see. May have tattoo on chest. Shoot positive feelings at you. You have no defense against goodness."
(Silence.) ASIAN: "I show you." (The Asian jumps up and rips open his shirt, revealing a giant tattoo of a heart on his chest. He pushes his chest forward and yells.)
ASIAN: "CARE BEAR STAAAAAAAAAAARE!!!!!" (Nothing happens. There are a few moments of silence and a tumbleweed floats by. Anarky slowly backs away and out of the room as the Asian breathes heavily, his chest pushed out.
Anarky walks back outside, and everything seems normal.) ANARKY: "Well it appears everything is back to normal, Lion. Good thing. I can't live in that world of yours.
"In your world, everything makes perfect sense... to you. In your world, you beat LOVE. In your world, you attained victory.
"Well if that's your version if victory, Lion, you can have it. Lying in a pool of your own blood. Unable to fend for yourself. Oh you can have it, Lion. You can have that victory every day. You can even call me at home, Lion, and I'll deliver it to your doorstop. I'm a nice guy like that.
"Or, Lion... you could open up your eyes and see things for what they are. You didn't beat me. You bored me. There's a difference. All I wanted was Scotty. But where's Scotty? He went and got himself suspended 'cause he KNEW what he'd done. He KNEW that he'd bitten off more than he could chew.
"So he ran home, crying like a little girl who had his Barbie taken away. "Fine. That leaves you, Lion. That leaves you and me. "But you don't get it. You think you struck the first blow. You're wrong, Lion. You didn't do anything. I could've saved Wells myself. And eventually, Lion... you would've fallen. But like I said, I was bored, and why be selfish when I can share the LOVE? My fellow partners Felix Red and Psycho were MORE than willing to come in and lend a hand in bleeding you like a stuck pig. They enjoy it. As do I.
"I struck the first blow when I laid you out. And now I'm going to strike you again. But this time, it won't be at your body. It'll be at your spirit. It'll be at your will.
"I know you don't think I can do such a thing, Lion. But you haven't begun to know who I am. You haven't begun to suffer.
"You want to know what I'm capable of, Lion? Go ask Stone Wolf. I know you don't know the name. And maybe if I'm feeling especially gregarious later, I'll tell you.
"But for now, I'll let you figure it out. "After all, you've got it all figured out, already, right? "CARE BEAR STAAAAAAAAAAAAAARE!!!!" (FADEOUT to a wild grin.) JABolich — 29 May 2004 (OORP: I apologize for my slow response. Been a busy week at work.) ------------------------- (FADEIN: The upper deck of the cruise ship. In the background is a large pool, frequented by several bathing costume-clad revelers and women in bikinis. The focus of the shot, however, is on the massive IRON LION, who sits in a chair in the center of the shot. Once again he wears his Hawaiian shirt, a concession to the casual atmosphere of the cruise liner. His face, however, is far from casual.)
IRON LION: You have a very narrow mind, Anarky. Apparently you and I have different concepts of what victory means. You'd have to be blind not to see that Shock And Awe was in fact a triumph for me - and a failure to you. Whether you admit it or not, I outclassed both you and Wells without the need for a partner. Yet you HAVE admitted it. The minute you summoned Psycho and Felix Red to save your tail, you admitted that I was so much stronger than you and Wells that you couldn't beat me without playing the numbers game.
In that moment, Anarky, you showed the world that deep down you fear me and what I am capable of. You showed the world that you KNEW you couldn't face me alone, or even with Wells at your side. Moreover, you showed that I was right all along.
YOU. ARE. WEAK. No amount of post-match bloodletting will defeat me, Anarky, nor will it change the fact that you couldn't handle the tandem of pride, strength, and honor. It will also not change the fact that I got up from your little beating rather quickly. I would've expected four men to do a more thorough job, but apparently you and your minions are incompetent as well as corrupt.
If you could've saved Wells yourself, Anarky, exactly WHY did you not do it? Because you were bored, you say? I say because you knew it was inevitable. I saw your face, Anarky, and I saw the understanding in your eyes. You experienced my fury firsthand, and you knew that you were overwhelmed. But like any weak-minded fool, rather than fight your own battles you summoned your mercenaries. I expected no less.
You claim that I'm the one living a fantasy? I say you need to find a mirror and look into it, because from where I stand YOU are the one swathed in illusion. You hide your fear by feigning boredom. You hide your incompetence by feigning vengeance. But when you get down to it, the truth is that you knew full well that I defeated you. Your little Care Bear act is simply another thread in your web of self-delusion, woven to hide your eyes from truths you find unpleasant.
At BOMB Voyage, the web is cast aside. Struggle as you will, but like it or not I will force you to experience the truth - I defeated you at Shock And Awe, and I will do so again. You could not stand against me with Wells at your side. How will you fare without your sycophant, hm?
As I said when last I spoke, the war has begun. Already the army of one has shown itself superior on the battlefield to the forces of evil, and despite the use of terror tactics I remain undaunted. Why? Because I have the strength within, and that strength will drive me to victory no matter what stands in my way. How, Anarky, will you counter my inner strength when all that you have within is weakness and doubt?
Bask in your illusory world while you can, because at BOMB Voyage, I will open your eyes. Whether you can handle what you will see is a choice I leave to you. What's your f*cking rank, soldier?" ANARKY: "Uhhhh... aren't you swearing a little bit much for a little girl? How old are you? Ten? Eleven?"

No, the real problem was, what was I going to do with the hostages? I could kill them, but murder is messy, and I'm supposed to be on vacation. I guess I could get the maid to clean it up, but then I'd have to tip her, and I'd rather just kill her instead of tipping her.

WELLS: "Yo, Narkenmeister, what's up bi*tch?" ANARKY: "Sh*t man, get down. There's red everywhere. You stand up for another couple of seconds and you're gonna get picked off. Jesus Christ, man, I'M SERIOUS, GET DOWN."
(Wells falls over a lounge chair.) ANARKY: "How much have you had to drink?" WELLS: "Not too much." (Wells' turns his head away and projectile vomits a stream of red fluid into the pool, which spreads out and contiminates it at an impressive speed. He wipes his mouth and turns back.
WELLS: "You were saying?" ANARKY: "That... was sick." WELLS: "What?" ANARKY: "What you just did." WELLS: "What?" (Wells turns around again and projectile vomits into the pool once again.) ANARKY: "You just did it again!" WELLS: "Dude, you're starting to creep me out. I'm gonna go find Jenna and get me a piece of ass."

(FADEIN to a dark hallway where Anarky is leaning up against the wall, sweating profusely. His camouflage facepaint has run and is now a grotesque mask of dark greens, browns and blacks. He's wearing a Marilyn Manson t-shirt and appears to be cutting himself.)

GOTH KID: "You don't recognize me?" ANARKY: "No." GOTH KID: "I'm you." ANARKY: "No... I'm me. You're some f*cking goth kid who likes to cut himself." GOTH KID: "Exactly... you. To the 'T.'" You get others to do what you cannot bring yourself to do."

GOTH KID: "And what about you, Goth Monster? You think you're original? You think this is all new and fun? The self-inflicted addiction? The love of all that is destruction and chaos? I could've come up with a better supervillain when I was 12. You should've, too."

ANARKY: "Yeah, but even if I beat him, it wouldn't have meant anything. A two on one victory? F*ck that. That was a lose-lose situation. If I win, then f*ck it, I was supposed to win. If I lose, then I'm a nobody. So what was the point? I could've taken him."
GOTH KID: "Could you?" ANARKY: "I can take anyone." GOTH KID: "Then prove it." ANARKY: "I don't owe you proof. I don't owe anyone proof. I know who I am. I know what I'm capable of."
GOTH KID: "But they've forgotten. The man who destroyed Maelstrom... and he can't even fight Lion on his own."
ANARKY: "F*ck you." GOTH KID: "Exactly. F*ck me and f*ck you, too. You're what I'm supposed to be? F*ck that. I should just kill myself now."
ANARKY: "Do it, then. End this charade. I don't need this sh*t." GOTH KID: "That's just like you... and me... to take the easy way out." (The kid stands up and pulls out a revolver and points it at his head. His finger begins to depress the trigger and a loud *BANG* can be heard.
(CUTTO: The clock again spinning, another hour.) (FADEIN to Anarky's room again, still destroyed. He is slumped over in a corner, and his sweat-laden hair is hanging over his face, which is still partially covered in camouflage. He's breathing heavily. Each wall is animated with an identical scene showing his match with Maelstrom. They go back and forth, destroying each other.)
ANARKY "War, Lion... you throw the word around as if it meant nothing. War. Terror. Evil. You sound like Dubya. The evil LOVE terrorist cells must be stopped, hm? Is that it? Your big solution?
"F*ck you, Lion. You didn't beat me. You didn't pin my shoulders to the mat. You didn't make me scream for mercy. No, Lion... all you did was lie there and get pummeled. All you did was bleed.
"Is that your grand proof? That I needed help to beat you down? How sad for you, Lion. Grasping for straws. Looking for anything you can hold onto. I can't say I blame you. But that doesn't matter now.
"For the field is evened, and that's exactly how I want it. I didn't want to beat you in a handicap match, Lion. Because that sh*t's for cowards, and I'm no coward. I never ran from a fight, Lion. I never turned my back in a challenge. And I'm not going to start now.

"And ultimately, Lion... it doesn't matter what you believe. Because I need you. And you need me. We are... connected in this dance.
"For without something evil... you have nothing to fight for. No cause. No hope. Just pointless destruction. And then you'd be just like me.

It's like a drug, Lion... because no matter how good that drug is... you'll always get used to it. And you'll need more and more... until you can't live without it. Until you're nothing...
"I've already been destroyed, Lion. I've already been beaten and battered and bruised. I've been robbed of glory and spit on in disrespect.
"The line has been crossed. There's no going back now. Whatever human part left of me is long gone now... whatever that's left is something more or less... animal... or evil... or nothing. It doesn't matter.
"You can't beat me, Lion. Because I'm already beaten and broken and destroyed. You can't stop LOVE because LOVE is more than just a couple of guys gang beating people. All you can hope for...
"... is three sweet seconds... three moments of respite... that may deliver you from your illusions... that may deliver you from LOVE.
"You have three seconds to stop me, Lion. "Then you're mine. From now until eternity." (FADEOUT.)
