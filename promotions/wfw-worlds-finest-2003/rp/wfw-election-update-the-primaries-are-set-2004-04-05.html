It is our goal to complete the double whammy of Wylde's career. First he will lose the WFW World Title to Michael Manson .. then he will lose the Presidency to me. (FADEOUT: To the words....) DON'T BE HESITANT ... VOTE EDMUNDS FOR PRESIDENT! JABolich — 9 April 2004 (FADEIN: A conference room somewhere. IRON LION stands behind a podium, dwarfing the hapless chunk of furniture. He actually looks reasonably dignified in a brown sports coat and trousers.)
Iron Lion: As most of you know, the primaries for the WFW presidential elections are coming up, and I have chosen to run for the position. Thus, I offer this campaign speech.
I could follow in the footsteps of Sean Edmunds and take the low road by running down my opponents one by one, but I'd rather not insult the intelligence of everyone here with such banter. If Edmunds wants to implicate something as meaningless as my loss in the So Cal Rumble into his campaign speech, that's fine - but considering his loss record against Jean Rabesque, he has no room to talk. Rather, I intend to speak on the plans I have should I be chosen for the office of President.
It seems that during these days, the latter hours of the tenure of Alex Wylde, the stability of WFW has begun to deteriorate. We have two violent groups running rampant throughout the promotion, beating people indiscriminately and getting involved in matches which they have no business interfering in. In response to such shenanigans, I intend to enact several security measures that will deter the actions of violent groups, including increasing the amount of security guards backstage and placing a second referee at ringside to take over officiating immediately in the event that the first official is knocked out in the line of duty.
As you know by now, I am a fair and honest man. Should I be elected, there will be no political favors or discrimination. I will treat all employees of WFW as equals under promotion law and guidelines. There will, of course, be no diplomatic immunity. As well, I will offer cash incentives to those who aid the promotion by turning in those seeking to circumvent the rules.
Above all, I urge you to cast your vote in my favor in the interest of giving World's Finest Wrestling a bold, honest, and honorable leader. For too long has mischief run rampant. Should you see fit to elect me President, I will do everything in my power to bring stability back to WFW and ensure a safe, secure, and above all REWARDING working environment for everyone.
Thank you. (LION steps down from the podium as we fade to black. Bold text appears on the screen:) A VOTE FOR IRON LION IS A VOTE FOR WFW. LION has pulled his flowing golden hair back into a ponytail and traded in yesterday's attire for a grey sport coat and slacks. Several reporters are clustered about the area.)
Iron Lion: Ladies and gentlemen, thank you for coming to this press conference. Given the accusations raised by one Sean Edmunds, I thought it best to put forth a response.
Mr. Edmunds claims that my campaign will deny wrestlers their freedom, but that couldn't be further from the truth. If I wanted to deny wrestlers freedom, I would remove ALL security and allow the federation to fall to anarchy. How free can you be when you're being beaten by three or four men every time you turn around? The reforms I plan to implement will allow wrestlers the freedom to come to work secure in the knowledge that they are safe from harm. As for Manson's... carnival behaviour, I see no problem with it unless people are hurt by it. I advocate safety in the workplace, not iron-fisted suppression of one's behaviour.
Sean Edmunds sees only what he wishes to see in things. He fails to see the many benefits of my plans for security and safety here in WFW. Further, he continues to insist that I lost exclusively to El Arco Iris in the So Cal Rumble despite the fact that it took six men working in tandem to push me over the top rope. His assuming that I lost to one-sixth of that group would be like me saying that Edmunds lost eleven times to Jean Rabesque's left leg. And I don't know about you, Sean, but I would rather lose one battle royal to six men than lose eleven traditional matches to one man.
I ask you this. Do you REALLY want to give the Presidency to a man like Sean Edmunds, who uses his press conferences as a venue for meaningless rants about Oprah Winfrey? Do you REALLY want to elect a man like Jared Wells, who will inevitably give favors to his LOVE buddies and generally make life miserable for everyone? You have a choice, people - a choice between two hyperactive juveniles and a man who behaves with a shred of DIGNITY. The choice seems fairly simple to me, at least.
The floor is now open for questions. Reporter #1: [name withheld], FWInsider.com. Mr. Lion, Backstage confrontations have always been an integral part of building a wrestling feud. Does the proposed secure-workplace legislation take that into account?
Iron Lion: It does, Mr. [name withheld]. Should a brawl occur backstage, the offenders will be given the opportunity to sort out their differences in a sanctioned match. As well, the legislation does not prohibit such things as promotional segments or exchanges of words - only all-out violence, especially from violent stables. I feel that feuds between wrestlers will come out stronger in the end without certain wrestlers constantly losing their credibility by being the victim of routine beatdowns.
Reporter #2: [name withheld], [detail withheld]. Do you believe that your status as a newcomer works to your disadvantage in your presidential campaign?
Iron Lion: Not as much as you'd think, Mr. [name withheld]. I may be new around here, but that gives me the advantage of being able to view WFW from an objective, unbiased standpoint. I bear no grudges against anyone here, nor do I have any favorites. As far as I'm concerned, everyone is equal under promotion law, and only those who break it will need to fear repercussions.
Reporter #3: [name withheld], [detail withheld]. ...What sort of hair care products do you use? Your hair is just so silky and shiny!
Iron Lion: Next question. (There seem to be no other questions.) Iron Lion: No further questions? Alright, then. Thank you all for coming. Take care. (The reporters begin to get up and leave as we FADEOUT to a black screen. Gold text appears.)

A VOTE FOR EDMUNDS ... IS A VOTE FOR EQUALITY ........ JABolich — 11 April 2004 (FADEIN: The conference room again. This time, IRON LION has traded his wardrobe in for a grey sport coat and slacks. The conference room is empty save for a few camera crews.)
Iron Lion: Ladies and gentlemen, it seems that my platform is under siege by the ignorance of one Sean Edmunds. As I said during my last conference, this Edmunds is a man of selective comprehension. He sees only what he wants to see and understands only that which benefits him directly.
Sean, did you not listen when I said my platform wasn't based on iron-fisted oppression? How many times must I tell you that the reforms I intend to institute are intended to INCREASE freedom for EVERYONE? You'd have to be blind not to see it - blind or biased. And do you even realize that by rambling about booking people six on one, you're actually WEAKENING YOUR OWN CAMPAIGN POSITION? Who in their right mind would elect a man willing to punish them like that?
It should be abundantly clear to everyone by now that voting for Sean Edmunds will spell doom for WFW, because Sean Edmunds is interested only in his own agenda. He claims I'm the dictator, but under his reign WFW would be subjected to an even more profound form of despotism.
But something occurs to me, Sean. You sit there and run down MY plans for reform - but what've YOU got on the docket, hm? We haven't heard a peep from you as to what benefits electing YOU would have. All we've heard is you running your mouth about how the sun only shines on Sean Edmunds and everyone else is bad because of this and this and this. So what's on YOUR plate, Seanny? What makes a vote for Sean Edmunds so special? The answer: NOTHING. You're flying by the seat of your pants and hoping to squeak by based on tenure and ego. I, on the other hand, have been planning my campaign carefully and paying attention to BUSINESS rather than ego.
Anyone with a modicum of sense should be able to see that a vote for you, Sean Edmunds, would be a vote for anarchy. A vote for Iron Lion, on the other hand, is a vote for safety, security, and SATISFACTION in the workplace.
Come back when you're ready to talk business, Sean. Until then, take care. (FADEOUT to golden text:) A Vote For Iron Lion Is A Vote For The Three S's: SAFETY, SECURITY, SATISFACTION!
