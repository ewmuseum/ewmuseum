WFW Paradise Lost: John Doe vs. Michael Dice

3 September 2004

PaulNJ21 — 3 September 2004 The RP/angle deadline is Wednesday, September 15 11:59 PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
John Doe — 6 September 2004 FADE IN… John Doe, WFW Backdrop. DOE: “I can’t believe that happened. I lost to Richard Gideon? Ah, oh well, you win some you lose some, but hell I take it that Mr. Dice is pretty good, ever since he left, and now Dice you come back to face me. How fun is that?”
“Actually it would have been better if you would have showed up for Cherry Bloom Chaos, then I would have faced you and it would have been debut for me and a comeback for you. But don’t take me so lightly, just cause your coming back doesn’t mean parades are going to go up and down the streets. More like me smacking from one ring post to the other”
“Honestly, I am not happy about this match at all, honestly I want to face some real competition. Well, now that I think about it, if I want real competition I should just leave WFW, because here there is nothing but scum, no good wrestlers like Mike Dice, who can leave and come back as they feel fit.”
“Let me just tell you something Dice, I will bash the WFW, I will talk all the crap I feel like, because when it comes right down to it, the WFW hired me because I am the best there is, because WFW has no quality stars, just John Doe, the best damn star in FW.”
“But hell, I hope you have heard of me, I hope that you understand what I can do in the ring. I mean I have no clue if WFW made you sign a wavier yet to go in the ring with me, but I suggest you do that as soon as possible. I am going to tear you limb from limb, I am going to separate your skin form your bones, I am going to put you through hell.”
“Dice I am going to show you what I can do in the ring, that I am that damn good. Remember that I am the best damn thing out there, that I have skill, and that I have talent. You have nothing, you are nothing. And I am going to show WFW that I am the best.”
“You will be my living example.” “See you in the ring chump” “I Am Greatness, I am God, I Am John Doe.” FADE OUT
