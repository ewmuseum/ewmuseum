Jared! Jared! Jared! Jared!

10 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[name withheld] — 10 August 2004 (Camera opens up to Jared passed out wearing the usual blue jeans, black sleeveless classic logo 'LOVE' on the front. Jared is is sitting on the floor with his head back on the couch. The table is covered with empty bottles and yay trails all over the table. All of the sudden the band Survivor who made the theme Eye of the Tiger appears in front of him. Jared obviously has not showered or shaved in days seems to be lost. Jimi Jamison the lead singer is sporting a purple silk shirt and is ready to jam. The band starts to play Eye of the Tiger theme with different words)
"Jared! Jared Jared! Jared! Jared Jared Jared! Jared Jared JAAARRREDDD!" (Jared is in the bathroom taking a leak with one hand up against the wall holding himself up. The band is right behind him playing the song and you can see them in the mirror)
"Jared's the man, going to the ring!" "Got his belt, got ambition" (Jared walks out of the bathroom scratching his butt and running into the wall) "Top superstar status is right in his hands, it's something he will never let go." "Jared's the man of the year, he's the king of the ring" (Jared sits on the couch with blood shot eyes, hair all messed up, dirty semi beard. He grabs an empty bottle, tries to drink it but then realizes it's empty then throws it against the wall)
"WFW staff have finally met their rival. Jared's tearin' up the roster at both ends on his way to the top."
(Camera gets a zoomed look at his face as he just sits there looking into space) "He knows one day he just could become..........world champion." (Jared grabs a bottle of something, takes a big chug. Camera zooms right up to his face as his eyes roll into the back of his head and he falls back on the couch flipping it with his feet in the air)
...L.O.V.E! L.O.V.E! L.O.V.E! L.O.V.E! (FADEOUT)
