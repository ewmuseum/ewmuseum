WFW Great Expectations: Felix Red (c) vs. Minion vs. Good Eddie

NotorisSTD — 7 February 2004 (the background music for this promo is “ya’ll don’t wanna step ta dis” by Against Me!) (…..as the rest of us search for meaning and love and such and such in our happy silly little world, Felix Red is lying, “fully clothed ina faded “happy noodle boy” T-shirt, white dickie pants, and subtler 1960es style sunglasses,” in his bathtub. He occasionally sips a glass of tap water, one arm and both legs hanging over the sides of the tub….)
“the boy who destroyed the world…” I know you’re lurking….I can see you both, in shadows, scratching the backs of your shoulders, waiting to grow wings….
To Good Eddie, I admire, if not envy your purity. You like to hurt people. You like to get paid for it. That’s all, and that’s wonderful except that purity and simplicity are beautiful yet hollow things. You have no idea how frail you truly are. How could one such as yourself begin to conceive of one such as I, or One such as Minion even. You see, we really don’t like to hurt people, and don’t really care about money, but we do it all anyway. The logic of your existence, when forced to absorb the shear insanity of mine though various incarnations of misery, will dissipate. Oh, you ain’t gonna hit me. You ain’t gonna hit me, the hell you ain’t, and how presumptuous to think anything you can ever do will make any difference at all to me….
And Dear Minion…Minion, who wanders through perpetual tragedy. Who remains abandoned and is abandoned again. The end is the beginning is the end. Mommy and Daddy left you. Tom Parker left you. Manson left you. I’d like you to….just for a second, consider that perhaps these are not bad things. If Tom Parker really was such a wonderful person, then why did he die? If you loved him and he you so, wouldn’t you honor his memory better by living life to the fullest and seeking out new adventures and joy and doing your laundry and driving to the video store and smiling at the pretty red haired girl you pass by walking in the park and feeding your cat and washing your hair and getting a solid eight hours of sleep everyday and eating deep dish pizza and so on? It’s possible your fixation on your incorporeal mentor has something to do with repressed memories of sexual abuse, but I’m just speculating here, and really it doesn’t matter at all, because the kind of person Tom Parker actually was is meaningless. What isn't meaningless, Minion, is that he told you who you were supposed to be and died. You asked Manson to take his place, and he left you not because it benefited him in anyway, but because he thought it was funny….With no one to guide you, Minion….you have become nothing.
Because you’re not supposed to be here. Think about it. If your whole life hasn’t been an accident, a miscarriage of fate, then wouldn’t you have your own identity by now? Predestination stumbled on a banana peal, and the result is you. Everything you’ve ever done has been a mistake, not of your own, but of destiny….
I can help you Minion. I can give you your place in this world. I only need you to stand absolutely still, and try to flinch, and your anguish, at last, will end….
DavidMc — 9 February 2004 (Screen fades in as the camera is walking along the street in front of Minions apartment. The camera crosses the street which is desolate minus the broken down cars and the only street light that flickers on and off. As the camera crosses the street Minion finishes up his cigarette and motions for the camera to follow him inside. The camera follows him into the hallway as Minion makes his way up the stair case. All along the walls leading up to Minions apartment are posters, holes, stains, blood stains, and various other stuff which cannot be said on television. Minion finally gets to his level and walks down a long dark hallway which is much like the stair walls in terms of looks and feel. Minion then gets to his apartment walks in and the camera follows him. As Minion gets in his apartment which is completely dark he lights up a cigarette and in the orange glow we see his face for just a moment, then he begins again.)
Minion: Hello darkness my old friend…you never judge people…you don’t care what they look like…what they have done…what they are ashamed off….no darkness you don’t care about any of that…you shield people like me away from the world…you shield the evil away from people like those I must face…you shield me away from my problems…allow me to escape from my dreams…my nightmares…my hallucinations…everything is escapable once I am in your loving arms…now darkness I must step out of the shadows and into the light…I must finally get something that has been alluding me for four years…four long hard years of pain…years of sadness…it is finally within my grasp…finally…it has been four years since I have been up for a title…four years of being ignored…pushed into the darkness…forced into the shadows and told never to come out…now mommy has let me out to play…now mommy has let her son…her horrible…horrible son come out to play with the world…now the world is not ready for such things they said…now the world must be ready for such things I said…four years has been a long time to wait…four years of waiting for my name to be called up for any sort of title…now the time has come…now the climax is about to be reached…I will soon be the BAD World Heavyweight champion…but there are people in my way…there are people who want to see me fail…there are people who would like nothing better than to push me back into the shadows from whence I have come from…but they are disillusioned…the passion that is in my blood…the passion that flows through me for something shiny…something golden…something like a championship…the red rain will be pouring down…the rain that taste like blood will pour down upon the mat…four years of waiting is all coming to an end…and a new era will begin.
(We can hear Minion taking a long drag off his cigarette and then he blows the smoke out of his mouth and begins again.)
Minion: Under different circumstances I would of preferred to fight just one person…under the circumstances beggars can’t be choosers and I should take what I get…Good Eddie and I have crossed paths earlier in my WFW career…oh yes we have…but things are different now…then he had people backing him up…then was a different time and nothing was the line…now things have changed…the situation is entirely new a different…there is actually something I want this time…winning doesn’t normally matter to me and didn’t matter to me then…winning now means I have to wait again…and that cannot happen…this time Eddie with no rules you are in my play ground…now Eddie I will end your career…now Eddie I will do all the things I should of done to you before…but Eddie don’t feel bad you aren’t really the apple of my eye…no no my concern is placed on another man…yes the man who holds the belt…the man who I really must strive to beat…Felix Red…you are in my way Red…you are just merely holding onto the belt for safe keeping because now I am going to take what is mine…you are just holding on to the belt because even though you beat whomever you did for that belt time has changed and whomever you beat was nothing compared to me…you are in my area of specialty…you are in my realm…the whole no rules things is another way in which the WFW is catering to my abilities…or my ability to literally destroy a man with one simple move…Red you might think you are hardcore…you might think you can handle pain but you can’t…at least the kind of pain that I bring to the table…have you ever looked into the eyes of man who fears nothing…have you ever looked into the eyes of a man who has nothing to live for…sure you think its silly to honor a dead man…that I should move on with life…no Red I honor the man who made me what I am today…I wear the shirt to fuel the hatred…I remember the things so that I can never forget what pain I went through to get to this point…the pain is not comparable to what I feel now.
(Minion takes another drag and flicks the cigarette to the ground. We hear flicks of a lighter and in the corner Minion is lighting a candle and then takes a seat on the chair sitting next to the candle. The camera stays where it is and Minion begins again.)
Minion: True in life I have depended on others for guidance…I depended on Tom he died…life went on and I became something…then I became fascinated with Manson…I became something else…but Manson was a slut…I became better than him…but that is not the point that it is none of your concern…all that concerns you Red and you should keep your mind on this…is what I am now…whatever I was before you will never see…if I was a good boy you will never know…if was pure hatred…you will never know that either…but somewhere along the path of history I became what I am…I am improvement over two people I asked for advice…now even though you see no true personality if you knew the past you would see something entirely new…I like to think of it as merged personalities…on one side I have the sick twisted side of Manson…on the other I have the dark desires of Tom Parker…and in the middle there was my true personality…the personality that keeps me from killing a man in the ring…the personality that yanks me back to reality as I wrap my hands around your throat with my hands raping you of your breath…see Red that personality is repressed…Tom and Manson’s guidance lock the middle personality in a closet and then take over…it is this sadistic personality that you see in the ring is what you have to worry about…you have to worry about me ripping your flesh…and enjoying it…you have to worry that every move I do to you will it be your last…my last…or the calling to the end of your career…everything I do in the ring has a purpose…every little twitch…every movement…every move feeds upon the one that came before it and has an ultimate purpose…especially now…I do not need a place in the world and I surely don’t need your help in finding me some pathetic cookie cutter sheet place…look into the hearts of the American public they all want to be me…they all idolize me…they boo me because they are jealous and realize they want what I have…they want the power I have…they want to say the things I say that they can only think in their pathetic skulls…they want to cause pain…they want to triumph over others while ripping away pieces of flesh…they want to stop being fallen angels and take back their lives the way I have taken control of others…life is like a dream and the American public want to go to my paradise…the truth of the matter is Red you have what I want…what I have been waiting for…now that I have come out of the shadows I have no intent on going back without something shiny and golden…I think that thing around your waist will do nicely…don’t you?
(FADE TO BLACK) :eek: NotorisSTD — 11 February 2004 (You can’t hear the background music too well. It’s is playing way too loudly out of a mono speaker somewhere in the building….)
(Felix Red, in giant mirror sunglasses, an orange “Fooly Cooly” T-shirt, and red and white spiral wrestling trunks sits Indian style on the floor of his locker room, electric tape wrapped around his arms, new giant beads sliding up several of the now blue dreadlocks, scowling, BAD world title with “SH#T” scribbled on it in black pen lies in front of him on the ground….)
“the boy who destroyed the world” Somehow I can’t stop feeling like I’ve been here before. Never have been able to, actually. Always have.
They always say I’m not crazy enough, always tell me they love pain, they hate everything and everyone, they’ll love me only when I suffer, that they love pain because it’s all they can feel, and that they’re crazy, they’re insane, reality is too much for them, And they keep falling…they keep falling. They’ll never stop falling. They’ve always been falling….
I already knew you wanted to be the new non-world champion, Minion. It’s the sort of thing that would probably appeal to you, not because you could ever appreciate the irony, the anti-logic, these things it represents that I so adore, but because you’re not smart.
I never accused you of being sane. Quite the contrary. I think you’re stark raving mad. Sadism was fashion to Psycho, insanity a mask with which to hide how dreadfully average he truly was. I never believed Psycho was crazy. He doesn’t have the tormented history that you do. I believe he had total, absolute control of his mental faculties. And look what I did to him. I killed him. I kicked him in the face one time, and he died. Rat Fink, on the other hand, who never claimed to be and would deny being so, is one of the maddest I’ve ever gazed upon. Now he’s dead too, and it’s all my fault.
I don’t have a point in all this exposition, only that I understand that there is no point and never is, but If I did have something I was trying to say, it was be that it doesn’t matter how crazy you are, or how much you love pain, or how much hate is inside you.
I’m not crazy. I only like pain in comparatively small doses and when it’s inflicted in an entirely different context by yer sister (grins, and gives a thumbs up), and really, I’m not that full of hate. Actually, my life isn’t that bad at all. In the end, It doesn’t matter. I destroyed the world. I’ll keep destroying things. I can’t get to a point where there’s nothing left. Everything’s already gone…
Including the darkness. Including any concept of the esoteric, or logic, or science. Light into Dark, good into evil, pain into pleasure, up into down, substance into emptiness, can you imagine, Minion?….An existence somewhat like a purgatory, except nothing at all like a purgatory, because we have no concept of anything and therefore are not cognoscente of the void it leaves? Where something’s there, only in that no one can understand that nothing is there? Where memories and feelings and lives aren’t there, where never there, and never could be there…..Where this isn’t happening. Never will, never has, nothing will, nothing has ever been….
I want to show this, my vision of the world, Minion. I want you to stare into it’s utter absolute horror, experience a sublime agony even you could never have imagined. Then I want to crumple you up like a discarded post-it note, and toss you into the oblivion, so you may never again bother me, you second tier goth monster simpleton….
DavidMc — 12 February 2004 (Screen fades in as the scene opens up the camera has been put on a tripod and the quality of the video footage is grainy black white. Minion is sitting in front of the camera hunched over with a cigarette in one hand and his hair covering his eyes. He pops his head up quickly so that the hair is gone from his face and then puts the cigarette up to his mouth and takes a couple of drags before beginning.)
Minion: True signs of a desperate man…oh yes…it is a desperate man who can’t find anything wrong …any space…any sort of position…it is a desperate man when that man relies on a man smarts to make fun of the opponent…Red I never took you as a desperate man…but I guess when animals are backed into a corner then I guess you had no choice but to lash out…you are so desperate that you go to siblings that don’t even exist to make some sort of silly retarded joke to somehow get the stupid ignorant fans to actually give two ****s about you…Ill tell you what…you don’t like pain…that’s fantastic good for you…to bad I don’t share in your philosophy of life…to bad I see the world as I do…to bad I see the world as some sort of play ground for sick people like me…people like me who have been abused…kicked…punched down to the level of nothingness and then rise above that nothingness only to make someone else feel as bad as I once did…to manipulate the once proud and strong…kind alike you Red…Red you are pathetic…I never wanted to say it but by your silly attempts to make yourself seem important are worthless…the fact of the matter is that it has been four ****ing years since I have gotten a title shot…any type of title shot…whether it was a fake title…a real title…or a trash title…four years is a long time you little prick…and you know what there is nothing that is going to stop me from ripping that belt off your waist…nothing is going to stop me from beating you into a bloody pulp before the thousands and thousands of fans...dads will cover the eyes of their sons…mothers will take their small infant children out of the arena and sue the WFW after this mouth…so much of your blood…so much of your flesh will end up on the mat…so many of your bones will go snap…crackle…when I finally think I am ready…they will go POP!!!
(Minion lowers his head and his hair falls back down over his eyes and he takes another drag off his cigarette. We see smoke rise from his mouth but he never raises his head as he speaks again.)
Minion: You think you can destroy the world…you are nothing but a poser…living the dream of a rock star…you are nothing but a fake in the world full of fake people…you think someone as weak as you…someone who can’t even handle pain can actually destroy the world…not only are you mentally unable to do that…you are physically unable to even dare destroy the world…you will never destroy me…I am indestructible…so many have tried to cure me…so many have tried to stop my madness… so many have tried to take me out of the game but all have failed…I am the ****ing cancer of the WFW…sure there are treatments but in the end I come back…better…craftier…stronger…and then I infect you more and more until there is nothing left and I move on…Red that is what’s going to happen…you will try and stop me…you will go by your rhetoric but when it all comes down to it…you will fail…you have used your intimidation on others…it has worked…but when you look at me across the ring…after I hang up Tom Parkers shirt…you will think you see something weak…you will search for it…you will look for that one glimmer of hope that maybe I am not what I say iam…you will not find it…you will see a psycho path…you will a stalker…you will see an abuser…you will see a man who is so hell bent on breaking his curse he will do anything…and I do mean anything to break it…Red you will se me…in all my sickening…madness…glory…you should run…run as far as you can…but if you don’t…I cannot be held accountable for your stupidity…then when it is all over…and you lay on the mat half between living and death…as the paramedics roll you out of the ring you will look back into the ring…right before you fade away you will see me…in the ring…smearing my blood on your gold…it all comes to an end and Great Expectations…I will finally rise above the fallen…I will finally taste the flesh of fallen angels…and you will be nothing but a chump…my destiny is beginning…yours is over.
