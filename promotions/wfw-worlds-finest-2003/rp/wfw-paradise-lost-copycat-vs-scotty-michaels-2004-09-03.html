WFW Paradise Lost: Copycat vs. Scotty Michaels

3 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 3 September 2004 Whoever wins will have the choice of naming a stipulation match of their choice for their match against whoever is WFW World Heavyweight Champion at the PPV, or kicking the other man out of the match in order to make it a one on one contest. The RP/angle deadline is Wednesday, September 15 11:59 PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
GARTHIsTheLaw — 8 September 2004 (Cueup: Dramatic orchestral music) (Fade in on a clip from Cherry Blossom Chaos II. We see Copycat picking up Sean Edmunds for the LitterBomb)
Brandon Jacobs: ...HE IS GOING FOR A LITTERBOMB! (Edmunds escapes the LitterBomb) Jacobs: ...WAIT A SECOND, EDMUNDS SLIPPED OUT BEFORE HE COULD GET HIM! (Copycat turns and Edmunds hooks him around the waist) Jacobs: ...EDMUNDS HOOKS ON A NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX AND NAILS HIM WITH IT! Jonathan Marx: I can’t believe, this can’t be happening! (Copycat tries to break the pin) Jacobs: ...1------------------------------------------------2------------------------------------ COPYCAT BREAKS FREE SOMEHOW AND KICKS OUT!
Vic Waters: Nobody has ever kicked out of the Northern Lights Suplex! (Copycat sinches in the Cat's Claw on Edmunds out of nowhere) Jacobs: ...COPYCAT CHARGES AND LOCKS ON THE CAT’S CLAW! (Edmunds is starting to fade) Jacobs: ...Edmunds is going out fast! (Edmunds is almost gone) Jacobs: ...THE BLOOD IS COMING DOWN COPYCAT FACE AS HE IS USING ALL HE HAS LEFT TO GET EDMUNDS TO TAP OUT….. Edmunds is trying to fight….. EDMUNDS FINALLY PASSES OUT! The referee is calling for bell! COPYCAT DID IT!
Marx: HOLY COW, COPYCAT DID IT! Ring announcer: The winner of the match….. COPYCAT! (Cut to a clip of Edmunds holding a mic) Edmunds: ...And now ..you’re going to get what you deserve … A WORLD TITLE SHOT! (Echoing) WORLD TITLE SHOT...WORLD TITLE SHOT...
(Cueup: "Heart Full of Black" by the Burning Brides) (The clip from Cherry Blossom Chaos II fades out. As it does, an image of Copycat standing in front of a black background fades in. He wears jeans, his trademark beret and a WFW Copycat "Smartest Player in the Game" T-shirt. He looks up at the camera and speaks)
Copycat: Sean Edmunds kept his word. I wouldn't have put money on it. I was promised a World Heavyweight Title shot if I beat Blue Cat, who -- shock of shocks -- turned out to be Sean himself. And now, just like I was promised, I've got my title shot.
(Copycat shoots a cynical glance at the camera) Copycat: And oh yeah -- I've gotta beat Scotty Michaels first. (He rolls his eyes) Copycat: Now, obviously, I'm not particularly enthused about there being a catch to my title shot. Sean Edmunds' presidency has been all about catches -- so again, shock of shocks -- but nobody likes a catch. Adding a match to my contention isn't a thrilling concept for me, particularly when it's against the only man who has been able to beat me in a singles match in the last two years. And did I mention that that same man has recently forged an alliance with two other WFW wrestlers, one of whom is the last man who beat me one-on-one prior to my current opponent? Yeah. Great stuff. But, lest you get the wrong idea...
Edmunds (V/O, from Cherry Blossom Chaos II): ...and believe me … after tonight, your b(BLEEP)ching and moaning better CEASE...
Copycat: ...I wouldn't want anything I've said to be construed as complaint. Because this isn't all about the WFW World Heavyweight Title for me. Oh, I'd LIKE to hold that title. But I've got other priorities these days. I went into Cherry Blossom Chaos II looking to rend Blue Cat limb from limb. One of the reasons for that was that title shot hanging in the balance. But I wanted something more than that.
(Copycat looks away from a moment, then focuses back on the camera) Copycat: Almost one year ago exactly, Scotty Michaels beat me with the help of three other men. A four-on-one assault, something I would soon have to learn to get used to in WFW. It was about the North American Title then. I asked for a rematch. Over and over again. I never got it. Oh, I got a brief chance to smack Scotty around a little bit in a triple threat tag match of some sort, but that didn't accomplish much. So I figured if I wasn't going to get a match against Scotty Michaels, I'd just have to get my payback some other way. So I cost Michaels the one thing he valued more than anything; his precious WFW World Heavyweight Title. I even made sure he wouldn't be able to get it back right away when I tossed him out of the So Cal Rumble. But still, no rematch was to be had. It was the will of WFW's esteemed President Wylde that I have a series of pointless matches with Anarky, a man to whom I had nothing to prove.
(Copycat adopts a more intense look) Copycat: Months later, I've suddenly got a match with Scotty, and it couldn't have come at a better time. I've beaten Dan Ryan. I've beaten Larry Tact. I've beaten Felix Red. I've beaten Jared Wells. I've even done something Scotty Michaels COULDN'T do -- beat Sean Edmunds. Over these last few months, I've been teaching a very important concept to everyone who crosses my path. And at Paradise Lost, I'm going to be able to teach Scotty Michaels a lesson I should have taught him a year ago. I'm going to teach Scotty Michaels some RESPECT.
(Copycat softens his gaze a bit) Copycat: Our issues may be old news, Scotty. You've come forward to oppose L.O.V.E., and that's noble. SOMEone has to tell those guys that they aren't any more relevant as a group than they were as individuals. So I'd say it's possible that, whether or not you respect ME, you understand the importance of respect. And if you do, then you'll respect the fact that we must find out which of the two of us is the better man. If you understand the importance of respect, you'll realize why it is important for us to prove to the WFW fans that we deserve to wrestle for the World Heavyweight Title. If you understand the importance of respect, you'll keep this match clean so we can see which of us really has the right to call himself the best. The Viewer's Choice or the Smartest Player in the Game. If, on the other hand, you HAVEN'T learned respect during the past year...
(Copycat glares into the camera) Copycat: ...Then it will be my duty to teach it to you. Bring Shawn Hart. Bring Richard Gideon. Try to stop my momentum this time. Really. I invite you to try. Because regardless of who comes out of our match the winner -- regardless of whether I leave Paradise Lost with my title shot -- I WILL leave Paradise Lost with your respect. If you come into this match with respect, we can have a fantastic match. If you come into this match without respect, then you will learn it before the night is through. You, and Shawn Hart, and Richard Gideon, and whoever else should try to get involved. The WFW World Heavyweight Title is important to me, Scotty. But it is not CRITICAL. What IS critical is respect. And one way or another, Scotty, at Paradise Lost, I will get the RESPECT...that I DESERVE.
(Fade out) ViewersChoice — 8 September 2004 (FADEIN to Scotty Michaels, sitting in the dark in his den. The only light comes from the tv, and we see the Copycat interview wrapping up. The video stops, and Michaels turns on the light next to his chair. He's visibly warn, his face unshaven... bags under his eyes. He takes a moment, and runs his hands through his hair, sighing..)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: It's 3 AM, and here I sit.. not being able to sleep. Not being able to get a chance to rest, because I've got too damn much on my mind. At Cherry Blossom Chaos, I beat Anarky... and I beat Larry Tact, all in one match, all at their own game. I took L.O.V.E. on just like I said I would, and I came out on top. However, I must thank Richard Gideon, Shawn Hart.. and everyone else who came to my assistance.
But that's where it ENDS! You see, I knew it was going to be a dogfight down in Washington, DC. When I stepped up to the plate against Anarky, I knew it was going to be one of the CRAZIEST things that I ever did in my life. That's why I've acquired the help of Shawn Hart and Richard Gideon, two SWORN ENEMIES... since the WFW opened its doors. Gideon... he beat me for the North American title around this time last year, ending my first title reign in the WFW. Shawn Hart... hell, I don't even think I need to get into my history with Shawn Hart. I pulled more SH*T over on Shawn Hart than any man ever deserves. But this time, it's bigger than Scotty Michaels. It's bigger than the stupid differences of opinion that we may have had over the past year and a half.
You know why I relied on them? Because they want the same thing as me. They want it all back.. they want to boot Anarky's ass out of the WFW, along with the rest of his cohorts in L.O.V.E. I mean, seriously... did you really think that I would just stand by idly, and watch Anarky destroy the WFW? NOT ON MY TIME THEY WON'T!
But moving on to another pain in my ass, another gnat... if you will, now we move on to Copycat. The man's track record has been great. He's done it all, but not here in the WFW. There's one thing he's absolutely DYING to do, and that's beat Scotty Michaels in a one-on-one match here in the WFW, if there really is such a thing... especially in my matches. (Scotty laughs)
Maybe there is. Maybe for once, we can get two of the best wrestlers in this league... yeah, I'm talking about me. Everyone knows it. And the other being you, Copycat. We've had an ongoing rivalry for over a year now, but it's never really gotten on the radar. But this time, things will be different.
You've finally worked yourself up to a world title shot, but the trick here is, you've gotta go through me... a man you've never beaten. If it were absolutely up to me, that would never change. But I've become a wiser man, the longer my tenure here in the WFW.
I'm tired of being the guy who wins by default, with the aid of everyone and their brother... hell, my fake brother for that matter.
I've got a deal for ya this time, buddy... think you're going to like this one more than catnip... more than disposing of last night's tuna in the litter tray.
Let's start Paradise Lost off in dramatic fashion. You, me... in that ring, not wrestling. Not right away, anyway... that gets saved for last.
SCOTTY MICHAELS! COPYCAT! In that VERY RING, signing a contract. What kind of contract you may ask? One that's set in stone.
You see, as much as I expect to beat you... there's always the teensy, weensiest chance that something could go AWRY! Something could go wrong, and I could be out of my title match at the pay per view. I mean, hell... I've got Anarky and the rest of L.O.V.E. just dying to attack me whenever I'm not paying attention. Hell, I DESERVE IT!
So anyone, back to the beginning of the show. Back to the contract signing. SCOTTY MICHAELS! COPYCAT! Signing a contract for the match later in the show, to be a STEEL CAGE MATCH!
And to quote an infomercial, WAIT... THERE'S MORE! In that very contract, it will state that if there's any sort of interference on my part, I will automatically be removed from the world title match at the pay per view.
However, win or lose... if there's no interference on my part, I will be GUARANTEED to be making the THREE WAY DANCE at the pay per view, against Copycat and against the man I've taken a title away from before, Mike Manson.
Since everyone's JACKED UP about stipulations, how do you like those? You wanted your damn RESPECT, Copycat. There it is. Plain and simple. I'll give you your just due, as long as I get mine... the world title rematch that I deserve.
The decision is yours to make. What's it going to be? Don't make the wrong choice there, buddy.. or you may regret it for the rest of your life..
(FADEOUT, as Michaels rewinds the Copycat interview to watch again...) GARTHIsTheLaw — 10 September 2004 (Cueup: "A Favor House Atlantic" by Coheed & Cambria) (Fade in on Copycat sitting at a table in front of a black background. He wears jeans, his trademark beret, and a WFW Copycat "Smartest Player in the Game" T-shirt. He is looking down at the table)
Copycat: You've got quite a way with words, Scotty. It's hard to tell what you mean from what you say. I also happen to be pretty good at arranging every word just so, making sure everything sounds EXACTLY as I want it to sound before I say it. So I guess that makes us similar in that respect.
(Copycat looks up at the camera) Copycat: So you want to win a match without outside help? That's admirable. I wish more people had that attitude. Seeing as I've only been pinned twice in WFW, and both times it was the result of copious outside interference, the idea of a match without it is certainly to my liking. You could liken it to a night out drinking. In fact, I intend to do just that.
(Copycat reaches under the table and comes up with a bottle of Jack Daniel's whiskey. He puts the bottle on the table in front of him)
Copycat: Now then. Everyone knows the night is more interesting when you mix up the drinks, but if you stick with one specific drink all night, you're probably going to be in much better shape the next morning. It's just you, the bottle, and whatever the night throws your way. But what happens if you happen to have a couple of things to contend with?
(Copycat reaches under the table again and produces a bottle of Bacardi rum and a bottle of Jose Cuervo tequila. He puts them on opposite sides of the whiskey bottle)
Copycat: You mess around with all three, and there's going to be trouble. So how do you just stick with one and stop the other two from interfering with your good time? Well, it just so happens to be your lucky night. A buddy generously offers to watch you and make sure the other two don't come near you. Nice of him. Now you're on even terms with the first bottle; one-on-one.
(Copycat pushes the rum and tequila aside. He picks up the whiskey and looks at it) Copycat: That scenario makes you my buddy, doesn't it Scotty? You're keeping our match one-on-one. No Shawn Hart. No Richard Gideon. That's what your stipulations state. I don't want to be in another situation where I lose because I'm gang-beaten. That gets old real fast, and it doesn't prove anything for either party. So that's a very nice offer on your part. You're guaranteed your title shot, and I'm guaranteed no outside interference. And hey...
(Copycat puts the whiskey bottle down) Copycat: ...I can even elect to have the match in a steel cage, as a bonus. Cool. I'm always open to suggestion. And given the total disaster the last cage match I was in became, I'm certainly eager to change my record. It ended in a gang-beating, actually. Irony. So I'll tell you what, Scotty. You want a cage match? You got it. You want a guaranteed title shot in exchange for a guaranteed lack of outside interference?
(Copycat glares into the camera) Copycat: Were you paying attention when you watched my last promo, Scotty? Have you been paying attention to anything I've said for the past few months? Oh, you're good at making your every word sound solid and convincing, but I'm VERY good at spotting deceit. You say you're offering me respect? You offered to nix all outside interference...IF I guarantee your title shot. And you have the nerve to call that RESPECT? Let me REVIEW here, Scotty.
(Copycat leans forward in his chair) Copycat: I said that if you go into that ring with respect, and if you keep your buddies at bay, we can have a fantastic match. You want credibility, don't you? You want to put on a good match, don't you? You want to prove that you DESERVE that title shot, don't you? So why in the HELL should I have to give you a gift to convince you to do something for yourself? Those promises of a match for the ages if you keep it clean weren't for MY benefit, Scotty. They were for YOURS. THAT is the gift I am giving you. Not some signed-and-sealed title shot covered in festive wrapping paper and a pretty ribbon. You give me respect, and in response, you earn not just my respect, but the respect of the fans. That's how the system works.
(Copycat brings the rum and tequila back to the center of the table) Copycat: You call your offer of "no outside interference for a guaranteed title shot" respect? The implication that I would BRIBE you to keep Hart and Gideon away from ringside isn't respect. It's a g(BLEEP)amn INSULT. If I win that match, it is my prerogative whether you still get your title shot, and I am not about to give up that right to decide. Maybe you'll get your shot and maybe you won't.
(Copycat grabs the rum with his left hand and the tequila with his right) Copycat: You can still tell Hart and Gideon not to interfere. That is YOUR prerogative. You can prove to me, and to everyone else in the world, that you have the courage to wrestle a match without outside interference on your behalf. That is your reward. But if my refusal to bribe you means you CAN'T keep your buddies away from ringside, then know this. There are OTHER ways to keep those nasty little interfering elements from getting in the way.
(Copycat picks up both bottles by the necks and smashes them on the table) Copycat: At Paradise Lost, our match can be an encounter hailed by all, or it can be a meaningless screwjob. Cheat if you must. But don't forget, Scotty. I said that title shot or no title shot, I WILL walk out of Paradise Lost with what I want. Maybe I'll get it from a spectacular wrestling match. Or maybe I'll get it by obliterating you AND your sidekicks. But one way or another, Scotty, I will get the RESPECT...that I DESERVE.
(Copycat gets up and walks away from the table, leaving the whiskey bottle standing among shards of glass and puddles of liquor)
(Fade out) ViewersChoice — 12 September 2004 (FADEIN to Scotty Michaels, inside of a ring that is surrounded by a steel cage. He's wearing his wrestling gear, pacing around the ring.. he finally stops pacing, and the camera zooms in on him..)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Since the day Copycat stepped into this federation, it's seemed like every time I turned around... there he was, being a PAIN IN MY ASS! While I'm willing to give due respect, I KNOW that Copycat is not a better competitor than I am.
When you look at the resumes of Copycat and Scotty Michaels as far as the WFW is concerned, you'll see a VAST difference. On one hand, you have Scotty Michaels. He's a former North American champion, beating Mike Manson for the prestigious championship. He's a former WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, beating Shawn Hart... for the top prize in all the game.
Then... you look at Copycat. He's got no championships to his name here in the WFW, and he's going to try his DAMNEST to change that once and for all.
I've been DUE a REMATCH for quite some time, as I dropped the world title in March. IT'S SIX MONTHS LATER! Come on, give me a break. What kind of crap is that?
But the looming presence in this match isn't about the STEEL CAGE, not at all.. (Michaels slams his hands off the cage..) It's not even about L.O.V.E. basically having a bounty on my head. This time, it's ALL about RESPECT! It's all about being able to sleep at night, and if Scotty Michaels is able to do so. Yeah, I've got a TRACK RECORD of being a LIAR here in the WFW. I've got a track record of being a CHEAT here in the WFW! Now taking all of that into consideration, I'm pretty much an AS*HOLE, through and through.
That looming presence in our steel cage match is whether or not I'm lying this time. It's almost laughable, because a lot of people know for sure that Shawn Hart and Richard Gideon will be out there to help me out, just as sure that L.O.V.E. will be there to kick MY ASS, at the best given moment.
I guess we'll all just have to wait and see, Copycat. Will he, or won't he.. say the word that will make this match a JOKE!
Has Scotty Michaels really matured to the point that it matters to him that he wins matches on the merit of his wrestling ability, and not on the merit of how many friends he has running around backing him up.
You ask yourself this question, OVER and OVER, Copycat... and you'll probably come up with the same answer all the time. When you look at the title history, there's no FOOTNOTE that says that Luster helped me pad my resume with those championships. It's all of the bitter people who will remember exactly how I won my titles.
But eventually, that memory will fade. The memory that won't fade, however, is how YOU... YOU, COPYCAT... YOU cost me my world heavyweight championship. If it wasn't for you, I'd still have that title belt strapped around my waist right now.
It's time for PAYBACK, it's time for RETRIBUTION... it's time, for REVENGE! So ask yourself the question over and over. Is Scotty Michaels ready to win a championship on his own?
I guess we will just have to wait and see.. At Paradise Lost, all of these questions will be answered. But if there's one thing I promise, I will be facing Manson for the world heavyweight championship at the pay per view.
If you're a part of that match, Copycat, then so be it... You want RESPECT? I'll make you wish you never knew the word... (FADEOUT, as Michaels kicks the steel cage...) GARTHIsTheLaw — 15 September 2004 (Cueup: "I'm Not Okay (I Promise)" by My Chemical Romance) (Fade in on Copycat standing in a small cage, more suited for large animals than for wrestling matches. He wears jeans, his trademark beret, and a black T-shirt that has something written on it but is inside-out so we can't see it)
Copycat: Scotty Michaels certainly is full of questions. And I suppose it's some subtle humor that Scotty, a man with a definite gift for rhetoric, made most of his questions rhetorical. Do I trust him? Does anyone trust him? Can he win this match without outside interference? That sort of thing. Questions that he wants me to think on long and hard. Questions that cast doubt upon his morals. Questions designed to make me overanalyze our match.
(Copycat looks up at the camera) Copycat: Questions I know better than to think about. See, Scotty, you and I may have only gone one-on-one on a single occasion, but we've had wars of words numerous times before. And something I've learned about you, Scotty, is that you only pose questions when you think your opponent has got you figured out. That little bit where Luster dressed up as your crippled brother in a wheelchair? Casual observers -- including, but not limited to, myself -- called you on that immediately. We all smelled a scam. But our opinions didn't matter, did they? You only needed to convince one person, and that person was Shawn Hart. He was as skeptical as the rest of us at first. But you just kept asking the right questions, and pretty soon poor Shawn just didn't know WHAT to think. Not that he usually does anyway, but the point is it worked. Everyone else was saying, "Oh come on, I saw that one a mile away!" But you had the last laugh, didn't you? The World Heavyweight Title was yours.
(Copycat looks off to the side) Copycat: 'Til I cost you the belt a few weeks later... (He looks back at the camera) Copycat: And come to think of it, Scotty, that's how you beat me the first time we met. I mean, strictly speaking, you beat me by sending your goons to quadruple-team me. But in your defense, I wasn't exactly prepared to face four people at once. After all, my best-known weakness is that I don't fare well against triple- or quadruple-teams. You discovered that weakness, Scotty! Well done.
(Copycat develops a sarcastic look) Copycat: But let's get something straight, Scotty. You took advantage of my tendency to overanalyze everything once before, and I commend you for it. But I won't let it happen again. Because for me, it's no longer about getting into my opponent's head. That's fun, but it's not what I'm after anymore. I'm after one very basic, very simple thing, Scotty. And I think you know what it is.
(Copycat grips the bars of the cage) Copycat: When I overanalyze, I'm vulnerable. But ever since respect became my number-one goal, I've been UNSTOPPABLE. L.O.V.E. can't stop me. President Edmunds can't stop me. And Scotty, you and your buddies can't stop me. Now, you could very well tell them to stay away from ringside, and that'd be fine. We can concentrate on having a great match and not worrying about interfering parties. And if you tell them to just go wild and interfere to their hearts' content? Well.
(Copycat tightens his grip on the cage bars) Copycat: Just think of it this way, Scotty. In wrestling, the steel cage is usually used as a way to keep outside parties from interrupting the battle between two people. But in recent years, it's been shown that the steel cage rarely prevents interference. Hell, you should know. Interference in a steel cage match is what got you the North American Title here in WFW. That cage's primary purpose is to keep people from getting in. But if it doesn't serve that primary purpose...it's going to have to serve its secondary purpose.
(Copycat glares at the camera, his hands squeezing the cage bars tightly) Copycat: And that is to keep people from getting OUT. If anyone climbs over that wall, if anybody walks in that door, they are MINE. I don't take kindly to people who interfere in my matches. All I want is a one-on-one match, and if anyone tries to change that, they are disrespecting me. I want my respect. And no matter how many people I have to teach it to, I WILL get what I'm due. One way or another, Scotty, I WILL get the RESPECT...
(With a wrench, Copycat yanks the two bars he's holding onto off the cage) Copycat: ...that I DESERVE. (Fade out) ViewersChoice — 16 September 2004 (FADEIN to Scotty, standing in front of a plain WFW backdrop...) SCOTTY MICHAELS: I thought about coming out here tonight, trying to set up some kind elaborate visual display for Copycat and for all the fans. I could've been standing inside of a steel cage, like in my last interview, and at that same point... I could've been acting like a complete psycho. I couldn't been bashing my damn head off the steel cage, until that ring looked liked the bloodshed saved for the likes of CSI: Miami.
I decided to stray away from that, though. I do have a story for ya, though. My uncle, my crazy Uncle Fred. That sum-b*tch is one of the craziest people I've ever met in my life. He lived out in the sticks, and on occasion, my parents would take me up to his cabin in the woods. He loved to sit on his porch with his buddies and drink beers, almost more than he loved to ride his Harley. The guy was into dogs, man. Man's best friend. He had a bunch of dogs running around, and every now and then, a stray cat would make its way on to his property. Those cats would be dumb enough to step up to the one dog, Ivan. Often times, Ivan chased them off. There was once though, when one of the cats blindsided Ivan, and scratched his face pretty badly... blood everywhere.
Well my Uncle Fred wouldn't have any of that. He pulled out his shotgun, and killed the damn thing. He then proceeded to get on his Harley, with cat in hand, and he'd ride to the nearest bridge and whip that thing into the river.. end of story, and that cat would never mess with his dogs again.
If you didn't get the point, Copycat, I'm the BIG DOG around here. I'm higher on the food chain than you are. You may have been able to get the best of me here and there, even costing me my world heavyweight championship. Why shouldn't I bring in Hart and Gideon? Why, because this dog doesn't need Uncle Fred to stand up for him.
This dog can fight his own battles, because believe it or not... I've done it before. (Scotty laughs)
So you want to talk RESPECT, Copycat... maybe you're not the only guy going into this match who truly needs some. Maybe Scotty Michaels, needs to prove that he can win on his own...
Or maybe, this is all ONE BIG LIE! Maybe I'm the same SUPERSTAR, who doesn't give a DAMN about how to get to the big prize... as long as it's a means to an end.
There's only one way to know for sure, that's to show up at Paradise Lost... then, we'll see who's walking out of there with RESPECT! (FADEOUT...)
