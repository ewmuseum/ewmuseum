WFW — Roleplay

Shock & Awe: Scotty Michaels vs. Mike McGee

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 31 March 2003 #1 Contender to North American Title match: Scotty Michaels vs. Mike McGee Cut your promos here! Showtime24 — 4 April 2003 (FADEIN to Scotty Michaels in his apartment, which was a Royal Rumble scene a week earlier. He looks calm, cool, and collected. He is by himself, with his sidekick Jerome nowhere to be found..)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: You know, at that last card... whatever the hell it was called, there was a Royal Rumble to decide who would be the number one contender to the WFW heavyweight championship of the world. Things didn't go quite as planned for the "VIEWER'S CHOICE," but you know what? I'll take it. After being on the shelf for all of these years, a lot of people didn't think I'd have it in me to last as late as I did in the Rumble. I was one of the last men thrown out, and it looks like the management here at the WFW has noticed that. All I've got to say to that, is THANK YOU! Thank you for giving me the OPPORTUNITY to step back into that squared circle, and do what it is I love doing best. THANK YOU, for giving me the CHANCE to sell t-shirts and to entertain all of the fans who put their ASSES IN THOSE SEATS, on a regular basis. If it wasn't for the WFW, I don't know exactly where I'd be right now. Back when I started out in this business in 1995, I never figured that I'd be washed up in 2003, at THIRTY-FOUR YEARS OLD! No one's given me a SHOT to rise to the TOP, since hell... 1998. That's a long time, that's A LOT OF FRUSTRATION! But you know, it'll all be worth it soon enough. The North American title may not be my championship of choice at the moment, but it's good enough for now. It's a start, on the COMEBACK TRAIL! 2003 is still pretty ripe, pretty fresh... and it's almost my FAVORITE time of the year. When the flowers are blossoming, the birds are chirping, and I'm in there WHIPPIN' SOME ASS! This summer, will be THE SUMMER OF SCOTTY! My ascent back to the top wouldn't be possible without the other wrestlers who bust THEIR asses, the management of the WFW, and most importantly the fans. Don't get me wrong, you're definitely NOT going to see this side of the SUPERSTAR on a regular basis, I just think that sometimes you gotta keep it real. As I sit in my living room right now, thinking, I don't need to come out here today and pull some bullsh*t stunt to make people laugh. I just need to be me. And that's exactly what I'm doing. To quote the great white hype, Eminem, "if it's gonna happen, it's gonna happen now." And at SHOCK and AWE, I will beat Mike McGee, ONE, TWO, THREE... in the center of that ring. And I will, move on to wrestle for the North American championship. And that's all I've got to say about that.
(FADE to black, as "Lose Yourself" by Eminem starts to play on Scotty's stereo..) Showtime24 — 11 April 2003 (FADEIN to 'Superstar' SCOTTY MICHAELS, watching a video clip from 1997, when he and Lex Taylor captured the CSWA world tag team championships. Scotty shakes his head slowly, as he turns to the camera...)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: You know, they say it's not a good thing to live in the past... that you should live for the HERE and the NOW! And that's all well and fine, but it's been SIX YEARS since I've held any kind of championship belt. That's a damn long time, when you were once one of the best in the business. I've gone through my periods of denial, depression, and now... DISGUST! Disgust, that a man such as Mike McGee doesn't even have a damn word to say about a match for the number one contendership for the North American title. At that Irish show, whatever the name was, I PROVED that I still got what it takes to hang in the world of professional wrestling. I made it to the final three of the Royal Rumble, and ALMOST... came THIS CLOSE (Holds up fingers closely together..) to getting a shot at the world heavyweight championship. But right now, I'm not too worried about that. I know my time will come, because I am THE VIEWER'S CHOICE! These people got me back here in the WFW because they wanted to see a REAL SUPERSTAR in the ranks of professional wrestling once again. SHOCK and AWE, that's exactly what it's going to be about when I continue my quest back to the top. Mike McGee, I don't got time for you anyway... and tomorrow, you're going to see why I'm still the man.
(FADEOUT...) GreggG — 2 May 2003 (CUT TO: The office setting of "Mr. Middle Management" Mike McGee. McGee holds a cup of coffee, and has his two-tone shirt and tie on.)
MCGEE: Now, I know it's been a while since I've been out here to address all of you low-lives and scumbags who don't understand a thing about what men such as myself do for a living. The beginning of the spring has been the busiest time of year around the Human Resources office. It's tax season, which means that I have to run around the office getting W-2's, W-9's and everything else to ensure that all of you people get your taxes in on time.
I'm sorry that you can't do basic math. I'm sorry that you can't write a simple check to our government. I'm sorry that you're poor and on welfare and don't have the time to do your duty to the United States government because you're too busy trying to rangle up all 14 of your dirty, disgusting, down syndromed, crippled children. BUT GUESS WHAT? There will be no company picnic at Six Flags this season. There will be no seminar on pro-active work behavior. There will only be one thing-- termination letters and one VERY upset, VERY angry administrative assistant.
My anger will be taken out on you Scotty Michaels. I'm sorry, Scotty, that no one in this sport remembers who you are or really cares about you. But that does not give you the right to improperly file your 401K form. No, Michaels, this promotion will *NOT* grant you matching funds as you did NOT check that off on your application-- hell, your application was FILED in crayons.
Shock and Awe? The Viewers Choice? Scotty Michaels, you're just like all those people at home-- another moron who does not appreciate the hard work that us folks who actually work for a living perform. As such, you can expect a Pink Slip in your mailbox the day after our match... after I leave you a broken, broken old man in the middle of the ring.
That's a command from me... your superior in middle management! (FTB)
