WFW — Roleplay

WFW Merrython: Winter Wonderland: Shawn Hart (c) vs. Scotty Michaels

13 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 13 December 2003 This match will be a Winter Wonderland Match held in snowy Central Park and will be for the WFW World Heavyweight Title.
The RP/angle deadline for this match is Wednesday, December 24th 11:59 PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
ShawnHartXXX — 15 December 2003 (FADE IN: Sitting at the desk of his personal office at his estate in Orlando, The Phenom... also known as Shawn Hart, fumbles confusedly through a droor, eventually retrieving a pencil and a pad of paper. Upon locating it, he places the pad before him and looks upward, pensively. He momentarily ponders some great perplexity, nods his head, then places the pencil onto the paper as he begins to write...)
V/O: "Dear... Mr. Michaels..." (A disgusted look graces the Phenom's face as he scoots back in his chair. He thinks to himself, the begins to chuckle.)
Hart: "HA! Dear Mr. Michaels... what the HELL am I thinkin'?!" (He shakes his head with a smile, erases his greeting, then proceeds to rewrite.) V/O: "DEAREST Scotty..." (He nods, approvingly.) V/O: "How... are... you?" (The Phenom pauses, then looks all around him, seemingly expecting some great ovation for this monumentous achievement.)
Hart: "Heh, this is easy!!" (He continues writing.) V/O: "Probably not too good after getting dropped on your melon, courtesy of a Sean Edmunds suplex, huh? I mean, don't you hate that?! I know, if I were you, I would hate that. You know what I do hate though, Scotty? I hate when some wack-ass WANKER like Minion feels the need to impose himself on my situation!! I mean, what in the BLUE BLAZES could be coursing through his obviously ravaged mind when he tries to pull a moronic stunt like that!! The whole thing is ridiculous, and I SWEAR to GOD, if he even thinks about..."
(The Phenom puts his hands to his temples, shakes his head about, then takes a deep breath.)
Hart: "Whoa, whoa... come on now PHAT Papa, gotta take it down a notch here. Calm down and cool off.
(He sits there, disappointed in himself, until finally coming to some great realization. He then rushes for another of the droors, whips it open, and busts out a 6 pack of imported mexican beer.)
Hart: "Whew... here we go..." (He cracks one of the bottles open, takes a couple BIG swallows, then slams it down on the desk, satisfied.)
V/O: *sings* "Muh-muh-muh-myyyy CORONA!!" (Hart licks the the excess brew from his lips, then resumes his letter.) V/O: "Anyway, I'm just writing you here to make sure that you know that, after I stymie your attempt at winning the WFW gold, causing you to miss that year-end deadline and send you packin', I hope there are no hard feelings. I think you're a very.... uhhh..."
(He taps the pencil against his chin in contemplation. Then returns it quickly to the paper.)
V/O: "NEAT!! I think you're a very neat guy, who's accomplished alot of very uhh... neat things during the course of one HELL of a NEAT career! Heck, in all honesty, if it hadn't been for you, I may not be in the position I am today! You clocked Manson, helped me go over on him, and by golly... I'll be DAMNED if I don't repay you for your deeds! In fact..."
(He pauses, then proceeds to dig through his pants pockets. He removes what appears to be an opened candom wrapper.)
Hart: "What the?!" (He holds it closer to his face, then looks inside the wrapper.) Hart: "GOOD GOD!!" (He tosses the wrapper off camera in disgust, shudders, the contiues to rummage through his pockets. After discovering and dismissing a pair of sunglasses, some pennies, and a pair of women's underwear, he finds a crumpled piece of paper, examines it, then snaps his fingers and returns to his writing.)
V/O: "In fact... enclosed is a two-for-one Grand Slam Breakfast coupon! Redeemable at participitating Denny's Restaurants nationwide!!"
(He stops, once again, then reexamines the coupon.) V/O: "Excuse me, redeemable at participating Denny's Restaurants in the Kissimmee/St. Cloud area. But either way, it's YOURS Scotty baby! Enjoy!"
(Hart glances quickly at his watch.) V/O: "I wonder what time Magnum is on?" (He ponders, once again, before bringing his correspondance to a close.) V/O: "Heh, bottom line... I appreciate you and what you've done here, but your time has come and gone my friend. Scotty Michaels is about to go the way of POGS, Yahoo Serious, and Coleco Vision. That bein' the hipetty dippety doo, just remember... we all LOOOOOOOVED you!! Deepest sympathy and utmost regards... Shawn J. Hart, Esquire!"
(He slams his pencil sharply the paper, forming the letter's last period. He then rubs his hands together and looks into the camera.)
Hart: "The PHENOM... has left... the building..." (FADE OUT.) ViewersChoice — 16 December 2003 (FADEIN to Central Park... the ground covered in a few inches of snow, from the various storms that have swept the North-East over the past week and a half. The camera is panning around Central Park, as a voice-over plays...)
V/O: Shawn Hart has held the WFW world heavyweight championship for close to 4 months, a long time in the world of professional wrestling these days. Scotty Michaels, a former North American champion... said he would win the WFW world title by the end of the year, or he'd be out. It all takes place here, it all takes place... Christmas day...
(The camera continues to pan around Central Park until it stops on a bench... where we see a man covered by a newspaper. The camera pulls in closer, and we see that it's Scotty Michaels. His hair grown out, and sporting a full beard. A car pulls up, and out steps Scotty's psychologist, Dr. Hartney. He runs over to the bench, tapping Scotty on the back..)
DR. HARTNEY: Scotty, what are you doing, man? (Scotty sits up, looking flustered...) SCOTTY MICHAELS: Where am I? What am I doing here? DR. HARTNEY: Scotty, I had an inside tip that you had wandered off from your hotel room. I've been searching the city for you, and I had an outside feeling that you'd end up here..
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Last thing I remember, I laid my head down in my hotel room at the Hilton... why am I here? Where is here?
DR. HARTNEY: The clerks at the hotel told me that you walked out of your hotel room, and that you looked out of it. I know you said you were having sleeping problems, but this is getting a little bit ridiculous. I thought you'd only wake up in places like on your couch, instead of the bed... or even on the toilet. This is outright ridiculous... I'm not sure that wrestling is the best idea for you, anymore.
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Wrestling? Who said anything about wrestli...?? Wait a minute, it's almost time, isn't it?
DR. HARTNEY: You need to put all of your family problems behind you, and really, I think you need to put wrestling behind you as well. I'm half-tempted to commit you...
SCOTTY MICHAELS: No, doc... I got this one. I'm facing Shawn Hart in just a couple of days. If I don't win that match, then you can bring out the straitjacket... because I'm sure I'll need it after that.
DR. HARTNEY: I just don't understand how it's gotten this bad. Before Halloween, you were fine... and then it was all downhill from there. I didn't think the realization of what you did would send you this far...
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Am I really crazy? DR. HARTNEY: You're not really crazy, you've just been ridiculously out of hand since you whacked Steve Matthews over the head with a chair at Turkey Day Terror.
SCOTTY MICHAELS: That was nearly two weeks ago, man... I need help. I don't think I have what it takes. Shawn Hart's at the top of his game, and where am I? The state that I'm in... I don't think I've got anything left...
(TO BE CONTINUED....) ViewersChoice — 21 December 2003 (FADEIN to the Hilton. We see Scotty Michaels laying next to his bed on the floor, sound asleep. The alarm clock goes off... it's 2 PM. Scotty tosses and turns for a moment, before standing up from the floor. He sits down on the bed, and puts his hand near his head. He's nearly unrecognizable to the Viewer's Choice that we once knew, with his longer matted hair and nappy beard. His cell phone rings, and we hear Scotty's side of the conversation...)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Yeah, I woke up in my own hotel room. I guess that's not too bad, but I was laying on the floor... so I'm not quite sure where the progress is there. (pause) Yeah, my title match is just a few days away, and I think I'm pretty focused on it. I'm glad that we got to talk a little more since that whole Central Park incident a few days back. (pause) Hey, like I said, man... if I don't win this match, I'm out of here. Not only out of the WFW, but professional wrestling as a whole... (pause) Alright doc, take care...
(Scotty hangs up the phone, and gets up from the bed and sits on a chair in the corner of the room.. his eyes baggy..)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Shawn Hart, I'm right where you want me. You wanted me to be at the bottom of my game so that you could get the easiest pinfall of your life, and it sure looks that way, doesn't it?
People have been doubting the downward spiral my life has taken over the past two months, but there's nothing to doubt here, buddy... and it couldn't have happened at a worse time. People think that this is all some big plot, because I'm such a desperate man that I just can't go in there and win the belt with my own ability... NOT TRUE!
I made a promise to the world, some time ago... I told EVERYONE, that I would walk out of the year 2003 with the WFW world heavyweight championship, and I'm not quite so sure I'm going to be able to back up those words.
You've been on quite the impressive streak since you won that belt several months back.. been a real great champ. But everybody knows that I should be the man wearing the title belt.
On the top of my game, NO DOUBT.. would I walk away with the belt. Right now, who really knows. My life has had some really strange twists over the past couple of months, and I'm not so sure that I like it.
My last hope of turning this thing around is to win the WFW world heavyweight title. If I don't do it, I might as well be committed. My doctor told me that my behavior has been really odd...
I've woken up in places I've never even visited before. It has nothing to do with being a one-time alcoholic... it has to do with my life, being FU**ED UP!
I hate it, Hart, I really hate it. But you do know, that as much as I need this championship... I will do ANYTHING I need to do at Merrython to become the champion. Try me, Hart.
Are you even there, or have you already written me off? I hope not, Shawnie.. because that will be your downfall. I need to do this, to prove everyone wrong. As a matter of fact, my family can go to hell, the fans can go to hell, and you, Shawn Hart... CAN GO TO HELL!
I won't stop Hart, I'll NEVER STOP! (FADEOUT, as Michaels slumps down in the chair, beginning to cry..) ShawnHartXXX — 22 December 2003 (FADE IN: At the Tiki Lounge, 'The Phenom' SHAWN HART, sporting a gloriously bright Hawaiian shirt and a wicker hat, along with his partner in crime, TINY, sits behind a grand piano. As he finishes the last of an apparent horde of cocktails, he tickles the ivory, singing from the bottom of his belly...)
Hart: "Waaaaaaaasted awwwwwwwaaaaaay again in MARGARITAVILLE!!!" (He closes his eyes momentarily, swaying his head from side to side like Stevie Wonder.) Hart: "Seeearchin' for mmmmmmmyyyyy... lost shaker of saaaaaaaalt..." (The Phenom licks his lips.) Hart: "Soooooooooome peeeeeeeeeeople claaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaim that there's a wooooooooooooo-maaaaaaaan to blaaaaaaaaaame...."
(Dramatic Pause...) Hart: "BUT I KNOOOOOOOOW..." Tiny: "Duh duh-duh-duh duuuuuuuuuummm..." Hart: "It's my OWN DAMN FAAAAULT!!!" Tiny: "Duh-duh-duh-duh DUUUUUUUM!" (As Shawn's rendition of the song reaches it's crescendo, the other patrons begin to whistle, cheer, and applaud. The Phenom obliges with a drunken bow, followed by a drunken stumble. He then falls backward, into his chair, runs a hand through his hair, and looks into the camera.)
Hart: "Mister... Scotty... MICHAELS!!" Tiny: "The man who refers to himself as 'Viewer's Choice..." (Hart glares at Tiny, annoyed.) Hart: "Kayfabe... Chester, c'mon now..." Tiny: "OH!! Beg your pardon, sir..." (Tiny shakes his head about, droops his eye, and begins to drool.) Hart: "AS I WAS SAYING... Scotty, my good man, you probably won't believe me, but I must say... it pains me to see you in such a bad way. It really does."
(A sullen expression graces the Phenom's face. He looks toward the ground, vexed by his opponent's misfortunes, then looks back to the camera.)
Hart: "Y'see, ever since I won that WFW title, ever since I knocked Doc Silver off the top of the hill, I've considered it my calling to take the ball that's been handed to me, and RUN... run like Jackie Joyner-Kersey, baby! But, unfortunately, I haven't been able to do that, Michaels. Nah-uh!"
(He glances off to the distance, seemingly pondering the happenings since his ascension to glory.)
Hart: "Cuz ever since I became WFW champion, I've been DENIED that spotlight I crave! Instead of riveting in-ring interviews, box-office press, and big money match-ups, week in and week out.. all it's been for the last FOUR MONTHS is meaningless tag team matches, weak opponents, and the complete and utter DEMISE of my most glorious creation, the C.O.C.K.S.! The whole shindig has been one missed cue after another and you, YOU Mr. Scotty Michaels, were to be my saving grace!"
(He looks straightforward, sternly.) Hart: "Hell, when I won this belt, Doc Silver was on his last legs. Yeah, I finally got that Manson monkey off my back... but let's face facts here... Michael Manson plateaued 2 or 3 years ago! All that bein' so, what does that leave yours truly, LE PHENOM, to be proud of? A 4 minute EXECUTION of Luster?! *hiccup* The ending of Diablo's time as a viable competitor and legitimate brother in arms? I mean, sorry if it sounds like I'm preachin' here, but DESPITE the fact that these were things I really couldn't control, the simple fact remains that, so long as we stick to the status quo, this reign has been nothing but a BLACK EYE on a career once held in high regard! I mean, I'm the PHENOM... by GOD!! I'm all about bein' PHENOMENAL... heads above the rest! Nowadays, I have trouble even getting RESPECT as a wrestler!!"
(Hart lowers his head in shame, nearly distraught.) Hart: "You see Michaels, you may have thought I came out here to belittle you and build myself up, but nothing could be further from the truth! I want you to be jiggy like jelly beans, up and rearin' to go, the one and onliest VIEWER'S CHOICE, Scotty god damn Michaels! Our pairing had potential to be something special, something to be remembered, and DAMMIT, it still can be!! I know you've set this silly deadline for yourself, and I know things haven't gone your way lately, but FORGET all that! Remember who you are and what you stand for! Drop the shrink, lose the schitzo act, and get yourself together!"
(Hart nabs another drink from a passing waitresses tray, climbs atop the piano, and raises his glass as he attempts to keep his balance.)
Hart: "You've been whinin' and moanin' like a pregnant woman, but HEY... here's to you, Scotty!! May you wake the hell UP, find your SACK, and give me the fight of my life!"
(He chugs down the liquor quickly, then hops back off the piano and gets right up near the camera's lens.)
Hart: "Now, if ya ask me... you try to go over, you're gonna go UNDER, nnnnnnndaddio!! But really, I'm BEGGIN' ya.... TRY to prove me wrong!! PLEASE!!"
(He chuckles to himself, then puts a hand to his forehead, as if to scan his surroundings.)
Hart: "Will the real Scotty Michaels PLEASE STAND UP... please stand up... Puh-llllleeeeease stand up! Heh...
(Hiccup.) Hart: "The PHENOM has left the building!" ViewersChoice — 22 December 2003 (FADEIN to 'Superstar' Scotty Michaels, walking the streets of Philadelphia. Michaels is wearing a pair of sweat pants and a hooded Eagles sweatshirt. He's got a bandana around his head, which holds his hair back. He looks run-down, worn-out, and the bottom of the barrel...)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: I figured that I'd return home for a few days, before the big match on Christmas day. I figured maybe the only thing that I'd have going for me would be to breathe the smell of the Philadelphia air, during the Christmas season. I've always been a big fan of Christmas.. one of the greatest holidays ever, as a child. While my family life has definitely taken a turn for the worse over the past few years, I still do enjoy the holiday season.
Anyhow, I figured that I'd come home because it would be nice to wake up in my OWN bed for a change, rather than in hotels and hell, in city parks. Well that didn't work out exactly as I planned, because my nightmares have carried on... and I've woken up in various spots around my apartment.
My nightmares, well... I must say nightmare has been the same thing... over and over again. I see myself, at Merrython, wrestling the best damn match of my life.. DESPITE ALL THE PROBLEMS, and still coming up JUST short of winning the world title. It's haunted me, and caused me more problems than anything I've ever done to the Matthews brothers, or vice versa...
And then we've got Shawn Hart, the Phenom... the best damn wrestler in this federation, since the day he signed a contract. I thought it was me for a long time, but what do I have to show for it? Yeah, I won the North American title, but I only ever got through a few title defenses and then I lost it. Yeah, I'm a great wrestler... right.
All of that doubt is everything that I said I was not since the day that *I* signed on the dotted line and became a part of the WFW. I was on top of the damn world, until my nightmares from that incident so many years ago changed all of that.
Little did I know that my demons would be my downfall in the end, and that it would ultimately lead to my demise in the WFW.
Yeah, self-confidence... what happened to it? It's all led me to this... the BIGGEST match of my life. Make or break time, and I don't even have faith in myself.
You want to call out the real Scotty Michaels, Hart... he he is. He's nothing! A shadow of the man that he once was.
Sometimes, I question even driving back up to New York, because I don't even know if I should waste my time... pack it up now, maybe.
But no, I'm not gonna do that, Hart. I'm gonna give it MY ALL, no matter what that is... and do my DAMNEST to take the championship belt from around your waist. Not too long ago, the announcers were talking about how Shawn Hart and Scotty Michaels are the top two wrestlers in the WFW...
That thought keeps me going. I hear it in my head every day, and it gives me the confidence to do what even I don't think that I can do.
So while you're out getting drunk and putting your yule log in Tiny's fireplace, I walk these streets... looking for the answer. Looking for the WILL, to win the world heavyweight championship...
I'll seeya at Merrython, Hart... I'll seeya at Merrython. (FADEOUT, as Scotty stops in front of one of his old hangouts, the Irish Pub, pauses... and then walks away...)
ShawnHartXXX — 24 December 2003 (CUE MUSIC: A slow, methodical strum of the acoustic guitar.) (FADE IN: 'The Phenom' SHAWN HART, appearing before us in all black and white video, strolls the windy streets of an empty city. As the slow tapping of a snare drum and cymbals kicks in, CUT TO: Hart, curled up in a corner, his clothing tattered and torn, looking to the sky, seemingly in pain...)
Hart V/O: *singing* "Scooooooooooooootttttyyyyyyyyyyyy..." (A roaring stab from an electrical guitar reverberates through your speakers.) V/O: "Yooooour naaaaaaaaaaaaame is SCOOOOOOOOOOOTTY!!" (CUT TO: TINY, sitting atop a rainbow-colored hill.) Tiny: "Dun-DUNNNNNNNNN!!" (CUT TO: Hart, curled up in the corner.) Hart: "You used to beeeeeee a SUPERSTAR..." (CUT TO: Hart at Pebble Beach, stuck in a sand trap on the 14th hole in full geezer attire.)
V/O: "Your golf scores alwaaaaaaaaaays UNDER PAR... but SCOTTYYYYYYYYYYYY!!!!" (CUT TO: Tiny, atop a yellow goat wearing sunglasses, singing in an eerily high tone.) Tiny: "SCOOOOOOOOOOTTY!!" (CUT TO: Hart, in black and white, walking down the street.) Hart: "It seeeeeems you've lost your waaaaaaaaaay..." (CUT TO: Hart, floating on his back in a large pool as a chilling violin solo commenses.) Hart: "You've DROOOOOOOOPPED your SACK, and BROKE your BACK, and SPILLED your GRAPE Kooooooooooool-AIIIIIIIIIID!!"
(As the cymbals crash, we cut to a shot of Hart, sitting alone in a dark room, his head set weepingly in his arms as he rests upon a wicker chair.)
V/O: "Buuut Scoooooooootty..." (CUT TO: Tiny, table dancing for a plethora of Japanese businessmen.) Tiny: "Mmmmmmmmmmmmmmmm...Scooooooooooootty..." Hart: "Meeeeeeerry Christmas to SCOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOTTYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYY YYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYY!!!"
