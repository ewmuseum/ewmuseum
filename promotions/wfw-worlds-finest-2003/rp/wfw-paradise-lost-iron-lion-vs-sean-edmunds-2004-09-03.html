WFW Paradise Lost: Iron Lion vs. Sean Edmunds

3 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 3 September 2004 The RP/angle deadline is Wednesday, September 15 11:59 PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
JABolich — 4 September 2004 (FADEIN: IRON LION sitting on a bench in the gym, a towel draped over his shoulders, hands resting over his thighs.)
IRON LION: And so here we are... Before I go any further, I'm going to address someone. Jared. Wells. I'm tempted, very tempted, to give you your due, Jared. But now that I think about it, I realize that you proved absolutely nothing. You went out to that ring and you gave it your best - which amounted to approximately three moves before I launched you into the third row and ripped your face off. The world saw me manhandle you without so much as batting an eyelash.
The world ALSO saw Anarky emerge at the beginning of the match to distract me, allowing you the window you needed to destroy my knee.
I knew all along that you couldn't defeat me on your own, Jared. You needed Anarky to help you gain the unfair advantage. Were it not for that knee giving out on me I would have destroyed you outright. Congratulations, Jared. You managed to secure a fluke win over a man with a bum knee thanks to your skull-faced buddy giving you a helping hand. I'm sure you take great pride in that, but it just proves to me how WEAK you really are.
You haven't heard the last of me, Wells. We will meet again, and when we do there will be no Anarky around to pull your ass out of the fire.
Now then. On to more pressing matters. Sean Edmunds, I'm not the type of man to hold grudges. Thus, I have no beef with you - merely observations. I've seen what kind of man you are. You're small - weak. Like so many others here you see only what you want to see, hear only what you want to hear, and know only what suits your purposes. You believe the world revolves around you and that nobody else can hold a candle to you. Obviously you are mistaken.
Don't take the beating I'm going to give you personally, Sean. I despise what you stand for, but you yourself have not wronged me; thus, my issue is not with you personally.
Paradise Lost marks my night of redemption. Jared Wells may have convinced himself and those around him that he proved me wrong, but when you and I step into the ring, Sean, I will be proven anything but. I may have been set back, but no war is won in a day - and each time I am turned back I return stronger than ever before, more determined to wage the war against corruption and emerge the victor. It is not a matter of whether or not I will win. It is merely a matter of when.
Prepare yourself, Sean. My redemption is nigh. (FADEOUT) TheOriginalSE — 11 September 2004 (FADEIN: 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds sitting behind his Presidential Desk, wearing a suit and tie. Edmunds shuffles some papers into place and puts them on his desk.)
EDMUNDS: "So it seems as if Copycat had, if you might want to say, earned his World title shot. (shrugs) What a shame, really. I kind of enjoyed keeping him down in the mid-card, along with others who try their hardest to make it to the top. But, alas, a win over 'Simply Sensational' guarantees him celebrity. It guarantees him an ascension to the WFW spotlight. Just as it did Alex Wylde. But it is going to end. I am not going to allow myself to make an instant celebrity, allow Iron Lion to become instantly credible, by failing to come out on top at Paradise Lost. My top priority is myself. And also to make sure that the WFW is just exactly that, the World's Finest Wrestling. It just can't be with people like Iron Lion ... Copycat ... Richard Gideon running around trying to be in the spotlight. I'm sorry, Iron Lion ... perhaps one day, when I'm not the President of the WFW, you'll have your shot. But as long as I'm in power, as long as I have a say, you can guaran-damn-tee that your time will be next to nothing. Your redemption, as you so call it, will be NIL at Paradise Lost. And that's a promise."
(Edmunds stands up and walks over to the window. He shuts the blinds and then proceeds to walk over to the door.)
EDMUNDS: "You see what I have become, Iron Lion? I have become larger than life here in World's Finest Wrestling. And there's good reasons for that. There are good reasons why everytime I listen to Copycat speak I can hear my own name five, six, seven times. There are good reasons why everytime I listen to LOVE, I can hear my name. It is because I have worked, and I have succeeded where you will fail; against the competitors who matter most. You talk about Jared Wells as if he were actually some type of challenge, Iron Lion. You say that you are going to give him some sort of props, which you eventually do ... and it makes me wonder ... just where do you lie? Am I overestimating your talent as I prepare for this match? It seems like just yesterday Jared Wells was my lackey, my b*tch. Running around getting me coffee, getting me girls ... getting me World titles. And today he's doing the same thing .. for Anarky. And I'm sure he couldn't have progressed much in the talent portion of wrestling. Jared Wells was always a one trick pony .. and that's why I am worried about you, Iron Lion."
(Edmunds pauses at the door as he turns and looks around the office. After making sure everything is okay and in perfect order, he opens the door. Placing one hand on each knob he looks at the camera.)
EDMUNDS: "Afterall, one of my job descriptions is to make sure that every single wrestler, yes, even those who may never make it to my plateau, are in good mind, and good health. For you to actually give recognition to Jared Wells ... scares me. The fact that you are talking about a bum knee ... scares me. Perhaps you should just pull out of this match, Iron Lion. Your mental and physical handicaps would only worsen after Paradise Lost. Help me, help you. But I get a kick out of you calling me weak. I never knew a weak man who could stay in the business for over 12 years, Lion. I never knew a weak man who could be a three-time World Champion in one promotion ... or the WFW World Champion. I have persevered through all that .. and yet I continue to work. I continue to move ahead and reach higher and higher. One of these days you will see me with the WFW World title back around my waist. And you will look up at me, standing high above you .. and think to yourself, 'I had the honor ... the privilege ... the luck ... to have my ass handed to me by Sean Edmunds.' And THAT, my second rate friend, is a fact."
(Edmunds flicks off the lights, yet he stands in the doorway. The light from the hallway illuminates half his face as he looks at the camera.)
EDMUNDS: "Don't adjust your television sets .... I *AM* this Sensational!" (FADEOUT: As Edmunds shuts the door, leaving the room in darkness.) JABolich — 15 September 2004 (FADEIN: IRON LION in front of a backdrop.) IRON LION: I don't need to adjust my television, Sean. I can see through your 'sensational' bull****.
Let's get one thing clear right now. I think you're full of sh*t. You don't seem to understand the nature of your weakness, Edmunds. It has nothing to do with your tenure - because when you get right down to it, tenure's just a number.
Take a look at Anarky over there. The man's been around for a decade. By your logic he should've handed me a severe beating. Yet who won that match, Sean? Oh yeah, that's right.
But go ahead, throw those years at me as if they really mean something. Go ahead and shill yourself as a three-time champion, but let's gloss over the fact that your last run at the top lasted all of one title defense. And let's also gloss over the fact that in those twelve years you've lost eleven times out of twelve to the same man.
Yes, let's just gloss over all the negatives, because the whole world knows that the sun only shines on Sean Edmunds and the rest of us can go fly a kite.
And THAT, Sean, is the source of your weakness - the very pedestal that you place yourself upon.
But don't worry too much about it. You're not entirely alone. You're not special. You don't stand out in a crowd. You're not really all that sensational. You, Sean, are another face in the crowd - another big mouth in a world full of them.
I bet you'd LIKE me to pull out of this match, wouldn't you, tough guy? What's wrong, got a few doubts simmering below the surface? Surely so sensational a guy like you wouldn't even THINK of asking his opponent to back out.
Get off your soapbox, Edmunds. You talk as if I care about gaining some sort of celebrity by defeating you - and in doing so you prove that you have NO IDEA WHAT I'M ALL ABOUT. If you haven't picked up on the fact that I didn't come here to gain fame yet, then there is no hope for you.
A wise man once offered some sage advice - "Know thine enemy." Congratulations. You don't know a damn thing about your enemy. Whether or not I get some sort of 'shot' is irrelevant to me. I come here to wrestle and prove to myself and to others that inner strength and moral fortitude still have a place in this sport. That, Sean, is why I intend to defeat you. You embody most of the virtues that I am against, and as such you are to be eliminated.
You only think you stand above me, Sean. But you'll be singing a different tune when I come along and kick that pedestal out from under you. Let's see how Sensational you are when you're on your back for the count with the lights glaring in your eyes.
Time to adjust YOUR television set, Sean. Not that it'll matter. I'm on all the networks. And wouldn't you know it. It's a regular marathon - Pride, Strength, and Honor, back to back to back.
Now THAT... THAT is sensational. (FADEOUT) TheOriginalSE — 16 September 2004 (FADEIN: 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds is walking along the beach somewhere undisclosed. The wind is blowing through his hair, and he's got some shades on protecting him from the sun's glare. He stops at a bike rack. Leaning on the rack he looks out into the ocean.)
EDMUNDS: "Know thine enemy, Iron Lion. In a match that I actually cared about, perhaps I would have taken the time to know my enemy. But in our match it isn't so. I do not consider you a challenge. Hell, nor do I consider you an enemy. To be an enemy, you must constitute some sort of worth, and you, Iron Lion, are worthless in my eyes. You come out here and talk about how if tenure truly mattered then Anarky should have beaten you in the ring. Yet you neglect to take into account the vast differences in talent that appear between Anarky and I. Anarky is a man of little talent, I, on the other hand, possess an exuberant amount. You also, like past opponents of mine have done, try to note my one title defense as the WFW World Champion as a negative mark on my record here. Yet you fail to take into account that I busted my ass for a year .. show in, show out .. wrestling men you could only DREAM of taking on. I at least have a World title to show for my time here in World's Finest Wrestling .. whereas you ... you have a loss to Jared Wells to show for yours. And then there's your attempt to instill the old-school, pure wrestling mentality into WFW .. as if you somehow, my vast repetoire of technical and aerial moves fails to impress Iron Lion. In that very ring, Iron Lion will come face to face with a TRUE mat technician. One who will not only match, but outwrestle, him in the ring. Try and take away my pedastal, Iron Lion, but in the meantime, you won't even see me as I am pulling your whole reason for existence here in the WFW right from underneath your feet."
(Edmunds continues to stare out at the water ... which .. for some reason .. hasn't been moving in and out like you'd expect from the ocean..)
EDMUNDS: "Iron Lion would almost make a nice politician. Distorting the facts for which he has no grasp on. Bringing up the fact that I have a very subpar record against Jean Rabesque. Yet what does he leave out? He leaves out the fact that he really has NO CLUE the history between Jean and I. The fact that Jean and I have only had two encounters in the past six years. The fact that of those two encounters I am 1 and 1 against him. The fact that the majority of those other losses came in the early being of my career ... but that doesn't matter to Lion. He likes to think that what happened twelve years ago, when I first started wrestling, is still indicative of what will happen today. Unfortunately for you, Lion, this isn't 1993. I am not one year into the business .. green as a $100 bill. With this so called tenure that you quickly dismiss as insignificant, comes the very significant experience. As one to talk about loud-mouths being lost in a sea of loud-mouths, there have been plenty of pure technical wrestlers who want to re-instate the old-school mentality back in the game. To bring honor back into wrestling. I've seen them rise and fall like the ocean, Iron Lion. You're just the latest reincarnation of a goal thats destined to fail. And soon I will watch as you, too, will slowly fade out of the WFW .. with nothing to leave in its history books. Merely a name among many. Merely a win for some, including myself, and a loss for others, like Anarky. But that's your issue to deal with, Iron Lion, because I don't meddle in such petty affairs."
(Edmunds takes off the shades and turns to the camera.) EDMUNDS: "I know you don't know your history, Lion. So I'll forgive your trespasses. But if you plan on walking into Paradise Lost and pinning my shoulders to the mat .. you are sorely mistaken. You see, Lion. I've got the most evasive shoulders in the business. In over a year here in WFW ... I've only been pinned ONCE. (pauses and smiles) ONCE, Lion. And that one time I wish I could take back... against Alex Wylde, when I lost the WFW World Title. But I had fought my way show after show .. putting myself on the line .. and I finally ran out of steam. But that won't happen again. So, Lion, you plan on doing something that Larry Tact couldn't do. That Copycat couldn't do. That Pulsar couldn't do. That Richard Gideon couldn't do. That Scotty Michaels couldn't do. You are telling me that you can pin my shoulders to the mat. (laughs) You're not half the wrestler those men are. I'm going to prove this to you at Paradise Lost. You might be on every network, Lion... but its for all the wrong reasons. While people look at me, they see a man they know. A man they appreciate... but more importantly, a man who has been pinned only ONE TIME in over a year. (smirks) When they look at Iron Lion on the television .. well .. they simply turn the channel."
(Edmunds slides the shades back on his face. As he does so, a technician walks into the shot on the left w/ a donut .. but he quickly steps out of the view as we hear some yelling..)
EDMUNDS: "Don't adjust your television set, Iron Lion ... I *AM* this sensational." (FADEOUT: As Edmunds holds up one finger ...)
