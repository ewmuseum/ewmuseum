WFW — Roleplay

Spirit of NGEN: Richard Gideon vs. Sean Edmunds

27 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 27 July 2003 The RP/angle deadline is August 11th. Send in all angles to [detail withheld] TheOriginalSE — 29 July 2003 ((FADEIN: A highway. Cars are zooming past the camera as it focuses on a figure standing on the other side. CUTTO: The other side as we now see that the figure is 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds. He is standing in on the side of the road. His car is a little ways down the road, but not too far. He is standing there looking at a dead racoon that has been swatted to the breakdown lane.))
EDMUNDS: "So this is the end of this creature's run. Left in a bloody heap on the side of the highway with carz zipping past not giving a second look. (shakes head) What a shame. Just imagine how many times people have drove by it, either not even noticing it, or glancing quickly to see what it was, then turning back to their lives once they realize it was just a racoon. It's a shame .. really. It's a shame because this racoon right here is one of thousands, possibly millions, of similar situations. Where they are stopped dead in their tracks, left at the side of the road, and never looked upon again. They just disintegrate and blow away in the night's breeze. And yet here I am. Standing above it, giving it the attention that the denizens of the city probably never even thought to do. Even those who drive this highway day and night, to and fro work, every day of the week."
((Edmunds bends down and looks at it. Cars continue to drive by, slower than usual though, because everyone wants to know what people are doing when their cars are pulled to the side..))
EDMUNDS: "It's funny how the small things in our lives are cast aside to make room for the more (stresses) important things, right? How we don't give recognition to those things in our lives that are simple. How we cast aside our personal racoons for the greater things. I'm not going to lie, (pauses) I do it, too. Which is why coming into this match I was originally going to come out and do a spiel about how I was going to completely overrun Richard Gideon. (smiles) But on second thought, something which I rarely do, I decided that perhaps for my first match in the WFW, especially for the Spirit of NGEN, I would take a different route. Instead of talking and talking, I am going to engage in the conversation with my opponent. I'm going to listen, for once, to what he's got to say. And every night, after he's decided to speak up, I am going to ponder the very words that come out of his mouth."
((Edmunds stands up and turns around. He begins to walk back to his car.)) EDMUNDS: "In my life I could just toss Richard Gideon to the side of the road, and pretend to not give him a second glance ... but you're lucky, Richard. Because instead I am going to treat you as a true challenge. I've been out of wrestling now for seven months. And likewise, I am not going to lie to you. I have not trained very much. I've kept myself in good shape, but I havent worked on endurance or skill definition. I've had a lot of time to myself and I've come to the conclusion through these past months, that no matter what I do, it will be proactive, and not reactive. I got stuck in a situation, a life, of reaction. I would sit back and wait, something would happen, and then I would act. Now that I've experienced life away from the ring, I can say that I am definitely going to be more proactive. I am going to take charge of what I had been missing .. a future, (pauses) MY future."
((Edmunds gets to his car. He hits the automated lock button to unlock his car.)) EDMUNDS: "Richard Gideon .. you are the first step in this proactive future. And while you may not think much of me, I will not place you in the proverbial breakdown lane just yet. The Spirit of NGEN runs through everyone who participates in Orlando .. and that spirit is alive and well in Sean Edmunds. I don't know much about you, Richard .. but I will not place you in the back of my mind, tucked away somewhere. I will not push you aside to make room for the (smirks) more important things in my life. I made the decision to come to WFW because I wanted something different. And I am not going to slip into my old ways .. I'll see you in Orlando, Richard .. but don't be surprised if afterwards, you find yourself down and out ... but don't make the same mistake that these people do every day. Do not drive by your failures or your shortcomings .. but stop, reflect, and move on. That's what I'm learning to do .. and that's what makes me 'SIMPLY SENSATIONAL'."
((FADEOUT: As Edmunds gets into his car. CUTTO: A shot further away which shows Edmunds pulling onto the highway and then pulling away.))
Methos17 — 30 July 2003 (The scene is a familiar one, albeit one we haven't opened to in a while: the study of one Richard "The System" Gideon. It seems bare now, almost empty. The books and tapes are gone, replaced with bare walls and glass. The desk, of which consists rich mahogany and brass is also gone, as is his chair. There is only Gideon, seated in the center of the room. He wears a WFW/NGEN T-shirt which bears the PPV's logo and date. His jeans are a bit tattered, but no more worse for wear than usual. As he notices the camera crew he beckons them forward, head remaining focused on the hard wood floor...)
Richard Gideon: Sean Edmunds...a name I have yet to hear of...but with the scant few months I've been here that should come as no surprise. I'm not going to start my opening salvo in this pre match diatribe with bravado or the usual gusto, since frankly, I'm just getting out of a slump and as my old football coach used to tell me, "a win is a win when you're getting out of a slump...take as many as you can until you're out of it."
(Gideon Pauses, and adjusts his neck a bit) Richard Gideon: So that means Mr. Edmunds, I will be prepping for this match more than I normally would. Why? Because the last PPV the WFW had I was in the Main Event, fighting for the title...now I'm barely semi - main if that, and facing a veteran who's looking to reclaim a lost luster of days gone by. I don't know you Edmunds, but I know ME. I know that when I get into that ring, anything less than a win is unacceptable. I'm going to climb up that mountain again, and this time I'm staying there for a loooong time. My time as Hart's lackey taught me one thing: attitude is everything. You say you're "Simply Sensational" and I dig that. But you my friend are going up against "The System", and it will always win out in the end. At this PPV in Orlando, The Spirit of NGEN will reborn in one Richard Gideon. I may not have been around for it the first time, but I'll be making up for that to be sure.
(Gideon Stands up and looks dead on into the camera) Gideon: So I await your rpply Mr Edmunds. I await it with bated breath, because the closer the PPV comes, the more I'm going to feel it....feel the need...the rush...to excite the crowd and prove I'm the best...and if I have to go through you to do it...I feel sorry for you. The Super Rookie has spoken. If you'll excuse me, I have a training session to get to.
(Gideon turns to leave, and then stops, talking back to the camera as he does so) Gideon: And if you don't think I'll be ready for you and whatever you got Sensational One...It's your own fault.
(Fade to White) TheOriginalSE — 31 July 2003 ((FADEIN: 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds standing in front of an WFW backdrop which is promoting the next pay per view in which he'll be facing Richard Gideon. Edmunds is in full attire, his white sequin top sparkling in the lighting. He stands there with his hands on his hips as he tries to decide when he wants to begin speaking..))
EDMUNDS: "I must have watched your last promo at least a dozen times, Richard. Studying your face and your voice for different inflections. For any hint of fear or lack of confidence. And I must say, you are really good at hiding it. But that doesn't mean that its not there .. and I'm sure soon enough you'll slip or forget, and it will present itself. And each time I watched this tape, I've tried to catch something new. A quick succession of blinking, or a slight stumble over a word.. Something that will give me the insight on what you're really thinking, because lets face the facts, Richard, you're not putting all your cards on the table. (smiles) Now whether or not that should worry me? I'll tell you right now that it doesn't. I've been in matches of much higher importance than this one. Not to say that this match isn't important. It's my very first match in the WFW, and on their pay per view giving homage to NGEN!"
((Edmunds smiles as he sighs. He folds his hands across his chest as he begins to speak again.))
EDMUNDS: "So make no mistake about it, Gideon, I will be very ready for this match. (eyes narrow) Now as for your comments about 'Simply Sensational' being a veteran trying to recapture my lost luster .. (shakes head) I haven't lost anything, Gideon. The last pay per view I was on, I was part of the triple main event in a Submissions Only match .. so don't tell me that I'm trying to regain something. Granted I haven't wrestled since January, but I keep myself in well enough shape both mentally and physically that there is nothing to regain. I came to the WFW for freshness.. for a change .. and I'm getting that change, Richard. You just happen to be the first person on my path. I'm looking forward to this match for a few reasons. The first and foremost is that I will be wrestling someone I've never wrestled before. That excites me. To test myself against someone who I haven't wrestled before will be a somewhat new experience. But its one that I will treasure and dream about until that fateful night when we meet in Orlando.."
((Edmunds starts to look around as the lighting dims a little ..)) EDMUNDS: "Our time tonight is almost up. "The System" is going to square off one on one with 'Simply Sensational' at this next pay per view. It's a shame you don't know me, Richard.. because then you'd know that I manipulate the system to my benefit .. and it will be no different on the pay per view, in front of thousands and thousands of people. I hope I haven't kept you waiting for too long with my response, Richard. I don't want the anticipation to dwell up inside of you to an unhealthy degree. I only want the best for your health up until we meet in the ring. Because I want everyone in the audience, everyone in the back, and everyone sitting at home, to watch as the system overloads when it realizes that Sean Edmunds IS 'Simply Sensational' ..."
((FADEOUT: As Edmunds walks off screen and two crew members take down the WFW backdrop revealing a blue hanging curtain. The camera slowly fades to black as "Brain Stew" by Green Day picks up.))
Methos17 — 1 August 2003 (The scene opens with various people setting up the stage and such for the WFW PPV in picturesque Orlando, FL. As the lighting guys test the various set-ups and such, Richard Gideon is seated in one of the rafter seats, looking on with a combination of fascination and detachment. His jeans and T-shirt haven't changed it seems and his beard is becoming unkempt a bit. He smiles, and begins to speak to no one in particular)
Gideon: Let's see...I'm going to overload, be manipulated and play the fool in our match? I think not Mr Edmunds. I'm Richard "The System" Gideon. I don't break down. I don't overload. Win or Lose at this PPV, the only thing that can beat me is me. You say that you've kept yourself in shape and will simply just come in and use me at your litmus test? If you are planning on that to work, I'm going to win this match faster than I thought. Let me break this down for you Sean. You think that you can go on a little vaca and take on one of the youngest and brightest stars in the WFW? I don't think so Jack! I've done too much to take a step back in being "utility talent". This here (taps chest) isn't utility talent. I am not a "tune up" for anyone! So you get ready...train, prepare...show me how Sensational you can get. Because when you try and pull that stuff on me in the ring you're getting a surprise. I'm not falling for it. I've been beaten by vets better than you, and come middle of August, in front of my largest crowd to date...I beat another vet.
(Gideon looks up toward the top of the arena) Gideon: Y'know its funny...I've taken guys to the limit here in every match, now they give me you, who wants me to take him to his...its what I do for a living Sean. I take anyone and anything on. To prove I'm the best. To prove that "The System" never fails. When I get in that ring with you...the spirit of NGEN will be proven. One representing its past, another its future. Sean Edmunds, I hope you are as Sensational as you say...because the Super Rook is looking for another vet notch on his 'ole belt, and you are looking like you're it.
(Gideon gets up and hops down the stairs to the center of the floor, where the WFW Ring will be.)
Gideon: This is the spot. The spot where we will collide. Where we will prove to the fans and each other the spirit of NGEN is alive. Don't prove me and the WFW board wrong Sean. Because if you don't show up giving it your all...you're a dead man in that ring. And when I say dead man I mean I will hurt you in ways people can only dream. I didn't spend months tearing my body apart to learn how to knit. I am a professional wrestler. I show up when its time to play and I take my craft seriously. You're a veteran, and to be one means you are good, or lucky, or both. We'll find out at the PPV. My name is Richard Gideon...and The System is coming for you.
(Fade to White) TheOriginalSE — 2 August 2003 ((FADEIN: Sean Edmunds is sitting in a rocking chair in his hotel room in Florida .. on his lap is a ball of yarn with a horribly knitted something .. he is listening to Enya and rocking slowly back and forth. He glances at the camera and puts down his needles. He just sighs as he begins to speak.))
EDMUNDS: "I guess all that time I've spent knitting hasn't done me any good, Richard. Perhaps I should have spent more time at it .. maybe have come back in a few months after I've gotten it down? You know (pauses) that way you could have probably been right when you said that all I did was learn how to knit. (smiles) And here I am digressing without anything before it to digress from! You seem to misunderstand me, Richard. I know you're a rookie. I have heard some good things about you, too.. now whether or not you're ACTUALLY good enough to take me on in that ring will be found out at Spirit of NGEN. Here you go hyping up our match in your head... dubbing it the past versus the future. Yet you do not realize, 'Simply Sensational' is the past, the present AND the future. Last time I checked, Richard, being 29 does not make me the disabled, disadvantaged veteran that you hope for. I've been wrestling in these rings, the rings that you are getting used to here in the WFW, for eleven years now. I've felt the sting of defeat plenty of times. I've felt my blood, sweat and tears fall upon deaf ears. I have been in matches where I was totally smashed into pieces .. hell, you can check my doctor's records if you want .. my knee has been through more (BLEEP) than its supposed to have been. (sighs) But that was in the past, Richard.. the past that you want to associate me with every chance you get."
((Edmunds throws the yarn on the bed next to him as he gets up from the rocking chair. He walks over to the cd player and turns off Enya.))
EDMUNDS: "I suppose in my spare time, knitting and gardening, I also picked up the habit of listening to Enya, right? (chuckles) But seriously, Richard, listen to yourself. You are competing against eleven years of experience and you're telling yourself that you can handle it. That 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds is going to be next on your 'vet' list. (shakes head) Don't get stuck in a dreamworld, Gideon. Because once it comes crashing to the floor it doesn't stop. You're right, Richard. I do want you to take me to my limit. I want you to try to take me to my limit. (loses smirk and becomes serious) But if you try, Richard, you better be ready for some serious eye opening. I don't have a limit that you can reach. I don't have a breaking point that you can touch. I don't have, Richard, weakness that you are ready to exploit. You are stuck with someone who is 'Simply Sensational' in every sense of the word. And if you want to try to compare gun size, Gideon, then its your prerogative. But don't whine and make excuses when you fail to live up to your OWN expectations. Unlike you, Richard, I know how to play the game the way its played best. And you are challenging me to play that game."
((Edmunds walks over to the bathroom door now and turns on the light. He walks in to take a piss, and he continues to talk from the bathroom while the camera stays where it is, on the other side of the wall, in the main room.))
EDMUNDS: "Whether I call you utility talent, the Super Rookie, or the System, you're still going to be one step under me, Richard. You just lack the necessary resources in this match. But I'm not going to stop you from trying. I came to the WFW to try something new. To face new people and get new experiences. You're the first person in my path, Richard, and no less at the pay per view. The Spirit of NGEN is alive and well, like you've said over and over. Unfortunately, Richard, NGEN is no longer around, and if you want to be a representation of a defunct entity, then go right ahead. Because I live, breathe, and wrestle for WFW. And perhaps, Richard, that'll be the biggest contribution that I can give to this match."
((Edmunds flushes the toilet and walks over to the sink and washes his hands. He dries them and then walks back into the main room.))
EDMUNDS: "I'm either good, lucky, or both, Richard. I'd say I'm both. I'm good enough to continue to make my mark, and I'm lucky enough to get naive opponents like yourself who believe that they can even attempt to put me to the test. (smiles) And Gideon, don't you worry. I'll give you every chance in the world to show you just why I am (pauses) 'Simply Sensational'."
((FADEOUT: As Edmunds takes the yarn from the bed and sits back in the rocking chair .. he starts rocking and knitting again as the shot fades to black.))
Methos17 — 7 August 2003 (The scene opens with Richard Gideon standing in the WFW Ring. Wearing a blue and red jumpsuit, he bounces off the ropes for a bit and then sits on the top rope. Beckoning the camera crew over from their ringside position, he begins to speak)
Gideon: Well Sean...seems that you are ready...lucky and good enough eh? Pardon my absence from our little tete a tete, but I do have some...pressing issues I just had to get to. Anyway, back to the task at hand a signed, sealed and delivered verbal [censored] slapping courtesy of one Richard Gideon. So you're just the king of all that is huh? Just STROLL on in here, no one can put you to the test...breeze in...win the belt...that it? That your "PLAN"? Like I said a few days ago...I've taken who some might arguably call THE Spirit of NGEN, Doc Silver, to his limit. You don't think I can take you to yours? Ok...to talk is one thing, to do is another...I'll see how you like me when I've got another notch in the win column post Spirit of NGEN.
(Gideon adjusts himself a little better on the top rope) Gideon: I'm not bragging when I say I'm one of the best up and coming stars...I'm just telling you what I've been told after every match I've been in here...win or lose. I bring my all into that ring, and though you may have forgotten things I have yet to learn...I'm still learning...and that my friend makes me dangerous. Unlike you, I appreciate that which has come before, which is why I am so focused on representing a 'new wave' NGEN come the PPV. The WFW is my home, but if the foundation it was built upon wasn't solid I never would have signed on. You live and breathe this league? OKAY. We'll see if you are come Spirit of NGEN. We'll see if the GREAT Sean Edmunds is able to hang with The System. I don't know about you Sean...but I know about me...I know I'm going to be you...you just haven't realized it yet.
(Gideon hops off the ropes and kneels) Gideon: After all...I'm just going to need three seconds of your time...those three seconds could happen 10, 20, 30 minutes in...but they'll happen. Three seconds, no more no less...I'm thank you for those three seconds Sean....they'll be the sweetest I've tasted here. Because they'll be my first...wait, Doc...no, he got knocked out...yeah...my first PPV pinfall. See you soon Mr. Sensational.
(Fade to White) TheOriginalSE — 11 August 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Aug-11-03 AT 09:28&nbsp;AM (EST)] ((FADEIN: "Simply Sensational" Sean Edmunds
walking to his dressing room. He is walking down a hallway carrying a few bags. He stops in front of his door and opens it. He tosses the bags inside and shuts the door, locking it. He turns and starts walking back, and catches the camera.)) EDMUNDS: "So here we are a few days before Spirit of NGEN, and my opponent, who had been MIA for a bit, decides to come back spewing lies and misconceptions. You ever said that I would come to WFW and walk right through everyone, Richard? Who said that "Simply Sensational" would come to WFW and take the title right away? (shakes head) Certainly not I, Richard.. so stop trying to put words in my mouth. Don't paint the picture if you're not the artist. If there is anyone who seems to think they can walk into WFW and go straight to the top its you. You talk a lot of smack for a rookie .. even a super rookie as you seem to like to call yourself. But the one thing that will never change, Richard, is the fact that you don't have it in you to compete with people like me. Because you're right. I have probably forgot more than you have learned. But the only reason that I have forgotten about them is because I found something more effective to do. You can talk about getting another notch in your win column at Spirit of NGEN .. but the brutal (pauses) honest truth is that you won't come close." ((Edmunds picks up his other bags and starts walking towards his room.)) EDMUNDS: "You need to be careful of who you have whispering in your ears, Richard. I fell victim to that once when I first started out too. And I paid for it greatly. Of course the difference was that I actually had the talent .. and I was being kept down. You (smiles) you need to make sure that you are not letting certain people feed your ego just to do their bidding. I mean, you want to have a new wave of NGEN? Why? Because you appreciate what has come before you? Please, Richard, spare me the bull(BLEEP) Face it. NGEN is dead and gone. The Spirit of NGEN will be a watered down, second rate version of its old self. And I know it would be great for you to get a win on a second rate show ... but the fact of the matter is that Sean Edmunds is no second rate talent. And I guess you'll find out first hand at Spirit of NGEN. All of that talent, Richard, that you might have inside of you.. all of that eagerness to learn ... will turn out to be a bunch of nothing. Because as you said, Richard.. you are still learning .. and that makes you dangerous .. (sighs) but only to yourself."
((Edmunds begins shutting the door but stops and leaves it open a crack.)) EDMUNDS: ".. At Spirit of NGEN ... The System will be overridden ... and it will be done by Sean Edmunds. Why? Because I'm (pauses) Simply Sensational." ((FADEOUT: As Edmunds slams the door.))
