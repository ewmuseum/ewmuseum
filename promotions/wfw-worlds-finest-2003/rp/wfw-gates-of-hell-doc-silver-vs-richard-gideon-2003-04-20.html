WFW — Roleplay

WFW Gates of Hell: Doc Silver vs. Richard Gideon

20 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 20 April 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Apr-20-03 AT 02:31&nbsp;PM (EST)]This match is for the WFW World Heavyweight title, RP/angle deadline is April 30th, post your promos here!
Methos17 — 20 April 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Apr-20-03 AT 05:55&nbsp;PM (EST)](The scene is ten minutes after the tag match. Gideon is in the WFW locker room, sweating and looking at the floor. As Mike the cameraman approaches, Gideon looks up. However, there is no smile, no cockiness. There is almost a reserved look in his face.)
Gideon: Doc...I don't know what to say. I got the duke in this match. No crazyness, no balcony diving. Just straight up out-wrestled you. Don't misunderstand me though, I'm not bragging. For once, I'm not bragging. I'm two weeks away...Two weeks away from proving I'm the real deal. That my training, all the people who believed and sacrificed for me can be validated. I want to thank you Doc. Tonight was historic in a number of ways. What my partner, Psycho, could not do, what many haven't ever done. I pinned you Doc. I wrestled in my first semi-main event...and in two weeks I wrestle in my first main event.
(Gideon grabs a water from his nearby gym back and slugs most of it down, looking at the camera after he does so.)
Gideon: In two weeks...WFW, The Gates of Hell...I'm going to do what no one else can ever say...I'm going to beat Doc Silver for the WFW title quicker than anyone else on the roster. I'm not bragging, bragging is what I do to who I don't think are worthy opponents...You Doc are the WFW World Heavyweight Champion, and I respect that more than you know. You will learn how much I respect you...and that title, when I win it Doc. Because when I do, I going to stick my hand out like a man and ask for your respect. Respect is earned and not given, and by God I will earn yours in that match. I don't care what you do to me in that match Doc. I am not tapping, and I am not staying down for a three count. I've come too far in the past month. I will not take a step back and I will not allow your "legend" to scare me or intimidate me.
(Gideon grabs his bag and gets ready to leave) Gideon: My name is Richard "The System" Gideon, and I am YOUR WFW World Heavyweight Championship #1 Contender...and in two weeks, at Gates of Hell I will be your WFW World Heavyweight Champion. If you don't see it coming Doc, if you don't smell the changing of the guard....
(Gideon smiles for first time this interview, looking intently at the camera) Gideon: And...It's...your...own...fault...... (Fade to White, with Richard Gideon, WFW Champion in black letters) Methos17 — 24 April 2003 (The scene opens with Richard Gideon in the WFW training facility, running the ropes. His bodied covered in sweat, Gideon collapses in the center of the ring, spent. As he coughs and attempts to regain his breath, Mike the Cameraman steps into the ring. Gideon barely gets into a seated position and begins to speak)
Gideon: (Panting) Gates of Hell...WFW Title...Mine.... (Gideon tries to stand up, but falls back down) Gideon: (Still breathing heavily) Must...breathe...need....air... (Gideon lays down, and begins to take several deep breaths. He then gets himself propped against a corner turnbuckle and resumes)
Gideon: Title...mine...I...need...the...belt...mine... (Gideon climbs to the top and attempts his System Failure. Right before he makes contact with the mat, he rotates so that the landing is a roll that results in him on one knee. He runs back to the rope, hops and moonsaults onto a mat placed outside and lays there for a good five minutes. After he's collected himself, he sits on a WFW foam chair and continues)
Gideon: That Title, THE TITLE, is mine Doc. I need it, I've been living, sleeping, eating and breathing it since I got here. Kazuo kept me from advancing, so I kept him from advancing past Manson. I made it to the number one contendership through a battle royal. I could brag about it but I won't. Like I said before Doc, bragging's for the unimportant matches. This is a big one, THE big one in my career. I'll be the youngest WFW Champion, the youngest person to beat you and the youngest to beat you for a major title. But for that to happen I have to be more prepared for this match than any match I've ever been in. Be proud Doc, I'm not training because I want to make you look good, I'm training to ensure that when I'm in that ring I'll know win or lose that I brought everything I had to the table and was able to take everything you had.
(Gideon gingerly hops back into the ring) Gideon: That's the long and short of it Doc. I'm training to beat you, become the champ and remain worthy of having the honor of being the WFW Champion. And if you can't handle that...It's your own fault.
(Fade to White) DocKGB — 27 April 2003 (FADEIN: Doc Silver standing in front of a WFW Banner, the WFW World Title over his shoulder. He smiles as the camera focuses on him.)
DOC: Train, bust your ass, break your back, work to make yourself that little bit better, that little bit stronger, that little bit tougher, put it all on the line, tax yourself to the limit, because when you come up short, and we're not talking a little short, we're talking VERY short...It's gonna be so much sweeter...
You got lucky last time...And for that, well, congrats, because you pissed away the luck you would have needed to beat me, for a match when this (Pats belt) wasn't on the line...It was like finding a scratch ticket on the ground that said you won 1 million dollars, but the little 'void if removed' section had been scratched off...It was a once in a life time event for you to beat me, you'll be able to tell the grandkids that you did in fact put my shoulders on the mat for 3 seconds...But then they'll ask you what did you do with the belt...Because they'll know that every where Doc Silver went, belts just couldn't help but go to him...And you'll have to tell them that you didn't win a World Title off me...That it was just a tag match, and you'll see the pride in their eyes dim a little, they'll still be in awe that ol' grandpa beat the Legendary Doc Silver, but they, like you, will wish it had been when all the chips were in the middle, and everything was on the line...
And that's what this is Rich...The big time, main event, the spotlight on you...And I want you to feel it...I want you to be in that ring alone, waiting, waiting for the music...And Shirley Manson's golden vocals will hit, the crowd will unify in hatred...And I will make my arrival...And it's gonna hit you...This is the big time...No tag partners, no accessories, nothing, just you, me, and a guy in a blue shirt asking us if we're quitting or counting our shoulders when they are down...
And you're going to feel it, they all do, the agony and the torment that comes with fighting me...The beating I'll endure, the bloodshed, the draining of your very soul and you hammer away at me...But I never go away...I'm a nightmare Rich...I'm going to test your mind and body...Because the fustration that kicks in after you hit that series of big moves...The agony that kicks in when your finisher fails to put me down...It will all build and build until finally you make that mistake...You screw up for just a moment...And it's over...Just like that...Aces Full...And you go down...The ref counts 3...And you leave with nothing...You drop the ball and fail yourself, fail the fans, and fail those grandkids you'll be talking to in 40 years...And that failure will be why from the moment that hand hits the mat the third time while your shoulders remain down...Till the day you die...You'll be a broken man, wondering the endless path of misery...Only Happy When It Rains...(FADEOUT)
Methos17 — 29 April 2003 (The camera fades in to Richard Gideon Standing in front of a WFW ring. Wearing a Buffalo Bills Cap and a Willis McGahee #45 Bills Jersey, he Inside the ring is a chair, and a small wooden table. Gideon looks up at the table and then looks back to Mike the cameraman, beckoning him to follow him into the ring. Gideon sits in the chair and begins to speak.)
Gideon: Doc, Doc, Doc...can't see the forest for the trees right now can you? Tick Tock, Tick Tock...the clock on your ownership of that belt is ticking away ever so slowly. They say a sign of a good veteran is one that is still performing, and performing well.
(Gideon begins to pull a manila envelope out from under the table) Gideon: As they set up this ring in the scenic locale for the show I watched these guys toil, and with every nut and bolt they put a piece of themselves into the work. I am going to do the same thing in our match, but in reverse. You're going to feel every nut, every bolt...every bit of that ring will make you howl in pain. And the conductor of this carngage-laden concerto? Me. I am going to beat you Doc, I will pin you or make you tap in the middle of that ring. The WFW is too grand a stage to have rookie fears and nervousness...you either shake it off or don't show up. When you look at those ropes and reach...and reach...and I drag you back and you tap you'll know...when I drop you on your head and I pin you...you'll know...and when the match is over, I'll open this (points to envelope) and I'll show you why I could not lose.
(Gideon gets up and seats himself on the top rope) Gideon: Doc, at Gates of Hell, we settle this. And if you can't see this rookie sensation biting at your heels like Cerberus at the GATES OF HELL...IT'S YOUR OWN FAULT!
DocKGB — 29 April 2003 (FADEIN: Doc Silver sitting on a couch. He is wearing a Miami Dolphins baseball cap. He has a look of apathy on his face, while Green Machine, wearing his brand new Rodney Harrison '37' Patriots Jersey, appears pleased as punch beside him. The WFW World Title rests on a shelf above them.)
DOC: You know how much it sucks to have to listen to Mel Kiper and the rest of ESPN tell me the Pats had the best draft? God, I hate football so much...
GREENIE: They even said your draft was worse then the BILLS! (Laughs) DOC: Now that's downright impossible, any team that wastes a first rounder drafting a one legged running back HAS to be the worst run organization in all of sports...So Rich, I feel for you, maybe the US Army can invade Ralph Wilson stadium and put in an ownership who won't be total crap...But I doubt it, the fact is, your moronic Bills and their gimp RB kinda fit you...And this isn't Norwood hooking a 47 yarder as the game ends...This is one of those three blowout losses they suffered...This is another asswhipping...And it's coming, from me, to you...
You think my ownership of the belt is slowly ticking away? You're right, I'm 34, I'm retiring from the ring at 40...So yes, in six short years, I will no longer be WFW World Champion...But before I retire the WFW World Title I have to beat you...I have to expose you for what you are, a rookie with a lot of hope, and a lot of determination, but in the end, not enough brains or talent to get it done. That's what you face in that ring, is to much skill, far to much intelligence.
You can't beat me Rich...I really feel like this is early in Windwaker and you're swinging that stupid sword at me, but it doesn't glow yet...So it won't work, but there will not be two little bird people to swoop--
GREENIE: GRASPED! DOC: Thank you Teen Goon Squad...No, there will not be two bird people to pull you and Zelda out of hard's way and there will be no Giant Dragon to burn my little fortress...No, you'll just have to stand there and relize you don't have the tools, or the skills needed to beat me, and you'll have to take your beating like a man...
And maybe after you spend years of your life training, improving, and getting better in this sport, becoming a more well rounded wrestler, you will get in that ring and face me on the day I turn 40, as the last hope WFW has of seeing me lose this title...And after 6 years of improving yourself, after 6 years of me being beaten down and aging, you'll get in that ring...AND YOU'LL STILL LOSE...
Because in reality, you are nightmarishly stupid... And I shall enjoy breaking you, and leaving you laying on the endless path of misery...Only Happy When it Rains...(FADEOUT)
Methos17 — 29 April 2003 (Richard Gideon is seen seated in the middle of the WFW ring. No table this time, no "mysterious envelope", just a man in a jumpsuit. Gideon points to a nearby chair for Mike the cameraman to sit in as he begins to speak)
Gideon: Doc, you say I'm going to be exposed? If I am, the only thing you're going to expose is how I will persevere and beat you. You're going to expose the talent I've hidden away for just such occasions. I will rise to the challenge, I will hold the "master sword" in my hand and defeat you. All the little stories you make, the allusions to our teams of choice, the zelda stuff...won't matter...
(Gideon stands up and hops over the top rope and to the floor, slowing standing erect again)
Gideon: At Gates of Hell...we'll go toe to toe, icon to rookie. Phenom to "The System"...I'm looking forward to it. See, I've overestimated you at best, and you're doing nothing but underestimating toward me. So think all you want Doc that you'll keep me down for a three. You won't. I'll die before I let you beat me. I have come too far here for you to deny me that belt. Gates of Hell, that bell rings 'ding ding' and it's on. You and me, mano a mano. No Green Machine, no Mike...
(Mike the cameraman nods, but puts his tickets to the event in view of the camera) Gideon: So you'll be there to cheer me on eh Mike? (The Camera nods) Gideon: Good man, good man! Doc, after Gates of Hell... (pauses for a second, then taps his left shoulder) Gideon: This (tapping shoulder again) is where the power will lie...The WFW Title, the richest prize one can attain...and I will await your slow...decrepid...climb...back up the ladder. Because I'LL be waiting...with "your" (snorts) title. And when Doc-Gideon II happens, be prepared for the same result. If you're collecting social security by the time you try and get my belt again...it's YOUR OWN FAULT.
(Gideon walks away...and then stops and you can almost not hear his whisper) Gideon: I'm going to enjoy facin' ya Doc...At this point, anything short of a classic demeans us both...
(Fade to White) DocKGB — 30 April 2003 (FADEIN: Doc Silver with the WFW World Title over his shoulder. A WFW banner hangs behind him.)
DOC: Rich, you say the Zelda stuff, and the football and all of it don't matter...But it does, it's called personality, it's something you lack...Now besides that there is another reality...And that's what you say matters, is us fighting tooth and nail till one of us wins...(Shakes head) Rich, you talk about not wanting to lose so bad that you'll DIE before you lose...I've got news for you, I'm not going to kill you, I'm just going to beat you...Which sucks for you, because that means you can't just die and not deal with the shame...No Rich, you have to suffer the effects of that loss, live with it, struggle with it...And come to grips with the fact that on that night...I was the better man...Because the Ace's Full...It'll put you into la-la land, for about 15 seconds...That's 12 more then it takes for me to cover you, hook the leg, and hear the ref hit the mat three times with his hand...But it wouldn't kill you...
If I catch you off guard and roll you up, and your shoulders are on the mat...BAM that first second comes so quick, you got to get your wits about you...BAM, crap, that was 2...Three's coming right around the corner...How do you get out? BAM!...It's over...I've rolled under the bottom rope, I'm running down the aisle towards the back, some stage hand will gimme my belt, I'll have beaten you...And that's it, you got caught for three seconds...That's all it takes...
If the ref went down and I hit you off the head with this belt, or a roll of quarters, or a chair...Hell, if I knocked you cold...And beat you...Sooner or later you wake up, and you get out of the hospital...And you know deep down one thing...You're a loser...(Smiles)
Classic? Son, this is gonna be a slaughter, I'm going to break you down mentally...And Physically...I'm going to hurt you, and then when you don't expect it, I'm going to make you understand how great the difference is between starry eyed rookie...And a World Champion...Between a man who's gotten it done time and time again...And a fool who'll soon be a broken body on the endless path of misery, Only Happy When It Rains...(FADEOUT)
