WWF Pandamonium: Mike Lane vs. Zach Rosman

18 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 18 June 2004 This show is a special fundraider for the World Wildlife Fund at the San Diego Zoo. The RP/Angle deadline is Wednesday, June 30th 11:59 PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
Badnews — 19 June 2004 (FADEIN to Mike Lane stuffing his belongings into a dufflebag in his small, cramped quarters on the cruise ship. A reporter wanders by seeing his door ajar lets himself in)
REPORTER: Mike Lane... (Lane pauses from stuffing his clothes in the bag, sighs, looks back at the reporter and goes back to filling his dufflebag.)
REPORTER: Mike... Mike, it was a hard fought match tonight against Jared Wells, unfortunatlly you just came out on the short end of the stick, its got to be a difficult night for you.
(Lane turns around slowly, painfully grimacing, pressing his right arm against his side.) LANE: Its not difficult at all, in fact I couldn't be happier. REPORTER: W..what do you mean? (Lane pauses, stares up at the ceiling breifly and turns back) LANE: Look everybody got what they wanted tonight. Wells wanted to beat me, he beat me, the fans wanted a bloodbath, they got one. Michael Dice wanted to escape with his life, and he was spared from a massacre by Bartleby. Everything worked out for everyone.
REPORTER: Well, what about yourself? LANE: I could ***** and moan for hours and hours (Lane grabs and looks at the reporters pass around his neck) Steve, But look facts are facts. I had no chance tonight, and no real reason to be here.
REPORTER: Is this it for you? LANE: (Lane turns away and bites his lip, then turns right back to the reporter) I dunno, probably not. I've been here for two matches. I went twenty minutes with one guy and another one pulled me over a railing into water. I wouldn't say I've actually disgraced myself.
REPORTER: About that match, you seemed to have things going your way. LANE: I didn't even want to be there. I had made up my mind. I was ready to end Michael Dice's career. I was going to piss kick the poor kid for another twenty minutes, twenty minutes of brutal torture. But Bartleby unexpectingly served as Dice's guardian angel and by rendering that kid unable to compete he saved him one hell of a bad night. And Dice can sit at home tonight with a big bandage on his head and think about how much WORSE it actually could have been.
REPORTER: Well what were your thoughts when you found out Michael Dice wasn't going to be able to compete?
LANE: I felt raped, Bartleby took something from me that was MINE. You see what happens in the wild when a Lion tries to take another lions kill, he himself becomes the feast and if I have to turn Bartleby into my prey then I'll do it when I deem the time to be right.
REPORTER: So is this a formal challenge to Bartleby? LANE: Not at all, I really actually like the guys style and the masterpeice of brutality he laid down on Dice was a thing of beauty, but he should know better then to do unto others what should be done by another.
REPORTER: I would think however that you would see it almost as a gift, you got to fight Wells tonight fresh.
LANE: But that wasn't the plan. When Dice got laid out I was finished for the night. My opertunity for redemption in both the past and present and flown out the window. I was done.
REPORTER: So what happened? LANE: I took a walk and there he was. REPORTER: He being? LANE: Jared Wells... shooting the <BLEEP> with someone who worked on the cruiseship or something, I don't know. I saw him, blood boiled and I attacked.
REPORTER: So you took him out to the arena to start the match. LANE: I made a wrong turn. REPORTER: You... what? LANE: I didn't want to head to the arena, I had no intentions of fighting that match. My intentions were to take Wells to the steam room and bury him. Unfortunatlly for me it seems to the world frowns on public acts of assassination. Especially ones caught on film. So I had to go through the motions and fight a match while pretending I cared one way or another.
REPORTER: You're not serious? LANE: (smirks) Naw, killing Wells would be too easy anyway. He's got a slow suffering coming for him down the road, bank on it. I won't lie to you and say I wasn't hoping Wells went overboard and his bloated body got found 5 weeks later by a group of girl scouts washed up on a rocky shore.
(awkward silence) LANE: Any more questions? REPORTER: Well you are scheduled to be at the next show WWF Pandimonium. LANE: Who am I going against? REPORTER: Uh (flips through notes) Its a newcomer... uh Zach Rosman. LANE: Never heard of the guy, whats the gimmick? REPORTER: Theres no gimmick just a straight up wrestling match. LANE: Then the guy has no chance. REPORTER: With all do respect you've never even heard of the.... LANE: (interupting) Look, if its going to be straight up one on one then trust me, he has no chance. If we're going to play it under some moronic rules were the first guy who falls over a 4 foot stepladder can lose then its up for grabs. But I've yet to be pinned and Im damn sure theres not a man alive that could make me submit under any circumstances. Zach Rosman can try, Zach Rosman will fail. The harvesting of sins begins at Pandamonium.
(FADEOUT) Badnews — 29 June 2004 (FADEIN to Mike Lane sitting slouched over in a chair with his hand on his chin, sitting close to a TV set watching his match from Bomb Voyage)
LANE: You know I could have had him there (shakes his head) There too. Geezus christ I could have had him any time I wanted, any time but in the end. And thats the part that really hurts. It wasn't the fact I didn't walk out of Bomb Voyage champion that hurts, Its the fact I see Jared Wells walking around to this very day with a smile on his face. I watched his promos. I check his forehead, I look for any scaring, thought I saw some during his last interview, it may have just been the lighting. Reguardless, its in the past.
(Lane picks up the remote and turns off the TV) LANE: And theres no sense living in the past. But to be one hundred percent honest with you I don't really have much else to go on. My opponent seems to be M.I.A, nobodies looking to solve any long standing bloodfueds with me so what do I got? I got last week at Bomb Voyage. And every day that goes by with no chance at redemption, no chance to make up for my failings I'm stuck living in the past. Go figure.
(Lane gets up and stretches) LANE: Mediocrity has never been a friend of mine, yet I've been quite buddy/buddy with him since I got here. I can take some joy in the fact that the only time we've seen Michael Dice since our match a couple weeks back was him getting bludgeoned by Bartleby.
(Lane inhales deeply) LANE: But lately I'm not even buying that. (Lane smirks and shakes his head) LANE: It all just seems too perfect. Dice knew what he was getting into with me. (chuckles) He knew if I got my hands on him it'd be over... it'd be over (SNAPS FINGERS) like that. Michael Dice would need a bib when he speaks, a cane when he walks and a prayer every night (Lane clenches his fist and grits his teeth) Cuz what I would ha....(Gains composure and holds his index finger in the air in a "wait a sec" manner) See here I go again... Living in the past. I said I wouldn't do it and now I am. Its difficult when the future is so uncertain. Its difficult when (turns sideways to the camera and gets just about an inch away) ZACH ROSMAN.... is nowhere to be seen or heard. Zach I'm begging you to feed my hunger, wake up from whatever pleasant little nap you're taking and throw me a bone here. Because Zach, in a future where almost everything is up for grabs one thing is certain.... YOU are my opponent at Pandamonium. YOU are my next obsticle and YOU need to BLEED AND SUFFER all to (smirks and pauses) all to make me feel better about myself. It's selfish I understand, but its also ME and thats the way I've always been.
LANE: Zach, if you don't show up and Pandamonium I'll find you and drag you to the arena because quite frankly I NEED this. The one thing I have going for me is the future and you're the first thing in front of me. So Zach, show up... I won't promise that it'll be quick, and it sure as hell won't be painless, but after Pandamonium it'll at least be OVER.
(FADEOUT)
