Methos17 — 1 April 2003 (The camera fades in to the WFW Locker room with Richard Gideon seated on a bench, still in his wrestling gear. The Vox Hiber Rumble Trophy is standing on the floor next to him, with a towel draped over the top. Gideon noticed the camera from the opening, but has held back on speaking for some reason. As he shifts his seat to face the camera, the sweat and blood from the Rumble are gone. However, as the camera gets a close-up of his face the bruises are readily apparent. He slowly begins to speak)
Gideon (talking with cotton mouth) - Fink, you rocked me. My jaw is aching, my body is a mass of bruises (points to his chest and arms)and I feel like I wanna throw up every five minutes. But, I'm the NUMBER ONE CONTENDER! And all the spitting (visibly angry still) and the shots you took you little rodent...you piece of trash...didn't do a thing in the long run. I won, you lost. I Gorilla-pressed that tiny frame of yours over the top and sent you packing. Now you get a shot against Manson, God help you. You're a dead man. D-E-A-D.
But enough talk about dead men. Doc, Hart, you two face myself and Psycho. Let me tell you from experience, that Psycho isn't known as Psycho because he switched to tea bills during the IBM boom - no, he is a guy that is as dangerous as he says he is, make no bones about it. We're in falls count anywhere territory gentlemen. I have no problem as you two can or cannot see from the Rumble. I have no problem that you two are premiere players and I'm working my way up. I do have a problem with one thing and one thing only: Doc, you're in violation of State Law 994.3312: Ownership of property that was not yours to begin with. I started that tournament saying that I was going to be the Heavyweight Champion. I said it, Mike recorded it (cameraman nods) and I WOULD have done it if not for unforseen circumstances. However, I have, as of last night done what you didn't - I've gone through four other men at once to win this.
(Gideon steps behind the lockers and changes into his "I'm Number 1 so don't bother trying" T-shirt and a pair of jeans.)
Gideon - Hart, I don't know you and frankly I could care less the history you have with your tag partner. I just hope you two get along long enough for myself and Psycho to wreck you guys Texas-style. I saw your work in the main event, you were phenomenal I'll give you that. But as good as you were, as great a job you did in that match it wasn't good enough. And that's the difference between you and I. I'll be the one to get things done and you didn't. Psycho and I are going to systematically decimate you two. And I don't mean in the normal way. I mean we're going to dehumanize you two to the point where you regret having a hand on this mortal coil.
(Gideon picks up his bag and trophy and prepares to leave) RG - So that just leaves you Psycho. You and I didn't come into contact during the Rumble, and we will meet in a one-on-one situation eventually. I've seen too many instances of this sort of situation to think otherwise. You and I, if we stay focused on the two of them will win. Simple as that. You wanna piece of me later? That's fine. We'll do a falls count anywhere singles match in a few weeks, tear the house down...whatever. As long as we stay focused for our match.
(Gideon opens the door, and turns back to the camera as he leaves) Gideon - I think that's all that needs to be said. The die have been cast, let us now see who will cross the proverbial line...This is WFW Number One Contender Richard Gideon saying if you Doc and you Hart aren't ready for Psych Ward...Psych ward, I like that team name...it's your own fault!
