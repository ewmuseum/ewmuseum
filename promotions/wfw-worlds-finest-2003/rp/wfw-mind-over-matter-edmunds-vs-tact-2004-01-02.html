Tact — 6 January 2004 Fade in: Times Square, post New Year's celebration, is not very different from prior to it. The main difference is that the people are cluttered along the sidewalks, rather than in the streets, so the various automobiles and motorcycles can resume their normal routine of crowding the streets. There aren't many signs of added debris, as the 'Big Apple Cleanup Crew' did their job very well, as they are paid to, leaving the streets only with the usual cigarette butts and occasional papers blowing along the concrete. The camera pans around this scene until it comes upon the front of the Virgin Megastore, one of the premier spots in the Square. The shot then cuts to inside, where we find Larry Tact sitting in front of a rectangular table set near the escalators. He has on a pair of stonewashed blue jeans; black boots; and a sleeveless grey t-shirt. Tact has his bleach blonde hair in its normal flat look, wtih his fashioned spikes reaching down to his ears. Dangling from the top of his shirt are a pair of sunglasses; blue lensed and silver framed. Tact is signing autographs on posters and photos, as well as taking a picture if someone should ask, and seems to be garnering a decent sized crowd at the moment. However, a man with a black shirt and jeans -- the shirt bearing the WFW logo on the upper right -- approaches Tact and says something to him, pointing at the camera. Tact signs a couple more autographs, then gets up.
Tact: Folks, it seems that I have to take a break for now... The people start to boo Tact, who gives a mock insulted expression. Tact: Well, if that's how you want to be. I was going to cut a promo for my upcoming match against Sean Edmunds...right here....
At this, the people give some cheers. Tact: I was thinking maybe you could all help provide some support? More cheers and a small, brief "Larry Tact" chant. Tact grins and nods. Tact: Alright then! Get yourselves into position, and let's do this! The crowd gathers around Tact, who stands away from the table and then a bit in front of the people. He also slips the sunglasses off of his shirt, flips them open, and slides them onto his face. Then, once he sees the cameras are running, he commences, with the crowd cheering for him here and there.
Tact: Well, it seems that Larry Tact is indeed back. Because at Merrython, I made good on what I said before the match. I had a match to win, and I won it..(crowd cheers).. Not only that...but I made a challenge to a number of people. One of those challenges went out to..(gives a snort).. 'Sensational'...Sean Edmunds. Not that he isn't possibly...sensational...after all, I wouldn't challenge someone that I didn't expect to be a worthy opponent. But all those tags seem to add a bit of...well, comedy to someone. 'Superstar'...'The Annihilator'...'The End'...'Sensational'.. When I was 'Showtime' Larry Tact, I was competing for an Entertainment Title. But that's all it is, a means of entertainment. You know, Edmunds, that your skills will be what shows how good you are or not. How sensational or how dull and numb you are. After a certain point, I didn't think it was all that necessry to call myself anything other than my own name. I didn't need any comedic boost, but hey, maybe that's why this has been made a good old fashioned wrestling match. I'm supposed to show you how to entertain without the use of a pet name, Sean. I'm here to show you how to cut right down to business. Cuz that's what I'm here for.
The people cheer some more as Tact pauses. He looks back at them and points a thumb over his shoulder as he turns back to the camera.
Tact: See, they're here cuz they know what I'm all about. But maybe you're a bit uninformed about my deal, Sean. It isn't very difficult to understand, so why don't I give you the rundown. This is our first meeting in the ring, after all, so you should get a proper introduction. I entertain using five tools, Sean. The first four mesh with each other. Confidence..calm..calculation..and determination. Just look at my match with Pulsar. I had confidence that I could beat him, thanks to my sound preparation and conditioning. That confidence resulted in my being ready and calm for the match, which I'd say was the case, overall. I kept my head and stucked to a calculated gameplan. I didn't go changing up strategy, or looking like a chicken without a head in that ring. And it all stayed concrete thanks to my determination to get the goal accomplished. Very simple formula. The execution, however, is another story.
Tact puts a hand in one of his jeans pockets and scratches his forehead for a moment while looking at the floor. The people quiet down to listen as Tact looks back up, chin propped up with a fist.
Tact: It isn't always so easy to execute a plan to perfection, is it Sean? I went into that match seeing Shawn Hart as the World Heavyweight Champion, and seeing my challenge as a sound choice. As I said, I've beaten Hart before, so if you were to beat someone who's done that, it should give you an idea of the level you need to be at. But Scotty Michaels went in and pulled a fast one on Hart. He played a role in order to deceive Hart, and it worked out for him. That's good planning on his part...but you know what? This is World's Finest Wrestling and somehow, I just don't buy Scotty Michaels as a better wrestler than Shawn Hart. Not if he needed to trick Hart so that he could make it out of that match as the new Champion. On some level, Hart did lose. He lost the mental aspect of that match, as he clearly wasn't prepared enough. But Scotty Michaels only hurt his own credibility as a Champion. Not just as the World Champion, but after what he said about the North American Title match in which he lost the belt...even as the North American Champion, he lost some amount of credibility. He took an out. But hey, to each his own. He'll have to deal with the consequences of his choices. The reason why this matters to you, though, Sean, is because you still have a title match upcoming. You have to face Scotty Michaels. I've never faced the guy before, but rest assured...you'll be plenty prepared to face a Champion by the Pay-Per-View. Because I don't put on dull and numb performances, Sean. There's a reason I'm going to teach you how to entertain without that pet name. There's a reason I'm capable of putting on a show in an old fashioned wrestling match. I'm as good as any Champion, so really, you're about to go through two Championship bouts for your next two matches. You can't get better preparation for Michaels than that.
Tact stops and faces front again, crossing his arms over his chest and grinning slightly. Tact: And if you doubt me, Sean, just look at the tools I use. I named four of them, but there is still one other that I have left. That's the ability to take...initiative! See, I came to WFW looking for the challenges that would be interesting and competitive for me. I went through my debut match to show that I meant business, but now that that's over with, it's time to get down to business. You, Sean, are the first to face my challenge. Just remember, this match wouldn't have happened without my making it happen, so don't think I'm not ready for this. I am going to be getting in that ring to show you, and the rest of this promotion that Larry Tact is not to be taken lightly. My goal is nothing short of attaining victory, whether by pinfall...or submission.
Tact roughly removes his shades from his face and holds them in hand as he glares into the camera sternly with his olive green eyes.
Tact: It may seem comical to people that a newcomer is challenging the number one contender for the Heavyweight Title. But the only thing that seems remotely comical in this match to me...is that goofy tag of yours..'sensational.' Just make sure your wrestling is not so easy to humor all these people with, when we get into the ring. After all, I would be disappointed if it was...
Tact chortles and walks back around the table, slipping the sunglasses back onto his shirt and taking a seat, to continue signing autographs and taking photos. Fade out.

Tact — 15 January 2004 Fade in: to a blue screen with a small disclaimer on it. "The following segment comes courtesy of WTHQ radio. Any use of WTHQs shows and segments by World's Finest Wrestling have been given the expressed consent for use by WTHQ."
The screen fades to black and then fades into a small radio studio, where a host sits comfortably in a chair, wearing a button-down blue shirt and black slacks. He has a slender figure and shoulder-length, straight brown hair. He brings a microphone toward him to speak as a producer gives him a cue and a red light turns on behind him.
Host: Welcome back all, to this, the debut show of the Midnight Oil. I'm your host, Et Cetera, and in this third hour of the show we're going to change pace a bit, and have a segment new to this station. We're going to delve a bit into the wrestling world for a few minutes, and to help me out with that we've got a wrestler for one of the regional promotions out there. With me is Mr. Larry Tact, who just recently signed on to work for World's Finest Wrestling, which over the past year has already made a name for itself on the wrestling circuit. Welcome, Mr. Tact...
Larry Tact walks into the studio as Et Cetera speaks, wearing a pair of black leather pants, boots, and long-sleeve pine green shirt. Tact takes a seat at the oval-shaped station setup; which has a few monitors placed on it, as well as headsets and some other equipment. Tact puts on a headset and speaks once Et Cetera finishes.
Tact: Thanks for having me here, on this debut show. Congratulations on that. Et Cetera: Thank you. Now I know you won your first match, last week, in the WFW. You also put out a challenge to Sean Edmunds, who you are now set to face in the ring at WFW: Mind Over Matter. Edmunds is the Number One Contender for the WFW World Heavyweight Title, so tell me, do you have that 'underdog' mindset going into this match?
Tact chuckles. Tact: Not to take anything away from Sean Edmunds, but I don't have that mindset at all. I know I haven't been around as long as Edmunds, not a lot of people have; he's a real veteran of this industry. But I'm not some green rookie, either. I've spent years in this industry, myself, and more importantly, I've been paying attention. I've learned about what works, what doesn't, and especially what works for me. This match will be a good one, but there's no doubt in my mind that I am more than capable of leaving Mind Over Matter victorious.
Et Cetera: You spoke of 'tools' that you use during matches, that help you succeed. Sean Edmunds seems to agree that there is a certain strategy needed to win -- call it a formula for success. But Edmunds seemed to have the points you mentioned covered, so what will you do to put yourself over the top?
Tact: That's an interesting point, Et. But I'm going to have to contest it, because I don't quite see things that way. See, Sean Edmunds would like to think that he has the tools for success, however in this case, the facts betray him. There's no doubt that Sean Edmunds has confidence...but that I knew already, because if he didn't have confidence, he would never have lasted in this industry for so long. The real question is, how long will that confidence last?
Et Cetera: What do you mean by that? Tact: Well see, Edmunds' current run of confidence is just about at its end. He lost some of his momentum at the WFWs last show, Merrython, when Alex Wylde pinned Jared Wells. It could have been worse; Edmunds himself could have gotten pinned, which could have shattered his confidence completely. But that match is still affecting him. Now he wants to beat me so he doesn't go into the next Pay-Per-View -- which is right after this show -- on a losing streak. Really, he's trying to salvage his confidence. The way I see it, a wrestler in Edmunds' position could have chosen to keep on the offensive after his last loss, or go on the defensive. He's chosen to go on the defensive, and try to prevent himself from losing, rather than going in to win. It sounds the same, but the difference is in the confidence level. I can tell you right now, unless Sean Edmunds changes that mentality, he's going to be very cautious in this match. He's not going to take any risks, try to make no mistakes. He's going to be sticking tight to his gameplan, rather than open to change up if it isn't working. But that's the strategy of a person who's beginning to question his confidence. All because of one loss? No. It's the result of Edmunds' confidence being on its last legs.
Et Cetera: Isn't that a little bold to say? I mean, this is a man who is getting the chance to win the Heavyweight Title...
Tact: That's true, and you can't take that away from the man. But you can see, already, that Edmunds is thinking like you, Et. He wants to get to the title match already, rather than put his confidence at any further risk. When someone knows that they have a title shot upcoming, the last thing they need is to have their methods questioned, and legitimately so. Edmunds would like to believe he doesn't factor in matches with more than one opponent as 'singles competition,' but that's not true. Whether he knows it or not, he is factoring them in. I mean, look at the match where he earned the number one contender spot in? It wasn't a one-on-one match. And if he wants to speak of one-on-one encounters, what about his match with Copycat, at New World Madness? The things is, Edmunds just wants to get on with this match, and then go to the title match. Edmunds said he's not taking me as lightly as he normally would. Aside from the fact that it would be foolish for him to take me lightly, I don't know if anyone should really read into that statement. The reason? Because he's on the defensive! He's probably thinking about the title match more and more every day, and Mind Over Matter less and less. And then he goes and says he thinks I can 'pull a fast one,' but nothing more. Well he's making it easier and easier for me to have no need to 'pull a fast one.' I'm just going to outwrestle him. And by the time the Pay-Per-View arrives, I doubt he'll have the confidence to go against Michaels at full strength...simply because he'll know that he couldn't even beat two people who are, in his mind, not at the level of the Champion. I would beg to differ, but it's only going to hurt his chances in the end.
Et Cetera: That sure put me in my place. Tact laughs. Tact: Don't think of it that way. Don't go on the defensive, also. I just explained why that's a bad idea.
Et Cetera: Yes, you did. Tact: As for determination? Well, Edmunds is determined to win the title, but he seems more determined to get to that title match than in the ring with me. Of course, that just shows how formidable I am. And yet Edmunds says I'm just an overzealous, headline grabber...strange, since he's also taking me more seriously, or so he says.
Et Cetera: He also said that he could have kept this match from happening, though. Don't you think he was making it known that he doesn't believe you're a legitimate threat?
Tact: Then why is he taking me more seriously? Edmunds knows I can add another loss to his record, and send him into his match with Michaels on the wrong track. Somewhere in that head of his, Edmunds knows this match can cost him. And instead of saying that he's not taking me as lightly as he normally would...maybe he should remember how much damage I can cause him. I guess he sort of admitted that, by saying I could cause some problems. He just doesn't know that he's in my crosshairs. Edmunds isn't calm right now. He's confused, and that's going to screw up his plans to the point where not even Sherri's claculation will be able to help him this time. She's been there to help him pull fast ones left and right; with purses, paintball guns, and the like. And he says she's got the calculation component of his gameplan covered? Well then...what I want to see is how Edmunds goes it alone. Perhaps we'll all find out, at Mind Over Matter.
Et Cetera: Would you know of a way to make that happen? Tact: That's part of my calculation, Et. You'll just have to wait and see. Et Cetera: Well you certainly seem calm, yourself, Larry. What about your own path in WFW? Where do you plan for it to lead you?
Tact: Like I said last week, I mean to find competitors who are going to challenge me, and I them. I feel WFW has that level of competition, and that's why I'm here. I don't wait around for things to happen anymore; I've learned to take initiative, and that's something Edmunds failed to note about himself. He knows I made this match happen. He can say whatever he wants about having the power to prevent it, but what does that say about him? I don't think Edmunds could turn down a match right now, when he's supposed to be getting a match with the so-called best of this promotion. If he can take on the top, why not a challenge from someone else? That's also why I had to make it happen; because Sean Edmunds won't look below where he is for a match, and I admit, I'm not at the rank he is in the WFW. I couldn't be, only having had one match here. But since this match is going to happen, and now that I laid out what's riding on this for Edmunds, it's clear that he has more riding on this match than I. For me, there's the personal interest of beating someone of Edmunds' level; but if I lose, what does it hurt me? I have no momentum built up yet. I'm not in line for any title shot, win or lose. Pride? Well sure, there might be pride, but I'm not afraid of losing. I know it's not likely to happen, because I'm well prepared to face Sean Edmunds. I wanted this match, and I mean to deliver on it. I prepare so that I can show I'm able to win against the top of this promotion. I go in on the offensive. I don't go with the intention of not losing; on the defensive. At Mind Over Matter, Edmunds will see just how different those two mindsets can be.
Et Cetera: We're going to take a commercial break, get the latest scores and updates from Chuck Barnett, and then talk more with WFW wrestler, Larry Tact! Just keep it tuned here, to the Midnight Oil!
The show goes to commercial as Tact and Et Cetera remove their headsets and continue talking with each other. Fade out.

And when I saw your challenge, and I saw the desire burning in your eyes, I knew what I had to do. I could very well be relaxing in the hot tub with Sherri right now, not even having to worry about performing on this card. But instead I'm going to take your hopes of being noticed, and end them. The same way I'm going to make Scotty Michaels' reign as World Champion the shortest in WFW history right behind Psycho. But maybe this is my lack of confidence speaking here. I'll let you decide on that one. Let's hope you make a less inane choice this time."
(Edmunds sits on a kitchen stool and takes a sip of water that was on the table.) EDMUNDS: "This is my REAL second match, Tact. The first was against Richard Gideon. You can bring up the Copycat match, Tact. But the fact of the matter was that it wasn't originally a one on one match up. Sure it turned out that way, but what happened? Did I lose? No. Whats the affect of that on my confidence? NOWHERE. I told Copycat after that match, and Copycat, you coward, if you're listening now, I'll tell you again; When Copycat and I are able to enter into a REAL one on one matchup, it'll be his first shot at the WFW World Title. That's the kind of guy I am, Tact. I offer matches to those who I know have paid almost as much as I have in this business. To those who I know have the same drive as I do. Copycat doesn't have that fool's desire in his eyes that you did when you came out and challenge half of India.. the only desire he has in his eye's is almost a hatred. A raging need to finally be cut down to size by 'Simply Sensational'. He'll deny it, you'll deny it. But its there."
(Edmunds takes out the letter one more time and takes it out to glance at it quickly.) EDMUNDS: (smirks) "I doubt you can outwrestle me, Tact. You may be a good technician. You might have some mat skills.. Hell, you might as well give it your best shot. But sometimes your best shot just isn't enough. Where is your confidence going to get you when your shoulders are on that mat? What determination will you have once your knocked out faster than (mouths Jared) Wells at a bar? Will you still be calm when you begin to realize that all of your calculations, no matter what they are or how they might intertwine w/ mine, start to snap in half? I don't think even YOU could answer these questions, Tact. I think that for all the shows you may do, for all the autograph signings that you so graciously give, you're still stuck in that spot when you really can't do much since you haven't given the time necessary to lay a foundation. You're like a house built on sand, Tact. You may try to make yourself look nice, you might have some decorations around .. but once that earth starts to tremble, everything will come crashing down, from TOP to BOTTOM. You're coming into this match with WAY more on your shoulders than I could ever have, Tact. Try to pass all the pressure off to me. Try to say this is a MUST WIN for 'Simply Sensational'. Only imbeciles like Et Cetera will believe you, and that's probably because he felt bad for you sitting there rambling on. The fact of the matter is 'Simply Sensational' is going to the pay per view and winning the god damned WFW World Title, while you LARRY TACT, are stuck in a no heat, no reason, match with some other second tier talent who the WFW needed to use to plug up some air time before I (pauses) TAKE (pauses) THE (pauses) STAGE!"
(Edmunds gets off his chair and walks right up to the camera, this time he is looking down into it.)
EDMUNDS: "So this time and take the right initiative, Tact. Wear lots of padding to the ring, because I know that your body has never taken the wear and tear that it will take at Mind Over Matter. You wanted someone at MY level, Tact, and you very damn well got me. But you've got a snowballs chance in HELL if you think that I'm going to let years of hard work go down the drain. I'm pushing 30, Tact. I've been toiling away my best years ... but its about to come to fruition. What have I done in the past? Won the IWC World Title? Child's play. Won the WWL World Title? So easy I did it THRICE times. The WFW gold is the only thing on my mind at this moment. I'm tossing you a cookie, Tact. Because maybe one day you might actually make it up the ladder ON YOUR OWN... but for now you're trying to make a name for yourself in the WFW at MY EXPENSE.. and that just isn't going to happen. Not now, not tomorrow, and definitely not while I'm WFW World Champion. Mark my words, Tact. I'm going into the pay per view to win the title. The same way I'm going into Mind Over Matter to teach you a lesson."
(Edmunds sighs.) EDMUNDS: "There are going to be two different mindsets at Mind Over Matter, Tact. There's going to be the concious one, and the unconcious one being wheeled to the back. You're not going to take this time away from me, Tact. You're just like everyone else here in the WFW .. trying to RIDE my (BLEEP) 'in COATTAILS! Well its not going to happen this time. First you, then Michaels.. and finally, World's Finest Wrestling will have a TRUE World Champion to guide it through 2004. Now (stresses) THATS 'Simply Sensational'...."
((FADEOUT: As Edmunds turns quickly and walks away. Without saying anything to the cameraman he walks through a door and slams it shut.))
