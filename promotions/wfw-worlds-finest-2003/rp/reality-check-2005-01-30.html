Reality check

30 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Great Eye — 30 January 2005 (FADEIN: the horrific images of the World Trade Center towers with the terrible gashes in them inflicted by the planes that had slammed into the buildings. Scenes of people, shocked, stunned, paniced and confused looking at the sky, wondering what's happened. The TV turns off...CUTTO: Bloodhunt standing in front of a dull grey brick wall. He wears a black T-Shirt with the sleeves cut off reading "9/11 is a joke" and desert camo pants.)
BLOODHUNT: "The pain of the moment, the shock of the situation, the events as they happen, hit so hard and so quick, that it's really impossible to understand what happened. A President gets gunned down on the streets of Dallas, and the only tape is kept in the vaults of Time Life for a decade. Clearly somebody was hiding something...The effect on a nation to see it's commander-in-chief having his head thrown backwards as a result of the fatal gunshot, clearly would have had them in a rage, double so when their goverment told them the shooter hit him from behind...Any rational person would understand that logic dictates you don't move in the direction you were shot in."
"So they waited ten years, and came up with excuses, muscle spasm, 'jet effect', and other lies, the public debate is left now to cranks writing books and posting on alt.conspiracy.jfk. Those who kept America in the dark won."
"But surely...Surely this was true. Those were planes with real innocent people in them, those towers were really struck by them, they really fell because of them, it was all on tape, the whole world saw it, it happened. Clearly it's true."
"That night, I was beaten, I couldn't believe what happened to my nation, my America, what could happen to any of us, in a split second, dead...It was the night I gave into the affections of the woman who is now my wife...I'd worried about our age difference, about my earning potental, about the future. 9/11 changed that, if 19 idiots with box cutters could kill thousands, who was to say I wouldn't be blown up the next time I entered some arena with 3,000 people?"
"So to those who did this, I offer my greatest thanks in improving my love life, the sex is amazing and Emily is the greatest woman I could ever imagine. That's about the end of the praise."
"Towers don't fall like that, not unless they are rigged to implode...Towers don't fall from planes hitting them, the WTC was built to withstand a plane hitting it. A tape that's been concealed from the press, and will hopefully be uncovered by a Freedom of Information Act, has 2 firefighters in one of the towers talking about the 'small, controllable' fire that they were about to deal with, they needed a couple of hoses to deal with the issue. These were not men dealing with a tower that mere moments from collaspe."
"Yet the towers did fall...Why? Why on earth would two of the most well built buildings in the world fall from impacts they been designed to survive? Why did they fall so quickly? (Snaps fingers.) WHAM they went down, almost as fast an an object in freefall from the top of the towers they went down. Unthinkable."
"But don't think, just be angry...Just be upset, just hate 'the terrorists' just hate Bin-Laden...Hate Saddam, hate those who we kill for freedom, because somehow, someway or another, they are to blame for this."
"Hate everyone they tell you to hate...Ignore the fact that WTC Building 7 fell for no good reason on 9/11...The fact that no building with a steel building frame has ever fallen due to fire, yet this one went down line a ton of bricks due to one...The Main Stream Media has it's orders, and it will tell you how to think, how to be loyal to the Beloved Leader."
"9/11 is a joke, at the best, it was an act allowed to happen, much as Pearl Harbor was. FDR wanted war, he wanted to stop Hitler and Japan, but America didn't, so he had to let a few thousand American die to get his war."
"Project for a New American Century, or PNAC, wanted Iraq, they wanted Afganistan, they want a new Middle East...So at the very best, they let this happen, at worst, they did it themselves..."
"The night of 9/11, Bush wrote in his diary, 'the Pearl Harbor of our generation happened today.' Pearl Harbor wasn't a suprise, but it was told to the public it was...Is this what Bush was thinking when he wrote this?"
"What does any of this have to do with Michael Manson, with LOVE, with Edmunds and Red fighting over the Presidency? (Smiles) it has to do with the fact that EYE am here now...That they and you will LEARN the TRUTH...And the truth hurts..."
"I'm here because you all need to WAKE UP...And I'll do it, one broken body at a time...I'll do it for the reasons Kennedy was shot, why the Towers fell, why we're in Iraq..."
"Power and Money...I have power, and I want more power...I have money...And I want money...Em's late, and if the doctor's visit goes as expected, I may well be a dad...So it's time to take a second job...A job of piling up bodies as high and as deep as requested of me by the highest bidder."
(Bloodhunt smiles and cracks his knuckles.) "The Bidding starts at 10,000 dollars...The Lone Gunman is back...And everyone is on notice..."
