Rage in the Cage: #1 Contender Match: Hart vs. Gideon

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 2 June 2003 The deadline for angles and RP is Friday, June 13. Send all angles to [detail withheld] Methos17 — 3 June 2003 (The WFW locker room thirty minutes after the show appears to be empty. The champagne from the Psycho title win and the lingering smell of grass is all that remains. Or is it? As the camera pans from row to row of lockers, Richard Gideon is seen seated on a bench, head in his hands. The cameraman steps on a piece of a bottle, alerting Gideon to his presence)
Gideon: (Smiling) Well, I didn't get eliminated first right? (Frowns) That sucked...seriously. Take a bad swimmer, throw him in water and watch him do his best drowned cat impression. Congratulations on the win Psycho, and Shawn you were the better man tonight. But, as the saying goes, on any given sunday you can win or lose. Come Rage in the Cage you and I square off again. This time for yet another shot at the WFW World Title.
(Gideon gets up and starts to smile again) Gideon: I am proud of one thing, I'll tell you what Hart...I didn't fall to a rookie mistake, I lost to veteran thinking. So I'm gonna move on, I'm gonna take this match with you and build some momentum back up. I'm going to face El Hombre Magnifiico, show him what System Failure is all about and face whoever wins between Psycho and the good Doctor. But before all that, I gotta go home, look at myself in the mirror and ask myself when I win this match...will I be ready for my third title match in 4 months?
(Gideon morosely walks away as the camera fades to white) ShawnHartXXX — 4 June 2003 (Fade In: To a fancy massage parlor, as a beautiful young lady is working her fingers on the back of a man who, just days earlier, came up just short of winning the WFW title, Shawn Hart. As Hart lay there, a towel covering his extremities, he opens his eyes, rests his head on his hands, and looks into the camera...)
Hart: "I may be a wounded dog here, but as you can see... I DON'T lick my own wounds! *snickers* But enough about yours truly, cuz Richard Gideon, wether I like it or not, I've got to give it up to you, brother. As much as I don't like your methods... or you in general, after seeing you in action last week at the P-P-V, it's become obvious to me that it's oooooooonly a matter of time before you reach that new plateau. I saw you there, in the locker room, after our match... and yeah, your spirits were down, but hey... don't focus on the fact that you lost. Let's take a look at the glass half-full, shall we? Cuz after a match like that, with some of the best this biz have to offer, after finishing JUST behind the one and onliest PHENOM of WFW, Shawn Hart, a guy like you can only be proud of what he's accomplished. You've proven, once and for all, that you can hang with the big boys. Sure, you didn't get the job done, but hey... at least you made a SPLASH in the main event! *grins* Aaaaand for all of that, I applaud you, Gideon. But ENOUGH with the brown nosin', TINY... how's that lady treating you?!"
(The camera cuts back revealing Tiny, completely nude and on his back. His 'lady', a man in pink short-shorts, a rainbow pattern belly shirt, and lip stick, notices the camera and quickly throws a towel over Tiny's backside. Realizing it only covers half of his target, he quickly grabs another towel, and another, and throws them over him as well. As this is happening, Tiny looks over at Hart, and speaks...)
Tiny: "Awhuppa-whoppa duh-day, Meyu-stuhr Hart. A whuppa... whoppa... duh-day..." (Tiny nods, then rests his head. Cut back to Hart...) Hart: "Indeed. NOW, Gideon, you may want to sit back and complain about not being able to conquer a one and a half foot pool, but the fact of the matter is that here in WFW, every single day, it's SINK... or SWIM! You talk about bein' ready for your third title match in 4 months, but what you fail to realize is that if you're already looking THAT far ahead, when it's the Phenom, Shawn Hart, standing before you... you're not even ready for THIS match! Let aloooooone any title bouts you're dreamin' of. As if the blood bath at CBC wasn't enough to knock you back down to reality, are you honestly telling me that I'm gonna have to rip that ass AGAIN?! Sure, I've admitted you got what it takes to HANG with a guy like me, but if you think you've got the goods to go over that hump, you've got another thing comin', bub! Call it an OUTRAGE, call it a MOCKERY, rest assured... it's the TRUTH!!"
(As the woman continues to rub down Hart's back and shoulders, the Phenom brushes her off with his hand, thanks her, then continues on...)
Hart: "And while we're on the subject, the TRUTH of the matter is that I'm peeved to no end that a JACKHOLE like Psycho is wearing my gold. I may have suffered REAL defeat for the first time here in WFW at his hands, but believe you me... it ain't NEVER gonna happen again!! So Psycho, Doc Silver, Richard Gideon, and anybody ELSE that tries to stand in my way, consider yourselves warned... PHAT Poppa Shawn's coming at you with relentless FURY!!! And from now on, I'm not alone. Oh noooooo!! Cuz in CASE you've already forgotten, the C.O.C.K.S. have been unleashed here in World's Finest Wrestling, and mark my words, come time for the next show... the first phase of our GRAND SCHEME takes effect. What is that grand scheme? Why have we come here? And last, but most CERTAINLY not least, where does DIABLO fit in? The answers to these questions, aaaaaaand more... will all come in good time. But either way, I can promise you one thing... there is another. And at Rage in the Cage, he makes his presence known. I just hope lil' Richie Gideon and the rest are fully prepared for what that means. The REAL WFW champion, and the chaaaaaampion... of aaaaall EARTH, has left... the building..."
(Fade to Yellow.) Methos17 — 7 June 2003 (As the scene fades in Richard Gideon is seen seated in his home, watching a direct feed from the WFW Four Way Title Match. He pauses it as he kicked out, gurgling at the same time. He spins his chair toward the camera and begins to speak)
Gideon: You're right Shawn. Absolutely, Positively right. About what you ask? Your prowess with the ladies I don't doubt, nor do I question, so it's not that. Your acumen in the ring is obvious since you beat me. What you're right about is, I've yet to prove I can "go over the hump" and beat you. I had the grandest stage of them all, the WFW ring on PPV to do it too. And I couldn't.
It's funny how we all have survival instincts. Something encoded in us, our DNA or what have you. My brain was screaming for me to kick out. Unfortunately my body was focused on one thing: getting the water out of my lungs and replacing it with air. When I kicked out after the three that was a combination of the two in synch with each other: to breath I had to expel the water, and to do that I had to get onto my stomach. To get onto my stomach I had to kick out. But, alas it was a second too late.
(Gideon gets up and turns off the tv) Gideon: But hey it happens. I'm not a brooder, not someone who'll be 50 and say "I shoulda kicked out"...I didn't, I lost. But we go mano a mano in a straight match this time. Nothing but you me and a ring. And don't EVEN think of going into that match thinking your C.O.C.K.S will have your back. Make no mistake I don't doubt you have something up your sleeve, however I think I...no I know I deserve a straight up match with you. No assistance. You beat me straight up I can take it. But I think this time things are going to be different. I think this time, this Rook Sensation is going to get the duke. I still have my eyes set on that gold and you're my PHASE ONE. I have no nefarious schemes, no master plans. I keep things simple. I beat you. THen I beat Psycho...or old man river again and then I am champion. Simple as that. And if you think that I'm not prepped to beat the Champ of ALLLLLL eearth, it's your own fault.
(Fade to white) ShawnHartXXX — 10 June 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Jun-10-03 AT 02:40&nbsp;AM (EST)](Fade In: To the front garden area of a sprawling estate. There, down on his hands and knees, all decked out in gardening attire (gloves included), spraying his award-winning roses is none other than Shawn Hart, the Phenom of World's Finest Wrestling. Shawn puts down the spray can, picks up a water bucket, then dabbles it over the flowers a little bit before finally getting to his feet, then looking into the camera...)
Hart: "Beautiful, aren't they?? ...Just another example of what hard work, determination, and jus' a lil bit of looooooove can accomplish. *grins* And I know it probably sounds funny, but Gideon... I think these roses could teach you a thing or two, bro! I mean sure, they look pretty... but ya get too close, what happens? BAM!! You get pricked by their thorns. Such is the case when underestimating the PHENOM as well. You know what I'm talking about. In the week's leading up to Cherry Blossom Chaos, you had the yes-men in your ear, and with every passing day, your confidence grew. Normally I'd say this is a good thing, but in your case, that confidence grew to the point where it began to cloud your judgement. You could see the neat n' pretty parts of Shawn Hart, but with those beer goggles you gave yourself... you couldn't find the sharp stuff until it was too late. Your mistake, and you paid for it dearly, and yeah... I'd be willing to bet you learned from that mistake, but buddy... I don't care about the lil' tid-bits you've picked up. Cuz quite frankly, I've forgotten more about this game than you will ever know. Just like roses are roses, weeds will aaaaaaalways be weeds. And you, my friend, are the latter. *he looks down* Hold that thought..."
(Hart reaches down to the ground and picks up a hose he had left running. He cautiously takes a step forward, then UNLEASHES with a thumb-spray into the bushes. Suddenly, a GOPHER darts out from behind the greenery and makes for the other side of the yard. The Phenom gives chase, but is no match for the furry creature, as it escapes through another set of bushes and into its whole. Hart angrily tosses the hose to the ground...)
Hart: "Damn you gopher!! Monkeys will fly out of my luscious backside before you get my ROSES!! Ya heaaaaaaaar meeeee?!"
(Hart angrily kickes the hose. He then mutters something to himself, walks over to the faucet, and negates the flow of water. He turns back to the camera and continues...)
Hart: "Ya know Richie, you and that little fuzz ball are alot alike. You're both smaller than me, you're both extremely hairy, and you both seem to have the same reason for living. That being to ANNOY the bejesus out of me!! No matter... at RRRRRAGE in the CAGE, I'll be rid of you once and for all. You can whine about how you had water in your lungs and you couldn't kick out in our last big match, but to put it more honestly for ya, you jus' didn't get the job done. And when you're in there with me a SECOND TIME, make no mistake about it, history will repeat itself. You talk about keepin' it simple, and that's fine... I mean REALLY, how else would a SIMPLETON operate? But the point I'm tryin' to make here is that there's nothing SIMPLE about going head to head with yours truly. Don't you worry your head about the C.O.C.K.S. as a whole. YOU'RE the one who's gonna need help. And STILL... you look beyond me!! Incredible. But hey, like the old saying goes... fool me once, shame on you, fool me twice, shame on ME. And there you are, making the same mistakes, aaaaaaaall over again. Foolin' yourself, aaaaaaaall over again. Hell, maybe I was giving you too much credit before. Maybe you really HAVEN'T learned from your past blunders. Ah well, it's your funeral, bub. And this time, you won't have any lame-ass wading-pool horrors to use as an excuse. Just the cold, hard reality of your shortcomings. You think about that and get back to me. The PHENOM... has left... the ROSE GARDEN!"
(Fade to the all-imposing C.O.C.K.S. logo.) Methos17 — 11 June 2003 (Fade in to Richard Gideon seated in his study. The walls are lined with tapes and books, and he seems transfixed on the video wall in front of his desk. Shawn Hart's latest interview plays to completion and afterward Gideon begins to speak)
Gideon: Fool me once, shame on you. Fool me twice, shame on me. That saying speaks volumes. See Shawn, after this last encounted you're not going to be fooling anyone. In the tag match I was focused on pinning Doc and making a statement. In the Four Way I was concerned with eliminating the champ and ensuring I'd have a shot at the title without three vets gunning for me. However, now it's you and me. You're "ever so gracious" enough to make this a C.O.C.K.S.-less match so I am just prepping for you hombre.
(Gideon takes a sip of water and then hops onto the desk) Gideon: Simpleton eh? Lot better than Psycho's "Dickie" jokes, so I guess its progress. You may have experience, and the better rose garden, but I've got my youth, I've got my health and I've got your number. I'm not going anywhere, so get used to me being in your face. You think that you've forgotten more than I'll learn? Fine, keep going senile, that'll only help me out in the long run. You're making a veteran mistake right now my friend and for your sake I hope you correct it: yet again, underestimating the learning power of this Super Rookie! After you and I tango, I got another dance to take care of. That the one for the WFW World Title. When I win this match in a few days, I'll make sure YOU have a nice front row seat to enjoy watching the man that beat you beat the title. After all by your logic, if I beat you and then won the belt I'm equal only to you right? And I won't need Doc to do it, I won't need a blindside attack to beat you. I'll beat you the way I beat Doc....with Style!
And if you don't think I'm ready for the bright lights, big city and Rage in the Cage....It's YOUR own fault! Booya!
(Fade to White)
