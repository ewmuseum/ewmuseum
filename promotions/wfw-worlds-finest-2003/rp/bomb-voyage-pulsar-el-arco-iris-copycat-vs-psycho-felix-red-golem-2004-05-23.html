Vertigo — 24 May 2004 (Cue Up: "The Day The World Went Away" by Nine Inch Nails. Fade an empty boat cabin. Golem walks in from a hallway at the far left of the shot and meanders aimlessly in front of a small bookshelf positioned beside the sole chair in the room. He cracks his back by flexing his wings and yawns. He strolls towards the camera, walking past the neatly made bed, and begins to talk.)
Golem: What is this? Again? I mean, thank you, Psycho, for the 'phenomonal' effort you gave at Shock and Awe. You tried interfering with my business and in the end it cost you. You had to hit some crazy high flying maneuver to prove to the everyone that your career isn't on a downward spiral and you had to take advantage of MANSON, the guy who Golem put on his back. Thanks, but no thanks, I didn't need your help then and I don't need it now. I hit my back drop driver, hell, I was considering maybe even going for a pin attempt. Of course, Manson probably would've kicked out. That's why he is in the main event. But Golem wasn't really tired, so I wasn’t in any hurry. I was working under the expectation that you wouldn't turn your back on your opponent and get knocked out. I thought the way we were squared off was working fine but, no, you had to be a glory hog and find a way to lose. Yes, I am not the spring chicken I used to be and I might even be led to believe I have lost a step but Golem can still take every ounce as much as he gives. If you've going to invite high impact moves, you should really be able to kick out of them.
(Golem scratches his chin, as though thinking aloud) Golem: And where were your LOVErs through all of this? Didn't think that maybe you could use some of their help? I was still momentarily recovering from my own spill on the floor with Manson and even a dabble of Felix Red would have hit the spot. But no...only now do I get graced by Mr. Red's glorious presence. When did it become LOVEAG? The Liars, Opressors, Violence Experiment...and Golem? You guys claim to run this place...I am starting to wonder if you two didn't go out of your way to get me back in your corner. As you two know, I will look out for you as best I can. Already because of a smidge too much trust on my part, I have slid from main event to third wheel in an otherwise pointless waste of my precious time. Therefore, I will protect my investment in this league. I can’t have you two besmirching my mighty fine name. Anyways, guys, if you are offering an invitation, which I naturally assume you are, I am declining. If you're not good on history, several years of my career were wasted thanks to a neck injury at the hands of your good friend Anarky. While I may bury hatchets with the best of them, I don't replace them with hugs and kisses. Keep your distance and I will keep mine. Do your duty, win the match, we all go home smiling. All of us, that is...except...
(Golem smirks and claps his hands together.) Golem: No, guys, I haven't forgotten about you. Hell, Copycat, you have been the god and creator of wasted talent, it seems, and for the damndest of me, I cannot figure out why. Sure, you are as well versed as they come. You are more fluid on the microphone than in the ring, perhaps, but when push comes to shove, you get the job done. And what benefits do you reap? You beat the World champion of the CSWA and yet you have to know that for the life of you you still won't get a whiff of any title anytime soon. So, instead, you have to entertain yourself with little six man tags, much the same as myself. I will be brutally honest, my newest and bestest friend, I have for a long time looked forward to a match against you. History has conspired against me, Copycat, and perhaps you have reaped the benefits of it. I suppose now is a barometer of whether or not that is true. To ascertain a victory over you would be a coup for me, to be sure, but I can assure you, for what it's worth, the same goes back for you. You may doubt that, and I am positive wrestlers like Manson or Anarky won't be quick to ante up such information, but after the Bomb Voyage tour you will see why I have the staying power I do. You will see why when Golem arrived, he was lead directly into the main event. Unfortunately, I do not think you will be an easy man for me to put on his back for three seconds or long enough to introduce to my Claw, at least not with two others waiting with eager anticipation to taste the very same. But, you never know sometimes, and I cross my fingers ever-so-excitedly. All it takes is one mistake, you know that. You are a psychological player much like myself, I don't expect you to get distracted in that ring one little bit. I expect you to bring out every single ounce of your genius catchphrases and throw them at me. Golem does believe he can play you out like so many of his beautiful marionettes, but no promises can made like normal. You aren't normal. Is that respect? Is that what you want? Well, congratulations, you have it, and unfortunately you are going to learn what a waste it was earning it. God doesn't smile upon those who try their hardest. Spin doctors might make losses sound better than they are, but they are still losses. So, enter this with an intent to earn some respect from a company manipulated by morons and leave with the gut satisfaction that, despite losing, you proved something. Specifically, you will have proved that despite your cunning and genius, you weren't able to think yourself out of a situation where you don’t have to do anything and you can lose. Trust me, I learned that lesson last week, and with a mildly altered strategy, I should be alright this time. Odds are, though, when push comes to shove, you'll be focused on my delectably entertaining teammates while I will be focused on you. Don't overlook the Golem, Copycat. LOVE isn't without their useful features, and I do believe I expect to utilize their ability to get under your skin. Or am I saying that for alternative reasons? Ooh...the possible situations...tingly.
(Golem feigns a shiver and smile) Golem: Yes, Pulsar, I do have much more of an interest in Copycat than in you, it isn't your imagination. Perhaps it is because he is better known, has beaten harder competition, has never lost to Sean Edmunds, actually possesses some form of brain activity, doesn't irritate me just to look at him, doesn't...well, I am not positive where I am heading with this, so I think I will end there. Perhaps pinning or making Copycat tap out would be a stroke of my ego, but I will pretend I don't have an oversized one of those. After all, this company has enough that mine actually does seem small by comparison. I will pretend like beating you will generate all the same feelings of satisfaction. You can talk a decent game, but in the end, you find ways to lose all the big ones. Shame, too, because I am wholly sympathetic with that trend. Perhaps, with the help of my friend, literature, you can develop an intellect equivalent of Golem and learn how to break out of the bride's maid doldrums. However, I am not the seasoned veteran quarterback a team brings in to nurse the pups and help them develop. I view young, fresh talent as competition and nothing more, and I will make a concerned effort to stunt any progress you will try to make. I will not let you get your legs under you from your loss to Edmunds versus myself. Again, yes, I do have partners, but, well, I doubt they really see you as much of a threat either. Yeah, you're just kind of here, and I suppose that is good for you. Try to learn something, and I will try to make sure that you don't. Except, of course, for the lesson I have written for you, in very small letters at the tip of my claw. You can make out the message very simply, all you have to do is get close enough.
(Golem cracks his neck as he strokes the underside of his claw with his off hand.) Golem: And finally, the third opponent....El Arco Iris. The Rainbow. He who wishes happiness upon the whole world. A man not to different from myself. We are like two different Horsemen riding towards the same acopalypse. Of course, I visualize myself as War. Bringer of chaos, he who manipulates others to do his fighting for him, always ending in the result that he wants. That was always my favorite Horsemen. I suppose that means you can be Famine. All you have to do is instead of loving bunnies and protecting them from harm...you have to starve them. Sure, it sounds like a stretch, but look at super models. They gave up eating years ago and look at the wads of money and years of mood swings it has given them. They get to be happy, sad, and then HAPPY all over again! That is like a two-for-one deal on happiness.But, sorry, Golem isn't here to talk about his own interests or yours, I’m sure there will be cookouts for that later. Instead, I am here to discuss the upcoming match. And, I would frankly like nothing more than to leave Bomb Voyage with a large smile etched on my face. That will come mostly from harming you and your teammates and ultimately defeating you. You can feel free to play Wishmaster and make my dreams come true. Or not. It will happen either way. Perhaps Golem could do you a favor and use his claw to tear a smily face onto your mask, supposing you will be smiling when he jams his claw down your throat. Shouldn't be a problem, though, you are always smiling, right? You reached into your bag of love last week and what did it get you? A loss, and a bunny that most likely ended up eaten by night's end anyway. Is that an accomplishment? Was it worth the dance? It's something that only you can really decide for yourself, but if they have sense, I do hope Copycat and Pulsar decide for you. No fun kicking a dead horse, which is basically what the odds of victory for a rainbow embracing puppet equate to when in the ring with a wrestler the level of myself.
(Golem smirks and talks over top of a slight chuckle.) Golem: All I do is lay warnings on the table. It isn't my responsibility for you to listen or react to them. I sincerely hope you do, though, because that will make my victory that much sweeter, knowing that even Golem couldn't save you guys from Golem. LOVE, do your job, and it's all smiles and celebrations. Because, in the end, isn't that really what LOVEAG is all about?
(Golem retires to the chair as his interest in the camera apparently fades. Likewise, the camera fades to black.)
NotorisSTD — 24 May 2004 (CUEUP: “Sorry” by the Marvels…) (Meanwhile at the deep end of the pool, Felix Red’s lying on an inflatable floating pool chair; In black swim trunks and sunglasses, smoking a black clove cigarette and sipping a syrupy green beverage out of a martini glass with a little umbrella in it.)
“the boy who destroyed the world” Lingering here on the cusp of infinity, now with the future I always wanted close enough to lick, I can’t help but wonder if I’ve made the right choices. Have I, like so many others, been conned into becoming someone I only thought I wanted to be? Have I lost the positive side of who I was before I became a career action figure?
Probably. But the advantage of being trapped in a cycle of consumerism and self exploitation is by the time you’re too far gone to fix, you don’t care. Ultimately it’s never been a choice. What most people think of as destiny is the unavoidable result of everything that’s ever happened. At one point, if I remember correctly, one of the announcers said it was Michael Manson who brought me to the WFW. I really don’t know why. Maybe he pulled political strings and got me hired and I just never found about it. Maybe not. Truth or lies, The idea appeals to me now. The way things we create betray us, 9 months ago, The one who would bring stability to the world title indirectly sends it back into entropy by inviting a time bomb to his dinner party. Meanwhile, The experiment plans for the undoing of all Manson’s worked for while he watches the WB. Not that most of this isn’t by many perspectives redundant, but that’s the only real problem. If Manson defeats Gideon, between what I do and what Anarky does, Manson will become a legendary antihero invalid while I become the world heavyweight champion. If Gideon defeats Manson, well, it’s been well established that Richard Gideon can’t beat me. He’s tried. Several times. And it simply never happens.
A series of events have been set into motion, and I doubt if even I myself could stop them if I wanted to. It doesn’t matter what anyone else does now. I’m going to be the heavyweight champion of the world. Some people see this coming, and for that reason, to them the whole venture seems a little contrived. But, again, I don’t care.
In a perfect world, Jarred Wells would be WFW president, but it’s almost as well when all we have to do is ask and Sean Edmunds gives us everything we want. He speech is so hostile towards the experiment, while his actions are docile. Yielding. Eager to please. He’s a wonderful politician. Iron Lion keeps saying LOVE is evil, and if something’s said enough times eventually it becomes true. To the constituency, LOVE is evil, and Edmunds need only tell then he’s against us for them to believe completely. He is, after all, the president; the ultimate authority figure, who the masses will obey and respect, the way they were taught to obey and respect their fathers. Or if their fathers left, big brothers. Sean Edmunds, you remember, is NOT a member of the liars oppressors and violence experiment. We, the other ones, will lie to you. But he would never.
I don’t mean to appear dubious. I understand the appeal of soldierhood. Assimilation of self into a collective leads to a division of responsibility. I know the glory of molesting a POW. The experiment is now held accountable for my actions. If I exploit El Arco Iris’s weakness of heart, Psycho gets punished, and whatever spoils I’ve made mine stay mine. All mine. I wonder, if I killed someone, would they send Anarky to prison? I found the Asian stereotype he hallucinated offensive, but I found it equally offensive when the ceiling of my cabin tried to eat me a few hours ago. I guess it’s too bad my sobriety thing fell apart, but it’s hard when you get a contact high sitting in the same room with Jarred and Anarky for twenty minutes. The free cocaine that accompanies celebrity status hasn’t helped either. C’est la vie, and that’s supposed to be the point, isn’t it? We’re on vacation. And isn’t it sad some of us still need to stay focused solely on the negative aspects of life?
I understand that you’re upset, Golem. You lost a relatively meaningless match without even being pinned. I imagine you nearly drowned in your own tears and mucus, as you couldn’t cease weeping in your oxygen chamber or whatever it is you sleep in. But I think you’re being a tad insensitive. Psycho got power bombed through a pool table. How do you think he feels?
We’re not asking you to join us. We’re not asking that you do anything you wouldn’t normally do. Mostly we ask only what you’ve asked of us. Show up to the match. Mutilate our opponents. Then go home. We wont even ask you to shower, although it would be a kind gesture on your part, as your unique scent isn’t my preferred sort of sensory rush. I only humbly request that you stop complaining. Remember, you’re a half man, half giant rat….green, um….evil muppet thing, not a prepubescent girl with a skinned knee. Remember that Psycho is very easily provoked, and quite honestly, I don’t really need a good reason to shred someone these days either. So relax. Enjoy the cruise. Remember that just because you’re not making friends doesn’t mean you have to make enemies. You seem like a somewhat intelligent mutant. I’m sure you’ll do the right thing.
Time still isn’t linear. Right now Iris and Copycat are horrified to discover I was never meant to be their tag team partner, That I am a liar. Right now Copycat is chained to a cage, Iris is passing out in a scorpion death lock while I’m strike him with my boot, and everyone is disgusted with my treachery. Iris refuses to stop trusting me, and as long as I have something to gain, I wont stop exploiting him. He has to stop forgiving me before he can get revenge, and if he stopped forgiving me he wouldn’t be El Arco Iris. War Games doesn’t end until fate takes us too far away from each other for me to do him any more harm, or I grow bored watching him squirm, and the later certainly isn’t happening anytime soon.
Meanwhile, Copycat does nothing. He’s failing to help his supposed friend, he’s failing in his petty quest for an abstract sense of respect, he’s failing to win the world title, he can’t quite seem to finish anything he sets out to do. Poor thing.
In hindsight, it was pretty obvious, wasn’t it? I had no concrete reason to help Copycat, and much more to gain by betraying him. It wasn’t a wrestling match, it was a scam, and clearly someone in authority must have known all about it from the start, yet did nothing at all. It was right there, being waved in your faces all the while.
And the smartest player in the game…did nothing. Maybe he was in on it. Maybe he and Anarky had a secret agreement, where if we tore apart Iris and left Copycat relatively intact he’d go along as if he didn't know. But somehow I don’t think so. Maybe “everything’s 20/20 in hindsight” wouldn’t be a cliché if it wasn’t true. Maybe for all Copycat’s intellectual bravado, he’s too self absorbed to be aware of what’s going on around him. Right in front of him. Unapplied wisdom isn’t much more useful than base stupidity, is it? Intellectuals don’t solve their problems by attacking strangers with a baseball back. Uneducated thugs do. The kind of person I’m always accused of being would do something like that.
And how so sadly predictable. Like most 300 pound people who fight for a living, Copycat really isn’t very bright. Articulate and well read. But never street smart. Never perceptive. Which doesn’t matter, because he’s a large gentleman, and contrary to the musings of wishful thinkers, gigantism is a very useful asset. Heightened strength generally isn’t good for much except a heightened capacity for violence. As I’ve demonstrated, twice now, A missionary complex just isn’t good for much. At all. Pulsars usefulness ends with his ability to fall down. To believe a giant, with teammates as effectual as baby rabbits can overcome an unhinged pain frenzied vampire, an athlete of unmatched fearlessness and versatility, and ah…whatever Golem is…again, it’s wishful thinking. Faith in Lies.
A faith, which leads all of them down the rabbit hole, to the nebulous end of forever…which is the beginning of another eternity. My eternity. My world.

It's people like you that let Alex Wylde rule WFW like a damned king, and it's people like you that are letting Edmunds do the same! I don't expect Edmunds to respect me until I FORCE him to, but YOU need to show me the respect I command!
Official: I'm just a backstage official! I don't have any power to-(interrupted) Copycat: EXCUSES! I'm tired of them! It's about time I start getting some respect around here, and I'm going to start with you!
(Copycat pushes the official up against the wall, choking him) Official: HELP! (The locker room door bursts open. Several security guards come into the locker room. Copycat releases the official and takes some steps back, his hands out in front of him and an enraged look in his eyes)

Security guard #2: I think maybe you'd better take a walk. (Copycat tenses up) Copycat: That's not a very respectful thing to say to me... Security guard #1 (thinking fast): Listen, Cat. Devil666 — 27 May 2004 (The morning sun rises upon the desk of the cruise ship. From overhead the camera pans in to a lone masked figure watching the night creep away and daylight emerge. The camera cuts closer to see the masked face of Psycho looking out over the open ocean upon the closet deck on the bow of the ship. His hands grip the steel safety railing as he watches the waved crash below. Slow he turns his head and a slight smile creeks from under his mask. Psycho: Ah...the start of another beautiful day at sea. Smell that fresh air. can you smell that boys. that's L.O.V.E. in the air and why not.
(He smiles wider) Psycho: We're everywhere else. We've got gold around our waists, Money in our pockets and the WFW in the palm of our hands.
