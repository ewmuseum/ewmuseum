WFW Great Expectations: Anarky vs. Mephisto

29 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 29 January 2004 This match is an Exploding Barbed Wire Death Match. The RP/Angle deadline is Wednesday, February 11 11:59 EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
PaulNJ21 — 31 January 2004 Open on MEPHISTO, Prince of Lies, pacing back and forth through his TOWER OF INFINITE SUFFERING. He walks in black leather pants and collared shirt with demonic lucha mask, also black, but with blood red markings around the mouth and eyes, which are slits. He walks over to his altar, plain and stripped, but resembling one of a Catholic design with a large pentagram design interlaced webbing behind it. MEPHISTO For now, my altar is bare and clean, but I have waited time and time for this and the sacrifice is set for WFW Great
Expectations. There are cloths to lay, chalices to polish, and incantation to speak, but we have days before the ritual. But merely days, I will not rest long, no, but this is a rest for which I have long needed and expected. For the time to come will be of blood and chaos…..
And I mean true chaos, the irrational striking and lashing of an ocean and not the unimaginative rants of Harold, or Anarky as he would like to be called. You never thought you would see me again, did you? You never would have expected to see me in this time, in this place, within the confines of the WFW? Yes, I have come and Alex Wylde has not an idea of what he has set upon the WFW. A true foul pestilence, the twisting stomach knots of famine, the rain of blood, these and many acts of worship in my name have yet to be enacted within this promotion. The whispers came from around the world and the Board of Directors assumed that they had a safe place. But I have eyes within the WFW…….and friends…..and followers….those Anarky whom you have thought of as colleagues have sold their souls to me and now it is you who will pay the price.
This is a reckoning long coming. Did you think nature had not a recording of your sins? Your random acts without plan…..without schemes……simply a pretender to the throne. Your mind could never conceive of the acts I have perpetrated upon this industry…and you fled……but now there is no running…no hiding……I have gorged documents in blood and we have it….the ultimate trial…..the ultimate form of karma coming for you Anarky……The Exploding Barbed Wire Death match…one that most be viewed outdoors…for no broadcasts will not carry it…..arenas will not sanction it…and the air is needed to carry away the stench.
And it will probably smell as a butcher shop…..and just of you….Anarky…..of your charred and scratched and dissected flesh. You have entered the trap….you shall be accounted……
You might think you know who lurks behind the mask…….you might think you can escape the ritual…..but this has been set in runes and time….this is not a mere match…but a morality play…reached the masses…..so they can learn…..and feast upon the knowledge and revelations provided by your wounds…..and it won’t be the first time.

ANARKY: "Wheels set in motion... tides pulling back and forth... and somewhere, a madman sits, his schemes set into motion. His destiny awaits him. But for this madman... schemes will not be enough. The bloodletting and violence cannot satiate his appetite. He is empty.
"Who is this madman, Mephisto? Is it you? Or is it me? Only time will tell. For though you may mask yourself in secrecy now, the time will come, when the cards are on the table, and then you will deal with the consequences, one way or another.
"What do I know, Mephisto? I could venture a guess into your identity. I could play games, as you choose to do. We could keep spinning in circles, but to no avail. No, Mephisto, the question isn't WHO you are... but WHY you are.
"You have come for me. For my sins against you and the world itself. You think, perhaps, that I should be judged. That I am guilty of any number of transgressions. That my time has come.
"Perhaps you are right, Mephisto. I will not belabor the point with arguments. I deserve the horrors you would conjure onto me. I deserve the anguish and suffering which you will certainly inflict me with. And in this match, in this, this most violent match... I will pay your price.
"But you will pay mine as well, Mephsito. You will not simply walk into that ring and have your way with me. For every scheme of yours is another of mine. And though you may sit high in your tower, and you may believe that you have it all figured out, as I have known all along, you NEVER have it all figured out. There are too many variables in this equation, Mephisto. Too many unknowns. Too much has yet to be seen.
"I am a patient man, however. And I will bide my time, and I will see what you are made of. And I have no intention of lying down and letting your own version of chaos take its path. For your path and my path are the same path... you just don't see it yet. In the end, it all becomes one... one reality... one truth. From that, you cannot escape."
(He pauses and reaches forward, his fingertips caressing the mirror, and he seems lost again... when he speaks, his voice has grown even quieter and fainter.)
ANARKY: "For all your pandering, Mephisto... for all your accusations hurled towards me... you haven't yet looked into the mirror. You think you're inside my head... by calling me Harold... but that won't do it, Mephisto. I am not impressed or intimidated by your antics.
"You speak of grand plans and acts that you have perpetrated. So, Mephisto... as I pay for my sins... you will atone for yours. Judge not lest ye be judged, hm? You should know the passage, with the namesake you have taken.
"You know what Chaos really is, Mephisto? Chaos... is when my plans.. intersect your plans.. intersect everyone's plans... and all that's left is the consequences for the decisions we've made. The things we cannot control, but that control us.
"You pull no strings, false puppet master. Nor do I. Nor does Copycat or Michaels or anyone else.
"But the chaos rages on... and I await it with open arms." (FADEOUT.)
