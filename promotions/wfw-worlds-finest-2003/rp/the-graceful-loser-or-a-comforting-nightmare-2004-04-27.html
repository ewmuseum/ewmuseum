WFW — Roleplay

The graceful loser or A comforting nightmare

27 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NotorisSTD — 27 April 2004 “Destroy. The salt from my eyes burns, as does the acid on my tongue. Might a tempest relieve me of sound and sight. Might I unearth the hatchet and put it to proper use. Only thunder gives me rest.
Dare me to breath when I can’t catch my breath. Sway my tempers balance.” Shai Hulud… They collect me off the floor. As I’m being dragged backstage some pimply imbecile with a wrestlingeek.com T-shirt on jams a tape-recorded in my face and shouts
-Felix! How does it feel to go from being a world title contender to a jobber? I make bitter eye contact with him, lizard like little freak licking his lips, they love to see us fail, hate us, even though they can’t ever seem to stop talking about us. Maybe that’s why.
I say nothing, a security guard tells him to fu<k off, and I’m dropped in my dressing room wishing I had been a better soccer player. I’m vaguely aware that I’m covered in a brownish yellow substance for some reason. I think my neck really really hurts, but I’m not sure, which is probably a very bad thing. A monster walks by, it’s a mask or face paint, either Psycho or Anarky or could it be El Arco Iris? Hands me a syringe and a belt and tells me this’ll keep me limber. Realizing I still have full use of my arms, I decide against going to the hospital, wonder what it is I’m shooting for a second, hit the plunger just in time to remember I’m not supposed to
(CUTTO: the world melts into a blurry mosaic of lights of colors and sounds, and I don’t care I just lost to a roll of TP and a guy named Santa Clause, and I don’t care that LOVE’s comedy relief sidekick just upstaged me on the biggest PPV like, ever. My whole career is meaningless, My life is all gone. Like it never happened. Like I was never born. My perception and cognisance have gone all lucid goosey. I have overdosed on drugs. I am dead.)
"SO YOU DON'T WANT TO DIE? Not really. I'd like to find out what exactly death is. I don't like the idea of becoming powerless. I don't like the idea of becoming unconscious of who I am."
So this is what it’s like to be a ghost. I’m experiencing something I might be confusing with enlightenment. As life stew ebbs and flows in front of me, I realize I was absolutely right when I thought I was just talking nonsense. Freudian slip. The collective unconscious. I thought one thing was a defense mechanism to protect me from reality, when it was actually the other way around. I don’t exist. None of this does. To me, what we think of as god is actually two painfully ordinary people. One is some malcontent dork who studies english and journalism at a grossly overpriced second rate college in Boston. The other’s been a few different people over the years. He’s a lot blurrier than the first god, but I know right now his AOL screen name is PaulNJ21. The second god only controls what happens to me when I’m wrestling, the other is the one has the real power. He built my life out of post adolescent angst and song lyrics and pop culture. His perpetual disappointment over how he’ll never be a superhero or a rock star. A fantasy of power and control for when he feels he has none, lost in the minutia of mindless whatever on the internet. Every sh!tty thing that’s ever happened to me or someone I cared about, my mom’s AIDS, my dad disappearing off the face of the earth to hide from the FBI, Toxick blowing his mind on coke, Krist turning into what she is, that poor dumb kid who broke his neck maybe kinda because of me, it’s his fault.
I’m his aborted revenge on whatever he’s pi$sed about on a given day… I bet he can hear me now. I bet they call can… You…..bastards…..Who gave you the right? Who the fu<k do you think you are? This isn’t a game. This is my life. You awful, evil fu<ks. I hope I’m about to go to hell, so when I see you down there, I can make you suffer the way you did to me. I hate you all.
There’s a god, and he torments us, not because we’ve done anything wrong, but because he thinks it’s fun…
"I'd like to see who else I could have been.... YOU WANT TO BE SOMEONE ELSE? We are all someone else. There's the cliche, where there's the person you are when talking to your grandmother and the person you are when you're haggling with a prositute, and that's all well and good, but it goes even deeper...The me in your mind, assuming you have one Mr. Haunted teleprompter, exists no where else. I am the me in your mind, I am the me in the mind of everyone else I come into contact with. I am all these people...Everyone is countless others. Every single person is legion.
BUT YOU'RE ALREADY SOMEONE ELSE...." Um…wait… Something comes back into focus, and it’s not exactly ethereal. I feel nothing, but some of my senses are returning, so maybe I’m not dead afterall. This has got to be some sort of synthesized K and PCP cocktail. They’re the only things I can even remotely compare what I’m feeling to, but at the same time it’s not even close to the same thing. I should get comfortable, because I don’t think whatever this is will leaving my system for some time.
“I’m a friggin’ dudgeon and dragons character.” Shear Insanity. The drugs talking. No question.
I’m back in my dressingroom, which is basically a converted public bathroom. Krist is leaning against the wall sipping a handle of banana rum looking bored, 3 groupies have been sent in, and there’s this thing, a boy monster, a displaced martial arts fighting game character, not a human being by any means. He’s drenched in mustard and blood, in red black and white spandex wrestling trunks and no shirt, screaming curse words and furious gibberish, punching holes in the wall and breaking what little furniture is available. “FU<K”s and “SUCHBULLSH1T”s and “GRRRAAAAAH”s layered ontop of the whips and cracks of a fist repeatedly colliding with metal and tile. Fingers into slivers of raw meat. Smooth white planes, cracked RED where his knuckles used to be. He should take a shower, or else the mustard will soak into his dreadlocks.
I haven’t said any of this outloud. That’s excellent. One of the girls, an either very brave or very stupid little thing, strides over to him, places her hands on his shoulders, and sweetly pecks him on the neck. He spits blood on the floor. She’s turned one of those “LOVE” T-shirts into a tank-top, her bellybutton and lip are both pierced, she’s one of those cute little goth girls you always see at the mall. I want to believe she’s eighteen. Really I do.
-Aw, relax babe. You’ll get ‘em next time. That guy with robots is way less cool than you anyway.
- Y’really think so? - Aw, def. She starts licking the crap off his chest and stomach, the other girls in the room shoot each other questioning glances. Wasn’t Luster supposed to be on the card tonight?
She looks up at him and goes – You’re cut out for way better anyway. Nobody cares about The BAD title. It’s like, under the other two, y’know?
-You’re saying I just fu<ked up trying to win a third tier title no one cares about? -Wha? "I'm not afraid of anything. I'm the recipient of ruthless acts of violence all the time, this will keep happening, and I don't care, because the more I'm broken down mentally and physically the closer I'll come to where I started."
BAM. Right to the little corner where the eye socket meets the temple. She doubles over, the other girls gasp, The guy stands there stiff as a board, mask of total apathy. Lips quivering, giant japanime eyes half looking up pleadingly, not too shocked to cry, wraps her arms around her head. Duck and cover. As if she’s rehearsed this. The mustard guy gives her a boot to the ribs for good measure, and kinda giggles. Keeps giggling. The other girls quietly exit, It’s me giggling, standing there over this terrified creature. What a porciline doll would look like if you gave it a soul. Sobs choking her, like a little kid, she says to the floor…
- I’m sorry. Just please don’t hit me again. I wouldn’t except I kinda want to, when a voice from in front of me cuts in. - Seriously. Leave her alone, a$$hole. And there’s Krist’s ridiculous oversized novelty sunglasses, like the kind I wear on camera so no one notices I’m Japanese, so no one accuses me of eating dogs and having a tiny penis. Clocked in a latex white trenchcoat, arm extended, the bottle of rum offered at the end.
(CUEUP: “Where is my mind” by Placebo) - For real? Chill out? I take the bottle and gulp down what tastes like water while the punching bag girl crawls out of the room. Unless she wants her face plastered all over newspapers and wants to be called a liar, she wont tell a soul. I’m getting staccato feeling back in my arms and legs, blinks on, blinks off.
- Hey aren’t I supposed to be your valet or something? - Yeah, but you never come out during matches or interfere or anything. I think there’s already too many valets on the show. You just stand around during my interviews.
- Oh. Well, I still keep getting paid the same as the other girls, right? - Yeah. - Cool. I remember when Krist was Christina, and I wasn’t Felix, and there was nothing but teenage death and ecstasy. Waiting for us beyond the decadence was the infinite. Our inside joke, a ghost of invincibility. Neither of us could have conceived of any of this then. What follows is squishy and pleasant. Soon there is no more rum, but everyone is jubilant regardless. I’m ringside, and we are victorious in a way that seems absolute. Someone announces that operation mid-card coup d’etat was a success, and I am basking in reflected glory but the celebration is suddenly interrupted by unhappy police officers. A career victim points a finger and from there on there are only cold unforgiving stares. My vindication is my certainty that I will remember none of this.
