Manson — 8 January 2004 ((FADEIN: Michael Manson, in long black leather overcoat and pants, sitting at a table with a list in front of him.))
MANSON: And so, I heard about it by way of mouth backstage, but of course, I didn't actually believe it. Who would? But then I found numbers, statistics, data...all encoded into files and disks...recorded for all time..and what did I learn?
That Mike Manson...scourge of the wrestling circuit..he who has brought promotions to their knees......the man once banned from the industry itself.......had been voted along with Iris no less.....as one of the wrestlers that children most wanted to see competing on Christmas for the WFW.
This is what I have been reduced to then, a champion of children. Seeing my merchandise given away to the homeless and whole families bonding over the Mike Manson chocolate cross on Easter. I've become a sell-out without even knowing about it. And I always prided myself on my self-awareness. Yet, another thing i seem to have lost.
Of course the WFW claims to be for all ages......which makes me wonder why they would sign me in the first place.....sure Alex Wylde is president....but this is the exact child-friendly environment he'd like to disturb. But me? I was recruited...heralded by these executives.....and I never knew....I never bothered to find out....that Mike Manson was now available on Saturday mornings along with Pokemon.
Any day now, I'm going to look into the mirror and actually see my own reflection. However, that still hasn't happened yet. There's still time and lacking mirrors, I have only others to turn to for reflection.
There's Minion....the extremely poor man's Manson..who spent his whole career wanting to be me..and had his chance at the last card to showe everyone he was better..and of course he failed....apparently because his inferiority couldn't even surpass me at my lowest..and teaming with the happiest man alive is my lowest.
Here's your second chance, forgive the cliche, try to be my mirror, so I can shatter it. And Rat Fink, I could go on and on about past sins, but there's no point to any of that now. maybe there never was. I don't need any excuses to bloody you though. Never did, never will.
That won't ever change. ChrisHorowitz — 12 January 2004
(Cue up "Whenever God Shines His Light" by Van Morrison) (Fade into the canvas Cathedral where Satan's Clause stands behind his pulpit. He wears his ceremonial robes as the light of God pours upon his face from a light bulb.)
Satan's Clause: My friends in Christ, we stand at a crossroads. My friends, we stand at a crossroads between the path of good, and the path of evil. Between the H Bomb and the pearly gates.
My friends, I woke up this morning with the crossroads on my mind. I woke up this morning and I saw what we must do. Before us are two men, two men who have the taint of satan deep within them! My friends, these are the type of men who are responsible for the evils that are destroying our country.
Drugs, women voters, women's basketball, immigration, these unChristian religions taking a foothold in America, this is all the fault of men like Michael Manson and Minion!
Men like these with their free and easy life styles, their "liberal" views, they have turned America from the fountain of God's love to the sewer of ignorance of salvation. The thoughts and actions of their followers have corrupted this country so much so that the glorious NAR could have its greatest treasure stolen from it.
This is disgusting, and a clear sign that America must be purified. The clensing which we had attempted to begin with our trophy will be completed with out it. The sign of our covenant with the lord, the our revolution was the one that he gave his blessing to, and one day it will be taken back from the heathens who stole it.
Michael Manson, we know you are one of the tainted heathens who are responsible for the theft of our holy relic. Our prayers will be lifted to the lord and he will grant me the power to strike you down. You will be struck down for every sin you have committed against your fellow man, and for your sins against the lord himself.
Minion, you over the hill worthless scum of the earth. You are lucky that NAR has not had to deal with you before as we have with those like Manson. In his encounters with us he has barely avoided complete destruction from the wrath of the lord, and you, such a weak and pathetic fool would clearly be destroyed. We advise you to save yourself Minion, throw yourself down at the alter of forgiveness, it is not too late, unless satan has gotten that deeply into your soul that you cannot even accept the love of Christ.
How a pair of souls could go so far astray is a mystery. Could satan have his claws that deeply into the soul of America that he could corrupt a pair like Manson and Minion so easily, and so completely? The power of the lord must come upon this land and destroy the darkness that has come upon it. The illusions that satan has used to blind you will be removed, and you will be left with only the light of Christ to guide you back to the path. If you reject that light, then, then you will be destroyed.
(fade out) Manson — 14 January 2004 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, in long trenchcoat made of leather, leaning back on a bench.)) MANSON: Ah, now back in my day, I used to mock and parody like no other. How many times did I stand at an altar or pulpit? I'd preach and quote.....interpeting and twisting all scripture to whatever I was thinking at the time. It was a good attempt, Satan Claus, really, but I can't help but compare you to a younger Manson so nihilistic he actually challenged Jesus Christ to a match.
Where did all the days go? Whatever your holy relic is, I do probably have it. I have a basement full of obscure crap like that and several storage bins more. It was always easy to find them, you know. People used to just bring them to me after awhile. That's how I met everyone. Bueno Excellente, Triple 6, Saint John, TUSK, even Lil' Digger...all the cast and characters I accumulated over the years.
That's another thing you've done there, Satan Claus, Rat Fink, all of you. Minion never picked up on any of that....always lacking that innate charm that would allow a violent sociopath to attract followers and fanatics. But I suppose that's not something you can really learn. All the times we fought before, all 3 of us, and everyone else involved......I never really thought about how alike we all were.
Of course that's because my influence on this circuit is far greater than I ever thought and you two (and more) are really trailing in my shadow....but we all already know that.
What I'd like.....what I'm looking for here..is a reason to go on. To keep trying..perhaps to change.. correct flaws in my character as I perchieve them.....adapt....revert..something. Anarky failed me in this, maybe I failed. It doesn't really matter. I'll look to the two of you to see how I was, how I could have been.
Show me insight. Show me change. Most of all, show me possibility. I want to know why I was Mike Manson in the first place and I suppose starting with the next next wave is as good a place as any.

But what you cannot see is how Satan and his minions have enslaved you, leaving you as nothing more than a puppet of their grand schemes. They have given you the illusion of freedom, the illusion that you can sass anyone you care to, when in fact you can only do what they desire.
It seems satan will not even let his disciple Minion speak. Minion exists only as cannon fodder in satan's war against good Christian values. He will throw men against the holy fire of God, but he will not fight for himself. Instead he will let others take his name as God would never let his soldiers take his. A king of cowards, and Michael Manson the general of his armies.
But Manson, you will not go into this match up with the advantage. Hardly. You may go into it with a fellow soldier of the damned in the ring, but I enter with the backing of the New Age Revolution, the good clean men of this earth, and the power of the Lord.
That is a power you will never comprehend Manson. Youc an try to mock it, you can try to point out when you have done so in the past, but no matter how many times you claim to understand it or ridicule it, it will always remain a mystery that no man can fully understand.
The only thing a man can do is fear it. And now you do not fear it Manson. You do not fear me, you do not fear the cause, and you do not fear the source of all life and all goodness in this universe.
You are a pathetic small little man who cannot begin to believe how tiny he is. But you will. When you lay there, overpowered by the light of the Lord you will see how you have been so utterly wrong. You will see how you have sinned, but Satan's power over you, your selfish pride will keep you from repenting.
You two fools, it is sad you cannot be shown the might and power of your true opponent. How one could oppose the Lord baffles any logician, but that is the taint of satan for you.
My friends in Christ, do not believe for a second that men like Michael Manson will be allowed to win in the end by our Lord. He will not allow them any power in this life of the next. They may have some fleeting glory, but that glory will be overshadowed by their fall, their terrible fall.
These men will suffer for all eternity, but first they will begin to suffer publically, before all God's creation.
(Fade out) Manson — 15 January 2004 ((MICHAEL MANSON, in black jeans and biker style leather jacket, sitting on an over-turned pulpit.))
MANSON: Well, that's certainly...interesting. Most people when doing the whole pseudo-Christian thing try not to overtly mention any specific religion, but we're all products of the cynical 90s so I guess Christ-bashing and parody are in. I think I may at least be partially responsible for that, but, how would anyone really know all the possible consequences of his actions?
So to play along, yes, I'm perfectly aware I'm a sinner. I'm perfectly aware of all of my sins, each and every one. I know the means that I used and the ends that I came to. None were noble, none were even that ambiguous gray.
I just never cared. Religion was always just a tool, like anything else, to use and manipulate so I'd get my way. Most of it was the product of a demeaning Catholic school education, but it's not like I had any control over that. However, I never thought I'd be sitting here today listening to someone saying all of this damnation and hellfire dialect to me. Well, sure, I attract all of them to my doorstep and I used to have to use tear gas to repel them, but I never thought this would be used in the ring.
There were always Manson impersonators and imitations throughout the years, even when I was younger. Just look at like Minion, which is all you can do, since he's been silent. But I look around at the vast landscape of the wrestling industry around me and I see my legacy in all those I inspired and you know, it's pathetic.
The influence I've had on others didn't make them queston anything, didn't drive to anything new, it just made them banal and repetitive. All anyone can ever do is go in circles, doing the same thing forever and forever. If they didn't start off doing, they'll just copy someone else. And that someone else is normally me one way or another.
I never really inspired loyalty or love, just the artifical layer of it which was enough to have lackeys and partners. But now, looking back, had I done something different, would the industry be a better place because of Mike Manson?
That was always something I wanted, despite all pretenses and incarnations. Promotions and opponents that were culled needed to be, survival of the fittest and all that.
But all I see around me, everything that has happened because of me, and all I see is recycle and waste.
I wanted a reason to stay and it might just be to cut away the waste and filth I indirectly created. That might be all that's left to do.
