PaulNJ21 — 20 April 2003 This match is under Ultimate Jeopardy rules... If Manson wins the match, he gets the title back from Rat Fink and faces Diablo in a title match at the PPV, and also gets the opportunity to face anyone he wants at any one show that he chooses, including Doc Silver for the title!
If Diablo wins the match, Manson gets his title back from Rat Fink and Diablo gets to pick the stipulation for his title match with Manson!
If Rat Fink wins the match, he can have a match of his choosing against Manson for the title at the PPV!
If Scotty Michaels wins the match, Michaels, Manson, and Rat Fink will face off in a ladder match for the WFW North American Title!

The emperors always going mad and the senate turning on them is also fascinating, but Doc Siver comes in due time. But for now, let's talk about the road to Rome was always lined with crucifixions.

Now most would expect me to be out in the woodshed getting a cross ready for Fink. But why waste the wood? If a cros is for dogs, what do you do for the excrement that's even lower? I don't know....apparently...Rome didn't know either...and the empire had a lot of time but it fell...and it's left to me to ponder and decide. But I will reach a conclusion to these matters.
