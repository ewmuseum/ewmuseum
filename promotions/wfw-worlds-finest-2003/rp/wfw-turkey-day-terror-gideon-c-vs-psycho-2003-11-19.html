WFW Turkey Day Terror - Gideon (c) vs. Psycho

19 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 19 November 2003 This match is for the WFW North American Title. The RP/Angle deadline is Tuesday, December 2nd, 11:59PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
Methos17 — 21 November 2003 (The scene opens with Richard Gideon sitting on the top turnbuckle of the WFW Ring, lost in thought. His newly-won WFW NORTH AMERICAN TITLE is set up on the turnbuckle opposite him. He stares at it, transfixed at every nook and cranny. A minute passes before he hops down, grabs the belt and begins to speak)
Richard Gideon - The more things change, the more they stay the same. That's so true for me, and for a lot of the stars here in the WFW. A few hours ago, I became the WFW North American Champion, doing something I had yet to do, win a big time match. I'd love to be cocky about my domination of my opposition, which I did, but I know Michaels' brother was...(pauses)...an assisting agent in my win.
And that leads me to you Psycho. You and I go 1-on-1 for this *taps belt* and don't think for a second I'm going to go in unprepared. We've tagged up in the past and have done pretty well when it counts. We've also beaten each other senseless in various matches in our careers here. This match will be no different. I'm going in the WFW North American Champ, I'm leaving the WFW North American Champ. No witticisms, no catchy quotes...The System is the North American Vunderkind now...and I won't let any ghosts from my past change that.
(Gideon hops out of the ring and sits down on a nearby folding chair) Gideon-Turkey Day Terror is coming up fast Psycho. I'd love to berate you and declare you ineffective in our match, but I can't. I know you too well. You've been WFW World Champ, I haven't. So I'm going to be very, very careful. The System has spread...so watch yourself. My name is Richard Gideon, and I am YOUR North American Champion. Thanks for your time.
(fade out) Devil666 — 23 November 2003 Fade In: (The camera pans up a long wooden table. Upon it resides the contents of a large Thanksgiving feats. Traditional fruits and vegatables, ham, turkey...all the works down to the cranberry sauce. Sitting at the head knife and fork in hand sits the masked Psycho. He looks forward with a wide smile upon his face. He gives the camera a wink before speaking)
Psycho: Well Holiday season is here. A time for giving and recieving. A time for being thankful for what you have. (smiles) Well I'm thankful...thankful for you Gideon. Not for the reasons you might think. As much as you boast as being MY North American Champion you better know that the sentiment is lost on me. Why? Well the answer is simple...I'd sooner spit in your face then look at you. you won't take the time time to berate me. Well can the sweet gestures. You think you know me well enough to not bother (laughs) Kid...you don't know anything about me. you don't know how far I'm willing to go...the line I'm willing to cross...the depths I can truly sink too. All you know is what I've shown you and that's a very shallow pool of knowledge your swimming in
(He looks at the turkey then slowly picks up a knife and fork) Psycho: See Dick...I have no more regard for you then this bird in front of me. All you are is sustenance...a name to add to the list and a stepping stone. As you pointed out I was once the WFW World champion and you stand in my way of regaining that. I'm not here for your North American title...it's just a bonus. What I am here for is to do what I do best...(smiles)...HURT PEOPLE!!! I hurt people gideon and I enjoy it. Look at Doc silver...where is he. sure he might have changed the rules to get the title off me, but what good did it do. I'm standing here having never been pinned or submitted for the title. Yet...Doc...well doc is off still licking his wounds. then we have Dormant...(laughs)...I heard his shoulder pop five minutes into our match, but I waited...let in drag on JUST a little longer to watch him suffer. Ah, but now it's your turn. your turn to HURT!!!
(He looks at the knife and fork in his hands. His eyes fixate on the shiny blade and the blunter fork for a few seconds. He then smiles and plunges the knife into the still warm bird)
Psycho: The knife would be too quick Gideon. Although I've been to Japan...I've felt the blade on my skin. that sharp slice followed by the flow of warm blood. (hold up the fork) No...I've got something better and mind. Something slower and more painful. See gideon...I'm going to tenderize you...like a cheap steak. Your youthful face needs character. I can do that...chicks dig scars. Those arms you used to pin your last opponents...(shakes his head)...nothing more then a target. I will make you bleed Gideon and why you might ask? Is it because I need a shortcut? Because I need the advantage to beat you? (shakes his head) No on both questions. the reason is much more simple. It's because I enjoy it
(He stands up quickly from the table and stares into the camera still clutching the fork in his hand)
Psycho: At Turkey Day Terror I sure hope people get done eating before out matches begin. Because when that bell rings Gideon...I'm going to tear your apart. I'm going to spill your blood...I'm going to watch the children in the front look away while thier mothers faint and dad holds back his puke. Why again you might ask would I do such a thing?
(He pauses for a second) Psycho: well I guess it's all in the name. Happy Holidays Gideon...at least until belltime (he let's loose a sadistic laugh before he turns and walks away) <FTB> Methos17 — 23 November 2003 (The camera fades in to Richard Gideon, WFW North American Champion watching the final moments of Psycho's interview in his study. Wearing a Buffalo Bills cap and a black and red "I'm Number One Don't Bother Trying" T-Shirt and Jeans he smiles as he turns towards the camera. The WFW North American Title is seen off to the left on his desk.)
Richard Gideon - OH me OH my, look who's started talking again! Well Psycho, it seems your 'haitus' post WFW Title Loss has not hurt your noodle...you're still just as uncreative as ever. Dick? You think 'Dick' is something that's going to get under my skin?
(Begins to mock cry) RG - Mommy, mommy...Psycho called me a name! Waaaa....(Stops) No, no no. I guess those chairshots and flaming tables and exploding rings have addled you more than you let on, because I am YOUR North American Champion....not just you, but all of North America. From the shores of Lake Minnitaqua to the arid desert of Meh-hee-co I am your representative. And in the spirit of Thanksgiving, I'm going to be giving a gift to all of you.
(Gideon produces a box from underneath his desk. He opens it and tilts it toward the camera, revealing his gear)
RG - This is going to be my Turkey Day Terror Gift Kit. Inside are all the tools I need to retain my WFW NA Title. And in doing so I give my first gift: The gift of Psycho not touching another WFW Title. In order to accomplish this task, I will be giving another gift to you, the WFW faithful: getting to watch Psycho get the sweet bejeezus beat out of him. You see Psycho, I started this little tete a tete espousing respect, sporstmanship and fair play. You want to talk about blood, barbwire and body fluids. That's fine too. I figured with our history you'd give me a little respect too. After all I've earned it. Instead of running away from this league when things got tough I hung in, I dug deep and I came out a winner (taps belt).
(Richard Gideon stands up and places the belt in direct view of the camera. His voice is audible off screen)
RG - Take a good look Psycho. Look at this belt. Examine the rubies, the nameplate that says 'Gideon'. Look at the WFW logo. And think about Turkey Day Terror. You and I are wrestling in a straight up match. No barbed wire. No Flaming Tables. You, me and a ring. We'll wrestle, and I'm going to give you 100%. What I won't give you however is what you want in this match: to cripple me and take MY Title. I'm daring you to try it. Because The System is up, running and ready to show you why I earned this belt. Lights out, the party's over.
(Gideon removes the WFW NA Title from the view of the camera and smiles broadly) RG - And the next two times you see this belt, will be around my waist or on my shoulder entering and leaving that ring, assuming you're still conscious.....
(Fade to black) Devil666 — 26 November 2003 (Fade In: Off camera a slow rythmatic pounding can be heard as the camera comes into focus on the masked face of Psycho. His eyes look down intently as he continues the work. Slowly he looks up his face making a crooked smile. The noise doesn't stop only slows slightly as he look into the camera. The smile turns into a dark and sinister laugh as the camera zooms in a little closer. Suddenly the noise stops and Psycho look coldly into the camera)
Psycho: So Dick...I'm not getting under your skin. (sighs) So you say, but I'm going to doubt you. I mean the mere fact that you made mention of it leads me to believe it got your attention. I guess water and ducks back just aren't flashing in your mind right now. No matter Dick...that fine. For you see even if my words are not getting under your skin (smiles) well I've got a much better way
(He holds up the fork) Psycho: Remember this Dickie? This is the what I told you I was going to make you bleed with. That I was going to rip open your flesh with and watch your very life's force flow out. I'm not sure if your took me seriously, but you should have. You really should. See Dick...I don't joke. Not a funny bone in my body. When I tell you I'm going to do something...well you had better prepare for just that. Prepare for that and whatever the next illogical step might be. Now I'm probably planning to far ahead for you, but I don't care. Sooner then later you'll find out just how my mind works.
(He stares at the fork and it leaves the camera view. Now once again the pounding continues and only slows as He looks upward once more)
Psycho: You want respect?...you want me to give it to you?...You think you earned it? (The pounding stops and he stares forward for a moment) Psycho: Why? Because you won the North American title? Is that why? You think some belt I don't even want deserves my respect? (shakes his head) No...no it doesn't You want my respect...you take it from me. Show me your willing to push further then I am. What you got all you need? Your gear? What...trunks and boots? (laughs) You think that makes you ready? (shrugs) Maybe. I'll tell you this though Dick. You get your gear ready. On that night you lace up your boots real tight and you pull that string on your trunks and extra tug. you collect yourself real good and bring everything that you feel you need to defeat me. I'm looking forward to that...and do you know what that will get you?
(again the pounding starts and the camera pans down to see Psycho is stabbing the fork into a large steak. the raw meat drips blood and it's nearly shreaded into large holes in some places. He looks at the camera, smiles and then drapes the beaten piece of bloody meat over his face)
Psycho: See this Dick? (laughs) This is what your face is going to look like when I get done with you. Nothing but a bloody meaty mess. Are you ready for that? For get about names Dick...worry about physical damage. If you let the fact I called you Dick pass through you mind then what does this do for you. Well you had better think hard and long.
(Holds up the fork and the meat slowly slides off his face leaving a bloody trail behind) Psycho: I'm bringing this to the ring Dick. I'm also bringing every sadistic feeling in my twisted mind. Halloween didn't thrill me this year...guess I got another chance on Thanksgiving. See you keep thinking this is all about that shiny belt around your waist. (shakes his head) Sorry to say, but in the ring...against me? your not fighting for a title Dick...you'll be fighting for your very soul
(He let's loose a sadistic laughs as the camera moves back and he plunges the fork into the table he was sitting at. Slowly he picks it backs up, turns his back and walks off)
<FTB> Methos17 — 28 November 2003 (Richard Gideon is seen standing in front of a WFW backdrop as the camera fades in. Smiling broadly from ear to ear he taps his WFW North American Title, covered with a black sheet. Eventually he turns toward the camera and begins to speak)
RG - So, Psycho says he's bringing the fork. Says he's going to turn me into a bloody piece of meat...Well, he wouldn't be called 'Psycho' if he didn't do crazy things. *Shrugs*
And I wouldn't be known as 'The System' if I didn't plan for everything. I'd love right now Psycho to say yes, you will bloody me. You will turn me into a piece of meat fit for fileting. But I can't. And neither can you. You can plan, sure. You can get all your little homicidal tendencies all bunched up and you can attempt to maim me like you have everyone else and...it won't work. In case you forgot Cletus, I cost you the WFW Title not too long ago. At the time it was viewed as an unsightly accident by the rookie by the WFW Announcing corp, but it was payback. I had told you I owed you from my match with Silver and you probably thought I was full of it. And instead you got a nice drop off a cage. (Chuckles)
(Gideon starts to stroke his beard) You think you're going to make this face deformed? Doubtful. You think you're going to beat me with your 'hardcore antics'? Impossible. See Psycho, you deluded fool hit me with your toys, and you get disqualified. And I'm sure even you in your infinite stupidity can understand that right?
(Gideon begins to speak really slow) PSYCHO...no win title....if PSYCHO...use ouch ouch stick. Can you grasp that muffin? Can you twist your few remaining synapses into a ball condusive enough for this little factoid to jump across them and reach you? Turkey Day Terror is coming closer, day by day, hour by hour...second by second. When we get in that ring you better be bringing some backup because buck-o you're not measuring up to the Vunderkind.
I'm WFW NA Champ now. That means I get to enjoy the life that is given to the best here, and enjoy all the amenities being a WFW champ has: I fly from place to place, my gym back home has my stuff ready an hour before I get there...Heck, I'm feeling great. And I won't be giving up my newly won lifestyle to the likes of a 'Psycho'...So think long and hard when you're talking Psycho. When you're talking to all the people listening to your rants, think of how you're going to have to back it up.
I asked for respect since we had been a good mixed tag in the past: you held up your end and I held up mine. But that wasn't enough for you. Well, come the Turkey Day Terror Match I'm going to give you a little something Doc Silver showed me. You'll remember it well once you see it. And when you do, there won't be anyone to help you..But when you try to stab me, when you try to assail me, I'll have my fans to feed off of. And when the WFW Faithful get behind me (taps belt) Good Things Happen.
My name is Richard Gideon. And The System now closes for this session. (Fade out) Devil666 — 1 December 2003 (Fade In: Psycho steps in before a WFW backdrop. Behind him upon a table a backstage monitor plays richard Gideon's previous promo. His words echo off the cement walls and Psycho nods knowingly as he continues to speak. The screen fades to black and using a remote he rewinds the tape and pauses a still frame onto Gideon's face. He then sits down on the table next to the tv, leans an elbow upon the top of it, taps it twice and looks towards the camera with a sarcastic smile)
Psycho: You know it's amazing to see how high one's opinion of themselves can go when you consider (sighs) in the grand scheme of things well they really haven't accomplished a whole lot. (taps the tv again) Ah, but to listen to young Gideon here...well his arrogance has swelled even beyond (taps the tv) this big mug. "I cost you the World title", "I'm the NA Champion" , "I fly from place to place", "My gym has stuff ready an hour early" (shakes his head) Hey Dick...I'm not sure who your trying to impress, but it's not me. (sighs) I know...I know...your young. Your new at the game and any little leap in progress seems like the whole world to you. (laughs) well junior give yourself a few years. Live the life...play the game...you'll see just how foolish I see you as. Maybe you'll realize this and laugh. (shrugs) Or chances our...you'll still be coming off sound like an ass
(He taps the screen again and mockingly pretends to pinch his chin) Psycho: That's ok though Dick...really it is. Because you can thank me latter for slapping a cold dose of reality moments after your shoulders are pinned to the mat. Look you didn't cost me the WFW title. I took doc silver to the top of that cage and fully expected to get dropped to the mat. My question for you though is this Dickie? If you wanted payback so badly...why did you keep Doc in the age for so long an allow me to climb up and get him? Why not just let Doc escape and cost me the title that way? Instead I went up and and suplexed Doc off the top of the cage. Knocked us both silly...I'll admit that. However you had nothing to do with the rule changes for that title match. Or the fact the WFW has rings with very thin mats and cages that don't reach the floor. Or is it this Dick? Has your ego gotten the better of you and your trying to take credit for something you never did? Yet you want me to show you respect? (shakes his head) I don't think so
(He pretends to wipe a bit of drool from Gideon's mouth) Psycho: Looks like your salivating at all your accomplishments. After all Your the NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION...all should bow to you. Well guess what Dick...your a champ now. respect doesn't come with that title. What does is hate, envy, jealousy, spite, anger, greed, and a whole host of other terrible emotions. Not on your part, but on others. On you it's seems only pride has gone to your head. And don't bother Dick...I have my very own reasons for wanting to defeat you. None of them have to do with ANYTHING I just mentioned. Wait...I'm wrong...I just called you a champion. My Mistake.
(He rewinds the tape to show gideon smiling broadly from ear-to-ear and taping the belt. Once more he pauses the screen)
Psycho: Your the NEW North American Champion. That means you just won that title. That also means you haven't defended it. an Dick...that's what a champion does. He defends his title...that's how he earns the name...that's how he truly becomes A CHAMPION!!! Now wait...I know you'll say the same thing about me and the WFW title. after all (grins) you did cost me my title. Just remember this...nobody pinned for that belt...I never submitted..only a silly rule took MY title from me. The case will be much different when I defeat you.
(He smiles broadly from ear-to-ear and pulls The Fork from his boot. He stares at it for a moment and then looks into the camera)
Psycho: ah, but of course we have what did you call it (pauses) Oh...yes..my ouch stick. well I suppose I don't need this. Ah, but where would the fun be in that. I'm sure you'll have the ref check and you'll be a stickler for the rules. (laughs) buy unlike you Dick...I'm not new at this game. All I need to do is hide this away. (slips the fork under his wrist tape) and then just happen to find it at the (winks) right time. You see i only need it once. Just that first initial gash. Then that blood will flow. Have you felt the blood flow yet Dick? That warm sensation of your life seeping out. I'm used to it...I've spilled my blood and others all around the world. for you though (grins) Well it's all new...just like your title. Just like you don't seem to understand the world your in right now...well you won't understand my bloody image of it during our match. That's where a monkey wrench fowls up The System. Oh...yes, but you'll have the fans to turn to right
(He laughs outloud and his face turns stone serious) Psycho: They going to break my submission? They going to kick out of a pin? They going to stop the blood from flowing? They going to get your second wind? Better yet Dick...think about this. what makes you think they'll be cheering for you? After all I've all but promised to give them what they want. After all wrestling is no different then combat from thousands of years ago. Your just a more civilized Christian to me...the lion. I've been there before...the crimson whipes them into a fury. It's a wonderious sight...a great spectacle...and you get to be at the center of it all. Try not to let that thought goto your head though dick (laughs) Allow me to do that for you
(He reaches behind and yanks the plug from the wall. The picture of the title vanishes. He turns and smiles back)
Psycho: See how quick it all vanished (He let's loose a sadistic laugh, turns his back and walks away) <FTB> Devil666 — 2 December 2003 (Fade In: Backstage a Turkey Day Terror is a mass of confusion. Trying to avoid any more voices then he already hears Psycho sits in the depths of the arena. alone with his thought for a few moments he listens carefully to the sounds echo through cement and steel. He smile and let's out a sinister laugh)
Psycho: Well tonight is the night Gideon. Are you ready? or are you scared? Your bold talk has grown silient...I wonder why? Did I give you something to think about? Make you question yourself? Well good. tonight will be more of the same and in the end...you'll thank me for it.
(He pauses and tlits his head) Psycho: What...don't beleive me? Well you soon will. see Gideon...despite all you THINK you've accomplished...I'm going to put some meaning behind your words. Simply tearing you apart and taking your title is not enough. When I'm done with you...well maybe...just maybe you'll be the man you THINK you are. That's if you survive my trail. See Gideon...while I don't see alot in you...I do see one thing....a hunger. You seem to really want everything you claim to have.
(He laughs and winks at the camera) Psycho: Well I'm going to put that too a test. tonight...in that ring Gideon...I'M GOING TO TAKE IT ALL AWAY!!! Your title...your pride and maybe even a piece of that face your so proud of. Then I'm going to sit back and see. I'm going to see just what kind of fight is in you. will you sit back and take it? or will you come after me with burning desire. (smiles) It's ok Gideon...I like fire.
(He turns his back to show a large patch of melted deformed skin on his back) Psycho: Although there is always the alternitive. You'll do just as I imagine. You'll turn your back and run. You'll let me hold that North American title over your head and you won't do the slightest thing. (sighs) Oh...well I guess it's not a perfect world.
(He smiles and spits) Psycho: but deep down...deep down I hope you've got some fire in you. I hope you've got that burning desire. That way...all my fun won't be done tonight. I'll have another match where I can torture you just a little bit more. It can be the end of the beginning tonight Gideon. When all is said and done...I guess we'll see just what kind of foolish choice you might make
(He let's loose a sadistic laugh and walks away) <FTB> Methos17 — 3 December 2003 (The camera fades in as it is moving toward the locker room of WFW North American Champion Richard Gideon. A hand stretches out and knocks on the door. A muffled "come in" is heard, and the cameraman opens the door and enters the room. Inside is Richard Gideon, seated on a bench in full gear, staring at the wall above the door. The WFW NA Title is nowhere to be seen.)
Richard Gideon - Well Psycho, I just got a view of your pre-match interview, and I'm impressed. You got me right on the money. I am hungry. I'm hungrier now with the title than I was fighting for the WFW World Title. Because for the first time in my career here since I was signed, I am in the driver's seat. I get to call my shots and I am quite honestly the happiest I've been in a while. But that comes at a cost.
(Gideon points to the wall he's been staring at. The cameraman turns and views the WFW NA Title placed up there with a poster that says "commitment to excellence")
Richard Gideon - That title was easy to win in the long run. The hard part comes tonight. Tonight I get to test my mettle against a former WFW World Heavyweight Champion. I can't knock you on that. But that means I gotta step it up. I'm going into that match to wrestle you Psycho. I'm not going in there to cripple or maim or burn or whatever. If I have to, I will. I don't like the thought of losing something I just got so soon. It irks me. It sits in my stomach, makes me uneasy. And that means to make that feeling go away I have to beat you.
(Gideon Stands up, pops the belt off of its perch and catches it. He places it on the seat next to him and stares at it as he continues to speak)
Gideon - This title is mine. You say I have a fire inside me and you are right on. I'm not looking for the royal treatment, I'm looking for respect. And I'm going to earn it in that ring. I started here cocky and arrogant, singing the praises of myself. I learned quickly that won't go....I've earned title shots and accolades perhaps before I was ready. Is this any different? Is this title just a gift of fate or a test? I choose the latter. Am I as good as I say I am? Yes, I am. And believe me Psycho there is not an ounce of ego or cockiness in those three words.
I'm a believer of self fulfilled prophecies in this sport. I'm not going out there with the plan to lose. Too many people trained me. Too many people helped me pay my way to this point. Those people are in the stands. They are at home watching me. I can't fail them any more than I can fail myself out there with a poor showing. I'm going to lay it all out in that ring Psycho. At Turkey Day Terror, Richard Gideon makes a statement.
You wanna scar me? Burn me? Stab me? If necessary, that's fine. If I leave that match a bloody mess with the title, I won't care. I will however hate myself if I went into that match avoiding it. Avoiding you. This is the penultimate prize in this league, and I have it. You will not take it away. You may make me bleed, you scar, but you will not win. And now if you excuse me, I have a title to retain.
(Gideon walks out of the room and the camera fades out)
