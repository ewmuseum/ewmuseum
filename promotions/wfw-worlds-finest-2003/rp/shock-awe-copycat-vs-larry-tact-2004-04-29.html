Shock & Awe: Copycat vs. Larry Tact

Starbreaker — 2 May 2004 Fade in: on a pristine, full-length mirror. In its reflection is part of a room. There is parque floor, a lavender sofa, a lamp on a small table, and part of a kitchenette in view. The sound of footsteps on the floor can be heard, and soon we see the form of Larry Tact. His hair is moppy looking, stringy and in disarray, a few locks hanging down to around his eyes, giving them a masked feeling. Tact's defined physique shows, as he only wears a pair of black shorts and boots. He holds a remote control in one hand, and presses a button on it, still looking into the mirror. Familiar voices can be heard from a television somewhere in the room.
VIC WATERS: I’m having second thoughts, I was leaning more towards Iron Lion, but with all of Larry Tact’s recent success, I’m going to predict an upset here tonight.
BRANDON JACOBS: That is a bold prediction by you. VIC WATERS: Tact is one of the finest technicians if not the greatest technician in wrestling today. Iron Lion may be big, but Tact’s skills are so refined that I think he may figure out a way to outsmart Iron Lion.
Still looking at his reflection, Tact speaks, looking deadpan at himself while the television continues in the background.
Tact: Recent success....elsewhere...I'm a Champion....but not here...not in WFW.... A couple clicks of the remote. BRANDON JACOBS: It is quite remarkable that El Arco Iris is even here today after what happened in the War Games cage.
VIC WATERS: L.O.V.E. gave him a hell of a beating. You could see that he was still feeling the effects of the attack when he was visiting those kids in the hospital.
BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion and Larry Tact are meeting in the center of the ring eye to eye and El Arco Iris is trying to make sure both men remain calm so the bout doesn’t turn into an angry brawl.
Tact: Why would it turn into an angry brawl? I'm a technician, first and foremost....I know how to keep control. I'm not so easily manipulated.....
BRANDON JACOBS: LARRY TACT CHOPS EL ARCO IRIS IN THE CHEST! Tact: Iris was at a handicap...had gone through War Games....he wasn't in condition to wrestle his top game. Iris was....at a disadvantage....I had the advantage.
*click..click* VIC WATERS: LOOK! IRON LION CAUGHT THE ARM AS TACT WAS GOING FOR A CHOP ON EL ARCO IRIS! BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion locks on an armbar on Larry Tact and he is wrenching it in… EL ARCO IRIS GRABS THE OTHER ARM OF LARRY TACT AND APPLIES AN ARMBAR!
Tact: ....and yet, who looked more the technicians...the high flyer and the big man....not the third wheel....
BRANDON JACOBS: LARRY TACT DROPS HIS LEGS OUT FROM UNDER HIM IN ORDER TO GET HIS OPPONENTS TO DROP HIM BUT AFTER A NEAR STUMBLE EL ARCO IRIS REPLANTS HIMSELF IN POSITION!
Tact: ....who couldn't even get out of a simple hold.... BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion and El Arco Iris swing him back and forth by his arms…. THEY LET GO AND HE CRASHES HARD ONTO THE MAT ON HIS BACK!
Tact: ....nothing but a simple hold...so what if it was two men...where was the technical wizard...where was the Champion....where is the Champion......
BRANDON JACOBS: Larry Tact goes to get up but Iron Lion catches him and HITS THE TRIPLE RIB BREAKER! Tact falls down to the mat and grabs his ribs!
Tape is wrapped around Tact's midsection. BRANDON JACOBS: Iron Lion holds Larry Tact down with a boot to the throat and he is calling to El Arco Iris…. EL ARCO IRIS CLIMBS UP TO THE TOP ROPE…. MOONSAULT FROM THE TOP ROPE ONTO TACT!
VIC WATERS: That was picture perfect. Tact: ...perfect..... *click..click* BRANDON JACOBS: ...Larry Tact isn’t showing any mercy! Larry Tact grabs the injured El Arco Iris and locks on the Sky Lock Inverted STF!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris is still banged up from the War Games match. The smart thing to do would be to give up.
Tact: ...he won't give up.... VIC WATERS: The referee is checking on El Arco Iris but he is repeatedly saying no. BRANDON JACOBS: EL ARCO IRIS STRUGGLES AND GETS TACT FLAT ON HIS BACK! The referee goes down to make the count! 1--------------------------------------2---------------------- TACT GETS HIS SHOULDER UP IN TIME!
Tact: ...that was very close.... VIC WATERS: That was very close. BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris is struggling to get Tact flat on his back again…. HE DOES IT AGAIN AND THE REF GOES DOWN FOR THE COUNT! 1----------------------------------------------2----------------------- TACT GETS HIS SHOULDER UP AND HE BREAKS THE HOLD!
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris was having too much success getting Tact in a pinning position to keep it on but the damage has been done to El Arco Iris.
Tact: ...success....a high flyer having too much success...countering...against a technician who couldn't counter.....
*click..click* VIC WATERS: Get up Tact, you can’t let Iron Lion do you in. BRANDON JACOBS: Tact is trying to get up…. IRON LION BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND HITS LARRY TACT WITH A YAKUZA KICK RIGHT IN THE FACE!
Tact reaches up his free hand, brushing away strands of hair from his left eye. A thin, deep purple ring surrounds it.
BRANDON JACOBS: ...TACT GRABS THE HAIR OF IRON LION SO HE CAN’T HIT HIS FINISHER! Tact: ...futile attempts...who looked more the team.... BRANDON JACOBS: …EL ARCO IRIS IS UP AND HE IS HEADING FOR THE TOP…. HE PERCHES ON THE TOP ROPE…. MISSILE DROPKICK RIGHT INTO THE FACE OF LARRY TACT AND IRON LION DELIVERS THE PRIDE HAMMER ON TACT! Iron Lion makes the cover! 1----------------------------2-----------------------------3!
Tact reaches again with his free hand, removing the remaining strands of hair blocking his face. An identical purple ring surrounds his other eye. The bridge of his nose is slightly puffy....the skin there a little pink.
RING ANNOUNCER: Larry Tact has been eliminated! The glare of his bruised eyes is indicative of his emotions at the moment. He peers into them without so much as a flinch.
*click..click* BRANDON JACOBS: El Arco Iris goes down for the pin! 1-----------------------------------------------------2-----------------------------------------------------3! YES!
Tact: ...what disadvantage.... RING ANNOUNCER: THE WINNER OF THE THREE WAY MATCH…. EL ARCO IRIS! BRANDON JACOBS: Holy cow, El Arco Iris did it! Tact: ...he did it.... *click* ...so why couldn't I do it! Static can be heard as the tape is stopped. Tact breaks from looking at the mirror, turning his back to it. He takes a couple steps away....then turns and throws the remote at the mirror, cracking it on impact, a few shards falling off. Tact looks at the cracked mirror, leaning over with his arm limply hanging in front of him. He glares at his disfigured reflection, then looks down to the ground. The static is still audible.
How could I have lost! Iris was already down going into the match...all I needed to do was take him out...and Lion was beatable! There isn't a wrestler alive that I don't have a shot at beating! Not just a shot, but a legitimate shot! I've torn through the ranks elsewhere, I've made my niche in other promotions...for four years! Why not here...what's the difference? What's making things different!
Tact lifts his head back up, still leaning down...knees bent...one arm hanging loosely, the other balled in a fist at his waist.
I can just hear the line of bull(BLEEP) coming from the rest of them....the garbage about how I can't cut it here...BULL(BLEEP)! I may not make bold, ignorant statements about how I'm "the best in the world" or some unbelievable trash like that, but that doesn't mean I'm some bottom feeding, wrestling carousel piece of (BLEEP), where everyone gets their turn to beat me! I'm not the one for that. I'm a winner. I'm the one who makes my way up and through the ceilings, who pulls out the stops, and who says to hell with the odds! I break the odds system. I make my own odds.
Tact's nostrils flare as he glares at the cracked mirror, his breathing intensifying. Then he walks over to the mirror, reaches down, picks up the remote, and walks near the television, the camera panning over to track Tact's movement. After jamming his finger into the remote button a few times, the tape begins playing again. Tact paces back and forth as the footage plays through.
VIC WATERS: El Arco Iris has an angel on his shoulder. BRANDON JACOBS: You may be right. Folks, when we comeback, it is time for one of the biggest grudge matches in WFW history, Shawn Hart will return for one night in order to get revenge at Scotty Michaels for what he did to him at Merrython, we will be back right after this commericlal break, STAY TUNED!
Static. The tape has been stopped, and starts rewinding. Tact has stopped, pointing the remote at the television. He looks at it, a look of incomprehension on his face.
*click..click* VIC WATERS: Get up Tact, you can’t let Iron Lion do you in. BRANDON JACOBS: Tact is trying to get up…. IRON LION BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND HITS LARRY TACT WITH A YAKUZA KICK RIGHT IN THE FACE!
*clickclick* Tact looks with regret. BRANDON JACOBS: …EL ARCO IRIS IS UP AND HE IS HEADING FOR THE TOP…. HE PERCHES ON THE TOP ROPE…. MISSILE DROPKICK RIGHT INTO THE FACE OF LARRY TACT AND IRON LION DELIVERS THE PRIDE HAMMER ON TACT! Iron Lion makes the cover! 1----------------------------2-----------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: Larry Tact has been eliminated! Disgust. *clickclick* BRANDON JACOBS: …EL ARCO IRIS IS UP AND HE IS HEADING FOR THE TOP…. HE PERCHES ON THE TOP ROPE…. MISSILE DROPKICK RIGHT INTO THE FACE OF LARRY TACT AND IRON LION DELIVERS THE PRIDE HAMMER ON TACT! Iron Lion makes the cover! 1----------------------------2-----------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: Larry Tact has been eliminated! Anger. *clickclick* BRANDON JACOBS: …EL ARCO IRIS IS UP AND HE IS HEADING FOR THE TOP…. HE PERCHES ON THE TOP ROPE…. MISSILE DROPKICK RIGHT INTO THE FACE OF LARRY TACT AND IRON LION DELIVERS THE PRIDE HAMMER ON TACT! Iron Lion makes the cover! 1----------------------------2-----------------------------3!
RING ANNOUNCER: Larry Tact has been eliminated! Static. Then the television screen shatters to pieces, as the remote control flies through the front. Camera pans to Larry Tact, looking with utter rage-- teeth clenched, purple-ringed eyes staring a hole into the hole that used to be the screen.
A door opens and a woman comes from it, then gasps as she sees the television, then the mirror. Her auburn hair is highlighted with blonde streaks, neatly brushed and straight, settled near her shoulders. Her emerald eyes look stunned, as does the rest of her face. Her lips, pursed, finally loosen when Tact looks at her with a deadened stare.
Woman: Larry...what are you doing! Tact: Is there a problem Cindy? Cindy: Yes, you! I thought you said you were fine. Tact's shoulders start shaking up and down as he laughs inside himself, not letting it escape his lips, curved upward in a small smirk.
Tact: Yeah....and you believed it. Cindy: What's wrong with you? Tact frowns. Tact: What wrong with me? You know full well what's wrong. Cindy: Are you going to let one loss get to you? I've never seen you like this....it's just one match, right? It's nothing to get so worked up over....
Tact begins walking towards Cindy, his voice raised. Tact: What do you know about it! You don't know anything! This isn't about one match. This is about the past five months! This is about everything that's happened since I came back!
Cindy steps back a little, looking fearful. Tact stops when she does, and lowers his voice. However, now it sounds more menacing, like a low growl.
Tact: I didn't come to WFW so I could be some (BLEEP)ing guinea pig. I came back to do what I've done ever since I came into this sport. I came back to win....to succeed. And I came back to succeed against the best....the world's finest wrestlers.
Cindy: Alright...so what's the problem? You've been winning, haven't you? You won the tag match--
Tact: That was a handicap match! Cindy: But you won. And in the So Cal Rumble you did well, too. Tact: I lost. And if you look at the time each person entered and was eliminated, I was one of the quickest ones to go.
Cindy: And before that you beat Luster. That was at the last Pay-Per-View. Tact: Narrowly beat him. Luster shouldn't have given me so much trouble. I almost got hit with his finisher, if I hadn't countered.
Cindy: But you did counter him. Tact: And even after that, I nearly got pinned, if I hadn't reversed it. Cindy: But you did reverse it! Cindy looks a little flustered. Cindy: Larry, I don't understand you! All you're doing is mentioning what-ifs and alternate scenarios. The fact is...you beat Luster. You went into the So Cal Rumble and eliminated Richard Gideon, which was one of the things you wanted to do. Even if you didn't win the Rumble, you still got the tag match. Luster got taken out, so what? Was that in your control? No. What were you supposed to do, lay down for Edmunds to pin you again?
Tact's eyes narrow. Tact: I never have, nor ever would lay down for Edmunds! Cindy: Exactly my point! You went out to the ring and did your job. Michaels was your partner, for one night! So you made the best of the situation and won the match. Then you made the card for the Superbowl of Wrestling, against two men who aren't pushovers themselves-- El Arco Iris and Iron Lion. You lost, but would you have preferred to not have been invited to the Superbowl of Wrestling instead?
Tact: You're right. I looked like the pushover. Cindy: So you'd rather WFW had not put you on the card at all, is that what you're saying? Tact: Of course not! I never said I didn't want to be on the show. Cindy: Then maybe you should just admit to yourself that you were beaten by two men who were better on that night!
Both are yelling. Tact: You don't understand! Cindy: What am I misunderstanding? You got pinned, didn't you? There was no interference!
Tact turns around and walks away from her. Cindy: You went after both of them. They came back and took you out of the match. Isn't that what happened, or did I see it wrong at the show? Have I been seeing and hearing it wrong on the tape?
Tact stops at the table, looking down, fists balled. Cindy: For Christ's sake, Larry, you've been playing the damn match over and over for the past three days. I think I've memorized what Waters and Jacobs said. I know what happened in that match, and so do you. So why can't you just admit it to yourself and move on? You were beaten by them, Larry!
Tact: YOU'RE WRONG!! Tact grabs the lamp from the table, suddenly turning and throwing it across the room. Cindy screams as it hits a wall and shatters.
Tact: That's not what happened! They didn't beat me....you know why? Cuz I don't even know who that was in the damn ring! Do you know how big an event that was? It was the Superbowl of Wrestling. It hadn't been held for four years, and do you know what happened at the last one? Stars were born. People who were already stars, showed as much. Other stars rose above what they had previously been known for. Everyone stepped their game up. And this year, people did it again. Except me! For some damn reason, I couldn't do it. Hell....I couldn't even do what I usually do. I couldn't break a damn submission hold! Not even a basic one! Much less properly apply one! Do you get what I'm saying, Cindy?
Cindy doesn't say anything, but just looks at him. Tact: I wasn't myself at that show. I've been back for five months, now. This was supposed to be a big month. It's my four year anniversary on the circuit. I wanted to make it a huge month. But what happened? I blew it. I went into the Superbowl of Wrestling and wrestled like an amateur. I wrestled like (BLEEP). I shouldn't even bother trying to explain. You can't understand--
Cindy: Why do you keep saying that! Cindy looks irate, suddenly. She walks across the room and gets right in Tact's face. Cindy: I don't understand. I can't understand. What's your problem! Who do you take me for, some uneducated dropout? Do you suddenly, for who knows what reason, think I've lived in city slums all my life? I'm an intelligent woman, damn you! I haven't been with you for two and a half years because I need someone to hang onto. I've got a job of my own, and you know what? I am successful at it!
Tact's eyebrows furrow. Tact: I never said-- Cindy: You know what? Shut up, because I'm tired of hearing you pick at my words! You've got a match with Copycat coming up, if you hadn't noticed. Did you even realize that, or were you too busy losing your common sense somewhere? And by the way, in case you forgot, this place isn't ours! You can't just go breaking things at your leisure. Not that it would be much better if this was our place....
Cindy pokes Tact in the chest. Cindy: I don't know what happened to you in Foxboro, but you're not the same. You're like a totally different man, Larry. And you picked the wrong time to hit a mid-life crisis, or whatever the hell this is...because if you don't pull yourself together, then you're going to miss the opportunity that you're getting.
Tact: What are you talking about, mid-life crisis? I'm not even thir-- Cindy: WILL YOU STOP ALREADY! Stop picking away! Look, you've been (BLEEP)ing about how WFW hasn't given you a chance to show you can compete at the top? Well now you're getting someone who's right near the top. Copycat. And don't you dare say he isn't. He just beat Dan Ryan, for Christ's sake!
Cindy turns and starts walking away. Tact: No, I know he's up there in the ranks. But after that match with Ryan, and since he won it....WFW figures they'd give one of their stars a little breather. That's what I feel. They're just feeding me to Copycat like a dead piece of meat for a tired, hungry wolf.
Cindy stops at the door to the other room. When she turns around, the look on her face can best be described as...pitiful.
Tact: What? Do you not understand that, at least? Cindy: No....I understand completely. Her lip begins to quiver, and her eyes turn soft. Cindy: I just never thought I'd see the day when...when Larry Tact admits he's got no fight in him. I never thought I'd see you, healthy enough to compete, admit you've got no chance to win.
Cindy puts her hands in her face and goes into the room, slamming the door shut. Tact glares with his eyes, but his fists are not balled. His arms hang at his sides, limp, and slowly his eyes turn from livid to empty, vacant balls. Tact reaches down and grabs a blue shirt and jeans from the couch, heading into the bathroom.
A minute later he re-emerges, changed, and walks over to the kitchenette area. Tact snatches his leather coat from a hook and puts it on. He opens the door to the apartment and exits, closing the door gently behind. Fade out.
GARTHIsTheLaw — 4 May 2004 (Cueup: "Gasoline" by Seether) (Fade in on a close-up shot of Larry Tact's face from his latest promo. He looks anguished. The shot sits frozen for a few seconds before Copycat walks in front of it; it is apparently set as a backdrop. Copycat wears jeans, his trademark beret, and a Marvelous 3 T-shirt. He looks at the camera emotionlessly)
Copycat: I weep for you, Larry. (He sarcastically wipes away an imaginary tear) Copycat: You've been dealt such a bad hand, haven't you? Losing to Iron Lion, getting embarrassed in the So Cal Rumble, having your aspirations of a North American Title shot ripped away because of the neverending issue between Psycho and Richard Gideon. And now you're being fed to me. Poor Larry.
(He shakes his head very slightly) Copycat: Please. Your little lover's quarrel with that Cindy gal was a waste of my time to watch, but at least it gave me an issue on which I can agree with her. LISTEN TO YOURSELF, LARRY. You seem to enjoy watching tapes of yourself, over and over again. Watch your latest promo. What on earth do you think gives you the right to have a nervous breakdown over a bad break or two? Do you think you're above making mistakes, Larry? Do you think you're above the occasional inconvenience? Do you really think those few instances of unpleasantness are a good reason to smash things, to obsess over the same video clip, to drive away your girlfriend slash wife slash whatever the f(BLEEP)k she is?
(Copycat's voice rises) Copycat: WAKE UP, LARRY! You're the victim of MISFORTUNE. That's something that happens to everyone. Jesus, just look at the WFW World Heavyweight Title history. There hasn't been a successful defense of that belt since Shawn Hart had it! What the HELL makes you think you're so damned AWESOME that things like that shouldn't happen to you?
(Copycat pauses for a second to stave off his temper, then resumes speaking) Copycat: You have no idea what you've got, Larry. A few bad breaks here and there are NO cause for tears. You want to see REAL career trouble? Have you been paying attention to what's been happening to ME lately? I've been getting gang-beaten practically every damned week. I've had titles dangled in front of me, only to be ripped away with the revelation that I'd never have had a chance at them to begin with. League officials, other wrestlers, our ESTEEMED thank-God-he's-now-EX-president Wylde...I've been REPEATEDLY denied everything I ask for in WFW. Not just victories...titles, opportunities, and most important of all...
(Copycat glares a hole through the camera) Copycat: RESPECT. Your last match was against Iron Lion and El Arco Iris, Larry...two men who clearly respected you. Me? I asked for a match with Scotty Michaels after he used three hired goons to beat me. And I didn't get it. I asked for a one-on-one match with Sean Edmunds to settle the issue we've had going for years. And I didn't get it. No, instead I was put into every type of match imaginable with Anarky and his ridiculous "L.O.V.E." group. Matches clearly set up so I would lose. Can anyone think it was a damned COINCIDENCE that Felix Red just so happened to be L.O.V.E.'s mystery partner in War Games? Absolutely not. He had no reason to dislike L.O.V.E., but he damn sure didn't like me.
(Copycat looks away, then shoots a displeased glance at the camera) Copycat: You complain that WFW is quote feeding unquote you to me? Be glad I'm just one man. I was fed to two, three, four men at a time for MONTHS, Larry. And when WFW FINALLY decided the fans had seen enough of four guys versus Copycat, what did they do? They put me in a match GUARANTEED to not advance my WFW career. What did I gain from beating Dan Ryan? He isn't in WFW. I can't move up the ladder in WFW with that victory. Instead, who does? Who's got the next title opportunity? El Arco Iris might deserve it. But Richard Gideon? Felix Red? Did no one notice that those two LOST at the Superbowl of Wrestling? I was told that my match with Dan Ryan was to "prove myself in WFW." But instead, it's just a ratings ploy. Felix Red -- FELIX F(BLEEP)KING RED -- gets a chance at a title match, while I get a qualifying match for something I should have gotten months ago anyway.
(The beginning of a smirk appears on Copycat's face) Copycat: But I didn't walk out of the Superbowl of Wrestling empty-handed. I knew I wouldn't gain anything from WFW for beating Dan Ryan. But I did gain SOMETHING. After I pinned Dan Ryan's shoulders to the mat for three seconds and then locked his prone, semi-conscious body in the Cat's Claw until half the officials in the back had to pull me off...he'll NEVER take me lightly again. He may not admit it, but I came out of the Superbowl of Wrestling with his RESPECT.
(Copycat visibly fights to keep his smirk from spreading) Copycat: And yet, Larry, you whine. You kvetch. You complain. I can be spat on by everyone I encounter for months on end, but when you're moderately inconvenienced, the world is ending. You were so wrapped up in feeling sorry for yourself, you practically forgot you had to face me. The whining, the forgetting, the implied superiority...there are a lot of ways I could take those things. But right now, I'm going to take them the only one way. It's painfully obvious, Larry, that you don't respect me.
(Copycat's hands tighten into fists) Copycat: And that's just something I WILL NOT STAND FOR. I don't care how unhappy you are with life right now, Larry. It's been much worse for me, and every complaint you make is a direct sign of disrespect to me. Now, maybe after you lost at the Superbowl of Wrestling, you went back to your hotel room to cry, Larry. I didn't look for you backstage. But if you happened to stick around, then maybe you saw what happens to people who don't show me respect. Good Eddie, the Mighty Impala, Alessandro Giovanni, Dan Ryan...these are people who lack respect, and at the Superbowl of Wrestling, I showed them what happens when they forget how to show respect to those who command it.
(Copycat crosses his arms in front of him to help alleviate the anger-driven tightening of his muscles)
Copycat: So you've got two choices, Larry. You can stop your whining and be thankful for what you've got, recognize what you're up against at Shock & Awe. Or you can go on feeling sorry for yourself. How you go about it is not for me to decide. But I will say this, Larry -- after Shock & Awe, I will have your respect, and if I have to take it by force, then that's what I'm going to do. One way or another, Larry, you will give me the RESPECT...that I deserve.
(Copycat glares at the camera as we fade out) Starbreaker — 12 May 2004 Fade in: on a shot of another nice looking apartment. A velvet red sofa, carpet floor, white painted walls with some contemporary art pieces hung by hooks. Sitting in a windowsill, legs bent, arms crossed, head staring up at the ceiling is Larry Tact. He's got on a pair of stonewashed blue jeans and a grey hooded sweatshirt on. Tact glances out of the window, seeing the rain pouring down on the city, stories below the apartment. The grey skies are reflective of his expression-- gloomy, downtrodden. When he speaks, Tact's voice has a bitter bite to it.
Tact: You speak as if you're looking in on my career from afar, Copycat. But the truth is, you haven't been watching very closely. I don't expect people to pay attention to me...not anymore. It's been made clear to me that I'm considered a "temporary accomodation" in wrestling promotions. People see me come in, and they think I'm going to disappear after a few months. I don't know where or when I gave that impression, but no matter. I don't care if opponents overlook me. I don't want people following my every movement, like some paparazzi. But if you don't know....then don't come out speaking as if you do.
Tact pauses and holds up a hand, extending one finger at a time, until all five fingers are raised.
Tact: Five months, Copycat...(lowers hand)...it may not seem like much to you, but to me the past five months have been a long walk down a very rocky road. I came back that long ago, looking to find new challenges and surpass my past accomplishments. I looked to rise up to a new level. In some respect, I have done that. I've recognized some parts of my game that could use tweaking, and made some adjustments, improving. But now, after not one match, Copycat, but five months' worth, do you know what I see? I've been going in a circle! That road has been one stupid cycle, and I've been caught up in it since I returned to the ring. So when I hear you speaking on how I've been "obsessing" and "whining" over my match at the Superbowl of Wrestling...when you imply that I've been overreacting....that's just a bunch of crap coming from you. Cuz you don't get it, Copycat. You don't know what I've gone through. I don't expect you to. After all, you're just another guy making an improbable claim. Smartest Player in the Game? You're no better than the self-proclaimed "best wrestler in the world"....correction, the many who are the self-proclaimed "best wrestler in the world." But I guess you wouldn't notice how off you are about me. Or how empty that tag is. You're too concerned about your own situation. The "REAL career trouble."
Tact shakes his hands, feigning concern, mometarily. Tact: Far be it for me to overlook all the trouble you've been facing, Copycat. After all....you've already had a chance to make your mark in WFW. You've been in a tournament for a shot at the number one contendership to the North American Title. You've had a match with a Heavyweight Title shot on the line. And you've gotten yourself into half the trouble you mentioned! Or, maybe it's been Icekold and you that have brought you trouble. Of course, it's clear now...I should have seen how badly you've been sleighted.
He scoffs, shaking his head. Tact: Please. You've had more chances than I, so I don't see how I'm somehow the unreasonable one. Anarky getting on your case? Hell, if you want to play at the top, of course people are going to try stepping in your way, Copycat. You're not getting the red carpet treatment.
