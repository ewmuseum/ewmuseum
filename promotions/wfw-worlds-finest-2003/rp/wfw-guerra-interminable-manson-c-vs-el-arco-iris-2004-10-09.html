WFW Guerra Interminable: Manson (c) vs. El Arco Iris

9 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 9 October 2004 This match is for the WFW World Heavyweight Title and will be under Lucha Rules. The RP deadline is Wednesday, October 20th 11:59 EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld].
Manson — 10 October 2004 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, in plain black t-shirt and jeans, in front of a counter pouring a container of greased fat into a stopped sink. The WFW World title belt hangs off a hook in the background.))
MANSON: I ,more than anyone else, was shocked to learn that I could make mistakes. In fact, even when I still do so, it shocks me. I know, I know....it was a tag team tavern brawl with many factors and probabilities that weigh in the decision...the whole place was burning down.....I'm still the WFW World Champion regardless...and of course Nark can have a token pin over me in tag team match since I've manipulated him for years and abused him in plans he still doesn't know about and probably never will unless I get really, really drunk.....
But what stings more than anything was the fact that I was saved from a burning building by Copycat. Now I clearly understand his motivation and anyone watching wrestling for the past 5 years should be able to recite it before even Copycat does, just as such a fan should also be able to figure out who hijacked the cage, and, no, it was not LOVE.
Now I've been in fires and worse, I'd be interested in seeing just badly I could burn or how I'd find a way out of it. I'd even be interested in the weeks of captivating WFW television to follow as they try to find out if the World's Finest Wrestler is dead or alive, and if he got out of that fire. However, worst of all, Copycat saves me like I'm a woman....a helpless cliche of a heroine who might as well have been tied to a pair of railroad tracks in that very same burning bar...if it could have been accomplished.
Not that I blame Copycat for this, but I'm sure I'll have this and more rage to vent at him and Scotty Michaels when the time comes and a cage is as good a place as any....especially when no one can get out....
That's in the future though. For now, I must deal with my frustration in a hobby I've picked up over the years.
Making soap. And to make soap...you need fat. And taking my cue from the celebrated film and novel Fight Club, I'm using the fat of real live human beings. But just any human beings...no...not just celebrities....No.....but the very fat sunctioned off of your favorite WFW superstars!
Yes, I went through all the files and folders most people are restricted from and found all the clinics and medical centers that take care of lipo and other cosmetic procedures for the wrestling world.
All your heroes have been through these places....getting facelifts....getting prescriptions....having their minor surgeries....and in the very odd case..getting breast implants. And I am talking about the men. They also get lipo, since for a lot of these guys its easier than just staying in shape. Then they throw out this priceless greased fat in the trash in the alleys, and luckily, I come by every other week at night and reap the benefits.
A few pounds off Anarky's ass? What about a bit of Scotty Michaels's thighs? Iron Lion's second chin? How about a piece of Felix Red's forearm? And, yes, the rumors are true...Jared Wells once had man-breasts...but they were sucked off...bagged....stolen...and then made into precious soap.
Soap that I generously leave at every hotel and arena the WFW tours through. Soap that I've given as gifts the odd holiday to the WFW offices and to the officials and board of directors themselves. I've even sold some of it as merchandise.
Imagine the lucky fan washing himself down with the greased fat leeched off the waistline of Shawn Hart.
And so every night, I toil away here....but not forever....because soon I go down to ole Mexico..for another title defense..this time against the ever-contented Iris Del Arco.
Or the albatross around my neck. How many times have I been paired with this specimen in a tag team match only to lose because he couldn't hold up his end? To take the fall myself because he was counting the stars he thought he could see in the ceiling?
I was even forced to be paired with him via the wishes of hospitalized children. How my reputation recovered, I can't tell you. Some things defy explanation.
But, now, finally is the reckoning. A man who tried to make me happy, a man who preaches good will, and a man who inadvertedly thwarted me will finally be brought to call.
Your only crime was living, Iris. And it's enough. Because while you're scrawny, there's enough fat to be taked off you,leaving you a wretched skeleton to be left out in the Mexican wastelands, some for the border-crossers to pick the coyote out of their teeth with.
And then I've sell your fat as soap to each and every one of them....because they all really need to take a bath anyway.
But continue to frustrate me..Iris....and other who will be locked in a cage with me and those who were in a burning bar with should take note....and I will remind you that there's always enough soap to go around...and with enough soap....you can always make explosives.
And I can burn down much than a bar, and, yes, I know how to make a person wash themselves with fire.
We'll see how it falls out, Iris, but you won't be happy again. Not after me...not after I throw the albatross's decayed body back into the ocean where it belongs.
Diablo — 13 October 2004 (“Boston” by the Blue Man Group) (FADEIN: El Arco Iris, in his ring gear with a long rainbow-colored, but mostly red tones, robe over it, in front of a rainbow back drop.)
El Arco Iris: So the time has finally come for El Arco Iris. This is one of the biggest events in the history of this company, in one of the most sacred venues of professional wrestling, and I am competing for its most important title against perhaps one of the greatest wrestlers this sport has seen. No words in the modern lexicon can describe how incredibly privileged El Arco Iris feels to be given this opportunity when it takes others years to get to this very same spot, but enough of being proud, opportunities are nothing without seizing them. El Arco Iris has pretty much said everything that needs to be said of who El Arco Iris is, what El Arco Iris stands for, and where El Arco Iris wants to go, and it is time to put words to rest and let actions take control.
(El Arco Iris crosses his arms across his chest.) Iris: And just as El Arco Iris says that, a thought comes to mind which immediately wants to contradict it. El Arco Iris will never belittle señor Manson’s vast knowledge of literature and philosophy, but need El Arco Iris remind you of what happened when the mariner tossed the albatross off of his neck? Do you remember, and most certainly you do, how when he let the bird’s body sink to the depths of the ocean, his ship turns derelict, is stranded in the middle of the ocean, and his crew slowly dies of thirst? It is quite a gruesome thought, really, and El Arco Iris really does not prefer Coleridge. But it is still something one should consider. When we teamed, yes, we had our setbacks, but surely, because you are infallible and almighty, it is completely El Arco Iris’ fault. You, señor Manson, had to pursue your own vendettas and work on your own, not even bothering to acknowledge El Arco Iris was there. If El Arco Iris had been able to help you, you wouldn’t have wanted it. You are so obsessed with being the be-all and end-all, the alpha and omega that you stumble over your own pride and fall flat on your face. And it has consumed you. You have become complacent, you have become cynical. You shot the albatross with your own cross bow and hung it around your own neck. And El Arco Iris can’t help you, nor is there any point.
(El Arco Iris shrugs his shoulders.) Iris: Yes. El Arco Iris has given up on any hope of helping you, because El Arco Iris has tried too many times as to be reasonable. But when El Arco Iris reaches that goal, and proves El Arco Iris’ mettle and worth, El Arco Iris’ message can be carried farther and wider than it ever has before. More people can open their hearts to the rosy-fingered touch of joy and be freed from the clouds of anger and hate than El Arco Iris could ever have hoped. Maybe then, if you can let yourself for once not be omnipotent, let yourself for once not be invincible, come to terms with the fact that you are human, you will realize that the crimes El Arco Iris commits are only crimes against an unjust law, a law that dictates that happiness is pointless, and that pain is all there is, the kind of law that turns men into what you have become. You maybe can help yourself, and then you’ll be far cleaner than any amount of soap can get you. At Arena Mexico, en la catedrál de la lucha libre...whether you want it our not, we will both be leaving with a SMIIIIIIILE…on our faces!
(Fade to Rainbow) Manson — 13 October 2004 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, WFW World Heavyweight Champion, sits at a black wodden round table alone, with only the title belt in front of him.))
MANSON: Iris, obviously, as always, you didn't heed my words. You complain that I want to be invincible? That I want to seem omnipotent? Were you paying attention when I admitted that not only I make mistakes, but that I was pinned in the tag team brawl? Of course, I seem omnipotent and invincible, more than others, that why I'm the champion.
However, you don't see me complaining about Mike Lane. But you complain that I wanted to pursue my own vendettas? Now, I wouldn't think that someone in a wrestling match would want to do something violent like that. Then again, not everyone is saddled with a partner who is has the body of a 12 year old.
Time and time again, I had to put up with you as a handicap. Time and time again, I had to sit around and listen to you harp about being happy and the joy of children. You and Copycat even went so far as to claim that you were my friends.
You realize this is 2 out of 3 falls, don't you, Iris? That means you're going to be fed to me twice. You were fortunate before that you were never in direct opposition to me. But because of your lagging, abysmal tag team partnership forced me too many times, you must be shown the proper comeuppance.
I cannot tolerate you any longer. However, if you want me to be happy, that will be likely. I won't be smiling, but neither will you. Not after I kick your teeth out of your mouth. But fear not, your loose teeth will fly into the crowd and there will be many a happy child that will receive a souvenir that night, if it doesn't fly in their eyes.
You see, you missed out the important part about Coleridge's poem. The mariner survived everything, no matter how harsh, and the hell with his crew and his ship.
That's how it works, Iris. I just have to push your head over the water myself. And, really, I should have handled this before.
Diablo — 14 October 2004 (CUE UP: "Mesa que mas apaulda" by Climax) (FADEIN: Arena Mexico, empty, save for El Arco Iris, sitting on the top turnbuckle in one arena, dressed in a rainbow tie-dye sweat jacket and pants. He's holding in each of his hands a mask, one is blue with a silver design surrounding the eyes and the other is silver.)
El Arco Iris: El Arco Iris was already going to come here anyway to check upon some old friends, so El Arco Iris figured it would be prudent to come by and get a feel for the place El Arco Iris hasn't been in for such a long time. El Arco Iris bought these two masks from a vendor outside, because they remind me of two men who El Arco Iris used to always come and see here in this very arena, and always made El Arco Iris smile. These two masks in their days were symbols of such heroics and such grandeur that El Arco Iris has always hoped some day this multi-colored mask would be sold along side them some day. These masks are symbols of a time when great men built this sport on a base of honor and sportsmanship, and were looked up to by all, which is something sorely lacking in almost any professional sport today. No heroes anymore.
(El Arco Iris weighs both masks thoughtfully in his hands, then rolls them up neatly and slides them into a pocket in his jacket.)
Iris: For all your greatness, and all your talent, Michael Manson, you honestly have blinded yourself with your own ego. El Arco Iris would only hope that in the last year that El Arco Iris has worked hard enough and risked his life enough that it would earn a little more than nonchalant dismissal at your hands. El Arco Iris could only hope that when outnumbered and cornered in a steel cage and locked in a painful hold and refusing to submit, to let El Arco Iris' resolve be broken, that it would earn more than being a simple speed bump. We're free from having to deal with any more foolishness from Rat Fink, or Psycho, or anybody who could skew the odds and make us point fingers. It will just be the two of us, and all El Arco Iris would have thought that perhaps after knowing that El Arco Iris MEANS not conceding, prevailing in the face of adversity, and never saying die, that I would not be cut down to a lamb being placed before the wolf. Well, there is a difference between being pure of spirit and being passive, and you have gravely confused the two, because El Arco Iris surely is not the latter. It can be 1, 3, 5, or 10 falls, El Arco Iris will not be denied, nor will El Arco Iris ever step down from the challenge!
(El Arco Iris pulls the masks back out again.) Iris: Because they wouldn't have, either. El Arco Iris' name will be etched next to all of those who stepped upon this very canvas and may have been the sure losers, the wimps, facing men who were champions, who were giants, some say even gods, but they still prevailed. El Arco Iris shall prevail, because even if El Arco Iris' body crumbles or mind falters, El Arco Iris' spirit will not be defeated, nor will all those whose hearts El Arco Iris has touched, and all of those whose lives have hopefully been made a little bit better just because El Arco Iris keeps doing what El Arco Iris knows needs to be done. You made El Arco Iris cripple you and handicap you when we were on the same side because of your own stubbornness, and your own paranoid lone wolf attitude, which will only serve to bring your own defeat here, between these ropes.
Here in Arena Mexico, El Arco Iris finds the gold at the end of the rainbow. And you'll just have to learn to tolerate that. (Iris gives another contemplative glance to the two masks in his hands.) And El Arco Iris knows at least two people who, somewhere, will have a SMIIIIIIIIIIILE...on their faces.
(Fade to Rainbow) Manson — 16 October 2004 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, the World's Finest Wrestler, lies back with his hands folded prayer-like in the corner the ring, with only the ring lights on. The WFW world title belt rests at the center, alone.))
MANSON: Tell me something, Iris, with all your apparent superior wisdom... Is it murder if you kill a midget? Now given your height and weight, I could just as easily call you a woman, but since you're obviously wrestling for the coveted WFW Women's World Title against Lady Whoradora, I must assume you're male. With your size, you expect me to be really, really impressed because you tried really, really hard and you won't give up, not ever?
You see, I try my hardest as well, and that's made me the WFW World Heavyweight Champion. You, Iris, have won less than a handful of matches and have gained a title shot only because the show is being held in Mexico. Did you have your spine shattered? Did fire sear your flesh? Did entire gangs attack you with blades?
Have you ever felt real pain in your life? Of course not. You could hardly go around claiming to be happy if you did. But that will change, extremely fast. You're not that much different from a soldier, Iris, a fresh recruit that is. Someone fighting for a cause that doesn't really exist and is just an excuse for people high-up than him to wage war because they like to wage war. And then, the recruit is shot, or he sees his friend exploding into parts, or he's taken and tortured.
I'll let you ponder which option I have in mind for you. Because if you are so intent on making me happy, on making the WFW fans happy, then you'll comply with my latest project. And that is to take you and make a real man out of you. No matter what it takes.
Do you know that men normally faint when bloody bandages are pulled off their bloodied legs?
That's barely anything, Iris. I've been breaking bodies and spirits since you were drinking rum backstage as a teenager with your father or uncle or whoever broke you into this industry as is the Mexican tradition. I've broken angels, Iris. Far better and harder men than you.
In one night, I even have two falls to do it. You're not going to impress me, Iris. You're not going to earn my respect. My standard for these things is myself, and until you've crawled bruised and beaten out of a urine-stained hospital bed because you cannot allow other wrestlers to go on presuming that they are greater than you, until you have risen nigh from the dead to conquer a promotion, you will never be within my circle.
A circle of one, a circle of fire. You only say your spirit can't be broken. But it's mine that is resistant, mine that always survives. My body breaks, time and circumstance can conspire against me, but my will is always strong.
Yours is weak. I might be able to put some steel in you though, I've done worse. But first, first...you have to know the meaning of tragedy. Not to others, no, to yourself.
You have to be selfish like that first. And you will be. For this world title, the finest heavyweight title in the world today, you will be.
Not to mention, you'll have to be selfish in more than one way to protect yourself...because this title belt that I earned by never letting my spirit break....I intend to polish it in your blood.
