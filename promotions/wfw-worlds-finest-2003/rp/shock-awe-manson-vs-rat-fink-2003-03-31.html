Shock & Awe: Manson vs. Rat Fink

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 31 March 2003 North American Title Defense Boot Camp Match Michael Manson (c) vs. Rat Fink Cut your promos here! Manson — 1 April 2003 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON in a plain black t-shirt and jeans sits on a bench with only a bulb above acting as a light source. Hanging from a cord on a hook is the North American title.))
MANSON: Now a lesser being might feel somewhat proud. After all, I'm from North America, I'm a champion and main eventing the next show and yes, I didn't even lose. But I didn't win either.
You see, I wanted to turn the wheel full circle on my career. Because we can only advance by laying waste to the past. But in this, I failed. Maybe I could have done something different. Perhaps the oucome would have been different. Reviewing the footage, I considered disciplining myself with 400 lashes or figuring a way to pull auto-crucifixion. But in the end, that would achieve nothing and I am not nothing when all I need to go on is in front of me.
So, I'll take the North American title for now. And I will accept the outcome for now. I will have what I want even if I hav to crucify this entire promotion like Roman slaves. Doc Silver did nothing in that match to win, only be chance did he. And nothing will come of nothing because I'll take this title and through the blood and the displaced punishment, I'll make this a title that people want for the clam of beating me...like I wanted with Silver. But since I did not receive what I wante..neither will anyone else....since now...every opponent will Doc Silver in spirit and subject to torture that should reserved for an Iraq soldier tied to a chair in a dark room. I'll do things so horrible people will want a match with Silver not for is title, but because they do not want to sep into my domain. And I'm done, Doc Silver will be like King Lear...a king for now..but eventually transformed into a mad beggar....he started with nothing and so shall he be reduced to it.
But that's in time, I can wait. I waited years, why not alittle longer? But first..first is Rat Fink..the realist supervillain. Who could not win the rumble...so he interjects himself it my circumstance no doubt in what he would describe as a plan to rise up in WFW and build his robot army or whatnot. But now he has coflicted with my own agenda..I do not care for his plans..his labor....or his robots.
He interfered on something that was mine and as the injured party, my rights are paramount. Do you want to be a villain? A realistic one at that? For years, there were people who called me the Devil..through misunderstanding..admiration....fear....and whatnot. As far as villains go, I succeeded. In the raal world, villains really can win. In fact, it can be argued that we are all villains.
But there can be only one devil. And if wrestling is hell,then I'm him. I earned that name too.....and now you want to step forward..you want to plan..to interfere..whatever the specifics are..you obey the highest principle..you want to rise as high as possible. And that brings you against me.
And now, like most villains when they are caught, are punished. It's not karma. It's because I'm angry with myself. Because I intend on seeing Silver in your eyes. It's because want to see if I build this title up to something. It's because you chose this for yourself, because you put your own head in the lion's mouth. And really, when you are bloody, screaming, trying to remember that speech aout your innae superiority..you'll look up at me....the North American campion you created...and you'll realize that I ultimately do these things to you because I can.
ChrisHorowitz — 6 April 2003 (Cue up "N.W.O." by Ministry) (Fade into the secret underground bunker of Rat Fink. Cut to the head of the table, where Rat Fink sits surrounded by officials of the Saddam Hussein regime, including the kickin' rad Information Minister.)
Rat Fink: Here I sit surrounded by some of the men responsible for so much human suffering in the last 20 years. Michael Manson, you can claim to be devoted to the cause of worldwide evil, pain and suffering, but my friend you cannot compare to the pure evil.
So if you are indeed the devil, you have set the bar pretty low for the rest of us. You may indeed prey on the weak, you may injure them, and you may take pleasure in it. But Manson, you are simply one man who does not have the ability to crush a people under your hand. You are not the devil, for the devil could do much better for himself than stretching the neck of some worthless journeyman.
Especially sad is that this is a devil who only could rise as high as a secondary title, he brags how I am not worthy due to a performence in a battle royale, yet you the champion of the consilation prize thinks he is above me? The man who claims to be the epitomy of evil, yet in comparison to myself and my associates his deeds amount to that of stealing candy from a baby!?
This is quite the annoyance. Sure the man who insults me has accomplished much in his career as a wrestler, but he is also a man who can only look to wrestling for his glory. Manson, as a being of evil, you must yourself admit you aren't living up to the standards set by previous evil men by simply committing vaguely despicable acts on American television, the cesspool of mediocrity. Except for that 24 show, Kiefer Sutherland sometimes makes me wonder if my terrorist ambitions are worth it.
Continue accepting your place below Silver Manson, continue to accept being inferior to your opponents, and assuming that at some point they will fall. Sadly you lack the feminine figure of King Lear's daughters, so it might be difficult to overthrow Silver if he is indeed Lear. But you keep telling yourself how you are able to do the work of a girl, it will really impress and frighten all of your opponents.
As a realist I must wonder why you go for such extravagent claims one moment, but yet sink down to the level of a realist in others. At one moment you are the devil, the next you are just able to due the work of a woman at some point in the future. These severe jumps in personality are intriguing, hopefully when it comes time for us to meet in the ring you will suddenly have a wave of defeatism come over you and propell me to an easy victory. But no matter what, Manson, we will show you what true evil, true domination, and true madness are... you will see that you are simply not demonic material.
(Fade out) Manson — 6 April 2003 ((FADEIN: Michael Manson stands in a plain room with posters of Adolph Hitler, Saddam Hussein, Josef Stalin, Anton LaVey, and Michael Jackson.))
MANSON: King Lear only failed because he was old and senile. I reaize that you aren't up there up, Fink, but you are apparently senile if you think anyone will actually believe that you have to do with Iraq. But case in point, you can find many people who would claim Iraq and Saddame on the side of righteousness and apple pie. That is one o thse morally convoluted situations that ultimately mean nothing.
There's a theory that things done out of love are beyond the field of good or evil. It is the intent that matters, as some religions say. And regardless of my intent....I have something the figures around me lack. Weeks and weeks of constant TV coverage and attention for me to influence and mold the mind of anyone watching with my rheoric and subliminal messages. That's my intent and really, I don't care if it is good or evil.
Because Fink, the reason you blatantly put yourself in controversy, why you come after me for no apparent reason, is because you are in my shadow. I'm not second to Silver, I've shown that already. But you, Fink? Your actions speak of someone trying to crawl out of my shadow. After all, you lost the rumble so you had to go and try something, anything for the leverage to get yourself attention. Of course in King Lear, everyone basically dies....because these types of thing never work out well for anyone. Bt I've been through them before, I'll show you.
And pay close attention, because you didn't to my words. I said people claimed I was the devil and went with that..in the bible...the carpenter never claimed anything either. The reason being is that anyone good, evil, ugly, diabolical...and whatnot would never need to go around saying it like you do Fink...No...they just are.
Aleister Crowley never ruled a country...wasn't arrested.....and lived out quite an interesting life..and there are those who said he was evil. He was just living his life. It's all relative. There's no sliding cosmic scale for these things because mostly everyone gets screwed over evetually.
Nihilism can be a truly wonder thing..it inspires novels like Grendel and movies like the Never-Ending Story....and because if everything amounts to nothing..then we apply our own meaning to things. The meaning I applied for myself was to finally beat Doc Silver...I don't need to prove I'm better than him or anyone...I just wanted things to have come full circle.
But using your logic Fink, if I'm below Silver, what does that make you hanging around an imaginary version of the cave and toliet Saddam is currently squatting in? Why would someone want to be associated with such a failure anyway? Did he conquer the world? Does he have respect or fear? No, like you, Fink, he just wants attention.
But hold onto your role model as long a you can, we do have to make our own meaning in this world. And now, mine is to the end I first wanted..it will just take longer...but it's all about the journey right? And as much as I'e enjoyed this philosophic discourse with you, I'll leave the rest of my motivations to be derived from actions.
They say the greatest trick the devil ever pulled was making the world think he didn't exist...maybe that's because he realized almost everyone who claims to be outright evil....loses...
((Manson produces a lighter from his pocket and sets the posters on fire starting with Michael Jackson.))
Or maybe....just maybe..he hoped everyone could figure it out for themselves.
