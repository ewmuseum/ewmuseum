WFW Ghoulish Games: Pulsar vs. Mike McGee

21 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 21 November 2004 This match is for Pulsar's right to face Mighty Imapala at the Turkey Day Terror PPV. The RP deadline is Friday, December 3rd 11:59 PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld] .
Paul stuclif — 22 November 2004 FADE IN: PULSAR IS SEEN SIGNING PAPER HANDED TO HIM BY A POLICE OFFICER. PRISON CELLS ARE SEEN ADJASCENT TO THE DESK WHERE PULSAR IS STANDING.
OFFICER: Your bail has been posted (PULSAR STARES DOWN THE OFFICER FOR A MOMENT, BEFORE FINISHING HIS PAPERWORK AND THEN TURNS TO THE CAMERA)
PULSAR: Thanks man. PC: No pro... PULSAR: Lets get outta here, I gotta figure out how the f**k to get back home. (TURNS AND BRISKLY WALKS AWAY FROM THE CAMERA,TO THE DOOR) PC: Well, I dont know how to get you out of the country, but I do know how you get to Impala.
(PULSAR STOPS DEAD IN HIS TRACKS, DOESNT TURN AROUND) PC: While you were lock... PULSAR: (TURNS HIS HEAD HALF WAY ROUND, SPEAKS VERY LOW) How do I get to Impala? PC: If yo... you beat Mike McGee Ghoulish Games you'll face the Mighty Impala at Turkey Day Terror.
(PULSAR'S HEAD DROPS INTO HIS RIGHT HAND AND STARTS RUBBING HIS FACE) PULSAR: McGee... at Ghoulish Games. (TURNS AROUND HAND NOW RUBBING HIS FOREHEAD, VOICE STILL LOW BUT MORE MENACING) For me to get my hands on the bloke thats basically holding my sister as a slave, I have to go through one of his lackies... (NOW YELLS) Does management want f**king blood on their hands. Cause I am going to tear this son-of-a-b**ch apart.
PC: What are you planning to do? (PULSAR DOESNT SAY A WORD, JUST BREATHES DEEPLY) PC: Pulsar? PULSAR: You know what, since the WFW carries on the BAD tradition, my time here has been filled with horrific injuries. I've been bashed with differeent weapons, thrown overboard from a boat, and just basically beaten from post to post... but the worst shot I've ever taken was a massive gut wrench suplex from the apron onto a heap of chairs, a devastating move.. and do you know where I got that.
PC: I think that was before me. PULSAR: It was an event called Mind Over Matter, a three way dance between myself, a try hard cowboy named Good Eddie, havent seen him since by the way, and the guy who delivered the suplex... Mike McGee. Till now I have always considered the move just opportunistic, but now here is the same guy getting involved of what will become a brutal conflict... and he is lining up for the wrong side.
McGee, your my way to Impala and saving my family... I already had a reason to hate you, what do you think I'm going to do to you know... Cue the damn excitement!
