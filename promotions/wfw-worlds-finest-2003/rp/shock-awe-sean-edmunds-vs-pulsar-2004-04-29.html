Shock & Awe: Sean Edmunds vs. Pulsar

29 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

stuclif — 30 April 2004 FADE IN: PULSAR IS WALKING OUT OF THE LOCAL HOSPITAL, LIMPING AND HOLDING IS LEFT ARM CLOSE TO HIS BODY.
PULSAR: (SLOWLY) I've seen hell... and it looks like a bathroom floor, covered in glass (SMALL SMILE). It shouldve been all worth it but one thing let me down.. or should I say one belief let me down. A belief that many people put faith in, and before last night, I was one of those people, but no more.
(HOLDS A CLENCHED FIST UP TO THE CAMERA) PULSAR: (EYES ON THE CLENCHED FIST) How does it slip out, it shouldnt (ROTATES HIS WRIST AND LOOKS CLOSER). Theres no gaps, no holes... its a strong, flawless grasp, and yet it cannot hold that which invites itself into its clenches in the first place. (EYES UP TO THE CAMERA) I'm not talking about the BAD World title, although, that which evades should have also bought me that strap... I'm talking about momentum. You put together a string of strong victories, peoples expectations become higher, momentum swings in your direction.... then you are first to be thrown out of the So Cal Rumble.. momentum stops dead. Being able then to pick yourself up out of the gutter makes you stronger, so your ready to compete again, to try and put yourself on the path that you were unable to stay on. So, what comes next? No, not more failure, but a pinfall on the then number one contender, the now World Champion, Michael Manson. Back comes the momentum, the confidence.. the belief of being able to walk into the Superbowl of Wrestling, kick some serious arse, and walk out with a new belt over your shoulder.... Didnt happen.. instead.. I was powerbombed onto shards of broken glass (FACE SCREWS UP IN PAIN) and again, first to be eliminated out of contention.
Well, its time for a new train of thought, the whole momentum theory can be thrown to the sh##. The match that we have just passed can be learnt from... and nothing more. I wont be drawing strength, adrenaline or any sort of push from useless history. (PUTS HIS HANDS UP) Hey, dont get me wrong, if you piss me off, I wont forget it and I will kick your arse, but other than that, my attention will be solely on the match in front of me.
Now after last night I believe that there is another who must consider this new theory about momentum being a load of sh##. Sean Edmunds, congratulations by the way, last night you snapped a.. what was it.. 86 to 0 losing streak. A victory, if momentum really was something more than just an ego boost in peoples minds, would never have happened now, would it? Of course not.
Edmunds, even though you should be a follower of the new faith.. so to speak, there is still some bad news for you. This whole new belief system will come into practice for the first time against you.. at Shock and Awe. You will stand across the ring from me and I will be completely focused on beating you down, with no nagging memories of previous experiences in the background... it will be a "holy experience". Now even though some things change, somethings always remain the same..so .. cue the excitement!
TheOriginalSE — 4 May 2004 ((FADEIN: A news telecast. A lady is sitting in front of the camera, as a picture of an elegant older lady is shown in the upper right hand corner.))
NEWSCASTER: "And sad news this morning as Marie Laura LaRoque, CEO and President of EC Enterprises, passed away this morning in her condo in Boston, Massachusetts surrounded by family and friends. LaRoque had been suffering from cancer of the esophogous for almost a year and had recently ended her chemotherapy treatments. LaRoque is probably best known for being 'the' General Electric model of the 50s. She recently took over EC Enterprises, which deals in a variety of business operations, from her deceased husband ten years ago. LaRoque is bereaved by her six children, and a multitude of grandchildren, the most famous of whom is Jean-Michel LaRoque, known to wrestling fans as World's Finest Wrestling's 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds and his brother, owner of wrestling promotion New ERA of Wrestling, Marquis (Marcus) LaRoque. No funeral arrangements have been made public at this time ...."
(CUTTO: To a blackness. The sun starts to rise from the east, as we are shown some drizzling rain dropping from the Heavens. CUTTO: 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds and Sensational Sherri are both standing at the door of a high rise condominium complex in Boston. Sherri's mascara is running as her eyes are still teary, and Edmunds looks somber.)
EDMUNDS: "Time. You always think that you have such a tight grasp on it. Like your momentum, Pulsar. It slipped through your hands .. because no matter how hard you push, no matter how numb your fingers get, everything passes through. But time is much more important to a person that momentum. I have always tried to justify my obvious shortcomings in life, with the drugs, the booze, everything, by saying that I am here for a good time, not a long time. It is because time, unlike anything else in the world, is the most valuable possession we have. And it is also the possession that we have the least amount of control over. I have learned this first hand today, Pulsar. As I stood at the foot of my grandmother's bed and watched the last breaths of life escape through her teeth. You're preaching to the choir, Pulsar. I know the fallibility of momentum. I am not a follower as you might like to believe. I've been around for far too long to fall victim to any type of witchery or superstition. After all, if it were momentum which dictates how well I do in the ring, then I would not have beaten Jean Rabesque at Superbowl of Wrestling, and if it were momentum that decides my matches, then I guess I am pretty much screwed at Shock and Awe. But you see, I've transcended those concepts years ago. But one concept was there that I always pushed to the back burner. And that was time."
(Sherri brings the hankerchief to her nose, finally she walks back inside.) EDMUNDS: "I turn thirty in four days, Pulsar. I'm not as motivated as I was years ago. I cannot pull double, triple duty in two or three leagues like I was when I was 19, 20, 21. I think, if I learn anything from all of this, its that I need to enjoy my time left in *professional wrestling as much as I can. And that I need to fulfill as many as my goals as I can. Let's face it, some guys can last longer and longer. Jean Rabesque, for example, is over 10 years older than I am. But when was the last time you saw the Rabesque of the early-mid 90's? This is where you come in Pulsar. For some reason, which remains inexplicable to me at this moment, but then again, I can't really focus too much, but the powers that be booked me in a match with you ... after I had just finished the biggest victory of mine in the past 5 years. I'm sure that this has something to do with our EX-president Alex Wylde ... because it wreaks of his dirt. But I promise you this ... as WFW President, no one will be safe. Not you, not Copycat, not Manson ... not Anarky. Because I am in control now. And just like I found out last night, this control is in place for a limited time."
(Edmunds opens up a black umbrella as Sherri comes out of the door.) SHERRI: (in between sobs) "It .... is ..... Friday." EDMUNDS: (looks down at the wet ground as he and Sherri step underneath the umbrella.) "I'm sorry that I couldn't explain more about how little time you have left, Pulsar ... but as you can see things do not always work out as well as they are supposed to. I should be in Raleigh right now ... instead I'm here. But you can guaran-damn-well-tee that I will be at Shock and Awe. Momentum blinds people .... but time .... time controls them."
[i] (FADEOUT: As Edmunds and Sherri walk off into the rain, towards their car. FTB.) stuclif — 12 May 2004 FADE IN: A DARK ROOM, PULSAR STANDING IN THE MIDDLE OF SHOT, LIT UP BY A SINGLE SPOT LIGHT. WEARING A BLACK SUIT AND TIE.
PULSAR: Edmunds... my deepest condolences to you and yours. A loss such as the one your experiencing is devastating and for you to honor your commitments, with the WFW, is a great way to work through such a hard time.
(ROOM NOW FULLY LIGHTS UP, THE WALL COVERED IN A 'SHOCK AND AWE' EMBLEM) PULSAR: Thats it... without meaning to sound heartless, but that is all the sympathy that, you will get from me... although that wont bother you to much. If you need more time to grieve, Shock & Awe will not be the place to do it. You see, despite being a sad time in your life, you still managed to pi** me off. What is it? Are you too good to be in the same ring as me. Why would a Pulsar v Edmunds match wreak of anyones dirt. Well, f**k you OK... I dont care want you think about me now... after Shock and Awe, I will dictate your head, and I will dictate the way you think and feel about me.
You have recently been elected as the new President of the WFW and I have no worries about telling you that you got to that position without my vote. I have absolutely no faith in your ability to lead a company. I also believe that your leadership skills, or lack thereof, are mirrored by your in-ring skills... a fact that I will be definately exploiting come our match. Yeah, I know that you are a former WFW Heavyweight Champion, and I also know that the first word in that title must sting every time you hear it... former... in other words, preceeding, prior, used to be, not any more and so on. And that is the reason why I am going to claim another victory the next show. You reach a mass high and only sustain such a high for a very short amount of time, only to come crashing down proves to me that you and large amounts of pressure do not mix well.. do they? And if you think that beating a 40 something year old shows an ability to sustain pressure, you should think again.
Another issue that is to come from our match is the so-called reward for the winner of the match. Edmunds, if you win you get to settle a feud Copycat.... a feud that I could care less about. If I was to win, I will fight who ever I choose... and believe me I list, although it hasnt been cut down to just one yet. Jared Wells, Iron Lion, Luster are just a few of the the 'finalists' that I would love to get another crack at... just one more to settle a few scores.
Despite everything that has happened, one thing remains the same since the last time I spoke to everyone... the new mind set. It will also go well with the fact the Edmunds has sh** me up the wall. The fans are gonna love it...... cue the excitement.
TheOriginalSE — 12 May 2004 (FADEIN: WFW Headquarters. 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds is directing movers as they take out a desk, a chair, some lamps.)
EDMUNDS: "Yes, dump it all. This is all worthless. For all the things in the world, Wylde didn't have any style."
(The moves take a drawer out. It shows a bunch of nudy magazines.) EDMUNDS: "Uh, I'll keep those.." (Sherri comes around the corner, wearing a women's business suit. She rips the magazines out of the hand of Edmunds.)
SHERRI: "Oh no you won't!" EDMUNDS: "Hey there. You're looking fine as always, my love." SHERRI: "Stop buttering me up after that one! We got a lot of work to do! We have to finish moving this **** out, then go over some strategies for Shock and Awe. You saw Pulsar's latest little bit, I presume."
EDMUNDS: "Of course I did. As President of the WFW I make sure that each and every piece of television released is to my liking."
SHERRI: "And you let that go?" EDMUNDS: "Of course I did. Pulsar is a good comedian. He makes me giggle." SHERRI: "Giggle?" EDMUNDS: "I always wanted to use that word .." SHERRI: "Ok." EDMUNDS: "You see, Pulsar is a misguided man. He seems to think that he is going to use me as a stepping stone here in the WFW. To think!"
SHERRI: "He doesn't seem very impressed." (Edmunds turns to the camera.) EDMUNDS: "Pulsar. Let me let you in on a little secret. Although you may believe deep down in your heart that you have the slightest chance against me at Shock and Awe, the fact of the matter is that you are way out of your league. I'm only telling you this because I feel sorry for you. You have had a tough go here in the WFW. Even you realize this. So you're doing what many before you have tried to do. My coattails hang long, but they're tough to ride. Continue to build up your vocal threats. Throw barbs at me as much as you can, my friend. The only salvation you'll have after Shock and Awe are the promo tapes that you've recorded."
(The movers take a picture of Wylde pinning Edmunds for the WFW World title.) EDMUNDS: "Hey! Give me that!" (Edmunds rips it up and then tosses it at the mover.) EDMUNDS: "Much better." (The mover looks at Edmunds and shakes his head.) EDMUNDS: "For months upon months I wrestled match after match, making my way up the ladder to get to the World Title. I pushed my body to its limit and it failed like I knew it eventually would. But I'm sure you don't care to hear this. And now you're wondering why I am telling you this. It's simple. I was in a tough stretch. Losing the World Title. Then losing the tag match. Then losing the one person in my life that stuck by me through all of my problems. So what does the WFW do? Before I become WFW President they stick me in a match that they knew I would win hands down. You can't have the President losing in his first match on the job, can you?"
SHERRI: "Hell no!" EDMUNDS: "Uh, thanks Sherri.. but I was talking to Pulsar." SHERRI: "I know. But I haven't said anything in a while." EDMUNDS: "Did you get it out of your system?" SHERRI: "Yea, I think I'm set for now." EDMUNDS: "I'm not going to waste much more of my time speaking to you, Pulsar .. because why I am off battling Copycat, why I am off on my quest to get back the World title and to wrangle it from the hands of that good-for-nothing backstabbing-on-FW-Survivor Manson, while I am out there making the WFW a better place ... you are content with your Lusters .. your Richard Gideons .. and God help us all, your Jared Wells. Enjoy this chance at trying to show the world what you're made of, because after Shock and Awe, after I bring you up a level, you'll be stuck lost in the shuffle for the rest of your career here. I'll make sure of it."
(Edmunds steps into the office, which is now empty. He looks back at the camera.) EDMUNDS: "Don't adjust your television set .... I AM this sensational!" (FADEOUT: As Edmunds rips Alex Wylde's name off the door and tosses it into the trash.)
