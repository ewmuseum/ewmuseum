JLevinson — 18 June 2004 (CUEUP: "Weak and Powerless" by Perfect Circle.) (FADEIN to an empty ring in an empty gym... Anarky is walking slowly in circles around the center of the ring... he's wearing jeans and a black sleeveless t-shirt with "LOVE" on the front and "WHORE" on the back.)
ANARKY: "Running around in circles... endlessly looping through the meaningless tides of my life... and I'm supposed to care, about the animals, about the poor, about the sick and hungry and tired... about the plight of Larry Tact and Copycat and their endless quests for concepts they cannot even comprehend... about even myself... my own stagnation... my own suffering.
"And yet... even this... even this tiny thing... that I seem to be able to wrap my mind around... all of it... utterly devoid of meaning or interpretation."
(He stops.) ANARKY: "I have no past, Larry. No legacy or honor or respect to give you. I have no false gold around my waist... no reputation to uphold... nothing to gain or lose... except, perhaps, vengeance...
"I have not forgotten, Larry. Quite the opposite... I remember full well what happened the last time in the ring...
"Perhaps, Larry, five years ago... I would've destroyed you for it. I would've hunted you down like a dog and made your life a Hell that you only vaguely understand. A Hell that Copycat knows all about.
"Because that's what whores deserve, Larry. Now that LOVE is here.. .and LOVE is here to stay... whores will always get what whores deserve. Like me and you. Is this a crossroads, Larry, or just another fight in an endless sequence of fights?
"Where was Felix? Where was Jared or Psycho? Backstage, watching as I was blindsided by yet another masked man, split open like a watermelon to bleed on the cold concrete... ?"
(He puts his arms out in the traditional martyr position and tilts his head to the right... )
ANARKY: "All in a day's work, Larry.. Something I'd expect you to understand. Because as I suffer, you suffer. As I stagnate, you stagnate. Perhaps this connects us, Larry. Perhaps this means something."
(He lowers his arms and spits on the ground.) ANARKY: "You know what *I* think, Larry? I think it's bullsh*t. I think all of this... this f*cking joke... I think it needs to be wiped out. And I think some part of you agrees with me. I think you know full well the anema that this place needs...
"I've got your halo right here... you wanna be a martyr, boy? You wanna be a hero? I'll make you a f*cking hero, Larry. I'll turn you into motherf*cking Jesus Christ.
"And y'know what, Larry? When I strike you down like the Lord would a sinner... when I break you and leave you like some dumb tramp...
"... you oughtta thank me. You oughtta get down on your knees and thank me. Because you need me... without me... without something to fight... you're nothing. I am the anti... the omega... the end...
"And if I'm the end, Larry... than this must be the beginning. No past, Larry. No future. Just right now. Waiting for you, Larry... waiting for you to open your eyes... and kill yourself now.
"Because you're already dead. Every motherf*cking last one of you. But don't worry, Larry...
"... you'll learn. They always do." (FADEOUT.) Starbreaker — 22 June 2004 Fade in: to the surroundings of an apartment. Furnishing the main room is an entertainment system, mini bar with a few stools in front of it, kitchenette, sofas, cocktail table...and one man. Larry Tact sits on one of the plush burgundy sofas, drinking from a glass filled with a blue-gray drink. Tact has on a pair of blue workout pants and a grey sleeveless shirt. He looks down at the cocktail table, swirling around his drink as the misty liquid circles the inside of the glass. Tact sets the glass down on the cocktail table after another sip. He looks up at the camera, his expression one of apathy, impassiveness. Tact gives a short grunt.
Tact: Anarky. What has it been around...two years? Counting WFW, two promotions later. I remember the last time we faced off in the ring, too. But times change, isn't that what they say? And if they say it, then it must be true, right?
Tact gives a brief, lopsided grin. Tact: Well, we both know the answer to that. Of course not. It doesn't matter what ANYONE says, does it? In both of our cases, it seems ACTIONS are our preferred manner of getting the agenda across. So I must agree, we do have something to level on. Maybe more than meets the eye, in fact. I can agree with you that I was stagnating. I was suffering. But now, as we finally are to meet again in the ring....after hearing what you had to say...I have to question my own purpose in this match.
Tact leans back on the sofa cushions, loosely crossing his arms over his chest. Tact: I mean, what can I say? You're right. I'm just a dying breed, aren't I. A wrestler in a sea of trash. Nothing's changed in the past year, has it. Wrestling is still being plagued. Just look around. Michael Manson is the "World's Finest Wrestler?" Psycho is a Champion? Jared Wells is a Champion? Golem's back? And so is Mike Lane? Yeah...wrestling is getting a lot better these days. I can tell.
Tact slaps his hands on his knees and slowly rises to his feet. Tact: So if we agree on this central issue...why are we facing each other? Is this Edmunds' way of rewarding you, Anarky? By having you face someone that isn't against your cause? Or has the world forgotten MY cause? MY values? MY LOVE for this sport?
Has everyone been blinded to the fact that Larry Tact...would be a good addition to the Liars....Oppressors....and Violence Experiment?
Or is everyone just hoping that it doesn't happen? What was it you said I should do, Anarky? Get down on my knees and thank you? Is that all I need to do to join the ranks of L.O.V.E.? I only need to do one simple little act? Well....
Tact pauses....then smirks, grabs his crotch and makes a flip off motion. Tact: ...there's a simple motion for you. Cuz in all honesty, Anarky....I think you're the one who needs to learn. I don't need to keep living in the past. I don't see the point of the continous harping on the mistakes of wrestling. I acknowledge the past, and I can look back on it with a little more clarity as each day passes. But you know, if there's one other thing my last hiatus taught me, it's that I can't change the world because I say so. And neither can you, Anarky. And neither can L.O.V.E. Just because you want to conduct an "experiment" doesn't mean anyone has to think anything of it. I personally have acknowledged what L.O.V.E. has been doing, but I've got more important things to do than watch others be all they can be. I've got my own goals, my own effort to expend. And nobody's going to keep me from pushing to the front of the line.
Tact sits back down on the couch, taking another drink from the glass. Tact: It's kind of ironic, that you and Manson are at each other's throats, and yet you've both compared me to figures of "good" and ''evil.'' I'm surely not the first to have such a thing happen, but even so. I suppose it shows that some things...some people...some characteristics, don't change. People still feel the need to press across their beliefs onto others without a second thought. I don't bother, anymore. I don't CARE whether anyone follows my "plight," Anarky. I don't CARE if you believe yourself to be linked to me, somehow. You aren't the center of the world, and I don't revolve around anyone. I have my own agenda, and you're in the way.
Tact gets up with the glass in hand, swirls it around another couple times as he walks towards the mini bar. He finishes the drink and places it on the counter, pulling out a bottle of the same drink, and pouring his glass full again. Tact takes a seat on one of the beige stools in front of the mini bar and takes a drink from the glass, setting it onto the counter, and looking with disdain.
Tact: You can say what you want, Anarky. I'll acknowledge your words as I do everyone else's. But you aren't special to me, not in any way. You're just like everyone else-- an opponent who I will give my best effort against. An opponent that I don't anticipate will stop for me....and so one that I will have to stop, so I can move on with my own mission. If you remember our last encounter, then you have some past, Anarky. You at least know I HAVE changed. I'm not concerning myself with anyone else's affairs, any longer. It's my own satisfaction that I've set out to find. And even if I HAVE suffered, or stagnated at any point...I'm not letting that keep me from moving forward, and achieving what I know I can. I'm not satisfied with doing that, so I won't stop until I AM satisfied.
Tact pauses and tilts his head downward, some strands of his colored blonde hair falling towards his face. Tact sniffs and looks back up, a small, lopsided grin etched his face.
Tact: You know, though, maybe I do owe you a thanks, Anarky. Cuz if there's one thing you HAVE shown me, with L.O.V.E. and what you say, it's that I AM on the right track. I'm more satisfied walking the humble road, and doing things the way I want to, then walking the trail of vengeance...or violence...or apathy. I'm better off having laid my past to rest, and focusing on the present. Cuz Anarky? There's a New Era coming to wrestling. New blood...new Champions rising up...new legacies being written. You may let it all pass you by, while you try to spearhead a purge that may never happen. Me? I'm going to forge a fresh path for wrestling, that the New Era can follow. A path that shows better days ahead for this industry. At Pandemonium, when both our causes clash, we'll see if either is worth it....
Tact picks up his glass and holds it up as if toasting. Tact: ...or if we're both better off just killing each other. Tact focuses back on the glass, continuing to drink from it. Fade out. Starbreaker — 26 June 2004 Fade in: on a shot of the sun setting over the valleys of San Diego, California. Then, switch to a shot of the Golden Gate bridge, where cars stream across it. Next a shot of the red, orange, and yellow sky, as the sun nears its refuge for the night, behind some clouds. The warm air is unmoving, the clouds sit in place, and everything seems to be in a dazed movement. The camera cuts to a shot of San Diego's Mission Bay. A few white sailboats course along the aquamarine water, which smoothly flows over the sand and rocks on the shore's edge. Seated on one rock, looking over the water to some cityscape in the far distance, dressed in sandblasted blue jeans and a green, ribbed t-shirt...is Larry Tact. Tact is caught from a side profile, his olive green eyes looking out to the water, his colored blonde hair neatly combed back and in place, the calm air not moving even a strand. Tact looks solemn, keeping his forearms bent over his knees.
Tact: Nobody cares. The question is, Anarky....should I feel hurt? Any different than how I feel right now? I've said it before and I'll keep on saying it. Respect is (BLEEP) in wrestling. There's no such thing. You got guys like Copycat...on a quest in search of their "due respect," when the fact is, there's no respect to be sought out. No quest to go on. The only thing people who expect respect in this industry are doing is giving themselves a distraction. A question with no answer. The fact is...he's still in the dark about the fundamentals. After all this time, he STILL doesn't get it. And he expects pulling stunts that interfere with other people's affairs -- and matches -- will somehow fix that? (snorts) No wonder Copycat still hasn't gotten a title shot.
Tact stretches out one leg, letting it bend over the rock. Tact: You understand the way this industry runs, Anarky. You've been around the block. And yet, you still manage to miss that I understand, too. I'm not about to give a thought to gaining respect and showing how honorable I can be. I'm here to do what I want to do, and I'll find the means to do it, no matter what they are. I haven't needed to use dishonorable methods to get things done, but hey, look back and you'll see it's not beyond me. (smirks) Your L.O.V.E. buddy Jared Wells will tell you that. So will Jared Justice, if he hasn't lost his mind completely yet. I know, Anarky...it IS survival of the fittest. But the fittest aren't always obvious to the eye. What defines the fittest? One who merely survives? Or is there more to it? For someone who denounces mercy, valor, and passion as myths, Anarky...you've certainly bought into a concept that's just as much of a myth. Survival of the fittest...but I'll tell you this. I don't care what anyone thinks that really means, cuz I've etched my own meaning, and it's worked out just fine for me. I've survived this far. There's no reason for me to turn my back on it now.
Tact stops and gazes out to the water. Some seagulls fly out to sea, flapping their wings tirelessly, making it look effortless.
Tact: Missions are for the strong willed, Anarky. Of course dreams will die, just as new ones will come along. I don't deny change, I accept it and adapt. Just as in the ring, I adapt to the situations presented to me. I don't look for a place to hide. I don't look for a way out or around. I face up whoever or whatever comes my way. And Anarky...I know how to make sacrifices. I don't live in some perfect world, where all my plans go just as I want. I don't live a dream, I strive for it. I don't have a mission because I'm bored. I have it because I believe.
Tact's expression toughens. Tact: It's too bad...you've seemed to lost your will to believe, Anarky. You go with this experiment, yet you haven't gained a SINGLE THING from it. You've wasted your time, Anarky. You're not putting L.O.V.E. to good use, I'd say. Doubt me, go ahead. But look what L.O.V.E. has brought you...beatings and the lucky job of spectator. You get to watch the rest of them -- Red, Psycho, Wells -- rise to higher achievement, while you're simultaneously stuck in the fire, taking the brunt of everyone's shots at L.O.V.E. And the fact is...you put yourself there. You put yourself in that position. Cuz you don't believe in what you're doing anymore. I don't have to be a psychic to tell you that. If you believed, then your win over Maelstrom would have been more. You would have made it into more. Instead, what? You're telling me that beating Maelstrom, something you've waited for how many years, didn't spark you? You must be on some HEAVY stuff, Anarky, for that to happen. Pitiful...no. I won't pity. It's just pathetic.
Two seagulls can be seen flying low over one sailboat. Soon after, a couple of guys on board start trying to get them away, swinging objects at them.
Tact: You leave some trail of (BLEEP), Anarky? That's your contribution? I won't disagree. You're everything someone would hate about themselves? Sure, that could be. I know I wouldn't want to see others who I associate with, who don't do anymore than I, get to shine first. But that's just me, Anarky. Cuz I AM all about me. I don't care if Copycat wants respect. I don't care if the WFW gives me no respect or all the respect, even some that I might not deserve. It makes no difference if I can't be satisfied with my performance, my game. I don't care if I'm opening shows or holding a World Heavyweight Championship. If I'm not satisfied, then I'm not going to stop striving. My mission won't end. But that's fine, cuz I need something to keep me on edge, don't I. I feed off of the mission. I think you did once, too, Anarky. But you've been fazed out...lost in your own haze of conception and overshadowed by your own vision. Yeah, you've got one, just like everyone else. Whether you wish to show it or not is a different story. But don't think I'm about to stop walking MY path, molding MY legacy, cuz you say it's senseless. I think I can find my own way, Anarky. Cuz I wouldn't be satisfied any other way.
Tact chuckles and turns his head to look squarely at the lens. Tact: It's funny, that we can agree on anything, much less more than any one thing. After all, we're supposed to be at each other's throats, aren't we? The despised, battle scarred and thrashing member of L.O.V.E., and the oft-portrayed "heroic" technical wizard. I guess half-truths are a funny thing like that, though, heh Anarky? When you bring those into play, then I guess even the "impossible" may be possible. Maybe they'll be screaming blasphemy about me in the locker room, saying how I shouldn't be "making remarks that I don't know about." Maybe they'll question my allegiance towards ending L.O.V.E.? No, they won't, though. They won't because, quite simply, there is no allegiance. If there was, L.O.V.E. wouldn't still be around. But wrestling isn't a team sport. Not in this current world. It's a dying concept, teamwork; a trend that's lost its appeal. Is that why everyone hates L.O.V.E. so much? Cuz they're capable of unifying? Well, I guess maybe WFW could learn something from L.O.V.E., then. But L.O.V.E. can learn something from WFW, too. Something about greed, desire, and maybe even violence. Cuz that's what happens, people learn too much about what they're studying...what they're experimenting on. Then they end up ruining the good thing they had going for them. When that happens though, Anarky...maybe you'll be relieved. Cuz right now, you're just a punching bag, absorbing those blows for the trifecta. Whether you accept it or not...well, I guess that's a question of your will to survive. Do you still believe in what you helped to begin? And more importantly, are you willing to make a sacrifice in the ring against me, at this time? Can you really put your belief, your will on the line against mine, and expect I won't keep on at what the rest of WFW has already started?
Tact blinks and glances out to the ocean for a few moments, then shakes his head and turns back.
Tact: Anarky...when you're wavering in the ring at Pandemonium, in another type of haze, and you wonder about how I could mean what I say...maybe then you'll understand where I'M coming from. Not many do, but perhaps you'll get an idea. See, you may have your imagery and the L.O.V.E., Anarky. But I don't need that. I have my truths, I have my mission...and there's nothing translucent about it. This all plays into why the New Era will pass you by. I will rise above to the next step of my mission. But make no mistake...I won't be doing it for "the good of WFW." No...I'll be doing it for ME. Cuz the New Era IS coming along, and at Pandemonium you're blocking the path, Anarky. So I'm going to come down to the ring, and I'm going to find you, and then...we're going to clash, and see if you can stop the wave of the New Era. But I know, right now, that I'm ready. Ready to wrestle...ready to test my will against ANYONE else's...and ready to leave L.O.V.E.'s dimmed star...all broken up.
A breeze begins to pick up. Tact looks up to the sky, where some clouds are gathering. He gets to his feet, hops off the rock, and walks along the shore, hands in his pockets, the tide rocking back and forth between the ocean and sand. Fade out.
