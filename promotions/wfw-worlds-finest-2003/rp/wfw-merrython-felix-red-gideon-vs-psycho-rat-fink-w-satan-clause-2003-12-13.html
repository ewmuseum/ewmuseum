This match originates from AAA Psicosis/Halloween vs Leon Negro/Ultraman 2000 (No Rope Barbed Wire Cage Street Fight Tornado Death Match - 12/25/95):
An insane match from Tijuana Extreme Lucha. Nasty barbwire bumps by all four. Ultraman gets smashed in the back with a flaming barbwire baseball bat. Psicosis legdrops Leon Negro from the top of the cage through a table to the floor.
The RP/angle deadline for this match is Wednesday, December 24th 11:59 PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]

(Felix Red sits hunched foreword ina leather arm chair in his basement, lights dim, sunglasses and purple dread locks hanging in his face making him even harder to see, no shirt, bandages wrapped around midsection, new white dickie pants, but wait, somebody took a sharpie and wrote "FAKE" across the right tigh and ruined a perfectly good new pair of pants...)
(CUEUP: "Gravity" by A Perfect Circle...) "The boy who destroyed the world..." "the BAD world nonchampion" I have become perfect.... I now owe Toxick my eternal gratitude alongside my disgust. Because of him what could've been a triumph became a sham. A lie. A miscarriage of justice. An abortion of a wrestling match. How was I to function under the banner of the absolute? How could I grasp the concept of being the nonchampion when I had actually won something?
Amidst the havoc I pinned Rat Fink, again, unless something else happened. The possibly has crossed my mind that I may have broken him well before I took his title. He sends others to speak for him. He sends others to fight for him. He simply hasn't the will to do any of this himself anymore. Perhaps it's finally gotten
through to him that he is wrong, was wrong, will continue to be completely off track...His quest for uniformity is a futile endeavor. A fools errand. A complete assimilation, an end to differentiation, is no ending at all. Deux es machina. an Incomplete, forced conclusion. The audience is left unsatisfied. They hunger for more, and they shall stay unappeased until peace is undone. Without dissent, without struggle, there is no life. Which makes Rat Fink dead. as his robots are dead. Perhaps they are undead, animate, but devoid of what makes us human beings. I embrace confusion, life incarnate, he embraces order, the logic of
death. In the end and the beginning, Rat Fink will just crumble and fall before me, like a propped up corpse, suddenly abandoned by it's crutch, left to stand on it's own...
Now how painfully appropriate, that I now find myself here. The warm, glowing center of a vacuum. unmitigated, lawless violence will be drawn to me, desire to possess me, make me it's own. Awful things are going to happen to me. Even worse things are going to happen to anyone who comes near me. Just because
I'm not sorry doesn't mean I don't care. I'm actually very interested in all this. This happened, is only happening, will happen because I was bored, and therefore boring. I am boring. Someday I will be boring...
There's nothing any of you could have done to stop it. None of this could've happened any other way. All of this has already happened.
It's quite possible I've gone and mentioned all this before, but basic logic indicates no truly insane person would ever self apply
the name Psycho, so why would Psycho be Psycho if not in some misguided attempt to intimidate his adversaries? He is misguided, only in that you can not frighten someone with something they don't believe exists, can you? There is no such thing as madness. If there was then I would be mad, and I here I sit quite sane, in total control of my surroundings. There is such a thing as stupidity. Then again, I don't believe in logic either, but just because a given person has no idea what's going on or where he is at any given time, doesn't mean I can't make his eye sockets bleed...
Of course I said I despise Richard Gideon and wish him dead, and of course I attempted to mangle him on several occasions, and it all began when random chance made us allies, and effective allies we were and could be again...What's done is done, already happened, doomed to happen again. To Mr. Gideon, I ask
that bygones become bygones become bygones forever for only a day. I ask that you understand, that at the moment of Fink and Psycho's vanquishing, you will once again become to me like a mucus film festering inside my cheek. Your existence will again become a loathsome thing to me. Understand that I do not
necessarily want this. I simply acknowledge that it's already happened. Perhaps you would hold fate accountable for this vicious circle. In fact, while I have and continue to change, you can't, wont, never will...
I'm paired with Richard Gideon. I'm facing Rat Fink. I'm being tossed from the top of the ring to the pavement to land on the small of my back. I've begun my training for a career in professional wrestling, only to realize my instructor has already spent my tuition on cocaine and whores. I'm 7 years old, listening
to my father explain how the democrats and the mass media are conspiring against him. I'm retiring from pro wrestling, to open an overpriced juice bar in San Diego. I'm 14 years old, and this is my first day of junior high. the end is the beginning. this world is a mirror. it goes on, and on, and on, only to return. To Me. Nothing I've ever seen was anything but an extension of myself. I don't need comfort, I don't need happiness, I don't need misery, I don't need entropy, I don't need violence, I don't need to exist at all....
Without necessities, I can do or have anything I want. You all breathe only because I allow you to. Methos17 — 22 December 2003 (The scene opens to Richard Gideon, covered in blood with the WFW NA Title Belt draped across his lap. He stares down at it and starts to speak)
RG-Well Psycho, you took a heck of a lot out of me. Left me for dead. But you didn't take my belt now did you?
*Gideon places the belt to the left of him on the bench* RG- But that all changes at Merrython. I take a piece of you, with this poor excuse of a partner Felix Red. Listen Red, you beat me, whoopdiedoo. If you're going to spout that Kurt Cobain wannabe crap our entire match I will stab you just for spite. However, if you can keep your lamenting that Sanka is no longer served in its previously delicious incarnation, then perhaps we have an accord.
*Gideon wipes a bit of the blood off of his face and stares blankly at the camera* RG- And don't start with the talk of oblivion and all that. Tis the season Red! Enjoy it! Go out there and put on a hat or something and complain that the snow is pale like your dungeon and dragon loving self! BUT......like I said...we'll get along fine.
As for Rat Fink and Psycho....well you made me bleed like a stuck pig and left me in my own fluids...I'd say you're up one. But I get mine at this hardcore little ppv match. And Fink, don't you dare bring Mr Roboto, or that will be an automiton that does not make it to the new year. Gents, this is Richard Gideon, resident blood donor signing off. See you at the Merrython.
