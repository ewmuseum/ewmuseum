WFW Great Expectations: Manson vs. Terrell Mason

29 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 29 January 2004 The RP/Angle deadline is Wednesday, February 11 11:59 EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
Manson — 31 January 2004 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, in long black leather trenchcoat and pants, standing at a fire's circle, holding hands, gloved but witht he fingers cut off, out, feeling the flames.))
MANSON: So we come to this.....Manson versus Mason in an obvious attempt to create typo's and confusion all across the WFW board. Of course, how I can be confused with a stereotypical black man who might as well be seen in KFC commercials and with a comb in his hair, I don't know. Alas, here we are, in a match I should have a personal incentive for, but I just can't bring myself to care.
Terrell's claim to fame for all of his stay here in the WFW is that he was just one of a group of guys who kept me from winning the title. Now, even a year ago, I likely would have hunted each of these down and exacted bloody, swift retaliation. Now, today, I only am reminded of Francis Bacon...since Terrell at most....was only that...part of a group and now Hart doesn't even have the title anymore.....which makes all seem somewhat pointless.
Or there's the other motive, that yet someone figures the fast track is to come and bother me, to make a name off of me. Like Minion and Rat Fink, and as seen at the last WFW event, perhaps they chose well. I'm sure Mason and whoever else would never come out and actually say any of this....No....during one of his diatribes I was able to make out somehow throught the monkey jabber he passes for English that he wants people to believe that for some reason he was hanging out in his car in the parking lot of an arena while a wrestling event was going on.
If most of the stereotype is to be believed, he was probably meeting his crack dealer. And since I know that neighborhood, I realize that's a far more likely explanation than it sounds. Yet, somehow, the slurred words, ignorant usage of grammar, and poor slang still makes question the good moral fiber of Terrell Mason.
But none of that matters, not anymore, we have a match, whether it's personal or not, and we'll have the match and someone will win. However, Terrell..you won't be making your name off me ...no one will anymore.....I already tired of it.....but I couldn't overcome changes in behavior and momentum.....no..I'm willing to go down..but I'll likely bring you with me. Because this isn't revenge....it's not for a title...it's not some part of my plans.....it's a match.....and I need something to get through this....winning is nice.....but if that is not available...
There's always the alternative. Yo Mamas Pimp — 6 February 2004 (FADE IN: Terrell Mason walks into the camera's view, clad in a blue, silky suit and black alligator shoes with a high-tech cellular phone held to his ear. Draped over his shoulder is the NYWO's World Heavyweight Championship he proudly calls his own since two weeks ago. He finishes finishes up his conversation and flips the cellphone clap off before shoving it down his pocket. As Mason adjusts his smoked sunglasses, he smirks vividly towards the camera.)
MASON: Finally, after a few weeks of well earned rest in this dump, I'm back. To be honest, I ain't really glad either. Since my debut, I haven't been havin' exactly the best luck in the WFW but it can be explained by one thing:
Flukes. But right now, this ain't about all that. This is about you and me Manson. But yeah, you right. We do have similar names, but that's as far as it goes, my nigga. Cuz you see, on the first hand, we have you. A muthaf-ckin' bum lookin' piece of sh-t, walkin' around campfires singin' kum-ba-ya while beggin' for pennies. Yeah, I'm reeeaaallllll scared of that, Manson.
(Mason walks around in a circle imitating a poor gimp with his hands out and a stale face.)
MASON: While on the other hand, we got me. Rich, young, successful, good lookin' and most importantly... more skilled. Truth is, nigga, I am what the haps of the hour. Mason be that nigga gettin' somewhere. And if you think my whole career's highlight is knockin' your ass out on the cold pavement, you flatterin' yourself a lil' too much. I think someone's been eatin' too many trash scraps and it's been goin' to they head. If you seriously be thinkin' there's a whole secret group out there in the world who's only goal is to f-ck your life up, better repass that thought in ya mind, nigga. You might've been some big shot in the past, Michael. But the past is over now. You WERE a champion, you WERE a contender, you WERE a somebody but now...
You ain't NOTHING! (Mason peels the NYWO World Title off his shoulder and shoves it in the camera's lens.) What you been doin' lately, huh? You had a chance to shine, a chance to be champion again - but I stopped it. Since then, you been doin' nothin' but messin' around with no talent dead weights. Until now, muthaf-cka, and I'ma stop your ass a second time, just like before. You gon' be layin' on the ground while I stand over you and laugh. You ain't got what it takes no more, nigga. Give it up, old man. Let's look at the facts.
In the WFW, you been prancin' around, talkin' **** like you was making a comeback. For what? Since your lil' misshap against Hart, you ain't been closer to being champion than when you started. You wastin' ya muthaf-ckin' time. Oh, and don't forget the NFW. Michael Manson was on top of the world, you was the leader of the whole North division but what now? You fell back, no longer on top. Instead, you second to a blue blood, homo version of Hunter Hearst Helmsley. Now, you lead a division filled with jokes, rookies and immigrant muthaf-ckas who couldn't fight their way out of a trash can. Yet you still thinkin' you a big shot? It comes down to this, nigga... As long as anyone else other than you breathes on planet earth...
You're number two. (Mason slings the belt back over his shoulder.) Talk about stereotypes all you want, nigga. But here's the truth, I speak the way I speak cuz I'm real.
(Mason switches up his voice and imitates a stereotypical white male.) I could of course, speak in this manner. But for what cost? It would be oblivious to the facts of our situation.
(He switches back to his regular voice.) At least I ain't walkin' around actin' like I'm Einstein while lookin' like a crackhead from Bum Fights Volume 2. Speak about my language, speak about my slang, speak about whatever the f-ck you want to, nigga. Sh-t still remains like this...
You can't ever speak about my skills in the ring. I'm a muthaf-ckin' champion of the world, it might not be in this rathole fed but that just goes to show I'm better than that. It shows ya'll punk *****es I ain't at your measly muthaf-ckin' level. Titles in this fed ain't important to me and beatin' you... that damn sure ain't important to me. So don't run your muthaf-ckin' mouth Manson, cuz you ain't all you cracked up to be.
And at Great Expectations, I'ma prove it. (FADE OUT) Manson — 7 February 2004 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, in long black leather coat crouched in front of a fireplace in a brick walled room, dimly-lit except for the fire itself.))
MANSON: When I was younger, I'd have been doing this wearing a white sheet over my head, have a cross set and burning, and yet, no one would find that racist. They'd call it, "performance art". Just Manson being.....you know....Manson.
Of course now, I see Mason and well, that's pretty much all I can do since I can barely understand a word he's said. I keep hearing "nigga", which has to be the first time I've ever been called that, especially since due to my aversion to sunlight, I must be the whitest man on the planet. Still, I went to college, I have a degree, I think I can get the gist of it. If not, I suppose I'll have to wander into the bad part of town and ask for some translation, and yes, they are afraid of the white man there. Well, at least one. After all, the white guy walking into there has to be crazy.
But until then, I'll give it a go. Now, Mason has finally surfaced which makes me think that he sees the WFW much as one would see the National Reserves, "If it happens on the weekend, we'll be there." Now I realize Mason is relatively new to the WFW and generally hasn't done anything and I don't even know if he's even won a match yet, or a tag match for that matter. Saying he cost me my last title match is even a stretch when he's only one of several. Of course, in this industry, we have only our ego and exaggeration.
It is nice to see that even though you're here only part-time or for the weekends, that you keep up with the WFW and the NFW, of course, you're off in you estimations, but given your grammar, I'd expect that. But even how degarded all that is, you have quickly progressed to that ultramodern stage of cynicism in which the promotion you're currently in is garbage and you are the one true shining star in all of that filth.
None of which is actually true and really at this point, banal and tired, but you seem quite young. Now I'm not that old myself, but I suppose it's a matter of perspective. Experience and all that, though I've never been one to give to the younger generation. In fact, when I'm an even more bitter old man, with amputated legs, and on painkillers, I will probably open fire on anyone passing on my lawn. But, I'm getting beside the point.
Which really is, that every aspect that counts, you are nothing but a boy to me. At one point or another, everyone proclaims themselves the world champion. Except, you don't have anything to show for it. I have a room full of title belts, pictures of the men I've injured and ruined getting those title nelts, along with those weapons I've stolen and used and kept for sentimental value.
Whether I've lost a step, whether I've lost it all, whether I never win again, mine is still the name that inspires fear and awe thorughout this circuit. And people were already doing that when I was at your point, Mason.
I could be that amputated old man and have to wheel myself to that ring, but I would still end the night either by beating you or taking a limb home with me. Everyone else has tried to make their own name off me, I suppose you were somewhere in line for that. However, unlike everyone else, you probably never deserved the chance.
You speak a crude form of English, rarely show up, and now say the promotion is what's foul and undeserving. No, you see, it was always supposed to be us, the wrestlers, we were the vermin, the chaos-bringers, the criminals, and generally those that would be a plague to humanity, at least on pay-per-view. You don't get it, you don't understand......
And you don't know what you're getting into. Because, boy, I'm still able to scratch and flay and whatever ghetto, warzone, city, or world you come from, you have never encountered the likes of me. There was never anything wrong with any promotion. It was always me. And that's how I became whatever I was and am now.
You'll never get the chance, and after the match, I just might not let you.
