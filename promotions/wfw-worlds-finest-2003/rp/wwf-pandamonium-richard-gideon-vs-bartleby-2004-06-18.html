WWF Pandamonium: Richard Gideon vs. Bartleby

18 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 18 June 2004 This show is a special fundraider for the World Wildlife Fund at the San Diego Zoo. The RP/Angle deadline is Wednesday, June 30th 11:59 PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
Methos17 — 22 June 2004 (The camera opens with Richard Gideon, on a beach somewhere in Oahu. In shorts, a Systems Go T-shirt and sneakers he lounges and watches the waves crash. Looking straight ahead, he starts to speak, barely acknowledging the camera)
Richard Gideon: As I sit here and ponder my match with Manson, I can't help but laugh. In a sixty-minute match, I had the advantage for about 25 minutes. And at 59 minutes and 59 seconds I had nothing left. There's no shame in doing your best and losing, but there is for me disgust.
But that is something for my own private thoughts, and not to be shared constantly as is the custom of my chosen vocation. I must now turn to the mysterious master of beat-downs known as only 'Bartleby'. Well Bart, I don't know if you know this, but I'm sort of in between gigs right now. I just finished a feud with Psycho and I just lost a match that should have defined my ascension. So I get to be your welcome wagon. Unfortunately, that means you're going up against a man with absolutely nothing to lose. Bart, I've taken these last few days to realize that I am young and eventually will win the WFW Title. It wasn't a few nights ago, but it will happen. In the meantime , I am going to learn and eventually, the hump will be crossed.
(Gideon turns toward the camera) Gideon: I say that to you to say this: don't sneak attack, don't get wily Bartleby. Just get in that ring and we'll beat the bejeezus out of each other. Win or lose, at least you'll be on camera for it. Now, if you'll excuse me, I have a ciesta to catch up on...
(Fade out as Gideon lowers his head and nods off) PaulNJ21 — 28 June 2004 FADE IN: INT. A DARK ROOM - EARLY MORNING/LATE NIGHT With the gloomy green glow of a digital clock flashing 2:13 A.M. marking the only visible activity, we hear a door open, then the pounding of footsteps upon a wood floor. Suddenly, the mask of Bartleby appears, though only faintly visible, by the clock’s light. BARTLEBY: Richard Gideon... a man who’s had his ups and downs, seen his share of successes and failures, but throughout it all, remained steadfast in his dedication to World’s Finest Wrestling. This week, he faces a new challenge in the form of an old foe. One who is no longer wanted, but prefers not to leave. Gideon, you’ve seen the chaos I’ve unleashed since anchoring myself here and now, as you yourself step to the forefront, more of the same is what you can expect. Bartleby chuckles, almost sadistically, then moves in closer to the clock, revealing more of his shrouded face. BARTLEBY: In our realm of competition, things move in cycles. What was once old can again be new. The competition ebbs and flows in such a way that, from year to year to year, you can never be quite certain as to what exactly you should expect. Everything has a shelf life, some longer than others, and regardless of how anybody feels about me, Mr. Michaels, even YOU, Richard... our resilience is one that is un-matched. Some simply fade away... as for me, things will always come full circle. WFW will change; people will come and go, champion after champion after champion will be crowned, but I will always be here. That you can be sure of. He pauses momentarily. Only the sounds of his deep breaths are heard. Upon reflecting, Bartleby breaks the silence once again. BARTLEBY: The time has come, Richard, for the cycle to be completed. For far too long, I’ve waited in the shadows, waited to take back what is mine, and now... the time has come. You are but one link on the chain to what we so desperately seek. All that being said, when the time arrives for PANDEMONIUM, the world will see, for the very first time, just how much POWER we possess. A word of caution... nothing they can do will ever prepare them for what is about to be unleashed. I am Bartleby.... aaaaaand I am not. FADE OUT Methos17 — 29 June 2004 (Richard Gideon is shown watching the end of the Bartleby interview in his spacious NY home. Wearing a t-shirt and pajamas, he groggily turns toward the camera he's set up on his table and begins to speak)
Richard Gideon: Well, "Bartleby", if that is your real name...you seem to know me better than I know you. Granted, with my entire body of work available on WFW home video I am sure that's not too hard to accomplish, but I applaud the legwork. That being said however, when we meet in that ring you get to put your research into action.
(Gideon puts his feet up on the table and continues) Richard Gideon: I mean, let's be honest here. I'm not going to lose every match I fight in, nor will I win them all. But I look at each of them as a challenge. Which is what I'm looking at you as Bartleby.......you are either a step in the direction of my renewed success or the harbinger of my continued failure.
I really have nothing left to do. I mean, I lost the match, I lost the shot at being the number one contender, but I will not lose my dignity. Good night cats and kittens...and Good luck to you Bartleby and I'll see you in the ring nnnndaddio!
(Fade to black)
