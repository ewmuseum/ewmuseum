Diablo — 2 November 2003 (Cue up: “Ho Jayegi Balle Balle” by Daler Mehndi) (FADEIN: El Arco Iris is standing in front of a rainbow backdrop. He is wearing his ring attire, only with a rainbow satin knee-length robe over it all.)
El Arco Iris: El Arco Iris is feeling in the greatest of spirits now, because El Arco Iris teams with Señor Copycat, and can help him to overcome the source of his distress. El Arco Iris knows that he will be successful, and Copycat will be free of that which troubles him, because El Arco Iris already sees a glaring flaw in the enemy team! Señor Edmunds has demonstrated to us that he already lacks the key ingredient in a match like this that shall lead us to victory.
(El Arco Iris points a finger towards the camera) El Arco Iris: Now, Señor Sensational, it is apparent that you do not trust Luster, your teammate. Now, El Arco Iris figures that perhaps you have a few reasons, as Luster is not the most trustworthy of individuals, but nevertheless, as a team, you must trust each other, or the battle will already be lost before the bell even rings. El Arco Iris realizes that perhaps with all your success as a singles wrestler, all your titles and fame, you think you probably can get the job done on your own, but El Arco Iris also realizes that two heads are almost always better than one. Both you AND Señor Luster will try to get your own jobs done, and only end up impeding each other. Selfishness gets nobody anywhere. El Arco Iris has made it a priority to communicate well and often with his partner, through whatever means necessary, just to make sure we are on the same page. El Arco Iris and Copycat shall prevail because we share a respect, a trust, and a friendship. Friendship is one of the most powerful forces in the universe, second only to love. And it is through this friendship that we will work to help each other, instead of helping only ourselves.
(El Arco Iris raises his hand to his chin and rubs it contemplatively) El Arco Iris: I think, Señor Edmunds, that since you are hesitant to trust Luster, that this shows a hesitance to trust yourself, and lack of trust in oneself is a very bad thing indeed. I think this lack of trust and lack of confidence may stem from the lack of ability to pull off a decisive victory against Copycat. El Arco Iris has realized that perhaps it may be another severe blow to your self esteem getting defeated again by Copycat, but maybe acceptance of the fact that sometimes there are just people who are better than others will allow you to have more confidence in your own ability, without gauging it against others. Remember, trust in your teammate can always help compensate for your own lacking. If you do not trust, then you will fall. El Arco Iris hopes that with the lessons in trust and believing in oneself that shall come with defeat, that even though your hand will not be raised, the corners of your mouth shall, in a SMIIIIIILE on your face!
(FTB) ------------------------------ "I blow your mind. You just blow." GARTHIsTheLaw — 6 November 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Nov-06-03 AT 03:17&nbsp;PM (EST)](Cueup: Weird background noise that is probably supposed to sound creepy)
(Fade in on a dark, fog-filled scene. We can see nothing but darkness...but soon, after a quick adjustment of eyes, we can make out a sillouette against the background. Could it be Copycat? It looks like the figure is wearing a beret, but for some reason it seems bulkier than Copycat. However, the mystery does not last long, as he speaks after a few moments)
Copycat: And once again, I am assigned to face Sean Edmunds in a match that, regardless of its ending, will likely prove inconclusive. This should probably bother me. It would have bothered me last time. And the silly stipulation thrown in...a double dog collar match? Our esteemed president is reeeeeeally grasping at strings for this one. It almost seems like, once again, the Cat is being pushed aside in favor of more irritating stars like Scotty Michaels and Felix Red...as though they're too stubborn to take a chance on some of the NEW talent.
(The word "UNDERAPPRECIATED" floats onscreen and begins flying around, animated as though it is alive)
Copycat: Or otherwise I suppose I could be bothered by the incessant plotting of the "Simply Sensational" one and his makeup-drenched assistant. They've made references before about just how very much smarter they are than me, just how very much more clever. And their jokes about how I'm so innovative that I'm "always coming up with new ways to lose"...I could really take that to heart. It would be crushing. What if I'm not as smart as I think I am? What if I'm merely overconfident?
(The word "OVERCONFIDENCE" floats onscreen and begins flying around with the word "UNDERAPPRECIATED")
Copycat: But I'm not so foolish. Letting such petty concerns get to me would surely decrease my chances of winning this match, and to top it off, the concerns are groundless. If I'm really being held down by stubborn league executives, why am I getting a chance to take out Sean Edmunds and Luster at the same time, all the while teaming with one of the top up-and-coming talents in WFW? And if Sean Edmunds and the Cosmetics Queen are so much wiser than I am, why is it that their master plan last time wasn't enough to get the job done? I can deal with these insecurities.
(The background music changes to something somewhat recognizable...but it's hard to tell what yet. Copycat reaches behind him to whatever the big bulky thing is and pulls something that looks vaguely like a hose off of it)
Copycat: Let me just show you a little example of how my mind works, Sean. (Suddenly the song's first downbeat kicks in and it becomes recognizable) (Cueup: "Ghostbusters" by Ray Parker, Jr.) (Copycat takes a few steps forward and the lighting changes to illuminate him. He is dressed in full Ghostbuster garb, complete with backpack and ray gun, though his trademark beret remains intact)
Copycat: Y'see, when I notice little nagging insecurities like these ones...(he motions to the words floating around above him) I don't dwell on them and let them get to me. I go right after the problem and stop it from being a threat to me.
Backup singers: GHOSTBUSTERS! Copycat: KEE-YAH! (Copycat blasts the word "UNDERAPPRECIATED" with the gun and it explodes. He then turns and blasts the word "OVERCONFIDENCE" and it also bursts into a million little computer-animated pieces. Copycat then returns to looking at the camera)
Copycat: When you're down in the dumps about a loss and about being used for league recognition and not name recognition, it's not too hard to let the nagging little insecurities start to bother you. But now, things are starting to look up a little bit for me, even if my one-on-one match with Mr. Edmunds was wholly inconclusive.
(The word "INCONCLUSIVE" floats onscreen and starts flitting around. Copycat looks up at it and points his ray gun)
Copycat & Ray Parker, Jr. (simultaneously): I ain't 'fraid 'a no ghost! (Copycat blasts the word to smithereens, then looks back at the camera) Copycat: In fact, this rematch is much better for me than it is for him. I have something to gain by taking out Luster - he cost me the North American Title a few weeks back, and although I cost him an even bigger title against Shawn Hart, I still have yet to get the satisfaction of kicking his ass myself. Plus, this match will give me the opportunity to team up with El Arco Iris, who is definitely headed for big things, even if he is - as Sean so eloquently put it - a bit...special. Whereas for Sean, this is just another painful rehash of the old Copycat vs. Edmunds battle, and if our last matchup is any indication, it looks like he will once again be unable to prove he has any sort of edge on me. He's constantly CLAIMING he's superior to me in one way or another...
(The word "INFERIORITY" appears above Copycat, and he wastes no time in aiming at it) Copycat & Ray Parker, Jr. (simultaneously): I ain't 'fraid 'a no ghost! (Copycat blasts the word into oblivion. He cracks his neck, then looks back at the camera)
Copycat: ...But he's yet to be able to present any proof. I told him before New World Madness that every time he develops a master plan devised to hold me down, it never works out. He tried to use his mysterious fetish for manly women against me last time, and it didn't work. 'Course, I didn't win the match either, but I'm comfortable with that - comfortable in the fact that, while that's a slight disappointment for me, it's got to be even more disappointing for Edmunds. I wasn't at the top of my game in that match; my attitudes were all in the wrong place. Whereas he's got NO excuse. And if his A-game can't beat my C-game, y'think that means I'm going to come out on top at Ghoulish Games?
(Copycat freezes, as though awaiting a response) Ray Parker, Jr.: YEAH YEAH YEAH YEAH! Copycat: Thank you. Point is, Sean, the only thing you've managed to prove is that, time and time again, you can't find a way to beat me. Our match at Ghoulish Games probably won't be too conclusive as relates to our battles. But when El Arco Iris and I take down you and that waste of life Luster in this ridiculous double dog collar match, it will prove one thing - that no matter how stupid the circumstances are, I'll still find a way to beat you. You've got Sherri, Luster, that punk P-Nut, hell even President Wylde seems to be on your side...
(The words "EDMUNDS," "SHERRI," "LUSTER," "P-NUT," and "WYLDE" all float onscreen and start swirling around. Though they're obviously computer-animated, Copycat takes a few swats at them before addressing the camera again)
Copycat: ...But if you think that's going to be enough to make me feel doomed, let me tell you something...
(He pulls out one of the yellow-and-black-striped "traps" from the Ghostbuster movies and places it on the ground)
Copycat & Ray Parker, Jr. (simultaneously): BUSTIN' MAKES ME FEEL GOOD! (Copycat drops to a knee, closes his eyes, and points to the sky rock-star-style as the trap sucks in all the words floating around. Once all the words are gone, he jumps back up to his feet)
Copycat: Even though it's not going to be the one-on-one match we want, Edmunds, bring everything you've got to Ghoulish Games anyway - I just want to give you a preview of what's going to happen when we finally do have that decisive match. I want you to see that, no matter how clever you think your devious plans are, it's not going to be enough to outwit the Smartest Player in the Game. Because once I've beaten you at Ghoulish Games, it's only going to be a matter of time before I finally beat you one-on-one. And that, my overconfident, manly-woman-loving friend...is just all there is to it.
