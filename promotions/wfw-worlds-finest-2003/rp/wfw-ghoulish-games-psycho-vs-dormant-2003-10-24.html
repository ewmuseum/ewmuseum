WFW Ghoulish Games - Psycho vs. Dormant

24 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 24 October 2003 The RP/Angle dealine is Monday, November 3rd 11:59 PM. If all all possible, make your RP Halloween or Day of the Dead themed.
Cut your promos here. Devil666 — 5 November 2003 (The camera opens on the masked face of Psycho coldly staring back) Psycho: (shakes his head) Challenge....that's what I want. Challenge. Here on pay-per-view I'm stuck facing a man appriatly name Dormant. To his credit he's lived up to his name, but it's not the challenge I'm looking for. No more time to waste on you Dormant...I'm not supporting Richard Gideon in some meaningless tag-team match. I've already given him enough success. Perhaps what I need to do is seek out what I want.
(Psycho steps forward staring behind his mask) Psycho: tonight I'll make a statement by defeating you, but for someone. For someone tongith will be a bad night. You see dormant...if you won't give me a fight...well I'll go out and find one. I'll make my own war and clear a path to the top. I was the WFW Champion once and NOBODY pinned my shoulders to the mat to get it back, So tonight Dormant...I'll beat you, but it only starts there. See you in the ring.
(Psycho let's loose a sadistic laugh and walks off) <FTB>
