Spirit of NGEN: Rat Fink vs. Felix Red

27 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 27 July 2003 The RP/angle deadline is August 11th. Send in all angles to [detail withheld] NotorisSTD — 1 August 2003 (CUEUP: The version of Mozart's Lux aeterna requiem they used for the Two Towers soundtrack...)
(FADEIN: We stumble upon Felix Red, crouched on the ground of a shadow laced cellar, rocking the obligatory sunglasses along with a black "F-Minus" T-shirt, black jeans, black X's drawn on the back of his hands, and steel toed boots. Greasy purple hair scattered every which way, we can almost make out a stoic and cynical smirk slithering across his mouth...)
"The boy who saved the world" FELIX RED: Let me tell you about accountability... Everything decision any of us have ever made was based on everything else we've ever experienced. When presented with any sort of crossroads, there's only one path we can choose to take. our past wont allow for anything except for the choice we inevitably make. If what we experience begins at childhood when all of our decisions are made for us, then we don't necessarily make any real choices in this life at all. The only other factor besides our decisions that effects our destiny is random chance, the very nature of which negates any attempt we could make to control it...Therefore, there is no free will. We are not in control...
Which is why it's impossible for me to lament my previous failure to defeat Rat Fink. I was attacked by a robot. I couldn't control this, just as Rat Fink couldn't control the factors that led to the robot's
interjection in our match. He was losing, because the predetermined choices the two of us had made left me the superior wrestler. In his mind, if he had lost, his plans for world domination...his hope for
a better world, his tireless labor to this end, his dream, his life...would have been wasted. Low and behold, random chance nullified his victory over me when his world title match fell all to pieces. His dreams burned to the ground regardless of the choices he made...Now, he could earn himself a rematch if he simply completes a task quite similar to one he just recently saw through...literally, with flying colors...Easy as Pie. In theory. Except for the very circumstance that ultimately led to his victory and my downfall in our previous encounter...He knows I am better than he is. So how now that he must, is he to pin me or make me submit, especially as I am now familiar with the specifics of his arsenal of offense and defensive techniques...Someone went and boarded up his backdoor. Trapped, huddled in a corner, with no means of escape...he is confronted with a specter from his immediate past, A boy who destroyed the world with little to loose and vindication to gain. His means to an end has become a dead end...Or rather, a Red end.
What do you say to a man, when he knows all is lost? What do you say to a man who tried so hard to change everything, is now helpless to change anything at all, and then realizes In the bleakest of epiphanies that he has always been precisely this helpless...and nothing else?
I say Die Rat Fink, and hope you get reincarnated as someone who doesn't suck. (grins, and gives a big double thumbs up...)
ChrisHorowitz — 6 August 2003
(Cue up "My Out of Style is Coming Back" by the Matthew Good Band.)
(Fade into Rat Fink's robotic facility, where the leader of the new world revolution pounds one of his broken minions with a sledge hammer. He then turns, sledge hammer in hand.)
Rat Fink: A crime has been committed, and it will not go unpunished. My friends, my loyal supporters, we have been insulted. WFW, while being a forum for the revolution, the voice of the new order has allowed to take place a crime so disturbing, so destructive that WFW has put progress back.
Singlehandedly, they have kept the world in the dark age. Why would this organization seek to punish you? They have obviously been corrupted by this crumbling world and see that its champion is about to fall. They realize that there is only one alternative.
Since Doctor Silver was in his waning days, they could only find Shawn Hart to replace him. They spit at the revolution and instead throw another pawn of the dying regimes of the past at the people and hope they will proclaim him their savior.
WFW does not care about saving you. They do not care about the cure of the world's ailing that is the soothing power of evil. They cannot fathom how they have set back progress, and they cannot possibly hope to help the world by what they have done. They have allowed the tyrants of today hold power for that much longer. They have let another one of their brainwashed pawns assume the throne while the masses demand change. They demand our terror to reclaim their reality.
But what has the WFW done. Not only have they allowed myself to be robbed by Shawn Hart, and allowed humanity in general to be robbed, they have just spun me around and put the revolution in the opposite direction.
I have gone from the verge of crushing Doctor Silver back around to again facing Felix Red.
And it seems Mr. Red has very little new to say. I defeated him on my way to the final victory of the revolution, and despite a set back the victory is no less certain. This revolution does not rely on the dream of a close victory Red, it does not rely on its goals being at hand. Felix, instead we stand only for reality, and to defeat one more of the legions of the surreal serves the purpose of our evil.
The reality of the world has once again crashed upon our shores and attempted to maintain its fleeting grasp. Doc silver will be replaced by Shawn Hart, and Felix Red will again fall allowing us another move at the top. At the symbol which will finally rally every man woman and child on the planet to our cause. Finally they will not be crushed by dreams of grandeur... Finally they will see reality.
(Fade out) NotorisSTD — 11 August 2003 (CUEUP: "Circle N” by Mourning Glory...) (Static spills from the television screen into the otherwise pitch dark room where Felix Red sits in a red leather arm chair, hands folded in his lap. Purple hair tied into a pony tail on the top of his head, sunglasses, old white with blue letting "Anime brigade" T-shirt, black jeans, and sinister grin...)
"The boy who destroyed the world" FELIX RED: For one immersed in humanity's polar opposite, you're quite prone to human folly, Rat Fink. Made a mistake so woefully typical. One mustn't confuse an idea for a thing. The "WFW" can't really do anything. It's a collective of individuals who can do things...like you. One thing you may or may not have done is earned a match against the world champion. Another thing you may or may not have done was pin Doc Silver. You claim to not rely on your goals being at hand, while relying on nothing but just that...Which makes sense enough. There's a phrase, cliché maybe, describing people who move in reverse, seeking vindication through what they've
already done. It's "living in the past." So Rat Fink, you, like me, strive to see your almost through to the end. We do not romanticize our past, we want to nullify it's relevance. This is productive. So kudos to you, Rat Fink.
I've been over this before. Fate guides us down the random path of chance. If you're to become WFW world champion and somehow use the prestige to alter your world you'll do this. It's my guess that you will do nothing of the sort, my opinion that your goals are inherently flawed and thus doomed to collapse, my perception that you are not your brother's savoir or even a megalomaniac. More a psychotic, embittered, insecure, egomaniacal misfit with too much time on his hands. I'm curious. Did women loose interest in you before or after you starting building robots?
As for me, if I am to undo my failure to defeat you previously, I will. My logic tells me if you had the physical and mental capacity to pin me, I would never have become one of those rare individuals
who can say "yes...I've been assaulted by an evil robot." Fate only guides us down the random path of chance. My resolve is sound. I have no fears or regrets. My world is already dead to me. Where
you fight out of lust for power, I fight to test my limits. Embrace the unknown. Tonight I answer the question...If I could travel through time...undo my life...would everything be different?
I ruined everything I could. What if I could've done more? Been more?...What if feeling like god is small potatoes next to what is truly possible?
Consider yourself my experiment, Mr. Rat Fink. Or my job doll. Whichever makes you feel better.
ChrisHorowitz — 13 August 2003
(Cue up "Never Alone" by Worthless) (Fade into Rat Fink's command bunker deep inside... a low rent warehouse.) Rat Fink: Red, I must wonder why you even bother. Perhaps because you are one of those who are so polluted by the old tyrants and their promises, their false hope... that you believe perhaps you do have a chance to defeat me. But I reside in the real world Felix. I see only the truth, and if you could you would not feel at all confident about this match.
I have defeated you once before, a match that drew more legions to support the revolution. The people saw that the evil legions of reality could crush the dreamers, the surreal.
But we will not stand for the WFW's actions any longer. We will not let them sabotage the revolution. I have determined the proper course of action now that they have gotten in the way. I must become president of WFW and force them out of our path.
The presidency of the WFW is the only way to achieve this. We will cease the halting of the revolution as the WFW has done. They have put handcuffs on the revolution because they fear our evil. They fear its cleansing power. They fear our allies. They fear our friend Saddam, Kim, Chuck Taylor. These men all add to us, and bring humanity forward to a place where we can be free of the shackles of the surreal.
Felix, we have offered you a chance for surrender. We have given you a chance to simply give yourself to the revolution. But you refuse. You are so commited to the lie that you will spit in the face of the truth. We have done nothing but help you Felix, even in defeating you we only tried to show you how the surreal tyrants of our day have lied to you, have given you nothing but the crushing blow of failure. But you still suckle them, hoping for more comforting hope.
You may live in denial, but you still protect the remnants of their empire. You are every bit as far in their service as Doc Silver, yet you have received none of the benefit. My armies are in place, victory is so close yet you continue to just deny the reality that the armies of evil have already won the war. You cling to the surreal, you cling to the dreams, you cling to it all because it is the only thing you can feel good about in your life. Everything else has let you down, all because of those dreams. Those dreams have made every accomplishment feel so small yet you will not fight back against them.
Red, you are simply a coward who will not accept his cowardice. If you could you could surrender yourself to our reality, to our new order. But you cannot accept this truth for the tyrants of the surreal have made you feel that surrender is the work of a lesser man. The lesser man that you are, not the superman that they promised you could become. And since you are this lesser man, you will not have a chance of victory, only defeat.
(Fade out)
