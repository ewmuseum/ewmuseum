WFW — Roleplay

Spirit of NGEN: Michael Manson (c) vs. Scotty Michaels

27 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 27 July 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Aug-06-03 AT 01:24&nbsp;PM (EST)]This match is a Cage Match for the WFW North American Heavyweight Title.
The RP/angle deadline is August 11th. Send in all angles to [detail withheld] Showtime24 — 27 July 2003 (FADEIN to Scotty Michaels, getting in a limo outside of Veteran's Stadium in Philadelphia... leaving a Bon Jovi concert, the last concert to ever be held at the Vet. Sitting in the limo are HEEL, and two lovely ladies on each side of Michaels... HEEL pours Michaels a glass of champagne. He sips from the glass, and then sets it down, turning to the camera...)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: God, I just can't believe actually how great I really am. I'm making history left and right. Tonight, I rocked out on stage with Jon Bon Jovi at the last concert ever at Veteran's Stadium. August 11th, Spirit of NGEN... the BIGGEST pay per view in the history of the WFW, I make history again... I walk out with the North American championship.
Now I know it's not going to be easy, because Mike Manson's definitely a joke by no means. Well, he has been losing left and right lately... pretty much thanks to me. I mean, at Paradise Lost... I was simply going up on the ring apron to get a better look at the action. It's not my fault that Manson's clumsy, and smacked his head off the chair.
Manson's had the belt since the inception of the championship, and honestly... he hasn't impressed me too much. He may be a legend in the sport of professional wrestling. He may have title reigns left and right. But I've got his number... and finally, I get it my way. I get my one on one match for the championship... FINALLY! I've been number one contender for this belt for some time, and now I get my match.
I get MY CHANCE, to shine. I get MY CHANCE, to strap up the WFW on my back and make it even more of a RATINGS POWERHOUSE! They don't call me the Viewer's Choice for no reason. I've failed to disappoint since I got here... and I'm not about to start now.
Mike Manson, your days as the North American champion are numbered... because NOTHING, absolutely NOTHING... is going to stop the Phenomenon, the Superstar, the Viewer's Choice... from capturing something that should've been his from the start.
I'll seeya at the pay per view, you better get ready... Manson. (FADEOUT, as Scotty gets out of the limo at a local hotel... with the ladies, and HEEL by his side...)
Manson — 27 July 2003 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, in black t-shirt and jeans, sitting on top of a polished amber casket. On a tablecloth in front of him, he has placed a Snapple, a BLT sandwich, and his Dracula pez dispenser. he tries some of the BLT and shakes his head.))
MANSON(knocking on the casket): Needs more salt. ((The door swings open and a hand reaches put with a salt shaker. MANSON takes it and pours it on the inside of his BLT and returns to the hand. He takes another bite and nods approvingly.))
MANSON: And people say I cavort with the living dead....I mean Bon Jovi? Those guys are still alive? They're like the walking dead except they do concerts for middled-aged men who still want to be down with the young people.
You can't be that old, Michaels, then again, there's accounting for taste either. Not to mention the fact that you would want to be up on stage with Bon Jovi. You might as well drive your car with a rainbow flag waving from the back. Come to think of it, you do seem a little too close to the oh-so cleverly named "HEEL".
I imagine within your thoughts is the image of you and him walking off, hand-in-hand with the North American title. Of course that would mean you have to go through me. Now I'm not ashamed of the fact that I lose to multiple opponents at a time. In fact, the more I get my ass kicked, the closer it gets to sex.
And yes, I am secure enough in my own manhood to say that. But nothing of the sort will occur at the ppv. Now, I understand your desire here, Scot, you're not that old, but you are going through a mid-life crisis. Going to Bon Jovi is proof enough of that.
You want to see if its all still there, if you can still draw the people like a cult leader and lock them inside with nothing but a pitcher of Kool-Aid. You want to beat or cripple me to show everyone and yourself that you're still the "Man", still important. And not just a broken down man in his mid 30s aging faster than he should and left with only enough creative power to nickname someone "HEEL".
You, Scot, are the walking dead. Or at least, your career is. I've lost matches before....all kinds....all kinds of injuries as well....and I work through things. Always do, that's why I'm one of those legends you talk about. And might I add, it's actually nice that you would call me so, instead of wearing extremely tight jeans and claim I'm always pathetic and that you care for nothing in the world because you'r so bad-ass. Still, I'm left with little recourse but to enhance this "phase" for you.
Because I'm not your friend, I'm not your therapist, I'm certainly not your priest, but I am your North American champion. And I also seem to appeal to that nihlistic fanbase developed in the 90s..while you hearken back to an earlier age...one where someone young might have saw Bon Jovi and been deluded or high enough to think they're cool.
But age is irrelevant, because regardless, I'm going to slap you around like you were my grandfather and I'm in your will. This does not require a healthy leg to do. Because I could lose my legs, my soul, my title, my face..but in the end....whether age or circumstance..I'm still me. I survive, I go on, I think things through..I think things out.
And it seems your only real ambition, underneath all these aging probems, is just to be another name in the body count.
((MANSON knocks on the casket.)) MANSON: Pepper? Showtime24 — 29 July 2003 (FADEIN to Scotty Michaels, sitting in the den of his Philadelphia loft. Michaels is sitting in a recliner, wearing a smokg jacket... a cigar is burning in the ashtray next to him. He takes a few puffs, and then sets it back down in the ashtray...)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Ah... Michael, Michael, Michael. You come up with these theories on the sexual preferences of myself and HEEL and you seem to be very proud with your final conclusion. To you, it seems as if my musical tastes influence this... as I am a huge Bon Jovi fan, and I am very happy to be hanging out with the band on stage. You see, that is what you call living a normal life. Listening to normal music, wearing normal clothes... living, like a normal person. Yeah, sure... some may make fun because Bon Jovi is a band of the 80's. Well I hate to break it to you, bud... but I listen to all sorts of music. Alright, well not so much the kind of GARBAGE that you listen to, but I am a very cultured person. Whether it be the finest wine, the finest foods, and even the finest pieces of ass... I live the life of a king.
(Scotty takes another puff on his cigar..) SCOTTY MICHAELS: I choose not to live in the dark, dreary underground world that includes the likes of you. I don't bow down to Marilyn Manson, the freak that he is. I think that is where the problem lies between you and me, Manson. Over the years, you're known for feuding with guys who have personality disorders... guys who are straight F**KED IN THE HEAD! I've got no demons. The only thing I really don't like, are people like you. Just because I'm sure you were a LOSER in high school, you picked up this whole goth thing... just like the story of every other loser out there. You probably got picked on and beat up on a regular basis. One day, you just SNAPPED... and became the man you are today.
(Scotty puffs some more on the cigar... and then sips from a glass of champagne..) SCOTTY MICHAELS: Maybe that's why I want to beat you so bad, because I know I'm better than you. While you were busy living the life I've just described, I was training at some of the finest wrestling schools in the world. I was busy living the life that I set out for myself. The word FAILURE is not in my vocabulary, Manson. The reason I've never won a world championship is because I decided for a while that I just really didn't want to be a part of professional wrestling. Up until a few months back, our paths never even crossed before. You showed up just about the time that I called it quits. And in that time, you've made quite a name for yourself... as one of the CRAZIEST, SICKEST SONS 'A B**CHES that ever stepped foot in a ring. And just like you've chosen to take that path... I'm taking my own. First, to the North American Championship at the pay per view. And then shortly after that, on to the world heavyweight championship.
(Scotty puts out the cigar and gets out of the chair. He rips the jacket off, and throws the glass of champagne...)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Manson, I've been waiting to get my hands on you for weeks now... and I finally get my chance in two weeks. You've been on quite the unlucky streak lately, buddy. About the only thing right you've done since you've gotten to the WFW is handcuff me to a ladder. Honestly though, I'm glad that I didn't win that match... I didn't want there to be doubt as to whether or not I could beat you in a regular wrestling match. But as a matter of fact, I will GUARANTEE that I can beat you without interference. At the Spirit of NGEN, I want to FACE YOU IN A CAGE! So do what you need to do. Talk to the commissioner, and make it happen. My sights are focused on you, Manson... and shortly after that pay per view, you'll wish that you never heard the name SCOTTY MICHAELS!
(FADE TO BLACK...) Manson — 30 July 2003 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, in black Metallica "Unforgiven" t-shirt and jeans, sits Indian-style on top of a glass coffee table.))
MANSON: I suppose I could go and ask for a cage match. Get a petition, sign some papers, look through some files...but then I wouldn't have all this time to contemplate the marketing possibilities of just being called "MANSON" instead of "Michael Manson" like it says on my driver's license. I'm sure you'd feel the same way Scot, if someone ever made a t-shirt for you and just had "MICHAELS" on it. You'll be edgey and favorably received in dozens of marketing polls.
However, I take enough time away from that when I address you or make a pez run. But rest assured, you have stated your desire and I'm sure, the powers that be, the powers to be, God, Odin, Thor, Zeus, and the devil have all heard you by now and will be taking it under consideration.
In the meantime, I feel the need to put out a paradox in your thinking. After all, you state that I often tend to deal with the mentally disturbed portion of our fine industry. And that you, represent the surbarbia of the normal.
Of course, anyone could see that no one around these parts is even remotely a normal human being. A normal human being would never think of getting into a cage and being locked in there with me. And a normal human being could probably think of a better name than "HEEL".
But you think I'm like this because I bow to some music idol or because I was an outcast in high school. Well...I could tell...
....that my parents used to lock me in a coffin whenever I did something wrong. ....that I am the son of a prostitute and a nameless priest. ....that I am what Ozzy Osbourne would be if he was sober. ....that my family is one of morticians and I grew up around the dead. But I would only be lying my ass off. Which isn't anything new. Why am I the way I am? Why is the sky blue, the grass green, and why is Freddie Prinze Jr. a writer?
I am what I have to be. That's why I've won a few of those world titles you bring up. I also, am not used to failure. But I adapt, I move on.
I don't care what schools you went to, I don't care if you think you are better than me. I'm Mike Manson, EVERYONE thinks they're better than me...and probably right.
You already failed once, Scott, in the ladder match. Mitigating factors aside, you couldn't beat me then when you just injured my leg. And now you think you can beat a sadistic pain freak in a steel cage?
Well, if that's what you really want.....then so be it....Thank God we're all normal here...
Showtime24 — 31 July 2003 (FADEIN to Scotty Michaels, again in his den... not looking as calm as he did during his last interview.)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Mike Manson, you continue to gloat about that ladder match... you continue to brag, that you were the victor in a match that involved THE VIEWER'S CHOICE! Whatever, Manson... I've said it a bajillion times before, but that doesn't make you better than me. You climbed a ladder and grabbed the belt. In my eyes, ladder matches are the lamest excuses for matches in the history of wrestling. They're agreed upon because some wrestlers don't want to take the chance that they might get pinned in the center of that ring. I didn't agree with that match-up. The powers that be here in the WFW decided that it would be pretty cool to put the 3 of us in the ring with a ladder, and a belt hanging from the ceiling.
(Michaels, seemingly calmer, sits down in his chair...) SCOTTY MICHAELS: And the way it looks, I got my match. I have you in a steel cage, just the way I want it. You may be a sick, sick man... and will do anything in your powers to put your body on the line, but I'm in this to show everybody that I can do the same. While I do represent NORMALCY in this strange world known as the WFW, I am not the every day run-of-the-mill professional wrestler... I can take the heat, and I can take anything that you have to dish out to me. While I pride myself on NOT being a FREAK as you are, within every man lies the passion to reach some sort of high in their life, whether it be natural or not. My high over the past couple of months has been making you suffer... and I've been doing a damn good job of it. Yeah, I'm sure you've had worse... attacks coming from many people considered the underbelly of our society. But don't worry, Manson... everything thing I've done so far has been very strategic.
(HEEL walks into the room, arms crossed...) SCOTTY MICHAELS: You continue to make fun of something you do not understand. You make fun of the man standing behind me because he was given the name HEEL. What you don't get is the fact that HEEL'S purpose is to watch my back.... and in that, he will prove to be one of the most VIOLENT, SADISTIC FORCES to ever step foot into the WFW. He doesn't need to speak... he doesn't need a creative nickname. One word, one bald head, and ONE BAD ATTITUDE. That's what he's all about. That's what he lives and breathes for. And you know what I live and breathe for right now? Making sure that the North American championship is not around your waist much longer. I'll make sure of that, as it all becomes a reality at the Spirit of NGEN... Manson, I've got your number, and soon you'll see why I am not only the far superb intellectual, but the further superb athlete as well. Spirit of NGEN, I take what should've been mine a long time ago...
(FADEOUT, as Michaels walks out to the living room... we see HEEL walk the other direction into a dark room..)
Manson — 31 July 2003 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, in plain black t-shirt and jeans, sitting on a ladder in a darkened area backstage.))
MANSON: You know, it will be worth beating and tormenting you, Scott, just to get you over this idea of normality you have. Let me point something out to you, you wear tights and fight people for a living.
Now there is another guy who does the exact same thing....his name is BATMAN. Now let's face it, this entire industry is full of drug addicts, egomaniacs, and people who in an office setting would have gone as insane as Mel Gibson's father. You're not normal, neither am I, neither is the 12 year old boy scout calling for blood in the 12th row. There is no normality, because we're all screwed up..
I realize you might think you cling to this idea by listening Bon Jovi.....and that's fine...because that is what most people who are not me would do. However, now, I think it's affecting your mind.
For one thing, you're going on about a ladder match when at the same time you're asking for a cage match. Both matches involve climbing to win, or can in some cases (Don't we all like the pinfall better?). And of course your excuse for losing is that its an obsolete match.
I'm thinking though, if you really were a strategic thinker, it wouldn't have mattered given you had weeks to think about the ladder match. Weeks to think about my leg injury.....and yet..you're hitting me in the back with a chair.
Not to mention you're still going on about being the Viewer's or Taster's Choice when you originally went after me because when you were climbing that ladder, figuratively and otherwise, the people wanted the blood-smeared maniac, being me, to win. Remember? Meaning, you weren't the viewer's choice, though I'm not sure why you'd want to be named after a generic Jewel brand in the first place.
Had you thought...had you considered..you would have known one thing. That being locked in a cage with me....alone..without your friend HEEL in there...you need to be more than normal....more than just another wrestler...the cliche would be you have to be the North American Champion, but that's me.
I'm sure HEEL will try to do something or other..lackies and minions always do..but that just means there'll be three of us again. I thought you didn't like that?
Regardless however, the cage is my home and generally I bleed so bad its like sex...and I always come out..ON TOP.
Showtime24 — 1 August 2003 (FADEIN to Scotty Michaels, standing in front of a barbed wire fence. He's wearing a shirt that says "VIEWER'S CHOICE" on the front, and a pair of sweatpants. Michaels begins to clap..)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: You know, Michael, I must hand it to you. Excellent. You know, you're right... I may not be normal, just because I'm here... getting ready to step into a BLOODBATH, I'm sure, with you. But I'm just about the closest thing to normal around these parts, and it sure as hell doesn't have anything to do with the music that I listen to. I'm normal because I lead an every-day normal life. I don't hang out around coffins. I don't sit in the dark. I don't eat Pez like it's a london broil steak. I do what I need to do.. and what I need to do, very soon, is beat you for the North American championship.
(Michaels laughs..) SCOTTY MICHAELS: Michael, your altered sense of reality makes you not realize what I'm getting at. While I continue to point out you beat me in a ladder match, yeah.. it eats me up a little bit inside, just by the mere fact that in the results section of Pro Wrestling Illustrated, it says that you beat me and some other guy in a match. Pin me, you didn't. But in case you didn't pick it up, and it sure looks that way... I don't want a cage that's open on top... I don't want a cage that has a door. I want a cage that drops from the ceiling, and gets locked to the floor. I want a cage where the only way to win, is to make your opponent, that being you, succumb to a pinfall or a submission. That's all I want, Manson... I'm calling you out, in your match, and I'm positive that I'll come out on top. While Heel may be my insurance policy, I don't need him to beat you... I'm absolutely sure of that one. I want you in your prime, YOUR MATCH!
(Michaels starts moving around anxiously, pushing the chain link fence every now and then...)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: I want to see the blood flow out of your head like a sieve. Yeah, maybe I'm not normal... maybe I ENJOY seeing you suffer. I want to beat you within an inch of your life, and then stop... just because, well, I want to see you suffer. I've got you this time, Manson. There's not a doubt in my mind. You better hope that I just pin you, and that I don't slap on the submission move that I've been perfecting in practice matches for quite some time. This time, there's no escape... this time, you see just how CRAZY Scotty Michaels can be..
(Michaels scrapes his face on the fence, turns around... and we see blood coming off of his forehead...)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Maybe I'm not as normal as I thought I was. You've brought out a side in me that the WFW has never experienced before. Spirit of NGEN, I take the championship.
(FADEOUT, as Michaels kicks the fence and walks out of the view of the camera...) Manson — 2 August 2003 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, in black leather jacket and leather pants, sitting in a darkened areans, within a steel cage. He sits up against the cage, his back against the wire mesh, because everyone hates the barred cage anyway.))
MANSON: Here you are, telling me I'm right and everything..and yet, Scott..it's so hollow. Unfulfilling. Like having sex with an overweight woman and having to do all the work yourself. It feels as if, I've done nothing.
Now, I've received something, since you so aptly described the type and specifications of the cage match for me. Not to mention that its MY MATCH.....which leads me to think that you've been talking to some people, perhaps the wrong kind of people, but it is of small importance now.
I'm happy I've gotten you to admit to your own faults as a human being. I'm happy that you're able to realize the intricate complexities of the hypocrises of this industry. I'm even happy that you want to make me bleed and that you're telling me ahead of time..as if I've never bleed before.
But bleeding is like rain, sometimes it happens. Still, there's this void...I should be even happier with your obsession to prove your manhood by locking yourself within a metal cell with the most dangerous and possibily cannibalistic man in North America...that you're basically clinging to a cross and asking me to bring the nails...but it's not enough.
No, it's not. After the ladder match,you were basically an after-thought to Scott. Already beaten in a match you had no place in to begin with..sure you tried to hurt my knee....but that didn't work out for you. (Maybe you should think these master plans more, by the way.)
But you're a persistent lad, indulging in a one-sided attraction. But unlike Michael Douglas in one of his signature roles, I'm going to have to cut you at the knees now, perhaps literally. Because you need to know what happens when you stalk MIKE MANSON...when you try to think that you could possibly think the thoughts of MIKE MANSON....or even think of things he couldn't.
So now, it's not really about the title, that's there and a motivating factor of course, but there's my image to be concerned with. And of course, an example never hurts. I'm pretty sure I'll bleed in the cage, normally do in this type of match...and you will too of course. But I won't stop there.
Even if you're drinking and lapping up your own blood, I won't even stop there. No, it will only end when I have brutalized you to the point where your mind snaps and believes that I murdered your parents which causes you to dress up as Batman to seek revenge.
You already have the tights. And..I already..have the cage..MY CAGE..as you pointed out. My match..my world....and you think you can stop me within my country? Whether you are insane, sick, or experiencing some form of super-sanity, I really don't care.
What matters is that you're taking a walk into the darkest, most bloody corner of this entire industry, by your own accord. The one place other wrestlers don't even speak about when giving their promos and bravado.
I've called it the Torture Chamber...others have less creative names. But here, in this place....names do not matter...not even really blood.
I matter. Only me, though, and others learn that the air on other worlds is not always breathable. PaulNJ21 — 6 August 2003 Sounds good to me, you have your cage match against Manson. SCOTTY MICHAELS: Michael, I'm so glad you agreed to this match... and I'm so glad that the powers that be in the WFW decided that it was okay. Since the last time I've come to all of my fans in the WFW, I've been getting a ton of e-mail basically telling me I'm the STUPIDEST MAN ALIVE! That I'm writing my own death certificate by wanting to step into a steel cage with probably the craziest man alive, Michael Manson. That's all fine and well, but I'm doing this not out of stupidity... I'm doing this because I know I can win. I know I can take Michael Manson to a limit that he's never reached before. Since the day I first crossed paths with him, he's basically been telling me that I'm not on his level... and a majority of the fans, they've even turned their backs on me because they love the "entity" known as Mike Manson. I don't need them, never did. They don't need to cheer me because I take some of the sickest spots in the history of this business, like Michael Manson. I'll tell you what, they may not like me... but they sure do respect me.
(Michaels begins to pace..) SCOTTY MICHAELS: Look at it, Manson. Look at the ratings, and notice that I am still the Viewer's Choice. I'm still the man that the people want to see. My intention going into the Spirit of NGEN is bigger than Mike Manson and the North American championship. My goal is for the people to be talking about the Manson-Michaels match for years to come. We're going to steal the spotlight right out from under Silver and the world heavyweight championship match. Truth be told, we should be fighting for the big prize, Manson. We should be in it to win it. But, I'll settle for the North American title for now.... hey, why not, right? It's just about a week away... until we're locked up in a steel cage... NO ESCAPE, just pure HELL. We're going to be locked up in this cage like a couple of animals, and that's just the way I like it. It's come to this because it needed to..
(Michaels hits his hands off the cage a few times, and continues pacing...) SCOTTY MICHAELS: Just about a week away, and I step into your world, Manson. A world of blood, pain, and TORTURE... the only problem is, it's not going to be you dishing it all out this time. Many people back down from the cage match with you, Manson... they don't even want to bring it up. Me, I look forward to the chance to take you on in a match that you enjoy so much... in a match that you are so great at. I've never seen Hell before, not many people have. I've felt Hell, but probably not to the point at which I will feel it at the Spirit of NGEN. Michael Manson, I'm not only going to take your title... I'm going to take your pride, in your world... and there's not a damn thing you can do to stop it...
(FADEOUT..) Manson — 7 August 2003 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, sitting backstage in front of a fenced cage wall. He is sitting Indian-style in black jeans and plain black t-shirt, a Dracula pez dispenser in his hand.))
MANSON: No, Scott, I must tell you, by official standards, you are not "crazy". You see, currently, one of the official definitions of insanity is repeating the same actions over and over and expecting different results. Now, while you may be ego-centric enough to qualify as a sociopath, you are not officially "insane".
Now, being trapped in an early mid-life crisis and having a death wish, possibly, but not crazy.
By virtue of requesting a cage match, you are not repeating your earlier actions, not trying to beat me in a ladder rematch or anything of the sort. It also implies that you must have some sort of plan in effect since you're basically walking to your death otherwise.
And that's how I succeed, Scott, its not being able to take punishment even though I can take a Catholic's amount of it. Its not even being more psychotic or violent than the next guy. Its being driven, its having a plan, and the will to execute that plan, to follow through on it.
Its the ability, the mindset to be able to do the thing no one else will do. Some people call it a crime, some call it a trainwreck, and others justify it. It doesn't matter that much to me, as long as it works.
To beat me, to take my title and maybe my pride, Scott, you have to think like me, like Mike Manson. You have to do the thing that I wouldn't do and I don't even know what that is. Then again, its not on me to think of that.
You don't have to be crazy for this, perhaps sick, perhaps creative, imaginative, and most likely inhuman. How far are you really willing to go, Scott?
As we learned before, just hurting my knee isn't going to end me. Telling me that you're the Viewer's Choice and that you have fans means even less to me. Do you really think any type of mindgame is going to bother me? Of all people, you think I would care? Do you really want to get anywhere near to inside my head?
But that's the irony, to win, you have to, don't you? It is my match, or maybe I'll have to make it my match again. Maybe that's the reason I'll do the things I'll do. Part of the reason is to retain the title, of course.
As for the people, what I do, what we do is art. People love or hate it. I know why they would like me, myself and the audience being products of the cynical 90s. We grew up on nihilism together and now I'm their postmodern death god. Or something to that effect. Since anyway, its quite clear this whole Viewer's Choice thing went out years ago.
But that's why you're after me, to show you're still here, to show you're still for real. Its not over and all that. Except, I'm not the one to be trying such a thing. As I said, I'm sure you have a plan. So do I, I always have a plan.
One thing for certain, I never forgot Doc Silver. I never forgot that I said I'd make the North American title more valuable than the belt strapped around him. And through your obsession, I'm succeeding. You obsess over me and my title as a marker, the title goes up.....and at the same time....I'm going to have to make an example.
Of you, of others like you...like Rat Fink..like Gideon....like anyone else who thinks they can make their name off me. Don't put your head under the guillotine because you want the attention. The blade is bound to fall.
Plans within plans within plans....you know.
