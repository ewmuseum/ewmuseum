WHO? vs L.O.V.E

28 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[name withheld] — 28 April 2004 (CUEUP: 'Busted In The Hood by Cypress Hill') B REAL from CYPRESS HILL singing to a black background WELLLLLLLLLLLLLL... here's a little story, I got to tell About a thugged out homey, I know so well It started way back, when I was a teen In the hood, on the corner, slangin dope to fiends Had the little stash that could serve your need You can do what you want but I got weed for me Ridin for my fam, tryin to hit a grand CRASH unit on my ass, now I'm in demand One lone-ly fami-ly I be All in the hood, I got no-bo-dy The sun is beatin down on my red dark hat Pigs are roamin through the alley so I gotta toss my gat Forgot about the sack, lookin for a place to hide If they ask me for my name - f**k 'em, I'ma lie!" (CUTTO: L.O.V.E logo in hot neon pink........the logo twitches on and off from pink/to black..........the logo catches on fire.......camera fades out to Jared Wells inside of a limo laid back by himself with B.A.D World Heavyweight title sitting in his lap. He is wearing the night life attire with a few bandages over his forehead)
JARED WELLS: What do you people want to believe? Wake up every morning believing everything is OK? Believe that your role model is really a great guy? All of the sudden you wake up in the middle of the night......lost......scared. Everything you believed in, everything you thought the person you looked up to was not really what he was. In fact, he was so REAL.......it hurt you. Point the fingers, look around like nothing happened. The fact of the matter is in April of the year 2004 I made history. Not only for myself but for B.A.D, and the WFW. Long story short here......I made a comeback in 2003 of January.....people said "NO! this guys sucks, he's washed up". Year later......I became the new B.A.D World Heavyweight Champion...........enough said.
Instead of gloating in front of all......I have resurrected the title out of the ruins of WFW. The once B.A.D company that said hey, your services are no longer required....they said Jared we have no room for you. B.A.D of the past is no longer! Everyone that was associated with B.A.D in the day can suck my <BLEEP>. They never gave me that chance. L.O.V.E. gave me that chance. L.O.V.E defines chaos....freedom. Who'd ever thought that guys like myself, Anarky, Felix Red, and Psycho on the same page? Nobody. Anarky, Felix Red, Psycho, Jared Wells equals HOLY <BLEEP>! These guys are <BLEEP> crazy. Then again you people run through the world thinking about later.
Scotty Michaels.......you dare to jump L.O.V.E? You dare to show your true colors? Masked man was cute, too bad your no threat. Anytime, anyplace, L.O.V.E will leave you like Pacino with a SCAR on your face. Evil lurks in the minds of mad men........the WFW will have to call L.O.V.E to put in work. Have you ever thought about the future? About how your born to die?
(Wells laughs in the background with the B.A.D title over his shoulder) Do you see the light in the sand Scotty Michaels? DO YOU? You sit there and show your <BLEEP> thinking that your the man now because you happen to appear in our conversation. Conversations kill my friend. Your only so far away from your goals. You drew the line.......L.O.V.E will be there to erase it.
(FADEOUT)
