They just needed something to rally around...something that was different. Trouble was, the concept of anarchy became so popular with dumb middle-schoolers, it stopped being something different. At first it was just retarded, but soon it became both retarded AND trendy...exactly what these so-called anarchists were REALLY against. 'Course, I was never in that culture, but damn if I didn't have to hear about it all...the...g(BLEEP)...damn...time. And, being one of the guys who realized how dumb the concept was right from the beginning, I just got sick of it. It started out as a cause, something that, if nothing else, stood out. But it didn't take long for it to become a clichè with no real meaning.
(He chuckles) Copycat: So how ironic that the wrestler by the same name - albeit with a different spelling - would follow the same pattern. Starting out as a powerful, if contrived, force...and deteriorating into a clichè. All the fear Anarky once brought to the game has been replaced by annoyance. The deadly hornet replaced by a buzzing gnat. Use whatever analogy you want. Just like the kids who once put all their faith in the CONCEPT of anarchy, those in the wrestling business who once saw the WRESTLER Anarky as an unstoppable force now just see his name as a buzzword on the promotional billboard. The difference, of course...
(The word "ANARCHY" fades out. A moment later, a large picture of Anarky's face appears in the background, looking intense as always)
Copycat: ...Is that while the concept of anarchy is just that...a concept...the person Anarky is capable of his own conscious thought. And while those who once respected him now hate his everpresence, HE certainly hasn't noticed it. To himself, he is still the center of the wrestling universe. It's obvious in everything he says, everything he does. He speaks as though everyone evaluates their place in the world relative to his. And his actions...he comes in and immediately considers himself the league's top draw. None of that piddly "paying dues" stuff. He just comes in and thinks he'll start right at the top. That could have been the case in the past, true. Nowadays, though...it's just not the way it works anymore. Still, to him, every day he spends in wrestling is another syndicated episode of the smash hit...
(Copycat turns around in his chair. He wears jeans, his trademark beret, and an old WWL Anarky T-shirt with duct tape tactfully obscuring the league logo. Copycat: ...The Anarky Show! (He gives an empty laugh) Copycat: Like the shirt? You'd be SURPRISED how little they go for on Ebay these days...but I digress. Fact is, Anarky, back when you were one of the top guys in this business, you decided that you were simply too good to be part of the world of wrestling with us mere mortals, and you created your OWN world just to show how much better you are. You're still in that world, Anarky, but it's gotten a lot smaller. The Anarky Show got canceled from the major networks years ago, and you're stuck in reruns on TV Land, between "Who's the Boss?" and "The Golden Girls." And you are just BLISSFULLY unaware of it.
(He leans back in his chair) Copycat: I ignored your pointless tirade against Michael Manson...you want to distract yourself, go crazy, I won't stop you. You spent so much time convincing me that you were such an unstoppable force in the wrestling industry, and I was just a worthless peon...and then I beat you. I beat you in your last WWL match. You backed out of the league after that...one in a moderate-sized line of people who did the same after I beat them. And every time you get reminded of that, it's going to be a little more painful, a little harder to recover from. To you, it was a defining battle, but to me, you were nothing more than a stepping stone.
(He shakes his head) Copycat: I don't need to mock you, Anarky. I hate to say a line that's so overused in this business...but you do a good enough job yourself. Nobody watches the Anarky Show because they think it's GOOD anymore. They watch it because the self-mockery is so entertaining. You want to reference Jihad? You want to convince everyone you're fighting a holy war? You want to save the wrestling business from itself?
(Copycat rips off his Anarky mask and tosses it aside) Copycat: Wake up! This business doesn't need to be saved from itself. It needs to be saved from people like you. People whose glory days are long past them, and who don't understand that they can't just pop in anywhere they want and overshadow everyone else there. You say you don't care about championships, but I know better. You came into WFW expecting no less than to become its World Heavyweight Champion. Not just because you think you can beat anyone here with one hand tied behind your back, but because you think it's your divine RIGHT just for being you. You don't say it out loud, but I've seen it before in others, and I can see it in you. Well guess what? It's only going to take one loss to bring you down again. All I have to do is beat you at Merrython, and you'll be pushed yet another step closer to REALITY.
(He grins) Copycat: And believe me, Anarky...I am NOT about to let you get on any grander an ego trip than the one you're on. Jerking you out of your own little world and back into the one you belong in won't be easy. But if there's anyone smart enough to do it...it's the Smartest Player in the Game. Come Merrython, Anarky, I WILL reintroduce you to reality, one way or another. And that, my deluded friend...is just all there is to it.
