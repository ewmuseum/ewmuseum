RStrawsma — 28 November 2004 SCENE BEGINS (We fade in on a late afternoon setting out in the Texan plains. Standing alone at a bench and a sign marking a bus stop along a barren two-lane road that stretches across the tan landscape is a man in denim. He wears plain carpenter jeans and a similar jacket over a black Killswitch Engage t-shirt. His short hair is unkempt and his jaw bears a five o’clock shadow. Over his shoulder he carries a duffel bag that presumably contains his wrestling gear. He looks tired… weathered even, as he waits on a ride.)
Rocko Daymon Well… my debut in World’s Finest Wrestling has been made. I can’t say I’m happy to have lost at Guerra Interminable—and worse yet, outlasted by John Doe—but being screwed over by guys like Anarky and Felix Red is nothing I’m not already used to.
To be honest, I wasn’t happy in the first place about being booked into a meaningless feud between a pair of individuals who have nothing to do with me. Team Hart lost, in spite of our combined efforts, although I’m hesitant in using the term “team”, as none of us really worked together toward anything.
(He glances up the road to see if anything is headed his way, but appears slightly dismayed to see that the horizon is unchanged since he looked last, whenever that was.)
Rocko Daymon I’m not about to start pointing fingers on the cause of our failure, nor am I eager to dismiss the FACT that we lost because of underhanded tactics employed by the members of Team Anarky, but I know why I was eliminated. I was ALLOWED to be pinned in that ring by my teammates… the same way Thirteen was ALLOWED to fight on his own in the final minutes of the match. When I look back at the way Anarky pinned me in the ring, I think of how I could have been saved… how someone from my side could have broken the pin before the third count was made…
But perhaps I’ll think twice the next time I’m required to put my faith into a total stranger.
(With a gruff clearing of his throat, he shrugs slightly to himself, kicking the dirt at his feet with a worn workman’s boot.)
Rocko Daymon The only thing I can do at this point is move on. Now, instead of finding myself randomly placed on one side of a meaningless battle, I’m given an opportunity to stand out and make something for myself. At Ghoulish Games, it’s Four Faces of Fear against Doe, Golem, and Hart. Winner gets a shot at Psycho for the WFW North American Title…
(His experienced face cracks a heart-warming smile. You can almost sense a twinkle in his deep brown eyes.)
Rocko Daymon My, that’s quite a generous gift. Perhaps I should be thanking the guys upstairs for this kind of opportunity. It’s not every day you walk into a strange fed practically off the street and get something this good after competing in a single match. I suppose that even though I failed to step above my teammates in the Torneo Cibernetico, I’ve at least earned this by proving myself in the ring.
It doesn’t matter that all this is a shot at a shot… nor does it matter how insignificant the WFW North American Title might be in the eyes of some others. Lately, I’ve been looking forward to expand my collection of titles throughout a variety of federations, and marking my territory in WFW would stand as a great personal triumph.
I guess only four men stand in my way, and I have the opportunity of dealing with three in a single night.
(Finally breaking away from his rigid posture, he takes a few steps back and seats himself on the dusty bench, setting his bag nearby. He clears his throat and continues.)
Rocko Daymon When it comes to Shawn Hart, I see a man who doesn’t look beyond himself. He stood at the helm of our team at Guerra Interminable, and failed to act as a sufficient leader. He only came to that match to fight his own battles, instead of bringing his team for victory. I really can’t admire that kind of ego-centralism.
He practically refused to stand up for his teammates when needed, and that only reveals his weakness and self-absorption. I’m sure flamboyance has its perks, but I’m in this league to win; I won’t let somebody like Shawn Hart stand in my way.
And Golem? The guy gets around through several feds, and I’ve seen his stuff every now and then. But wherever he is, he doesn’t stand out from the regular crowd of mid-carders. He seems to be perfectly comfortable nestled in the unnoticed cluster of run-of-the-mill gimmicks and jobbers that make up the bulk of the dispensable part of the roster. Sure, he earned his spot to be in this match… but only because he lasted that much longer than the other guys who got eliminated earlier on. The man is canon-fodder; his purpose in this match will serve to be nothing more than an easy warm-up.
Which brings us to John Doe… (He pauses slightly, then chuckles, absolutely bemused by some internal pondering.) Rocko Daymon Heh, if I had all the time in the world, I don’t think I could ever stop talking about this guy.
I’ll say this as simple as I can: John Doe characterizes weakness. He is a weak man, with a weak mind and weak personality. He practically ran away from me in the Torneo Cibernetico whenever I was tagged in, and actually pandered me off to Anarky in what would be my elimination duel. It disgusts me, knowing I was outlasted by John Doe…
But even so, he has yet to pin my shoulders to any ring surface in any federation. Yet this guy has made quite a habit of talking trash about me without even knowing who I am or what I’ve been through.
I think, at Ghoulish Games, John Doe will be subjected to a few lessons in schooling… to help him understand that your mouth is best kept shut when on the subject of Rocko Daymon.
(On the horizon appears the silver shape of a greyhound bus. Daymon sees this and comes to his feet, gathering his things. With a little more time before its arrival, he turns to the camera again.)
Rocko Daymon I look at this match, and I see a Narcissist, a Second-Rate, and a Dope. I am the only man worthy of facing Psycho, and I am the only one worthy of taking the belt he now holds.
At Ghoulish Games, I intend to step into that ring, successfully out-wrestling all three of my opponents and showing everybody that in a match where I have something to gain, my determination will lead me there.
I have nothing to lose… (The bus completes its trip, coming to a stop. The door opens, and two men get off—one a few inches shorter than Daymon, wearing a Colts jersey, and the other gargantuan in size, towering over many average men. Rocko passes them as they disembark and boards the bus. The camera follows him as he shows the driver his ticket and moves to the back, taking a seat next to an odd, dirty-looking fellow with long greasy hair, a soiled green shirt, and an unusual red mark spread on his face.)
Hitchhiker Hey-hey… d’you likes Head Cheese? Rocko Daymon …get the hell away from me. (Fade to black.)
