WFW Gates of Hell: "The Wolf" Chris McMillan vs. Sugawara

20 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 20 April 2003 RP/angles are due April 30th, post your promos here! Mister Dread — 20 April 2003 ::FADEIN on the WFW banner. "The Wolf" Chris McMillan stands there, attired as usual in jeans, harness boots, and a "Whatever" t-shirt under a heavy motorcycle jacket. His eyes are wild, and there's a big grin plastered over his face::
THE WOLF: World's Finest Wrestling, baby! And where else would you expect me, the world's FINEST wrestler, to pop up? What better home for the greatest mat technician EVER to grace a wrestling ring? What grander theater for the FUTURE of professional wrestling to ply his trade? Why, right here, of course!
::The Wolf begins to pace, fidgeting with the sleeves on his jacket:: THE WOLF: Man, I'm excited. For the first time in a long time, I feel good. There's a lot of old faces here. Lotsa guys I've wrestled, lotsa guys I've seen in the locker room. But you know what? I don't think I've ever seen my opponent for my debut match in the locker room. Kind of a bummer, eh? Oh, I know who she is. Without mentioning other promotions, I've had a brush or two with some of her associates. But come on, guys. A woman? I mean, I don't have a problem wrestling women. If they wanna compete with the guys, that's all the same to me. Equal oppertunity, and all that. But do you HONESTLY think Aitsu Sugawara can hold her own with me? Oh sure, she's puroresu, I know. One of the most effective and dangerous styles of wrestling in the world. But guess what? I've done more than my fair share of time in a Japanese ring. I know my way around that ring in and out, up and down, side-to-side, sixty-nine ... well, nevermind that. The point is this: I'm sure Aitsu can hold her own, but she's dealing with a completely different animal in the Wolf. She ain't ready. NOBODY here is ready, and that's the way it always is.
::The camera comes in close:: THE WOLF: The eternal underdog, the little man, the no-consequence, no impact, non-factor Chris McMillan. That's how they see me. That's how they've always seen me, and every time I go out there I've got to prove them different. Sometimes I'm successful. Other times not so successful. But no matter what, when you come out of a match with me, you know you've been in a fight. And you know that you were just one step away from being pinned at any given moment. Now, I ain't trying to detract from my opponents ... well okay, I guess I am. I ain't never ... and I mean EVER been pinned clean. Oh sure, I've lost ... but ain't nobody had the right combination of guts, determination, willpower, intellect, and damn downright SKILL to beat me clean in the center of the ring. And do you know why? It's simple; nobody, and I mean NOBODY is better than me. Plain and simple. Faster, sure. Stronger? Of course. Bigger? Just about every time. But BETTER? Not on your life, Chester.
::The Wolf's voice drops down low:: THE WOLF: How are you gonna deal with that, Aitsu? Are you gonna try to pin me? Are you gonna cheat? Are you gonna employ the services of some hired mercenary to take me out? I've seen it all before, sugar. Sometimes I get caught, outmaneuvered by backstage covert ops, so to speak. Sometimes I come out on top of the situation. But I always, ALWAYS forge ahead. On to the next battle. On to the next victory ... or the next defeat. No matter how glib I might come off at times, each and every match is a momentus occasion for me. A life-or-death struggle to stay on the top of the food chain. It's a quest, girl, a grand adventure. And MY adventure starts here in WFW ... with you.
::FADE TO BLACK::
