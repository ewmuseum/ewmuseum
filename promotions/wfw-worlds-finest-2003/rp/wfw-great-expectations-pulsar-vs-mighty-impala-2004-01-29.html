PaulNJ21 — 29 January 2004 The RP/Angle deadline is Wednesday, February 11 11:59 EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
stuclif — 3 February 2004 FADE IN: CAMERA APPROACHES PULSAR WITH THE IMPRESSION THAT THE CAMERA MAN HAS STARTED WALKING QUICKLY AS PULSAR IS SHOWN GETTING IN A CAR ABOUT TO LEAVE, WEARING CASUAL CLOTHES WITH A TRAINING BAG OVER HIS SHOULDER.
CAMERA MAN: Pulsar, Pulsar...... hold on a second, we just want to get a quick word. PULSAR: (LOOKS THE CAMERA MAN UP AND DOWN) Bout what? CAMERA MAN: (SUCKING IN THE AIR) Well....... what are... your.... thoughts on your three-way dance at Mind Over Matter?
PULSAR: (LOOKS AND SOUNDS ANGRY) Did you watch the damn match? (CAMERA NODS FOR YES) You watched the match and you want to my thoughts on the match? (CAMERA NODS AGAIN) (PULSAR STARTS TO BREATHE QUICKLY) I got suplexed of the damn apron onto the chairs in the front row. I was unconscious for the second time in as many matches and I still lost. How do you think I feel? (ALL THE MUSCLES CAN BE SEEN TENSING UP THROUGH PULSARS CLOTHES)(HE STARTS TO BREATHE DEEPLY AND TALK AGAIN, CALMLY) You know, before that match I was walking around here saying that the I was getting the s### out of my game and that the focus was back, just like when I won those 6-man tags, that was focus, thats what you need to succeed around here. I know that and I still blew it. I took my eye of the ball and I just left myself hanging out there like Janet Jacksons nipple.
CAMERA MAN: And now you have a match against The Mighty Impala.... PULSAR: Never met him, never seen him, but I'm gonna have to tear him apart. I've fallen, but I'm not going to be left here flat on my arse, I owe it to the people out there that cheer for Pulsar, I owe it to the company that put its faith in me and most of all I owe it to myself. Impala, your timing couldnt have been worse, come Great Expectations I will be be pulling myself out of this hole and in doing so I going to blow the roof of the WFW and anybody that stands in my way. Whether your "mighty" or a special guest of (SARCASTICALLY) the honorable Alex Wylde, if you stand on the opposite side of the ring to me you will feel yourself crushed, you will feel the Black Hole. Now without any further delay, cue the excitement.
OORP Had to throw the nipple in there somewhere GreggG — 5 February 2004 (CUT TO: The Mighty Impala, standing in front of the multi-colored flag of Cote d'Ivoire. TMI is wearing a multi-colored robe poncho-style, his nose turned upwards in an air of dignification, his blonde ponytail angled backwards.)
TMI: (his voice accented in a high-pitch) Pulsar, my young opponent... there is one thing and one thing only that you got right in your interview. I am indeed an invited guest of this league's president, Mister Alex Wylde.
(Impala turns towards the camera, pursing his lips.) Aside from that, you proved yourself to be like all of the rest of the scoundrels and neer-do-wells who perform in this sport-- an uneducated, unread ignoramous. You are nothing more than a common peasant, whereas I am a young man of grace and prestige. My royal heritage dates back several thousands of years, to a time when AF-REE-CA was the leader of the world's civilization, when my DIRECT ANCESTORS ruled on the throne of the ENTIRE PLAH-NET.
I am a man of prestige, Pulsar. I am a man of wealth and means, Pulsar. In COTE DIH-VWOIS... if a peasant such as yourself were to talk to your superior with your tone, you would be dragged by your feet to the village square and PELTED with expired foot before you would be ceremoniously beaten by our regime's POLICE AGENCY. Now, I know that I am no longer in my HOMELAND-- the place where the gazelle run free-- but I am still a man of MEANS.
I also do not appreciate your threats of man-rape against me, Pulsar. I will not, ever, feel your black hole, despite your promises. I know that in this country-- a country of undignified swine who push their will upon the rest of the world, a country without TRUE leaders, except for President Alex Wylde-- it has become much discussed about allowing men to marry other men. In my country, homosexuality is regarded as a SIN and a crime against the law of the nature, a crime against the bubbling brook and mountain creek. So, your threat of me feeling your black hole not only is a crime against mankind, it is also an undignified threat against a man of royal heritage,
For your words and misdeeds and AHC-SHUNS against myself, I will do what is done to peasants in my home village. I shall publicly give you both EMBARRASSMENT and a BEATING. Pulsar, when you leave the wrestling ring after facing me, you will feel two things that The Mighty Impala shall never, ever feel in his lifetime-- that is personal SHAME and PHYSICAL PAIN.
Pulsar, my dimwitted COMMON PEASANT of an opponent... come our match, your name and face will be dragged in the dirt.. You shall kiss that ground and be honoured doing so... for it is the ground that The Mighty Impala walks on. (FTB)
stuclif — 6 February 2004 FADE IN: REGULAR DIMLY LIT GYM, PULSAR SITTING AT A DESK, LOOKS TO BE DEEP IN THOUGHT. PULSAR: You know, I was doing the usual workout, with the TV on, for a change, when I heard my opponent at Great Expectations, The Mighty Impala, spew words from his mouth that really left me thinkin, and I mean thinkin hard. (HOLDS HIS RIGHT HAND OUT IN FRONT OF HIM, AS IF WEIGHING SOMETHING) Is this guy serious in thinking that he will embarrass me in our match.... or (HOLDS OUT HIS LEFT HAND) does this guy have a few screws loose upstairs. What I'm saying is that I've heard life in those rich, snobby schools can mess with a guys head, it was probably one those all boy schools, (CRINGES) thats even worse, poor fella. That explains why he thought I wanted to moleste him, it was only a reflex after being picked on by all those (SOUNDS SCARED) bigger boys. Whatever this guy was thinking, he still managed to piss me off, his second mistake, his first was excepting the match.
PULSAR GETS OUT OF THE CHAIR AND POINTS AT THE CAMERA, STARTING TO BECOME AGITATED PULSAR: You see Impala, where I come from, and here in America, people have the right to say what they want, without the fear of having the class above them stomping them back into place. This means that come Great Expectations, despite your upbringing and however much money you have, this will be a fair and even match...... (SMIRKS) that is until I pound your head into the mat over and over again (LAUGHS TO HIMSELF). One thing that I will do for you though, as a favour not as a servent, is............ CUUUEE THE EXCCITEMENT!!!!
GreggG — 9 February 2004 (CUT TO: The Mighty Impala, standing in his tribal robes, in front of the flag of Cote D'Ivoire. Standing next to him, in his power suit and tie, is "Mr. Middle Management" Mike McGee.)
TMI: McGee, myself and the esteemed Alex Wylde have hired you to do a job for us. That job was to rid ourselves of a THORN in our side.
(McGee's head is down as The Mighty Impala points his finger in his face.) TMI: Pulsar's presence in this sport is one that infuriates BOTH of us. The more he does not get defeated, the more he is not routinely humiliated, the more he catches on with the poor people of this world that we call wrestling fans. The more popular he becomes, the more of a threat he becomes to Alex Wylde's presidency-- and the more of a threat he becomes in my attempts at showing this country that men of superior lineage WILL BE your wrestling heroes.
McGee: I'm sorry, I'm sorry. I know I-- (TMI slaps McGee in the face. He just sits there and takes it, head bowed.) TMI: Your apology is meaningless. In my homeland of... COAT-UH DIHVOIR... a person who fails a member of a royal family gets PUNISHED. A person who MAKES A PROMISE and swears that he will be able to CRUSH his foe but does not DO AS HE SWEARS on the horn of the anteh-lope MUST BE SHAMED. (Pause.) McGee-- look at me now!
(McGee looks up. TMI kicks him in the mid-section. McGee falls to his hands and knees.) TMI: Now, go scurry off like the INSECT that you are. Make sure you have my file on Pulsar complete by my training time this afternoon, and then go see President Wylde and see if he needs any help around his office, you human ant. (McGee crawls off.)
TMI: Pulsar, I have warned you on several occasions about what happens when my commands are not met. You have repeatedly threatened myself-- A MAN OF FAMILY DIGNITY-- and have not bowed your head while addressing a man of my lineage, while also insinuating that it is *I* and not yourself who wishes to participate against the will of the mountain creek and participate in homosexual ahc-shuns.
Pulsar, it has come to my attention that you come from the nation of Australia-- a nation which was founded to be the home of England's lower THIEVES and BARNACLES. Pulsar, it is good to know that human genetics do not EVOLVE as fast as the rest of this planet. It is good to know that I, The Mighty Impala, shall always be better than the world that surrounds him for his ancestors were KINGS... and it is good to know that you will always be a grovelling COMMON PEASANT whose ancestors attempted to peddle false gold shillings.
Pulsar, in a few weeks time, you shall end up just like my faithful manservant... crawling on the ground before me, your face equal to my boots. You shall KISS the ground that I walk upon and be honoured doing so... for it is the ground that I, The Mighty Impala, walks upon. (FTB)
