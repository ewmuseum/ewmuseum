Almost Live: Manson vs. Doc Silver

8 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 8 September 2003 RP here. Winner of the match gets a WFW World Heavyweight Title shot at the PPV. The RP/Angle deadline is Thursday, September 18th 11:59 PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
Manson — 9 September 2003 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, dressed in undiscernible black, sits in a darkened corner of a locker room, his face and most of his time shaded. He sits, one leg stretched out and the other tucked under, his hand resting together on his mid-section.))
MANSON: And so I said before, that I was tired. Tired of everyone using me to elevate themselves, as if I were a whore that could be bartered for the night. Then, there's that saying, where good things come if you wait. And I was patient, waiting out my time, taking on the middle-aged and absurd, with a title I wanted to make more important despite never wanting, until I lost that. Then a week later, I finally get what I want, sort of.
And finally, its Doc and me, this time without Shawn Hart, who before ruined whatever chance, whatever dream I had then. I still get that title shot after this, yes, but its separated, and not the same. Still Shawn Hart in where he should not be, but never what I wanted.
I didn't even get the chance to make the pin to earn this, but now that its here, like the North American title, I'll take it. There's another saying, a belief,that if you wait long enough, everything comes back to you.
There's no ladder, but its the same territory, and really, the first time, there wan't a title on the line either. Just a promotion I never really cared about in the first place. But its my selfish plotting that makes the kids think I'm so cool.
((MANSON stops, looks down.)) So here we are. Should be the main event, could have been, would have been..but it doesn't matter now. I toiled for this, for other things, and now I'm just tired. The type that sleep doesn't help, not that I'm able to sleep that much to begin with.
All the same, its Silver/Manson for a title shot. I was disappointed in you for a while, Doc. First you lost to someone who actually called himself 'Psycho' and now, Hart. I thought for a moment, when you pushed me aside to gain that pin, to set this match, that you might be coming back, recovering, and all that. Even though, one may argue I might have talked...or convinced..Wylde to behave in such a way to set that up....or maybe I'd just take credit for it.
Either way, I basically let you get the pin, since it didn't matter. But now, that's the last of you getting what you want. I started here, I came for the two things now within my reach. You and the WFW title. Together would have been better, but I will take what I can get.
Because this was the last time, I ever do anything for you. It might have been for the common good before, but now....now there's only me. You can say its always been only me and you wouldn't be wrong, but I always had that philanthropic streak in me, even if it came from that Machivellian side.
I toiled and finally, it has paid off. Not in the way I wanted, but that's life. And no one needs a lecture from me on the lack of equity in the world. I was tired, but soon, I'll be strong enough to rise.
In the end, I still have me and what, I suppose would be a dream, if I really have them. And the time has come to stop waiting, to stop being reactive, and make them my reality.
"I, who am poor, have only my dreams. I spread my dreams out under your feet. Tread softly because you tread on my dreams."
