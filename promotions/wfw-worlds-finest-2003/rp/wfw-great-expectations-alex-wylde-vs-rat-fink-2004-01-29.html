WFW Great Expectations: Alex Wylde vs. Rat Fink

29 January 2004

PaulNJ21 — 29 January 2004 The RP/Angle deadline is Wednesday, February 11 11:59 EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
EGarrett — 5 February 2004 AND NOW...A MESSAGE FROM YOUR WFW PRESIDENT... INT. PRESIDENTIAL OFFICE Alex Wylde is behind his desk in his Jersey City Presidential Office. He's wearing a form-fitting black t-shirt and hanging up the phone.
WYLDE "I don't have much to offer you in terms of threats at the moment. But I can tell you that it feels good to finally have my ultimate goal back in my sights. That being the WFW World Title...which has yet to lose it's luster to me despite the incredibly dull human being who now holds it. Thus now...I can continue on the path safe in the knowledge that the belt is heading home to where it is supposed to be.
But of course...there will be bumps in the road. In fact...(he produces a promotional picture of himself across from Rat Fink)...I'm staring at a particularly ugly one right now. Observe one Rat Fink ladies and gentlemen. This is a wrestler who was already signed when I took over this league's daily operations. Because...trust me...were he to apply with his current persona while I was in office...he'd walk away crying like an idiot 16-year-old on American Idol.
The unfortunate fact is that you're nearly useless, young man. How on earth am I supposed to sell Boris and Natasha's retarded cousin to rational adults? And how are you supposed to succeed on your wrestling talent when you try and grapple people while wearing a cape? It's really a shame...and if you doffed the stupidity you might make a fair enough cannon fodder type person. Fortunately, however...you're going to get plenty of practice at that at Great Expectations...so we can see how it turns out."
He picks the phone back up and starts dialing another number. FADEOUT. THE PRECEDING MESSAGE HAS BEEN PAID FOR BY YOUTH UNITED FOR ALEX WYLDE AND NEW FRONTIER WRESTLING.
ChrisHorowitz — 8 February 2004
(Cue up "Dweller on the Treshold" by Van Morrison.) (Fade into the Canvas Cathedral of Satan's Clause who stands in the middle of the aisle, microphone in hand, holy robes over his shoulders, and devoted subjects of God in the pews.)
Satan's Clause: Brothers in Christ, the devil has sent forth another soldier of darkness. Alex Wylde, the so called president of WFW has been sent by Lucifer as a last grab at the soul's of our nation.
If this Alex Wylde is anything like the president of the United States, you will know what I say is true! Our president George Bush is a godless communist who claims to speak for the lord when he refuses to do the lord's work. He will not convert the people of the Middle East to the way of our Lord God, his son Jesus Christ, and the Holy Spirit. He will not end the tyranny of communism in the United States, he will not end the tyranny of perverse imagery on television, nor the perverse sounds of music. Alex Wylde is much the same as our heathen dictator I suspect.
Does Alex Wylde show reverance to our lord? Does he debate each of his actions first, making sure each will help the goal of a worldwide theocratic state?
If you cannot answer that question honestly and come up with a yes, then you must rethink your entire life ever since that answer became a no. For if you are not working for the glory of god, you are working for Satan.
Alex Wylde, you are more than some of the worthless legions that the New Age Revolution has defeated. You can be more.
You can be a messanger for the word of god. An angelic knight in his service. Or you can be a meaningless thug in the employ of satan, a zombie, a traitor to all of humanity.
Is this how you want to lead your life? Do you want to be on the same side as the hompolitans, the metrosexuals, the Feminazis, all of the people who threaten the goal of a pure theocratic planet Earth? Of course you do not, you want as we all do to follow God's plan for us, and join in the crusade to whipe out such abominations from the surface of the planet. You like all good men want to make the world a place where we can each bow our heads and pray for God's blessing, and know that we have blessed the word of God with our actions, and thus his blessing will be returned to us. Imagine how much richer our lives will be on the glorious day when our world is what God has intended it, a glorious new eden!
But, you can ignore all of this, as all of those who have faced us have. You can simply allow the taint of Satan to continue to control you, and you can be plowed under by the forces of N.A.R. Is this how you want your presidency to end? To lay there battered, bruised... beaten. To see then that it was Satan who mislead you, not God? That God only wanted your service, God never wanted you to be injured, Satan did for his own perverse purposes. And it was because of Satan that the warriors of light were forced to take action against you.
Do not let the taint of Satan destroy you. Do not let it force the hand of the warriors of light and their gilded sword. Repent now before Satan can tighten his grasp on your life.
(Fade out)
