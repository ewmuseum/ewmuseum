WFW Vox Part Deux: Manson/Fink vs. Wylde/Pulsar

12 March 2004

EGarrett — 12 March 2004 AND NOW...A MESSAGE FROM THE MOST POWERFUL MAN IN WRESTLING... INT. WFW LOCKER ROOM Various wrestlers are leaving the Presidential Office. They've just congratulated Alex Wylde on winning the WFW World Title. "Mr. Middle Management" Mike McGee is dutifully mopping up champagne that's been spilled around the room. Alex Wylde is sitting on the edge of his desk...still in his wrestling gear...with the WFW World Title next to him. Elaine is ushering the last of the boys out.
ELAINE Thanks...thank you...thanks guys. Call some cabs before you kill somebody. Later. Wylde watches them go. Then looks down at the belt. WYLDE Well...now that that's taken care of...focus the camera because I've got plenty to say. First of all...you can't rule a league without good subjects...so I'd like to give a nod of thanks to the lovely Elaine (she nods)...Mike McGee (he gives a big smile and continues to mop)...and Youth United for Alex Wylde. May I never need your assistance...boys. They knew when to have my back and when to step aside and let me train in peace.
But now that that's taken care of...I can also announce that after NINE LONG YEARS IN WRESTLING. And after NINE LONG MONTHS in WFW...AMATEUR HOUR...is FINALLY...OVER. World's Finest Wrestling has suffered through an endless cavalcade of has-beens...wanna-be's and never-wills who have pretended to be worthy world champions. People who have not done justice to what this belt signifies. Men who's wrinkled bodies turned to dust whenever they tried to wrestle. Fools who stumbled into the belt and couldn't hold it. But like I've always said...this title is like the One Ring. It knew where it belonged from the moment Alex Wylde revealed himself as a member of this organization. Now...inevitably the belt and the seat of power has found it's proper holder.
Now...I am not ONLY the esteemed President of WFW. The intellectual engine that runs this league behind-the-scenes. But I am now the WFW WORLD CHAMPION...the leader in the RING as well. I SQUASHED MY OWN other champion on the card before last. It's a list of accomplishments that would take FAR too long to read as a nickname or introduction. So we can just shorten it from now on. If you want to address your President and Champion...you can just call me...
He points a finger at Elaine...who answers on on cue in her announcer voice... ELAINE THE MOST POWERFUL MAN IN WRESTLING. WYLDE It's just that simple. And now...I've chosen to entertain you WFW fans this next card with a throwback to the old days. TEAM competition...at its BLOODY finest. That's right...a little WAR GAMES action...where I get to advertise my cold and calculating heart. I will lead my partners to total victory over my eventual Superbowl opponent. And I intend to do it with STYLE. That's because in New Jersey we know all about gangs...brawls and organized violence. Even a technical wrestler like myself can whack somebody over the head with a jimmy stick. If you've watched the Sopranos it's just the beginning. My partners could not be any more perfect. Who better to serve this league's master than a man who calls himself MINION. And Pulsar's massive physique is inversely proportional to his mental prowess. I learned this from teaming with him long ago. He's the type of man who would run into a brick wall for a cause...and I intend to make him do just that for me when the time's right.
And the ultimate benefactor of this situation? Each and every one of you...the WFW fans...who get to watch the first edition of the NEW WFW. Written...Directed...Booked...and STARRING Alex Wylde. The Franchise Player. The Focal Point. Or...say it with me...
"THE MOST POWERFUL MAN IN WRESTLING." Wylde cracks his knuckles and watches as Elaine and Mike McGee pack up the office. FADEOUT. THE PRECEDING MESSAGE HAS BEEN PAID FOR BY WORLD'S FINEST WRESTLING AND YOUTH UNITED FOR ALEX WYLDE.
Manson — 13 March 2004 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON sits Indian-style on a stone bench in black leather pants and a black t-shirt. His hands are folded neatly on his lap and he looks forward.))
MANSON: Nine months...nine months....and what was it? Nine years before that...nine years and nine months..just about before Alex Wylde actually won a world title of any note or significance. I suppose I should be the one above all others to welcome you to humanity, but I don't really see the point.
I realize I haven't seen the point to a lot of things lately, but I mean it this time. You might be president for now..but that re-election is just weeks away and you haven't really done much to rise that voter spirit. And, of course, there is that title, the title that should actually have belonged to me since the day this promotion opened..not that I'm bitter.
But the wheel turns as they say..... And now I stand back at the top. It only took a store of red kryptonite pez and a demonic talk show, but I'm back. You of all people should know that. You might have acknowledged on air that you and Edmunds had deals worked out, business that the two of have gone over time and again, alliances and all that.
Also on air, mentioned once or twice, Alex, was the fact that while Edmunds might have been an associate, Mike McGee might be your lackey, and you might see the rest of the WFW roster as utter trash, but I'm your friend.
Or rather, I'm as much a friend as I can be. We were both into that whole chaos for the sake of chaos thing years back and since, we were in and out of dozens of promotions. The both of us together were banned from this entire circuit once.
And I was the one who helped you into the WFW, when you had burned every bridge, not knowing like I did, that you could starve a country but they'd still want you when you're in demand..I helped you become president.
You tried to help me..with Anarky...and a few other things...but not much else. As president, you've really been ineffective. Some people might have wondered why I haven't exploited my connection to the presidential office and I myself don't know the answer to that.
Maybe because despite all the years and talks, we don't turn our backs on each other. Maybe because we do actually know other, well enough to know what the other would do.
Or maybe Alex, you just crossed a line.....not in morality or decency because I don't care about that..but you crossed one of my lines....one of my plans....and you know for certain, that is unforgivable.
Granted, you didn't try to cost me the Rumble or the title shot, but still, all crimes need to be accounted for. Some things can only be taught with the use of a large stick. You'll learn to respect that. You'll learn that after all these years that when you cut a promo about me you do not refer to the only person in this entire industry that ever backed you up anonymously, as if I never mattered.
Because I am the number one contender, I am the plague that this industry has endured for years on end, and I was toppling benevolent regimes when you were getting cut up in the opening match of WOOG.
I am me, really, this is me. And you should know that. You should know that to have real power over anything, anything at all, is to be able to take something away. I never told you, but you should have caught on.
Think of the people in this prelude, we have Diablo who took my old face and name, Rat Fink who tried to make a name off me, now both being teamed with me. I don't like them, but I'll use them.
You have Minion who always really idoled me, Pulsar who as a younger roster member probably thinks he has to look up to me. Five people in the match besides me, Alex, and each of them has wanted to be me.
But this is just the start, we have time to go, time to teach and time to learn..and you might never have seen me across from you in a ring before but you already know that you should be afraid. Damn afraid.
Everyone tried to make their name off me this past year, everyone. But you will not. I'm tired of helping you, of giving.
It's time to take. stuclif — 14 March 2004 FADE IN: THE SO CAL RUMBLE FROM ROAD TO GLORY COMES ONTO SCREEN, THE SHOT PAUSES JUST AS PULSAR IS THROWN OUT BY THE IRON LION. CAMERA PANS OUT TO SEE PULSAR STARRING AT THE SCREEN.
PULSAR: (SMILING) Well, so much for the big plan. I think we'll just have to forget about that messy little rumble.
(LOOKS UP AT THE CAMERA) PULSAR: I'm still not sure what excuse to go with (JOKINGLY)... it was my first rumble maybe, or I could go with my early entry. Nah, just kiddin (NOW SERIOUS), I wont be making any excuses.... I was thown out first, put up a bit of a fight, but still thrown out first. Road to Glory was put to the back of my mind as soon as it was over, no point in dwelling on failures.
(PULSAR PICKS A REMOTE CONTROL UP OF A NEAR BY DESK AND TURNS OF THE TV) PULSAR: Time now to turn towards other options for putting my name into some sort of contention. A six-man street fight.... Is it just me or does the name Pulsar and the mention of three on three matches just seem to come together and generate a feeling of genuine... excitement (WINKS AT THE CAMERA), not only for myself but for the people at home and those who purchased their tickets, hoping to be part of history. For those who dont know what I'm talking about lets just say that through my short run here in the WFW, Pulsar and six-man matches have had a perfect relationship, a tradition that will remain true at Vox Part Deux. And for those who have been following my short career..... there are more surprises to come.
(PAUSES FOR A MOMENT) PULSAR: Now, the line-up for this match has some of the fiercest competitors on the WFW roster. Which means that this match is going to be one brutal and bloody affair, and with that next level in reach, it will be time to step up again and either put up or shut up. I had to step up when I arrived here in the WFW, I had to step up in my first six-man tag match and now its time take the next logical step to becoming a champion in this business.
I've heard a couple of the guys talking, and there's a couple of good points being put out there. Alex Wylde, firstly a huge up for your winning the WFW strap, the ultimate prize in the game, awesome party.. I need that keg back too, cheers. Alex, you also talked about giving the fans what they want, which is what we should be trying to do every time we step into that ring, so I cant disagree with you there. But what I can disagree with is if you think your going to be calling all of the shots at Vox Part Deux, you've got another thing coming to you. I'll put it all on the line for a cause, but thats not stupidity, thats pride, thats the will to win, thats loaylty, thats guts. Something I hope everyone will bring to the dance.
Who wants to be like Michael Manson? I do, well, sort of. I think that question can be answered in two parts. Manson, whether you like him or not is an icon in this business and has done it all... something every young wrestler should aspire to. But then you look at the sort of person he is and you use it as an example of the type of person what not to emulate.
With a match like this one anger can also drive a competitor to do great things, not the honorable feats that people idolise, but great things that include the dealing out such pain that not even hospital treatment will be able to relieve. And I'm lookin at you with this one Fink. You know why and payback is a *****. It'll be good to see you again.
(TAKES A MOMENT TO RELAX) I always tell myself not to talk about my matches to early, I just get myself too damn pumped. I'm new, I dont care. Come Vox Part Deux there'll be drive, there'll be strength, there'll be will.... so just cue the damn excitement!
Manson — 18 March 2004 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, in his new "Have You Taken MICHAEL MANSON As Your Savior?" t-shirt and leather pants, sitting in a lotus position on a bare mat in a basement lit with fluorescent candles and a cheap glowing disfigured Easter Bunny.))
MANSON: As usual, I find that I alone speak for my tag team, I alone am the only one with communication within my grasp. Or maybe I'm the only one who cares. But regardless, here I am.
As he normally does during the week, Alex has gone into a bear-like hiberation which we all can only hope helps the WFW administration. Minion, as usual, has likely locked himself in a room filled with pictures of me. Diablo is probably on the mexican version of ebay trying to sell the Mephisto mask. Rat Fink must be conquering or ruling an imaginary third world country.
But thankfully, Pulsar is about for me to converse with. It's not much of a conversation, but if I really wanted high-level dialogue I'd just have to talk to myself all the time, so I'll have to humor you.
Yes, I am aware that my achievements are normally things to be applauded and dreamed about. It's the method to my madness that you and so many others have complained about. That I'm not a good person, that I lack morality.
And you're right, but that doesn't matter. Because, Pulsar, you haven't been around like I have...you haven't met and bitten the people I've known.....been to and torched the places I've been.....you can still be naive. For as far as that'll take you.
Everyone in this industry doesn't have a closet of skeletons, but a fortress. We are a mass collective of perverts, fetishists, and addicts. I always knew this, so did Alex.
How do you think he got so many votes? While you may disagree with my method, Pulsar, don't forget that you align yourself with Alex Wylde who was my sidekick for many a venture. Someone who tries to emulate me, but in the end, falls short, for not being me.
Alex won the WFW title without interference or foulplay, but trust me at least on this, that that was a rare thing.
He's my friend and I'm fine with it. You're his supporter, but you take a hypocritical stance. You don't know the things I know, Pulsar, you haven't done the things I have.
That's all right. But never assume you're my moral superior again. People in glass houses you know.... And while you might enjoy the 6 man format, my method tends to go against all formulas and strategy.....and it may even deny you that success you've become accustomed to.
You might want Rat Fink....so did I for a while....but for this night...one night...he's my partner....my responsibility...so you'll have to wait. If he needs to be tortured, beatened, or locked in a cellar to be forgotten, I'll handle it.
I'll handle everything. ChrisHorowitz — 19 March 2004 (Fade into the head office of the New Age Revolution where Rat Fink stands in front of "THE WHEEL OF POSSIBLE OPPONENTS".)
Rat Fink: My friends, it is a wonderful day in the history of the New Age Revolution. Why do you ask? What could make this day so spectacular that it is not only a wonderful utterly realistic day, but also a wonderful utterly realistic day in the history of the glorious NAR Revolution? It is simple really. It has to do with the latest addition to the WHEEL OF POSSIBLE OPPONENTS.
That is right. Like so many game shows that fill your mind with surreal unreachable expectations, the NAR revolution has added a BONUS triangle to the WHEEL OF POSSIBLE OPPONENTS.
This BONUS TRIANGLE can only spell doom for Alex Wylde and his team mates... And possibly for the teammates of the NAR.
You ask what could be unleashed if the wheel lands on the BONUS TRIANGLE? Well it could be anything. Perhaps you will have to do battle with one of my MONSTERS.
(Cut to stock footage from "THE MOLE PEOPLE.") Or perhaps you will have to do battle with Professor Neon's DEATH RAY. (Cut to stock footage of Dr. Neon's DEATH RAY.) Or perhaps the result will be something even more horrific... EVEN MORE DEADLY. EVEN MORE... evil. Only fate knows, and your fate is in the hands of THE WHEEL OF POSSIBLE OPPONENTS.
Alex Wylde, you and your peons will have to face this fate, as will pathetic fools such as Diablo. He, like you and your surreal warriors of gold and silver will not be spared by my creations. My army will not have mercy, it will never stop, and it will never surrender.
I'm sure you, the soldiers of the surreal see this threat in front of you and now quake in your boots. You wish that this could not be. That the WHEEL OF POSSIBLE OPPONENTS did not have this option. But the wheel does, and the option could very well be your future.
So now, let us spin THE WHEEL OF POSSIBLE OPPONENT and find out who the actual opponent of these three men will be.
(Rat Fink gives the wheel a mighty spin. It spins for a moment, and then slows, stopping on THE BONUS TRIANGLE.)
Rat Fink: Well well well, it seems that you will indeed face what lurks in... THE BONUS TRIANGLE. Now, let us unfold the BONUS TRIANGLE and see who the BONUS TRIANGLE has hidden.
(Rat Fink opens the BONUS TRIANGLE and reads aloud.) Rat Fink: 1,000 clones of Rat Fink. Well, you have heard what the BONUS TRIANGLE has to offer, and you know your possible opponent with great certainty. You will face... 1,000 clones OF RAT FINK!
(Rat fink laughs as we fade out.) EGarrett — 21 March 2004 AND NOW, A MESSAGE FROM THE MOST POWERFUL MAN IN WRESTLING... INT. PRESIDENTIAL OFFICE Alex Wylde walks into the entrance of his Jersey City Presidential Headquarters. Elaine is already inside, setting things up for the morning. Alex is followed in by "Mr. Middle Management" Mike McGee, who is carrying a carton of Orange Juice for him, as well as the WFW World Title. Elaine hands Alex a stack of papers.
ELAINE Authorization forms...mostly... WYLDE (Looking through the stack) What's this one with the staples? ELAINE A legal statement on behalf of WFW, Inc... WYLDE (skimming) "We neither endorse nor regulate our wrestler's sexual conduct?" ELAINE Jared Wells got caught with another one of those shemale hook- WYLDE Ugh...say no more. ELAINE It gets worse... WYLDE How long does he expect us to keep this quiet? No...y'know what...forget it. ELAINE The rest of the stuff just requires a few signatures...and there's also some other news. WYLDE That being? ELAINE Well...I could've sworn the War Games match was three... WYLDE War Games match? What War Games match? ELAINE The Vox mat- WYLDE I'm in a tag team street fight at Vox... ELAINE What happened to Minion? WYLDE Who? Maybe you had a dream that there was someone named Minion who knew a secret that could be potentially damaging to official WFW History...but dreams aren't real. I've never heard of a Minion in real life and neither have you.
ELAINE Diablo? WYLDE I don't know why people insist on believing in this Diablo myth...and if there was a man trying to dress as Diablo to perpetrate a hoax...maybe it's now part of the legend that he was erased in order to keep the match sides even after this non-existent Minion person was made non-existent.
ELAINE You're not making any sense...what am I supposed to tell people on the phones? Minion's mother called!
WYLDE Tell her that her son was just an unfortunate figment of her imagination...and this entire issue has been a figment of yours.
ELAINE Eh... WYLDE Now pardon me Elaine...I've got some opposition to address before I head back to the paperwork.
He hands the papers to Elaine and turns directly to the camera...leaning against a nearby filing cabinet.
WYLDE The first of whom being Michael Manson. A man who...tragically...is all too quick to drag out the oldest attacks possible like rusty B-52 bombers in a modern war. The problem is that...while those junkers are easy to pilot...they're also obsolete. Especially when you chide me for a title count when...the last time I checked...you've actually won less belts than me. I don't seem to recall you ever being a World Champion while I was around either...meanwhile the most important piece of hardware at the moment is figuratively around my waist. But if you want to tell people I consider you a friend...that's all well and good. Of course...I also considered Sean Edmunds a friend...and I polished off some paperwork a few minutes ago that he isn't going to like. I didn't hate Minion either...and for all intents and purposes...there is no longer such a man. In other words...friendship is for outside of the ring. Anyone who threatens my status is someone who threatens my income. When you threaten my income then you are an enemy. We can leave friendship at ringside for the time being. Besides...I know better than to ever turn my back on you Michael...it's sort of like keeping a pit bull in your home. What you need to realize though...is that the man who willingly does that is also well-prepared to put it to sleep when things go bad. Moving on...we have Rat Fink...a man who is an amusing fixture of WFW...but who is getting in way over his head. Especially if he thinks that his NAR or whatever it may be has the power to go one-on-one with the WFW Governmental Machine. I have no problems with the group itself...for as Thomas Jefferson said...a little revolution is good now and then. But don't overstep your bounds. Donald Trump isn't the only guy who can fire your ass on national TV. And I'd like to conclude with a note to my now sole partner...Pulsar. I know you like to hit the gym, young man. And I want you to hit that gym hard. Down your "protein" pills and vitamins and whatever else puts that pop in your veins...because I want you in full rage for this match. I learned last time we teamed that you are extremely good at running into whatever brick wall I should happen to point out. This is a street fight...and you have to be prepared to have that monumentally thick skull of yours tested like never before, eager young cadet. So pump that iron and pop those pills...and knowing the competition I recommend a gas mask when we go to battle. Now, if you don't mind...I have another important announcement to make. Thank you and god bless.
He turns and leaves to find Elaine. FADEOUT THE PRECEDING MESSAGE HAS BEEN PAID FOR BY YOUTH UNITED FOR ALEX WYLDE AND WORLD'S FINEST WRESTLING.
