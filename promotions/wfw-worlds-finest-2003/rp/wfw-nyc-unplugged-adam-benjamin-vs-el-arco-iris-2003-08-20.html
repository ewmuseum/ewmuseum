WFW: NYC Unplugged - Adam Benjamin vs. El Arco Iris

20 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 20 August 2003 Cut your promos for the match here. All RP/Promos are due on Friday, August 29th at 11:59 PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
Diablo — 25 August 2003 (Black screen. Cue up "Tunak Tunak Tun" by Daler Mehndi. As soon as the song kicks into high gear, the screen is bombarded simulatneously with all the colors of the spectrum, which can mean only one thing.)
(FADEIN: El Arco Iris is standing in front of a rainbow backdrop, wearing his mask, neon green shorts, and a tie-dyed rainbow t-shirt. Even though we can't see his mouth that well, we can tell from his upright posture and sprightly demeanor that he is indeed smiling and happy.)
El Arco Iris: El Arco Iris is happier than normal human parameters of happy today because El Arco Iris' wave of good feelings has begun its inundation! Even though I did not win that triple threat match, an important thing was accomplished as Luster's losing streak was broken, and Luster is once again happy and shiny. Seeing happy shiny things makes El Arco Iris happy, and El Arco Iris likes being happy.
(El Arco Iris' shoulders slump and he hangs his head to show sadness.) El Arco Iris: Unfortunetely, El Arco Iris is once again in the presence of these horrible vibes of bad feelings. El Arco Iris' new opponent, a Señor Benjamin, is apparently a very sad man, because he has not talked one bit. El Arco Iris knows that those who refuse to speak are ones who are so strangled by their bad feelings that dwell within their troubled souls. This is the problem El Arco Iris was born to remedy. Señor Benjamin, El Arco Iris would like you to know that El Arco Iris is always here as an open ear to communicate your sad feelings to. There's no need to shut yourself out from everyone else because you think nobody will listen to you.
(El Arco Iris straightens back up and gestures his hands in an explanatory manner) El Arco Iris: El Arco Iris knows that the key to eradicating the bad feelings that so plague this very planet is to stop people from bottling them up inside themselves, and to release them, and so your angers and fears and stresses all simply float away with the breeze, and you are left feeling only the happiest sunniest feelings, frolicking on the finest Sunday afternoon! El Arco Iris always knows the perfect way to make someone feel better about themselves and others, and that is simply by being someone to talk to. That is why El Arco Iris came to the WFW, because it is full of people who obviously are insecure about themselves, and fear other people, and harbor such hate in their hearts that it simply warps them into inhuman, loveless creatures.
(El Arco Iris gives himself a big hug.) El Arco Iris: El Arco Iris hopes to bring back the love. And will get you talking to many new people, and open up your heart to the world! El Arco Iris is here to help...
(Emphatic zoom into El Arco Iris' masked face, so we can see his big grin.) El Arco Iris: ...And to put a SMIIIIILE on your face! (FTB) ------------------------------ "I blow your mind. You just blow." Adam_Benjamin — 27 August 2003 (As the camera comes on "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin is seen standing in front of his locker at his gym. Towel in hand Benjamin clears his face and begins talking)
Benjamin: Come close and look at the truth of professional wrestling. Look deep into my eyes and see what the burning sensation is all about. Take a peek at what a true purest looks like. Now take a look at yourself, take a look at society, and most of all take a look at what you will never be, a winner.
(Adam smiles as he looks into the camera) Benjamin: This week Yours Truly has been extremly busy training for my grand debut, the very match that will show all what true wrestling is all about. I am not some gimick like my opponent, i am a pure intensity that is here for business and business only. In England we call a fellow like yourself pretty man, or in the States a Funny Atheltic Guy, in case you need a closer tip of the name simply spell the letters out.
(Adam laughs) Benjamin: You think that I am a upset soul. Well Guess what I am actually. I am made that I have to debut against a guy of you liking. I want a challenge not a positive speaker. You tell me that you have an open ear for my problems, good let me introduce my foot to your ever so happy ear. Because this week you enter the world of pain, and I am your tour guide. The pain game in just starting and when it ends I beg you to try and spit your happy lirics in my directions again you fool. Wake up and realise who you are facing, wake up and realise what I do in the ring. You know what forget realising it now, I am simple going to beat it into you. I am going to lay into you, pound you with every move I can think of, and when the ref says "Adam he has had enough" I am going to smile at him with a "Positive" smile and say I beg to differ sir, I think he likes it.
(Adam smiles as he again clears his face) Benjamin: Yours Truly is not a loveless creature, he is not a upset man, he is simply a wrestler, the best that this company has to offer. This week is the begining, next week I will continue, it will not stop till I reach the top as the one and only survor!!! Fear what you are not, talented!!!
(Fade to black)
