NotorisSTD — 23 November 2003 (Blank screen. CUEUP: Real Audio clip linked to on wrestlingeek.com, a shoot interview with Teddy Blendel, aka M.W. Grossard....)
MWG V/O: I guess I of all people shouldn't have been surprised, but after a while I got the distinct impression the only reason they gave me a job was they assumed I'd be a good connection.
GEEK INTERVIEWER V/O: Ironic. MWG V/O: (chuckles) yeah! now when drug testing in pro sports is getting to be a bigger and bigger deal, you couldn't get a job in that company UNLESS you were all messed up all the time. I just remember like...one day I walked into Scotty's office cuz I thought my merch royalty check was a little short, turns out it wasn't, by the way, but anyway, I walk in there, and him and Mael were shooting up some ungodly synthetic testosterone
sh(bleep) I think they bought of some east European women's Olympic swim team. Watching those guys pulling needles out of their asses, I just thought to myself "Wow, there's no way this can last..." But ch'know. I made some money and was reasonably successful. So all around it's not like I'm bitter or anything.
GEEK INTERVIEWER V/O: Speaking of success, A lot of guys you actually trained have been able to surpassed you just as far as getting over goes. Midiot was IWF champion, and Felix Red is getting a huge push in WFW. What's your take on that?
MWG V/O: Oh, I think it's great. I mean, a lot of people for a long time thought I was this gimmick who couldn't really work, and they can't say that anymore, cuz I trained all these guys who have had amazing matches. So I've gotta know what I'm doing in some respect, right? (chuckles) Like, I knew Mitch was a solid
all around talent, I can't say I ever expected him to be as good as he got, but go figure. I was also surprised it took Felix as long as it did to find his niche. Like, when I first met him he could do all that crazy martial arts sh(bleep), so that would set him apart from the pack, but he wasn't real confident at first. I think that
might've factored into how he was always changing his ring name and wearing masks 'n what have you. Like he was trying to hide himself. Now that he's stopped doing that I think he's really gonna be big....
GEEK V/O: You think the boy who destroyed the world is the real Felix? MWG V/O:....what the f(bleep) are you talking about? (CUEUP: "Backmaskwarning" by Mindless Self Indulgence...) (FADEIN: We've been here before. we went to a few other places, but now we're back, and yet enough has changed so we really have never seen this place. Shadow laced cellar, flickering light bulb patternlessly revealing and vanishing Felix Red in sunglasses, black dickie pants, and a Perfect Circle T-shirt...beside him sits an old television set. there's a bit of broken glass on the floor, apparently because someone shattered the TV screen and stuffed it with rotted jack-o-lanterns....)
"the boy who destroyed the world..." When your long term goal is oblivion, you always and never get what you want. I wanted to wipe Mr. Michaels and Mr. Gideon from the face of the earth. I succeeded, in part, by accident. I assumed violence and entropy would see me through, when all I had to do was wax hypothetical to turn Mr. Michael's brother against him. He is now without his title, without his pride, without peace of mind. His life is ruined, and I barely had to touch him. Of course, Richard Gideon is alive, well, and truly happy for the first time in months. In that respect, the night was rather a
bust, a waste, and that's precisely what I was after from the beginning is the end. Various incarnations of struggles between myself and Rat Fink have been taking place once or twice a week, unless they haven't, couldn't, neither of us tangible, no capacity to make it so. At times I have bested him, at others it has not been so. These encounters have not been entirely fruitless. I've regained an amount of wisdom. It's rather pointless to force the fallacy of sanity down the throat one whom is already out of his friggin'
gourd. It's equally impractical to illustrate the agony I shall inflict. This Personified deus es machina, god out of the machine, to become god of the machine, was born knowing how much I will hurt him. My strife is engrained too deeply in the collective unconscious for this to not be so, as is Fink's, as is mankind's...What separates this meeting from the others is the presence of a remnant, a leftover...I keep saying I don't want to be a champion, unless I do, if I was to become the BAD world champion, in fact, I would not be a champion at all. I will, again,
and again, and again, get everything I've ever wanted all this is hollow and worthless, a waste of my attention. I only get what I want when I don't. I want a legion of mindless drones who wish me destroyed. I want every scrap of nonsensical rhetoric Rat Fink can conjure from the bowels of his brain, strangled by maladity, with no hope for peace. I want his violence, and I want to reduce it all to scraps of blood and meat, and I want to become the nonchampion. Except I don't. I take comfort in that I am not condemned to never get what I want, as is Fink. A self designed perpetual denial of gratification, well, that's basically suicide
isn't it? if Madness is to wake up screaming only to realize you haven't slept at all, the kindest thing I could ever do for Rat Fink is to beat him into unconsciousness. Only then could he be sure he's only been dreaming...
ChrisHorowitz — 26 November 2003
(Cue up "Dweller on the Threshold" by Van Morrison.) (Fade into Satan's Clause, standing behind the pulpit of his crystal cathedral, it's glass walls letting in the divine light of God Almighty.)
Satan's Clause: My fellow followers of God Almighty, I come to you this day to tell you that the taint of Lucifer remains strong in this world, and in World's Finest Wrestling especially. My friends in Christ, one of satan's highest ranking henchmen here on this earth our heavenly father has bestowed upon his children is threatening our glory.
The BAD World Heavyweight Championship, the NAR's offering to our lord, our sacrifice to him, our symbol of our convenant with the Father, the son, and the Holy Spirit.
But Felix Red stands in our way, a man emersed in the evil twisted logic of satan. This promise of knowledge is how the devil reaches into your mind and curses you with his foul stench, forcing you to do his bidding. Satan promises his followers the surreal and blocks reality from their mind, promising them only the unattainable so they will do his twisted work.
But my friends, he will not attain his goals through Felix Red. The World Heavyweight championship, the symbol that god and his children are the destined rulers of this earth will not fall into the hands of the evil one. His tainted subjects will not steal from the children of the almighty. He is powerless to take from us the gifts that God himself has bestowed upon his chosen people. The NAR will not stand for Satan's horrific works, and we will continue on with our Crusade to defeat them. Our Holy War will not end until each and every one of his followers have been removed from our fair planet.
Children of the lord, your prayers are needed at this hour! We must have our song of glory sung to the lord so he knows his children, his army of light is fighting back against the darkness. He must know that Felix Red and those who will follow him will be driven back to the lake of fire!
Felix Red, you have been given the damned gift of knowledge, you feel your mind has been awakened when in fact it has been killed. The devil has taken from you your ability to see the glory of God, and replaced it with the darkness of eternal damnation. But there would be hope if you could only turn back from the darkness and embrace the reality that only Jesus can save you from satan's grasp.
I fear though that you cannot do this. I fear that you have fallen too far into the arms of your dark lord and that he is your master for all eternity. I have seen it too many times, Satan having such a firm grip on the human mind, the mind god has given you and hoped you could use it to do only kindness in his kingdom. If this is the case and there is no hope for you, then we are left with one choice.
The followers of Christ must destroy you Felix, we must punish you for your sins against the father, we must drive you back before you can help your evil controller destroy more lives, and attempt to enslave more of the world. Our hand is infused with the power and light of Christ, his might will pound you like you have never been pounded before. Your sins will not be clensed this way, but you will be cleansed from this earth, and you will not ruin any others.
My friends, the hour for the next confrontation between good an evil is at hand. Everywhere we see the signs of it approaching, the devil doing his work. Gays allowed to marry, sodomy legalized, women in the workplace, the government protested by the young, the elderly taken from gods hands by doctors who keep them alive, punishing them by keeping them from paradise. These evils MUST END.
We cannot stand for it any longer. My friends I call upon you to rise up! Felix Red will be the next target for destruction, but not the last. The taint of satan must be eliminated or we will lose the keys to the kingdom! Come with me and free the people of earth from his blight.
(Fade out) NotorisSTD — 27 November 2003 (CUEUP: "Halo" by a Perfect Circle "I think that's the name of the song. it's track 3 on 13th step"....)
(empty white room. no doors, no windows, nawtin. Just a plastic tree with apples scotch taped to the branches. Upon each apple the number six hundred and sixty six has been scribbled with black marker. Felix Red sits under the tree in the shade, decked out in baggy lavender pants 'n an old "LiGhT bRiGaDe" T-shirt left over from the last incarnation of the WWL. From the way his head's tilted, Our subject appears to stare aimlessly at the wall, except the sunglasses make it impossible to know where his
eyes are, so maybe he's looking at something else...) "the boy who destroyed the world..." I suppose everyone can hardly wait to hear me denounce Christianity, or at least Christian ideals, in a superficial attempt to skew Rat Fink's attention on matters of real significance. Just because I'm supposed to be nihilistic doesn't mean I can't believe in God. I destroyed the world. I engineered Armageddon. Of course there's a God. It's me. And you. All of us. Mankind is the highest power in the universe. If there is, in fact, one greater who has chosen to be totally ineffectual, it means nothing at all. What's the point of having power and refusing to use it? Not to say an almighty omniscient force that exists except that it doesn't isn't interesting enough to discuss. What's even more interesting, is I was once just like you, Mr. Clause...I thumped the proverbial bible, and the most interesting thing of all, is in those days I didn't mean anything I said. Nothing at all like how I am now....Honestly...
What could it all mean? Is Mr. Clause as I was? Is he preaching gibberish to blend with a xenophobic collective of those who want an excuse to be powerless? Or is he genuinely that stupid? Furthermore, why should I care? Why am I addressing him instead of Rat Fink? Has the frequency of my company caused
my dear Rat Fink to grow weary of me? Is he attempting to stroke the confidence of this lipspittle Clause by conceding TV time to him? Is Rat Fink f(bleep)king afraid of me?
(Felix pulls an apple off the tree and takes a bite...) It doesn't matter. If anything does, it's blood. Fluids. The most spiritual sensation, the sublime rapture, the suspension of all things rational...Is to stare into the havoc and to realize what you're seeing is a reflection. Birth is madness and pain, as is death. The beginning is the end. The briefest of touches, to run the tips of our fingers against the entropy, to which we are so rarely privy to in this life, this the only true grace, the only flickering glimpses we get of our true selves. Monsters. Demons. Gods...
Why do you think Eve would ever eat the apple, except that endless pleasure was more wretched than she could endure? Do you honestly believe Lucifer turned on his creator because he thought God's power could be his? All he wanted was to be banished to somewhere other than heaven...Ecstasy is agony
is ecstasy is agony. There is no pleasure without pain, and it is through each we are reminded that we are alive, except we're not. Not until I beat Rat Fink half to death, will he be sure he's half alive....
