Starbreaker — 13 March 2004 Fade in: on a park bench where Larry Tact sits. He has on black leather pants and coat with grey sleeveless shirt underneath, black boots, and a gray New York Giants winter hat, the logo in blue on the front. Tact looks ahead as he speaks with a calm demeanor.
Tact: Well, I prepared. I scouted out the competition, and went through the effort to make it to the Rumble in top condition. But sometimes that just isn't enough. I knew it'd be chaos in that ring, and I would have to be prepared for every second of it. From one instant to another, though, I got caught up and taken for prey.
Tact shrugs. Tact: It could have happened to anyone, and it did happen to others, and I just happened to be one. Losing the Rumble isn't the end of the world, though. It would certainly have been a quick ticket to a World Title shot, and at the Superbowl of Wrestling, no less. Definitely an opportunity any respectable wrestler would go for. But that wasn't my fate, it seems. I'm not satisfied in the least. However, that doesn't mean I'm not just as determined to get to the top. I've made a habit of getting there through fighting my way up, as it seems the 'easy' way isn't what's ever been in store for me. Few get that path, though, and this Rumble really wasn't the 'easy' way for anyone, even Manson. Now, though, the time for reflecting on that has passed. It's time to move on.
The wind begins to pick up, dragging some stray branches and newspaper along the walkway behind the bench, and sending the nearby tree branches swaying.
Tact: If there's one thing I can say without a doubt, it's that I always bring my best to the building, whatever I can muster. Some people didn't even bother to enter that Rumble. People like Luster...people who didn't think they had a chance. Let's face it, a guy like Psycho can say what he wants, but there's no excuse for any eligible WFW wrestler to not have competed in that Rumble. Luster....we've been down this road recently. You gave an effort, but I put you down, and I have no problem doing it twice in three shows. You're a guy who has almost as many delusions as your partner....except you have even less ground for those delusions. I don't know if you'll be able to pull yourself out of that fantasy you live in, but if you walk to that ring for this match, you'd best be in reality. Because I'm more than prepared to humble you again.
Tact pauses and rubs his chin for a moment or two, then grins and snorts. Tact: And then there's Sean Edmunds. The transition Champion. I guess you enjoyed your time at the top, and I wouldn't blame you for saying that it's better to be a transitional Champion than no Champion. But since I've always held Championships for longer than a couple weeks, and will soon be showing as much when I get my North American title shot...well, I really couldn't care what the value of being a transitional Champion holds. All prodding aside, though, I doubt you're feeling real well right now, Edmunds. I can only imagine that WFW is thinking of running you through the (BLEEP) list that you've compiled for yourself...with the most recent first. Which is why I've been paired with Scotty Michaels, of all people, for this match against you. But the gates are about to open on you, Edmunds, and I'm leading the way.
Tact snickers. Tact: I'm sure you've already got it in your head that you're not falling for long....that you're going right back up to the top, to reclaim 'your' title. This is just a brief reprieve from 'competition' for you. Well before you get set to kick back and coast through this match, let me just remind you of what you're up against, cuz it's not quite the same as last time, Edmunds. See, you got myself, first of all, which is enough to leave you flat on the canvas to start. I won't go into Sherri's involvement in our last encounter, but you can be sure she saved your (BLEEP), along with Wylde for letting her be there at all. But that's another thing, a new factor in this. Wylde doesn't care what happens to you anymore, Edmunds. I don't know if he ever did, but I doubt he'll be supporting you in any way this match. After all, if I were in his position, after what you had to say to him, I might ban Sherri from ringside just to stick it to you that extra bit more. If he doesn't do as much, it won't matter. It's already open season on your (BLEEP), Edmunds. And Michaels and I get first cracks. Plus, you know as well as I that with Luster, you're only more susceptible. You can't cover for the two of you in this one. Just consider yourself condemned, you bastard.
Tact stands up and buttons up his coat while the wind continues to pick up. Tact: As for my partner? Well, Michaels, you're not going through the golden time right now, either. But at least I know you can hold your own, which is more than Edmunds can say for his partner. We don't have to agree with each other, Michaels, and we don't have to compare notes. All we have to do...is work together for one match. Then we can be rid of each other. I don't think that's too difficult, and I think dishing out a well deserved thrashing to this particular opposition is just enough incentive to keep us on the same page...for the night.
Tact turns the collar of the coat up as thunder rumbles above. Rain begins to fall as he walks off. Fade out.
ViewersChoice — 14 March 2004 (FADEIN to Scotty Michaels, standing in front of a WFW backdrop..) SCOTTY MICHAELS: Sean Edmunds, all I've got to say man is.. and you call ME the transitional champion? Brother, you didn't even hold that belt past one card. At least I held the damn thing for two months. So we're back at point A. No titles involved in this one... all that's involved, is RESPECT!
I've realized.. that you do deserve my respect, Edmunds. You beat me at my own game, and you did it pretty well. But when it all came down to it, you proved that you're definitely not a better wrestler than I am. You lost the title to Alex Wylde... I really can't get over that, but it's all said and done. You've beaten me twice, Edmunds, and for that.. you'll pay. FOR THAT, you're going to get exactly what you deserve, and that's me kicking your ass all over that ring at Vox Hibernation.. whatever it's called.
Finally, that brings us to Luster. A man that I once called my friend.. a man, who I thought, was on my side. A man.. who's never going to make it ANYWHERE in this sport. You didn't even waste your time in the battle royal. Some say it's because you knew you couldn't win... others say that it's because you're afraid of me. Afraid of what I'm going to do to you when I see you across from me in that wrestling ring.
You always knew that I was the driving force beside our partnership. Hell, it wouldn't have been credible if I was the one helping you win championships. That's all you are, Luster, a JOKE! And when it all comes down to it, you were exactly what you said you were... MY B*TCH! Deal with it, Luster.. but you need to make like the Tin Man, realize you don't have a brain, and just not show up at Vox. If it wasn't for me, Luster... you wouldn't even have a job right now. You'd be stripping for fiftieth birthday parties, with old, GROSS women rubbing their hands all over you.
Larry Tact, you do your part... I'll do mine, and I'm sure everything will go exactly as planned. Just don't try to be a hero, man, 'cuz you don't have it in you. Vox, I GET MY REVENGE!
