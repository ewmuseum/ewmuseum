WFW Paradise Lost: Rat Fink vs. Pulsar

3 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 3 September 2004 The RP/angle deadline is Wednesday, September 15 11:59 PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
stuclif — 8 September 2004 FADE IN: PULSAR, BACK IN THE DIMLY LIT, OLD GYM WEARING KICK BOXING SHORTS AND A GREY TRAINING SINGLET. HITTING THE FAMILIAR TAPPED UP BAG.
PULSAR: (TURNS TO THE CAMERA) OK, this is it, Paradise Lost, this is where its all gonna come to an end. Fink, I dont know if youve noticed or not, but in my short time in WFW, you and I have beaten the hell out of each other nearly half a dozen times, in both three and four man matches, tag matches and the supposed single matches. Now when I say supposed, I mean that its never really a singles match with you, is it Fink, you always have that 'Army' of yours waiting in the wings, and I dont expect this match to be any different. The cheating and crap that you carry on with only ever bothered me so much... there was no rules against it in our match for the BAD strap, and other times I have never minded beating the s**t out of those other clowns... (TONE BECOMES MENACINGLY LOW AND STEADY) but.. at Cherry Blossom Chaos, your interference cost me the second highest prize this company has to offer.. the North American title.
(PULSAR SLAMS ANOTHER HARD RIGHT INTO THE BAG WHICH ALMOST FOLDS IN HALF) PULSAR: All four of us guys worked our arses off in the match.. man, we beat the hell out of each other. (POINTS TO THE CAMERA) You were eliminated... you know what you should have done... takin it like a f**king man and got out of the match. (TAKES DEEP BREATHES) I dont know what was going through your damn head, I dont think anyone ever does, but I'll tell you what I do know, that North American title which I was cheated of is a major stepping stone towards receiving a shot at the World title, a shot at immortality, a chance for the fans to see what they have been hanging for since I debuted in the WFW... Pulsar wearing the ultimate prize.
So Fink, you now know how I feel about about what youve done, but I dont think you know what I'm going to do. We've had some bloody battles in the past, we've almost got a bit of a rivalry going on, but come Paradise Lost, I wont be just going through the motions... this is it.. I'll be setting out to right a few wrongs that have been happening, and by doing so I plan putting on some serious pain, and I normally wouldnt wish that on anybody. I'm not a guy that can just hate somebody, in fact its very rare, there are only a small few that actually lay claim to being on that list. So to you Rat Fink I say congratulations on acheiving such a rare feat. I'm afaraid its an honour that you may soon come to regret. Cue the excitement.
