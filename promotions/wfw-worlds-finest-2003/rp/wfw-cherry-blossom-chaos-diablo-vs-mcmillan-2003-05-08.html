WFW Cherry Blossom Chaos: Diablo vs. McMillan

8 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Diablo — 9 May 2003 (FADEIN: We see a park-type setting, in Washington D.C. of course. Children are playing off in the distance, the camera pans to find Diablo, underneath the shade of a tree.)
Diablo: So here I am. Thrust off my pedestal of greatness. Out of the title scheme. Still my cause remains undeterred. It still hungers...hungers to consume the light! Yes...for however brighter you may think the light to get, the darkness only becomes that much bleaker...I see new illumination...no, not from shining gold on leather...illumination in this so called wolf, Chris McMillan...
(Diablo gets up and wanders around the park, and notices some cherry blossom trees. He walks by them and notices the petals are falling. He catches a few of them in his outstretched hand.)
Diablo: It's a shame...a shame that I must perform such acts of horror, when we so reverantly observe acts of nature, acts of beauty...
(Diablo clenches a tight first around the petals in his hands, then let's the wind carry the smashed pedals away.)
Diablo: Chris, you may think yourself a wolf, indeed. You may consider yourself the finest of predators, stalking your prey, chasing it down until it can't run anymore, then conquering it with your powerful claws and fangs. Well, you have to wonder...what does the wolf do...when it finds its prey does not run? What does the wolf do...when it's prey has sharper claws...
(Diablo looks straight into the camera and bares his teeth in a smirk as he whips his tongue over his canines.)
Diablo: ...And more terrible fangs? Diablo: These fangs... they belong to the gaping maw of the darkness, Chris. It is a black, seething mouth that fests on the will of men. It's teeth will incise and swallow all hope. It devours the light, your light, my light. This is not a force I can control, but merely am subject to. You have no choice when you step in the ring to me, but find yourself but the next one to be fed upon. Consumed, as I have been. Chris McMillan, when the cherry blossoms fall, so shall you...I will put my evil inside you!
(FTB) ------------------------------ "I blow your mind. You just blow." Mister Dread — 14 May 2003 ::FADEIN on the same park from which Diablo made his last appearance. The camera journeys back amongst the hardwoods, into a denser stand of trees. It is shady in this bit of hardwood forest, and the light flickers as the leaves shift overhead. "The Wolf" Chris McMillan leans up against an ironwood tree, hands stuffed in the pockets of his jeans::
THE WOLF: Diablo, huh? That's cute. I like it. Anyway, you want to know what the wolf does when the prey does not run, what the wolf does when the prey has more terrible claws and fangs ... well, the wolf does as he always does. He follows the instincts that have driven him to hunt, consume, and kill since the rememberance of time. But more than that, the wolf is a thinking animal. He knows when to exercise caution. And so, when faced with such a foe the wolf launches an swift, viscous, unrelenting attack. He attacks from every angle, keeping always out of reach. He allows the prey to persue HIM, all the while inflicting seemingly minor wounds that over time contrive to bring down his prey. In short, the wolf kills. Just as he always does. But where is this creature? This prey that does not run? This prey with the terrible claws and fangs? Is it YOU? Hardly.
::The Wolf paces, threading through the trees. Light and shadow play across his face as the breeze rustles the leaves overhead::
THE WOLF: I've gotta say, Diablo, you've got presence. Your words make an impact, but do your actions have the same impact? All too often I am confronted with someone who considers himself a superior predator. Someone who postures and growls incessantly, right up until he gets into the ring. Once he steps between those ropes, though, the horrifying realization of just how hollow those words usually are sinks in. It's a sweet moment for me indeed when the self-proclaimed superior animal finds out that he's just more meat for the beast. THAT is what's going to happen to you, and I'm going to savor every moment of it.
THE WOLF: See, what I did to Aitsu Sugawara last week was only a sample, a preview of what I am capable of. I have committed atrocities that NOBODY ... okay, nobody short of Micheal Manson, anyway, could even CONCIEVE of. You're the superior animal? The dominant predator? Not hardly. You, my friend, hunt at the back of the pack. You're the animal who eats the scraps of the others. The last to mate, the last to hunt ... and you're certainly in no position to tangle with me, the alpha male.
::The Wolf finds a shady spot and sits, leaning his back against a young poplar tree:: THE WOLF: My body has been broken. My mind comes and goes. But one thing that has never been broken, one thing that NO ONE can rend ... is my spirit. My WILL. You say it will be comsumed? You want to consume me? Okay. Go ahead and EAT ME. And as far as putting your evil INSIDE me, well, with all your talk about gaping maws, and consumption, and now putting things inside me ... frankly, I'm a little concerned about sharing a locker room with you now. But seriously, I am here to achieve something. To make an impact. My legacy in WFW started last week with Aitsu Sugawara. It continues at Cherry Blossom Chaos with YOU, Diablo. You will be a footnote in the foundation that my legend is built on. You should be honored.
::FADE TO BLACK:: Diablo — 15 May 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON May-15-03 AT 07:15&nbsp;PM (EST)](FADEIN: Diablo is seen walking down the ragged streets of a D.C. ghetto, at about noon- time. He is wearing a white tank-top with a motocycle vest with flames across the bottom hem, and army-green cargo pants.)
Diablo: You had me going in the beginning there, Chris. I was starting to think you weren't as feeble as I originally had thought. I may have actually thought you to be the man who can truly challenge me, but then you go ahead and try to intimidate me by saying that beating up a young Asian woman is some awesome feat of strength. If anything, I now think you even more ridiculous than before. Everyone has to start somewhere, but shouldn't you maybe have picked someone that people would notice a little bit more if you did something to them? Not that it matters to me, though. No matter what displays you can put on, no matter what you could possibly do to show me how brutal you can be in that ring, it can't demonstrate a strength greater than the power that possesses me, courses through my veins, and shapes my flesh...
(Diablo pauses, closes his eyes, and shudders, before continuing.) Diablo: You say your one great asset is your spirit. Spirit? Mine was taken from me, a long time ago. Replaced with the abyss, this all-consuming blackness. I am no longer able to be a kind, loving man who contributes to society. I am doomed now to walk this Earth a soulless pawn of the darkness...its vehicle of destruction...where my spirit may have driven me like any other man, my body is now taken over by another force in combat...it endows me with its craving, its hunger for light...to consume, like a flame...All humanly remorse and compassion stripped away. The fire burns, yet I do not kindle it.
(Diablo shudders a bit some more, winces, but then his face takes on a very insidious expression and he look dead into the camera, his voice with a more sinister tone.)
Diablo: Let me just say, Chris McMillain, now it is no longer a matter of being the alpha male, or having superior claws and teeth. You reign supreme over the lower species, but I am higher on the evolutionary chain! Your ilk will be simply driven to the brink of extinction courtesy of MY terrible plague...the plague of terminal blackness. Your spirit won't be "broken", no. These hands will leave all your being, your spirit...SHATTERED! SWALLOWED! Engulfed by the abyss...the flame of DIABLO FUEGO will ignite your soul, and the flesh will yield to it's scalding lick, whether you like it or not! Mark my words, when the last cherry blossom petals fall...my evil will be INSIDE you!
(FTB as Diablo suddenly shakes his head furiously, then falls to his knees, seemingly drained.)

Mister Dread — 20 May 2003 ::FADEIN on the WFW banner backstage. “The Wolf” Chris McMillan stands there, attired as usual in jeans, harness boots, and a Chris Benoit “Toothless Aggression” t-shirt under a worn and faded motorcycle jacket::
THE WOLF: Had you going, did I? Well, I’m really starting to look forward to the moment when you realize that everything I say is the absolute truth. Do you think for one second that I believe all that crap about you being a soulless monster who’s full of hellfire and all-consuming evil? Not a chance, pal. However, it would serve you well for you to believe me when I say that I AM one of the best pure wrestlers this sport has ever seen. It would behoove you to listen when I say that when I get into the ring, I hurt people. I don’t think you’re bright enough to take the hint, though. You’re gonna blow me off, counting on your dark, angsty image to carry you through this match with me. Well let me tell you, Diablo, that the only things that are gonna get you through this match are guts, will power, and damn raw SKILL.
::The Wolf begins to pace, wiping a sheen of sweat from his forehead:: THE WOLF: Go ahead and waste your time. Come on out in front of these people, or on camera, and holler to the world about how eeeeevil you are. “Hey, lookit meeeee! I’m the DEVIL!” Right. While you’re busy with that, I’m gonna be in the gym. I’m gonna be in the ring. I’m gonna be watching the tapes. Hell, I’ll even be saying my prayers and taking my vitamins, for what it’s worth. When we get into the ring, though … then what? Do you think that your soul-consuming abyss thingie is gonna pop out of the center of the ring and swallow me whole? Well, I hope it does, Diablo. For your sake. Because barring total consumption from an otherworldly source, you’re not gonna beat me. Hell, you’re not even gonna touch me. I will take you apart, son. Piece by piece, bit by bit. Can you go hold-for-hold and counter-for-counter with ME? I don’t think so. It’s called catch-as-catch-can, and I CAN.
::FADE TO BLACK::
