WFW Ghoulish Games - Minion vs. Adam Benjamin

24 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 24 October 2003 The RP/Angle dealine is Monday, November 3rd 11:59 PM. If all all possible, make your RP Halloween or Day of the Dead themed.
Cut your promos here. DavidMc — 31 October 2003 (Screen fades in as we see a desolate apartment. Very little furniture and other furnishings that make an apartment a home are missing. The light is deem and what light there is comes from a couple of candles placed sporadically around the room. Pans are lined around the apartment to collect water that is dripping into some while others appear to be as dry as a bone. The walls are lined with ripped news paper articles with every topic ranging from war to wrestling to obituaries. The space that is not covered by news paper is written on with bright red paint. Some of the scribbling say “Flesh of fallen angles” others say “I was once a man once but now just a shell”. A slight draft circles the apartment and the news articles rustle with the wind that comes in. As the camera circles the apartment a dark shadowy figure emerges from one of the rooms. It is the Minion but something has changed. He has a couple of scars on his face and his eyes are heavy set and seem to be further back in his head like a skeleton. He appears as if he has had little to no sleep. He is wearing a t-shirt that used to say Tom Parker lives but the part that reads Tom Parker has been ripped out and now it just says “Lives”. He passes by the camera, as if he is floating, ignoring it and sit in the corner. He motions for the camera to come closer and begins.
Minion: it has been a while since I have stepped into the ring and I know the world misses me. Surely they miss me…surely I have not fallen through the cracks like others before me have….have I? Surely they remember me…but why haven’t they sent me anything. Why haven’t my fans sent me letters? Why haven’t the feds come knocking on my door…trying to sign me to multi million dollar contracts? The truth of the matter is I have sat in this apartment…eating…sleeping…waiting for the door bell to ring or a piece of mail to be slid underneath the door…I have been nothing but disappointed. I have been forgotten...I have fallen through the cracks and it makes me angry. I have done nothing to deserve this treatment….have I? I have risked my life…I risked my sanity…I have done everything in my power to make the cows happy…the fans did understand my logic they turned to the other has-beens of the wrestling world….They turned to the new kids…the younger generations…the flashy gimmicks…and the dry sense of humor. What happened to the smart fan…the ones who remember Tom Parker? What happened to the fans who remember the fights between me and Maelstrom…Golem…or the veterans of the wrestling…that are written on the wind…and only spoken about in whispers? We were giants in a playground…and I was forgotten what happened to the wrestling world? what happened to my fans….I will tell you what happened? They turned their back on me. They shunned me. They went they way of the dodo and became extinct. So…I sat here…in my apartment for months…I watched the GLCW close without even a phone call…and then it went silent nothing happened for months…and when nothing happens and my mind is allowed to wander into the darkest parts of my sanity. I got up one morning…got on a flight….went to WFW offices….put my name on a piece of paper…said sign it…they signed it and they put me on the roster…little did they know the mistake they had just made. I have been cooped up in this apartment for to long…my mind has wandered from sanity…to insanity…to borderline neurotic. The only thing that keeps me sane in this pathetic place…are thoughts of fear and desperation of my victims…the taste of flesh from fallen angels.
(Minion rocks back in forth in glee and rapture. He then stops rocking suddenly and looks back into the camera with a death glare and begins again.)
Minion: Two weeks passed…nothing happened. My mind began to wander again…I began to think unsure thoughts…thoughts of neglect…forlorn…and depression. Then it happened…as I was popping pills and sipping absinthe…a note slipped under the door. It was from the WFW office requesting my presence at an event…in it was my first pay check. I took the letter and ran it down my body…I could feel the heat that once existed on the paper against my cold body…it felt good. I re opened the letter and read every sentence…every word…every syllable…then I read my opponent…Adam Benjamin…and the thoughts began to rush through my heat. All the frustration that had been held down…all the contempt…all the misery…every un pure thought…every violent reaction to everything I had heard and read boiled over…rushed into my head… I fell to the ground like a druggie taking a huge hit…I lay on the ground thinking of all the things I wanted to do…all the horrible things. Adam you are what is wrong with the world wrestling…your breed of wrestler is why the fans forgot about me. You came into the wrestling world pure but now you are tainted with the scent that attracts people to you and away from me…you took my fans…you divided the peoples attention so it could not be placed on me…you worthless pig. I have nothing but disdain for your breed…I have nothing but contempt and hatred…I will punish you for your actions…I will make you bleed...I will rip your pretty boy face off...would you like that pretty boy? Would you like to bleed out all over the ring…all over the mats as the emergency crews try to take your body? Nothing can save you Adam. I am the ring master and welcome to the circus of the sadistic...I live in the dark…eat in the dark...you are no different than anyone that as ever fought me before…you are generic nothing special and you are worthless. I know your kind. You will shrug all of this off as some hardcore gimmick…some sick person wanting to get his jollies off over perverted stuff like this.…I will not lie to you pretty boy…I am the gimmick this is as real as it gets…and I am hear to welcome you with arms filled with razor blades...As the emergency crews take your carcass out of the ring and you look back…in the ring and with that last glimmer you have you will see me dancing like the devil with blood dripping from mouth, a smile like a cheshire cat and teeth like gods shoe shine.
(Minion looks down and starts rock again but doesn’t stop this time as he begins to laugh sadistically and then talks)
Minion: I am mildly happy…I guess that’s what absinthe does to you…they do say it rots your brain…but I don’ believe lies…I only create them. WFW are you prepared for the pestilence…are you prepared for the circus that I bring…the circus of the sadistic…I sure hope you are…but doesn’t matter if you are ready all that matters is that if Adam is ready…I can assure the world he is not…at least not for something like me. I have changed…the world has changed…I have adapted accordingly. In order to survive in this world you must adapt...or else you die and I am the most adaptable virus ever. This is going to be fun. Life is but a dream, but it hurts so much more when you fall...the divine taste of fallen angels.
