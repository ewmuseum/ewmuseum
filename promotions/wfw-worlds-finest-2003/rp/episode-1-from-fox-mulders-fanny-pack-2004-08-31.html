Episode 1: From Fox Mulder's Fanny Pack

31 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ShawnHartXXX — 31 August 2004 http://fwrestling.com/host/fmll/images/funbus.bmp Olive oil enhances taste. FADE IN: INT. JOE'S DINER - 11:43 A.M. It's slow time at the off-highway burger joint, and only two tables are occupied. At one, a 20-ish YOUNG MAN and his signifcant other, a PREGNANT WOMAN of the same age, pick at salads. At another, several JAPANESE TRAVELERS on their way from one tourist trap to the next sit. At the bar, our heroes, MR. SHAWN J. HART and his accountant EL SEGUNDO sit nervously.
HART (V/O): It was a blistering midday, the 5th of June, in the year of our lord... 2004, when we found ourselves stranded in that horrible place.
CUT TO: Segundo, scratching relentlessly at the back of his silver mask. HART (V/O): After having replaced the water in his canteen with original golden death-flavored Listerine and watching him consume large amounts of the substance, my accountant was convinced that an alien parasite had latched on to the back of his neck and was feeding on his brain. A lame attempt at a prank when dealing with any normal human being, but cruel and unusal punishment indeed when performed on a man without functioning taste buds.
CUT TO: The Phenom, his eyes filled with guilt. HART (V/O): Feeling the remorse that inevitably comes after drugging a family friend with mouth wash, I suddenly felt inclined to reveal to him my hidden secret as a means of evening the score...
HART: I'm a Russian spy. EL SEGUNDO: You.... TERRIBLE bastard!! It was you, YOU, wasn't it? You've... injected me with this...... .......BEAST!!
HART: You spoony bard! I HAD to! You were threatening to shave my sister's head in her sleep.... then shave my chest in MY sleep and tape that hair to her head!
Angered by this accusation, Segundo quickly grabs a salt shaker from the counter and pokes it violently into Hart's chest.
EL SEGUNDO: I know... I.... KNOW, what you two are up to! so why don't you get up slowly and... MOVE toward the back of the diner!
HART (V/O): Now that he was armed, I had no choice but to submit to his will... or DID I?! The Phenom eases off of his stool and slowly backs away. HART: Whoa, whoa, whoa. What you are doing lacks jggy freshness, my friend. You just be careful with that thing... my God, where's the LOVE?!
EL SEGUNDO: I tried... OH how I tried... to love you. But EL SEGUNDO doesn't give love to Lenin! As the two approach the young couple's table, Hart alertly yanks the woman out of her chair, prompting a scream, and holds his tinted shades to her neck. The japanese tourists let the cameras flash.
HART: One wrong move and the girl gets it!! The woman's boyfriend leaps from his seat. YOUNG MAN: HEY!! That's my... AAAAAAAAAAAAHHHH!!! Before he can finish his sentence, El Segundo shakes salt into his eyes, sending him tumbling to the ground.
HART: RUN!! Withought a second thought, the two drop their weapons and make for the exit. The Japanese contingent stands in applause.
CUT TO: The Phenom and Segundo, hopping into their convertible. EL SEGUNDO: How... HOW can we.... spread love when the world is... FULL with sickos like that!! With a grin on his face, Hart pats El Segundo's shoulder and replies. HART: We'll drug them with Listerine. FADE OUT.
