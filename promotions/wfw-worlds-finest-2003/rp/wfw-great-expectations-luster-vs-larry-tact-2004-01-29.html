WFW Great Expectations: Luster vs. Larry Tact

29 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 29 January 2004 The RP/Angle deadline is Wednesday, February 11 11:59 EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
Starbreaker — 29 January 2004 Fade in: Larry Tact stands in front of a WFW backdrop; dressed in stonewashed blue jeans, black boots, and a white, sleeveless ribbed shirt. Tact looks calm, save for a bit of curiosity showing through a small grin.
Tact: Great Expectations....I know that I've come into the right promotion, at the right time, when even their shows are coordinated with my own thoughts or feelings, in some shape or form..(chuckles).. But before I get onto that, I should take a bit of time to go back and speak on Sean Edmunds. Yes, I bet a part of you is looking out for me to do just what you said, Edmunds. It's no secret to anyone, with common sense, that our match at Mind Over Matter saw me at a disadvantage. I knew your little toy Sherri would play some part in your match. I just didn't know how large a part, and whether she'd pay for it. Well, in the end she didn't pay for it, and played a big part. Even the impartial announcers couldn't avoid recognizing it. But just the same, a win is a win, and victory went to you, Edmunds. I doubt you proved to anyone that you were the better wrestler; seeing as your wrestling didn't come to save you from mine, when our respective repetoires collided. And clearly, you didn't wipe me from the face of WFW. But for you, seeing that 'W' in the column is what you look out for most, and so good for you.
Tact pauses and chuckles. Tact: It's just too bad you didn't get more out of that win, Edmunds! I mean, Scotty Michaels is probably already getting set to call you a coward and a 'weak' man who 'needs' a woman to save his ***. I guess you did mislead people into thinking that, what with your match against Copycat...then the triple threat, and now this last match. Your singles record looks a bit shabby, underneath the cover of numbers. Don't get me wrong, I'm not trying to completely discredit your image. You have gone through the places you've needed to in order to get that Heavyweight Title shot. It's just that...well, I don't value title shots, or belts, quite that much, Edmunds. Not enough to make myself look like a coward, weak, and lacking confidence in myself, to the point where I need certain...assistance. That's why I knew you'd more than likely hurt yourself, more than I, in our match....which is exactly what happened. Yeah, I lost, but I showed more. I was the more impressive between the two of us, Edmunds. You won, and rather than grabbing the opportunity and earning one more win, outwrestling me and cementing your status....you botched. You showed how your time back has worn on you...made you lazy. Perhaps you do need that crutch at ringside. Or maybe you just need to get rid of it, and start proving on your own, once again, what you seem to believe inside of yourself. In any case, I'll be the bearer of more bad news for you. That match just wasn't very conclusive, at Mind Over Matter. And something tells me...you'll be seeing me again far sooner than you'd like. It might not be in the immediate future, as I'm still new here. But I'm not going anywhere...and I'm only getting better. So don't expect me to remain idle and wait for you to fall. Like I said, Edmunds, I don't wait for things to happen. I take iniatiative and make them happen! So if that means going through the ranks to you, then so be it. I hardly have a problem with earning the place at which I deserve to be in this promotion. So I'll be seeing you....
Tact takes a minute to pace around in a circle, rubbing his chin with his hand and, upon coming full circle, looking back up at the camera with a smirk.
Tact: Right now, I'm where I should be. I'm on the match card for Great Expectations. If I'm opening up the show, so be it. If I'm not far up from there...then so be it. But I'm on the card and have my third match, upcoming, against this guy Luster. I haven't seen much of you, Luster, but I know this much: if you're ahead of me in the ranks of the WFW, and we're scheduled to face off...then I need to go through and surpass you. It's pretty cut and dry, right? I know you're something of a lady's man, correct? Well don't get too involved this week, or else you won't have much energy left to face me...and that wouldn't spell good things for you. I don't care what you do with you personal life, so long as you sleep in the bed that you make. I'd be more than happy to help you to rest. All it takes is you showing up, and accepting a tactful surrender. Whether you do or not, though, I will not remain where I currently am in the ranks. And I won't let you stop me from moving upward, Luster. I don't let a loss drop my determination, or affect my confidence. I know I did everything in my power to win, and it just didn't come to fruition. There's little time to stop and gaze glassy-eyed at mistakes; one needs to put them into perspective, learn from them, and move on. Which is why Luster, one way or another...you are going down at Great Expectations! Just as Edmunds knows within himself how far he can reach, I know within myself that I am far from reaching my limit. The difference is, I'll be showing it through every one of my matches, and it will continue with this match against you, Luster. So come with your best, but make sure your expectations are not too great for you to handle. In retrospect, since my return, I realize that I haven't been as forward as I should have been; not as blunt as I typically am. Well, starting now that will change. Luster, don't forget what you believe, but don't try stretching it into something you cannot bring about. And don't worry about my own expectations. I expect you to bring a challenge to me. However, don't expect me to lower what I expect of myself. I know I have what it takes to beat you, and I will show as much at the Pay-Per-View. So don't expect to have a pleasant night with the ladies after our match. You'd be better off expecting...a humbling!
Fade out. coolmint21 — 2 February 2004 FADEIN: Luster is sitting and relaxing with his ladies at his newly built home in Atlanta. The camera zooms in on Luster as he begins to talk about his upcoming match at Great Expectations against the ever popular Larry Tact. He is wearing his Luster Wear t-shirt that he bought while taking the Scotty Michaels shopping. P-Nut, his assistant, is at his side.
(Luster looks upset about Larry Tact's comments and P-Nut is listening to some music on his headphones. The ladies are rubbing Luster up and down.)
Luster: You want to be at the ranks of the Funkiest Wrestler in the WFW? Luster has come a long way in this pathetic federation and made it his own, main squeeze. Luster has helped the man who should be champ. Luster ain't gonna sit down and let another jealous spectator take his spotlight. You maybe determined and have what it takes but Luster is smarter. Luster gots Playas Inc. at his side and of course he gots all those b*tches, too.
(P-Nut with his headphones on, yells out) P-Nut: Tell that mothaf*cka who's boss. He's a punk. Luster: Don't be likin' you talkin' all that smackdiddly to him. Shut your mouth, punka** b*tch. Luster will drop the package like he never done before. If you ain't seen the package you will and you know why the ladies love, Luster. Oh yes they love the package and they even love the taste of it.
Luster: Luster can hear the ladies cheerin' for him already. They say "Luster, Luster." "We love your package, Lusty." "Kick that Larry Crap at Great Lustations." That is what they would say, mandingo. Luster is back to his old self so don't "expect" to win, b*tch.
(Luster kicks the camera down and onto the floor.) FADEOUT: The scene fades to black. Starbreaker — 4 February 2004 Fade in: on a WFW backdrop, Larry Tact standing in front of it. He has on a pair of black leather pants, boots, and a blue t-shirt. His expression can be described as looking amused before he begins.
Tact: Luster, is that what you like to do? Is that 'your' element? Hanging around the ladies, making sure to look cool...probably on something....is that the image you want to project? If so, well that's all good and fine. But don't expect people to believe that you've made this promotion 'your own.' Don't expect people to do more than shake their heads in disbelief when you call the WFW 'pathetic.' Saying things like that will get you just the opposite reaction of what you seem to want. Nobody's going to see you as 'cool' because you're dissing WFW. Nobody's going to care about a word you have to say if you're delusional. So the question I have for you is...why say it at all?
Tact pauses, crossing his arms over his chest, as his expression grows slightly more grim. Tact: I'm not preaching to know the exact way to make a good representation of yourself, because there really isn't any one way. But what you choose to do, Luster...it's just disgusting. It disgusts me. Last year I sought to clean out a promotion of its trash. I wanted to make it so that the place had only the finest wrestling talent; entertaining performers on their roster who deserved to be there. People who worked their ***es off each and every night. Eventually, I realized that even doing that in one promotion wouldn't help all of wrestling. I'd have to go to every single promotion doing the same thing. But the point is that I cast the image that I wanted to, of myself. I showed, quite clearly, why I was there and what I intended to accomplish. I was there to humble those who thought they could half-*** it around. I made people see that I wasn't fooling around. But Luster, you're unfocused. You lack that precise aim. You don't seem to have a goal. Instead, you're helping Scotty Michaels and letting him have all the glory. There's nothing much in it for you, as we all can see, because Michaels barely acknowledges anyone but himself now that he has the title. Sure, you got in that Triangle tag match, but what good did it do for you? You have a common problem....you're not using your skills to show what you can really do, Luster.
Tact uncrosses his arms and puts a hand to his chin, propping it up and using his other arm to support his elbow.
Tact: But you aren't trash, Luster. You're just someone who hasn't tapped into their own potential yet. Maybe that's why we're facing off at this Pay-Per-View. They know I was on my way to giving Edmunds a flogging via wrestling him down. He knows it; like a black blotch amongst his visions of glory. But perhaps the Board of Directors want you to see, first hand, just how good someone can be when they've tapped into their potential. I'm not the best that I can be; maybe I never will be. But I will always strive to become better. And thinking over it....
Tact pauses and shakes his head, laughing. Tact: Nah....I doubt you're anything more than an appeasement to me, Luster! That's right...all you are is a way for me to, once again, display what a multiversatile wrestler such as myself can do. And in the process, I can show what Edmunds had coming to him at Mind Over Matter. It's nice to think the Board would be looking to have their lesser talented wrestlers find some guidance through people like me...but I doubt they'd think of that. So I won't fool myself, and I won't fool you, Luster. This is about me going into Great Expectations, and showing that I'm not going to remain on this end of the card for long. But in the process, perhaps you'll find something that might cause you to turn yourself around, Luster. Perhaps you'll find where you can tap that potential of yours after you've tapped in the ring, and accepted....a tactful surrender.
Fade out. coolmint21 — 4 February 2004 FADEIN: Luster is in the WFW Arena. He was being interviewed and walks away. He now is heading towards the bathroom to take a dump and on his way, he is interupted by his assistant, P-Nut. Luster is wearing his new t-shirt that says "I be the Luster King."
(Luster and P-Nut slap hands as usual and give each other the man hug.) P-Nut: Playa, you gots to puts a stop to the punka** talking smackdiddly on your a**. He be saying you ain't gots no skillz and sh*t. He don't know you and he just a no p*ssy gettin' mothaf*cka. He ain't been laid since I had real teeth. Know what I mean, playa.
Luster: Luster knows what be goi...wait he said Luster ain't got no skillz. Luster made this federation. He brought the package. Luster made Playaz Inc. Luster gots his own clothing line and sh*t. Luster ain't no punk.
P-Nut: He said you were Scotty's b*tch. He said you work for him, playa. He also said yo girl Skittle is a schank. He said she looks all busted up.
Luster: Now that motha****a has gone too far. Luster gots something for his a**. You don't just be takin sh*t on some dudes girl. Luster is gonna break him off like Luster did to Shawn Hart. Luster didn't do that by being Michael's b*tch.
(Luster turns to the camera.) Luster: Luster knows you saw what Luster did to Shawn Fart. Luster bashed his head in like some melons. Is that what you want, Larry Crap? You want Luster to hurt your head and your heart. Consider the defeat Fart had to face after Luster screwed him over like the girl Luster gots at home. Luster has the skillz, he has the package, and he has the girls. You don't just show up and talk sh*t. You must win to talk smack, Crap. You must want yo a** kicked. Luster ain't scared.
P-Nut: Luster gots it goin' on, Larry Playa. He knows the game better then you. Luster: Luster will see you at Great Lustations, mandingo. Luster gonna ride the Funkytown Express straight up your a**, mandingo.
(Luster turns away from the camera.) Luster: P-Nut, Luster gots to go take a dump or a Larry Crap. (Luster and P-Nut laugh their a**es off on their way to the bathroom.) FADEOUT: Fade to Black. Starbreaker — 6 February 2004 Fade in: on Larry Tact, standing in front of a WFW backdrop wearing stonewashed blue jeans, a black shirt and long, leather coat. He laughs but doesn't look very happy as he stands with his arms crossed.
Tact: What is this, the three ring circus act, minus one? Luster, the obedient, all-gullible trained chimp and his master, P-Nut? Come on! I didn't expect such low standards of you, Luster.
Tact gives a disgusted snort. Tact: Are you feeling some sort of obligation to try and make me, along with the rest of WFW, stop watching your promos? Stop taking you seriously? I didn't come here to find a punk with the vocabulary of a high school kid looking for a fight! I didn't come to Worlds Finest Wrestling to find a couple of stooges who want to see how much profanity they can ring off in each promo! I'm here to wrestle solid competition. But at Great Expectations, Luster, I think I'm going to be greatly disappointed. You're falling way short of anything 'great'...at least in a positive way.
Tact puts his arms in his coat pockets and shrugs. Tact: It's your choice to make your image what it will be. I can't say what you want out of being here, only you can. But screw around and take me lightly? Do that, Luster, and you'll find yourself experiencing a whole lot of pain during and after our match. Because the thing I control, is how much pressure to put on. I dictate how much I put out for each match. And I always give it everything I've got. Whether it crushes you or narrowly beats you is not my concern. As long as I gain the pinfall or submission, that's what counts. But how well or poorly you weather my force, is up to you. Stop screwing around would be my advice to you, Luster. It's past time to get serious. When you do, I'll be waiting. And if you don't...well, then I'll see you in the ring, cuz time is previous, and I don't intend to waste anymore of it on the circus act.
Tact spits on the floor and walks off. Fade out. coolmint21 — 9 February 2004 FADEIN: Luster is hitting the bag in WFW Lockeroom. He is wearing his "Luster King" t-shirt. He looks pretty serious, days before his match with Larry Tact.
(Luster turns to the camera and begins to address Tact's latest comments.)
Luster: That MoFo wants to see the seriousness of Luster. Luster don't be gettin' paid enough to be serious, mandingo. Luster do get paid enough to whoop Larry Crap's a** at Great Lustations.
Luster: Luster is already on the path to greatness and Crap is on his way to the bottom once Luster hurts him so bad that he don't wanna be in no ring again. Luster may not be educated in the fine arts and sh*t, but he gots all the ladies. The ladies are Luster's fuel that be what keep him going in this second rate federation. They should change the name to LFW. Luster's Finest Wrestling. Luster put this fed on the map with his style and funkyness.
Luster: When ever you gots something to say to Luster you will refer to him as His Funkyness. You will be giving Luster respect after he busts yo a**. Luster don't be takin' kindly to disrespect, mandingo.
Luster: Luster will see you later. Be careful out there, it is dangerously funky. FADEOUT: fade to black
